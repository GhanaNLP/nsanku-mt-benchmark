SƐNEA ƐBAA BƐYƐ SƐ ƆBONTOYA, MPATWA KETEWA NO, DAA "WALK" FAA NSUO NO MU BASABASAYƐ HO

WƆKYERƐ SƐ, na Ɔbaa Panyin-nananom wɔ hɔ, na ne nneɛma ayera. Ɔkaa sɛ "the water animals" na wɔde (nneɛma) no kɔeɛ. Na kɔtɔ no te hɔ, na ɔka sɛ, "Ah, wode yɛn ani abere (wɔ aniwuo mu), na nea yɛrehyɛ sɛ yɛbɛyɛ de akyerɛ wo nea ɛwɔ yɛn ti mu ne (eyi): kɔfa kɔbere adididan bi ba na yɛn nyinaa mfa (yɛn) nusu nhyɛ mu ma, na nea ɔntumi nnyigye mma no nhyɛ mu ma no, ɔno na ɔagye (nneɛma no). "

Ɔde kɔbere sanku no bae. Okoto, kɔtɔ no, sui na ɔhyɛɛ mu ma; wɔtoo mu ma; Mercy baeɛ na ɔsuo; Okawa baeɛ na ɔsuo; Oyoyo, ɔwɔ-nsuo no, baeɛ na ɔsuo; Otidi baeɛ na ɔsuo; Obonko, kɔtɔ no, baeɛ na ɔsuo; Akresresɛ baeɛ na ɔsuo; Opitiri baeɛ na ɔsuo; Ɔbɔfoɔ baeɛ na ɔsuo; nsuo mu mmoa no nyinaa baeɛ na wɔsuo. Awieeɛ koraa ne Obontoya, Mpatwa ketewa no, baeɛ sɛ ɔbɛsu; ɔnnyaa nsuo firii nani mu; enti wɔhunuu sɛ Obontoya na ɔde (nneɛma) no kɔeɛ.

Nanso Ɔbaa Panyin-nananom kaa sɛ, "Me renyɛ wo hwee. " Ɛhɔ ara na abɔdeɛ no nyinaa kaa sɛ, "Yɛn deɛ, yɛbɛdi no so nkonim, ɛfiri sɛ wama yɛn anim agu ase. " Enti wɔbɔɔ no.

Bere a wogyaee no bɔɔ no wiei no, Nana Panyin no kae sɛ: "Megyaw wo hɔ ma woretu kwan (daa); obiara mpɛ sɛ ɔne wo yɛ hwee da. "

Wei nti na, sɛ mmaa "go splashing" (ma mpataa) a, wɔhu Ɔbontoya kyinkyin (mprempren) wɔ ha ne hɔ.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
