ƐKƆƆ SO SƐ NA ƆBONTOYA, MPATWA KETEWA NO, DAA "BASABASA" KWAN SO "WALKING" WƆ NSUO NO MU.

WƆKA SƐ, na aberewa nana wɔ hɔ, na ne agyapadeɛ ayera. Ɔkae sɛ, "nsuo mu mmoa" na wɔde nneɛma no kɔeɛ. Na kɔtɔ no te hɔ, na ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Ah, woama yɛn aniwa atɔ aniwu, na nea yɛrehyɛ ho nyansa sɛ yɛbɛyɛ de akyerɛ wo nea ɛwɔ yɛn etire no ne sɛ, kɔfa kɔbere dadesɛn bi mmra na yɛn nyinaa mfa nnisuo nhyɛ mu, na nea ɔntumi nnsu mfa nhyɛ mu no, ɔno na wanya nneɛma no. "

Ɔde kɔbere dadesɛn no baeɛ. Okoto, kɔtɔ no, suiɛ na ɛhyɛɛ mu ma; wɔyii no gui; Mmɔborɔhunu baeɛ na ɔwii; Okawa baeɛ na ɔwii; Oyoyo, ɔwɔ-nsuom baeɛ na ɔwii; Otidi baeɛ na ɔwii; Ɔbonko, mmɔtɔ baeɛ na ɔwii; Akɔreseɛ baeɛ na wɔwii; Opitiri baeɛ na ɔwii; Ɔkɔbɔ baeɛ na ɔwii; nsuo mu mmoa nyinaa baeɛ na wɔwii. Akyire (of all) Ɔbontoya, Mpatwa ketewa no, baeɛ sɛ ɔrebɛsu; ɔnnyaa nsuo mfi naniwa mu, nti na wɔhunuu sɛ Ɔbontoya agye nneɛma no.

Nanso aberewa nana kae sɛ, "Me renyɛ wo hwee. " Ɛhɔ ara na abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no nyinaa kae sɛ, "Yɛn deɛ, yɛbɛboro no, ɛfiri sɛ wama yɛn aniwu. " Enti wɔbɔɔ no.

Bere a wowiee no pempem no, aberewa nana kae sɛ: "Megyaw wo ma woretu mmirika a wonni botae biara; obiara mpɛ sɛ ɔne wo di nkitaho da. "

Ɛno ne sianteɛ a ɛwɔ hɔ sɛ, bere a mmaa "go splashing" (pɛ apataa) no, wɔhu Ɔbontoya kyinkyin (seesei) wɔ ha ne hɔ.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
