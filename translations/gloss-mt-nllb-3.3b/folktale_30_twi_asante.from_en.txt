Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA NTƆKWAW BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU

ƐDII BERE BI A ƆBARIMA BI A WƆFRƐ NO Otan-to-be-contradicted, na ɛno nti, ɔkɔɔ akyekye akuraa ketewa bi (ne nkutoo) kɔtenaa mu. Na abɔdeɛ a wɔfrɛ no Ɔtwee no kɔɔ ne nkyɛn, na ɔne no kɔtenaa nnuadewa dua bi ase. Na nnuadewa no bi hwee ase. Ɔtwee kaa sɛ, "Agya Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted, wo mmɛ no abere. " Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted kaa sɛ, "Saa ara na bankye no teɛ; Sɛ abere a, nsã abiɛsa no abere prɛko pɛ; Sɛ abere a, mitwitwa no, na sɛ meyow ngo no a, ɛyɛ nsu nkuku abiɛsa a ngo ahyɛ mu ma. Na mede ngo no kɔ Akase kɔtɔ Akase abeawa bi; Akase abeawa no ba na ɔde ma. awoɔ ma me nana, a ɔwo me maame, a ɔwo me nso; ɛna awo me a, me gyina hɔ dedaw. " Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, "As for all that, you lie. " Na Hater-to-be-contradicted faa poma bɔɔ Ɔtwee no ti, na ɔkunkum no. Akyire yi na abea ketewa no nso baeɛ. Na ɔde no kɔtenaa palm dua no ase, na saa ara na ɛkɔɔ so. Na saa ara na ɛbaa mmoa no nyinaa so. Afei, Kwaku Anans, Anans no, kɔfaa natadeɛ no, na ɔfaa ne bag, de guu ne mmati so, na ɔkɔɔ Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted kuro no mu. Ɔmaa no akwaaba sɛ, "Agya, anɔpa. " Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted buae sɛ, "Y'Aku, na ɛhe na worekɔ? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Mereba wo nkyɛn. " Na ɔfaa nakonnwa no de sii mmɛdua no ase. Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted kaa sɛ, "Bɔ aduane ma Ananse nni. " Na bere a na ɛreyɛ adididan no, Kwaku Ananse ne Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted tenaa mmɛdua no ase. Na mmɛdua no bi hwee ase. Na Kwaku Ananse faa wɔn de guu ne kotoku mu. Na ɔkɔɔ so yɛɛ saa kosii sɛ ne kotoku no mu yɛɛ ma. Wɔde aduan no bae, na Kwaku Ananse dii. Bere a owiee didi no, mmɛw no bi hwee ase, na Kwaku Anans kae sɛ, "Agya Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted, wo mmɛw abere. " Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ wɔn su sɛ wobedua saa ara; Sɛ wɔn abere a, nsã abiɛsa abere prɛko pɛ; Sɛ wɔn abere a, mitwitwa wɔn, na sɛ mema wɔnoa ngo no a, ɛyɛ nsu nkuku abiɛsa a ngo ahyɛ mu ma, na mede ngo no kɔ Akas. e, sɛ yɛbɛtɔ Akase aberewa bi; Akase aberewa no ba bɛwo ma me nana a ɔwo me maame, na ɔno nso awo me. Bere a ɛna no wo me no, na me gyina hɔ dedaw. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Wo nni atoro, nea woka no yɛ nokware; Me deɛ, mewɔ nkraman bi a wɔgyina me afuo mu, na sɛ wɔn ho akye a, me ne nnua aduɔson nson a ɛwɔ ntokuru tenten (sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a metumi adu fam), nanso ɛno mpo nnu, enti meda me akyi, na metumi de me kotodwe twitwa wɔn. " Hate-to-be-contradicted kaa sɛ, "Oh, me te ase. d; ɛnnɛ-ɛkyena mɛba abɛhwɛ. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Surely. " Bere a Ananse no rekɔ (home), ɔkekaa mmɛ a ɔaboaboa ano no, na ɔhwie guu kwan no so. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɛrekyerɛ no, Hate-to-be-contradicted bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛkɔ Ananse no akuraa ase. Afei bere a Ananse no dii kan bae (da a edi kan no), na wakɔ ka kyerɛɛ ne mma sɛ, "Ɔbarima bi bɛba ha, na ɔmpɛ sɛ wɔkasa tia no, na sɛ ɔba abɛbisa me nsɛm a, ɛsɛ sɛ woka kyerɛ no sɛ ɛnora na mekae sɛ merekɔ baabi, bere a me kotodwe no bubuu mmeae ason, na ɛsɛ sɛ mede kɔma ɔteteefo bi ma wosiesie, na esiane sɛ wantumi anwie wɔ berɛ no, ɔka sɛ wakɔma adwuma no asa. Ɛnkyɛreeɛ, na Hate-to-be-contradicted nso aba. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ɛhe na wo papa kɔeɛ? Wɔbuaa sɛ, Alas, papa kɔɔ baabi bi ɛnora, na ne kɔn mu abubu wɔ mmeaeɛ nson, na ɔde kɔmaa ɔgyefoɔ bi, nanso wantumi anwie adwuma no wɔ berɛ a ɛsɛ mu, na papa akɔhwɛ sɛ wɔwie, na (wo) papa, woanhunu mogya a ɛwɔ kwan no so anaa? Hater-to-be-contradicted kaa sɛ, Mahunu. Hater-to-be-contradicted bisaa sɛ, Na Ɛhe na wo maame wɔ? (Nkekaho no ba no buae,) Maame nso, ɛnora ɔkɔ asubɔnten no mu, na anka ne nsu kuruwa no bɛhwe ase na abubu sɛ wamfa ne ho anhyɛ mu denam akyere a ɔkyeree no wɔ bere ano no so, nanso wanwie koraa sɛ obegye no afi asehwe ase, na wasan aba nnɛ. Hatred-to-be-contradicted anka hwee. Afei Kwaku Ananse nso bae. Ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, Monnoa aduane bi a Hate-to-be-contradicted bɛtumi adi. Bere a mmofra no resiesie aduane no, wɔde afiri ketewaa bi pɛ na ɛyɛ, nanso wɔde cassava pii na ɛyɛe; Wɔmaa nkwan no yɛɛ hyew paa. Bere a wowiei no, wɔde sii Hate-to-be-contradicted anim. Hate-to-be-contradicted dii. Afei na cassava no yɛ no yaw, na ɔpɛ sɛ owu. Ɔkae sɛ, Ntikuma (Ananse ba no), fa nsuo ma me ma mennom. Ntikuma kɔɔ asuo no nkyɛn, wanfa nsuo no amma da. Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted kaa sɛ, Ntikuma, ɛhe na saa nsuo no wɔ? Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Ah, nsuo a yɛwɔ ha (wɔ yɛn nsuo-kutu no mu) no yɛ nneɛma mmiɛnsa; ɛno a ɛyɛ papa deɛ no di kan, ɛno a ɛyɛ me maame yɔnko yere deɛ no wɔ mfinimfini, na ɛno a ɛyɛ me maame deɛ no wɔ ase (kutu no mu); na me pɛ sɛ meyi ma mo nsuo a ɛyɛ me maame deɛ no nko ara nti, sɛ me nni t ake ahwɛyiye kɛse bere a woretwe no, ɛbɛma abusuakuw ntam akasakasa aba. Hater-to-be-contradicted kae sɛ, Wo brat ketewa, wodi atoro. Ɛntɛm ara, Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Bɔɔ no ma onwu. Hater-to-be-contradicted kae sɛ, Dɛn nti na wɔbɛbɔ me ma moawu? Ananse kae sɛ, Wose wani nnye sɛ obi bɛbɔ wo so, nanso woapo obi so, ɛno nti na meka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wɔbɔ wo ma wo d ie. Enti wɔbɔɔ Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted ma owui. Afei Kwaku Anans twitwaa ne nam no asinasin nketenkete na ɔhwetee so. Ɛno nti na nnipa pii a wɔtan sɛ wɔbɛbɔ wɔn ho abira no wɔ abusuakuw no mu.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
