Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ABIRABƆ YI BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU

NA ƐYƐ obarima bi a wɔfrɛ no Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted, na ɛno nti, ɔkɔsii akuraa ketewa bi (ne nkutoo) kɔtenaa mu. Na abɔdeɛ a wɔfrɛ no Ɔtwee no kɔsraa no, na ɔne no kɔtenaa nnuadewa bi nan ase. Na mmɛ no bi hwe ase. Ɔtwee no kae sɛ, "Agya Ɔtan, nea ɔne no bɔ abira no, wo mmɛ abere." Ɔtan kaa sɛ, "Ɛno ne abɛ mu abɔdeɛ. Sɛ wɔabere a, asã ahorow mmiɛnsa na ɛbere prɛko pɛ; sɛ wɔabere a, mitwa, na sɛ memia ngo no a, ɛyɛ nsuo nkukuo mmiɛnsa a ngo ahyɛ mu ma. Na mede ngo no kɔ Akase kɔtɔ Akase aberewa. Akase aberewa no ba na ɔde ma. me nanabea, a ɔso me maame, a dane so wo me; bere a maame so me no, na megyina hɔ dedaw. " Ɔtwee no kae sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ nyinaa deɛ, wodi atoro. " Na Ɔtan-a-bɛyɛ-nsɛm no faa poma bɔɔ Ɔtwee no etire, kunkum no. Afei abedee ketewa no nso bae. Na ɔde no kɔtenaa nnuadewa no ase, na saa adeɛ no ara na ɛkɔɔ so. Na saa na ɛkɔɔ so wɔ mmoa no nyinaa mu. Afei, Kwaku Ananse, Ananse, kɔgyee ne ntoma, kɔgyee ne kotokuo, kɔtoo ne mmatiri so, kɔɔ Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted akuraa ase. Ɔmaa no akwaaba sɛ, "Agya, anɔpa pa. " Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted buae sɛ, "Y'Aku, na wo nso worekɔ he? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Mereba wo nkyɛn. " Na ɔfaa n'akonnwa no de sii nnua no ase. Ɔtan a ɔbɛsɔre atia no kaa sɛ, "Didi aduane ma Ananse no. " Na bere a wɔresiesie aduane no, Kwaku Ananse ne Ɔtan a wɔbɛsɔre atia no tenaa nnuadewa no ase. Na mmɛ no bi hwe ase. Na Kwaku Ananse faa no kɔtoo ne kotokuo no mu. Na ɔkɔɔ so yɛɛ saa kosii sɛ ne kotokuo no bɛhyɛɛ ma. Wɔde aduane no bae, na Kwaku Ananse dii. Bere a wɔdii no wiei no, mmɛ no bi hwee ase, na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Agya, Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted, wo mmɛ no abere. " Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ wɔn abɔdeɛ sɛ wɔbɛbere saa. Sɛ wɔbere a, asã ahorow abiɛsa na ɛbere prɛko pɛ; sɛ wɔbere a, mitwa, na sɛ meboaboa ano de yi ngo no a, ɛyɛ nsuo nkukuo mmiɛnsa a ngo ahyɛ mu ma, na mede ngo no kɔ Akas e, sɛ mɛtɔ Akase aberewa; Akase aberewa no ba na ɔma me nanabea a ɔwoo me maame no awoɔ, sɛ ɔmfa dane nwo me. Bere a maame asoa me no, megyina hɔ dedaw. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Wo nni atoro, nea woka no yɛ nokware; Me deɛ, mewɔ nkruma bi a ɛregyina wɔ me afuo mu, na sɛ ɛduru a, mefa nnyinasoɔ a ɛyɛ tenten aduɔson nson bɔ mu, nanso ɛnnu mpo, enti medana m'akyi, na metumi de me kɔteɛ twitwa. " Ɔtanfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "O, metumi ate ase. d; ɔkyena mɛba abɛhwɛ. " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho. " Bere a na Ananse rekɔ fie no, ɔweee mmɛ a na wɔaboaboa ano no, na ɔprapraa guu kwan no so. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma fii aseɛ bɛdaa adi no, Hate-to-be-contradicted sii gyinaeɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ Ananse akuraa no ase. Afei, berɛ a Ananse dii kan baeɛ no, na wakɔka akyerɛ ne mmofra sɛ, obarima bi bɛba ha, na ɔmpɛ sɛ obiara ne no bɔ abira, na berɛ a ɔbɛba abɛbisa me nsɛm no, ɛsɛ sɛ woka kyerɛ no sɛ, ɛnora na mekaeɛ sɛ merekɔ baabi, berɛ a me kɔteɛ no bubuu wɔ mbeaeɛ nson, na ɛsɛ sɛ mede kɔmaa tomfoɔ ma wɔsiesie, na ɛnam sɛ wɔantumi anwie no wɔ ɛberɛ no, ɔka sɛ wakɔma wɔawie adwuma no. Ɛnkyɛree na Ɔtan-a-bɛyɛ-ɔsɔretia no baeɛ. Ɔkae sɛ, Wo agya no akɔ he? Wɔbuaa no sɛ, Awerɛhosɛm ne sɛ, agya kɔɔ baabi ɛnora, na ne kɔteɛ no abubu wɔ mmeae ahodoɔ nson, na ɔde kɔmaa tomfoɔ bi, nanso wantumi anwie adwuma no wɔ berɛ a ɛsɛ mu, na agya nso akɔma wɔawie no, na (wo,) Agya, woanhunu mogya no wɔ kwan no so anaa? Ɔtan kaa sɛ, Mihuu no. Ɔtan bisaa sɛ, Na ɛhe na wo maame wɔ? (Spider ba no buaa,) Maame nso, ɛnora kɔɔ asuwa hɔ, na anka ne nsuo kuruwa no bɛhwe aseɛ abubu sɛ wamfa ne nsa anka no berɛ so, nanso wamfa ne nsa anka no nwie koraa, na wasan aba da a ɛdi hɔ no. Ɔtan anka hwee. Afei na Kwaku Ananse adu. Ɔkae sɛ, Bɔ aduane bi a Ɔtan a ɔrebɛyɛ ɔsɔretia no betumi adi. Bere a mmofra no noa aduane no, wɔde apatre ketewa baako pɛ na ɛyɛɛ, nanso wɔde ɛmo dodoɔ kɛse na ɛyɛɛ nkwan no hyew paa. Bere a wowiee no, wɔde sii hɔ wɔ Ɔtan a ɔrebɛyɛ ɔsɔretia no anim. Ɔtan dii nea ɔne no nhyia no. Afei na ɛmo no yɛ no yaw, na ɔpɛe sɛ owu. Ɔkaeɛ sɛ, Ntikuma (Ananse no abofra) yi nsuo ma me mennom. Ntikuma kɔɔ nsubea hɔ, wamfa nsuo no amma da. Ɔtan-a-bɛyɛ-nkonnye kaa sɛ, Ntikuma, ɛhe na saa nsuo no wɔ? Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Ah, nsuo a yɛwɔ ha (yɛn nsuo-kuku no mu) no yɛ ahodoɔ mmiɛnsa; ɛno a ɛyɛ agya dea no di kan, ɛno a ɛyɛ me maame yere dea no wɔ mfinimfini, na ɛno nso a ɛyɛ me maame dea no wɔ fam (kuku no mu); na ɛfiri sɛ mepɛ sɛ metwe ma wo nkutoo nsuo a ɛyɛ me maame dea no, sɛ mennyɛ a ake ahwɛyie kɛseɛ bere a ɔrebo no, ɛbɛma abusuakuw mu mansotwe aba. Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted kae sɛ, You little brat, you lie. Ntɛm ara, Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Beat him so that he may die. Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted kae sɛ, Why should they beat me so that I may die? Ananse kae sɛ, You say you ɔtan to be contradicted, and yet you have contradicted some one, that is why I say they must beat you so that you may d ie. Enti wɔbɔɔ Ɔtan a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔkasa tia no ma owui. Afei Kwaku Ananse twitwaa ne honam yɛɛ no asinasin nketenkete na wɔhwetee hɔ. Ɛno nti na nnipa a ɔtan sɛ wɔbɛkasa tia wɔn no dɔɔso wɔ abusuakuo no mu.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
