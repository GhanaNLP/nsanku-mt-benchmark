Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐSE YADEƐ NE NNOMAA BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU

WƆKYERƐ SƐ, na kuro kɛseɛ paa bi wɔ hɔ, na saa anomaa bi sɔre da biara da sɛ ɔbɛba abɛyi nkurɔfoɔ ano. Afei Kwaku Ananse, Ananse no, tee asɛm yi, na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ saa kuro no mu. Bere a ɔduruu kuro no mu no, ɔka kyerɛɛ kurom hɔfoɔ no sɛ, Dɛn nti na kuro a ɛte sɛɛ mu no, nnipa a wɔwɔ mu no nyinaa abɔ wɔn abɔso? Ɔmanpanin a ɔwɔ kuro no mu no kaa sɛ, Agya, anomaa ketewa bi a ɛte sɛɛ, bere a owia no gyina hɔ a ɛte sɛɛ (a ɔde ne nsa kyerɛ soro), baeɛ bɛkyeree yɛn, na ɔtwee yɛn abɔso. Kwaku Ananse, Ananse no, kaa sɛ, Me deɛ, mepene saa adeɛ no so, na me m yself kill it. The head-man of the village said, Oh, this town was helpless, and you, a neck standing all alone, what can you do? Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Look at this, my knife; as soon as I have sharpened it, I shall cut off its head like this, bam! Bere a owia no gyinaa hɔ (i. e. at sunset) no, Bird no bae bɛtenaa dua bi so, na ɔmaa ne nne so sɛ:

E, e, jaws, jaws, e, E!

Jaws, me kɔ, E!

Jaws, maba, oh!

Na Ananse ama ne nne so sɛ:

Abɔdweɛ ahorow, nagya, e, E!

Abɔdwoesɛm, ne papa, E!

Ne papa, ne papa, E!

Nagya, maba, oh!

E! ne papa.

Ɛnna Bird no baeɛ na ɔgyinaa abɔnten kwan no ano. Ɔkaa sɛ, Hwan nne na ɛyɛ saa, a ɛbua me san, na ɛrebu manananom animtiaa? Mepae akuraa asefoɔ no nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn nyinaa, obiara ntumi nkasa. Na ɔsan nso de ne nne so:

E, e, jaws, jaws, e, E!

Jaws, me kɔ, E!

Jaws, maba, oh!

Na Kwaku Ananse buaa sɛ:

Abɔdweɛ ahorow, nagya, e, E!

Abɔdwoesɛm, ne papa, E!

Ne papa, ne papa, E!

Nagya, maba, oh!

E, ne papa.

Afei Kwaku Ananse ba bɛtenaa napon (ne fie) ano. Afei nsɛm ne wɔn akyi (i. e. me werɛ fi sɛ mɛka) sɛ kuro no mufoɔ nyinaa adwane afiri Kwaku Ananse hɔ. Na Nnomaa no adwane abɛdu Kwaku Ananse (fie) ano; na Kwaku Ananse adwane akɔhyɛ ne dan mu, na ɔgyaa sheet no (pon no so), na Nnomaa no maa ne nne so sɛ:

E, e, jaws, jaws, e, E!

Jaws, me kɔ, E!

Afei Kwaku Ananse dwanee kɔɔ akraman a wɔde di dwuma no mu, na ɔtee ne nne dwoodwoo paa:

Abɔdwe ahodoɔ, nagya, e, E!

Abɔdwoesɛm, ne papa, E!

Nagya, maba, oh!

E! ne papa.

Afei na Anomaa no reforo akɔ dan no mu sɛ ɔrekɔyi asau a wɔde di dwuma no, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛkyere Kwaku Anans, nanso Kwaku Anans faa ne sekan twitwaa ne ti, bam! na Anomaa no fã bi tuu kwan kɔɔ Po no Ano. Kwaku, Ananse, faa nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn a ɛkyekyere Nnomaa no, de sii hɔ; na ɔde Nnomaa no ti sii so; na ɔkɔɔ kɔfrɛɛ mpɔtam hɔfoɔ sɛ, Mubetumi aba, efisɛ makum saa Nnomaa no. Wɔkae sɛ, Aane, ɛno ne Kwaku Ananse, ɔyɛ ɔbenfoɔ a wagye din, ebia ɔnka nokware. Wɔkae sɛ, Paw abofra baako a ɔyɛ anyansafoɔ na kɔhwɛ. Nyansafoɔ no kɔɔ hɔ kɔhwɛɛ. ɔde ne ho sieeɛ wɔ baabi, na ɔhwɛɛ, na ɔhunuu sɛ aboa no ti da hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔkaa sɛ, Mommra, nnamfoɔ, Kwaku Ananse nni atoro. Nnipa no nyinaa baeɛ. Afei yi, bere a ɔgyee nabɔgye no, ɔde bɛbɔɔ so. Nanso wo a wannya wo deɛ no, wokɔfaa wo yɔnko deɛ; ɛno nti na wunya nsensan yareɛ no. Afei, wɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔfaa saa Nnomaa no firii Po no Ano, na wɔtwitwaa no nketenkete paa, na wɔhwetee no baabiara. Saa kwan yi so na nnomaa dodoɔ bi baa mmusuakuo no mu.

Me kyerɛwtohɔ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na fa a aka no nso kamfo me.
