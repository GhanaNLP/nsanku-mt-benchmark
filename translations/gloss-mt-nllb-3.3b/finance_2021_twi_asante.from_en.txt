iv Ghana | aban - 2021 ɔmanfoɔ sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ

Asɛmti: Sikasɛm mu ahoɔden foforo denam Awieeɛ, Nkabom ne Toatoa soɔ so

NKRA A ƐFIRI ƆSOAFOƆ A ƆHWƐ SIKASƐM SO

Ghana nnipa pa wɔ Ɔpɛnimba 2020 mu no, wɔtoo aba sɛ H. E. Ɔmampanyin, Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo, bɛtena hɔ ama asɛmfua foforo de akɔ so ayɛ adwuma pa. Aban yi mu ahotɔ a yɛn ghanafo mfɛfo anya no, ɛnyɛ adeɛ a yɛbɛfa no kwa.

2021 sika nhyehyɛeɛ no yɛ sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ soronko a ɛbɛsan asiesie yɛn ɔman sikasɛm de abɔ asetena ho ban, ahyɛ nnwuma a ɛwɔ haw mu no den na yɛde yɛn ahoɔden asi hɔ sɛ yɛbɛsan akyekye ghana pa wɔ COVID-19 akyi. Sika nhyehyɛeɛ no gyina yɛn anisoadehunu a ɛfa ɔman yi a yɛbɛsesa na yɛakyekye sikasɛm a ɛbɛtumi agyina ne ho so.

Wɔ asɛmfua a edi kan no fã kɛse no ara mu no, yɛsan de sikasɛm no sii mmirikatuo kwan so, denam nnidisoɔ a ɛfa sikasɛm kɛseɛ nsakraeɛ ho dwumadie a wɔahyehyɛ no yie, akwan a wɔbɛfa so ama sika a wɔnya no akɔ soro, de nneɛma a wɔde wɔn ho bɛhyɛ mu a ɛma nkɔsoɔ ba, mmoa a wɔde ma ankorankoro adwumayɛbea ne asetena mu mmoa nhyehyɛe ahorow so. Ɛbɛduruu afe 2019 awieeɛ no, na yɛwɔ sikasɛm mu nsakraeɛ kwan so denneennen sɛ ɔman a ɛrekɔ kɔ soro, na sikasɛm mu nsɛnkyerɛnne a ɛyɛ anigye ne amanaman agyede nso aka yɛn ho. Kovid-19 yarebɔne a ɛtrɛɛ wiase nyinaa no kaa yɛn. Saa yaredom yi nsunsuansoɔ bɔne kɛseɛ no, sikasɛm ahodoɔ a ɛwɔ wiase nyinaa, a Ghana ka ho, tee nka.

Kovid-19 yarebɔne a ɛtrɛɛ wiase nyinaa no de ahokyerɛ bebree bae, na ɛkaa yɛn nnwuma ne yɛn da biara asetena kwan. Aban deɛ, wɔde dadwene kɛseɛ ahunu nsunsuansoɔ a oyaredɔm no de aba yɛn so sɛ nnipa, na wɔahyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ase sɛ wɔbɛsan akanyan nnwuma na wɔasan de yɛn asetena aba tebea a ɛfata mu. Yɛn anidasoɔ nyinaa nyera ɛfiri sɛ yɛanya akwan foforɔ a yɛfa so yɛ nneɛma wɔ kwan ahodoɔ a ɛnni awieeɛ so. Aban no asi ne bo sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ sɛ yɛbɛnya nnipa apɔmudenfoɔ na wayɛ nhyehyɛeɛ sɛ wɔde vaccine bɛma Ghanafoɔ sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a yɛn nyinaa bɛtena ase ayɛ den ama Ghana a yɛpɛ sɛ yɛhunu no.

Yɛne mo ayɛ adwuma wɔ ahokyerɛ mfinimfini mpo sɛ yɛn nkɔsoɔ kwan no fã na yɛatu anammɔntuo a akokoduru wom de asan de yɛn sikasɛm no asi mmirikatuo kwan so akɔ nkɔsoɔ mu. Mprempren ghanafoɔ ɔpepem 1.2 renya ntoaso sukuu a ɛyɛ papa na ɛyɛ papa ama wɔn nkwa, yɛatew sikakorabea dwumadie no ho na adetɔfoɔ dodoɔ no ara anya wɔn sika a wɔde sie afiri sikakorabea ahorow a abubu no mu. Wɔkyekyeree kar kabombea nso, na yɛhuu kar a wɔaboaboa ano no afa a edi kan wɔ yɛn dwaso ne akwan so.

Yegye tom sɛ yehyiaa nsɛnnennen bi bere a na yɛrebɔ mmɔden sɛ yɛbɛma yɛn nkɔsoɔ botaeɛ no anya nkɔsoɔ. Aban de nsi reyɛ anammɔntuo de adi saa mhaw yi ho dwuma na ɛde ahotɔ aba nnwuma mu. Sɛ nhwɛsoɔ no, yɛde CAP-BuSS nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛyɛ GH¢750 million no sii hɔ wɔ May 2020, a ɛde mmoa maa Ketewa, Adwuma nketewa ne mfinimfini ne nnwuma foforɔ pii.

Sɛ yɛntoa so wɔ anammɔntuo a akokooduru wom no dwumadie mu na yɛanya nkɔsoɔ ama obiara a, ɛno ne frɛ a ɛne sɛ yɛnka nsa nkabom mma yɛn ɔman no nnya nkɔsoɔ. Yɛde yɛn ahoɔden ne yɛn akoma nyinaa hyɛɛ bɔ sɛ yɛde yɛn ho bɛma Ghana ɔsom.

Onyankopɔn nhyira yɛn nyinaa.

Bra Ghana!

Ken Ofori-Atta, Sikasɛm soafoɔ

v Ghana | aban - 2021 ɔmanfoɔ sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ

Asɛmti: Sikasɛm mu ahoɔden foforo denam Awieeɛ, Nkabom ne Toatoa soɔ so

NNIANIM

1. Afe 2021 ɔman no sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ no yɛ nea Aban no tumi yɛeɛ wɔ afe 2020 mu no mmuano ne dodoɔ a ɛwɔ nhyehyɛeɛ sɛ wɔbɛboaboa ano wɔ sika a wɔnya mu de adi nhyehyɛeɛ ne dwumadi ahorow a ɛwɔ afe 2021 mu no ho dwuma. 2021 sika nhyehyɛeɛ no asɛmti ne Awieeɛ, Nkabom ne Toaso. Saa asɛmti yi gyina nokwasɛm a ɛyɛ sɛ mfeɛ a atwam no, sikasɛm nhyehyɛe foforo biara kura nnwuma foforɔ dwumadiwura a wɔbɛbɔ wɔn ho ban wɔ nnwuma a ɛrekɔ so ho ka ho. Ɛnyɛ sɛ eyi de nhyɛsoɔ ba yɛn sika nsɛm so nko, na mmom abakɔsɛm mu no ɛde akatua a aka a wɔantua aba, na wɔantua kontraktafoɔ pii ka wɔ berɛ a ɛsɛ mu wɔ adwuma a wɔayɛ ho. Eyi ma dwumadi ahodoɔ a ne nyinaa boɔ kɔ soro ɛfiri sɛ sikatua a wɔatwentwɛn so no twetwe mfasoɔ boɔ.

2. Aban ayɛ nhyehyɛe sɛ wɔbɛhyehyɛ akwankyerɛ foforɔ wɔ afe 2021 mu. Wɔn botaeɛ ne sɛ wɔde amammerɛ a ɛne sɛ wɔbɛwie dwumadie a ɛnni awieeɛ no bɛba awieeɛ denam bɔhyɛ ahorow a wɔde bewie dwumadi ahorow a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw no a wobedi ho dwuma so.

SIKASƐM MU NKƆSOƆ A ƐBƐBA WƆ AFE 2020 MU

Sikasɛm kɛseɛ dwumadie

3. Nsɛm a wosusuw wɔ sikasɛm mmɔdenbɔ ho wɔ afe 2020 mu ne nea edidi so yi:

• Wɔ afe no abosome nkron a ɛdi kan no mu no, gDP ankasa nkɔsoɔ mfinimfini a ɛyɛ 0. 2 ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu. Ɔman agyapade nkɔso dodow a wɔhwɛ kwan sɛ ɛbɛyɛ 0. 9 ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu wɔ afe no awieeɛ;

• GDP ankasa a ɛnyɛ ngo wɔ afe no abosome akron a edi kan no mu no nyaa 0.4 ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu mpuntuo na wɔhwɛ kwan sɛ ɛbɛwie afe no wɔ ɔha biara mu 1.6.

• December awiei nneɛma bo a ɛkɔɔ soro no yɛ ɔha biara mu 10.4 bere a wɔde toto botaeɛ a wɔasesa mu a ɛyɛ ɔha biara mu 11.0 no ho no;

• Kabimɛn kɛseɛ a ɛtɔ sin wɔ sika pɔtee gyinabea so no yɛ GDP ɔha biara mu 11.7 sɛ wɔde toto GDP ɔha biara mu 11.4 a ɛwɔ botaeɛ a wɔasesa mu no ho a;

• Wɔkyerɛw sika a ɛkari pɛ titire no ka a ɛbɔ GDP ɔha biara mu 5.3 bere a wɔde toto ka a wɔahyɛ da bɔeɛ a ɛyɛ GDP ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 4.6 ho no; na

• Amanaman ntam akoradeɛ nyinaa yɛ US$8. 6 ɔpepepem, a yƐ PƐ abosome 4.1 wɔ nneɛma a wɔde ba ɔman mu ho banbɔ mu.

Ɔman ka ho nkɔsoɔ 2020

7. Wɔ December 2020 mu no, ka a ɛda hɔ nyinaa gyinaa GH¢291. 6 billion (US$50. 8 billion), a ɛyɛ GDP ɔha mu nkyekyem 76. 1. Saa nkyekyɛmu yi ka sika a Aban no boaboaa ano de tuaa sikasɛm dwumadibea mmoa sika ne ahoɔden dwumadibea Anyinam ahoɔden a wɔtɔ a ɛde ne ho (IPPs) ahoɔden ntoboa ho ka. Sɛ sikasɛm dwumadibea mmoa sika no nka ho a, 2020 ɛka dodoɔ no yɛ GDP ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 71.5. Wɔayɛ ka a ɛda hɔ nyinaa ho asɛeɛ sɛdeɛ ɛdidi soɔ yi:

• Amannɔne bosea: GH¢141. 7 billion (US$24. 7 billion) a egyina hɔ ma ɛka a ɛda hɔ nyinaa mu ɔha biara mu 48. 6; na

• Ɔman mu ka: GH¢149. 8 billion (US$26. 1 billion) a egyina hɔ ma ɛka a ɛda hɔ nyinaa mu ɔha biara mu 51. 4.

2021 SIKA NHYEHYƐEƐ NE SIKASƐM

Sikasɛm kɛseɛ botaeɛ ma afe 2021

8. Aban sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ ne nneyɛeɛ (kyerɛ sɛ tow ne sika sɛeɛ ho gyinaeɛ horoɔ) nya sikasɛm mu nkɔsoɔ so nkɛntɛnsoɔ wɔ mfimfini berɛ mu. 2021 sika nhyehyɛeɛ no gyina sikasɛm botaeɛ akɛseɛ a ɛdidi so yi anaa ahyɛnsodeɛ ahoroɔ so:

• GDP ankasa nkɔso nyinaa yɛ ɔha biara mu 5.0;

• GDP mu nkɔsoɔ ankasa a ɛnyɛ ngoɔ a ɛyɛ ɔha mu nkyekyem 6.7;

• December awiei nneɛma bo a ɛkɔɔ soro no duu ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 8.0;

• Aban sika tɔsin a ɛyɛ GDP ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 9.5;

• Mfitiase ka a ɛyɛ GDP 1.3 ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu; na

• Amanaman ntam sika a wɔde asie nyinaa kata nnoɔma a wɔde firi amannɔne ba wɔ nneɛma ne ɔsom mu no nnu abosome 4.0.

1D1F (One District One Factory): Ɛyɛ adwumayɛbea ne nnwumakuo tebea a awieeɛ koraa no ɛde ɔman no kɔ mfididwuma kɛse mu dane. Ɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɛbɛma Ghanafoɔ anya nnwuma pii wɔ ɔman no tenten ne ne trɛw mu.

Nkyekyɛmu: Aban asoeɛ, dwumadibea ahodoɔ ne adwumakuo ahodoɔ (Asɔeɛ ne amansan adwumayɛbea ahodoɔ) kwan a wɔfa so kyekyɛ anaa wɔkyekyɛ ahonyadeɛ.

Alleviation: Eyi ne kwan a wɔfa so ma ɔhaw no so tew.

BSTEM (Sayanse nnyinasosɛm, Mfiridwuma, Mfididwuma ne Nkontaabu): asɛmfua a wɔde ka saa adesua nkyekyɛmu ahodoɔ yi bom. Wɔtaa de saa asɛmfua yi di dwuma de ma akansie a ɛwɔ nyansahu ne mfiridwuma mu nkɔsoɔ tu mpɔn wɔ sukuu ahodoɔ mu denam nhomasua ho nhyehyɛe ne adesua nhyehyɛeɛ mu apaw a wɔka ho asɛm no so.

Sika nhyehyɛeɛ: Sika nhyehyɛeɛ yɛ nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛkyerɛ baabi a wɔbɛnya sika ne deɛ wɔde bɛdi dwuma. Sɛ nhwɛsoɔ no, abusua bɛtumi ayɛ sika nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛkyerɛ sika dodoɔ a ɛwɔ hɔ ne sɛdeɛ wɔde bedi kan ama sika sɛeɛ (sɛ nhwɛsoɔ no, dan ka, amansan dwumadie nnoɔma ne aduane). Sɛ abusua no hia sika pii a, ɛsɛ sɛ wosusuw ho sɛ wɔbɛma akatua pii akɔ soro anaasɛ wɔde bɔseɛ bɛma.

Budget deficit: Eyi ba bere a Aban sikatu boro sika a ɛba no so.

Capital Expenditure: Eyi yɛ sika a wɔsɛe no wɔ mpuntudwuma akɛseɛ te sɛ akwan, sukuu ahodoɔ, ayaresabea ahodoɔ, twene, akwantuo, nsuo nhyehyɛeɛ, afifideɛ ne mfidie ho.

Adwumayɛfoɔ akatua: Eyi yɛ akatua ne akatua ne mmoa a ɛfa akatua ho te sɛ asetena bammɔ, anisɔ akyɛdeɛ sika ne pɛnhyen a wɔtua ma aban adwumayɛfoɔ.
