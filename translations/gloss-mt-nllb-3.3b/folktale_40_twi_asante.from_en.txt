Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

GYINA HƆ NA WOHU

ƐWƆ HƆ BERE BI A NA ɔbaa bi ne ne abofra te. Bere a ɔsomaa no (baabiara), na ɔka sɛ, " I shan't go. " Na ne maame taa ka sɛ, " Wait there and you will see. " Bere nyinaa, bere a ɔsomaa no (baabiara), na ɔka sɛ, " I shan't go. " Afei na ne maame ka kyerɛ no sɛ, " Wait there and you will see. " Ɛda bi na abofra no kaa sɛ, " Daa, maame reka kyerɛ me sɛ, ' Wait there and you will see'; ɛnnɛ ara me rebɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ 'I-will-see', na mehwɛ so. " Enti ofii aseɛ. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na Pea dua bi a sika kɔkɔɔ a wɔabɔ no asinasin wɔ so. Pea dua no bisaa no sɛ, "Adɛn nti na ɛyɛ sɛ obiara mmaa ha da, afei na woaba ha? " Na abofra no kaa sɛ, "Me ne me maame te hɔ daa; ɔkae sɛ, Twɛn hɔ na wubehu, na mekae sɛ mɛba abɛhwehwɛ me-bɛhu no, na mahwɛ no. " Pea-tree no kaa sɛ, Meyɛ wɔn mu biako, nanso yɛn mu kɛse no wɔ akyiri. Na abofra no san fii ase bio. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu sɛ nnyina tenten bi reba abɛhyia no. Nnnyina tenten no bisaa no sɛ, "Dɛn nti na obiara mfaa saa kwan yi so, afei na woatwa mu? " abofra no buae sɛ, "Bere nyinaa, na maame ka sɛ, Mehu, enti mekae sɛ merebɛhwehwɛ mehu, sɛ ebia mɛhwɛ. Nnnyina tenten no buae sɛ, Meyɛ wɔn mu bi, nanso kɔ wanim. Na abofra no san fii ase bio. Berɛ a ɔkɔɔ so no, na Glowing-Sparks wɔ hɔ, na ɔda kuku a abubu no sin so, na ɔreba ne nkyɛn. Ɔhyiaa no. Glowing-Sparks kae sɛ, Dɛn nti na, bere a obiara mmaa ha da no, woaba ha? abofra no buae sɛ, Bere a na me ne me maame te hɔ no, ɔka sɛ, Mɛhu, enti mekae sɛ mɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ Mɛhu, wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ mehwɛ no. Glowing-Sparks kae sɛ, Meyɛ wɔn mu biako, nanso kɔ wanim, efisɛ yɛn mu kɛse no wɔ hɔ. abofra no fii ase bio. Ɔrekɔ no, na Nkoo rehyɛ fie bi so hwiirim nnyegyeeɛ. Nkoo no kaa sɛ, Mprempren na yɛbɛnya obi mogya de asra yɛn fie yi. Afei, bere a abofra no duu hɔ no, wɔkae sɛ, Dɛn nti na obiara mmaa ha da, afei na woaba ha? Ɔbuaa sɛ, Me ne maame te daa, ɔkae sɛ, Twɛn hɔ na wobehu, enti mekae sɛ mɛkɔ hwehwɛ I-will-see, na mahwɛ no. Ɛhɔ ara na Nkoo no faa abofra no, na wotwaa no ti. ad, na wɔgyee ne mogya no guu kɔbere kuku bi mu, na wɔfaa bi nso de guu fie hɔ. Afei Nkoo no tu fii hɔ kɔtenaa dua tenten bi so, na wɔbaa baako akyi baako de wɔn adua ahoroɔ no guu mogya no mu, na wɔn adua ahoroɔ no yɛɛ kɔkɔɔ. Nkoo no nyinaa faa wɔn adua ahoroɔ no guu mu, sɛ ɛyɛ dwumadie no ho nkaedi ade. Afei, da bi, Nkoo no mu baako kae sɛ ɔbɛdidi. Ɔkɔɔ kɔhyiaa abofra no maame. Na maame no bisaa Akoo sɛ, Me abofra a me ne no te no, na ɔyɛ kwasea te sɛ biribiara. Na sɛ me sɔre a, na meka sɛ ɔntwɛn hɔ na ɔnhwɛ, na ɔno de, sɛ mepa wo kyɛw sɛ mede asɛmfua no bedi dwuma a, na ɔka sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ I-will-see, na wahwɛ no. Ɛyɛ nnɔnwɔtwe a atwam ne da a mehuu no, na me srɛ mo sɛ, sɛ moahu no a, momfa no mmra me nkyɛn. Akoo no kaa sɛ, Aane, ɔbaa yɛn akuraa hɔ, na yɛn nso, yɛkunkum no. Ne mogya a yɛde kɔyɛɛ dwumadie no ho nkaeɛ no bi wɔ me dua yi so. Enti, sɛ wokɔ a, kɔka kyerɛ wo nnipa nyinaa sɛ, sɛ obi bɛka kyerɛ obi sɛ, Twɛn hɔ na wobɛhu, ɛnde wabɔ no nnome.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
