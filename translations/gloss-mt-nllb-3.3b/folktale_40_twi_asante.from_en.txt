Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

TWEN HƆ NA WOBƐHU

ƐHƆ NA ɔbaa bi ne ne ba teɛ da bi. Bere a ɔsomaa no (baa baabiara) no, na ɔka sɛ, " I shan't go. " Na ne maame taa ka sɛ, " Twɛn hɔ na wobɛhu. " Daa, bere a ɔsomaa no (baa baabiara), na ɔka sɛ, " I shan't go. " Ɛneɛ na ne maame ka kyerɛ no sɛ, " Twɛn hɔ na wobɛhu. " Ɛda bi a abofra no kae sɛ, " Daa, maame reka kyerɛ me sɛ, ' twɛn hɔ na wobɛhu '; ɛda yi ara na merekɔ akɔhwehwɛ ' I-will-see ', na mehwɛ so. " Enti ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, (ɛwɔ hɔ) pea dua bi a ɛkura sika kɔkɔɔ pɔtee. Pea dua no bisaa no sɛ, Dɛn nti na bere a obiara mma ha da, afei na woaba ha? " Abofra no kae sɛ, "Me ne maame te hɔ daa; ɔkae sɛ, Twɛn hɔ na woahu, na mekae sɛ mɛba abɛhwehwɛ I-will-see no, na mahwɛ no. " Pea-tree no kae sɛ, Me yɛ wɔn mu baako, nanso Yɛn mu kɛseɛ no wɔ akyirikyiri. Abofra no san fii nakwantu no ase bio. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu sɛ Long-piece-of-standing rebɛhyia no. Long-Piece-of-Standing bisaa no sɛ, Dɛn nti, bere a obiara ankɔ saa kwan yi so no, afei worefa so? Abofra no buae sɛ, Bere nyinaa, na maame ka sɛ, Mehu, enti mekae sɛ merebɛhwehwɛ Mehu. Long-Piece-of-Standing buae sɛ, Meyɛ wɔn mu bi, nanso kɔ wanim. Na abofra no san fii ase bio. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, Glowing-Sparks, na ɔda crate a abubu bi so, na ɔreba (ne nkyɛn). Ɔhyiaa no. Glowing-Sparks kaa sɛ, Dɛn nti, bere a obiara nni hɔ a ɔbɛba ha no, woaba ha? Abofra no buae sɛ, Bere a na me ne maame te hɔ no, ɔka sɛ, Me bɛhu, enti mekae sɛ mɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ Me-bɛhu, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ na mahwɛ no. Glowing-Sparks kaa sɛ, Me yɛ Wɔn mu baako, nanso kɔ anim, ɛfiri sɛ Ɔkɛseɛ (yɛn mu) no wɔ hɔ. Abofra no san sii kwan bio. Bere a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, na Nkoofoɔ no de kɔkɔɔ bɔ dan bi ho. Nkoo no kae sɛ, Afei na yɛbɛnya obi mogya de agu fie yi so. Afei a abofra no duu hɔ no, wɔkae sɛ, Dɛn nti, bere a obiara mma ha da no, afei na woaba ha? Ɔbuaa sɛ, Me ne maame te daa; ɔkae sɛ, Twɛn hɔ na wubehu, enti mekae sɛ mɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ I-will-see no, na mahwɛ no. Ɛhɔ ara na Nkoo no kyeree abofra no, na ɔtwaa ne ti. ad, na wɔgyee ne mogya no guu kɔbere kukuo mu, wɔgyee bi de guu fie hɔ. Afei Nkoo no tu fii hɔ kɔtenaa dua tenten bi so, na wɔbaa baako akyi baako de wɔn nnua no guu mogya no mu, na wɔn nnua no yɛɛ kɔkɔɔ. Nkoo no nyinaa faa wɔn nnua no guu mu, sɛ ɛbɛyɛ afahyɛ no nkaeɛ. Afei, da bi, Nkoo no mu baako kae sɛ ɔbɛdidi. Ɔkɔɔ akɔhyia abofra no maame. Na ɛna no bisaa Akoo no sɛ, Me ba a me ne no te no, ne ho akyea sɛ biribiara. Bere a na me sɔre no, na meka sɛ, ɔntwɛn hɔ na ɔhu; ɔno, te sɛ ɔkwasea, excuse me sɛ mede asɛmfua no di dwuma, na ɔka sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ I-will-see, na wahwɛ no. Ɛyɛ nnawɔtwe ni kɔsi nnɛ na minhuu no, na me srɛ mo sɛ, sɛ moahu no a, momfa no mmra me nkyɛn. Akoo no kaa sɛ, Aane, ɔbaa yɛn akuraa hɔ, na yɛn nso, yɛkunkum no. Ne mogya a yɛgyee no sɛ nkaeɛ wɔ dwumadie no ho no, ebi wɔ me dua yi so. Enti, sɛ worekɔ a, kɔka kyerɛ wo nkurɔfoɔ nyinaa sɛ, sɛ obi ka kyerɛ obi sɛ twɛn hɔ na woahu a, ɛnde wafa ne ho nnome.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
