Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

ƐNAM SƐN NA MPANYIMFOƆ KYERƐ SƐ YƐNKA NKASA A ƐFA SLEEPING-MAT HO NSO

WƆKYERƐ SƐ (ɛda bi) Nyame Kwame, Ɔsoro-nyame no, yii afuo kɛseɛ paa bi, na odua nkruma, ɛmo, akutu, turo-nsuo, ɛmo a ɛyɛ dɛ, ne akutu. Afifideɛ no mu nwura no yɛɛ den na honhon nyin. Afei Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no de gong-gong too gua sɛ nwura ayɛ nafuw no so ma na obiara a obetumi ayi nwura afi hɔ a ɔrensɛe ne ho no betumi aba abɛware ne babaa Abena Nkroma (a ɛkyerɛ Abena ba a ɔto so akron). Ɛno deɛ, berɛ a ɔkɔɔ (to try), ɔtwee ne ho (wɔ baabi a honhon no tickled), na wɔbɔɔ no hood; ɛno nso kɔeɛ na wɔbɔɔ no hood; mmarima no nyinaa kɔeɛ (na wɔbɔɔ mmɔden); wɔdii nkoguo. Afei Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Me deɛ, metumi. Afei na Sky-God plantation no wɔ kwan no nkyɛn, na saa kwan no na nkurɔfoɔ fa so kɔ market no Fida biara. Afei Ananse no, esiane sɛ na ɔnim saa nokwasɛm yi nti, na ɔtaa kɔsiesie nwura no nkutoo Fida biara. Ɛfiri sɛ, berɛ a na ɔrepae, na nkurɔfoɔ a wɔretwam no de Hail to you at your work, Agya Ananse! ma no akwaaba no, na ɔbua sɛ, Thank you, Aku. Wɔkɔɔ so kaa sɛ, A large field, which no one has been able to clear, (do you mean to say) you are weeding it? Ananse no bɛbua sɛ, Ah, its all because of one girl that I am wearing myself out like this. Ne nsa baako te sɛɛ. " Na ɔbɛbɔ ne nsa na wahoro ne nsa wɔ nea ɛkanyan no no, na sɛ ɔyɛ saa a, ɔbɛnya ahotɔ afiri abufuo no mu. Ɔfoforo bi bɛtwa mu bio akɔma no akwaaba wɔ nadwuma no mu, na ɔbɛfa ne nsa na wahoro ne nsa wɔ nea ɛkanyan no no. Sɛ nhwɛsoɔ no, sɛ ɔresresresere a, na ɔka sɛ, "Saa abeawa no sigyafoɔ, wɔka sɛ ne sereɛ te sɛɛ" (slapping and rubbing his own). Saa kwan yi so na ɔwiee afuo kɛseɛ no hohoro. Afei ɔfiri hɔ kɔkaa kyerɛɛ Ɔsoro Nyame no sɛnea wawie n'afuo no hohoro. Ɔsoro nyame no bisaa ɔbɔfo no sɛ, "So wawie ampa? " Ɔbɔfo no kae sɛ, "Yiw. " Ɔsoro nyame no bisaa no sɛ, "So ɔhyɛɛ ne ho so? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Dabi, wantwitwa ne ho. " Ɔsoro nyame no faa Abena Nkroma de no maa Kwaku Anans sɛ ɔware no. Anadwo koro bi Kwaku Ananse ne nayeforokunu no kɔɔ ahomegyeɛ, na ayeforokunu no bisaa no sɛ, "Nanso ɛyɛ wo a wode nnipa nyinaa tumi yii wura wɔ papa afuo no mu, afuo a obiara a ɔkɔɔ so no, ɔdan nakyi, nanso wo tumi yii wei? " Ɛnna Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Wosusuw sɛ meyɛ ɔkwasea anaa? Na mebɔ akuturuku, na sɛ obi a ɔretwam bisa me sɛ, Ananse, woretutu afuo yi a obiara ntumi ntutu a, mede me nsa bɛbɔ me honam ani a ɛreyɛ me akisikuru no baabiara, na matwetwe, na maka akyerɛ onipa no sɛ, sɛ nhwɛso no, wo sere te sɛ akraman sere, na ɛyɛ fɛ na wɔayɛ no fɛfɛɛfɛ. Ɛneɛ na ɛbaeɛ sɛ metumi yii no. " Ɛhɔ na Abena, a ɔyɛ ne ba a ɔtɔ so nkron no, kaa sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ, ɔkyena mɛka akyerɛ papa sɛ woabɔ wo ho (ɛwieeɛ koraa no). " Ananse no kasa kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛnsɛ sɛ woka ho asɛm, eyi yɛ sleeping mat confidence. " Abena, a ɔyɛ ne ba a ɔtɔ so nkron no, kaa sɛ, "Me nnim hwee wɔ sleeping mat confidences ho, na mɛka. " Abena Nkroma faa ne sleeper no. eping-mat kwan (firi Kwaku Anans nkyɛn) na ɔkɔda ɔdan no ano. Afei Kwaku Ananse ani abere kɔkɔɔ (te sɛ awerɛhow), na ɔkɔfa ne banta na ɔbɔ (fade) na ɔto dwom sɛ:

"Abena, ɔba a ɔtɔ so nkron no, eyi nyɛ asɛm a ɛsɛ sɛ yɛgye akyinnye.

Momma yɛmfa no sɛ mpa so ahotoso.

Ɔkae sɛ: Dabi.

Ɔne me wɔ asɛm, ɔfoforɔ nso ne no wɔ asɛm a ɛrekɔ so dedaw. "

Afei Ananse sɔre, na ɔkɔda. Ananse da hɔ kakra, na afei ɔsɔre. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abena Nkroma" - ɛnnyɛ nnyigyei (gye nnyigyei a ɛwɔ bamboo plant no mu). Ananse kaa sɛ, "Me nsa aka wo. " Ɔfaa sanku ketewa bi a nsuo ahyɛ mu ma, na ɔhwie guu Abena Nkroma mpa so. Afei Ananse kɔdaeɛ. Ɔdaa hɔ kakra akyi no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Ko! Abena Nkroma, dɛn na ɛyɛ yi? Woama mat no nsuo, mo abɔnefoɔ, akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho sɛ monnyɛ fɛ koraa; nneɛma bɛda adi a, mɛka akyerɛ obiara. Na ɛyɛ nokware, ɛnneɛ (deɛ wɔn nyinaa kaeɛ), sɛ obiara a ɔkɔɔ saa afuom kɛseɛ no, na ɔka sɛ, A girl who wets...! Me renyɛ honhon afuo (ma saa nipa no). " Ɛnna Abena ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Me srɛ wo, gyae, na ma asɛm no nkɔ. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Meremfi hɔ nkɔ, me asɛm no na ɛdii kan baeɛ; wokaeɛ sɛ wobɛka akyerɛ wo papa; mekaeɛ sɛ, gyae; wokaeɛ sɛ, dabi. Ɛno nti, merennyae asɛm no. " Na Abena, a ɔyɛ ne ba a ɔtɔ so nkron no, kaa sɛ, Gyae me asɛm, na wo asɛm nso, a me sp fa ho no. oke, me bɛto hɔ; ɛfiri sɛ sɛ wo nnyae me deɛ a, mani bɛwu ama animguaseɛ. Ɛnna Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Mate, ɛfiri sɛ wopɛ saa a, ma ɛyɛ sleeping-mat confidence, ma asɛm no si hɔ. Saa na mpanimfoɔ no ba bɛkaa sɛ Sleeping-mat confidences are not repeated.

Me kyerɛwtohɔ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na fa a aka no nso kamfo me.
