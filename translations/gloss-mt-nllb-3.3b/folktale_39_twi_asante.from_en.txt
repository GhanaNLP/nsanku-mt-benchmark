Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ASONO NO BAA SƐ ƐKƆƆ FAM WƆ ƆMAN A ASASE TENTEN MU NO

NA Ɛsono bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔkaeɛ sɛ ɔpɛ obi a ɔne no bɛdi agorɔ a wɔfrɛ no "nti a wɔde bobɔ wɔn ho". Wɔn mmoa no nyinaa kaa sɛ wɔn ho nni hɔ (sɛ wɔne Ɛsono bɛdi agorɔ yi). Na Kwaku Ananse wɔ hɔ, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, mɛtumi, nanso tebea a me ne wo si no ne sɛ, sɛ wokum me a, ɛnneɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wosie me wɔ baabi foforo gye sɛ ɔbotan mu. " Ɛsono kaa sɛ, "Mate, na mɛba abɛbu wo ndanson, na wo nso wobɛba abɛbu me ndanson. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ bayerɛ, na ɔ wɔde guu ogya so, sɛ wɔrebɛhyew wɔn, na ɔkɔɔ so yɛɛ tiafi wɔ kwan bi ho. Afei, bere biara a Kwaku Ananse bɛsɔre no, na ɔde bayere bi wɔ ne nsa, na ɔkɔɔ so kɔtenaa tiafi no so. Ɔhuu sɛ adowa ketewa bi retwa mu. Afei nsɛmfua a wɔde wɔn benkum akyi (i. e. Me werɛ fi asɛm bi aka) no, na ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bi aba (wɔ berɛ no). Bere a ɔretwam no, Ananse de bayere no too kwan no so. Ntɛm ara na Adowa no faa no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Agya Ananse, ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bi te sɛɛ na aba, na ɛno na wode bɛdi dwuma bere a wokɔhome wo ho? " Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "Me fie ha aduane ho ɔhaw biara nni hɔ. " Adowa no kae sɛ, "Me ne wo bɛkɔ akɔfa bi. " Kwaku Ananse duruu fie hɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ Adowa no sɛ, "obarima bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔno na ɔde aduane brɛ me anadwo, kɔda hɔ wɔ abɔnten. na ɔba a, ɔbɛka sɛ, Asikoto! (na wobɛbɔ wo etire) na wubua sɛ, Megye me etire no ama no, na sɛ woka no pɛ a, ɔbɛfa aduane no bi de ato hɔ ama wo. " Adowa no kaa sɛ, "Meda wo ase, Aku, na wo, Agya Ananse, ampa ara, menhunuu ɔbarima a ɔte sɛ wo da. " Adowa no kɔɔ kwanano kɔda adiwo. Ɛnkyɛree na Ɛsono no baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Asikoto. " Adowa no buaa sɛ, "Megye me etire no agye," na Ɛsono no bɔɔ no pan! na Adowa no awu. Ɛsono no kɔeɛ. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, Ananse no faa Adowa kɔmaa "Aso" (ne yere) sɛ ɔnyɛ no soup-stew. Afei na Kwaku Ananse akɔ Ɛsono sɛ ɔrekɔma no anɔpa nkyea. Ɛsono no kaa sɛ, "Wo ho yɛ wo yie anaa? " Kwaku Ananse buaa sɛ, "Me ho yɛ me yie. " Kwaku Ananse sii kwan so baa ne fie. Ɔfaa bayerɛ foforɔ, na ɔsan kɔɔ tiafi hɔ. Ɔhunuu sɛ Ɔtwe, Ɔtwee, retwa mu, na ɔtoo bayere no gui. Ɔtwee no kae sɛ, "Ao, Father Kwaku Ananse, ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bi te sɛɛ aba, na ɛno na wode wo kɔ tiafi? " Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "Sɛ wopɛ bi a, bra, yɛnkɔ me fie, na menkɔma wo bi. " Ɔtwee no ne Kwaku Ananse no kɔɔ wɔn kwan so. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Da fam wɔ kar dan mu kwanano ha, na anwummere yi me akoa bɛba ne aduane, na ɔbɛduru ha a, ɔbɛka sɛ, 'Asikoto'; wo nso ka sɛ, 'Megye me etire agye.' " Ɔtwee kae sɛ, "Meda wo ase, Aku, wo na wutumi hyehyɛ nneɛma a ɛte saa. " Ɔtwee kɔɔ hɔ kɔdae. Anwummere no na Ɛsono bae. Ɔkae sɛ, "Asikoto. " Ɔtwee no kae sɛ, "Megye me etire no agye. " Na ɔbɔɔ Ɔtwee no, na ɔhwee ase, (na ne nnyigyei a ɛyɛ sɛ ɔhwee ase no ne) kum! Afei Ɛsono no kɔɔ. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛdaa adi, na Kwaku Ananse faa Ɔtwee no kɔmaa "Aso", na wɔkɔɔ Esono fie sɛ wɔrekɔma no akwaaba. Ɛsono no kae sɛ, "Okuamoa ni, wo ho ye paa? " Okuamoa buae sɛ, "Me ho ye paa. " Afei wɔkɔɔ nkɔmmɔ kakra, na Kwaku Ananse srɛɛ sɛ wɔmfa no nkɔ, na ɔsan kɔɔ ne fie. Na afei Kwaku Ananse nso yɛɛ saa ara ne Ɔyuo, kwaduo, Wansane; Kokotee-Asamoa, Bush-pig; Ɔtromo, Bongo; ne Oko, Ɔpɔnkɔ kɔkɔɔ-fitaa. Afei na Kwaku Ananse bere aso sɛ ɔkɔtow hyɛ Ɛsono so. Afei ɔkɔɔ blacksmiths forge kɔyɛɛ dade payeɛ. Na ɔyɛɛ dade hama a ɔde bɛkɔ. Wɔ anwummere mu no, ɔkɔɔ Ɛsono fie. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Asikoto. " Ɛsono kae sɛ, "Megye metire no ama wo. Kwaku Ananse na ɛbaa fie. Ɛduruu anɔpa kɛseɛ, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, Ɛsono ka kyerɛɛ ne yere sɛ, "Me wɔ tipae a emu yɛ den," na ne yere no kaa sɛ, "Nka asɛm saa, w'ani bɛba wo ho so; Kwaku Ananse mpo a wobɔɔ no ndanson no, onnyaa tipaeɛ, onwuiɛ, na woka asɛm saa. Afei kɔ na fa anɔpa pa ma Kwaku Ananse sɛnea na ɔyɛ daa. " Ɛsono hyɛɛ n'aniwa so (i. e. Kwaku Ananse bɔɔ mmɔdenbɔ kɛseɛ, na ɔkɔɔ so kɔduruu baabi a ɔteɛ no. Ananse no bisaa no sɛ, "Ɔhoɔdenfo kɛse, wo ho yɛ wo den anaa? " Ɛsono no buaa sɛ, "Aane, me ho yɛ me den. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nokware. " Kwaku Ananse no kɔɔ so yɛɛ teaa foforɔ. Bere a anadwo no yɛɛ nwini no, ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ Ɛsono hɔ. "Asikoto," ɔkaa saa. Ɛsono no buaa sɛ, "Megye me etire no. " Kwaku Ananse faa teaa no sii hɔ, na ɔde dade hama no bɔɔ no, pan! pan! pan! Kwaku Ananse san baa ne fie. Afei na ɔwɔ hɔ, bere a messenger bi bae bɛkaa sɛ, Kwaku Ananse, Asono nyinaa nana, se ɛsɛ sɛ meba na mebɔ wo amanneɛ sɛ wo adamfo Ɛsono no, bere a ɔdae no, wanyane bio, nti bra bɛbuee ɔbotan bi mu na wofa no nkɔhyɛ mu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Dabi da, ɛnyɛ saa na yɛyɛɛ nhyehyɛeɛ no. Yɛkaa sɛ, sɛ ɔbaako wu a, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔfoforɔ no nso noa no ma ɔwe. " Saa messenger no kɔgyaa hɔ kɔmaa nkra no kɔmaa ɛsono (wu) no nuanom. Wɔkae sɛ, "Kɔfa no na fa no ba mprempren ara. " Ɛhɔ ara na messenger no baa Ananse nkyɛn bɛkaa sɛ, "Wɔse wobɛtumi aba mprempren ara. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ wotwɛn me. " Ananse kɔɔ nwura so kɔtwaa dua bi, na ɔtwaa dua bi yɛɛ no nnua-ni, na ɔtwaa tuo bi yɛɛ no nsa, na ɔde maa "Aso" sɛ, "Fa no, kɔ anim, fa si fam wɔ baabi a wɔka no. Ɛsɛ sɛ mepae ɔbotan no mu, na wo nso wugyina hɔ, na meba a, wobɛfrɛ me." "Aso" faa dua honi no na wɔkɔe. Ananse no nso, wɔne asɛnkafoɔ no sii kwan. Kwaku Ananse duruu Asono anim, na wɔde asɔ, ne ananse, ne agyan, ne sekkan hyɛɛ ne nsa. Wɔkae sɛ, "Monkɔ so. " Afei wɔde Ananse no kɔɔ baabi a botan kɛseɛ bi wɔ. Wɔkae sɛ, "Munyi no mu, tutu ɛtokuro, na fa asono a wawu no gu mu. " Ananse kɔɔ no nkyɛn, pE! pE! pE (bɔɔ ne nnwinnadeɛ). Ɛhɔ na na Kwaku Ananse wɔ, bere a ɔtee (suddenly called out) sɛ "Yiw. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Asono, wote anaa, ɛte sɛ nea obi refrɛ me? " Asono no kaa sɛ, "Firi mu, wopɛ kwan bi a wobɛfa so adwane. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, " Metee. " Na ɔkɔɔ so yɛɛ nadwuma, pE! pE! pE! Afei ɔsan teɛɛ mu sɛ, " Yiw. " Ɔkaa sɛ, " Obi refrɛ me. " Nnipa no kaa sɛ, " Yɛremma wo kwan mma wo nkɔ. " Ɛyɛɛ saa, saa, mpɛn nson. Ɛduruu tenten no, wɔkae sɛ, "Kɔ na wotie (nea ɛyɛ) na san ba ntɛm ara, na sɛ woguan a, yɛbɛkyere wo. " Kwaku Ananse kɔeɛ baabi a Akua ba no wɔ. Afei Kwaku Ananse tuu mmirika kɔɔ nakyi, kiri! kiri! kiri! (Ɛyɛ ne anan nnyigyei). Asono kaa sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse, ɛyɛ dɛn? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Aane, ɛsɛ sɛ woyi wo ho fi hɔ, ɛfiri sɛ ɔbɔfoɔ bi wɔ hɔ a ɔregyina hɔ, na ɔse ɛsɛ sɛ metwe me ho kakra kɔ afa bi na ama watumi ato ne tuo abɔ wo. Ɛno nti na wafrɛ me, na wafrɛ me sɛ memfa me ho nnyina nkyɛn. " Bere a Asono rebɛyɛ hwehwɛ wɔ hɔ wɔ nwura no mu no, nokware ne sɛ wɔhunuu sɛ obi gyina hɔ, na wɔdwanee. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Father hunter, kill them for me. " Asono no dwanee, ɛkɔtoo Tall-grass-country hɔ, na wɔgyaa ɔsono a wawu no wɔ hɔ, na Kwaku Ananse faa no kɔdweee no. Saa na Asono baa sɛ ɔbɛkɔ Tall-grass-country no, ɛfiri sɛ na berɛ bi a atwam no, na Asono nko ara na ɔte kwaeɛ hɔ.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi mo nnye nni, na ahomegyeɛ no monkamfo me mma ho asɛmka.
