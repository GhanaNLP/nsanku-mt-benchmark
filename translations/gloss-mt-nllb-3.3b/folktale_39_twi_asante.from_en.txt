Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ASONO NO BAEƐ SƐ ƐREKƆ FIRI HƆ KƆ YƐN MAN A ƐWƆ ASASE TENTEN NO MU

ƐDA KORO BI A, NA ASON BI WƆ HƆ, NA ƆKA SƐ ƆPƐ OBI A ƆNE NO BƐDI Agorɔ a wɔfrɛ no "ring toss". Mmoa no nyinaa kaa sɛ wɔntumi nni saa agorɔ yi (with the Elephant). Afei na Kwaku Ananse (The Elephant) wɔ hɔ, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, metumi, nanso asɛm a me ne wo bɛyɛ ne sɛ, sɛ ɛba sɛ wokum me a, ɛnneɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wosie me wɔ baabi foforɔ gye sɛ ɔbotan mu. " Ɔsono no kaa sɛ, "Mate, na mɛba abɛbɔ wo nna nson, na wo nso wobɛba abɛbɔ me nna nson. " Kwaku Ananse kɔtoo amannɔne, na ɔtee Ɔde wɔn guu ogya mu sɛ wɔmfa nyɛ hyew, na ɔkɔyɛɛ tiafi wɔ baabi a ɛbɛn kwan. Afei, bere biara a Kwaku Ananse bɛsɔre no, na ɔfa bankye bi wɔ ne nsa mu, na ɔkɔtena tiafi no so. Ɔhunuu bankye ketewa no a ɛretwam. Afei nsɛm a ɛwɔ akyi benkum (i. e. me werɛ fi sɛ mɛka asɛm bi) no, na ɔkɔm kɛse bi aba (wɔ saa bere no). Bere a ɔretwam no, Ananse no de bankye no guu kwan no so. Ɛhɔ ara na Adowa no faa no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya Ananse, ɔkɔm a ɛte sɛɛ aba, na wei na wode di dwuma bere a worekɔgye wo ho? " Kwaku Ananse buaa sɛ, "Me fie aduane ho asɛm nni hɔ koraa. " Adowa no kaa sɛ, "Me ne wo bɛkɔ akɔfa bi. " Kwaku Ananse na ɔduruu fie hɔ; ɔka kyerɛɛ Adowa no sɛ, "Ɔbarima bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔno na ɔde aduane no brɛ me anadwo; enti kɔda hɔ wɔ ɔpon no ano. y, na sɛ ɔba a, ɔbɛka sɛ, 'Sikoto! ' (na wobɛbɔ wo ti ase) na wabua sɛ, 'Megye me ti agye,' na woaka no ara pɛ, ɔbɛfa aduane no bi na ɔde ato hɔ ama wo. " Adowa no kaa sɛ, "Me da wo ase, Aku; Wo deɛ, Agya Ananse, ampa ara, minhuu ɔbarima a ɔte sɛ wo da. " Adowa no kɔdaa adiwo kwan no ano. Ɛnkyɛreeɛ, asono no baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sikoto. " Asono no buaa sɛ, "Mefa me ti gye," na asono no bɔɔ no, pan! na Asono no awu. Asono no kɔeɛ. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma no bɛdaa adi no, Ananse no de Adowa maa Aso (ne yere) sɛ ɔnyɛ no soup-stew. Kwaku Ananse deɛ, ɔkɔɔ Asono nkyɛn kɔmaa no anɔpa. Ɔsono no bisaa no sɛ, "Wo ho ye paa anaa? " Kwaku Anans buae sɛ, "Me ho ye. " Kwaku Anans kɔɔ ne fie. Ɔfaa sare foforo, na ɔsan kɔɔ tiafi no mu. Ɔhunuu Dragon, Dragon no, sɛ ɔretwam, na ɔtow sare no gui. Ɔtwee no kae sɛ, "Oh, Father Kwaku Ananse, ɔkɔm a ɛte sɛɛ aba yi, na eyi na wode kɔ tiafi? " Kwaku Ananse buaa sɛ, "Sɛ wopɛ bi a, bra, ma yɛnkɔ me fie, na menkɔma wo bi. " Ɔtwee no ne Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ kwan. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Fa wo ho kɔda fikyire no kwan ano ha, na anwummerɛ yi, me somfo no de aduane bɛba, na sɛ ɔduru ha a, ɔbɛka sɛ, 'Sikoto'; wo nso ka sɛ, 'Mefa me ti gye.' " Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, "Me da wo ase, Aku, wo nko ara na wotumi hyehyɛ nneɛma a ɛte sɛɛ. " Ɔtwee no kɔɔ hɔ kɔdaeɛ. Anwummerɛ no na asono no baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Asikoto. " Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, "Mefa me ti gye no. " Na ɔbɔɔ Ɔtwee no, na ɔhwee aseɛ, (na ne hweɛ no nnyigyeɛ ne) owuo! Ɛneɛ na asono no kɔeɛ. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, na Kwaku Ananse de Ɔtwee no maa Aso, na ɔfirii aseɛ kɔɔ Asono fie sɛ ɔbɛma no anɔpa pa. Ɔsono no kae sɛ, "Okumoa ni, wo ho ye paa anaa? " Okumoa buae sɛ, "Eye koraa. " Afei wɔbɔɔ nkɔmmɔ kakra, na Anans srɛɛ kwan sɛ ɔnkɔ na ɔsan kɔɔ ne fie. Na afei Anans yɛɛ saa ara ne Oyuo, Kwadwo; Wansane, Bush-buck; Kokotee-Asamoa, Bush-pig; Ɔtromo, Bongo; ne Oko, ɔpɔnkɔ kɔkɔɔ- fitaa. Afei, na ɛyɛ Anans de no na ɔrekɔbɔ ɔsono no. Afei ɔkɔɔ adwumfo bi adwumayɛbea kɔyɛɛ dade nhama; na ɔyɛɛ nhama de kaa ho. Anwummere no, ɔkɔɔ asono no fie. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Sikoto. " Asono no kae sɛ, "Megye me ti no. " Ananse no faa dade nhama no de guu asono no ti so, na ɔfaa nhama no bɔɔ no, pan! pan! pan! na ɛyeraeɛ (wɔ asono no ti mu). Kwaku Ananse deɛ, ɔbaa fie. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma no bɛdaa adi no, Asono no ka kyerɛɛ ne yere sɛ, "Me ho akyea paa," na ne yere nso kaa sɛ, "Nka saa, ɛsɛ sɛ wani gu ho; Kwaku Ananse mpo a wobɔɔ no nnanson no, wannya ne ho akyea, na onwui nso, na woka saa; afei kɔ na ma Kwaku Ananse anɔpa pa sɛnea na ɔyɛ daa no. " Asono no bɔɔ nani (i. e. Ɔbɔɔ mmɔden paa), na ɔfirii hɔ kɔduruu baabi a Kwaku Ananse te. Asono no kae sɛ, "Otumfoɔ, wo ho ye paa? " Asono no buae sɛ, "Oh, me ho ye. " Asono no kae sɛ, "Nokwarem. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ hɔ kɔyɛɛ teaseɛnam foforo. Bere a anadwo no yɛɛ nwini no, ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ Asono a ɔwɔ hɔ no nkyɛn. "Asikoto," ɔkae. Ɔsono no buae sɛ, "Megye me ti gye. " Kwaku Ananse faa teaseɛnam no, de sii hɔ, na ɔde dadeɛ hama no bɔɔ no pan! pan! pan! Kwaku Ananse san baa ne fie. Ɛnneɛ na ɔwɔ hɔ, berɛ a ɔbɔfo bi bɛkaa sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse, Asono nyinaa nana, kyerɛ sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meba abɛbɔ wo amanneɛ sɛ wadamfo, Asono no, berɛ a ɔdaeɛ no, wanyane bio; enti bra na bue ɔbotan na fa no fa no gu mu. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Dabi da, ɛno na ɛnyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ a yɛyɛɛ no. Yɛkaa sɛ, sɛ ɔbaako wu a, ɔfoforɔ no nwene no mma ɔnwe. " Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔɔ kwan so kɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ asono (a wawu) no mogya abusuafoɔ. Wɔkae sɛ, "Kɔ na fa no na fa no ba (ha) ntɛm ara. " Ɔbɔfoɔ no baa hɔ kyerɛɛ Ananse no na ɔkaa sɛ, "Wɔse ɛsɛ sɛ woba ntɛm ara. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ wotwɛn me. " Ananse no kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu na ɔtwaa dua bi, na ɔtwaa no yɛɛ no dua-nipa, na ɔtwaa tuo bi, na ɔde hyɛɛ ne nsa, na ɔde maa Aso sɛ, "Fa no, kɔ anim, fa si fam wɔ baabi a wɔka no. Ɛsɛ sɛ mepae ɔbotan no mu, na wo nso wobɛgyina hɔ, na sɛ meba a, wobɛfrɛ me. " Aso faa (dua honi) no kɔeɛ. Ananse nso ne abɔfo no kɔeɛ. Kwaku Ananse bɛduruu Asono no anim, na wɔfaa axe, ne crowbar, ne hammer ne sickle, de hyɛɛ ne nsa. Wɔkae sɛ, "Foro so. " Afei wɔde Ananse no kɔɔ baabi a ɔbotan kɛseɛ bi wɔ. Wɔkae sɛ, "Twa no mu, tutu kuruwa no, na fa asono (a wawu) no hyɛ mu. " Ananse kɔɔ hɔ, exercise! exercise! exercise! (bɔɔ ne nnwinnadeɛ no. Kwaku Ananse na ɔwɔ hɔ, bere a ɔtoo (ɛhɔhɔhɔ) "Yiw. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Asono, wote, (ɛte sɛ nea obi refrɛ me? " Elephant no kaa sɛ, "Firi adi, wopɛ kwan a wobɛfa so adwane. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, " Mate. " Na ɔkɔɔ nadwuma no so, exercise! exercise! exercise! Ɛneɛ ɔsan teɛɛ mu sɛ, " Yiw. " Ɔkaa sɛ, " Obi refrɛ me. " Nkurɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, " Yɛremma wo kwan mma wo nkɔ. " Saa ara, saa ara, mpɛn nson. Ewiei koraa no wɔkae sɛ, "Kɔ na tie (nea ɔyɛ) na san ba ntɛm ara, na sɛ woguan a, yɛbɛkyere wo. " Kwaku Ananse fii hɔ kɔɔ baabi a Akua ba no wɔ. Afei Kwaku Ananse tuu mmirika (asan) kiri! kiri! kiri! (ɛno ne nan nnyigyei). Asono no kae sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse, ɛyɛ dɛn? " Ɔkae sɛ, " Oh, ɛsɛ sɛ wo kɔ, ɛfiri sɛ akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ ɔbɔfoɔ bi gyina hɔ, na ɔka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ metwe me ho kakra kɔ nkyɛn bi na ama watumi atu ne tuo abɔ wo. Ɛno nti na ɔfrɛ me, na ɔfrɛ me sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ me gyina nkyɛn. " Bere a Elephants no rebɛyɛ nhwehwɛmu wɔ hɔ wɔ nwura no mu no, akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ wɔhunuu sɛ obi gyina hɔ, na wɔdwanee. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Father hunter, kill them for me. " Asono no dwanee, na ɛwɔ Tall-grass-country no mu, na wɔgyaa asono a awu no wɔ hɔ, na Kwaku Ananse faa no kɔdweee no. Saa na Asono baeɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ Tall-grass-country no, ɛfiri sɛ bere bi na Asono nko ara na ɔte kwaeɛ no mu.

Me kyerɛwtohɔ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na fa a aka no nso kamfo me.
