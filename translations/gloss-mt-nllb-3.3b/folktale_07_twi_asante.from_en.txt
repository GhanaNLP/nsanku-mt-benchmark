SƐNEA ƐKƆƆ SO SƐ WOHUA LEOPARD FUNU NO

ƐHƆ na Nwansena bi ne Daboɔ bi tenaa, na Daboɔ no ka kyerɛɛ Nwansena no sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔhwehwɛ mmaa na yɛaware. " Nwansena no kaa sɛ, "Ho. " Afei Daboɔ no hohoroo ne ho, na ɔde ne sika kɔkɔɔ agude siesiee ne ho, na ɔkyekyeree ne mpa a ɛyɛ dedaw no, na ɔde maa Nwansena no sɛ ɔnkɔtoa ne etire no so. Na wɔkɔeɛ no, wɔduruu akuraase bi. Ɛhɔ na Nwansena no kae sɛ, "Ɛnanom nyinaa ne Agyanom nyinaa, mema mo anwummere nkyea. " Wɔkae sɛ, "Yɛn kunu pa. " Daboɔ no nso bɛduruu hɔ, ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛnanom nyinaa ne Agyanom nyinaa, mema mo anwummere nkyea. " Wɔkae sɛ, "Mo a mo ano fĩ yi, mo ho akokwaw sɛ mobɛma yɛn anwummere nkyea? " Wɔkɔɔ so kɔduruu akuraa a ɛdi hɔ no. 'Wansana, Nwansena no, kae sɛ, "Momma nyinaa ne Agyanom nyinaa, mema mo anwummere nkyea. " Wɔn nso buae saa kwan koro no ara. Daboɔ no nso duu hɔ, na ɔkyee wɔn, na wɔbuaa sɛ,

"Wo a wo ho yɛ ano fĩ no, wopɛ sɛ yɛma wo anwummere nkyea? " Wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ, wɔkɔɔ kwan so, Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ ha! fa kɛtɛ dada no brɛ me na fa sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ yi siesie wo ho, sɛ kɛtɛ dada no nti na mmabaawa no dɔ wo a, mehu. " Na Nwansena no gyee ahosiesieedeɛ no, siesiee ne ho, na wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔduruu akuraa a ɛdi hɔ no. Daboɔ no kɔɔ nanim, na ntama dada no kɔɔ ne etire so, ɔde nkra kɔmaa nkuraasefoɔ no. Wɔkae sɛ, "Mo a mo ano fĩ yi, monnye yɛn nkyia, ɛyɛ mo sɛ monsoa saa dɔteɛ dedaw yi. " Ɛneɛ na Nwansena bɛduruu hɔ no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnanom nyinaa ne Agyanom nyinaa, mema mo anwummere nkyea. " Na wɔbuaa sɛ, "O mo a moyɛ dɔfo, mmabunu bi wɔ hɔ a ɛfata sɛ yɛhwɛ no yiye; sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ a ɛyɛ dɛn na ɛbɛyɛ mo; sɛ ɛnyɛ ɔborɔ a yɛbɛnya no, anka yɛbɛka mo ho wɔ mo akwantu no mu. akwantu. " Na wɔkɔɔ so. Ɛntɛm ara na wɔduruu kwan no so no, Daboɔ kaa sɛ, "Hei! ɔyɔnko, gyina hɔ ara, tew me sika agudeɛ no na fa ma me ntɛm, ntɛm, ntɛm. " Bere a Nwansena no wiee sɛ ɔretwitwa ahosiesiedeɛ no na ɔde ama no, Daboɔ no hwɛɛ kwan no so, na ɔtwee nhabannwono bi a ɔde bɛkyekyere Nwansena no. Ɔkae sɛ, Mɛkyekyere wo, na dɛn na wunya ka? Na Nwansena no kaa sɛ, Me nni hwee ka. Daboɔ no kɔɔ so kaa sɛ, Me ne wo nnya nnu yɛn beae a obi rekɔ mpo . . (na nanso mmaa no redi kan adɔ wo). Afei Daboɔ no faa no nsɔmu kyekyere no dua. Na ɔno nso kɔɔ baabi foforo kɔhyehyɛɛ ne ho. Ɛnkyɛreeɛ, Borɔdeɛ no faa dwaso kwan so, na berɛ a ɔhunuu no (Nwansena) no, ɔkaa sɛ:

Hwan na ɔda hɔ a ne ho yɛ tuntum paa?

Hena na ɔda hɔ a ne ho yɛ fɛ paa?

(Nwansena) kaa sɛ:

Ɛyɛ me, Nwansena, Kwao Boakye.

Adenen!

Gyahene Kofi Amo, Adenen!

Ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meba, na yɛakɔ hwehwɛ mmabaawa.

Adenen!

Ne mmabaawa no reka sɛ wɔmpɛ no.

Adenen!

Ɔde me akyekyere me.

Adenen!

Ɔakyekyere me koraa.

Adenen!

Agyenkwa, gye me.

Adenen!

Sɛ woagye me a, fa kwan so kɔ.

Adenen! Nanso Borɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Daboɔ no akyekyere wo, enti merentumi nnyae wo"; na ɔkɔɔ so. Half-a-ball of Dɔkono kae sɛ, "Metee, mɛtew wo so. " Ɔkɔɔ hɔ, na ɔtee no so. Bere a ɔyɛɛ saa wiei no, mpofirim ara na Daboɔ no puee. Ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woagyae me ɔbarima no? " Ɔno (Dɔkono bɔɔlo fã no) kae sɛ, "Megye no, na nea mobɛyɛ no, monyɛ. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, me ne wo renyɛ hwee koraa, gye sɛ me ne wo de yɛn nkuturu bɛko. " Dɔkono bɔɔlo fã no kae sɛ, "Sɛ yɛbɛko a, momma yɛnkɔ nwura no ho, na yɛmmɔ nnyina nsi sisian. " Enti wɔkɔbɔɔ nnyina no, na ɔsan bae. Na ɔno (Half-a-ball-of-Dɔkono) kae sɛ, "Momma yɛmfa ogya nto no so. " Na Daboɔ no hyɛɛ no sisian. Ntɛm ara na wɔde wɔn ho bɔɔ ɔko, (na ne nnyigyei no te sɛ) Yiri! Yiri! Yiri! Na Daboɔ no atetew Half-a-ball-of-Dɔkono afiri ne ho, na watow no, bam! Half-a-ball-of-Dɔkono kae sɛ, "(Adeɛ a ɛte saa no, ɛnyɛ hwee), fa saa adeɛ no hohoro baabi a wo yere bɔ ne hohoro. " Na wɔsan hyiaa bio. Na Half-a-ball-of-Dɔkono nso de Daboɔ no ato no, bam! Na Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Me nso mefa adeɛ a ɛte saa ara de hohoro me yere bathroom, (nanso) yɛn ahyiadie a ɛdi hɔ no, sɛ baako tu ɔfoforɔ gu a, ɛnneɛ ɛno ne tow a etwa toɔ. " Na wɔde bɔɔ ɔko no, na nnyigyei no te sɛ nea Kikiri! Kikiri! Kikiri! Na Half-a-bɔɔlo-of-Dɔkono maa Daboɔ no so, na ɔtow no guu ogya no mu. Daboɔ no kae sɛ, "_Me firim, me firim, me firim. " Saa na Daboɔ no nyaa ne su "_Me firim. " (Afei) wɔhunuu sɛ ne nipadua no te sɛɛ, kurukyire, kurukyire, kurukyire nyinaa. Baabi a na fitaa no wɔ no, ɛhɔ na na nso aka no; na baabi a na tuntum no wɔ no, ɛhɔ na bidie tuntum no aka no. Na sɛ worenantew, na woahunu ahaban a ɛda hɔ a wɔde Dɔkono bɔɔlo fã bi akyekyere mu a, wubehu sɛ nwansena pii abɔ so; na ɛno mpo, sianteɛ biara nni ho (gye eyi nkutoo), wɔda no ase.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi mo nnye nni, na ahomegyeɛ no monkamfo me (asɛmka no).
