FOREWORD

Ɛbɛka sɛ afe yi yɛ afe a ɛyɛ den paa wɔ yɛn ɔman yi sikasɛm abakɔsɛm mu. Kovid-19 yarebɔne a ɛtrɛɛ wiase nyinaa no baa yɛn so wɔ afe 2020 nan mu baako, na ɛno na ɛmaa sikasɛm dwumadie kɔɔ brɛooyɛ mu, na ɛno na ɛmaa yɛn sika a wɔnya no kɔɔ soteɛ, na ɛno na ɛmaa yɛn sika a ɛbɛka ho wɔ yaredom yi ho no kɔɔ so kɔɔ soro. Sɛnea ewiase ahomegyeɛ teɛ no, saa na Kovid-19 yarebɔne a ɛtrɛɛ wiase nyinaa no nso teɛ.

Bere a na yɛsusu sɛ yɛreyɛ kɔ anim paa wɔ yɛn ayaresa mu firi "pandemic" no nsunsuanso bɔne no mfitiaseɛ kɔsi yɛn nsakraeɛ nhyehyɛeɛ no dwumadie no mfitiaseɛ no, dwumadi ahodoɔ bi bɛbɔɔ yɛn a berɛ kɔɔ so no, ɛsɛee sikasɛm kɛseɛ nkonimdiɛ a yɛkyerɛw too hɔ wɔ afe 2017 ne 2019 ntam hɔ no. Nea ɛdi kan ne ɔko a ɛkɔɔ so wɔ Russia ne Ukraine ntam, afei nso sika a wɔboaboa ano akwan atitire a ɛkyɛe wɔ 2022 sika nhyehyɛeɛ no mu no, ɛno na ɛmaa femgyinaeɛ nsian bae. Ɛduruu berɛ a ɛdi kan wɔ mfeɛ du mu no, Amanaman dwadibea kɛseɛ no too mu maa yɛn mpofirim, ɔman mu sika a wɔde boa tebea no yɛɛ den, na boseagye boɔ nso kɔɔ soro, na ɛno na ɛmaa ɛka a emu yɛ duru tua ne sika boɔ a ɛresiane ntɛmntɛm wɔ sidi no ho.

Enti, bere a amannɔne sika mmoa a efi dwetiri gua so ne amannɔne sika a yenya no so kɔ soro no, nnoɔma a wɔde ba man mu ho ka a ɛkɔ soro esiane akɔnnɔ a ɛnso da adi sɛ yebenya nnoɔma a wɔde firi amannɔne ba sɛ ɔman nti, ne ɛka a wotua ho ka a ɛrekɔ soro no ama mfasoɔ a ɛwɔ yɛn sika so no ayɛ mmerɛ bio. Aban ne ankorankoro nyinaa boɔ a ɛyɛ den na ɛwɔ hɔ ma yɛn. Fango bo a ɛrokɔ soro no ama nneɛma ne ɔsom dodow no ara boɔ anya nkɔanim, na sɛ wɔkyekyɛ mu a, boɔ kɔ soro nea ɛkorɔn 40.4% wɔ Ahinime 2022 mu.

Wɔasɔ yɛn ahwɛ ampa sɛ nnipa, na Aban wɔ tema kɛse ma wo wɔ nhyɛsoɔ a ɛboro so a eyi de aba no ho. Sɛ́ Aban no, yɛpɛ sɛ yɛda yɛn aseda adi kyerɛ yɛn kyɛfafoɔ ahodoɔ a Mmarahyɛbadwa, Adwumayɛfoɔ Fekuo, Adwumayɛfoɔ nkabomkuo, Ɔmanfoɔ fekuo, Nyamesom ahyehyɛdeɛ, Ghana nnwumakuo Fekuo, Ghana Aguadifoɔ ka ho.

NNIANIM

1. Afe 2023 ɔmanfoɔ sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ no yɛ nea Aban no tumi yɛeɛ wɔ afe 2022 mu no mmuano ne sɛdeɛ wɔabɔ wɔn tirim sɛ wɔbɛboaboa sika a wɔnya ano na wɔde adi dwuma de ayɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ne dwumadi ahorow. 2023 sika nhyehyɛeɛ no asɛmti ne Sikasɛm mu timsɛm ne Pintinnyɛ a wɔbɛsan de asi hɔ na wɔakura mu ama Nyini a ɛka obiara bom ne Mfasoɔ a wɔde ka ho.

2. Wɔ mfeɛ a atwam no mu no, sikasɛm nhyehyɛe foforo biara kura nnwuma foforɔ dwumadiwura a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔhyɛ aseɛ wɔ nnwuma a ɛrekɔ so ho ka ho. Ɛnyɛ sɛ eyi de nhyɛsoɔ ba yɛn sika nsɛm so nko, na mmom ɛnam sikatua a wɔatwentwɛn so no twetwe mfasoɔ boɔ nti, abakɔsɛm mu no, ntua a wɔbɔ wɔ ka a aka ho no ama dwumadi ahodoɔ no ne nyinaa boɔ akɔ soro.

3. Aban wɔ afe 2023 mu no, wɔde nhyehyɛeɛ foforɔ ne dwumadi ahorow bedi dwuma, na ahyɛ amammerɛ a ɛne sɛ wɔbɛwie dwumadi ahorow a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw nyinaa nso ho nkuran.

Sika a ɛba nhyehyɛeɛ

18. Aban de sika a ɛba nhyehyɛeɛ a ne otwa nyinaa a wɔasusuw sɛ ɛyɛ GDP mu 1. 35 ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu bɛma Mmarahyɛbadwa no ama nsusuhoɔ ne ngyetomu de aboa sikasɛm mu biakoyɛ nhyehyɛe no. Eyinom ne afoforo:

• Wɔbɛsan ahwɛ toɔgyeɛ a ɛwɔ sigarɛte ahorow ne tawa nneɛma mu ma ɛne ECOWAS apam ahorow ahyia, wɔbɛma ahonhom ho akwankyerɛtoɔ dodow akɔ soro asen nsã biya ahorow.

• Wɔbɛma toɔ a wɔde ka ho (VAT) dodoɔ akɔ soro mmienu ne nkyekyɛm ɔha mu nkyem fa afiri ɔha biara mu 12.5 akɔsi ɔha biara mu nkyɛm 15 na wɔahwɛ VAT tow hye a ɛsɛ sɛ wodu no mu na wɔayɛ nkɔso kɛse denam obuo a wɔde bɛma wɔ Towtua a wɔayi afi mu no so.

• Obi a wasakra Ɔman sika ahotɔ toɔ (NFSL) no bɛyɛɛ Nkɔsoɔ ne Nkɔsoɔ Toɔ (GSL) a ɛkata akuo nyinaa so.

• Abɛɛfo sikatua toɔ dodow titiriw a wɔbɛtew so afi 1. 5 percent akɔ ɔha mu biako wɔ dwadie boɔ mu denam da biara kwan a yi firi hɔ no so.

• Nneɛma afoforo a nneɛma bi a wɔahyɛ ho nyansa ne

o 5G Electromagnetic Spectrum a wɔbɛtɔn;

o Ma towtua a wodi so wɔ dan ka ho no ntu mpɔn;

o Di ngo hokwan foforo (AEO) akyi wɔ abusuabɔ a ɛda Jubilee field no ntam; na

o Akatua tow a wɔasan ahwɛ mu de ayi sika sesa mu nkogu a ɛnsii mfi ntewsoɔ mu na wɔde sika sesa mu nkogu a ɛwɔ agyapadeɛ titire so no ayɛ kɛseɛ.

Mmoa ntoɔ (VAT): Wɔde tɔoo a wogye sɛ mfasoɔ ka nneɛma ne ɔsom ho wɔ gyinabea biara a nneɛma yɛ.

V20 (MMERƐW aduonu): Ɛyɛ sikasɛm mu ɔsɛe kɛse a emu dɔ a wiem nsakraeɛ de ba sikasɛm ahodoɔ so, a ɛgyina hɔ ma aman a wɔbu wɔn sɛ wɔyɛ mMERƐW ma wiem nsakraeɛ.

Afa ketewa - sikasɛm dwumadie mpotam a ɛyɛ mmeae akɛse a wɔakyekyɛ ɔman bi sikasɛm dwumadie mu no mu baako fã.

YouStart: Aban ayɛ nhyehyɛɛ sɛ ɛde sika mmoa ne mfididwuma mu mmoa bɛma mmabunu (18-40) ne nnwuma a mmabunu di anim a wɔhwe saa nkyekyɛmu yi mu de ahyɛ wɔn ankasa nnwuma ase, akyekye na wɔanyin.

Mmeranteɛ ne Mmabaawa Adwuma Nya Bea (YEA): Mmabun Adwumayɛ Mmara 2015 (Mmara 887) na ɛde YEA sii hɔ sɛ ɛbɛhwɛ nkɔsoɔ, ayɔnkofa nhyehyɛeɛ, sohwɛ ne nneɛma a ɛyɛ mmerɛw a ɛfa adwumanya ho ama mmabunu ne ahokafoɔ nsɛm wɔ Ghana.
