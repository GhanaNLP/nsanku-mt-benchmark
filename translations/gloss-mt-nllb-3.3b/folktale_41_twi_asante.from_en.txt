Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐ WOREKƆ BAABI A, SƐ WO BUSUANI KYERƐ SƐ ƆBƐKA WO HO A, NA FA NO KƆ AKYIRI

NA ƐYƐ ɔbaa bi, na ɔwoo mmofra baasa. Wɔn mu ketewa no yare yaws. Nanuanom no mu panyin no ka kyerɛɛ wɔn maame sɛ wɔmma wɔn sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma na wɔnkɔ dwadie. Wɔn mu ketewa no nso kaa sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔne no kɔ. Wɔbɔɔ dawuro sɛ ɔne wɔn nkɔ. Maame no kaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ ɔne wɔn kɔ. Afei wɔn maame no de sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma a ɛwɔ mfasoɔ £5 maa ɔpanyin no, ne sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma a ɛwɔ mfasoɔ £2 nso maa mmabarima no. Bere a wɔhunuu nneɛma no, wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔeɛ. Wɔn mpanimfoɔ no kɔɔ wɔn anim, na wɔgyaa abofra no hɔ sɛ ɔnni nakyi. Abofra no de nkakrankakra baa hɔ. Na mpanimfo no hyiaa obarima bi a ɔde apataa rema. Ɔka kyerɛɛ mpanimfoɔ no sɛ, "Tɔ! " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Kɔ, na wobɛhyia abofra bi; ma no tɔ, na sɛ ɔka sɛ ɔntɔ a, bɔ no na ma no tɔ; na sɛ ɔda so ara po sɛ ɔbɛtɔ a, fa no san ne no nkɔ ne maame nkyɛn. " Saa yɔnkoɔ no kɔɔ so, na akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho sɛ ɔbɛhyia abofra no. Ɔkae sɛ, "mpanimfoɔ bi, a mehyiaa wɔn no, kae sɛ montɔ apataa yi. " abofra no kae sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ me tɔ, bere a wɔn a wɔyɛ me mpanimfoɔ no annɔ no? " yɔnkoɔ no kae sɛ, "Wɔse sɛ woantɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ me bɔ wo, na ɛsɛ sɛ mede wo kɔma wo maame. " abofra no kae sɛ, "Ɛdɔɔso ahe? " yɔnkoɔ no kae sɛ, "Sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. e. £2) a emu duru yɛ osua baako. " abofra no tuaa ho ka gyee. Ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔdii mpanimfoɔ no so wɔ akuraase bi. Wɔayɛ atosodeɛ, nanso wɔnni nam biara a wɔde bɛkɔ. abofra no kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ apataa bi a metɔɔ no. " Wɔde guu soup-stew no mu. Bere a wɔwiei no, mpanimfoɔ no maa no etire a ɛwɔ apataa no mu. Bere a abofra no rebɛpae no, ɔhunuu sika kɔkɔɔ trɔm a ɛwɔ mu, na ɔde kyekyeree no wɔ ne ntoma ano. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, mpanimfoɔ no sii gyinaeɛ, na wɔrekɔ no, wɔhyiaa ɔbarima bi, na akokɔnini bi sii nneɛma no so, a na ɔresoa. Ɛberɛ a ɔne mpanimfoɔ no kɔeɛ ara pɛ, ɔkaa sɛ, "Tɔ akokɔnini yi. " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Kɔ so, na wobɛhyia abofra bi; ma no tɔ, na sɛ ɔtɔ a, bɔ no. " Ɛyɛ nokware sɛ ɔbɛhyiaa abofra no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Wo abusuafo mpanimfo kae sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ wotɔ akokɔnini yi, na sɛ woantɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ mebɔ wo, na mede wo kɔ wo maame nkyɛn. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Hwɛ, fa nea mewɔ yi. " Ne yɔnkoɔ no nso de akokɔnini no maa no. Wɔduruu akuraase bi. Wɔ akuraa hɔ nyinaa, na akokɔnini biara nni hɔ a wɔde kwaakwaadabi bɛkɔ. Ɛduruu anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛdaa adi, na akokɔnini a ɛyɛ abofra crew no dea no. Na akuraa no mu panyin kae sɛ, "Abofra, fa akokɔnini no bra na ma mentɔ. " Na abofra no kae sɛ, "Saa boɔ no yɛ osua ne sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. e. £3). " Na ɔbarima no tuaa sika no. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa hɔ no, wɔsan fii aseɛ bio. Bere a wɔrekɔ no, mpanimfoɔ no hyiaa ɔbarima bi a ɔwɔ agyinamoa. Ɔkae sɛ, "Tɔ agyinamoa yi. " Na mpanimfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Fa no kɔ, na wo ne abofra bi bɛhyia, fa no ma watɔ. Sɛ ɔka sɛ ɔntɔ a, monkyere no na momfa no nkɔma ne maame. " Na yɔnkoɔ no kɔɔ so kɔhyiaa abofra no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Wo mpanimfoɔ akyerɛ sɛ tɔ agyinamoa yi, na sɛ woantɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ mebɔ wo na mede wo kɔ wo maame nkyɛn. " abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ɛdɔɔso sɛn? " Na yɔnkoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ osua ne suru bi a ɛyɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma mu duru (i. e. £3). " Abofra no tuaa boɔ no, na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔne ne mpanimfoɔ no baa akuraase bi. Na nkura no taa keka ɔpanyin a ɔwɔ hɔ no anan bere biara a ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛda. Bere a abofra no faa agyinamoa no kɔɔ hɔ no, ɛkɔɔ ɔpanyin no fie kɔkyeree nkura a wɔwɔ hɔ no. Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, Mepɛ sɛ medɔ saa agyinamoa yi fi onipa a ɔwɔ hɔ no hɔ. Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, Ɛyɛ me deɛ. Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, Ɛdɔɔso dɛn? abofra no kae sɛ, Osua ne suru mu duru yɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. e. £3). Ɔpanyin no faa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no de hyɛɛ ne nsa. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔeɛ. Na mpanimfoɔ no rekɔ so no, wɔhyiaa awifoɔ bi a wɔawia ɔhene bi funu. awifoɔ no kaa sɛ, Ɛsɛ sɛ wotɔ saa funu yi. Mpanimfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Fa no, kɔ so, na wobɛhyia abofra bi; fa no ma no tɔ. Sɛ ɔka sɛ ɔrenntɔ a, bɔ no, na fa no kɔma ne maame. awifoɔ no kɔɔ so, na wɔhyiaa abofra no; wɔkaa sɛ, Wɔ mpanimfoɔ no ka sɛ moaba abɛtɔ saa funu yi. abofra no kaa sɛ, Eh! dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ metɔ funu a, mentumi nkura so; nea mede bɛyɛ biara, ɛrenyɛ me dɛ. awifoɔ no kaa sɛ, Wo mpanimfoɔ no kae sɛ, sɛ woantɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛboro wo, na yɛfa wo kɔma wo maame. abofra no kaa sɛ, Ɛdɔɔso sɛn? Wɔkae sɛ, Osua ne suru yɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma. Abofra no tuaa boɔ no, faa funu no, na ɔtoo nwura no mu. Afei ɔkɔeɛ, na ɔduruu akuraase bi, na ɔkɔɔ fie baako bi kɔsrɛ aduane. Ɛhɔ na fie wura no kaa sɛ, Ebia biara nni hɔ. Ɛhɔ na abofra no kaa sɛ, Agyapa, me srɛ wo. Ɛhɔ na fie wura no kaa sɛ, Ebia biara nni hɔ. Ɛhɔ na abofra no kaa sɛ, Agyapa, me srɛ wo. Ɛhɔ na fie wura no kaa sɛ, Adɛn nti na abofra yi haw me sɛɛ? Yɛn Ɔhene no awu, yɛredi mmuada; eyi ne da a ɛtɔ so nwɔtwe; nanso yɛhwehwɛ sɛ yɛrenhu ne funu. Wɔbɔɔ aberewa bi din sitti. Ng wɔ nkyɛn hɔ, ɔkae sɛ, Ma no bi. Ɔbaa no maa no aduane ne nam. Bere a abofra no dii aduane wiei no, ɔkae sɛ, Master of the fie, mahunu Chief no funu, ɛnora a na me reba no, awifoɔ bi maa me kɔtɔɔ no maa osua ne surusika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma mu duru. Ɔbaa no tuu mmirika, yiridi, yiridi, yiridi! (ɛno ne nnyigyei a na ɔwɔ sɛ ɔretu mmirika no) na ɔkaa amaneɛbɔ no kyerɛɛ mpaninfoɔ (akuraa). Wɔfaa abofra no, na ɔkɔkyerɛɛ wɔn nipadua no. Wɔkɔɔ kɔgyee no, na wɔsiee no yie. Wɔkae sɛ, Afei na wubedi Ɔhene no so nkonim. Enti abofra no bɛyɛɛ Ɔhene. Afei, bere a ne nuabarimanom mpanimfo tee ne ho asɛm no, wɔbae, na afei wɔkae sɛ wɔne no yɛ mogya abusua. Abofra no kae sɛ, Mopue mfi hɔ, minnim mo, monkɔ. Na ɔmaa ne nkoa no pam wɔn. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, Sɛ wo rekɔ baabi, na wo nuabarima kumaa no aka sɛ ɔne wo bɛkɔ a, wo ne no nkɔ.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
