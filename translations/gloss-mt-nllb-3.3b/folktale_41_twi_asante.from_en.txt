Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐ WOREKƆ BAABI A, SƐ WAPANYIN KYERƐ SƐ ƆBƐKA WO HO A, FRA NE HO

ƐDII BERE BI A ƆBEA BI WƆ HƆ, na ɔwoo mma baasa. Wɔn mu kumaa no na ɔwɔ akisikuru yareɛ. Ne nuanom no mu panyin no ka kyerɛɛ wɔn maame sɛ ɔmma wɔn sika a ɛbɛtumi ama wɔakɔ aguadi. Wɔn mu kumaa no nso kaa sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔne no kɔ. Wɔkaa sɛ ɔne wɔn nkɔ. Ɛna no kaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wɔne no kɔ. Afei wɔn maame de sika a ne boɔ yɛ £5 maa ɔpanyin no, ne sika a ne boɔ yɛ £2 nso maa abofra no. Bere a nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ. Mpanimfoɔ no kɔɔ (wɔn anim) na wɔgyaa abofra no (wɔn akyi). Abofra no de nkakrankakra bae. Mpanimfoɔ no hyiaa ɔbarima bi a ɔde mpataa reba. Ɔka kyerɛɛ mpanimfoɔ no sɛ, "Tɔ! " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Kɔ so, na wobɛhyia abofra bi; ma no tɔ, na sɛ ɔka sɛ ɔrentɔ a, bɔ no na ma no tɔ; na sɛ ɔda so ara pow sɛ ɔrentɔ a, fa no san ne no nkɔ ne maame nkyɛn. " Ɔyɔnko no kɔɔ so, na akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ ohyiaa abofra no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mpanimfoɔ bi a me ne wɔn hyiaa no, wɔkaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wotɔ mpataa yi. " Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ metɔ, berɛ a wɔn a wɔyɛ me mpanimfoɔ no antɔ? " Ɔyɔnko no kaa sɛ, "Wɔka sɛ sɛ woantɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ mebɔ wo, na ɛsɛ sɛ mede wo san kɔma wo maame. " Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dodoɔ bɛn? " Ɔyɔnko no kaa sɛ, "An osua's weight of money futuro (i. e. £2). " Abofra no tuaa no na ɔnyaa no. Ɔkɔɔ so kɔtoo mpanimfoɔ wɔ akuraa bi ase. Wɔde nhabannuru ayɛ aduane, nanso wɔnni nam biara a wɔde bɛkɔ. Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Me nam a metɔ no ni. " Wɔde guu soup-stew no mu. Bere a wɔwiee no, mpanimfoɔ no de mpataa no ti maa no. Bere a abofra no rebɛpae no, ɔhunuu sika kɔkɔɔ a ɛwɔ mu, na ɔde kyekyeree natadeɛ ano. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, mpanimfoɔ no sii kwan, na wɔrekɔ no, wɔhyiaa ɔbarima bi, na akokɔ ba bi sii nneɛma no so, a na ɔso. Ɛhɔ ara na ɔne mpanimfoɔ no kɔɔ soro no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Tɔ akokɔ yi. " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Kɔ so, na wobɛhyia abofra bi; ma no ntɔ, na sɛ ɔmpɛ a, bɔ no. " Akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho sɛ ɔne abofra no hyiaa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wabusua mpanimfoɔ aka sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ wotɔ akokɔ yi, na sɛ woantɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ mebɔ wo, na mede wo kɔ wo maame nkyɛn. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Here, take what I have. " Ne yɔnko no nso de akokɔ no maa no. Wɔduruu akuraa bi ase. Wɔ akuraa no nyinaa mu no, na akokɔ biara nni hɔ a wɔde bɛtwa kwaakwaadabi. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, na akokɔ a ɛyɛ abofra no dea no wɔ hɔ. Ɔpanyin a ɔwɔ akuraa ase no kaa sɛ, "Abofra, fa akokɔ no bra na ma mentɔ. " Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Price is an osua and suru's weight of money futuro (i. e. £3). " Ɔpanyin no tuaa sika no. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma no mu yɛɛ fann no, wɔsan fii aseɛ bio. Bere a wɔrekɔ no, mpanimfoɔ no hyiaa ɔbarima bi a ɔkura ɔkraman ba. Ɔkae sɛ, "Tɔ akraman yi. " Mpanimfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Fa no kɔ, na wobɛhyia abofra bi; fa no na ma no tɔ. Sɛ ɔka sɛ ɔrentɔ a, bɔ no, na fa no kɔma ne maame. " Ɔyɔnko no kɔɔ anim, na ɔne abofra no hyiaa. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Wo mpanimfoɔ ka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wotɔ akraman yi, na sɛ woantɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ mebɔ wo na mede wo kɔma wo maame. " Abofra no kaa sɛ, Ɛdɔɔso dɛn? Ɔyɔnko no kaa sɛ, An osua ne surus weight of sika futuro (i. e. £3). Abofra no tuaa ka no, na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔne ne mpanimfoɔ no kɔɔ akuraa bi ase. Na mprako no taa ka ɔpanyin a ɔwɔ hɔ no nan bere biara a ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛda. Bere a abofra no faa akraman no kɔɔ hɔ no, ɛkɔɔ ɔpanyin no fie na ɛkyeree mprako a wɔwɔ hɔ no. Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, Me bɛtɔ aboa yi afiri onipa a ɔwɔ no hɔ. Abofra no kae sɛ, Ɛyɛ me deɛ. Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, Ɛdɔɔso dɛn? Abofra no kae sɛ, An osua and surus weight of money futuro (i. e. £3). Ɔpanyin no faa sika futuro no de hyɛɛ ne nsa. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi, na wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ. Mpanimfoɔ no nam kwan so; wɔhyiaa akorɔmfoɔ, a na wɔawia ɔhene bi funu. Ɔkorɔmfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Ɛsɛ sɛ wotɔ funu yi. Mpanimfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Fa no, kɔ so, na wobɛhyia abofra ketewa bi; fa no ma no tɔ. Sɛ ɔka sɛ ɔrentɔ a, bɔ no, na fa no kɔma ne maame. Thieves no kɔɔ so, na wɔne abofra no hyiaa; wɔkae sɛ, The officials say you are to buy this corpse. Abofra no kae sɛ, Eh! ɛdɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ metɔ amu a, anka mentumi mfa nni; nea mede bɛyɛ biara, ɛrenyɛ me hwee. Ɔkorɔmfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Wapanyin aka sɛ, sɛ woretɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛbɔ wo, na yɛfa wo, na yɛsan de wo kɔma wo maame. Abofra no kaa sɛ, Ɛdɛn na ɛyɛ? Wɔkae sɛ, An osua kuruwa ne sika futuru mu duru. Abofra no tuaa ka no, faa amu no, na ɔde guu nwura no mu. Afei ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ, na ɔduruu akuraa bi ase, na ɔkɔɔ ofie bi nkyɛn kɔsrɛɛ aduane. Ɔpanyin a ɔwɔ fie hɔ no kaa sɛ, Ɛnni hɔ. Abofra no kaa sɛ, Agyapa, me srɛ wo. Ɔpanyin a ɔwɔ fie hɔ no kaa sɛ, Ɛnni hɔ. Abofra no kaa sɛ, Agyapa, me srɛ wo. Ɔpanyin a ɔwɔ fie hɔ no kaa sɛ, Dɛn nti na abofra yi haw me sɛɛ? Yɛn hene no awu, yɛredi mmuada; eyi ne da a ɛtɔ so nwɔtwe; nanso yɛhwehwɛ sɛnea yɛbɛtumi biara, yɛntumi nnya namu. Ɔbaa panyin bi a na ɔwɔ hɔ no, na ɔyɛ Sita. ng a ɛbɛn hɔ; ɔkae sɛ, Ma no bi. Ɔbaa no maa no aduane ne nam. Bere a abofra no dii aduane no wieeɛ no, ɔkae sɛ, Master of the house, I have seen the Chiefs body; for yesterday as I was coming, some thieves made me buy it for a osua and surus weight of money futuro. Ɔbaa no dwanee, writhing, writhing, writhing! (was the sound of her running) na ɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ mpanimfoɔ no. Wɔfaa abofra no, na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kyerɛɛ wɔn amu no. Wɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔfaa no, na wɔsiee no sɛnea ɛfata. Wɔkae sɛ, Afei wobɛdi hene no ananmu. Enti abofra no bɛyɛɛ hene. Afei, bere a ne nuanom mpanin no tee ne ho asɛm no, wɔbaa hɔ, na afei wɔrekyerɛ sɛ wɔne no wɔ mogya mu abusuabɔ. Abofra no kae sɛ, Wubetumi afi hɔ, minnim wo, enti kɔ. Na ɔmaa nasomfo no tuu wɔn fii hɔ. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, Sɛ worekɔ baabiara a, na sɛ wo nua kumaa no ka sɛ ɔne wo bɛkɔ a, wo ne no nkɔ.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
