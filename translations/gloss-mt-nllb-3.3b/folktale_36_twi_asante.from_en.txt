Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA KWAKU ANANSE (ANANSE) NYAA ASO WƆ AWAREƐ MU

NA ƆBARIMA BI a wɔfrɛ no Akwasi-the-jealous-one ne ne yere te hɔ, na ne din de Aso, na ɔmpɛ sɛ obiara hu Aso anaa obiara ne no kasa. Enti ɔkɔɔ akyekye akuraa ketewa bi maa Aso sɛ ɔntena mu.

Na obiara nkɔ akuraa no mu da. Afei ɔno, Akwasi-the-jealous-one, antumi anwo mma. Ɛno nti, sɛ ɔne ne yere te kuro no mu a, na obi bɛfa no akɔ. Afei, Ɔsoro-nyame no ka kyerɛɛ mmeranteɛ no sɛ, Akwasi-the-jealous-one de bere tenten paa na waware Aso; ɔnnyinsɛn na ɔnwo ba, enti nea obetumi biara, ɔmfa no nkɔfa Aso, na sɛ ɔnyinsɛn no a, ɔmfa no nkɔfa no (sɛ ne yere). Mmabun no nyinaa bɔɔ mmɔden sɛnea wobetumi sɛ wɔde wɔn nsa bɛka no, nanso obiara antumi. Afei Kwaku Ananse a na ɔwɔ hɔ no rehwɛ saa nsɛm yi, (na) ɔkaa sɛ, Mɛtumi akɔ Akwasi-the-jealous-ones village. Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, So wobɛtumi ayɛ saa ampa? Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Sɛ wobɛma me nea mehwehwɛ no. Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, Ɛdeɛn adeɛ? Ɔkae sɛ, Aduro a wɔde bɔ atuo ne atuo ano aduru. Na Ɔsoro-nyame no maa no. Na Kwaku Ananse de powder ne mashed potato no kɔɔ nkuraa nketewa ( ahodoɔ) mu, na ɔkaa sɛ, Ɔsoro-nyame no ahyɛ me sɛ memfa powder ne mashed potato no mma mo, na moakɔkum nam, na ɛda a mɛsan aba ha no, mefa no kɔ. Ɔkyekyɛɛ powder ne mashed potato no wɔ nkuraa nketewa pii mu, kosi sɛ ne nyinaa wiee. Nkuraasefoɔ no nyinaa maa no nam bi. Ɛda bi, Kwaku Ananse nwenee kɛntɛn bi a wɔde mmɛw ahaban ayɛ. Ɛkame ayɛ sɛ ne tenten fi ha kosi baabi a ɛwɔ hɔ. Kwaku Ananse de no kɔɔ akuraa nketewa no mu, faako a ɔkyekyɛɛ powder ne pulp no, sɛ ɔmmɛgye nam a wɔakum no nyinaa. Agya Anans faa nam ne kɛntɛn a wɔde mmɛw ahaban ayɛ no de sii natifi so, na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ Akwasi-the-jealous-one nkuraa ase. Bere a oduu asubɔnten a Aquasi ne ne yere nom nom no ho no, ɔfaa nam bi de guu mu. Anans bɔɔ mmɔden de mmɛdua ahaban (a nam ahyɛ mu ma) bae na ɔfaa ɔpon a ɛkɔ Aquasi-the-jealous-one compound no mu. Na Aso huu no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Akwasi E! bra bɛhwɛ adeɛ a ɛreba fie ha, dɛn na ɛbɛtumi ayɛ? Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ Sky-god na ɔresoma me, na me ho abere me, na mereba abɛda ha. " Akwasi-the-elder-one kaa sɛ, "Metee me wura akoa. " Aso ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, "Agya man, wo nam bi atɔ fam wɔ ɔpon ano kɔ fie hɔ. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Ao, sɛ woyɛ kraman a, ma ɔnkɔfa no na ɔnwe. " Enti Aso kɔfaa no de maa ne kunu. Afei Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Maame, fa aduane (wɔ ogya no so) bi ma me. " Aso de bi guu so, na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Maame, fufuo na woreyɛ yi anaa eto? " Aso buaa sɛ, "Fufuo. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛneɛ ɛyɛ ketewa dodo; kɔfa kɛseɛ. " Aso kɔfa kɛseɛ, na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Bra na nya nam. " Na mmoa akɛseɛ akyi nnan aduanan wɔ hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, "Fa eyinom nkutoo na fa gu mu, na sɛ wowɔ bokiti a ɛdɔɔso a, anka mɛma wo nam a ɛdɔɔso a wode bɛwe ama wo se ahwe ase. " Aso wiee aduan no, ɔdan no (firi bokiti no mu), na ɔde guu pon bi so, nsu a ayow, na ɔde sii nkyɛn (aduan a aka no). Afei Aso faa ne fã no kɔtenaa ogya no nkyɛn, na mmarima no nso kɔɔ pon no nkyɛn kɔtenaa ase. Wɔn nsa ka wɔn ho wɔn ho akyi (i.e. wodi aduan koro). Bere a wɔredidi no nyinaa, Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Nkyene nni fufu yi mu. " Akwasi ka kyerɛɛ Aso sɛ, "Fa bi bra. " Nanso Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Dabi da, bere a ɔbaa no redidi no, woka kyerɛ no sɛ ɔnsɔre mfa nkyene mmra, wo ara kɔfa. " Akwasi sɔre, na Kwaku Ananse hwɛɛ ne bag mu, na ɔyii ɛmo a ɛyɛ duru no mu de guu fufu no mu. Afei ɔfrɛɛ Akwasi sɛ, "Bra, ɛfiri sɛ na me ne me de bi aba. " Bere a Akwasi baeɛ no, Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "O, merenni bio, me ho akokwaw. " Akwasi a na ɔnnye hwee nni no, ɔkɔɔ so dii. Bere a wɔwiee aduane no, Akwasi kaa sɛ, "Adamfoɔ, yɛn ne wo te ha nanso yɛnnim wo din. " Kwaku Ananse buaa sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ me Rise-up-and-make-love-to-Aso. " Akwasi kaa sɛ, "Mate, na wo, Aso, woate ɔbarima yi din anaa? " Aso buaa sɛ, "Yiw, mate. " Akwasi sɔre sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔsiesie asa a ɛnyɛ hwee no mu baako, na wama obiara ho atɔ no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Rise-up-and-make-love-to-Aso, eyi ne wo dan, kɔda hɔ. " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Meyɛ ɔkra hohorofo ma Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn, na meda abrannaa a wɔabue mu; Efi bere a me maame woo me na me papa woo me no, menda abrannaa a wɔato mu da. " Akwasi kae sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ ɛhe na wobɛda? " Obuae sɛ, "Sɛ mɛda abrannaa a wɔabue mu yi mu a, ɛno bɛyɛ sɛ mɛma mo ayɛ pɛ. Sky-god no, ɛfiri sɛ ɛkyerɛ sɛ na meda Sky-god no veranda dan a ɛda hɔ no mu, ɛfiri sɛ ɛnsɛ sɛ mɛda obiara dan a ɛda hɔ (kyɛn Sky-god deɛ), na ɛfiri sɛ ɛte saa no, mɛda mpa a wɔato mu yi anim ara kwa a worehome. " Ɔbarima no yii mpa so mpa na ɔde too hɔ maa no. Akwasi ne ne yere kɔɔ hɔ kɔhomee, na Kwaku Ananse nso kɔdaa hɔ. Kwaku Ananse da hɔ na ɔde ne ho kɔhyɛɛ cross-dressing room no mu. Kwaku Ananse da hɔ, na ɔfa ne bentaa (na ɔto dwom):

"Akuamoa Ananse, ɛnnɛ yɛbɛduru ɛnnɛ.

Kwaku Ananse, Nsia ba, Nyame maame, Ɔsoro Nyame, Da-da yɛbɛma biribi adu, da-da.

Ananse, ɔkra hohorofoɔ ma Nyame, Ɔsoro Nyame no, To-da mehu biribi.

Afei ogyaee ne sanku a ɔbɔ no, de too nkyɛn, na ɔdaa fam. Ɔdaa berɛ bi, berɛ a ɔtee sɛ Akwasi-aniberefoɔ no refrɛ sɛ, "Agya man - ɛnne biara nni ho mmuaeɛ (gye nnomaa a wɔwɔ dua no so no su nko ara), komm! " "Agya man - ɛnne biara nni ho mmuaeɛ (gye nnomaa a wɔwɔ dua no so no su nko ara), komm! Akwasi-ɔpanyin no rewu; Aduru no anya ne so nkɛntɛnso, nanso ɔfrɛ sɛ, Agya mannnyɛ nnyigyei biara (gye nnomaa a wɔwɔ dua no so no, dinn! Awiei koraa no ɔkae sɛ, Rise-up-and-make-love-to-Easy. Ananse no kae sɛ, M! M! M! Ɔno, Akwasi, kae sɛ, Bue (pon no) ma me. Kwaku Ananse buee (pon no), na Akwasi kɔɔ baabi. Na Ananse no sɔre kɔɔ dan no mu hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, So, woante nea wo kunu kae no? Ɔbuaa sɛ, Dɛn na ɔkaeɛ? Ɔ (Kwaku Ananse) buaa sɛ, Ɔkaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ me sɔre na me dɔ wo. Aso kaa sɛ, Wo nni atoro. Na ɔyɛɛ no maa no, na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔdaeɛ. Saa anadwo no, Akwasi sɔre mpɛn nkron; Ananse nso kɔɔ baabi a Aso wɔ no mpɛn nkron. Bere a nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, Kwaku Ananse fii aseɛ. Ɛbɛyɛɛ bɛyɛ asram abien akyi bere a Aso yafunu mu bɛyɛɛ kɛse no. Akwasi bisaa no sɛ, Dɛn nti na wo yafunu anya saa, ebia na wo yare, efisɛ (wonim) sɛ me a me ne wo te ha yi, mentumi nwo mma? Aso buae sɛ, Worewerɛ fi sɛ ɔbarima a ɔbaa ha a woka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔnsɔre na ɔnyɛ ɔdɔ mma Aso no, ɛneɛ, ɔde me kɔeɛ na mefiri ne mu nyinsɛn. Akwasi-the-grandson-one kae sɛ, Sɔre na ma menfa wo nkɔma no. Yɛkɔɔ Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn kuro no mu. Wɔ kwan so no, Aso woo ba. Wɔduruu Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn kuro no mu, na wɔkɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no sɛ, Wo ba bi a wosomaa no no, ɔdaa me fie, na ɔfaa Aso, na ɔne no nyinsɛnee. Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no kaa sɛ, Madesua no nyinaa da mpaapaemu no so, kɔkyerɛ nea wopɛ. Wɔkɔeɛ no, na Ananse no te ridge-pole bi so. Aso kaa sɛ, Ɛhɔ na ɔwɔ. Na Kwaku Ananse tuu mmirika kɔtenaa mfinimfini. Na Aso san kaa sɛ, Ɛhɔ na ɔwɔ. Ɛnna Kwaku Ananse firi soro bɛhwee fam. Afei na saa da no yɛ Friday. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Me, a mehohoro Ɔsoro-nyame no kra, woagye wo nsa akyerɛ me, ma mahwe ase na manya dɔteɛ kɔkɔɔ wɔ me so. Ntɛm ara na asomfoɔ no faa Akwasi-anibereɛ-baako no poma na wɔbɔɔ no afɔre. Bere a Akwasi-the-jealous-one no wiee nguan no bɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ Sky-god no sɛ, Ɔbaa no ni, ma Kwaku Ananse mfa no. Enti Kwaku Ananse faa Aso, nanso abofra no deɛ, wokum no, twitwaa no asinasin, na wɔhwetee no. Saa kwan yi so na anibereɛ baa mmusuakuo no mu.

Me kyerɛwtohɔ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na fa a aka no nso kamfo me.
