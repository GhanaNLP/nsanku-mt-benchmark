Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ YAREƐ BEBREE BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU

ƐNNƐ, na Kwaku Ananse te hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ Onyankopɔn, Ɔsoro-Onyame no nkyɛn, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Nanaba, fa wo nguan a wɔfrɛ no Kra Kwame (i. e. nea wokura no sɛ afɔrebɔ ma wo kra wɔ Memeneda) na ma minkum nni, na ma menkɔfa ɔbabea fɛfɛɛfɛ bi mma wo (wɔ exchange). " Ɔsoro-Onyame no de nguan no maa no, na Kwaku Ananse fii hɔ san kɔɔ ne akuraa ase, kunkum nguan no na odii. Ananse abusua no kɔɔ akuraa bi ase wɔ saa akuraa no ase no, na ɔbarima baako mpo nni hɔ a ɔnware; na wɔn nyinaa yɛ mmaa. Kwaku Ananse waree wɔn nyinaa na ɔne wɔn tenaa hɔ. Ɛdii da bi a, ɔbɔfoɔ bi baa hɔ. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔeɛ no, ɔkɔɔ ka kyerɛɛ Ɔsoro-Nyame no sɛ, "Na Kwaku Ananse deɛ, waguane a ɔnyaeɛ no, ɔakum no de ama mmaa bi sɛ wɔnni na ɔware wɔn. " Ɔsoro-Nyame no bisaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nokware anaa? " Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Grandsir, ɛyɛ nokware. " Ɔsoro-Nyame no somaa abɔfo sɛ wɔnkɔ saa akuraa no so na wɔnkɔfa mmaa a wɔwɔ hɔ no nyinaa mmra. Abɔfoɔ no kɔɔ hɔ kɔhyiaa mmaa no, na wɔde wɔn nyinaa kɔɔ Ɔsoro-nyame nkyɛn gye ɔbaa baako a na ɔyare no. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Wo a woaka no, dɛn na metumi de wo ayɛ, wo ntumi nyɛ hwee mma me? " Ɔbaa a ɔyare no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na fa akutu bi brɛ me. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ na ɔde akutu bi bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Bathe me and take (the water you have used) and pour it into this basin. " Kwaku Ananse hohoroo ne ho na ɔhwiee (the water he had used) guu basin no mu. Ɔbaa yi bɛyɛɛ fɛ paa, na ɔbaa biara nni hɔ a ɔte sɛ ɔno wɔ mmusuakuo no mu. Kwaku Ananse waree nea na ɔyɛ ne dea dedaw. Afei ɔbɔfoɔ no san bae bio, na ɔhunuu saa ababaa yi. Ɔkɔbɔɔ Ɔsoro-Nyame no amaneɛ sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse ayɛ wo nkwasea, ɔde mmea a wɔn ho yɛ fɛ no aba wo nkyɛn na ɔde nea ɔyɛ fɛ no akura hɔ. " Ɔsoro-Nyame no somaa abɔfo na ɔmaa wɔn kɔɔ hɔ baabi a Ananse no wɔ, sɛ wɔnkɔfa saa ɔbaa no mma no. Wɔde saa nkra yi kɔmaa Kwaku Ananse. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ ɔno nso bɛpɛ me anaa? " Abɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Ɔsoro-nyame no reka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ yɛfa ɔbaa no kɔ ne nkyɛn. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɔno ne no, ɔte hɔ, fa no kɔ. " Afei, Kwaku Ananse kɔgyee akutu a na ɔyareɛ a ɔagye afiri ɔbaa no mu nyinaa agu mu no, na ɔtrɛw wedeɛ bi mu wɔ ano, na ɔtrɛw wedeɛ bi mu wɔ akutu foforɔ mu de maa ne ba Ntikuma, na Kwaku Ananse bɔɔ ne ho (wɔ bridge a wayɛ no) na ɔtoo dwom sɛ:

"Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

Aso Ya E!

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

Wani abere ara kwa!

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

Wo ho akura akode a ɛyɛ hu!

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

Aso Ya ne no anaa?

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

Wo abɔ wo ho akuturuku!

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

Wo hwene yɛ nsɛntitiriw wɔ wanim!

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

Wanan te sɛ akoa pavedles!

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

Wo ti te sɛ nantwi ba!

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den. "

Ntikuma (bɔ sanku na wɔto dwom):

"Ababaawa fɛfɛɛfɛ, Ababaawa fɛfɛɛfɛ!"

Na Afudotwedotwe ne Nyiwankɔnfwea asaw. Anene, kwaakwaadabi no, de ahopere tuu mmirika kɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔsoro Nyame no sɛ, Kwaku Ananse wɔ asaw bi a ɛfata wo nanso ɛnyɛ Ananse. Ntɛm ara na Ɔsoro Nyame no somaa abɔfo kɔɔ hɔ sɛ wɔmmɛfa Kwaku Ananse asaw no mmra. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Me asa yi, yɛdi no ahenfie hɔ nko ara, na sɛ Ɔsoro Nyame no pene so a, me nso mede bɛba. Wɔkɔɔ ka kyerɛɛ Ɔsoro Nyame no. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ɛno nni hwee, ma ɔmfa (ɛno) mmra. Kwaku Ananse ne (singer) no kɔɔ ahenfie hɔ, na Ɔsoro Nyame no bae bɛsaw, na ne yerenom nyinaa bɛsaw. Afei, na ɛno nko ara na aka a na ɔyare. Bere a ɔhunuu sɛ Kwaku Ananse atrɛw wedeɛ bi mu wɔ towa no so a ne yareɛ nyinaa wɔ mu no, ɛno nti, ɔkaa sɛ ɔrensa. Na afei Sky-god hyɛɛ no, na ɔbaeɛ; na berɛ a ɔrebɛsa no, Kwaku Ananse maa towa no so na ɔde bɔɔ ɔbaa no, na yareɛ no dwirii (wɔ nnyigyeɛ a ɛte sɛ), tese! Ɛneɛ na syphilis, stomach-ache, headache, leprosy, guinea-worm, small-pox, yaws, fits, diabetes, diarrhea baa mmusuakuo no mu. Bere bi na yareɛ biara nni nnipa mu, nanso ɔsoro-nyame Kwaku Ananse na ɔde yareɛ baa mmusuakuo no mu.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
