KƆKƆKYINAKA, TURACOU A ƆNE BO YƐ BLU, ANKƆ KWAKU ANANSE, ANANSE, KƆYƐ BƆSEƐ BI.

ƐKƆƆ SO WƆ berɛ bi a Kɔkɔkyinaka anomaa kɔɔ Kwaku Ananse, Ananse, kɔhwehwɛ bɔseɛ a emu duru yɛ asuanu sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (£4). Seesei, Kwaku Ananse ankasa nni bi, enti ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔhuu nadamfo Ntontom sɛ ɔrekɔfa bi ama nadamfo Kɔkɔkyinaka anomaa no. Ɛduruu da a Ntontom ahyɛ sɛ wɔbɛbɔ bɔseɛ no ntua no, na Ntontom ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, Kɔfa me sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no ma me. Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, Kɔkɔkyinaka anomaa no te saa silk-cotton-dua no so, enti kɔfa wo sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no. Ntontom bisaa sɛ, Ɛbɛyɛ dɛn na mɛkɔfa no? Ɛyɛ abɔdeɛ a ɛtumi tu. Ananse no buae sɛ, Kɔfa dua no so, na bere a woahunu no, fa akwantu kɔ soro. , dua no bɛbubu a, na woanya Kɔkɔkyinaka anomaa no, na woanya wo sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no asan. Ntontom no kɔbɔɔ dua no so. Bere a na ɛrebɛbu no, Kɔkɔkyinaka anomaa no tu fii hɔ kɔeɛ, pa bu bu! Afei Ntontom no bae, na ɔkae sɛ, Congratulations, Kwaku Ananse. Ɛno nti na yɛwɔ abebuo a ɛtu mmirika no, Abɔdeɛ a wɔfrɛ no Kɔkɔkyinaka anomaa no amfa nkrataa mfi Kwaku Ananse hɔ sɛ Ntontom no nkɔtwa Silk-cotton-tree no so. Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
