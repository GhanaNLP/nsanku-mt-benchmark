ƐKƆƆ SO SƐNEA ƐKƆƆ SO SƐ ƆPƐTƐ NO TE FI NTAM DAA

NA Ɔpɛtɛ bi ne Kwaakwaadabi bi te hɔ, na Kwaakwaadabi no kae sɛ, Momma yɛnkɔ twitwa twene bi nni so agorɔ. Wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ nwura no mu, na wɔbaa silk-cotton dua bi ho. Ɛduruu sɛ Ɔpɛtɛ no hunuu dua a ɛyɛ silk-cotton no, ɔkaa sɛ, Eyi yɛ me deɛ, ɛfiri sɛ me deɛ, meyɛ ɔpanin, enti ɛsɛ sɛ mitwa dua kɛseɛ yi gu. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, Metee. Kwaakwaadabi no nso, berɛ a ɔrehwɛ ne ho no, ɔhunuu dua a ɛyɛ Cedar a ɛda hɔ, ɔno nso kɔtwaa no. Wɔn mmienu nyinaa yɛɛ akyene. Ɔpɛtɛ no bɔɔ ne twene no, na ɛteɛ sɛ nea ɛteɛ yi ara:

Futuro! Futuro! Me nsa aka (mine) ntɛm!

Ɔpɛtɛ Kwakye! Me nsa aka (mine) ntɛm!

Kwaakwaadabi bɔɔ no, na ɛte sɛɛ:

Kwaakwaadabi, Kwa! Kwa!

Kwaakwaadabi, Kwa! Kwa!

Wɔkɔɔ so bɔɔ nnwom, na ɔsoro nyameɛ no tee, na ɔsomaa Akrɔma no sɛ wɔnkɔfrɛ wɔn; na Akrɔma no baeɛ no, wɔmaa wɔn akwaaba sɛ, "Berɛ a atwam! " na wɔbuaeɛ sɛ, "Ame! " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔsoro nyameɛ no ka sɛ wate asaw nnwom bi wɔ ha, enti ɛsɛ sɛ wode ba bɛbɔ ama no ate. " Na abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no sii hɔ kɔɔ ɔsoro nyameɛ anim, na wɔdii agorɔ, dii agorɔ, dii agorɔ, na ɔsoro nyameɛ no ani gyee wɔn ho. boa, "Metee sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ; nnwom nnyigyei mmienu no nyinaa yɛ dɛ, enti mema mo biara biribi. " Ɛneɛ Ɔsoro Nyame no kɔfaa nnaka mmienu na ɔde sii hɔ, baako a ɛho akyi yɛ fɛ paa sɛ wobɛhu; dɔteɛ a ɛkɔ ma sɛe saa wɔ mu; foforo nso, nea ɛyɛ tan paa; sika kɔkɔɔ kɔ ma sɛe saa wɔ mu. Ɔsoro nyame no kasae sɛ, "Mo baanu biara mmra na monnye. " Ɔpɛtɛ no kae sɛ, "Me na meyɛ ɔpanin, enti ma menkɔfa baako nni kan. " Bere a ɔkɔɔ no, ɔyii baako a ɛyɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ paa, bere a Kwaakwaadabi nso faa baako a ɛyɛ dɔteɛ paa. Wɔmaa wɔn so kɔduruu wɔn akuraa. Bere a wɔkɔe sɛ wɔrebɛbue no, Ɔpɛtɛ no, bere a ɔhwɛɛ ne mu no, atɛkyɛ fĩ. Wɔ Kwaakwaadabi hɔ no, bere a ɔhwɛɛ mu no, na sika kɔkɔɔ a ɛhyerɛn sɛ ade biara. Ɛnkyɛree na messenger bi bɛkaa asɛm kyerɛɛ Ɔpɛtɛ sɛ, "Wɔasoma me sɛ memmɛka nkyerɛ wo sɛ wo maame mmara awu. " Ɔno (Ɔpɛtɛ) kae sɛ, "Kwaakwaadabi, fa sikakɔkɔɔ pow baako bɛkyekyere me basa sɛ menkɔ ayie. " Ɔgyee sikakɔkɔɔ sin no kyekyeree no, na ɔkɔɔ ayie hɔ. Ɔduruu hɔ no, ɔkaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ ɔkɔhome ne ho, ɔkotow fam, nanso berɛ a na ɔrebɛsɔre no, sikakɔkɔɔ sin no hwee ase na ɛyeraeɛ. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ao, Kwaakwaadabi agyapadeɛ no ayera, ɛbɛyɛ dɛn na mɛsan aba fie? " Enti ɔhuruhuruw kɔɔ fi no mu kɔhwehwɛɛ sikakɔkɔɔ sin no. Ɛno nti na wobehu Ɔpɛtɛ a, daa na ɔteɛteɛ, ɔteɛteɛ, wɔ tiafi hɔ, efisɛ na ɔrehwehwɛ sɛ ɔkɔkyɛm no nugget no de brɛ no.

Ɛno nti na Mpanimfoɔ se, "Anibereɛ nnyɛ yie".

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi mo nnye nni, na ahomegyeɛ no monkamfo me (asɛmka no).
