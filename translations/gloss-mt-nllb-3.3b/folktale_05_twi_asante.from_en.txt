SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ ƆPƐTƆ NO TAA TE FĨ NTAM

ƐDA KORO BI A, Ɔpɛtɛ ne Kwaakwaadabi bi te hɔ, na Kwaakwaadabi no kae sɛ, Yɛnkɔ na yɛntwa abran na yɛnni agorɔ wɔ so. Wɔfirii kwan so kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu, na wɔhunuu silk-cotton dua bi. Ɛduruu sɛ Ɔpɛtɛ no hunuu dua a ɛyɛ silk-cotton no, ɔkaa sɛ, Eyi deɛ, ɛyɛ me deɛ, ɛfiri sɛ me deɛ, me ne ɔpanin, ɛno nti, mɛtwa kɛseɛ yi. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, Mate. Kwaakwaadabi no nso, bere a ɔrehwɛ nakyi no, ɔhunuu dua a ɛyɛ cedar a ɛgyina hɔ, ɔno nso kɔtwaeɛ. Wɔn mmienu nyinaa yɛɛ ahwehwɛ. Bere a Ɔpɛtɛ no bɔɔ nabran no, na ɛteɛ ara ne sɛ:

Futuro! Futuro! Me nsa aka (mine) ntɛm!

Ɔpɛtɛ Kwakye! Me nsa aka (mine) ntɛm!

Bere a Kwaakwaadabi bɔɔ ne de no, na ɛte sɛ nea eyi:

Kwaakwaadabi, Kwa! Kwa!

Kwaakwaadabi, Kwa! Kwa!

Wɔkɔɔ so bɔɔ nnwom, na ɔsoro-nyame no tee, na ɔsomaa abɔfo (bɛfrɛɛ wɔn); na abɔfo no bae, (na wɔmaa wɔn akwaaba) "Bere bi a atwam! " Na wɔkae sɛ, "Amen! " Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔsoro-nyame no ka sɛ wate asaw-nnwom bi wɔ ha, enti ɛsɛ sɛ wode ba na wode bɔ ma no sɛ ontie. " Abɔdeɛ no kɔɔ ɔsoro-nyame no anim; na wɔdii agorɔ, na wɔdii agorɔ, na wɔdii agorɔ, na ɔsoro-nyame no aboa, "Metee sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ; nnwom no nnyigyei mmienu no yɛ dɛ, enti mede biribi ma mo (obiara). " Ɛneɛ, Ɔsoro-nyame no kɔfaa nnaka mmienu na ɔde sii hɔ, baako akyi no yɛɛ fɛ paa sɛ wobɛhu; dɔteɛ a ɛwɔ ano te sɛ nea ɛwɔ mu yi; foforɔ nso, ɛyɛ nea ɛyɛ fi paa; sika kɔkɔɔ a ɛwɔ ano te sɛ nea ɛwɔ mu yi. Ɔsoro-nyame no kasae sɛ, "Mo baanu biara mmra na monnye. " Ɔpɛtɛ no kae sɛ, "Me na meyɛ panyin, enti ma menkɔfa baako nni kan. " Bere a ɔkɔɔ no, ɔyii nea ɛyɛ fɛ paa no, bere a Kwaakwaadabi no nso faa nea ɛyɛ tan paa no. Wɔmaa wɔn so kɔɔ wɔn akuraa ase. Bere a wɔkɔɔ sɛ wɔrebue wɔn ano no, Ɔpɛtɛ no, bere a ɔhwɛɛ ne mu no, na dɔteɛ no ayɛ fĩ. Wɔ Kwaakwaadabi fie hɔ, bere a ɔhwɛɛ mu no, na sika kɔkɔɔ a ɛhyerɛn sɛ biribiara. Ɛnkyɛree koraa na ɔbɔfo bi bɛkaa kyerɛɛ Ɔpɛtɛ no sɛ, "Wɔasoma me sɛ memmɛka nkyerɛ wo sɛ wo maame ba no awu. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Kwaakwaadabi, ma me sika kɔkɔɔ powder baako a mede bɛkyekyere me nsa sɛ menkɔ ayie no. " Ɔgyee sika kɔkɔɔ no na ɔkyekyeree no, na ɔfirii hɔ kɔɔ ayie no ase. Ɛduruu hɔ no, ɔkaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ ɔkɔgye nahome, ɔkotowee, nanso berɛ a ɔrebɛsɔre no, sika kɔkɔɔ no hwee aseɛ, na ɛyeraeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "O, Kwaakwaadabi agyapadeɛ no ayera, na ɛbɛyɛ dɛn na mɛsan aba fie? " Enti ɔhuruw kɔɔ amena no mu na ɔhwehwɛɛ (sika kɔkɔɔ no). Ɛno nti na wobɛhu Ɔpɛtɛ no, daa ɔreteɛteɛ, reteɛteɛ, wɔ tiafi hɔ, ɛfiri sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ Crow no nugget sɛ ɔde bɛba ne nkyɛn.

Ɛno nti na mpanimfoɔ no ka sɛ, "Greed is not good. "

Eyi, me asɛm a mekaeɛ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi wɔ hɔ a wobɛtumi agye sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na ebi nso wɔ hɔ a wobɛtumi akamfo me.
