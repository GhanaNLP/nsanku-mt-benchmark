Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ MMOFRA NA WƆBƆƆ WƆN MMƆDEN (NEA EDI KAN)

WƆKYERƐ SƐ (once upon a time) ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bi baeɛ, na Agya Ananse, Ananse, ne ne yere "Aso", ne ne mmofra, Ntikuma, Nyiwankɔnfwea (Thin-shanks), Afudototwewed (Belly-like-to-burst), ne Tikononkono (Big-bighead) sii atenaeɛ ketewa bi na wɔtenaa mu. Da biara na Ananse no kɔfa aduane, wuram bayere, na wɔnoa na wɔnoa. Afei da bi, Agya Ananse kɔɔ nwura no so, ɔhunuu sɛ aduan dɛdɛ bi gyina hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. Aduane no kaa sɛ, Me din nnyɛ beautiful. Ananse no kaa sɛ, Wofrɛ wo dɛn? Ɛbuaa sɛ, Me frɛ wo, Fill-up-some-and-eat. Ananse no kaa sɛ, Fill up some that I may see. Aduane no hyɛɛ palm-oil soup ma, na Kwaku Ananse dii ne nyinaa. Ɔwieeɛ no, ɔbisaa aduane no sɛ, Wo akyiwadeɛ bɛn? Aduane no nso buaeɛ sɛ, Mekyi otuo tɛtea, ne gourd cup ketewa bi. Ananse no faa nkyɛnsee fie no, na ɔkɔtoo silin no so. Afei Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ nwura so kɔgyee aduane, na "Aso", bere a ɔwiee aduannoa no, ɔfrɛɛ Kwaku Ananse, na ɔkae sɛ, Ao, wo deɛ ɛyɛ ahiade ankasa, me deɛ, me ne akoraa no, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ me ne aduane yɛ, wo deɛ ne mmofra yi deɛ yɛ ade a ɛho hia ankasa; sɛ wani abere a, na maso ayɛ dinn (wo kwadwom no nnyegyeeɛ nti). Bere a wɔwiee didi no, na Spider pa no ssed behind the aseseɛ, and went (and sat) on the silin where the aduane was. Ɔkae sɛ, This aduane is beautiful. It answered, My name is not beautiful. He said, What is your name? It said, I am called, Fill-up-some-and-eat. Kwaku Ananse said, Fill up some for me to see. Na ɛhyɛ prɛte a ground-nut soup ayɛ no ma, na Kwaku Ananse dii. Everyday when he woke up it was like this. Afei, Ntikuma bɛhunuu sɛ ne agya no nnyaeɛ, nitan wɔ nokwasɛm a ɛne sɛ ɔne no nni dɔn, na Ntikuma kuraa nagya no so na ɔhunuu nea agya no anya ho nsɔmu. Bere a agya no kɔɔ nwura so no, Ntikuma foro kɔɔ silin no so na ɔhunuu aduane no. Ɔfrɛɛ ne maame ne ne anuanom, na wɔkɔɔ soro. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. Ɛkaa sɛ, Mfrɛ me beautiful. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ɛnneɛ wɔfrɛ wo dɛn? Ɛkaa sɛ, Me din ne Fill-up-some-and-eat. Ɔkaa sɛ, Fill up a little that I may see. Na aduane no hyɛɛ palm-oil soup no ma kɔduruu ma sɛe. Ntikuma ne ne maame ne ne anuanom dii ma ne nyinaa wiei. Na afei Ntikuma bisaa aduane no sɛ, Dɛn na wɔabara wo? Aduane no kaa sɛ, Mekyi otuo tɛtea ne kora ketewa. Ntikuma ka kyerɛɛ Afudotwedotwe sɛ, Kɔfa bi brɛ me. Na ɔde wɔn bae, na ɔfaa otuo tɛtea no kaa no (aduane no), ne kora ketewa no nso kaa no (aduane no). Afei wɔn nyinaa sii fam. Na Agya Ananse nso aba (firi nwura a ɛwɔ wuram bayere no mu). "Aso" noa no wiei. Wɔfrɛɛ Kwaku Ananse, na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Ebia woante nea mekaeɛ no, mekaeɛ sɛ ɛfiri da a mede aduane bɛba fie no, wobɛtumi adidi, ɛfiri sɛ woyɛ ebinom wɔ ahiadeɛ mu. " "Aso" ne ne mmofra dii. Agya Ananse hohoroo ne ho, ɔforo kɔɔ silin no so, ɔkaa sɛ, Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ. Kommm koraa! Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ. Kommm koraa! Agya Ananse kaa sɛ, Ah! ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ ntoma yi ho akawnto sɛ ɛnyɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ, mɛkɔ akɔfa Ɔyokofoɔ kente no ahyɛ. Na ɔhwee aseɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔfa Ɔyoko abusuakuo kente ntoma no. Ɔhyɛɛ ne mpaboa no, ɔforoo hɔ bio. Ɔkaa sɛ, Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. Kommm koraa! Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. Kommm koraa! Ɔhwɛɛ dan no mu hyiaeɛ na ɔhunuu sɛ otuo tɛtea bi ne kora ketewa bi wɔ mu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Ɛnyɛ adeɛ baako, ɛnyɛ nneɛma mmienu (i.e. a ɛnni akyinnyeɛ biara) no, ɛyɛ Ntikuma. Kwaku Ananse bubuu aduane no, baa fam; ɔyii Ɔyoko kente ntoma no guu fam, na ɔtuu kwan kɔɔ nwura no ho. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu adeɛ bi a ɛyɛ fɛ paa (nea wɔfrɛ no) Mpere, abapra no, sɛn hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, Oh, wonderful! adeɛ yi yɛ fɛ sen nea ɛtwa toɔ no; abapra yi yɛ fɛ. Ananse no kaa sɛ, Me din nnyɛ beautiful. Ɔkaa sɛ, Afei wo din ne dɛn? Ɔkaa sɛ, Me din ne Abiridibrada. (Ɛne sɛ, Swish-and-raise-weals). Na ɔkaa sɛ, Swish kakra ma mehu. Na abapra no bɛtoo no so, Swish, swish, swish. Agya Ananse teɛɛ sɛ, Pui! pui! Na anomaa bi a ɔte bɛn no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, Ka sɛ Adwobere (a ɛkyerɛ sɛ, cool-and-easy-now). Na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Adwobere. Na abapra no gyaee no bɔ. Na Kwaku Ananse de abapra yi baa fie; na ɔkɔtoo silin so. "Aso" wiee aduane no, ɔkae sɛ, Kwaku Ananse, bra bɛdidi. Ɔbuaa sɛ, Wo da so wɔ asase so nti, ebia na ɛtokuro biara nni waso (na wo nte nea mekae no), enti merenni. Kwaku Ananse foro kɔɔ soro kɔtenaa aseɛ dwoodwoo. Afei ɔsan baa fam, na afei ɔsan kɔɔ baabi kɔhyehyɛɛ ne ho. Afei Ntikuma foro kɔɔ soro. Ɔkae sɛ, "O, saa agya no de biribi aba bio. " Ntikuma kae sɛ, "Ɛna, Nyiwankonfwea, Afudotwedotwe, bra ha, efisɛ nea agya de aba yi sen nea etwa to no koraa. " Afei wɔn nyinaa foro kɔɔ silin no so. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Saa adeɛ yi yɛ fɛ. " Ɛbuaa sɛ, "Wɔmfrɛ me 'beautiful'. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo din ne dɛn? " Ɛbisaa sɛ, "Me din ne 'Swish-and-raise-weals'. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Swish kakra ma mehu. " Na saa abapra no bɛtoo no so na ɔbɔɔ wɔn apira kɛse. Kwaku Ananse gyinaa nkyɛn, na ɔbɔɔ dawuru sɛ, "To no so, to no so, titiriw wɔ Ntikuma so, to no so. " Mprempren, bere a Kwaku Ananse hwɛɛ na ɔhunuu sɛ wɔabɔ wɔn mpire yiye no, ɔkae sɛ, "Adwobere" (Cool-and-easy-now). Kwaku Ananse baeɛ na ɔfaa abapra no twitwaa mu yɛɛ no nketenkete na ɔhwetee ho. Ɛno na ɛmaa abapra no baa abusuakuo no mu. Enti ɛba no sɛ, sɛ woka asɛm bi kyerɛ wo abofra, na ontie wo a, yɛbɔ no mpire.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
