Da biara na Ananse no kɔfa aduane, wuram yam, na wɔnoa na wɔdi. Ɛnneɛ da bi, Agya Ananse kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu, ɔhunuu sɛ aduane a ɛyɛ dɛ bi gyina hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. Aduane no kaa sɛ, Me din nnyɛ beautiful. Ananse no kaa sɛ, Wo din de dɛn? Ɛbuaa sɛ, Me din de, Fill-up-some-and-eat. Ananse no kaa sɛ, Fill up some that I may see. Aduane no de palm-oil soup bɛhyɛɛ mu ma, na Kwaku Ananse dii ne nyinaa. Bere a owiei no, obisaa aduan no sɛ, Dɛn na ɛhaw wo? Aduan no buae sɛ, Me tan tomato tea ne gourd kuruwa ketewaa bi. Ananse no faa sink no kɔɔ dan mu na ɔde sii dan no atifi. Ɔkaa sɛ, This food is beautiful. It replied, My name is not beautiful. He said, What is your name? It said, I am called, Fill-up-some-and-eat. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Fill up some for me to see. And it filled up a plate full of ground-nut soup, and Kwaku Ananse ate. Da biara a ɔbɛsɔre no, na ɛte sɛɛ. Afei, Ntikuma hunuu sɛ ne papa no mu nyɛ hare, ɛfiri sɛ ɔne no annidi, na Ntikuma kɔɔ so hwɛɛ ne papa na ɔhunuu deɛ ne papa anya. Bere a papa no kɔɔ sare no so no, Ntikuma foro kɔɔ soro (of the terrace) na ɔhunuu aduane no. Ɔfrɛɛ ne maame ne ne nuanom, na wɔkɔɔ soro. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Adidie yi yɛ fɛ. Ɛkaa sɛ, Me din nnyɛ beautiful. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ɛnneɛ wo din de dɛn? Ɛkaa sɛ, Me din ne Fill-up-some-and-eat. Ɔkaa sɛ, Fill up a little that I may see. Na aduane no de palm-oil soup bɛhyɛɛ mu kɔsii ano. Ntikuma ne ne maame ne ne nuanom dii kosii sɛ ne nyinaa wieeɛ. Na afei Ntikuma bisaa aduane no sɛ, Dɛn na wani nnye ho? Aduane no kaa sɛ, Mekyi tomato tea ne casserole. Ntikuma ka kyerɛɛ Afudotwedotwe sɛ, Kɔ na fa bi ma me. Na ɔde wɔn bae, na ɔfaa tomato tea no kaa ho (aduan no), na casserole no nso kaa ho (aduan no). Afei wɔn nyinaa kɔɔ fam. Na Agya Ananse (mean-time) asan aba (from the waste with the wild berries). Aso wiee wɔn aduane no. Wɔfrɛɛ Kwaku Ananse; ɔbuaa sɛ, Ebia moante nea mekaeɛ no; mekaeɛ sɛ, ɛfiri nnɛ kɔsi, bere a mede aduane bɛba fie no, mo bɛtumi adi, ɛfiri sɛ mo na mo ho hia. Aso ne ne mma no dii. Agya Ananse hohoroo ne ho, ɔforo kɔɔ soro, ɔkaa sɛ, Adidie yi yɛ fɛ. Kommm koraa! Adidie yi yɛ fɛ. Kommm koraa! Agya Ananse kaa sɛ, Ah! Ɛbɛyɛ sɛ ntama yi nnyɛ fɛ nti, me kɔfa Oyokofoɔ kente no mu baako bɛhyɛ. Na ɔfiri fam kɔfa Oyoko abusuakuo kente ntama no. Ɔhyɛɛ ne mpaboa, ɔforo kɔɔ hɔ (sane). Ɔkaa sɛ, Adidie yi yɛ fɛ. Kommm koraa! Adidie yi yɛ fɛ. Kommm koraa! Ɔhwɛɛ dan no mu hyiaeɛ na ɔhunuu sɛ tomato pie ne basket ketewa bi na ɛwɔ mu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Ɛnyɛ adeɛ baako, ɛnyɛ adeɛ mmienu (ie. akyinnye biara nni ho), ɛyɛ Ntikuma. Kwaku Ananse bubuu aduane no, na ɔbaa fam; ɔyii Oyoko kente ntama no guu hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ sare so. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu fɛfɛɛfɛ bi a ɛyɛ fɛ paa (nea wɔfrɛ no) Mpere, akraman no, sɛ ɛsɛn hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, Ao, ɛyɛ nwanwa! saa adeɛ yi yɛ fɛ sen deɛ ɛtwa toɔ no; saa ananseɛ yi yɛ fɛ. Ananseɛ no kaa sɛ, Me din nnyɛ beautiful. Ɔkaa sɛ, Afei wo din de dɛn? Ɔkaa sɛ, Me din ne Abiribrada (ɛne sɛ, Swish-and-raise-weals). Na ɔkaa sɛ, Swish a little for me to see. Na ananseɛ no bɛtoo no so sɛ, Swish, swish, swish Agya. Ananseɛ no teɛɛ sɛ, Pui! pui! Anomaa bi (a ɔte bɛn no) ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, Say Adwobere (a ɛkyerɛ sɛ, cool-and-easy-now). Na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Adwobere. Na akraman no gyaee sɛ ɛbɔ no. Na Kwaku Ananse de akraman yi baa fie; na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔtoo tile no so. Aso wiee aduane no, ɔkaa sɛ, Kwaku Ananse, bra bɛdidi. Ɔbuaa sɛ, Ɛsiane sɛ woda so ara wɔ ha (asase so), ebia waso nni ntokuro biara (na woante nea mekaeɛ no), me nni aduane. Kwaku Ananse foro kɔɔ soro kɔtenaa aseɛ dwoodwoo. Afei ɔsan baa fam; na afei ɔsan kɔɔ hɔ kɔhyɛɛ baabi. Afei Ntikuma foro kɔɔ soro. Ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, saa agya (me papa) no de biribi aba bio. " Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Ɔma, Nyiwankonfwea, Afudotwedotwe, bra ha, efisɛ nea agya de aba yi sen nea ɛtwa toɔ no koraa. " Ɛnna wɔn nyinaa foro kɔɔ soro. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Saa adeɛ yi yɛ fɛ. " Ɛbuaa sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ me 'beautiful'. " Ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Wo din de dɛn? " Ɛkaeɛ sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ me 'Swish-and-raise-weals'. " Ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Swish kakra ma mehu. " Na saa akraman no bɛtoo no na ɔbɔɔ wɔn kɛse. Kwaku Ananse gyinaa nkyɛn, na ɔtoo dwom sɛ, "Lay it on, lay it on, especially on Ntikuma, lay it on him. " Afei a Kwaku Ananse ahwɛ na wahu sɛ wɔabɔ wɔn mpire a ɛfata no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Adwobere" (Cool-and-easy-now). Kwaku Ananse baeɛ na ɔfaa akpra no na ɔtwaa no nketenkete na ɔhwetee hɔ. Ɛno na ɛmaa akpra no baa abusuakuo no mu. Enti ɛba no sɛ, sɛ woka asɛm bi kyerɛ wo ba, na ɔrentie wo a, yɛbɔ no mpire.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
