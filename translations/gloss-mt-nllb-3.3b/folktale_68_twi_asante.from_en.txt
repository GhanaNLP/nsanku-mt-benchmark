Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (that what we are going to say is true)

SƐNEA KWAKU ANANSE, ANANSE NO, BƐYƐƐ MMERƐW

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse no, kɔɔ Onyankopɔn, Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no nkyɛn kɔkaa sɛ, Ma me aburow aba baako na memfa nnipa akuraa nyinaa mma wo. Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no kaa sɛ, Wuntumi. Ananse no buae sɛ, Sɛ mentumi a, mehyɛ wo maame Nsia ntam sɛ me ne me ti bedi ntam. Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no kae sɛ, Gye no (aburow aba) na wo ho ntɔ wo. ɔfirii hɔ. Ananse no fii aseɛ kɔɔ akuraa bi ase. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no asɛm a ɛdidi soɔ yi. Ɔsoro-nyame no asoma me, ɔka sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ mefa aburoo aba baako yi kɔ baabi, na meba a, ɛsɛ sɛ mefa saa aburoo aba yi nna akokɔ no. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, Yiw! sɛ akokɔ ne atokoɔ bɔ mu a, ɛbɛyɛ yie anaa? Kwaku Ananse buaa sɛ, All you have to do is allow them to sleep together. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, Go and place it there. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, Kwaku Ananse kyerɛɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ, nanso atokoɔ no nni hɔ bio. Ananse kaa sɛ, Head-man, the chickens have eaten the Sky-gods corn. The head-man said, I am not able to have any dispute about this, so take the chicken and go. Kwaku Ananse kɔfaa akokɔ kɛseɛ baako, na ɔde no kɔɔ akuraa foforɔ so. Ɛduruu hɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔmanpanyin no asɛm bi sɛ, Ɔsoro Nyame no na ɔsomaa me, na mɛda ha, na akokɔ yi a me kura no, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔne nguan no da. Ɔmanpanyin no kaa sɛ, Fa no bra na fa no si hɔ. Anadwo no, Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ nguandan mu kɔkunkum akokɔ no. Ɔfaa akokɔ no yam na ɔde bɛtoo ɔpɔnkɔ kɛseɛ bi mmati so. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ anɔpatutuutu no, ɔkɔɔ ɔpanyin a ɔwɔ hɔ no nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Fa akokɔ no ma me. " Bere a anka wɔbɛkɔ akɔfa ama no no, hwɛ, akokɔ no awu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ah! ɛno ne Sky-god no akokɔ, a oguan no akum no. " Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Mentumi nni ho akasakasa biara, enti fa oguan no kɔ. " Kwaku Ananse faa oguan no na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ. Ɔduruu akuraa foforɔ bi ase. Ɔkɔɔ ɔpanyin no fie. Ɔpanyin no bisaa no sɛ, "Asɛm bɛn? " Ananse no buae sɛ, "Ɔsoro-nyame no asoma me, na me na me kura oguan no; ɛne anantwi no bɛbom ada. " Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Ho! momma ɛnhome wɔ nguan no nkyɛn. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Dabi, ɛfiri sɛ eyi yɛ Ɔsoro-nyame no mpaboa soronko, ɛnhome wɔ anantwi no nkyɛn. " Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na fa gu hɔ, afei. " Ananse no kɔtoo hɔ. Anadwo no, Kwaku Ananse kɔkunkum oguan no, na ɔde ne mogya guu anantwi no mu baako ti so. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Head-man, ma me me dwan. " Wɔkɔɔ fie hɔ, na ɛhɔ na dwan no wɔ, awu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Head-man, ɛyɛ Sky-god no dwan a anantwi no akum no. " Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Me ntumi nni akasakasa biara ne wo wɔ saa asɛm yi ho, enti fa nantwi a okum no no kɔ. " Kwaku Ananse faa nantwi no, na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ, na ɔbaa akuraa foforɔ bi so. Ɔfaa nantwi ba no kyekyeree no wɔ hɔ, kɔdaa. Bere a ɔda hɔ no, wɔbɛkaa kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no sɛ ne nkoa no mu baako awu. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ woyi no gu, ɛfiri sɛ menni adaka. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, mepɛ, ɛfiri sɛ Sky-god no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, sɛ menya funu a wɔrebɛto agu a, ɛsɛ sɛ mesesa no kɔ nantwi. " Nkuraasefoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Dabi. " (Nanso) Kwaku Ananse (hyɛɛ no den) kaa sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ wode no ba me nkyɛn, na sɛ wokɔtɔn nantwi no a, wubɛnya asuanu sika a ɛfata future (ie. £4), na wobɛtumi de (sika yi) akɔtɔ grill foforɔ. " Nnipa no kaa sɛ, "There is truth in that. " Enti wɔyɛɛ nsakraeɛ no. Kwaku Ananse faa amu no kɔɔ akuraa foforɔ bi so. Ɛduruu hɔ no, ɔkasa kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no sɛ, "Ma me ba yi kwan ma ɔne wo mma no nkɔda, wɔ pato yi mu; afei (me ka kyerɛ wo) abofra yi nnyɛ fɛ, ɔwɔ suban a ɛne sɛ ɔbɛbu mframa anadwo, enti sɛ ɔbɛbu mframa a, mma no mmɔ no. " Afei na ɔbarima no awu nnansa ni. Mmofra no ne no (namu) da hɔ. Afei na wɔte nka sɛ biribi a ɛyɛ huam. Wɔkae sɛ, "Saa abofra no abubu mframa, momma yɛmmɔ no. " Wɔhwee no so, titim! titim! titim! (ɛno ne wɔn nnyigyei a wɔreka no). Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ sɛ ɔrekɔfrɛ ne ba no. Bere a ɔkɔɔ no, na ɔwɔ hɔ a wawu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Head-man, wo mma no akunkum Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no bɔfoɔ a na ɔka me ho no. " Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Me ntumi nni asɛm biara wɔ saa asɛm yi ho, enti wubetumi de mmofra no akɔ. " Ananse no buae sɛ, "(Me ntumi nnye ntom) gye sɛ wo, head-man, ne nnipa a wɔwɔ kuro yi mu nyinaa kɔ Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn anim, kɔka wo asɛm. " Ɔpanyin no bɔɔ dawuru sɛ, "Yɛn nyinaa. ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ Ɔsoro Nyame no anim wɔ hɔ, kɔka yɛn asɛm. " Ananse no na ɔdii anim, nkurɔfoɔ no nyinaa dii nakyi. Berɛ a Kwaku Ananse duu kuro no akyi no, ɔsomaa ɔbɔfoɔ bi sɛ ɔmmɛka nkyerɛ Ɔsoro Nyame no sɛ ɔne kuro bi mu nkurɔfoɔ reba. Ɔsoro Nyame no frɛɛ nasɔretia no, na ɔtenaa aseɛ (wɔ nahenfo mu). Berɛ a Kwaku Ananse tuu kwan baa mu no, ɔtoo dwom sɛ: "Kwaku Ananse, alas, alas, menya atokoɔ aba baako sɛ mɛkɔ akɔgye akokɔ.

Kwaku Ananse, alas, alas, mede akokɔ no kɔgyee nguan.

Me faa nguan no na me kɔfaa nantwi ba, me faa nantwi ba no na me kɔfaa funu, me faa funu no na me kɔfaa kuro bi mu nnipa. "

Obiara kae sɛ, "Wo te dwom a Kwaku Ananse reto no? " Kwaku Ananse san tuu ne dwom bio sɛ:

"Kwaku Ananse, alas, alas, me nyaa aburoo aba baako sɛ mɛkɔ akɔgye akokɔ.

Kwaku Ananse, alas, alas, mede akokɔ no kɔgyee nguan.

Me faa nguan no na me kɔfaa nantwi ba, me faa nantwi ba no na me kɔfaa funu, me faa funu no na me kɔfaa kuro bi mu nnipa. "

Kwaku Ananse bɛduruu Ɔsoro-nyame anim, na Ɔsoro-nyame no bisaa Kwaku Ananse asɛm no. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Aburoo aba baako a manya no, wɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ me ne kuro bi mufoɔ bɛba; kuro bi mufoɔ na wɔgyina hɔ. " Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Momfa no nkamfo. " (Wɔteɛɛ mu sɛ,) "E! e! e! Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mede ma wo de kyerɛ aseda? " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Me kɔn nnye hwee; (place) a ɛwɔ aprannaa no so no ara na mepɛ. " Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Oh, oh! me subject yi, wubewu wɔ ohia mu, wo a wokɔeɛ na wode pii baeɛ, nanso woka sɛ wo kɔn nnye hwee. " Ɛno nti na ohia remma Kwaku Ananse nkyɛn da.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
