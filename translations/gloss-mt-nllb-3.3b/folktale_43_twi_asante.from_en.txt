Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA BƐYƐ SƐ BERE A ADWANSENA, ADWANSENA NO, ƐTENA KRAMAN SO, KRAMAN NO, ƆKYERƐ NO KAM! KAM!

Na Angel bi wɔ kraman baanan; na wɔfrɛ baako Sniff-sniff; na wɔfrɛ baako nso Lick-lick; na wɔfrɛ baako nso Tie-in-Boo; na wɔfrɛ baako nso Mene-Big-down. Ná ɔne ne kraman no kɔ abɔmmɔ. Daa, bere biara a ɔbɛkɔ kwae mu no, na ɔne wɔn kɔ bom. Ɛda bi a ɔkɔɔ kwae mu no, ɔhunuu kola dua bi; na nnuaba akata so a ɛkyekyere wɔ nkorabata a ɛte sɛɛ mu. Afei ɔsan baa fie, na ɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ ne yere, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Cola dua bi a mahunu no, ɛwɔ nnuaba a ɛkyekyere wɔ nkorabata a ɛte sɛɛ mu. " Ne yere kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔfa wɔn, ɛfiri sɛ wunim sɛ yɛnni kola dua baako mpo a yɛbɛwe na yɛantɔn. " Angel no fii kwan no so, nanso ɔgyaa akraman no wɔ fie hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a kola dua no wɔ. Afei ɔforo kɔɔ so, na ɔfaa kola-nuts no bi. Afei ɔtee fi baabi a ɛwɔ hɔ no, "Twa! be! kukuru! kukuru! hu! pi! " Ɔbonsam bi (gyinaa hɔ) nanim. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Hwan na ɔtutu me kola-nuts? " Ɔsomafo no kaa sɛ, "Me. " Satan kaa sɛ, "Mɛkum wo ntɛm ara wɔ saa adeɛ a woayɛ yi ho. " Ɔsomafo no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ worebɛkum me a, ma me di kan frɛ me akraman. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Yɛfrɛ, nanso sɛ woabɔ mmɔden mprɛnsa sɛ wobɛfrɛ wɔn na wɔamma a, ɛnde mɛkum wo. " Ɛnna ɔbɔfo no maa ne nne so:

"Sniff-sniff, (ba na) hyia me, lick-lick, (ba na) hyia me, tie-in-knee, (ba na) hyia me, Mene size-down, (ba na) hyia me.

Wai, wai, wai, wɔn akyɛ ba!

Wai, wai, wai, wɔn akyɛ ba!

Wai, wai, wai, wɔn akyɛ ba!

Wai, wai, wai, wɔn akyɛ ba!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔkyɛ wɔ reba!

Na akraman a wɔwɔ fie hɔ no tee no kakra paa, na wɔde mmirika kɔɔ kuro no akyi. Ɛneɛ ɔbɔfo no san too ne nne so bio sɛ:

Sniff-sniff, (ba na) hyia me, lick-lick, (ba na) hyia me, tie-in, (ba na) hyia me, gulp-down, (ba na) hyia me.

Wai, wai, wai, wɔn akyɛ ba!

Wai, wai, wai, wɔn akyɛ ba!

Wai, wai, wai, wɔn akyɛ ba!

Wai, wai, wai, wɔn akyɛ ba!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔkyɛ wɔ reba!

Seesei akraman no ate; wɔdwanee, prada! prada! Satan kae sɛ, Call again, na ɛno wie ne mprɛnsa so. Angel no de ne nne kɔɔ soro paa:

Sniff-sniff, (ba na) hyia me, lick-lick, (ba na) hyia me, tie-in, (ba na) hyia me, gulp-down, (ba na) hyia me.

Wai, wai, wai, wɔn akyɛ ba!

Wai, wai, wai, wɔn akyɛ ba!

Wai, wai, wai, wɔn akyɛ ba!

Wai, wai, wai, wɔn akyɛ ba!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔbɛkyɛ! Na nano nnya nnyina hɔ, berɛ a na akraman no wɔ nanim. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, Sniff-sniff, ɔbonsam bi wɔ hɔ, sniff no. Na Sniff-sniff no sniff no. Ɔkae sɛ, Lick-lick, lick him over. Ɔkae sɛ, Tie-in-knot, tie him in a point. Tie-in-knot kyekyeree ne moma wɔ ntam; na Satan no hwee ase wɔ hɔ, wui! Afei Mene kɛse no faa no kɔe na ɔdomee no. Ɔbɔfo no ne akraman no boaboaa kola nnuaba no ano na wɔde baa fie. Bere a ɔbɔfo no san bae no, ɔyɛɛ aduan a edi mũ a ɛyɛ asase so nnuaba aduannoa na ɔde sii hɔ maa akraman no; ɔde nsu petepete so na ɔde sii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn kɔdidii. Bere a wadidi wiei no, ɔkɔɔ abɔnten kɔtenaa akutu (dua) bi ase. Afei ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no akoa, Nwansena, kɔɔ hɔ kɔhwɛɛ aduane no (a wɔde asi hɔ ama akraman no), na ɔgyee no de sii soro. Akraman no firi abɔnten baa mu na wɔn kɔm de wɔn. Lick-lick ka kyerɛɛ afoforɔ no sɛ, "Moahunu nea yɛayɛ ama yɛn wura, nanso ɛde besi nnɛ, ɔmma yɛn aduane mpo nni, enti momma yɛnkɔ na yɛnkum no. " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Wo nni atoro, wo nni atoro. " Lick-lick kɔɔ abɔnten kɔfee ɔbɔfoɔ no funu, na ɔne ɔbɔfoɔ no dii agorɔ; na Sniff-sniff nso bae; na ɔfeee ɔbɔfoɔ no funu; na ɔno nso kɔɔ. Ɛnna ntɛm ara a Tie-in-Bow bae sɛ ɔrebɛkyekyere nakɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn no, Mene-down nso bae sɛ ɔrebɛwe no. Afei akraman no san baa fie; na wɔrebɛhwɛ dɔteɛ no mu, bere a wohuu nkwan a ɛwɔ hɔ no. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Fly, ebia ɔbɔfo no de aduane bi gyaw yɛn na woadi? " Fly kaa sɛ, "M'anhunu no baabiara. " Sniff-sniff, bere a ɔmaa n'ani so no, ɔhunuu aduane no a ɛda hɔ (wɔ ceiling no so). Ɔkaa sɛ, "O, papa! yɛakum yɛn wura kwa, ɛfiri sɛ ampa ara aduane a ɔde asie ama yɛn no wɔ hɔ, na ɛnyɛ baako, na ɛnyɛ mmienu nso, ɛyɛ Wasp no na ɔde sii hɔ. " Wasp no dwane firii hɔ kɔeɛ. Ɛno nti, sɛ ɔkraman te hɔ a, na wasp si ne so a, ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkyere no.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
