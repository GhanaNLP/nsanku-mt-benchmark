Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐBƐYƐ NEA ƐBAA SƐ BERE A NWANSANA, NWANSENA NO, TE KRAMAN, KRAMAN NO, ƆBƆƆ NO KAM! KAM!

Ɔbɔfoɔ bi wɔ nkraman nnan; wɔfrɛ baako sɛ Sniff-sniff; wɔfrɛ foforɔ sɛ Lick-lick; wɔfrɛ foforɔ nso sɛ Tie-in-apɔ; na wɔfrɛ foforɔ nso sɛ Mene kɛse-down. Ɔde ne nkraman na ɛhwehwɛɛ mmoa. Bere nyinaa, sɛ ɔkɔ nwura so a, na ɔne wɔn kɔ bɔ mu. Ɛda bi a ɔkɔɔ nwura so no, ɔhunuu kola dua bi; na aduaba akata so a ɛda fam wɔ asã ahorow te sɛ saa. Afei ɔsan baa fie, na ɔkaa amaneɛbɔ kyerɛɛ ne yere sɛ, kola dua bi a mahunu no, aduaba akata so a ɛtwetwe wɔ asã ahorow mu saa. Ne yere kae sɛ, "Kɔfa wɔn, ɛfiri sɛ wunim sɛ yɛnni kola baako mpo a yɛbɛwe na yɛnni bi nso a yɛbɛtɔn. " Ɔbɔfoɔ no de nani sii ɔkwan so, nanso ɔgyaa nkraman no wɔ fie, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a kola dua no wɔ. Afei ɔforo kɔɔ so, na ɔfaa kola-nuts no bi. Afei ɔtee nne fi baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ sɛ, "Twa! Be! kukuru! kukuru! hu! pi! " Sasabonsam bi na ɔbɛgyinaa nanim. Ɔkae sɛ, "Hwan na watwe me kola-nuts? " ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Me. " Sasabonsam no kaa sɛ, "Mekum wo ntɛm ara wɔ akawnto a ɛwɔ wo adeɛ a woayɛ yi ho. " Ɔbisaa ɔbɔfoɔ no sɛ, "Sɛ wobekum me a, ma me nni kan frɛ me nkraman no. " Ɔkae sɛ, "To nsa frɛ wɔn, nanso bere a woabɔ mmɔdenbɔ mprɛnsa sɛ wobɛfrɛ wɔn na wɔamma no, ɛnde mɛkum wo. " Ɛnna ɔbɔfoɔ no maa ne nne so:

"Sniff-sniff, (come and) meet me, Lick-lick, (come and) meet me, Tie-in-apɔ, (come and) meet me, Mene kɛse-down, (come and) meet me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ!

Na nkraman a wɔwɔ fie hɔ no tee no dɛɛdɛɛ paa, na wɔdwanee kɔɔ kuro no kuro akyi hɔ. Ɛnna ɔbɔfoɔ no san yii ne nne bio:

Sniff-sniff, (come and) meet me, Lick-lick, (come and) meet me, Tie-in-apɔ, (come and) meet me, Gulp-down, (come and) meet me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ!

Afei nkraman no ate; wɔdwanee, prada! prada! Sasabonsam kae sɛ, Bɔ no bio, na ɛno wiee berɛ a ɛtɔ so mmiɛnsa. Ɔbɔfoɔ no maa ne nne so kɛse paa:

Sniff-sniff, (come and) meet me, Lick-lick, (come and) meet me, Tie-in-apɔ, (come and) meet me, Gulp-down, (come and) meet me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Ɛkyɛe na wɔreba! Nano nnyina hɔ, nkraman no wɔ nanim. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no ka sɛ, "Sniff-sniff, Sasabonsam bi wɔ ha, sniff no. " Na Sniff-sniff no twetwe no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Lick-lick, lick him over. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Tie-in-apɔ, kyekyere no wɔ pɔ bi mu. " Tie-in-apɔ kyekyeree ne menem wɔ apɔ mu, na Sasabonsam no hwee ase wɔ hɔ, kum! Afei Mene kɛse no faa no kɔe na ɔwewee no. Kola nkutu no, ɔbɔfoɔ no ne nkraman no boaboaa ano de baa fie. Bere a ɔbɔfoɔ no san bae no, ɔde atoko soup aduane a ɛyɛ ma nyinaa yɛɛ aduan no, na ɔde siee hɔ maa nkraman no, na ɔde nsuo petepete so guu hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn kɔdii. Bere a ɔdii no wiei no, ɔkɔɔ so kɔɔ abɔnten hɔ kɔtenaa dua bi ase wɔ gyedua bi ase. Afei ɔbɔmmɔfo no akoa, Nwansena, kɔɔ hɔ kɔhwɛɛ aduane no a wɔde ahyɛ nkraman no ahoboa mu, na ɔmaa so kɔtoo silin no so. Na nkraman no fi abɔnten baeɛ a, wɔn ani bere. Lick-lick ka kyerɛɛ afoforɔ no sɛ, "Moahunu nea yɛayɛ ama yɛn wura no, nanso ɛde besi nnɛ, wamma yɛn aduane biara mpo a yɛbɛdi, ɛno nti momma yɛnkɔkum no. " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Worenni atoro, monni atoro. " Lick-lick kɔɔ abɔnten hɔ kɔfeɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no amu, na ɔne ɔbɔfoɔ no dii agorɔ. Na Sniff-sniff nso baeɛ, na ɔfeɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no amu, na ɔno nso kɔeɛ. Afei ankyɛ na Tie-in-apɔ bɛkyekyeree ne menem wɔ apɔ mu, sen sɛ Mene kɛse-down nso bɛba abɛwe no. Afei nkraman no san baa fie hɔ; na wɔrebɛhwɛ pato no mu, bere a wɔhuu nkwan no nsunsuansoɔ wɔ hɔ. Wɔkae sɛ, "Fly, ebia ɔbɔfoɔ no gyaw yɛn aduane na woadi anaa? " Fly kae sɛ, "M'anhunu no baabiara. " Sniff-sniff, bere a ɔyii n'aniwa, huu aduane no a ɛda hɔ (wɔ silin no so). Ɔkae sɛ, "Ao, agya! yɛn wura no kum no kwa, ampa ara aduane a na ɔde asie ama yɛn no wɔ hɔ, na ɛnyɛ baako mpo, na ɛnyɛ mmienu nso, ɛyɛ Nwansena na ɛgyee hɔ siee. " Nwansena no dwane firii hɔ kɔeɛ. Ɛno nti, sɛ kraman bi te hɔ a, na nwansena bi atena ne so a, na ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkyere no.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
