Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

MPANIMFOƆ KYERƐ SƐ, "MMA ƐNYƐ WO BUSUANI, ANAA KUNU, ANAA OBIARA KORAA A ƆWƆ ADWUMA A ƆBƐYƐ, SƐ ƆBISA WO A, BOA NO"

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse no, ka kyerɛɛ Aso (ne yere) sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔyɛ turo. " Aso kaa sɛ, "Merenkɔ. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ hɔ kɔsiesiee asase no. Bere a ɔsan bae no, ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nokware paa sɛ worenkɔ (mmoa me) anaa? " Aso buae sɛ, "Ɔbaa bi a ɔreyi asase no. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɔkyena yɛnkɔ na yɛnhyew turo mu nwura. " Aso kaa sɛ, "Ɔbaa bi a ɔrehyew turo mu nwura, na me deɛ, menkɔ gya no so mma me? " Enti Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ na ɔsan bae. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Aso, me srɛ mo, momma yɛnkɔ bom na yɛnkɔdua nneɛma. " Ɔkaa sɛ, " mekaeɛ sɛ, merenkɔ. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, " Mahunu mmusuo. " Ɛneɛ ɔkɔduaa nneɛma no nyinaa yie. Ɛnneɛ na ɛwɔ hɔ, na nnɔbaeɛ no baeɛ. Ɛda bi Aso kɔɔ afuo a ɛwɔ hɔ no. Ɔbaa abɛka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, " Kwaku, nnɔbaeɛ no aba turo no mu, na berɛ bɛn na yɛbɛkɔ hɔ (to gather them in)? " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, " Ho! deɛ saa Moslemni no kaeɛ no, mentumi nte aseɛ koraa. " Aso kaa sɛ, " Dɛn na ɛyɛ saa, ka no pefee na mate wo ase. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, " Me kɔ na me san ba. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ hɔ kɔɔ turo no mu, na ɛhɔ na ɔgyinaa hɔ tenten, na ɔkaa (ne ho) sɛ, " Me, Kwaku the Ananse, me ni, na ɛsɛ sɛ metena na mema ɔbaa yi, ɔbaa kɛkɛ, di me ho fɛw anaa? " Kwaku Ananse faa sekan, plow, ne axe, na ɔtutuu amena kɛseɛ pa ara. Ɔfaa nnɔbaeɛ a ɛwɔ afuo no mu nyinaa guu mu, na ɔdane kɔɔ fie. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Aso, bra ha, na mɛka asɛm no akyerɛ wo. " Aso baeɛ. Ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Mekɔɔ akɔbisa ɔbenfoɔ bi mahonyadeɛ ho asɛm; ɔka sɛ nnawɔtwe na aka ma me ansa na mawu, enti menkɔ afuom bio, na mo nso monkɔ hɔ bio, na sɛ mewu a, ɛhɔ na mobɛsie me. Mepae amena kɛseɛ bi wɔ hɔ, na emu na mobɛsie me. " Nnawɔtwe no baa mu; Kwaku Ananse yɛɛ ne ho sɛ ɔwuo. Nnipa no yii Ananse no so na wɔkɔsiee no wɔ hɔ wɔ ne turo no mu. Wɔsan baa fie na wɔyɛɛ funeral rites a ɛfata paa. Aso nso dii nayie no ho dwuma. Bere a wɔreyɛ nayie a ɛtɔ so nwɔtwe no, Aso kaa sɛ, "Ah, ma menkɔhwɛ me kunu turo no. " Aso kɔeɛ; ɛhɔ na turo no da, dɛm! Aso kaa sɛ, "Ao, turo a me kunu yɛeɛ a na mani da so no, nkurɔfoɔ bi abɛdi emu nneɛma nyinaa. " Ɛneɛ ɔsu frɛɛ nnome sɛ, Kwaku Ananse yam, wafuo no mu nnuaba a wogyaee no maa me no, nkurɔfoɔ abɛdi.

Kwaku Ananse maa ne nne so (too dwom):

" Gyae (complaining)! Nea ɛyɛ me nkutoo na medidi!

Gyae (anwiinwii), Aso siw nwura a woyi fi hɔ ano.

Gyae (complaining), me deɛ na medidi nko ara!

Gyae (complaining), Aso akyiwadeɛ rehyew (the garden weed). Gyae (complaining), me nni nea ɛyɛ me nko ara!

Gyae (complaining), Aso tabu afifideɛ.

Gyae (anwiinwii), me ara me deɛ na midi! "

Ɛhɔ ara na Aso gyinaa hɔ komm, na ne ho yɛɛ no hu. Ɔkɔɔ fie na ɔfrɛɛ Adanko, Adanko no, sɛ ɔmmra sɛ ɔmmɛte nsɛm bi a ɛwɔ ne kunu afuo mu hɔ. Adanko no ne no kɔɔ akwantu no mu. Aso bɛduruu afuo no mu hɔ, na ɔsan maa ne nne so bio na ɔtoo dwom sɛ:

"Me kunu Kwaku, a ɔyɛ Ananse, wuiɛ na ɔgyaee ne afuo no maa me, na nkurɔfoɔ baeɛ bɛdii aduane a ɛwɔ mu no.

Ghost of Kwaku, Ananse, mekotow wo, mesrɛ wo, kum wɔn ma me. "

Kwaku Ananse de ne nne too mu sɛ:

"Gyae (nwiinwii), nea na ɛyɛ me nkutoo na midii!

Gyae (anwiinwii), Aso siw nwura a woyi fi hɔ ano.

Gyae (complaining), me deɛ na medidi nko ara!

Gyae (anwiinwii), Aso akyiwadeɛ rehyew (afuw mu fĩ).

Gyae (complaining), nea ɛyɛ me nkutoo na medidi!

Gyae (complaining), Aso tabu afifideɛ.

Gyae (complaining), nea ɛyɛ me nkutoo na midii; gyae (complaining)!

Ɛfiri berɛ a Ɔbɔfoɔ bɔɔ adeɛ no, woahunu ɔbaa a ɔnboa ne kunu?

Gyae (anwiinwii), midii mankasa aduan nkutoo; gyae (anwiinwii)! "

Adanko no kaa sɛ, "Metie no awie, yɛnkɔ fie na mɛka nea ɛfa ho nyinaa akyerɛ mo. " Ɔne Aso kɔɔ fie. Adanko kae sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ, Yɛnkɔsiesie turo bi'; wokae sɛ worenkɔ; Yɛnkɔhyew (weeds dry) ; na wokae sɛ worenkɔ; Yɛnkɔdua dua; na wokae sɛ worenkɔ; nti, wobɛtumi agyae su, ɛfiri sɛ ɔredidi (only) nea ɛyɛ ne dea, ɛfiri sɛ, ɛfiri berɛ a Ɔbɔadeɛ no bɔɔ no, onhuu ɔbaa a ɔmmoa ne kunu; nti gyae anwiinwii. ng (ɔse), ɛfiri sɛ wadidi ne deɛ nyinaa nko ara. " Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Sɛ obiara koraa de ne ho hyɛ adwuma biara mu na ɔsrɛ mo a, mommoa no. "

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
