Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

MPANIMFOƆ KYERƐ SƐ, "ƐYƐ WO BUSUANI, ANAA KUNU, ANAA OBIARA WƆ HƆ A WƆADI NAdWUMA YƐ A, SƐ ƆBISA WO A, BOA NO"

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse, ka kyerɛɛ "Aso" (ne yere), sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔyɛ turo. " "Aso" kaa sɛ, "Me nkɔ. " Kwaku Ananse kɔsiesiee fam. Bere a ɔsan bae no, ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nokware sɛ worenkɔ? " "Aso" buae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ ɔbea akyiwade a ɔreyi fam. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɛbɛyɛ ɔkyena a yɛbɛkɔ akɔhyew turo mu wira no. " "Aso" kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ ɔbea akyiwade a ɔreyi turo mu wira, na me nso, mɛkɔ akɔhyew me anaa? " Enti Kwaku Ananse san bae. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Aso", me srɛ wo, ma yɛnka bom nkɔdwa nneɛma. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mekaa sɛ, merenkɔ. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Mahunu mmusu. " Ɛneɛ ɔkɔdwaa nneɛma nyinaa fɛfɛɛfɛ. Ɛnneɛ na ɛwɔ hɔ, na nnɔbaeɛ no baeɛ. Ɛda bi "Aso" kɔɔ afuo hɔ. Ɔbɛkaa kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, Kwaku, nnɔbaeɛ no aba turo no mu, na da bɛn na yɛbɛkɔ hɔ akɔboaboa wɔn ano? Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Ho! nea saa Nkramoni no kae no, mentumi nte aseɛ koraa. " "Aso" kae sɛ, "Ɛdɛn na ɛyɛ saa; bɔ dawuru na mate wo ase. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Merekɔ na meresane aba. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ hɔ kɔtoo turo no, na ɛhɔ na ɔgyinaa hɔ kyɛe, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ, "Me, Kwaku Ananse no, me na me wɔ ha, na ɛsɛ sɛ metena ase ma ɔbaa kɛkɛ yi di me ho kwasea? " Kwaku Ananse faa sekkan, agyan, ne asɔ, na ɔtutuu amena kɛse pa ara. Ɔfaa nnɔbaeɛ a ɛwɔ afuo no mu nyinaa guu mu, na ɔdane kɔɔ fie. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Aso", bra ha, na mɛka wo amaneɛbɔ. "Aso" baeɛ. Ɔkae sɛ, "Mekɔɔ kramoni bi nkyɛn kɔbisaa m'ahonyadeɛ; ɔka sɛ aka me nna nwɔtwe ansa na mawu, enti menkɔ afuo hɔ bio, na mo nso monkɔ hɔ bio, na sɛ miwu a, ɛhɔ na mobɛsie me. Meatutu amena kɛseɛ wɔ hɔ, na mu na mobɛsie me. " Nna nwɔtwe no baa mu; Kwaku Ananse yɛɛ ne ho sɛ wawu. Ananse no nnipa no maa no so, na wɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔsiee no wɔ ne turo no mu. Wɔsan baa fie kɔyɛɛ ayiyɛ amanne pa. "Aso" nso dii kununa amanneɛ. Bere a na wɔreyɛ da a ɛto so nwɔtwe ayi no, "Aso" kae sɛ, "Ah, ma menkɔhwɛ me kunu turo. " "Aso" kɔɔ hɔ, turo no da hɔ paa, papa! "Aso" kae sɛ, "Ao, turo a me kunu yɛɛ no a na maniwa da so (ma me aduane) no, nnipa bi aba na wɔadidi biribiara a ɛwɔ mu. " Afei ɔsu frɛɛ no nnome, na ɔkae sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse obi a ɔwɔ honhom, mefrɛ wo afuo no mu nnɔbaeɛ a wogyaw maa me no nyinaa, nnipa abɛdi. "

Kwaku Ananse maa ne nne so too dwom sɛ:

"Gyae (complaining)! Me deɛ midi nko ara!

Gyae "Aso" tabuo a wosiesie nwira.

Gyae (complaining), me deɛ medidi nko ara!

Gyae (complaining), Aso akyiwadeɛ rehyew (the turo mu wira). Gyae (complaining), me adidi nea ɛyɛ me nko ara!

Gyae (anwiinwii), "Aso" tabu a wodua.

Gyae (anwiinwii), madi mankasa aduan nkutoo!

Ɛhɔ ara na "Aso" gyinaa hɔ, na ne ho yɛɛ no hu. Ɔkɔɔ fie kɔfrɛɛ Adanko, Hare no, sɛ ɔmmra na ɔmmɛte nsɛmfua bi a ɛwɔ ne kunu afuo hɔ. Hare no ne no fii aseɛ kɔeɛ. "Aso" bɛduruu afuo hɔ, na ɔmaa ne nne so bio na ɔsuanee sɛ:

"Na me kunu ne Kwaku, Ananse, Na ɔwuiɛ gyaa me nafuo, Na nnipa baeɛ bɛwe aduane a ɛwɔ mu no.

Kwaku honhom, Ananse, mibu nkotodwe ma wo, mesrɛ wo, kunkum wɔn ma me. "

Kwaku Ananse maa ne nne so:

"Gyae (anwiinwii), midii nea ɛyɛ me nkutoo!

Gyae "Aso" tabuo a wosiesie nwira.

Gyae (complaining), me deɛ medidi nko ara!

Gyae (anwiinwii), Aso akyiwadeɛ hyew (turo mu wira).

Gyae (complaining), me di me deɛ nko ara!

Gyae (anwiinwii), "Aso" tabu a wodua.

Gyae (complaining), me di me deɛ nko ara; gyae (complaining)!

Ɔbɔadeɛ firi bere a ɔbɔɔ nneɛma no, woahunu ɔbaa a ɔnboa ne kunu?

Gyae (complaining), midii me ara me deɛ nko ara; gyae (complaining)! "

Hare no kae sɛ, "Me tie no wie, momma yɛnkɔ fie na menka nkyerɛ mo nea ɛwɔ ho nyinaa. " Ɔne "Aso" no kɔɔ fie. Hare kae sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ, 'Momma yɛnkɔdwiri turo' bi, wokae sɛ worenkɔ; 'Momma yɛnkɔhyew (the dry weeds) ' na wokae sɛ worenkɔ; 'Momma yɛnkɔ dua' na wokae sɛ worenkɔ; enti gyae su, ɛfiri sɛ ɔredidi nankasa deɛ, ɛfiri sɛ, ɛfiri berɛ a Ɔbɔadeɛ bɔɔ no, onhuu ɔbaa a ɔmmoa ne kunu; nti gyae su. ng (ɔse), ɛfiri sɛ wadidi ne deɛ nyinaa nko ara. " Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Berɛ a obiara koraa de ne ho hyɛ adwuma biara ho na ɔbisa wo, boa no. "

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
