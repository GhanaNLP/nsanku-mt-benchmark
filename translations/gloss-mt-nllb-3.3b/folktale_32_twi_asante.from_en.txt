SƐNEA ANANSE NO NYAA TIKWAA

WƆKYERƐ SƐ Kwaku Ananse, the Ananse, ne ne yere Aso na na wɔte hɔ pɛn. Ɛda bi, na wɔakɔ afuom na wɔasan aba; ɛnkyɛ, ɔbɔfo bi baeɛ. Kwaku Ananse bisaa no nadwuma. Ɔkaa sɛ, "The only news is that your mother-in-law has laid down yesterday and has not risen. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "So, this matter is your matter. " Aso de ne nsa guu ne ti so teɛteɛɛ sɛ, pue! pue! Kwaku Ananse maa no kwan sɛ ɔnkɔ ayie no ase. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi, Kwaku Ananse kɔsrɛɛ Odwan, Odwan, Nkruma, Agyinamoa, Ɔkraman, Ɔkraman, Akokɔ, Akokɔ, ne Abirekyi, Abirekyi sɛ, "Eyi ne nea mahunu yi, me srɛ mo sɛ monka me ho nkɔ me maame-me-ase ayie no ase. " Nnipa yi nyinaa penee so. Kwaku Ananse baa ne fie, na ɔhyɛɛ akraman honam ani, na ɔde kɔla a ɛyɛ russet-brown yɛɛ ne ntama no. Dwoda a ɛbae no, ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔfrɛɛ nnipa a ɔsrɛɛ wɔn no nyinaa, na wɔn nyinaa fii adi. Wɔfaa sanku ne atuo ne palm-wine. Kwaku Ananse baa nasewfoɔ akuraa no akyi, atuo no rebɔ pum! pum! Wɔfaa asaw-apontow no na wɔkɔtenaa fie a wɔreyɛ ayi no. Ɔmaa ayarefo no nsa. Bere a owiee no, ɔde nnsa ntoboa a ɔde maa ayie ho ka, sika futuris nkaribo asia (te sɛ £48), velvet blanket, ntama abien, ntama, sika (a wɔde bedi dwuma wɔ abosonsomfo asase so), nguan ne mmɛw nsa mae. Ɛno ne ntoboa a Ananse no de kɔsiee ne maame ba. Anwummere no dwoeɛ no, wɔbae sɛ wɔrebɛma Ananse no fam a ɛyɛ asuanu ne nsano duru a ɛyɛ sika futuro (£4 13s). Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi, na obiara san dii naduan, na wɔbae sɛ wɔrebɛka akyerɛ Ananse sɛ ɔmmɛdidi. Ɔbuaa sɛ, Me, Kwaku, me maame-wɔ-baa no awu; Me nni aduane da a ɛtɔ so nwɔtwe wɔ asieeɛ dwumadie mu. Me nni aduane, nanso monya biribi mma me nnamfoɔ, a me ne wɔn bae no, nni, na momma wɔnkɔ. Enti Aso kɔɔ kwan so kɔhwehwɛɛ biribi a ɔde bɛbrɛ mmarima yi aduane. Enti wɔkɔeɛ, na Kwaku Ananse na ɔtenaa hɔ. Ɛhɔ na Ananse no te, na afei da a ɛtɔ so nnan no bae, na ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛnea ɔbɛtumi biara, ɔrentumi nnyina bio. Afei, wɔ fie a na ɔte hɔ no, adua a wɔreto no, na ɛgyina ogya no so. Kwaku Ananse hwɛɛ ne ho nyinaa sɛ ebia obi rehwɛ, afei ɔyii ne leopard-skin hat no, yii adua no bi guu mu, na ɔsan hyɛɛ no bio. Saa bere no ara na Aso baeɛ. Ɔkae sɛ, So, I have just remembered that at my fathers village, the hat-shaking festival has come (and is being held), so I am going off at once. Aso kae sɛ, Oh, dɛn nti na woanka eyi nyinaa nkyerɛ me ansa, nea ɛka ho ne sɛ, efi bere a wobaa funeral yi, woanni aduane biara; enti me srɛ wo sɛ kɔ-ɔkyena. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, You dont know what has come, thats why you talk. sɛnea woyɛ no. Aso kɔtoo nsa frɛɛ nkurɔfoɔ no sɛ wɔmmra mmɛka nkyerɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ ɔnkyɛ ne kɔ. Nkurɔfoɔ no baeɛ. Ɛnkyɛree a Kwaku Ananse huu wɔn no, na ɔgyee nakyɛdeɛ no ho kyereɛ (na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ too dwom): Seesei ara na me papa akuraa ase rekyere nkongua.

Saworowa, Wɔrekyere wɔn ho.

Saworowa, E, wɔredwiriw wɔn ho, oh, wɔredwiriw wɔn ho.

Saworowa. "

Afei (eyi nyinaa nti) na nnua no rehyew ne ti yie. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "I shall never agree (to stay), I shall go. " Ananse no fii aseɛ kɔɔ kwan no so, na nkurɔfoɔ no dii nakyi. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dane kɔ, ɛfiri sɛ:

Ɛnkane yi ara na me papa kuro no mu rekyinkyin.

Saworowa, Wɔrekyere wɔn ho.

E, wɔwosow wɔn ti, oh, wɔwosow wɔn ti.

Saworowa. "

Ɛbɛduruu saa berɛ yi, na saa nneɛma yi (adum no) rehyew no, na ama wahu nea ɔhyew no kyerɛ ankasa. Ɛmfa ho sɛnea ɔbɔɔ mmɔden no, na ontumi nnyina ano bio. Ɔtoo bankye ne adum no gui. Aso ne nkurɔfoɔ no bɔɔ wɔn ho ban. Kwaku Ananse huruw kɔɔ kwan no mu, sora! (ɛno ne nnyigyei a ɛfiri parting-grass no mu. Ɔkae sɛ, "Kwan, gye me, na da bi a mɛba me ho mu no, mɛda wo ase. " Kwan kaa sɛ, "Mate. " Kwan no hwɛɛ no na wɔmaa no aduru. Ɛno nti na wobɛhu Kwaku Ananse a ɔwɔ tikwaa sukyi; ɛfiri ahantan a ɔde maa ne ho wɔ ne maame-me-aseɛ ayie ase no.

Me kyerɛwtohɔ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na fa a aka no nso kamfo me.
