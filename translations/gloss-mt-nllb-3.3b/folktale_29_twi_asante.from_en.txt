Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA OBI NYAA YERE (NANSO NEA WƆAGYE NO NKWA) NE NE NKWA WƆ AKWAN A WƆFA SO KYERƐKYERƐ NSƐNKYERƐNNE AHODOƆ MU

ƐWƆ BERE BI A NA mmarima MMIƐNSA BI TE HƆ A NA WƆPƐ adwuma. Wɔkɔɔ Tall-grass-country hɔ kɔtɔɔ nkyekye ntoma ne nneɛma foforɔ. (Wɔ wɔn san ba Asante no,) wɔma wɔn wofase ba no soaa nneɛma no bi, na wɔkɔɔ so tɔn nneɛma. Wɔbaa kuro kɛseɛ bi mu. Ɛwɔ kuro no mu na na Queen Mother ba bi wɔ hɔ a na ɔpow sɛ ɔbɛma obiara a ɔbɛma no awareɛ. Afei na ahɔhoɔ yi kɔɔ hɔ (ne fie), nanso na Ɔhemmaa no abofra no akɔ "beat" ahohoro no. Ɔhemmaa no deɛ, ɔtɔɔ ntoma mmienu a ɔde sie maa ne babaa no. Bere a ne abofra no san bae no, ɔhunuu mmarima mmiɛnsa no. Ɔka kyerɛɛ naberewa no sɛ, "Ɛnnɛ da mahu nea mepɛ no, kunkum odwan bi na memfa nkɔma wɔn. " Bere a wɔnoaa aduane no wiei no, abaayewa no kɔfaa esɔ, asaawa, bese ne nkateɛ dua bi kɔtoo aduane no so kɔmaa mmarima no. Na ɔkaa sɛ, " Ɔno a ɔbɛtumi akyerɛkyerɛ wɔn aseɛ no, ɔno na mɛware no. " Na wɔne aduane no kɔɔ so. Na mmarima no yii ano, na wɔkaa sɛ, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ Ɔhemmaa Maame no abofra na ɔde saa aduane yi baeɛ a, anka ɛsɛ sɛ yɛnya nsɛmfua bi bisa no. " Na mmarima no mu wofase ba no kae sɛ, "Sɛ me nsuro wo a, anka metumi aka saa nneɛma no aseɛ akyerɛ wo. " Wɔkae sɛ, "Kyerɛkyerɛ mu. " Na abofra no buae sɛ, "Saa abaayewa no ka sɛ wama nadwene ayɛ fitaa sɛ asaawa, na sɛ ɔtumi gye yɛn a yɛaba yi mu biara a, ɛnneɛ, ɛnsɛ sɛ ɔware anaa? Saa esɔ no aseɛ ne sɛ, da bi bɛba a ɔbɛyera; bese no nso kyerɛ sɛ da bi bɛba a ɔbɛma no (ne kunu) adidi kola (i. e. ayɛ awerɛhow). Ne nkateɛ no kyerɛ sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ wogu nkateɛ ama no, na afe biara, sɛ ɔkɔhwɛ nhini no a, wanya bi atwe. " Enti, Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no waree ɔbarima no wɔfase. Bere a ɔwaree no, Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no bisaa no sɛ, "Wo din de sɛn? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me din ne Kwaku Susufoɔ," ɛno ne Kwaku-a-gye-ɔhaw-nsusuw-mu. Na abaayewa no buae sɛ, "Me nso, wɔfrɛ me Akua-who-takes-ɔhaw-to-think. " Ɔde afahyɛ a ɛfata waree no. Ade kyee anɔpa no, wɔfafoɔ baasa no fii aseɛ kɔɔ wɔn kuro mu. Kwaku-who-takes-ɔhaw-to-think no kɔtenaa ne yere kuro no mu. Ɔtenaa hɔ afe baako, na sɛ ɔsɔre anɔpa a, na ɔne ne nnamfofoɔ no tena ase di nte. Afei, da bi a na ɔrebɔ saa no, ɔbɔɔ marbuls mmienu wɔ tuo toɔ baako so, nanso nnipa a wɔne no redi agorɔ no kae sɛ wɔremma no, na mpo wɔpɛ sɛ wɔboro no. Ɔtoo sɛnea bi wɔ nakyi, na ɔhunuu abofra bi a ogyina hɔ, ɔfaa ɔboɔ bi de maa abofra no sɛ ɔmfa nkɔma ne yere. Afei berɛ a ne yere gyee ɔboɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Me kunu no pɛ sɛ ɔsan kɔ ne kuro no mu, ɛfiri sɛ ɔka sɛ, sɛ anomaa tena fam kyɛ dodo a, ɛnya ɔboɔ. " Na Ɔhemmaa no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ ɔkɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ wo ne no kɔ. " Ɛyɛ nokware sɛ ɔne no kɔeɛ. Na wɔhyiaa Kwaku-who-gye-ɔhaw-nsusuw-mu-nananom. Afei, bere bi akyi yi, wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Kwaku-a-ɔhaw-dwene-dwene, nya hama a etwa to no bi sɛ yɛnkɔ nwura hɔ nkɔhyehyɛ fidie wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ yɛbɛkyere mborɔnoma a yɛbɛtumi de ahɔhoɔgyeɛ ama wo yere a ɔyɛ ɔhɔhoɔ no. Enti wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ, nanso na saa mpanimfoɔ yi apene so sɛ wɔbɛkum no (nokware). Wɔn ne Kwaku-a-ɔhaw-adwene no kɔɔ so; wɔduruu beaeɛ a ɛyɛ ankonam wɔ ɔkwan no so; wɔkyekyeree no, na wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Yɛbɛkum wo. Afei ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, Berɛ a mekɔɔ me yere akuraa ase no, mehunuu sika bebree, na sɛ moamma kwan mma menkyerɛ mo baabi a ɛwɔ, na mewu a, morenhu. Wɔbisaa no sɛ, Ɛhe na sika no wɔ? Obuae sɛ, Ɛsɛ sɛ mo mekɔ me yere nkyɛn na mekyerɛ no sɛ, matɔ nam, na ɛno deɛ, adwetɔfoɔ ka sɛ ne boɔ yɛ du peredwan (£80), na ɛsɛ sɛ ɔhwehwɛ kotokuo a wɔde ntoma nketewa ayɛ no mu; sɛ ɔkɔhwɛ hɔ na wannya a, ɔmfa nhwɛ Ɔkrampan kotoku no mu, sɛ ɔnhunu hɔ a, ɔmfa nhwɛ abɔsoɔ a nsekan wɔ mu no mu. Enti wɔbaa sɛ wɔrebɛma ne yere nkrasɛm no. Na ɔno nso kaa sɛ, Saa nneɛma no wɔ kuro no mu panin no fie, momma yɛnkɔfa. Ɔne wɔn kɔɔ ɔpanyin no fie, na ɔbaa no bɔɔ amaneɛbɔ kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no, na ɔkyerɛkyerɛɛ mu sɛ, Saa hama kotokuo a wɔde nguan nhoma ayɛ no kyerɛ sɛ wɔakyekyere no, Ɔkrampan no kyerɛ sɛ apɛtɛ no rebɔ mmɔden sɛ wɔbɛwe ne honam, abɔsoɔ a ɛkura nsekan no kyerɛ sɛ wɔrebɛtwa ne menem. Ntɛm ara na ɔpanyin no bɔɔ gong-gong sɛ wɔnkɔ so wɔ hɔ nnye Kwaku-wɔn-a-gye-ɔhaw-to-adwene. Na wɔne no san bae. Ɔmaa wɔn kunkum mpanimfoɔ mmiɛnsa no. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, Ɛnam nsɛnkyerɛnne ahodoɔ akwan so na obi nyaa yere na ɔsan nso gyee ne nkwa. Eyi, me abasɛm a aka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
