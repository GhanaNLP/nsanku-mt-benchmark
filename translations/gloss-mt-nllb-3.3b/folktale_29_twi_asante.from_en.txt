Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA AKWAN A WƆFA SO KYERƐ NSƐNKYERƐNNE ASE NO YƐƐ SƐNEA EBINOM NYAA WƆN NKWA (NA WƆAGYE WƆN NKWA NSO)

ƐDII BERE BI A NA MMABUN BAAKO MMIƐNSA TE HƆ NA WƆHWEHWƐ ADWUMA. Wɔkɔɔ Tall-grass-country kɔtɔɔ dyeing cloth ne nneɛma foforɔ. (Wɔ wɔn sanba a wɔbaa Asante) maa wɔn cousin no de nneɛma no bi kɔmaa wɔn, na wɔkɔɔ so tɔn. Wɔbaa kuro kɛseɛ bi mu. Na kuro no mu yɛ Queen Mother babea bi a ɔpow sɛ ɔbɛware obiara. Afei na ahɔhoɔ yi (adwadifoɔ) akɔ hɔ (ne fie), nanso na ɔhemmaa ba no akɔ "beat" the wash. Ɔhemmaa no (nneɛma nyinaa akyi) tɔɔ ntama mmienu na ɔde sii hɔ maa ne babea no. Bere a ne ba no san bae no, ɔhunuu mmarima mmiɛnsa no. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame panyin no sɛ, "Today mahunu nea mepɛ no, nti kum oguan na memfa mma wɔn. " Bere a wɔwiee aduane no, ababaawa no kɔfaa esum dua, asaawa, ɛmo ne ɛmo nnua, na ɔde guu aduane no so kɔmaa mmarima no. Na ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, " Ɔno a ɔbɛtumi akyerɛkyerɛ ne nkyerɛaseɛ mu no, ɔno na meware no. " Na wɔne aduane no kɔe. Mmarima no yii no adi, na wɔkae sɛ, " Sɛ ɛnyɛ Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba na ɔde aduane yi aba a, anka ɛsɛ sɛ yɛnya nsɛm bi bisa no. " Mmarima no wɔfase no kae sɛ, " Sɛ me nsuro wo a, anka metumi aka saa nneɛma no nkyerɛaseɛ akyerɛ wo. " Wɔkae sɛ, " Kyerɛkyerɛ mu. " Mmofra no buae sɛ, " Ababaawa no ka sɛ, wama nadwene ayɛ fitaa sɛ asaawa, na sɛ ɔtumi nya yɛn a yɛaba yi mu baako a, ɛnneɛ, ɛnsɛ sɛ ɔware anaa? Ɔsa no nkyerɛaseɛ ne sɛ, da bi bɛba a ɔbɛyera; bese no nso kyerɛ sɛ da bi bɛba a ɔbɛma no (ne kunu) akye bese (i. e. ɔnya awerɛhoɔ). Nsateaa no kyerɛ sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ wodua nsateaa ma no, na afe biara, bere a ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwɛ ntini no, ɔbɛnya bi a ɔbɛtwe. " Nti, Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no waree mmarima no wɔfase. Bere a ɔwaree no, Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no bisaa no sɛ, "Wo din de dɛn? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me din ne Kwaku Susufoɔ," kyerɛ sɛ, Kwaku a ɔyɛ ɔhaw-dwene. Ababaawa no buae sɛ, "Me nso wɔfrɛ me Akua-who-takes-to-think. " Ɔwaree no wɔ afahyɛ a ɛfata mu. Anɔpa a edi hɔ no, wɔn nuabarimanom baasa no fii ase kɔɔ wɔn kurom. Kwaku-who-takes-to-think da so ara wɔ ne yere akuraa ase. Ɔtraa hɔ afe, na sɛ ɔsɔre (ɔkyena) a, ɔne ne nnamfonom bɛtra ase adi tennis. Ɛnneɛ, da bi a na ɔreyɛ agorɔ saa no, ɔbɔɔ marbles mmienu wɔ bɔɔlo baako mu, nanso nnipa a na wɔne no redi agorɔ no, wɔkae sɛ wɔremfa mma no, na wɔpɛ mpo sɛ wɔboro no. Ɔtoo nakyi, na ɔhunuu abofra bi a ɔregyina hɔ, ɔfaa ɔboɔ bi maa abofra no sɛ ɔmfa nkɔma ne yere. Afei berɛ a ne yere gyee ɔboɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, Me kunu pɛ sɛ ɔsan kɔ nakuraa, ɛfiri sɛ ɔka sɛ, sɛ anomaa tena asase so kyɛ a, ɔgye ɔboɔ. Na Queen no kaa sɛ, Sɛ ɔkɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ wo ne no kɔ. Akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho sɛ ɔne no kɔeɛ. Na wɔhyiaa Kwaku-a ɔgye ɔhaw-dwen-ho-nsɛm-nananom. Afei, berɛ bi akyi yi, wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, " Kwaku-a ɔyɛ ɔhaw-dwene-mu, fa nhama bi a yɛbɛtumi akɔ kwaeɛ no mu akɔto afiri mu akyere akokɔ mma a yɛbɛtumi de ahɔhoyɛ ama wo yere, a ɔyɛ ɔhɔhoɔ. " Enti wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ, nanso na mpanimfoɔ yi (really) apene so sɛ wɔbɛkum no. Wɔn ne Kwaku-a ɔyɛ ɔhaw-dwen-mu-kɔɔ so; wɔduruu (an isolated place) wɔ kwan no so; wɔkyekyeree no na wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Yɛbɛkum wo. Afei ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, Bere a me kɔɔ me yere akuraa ase no, mehuu sika pii, na sɛ moamma me ankyerɛ mo baabi a ɛwɔ, na me wu a, morenhu. Wɔbisaa no sɛ, Sika no da he? Obuae sɛ, Ɛsɛ sɛ kɔ me yere nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛ no sɛ, me atɔ nam, na ɛno (vendor no) kyerɛ sɛ ne boɔ yɛ du peredwan (£80), na ɛsɛ sɛ ɔhwehwɛ bag a wɔde nguan nhoma nketenkete ayɛ no mu; sɛ ɔhwɛ hɔ na ɔnhunu a, ɔmfa nhwɛ Cape Coast bag no mu, sɛ ɔnhunu wɔ hɔ a, ɔmfa nhwɛ belt a nkatanim wɔ mu no mu. Enti wɔbaa bɛkaa nsɛm no kyerɛɛ ne yere. Na ɔno nso kae sɛ, Nneɛma no wɔ akuraa no panyin no fie, enti yɛnkɔfa. Ɔne wɔn kɔɔ ɔmanpanyin no fie; na ɔbaa no ka kyerɛɛ ɔmanpanyin no asɛm no, kyerɛkyerɛɛ mu sɛ, Bag of ropes made of sheep-skin means that they have bound him; the rope means that the rats are trying to eat his flesh; the belt holding the rope means they are about to cut his throat. Ɛhɔ ara na ɔpanyin no bɔɔ gong-gong sɛ wɔnkɔ hɔ nkɔgye Kwaku a ɔyɛ ɔhaw-dwene. Na wɔne no san bae. Ɔmaa wɔkunkum mpanimfoɔ mmiɛnsa no. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, By ways of interpreting signs some one got a wife and (also) saved his life. Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
