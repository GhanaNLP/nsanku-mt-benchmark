Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ABOSOM, A ƐYƐ ANOM NKETEWA NO, BAA WƆ WIASE

ƐDII BERE BI A ƆBEA BI WƆ HƆ; Ɔwoo mmɔfra du-baako, na da biara a ɔbɛsɔre ayɛ aduane no, mmɔfra no di ne nyinaa. Wɔn maame annya bi nni. Afei ɔdwenee ho pii, na ɔkɔɔ afuom hɔ kɔbɔɔ silk-cotton dua no amanneɛ sɛ, "Mɛsoma me mma du-baako aba wo ase ha sɛ wɔrebɛtete asabuntum; na sɛ wɔba a, twa wo nkorabata du-baako na kum me mma yi. " Silk-cotton dua no kae sɛ, "Mate, na mɛyɛ ama wo. " Ɛna no fii ase kɔɔ fie, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne mma no sɛ, "Mo mus t kɔ afuom kɛseɛ no mu, wɔ Silk-cotton dua no ase; cycars wɔ hɔ, kɔfa wɔn na bra. " Mmofra no fii aseɛ. Wɔkɔɔ Silk-cotton dua no ho. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Number One, nnyina hɔ; Number Two, nnyina hɔ; Number Three, nnyina hɔ; Number Four, nnyina hɔ; Number Five, nnyina hɔ; Number Six, nnyina hɔ; Number Seven, nnyina hɔ; Number Eight, nnyina hɔ; Number Nine, nnyina hɔ; Number Ten, nnyina hɔ; Na me, Number Eleven, megyina hɔ. " Afei Number Eleven kasa kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Munnim r sole eason dɛn nti na maame kae sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ yɛkɔfa sakre? Ne nuanom no kae sɛ, "Dabi. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Waka akyerɛ Silk-cotton dua yi sɛ, sɛ yɛkɔ hɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ ɛtwa nkorabata na ɛbɔ yɛn; nti mo nyinaa twa nnua, na momfa nto Silk-cotton dua yi so. " Wɔtwaa nnua no, na wɔde too Silk-cotton dua no so, pim! kyerɛw! pim! kyerɛw! (ɛno ne nnyigyei a wɔyɛɛ no. Silk-cotton dua no susuwii sɛ mmofra no aba. Ɔtwee ne nkorabata du-baako no, na ɔma ɛhwee fam. Du-baako ketewa no kae sɛ, "Woahunu, sɛ yɛkɔ hɔ a, anka silk-cotton dua no bɛkum yɛn. " Wɔfaa nnuaba no de kɔmaa wɔn maame. Ɔnoaa no, oh! prɛko pɛ na mmofra no dii (ne nyinaa). Wɔn maame kae sɛ, "Ah! Na asɛm yi deɛ, mentumi nnyina so, enti mɛfa mmofra yi na mede ama Ɔsoro Nyame no. " Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi, ɔkɔkaa asɛm no nyinaa kyerɛɛ Ɔsoro Nyame no sɛ, "Mmofra bi a woawo wɔn no, bere a mepɛ sɛ medidi no, mentumi nnya biribi nni (efiri sɛ wɔdidi ntɛm ara na ɛdɔɔso); ɔkɔm rewu me; enti mesrɛ wo, ma mmofra no nnya brough t and killed, so that I may get something to eat. " The Sky-god said, "Is that really the case? " Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "I am talking with a head, the inside of which is white. " Sky-god picked angels, and they went and dug a hole, in which they placed (broken) bottles; ɔkɔɔ faa ɔwɔ ne ɔsebɔ de guu amena no mu, na ɔkataa so. Na seseiara ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔfrɛɛ mmofra no. Ɛnkyɛe koraa na woduu baabi a amena no da no, na Dumien ka sɛ, "Number One, nnyina hɔ; Number Two, nnyina hɔ; Number Three, nnyina hɔ; Number Four, nnyina hɔ; Number Five, nnyina hɔ; Number Six, nnyina hɔ; Number Seven, nnyina hɔ; Number Eight, nnyina hɔ; Number Nine, nnyina hɔ; Number Ten, nnyina hɔ; Na me nso, nnipa kakraa bi du-baako, megyina hɔ. Ɛsɛ sɛ wo twa mu. S yi, ɛnsɛ sɛ wotwa fa hɔ. " Ne nuanom no kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti, bere a ɛkwan a ɛtrɛw da hɔ no, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛfa sare no so? " Afei, bere a wɔrekɔ no, na wɔn nyinaa kura clubs. Number Eleven kae sɛ, "Monto nnua yi mu biako gu kwan yi so. " Wɔtoo no fam hɔ, na ɛhwee aseɛ (wɔ amena no mu), na ɛhɔre (ɛno ne ne hwe ase nnyigyei). Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Ɛhɔ na wo wɔ, wuhu, anka sɛ yɛtwa hɔ a, anka yɛn nyinaa bɛwuwu. " Enti wɔfaa kwan bi so na wɔfiri hɔ kɔɔ Sky-god no nkyɛn. Sky-god no maa wotuu abura, na wɔde nkongua guu so, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mmofra no bɛba abɛtena so a, wɔbɛkɔ akɔtɔ abura no mu. Mmofra no duu Sky-god no anim. Sky-god no kaa sɛ, "Beds are set there, you may go and be seated on them. " Ɛnna Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Hwan na yɛyɛ, sɛ yɛbɛtumi atena nkongua a ɛyɛ fɛ paa so; enti, Sire, yɛbɛtena nkyɛn ha. " Ɛhɔ na Sky-god no hwɛɛ mmofra no, ɔkae sɛ, "I will send these children to Death's village. " Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi, ɔfrɛɛ mmofra no, ɔkae sɛ, " Ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ Owuo (a ɔte aseɛ) nkyɛn, na wokɔgye sika kɔkɔɔ poma, sika kɔkɔɔ chewing-stick, sika kɔkɔɔ snuff-box, sika kɔkɔɔ whet-stone, ne sika kɔkɔɔ fly-switch. " Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Wo na woyɛ yɛn wura, baabiara a wobɛsoma yɛn no, yɛbɛkɔ. " Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Monkɔ. " Mmofra no fii akwantu no ase kɔɔ Owuo akuraa ase. Wɔduruu hɔ; Owuo kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti, berɛ a ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara ba ha da no, moaba ha anaa? " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Na yɛnam akwantu so na yɛbaa ha (by chance). " Owuo kaa sɛ, "Oh, all right then. " Afei na Owuo wɔ mma du; ne ho a ɔde kaa ho no, ɛyɛɛ du-baako. Bere a nneɛma fii ase yerae (i. e. wɔ sum mu), Owuo kyekyɛɛ mmofra no mu mmiako mmiako na ɔde biako maa ne mma no mu biara, bere a ɔno ankasa ne Dubiako kɔɔ ahomegyebea. Bere a ɛyɛɛ sum no, afei Owuo hyerɛn ne se (kosi sɛ ɛhyerɛn) kɔkɔɔ, sɛ ɔne wɔn bɛkyere Dubiako. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Owuo, meda so ara nni nna. " Owuo kaa sɛ, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda? " Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wobɛma me sika a mede bɛtwa sigaret kakra a, anka ebia mɛda. " Na Owuo de maa no. Ɛnkyɛreeɛ, Owuo san bɔɔ nase sɛ ɔne wɔn bɛkɔ akɔfa Number Eleven. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Owuo, mennya nnaeɛ. " Owuo kaa sɛ, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda? " Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wode sika kɔkɔɔ snuff-box bɛba a, ebia mɛda. " Na Owuo no de no brɛɛ no. Bio nso, na ɛrenkyɛ na Owuo no agye Number Eleven. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Minnda so ara. " Owuo kaa sɛ, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda? " Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa sika kɔkɔɔ poma a wode bɛwe adeɛ ama me, sɛ mɛwe kakra a, ɛnneɛ mɛtumi ada. " Owuo no de maa no. Bere tiaa bi twaam, na na Owuo no rebɛfa no. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Anananom, mennya nnada. " Owuo kaa sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ, berɛ bɛn na wobɛda? " Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Anananom, sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa sika kɔkɔɔ abotan ama me a, anka mɛtumi ada. " Na Owuo no kɔeɛ na ɔde bae. Bio nso, ɛnkyɛreeɛ, Owuo no sɔre bio. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "O, Nana, mekaeɛ sɛ me da so ara nni nna. " Owuo kaa sɛ, "Na ɛbɛyɛ dɛn da a wobɛda? " Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa basket a tomato ayɛ mu ma na woakɔhwie nsuo agu mu, na woanoa aduane bi ama me adi a, ɛnneɛ mɛtumi ada. " Owuo maa sanku no so na ɔkɔɔ asubɔnten no ho. Bere a ɔhwiee nsuo no guu mu no, sanku a ɛwɔ sanku no mu no maa nsuo no faa mu. Afei Number Eleven ka kyerɛɛ ne nuanom no sɛ, "Monsɔre na monnwane nkɔ. " Na wɔsɔree na wɔdwanee; na Number Eleven nso kɔtwitwaa borɔdɔma dua, de sii baabi a ne nuanom no da, na ɔfaa ntama kataa so. Afei na Death wɔ asubɔnten no ho, ɔrehwie nsuo no gu mu. Afei Ɔbarima Wu frɛɛ Ɔbea Wu sɛ, "Ho wɔ hɔ, Owuo. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Adwo. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn na woreyɛ yi? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Alas, ɛnyɛ abofra ketewa bi na manya no; bere a merebɛkyere no, ɔka sɛ, Minnda so ara nni nna mu; ɔde me nneɛma nyinaa akɔ, na afei ɔka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mefa scissors na metwiri nsuo. " Ɔbarima Wu kaa sɛ, Ah, woyɛ abofra ketewa; afei deɛ, sɛ wotwitwa nhaban ne li Na ɛbɛyɛ yie sɛ wobɛhwie nkukuo no mu, na afei woahwie nsuo agu mu anaa? Ɔbaa a wawuo no kaa sɛ, "Oh, ɛyɛ nokware paa. " Ɔtwee nhaban, de guu mu, na ɔhwiee nsuo no guu mu na ɔkɔeɛ. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Death, woaba dedaw anaa? Boil aduane no. " Death na ɔyɛɛ aduane no, ɔhyehyɛɛ ne kanea sɛ ɔbɛkum Number Eleven nuanom no na wayɛ wɔn aduane. Ɛduruu sɛ ɔkɔeɛ no, ɔnhwehwɛɛ mu yie, na ɔno ara na okum ne mma du no nyinaa. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, adeɛ kye paa, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, na Owuo no asɔre, na ɔte hɔ wɔ ogya ho. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Nanabea, ɔyareɛ bi te wo nufu so. " Death kaa sɛ, "Fa fly-switch a ɛda hɔ no, na kum no ma me. " Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Oh me Nyame! me; wo nsunsuansoɔ nipa bi, sɛ ɔyareɛ bi si wo so, na sika kɔkɔɔ fly-switch da hɔ (wo de saa tete adeɛ yi bedi dwuma), ma memfa mma no na memfa mma no. " Death kaa sɛ, "Kɔfa no firi r oom. " Number Eleven kɔeɛ na ɔde baeɛ. Ɔhyɛɛ da yii nwansena no firii hɔ, wankum no. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Oh, nnɛ, baabi a saa tsetse nwansena yi bɛhome no, ɛhɔ na me ne no bɛhome. " Afei Number Eleven kɔɔ ɔdan no mu na ɔfaa ne bag a sika no ne nneɛma nyinaa da mu no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Anananom Owuo, biribiara nnɔɔso gye sɛ menya asramma no, de ahyɛ bag yi mu, na mede abɛma wo. " Number Eleven fii aseɛ sɛ, ɔyareɛ! ɔyareɛ! ɔyareɛ! Ɔduruu kuro no ano; ɔkaa sɛ, "O, ɛhɔ, Nanabaa wuo, fa me bɔne kyɛ me, nanso sɛ ɛnyɛ ɔbaa pa na woyɛ a, anka metumi ayi wo nneɛma nyinaa afiri wo nsam? Anka me nuanom a me ne wɔn bae no bɛtumi anya kwan adwane? Na anka mɛtumi ama wo nso akum wo mma du no nyinaa? Na me deɛ, merekɔ. " Owuo kaa sɛ, "Wo a woyɛ abofra a ɛte sɛɛ yi, baabiara a wobɛhome no, ɛhɔ na mɛhome. " Number Eleven huruw fii hɔ, yaw! yaw! yaw! na Owuo nso dii nakyi. Bere a Number Eleven rekɔ no, ɔtoo ne nuanom no so, na wɔte kwan no so. Wɔreyɛ nnomaa afiri. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Wɔnnya nkɔeɛ anaa? Owuo no reba, enti momma yɛnhwehwɛ ɔkwan bi a yɛbɛfa so adwane. " Afei na Owuo no kɔɔ wɔn so. Number Eleven faa aduru no guu ne nuanom no so, na wɔkɔɔ silk-cotton dua no atifi. Na Owuo no gyina silk-cotton dua no ase. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Hwɛ, mehuu mmofra yi, na wɔkɔɔ he? " Na Number Eleven te soro, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuanom sɛ, "Merebɛma no nsuo. " Ne nuanom no kaa sɛ, "Yiw, ɔrehwehwɛ yɛn sɛ ɔbɛkyere yɛn, na yɛadwane abɛtena ha, na afei woka sɛ, Merebɛma no nsuo. " Number Eleven ampene so, na ɔhyɛɛ Death nsuo. Owuo kaa sɛ, "Ah, wo ni, nnɛ woahunu ɔhaw. " Owuo kaa sɛ, "Wo, abofra, a wote hɔ no, Kyere-he-ne, Kyere-he-ne. Ɛhɔ ara na mmofra no mu biako hwee ase. Kyere-he-ne, a ɛtɔ so mmienu no hwee ase. Afei na Number Eleven pɛ na aka. Owuo kaa sɛ, Abofra, kyere-he-ne, na Number Eleven tui kɔtoo fam, kirim! Na owuo nso kɔɔ silk-cotton dua no so. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Wo, ɔbaa kɛseɛ, wo nso, 'kyere-he-ne'. " Na owuo nso baa fam, tum! na wawu. Number Eleven kɔfaa aduru bi, na ɔbobɔɔ no wɔ ne nsaman no ntam, na ɔhwie guu ne nuanom no so, na wɔsɔree. Number Eleven na ɔrebɛtow aduru no agu, berɛ a emu bi bɛtoo Death so, na Death wui. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Woakum me, na woasan nso akanyan me; ɛnnɛ wo ne me bɛyɛ asɛm. " Ɛneɛ wɔn nyinaa hyɛɛ aseɛ dwanee prɛko pɛ, kiri! Kiri! Kiri! Seesei na Owuo no di wɔn akyi. Bere a wɔrekɔ no, na asubɔnten kɛseɛ bi da nsuo mu. Bere a Number Eleven ne ne nuanom bɛduruu hɔ no, na wɔnim sɛnea wɔsi nsuo, na wɔsi nsuo no mu. Number Eleven nko ara na ɔnnim sɛnea wɔsi nsuo. Mmofra no gyinaa ɔfã foforo; wɔteɛteɛ, na wɔteɛteɛ, na wɔteɛteɛ; wɔn ano yɛɛ wɔn fĩ. Number Eleven deɛ, ɔbɛyɛɛ ɔboɔ. Owuo no bɛduruu asubɔnten no ho. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, mmofra yi, mo nnyina hɔ, momma memfa ɔboɔ bi nto wɔn ano a ɛyɛ huam. " Owuo no, bere a ɔhwɛɛ fam no, ɔhunuu sɛ ɔboɔ bi da hɔ; ɔboaa no na ɔtoo no; ɔboɔ no nam kwan so no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Winds, fa me na fa me si ɔfã foforɔ. " Ɛhyerɛn wɔ ɔfã foforɔ no so. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Me ni. " Owuo no kaa sɛ, "Ah, abofra yi! Me nni asɛm foforo biara a mɛka akyerɛ wo, nanso nea ɛsɛ sɛ meka kyerɛ wo ne sɛ: kɔtena fie, na sesa wo ho kɔyɛ anyame nketewa no mu biako, na sɛ obi biara a mepɛ sɛ mefa no ba baabi a wowɔ a, wobɛka akyerɛ me; sɛ mepɛ a, mɛgya no na mayi no ama wo; nanso nea mepɛ (wɔ exchange) no, ɛsɛ sɛ wogye ma me. " (Ɛno ne afɔrebɔ.) Saa ara na Abosom (lesser -anyame) baa wiase. Wɔyɛ mmofra du-baako asefo.

Me kyerɛwtohɔ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na fa a aka no nso kamfo me.
