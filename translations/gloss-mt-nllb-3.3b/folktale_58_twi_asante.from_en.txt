Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

WƆ KWAN BIARA SO, BAABI A WOHU SƐ SAA OSEBO, DABOƆ NO, AKYERƐW, ƐHƆ ADANKO, HARE NO, ATWAM (NO DEDAW)

WƆKYERƐ SƐ, na ɔsebo nini bi ne ɔsebɔ bedeɛ bi, ne adanko nini bi ne adanko bedeɛ bi, wɔtenaa akuraa ketewa bi so, na ɛkɔm bɛbaa. Ɛneɛ na ɔsebɔ bedeɛ no kyerɛɛ sɛ ɔrekɔ asuwa. Bere a na ɔrekɔ no, na ɔne adanko bedeɛ a ɔredwiri aburoo no hyia. Ɔbisaa ɔsebɔ bedeɛ no sɛ, "Ah, maame, ɛhe na woanya saa adeɛ yi? " Na adanko bedeɛ no buae sɛ, "Ɛwɔ kwan a ɛtrɛw wɔ akyi ha, na na me kunu no di akyi kɔ hwehwɛ wuram bayere, na ɔkɔgyee saa aburoo yi, enti fa yi kɔnoa na wotow. " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Meda wo ase, Aku. " Ɛnna ɔsebɔ bedeɛ no yii no firii fie hɔ, kɔtoo ogya no so. Na ne kunu no baeɛ, na ɔbisaa no (he had he got the aburoo), na ɔkaa sɛ, The adanko bedeɛ gave it to me, na ɔkaa sɛ kwan a ɛtrɛw da nakyi hɔ, na ɛno na ne kunu no faa so, na ɔde aburoo bi baeɛ. The ɔsebo nini said, Then to-ɔkyena I shall go there very, very early in the anɔpa. So he set out. Bere a ɔkɔɔ no, na aburoo wɔ bebrebee. Afei ɔfaa ne sekkan no bi twitwaa bi, na ɔyɛɛ aburoo a wɔde ayɛ kɛntɛn, boaboaa ano. Afei na Adanko a ɔyɛ adanko nini no wɔ ne fie, na ɔhunuu sɛ ɔsebo nini no atwam akɔ afuo hɔ. Ɛhɔ ara na ɔno nso fii aseɛ. Ɔduruu kuro no awieeɛ no, na ɔdane Ɔtwee. Fa a ɛwɔ hɔ no, na ɛyɛ fɔm, nanso na fa no nnya nyɛɛ fɔm. Na ɔkɔɔ so kɔɔ akyiri, na ɔbɛyɛɛ Oyuo (antelope). Fa a na ɛyɛ fɔm no, na ne fa no bi nnya nsɛe. Na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔbɛyɛɛ Bongo. Fa a na ɛyɛ fɔm no, na ne fa no bi nnya nsɛe. Na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔbɛyɛɛ Ɛkoɔ. Na ne nyinaa yɛ foforo. Afei na Daboɔ no reba a kɛntɛn ne aburoo no wɔ ne etire so. Ɔbɛhyiaa Ɛkoɔ no. Daboɔ no kaa ne ho sɛ, A ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bɛba, nanso ɛnnya mmaa mu, nanso saa mmoadoma yi rewu. Na ɛfa ho kɔduruu Bongo ho, na ɛfa ho kɔduruu Ɔyuo ho, na ɛfa ho kɔduruu Ɔtwee ho. Afei ɔkae sɛ, Aane, saa mmoadoma yi dɔɔso dodo, enti mɛkɔ akɔboaboa ano. Ɔtoo kɛntɛn ne aburoo no guu fam, na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ firi deɛ ɛdi kan no so sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔfa mmoa no. Bere a ɔkɔɔ no, na wɔn ho nni hɔ bio. Afei ɔsan bae, nanso na kɛntɛn ne aburoo no nni hɔ bio. Ɔfirii fie kɔtenaa kommyɛ mu. Na ɔsebɔ bedeɛ no bisaa no sɛ, "Ɛhe na aburoo no wɔ? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me kɔɔ hɔ, nanso mannya bi ntɔ. " Afei ɔsebɔ bedeɛ no kɔɔ asuwa no ho, na ɛhɔ na adanko bedeɛ no hyiaa no. Na ɔsebɔ bedeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Me deɛ mewɔ ka ne sɛ, saa ɛnora no, me kunu no kaa sɛ ɔkɔeɛ no, wannya aburoo biara a ɔbɛtɔ. " Na adanko bedeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Ɔdaadaa wo, ɛfiri sɛ ɛnora no kɔeɛ no, me kunu no bɔɔ no na ɔgyaee no. " Na ɔsebɔ bedeɛ no kɔɔ fie. Ɔbɔɔ ne kunu no amanneɛ sɛ, "Eye! Eye! wokae sɛ wokɔɔ na woannye aburoo biara, bere a na ɛyɛ saa Hare no na ɔbɔɔ wo yii wo nsa. Daboɔ no kae sɛ, Hwan ne Hare no? Ɛnnɛ-ɔkyena mɛkɔ ne no ko wɔ nnua so. Na adanko nini no te fie na ɔteɛ. Ɔkɔɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere sɛ, Ɛnnɛ-ɔkyena, ɔsebo nini no bɛba ha sɛ ɔne me bɛko, enti mɛkɔ akɔhyia no wɔ kwan so, ɛfiri sɛ ebia wobɛsuro. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, Hare no hyɛɛ aseɛ. Ɔduruu kuro no kuro akyi hɔ no, na ɔdane Adowa, na ɔrefew dɔte fitaa, ɛbidi, ne mogya. Ɔkɔɔ so wɔ anim, na ɔbɛdane Bush-buck, na ɔno nso reyi ne yam wɔ kwan koro no ara so. Ɔkɔɔ so wɔ anim, na ɔbɛdane Ɛkoɔ. Daboɔ no reba fam. Adowa no kaa sɛ, Agya Ɔsebɔ, boa me, me rewu. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, Ɛdɛn na ɛyɛ asɛm? Adowa no kaa sɛ, Me ne Hare no kɔsiesiee mansotwe bi wɔ aburoo ho, na ɔbɔɔ me, na me rewu. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, Ma me nkɔ. Ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔbɛhyiaa Bush-buck no. Bush-buck no kae sɛ, "Daboɔ, boa me, ɛfiri sɛ saa Hare no nyɛ nea wo ne no bɛdi aburoo ho manso. " Daboɔ no kae sɛ, "Ɛdɛn na ɛyɛ asɛm? " Bush-buck no kae sɛ, "Me kɔeɛ na ɔbɔɔ me, ɛno nti na me rewu yi. " Daboɔ no kae sɛ, "Ma me nkɔ so. " Ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔbaa Ɛkoɔ no ho. Ɛkoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Agya Osebo, boa me, me rewu. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ asɛm bɛn? " Ɛkoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Me ne Hare kɔɔ mansotwe wɔ aburoo ho, na ɔbɔɔ me, na me rewu. " Ɛno akyi no, Daboɔ no dwenee ho tenten (na ɔkaa ne tirim sɛ), "E! Mɛdane me ho, ɛfiri sɛ ɛyɛ aburoo asɛm a ɛte saa ara na ɛsɛ sɛ me ne no si, na yɛsusuw sɛ ɔbɔɔ me..." Na Daboɔ no hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔreguan, kiri! kiri! Ntɛm ara na Hare no nso sɔreeeɛ. Ɔdii nakyi, na Daboɔ no teɛɛm kɛseɛ sɛ, "Ɔsebɔ bedeɛ, dwane o! " Daboɔ no duruu ne fie hɔ, na ɔyerae wɔ ne dan no mu, ɔfaa ne etire no bɔɔ afasuo no mu baako na ɔfaa mu, nanso Hare no de nakyi nan no nyinaa kyeree no nsɔmu. Na Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Agya Hare, ma me nkɔ. " Hare no kaa sɛ, "Meremma wo nkɔ, mɛbɔ wo. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Me srɛ wo, ntua me, nanso ma me anyame ma mennom na meka ntam sɛ, kwan biara a wobɛfa so no, me bɛba abɛpopa. " Ɛno nti, sɛ wofa kwan biara so a, na wuhu sɛ Daboɔ no abɔ ne nsa wɔ hɔ a, wunim sɛ Hare no atwam saa kwan no.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
