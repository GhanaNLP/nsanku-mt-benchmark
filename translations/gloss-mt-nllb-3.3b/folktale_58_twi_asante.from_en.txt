Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

WƆ KWAN BIARA SO, BAABI A WUHU SƐ ƆSEBƆ NO, ƆSEBƆ NO, AKƆ AKYIRI, HƆ ADANKO, ADANKO NO, ATWAM (MRE YI)

WƆKYERƐ SƐ, ɔseboɔ kɛseɛ bi ne ɔsebɔ bedeɛ bi, ne ɔseboɔ kɛseɛ bi ne ɔsebɔ bedeɛ bi, tenaa akuraa ketewa bi mu pɛn, na ɔkɔm bi bae. Ɛneɛ na ɔsebɔ bedeɛ no kyerɛ sɛ ɔrekɔ asubɔnten no ho. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa ɔsebɔ bedeɛ a ɔrebu aburow. Ɔseane no kaa sɛ, "Ah, maame, ɛhe na woanya saa adeɛ yi? " Ɔseane no buaa sɛ, "Ɛkwan kɛseɛ bi wɔ hɔ a ɛkɔ akyiri wɔ ha, na na me kunu nam so rekɔhwehwɛ kwaeɛ yam, na ɔkɔɔ kɔgyee aburoo yi, enti fa yi kɔnoa na mia. " Ɔseane no buaa sɛ, "Aseda, Aku. " Afei ɔseane no de no firii fie, kɔtoo ogya no so. Na ne kunu baeɛ, na ɔbisaa no (ɛhe na wanya atokoɔ no), na ɔkaeɛ sɛ, The duck yard gave (it) me, na ɔkaeɛ sɛ broad road bi da nakyi hɔ, na ne kunu de no kɔeɛ, na ɔde (some maize) baeɛ. The donkey mother said, Then to-morrow I will go there very, very early in the morning. Enti ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ. Bere a ɔkɔɔ no, na atokoɔ pii wɔ hɔ. Afei ɔfaa ne sekan twitwaa bi, na ɔyɛɛ basket a ɛyɛ palm leaf, na ɔboaboaa (corn) no ano, na ɔde (hyɛɛ mu). Afei Adanko, adanko kɛseɛ no, na ɔwɔ ne fie, na ɔhunuu sɛ ɔboɔ kɛseɛ no atwa afuo no mu. Ɛhɔ ara na ɔno nso firii hɔ. Ɔbaa kuro no ano na ɔbɛyɛɛ Ɔtwee. Na emu fã asɛe, nanso na emu fã no nnya nyɛɛ porɔwee. Na ɔkɔɔ so kɔɔ akyiri, na ɔbɛyɛɛ Oyuo (antelope). Ne fã bi na ɛreporɔ, ne fã bi nso nnya nporɔ. Na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔbɛyɛɛ Bongo. Ne fã bi na ɛreporɔ, ne fã bi nso nnya nporɔ. Na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔbɛyɛɛ Ɛko. Ne nyinaa na ɛyɛ foforo. Afei na Ɔsebɔ no reba a ɔkura kɛntɛn ne atokoɔ wɔ ne ti so. Ɔbaa bɛhyiaa Ɛko no. Ɔsebɔfoɔ no kaa ne ho sɛ, Ɔkɔm kɛseɛ bi bɛba, nanso ɛnnya nyɛ (severe), nanso mmoa yi rewu. Na ɔtwa mu kɔduruu Bongo; na ɔtwa mu kɔduruu Oyuo; na ɔtwa mu kɔduruu Otwii. Afei ɔkae sɛ, Aane, mmoa yi dɔɔso paa, enti mɛkɔ akɔboaboa ano. Ɔtoo basket no ne aburow no guu fam, na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔfa mmoa no, ɛfiri saa nea ɛdi kan no. Bere a ɔkɔɔ no, na wɔn ho nni hɔ bio. Afei ɔsan bae, nanso basket no ne aburow no nni hɔ bio. Ɔfirii fie kɔtenaa dinn. Ɔsebɔ bedeɛ no bisaa no sɛ, Aburo no wɔ he? Ɔkae sɛ, Mekɔeɛ (hɔ), nanso mantumi annye biara atɔ. Afei ɔsebɔ bedeɛ no kɔɔ asubɔnten no ho, na (hɔ) ɔkɔhyiaa akokɔ bedeɛ no. Ɔsebɔ bɛkaa sɛ, All I have to say is that Blackberry my husband said that when he went, he did not get any maize to buy. The rabbit replied, He is deceiving you, for Blackberry, when he went and got (some), my husband beat him and relieved him of it. The rabbit set off home. Ɔbɔɔ ne kunu amanneɛ sɛ, Well done! well done! well done! woka sɛ wokɔeɛ na wannya aburoo biara, berɛ a saa Adanko no na ɔbɔɔ wo na ɔgyee no firii wo nsam. Ɔsebɔ no kaa sɛ, Hwan ne Adanko? Ɛnnɛ-ɛkyena mɛkɔ akɔko ne no nnua. Adanko kɛseɛ no tenaa fie na ɔtee (eyi). Ɔkɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Ɔkyena, ɔseboɔ kɛseɛ no bɛba ha sɛ ɔne me bɛko, enti mɛkɔ akɔhyia no wɔ kwan no so, ebia wobɛsuro. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, Adanko no fii aseɛ. Ɔduruu kuro no akyi, na ɔbɛdane Adowa, na ɔregyea nsuo, nsuo, ne mogya. Ɔkɔɔ so wɔ anim, na ɔbɛyɛɛ Bush-buck, na ɔregye ɔfe wɔ kwan koro no ara so. Ɔkɔɔ so wɔ anim, na ɔbɛyɛɛ Ɔko. Na ɔyaredɔm no reba. Adowa no kaa sɛ, Agya Bɛnom, boa me, ɛfiri sɛ merewu. Bɛnom no kaa sɛ, Ɛdeɛn na ɛrekɔ so? Bɛnom no kaa sɛ, Me ne Adanko kɔɔ akasakasa bi a ɛfa aburoo ho, na ɔbɔɔ me, na merewu. Bɛnom no kaa sɛ, Ma me kwan. Ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔne Bush-buck no hyiaa. Bush-buck no kaa sɛ, "Seaball, support me, for that Adanko is not one with whom to have a maize dispute. " Bush-buck no kaa sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na ɛrekɔ so yi? " Bush-buck no kaa sɛ, "Me kɔeɛ na ɔbɔɔ me, ɛno nti na merewu yi. " Bush-buck no kaa sɛ, "Let me pass on. " Ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔtoo Ɛko no. Ɔko no kaa sɛ, "Father Osebo, support me, for I am dying. " Lion no kaa sɛ, "What is the matter? " Ɛko no kaa sɛ, "I went and had a dispute about maize with the Adanko, and he beat me, and I am dying. " Ɛhɔ na Lion no dwenee ho pii (and at length he said to himself), "E! Mɛdane, ɛfiri sɛ ɛyɛ asɛm a ɛte saa ara na ɛsɛ sɛ me ne no siesie, na yɛmfa no sɛ ɔbɔɔ me..." Na Ɔsebɔ no hyɛɛ aseɛ dwanee, kiri! Kiri! Ɛhɔ ara na Adanko no nso sɔreeeɛ. Ɔdii nakyi, na ɔsono no de nne kɛse teɛɛm sɛ, Osebo bedeɛ, guan! Ɔsono no duu ne fie hɔ, na ɔyerae wɔ ne dan mu, na ɔfaa ne ti bɔɔ ɔfasu no biako na ɔfaa mu, nanso Adankoro no kyeree no wɔ nakyi nan abien no so. Na ɔsono no kaa sɛ, "Agya Adanko, ma me kwan. " Adanko kaa sɛ, "Memma wo nkɔ, mɛbɔ wo. " Ɔsono no kaa sɛ, "Me srɛ wo sɛ, mma me nni, na mmom ma me anyame no nom na woka ntam sɛ ɔkwan biara a wobɛfa so no, me bɛba na masiesie. " Ɛno nti, sɛ wofa kwan biara a, na wuhu sɛ ɔsono no abɔ hɔ a, (onim) Adanko atwa saa kwan no mu.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
