Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

ƐSƐ SƐ ABAAYEWA BIARA WARE NEA WƆAGYE NO NO

NA ƐYƐ BERE BI A ABEAWA BI A WƆFRƐ NO KWABOASO WƆ HƆ. Na obiara a wɔde no bɛma no aware no, na ɔka sɛ, "Mempɛ no. " Wɔde no maa ɔbɔfoɔ bi, na ɔkae sɛ, "Ugh! ɔbarima yi wɔ ntontom wɔ ne ho, mempɛ no. "

Ɛda bi a ɔkɔɔ afuom hɔ no, ɔkaa sɛ ɔrebɛtwa borɔdeɛ. Ɔfaa sekan na ɔbɔɔ borɔdeɛ no, kɔ! berɛ a ɔhunuu sɛ nkurɔfoɔ nketewa no te borɔdeɛ no so; wɔtee aseɛ, na wɔbaa bɛkyeree Kwaboaso; wɔkae sɛ, "Wo na sɛ wɔde ma obiara a, woso wo ti, pusu! pusu! " Na aboa no kyeree no na ɔkae sɛ: "Bra, ma yɛnkyekyere no.

Yɛkyere no, O!

Yɛkyere Kwaboaso.

Bra, momma yɛnkyekyere no.

Yɛkyere no, Oh!

Ɛnneɛ, berɛ a saa ɔbɔfoɔ a wɔde Kwaboaso ama no tee Kwaboaso nne no, ɔkaa sɛ, Mekɔhwɛ nea asɛm no yɛ, ɛfiri sɛ yɛnfa bɔne ntua bɔne. Bere a ɔkɔɔ no, na Kwaboaso wɔ hɔ, na mmoatia no rehyɛ no. Ɛneɛ na ɔbɔfoɔ no de atuo kɔtoo mmoatia no so, na baako bɛhwee fam. Mmoa no mu panyin no kae (maa afoforo no): "Ɔwɔ nsa a wɔnom, na wabow; Fa no si hɔ mprempren, (na afei kɔ so twitwiw no)." Ɔsomafo no tow tuo bio, na foforo nso hwee ase. Mmoa no mu panyin no kae sɛ: "Ɔsraani no wɔ nsa a wɔnom na ne ho akokwaw, fa no na fa no si nkyɛn. " Ɔsraani no kunkum mmoawa no nyinaa gye ɔpanyin no nkutoo. Ɔpanyin no frɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no sɛ, "Bra. " Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Meremma. " Mmoa no mu panyin no kaa sɛ, "Ao, bra, mennyɛ wo hwee. " Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔeɛ. Mmoa no mu panyin no kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ me dan mu wɔ hɔ, na wubehu tuo no ano aduru (i.e. gunpowder no) ne atuo a woato no nyinaa, fa nea ɛyɛ wo de no, na fa Kwaboaso nso, nanso ansa na woakɔ no, kɔtwa akutu, na sɛ worekɔ a, tow gu, na sɛ mmoawa afoforo no sɔre na wɔba abɛkyere wo a, na bere a wɔagyae sɛ wɔbɛfa wɔn baako baako no, na woakɔ akyɛ, akyɛ, akyɛ. " Na sɛnea ɛfata no, ɔkɔɔ so twitwaa akutu, na ɔfaa K Afei nso, ɔtow bankye biako guu Kwaboaso so bere a oduu kwan no so no. Ɔkɔɔ so yɛɛ eyi kosii fie. Na bere tiaa bi pɛ na aka ansa na wɔredu fie hɔ no, hwɛ, mmoa a wɔsɛe nnɔbae no di wɔn akyi. Na ɔtow bankye biako pɛ a aka no guu fam, na mmoa a wɔsɛe nnɔbae no dii akyi (awiei koraa no wɔdanee wɔn akyi). Na ɔbɔmmɔfo no san de Kwaboaso baa ne mogya mu abusuabɔ mu. Afei ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔɔ ne fie. Afei, na ɔbɔfoɔ no te hɔ, bere a ɔhunuu sɛ abɔfo no adu ne baabi no, ɔbisaa sɛ, "Asɛm no bɛn? " Abɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Kwaboaso kyerɛ sɛ ɔabisa akuraa no so panyin sɛ ɔnyɛ nanim asɛm, kyerɛ sɛ afei deɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔware wo. " Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Meda mo ase wɔ nsɛm a ɛfiri akuraa no Odikro ano no ho, nanso merentumi nware ababaa no, ɛfiri sɛ meda so ara wɔ t icks on my body. " Ɛno nti na mpanimfoɔ no ka sɛ, "When they take you to give you in marriage to any one, marry him, for you do not know but that some day when you are in need, he will rescue you. "

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
