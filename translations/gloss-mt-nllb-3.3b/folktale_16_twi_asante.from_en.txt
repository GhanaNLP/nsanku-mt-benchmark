Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ABAAYEWA BIARA NWARE ƆN A WƆDE NO AMA NO WƆ AWAREƐ MU

NA abaayewa bi wɔ hɔ a wɔfrɛ no Kwaboaso. Obiara a wɔbɛma no aware no, na ɔse, "Mempɛ no. " Wɔde no maa ɔbɔfoɔ bi, na ɔse, "Ugh! saa ɔbarima yi ntontom wɔ ne so, mempɛ no. "

Ɛdii da bi a ɔkɔɔ afuw hɔ sɛ ɔrekɔtwa bɔrɔde. Ɔfaa sekkan bɔɔ borɔdeɛ no, kɔ! na nnipa nketewa no te bɔrɔde no so, wɔsii fam baa bɛkyeree Kwaboaso, na wɔkae sɛ, "Wone nea wɔde wo kɔma obiara a, wosow wo etire, pusu! pusu! " Na mmoatia no kyeree no nsɔmu kae sɛ: "Mommra, momma yɛnkyekyere no.

Yɛpiapia no, O!

Yɛretwe Kwaboaso.

Bra, momma yɛnkyere no.

Yɛpiapia no, O!

Afei, berɛ a saa ɔbɔfoɔ a na wɔde Kwaboaso ama no tee Kwaboaso nne no, ɔkae sɛ, "Mekɔhwɛ sɛ asɛm no yɛ dɛn, ɛfiri sɛ yɛnni bɔne mma bɔne. " Ɛberɛ a ɔkɔɔ no, na Kwaboaso wɔ hɔ, na mmoatia no rehyɛ no. Ɛneɛ na ɔbɔfoɔ no de tuo no too mmoatia no so, na ebi hwee ase. Na mmoatia no mu panyin no kae sɛ, Ɔwɔ nsafufuo a wɔanom, na wabow nsa, fa no si hɔ saa berɛ yi. Afei ɔbɔfoɔ no san too tuo no, na foforɔ bi nso hwee ase. Mmoatia no mu panyin no kae sɛ, Ɔwɔ nsafufuo a wɔanom, koodurufoɔ no adi ne so nkonim, monkyere no nkɔto nkyɛn wɔ hɔ. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no kunkum mmoatia no nyinaa gye mmoatia a ɔsen biara no. Ɔpanyin no frɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no sɛ, "Bra. " ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Meremma. " Ɔpanyin no a ɔwɔ mmoatia no mu kaa sɛ, "Aane, bra, merennye wo hwee. " Na ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔeɛ. Ɔpanyin no a ɔwɔ mmoatia no mu kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ me dan no mu wɔ hɔ, na wubehu aduro a wɔde tuo (i.e. atuduro) ne otuo ano aba a woatu no nyinaa, fa nea ɛyɛ wo deɛ no, na fa Kwaboaso nso kɔ, nanso ansa na woakɔ no, kɔtwa banana, na woakɔ no, tow gu, na sɛ mmoatia foforɔ no renyane a, wɔabɛkyere wo, na ɛneɛ berɛ a wɔagyae abɛfa baako biara no, na woakɔ akyɛre, akyɛre, akyɛre. " Na ɛneɛ ɔkɔtwaa banana no, na ɔfaa K waboaso nso, na berɛ a ɔduruu kwan no so no, ɔtoo kwadu baako. Ɔtoaa so ara koduu fie. Na berɛ a aka berɛ tiawa pɛ ansa na wɔredu fie no, hwɛ, mmoatia no redi wɔn akyi. Na ɔtoo ne kwadu baako pɛ a aka no gui, na mmoatia no dii nakyi kɔdanee. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no san de Kwaboaso baa nabusuafoɔ nkyɛn. Afei ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔɔ nankasa fie. Afei, na ɔbɔfoɔ no te hɔ, bere a ɔhunuu sɛ asɛnkafoɔ bi adu ne baabi no, ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛdɛn ne amaneɛbɔ? " Na asɛnkafoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Kwaboaso ka sɛ, ɔsrɛɛ akuraa no mu Etire sɛ ɔnnye n'anim mma no, kyerɛ sɛ afei deɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔware wo. " Na ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Meda wo ase wɔ nsɛmfua a ɛfiri akuraa no mu Odikro no ano no ho, nanso merentumi nnware abaayewa no, ɛfiri sɛ m'ani nna hɔ. icks on my nipadua. " Ɛno nti na Mpanimfoɔ se, "Sɛ wɔde wo kɔ ma obiara awareɛ a, ware no, wo nnim nanso sɛ daakye bi a wode bɛsi ahiadeɛ mu no, ɔbɛgye wo. "

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
