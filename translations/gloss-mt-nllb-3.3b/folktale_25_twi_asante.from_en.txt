SƐ OBI SƐE WAGYE A, MMA WƆNNYA NKƆSO MMA NO

WƆKYERƐ SƐ, bere bi na mmanoma bi te hɔ a wɔn maame awu na wagyaw wɔn nkutoo. Na afahyɛ bi rekɔ so, na Ata Panyin (Ata Panin) faa ahene a ɛsom bo ne sika kɔkɔɔ agude bi kyekyeree Ata Kuma kɔn ho. Ata Kuma ne ne nnamfonom no kae sɛ, wɔrekɔ asuo no mu akɔhohoro wɔn ho. Bere a wɔrehohoro wɔn ho no, hama a wɔde sika kɔkɔɔ agude no kyekyere Ata Kuma kɔn no mu no bubui, na agude no hwee nsu no mu. Ɔsan baa fie bɛbɔɔ ne nuabea amanneɛ sɛ: "Sika kɔkɔɔ agude no ayera. " Ata Panin (ata panyin) kae sɛ: "Minnye nni sɛ ayera, na sɛ ayera mpo a, biribi wɔ hɔ a mɛka afi wo hɔ. " Ata Kumaa (ata kumaa) kae sɛ: "Dɛn na wobɛhwehwɛ afi me hɔ? " Ɔkae sɛ: "Mɛhwehwɛ afi wo hɔ ɔsebɔ akuturuku, aserewa kumaa no bere, nsu a ɛreworo so no. gles ponponponponsa, nhaban a ɛyɛ baako pɛ (wɔ dua a wotwa no so), ne Elephant Queen no dua. Ata Kumaa kaa sɛ, "Mate. " Ata Kumaa fii aseɛ (sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ saa nneɛma yi), na ɔbaa beaeɛ bi a dua baako na ɛda hɔ. Dua no huu no a, ɔkae sɛ, "Eyi ne Ata Kumaa, (a nkurɔfoɔ ka ne ho asɛm sɛ) ɔda adi wɔ kuro no akyi te sɛ osu a ɛtɔ. " (Ata Kumaa) kaa sɛ, "Me nko ara nti na maba ha ne sɛ, agya ba no yɛ Ata Panin baako, na ɛna ba no nso yɛ Ata Panin baako, na ɔde nhwiren a ɛsono ne kɔla kyekyere me kɔn, na ɛpaee na ɛyeraeɛ. Kojo Nsa a a.

Na Single-leaf-on-a-tree no kaa sɛ, Na ɛno nti na woresu no, bra na gye biribi. Na Ata Kumaa kɔgyee (leave no firi dua bi a na nhaban baako pɛ na ɛwɔ so), na ɔdaa no ase (dua no). Na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔhyiaa Aserewa anomaa bi. Na Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, Eyi ne Ata Kumaa, a sɛ ɔda kuro no akyi a, obiara ka sɛ ɔte sɛ osuo a ɛretu. Ata Kumaa buae sɛ, Nea enti a maba ha ne sɛ, agya ba no yɛ Ata biako Panin, na ɛna ba no yɛ Ata biako Panin, na ɔde nhwiren a ɛyɛ ahorow kyekyere me ho, na ɛpaee na ɛyerae. Kojo Nsa a a.

Aserewa nnomaa no kaa sɛ, Ɛno nti na wosu, bra na gye biribi. Na Ata Kumaa kɔgyee (the arrival of the Aserewa bird), na ɔdaa no ase. Na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ, na ɔhyiaa Nsuo a ɛrebɔ ponponponponpon. Ɔkae sɛ, Eyi ne Ata Kumaa, a sɛ ɔda adi wɔ akuraa ase kwan no awiei a, nkurɔfo gye no sɛ osu a ɛtɔ. Ata Kumaa ka kyerɛɛ no nsɛm a na waka bi dedaw akyerɛ afoforo no. Na Gurgling-water no maa no bi, na ɔfaa no kɔɔ so. Afei ɔne Ɔbɔfoɔ no hyiaa. Bere a ɔsono no huu no no, ɔkae sɛ, Ɛha ne Ata Kumaa; sɛ ɔda adi wɔ akuraa ase kwan no awiei a, ɔte sɛ osu a ɛtɔ a ɛma ahotɔ. Ata Kumaa ka kyerɛɛ no nasɛm. Ɔkae sɛ, Bra na gye biribi tom, (na ɔde ne mmabaa no mu biako mae). Ɛnna Ata Kumaa kaa sɛ, Father Leopard, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ na manya Elephant Queen no dua? Ɔkae sɛ, Ah, bere a worekɔ no, mmoa no nyinaa bɛda hɔ, na Elephant Queen no bɛda mfinimfini, na bere a worekɔ no, fa wɔn so denneennen, na bere a woadu Elephant Queen no nkyɛn no, twa ne dua no, nanso sɛ wode ahwɛyiye nantew wɔn so a, w ill kill you. " Ata Kumaa kaa sɛ, "Meda mo ase. " Na ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu mmoa yi, ɔhyɛɛ wɔn so den paa, na ɔduruu ɔhemmaa no nkyɛn. Ɔtwaa ne dua, na ɔdanee. Wɔ kwan no so no ɔtɔɔ ahwehwɛ, na ɔde kɔɔ fie. Na ɔduruu fie. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panyin, nneɛma a wokaeɛ sɛ wode bɛgye afiri me nsam no, ɛwɔ ha. " Ata 'Panyin kaa sɛ, "Me nsa aka wɔn. " Ɛnneɛ, da bi a wɔte hɔ bom no, Ata Kumaa faa ahwehwɛ no na ɔhwɛɛ mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Saa ahwehwɛ yi, sɛ obi hwɛ mu a, ɔhu ɛna a ɔwɔ Asamanfoɔ asase so. " Ata 'Panyin kaa sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ, fa ba, na ma mehwɛ. " Ɔde maa no. Bere a ɔrebɛhwɛ mu no, ɛguan firii ne nsa mu, na ebubui. Ata Kumaa kaa sɛ, "Eyi deɛ, nea woagye afiri me hɔ no, mebisa wo. " Ɛneɛ na Ata Panyin no kɔfa nneɛma a Ata Kumaa de ama no no. Ata Kumaa kaa sɛ, "Mempɛ (eyinom), nea mepɛ ne sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa foforɔ ama me. " Ata Panyin no fii nakwantu no ase, na ɔkɔhyiaa dua bi a ɛwɔ nhaban baako. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛha ne Ata Panyin, a sɛ ɔda adi wɔ akuraa ase kwan no awiei a, wɔgye no sɛ osu a ɛtɔ. " Ata Panyin buae sɛ, "Mappeared wo maame! ne wo papa! Me deɛ, fa wo nhaban baako ma me na ma menkɔ. " (Nua a ɛwɔ) nhaban baako no kae sɛ, "Fa so, nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi) no wɔ hɔ. " Ɔkɔɔ so, na ohyiaa Aserewa anomaa bi. Aserewa anomaa no kae sɛ, "Eyi ne Ata Panyin; Sɛ ɔda adi wɔ akuraa ase kwan no awiei a, ɔyɛ hyew te sɛ osu a ɛreworo so. " Ata Panyin kae sɛ, "Sɛ mepue wo maame ne wo papa a, nea mepɛ ara ne wo nnomaa no biako pɛ, na ma menkɔ. " Aserewa anomaa no kae sɛ, "Kɔ so, nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi) no wɔ hɔ. " Afei ɔbaa Gurgling-nsu no nkyɛn. Gurgling-nsuo kaa sɛ, "Here is Ata 'Chief; When she appears at the end of the town, she is like a soothing shower of rain. " Ata 'Chief said, "When I 'appear' your mother, when I 'appear' your father, as for me, give me some of your water and let me pass on. " Gurgling-nsuo kaa sɛ, "Pass on in front, he who knows (how to deal with you) is there. " Ɛno akyi no, ɔne Ɔsebɔfoɔ no hyiaa. Ɔsomafo no kae sɛ: "Ata Panyin ni; Sɛ ɔba abɔnten awiei a, ɔma ne ho tɔ no te sɛ osu a ɛtɔ. " Ata Panyin buae sɛ: "Me yɛ wo maame! ne wo papa! Me deɛ, fa wo mmeranteɛ no bi ma me na ma menkɔ so. " Ɔsono no kaa sɛ, "He who knows (how to deal with you) is there. " Ɔde ne ho kɔeɛ, na ɔkɔhyiaa asono dɔm bi, na Asono Hemaa no da mfinimfini. Afei ɔkɔɔ so twitwaa ne dua. Asono dɔm no nyinaa kuraa ne ho, na wɔhyɛɛ no so, na owuiɛ. Ata Kumaa hwehwɛɛ ne nuabea wɔ kwan no so. Ɔmmɛbaeɛ. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Sɛ wo ne wo mogya mu biara te hɔ, na wɔn mu biara sɛe wo deɛ a, mma no ntua, ɛfiri sɛ Ata Panyin no yɛɛ saa, na ɔkɔ wuiɛ. "

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
