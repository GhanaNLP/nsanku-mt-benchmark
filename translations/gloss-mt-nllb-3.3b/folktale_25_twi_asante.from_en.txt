SƐ OBI SƐE WO DEA (AKWANHYIA MU A), MFA HO NTUA

WƆKYERƐ SƐ, bere bi na maame mmienu bi te hɔ a wɔn maame awu na wɔagyaw wɔn nko. Na afahyɛ bi rekɔ so, na ataapanyin (Ata Panin) faa ahene a ɛsom bo bi ne sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ bi kyekyeree ata kumaa, Ata Kuma, kɔn mu. Na ata kumaa no ne ne nnamfofoɔ no reka sɛ wɔrebɛkɔ asuwa hɔ akɔhohoro wɔn ho. Bere a wɔrehohoro wɔn ho no, hama a etwa to no a wɔde sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ kyekyeree Ata Kuma kɔn no bubuu, na ahosiesiedeɛ no hwee nsuo mu. Ɛwom sɛ ɔhwehwɛɛ hɔ deɛ, nanso wɔanhu. Ɔsan baa fie bɛbɔɔ ne nuabea amanneɛ sɛ, "Woahwere sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ no." Ata Panin (ataapanyin) kae sɛ, "Menni ahotoso sɛ wɔayera, na sɛ mpo wɔayera a, biribi wɔ hɔ a mɛka akyerɛ wo." Ata Kumaa (ata kumaa) kae sɛ, "Dɛn na wobɛhwehwɛ afi me hɔ? Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mebisa wo daboɔ mmɔwerɛ, aserewa ketewa no berebuo, Nsuo a gur gles ponponponsa, ahaban a ɛyɛ baako pɛ (wɔ dua a woyi fi mu), ne Asono Ɔhemmaa no dua. Ata Kumaa kae sɛ, "Metee. " Ata Kumaa fii aseɛ kɔhwehwɛɛ saa nneɛma yi, na ɔbaa beaeɛ pɔtee bi a dua baako na ɛda hɔ. Bere a dua no huu no no, ɔkae sɛ, "Eyi ne Ata Kumaa, (nea nnipa ka sɛ) ɔda adi wɔ kuro no kuro akyi sɛ osu a ɛma ahomeka osuo. " (Ata Kumaa) kae sɛ, "Me sianteɛ baako pɛ a me baa ha ne sɛ, agya ba baako ne Ata Panin, ɛna ɛna ba baako ne Ata Panin, na ɔde ahweneɛ a ɛyɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ kyekyeree me kɔn, na ɛbubuu na ɛyeraa. " (Here broke into ɔbabarima Ata Kumaa g): nea

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔregye ahaban afi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ Aserewa anomaa no berebuo.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Nsuo, pɔnpɔnpɔnsa a ɛsen gogorogogoro.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ. Ɔrehwehwɛ Agya Ɔsebɔ mmɔwerɛ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔregye Asono Ɔhemmaa no dua akosi ne ntini so.

Na ɛdɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ, Owura, na manya bi?

Ɔse (ɛha ne) abofra ketewa, abofra ketewa, Akwasi Toboro Kojo babaa.

Kojo Nsa.

Agya Asono, ɛbɛyɛ yiye sɛ wode wo ho bɛkɔ, ɛbɛyɛ yiye sɛ wubefi hɔ.

Kojo Nsa a a.

Na Single-ahaban-on-a-dua no kae sɛ, And that is why you are weeping, come and receive something. Na Ata Kumaa kɔgyee (dua a ahaban baako pɛ na ɛwɔ so no ahaban no), na ɔdaa no ase (dua no). Na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔhyiaa Aserewa anomaa bi. Na Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, Eyi ne Ata Kumaa, a sɛ ɔba kuro no kuro akyi a, obiara ka sɛ ɔyɛ osu a ɛma ahomeka. Ata Kumaa buaa sɛ, Me sianteɛ baako pɛ nti na mebaa ha ne sɛ, agya ba baako ne Ata Panin, na ɛna ba baako ne Ata Panin, na ɔde ahweneɛ a ɛyɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ kyekyeree me ho, na ɛbɔɔ mu na ɛyeraa. (Na ha na Ata Kumaa abubu. wɔ dwom mu):

Ɔse ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ ahaban afi me hɔ afi dua a ɛsow ahaban biako pɛ no hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔregye ahaban afiri me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔse (ɛha ne) abofra ketewa, abofra ketewa, Akwasi Toboro Kojo babaa.

Kojo Nsa.

Agya Asono, ɛbɛyɛ yiye sɛ wode wo ho bɛkɔ, ɛbɛyɛ yiye sɛ wubefi hɔ.

Kojo Nsa a a.

Aserewa anomaa no kae sɛ, Ɛno nti na wosu, bra bɛgye biribi atom. Na Ata Kumaa kɔgyee Aserewa anomaa no berebuo no, na ɔdaa no ase. Na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ, na ɔhyiaa nsuo a ɛrebɔ ponponponponsa. Ɔkae sɛ, Hwɛ Ata Kumaa yi, sɛ ɔda adi wɔ akuraase abɔnten no awieeɛ a, nnipa gye no sɛ osu a ɛma ahomeka. Ata Kumaa ka kyerɛɛ no amaneɛbɔ a na waka akyerɛ afoforɔ dedaw. Na Gurgling nsuo no maa no bi (ne nsu a ɛresen pɔnpɔnpɔn), na ɔfaa no kɔɔ so. Afei ɔne Daboɔ no hyiaa. Daboɔ no hunuu no a, ɔkaa sɛ, Hwɛ Ata Kumaa ni; sɛ ɔda adi wɔ akuraase abɔnten no awieeɛ a, ɔte sɛ osuo osu a ɛma ahomeka. Ata Kumaa ka kyerɛɛ no eRRAND. Ɔkaa sɛ, Bra na gye biribi tom, (na ɔde ne mmɔwere no baako maa no). Afei Ata Kumaa kae sɛ, "Father Leopard, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ woyɛ na manya Asono Ɔhemmaa no dua? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Ah, bere a worekɔ no, mmoa no nyinaa bɛda, na Asono Ɔhemmaa no bɛda mfinimfini, na bere a worekɔ no, fa wɔn so denneennen, na sɛ wodu Asono Ɔhemmaa no nkyɛn a, twa ne dua no, nanso sɛ wode ahwɛyiye nantew wɔn so a, w nkate bɔne kunkum wo. " Ata Kumaa kae sɛ, "Meda wo ase. " Na ɔkɔɔ so no, ɔhunuu saa mmoa yi, ɔde ne nan sii so denneennen paa, na ɔduruu Ɔhemmaa no nkyɛn. Ɔtwaa ne dua no, na ɔdanee nakyi. Wɔ kwan so no, ɔtɔɔ ahwehwɛ, na ɔde kɔɔ fie. Na ɔduruu fie hɔ. Ɔkaeɛ sɛ, AtaPanyin, hwɛ nneɛma a wokaeɛ sɛ wobɛgye afiri me hɔ no. AtaPanyin kaa sɛ, Me nsa aka wɔn. Afei, da bi a na wɔte aseɛ bɔ mu no, Ata Kumaa faa ahwehwɛ no hwɛɛ mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, Saa ahwehwɛ yi, sɛ obi hwɛ mu a, ohu maame a ɔwɔ Nsaman-saman asase so. Ata Panyin kaa sɛ, Ɛyɛ saa anaa, fa ba, na ma mehwɛ. Ɔde maa no. Bere a ɔrebɛhwɛ mu no, ɛguan fii ne nsa mu, na ɛpaee. Ata Kumaa kae sɛ, "Eyi deɛ, wugyee me hɔ no, me bɛbisa wo. " Afei Ata 'Panyin no kɔɔ so kɔgyee nneɛma a Ata Kumaa de brɛɛ no no. Ata Kumaa kae sɛ, "Mempɛ (eyinom) no, nea mepɛ ne sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa afoforɔ aba ama me. " Ata 'Panyin no de ne ho sii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ahaban baako dua. Ɔkae sɛ, "Hwɛ Ata 'Panyin, sɛ ɔba wɔ akuraase abɔnten no awieeɛ a, yɛgye no sɛ osuo asuguare. " Ata 'Panyin buae sɛ, "Me 'appeared' wo maame! ne wo agya! Me deɛ, fa wo ahaban baako ma me na ma menkɔ. " (Ahaban koro no ara dua) kae sɛ, "Fa wo ho kɔ, nea onim wo ho asɛm no wɔ hɔ. " Ɔkɔɔ so, na Aserewa anomaa bi hyiaa no. Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panyin' ni ha; sɛ ɔba wɔ akuraase abɔnten no awieeɛ a, ne ho tɔ no sɛ osuo asuguare. " Ata 'Panyin kaa sɛ, "Sɛ me 'appear' wo maame ne wo agya a, me deɛ, mepɛ wo anomaabuo bere no mu baako pɛ, na ma menkɔ. " Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, "Fa so, nea onim wo ho asɛm no wɔ hɔ. " Afei ɔbaa Gurgling nsuo no ho. Gurgling-nsuo kaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panyin' ni; sɛ ɔba kuro no awieeɛ a, ɔte sɛ osu a ɛma ahomeka wɔ osuo mu. " Ata 'Panyin kaa sɛ, "Sɛ me 'appear' wo maame, me 'appear' wo agya, ma me wo nsuo no bi na memfa me ho nkɔ. " Gurgling-nsuo kaa sɛ, "Pass on in front, he who knows (how to deal with you) is there. " Ɛno akyi no, ɔhyiaa Daboɔ no. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Ɛha na Ata 'Panyin' wɔ, sɛ ɔba wɔ abɔnten no awieeɛ a, ɛma wo ho tɔ wo sɛ osuo asuguare. " Ata 'Panyin buae sɛ, "Me 'appear' wo maame! ne wo agya! Na me deɛ, fa wo mmɔwere no bi ma me na memfa nkɔ. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Obiara a ɔnim wo ho asɛm no wɔ hɔ. " Ɔkɔeɛ no, ɔhyiaa Asono mmoakuo bi, na Asono Ɔhemmaa no da mfinimfini. Afei ɔkɔɔ so twitwaa ne dua no. Asono mmoakuo no nyinaa faa no nsɔmu, hwee no, na ɔwui. Ata Kumaa hwehwɛɛ ne nuabea wɔ kwan so. Ɔmbaeɛ. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Sɛ wo ne wo mogya biara te hɔ, na wɔn mu biara asɛe wo adeɛ a, ɛnsɛ sɛ wotwe ntua, ɛfiri sɛ Ata Panyin no na ɔyɛɛ saa, na ɔkɔ wuiɛ. "

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
