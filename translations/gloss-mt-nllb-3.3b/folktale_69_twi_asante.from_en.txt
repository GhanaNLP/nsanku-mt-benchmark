Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA NE SƐ, SƐ OBI DƆ NWIADOWA, DWUMADIE-NKUO A, WƆYƐ NO WƆ FAM, BERE A NKRUMA, NKRUMA NO, GYINA HƆ KƐSEƐ

GARDEN-EGGS NE Nkruma bere bi na wɔaka abom wɔ dan biako mu; dan fa a na wɔde yɛ nwura a wɔpopae, fa a na wɔde yɛ mpa-dan. Wɔn adwuma a na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔyɛ no, na wɔyɛ no nyinaa wɔ saa dan koro no ara mu. Afei Garden Eggs ka kyerɛɛ Nkruma sɛ, "Me werɛ ahow wɔ ɔman yi ho, enti momma yɛnkɔ tutu ɛtokuro na yɛmfa nwura no nhyɛ mu." Nkruma kaa sɛ, "Menkɔ (tu)." Garden Eggs sii kwan so na wotuu amena. Bere a otutuu no wiei no, Nkruma teɛɛm guu no so, na Garden Eggs buaa no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ma ɛtokuro no so na fa brɛ me." Garden Eggs buae sɛ, "Aha! ɛfiri berɛ a Ɔbɔadeɛ bɔɔ no, woahunu onipa a wama ɛtokuro so pɛn anaa? " Nkruma kaa sɛ, "Sɛ woanhyɛ wo ano a, mɛba abɛbɔ wo. " Garden-eggs kaa sɛ, "Afei nya sɔneɛ, fa nsuo hyɛ mu ma ma me nom, na mɛma wo so. " Nkruma buaa sɛ, "Wobɛfa me sɛ kwasea, ɛfiri berɛ a yɛdii kan tenaa ha sɛ abusuakuo no, yɛde sɔneɛ na ɛbɔɔ yɛn ho. lash nsu? Nkruma gyaee nea ɔteɛ no, na ɔkɔɔ ɛtokuro no mu sɛ ɔrekɔbɔ Garden-eggs no. Afei, berɛ a ɔrebɔ no, Ɔsoro-Onyame tee, na ɔsomaa Kɔkɔsakyi, Ɔpɛtɛ, sɛ ɔmmɛgye wɔn mmienu no mmra. Wɔn mmienu no kɔɔ. Garden-eggs no kaa nsɛmfua a ɛwɔ nano, Nkruma kaa deɛ ɛwɔ ne deɛ mu. Afei, Ewiem-onyankopɔn yɛɛ ne kasafoɔ sɛ ɔbɛma Nkruma atɛmmuo, na ɔwɔ Garden-eggs adom mu. Ɛno nti, sɛ yɛdua Garden-eggs a, ɔda fam wɔ fam, ɔda Ewiem-onyankopɔn ne ne mpɔtam badwafoɔ ase. Na Nkruma deɛ, ogyina hɔ tenten, ɔka sɛ ɔne Ewiem-onyankopɔn bɛko sɛ wama no atɛnkyea.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
