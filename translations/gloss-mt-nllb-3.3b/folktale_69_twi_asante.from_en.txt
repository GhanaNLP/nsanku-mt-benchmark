Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA BƐYƐ SƐ BERE A OBI BƐFUA NWIADOWA, GARDEN-EGGS NO, WƆN NYIN WƆ AFURA FAM, BERE A NKRUMA, A ƐYƐ NKRUMA NO, GYINA HƆ KYƐN

GARDEN-EGGS ne Nkruma bɔɔ mu tenaa dan baako mu pɛn; ɔdan no fa a wɔde siee nneɛma a wɔhohoro, fa nso a wɔde yɛɛ baabi a wɔda. Wɔn adwuma a wɔwɔ sɛ wɔyɛ no, na wɔyɛ no wɔ saa dan baako no mu. Afei Garden-eggs ka kyerɛɛ Nkruma sɛ, "Me ho rewu me wɔ saa dan yi ho, enti yɛnkɔyɛ tokuru na yɛnhyɛ nwura no mu. " Nkruma kaa sɛ, "Merenkɔ. " Garden-eggs fii hɔ na wotuu amena bi. Bere a ɔtwee no wiei no, Nkruma teɛɛm kyerɛɛ no, na Garden-eggs buaa no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa tokuru no so na fa bra ma mehu. " Garden-eggs buae sɛ, "Ah! Ɛfiri berɛ a Ɔbɔadeɛ no bɔɔ no, woahunu sɛ onipa bi ama abura bi so anaa? Nkruma kaa sɛ, "Sɛ woanmu wano a, mɛba abɛbɔ wo. " Garden-eggs kaa sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ gye sanku, fa nsuo gu mu ma me nom, na mɛma (sanku no so) na mede abɛbrɛ wo. " Nkruma buaa sɛ, "Wobɛfa me sɛ ɔkwasea; ɛfiri berɛ a yɛdii kan tenaa ha sɛ mmusuakuo no, yɛde sanku a yɛde sp lash nsuo? " Nkruma firii baabi a ɔteɛ no, na ɔkɔɔ abura no mu sɛ ɔrebɛbɔ Garden-eggs. Afei, berɛ a ɔrebu no, Ɔsoro-nyame no tee, na ɔsomaa Kokosakyi, Ɔpɛtɛ, sɛ ɔmfa wɔn mmienu no mmra. Wɔn mmienu no kɔɔ. Garden-eggs kaa nsɛm a ɛwɔ nano; Nkruma kaa ne deɛ. Afei, Ɔsoro Nyame no maa nakasamfoɔ sɛ ɔmmɛbu Nkruma atɛn, na ɔmmɛboa Garden-eggs. Ɛno nti, bere a yɛreplant Garden-eggs no, ɔda fam tẽẽ; ɔda Ɔsoro Nyame ne ne community council ase. Na Nkruma deɛ, ɔgyina hɔ tenten; ɔpae mu ka sɛ ɔne Ɔsoro Nyame no rebɛko sɛ wama no atɛntrenee.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
