Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐKƆƆ SO SƐ 'ƆKRAMAN, KRAMAN, NTUMI NNYƐ NE AKORƆMMƆ (AKWAN) NO AYARESA A ANYA DA.

NA Kraman bi wɔ hɔ, na ɛnam akorɔmmɔ suban nti, na ɔntumi nnya ɔbaa mma ɔnware no. Enti ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ kuro bi mu sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔpɛ ɔbaa ama ɔne no aware. Ɔwaree, na ɔne no san baa nakuraa so. Ɔne ne yere tenaa hɔ (berɛ bi), bere a ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ no, ɔsan kɔɔ nakuraa so. Ɛyɛɛ berɛ bi akyi no, ne kunu no somaa nkra sɛ ɔreba hɔ. Ne mmara agya faa ne tuo kɔɔ nwura so sɛ ɔrekɔkum adowa bi de ahoboa ama Kraman ba no. Ɛbɛyɛ nnansa akyi no, Kraman kɔɔ hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ no, ɔhunuu sɛ adowa bi honam wɔ ogya so, adiwo. Bere a anadwo bae no, ɔne ne yere no kɔɔ da. Na Kraman rebɛpue abɔnten akɔyɛ nsuo, berɛ a ɔhunuu sɛ srade no nam no regu ogya no mu. Kraman san baa ne nkyɛn kɔdaa, ɔde ne etire guu sumiiɛ no so na ɔhwɛɛ nam no kyɛɛ. Afei nam no hwam no duu ne nkyɛn, ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛsoa nanso wantumi. A! ɔsɔre kɔɔ baabi a nam no wɔ, na ɔwe ne nyinaa, na ɔsan baa ne nkyɛn kɔdaa. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ anɔpa, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, berɛ a na ne yere rebɛpope no, ɔhwehwɛɛ nam no, sin baako mpo nna hɔ. Ne yere kae sɛ, "O, maame! oh, maame! obi akeka nam no nyinaa. " Kraman no tee saa asɛm yi a, naniwa wui (i.e. na nani awu), na ɔde ne ntoma kataa ne etire so, na ɔdaa hɔ komm. Afei prɛko pɛ ɔsɔreɛ na ɔhyɛɛ kwan; ɔdwane kɔɔ nankasa akuraa, na ɔkaa amaneɛbɔ no, sɛdeɛ ɔkɔdanee ne maame kunu mmara nam, ne sɛdeɛ naniwa wuiɛ wɔ aniwu ho, ne sɛdeɛ ɛno nti na na ɔrehwehwɛ obi a ɔwɔ awuduro ano aduro a wɔde yɛ akorɔmmɔ, na ama wɔama no bi. Nnipa no kae sɛ, "Adanko, Hare no, ɔwɔ aduro bi a ɛyɛ den paa, ahoɔden paa. " Kraman no fii aseɛ, na ɛde hareyɛ bɛduruu Hare akuraa no ase. Ɔkae sɛ, "Agya Hare, metu mmirika kɔ wo nkyɛn kɔhwehwɛ mmoa; Meyɛ ɔfɔmfoɔ, ɔfɔmfoɔ, ɔfɔmfoɔ, na wɔka sɛ wowɔ aduro (nea ɛyɛ ayaresa) ma ɛno, nti me srɛ wo, ma me bi, na nea ɛbɛyɛ wo boɔ biara, mɛtua. " Hare buae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nokware sɛ mewɔ bi, enti kɔfa mprako nam bi brɛ me. "

Kraman no firii aseɛ kɔɔ akuraase bi na ɔnyaa akyi nan baako a ne mu duru yɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i.e. 3s. 6d.) na ɔde bae. Hare no noa, twitwaa mu, guu nnua nketewa so, na ɔyam ɛmo, nkyene ne gyeene, de guu nam so na ɔde guu ogya so. Bere a ogya no kaa no, berɛ kɔɔ so no, ɔtee sɛ Kraman no rebɔ, hyee, e e! Kraman no hwɛɛ no berɛ tenten. Afei Hare no kae sɛ, "Kraman, kɔ da wɔ dan ahodoɔ no mu baako mu, na me nso mɛkɔ da foforɔ mu, na ɔkyena anɔpa no, mɛyi nam yi (ogya no) na mayɛ ho aduro ama wo. " Kraman no kɔdae. Hare no nso kɔdae. Afei na Kraman da so ara da, na nam no hwam no faa ne ɛhwene so baa ne so, nanso wankasa. Afei na ɔsan hu hwam no bio. Ɔkae sɛ, "Agya Hare. " Hare no buae sɛ, "Aane! " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wo akuraa no mu nkura wɔ hɔ, enti mɛkɔ akɔfa nam no, na mede asie me nkyɛn. " Hare no kae sɛ, "Kɔfa no, na me deɛ, me pɛ ne sɛ me nsa bɛka no kosi ɔkyena, ahyɛdeɛ sɛ mɛtumi de ayɛ aduro ama wo. " Enti Kraman no kɔe kɔfaa no. Afei na Kraman no da hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, "Father Hare. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Aane! " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me nkura no bɛwe no, enti mɛto no ahyɛ me sumiiɛ ase, na mada. " Hare kaa sɛ, "Nea me kɔn dɔ ara ne sɛ ɔkyena, bere a nneɛma bɛda adi no, mɛde ayɛ aduro ama wo. " Kraman no san too ne ho bio, ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden, nanso wantumi ansi ne bo. Ɔkae sɛ, "Agya Hare, nkura yi aba me sumiiɛ ase, enti mɛfa nam no ato me etire so, na mɛgyina hɔ kɔsi sɛ nneɛma bɛda adi. " Hare buaa sɛ, "Aane, mekɔ so ka sɛ, nea ɛfa me ho nyinaa ne sɛ mɛgye nam no akɔ ɔkyena ama wo ayɛ aduro. " Kraman no faa nam no sii ne etire so, na ɔgyinaa hɔ. Afei forowe no bi tew kɔɔ nanim, na ɔfee no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Owura Hare, saa nam yi deɛ, saa nkura no aforo me, na wɔabɛn me etire no so, enti mɛkura (nam no) wɔ me ano." Hare buae sɛ, "Oho o! Meka daa sɛ, ɛbɛduru ɔkyena, bere a nneɛma bɛda adi no, mɛma no ayɛ aduro ama wo. " Seesei na ɛyɛ ntɛm wɔ nano, na ɔdii akɔneaba wɔ dan a ɛwɔ hɔ no mu; ɔde ne dua no bɔnee (wɔ nsakraeɛ nnyigyei mu) sɛ, fre! fre! fre! Ɔkae sɛ, "Agya Hare, ma me ɛka wo wɔ saa nam yi ho. " Hare no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na wopɛ sɛ mebɔ wo ho ɛka? " Kraman no buae sɛ, "Me kyerɛ sɛ, sen sɛ nkura yi bɛdidi no, ma metua wo ka. " Hare kae sɛ, "Aane! ma ɔkyena anɔpa mmra. " Ah! Nanso afei Kraman no gyaee no (nam) no kɔɔ menem. Afei ɔhwee ase dae. Ɛduruu anɔpa kɛseɛ no, Hare no kaa sɛ, "Kraman, fa saa nam no bra. " Kraman no buaa sɛ, "Ho, Father Hare, adɛn nti na woante nea meka kyerɛɛ wo ɛnora, sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ me ne wo ɛka wɔ hɔ ma nam no? " Hare no buaa sɛ, "So you have eaten it? " Kraman kaa sɛ, "Yes! I ate it, for I bought it. " Hare no kaa sɛ, "Wo, Kraman, korɔno a wɔfrɛ no korɔno no remma wo nkyɛn da. "

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
