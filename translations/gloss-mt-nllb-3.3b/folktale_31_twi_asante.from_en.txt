Enti, ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔbɛbisa anyame nketewa no mu baako asɛm, na waka akyerɛ no sɛ ɔpɛ abofra. Ɔbosom ketewa no kaa sɛ, "Mɛma wo baako, nanso abofra no deɛ, nadwuma a ɔbɛyɛ nyinaa ne sɛ ɔbɛma woabɔ ka, nanso ɛda bi ɔbɛtua wo ka. " Ɛnyɛ nna mmienu, ɛnyɛ nnansa (wɔbisabisaa bosom ketewa no akyi) na ɔbaa no nyinsɛneeɛ. Ɔwoo - akraman ba a na ɔyɛ - ɛfiri sɛ na ɔnnyin akyɛ. Abofra no nyin bɛyɛɛ aberanteɛ a ne ho yɛ fɛ. Ɛdii da bi a na ɔne ne maame wɔ hɔ, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Maame, ma me sika futuro, na ma menkɔ Mouth-of-the-Sea, kɔtɔ nkyene. " Ɛna no kaa sɛ, "Dodow bɛn? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Asuanu" (i. e. £4). Na maame no faa no maa no, na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ akwantu no mu. Afei, bere a na ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa ɔbarima bi ne ne kraman a ɔyɛ soronko. Ɔkae sɛ, "Fa no bra ma metɔ. " Ɔkraman no wura kae sɛ, "Wuntumi ntɔ. " Abofra no kae sɛ, "Ɛdɔɔso dɛn? " Ɔkraman no wura kae sɛ, "An Asuanu's weight of money futuro" (i. e. £4). Ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛno (ma me), fa £4 yi boɔ yɛ sika futuro. " Ɔgyee kraman no, na ɔde no baa fie. Ɛduruu sɛ ɔsan baeɛ no, ne maame bisaa no sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woankɔ baabi a worekɔ no? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Me de sika a ɛbɛba no tɔɔ kraman. " Ne maame kaa sɛ, "Ho! " Seesei wɔte hɔ a, ɛbɛyɛ bosome baako. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mma, ma me sika a ɛbɛtumi akɔ aguadi. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Na wo deɛ, sɛnea woyɛ no, wode sika a ɛbɛba no bɛsan ato hɔ, nanso wopɛ dodoɔ bɛn? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "An Asuanu and suru's worth of money futuro" (i. e. £5). Ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa no, ɛnneɛ. " Enti ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ faa aguadi kwan no so. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa ɔbarima bi a ɔkura ɔkraman ba. Ɔkae sɛ, "Man, bring that animal that always falls on its feet, that I may buy it. " Ɔkae sɛ, "When I lie down in my room, the pigs gnaw my feet, for that reason I bought it. " Ɔkae sɛ, "I beg you let me have it. " Ɔkae sɛ, "You can't buy it. " Abofra no kae sɛ, "How much will you take for it? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "An Asuanu and suru's worth of money futuro. " Ɔkae sɛ, "So that's why you say, I can't buy it; yi, fa. " Abofra no gyee aboa no, na ɔde no kɔɔ fie. Bere a ɔduruu fie no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Mama, hwɛ nea mede aba yi. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Ah, ɛno ara ne nea wɔaka no. " Abofra no tenaa hɔ ara. Ɛbɛyɛ adaduanan akyi no, ɔkasa kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, Ma me sika daakye na menkɔ aguadi. Ɛna no kaa sɛ, Me sika a ɛwɔ me ho no nyinaa asa, gye Asuasa duru a ɛyɛ sika daakye (i. e. £6); bere a mede eyi bɛma wo, na woakɔ, na woamfa ntɔ nneɛma no, ɛno ne adwuma yi awiei. " Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Mate. " Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔfaa ne bag, na ɔfiri hɔ kɔeɛ, pa! Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa ɔbenfo bi, na ɔkura akraman ba. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Adamfo, fa wo abɔdeɛ no bra, na matɔ. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Menntɔn, ɛfiri sɛ mede gye mani. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mɛtɔ. " Nnomaa no wura kaa sɛ, "Meremfa ntɔn, ɛfiri sɛ menim deɛ ɛbɛtumi ayɛ ama me. " Ɔba no kaa sɛ, "Oh, fa ma me. " Ɔbisaa sɛ, "Wobɛtumi atɔ no anaa? " Ɔba no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Anasa Asu sika a emu duru yɛ futuro" (ɛneɛ £6). Ɔkae sɛ, "So suppose that's why I wouldn't buy it, here is the reference quantity. " Ɔde nnomaa akraman no bae. Ne maame kaa sɛ, "Eyi annyɛ yie sene kan no, enti eyi na wode aba? Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, ɛyɛ nokware. " Ɛneɛ na wɔnyaa sofa bed ne chairs no na wɔkɔfaa ɔhene no baa ne fie. " Na ɔde kawa no kɔɔ nakuraa; na ɔde sika a ɛbɛba daakye ne kawa no kyerɛɛ ne maame. Afei maame no kae sɛ, "Akwaaba Aku, akwaaba Aku.
