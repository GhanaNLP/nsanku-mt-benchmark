Yɛnkyerɛ aseɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ aseɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐKƆƆ SO SƐ YƐBƐHUNU NKRUMA, AGYINAMOA, DAA WƆ VELVƐT SƆRESƆRE SO, BERE A ƆKRAMAN, KRAMAN, BƐDA GYAADE GYA NSO NTAM DAA.

WƆKYERƐ SƐ ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ a na ɔyɛ awerɛhow paa sɛ bere biara a ɔbɛwo abofra no, na awoɔ no wu. Enti ɔyɛɛ nadwene sɛ ɔbɛbisa anyame nketewa no mu baako asɛm no, na waka akyerɛ no sɛ ɔpɛ abofra. Onyame kumaa no kae sɛ, "Mɛma wo biako, nanso abofra no de, ne adwuma nyinaa ne sɛ obegye wo ɛka, nanso da bi obetua wo ka. " Ɛnyɛ nna abien, ɛnyɛ nna abiɛsa (bere a ɔne onyame kumaa no susuw ho akyi) na ɔbaa no nyinsɛnee. Ɔmaa abofra bi awoɔ - na ɛyɛ akɔre ho asɛm, efisɛ na ɛnkyɛ na wanyin. abofra no nyin bɛyɛɛ aberante fɛfɛ. Ɛdii da bi a na ɔne ne maame wɔ hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ, "Maame, ma me sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma, na menkɔ Ano-of-the-po-ɔman no mu, nkɔtɔ nkyene. " Na maame no kae sɛ, "Ɛdɔɔso dɛn? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Asuanu" (i. e. £4). Na maame no faa no kɔmaa no, na ɔhyɛɛ akwantu no ase. Afei, bere a na ɔrekɔ no, na ɔne obarima bi ne ne ɔkraman a ne ho asonsono no hyia. Ɔkae sɛ, "Fa brɛ me ma metɔ. " Kraman wura no kae sɛ, "Worentumi ntɔ. " Aberante no kae sɛ, "Ɛdɔɔso ahe? " Kraman wura no kae sɛ, "Asuanu boɔ a ɛyɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma" (i. e. £4). Ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn ne eyi (ma me), fa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma a ne bo yɛ £4 yi. " Ɔgyee kraman no na ɔde baa fie. Bere a ɔsan bae no, ne maame bisaa no sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wunnuruu beae a obi rekɔ no? " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Mede sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no tɔɔ kraman. " Ne maame kae sɛ, "Ho! " Seesei na wɔte hɔ a, ɛbɛyɛ ɔsrane koro. Ɔkae sɛ, "Maame, fa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma ma me na menkɔ dwadie. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Na wo nso, sɛnea woyɛ daa no, wubegye sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no na woatow agu bio, nanso wopɛ ahe? " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Asuanu ne suru bi na ɛyɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. e. £5). " Ɔkae sɛ, "Fa no, afei. " Enti ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ faa dwadie kwan no so. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa obarima bi a okura agyinamoa. Ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Owura, fa saa aboa a ɛtɔ nanan so daa no ba, na mɛtɔ. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Berɛ a meda me dan mu no, nkura no ka me anan, saa sianteɛ no nti na metɔeɛ. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mesrɛ wo, ma me. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wontumi ntɔ. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wobɛgye sika ahe? " abofra no kaa sɛ, "Wobɛgye sika ahe? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Ananu Asu suru ne sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛneɛ ɛno nti na wose mentumi ntɔ, yi, fa. " Na abofra no gyee agyinamoa no, na ɔde kɔɔ fie. Berɛ a ɔduruu fie no, ɔse, "Ɛna, hwɛ nea mede aba yi. " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Ah, ɛno ara ne nea wɔaka no. " Na abofra no da so wɔ fie hɔ. Ɛbɛyɛ sɛ adaduanan akyi no, ɔkasa kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Ma me sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma na menkɔ dwadie. " Ne maame kae sɛ, "Me ho sika no nyinaa asa, na ade a ɛda nkyɛn ne Asuasa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. e. £6); Bere a mede eyi ma wo, na woakɔ, na woamfa annye nneɛma no, ɛno ne eguadwa yi awieeɛ. " abofra no kaa sɛ, "Metee. " Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔfaa ne kotokuo no, na na ɔrekɔ, pa! Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na ɔne asanteni bi rehyia, na na ɔso aborɔnoma. Ɔkae sɛ, "Adamfo, fa wo abɔdeɛ no bra, na mɛtɔ. " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Meremfa ndɔn, efisɛ m'ani gye ho. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Mɛtɔ. " Nnomaa wura no kae sɛ, "Meremfa ndɔn, efisɛ minim nea ebetumi ayɛ ama me. " abofra no kae sɛ, "Ao, fa ma me. " Ɔkae sɛ, "So wubetumi atɔ? " Abofra no kae sɛ, "Ɛdɔɔso ahe? " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Anasa Asu sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma mu duru (£6). Ɔkae sɛ, "Wususuw sɛ ɛno nti na merempɛ no, hwɛ nkaebɔ dodoɔ. " Ɔde anomaa berebuw no bae. Ne maame kae sɛ, "Eyi nnyɛ nea ɛsen kan no, enti eyi na wode aba yi? " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Nanso, eyi ne nea mede aba yi. " Mprempren (da bi) na abofra no te hɔ (wɔ fie), bere a Aborɔnoma no frɛɛ no sɛ, "Bra. " Bere a ɔforo kɔɔ hɔ no, Aborɔnoma no bɔɔ no amanneɛ sɛ, "Me ara me akuraa so na meyɛ ɔhene, na na merebɛkɔ akwantu, bere a yɔnko bi c me nsɔmu, bere a wofiri wo ayamye mu tɔɔ me, na afei me srɛ wo, sɛ wobɛsan de me akɔ me kuro mu nko ara a, ɛnneɛ wɔbɛda wo ase paa. " Aborɔnoma no kaa sɛ, "Wo reka atorosɛm akyerɛ me, wubeguan. " Aborɔnoma no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ woantumi anhu wo kwan a wobɛtumi ayɛ nea merebisa wo no a, fa hama a etwa to no kyekyere me nan, na wo ne me nkɔ. " Abarɔnoma no faa hama a etwa to no kyekyeree. ɔde kyerɛɛ ne nan so, na ɔde nkakrankakra dii nakyi kɔsii sɛ wɔduruu Pigeon kuro no ho. Wɔduruu kuro no skɛɛte ahorow no akyi no, na mmofra no redi nte. Wɔhunuu no a, wɔkae sɛ, "Ɔhene no ni! Ɔhene no ni! " Wɔn mu baako tuu mmirika kɔka kyerɛɛ Korintofoɔ ɔhene no, nanso wɔkyeree no twitwaa ne menem sɛ, "Worema yɛrekae yɛn awerɛhoɔ a ɛba akyire. " Nanso ɔfoforɔ nso de amanneɛbɔ korɔ no ara kɔɔ bio. Na afei Akwamuhene no kaa sɛ, "Wo, Gyaasehene, wo ara kɔ na kɔhwɛ nea asɛm no nyinaa fa ho. " Ɔkɔɔ kɔhwɛɛ, na ɔsan baeɛ. Ɔkae sɛ, "Aane, ɛyɛ nokware. " Afei na wɔnyaa mpa a wɔasɛn ne ahennneɛma no na wɔkɔfaa Ɔhene no baa ne fie. Wɔkaa amaneɛbɔ no kyerɛɛ abusuakuo no nyinaa, sɛnea ɔrehyɛ akwantu, ne sɛnea onipa bi kyeree no, ne sɛnea abofra yi nam nayamye so atɔ no, ne sɛnea ɛbɛduruu da a ɔde no baa fie. Mpanimfoɔ ne mmabun nyinaa sɔre daa abofra no ase. Ɔhemmaa no de nsuo toa a sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma ahyɛ mu ma bae; mpanimfoɔ no nyinaa, nsuo toa a sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma ahyɛ mu ma, ebiara. Ɔhene no ankasa de ne nsa kyerɛɛ soro, na ɔtee kawa bi maa no. Na ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa kawa yi, na nea wopɛ biara, kawa yi bɛma wo. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Metee. " Na ɔde kawa no kɔɔ nakuraa, na ɔde sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no ne kawa no kyerɛɛ ne maame. Afei maame no kae sɛ, "Akwaaba Aku, akwaaba Aku. " Kan no, bere a na "ɔhyew" ne sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no, na bere a na ɔsan fi nakwantu ahodoɔ mu ba no, na ɔma ne maame akwaaba a, na ɔntie no. Na ɔde amaneɛbɔ maa ne maame sɛ, "Woahunu saa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma ne saa kawa yi anaa? Mɛkɔ akyekye akuraa kɛseɛ ama yɛn atena mu. " Na maame no kae sɛ, "Fa waniwa so denneennen. try your best to do so). " Na abofra no fii aseɛ gyinaa nwura no mu, ɔtwee kawa no firii so de sii fam, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Kawa, tew kwaeɛ ne nwura yi nyinaa ma me. " Na saa baabi no nyinaa bɛyɛɛ clear. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mo boaboa nea moawe no nyinaa ano ma eboɔ no. " Na ɛyɛ saa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mompue afie. " Na ɛtwee afie pii. Ɔkae sɛ, "Kawa, ma nnipa mmra na wɔntena afie yi mu. " Na nnipa no bae. Ɔyɛɛ ne maame Ɔhemmaa, ɔno nso bɛyɛɛ ɔhene. Afei (i.e. na asɛm bi wɔ hɔ a me werɛ fi sɛ mɛka), Kwaku Ananse, Ananse no, na ɔyɛ wɔn adamfo paa. Ɛda bi a na ɔte hɔ (wɔn akuraa foforɔ) no, Kwaku Ananse sii gyinaeɛ sɛ ɔbɛba ne mmeranteɛ yi akuraa ase. (Ɔduruu hɔ,) ɔkaa sɛ, "Ao, maame abofra ketewa, agya abofra ketewa, woayɛ akwannya na woadi yie, na woanhwɛ me bio sɛ wobɛhwɛ me, nanso dɛn na asi a ɛde eyi nyinaa aba? " Ɛneɛ na abofra no kaa amaneɛbɔ no nyinaa kyerɛɛ no. Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Mɛkɔ me akuraa hɔ na masan aba. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ nakuraa hɔ, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nua ba baa no sɛ, "Mɛsoma wo akɔ me adamfo no nkyɛn wɔ baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ, na sɛ wokɔ a, fa bobesa fitaa yi ma no na fa adwene yɛ nea ɔbɛhyɛ wo sɛ yɛ, na bɔ mmɔden sɛ wode wo nsa bɛka saa kawa no ho wɔ kokoam. " Abarimaa no kɔɔ ababaawa no akuraa hɔ. Na ɔbarima no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Na eyi deɛ, mehwɛ sɛ worensan nkɔ, ɛfiri sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wotena me nkyɛn nnansa ansa na woasan aba. " Na ababaawa no kaa sɛ, "Mate. " Seisei na ɔne ababaawa no wɔ hɔ bom, na ɔbarima no kɔhohoroo ne ho, na ɔyii kawa no guu ɛpono bi so, na ababaawa no faa no de kɔɔ wofa, Kwaku Ananse nkyɛn. Ɛduruu sɛ Kwaku Ananse de ne nsa guu so pɛ, ɔyɛɛ kawa no ho dwumadie sɛ ɔbɛsi kuropɔn. Afei, ɔbarima no, bere a ɔbɛhwehwɛɛ ne kawa no, wannya bi. Ɔbɛtee sɛ Ananse akyekye kuropɔn a ɛkyɛn nankasa de. Afei ɔkɔbisaa anyame nketewa a wɔwɔ hɔ no bi. Ɔbosom ketewa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ananse abusuani a ɔbaa hɔ bɛhyiaa wo no, ɔde kɔmaa wofa. Ananse no nso kɔɔ consult (Onyame ketewa no na wɔdaa no adi kyerɛɛ no) sɛ Nkruma, Agyinamoa, ne Ɔkraman, Kraman, bɛkɔ akɔfa kawa no, nti ɔkɔgyee aduro a wɔde bɛsa nam no, de bɛtoo kwan no so, na sɛ mmoa no rekɔ a, sɛ wɔde bi kɔdidi a, wɔrentumi nkɔ baabiara bio. Na ɔbarima (a na wahwere ne kawa) no baa fie, na ɔka kyerɛɛ Agyinamoa ne Kraman sɛ, "Mprempren berɛ no aba sɛ mɛka akyerɛ mo sɛ sianteɛ a medɔɔ mo no, (na ɛyɛ yi) me deɛ bi ayera, na wɔka sɛ ɛwɔ Ananse agyapadeɛ mu, nanso ɛno da adaka bi a ɛwɔ ahomegyeɛ no nyinaa mfinimfini. Na bio, wɔka sɛ ɔde aduro bi afra odwan no honam mu de asisi kwan no so, enti sɛ woba hɔ a, nkɔfa no na mmom huruw fa so. " Kraman no kaa sɛ, "Agyinamoa, woate, wo na wobɛwe no. " Agyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Pue, wo a wugye bere biara fa wo ɛhwene kɔ hwamhwam ne hwamhwam. " Afei wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kasaa pii, na wɔn wura no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ yie, monyɛ yie. na ɛfii aseɛ. " Mmoa mmienu no sii hɔ, na wɔrekɔ so. Afei na berɛ tiawa na aka sɛ wɔbɛduru baabi a nam no wɔ, berɛ a Kraman "tee" nam no hua. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agyinamoa, me yafunu mu yaw bi wɔ me, na merentumi nkɔ so. " Agyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔ so, nsa so eguadwa no hia. " (Kraman) kaa sɛ, "Agyinamoa, merentumi. " Agyinamoa kɔɔ so. Kraman no kɔɔ baabi a nam no wɔ no, ɔweɛ nam no nyinaa, ɔdaa hɔ, na wantumi ankɔ nanim bio. Na Agyinamoa no duru Ananse akuraa ase, na ɔdaa Ananse dan mu, wɔ silin no so. Bere a ɔda hɔ no, ɔhunuu nam bi a ɔretwam. Kyeeka! bere a ɔda ne etire so no. (akura no) kae sɛ, "Nkyere me, asɛm no yɛ dɛn? " Agyinamoa no buae sɛ, "Me wura no nsateaa no ayera, na wɔka sɛ ɛda Ananse adaka a ɛwɔ nnaka no ahomegyeɛ no mfinimfini no mu, na sɛ wutumi kɔfa brɛ me a, mɛma wo kwan. " Akura no kaa sɛ, "Me tumi yɛ saa. " Agyinamoa no kaa sɛ, "Yɛbɛfa no sɛ mema wo kwan, na woakɔfa amma, nanso wuguan? " Akura no kae sɛ, "Sɛ wopɛ a, kyekyere hama a etwa to no kyekyere me asene. " Agyinamoa no faa hama a etwa to no kyekyeree ne asene. Afei Akura no kɔɔ Spider no dan mu, na ɔkekaa ɛtokuro bi a ɛwɔ adaka a ɛgyina ahomegyeɛ no nyinaa mfinimfini no mu. Nkakrankakra ɔyɛɛ mmueano, na ɛbɛyɛɛ kɛse. Ɔfaa mu, sɛ ɔrekɔfa kawa no akɔma Agyinamoa. Ɛduruu sɛ Agyinamoa no de ne nsa too so no, ɔdwanee bɛtoo Kraman no, na na ɔda nea ɔgyaee no hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo da so ara wɔ ha, na ɛhe na saa nam no wɔ? " Kraman no kaa sɛ, "Aane, manhu (nea ɛbɛyɛɛ no), ebia nnipa a wɔwɔ no na wɔyii no kɔe; nanso ɛhe na kawa no wɔ? " Agyinamoa no kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ ha. " Kraman kaa sɛ, "Wɔka sɛ asubonten a ɛda kwan ho no wɔ nsuyiri mu, na sɛnea wo, wotwa nsuo nantew fam no, ɛbɛyɛ sɛ ɛbɛhwe ase, saa ara na fa ma me, ɛfiri sɛ, me deɛ, wonim sɛ metwa nsuo no ani. " Agyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Ɛno ara ni, fa. " Wɔduruu asubonten no ho no, Kraman huruw guu mu. Saa ara na Agyinamoa no nso yɛe. Ntɛm ara na Agyinamoa no twaa mu. Kraman no nso, ɔduruu asuwa no mfinimfini no, ne ho tɔɔ no, na ɔrebɛyɛ ahomegye kɛse a emu dɔ no, na ne kawa no afiri ne ano atɔ nsuo no mu. Ɔtwaa mu baa baabi a Agyinamoa no wɔ. Agyinamoa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛhe na saa kawa no wɔ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛhwee aseɛ firii me ano kɔɔ nsuo no mu. " Agyinamoa no tuu mmirika kɔɔ nsuo no mu, ɔhunuu sɛ apataa kɛseɛ bi retwa mu. Agyinamoa no nso kyeree no. Apataa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dɛn? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me kawa no ahwe aseɛ wɔ asubonten yi mu, enti sɛ wompɛ ɔhaw a, fa nea ɛyɛ me dea no ma me mprempren. Sɛ woamfa amma a, mɛkum wo ntɛm ara. " Apataa no kaa sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔ asubonten ano na memfa nea ɛyɛ mo mma mo. " Bere a wɔn mmienu nyinaa duu sikakorabea hɔ no, Apataa no fɔree, na kawa no firii adi. Agyinamoa no faa no baeɛ na wɔde kyerɛɛ Kraman no. Kraman no kaa sɛ, "Agya, me srɛ wo, sɛ wokɔ a, nka nea asi no ho asɛm. " Agyinamoa no kuraa nano to mu. Wɔduruu fie hɔ no, Agyinamoa ka kyerɛɛ ne wura no nea ɛkɔɔ so nyinaa sɛ, "Kraman no nam a yɛrekɔ no nti, ɔbɛyɛɛ nea ɔrentumi nkɔ akyiri bio. Na bio, na merekɔgye kawa no na mede aba, Kraman no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, bere a ɔtwaa asubonten bi a ɔtwaa nani no, ɛsɛ sɛ mede kawa no ma no. Mede maa no, na ɔtow guu asubonten no mu. Ɛdii kakra pɛ na me nsa bɛtoo so bio. " Nnipa a wɔwɔ hɔ no nyinaa kae sɛ, "Ayeyi mma Agyinamoa. " Na wɔteɛm sɛ, "E! " Ɛnna ɔhene no kaa sɛ, "Wo, Agyinamoa, aduane ayamye biara a meredi no, mehwɛ sɛ mebubu bi gu wo asanka ketewa no mu. Ɛmfa ho kɛtɛ a mɛda so no, sɛ woda so bi pɛ a, mɛda so. Na wo nso, akraman, anadwo nwunu bɛba a, wobɛda ogya a adum no nnyansramma a ɛredɛ no so nko ara. Sɛ woyɛ abaa bɔ nko ara a, folk bɛbɔ wo mpire. " Ɛno nti na nna nyinaa wuhu Agyinamoa no, ɔnda baabiara gye sɛ kɛtɛ a ɛye pa ara so. Afei nso, sɛ wotow aduane bi gu fam ma no a, ɔnni bi (gyina plate so). Nanso Kraman deɛ, daa yɛbɛhunu no sɛ ɔda adibea hɔ wɔ da no gya nsõ so. Afei nso wobɛhunu sɛ ɔwɔ hɔ a wɔrebu no, ɔbɛteɛm sɛ, "Kao! " Ne nyinaa wɔ berɛ a wɔsomaa wɔn no akawnto so (wɔ kawa eguadwa yi so).

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi mo nnye nni, na ahomegyeɛ no monkamfo me (asɛmka no).
