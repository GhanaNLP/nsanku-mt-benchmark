SƐNEA BABADUA NO NYAA NSƆMU NO (WƆ NE DUA SO)

Ɔbea bi nyinsɛnee (bere bi), na ɔkɔɔ abosonsomfo no biako nkyɛn kɔbisabisaa ne ho asɛm (wɔ abofra no ho). Na onyame no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "It is probable that when you bear the child it will die, but I will do all I can to give it to you, but the thing which I must tell you is this, the child is (really) the child of a Queen up there with God, the Almighty God, and when you bear it, don't let it do anything, but when you rise up, you must bathe it, oil it, and take it, and set it on a stool. " Ɛyɛ nokware. Ɛhɔ na ɔwoo no. (Sɛnea mmofra nyinaa te) wɔ anansesɛm mu no, ɛnkyɛ na wanyin. Wɔfrɛɛ abofra no schooness. Sɛnea na onyame kumaa no akyerɛ no, ɛna no sɔre no, ɔhohoroo no, de ngo guu ne so, na ɔyɛɛ aduan maa no, na afei ɔkɔɔ nafum. Ɛnneɛ, da bi a na ɔnni berɛ a ɔde bɛnoa aduane biara mma no, ɔhyɛɛ ne ba abaawa, Soul, sɛ, Me rekɔ afuom, enti yɛ biribi ma Akosua Dentaa nni. Na maame no fii aseɛ. Afei, bere a Soul wiee borɔdɔma no, ɔkae sɛ, "Akosua Dentaa, bra ha na bɔ saa _fufu_ yi, wo ne wo Queen-Mother asɛm no. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Meremma. " Soul kae sɛ, "Bra, wo ne wo ahantan. " Na Akosua Dentaa sɔre kɔɔ, ɔkɔgyee poma no, ɔbɔɔ _fufu_ no. Ɛduruu sɛ ɔwieeɛ no, ɔkɔdaa dan no mu, ɔfaa ne ntama, na ɔkataa ne ti so. Ɔkra no kɔɔ (dii nakyi), ɔkae sɛ, "Denta"; mmuaeɛ biara nni hɔ. Ɔyii ntama no firii ne ti so; na Akosua Dentaa awu; ɔde ntama no sii ne so. Ɛnkyɛreeɛ, ɛna no firi afuo mu baeɛ. Ɛduruu sɛ ɔbaeɛ no, na ɔnhwehwɛ ne ba no (immediately), na mmom ɔkɔɔ adididan no mu sɛ ɔrekɔwie aduane no. Ɛnna ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Soul, ɛhe na Denta wɔ? " Ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Grandmother, ne aduane mpo a meyɛɛ no na mede sii hɔ no, ɛhɔ na ɛda so ara gyina. " Ɛna no kaa sɛ, "Mate. " Ɛna no de ne ho kɔhyɛɛ dan no mu sɛ ɔrekɔfa ntama (to wear) na wafiri abɔnten akɔhwehwɛ ne ba no. Bere a ɔkɔɔ dan no mu no, na biribi a ɛyɛ kusuu da hɔ wɔ akutu bi mu. Ɔmaa ntama no so, na ɔhunuu sɛ Akosua Dentaa awu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Soul, woka sɛ Denta akɔ abɔnten, na afei na ɔwɔ ha, awu? " Soul kaa sɛ, "Agyapa a menhunuu no. " Ɛneɛ ɔbaa no kyekyeree ntama kyekyeree nasen na ɔde ne ba (a wawu) no kɔɔ nakyi, na ɔne no baa abɔnten so, na ɔsu; na akuraa no mu panyin no baeɛ, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa wo ba (a wawu) yi firi wakyi na yɛnkɔsie no. " Ɔbaa no buaeɛ sɛ:

"Na me ne me ba no bɛsua hardta, na me nyinsɛn no, me ne no kɔɔ consult nsuo-gazer no.

Wɔka sɛ, Ɛyɛ Ɔhemmaa Ɛna ba.

Mepɛ sɛ obi kɔka sɛ, Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba, Siankwa Adamowa ba, oyi, me ba, meremfa no nsie ha. "

Na ɔfiri hɔ kɔɔ akuraa bi ase, na ɔduruu hɔ no, akuraa no mu panyin no kae sɛ, "Fa abofra no mfi (wo akyi) na bra mma memmɛsie no, ɛfiri sɛ yɛn taboo (deɛ ɛte sɛɛ) no. " Nanso ɔbaa no kae sɛ:

"Na me ne me ba no bɛsua hardta, na me nyinsɛn no, me ne no kɔɔ consult nsuo-gazer no.

Wɔka sɛ, Ɛyɛ Ɔhemmaa Ɛna ba.

Mepɛ sɛ obi kɔka sɛ, Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba, Siankwa Adamowa ba, oyi, me ba, meremfa no nsie ha. "

Na ɔsan fii ase bio. Ɔkɔɔ, na ɔkɔɔ, na ɔkɔɔ, wɔ kwaeɛ akɛseɛ bi mu, na afei deɛ, abofra no asɛe, na ɔyii no (nakyi) de guu hɔ, na ɔsan kɔɔ fie. Na kuro no mu panyin no bisaa no sɛ, "Ɛhe na abofra no wɔ? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me na mede no kɔtoo hɔ. " Nkuro no mu panyin no kaa sɛ, "W'ani abere, anka sɛ woma no kwan wɔ ha a, anka ɛsɛ sɛ yɛsie no wɔ Asamanfoɔ kwaeɛ no mu, ɛno na ɛbɛyɛ nea ɛfata. " Saa na mpanimfoɔ no ka sɛ, "Sɛ wo ba wu a, ɛnneɛ di awerɛhow wɔ karipe mu; mma ɛnnyɛ wo yaw. Ɛfiri sɛ saa ɔbaa no yɛɛ saa, na ɔtoo ne ba no gui berɛ a na ɛnsɛ sɛ ɔyɛ saa. " Na afei ɔbaa no kae sɛ, "Soul yi deɛ, fa no na fa no gu moto mu na bɔ no ma me. " Ɛneɛ na nkurasefoɔ no kɔɔ hɔ (baabi a na Soul wɔ no, na wɔbɔ no bɔ no nnyigyei) Pu! Pu! Pu! na wɔbɔɔ no bɔɔ no kɔsii sɛ ɔbɛyɛɛ dɛɛdɛɛ paa. Na afei na "the kind one" no da so ara wɔ hɔ; sɛ wɔbɔ no a, na ɛyɛ den, na sɛ wɔbɔ no nso a, na ɛtu kɔfam Babadua no ho. Ɛno nti na Babadua no wɔ "bone" (wɔ ne dua so), kan no na ɛyɛ trom, trom, kyerɛ sɛ ɛyɛ dɛɛdɛɛ paa.

Me kyerɛwtohɔ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na fa a aka no nso kamfo me.
