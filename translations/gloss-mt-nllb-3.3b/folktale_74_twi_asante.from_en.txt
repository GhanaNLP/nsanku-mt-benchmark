Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

KWAMENA, ANTIRED ANT-HILL A ƐGYINA NHINI A AGYINA PINTINN NO NTAM

WƆKYERƐ SƐ, na Ɔhemmaa bi babaa wɔ hɔ, na obiara a wɔbɛma no awareɛ no, ɔbɛka sɛ ɔmpɛ no. Nanso, Ɔhene a ɔwɔ Nsaman-saman, obi a wɔfrɛ no Adu Gyamfi, baeɛ na ɔwaree saa Ɔhemmaa no abofra no. Ɔhemmaa no ba no ka kyerɛɛ ne kunu no sɛ, "Me ne wo bɛkɔ wo kuro no mu. " Na ɔbarima no buae sɛ, "Ma me nkɔ (di kan), na sɛ Adae tɔ wɔ Kwasiada a, ɛdwoada a ɛdi hɔ yi mɛba abɛfa wo. " Na ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "Aane, me ne wo bɛkɔ abom. " Enti wɔkɔe. Bere a wɔrekɔ no, wɔhunuu kuropɔn bi a dadeɛ pono wɔ ne kwanano, na dadeɛ atwa kuro no ho ahyia. Afei, wɔ kuro yi mu, sɛ wo busuani biara anwu a, (na) wogyina ɛpono no akyi teɛɛ sɛ obi mmua wo. Afei, Ɔhemmaa Maame yi ba no, ne busuani biara anwu da. Ɔkasa no ne nea aka akyi no (i. e. Me werɛ fi sɛ mɛka saa) bere a Adu Gyamfi bɛduruu dadeɛ pono no so no, na wakɔ ne fie; ɔne Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no ankɔkasa bio. Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no babea ne ne akoa abeawa, Ɔkra, na wɔtenaa hɔ, na wɔgyinaa dadeɛ pono no akyi, na wɔannya kwan ankɔ fie. Na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no maa ne nne so (na ɔtoo dwom):

"Adodowa, bu me kwan, ɛyɛ me na me aba yi, Adodowa Kwasi Kampire, bu me kwan, Adodowa, bu me kwan o. "

Nanso Adodowa ammu no. Ɔno nso antumi anhu kwan a ɔbɛfa so asan aba ne kuro mu. Afei ɔne Ɔkra kɔɔ nhini a agyina pintinn ntam. Na ɔhene ba no faa kɔbere dadesɛn kɔtenaa so, na ɔfaa foforɔ kataa ne etire so, na Ɔkra bɛtenaa ne nkyɛn. Ɛno nti, sɛ wokɔ baabi a nhini a agyina pintinn no, ɛhɔ na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba ne ne babaa, Kra, te; wɔadane antwumayɛbea a wɔfrɛ no "Kwamena" a ɛgyina gyinabea so gyinabea.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
