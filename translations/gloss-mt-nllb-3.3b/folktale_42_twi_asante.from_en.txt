Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ ƆSEBƆ, ƆSEBƆ NE ADOWA, ADOWA NO BƐYƐƐ NNAMFOƆ

ƆKUROTWIAMANSA (He-who-holds-the-nation-in-fear) na ɔte hɔ, na ɔkaa sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ obi a ɔbɛpɛ no asɛm (and not be afraid to tell him so to his face). Mmoa no nyinaa, baako mpo antumi anyɛ saa. Afei, na adwoa ketewa no te hɔ; ɔkae sɛ, "Agya Beast, dɛn na woka fa me ne wo ho sɛ yɛbɛyɛ atamfo? " Beast, the Beast, kae sɛ, "Ɛno ara ne nea mepɛ. " Enti wɔbɛyɛɛ atamfo. Beast no bɔɔ palm-wine. Off the Beast went, drank the palm-wine, and became drunk; he could not rise, and he lay under the palm-tree. Anwummere no sunsuma mu no, na Ɔsebɔ no aka sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwɛ nagyapadeɛ. Berɛ a ɔkɔɔ no, na Adowa no anom ne nsa na ɔda hɔ a wanom nsa. Ɔsono no kae sɛ, "Eye, wo na woka sɛ me ne wo bɛyɛ atamfo a ɛda adi pefee; dɛn nti, asono mpo ntumi nyɛ matamfo a ɔda adi pefee, akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ worehyia ɔhaw. " Ɔsono no kɔɔ hɔ na ɔtwaa mmɛw nhaban, na ɔpam kɛntɛn a wɔde mmɛw nhaban ayɛ, na ɔde Adowa no guu mu; na ɔfaa no, na ɔsoaa no wɔ ne ti so. Bere a ɔreba na ɔreyɛ adu ɔkwan no fã no, na Ɛdwoa no abere. Ohui sɛ Ɔsebɔ no kura no. Ɛdwoa no hwɛɛ soro, na ɔbran a ne honam ani yɛ mmerɛw bi rekotwa wɔ hɔ. Ɛdwoa no gyee ne ti nhwi. Afei ɔkasa ne nea aka (te sɛ nea mepaa ho ka), na ɛdwoa no mmoa bin ahorow bi wɔ nnuaba ahaban mu. Na ɔsono no nnim nea ɛrekɔ so no ho hwee; na ɔkura no (sɛnea ɔdwenee) fie. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Saa ɔbenfoɔ panyin, Adowa no, ɔkaa sɛ ɔne me bɛyɛ atamfoɔ, enti mede no aba; fa palm-nuts gu gya no so. " Ɔseane no maame ka kyerɛɛ ne ba no sɛ, "Soup no ayɛ krado, enti fa no bra mma memmɛkum no. " Bere a ɔseane no hwɛɛ basket no mu no, na hwee nni mu. Na ɔwɔ hɔ, na nea aka ara ne ne supplies. Ɔsono no kae sɛ, "Ɔma, ɔkɔe, na nanantwi nkukuo da so ara wɔ hɔ; momma yɛnfa mfa nsie nkukuo no mu. " Enti wɔde guu nkukuo no mu, na wɔwiee aduane no nyinaa (cooking); na wɔtenaa ho. Ɔbaa kumaa no de ne nsateaa guu nkukuo no mu na ɔfee no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, Kwadwo (for the Bear's birthday was a Monday), na eyi yɛ ne pet peeve nko ara, anka ne nam renyɛ dɛn? Anka ɛyɛ dɛ sen tiger-nuts, enti baabiara a ɔbɛtumi ada no, bɔ mmɔden na fa no mmra. " Na Bear no fii nakwantu no ase. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na Adowa no da hɔ bio a ɔnom nsa. Ɔsebɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Oh, nea ɛdi kan no, sɛ meyɛɛ me ho nkwaseasɛm a, afei mɛkɔ fie akɔfa adaka bi na masan aba. " Ɔsebɔfoɔ no firii fie, yii natadeɛ nyinaa (firi ne adaka mu), de sii hɔ, na ɔde adaka no ne safe no kɔɔ palm dua no so. Ɔfaa Adowa no, de hyɛɛ adaka no mu, too mu, faa no, na ɔde no kɔɔ natifi. Berɛ a ɔduruu fie hɔ no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔma, mede no aba; bue dan mu pon no na memfa no nkɔ mu. " Ɔbaa kumaa no buee mu, na ɔde no kɔtoo mu. Afei ɔsono no ka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Merekɔ Tall-grass-country akɔhwehwɛ yam, nkyene, ne lime yam, na mede aba, na yɛbɛfa Adowa no na yɛayɛ no froze a yɛbɛtumi adi. " Ɔsebɔ no fii nakwantu no ase. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, na ɔbaa kumaa no rebɔ palm-nuts, na berɛ a Adowa de nani too ɛtokuro no so no, ɔhunuu ɔbaa panyin no. Ɔtwee kaa sɛ, "Obaabun, wo ho yɛ mmɔbɔ; Sɛ wubue ma me a, mɛbɔ wo." Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ, "Wo, Ɔtwee, wubeguan." Ɔtwee buae sɛ, "Sɛ wubue ma me a, (anyame nkumaa) Aserampon, ne Ta' Kese, ne Dwomo, ne Apia, ne Konkroma, ne Ta' Kora, ne Krakye Dente, kunkum me. Sɛ wopɛ mpo a, to apon no nyinaa mu. Ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, "Mate. " Ɔtoo apon no mu, na ɔbuee (dan no mu apon a) Ɔtwee no wɔ hɔ. Ɔtwee no bae, na ɔkura afiri no mu, na ɔbɔɔ mmɛw nnuaba no. Ná ɔbaa kumaa no resi fam sɛ ɔrekɔfa biribi afi fam, bere a Ɔtwee no faa afiri no mu na ɔbɔɔ no ti akyi, pe! na owui. Ɔtwe no yii ne honam ani nhama na ɔde kataa ne ho, twitwaa ne nam no na ɔde too ogya so. Ɛnkyɛe na Kwadwo duu hɔ. Ɔtwe no kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woakyɛ akyɛ saa? Me abue ama Ɔtwee no na makum no, ha mpo ne ne nam wɔ gya so. " Ɔseane no kaa sɛ, "Well done, Apeafo. " Ɔno nso faa nneɛma a ɔde aba no, de guu soup-stew no mu. Na wɔawie aduane no yɛ; bere a wɔkɔtenaa aseɛ sɛ wɔredidi no, Ɔseane no faa dompeɛ bi. Bere a ɔkyekyeree no, na ɛkɔ pu! pu! Ɔtwe no guu ne hwene mu sɛ, Wo maame nnompe na worekyea yi. Ɔsono no kae sɛ, Dɛn na woreka yi? Ɔsono no buae sɛ, Mekae sɛ saa Ɔtwe yi anyin yiye; ne nnompe no mu yɛ den. Ɔsono no kae sɛ, Ɛnte saa ara, me ara mpo mentumi mmu mu. Afei ɔsono no de ahurututu no guu nano, na ɔde nnyigyei te sɛ FEAO! FEAO! Ɔtwee no kae sɛ, "Ah, ɛno ne soup-stew a wofiri wo maame ti mu a worehwie agu wo anom. " Ɔsono no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na woka yi? " Ɔsono no buae sɛ, "Na me werɛ refrɛ, sɛ ne porridge mpo (a na yɛdwen sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ), ne nam no de, woahu dɛ no? " Ɔsono no kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ koraa, koraa, koraa sɛ yɛbɛka saa mpo. " Afei, na wɔawie aduane no. . Ɔtwee fie a ɛwɔ hɔ no nyinaa, ɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔsebɔfoɔ no sɛ, "Mɛtow nwura no agu mfinimfini. " Ɔsebɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ, ɛnneɛ, ɛdeɛn na ɛwɔ me ho, meyɛ ɔbaa? " Ɔtwee no kɔduruu mfinimfini. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ho wɔ hɔ, Ɔsebɔfoɔ! Ho wɔ hɔ, Ɔsebɔfoɔ! Sɛ ɛnyɛ ɔkwasea a, sɛ ɛnyɛ aboa a, anka mɛkum wo maame na mayɛ no soup-stew ama wo adi? Ɔsebɔ no kae sɛ, "Mehunu wo (a woda so ara gyina hɔ, sɛ meba wo nkyɛn a)? " Drag no kae sɛ, "Wo bɛtwa me mpo mu. " Ɔsebɔ no tuu mmirika, yiridi! yiridi! (na ɛyɛ ne nan nnyigyei), na Drag no nso fii ase tuu mmirika, prada! prada! prada! Ɔtwee no bɛduruu ne fie, na ɔyeraa kɔɔ ne dan mu. Bere a ɔsii (fie no), na onnyaw kwan biara wɔ mu, gye kwan biako a na wayɛ no. Bere a Ɔtwee no baa mu no, ɔtoo napon no mu, bam! Afei, hwɛ sɛnea Ɔbɔntene no bɛyɛ, na onhuu ɔkwan a Ɔtwee no faa so kɔɔ mu no koraa. Ɔsebɔfoɔ no kɔɔ akyirikyiri kɔtoo mmoa no nyinaa, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Mommra, na yɛnkɔkyere Ɔtwee no mma me, ɛfiri sɛ nkwaseasɛm a wayɛ me yi, mentumi nka nsɛm no nyinaa nkyerɛ mo kosi sɛ mobɛkyere no ama me. Mmoa no nyinaa baa hɔ wɔ Ɔtwee no fie. Bere a Ɔtwee no tee sɛ mmoa no aba ne fie hɔ sɛ wɔrebɛkyere no, ɔfaa ɛmo kɔkɔɔ, nkyene, ne ginger, na ɔyam no guu hɔ. Seesei mmoa no nyinaa gyina hɔ hyia. Eyi kɔhwɛɛ saa obi no anim. Ɛnna Okusie, Okisi no, kae sɛ, Ɔkwakuo, Kwaadu no wɔ hɔ, na ɔwɔ dua tenten, enti ma ɔnkɔ soro kan, na yɛmfa ne dua no nkɔ fie. Ɔkwakuo, Kwaadu no, sii hɔ sɛ ɔkra! ɔkra! ɔkra! na ɔforo kɔɔ soro. Ɔtenaa dan no atifi, nanso ankyɛ na ɔbɔɔ ne ani hwɛɛ Ɔtwee no, Ɔtwee no faa ɛmo a watwitwa no, de guu nano, na ɔhwie guu Kwaadu ani so. Afei Kwaadu no gyaee na ɔhwee ase. Mmoa no bae na wohui sɛ biribi wɔ nani mu. Ɔbaako kaa bi, na ɔfee ne nsateaa. Ɔkaa sɛ, Oh, nnamfoɔ, mommra (and see) nea Grasshopper akɔfa aba. Supports no nyinaa baeɛ. Wɔdɔɔ, na wɔdɔɔ, na wɔdɔɔ. Ɛno nti na Grasshopper, Kwaadu no, wɔ nani a ɛmu dɔ saa. Afei na wɔka sɛ, Kokotee (Bush-pig), wo deɛ, woretu kwan daa, ntu! ntu! enti wobɛtumi akɔ fie hɔ. Bere a Ɔtwee no tee saa asɛm no, ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔhyɛɛ ne sekan no ano a ɛyɛ nnam paa. Bush-pig no tutuu na ɔtutuu, na ne hwene puee fie hɔ. Ɛnna Ɔtwee no faa ne sekan no, de sii Bush-pig no ano, na ɔretwitwa. Bush-pig no kaa sɛ, Ao, Bush-pig Asamoa, ɔretwitwa mano. Mmoa a na wɔgyina nakyi (na wɔantumi ante nea ɔreka no yie) no kaa sɛ, Well done, Grasshopper; press your eye, and bring him out. Kokotee, Bush-pig no, yii ne ti, ɔne nano a na mogya gu mu. Bere a mmoa no huu sɛ Kokotee Asamoa, Bush-pig no, nano atwa no, wɔfiri saa beaeɛ no, wɔbaa akyirikyiri, na wɔtee! (ɛno ne wɔn nnyigyei a ɛyɛ hu). Saa kwan yi so na Bush-pig no nyaa hwene a ɛyɛ kusuu. Afei, bere a mmoa no dwanee no, na Ɔsebɔ, Ɔsebɔ no, nhu nea ɔbɛyɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Anyhow, I shall go nowhere else; here I remain forever, till I die, unless I kill Adowa, the Dragon. Bere a Adowa tee saa asɛm no, afei ɔfaa (the Beast's) maame wedeɛ no na ɔde kyekyeree ne ho wɔ mu, na ɔforoo fie hɔ kɔɔ fam wɔ akyi. Afei ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔɔ Beast no nkyɛn. Ɔkaa sɛ, Oh, Kwadwo, dɛn na ama wo ho ayɛ wo dɛm saa, adɛn nti na worehwere wo ho wɔ Dragon ho? Momma yɛnkɔ fie na yɛnhyehyɛ yɛn baabi, na yɛnsan mmra. Wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ fie. Ɔtwee no ka kyerɛɛ Ɔsebɔfoɔ no sɛ, Yi wo nneɛma nyinaa na fa ma me, na mɛkora so ama wo. Ɔsebɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Ɛnyɛ adwene bɔne. Afei ɔyii ne nneɛma nyinaa de maa Ɔtwee no. Ɛhɔ ara na Ɔtwee no de ne nsa guu wɔn so, de wɔn siee hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ baabi kɔgyinaa hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ho wɔ hɔ, Ɔsebɔ! Ɔbuaa sɛ, Yiw, onua, yiw! Ɔkaa sɛ, Sɛ ɛnyɛ wo na woyɛ ɔkwasea, sɛ ɛnyɛ wo na woyɛ aboa kɛseɛ a, anka metumi akum wo maame na mayɛ no soup-stew ama wo adi? Afei nso, me daadaa wo na me faa wo nneɛma nyinaa. Ɔsebɔ no kae sɛ, Mehyia wo wɔ hɔ (sɛ meba a)? Ɔtwee no kae sɛ, Wobɛtwam me mpo. Ɛneɛ na Ɔsebɔ no tu kɔɔ ne nkyɛn, kiri! Kiri! Kiri! Nanso, Ɔtwee no nso twaa mu. Ɔkɔɔ ne fie kɔtoo ɔpon no mu, bam! Afei ɔpɔnkɔ no gyinaa hɔ, na ne moma so yɛɛ den; na ne dua no wosow fi nkyɛn kɔ nkyɛn (ɛyɛ nnyigyei te sɛ) frɛ! frɛ! frɛ! Na ontumi nnya asɛm biara nka. Awiei koraa no ɔka kyerɛɛ Draw no sɛ, "Meka Dwoda ntam sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ woba, na yɛnyɛ nnamfo. " Draw no kae sɛ, "Meremma, dodow biara a wopɛ, gye sɛ woyɛ nhyehyɛe ma yɛnom anyame no. " Beast no kae sɛ, "Mepene so, ɛnneɛ merentumi nyɛ wo hwee da. " Draw no ne Beast no bae sɛ wɔrebɛma anyame no anom. Ɔtwee no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Nkyere me da; sɛ wuhyia me a, na sɛ wɔkyere me wɔ afiri mu a, gyaa me. " Ɔsoro Bɛnnaa no kaa sɛ, "Mate. " Na ɔsoro no nso kasae sɛ, "Ɛwɔ ɔkwan a wobɛfa so no so mpo, mefa me nsa, na metwitwa so, na ama woahu sɛ me ne wo ntam nni asɛm biara. " Ɛno nti, bere a ɔsoro bɛnnaa biara wɔ hɔ no, ɔnkyere. Ɔtwee no. Afei nso, sɛ asau bi kyere Ɔtwee no a, ɔkɔ na ogyae no.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
