Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

ABISAA ABISAA

ƐDII BERE BI A NA MMABUN BAAKO MMIƐNSA WƆ HƆ, na wɔn nyinaa dɔ ababaawa bi. Afei mmeranteɛ baasa yi firi Bontuku kɔɔ Cape Coast sɛ wɔrekɔtɔ nneɛma. Bere a wɔduruu Cape Coast no, na wɔn mu baako wɔ ahwehwɛ. Ɔfaa ahwehwɛ no, na ɔtwitwirii bi de sii ahwehwɛ no so, na ɔhwɛɛ mu. Ɔhunuu sɛ wɔn dɔfoɔ no awu, na ɔda veranda-room a ɛda hɔ fann. Ɔkae sɛ, "Mfɛfo, yɛn dɔfo no awu. " Wɔn mu biako kae sɛ, "Mɛma yɛadu Bontuku ntɛm ara sɛ yɛbɛkɔ asie no. " Ɔfoforo nso kae sɛ, "Sɛ wutumi ma yɛadu hɔ a, mɛma no asɔre ntɛm ara. " Afei obiako kɔfaa aduru, de guu wɔn nkotodwe so, na wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ. Ɛhɔ ara na wɔduruu hɔ. Ɔfoforo nso kɔfaa aduru bi, na ɔde guu kɔbere yam bi mu, na ɔfaa ne nantwi aba no, na ɔde guu aduru no mu, na ɔde guu funu no so. Ɔkae sɛ, "Funu, nyan; funu, nyan! " na ɔsɔree. Afei nnipa baasa yi mu hena na ɔyɛɛ adwuma yiye?
