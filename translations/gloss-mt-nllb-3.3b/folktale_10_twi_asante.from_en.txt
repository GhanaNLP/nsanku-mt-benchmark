SƐNEA ANENE KƆN, KWAAKWAADABI NO, BƐYƐEE NA NE AKYIRI YƐƐ TUNTUM

Ɔbaa bi a wɔfrɛ no Ata (The Duck) de ne ba kɔɔ splash (for fish). Bere a wɔduruu asubɔnten no ho no, ne ba no kae sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔ nsuo no atifi. " Nanso, ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔ asuogya, ɛfiri sɛ asuogya no wɔ asuogya hɔ. " Ne ba no kae sɛ, "Me deɛ, merekɔ asuogya no atifi, ɛfiri sɛ asuogya no wɔ asuogya hɔ. " Enti ne maame kɔɔ asuogya no ase. Wɔkyɛɛ hɔ pii, na ne ba no nyaa bi, na ɔbaa bɛdii nakyi bɛhwehwɛɛ ne maame. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na ne maame calabash no rekyinkyin nsuo no ani. Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ah! ɛnyɛ baako, ɛnyɛ mmienu, ɛyɛ maame calabash. " Na ɔmaa ne nne so su frɛɛ ne maame sɛ:

"Meka kyerɛɛ mo, na moantie, meka kyerɛɛ mo, na moantie.

mekae sɛ, 'Ɔma, yɛnkɔ asuogya, ɛfiri sɛ asuogya na nkaeɛ nketewa no te';

Na ɔkaeɛ sɛ ɔrekɔ nsuo no ase, ɛfiri sɛ nsuo no ase na nkaeɛ nketewa no te.

Na dɛn na menyɛ? Alas me maame, Ata, mhm! "

Na Akrɔma no baeɛ; ɔkaa sɛ, "Abofra, wo dwom no yɛ dɛ, to na mɛyi wo maame ama wo. " Na abofra no maa ne nne so:

"Meka kyerɛɛ mo, na moantie, meka kyerɛɛ mo, na moantie.

mekae sɛ, 'Ɔma, yɛnkɔ asuogya, ɛfiri sɛ asuogya na nkaeɛ nketewa no te';

Na ɔkaeɛ sɛ ɔrekɔ nsuo no ase, ɛfiri sɛ nsuo no ase na nkaeɛ nketewa no te.

Na dɛn na menyɛ? Alas me maame, Ata, mhm! "

Na Crocodile no faa ne (magic) aboa tail-switch no bɔɔ nsuo no ani, nanso nsuo no gyinaa nea ɛgyina. Na ɔno (Crocodile no) kɔɔ so. Na Kokosakyi, Ɔpɛtɛ, baa nkyɛn, na ɔno nso kaa sɛ, "Monto dwom no mma me ntie, na manya wo maame afiri ama wo. " Na abofra no too dwom no sɛ:

"Meka kyerɛɛ mo, na moantie, meka kyerɛɛ mo, na moantie.

mekae sɛ, 'Ɔma, yɛnkɔ asuogya, ɛfiri sɛ asuogya na nkaeɛ nketewa no te';

Na ɔkaeɛ sɛ ɔrekɔ nsuo no ase, ɛfiri sɛ nsuo no ase na nkaeɛ nketewa no te.

Na dɛn na menyɛ? Alas me maame, Ata, mhm! "

Na ɔno nso de ne (magic) tail bɔɔ asubɔnten no anim, nanso hwee ankɔ so. Na ɔno nso kɔɔ so. Na (seesei) Anene, Kwaakwaadabi no, nso bɛkaa ho. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abofra, adɛn nti na wosu saa? Su na ma mente sɛ metumi ayi wo maame ama wo. Na abofra no too dwom sɛ:

"Meka kyerɛɛ mo, na moantie, meka kyerɛɛ mo, na moantie.

mekae sɛ, 'Ɔma, yɛnkɔ asuogya, ɛfiri sɛ asuogya na nkaeɛ nketewa no te';

Na ɔkaeɛ sɛ ɔrekɔ nsuo no ase, ɛfiri sɛ nsuo no ase na nkaeɛ nketewa no te.

Na dɛn na menyɛ? Alas me maame, Ata, mhm! "

Kwaakwaadabi no faa aduru kuruwa bi tow guu nsuo no mu, na ɔde ne poma bɔɔ nsuo no ani, na nsuo no mu kyekyɛɛ mmienu; na ɔkaa sɛ, "Monto dwom no", na abofra no too dwom. Na ɔhuu ne maame ti nhwi; na ɔserewee kakra. Ɔkae sɛ, "Monto dwom bio," na ɔtoo dwom. Na ne maame pue baa abura no so. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ so nnwom. " Na abofra no too dwom, na ne maame pue bɛduruu ne nan ho. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Tumi to dwom bio"; na ɔtoo dwom. Ne maame puee prɛko pɛ na ɔgyinaa bench no so. Ata, Ntama no, kae sɛ, "Kwaakwaadabi, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mefa de da wo ase? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, mempɛ hwee koraa, kɔtɔ ntama tuntum ne fitaa. " Ata tuu mmirika kɔtɔɔ bi, na ɔde no baa Kwaakwaadabi, na Kwaakwaadabi no de tuntum-buluu no kataa ne ho, na fitaa no kyekyeree ne kɔn ho. Ɛno nti na wubehu Kwaakwaadabi no, nakyi yɛ tuntum, nanso ne kɔn no yɛ fitaa. Ɛyɛ akyɛdeɛ a ɔnya firii Ata, Nta abusua no hɔ.

Me kyerɛwtohɔ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na fa a aka no nso kamfo me.
