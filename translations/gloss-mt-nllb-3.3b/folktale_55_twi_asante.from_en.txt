Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA SPEAR-GRASS BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU

Ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bi baeɛ na Ntikuma hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔrebɛkɔ hwehwɛ aduane a ɔde bɛbrɛ nagya, Agya Ananse, Ananse. Ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ akuraase bi, na ɛhɔ na ɔne Aberewa nana kɔhyiaa. Na aberewa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Me ba, adɛn nti na obiara ntumi mma ha a, woaba ha? Ntikuma buae sɛ, Ɛyɛ ɛkɔm nti, na me aba hwehwɛ biribi a mɛkɔ adidi. Aberewa nana kaa sɛ, Kɔ me afuw hɔ, nanso bayere a ɛbɛka sɛ Pluck me, pluck me, nnyɛ no, nanso bayere a ɛbɛka sɛ Dont pluck me, dont pluck me, pluck no na fa ba. ɛ. Ntikuma kɔɔ so. Wɔ bayerɛ no bi kae sɛ, Pluck me, pluck me; afoforɔ nso kae sɛ, Dont pluck me, dont pluck me. Ɛneɛ na Ntikuma tuu nea ɛka sɛ, Dont pluck me, dont pluck me. Ɔde wɔn baa fie hɔ, ɔyii wɔn ho. Afei, aberewa nana kaa sɛ, Gyae ankasa fa no ma aduane, na fa nwoma ahoroɔ no gu kuku no mu gu ogya no so. Ntikuma yii no, faa nwoma ahoroɔ no gu gya no so. Bere a wɔasiesie wɔn ho no, ɔyii no firii ogya no so, yii kuku no emu nsɛm no, na emu nneɛma no nyinaa adane aduane pa. Ntikuma kae sɛ, Mawie, nanso fa nam ma me mfa nsie. Ɔbaa panyin nana kae sɛ, Kɔka kyerɛ Nkwakuo, Asono, a wɔwɔ dua no so, sɛ Old-woman-grandmother se, mo nyinaa nso, mo ɛnanom! Asono kae sɛ, Sɛ ɛnyɛ Old-woman-grandmother a, anka yɛbɛbɔ mo. Ɔbaa panyin nana kae sɛ, Me deɛ, ɛnyɛ me eguadwa, na mo moma Ay ba bɛboro no. Saa mmoa no baa fam wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ wɔrebɛboro no. Ɔbaa panyin bi nana kae sɛ, Me Sharp-atwene, twa mmoa yi atitirefoɔ mma me. Afei sharp-one twaa mmoa no atitirefoɔ. Ntikuma boaboaa mmoa no nyinaa ano, na ɔtwee bayerɛ bi wɔ nkaaho mu, na ɔne wɔn nyinaa fii aseɛ kɔɔ nakuraa. Agya Ananse hunuu Ntikuma (ne ɔbabarima) sɛ ogyina nanim, na ɔkaa sɛ, Nkyea waba so, ant-hill breaker (i. e. first-born). Ɔtoaa so sɛ, Ma memfa wo nsane so. Ntikuma kɔɔ hɔ kɔsɛn ne ho. Afei Kwaku Ananse kɔfaa bayere baako, ɔkae sɛ, Bere a menya me abosonsoɔ no, mɛdidi yi. (Ne yere) "Aso" kae sɛ, Efi bere a Ɔbɔadeɛ yɛɛ nneɛma no, woahunu ɔbarima a ɔwɔ abosonsoɔ? Kwaku Ananse ne ne yere, ne ne mmofra, dii bayerɛ no wieeɛ, na ɛno nko ara na na aka a ɛyɛ Agya Ananse deɛ no. Ɛduruu anɔpa a, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, na Kwaku Ananse aka sɛ, Manya me abosonsoɔ. Na ɔkɔɔ fie no akyi kɔtenaa akwankyɛn tena, yɛɛ bayere no, na ɔdidii, kɔsii sɛ na ne nyinaa asa. Ntikuma kae sɛ, Mepue bio. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Me ne wo bɛkɔ. Ntikuma kae sɛ, Me ne wo renkɔ. Bere a Ntikuma kɔɔ da no, na Kwaku Ananse akɔfa hunters skin bag bi, de amena nketewa akuturuku ahyɛ mu ma, de nso ahyɛ mu ma, na wɔde akuturuku akyekyere Ntikuma ntama no ano. Bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ntɛm pa ara, Ntikuma bɔɔ kwan. Ɛduruu sɛ nneɛma no anim apaeɛ no, Ananse, hwehwɛɛ Ntikuma sɛ ɔbɛpɛ a, wamma no, ɛnneɛ ɔkɔhwehwɛɛ nso no. Ɛduruu sɛ ɔhunuu wɔn no, ɔde aboa anammɔn no kɔɔ so kɔɔ so. Na ɔrekɔ no, na Ntikuma ne Aberewa nanaa na wɔte hɔ. Ɔbaa panyin nana kae sɛ, "Agya, wo eguadwa yɛ dɛn? " Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "Ɛkɔm aka me, enti fa biribi ma me nni. " Ɔbaa panyin nana kae sɛ, "Ɛhe na menya "something" afiri? " Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "Wose me yɛ kwasea, me abofra Ntikuma mpo aba, na woama no bi. " Ɔbaa panyin nana kae sɛ, "Kɔ afuw a ɛwɔ hɔ no, yam a ɛka sɛ, "Twe me so, twe me so. " ,moma wɔn so, nanso ebinom a wɔka sɛ, Mma me so, mma me so, momma saa ebinom so. Ananse no buaa sɛ, Ah! dɛn ara na ɛyɛ asɛm a woka kyerɛ me, bere a obi reka sɛ ɔdɔ obi, wo hyɛ me sɛ menkyiri no, me nte sɛɛ da? Nanso Ananse no kɔɔ afuw no mu. Ɛduruu hɔ no, bayerɛ no bi kae sɛ, Twi me, ti me, bi nso kae sɛ, Dont pull me up, dont pull me up. Na ɔtwee ebinom a ɛka sɛ Twi me, ti me, no baa fie ne wɔn. Ɔbaa no wiee no, ɔbaa no nana kaa sɛ, Kɔ na wotow aduane pa no fã no gu, na fa nwoma ahoroɔ no gu kuku no mu, na fa gu ogya so. Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, A, ɛyɛ nkasa a ɛyɛ fĩ bɛn na woka yi kyerɛ me? Ɔbɔadeɛ na ɔbɔɔ nneɛma, enti yɛde aduane pa no gu, na yɛkora nsensanee no so anaa? Ananse no faa aduane no fã a ɛyɛ papa no, na ɔde too ogya so, nanso na ɔfaa wedeɛ no kakra bi de sii so. Bere a aduane no noa no, ɔyii no, na ne nyinaa adan aboɔ, nanso wedeɛ no sin baako a ɔde sii so no, na adan aduane pa. Agya Ananse kae sɛ, "Eh! kwasampani bi awosankɔ yi anka atoro (akyire yi). " Na Ananse no kɔsraa nwoma ahoroɔ no de bae na ɔde sii ogya no so. Bere a aduane no noa no, ɔyii ogya no fii mu, na ɛbɛyɛɛ bayerɛ a wɔanoa yie sɛnea na wopɛ sɛ wuhu no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Obaa nana, eyi nyinaa ye paa, nanso afei ɛsɛ sɛ woma me nam ka ho. " Obaa nana kae sɛ, "Kɔhwɛ dua no, Asono wɔ so. Sɛ wokɔ a, wobɛka sɛ, 'Woyɛ mmoadoma. '" Ananse no kae sɛ, "Saa ɔbaa yi! Me ne Asono nni nea ɛde ba manso, na ɛdɛn nti na ɛsɛ sɛ mekɔyɛ wɔn ayayadeɛ wɔ saa ɔkwan yi so? " Ɔbaa panyin nana kae sɛ, "Kɔ, na sɛ ɔhaw ba a, ɛno ne me abusuabɔ. " Ananse no fii aseɛ. Ɔkae sɛ, "Wo a woyɛ Asono, wo a wote nnua yi so no, Nanabaa panyin no ka sɛ woyɛ mmoadoma. " Mmoa no kae sɛ, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ Nanabaa panyin nti na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛbɔ wo. " Nanabaa panyin no kae sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ bra na bɔ no".

Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Aberewa, ɛno nni kwan. " Ɛneɛ na mmoa no bɛhwee Kwaku Ananse so baeɛ bɛbɔɔ no. Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Me Sekkan The-sharp-one, twa wɔn atitirefoɔ no. " Tenten! (ɛno ne nnyigyei a sekkan no bɔeɛ bere a na ɛreyɛ nadwuma no. Aberewa nanaa no kaa sɛ, "Softly and easy now. " Nanso Ananse no antie. Afei, Ananse boaboaa nam no ano san baa fie hɔ, na Aberewa kɔɔ nkyɛn kakra. Ɛnna Kwaku Ananse wiaa ne sekkan no de guu ne kotokuo no mu, dwanee san baa nakuraa hɔ. Bere a ɔsan baa nakuraa so no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔbabarima no sɛ, "Ntikuma, saa sekkan yi nti, a me nsa aka no, momma yɛnkɔ nwura so. " Enti wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ, na wɔkɔhunuu Wuram mprako bi. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ntikuma, kɔyɛ wɔn ayayadeɛ. Ntikuma kɔɔ hɔ kɔkaa sɛ, "Bush-pigs, woyɛ dɔteɛ. " Bush-pigs no buaa sɛ, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ sɛ woyɛ Kwaku Ananse, Spider ba no a, anka ɛsɛ sɛ yɛba abɛbɔ wo. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Bra na yɛnkɔbɔ no wɔ akwan nyinaa so, ɔyɛ bɔne koraa, nanimtiaa nni aseresɛm. " Bush-pigs no rebɛbɔ no, Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Old womans knife, the sharp one, cut their atitirefoɔ for me. " h! na Sekkan no bɛtoo wɔn so, na ɔtwaa mmoa no nyinaa ti. Afei ɔde sii hɔ sɛ ɔbɛtwitwa nnua no. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Hai! dɛn na eyi yɛ ma wo? Mekae sɛ wotwitwaa mmoa no ti, na wowiee saa no, na afei dɛn na eyi nyinaa yɛ? " Afei Ntikuma hwɛɛ no kakra, afei ɔkae sɛ, "Softly and easy now. " Kwaku Ananse antie no, na Sekkan no baa fam hɔ, na Kwaku Ananse kɔgyee no. Wɔsan baa fie. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ wɔbɛkɔ Sky-god nkyɛn wɔ hɔ. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Agya, nkɔ na sɛ ɛyɛ Sekkan yi akawnto so a. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Firi hɔ kɔ. " Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Fa wo kwan so kɔ. " Kwaku Ananse de nakwan sii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ɔsoro nyame nkyɛn wɔ hɔ, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Agya, ɛnnɛ da, sɛ obi wɔ hɔ a wayɛ wo bɔne a, me nko ara, me Kwaku Ananse, mɛtwa wɔn atitirefoɔ nyinaa wɔ da koro pɛ mu ama wo. " Ɔsoro nyame kaa sɛ, "Meda ase wɔ hɔ ma obiara a wobɛtumi ayɛ saa, ɛfiri sɛ atamfo asraafodɔm kɛseɛ bi regyina kuro no kuro akyi nti, monkɔ. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Momma wɔmfa nsa ne odwan baako mmra. Ntɛm ara na wɔde bae. Kwaku Ananse firii adi kɔɔ kwan no so; Kwaku Ananse bisaa atamfo asraafo no sɛ, Mo na moka sɛ mobɛko atia Ewiem-onyankopɔn no? Wɔbuaa sɛ, Aane, ɛno na yɛka. Seesei Ewiem-onyankopɔn dɔm no gyinaa Kwaku Ananse akyi. Afei Kwaku Ananse kasa kyerɛɛ ɔbea panyin no Sekan, The-sharp-one, sɛ, Twa atamfo asraafo yi atitirefoɔ mma me. Na Sekkan no twaa wɔn atitirefoɔ no tan! tan! na wɔwiee. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Eye, gyae. Nanso Sekkan no hwee Ewiem-onyankopɔn asraadom no so na na ɛretwa (wɔn atitirefoɔ). Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Ao, ɛno ne Ewiem-onyankopɔn asraadom, gyae. Nanso twaa so. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Sɛ wo nufuɔ akye a, ma ɛnnyɛ nwunu. A! Ɔsoro-Onyame dɔm no nyinaa ayera. Mprempren Sekkan no nam mframa so tuu kwan no, ɛbɛduruu baabi a Kwaku Ananse wɔ no. Ɔkaa sɛ, Hwɛ yie, wonnim me? Wunnim me? Me ne Kwaku Ananse, mede wo firi aberewa fie hɔ. A! Mprempren Kwaku Ananse ti bɔɔ no tan! Na Sekkan no kɔɔ so kɔɔ so, na baabi a ɛgyinaa no, ɛbɛdane ɛserɛ bi a yɛfrɛ no Etene (spear-grass); nano yɛ nnam paa, paa, paa.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
