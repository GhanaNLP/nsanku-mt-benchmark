Ɔkɔm kɛse bi bae na Ntikuma fii hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ ɔrekɔhwehwɛ aduane de akɔma ne papa, Agya Ananse, Ananse. Ɔfiri hɔ kɔɔ akuraa bi ase, na ɛhɔ na ohyiaa Obaa Panyin-nananom. Ɔbaa panyin no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Me nana, adɛn nti, bere a obiara ntumi mma ha no, woaba ha? Ntikuma buae sɛ, Ɛnam ɔkɔm no nti, na mebaa ha sɛ merehwehwɛ biribi a mɛkɔ adidi. Ɔbaa panyin nana kae sɛ, Kɔ me afuom hɔ, nanso ɛmo a ɛbɛka sɛ, Pluck me, pluck me, nnyi no, nanso ɛmo a ɛbɛka sɛ, Dont pluck me, nnyi me, nyi no na fa bra. ɛnneɛ. Ntikuma bɔɔ kɔkɔɔ. Bayerɛ no mu bi kae sɛ, Pluck me, pluck me; afoforo nso kae sɛ, Dont pluck me, dont pluck me. Ɛneɛ Ntikuma tuu wɔn a wɔkaa sɛ, Dont pluck me, dont pluck me. Ɔde wɔn baa fie hɔ, ɔyii wɔn. Afei Ɔbaa Panyin-Nkokoaa kaa sɛ, Tow original part no gu sɛ aduane, na fa skins no gu kukuo no mu na fa gu gya so. Ntikuma yii wɔn ho na ɔfaa skins no de guu gya so. Bere a wɔasiesie wɔn ho no, ɔyii no firii gya no mu, kɔtoo kukuo no mu, na ne nyinaa (inside) adane aduane pa. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Mawie, nanso ma me nam a mede bɛhyɛ (the stew). Ɔpanyin-nananom kaa sɛ, Kɔ na kɔka kyerɛ Nkwakuo, Nkwakuo a wɔwɔ dua no so, sɛ Old-woman-grandmother ka sɛ, mo nyinaa deɛ, mo maame! Nkwakuo no kaa sɛ, Sɛ ɛnyɛ Ɔpanyin-nananom a, anka yɛbɛbɔ mo. Ɔpanyin-nananom kaa sɛ, Me deɛ, ɛnyɛ me adwuma, na mo nso monyɛ madwuma. Ay ba bɛbɔɔ no. Mmoa no sii fam sɛ wɔrebɛbɔ no. Ɔbaa panyin, nana, kae sɛ, Me Sharp Knife, twa mmoa yi ti ma me. Afei Sharp-One twaa mmoa no ti. Ntikuma boaboaa mmoa no ti nyinaa ano, na ɔboaboaa yam bi ano, na ɔde wɔn nyinaa kɔɔ ne akuraa ase. Agya Ananse hunuu Ntikuma (ne babarima) sɛ ɔgyina nanim, na ɔkaa sɛ, Nkyea wɔ waba, ant-hill breaker (i. e. first-born). Ɔtoaa so sɛ, Ma memfa wo nkɔ me akyi. Ntikuma kɔɔ hɔ kɔsɛn no. Afei Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ hɔ kɔyii yam baako, ɔkaa sɛ, Sɛ menya me bracelet a, mɛdidi yi. (Ne yere) Aso kaa sɛ, Efi bere a Ɔbɔfoɔ no yɛɛ nneɛma no, woahunu ɔbarima a ɔwɔ bracelet? Kwaku Ananse ne ne yere, ne ne mma, dii yam no wieeɛ, na ɛno nko ara na aka a ɛyɛ Agya Ananse deɛ no. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, I have my miracle. Na ɔfiri hɔ kɔtenaa (in queue) fie no akyi, na ɔnoaa yam no, na ɔdii, kɔsii sɛ (ɛnyinaa) wieeɛ. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Me bɛkɔ bio. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Me ne wo bɛkɔ (bom). Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Me ne wo renkɔ. Bere a Ntikuma kɔdaeɛ no, na Kwaku Ananse akɔfa abɔmmɔfoɔ aboa nhoma bag bi, na ɔde ntokuru nketenkete ahyɛ mu ma, na ɔde zinc ahyɛ mu ma, na ɔde abɔ Ntikuma ntama ano. Bere a nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ntɛm pa ara, ntɛm pa ara, Ntikuma fii kwan no so. Bere a (dark concealment) no tetew kɔɔ nkyɛn no, Ananse hwehwɛɛ Ntikuma sɛnea ɔbɛpɛ, antumi anhu no, afei ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔhwehwɛɛ baabi a aso no ahwe ase. Bere a ɔhunuu wɔn no, ɔdii aboa no anammɔn akyi, kɔɔ so kɔɔ so. Ɔrekɔ no, na Ntikuma ne ɔbaa panyin no te hɔ. Ɔbaa panyin nana kae sɛ, Agya barima, wo adwuma ne dɛn? Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, Ɔkɔm akye me, enti ma me biribi nni. Ɔbaa panyin nana kae sɛ, Ɛhe na menya something afiri? Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, Wosusuw sɛ meyɛ ɔkwasea, me ba Ntikuma mpo aba, na wode bi ama no. Ɔbaa panyin nana kae sɛ, Kɔ afuom kɛse a ɛwɔ hɔ no; yams a ɛka sɛ, Twe me so, twe me so. ,mma wɔn so, nanso wɔn a wɔka sɛ, Mma me so, mma me so, ma wɔn so. Ananse no (replied), Ah! nkwaseasɛm bɛn na eyi yɛ a woka kyerɛ me sɛ, bere a obi ka sɛ ɔdɔ obi, woka kyerɛ me sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ me tan no, mentee saa da? Nanso Ananse no kɔɔ afuom. Ɛduruu hɔ no, bayerɛ no mu bi kae sɛ, Tull me up, pull me up, foforo nso kae sɛ, Dont pull me up, dont pull me up. Na ɔtwee ne deɛ aka no sɛ, Tull me up, pull me up, na ɔne wɔn baa fie. Ɔbae na ɔwiee wɔn hohoro; Ɔpanyin-baa-nananom kaa sɛ, Kɔ na tow aduane no fã pa no gu, na fa nwoma no, fa gu kukuo no mu, na fa gu gya so. Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, Ah, asɛm a ɛporɔw bɛn na woka yi kyerɛ me; Ɔbɔadeɛ no na ɔyɛɛ nneɛma no, enti yɛtow aduane pa no gu, na yɛkura mmoawa no? Ananse no faa aduane no fã pa no, na ɔde sii gya so, nanso ɔno ɔfaa bankyeɛ no mu baako na ɔde sii so. Bere a aduane no wieeɛ no, ɔyii no, na ne nyinaa adan abotan, nanso bankyeɛ baako a ɔde sii so no, na adan aduane pa. Agya Ananse kaa sɛ, "Eh! Kwasampani awosuo yi anka atoro (ɛwieeɛ koraa). " Na Ananse no kɔsesaa nkrataa no na ɔde baa gya no so. Bere a aduane no noa no, oyii firi ogya no mu, na ɛbɛyɛɛ porridge a ɛyɛ dɛ sɛnea wopɛ sɛ wuhu no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Mo Nkwakuo, mo a mowɔ nnua yi so, Nana Panyin ka sɛ moyɛ mmoa. " Mmoa no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ Nana Panyin nti a, anka ɛsɛ sɛ yɛboro mo. " Nana Panyin kaa sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ bra na bɔ no. "
