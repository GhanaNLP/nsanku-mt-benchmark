Ade kyee no, wɔ Royal Albert Hall no, Onua Rutherford kasae wɔ asɛmti Dɛn nti na Wiase atumfo akɛse yɛ AhinhimAnoyie no ho. Bere a wobuee apono no, nnipa 13,000 hyɛɛ asa kɛseɛ no ma wɔ simma ahodoɔ mu. Nnipadɔm a abu so nam abɔnten akasam akwan so tiee dwumadie no yiye.

Wɔ ne nkasa no mu no, Onua Rutherford kyerɛkyerɛɛ mu sɛ wiase atumfo akɛse hwe ase esiane Yehowa ne nAhennie no apowoo nti. Onua Rutherford kasa tiaa Aman Agorɔ kuo no na ɔhyɛɛ nkɔm sɛ wɔbɛsɛe no.

NSUAHU

Yehowa ma gyidi akatua

Emma yɛɛ adwuma wɔ ahɔhodan bi mu nna abiɛsa dapɛn biara, nnɔnhwerew akron da biara. Ɔka kyerɛɛ nɔhwɛfoɔ no sɛ ɔgye da baako na wakɔ ne mantam nhyiam no ase. Sɛ́ anka ɔhwɛfoɔ no bɛma no abisa no, ɔsesaa ne berɛ nhyehyɛeɛ no kɔɔ nna anum dapɛn biara na ɔmaa no "ultimatum": gye bere nhyehyɛe foforɔ no tom anaasɛ adwumagyae. Esiane sɛ na Emma de ne ho to Yehowa so nti, ɔpaw sɛ obegyae adwumagyae na wakɔ nhyiamukɛseɛ no ase.

Bere a Emma kɔɔ nadwumagyaeɛ no, na ahɔhodan no mu adwumayɛfoɔ panin kɛseɛ no wɔ hɔ. Bere a ɔtee tebea no ho asɛm no, ɔhyɛɛ bɔ sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ asɛm no ho nhwɛeɛ. Nanso, adapɛn abien akyi no, wɔka kyerɛɛ Emma sɛ wɔayi no afi adwumam. Ɔsan hyiaa adwumayɛfoɔ panin kɛseɛ no a na ne bo afuw sɛ wɔbuu nani guu nakwankyerɛ ahodoɔ no so. Osiesiee asɛm no, na wɔmaa Emma bere nhyehyɛe foforɔ: nnanu dapɛn biara, nnɔnhwerew nnan da biara. Ɛdaa adi sɛ eye sen ne mfitiaseɛ nhyehyeɛ mpo; Ɛmaa nteokrase dwumadi ahorow no yɛɛ yiye! Yehowa faa nahiadeɛ ho nhwɛeɛ wɔ kwan a ɛboro nanidasoɔ so.
