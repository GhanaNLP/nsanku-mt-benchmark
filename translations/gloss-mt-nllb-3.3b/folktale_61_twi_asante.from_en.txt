Ɛno nti na mede me nnyina maa no sɛ ɔnsoa. " Ɔsoro nyame no kaa sɛ, "O, fa no kɔkyekyere no wɔ dunsin bi so, na kɔfa Tamiriwa, tuntum snail no mmra. " Black snail no baeɛ. Ɛda a ɛtwaa mu no, na mewɔ hɔ, bere a Palm dua no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ maso ayɛ tuntum, na me ho yɛ me nkumaa saa no, me susuwii sɛ me kɔ Ɛsono hɔ, mɛhwehwɛ nam a mɛdi na manya kɛse. Mfinimfini-dua no baeɛ; ɔkaa sɛ, Sire, Abena, Fa a ɛteateaa dua, a sɛ wɔsesa nwura wɔ dan mu a, wɔde ma no sɛ ɔmfa mmra me nkyɛn wɔ dane mu. Ɛno nti, me nso, mebɔɔ Prae, Mpopaho no ahohora. Ɔsoro nyame no kaa sɛ, Fa no kɔkyekyere no wɔ dunsin bi ho, na kɔgye Akwanya ne Awieeɛ. Akwanya ne Awieeɛ bae.
