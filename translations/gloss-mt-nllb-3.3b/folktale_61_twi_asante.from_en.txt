SƐNEA ƐBAA NEA ƐKƆƆ SO NO, SƐ OBI KƆ DAN MU WƆ ANADWO A, ƐYƐ DU

WƆKYERƐ SƐ Kuntunkurunku, Akwanya, ne Kuntunamoa, Awieeɛ, firi soro hwee aseɛ. Wɔkae sɛ, "Old mother-earth, you are (too) flat. " Asase Yaa kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wose me yɛ (too) flat? " Wɔkae sɛ, "Efisɛ woantutu tokuru a yɛbɛda mu. " Old mother-earth danee ne ho kyerɛɛ Prae, Prae no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Wo ano (a ɛwɔ hɔ) no, nkokɔ asinasin akata so. " Obuae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wuhaw me? " Old mother-earth kae sɛ, "Sɛ mehaw wo a, mehaw wo; sɛ woanyɛ saa a, Nte nea enti a ɛte saa no ase, gyae bisabisa. " Ɔfuntum no danee ne ho kɔɔ Abena Flat-Heap nkyɛn (a wɔhohoro nwura gu mu) na ɔkae sɛ, "Fa (nneɛma) na kɔtow gu. " Flat-Heap kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wohaw me? " Ɔfuntum no kae sɛ, " (Nea ɛte biara no) mayɛ saa. " Flat-Heap nso kasa kyerɛɛ Sumina, Middle-Heap, na ɔka sɛ, "Wogye nea wunya no. " Mfinimfini Kuw no kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wohaw me? " Ɔ (Flat-Heap) r Ɔbuaa no sɛ, "Madi wo ho dwuma ɔkwammɔne so, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a, gyae bisa." Mfinimfini Kuw no nso kasa kyerɛɛ Abbe, mmɛw dua no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Wo kɔn no nyinaa ayɛ nwura. " Mmɛw dua no kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wudi me ho dwuma ɔkwammɔne so? " Mfinimfini Kuw no buae sɛ, "Madi wo ho dwuma ɔkwammɔne so, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a, gyae bisabisa. " Mmɛw dua no nso danee ne ho kyerɛɛ Tetea, anantwi tuntum kumaa no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Wo aso ayɛ kusuu. ck. " Black ant ketewa no kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wohaw me? " Palm dua no buae sɛ, "Mayɛ wo ayayade, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a, gyae bisa. " Black ant ketewa no nso kɔɔ Elephant hɔ na ɔkɔɔ ne nan so, na ɔkekaa Elephant no. Bere a (asono no) rema ne nan so no, afei ɔhyɛɛ Tamiriwa, black-snail no aniwa ase pɛɛ, na black-snail no ani fii aseɛ sui, na ɔpopaa nsuo no wɔ fan leaf (a enyin) a ɛbɛn hɔ no so. (Seesei), aduane a ɔbaa panyin-nananom di ne fan dua no, na na Tamiriwa, black-snail no, apopa nani so nsuo no. Na kraman, kraman no retwam, na ɔfrɛɛ kraman no na ɔfaa dua no (ɔsoaa) na ɔmaa kraman no soaa no wɔ ne ti so. Bere a kraman no rekɔ no, ɔfaa kola-nut, na ɔkyekyɛɛ biako mu abien, na ɔde biako guu Okagya, akraman no, amena no ano. Okagya nso faa kola-nut no na ɔde bɔɔ Stand, silk-cotton dua no. Silk-cotton dua no nso, abufuo bɔɔ ne nufu. Ɔhunuu sɛ abofra ketewa bi retwam, na ɔtwee ne dua no na ɔtoo no kum no. Afei, na saa abofra ketewa no yɛ ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no ba. Ɛhɔ ara na wɔkyeree silk-cotton dua no, na wɔde no kɔɔ ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no anim. Wɔbisaa no (the Silk-cotton-tree), sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ ne nyinaa? " Silk-cotton-tree no kaa sɛ, "Na megyina hɔ wɔ me ara me baabi, berɛ a Opuro yi - abofra a ɔyɛ no - de nakokoduru yii kola-nut bɔɔ me, enti me deɛ, berɛ a abofra yi retwam no, na misusuw sɛ ɔno ne Okagya, Opuro no, na meyii me nkorabata no bi bɔɔ no, na hwɛ, na ɛyɛ wo ba. " Ɔsoro-nyame no, ɔkae sɛ, "Fa no na fa kyekyere no wɔ dua bi so, na frɛ Okagya, Opuro, sɛ ɔmmra. " Okagya bae, ɔkae sɛ, "Sire, wunim sɛ me haw ne kola-nut, nanso Ɔkraman, Ɔkraman no, kɔgyee baako, na ɔtoo no wɔ me ɛtokuro no kwan ano, na bere a me rebɔ no, ɛkɔɔ Old-man-silk-cotton-dua no so. " Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Fa no, fa kyekyere no wɔ dua bi so, na frɛ Ɔkraman no sɛ ɔmmra. " hey kɔfrɛɛ Ɔkraman no sɛ ɔmmra. Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Sire, na menam me ho wɔ baabi - seseiara woahunu sɛnea me ho yɛ (delicate) " berɛ a Ɔbaa Panyin no faa ne nnua bɔsoɔ no de sii me ti so; seseiara Okagya, Opuro no, ɔno na ɔda natɔeɛ a ɛwɔ afuo mu a Ɔbaa Panyin no ayɛ no sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛnya biribi adi, nanso me na me ne Ɔbaa Panyin no maa ɔsoaa ne nnua bɔsoɔ no; me nso, mede kola-nut a nd de guu nadekoradan no ano. " Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Fa no, kyekyere no dua bi so, na frɛ ɔbaa panyin no sɛ ɔmmra. " Ɔbaa panyin no bae. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ah, me mmanana, monhwɛ mano mu, me a mintumi nnom nam no, fan nhaban na midi; nea ɛka ho no, mehohoro Bosompra; me akyiwadeɛ ne (ɛneɛ) Tamiriwa, black-snail no; ɔkraman no nko ara na ɔnim eyi, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ Tamiriwa, na ɔma no fa nsuo firi nano, na ɔde gu fan nhaban a mete so no mu. Ɛno nti na mede me nnua no maa no sɛ ɔnsoa. " Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, "O, fa no na fa kyekyere no wɔ adum bi so, na kɔfa Tamiriwa, black-snail no mmra. " Black-snail no baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sir, na me nko ara me wɔ baabi, hwɛ, asono kɛseɛ kɛseɛ bi tiatiaa beaeɛ a ɛwɔ mani ase no so, na me kɔfaa nhaban sɛ mɛpopa. Ɛno nko ara na menim fa ho. Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Fa no na fa no kyekyere no wɔ dua bi so, na fa Asono, Asono no, bra. " Wɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔfaa Asono. Ɔsono no kaa sɛ, "Mfeɛ mmiɛnsa a atwam ni no, na mete hɔ, na me honam ani fii aseɛ yɛɛ me yaw, na bere a mehwɛɛ beaeɛ no - mepa kyɛw sɛ mede asɛmfua no bedi dwuma - na ɔkwasea yi, black ant ketewa no akɔhyɛ me honam ani (me honam ani) mu sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ nam a ɔbɛwe, na ama wanyin kɛse; na bere a mereyi manammɔn no, ɛkɔhyɛɛ Tamiriwa, black snail no so. " Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Fa no kyekyere no wɔ nkatanim mu, na fa no bra. Tetea, nantwi tuntum ketewa no. Bere a Tetea bae no, ɔkae sɛ, "Sire, palm-tree bi wɔ hɔ; sɛ ɛso a, (ne nuts) yɛ tuntum kan; sɛ me, nantwi tuntum ketewa no, nantew so a, ɛma ɛbere. Ɛda a ɛtwaa toɔ no na mewɔ hɔ, berɛ a Palm-dua no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ masoɔ yɛ tuntum, na me ho yɛ me nketenkete paa nti, me dwene sɛ mɛkɔ Asono a ɔwɔ hɔ no nkyɛn, na mahwehwɛ nam a mɛdi na mayɛ kɛseɛ. Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, Fa no na fa no kyekyere no wɔ dua bi so, na kɔfrɛ Palm-dua no sɛ ɔmmra. Abɛ, Palm-dua no, baeɛ; ɔkae sɛ, Owura, eyi ne nea ɛwɔ me sɛ mɛka: me ne Sumina, Midden-heap no, te aseɛ ne nyinaa, na sɛ nwura a wɔtow gu no so no dɔɔso dodo, na ontumi nnyina bio a, megye bi na mede kyekyere me kɔn ho; afei deɛ, ɔdi me ho fɛw sɛ mewɔ nwura wɔ me kɔn ho. Ɛno nti, me deɛ, mani yɛɛ me kɔkɔɔ, na me nso metwirii ant-black-ant ketewa no. Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, Fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi so, na frɛ Sumina, Middle-heap, sɛ ɔmmra. Mfinimfini-heap baeɛ; ɔkae sɛ, Sir, Abena, Flat-of-wood hono, a sɛ wɔhohoro nwura a ɛwɔ dan bi mu a, wɔde ma no sɛ ɔno nso mfa mmra me nkyɛn. Ɛda a ɛtwaa toɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, You store up what you receive (i. e. wɔyɛ adifudepɛfoɔ); mekaeɛ sɛ, a, na berɛ a na nnuadewa dua no bɛn hɔ no, me nso meyɛɛ no basabasa. Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, Fa no na fa no kyekyere no wɔ dua bi so, na frɛ Abena, Straight-of-tree honour, sɛ ɔmmra. Straight-of-tree honour baeɛ; ɔkaa sɛ, Sir, berɛ a Prae, the Praech, resɛe dan bi, sɛ wɔmma me (ne compost) a, mframa no bɔ a, nwura no nyinaa san ba bio; sesei, da a atwam no, ɛwom sɛ menyɛɛ no hwee de, nanso ɔ kae sɛ, Wofa nneɛma nko ara na wode ato hɔ. Ɛno nti, me nso mani abere, na me kɔbɔɔ Sumina, Mfinimfini-kupo no. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Fa no, fa no kyekyere dua bi so, na frɛ Prae, Praeɛ no, ma ɔmmra ha. Praeɛ no bae; ɔkae sɛ, Agya, Old-mother-earth da hɔ a ɛyɛ dɛɛdɛɛ, nani yɛ no dɔteɛ, enti bere biara a me sɔre no, me hohoro dɔteɛ no nyinaa gu ne so na me ma ɔyɛ autiful; nanso da a ɛtwaa toɔ no, Ɔba-Asase kasa kyerɛɛ me sɛ, Wo ano yɛ akokɔ mmoa bins, na me nufu nso, me ho dwirii me, na me kɔbɔɔ Abena, Flat-of-wood animuonyam no. Ɛberɛ a Ɔba-Asase baeɛ no, ɔkaa sɛ, Owura, me nna hɔ; wɔtiatia me so; menka hwee da; wɔyɛ nneɛma ahorow nyinaa. s me so, nanso menka hwee da. Afei da foforo a na mewɔ hɔ no, Kuntunkurunku ne Kuntunamoa, Akwanya ne Awieeɛ, bɛtoo me so, na mekae sɛ, O! asɛm no ne dɛn? na wɔkae sɛ, Woantutu tokuru a yɛbɛtumi ada mu, na wo ne wo traits nyinaa nni mfasoɔ (ma obiara). Ɛno nti, me nso metwirii Prae, Prae no. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Fa no kyekyere no wɔ dua bi so, na kɔfa Akwanya ne Funeral. Akwanya ne Funeral no bae. Wɔkae sɛ, Owura, na yɛwɔ soro, na yɛredi agorɔ, na yɛhwee ase na yɛpirapiraa yɛn ho; ɛno nti na yɛbɔɔ Maame-asase animtiaa sɛ ɔyɛ dɛɛdɛɛ. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Councillors, yɛate asɛm yi nyinaa, enti momma yɛnsɔre nkɔ dan no mu na yɛnhwɛ adanseɛ no na yɛmmra mmɛbu atɛn. Enti wɔsɔre kɔɔ dan bi mu, na wɔtoo mu. Ɛno nti, sɛ wokɔ dan bi mu anadwo a, dark incarnate Nyame ne ne Local Council na ɛwɔ hɔ resusu ho.
