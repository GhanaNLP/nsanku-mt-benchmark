Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

ƐDƐN NTI A ANANSE, ANANSE YƐ MMIRIKA SƐ ƆWƆ NSUO ANI SO A

KWAKU ANANSE, Ɔseane no, kɔɔ Ɔkraman, Ɔseane no nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Mepɛ sɛ me ne wo kɔ akyekye akuraa ketewa bi a yɛbɛtena mu. Ɔseane no kaa sɛ, Me nso, mepɛ saa. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Sɛ Sunday-Adae bi tɔ a, wɔ dwoada a ɛdi hɔ yi, kɔfa rope-creeper; me nso, mɛfa rope-creeper na yɛahyɛ aseɛ. Bio nso, mefa toa bi hyɛ mu nsuo, na mo nso, monhwie nsuo no bi gu mu, ɛfiri sɛ ebia, baabi a yɛrekɔ no, nsuo nni hɔ. Kwaku Ananse hwiee nsuo no bi gu mu, Kraman no nso nyaa ɛwoɔ a ɔde bɛhyɛ toa no mu. Wɔrekɔ no, na wɔadu kwan no fã, na wɔn ho abere. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Yɛnnom yɛn nsuo no bi. Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, Fa me deɛ bi nom. Kwaku Ananse faa bi, na ɔde ne nsa kaa nano, ɔkae sɛ, Oh, ɛhe na eyi firi? Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, Ɛwɔ me yafunu mu. Ananse kaa sɛ, Yiw! Bere a wɔte hɔ sɛ wɔregye wɔn ahome no, Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Kraman, momma yɛnka yɛn ho wɔ agorɔ mu. Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, Agorɔ bɛn? Ananse buaa sɛ, Agorɔ no kyekyere agorɔ; y Ou kyekyere me kan, ɛnneɛ me nso mɛkyekyere wo, nanso bere a wote sɛ meka sɛ, Wui! ɛnneɛ wofiri hɔ. Ɔkraman no kae sɛ, Dabi, wo kyekyere me ansa. Ananse no kae sɛ, Yi wo ho fi hɔ, bere a wo panyin reka asɛm bi akyerɛ wo no, wosan gye ho akyinnye anaa? Ɔkraman no kae sɛ, Yiw, ma ɛntra hɔ (sɛnea wopɛ). Ananse no kae sɛ, Bra na kyekyere me. Ɔkraman no faa ne rope-creeper no kyekyere Ananse no. Na ɔde no kɔɔ ne ti so sɛ ɔrekɔtɔn no ama wanya biribi adi. Bere a Kwaku Ananse tee saa asɛm no, ɔdaa nawerɛhow adi kae sɛ:

Me, Ananse Akuamoa, minnya aboa mpo a ɛfata sɛ ɔtɔn me;

Ɔkraman yi nkwaseasɛm nko ara, ɔbɛtɔn me.

Hena na menya no a obegye me?

O, Path, gye me!

Kwaku Ananse antumi annsu nko ara. Ɔkraman no soaa no kɔsii sɛ ɔduruu nsuo bi ho. Afei, Diamond, Diamond no, tee Spider no su. Bere a ɔbaeɛ no, ɔbisaa Dog no sɛ asɛm no yɛ dɛn. Ɔkraman no de Kwaku Ananse guu fam, na ɔdwanee, na Diamond no yii Kwaku Ananse. Na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Agya, dɛn na metumi de ama wo de akyerɛ sɛnea meda wo ase? Diamond no kaa sɛ, Yɛmpɛ hwee koraa. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Sɛ wowɔ mma a, ɛnneɛ ɔkyena mɛba abɛhyɛ wɔn ti nhwi ma wɔn sɛnea ɛfata paa. Diamond no kaa sɛ, Mate. Kwaku Ananse baa fie; ɔkae sɛ, Enti kɔhwehwɛ palm-nuts ne bran, ɛfiri sɛ ɔkyena mɛkunkum brin. g san fa crocodile no mma yɛnnya nkwanom nni. " Aso kɔgyee bi, na ananse no bɔɔ ne sekan ne ne sanku ano, na ɔde kɔɔ asubɔnten no mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Father Crocodile, the Water-animal, I am coming to reward you. " Na ɔfaa nsu no guu nsuo no mu. Ɔkyɛm no baeɛ, na ɔrebɛfa fufuo no adi, berɛ a Ananse no faa ne sekan twaa no, ogya! nanso wannya no (ɛfata), na ɔfirii hɔ kɔeɛ. Kwaku Ananse san baa fie. Aso bisaa no sɛ, "Ɛhe na aboa no wɔ? " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Fa wo ho kɔ; onipa bi firi akwantu mu ba, na ɔnnya nahome mpo, na wofiri aseɛ rebisa no nsɛm. " Aso kaa sɛ, "Mahunu sɛ woannye no. " Kwaku Ananse anka hwee. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, ntɛm pa ara, na Aso aka sɛ ɔrekɔ asuo no ho. Bere a oduu hɔ no, na Diamond da asubɔnten no ano, na na Blue Bottle nwansena no rekyinkyin ne so. Aso san kɔɔ fie kɔka kyerɛɛ ne kunu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Afei na woahu sɛnea aduru bi a mewɔ no, ɛyɛ adwuma; sɛ mekum nam (da baako) a, ɛnyɛ kosi sɛ nneɛma bɛda adi, da foforo no, mekɔfa no, nanso ne nyinaa akyi no, mema wo akwaaba sɛ woahu no. " Kwaku Ananse twaa dua bi na ɔde kɔɔ asubɔnten no ho wɔ hɔ, na ɛhɔ na ɔhunuu Diamond no. Ɔde dua no bɔɔ no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Dick, wo awu anaa? Diamond, wo awu anaa? " na ɔdanee no bio. Na Diamond no da hɔ komm, nanso na onwui. Bere a Kwaku Ananse rebɛfa ne nsa aka no, Diamond no bɔɔ no mpofirim, kuadwuma! Kwaku Ananse bɔɔ mmɔden, peter! peter! na ɔnyaa kwan a ɔbɛfa so adwane. Ɛno nti, bere a Diamond no fa nsuo no so no, ɔde ahopere kɔ; osuro sɛ Diamond no bɛba abɛkyere no bio.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
