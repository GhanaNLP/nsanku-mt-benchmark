Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐDƐN NTI NA ANANSE, ANANSE NO, MMIRIKA AHOROƆ BERE A ƆWƆ NSUO NO ANI NO

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse no, kɔɔ Ɔkraman nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Mepɛ sɛ me ne wo si akuraa ketewa a yɛbɛtena mu. Kraman no kaa sɛ, Me nso mepɛ saa. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Sɛ Sunday-Adae bi tɔ a, ɛdwoada a ɛdi hɔ yi, kɔfa hama kɔbere, me nso mɛfa hama kɔbere na yɛakɔ. Bio, mɛfa towa bi ahyɛ mu ma nsuo, na mo nso, monhyɛ no kakra, ɛfiri sɛ ebia, baabi a yɛrekɔ yi, nsuo biara nni hɔ. Kwaku Ananse no hyɛɛ nsuo kakra, Kraman no nso nyaa ɛwoɔ bɛhyɛɛ ne towa no mu. Na wɔrekɔ no, na wɔaduru mfimfini no, na wɔn abere abrɛ. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Momma yɛnnom yɛn nsuo no bi. Kraman no kaa sɛ, Momma me deɛ bi nnom. Kwaku Ananse faa bi, na ɔde ne nsa kaa nano, ɔkae sɛ, Ao, ɛhe na eyi firi? Kraman no kaa sɛ, Ɛwɔ me yafunu mu. Ananse no kaa sɛ, Aane! Bere a wɔte hɔ renya wɔn ahome no, Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Kraman, momma yɛnka bom nni agorɔ. Kraman no kaa sɛ, Ɛyɛ agorɔ bɛn? Ananse no buaa sɛ, The binding-binding game; y ou bind me first, then I, too, shall bind you, but when you hear me say, Wui! then you leave off. The Kraman said, No, do you bind me first. The Ananse said, Take yourself off, when your ɔpanin is telling you something, do you argue about it? The Kraman said, Well, let it remain (as you wish). The Ananse said, Come and bind me up. The Kraman took his rope-creeper and bound the Ananse. Na ɔde no kɔɔ ne etire so sɛ ɔrekɔtɔn no ama wanya biribi adi. Bere a Kwaku Ananse tee saa asɛm no, ɔmaa awerɛhosu so kae sɛ:

Me, Ananse Akuamoa, minni aboa kɛseɛ nea ɛfata mpo a mebɛtɔn;

Kraman kwasea yi nko ara, ɔbɛtɔn me.

Hena na menya no a obegye me?

O, Kwan, gye me!

Kwaku Ananse bɛtumi asu na waasu nko ara. Kraman no soaa no kɔsii sɛ ɔduruu asuwa bi ho. Afei, Ɔdɛnkyɛm, Akokɔsrade no, tee sɛ nkran no su. Ɔbaa no, ɔbisaa Kraman no sɛ asɛm no yɛ dɛn. Kraman no de Kwaku Ananse guu fam, na wɔdwanee, na Akokɔsrade no yii Kwaku Ananse no. Na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Agya, ayamye bɛn na metumi de ama wo de akyerɛ sɛ meda wo ase? Akokɔsrade no kaa sɛ, Yɛn ani nni adeɛ koraa. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Sɛ wowɔ mmofra a, ɛnneɛ ɔkyena mɛba abɛhyɛ wɔn ti nwi no fɛfɛɛfɛ paa. Akokɔsrade no kaa sɛ, Metee. Kwaku Ananse baa fie, ɔkaa sɛ, Aso, kɔ na kɔhwehwɛ nnuadewa ne gyeene, ɛfiri sɛ ɔkyena mɛkɔ akunkum na mabere. g back a crocodile that we may have a nkwan ne foroe to eat. " Aso" kɔgyee bi, na Ananse no hyɛɛ ne sekkan no ano yɛɛ no ɛtɔ, na ɔde kɔɔ asuwa hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, "Father Crocodile, Nsuo aboa no, meba sɛ merebɛtua wo ka. " Na ɔgyee fufuo no kɔtoo nsuo no mu. Akokɔsrade no bae, na ɔrebɛfa fufuo no akɔ adidi no, na Ananse no faa ne sekkan no twitwaa no, gya! nanso wannya no (properly), na ɔkɔe. Kwaku Ananse san baa fie. "Aso" bisaa no sɛ, "Ɛhe na saa aboa no wɔ? " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Fa wo ho kɔ; onipa bi firi akwantu mu ba, na nahome mpo nnya mma no, na wofiri aseɛ rehyɛ no nsɛmmisa. " "Aso" kaa sɛ, "Mahunu sɛ woannye no. " Kwaku Ananse anka hwee. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, anɔpa paa, "Aso" kaa sɛ ɔrekɔ asuwa. Ɔduruu hɔ no, na Akokɔsrade no da asubonten ano, na nkotokuo bruu no kurukuru wɔ ne so. "Aso" san baa fie kɔka kyerɛɛ ne kunu no. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Seesei wuhu sɛnea aduru bi a mewɔ no yɛ adwuma; sɛ mekum nam (da bi), ɛnyɛ kosi sɛ nneɛma bɛda adi, da a edi hɔ no, mekɔfa, nanso me ma wo akwaaba sɛ woahu. " Kwaku Ananse twitwaa poma bi de kɔɔ asuwa hɔ, na ɛhɔ na ɔhyiaa Akokɔsrade. Ɔde ne poma no bɔɔ no sɛ, "Akokɔsrade, wo awu anaa? Akokɔsrade, wo awu anaa? " na ɔdan no kɔɔ ne ho. Na Akokɔsrade no da so ara, nanso onwui. Bere a Kwaku Ananse rebɛfa ne nsa aka no, mpofirim ara na Akokɔsrade no bɔɔ no, kuadwuma! Kwaku Ananse bɔɔ mmɔden, petere! petere! na ɔhunuu kwan bi a ɔbɛfa so aguan. Ɛno nti, sɛ Ananse no fa nsuo no so a, ɔtu mmirika kɔ; ɔsuro sɛ Akokɔsrade no bɛba abɛkyere no bio.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
