SƐNEA ƆKƆTƆ, KƆTƆ, NYAA HONO, NA ABEREWA, ABEREWA, NYAA DWEN

Ɔbaa bi woo ne abofra, bere a ɔsomaa no (baabiara) no, ɔkae sɛ, "Merenkɔ. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Afei tena hɔ, wo ne w'anitefoɔ. " Bere biara a ɔsomaa no (wɔ eRRAND biara so) no, ɔkae sɛ, "Merenkɔ. " Afei da bi ɔkae sɛ, "Kɔbɔ nsuo na fa brɛ me. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Merenkɔ. " Na aberewa no bɔɔ no na ɔkae sɛ, "Twɛn hɔ ara, na wobehu. " Na t abeawa no kasae na ɔkae sɛ, "Mɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ 'mebehu' sɛ mehu (sɛnea ɛteɛ). " Na ɔhyɛɛ kwan no so, na ɔkɔɔ mmirikatuo kɛseɛ bi, (na ne anan mmirika no nnyigyei te sɛ) yiridi, yiridi, yiridi. Ɛduruu tenten no, ɔduruu akuraa bi ho, na ɔhunuu aberewa bi a ɔte hɔ. Bere a ɔhunuu no no, na ɔrebɛntwe nakyi. Na aberewa no kae sɛ, "Me ba, ntwe wo ho mma sɛ mahu wo. " Na abofra no kɔɔ anim, na aberewa no kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wo aniwa mu nsuo no hohoro wo saa? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me ne me maame te hɔ bɔ mu, da biara a ɔbɛsɔre no, na ɔka sɛ, "Twɛn hɔ ara na wubehu," na ɔbɔɔ me, enti mekae sɛ, mɛba abɛhwehwɛ sɛ mehu (nea ɛyɛ te sɛ)." Na aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Mene ɔno. " Afei ɔkaa sɛ, "Kɔ fie no akyi hɔ na wotutu bayerɛ no na fa ba; sɛ wokɔduru hɔ a, deɛ ɛbɛka sɛ, Tutu me, tutu me, tutu me, no, ntutu; nanso deɛ ɛka sɛ, Ntu me, ntu me, ɛno deɛ, tutu. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Ho! " Ɛyɛ nokware a ɔkɔɔ hɔ; bayerɛ no mu bi ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Tutu me u p, munkotum me, munkotum me;" afoforo nso kae sɛ, "Mommɔ me, monkotum me, monkotum me. " Ebinom kae sɛ, "Mommɔ me, monkotum me," na ɛyɛ ebinom a ɔtutuu no. Na ɔne wɔn bae, na aberewa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Fum wɔn ho, fa gu ogya no so. " Na ɔfuntum wɔn ho, na ɔde guu ogya no so, na sɛ wɔnoa a, ɔyi no ma ɛyɛ aduane, na ɔde siee aberewa no anim. Na ɔkaa sɛ, "Kɔ kuro no kuro akyi, na kɔhwɛ asaworowa bi a nam da so, kɔhwɛ nam no ntam na fa wo apawde no. " Na abofra no faa bi, na ɔba bɛtoo ogya no so, na ɔyɛɛ nkwan. Ɔwieeɛ no, ɔde sii aberewa no anim. Na aberewa no kae sɛ, "Woawie anaa? " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Aane, nana. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Bra bɛtu fufuo no. " Na aberewa no kɔɔ ne nkyɛn kɔdaa nakyi, ɔkae sɛ, "Mommoaboa bayerɛ a wɔanoa no ano, fa gu ɛhwene no mu, na mobɛtu. " Na abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ɛha yɛ nea ɛyɛ nwanwa, sɛ yɛtu fufuo wɔ nipa ɛhwene mu, eyi ne nea maame no kaa ho asɛm no. " Ɔkaa eyinom nyinaa wɔ ne etire mu. Ɔkɔɔ so bɔɔ; nanso bere a ɔbɔɔ no, ɔbɔɔ no brɛoo paa. Ɔbaa aberewa no kae sɛ, "Bɔ no den, sɛ woanbɔ no den a, wo kokoɔ no bɛpae. " Na ɔbɔɔ no, pi! pi! pi! na berɛ a wawiee no, ɔhwie guu nkwan no so, na ɔde sii hɔ wɔ aberewa no anim. Na aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Me nana, bɔ me din na fa didi. " Na ɔkaa sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ wo Nana Aberewa. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Aane! onhunuu adeɛ nti, ɔmmɛdi. " Na aberewa no dii ne nyinaa. Ɛduruu nna nwɔtwe no, saa na ɔyɛ no daa. Na da bi abofra no kɔɔ asuwa hɔ sɛ ɔrekɔpra nsuo. Kɔtɔ no huu no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Adɛn nti na obi ba no so ate sɛɛ? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔnananom panyin bi wɔ hɔ a me ne no te, na sɛ meyɛ aduane ma no a, ɔka sɛ, Ka me din na fa nni, meka sɛ, Wɔfrɛ wo Nana Ɔpanin, na ɔka sɛ, Ee! woanhunu kwan a wobɛfa so adidi, na afei na ɔdi; ɛno nti na me ho yɛ me mmerɛ. " Kɔtɔ no kaa sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ no ɔbaa panyin-Mosono. " Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ho. " Na ɔde ne nsuo koraa no sii ne etire so. Kɔtɔ no kaa sɛ, "Nka wo werɛ mfi sɛ woresan akɔ. " Ɛhɔ na abofra no kɔɔ nanim, bere a ɔreba no nyinaa, na ɔda so ara ka sɛ, "Ababaawa-Mosono, Mosono, Mosono. " Bere a ɔduruu kuro no kuro akyi no, ne werɛ fi, na ɔhwe ase na ne nsuo ketekete no abubu, na ɔkɔɔ fie. Na aberewa no kae sɛ, "Nsuo a ɛwɔ hɔ no wɔ he? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Mereba no, mehwehwee ase, na ɛbɔɔ. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Fa foforɔ kɔ. " Na ɔfaa no kɔeɛ; na Kɔtɔ kae sɛ, "Woasan aba ana? " Na ɔkae sɛ, "Saa din a woka kyerɛɛ me no, mefiri hɔ na me werɛ afi, na maba sɛ merebisa bio. " Kɔtɔ kae sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ no Ɔbaa-Daberewa-Mosono. " Na ɔkae sɛ, "Ho. " Ɔkɔɔ so kae no daa. kwan so fie, na ɔduruu fie no, ɔtenaa kuku no ase. Na aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ tutura bayerɛ no na fa ba. " Ɔyɛɛ ne nyinaa sɛdeɛ na ɔyɛ dada no, na ɔde aduane no baeɛ, na ɔde sii aberewa no anim, na aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Ka me din na fa didi. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ wo Nana Aberewa. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Mekyerɛ sɛ me ankasa din. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wo ankasa din? " Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Yiw. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ wo Nana Aberewa-Mosono. " Na aberewa no kaa sɛ, kae sɛ, "Fa didi. " Abofra no faa dii. Na aberewa no sii kwan, na ɔkae sɛ, "Mekɔ hwehwɛ onipa a ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ me din ne Mosono no. " Na ɔkɔɔ asuwa, na ɔhyiaa Ɔbontoya, abofra ketewa no, (na ɔtoo no ahohora sɛ), "Wo 'Bonto, wo maame! Wo 'Bonto, wo agya! woaka akyerɛ me banana akokoaa no sɛ wɔfrɛ me Mosono anaa? Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ me. " Ɔbaa aberewa no huu sɛ Adwene apataa no gyina nkyɛn hɔ. Ɔbɔɔ no ahohora sɛ, "Wo Adwene, wo maame! maame! Adwene, wo agya! Wo agya! Wo agya! Woaka akyerɛ me banana akokoaa no sɛ wɔfrɛ me Mosono anaa? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ me. " Ɔhuu sɛ Kɔtɔ no gyina hɔ. Ɔbɔɔ no ahohora sɛ, "Wo Kɔtɔ, wo maame! Wo maame! Wo Kɔtɔ, wo agya! Wo agya! Wo agya! anaa wo na woka kyerɛɛ me banana akokoaa sɛ mete Nkran, Ɔdehye mogya na wɔfrɛ me Mosono? " Kɔtɔ no kae sɛ, "Aane, me na meka kyerɛɛ no, na adɛn na woama obi ba abɛyɛ hare saa? " Na aberewa no bo fuwii, na ne calabash a na okura no, ɔmaa so kɔtoo Kɔtɔ no. Ɛno nti na Kɔtɔ no wɔ hono wɔ nakyi no. Ɛduruu tete wɔ Kɔtɔ no akyi no, na hono biara nni hɔ. Na Kɔtɔ no nso faa dɔte fitaa, na ɔde guu aberewa no ti so. Ɛno nti na atifi ntaadeɛ a ɛwɔ aberewa ti no, ɛwɔ ti nwi fitaa.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi mo nnye nni, na ahomegyeɛ no monkamfo me mma ho asɛmka.
