SƐNEA ƆKƆTƆ, ƆKƆTƆ NO, NYAA HONO, NA ABEREWA, ABEREWA NO, NYAA DWEN

Ɔbaa bi woo ne ba; bere a ɔsomaa no (baa baabiara) no, ɔkae sɛ, "Merenkɔ. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ tena hɔ, wo ne wo nyansa akwan. " Daa bere a ɔsomaa no (wɔ ɔsoma biara so) no, ɔkae sɛ, "Merenkɔ. " Ɛnneɛ da koro bi ɔkae sɛ, "Kɔbɔ nsuo na fa brɛ me. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Merenkɔ. " Na ɔbaa panyin no bɔɔ no na ɔkae sɛ, "Twɛn hɔ ara, na wubehu. " Na t Abofra no kasaeɛ na ɔkaa sɛ, "Mɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ 'I will see' no na mahunu (sɛnea ɛteɛ). " Na ɔhyɛɛ kwan no so, na ɔkɔɔ mmirika kɛseɛ, (na ne nan mmirika no nnyigyeɛ te sɛ) yiridi, yiridi, yiridi. Akyiri yi ɔduruu akuraa bi ase, na ɔhunuu ɔbaa panyin bi a ɔte hɔ. Bere a ɔhunuu no, na ɔrebɛtwe nakyi. Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, "Me mmanana, ntwe wo ho nsane, ɛfiri sɛ mahunu wo. " Na abofra no kɔɔ anim; na ɔpanin no kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na nsuo a ɛfiri wani mu no hohoro wo saa? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Na me maame ne me bɔ mu te hɔ, da biara a ɔbɛsɔre no, na ɔka sɛ, Twɛn hɔ ara na woahu, na ɔbɔɔ me nti, mekae sɛ, mɛba abɛhwehwɛ mehu na mahunu (nea ɛyɛ ɛte sɛ). " Na ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, "Me na meyɛ no. " Afei ɔkaa sɛ, "Kɔ fie no akyi hɔ na wotutu sare na wode ba; sɛ wokɔduru hɔ a, nea ɛbɛka sɛ, Tutu me, tutu me, tutu me no, ntutu; nanso nea ɛka sɛ, Ntutu me, ntutu me, ɛno deɛ, tutu. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, Ho! Ɛyɛ nokware sɛ ɔkɔɔ hɔ, sare no mu bi ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Tutu me u p, tu me, tu me;" afoforo nso kae sɛ, "Munntu me, montu me, montu me. " Wɔn a wɔkae sɛ, "Munntu me," wɔn na ɔtu no. Na ɔne wɔn bae; na ɔbaa panyin no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Skin wɔn na fa gu gya no so. " Na ɔfura wɔn ho na ɔde gu gya no so, na sɛ wɔnoa a, ɔdan wɔn ba aduane bi so, na ɔde si ɔbaa panyin no anim. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Kɔ kuro no akyi, na kɔhwɛ asie a nam da so, kɔhwɛ nam no mu, na fa deɛ wopɛ. " Na abofra no de bi bae, na ɔde guu ogya no so, yɛɛ nkuku. Bere a owiee no, ɔde sii ɔbaa panyin no anim. Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, "Wowiee anaa? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Yiw, nana. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Bra na bɔ fufu no. " Na ɔbaa panyin no kɔdaa nakyi, ɔkae sɛ, "Boaboa cooking jars no ano, fa gu me hwene mu, na bɔ fufu. " Abofra no kae sɛ, "Here is something wonderful, that we pound fufu in a person's nose; this is what mother spoke about. " Eyi nyinaa na ɔkaa no wɔ ne tirim. Ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔbɔɔ no, nanso berɛ a ɔbɔɔ no, ɔbɔɔ no brɛoo. Ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, "Bɔɔ no den, sɛ woanbɔ no den a, wo moma bɛpae. " Na ɔbɔɔ no, pi! pi! pi! na berɛ a ɔwieeɛ no, ɔhwie guu nkukuo no so, na ɔde sii hɔ wɔ ɔbaa panyin no anim. Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, "Me nana, fa me din ka na fa nni. " Na ɔkae sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ wo sɛ Ɔbaa Panyin. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Aane! onhunuu, sɛ ɔmmɛdidi. " Na ɔbaa panyin no dii ne nyinaa. Nna nwɔtwe no, ɔyɛɛ no saa bere nyinaa. Na da bi abofra no kɔɔ asubɔnten no ho kɔbɔɔ nsuo. Kɔtɔ no huu no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na obi ba abɛyɛ mmerɛw saa? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Dada bi wɔ hɔ a me ne no te, na sɛ meyɛ aduane na mema no a, ɔka sɛ, Ka me din na fa nni. Mese, Wɔfrɛ wo Dada, na ɔka sɛ, Aane! wunhuu ɔkwan a wobɛfa so adi,' na afei ɔdidi; ɛno nti na me ho yɛ hare yi. " Box no kae sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ no Old-woman-Mosono. " Abofra no kae sɛ, "Ho. " Na ɔde ne nsu-kutu no sii ne ti so. Kɔtɔ no kae sɛ, "Mommma mo werɛ mfi sɛnea worekɔ no. " Abofra no fii hɔ, bere a ɔreba no nyinaa, na ɔreka sɛ, "Old woman-Mosono, Mosono, Mosono. " Bere a ɔduruu kuro no ano no, ne werɛ fi, na ɔhwee ase na ne nsu-kannu no bubui, na ɔkɔɔ fie. Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, "Nsuo no wɔ he? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Bere a na mereba no, metwirii, na ebubui. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Fa foforo na kɔ. " Na ɔfaa no kɔe; na Rat no kae sɛ, "Woasan aba dedaw anaa? " Na ɔkae sɛ, "Saa din a woka kyerɛɛ me no, mefiri hɔ na me werɛ afi, na maba sɛ merebisa bio. " Rat no kae sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ no Ɔpanyin-baa-Mosono. " Na ɔkae sɛ, "Ho. " Ɔkɔɔ so kaa no bere nyinaa. kwan so fie, na ɔduruu fie hɔ a, ɔtoo (the cocoon) no ase. Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, "Kɔ na tutu yam na fa bra. " Ɔyɛɛ ne nyinaa sɛnea na ɔyɛ kan no, na ɔde aduane no bae, na ɔde sii ɔbaa no anim, na ɔbaa no kae sɛ, "Ka me din na fa nni. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ wo sɛ ɔbaa panyin. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me kyerɛ sɛ me din ankasa. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wo din ankasa? " Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, "Yiw. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ wo sɛ ɔbaa panyin. " Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, kae sɛ, "Fa na didi. " Abofra no gyee na ɔdii. Ɔpanyin no fii adi kɔɔ kwan no so, na ɔkae sɛ, "Merekɔhwehwɛ onipa a ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ me din ne Elephant no. " Na ɔkɔɔ asubɔnten no ho, na ɔhyiaa Obontoya, minnow no, (na ɔtoo no mmonnaa sɛ), "Wo 'Bonto, wo maame! wo 'Bonto, wo papa! woka kyerɛɛ me ba kumaa sɛ wɔfrɛ me Elephant? Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ me. " Ɔpanyin no hunuu sɛ Mind (mpataa no) gyina hɔ bɛn no, ɔdanee no sɛ, "Wo Mind, wo maame! maame! maame! wo Mind, wo papa! papa! woka kyerɛɛ me ba kumaa sɛ wɔfrɛ me Elephant? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ me. " Ɔhunuu sɛ Grasshopper no gyina hɔ. Ɔdanee no sɛ, "Wo kɔtɔ, wo maame! maame! maame! wo kɔtɔ, wo agya! agya! agya! ɛyɛ wo na woka kyerɛɛ me banana ba no sɛ me te Accra, me yɛ Ɔdehye mogya, na wɔfrɛ me Mosono? " Ntɛtea no kaa sɛ, "Yiw, me na meka kyerɛɛ no, na adɛn nti na woma obi ba no so tew saa? " Na ɔbaa panyin no bo fuwii, na ne calabash a na ɔwɔ no, ɔtwee no kɔɔ soro na ɔtoo no bɔɔ Ntɛtea no. Ɛno nti na Ntɛtea no wɔ akisikuru wɔ nakyi. Ɛnkyɛreeɛ wɔ Kɔtɔ no akyi no, na anidie biara nni hɔ. Na Kɔtɔ no nso faa sare, na ɔde guu ɔbaa panyin no ti so. Ɛno nti na mmea panyin ti ho atadeɛ no ho yɛ fitaa.

Me kyerɛwtohɔ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na fa a aka no nso kamfo me.
