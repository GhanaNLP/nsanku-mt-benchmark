SƐNEA KWATIMA, ODWENNINI NO, BAA BƐNYAA DIN ODWANINI

ƐHƆ (bere bi) na ɔpanyin bi a wɔfrɛ no Kwatima, na ɔka sɛ, "Merekɔhwehwɛ asase bi a mɛsi fie wɔ so. " Na Ɔsebɔ nso, saa bere koro no ara, na ɔwɔ adwene koro no ara pɛpɛɛpɛ. Ɛneɛ Kwatima, ɔpanyin no, fii aseɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwɛ asase a ɔpɛ no, na ɔayiyi no, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ na watumi asi fie. Na Ɔsebɔ, Ɔsebɔ no, nso kɔɔ (na bere a ɔduruu baabi a Odwennini no atu kwan no), ɔkaa sɛ, "Ayamye me! Akuraa a na mɛsi no, nanso na wɔnnyɛɛ ho adwuma biara no, manananom ahyɛ aseɛ dedaw. " Na ɔno nso, ɔyɛɛ peteɛ ketewaa bi na ɔkɔeɛ. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma fii aseɛ pueeɛ (i. e. anɔpa anɔpa) no, Odwennini no san kɔɔ bio. Bere a ɔduruu beaeɛ hɔ no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Ayamye me! Akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho sɛ me nananom rebɛboa me, (hwɛ) asase a wɔayiyi no. " Na ɔno nso, ɔprapraa hɔ kɔeɛ. Na Ɔsebɔfoɔ no nso baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh! eyi yɛ asɛm bi; ɛkame ayɛ sɛ me ho yɛ me hu. " Na ɔno nso, ɔkɔeɛ kɔɔ sare no so; ɔtwaa nnua no na ɔde sii hɔ. Na Shepherd no nso baeɛ, na ɔno nso hwɛɛ akyiri wɔ ahodwiriw mu. Ɔsan nso kɔɔ sare so sɛ ɔrekɔtwa nhaban, na ɔtoo hɔ kɔeɛ. Na sanba no baeɛ. Ɔno nso hwɛɛ akyiri (wɔ nkɔsoɔ a wɔayɛ), na ɔgyee (fie no) na ɔfirii hɔ kɔeɛ. Kwatima san baeɛ, na ɔtutu potholes na ɔde nkukuo no guu mu kɔeɛ. Na sanba no baeɛ, na ɔde nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn no kyekyeree nnua no so kɔeɛ. Na Kwatima san bae, na ɔkɔboaboaa nneɛma a asɛe no ano de sii hɔ na ɔkɔɔ. Na Ɔsebɔ no san bae, na ɔde dɔteɛ no fraa mu na ɔyɛɛ adum; na ɔkɔɔ. Na Kwatima san bae na ɔyɛɛ ɔdan atifi na ɔkɔɔ. Na Ɔsebɔ no san bae, ɔbɔɔ fam na ɔkɔɔ. Afei Kwatima nso bae, na ɔfaa mortar na ɔde plaster (fasu) yɛɛ no, na ɔkɔɔ fie. Ɛduruu berɛ a ɔkɔɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ba no sɛ, "Ɛde bɛsi nnɛ mewie fie no, na sɛ Akwasidae no bɛhwe aseɛ a, Monday no na yɛbɛyɛ fie foforɔ no inauguration, enti monhwehwɛ nkurɔfoɔ a wɔwɔ atuo a wɔde bɛkɔ akɔto ɔdan no mu. " Afei na Demon no nso aba (na ɔhunuu fie a wɔawie no). Ɔkaa sɛ, "Eh! Me nni hwee ka, gye sɛ megye me nneɛma na masan aba. " Afei ɔkɔɔ nakyi kɔka kyerɛɛ ne ba no sɛ, "Tu mmirika ntɛm ara sɛnea wubetumi na ka kyerɛ Ghost-hunter no sɛ, bere a Dream bɛtɔ Kwasida no, Memeneda (a edi hɔ no) mɛhyɛ inauguration ceremony no, enti ɔmmra na ɔmmɔ tuo mma me. " Na ne ba no kɔɔ nakyi kɔka kyerɛɛ no. Ɔkae sɛ (ghost-hunter no) "mate. " Afei da a edii Dream no anim no, Kwatima boaboaa ne nneɛma ano, na ɔde kɔtoo hɔ wɔ fie hɔ, na ɔsan bae. Na Satan nso boaboaa ne nneɛma ano, na ɔde kɔɔ hɔ. Ɛduruu berɛ a na ɛyɛ pefee sɛ yɛbɛhu nneɛma no, wɔ Akwasidae no so, Kwatima twaa ne ho hyiaeɛ kɔmaa ne nkurɔfoɔ a ɔne wɔn te no akwaaba; na Ɔsebɔfoɔ no nso, ɔyɛɛ nakwanya. Ɛwɔ Dwoada, berɛ a nneɛma no bɛdaa adi (i. e. anɔpa anɔpa), Kwatima ne ne ba no kɔɔ (to the new house); afei ɔkɔtoo tuo bi kɔɔ dan no mu. Na Ghost no nso baeɛ; ɔtee tuo no, ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛnnɛ nyɛ da pa, ɛnnɛ ahonhom no reyi tuo. " Na ɔno nso baeɛ; na Ghost-hunter no nso kɔɔ fie hɔ, na ɔmaa tuo no teɛ sɛ, pomm! Ghost no baeɛ na ɔhunuu Kwatima ne ne ba no a wɔte hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo adwuma ne dɛn? " Kwatima nso kaa sɛ, "Wo nso, wo adwuma ne dɛn? " Bɛnsoa no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wunni ho nteaseɛ a, me nso, minni bi. " Na Kwatima kaa sɛ, "Sɛ saa a, ɛnneɛ yɛn mmienu nyinaa bɛtena ha. " Enti wɔtenaa hɔ. Da biara, bere a Bɛnsoa no sɔre no, ɔkɔ abɔmmɔ, na ɔde nam ba. Ɛhɔ ara na na ɛteɛ daa. Nanso Kwatima deɛ, ɔamfa hwee amma. Afei da bi a ɔsurofoɔ no kɔɔ baabi a wɔtow nneɛma gu no, ɔbɔɔ ne ho mmɔden paa sɛ ɔbɛkyere biribi, nanso antumi. Ɔsan baa fie, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Kwatima, sɛ nnɛ woankɔ baabi a wɔtow nneɛma gu no na woamfa nam bi aba a, nea mɛyɛ wo yi, ɛrenyɛ wo dɛ. " Kwatima fii nakwantu no ase, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a wɔtow nneɛma gu no, na ɔde nwura-bokɔ bi bae na ɔtoo hɔ. Bere a Lion no huu aboa no, ɛyɛɛ no nwanwa sɛ biribiara. Bere a nneɛma no bɛdaa adi (i.e. adeɛ kye a, Kwatima kɔeɛ, na ɔsan de foforo (antelope) baeɛ. Elephant no kaa sɛ, "Yiw! " Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ba no sɛ, "Hwɛ ha, merekɔ kwaeɛ no so, na me ne Kwatima nso de wɔn rekɔ, na abɔfra kumaa a mede no brɛɛ wo da a ɛtwaa toɔ no, bere a yɛakɔ no, frɛ Kwatima ba no na bisa no sɛ, Sɛ wo papa kɔ kwaeɛ no so kɔkyere nam a, kyerɛ yɛn wɔ drawing yi so sɛnea ɔyɛ no. Na nnipa no (Elephant ne Kwatima) fii aseɛ. Na Leopard ba no kae sɛ, "Kwatima ba, bra na yɛnni agorɔ. " Ɔbae, ɔkae sɛ, "Papa kɔ sare so a, saa na ɔkyere agorɔ. " Na ɔhuruw, na ɔkyeree ɔkraman ba a ɔtwa toɔ no na ɔgyee ne mene. Kwatima ba no kae sɛ, "Wo kɔfa ɔboɔ bi. " Leopard ba no kɔfaa baako bae. Ɔde sii hɔ. Kwatima ba no twee ne ho fii ho na ɔbaa hɔ na ɔde ne ti bɔɔ ɔboɔ no, pim! na ɛbubui yɛɛ nketenkete. Ɔkraman ba no kaa sɛ, "Ah. " Na wɔn agyanom firi kwaeɛ no mu baeɛ, na ɔkraman ba no ka kyerɛɛ nagya nea ɛsiiɛ, na Kwatima ba no nso ka kyerɛɛ nagya nea ɛsiiɛ. Ɛnneɛ na wɔwɔ hɔ, berɛ a da bi adeɛ bi bɛkyeree Kwatima nan ho, na na ɔreyɛ afiri hɔ, nanso na ɔsono no dwen sɛ ɔregye nakyi sɛ ɔrebɛkum no. Ɛhɔ ara na ɔsono no tuu mmirika kɔɔ Kwatima so sɛ ɔrebɛkyere no. Na Kwatima adwane, adane, adane. Ɔkɔɔ ɔtare bi a ɛda hɔ, a na Ɔnanka bi wɔ mu. Kwatima kaa sɛ, "Father Rider, I flee to you for protection that the Lion may not catch me. " Ɛhɔ ara na Rider no kaa sɛ, "You, friend, you have done right (to come to me), so take my hand. " Kwatima bɔɔ ne ho mmɔden na ɔtoo ne nsa. Saa kwan yi so na Kwatima nyaa Odwa-nini (lit. Shake hand with the Rider) din. Ɛfiri ne nsa a ɔne Rider no bɔeɛ no mu.

Me kyerɛwtohɔ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na fa a aka no nso kamfo me.
