SƐNEA KWATIMA, ODWENNINI NO, BAA BƐGYEE ODWANINI DIN

NA Odwennini bi wɔ hɔ, Kwatima, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Mekɔhwehwɛ asase bi a mɛsi fie wɔ so. " Na Daboɔ no nso, saa berɛ korɔ no ara na nadwene yɛɛ pɛ. Ɛnna Kwatima, Odwennini no, bɔɔ ne ho sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwɛ asase a ɔpɛ no, na ɔyii no, wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ ɔbɛsi fie. Na Osebo, Daboɔ no, nso kɔeɛ (na ɔduruu baabi a Odwennini no apueeɛ no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Ayamye me! Saa akuraa a na merekyekye no, nanso wɔnnyɛɛ ho adwuma biara no, mnananom nsamanfo ahyɛ aseɛ dedaw. " Na ɔno nso, ɔyɛɛ petee ketewa na ɔkɔeɛ. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma fii aseɛ pueeɛ (i. e. anɔpatutuutu no), Odwennini no san kɔɔ bio. Bere a ɔduruu baabi no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Ayamye me! Ɛyɛ nokware sɛ me nananom nsamanfo rebɛboa me, (hwɛ) fam a wɔapopa no. " Na ɔno nso, ɔprapraa nwura no kɔɔ. Na Daboɔ no nso baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Aane! eyi yɛ asɛm bi; ɛkame ayɛ sɛ ɛyɛ me hu. " Na ɔno nso, ɔkɔɔ nwura no ho, twaa adunsina no kɔtoo hɔ. Na Odwennini no nso baeɛ, na ɔno nso hwɛɛ akyiri wɔ ahodwiriw mu. Ɔno nso kɔɔ nwura so bɛtwaa nhahamama no, na ɔtoo guu hɔ kɔeɛ. Na Daboɔ no san baeɛ. Ɔno nso, ɔhwɛɛ akyiri (sɛdeɛ nkɔsoɔ no teɛ), na ɔtoo ne boɔ kyerɛɛ fie hɔ kɔeɛ. Kwatima san baeɛ, na ɔtwaa atokuro no hyɛɛ mu adunsina no, na ɔkɔeɛ. Na Daboɔ no san baeɛ, na ɔkyekyeree nnua a ɛtwam no, na ɔkɔeɛ. Na bio Kwatima baeɛ, na ɔkɔgyee sare no de sii hɔ na ɔkɔeɛ. Na Daboɔ no san baeɛ, na ɔde atɛkyɛ no fraa mu yɛɛ no plaster, na ɔkɔeɛ. Na bio Kwatima baeɛ na ɔyɛɛ ɛdan atifi kata no, na ɔkɔeɛ. Na bio Daboɔ no baeɛ, na wɔbɔɔ fam ahodoɔ no, na wɔkɔeɛ. Afei Kwatima nso baeɛ, na ɔyii dɔte fitaa no na ɔde plaster bɔɔ afasuo no, na ɔkɔeɛ fie. Bere a ɔkɔɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne abofra no sɛ, "Ɛde besi da a mewie fie no, na bere a Akwasidae bɛtɔ no, ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, yɛbɛyɛ ofie foforɔ hyɛ ase afahyɛ, nti monhwehwɛ nnipa a wɔwɔ atuo a wɔde bɛkɔ akɔto dan no mu. " Afei na Daboɔ no nso baeɛ (na ɔhunuu fie a awie no). Ɔkaa sɛ, "Eh! Me nni hwee ka, gye sɛ mɛgye magyapadeɛ na san ba. Afei ɔkɔɔ ne ho, na ɔkɔɔ ka kyerɛɛ nabofra no sɛ, "Dwane ntɛm ara sɛdeɛ wobɛtumi, na kɔka kyerɛ Ghost-hunter no sɛ, sɛ Adae tɔ Kwasiada a, Memeneda a ɛdi hɔ no mɛdi ofie foforɔ hyɛ ase afahyɛ, enti ma no mmra na ɔmmɔ tuo mma me. " Na nabofra no kɔɔ ka kyerɛɛ no. Ɔno (the Ghost-hunter) kae sɛ, "Metee. " Afei wɔ da a ɛdi Adae no anim no, Kwatima boaboaa nagyapadeɛ ano, de kɔsiee hɔ wɔ fie hɔ, na ɔsan bae. Na Daboɔ no nso, ɔboaboaa nadeɛ ano de kɔɔ hɔ. Bere a ɛhɔ hyerɛn sɛ wobɛhu nneɛma no, Akwasidae no so, Kwatima kyinkyin kɔtoo nnipa a wɔne no te no ma wɔgyaa wɔn; na Daboɔ no nso bɔɔ ne nkrabew. Ɛdwoada, bere a nneɛma bɛpuee (i.e. anɔpatutuutu) no, Kwatima ne ne abofra no kɔɔ (ofie foforɔ no); afei wɔkɔtoo tuo guu dan no mu. Na Daboɔ no nso baeɛ; ɔtee tuo no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnnɛ da nnyɛ da pa, ɛnnɛ ahonhom no reto atuo. " Na ɔno nso baeɛ; na Ghost-hunter no nso kɔɔ fie hɔ, na ɔmaa tuo no teɛteɛɛ sɛ, pomm! Daboɔ no baeɛ na wɔhunuu Kwatima ne ne abofra no a wɔte hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo eguadwa bɛn? " Kwatima nso kaa sɛ, "Wo nso, wo eguadwa bɛn? " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wo nni sianteɛ a, me nso me nni bi. " Na Kwatima kaa sɛ, "Sɛ saa na ɛteɛ a, ɛnneɛ yɛn mmienu nyinaa bɛtena ha. " Enti wɔtenaa hɔ. Da biara, sɛ Daboɔ no sɔre a, na ɔkɔ ahayɔ, na ɔsan de nam ba. Ná ɛte saa daa. Nanso Kwatima deɛ, na ɔmfa hwee mma. Afei da bi a osurofoɔ no kɔɔ nwura mu no, ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛkyere adeɛ, nanso wɔantumi. Ɔsan baa fie, na ɔkaa sɛ, Kwatima, sɛ ɛnnɛ da woankɔ nwura mu na woamfa nam bi amma a, nea mɛyɛ wo yi, wo ani rennye ho. Kwatima de sii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ nwura no mu, na ɔde nwura baeɛ na ɔtoo no guu hɔ. Daboɔ no hunuu aboa no a, ɛyɛɛ no nwanwa sɛ adeɛ biara. Bere a nneɛma bɛpuee (i.e. anɔpatutuutu) no, na Kwatima akɔ, na bio ɔde antelope foforɔ bi aba. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "E! " Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne abofra no sɛ, "Hwɛ ha, merekɔ nwura so, na me ne Kwatima nso rekɔ, na ɔtwa ba a mede no brɛɛ wo da a etwaam no, sɛ yɛakɔ a, frɛ Kwatima abofra no na bisa no sɛ, Sɛ wo agya kɔ nwura so kɔkyere nam a, kyerɛ yɛn sɛnea ɔyɛ no wɔ ɔtwee yi so. Na nnipa no (Osebo ne Kwatima) sii kwan. Na Leopard ba no kae sɛ, "Kwatima ba, bra na yɛnni agoru. " Ɔbae no, ɔkae sɛ, "Sɛ agya kɔ nwura so a, saa na ɔkyere agorɔ. " Na ɔpuee, na ɔkyeree ɔtwa ba no na ɔkyeree ne menem. Kwatima ba no kae sɛ, "So wokɔfa ɔbotan bi? " Leopard ba no kɔe na ɔde baako bae. Ɔde sii hɔ. Kwatima ba no twee ne ho fii ho baeɛ na afei ɔde ne etire bɔɔ ɔboɔ no so, pim! na ɛbɛbɔɔ nsinnsin. Ɔkraman ba no kaeɛ sɛ, "Ah. " Na wɔn agyanom firi nwura mu baeɛ, na ɔkraman ba no kaa nea ɛsii no kyerɛɛ nagya, na Kwatima ba no nso kaa nea ɛsii no kyerɛɛ nagya. Afei na wɔwɔ hɔ, bere a da bi biribi kuraa Kwatima nan ho, na ɔrebɛpopa no, nanso Daboɔ no susui sɛ ɔsan nakyi wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ ɔbɛku no. Ntɛm ara na Daboɔ no tuu mmirika baa Kwatima so wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ wɔbɛkyere no. Na Kwatima kiri, kiri, kiri. Ɔkɔɔ asuwea bi a ɛda hɔ a Ɔnanka bi wɔ mu no nkyɛn. Kwatima kae sɛ, "Agya, Ɔnanka, miguan kɔ wo nkyɛn kɔhwehwɛ bammɔ sɛ Daboɔ no renkyere me. " Ɛhɔ ara na Ɔnanka no kae sɛ, "Wo, yɔnkoɔ, woadi hokwan sɛ woba me nkyɛn, enti fa me nsa. " Kwatima bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛma no nsa. Saa na Kwatima nyaa din Odwa-nini (lit. Shake your hand with the Ɔnanka). Ɛfiri ne nsamukyea a ɔne Ɔnanka no dii no.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi mo nnye nni, na ahomegyeɛ no monkamfo me mma ho asɛmka.
