Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA NEA ƐKƆƆ SO NO, ƆPƐPƐN NO, KƆƆ NWURA NO MU, NA ƆMAA AKƆKƆ, ƆPƐN NO, BAA ADAN NO MU

AKOKO, Ɔhen, ne Akokɔ, Akokɔ-dwoa, firi agya baako na wɔfiri ɛna baako. Afei Akokɔ no ka kyerɛɛ Akokɔ-dwoa no sɛ, Kɔ Tall-grass-country, na me nso, mɛkɔ Thick-forest-country, na sɛ wunya aduane a, ɛnneɛ wobɛba abɛka akyerɛ me. Ɛnna wɔtetew mu. Mfeɛ mmiɛnsa ni na Akokɔ no anhu ne nuabea. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne kunu sɛ, Wo ne me nkɔ, ɛfiri sɛ merekɔhwehwɛ me nuabea. Ɛnna ne kunu kaa sɛ, Wo akasa yi, mate, kɔ, na ma me ne wo nkɔ. Na ɔne no kɔɔ kuro bi akyi (baabi a ne nuabea te). Wɔhunuu dɔteɛ pɔtokɔ bi wɔ hɔ, na ɛhɔ na ɔne ne nuabea no hyiaa. Na Hene bisaa Akokɔ no sɛ, Bere a wokɔɔ Tall-grass-country no, wunyaa aduan a wubedi anaa? Obuae sɛ, Baabi a mekɔe no, na guinea-grain nkutoo na ɛwɔ hɔ; Na bere a mewoo me mma bɛyɛ baanum no, wɔde wɔn ho bɔɔ me ho, na bere a na wɔn kɛse te sɛɛ (a ɛkyerɛ wɔn kɛse), mede wɔn too manim, na ɔbɔmmɔfo bi too tuo no, pom! na wɔkunkum me mma, na anka sɛ mennim adwene a, anka wɔbɛkunkum me nso. Bere a ɔrekasa wiei no, Ɔhen no kae sɛ, Baabi a mekɔe no, ntɛm ara na meyɛɛ obi a ɛho hia paa. Aturukuku bisaa no sɛ, Ka nea wopɛ sɛ woka wɔ wo person of importance ho no kyerɛ me. Afei Ɔhen no kae sɛ, Nkyerɛaseɛ a ɛwɔ person of importance mu ne sɛ, sɛ me wura trɛw nkatanim pa mu a, t Ɛhɔ na mekɔda so, na me mpapoɔ gu so, na sɛ me wura fa nam de gu soup-stew no mu a, ɛnneɛ meyi nea mepɛ; aduane biara a me wura bɛdi no, mekɔfa bi, na sɛ medidi saa a, me wura ntumi mfa ne tuo anaa poma mmɔ me. Kyerɛ sɛ nea enti a meka sɛ meyɛ nipa a ɛho hia paa. Ɔhen no kae sɛ, Baabi a wote hɔ no, ɛnyɛ baabi pa, enti wɔ Memeneda, ansa na Akwasidae bi bɛba, fa wo mma, ne wo kunu, na ma wɔn nyinaa ntena yɛn fie. Aturukuku kae sɛ, Metee sɛ, mɛkɔ, na Memeneda ansa na Akwasidae bi bɛba no, na maba. Afei ɔsɔfoɔ bi baa Ɔhen no akuraa ase, ɔbɔɔ ne ho ban ing ne dɔn ketewa no. Wɔbisaa no sɛ, "Agyapa, ɛyɛ dɛn? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Dente ka sɛ woama no kɔm de no mfeɛ mmienu, enti ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ ɔhene nkyɛn na woka kyerɛ no sɛ ɔmfa nkokɔ (ne) nkesua dumienu mmra. " Afei ɔhene no kɔkyeree nkokɔ nnan, de nkesua dumien bae, faa nam a wɔayam, na ɔde maa Dente bosom no wɔ nafɔremma afɔremuka no so, twaa nkokɔ anan no ti na ɔtwee wɔn p Ɔkyekyeree wɔn wɔ hɔ, na ɔfaa wɔn ntaban ne wɔn ti ne wɔn nan, na ɔde gluu guu dɔte afɔremuka no nkyɛn; na ɔde gluu a ɛto so du nso guu so. Afei, da a edii Dream no anim no, na Aturukuku rekɔhyia ne nuabea. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu ne nuabea no agyanom ne ne ti, ne nan, ne ntaban, ne mmerɛboɔ ne ne stern wɔ afɔremuka a ɛyɛ kronkron ma Dente no so. Na Aturukuku kaa sɛ, " Me renkɔ Kwaeɛ Man no mu da bio, ɛfiri sɛ kwaeɛ mufoɔ no nyɛ papa. " Saa na Aturukuku ba bɛtenaa Tall-grass-country, berɛ a Ɔbenfoɔ no tenaa fie.

Me kyerɛwtohɔ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na fa a aka no nso kamfo me.
