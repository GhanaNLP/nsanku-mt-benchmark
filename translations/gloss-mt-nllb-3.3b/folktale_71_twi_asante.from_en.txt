Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

WO TE SƐ NWONWA SƐ KWAKU ANANSE, AKURUKURU NO

Bere bi a na KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse no te hɔ no, Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no maame, Nisia yaree. Wɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔfaa aduruyɛni bi sɛ ɔmmɛhwɛ no. Bere a ɔbae no, ɔkae sɛ wɔmfa sika a ɛyɛ future (te sɛ £8) mma no sɛ wɔmfa nhwɛ ne yare no. Afei, Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ Sky-God nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɔyɔnko yi refa wo sika daakye kwa; ontumi nhwɛ no, enti ma menhwɛ no mma wo, na sɛ menhwɛ no a, me ti betumi ayera. " Kwaku Ananse faa sika daakye a ne bo yɛ asuanu (i. e. £4), na ɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔsoro-nyame no sɛ, "Sɛ me ntumi nnsa yareɛ yi a, mɛma wo gyata a ɔte aseɛ. " Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, na Nsia, Onyankopɔn maame, Ɔsoro-nyame no, afiri hɔ. Wɔkae sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse, ɛyɛ dɛn? " Kwaku Ananse afiri saa beaeɛ no; ɔadwane. Afei, ɔkasa ne nakyi-akyi (i. e. me werɛ firii sɛ mɛka saa asɛm no) Kwaku Ananse adamfo paa ne Okusie, Okisi no. Ɔka kyerɛɛ Okisi nea ɛkɔɔ so no, na ɔka sɛ, "Ɛno ne nea mahunu, enti seesei ɛsɛ sɛ wo boa me. " Okisi no buaa sɛ, "Ɛkwan bɛn so na metumi aboa wo? " (Kwaku Ananse) buaa sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ wo kɔ tu asase ase kosi sɛ ɛbɛduru pempe a Sky-god no te so no ase. " Okisi no kaa sɛ, "Me na mɛyɛ ama wo. " Enti ɔyɛɛ no. Okisi bɛbɔɔ Kwaku Ananse amanneɛ sɛ, Mawie. Kwaku Ananse kɔfrɛɛ ne ba Ntikuma; na ɔtwaa abɛn maa no; na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔnkɔ amena no mu na ɔntena abɛn no ase, na sɛ ohu sɛ Ɔsoro-nyame no atena abɛn no so a, ɔmmɔ nabɛn no na ɔse:

"Sɛ wokum Kwaku Ananse a, mmusuakuo no bɛba asɛe!

Sɛ wogye Kwaku Ananse kyɛw a, mmusuakuo no nyinaa de nne bɛwosow! "

Ntikuma kɔɔ ahɔhodan no ase kɔtenaa hɔ, na Kwaku Ananse fii nakwantu no ase sɛ ɔrekɔ Nyame, Ɔsoro Nyame no nkyɛn wɔ nankasa ɔpɛ mu. Bere a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, na Ɔsoro Nyame no nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn akyere no; wɔde no kɔɔ Ɔsoro Nyame no anim. Afei ɔkasafoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Wo, Kwaku Ananse, woka kyerɛɛ Onyankopɔn, Ɔsoro-Onyame, sɛ sɛ ne maame, Nsia, awu a, ɔbɛtumi akum wo; enti esiane sɛ Nsia afiri hɔ nti, yɛn na yɛbɛkum wo. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ woma me kwan ma meka me asɛm. " Nanso kyerɛkyerɛfoɔ no kyeree no sɛ wɔnkɔkum no. Ntikuma de ne nne so kae sɛ, "Sɛ wokum Kwaku Ananse a, mmusuakuo no bɛba asɛe!

Sɛ wogye Kwaku Ananse kyɛw a, mmusuakuo no nyinaa de nne bɛwosow! "

Wɔkaa sɛ, "Tie, tie! " Na bio abɛn no bɔɔ:

"Sɛ wokum Kwaku Ananse a, mmusuakuo no bɛba asɛe!

Sɛ wogye Kwaku Ananse kyɛw a, mmusuakuo no nyinaa de nne bɛwosow! "

Afei Krontihene sɔre bɔɔ Ɔsoro-Nyame no amaneɛ sɛ, "Saa abusuakuo yi yɛ wo dea, Ɔsoro-Nyame, ne Ya, Tete-ma-asase. Ɛde bɛsi nnɛ, mo nsa aka Kwaku Ananse na mo rebɛkum no. Ya, Tete-ma-asase, ka sɛ, Ma no nkɔ, enti sɛ woma no kɔ a, ɛbɛyɛ yiye. " Ɛneɛ Ɔsoro-Nyame no maa akyerɛkyerɛfoɔ no gyaee no. Ɛno nti na asɛm bi wɔ hɔ sɛ, Wo ho yɛ nwanwa sɛ Kwaku Ananse.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
