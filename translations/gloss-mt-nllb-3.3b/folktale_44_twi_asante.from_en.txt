Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBƐYƐƐ SƐ PARROT NO TAIL NO BƐYƐƐ KAR

WƆKYERƐ SƐ, na ɔhemmaa bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔwoo ɔbabea a ne ho yɛ fɛ. Ɛhɔ na na ɔwɔ, nanso bere a wɔde no maa obiara aware no, ɔpowee sɛ ɔbɛware. Daa, bere a nkurɔfo (ne nkurɔfo) kɔkasa (ne no, wɔ aware ho) no, na ɔka sɛ, Ɛnyɛ saa koraa. Afei abɔdeɛ no, Ɔnini (the Monkey), na ɔwɔ baabi wɔ kwaeɛ no mu, na ɔte ɔbɔn kɛseɛ bi mu. Ɛdii da bi a na ɔwɔ hɔ (wɔ ne ɔbodan mu) no, ɔtee sɛ abɔfoɔ bi retwam, na wɔrekasa fa ababaa fɛfɛ no ho asɛm sɛ, bere a wɔde no ama obiara awareɛ no, ɔkae sɛ ɔmpɛ no. Nana no da hɔ, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ, Mebɛsakra me ho ayɛ me ho sɛ aberanteɛ pa, na meware Ɔhemmaa Maame ba. Ɛyɛ nokware, nana no danee ne ho sɛ aberanteɛ, a na nani nna hɔ yie. Ɔde nani sii akuraa ase. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na ɔregye kola-nuts. Bere a ɔduruu kuro no ano no, na ɔhemmaa maame no ba a na ɔkɔɔ akuturuku no abɔ no, asan aba na ɔretete ntadeɛ no ama ayow. Ɔnanka no twitwiw, na ɛbɛhwee ɔhemmaa maame no ba no so. Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na worehwie ahurututu agu me ho so? " Ɔnanka no kae sɛ, "Wo na wofata (wɔ saa asɛm no mu), Amu. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Maguadi abodin no nyɛ Amu. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Wowɔ hokwan (wɔ saa asɛm no mu), Esono. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Maguadi abodin no nyɛ Esono. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Wo na wofata (wɔ saa asɛm no mu), me yere. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ, meware wo. " Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no ne Ɔnanka no sii hɔ. ff ma ne fie. Ɛhɔ ara na ɔhemmaa ba no kɔɔ hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, Eh! eh! eh! (ɔde anigyeɛ rekasa akuturuku), mahu nea mepɛ no. Ɔhemmaa no kaa sɛ, Awiei koraa no! Ɛhe na me kunu no wɔ? Ɔkaa sɛ, Me ni, nana. Ɔhemmaa no kyeree Ɔnanka no nsa, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Wo ne wo yere ntena ase anigyeɛ mu. Bere a anwummere no yɛɛ nwini no, ɔkɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn kɔhomee. Ɛyɛɛ saa nnawɔtwe. Ɛduruu akyiri no, ɔkaa sɛ, Me yere, merekɔ me kuro no mu nanso mɛsan aba. Ne yere kaa sɛ, Ɛneɛ me ne wo bɛkɔ (bom). Ɔnanka no kaa sɛ, Dabi. Ɔnanka no kasaa kasaa (na ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛgye no adi sɛ ɔnkɔ), nanso Queen Mother ba no antie (no). Bere a nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔne ne kunu no sii kwan so. Bere a wɔrekɔ no, Nana no twetwee ne ho (firi kwan no so) na ɔde kɔɔ baabi a wɔtow nneɛma gu. Ɔhemmaa maame no ba no kae sɛ, Dɛn na woreyɛ yi, ɛyɛ kwan yi? Nana no kae sɛ, Wo deɛ, to wano na di makyi. Ɔno ne no sii hɔ, na wɔkɔɔ so, na (ɛwieeɛ koraa no) Nana no yeraa wɔ ɔbodan bi mu. Ɔhemmaa maame no ba no nso kɔɔ mu. Ntɛm ara na Ɔnanka no danee ne ho yɛɛ no aboa kɛseɛ; Afei ɔkasa kyerɛɛ Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no sɛ, Eyi ne nea meyɛ ampa, ɛfiri sɛ mesesaa me ho sɛ mɛware wo, ɛfiri sɛ na wote wo maame ne wo papa mmati so na woka sɛ, Biribiara nni hɔ (mesuro) ; Mɛkum wo (mprempren); twitwiw me ti na kyere akokɔ no ma me; sɛ wunnya bi a, wowie. d ne aduane. Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no tenaa ne nkyɛn, ɔresaw na ɔrehwehwɛ nsuo. Afei, na wɔwɔ hɔ, berɛ a Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no hunuu sɛ Akoo bi retwam. Na Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no rebɛbue nano aka sɛ, "Agya Ako," berɛ a Akoo no de ne nsa guu nano no so. Akoo no faa nakodeɛ no, ɔsiesiee ne nyinaa, na ɔde (to) no sii ne moma so. Nana no kae sɛ, "Tetew me ti. " Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no kae sɛ, "Mabrɛ sɛ mɛtew; Sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wokum me a, kum me. " Na Nana no reyɛ ama ne ti so sɛ ɔbɛkyere Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no, bere a Ako no too dwom sɛ, boom! Akoo no kɔɔ ɔbodan no mu kɔgyee Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no ne Nana ma ɔde wɔn kɔɔ fie sɛ ɔrekɔbɔ amanneɛ sɛnea ɛbaa sɛ wahu Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no ne Nana no, na waka ɔkwan a ɔfa so kum Nana no kyerɛɛ wɔn. Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no nso, ɔde nasɛm no nyinaa kyerɛɛ. Ɔhemmaa no kae sɛ, "Akoo, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mefa de da wo ase? " Akoo no kae sɛ, "Akokoraa, mempɛ hwee, me deɛ mepɛ palm-oil kakra. " Ɔhemmaa no kɔfaa palm-oil bae. Ɔkae sɛ, "Akokoraa, mefa me dua no na mehyɛ no wɔ saa palm-oil yi mu, na mefa so yɛ no agyiraehyɛdeɛ (wɔ akwantu a ɛyɛ akokoduru yi). " Saa kwan yi so na Akoo bi dua no bɛyɛɛ kɔkɔɔ. Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
