Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐBƐYƐ NEA ƐBAA SƐ PARROT NO NSA YƐƐ RODO

WƆKYERƐ SƐ Ɔhemmaa bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔwoo abaayewa fɛfɛ bi. Na ɔwɔ hɔ, nanso bere a wɔde no maa obiara awareɛ no, na ɔmpɛ sɛ ɔware. Bere biara a nnipa bɛkɔ akɔka awareɛ ho asɛm no, na ɔka sɛ, Akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho. Afei na abɔdeɛ no, Ɔnini (Ɔnanka) no, wɔ baabi wɔ nwura no mu, na ɔte ɔbɔn kɛse bi so. Ɛyɛɛ da bi a na ɔwɔ hɔ (wɔ ne ɔbodan no mu) no, ɔtee sɛ abɔfoɔ bi retwa mu, na wɔredi ababaawa fɛfɛ no ho asɛmti, sɛnea ɔde ama obiara wɔ awareɛ mu no, ɔkae sɛ ɔmpɛ no. Ɔnanka no da hɔ, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ, Mɛsesa me ho ayɛ me aberante pa, na mayi Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba aware. Ɛwɔ nokware mu no, Ɔnanka no danee ne ho yɛɛ no mmabunu; na ɔnhwɛ nea ɔbɛkyɛ no, na ɛnyɛ kwan. Ɔhyɛɛ nase kɔɔ akuraa hɔ. Bere a na ɔrekɔ no, na ɔrewe kola-nuts. Ɛduruu kuro no kwanano no, na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba a na wakɔtu ahohoro no asan aba na ɔrehyɛ ntaadeɛ no ma ɛyow. Ɔnanka ntasuo no, na ɛbɛtoo Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no gyegyeegye so. Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no kae sɛ, Adɛn nti na wogu me gyegyeegye no? Ɔnanka no kae sɛ, Wo na wowɔ hokwan, Amu. Ɔkae sɛ, Mntɔn abodin no nyɛ Amu. Obuae sɛ, Wowɔ hokwan, Esono. Ɔkae sɛ, Mntɔn abodin no nyɛ Esono. Obuae sɛ, Woyɛ hokwan, me yere. Ɔkae sɛ, Afei meware wo. Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba ne Ɔnanka no sii hɔ. ff ma (ne) fie. Ntɛm ara na Ɔhemmaa ba no kɔɔ hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, Eh! eh! eh! (na ɔde anigyeɛ a emu yɛ den rekuturuku ne nne), mahu nea mepɛ no. Ɔhemmaa kaa sɛ, Awieeɛ koraa no! Ɛhe na me ɔbabarima-in-law wɔ? Ɔkae sɛ, Me wɔ ha, nanabea. Ɔhemmaa no ne Ɔnanka no bɔɔ nsa, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Ma wo ne wo yere ntena ase anigye mu. Bere a anwummere yɛɛ nwini no, ɔne ne yere no kɔdae. Ɛyɛɛ saa nna nwɔtwe. Na ɛduruu tenten no, ɔkae sɛ, Me yere, merekɔ makuraa hɔ nanso mɛsan aba. Ne yere kae sɛ, Afei me ne wo bɛkɔ. Ɔnanka no kae sɛ, Dabi. Ɔnanka no bɔɔ nkɔmmɔ kɔkɔɔ, nanso Ɔhemmaa no ba no antie no. Bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔne ne kunu no sii kwan. Bere a wɔrekɔ no, Ɔnanka no twaa ne ho fii kwan no so kɔɔ nwura no mu. Ɔhemmaa no ba no kae sɛ, Dɛn na woreyɛ, kwan no ni? Ɔnanka no kae sɛ, Wo nso, to wo ano na di me akyi. Ɔne no kɔeɛ, na wɔkɔɔ so kɔeɛ, na (ɛdane, awieeɛ koraa no) Ɔnanka no yeraa wɔ ɔbodan bi mu. Ɔhemmaa no ba no nso baa mu. Ntɛm ara na Ɔnanka no danee ne ho yɛɛ no aboa kɛseɛ. Afei ɔkasa kyerɛɛ Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no sɛ, "Eyi ne me ankasa, ɛfiri sɛ mesesaa me ho wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ mɛware wo, ɛfiri sɛ na wote wo maame ne wo papa mpa so, na woka sɛ, Me nsuro hwee (se) ; Mɛkum wo (seesei); twitwiw me etire na kyere ntontom no ma me; Sɛ woannya bi a, woawie. d aduane. Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no tenaa ne nkyɛn, na ɔreporɔw na ɔrehwehwɛ ntontom. Afei, na wɔwɔ hɔ, bere a Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no huu sɛ Akoo bi retwa mu. Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no rebɛbue nano aka sɛ, "Agya Ako," bere a Akoo no de ne nsa guu ne ano no. Akoo no faa ne tuo no, siesiee ne nyinaa, na ɔde kokoam fã no too ne kokoɔ no so. Ɔnanka no kae sɛ, "Twetwe me etire." Ɔhemmaa no maame ba no kae sɛ, "Mebrɛ sɛ mɛwe; sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mokum me a, munkum me." Ɔnanka no rebɛma ne etire so akura Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no, na Akoo tuo teɛɛm sɛ, pomm! Akoo no kɔɔ ɔbodan no mu kɔgyee Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no ne Ɔnanka no sɛ wɔmfa wɔn nkɔ fie nkɔma amaneɛbɔ no sɛnea ɛbaa sɛ wahu Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no ne Ɔnanka no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛnea wakum Ɔnanka no. Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no nso, ɔde nabasɛm no ho nsakyerɛeɛ no nyinaa maeɛ. Ɔhemmaa no kae sɛ, "Akoo, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mede kɔda wo ase? " Akoo no kae sɛ, "Nana, mempɛ hwee, me deɛ mepɛ palm-oil kakra. " Ɔhemmaa no kɔfaa palm-oil no bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Nana, me refa me dua yi na mehyɛ no palm-oil yi mu, na mefa so yɛ no symbol (kyerɛwtohɔ) (akwantu a akokoɔduro wom yi). " Saa na Akoo bi dua no yɛɛ no kɔkɔɔ. Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
