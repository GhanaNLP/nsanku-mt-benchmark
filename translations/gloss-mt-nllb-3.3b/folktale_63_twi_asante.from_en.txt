Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ABOSOM, NNYANKOPƆN NKETEWA NO, BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU

Ɔbɔfoɔ bi ne ne yere tenaa ɔbɔfoɔ nsraban mu. Na wɔyɛ ahiafoɔ, na wɔtenaa hɔ sɛ wɔrepere wɔn ho sɛ wɔhɔyɛ. Ná ɔbɔfoɔ no sɔre kɔ kwaeɛ hɔ ahuhudeɛ mu. Bere a ɔbaa fie bɛtoo apataa afiri wɔ asubonten mu, na ɔde apataa ne nsunam nketewa no kɔmaa ne yere. Afei na wɔwɔ hɔ, na ne yere no rebisabisa no sɛ, Me gye di sɛ sɛ wokum nam a, wode ma, anaasɛ na wotɔn na wodi (mfasoɔ no), nti ɛnnɛ da sɛ wokɔ nwura mu na woamfa nam biara amma a, mɛsan akɔ me akuraa. Ɔhɔho no de nani sii kwaeɛ no so. Ɔkɔɔ so nanteɛɛ, wanhu ayamye aboa biara. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ, Aane, mahu mmusu. Afei, bere a ɔrehwɛ ne ho no, ɔhunuu Ɔtwee bi a ɔnam baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Na wo nso, Ɔtwee, mɛkum wo. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no dii nakyi, kyiri! kyiri! kyiri! (Ɛyɛ ne anan nnyigyei). Ɔtwee no dwanee kɔɔ dua bi nhini a agyina pintinn ntam. Ɔno (Ɔtwee) kae sɛ, Owura Tano, metu kwan kɔ wo nkyɛn bammɔ, na ɔbɔfoɔ no agyae me. Ɔɔbɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, Me deɛ, minnim adeɛ a wɔfrɛ no Tano no, enti fa kɔ ma menkɔfa wo nkɔma me yere no. Tano gyinaa hɔ, na ɔhyɛɛ ɔkasa no mu, kae sɛ, Ɔbɔfoɔ, gyae Ɔtwee no na fa me fie kɔ, ɛfiri sɛ mfasoɔ kɛseɛ na ɛbɛyɛ wo wɔ me mu. Ɔbɔfoɔ no gyinaa hɔ, na ɔwosow, biribiri. Ɔfaa no (Tano) de no too ne etire so. Bere a ɔde no reba no, na ɔne Owuo rehyia. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔbɔfoɔ, ɛhe na wode me ɔkyeame no rekɔ? " Na ɔbɔfoɔ no buae sɛ, "Paapa, m'aniwa abere; na merehwehwɛ nam a mɛkɔ ama me yere no, bere a me kɔhunuu Ɔtwee no, na me rebɛkum no no, ɔkae sɛ waguan akɔfa bammɔ ama saa adeɛ yi. Me kae sɛ mennim ho hwee. Saa adeɛ yi na ɔkaeɛ sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mede kɔ fie, ɛfiri sɛ na mfasoɔ pii wɔ so. " Owuo bisaa Tano sɛ, "Worekɔ fie hɔ a, wunim sɛ sɛ obi yare a, mede me mpa a wɔasɛn no bɛba abɛto ne nkyɛn, ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara hyɛ mu kwan, enti sɛ worekɔ fie hɔ a, nea ɛwɔ sɛ meka kyerɛ wo ne sɛ, sɛ obi yare a, meremfa no nkɔ a, mɛbɔ yo amanneɛ. u, na wubɛnya biribi ayɛ me akatua, na saa akatua no deɛ, mogya nko ara na mepɛ, na woanya honam no. " Nyame kumaa no ka kyerɛɛ Owuo sɛ, "Grandse, mate, master. " ɔbɔfoɔ no faa Tano, na wɔne no baa fie. Bere a Tano gyina hɔ no, ɔtoo dwom, na wɔde wɔn ho bɔɔ kuw no mu. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi, na berɛ a obi yareɛ no, wɔde no kɔɔ hɔ kɔtoo Tano. Ɔno (Tano) kae sɛ, "Wobɛwu. " Na ɛyɛ nokware, ɔwuiɛ. (To another) he said, "You will not die, so bring this and that. " Berɛ a obi de nneɛma no kɔtoo hɔ no, na ɔno (the lesser-god) aka sɛ, "I go to beg nkwa for you. " Na ɛnam sɛ wode afɔdeɛ yi kɔmaa Tano nti, wo ho yɛɛ wo den. Saa na yɛn hunu sɛ anyame nketewa no wɔ adom; enti nnipa kɔ hɔ kɔ Tano na wogye ne mmofra no bi fi ne hɔ.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
