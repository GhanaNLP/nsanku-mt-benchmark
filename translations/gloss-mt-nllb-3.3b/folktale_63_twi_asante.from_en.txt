Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ABOSOM, NNYAMESOM NKETEWA NO BAA ABUSUAKUW NO MU

Anwumabɔfoɔ bi ne ne yere tenaa abɔfra bi nsraban mu pɛn. Na wɔyɛ ahiafoɔ, na wɔte hɔ a wɔreko ama wɔn nkwa. Na abɔfra no sɔre kɔ kwaeɛ no mu wɔ ahum mu. Bere a ɔbaa fie no, ɔde mpataayie sii asubɔnten no mu, na ɔde mpataayie ne nsuo nketewa no kɔmaa ne yere. Ɛnneɛ na wɔwɔ hɔ, na ne yere no bisaa no sɛ, Me gye di sɛ, sɛ wuwui nam a, wode ma, anaa anka wotɔn na wodi (profit), enti nnɛ-da sɛ woakɔ kwaeɛ mu na woamfa nam biara amma a, mɛsan akɔ me kuro no mu. Ɔbɔfoɔ no de ne ho sii kwan so kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu. Ɔkɔɔ nanteɛ no, wanhu aboa biara a ne ho yɛ fɛ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ, Ao, mahunu mmusuusuusuo. Afei, berɛ a ɔhwɛɛ ne ho no, ɔhunuu Ɔtweaseɛ bi a ɔnam beaeɛ hɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Na wo deɛ, Ɔtweaseɛ, mekum wo. Ɔsoro bɔfoɔ no dii nakyi, kyiri! kyiri! kyiri! (ɛno ne ne nan nnyigyei). Ɔtweaseɛ no dwanee kɔɔ dua bi ntini a ɛyɛ den ntam. Ɔkaa sɛ, Grandfather Tano, miguan kɔ wo nkyɛn kɔpɛ ahobammɔ, na ɔbɔfo no nnyae me. Ɔsomafo no kaa sɛ, Me deɛ, minnim adeɛ a wɔfrɛ no Tano, enti fa so ma memfa wo nkɔma me yere. Tano gyinaa hɔ, na ɔhyɛɛ kasa mu sɛ, Angel, gyae Draw no na fa me kɔ fie, ɛfiri sɛ mfasoɔ pii wɔ hɔ ma wo wɔ me mu. Ɔsomafo no gyinaa hɔ, na ɔkunkun, biribiri. Ɔfaa no (Tano) de no sii ne ti so. Bere a ɔde no reba no, ɔhyiaa Owuo. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Angel, ɛhe na wode me "spokesman" no rekɔ? " Angel no buaa sɛ, "Papa, mani abere; na merehwehwɛ nam a mede bɛma me yere, bere a mekɔeɛ na mehuu Ɔtwee no, na bere a mɛkum no no, ɔkaa sɛ ɔdwane kɔhyɛɛ saa adeɛ yi nkyɛn, mekaeɛ sɛ mennim ho hwee. Saa adeɛ yi (ɛneɛ) ɔkaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mede no kɔ fie, ɛfiri sɛ na (ɛkaa) mfasoɔ pii wɔ mu. " Owuo bisaa Tano sɛ, "Wo na worekɔ fie no, na wunim sɛ sɛ obiara yareɛ a, na mefa me lounging bed no, na maba abɛto ne nkyɛn a, ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara hyɛ mu, enti sɛ wokɔ fie no a, nea ɛwɔ sɛ meka kyerɛ wo ne sɛ, sɛ obiara yareɛ a, na menkɔfa no, mɛbɔ wo amanneɛ wu, na wubɛnya biribi atua me ka, na saa akatua no deɛ, mogya nko ara na mepɛ, na wobɛtumi anya nam no. " Ɔbosom ketewa no ka kyerɛɛ Owuo sɛ, "Grandfather, mate, owura. " Ɔbɔfo no faa Tano, na ɔne no baa fie. Bere a Tano gyina hɔ no, ɔtoo dwom, na wɔde wɔn ho hyɛɛ dwom no mu. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi, na berɛ a obi yareɛ no, wɔde no kɔɔ hɔ kɔtoo Tano. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wobɛwu. " Ɛyɛ nokware, ɔwuiɛ. (Ɔfoforo bi nso) ɔkaa sɛ, "Worenwu, enti fa wei ne wei ba. " Bere a obi kɔtoo nneɛma no, ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Mekɔ srɛ nkwa ma wo. " Na ɛnam sɛ wode (sacrifices) no maa Tano nti, ampa ara na wo ho tɔɔ wo. Saa kwan yi so na yɛhu sɛ bosom nketewa no wɔ mfasoɔ, enti nkurɔfoɔ kɔ hɔ kɔ Tano na wɔgye ne mma no bi firi ne nkyɛn.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
