Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐDURUU NEA ƐBAA SƐ KWAKU ANANSE, ANANSE NO, KƆƆ SORO WƆ APRANNAA SO.

ANANSE O! ɔwaree Nsia babaa, Ewiem onyankopɔn maame, na ɔkɔɔ ka kyerɛɛ Niamey (Ewiem onyankopɔn) sɛ, "Ɛda a wo maame Nsia bɛwu no, bra bɛka ɛda a wɔapaw ama ayi amanneɛ. "

Bɛyɛ nnansa akyi no, wɔbɛkaa sɛ Nsia, Ewiem onyankopɔn maame no awu. Afei Kwaku Ananse mogya mmusuakuw ne Berekuo, King Jay, Ɔkwakuo, Kwaadu ne Odwan, Odwan. Ɛnna Kwaku Ananse kasa kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, Wɔaka ayi amanneɛ bi akyerɛ me, enti ɛsɛ sɛ mo ne me kɔ. Enti Kwaku Ananse de nkra kɔmaa Ewiem onyankopɔn sɛ ɔreba dwumadie no ase. Afei ɔsoro nyame no boaboaa nadesuadeɛ nyinaa ano, te sɛ Omanhene asɛmbli. Na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Sɛ anwummere tɔ a, mɛba. " Ananse no yɛɛ kotokuo bi, na ɔde Berekuo, King Jay, Ɔkwakuo, Kwaadu ne Odwan, Odwan guu mu, na ɔde kotokuo no guu nabatiri so, na ɔde ne ntoma kyekyeree so. Afei ɔkasa kyerɛɛ abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no sɛ, "Sɛ me duru Ɔsoro Nyame no nkyɛn a, ɛwɔ sɛ wosu. " Berɛ a ɔduruu Ɔsoro Nyame no anim no, Berekuo no maa ne nne so:

"Kurukusu, kuw! kuw! kuw!

Kurukusu, kuw! kuw! kuw! nI. "

Na Kwaadu maa ne nne so:

"Kakum!"

Na Odwan ma ne nne so:

"Me ara me! "

Ɛno ne Kwaku Ananse awerɛhow. Nnipa no nyinaa kae sɛ, "Woayɛ yie, ɔbarima bi wɔ hɔ a ɔtumi su na ɔdi awerɛhow wɔ akwan ahodoɔ mmiɛnsa so. " Bere a ɔwiee nawerɛhow no, Ɔsoro Nyame no hwehwɛɛ ofie fɛfɛ a ɔbɛtena. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, Ɔsoro Nyame no yɛɛ aduan pa na ɔde maa Kwaku Ananse. Bere a Kwaku Ananse huu saa aduane yi, ɔkae wɔ ne tirim sɛ, Meremfa biara mma Berekuo, meremfa biara mma Ɔkwakuo, meremfa biara mma Odwan, ɛfiri sɛ madi mmuada berɛ tenten. Enti Kwaku Ananse dii, wamfa biara amma ne nuanom. Afei, ɔne wɔn tenaa so ara, na Ɔsoro Nyame no kaa sɛ, Yɛbɛdi da no ayiyɛ. Nanso Odwan no kaa (kyerɛ Kwaku Ananse) sɛ, Ɛkɔm rekum me, mennkɔ. Ɔkwakuo ne Berekuo kaa adeɛ koro. Afei na Ɔsoro Nyame no kɔtenaa nnipa a wɔahyia no ntam, na ɔmaa wɔbɔɔ Kwaku Ananse amanneɛ sɛ, nea ɔyɛɛ no fɛfɛɛfɛ wɔ ɔkyena no, ɔmmɛyɛ bio, efisɛ ɔne ne mpanimfoɔ ahyia hɔ retie. Kwaku Ananse buaa no sɛ, "Me ho nyɛ me dɛ. " Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, ɔsoro nyame no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, "Woama m'aniwa atɔ aniwu. " Na 'Berekuo, Ɔkwakuo ne Odwan kaa sɛ, "Yɛn nso, woama yɛn kɔm de yɛn ne wo afiri abusuakuw no mu. " Enti Ɔkwakuo ne 'Berekuo kɔɔ nwura mu, nanso Odwan, Odwan, tenaa fie. Ananse huruw na ɔde ne ho bɔɔ aprannaa no so. ne sɛ na ne ho fɛre no.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
