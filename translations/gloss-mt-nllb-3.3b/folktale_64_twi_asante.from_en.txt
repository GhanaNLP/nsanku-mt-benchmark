Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBƐYƐƐ SƐ KWAKU ANANSE, ANANSE NO, KƆƆ APRANNAA SO

ANANSE O! ɔwaree Nsia babea, Ɔsoro Nyame no maame, na ɔkɔɔ Nyame nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛda a wo maame Nsia bɛwu no, bra na kyerɛ me da a woayi ama ayie no. "

Ɛbɛyɛ nnansa akyi no, wɔbɛkaa kyerɛɛ no sɛ Nsia, Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no maame, awu. Afei Kwaku Anans mogya mu abusuabɔ ne Berekuo, King Jay, Owakuo, Kwaadu ne Odwan, Odwan. Afei Kwaku Anans kasa kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, Wɔabɛka ayi bi ho asɛm akyerɛ me, enti ɛsɛ sɛ mo ne me kɔ. Enti Kwaku Anans de (nkra) kɔmaa Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no sɛ ɔreba dwumadi no ase. Afei Ɔsoro-nyame no boaboaa nadesuade nyinaa ano, te sɛ ɔpanyin bi nhyiam. Na Kwaku Anans kae sɛ, "Sɛ anwummere no dwo a, mɛba. " Anans no yɛɛ bag, na ɔde Berekuo, King Jay, Ockwakuo, Kwaadu ne Odwan, Odwan guu mu, na ɔde bag no too natifi so, na ɔde ntama kyekyeree so. Afei ɔkasa kyerɛɛ abɔdeɛ no sɛ, "Sɛ me duru ɔsoro-nyame no nkyɛn a, ɛsɛ sɛ wosu. " Bere a ɔduruu ɔsoro-nyame no anim no, Berekuo no maa ne nne so sɛ:

"Kurukusu, kuw! kuw! kuw!

Kurukusu, kuw! kuw! kuw! nI. "

Na Kwaadu de ne nne so kae sɛ:

"Kakum! "

Na Odwan no maa ne nne so sɛ:

"Me ara ni! "

Ɛno ne Kwaku Ananse ayeyi dwom. Nnipa no nyinaa kae sɛ, "Mo ayɛ yie, ɔbarima baako na ɔwɔ ha a ɔtumi su na ɔdi awerɛhoɔ wɔ akwan mmiɛnsa so. " Bere a ɔwiee (ne ayeyi dwom no), Ɔsoro-nyame no hwɛɛ sɛ ɔbɛnya fie a ɛyɛ fɛ a ɔbɛtena mu. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, Ɔsoro-nyame no yɛɛ aduane a ɛyɛ dɛ na ɔde maa Kwaku Ananse. Berɛ a Kwaku Ananse huu aduane yi, ɔkaa ne ho sɛ, "Meremma 'Berekuo' biara, meremma 'Okwakuo' biara, meremma 'Oddwan' biara, ɛfiri sɛ mabɔ mmuada akyɛ. " Enti Kwaku Ananse dii no, wamma ne mogya-abusuafoɔ no biara. Afei, ɔne wɔn tenaa hɔ kɔɔ so, na Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Yɛbɛyɛ nna nwɔtwe ayi no. " Nanso Odwan no kaa (kyerɛ Kwaku Ananse) sɛ, "Ɔkɔm rekum me, menkɔ"; Ɔkwakuo ne Berekuo nso kaa saa ara. Afei na Ɔsoro-nyame no akɔtena nkurɔfoɔ a wɔahyia no ntam, na wama wɔn abɛbɔ Kwaku Ananse amanneɛ sɛ, ɔfɛdeɛ a ɔyɛeɛ wɔ da a ɛtwaa toɔ no, ɔbɛtumi ayɛ no bio, ɛfiri sɛ ɔne ne mpanimfoɔ no ahyia (bɛtie no). Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "Me ho nyɛ me yie. " Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, Ɔsoro-nyame no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, "Woama mani abere sɛ mɛfɛre. " Na Berekuu, ne Okwakuo, ne Odwan kae sɛ, Yɛn nso, woama yɛn kɔm, enti yɛn ne wo bɛtetew yɛn ho afi abusua no ho. Enti Okwakuo ne Berekuo kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu, nanso Odwan, Odwan no, tenaa fie; na Ananse huruw ne ho hwee ahum no so; ɛno na ɛyɛ sɛ na ne ho yɛ no fɛre.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
