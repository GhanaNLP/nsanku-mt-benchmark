Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐBƐDURUU SƐ ONIPA BI NKYERƐ MFITIASEƐ A ƐFIRI NEA ONIPA FOFORƆ PUEEƐ MU

ƐWƆ HƆ BERE BI A NA ɔbɔfoɔ bi wɔ hɔ. Sɛ ɔsɔre a, na ɔkɔ nwura mu kɔhwehwɛ agorɔ a ɔbɛkum bi, na wanya bi adi, na wanya bi nso atɔn. Ɛnneɛ da bi a ɔkɔɔ nwura so no, ɔtee sɛ Kokotee, ɔwura-PONKYERƐNI, refrɛ ne busuani sɛ, "Kokotee Asamoa e! " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Aane, me nua, yiw. " Ɔno (ɔyɛ PONKYERƐNI a odi kan) kaa sɛ, "Yɛn mfuo so adwuma berɛ adu, enti yɛnkɔ adwumfo no nkyɛn, yɛnkɔyɛ dadeɛ no na yɛmfa ano nhyɛ so (yɛn twatwa nnwinneɛ), na sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ yɛtwa nnua biara a, yɛn nso yɛbɛtumi atwa. . " Bere a kwae mu mprako no frɛɛ ne anuanom no, ɔbɔfoɔ no kotoo ehuntahunu, na ɔtee nkɔmmɔdie no nyinaa. Afei na onuabarima a ɔwɔ Bush-pig (ɔdi kan) no bisaa ne onuabarima Bush-pig sɛ, "Na akuraa bɛn na yɛbɛkɔ, ama dadeɛ abɔ yɛn? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Yɛbɛkɔ akuraa a wɔfrɛ no Across-the-asuwa. " Na ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛda bɛn? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Monday. " Na ɔbɔfoɔ no tee saa nhyehyɛeɛ yi nyinaa, na wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ fie. Afei, bere a ɔbaa fie no, ɔkaa asuwa no ho odikro no, nea ɛne sɛ, nea na wate bere a na ɔwɔ nwura no mu no. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ akuraa no panin sɛ, "Ma mmofra no nkɔtwa nnyina no na wɔmfa mmra, na sɛ Ɛdwoada no, mmofra no bɛsesa wɔn ho (bɛyɛ nnipa) na wɔaba a, yɛbɛfa wɔn kɔkyekyere wɔn wɔ nnyina no so. " Na akuraa no panin no kɔtwa nnyina no na ɔde baeɛ. Na akuraa no panin no kɔka kyerɛɛ akuraa no tomfoɔ no sɛ wɔntu dade stapo mma no. Na tomfoɔ no bisaa Ɔpanyin no sɛ, Na saa dade stapo a ɛdɔɔso yi nyinaa, a wose ɛsɛ sɛ mepam no, dɛn na wode bɛyɛ? Na ɔka kyerɛɛ no amaneɛbɔ, sɛnea na ɔbɔfoɔ bi akɔ nwura so, na wasan abɛbɔ amanneɛ sɛ Ɛdwoada no mmoadoma bi bɛdane wɔn ho ayɛ nnipa, wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ wɔmmra ne dɔnka nkyɛn abɛhyɛ nnwinnadeɛ. Ɛhɔ ara na tomfoɔ tuu mmirika sɛ wɔrebɛboro dade stapo no. Ɛduruu berɛ a ɔhene no de ne nsa too nnyina ne staplosa dade mma no so wieeɛ no, ɔmaa kuro no so ɔfrɛfoɔ no bɔɔ dawuru sɛ, Ɛnnɛ Ɛdwoada, ɔbaa anaa ɔbarima biara nkɔ baabiara. Ɛdwoada bɛduruu no, anɔpa no, aberewa bi ka kyerɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no sɛ, Kɔ na kɔdwiri ɛmo, nkyene, gyeene, ne baabi a mmoadoma no bɛpue wɔn nwoma ahoroɔ no na wɔde ato hɔ. enti, sɛ wokɔ hɔ a, fa ɛmo no hohoro wɔn, na mmoadoma no ba ha a, na yɛakyere wɔn, na ɛsɛ sɛ ebinom dwane kɔfa wɔn nwoma ahoroɔ no, hyɛ wɔn so, bere a ɛmo no apira wɔn, na wɔatow agu a, wɔbɛdane nnipa bio. Ɛhɔ na ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔhyehyɛɛ ne ho wɔ hɔ. Na ɔte sɛ wɔrefrɛ, Kokotee Asamoa, Ɛdwoada adu, yɛnkɔ. Enti wɔn nyinaa baeɛ. Wɔyii wɔn nwoma ahoroɔ no, na wɔde sii hɔ. Afei, (Mprako no mu baako,) a na ɔyɛ ɔyaresafoɔ a ɔwɔ habannuro mu no, na ɔka wɔn ho, na ɔde ne wedeɛ no kɔtoo baabi foforɔ. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no hwɛ wɔn, na wɔma wɔkɔeɛ. Ɔkɔɔfa nwoma ahoroɔ no nyinaa, na ɔpraa ɛmo a wɔayam no, na ɔde guu asuwa no mu maa nsuo no kɔe. Na Nhabannuru ho ɔbenfo no deɛ, na ɔde no ato nkyɛn wɔ ahomegyeɛ no ho no, ɔbɔfoɔ no anhu. Ɔbɔfoɔ no baa fie, na Ɔhene nso frɛɛ nnipa a wɔahyia wɔ adwinnadeɛ no nkyɛn no ma wɔbɛtoo nsa frɛɛ wɔn baa ne fie. Saa nnipa yi baa Ɔhene fie no, wɔbisaa Ɔhene no sɛ, Dɛn nti na wofrɛɛ yɛn? Ɔhene no kaa sɛ, Wo na woyɛ me mmarima, na wuguan (once) kɔtenaa baabi foforɔ, kɔsi da a woasan aba yi, ɛno ne sianteɛ a meka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ woba, ɛfiri sɛ meremma wo kwan mma wo nkɔ bio. Na mmoadoma no ka kyerɛɛ Ɔhene no sɛ, Nea woaka no, yɛate, nanso yɛnim sɛ adeɛbɔfoɔ no hu nter baa bɛka yɛn ho asɛm nyinaa kyerɛɛ wo, nanso ɛno mfa ho, yɛn ne wo bɛtena ase; nanso yɛnim sɛ nea woka no yɛ atoro; nanso, ɛno mfa ho; yɛda saa ɔbɔfoɔ no ase. Nti yɛn ne mo bɛtena ase; nanso adeɛ bi wɔ hɔ a yɛsi ho kwan, ɛne sɛ, monkyerɛ yɛn mfitiaseɛ; anaa sɛ mo adesuadeɛ biara nkyerɛ yɛn mfitiaseɛ; sɛ ɛba saa a, yɛbɛbu mo abusuakuo yi mu na yɛakɔ. Afei, berɛ a Ɔhene kɔfrɛɛ wɔn no, na dunsini no ne afoforo bi adwane. Na dunsini no kɔgyee ne wedeɛ no, ɔdwaneeɛ, nanso ahomegyeɛ no san nakyi. Ɛfiri sɛ wɔanhunu wɔn nwoma ahoroɔ no. Ɔhene no penee tebea a mmoadoma no de sii hɔ no so, na wɔne mmoadoma no tenaa ase anigyeɛ mu. Afei akuraa hɔ mmarima no waree mmoadoma no bi, na wɔwoo mmofra. Afei, da bi a na mmoadoma no mu baako ne nkuraasefoɔ no mu baako de wɔn nkuturu reko. Ɛhɔ ara na akuraaseni no ka kyerɛɛ aboa kɛseɛ no sɛ, Fa wo ho firi hɔ, aboa a wote sɛ wo, a wote Bush-pig adwumakuo no mu. Ɛnkyɛe na ɔkaa saa no, mmoadoma no nyinaa aniwa bɛyɛɛ kɔkɔɔ, na wɔkɔɔ Ɔhene no fie kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Saa kwan a ɛbɛtumi ayɛ yiye a yɛaka akyerɛ mo no, afei yi aba mu ampa, enti dɛn na mobɛyɛ? Ɔhene no maa wɔkɔfrɛɛ nnipa no. wɔn na wɔde mansotwe no bae. Ɔhene no hwɛɛ asɛm no mu yie na ɔmaa atɛmmuo sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mmoadoma no gyae sɔboɔ no gu, ɛfiri sɛ (ɔkaa sɛ) wɔn baeɛ no, na ɛyɛ berɛ tenten, na ɛno ne dwumadie a ɛdi kan a obi bɛka asɛm bi akyerɛ wɔn (wɔ wɔn mfitiaseɛ no ho). Nanso mmoadoma no kaa sɛ, "Yɛrennye ntom. " Na Ɔhene no kaa sɛ, "Worentie, na wosusu sɛ nea ɔbarima yi kae no yɛ atoro, wo nyɛ kwae mu mprako? " Na mmoadoma no kaa sɛ, "Aane, yɛate. " Ɛhɔ na mmoadoma no ne nsuo no ho nnipa no koe. Mpɔtam hɔ mmoadoma no sɛee akuraa no, na nnipa du pɛ na wɔaka. Na wɔsrɛɛ mmɔborɔhunu kyerɛɛ mmoadoma no sɛ wɔn (mmoadoma no) wɔ kwan wɔ wɔn nkyɛn. Ɛnna mmoadoma no tiee, na wɔbɔɔ nnipa no amanneɛ sɛ, asɛm bi a wɔaka dada no, ɛnyɛ den sɛ yɛbɛte aseɛ, na sɛ wo ne yɛn bɛtena a, yɛbara kasamfonin a ɛtwe adwene nyinaa ma yɛn mfitiaseɛ. Sɛ wosusuw anaa woka ho asɛm bio a, ɛnneɛ (yɛbɛsrɛ wo) kyerɛ yɛn saa baabi a yɛfiri baeɛ a ɛhɔ na yɛbɛsan aba. Na nnipa no kaa sɛ, Yɛntumi nyɛ saa adeɛ yi bio da. Nea yɛayɛ yi ama yɛn abusuakuo no asɛe; yɛrenyɛ saa bio da. Enti, wɔbɔɔ amansan dawubɔ sɛ wɔmfa dawuro no mmɔ nsunsuansoɔ sɛ ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara ka obi ho asɛm da, na nsɛm a wɔada no adi no ama kuro no asɛe. Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi mo nnye nni, na ahomegyeɛ no monkamfo me mma ho asɛmka.
