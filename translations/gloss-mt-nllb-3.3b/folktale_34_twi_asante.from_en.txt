Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ ONIPA BI NKYERƐ MFITIASEƐ A ƐFIRI NEAONIPA FOFORƆ FI MU NO

ƐDII BERE BI A ƆKUMFO BI WƆ HƆ. Bere a ɔsɔre no, na ɔtaa kɔ sare so kɔhwehwɛ mmoadabi, sɛ ɔbɛnya bi adi, na ebi nso atɔn. Afei da bi a ɔkɔɔ sare so no, ɔtee sɛ Kokotee, Nwura-prako, refrɛ ne nua no sɛ, "Kokotee Asamoa e! " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Yiw, nua, yiw. " Ɔ (prako a odi kan no) kaa sɛ, "Adwuma no bere adu (wɔ yɛn afuo mu), enti yɛnkɔ adwumfo no adwumayɛbea hɔ, ma ɔnhyehyɛ dadeɛ no na ɔmfa ano (yɛn cutting tools) no, na sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ yɛtwa nnua biara a, yɛatumi atwa. . " Bere a kwae mu aboa no frɛɛ ne nuanom no, ɔbɔmmɔfo no bɔɔ ne ho ban, na ɔtee nkɔmmɔbɔ no nyinaa. Afei, Bush-pig nua no bisaa ne nua Bush-pig sɛ, "Na akuraa bɛn na yɛbɛkɔ, ama dadeɛ no abɔ? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Yɛkɔ akuraa a wɔfrɛ no Across-the-street. " Na ɔka sɛ, "Ɛda bɛn? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Monday. " Ɔbɔfoɔ no tee saa nhyehyɛeɛ yi nyinaa, na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ fie. Afei, berɛ a ɔbaa fie no, ɔka kyerɛɛ Across-the-Suit Circle no asɛm no, ɛne nea ɔate berɛ a ɔkɔɔ sare so no. Na ɔbɔfo no ka kyerɛɛ akuraa no mu panyin no sɛ, "Ma mmofra no nkɔtwa nnua na wɔmfa mmra, na sɛ Monday, Bush-pigs no sesa wɔn ho (bɛyɛ nnipa) na wɔba a, yɛbɛfa wɔn na yɛakyekyere wɔn wɔ nnua no so. Mmofra no kɔtwa nnua no, na wɔde ba. Akuraa no mu panyin no kɔka kyerɛɛ akuraa no mufoɔ sɛ wɔntwe dadeɛ poma mma no. Na tomfoɔ no bisaa Ɔpanyin no sɛ, Na dadeɛ nnua dodoɔ yi nyinaa, a wose ɛsɛ sɛ mebɔ no, wode bɛyɛ dɛn? Na ɔka kyerɛɛ no asɛm no, sɛnea ɔbɔfoɔ bi akɔ kwaeɛ no mu, na wasan abɛbɔ amanneɛ sɛ, wɔ Dwoada no, mmoa bi bɛdane wɔn ho nnipa, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wɔbɛba ne dɔn no mu ama wɔabɔ wɔn nnwinnadeɛ. Ɔtowfo no tuu mmirika de yɛɛ dade poma no ntɛm ara. Ɛduruu sɛ ɔhene no de ne nsa sii dua ne staplosa no so wieeɛ no, ɔmaa kuro no mu dawurubɔfoɔ no bɔɔ dawuro no sɛ, Wɔ Ɛdwoada, sɛ ɔyɛ ɔbaa, anaa ɔyɛ ɔbarima, ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara kɔ baabiara. Ɛdwoada no bɛduruu hɔ, na anɔpa no, ɔbaa panyin bi ka kyerɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no sɛ, Kɔ na mia mako, ne nkyene, ne plantain, ne baabi a mmoa no bɛyiyi wɔn ho na wɔato; woyɛ al enti, sɛ wokɔ hɔ a, fa nkokɔ no gu so, na sɛ mmoa no ba ha a, na sɛ yɛkyere wɔn, na ɛsɛ sɛ ebinom dwane kɔfa wɔn nwoma no, gu so, sɛ nkokɔ no bɔ wɔn yaw a, na wɔtow gu a, wɔbɛsan ayɛ nnipa. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔeɛ na ɔde ne ho siee hɔ. Na ɔtee sɛ wɔrefrɛ, Kokotee Asamoa, Monday aduru, yɛnkɔ. Enti wɔn nyinaa baeɛ. Wɔyii wɔn nhoma no, na wɔde sii hɔ. Afei, (Mprako no mu baako,) a ɔyɛ aduruyɛ ho ɔbenfoɔ, na ɔka wɔn ho, na ɔde ne honam ani de sii baabi foforɔ. Ɔsoro ɔbɔfoɔ no hwɛɛ wɔn, na ɔmaa wɔn kwan ma wɔkɔeɛ. Ɔkɔɔ faa nwoma no nyinaa, na ɔde nkotokuo a wɔde ahyɛ mu no hohoroo ho, na ɔde guu asubɔnten no mu, maa nsuo no de kɔe. Odunsini deɛ, na ɔde no ato nkyɛn wɔ nkaeɛ no ho no, ɔbɔfoɔ no anhu. Ɔbɔfoɔ no san baa fie, na ɔhene no nso frɛɛ nkurɔfoɔ a wɔahyia wɔ blacksmiths forge no ho no ma wɔbaa ne fie. Berɛ a saa nkurɔfoɔ yi baa ɔhene fie no, wɔbisaa ɔhene no sɛ, Dɛn nti na wofrɛɛ yɛn? Ɔhene no kaa sɛ, Na moyɛ me mmarima, na mo dwane (once) kɔtenaa baabi foforɔ; nnɛ a moasan aba yi, ɛno nti na meka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mobɛba, ɛfiri sɛ meremma mo kwan sɛ monkɔ bio. Mmoa no ka kyerɛɛ ɔhene no sɛ, Nea woka no, yɛate; nanso yɛnim sɛ Ɔbɔfoɔ no hu no, nter baa abɛka yɛn ho asɛm nyinaa kyerɛɛ mo, nanso ɛno mfa ho, yɛn ne mo bɛtena abɔ mu; nanso yɛnim sɛ nea woka no yɛ atoro; nanso, ɛno mfa ho; yɛda saa ɔbɔfoɔ no ase. Enti, yɛn ne mo bɛtena abom; nanso asɛm baako na ɛwɔ hɔ a yɛnni ho kwan, ɛne sɛ, monkyerɛ yɛn fi, anaasɛ mo adesua biara nkyerɛ yɛn fi. Sɛ ɛba saa a, yɛbɛpaapae mo mmusuakuo yi mu na yɛakɔ. Afei, wɔ berɛ a ɔhene no kɔɔ sɛ ɔrefrɛ wɔn no, dunsini no ne afoforɔ bi dwanee. Na dunsini no kɔɔ sɛ ɔrekɔfa ne wedeɛ na ɔdwanee; nanso nkaeɛ no san kɔɔ wɔn akyi. Ɛfiri sɛ wɔantumi annya wɔn nwoma no. Ɔhene no penee nsɛm a mmoa no de too hɔ no so, na wɔne mmoa no tenaa ase anigyeɛ mu. Afei akuraa ase mmarima no waree mmoa no bi; wɔwoo mma. Ɛnneɛ, da bi a na mmoa no mu baako ne nkurasefoɔ no mu baako de wɔn nkutukutukuru reko. Ɛhɔ ara na akuraasefoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ aboa kɛseɛ no sɛ, Fa wo ho firi hɔ, aboa a ɔte sɛ wo, a woka Bush-pig adwumakuo no ho. Ɛnkyɛe na ɔkaa saa no, na mmoa no nyinaa ani abere, na wɔkɔɔ Ɔhene fie kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Akwan a yɛaka akyerɛ wo no, afei deɛ ɛaba mu ampa, enti dɛn na wobɛyɛ? Ɔhene no maa wɔn kɔfrɛɛ nkurɔfoɔ no. nea ɔde akasakasa no bae. Ɔhene no de nani hwɛɛ asɛm no mu yie na ɔde atemmuo maeɛ sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mmoa no gyae ɔboɔ no, ɛfiri sɛ (ɔkae sɛ) na ɛyɛ bere tenten a wɔaba na eyi nso yɛ adeyɛ a edi kan a obiara aka asɛm bi akyerɛ wɔn (wɔ wɔn mfitiaseɛ ho). Nanso mmoa no kae sɛ, Yɛnnye ntom. Na ɔhene no kae sɛ, Worentie, na wosusuw sɛ nea ɔbarima yi kae no yɛ atoro, wunnyɛ Bush-pigs anaa? Na mmoa no kae sɛ, Aane, yɛate. Ɛhɔ ara na mmoa no ne Cross-the-water nkurɔfo no koe. Mmoa no sɛee akuraa no, kosi sɛ nnipa bɛyɛ du pɛ na wɔaka. Na wɔsrɛɛ ahummɔborɔ na wɔka kyerɛɛ mmoa no sɛ wɔwɔ kwan wɔ wɔn nkyɛn. Mmoa no tiee, na wɔbɔɔ nkurɔfoɔ no amaneɛ sɛ, Asɛm bi a wɔaka dada no, ɛnyɛ den sɛ yɛbɛte aseɛ, na sɛ mo ne yɛn bɛtena abom a, yɛ tabu sensual imagery nyinaa ama yɛn mfitiaseɛ. Sɛ wosusu anaa woka ho asɛm (san), ɛnde (we will ask you) kyerɛ yɛn saa kusuu a yɛfiri baa ha no, na yɛasan akɔ hɔ. Na nnipa no kaa sɛ, We can never do such a thing again. Nea yɛayɛ yi ama yɛn mmusuakuo asɛe; yɛrenyɛ saa bio da. Nti, wɔbɔɔ dawuro no wɔ amansan mu sɛ, wɔmmɔ dawuro no wɔ gong mu sɛ obiara nnka onipa foforo ho asɛm da, na saa nsɛm a wɔbɛda no adi yi ama kuro no asɛe. Eyi, me asɛm a mekaeɛ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi wɔ hɔ a wobɛtumi agye sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na ebi nso wɔ hɔ a wobɛtumi akamfo me.
