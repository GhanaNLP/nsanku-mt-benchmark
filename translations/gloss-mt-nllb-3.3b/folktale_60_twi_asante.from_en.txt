Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA WƆDE "KURONTO" NE "BURU" BƐDII DWUMA

ƐWƆ HƆ BERE BI A NA ɔbɔfoɔ bi (a wɔfrɛ no) Kwaku, ne ne yere (a wɔfrɛ no Buruwa) wɔ hɔ, na esiane sɛ na wɔyɛ ahiafoɔ nti, wɔtuu kwan sɛ wɔbɛsi atenae foforɔ. Kwaku, ɔbɔfoɔ no, bere a ɔkɔɔ nwura so no, ɔkunkum eku bi, nanso bere a ɔde san baa fie ne asase so no, ɔkae sɛ, "Eyi nyɛ yiye (aduane). " Enti na ɔkɔtɔn. Bere biara a ɔbɛkunkum adeɛ no, na ɛyɛ saa. Nanso ne yere no anhu sika no da, na ɔbisaa no sɛ, "Wɔmfa sika nsan mma, na womfa nam nsan mma, na ɛdeɛn na ɛyɛ aseɛ? " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Sɛ mekɔ na nnipa ntɔ nam no, na ɛbɛyɛ sɛ ɛreporɔ a, mebɛtow agu. " Ɛda bi, ɔkunkum adeɛ bi, na ɔde too kwan so, na ne yere no dii nakyi kɔhwɛɛ no. Afei ɔhunuu sɛ ɔtɔn saa kwaadu no, ɔde sika no kɔtɔɔ nsafufuo kuku (nea wɔfrɛ no dɔnto) bi nom. Afei ne yere no danee kɔɔ fie. Ɛnkyɛ na Kwaku a ɔyɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no nso baeɛ. Bere biara a ɔbɛduru fie hɔ no, ɔka sɛ, "Anwummere pa, Buruwa. " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Ya Ekudonto (Yiw, monkey ma nsafufuo kuku. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wubua me saa kwan yi so? " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuaeɛ a ɛfata ma nea ɛwɔ wo etire mu. " Na ɔbɔfoɔ no anka hwee. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔkɔɔ afuo hɔ na ɔsan baeɛ; ɔkaa sɛ, "Anwummere pa. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Ya buru. " Buruwa kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wosea me nkyea no saa ɔkwan yi so? " ɔbɔfoɔ no buaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuae a ɛfata ma nea ɛwɔ wo etire mu. " Saa na ɛyɛɛ amammrɛ wɔ abusuakuo no mu sɛ wɔde nkyea ahorow no "Ya kuronto" ne "Ya buru" di dwuma. Wɔn mfitiaseɛ no, ɔbɔfoɔ Kwaku ne ne yere Buruwa na wɔgyee wɔn. Eyi, me abasɛm a mekaeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nso san ba me nkyɛn.
