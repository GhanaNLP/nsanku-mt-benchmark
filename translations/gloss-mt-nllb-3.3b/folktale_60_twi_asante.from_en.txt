Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA (SALUTATIONS) "KURONTO" NE "BURU" BAA SƐ WƆDE DI DWUMA

ƐDII BERE BI A ƆKUNIMFOƆ BI (a wɔfrɛ no Kwaku) ne ne yere (a wɔfrɛ no Buruwa), na wɔn ho ayɛ wɔn na, wɔtuu kwan sɛ wɔrebɛsi atenaeɛ foforɔ. Kwaku, ɔkunkunimfoɔ no, bere a ɔkɔɔ kwaeɛ no, ɔkunkum dɔm bi, nanso bere a ɔsan de baa fie no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Eyi nyɛ papa (ma aduane). " Enti ɔkɔɔ tɔn no. Bere biara a ɔkunkum biribi no, na ɛyɛ saa. Nanso ne yere anhu sika no da, na obisaa no sɛ, "Woamfa sika mma, na woamfa nam mma, na eyi nyinaa kyerɛ dɛn? " Obuae sɛ, "Sɛ mekɔ na nkurɔfo ntɔ (nam no), na ɛporɔw a, metow gu. " Ɛda bi kum biribi, na ɔde sii kwan so, na ne yere dii nakyi hwɛɛ no. Afei ɔhunuu sɛ ɔtɔn saa plantain no, ɔgyee sika no, na ɔtɔɔ nsafufuo (a wɔfrɛ no dɔnto) kuruwa bi nom. Afei ne yere san kɔɔ fie. Ɛnkyɛreeɛ, Kwaku, ɔbɔfoɔ no nso baeɛ. Bere a oduu fie hɔ ara pɛ na ɔkae sɛ, "Anwummere pa, Buruwa. " Obuae sɛ, "Ya Ekudonto (Yiw, Nkoko de ma bobesa kuruwa)." Ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wubua me saa? " Obuae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuae a ɛfata ma nea ɛwɔ wo tirim. " Ɔbɔfo no anka hwee. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi, na ɔkɔɔ afuo no mu na ɔsan bae; ɔkae sɛ, "Anwummere pa. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Ya buru. " Buruwa kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na worebua (me nkyea) saa? " Ɔbɔfoɔ no buaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuaeɛ a ɛfata ma nea ɛwɔ wo ti mu. " Saa na ɛbɛyɛɛ amammerɛ wɔ abusuakuo no mu sɛ wɔde nkyea "Ya kuronto" ne "Ya buru" bedi dwuma. Wɔn mfitiaseɛ firi ɔbɔfoɔ, Kwaku, ne ne yere, Buruwa. Eyi, me asɛm a mekaeɛ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
