Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

NNEƐMA BIARA NNI HƆ (ƐNEA YƐSƐRE HU)

WƆKYERƐ SƐ, Ɔsebɔ bi ne ne yere tenaa hɔ pɛn, na wɔwɔ mma mmienu. Ɛnneɛ, maame ne agya a wɔwɔ mma yi, na wɔtaa kɔ kwaeɛ no mu da biara kɔkyere nam ma wɔn. Ɛda bi a wɔn maame ne wɔn agya kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Sɛ motete aseɛ a, ɛsɛ sɛ mo teɛm ka sɛ, Biribiara nni hɔ (a yɛsuro), hwee nni hɔ (a yɛsuro). " Ɛneɛ wɔfirii hɔ kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu, na wɔgyaa wɔn mma hɔ. Ɛneɛ na Dankwanta, Night-jar no, baeɛ, na mmofra no teɛɛ so sɛ:

"There is nothing anywhere (that we fear), There is nothing anywhere (that we fear)! "

Night-jar no bisaa sɛ, "Hwan na ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ woto saa nne no? " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Agya ne ɛna na wɔkae. " Night-jar no kaa sɛ, "Wɔadi mfomsoɔ, ɛsɛ sɛ wotoo so sɛ, Time soon change, times soon change. Afei, saa nkokoaa no deɛ, wɔn maame ne wɔn papa san bae, na wɔtee wɔn mma su:

"Mmerɛ sesa, ɛnkyɛ na mmerɛ sesa! "

Wɔbisaa sɛ, "Hwan na ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ woto saa nne no? " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Abarimaa bi na ɔka kyerɛɛ yɛn. " Ɛneɛ wɔn maame ne wɔn papa kaa sɛ, "Ɛnsɛ sɛ woto saa nne no, ɛsɛ sɛ woto ne nne sɛ, 'Ɛnyɛ hwee wɔ baabiara (a yɛsuro), ɛnyɛ hwee wɔ baabiara (a yɛsuro).' " Wɔmaa nkokoaa no nam na wɔkɔɔ wɔn kwan. Nightjar no san bae, na na wayɛ peaw mmienu. Ɛnneɛ, baabi a mmofra no da no, nnua mmienu na ɛda hɔ. Bere a Night-jar no bae no, obubuu nnua mmienu no, na ɔde pea no hyɛɛ adididan no mu. Ɔka kyerɛɛ mmofra no sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ wosu sɛ, Mmerɛ sesa, mmere sesa, na sɛ wo maame ne wo papa san ba a, ɛsɛ sɛ woka sɛ ɛyɛ me na meka kyerɛɛ wo. " Night-jar no tuu kwan kɔtenaa pea no baako so. Ankyɛ na mmofra no maame ne wɔn papa san bae. Wɔkae sɛ, "Hwan na ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ to saa nne yi? " Anadwo-jar buae sɛ, Me na meyɛe. Na ɔbo kɛse no resaw akɔ ne so sɛ ɔrebɛkyere no, bere a anadwo-jar no tuu kwan na ɔsebɔfo no bɔɔ ne ho wɔ pea no so no. Anadwo-jar no tuu kwan wɔ pea a ɛto so abien no so. Bere a ɔsebɔfo no huruw sɛ ɔrebɛkyere no no, wɔbɔɔ no nso wɔ pea no so. Night-jar no ka kyerɛɛ mmofra no sɛ, Afei, moahu sɛ morennya nam biara nni. Ɛno na meka kyerɛɛ mo sɛ, times soon change.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
