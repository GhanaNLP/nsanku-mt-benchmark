Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

HWEE BIARA NNI HƆ (NEA YƐSuro)

WƆKYERƐ SƐ, Daboɔ bi ne ne yere tenaa hɔ, na wɔwɔ mmofra mmienu. Mprempren, na mmofra yi maame ne wɔn agya kɔ nwura so da biara kɔyi nam ba ma wɔn. Ɛdii da bi a wɔn maame ne wɔn agya kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Sɛ monsɔre a, ɛsɛ sɛ mobue mu ka sɛ, 'biribiara nni hɔ (a yɛsuro), biribiara nni hɔ (a yɛsuro).' " Afei wɔtuu kwan kɔɔ nwura no mu, na wɔgyaa wɔn mmofra no wɔ hɔ. Ɛneɛ na Dankwanta, anadwo-kran no, baeɛ, na mmofra no teɛɛ mu sɛ:

"There is nothing anywhere (that we fear) ", Nada nni hɔ baabiara (that we fear)!

Anadwo-jar no kae sɛ, "Hwan na ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ wobɛteɛm saa adeɛ? " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ agya ne maame na wɔkae. " Anadwo-jar no kae sɛ, "Wɔbɔ mfomsoɔ, ɛsɛ sɛ woteɛm sɛ, Mmere nnya nsesae, mmere nnya nsesae. Afei, saa nkokoaa no deɛ, wɔn maame ne wɔn agya san baeɛ, na wɔtee wɔn mmofra su sɛ:

"Mmere sesa ntɛm, Mmere sesa ntɛm! "

Wɔkaa sɛ, "Hwan na ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ wobɛteɛm saa? " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "obarima bi na ɔka kyerɛɛ yɛn. " Ɛneɛ wɔn maame ne wɔn agya kaa sɛ, "Ɛnsɛ sɛ woteɛm saa, ɛsɛ sɛ woteɛm sɛ, 'Biribiara nni hɔ (a yɛsuro) ', hwee nni hɔ (a yɛsuro). ' " Wɔde nkokoaa no nam no kɔeɛ. Anadwo-kran no san baeɛ a, na wayɛ peaw mmienu. Afei baabi a nkokoaa no da no, nnua abien na ɛda hɔ. Anadwo kɔbere no bae no, obubuu nnua abien no na ɔde peaw no hyɛɛ stumps no mu. Ɔka kyerɛɛ nkokoaa no sɛ, Ɛsɛ sɛ wosu sɛ Mmere sesa, mmere sesa, na sɛ wo maame ne wo agya san ba a, ɛsɛ sɛ woka sɛ me na meka kyerɛɛ wo. Anadwo kɔbere no tuu wim kɔtenaa peaw no bi pɔ so. Ankyɛ na nkokoaa no maame ne wɔn agya san bae. Wɔkae sɛ, "Hwan na ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ monsu saa? " Anadwoɔ kuruwa no buae sɛ, "Mene no. " Bere a na ɔsebo nini no repue akɔkyere no no, anadwo kuruwa no huruw kɔe na Daboɔ no de ne ho bɔɔ peaw no so. Anadwoɔ kuruwa no tuu wɔ peaw a ɛto so mmienu so. Bere a ɔse bedebɔɛ no huruw sɛ ɔrebɛkyere no no, wɔde no nso bɔɔ peaw no so. Anadwo toa no ka kyerɛɛ mmofra no sɛ, Afei deɛ, moahu sɛ morennya nam nni bio. Ɛno na me kyerɛɛ sɛ meka kyerɛɛ mo sɛ, mmere bɛsesa ntɛm.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
