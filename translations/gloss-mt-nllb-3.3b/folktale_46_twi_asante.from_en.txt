Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

ƐNSƐ SƐ OBIARA KA SƐ, "EYI NE BAABI A MANI GYE HO"

ƆBEREKUO, Wild-Pigeon, ne Ɔnwam, Anwam, ne Okusie, Okisi, nyinaa waree ɔbarima baako. Afei ɔbarima no yɛɛ Okusie, Okisi, ne yere dɔfo; na Ɔberekuo, Wild-Pigeon, ne Anwam deɛ, wanhwɛ wɔn yiye koraa. Bere a na ɔsɔre kɔ abɔnten na ohu biribi a ɛyɛ fɛ, na ɔtɔ no de ba Adueku (a ɛyɛ din foforo ma Okusie). Afei na ɛnyɛ nna mmienu, na ɛnyɛ nna mmiɛnsa, berɛ a yareɛ bi bɛkaa wɔn kunu ma ɔwuiɛ. Bere a ɔwuiɛ no, nabusuafoɔ frɛɛ akunafoɔ no sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ moba bɛsu. " Wild-pigeon baeɛ na ɔmaa ne nne so sɛ: "Du du, du du du du du!

Du Du! Ɛyɛ me dɛ!

Du du, du du du du!

Kunu awu, Du Du!

Merenni bio da, Du Du!

Kosi sɛ mehyia me kunu.

Du Du! "

Ɔbarima (a wawuo) no nua mmaa no kae sɛ, "Woayɛ biribi, woayɛ biribi (worthy of praise); wo nso, Ɔnwam, bra (here). " Ɔnwam baeɛ, na ɔdaa nawerɛhoɔ dwom no adi sɛ:

"Kya, kya, me kunu awu!

Kya, kya.

Me kunu awu, Kya, Kya.

Ne kunu awu.

Kya, kya.

Merenni bio da!

Kya, kya.

Kosi sɛ mehyia me kunu (me kunu).

Kya, kya. "

(Late) husband no nua mmaa no kae sɛ, "Onwam, woayɛ biribi, woayɛ ade (worthy of praise); Okusie, bra (here), ɛfiri sɛ wo ne wo kunu adi biribi a ɛyɛ dɛ paa. " Okusie kae sɛ, "Oh, don't bother me so. " Na ɔba bɛtoo ne nne so sɛ:

"Sankuroro Dampofo!

Nsankurow.

Me deɛ, me kunu nni hɔ den, Sankuroro Dampofo.

Sɛ wawu a, wawu!

Sankuroro. "

Afei deɛ ɔwieeɛ, na obiara faa no sɛ ne ho adesua sɛ, "In this tribe no one should say, 'This wife is my wife beloved. '" (The proverb) owes its origin to Okusie's (mock) lament.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
