Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ ABƆFOƆ YƐ MMERƐW

ƆBƆFO BI (BERE BI) kɔgyee powder ne kɔbere nkotokuo mmienu a wɔde bɛko atia atuo, na ɔne ne yere ne ne ba kumaa no kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu sɛ wɔrekɔkum nam. Na ɔsii abɔmmɔfoɔ dan na ɔne ne yere ne ne ba no tenaa mu. Bere a nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔkɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu. Bere a ohuu aboa kɛse bi na ɔtoo nakatua no, ɔpaee. Da biara na ɛte saa, bɛyɛ nnawɔtwe mmienu. Na ne yere kae sɛ, Esiane sɛ minnya nam biara nni nni nti, mɛfiri fie. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ a ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu no, ne yere ne ne ba no nso kɔɔ wɔn akuraa ase. Afei na atuo baako pɛ na aka. Afei me werɛ fi sɛ mɛka sɛ saa ɔbɔfoɔ no din ne Gyekye, na ne yere nso ne Boniaye-kae-dabi (a ɛkyerɛ sɛ, Someday-the-ungrateful-one-will-remember). Berɛ a na ɔbɔfoɔ no rekyinkyin no, na ɔadu odum dua bi ase, na ɛhɔ na ɛyɛ beaeɛ a ɛyɛ fɛ. Ɛhɔ na ɔbɔfoɔ no susuiɛ wɔ ne tirim sɛ, Sɛ me kɔ fie a wɔbɛgye future no ama powder no, na me nni bi, enti mɛfa saa atuo yi atoto me ho wɔ mene mu sɛ ebia mɛwu. Ɛhɔ ara na Biribi frɛɛ no sɛ, Creators angel. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Father. Na Biribi no kaa sɛ, Adɛn nti na wani abere sɛɛ? afei anka ɔno a wɔfrɛ no Go-and-spoil-the-house no nni wo fie a, anka mede biribi bɛma wo. Ɛnna ɔbɔfo no kae sɛ, Ɛnyɛ hwee wɔ fie hɔ. Biribi kae sɛ, Ɛfiri sɛ hwee nni fie hɔ nti, kɔ bɔn a ɛwɔ palm dua no dua no mu, na nhabannuru mmienu da hɔ, enti fa bra. Na ɔbɔfo no kɔfaa wɔn bae. Na Biribi kae sɛ, Kɔ na wotwitwa lime nnua mmienu, na to wani, na dwane fa baako na fa si beaeɛ a ɛwɔ hɔ, na fa foforɔ no san fa si beaeɛ a ɛwɔ hɔ. Bere a ɔbaeɛ no, ɔkae sɛ, Mawie. Biribi kae sɛ, Fa dawuru (poison) kɛse no na bɔ no. Na ɔbɔɔ no, na sare no bɛyɛɛ akuraa a ɛtrɛw wɔ nnua mmienu no ntam. Na oum dua no gyinaa ne mfinimfini. Na saa Biribi no yii ne ho adi sɛ ɛyɛ Diamond. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no sɛ, sɛ asɛm biara a ɛho hia ba sɛ wɔbɛsiesie a, ɛsɛ sɛ wɔtie wɔ Oak dua no ase. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Ho, mate. Bere a Diamond no aka eyi akyerɛ no, ɔnyaa ɔyerenom, community councillors, ne asomfo. Ɛbɛduruu saa berɛ no, ne yere (a wɔfrɛ no) Some-day-the-ungrateful-one-will-remember nyaa ne ho fĩ sɛ wabɛyɛ ɔhene, enti ɔtɔɔ nnuadewa bobesa, na ɔde kɔɔ ɔhene no akuraa ase, na na ne ba no wɔ nakyi. Bere a ɔduruu hɔ no, ɔkaa nea enti a ɔbaa hɔ no ho asɛm sɛ, Wo ba no ka sɛ ɔde nsa reba sɛ ɔrebɛhwɛ wanim. Na ɔgyee nsa no, na ɔmaa obiara nsa a ɔde bɛba. Na ɔde ɔbaa no kɔmaa Gyaasehene sɛ ɔmfa no nkɔ ne fie. Bere a anwummere no yɛɛ nwini no, ɔbaa no hohoroo ne ho na ɔkɔɔ nea ɔhene no wɔ ne dan mu. Ɔbaa no ka kyerɛɛ ɔhene no sɛ, Wo ne me anya osuo ne owia mu osuahu, kɔsi nnɛ woanya baabi a wobɛtena, na anka ɛnyɛ nea ɛfata sɛ wode nkra bɛfrɛ me? Afei bere a ɔrekasa saa no, sɛnea mmarima ne wɔn nkitahodiɛ teɛ no, wantumi annya hwee anka. Ɛneɛ na ɛhɔ ara na ɔkyeree ɔbaa no na ɔne no kɔdaeɛ. Ade kye anɔpatutuutu no, oyii no fii ne tenabea a ɛwɔ Gyasehenes hɔ no mu de no kɔɔ ɔhene no harem. Ɛhɔ na ɔtraa kosii sɛ ɔhaw kɛse bi bae. Ɔhene no ne nafoforo nyinaa kɔɔ dua no ase kɔtenaa ase. Ɔbea no ba no gyinaa nagya anim, na ɔhunuu ɔkɔre no ba wɔ elm dua no atifi, na ɔkaa sɛ, Agya, mepɛ. Na nagya bɔɔ no ase, na ɔtoo ne ti akyi, na ɔmaa no kɔɔ fie. Na mmea afoforo no mma no mu biako nso bae, na ɔno nso gyinaa nanim. Ɔsan nso huu Eagle ba no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Agya, mepɛ. " Na ɔbɔɔ ne fam wɔ ɔkwan koro no ara so na ɔhyɛɛ ne ti akyi ma ɛhwee ase, na bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔsu. Na ne maame kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woresu? " Na ɔkae sɛ, "Agya bɔɔ me. " Na ne maame kae sɛ, "O! dɛn nti na ɔmmɔ wo, sɛ anka ɛyɛ Obi-da-a-nni-ase-biako-bɛkae no ba a, anka ɔmmɔ no. Na Obi-da-a-nni-ase-biako-bɛkae no kae sɛ, Eyi deɛ, menyɛɛ wo hwee sɛ wobɛyɛ saa ama me. Ntɛm ara na ɔkaa saa no, wɔn baanu nyinaa boaboaa wɔn ho ano na wɔkoeɛ. Na asomfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Saa adeɛ a ɛwɔ dua yi atifi yi nti, yɛn agya yerenom reko; yɛbɛtu no. Ɛntɛm ara na wɔnyaa wɔn nkuma, pimpen! pimpen! (ɛno ne nnyigyeɛ a wɔyɛɛ no.) Saa berɛ no na Gold no akɔkyinkyin na ɔde ne mma no akɔto Koterɛ ne Old-Hawk nkyɛn. Ɛduruu berɛ a nkurɔfoɔ no twaa dua no na ɛrehwe ase no, Cobra no too dwom sɛ, "Pimpeao! " Na dua no baeɛ bom. Na wɔsan baa ne nkyɛn bio. Na Cobra no ka kyerɛɛ Old Hawk sɛ, "Kɔhwehwɛ Goldie sɛ ɔmmɛhwɛ ne mma, ɛfiri sɛ me deɛ, owuo no aba me so. " Ɛhɔ ara na nkurɔfoɔ no kɔgyee wɔn atuo na wɔbɔɔ no tuo kum no. Na Old Hawk tuu kwan sɛ ɔrekɔfrɛ Order no. Ɛkwan a ɔrekɔ no nyinaa, na ɔsu ka sɛ:

"Grandmother e! wo mma o!

Nanabaa e! wo mma o! "

Bere a ɔfrɛɛ saa na Gold no antie no, ɔmaa ne nne so teɛteɛɛ sɛ:

"Sankur e, Anomaa E, sankur e, Anomaa no, Ɔkɔdeɛ.

Sankur e, anomaa no E, sankur e, sɛ woakɔ adidi a, san bra. "

Na Ɔdɔdeɛ no tee. Na ɔbaeɛ, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn na asɛm yi nyinaa fa ho? " Na Old Hawk kaa sɛ, "Wɔabɔ dua no sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wɔbɛfa wo mma, na Cooter deɛ, wɔde atuo abɔ no na wɔakum no. " Ɛhɔ ara na Golden rose up, wa (ɛno ne nnyigyei a ne ntaban no bɔeɛ), na ɔtenaa dua bi a ɛwɔ kuro no ano, na ɔmaa nani so, na ɛyɛ nokware sɛ wɔretwa dua no. Ɔkaa hwee. Na afei berɛ a dua no hwee ase no, Ɔkɔdeɛ no tuu mmirika kɔɔ hɔ, ho (ɛno ne ne ntaban nnyigyei), na ɔtwee ne mma no. Ɔfaa wɔn na ɔde wɔn sii dua no so wɔ baabi a ɔteeɛ no. Na afei ɔbaa fam na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ho (lit. wɔ ne ti mu) sɛ, "Ah, mmarima te sɛɛ, anaa? " Na ɛyɛɛ no awerɛhoɔ. Na ɔsu, na ɔkaa sɛ:

"Ná tuo no da nkyɛn, na ɔwɔ no da nkyɛn. "

Na ɔyii ne ase, na ɔbɛnya ntini mmienu, na ɔbɔɔ wɔn na ɔde nkra yi kɔmaa:

"Ma abɔdeɛ a ɛwɔ ntaban nyinaa mmra, (kosi sɛ ɔsoro bɛyɛ) tuntum, tuntum, tuntum. "

Na mmoa a wɔwɔ ntaban no nyinaa baeɛ. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn asɛm a esiiɛ no nyinaa, na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ wɔne no nkɔda Gyekye ase, na obiara a ɔnim su a ɔsu no, ɔsu. Bird a wɔfrɛ no Monkey no na ɔdii kan fii aseɛ sueɛ:

"Owu mpofirim na mɛwu!

Mɛwu mpofirim!"

Apatupere nnomaa no teɛɛm sɛ:

"M'ani nnye ntawntawdi ho!

Mani nnye ntawntawdi ho!

Kosante ba no ntama no, mempɛ. "

Akoko, ɔkraman a ne nan yɛ kɔkɔɔ, teɛɛm sɛ:

"Owuraye's Hoe, Hoe, Oh!

Owuraye's asɔ, asɔ, O!

Owuraye s asɔ, asɔ, asɔ, O! " Ntɛtea no teɛɛ so sɛ:

"Doy!

Doye! Doye!

Afua Anwea ne ne kunu Ano Kofi, Sɛ abofra nanteɛ firi awoɔ mu a, ɛno nnyɛ adeɛ pa, Kwaku Amoaten (a name).

Doye! Doye!

Doye! Doye!

Odiawuo ne ne Ano Mmirikyi a ɔda Ɔsono Se no, Wafi hɔ, Wafi hɔ.

Agya Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ, sɔre na kɔhwehwɛ putty-nosed monkey no na woayɛ nkwan ne anonneɛ, (bere a ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no yere kasa saa no,)

Abɔdeɛ biara (a ɔte ne nne sɛ ɔka saa) remma dua no ase (ba a wɔwɔ hɔ)."

Ɔpɔdee no teɛɛ so sɛ:

"Kyakyakya! nkawurubi!

(Hwɛ) abofra kumaa no tuo tuntum tenten no, ɔde rekyerɛ me sɛ ɔmfa ne nkrante nketewa no nkum me. "

Ɔpatuo, Patu no, teɛɛ:

"E! Mm!

E! Mm! "

Ɔnwam, Anwam no, teɛɛ:

"(Hunter) Akuaferi-Saka kɔkɔɔ ketewa no! "

Hornbill yere no ka sɛ:

"Ɔnni hɔ. "

Anwam no ka sɛ:

"Enti (ne yere), didi na yɛnkɔ. "

Na Saka bɔ ne tuo na ɛbɔ no, pa! Na ne yere (Hornbill no) tu kɔ na ɔka sɛ:

"Ɔwɔ hɔ!

Ɔwɔ hɔ! "

Na aserewa kumaa no teɛɛ so sɛ:

"Obeko Asamoa, When the common fool taps his palm-wine tree, early in the morning people are going backwards and forwards to it! "

Aburuburo, Aburuburo, teɛɛ mu sɛ:

"Bere a na me maame rewu no, ogyaw me wɔ dua bi a wɔatwitwa mu;

Bere a fork-branch no wui no, ɛgyaee me wɔ apple nhaban mfeɛ no mu;

Bere a apple nhaban no mfeɛ wuiɛ no, ɛgyaee me wɔ akɔntɔn baa dwumadibea;

Bere a ɔbran no nyaa me no, ɔde me dii dwuma atirimɔden so, atirimɔden so, atirimɔden so! "

Akyenkyena nnomaa no teɛɛ so sɛ:

Ɛyɛ me, ɛyɛ me, ɛyɛ me. " Na afei Brand no ankasa baeɛ, na ɔtoo dwom sɛ:

"Nkensen nkensen, mmoa a wɔwɔ ntaban pii!

Meda Gyekye ase!

Nkensen nkensen, nhwiren mmoa ya ya! "

(Na berɛ a ɔsu frɛɛ saa no) kuro no mu nkurɔfoɔ nyinaa dwane firii hɔ, na Gyekye anhu baabi a mmarima, nguan, mmaa, yerenom, ne nkokɔ anaa biribiara akɔ. Ɔno nkutoo ne ne yere ne ne ba no na wɔaka wɔ akutu dua no ase. Na Ɔkɔde no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Efi nnɛ kɔ, ɔbɔfoɔ biara renyɛ ɔdefoɔ da. " Eyi, me asɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi wɔ hɔ a wobɛtumi agye sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na ebi nso wɔ hɔ a wobɛtumi akamfo me.
