Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐKƆƆ SO SƐ ABƆFOƆ YƐ MMERƐHOƆ

ƆBƆFOƆ bi (once) kɔgyee tuo-nnuro (powder) ne nsa adwumayɛbea ahoroɔ mmienu kannifoɔ maa otuo ano aba, wɔ ayeyi ho, na ɔne ne yere ne abofra ketewa no kɔɔ nwura mu wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ wɔbɛkunkum nam. Na ɔsii ɔbɔfoɔ dan bi na ɔne ne yere ne ne abofra no tenaa mu. Berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔkɔɔ nwura no ho. Bere a ɔhunuu aboa kɛseɛ bi na ɔde ne tuo no bɔɔ no, ɔfom. Da biara na ɛyɛ saa, bɛyɛ mprenu nnawɔtwe. Na ne yere kae sɛ, Esiane sɛ minnya nam biara nni nni nti, mɛkɔ fie. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ a ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔɔ nwura ho no, na ne yere ne ne abofra nso afiri wɔn akuraa. Afei na tuo baako pɛ na aka. Afei me werɛ fi sɛ mekae sɛ, saa ɔbɔfoɔ no din ne Gyekye, na ne yere nso de Boniaye-kae-dabi (a ɛkyerɛ sɛ Some-day-the-ungrateful-one-will-remember). Bere a ɔbɔfoɔ no rekyinkyin no, na ɔadu odum dua bi ase, na ɛhɔ yɛ beae fɛfɛ. Ɛhɔ na ɔbɔfoɔ no dwendwenii ne etire mu sɛ, Sɛ me kɔ fie a, wɔbɛgye sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma ama powder no, na me nni bi, enti mɛfa saa tuo yi na mabɔ me ho menem sɛ ebia mɛwu. Ɛhɔ ara na adeɛ bi frɛɛ no sɛ, Creators Son. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Papa. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Dɛn nti na waniwa abere sɛɛ? anka afei na ɔbaa a wɔfrɛ no Go-and-spoil-the-fie no nni wfie hɔ a, anka mɛma wo biribi. Ɛneɛ na ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Ɛnyɛ hwee wɔ fie. Biribi kaa sɛ, Ɛnyɛ hwee wɔ fie nti, kɔ bon a ɛwɔ odum dua no dua sini no mu, na nnawuro mmienu nna hɔ nti fa ba. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔeɛ na ɔde baeɛ. Na adeɛ bi kae sɛ, Kɔtwa dua mma mmienu a ɛyɛ ɔdwen, na woto waniwa mu, na dwane fa baako si baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ, na fa foforɔ no nso dan na si baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ. Ɔbaa no, ɔkaa sɛ, Mawie. Biribi kae sɛ, Fa dawuru kɛseɛ no na bɔ no. Na ɔbɔɔ no, na nwura no bɛyɛɛ akuraa a ɛda dua mma mmienu no ntam. Na odum dua no gyina ne mfinimfini. Na saa Biribi bɛdaa ne ho adi sɛ ɛyɛ Ɔkɔdeɛ. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no sɛ, sɛ asɛm titiriw bi ba atenaeɛ a, ɛsɛ sɛ wɔtie no wɔ odum dua no ase. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Ho, mate. Bere a Ɔkɔdeɛ no aka eyi akyerɛ no, na wanya yernom, mpɔtam badwafoɔ, ne asomfoɔ. Ɛbɛduruu saa berɛ no, ne yere Some-day-the-ungrateful-one-will-remember nyaa hwam sɛ wabɛyɛ ɔhene, enti ɔtɔɔ mmɛwure nsã no, na ɔde kɔɔ ɔhene no akuraa ase, a na ne abofra no wɔ nakyi. Ɔbaa no, ɔbɔɔ ne sianteɛ sɛ ɔrebɛba, Wo abofra no se ɔmfa ne nsa mma sɛ ɔnhwɛ wo anim. Na ɔgyee nsa no, na ɔyɛɛ nsa a wɔde ba no nyinaa. Na ɔde ɔbaa no kɔmaa Gyaasehene sɛ ɔmfa no nkɔ ne fie. Bere a anwummere yɛɛ nwini no, ɔbaa no hohoroo no, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a ɔhene no wɔ ne dan mu. Na ɔbaa no ka kyerɛɛ ɔhene no sɛ, Wo ne me anya osuo ne awia, kɔsi da a woanya baabi tena, na anka ɛnyɛ nea ɛfata sɛ wode me bɛkɔ? Afei berɛ a ɔrekasa saa no, sɛ mmarima ne wɔn nkwasiasɛm no, na ɔnnya asɛm biara nka. Ɛneɛ na ɛhɔ ara na ɔkyeree ɔbaa no nsɔmu no na ɔne no daeɛ. Ɛduruu anɔpa kɛseɛ a ɛdi hɔ no, ɔyii no firii natenaeɛ wɔ Gyaasehene fie na ɔde no kɔɔ Ɔhene fie. Ɛhɔ na ɔtenaa kɔsii sɛ akyinnyegyeɛ kɛseɛ bi baeɛ. Ɔhene no ne ne mpɔtam badwafoɔ nyinaa kɔtenaa odum dua no ase. Ɔbaa no ba no gyinaa nagya anim, na ɔhunuu akɔre no ba no wɔ odum dua no so, na ɔkaa sɛ, Agya, mepɛ. Na nagya bɔɔ no asotire, na ɔpiaa ne etire akyi kɔɔ fie. Ɛnna mmaa foforɔ no mu baako ba bɛgyinaa nanim. Ɔsan nso huu akɔbere no ba no, na ɔkaa sɛ, Agya, mepɛ. Na ɔbɔɔ no ase wɔ saa kwan koro no ara so na ɔpiaa ne etire no akyi maa no hwee ase, na bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔsu. Na ne maame bisaa no sɛ, Dɛn nti na woresu? Na ɔkae sɛ, Agya bɔɔ me. Na ne maame kae sɛ, Ao! dɛn nti na ɔnka wo, sɛ anka ɛyɛ Obi-da-a-nkyerɛ-aseda-biako-bɛkae no abofra a, anka ɔrenni no so nkonim. Na Obi-da-a-nkyerɛ-aseda-biako-bɛkae no kae sɛ, Me deɛ, menyɛɛ wo hwee a ɛsɛ sɛ woyɛ me saa. Ntɛm ara na ɔkaa saa no, na wɔn baanu nyinaa tui kɔbɔɔ mu. Na asomfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Saa ade pɔtee a ɛwɔ saa dua yi so no nti, yɛn agya yerenom reko; yɛbɛtu no agu. Ntɛm ara na wɔnyaa wɔn nkuma, pimpen! pimpen! (saa na ɛyɛ nnyigyei a wɔbɔeɛ.) Wɔ saa berɛ no mu na na Ɔkɔdeɛ no akɔkyinkyin afa ne mmofra no akɔto Koterɛ, ne Old-Hawk sɔboɔ no so. Ɛduruu berɛ a nnipa no twaa dua no na ɛrehwe ase no, Koterɛ bɔɔ nkonyaayisɛm sɛ, "Pimpeao! " Na dua no baeɛ bom. Na wɔsan kɔɔ so bio. Na Koterɛ ka kyerɛɛ Old Hawk no sɛ, "Kɔpɛ Ɔkɔdeɛ sɛ ɔmmɛhwɛ ne mmofra, ɛfiri sɛ me deɛ, owuo aba me so. " Ntɛm ara na nnipa no kɔgyee wɔn atuo no kum no. Na Old-Hawk tuu kwan wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ ɔrekɔfrɛ Ɔkɔdeɛ. Ɛkwan a ɔrekɔ no nyinaa, na ɔsu sɛ:

"Grandma e! wo mma o!

Nanabaa e! wo mmofra o!

Bere a ɔfrɛɛ saa na Ɔkɔdeɛ antie no, ɔmaa ne nne so sui sɛ:

"Sankur" e, Anomaa E, sankur' e, Anomaa, Ɔkɔdeɛ.

Sankur' e, Anomaa E, sankur' e, sɛ woakɔ adidi a, san bra".

Na Ɔkɔdeɛ no tee. Na ɔbaeɛ, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn na eyi nyinaa kyerɛ? " Na Old Hawk kaa sɛ, "Wɔabubu dua no wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ wɔbɛfa wo mmofra no, na Koterɛ deɛ, wɔde tuo abɔ no na wɔakum no. " Ɛhɔ ara na Ɔkɔdeɛ no sɔreeɛ, wa (ɛno ne nnyigyei a na ne ntaban no bɔ), na ɔtee dua bi a ɛwɔ kuro no kwanano, na ɔmaa naniwa so, na na ɛyɛ nokware sɛ wɔrebu dua no. Na ɔnka hwee. Na afei berɛ a dua no bɔɔ na ɛhwee aseɛ no, Ɔkɔdeɛ tuu mmirika baa hɔ, ho (ɛno na ɛyɛ ne ntaban no mu nnyigyei), na ɔwiaa ne mmofra no. Ɔfaa wɔn kɔtenaa dua no so wɔ baabi a ɔahyew no. Na afei ɔbaa fam bɛkaa ne ho (lit. wɔ ne etire mu) sɛ, "Ah, mmarima te sɛɛ anaa? " Na ɛma no werɛ how. Na ɔsu kae sɛ:

"Ɛnyɛ tuo nko, gye ɔwɔ. "

Na ɔyii ne nsi, na ɛmaa ɔnyaa atwene bedeɛ mmienu, na ɔbɔɔ wɔn nkra yi:

"Momma abɔdeɛ a wɔwɔ ntakra nyinaa mmra, (kosi sɛ ewiem bɛyɛ) tuntum, tuntum, tuntum. "

Na abɔdeɛ a wɔwɔ ntakra no nyinaa baeɛ. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn nea ɛkɔɔ so no nyinaa, na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ wɔne no kɔ aseda Gyekye, na wɔn a wɔtumi su no nyinaa su. Ɛhɔ na anomaa a wɔfrɛ no Apitie no dii kan hyɛɛ aseɛ su:

"Merewuwu sɛ wu mpofirim!

Mɛwu wu mpofirim!"

Apatupere anomaa no suanee sɛ:

"M'ani nnye ntawntawdi ho!

Me kɔn nnye akasakasa ho!

Me kɔn nnye Kosante ba no ntama ho. "

Akɔkɔhwedɛɛ, ɔkorow a ne nan yɛ kɔkɔɔ no, teɛɛ mu sɛ:

"Owuraye abura, abura, O!

Owuraye asɔ, asɔ, O!

Owuraye asɔ, asɔ, asɔ, O! " Na atewa ketewa no teɛɛm sɛ:

"Doye!

Doye!

Afua Anwea ne ne kunu Ano Kofi, Sɛ abofra nanante pɛ a wɔawo no, ɛno nyɛ ade pa, Kwaku Amoaten (a ɔwɔ din).

Doye!

Doye!

Odiawuo ne ne Ano Mmirikisie a ɔda Ɔsono se, Wa gyaa no, Wa gyaa no.

Agya Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ, sɔre na kɔhwehwɛ potty-nosed monkey no na woayɛ no nkwan ne anonne, (bere a hunter no yere ka saa no,)

Ɛnnyɛ abɔdeɛ (who hear her say so) biara na ɛbɛba dua no ase. "

Ɔpɔdee no suanee sɛ:

"Kyakyakya! Nkawurubi!

Saa abofra ketewa no tuo tuntum tenten no, ɔde rekyerɛ me, wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ ɔmfa ne plantain a wɔayam no nkyene me. "

Ɔpatuo, Patu, teɛɛm sɛ:

"E! Mm!

E! Mm! "

Ɔnwam, Anwam, su frɛɛ sɛ:

" ((Hunter) Akuaferi-Saka the Little Red One! "

Hornbill yere kae sɛ:

"Ɔnni hɔ. "

Anwam no ka sɛ:

""Aso" (ne yere) didi na yɛnkɔ. "

Na Saka bɔɔ ne tuo bɔɔ no, pa! Na ne yere (the Hornbill) tu kɔeɛ na ɔkaeɛ sɛ:

"Ɔwɔ hɔ!

Ɔwɔ hɔ! "

Na aserewa ketewa no teɛɛm sɛ:

"Obeko Asamoa, Bere a kwasea kɛkɛ bɔ ne nnuadewa dua no, anɔpa anɔpa no, nnipa rekɔ nakyi kɔ! "

Aburuburo, Aburuburo, teɛɛm sɛ:

"Bere a na maame rewu no, ɔgyaee me wɔ sɔboɔ bi a ɛyɛ nnuadewa mu;

Bere a fork-branch no wui no, ɛgyaa me sɔboɔ a ɛyɛ mfeɛ a abe nhaban adi;

Bere a abe nhaban no mfeɛ wuiɛ no, ɛgyaa me sɔboɔ a ɛyɛ baa a akɔntɔn.

Bere a baa a akɔntɔn no nyaa me no, ɛde me dii dwuma katee, katee, katee! "

Akyenkyena anomaa no suanee sɛ:

Ɛyɛ me, ɛyɛ me, ɛyɛ me. " Na ɛnneɛ Ɔkɔdeɛ no ankasa baeɛ, na ɔsuanee sɛ:

"Nkensen nkensen, abɔdeɛ a wɔwɔ ntakra bebree!

Meda Gyekye ase!

Nkensen nkensen, abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ ya ya!"

(Na berɛ a ɔsu sɛɛ) no, kuro no mu nnipa nyinaa dwane firii ho, na Gyekye anhu baabi a mmarima, odwan, mmaa, yernom, ne nkokɔ anaa biribiara kɔɔ. Ɔno nko ara ne ne yere ne ne abofra na aka, wɔ odum dua no ho dua sin no nkyɛn. Na Ɔkɔdeɛ ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛfiri da yi, ɔbɔfoɔ biara renyɛ ɔdefoɔ da. " Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi wo betumi agye sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na ahomegyeɛ no nso wo betumi akamfo me ama no asɛmka.
