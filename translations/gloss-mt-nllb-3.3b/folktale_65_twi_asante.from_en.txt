Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA AKETEKYIRE, SAKRAWI, NYAA NASE A AHYE

AKETEKYIRE, Sakrawi, ne Kwaku Ananse, Ananse, fii aseɛ sɛ wɔrebɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ atokoɔ a wɔde bɛdua. Na wɔnya atokoɔ aba mmienu pɛ. Na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Me rekɔdua me deɛ. " Na Sakrawi kaa sɛ, "Me rekɔroast me deɛ sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a me bɛwe no. " Kwaku Ananse kɔduaa ne deɛ. Sakrawi no nso, ɔgyee ne deɛ, na ɔde guu gya no so. Ɛkyɛe sɛ wɔnoa aduane, na ɔbɛtenaa hɔ (ne nkyɛn). Ananse no nso, ɔkɔɔ afuom hɔ; na ɔrebɛhwɛ nkranteɛ a ɛkata aburow no so no ase. Aburoo no kaa sɛ, "Nhwɛ mani mu, anyɛ saa a, wo nsateaa bɛkɔ mani mu. " Kwaku Ananse de nani kyerɛɛ ne Kraman no so, na ne Kraman no kaa sɛ, "Nhwɛ me, ɛnyɛ me na meyɛeɛ. " Kwaku Ananse rebɛbubu dua a ɔde bɛbɔ Kraman no, berɛ a dua no kaa sɛ, "Nsɛe me, ɛfiri sɛ (sɛ woyɛ saa a) wo dua no bɛkeka me. " Kwaku Ananse baa fie bɛkaa ne nyinaa kyerɛɛ Sakrawi no. Bere a ɔresere no, ɔhwee ogya mu. Saa na Sacrawi no ho hyehye no. Eyi, me asɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
