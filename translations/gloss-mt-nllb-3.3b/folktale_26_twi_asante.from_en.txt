Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

ƐYƐ DEƐ ƐNAM SO NA MUSSEL (FRESH-WATER MUSSEL) A ƐMAA NE NSUBƆNTEN NO TUM NO, NANKASA DA ASUBƆNTEN ANO

WƆKYERƐ SƐ Okawa, Opitire, Bonko, Mmɔtɔ, ne nsuo mu mmoa nyinaa (te nkabom) na ɔpɛ kɛseɛ bi bae na nsutene no nyinaa wee, na ɛmaa mmoa yi amaneɛ bebree. Wɔdaa anhwea no so (nsutene no ase). Akyire koraa no wɔkae sɛ, "As for this misery which we have seen, let us go there to God, the Sky-God, and let us cry to tell him that he may bring us rain. " Abɔdeɛ ahorow no nyinaa sii kwan so. Na Okawa maa ne nne so (na ɔtoo dwom):

" Ɛnyɛ me, Oka' Abena, seesei mekɔ ha, seesei mekɔ hɔ?

Mehuruw gyina me nan so sɛ merebɛfrɛ wo.

Me hohoro akutu a ɛwɔ me nan so no.

Aberefi Sempa, oyi ne me yere, Abuya. "

Nanso osuo no antɔ. Na Opitire nso, ɔba bɛtoo dwom sɛ:

" Ɛnyɛ me, Opitire Abena, seesei mekɔ ha, seesei mekɔ hɔ?

Mehuruw gyina me nan so sɛ merebɛfrɛ wo.

Me hohoro akutu a ɛwɔ me nan so no.

Aberefi Sempa, oyi ne me yere, Abuya. "

Nanso osuo no antɔ. Na Bonko nso bɛtoo dwom sɛ:

" Ɛnyɛ me, 'Bonko Abena, sesei mekɔ ha, sesei mekɔ hɔ?

Mehuruw gyina me nan so sɛ merebɛfrɛ wo.

Me hohoro akutu a ɛwɔ me nan so no.

Aberefi Sempa, oyi ne me yere, Abuya. "

Nanso osuo no antɔ. Abɔdeɛ no nyinaa baeɛ bɛsu, na Fresh-water mussel no nko ara na aka. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ma me kwan ma mensu kakra. Wɔkae sɛ, Firi adi, yɛn osuo no anyɛ yie biara, na wo deɛ, dɛn na wopɛ sɛ wunya? Ɔkaa sɛ, Ma me kwan ma mesu. Nanso wɔbɔɔ no sɛ, wɔadi kan aka akyerɛ no sɛ ɔnsu. Nanso Fresh-water mussel no kae sɛ, " I shall weep. " Nti wɔkae sɛ, " Weep away. " Na ɔdaa ne nne so sɛ:

" Ɛnyɛ me, Apupuo Abena, seesei mekɔ ha, seesei mekɔ hɔ?

Mehuruw gyina me nan so sɛ merebɛfrɛ wo.

Me hohoro akutu a ɛwɔ me nan so no.

Aberefi Sempa, oyi ne me yere, Abuya. "

Na Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no bɔɔ ne aprannaa kɛseɛ! Abɔdeɛ no nyinaa kaa sɛ, Kɔ so, kɔ so. Na ɔsan nso teɛteɛɛ, na Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no bɔɔ ne ho kɔkɔɔ. Na afei abɔdeɛ no maa no so wɔ wɔn mmati so, kae sɛ, Kɔ so bio. Na ɔsan nso maa no so saa ara. Na afei Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no gyaee (osuo no), na ɛbɔɔ fam, ta! ta! ta! ta! na nsubɔnten no nyinaa ayɛ ma. Afei yi (now that) sɔre, na, twom! nsuo no kɔɔ mu (as they jumped in). Abɔdeɛ ahorow no nyinaa kɔɔ mu. Fresh-water mussel no, berɛ a anka ɔbɛkɔ nsuo no mu no, ɔsan kɔɔ nakyi (by the rest), na ɔdaa asubɔnten no ano. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Nsuo a ɛma ne nsutene no, ɛda bank. " Eyi, me asɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
