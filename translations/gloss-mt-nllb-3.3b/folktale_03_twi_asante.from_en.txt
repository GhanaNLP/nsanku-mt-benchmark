SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ NYANSA YƐƐ ABUSUAKUO NO NTAM

WƆKYERƐ SƐ Kwaku, Ananse no, na ɔwɔ hɔ, na ɔprapraa nimdeɛ nyinaa, na ɔboaboaa ano wɔ baabiako, na ɔde guu dwensɔtwaa mu. Afei ɔdaa no adi sɛ ɔbɛforo dua bi so na wakɔsɛn no, na ama nyansa a ɛwɔ asase so nyinaa awie.

Enti ɔfaa no kɔɔ soro sɛ ɔne no nkɔ, na bere a oduu dua no ase baabi a ɔrebɛsɛn no, ɔfaa hama, de kyekyeree dua no ho, de guu nanim, na ɔde ne ho sii hɔ sɛ ɔbɛforo dua no. Ɔforoo, ɔforoo, ɔforoo, nanso ne nyinaa yɛɛ kwa. Ɔsan bɔɔ mmɔden bio, ɔsan bɔɔ mmɔden bio sɛ ɔbɛforo, ɔforo, ɔforo, na ɔforo, nanso ne nyinaa yɛɛ kwa.

Afei, ne ba Ntikuma a na ɔgyina hɔ no kaa sɛ, "Ao, wani awu (wɔ aniwuo nti), anka ɛbɛyɛ yiye sɛ wobɛkyinkyin toa no na wode ato wo akyi, ɛnneɛ akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ wobɛtumi aforo? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Clear out, wo ne wo tete asɛm no. " Afei ɔdanee sɛ ɔbɛforo bio sɛ kan no, nanso bio, aba biara nni mu. Afei ɔdwenee ho pii, na (ɛwieeɛ koraa no) ɔfaa dua no de sii nakyi. Afei ɔhyɛɛ ne ho sɛ ɔbɛforo, na ɔforo ntɛm ara, Ɔkra! Ɔkra! Ɔkra! (ɛno ne foro no nnyigyei); ɛhɔ na ɔkɔ. Ɔduruu baabi a nkorabata no hyɛɛ aseɛ trɛw firii dua dua no mu, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ, "Me, Kwaku Ananse, wɔ onyame kumaa, Afio! Anka anka mewu, me ba a ɔyɛ nketenkete, nketenkete, nketenkete - na me wɔ hɔ, me boaboaa nyansa nyinaa ano (sɛnea me dwenee) wɔ faako, nanso ebi da so ara wɔ hɔ a me mpo minnim, na hwɛ! me ba, abofra a ɔda so ara yɛ nufu, akyerɛ me. " Afei ɔgyee saa table no, na nteɛsoɔ bi wɔ hɔ, print! na ɔtoo no gui, na nteɛsoɔ bi wɔ hɔ, tese! Saa kwan yi so na obiara nyaa nyansa; na obiara a wankɔ hɔ wɔ bere ano (sɛ ɔbɛfa bi) yɛ me "excuse me" nkwaseasɛm.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
