Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBƐYƐƐ SƐ MMARIMA YƐ BƆNE WƆ ANADWO SƐNEA ƐBƐYƐ SƐ MMOFRA DI AGORƆ WƆ BOSOME ANIGYE SƐNEA ƐBƐYƐ SƐNEA ƐBƐYƐ SƐ NSƐM A ƐFA MANSOTWE HO NO, WƆTE WƆ AWIA NO MU

Ɔsoro Nyame no woo mma baasa, a wɔn din ne Esum, Sum; Ɔsram, Ɔsram; ne Owia, Awia. Bere a ne mma baasa no nyinii no, Ɔsoro Nyame no maa wɔn kɔɔ nkuraase. Wei kyekyee ne akuraa; wei nso kyekyee ne akuraa, na wei nso kyekyee ne akuraa. Na wɔwɔ hɔ, na wɔn papa dɔ Awia. Na Sky-God no wɔ hɔ (reign) no, ɔmaa Akonnwa bi yɛɛ tuntum, na ɔka kyerɛɛ nasomfoɔ no sɛ, "Hena na onim nea me adwene (lit. the words in my head) yɛ? " Na Kwaku Ananse, Ananse no, kae sɛ, "Me deɛ, menim. " Wɔ bere a ɔkaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, menim," ɛnneɛ Sky-God no maa asomfoɔ no nyinaa sɔree. Ɛhɔ ara na Ananse nso sɔree sɛ ɔrekɔ Sky-god mma nkuraa ase. Berɛ a Kwaku Ananse duruu kwan no so no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ, "Mennim nadwene, nanso mekaeɛ sɛ, menim wɔn. " Na ɔtwee ntaban bi firii nnomaa biara mu de hyɛɛ ne ho mu, na ɔfirii hɔ kɔtenaa Sky-god dua bi so wɔ Sky-god akuraa ase. Na nkurɔfoɔ no hunuu nnomaa no, wɔn nyinaa yɛɛ nnyigyei kɛseɛ (a na ɛte sɛ) "Ye e e e! " Na Ɔsoro-nyame no firi fie baeɛ na ɔbaeɛ wɔ ginger dua no ase na ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa Kwaku Ananse yi, anka ɔbɛhunu nnomaa yi din, ɛfiri sɛ na masi gyinaeɛ sɛ Owia, Owia, ne nea mepɛ sɛ meyɛ no ɔhene, na mebisae sɛ hena na onim nea ɛwɔ me ti mu, na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ ɔyɛ, na me akɔ a d twee saa Beere a wɔfrɛ no 'Kintinkyi' no kɔɔ soro, na nea ɔbɛtumi ahu ne din aka no, ɔno na mede no bɛma no (akonnwa no). Ɛno nti na ɔkɔeɛ sɛ ɔbɛfa me mma no aba, na anka ɔwɔ ha a, anka ɔbɛhunu nnomaa yi din. Na afei nnomaa no tu fii hɔ, na ɔtwee ne ntaban no guu hɔ, na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔduruu Anadwo akuraa ase, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo papa aka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ woka me ho. " Na Anadwo kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ yie, me ne wo bɛkɔ. " Na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Me rekɔ sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ na me ho atɔ me. Owusrane ne Awia. " Na Anadwo ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "ma menhwehwɛ biribi mma wo nni. " Na Ananse no buae sɛ, "Ho. " Anadwo kɔfaa ɛmo a wɔayam bi de maa Kwaku Ananse. Bere a ɔwiee sɛ ɔwe no, ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ Owusrane akuraa ase. Bere a ɔduruu Ɔsrane akuraa ase no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo papa ka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wo ne me kɔ. " Na Ɔsrane kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ yie, me bɛkɔ. " Na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Me bɛkɔ Sun akuraa ase sɛ merekɔfa no aba. " Na Ɔsrane kaa sɛ, "Ma memfa biribi mma wo nni. " Na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ho. " Na Ɔsrane yɛɛ ɛmo maa no sɛ ɔnni. Na Ananse no sii kwan kɔɔ Sun akuraa ase. Bere a ɔduruu Awia akuraa ase no, ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Wo papa ka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wo ne me kɔ. " Na Awia kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ yie, me ne wo bɛkɔ, nanso ma menkɔma wo biribi nni. " Na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ho. " Na Awia kɔkyeree oguan bi. Bere a ɔsan bae no, ɔka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ anka me papa ba ha a, anka ɔbɛhu nea meyɛ, sɛ ɛyɛ papa, anaa ɛyɛ bɔne, asɛm biara mu no anka ɔbɛhu; nanso esiane sɛ ɔmmɛba, na woaba no, ɛte sɛ nea agya aba, nti, eyi ne me nguan a mɛkum no ama woadi. " Na ɔkunkum nguan no na ɔsiesiee no fɛfɛɛfɛ maa Ananse bɛdii. Na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔ dua (a ahwe ase) bi so. " Na berɛ a wɔakɔ (hɔ) no, na Kwaku Ananse ka kyerɛɛ Awia sɛ, "Wo papa ayɛ akonnwa bi a ɛwɔ hɔ no tuntum, na ɔpɛ sɛ wodi nkonim kɔ saa akonnwa no so, na wayi bayere bi na ɛda hɔ, na sɛ wonim sɛnea wɔbɔ ne din a, ɔbɛfa akonnwa no ama wo, na wɔfrɛ no 'Kintinkyi'. Na sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na wo werɛ amfi ne din no, mɛtwa bridge tiawa bi ama wo, na mɛyɛ platform bi a ɛne no bɛkɔ, na berɛ a wɔbɛbɔ (short) drum no ne platform no ara na wo werɛ remfi da, ɛfiri sɛ bridge tiawa no bɛkasa na waka sɛ:

Firi Bomba!

Firi bɔm!'

Ɛneɛ platform no bɛka sɛ:

Kintinkyi bɔm!

Kintinkyi bɔm!'"

Na wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ wɔbɛkɔ Ɔsram-Onyame kuro no mu. Na afei wɔduruu Moon kuro no mu na wɔfaa no, na wɔduruu Night kuro no mu nso na wɔfaa no. Ɛkwan no nyinaa mu no, na wɔbɔ stage bridge. Na berɛ a wɔduruu kuro no akyi no, Kwaku Ananse hunuu ɔbarima bi, na ɔsomaa no sɛ ɔnkɔka nkyerɛ Ɔsram-Onyame sɛ wɔreba. Na Ɔsoro-Onyame no frɛɛ assembly bi bom, na Kwaku Ananse nso bae na wɔbɛkyeaa (obiara). Na Kwaku Ananse de asɛm no kɔmaa Ɔkasafoɔ no sɛ, "Ɔpanyin no adwuma a wɔsomaa me no, mede aba. " Na Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Me mma, nea enti a meyii mo baa hɔ ne sɛ, me ayɛ adididan a ɛda hɔ no tuntum, na me nso mayi sare a ɛda hɔ no; mefa sare yi mma nea ɔhu sare no din. Ɛfiri sɛ me ba a ɔdi kan ne Anadwo, ma ɔmmɔ ne din. " Na Anadwo kaa sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ no 'Pona'. " Na nkurɔfoɔ no nyinaa kaa sɛ, "Ye e e. " Na Sky-god no kaa sɛ, "Me ba a ɔtɔ so mmienu ne Osun, enti ma ɔnka ne din. " Na Osun kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ bayere a wɔfrɛ no 'Asante'. " Wɔkae sɛ, "Ye e e. " Sky-god no kaa sɛ, "Me ba a ɔdi mmiensa no yɛ Owia, enti ma ɔmmɔ ne din. " Afei, me werɛ fi sɛ mɛka sɛ danc no e music no na ɛrekɔ so:

"Kintinkyi bɔm!

Kintinkyi bɔm! "

na Kwaku Ananse rekyinkyin nteaseɛnam no, na owia sɔre gyinaa hɔ na ɔgyee bankye no, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, eyi deɛ, ɛfiri berɛ a mefii aseɛ nanteɛ wɔ me papa nkyɛn na me nketewa paa no, na ɔka ne din kyerɛ me, na me werɛ mfi; wɔfrɛ no 'Kintinkyi'. " Na abusuakuo no bɔɔ dawuru mprɛnsa sɛ, "E! E! E! " Na ne papa sɔre gyinaa hɔ, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo, Night, wo na woyɛ panyin, nanso nsɛm a meka kyerɛɛ wo no, woama wo ho kwan ma wo werɛ afi, ɛfiri sɛ woamfa wadwene anhyɛ me nsɛm no so; ɛno nti, (it is decreed) sɛ bɔne nko ara na ɛbɛba wo bere so. Na wo nso, Ɔsrane, nsɛm a wo ne me nam no na meka kyerɛɛ wo no, woanni so, (ɛno nti na wɔahyɛ) sɛ mmofra nko ara na wɔbɛdi agorɔ wɔ wo ohendie no mu. Na wo, Awia, bere a mekaa nsɛm kyerɛɛ wo no, wo werɛ amfi, wotiee me afotuo, enti woyɛ Ɔhene; sɛ obiara wɔ asɛm bi a ɔbɛsiesie a, ma wɔntie no wɔ wo bere so, fie nsɛm nko ara, ntiantiaa biara nni hɔ mma nea wɔtie no anwummere. Na fa ɔkwan a mede asi wo so no, na sɛ Owia no pɛ sɛ ɔtwa mu a, ɔntow ne ho atwa wo ho ahyia, na ɔrentumi mma mmɛka wo; na bio, sɛ osutɔ-mununkum no boaboa ano a, Ɔsoro-nyame no bow no bɛtow (ɔsoro) ama mo mma a wɔwɔ mo ase no ahu bere a mɛtow (su no) no. t nsu yiri fa mu kɔ. Na bio, saa nsɛm yi a kan no na wɔfrɛ no 'Mmɛs of the Sky-God' no, esiane sɛ Kwaku Ananse, Ananse no, atumi akenkan (ahu) nsɛm a ɛwɔ me ti mu nti, momma wɔnnim (from henceforth) sɛ 'The Mmɛs of the Ananse'. Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi a wubegye no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na ebi a aka no nso a, wubetumi akamfo me.
