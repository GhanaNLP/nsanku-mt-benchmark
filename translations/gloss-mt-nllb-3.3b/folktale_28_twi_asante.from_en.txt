Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA NE SƐ, OKA NAM DUA NO YƐ RODO, NA 'KƆBƆ NAM MENEWA NO YƐ NSENSANEE

BERE BI A NA ɔbaa bi te hɔ a na ɔyɛ Ɔhemmaa, na na onni abofra, na ɔkɔɔ anyame nketewa a wɔwɔ hɔ no mu biako nkyɛn wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ ebia onyankopɔn bɛma no awo abofra. Na ɔde baako nso maa no, nanso ɔbɔɔ no amanneɛ sɛ, "Ɛnsɛ sɛ abofra a wobɛwo no di nam biara gye abofra mmerɛbo, ɛfiri sɛ ne honam mpo yɛ tabu. " Afei, da bi (bere a ɔbaa no woo abofra no akyi) na maame no kɔɔ afuw hɔ, na ɔde nguan mmerɛbo no maa ne maame Ɔkra sɛ ɔnnoa mma ne abofra no. Afei Ɔkra faa no, siesiee aduane no, nanso ɔkɔɔ kɔgyee odwan no nam ankasa bi, na ɔde bi guu mu (ne berɛboɔ no). Ɔhene Maame ba no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Maame aka sɛ makyiwadeɛ no yɛ nam ankasa, na woahwie bi agu, na sɛ mewe a, mɛwuwu." Ɔkra buae sɛ, "Aane, meahwie bi agu, na sɛ wuwu a, wuwu." Na Maame maame ba no buae sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ mididi na miwu anaa? " Na ɔkɔɔ dan no mu sɛ ɔrekɔfa ne maame kɔkɔɔ silk ntama no de akata ne ho. Na abofra no hyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ kwan no so, na Ɔkra dii nakyi. Ɔkra no suro sɛ, sɛ ne mpena baa no hyia no a, ɔbɛkɔ ɔhaw mu. Na wɔrekɔ no, na Ɔhene Mma no ba no ka sɛ, "Ɔkra! " Ɔkra kaa sɛ, "Aane! " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sɛ mema wo nkra a, wobɛkeka? " (i.e. wode bɛma). Ɔkra kaa sɛ, "Mɛka, mɛka, ansa na mawu; mɛka, mɛka, ansa na mawu.

Bere a me kɔɔ hɔ tete no, na bayere bi gyina hɔ.

Seesei me aba na kokote no da hɔ.

Kwae mu mprako a wɔfrɛ no Asamoa, Kuruti Amoa, na ɔda hɔ.

Gyanoa sika mfutuma no, minni bi, Boafo Denkye, Ɔpanin, kɔsii da a me nkwa bɛwie.

Ɔka sɛ, wɔnkum odwan mma no nni, wɔnkum anomaa mma no nni.

Ɔse, 'Awarefo, anɔpa pa.'

Ɔbuae sɛ, 'Ya'ku.'

Ɔkae sɛ: 'Owudifo no pɛ sɛ woma wo anɔpa pa.'

Ɔse, Yiw, Abrawo.

Ɔka sɛ, 'Me nuabea, ɛhe na worekɔ, na memfa nkra bi mma wo?'

Ɔkae sɛ: Merekɔ me nana akuraa ase, Sɛkyerɛ Gyamase.

Ɔka sɛ, Dɛn na ɛbaa nana kurom, Sɛkyerɛ Gyamase, sɛ akyene no nna da? Oboama agya, Agyei Ampɔn, agya no akɔ ɔko, anaa agya no akɔ dwadie a ɔrehu sɛ demmire tenten no ahwe ase afra wuram nhama no?

Mmienu san amma, Ntim Sekyere ne Kwakye.

Ɔkae sɛ, Ao, parrot, Boama, woabue nkɔmmɔ na woabue nkɔmmɔ, gyae na ma me nkasa kakra, efisɛ waniwa rekyinkyin nsuo so.

Wo nana Mpampinam kɔɔ mpataayi, na ɔyii ɔtwee kɛse pa ara.

Kwao Adu Dakowa, Bantama Mpedu Okyere, Adwoa Kɛseɛ, Odum Abena ne ne fetish (suman) a wɔfrɛ no "you walk in vain".

Adwoa e, Adwoa e, Abɔnten Kobi asewaa, Tontoroni ya, ewiase nyinaa yɛ wo dea. ' "

Na ɛnneɛ na abofra no hyiaa ne maame. Na maame no kaa sɛ, "Eyi, me abofra yi, a me ne no hyia ha yi, ɛwom sɛ medaa no wɔ fie nanso dɛn na aka no ma ɔreguan, yiridi yiridi? " Ɔhemmaa no bɔɔ ne nkoa a wɔdii nakyi no sɛ wɔnkyere no mma no. Nanso wɔantumi annye no, na ɔkɔɔ so ara na ɔtuu mmirika hwee asubonten no mu, na Ɔkra (afenaa) no nso hwee mu. Bere a woduu wɔn nkyɛn no, na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no adan mpataayi a dua kɔkɔɔ wɔ so, na Ɔkra nso danee Ɔkɔbɔ nam, na na nnsenene wɔ nafono so. Oka no dua kɔkɔɔ no yɛ ntoma kɔkɔɔ a na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no hyɛ, a ɔhwee nsuo no mu; Na nnsenene a ɛwɔ Ɔkɔbɔ no nsawa so no yɛ afenaa, Ɔkra, nsonsoo.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
