Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA YI NE SƐ, OKA NAM NO ADUA NO YƐ KƆLA, NA 'COLOR NAM' NO NSAWA NO YƐ NSENSANEE

BERE BI A ƐKƆƆ SO NO, na ɔbaa bi te hɔ a ɔyɛ ɔhemmaa, na ɔnni ba, na ɔkɔɔ anyame nketewa a wɔwɔ hɔ no mu biako nkyɛn sɛ onyankopɔn no mma no nwo ba. Na ɔmaa no baako, nanso ɔbɔɔ no amanneɛ sɛ, "Abofra no, sɛ wowo no a, ɛnsɛ sɛ odi nam biara gye nguan mmerɛbo, efisɛ ne nam mpo yɛ tabu. " Afei, da bi, (bere a ɔbaa no awo abofra no akyi) ɛna no kɔɔ afuom, na ɔde nguan mmerɛbo maa nafenaa, Ɔkra, sɛ ɔnnoa no mma ne ba. Afei Soul faa no, na ɔnoaa aduane no, nanso ɔkɔfaa nguan no nam ankasa bi, na ɔde bi guu mu (ne mmerɛboɔ no). Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Maame kae sɛ me snack no yɛ nam ankasa, na wode bi ahyɛ (me aduane) mu, na sɛ medidi a, mewu. " Ɔkra no buae sɛ, "Yiw, mede bi ahyɛ mu, na sɛ wubewu a, wuwu. " Na Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no buae sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ medidi na ama mawu anaa? " Na ɔkɔɔ dan no mu kɔfaa ne maame kɔkɔɔ silk ntama no de kyekyeree ne ho. Na abofra no fii kwan no so, na Soul dii nakyi. Soul bɔɔ hu sɛ sɛ nadamfo no hyia no a, ɔbɛkɔ ɔhaw mu. Na wɔrekɔ no, Queen Mother no ba no kae sɛ, "Soul! " Soul kae sɛ, "Yiw! " Ɔkae sɛ, "Sɛ mema wo nkra a, wobɛka anaa? " Soul kae sɛ, "Mɛka, mɛka, ansa na mawu; mɛka, mɛka, ansa na mawu.

Bere a mebaa hɔ kan no, beetle bi na ɛgyina hɔ.

Afei na maba na akokɔ no da hɔ.

Wild pig a wɔfrɛ no Asamoa, Kuruti Amoa, ɔno na ɔda hɔ.

Me nni Gyanoa sika kɔkɔɔ dɔteɛ biara, Boafo Denkye, Ɔpanin no, de bɛsi nnɛ yi me nkwa atwa mu.

Ɔka sɛ, wɔmma wɔnkum oguan na wɔmma no nni, wɔmma wɔnkum akokɔ na wɔmma no nni.

Ɔka sɛ, 'Me kunu, anɔpa pa.'

Obua sɛ, Yaku.

Ɔka sɛ, Ɔtumfoɔ no ka sɛ anɔpa pa ma wo.

Ɔka sɛ, Yiw, Abrawo.

Ɔka sɛ, 'Me nuabea, ɛhe na worekɔ a, ma memfa nkrasɛm bi mma wo?'

Ɔka sɛ, Merekɔ me nana Safia Gyamase akuraa ase.

Ɔka sɛ, Dɛn na aba nana akuraa, Sɛkyerɛ Gyamase, sɛ akokɔ no nna da? Oboama papa, Agyei Ampɔn, agya akɔ akodi, anaa agya akɔ dwadie a ɔhunu sɛ abaa tenten no ahwe ase na ɛne kwaeɛ nhama no abɔ mu?

Baanu nsan mmaa bio, Ntim Sekyere ne Kwakye nsan mmaa bio.

Ɔka sɛ, Oh, Parrot, Boama, woakasa akasa, gyae na ma me nkasa kakra, efisɛ wani so hyɛn nsu no so.

Wo nanabarima, Mpampinam, kɔɔ mpataayi, na ɔkyeree asau a ɛyɛ hu.

Kwao Adu Dakowa, Bantama Mpedu Okyere, Adwoa Kɛse, Odum Abena ne ne fetish (suman) a wɔfrɛ no "you walk in vain".

Adwoa e, Adwoa e, Asubɔnten Kobi banana, Tontoroni ya, wiase nyinaa yɛ ne dea. "

Na afei abofra no hyiaa ne maame. Na ɛna no kaa sɛ, "Me ba yi, a mehyia no ha, ɛwom sɛ me gyaw no wɔ fie deɛ, nanso dɛn na ɛkaa no sɛ ɔreguan, ɔyareɛ yareɛ? " Ɔhemmaa no bɔɔ ne nkoa a wɔdii nakyi no sɛ wɔnkyere no mma no. Nanso wɔantumi annye no, na ɔsan tuu mmirika kɔtoo asubɔnten no mu, na Soul (afenaa no) nso kɔtoo mu. Bere a woduu wɔn nkyɛn no, na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no adan mpataayi a ɔwɔ poma kɔkɔɔ, na na Ɔkra no nso adan ɔkraman nam, na nsensanee wɔ ne moma so. Oka dua kɔkɔɔ no yɛ ntama kɔkɔɔ a Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no hyɛe bere a ɔhwee nsu no mu no; Nsensanee a ɛwɔ ɔkraman no nsa so no yɛ nafenaa, Ɔkra no, aso.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
