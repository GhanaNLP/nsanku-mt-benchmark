Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA AWAREGYAEƐ BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU

Ɔbɔfoɔ bi, a wɔfrɛ no Kwasi Gyaba, ne ne yere kɔɔ pɛn bi kɔtenaa nsraban mu sɛ wɔrebɛkɔ abɔmmɔ. Afei, bere a Kwasi Gyaba kɔɔ kwaeɛ no, ɔamfa nam biara amma. Ɔsan amfa nam biara amma da.

Afei, da bi a ne yere bisaa no sɛ, "Me gye di sɛ, sɛ wokum nam a, wotɔn, na wodi (profit) anaa afei nso wode ma wɔ akyɛdeɛ mu, enti nnɛ-nnɛ yi, sɛ wokɔ kwaeɛ mu a, na woamfa nam mma a, mefiri hɔ rekɔ me maame nkyɛn, ɛfiri sɛ me ho akye sɛ mɛtena akraman, ne mpataa, ne snails, ne nea ɛkeka ho. " Ɔbɔfoɔ no, Kwasi Gyaba, fii aseɛ kɔɔ baabi a wɔtow nneɛma gu. Ɔhunuu trawl bi a ɛretwam. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ, Twee, baabi a woda no, ɛhɔ na me ne wo bɛhome. Ɔtwee no kɔɔ dua bi ase kɔgyinaa hɔ. Ɔmaa tuo no so, bɔɔ! Ɔnanka bi a ɔnim baabi a ɔfiri baeɛ no bɛtoo Ɔtwee no so, na asono bi nso tui wɔ so. Ɔbɔfoɔ no de ne ho sieeɛ na ɔyɛɛ dinn. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ, Ao! mmusuo bɛn na mahunu. " Ɔnanka no kaa sɛ, "Agya Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ, bra, nea ti bi redwene no, ɛtɔ da a, ti foforɔ nso ate, enti, ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ, bra ha, na yɛnkɔ yɛn akuraa, na yɛnkyɛ saa draw yi, ɛfiri sɛ yɛrenyɛ wo bɔne biara. " Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no faa nakodeɛ na ɔdii wɔn akyi. Wɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔkyɛ nam no. Ɔnanka no kaa sɛ, "Agya ɔbɔfoɔ, mepɛ wo, enti da ha nnɛ, na mɛma wo aduru; afei aduru yi, sɛ wode gu w'aso a, wobɛte nhaban, nnua, ne asubɔnten, ne mmoa nyinaa kasa. Enti anadwo yi kɔda, nanso nnhome. " Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔdaeɛ; ɔgyaee ahomegyeɛ. Ɔnanka no faa aduru no de guu n'aso mu. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔbɔfoɔ no tee nneɛma nyinaa kasa ase. Ɔfirii hɔ, na ɔbaa ne kuro no mu. Afei, berɛ a ɔbaa no, ɔtee sɛ ne maame ase ba ne babea (fie). Seesei ne maame ase no ani afura wɔ ne nifa ani. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔtenaa dan no mu. Seesei, kasa ne nakyi benkum (i. e. Me werɛ fi sɛ mɛka), kae sɛ bere a ohyiaa nasew no, na ɔreto atoko. Ɔbɔfoɔ no tenaa hɔ dwoodwoo, na akokɔ mmienu bi a wɔwɔ hɔ no (hyɛɛ aseɛ kasaeɛ); baako bisaa ɔfoforɔ no sɛ, "Woyɛ dɛn na wotumi nya aburow no, berɛ a, me kɔ (kɔfa) a, ɔpam me kɔ? " Ɔfoforɔ no buaeɛ sɛ, "Dɛn nti, woanhunu sɛ nani nifa no ani afura, enti bere a (wopɛ) sɛ wofa aburow no, sɛ wofa ɔsɛeɛ no ho a, ɔrentumi nhu wo? " Kwasi Gyaba na ɔtee (eyi); Ɔhwee ne ho wɔ nakyi serewee, twitwiw! twitwiw! twitwiw! Nasew no kae sɛ, "Ao, me kunu, dɛn nti na woreserew me saa? " Nanso Kwasi Gyaba kɔdaa asase a ɛyɛ serew so na ɔkɔɔ so serewee. Ɛhɔ ara na ne maame nuabea no kaa ɔsoro nyame no asɛm a wɔabara no kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔnkyerɛ nea enti a ɔsere no wɔ ɔsoro nyame no anim. Ɔsoro-nyame no somaa abɔfo sɛ wɔmmɛfa Kwasi Gyaba mmra. Bere a ɔduruu kuro no ano no, ɔdaa ne dwom no adi sɛ:

"Mprempren merekasa na mawu, Kwasi Gyaba e!

Me rebɛkasa na moawu, eyi ba a, ɛnsɛ sɛ mo su me. Kwasi Mpuroko Gyaba, ɔno na ɔrekɔ, Eyi ba a, ɛnsɛ sɛ mo su me.

Ɔsoro Nyame no mpanimfoɔ kaa sɛ, Tie ɔdeduani no dwom. Wɔde Kwasi Gyaba kɔɔ Ɔsoro Nyame no anim. Ɔkyeame (ɔkasafo) no sɔre kae sɛ, Kwasi Gyaba, wosere wo maame ase ampa, anaasɛ ɔredi atoro? Kwasi Gyaba buae sɛ, Agya, aduru bi a ɛwɔ me so no nti, sɛ mekyerɛ nea enti a mesere no a, mewu. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Esiane sɛ woyɛ me bɔfo nti, meremma wo wu. Ne maame ase kae sɛ, Agya, ɔredi atoro, na ɔresere kɛkɛ. Ɔkasafo no kae sɛ, Kwasi Gyaba, woate anaa? Kwasi Gyaba buae sɛ, Afei me srɛ da koro, na sɛ miwu a, fa me gu mu. Ɛnkyɛe koraa na nano gyinaa hɔ no, ɔpanyin bi ne ɔpanyin bi baa hɔ; na ɔpanyin no ka kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no sɛ, Gyina hɔ na ma menkɔ wo so. Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, Dɛn, nnipa yi anim? Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, Sɛ wunnyina hɔ ara a, mɛka interlocutor no asɛm sɛ wubetumi ayi wo ho. Afei na Kwasi Gyaba ate saa asɛm no. Ɛntɛm ara na ɔkaa sɛ, Sire, Sky-God, ma wɔn nnyae dɔteɛ a wɔtu, ɛfiri sɛ mewɔ asɛm bi a mɛka. Sky-God no kaa sɛ, Kasa. Kwasi Gyaba kaa sɛ, Meka asɛm a ɛnsɛ, sɛ me maame-wɔ-mmarima no mfa ne ba no nkɔ. Mpanimfoɔ no nyinaa kaa sɛ, Ɛyɛ nokware, ɛyɛ nokware, asɛm no awie. Ɛhɔ ara na Sky-God no hyɛɛ mmara sɛ, obiara, wo yɔnko mpo, sɛ ɔhaw wo a, s peak the ban upon him, or if it be your wife who does so, speak the ban and divorce her. Saa na ɛbaeɛ sɛ awaregyae no baa abusuakuo no mu. Ɛyɛ Akokɔnini a yɛhwɛɛ no.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
