Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA NE SƐ, SKY-GOD NSƐM NO BƐYƐƐ NEA WƆFRƐ NO "SPIDER-STORIES"

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse, bere bi kɔɔ Nyankopɔn, Ɔsoro onyankopɔn, nkyɛn wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ ɔbɛtɔ Ɔsoro onyankopɔn no nsɛm. Ɔsoro Nyame no kae sɛ, "Wobɛtumi atɔ anaa? " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Dabi, mɛtumi. " Ɔsoro Nyame no kae sɛ, "Nkurow akɛseɛ a ɛyɛ den te sɛ Kokofu, Bekwai, Asumegya aba, nanso wɔantumi annɔ, na wo a woyɛ ɔbarima kɛkɛ a ɔnni wura no, (woaka sɛ) wobɛtumi? " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Ɛdɛn ne nsɛm no boɔ? " Ɔsoro Nyame no kae sɛ, "Worentumi ntɔ no hwee. Abɔdeɛ ɔnini no nko ara ne Ɔnanka; Osebo, Daboɔ; Mmoatia, Mmoatia; (ne) Mmɔborɔ, Nwonwone. " Ananse kaa sɛ, "Mede saa nneɛma yi nyinaa bi bɛba, na nea ɛkyɛn so ne sɛ, mede me aberewa Nsia bɛka bebree no ho." Ɔsoro Nyame no kae sɛ, "Kɔfa ba." Ananse bae, na ɔkaa ne maame no nyinaa ho asɛm, na ɔkae sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ mɛtɔ Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn ne Skye nsɛm. y-onyankopɔn ka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mede Ɔnini, Ɔnanka no ba; Osebo, Daboɔ; Mmoatia, Mmoatia bi; ne Mmɔborɔ, Nwonwone; na mekae sɛ mɛhyɛ wo bebree no mu na mede wo akɔma Ɔsoro onyankopɔn. " Ananse no bisaa ne yere "Aso" sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ na yɛanya Ɔnini, Ɔnanka no? " Na "Aso" ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Kɔ na kotwitwa nnuadewa baa bi, kotwitwa hama a ɛfow bi nso na fa brɛ me. " na Spider nso ne wɔn san bae. Na "Aso" kae sɛ, "Fa wɔn kɔ asuwa ho. " Na Kwaku Ananse de wɔn kɔe; na ɔreyɛ akɔneaba no, ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛware sen no, ɛnyɛ tenten sɛ ɔno; wodi atoro, ɛyɛ tenten sen no. " Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɛhɔ na ɔda, baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ. " Ɔnanka (a na wate saa nkɔmmɔbɔ a wɔsusuwii yi) kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dɛn ne nyinaa? " Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ me yere "Aso, a ɔne me regye akyinnye sɛ saa nnuadewa yi yɛ l sen wo, na meka sɛ ɔyɛ ɔtorofoɔ. " Na Ɔnini, Ɔnanka no, kae sɛ, "Fa ma, na bra bɛsusuw me. " Ɔfaa nnuadewa no, na ɔde too Ɔnanka no ho. Ɔkae sɛ, "Trɛw wo ho. " Na Ɔnanka no trɛw ne ho, na Kwaku Ananse faa hama no na ɔpirapiraa no (na akyekyereɛ no nnyigyei no yɛ nwenene! nwenene! nwenene! kosi sɛ ɔbɛduruu etire. Kwaku Ananse, Ananse, kae sɛ, "Okwasea, mede wo bɛkɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn nkyɛn na manya Ewiem-onyankopɔn anansesɛm (wɔ nsasaeɛ mu)." Kwaku Ananse de no kɔɔ Niamey (Ewiem-onyankopɔn). Ewiem-onyankopɔn kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka (i. e. Me nsa aka no), ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ a aka. " Ananse san bae, na ɔbɛkaa nea ɛkɔɔ so no kyerɛɛ ne yere sɛ, "Nwonwone no da so ara wɔ hɔ. " Ne yere kae sɛ, "Hwehwɛ gourd, na fa nsuo hyɛ mu ma, na wo ne no nkɔ. " Ananse kɔɔ so faa nwura no mu, bere a ohuu (a) Nwonwone dɔm bi a wɔakyekyere wɔ hɔ no, ɔhwiee nsuo no bi guu Nwonwone no so. Afei Ananse hwiee deɛ ɛkae no guu ne ho so, na ɔtwaa borɔdeɛ ahaban bi de kataa ne etire so. Na afei ɔkasa kyerɛɛ Nwonwone no sɛ, "Sɛdeɛ osuo no aba no, anka ɛnsɛ sɛ woba bɛhyɛ me dɔteɛ yi mu, na osuo no ankɔbɔ wo anaa? So wo nhu sɛ mede borɔdeɛ ahahan akata me ho? " Ɛnna Nwonwone no kaa sɛ, "Yɛda wo ase Aku, yɛda wo ase Aku. " Nwonwone no nyinaa tuu wim kɔeɛ wɔ dɔteɛ no mu, fom! Agya Ananse kataa nano, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Anyansafoɔ, manya mo, na mede mo rekɔgye Ewiem-onyankopɔn anansesɛm (wɔ nsasaeɛ mu)." Na ɔde Nwonwone no kɔɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn nkyɛn. Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka, nea aka no, ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ. " Ananse no san baa bio, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Osebo a ɔyɛ Daboɔ no na ɔda so ara wɔ hɔ. " "Aso" kae sɛ, "Kɔtu ɛtokuro bi. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɛno ara dɔɔ so, mate aseɛ. " Afei Ananse no kɔɔ sɛ ɔrebɛhwehwɛ Daboɔ no akwan, na ɔhunuu no, ɔtutuu amoa a emu dɔ, na ɔkata so, na ɔsan baa fie. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ anɔpa paa, berɛ a nneɛma fii aseɛ bɛdaa adi no, Ananse kyerɛɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ, na berɛ a ɔkɔɔ lO no, na Daboɔ bi da amena no mu. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ: "Agya ba ketewa, ɛna ba ketewa, maka akyerɛ wo sɛ nnom nsa, na afei, sɛnea obi bɛhwɛ kwan wɔ wo ho no, woanom nsa, na ɛno nti na woahwe amena no mu. Sɛ meka sɛ mɛyi wo afi mu a, anka ɔkyena, sɛ wuhu me anaa me mmofra no mu biara a, wobɛkɔ akɔkyere wɔn. " Daboɔ no kae sɛ, "O! Na mentumi nyɛ saa adeɛ no. " Kwaku Ananse kɔtwaa nnua mmienu, de baako sii ha, na baako nso sii hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa wo mmoa nanpɔw baako si ha, na fa wo mmoa nanpɔw baako nso si ha. " Na Daboɔ no de wɔn sii hɔ. Bere a ɔrebɛforo akɔ soro no, Kwaku Ananse maa ne sekkan no so, na hannrɛ fɛfɛ bi baa fam wɔ ne etire so, gao! (ɛno ne nnyigyei a ɔyɛeɛ). Daboɔ no nyaa amena (ne) fOM! (Ɛyɛɛ ne nnyigyei a ɔhwee ase no). Kwaku Ananse nyaa atwerɛ sɛ ɔmmerɛ amena so na ɔrekɔyi Daboɔ no afiri hɔ. Ɔyii Daboɔ no firii hɔ na ɔne no baeɛ; ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔkwasea, mede wo rekɔsesa ama Ɔsoro Nyame no nsɛm. " Ɔmaa Daboɔ no so sɛ ɔnkɔma Niamey, Ɔsoro Nyame no. Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, "Mnsa aka no, nea aka no da so ara wɔ hɔ." Ananse no bae, na ɔtwaa Akua ba (dua) bi, na ɔde amamfoɔ a ɛkamfam bɔɔ dua no, na ɔde ɛto (bayerɛ ɛtɔ) bɔɔ no, na ɔde bi guu dua no nsa, na ɔde bi guu ayowa no mu. na ɔde kɔɔ odum dua bi nan ase, baabi a Mmoatia ba bɛdii agoru. Na Mmoatia bi baeɛ. Ɔkaeɛ sɛ, Akua, mɛtumi adidi saa pɔtɔ yi bi anaa? Kwaku Ananse twitwiw hama a etwa to no, na akuaaba no bɔɔ ne etire. Mmoatia ka kyerɛɛ ne nuammaa no mu baako sɛ, Ɔse metumi adi bi. Ɔkaa sɛ, Di bi, afei. Na ɔdi wieeɛ, na ɔdaa nase maa akuaaba no. Nanso bere a ɔdaa no ase no, wammua. Na Mmoatia ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no sɛ, Sɛ meda no ase a, ɔnka. Mmoatia a odi kan no nuabea no kae sɛ, Ma no asotwi. Na ɔbɔɔ no, pa! Na ne nsa kyeree hɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no sɛ, Me nsa kyeree hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, Fa nea aka no na san bɔ no asotwi. Na ɔfaa no bɔɔ no, pa! na eyi nso, ɛkyeree ntɛm ara. Na Mmoatia ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no sɛ, Me nsa abien no nyinaa akyekyere ntɛm ara. Ɔkaa sɛ, Fa wo yafunu no tu no. Ɔpueeɛ, na ne yafunu no bɛkyeree no. Na Kwaku Ananse baeɛ bɛkyekyeree no, na ɔkaa sɛ, Ɔkwasea, manya wo, mede wo bɛkɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn nkyɛn nsasaeɛ ama ne nsɛm. Na ɔne no kɔɔ fie. Na Kwaku Ananse ka kyerɛɛ ne maame, Ya nsia (ba a ɔtɔ so nsia), sɛ: Sɔre, ma yɛnkɔ, ɛfiri sɛ me ne Mmoatia rekɔfa wo akɔma Ewiem-onyankopɔn (wɔ nsasaeɛ mu) wɔ ne nsɛm ho. Ɔmaa wɔn so, na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔsii sɛ Ewiem-onyankopɔn no wɔ hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, Ewiem-onyankopɔn, Mmoatia bi wɔ ha, na me yere a meka ne ho asɛm no nso wɔ ha. Afei Ewiem-onyankopɔn frɛɛ ne mpanimfoɔ, Kontire ne Akwam mpanimfoɔ, Adonten (asraadom no mu kuw titiriw no mu ɔkannifoɔ), Gyaase (major-domo); Oyoko, Ankɔbea ne Kyidɔm (akyi hɔ ɔkannifoɔ). Na ɔde asɛm no too wɔn anim sɛ, ahemfo akɛseɛ pii aba, na wɔantumi annɔ ɔsoro-Onyame nsɛm no, nanso Kwaku Ananse, Ananse, atumi atua boɔ no. Me nsa aka Mmɔborɔ, Nwonwone, afiri ne hɔ; Me nsa aka Mmoatia, Mmoatia, afiri ne hɔ; Me nsa aka Osebo, Daboɔ, afiri ne hɔ; Me nsa aka Ɔnini, Ɔnanka, afiri ne hɔ; na nankasa napam, Kwaku Ananse de aka ho. ed ne maame bebree; saa nneɛma yi nyinaa da ha. Ɔkaa sɛ, Momoto nayeyi. Eee! (wɔteɛɛm). Ɔsoro Nyame no kaa sɛ, Kwaku Ananse, ɛfiri nnɛ kɔsi da ne daa, mefa me Ɔsoro Nyame no nsɛm na mede rema wo, kosɛ! kosɛ! kosɛ! me nhyira, nhyira, nhyira. Daabi bio yɛremfrɛ no Ɔsoro Nyame no nsɛm, na mmom yɛbɛfrɛ no Spider-nsɛm.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
