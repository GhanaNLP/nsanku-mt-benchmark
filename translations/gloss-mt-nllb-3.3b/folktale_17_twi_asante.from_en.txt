SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ SKY-GOD NSƐM NO BƐYƐƐ NEA WƆFRƐ NO "SPIDER-STORIES"

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse no, da bi kɔɔ Nyame, Ɔsoro Nyame no nkyɛn sɛ ɔrekɔtɔ Ɔsoro Nyame no nsɛm. Ɔsoro-nyame no bisaa sɛ, "Wobɛtumi atɔ wɔn anaa? " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Dabi, metumi. " Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Nkurow akɛseɛ ne ahoɔdenne te sɛ Kokofu, Bekwai, Asumegya aba, nanso wɔantumi annye wɔn, na wo a woyɛ ɔbarima kɛkɛ, (ose) wobɛtumi? " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn ne (nsɛm) no boɔ? " Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Wontumi ntɔ wɔn mma hwee. gye sɛ Abɔdeɛ a Ɔnyini, Ɔnanka; Ɔsebɔ, Ɔsebɔ; Mmoatia, Mmoatia; (ne) Mmɔborɔ, Nwonwone. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Mede nneɛma yi nyinaa mu bi bɛba, na (nea ɛka ho ne sɛ), mede me maame panyin, Nsia (ɔba a ɔto so nsia) bɛka nea ɛdɔɔso no ho. " Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na fa bra saa ara. " Ananse no baeɛ, na ɔkaa ne maame asɛm no nyinaa kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ metɔ ɔsoro-nyame ne Sk nsɛm no. y-nyame se ɛsɛ sɛ mede Ɔnini, Ɔnanka no ba; Ɔsebo, Ɔsebo; Mmoatia, Mmoatia; ne Mmercy, Nwonwone; na mekae sɛ mɛka wo ho aka pii no ho na makɔma Sky-nyame. " Afei Ananse no bisaa ne yere Aso sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ na yɛanya Ɔnini, Ɔnanka no? " Na Aso ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Kɔ na wotwitwa mmɛdua dua bi baa, na wotwitwa nhama a awuduru wɔ mu nso, na fa bra. " Na th E Spider ne wɔn san bae. Na Aso kaa sɛ, "Fa wɔn kɔ asuo no mu. " Na Kwaku Ananse de wɔn kɔe; bere a ɔrekɔ no ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛware sen ɔno, ɛnware sɛ ɔno; wo di atoro, ɛware sen no. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Ɔwɔ hɔ a ɔda hɔ. " Ɔnanka (a na wate saa nkɔmmɔ yi) kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dɛn koraa? " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ (me yere) Aso na ɔne me rekasa sɛ saa palm-branch yi yɛ l Onger sen wo, na meka sɛ ɔyɛ ɔtorofo. " Na Ɔnini, Nana no, kae sɛ, "Fa no bra, na bra bɛsɔ me. " Ɔfaa nnuaba dua no na ɔde too Nana no nipadua so. Ɔkae sɛ, "Trɛw wo ho mu. " Na Nana no trɛw ne ho mu, na Kwaku Anans faa hama a wɔde twitwiw nneɛma no na opiraa no (na sanku no nnyigyei ne nwiinwii! nwiinwii! nwiinwii! kosii sɛ ɔbaa ti no so. Kwaku Ananse, Ananse no, kaa sɛ, "Ɔkwasea, mede wo bɛkɔ Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn nkyɛn na magye Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no nsɛm (wɔ nsakraeɛ mu)." Kwaku Anans de no kɔɔ Nyame nkyɛn. Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no kaa sɛ, "Me nsa aka (ie. Me nsa aka no), ɛda so ara wɔ nea ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ. " Ananse no san bae, na ɔbɛkaa nea esii no kyerɛɛ ne yere, na ɔkae sɛ, "Nwonwone no da so ara wɔ hɔ. " Ne yere kaa sɛ, "Hwehwɛ gourd bi, fa nsuo hyɛ mu, na fa kɔ. " Ananse no de ne ho kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu, bere a ɔhunuu Nwonwone (adɔm bi) a wɔakyere wɔn ho wɔ hɔ no, ɔhwiee nsuo no bi guu Nwonwone no so. Afei akraman no hwiee nea aka no guu ne ho so, twaa akutu ahaban na ɔde kataa ne ti so. Na afei ɔkasa kyerɛɛ Nwonwone sɛ, "Sɛdeɛ osuo no aba no, anka ɛnsɛ sɛ woba bɛhyɛn yi mu, me gourd, na osuo no ammɛbɔ wo; wunhu sɛ mede borɔdeɛ ahahan akata me ho? " Ɛnna Nwonwone kaa sɛ, "Yɛda wo ase Aku, yɛda wo ase Aku. " Nwonwone no nyinaa tuu kwan kɔhyɛɛ gourd no mu, fom! Agya Ananse kataa nano, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔkwaseafoɔ, me nsa aka mo, na mede mo rekɔgye Ɔsoro Nyame no anansesɛm (wɔ exchange)." Na ɔde Nwonwone no kɔmaa Ɔsoro Nyame no. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka, nea aka (da so ara) da so ara. " Ananse no san bae bio, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere, na ɔkae sɛ, "Seabel da so ara wɔ hɔ, Seabel. " Aso kae sɛ, "Kɔ na tu ɔtokuro bi. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɛno ara dɔɔso, mete aseɛ. " Afei Ananse no kɔɔ baabi kɔhwehwɛɛ Seabel no kwan, na (ɛhunuu no) ɔtoo amena a emu dɔ paa, na ɔtoo mu, na ɔsan baa fie. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no anɔpatutuutu, berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɛda adi no, Ananse no kyerɛɛ sɛ ɔrebɛkɔ, na berɛ a ɔkɔɔ no, (ahwɛ) ɔsono bi da amena no mu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ: "Agya ba ketewa, ɛna ba ketewa, meka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ nnom nsa, na afei deɛ, sɛnea obi bɛhwɛ kwan wɔ wo ho no, woaboro nsa, na ɛno nti na woahwe amena no mu; sɛ anka mɛka sɛ mɛyi wo, ɔkyena no, sɛ wuhuu me, anaa saa ara nso na wohuu me mma no mu biara a, wobɛkɔ akɔkyere wɔn. " Ɔsebɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "O! Merentumi nyɛ saa adeɛ no. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ hɔ kɔtwaa nnua mmienu, de baako sii ha, na baako nso sii hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa wo nan baako si ha, na fa wo nan baako nso si ha. " Na ɔbɔfoɔ no de wɔn sii hɔ (wɔ baabi a wɔaka akyerɛ no). Ɔrebɛn soro no, Kwaku Ananse maa ne sekan no so, na hann fɛfɛɛfɛ bi bɛtoo ne ti so sɛ, gao! (ɛno ne nnyigyeɛ a ɔyɛeɛ). Abura no gyee ɔbɔfoɔ no (na) fOM! (ɛno ne nnyigyei a ɔhwee ase no). Kwaku Ananse nyaa krataa sɛ ɔmmra abura no mu na ɔmmɛyi Ɔsebɔ no. Ɔyii Ɔsebɔ no na ɔne no bae, ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔkwasea, mede wo rekɔ sesa ɔsoro-nyame no nsɛm. " Ɔmaa Ɔsebɔ no so sɛ ɔnkɔma Nyame, ɔsoro-nyame no. Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka, nea aka no da so ara wɔ hɔ. " Ananse no bae, na ɔyɛɛ Akua abofra (dua a ɛyɛ tuntum a nanim yɛ tratraa), na ɔde nnua bi a ɛyɛ tratraa (a efi dua bi so) bɔɔ no na ɔde yɛɛ abeawa no nipadua ho adwuma, na ɔbɔɔ no akuturuku (bayerɛ tent), na ɔde bi guu abeawa no nsa mu, na ɔbɔɔ no pii na ɔde guu kɔbere ahina mu; Ɔkyekyeree nhama no wɔ abeawa no asen mu, na ɔde no kɔtoo elm dua bi ase, baabi a Mmoatia no ba bɛdi agoru. Na Mmoatia bi baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Akua, metumi adi pasta yi bi anaa? " Kwaku Ananse twitwiw nhama no, na ɔbaa no bɔɔ ne ti. Mmoatia no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea bi sɛ, "Ɔse metumi adi bi. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Didi bi, afei. " Na ɔwiee aduane no, na ɔdaa no ase. Nanso bere a ɔdaa no ase no, wammua. Na Mmoa no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no sɛ, Bere a meda no ase no, ɔnka. Mmoa no nuabea (a odi kan) kae sɛ, Slap her weeping-place. Na ɔbɔɔ no, pa! Na ne nsa bɛkaa hɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no sɛ, Me nsa aka hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, Fa nea aka no na bɔ no bio. Na ɔfaa no bɔɔ no, pa! na eyi nso, ɛkyeree no ntɛm ara. Na Cat no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no sɛ, Me nsa mmienu no kyeree me. Ɔkae sɛ, Fa wo yafunu bɔ no. Ɔde bɔɔ no, na ne yafunu no kyeree no. Na Kwaku Ananse ba bɛkyekyeree no, na ɔkae sɛ, Ɔkwasea, me nsa aka wo, mede wo bɛkɔ Ɔsoro Nyame nkyɛn (wɔ exchange) ama nasɛm. Na ɔne no kɔɔ fie. Na Kwaku Ananse ka kyerɛɛ ne maame, Ya Nsia (ne ba a ɔtɔ so nsia), sɛ, Sɔre, yɛnkɔ, ɛfiri sɛ me ne Mmoatia rekɔfa wo akɔma Ɔsoro-Nyame no (wɔ exchange) ama ne nsɛm. Ɔmaa wɔn so, na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔsii sɛ Ɔsoro-Nyame no wɔ hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, Ewiem-nyame, Mmoatia bi ni, na me yere dedaw a mekaa ne ho asɛm no, ɔno nso ni. Afei na Ɔsoro-nyame no afrɛ (nadwumayɛfoɔ), Kontire ne Akwam mpaninfoɔ, Adonten (asraafoɔ kuo titire no kannifoɔ), Gyaase (major-domo); Oyoko, Ankbea ne Kyidom (akyire ahobanbɔfoɔ no kannifoɔ). Na ɔde asɛm no sii wɔn anim sɛ, Ahenfo akɛseɛ bebree aba, na wɔantumi anntɔ Sky-gods no ho nsɛm, nanso Kwaku Ananse, Ananse no, atumi atua ka no; Me nsa aka afiri ne hɔ Mercy, the Wonderwone; Me nsa aka afiri ne hɔ Animation, the Animation; Me nsa aka afiri ne hɔ Beast, the Beast; Me nsa aka afiri ne hɔ Onini, the Chief; na ɔno ara napam no, Kwaku Ananse de aka ho. Ed ne maame pii; nneɛma yi nyinaa da ha. Ɔkaa sɛ, Monto nayeyi dwom. Yiw! (wɔteɛɛ mu). Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, Kwaku Ananse, ɛfiri nnɛ kɔsi daa, mefa me Ɔsoro-nyame nsɛm yi na mede ma mo, koshe! koshe! koshe! me nhyira, nhyira, nhyira. Yɛnfrɛ no bio sɛ Ɔsoro-nyame nsɛm, na mmom yɛbɛfrɛ no Spider-nsɛm.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
