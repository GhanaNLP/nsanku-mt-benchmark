Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

KWAKU ANANSE, ANANSE NO, KAE SƐ ƆREKƆ KWASEA BI HWEHWƐ MU, BERE A NA NE HO NYINAA YƐ KWASEA

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse no, na ɔte hɔ pɛn, na ɔka sɛ ɔpɛ kwasea bi a ɔbɛkɔ ne no akɔto mpataa afiri. Ɔkyinkyin kɔɔ baabiara, nanso wannya bi. Afei na ɔne Osansa, Akrɔma no hyiaa. Ɔkaeɛ sɛ, Momma yɛnkɔ si mpataa afiri. Akrɔma no buaa sɛ, Me wɔ nam (plenty) a mɛdi. Ɛno akyi no, ɔkɔhyiaa Anene, Kwaakwaadabi no, na ɔkaeɛ sɛ, Momma yɛnkɔ si mpataa afiri. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, Metee. Afei, Akrɔma no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaakwaadabi no sɛ, Afei asɛm a ɛfa mpataa afiri yi a wo ne Kwaku Ananse rekɔ no, monkɔ, ɛfiri sɛ Kwaku Ananse ka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ kwasea, ɛno na ɔne kwasea no si mpataa afiri no. s, ɔbɛnya sika no nyinaa. Kwaakwaadabi buaa no sɛ, Wo ho nhia sɛ woka asɛm foforo, ɛfiri sɛ mehwɛ sɛ mɛnya sika no nyinaa. Kwaakwaadabi ne Ananse kɔɔ nwura mu sɛ wɔrekɔtwa mmɛbaha. Kwaakwaadabi no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, Ma me sekkan no na menkɔ twa, na wo adwuma ne sɛ wobɛsoa brɛ (nea efi me adwumayɛ mu ba). Ananse no buaa sɛ, Wofa me sɛ kwasea, ma menyɛ atwaeɛ no, na wofa brɛ no. Kwaku Ananse ne atwaeɛ (pALMS) berɛ a Anene, Kwaakwaadabi no, te hɔ teɛteɛ. Bere a wɔtwaa no wiei no, wɔde guu babaso mu. Kwaakwaadabi no kae sɛ, Ananse, ma memfa wɔn nkɔ, bere a wo bɛsoa brɛ no. Ananse no nso kae sɛ, Mopue, mofa me sɛ kwasea anaa? Na ɔde guu etire no so. Kwaakwaadabi no dii nakyi teɛteɛɛm. Bere a woduu fie no, Kwaakwaadabi no kae sɛ, "Ma menyɛ mpataa afiri no na fa me nnwuma no ho brɛ. " Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "Dabi; ma menyɛ na fa brɛ no. " Afei Kwaakwaadabi no kɔtenaa nakyi, na ɔresu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Woyɛ ɔkwasea, woka sɛ wowɔ adwene, nanso worewu. " Ananse no wiee mpataa afiri no. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Ma mensoa mpataa no nkɔ nsuo no ho, na fa brɛ no. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Dabi, me ne wɔn bɛkɔ, na fa brɛ no. " Wɔduruu nsuo no ho no, Kwaakwaadabi kaa sɛ, "Agya Ananse, aboa kɛseɛ bi te nsuo no mu, enti ma mennyina nsuo no mu nsi mpataa no, na sɛ aboa kɛseɛ no ka me a, fa owuo no. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Aane, wofa me sɛ ɔkwasea ankasa, me ara na me bɛhyehyɛ afidie no, na sɛ ɔka me a, wo bɛgye owuo no. " Kwaku Ananse faa afidie-apataa no kɔtoo nsuo no mu, na wɔsan baa fie. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛdaa adi a, wɔkɔhwehwɛɛ afidie no mu, na wɔanya apataa mmienu. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Ananse, fa mmienu yi kɔ, na ɔkyena a yɛbɛba na afidie no akyere nnan no, me dane so mɛfa wɔn akɔ. " Ananse no nso kaa sɛ, "Saa yɔnkoɔ yi deɛ, wofa me sɛ ɔkwasea ankasa, wofa mmienu yi kɔ, na ɔkyena me deɛ, me dane so mɛfa nnan no. " Kwaakwaadabi no de wɔn kɔeɛ. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, wɔkɔɔ afidie no mu bio, na wɔnyaa anan (apataa). Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Fa nnan yi, na ɔkyena a yɛbɛba abɛyi awotwe no, me dane so bɛfa wɔn. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Wofa me sɛ kwasea, wowɔ nnan yi, na ɔkyena mɛfa awotwe no. " Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, wɔkɔsraa afidie no na wɔyii awotwe no. Kwaakwaadabi kae sɛ, "Fa wɔn, na ɔkyena mɛfa dunsia no." Ananse kae sɛ, "Wobu me sɛ meyɛ kwasea. Fa awotwe no, na ɔkyena mɛfa dunsia no. Seesei mpataa afiri no asɛe, na Kwaakwaadabi kasa kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, Kɔfa mpataa afiri a asɛe yi ba, na sɛ wɔtɔn a, wobɛnya ntanu (i. e. (£16) for each single (trap), so do you, Agya Ananse, take these apataa, and take the rotten paper traps and give them to me. Ananse said, No, do you take the apataa and go sell them, and I will take the rotten paper traps and take (the mfasoɔ). They carried (their nneɛma) away to a dwaso. Immediately the Kwaakwaadabi had disposed of all his nneɛma; ɔnyaa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma pii. Nanso na Ananse nneɛma no da so wɔ hɔ. Ɛnna Kwaakwaadabi no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Fa mpataa afiri a ɛyɛ fĩ yi na hyehyɛ no hyia na wɔbɛtɔ. Ananse no maa wɔn so, ɔkae sɛ, Merekɔn mpataa afiri a ɛyɛ fĩ! Afei akuraa no mu hene no tee no, na ɔkae sɛ, Ɛhe na yɔnkoɔ yi firi? Kɔfrɛ no sɛ ɔmmra. Ananse no kɔe. Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, So wosusuw sɛ yɛn a yɛwɔ akuraa ha no yɛ nkwaseafoɔ? Seesei wo adamfo yi baeɛ na ɔtɔn apataa a ɛwɔ mfasoɔ, woanhwɛ no, nanso wopɛ sɛ woyi apataa a ɛyɛ fĩ gu yɛn so? Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, Flog no. Enti wɔbɔɔ no. Afei Kwaakwaadabi kae sɛ, Spider, woahu, waniwa awu aniwu; wokae sɛ na woakɔ hwehwɛ kwasea bi a ɔne wo bɛyɛ adwuma, na woahu (seisei) sɛ wo ara na woyɛ kwasea no.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
