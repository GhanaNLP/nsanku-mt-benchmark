Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

KWAKU ANANSE, ANANSE NO, KAE SƐ ƆREKƆ KWASEA BI HO HWEHWƐ MU, BERE A NE KORO YƐƐ KWASEA

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse no, na ɔte hɔ pɛn, na ɔkaa sɛ ɔpɛ ɔbenfoɔ bi a ɔne no bɛkɔ akyekyere mpataa afiri. Ɔkyinkyin baabiara, nanso wannya bi. Afei ɔkɔhyiaa Osansa, Akrɔma no. Ɔkaa sɛ, Yɛnkɔyɛ fish-traps. Akrɔma no buaa sɛ, Mewɔ (plenty) nam a mɛdi. Ɛno akyi no, ɔne Anene, Kwaakwaadabi no hyiaa, na ɔkaa sɛ, Yɛnkɔyɛ fish-traps. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, Metee. Afei, Akrɔma no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaakwaadabi no sɛ, Saa fish-trap-setting expedition a wo ne Kwaku Ananse rekɔ no, nkɔ, Kwaku Ananse ka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ kwasea, a ɔne kwasea no de fish-trap sii hɔ no, s, ɔbɛnya sika no nyinaa. Kwaakwaadabi no buaa sɛ, Ɛho nhia sɛ woka asɛm foforo, ɛfiri sɛ me na mehwɛ sɛ menya sika no nyinaa. Kwaakwaadabi ne Ananse no tuu kwan kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu sɛ wɔrekɔtwa akutu. Kwaakwaadabi no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Anans sɛ, Fa sekan no ma me na menkɔ twa, na wo adwuma ne sɛ wobɛfa brɛoo (a efi madwuma mu ba). Anans no buae sɛ, So wofa me sɛ meyɛ ɔkwasea? Ma menyɛ twitwa no, bere a wofa brɛoo no. Wɔtwaa Kwaku Anans bere a Anene, Kwaakwaadabi no, te hɔ teɛteɛm. Bere a otwaa (wɔn) wiei no, wɔkyekyeree wɔn (wɔ bamboo mu). Kwaakwaadabi no kae sɛ, Ananse, ma menkɔfa wɔn na memfa wɔn, bere a wubegye wɔn adesoa no. Ananse no buae sɛ, Firi mu, wubu me sɛ meyɛ ɔkwasea anaa? Na ɔfaa wɔn de guu ne ti so. Kwaakwaadabi no dii nakyi na na ɔresu. Bere a woduu fie no, Kwaakwaadabi kae sɛ, "Ma menyɛ mpataa afiri na fa me nnwuma no brɛoo. " Kwaku Anans buae sɛ, "Dabi da; ma menyɛ na fa brɛoo no. " Ɛnna Kwaakwaadabi no kɔtenaa nakyi, na ɔresu. Kwaku Anans kae sɛ, "Ɔkwasea, woka sɛ wowɔ adwene, nanso worewuwu. " Anans wiee mpataa afiri no. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Ma memfa mpataa afiri no nkɔ nsuo no mu, na fa emu adesoa no. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Dabi, me ne wɔn bɛkɔ, na fa emu adesoa no. " Wɔduruu nsuo no mu no, Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Papa Ananse, aboa kɛseɛ bi te nsuo no mu, enti ma minnyina nsuo no mu nsi mpataa afiri no, sɛ aboa kɛseɛ no ka me a, wobɛfa ne wuo. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ah, wofa me sɛ meyɛ ɔkwasea paa; me ara na mɛsi afiri no, na sɛ ɛkeka me a, wobɛfa ne wu no. " Kwaku Ananse faa afiri no kɔtoo nsuo no mu, na wɔsan baa fie. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi, wɔkɔhwɛɛ afiri no mu, na wɔanya mpataa mmienu. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Ananse, fa yi mmienu yi, na sɛ ɔkyena ba a, na (mishini no) akyere nnan, me nso mɛfa wɔn. " Ananse no buaa sɛ, "Ɔyɔnko yi! Wode me yɛ ɔkwasea paa; fa yi mmienu yi, na ɔkyena, me nso mɛfa nnan no. " Kwaakwaadabi no de wɔn kɔe. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, wɔkɔɔ (san kɔsraa mishini no), na wɔyii nnan (mpataa). Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Fa yi nnan, na sɛ ɔkyena yɛbɛba abɛkyere awotwe a, me nso mɛfa. " Ananse no buae sɛ, "Wobɛfa me sɛ ɔkwasea; wowɔ yi nnan, na ɔkyena me nso, mɛfa awotwe no. " Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, wɔkɔɔ (bɛhwɛɛ afiri no) na wɔfirii awotwe no. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Fa wɔn, na ɔkyena me nso mɛfa dunsia no. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Wobɛfa me sɛ ɔkwasea; fa awotwe no, na ɔkyena menya dunsia no. " Mprempren mpataa afiri no abere, na Kwaakwaadabi no kasa kyerɛɛ Ananse no sɛ, Kɔfa mpataa afiri a aporɔw yi, na sɛ obi tɔn a, ɔbɛnya ntanu (i.e. (£16) for each single (trap), so do you, Father Ananse, take these fish, and take the rotten fish-traps and give them to me. The Ananse said, No, do you take the fish and go and sell them, and I will take the rotten fish-traps and take (the profit). They carried (their goods) off to a market. Immediately the Kwaakwaadabi had disposed of all his goods; he got much money futur. Nanso Ananse nneɛma no da so ara wɔ hɔ. Ɛnna Kwaakwaadabi no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Fa mpataa-atadeɛ a ɛporɔw yi na wotwa ho hyia na wɔbɛtɔ. Ananse no maa wɔn so, ɔkae sɛ, Merekɔntɔn mpataa-atadeɛ a ɛporɔw! Afei akuraa no mu panyin no tee (no), na ɔkae sɛ, Ɛhe na ɔyɔnko yi firi? Kɔfrɛ no sɛ ɔmmra. Ananse no kɔɔ. Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, Wofa no sɛ yɛn nkuraasefoɔ yi yɛ nkwasea, afei wo yɔnko (wɔ ha) bɛtɔn mpataa (with profit), woanhu no, nanso wode mpataa-atɛkyɛ a ɛporɔw bɛhyɛ yɛn? Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, Flog no. Enti wɔbɔɔ no. Ɛnna Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, Spider, woahu, wani awu wɔ animguaseɛ ho; wokaeɛ sɛ worehwehwɛ ɔkwasea na ɔne wo ayɛ adwuma, na woahu sɛ wo ankasa na woyɛ ɔkwasea no.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
