SƐ OBI YƐ WƆN PAPA A, KYERƐ WASEDA SƐ WOBƐYƐ NO PAPA WƆ SANBA

WƆKYERƐ SƐ ƐKAN BI NA ƆKRANE BI WƆ HƆ, NA WƆ NAKWANKYERƐ MU NO, ƆKƆƆ AKƆHYIA ƆPANYIN BI A NE SANKU WƆ AKURAN. Na ƆKRANE no kaa sɛ, "Grace me! Eyi yɛ adesuakuw mu kuruwa a ɛwɔ ne ho; a ɛte sɛɛ no, ɛmfa ho sɛ wobɔ mmɔden dɛn no, wubetumi anantew anaa? Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, "Oh, kakra koraa. " Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, "Mo nkurɔfoɔ, ɛnnɛ yi, sɛ mɛyɛ biribi pa ama mo (today), ɔkyena mobɛfa biribi bɔne de aseda me. " Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, "Oh, anka mentumi nyɛ (that). " Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, "Sɛ wuntumi nyɛ saa a, mate. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Too wani na bue. " Na ɔbaa panyin no too nani na obuee mu. Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ wo kuruwa no. " Na berɛ a ɔbaa panyin no bɛhwɛɛ no - ɔnkae biara. Afei ɔtoo nani bio; ɔbuee mu, na ɔhunuu sɛ asase no nyinaa ayɛ fann. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Too mu bio. " Ɔtoo mu, na obuee mu, na adan no gyinaa hɔ pintinn. Na ɔma ɔtoo mu bio. Ɔbuee mu sɛ ɔbɛhunu kuro kɛseɛ bi a ɛte sɛɛ; ɛhɔ na ɛyɛ huge. Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, Old woman, its yours (lit. wɔ wo nsa mu). " Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Meda wo ase. " Afei ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mefa de da wo ase? " Ɔkɔndɔm no kaa sɛ, "Mempɛ asɛm ketewa biara mpo, me deɛ, me pɛ ara ne saa silk-cotton-tree a ɛda hɔ yi. " Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Saa adeɛ yi (wo bisa) deɛ, fa. " Ɛneɛ Ɔkɔndɔm no tuu kwan, na ɔtenaa so, na ɔpam akraman, na ɔtoo nkesua mmienu. Ɔde wɔn guu mu. Na ɔyii nkesua mmienu no, na ɔkɔhwehwɛɛ biribi a ne mma bɛdidi. Ɔpanyin no banana a ɔne no te no, ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ teɛteɛɛm sɛ, "Ehe! Ehe! " Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na ɛrekɔ so? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma menwe Eagle mma. " Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Ɛhe na mebɛnya Eagle mma? " Nana kumaa no hyɛɛ aseɛ bio sɛ, quique! quique! Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na ɛrekɔ so? " Abofra no kae sɛ, "Ma menwe Eagle mma, ɛfiri sɛ sɛ minnya bi nni a, mewu. " Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, "Ah! ɛsɛ sɛ eyi, me nana wu esiane Eagle mma a ɔhia sɛ ɔwe nti? Sɔre, fa hammer na kɔbɔ silk-cotton dua no na fa Eagle mma no brɛ me. " Nkuraasefoɔ no kɔɔ hɔ, (hammer no bɔeɛ), Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! Ɛkame ayɛ sɛ dua no rekɔ fam, berɛ a Eagle mma no panyin no tui, na ɔba bɛgyinaa akutu no ano, na ɔtee nne, ɔfrɛɛ ɛna no sɛ:

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, anomaa no, Eagle no ba!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔdidi a, san bra!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, o! o!

Ɛna no tee sɛ ne ba no resu; ɔsɔre, (na ne ntaban a ɛrebɔ no nnyigyei ne,) fa! Ɔbaa, ɔkae sɛ, "Sanguri," na saa dua no (a na ɛkame ayɛ sɛ wɔatwitwa no) san bɛka ho bio, na nnipa a na wɔbɔ no no nyinaa, wɔwe no. Afei ɔfaa aduane a ɔde aba no maa ne mma no. Afei ɔpaa wɔn kyɛw sɛ, Me rekɔ, sɛ ɔbaa panyin no ba abɛfa mo a, momma ɔmfa mo nkɔ. Na ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, Kɔ bɔ dua no na fa mmoa no brɛ me nana ma ɔnwe. Na wɔkɔɔ hɔ ne mprenu so, Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! Ɛkame ayɛ sɛ dua no rebɛkɔ fam, berɛ a (Eagle) ba no firi baeɛ na ɔbɛgyinaa akraman no ano, na ɔfrɛɛ ne maame sɛ:

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, anomaa no, Eagle no ba!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔdidi a, san bra!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, o! o!

Ɔfrɛɛ ne maame, na ɔfrɛɛ, na ɔfrɛɛ, na ɔfrɛɛ mmuaeɛ biara nni hɔ, na afei dua no kasae sɛ ɛbɛbɔ fam, Anoano! (ɛkaa). Wɔde Eagle no mma no kɔeɛ, wɔde baako maa ɔbaa panyin no, nanso baako a aka no tu faa Wawa dua no so. Baako no, wɔayam no, na wɔde maa ɔbaa panyin no mmananaba, a ɔde ka apɔw-mu-teɛteɛ no ho (ɔredi). Ɛnkyɛreeɛ, na Call no baeɛ. Berɛ a ɔduruu dua a wɔatwa no, ɔhunuu ne mma no mu baako a ɔte hɔ, ɔbisaa no nea ɛkɔɔ so, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no nsɛm no nyinaa. Call no kɔɔ ɔbaa panyin no akuraa ase. Berɛ a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, ɔbaa panyin no babarima na na ɔredidi ne mma no mu baako. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Old woman, I congratulate you. " Ɔfiri ɔbaa panyin no fie baeɛ na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ wɔ kuro no ano. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sanguri," na nnipa no nyinaa yeraeɛ, na bio ɔkaa sɛ, "Sanguri," na fie biara bubuu prɛko pɛ na atenaeɛ biara nni hɔ bio. "Sanguri," akuraa no san bɛyɛɛ kwaeɛ. "Sanguri," na ɔbaa panyin no ho akuru no san bae. Na ɔkaa sɛ, "Old woman, woahunu, ɛno nti na mpanimfoɔ ka sɛ, sɛ obi yɛ wo yiye a, fa yiye a woyɛ no da no ase na mfa bɔne mma no aseda. " Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi bɛtumi ayɛ wo nokware, na ebi nso bɛtumi ayɛ wo nkamfo (wɔ ne ka no ho).
