SƐ OBI YƐ WO PAPA A, KYERƐ WASEDA DENAM NO PAPA A WOBƐYƐ NO WƆ SAN BA

WƆKA SƐ na Ɔkɔdeɛ bi wɔ hɔ, na wɔ nakyinkyinakyinkyin no, ɔkɔhyiaa aberewa bi a na ɔwɔ kuru wɔ ne nan so. Na Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Grace me! Eyi yɛ kuru a ɛwɔ klaase bi mu; kuru a ɛte sɛɛ no, ɛmfa ho mmɔden a wobɔ no, wutumi nantew anaa? " Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Aane, kakra paa. " Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Mo nkurɔfoɔ, ɛnnɛ yi, sɛ mɛyɛ mo yie bi (to-day), anka ɔkyena mobɛfa bɔne bi aseda me. " Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Aane, anka merentumi nyɛ saa. " Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ moantumi nni saa a, mate. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Dum wo aniwa na bue ano. " Na aberewa no too naniwa mu buee ano. Ɔkɔdeɛ no kae sɛ, "Hwɛ wo kuru no. " Na bere a aberewa no bae sɛ ɔrebɛhwɛ no, na ɛnyɛ nkaedum pɛ. Afei ɔtoo naniwa mu bio, ɔbuee ano, na ɔhunuu sɛ asase no nyinaa ho atew. Ɔkae sɛ, "Dum wɔn bio. " Ɔtoo naniwa mu, na obuee ano, na afie no gyinaa hɔ pintinn. Na ɔmaa no bɛtoo mu bio. Ɔbuee mu sɛ ɔrehu kuro a ɛyɛ kɛseɛ yi, ɛhɔ na na ɛyɛ huge. Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Old woman, its yours (lit. wɔ wo nsa)." Aberewa no kae sɛ, "Medaase na mede ma wo. " Afei aberewa no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mefa so da wo ase? " Ɔkɔdeɛ no kae sɛ, "Mempɛ ade ketewa a ɛho nhia mpo, me deɛ, nea mepɛ ara ne saa silk-cotton-tree a ɛda hɔ no. " Aberewa no kae sɛ, "Saa ade yi (wobisa) nea ɛwɔ mu biara, fa. " Afei Ɔkɔdeɛ no tuu kwan, na ɔhyerɛn guu so, na ɔhunuu berebuo, na ɔde nkosua mmienu guu so. na ɔde wɔn hyɛɛ mu. Na ɔyii nkosua mmienu no, na ɔkɔhwehwɛɛ deɛ ɔbɛma ne mmofra adi. Aberewa no babarima ba a ɔne no te no, ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ yɛɛ kwiikwii sɛ, "Ehe! Ehe! " Na aberewa no kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ asɛm bɛn? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Ma menwe aberewa mmofra. " Na aberewa no kae sɛ, "Ɛhe na menya Eagle mma? " Na nana ketewa no hyɛɛ aseɛ bio sɛ, kwiikwii! kwiikwii! Na aberewa no kae sɛ, "Ɛdɛn ne asɛm no? " Abofra no kae sɛ, "Ma me nwiinwii Eagle mma, efisɛ sɛ mannya bi a mɛwe a, me bɛwuwu. " Na aberewa no kae sɛ, "Ah! ɛsɛ sɛ eyi, me nana wu wɔ Eagle mma ahiasɛm nti? Sɔre, fa nkuma na kɔbɔ silk-cotton-tree no na fa Eagle mma no brɛ me. " Na akuraasefoɔ no kɔɔ hɔ, (wɔtoo nkuma), Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! Ɛyɛɛ sɛ dua no reba fam, berɛ a ɔkɔbereɛ no mma no mu ɔpanin tuu mpire, ba bɛgyinaa berebuo no ano, maa ne su so, ɔfrɛɛ maame no sɛ:

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, anomaa, ɔkɔre ba!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔɔ adidi a, san bra!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, oh! oh!

Na maame no tee sɛ ne abofra no resu; ɔsɔre, (na ne ntaban a ɛrebɔ no nnyigyei ne,) fa! Ɔbae, ɔkae sɛ, "Sanguri," na saa dua no (a na ɛkame ayɛ sɛ wɔatwitwa no) san bae bio, na nnipa a na wɔreka no nyinaa, wɔwewee. Afei ɔgyee aduane a ɔde bae no, na ɔde maa ne mmofra no. Afei ɔmaa wɔn akwaaba sɛ, "Me deɛ, merekɔ, sɛ aberewa no ba abɛfa wo a, ma ɔmfa wo nkɔ. " Na aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Kɔbɔ dua no na fa abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no ba ma me nana no bɛwe. " Na wɔkɔɔ hɔ ne mprenu so, Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! Ɛyɛɛ sɛ dua no rekɔ fam, berɛ a ɔkɔbere ba no pue baeɛ bɛgyinaa berebuo no ano, na ɔfrɛɛ ne maame sɛ:

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, anomaa, ɔkɔre ba!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔɔ adidi a, san bra!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, oh! oh!

Ɔfrɛɛ ne maame, na ɔfrɛɛ, na ɔfrɛɛ, na ɔfrɛɛ, na mmuaeɛ biara nni hɔ, na afei deɛ dua no bɔɔ ne fam, na ɛkaa sɛ, "Ma sɛe! " (ɛkaa sɛ). Wɔfaa ɔkɔre no mma no, baako kɔmaa aberewa no, nanso baako a aka no tu kɔɔ Wawa dua so. Baako no, wɔnoaa no, na wɔde maa aberewa no babarima ba no, na ɔde kaa bɔdeɛ a wɔato no ho (na ɔredidi). Ɛnkyɛree na Ɔkɔdeɛ no baeɛ. Bere a ɔduruu dua a wɔatwa no ho no, ɔhunuu ne mmofra no mu baako a ɔte hɔ, ɔbisaa no sɛ ɛdeɛn na ɛkɔɔ so, na ɔno nso ka kyerɛɛ no amaneɛbɔ no nyinaa. Ɔkɔdeɛ no hyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ aberewa no kuro no mu. Bere a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, na aberewa no babarima renoa ne mmofra no mu baako. Ɔkae sɛ, "Old woman, I congratulate you. " Ɔfirii adi firi aberewa no fie na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ kuro no kuro akyi. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sanguri," na onipa biara yeraa, na bio ɔkaa sɛ, "Sanguri," na fie no nyinaa mu paee prɛko pɛ na tenabea biara nni hɔ. "Sanguri," akuraa no san bɛyɛɛ kwaeɛ. "Sanguri," na aberewa no ho tɔɔ no bio. Na ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Old woman, woahunu, ɛno nti na mpanimfoɔ kyerɛ sɛ, sɛ obi yɛ wo papa a, sɔ no papa na mma no nni bɔne sɛ wobɛda no ase. " Eyi, me abasɛm a mekaeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi mo betumi agye sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a mo betumi akamfo me (asɛmka no ho).
