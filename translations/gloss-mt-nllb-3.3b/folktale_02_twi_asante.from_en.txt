MPITRIPI NE MPATRAPA

MPITRIPI ne Mpatrapa ne Anyankama-Kyerebi ne Kyerebi-Koso-Adawurampon kɔtuee palm-wine nnua aduasa, na wɔkae sɛ, "Obiara mfa ne nsa nka palm-wine no ahurututu ne nahoɔfɛ (ne nsateaa)."

Afei Agya Ananse kɔɔ ne nsa ka palm-wine no ahurututu na ɔfee (ne nsateaa), na nnipa (lit. Wɔ) kae sɛ, "Fa ɛka no. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɛka bɛn na manya? " Wɔkae sɛ, "Wanya ɛka no efisɛ, Mpitripi ne Mpatrapa ne Anyankama-Kyerebi ne Kyerebi-Koso-Adawurampon akɔtu palm-wine nnua aduasa, na wɔaka sɛ obiara mfa ne nsa nka palm-wine no ahurututu ne ahoɔfɛ (ne nsateaa), na wode wo nsa aka palm-wine no ahurututu na woama (wo nsateaa). ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, fa ka no (i.e. ɛsɛ sɛ wotua asotwe no); na wobɔɔ Ananse no akuturuku sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obenya (akatua no), enti ɔhuruw kɔhyɛɛ dan atifi hɔ, (wɔ nnyigyei bi te sɛ) Kado!

Ɛno nti na wɔka sɛ ɛnkane yi, obi bɛhunu Kwaku Ananse plastered against the ceiling, ɛwom sɛ tete no na ɔnam asase so te sɛ Ɔkɔtɔ, Ɔkɔtɔ no.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
