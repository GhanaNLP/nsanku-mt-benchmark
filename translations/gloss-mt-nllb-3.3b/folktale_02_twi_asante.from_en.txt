MPITRIPI NE MPATRAPA

MPITRIPI NE Mpatrapa ne Anyankama-Kyerebi ne Kyerebi-Koso-Adawurampon kɔɔ hɔ kɔtutuu nnuadewa nnua aduasa, na wɔkae sɛ, "Obiara nnka nnuadewa no ahurututu ne fɛfɛeɛ (ne nsateaa ahodoɔ) ho. "

Afei Agya Ananse kɔɔ so kɔkaa ahurututu a ɛwɔ nnuadewa no mu na ɔfefee ne nsateaa ahodoɔ, na nnipa (lit. wɔn) kae sɛ, "Momfa ɛka no. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Me ɛka bɛn na me bɔ? " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Wɔbɔ wo ɛka, ɛfiri sɛ, Mpitripi ne Mpatrapa ne Anyankama-Kyerebi ne Kyerebi-Koso-Adawurampon akɔtutu nnuadewa nnua aduasa, na wɔaka sɛ obiara nnka nnuadewa no ahurututu ne fɛfɛeɛ (ne nsateaa ahodoɔ) no, na woaka nnuadewa no ahurututu na woasu (wo nsateaa ahodoɔ) no, ɛno nti, ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, fa ɛka no! (ɛne sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ wotua asotwe no). Ananse no nso, wɔbɔɔ no ahyɛdeɛ sɛ ɔmfa akatua no mma no, ɔpuee kɔɔ silin no so, (a na ɔwɔ dede a ɛte sɛ) Kado!

Ɛno nti na wɔka sɛ daa, obi bɛhu Kwaku Ananse a wɔde dɔteɛ abɔ no silin, ɛwom sɛ tete no na ɔnam fam te sɛ Ɔkɔtɔ, Kɔtɔ no.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
