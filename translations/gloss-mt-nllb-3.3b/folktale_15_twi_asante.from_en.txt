Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐKƆƆ SO SƐ NNIPA BI YƐ FƐ, NA FOFORƆ NSO YƐ NEA ƐNYƐ FƐ

WƆKYERƐ SƐ, na ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ dua bi a ɛgyina hɔ wɔ nsuo mu sɛ ɔbɛbisa no awoɔ ho asɛm. Na berɛ a ɔkɔɔ no, ɛmo dua no kaa sɛ, Mɛma wo deɛ wopɛ, nanso abofra a mede no bɛma wo no, sɛ ɔsɔre a, ɔrenyɛ adwuma biara da. Ɔkaa sɛ, Mepene so. Ɛnyɛ nna mmienu, ɛnyɛ nna mmiɛnsa, na ɔnyinsɛnee na awoɔ. Te sɛ mmofra a wɔwɔ Spider-stories no, na ɔnnkyɛree nnyiniiɛ, wanyin prɛko pɛ. Na ne maame ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Between all the tribes taboos, there are none which you need to observe save the Palm-tree, you must never climb one. He said, Maame, I have heard. Now, one day the maame left him there to go to the afuo. Ɔkɔfrɛɛ m'adɔfo no, na ɔne no hyɛɛ aseɛ dii agorɔ, na ɔkyeree ne dwam-nsotwa no nsɔmu bi na ɛbueeɛ hwee ase. Na abaayewa no kaa sɛ, Waist-beads yi hama a etwa to no, kɔfa palm-tree fiber bi brɛ me ma menhyɛ no den. abarimaa no kɔtɔɔ bi de bae. Abaayewa no kaa sɛ, Mempɛ saa, gye nea woahwehwɛ no wɔ palm-tree bi a ɛwɔ nsuo mu no. Enti abofra no fii aseɛ hwehwɛɛ bi. Bere a ɔkɔɔ no, na palm-tree bi gyina nsuo no mu. Ɔforoo soro, ɔkra! ɔkra! ɔkra! Kɔsii sɛ ɔduruu dua no kɔn mu. Ɔyii ne sekkan no, na berɛ a ɔde ne nsa kaa no ara pɛ, na dua no mu paee mmienu, na abarimaa no yam kɔɔ mu, na dua no mu paee bio. Na ɔwɔ hɔ a, ɛrekyere no. Ɛnkyɛree na ne maame no baeɛ; ɔduruu nsuo no ho no, ɔhunuu shadow bi wɔ so, na ɔkaa sɛ:

Hwan shadow na ɛda nsuo so saa te sɛ me abofra Akwasi Kwasaman?

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnanom, ɛyɛ me. "

Ɔkae sɛ, Dɛn nti na aburow dua no rekyere wo?

Ɔkae sɛ, Palm fiber na meyii no sɛ mede bɛma me dɔfo na Palm tree no hyɛɛ me.

Na maame no kaa sɛ, "Wo, saa nnuadewa yi, twitwiw no, twitwiw no, twitwiw no, me ba Kwasaman.

Palm dua twitwiw no, twitwiw no, twitwiw no, palm dua twitwiw no.

Na maame no kɔe. Ɛnna agya no nso bae, na ɔkae sɛ:

Hwan shadow na ɛda nsuo yi so te sɛ me abofra kwasaman no?

Ɔkae sɛ, "Agya, ɛyɛ me. "

Ɔkae sɛ (agya no): "Dɛn nti na aburow dua no rekyere wo?

Ɔkae sɛ: "Palm fiber na meyii mu sɛ mede bɛma me dɔfo, na Palm tree no wee me."

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo, saa nnuadewa yi, twitwiw no, twitwiw no, me ba Kwasaman.

Palm dua twitwiw no, twitwiw no, palm dua twitwiw no. "

Na agya no kɔɔ fie kɔka kyerɛɛ Etire a ɔwɔ akuraa hɔ, na Etire a ɔwɔ akuraa hɔ nso frɛɛ nnipa no nyinaa, na wɔbaa nnuadewa no nan ho, na Etire a ɔwɔ akuraa hɔ no maa ne nne so, na ɔkae sɛ:

"Hwan shadow na ɛda nsuo so saa sɛ me nana Akwasi Kwasaman? "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Agyapa, ɛyɛ me. "

Ɔkae sɛ (akuraa no mu Etire) se, "Dɛn nti na akuraa no rekyere wo? "

Ɔkae sɛ: "Palm fiber na meyii mu sɛ mede bɛma me dɔfo, na Palm tree no wee me."

Etire a ɔwɔ akuraa hɔ no kae sɛ, "Wo, saa nnuadewa yi, twitwiw no, twitwiw no, twitwiw no, me nana Kwasaman.

Palm dua twitwiw no, twitwiw no, palm dua twitwiw no. "

Na Etire akuraa no kɔɔ so, na nkuraasefoɔ nyinaa too dwom koro kyerɛɛ Palm-tree sɛ ɔnkyere no. Na afei ne dɔfo no nso baeɛ, na ɔkae sɛ: "Hwan shadow na ɛda nsuo no so, te sɛ me dɔfo Akwasi Kwasaman de no? "

Ɔkae sɛ: "Ɛyɛ me, wo ɔdɔfo ankasa. "

Ɔkae sɛ: "Dɛn nti na aburow dua no rekyere wo?

Ɔkae sɛ, "Mekɔɔ hɔ sɛ merebɛbu Palm fiber ama wo, na palm dua no twetwe me."

Ɔkae sɛ, "Palm-tree gyae no, gyae no, gyae no.

Palm-tree gyae me dɔfo Akwasi Kwasaman.

Palm dua no gyae no, gyae no, gyae no, Palm dua no gyae no. "

Ɔkaa saa no, nnuadewa no bueeɛ kakra, na nadɔfo no yam yii kakra. Na ɔmaa ne nne so bio sɛ:

"Hwan shadow na ɛda nsuo so saa, te sɛ me dɔfo Akwasi Kwasaman? "

Ɔkae sɛ: "Ɛyɛ me, wo ɔdɔfo ankasa. "

Ɔkae sɛ: "Dɛn nti na aburow dua no rekyere wo?

Ɔkae sɛ, "Mekɔɔ hɔ sɛ merebɛbu Palm fiber ama wo, na palm dua no twetwe me."

Ɔkae sɛ, "Palm-tree gyae no, gyae no, gyae no.

Palm-tree gyae me dɔfo Akwasi Kwasaman.

Palm dua no gyae no, gyae no, gyae no, Palm dua no gyae no. "

Na afei ne dɔfo no puee prɛko pɛ, na ɔba bɛkyeree m'adɔfo no bosom, na wɔwosow, na wɔsesaa bɛyɛɛ ngo. Na nnipa a wɔkɔɔ hɔ ntɛm-ntɛm bɛperee bi guu wɔn anim no, ɛyɛ wɔn a wɔn ho yɛ fɛ paa; nanso wɔn a wɔnnyaa bi no, wɔyɛ wɔn a wɔn ho nyɛ fɛ. Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
