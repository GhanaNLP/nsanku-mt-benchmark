Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ NNIPA BI YƐ ANIGYE, NA AFOFORO NNI ANIGYE

WƆKYERƐ SƐ NA ƆBEA BI WƆ HƆ, NA ƆKƆƆ AKUTU BI A ƐGYINA HƆ WƆ NSUO NO MU SƐ ƆREBƐbisa no awoɔ ho asɛm. Na ɔkɔeɛ no, palm dua no kaa sɛ, Mɛma wo deɛ wopɛ, nanso abofra a me ne no bɛma wo no, sɛ ɔsɔre a, ɔrenyɛ adwuma biara da. Ɔkaa sɛ, Me pene so (to that). Ɛnyɛ nna mmienu, ɛnyɛ nna mmiɛnsa, na ɔnyinsɛn na ɔwoo. Te sɛ mmofra a wɔwɔ Spider-stories no, ankyɛ na wanyin, wanyin prɛko pɛ. Na ne maame ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Wɔ mmusuakuo no mu mmarahyɛ no nyinaa mu no, emu biara nni hɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ wodi so gye mmɛdua dua no, ɛnsɛ sɛ woforo baako da. Ɔkae sɛ, Maame, mate. Afei, da bi maame no gyaw no hɔ sɛ ɔrekɔ afuw mu. Ɔkɔfrɛɛ nadɔfo, na ɔne wɔn fii ase dii agoru, na ɔkyeree nasen mu nhwiren no fã bi na ɛpaee na ɛhwee ase. Na ababaawa no kaa sɛ, Saa abɔsoɔ a woabobɔ yi, kɔfa nnuaba nhama bi brɛ me ma memfa nsi so. Abarimaa no kɔtɔɔ bi de bae. Ababaawa no kaa sɛ, Mempɛ saa, gye sɛ wohwehwɛ bi firi dua bi a ɛwɔ nsuo no mu. Enti abofra no fii aseɛ hwehwɛɛ bi. Bere a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, na dua bi a ɛwɔ nsuo no mu. Ɔforo kɔɔ soro, ɔkra! ɔkra! ɔkra! kosii sɛ ɔduruu dua no kɔn mu. Ɔyii ne sekan no, na berɛ a ɔde ne nsa kaa no, ɛdua no mu paee mmienu, na abofra no yafunu kɔɔ mu, na ɛdua no san too mu bio. Ɛhɔ na na ɛrekyere no. Ɛnkyɛree na ne maame no baeɛ, ɔduruu nsuo no ho no, ɔhunuu sunsuma bi wɔ so, na ɔkaa sɛ:

Hwan sunsuma na ɛda nsuo no so te sɛ me ba Akwasi Kwasaman deɛ?

Ɔkaa sɛ, Ɔma, ɛyɛ me.

Ɔkae sɛ, Dɛn nti na nnuadewa dua no rehinhim wo?

Ɔkae sɛ, Mekɔɔ sɛ merekyi Beta ama me dɔfo na palm dua no bɛkyekyeree me.

Ɛna no kaa sɛ, Wo, saa Palm dua yi squeeze no, squeeze no, squeeze no, me ba Kwasaman.

Palm twitwiw no, twitwiw no, twitwiw no, palm twitwiw no.

Na maame no kɔeɛ. Na agya no nso baeɛ, na ɔkaa sɛ:

Hwan sunsuma na ɛda nsuo a ɛte sɛ me ba kwasaman yi so?

Ɔkae sɛ, Papa, ɛyɛ me.

Ɔkae sɛ: "Dɛn nti na nnuadewa dua no rehinhim wo? "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Mekɔɔ sɛ merekyi Beta ama me dɔfo na palm dua no kyekyeree me. "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Wo, saa palm dua yi squeeze no, squeeze no, Me ba Kwasaman.

Palm squeeze no, squeeze no, palm squeeze no. "

Na agya no kɔɔ fie kɔka kyerɛɛ akuraa no panyin, na akuraa no panyin nso frɛɛ nnipa no nyinaa; na wɔbaa akutu dua no ase, na akuraa no panyin no maa ne nne so kae sɛ:

"Hwan sunsuma na ɛda nsuo no so te sɛ me nana Akwasi Kwasaman deɛ no? "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Agyapa, ɛyɛ me. "

Ɔkae sɛ: "Dɛn nti na nnuadewa dua no rehinhim wo? "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Mekɔɔ sɛ merekyi Beta ama me dɔfo na palm dua no kyekyeree me. "

Ɔpanyin a ɔwɔ akuraa ase no kaa sɛ, "Wo, saa nnuadewa yi, mia no, mia no, mia no, me babarima kumaa Kwasaman.

Palm squeeze no, squeeze no, palm squeeze no. "

Na akuraa no mu panyin no kɔeɛ, na akuraa no mufoɔ nyinaa too dwom korɔ no ara kyerɛɛ Palm dua no sɛ ɔnkyere no. Na afei ne dɔfoɔ no baeɛ, na ɔkaa sɛ: "Hwan sunsuma na ɛda nsuo no so, te sɛ me dɔfoɔ Akwasi Kwasaman deɛ no? "

Ɔkae sɛ: "Ɛyɛ me, wo dɔfo ankasa. "

Ɔkae sɛ: "Dɛn nti na mmɛw dua no kyekyere wo?"

Ɔkae sɛ, "Mekɔɔ hɔ sɛ merekyekye Beth ama wo, na mmɛw dua no kyekyeree me. "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Palm-tree gyae no, gyae no, gyae no.

Palm-tree gyae me dɔfo, Akwasi Kwasaman.

Palm-tree gyae no, gyae no, gyae no, Palm-tree gyae no. "

Ɔkasaeɛ no, na nnuadewa dua no abue kakra, na nadɔfoɔ no yafunu afiri mu kakra. Na ɔsan de ne nne so bio sɛ:

"Hwan sunsuma na ɛda nsuo no so, te sɛ me dɔfo Akwasi Kwasaman deɛ no? "

Ɔkae sɛ: "Ɛyɛ me, wo dɔfo ankasa. "

Ɔkae sɛ: "Dɛn nti na mmɛw dua no kyekyere wo?"

Ɔkae sɛ, "Mekɔɔ hɔ sɛ merekyekye Beth ama wo, na mmɛw dua no kyekyeree me. "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Palm-tree gyae no, gyae no, gyae no.

Palm-tree gyae me dɔfo, Akwasi Kwasaman.

Palm-tree gyae no, gyae no, gyae no, Palm-tree gyae no. "

Na afei nadɔfoɔ no puei prɛko pɛ, na ɔba bɛkuraa nadɔfoɔ no moma so, na wɔn ho dwiriw wɔn; na wɔsesaeɛ bɛyɛɛ ngo. Na nnipa a wɔkɔɔ hɔ ntɛm ara na wɔde bi guu wɔn anim no, wɔn na wɔn ho yɛ fɛ paa; nanso wɔn a wɔannya bi no, wɔn na wɔn ho nyɛ fɛ. Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
