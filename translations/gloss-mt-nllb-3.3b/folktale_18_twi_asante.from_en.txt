Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐDEƐ ABAKƆSƐM BAA ABUSUAKUO MU

WƆKA SƐ ɔbɔfoɔ bi kɔɔ nwura so, ɔbaeɛ na ɔhwɛɛ amena bi mu, na emu na na Onipa, Ɔbarima, Osebo, Daboɔ, Ɔwɔ, Ɔwɔ, ne Okusie, Okisi, gyina. Afei berɛ a saa nnipa yi hunuu Ɔbɔfoɔ no, wei kaa sɛ, Agya Ɔbɔfoɔ, bra na yi me firi mu. Ɔno kaa sɛ, Agya, bra na yi me firi mu. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Meremma, ɛfiri sɛ ɔkyena mpo, sɛ mɛgye wo, na woanya me a, nea wobɛyɛ me no (me nim) no, ɛnsɛ sɛ me dwene ho. Wei kaa sɛ, Muntumi (act so). Ɛno kaa sɛ, Muntumi (act so). Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, , Sɛ saa na ɛyɛ asɛm no ampa a, meyi wo adi. Na ɛyɛ nokware sɛ ɔde nnipa no nyinaa fii adi kɔɔ akuraa hɔ. Ɛda koro bi, bere a na Ɔbɔfoɔ wɔ hɔ no, Osebo, Daboɔ no, de nam pii bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, Agya Ɔbɔfoɔ, eyi yɛ aseda ma ayamye a woayɛ me, meda wo ase, Apiafo. Ɔde nnuaba nkotokuo dumien a ɛyɛ nam maa Ɔbɔfoɔ no. Ɔbɔfoɔ no daa no ase na Daboɔ no kɔɔ nanim. Wɔ dwumadie foforɔ bi nso mu no, na Ɔbɔfoɔ no wɔ hɔ, na Okusie, Okisi, baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Agya Ɔbɔfoɔ, wopɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma bi? Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Ho! dɛn nti na ɛnyɛ saa, ɛno ne adeɛ a mmarima nyin hwehwɛ. Okisi no sii kwan, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a Nyankopɔn, Ɔsoro-Onyame, de ne sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no sieeɛ. Ɔkɔɔ ɛtokuro bi tutuu wɔ dan no ase, na ɔkɔɔ dan no mu, na ɔkɔfaa ɔsoro nyame no sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no nyinaa de kɔmaa Ɔbɔfoɔ no. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Meda wo ase, Aku. Na Okisi no kɔɔ so. Ɛda foforɔ nso, na ɔwɔ hɔ, na ɔtee sɛ obi rema no akwaaba, Berɛ a atwam. Ɔbɔfoɔ no buaa sɛ, Ame. Ɔbarima no bae, ɔkae sɛ, Agya Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ, anɔpa pa. Ɔno (Ɔbɔfoɔ) buae sɛ, Aane, ɔpanyin. Ɔkae sɛ, Eyi yɛ palm-wine kukuo ketewa bi a mede aseda ama wo. Agyabɔ Ɔmmɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, Mempɛ; na wo, Ɔbarima, ɛte sɛ nea mekaeɛ sɛ meremfa wo nkɔ, hwɛ nea Daboɔ no de brɛɛ me, hwɛ nea Okisi de brɛɛ me. Ɔbarima no yii ne palm-wine no kɔe. na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ Ewiem-onyankopɔn sɛ, Wo sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma a ɛyerae no, saa Okisi no na ɔbaa abɛfa kɔmaa Ɔbɔfoɔ no. Ewiem-onyankopɔn no kaa sɛ, Ɛyɛ saa anaa? Ɔkaa sɛ, Grandsire, ɛyɛ nokorɛ pefee. Ewiem-onyankopɔn no somaa asɛnkafoɔ sɛ wɔnkɔkyere Ɔbɔfoɔ no, na wɔde hama kyekyeree nasene. Wɔbɔɔ no kuruwa kɛseɛ. Afei berɛ a wɔne Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔduruu kwan no, ɔkaa sɛ, Mpanimfoɔ, mepɛ sɛ mekɔhome me ho. Asɛnkafoɔ no kaa sɛ, Dabi, ɔfɔmfoɔ, mobɛdwane. Ɔkaa sɛ, Dabi, merennwane, na sɛ wopɛ a, fa hama no kyekyere me asene, fa dunsin no kyekyere no, na fa me kɔ. Asɛnkafoɔ no tiee no, na wɔmaa no kwan. Ɛhɔ na na ɔredi ne ho fɛfɛɛfɛ, berɛ a aboa a ne tenten na ne ho yɛ fɛ no baeɛ. Ɔkae sɛ, Hwɛ ha, me adamfo, gye aduro yi kɔɔn ketewa yi, na fa ka wo ho, na sɛ wokɔ, na wɔde wo gyina asɛmbli anim, na ɛba sɛ wɔbɛku wo a, mɛkɔ ɔhene harem, sɛ memmɛka ne yere a ɔdɔ no no ba no; sɛ mewe no a, wubehu sɛ obiara asɔre, na wo nso woka, na woka sɛ, Me wɔ aduro, na sɛ wode bedi dwuma wɔ abofra a ɔbɛsan anya no so. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Meda wo ase, Aku. Asɛnkafoɔ no faa Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔgyinaa Ɔsoro-Onyame anim, (ne ne Mpɔtam badwafoɔ anim) Kronti, Akwum, Adonten, Gyaase ne Kɔmyid ahemfo. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa nasɛm, na berɛ a Kasafoɔ no sɔre maa atɛmmuo no, wɔtee awerɛhow dede sɛ, pui pui! wɔ akyirikyiri wɔ ahemfo mpenafo fie. Wɔkae sɛ, Ɛdɛn na ɛyɛ yi? Wɔkae sɛ, ɔwɔ bi akye Ɔsoro Nyame no yere a ɔdɔ no no abofra. Wɔn nyinaa kɔɔ wɔn ho. Na Ɔbɔfoɔ no gyina hɔ kyerɛ sɛ, Sɛ mennyɛ deduani a, anka anka menya aduro (awuduro ano aduro). Ɛhɔ na onipa bi a ɔtee (him) kɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, Monkɔfa deduani no mmra ha. Ɛhɔ na Ɔbɔfoɔ no baeɛ. Ɔsoro Nyame no kae sɛ, Sɛ wutumi hwɛ eyi, me abofra yi, na wamma no anwu a, mede wo etire no bɛma. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, Metee, Kind-one. Na Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ (bɛsan) Nanabarima, nea merebɛkyerɛ wo yi, sɛ wutumi nya bi a, ɛnneɛ mɛtumi ahwɛ wo abofra no. Wɔkae sɛ, Ɛyɛ adeɛ bɛn? Ɔkae sɛ, Tell-tale mogya a wode bɛfra aduru pound no mu. ine. Wɔkaa sɛ, Ɛhe na yɛbɛnya Tell-tale bi mogya? Kasamafoɔ no mu baako a na ɔyɛ ɔnyansani no kaa sɛ, Na yɔnkoɔ a ɔbɛka kyerɛɛ Ewiem-onyankopɔn sɛ Ɔbɔfoɔ no aba abɛwia Ewiem-onyankopɔn no sika no, ɔwɔ he? Wɔkae sɛ, Wɔmfom wo, monfom wo. Wɔkɔɔ kɔfaa yɔnkoɔ no bae. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Bura ne nsa, na fa ne mogya no ma me. Ntɛm ara na wɔtwaa mu. Ɔbɔfoɔ no faa mogya no yɛɛ no aduro, na ɔde guu ɔhene no ba no nan so. Ntɛm ara na ɔsɔree. Wɔkae sɛ, Mommɔ Hunter no anuonyam. Wɔteɛɛm sɛ, Eee! Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, Megye wo etire no na mede rema wo, na Tell-tale no deɛ, munkum no, na muntwa ne honam no, na montow ngu afã ahodoɔ so. Wokum no, na wotwaa ne honam no mu yɛɛ no nketenkete, na wɔtow guu ne nyinaa so. Saa na Tell-tales pii baa abusuakuo no mu.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
