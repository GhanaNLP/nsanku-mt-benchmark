SƐNEA AKONTAABU BAA ABUSUAKUW NO MU

WƆKA SƐ ɔbɔfoɔ bi (bere bi) kɔɔ sare so; ɔbaeɛ na ɔhwɛɛ amena bi mu, na emu na na wogyina hɔ Onipa, Ɔbarima, Ɔsebɔ, Ɔsebɔ, Ɔwɔ, Ɔwɔ, ne Okusie, Okisi. Afei berɛ a saa nkurɔfoɔ yi hunuu Ɔbɔfoɔ no, baako yi kae sɛ, Father Angel, come and take me out. Ɔfoforɔ no kae sɛ, Father Angel, come and take me out. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, I shall not come, for by-tomorrow even, if I were to rescue you, and you were to get at me, what you would do to me (I know) I should not care about. Ɔfoforɔ yi kae sɛ, I couldnt (act so). Ɔfoforɔ no kae sɛ, I couldnt (act so). Ɔbɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, , Sɛ saa na ɛteɛ ampa a, mede wo bɛkɔ abɔnten. Na ɛyɛ nokware sɛ ɔde nnipa no nyinaa kɔɔ abɔnten na ɔkɔɔ nakuraa ase. Ɛda bi, bere a Angel no wɔ hɔ no, Ɔbɔfoɔ, Ɔbɔfoɔ no, de nam dodoɔ bi bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, Father Angel, eyi yɛ aseda afɔrebɔ ma adom a wode ama me, meda wo ase, Apiafo. Ɔde nam a ɛyɛ palm-leaf basket dumien bae maa Angel no. Angel no daa no ase na Ɔsebɔ no kɔɔ nanim. Wɔ kwan foforɔ so no, na Angel no wɔ hɔ, na Okusie, Okisi no, baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Father Angel, would you like some money futuro? Angel no kaa sɛ, Ho! dɛn nti na ɛnyɛ saa, ɛno ne adeɛ a mmarima abere wɔ ho hwehwɛ mu. Okisi no tuu kwan, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a Nyame, Ɔsoro-nyame no, de ne sika futuro no sieeɛ. Ɔkɔtutuo tokuru bi wɔ dan no ase, na ɔkɔɔ dan no mu, na ɔkɔfaa Sky-god sika kɔkɔɔ dɔteɛ no nyinaa, na ɔde kɔmaa ɔbɔfoɔ no. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Me da wo ase, Aku. Na Okisi no kɔɔ. Ɛda foforɔ nso, na ɔwɔ hɔ, na ɔtee sɛ obi reyi no ayɛ, Bere a atwam no. Ɔbɔfoɔ no buaa sɛ, Ame (ba mu). Ɔbarima no bae; ɔkae sɛ, Agya Prayer, anɔpa pa. Ɔ (Angel) buaa sɛ, Yiw, kinsman. Ɔkae sɛ, Eyi yɛ nkotokuo ketewa bi a ɛyɛ palm-wine a mede aseda ama wo. Agya Prayer kae sɛ, Mempɛ; wo deɛ, Ɔbarima, ɛyɛ sɛnea mekae sɛ ɛbɛyɛ, bere a mekae sɛ merennye wo nni no; hwɛ nea Ɔbɔfoɔ no de maa me, hwɛ nea Okisi de maa me. Ɔbarima no de ne palm-wine no kɔe. na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔsoro Nyame no sɛ, Wo sika futuru a ɛyerae no, saa Okisi no ampa aba abɛfa akɔma Ɔbɔfoɔ no. Ɔsoro Nyame no kae sɛ, Ɛyɛ saa anaa? Ɔkae sɛ, Grandsir, ɛyɛ the plain truth. Ɔsoro Nyame no somaa abɔfo sɛ wɔnkɔkyere Ɔbɔfoɔ no, na wɔde nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn kyekyeree nasen. Wɔbɔɔ no kɛse. Afei, berɛ a wɔne Ɔbɔfoɔ no adu kwan no so no, ɔkaa sɛ, Apaninfoɔ, mepɛ sɛ mekɔ ma me ho tɔ me. Abɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Dabi, owifoɔ, wobɛdwane. Ɔkaa sɛ, Dabi, merenguan, na sɛ wopɛ a, fa nhama no, fa kyekyere me asene, fa kyekyere no wɔ adum no so, na ma me nkɔ. Abɔfoɔ no tiee no, na wɔmaa no kɔeɛ. Ɛhɔ na na ɔreyɛ ne ho yie, berɛ a aboa tenten na ne ho yɛ hare no baeɛ. Ɔkae sɛ, Hwɛ ha, madamfo, gye aduro yi, na fa fa wo ho, na bere a woakɔ, na wɔagyina wo wɔ agyinatukuo anim, na ɛba sɛ wɔbɛkum wo no, mɛkɔ ɔhene harem, sɛ anka mɛkeka ne yere a ɔpɛ nasɛm no ba; bere a mɛkeka no, wubehu sɛ obiara asɔre, na wo nso wobɛka, na woaka sɛ, Mewɔ aduro, na sɛ wode bedi dwuma wɔ abofra a ɔbɛsan anya ahoɔden no so. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Me da wo ase, Aku. Abɔfoɔ no faa ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔgyinaa Ɔsoro-nyame no anim, (ne ne Community Council anim) Kronti, Akwum, Adonten, Gyaase ne Kydom mpanimfoɔ. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa nasɛm no, na berɛ a Ɔkasafoɔ no sɔre sɛ ɔrebɛbu atɛn no, wɔtee nnyigyei a ɛyɛ dɛ, pui! pui! wɔ akyirikyiri wɔ ahenfo mpena fie. Wɔkae sɛ, Dɛn na ɛyɛ saa? Wɔkae sɛ, Ɔwɔ bi akye Sky-god yere dɔfo no ba. Wɔn nyinaa kɔɔ wɔn kwan so. Afei na Angel no gyina hɔ; ɔkae sɛ, Sɛ ɛnyɛ me na meyɛ ɔdeduani a, anka ebia na mewɔ aduro (an antidote for the poison). Nipa bi a ɔtee (him) na ɔkɔɔ ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, Monkɔfa ɔdeduani no mmra ha. Ɛneɛ na Angel no baeɛ. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Sɛ wutumi hwɛ eyi so, me ba, na wanwu a, mede wo ti bɛma wo. Angel no kae sɛ, Mate, Kind-one. Na Angel no san kae sɛ, Obabarima, nea merekyerɛ wo yi, sɛ wutumi nya bi a, ɛnde mɛtumi ahwɛ wo ba. Wɔkae sɛ, Ɛyɛ ade bɛn? Ɔkae sɛ, Tell-tale mogya a wɔde bɛfra pounded medic no mu. ine. Wɔkae sɛ, Ɛhe na yɛwɔ sɛ yɛbɛnya Tell-tale mogya? Spokesperson no mu baako a ɔyɛ anyansafoɔ no kae sɛ, Na yɔnkoɔ a ɔbɛkaa kyerɛɛ Sky-God sɛ Angel no aba abɛwia Sky-God sika no, ɔwɔ he? Wɔkae sɛ, Wɔnni mfomsoɔ, wɔnni mfomsoɔ. Wɔkɔɔ na wɔde yɔnkoɔ no bae. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Bubu ne nsa wɔ nkwaa no so, na fa ne mogya no ma me. Ɛhɔ ara na wɔbubuu mu. Ɔbɔfoɔ no de aduru no yɛɛ mogya no na ɔde guu ɔhene ba no nan so. Ɛhɔ ara na ɔsɔre. Wɔkae sɛ, Monto Hunters praises. Wɔteɛɛm sɛ, Yiw! Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Megye wo ti na mede rema wo, na Tell-tale no deɛ, ma wɔkum no, na wotwitwa ne nam, na wɔtow gu nkyɛn nkyɛn nkyɛn. Wokum no, na wotwitwa ne nam no nketenkete, na wɔtow gu nkyɛn. Saa na Tell-tale pii baa abusuakuw no mu.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
