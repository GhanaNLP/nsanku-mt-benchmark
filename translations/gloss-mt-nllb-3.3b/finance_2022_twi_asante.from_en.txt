| 5 | aban a ɛwɔ Ghana - 2022 ɔmanfoɔ sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ

Adwumayɛ man a ɛtumi tena hɔ daa a wɔbɛkyekyere: Sikakora ho nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛhyɛ den ne Adwuma mmɔeɛ

FOREWORD

Covid-19 yaredom no nsunsuansoɔ bɔne kɛseɛ no, sikasɛm ahodoɔ a Ghana ka ho ate. Aban no nso nka ahohia a Ghanafoɔ asetena, asetena kwan ne nnwuma nkogu no ho asɛm. Mehoahoa me ho sɛ yɛn mmuaeɛ a yɛde dii yaredom no nkɛntɛnsoɔ ho dwuma no yɛɛ akokoduru na ɛyɛ pintinn na ewiase nyinaa ani gyee ho. Aban nso afi aseɛ ayɛ nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛbɛboa ma yɛasan akanyan nnwuma na yɛasan de yɛn asetena aba tebea a ɛfata mu.

Bio nso, de mmuaeɛ maa nhunahuna ne hokwan ahodoɔ a ɛnam ɔyaredɔm no ho no, Aban, wɔ H.E. Ɔmanpanyin Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo akandifoɔ pa ase, ayɛ GH¢100 ɔpepepem Covid-19 ano aduru ne Nnwumakuo a wɔbɛsan akanyan wɔn (GhanaCARES Obaatan Pa nhyehyɛeɛ) nhyehyɛeɛ. Eyi ama yɛn nnipa ho atɔ wɔn ntɛm ara na kɛse, na ɛresan akanyan yɛn ɔman yi.

Aban akyerɛ wo gyidie a wɔkura mu no ase, bere a yɛreyɛ adwuma de ahwɛ sɛ wobenya ayaresa a emu yɛ den afi yaredom no mu. Ɛnam saa berɛ yi so na ɛsɔɔ yɛn pintinnyɛ sɛ yɛbɛtena ase sɛ ɔman no hwɛe. Baebolo no de kwankyerɛ a ɛho hia ma wɔ mmere a ɛte sɛɛ mu. Titiriw no, sɛnea wɔaka wɔ Yesaia 9:10 no, Wɔabubu ntayaa no, nanso yɛde aboɔ a wɔasen bɛsi; Wɔatwa sikamore nnua no, nanso yɛbɛsan de sida nnua asi wɔn baabi. Aban nso asi ne bo sɛ obedi ne abɔhyɛ so wɔ saa ntini koro no ara mu. Saa sianteɛ yi nti, yɛbɛkɔ so de nhyehyɛeɛ ne dwumadi ahorow a ɛbɛma mmeaeɛ a ayera nyinaa asan aba na ama ɔmanfoɔ abrabɔ gyinabea atu mpɔn no adi dwuma.

2022 sika nhyehyɛeɛ no botae atitire ne mfeɛ mmiɛnsa (3) a ɛdi hɔ no mu baako ne sɛ wɔbɛkyekyere adwumayɛ man a ɛbɛtumi atena hɔ daa a ɛbɛtumi de sikasɛm mu nkɔsoɔ a ɛyɛ den, ɛbɛtena hɔ daa na ɛka obiara ho ama obiara anya adwuma pa, titire mmabun. Aban nhyehyɛeɛ nti, wɔde wɔn ani besi so sɛ wɔbɛboa mmabunu ma wɔde wɔn akyɛdeɛ ahodoɔ adi dwuma na wɔabue wɔn ahoɔden ama obiara anya yiedie denam akwanya pɛpɛɛpɛ so.

Aban bɛma mmabunu ahotɔ akɔ soro na akyekye wɔn adwumayɛ honhom denam YouStart Programme no a wɔbɛma ayɛ adwuma wɔ afe 2022 mu de ama mmabun adwumawuranom anya ayeyi kwan a wɔde besi hɔ anaasɛ wɔbɛtrɛw wɔn eguadwa mu.

Wɔ 2022 sika nhyehyɛeɛ no mu no, yɛahyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛbɛma yɛakɔ so adi sikasɛm mu nsɛnnennen a ɛwɔ hɔ seesei no ho dwuma na yɛapue denneennen wɔ ɔyaredɔm no akyi. Aban no ahyɛ bɔ sɛ wɔbɛyɛ nnwuma a ɛkɔ akyiri na wɔayɛ no biako de ama mmabunu anya nimdeɛ na wɔahyɛ adwumayɛbɔ ho nkuran. Bere a yɛredi Ɔmampanyin no akyi no,

NNIANIM

1. Afe 2022 ɔmanfoɔ sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ no yɛ nea Aban no tumi yɛeɛ wɔ afe 2021 mu no mmuano ne sika dodoɔ a Aban no abɔ ne tirim sɛ wɔbɛgye wɔ sika a wɔnya mu de adi ne nhyehyɛeɛ ne dwumadi ahorow ho dwuma wɔ afe 2022 ne mfimfini berɛ mu.

2. Sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wɔbɛboa asetena ne sikasɛm mu ahotɔ mmɔdenbɔ ne ɔman nkɔsoɔ botaeɛ no, 2022 sika nhyehyɛeɛ no twe adwene si Adwumayɛ man a ɛtumi tena hɔ daa a wɔbɛkyekyere denam Sikakora ho nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛhyɛ den ne Adwuma mmɔeɛ so.

SIKASƐM MU NKƆSOƆ A ƐBƐBA WƆ AFE 2021 MU

Sikasɛm kɛseɛ dwumadie

3. Nsɛm a wosusuw wɔ sikasɛm mmɔdenbɔ ho wɔ afe 2021 mu ne nea edidi so yi:

• Sikasɛm mu nkɔsoɔ ankasa nyinaa nyaa 3. 5 ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu wɔ afe 2021 fa a ɛdi kan no mu, berɛ a 0. 7 ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu mpuntuo wɔ afe 2020 berɛ korɔ no ara mu;

• GDP ankasa a ɛnyɛ ngo nyaa nkɔanim wɔ ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 4.8 wɔ afe fa a ɛdi kan wɔ afe 2021 mu, sɛ wɔde toto 1.1 ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu ho wɔ 2020 berɛ korɔ no ara mu a;

• Boɔ kɔ soro wɔ afe 2020 awieeɛ-Ɛbɔ no yɛ ɔha biara mu 10.6, sɛ wɔde toto afe 10.4 berɛ korɔ no ara ho a;

• Kabimɛn kɛseɛ a ɛtɔ sin (sika kasɛɛ) a ɛyɛ GDP ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 7.7 sɛ wɔde toto GDP ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 7.4 botaeɛ ho a;

• Sika a ɛkari pɛ titire no nyaa GDP mu ka a ɛbɔ ɔha biara mu 2.0 bere a ka a wɔahyɛ da bɔeɛ sɛ ɛbɛyɛ ɔha mu 1.7;

• Ɔman ka (sɛ GDP mu ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu) gyinaa ɔha mu nkyekyem 77. 7 wɔ Ɛbɔ 2021 awieeɛ, ɛfiri ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 76. 1 wɔ Ɔpɛnimba 2020 awieeɛ;

• Amanaman ntam akoradeɛ nyinaa duu US$11. 44 billion wɔ August 2021 awieeɛ, ɛdɔɔso sɛ ɛbɛtua nnoɔma a wɔde firi amannɔne ba abosome 5.2, sɛ wɔde toto US$8. 62 billion a ɛne nnoɔma a wɔde firi amannɔne ba abosome 4.1 yɛ pɛ wɔ December 2020 awieeɛ; na

• Sika sesa gyinabea kɔɔ so yɛɛ nea ɛyɛ den wɔ amannɔne sika akɛse mu wɔ afe 2021 atenaeɛ mmiɛnsa no nyinaa mu. Titiriw no, Ghana sidi bo kɔɔ fam ɔha biara mu 1.7 bere a wɔde toto Amerika Dɔla ho wɔ afe 2021 Ɛbɔ no awieeɛ, sɛ wɔde toto bo a ɛkɔɔ fam ɔha biara mu 2.9 wɔ berɛ korɔ no ara mu wɔ afe 2020 mu no ho a.

SIKA A ƐBA NE SIKASƐEE MMƆDENBƆ WƆ AFE 2021 MU

Revenue performance

4. Sika a ɛba ne Mmoa sika nyinaa wɔ berɛ a ɛfiri Ɔpɛpɔn kɔsi Ɛbɔ 2021 no mu no yɛ GH¢46. 85 ɔpepepem (10. 7% of GDP) firi dwumadi botae a wɔasan asiesie a ɛyɛ GH¢51. 31 ɔpepepem (11. 7% of GDP). Sɛnea wɔada no adi wɔ Akontaahyɛdeɛ 1 mu no, sika a ɛbaeɛ nyinaa yɛ sika a ɛnyɛ ngo toɔ (74%), sika a ɛnyɛ ngo toɔ (9%), ngo ne gas a wɔnya (9%), Sika a ɛba (6%) ne Mmoa sika (2%).

5. Wɔ afe 2021 mu no, sika a ɛba nyinaa ne mmoa sika a ɛkɔɔ soteɛ no sua sene botaeɛ a ɛfa Nnwumakuw sika tow, Ankorankoro sika tow, Fam agyapade ho toɔ, Ɔman mu nnoɔma toɔ ne Nkitahodi som toɔ ho.

Budget Balance ne Sikasɛm Ho Dwumadie

7. Aban sikasɛm mu dwumadie no maa sika a ɛtɔɔ sin a ɛyɛ GH¢33. 86 billion (7. 7% of GDP) sɛ wɔde toto botaeɛ a wɔasesa mu a ɛyɛ GH¢32. 58 billion (7. 4% of GDP) no ho a. Wɔde sika a wɔde tua ka a ɛbɔ no fi ɔman no mu ne amannɔne nyinaa. Ɔman mu sika a wɔde bɔboa nyinaa yɛ GH¢20. 39 billion, Amannɔne sika mmoa yɛ GH¢13. 47 billion, a ɛyɛ dodoɔ nyinaa mu ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 60. 2 ne ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 39. 8.

Sika a wɔsɛe no boro so: Eyi ba bere a Aban sikasɛeɛ boro Sika nhyehyɛeɛ a Mmarahyɛbadwa no apene so no so.

SHS A WONtua HWƐ: Ntoaso sukuu a wontua hwee (SHS) nhyehyɛe no yɛ Ghana Aban nhyehyeɛ a Ɔmampanyin Nana Akufo-Addo nnisoɔ de baeɛ wɔ Ɛbɔ 2017 mu a SHS adesua kwan a ahofadi wom ma jHS suani a ɔfata wɔ adesua mu.

Grant: Eyi yɛ sika fam mmoa ahodoɔ a nkɔsoɔ ahokafoɔ de ama Aban a ɛnsɛ sɛ wɔtua.

Mmoa sika a wɔde ma Government units afoforo: Saa akatua a wɔyɛ yi yɛ nea mmara te sɛ District Assemblies Common Fund (DACF), Road Fund, Ghana Nhomasua Ahotoso Foto (GETFUND), Petroleum ho sika a wɔahyehyɛ ne Ɔman apɔmuden sika korabea hwehwɛ.

Ɔman mu nnoɔma a wɔyɛ nyinaa boɔ (GDP): Eyi kyerɛ boɔ a ɛsom nyinaa wɔ nnoɔma a etwa toɔ a wɔhwehwɛ ne ɔsom a wɔyɛ wɔ ɔman no mu wɔ berɛ pɔtee bi mu.

Amanaman ntam akoradeɛ nyinaa: Eyi susuw amannɔne sika a ɔman bi tumi tɔ nneɛma ne ɔsom fi amamfra aman hɔ wɔ berɛ bi mu; Ɛsan nso foa ahoɔden a ɔman no mu sika wɔ wɔ abusuabɔ a ɛda ɛne aman afoforo sika ahoroɔ ntam no so.

Boɔ kɔ soro: mponosoɔ dodow a ɛwɔ nneɛma boɔ nyinaa gyinabea wɔ nneɛma ne ɔsom mu wɔ berɛ bi mu.

IPEP (Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme): Infrastructure for Poverty Alleviation Program (IPEP) yɛ NPP aban no flagship programmes no mu baako a ne botaeɛ ne sɛ ɛbɛtew ohia so na ɛtew pɛyɛ a ɛnni hɔ so, titire wɔ nkuraaseɛ ne nnipa a wɔdi hia mu, denam ahotɔ nnoɔma titiriw a wɔde nneɛma a wɔde ma so.

Sikasɛm mu Botaeɛ Kɛseɛ: Eyinom yɛ nsusuhoɔ a wɔde di dwuma de nya sikasɛm mu mmɔdenbɔ. Nhwɛso ahorow bi ne sikasɛm mu nkɔsoɔ, boɔ kɔ soro, nsesaɛ a ɛba wɔ adwumanya gyinabea mu, dwadie mu mmɔdenbɔ a wɔne aman kakra wɔ, nkonimdi anaa nkogu a aban sikasɛm nhyehyɛe ahorow nya ne gyinaeɛ horoɔ a Ghana Sikakorabea no asi a ɛka sika ahwehwɛdeɛ ne nneɛma a wɔde ma wɔ sikasɛm mu.

ADWUMA NKETENKETE, NKETEWA NE MFINIMFINI (Ketewa NE Nnwuma nketewa mpuntuo nhyehyɛeɛ): Wɔtaa kyerɛkyerɛ adwumakuo ketewa (anaasɛ adwuma ketewa) mu sɛ adwuma ketewaa bi a ɛma nnipa dodow a ɛsua koraa yɛ adwuma na ne akontaabu krataa anaa adetɔn sika a ɛkɔba nnu sika pɔtee bi.

NABCO (Ɔman Adansifo Kuw): A wɔsan frɛ no NABCO no yɛ ankorankoro nyansapɛ a Ghana Aban de asi hɔ de ama suapɔn mu awiefoɔ a wonni adwuma no nnwuma a wɔde bedi asetena mu haw ahorow ho dwuma wɔ ankorankoro ne ɔman adwumayɛkuo mu na wɔakyekye wɔn nimdeɛ de ahyɛ sikasɛm mu nkɔsoɔ ho nkuran wɔ ɔman no mu.

NBSSI (Ɔman mu badwa a wɔde ma nnwuma nketewa ne akɛse): Ɔman mu badwa a ɛhwɛ Nnwuma nketewa so (NBSSI) yɛ ɔman adwumayɛkuo a ɛnyɛ mfasoɔ a ɛhyɛ Dwadiɛ ne Nnwuma Asoeɛ ase na ɛhyɛ sɛ ɛmma Ketewa ne Nnwuma nketewa (Adwuma nketewa ne mfinimfini) nkɔsoɔ ne nkɔsoɔ mmra.

GDP ankasa a ɛnyɛ ngo: Ɛyɛ sikasɛm mu aba a ɛfiri mu baeɛ nyinaa a dwumadi ahodoɔ a ɛwɔ fango adwumayɛ mu ka ho, a ɛsusu boɔ kɔ soro gyinabea ho akawnto.

Sika a ɛmfiri tow mu: Aban sika a wɔnnya mfi toɔ mu, nhwɛso ahorow ne sotua a wɔde ma wɔ tumi krataa anaa tumi nkrataa ho, dwumadifoɔ sika ne asodi foforɔ.

Asɛdeɛ a aka: asɛdeɛ a aka no bi ne MDAfo bɔhyɛ ahorow a wɔne Sikasɛm Asoeɛ no ayɛ ne 2016 akatua a aka wɔ Mmara hyɛ sika ho.

Primary balance: Eyi ne nsonsonoeɛ a ɛda Aban sikanya ne sikasɛee a mfɛntom nka ho (a ka tua nka ho). Wobetumi asusuw eyi sɛ ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu a ɛwɔ Ɔman mu nnoɔma a wɔyɛ nyinaa boɔ (GDP) mu.

Nkɔso dodow a wɔhwɛ kwan: Sɛnea Ghana sikasɛm bɛnyin wɔ afe biako mu.

Sika a ɛba: Eyi ne sika dodoɔ nyinaa a Aban nya firi ɔman no mu ne amannɔne de yɛ ne dwumadi ahodoɔ.

RFJ (Rearing for Aduane and Nnwuma): Yɛ mmoa adwumayɛ a akansi ne adwumayɛ yie, ɛma ɔman mu nnoɔma yɛ kɔ soro, ɛtew nneɛma a wɔde firi amannɔne ba a ɛfa mmoa nneɛma ho no so, ɛboa ma adwuma bɔ na ɛma mmoayɛn dwumadie mu adwumayɛfoɔ asetena kwan tu mpɔn.
