Adwumayɛkuo a ɛbɛtena hɔ daa a wɔbɛkyekyere: sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛyɛ den ne adwumayɛ nkɔsoɔ

NNIANIM ASƐM

COVID-19 ɔyaredɔm no nsunsuansoɔ a ɛyɛ hu no, sikasɛm ahodoɔ nyinaa aka ho, a Ghana ka ho. Nhyɛsoɔ a Ghanafoɔ ahwere asetena, asetena ne nnwuma mu no nyera wɔ aban no so. Mani gye ho sɛ mɛka sɛ mmuae a yɛde mae wɔ ɔyaredɔm no nkɛntɛnso ho no yɛ akokoduru ne gyinaesi na wiase nyinaa ani agye ho. Aban nso ahyɛ aseɛ ayɛ nneɛma bi a ɛbɛboa ma nnwuma no asan akanyan na ama yɛn asetena asan ayɛ sɛnea na ɛteɛ dada no.

Bio nso, de abua ahunahuna ne hokwan ahorow a efi ɔyaredɔm no mu aba no, aban a ɛwɔ H.E. Ɔmanpanyin Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo akanni ase no ayɛ GH¢100 ɔpepepem covid-19 ano aduru ne nnwuma a wɔbɛsan akanyan (Ghanacares Obaatan Pa nhyehyɛe) ntɛmntɛm. Eyi ama yɛn nkurɔfo anya ahotɔ kɛse, na ɛsan nso rema yɛn man no anya ahoɔden foforo.

Yɛn ani sɔ ahotoso a wowɔ wɔ aban no mu bere a yɛrehwɛ sɛ obefi ɔyaredɔm no mu no. Wɔ saa bere yi mu no, wɔasɔ yɛn bo a yɛasi sɛ yɛbɛtra ase sɛ ɔman no ahwɛ. Bible no de akwankyerɛ a ɛho hia ma bere nyinaa wɔ mmere a ɛtete saa mu. Titiriw no, sɛnea wɔaka wɔ Yesaia 9:10 sɛ, Brick no ahwe ase, nanso yɛde abo a wɔasen besi; Sikamore nnua atwa, nanso yɛde cedar besi ananmu. Saa ara nso na aban no asi ne bo sɛ obedi ne bɔhyɛ so. Ɛno nti, yɛbɛkɔ so de nhyehyeɛ ne dwumadie a ɛbɛma wɔasan de mmeaeɛ a ayera nyinaa aba na ama ɔmanfoɔ asetena atu mpɔn no adi dwuma.

Afe 2022 sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ no botaeɛ titire baako ne sɛ wɔbɛkyekyere adwumayɛkuo a ɛbɛtena hɔ daa ama obiara, titire mmabun, de sikasɛm mu nkɔsoɔ a ɛyɛ den, ɛbɛtena hɔ daa na ɛka obiara ho a ɛbɛma wɔanya nnwuma pa ama obiara. Aban nhyehyeɛ nti, wɔde wɔn ani asi so sɛ wɔbɛboa mmabun ma wɔde wɔn talente adi dwuma na wɔahwɛ ahu sɛ obiara bɛnya yiedie denam hokwan a ɛyɛ pɛ so.

Sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mmerante ne mmabaa ahotoso bɛkɔ soro na wɔakyekye adwumayɛ ho adwene no, aban no bɛma YouStart nhyehyɛe no ayɛ adwuma wɔ 2022 mu de bosea ama mmerante ne mmabaa a wɔyɛ nnwuma sɛ wɔmfa wɔn adwuma nsi hɔ anaasɛ wɔntrɛw mu.

Wɔ afe 2022 sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ mu no, yɛaka nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛbɛma yɛakɔ so adi sikasɛm mu nsɛnnennen a ɛwɔ hɔ seesei no ho dwuma na yɛapue ayɛ den wɔ ɔyaredɔm no akyi berɛ no mu. Aban no asi ne bo sɛ ɔbɛfa akwan a ɛkɔ akyiri na wɔayɛ nhyehyɛe de ahokokwaw ama na wahyɛ adwumayɛ ho nkuran wɔ mmerante ne mmabaa mu.

2. Sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɛbɛboa mmɔden a wɔbɔ sɛ wɔbɛsan anya asetra ne sikasɛm mu ahoɔden ne ɔman no nkɔso botae ahorow no, 2022 sikasɛm nhyehyɛe no twe adwene si adwumayɛkuw a ɛbɛkɔ so atra hɔ daa a wɔbɛkyekye denam sikasɛm nhyehyɛe a emu yɛ den ne adwumayɛ hokwan ahorow a wɔbɛma aba so.

SIKASƐM MU NKƆSOƆ MA AFE 2021

Sikasɛm mu Dwumadie Kɛseɛ

3. Nsɛm a ɛfa sikasɛm adwumayɛ ho wɔ afe 2021 mu no te sɛ nea edidi so yi:

• Sɛ wɔkyekyɛ mu a, sikasɛm mu nkɔsoɔ ankasa nyinaa yɛ 3.5% wɔ afe 2021 fã a ɛdi kan no mu, 0.7% wɔ 2020 berɛ korɔ no ara mu.

• GDP ankasa a ɛnyɛ ngo nyaa nkɔsoɔ 4.8% wɔ afe 2021 fã a ɛdi kan no mu berɛ a wɔde toto 1.1% a ɛwɔ 2020 berɛ korɔ no ara mu no ho;

• Nneɛma bo a ɛkɔɔ soro wɔ September awiei no yɛ 10. 6%, bere a wɔde toto 10. 4% a ɛkɔɔ soro wɔ bere koro no ara mu wɔ 2020 mu no ho no;

• Ɔha biara mu nkyem 7.7 a ɛba fam sen biara (sika a wɔde bɛto mu) a ɛyɛ GDP ɔha biara mu nkyem 7.4 a wɔde asi wɔn ani so sɛ wɔbɛyɛ GDP ɔha biara mu nkyem 7.4;

• Sikasɛm titiriw no bɔɔ ka a ɛyɛ GDP ɔha biara mu nkyem 2.0, bere a na wɔahyɛ sɛ ɛbɛbɔ ka ɔha biara mu nkyem 1.7;

• Ɔman ka (ɔha biara mu nkyem 100 wɔ GDP mu) gyinaa ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 77. 7 wɔ September 2021 awieeɛ, ɛboro ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 76. 1 wɔ December 2020 awieeɛ;

• Amanaman ntam sika a ɛwɔ hɔ nyinaa wɔ August 2021 awiei no yɛ dɔla ɔpepepem 11. 44, na ɛdɔɔso sɛ ɛbɛtua asram 5.2 a wɔde bɛkɔ amannɔne no ho ka, bere a wɔde toto dɔla ɔpepepem 8. 62 a ɛne asram 4.1 a wɔakyerɛw wɔ December 2020 awiei no yɛ pɛ no ho no; na

• Sika a wɔde sesa nneɛma no kɔɔ so yɛɛ den wɔ amannɔne sika akɛse abiɛsa no nyinaa mu wɔ afe 2021 mu. Titiriw no, Ghana cedi bo kɔɔ fam ɔha biara mu 1. 7 bere a wɔde toto U.S. dɔla ho wɔ September 2021 awiei, bere a ɛkɔɔ fam 2. 9 wɔ 2020 bere koro no ara mu no.

Adwumayɛkuo a ɛbɛtena hɔ daa a wɔbɛkyekyere: sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛyɛ den ne adwumayɛ nkɔsoɔ

Nkyerɛaseɛ (Sika Nhyehyɛeɛ Nsɛmfua)

1D1F (One District, One Factory): Adwumayɛbea ne nnwuma a ɛbɛma ɔman no akɔ mfiridwuma mu no a wɔbɛma akɔ soro. Ɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɛbɛma Ghanafoɔ anya adwumayɛ hokwan pii wɔ ɔman no tenten ne ne tɛtrɛtɛ nyinaa mu.

Akontaabuo: Eyi ne bere a aban no bu nasetra ho akontaa kyerɛ ne manfo denam ne dwumadi ahorow a ɛda adi na ɛda nea efi nneyɛe a ɛtete saa mu ba no adi no so. Ebia eyi bɛka sikasɛm anaa agyapade afoforo a aban no kura ho asɛyɛde ho.

Nkyekyɛmu: Ɔkwan a wɔfa so kyekyɛ anaa wɔkyekyɛ nneɛma a ɛwɔ ɔman ahyehyɛde ahorow ne dwumadibea ahorow mu.

Nneɛma a wɔtew so: Eyi yɛ ɔkwan a wɔfa so ma ɔhaw bi a emu yɛ den so tew.

Sikasɛm nhyehyɛe: Budget yɛ nhyehyɛe a ɛkyerɛ baabi a wobenya sika afi ne nea wɔde bedi dwuma. Sɛ nhwɛso no, abusua bi betumi ayɛ sikasɛm nhyehyɛe a ɛkyerɛ sika dodow a ɛwɔ hɔ ne sɛnea ɛsɛ sɛ wɔde di kan wɔ sika a wɔsɛe no ho (sɛ nhwɛso no, dan ka, nneɛma a wɔde di dwuma ne aduan). Sɛ abusua no hia sika pii a, ɛsɛ sɛ wosusuw ho sɛ wɔbɛma sika pii akɔ soro anaasɛ wɔbɛbɔ bosea.

Budget deficit: Eyi ba bere a aban sika a wɔsɛe no boro sika a wonya so no.

Sika kɛseɛ a wɔsɛe no: Eyi yɛ sika a wɔsɛe no wɔ nnwuma akɛseɛ te sɛ akwan, sukuu, ayaresabea, abɔntenban, akwantuo, nsuo a wɔde fa nsuo mu, afifideɛ ne mfiri, ne nea ɛkeka ho ho.

Adwumayɛfoɔ akatua: Eyi yɛ akatua ne mmoa a ɛfa akatua ho te sɛ asetena mu ahobammɔ, mmoa a wɔde ma ne pɛnhyen sika a wotua ma aban adwumayɛfo.

Nkɔsoɔ Ahokafoɔ (DPS): Wei kyerɛ aman ne/anaasɛ ahyehyɛdeɛ a wɔne aman a afei na wɔrenya nkɔsoɔ yɛ adwuma bom de du wɔn nkɔsoɔ botaeɛ ho. DPS betumi ayɛ aman pii, te sɛ Amanaman Ntam Sikakorabea (IMF), Wiase Nyinaa Sikakorabea, Afrika Nkɔso Sikakorabea (AfDB) anaa aman mmienu a ɛka bom yɛ aman te sɛ Germany, United Kingdom ne United States of America (USA).

Afe awiei nneɛma bo a ɛkɔ soro: Nneɛma bo a ɛkɔɔ soro wɔ December mu wɔ afe a wɔde susuw ho no mu.

Adwumayɛkuo a ɛbɛtena hɔ daa a wɔbɛkyekyere: sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛyɛ den ne adwumayɛ nkɔsoɔ

Sika a wɔsɛe no boro so: Eyi ba bere a aban sika a wɔsɛe no boro sikasɛm nhyehyɛe a Mmarahyɛ Bagua no apene so no so no.

SHS a wontua hwee: Free SHS (Free Higher Secondary Education) nhyehyeɛ no yɛ Ghana Aban nhyehyɛɛ a Ɔmampanyin Nana Akufo-Addo aban no de sii hɔ wɔ September 2017 mu, a ɛhwɛ sɛ JHS asuafoɔ a wɔfata no bɛnya SHS nwomasua a wontua hwee.

Boa: Eyi yɛ sikasɛm mu mmoa a nkɔsoɔ ahokafoɔ de ma aban a ɛnsɛ sɛ wɔsan tua.

Sika a wɔde ma ɔman adwumayɛbea afoforo: Saa sika yi yɛ nea mmara hwehwɛ te sɛ District Assemblies Common Fund (DACF), Roads Trust, Ghana Education Board Funds (GETFUND), Petroleum Revenue Fund ne National Health Fund.

Ɔman no mu nneɛma nyinaa bo (GDP): Eyi kyerɛ nneɛma a etwa to a wɔhwehwɛ ne nnwuma a wɔyɛ wɔ ɔman bi mu nyinaa bo a ɛsom wɔ bere pɔtee bi mu.

Amanaman Ntam Sikakorabea: Eyi kyerɛ sɛnea ɔman bi tumi tɔ nneɛma ne nnwuma fi aman a wɔyɛ atubrafo hɔ wɔ bere bi mu; Ɛsan nso kyerɛ sɛnea ɛhɔnom sika mu yɛ den bere a wɔde toto aman afoforo sika ho no.

Nneɛma bo a ɛkɔ soro: Ɛyɛ nkɔanim a ɛba wɔ nneɛma ne nnwuma bo nyinaa mu wɔ bere bi mu.

IPEP (Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Program): Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Program (IPEP) yɛ NPP aban no nnwuma titire no mu baako a ne botaeɛ ne sɛ ɛbɛtew ohia so na ɛtew pɛyɛ a ɛnni hɔ so, titire wɔ nkuraaseɛ ne mpɔtam a wɔnni ahotɔ denam nneɛma a ɛho hia a wɔde bɛma so.

Sikasɛm mu botae akɛse: Eyinom yɛ nneɛma a wɔde susuw sikasɛm mu adwumayɛ ho. Nhwɛsoɔ bi ne sikasɛm mu nkɔsoɔ, nneɛma boɔ a ɛkɔ soro, nsakraeɛ a ɛba adwumayɛ mu, aguadiɛ mu mmɔdenbɔ a wɔne aman foforɔ di, aban sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛdi nkonim anaa ɛdi nkoguo kakra ne gyinaesie a Ghana Sikakorabea no asi a ɛka sikasɛm mu ahwehwɛdeɛ ne sikasɛm mu nneɛma a wɔde ma.

NNWUMA NKETEWA, NKETEWA NE NKETEWA (MSME): Wɔtaa kyerɛkyerɛ adwumakuw ketewa (anaasɛ adwumayɛkuw ketewa) mu sɛ adwumayɛkuw ketewa a ɛma nnipa kakraa bi na sikasɛm ho akontaabu anaa sika a wonya no nnu sika pɔtee bi.

NABCO (Nation Builders Corps): a wɔsan frɛ no NABCO no yɛ nhyehyeɛ a Ghana Aban de aba sɛ wɔde nnwuma bɛma wɔn a wɔawie sukuu a wɔnni adwuma de adi asetena mu haw ahodoɔ ho dwuma wɔ ankorankoro ne ɔman adwumayɛkuo mu na wɔakyekye wɔn nimdeɛ a ɛbɛma sikasɛm anya nkɔsoɔ wɔ ɔman no mu.

NBSSI (Ɔman Bagua a Ɛhwɛ Nnwuma Nketewa ne Mfinimfini So): Ɔman Nnwuma Nketewa Bagua (NBSSI) yɛ ɔman adwumayɛkuo ahyehyɛdeɛ a ɛnyɛ mfasoɔ a ɛhyɛ Aguadi ne Nnwuma Dwumadibea ase a ɛwɔ asɛdeɛ sɛ ɛhyɛ nnwuma nketewa ne akɛseɛ nkɔsoɔ ne nkɔsoɔ ho nkuran.

GDP ankasa a ɛnyɛ ngo: Ɛyɛ nneɛma a wonya fi sikasɛm mu nyinaa a dwumadi ahorow a ɛwɔ ngo adwumayɛbea no nka ho, a wosusuw nneɛma bo a ɛkɔ soro ho.

Sika a wontua tow: Aban sika a wonya fi towtua mu, nhwɛso ahorow ne tumi krataa anaa tumi krataa ho ka, wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no ka ne ɛka afoforo.

Asɛdeɛ a aka: Asɛdeɛ a aka no bi ne MDAfoɔ bɔhyɛ a wɔne Sikasɛm Dwumadibea no ayɛ ne sika a wɔde bɛto mu wɔ afe 2016 mu.

Primary balance: Eyi ne nsonsonoe a ɛda aban sika a wonya ne sika a wɔsɛe no daa (a ɛka a wɔbɔ ka ho) ntam. Yebetumi asusuw eyi sɛ ɔman no mu nneɛma nyinaa bo (GDP) ɔha biara mu nkyem 100.

Growth prospects: Sɛnea Ghana economy no bɛnyin wɔ afe biako mu.

Sika a wɔnya: Wei ne sika a aban no nya firi ɔman no mu ne amannɔne nyinaa ma ne dwumadie.

RFJ (Raising for Food and Jobs): Ɛbɛma mmoa adwuma a ɛyɛ akansi na ɛyɛ adwuma yiye, ama ɔman no mu nneɛma a wɔyɛ no akɔ soro, ɛbɛtew mmoa a wɔde fi amannɔne ba no so, aboa ma wɔanya adwuma na ama mmoa adwumayɛfo asetra atu mpɔn.
