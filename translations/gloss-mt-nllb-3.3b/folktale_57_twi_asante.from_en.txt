Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

CHILD-KWASI-GYINAMOA

NA abofra bi wɔ hɔ, Kwasi Gyinamoa, na ɔwɔ nuammaa nwɔtwe, na wɔn mu biara nni ɔhokafoɔ, enti wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ wɔrebɛkɔ baabi akɔhwehwɛ okununom. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, Me ne wo bɛkɔ. Wɔbuaa sɛ, Yɛn ne wo renkɔ. Wɔn mu panyin no kaa sɛ, Ma ɔne yɛn nkɔ, na sɛ yɛduru kwan no so a, na akutu atwa yɛn mu a, ɔbɛtumi atwi ama yɛn. Wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa ne wɔn kɔe. Wɔbaa asubɔnten bi ho, na emu fã bi sen sɛ dɔte fitaa, emu fã bi sen sɛ dɔte kɔkɔɔ, na emu fã bi sen sɛ dɔte tuntum. Wɔteɛɛm sɛ, Eh, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ na yɛatumi atwa asuwa yi? Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa ne (magic) cow-tail-switch no, ɔbɔɔ no (nsuo no), na ɛmaa nsuo no mu paee mmienu, na wɔfaa mu kɔɔ so. Wɔbaa kuro bi mu; na ɛyɛ kuro kɛseɛ paa. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, Saa kuro yi yɛ dinn (kyerɛ sɛ silent save ma nnomaa su a ɛwɔ babadua mu), onipa biara nni mu anaa? Afei Anomaa kasae sɛ, Obi wɔ fie hɔ, monkɔ so. Wɔkɔɔ so, na aberewa bi wɔ hɔ a, na ne etire da nanan so, na ɔrehwehwɛ ntontom wɔ mu. Ɛberɛ a ɔhunuu Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, afei ɔmaa ne etire so na ɔde sii ananmu, ɔkae sɛ, Me mmanana, dɛn nti na moaba ha bere a obiara nni ha bɛba? Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, Me nuammaa no adi nwɔtwe, na wɔnnya okununom a wɔne wɔn bɛware, na enti wɔkae sɛ wɔbɛba abɛhwehwɛ okununom a wɔbɛware wɔn. Aberewa nanaa kae sɛ, Aane, ɛno ara ne no nyinaa righ me nso, me wɔ mmofra nwɔtwe. Nana panyin na ɔsomaa ne nana sɛ ɔmmɛfrɛ ne mmofra. Bere a wɔbaa no, na oyi ka sɛ, Me kunu ni, na ɔno nso ka sɛ, Me kunu ni. Bere a wɔn nyinaa dii nkitaho wiei no, na aberewa no kɔfa suman bi a wɔfrɛ no _dokum_, na ɔkyekyere no wɔ ne mmofra no akɔn so. Afei na wɔn mu biara kɔ ne yere nna. Na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso ne aberewa no kɔdii nahomeɛ. Ɛduruu anadwo no, aberewa no hyɛɛ nse, kɔkɔɔ fann, wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ ɔne wɔn bɛkunkum mmabaawa no. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, E! e! e! Na aberewa no kaa sɛ, Ɛyɛ dɛn? Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buaa sɛ, Berɛ a me wɔ me ara me akuraa, na sɛ mekɔ ahomegyeɛ nanso mantumi nna a, na maame twitwa bayere slice gu ogya mu, na ɔfa kora a ɛtokurotokuro wom kɔpra nsuo ma me nom. Ɔbaa panyin nana sii hɔ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ asuwa hɔ, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kɔhwehwɛɛ suman a wɔfrɛ no _dokuman no. um_ fii aberewa no mma no akɔn so, na ɔde wɔn kɔsɛn ne nuammaa no so. Na aberewa no fi nsuo no mu san bae, na ɔkaa sɛ, Mebɔɔ mmɔden sɛ mɛpete nsuo (wɔ saa sɔneɛ no mu), nanso mantumi anpete. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, Kɔfa atɛkyɛ bi, na fa nhaban gu mu (wɔ sɔneɛ no mu), fa na petepete. Ɔbaa no kɔyɛɛ saa, na ɔde nsuo petepete mu, na ɛyɛɛ yie. Ɔde bae, na ɔyii bayere no fii ogya no so, na ɔmaa Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa dii, na ɔnom nsuo no nso. Afei ɔtow ne ho too fam daeɛ. Aberewa sɔre, ɔkae sɛ, Me ni. Ɔhyerɛn ne se, kɔkɔɔ (kɔkɔɔ), na ɔne wɔn kɔɔ dan no mu, ɔde ne nsa bɔɔ mmofra no kɔn ho, na nea ne kɔn so na dokum suman no nna no, ɔde ne se hyehye no. Saa kwan yi so na okum mmofra baawɔtwe no. Na onnim sɛ nankasa mmofra na wakum wɔn. Bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ntɛm koraa, ntɛm koraa na Aberewa nana sii gyinaeɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ afuw hɔ. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuammaa no ma wɔdwanee. Aberewa nana firi afuw baeɛ. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, Aberewa nana e! Aberewa nana e! Sɛ woanyɛ kwasea a, anka wubekum wo ankasa wo mmofra? Na me deɛ, me ne me nuammaa rekɔ. Ɔbaa panyin nana kae sɛ, Ɛnyɛ nsunsuansoɔ biara, nea onim sɛnea ɔne wo bɛdi no) ne baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ. Ɔkɔɔ, na Ɔbaa panyin nana nso danee ne ho bɛyɛɛ ababaawa fɛfɛ, na ɔkuraa nakuturuku, na ɔkɔɔ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa akuraa hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Obiara a ɔbɛtumi de agyan atwa saa gourd-rattle yi, me bɛware no. Nnipa a wɔwɔ akuraa hɔ no nyinaa bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ wɔbɛtwa no, nanso wɔantumi. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa na ɔbɔɔ no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mayɛ wo aware. " Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, merennware wo. " Enti ne wofa no kɔwaree no. Ɔne no daeɛ, nanso ɔtwee naniwa mmienu no, na ɔne wɔn kɔe. Ɛnna abaayewa no kaa sɛ, "Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa wɔ hɔ! Me apete wo wofa aniwa mmienu no, na me ne wɔn rekɔ. " Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buaa sɛ, "Fa wɔn kɔ, ɛfiri sɛ nea ɔnim wo ho dwuma ne baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ. " Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, wɔkae sɛ, "Abofra, kɔ na gye wo ho na fa wo wofa aniwa no ba. Ɔkae sɛ, "Merenkɔ. " Wɔhyɛɛ no den, wɔhyɛɛ no den, na awieeɛ no ɔkɔeɛ. Afei, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nim sɛ Ɔbaa-Nkokoraa no banana no yɛ _enceinte_, enti ɔno nso faa koraa bɔɔ ne yafunu, na ɔde ne ntoma kyekyeree so, na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ ne nana akuraa ase. Bere a ɔduruu kuro no awieeɛ no, ɔsuiɛ. Aberewa nana kae sɛ, "Ɛdɛn na ɛyɛ asɛm? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me ne me kunu no na yɛreba, na bere a ɔduruu bepɔwa no atifi no, ɔhwee ase na ɛno maa naniwa mmienu no firii baeɛ bɛhwee ase. " Aberewa nana kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ koraa, saa ɔsɛmmɔnedifo a wasan aba wiase bio no, Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nuabarima no ani benkum ni, na ne nifa ani no nso ni. Sɛ wokɔ (baabi a wo kunu onifirani gyina) a, ɛsɛ sɛ woka sɛ, Maniwa tui, maniwa yɛ me fefeefe. " Ɛntɛm ara na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nsa kaa wɔn, na ɔkaa sɛ, Wo a wowɔ hɔ yi, akwakoraa nana, woyii me wɔfa aniwa yi, na me ne wɔn rekɔ. Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, Eno mfa ho, nea onim sɛnea ɔne wo bɛdi no, ɔyɛ baabi a ɛwɔ hɔ no. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa. -Gyinamoa baa fie, na ɔde aniwa no kɔsisii ne kanyane afidie hyɛbea a ɛwɔ ne wofa no mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Maniwa huruw, maniwa da hɔ," na wofa aniwa nso buee pefee. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, na mmofra no nyinaa (wɔ akuraa hɔ) akɔhohoro wɔn ho wɔ asuwa no mu. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, "Merenhohoro. " Wɔkae sɛ, "Ebia wowɔ awotwaa dua? " Bere a wɔkae saa no, ne nufuɔ yɛɛ no yaw, na ɔhohoroo ne ho. Ɛntɛm ara na ɔhuruw kɔɔ nsuo no mu, na aberewa nanaa kyeree no, mmienu mmienu! Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, manya wo," na ɔne no kɔɔ nakuraa. Afei, aberewa nana kɔfaa kɛntɛn bi kataa no so, na ɔfaa nnuadewa nketenkete bi, na ɔde too ogya so. Ɔkae sɛ, "Mekɔ gyeene abusua no nkyɛn, merekɔ nnuahwamfo abusuakuo no nkyɛn, na afei mɛba abɛku Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa. " Ɔde ne ho kɔeɛ, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa boaboaa abotan ano na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ piraa no, pupru! pupru! Ɔbaa panyin bi nana kae sɛ: "Dɛn na worenoa yi? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Tiger-nuts. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Ma me bi mennom. " " Obuae sɛ, "Bue m'anim. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wobɛkɔ. " Obuae sɛ, "Dabi, merenkɔ. " Ɔbuee n'anim, na ofii adi; na aberewa no nana rebɔ nnuadewa. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa nnua a wɔde yɛ nnuadewa, kum aberewa nana abeawa. Afei oyii aberewa nana abeawa no wedeɛ na ɔhyɛɛ no. Nana aberewa san bae, ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na mo nyinaa ho agu fĩ saa? " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Mekum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, mede no ato ogya so. " Saa amaneɛbɔ yi yɛɛ dɛ ma Nana Aberewa, na ɔsaw maa ne nne so (na ɔtoo dwom sɛ): "Abofra, wokum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, daakye bi wobɛyɛ obi. "

Ɔnoaa aduane no wiei, wɔtenaa nkyɛn didii. Aberewa nana de dede kɛseɛ petepetee nkwan no ano sɛ, fweo! fweo! Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Worehwie nkwan a wɔde wo banana ti ayɛ agu wo ano. " Aberewa nana kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na woka yi? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Na meka sɛ nkyene biara nni saa nkwan yi mu. " Aberewa nana kaa sɛ, "Kɔfa bi. " Enti ɔkɔɔ na ɔnyaa bi. Wɔdidii wieeɛ no, aberewa nana kaa sɛ, "Seesei a mayɛ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa awu no, bra ha, me nana. " Ɔbaa no, ɔfaa sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ hyɛɛ no fɛfɛɛfɛ. Mprempren, aberewa nana hwɛɛ nanim berɛ bi. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Aha! abofra yi anim ma mekae Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa. " Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Nanabea, dɛn nti, bere a wunim sɛ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa baeɛ na ɔkunkum me abusuafoɔ nwɔtwe no nyinaa (wokasa saa) no, w'abere yɛ me yaw. " Ɔbaa panyin nanabea kaa sɛ, "Shut your ano. " Afei na abofra no adwo ne ho wɔ fie hɔ. Nanabea no yii no fii hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ: "Momma menkɔtow ngu mfimfini agude no so." Enti Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa soaa no kɔe. Bere a oduu mfinimfini agude no ho no, oyii sika kɔkɔɔ agude a na aberewa no de asiesie nani no nyinaa na ɔde kyekyeree no. Afei ɔkae sɛ, "Ho hɔ, ɔbaa panyin nana, ho hɔ, ɔbaa panyin nana, me ne no, abofra Kwasi-Gyinamoa, mprempren mprɛnu so, maba sɛ merebɛkum wo nana, na afei nso mayi wo sika kɔkɔɔ agude." Ɔbaa panyin nana buae sɛ, "Mekae sɛ wanim te sɛ abofra Kwasi-Gyinamoa; afei baabiara a wobɛkɔ no, me ne wo bɛkɔ." Afei wɔn baanu nyinaa dwanee, kiri! kiri! kiri! Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa bɛduruu nakuraa hɔ, na ɔde sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ no kɔmaa nabusuafoɔ, na ɔkɔɔ kwan ho, kɔhyehyɛɛ ne nkyɛn, na Aberewa-nananom nso baa hɔ. Ɛno nti, sɛ wo nante kwan so a, wobɛte kakasakakasa nnyigyei sɛ, "pe. " Ɛno ne Aberewa-nananom a wɔredi Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa akyi.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
