Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

ABAKƆSƐM-KWASI-GYINAMOA

ƐDII BERE BI A NA ABARIMAA BI, Kwasi Gyinamoa, ne ne nuanom mmaa baawɔtwe wɔ hɔ, na wɔn mu biara nni ɔhokafoɔ, enti wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ wɔrebɛkɔ baabi akɔhwehwɛ kunu. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, Me ne wo bɛkɔ. Wɔbuaa sɛ, Yɛn ne wo renkɔ. Wɔn mu panyin no kaa sɛ, Ma ɔne yɛn nkɔ, na sɛ yɛduru kwan no so a, na nsɔeɛ bi akye yɛn a, ɔbɛtumi atwe ama yɛn. Wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso ne wɔn kɔɔ. Wɔduruu asubɔnten bi ho, na emu fã bi sen sɛ dɔteɛ, na emu fã bi sen sɛ dɔteɛ kɔkɔɔ, na emu fã bi sen sɛ dɔteɛ tuntum. Wɔkae sɛ, Aane, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ na yɛatumi atwa asubɔnten yi? Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa ne (magic) cow-tail-switch, bɔɔ (nsuo no), na ɛmaa nsuo no mu yɛɛ mmienu, na wɔtwaa mu kɔɔ so. Wɔbaa kuro bi mu; na ɛyɛ kuro kɛseɛ pa ara. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, Kuro no mu yɛ dwoodwoo, onipa biara nni mu anaa? Afei na Bird bi akasa sɛ, Obi wɔ fie hɔ, monkɔ so. Wɔkɔɔ so, na ɔbaa panyin bi wɔ hɔ, na ne ti da ne nan so, na ɔrehwehwɛ nsuo wɔ mu. Ɛberɛ a ɔhunuu Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, afei ɔmaa ne ti so sii nananmu; ɔkae sɛ, Me mmanana, adɛn nti na, berɛ a obiara nni hɔ a ɔbɛba ha no, moaba ha? Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buaa sɛ, Me nuanom mmaa no adi mfeɛ nwɔtwe, na wɔn nni mmarima a wɔne wɔn bɛware, na enti wɔaka sɛ wɔbɛba abɛhwehwɛ mmarima a wɔne wɔn bɛware. Ɔbaa panyin-nananom kaa sɛ, Oh, that is all righ t, me nso, mewɔ mma nwɔtwe. Ɔpanyin-nananom na ɔsomaa ne nana sɛ ɔmmɛfrɛ ne mma. Wɔn baeɛ no, baako kaa sɛ, Me kunu ni, baako nso kaa sɛ, Me kunu ni. Bere a wɔn nyinaa bɔɔ mu wieeɛ no, na ɔpanin no kɔfa sare a wɔfrɛ no _dokum_, na ɔde kyekyere ne mma kɔn mu. Afei obiara kɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn kɔdaeɛ. Na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔɔ akɔhome ne ɔbaa panyin no. Bere a anadwo bɛduruu no, ɔbaa panyin no hyerɛɛ ne fononoo kɔkɔɔ no sɛ ɔne wɔn nkɔkum mmabaa no. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, E! e! e! Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, Ɛyɛ dɛn? Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, Bere a mewɔ mankasa me akuraa ase, na bere a merekɔ ahomegyeɛ nanso mentumi nna, ɛnneɛ maame twitwa yam bi na ɔde gu gya mu, na ɔfa nkotokuo a ɛyɛ kurukuruwa na ɔkɔhwie nsuo gu mu ma me ma menom. Ɔbaa panyin-nananom sii kwan so sɛ wɔbɛkɔ asubɔnten no ho, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kɔtoo nsuo a wɔfrɛ no _dok um_ ɛfiri ɔbaa panyin no mma kɔn mu, na ɔde wɔn kɔsɛn ne nuanom mmea so. Ɔbaa panyin no firi nsuo no mu san baeɛ, na ɔkaa sɛ, Mebɔɔ mmɔden sɛ mɛhwie nsuo (wɔ saa basin no mu), nanso mantumi anhwie. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buaa sɛ, Kɔ na fa dɔteɛ bi, na fa nhaban gu mu, fa hwie gu mu, na fa hwie gu mu. Ɔbaa panyin no kɔyɛɛ saa, na ɔhwiee nsuo guu mu, na ɛyɛɛ yie. Ɔde bae, na oyii yam no fii ogya no mu maa abofra Kwasi Gyinamoa dii, na ɔnom nsu no nso. Afei ɔtow ne ho guu fam na ɔdae. Ɔbaa panyin nanaa sɔre, ɔkae sɛ, Ɛyɛ me. Ɔhyerɛn nano kɔkɔɔ, na ɔne wɔn kɔɔ dan no mu, ɔde ne nsa kaa mmofra no kɔn ho, na nea ne kɔn mu _dokum_ akutu no nna no, ɔde nano hyehye no. Saa kwan yi so na okum mmofra nwɔtwe no. Na onnim sɛ nankasa mma na okum wɔn. Ɛduruu sɛ nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ntɛm pa ara, ntɛm pa ara, Ɔbaa Panyin-nananom no sii kwan sɛ wɔbɛkɔ afuom hɔ. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ ne nuanom mmea no ma wɔdwanee. Ɔbaa Panyin-nananom no san fii afuom hɔ bae. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, Ɔbaa Panyin-nananom e! Ɔbaa Panyin-nananom e! Sɛ ɛnyɛ wo na woyɛ ɔkwasea a, anka wubekum wo mma? Me deɛ, me ne me nuanom rebɛkɔ. Ɔpanyin-nananom kaa sɛ, Ɛnyɛ asɛm biara, nea ɔnim (sɛnea ɔbɛdi wo ho dwuma) no, ɔwɔ hɔ. Ɔkɔeɛ, na Ɔpanyin-nananom nso danee ne ho yɛɛ no ababaawa fɛfɛɛfɛ, na ɔkura gourd-rattle, na ɔkɔɔ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa akuraa ase. Ɔkaa sɛ, The person who can take a boat to pierce this gourd-rattle, I will marry him. Nnipa a wɔwɔ akuraa ase no nyinaa bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ wɔbɛbɔ no, nanso wɔantumi. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa na ɔbɔɔ no. Ɔkaa sɛ, I have married you. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, Me deɛ, merenware wo. Enti ne maame ba no waree no. Ɔkɔɔ ne nkyɛn kɔdaeɛ, nanso ɔtwee nani mmienu no, na ɔne wɔn kɔeɛ. Ɛnna ababaawa no kaa sɛ, "Ɔba-Kwasi-Gyinamoa wɔ hɔ! Metu wo wofa ani mmienu no na me ne wɔn rekɔ. " Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buaa sɛ, "Fa wɔn na kɔ, ɛfiri sɛ nea ɔnim (sɛnea ɔbɛdi wo ho dwuma) no nni hɔ. " Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi, wɔkae sɛ, "Ɔba, kɔ na gye wo ho na fa wo wo wofa ani no san ba. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Merenkɔ. " Wɔhyɛɛ no den, wɔhyɛɛ no den, na (awiei koraa no) ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ. Afei, na Child-Kwasi-Ginamoa nim sɛ ɔbaa panyin no banana yɛ _enceinte_, enti ɔno nso nyaa kyɛw na ɔbɔɔ ne yam, kyekyeree natade guu so, na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ ɔbaa panyin no akuraa ase. Bere a oduu kurow no awiei no, ɔsu. Ɔbaa panyin nana bisaa sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na ɛrekɔ so? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me ne me kunu na yɛreba, na berɛ a ɔduruu bepɔw no atifi no, ɔhwee aseɛ na ɛno maa nani mmienu no firii adi kɔhwee aseɛ. " Ɔbaa panyin nana kaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ saa koraa, yi ne saa ɔmammɔfo a ɔasan aba bio no, Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, ne wɔfaseɛ, ne nani benkum, na yi nso ne nifa. Sɛ wokɔ (wɔ baabi a wo kunu onifirani no gyina) a, ɛsɛ sɛ woka sɛ, Mani tu, mani da hɔ. " Ɛnkyɛe na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nsa aka no, na ɔka sɛ, Wo a wowɔ hɔ, ɔbaa panyin, eyinom yɛ me wɔfaseɛ aniwa a woyii no, na me ne wɔn rekɔ. Ɔbaa panyin, ɔbaa panyin kaa sɛ, Ɛno mfa ho, nea ɔnim (sɛnea ɔne wo bɛdi) no nni hɔ. Child-Kwasi. -Gyinamoa baa fie, na ɔfaa aniwa no na ɔde sii ne wofa kanea dan mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mani tu mmirika, mani da hɔ," na ne wofa no ani buee yie. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi, na mmofra no nyinaa (wɔ akuraa ase) akɔguare wɔ asuo no mu. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Merenhohoro me ho. " Wɔkaa sɛ, "Ebia wowɔ awotwaa dua? " Wɔkaa saa no, ne nufu yɛɛ den, na ɔhohoroo ne ho. Ɛnkyɛe koraa na ɔhuruhuruw kɔɔ nsuo no mu, na ɔbaa panyin, nana, kyeree no, twom! Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔba, Kwasigyinamoa, me nsa aka wo," na ɔne no kɔɔ ne akuraa ase. Afei Nana Panyin no kɔfaa kɛntɛn na ɔkataa so, na ɔfaa mmɛdua nnua, na ɔde sii ogya so. Ɔkaa sɛ, Merekɔ sare abusua no nkyɛn, merekɔ nnuabɔfoɔ mmusuakuo no nkyɛn, na afei mɛba abɛku Child-Kwasi-Ginamoa. Ɔfirii aseɛ, na Child-Kwasi-Ginamoa boaboaa nkokoaa ano na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ bubuu wɔn, pupru! pupru! Nana panyin bi kae sɛ, "Dɛn na woretwe yi? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Tiger-nuts. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Ma me bi. " Obuae sɛ, "Bue me ho. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wo bɛkɔ. " Obuae sɛ, "Dabi, merenkɔ. " Ɔde ne ho bɔɔ mu, na ofii adi. Ɔpanyin no nana rebɔ mmɛw nnuaba. Abofra-Kwasi-Ginamoa faa palm-nut pestle, ɔbɔɔ ɔbaa panyin no banana kum no. Afei oyii ɔbaa panyin no banana no ti nhwi na ɔhyɛɛ no. Nana panyin no san bae, ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na mo nyinaa ho agu fĩ saa? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Makum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa; Mede no ato ogya so. " Saa asɛm yi yɛɛ Nana panyin no dɛ, na ɔsaw de ne nne so: "Ɔba, wokum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, Da bi wobɛyɛ obi. "

Ɔnoa aduane no wieeɛ no, wɔtenaa nkyɛn didiiɛ. Ɔpanyin-baa-nananom splashed the pan (into her mouth with a loud noise), fweo! fweo! Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "You are splashing jellies (into your mouth) made from your grandchild's head. " Ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na woka yi? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Na meka sɛ nkyene nni jellies stew yi mu. " Ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, "Go and fetch some. " Enti ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔfaa bi. Wɔdii aduane no wieeɛ no, nana kumaa no kaa sɛ, "Seesei a mayɛ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa awu no, bra ha, me nana. " Bere a ɔbaeɛ no, ɔfaa sika kɔkɔɔ agudeɛ na ɔhyɛɛ no fɛfɛɛfɛ. Afei, nana kumaa no hwɛɛ nanim kakra. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ah! abofra yi anim ma mekae Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa deɛ no. " Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Nana, dɛn nti, bere a wunim sɛ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa baeɛ na ɔkunkum me abusuafoɔ baawɔtwe no nyinaa (wokasa saa anaa), wo nti ɛma me werɛ how. " Ɔpanyin-baa-nananom kaa sɛ, "Mua wano. " Afei abofra no dwoeɛ ne ho wɔ hɔ wɔ fie hɔ. Nana no yii no adi. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ma menkɔtow gu mfinimfini kuw no so. " Enti Child-Kwasi-Ginamoa soaa no kɔe. Bere a oduu mfinimfini kuw no mu no, oyii sika kɔkɔɔ agude a ɔbea panyin no de siesiee ne ho no nyinaa na ɔkyekyeree no. Afei ɔkaa sɛ, "Ho hɔ, ɔbaa panyin-nananom, ho hɔ, ɔbaa panyin-nananom, me ne no, abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, seesei mprenu so, maba sɛ merebɛkum wo nana, na ɛno akyi no, mayi wo sika kɔkɔɔ agudeɛ. " Ɔbaa panyin-nananom buaa sɛ, "Mekaeɛ sɛ wanim te sɛ abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa deɛ; seesei baabiara a wokɔ no, me ne wo bɛkɔ. " Ɛnna wɔn baanu nyinaa dwanee, kiri kiri! Kiri! Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa duruu ne kuro no mu, na ɔde sika kɔkɔɔ agudeɛ no maa ne mogya-abusuafoɔ, na ɔkɔɔ kwan no ho, na ɔde ne ho siee nkyɛn, na ɔbaa panyin-nananom nso baa hɔ. Ɛno nti, sɛ wotwam wɔ kwan bi so a, wobɛte nnyigyei a ɛyɛ "pe". Ɛno ne ɔbaa panyin-nananom a wɔredi Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa akyi.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
