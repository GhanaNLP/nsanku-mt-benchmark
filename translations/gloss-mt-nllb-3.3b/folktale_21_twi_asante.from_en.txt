Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƆWEA (TREE-BEAR) NE ABOSOM-AKOTERE (ABOSOMAKOTERƐ) NE ƆPATUO (PATU) BAA BƐGYEE WƆN OSU SORONKO

ƆWEA, Dua-Bear (a ɔwɔ abodin) Kwaduampɔn Kyerɛfo, Ɔpatuo, Patu, ne Abosom-Akotere, Abosomakoterɛ (a wɔn abodin ne), Creators death, The take-a-year-to-do-anything-creature, He-walks-very-slow-but-he-will-reach-the-asase-of-white-men-far-away, (saa mmoa yi nyinaa) traa bom pɛn. Afei, na wɔn mu biara nni yere. Na Adobe-tree ankasa tɔn ne ho maa wɔn. Ɛda biara, Adobe dua no sɔre a, na ɔkɔ twɛn yi; da a ɛdi hɔ no, na ɔtwɛn foforo nso; na da a ɛdi hɔ no, na ɔtwɛn foforo nso. Ɛda bi, berɛ a na ɛyɛ Ɔbɔfoɔ no owuo dane (sɛ ɔbɛhyɛ Adobe dwumadie no) na ɔhwehwɛɛ Adobe dua no, na ɛhe na ɔbɛtumi ahu no? Na ɔkɔe sɛ ɔbɛka amaneɛbɔ no akyerɛ Dua-Dua ne Patu. Na wɔkae sɛ, Kɔfrɛ no. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no wuo no maa ne nne so: Adobe o! Adobe o!

Dua-kyekyere no teɛɛm sɛ:

Ɔkɔeɛ no, wankɔ bio; ɔkɔeɛ no, wankɔ bio, wankɔ bio, wankɔ bio!

Na Patu frɛɛ sɛ:

Yɛakari (sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma) na anka yɛbɛtumi atow agu, m! Yɛakari (sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma) na anka yɛbɛtumi atow agu, m! m!

Na ɛno ne abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ mmiɛnsa yi nyinaa osuo mfitiaseɛ.

Eyi, me abasɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, monfa bi nkɔ baabi foforo, na momfa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
