Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƆWEA (DREE-BEAR), ABOSOM-AKOTERE (ABOSOMAKOTERE), NE ƆPATUO (PATU) BAA BƐNYAA WƆN NISO DWUMADIE NO

ƆWEA, Dua-Obere, (a ɔwɔ abodin) Kwaduampɔn Ɔkyerɛwfo, Opatutuo, Patu, ne Abosom-Akotere, Abosomakotere, (a ne abodin ne), Ɔbɔfo no wu, ɔde-afe-bɛyɛ-biribiara-abɔde, ɔkwan-kumaa-kumaa-nanso-ɔbɛduru-asase-a-abibifo-mmarima-akyirikyiri, (mmoa yi nyinaa) boom traa bere bi. Afei, na wɔn mu biara nni ɔyere. Na Adobe dua no tɔn ne ho maa wɔn. Da biara, berɛ a Adobe-dua sɔre no, ɔkɔhwɛɛ wei; Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no ɔhwɛɛ wɔn mu foforɔ; na ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no nso wɔ foforɔ so. Ɛda bi, berɛ a na ɛyɛ Ɔbɔfoɔ-wuo (sɛ ɔbɛhyɛ Adobe dwumadie no) na ɔhwehwɛɛ Adobe-dua no, ɛhe na ɔbɛtumi ahu no? Na ɔfiri hɔ kɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ Dua-bear ne Patu. Na wɔkae sɛ, Kɔfrɛ no. Na Ɔbɔfoɔ no wuo no maa ne nne so sɛ: Adobe o! Adobe o!

Tree-bear no teɛɛ so sɛ:

Ɔkɔeɛ no, ɔnsane mmaeɛ; ɔkɔeɛ no, ɔnsane mmaeɛ, ɔnsane mmaeɛ, ɔsan mmaeɛ!

Na Patu frɛɛ sɛ:

Yɛakari (the money future) na anka yɛbɛtumi atow agu, m! Yɛakari (the money future) na anka yɛbɛtumi atow agu, m! m!

Na ɛno ne nsuo a ɛhyeɛ ahodoɔ mmiɛnsa yi mfitiaseɛ.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
