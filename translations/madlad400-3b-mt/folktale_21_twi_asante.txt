SEDIE OWEA, ABOSOM-AKOTERE NE OPATUO SU

OWEA, Kwaduampon Kyerefo, Opatuo, ne Abosom-Akotere, Odomankoma-wuo, Aferehyia'boa, Onam-bere-bere-be-ko Aburokyire na wo ho. Na ye mu fuara enni ye'. Na Adobe be ton no ho maa yen. Adobe sore a, na wako som wii; edakye a na wako som wii so; edakye a na wako som wii so. Dakoro bi ekoduruu Odoankoma-wuo so, ope Adobe he a na ohu no? Na oko boo Owea ne Opatuo amanee. Na ye see, "Ko fre no." Odomankoma-wuo maa so:

"Adobe o! Adobe o!"

Owea see:

"Okora! omma! oko, omma, omma, omma!"

Na Opatuo see:

"Ye kari gu yi a, m! Ye kari gu yi a, m! m!"

And let the beasts of the field come upon him and kill him.

I'm not sure if I'll be able to do it, but I'm sure I'll be able to see it, I'm sure I'll be able to smell it, I'm sure I'll be able to smell it.
