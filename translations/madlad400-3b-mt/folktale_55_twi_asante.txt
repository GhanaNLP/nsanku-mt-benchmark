SE YOYE A ETENE BAA 'MANM'

NTIKUMA, na okom kesie bi baye, na osiim' se o'ko pe aduane abere ose, Agnya Ananse. Osiim' koo kuro bi so; oko too Nana Aberewa. Aberewa ka kyeree no se, "Me nana, aden na obi mma ha, na w'aba ha?" Ntikuma see, "Okom ntira, na me'bepe biri m'ako di." Nana Aberewa see, "Ko m'afuom' ho, na bayere a ebeka se, 'Tu me, tu me,' ntu no, na bayere a ebeka se, 'Ntu me, ntu me,' no, tu no bra." Ntikuma koeyo. Bayere no bi see, "Tu me, tu me"; ebi so see, "Ntu me, ntu me." Na Ntikuma tuu die osee, "Ntu me, ntu me" no. Ode baa 'fie; osensenye. Na Nana Aberewa see, "Ko to aduane pa no gu, na fa no hono gu kukuom' si 'gya so." Ntikuma sensenye na ode no hono sii 'gya so. Ebeneye, na otoe na ofwie, na ne nyina adane aduane pa. Ntikuma see, "M'awie, na ma me nam me ntom." Nana Aberewa see, "Ko ka kyere Nkwakuo a egu dua no so se, 'Nana Aberewa se mo nyina mo 'ni twe ase." Nkwakuo no see, "Se nye Nana Aberewa ntira a, nkra ye'bebo wo."

Nana Aberewa see, "Me die nye m'asem, na mo mmera mmebo no." Mmoa no sii fam' se ye'bebo no. Nana Aberewa see, "Me Sekan Mfamfinam twa biby ti ma me." Na Mfamfinam atwa biby ti. Ntikuma sesaa biby no nyina, na otuu bayere kaa ho, de siim' baa ne kurom'. Age Ananse hunoo Ntikuma anim' osee, "'Piesie 'kwaba." Osee, "Behye m'akyi." Ntikuma ko hyee ho. Efei Ananse ko yii bayere bako; osee, "Yei die me ye bara a, m'adi." Aso see, "Odomankoma boo adie, w'ahu omwana a oye bara?" Ananse ne no yere ne no mma diye bayere no wieye na eko kaa Age Ananse die. Adeekyeye, Ananse see, "M'aye bara." Na okotenaa afikyire, noaa bayere no die maa no saye. Ntikuma see, "Meko bio." Ananse see, "Me ne wo beko." Ntikuma see, "Me ne wo'nnko." Se Ntikuma ko daye, na Ananse akofa aperetwa, afitifitim' de nso agum', na ode abo Ntikuma ntama ano. Adeekyeye, anopa kabisa, na Ntikuma sii 'kwan so. Anim’ teteye, Ananse pee Ntikuma, onhuno no, oko pee nso no, onoye, na otoaye. Obekoro, Ntikuma na one Nana Aberewa te ho yi. Nana Aberewa see, “Agya ’Barima, amanee?” Ananse see, “’Okom de me, na ma me biri nni.” Nana Aberewa see, “Biribi die me nya no he?” Ananse see, “Me na wo bu me ’wasea, me ’ba Ntikuma mpo baye, w’ama no bi.” Nana Aberewa see, “Ko afuom’ ho, bayere a ebeka se, ‘Tu me, tu me,’ ntu no, na die ebeka se, ‘Ntu me, ntu me,’ no, ono na tu no.” Ananse see, “A! nkwaseasem, na wo’ka kyere me, adie se odo wo a, wo se me die me tan no, me nte bi da?” Na Ananse koo afuom’ ho. Oduruu ho, bayere bi see, “Tu me, tu me,” ebi soso see, “Ntu me, ntu me.” Na watu die osee, “Tu me, tu me,” no, na ode baa ’fie. Obe sensene wieye; Nana Aberewa see, “Ko to aduane pa no gu, na fa ne hono gu kukuom’ si ’gya so.” Ananse see, “A! adieben nsemhunu na wo’ka kyere me yi, Odomankoma boo adie, ye’to aduane pa twene, na y’afa ne hono?” Ananse faa aduane pa no, de sii ’gya so, na ode ehono no bako ato so. Aduane no beneye, otoe na ne mother adane aboo, na ehono no bako a ode too soo no, adane aduane pa. Aabe Ananse see, “E! se oba tofuo yi biby.” Na Ananse ko sesaa ehono no, de besii ’gya so. Aduane no beneye, okotoe na adane bayere seyi’a fokye! Osee, “Nana Aberewa yei die ma me nam me mmo so.” Nana Aberewa see, “Ko fwe dua no so, Afuo bi gu so, wo kor’a, wo se, ‘mo’ biby’.” Ananse see, “Oba yi! me ne Afuo yi nni asem, na me nkodidi y’atem sen?” Nana Aberewa see, “Ko na asem biara ba, me ara m’asem.” Ananse simm’ koye. Osee, “Mo Afuo a mo gu nnua so yi, Nana Aberewa se mo’ biby.” Mmoa no see, “Se nye Nana Aberewa ntira a, nkra ye’bebo wo.” Nana Aberewa see, “Enee mo mmera mmebo no.” Ananse see, “Aberewa sa nye.” Na biby no gu so, ye'bebo Ananse. Aberewa see, “Me Sekan 'Famfinam twitwa ye'ti.” Tintin! Aberewa see, "Adwobere!" Na Ananse ate. Efei na Ananse sesaa nam no baa'fie, na Aberewa twee ne ho kidogo. Na Ananse awambia sekno ahye ne botom', na ode adwane aba ne kurom'. Oduruu ne kurom', osee, "Ntikuma, sekna na me nsa aka no nti mo mma yenko wuram'." Na ye'siim' kuko, na ye'ko hunoo Nkokotee. Ananse see, "Ntikuma ko didi y'atem." Ntikuma koreye osee, "Nkokotee mo ho fi." Nkokotee see, "Se nye Kwaku Ananse na ne 'ba ne wo, nkra yebeba abebo wo." Kwaku Ananse see, "Mo mmebo no, onnye kora, ne dwaye nye agoro." Nkokotee see ye'bebo no, Kwaku Ananse see, "Aberewa Sekan 'Famfinam, twitwa ye'ti ma me." O! na Sekan no e'gu so, na some animals no mother 'ti aye. Na efei egu so some nuwa. Kwaku Ananse see, "Ai! wo'ye dien ni, me see twa animals ti, wo so w'atwa aye, na efei emu yei?" Na Ntikuma fwe ha; osee, "Adwobere!" Kwaku Ananse ante, na Sekan no baa fam', na Ananse ako fa. He'san baa 'fie. Adeekyeye Kwaku Ananse see ye'ko Nyankonpon ho. Ntikuma see, "Agya Sekan yi ntira' nnko." Ananse see, "Fa so' ho'ra!" Ntikuma see, "Kor'e." Ananse siim' koo Nyankonpon ho, ko ka kyeree no se, "Nana, nne die se kowa ka wo a, me nko'a, Kwaku Ananse, metwa yen nyina ti doko' m'ama wo." Nyankonpon see, "Yei die aseda na ewom', na edom kesie bi na ejiya kurotia no, na kor'e!" Ananse see, "Mo mfa nsa ne odwane bako." Seisei na ye'de aba. Ananse sii 'kwan so; Ananse asked the 'dom no se, "Mo na mo see mo ne Nyankonpon beko?"
