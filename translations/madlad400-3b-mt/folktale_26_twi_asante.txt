“Nye me Oka’ Abena, apimpim-a-ko-ha, apimpim-aba-ha?

Mahuri magira datahin so, masu mafre wo.

Kill me nanase aburobia.

Aberefi Sempa me yere no, Abuya.”

Na osuo anto. Na Opitire so aba, osee:

“My name is Opitire Abena, apimpim-a-ko-ha, apimpim-aba-ha?

Mahuri magira datahin so, masu mafre wo.

Kill me nanase aburobia.

Aberefi Sempa me yere no, Abuya.”

Na osuo anto. Na Bonko so aba, osee:

“I’m Bonko Abena, apimpim-a-ko-ha, apimpim-aba-ha?

Mahuri majiya datahin so, masu mafre wo. Mate me nanase aburobia.

Aberefi Sempa me yere no, Abuya.”

Na osuo anto. Mmoa nyina besuye, na eko kaa Apupuo. Osee, “Momma me mmesu bi.” Na ye see, “So’ ho y’asu annye yie, na wo dee na ebeye den?” Osee, “Momma me nsu.” Na yeboroo no se ke keree no pen se eransu. Na Apupuo se, “Mesu.” Na ye see, “Su!” Na omaa so:
