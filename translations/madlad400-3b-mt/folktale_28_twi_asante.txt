And the castle was against the mountains. And the mountains said, "Me 'ba yi na me'hita no yi, me 'ba gyaa no 'fie, na deeben na aka no, na ode waterka tiridi tiridi? " And Ohema kiere ne nkoa a edi ne'nkye no mma no. And ye'anya no ; na ode waterka'a, ko too nsuom', na Kra so ko toom' bi. Ye'ko toom', na Ohema 'ba danee Oka', na dua ko ; na Kra so danee Okobo, na ani ho nsensansensan. Oka' dua koko no, ene Ohema 'ba sirikye 'tam a emo no a ode ko too nsuom' ; na Okobo soso, ene 'donko Kra, na ani ho nsensansensan no.
