SE YOYE A WOKO DANM' ANADWO A, NA TUMM

Ye'see Kuntukurunku ne Kuntunamoa na ye'firii pata befwee fam'. Ye'see, "Asase, wo tetere." Asase see, "Aden nti na mo se me tere? " Ye'see, "Wo mmo amena mma ye'nnam' ntira." Asase ka kyeree 'Prae se, "W'ano kokobini." Osee, "Aden na wo didi m'atem? " Asase see, "M'adi a m'adi, na wo nte asee a, gyae." 'Prae ka kyeree Abena se, "Wo, fa-ko-guo." Abena see, "Aden na wo di m'atem? " 'Prae see, "M'adi." Abena so ka kyeree Sumina se, "Wo, gyesie." Sumina see, "Aden na wo di m'atem?" "Osee, "M'adi w'atem, na wo nte asee a, gyae." Sumina so, ka kyeree Abe so se, "Wo koman' lord." Abe see, "Aden na wo di m'atem? " Sumina see, "M'adi, na wo nte asee a, gyae." Abe so ka kyeree Tetea se, "W'aso tuntum." Tetea see, "Aden na wo di m'atem? " Abe see, "M'adi w'atem na wo nte asee a, gyae." Tetea so okoo Esono ho, ko wuraa ne nanm' ; na yeege Esono ho. Na ose o'tu ne, na otiea Tamiriwa na ase, na Tamiriwa na ase e'firi nsuo, na ode ko yee efan ho. Nana Aberewa duane a odie ne 'fan, na Tamiriwa de n'ani nsuo akoe ye ho. 'Kraman e'twam' na ofree 'Kraman na ode 'gya soaa no. 'Kraman o'koro, onyaa bese paeem' awiri de ko too Okagya bon ano. Na Okagya so faa bese no to boo Onyina. Onyina so bo fuye. Obefwe se akoda bi nam ho, na oyii ne ban boo no kum no. Se akoda no Nyankonpon 'ba. Enee eho ara ye'bekyeree Onyina, de no koo Nyankonpon anim'. Ye'bisaa no se, "Amanee?" Onyina see, "Me si me babi, Okagya, akoda te se no, maa bese soo na ode abebo me, na me so, akoda yi e'twam' me see Okagya, na me yii me ban boo no, na se wo 'ba'a." Nyame see, "Momfa no nko to dam', na mo mfre Okagya mma no mmera." Okagya baye; osee, "Nana, wo nim se m'akyidie ene bese, na Okraman akofa abeto me bon ano, na me see m'to atwene na eko boo Akora Onyina." Nyame see, "Mo mfa no nko to dam', na mo mfre 'Kraman mmera." Ye'ko faa 'Kraman baye. Okraman see, "Nana, me nam me babi, monfwe se me tee, na Nana Aberewa de 'gya asoa me; na Okagya, Nana Aberewa ado n'afuo a odidim' onee oko da afuo nom' ne bonm', na me na Aberewa de 'gya asoa me; sa nti na me so me de bese too ne bon ano." Nyame see, "Mo mfa no nko to dam', na mo nko fre Nana Aberewa mmera." Nana Aberewa baye. Osee, "E! me a me nananom, mo nfwe m'anom', me ntimi nwe nam, efan na me die; me so me dware Bosompra; me gindi Tamiriwa; Okraman nko'a na onim, na oka kyeree Tamiriwa maa ode n'ano nsuo beyee efan a me die ho. Sa nti na me de 'gya soaa no." Nyame see, "O! Osee, "Nana me da me babi, fwe, Esono kakraka yi betiaa m'ani ase, na me ko faa efan me pepaye. Na se me nim no no." Nyame see, "Mo mfa no nto dam', na mo nko fa Esono mmera." Ye'ko faa Esono baye. Esono see, "Nnansa yi me te ho'a, na me weree ye me hene; me ko fwe a, sebe, Tetea 'kunyea yi na wako hyem' se o'pe nam awe na waye kesie; na me se me'tu me nan, na ako tia Tamiriwa." Nyame see, "Mo mfa no nto duam' na mo nko fa Tetea mmera." Tetea baye'a, osee, "Nana, Abe wo ho yi, se eso a, na aye tumm; na se me, Tetea, me fa ho a, ebere. Nano, me wo ho'a, Abe ka kyeree me se m'aso tutum, na me ye ketewa, na me so se me'ko Esono ho m'ako pe nam adi se meye kesie." Nyame see, "Mo mfa no nto dam', na mo nko fre Abe mma no mmera." Abe baye; osee, "Nana m'asem ne se: me ne Sumina na etee, se ewura no ye'de bere no, na se ontimi a, na m'agye bi m'agu me kaman'; efeidie odidii m'atem se me koman' wura. Sa nti na me so m'ani bereye, na me didii Tetea so atem." Nyame see, "Mo mfa no nto dam', na mo nko fre Sumina." Sumina baye; osee, "Nana, Abena na ye'sesa wura 'fie a, ye'de ma no, na ono so de abere me. When I told her nothing, she said, 'Sumina, you go-see'; I said, 'a', and Abe said, 'Abe, you're on my side and I'm on your side and I'm on your side.' When I told her nothing, she said, 'You go-guo'.
