Kɛ́ wɔye wɔbua mɛi ni yɔɔ asafo lɛ mli lɛ, no tsɔɔ akɛ wɔsumɔɔ amɛ waa. (Heb. 10:24, 25) Mɛɛ gbɛ nɔ wɔbaanyɛ wɔtsɔ nɔ wɔye wɔbua mɛi ni yɔɔ asafo lɛ mli lɛ?

• Bi Yehowa akɛ ebaaye ebua bo ni ofee nɔ ko ni he hiaa lɛ, ni ebaaye ebua mɛi krokomɛi hu ni amɛye amɛbua amɛ.—Ps. 35:18.

Kɛ́ okɛɛ sane ko yɛ ojwɛŋmɔ mli lɛ, mɛi krokomɛi baaná nɔmimaa akɛ okɛɛ amɛ nɔmimaa lɛ, ni amɛ hu amɛbaaná nɔmimaa akɛ okɛɛ amɛ nɔmimaa lɛ.

• Kpãa gbee yɛ nɔ ko he. Ŋmalɛ ni kpãa gbee kɛ nɔ ko ni sa kadimɔ waa ji nɔ ni woɔ mɛi hewalɛ ni amɛyeɔ amɛbuaa mɛi krokomɛi ni amɛyeɔ amɛbuaa amɛ.—1 Korintobii 10:19.

• Kpãa gbee yɛ nɔ ko he. Kpãa gbee yɛ nɔ ko he. Kpãa gbee yɛ nɔ ko he. Kpãa gbee yɛ nɔ ko he. Kpãa gbee yɛ nɔ ko he.

Kɛ́ mɔ ko bi bo akɛ okɛɛ nɔ ko lɛ, obaanyɛ okɛɛ nɔ ko ni baanyɛ aye abua bo ni ofee nɔ ko ni sa kadimɔ waa.—Abɛi 10:21.

Ŋmalɛ ni ji enɛ ji nɔ ni ajieɔ kpo yɛ jeŋ fɛɛ, ni ajieɔ lɛ kɛjɛɔ mɛi ni kɛ amɛhe woɔ nitsumɔ lɛ mli lɛ adɛŋ. Kɛ́ osumɔɔɔ ni ojie nɔ ko kpo lɛ, kwɛmɔ donate.jw.org.
