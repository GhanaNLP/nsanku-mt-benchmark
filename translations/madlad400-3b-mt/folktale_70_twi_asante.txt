Ye' nse se, nse se o

MMORO

MMERANTEE miensa na ye'wo ho; ye'nnyina mpena bako. Na mmerantee miensa yi firii Bontuku koo Odwa se ye'ko to mpoa. Yeduruu Odwa no, na obako wo abwefwe bi. Omaa abwefwe no so, na otee aduro soo abwefwe no so na ofweem'. Ofwee se ye'mpena no awuo, na oda pato soo. Osee, "Yanom ye' mpena no awu." Obako see, "Mema y'aduru Bontuku seisei, na y'ako sie no." Obako too see, "Se wo timi ma ye'duru ho a, me'nyane no seisei." And the obako kotee aduro; ode yenie yen nkotodwe, na ye' siim'. Seisei ara na y'duru ho. And he went up into the house, and stood there a long time; and he took his clothes, and stood up, and fell on the ground. And he cried, "Son, son, son!" And he fell on the ground.
