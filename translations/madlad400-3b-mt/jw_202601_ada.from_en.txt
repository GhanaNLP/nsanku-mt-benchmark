Kɛ́ okane Biblia lɛ kɛ hiɛdɔɔ lɛ, no baanyɛ aye abua bo ni osusu nibii ni yɔɔ mli lɛ ahe jogbaŋŋ. Kɛ́ okane Biblia lɛ kɛ ohefatalɔ ko teŋ lɛ, obaakɛɛ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ bɔ ni ebaafee ekane Biblia lɛ.

• Kpãtã nibii ni yɔɔ mli lɛ anɔ. Kpãtã nɔ ni mɛi ni yɔɔ mli lɛ susuɔ kɛ nɔ ni amɛnaɔ. Kɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, bi ohe akɛ: ‘Mɛni hewɔ mɔ lɛ kɛɛ nɔ ko loo efeɔ nɔ ko tamɔ nɔ ni efeɔ lɛ? Mɛni mibaafee kɛfee kɛ́ miná nɔ ko tamɔ nɔ ni miná lɛ?’
