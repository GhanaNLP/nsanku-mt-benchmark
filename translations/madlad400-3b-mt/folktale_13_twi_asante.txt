SE EYOYE A AKOKONINI ANO EYE FEA

AGA ANANSE and one of her friends, Afudotwedotwe, live in Nyiwan-konfwea, Tikonokono. Ananse says, "I don't care about my own farm." Enie nkoda yi siim'; ye nam, nam, nam, na ye ko too odoma no bi na abede, na nkoda no koo asee. Afudotwedotwe se, Nyiwan-konfwea se, "Foro ko te bi bra." Osee, "E! me foro a me nan bebubu." Tikonokono se, "Afudotwedotwe wo so foro." Ose, "E! me foro a m'furu bepae, na wo ara foro." Na Tikonokono foroye. When the odoma 'ba ate, osee, “Aye yie?” The crooked see, “Aa.” And the crooked see, “Aye yie?” The crooked see, “Before you tiri ase na fwe.” Osee older ne tiri ase, na wafwe, na ne tiri no kasa fam’, kum! Afudotwedotwe see, osere, na n’afuru paeye, ta! Nyiwankonfwea see ofuntum mmirika abeka akyere ose, na ne nan be bubu kaa pampim’ nyuma. Akokonini edware ne kra, osee obeto ne nkosua-hono agu, obefwe Nyiwankonfwea na wafwe ase na ne nan abubu aka pampim’ nyumare’i. Ose osere, ene n’ano fea:

“Ose osere, Ene n’anno orribile.

Ose osere, Ene n’ano fea.”

’Nti na mpan’infuo se, “Se obi fwe ase a, ko fwe no, na nsere.” Na Akokonini dee oyoo sa ko seree ’nti na n’ano yee fea, na nkra ’kane no na n’ano fe-fe fe-fe.
