The COVID-19 pandemic has affected the economic situation of all sectors and Ghana is not alone in this. I am pleased to say that we are working hard to address these problems and we are working on them with great dedication. In fact, all sectors have appreciated our efforts. The Government has taken steps to address the problems of unemployment in order to ensure that our lives do not return to the previous state.

In addition, in response to the challenges and opportunities posed by the pandemic, the Government has launched the COVID-19 Pandemic Impact Assessment, Recovery and Resilience Plan with a budget of 100 billion Ghanaian shillings under the leadership of President Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo.
