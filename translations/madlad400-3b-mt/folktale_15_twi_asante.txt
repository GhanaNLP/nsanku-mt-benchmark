Ye so oba bi na one ho, and okoo Abe bi a esi nsuom' ho se oko pe awoo. And okoye, and Abe see, "Me ma wo bi, but akoda a me de no bema woo no, osode a, onnye fwe." Osee, "Me pe." Anne four, anne two, and onyinsenee na owoye. Anansesem 'ba a enkye nyin, seisei na wa ye 'pan'in. Na oni ka kyeree no see, "Oman yim' wo nkyi fwe ge Abe nko'a, na mforo bi da." Osee, "Ena mate."
