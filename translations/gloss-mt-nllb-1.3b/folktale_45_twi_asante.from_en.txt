Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐ KƆKƆSAKYI, ƆPƐTƐ NO, KASA WƆ ABƐBUO AHODOƆ MU KYERƐ BƆNEKYERƐFOƆ, PATAKU NO, ƆTE NNEƐMA ASE.

Na Pataku BƆNEKYERƐFOƆ te hɔ, na wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ne ɛna panin awu. Pataku no suanee, suanee, suanee, na ofii hɔ kɔsrɛɛ Kɔkɔsakyi, Ɔpɛtɛ no; Akɔkɔ, Hɛn no; Odwan, Odwan no; Nkruma, Agyinamoa no; ne Ɔkraman, Kraman no, sɛ wɔne no nkɔ ne maame ayi ase. Wɔn mmoa no nyinaa fii aseɛ kɔɔ, na wɔne no kɔɔ. Bere a wɔduruu hɔ no, wɔwiee ayi amanneɛ no, na nea aka ne sɛ wɔbɛhohoro wɔn ho. Wɔkɔɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ Pataku sɛ, "Hohoro, na yɛn nso yɛnhohoro yɛn ho. " Pataku buaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, me ɛna panin da fam, na meremfi me ho da kɔsi sɛ da no ayiyɛ bɛwie. " Ne nnamfofoɔ kaa sɛ, "Wei yɛ asɛm a ɛyɛ duru a yɛahu no (efiri sɛ na ɛkyerɛ sɛ wɔn nso, ɛsɛ sɛ wɔdi mmuada nna aduanan). Da biara na wɔsɔre, na wɔn afurum apae (ɛkɔm), nanso Pataku deɛ, ɔkoraa ahoɔden a edi mu. Afei, da bi a Ɔpɛtɛ no kaa sɛ, "Ah, saa Pataku no, menim no; saa berɛ yi ara na ɔsɔre kɔwe ne maame funu. " Ɛduruu anwummere no, Kɔkɔsakyi, Ɔpɛtɛ no, huruwii kɔɔ nanim (na ne ntaban no nnyigyei yɛ) fA, na ɔbɛtenaa dua tenten bi a ɛwɔ hɔ wɔ nsaman-saman kwaebirentu no mu, na ɛhɔ na ɔhunuu Pataku a ɔde ne ho rekyinkyin. Na ɔbaa, na ɔkɔɔ ɔdamoa no so, ɔbɔɔ, ɔbɔɔ, na ɔyii ne maame funu no, na ɔkɔweɛ no. Ɔpɛtɛ no tenaa hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Pataku, mo ne yɔ, yɛma wo aseda, nanso ɛyɛ aseda a yɛdi ho fɛw. Pataku kaa sɛ, Me srɛ wo, nka eyi nkyerɛ obiara; biribiara a wobɛka akyerɛ me no, me bɛte wo aseɛ ase na mayɛ ho biribi ntɛm ara. Ɔpɛtɛ no faa wimhyɛn kɔɔ nanim, faa (yɛɛ ne ntaban no nnyigyei), na ɛbaa fie. Ɛnkyɛree biara na Pataku no baeɛ. Ɔpɛtɛ no ka kyerɛɛ ne nnamfofoɔ sɛ, "Ɛnnɛ da no, sɛ ɛbɛba sɛ ɛyɛ den sɛ ɔboɔ a yɛbɛnya a, yɛbɛdidi; ɛno akyi no, yɛbɛkɔ yɛn akuraa. " Mmoa no kaa sɛ, "Akyinnyeɛ? " Ɔpɛtɛ no nso buae sɛ, "You may look to me to arrange it. " Ɔpɛtɛ no kaa sɛ, "Come, let us go. " Wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ Hyena fie. Ɔpɛtɛ no kaa sɛ, Pataku, tie na menka nkyerɛ wo sɛ, ɛnnɛ yɛpɛ sɛ yɛdi, na wo nso, woredidi; ɛno akyi no, yɛbɛkɔ yɛn akuraa da, na dɛn na wowɔ ka? Pataku kaa sɛ, Mepene so, mebedidi, na wo nso, di. Ntɛm ara na Pataku no kɔkyeree odwan mmiɛnsa, de kyekyeree wɔn ɛkɔm, na ɔde maa ne nnamfofoɔ sɛ wɔnni, na ɔno nso, ɔkɔgyee sordook. ome. Ɛnna ne nnamfofoɔ no tuu kwan san baa fie. Ɛno nti na yɛwɔ abebuo sɛ, "Sɛ Ɔpɛtɛ no de abɛbuo ahodoɔ kasa kyerɛ Pataku no a, ɔte ne ntease a ahintaw. "

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
