SƐNEA BABADUA NO NYAA NSƆMU (WƆ NE DUA SO)

Ɔbaa bi (baako) nyinsɛnee, na ɔkɔɔ abosom nkumaa no mu baako nkyɛn kɔbisaa no (nsɛm fa abofra no ho). Na onyankopɔn ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "It is probable that when you bear the abofra it will die, but I will do all I can to give it to you, but the adeɛ which I must tell you is this, the abofra is (really) the abofra of a Ɔhemmaa up there with Niamey, the Ewiem-onyankopɔn, and when you bear it, don't let it do anything, but when you wake up, you must bathe it, ngo it, and take it, and set it on a akonnwa. " Of a tru na ɔwoo no. (Like all) mmofra in Anansesɛm no, ɛnkyɛ na ɛnyin. Wɔfrɛɛ abofra akosua dentaa. Sɛnea obosom kumaa no kyerɛɛ no, maame no, berɛ a ɔsɔre, ɔhohoroo no, faa no ngo, na ɔnoaa biribi maa no dii, na afei ɔkɔɔ ne afuo so. Afei, da bi a na ɔnni berɛ a ɔbɛnoa biribi adi no, ɔde ahyɛdeɛ maa ne afenaa Ɔkra sɛ, "Merekɔ afuo, enti noa biribi na fa ma Akosua Dentaa nni. " Na maame no kɔɔ so. Afei, berɛ a Ɔkra wiee borɔdeɛ no, ɔkae sɛ, Akosua Dentaa, bra ha na pound saa fufu yi, wo ne wo Ɔhemmaa-Ɛna abakɔsɛm no. Ɔkaa sɛ, Merenkɔ. Ɔkra kaa sɛ, Bra, wubedi, wo ne wo ahantan. Na Akosua Dentaa sɔre kɔɔ, ɔkɔgyee pestle no, ɔbɔɔ fufu no. Ɛduruu berɛ a ɔwieeɛ no ara pɛ, ɔkɔdaa dan no mu, faa ne ntoma, na ɔde kataa ne etire so. Ɔkra kɔɔ (akyi no), ɔkae sɛ, "Denta! " annyɛ hwee. Ɔyii ntoma no firii ne etire so, Akosua Dentaa awu, ɔde ntoma no sii ne so. Ɛnkyɛree biara, maame no firii afuo mu baeɛ. Ɔbaa no, wamma (ntɛm ara) anhwehwɛ ne abofra no, na mmom ɔkɔɔ Mukyea ho kɔwiee aduannoa. Ɛnna ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔkra, ɛhe na Denta wɔ? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Nana, ne aduane a me no ayɛ na mede asie hɔ no mpo, ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ. " Ɛnna maame no kaa sɛ, "Me wate. " Ɛnna maame no de ahyɛdeɛ kɔɔ dan no mu sɛ ɔrekɔfa ntoma bi na ɔrekɔhwehwɛ ne abofra no wɔ abɔnten so. Bere a ɔkɔɔ dan no mu no, na adeɛ tuntum bi da hɔ a ɛwɔ ebuo. Ɔmaa ntoma no so, na ɔhunuu sɛ Akosua Dentaa awu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔkra, wose Denta akɔ abɔnten so, na afei ha na ɔwɔ yi, wawu anaa? " Ɔkra kaa sɛ, "Nana me nhuu no da. " Ɛnna ɔbaa no kyekyeree ntoma abɔso wɔ nasene so, na ɔfaa ne (dead) abofra no wɔ nakyi, na ɔfirii abɔnten so bae, na ɔsu; na akuraa no mu Etire bae, na ɔkae sɛ, "Fa wo abofra no na yɛnkɔsie no. " Ɔbaa no buae sɛ:

"Na me ne me abofra akosua dentaa, Na me nyinsɛ no, me ne no kɔɔ nsuo-gazer no kɔbisabisaa.

Wɔka sɛ, 'Ɛyɛ Ɔhemmaa Ɛna ba.'

Mepɛ sɛ obi kɔka sɛ, The Queen Mother abofra, Siankwa Adamowa abofra, oyi, me abofra no, meremfa no nsie ha. "

Na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ no, ɔkɔɔ akuraase bi, na bere a ɔduruu hɔ no, akuraa no Etire kaa sɛ, Fa abofra no firi (wo back) na bra na memfa nsie no, efisɛ yɛbara adeɛ a ɛte saa. Nanso ɔbaa no kaa sɛ:

"Na me ne me abofra akosua dentaa, Na me nyinsɛ no, me ne no kɔɔ nsuo-gazer no kɔbisabisaa.

Wɔka sɛ, 'Ɛyɛ Ɔhemmaa Ɛna ba.'

Mepɛ sɛ obi kɔka sɛ, The Queen Mother abofra, Siankwa Adamowa abofra, oyi, me abofra no, meremfa no nsie ha. "

Na ɔsan fii hɔ bio. Ɔante, na ɔnam, na ɔnam, faa kwaebirentuo bi mu, na afei deɛ, na abofra no aporɔ, na ɔyii no guu nakyi, na ɔsan baa fie. Na akuraa no mu Etire bisaa no sɛ, "Ɛhe na abofra no wɔ? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Mede no kɔtoo hɔ. " Akura no mu Etire kaa sɛ, "Woyɛ animguase, sɛ woama kwan wɔ ha a, anka ɛsɛ sɛ yɛsie no wɔ nsaman-saman kwaebiretutu mu, ɛno na ɛbɛyɛ nea ɛfata. " Saa na mpanimfoɔ kyerɛ sɛ, "Sɛ wo abofra wu a, ɛnde di "moderation", mma no nhaw wo. Na saa ɔbaa no yɛɛ saa, na ɔtwee ne abofra no kɔeɛ bere a na ɛnsɛ sɛ ɔyɛ saa. " Na afei ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "Ɔkra yi deɛ, monfa no nkɔto mɔta mu na monto no mmopaeɛ mma me. " Ɛnna nkuraasefoɔ no kɔɔ hɔ (baabi a na Ɔkra wɔ, na na ɛyɛ nnyigyei sɛ wɔrebɔ no mmopaeɛ) Pu! Pu! Pu! na wɔbɔɔ no mmopaeɛ kɔsii sɛ ɔdwoɔ paa. Na afei nea na aka ne "the ayamye so one" no; sɛ wɔbobɔ no a, na ɛyɛ den, na sɛ wɔbobɔ no nso a, na ɛsɔre kɔbata Babadua no ho. Ɛno nti na Babadua no wɔ apɔ (on its dua), kan no na ɛyɛ trom, kyerɛ sɛ, ɛyɛ torotoro koraa.

Ɛyi, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (ana) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi afa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a wode bɛkamfo me (asɛmka no.
