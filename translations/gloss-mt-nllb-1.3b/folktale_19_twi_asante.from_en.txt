Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBƐBAA SƐ WƆBƆƆ MMOFRA MPABOƆ (DI KAN)

WƆKA SƐ (bere bi) na ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bae, na Agya Ananse, Ananse, ne ne yere "Aso", ne ne mmofra, Ntikuma, Nyiwankɔnfwea (Thin-shanks), Afudotwedotwe (Belly-like-to-burst), ne Tikononkono (Big-head-big), sii atenaeɛ ketewa bi na wɔtenaa mu. Ɛda biara na Ananse kɔ de aduane, wuram bayere, na wɔnoa na wɔdi. Afei da bi, Agya Ananse kɔɔ nwura no nkyɛn; ɔhunuu sɛ aduan dɛdɛ bi na ɛsi hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. Aduane no kaa sɛ, Me din nnyɛ beautiful. Ananse no kaa sɛ, Wo din de dɛn? Ɛbuaa sɛ, Me din de me, Fill-up-some-and-eat. Ananse no kaa sɛ, Fill up some that I may see. Aduane no hyɛɛ mu ma ne nnuadewa ngo soup, na Kwaku Ananse dii ne nyinaa. Bere a ɔwieeɛ no, ɔbisaa aduane no sɛ, Dɛn ne wo akyiwadeɛ? Aduane no buaa sɛ, Metan otuo tɛtea, ne gourd kuruwa ketewa. Ananse no faa nkyɛnsee fie no, na wɔkɔtoo silin no so. Ɛnna Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ nwura so de aduane bae, na "Aso", bere a na wawie aduannoa no, ɔfrɛɛ Kwaku Ananse, na ɔkae sɛ, Oh, wo deɛ, ɛyɛ ahiade ankasa, me deɛ, me ne akoraa no, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ me ne aduane di, wo deɛ ne mmofra yi deɛ, ɛyɛ ade a ɛho hia ankasa; sɛ wani abere a, na maso adwo (wkwadwom no nnyeeɛ). Wɔwieeɛ no, na nwansena no deɛ, ɛyɛ pa ara. SSed kɔɔ aseseɛ no akyi, na ɔkɔɔ (na ɔbɛtenaa) silin a aduane no wɔ so no so. Ɔkaa sɛ, Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. Ɛbuaa sɛ, Me din nni beautiful. Ɔkaa sɛ, Wo din deɛn? Ɛkaa sɛ, Me frɛ wo, Fill-up-some-and-eat. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Fill up some ma menhu. Na ɛhyɛɛ prɛte a ɛyɛ groundnut soup ma, na Kwaku Ananse dii. Ɛda biara a ɔsɔre no, na ɛte saa. Ɛnneɛ, Ntikuma hyɛɛ no nsow sɛ ne agya no nnyinii, wɔ nokwasɛm a ɛyɛ sɛ ɔne no nnidi nkabom no ho nitan mu, na Ntikuma kuraa ne dɔn wɔ ne agya ho na ɔhunuu nea agya no anya ho nsɔmu. Bere a agya no kɔɔ nwura no mu no, Ntikuma foro kɔɔ soro (of the silin) no na ɔhunuu aduane no. Ɔfrɛɛ ne maame ne ne anuanom, na wɔkɔɔ soro. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. Ɛkae sɛ, Me din nni beautiful. Ɛkae sɛ, Ɛnne wo din de dɛn? Ɛkae sɛ, Me din ne Fill-up-some-and-eat. Ɔkaa sɛ, Fill up a little so that I may see. Na aduane no de nnuadewa ngo soup hyɛɛ mu ma kɔsii ma sɛe. Ntikuma ne ne maame ne ne anuanom no dii ma ne nyinaa wieeɛ. Na afei Ntikuma bisaa aduane no, Dɛn na wuyi fi hɔ? Aduane no kae sɛ, Metan otuo tɛtea ne kora ketewa. Ntikuma ka kyerɛɛ Afudotwedotwe sɛ, Kɔ na fa bi mma me. Na ɔde bae, na ɔfaa otuo tɛtea no kaa no (aduane no), na ɔde kora ketewa no kaa no (aduane). Ɛnna wɔn nyinaa sian bae. Na Agya Ananse (mean-time) asan aba (from the nwura with the wuram bayere). "Aso" wiee wɔn aduane no. Wɔfrɛɛ Kwaku Ananse, ɔbuaa sɛ, Ebia na woante nea mekaeɛ no; mekaeɛ sɛ, ɛfiri da yi kɔ, sɛ meba fie aduane a, wobɛtumi adidie, efisɛ wone ebinom na ɛwɔ ahiadeɛ. Aso ne ne mmofra no dii. Agya Ananse hohoroo ne ho, ɔkɔɔ silin no so, ɔkae sɛ, Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ. Kommm koraa! Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ. Kommm koraa! Agya Ananse kae sɛ, Ah! ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ akawnto sɛ saa ntoma yi nyɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ, mɛkɔ akɔfa Ɔyokofoɔ kente no bi abɛhyɛ. Na ɔsii fam sɛ ɔrekɔfa Ɔyoko abusuakuo kente ntoma no bi abɛhyɛ. Ɔhyɛɛ ne mpaboa, ɔkɔɔ soro hɔ (san). Ɔkaa sɛ, Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. Kommm koraa! Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. Kommm koraa! Ɔtwaa ne ho hyiaeɛ na ɔhunuu otuo tɛtea ne kora ketewa bi a ɛhyɛ mu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Its not one adeɛ, its not two nneɛma (i.e. a akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho) no, ɛyɛ Ntikuma. Kwaku Ananse bubuu aduane no, na ɔbaa fam; ɔyii Ɔyoko kente ntoma no sii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ nwura no so. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu adeɛ bi a ɛyɛ fɛ papa (adeɛ a wɔfrɛ no) Mpere, abapra no, sɛn hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Oh, ɛyɛ nwanwa! saa adeɛ yi yɛ fɛ sene deɛ ɛtwa toɔ no; saa abapra yi yɛ fɛ. Abapra no kae sɛ, Me din nnyɛ beautiful. Ɔ (Ananse) kae sɛ, Nneɛ wo din de dɛn? Ɛkae sɛ, Me din ne Abiridibrada (a ɛne sɛ, swish-and-raise-weals). Na ɔkae sɛ, Switch me kakra ma mehu. Na abapra no bɛtoo no so, Switch, swish, swish. Agya Ananse teɛɛ mu sɛ, Pui! pui! Anomaa bi a ɔwɔ anomaa bi a ɔte ne nkyɛn no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, Say Adwobere (a ɛkyerɛ sɛ, ɛyɛ nwini na ɛyɛ hare). Na Kwaku Ananse ka sɛ, Adwobere. Na abapra no gyaee no bɔ. Na Kwaku Ananse de abapra yi baa fie; na ɔkɔtoo silin no so. "Aso" wiee aduane no, ɔkae sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse, bra na di. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Esiane sɛ woda so ara wɔ ha (asase so), ebia wunni ɛtokuro biara wɔ waso (na woante nea mekae no), merenni. " Kwaku Ananse foro kɔɔ soro kɔtenaa ase komm. Afei ɔsan baa fam bio; na afei ɔsan kɔɔ baabi kɔhuntaee ne ho. Ɛneɛ Ntikuma foro kɔɔ soro. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, that agya (of mine) has brought something again. " Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Mama, Nyiwankonfwea, Afudotwedotwe, bra ha, na nea agya de aba yi (saa berɛ yi) sen nea ɛtwa toɔ koraa. " Ɛnna wɔn nyinaa foro kɔɔ soro (on the silin). Ntikuma kae sɛ, "Saa adeɛ yi yɛ fɛ. " Ɛbuaa no sɛ, "Me din nni 'beautiful'. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo din de dɛn? " Ɛkaa sɛ, "Me din de 'Swish-and-raise-weals'. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Switch me kakra ma mehu. " Na saa abapra no sian baa ne so bɔɔ wɔn mpire pii. Kwaku Ananse gyinaa nkyɛn, na ɔteeɛm sɛ, "Lay it on, lay it on, especially on Ntikuma, lay it on him. " Afei bere a Kwaku Ananse ahwɛ na wahu sɛ wɔabɔ wɔn mpire pɛpɛɛpɛ no, ɔkae sɛ, "Adwobere" (Cool-and-easy-now). Kwaku Ananse baeɛ na ɔfaa abapra no twaa mu nketenkete na ɔtrɛw ho. Ɛno na ɛmaa abapra no baa abusuakuo no mu. Enti ɛba no sɛ sɛ woka biribi kyerɛ wo abofra, na ɔmpɛ sɛ ɔtie wo a, yɛbɔ no mpire.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
