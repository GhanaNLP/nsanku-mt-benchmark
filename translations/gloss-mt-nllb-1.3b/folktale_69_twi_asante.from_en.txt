Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ BERE A AFIFIDE KORO BI NWIADOWA, GARDEN-EGGS, NYIN A, ƐWƆ FAM, BERE A NKRUMA, NKRUMA NO, GYINA SORO KƐSE.

GARDEN-EGGS ne Nkruma pɛn bi na wɔtenaa dan baako mu; fa a wɔde nwura a wɔprapra so no, fa a wɔde nna so no. Nea ɛsɛ sɛ wɔyɛ nyinaa, na wɔyɛ no wɔ saa dan koro no ara mu. Afei Garden-eggs ka kyerɛɛ Nkruma sɛ, "M'ani wu wɔ ɔman yi ho, enti yɛnkɔ tutu ɛtokuro, na yɛmfa nwura no nsie mu. " Nkruma kaa sɛ, "Menkɔ (na mentu). " Garden-eggs no kɔɔ so kɔtu amena bi. Bere a ɔtuuiɛ no, Nkruma teɛɛ ne so, na Garden-eggs buaa no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa ɛtokuro no so na fa ba na memfa nhwɛ. " Garden-eggs buae sɛ, "Ah! Ɔbɔadeɛ bɔɔ no, woahunu onipa a ɔso ɛtokuro pɛn? Nkruma kaa sɛ, "Sɛ woanka wo ano a, mebɛba abɛbɔ wo. " Garden-eggs kaa sɛ, "Ɛnne fa sɔneɛ bi, fa nsuo hyɛ mu ma me nom, na mɛso ɛtokuro no ama wo. " Nkruma buaa sɛ, "Wofa me sɛ kwasea; ɛfiri sɛ na yɛte ha sɛ abusuakuo nti, yɛde sɔneɛ na ɛbɔ yɛn ho nkɔmmɔ. lash nsuo? " Nkruma firii nea na ɔteɛ no, na ɔkɔɔ ɛtokuro no mu kɔbɔɔ Garden-eggs. Mprempren, berɛ a na ɔreko no, ɔsoro-nyame no tee, na ɔsomaa Kɔkɔsakyi, Ɔpɛtɛ, sɛ ɔmmɛfa wɔn mmienu mmra. Wɔn mmienu no kɔɔ. Garden-eggs kaa nsɛmfua a ɛwɔ nano; Nkruma kaa ne deɛ. Afei, Ewiem-onyankopɔn no yɛɛ ne kasafoɔ sɛ ɔmfa atɛmmuo mma Nkruma, na ɛyɛ adom a ɛwɔ onyankopɔn mu. Ɛno nti, sɛ yɛdua Ewiem-egg a, ɔda fam wɔ fam, ɔdaa Ewiem-onyankopɔn ne ne mpɔtam badwafoɔ ase. Na Nkruma deɛ, ɔgyina hɔ tenten, ɔse ɔne Ewiem-onyankopɔn bɛko sɛ wama atɛnkyea.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
