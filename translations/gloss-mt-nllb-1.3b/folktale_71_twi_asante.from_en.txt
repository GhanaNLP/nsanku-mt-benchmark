Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

WOYƐ NWONWASO SƐ KWAKU ANANSE, ANAA.

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse, na na ɔte hɔ pɛn, berɛ a Nsia, ɔsoro-nyame maame, yareeɛ. Wɔkɔfaa aduruyɛni bi baeɛ sɛ ɔmmɛhwɛ no. Ɔbaeɛ no, ɔgyee sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. e. £8) peredwan sikasiee de saaa ne yareɛ. Afei, Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ Ewiem onyankopɔn nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Saa yɔnkoɔ yi regye wo sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma kwa, ɔrentumi nhwɛ no, enti ma me nhwɛ no mma wo, na sɛ mehwɛ no a, me etire betumi ayera. " Kwaku Ananse faa asuanu a ne bo yɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. e. £4), na ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔsoro-nyame no sɛ, "Sɛ mantumi ansa yareɛ yi a, mɛma wo gyata a ɔte ase. " Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ anɔpa no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi pefee no, na Nsia, Nyankopɔn, ɔsoro-nyame no maame, akɔ. Wɔkae sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse, ɛyɛ dɛn? " Kwaku Ananse firi baabi kɔeɛ, ɔdwanee. Seesei, ɔkasa ne nea aka akyi (i. e. me werɛ firii sɛ mebɛka sɛ) Kwaku Ananse nnamfoɔ paa ne Okusie, Okisi no. Ɔka kyerɛɛ Okisi no nea asi, na ɔkae sɛ, "Wei ne nea mahu no, enti sesei ɛsɛ sɛ woboa me. " Okisi buae sɛ, "Ɛwɔ kwan bɛn so na metumi aboa wo? " (Kwaku Ananse) buae sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ wokɔ tu ɔkwan no kosi sɛ ɛbɛdu pempe a ɔsoro-nyame no te so no ase. " Okisi kae sɛ, "Mɛyɛ ama wo. " Enti ɔyɛɛ no. Okisi no bae na ɔbɔɔ Kwaku Ananse amanneɛ sɛ, "Mawie. " Kwaku Ananse kɔe na ɔfrɛɛ ne ɔbabarima Ntikuma, na ɔtwaa aben maa no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔnkɔ ɔkwan no mu na ɔntena pempe no ase, na sɛ ɔhu sɛ ɔsoro nyame no atena pempe no so a, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔbɔ ne aben na ɔka sɛ:

"Sɛ wokum Kwaku Ananse a, abusuakuo no bɛba abɛsɛe!

Sɛ wode Kwaku Ananse bɔne kyɛ a, abusuakuo no nne bɛhinhim! "

Ntikuma kɔtenaa ahɔhogyeɛ akongua no ase, na Kwaku Ananse de ne ho sii hɔ sɛ ɔrekɔ Nyankopɔn, Ewiem-onyankopɔn, nkyɛn wɔ napam no mu. Bere a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, ɛnneɛ na Ewiem-onyankopɔn mmapɔmma no akyere no nsɔmu; wɔde no kɔɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn anim. Ɛnna kasafoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Wo, Kwaku Ananse, woaka akyerɛ Nyankopɔn, Ewiem-onyankopɔn, sɛ sɛ ne maame Nsia wuwu a, anka ɔbɛtumi akum wo; enti esiane sɛ Nsia kɔ nti, ɛno nti na yɛbɛkum wo. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ wogyae me kwan ma meka me asɛm. " Nanso akyerɛfoɔ no kyeree no nsɔmu wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ wɔnkɔ kunkum no. Ntikuma maa ne nne so sɛ: "Sɛ wokum Kwaku Ananse a, abusuakuo no bɛba asɛeɛ!

Sɛ wode Kwaku Ananse bɔne kyɛ a, abusuakuo no nne bɛhinhim! "

Wɔkae sɛ, "Montie, montie! " Na aben no san bɔɔ bio:

"Sɛ wokum Kwaku Ananse a, abusuakuo no bɛba abɛsɛe!

Sɛ wode Kwaku Ananse bɔne kyɛ a, abusuakuo no nne bɛhinhim! "

Ɛnna Krontihene sɔre bɔɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn, abebuo no, amanneɛ sɛ, "Saa abusuakuo yi yɛ wo dea, Ewiem-onyankopɔn, ne Ya, Old-Mother-Earth. Ɛnɛ da a woanya Kwaku Ananse na worekum no. Ya, Old-Mother-Earth, reka sɛ, 'Momma no nkɔ,' enti sɛ wogyae no a, ɛbɛyɛ yie. " Ɛnna Ewiem-onyankopɔn maa akyerɛfoɔ no gyaee no. Ɛno nti na abebuo bi wɔ hɔ sɛ, "Woyɛ nwanwa te sɛ Kwaku Ananse. "

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
