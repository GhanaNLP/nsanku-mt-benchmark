DWOM 94 Anisɔ ma Onyankopɔn Asɛm

Fapem a emu yɛ den so kyekye wo Gyidie

BERE A wo ne Yehowa Adansefo suaa Ebolo no, dɛn na ɛmaa wogye dii sɛ woanya gyidi biako pɛ, ɔkwan biako pɛ a wɔfa so som osom a Onyankopɔn gye tom? (Efe. 4:4-6) So watwe wo aba Yehowa nkurɔfo nkyɛn esiane ɔdɔ a wuhu wɔ wɔn mu nti? Wɔn abrabɔ tenenee no ma wunya adwene pa wɔ wo ho anaa? Nnipa pii twe wɔn ho kɔ nokware no ho efisɛ Onyankopɔn ahyehyɛde no ma wɔte nka sɛ wɔwɔ ahobammɔ na wɔdɔ wɔn ankasa, na ɛyɛ ntease. Ɔdɔ ne abrabɔ pa yɛ nokware som ho nsɛnkyerɛnne. - Yoh. 13:35; 1 Pet. 2:12.

Nanso sɛ yɔnko gyidini bi hyɛ wo abufuw anaasɛ ontumi nkyerɛ wo dɔ wɔ kwan foforo so nso ɛ? Anaa sɛ yɔnko gyidini bi de ne ho hyɛ bɔne a anibere wom mu - animguaseɛ suban mpo, sɛdeɛ ɛtoo ankorankoro bi a ɔwɔ mfeɛ ɔha asafo a ɛdi kan mu wɔ Korinto no? (1 Kor. 5:1) Sɛ wo gyidie gyina sɛdeɛ afoforo nneyɛeɛ ma wote nka nko ara so a, ɛne sɛ wode wo ho bɛto fapem a ɛwoso so. So fapem a ɛtim wɔ hɔ ma wo gyidie?

FAPEM A ƐTIM MA GYIDIE

Wobɛtumi akyekye gyidie fapem a ɛyɛ den denam wo fɔbuo a wobɛma ayɛ kɛseɛ wɔ nokware atitiriw a woresua firi Ebolo mu no so. Ɔkwan bɛn so na ɛno betumi aboa? Susuw nhwɛsoɔ bi ho.

Wɔ dwumadie bi ase, na Yesu asuafoɔ bebree agyae no. Wɔn ani abere wɔ ne mpaemuka a ɛfa ne honam a wɔdi ne ne mogya a wɔbɛnom no ho. Wɔkae sɛ: "Saa ɔkasa yi yɛ nea ɛma ahodwiriw. " Nea Yesu kae no yɛɛ wɔn ya araa ma wɔgyaee no akyi kɔ. John 6:56-60, 66.

Akyire yi, Yesu bisaa nasomafoɔ no sɛ: Wo nso wopɛ sɛ wokɔ? Ɛnna Petro buaa no sɛ: Awurade, hena nkyɛn na yɛbɛkɔ? Wo na wowɔ daa nkwa mmɛ. Yɛagye adi na yɛahu sɛ wone Onyankopɔn Kronkron. John 6:67-69.

Dɛn na ɛmaa Petro ne asomafoɔ foforɔ no yɛɛ soronko wɔ wɔn a wɔhinhim no ho? Wɔn gyidie no gyinaa adeɛ a ɛyɛ den sene wɔn atenka. Petro kaa sɛ: Yɛagye adi na yɛahu sɛ wone Onyankopɔn Kronkron. Na saa anokwafoɔ yi nim deɛ wɔgye di ne deɛ enti a wɔgye di. Bere a wɔde Yesu nsɛm ne ne nneyɛeɛ totoo Twerɛsɛm no ho deɛ na wɔnim no ho akyi no, wɔgye dii sɛ Yesu ne Mesia. Gyinaesi a ɛyɛ den a wosii wɔ saa nokware titiriw yi ho no maa wɔn gyidie yɛɛ den berɛ a afoforɔ ani beree Yesu nsɛm no.

Dɛn na saa akawnto no kyerɛ yɛn? Nea yenim no betumi ayɛ nea tumi wom sen sɛnea yɛte nka. Dɛn nti na ɛte saa? Efisɛ Ebelo mu nkyerɛkyerɛ a nokware ahodoɔ a yɛasua afa Onyankopɔn din, ne botaeɛ ne ne agyedeɛ ho ka hoda so ara yɛ nea ɛkɔ so daa. Nea ɛnte sɛ yɛn atenka no, Bible nokware ahorow nni nkɛntɛnso wɔ nea nnipa ka anaa wɔyɛ no so.

30 THE WATCHTOWER

Enti, sɛ yɛgye Bible nokware ahorow di na yɛde saa nokware ahodoɔ yi bata kwan a Yehowa fa so di dwuma no ho a, yɛbɛtumi akwati sɛ afoforo suban bɛto yɛn hintidua.

Ɛwom, yɛte nka sɛ ɔdɔ a ayɔnkofoɔ agyidifoɔ wɔ no hyɛ yɛn nkuran. Na yɛte nka sɛ ayɔnkofoɔ agyidifoɔ abrabɔ pa ma yɛn so. Nanso sɛ saa atenka no fi ase hinhim esiane afoforo nneyɛeɛ nti a, yɛn fɔbuo a yɛde ma wɔ Bible nokware ahorow mu no ma yɛn gyidie mu yɛ den. Ɛboa yɛn ma yɛhunu sɛ yɛwɔ ahyehyɛde a ɛteɛ mu, a ankorankoro kakraa bi nneyɛeɛ mfa ho.

Sɛ yɛwɔ ankorankoro Bible adesua nhyehyɛe pa a, yɛhyɛ yɛn fɔbuo mu den wɔ nokware ahodoɔ a yɛasua afiri Onyankopɔn Asɛm mu no mu. Saa fɔbuo no, sɛ yɛkyekyɛ mu dane a, ɛbɛboa yɛn ma yɛakɔ so agye adi sɛ yɛanya asom kwan baako pɛ a Onyankopɔn gye tom. Ɛneɛ yɛn gyidie bɛtena fapem a emu yɛ den so na afoforɔ nsɛmfua anaa nneyɛeɛ rensunsu. Dw. 16:8; hwɛ adaka Tena Kwan a ɛfata so.

Kwan a ɛfata so tra hɔ.

Fa no sɛ woreda bɔse bi a ɛde akwansin ahanhan pii rekɔ beae fɛfɛ a wɔkɔ no ho mfonini wɔ wadwenem. Wɔ saa akwantenten no mu no, ebia wubenya atenka ahorow bebreea ne fã kɛse no ara yɛ anigyeɛ wɔ nokwasɛm a ɛyɛ sɛ worekɔ akwantu no mu.
