SƐNEA LEOPARD NO FUNU HO

ƐHƆ NA NA Nwansena BI NE Daboɔ BI TE, na Daboɔ no ka kyerɛɛ Nwansena no sɛ, Yɛnkɔ na yɛnhwehwɛ mmaa a wɔbɛware yɛn. Nwansena no kaa sɛ, Ho. Ɛnna Daboɔ no hohoroo ne ho, na ɔde ngo guu ne ho, na ɔde ne sika kɔkɔɔ agude siesiee ne ho, na ɔkyekyeree ne mpaboa dada no, na ɔde maa Nwansena sɛ ɔmfa netire. Na wɔrekɔ no, wɔbɛduruu akuraase bi. Ɛnna Nwansena no kaa sɛ, "Mothers all and Agyanom all, me ma mo anwummere nkyea. " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Yɛn kunu pa. " Daboɔ no nso duu hɔ; ɔkae sɛ, "Mothers all and Agyanom all, me ma mo anwummere nkyea. " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Wo ne ano fĩ a wode akokoduru rema yɛn anwummere nkyea? " Wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ, na wɔduruu akuraa a ɛdi hɔ no. 'Wansana, Nwansena no, kae sɛ, "Ɛnanom ne Agyanom nyinaa, mema mo anwummere nkyea. " Wɔbuaa no saa ara. Daboɔ no nso duu hɔ, na ɔgyee wɔn, na wɔbuaa sɛ,

"Wo a wowɔ ano fĩ no, wopɛ sɛ yɛdi anwummere nkyea? " Wɔpueeɛ no, wɔkɔɔ kwan so, Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ ha! fa kɛtɛ dada no brɛ me na wofa sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ yi siesie wo ho, sɛ kɛtɛ dada no nti na mmabaawa no dɔ wo a, mebɛhu. " Na (Nwansena) gyeɛ ahosiesieɛ no, siesie ne ho, na wɔpueeɛ kɔduruu akuraa a ɛdi hɔ no. Daboɔ no na ɛwɔ anim, na kapet dedaw no nso wɔ netire so, na ɔregyegye (nkuraasefoɔ). Wɔkae sɛ, "Mo nkyia yɛn, mo a mo ano fĩ; ɛfata sɛ motwa saa mpa dedaw yi. " Ɛnna Nwansena bɛduruu hɔ; ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛnanom ne Agyanom nyinaa, mema mo anwummere nkyea. " Na wɔbuae sɛ, "Oh you dɔfo; ha deɛ, mmabunu bi wɔ hɔ a ɛfata sɛ yɛhwɛ; sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ; sɛnea wɔyɛ wo; sɛ ɛnyɛ borɔ a yɛbɛnya no, anka yɛbɛka wo ho wɔ wo Wɔkɔɔ so. Ɛnkyɛree na wɔduruu kwan no so no, Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, Ayi! adamfo, gyina hɔ ara, yi me sika agudeɛ no na fa ma me ntɛm, ntɛm, ntɛm. Bere a Nwansena no yii ne ahosiesiedeɛ no maa no wieeɛ no, Daboɔ no hwɛɛ kwan no so na ɔtwee nhabannwono bi a ɔde bɛkyekyere Nwansena no. Ɔkaa sɛ, Mɛkyekyere wo, na dɛn na woanya ka? Na Nwansena no kaa sɛ, Me nni hwee ka. Daboɔ no kɔɔ so sɛ, Me ne wo nnyinii yɛn beae a obi rekɔ mpo. . . (na mmaa no redan adɔ wo dedaw). Ɛnna Daboɔ no kyeree no nsɔmu bi kyekyeree no dua bi so. Na ɔno nso kɔɔ baabi foforɔ kɔhunuu. Ɛnkyɛree biara, Borɔdeɛ no faa kwan a ɛkɔ dwaso no so, na bere a ɔhunuu no (Nwansena no) kae sɛ:

Hwan na ɛsɛn hɔ kɔkɔɔ saa?

Hene na ɛsɛn hɔ a ɛhyerɛn saa?

(Nwansena) kae sɛ:

Ɛyɛ me, Nwansena, Kwao Boakye.

Adenen!

Gyahene Kofi Amo, Adenen!

Adenen!

Half-a-ball-of-Dɔkono kae sɛ, "Me wate, mebɛgyaa wo. " Ɔkɔɔ hɔ, na ɔgyaa no. Bere a wawie ayɛ saa no, prɛko pɛ na Daboɔ no pueeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woayiyi me ɔbarima yi? " Ɔ (Dɔkono fã bɔɔlo no) kaa sɛ, "Meayiyi no, na nea wobɛyɛ no, yɛ. " Ɔ (Daboɔ no) kaa sɛ, "Me ne wo renyɛ hwee koraa, me ne wo nko na yɛde yɛn nkuturu bɛko. " Dɔkono fã bɔɔlo no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ yɛbɛko a, yɛnkɔ nwura no mu, na yɛnbubu nnyina nsi sisian. " Enti wɔkɔbɔɔ nnyina, na ɔsan bae. Na ɔno (Half-a-ball-of-Dɔkono) kae sɛ, "Momma yɛmfa ogya nhyɛ no. " Na Daboɔ no de sisian sii hɔ. Saa bere koro no ara na wɔde ɔko bɔɔ mu, (na ne nnyigyei te sɛ) Yiri! Yiri! Yiri! Na Daboɔ no atete Half-a-ball-of-Dɔkono (afi ne ho), na ɔatow agu, bam! Half-a-ball-of-Dɔkono kae sɛ, "(Adeɛ a ɛte saa no nyɛ hwee), obi fa adeɛ a ɛte saa na ɔde hohoro ne yere baabi a ɔhoro ne ho. " Na wɔsan hyiaa bio. Na Half-a-ball-of-Dɔkono nso abɔ ne ho Daboɔ sɛ, bam! Na Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Me nso mefa adeɛ a ɛte saa ara bɛhoro me yere bathroom, (nanso) yɛn ahyiadie a ɛdi hɔ no, sɛ obiako tu ɔfoforɔ agu a, ɛneɛ na ɛyɛ tow a etwa toɔ. " Na wɔaka abom (ɔko no, na nnyigyei no te sɛ) Kikiri! Kikiri! Kikisi! Na Dɔkono fã bɔɔlo no maa Daboɔ no so na ɔtoo no ogya no mu. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "_Me firim, me firim, me firim_" (Me firi, me firi, me firi. Saa na Daboɔ no nyaa ne su "_Me firim_" no). (Afei) na wɔhu sɛ ne nipadua te sɛɛ, kurukyire nyinaa, kurukyire, kurukyire (ɛne sɛ, nyinaa spotted); baabi a fitaa no wɔ no, ɛhɔ na nso no aka no; na baabi a tuntum no wɔ no, ɛhɔ nso na bidie tuntum no aka no. Na berɛ a worenantew, na wuhu ahaban a ɛda hɔ a Half-a-bɔɔlo-of-Dɔkono (has been wrapped) da mu no, wubehu sɛ nwansena pii adɔɔso wɔ so; na ɛno nso nni sianteɛ foforɔ biara (except this only) nti, wɔda no ase.

Ɛyi, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (ana) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi afa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a wode bɛkamfo me (asɛmka no).
