Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐDƐN NA ƐBAA SƐ PARROT NO SƐEƐ BƐYƐƐ KƆKƆSƐM

WƆKYERƐ SƐ, BERE BI NA Ɔhemmaa BI WƆ HƆ, NA ƆWOO abaayewa fɛfɛ. Na ɔwɔ HƆ, nanso berɛ a wɔde no kɔhyɛɛ awareɛ mu maa obiara no, ɔpoo sɛ ɔbɛware. Daa a (ne) nnipa kɔkasa (kyerɛ no, awareɛ ho asɛm) no, na ɔka sɛ, Akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho. Afei na abɔdeɛ, Ɔnini (Ɔnanka), wɔ baabi wɔ nwura mu, na ɔte ɔbɔn kɛse bi mu. Ɛda bi a ɔwɔ hɔ (wɔ ne ɔbodan mu) no, ɔtee abɔfoɔ bi a wɔretwam, na wɔredi ababaawa fɛfɛ no ho asɛmti, sɛnea ɔde no maa obiara wɔ awareɛ mu no, ɔkae sɛ ɔmpɛ no. Ɔnanka no da hɔ, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ, Mɛbɛsesa me ho ayɛ me aberante pa, na makɔware Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no. Ɛyɛ nokware, Ɔnanka no danee ne ho yɛɛ no mmabunu; ɔbɛhwɛ no akyɛ, na ɛrentumi nyɛ yie. Ɔde ne ho sii akuraa hɔ. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na ɔredidi kola-nuts. Berɛ a ɔduruu kuro no kwanano no, naQueen Mother no ba a na wakɔko atia ahohoro no asan aba na ɔretrɛw ntaadeɛ no ma ɛyow. Ɔnanka ntasuo no, na ɛhwe aseɛ wɔ Queen Mother no ba no gyegyeegye so. Ɔhemmaa no ba no kaa sɛ, Dɛn nti na woretwitwa wanim gyegyeegye? Ɔnanka no kaa sɛ, Wo ne hokwan no, Amu. Ɔkaa sɛ, Me ntɔn abodin no nyɛ Amu. Ɔbuaa sɛ, Wo ne hokwan no, Esono. Ɔkaa sɛ, Me ntɔn abodin no nyɛ Esono. Ɔbuaa sɛ, Wo ne hokwan no, me yere. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ɛneɛ meware wo. Ɔhemmaa no ba no ne Ɔnanka no de sii hɔ sɛ, FF ma (her) fie. Ntɛm ara na Ɔhemmaa no ba no kɔɔ ne maame nkyɛn kɔkaa sɛ, Ehe! eh! eh! (na ɔde anigyeɛ a emu yɛ den rekeka ne ho), mahu nea mepɛ no. Ɔhemmaa no kaa sɛ, Awiei koraa! me ɔbabarima mmara mu no wɔ he? Ɔkaa sɛ, Me ni, nanabea. Ɔhemmaa no de nsa kyerɛɛ Ɔnanka no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Wo ne wo yere ntena mu anigyeɛ mu. Bere a anwummere dwoe no, ɔne ne yere no kɔdaeɛ. Ɛyɛɛ saa nna nwɔtwe. Ɛduruu tenten so no, ɔkae sɛ, Me yere, merekɔ me akuraa nanso mɛsan aba. Ne yere kae sɛ, Ɛnneɛ me ne wo bɛkɔ. Ɔnanka no kae sɛ, Dabi. Ɔnanka no kɔɔ so kasae (na ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛma no adwene sɛ ɔnkɔ), nanso Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no antie. Ɛduruu berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔne ne kunu no firii hɔ. Bere a wɔrekɔ no, Ɔnanka no dwiriw ne ho kɔɔ nwura no ho. Ɔhemmaa no ba no kae sɛ, Dɛn na woreyɛ yi, ɛyɛ kwan yi? Ɔnanka no kae sɛ, Wo deɛ, to wo ano na di makyi. Ɔne no sii kwan, na wɔkɔɔ so, na (ɛnneɛ finally) Ɔnanka no yeraeɛ wɔ ɔbodan bi mu. Ɔhemmaa no ba no nso hyɛn mu. Ntɛm ara na Ɔnanka no danee ne ho yɛɛ no aboa kɛseɛ, afei ɔkasa kyerɛɛ Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no sɛ, Eyi ne nea meyɛ ampa, ɛfiri sɛ mesesa me ho wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ meware wo, ɛfiri sɛ na wote wo maame ne wo papa kɔn so ka sɛ, Biribiara nni hɔ (suro) ; Mɛkum wo (seisei); Bɔ me etire na kyere me ntontom; sɛ woannya bi a, wowie me. d aduane. Ɔhemmaa ɛna no ba no tenaa ne nkyɛn, na ɔresusu na ɔrehwehwɛ ntontom. Afei, ɛhɔ na na wɔwɔ, berɛ a ɔhemmaa ɛna no ba no hunuu Akoo bi a ɔretwam. Na ɔhemmaa ɛna no ba no rebɛbue nano aka sɛ, "Agya Ako," berɛ a Akoo no de ne nsa hyɛɛ nano. Akoo no faa ne tuo no, siesiee ne nyinaa, na ɔde kokoam fã no too ne kokoɔ so. Ɔnanka no kae sɛ, "Kwatiri me etire. " Ɔhene yere no ba no kae sɛ, "Mebrɛ sɛ mɛkwatiri; sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wokum me a, ɛnde kum me. " Ɔnanka no resi pɔ sɛ ɔbɛma ne etire so sɛ ɔbɛkyere Ɔhene yere no ba no, bere a Akoo no bɔɔ ne tuo sɛ, pomm! Akoo no kɔɔ ɔbodan no mu kɔgyee Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba ne Ɔnanka no de wɔn kɔɔ fie kɔbɔɔ amaneɛbɔ sɛnea ɛbaa sɛ wahu Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba ne Ɔnanka no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛnea wakum Ɔnanka no. Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no nso de nabasɛm no ho nsakyerɛeɛ nyinaa maeɛ. Ɔhemmaa no kaa sɛ, "Akoo, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mefa de da wo ase? " Akoo no kaa sɛ, "Nana, mempɛ biribiara, me ara mepɛ nnuadewa ngo kɛkɛ. " Ɔhemmaa no kɔeɛ na ɔde nnuadewa ngo baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Nana, mefa me dua yi na mede gu nnuadewa ngo yi mu, na saa kwan yi so na mɛyɛ no sɛnkyerɛnneɛ (akwantu a akokoɔduro wom yi). " Akoo dua no bɛyɛɛ kɔkɔɔ saa. Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
