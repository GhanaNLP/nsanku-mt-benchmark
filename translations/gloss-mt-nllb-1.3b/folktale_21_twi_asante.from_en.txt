SƐNEA ƆWEA (THE THREE-BEAR), ABOSOM-AKOTERE (THE ABOSOMAKOTERƐ) NE ƆPATUO (THE PATU) BAA BƐNYAA WƆN OSU SORONKO

ƆWEA, Dua-Bear, (a ɔwɔ abodin nso sɛ) Kwaduampɔn Kyerɛfo, Ɔpatuo, Patu ne Abosom-Akotere, Abosomakoterɛ, (a wɔn abodin ne), Ɔbɔadeɛ no wuo, Afe-baako-nneɛma-yɛ-abɔdeɛ, Ɔnam-ntɛm-ntɛm-nanso-obedu-asase-a-mmarima-ahotew-wɔ-akyirikyiri, (saa mmoa yi nyinaa) traa faako. Mprempren, na wɔn mu biara nni yere. Na Adobe dua no tɔn ne ho maa wɔn. Da biara, Adobe-tree sɔre a, na ɔkɔ twɛn yi; da a edi hɔ no na ɔtwɛn foforo bi nso; na da a edi hɔ no nso wɔ foforo bi so. Ɛda bi, berɛ a ɛyɛ Creators owuo dane (sɛ ɔbɛhyɛ Adobes services) na ɔhwehwɛɛ Adobe-tree no, ɛhe na ɔbɛhu no? Na ɔfiri hɔ kɔka amaneɛbɔ no kyerɛɛ Tree-bear ne Patu no. Ɛnna wɔkae sɛ, Kɔ frɛ no. Na Ɔbɔadeɛ wuo no maa ne nne so sɛ: Adobe o! Adobe o!

Dua bear no teɛɛm sɛ:

Bere a ɔkɔeɛ no, ɔsan mmaeɛ; ɔkɔɔeɛ no, ɔsan mmaeɛ, ɔsan mmaeɛ, ɔsan mmaeɛ!

Ɛnna Patu no frɛɛ sɛ:

"Yɛakari (sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma) na anka yɛbɛto ato hɔ, m! Yɛakari (sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma) na anka yɛbɛto ato hɔ, m!

Na ɛno ne abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ mmiɛnsa yi osuo mfitiaseɛ.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
