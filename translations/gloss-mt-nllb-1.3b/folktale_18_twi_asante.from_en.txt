Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ASƐM A ƐYƐ ANIGYE BAA ABUSUAKUO MU

WƆKA SƐ ɔbɔfoɔ bi (Once) KƆƆ nwura MU; Ɔbaa HWEHWƐƐ amena bi mu, na emu na ƐGYINAA Onipa, Ɔbarima, Osebo, Daboɔ, Ɔwɔ, Ɔwɔ, ne Okusie, Okisi. Afei berɛ a saa nnipa yi hunuu Ɔbɔfoɔ no, oyi kaa sɛ, Agya Ɔbɔfoɔ, bra na fa me kɔ. Ɛno kaa sɛ, Agya, bra na fa me kɔ. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Merenkɔ, ɛfiri sɛ ɛbɛyɛ ɔkyena mpo, sɛ mebɛgye wo, na woanya me, nea wobɛyɛ me no, menim sɛ ɛnsɛ sɛ me dwene ho. Eyi kaa sɛ, Menntumi (act so). Ɛno kaa sɛ, Menntumi (act so). Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, , Sɛ saa na ɛyɛ asɛm ampa a, mebɛyi wo adi. Na nokware ne sɛ ɔyii nnipa no nyinaa kɔɔ akuraa. Ɛda bi, berɛ a Ɔbɔfoɔ no wɔ hɔ no, Osebo, Daboɔ no, de nam pii bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, Agya Ɔbɔfoɔ, eyi yɛ me aseda wɔ ayamye a woyɛ maa me no ho, meda wo ase, Apiafo. Ɔde nkukuo dumienu a ɛyɛ nam brɛɛ Ɔbɔfoɔ no. Ɔbɔfoɔ no daa no ase na Daboɔ no kɔeɛ. Wɔ dwumadie foforɔ bi so no, na Ɔbɔfoɔ no wɔ hɔ, na Okusie, Okisi no, aba. Ɔkaa sɛ, Agya Ɔbɔfoɔ, wopɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma bi? Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Ho! adɛn nti na ɛnyɛ saa, ɛno ne adeɛ a mmarima hwehwɛ mu yera. Okisi no de nadwene sii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a Nyankopɔn, ɔsoro-nyame, de ne sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma sieeɛ. Ɔkɔtui ɛtokuro bi wɔ dan no ase, na ɔkɔɔ dan no mu, na ɔkɔgyee ɔsoro-nyame no sika kɔkɔɔ dɔteɛ no nyinaa, na ɔde kɔmaa Ɔbɔfoɔ no. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Meda wo ase, Aku. " Na Okisi no kɔe. Ɛda foforɔ nso, na ɔwɔ hɔ, na ɔtee sɛ obi kyea no, "Berɛ a atwam. " Ɔbɔfoɔ no buaa no sɛ, "Ame. " Ɔbarima no baeɛ, ɔkaa sɛ, Agya Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ, anɔpa pa. Ɔ (Ɔbɔfoɔ no) buaeɛ sɛ, Aane, kinsman. Ɔkaa sɛ, Eyi yɛ kukuo ketewa a ɛyɛ nnuadewa a mede aba sɛ mebɛda wo ase. Agya Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Mempɛ; Ɔbarima, wo deɛ, ɛte sɛ nea mekaeɛ sɛ ɛbɛyɛ, berɛ a mekaeɛ sɛ meremfa wo nkɔ; hwɛ nea Daboɔ de me baeɛ, hwɛ nea Okisi de me baeɛ. Ɔbarima no de ne nnuadewa kɔeɛ. na wɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ Ewiem-onyankopɔn sɛ, Wo sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma a ɛyeraeɛ no, Okisi bɛgyee no kɔmaa Ɔbɔfoɔ no. Ewiem-onyankopɔn kaa sɛ, Saa na ɛteɛ? Ɔkaa sɛ, Grandsire, ɛyɛ nokorɛ pefee. Ewiem-onyankopɔn de asɛnkafoɔ kɔkyeree Ɔbɔfoɔ no, na wɔde hama kyekyeree no asene. Wɔbɔɔ no mpire kɛseɛ. Afei berɛ a wɔne Ɔbɔfoɔ no aduru kwan so no, ɔkae sɛ, Elders, I require to go and ease myself. The asɛnkafoɔ said, No, ɔfɔmfoɔ, you will run away. He said, No, I will not run away, and if you want, take the hama, bind it to my asene, tie it on a dunsin, and let me go. The asɛnkafoɔ listened to him, and let him go. Ɛhɔ na na ɔwɔ, wama ne ho atɔ no, berɛ a aboa a ne tenten yɛ teateaa no baeɛ. Ɔkae sɛ, Hwɛ ha, me adamfo, gye aduro kɔɔn ketewa yi, na fa fa kɔ, na sɛ wokɔ, na wɔde wo kɔ asɛmbli anim, na ɛba sɛ wɔbɛkum wo a, me kɔ ɔhene no harem, na mɛtumi akye ne yere dɔfoɔ no ba no; sɛ mekyekye no a, wubehu sɛ obiara asɔre, na wo nso wobɛkasa, na woaka sɛ, Mewɔ aduro, na sɛ wode bedi dwuma wɔ abofra a ɔbɛsa no no so. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Meda wo ase, Aku. Na asɛnkafoɔ no faa Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔɔ kɔgyinaa ɔsoro-nyame no anim, (nanim ne ne Mpɔtam badwafoɔ) Kronti, Akwum, Adonten, Gyaase ne Kyidɔm ahemfo. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa nasɛm, na berɛ a Kasafoɔ no sɔre maa atɛmmuo no, wɔtee awerɛhow dede, pui! pui! akyirikyiri wɔ ahemfo mpenafo fie hɔ. Wɔkae sɛ, Ɛyɛ dɛn? Wɔkae sɛ, Ɔwɔ bi aka ɔsoro-nyame yere a ɔdɔ no no abofra. Ebiara kɔɔ so. Afei na Ɔbɔfoɔ no gyina hɔ; ɔkae sɛ, Sɛ menyɛ deduani a, anka ebia menya aduro (awuduro ano aduro). Ɔbarima bi a ɔtee (him) na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, Monkɔfa deduani no mmra ha. Ɛneɛ na Ɔbɔfoɔ no baeɛ. Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, Sɛ wotumi hwɛ yi, me abofra yi, na wanwu a, mede wo etire bɛma wo. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Me te, One-Kind. Na Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ (san), Nanabarima, nea mekyerɛ wo yi, sɛ wotumi nya bi a, na mɛtumi ahwɛ wo abofra no. Wɔkaa sɛ, Ɛyɛ adeɛ bɛn? Ɔkaa sɛ, Tell-tale mogya a ɛbɛfra aduru-pound no mu. ine. Wɔkaa sɛ, Ɛhe na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛgye Tell-tale mogya? Kasamafoɔ no mu baako a na ɔyɛ ɔnyansani no kaa sɛ, Na yɔnkoɔ a ɔbɛka kyerɛɛ Ewiem-onyankopɔn sɛ Ɔbɔfoɔ no aba abɛwia Ewiem-onyankopɔn sika no nso, ɔwɔ he? Wɔkaa sɛ, Wo nni mfomsoɔ, wo nni mfomsoɔ. Wɔkɔ de yɔnkoɔ no bae. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Twitwa ne nsa wɔ joint no so, na fa ne mogya no ma me. Ntɛm ara na wɔtwaa no mu. Ɔbɔfoɔ no faa mogya no de aduro no wee, na ɔde guu ɔhene ba no nan so. Ntɛm ara na ɔsɔre gyinaa. Wɔkae sɛ, Sing the Hunters praises. Wɔteɛɛm sɛ, Eee! Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Mefa wo etire na mede ma wo, na Tell-tale deɛ, ma wɔnkum no, na wɔntwitwa ne honam, na wɔtow gu afã ahodoɔ nyinaa. Wɔkum no, na wɔtwitwa ne honam mu nketenkete, na wɔtow gu ne nyinaa ho. Saa na Tell-tale pii baa abusuakuo no mu.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
