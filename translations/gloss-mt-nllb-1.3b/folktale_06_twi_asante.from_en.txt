SƐ OBI PAPA WO A, KYERƐ WƆN ASEDA SƐ WƆYƐ NO PAPA WƆ SAN BA MU

WƆKYERƐ SƐ NA Ɔkɔdeɛ BI WƆ HƆ (BAAKO) NA ƆKƆƆ akyinkyinakyinkyin no, ɔkɔhyiaa aberewa bi a na ɔwɔ kuru wɔ ne nan so. Na Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Gracy me! Eyi yɛ kuru a ɛwɔ claase mu ankasa; ɛne nea ɛte sɛɛ yi, ɛmfa ho sɛ wobɔ mmɔden dɛn ara, wotumi nantew? " Ɔbaa aberewa no kae sɛ, "Oh, kakra koraa. " Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Mo nkurɔfoɔ, ɛnnɛ yi, sɛ anka mɛyɛ mo papa (to-da) a, to-morrow mobɛfa bɔne de aseda me. " Ɔbaa aberewa no kae sɛ, "Oh, anka merentumi nyɛ saa. " Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ woantumi nni saa a, mate. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Hyɛ waniwa mu na bue wɔn. " Ɛnna aberewa no hyɛɛ naniwa mu na ɔbuee wɔn ano. Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ wo kuru no. " Na bere a aberewa no bae sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ no, na ɛnyɛ ne nkaedum. Ɛnna ɔmaa no bɛn (naniwa) bio; ɔbuee wɔn ano, na ɔhunuu sɛ asase no nyinaa apue. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Close them again. " Ɔtoo wɔn mu, na ɔbuee wɔn ano, na afie no gyinaa hɔ pintinn. Na ɔhyɛɛ no sɛ ɔnto wɔn mu bio. Ɔbuee wɔn ani hunuu kuro a ne kɛseɛ te sɛɛ; ɛhɔ na ɛyɛ huge. Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, Old woman, its yours (lit. wɔ wo nsa. " Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Meda wo ase. " Ɛnna aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mefa de da wo ase? " Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Mempɛ ade ketewa a ɛho nhia mpo, me deɛ, nea mepɛ ne saa silk-cotton-tree a ɛda hɔ no. " Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Saa ade yi (you ask for) no, fa. " Ɛnna Ɔkɔdeɛ no tuu mmirika kɔɔ so, na ɔdaa so, na ɔpam berebuo, na ɔhyɛɛ nkosua mmienu, de siee. na ɔhyɛɛ nkosua mmienu no, na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔhwehwɛɛ biribi ma ne mmofra nni. Aberewa no banana a ɔne no te no hyɛɛ aseɛ kwiikwii sɛ, "Ehe! Ehe! " Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn asɛm na ɛwɔ hɔ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma menwe akɔre mmofra. " Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛhe na mebɛnya akɔre mmofra? " Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛhe na mebɛnya? " Aberewa no nana ketewa no hyɛɛ aseɛ bio sɛ, "kwiikwii! kwiikwii! Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn ne asɛm no? " Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Ma menwe akɔre mma, na sɛ me nnya bi nnwe a, me bɛwu. " Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Ah! ɛsɛ sɛ eyi, me nana wuwu ma ahiasɛm a ɛfa akɔre mma a wɔbɛwe? Sɔre, fa nkuma na kɔ bɔ silk-cotton-tree no na fa kɔre mma no brɛ me. " Aberewa no akuraasefoɔ kɔɔ hɔ, (nkuma no bɔɔ) sɛ, Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! Ɛyɛɛ sɛ dua no rekɔ fam, berɛ a Ɔkɔre mma no mu ɔpanin no huri kɔɔ soro, na ɔbɛgyinaa berebuo no ano, na ɔtee su bi; ɔfrɛɛ maame no sɛ:

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, anomaa no, Ɔkɔre ba!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔdidi a, san bra!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, oh! oh!

Ɛduruu hɔ no, maame no tee sɛ ne abofra no resu; ɔsɔre, (na ne ntaban a ɛrebɔ no nnyigyei yɛ fa! fa! ɔbaeɛ no, ɔkae sɛ "Sanguri," na saa dua no (a ɛkame ayɛ sɛ ɛtwitwa) no san hyiaa bio, na nnipa a na wɔabɔ no no nyinaa, wɔmmɛwe wɔn. Afei ɔfaa aduane a na ɔde abere no, na ɔde maa ne mmofra no. Ɛnna ɔregyaa wɔn sɛ, "Me kɔ, sɛ aberewa no ba abɛfa mo a, momma ɔmfa mo nkɔ. " Na aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Monkɔ bɔ dua no na monnye abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no mma me nana no nnwe. " Na wɔkɔɔ hɔ ne mprenu so bio, Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! Ɛyɛɛ sɛ dua no bɛkɔ fam, berɛ a akɔre ba no puei bɛgyinaa berebuo no ano, na ɔfrɛɛ ne maame sɛ:

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, anomaa no, Ɔkɔre ba!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔdidi a, san bra!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, oh! oh!

Ɔfrɛɛ ne maame, na ɔfrɛɛ, na ɔfrɛɛ, na ɔfrɛɛ sɛno mmuaeɛna afei dua no bɛkasaeɛ berɛ a ɛrebɛto fam sɛ, "Ma sɛe! " Wɔfaa akɔre no mma, baako de maa aberewa no, baako a aka no nso faa wim kɔɔ soro kɔtenaa Wawa-dua so. Baako no, wɔhyɛɛ no, na wɔde maa aberewa no banana, na ɔde kaa bɔdeɛ a wɔato (na ɔredidi) ho. Ɛnkyɛree na Ɔkɔdeɛ no baeɛ. Bere a ɔduruu dua a wɔatwa no, ɔhunuu ne mmofra no mu baako a ɔte hɔ; ɔbisaa no nea asi, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no amaneɛbɔ no nyinaa. Ɔkɔdeɛ no de nakwan kɔɔ ɔbaa no akuraa ase. Bere a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, na ɔbaa no banana no redidi ne mmofra no mu baako. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Old woman, I congratulate you. " Ɔpueeɛ wɔ ɔbaa panyin no fie na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ ne nkonyaayi no wɔ kuro no kuro akyi. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sanguri," na onipa biara ayera, na ɔsan kaa sɛ, "Sanguri," na fie biara abubu prɛko pɛ na tenabea biara nni hɔ bio. "Sanguri," akuraa no san bɛyɛɛ kwaeɛ. "Sanguri," na ɔbaa panyin no yaw san bae. Na ɔse, "Old woman, woahu, ɛno nti na mpanimfoɔ ka sɛ, sɛ obi yɛ wo papa a, da no ase sɛ woyɛ no papa na woannye bɔne nka nkyerɛ no sɛ meda no ase. " Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi fa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a wode bɛkamfo me (asɛmka no).
