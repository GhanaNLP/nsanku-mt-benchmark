Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

HWEE NNI HƆ KORAA (ƐNO NA YƐSuro)

WƆKYERƐ SƐ, bere bi na Daboɔ bi ne ne yere te hɔ, na wɔwɔ mmofra mmienu. Sesei, na mmofra yi maame ne wɔn agya kɔ nwura hɔ daa kɔyi nam de ba. Ɛda bi a wɔn maame ne wɔn agya ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Sɛ mosɔre a, ɛsɛ sɛ mobɔ dawuru na monka sɛ, 'Hwee nni hɔ (a yɛsuro) ', hwee nni hɔ (a yɛsuro). '" Ɛnna wɔkɔɔ nwura no mu, na wɔgyaee wɔn mmofra no hɔ. Ɛneɛ na Dankwanta, anadwo-nsukorade no, bɛkaa ho, na mmofra no teɛɛm sɛ:

"Hwee nni hɛɛ (a yɛsuro)), hwee nni hɛɛ (a yɛsuro)! "

Night-jar no kaa sɛ, "Hwan na ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ suane adeɛ a ɛte saa? " Wɔbuaeɛ sɛ, "Ɛyɛ agya ne maame na wɔkaa no. " Night-jar no kaa sɛ, "Wɔdi mfomsoɔ; ɛsɛ sɛ woteɛm sɛ, 'Mmere nsesa ntɛm, mmere nsesa ntɛm. '" Afei, saa nkokoaa no deɛ, wɔn maame ne wɔn agya san baeɛ, na wɔtee sɛ wɔn mmofra su:

"Mmere bɛsesa, Mmere bɛsesa! "

Wɔkaa sɛ, "Hwan na ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ suane adeɛ a ɛte saa? " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "obarima bi na ɔka kyerɛɛ yɛn. " Ɛnna wɔn maame ne wɔn agya kaa sɛ, "Ɛnsɛ sɛ wose saa, ɛsɛ sɛ wose, 'Biribiara nni hɔ (a yɛsuro) ',biribiara nni hɔ (a yɛsuro). '" Wɔmaa nkokoaa no nam kɔeɛ. Night-jar no san baeɛ, na na wayɛ peaw mmienu. Afei wɔ baabi a nkokoaa no da no, na nnua abien bi gyina. Bere a Night-jar no bae no, obubuu nnua abien no, na ɔde peaw no hyɛɛ stumps no mu. Ɔka kyerɛɛ nkokoaa no sɛ, Ɛsɛ sɛ modwane ka sɛ, Mmere nsesa ntɛm, mmere nsesa ntɛm, na sɛ mo maame ne mo agya san ba a, ɛsɛ sɛ monka sɛ, me na meka kyerɛɛ mo. Night-jar no tuu mmirika kɔtoo peaw no mu baako pɔ so. Ɛnkyɛree na nkokoaa no maame ne wɔn agya san baeɛ. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Hwan na ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ teɛm yi? " Night-jar no buaeɛ sɛ, "Me na meyɛeɛ. " Na ɔsebo nini repue wɔ ne so sɛ ɔbɛkyere no, berɛ a Night-jar no tu kɔeɛ na Daboɔ no de ne ho bɔɔ peaw no so. Night-jar no tuu kɔeɛ wɔ peaw a ɛto so mmienu no so. Sɛ ɔsebɔ Bedeɛ tuu mmirika bɛkyere no a, na ɔno nso wɔ peaw no so. Night-jar no ka kyerɛɛ mmofra no sɛ, Afei deɛ, moahu sɛ morennya nam nni bio. Ɛno na me kyerɛɛ mo sɛ, mmere bɛsesa.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
