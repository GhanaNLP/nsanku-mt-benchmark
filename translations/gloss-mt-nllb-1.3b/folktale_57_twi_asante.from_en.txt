Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

CHILD-KWASI-GYINAMOA

NA abofra bi wɔ hɔ, Kwasi Gyinamoa, na ɔwɔ nuammaa nwɔtwe, na wɔn mu biara nni ɔhokafoɔ, enti wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ wɔrebɛkɔ baabi akɔ okununom hwehwɛ. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, Me ne wo bɛkɔ. Wɔbuaa sɛ, Yɛn ne wo renkɔ. Wɔn mu panyin kae sɛ, Ma ɔne yɛn nkɔ, na sɛ yɛduru kwan so na akutu bi tui yɛn a, obetumi atwe ama yɛn. Wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ no, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa ne wɔn kɔeɛ. Wɔduruu asubɔnten bi ho no, na ne fã bi te sɛ dɔte fitaa, ne fã bi te sɛ dɔte kɔkɔɔ, ne fã bi nso te sɛ ahaban tuntum. Wɔteɛɛm sɛ, Ehe, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ na yɛatwa asuwa yi? Na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa de ne (magic) cow-tail switch bɔɔ nsuo no, na ɔmaa nsuo no mu paapaee mmienu, na wɔnam mu kɔeɛ. Wɔbaa kuro bi so, na ɛyɛ kuro kɛseɛ paa. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, Kuro no dinn (kyerɛ sɛ silent save for the nnomaa su of babadua), onipa nni mu anaa? Anomaa bi kaa sɛ, Obi wɔ fie hɔ, kɔ so. Wɔkɔɔ so, na aberewa bi wɔ hɔ, na ne etire da ne anan so, na ɔrehwehwɛ ne mu ntontom. Bere a ɔhunuu Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, afei ɔmaa ne etire so sii ananmu; ɔkae sɛ, Me mmanana, adɛn nti, bere a obiara remma ha no, moaba ha? Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, Me nuammaa no adi nwɔtwe, na wɔnnyaa okununom a wɔne wɔn bɛbɔ, na enti wɔkae sɛ wɔrebɛba abɛhwehwɛ okununom a wɔbɛware wɔn. Ɔbaa panyin-nananom kae sɛ, Oh, ɛno nko ara ne no. t, na me nso, mewɔ mmofra nwɔtwe. Ɔbaa panyin nana na ɔsomaa ne nana sɛ ɔmmɛfrɛ ne mmofra. Wɔn baeɛ no, na baako ka sɛ, Here is my kunu, na baako nso ka sɛ, Here is my kunu. Ɛberɛ a wɔn nyinaa wiee nea wɔyɛ mmienu mmienu no, na aberewa no kɔfa suman bi a wɔfrɛ no dokum_, na ɔde kyekyere ne mmofra akɔn so. Ɛnna wɔn mu biara kɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn kɔdaeɛ. Na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso ne aberewa no kɔdaeɛ. Ɛbɛduruu anadwo no, aberewa no hyerɛɛ ne se, kɔkɔɔ fann, wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ ɔne wɔn rekɔ akunkum mmabaawa no. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, E! e! e! Aberewa no kae sɛ, Dɛn ne no? Baby-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, Bere a meda me akuraa, na sɛ me kɔ ahomegyeɛ nanso mantumi anda, na maame twa bayere slice bi gu ogya mu, na ɔfa kora a ɛtokurotokuro wom kɔpue nsuo ma me nom. Nana panyin no sii gyinae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ asuwa no so, na Baby-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kɔtoo suman a wɔfrɛ no dokdok no. um_ fii ɔbaa panyin no mma akɔn mu, na ɔfaa wɔn sɛn ne nuammaa so. Aberewa no san firii nsuo no mu; ɔkae sɛ, Mebɔɔ mmɔden sɛ mebɛpete nsuo (in that sɔneɛ), nanso mantumi anpete. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, Kɔ na fa atɛkyɛ bi, na yi nhaban na fa gu mu (the sɔneɛ), na fa nsɛe. Aberewa no kɔe na ɔyɛɛ saa, na ɔde nsuo guu mu, na ɛyɛɛ yie. Ɔde bae, na oyii bayere no fii ogya no mu, na ɔde maa Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa sɛ ɔnnom, na ɔnnom nsuo no nso. Afei ɔhwee ase na ɔdaeɛ. Ɔbaa panyin nana sɔreɛ, ɔkae sɛ, Ɛyɛ me. Ɔsɔɔ ne se, (kɔkɔɔ) kɔsii sɛ ɛhyerɛn, na ɔne wɔn kɔeɛ kɔɔ dan no mu; ɔde ne nsa kaa mmofra no akɔn, na nea na ne kɔn so dokum_ suman no nni no, ogya tɔɔ no ne ne se. Saa kwan yi so na okum mmofra nwɔtwe no. Na onnim sɛ ne mmofra ankasa na wakum wɔn. Berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, awuo paa, awuo paa, ɔbaa panyin nana sii hɔ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ afuw no mu. Mmofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuammaa no ma wɔdwanee. Ɔbaa panyin nana firi afuw no mu san bae. Mmofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, Old-woman-grandmother e! Old-woman-grandmother e! anka sɛ wonyɛ kwasea a, wubekum wo mmofra? Me deɛ, me ne me nuammaa rekɔ. Ɔbaa-nananom kaa sɛ, It is of no nsunsuansoɔ, nea onim ne baabi a ɛwɔ hɔ. Ɔkɔeɛ no, na ɔbaa-nananom no nso danee ne ho yɛɛ ababaawa fɛfɛ, na ɔkura kɔkɔɔ-rattle, na ɔkɔɔ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa akuraa ase. Ɔkae sɛ, Obiara a obetumi afa agyan atwitwa saa gourd-rattle yi mu, me ne no bɛware. Nnipa a wɔwɔ akuraa hɔ no nyinaa bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ wɔbɛtwa mu, nanso wɔantumi. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa twaa mu. Ɔkae sɛ, Me aware wo. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, Me deɛ, me ne wo nntwa. Enti ne wofa no waree no. Ɔkɔɔ ne nkyɛn kɔdaa no, nanso ɔtwee naniwa abien no na ɔde kɔe. Ɛnna abaayewa no kae sɛ, "Awofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa wɔ hɔ! Metiatia wo wofa aniwa mmienu no na me ne wɔn rekɔ. " Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, "Fa wɔn na kɔ, na nea onim ne wo yɛ no, baabi a ɛwɔ hɔ. " Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, wɔkae sɛ, "Me ba, kɔ fa wo wofa no san ba. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me nkɔ. " Wɔhyɛɛ no sɛ ɔnkɔ, na wɔhyɛɛ no sɛ ɔnkɔ. Afei, na abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nim sɛ ɔbaa-kwakoraa no banana yɛ enceinte, enti ɔno nso faa koraa bɔɔ nyafunu, de ne ntoma kyekyeree so, na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔrekɔ ɔbaa-kwakoraa no akuraa ase. Berɛ a ɔduruu kuro no awieeɛ no, na ɔresu. Ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, "Ɛdɛn ne asɛm no? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Na me ne me kunu reba, na bere a ɔduruu bepɔwa no atifi no ɔhwee ase na ɛno ma ne aniwa mmienu puee gui. " Ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, "Ɛno yie, ha na ɛwɔ saa ɔsɛmmɔnedifo a wasan aba wiase bio no ani benkum, Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, ne nuabarima, na ha nso na ɛwɔ ne nifa ani no. Wokɔ (baabi a wkunu onifirani no gyina) a, ɛsɛ sɛ woka sɛ, 'Me aniwa huruw, me aniwa ho atew. '" Ɛnkyɛree na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nsa bɛkaa wɔn, na ɔkae sɛ, "Wo ni, ɔbaa-kɛseɛ, eyinom ne me uncle aniwa a wopuee no, na me ne wɔn rekɔ. " Ɔbaa-kɛseɛ no kaa sɛ, "Ɛno mfa ho, nea onim ne baabi a ɛwɔ hɔ. " -Gyinamoa baa fie, na ɔfaa aniwa no sii nananmu wɔ kanyane afidie hyɛbea a ɛwɔ wofa no mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me aniwa huruwa, me aniwa mu da hɔ," na ne wofa no aniwa buee pefee. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, mmofra a wɔwɔ akuraa hɔ no nyinaa kɔdwaree asuwa no mu. Baby-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, "Me nni hohoro. " Wɔkae sɛ, "Ebia wowɔ awotwaa dua? " Bere a wɔkaa saa no, ne nufuɔ yɛɛ ne mene, na ɔhohoroo ne ho. Ɛnkyɛree na ɔhwee nsuo no mu, ɛna ɔbaa-kokoo faa no, twom! Ɔkaa sɛ, "Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, me wɔ wo," na ɔde no kɔɔ ne akuraa. Ɛnna ɔbaa dada kɔfaa kɛntɛn kataa no so, na ɔfaa nnuadewa, na ɔde sii ogya so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mekɔ gyeene abusua no nkyɛn, merekɔ nnuahwamfo abusuakuo no nkyɛn, na afei mɛba abekum Baby-Kwasi-Gyinamoa. " Ɔde ne ho sii hɔ, na Baby-Kwasi-Gyinamoa boaboaa abotan ano na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔrebɔ wɔn akuturuku, pupru! pupru! Ɔbaa panyin nana no bisaa sɛ, "Dɛn na woretwe? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Tiger-nuts. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Mema me bi memmewe. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Uncover me. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wo bɛkɔ. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Dabi, merennkɔ. " Ɔbɔɔ no mua sɛ, "Dabi, merennkɔ. " Ɔbɔɔ no mua, na ɔpueeɛ, na aberewa no nana reyi ne ho adi. Mmofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa nnuadewa tuo, ɔbɔɔ ɔbaa-kwakoraa no banana na okum no. Afei oyii ɔbaa-kwakoraa no banana wedeɛ no na ɔhyɛɛ ne ho. Ɔbaa panyin nana no san bae, ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na mo nyinaa yɛ dɔte sɛɛ? " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Mekum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa; mede no ato ogya so. " Saa amaneɛbɔ yi yɛɛ ɔbaa panyin nana no dɛ, na ɔresaw na ɔrema ne nne so (na ɔreto dwom): "Mma, wokum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, da bi na wobɛyɛ obi. "

Owiee aduane no ano aduan no, wɔtraa ase wɔ ne nkyɛn, na wɔdidii. Ɔbaa dadaa no de dede kɛseɛ petepete nkwan no (too nano), fweo! fweo! Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, "Woretew nkwan a wɔde wo banana ti ayɛ no ano. " Ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, "Wo na wose dɛn? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Na meka sɛ nkyene nni nkwan yi mu. " Ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔgye bi. " Enti ɔkɔe na ɔgyee bi. Wɔdidi wieeɛ no, na ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, "Seesei a meku Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa yi, bra ha, me nana. " Ɔbaa no baeɛ no, ɔfaa sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ na ɔhyɛɛ no fɛfɛɛfɛ. Seesei, ɔbaa panyin no hwɛɛ ne anim berɛ bi. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ah! saa abofra yi anim nka me nka Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa deɛ. " Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Nanabea, adɛn, bere a wunim sɛ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa baeɛ na ɔkumm me abusuafoɔ nwɔtwe nyinaa (do you speak like that), na wohyɛ me awerɛhow. " Ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, "Huli wo ano. " Ɛnneɛ na abofra no dwoɔ wɔ fie hɔ ara. Nanabea no yii no kɔeɛ. Ɔkae sɛ: "Ma menkɔtow ngu mfinimfini ɔbo no so. " Enti Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa soa kɔɔ so. Bere a oduu mfinimfini ɔbo no ho no, oyii ne sika kɔkɔɔ agude a na aberewa no de asiesie no no nyinaa na ɔkyekyeree no. Ɛnna ɔkae sɛ, "Ho wɔ hɔ, ɔbaa-kwakoraa, ho wɔ hɔ, ɔbaa-kwakoraa, me ne no, Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, mprempren mprɛnu so na mebae sɛ merebɛkum wo nana, na nka ho no, mefa wo sika kɔkɔɔ agude. " Ɔbaa-kwakoraa buae sɛ, "Mekaa sɛ wanim te sɛ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa deɛ; afei baabiara a wobɛkɔ no, me ne wo bɛkɔ. " Ɛnna wɔn mmienu nyinaa tuu mmirika kɔeɛ sɛ, kiri! kiri! Baby-Kwasi-Gyinamoa duu ne akuraa, na ɔde sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ no maa ne mogya-abusuafoɔ, na ɔkɔɔ kwan no so, kɔhuntaa ne nkyɛn, na ɔbaa-kokoɔ no nso baa hɔ. Ɛno nti, sɛ wonam kwan so a, wobɛte kakasakakasa nnyigyei sɛ, "pe. " Ɛno ne ɔbaa-kokoɔ a ɔdi Baby-Kwasi-Gyinamoa akyi.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
