Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ASONO NO BAA KƆƆ SO WƆ ƆMAN A ƐWƆ NHABANNURU TENTEN NO MU

NA ƐSONO BI WƆ HƆ, na ƆKAEƐ SƐ ƆPƐ OBI A ƆNE NO BƐDI agorɔ a wɔfrɛ no "nti a wɔde bobɔ wɔn ho". Na mmoa no nyinaa kyerɛ sɛ wɔntumi nni saa agorɔ yi wɔ Ɛsono no ho. Afei na Kwaku Ananse (Ananse) wɔ hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ, "Me deɛ, mɛtumi, nanso tebea a me ne wo bɛhyehyɛ ne sɛ, sɛ ɛba sɛ wokum me a, na ɛsɛ sɛ wosie me wɔ baabi foforo biara gye sɛ ɔbotan mu. " Ɛsono no kae sɛ, "Me na metie, na mɛba abɛbɔ wo ndanson, na wo nso, wobɛba abɛbɔ me ndanson. " Kwaku Ananse kɔtu bayerɛ, na ɔkɔtu ɔde wɔn too ogya mu sɛ ɔrebɛhyɛ wɔn, na ɔkɔyɛɛ tiafi wɔ kwan bi nkyɛn. Afei, bere biara a Kwaku Ananse bɛsɔre, na ɔde bayere bi hyɛ ne nsa, na ɔkɔtena ase wɔ tiafi no so. Ɔhunuu adowa ketewa a ɛtwam no. Afei nsɛmfua a aka wɔn akyi (i. e. me werɛ firii sɛ mebɛka asɛm bi) no, na ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bi aba (wɔ berɛ no mu). Bere a ɔretwam no, Ananse no tow bayere no gui wɔ kwan no so. Ntɛm ara na Adowa faa no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Agya Ananse, ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bi te sɛɛ aba, na ɛno na wode kɔdwo wo ho anaa? " Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "Me fie aduane ho ɔhaw biara nni hɔ. " Adowa no kae sɛ, "Me ne wo bɛkɔ akɔtɔ bi. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ fie kɔduruu hɔ, na ɔka kyerɛɛ Adowa no sɛ, "obarima bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔno na ɔde aduane ba me anadwo, enti kɔ da hɔ wɔ pon no ho. na sɛ ɔba a ɔbɛka sɛ, 'Asikoto!' (na wobɛma wo etire abɔ) na wabua sɛ, 'Me de me etire na ɛregye,' na sɛ woka saa pɛ a, ɔbɛfa aduane no bi ato hɔ ama wo de agye. " Adowa no kaa sɛ, "Meda wo ase, Aku; na wo deɛ, Agya Ananse, ankasa me nhuu ɔbarima a ɔte sɛ wo da. " Adowa no kɔtoo kwanano wɔ adiwo no mu. Ɛnkyɛree na Ɛsono no bae. Ɔkae sɛ, "Asikoto. " Adowa no buae sɛ, "Mefa me etire gye," na Ɛsono no bɔɔ no, pan! na Adowa no awu. Ɛsono no kɔeɛ. Anɔpa kɛseɛ a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, Ananse no de Adowa kɔmaa "Aso" (ne yere) sɛ ɔnyɛ no soup-stew. Kwaku Ananse firii aseɛ kɔɔ Ɛsono kɔmaa no anɔpa nkyea. Ɛsono no kae sɛ, "Wo ho tɔ wo koraa? " Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "Me ho tɔ me. " Kwaku Ananse de nadwene sii hɔ baa ne fie. Ɔfaa bayerɛ no foforɔ, na ɔkɔɔ tiafi hɔ bio. Ɔhunuu Ɔtwe, Ɔtwee no, na ɔtwee bayere no gui. Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, "Oh, Father Kwaku Ananse, ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bi te sɛɛ aba, na ɛno na wode kɔ tiafi a? " Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "Wo kɔn dɔ bi a, bra, ma yɛnkɔ me fie, na ebia me kɔ ma wo bi. " Ɔtwee no ne Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ so. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Da ha wɔ kwanano a ɛkɔ kar dan mu, na anwummere yi me akoa de aduane bɛba, na bere a ɔduruu ha no, ɔse, Asikoto; woka nso sɛ, Mefa me etire gye. Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, Me da wo ase, Aku, wo (alone) na wotumi hyehyɛ nneɛma a ɛte saa. Ɔtwee no kɔɔ hɔ kɔdaa. Ɛwɔ anwummere no na Ɛsono no bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Asikoto. " Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, "Mefa me etire gye. " Na ɔbɔɔ Ɔtwee no, na ɔhwee ase, (na ne hwe nnyigyei ne) kum! Ɛnna Ɛsono no kɔeɛ. Anɔpa kɛseɛ a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, na Kwaku Ananse faa Ɔtwee no de maa "Aso", na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ Ɛsono no fie kɔmaa no anɔpa pa. Ɛsono no kae sɛ, "Okuamoa ni, wo ho yɛ wo den anaa? " Okuamoa buae sɛ, "Wo ho yɛ wo den koraa. " Ɛnna wɔkasaa kakra, na Kwaku Ananse srɛɛ sɛ wɔnkɔ, na ɔsan kɔɔ ne fie. Na sesei Kwaku Ananse yɛɛ adeɛ koro no ara ne Ɔyuo, kwaduo no; Wansane, Bush-buck no; Kokotee-Asamoa, Bush-pig no; Ɔtromo, Bongo no; ne Oko, Ɔpɔnkɔ kɔkɔɔ-fitaa no. Afei, na ɛyɛ Kwaku Ananse deɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔbɔ Ɛsono no. Afei ɔkɔɔ dadeɛ adwumfo no hyewbea hɔ kɔyɛɛ dade payɛ, na ɔyɛɛ dade hama bi a ɔde bɛkɔ. Anwummere no, ɔkɔɔ Ɛsono fie. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Asikoto. " Ɛsono kae sɛ, "Mefa me etire no gye. " Ananse no faa dade payɛ no de sii Ɛsono etire no ahenkyɛ so, na ɔfaa dade hama no bɔɔ no sɛ, pan! pan! pan! na ɛ (teaa) no yeraeɛ (bɛhyɛɛ Elephant no ti mu). Kwaku Ananse afei baa fie. Anɔpa kɛseɛ a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, Ɛsono no ka kyerɛɛ ne yere sɛ, "Mewɔ tipae a emu yɛ den," na ne yere no kaa sɛ, "Nka asɛm saa, ɛsɛ sɛ wogu wanim ase wɔ ho; Kwaku Ananse mpo a wopiraa no ndanson no, wannya tipaeɛ, na ɔwuiɛ nso, na woka asɛm a ɛte sɛɛ; afei kɔ na fa anɔpa pa ma Kwaku Ananse sɛnea na ɔtaa yɛ no. " Ɛsono no hyɛɛ naniwa (i. e. ɔbɔɔ mmɔdenbɔ kɛseɛ) na ɔkɔɔ so; ɔduruu baabi a Kwaku Ananse te. Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Ɔhoɔdenfo kɛse, wote yie anaa? " Ɛsono no buae sɛ, "Oh, me ho yɛ me dɛ. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Really. " Kwaku Ananse kɔbɔɔ teaa foforɔ. Bere a anadwo dwoeɛ no, ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ Ɛsono hɔ. "Asikoto," na ɔkaeɛ. Ɛsono no buae sɛ, "Me de me etire na ɛregye. " Kwaku Ananse faa teaa no, ɔde sii hɔ, na ɔde dade hama no bɔɔ no, pan! pan! pan! Kwaku Ananse san baa ne fie. Ɛhɔ na na ɔwɔ, berɛ a messenger bi baeɛ na ɔkaa sɛ, Kwaku Ananse, Asono nyinaa nana kyerɛ sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meba bɔ wo amanneɛ sɛ wo adamfo, Ɛsono no, berɛ a ɔdaeɛ no, ɔnsɔree bio; enti bra bue ɔbotan bi na fa no fa no hyɛ mu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Dabi koraa, ɛnyɛ saa na yɛyɛɛ nhyehyɛeɛ no. Yɛkaa sɛ baako wu a, baako nso nwie no ma ɔnwe. " Asɛm no yɛɛ messenger no nkra ma Elephant (dead) no mogya-abusua. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Kɔ na fa no fa no ntɛm ara. " Ananse messenger no baa hɔ kyerɛɛ Ananse no, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Wɔse woreba ntɛm ara. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ wotwɛn me. " Ananse no kɔɔ nwura no mu kɔtwaa dua bi, na ɔtwee no yɛɛ no nnua-man, na ɔtwee tuo bi, na ɔde hyɛɛ ne nsa, na ɔde maa "Aso" sɛ, "Fa no, kɔ anim, fa to baabi a wɔka sɛ Ɛsɛ sɛ mepae ɔbotan no, na wo nso wobɛgyina hɔ, na meba a, wobɛfrɛ me. " "Aso" faa dua honi no kɔeɛ. Ananse no nso, wɔne asɛnkafoɔ no tuu kwan. Kwaku Ananse bɛduruu Asono anim, na wɔfaa asɔ, ne crow-bar, ne agyan ne Sekkan, de hyɛɛ ne nsa. Wɔkae sɛ, "Monkɔ so. " Ɛnna wɔde Ananse no kɔɔ baabi a na botan kɛseɛ bi wɔ. Wɔkae sɛ, "Tete no mu, tutu ɛtokuro, na fa Elephant (dead) no hyɛ mu. " Ananse kɔɔ ho, pE! pE! pE! (sounded his nnwinnadeɛ). Ɛhɔ na na Kwaku Ananse wɔ, berɛ a ɔfrɛɛ no (suddenly) "Aane. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Elephants, do you hear, (it is) as if someone is calling me? " Asono no kae sɛ, " Fi adi, wopɛ kwan a wobɛfa so adwane. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, " Metee. " Na ɔkɔɔ so ne nadwuma no, pE! pE! pE! Afei bio ɔteeɛm sɛ, " Yiw. " Ɔkae sɛ, " Obi refrɛ me. " Nnipa no kae sɛ, " Yɛremma wo kwan. " (Na ɛyɛ) saa, saa, mpɛn nson. Wɔ tenten so na wɔkae sɛ, "Kɔ na tie (who it is) na san ba ntɛm ara, na sɛ wuguan a, yɛbɛkyere wo. " Kwaku Ananse de ne ho kɔɔ baabi a Akua ba no wɔ. Mprempren Kwaku Ananse de mmirika (back), kiri! kiri! kiri! (na ɛyɛ ne anan nnyigyei). Asono kae sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse, ɛyɛ dɛn? " Ɔkaa sɛ, " Oh, ɛsɛ sɛ woyi wo ho, efisɛ akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho sɛ ɔbɔfoɔ bi gyina hɔ, na ɔse ɛsɛ sɛ me twe me ho kakra kɔ nkyɛn na wama no kwan ma ɔtow ne tuo abɔ wo. Ɛno nti na ɔfrɛɛ me, na ɔfrɛɛ me sɛ memfa me ho nnyina nkyɛn. " Berɛ a na Asono rebɛyɛ hwehwɛ wɔ nwura no mu hɔ no, nokware bi a wɔhunuu no ne sɛ obi gyina hɔ, na wɔdwanee. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, " Father hunter, kill them for me. " Asono no dwanee, na ɛwɔ Tall-grass-country no mu, na wɔgyaa ɔsono a wawu no hɔ, na Kwaku Ananse faa no kɔweee no. Saa na Asono baa sɛ ɔbɛkɔ Tall-grass-country no, efisɛ na ɛwɔ berɛ bi a Asono (nankasa) te kwaeɛ mu.

Ɛyi, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (ana) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi afa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a wode bɛkamfo me (asɛmka no.
