SƐNEA ANENE KƆN, KWAAKWAADABI NO, BƐYƐƐ ABERƐ NA NAKYI ABƐYƐ TUNTUM

Ɔbaa bi a wɔfrɛ no Ata (a ɔyɛ Nta) de ne abofra no kɔe sɛ wɔrebɛtwa apataa. Bere a wɔduu asuwa no ho no, ne abofra no kaa sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔ soro nsuo no mu. " Nanso aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔ fam, ɛfiri sɛ ɛhɔ na nkawa nketewa no wɔ. " Ne abofra no kaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, me kɔ soro, ɛfiri sɛ ɛhɔ na nkawa nketewa no wɔ. " Enti ne maame no kɔɔ fam. Wɔtenaa hɔ berɛ tenten, na ne abofra no nyaa bi, na ɔbaa abɛtwa mu akɔhwehwɛ ne maame. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na ne maame calabash (nneɛma) resow wɔ nsuo no anim. Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ah! ɛnyɛ baako (adeɛ), ɛnyɛ mmienu (nneɛma), ɛyɛ maame calabash. " Na ɔmaa ne nne so, na ɔsu frɛɛ ne maame no sɛ:

"Maka akyerɛ mo, na moantie, meka akyerɛ mo, na moantie.

Mekaa sɛ, 'Maame, ma yɛnkɔ nsuo no atifi, ɛfiri sɛ nsuo no atifi na nkawa nketewa te';

Na ɔka sɛ ɔrekɔ nsuo no ase, ɛfiri sɛ asuwa no ase na nkawa nketewa te.

Na dɛn na menyɛ? O me maame, Ata, mhm! "

Ɛnna Akrɔma no bɛkaa ho, ɔkae sɛ, "Abofra, wo dwom no yɛ dɛ, to na memfa wo maame mfi adi mma wo. " Na abofra no maa ne nne so:

"Maka akyerɛ mo, na moantie, meka akyerɛ mo, na moantie.

Mekaa sɛ, 'Maame, ma yɛnkɔ nsuo no atifi, ɛfiri sɛ nsuo no atifi na nkawa nketewa te';

Na ɔka sɛ ɔrekɔ nsuo no ase, ɛfiri sɛ asuwa no ase na nkawa nketewa te.

Na dɛn na menyɛ? O me maame, Ata, mhm! "

Na Akrɔma no faa ne mmoa (magic) tail-switch no bɔɔ nsuo no anim, nanso nsuo no gyinaa baabi a ɛgyinaa. Na ɔno (Akrɔma no) kɔɔ so. Na Kɔkɔsakyi, Ɔpɛtɛ no, bɛkaa ho, na ɔno nso kae sɛ, "Mo nto saa dwom no ma mete, na memfa mo maame mfi adi mma mo. " Na abofra no reto dwom no:

"Maka akyerɛ mo, na moantie, meka akyerɛ mo, na moantie.

Mekaa sɛ, 'Maame, ma yɛnkɔ nsuo no atifi, ɛfiri sɛ nsuo no atifi na nkawa nketewa te';

Na ɔka sɛ ɔrekɔ nsuo no ase, ɛfiri sɛ asuwa no ase na nkawa nketewa te.

Na dɛn na menyɛ? O me maame, Ata, mhm! "

Na ɔno nso faa ne (magic) tail bɔɔ asubonten no anim, nanso hwee ansi. Na ɔno nso kɔɔ so. Na (now) Anene, Kwaakwaadabi no, ɔno nso ka ho. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abofra, adɛn na woresu saa? Su na ma mete sɛ ebia mebɛyi wo maame ama wo. " Na abofra no too dwom no:

"Maka akyerɛ mo, na moantie, meka akyerɛ mo, na moantie.

Mekaa sɛ, 'Maame, ma yɛnkɔ nsuo no atifi, ɛfiri sɛ nsuo no atifi na nkawa nketewa te';

Na ɔka sɛ ɔrekɔ nsuo no ase, ɛfiri sɛ asuwa no ase na nkawa nketewa te.

Na dɛn na menyɛ? O me maame, Ata, mhm! "

Kwaakwaadabi no faa aduro cone bi tow guu nsuo no mu, na ɔfaa ne bodua suman no bɔɔ nsuo no anim, na nsuo no mu paapaee mmienu, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Sing the dwom," na abofra no too dwom. Na ɔhunuu ne maame ti ti nwi, na ɔreserew kakra. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sing again," na ɔtoo dwom. Na ne maame pue kɔɔ asene no so. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ so dwomtoɔ. " Na abofra no too dwom, na ne maame pue bɛduruu ne nanpɔ so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sing again;" na ɔtoo dwom. Ne maame no huri firii adi nyinaa prɛko pɛ na ɔbɛgyinaa sikakorabea no so. Ata a ɔyɛ Nta no kae sɛ, "Kwaakwaadabi, dɛn na memfa nkɔda wo ase? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, mempɛ hwee, kɔtɔ dark-blue ne fitaa ntama nko ara. " Ata tuu mmirika kɔtɔɔ bi, na ɔde kɔeɛ Kwaakwaadabi, na Kwaakwaadabi de dark-blue no kataa ne ho, na fitaa a ɔde kyekyere ne kɔn ho no. Ɛno nti na wubehu Kwaakwaadabi no, ne akyi yɛ dark-blue, nanso ne kɔn yɛ fitaa. Ɛyɛ akyɛdeɛ a ɔgyee firii Nta Ata hɔ.

Ɛyi, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (ana) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi afa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a wode bɛkamfo me (asɛmka no.
