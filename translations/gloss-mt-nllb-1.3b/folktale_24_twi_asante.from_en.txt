Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐDƐN NA ƐBAA SƐ ABƆFOƆ YƐ DƆM

ƆBƆFOƆ bi (Once) kɔgyee tuo-nnuru (powder) ne nsa adwumayɛbea ahoroɔ mmienu a ɛyɛ kannifoɔ de otuo ano aba, wɔ ayeyi, na ɔne ne yere ne abofra ketewa no kɔɔ nwura no mu wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ wɔbɛkum nam. Na ɔsii ɔbɔfoɔ adan bi na ɛhɔ na ɔne ne yere ne ne abofra tenaeɛ. Bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔkɔɔ nwura no mu. Bere a ɔhunuu aboa kɛseɛ bi na ɔtoo ne tuo guu no so no, ɔpaa kwan. Da biara na ɛte saa, bɛyɛ nnanu ne nwɔtwe. Na ne yere kae sɛ, Since I havent got any nam to eat, I shall go off fie. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no bere a ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔɔ nwura no, na ne yere ne ne abofra no nso afiri wɔn akuraa. Seesei tuo baako pɛ na ɛda so. Afei me werɛ firii sɛ mekae sɛ saa ɔbɔfoɔ no din ne Gyekye, na ne yere nso din ne Boniaye-kae-dabi (a ɛkyerɛ sɛ, Some-day-the-ungrateful-one-will-remember). Berɛ a na ɔbɔfoɔ no rekyinkyin no, na ɔduruu beae bi a ɛwɔ odum dua bi ase, na ɛhɔ na ɛyɛ beae fɛfɛ. Ɛhɔ na ɔbɔfoɔ no dwenee ne etire mu sɛ, Sɛ me kɔ fie a wɔbɛgye sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no ama powder no, na me nsa nka bi, nti mɛfa saa tuo yi na matwa me ho menem sɛ mɛwu. Ɛhɔ ara na Biribi frɛɛ no sɛ, Creators ɔbɔfoɔ. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, Father. Na Biribi kae sɛ, Adɛn nti na waniwa abere saa? seseiara na ɔno a wɔfrɛ no Go-and-spoil-the-fie no nni wfie a, anka mɛma wo biribi. Ɛneɛ na ɔbɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, Hwee nni fie. Asɛm bi kae sɛ, Sei na hwee nni fie, kɔ bon a ɛwɔ odum-dua no dua sini so, na fa nnawuro mmienu bɛda hɔ, nti fa ba. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔ de baeɛ. Na Biribi kae sɛ, Kɔ twitwa ɔdwen dua mma mmienu, na to wo aniwa mu, na dwane fa baako na fa si baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ, na fa baako no nso na dwane fa si baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ. Ɔbaa no, ɔkae sɛ, Mawie. Biribi kae sɛ, Fa dawuru (dawuro) kɛseɛ no na bɔ no. Na ɔbɔɔ no, na nwura no bɛyɛɛ akuraa a ɛda dua mma mmienu no ntam. Na odum-dua no gyina ne mfinimfini. Na saa Ade no bɛdaa ne ho adi sɛ ɛyɛ Ɔkɔdeɛ. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no sɛ, sɛ asɛmpɔw titiriw bi ba atenaeɛ a, ɛsɛ sɛ wɔtie no wɔ odum-dua no ase. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Ho, mate. Bere a Ɔkɔdeɛ no aka eyi akyerɛ no, na ɔno (ɔbɔfoɔ no) anya yernom, mpɔtam badwafoɔ, ne asomfoɔ. Ɛduruu saa berɛ no, na ne yere (a wɔfrɛ no) Some-day-the-ungrateful-one-will-remember no anya ne ho hwam sɛ ɔyɛ ɔhene, enti ɔtɔɔ nnuadewa, na ɔde sii kwan so kɔɔ ɔhene akuraa ase, na na ne abofra no wɔ nakyire. Ɛduruu hɔ no, ɔbɔɔ sianteɛ sɛ ɔreba, na ɔkaa sɛ, Wo abofra no ka sɛ ɔde nsa na ɛreba sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ wanim. Na ɔgyee nsa no, na ɔyɛɛ nsa a wɔde ba biara. Na ɔfaa ɔbaa no de no maa Gyaasehene sɛ ɔmfa no nkɔ ne fie. Ɛduruu anwummere a ɛdwoɔ no, ɔbaa no hohoroo ne ho kɔɔ baabi a na ɔhene no wɔ ne dan mu. Ɔbaa no ka kyerɛɛ ɔhene no sɛ, Wo ne me anya osuo ne awia, ɛnnɛ da a woanya baabi atena, na anka ɛnyɛ na wode nkra bɛfrɛ me? Afei berɛ a ɔrekasa sɛɛte sɛ mmarima ne wɔn nkwasiasɛmno, na ɔnni hwee a ɔbɛka. Ɛhɔ ara na ɔkyeree ɔbaa no nsɔmu, na ɔne no daeɛ. Anɔpa kɛseɛ a ɛdi hɔ no, ɔyii no firii ne atenaeɛ a ɛwɔ Gyaasehene no, na ɔde no kɔɔ ɔhene no atenaeɛ. Ɛhɔ na ɔtenaa kɔsii sɛ akyinnyegyeɛ kɛseɛ bi baeɛ. Ɔhene ne ne mpɔtam badwafoɔ nyinaa kɔtenaa odum dua no ase. Ɔbaa no ba no gyinaa nagya anim, na ɔhunuu akɔre ba no wɔ odum dua no atifi, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya, mepɛ no. " Na nagya bɔɔ no akyi, na ɔpiapiaa netire no akyi, na ɔmaa no kɔɔ ne fie. Na mmea no mma no mu baako nso baeɛ, na ɔno nso bɛgyinaa nanim. Ɔsan nso hunuu akɔre ba no, na ɔkaa sɛ, Agya, mepɛ no. Na ɔbɔɔ no fam wɔ saa kwan koro no ara so na ɔhyɛɛ netire no akyi ma ɛhwee ase, na ɔrekɔ no, ɔtee su. Na ne maame kaa sɛ, Dɛn na woresu? Na ɔkae sɛ, Agya bɔɔ me. Na ne maame kaa sɛ, Oh! dɛn nti na ɔmmɔɔ wo, sɛ Some-day-the-ungrateful-one-will-remember abofra no na ɛyɛ a, anka ɔremmɔ no. Na Some-day-the-ungrateful-one-will-remember kaa sɛ, Me deɛ, menyɛɛ wo hwee sɛ wobɛyɛ me saa. Ɔkaa no pɛ na wɔn mmienu nyinaa tuu mmirika kɔbɔɔ mu ko. Na asomfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Saa ade pɔtee bi a ɛwɔ dua yi atifi nti, yɛn agya yerenom reko; yɛbɛtwa no. Ntɛm ara na wɔnyaa wɔn nkuma, pimpɛn! pimpɛn! (na ɛyɛ nnyigyei a wɔyɛeɛ no.) Saa berɛ no na Ɔkɔdeɛ no akɔ akɔnkyini na ɔde ne mmofra kɔgyaa wɔn wɔ Koterɛ ne Old Hawks sɔboɔ no mu. Wɔ berɛ a nnipa no hwee dua no gui na ɛrehwe ase no, na Koterɛ no teɛɛ nkonyaayisɛm sɛ, "Pimpeao! " Na dua no abom. Na wɔsan baa so bio. Na Koterɛ no ka kyerɛɛ Old Hawk no sɛ, "Kɔ hwehwɛ Ɔkɔdeɛ sɛ ɔmmɛhwɛ ne mmofra no, na me deɛ, owuo aba me so. " Ntɛm ara na nnipa no kɔe kɔgyee wɔn atuo na wɔtoo no tuo kunkum no. Na Old Hawk tuu mmirika de ahyɛdeɛ kɔɔ frɛ Ɔkɔdeɛ. Ɛkwan no nyinaa a na ɔnam so no, na ɔsu frɛ sɛ:

"Nnananom e! mo mma o!

Nanabaa e! wo mmofra o! "

Bere a ɔfrɛɛ saa na Ɔkɔdeɛ no antie no, ɔmaa ne nne so su:

"Sankur" e, Anomaa E, sankur' e, Anomaa, Ɔkɔdeɛ.

Sankur' e, Anomaa E, sankur' e, sɛ woakɔdidi a, san ba. "

Na Ɔkɔdeɛ no tee. Na ɔbaa no bɛkaa sɛ, "Dɛn na eyi nyinaa kyerɛ? " Na Old Hawk kaa sɛ, "Wɔabɔ dua no wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ wɔbɛfa wo mmofra no, na Koterɛ deɛ, wɔabɔ no tuo akum no. " Ɛhɔ ara na Ɔkɔdeɛ no sɔreeeɛ, wa (ɛno ne nnyigyei a ne ntaban no yɛeɛ), na ɔtenaa dua bi so wɔ kuro no kwanano, na ɔmaa naniwa so, na ɛyɛ nokware sɛ wɔretwa dua no. Ɔkaa hwee. Na afei bere a dua no bɔeɛ na ɛkɔɔ fam no, Ɔkɔdeɛ no tuu mmirika kɔɔ hɔ, ho (na ɛyɛ ne ntaban no nnyigyei), na ɔkyeree ne mmofra no. Ɔfaa wɔn kɔtoo dua a ɔdaa hɔ no so. Na afei ɔbaa fam na ɔbisaa ne ho (lit. wɔ ne etire mu), "Ah, so mmarima te sɛɛ? " Na ɛma no werɛ how. Na ɔsu, na ɔka sɛ:

"Except the tuo, except the ɔwɔ. "

Na ɔtui ne fam, na ɔgyee atwene Bedeɛ mmienu, na ɔhwee wɔn na ɔde nkra yi kɔmaa:

"Ma abɔdeɛ a wɔwɔ ntakra nyinaa mmra, (nkosi sɛ ewiem bɛyɛ) tuntum, tuntum, tuntum. "

Na abɔdeɛ a wɔwɔ ntakra no nyinaa baeɛ. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn nyinaa nea asi, na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wɔne no kɔ da Gyekye ase, na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔn a wɔnim su nyinaa su. Anomaa kumaa bi a wɔfrɛ no Apitie na ɔdii kan hyɛɛ aseɛ su:

"Mewu wu mpofirim!

Mewu wu mpofirim! "

Apatupere anomaa no teɛɛm sɛ:

"Mempɛ akasakasa!

Mempɛ akasakasa!

Mempɛ Kosante ba no ntoma. "

Akɔkɔhwedɛɛ a ɔwɔ nan kɔkɔɔ no teɛɛm sɛ:

"Owuraye no hoe, hoe, O!

Owuraye asɔ, asɔ, O!

Owuraye asɔ, asɔ, asɔ, O! " Na atewa ketewa no teɛɛm sɛ:

"Doye!

Doye! Doye!

Afua Anwea ne ne kunu Ano Kofi, Sɛ abofra tu kwan bɛwo no ara pɛ a, ɛno nyɛ ade pa, Kwaku Amoaten (din).

Doye! Doye!

Doye! Doye!

Odiawuo ne ne Ano Mmirikisiie a ɛbɔ Ɔsono se no, Wafi no, Wafi no.

Agya Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ, sɔre na kɔpɛ Patti-nosed monkyi a wubetumi ayɛ no nkwan ne anonne, (bere a ɔbɔmmɔfo no yere keka saa no,)

Ɛnyɛ abɔdeɛ (who hears her say so) na ɛrebɛba dua no ase (where they are). "

Ɔpɔdee no teɛɛm sɛ:

"Kyakyakya! nkawurubi!

(Hwɛ) saa akokoaa no tuo tuntum tenten no, ɔde reto me so, ahyɛdeɛ sɛ ebia ɔbɛwe me ne ne plantain a wɔayam no bi. "

Ɔpatuo, Patu no, teɛɛm sɛ:

"E! Mm!

E! Mm!"

Ɔnwam, Anwam no, teɛɛm sɛ:

"(Hunter) Akuaferi-Saka the little red one! "

Hornbill yere no ka sɛ:

"Ɔnni hɔ. "

Anwam no ka sɛ:

""Aso" (ne yere), didi na yɛnkɔ. "

Na Saka de ne tuo bɔ no, pa! Na ne yere tu kɔ na ɔka sɛ:

"Ɔwɔ hɔ!

Ɔwɔ hɔ! "

Na aserewa ketewa no teɛɛm sɛ:

"Obeko Asamoa, Bere a kwasea kɛkɛ bɔ ne nnuadewa-bobe no, anɔpa ntɛm ara na nnipa rekɔ nakyi ne nanim! "

Aburuburo, Aburuburo no, teɛɛm sɛ:

"Bere a maame rewu no, ɔgyaee me wɔ nkorabata sɔboɔ bi mu;

Bere a branch a ɛyɛ forked no wui no, ɛgyaa me wɔ sɔboɔ a ɛyɛ mfeɛ a abe nhaban adi;

Bere a abe nhaban no mfeɛ wuiɛ no, ɛmaa me kɔɔ baa a akɔntɔn sɔboɔ mu;

Bere a baa a akɔntɔn no kyeree me no, ɛyɛɛ me basabasa, basabasa, basabasa! "

Akyenkyena anomaa no teɛɛm sɛ:

Ɛyɛ me, ɛyɛ me, ɛyɛ me. " Na afei na Ɔkɔdeɛ no ankasa baeɛ, na ɔteɛm sɛ:

"Nkensen nkensen, abɔdeɛ a wɔwɔ ntakra bebree!

meda Gyekye ase!

Nkensen nkensen, feathered abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ ya ya! "

(Na bere a ɔresu saa no) nnipa a wɔwɔ kuro no mu nyinaa dwanee fii ho, na Gyekye anhu baabi a mmarima, odwan, mmaa, yernom ne nkokɔ anaa biribiara kɔeɛ. Na ɔno ne ne yere ne ne abofra nko ara na wɔaka, wɔ odum dua no dua sin no nkyɛn. Na Ɔkɔdeɛ no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Efi da yi kɔsi nnɛ ɔbɔfoɔ biara renyɛ ɔdefoɔ da. " Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi fa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na ahomegyeɛ no nso fa kamfo me (ka ho asɛmka).
