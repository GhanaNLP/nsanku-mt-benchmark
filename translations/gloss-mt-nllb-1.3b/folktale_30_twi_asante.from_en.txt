Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ABIRABƆ YI BAA ABUSUAKUO MU

NA obarima bi a wɔfrɛ no Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted wɔ hɔ, na ɛno nti, ɔkɔsii akuraa ketewa bi na ɔkɔtenaa mu. Na abɔdeɛ a wɔfrɛ no Ɔtwee no kɔsraa no, na ɔne no kɔtenaa hɔ wɔ abotan bi nan ase. Na mmɛ no bi atɔ. Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, "Agya Ɔtan a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔsɔre tia no, wo mmɛ abere. " Ɔtan a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔsɔre tia no kaa sɛ, "Saa na ɛyɛ abɛ abɔdeɛ; sɛ abere a, asaã ahorow mmiɛnsa na ɛbere prɛko pɛ; sɛ abere a, na metwitwa, na sɛ mɛnoa de yi ngo no, na ɛyɛ nsuo abura mmiɛnsa a ngo ahyɛ mu ma; na mede ngo no kɔ Akase kɔtɔ Akase aberewa; Akase aberewa no ba na ɔde ma awoɔ ma me nanabea, a ɔsoa me maame, a dane mu no ɔsoa me; maame bere a ɔsoa me no, na me gyina hɔ dedaw. " Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, "Ne nyinaa ni, wo di atoro. " Na Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted no faa poma bɔɔ Ɔtwee no etire, na okum no. Afei abedee ketewa no nso baeɛ. Na ɔne no kɔtenaa nnuadewa no ase, na adeɛ korɔ no ara na ɛkɔɔ so. Na saa na ɛyɛɛ mmoa no nyinaa. Afei, Kwaku Ananse, Ananse no, kɔfaa ne ntoma, na ɔfaa ne kotokuo, de too ne mmatiri so, na ɔkɔɔ Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted no akuraa ase. Ɔkuraa no ase sɛ, "Agya, anɔpa pa. " Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted no buae sɛ, "Y'Aku, na ɛhe na worekɔ? " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Meba wo nkyɛn. " Na ɔfaa ne akonnwa no, na ɔde siee nnuadewa no ase. Ɔtan a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔsɔre tia no kae sɛ, "Monnoa aduane mma Ananse nni. " Na bere a ɛnoa no, na Kwaku Ananse ne Ɔtan a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔsɔre tia no te hɔ wɔ aduane no ase. Na mmɛ no bi atɔ ase. Na Kwaku Ananse faa no siee ne kotokuo mu. Na ɔkɔ so yɛ saa kosii sɛ ne kotokuo no hyɛɛ ma. Wɔde aduane no bae, na Kwaku Ananse dii. Bere a owiee adidi no, mmɛ no bi guu fam, na Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Agya Ɔtan a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔsɔre tia no, wo mmɛ abere. " Ɔtan a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔsɔre tia no kae sɛ, "Wɔn abɔdeɛ ne sɛ wobere saa; sɛ wɔabere a, asaã ahorow abiɛsa na ɛbere prɛko pɛ; sɛ wɔabere a, mitwitwa, na sɛ mehyɛ no ma menyi ngo no a, ɛyɛ nsuo abura mmiɛnsa a ngo ahyɛ mu ma, na mema ngo no kɔ Akas e, kɔtɔ Akase aberewa; Akase aberewa no ba ma me nanabea a ɔwo me maame no awoɔ, sɛ ɔnwo me dane. Bere a maame bɛwo me no, na migyina hɔ dedaw. " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Worenni atoro, nea woka no yɛ nokware; me de, mewɔ nkruma bi a ɛsi wɔ mafuo mu, na sɛ ɛbere a, me ne nnua tenten aduɔson nson bom (ma metumi atwe wɔn aba fam), nanso ɛno mpo ntumi nkɔ, enti meda makyi, na mitumi de me kɔteɛ tu. " Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted kae sɛ, "Oh, mete ase. d; kɔsi ɔkyena mebɛhwɛ. " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Surely. " Bere a Ananse no rekɔ (home), ɔwe mmɛ a ɔaboaboa ano no, na ɔpae agu kwan no so. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma fii aseɛ bɛdaa adi no, na Hate-to-be-contradicted no asiesie ne ho sɛ ɔbɛkɔ Ananse no akuraa ase. Afei berɛ a Ananse no dii kan baeɛ (da a ɛdi kan), na wakɔka kyerɛɛ ne mmofra no sɛ, obarima bi bɛba ha, na ɔtan sɛ wɔkasa tia no, na berɛ a ɔbɛba abɛbisa me akyi no, ɛsɛ sɛ woka kyerɛ no sɛ ɛnora na mekaeɛ sɛ na mefiri baabi rekɔ, berɛ a me kɔteɛ no bubuu wɔ mbeaeɛ nson, na ɛsɛ sɛ mede kɔma tomfoɔ sɛ ɔnsiesie no, na ɔantumi anwie no wɔ baabi. bere, na ɔse wakɔ ama adwuma no asa. Ɛnkyɛ na Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted nso baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Wagya no akɔ he? Wɔbuaa sɛ, Awerɛhoɔ, agya no kɔɔ baabi ɛnora, na ne kɔteɛ no abubu wɔ mmeae ahodoɔ nson, na ɔde kɔmaa tomfoɔ bi, nanso wantumi anwie adwuma no wɔ berɛ ano, na agya no akɔma no awie, na wo, Agya, woanhu mogya no wɔ kwan mu anaa? Ɔtan a wɔsɔre tia no kaa sɛ, Me na mehuu no. Na Ɔtan a wɔsɔre tia no bisaa sɛ, ɛhe na wo maame wɔ? (The Spiders child answered,) Maame nso, ɛnora na ɔkɔɔ asuwa no, na ne nsuo-kotoku no bɛhwe aseɛ abubu sɛ wannye no ankɔyɛ saa ara denam berɛ a ɔbɛkyere no nko ara so, nanso wanwie koraa annye no ankɔhwe ase, na wasan aba da a ɛsɛ sɛ ɔyɛ saa. Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted no anka hwee. Seesei Kwaku Ananse aba. Ɔkae sɛ, Cook some aduane that Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted may eat. Bere a mmofra no resi aduane no, na wɔde apatre ketewa baako pɛ na ɛmo dodoɔ kɛse na ɛyɛ, na wɔmaa nkwan no yɛɛ hyew paa. Sɛ wɔwie a, na wɔde gu Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted anim. Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted no dii. Afei na ɛmo no yɛ no yaw, na ɔpɛ sɛ ɔwu. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ntikuma (Ananse abofra), fa nsuo ma me nnom. Ntikuma kɔɔ nsuo no mu, wamfa nsuo no amba da. Ɔtan a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔsɔre tia no kae sɛ, Ntikuma, ɛhe na saa nsuo no wɔ? Ntikuma kae sɛ, Ah, nsuo a yɛwɔ ha (yɛn nsuo kuku no mu) no yɛ ahodoɔ mmiɛnsa; ɛno deɛ ɛyɛ agya deɛ no, ɛdi kan, ɛno deɛ ɛyɛ me maame yere deɛ no wɔ mfinimfini, na ɛno deɛ ɛyɛ me ankasa me maame deɛ no wɔ fam (kuku no mu); na mepɛ sɛ metwe ma wo nsuo a ɛyɛ me ankasa me maame deɛ no nko ara, sɛ ɛnyɛ saa a make ahwɛyie kɛseɛ berɛ a wɔretwe no, ɛbɛma abusuakuw mu mansotwe aba. Ɔtan a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔsɔre tia no kae sɛ, Wɔmɔ abofra, wodi atoro. Ntɛm ara, Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Bɔ no ma onwu. Ɔtan a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔsɔre tia no kae sɛ, Adɛn nti na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔboro me ma miwu? Ananse no kae sɛ, Wose ɔtan sɛ wɔbɛsɔre atia wo, na woakasa atia obi, ɛno nti na meka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wɔboro wo ma wo d ie. Enti wɔbɔɔ Ɔtan a wɔrebɛkasa atia no ma ɔwuiɛ. Afei Kwaku Ananse twaa ne honam mu asinasin nketenkete na wɔtrɛw mu. Ɛno nti na nnipa pii a ɔtan sɛ wɔbɛkasa atia wɔn no wɔ abusuakuo no mu.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
