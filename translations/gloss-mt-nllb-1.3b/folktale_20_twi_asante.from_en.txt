Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

WƆKYERƐ SƐ, bere bi a ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bae no, na saa Kwaku Ananse no aka sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ nam ne nhabannuane na ɔde aba sɛ ɔne ne yere "Aso" nni. Ɔkɔɔ asuo bi mu na ɔkɔhyiaa nnipa, sesei saa nnipa yi a ɔkɔhyiaa wɔn no, kyɛ me abebuo sɛ, na wɔyɛ ahonhom. Bere a Kwaku Ananse hyiaa Ahonhom no, na wɔregyina nsuo no mu na wɔrepete nsubɔnten no mu ma ɛyow sɛ wɔrebɛyi apataa. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Brothers, may I come and splash a little too? " Ahonhom no kae sɛ, "Come. " Kwaku Ananse kɔe, na ɔhunuu sɛ wɔde wɔn ti nnompe rebɔ nsutene no ma ɛyow. Ahonhom no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, "Woahunu sɛ nea yɛde petepete nsubɔnten no mu ma ɛyow no, wobɛma yɛn kwan ma yɛayi wo tifi dompe no wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ wobɛtete nso? " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Mepene so, yi fi me so. " Ɛyɛ nokware, Ahonhom no yii no maa no. Kwaku Ananse ne Ahonhom no bɔɔ mu petepete nsubɔnten a ɛyow no mpa. Bere a wɔrepete no, Ahonhom no too dwom sɛ:

"Yɛn, Ahonhom, sɛ yɛpete asubɔnten no mu ma ɛyow de akyere apataa a, yɛde yɛn atitirefoɔ petepete nsuo no mu,

Oh, the Spirits, we're splashing the nsuo. "

Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Saa dwom yi yɛ dɛ, metumi ato bi ato hɔ? " Ahonhom no kaa sɛ, "Toto bi. " Na ɔmaa ne nne so sɛ,

"The Spirits, yɛrepɔ nsuo no, yɛde yɛn atitirefoɔ rekɔpɔ nsuo no.

Oh, the Spirits, we're splashing the nsuo.

Ɔbɔadeɛ yɛɛ nneɛma nti, yɛde yɛn atitirefoɔ kɔpue nsuo anaa?

Oh, Ahonhom, yɛrehyɛ nsuo no. Mefa me etire bɛhyɛ nsuo no dry-to-da oh, Ahonhom, yɛrehyɛ nsuo no. "

Kwaku Ananse wiee dwom no, na Ahonhom no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Yɛsplashed, yɛanya apataa, wo kyɛfa yɛ kɛntɛn ma, fa kɔ di, fa wo tifi dompe, bɔ (wo nipadua so), na kɔ kɔ; nanso nea yɛwɔ ka titiriw ne sɛ, da a wobɛto saa dwom no mu biara no, wo tifi dompe bɛbue na atɔ. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Apataa a ɛwɔ mborosoɔ a wode ama me no, ne nyinaa. Me kɔn dɔ, na dwom deɛ, ɛdɛn sianteɛ nti na ɛsɛ sɛ me to? " Ahonhom no kaa sɛ, "Ɛno yie, kɔ. " Ananse no kɔɔ kwan so. Ahonhom no nso, wɔn nsa kaa ne nyinaa, na wɔn nso kɔe. Ahonhom no duu baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ no, anka ɛbɛyɛ sɛ wɔmaa wɔn dwom so sɛ:

"Yɛn, Ahonhom, sɛ yɛpete asubɔnten no mu ma ɛyow de kɔyi apataa a, yɛn atitirefoɔ na yɛde petepete nsuo no mu.

Oh, the Spirits, we're splashing the nsuo. "

Na Ananse tee dwom no, na ɔno nso faa so:

"Ɛfiri berɛ a Ɔbɔadeɛ yɛɛ nneɛma no, yɛde yɛn atitirefoɔ akɔ petepete nsuo no anaa? "

Oh, the Spirits, we're splashing the nsuo. "

Ɛnkyɛree na ɔwieeɛ no, ne tifi dompe no bueeɛ na ɛhwee aseɛ. Kwaku Ananse maa no so, na ɔde hyɛɛ ne kokoɔ so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ahonhom, Ahonhom, me etire no abɔ ase. " Ahonhom no tee, na wɔkae sɛ, "Ɛno ne Ananse no, ɔrentie nea yɛaka akyerɛ no, na ɔfrɛ yɛn, yɛn nyinaa yɛnni akyi nkɔtie nea ɔbɛka. " Ɛkame ayɛ sɛ ɛhɔ ara na Ananse no de ahopere baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Puo! Me agya mmofra, me etire abue na atɔ fam, enti me srɛ mo, sɛ mayɛ mo opira bi a, mom fa kyɛ me; mo wɔ hokwan, nanso fa me etire na fa si ne baabi bio ma me. " Ahonhom no faa no, na wɔde sii ananmu. Wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Seesei sɛ woto dwom yi bio a, na ɛtɔ fam bio a, sɛ wo frɛ yɛn a, yɛr mmua, enti fa wo ho kɔ. " Ahonhom no san fii aseɛ bio. Na wɔrekɔ no, wɔtoo wɔn dwom. Na sesei Kwaku Ananse refiri aseɛ reto dwom bio, na ne etire no mu apaeɛ na ɛhwee ase, kutukum! Na ɔfaa no, na ɔpaa me kasafĩ ne ahohwisɛm, ɔbɔɔ no akyi berɛmo, na ɔhwee aseɛ kɔɔ kwan nkyɛn, sora! (yɛɛ wira mu paepae no nnyigyei bere a ɔhyɛn mu no). Ɔkaa sɛ, Kwan, gye me, na sɛ adeɛ kyee a meyɛ ɔdefoɔ a, mɛma wo bi. Ɛno nti na wobɛhu Kwaku Ananse sɛ ɔwɔ ti ketewaa bi ne ase a ɛyɛ kɛse paa; ne nyinaa fi naso denyeɛ.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
