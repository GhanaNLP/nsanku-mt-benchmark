Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

KWAMENA, ANT-HILL A ƐWƆ DIBEA A ƐTO SO ABIEN, A ƐWƆ NHINI A AGYINA PINTINN MU

WƆKA SƐ, bere bi na Ɔhemmaa babaa bi wɔ hɔ, na ɛmfa ho nea wɔde no kɔhyɛɛ awareɛ no, na ɔbɛka sɛ ɔmpɛ no. Nanso (awiei koraa) Nsaman-saman Ɔhene, Adu Gyamfi bi, baeɛ na ɔwaree saa Ɔhemmaa abofra no. Ɔhemmaa no ba no ka kyerɛɛ ne kunu no sɛ, "Me ne wo bɛkɔ wo kuro mu. " Ɔbarima no buaa no sɛ, "Ma me nni kan, na sɛ Adae tɔ Kwasiada a, ɛdwoada a ɛdi hɔ yi na me bɛba abɛfa wo. " Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "Oh no, me ne wo bɛkɔ abom. " Enti wɔkɔɔ so. Bere a wɔrekɔ no, wɔhunuu kuropɔn bi a ɛwɔ dadeɛ pono wɔ ne kwan ano, na dadeɛ atwa kuro no ho ahyia. Afei, wɔ kuro yi mu, sɛ wo mogya-busuani biara anwu, (na) wogyinaa ɛpono no akyi frɛɛ a, obiara mmua wo. Afei, saa Queen Mother no ba no deɛ, ne busuani biara anwu da. Ɔkasa ne ne mfɛfoɔ (i. e. me werɛ firii sɛ mebɛka saa) bere a Adu Gyamfi duu dadeɛ pono no, afei ɔkɔɔ ne fie, ɔnne Queen Mother ba no nni nkɔmmɔ bio. Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no babea ne nafena Ɔkra tenaa hɔ, na wɔgyinaa dadeɛ pono no akyi, na wɔannya kwan ankɔ fie. Na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no maa ne nne so (na ɔtoo dwom):

"Adodowa, bue me, me na aba yi, Adodowa Kwasi Kampire, bue me, Adodowa, bue me o. "

Nanso Adodowa mmue kwan mma no. Ɔno nso anhu kwan a ɔfa so san kɔ ne kuro mu. Ɛnna ɔne Ɔkra kɔɔ nhini a agyina pintinn mmienu ntam. Na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no faa kɔbereandesɛn bi tenaa so, na ɔfaa foforɔ de kataa ne etire so, na Ɔkra tenaa ne nkyɛn. Ɛno nti, sɛ wokɔ faako a nhini a agyina pintinn no, ɛhɔ na ahene ɛna no ba ne ne abaawa Kra te; wɔadane nwansena a wɔfrɛ no "Kwamena" a ɛsɔre gyinabea so gyinabea.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
