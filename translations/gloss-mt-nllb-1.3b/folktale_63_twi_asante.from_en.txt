Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ABOSOM, NYAMESOM NKETEWA NO, BAA ABUSUAKUO MU

Ɔbɔfoɔ bi ne ne yere tenaa abɔfoɔ bi atenaeɛ. Na wɔyɛ ahiafoɔ, na wɔte hɔ reko ama wɔn hɔyɛ. Ná ɔbɔfoɔ no sɔre kɔ kwaeɛwɔ ahuhudeɛ mu. Berɛ a ɔbaa fie no, na ɔhyehyɛ apataa-ntokuro wɔ asubonten mu, na ɔfa apataa ne nsunam nketewa no de kɔma ne yere. Ɛhɔ na na wɔwɔ, na ne yere rebisa no asɛm sɛ, Gye di sɛ sɛ wokum nam a, wode ma, anaa wotra tɔn na wodi (mfasoɔ)), ɛnneɛ da a wobɛkɔ nwura na woamfa nam biara ankɔ, mebɛsan akɔ me akuraa. Ɔmanpanyin no de nadwene kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu. Ɔnam ne nan so, na wannya ayamye aboa biara. Ɔkaa ne ho sɛ, Oh, mahu mmusu. Afei, berɛ a ɔrehwɛ no, ɔhunuu Ɔtwee a ɔnam baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Wo deɛ, Ɔtwee, mɛkum wo. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no dii nakyi sɛ, kyiri! kyiri! kyiri! (na ɛyɛ ne anan nnyigyei). Ɔtwee no dwanee, na ɔkɔɔ dua bi nhini a agyina pintinn ntam. Ɔ (Ɔtwee no) kaa sɛ, Grandfather Tano, me dwane kɔ wo nkyɛn bammɔ sɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no bɛgyaa me. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Me deɛ, mennim adeɛ a wɔfrɛ no Tano, enti pass on that I may take you and go give it to my yere. Tano gyinaa hɔ, na ɔhyɛɛ ɔkasa mu sɛ, Ɔbɔfoɔ, gyae Ɔtwee no na fa *me fie, na mfasoɔ pii wɔ me so ma wo. Ɔbɔfoɔ no gyinaa hɔ, na ɔwosow, biribiri. Ɔfaa no (Tano) de no too ne etire so. Na ɔde no reba no, na Owuo ahyia no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔbɔfoɔ, ɛhe na wode me < ɔkyeame (spokesman) no rekɔ? " Ɛnna ɔbɔfoɔ no buaa sɛ, "Agya, maniwa abere; na merehwehwɛ nam akɔma me yere no, berɛ a me kɔhunuu Ɔtwee no, na berɛ a merebɛkum no, na ɔka sɛ waguan bammɔ ama saa adeɛ yi; mekaeɛ sɛ menyim hwee. Saa adeɛ yi (ɛneɛ) ɔkae sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mefa no fie, ɛfiri sɛ na mfasoɔ pii wɔ mu. " Owuo bisaa Tano sɛ, "Worekɔ fie, na wunim sɛ sɛ obi yare a, mefa me mpa a wɔasɛn, na meba abɛto ne nkyɛn a, ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara hyɛ mu, enti sɛ worekɔ fie a, nea mewɔ ka kyerɛ wo ne sɛ, sɛ obi yare a, na menfa no nkɔ, mebɛbɔ yo amanneɛ u, na wubegye biribi akatua ama me, na saa akatua no de, mogya nko na mepɛ, na woanya honam no. " Minor-god no ka kyerɛɛ Owuo sɛ, "Grandsire, metee, master. " Ɛneɛ na ɔbɔfoɔ no faa Tano, na ɔde no baa fie. Tano gyinaa hɔ no, ɔtoo dwom, na wɔde wɔn ho bɔɔ kuw no mu. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi, na berɛ a obi bi yareeɛ no, wɔde no kɔɔ hɔ kɔɔ Tano. Ɔ (Tano) kaa sɛ, "Wobɛwu. " Ɛyɛ nokware, ɔwuiɛ. (To another) ɔkaa sɛ, "Worentumi nwu, enti fa eyi ne nea no mmra. " Berɛ a obi bɛgyee nneɛma no, na ɔno (the lesser-god) aka sɛ, "Me kɔ srɛ nkwa ma wo. " Na ɛnam sɛ wode (saa afɔdeɛ yi) kɔmaa Tano nti, nokware koraa na woayɛ yie. Saa kwan yi so na yɛhu sɛ lesser-gods no yɛ benevolent; enti nnipa kɔ hɔ kɔ Tano na wɔgye ne mmofra no bi.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
