Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ ATURUKUKU, AKOKOƆ MMOA NO, KƆƆ NWURA SO, NA ƆMAA AKƆKƆƆ, HƐN NO, BAA FIE HƆ

AKƆKƆ, Hɛn, ne Aturukuku, Dapota no, wɔfiri agya baako mu na wɔfiri maame baako mu. Mprempren Hɛn ka kyerɛɛ Dapota no sɛ, Kɔ sare tenten so ɔman no mu, na me nso mɛkɔ kwaeɛ kɛseɛ no mu, na sɛ woanya aduane a, wobɛba abɛka akyerɛ me. Ɛnna wɔtetew mu. Aturukuku anhu ne nuabea mfeɛ mmiɛnsa. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne kunu no sɛ, Accompany me, efisɛ me rekɔ hwehwɛ me nuabea. Ɛnna ne kunu no kaa sɛ, Sɛ woaka yi, metee, kɔ, na ma me ne wo nkɔ. Na ɔne no fii hɔ kɔduruu kuro bi kuro akyi (baabi a na ne nuabea no te). Wɔhunuu pɔkɔsɔ bepɔw ketewa bi (atɛkyɛ) wɔ hɔ, na ɛhɔ na ɔne ne nuabea no hyiaa. Na Hɛn bisaa Turtle-Dove no sɛ, Bere a wokɔɔ Tall-Grass-country no, wunyaa aduane a wobɛdi anaa? Ɔbuaa sɛ, Baabi a mekɔɔ no, na guinea-grain nko ara na ɛwɔ hɔ; na berɛ a me yii me mmofra, bɛyɛ baanum, na wɔde wɔn ho bɔɔ me, na berɛ a na wɔn kɛseɛ te sɛɛ (a ɛkyerɛ wɔn kɛseɛ) no, mehyɛɛ wɔn bɛn me, na ɔbɔfoɔ bi tuu ne tuo, pom! na wɔakum me mmofra, na anka sɛ minnim adwene a, anka wɔbɛkum me nso. Bere a ɔkasae wiei no, Hɛn kae sɛ, Baabi a mekɔɔ no, ntɛm ara na me bɛyɛɛ onipa a hia a ɛho hia ankasa. Aturukuku bisaa no sɛ, Ka kyerɛ me, dɛn na wopɛ sɛ woka fa wo onipa a hia a ɛho hia ankasa no ho? Ɛnna Hɛn kae sɛ, The aseɛ a ɛyɛ onipa a hia a ɛho hia ankasa no ne sɛ, sɛ nhwɛsoɔ no, sɛ me wura trɛw kɛtɛ pa a, t Hen me kɔ da so, na me mmoa bin tɔ so, na sɛ me wura fa nam bɛto soup-stew no mu a, na meyi nea mepɛ; aduane biara a me wura bɛdi no, me kɔ fa bi, na sɛ me di saa a, me wura ntumi mfa ne tuo anaa ne poma mmɔ me. Kyerɛ sɛ me nti na meka sɛ meyɛ onipa a ɔwɔ hia a ɛho hia ankasa. Hɛn kae sɛ, Wote hɔ no, ɛnyɛ baabi pa, enti Memeneda bi, ansa na Akwasidae bɛba no, fa wo mmofra, ne wo kunu, na momma wɔn nyinaa ntena yɛn fie ha. Aturukuku kae sɛ, Mate, mebɛkɔ, na Memeneda a ɛdi Akwasidae anim no, mebɛba. Afei na ɔsɔfoɔ bi baa Hen akuraa no ase, na ɔhinhim. ing ne dɔn nkumaa. Wɔbisaa no sɛ, Nana, ɛyɛ dɛn? Ɔkaa sɛ, Dente kyerɛ sɛ woama no kwan sɛ ɔnni kɔm mfeɛ mmienu ni, enti ɛsɛ sɛ wo kɔ ɔhene nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛ no sɛ ɔmfa nkokɔ ne nkosua dumienu mmra. Ɛnna Ɔhene no kɔkyeree nkokɔ anan, de nkosua dumien bae, kɔfaa na ɛtɔ a wɔawɔ, na ɔde kɔmaa dente bosom no wɔ ne dɔte afɔrebukyia a ano yɛ sɔnn no mu, na ɔtwaa nkokɔ anan no atitirefoɔ no na ɔhyɛɛ wɔn. na ɔkyekyeree wɔn wɔ hɔ, na ɔfaa wɔn ntakra ne wɔn atitirefoɔ, ne wɔn anan, na ɔde gluu guu dɔte afɔrebukyea no nkyɛn, na ɔsan nso de ɛtɔ no hyɛɛ mu. Afei, da a ɛdi Adae no, na Aturukuku rekɔhyia ne nuabea no. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu afɔrebukyia a ɛyɛ kronkron ma Dente, ne nuabea no agyanom, ne ne etire, ne anan, ne ntaban, ne berɛboɔ, ne ne nsono. Aturukuku kaa sɛ, "Eyi nyinaa akyi no, merentumi nkɔ hɔ bio. " Na turtle-dove no tu kɔɔ wim, kɔtenaa silk-cotton dua bi so, na ɔkaa (too dwom) sɛ, "Me werɛ how ma me nuabea, Awerɛhow o!

Wo Hɛn, a na woyɛ onipa a ɔwɔ mfasoɔ bi, ɛno ne wo etire?

Hɛn, wo a na woyɛ onipa a ɔwɔ mfasoɔ bi, ɛyɛ wo ntaban yi?

Wo Hɛn, a na woyɛ onipa a ɔwɔ mfasoɔ bi, ɛyɛ wo anan ni?

Hɛn, wo a na woyɛ onipa a ɔwɔ mfasoɔ bi, wo berɛboɔ ne yi?

Hɛn, wo a na woyɛ onipa a wowɔ mfasoɔ bi no, ɛyɛ wo nsono yi? "

Na Aturukuku kaa sɛ, "Me nkɔ Kwaeɛ man mu bio, ɛfiri sɛ kwaemfoɔ nni yie. " Saa na Aturukuku bɛtenaa Tall-grass-country, berɛ a Hɛn bɛtenaa fie.

Ɛyi, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (ana) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi afa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a wode bɛkamfo me (asɛmka no.
