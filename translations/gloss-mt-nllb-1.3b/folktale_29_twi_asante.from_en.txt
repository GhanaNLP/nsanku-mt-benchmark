Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA OBI NYAA YERE (NA NEA WƆAGYE NO NKWA) WƆ NKWAN A WƆFA SO KYERƐ NSƐNKYERƐNNE AHODOƆ ASE NO AKWAN SO

NA ƐBAAKO BI A NA ƐWƆ HƆ NO, NA mmarima MMIƐNSA BI TE HƆ A NA WƆPƐ adwuma. Wɔn kɔɔ Tall-grass-country no mu sɛ wɔrekɔtɔ nkyekye ntoma ne nneɛma foforɔ bi. (Wɔ san ba Asante no, wɔmaa wɔn wofase ba no so nneɛma no bi, na wɔkɔɔ so kɔtɔn. Wɔbaa kuro kɛseɛ bi mu. Ɛwɔ kuro no mu no, na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna ba bi wɔ hɔ a ɔpow sɛ ɔbɛware obiara a wɔde no bɛma no awareɛ. Afei na ahɔhoɔ yi (adwadifoɔ) akɔ hɔ (fie), nanso na Ɔhemmaa no abofra no akɔ "ti" ahohoro no. Ɔhemmaa no tɔɔ ntoma mmienu na ɔde siee ne babaa no. Bere a abofra no san bae no, ɔhunuu mmarima mmiɛnsa no. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne aberewa no sɛ, "Ɛnnɛ da yi mahu nea mepɛ no, enti kunkum odwan na fa ma memfa mma wɔn. " Ɛberɛ a wɔwiee aduane no, abaayewa no kɔfaa esɔ, asaawa, bese, ne nkateɛ dua, na wɔde too aduane no so de kɔmaa mmarima no. Na ɔse, " Nea ɔbɛtumi akyerɛkyerɛ wɔn aseɛ mu no, ɔno na meware no. " Na wɔne aduane no kɔe. Ɛnna mmarima no yii ano, na wɔkae sɛ, Sɛ ɛnyɛ Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no na ɔde aduane yi bae a, anka yɛwɔ nsɛmfua bi a yɛbɛbisa no. Na mmarima no wofase ba no kae sɛ, Sɛ mennsuro mo a, anka mɛka saa nneɛma no aseɛ akyerɛ mo. Wɔkae sɛ, Kyerɛkyerɛ mu. Na abofra no buae sɛ, Na abaayewa no kyerɛ sɛ wama nadwene ayɛ fitaa te sɛ asaawa, na sɛ ɔbɛtumi anya yɛn a yɛaba yi mu baako a, ha no, ɛnsɛ sɛ ɔware? Ɛne aseɛ a ɛwɔ esɔ no mu ne sɛ, da bi bɛba a ɔbɛyera; bese no nso kyerɛ sɛ da bi bɛba a ɔbɛma no (ne kunu) anom kola (i. e. ɔbɛdi awerɛho). Na nkateɛ no kyerɛ sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ wodua nkateɛ ma no, na ama afe biara, sɛ ɔkɔhwɛ ne nhini a, wanya bi atwe aba. " Enti, ɔhene ba no ba no waree mmarima no nua ba no. Bere a ɔwaree no, ɔhene ba no ba no bisaa no sɛ, "Wo din de dɛn? " Na ɔkae sɛ, "Me din de Kwaku Susufoɔ," a ɛne sɛ Kwaku-who-takes-to-think ɔhaw. Ɛnna abaayewa no buae sɛ, "Me nso, wɔfrɛ me Akua-who-takes-ɔhaw-to-think. " Ɔde afahyɛ a ɛfata waree no. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ anɔpa no, wɔfafoɔ mmiɛnsa no tu kwan kɔ wɔn kuro mu. Kwaku a ɔfa-ɔhaw-to-think no tenaa ne yere akuraa ase. Ɔtenaa hɔ bɛyɛ afe, na sɛ ɔsɔre anɔpa a, ɔne ne nnamfofoɔ no taa tra ase di agorɔ wɔ nte. Afei, da bi a ɔreyɛ agorɔ yi, ɔbɔɔ marbuls mmienu wɔ tuo toɔ bi so, nanso nnipa a wɔne no redi agorɔ no, wɔkae sɛ wɔremfa mma no, na mpo wɔpɛ sɛ wɔboro no. Ɔtoo sɛnea wɔ nakyi, na ɔhunuu abofra bi a ɔgyina hɔ; ɔfaa ɔboɔ no de maa abofra no sɛ ɔmfa nkɔma ne yere. Afei bere a ne yere nsa kaa ɔboɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Me kunu pɛ sɛ ɔsan kɔ ne kuro mu, efisɛ ɔka sɛ sɛ anomaa tena fam kyɛ dodo a, ɛgye ɔboɔ. " Na Ɔhemmaa no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ ɔkɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ wone no kɔ. " Ɛyɛ nokware, ɔne no kɔeɛ. Na wɔkɔhyiaa Kwaku-who-takes-ɔhaw-to-think uncles no. Afei, bere bi akyi no, wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, " Kwaku-who-takes-ɔhaw-to-think, nya hama a etwa to no bi sɛ yɛbɛkɔ nwura so akyekye fidie wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu akye mborɔnoma a yɛbɛtumi de ahɔhoɔgyeɛ ama wo yere a ɔyɛ ɔhɔhoɔ no. " Enti wɔkɔɔ so, nanso na saa mpanimfoɔ yi (really) apene so sɛ wɔbɛkum no. Wɔn ne Kwaku-who-takes-ɔhaw-to-think kɔɔ so; wɔduruu (a beaeɛ a ɛyɛ ankonam) wɔ ɔkwan so; wɔkyekyeree no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Yɛbɛkum wo. Ɛnna ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, Bere a mekɔɔ me yere akuraa ase no, mihuu sika bebree, na sɛ moamma me kwan ankyerɛ mo baabi a ɛwɔ, na mewu a, morenhu. Wɔbisaa no sɛ, Ɛhe na sika no da? Ɔbuaeɛ sɛ, Ɛwɔ sɛ woyɛ saa. kɔka kyerɛ me yere sɛ matɔ nam, na ɛno (adwetɔfoɔ) kyerɛ sɛ ne boɔ yɛ peredwan du (£80), na ɛsɛ sɛ ɔhwehwɛ kotokuo a wɔde ntoma nketewa ayɛ no mu; na sɛ ɔhwehwɛ hɔ na wanhu a, ma ɔnhwɛ Ɔkrampan kotoku no mu, sɛ wanhu a, ma ɔnhwɛ abɔsoɔ a nsekan wɔ mu no mu. Enti wɔbaa bɛbɔɔ ne yere nkrasɛm no. Na ɔno nso kaa sɛ, The nneɛma are in the village head-man's fie, so let us go and get them. Ɔne wɔn kɔɔ ɔhene no fie, na ɔbaa no kaa amaneɛbɔ no kyerɛɛ ɔhene no sɛ, The lambskin kotokuo means they have bound him; Ɔkrampan no kyerɛ sɛ apɛtɛ no rebɔ mmɔden sɛ wɔbɛdi ne honam; abɔsoɔ a ɛkura nsekan no kyerɛ sɛ wɔrebɛtwa ne menem. Ntɛm ara na ɔpanyin-barima no bɔɔ gong-gong sɛ, wɔnkɔ so nni hɔ nkɔgye Kwaku-who-takes-ɔhaw-to-think. Na wɔne no san baeɛ. Ɔmaa wɔkum mpanimfoɔ mmiɛnsa no. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, Ɛnam akwan a wɔfa so kyerɛ nsɛnkyerɛnne ahodoɔ ase so na obi nya yere na (also) gye ne nkwa. Eyi, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
