Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐSƐ SƐ ABAAYEWA BIARA Ware OBI A WƆDE NO AMA NO WƆ AWAREƐ MU

NA abaayewa bi wɔ hɔ a wɔfrɛ no Kwaboaso. Ɔbarima biara a wɔde no bɛma no awareɛ no, na ɔka sɛ, "Mempɛ no. " Wɔde no maa ɔbɔfoɔ bi, na ɔno nso kaa sɛ, "Ugh! saa ɔbarima yi ntontom wɔ ne ho, mempɛ no. "

Ɛda bi na ɔkɔɔ afuw no mu sɛ ɔrekɔtwa bɔrɔde. Ɔfaa Sekkan bɔɔ borɔdeɛ no, kɔ! na nnipa nketewa no te bɔrɔde no so; wɔhwee aseɛ, na wɔbae, na wɔkyeree Kwaboaso; wɔkae sɛ, "Wo ne nea wɔde no bɛma obiara a, wosow etire, pus! pusu! " Na mmoatia no kyeree ne nsɔmu kae sɛ: "Bra, momma yɛn piapia no.

Yɛpiapia no, O!

Yyɛ Kwaboaso.

Bra, momma yɛn piapia no.

Yɛpiapia no, O! "

Ɛnneɛ, berɛ a saa ɔbɔfoɔ a wɔde Kwaboaso ama no no tee Kwaboaso nne no, ɔkae sɛ, "Mekɔhwɛ nea ɛyɛ asɛm, efisɛ yɛnnye bɔne mma bɔne. " Ɔkɔɔ no, na Kwaboaso wɔ hɔ, na mmoatia no rehyɛ no. Ɛneɛ na ɔbɔfoɔ no too tuo guu mmoatia no so, na baako gui. Wɔn mu panyin no kaa sɛ, Ɔwɔ nsafufuo a wɔanom, na ɔnom nsa; fa no to hɔ wɔ saa berɛ yi mu, (na afei kɔ so squeeze no). Afei ɔbɔfoɔ no san tuu tuo bio, na baako nso hwee ase. Wɔn mu panyin no kaa sɛ, Saa mmoatia yi wɔ nsafufuo a wɔanom, na wɔadi no so nkonim, fa no kɔto nkyɛn wɔ hɔ. Wɔn ɔbɔfoɔ no kunkum mmoatia no nyinaa gyesɛ wɔn mu panyin no nko ara. Ɔpanyin no frɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no sɛ ɔmmra. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Me nni ba. " Mmoa no mu panyin no kaa sɛ, "O, bra, me ne wo renyɛ hwee. " Mmoa no mu panyin no kɔɔ. Mmoa no mu panyin no kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ me dan a ɛwɔ hɔ no, na wubehu aduro a wɔde tuo (i.e. atuduro) ne otuo ano aba a woapue no nyinaa, fa nea ɛyɛ wo deɛ, na fa Kwaboaso nso fa, nanso ansa na wobɛkɔ no, kɔ twitwa banana, na sɛ worekɔ no, tow gu, na ama berɛ a mmoatia foforɔ no bɛsɔre, na wɔaba abɛkyere wo no, na wɔagyae abɛfa wɔn, baako baako, na woakɔ akyɛ, akyɛ. " Na sɛnea ɛfata no na ɔkɔ twitwa banana, na ɔfa K waboaso nso, na ɔduruu kwan so no, ɔtwee kwadu baako too hɔ. Ɔkɔɔ so yɛɛ saa kwan no nyinaa kɔɔ fie. Na berɛ a aka berɛ tiawa pɛ na wɔbɛduruu fie no, na mmoatia no redi wɔn akyi. Na ɔtwee kwadu baako pɛ a aka no too hɔ, na mmoatia no dii nakyi kɔdanee. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no san de Kwaboaso baa ne mogya-abusua nkyɛn. Ɛnna ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔɔ ne fie. Ɛnneɛ, na ɔbɔfoɔ no te hɔ, berɛ a ɔhunuu sɛ asɛnkafoɔ no adu ne baabi no, na ɔbisaa sɛ, "Ɛdɛn ne amaneɛbɔ no? " Bɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Kwaboaso kyerɛ sɛ ɔsrɛɛ akuraa no Etire sɛ ɔnyɛ ntamgyinafoɔ mma no, na ɔka sɛ afei deɛ, ɔpɛ sɛ ɔware wo. " Ɛnna ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Meda mo ase wɔ nsɛmfua a ɛfiri akuraa no Odikro ano no ho, nanso mentumi nnyae abaayewa no awareɛ, ɛfiri sɛ meda so ara wɔ t icks on my nipadua. " Ɛno nti na Mpanimfoɔ ka sɛ, "Sɛ wɔde wo kɔ ma obi de wo kɔ awareɛ a, wo ne no nware, efisɛ wonnim da bi a wobɛwɔ ahiadeɛ mu a, ɔbɛgye wo. "

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
