SƐNEA EFUO, KƆLƆBO TITIRIW NO DUA BƐYƐƐ FITAA

WƆKA SƐ (Once upon a berɛ) na Esono a ɔyɛ Ɛsono no te hɔ; Ɔkwakuo a ɔyɛ Kwaadu; Anyinhima a ɔyɛ Putty-nosed Kwaadu; ne Efuo a ɔyɛ Black-Colobus Kwaadu; na wɔkae sɛ wɔbɛkɔ akuraa bi akɔpɛ mmaa a wɔbɛware wɔn. Ɛneɛ wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ. Wɔrekɔ no, wɔhyiaa atɛkyɛ tadeɛ bi. Kwaadu a ɔwɔ hwene a ɛyɛ putty no kaa sɛ, "Gyina hɛɛ berɛ a mebisa wo asɛm bi. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wɔ akwantu a yɛrekɔ yi mu no, hena na ɔyɛ fɛ? " Ɛsono no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ Efuo, Black-Colobus Kwaadu no. " Ɔkwakuo (kwaadu foforɔ no) kaa sɛ, "Sɛ saa na ɛteɛ a, momma yɛmmɔ no na yɛnto no atɛkyɛ tadeɛ no mu. " Ɛyɛ nokware sɛ wɔbɔɔ no, na wɔfaa no kɔtoo no atɛkyɛ tadeɛ no mu, na wɔkɔeɛ. on. Ɛnna Efuo no sɔre (firi asuwea no mu), na ɔde anifere dii wɔn akyi, (walking) brɛoo, na ɔkɔtenaa akuraa a ɛwɔ mfinimfini hɔ no so. Na nnipa no ahomegyeɛ no aduru akuraa no, wɔhunuu mmabaawa a wɔahyia wɔ Gyedua dua bi ase. Mmabaawa no hunuu wɔn. Ebi kae sɛ "Me kɔn dɔ yi"; ebi nso kae sɛ "Me kɔn dɔ yi. " Na wɔware mmabaawa no nyinaa. Ɛnneɛ, na ababaawa baako wɔ hɔ a, na ɔkɔɔ afuo, (nanso ansa na ɔbɛfiri hɔ) na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Sɛ mmeranteɛ no ba a, yi baako na fa no sie ma me. " Na ɔsan firi afuo no, ɔbisaa ne maame sɛ, "Me deɛ, ɛhe na ɛwɔ? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Me kɔeɛ no, na wɔakyekyɛ mmeranteɛ no ntam dedaw. " Abaayewa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ hwee. . " Na ɔyii bɔrɔde bi, na ɔrekɔto nsensanee no agu mfinimfini pile no so.

Ɛneɛ, na saa Efuo no aba abɛtenatena mfinimfini-pɔ no so. Na abaayewa no rebɛto nwura no agu so, bere a Efuo no kae sɛ, Ntow ngu me so. Abaayewa no kae sɛ, Hwan ne no? Ɔkae sɛ, Ɛyɛ me. Abaayewa no kae sɛ, Dɛn asɛm na ɛwɔ hɔ? Ɔkae sɛ, Me ne me agorɔkonom ahon no na yɛreba sɛ yɛbɛware (you) mmabaawa no, bere a wɔboroo me, na wɔhyɛɛ me nipadua nyinaa fĩ no, na ɛno nti na mebaa ha bɛtra. Ɔkaa sɛ, Me deɛ, medɔ wo, enti bra na yɛnkɔ fie. Ɔne no kɔeɛ, na ɔfaa nsuo na ɔhohoroo no. Ɛduruu berɛ a ɔfaa sapɔ de guu ne wedeɛ so kakra no, ɔhunuu sɛ ne wedeɛ no yɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ paa. Ɛnna abaayewa no kaa sɛ, Maame, bra bɛhwɛ, ahoɔfɛ a ɛte sɛɛ nyɛ adeɛ a wode bɛdi agorɔ. Na maame no kaa sɛ, Gyae hohoro no. Na ɔwiee hohoro no, na ɔhyɛɛ no ngo, na ɔfaa ne mpaboa, na ɔfaa ne sankuo (bentaa) no, na ɔkɔtenaa Gyedua-dua no ase, na ɔdii agorɔ. Ɛnna mmabaawa a wɔaware dedaw no, ntɛm ara a wɔhunuu no, eyi kaa sɛ, Mempɛ me kunu bio, eyi kaa sɛ, Mempɛ me kunu bio, eyi kaa sɛ, Mempɛ me kunu bio, ɛyɛ Black-Colobus Monkyi a medɔ no no. Black-Colobus Monkyi no kaa sɛ, Ɔno a manya no, medɔ no. Afei na mmoa no nyinaa akyinkyin sɛ wɔrebɛgye wɔn agyapadeɛ ne wɔn san ba. Na berɛ a mmoadoma no gyaee no, mmom sɛ wɔbɛkɔ (on), wɔkɔtenaa kwan no so, na wɔhwɛɛ sɛ Black-Colobus Monk no bɛdi ahyɛdeɛ sɛ ɔbɛbɔ no na wakum no berɛ a waba no. Black-Colobus Monk no nso kyinkyin kɔgyaa no sɛ, Me nso, merekɔgye me nneɛma na san ba. Na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ. Ɛduruu kwan no so no, mmoa no hunuu no, wɔkaa sɛ, Ɛhɔ ne Black-Colobus Monk, ɛhɔ ne Black-Colobus Monk. Ɛneɛ Black-Colobus Monk no bɛgyee ne dua bi, ɔkra! ɔkra! ɔkra!
