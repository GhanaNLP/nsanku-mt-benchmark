Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ YƐBƐHUNU NKRUMA, AGYINAMOA, DAA WƆ VELVƐT SƆRESƆRE SO, BERE A ƆKRAMAN, KRAMAN, DA GYAADE GYA NSO MU

WƆKYERƐ SƐ, bere bi na ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ a, ne awoɔ biara a ɔwo abofra no, na ɛwu. Ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔbɛbisabodan no mu baako asɛm, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, ne kɔn dɔ abofra. Minor god no kae sɛ, "Mɛma wo baako, nanso abofra no deɛ, ne adwuma nyinaa ne sɛ ɔbɛnya wo ɛka, nanso nanso da bi ɔbɛtua wo ka. " Ɛnyɛ nna mmienu, ɛnyɛ nna mmiɛnsa (after consulting the lesser god) na ɔbaa no faa awoɔ. Ɔmaa no abofra a ɔte sɛ akɔre, efisɛ ɛnkyɛ na wanyin. Na ne ba no nyin bɛyɛ aberante fɛfɛ. Ɛda bi na ɔne ne maame wɔ hɔ, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Maame, ma me sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma, na menkɔ Ano-of-the-po ɔman no mu, nkɔtɔ nkyene. " Ɔbea no kaa sɛ, "Sika dodoɔ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Asuanu" (i. e. £4). Na maame no de kɔmaa no, na ɔhyɛɛ akwantu no ase. Seesei, bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔkɔhyiaa obarima bi ne ne ɔkraman a ne ho asonsono. Ɔkae sɛ, "Momfa mmra na mentumi ntɔ. " Kraman no wura kae sɛ, "Wontumi ntɔ. " Asɛm a abofra no kae ne sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ? " Kraman no wura kae sɛ, "An Asuanu weight of sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma" (i. e. £4). Ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ saa, fa saa £4 yi boɔ a ɛyɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma. " Ɔgyee kraman no, na ɔsan de baa fie. Berɛ a ɔsan baeɛ no, ne maame bisaa no sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woannue wo beae a obi rekɔ? " Ɔbuaeɛ sɛ, "Me de sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no tɔɔ kraman. " Ne maame kaa sɛ, "Ho! " Seesei na wɔte hɔ a, ɛbɛyɛ sɛ ɔsrane baako. Ɔkae sɛ, "Maame, ma me sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma na menkɔdwadie. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wo deɛ, sɛnea ɛyɛ wo wont, wobɛfa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no nkutoo na woatow ato hɔ bio, nanso wopɛ sika dodoɔ? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "An Asuanu ne sur worth of sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma" (i. e. £5). Ɔkae sɛ, "Fa no, ɛnneɛ. " Enti ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ dwadie kwan no so. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔkɔhyiaa obarima bi a ɔso agyinamoa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Man, bring that aboa that always falls on its anan, that I may buy it. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "When I lie in my dan, the nkura bites my anan, for that sianteɛ I bought it. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "I beg you let me have it. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "You can't buy it. " Ɛneɛ abofra no kaa sɛ, "How much will you take for it? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Ananu ne Asu sure sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "So that's why you say, I can't buy it; ha, fa no. " Aberewa no gyee agyinamoa no, na ɔde kɔɔ fie. Bere a ɔduruu fie no, ɔkae sɛ, "Maame, hwɛ ha nea mede aba yi. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Ah, ɛno ara ne nea wɔkae (bɛsi). " Aberewa no kɔɔ so tenaa hɔ (at home). Ɛbɛyɛ nnanan akyi na ɔkasa kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Momma me sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma na menkɔ dwadie. " Ne maame kae sɛ, "Me sika a mewɔ nyinaa asa, gye ade a ɛda nkyɛn a ɛyɛ anasa duru sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. e. £6); me de yi ma wo, na woakɔ, na womfa nni nneɛma tɔ a, ɛno na ɛyɛ eguadwa yi awieeɛ. " Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Me wate. " Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔfaa ne kotokuo no, na ɔfirii hɔ kɔeɛ, pa! Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔkɔtoo asanteni bi, na na ɔso aborɔnoma. Ɔkae sɛ, "Adamfo, fa wo abɔdeɛ no mmra na mentɔ. " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Mentɔn no, efisɛ mede me ho gye mu. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Mɛtɔ. " Anomaa no wura kae sɛ, "Merentɔn no, efisɛ minim nea ebetumi ayɛ me. " Aberewa no kae sɛ, "Oh, fa ma me. " Ɔkae sɛ, "So wubetumi atɔ? " Aberewa no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ? " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Anasa Asu sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i.e. £6). " Ɔkae sɛ, "So you suppose because of that I would not buy it, here is the nkaebɔ dodoɔ. " Ɔde anomaa berebuw no bae. Ne maame kae sɛ, "Eyi nso anyɛ yiye sen kan no, enti eyi na wode aba? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Ne nyinaa akyi no, eyi na mede aba. " Seesei (da bi) na abofra no te hɔ (at fie), bere a Aborɔnoma frɛɛ no sɛ, "Bra. " Bere a ɔkɔɔ so no, Aborɔnoma bɔɔ no amanneɛ sɛ, "Me ara me akuraa so na meyɛ ɔhene, na anka mɛkɔ akwantu, bere a ɔyɔnko bi a ɔwɔ c ame na ɛgyee me nsɔmu, bere a wo nso wo ayamye mu tɔɔ me, na sesei me srɛ wo sɛ, sɛ wobɛfa me akɔ me kuro mu pɛ a, anka wɔbɛda wo ase paa. " Abɔfra no kaa sɛ, "Wose me atorosɛm, wobɛdwane. " Aborɔnoma no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ woantumi anhu wo kwan a wobɛyɛ nea me bisa no a, fa hama a etwa to no, na kyekyere me nan, na fa me kɔ. " Abɔfra no faa hama a etwa to no, na ɛkyekyeree no. ɛde no baa ne nan so, na odii nakyi brɛoo, kɔsii sɛ wɔduruu Pigeon kuro no mu. Bere a wɔduruu kuro no skɛɛte ahorow no mu no, na mmofra no redi nte. Bere a wɔhuu no no, wɔkae sɛ, "Hwɛ, Ɔhene no ni! Ɔhene no ni! " Wɔn mu baako tuu mmirika kɔka kyerɛɛ Korenti hene no, nanso wɔkyeree no, na wɔtwaa ne menem sɛ, "Worema yɛakae yɛn awerɛhoɔ a ɛba akyire. " Nanso ɔfoforɔ bi nso de amanneɛbɔ korɔ no ara san kɔe. Na afei Akwamuhene no kaa sɛ, "Wo, Gyaasehene, wo ara kɔ na hwɛ nea eyi nyinaa fa ho. " Ɔkɔɔ kɔhwɛɛ, na ɔsan bae. Ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, ɛyɛ nokware. " Ɛnna wɔgyee mpa a wɔasɛn ne ahennneɛma no na wɔkɔɔ de Ɔhene no baa ne fie. Wɔka kyerɛɛ abusuakuo no nyinaa amaneɛbɔ no, sɛnea na ɔrehyɛ akwantu, ne sɛnea onipa bi kyeree no, ne sɛnea na abofra yi, nayamye nti, atɔ no, ne sɛnea ɛde bɛsi da yi na ɔde no aba fie. Mpanimfoɔ ne mmabun nyinaa sɔre daa abofra no ase. Ɔhemmaa no de nsuo kukuo a sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma ahyɛ mu ma baeɛ; mpanimfoɔ no nyinaa, nsuo kukuo a sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma ahyɛ mu ma, wɔn mu biara. Ɔhene no ankasa hwɛɛ ne nsa, na ɔhwee kawa bi so, na ɔde maa no. Na ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa kawa yi, na nea wopɛ nyinaa, kawa yi bɛma wo. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Metee. " Na ɔde kawa no kɔɔ nakuraa; na ɔde sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma ne kawa no kyerɛɛ ne maame. Ɛnna maame no kaa sɛ, "Akwaaba Aku, akwaaba Aku. " Ansa no, na ɔtaa "burn" ne sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma, na sɛ ɔsan fi akwantu ahodoɔ mu ba a, na ɔtaa kyia ne maame no a, na ɔmmua no. Na ɔde amaneɛbɔ no maa ne maame no sɛ, "Woahunu sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma ne kawa yi; mɛkɔ akyekye akuraa kɛseɛ ma yɛn atena mu. " Ɔmaame no kaa sɛ, "Press your aniwa hard (i.e. bɔ mmɔden yɛ saa). " Na abofra no puei kɔgyinaa nwura no mu; ɔhwee kawa no so de sii fam; ɔkae sɛ, "Kawa, yi nyinaa (land) kwaeɛ ne nwura mma me. " Na saa baabi no nyinaa bɛyɛɛ nea ɛho tew. Ɔkae sɛ, "Boa nea woayiyi nyinaa eboɔ mu ma yɛnhyew. " Na ɛyɛɛ saa. Ɔkae sɛ, "Si afie. " Na ɛyɛɛ afie bebree. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Kawa, ma nnipa mmra mmɛtena afie yi mu. " Ɛnna nnipa baeɛ. Ɔyɛɛ ne maame Ɔhemmaa, ɔno nso bɛyɛɛ ɔpanyin. Afei (i.e. na asɛm bi wɔ hɔ a me werɛ firi sɛ mɛka), Kwaku Ananse, Ananse no, na ɔyɛ wɔn adamfo paa. Ɛda bi na ɔte hɔ (ne akuraa foforɔ mu) na Kwaku Ananse sii kwan sɛ ɔbɛba aberanteɛ yi akuraa ase. (Bere a ɔduruu hɔ no,) ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, little mothers abofra, little fathers abofra, woanya ne ti yiye na woadi nkonim, na worempɛ sɛ wohwɛ me bio, nanso dɛn na asi a ɛde eyi nyinaa aba? " Ɛneɛ na abofra no kaa amaneɛbɔ no nyinaa kyerɛɛ no. Ananse no kaa sɛ, Mekɔ me akuraa na masane aba. Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ ne akuraa, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nua ba baa sɛ, Merebɛsoma wo akɔ me adamfo baabi a ɛwɔ hɔ no, na sɛ wokɔ a, wobɛfa saa bobesa fitaa yi ama no na woabua adwene na woayɛ nea ɔbɛhyɛ wo biara; na ɛsɛ sɛ wobɔ mmɔden wɔ kokoam sɛ wode wo nsa bɛka saa kawa no. Ananse ababaawa no de nadwene sii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ɔbarima no akuraa. Ɛnna ɔbarima no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Wei deɛ, mehwɛ na woankɔ bio, na ɛsɛ sɛ wone me tena hɔ nnansa ansa na woasan aba. " Ɔbaawa no kaa sɛ, "Me wate. " Afei ɔne ababaawa no nyinaa wɔ hɔ, na ɔbarima no kɔdwaree, ɔyii ne kawa no sii ɛpono so, na ababaawa no faa no, na ɔde kɔɔ ne wofa, Kwaku Ananse nkyɛn. Ɛduruu berɛ a Kwaku Ananse de ne nsa sii so no, ɔyɛɛ kawa no ho dwumadie sɛ ɔbɛsi kuropɔn. Ɛnneɛ, ɔbarima no, berɛ a ɔbaa sɛ ɔbɛhwehwɛ ne kawa no, wannya bi. Ɔbaa sɛ ɔbɛte sɛ Ananse asi kuropɔn a ɛkyɛn ne deɛ no. Ɛneɛ ɔkɔɔ kɔbisaa nsamanfoɔ a wɔwɔ hɔ no mu baako. Minor-god no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ananse nuabaa a ɔbaa hɔ kyerɛɛ wo no, wagye akɔma ne wofa. Ananse no nso kɔpɛɛ afotuo (the lesser-god and it was revealed to him) sɛ Nkruma, Agyinamoa, ne Ɔkraman, Kraman, na wɔbɛsoma wɔn (to recover the kawa), ɛno nti na ɔkɔgyee aduro a wɔde bɛsa nam, de bɛsi kwan so, na ama sɛ mmoa no rekɔ a, sɛ wɔfa bi adi a, wɔrentumi nkɔ baabi bio koraa. Ɔbarima no (a na ɔhwere ne kawa) baa fie, na ɔka kyerɛɛ Agyinamoa ne Kraman no sɛ, "Me berɛ adu sɛ mɛka sianteɛ a matɔ maa mo no, (na ɛne yi) me deɛ bi ayera, na wɔka sɛ ɛwɔ Ananse agyapadeɛ mu, nanso ɛwɔ adaka a ɛwɔ ahomegyeɛ no nyinaa mfinimfini. Na bio, wɔka sɛ ɔde aduro bi afra odwan no honam mu de asisi kwan so, enti bere a woba hɔ a, nnye nnye nni na huruw fa so. " Kraman no kaa sɛ, "Agyinamoa, woate, wo na wobɛwe no. " Agyinamoa no kaa sɛ, "Firi adi, wo a wote kakra kakra kakra na wode ɛhwene kɔ hwamhwam ne amhwam. " Ɛnna wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kasae pii, na wɔn wura no kaa sɛ, "Ɛno yie, nyɛ yie. Wɔn mmoa mmienu no fii hɔ, na wɔrekɔ so. Afei na aka berɛ tiawa pɛ na wɔbɛduruu baabi a nam no wɔ, berɛ a Kraman no "tee" nam no hua. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agyinamoa, me yafunu mu yaw wɔ me, na merentumi nkɔ so. " Agyinamoa no kaa sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔ so, nsa so eguadwa no ho hia. " (Kraman) kaa sɛ, "Agyinamoa, mentumi. " Agyinamoa no kɔɔ so. Kraman no kɔɔ baabi a nam no wɔ, ɔwe nam no nyinaa; ɛhɔ na ɔdaeɛ, wantumi ankɔ so bio. Na Agyinamoa no kɔduruu Ananse akuraa ase, na ɔbɛdaa Ananse da dan no mu, wɔ silin (dan no atifi) hɔ. Bere a ɔdaeɛ no, ɔhunuu sɛ ɔpɔnkɔ bi retwam. Kyeeka! sɛdeɛ ɔbaa ne etire so no. (The akura) said, "Don't catch me, what is the asɛm? " Agyinamoa answered, "My Master's ring has been lost, and they say it lies in Ananse's box, which stands in the mfinimfini (of all his nnaka ahomegyeɛ), and if you can go and bring it to me, then I shall let you go. " Akura said, "I can. " Agyinamoa said, "Suppose I let you go, and you go and don't bring it, but run away? " Akura no kae sɛ, "Sɛ wopɛ a, kyekyere hama a etwa to no wɔ me asene ho. " Agyinamoa no faa hama a etwa to no kyekyeree ne asene ho. Afei Akura no kɔɔ kɔkɔɔ no dan mu, na ɔkwee ɛtokuro bi a ɛwɔ adaka no mu a ɛgyina ahomegyeɛ no nyinaa mfinimfini. Nkakrankakra ɔyɛɛ mmueano, na ɛbɛyɛɛ kɛseɛ. Ɔfaa mu, sɛ ɔrekɔfa kawa a ɔde bɛkɔ Agyinamoa. Ɛnkyɛ na Agyinamoa no de ne nsa guu so, na ɔdwanee kɔtoo Kraman, na ɔhwee nea ɔgyaee no hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, Wo da so da ha, na ɛhe na saa nam no wɔ? Kraman no kae sɛ, Oh, manhu (nea ɛbɛyɛɛ no), ebia na nnipa a wɔyɛ ne dea no bae na wɔyii no; nanso ɛhe na kawa no wɔ? Agyinamoa no kae sɛ, Ɛha na ɛwɔ. Kraman no kae sɛ, Wɔka sɛ asubon a ɛda kwan so no wɔ nsuyiri mu, na sɛnea wo, sɛ wotwa nsuo a, nantew ase no, ɛbɛyɛ sɛ ɛbɛhwe ase nti, ma me, efisɛ me deɛ, wunim sɛ mefa nsuo no ani so. " Agyinamoa no kae sɛ, "Ɛno ni, fa. " Wɔduruu asubonten no, Kraman tui kɔɔ mu. Saa ara na Agyinamoa no nso tui kɔɔ mu. Ɛhɔ ara na Agyinamoa no twaa mu. Kraman no nso kɔduruu asuo no mfinimfini no, ne ho yeraa no, na ɔrebɛfa ahomegye kɛse a emu dɔ no, ne kawa no firi ne ano kɔhwee nsuo no mu. Ɔtwaa mu baa baabi a Agyinamoa wɔ. Agyinamoa no kae sɛ, "Ɛhe na saa kawa no wɔ? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛhwee ase (out of my ano) kɔɔ nsuo no mu. " Agyinamoa no tuu mmirika kɔɔ nsuo no mu, ɔhunuu apataa kɛseɛ bi a ɛretwam. Na Agyinamoa no kyeree no. Apataa no kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dɛn? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me kawa no atɔ asubonten yi mu nnansa yi ara, enti gye sɛ wopɛ ɔhaw a, fa nea ɛyɛ me deɛ no ma me ntɛm ara. Sɛ womfa mma me a, me bekum wo mprempren ara. " Apataa no kaa sɛ, "Ma yɛnkɔ asubonten ano na memfa nea ɛyɛ wo mma wo. " Bere a wɔn baanu nyinaa duu sikakorabea hɔ no, Apataa no yii ne yam, na kawa no firii adi. Agyinamoa no faa no baa bɛkyerɛɛ Kraman no. Kraman no kaa sɛ, "Agya, me srɛ wo sɛ, sɛ wokɔ a, nka asɛm a asi no ho asɛm. " Agyinamoa no kuraa ne ano to mu. Wɔduruu fie no, na Agyinamoa no ka nea asi no nyinaa kyerɛɛ ne wura no sɛ, "Kraman no nam a ɔweɛ bere a yɛrekɔ no nti, ɔbɛyɛɛ nea ontumi nkɔ akyiri. Na bio, na me kɔfa kawa no san baeɛ no, na Kraman no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, bere a ɔtwaa asubonten bi a ɔtwaa nani so no, ɛsɛ sɛ mede kawa no ma no. Memaa no, na ɔtwee no kɔtoo asubonten no mu. Ɛyɛ kakra akyi nko na mede me nsa too so bio. " Na nnipa a wɔwɔ hɔ no nyinaa reka sɛ, "Ayeyi nka Agyinamoa. " Na wɔteɛm sɛ, "E! " Ɛnna ɔhene no kaa sɛ, "Wo, Agyinamoa, aduane ayamye biara a meredi no, mɛhwɛ sɛ mebubu bi ato wo asanka ketewa no mu. Me kɛtɛ biara a meda so no, (mebɛda so) sɛ woda so bi. Na wo nso, Dog, anadwo nwunu bɛba a, wobɛda ogya a adum nnyansramma a ɛredɛ nko ara so. Sɛ wode abaa bɔ nko ara a, folk bɛbɔ wo akuturuku. " Ɛno nti na nna a wode bɛhu Agyinamoa no, baabiara nna gye sɛ kɛtɛ a ɛye pa ara so. Afei nso, sɛ woto aduane bi gu fam ma no a, ɔrenni (gyae mprɛte so). Nanso Kraman no de, bere nyinaa yɛbɛhu no sɛ ɔda abɔnten wɔ awia nsõ so. Afei nso wobɛhu no sɛ wɔrebu no wɔ hɔ, ɔbɛteɛm sɛ, "Kao! " Ɛyɛ ne nyinaa wɔ berɛ a wɔde no kɔeɛ no akawnto so (kawa no eguadwa yi so).

Ɛyi, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (ana) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi afa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a wode bɛkamfo me (asɛmka no).
