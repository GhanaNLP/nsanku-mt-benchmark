Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA WƆDE "KURONTO" NE "BURU" DII DWUMA (THE SALUTATIONS)

NA ƐBAA BI SƐ, NA ɔbɔfoɔ bi (a wɔfrɛ no) Kwaku ne ne yere (a wɔfrɛ no) Buruwa, na wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ wɔbɛyɛ atenae foforɔ. Kwaku a ɔyɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no, berɛ a ɔkɔɔ nwura so no, ɔkunkum eku bi, nanso berɛ a ɔsan de no baa fie ne asase so no, na ɔka sɛ, "Eyi nyɛ aduane. " Enti ɔkɔtɔn no. Daa a ɔkunkum adeɛ no, na ɛte sɛɛ. Ne yere no deɛ, na onhuu sika no da, na ɔbisaa no sɛ, "Worensi sika bio, na worensi nam nso bio, na 'ɛno nyinaa aseɛ ne dɛn? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Me kɔ a, na nnipa mpɛ tɔ (nam) no, na sɛ ɛporɔw a, mema ɛyera. " Ɛda bi na ɔkunkum adeɛ bi, na ɔde sii kwan so, na ne yere dii nakyi kɔhwɛɛ no. Ɛnna ɔhunuu sɛ ɔtɔn saa kwaadu no, na ɔfaa sika no, na ɔtɔɔ nsafufuo kuku (a wɔfrɛ no dɔnto) nom. Afei ne yere danee ne ho kɔɔ fie. Ɛnkyɛree na ɔbɔfoɔ no, Kwaku nso baeɛ. Ɛduruu fie hɔ ara pɛ na ɔkaa sɛ, "Anwummere pa, Buruwa. " Ɔbuaeɛ sɛ, "Ya Ekudonto (Yes, Monkey for nsafufuo kuku). " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wobua me saa kwan yi? " Ɔbuaeɛ sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuaeɛ a ɛfata ma nea ɛwɔ wo etire mu. " ɔbɔfoɔ no anka hwee. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔkɔɔ afuo hɔ na ɔsan baeɛ; ɔkae sɛ, "Anwummere pa. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Ya buru. " Buruwa kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wobua (me nkyea) saa? " ɔbɔfoɔ no nso kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuae a ɛfata ma nea ɛwɔ wo etire mu. " Saa na ɛbɛyɛɛ amammrɛ wɔ abusuakuo no mu sɛ wɔde nkyea ahorow "Ya kuronto" ne "Ya buru" bɛdi dwuma. Wɔde wɔn mfitiaseɛ no ka ɔbɔfoɔ, Kwaku, ne ne yere, Buruwa. Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
