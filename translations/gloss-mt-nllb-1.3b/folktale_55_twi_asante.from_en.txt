Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA SPEAR-GRASS BAA ABUSUAKUO MU

Ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bi bae na Ntikuma hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔrekɔ aduane hwehwɛ de kɔma ne agya, Agya Ananse, Ananse no. Ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ akuraase bi; (hɔ) na ɔkɔhyiaa Ɔbaa Kɛkɛseɛ. Aberewa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Me banana, adɛn nti na, bere a obiara ntumi mma ha no, woaba ha? Ntikuma buae sɛ, Ɛyɛ ɛkɔm nti, na mebaa hwehwɛ biribi a mebɛkɔ adi. Ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, Kɔ me afuw hɔ, nanso bayere a ɛbɛka sɛ Pluck me, pluck me, no nntu, na mmom bayere a ɛbɛka sɛ Dont pull me, dont pull me, pick it and bring. it. Ntikuma firee. Bayerɛ no bi kae sɛ, Pluck me, pluck me; ebinom nso kae sɛ, Dont pluck me, dont pluck me. Ɛnneɛ Ntikuma tui wɔn a wɔkae sɛ, Dont luck me, dont luck me. Ɔde wɔn baa fie; ɔyii wɔn. Ɛnna ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, Toto ankasa fa a wɔde yɛ aduane no gu, na fa nwoma ahoroɔ no hyɛ kuku no mu na fa si ogya no so. Ntikuma yii wɔn ho na ɔfaa nwoma ahoroɔ no de sii ogya no so. Bere a wɔsiesiee wɔn ho no, ɔyii wɔn fii ogya no so, danee kuku no emu nsɛm, na ne nyinaa (inside) adane aduane pa. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Mewiei, nanso momma me nam mfa nhyɛ mu. Ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, Kɔ ka kyerɛ Nkwakuo, Asono, a wɔwɔ dua no so, sɛ, Old woman-grandma says, as for all of you, your ɛnanom! Asono kaa sɛ, If it were not for Old woman-grandma, we should have beaten you. Ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, Asono, as for me, it is not my eguadwa, and you m ay ba bɛbɔ no. Na mmoa no resiane ahyɛdeɛ sɛ wɔbɛbɔ no. Ɔbaa-nananom kaa sɛ, Me Sharp-knife no, twa mmoa yi atitirefoɔ mma me. Ɛnna The One-sharp no twaa mmoa no atitirefoɔ no. Ntikuma boaboaa mmoa atitirefoɔ no nyinaa ano, yii bayerɛ bi wɔ nkaaho, na ɔde wɔn nyinaa kɔɔ nakuraa. Agya Ananse hunuu (ne ɔbabarima) Ntikuma sɛ ɔgyina nanim, na ɔkaa sɛ, Nkyea rebɛba wo, ant-hill breaker (i.e. abereɛ). Ɔkɔɔ so kaa sɛ, Ma memfa wo so. Ntikuma kɔɔ hɔ kɔtoo ne ho. Afei Kwaku Ananse kɔyii bayere baako, ɔkae sɛ, Bere a me abosonsoɔ bɛba no, me bedidi yi. (His yere) "Aso" kae sɛ, Efi bere a Ɔbɔadeɛ yɛɛ nneɛma, woahunu ɔbarima a ɔwɔ abosonsoɔ? Kwaku Ananse ne ne yere, ne ne mmofra, wiee bayerɛ no di, na baako pɛ na ɛka sii a ɛyɛ Agya Ananse deɛ no. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Me nsa aka me abosonsoɔ. Na ɔkɔtenaa (akwankyɛn tena) fie akyi, na ɔnoaa bayere no, na ɔdii, kɔsii sɛ ɛdii (ne nyinaa) wieeɛ. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Me bɛfiri bio. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Me ne wo bɛkɔ. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Me ne wo renkɔ bɔ mu. Berɛ a Ntikuma kɔdaeɛ no, na Kwaku Ananse akɔfa hunters skin bag bi, de amena nketewa ahyɛ mu ma, na ɔde nso ahyɛ mu ma, na ɔde akyekyere Ntikuma ntoma no ano. Berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, na ɛyɛ kɔkɔɔ, kɔkɔɔ, kɔkɔɔ, Ntikuma de ne ho too kwan so. Bere a (the sum hiding) anim (of nneɛma) atetew nkyɛn no, Ananse, hwehwɛɛ Ntikuma sɛnea ɔbɛpɛ, annya no, ɛnneɛ ɔkɔhwehwɛɛ nso no. Bere a ɔhunuu wɔn no ɔdii aboa anammɔn no akyi, kɔɔ so kɔɔ so. Na ɔrekɔ, berɛ a, na Ntikuma ne Ɔbaa-nena te hɔ. Ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, Agya, wo eguadwa ne dɛn? Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, Ɛkɔm aka me, enti ma me biribi nni. Ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, Ɛhe na mebɛnya something afiri? Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, So wosusuw sɛ meyɛ kwasea, mpo me abofra Ntikuma bae a, wode bi maa no. Ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, Kɔ afuw a ɛwɔ hɔ no mu; Yams a ɛka sɛ Twi me, twi me no. , dont pull them up, but the ebinom that say, Dont pull me up, dont pull me up, pull up those ebinom. Ananse (replied), Ah! ɛdeɛn na nkwaseasɛm ni a wose me sɛ, bere a obi ka sɛ ɔdɔ obi, wose me sɛ menkyiri no, me ntee sɛɛ da. Nanso Ananse de ne ho kɔɔ afuw mu. Ɛduruu hɔ no, bayerɛ no bi kae sɛ, Twi me so, tiri me so, ebinom nso kae sɛ, Dont pull me up, dont pull me up. Na ɔtwee ebinom a na ɛka sɛ, Twi me so, tiri me so, no kɔɔ soro na ɔne wɔn baa fie. Ɔbaa na ɔwiee wɔn skinning no, ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, Kɔ na tow aduane pa no fa no gu, na fa nwoma ahoroɔ no, fa sie kuku no mu, na fa si ogya no so. Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, Ah, ɛyɛ nkasa a ɛporɔw bɛn na woreka yi kyerɛ me; Ɔbɔadeɛ no yɛɛ nneɛma nti, yɛtow aduane pa no gu, na yɛkora nsensanee no so? Ananse no de aduane no fa papa no, na ɔde si ogya no so, nanso ɔno deɛ, faa wedeɛ no kakra baako de sii soro. Bere a aduane no asa no, ɔyii no, na ne nyinaa adan aboɔ, nanso wedeɛ sin baako a ɔde sii soro no adan aduane pa. Agya Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ehe! kwasampani awosankɔ yi anka atoro. " Na Ananse no kɔpuee nwoma ahoroɔ no de baa ogya no so. Bere a wɔnoaa aduane no, oyii no fii ogya no mu, na ɛdane bɛyɛɛ bayerɛ a wɔanoa sɛnea wopɛ sɛ wuhu no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Awoɔ nana, eyi nyinaa ye yiye paa, nanso afei ɛsɛ sɛ wode nam ma me de ka ho. " Ananse kaa sɛ, "Kɔ hwɛ dua no, ɔbaa no ho nsonsonoe wɔ so; sɛ wokɔ a, wobɛka sɛ, 'Woyɛ mmoadoma!' " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Saa ɔbaa yi! Me ne Asono no nni nea ɛde ba manso, na dɛn nti na ɛsɛ sɛ me kɔyɛ wɔn ayayadeɛ saa ɔkwan yi so? " Ɔbaa dada no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ, na sɛ ɔhaw ba a, ɛno ne me abusuabɔ. " Ananse no kɔɔ so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo Asono, wo a wote nnua yi so no, aberewa-nananom ka sɛ woyɛ mmoadoma. " Wɔn mmoa no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ aberewa-nananom na anka ɛsɛ sɛ yɛbɔ wo. " Aberewa-nananom kaa sɛ, "Ɛnne bra na bɔ no. "

Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Aberewa, ɛno nyɛ nea ɛfata. " Ɛnna mmoa no too ne so bɛtoo Kwaku Ananse. Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Me Sekkan The-sharp-one no, twa wɔn atitirefoɔ no. " Tenten! (na ɛyɛ nnyigyei a Sekkan no yɛe bere a ɛrehyɛ ne adwuma ase no). Ɔbaawa-nananom kaa sɛ, "Softly and easy now. " Nanso Ananse no antie (her say that). Ɛnneɛ, Ananse no faa nam no san baa fie, na Aberewa no kɔɔ nkyɛn kakra. Ɛneɛ na Kwaku Ananse awia ne sekkan no hyɛɛ ne kotokuo mu, na ɔdwanee san baa ne akuraa. Berɛ a ɔsan baa nakuraa hɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ (ne ɔbabarima) sɛ, "Ntikuma, saa Sekkan a mede me nsa aka yi nti, ma yɛnkɔ nwura no so. " Wɔtuu kwan no, na wɔkɔhunuu Wuram mprako bi. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ntikuma, kɔ na wo wɔn ayayadeɛ. Ntikuma kɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Bush-pigs, you are filthy. " Bush-pigs no buaeɛ sɛ, "If it were not that you are Kwaku Ananse, the son of the Spider, we should have come and beaten you. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Mommra mmɛbɔ no akwan nyinaa so, ɔyɛ bɔne koraa, nanimtiaa no nni aseresɛm. " Bush-pigs no rebɛbɔ no, bere a Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Old womans knife, The-sharp-one, cut their atitirefoɔ for me. " O h! na Sekkan no bɛtoo wɔn so, na ɛwiee mmoa no ti nyinaa twa. Afei ɛbɛhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɛbɛtwa nnua no. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ei! ɛdeɛn na woreyɛ yi? Mekaa sɛ twitwa mmoa no ti, na wowiee saa yɛ, na afei dɛn na eyi nyinaa yɛ? " Afei Ntikuma hwɛɛ kwan kakra, afei ɔkae sɛ, "Softly and easy now. " Kwaku Ananse antie no, na Sekkan no baa fam hɔ, na Kwaku Ananse kɔgyee no. Wɔsan baa fie. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, na Kwaku Ananse aka sɛ wɔbɛkɔ ɔsoro-nyame nkyɛn wɔ hɔ. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Agya, nkɔ sɛ ɛyɛ Sekkan yi akawnto so. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Firi hɔ. " Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Fa wo kwan so. " Kwaku Ananse de ne ho sii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ɔsoro-nyame nkyɛn wɔ hɔ, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Agya, to-da, sɛ obi aka wo asɛm bi a, me nko ara, me, Kwaku Ananse, mɛtwa wɔn atitirefoɔ nyinaa wɔ da koro pɛ mu de ama wo. " Ɔsoro-nyame kaa sɛ, "Aseda afɔreɛ wɔ hɔ ma obiara a ɔbɛba bɛtumi ayɛ saa, na atamfo asraafodɔm kɛseɛ bi na ɛregyina kuro no kuro akyi, enti kɔ wanim. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Ma wɔmfa nsa ne odwan baako mmra. Ntɛm ara na wɔde baeɛ. Kwaku Ananse firii adi kɔɔ kwan so; Kwaku Ananse bisaa atamfo asraafo no sɛ, Ɛyɛ mo na moka sɛ mo ne Ewiem-onyankopɔn bɛko? Wɔbuaa sɛ, Aane, ɛno na yɛka. Afei Ewiem-onyankopɔn asraafoɔ no gyinaa Kwaku Ananse akyi. Ɛnna Kwaku Ananse bɛkasa kyerɛɛ Ɔbaa Panyin no Sekan, The-Sharp One, sɛ, Twitwa yɛn atamfo asraafo yi atitirefoɔ ma me. Na Sekkan no twa wɔn atitirefoɔ no, tan! tan! na wɔwiee. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Ɛno yɛ papa, gyae. Nanso Sekkan no bɛtoo Ewiem-onyankopɔn asradom so na ɛretwitwa (wɔn atitirefoɔ). Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Oh, ɛno ne Ewiem-onyankopɔn asradom, enti gyae. Nanso ɛtwaa mu. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Sɛ wo nufuɔ aka wo a, ma ɛnnyɛ nwini. Oh! Na ɔsoro-nyame no dɔm nyinaa apopa. Mprempren Sekkan no tuu kwan wɔ mframa mu; ɛbaa nea Kwaku Ananse wɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Hwɛ yie, wunnim me anaa? Wunnim me anaa? Ɛyɛ me Kwaku Ananse, mede wo firi Old-woman no nkyɛn baa hɔ. Oh! mprempren Kwaku Ananse ti bɔɔ fam koraa, tan! Na ɛkɔɔ so ara na Sekkan no kɔɔ so; na baabi a ɛsii no danee ɛserɛ bi, a yɛfrɛ no Etene (spear-grass); nano yɛ nnam papa papa.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
