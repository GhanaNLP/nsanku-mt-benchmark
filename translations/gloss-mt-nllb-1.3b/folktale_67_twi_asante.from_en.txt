Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

KWAKU ANANSE, ANANSE NO, KAA SƐ ƆREKƆ KWASEA BI HWEHWƐ MU, BERE A NANKASA NYINAA YƐ KWASEA

KWAKU ANANSE a ɔyɛ Ananse no na na ɔte hɔ pɛn, na ɔkaa sɛ ɔpɛ kwasea bi a ɔbɛka ne ho akɔto mpataayi. Ɔkyinkyin hɔ nyinaa, nanso wannya bi. Ɛneɛ na ɔkɔhyiaa Osansa a ɔyɛ Akrɔma no. Ɔkae sɛ, Yɛnkɔ na yɛmfa mpataa-ntokuru nsi hɔ. Akrɔma no buae sɛ, Mewɔ nam a mɛdi. Akyire no ohyiaa Anene, Kwaakwaadabi no, na ɔkae sɛ, Yɛnkɔ na yɛmfa mpataa-ntokuru nsi hɔ. Kwaakwaadabi no kae sɛ, Me na metee. Afei, Akrɔma no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaakwaadabi no sɛ, Wei mpataa-ntokuru a wo ne Kwaku Ananse rekɔ no, nkɔ, efisɛ Kwaku Ananse kyerɛ sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ kwasea, sɛ bere a ɔne kwasea no resi mpataa-ntokuru no, s, ɔbɛnya sika no nyinaa. Kwaakwaadabi nso buae sɛ, Wo nhia sɛ woka foforo, efisɛ mebɛhwɛ na manya sika no nyinaa. Kwaakwaadabi ne Ananse no sii kwan sɛ nwura no bɛtwa mmɛbaha. Kwaakwaadabi no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, Ma me sekkan no na menkɔ twitwa, na wadwuma bɛyɛ sɛ wobɛfa brɛ no. Ananse no buae sɛ, Wofa me sɛ kwasea, ma me nyɛ atwaeɛ no, na wo bɛfa ne brɛ no. Kwaku Ananse na ɔyɛ atwaeɛ (pALMS) no, berɛ a Anene a ɔyɛ Kwaakwaadabi no te hɔ a ɔresu. Bere a ɔtwaa (them) wiee no, wɔkyekyeree wɔn (in a babaso). Kwaakwaadabi kae sɛ, Ananse, ma mennfa wɔn so, na wo bɛfa ne brɛ. Ananse nso buae sɛ, Fi adi, wofa me sɛ meyɛ kwasea? Na ɔfaa wɔn de wɔn too ne etire so. Kwaakwaadabi dii nakyi na na ɔresu. Bere a wɔduruu fie no, Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, Ma menyɛ mpataa-pɛw no na woagye me nnwuma no brɛ. Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, Dabi; ma menyɛ no na woagye brɛ no. Ɛnna Kwaakwaadabi no kɔtenaa nakyi, na na ɔresu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Ɔkwasea, wose wowɔ adwene, na woreku. Ananse no wiee mpataa-pɛw no. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Ma mennsoa mpataa no na memfa nkɔ nsuo no mu, na wo na wofa ne brɛ. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Dabi, me ne wɔn bɛkɔ, na wo na wofa ne brɛ. " Wɔduruu nsuo no mu no, Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Agya Ananse, nsuo no mu na aboa kɛseɛ bi te, enti ma mennyina nsuo no mu nsi mpataa no kwan, na sɛ aboa kɛseɛ no ka me a, wofa ne owuo. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Ah, wofa me sɛ ɔkwasea ankasa; me ara mɛsi afidie no, na sɛ ɔkeka me a, wo bɛfa ne owuo. " Kwaku Ananse faa apataa-afidie no sii nsuo no mu, na wɔsan baa fie. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, wɔkɔhwɛɛ afidie no, na wɔakyere apataa mmienu. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Ananse, wofa mmienu yi, na sɛ kɔsi ɔkyena na yɛaba, na (afidie no aka nnan a, me ne dane bɛgye wɔn. " Ananse no buaa sɛ, "Saa yɔnkoɔ yi! Wofa me sɛ ɔkwasea ankasa, wofa mmienu yi, na kɔsi ɔkyena no, me ne nnan no bɛgye. " Kwaakwaadabi no de wɔn kɔeɛ. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no anɔpa na wɔkɔeɛ (san kɔsra afidie no), na wɔyii anan (apataa). Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Wofa ɛnan yi, na ɔkyena a yɛbɛba akyere nwɔtwe no, me dane bɛgye wɔn. " Ananse no buaa sɛ, "Wofa me sɛ kwasea; wowɔ ɛnan yi, na ɔkyena me nso mefa nwɔtwe no. " Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no wɔkɔe (kɔsra afidie no) na wɔyii nwɔtwe no. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Fa (them), na to-ɔkyena me nso mefa the sixteen. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Wofa me sɛ kwasea; fa nwɔtwe no, na to-ɔkyena me bɛnya the sixteen. " Saa berɛ yi na mpataa no afiri no asɛe, na Kwaakwaadabi de ne nne kyerɛɛ Ananse no sɛ, "Kɔ na fa saa mpataa afiri a aporɔw yi, na sɛ obi tɔn a, ɔbɛnya ntanu (i. e. (£16) biara nti, saa ara na wo nso, Agya Ananse, fa apataa yi, na fa apataa a ɛporɔw no ma me. Ananse no kaa sɛ, Dabi, wode apataa no kɔtɔn, na me nso megye mfasoɔ a ɛwɔ apataa a ɛporɔw no. Wɔde nneɛma no kɔɔ dwaso bi so. Ntɛm ara na Kwaakwaadabi de ne nneɛma nyinaa kɔtoo hɔ, ɔnyaa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma pii. Nanso Ananse deɛ, na ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ. Ɛnna Kwaakwaadabi no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Fa saa mpataa-nneɛma a ɛporɔw yi kyinkyin na wɔntɔ. Ananse no maa wɔn so; ɔkae sɛ, Me retɔn mpataa-nneɛma a ɛporɔw! Afei na akuraa no mu panyin no tee, na ɔkae sɛ, Ɛhe na saa yɔnkoɔ yi fi ba? Kɔfrɛ no mma ɔmmra. Ananse no kɔɔ. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, Wosusu sɛ yɛn nkurɔfoɔ a yɛwɔ akuraa ha no yɛ nkwaseafoɔ; sesei wadamfo no baeɛ na ɔtɔn apataa (a ɛwɔ mfasoɔ), woanhyɛ no nsow, na wode apataa asau a aporɔw bɛto yɛn so? Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, Flog no. Enti wɔbɔɔ no. Ɛnna Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, Spider, woahu, waniwa awu ama aniwu; woahu sɛ na wokɔ hwehwɛ kwasea bi sɛ ɔne wo bɛyɛ adwuma, na afei woahu sɛ wo ankasa na woyɛ kwasea.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
