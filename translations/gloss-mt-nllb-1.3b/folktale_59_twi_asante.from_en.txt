Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ ƆKRAMAN, KRAMAN, NTUMI NNYƐ NE AKORƆMMƆ (AKWAN) MU AYARESA A ANYA DA

NA Kraman BI WƆ HƆ, na akorɔmmɔ suban nti, wantumi annya ɔbaa a ɔbɛware no. Enti ɔhyɛɛ da kɔɔ kuro bi so sɛ ɔrekɔpɛ ɔbaa a ɔbɛtena ne nkyɛn. (Ɔwaree, na ɔne no san kɔɔ ne akuraa so.) Ɔne ne yere no tenaa hɔ (berɛ bi), na ɛno na ɔhyɛɛ da, na ɔsan kɔɔ ne akuraa so. Ɛbɛduruu berɛ bi akyi no, ne kunu no de nkra kɔmaa sɛ ɔreba hɔ. Ne agya mmara faa ne tuo kɔɔ nwura mu sɛ ɔrekɔkum adowa bi wɔ ahoboa mu ma Kraman ba no. Ɛbɛyɛ nnansa akyi no, Kraman kɔɔ hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ no, ɔhunuu sɛ adowa bi honam da ogya so, adiwo mu. Ɛduruu anadwo no, ɔne ne yere no kɔɔ da. Na Kraman no rebɛtu anammɔn akɔ abɔnten akɔyɛ nsuo, berɛ a ɔhunuu nam a srade no refiri gu ogya no mu. Kraman no san kɔdii, na ɔde ne etire too ne sumiiɛ so (na ɔda) na ɔhwɛɛ nam no kyɛree. Afei na nam no hwam no kɔduruu ne nkyɛn, ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛsoa, nanso wantumi. Oh! sɔre na ɔkɔɔ baabi a nam no wɔ; ɔweɛ ne nyinaa, na san kɔdii. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, ɛduruu anɔpa kɛseɛ, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi, berɛ a na ne yere rekɔ sweep no, ɔhwehwɛɛ nam no, sin baako mpo nna hɔ. Ne yere kae sɛ, "Oh, maame! oh, maame! obi adidi nam no nyinaa. " Kraman no tee eyi no, naniwa wui (i.e. na ɔfɛre), na ɔfaa ne ntoma kataa ne etire so, na ɔda hɔ komm. Ɛnna ɔsɔre ntɛm ara na ɔhyɛɛ kwan so; ɔdwanee kɔɔ nankasa akuraa, na ɔkaa amaneɛbɔ no, sɛdeɛ ɔkɔwe ne mmara agya nam, ne sɛdeɛ naniwa awu aniwu, na ɛno nti, na ɔrekɔ hwehwɛ obi a ɔwɔ awuduro ano aduro ama akorɔmmɔ, na ebia wɔbɛma no bi. Na nnipa no reka sɛ, "Adanko, Hare no, wɔ aduru bi a ɛyɛ den paa, paa, paa. " Kraman no fii hɔ, na ɔde hareyɛ kɔduruu Hare no akuraa ase. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya Hare, medwane kɔ wo nkyɛn hwehwɛ mmoa; meyɛ ɔfɔmfoɔ, ɔfɔmfoɔ, ɔfɔmfoɔ, na wɔka sɛ wowɔ aduro a wode yɛ saa, enti me srɛ wo sɛ fa bi ma me, na nea ne boɔ bɛyɛ biara, metua. " Hare buae sɛ, "Ɛwom sɛ mewɔ bi, nanso kɔ fa mprako nam bi brɛ me. "

Kraman no fii adi kɔɔ akuraase bi na ɔfaa akyi nan biako a emu duru yɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i.e. 3s. 6d.) na ɔde bae. Hare no noa no, na ɔtwaa mu, na ɔhwee nnua nketewa so, na ɔyam ɛmo, ne nkyene, ne gyeene, na ɔde guu nam no mu, na ɔde too ogya so. Bere a ogya no bɛkaa no, berɛ bi akyi no, ɔtee (Kraman no) sɛ ɛrehyew, hyee, e e! Kraman no hwɛɛ no berɛ tenten. Afei Hare no kae sɛ, "Kraman, kɔ da dan ahodoɔ no mu baako, na me nso me bɛkɔ da foforɔ, na ɔkyena anɔpa, meyi nam yi (ogya) na mɛyɛ ho aduro ama wo. " Kraman no kɔɔ da. Hare no nso kɔɔ da. Afei Kraman no daadaa na ɔdaadaa (nanso ɔrentumi nna), na nam hwam no baa ne so wɔ ɛhwene no mu, nanso wanka hwee. Afei ɔsan hwenee bio. Ɔkae sɛ, "Agya Hare. " Hare no buae sɛ, "Aane! " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wo akuraa no mu nkura wɔ hɔ, enti merekɔfa nam no, na mede asie me nkyɛn. " Hare no kae sɛ, "Kɔfa no; me deɛ, nea mepɛ ne sɛ mede me nsa bɛto so ma-ɔkyena, ahyɛdeɛ sɛ mɛyɛ no aduro ama wo. " Enti Kraman no kɔfa no. Seesei Kraman da hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Father Hare. " Ɔbuaeɛ sɛ, "Aane! " Ɔkaa sɛ, "The nkura will chew it, so I am going to put it under my sumiiɛ, and go sleep. " Hare no kaa sɛ, "All I care about is that to-ɔkyena, when nneɛma become clear, I will make it aduro for you. " Kraman no da hɔ bio; bɔ mmɔden sɛnea ɔbɛtumi, wantumi ankura ne ho. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya Hare, nkura yi aba me sumiiɛ ase, enti mebɛfa nam no atoto me etire so, na ɛhɔ na mebɛgyina kɔsi sɛ nneɛma bɛda adi. " Hare no nso buae sɛ, "Oh, me kɔ so ka sɛ, nea ɛfa me ho ne sɛ mebɛnya nam no kɔsi-ɔkyena de ayɛ aduro ama wo. " Kraman no faa nam no atoto ne etire so, na ɛhɔ na ɔgyinaa. Ɛnna forowe no bi tui baa nanim, na ɔfeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, Father Hare, saa nam yi deɛ, saa nkura no foro me so, na ɛba me etire so, enti mɛkura (nam) no wɔ me ano. " Hare buae sɛ, "Oho o! Meka no daa sɛ kɔsi ɔkyena, bere a nneɛma bɛda adi no, me ne wo bɛyɛ aduro. " Afei na ɛyɛ ntɛm wɔ nano, na ɔdwannii dan no mu hɔ; ɔbɔɔ ne dua (with a nsakraeɛ nnyigyei) sɛ fre! fre! fre! Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya Hare, ma me nni wo ɛka wɔ nam yi ho. " Hare no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na wopɛ sɛ wodi ho ɛka? " Na Kraman no nso buae sɛ, "Me kyerɛ sɛ, mmom sen sɛ saa nkura yi bɛdi no, ma me ntua wo. " Hare no kaa sɛ, "Oh no! ma ɛnkyena anɔpa mmra. " Oh! nanso sesei Kraman no maa (nam no kɔɔ (n'menem fam). Ɛnna ɔdaeɛ. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, ɛyɛ anɔpa paa, Hare no kae sɛ, "Kraman, fa saa nam no mmra. " Kraman no buae sɛ, "Ho, Father Hare, adɛn nti na woante nea meka kyerɛɛ wo ɛnora sɛ me nni wo ɛka (wɔ nam no ho)? " Hare no buae sɛ, "Enti woadi no? " Kraman no kae sɛ, "Aane! Me dii no, efisɛ matɔ no. " Hare no kae sɛ, "Wo, Kraman, korɔno a wɔfrɛ no 'korɔno' no remmɛn wo da. "

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
