Ɔbaa bi woo ne abofra; bere a ɔresoma no (anywhere), ɔkae sɛ, "Me nkɔ. " Ɔ (maame) kae sɛ, "Ɛnne tena hɔ, wo ne wo nyansa akwan. " Daa a ɔresoma no (on any eRRAND) no, ɔkae sɛ, "Me nkɔ. " Ɛnna da bi ɔkae sɛ, "Kɔ sprinkle nsuo na fa brɛ me. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me nkɔ. " Ɛnna aberewa no bɔɔ no na ɔkae sɛ, "Twɛn hɔ ara, na wobɛhu. " Na t he child spoke and said, "I shall go and search for 'I will see' that I may see (what it is like). " Na ɔhyɛɛ kwan no, na ɔkɔɔ mmirikatuo kɛseɛ, (na ne anan nnyigyei te sɛ) yiridi, yiridi, yiridi. Ɛbɛduruu tenten so no, ɔduruu akuraa bi, na ɔhunuu aberewa bi a ɔte hɔ. Bere a ɔhunuu no, na ɔrebɛtwe ne ho. Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Me mmɔfra, nsan nsane mma efisɛ mahu wo. " Ɛnna abofra no kɔɔ anim; na aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na nsuo a ɛfiri waniwa mu no abɔ wo saa? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me ne me maame na yɛte hɔ bɔ mu, da biara a ɔsɔre no, na ɔka sɛ, Twɛn hɔ ara na wubehu, na ɔboroo me, enti mekaeɛ sɛ, mebɛba abɛhwehwɛ sɛ mebɛhu (sɛnea ɛteɛ no). na ɛte sɛ). Ɛnna aberewa no kae sɛ, "Me ne no. " Ɛnna ɔkae sɛ, "Kɔ fie no akyi hɔ kɔtu bayerɛ na fa ba; bere a wobɛkɔ akɔdu hɔ no, nea ɛbɛka sɛ, 'Dig me up, dig me up, dig me up' no, ntu no; nanso nea ɛbɛka sɛ, 'Don't dig me up, don't dig me up' no, ɛno na ɛkae. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Ho! " Ɛyɛ nokware sɛ ɔkɔɔ hɔ, bayerɛ no bi ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Dig me up, dig me up. " p, dig me up, dig me up; afoforo nso kae sɛ, "Don't dig me up, don't dig me up, don't dig me up. " Wɔn ebinom a wɔkae sɛ, "Don't dig me up," na ɛyɛ ebinom a ɔtutuu. Na ɔde bae; na aberewa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Skin them and put them on the ogya. " Na ɔtutu wɔn na ɔde gu (ogya) so, na sɛ wɔnoa a, na ɔyiyi gu aduane so, na ɔfa si aberewa no anim. Na ɔse, "Kɔ kuro no kuro akyi, na hwɛ asaworowa bi a nam da so, hwɛ nam no ntam na fa wo apawde no. " Na abofra no faa bi, na ɔbaa, na ɔde too ogya no so, na ɔyɛɛ nkwan. Bere a owiee no, ɔde too aberewa no anim. Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Wowiee anaa? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Aane, Nana. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Bra bɔ fufuo no. " Na aberewa no kɔtoo nakyiri, ɔkae sɛ, "Boaboa bayerɛ a wɔanoa no ano, fa gu ɛhwene mu, na bɔ no. " Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛha na ɛyɛ nwanwa, sɛ yɛbɔ fufuo wɔ onipa ɛhwene mu a, eyi ne nea maame kaa ho asɛm. " Wei nyinaa na ɔkaeɛ wɔ netire mu. Ɔkɔɔ na ɔbɔɔ, nanso bere a ɔbɔɔ no, ɔbɔɔ no dɛɛdɛɛ paa. Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Bɔ no den, sɛ woammɔ no den a, wo kokoɔ no bɛpae. " Na ɔbɔɔ no pi! pi! pi! na bere a wawiee no, ɔhwiee guu nkwan no so, na ɔde sii hɔ wɔ aberewa no anim. Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Me nana, bɔ me din na fa nni. " Na ɔkae sɛ, "You are called Old-woman-grandma. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Ei! ɔnyɛɛ nani so sɛ ɔbɛtumi adi. " Na aberewa no dii ne nyinaa. Nnawɔtwe nyinaa, ɔyɛɛ no saa daa. Na da koro bi na abofra no kɔɔ asuo no so sɛ ɔrebɔ nsuo. Kɔtɔ no huu no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na obi ba abɛyɛ teateaa saa? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Owurayere bi wɔ hɔ a me ne no te, na sɛ mehyɛ no aduane na mema no a, ɔka sɛ, 'Ka me din na wowe nni'; Meka sɛ, 'Wɔfrɛ wo Owurayere-ɔbea,' na ɔka sɛ, 'Ei! wunhuu kwan sɛ wobɛdidi,' na afei ɔdidi, ɛno nti na me ho yɛ me den. " Kɔtɔ no kaa sɛ, "Ne din ne Ɔpanyin-Mosono. " Ɛnna abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ho. " Na ɔde ne nsuo-bɔtɔ no sii netire so. Kɔtɔ no kaa sɛ, "Don't forget as you're going back. " Na abofra no hyɛ aseɛ; ɛberɛ a na ɔreba no nyinaa, na ɔreka sɛ, "Old-woman-Mosono, Mosono, Mosono. " Na ɔduruu kuro no kuro akyi no, na ne werɛ afiri, na ɔhinhim na ne nsuo-nkuku no abubu, na ɔkɔɔ ne fie. Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Nsuo-bɔ no wɔ he? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Na meba no, mebɛtuiɛ, na ɛbubuiɛ. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa foforɔ kɔ. " Na ɔfaa no kɔeɛ; na Kɔtɔ no kaa sɛ, "Woasan aba dedaw? " Na ɔkaa sɛ, "Saa din a woka kyerɛɛ me no, me kɔeɛ na me werɛ firi, na meba abɛbisa no bio. " Kɔtɔ no kaa sɛ, "Ne din ne Ɔpanyin-Mosono. " Na ɔse, "Ho. " Ɔkɔɔ so kaa no daa. kwan fie, na ɔduruu fie no ɔtenaa ase (kuku no). Aberewa no kaa sɛ, Kɔ tu bayerɛ na fa ba. Ɔyɛɛ ne nyinaa sɛdeɛ ɛteɛ biara, na ɔde aduane no baeɛ, na ɔde sii aberewa no anim, na aberewa no kaa sɛ, Ka me din na fa nni. Ɔkaa sɛ, Wɔfrɛ wo Aberewa-Nana. Ɔkaa sɛ, Me kyerɛ me ankasa din. Ɔkaa sɛ, Wo din ne sɛn? Aberewa no kaa sɛ, Aane. Ɔkaa sɛ, Wo din ne sɛn? Aberewa no kaa sɛ, Aane. Ɔkaa sɛ, Wo din ne Aberewa-Mosono. Aberewa no kaa sɛ, Wo din ne sɛn? kae sɛ, "Fa nni. " Na abofra no gyee dii. Na aberewa no de nani kyerɛ kwan, na ɔkae sɛ, "Mekɔ hwehwɛ onipa a ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ me din ne Mosono no ara. " Na ɔkɔɔ asuwa no so, na ɔkɔhyiaa Ɔbontoya, ɔkraman no, (na ɔbɔɔ no ahohora sɛ), "Wo 'Bonto, wo maame! wo 'Bonto, wo agya! woka kyerɛɛ me banana akokoaa no sɛ me din ne Mosono? Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ me. " Aberewa no huu sɛ Adwene (apataa) no gyina nkyɛn, ɔhyɛɛ no ahohora sɛ, "Wo Adwene, wo maame! Wo maame! Wo maame! Wo Adwene, wo agya! Wo agya! Wo banana akokoaa no, woka kyerɛɛ me sɛ me din ne Mosono? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ me. " Ɔhunuu sɛ Kɔtɔ no gyina hɔ. Ɔbɔɔ no nnome sɛ, "Wo Kɔtɔ, wo maame! Me maame! Me maame! Wo Kɔtɔ, wo agya! Me agya! wo na woka kyerɛɛ me banana akokoaa no sɛ me te Nkran, na meyɛ Ɔdehye mogya, na me din de Mosono? " Kɔtɔ no kaa sɛ, "Aane, me na meka kyerɛɛ no, na adɛn nti na woama obi ba abere saa? " Na aberewa no de ne bo fuu, na ne calabash a na okura no, ɔmaa so tow bɔɔ Kɔtɔ no. Ɛno nti na Kɔtɔ no wɔ hono wɔ nakyi. Ɛduruu tete no na Kɔtɔ no akyi no na hono biara nni hɔ. Na Kɔtɔ no nso faa dɔte fitaa, na ɔtow guu aberewa no ti atifi. Ɛno nti na aberewa no ti atifi ntaadeɛ no ti nwi fitaa.
