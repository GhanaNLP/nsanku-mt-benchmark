SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ ƆBONTOYA, MPATWA KETEWA NO, BƐDAA "BASABASA" WƆ NSUO MU BERE NYINAA.

WƆKYERƐ SƐ NA ƆBAA KAKUO WƆ HƆ, NA NE agyapadeɛ NO AYera. Ɔkaa sɛ "nsuo mu mmoa" na ɛagye (nneɛma no. Na kɔtɔ no te hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ, "Ah, woama yɛn aniwa ato yɛn aniwu, na nea yɛrehyɛ ho nyansa sɛ yɛbɛyɛ de akyerɛ wo nea ɛwɔ yɛn etire ne sɛ: kɔfa kɔbere dasɛn na yɛn nyinaa mfa (yɛn) nnisuo nhyɛ mu, na nea ɔntumi nsu nhyɛ mu no, ɔno na wagye (nneɛma no. "

Ɔde kɔbere dimensiɛn no bae. Okoto, kɔtɔ no, hyɛɛ no ma; wɔyii no gui; Mmɔborɔhunu bae wee; Okawa bae wee; Oyoyo, ɔwɔ-nsuom bae wee; Otidi bae wee; Ɔbonko, mmɔtɔ bae wee; Akɔreseɛ bae wee; Opitiri bae wee; Ɔkɔbɔ bae wee; nsuo mu mmoa nyinaa bae wee. Nea ɛtwa toɔ ne Ɔbontoya, Mpatwa ketewa no, ɔbaa su; ɔammɛnya nsuo amfiri naniwa so, ɛno nti na wɔsusuu sɛ Ɔbontoya na ɔfa nneɛma no.

Nanso Ɔbaa Kɛseɛ no kaa sɛ, "Merenyɛ wo hwee. " Ɛneɛ, abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no nyinaa kaa sɛ, "Yɛn deɛ, yɛbɛbɔ no, efisɛ wama yɛn aniwu. " Enti wɔbɔɔ no.

Bere a wɔboroo no wiei no, ɔbaa dada no kaa sɛ, "Megyaw wo (daa) sɛ guguan a wunni botae biara; obiara rempɛ sɛ ɔne wo di hwee da. "

Ɛno ne sianteɛ a ɛne sɛ, berɛ a mmaa "go splashing" (apataa no), wɔhu Ɔbontoya kyinkyin (seesei) wɔ ha ne hɔ.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
