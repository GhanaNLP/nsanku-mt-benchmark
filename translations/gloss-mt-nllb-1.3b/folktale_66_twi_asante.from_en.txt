Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

KƆKƆKYINAKA, TURACOU A NE HO YƐ BLU, ANKƆ KWAKU ANANSE, ANANSE, KƆƆ BƆSEƐ BI NTI

ƐBAA berɛ bi a, Kɔkɔkyinaka anomaa no kɔɔ Kwaku Ananse, Ananse, kɔhwehwɛɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (£4) bɔseɛ a ne mu duru yɛ asuanu. Seesei, Kwaku Ananse ankasa nni bi, enti ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔgyee ne adamfo Ntontom, Ntontom, kɔmaa Kɔkɔkyinaka anomaa no. Ɛduruu da a ɛtwaa mu a na Ntontom no ahyɛ da (for the bɔseɛ no ntua), na Ntontom no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, Kɔ na fa me sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no ma me. Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, Kɔkɔkyinaka anomaa no te saa silk-cotton dua no atifi, enti kɔ na gye wo sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no. Ntontom no bisaa sɛ, Ɛbɛyɛ dɛn na me ne no bɛkɔ, abɔdeɛ no tumi tu. Ananse no buae sɛ, Kɔ na dwane kɔ dua no so, na bere a wubehu , dua bɛpae, ɛne sɛ woakyere Kɔkɔkyinaka anomaa no, na woanya wo sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no bio. Ntontom no kɔtoo dua no so. Berɛ a ɛrebɛpae no, na Kɔkɔkyinaka anomaa no afiri hɔ akɔ, pa bu bu! Ɛnna Ntontom no baeɛ, na ɔkaa sɛ, Congratulations, Kwaku Ananse. Ɛno nti na yɛwɔ abebuo bi a ɛtu mmirika sɛ, Abɔdeɛ a wɔfrɛ no Kɔkɔkyinaka anomaa no amfa Kwaku Ananse hɔ sɛ Ntontom no nkɔtu Silk-cotton-tree no ase. Wei deɛ, me abasɛm a mekaeɛ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
