Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

NA ƐYƐ SƐ OKA NAM DUA YƐ KƆKƆ, NA KƆBƆ NAM NO MENEWA YƐ NSENSANEE

NA ƆBAA BI TE HƆ A ƆYƐ Ɔhemmaa, NA ƆNNI abofra, NA ƆKƆƆ ƐHƆ NSOMATONO NO MU BIAKO NKYƐƐ ahyɛdeɛ SƐ onyankopɔn MMA NO NYINA AWO abofra. Na ɔde bi maa no, nanso ɔbɔɔ no amanneɛ sɛ, "The abofra, when you bear it, must not eat any nam except sheep's liver only, for even its flesh is taboo. " Ɛnneɛ, da bi, (after the ɔbaa had borne the abofra) maame no kɔɔ afuw no mu, na ɔde abofra no mmerɛboɔ maa ne mmaawa Ɔkra sɛ ɔnoa na ɔmfa mma ne abofra no. Afei Ɔkra faa no, noa aduane no, nanso ɔkɔfaa odwan no nam ankasa bi, na ɔde bi nso hyɛɛ mu (kaa berɛboɔ no ho). Ɔhene babea no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Maame kae sɛ me akyiwadeɛ ne nam ankasa, na wode bi ahyɛ mu, na sɛ me di a, mewu. " Ɔkra buae sɛ, "Aane, mede bi ahyɛ mu, na sɛ wuwu a, wuwu. " Ɛnna Ɔhene babea no buae sɛ, "Medi nni na mewu? " Na ɔkɔɔ dan no mu sɛ ɔrekɔfa ne maame silk ntoma kɔkɔɔ no de kyekyere ne ho. Ɛnna abofra no sii kwan so, na Ɔkra dii nakyi. Ɔkra suroe sɛ sɛ ne mpena baa no hyia no a, ɔbɛnya ɔhaw. Na wɔrekɔ so no, Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no abofra no kae sɛ, "Ɔkra! " Ɔkra kae sɛ, "Aane! " Ɔkae sɛ, "Sɛ mema wo nkra a, wobɛka? " (i.e. mede bɛma). Ɔkra kae sɛ, "Mɛka no, me bɛka no, ansa na mewu; me bɛka no, me bɛka no, ansa na mewu.

Bere a mekɔɔ hɔ dada no, bayere bi bɛsii hɔ.

Seesei mebaa na kokote da hɔ.

Kwae mu mprako a wɔfrɛ no Asamoa, Kuruti Amoa, na ɛyɛ nea ɔda hɔ.

Me nni Gyanoa sika kɔkɔɔ dɔteɛ no mu biara, Boafo Denkye, Ɔpanin no, kɔsi da a me nkwa asa.

Ɔse, wɔnkum odwan mma no nni, wɔnkum anomaa mma no nni.

Ɔka sɛ, 'Me kunu, anɔpa pa.'

Ɔbua sɛ, 'Ya'ku.'

Ɔka sɛ, 'Owudifo no pɛ sɛ monya anɔpa pa.'

Ɔse, 'Yiw, Abrawo.'

Ɔka sɛ, 'Me nuabea, ɛhe na worekɔ na memfa nkra mma wo?'

Ɔse, 'Mekɔ Nana akuraa ase, Sɛkyerɛ Gyamase.'

Ɔka sɛ, 'Ɛdeɛn na aba wɔ nananom akuraa, Sɛkyerɛ Gyamase, a akyene nna da? Oboama agya, Agyei Ampɔn; agya akɔ ɔko, anaa agya akɔ dwadie sɛ wahu sɛ demmire tenten atɔ na ɛne wuram nhama abɔ?

Mmienu nsan mmae da, Ntim Sekyere ne Kwakye nsan mmae da.'

Ɔka sɛ, 'Oh, Parrot, Boama, woaka asɛm bebree, gyae na ma me nkasa kakra, wo aniwa nane wɔ nsuo mu.

Wo nana Mpampinam kɔɔ mpataayi, na ɔkyeree ɔtwee kɛse pa ara.

Kwao Adu Dakowa, Bantama Mpedu Okyere, Adwoa Kɛseɛ, Odum Abena ne ne fetish (suman) a wɔfrɛ no you walk in vain.

Adwoa e, Adwoa e, Kobi Asubɔnten no nua ba, Tontoroni ya, a ewiase yɛ wo dea.' "

Na afei abofra no hyiaa ne maame. Na maame no kae sɛ, Eyi, me abofra, a mehyia no ha, ɛwom sɛ migyaw no fie de, nanso dɛn (bɔne) na aka no ma ɔreguan, yiridi yiridi? Ɔhemmaa no hyɛɛ ne nkoa a wɔdii nakyi no sɛ wɔnkyere no mma no. Nanso wɔantumi annye no; na ɔkɔɔ so ara na ɔdwanee, na ɔhwee asubonten no mu, na Ɔkra (afenaa) no nso hwee mu. Bere a woduu wɔn nkyɛn no, Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no danee apataa a ɔwɔ dua kɔkɔɔ, na Ɔkra danee Ɔkɔbɔ nam a na nnsenene wɔ nafono so. Oka dua kɔkɔɔ no yɛ ntoma kɔkɔɔ a Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no hyɛɛ na ɔhwee nsuo mu, na nnsenene a ɛwɔ Ɔkɔbɔ no nsawa so no yɛ Ɔkra afenaa no nsonsoo.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
