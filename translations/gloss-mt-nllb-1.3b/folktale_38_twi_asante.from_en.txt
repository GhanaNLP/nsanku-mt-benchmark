Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

MPANIMFOƆ KYERƐ SƐ, "ƐYƐ WBUSUANI, ANAA KUNU, ANAA OBIARA A ƆWƆ ADWUMA BI YƐ, SƐ ƆBISA WO A, BOA NO"

KWAKU ANANSE a ɔyɛ Ananse no ka kyerɛɛ "Aso" (ne yere) sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔ yɛ turo. " "Aso" kae sɛ, "Me nkɔ. " Kwaku Ananse kɔtuaa fam. Bere a ɔsan bae no, ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Woyɛ nokware ampa sɛ worekɔ? " "Aso" buae sɛ, "ɔbea akyiwade bi a ɔreyi fam. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "To-ɔkyena ma yɛnkɔ hye turo mu wira no. " Aso" kae sɛ, "ɔbea akyiwade bi a ɛrehyew turo mu wira, na me deɛ, me kɔ ma ogya no hye me anaa? " Enti Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ na ɔsan bae. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔkaa sɛ, ""Aso", me srɛ mo, momma yɛn nkɔ bɔ mu nsisi nneɛma. " Ɔkaa sɛ, " mekaeɛ sɛ, me nkɔ. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, " Me hu mmusu. " Ɛnna ɔkɔsii nneɛma nyinaa fɛfɛɛfɛ. Seesei na wɔwɔ hɔ, na nnɔbaeɛ no aba. Ɛda bi "Aso" kɔɔ afuo hɔ. Ɔbaa bɛka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, " Kwaku, nnɔbaeɛ no aba wɔ turo no mu, na yɛbɛkɔ hɔ berɛ bɛn? " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, " Ho! nea saa Nkramoni no kaeɛ no, merentumi nte aseɛ koraa. " "Aso" kaeɛ sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ no; kasa na me nte wo ase. " Kwaku Ananse kaeɛ sɛ, "Mekɔ na masane aba. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ hɔ kɔtoo turo no mu, na ɛhɔ na ɔgyinaa hɔ kyɛree, na ɔkaa (ne ho) sɛ, "Me, Ananse Kwaku, me ne ha, na mebɛtra ase ama yi, ɔbaa kɛkɛ yi, ayɛ me kwasea? " Kwaku Ananse faa Sekkan, agyan, ne asɔ, na ɔtutuu amena kɛse pa ara. Ɔfaa nnɔbaeɛ a ɛwɔ afuo no mu nyinaa siee mu, na ɔsan kɔɔ ne fie. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Aso", bra ha na menka amaneɛbɔ nkyerɛ wo. " Aso" baeɛ. Ɔkae sɛ, "Na mekɔɔ kramoni bi nkyɛn kɔhwehwɛɛ me ahonyadeɛ ho asɛm; ɔse me nna nwɔtwe na aka ansa na mewu, enti menkɔ afuo bio, na wo nso nkɔ hɔ bio, na sɛ mewu a, wobɛkɔ hɔ adwiri me. Mebɔ amena kɛseɛ bi wɔ hɔ, na wobɛsie me. " Nna nwɔtwe no baa mu; Kwaku Ananse yɛɛ ne ho sɛ ɔbɛwu. Ananse no, nnipa no faa nnanse no so, na wɔkɔsiee no wɔ ne turo no mu wɔ hɔ. Wɔsan baa fie, na wɔyɛɛ ayiyɛ amanne pa. "Aso" nso yɛɛ kununa amanneɛ. Berɛ a wɔreyɛ nna nwɔtwe ayi no, "Aso" kaa sɛ, "Ah, ma me nkɔhwɛ me kunu turo. " "Aso" kɔeɛ, ɛhɔ na turo no da, dadwa! "Aso" kae sɛ, Oh, turo a me kunu yɛɛ na na me aniwa da so (ma me aduane), nnipa bi aba abɛdi nea ɛwɔ mu nyinaa. Ɛnna ɔtee su guu wɔn so nnome, kae sɛ, (Me frɛ) obi a ɔwɔ honhom wɔ Kwaku Ananse, wo afuo mu nnɔbaeɛ a wode gyaw me no nyinaa, nnipa aba abɛdi.

Kwaku Ananse maa ne nne so (na ɔtoo dwom):

"Gyae (complaining)! Me ara me deɛ na medii!

Gyae (anwiinwii), "Aso" tabuos clearing nwira.

Gyae (anwiinwii), me ara me deɛ na medii!

Cease (complaining), Aso akyiwadeɛ a ɛrehyew (the turo mu wira). Cease (complaining), I have only eaten what is mine!

Gyae (complaining), "Aso" tabu planting.

Gyae (anwiinwii), me ara mankasa aduan na madi! "

Ɛhɔ na "Aso" gyinaa hɔ pintinn, na na ɔsuro. Ɔkɔɔ fie na ɔfrɛɛ Adanko, Hare no, sɛ ɔmmra na ɔmmɛte nsɛmfua bi a ɛwɔ ne kunu afuo hɔ. Hare no na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ. "Aso" kɔduruu afuo hɔ, na ɔsan maa ne nne so sɛ:

"Na me kunu ne Kwaku, Ananse no, Na ɔwui na ɔgyaee ne afuo no maa me, Na nnipa bae bɛdii aduane a ɛwɔ mu no.

Kwaku honhom, Ananse, me koto wo, me srɛ wo, kum wɔn mma me. "

Kwaku Ananse maa ne nne so:

"Gyae (anwiinwii), medii nea ɛyɛ me dea nko ara!

Gyae (anwiinwii), "Aso" tabuos clearing nwira.

Gyae (anwiinwii), me ara me deɛ na medii!

Cease (complaining), Aso akyiwadeɛ a ɛrehyew (the turo mu wira).

Gyae (anwiinwii), mewee nea ɛyɛ me dea nko ara!

Gyae (complaining), "Aso" tabu planting.

Gyae (anwiinwii), mewee nea ɛyɛ me dea nko ara; gyae (anwiinwii).

Ɛfiri berɛ a Ɔbɔadeɛ yɛɛ nneɛma, woahunu ɔbaa a ɔmmoa ne kunu?

Gyae (anwiinwii), medii nea ɛyɛ me dea nko ara; gyae (anwiinwii)! "

Hare no kaa sɛ, "Mewie tie, ma yɛnkɔ fie na mɛtumi aka nea ɛyɛ nyinaa akyerɛ mo. " Ɔne "Aso" kɔɔ fie. Hare kaa sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ, 'Yɛnkɔ yi turo bi;' wokae sɛ worekɔ; 'Yɛnkɔ yɛnkɔhyew (the dry weeds) '; na wokae sɛ worekɔ; 'Yɛnkɔ yɛnkɔ dua'; na wokae sɛ worekɔ; enti ebia wogyae su, efisɛ odidi ne deɛ, ɛfiri berɛ a Ɔbɔadeɛ bɔɔ adeɛ no, onhuu ɔbaa biara a ɔmmoa ne kunu, enti gyae lamenti ng (he says), for he has only eaten all that is his. " Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "When any one at all is engaged in any adwuma and he asks you, help him. "

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
