Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐDƐN NTI NA ANANSE, ANANSE, MMIRIKA AHOROƆ BERE A ƆWƆ NSUO ANI SO

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse, kɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔkraman, Kraman, Me kɔn dɔ sɛ me ne wo kɔ yɛ akuraa ketewa bi a yɛbɛtena mu. Kraman kaa sɛ, Me nso mepɛ saa. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Sɛ Kwasida-Adae bi tɔ a, ɛdwoada a ɛdi hɔ yi, kɔ fa hama-creeper bi; me nso me bɛfa hama-creeper bi na yɛbɛfiri hɔ. Bio nso, mebɛfa towa bi ahyɛ mu nsuo, na wo nso splash bi, ɛfiri sɛ ebia, baabi a yɛrekɔ no, ebia nsuo biara nni hɔ. Kwaku Ananse splashed some nsuo, Kraman no nso, ɛwoɔ de hyɛɛ towa no mu. Na wɔrekɔ, na wɔadu kwan mfinimfini, berɛ a wɔabrɛ. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Momma yɛnnom yɛn nsuo no bi. Kraman no kaa sɛ, Mowɔ me deɛ bi nnom. Kwaku Ananse faa bi, na ɔde ne ano kaa no, ɔkae sɛ, Oh, ɛhe na eyi firi? Kraman no kaa sɛ, Ɛwɔ me yafunu mu. Ananse no kaa sɛ, Aane! Bere a wɔte hɔ sɛ wɔrebɛgye wɔn ahome no, Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Kraman, momma yɛnka yɛn ho wɔ agorɔ mu. Kraman no kaa sɛ, Ɛyɛ agorɔ bɛn? Ananse no buae sɛ, The binding-binding game; y ou bind me first, then I, too, shall bind you, but when you hear me say, Wei! then you leave off. Kraman no kaa sɛ, No, do you bind me first. Ananse no kaa sɛ, Take yourself off, when your ɔpanin is telling you something, do you argue about it? Kraman no kaa sɛ, Well, let it remain (as you wish). Ananse no kaa sɛ, Come and bind me. Kraman no faa ne rope-creeper no kyekyeree Ananse no. Na ɔde no too ne etire so sɛ ɔrebɛtɔn no na wanya biribi adi. Kwaku Ananse tee saa no, ɔmaa awerɛhosu so kae sɛ:

Me, Ananse Akuamoa, minnya aboa kɛseɛ nea ɛfata mpo a wɔde bɛtɔn me;

Kraman yi nko ara deɛ, ɔrebɛtɔn me.

Hena na metumi anya no na wagye me?

O, ɔkwan, gye me!

Kwaku Ananse antumi anka hwee gyesɛ ɔsu. Kraman no soaa no kɔsii sɛ ɔduruu asuwa bi so. Ɛnneɛ, Ɔdɛnkyɛm, Akokɔsrade no, tee sɛ Akraman no su. Ɔbaa no, ɔbisaa Kraman no sɛ asɛm bɛn na ɛwɔ hɔ. Kraman no tuu Kwaku Ananse gui, dwanee, na Akokɔsrade no gyaee Kwaku Ananse. Na Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Agya, ayamye adeɛ bɛn na metumi de ama wo de akyerɛ sɛ meda wo ase? Akokɔsrade no kae sɛ, Yɛn ani nni hwee koraa. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Sɛ wowɔ mmofra a, ɛnneɛ ɔkyena me bɛba abɛhyɛ wɔn ti nwi ma ɛfata wɔn paa. Akokɔsrade no kae sɛ, Me wate. Kwaku Ananse baa fie; ɔkae sɛ, Aso, kɔ hwehwɛ nnuadewa ne gyeene, ɛnneɛ ɔkyena me rebɛkum na meabɔ ntadeɛ. g back a crocodile that we may have a nkwan ne foroe to eat. " "Aso" kɔe na ɔgyee bi, na Ananse no bɔɔ ne sekkan ne ne ɛtɔ, na ɔde kɔɔ asuo no so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Father Crocodile, the Nsuo-animal, I am coming to reward you. " Na ɔfaa fufuo no de wɔn kɔɔ nsuo no mu. Akokɔsrade no bae, na ɔrebɛfa fufuo no adi, bere a Ananse no faa ne sekkan no twitwaa no sɛ, gya! nanso wannya no (properly), na ɔde ne ho kɔeɛ. Kwaku Ananse san baa fie. "Aso" bisaa no sɛ, "Ɛhe na saa aboa no wɔ? " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Wo ho nnye; onipa bi fi akwantu ba, na wannya nahome mpo aba, na wofiri aseɛ de nsɛmmisa reha no. " "Aso" kae sɛ, "Me hu sɛ woannye no. " Kwaku Ananse anka hwee. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, anɔpa kɛseɛ, "Aso" kaa sɛ ɔrekɔ asuwa. Bere a ɔduruu hɔ no, na Akokɔsrade da asubonten ano, na blue-bottle-flies no resu kurukuru no. "Aso" san baa fie kɔka kyerɛɛ ne kunu. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Seesei wuhu sɛnea aduru bi a mewɔ no, ɛyɛ adwuma; sɛ mekum nam (da bi), ɛnyɛ kosi sɛ nneɛma bɛda adi, da a edi hɔ no, me kɔfa no, nanso meda wo ase sɛ woahu no. Kwaku Ananse twaa poma bi de no kɔɔ asuwa a ɛwɔ hɔ no so, na ɛhɔ na ɔhunuu Akokɔsrade. Ɔde poma no bɔɔ no sɛ, Akokɔsrade, wo awu anaa? Akokɔsrade, wowui anaa? " na ɔdane no kɔ ne nkyɛn. Na Akokɔsrade no da hɔ ara, nanso na onwuiɛ. Bere a Kwaku Ananse rebɛfa ne nsa aka no, na Akokɔsrade no akyere no mpofirim, kuadwuma! Kwaku Ananse peree ne ho, petere! petere! na ɔnyaa kwan a ɔbɛfa so aguan. Ɛno nti, sɛ Ananse no fa nsuo no so a, na ɔyɛ ntɛm; na ɔsuro sɛ ebia Akokɔsrade no bɛba abɛkyere no bio.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
