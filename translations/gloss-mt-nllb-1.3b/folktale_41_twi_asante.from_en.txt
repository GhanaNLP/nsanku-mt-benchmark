Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐ WOREKƆ BAABIARA A, SƐ WO BUSUANI KYERƐ SƐ ƆBƐKA WO HO A, ƐNE NO KƆ

NA ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔwoo mmofra baasa. Wɔn mu kumaa no na ɔyare yaws. Wɔn anuanom no mu panyin no ka kyerɛɛ wɔn maame sɛ wɔmma wɔn nni sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma sɛ wɔbɛtumi akɔ dwadie. Wɔn mu kumaa no nso kaa sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkɔ nso. Wɔbɔɔ amanneɛ sɛ ɔne wɔn nkɔ. Wɔn maame no kaa sɛ wɔne no nkɔ. Ɛnna wɔn maame de sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma maa ɔpanyin (mmabarima) no de maa no mfasoɔ a ɛyɛ £5, ɛne sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma nso de maa no mfasoɔ a ɛyɛ £2, maa kumaa no. Ɛduruu sɛ nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ. Mpanyimfoɔ no kɔɔ (di kan) na wɔgyaee abofra no (di akyi). Abofra no de nkakrankakra na ɛbaeɛ. Mpanyimfoɔ no hyiaa obarima bi a ɔde apataa reba. Ɔka kyerɛɛ mpanimfoɔ no sɛ, "Tɔ! " Wɔbuaa no sɛ, "Kɔ so, na wobɛhyia abofra bi; ma no ntɔ, na sɛ ɔbɛka sɛ ɔrentɔ a, bɔ no, na ma no ntɔ; na sɛ ɔda so ara mpene so sɛ wobɛtɔ a, fa no na wo ne no nsan nkɔ ne maame nkyɛn. " Saa yɔnkoɔ no kɔɔ so, na akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho sɛ ɔbɛhyia abofra no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mpanimfoɔ bi a mehyiaa wɔn no, wɔkae sɛ woretɔ apataa yi. " Ɔba no kaa sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ metɔ, berɛ a wɔn a wɔyɛ me mpanimfoɔ no anntɔ? " Ɔba no kaa sɛ, "Wɔse sɛ wontɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ mebɔ wo akuturuku, na ɛsɛ sɛ mefa wo san de wo ma wo maame. " Ɔba no kaa sɛ, "Ɛdɔɔso dɛn? " Ɔba no kaa sɛ, "An osua mu duru a ɛyɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. e. £2). " Ɔba no tuaa ka no, na ɔgyee. Ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔkɔtoo mpanimfoɔ no wɔ akuraase bi. Wɔayɛ atosodeɛ, nanso wɔnni nam a wɔde bɛkɔ. Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ apataa bi a matɔ. " Wɔde no too soup-stew no mu. Wɔwieeɛ no, mpanimfoɔ no de apataa no etire maa no. Bere a abofra no rebɛpae no, ɔhunuu sika kɔkɔɔ trɔm a ɛwɔ mu, na ɔde kyekyeree ne ntoma ano. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, mpanimfoɔ no sii gyinaeɛ, na wɔrekɔ no, wɔkɔhyiaa ɔbarima bi, na akokɔnini bi ato nneɛma no atifi, a na ɔsoa. Ɛberɛ a ɔne mpanimfoɔ no bɛn no, ɔkae sɛ, "Tɔ akokɔnini yi. " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Kɔ so, na wobɛhyia abofra bi; ma ɔntɔ, na sɛ ɔrentɔ a, bɔ no. " Ɛyɛ nokware a ɛne sɛ ɔkɔhyiaa abofra no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo abusuafo mpanimfo kae sɛ, woretɔ akokɔnini yi, na sɛ woantɔ a, mebɛbɔ wo, na mede wo akɔ wo maame nkyɛn. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Hwɛ, fa nea mewɔ. " Ne yɔnkoɔ nso de no maa no akokɔnini no. Wɔduruu akuraase bi. Wɔ akuraa no nyinaa mu no, na akokɔnini nni kwaakwaadabi. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ anɔpa, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, akokɔnini a ɛyɛ abofra no deɛ no, ɛdɔm. Ɔpanyin a ɔwɔ akuraa hɔ no kaa sɛ, "Abofra, fa akokɔnini no mmra na memfa ntɔ. " Ɔ abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ne boɔ yɛ osua ne sure sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. e. £3). " Ɔpanyin no tuaa sika no. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛdaa hɔ no, wɔsan hyɛɛ aseɛ bio. Bere a wɔrekɔ no, mpanimfoɔ no hyiaa ɔbarima bi a ɔkura agyinamoa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Tɔ saa agyinamoa yi. " Ɛneɛ na mpanimfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Fa no kɔ, na wobɛhyia abofra bi; fa no na woama no atɔ. Ɛsɛ sɛ ɔse ɔrentɔ, bɔ no, na fa no san kɔma ne maame. " Saa yɔnkoɔ no twaam kɔtoo, na ɛkɔɔ so hyiaa abofra no. Ɔkaa sɛ, Wo mpanimfoɔ reka sɛ wobɛtɔ saa agyinamoa yi, na sɛ woantɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ mebɔ wo na mede wo kɔ wo maame nkyɛn. Ɔba no kaa sɛ, How much? Ɔ yɔnkoɔ no kaa sɛ, An osua ne surus mu duru a ɛyɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. e. £3). Ɔba no tuaa boɔ no, na ɔkɔɔ so ne ne mpanimfoɔ baa akuraase bi. Afei na nkura no di kan bɔ Head-man a ɔwɔ hɔ no anan bere biara a ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛda. Ɛberɛ a abofra no faa agyinamoa no kɔɔ hɔ no, ɛkɔɔ ɔhene no fie hɔ kɔkyeree nkura a ɛwɔ hɔ no. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, Mɛtɔ saa agyinamoa yi afiri onipa a ɛyɛ me dea no hɔ. Ɔba no kaa sɛ, Ɛyɛ me dea. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, Dɛn na ɛyɛ? Ɔba no kaa sɛ, An osua ne surs weight of sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. e. £3). Ɔpanyin no faa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no hyɛɛ ne nsa. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ. Na mpanimfoɔ no rekɔ so, wɔkɔhyiaa awifoɔ bi a na wɔawia ɔhene bi funu. Ɔawifoɔ no kaa sɛ, Ɛsɛ sɛ wotɔ funu yi. Ɛnna mpanimfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Fa no, na kɔ so, na wobɛhyia abofra bi; fa no ma no na ɔtɔ. Sɛ ɔka sɛ ɔrentɔ a, bɔ no, na fa no kɔma no bio ma ne maame. Ɛnneɛ awifoɔ no kɔɔ so ara, na wɔne abofra no hyiaa, wɔkae sɛ, Wɔn mpanimfoɔ no reka sɛ woretɔ funu yi. Asɛm a abofra no kaeɛ ne sɛ, Ehe! nea ɛsɛ sɛ me tɔ funu ma no, mantumi so; nea me ne no bɛyɛ nyinaa, ɛrenyɛ me hwee. Na awifoɔ no kaa sɛ, Wmpanimfoɔ no kaa sɛ, sɛ woantɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛbɔ wo, na yɛfa wo, na yɛsan de wo ma wo maame. Na abofra no kaa sɛ, How much? Na wɔka sɛ, An osua ne surus weight of sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma. Na abofra no tuaa boɔ no, faa funu no, na wɔde too nwura no mu. Afei ɔde nakwantu no sii hɔ, na ɔbaa akuraase bi, na ɔkɔɔ fie baako bi kɔsrɛɛ aduane. Fie no wura kae sɛ, Ebiara nni hɔ. Ɔba no kae sɛ, Agya, me srɛ wo. Ɔba no wura kae sɛ, Ebiara nni hɔ. Ɔba no kae sɛ, Agya, me srɛ wo. Ɔba no kae sɛ, Agya, me srɛ wo. Ɔba no wura kae sɛ, Adɛn nti na abofra yi haw me saa? Yɛn Ɔhene awu, yɛ di mmuada; ɛnnɛ ne da a ɛtɔ so nwɔtwe; nanso hwehwɛ sɛnea yɛbɛtumi biara, yɛnhunu ne funu. Wɔkyeree aberewa bi. ng by near; she said, Give him some. The ɔbaa no maa no aduane ne nam. Bere a abofra no wiee adidi no, ɔkae sɛ, Master of the fie, I have seen the Chiefs corpse; for ɛnora na mereba yi, awifoɔ bi maa me tɔɔ no osua ne suru a ne mu duru yɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma. Ɔbaa no tuu mmirika, yiridi, yiridi, yiridi! (was the nnyigyei of her running) na ɔka kyerɛɛ akuraa mpanin no amaneɛbɔ no. Wɔfaa abofra no, na ɔkɔe kyerɛɛ wɔn nipadua no. Wɔkɔɔ de no bae, na wɔsiee no yiye. Wɔkaa sɛ, Afei na wubedi Ɔhene no akyi. Enti abofra no bɛyɛɛ Ɔhene. Afei, bere a ne nuabarimanom mpanimfo tee ne ho asɛm no, wɔbae, na afei na wɔreka sɛ wɔne no wɔ mogya-abusuabɔ. Abofra no kaa sɛ, You may clear out; I dont know you, so be gone. Na ɔmaa ne nkoa no pam wɔn. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, Sɛ worekɔ baabiara a, na wo nuabarima kumaa aka sɛ ɔbɛka wo ho akɔ, fa no kɔ.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
