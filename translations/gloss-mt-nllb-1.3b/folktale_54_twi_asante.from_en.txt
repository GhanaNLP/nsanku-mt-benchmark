Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ADAMFOFA NKITAHODI BAA ABUSUAKUO MU

ƆBƆMMƆFOƆ ne ne yere tuu kwan bere bi kɔɔ ɔbɔfoɔ bi atraeɛ. Na Osebo a ɔyɛ Daboɔ no kyeree ɔbɔfoɔ no na ɔdii no, nanso ne yere a na ɔyem no deɛ, ɔtenaa hɔ. Ɔbaa no kɔɔ (da bi) aborɔbɛ no ntam, na ɔkɔhyiaa Daboɔ ba no, na ɔbisaa no sɛ, Ɛma, dɛn na wopɛ? Ɔbaa no buaa sɛ, Me ne me kunu na yɛbaa ha, nanso Daboɔ bi na akum no. Daboɔ ba no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Mepɛ wo, enti mma ha bio, efisɛ sɛ woba ha a, agya bɛhyia wo a, ɔbɛkum wo. Ɔbaa no antua aTT biara. ention, na ɔsan kɔɔ pine-apples no ntam, na Daboɔ kyeree no, na ɔfaa no kɔɔ ne fie. Daboɔ ba no hunuu ɔbaa no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne agya sɛ, "Me yareɛ. " Na ɛyɛ ɔbaa a na wahu no dada no nti. Daboɔ no ka kyerɛɛ ne abofra no sɛ, You may be sick, but come, pass on and cut the nam. Ɔtwaa (her) mu, na ɔhunuu sɛ abofra bi da ne yafunu mu, na ɔyii no, na ɔde no sii baabi. Mprempren agya no bisaa no sɛ, Dɛn na wode akɔto hɔ? Na abofra no buae sɛ, Dɛn na mede akɔto hɔ no, sɛ wuhuu no a, anka worenni nam yi. Daboɔ no na ɛyɛɛ aduane no. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne abofra no sɛ ɔmmɛdi, nanso ɔbuaa sɛ, Nam, a me hwɛɛ no ara ma me yaree, ɛsɛ sɛ me dwene sɛ, sɛ me di bi a, me wu. Agya no buae sɛ, Wo nteɛ koraa. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi, berɛ a agya no sii gyina sɛ ɔbɛkɔ nwura no, ɛneɛ na ne abofra no kɔgyee abofra no, de no baa fie, na ɔhohoroo no. Afei ɔkɔfa banana, ɔnoa, oyi aba nsuo no, na ɔma ne ba no nom. Ɔfaa nkyerɛwee a ɛfa berɛ a agya san baeɛ no, ɔfaa abofra no, na ɔde no sieeɛ. Ná ne ba no adi bɛyɛ afe baako berɛ a ɔduruu mmabunbere muabofra a ɔwɔ akɔre ho asɛm mu no nkyɛ na wanyin. Afei abarimaa no bisaa akɔre ba no sɛ, Wo din de dɛn? Ɔbuaa sɛ, Me din ne Kofi-the-beasts-child. Ɔkaa sɛ, Me nso, me din ne Kofi-the-beasts-child. Ɛnna Kofi-the-beasts-child no ka kyerɛɛ Kofi-the-beasts-child no sɛ, Mepɛ sɛ me ne mo di agoru, nanso mesuro agya, na mo nso mosuro no, enti, nna mmiɛnsa a ɛdi hɔ no, mɛka akyerɛ no sɛ ɔnkyerɛkyerɛ me. me sɛnea spring, na bere a ɔakyerɛkyerɛ me, na menim sɛnea wɔyɛ no, me spring na mɛkyere no, na bere a wote sɛ me ne no wɔ, oh! wobɛfa sekkan, na woaba atwitwa ne etire. Ɛneɛ na agya no baeɛ, na Kofi-the-beasts abofra no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Teach me how to spring. Ɔkaa sɛ, To-ɔkyena anɔpa. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔne agya no kɔɔ baabi, faa baabi hohoroo, na agya no huruiɛ, na ne abofra no nso, ɔhweeɛ, na agya no da nakyiri. Ɔkae agya no sɛ, Huruw na tɔ me so. Kofi-the-beasts-child huruw, na ɔhyee ne ho, na Kofi-the-beasts-child no kuraa ne menem. Ɔteɛɛm guu Kofi-the-beasts-child no so sɛ, Me wɔ no, enti bra. Ɔtuu mmirika ntɛm ara, kɔfaa ne sekkan, na ɔtwaa ne etire. Kofi-the-beasts-child ka kyerɛɛ Kofi-the-human-beings-child sɛ, Afei, ampa ara, me ne wo ayɛ sɛ nnipa a wɔyɛ pɛ, enti yɛnkɔsie no. Wɔkɔsie no, na wɔsan baa fie. Kofi-the-beast-child ka kyerɛɛ Kofi-the-beast-child sɛ, I expect that you would like to go to your akuraa. Kofi-the-beast-child said, Yes, but I shall not (be able) to know where it is. Kofi-the-beast-child said, Me ankasa mɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ hɔ, na masane aba. Ɔtuu kwan, na ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu nnipa bi wɔ sumina bi ho, na wɔredi nkɔmmɔ (na wɔreka) sɛ, Yes, ɛyɛ nokware paa sɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no ne ne yere no kɔɔ ɔbɔfoɔ no nsraban mu, na obiara annya wɔn ho hua bio. Ɛhɔ na ɛhɔ na Kofi-the-beast-child no tuu mmirika kɔbɔɔ Kofi-the-human-being-child amanneɛ sɛ, Mehuu wo akuraa, na nnipa a mehuu wɔn no wɔ ntoma mu nti, mɛhwehwɛ bi ama wo na memfa mmra wo. Kofi-the-beast-child no de ne ho sii hɔ na ɔkɔɔ kwan so, na adwadifoɔ retwam. Ɔtuu mmirika guu wɔn so, na adwadifoɔ no de wɔn nnesoa guu fam, dwanee. Kofi-the-beast-child no faa ntoma de kɔmaa Kofi-the-onipa-being-child. Seesei Kofi-the-beast-child no kae sɛ, There remains a gun and gun-medicine (powder). Kofi-the-beast-child no hwehwɛɛ bi na ɔde no bae. Kofi-the-beast-child bisaa Kofi-the-onipa-being-child sɛ, Wobɛda me ase anaa? Kofi-the-beast-child ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Menntumi nkasa mprempren ara, me gyina wanim sɛɛ. Kofi-the-beast-child faa Kofi-the-onipa-being-child no kɔɔ nakuraa sɛ ɔbɛfa no. Bere a wɔduruu kuro no kuro akyi no, Kofi-the-beasts-child ka kyerɛɛ Kofi-the-human-beings-child, Here is your akuraa, na saa baabi a worekɔ yi, da biara mebɛma wo nam pɔ kɛseɛ bi; na anwummere nso bɛba a, mebɛba wo nkyɛn, enti, sɛ nea worekɔ yi, woware a, na ma wo yere nna dan no fa bi, na wo nso nna foforo fa bi. Kofi-the-beasts-child fi-the-being-humans-child duu fie, na ɔkaa abasɛm no sɛnea ɔne ne maame ne ne agya kɔɔ nwura no mu, na sɛnea wɔn nyinaa awuwu, na sɛnea ɔno nso afi hɔ aba bio. Nnipa no suu ne ho. Ɔtoo atuo, yɛɛ ayie, na ɔwiee ne nyinaa. Ɛnneɛ, Kofi-the-beasts-child no, berɛ a ɔsɔre no, ɔde nam baeɛ. Ɛduruu anadwo a ɛdwoɔ no, ɔba ne nkyɛn. Afei na ɛnkyɛ na ɔwaree. Sɛ ɔbaa no kɔ nna a, na ɔyɛ ne nna wɔ baabi foforɔ (than by his side). Ɛduruu anɔpatutuutu no, na ɔde nam pɔ bi nyinaa ba ne yere nkyɛn. Ɛnneɛ, na ne yere no hu sɛ ɔnkɔ nwura, nanso ɛno nitan mu no, na onya nam daa, nanso na ɔmfa nsɛm tiawa biara mma. Ɛda bi nso anadwo, berɛ a na wɔda hɔ (wɔ ne dan mu) no, na ɔbaa no rebɛfa ne nsa aka ne kunu, na ɔkɔka adeɛ a ɛyɛ dɛɛdɛɛ, na ne kunu no nso, ɔda hɔ. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ folk no amaneɛbɔ no sɛ, "Surely my husband's friend is a Daboɔ, and surely he is the one who brings him nam. " Ɛneɛ nnipa no de wɔn atuo no kɔhyɛɛ asaworowa no nkyɛn (baabi a na nam no da), na ɛdwoɔ no, na Daboɔ no rebɛfa nam no aba abɛto asaworowa no so no, wɔde wɔn atuo no bɔɔ no. Kofi-the-beast-child no kae sɛ, "Kofi-the-onipa-being's-child, nnipa dasani nni aseda. " Kofi-the-beast's-child no tee, na ɔde ahopere firii adi, na ɔfaa no, na ɔde no too hɔ, na ɔkɔkaa ne nyinaa kyerɛɛ akuraa no etire, sɛdeɛ ɛbaa sɛ ɔne ne maame ne ne agya kɔeɛ ne amanehunu ahorow a wɔahunu no nyinaa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Saa aboa kɛseɛ yi awu nti, me ne no bɛkɔ, na me mfomsoɔ nyinaa ne sɛ wawu. " Na ɔmaa wɔyɛɛ funnaka mmienu; na wɔde Kofi-aboa kɛseɛ no ba no too baako mu; na ɔno nso (Kofi-onipa-yɛ-ba no ba no) too ne ho tuo, na wɔde no too adaka no foforɔ mu. Ɛnna wɔsiee wɔn. Na wɔse, "Monkɔ nni agorɔ na munnye mo ani wɔ mo adamfofa nkitahodi mu. " Saa na adamfo fadie baa abusuakuo no mu.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
