SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ WƆBƐFRƐƐ SUMAN ANAA HO NSƐM SƐ "SPIDER-STORIES"

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse, bere bi na ɔkɔɔ Nyankopɔn, ɔsoro onyankopɔn, nkyɛn wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ ɔrekɔtɔ ɔsoro onyankopɔn nsɛm. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, So wubetumi atɔ wɔn? Ananse no kae sɛ, Dabi, mɛtumi. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Nkuro akɛseɛ ne tumi te sɛ Kokofu, Bekwai, Asumegya aba, nanso wɔantumi atɔ wɔn, na wo a woyɛ ɔbarima kɛkɛ a ɔnni wura no, (wose) wubetumi? Ananse no kae sɛ, Dɛn ne nsɛm no boɔ? Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Wontumi ntɔ wɔn kwa. gye Abɔdeɛ ɔnini a ɛyɛ Ɔnanka; Osebo a ɔyɛ Daboɔ; Mmoatia a ɔyɛ Mmoatia; ne) Mmɔborɔ a ɔyɛ Nwonwon. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Me de saa nneɛma yi nyinaa bi bɛba, na (nea ɛsen saa) nso, me de me aberewa Nsia (The Sixth-child) bɛka bebree no ho. " Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na fa wɔn mmra. " Ananse no baeɛ, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame no asɛm no nyinaa sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ metɔ Ɔsoro-nyame no nsɛm, na Skye y-onyankopɔn kyerɛ sɛ memfa Ɔnini, Ɔnanka, mmra; Osebo, Daboɔ; Mmoatia, Mmoatia; ne Mmɔborɔ, Nwonwon; na mekae sɛ mebɛka wo ho bebree na makɔma Ɔsoro-onyankopɔn. " Mprempren Ananse bisaa ne yere "Aso" sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ na yɛanya Ɔnini, Ɔnanka? " Na "Aso" ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Kɔ twitwa nnuade baa, na twitwa hama a ɛfow nso bi, na fa mmra. " Na ɔtee saa. e Spider ne wɔn san bae. Na "Aso" kae sɛ, "Momma wɔn nkɔ asuwa no so. " Na Kwaku Ananse de wɔn kɔe; na ɔrekɔ no ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛware sen no, ɛnyɛ ne tenten; wogu, ɛware sen no. " Ananse kae sɛ, "Hɔ na ɔda, ɔda baabi a ɛwɔ hɔ no. " Ɔnanka (a na wɔate nkɔmmɔbɔ a wɔsusuwii yi) kae sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na ɛreka ho asɛm yi nyinaa? " Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ (my wife) "Aso" na ɔne me regye akyinnye sɛ saa nnuadewa no yɛ l onger than you, and I say she is a ɔtorofoɔ. " Na Ɔnini, Ɔnanka no, kae sɛ, "Brɛ no, na bra bɛsusuu me. " Ɔfaa nnuadewa no, na ɔde too ɔnanka no so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Trɛ wo ho. " Na Ɔnanka no nso, ɔtrɛɛ ne ho, na Kwaku Ananse faa hama no, na ɔpirapiraa no (na akyekyereɛ no nnyigyei yɛ n indeed! n indeed! n indeed! bɛsii sɛ ɛbaa etire. Ananse Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Ɔkwasea, me de wo bɛkɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn nkyɛn na magye Ewiem-onyankopɔn anansesɛm (wɔ nsasaeɛ mu). Kwaku Ananse de no kɔɔ Niamey (Ewiem-onyankopɔn) nkyɛn. Ewiem-onyankopɔn kae sɛ, Me nsa aka (i. e. Magye ato mu), ɛda so ara wɔ nea ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ. " Ananse no san bae, na ɔbɛkaa nea asi no kyerɛɛ ne yere sɛ, "Nwonwone da so ara wɔ hɔ. " Ne yere kae sɛ, "Hwehwɛ akutu bi, na fa nsuo hyɛ mu ma, na fa kɔ. " Ananse no nam nwura no mu, berɛ a ɔhunuu Nwonwone dɔm bi sɛn hɔ, na ɔhwiee nsuo no bi guu Nwonwone so. Ananse no hwiee deɛ ɛkae no guu ne ho, na ɔtwaa borɔdeɛ no ahaban no mu baako na ɔde kataa ne etire so. Na afei ɔkasa kyerɛɛ Nwonwon no sɛ, "Sɛnea osuo no aba no, anka ɛsɛ sɛ woba bɛhyɛ yi mu, me kɔnko, na osuo no anto wo; wunhu sɛ mefaa borɔdeɛ ahahan de kataa me ho? " Ɛnna Nwonwon no kaa sɛ, "Yɛda wo ase, Aku, yɛda wo ase. " Nwonwon no nyinaa tuu mmirika kɔyeraeɛ wɔ kɔnko no mu, fom! Agya Ananse kataa ano, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Nkwaseafo, me wɔ mo, na me de mo rekɔ akɔgye Ewiem-onyankopɔn anansesɛm (in nsasaeɛ). " Na ɔde Nwonwade no kɔɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn nkyɛn. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka, nea aka no da so ara wɔ hɔ. " Ananse no san bae pɛnkoro bio, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Osebo, Daboɔ no, da so ara wɔ hɔ. " "Aso" kae sɛ, "Kɔ tu ɛtokuro. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɛno dɔɔso, me nte aseɛ. " Ɛnna Ananse no kɔeɛ sɛ ɔbɛhwehwɛ Daboɔ no akyi, na (wɔnyaeɛ no) ɔtuu amoa a emu dɔ paa, na ɔkataa so, na ɔsan baa fie. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, ɛduruu anɔpa paa, berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ aseɛ rehu no, na Ananse no aka sɛ ɔbɛkɔ, na berɛ a ɔkɔeɛ no, na lO Daboɔ bi da amena mu. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ: "Agya ba ketewa, ɛna ba ketewa, meka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ nnnom nsa, na afei, sɛnea obi bɛhwɛ wo kwan no, woabɛyɛ nsa, na ɛno nti na woakɔtɔ amena no mu yi; anka mɛka sɛ mebɛyi wo, ɔkyena, sɛ wuhu me anaa me mmofra no mu biara a, wobɛkɔ akyere wɔn. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "O! Me antumi anyɛ saa adeɛ. " Kwaku Ananse kɔtwaa nnua mmienu, de baako sii ha, na baako nso sii hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mommfa mo mmoa nanpɔw baako nsi ha, na momfa mo mmoa nanpɔw baako nso nsi ha. " Na Daboɔ no de wɔn sii hɔ (baabi a wɔka kyerɛɛ no). Bere a ɔrebɛforo akɔ soro no, Kwaku Ananse maa ne sekkan so, na hann fɛfɛfɛfɛfɛ mu na ɛbaa ne etire so, gao! (saa na ɛyɛɛ nnyigyei). Daboɔ no gyee amena (na) fOM! (na ɛyɛ nnyigyei a ɛyɛ sɛ ɔhwee ase). Kwaku Ananse nyaa atwerɛ sɛ ɔmmra amena no mu nkɔgye Daboɔ no. Ɔyii Daboɔ no de bae, ɔkae sɛ, "Wo nkwaseasɛm, mefa wo kɔgye ɔsoro-nyame no nsɛm. " Ɔmaa Daboɔ no so kɔmaa Niamey, ɔsoro-nyame no. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka, nea aka no da so ara wɔ hɔ. " Ananse no bae, na ɔsii Akua ba (dwoodwoo tuntum a ɛwɔ anim a ɛyɛ petee), na ɔbɔɔ amamfoɔ a ɛkamfam (a ɛfiri dua so) na ɔde bɔɔdɔɔ no nipadua, na ɔbɔɔ ɛto (bayerɛ ɛtɔ), na ɔde bi hyɛɛ dadaa no nsa mu, na ɔbɔɔ kakra bio de hyɛɛ ayowa mu, na ɔde hama kyekyeree dadaa no asen, na ɔde kɔtoo odum dua bi nan ase, baabi a Mmoatia ba bɛdi agorɔ. Na Mmoatia bi nso baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Akua, metumi adi pɔtɔ yi bi? Kwaku Ananse tui hama a etwa to no, na akuaaba no de ne ti bɔɔ ne etire. Mmoatia no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuammaa no mu baako sɛ, She says I may eat some. Ɔkaa sɛ, Eat some, then. Na ɔdii wieeɛ no, ɔdaa no ase (akuaaba no). Nanso bere a ɔdaa no ase no, wamma ne so. Na Mmoatia no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no sɛ, "Sɛ meda no ase a, ɔmmua. " Mmoatia a ɔdi kan no nuabea no kaa sɛ, "Slap her cry-place. " Na ɔbɔɔ no, pa! Na ne nsa bɛkaa hɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no sɛ, "Me nsa aka hɔ. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa nea aka no na bɔ ne cry-place no bio. " Na ɔfaa no bɔɔ no, pa! na eyi nso, ɛkyeree ntɛm. Na Mmoatia no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no sɛ, Mnsa mmienu no akyekyere ntɛm. Ɔkaa sɛ, Fa wo yafunu pusue. Ɔpiapiaa no, na ne yafunu no bɛkyeree no. Na Kwaku Ananse baeɛ bɛkyekyeree no, na ɔkaa sɛ, Ɔkwasea, me nsa aka wo, me de wo bɛkɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn nkyɛn (wɔ nsasaeɛ mu) ama ne nsɛm. Na ɔne no kɔɔ fie. Na Kwaku Ananse ka kyerɛɛ ne maame, Ya nsia (ba a ɔtɔ so nsia), sɛ, Sɔre, ma yɛnkɔ, ɛfiri sɛ mefa wo ne Mmoatia kɔ ma Ewiem-onyankopɔn (in nsasaeɛ) wɔ ne nsɛm ho. Ɔmaa wɔn so, na ɔfirii hɔ kɔɔ baabi a Ewiem-onyankopɔn wɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, Ewiem-onyankopɔn, ha na Mmoatia bi wɔ, na me dada a mekaa ne ho asɛm no, ha na ɔwɔ. Afei Ewiem-onyankopɔn frɛɛ (ne mpanimfoɔ), Contire ne Akwam mpanyimfoɔ, Adonten (asradom kuw titiriw no ɔkannifoɔ), Gyaase (major-domo); Oyoko, Ankɔbea ne Kyidɔm (ɔkannifoɔ a wɔhwɛ akyi). Na ɔde asɛm no too wɔn anim sɛ, ahemfo akɛseɛ pii aba, na wɔantumi antɔ ɔsoro-nyame no nsɛm, nanso Kwaku Ananse, Ananse, atumi atua boɔ no. Ɔkaa sɛ, Sing his ayeyi. Eee! (wɔteɛɛm). Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, Kwaku Ananse, ɛfiri nnɛ da ne daa, mefa me ɔsoro-nyame no nsɛm na mede ma wo, kosɛ! kosɛ! kosɛ! me nhyira, nhyira, nhyira. Ɛnyɛ sɛ yɛbɛfrɛ no Ɔsoro-nyame no nsɛm bio, na mmom yɛbɛfrɛ no spider-nsɛm.
