Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA AWAREGYAEƐ BAA ABUSUAKUO MU

Ɔbɔfoɔ bi, a na wɔfrɛ no Kwasi Gyaba, ne ne yere kɔtenaa nsra bi mu wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ wɔrekɔhwehwɛ mmoa. Seesei, Kwasi Gyaba kɔ nwura mu no, wamfa nam biara anka ho. Ɔmfa nam biara mma bio da.

Afei, da koro bi ne yere bisaa no asɛm, na ɔkae sɛ, "Me gye di, sɛ wokum nam, wotɔn, na wudidi (ne mfasoɔ) anaasɛ wode akyɛde ahodoɔ ma a, ɛnneɛ da yi, sɛ wokɔ kwaeɛ, na womfa nam nsan mma a, me ne me maame baabi a ɛwɔ hɔ no rekɔ, na me werɛ how sɛ mebɛtena nkura akɛse, ne mpataa, ne nkraman, ne nneɛma a ɛte saa. " Asɛm yi maa ɔbɔfoɔ, Kwasi Gyaba, de nani kyerɛɛ nwura no so. Ɔhunuu ɔtwee bi a ɛretwam. Ɔkaa ne ho sɛ, Ɔtwee, baabi a woda no, ɛhɔ na me ne wo bɛda. Ɔtwee no kɔɔ kɔgyinaa dua bi ase. Ɔ (ɔbɔfoɔ no) maa ne tuo so sɛ, pAM! Ɔnanka bi a ɛfiri papayɛ nko ara na ɛnim ne baabi a ɔfiri no, sii Ɔtwee no so, na Daboɔ bi nso huri kɔɔ so. Ɔbɔɔfoɔ no de ne ho siei yɛɛ komm. Ɔkaa ne ho sɛ, Ay! mehunuu mmusu bɛn. " Ɔnanka no kaa sɛ, "Agya Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ, bra, nea etire bi resusu no, etire foforɔ nso ɛtɔ da bi a wɔate, ɛnneɛ, ɔbɔfoɔ, bra ha, na yɛnkɔ yɛn akuraa, na yɛnkyɛ ɔtwee yi, efisɛ yɛrenyɛ wo opira biara. " Asɛm yi maa ɔbɔfoɔ no faa ne tuo dii wɔn akyi. Wɔkɔkyɛɛ nam no. Ɔnanka no kaa sɛ, "Agya ɔbɔfoɔ, mepɛ wo nti, da ha nnɛ, na mɛma wo aduro; sesei saa aduro yi, sɛ wode bɛhyɛ w'aso a, wobɛte nhaban, nnua, ne asubɔnten, ne mmoa nyinaa nne. Enti anadwo yi kɔ da, nanso home. " Ɔbɔɔfoɔ no kɔdaeɛ no, ɔgyaee ahomegye no. Ɔnanka no de aduro bɛhyɛɛ n'aso. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, na ɔbɔfoɔ no te nneɛma nyinaa kasa ase. Ɔtoaa so, na ɔbaa ne akuraa. Mprempren, berɛ a ɔbaa no, ɔtee sɛ ne maame mmara aba ne babaa fie. Mprempren na ne maame mmara ani afura wɔ ne nifa ani no. Ɔbɔɔfoɔ no kɔɔ dan no mu kɔtenaa aseɛ. Mprempren, ɔkasa ne ne mmara a aka (i. e. Me werɛ firi kae), kae sɛ bere a ohyiaa ne maame mmara no, na ɔrebɔ aburoo. Ɛhɔ na ɔbɔfoɔ no tenaa hɔ kommyɛ, na nkokɔ mmienu bi a wɔwɔ hɔ no (hyɛɛ aseɛ kasae), baako bisaa ɔfoforɔ sɛ, "Woyɛ dɛn na wotumi nya aburoo no, na me kɔ a, me kɔ na ɔpam me? " Ɔfoforɔ nso buae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woanhwɛ sɛ ne nifa ani no mu yɛ kusuu, enti sɛ (wopɛ) sɛ wofa aburoo a, sɛ wotwam asɛe no a, ɔrentumi nhu wo? " Kwasi Gyaba tee (eyi); ɔtoo ne ho hwee nakyi seree sɛ, ɔtwe! ɔtwe! ɔtwe! Ne maame mmara kaa sɛ, "Ah, me sereɛ ba, adɛn nti na woresere me saa? " Nanso Kwasi Gyaba da serew hwe fam, na ɔkɔ so sere. Ɛhɔ na ɛhɔ na ne maame mmara kaa ɔsoro-nyame asɛm a wɔabara no kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Kwasi Gyaba nkyerɛ nea ɛde ba ne sereɛ no wɔ ɔsoro-nyame anim. Ɔsoro-nyame no somaa asɛnkafoɔ sɛ wɔmfa Kwasi Gyaba mmra. Berɛ a ɔduruu kuro no awieeɛ no, ɔmaa ne dwom so sɛ:

"Mprempren mebɛkasa na mewu, Kwasi Gyaba e!

Me rebɛkasa awu, saa asɛm yi si a ɛnsɛ sɛ wusu me. Kwasi Mpuroko Gyaba, ɔrekɔ, Saa asɛm yi si a ɛnsɛ sɛ wusu me.

Ewiem-onyankopɔn mpanimfoɔ kae sɛ, "Mo ntie nneduafo dwom. " Wɔde Kwasi Gyaba kɔɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn anim. Ɔkyeame (kasafoɔ) no sɔre kae sɛ, Kwasi Gyaba, wosere wo maame mmara no ampa, anaa ɔredi atoro? Kwasi Gyaba buae sɛ, Agya, aduru bi nti, a mehyɛ so nkɛntɛnsoɔ no, sɛ meyi me sereɛ sianteɛ adi a, mewu. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Woyɛ me ɔbɔfoɔ nti, merennyae wo sɛ wuwu. Ne maame mmara kae sɛ, Agya, ɔredi atoro, na ɔresere ara kwa. Ing at me, so I will be content with nothing but a nkyerɛkyerɛmu. The kasafoɔ said, Kwasi Gyaba, have you heard? Kwasi Gyaba answered, Then I beg for a ɛda, and when I am dead, lay me in it. Just as soon as his ano had stopped still, a Akokɔnini and a Hɛn came by; and the Akokɔnini said to the Hɛn, Stand still and let me mount you. Hɛn said, What, in these nnipa front? Akokɔnini said, Sɛ woannyina hɔ pintinn a, mebɛka asɛm a wɔabara kɔsi nsunsuansoɔ sɛ wutumi yi wo ho. Afei na Kwasi Gyaba te saa. Ntɛm ara na ɔkaa sɛ, Sire, Ewiem-onyankopɔn, gyae ma wɔn ntu ɛda, efisɛ mewɔ asɛm bi a mɛka. Ewiem-onyankopɔn kaa sɛ, Ka. Kwasi Gyaba kaa sɛ, Meka asɛm a wɔabara sɛ, me maame mmara mma no kwan ma ɔmfa ne abofra nkɔ. Mpanimfoɔ no nyinaa kaa sɛ, Ɛno teɛ, ɛno teɛ, asɛm no asa. Ɛhɔ ara na Ewiem-onyankopɔn hyɛɛ mmara maa nsunsuansoɔ no sɛ, obiara, mpo wo adamfo, sɛ ɔhaw wo a, s peak asɛm a wɔabara no so, anaa sɛ ɛyɛ wo yere na ɛyɛ a, ka asɛm a wɔabara no na awaregyaeɛ no. Ɛneɛ na ɛbaa sɛ awaregyaeɛ no baa abusuakuo no mu. Ɛyɛ Akokɔnini a yɛatwa no bi.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
