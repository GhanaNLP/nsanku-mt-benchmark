SƐNEA AKƆKONINI, AKOKƆNINI NO, NYAA ANO A ANO YƐ NNAM

Na Ananse AGYA ANANSE ne ne mmofra Afudotwedotwe (a ne ti yɛ kɛse), Niwankonfwea (a ne ti yɛ tratraa) (ne) Tikononkono (a ne ti yɛ kɛse) te hɔ. Na Ananse no ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, Ɛsɛ sɛ monkɔ hwehwɛ borɔdɔma bi ma me nni. Enti saa mmofra yi fii hɔ; wɔkɔɔ so, na wɔkɔɔ so, na wɔbaa dua bi so, na borɔdɔma no abere, na mmofra no kɔɔ aseɛ (the dua). Afudotwedotwe (Yafunu-like-to-paeeɛ) ka kyerɛɛ Nyiwankɔnfwea (Thin-shanks) sɛ, "Foro soro na yi bi. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, sɛ meforo so a, me anan bɛbubu. " Tikononkono (Big-headed) kaa sɛ, "Wo, Afudotwedotwe, foro so. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, sɛ meforo so a, me yafunu bɛpaeeɛ; foro wo ho. " Enti Tikononkono (Big-headed) foro so. Ɔtwee mmofra no bi fii fig-tree no mu na ɔkɔɔ so yii na yii, afei ɔkae sɛ, "Will that do? " Wɔn ahomegyeɛ ne sɛ, "Ah, (how do we know?). " Enti ɔkɔɔ so twee wɔn; afei ɔsan bisaa wɔn bio sɛ, "Will that do? " Na wɔbuae sɛ, "Lower your etire and look. " Na bere a ɔrebɛtew ne etire no so ahwɛ no, na ne etire no abɔ fam, na ɔreka (asɛmfua a ɛte sɛ) "Kum! " Bere a Belly-lik anka e-to-paeeɛ asere, na ne yafunu paeeɛ ɔtopae, ta! Tin-shanks, berɛ a ɔbɛdwane akɔka asɛm akyerɛ ne agya no, nanan bubui na ɛkɔɔ so traa ɛban no akyi. Akokɔnini no rehoro ne ɔkra, na ɔrekɔto egg-shells (afɔdeɛ no) agu, berɛ a ɔhunuu sɛ Tin-shanks abɔ ne fam na nanan abu, na wɔagyaw no wɔ ɛban no nkyɛn. Ɛduruu berɛ a ɔrebere asere no, nano kyerɛɛ sɛ:

"Bere a ɔrebɛsere no, nano bɛyɛɛ nea ɛyɛ nnam.

Bere a ɔrebɛserew no, nano bɛyɛɛ nea ɛyɛ nnam. "

Ɛno nti na Mpanimfoɔ ka sɛ, "Sɛ obi hwe ase a, kɔ hwɛ no, na woansere. " Mprempren Akokɔnini no deɛ, ɔyɛɛ no sɛɛ, na ɔsereeɛ nti, nano bɛyɛɛ nea ɛyɛ nnam; kan no na ɔwɔ ano a ɛyɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
