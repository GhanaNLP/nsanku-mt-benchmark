MPITRIPI NE MPATRAPA

MPITRIPI ne Mpatrapa ne Anyankama-Kyerebi ne Kyerebi-Koso-Adawurampon kɔtuii mmɛw bobesa aduasa, na wɔkae sɛ, "Mommma obiara nka ahurututu a ɛwɔ mmɛw bobesa ne fɛfɛeɛ (ne nsateaa ahodoɔ) mu. "

Afei Agya Ananse kɔka kyerɛɛ ahurututu a ɛwɔ nnuadewa bobe no mu na ɔlese (ne nsateaa ahodoɔ) na nnipa (lit. wɔkaa sɛ, "Mofa ɛka no. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ ɛka bɛn na matumi atua? " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Woayɛ ɛka ɛfiri sɛ, Mpitripi ne Mpatrapa ne Anyankama-Kyerebi ne Kyerebi-Koso-Adawurampon akɔtu nnuadewa aduasa, na wɔaka sɛ obiara nnka nnuadewa ne fɛfɛ nsateaa ahodoɔ no ahurututu, na woaka sɛ, nti na yɛka sɛ, 'gye ɛka no' (i.e. ɛsɛ sɛ wotua asotwe no); na wɔbɔɔ Ananse no ahyɛdeɛ sɛ ɔngye (akatua no), enti ɔhwee aseɛ kuraa silin no mu, (a na ɔwɔ dede a ɛte sɛ) Kado!

Ɛno nti na wɔka sɛ daa, obi bɛhu Kwaku Ananse a wɔde plaster abɔ ne silin, ɛwom sɛ tete no na ɔnam fam te sɛ Ɔkɔtɔ, Kɔtɔ no.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
