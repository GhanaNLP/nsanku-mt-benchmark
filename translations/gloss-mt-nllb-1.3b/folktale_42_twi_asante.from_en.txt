Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ OSEBO, DABOƆ NO, NE ADOWA, ADOWA NO, BƐYƐƐ NNAMFOFOƆ

ƆKUROTWIAMANSA (He-who-holds-the-ɔman-in-ehu) na ɔte hɔ, na ɔkaa sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ obi a ɔmpɛ no (na ɔnsuro sɛ ɔbɛka akyerɛ no anim). Ɛwɔ mmoa no nyinaa mu no, obiako mpo antumi ankɔ. Afei, na adowa ketewa no te hɔ; ɔkae sɛ, "Agya Daboɔ, dɛn na woka fa me ne wo bɛyɛ (open and confessed) atamfo? " Osebo, Daboɔ no, kae sɛ, "Ɛno ara ne nea mepɛ. " Enti wɔbɛyɛɛ (open and confessed) atamfo. Daboɔ no tui mmɛw bobesa. Adowa no so, ɔnom mmɛw bobesa, ɔnom mmɛw bobesa, wantumi ansɔre, ɔdaa nsamu dua no ase. Wɔ anwummere nwunu mu no, Daboɔ no kaa sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwɛ ne agyapadeɛ. Bere a ɔkɔɔ no, na Adowa no anom ne nsa na ɔda hɔ nnom nsa. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Afei, wo na woka sɛ me ne wo bɛyɛ atamfo a wɔda adi pefee; adɛn nti, Esono mpo, Ɛsono no, antumi anyɛ me ɔtamfo a ɔda adi pefee, akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho sɛ worehyia ɔhaw. " Daboɔ no kɔtwaa nnuadewa ahaban, na ɔkyekyeree nnuadewa ahaban kɛntɛn, na ɔde Adowa no too mu; na ɔfaa no, na ɔsoaa no wɔ ne etire so. Bere a ɔreba na ɔduruu bɛyɛ kwan mfinimfini no, Adowa no dwoeɛ. Ɔhunuu sɛ Daboɔ na ɛresoa no. Adowa no de nani kyerɛɛ soro, na ɔhunuu homa bi a ɛsɛn hɔ. Adowa no yii ne nsɔmu. Afei ɔkasa ne ne nneɛma a yɛagyae mu (i. e. mepaa asɛm bi) no, na Adowa no mmoa bin bi wɔ kɛntɛn a ɛwɔ ahaban mu. Daboɔ no anhu hwee a ɛrekɔ so; na ɔsoaa no (sɛnea ɔdwen) fie. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Saa nkwaseafoɔ panyin no, Adowa no, ɔkae sɛ ɔne me bɛyɛ atamfo a wɔagye atom, enti mede no aba yi; fa nnuadewa nsie ogya no so. " Daboɔ maame no ka kyerɛɛ ne abofra no sɛ, " (Palm-nut) soup no ayɛ krado, enti fa no mmra na memma no na wakum no. " Berɛ a Daboɔ no hwɛɛ kɛntɛn no mu no, na ɛhɔ nni hwee. Na ɔkɔ, na nea aka no yɛ ne mmoa bin no. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Maame, ɔkɔeɛ, na mmoa bin no da so ara wɔ hɔ; momma yɛnfa wɔn nkɔto nkwan no mu. " Enti wɔde wɔn kɔtoo nkwan no mu, na wɔwiee aduannoa aduane no nyinaa; na wɔtenaa ase maa no. Ɔbaawa ketewa no de ne nsateaa hyɛɛ nkwan no mu, na ɔyam no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, Kwadwo (Daboɔ no awoda yɛ Ɛdwoada), na eyi yɛ ne mmoa bin nko ara, dɛn na anka ne honam renyɛ dɛ; anka ɛbɛyɛ dɛ sen tiger-nuts, enti baabiara a ɔbɛhome no, bɔ mmɔden fa no ba. " Na Daboɔ no de nani kyerɛɛ ne mmuaeɛ dua no so. Na ɔrekɔ no, na Adowa no da hɔ nso, na wabow nsa bio. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Oh, first time if I made a kwasea of myself, now I shall go fie to go and bring an adaka and san ba. " Daboɔ no firii fie kɔfaa ne ntaadeɛ nyinaa, de siee hɔ, na ɔde adaka ne safeɛ no kɔɔ nnuadewa no so. Ɔfaa Adowa no, ɔde siee adaka no mu, too mu, faa no, na ɔde kɔɔ ne etire so. Bere a ɔduruu fie no, ɔkae sɛ, "Maame, mede no aba; bue dampon (closed sleeping-room) pon no na memfa no nkɔto mu. " Aberewa ketewa no buee no, na ɔde no kɔtoo mu. Ɛnna Daboɔ no ka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Me rekɔ Tall-grass-country no mu akɔhwehwɛ gyeene, ne nkyene, ne akakaduro yamye, na memfa mmra, na yɛbɛfa Adowa no ayɛ no stew a yɛbɛdi. " Daboɔ no kɔɔ nakwantu no ase. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, na akwakoraa ketewa no rebɔ nnuadewa, na berɛ a Adowa de naniwa kyerɛɛ ɛtokuro (wɔ ɔpono mu) no na ɔhunuu aberewa no. Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, "Old-woman-grandmother, you are to be pitied; if you would open for me, I would do the nteɛm for you. " Ɔbaawa aberewa no kaa sɛ, "You, Ɔtwee, you will run away. " Ɔtwee no buae sɛ, "May (the lesser-gods) Aserampon, ne Ta Kese, ne Dwomo, ne Apeya, ne Konkroma, ne Ta Kora, ne Krakye Dente, kill me, were I to run away when you open to me. Sɛ wopɛ mpo a, to apono no nyinaa mu. " Ɛnna aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Me wate. " Ɔtoo apono no mu, na ɔbuee (ɔpono a ɛwɔ dan a Ɔtwee no wɔ mu no). Ɔtwee no baeɛ, ɔkuraa pestle no, na ɔbɔɔ nsamu nnua no. Na abeawa ketewa no rebɛkotow akɔfa biribi afiri fam, berɛ a Ɔtwee no faa pestle no bɔɔ netire akyi pe! na ɔwuiɛ. Ɔtwee yii ne wedeɛ no, de akata ne ho, na watwitwa ne honam de too ogya no so. Ɛnkyɛree biara na Kwadwo baeɛ. Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woretwɛn saa? M'abue ɔpono maa Ɔtwee no na meku no, hwɛ ne honam a ɛda ogya no so. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Woayɛ no yie, Apeafo. " Ɔno nso faa ne nneɛma a ɔde aba no, na ɔde guu soup-stew no mu. Na wɔawie aduannoa no; bere a wɔkɔtenaa ase sɛ wɔbɛdi no, Daboɔ no faa dompe. Bere a ɔbɔɔ no, na ɛyɛ pu! pu! Ɔtwee no bɔɔ ɛhwene sɛ, "Ɛno ne wo maame bone a woretwitwa no. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na woka? " Ɔtwee no buae sɛ, "Me (nko) kae sɛ Ɔtwee yi yɛ dadaa, ne nnompe no yɛ den. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Saa na ɛteɛ, na ɛyɛ den sɛ me mpo bɛtumi abubu. " Seesei Daboɔ no de stew no akɔto nano, na ɛyɛ dede a ɛte sɛ fEAO! fEAO! Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, "Ah, ɛno yɛ soup-stew a wɔde wo maame ti ayɛ a woretwitwa gu wano. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dɛn na woka? " Ɔtwee no buae sɛ, "Me na mekae sɛ, ne mmoa bin no mpo (na yɛdwen sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ), ne honam no deɛ, woahunu ne dɛ anaa? " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "It is quite, quite, quite unnecessary to say so. " Seesei deɛ, wɔawie aduane no. . Ɔtwee hɔ faa fie hɔ, ɔka kyerɛɛ Daboɔ no sɛ, "Me rekɔto mpopaho no gu mfinimfini-popaho no so. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ, na me ne wo nni hwee yɛ, meyɛ ɔbaa anaa? " Ɔtwee no bɛduruu mfinimfini-popaho no so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ho wɔ hɔ, Daboɔ! Ho wɔ hɔ, Daboɔ! anka sɛ wonyɛ kwasea, sɛ wonyɛ aboa kɛseɛ a, anka bekum wo maame na mayɛ no soup-stew ma wodi? Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Mehunu wo (da so ara gyina hɔ, sɛ meba wo nkyɛn a)? " Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, "Woatwam mpo me so. " Daboɔ no tuu mmirika, yiridi! yiridi! (na ɛyɛ ne anan nnyigyei), na Ɔtwee no nso hyɛɛ aseɛ tuu mmirika, prada! prada! prada! Ɔtwee no kɔduruu ne fie, na ɛyeraeɛ wɔ ne dan mu. Bere a ɔsii fie no, na onnyaa kwan biara wɔ mu, ɛne ade a ɛda nkyɛn wɔ kwan baako a ɔsii no ho. Bere a Ɔtwee no baa mu no, ɔtoo ne kwan mu, bam! Seesei, hwɛ sɛnea Daboɔ no bɛhwɛ no, na ɔnhunu kwan biara a Ɔtwee no faa so kɔɔ mu. Daboɔ no kɔɔ so frɛɛ mmoa no nyinaa sɛ, "Mommra, na yɛnkɔ nkyere Ɔtwee no mma me, efisɛ ɔyɛɛ me kwasea saa ara a, merentumi mpo nka amaneɛbɔ no nyinaa nkyerɛ mo kosi sɛ mobɛkyere no ama me. " Mmoa no nyinaa baa hɔ koduruu Ɔtwee fie. Ɔtwee no tee sɛ mmoa no aba ne fie hɔ bɛkyere no, ɔfaa mako kɔkɔɔ, nkyene, ne gyeene, na ɔyam no, na ɔde sii hɔ. Mprempren na mmoa no nyinaa gyina hɔ kyɛn. Wei deɛ ɔhwɛɛ nea ɛwɔ anim. Ɛnna Okusie, Okisi no, kae sɛ, Ɔkwakuo bi wɔ hɔ, Kwaadu no, na ɔwɔ dua tenten, enti ma ɔnforo no kan, na yɛmfa ne dua no nkɔ fie hɔ. Ɔkwakuo, Kwaadu no, de ne ho sii hɔ, ɔkra! Ɔkra! Ɔkra! na ɔkɔɔ soro. Ɔtenaa fie atifi, nanso ɛnkyɛ na ɔhwee ne aniwa hwehwɛɛ Ɔtwee no, na Ɔtwee no faa ɛmo a ɔapete no, de hyɛɛ nano, na ɔhwee guu Kwaadu aniwa mu. Ɛnna Kwaadu no gyaee no hwee ase. Na mmoa no baeɛ na wɔhunuu sɛ biribi wɔ naniwa mu. Baako faa ne bi, na ɔleseɛ ne nsateaa ahodoɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Oh, nnamfoɔ, monkɔhwɛ nea Ɔkwakuo akɔ de aba. Ne mmoa nyinaa baeɛ. Wɔbɛgyee, na wɔbɛgyee, na wɔbɛgyee. Ɛno nti na Ɔkwakuo, Kwaadu no, ani da hɔ kɛseɛ saa. Mprempren na wɔaka sɛ, Kokotee (Bush-pig), wo deɛ, woyɛ digging daa, nuntun! nuntun! na woatumi akɔ fie hɔ. Ɔtwee no tee saa no, ɔkɔtui ne Sekkan no, hyɛɛ ano a ɛyɛ nnam paa. Bush-pig no kɔtoo na kɔtoo, na ne aboa hwene no puee fie hɔ. Ɛnna Ɔtwee no faa ne Sekkan no, de too Bush-pig no ano, na ɛretwitwa. Bush-pig no kaa sɛ, Oh, Bush-pig Asamoa, ɔretwa me ano. Wɔn mmoa a na wɔgyina nakyi (na wɔantumi antie nea ɔreka no pefee) no kaa sɛ, Well done, Apeafo; press your aniwa, na yi no adi. Kokotee, the Bush-pig, withdrew his etire; na ɔne ne ano ayɛ bloody. Bere a mmoa no hunuu sɛ wɔatwa Kokotee Asamoa, Bush-pig no, ano no, wɔfirii baabi hɔ baeɛ, na yii! (yɛɛ wɔn nnipadɔm guanguan no nnyigyei). Saa na Bush-pig no nyaa hwene kumpɔ. Mprempren, bere a mmoadoma no adwane akɔ, na Osebo, Daboɔ no, anhu nea ɔbɛtumi ayɛ. Ɔbɔɔ dawuro sɛ, Anyhow, I will go nowhere else; here I remain forever, until I die, except I kill Adowa the Ɔtwee. Bere a Adowa tee saa asɛm no, afei ɔfaa Daboɔ maame wedeɛ no de kyekyeree ne nipadua, na ɔforoo fie hɔ sian kɔɔ akyiri. Afei ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔtoo Daboɔ no. Ɔkaa sɛ, Oh, Kwadwo, dɛn na ama woayɛ mmerɛw saa; adɛn nti na worehunahuna wo ho wɔ Ɔtwee ho? Yɛnkɔ fie na yɛnsi yɛn baabi wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu, na yɛnsan mmra. Wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ wɔrekɔ fie. Ɔtwee no ka kyerɛɛ Daboɔ no sɛ, Yi wo agyapadeɛ nyinaa na fa ma me, na mɛkora so ama wo. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, Thats not a adwene bɔne. Ɛneɛ ɔyii ne nneɛma nyinaa na ɔde maa Ɔtwee no. Ɛnkyɛree na Ɔtwee no de ne nsa guu wɔn so, na ɔde siee (safely) no, ɔkɔgyinaa baabi. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ho wɔ hɔ, Daboɔ! Ɔbuaa sɛ, Yiw, onua, yiw! Ɔkaa sɛ, Sɛ na wonyɛ kwasea, sɛ na wonyɛ aboa kɛseɛ, anka metumi akum wo maame na mayɛ no soup-stew ama wo adi? Afei bio na matwa wo kwan na mefa wo agyapadeɛ nyinaa. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, So me ne wo bɛhyia wɔ hɔ (sɛ meba)? Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, You will even pass me. Ɛneɛ Daboɔ no huri kɔɔ ne ho, kiri! kiri! kiri! Nanso Ɔtwee no nso bɔɔ ne ho kwan. Ɔkɔɔ ne fie kɔtoo ɔpono no mu, bam! Afei Daboɔ no gyinaa hɔ a ne kokoɔ reyɛ akɔkɔtɔ, na ne dua no dwiri firii nkyɛn kɔ nkyɛn (yɛɛ sɛ) frɛ! frɛ! frɛ! Wannya asɛmfua anka. Ɛduruu tenten so no, ɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔtwee no sɛ, Meka Yawoada din akyiwadeɛ sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ woba, na yɛbɛyɛ nnamfofoɔ. Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, Merenba, dodoɔ a wopɛ biara, gye sɛ woahyehyɛ sɛ yɛnnom anyame no. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, Mepene so, ɛnneɛ merentumi nyɛ wo hwee da. Ɔtwee no ne Daboɔ no baa anyame no bɛnom nsuo. Ɔtwee no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Nkyere me da; sɛ wuhyia me a, na sɛ wɔkyere me wɔ fidie mu a, gyae me. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Metee. " Na Daboɔ no nso kasae sɛ, "Me nsa bɛka kwan a wobɛfa so no mpo, na matwe so, na ama woahu sɛ me ne wo ntam nni nea ɛde ba manso. " Ɛno nti, bere a Daboɔ biara bɛn no, ɔnkyere. Ɔtwee no. Afei nso, sɛ fidie akyere Ɔtwee a, ɔno (Daboɔ no) kɔ gya no.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
