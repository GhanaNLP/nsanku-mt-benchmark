Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA KWAKU ANANSE (ANANSE) NYAA "ASO" WƆ AWAREƐ MU

NA obarima bi te hɔ a wɔfrɛ no Akwasi-the-jealous-one na ne yere ne "Aso", na ɔmpɛ sɛ obiara hu "Aso" anaasɛ obiara ne no kasa. Enti ɔkɔsii akuraa ketewa bi maa "Aso" sɛ ɔntena mu.

Obiara ankɔ akuraa hɔ da. Mprempren na ɔno, Akwasi-the-jealous-one, antumi anwo mmofra. Ɛno nti, sɛ ɔne ne yere tena kuro mu a, obi bɛfa no akɔ. Afei, ɔsoro-nyame no ka kyerɛɛ mmeranteɛ no sɛ, Akwasi-the-jealous-one aware "Aso" berɛ tenten paa, ɔnyɛɛ abofra wɔ ne ho, enti nea ɔbɛtumi no, ɔnkɔfa "Aso", na sɛ ɔnyɛɛ abofra wɔ ne ho a, ɔmfa no nyɛ ne yere. Na mmeranteɛ no nyinaa bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ wɔbɛto ne nsa ama no, nanso obiara antumi. Afei na Kwaku Ananse wɔ hɔ a ɔrehwɛ saa dwumadi yi, na ɔkae sɛ, Metumi akɔ Akwasi-the-jealous-ones village. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, So wutumi yɛ saa ankasa? Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Sɛ wobɛma me nea me hia no. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Ɛyɛ adeɛ bɛn? Ɔkaa sɛ, Aduro a wɔde tuo ne otuo ano aba. Na Ɔsoro-nyame no maa no. Na Kwaku Ananse de powder ne otuo ano aba no kɔɔ nkuraase nkumaa, na ɔkae sɛ, Ɔsoro-nyame ahyɛ me sɛ memfa powder ne otuo ano aba mma wo, na wo kɔkum nam, na da a mɛsan aba ha no, mebɛfa akɔ. Ɔkyekyɛɛ powder ne otuo ano aba no wɔ nkuraase nkumaa pii ntam, kɔsii sɛ ne nyinaa wieeɛ. Na nkuraasefoɔ no nyinaa nya nam bi ma no. Wɔ da bi, Kwaku Ananse nwene kɛntɛn a nhaban ayɛ. Sɛnea ɛte no, na ne tenten fi ha kɔ baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ. Kwaku Ananse de kɔɔ nkuraase nkumaa, faako a ɔkyekyɛɛ powder ne otuo ano aba, na ɔgyee nam a wɔakum no nyinaa. Agya Ananse faa nam ne kɛntɛn a nhaban ayɛ no hyɛɛ netire so, na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ kwan a ɛkɔ Akwasi-The-Jalous-One atraeɛ no so. Bere a oduu asuwa a Akwasi ne ne yere nom fi mu no, ɔpaw nam bi na ɔde guu mu. Kwaku Ananse bɔɔ mmɔden de kɛntɛn a nhaban wom (a nam ahyɛ mu ma) bae na ɔde faa ponkɛse ano a ɛkɔ Kwasi-the-Jealous compound no mu. Na "Aso" hu no. Ɔse, "Akwasi E! bra na hwɛ adeɛ bi a ɛreba fie ha, ɛbɛyɛ dɛn? " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ ɔsoro-nyame na ɔsoma me, na me werɛ ahow, na meba ha sɛ mebɛda. " Akwasi ninkunu no kaa sɛ, "Me te me wura akoa. " "Aso" ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, "Agya ɔbarima, wo nam bi atɔ fam wɔ fie mu ponkɛse ano. " Ananse kae sɛ, "Oh, sɛ wowɔ kraman a ma ɔnkɔfa nni. " Ɛnna "Aso" kɔgyee no de maa ne kunu. Ɛnna Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Maame, hyehyɛ aduane bi (on the ogya) ma me. " "Aso" de bi sii hɔ, na Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Maame, ɛyɛ fufuo na woreyɛ anaa eto? " "Aso" buae sɛ, "Fufuo. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ ɛyɛ kakra dodo; kɔ na fa kukukɛse bi. " "Aso" kɔfaa kɛse bi, na Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Bra na fa nam. " Na mmoa akɛseɛ aduanan na ɛwɔ akyire hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, "Fa eyinom nko ara na fa hyɛ mu, na sɛ wowɔ kuku a, anka mɛma wo nam a wobɛwe a ɛbɛma wo se ahwe ase. " "Aso" wiee aduane no, ɔdan fii mu (kuku no mu), de siee ɛpono, nsu a apete so, na ɔde siee nkyɛn (aduane no ahomegyeɛ). Afei "Aso" faa ne kyɛfa no kɔtoo ogya no nkyɛn, na mmarima no kɔtenaa ɛpono no nkyɛn. Wɔde wɔn nsa akyi kaa wɔn ho (kyerɛ sɛ, wodi aduane koro). Bere a wɔredidi no nyinaa, na Kwaku Ananse reka sɛ, "nkyene nni fufuo yi mu. " Akwasi ka kyerɛɛ "Aso" sɛ, "Mfa bi mmra. " Nanso Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Dabi koraa, ɔbaa no bere a ɔredidi no, woka kyerɛ no sɛ ɔnsɔre mfa nkyene mmra, wo ankasa kɔ na fa. " Akwasi sɔre, na Kwaku Ananse de nani kyerɛɛ ne kotokuo mu, na ɔyii ayamtuo duro nsiho bi de hyɛɛ fufuo no mu. Ɛnna ɔfrɛɛ Akwasi sɛ ɔmmra, na ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Bra, na anka mede bi abɛka me ho. " Akwasi baeɛ no, Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Oh, merenni bio, mani abere. " Akwasi a na ɔnni adwene biara no, ɔkɔɔ so dii. Bere a wɔwiee adidi no, Akwasi kae sɛ, "Damfo, yɛn ne wo te ha a yennim wo din. " Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "Me din ne Kɔ soro-up-and-make-love-to-"Aso". " Akwasi" kae sɛ, "Me na mate, na wo "Aso", woate ɔbarima yi din anaa? " Aso" buae sɛ, "Aane, mate. " Akwasi sɔre sɛ ɔbɛkɔ asiesie nnaadan a yɛmfa nyɛ hwee no mu baako, na wama wɔn nyinaa ho atɔ wɔn. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sɔre-na-yɛ-dɔ-to-"Aso", eyi ne wo dan, kɔ na kɔda hɔ. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Me ne ɔkra-hohorofoɔ ma Ewiem-onyankopɔn, na meda abrannaa dan a ɛbue mu; ɛfiri berɛ a maame woo me na agya woo me, menna pia a wɔato mu da. " Akwasi kaa sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ, ɛhe na wobɛda? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Anka sɛ meda abrannaa dan a ɛbue yi mu ha a, anka ɛbɛyɛ sɛ me ne wo bɛkari pɛ. kɔ ɔsoro-nyame nkyɛn, efisɛ ɛbɛkyerɛ sɛ na meda ɔsoro-nyame no dan a ɛwɔ abɔnten veranda dan mu, ɛfiri sɛ me ne obiara nna da wɔ dan a ɛbue (Gyae ɔsoro-nyame) mu, na ɛfiri sɛ saa na ɛteɛ no, mebɛda kɛkɛ wɔ mpa a wɔatoto mu yi anim na wogye home. " Ɛhɔ na ɔbarima no yii mpa bi sii hɔ maa no. Akwasi ne ne yere no kɔɔ hɔ kɔhomee, na Kwaku Ananse nso kɔtoo hɔ. Kwaku Ananse daeɛ na ɔhwee aseɛ wɔ nsanombea a ɛwɔ pia ano pono no mu. Kwaku Ananse daeɛ, na ɔfaa ne bentaa (na ɔtoo dwom):

"Akuamoa Ananse, to-da yɛbɛdu biribi ho to-da.

Kwaku Ananse, Nsia abofra, Niamey maame, Ewiem-onyankopɔn, Ɛn-da a yɛbɛnya biribi, ɛn-da.

Ananse, Soul-washer to the Niamey, Ewiem-onyankopɔn, Ɛnɛ da mebɛhu adeɛ.

Afei, ogyaee sɛpɛrɛwa no bɔ, na ɔde too nkyɛn, na ɔdaeɛ. Ɔdaeɛ berɛ bi, berɛ a ɔtee sɛ Akwasi-jealous-one refrɛ, Agya mannot a nnyigyei wɔ mmuaeɛ mu (except the nnomaa su of the babadua), dinn! Agya mannot a nnyigyei wɔ mmuaeɛ mu (except the nnomaa su of the babadua), dinn! Akwasi-ninkunu-biako no rewu; aduro no afa ne so nsunsuansoɔ, nanso ɔfrɛ sɛ, Agya mannot a nnyigyei wɔ mmuaeɛ (except the nnomaa su of the babadua), dinn! Awiei koraa no ɔkae sɛ, Rise-up-and-make-love-to-"Aso". Ananse no kaa sɛ, M! M! M! Ɔkaa sɛ, Akwasi, bue ma me. Kwaku Ananse buee ɔpono no, na Akwasi kɔɔ baabi. Na Ananse no sɔre kɔɔ dan a ɛwɔ hɔ no mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Aso", woante nea wo kunu no kaeɛ? Ɔbuaeɛ sɛ, Dɛn na ɔkaeɛ? Ɔ (Kwaku Ananse) buaeɛ sɛ, Ɔkaeɛ sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ ɔsɔre na medɔ wo. Aso" kaa sɛ, Wo nni atoro. Na ɔyɛɛ no maa no, na ɔkɔɔ daeɛ. Anadwo no, Akwasi sɔree mpɛn nkron; Ananse no nso kɔɔ mpɛn nkron kɔsii nea "Aso" wɔ. Bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, Kwaku Ananse hyɛɛ aseɛ. Ɛbɛyɛ asram abien akyi no na Aso yafunu yɛɛ kɛse. Akwasi bisaa no sɛ, Dɛn nti na wyafunu ayɛ sɛɛ, ebia wo yare, ma (wunim) sɛ me a me ne wo te ha no, mentumi nwo mmofra? "Aso" buae sɛ, Wo werɛ fi sɛ saa ɔbarima a ɔbaa ha na woka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔnsɔre na ɔnni ɔdɔ mma "Aso" no, ɛneɛ, ɔfaa me na me ne no nyinsɛnee. Akwasi-ninkunu-baako kae sɛ, Sɔre na memfa wo nkɔma no. na ɛkɔɔ Ewiem-Onyankopɔn kuro no mu. Wɔ kwan so no, "Aso" bɔɔ awoɔ. Wɔduruu Ewiem-Onyankopɔn kuro no mu, na wɔkɔkaa nea asi kyerɛɛ Ewiem-onyankopɔn sɛ, Wo asɛmti bi a wosomaa no, ɔdaa me fie, na ɔfaa "Aso", na ɔnyinsɛnee ne so. Ewiem-onyankopɔn kaa sɛ, Madesuadeɛ nyinaa akata asese so, kɔ kyerɛ nea wopɛ. Wɔkɔeɛ no, na Ananse no te ridge-pole bi so. "Aso" kae sɛ, "Ɔwɔ hɔ. " Na Kwaku Ananse tuu mmirika kɔtenaa mfinimfini hɔ. Na "Aso" san kae sɛ, "Ɔwɔ hɔ. " Ɛnna Kwaku Ananse firii soro (baabi a na ɔte) bɛhwee fam. Afei saa da no na ɛyɛ Efiada. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Me a mehoro ɔsoro-nyame no kra, woagye wo nsa akyerɛ me, ma mehwe ase na me nya dɔteɛ kɔkɔɔ. Ntɛm ara na asomfoɔ no kyeree Akwasi-the-jealous no nsɔmu na wɔbɔɔ no odwan. Bere a Akwasi-the-jealous-one wiee odwan no bɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔsoro-nyame no sɛ, Hwɛ ɔbaa no, ma Kwaku Ananse nnye no. Enti Kwaku Ananse gyee "Aso", nanso ne ba no deɛ, wɔkum no, wɔtwaa no nsinnsin, na wɔpetepete wɔn ho. Saa na ahoɔyaw no baa abusuakuo no mu.

Ɛyi, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (ana) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi afa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a wode bɛkamfo me (asɛmka no.
