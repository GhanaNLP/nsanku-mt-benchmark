WƆKA SƐ, bere bi na mmanana mmienu bi te hɔ a wɔn maame awu na wɔagyaw wɔn hɔ. Na ɛyɛ afahyɛ, na ataapanyin (Ata 'Panin) de ahene a ɛsom bo ne sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ bi sɛn ata kumaa no kɔn ho. Ata Kuma ne ne nnamfofoɔ kaa sɛ wɔrekɔ asuwa no so akɔdwo. Bere a wɔreguguare no, hama a etwa to no a wɔde kyekyeree sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ a ɛwɔ Ata Kuma kɔn mu no bubui, na ahosiesiedeɛ no hwee nsuo no mu. Ɔhwehwɛɛ sɛnea ɔbɛhwehwɛ nanso wanhu. Ɔsan baa fie bɛbɔɔ ne nuabea amanneɛ sɛ, "Me sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ no ayera. " Ata 'Panin (a ɔyɛ ataapanyin no) kaa sɛ, "Mennye nni koraa sɛ ɛyeraeɛ, na sɛ yeraeɛ mpo a, biribi wɔ hɔ a mepɛ sɛ mebisa wo. " Ata Kumaa (a ɔyɛ ata kumaa no) kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na wopɛ sɛ mebisa wo? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ mebisa wo daboɔ werɛmmɔ, aserewa ketewa no berebuo, Nsuo a ɛyɛ Gur gles ponponponpuni, ahaban a ɛyɛ baako pɛ (wɔ dua a wotwe fi mu) ne Asono Ɔhemmaa dua. Ata Kumaa kaa sɛ, "Me wate. " Ata Kumaa de nadwene sii hɔ (sɛ ɔbɛpɛ saa nneɛma yi), na ɔbɛduruu beae pɔtee bi a dua baako na ɛsi hɔ. Bere a dua no hunuu no, ɔkae sɛ, "Hwɛ Ata Kumaa, (nea nnipa ka sɛ) ɔda kuro no kuro akyi te sɛ osuo osu a ɛma ahomeka. " (Ata Kumaa) kaa sɛ, "Me sianteɛ a mebaa ha no nko ara ne sɛ, agya ba no yɛ Ata 'Panin baako, na ɛna ba no yɛ Ata 'Panin baako, na ɔde ahweneɛ a ɛsono emu biara kyekyeree me kɔn mu, na ɛbubu yeraeɛ. Na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔkɔhyiaa Aserewa anomaa bi. Na Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, Here is Ata Kumaa, a sɛ ɔpue wɔ kuro no kuro akyi a, obiara ka sɛ ɔte sɛ osuo osu a ɛma ahomeka. Kojo Nsa a a.

Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, Ɛno nti na woresu, bra na gye biribi. Na Ata Kumaa kɔgyee (Aserewa anomaa no berebuo) no, na ɔdaa no ase. Na ɔtu kwan no, na ɔkɔhyiaa Nsuo a ɛrehuruhuruw ponponpon. Ɔkae sɛ, Hwɛ Ata Kumaa a, sɛ ɔba akuraase abɔnten awieeɛ a, nnipa gye no sɛ osuo osu a ɛma ahomeka. Ata Kumaa kaa amaneɛbɔ a ɔka kyerɛɛ afoforɔ dada no kyerɛɛ no. Na Gurgle-water maa no nsu a ɛresen pɔnpɔn bi, na ɔgyee kɔɔ so. Afei ɔne Daboɔ no hyiaa. Bere a Daboɔ no hunuu no, ɔkae sɛ, Hwɛ Ata Kumaa; bere a ɔpue wɔ akuraase abɔnten no awieeɛ no, na ɔte sɛ osuo osu a ɛma ahomeka. Ata Kumaa ka kyerɛɛ no eRRAND. Ɔkaa sɛ, Bra gye biribi, (na ɔde ne mmɔwere no mu baako maa no). Ɛnna Ata Kumaa bisaa sɛ, Agya Akraman, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ na manya Asono Ɔhemmaa no dua? Ɔkaa sɛ, Ah, sɛ worekɔ a, mmoa no nyinaa bɛda hɔ, na Asono Ɔhemmaa no bɛda mfinimfini, na worekɔ a, dwane wɔn so denden, na sɛ wuduru Asono Ɔhemmaa no nkyɛn a, twitwa ne dua, nanso sɛ wotwiri wɔn yiye a, w nkate bɔne na ɛrekum wo. " Ata Kumaa kaa sɛ, "Me da mo ase. " Na ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu saa mmoa yi; ɔhyɛɛ wɔn so den paa, kɔduruu Ɔhemmaa nkyɛn. Ɔtwaa ne dua, na ɔdanee nakyi. Wɔ kwan so no ɔtɔɔ looking-glass, na ɔde kɔɔ fie. Na ɔduruu fie. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ata" Panyin, hwɛ nneɛma a wokaeɛ sɛ wobɛfa afiri me nsam no. " Ata "Panyin kaa sɛ, "Me nsa aka wɔn. " Ɛnneɛ, da bi a na wɔte nkabom no, na Ata Kumaa de ahwehwɛ no kɔhwɛɛ mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Saa ahwehwɛ yi, sɛ obi hwɛ mu a, na ɔhu maame a ɔwɔ Nsaman-saman asase so. " Ata" Panyin kaa sɛ, "Saa na ɛteɛ, fa ba na memfa nhwɛ. " Ɔde maa no. Ɛduruu berɛ a ɔrebɛhwɛ mu no, ɛgyaa ne nsa mu, na ɛbubuu. Ata Kumaa kaa sɛ, "Eyi deɛ, nea wugyee me no, me bɛhwehwɛ afiri wo hɔ. " Ɛneɛ Ata 'Panyin' no kɔfaa nneɛma a na Ata Kumaa de ama no no. Ata Kumaa kaa sɛ, "Mempɛ, nea mepɛ ne sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa afoforɔ aba me. " Ata 'Panyin no de nadwene sii hɔ, na ɔkɔhyiaa dua bi a ɔwɔ ahaban baako. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ata Panyin ni, a sɛ ɔba akuraase abɔnten awieeɛ a, wɔma no akwaaba sɛ osuo asuguare. Ata Panyin buae sɛ, Me appeared wo maame! ne wo agya! me deɛ, fa wo ahaban baako ma me na menkɔ. Asomaa anerewa no kaa sɛ, Pass on, he who knows (how to deal with you) is there. Ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔkɔhyiaa dua bi. Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, Ata ni Panyin no; bere a ɔpue wɔ akuraase abɔnten awieeɛ no, na ne ho adwo no sɛ osuo asuguare. Ata Panyin no kaa sɛ, Bere a me appeare wo maame ne wo agya no, me deɛ, mepɛ wo anomaa berebuo baako pɛ, na mema me nkɔ. Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, Twam kɔ, nea onim ne nea ɔwɔ hɔ. Ɛneɛ ɔbaa Gurgling-nsuo no nkyɛn. Gurgling-nsuo kaa sɛ, Ata Panyin ni; sɛ ɔpue wɔ kuro no awieeɛ a, na ɔte sɛ osuo osu a ɛma ahomeka. Ata Panyin kaa sɛ, Sɛ me pue wo maame, sɛ me pue wo agya, ma me wo nsuo no bi na memfa nkɔ. Gurgling-nsuo kaa sɛ, Fa kɔ wanim, nea onim ne ho (how to deal with you) no wɔ hɔ. Afei ɔkɔhyiaa Daboɔ no. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, Ata Panyin no ni, a sɛ ɔda abɔnten no awieeɛ a, ɔyɛ foforɔ te sɛ osuo asuguare. Ata Panyin no buae sɛ, Meyɛ wo maame ne wo agya appear! Me deɛ, momma me mo mmɔwere no bi na menkɔ. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Obi a ɔnim (sɛnea ɔne mo di) wɔ hɔ. " Ɔtuu kwan no, na ɔkɔhyiaa Asono mmoakuo bi, na Asono Ɔhemmaa no da wɔn mfinimfini hɔ. Afei ɔkɔɔ so twaa ne dua. Asono mmoakuo no nyinaa kyeree no nsɔmu, na wɔhyɛɛ no so ma ɔwuiɛ. Ata Kumaa hwehwɛɛ ne nuabea no wɔ kwan mu. Ɔbaa no ambaeɛ. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Sɛ wo ne wo mogya biara te hɔ, na emu baako sɛe wo adeɛ biara a, nhwɛ ntua, ɛfiri sɛ Ata Panyin na ɔyɛɛ saa, na ɔkɔwuwuiɛ. "

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
