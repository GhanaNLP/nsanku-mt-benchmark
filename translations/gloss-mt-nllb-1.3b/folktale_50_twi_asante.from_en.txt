Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ABOSOM, NYAMESOM NKETEWA NO, BAA EWIASE MU

NA ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ, ɔwoo mmofra du-baako, na da biara a ɔsɔre na ɔnoa aduane no, na mmofra no di nyinaa. Wɔn maame annya bi nni. Afei ɔdwenee ho pii, na ɔkɔɔ afuw hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ Silk-cotton dua no sɛ, "Meresoma me mmofra du-baako aba wo nkyɛn ha wɔ asafuntum tetew mu; na sɛ wɔba a, twitwa wo nkorabata du-baako na kunkum me mmofra no. " Silk-cotton dua no kae sɛ, "Me wate, na mɛyɛ ama wo. " Ɛnna maame no fii hɔ kɔɔ ne fie, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne mmofra no sɛ, "Wo mus kɔ afuw no mu, wɔ Silk-cotton dua no ase; ɛhɔ na sakora ahorow wɔ, kɔ yi na bra (back). " Na mmofra no fii aseɛ. Wɔkɔɔ kɔduruu Silk-cotton dua no ho. Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Nɔmba Baako, gyina hɔ pintinn; Nɔmba Mmienu, gyina hɔ pintinn; Nɔmba Mmiɛnsa, gyina hɔ pintinn; Nɔmba Anan, gyina hɔ pintinn; Nɔmba Anum, gyina hɔ pintinn; Nɔmba Nsia, gyina hɔ pintinn; Nɔmba Ason, gyina hɔ pintinn; Nɔmba Awotwe, gyina hɔ pintinn; Nɔmba Nkron, gyina hɔ pintinn; Nɔmba Du, gyina hɔ pintinn; Na me, Nɔmba Dubiako, migyina hɔ pintinn. " Ɛneɛ Nɔmba Dubiako bɛkasa kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Dabi, monnim r baako pɛ. eason dɛn nti na maame kae sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ yɛkɔ fa sakora ahorow? Ne anuanom kae sɛ, "Dabi. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Waka akyerɛ Silk-cotton dua yi sɛ, sɛ yɛkɔ hɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ ɛtu nkorabata na ɛbɔ yɛn; ɛno nti, mo nyinaa ntwa nnua, na montow ngu Silk-cotton dua yi so. " Wɔtwaa nnua no, na wɔtoo ngu Silk-cotton dua no so, pim! twerɛdeɛ! pim! twerɛdeɛ! (yɛɛ nnyigyei a wɔyɛeɛ). Silk-cotton dua no susuwii sɛ mmofra no aba. Ɔyii ne nkorabata du-baako, na ɔmaa ɛhwe aseɛ wɔ fam. Dodoɔ ketewa Du-baako kae sɛ, "Woahwɛ, sɛ yɛkɔ so wɔ hɔ a, anka Silk-cotton dua no bɛkum yɛn. " Wɔgyee sakora ahorow no de kɔɔ wɔn maame nkyɛn. Ɔno na ɔnoaa no; oh! prɛko pɛ na mmofra no adii (ne nyinaa). Wɔn maame kae sɛ, "Ah! saa asɛm yi deɛ, me ntumi nnyina so, enti me bɛfa mmofra yi ama Ewiem-onyankopɔn. " Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔkɔkaa ne nyinaa kyerɛɛ Ewiem-onyankopɔn sɛ, "Me mmofra bi a mema wɔn awoɔ no, berɛ a mepɛ sɛ me di no, mentumi nnya biribi nni (efiri sɛ wɔdi no ntem na ɛdɔɔso dodo); ɛkɔm rekum me; ɛno nti, me srɛ wo, ma mmofra no mmra ha na wɔakum wɔn, na ama manya biribi adi. " Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "So ɛno na ɛyɛ asɛm no ampa? " Na ɔbaa no kae sɛ, "Me ne etire bi a ne emu yɛ fitaa na ɛrekasa. " Ɔsoro-nyame no paw asɛnkafoɔ, na wɔkɔtu amoa kɛseɛ bi, a wɔde nkukuo a abubu gu mu; ɔkɔfa ɔwɔ ne daboɔ kɔto amena no mu, na ɔkata so. Na afei messenger no kɔfrɛɛ mmofra no. Wɔduruu baabi a amena no da no ara pɛ, Nɔmba Dubiako kaa sɛ, "Nɔmba Baako, gyina hɔ pintinn; Nɔmba Mmienu, gyina hɔ pintinn; Nɔmba Mmiɛnsa, gyina hɔ pintinn; Nɔmba Anan, gyina hɔ pintinn; Nɔmba Anum, gyina hɔ pintinn; Nɔmba Nsia, gyina hɔ pintinn; Nɔmba Ason, gyina hɔ pintinn; Nɔmba Awotwe, gyina hɔ pintinn; Nɔmba Nkron, gyina hɔ pintinn; Nɔmba Du, gyina hɔ pintinn; na me, dodoɔ ketewa Dubiako, migyina hɔ pintinn; ɛsɛ sɛ wotwam. s yi, ɛnsɛ sɛ wofa hɔ. " Ne anuanom kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti, bere a kwan a ɛtrɛw da hɔ no, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛfa nwura no mu? " Seesei, bere a wɔrekɔ no, na wɔn nyinaa de abaa akɔ. Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Montow nnua yi mu baako ngu kwan yi so. " Wɔtoo no hɔ fam, na ɛbɛhwee mu (kɔɔ amena no mu), yiridi (na ɛyɛ ne ahweaseɛ nnyigyei). Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Wo ni, wo hu sɛ, anka sɛ yɛtwa mu kɔ hɔ a, anka yɛn nyinaa awu. " Enti wɔfaa kwan bi so na wɔfirii hɔ kɔɔ ɔsoro-nyame nkyɛn. Na ɔsoro-nyame no ahyɛ sɛ wɔtu atokuro, (covered over) na wɔde nkonnwa gu so, na sɛ mmofra no ba bɛtra so a, wɔbɛkɔ ahwe ase wɔ tokuro no mu. Mmofra no duruu ɔsoro-nyame no anim. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Nkonnwa ato hɔ, wubetumi akɔtena so. Ɛnna Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, Hwan ne yɛn, sɛ yɛbɛtumi atena nkongua fɛfɛ saa ara so; ɛnneɛ, Sire, yɛbɛtena nkyɛn ha. Ɛneɛ na ɔsoro-nyame no de nani kyerɛɛ mmofra no, ɔkae sɛ, Mede saa mmofra yi bɛkɔ owuo akuraa. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda hɔ no, ɔfrɛɛ mmofra no, ɔkae sɛ, Ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ Owuo (baabi a ɛwɔ hɔ no), na wokɔgye ne nkyɛn sika abua, sika kɔkɔɔ nam, sika kɔkɔɔ whet-box, sika kɔkɔɔ whet-stone, ne sika kɔkɔɔ fly-switch. " Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Wo ne yɛn wura, baabiara a wobɛsoma yɛn no, yɛbɛkɔ. " Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Nnye nni. " Na mmofra no de wɔn ani kyerɛɛ Owuo akuraa ase. Wɔduruu hɔ no, Owuo bisaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na bere a ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara ba ha da no, wobaa ha? " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Yɛkyinkyin na yɛbaa ha (wɔ hokwan so koraa). " Owuo kaa sɛ, "Oh, yie koraa. " Seesei na Owuo wɔ mmofra du, na ɔne ne ho ka ho no, na wɔn nyinaa yɛ du-baako. Ɛberɛ a nneɛma fii aseɛ yeraeɛ (i. e. bere a esum da), Owuo kyekyɛɛ mmofra no mu mmiako mmiako de biako maa ne mmofra no mu biara, bere a ɔne Nɔmba Dubiako no kɔdaeɛ. Sɛ esum da a, na Owuo ma ne se (nkyɛ) yɛ kɔkɔɔ sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛkyere Nɔmba Dubiako aka wɔn ho. Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Owuo, meda so ara nna. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Dabi na wobɛda? " Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Sɛ wode sika abua ma me nnom sigaret kakra a, ɛnde ebia mɛda. " Na Owuo de bae maa no. Ɛnkyɛree biara akyi no, Owuo san hyerɛɛ ne se bio, wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akye Nɔmba Dubiako aka wɔn ho. Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Owuo, meda so ara nna. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Dabi na wobɛda? " Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Sɛ wode sika kɔkɔɔ sniff-box bɛba me a, ebia me bɛkɔ ada. " Na Owuo de no baa ne nkyɛn. Bio nso, ɛno akyi bere tiaa bi, na Owuo rekɔ akyere Nɔmba Dubiako. Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Mennae. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Dabi na wobɛda? " Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa sika kɔkɔɔ nam a mebɛwe no kakra a, anka mɛda. " Owuo de no bae. Ɛduruu berɛ tiawa bi a na Owuo rebɛkyere no. Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Nana, meda so ara nna. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Ɛnne, bere bɛn na wobɛda? " Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Nana, sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa sika kɔkɔɔ whet-stone bi aba me a, anka mɛtumi ada. " Na Owuo kɔe na ɔde bae. Afei nso, ankyɛ na Owuo sɔreɛ bio. Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Oh, Nana, mekaeɛ sɛ mennya nnaeɛ. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Na ɛdɛn ne da a wobɛda? " Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Sɛ wobɛfa koraa a atokuro ahyɛ mu ma na woapete nsuo agu mu, na woanoa aduane bi ama me adi a, anka mɛtumi ada. " Owuo maa ne sɔneɛ so na ɔkɔɔ asuwa no so. Bere a ɔhwiehwee nsuo no guu mu no, sɔneɛ no mu atokuro no maa ɛtwaa mu. Afei Nɔmba Dubiako ka kyerɛɛ ne anuanom sɛ, "Sɔre na dwane. " Ɛnna wɔsɔree na wɔdwanee; na Nɔmba Dubiako no kɔtwaa borɔdeɛ nnuadewa, na wɔde siee baabi a na ne anuanom ada, na ɔfaa ntoma de kataa so. Afei na Owuo wɔ asuwa no so, ɔrehwie nsuo gu so. Afei na Ɔbarima wuo frɛɛ Ɔbaa wuo sɛ, "Ho wɔ hɔ, Owu. " Ɔbuaa no sɛ, "Adwo. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn na woreyɛ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Awerɛhoɔ, ɛnyɛ abofra ketewa bi na me nsa aka no; berɛ a merebɛkyere no, ɔse, 'Menneda', wagye me nneɛma nyinaa, na afei ɔse ɛsɛ sɛ mefa sɔneɛ na mepete nsuo. " Ɔbarima wuo kaa sɛ, "Ah, woyɛ abofra ketewa, afei, sɛ woyi nhaban na wotow a, ne sɔneɛ no emu, na afei wopete nsuo no, anka ɛnyɛ yie? " Ɔbaa wuo no kaa sɛ, "Oh, ɛyɛ nokware paa. " Ɔtuu nhaban, de emu hyɛɛ mu, na ɔpete nsuo no na ɔkɔɔ. Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Owuo, woaba dedaw? Yɛ aduane no. " Owuo no noa aduane no, ɔsɔɔ ne se no wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ ɔbɛkum Nɔmba Dubiako nuanom na ɔnoa wɔn aduane. Ɔkɔɔ no, wanhwehwɛ wɔn mu, na ɔno ankasa kum ne mmofra du no nyinaa. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, anɔpa paa, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, Owuo sɔreeeɛ, na ɔbɛtenaa hɔ wɔ ogya no nkyɛn. Nɔmba du-baako kae sɛ, "Nanabea, huruiɛ bi te wo nufuɔ so. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Kyere nwansena-switch a ɛda hɔ no, na kum no ma me. " Nɔmba du-baako kae sɛ, "Ao me Nyame! me; me nipa a me yɛ wo nsunsuansoɔ, bere a huruiɛ bi te wo so, na sika kɔkɔɔ nwansena-switch bi da hɔ (wo de biribi dada bɛdi dwuma), ma menyi no na memmɛkum no. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔfa no firi r oom. " Nɔmba Dubiako no kɔeɛ na ɔde baeɛ. Ɔhyɛɛ da pam nwansena no; wankum no. Nɔmba Dubiako no kaa sɛ, "Oh, to-da, baabi a tsetse nwansena yi bɛhome no, ɛhɔ na me ne no bɛhome. " Ɛneɛ na Nɔmba Dubiako no kɔɔ dan no mu na ɔfaa ne kotokuo a sika abua no wɔ mu ne nneɛma no nyinaa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Nana owuo, hwee rennɔɔso gye sɛ me nsa ka huruiɛ no, fa to kotokuo yi mu, na mede ba wo nkyɛn. " Nɔmba Dubiako no de hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! Ɔduruu kuro no awieeɛ no, ɔkaa sɛ, Ho, ɛhɔ na Nanabaa wuo, kyɛ me abebuo yi, nanso sɛ woanyɛ kwaseampa a, anka mɛtumi ayi wo nneɛma nyinaa afiri wo so; anka me anuanom a me ne wɔn aba yi, wɔbɛtumi anya kwan a wɔbɛfa so dwane? Na anka mɛtumi ama wo nso akum wo mmofra du no nyinaa? Me deɛ, me kɔ. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Wo, abofra a wote sɛɛ, baabiara a wobɛhome no, ɛhɔ na me nso mɛhome. " Nɔmba Dubiako tuu mmirika, yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! Na Owuo nso dii nakyi. Bere a Nɔmba Dubiako rekɔ no, ɔkɔtoo ne anuanom, na wɔte kwan so. Na wɔreyɛ nnomaa afiri. Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Wo nnya nkɔe anaa? Owuo reba, enti momma yɛnhwehwɛ kwan bi a yɛbɛfa so adwane. " Mprempren na Owuo baa wɔn so. Nɔmba Dubiako faa aduro na ɔhwie guu ne anuanom so, na wɔkɔɔ silk-cotton dua bi atifi. Na Owuo gyina Silk-cotton dua no nan ase. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ha ara na mehuu mmofra yi, na ɛhe na wɔakɔ? " Nɔmba Dubiako na ɔte soro hɔ, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne anuanom sɛ, "Merebɛbɔ no nsuo. " Ne anuanom kae sɛ, "E, ɔrehwehwɛ sɛ yɛkyere no, na yɛadwane abɛtra ha, na woreka sɛ, 'Merebɛbɔ no nsuo.' " Nɔmba Dubiako antie, na ɔhyɛɛ Owuo nsuo. Owuo kaa sɛ, "Ah, wo ni, nnɛ da a woahu ɔhaw. " Owuo kaa sɛ, "Wo, abofra, a wote hɔ no, 'Kyere-he-ne, Kyere-he-ne'. " Ɛhɔ na mmofra no mu baako hwee ase. "Kyere-he-ne," sɛkɛnd baako hwee ase. Afei na Nɔmba Dubiako pɛ na aka. Owuo kaa sɛ, "Abofra, 'kyere-he-ne '," na Nɔmba Dubiako huruw sii fam, "Kir! Na Owuo nso kɔɔ so kɔɔ Silk-cotton dua no nkyɛn. Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Wo, ɔbaa kɛse pa ara, wo nso, 'kyere-he-ne'. " Na Owuo nso sian bae, tum! wawu. Nɔmba Dubiako kɔbɔɔ aduro, na ɔkyinkyim wɔ ne pALMS ntam, na ɔde petepete ne anuanom so, na wɔsɔre. Nɔmba Dubiako pɛɛ sɛ ɔtow aduro no gu, bere a ne fã bi guu Owuo so, na Owuo nyan. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Woakum me, na woasan nso akanyan me; da yi na wo ne me bɛnya asɛm. " Ɛnna wɔn nyinaa hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ wɔreguan ntɛm ara, kiri! kiri! kiri! Afei na Owuo redi wɔn akyi. Bere a wɔrekɔ no, na asuo kɛseɛ bi da nsuyiri mu. Berɛ a Nɔmba Dubiako ne ne anuanom duu hɔ no, na wɔnim asuguareɛ, na wɔaguareɛ atwa mu. Nɔmba Dubiako nko ara na ɔnnim asuguareɛ. Na mmofra no gyina nkyɛn bi, wɔsu, na wɔsu, na wɔsu; na wɔn anom abere. Nɔmba Dubiako deɛ, ɔdanee ɔboɔ. Owuo kɔduruu asubonten so. Ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, mmofra yi, mo nnyina hɔ, momma minnya ɔboɔ a mebɛtow abɔ wɔn anom a ahonhon. " Owuo, bere a ɔhwɛɛ fam no, ɔhunuu ɔboɔ bi a ɛda hɔ; ɔfaa no na ɔtoo no; bere a ɔboɔ no retu kwan no, ɔkae sɛ, "Winds, take me and set me on the other side. " Ɛhwee fam wɔ afa foforo. Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Here am I. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Ah, abofra yi! Me nni asɛm foforɔ biara ka kyerɛ wo, nanso nea mewɔ ka kyerɛ wo ne sɛ, kɔ tena fie, na sesa kɔyɛ anyame nketewa no mu baako, na sɛ obi biara a mepɛ sɛ mefa no ba baabi a wowɔ a, wobɛma me nim; sɛ mepɛ a, mɛgyae no na mede no akyɛ wo; nanso nea mepɛ (in nsasaeɛ) no, gye no ma me. Ɛne sɛnea Abosom (lesser) yɛ nafɔdeɛ. -gods) baa ewiase. Wɔfiri mmɔfra nketewa dodoɔ Dubiako.
