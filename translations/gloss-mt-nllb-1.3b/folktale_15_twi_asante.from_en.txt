Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ NNIPA BI REHWƐ YIE, NA AFOFORƆ NNI HWƐ YIE

WƆKYERƐ SƐ NA ɔbaa BI WƆ HƆ (BAAKO) NA ƆKƆƆ NNƆBAA BI NKYƐN A ƐGYINAA HƆ WƆ Nsuo MU SƐ ƆBƐHWEHWƐ MU WƆ AWO HO. Na ɔkɔɔ no, nnuadewa no kaa sɛ, Mɛma wo nea wopɛ, nanso abofra a me ne no bɛgyina wo anim no, sɛ ɔsɔre a, ɔrenyɛ adwuma biara da. Ɔkaa sɛ, Mepene so. Ɛnyɛ nna mmienu, ɛnyɛ nna mmiɛnsa, na ɔnyinsɛnee na ɔwoo awoɔ. Te sɛ mmofra a wɔwɔ Spider-stories no, na ɔnnyin ntɛm ara, na wanyin prɛko pɛ. Na ne maame ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Among the whole tribes taboos, none are which you need to observe except the Palm-tree, you must never climb one. Ɔkaa sɛ, Maame, me na metie. Ɛnneɛ, da bi na maame no gyaee no hɔ kɔɔ afuo. Ɔkɔfrɛɛ m'adɔfo no, na ɔne no hyɛɛ aseɛ bɔɔ agoru, na ɔkyeree ne kɔm-nsɔmu nsɔmu na ɛbubu gui. Na abaayewa no kae sɛ, Saa hama a etwa to no a wopwaa no, kɔ na fa me nnuadewa fiber bi na memfa nkyekyere no. Ɛnna abarimaa no kɔe kɔtɔɔ bi na ɔde bae. Na abaayewa no kae sɛ, Mempɛ saa, gye nea woapɛ afi nnuadewa a ɛwɔ nsuo mu no bi. Enti abofra no fii adi kɔhwehwɛɛ bi. Bere a ɔkɔɔ no, na nnuadewa dua bi gyina nsuo no mu. Ɔforo kɔɔ soro, ɔkra! ɔkra! ɔkra! Ɛduruu sɛ ɔduruu dua no kɔn mu. Ɔyii ne Sekkan no, na ɔduruu hɔ ara pɛ, dua no mu paapaee mmienu, na abarimaa no yafunu kɔɔ mu, na dua no mu toe bio. Ɛhɔ na na ɛretwiw no. Ɛnkyɛree biara na ne maame no baeɛ, ɔduruu nsuo no mu no, ɔhunuu shadow bi wɔ so, na ɔkaa sɛ:

Hwan shadow na ɛda nsuo so te sɛ me abofra Akwasi Kwasaman yi?

Ɔkaa sɛ, Ɛma, ɛyɛ me.

Ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na mmɛw dua no rehyɛ wo? "

Ɔkaa sɛ, Mekɔɔ so yii Palm fiber de kɔmaa me dɔfo no na mmɛw dua no bɛhyɛɛ me.

Na maame no kae sɛ, Wo, saa nnuadewa yi mia no, mia no, mia no, me ba Kwasaman.

Palm-tree squeeze no, squeeze no, squeeze no, Palm-tree squeeze no.

Ɛnna maame no kɔe. Ɛnna agya no nso bae, na ɔkae sɛ:

Hwan shadow na ɛda nsuo a ɛte sɛ me abofra kwasaman yi so?

Ɔkae sɛ, Agya, ɛyɛ me.

Ɔ (agya) bisaa no sɛ, "Dɛn nti na nnuadewa no rehyɛ wo so? "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Mekɔɔ so yii Palm fiber de kɔmaa me dɔfo no na mmɛw dua no bɛhyɛɛ me. "

Ɔ (agya) kae sɛ, "Wo, nnuadewa yi mia no, mia no, me ba Kwasaman.

Palm-tree squeeze no, squeeze no, palm-tree squeeze no. "

Ɛnna agya no firii fie kɔka kyerɛɛ akuraa no mu Etire, na akuraa no mu Etire nso frɛɛ nnipa no nyinaa; na wɔbaa nnuadewa no nan ase, na akuraa no mu Etire maa ne nne so kae sɛ:

"Hwan shadow na ɛda nsuo so te sɛ me nana Akwasi Kwasaman deɛ no? "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔpanyin, ɛyɛ me. "

Ɔ (Etire a ɔwɔ akuraa no so) bisaa no sɛ, "Adua no dɛn nti na ɛrehyɛ wo? "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Mekɔɔ so yii Palm fiber de kɔmaa me dɔfo no na mmɛw dua no bɛhyɛɛ me. "

Etire a ɛwɔ akuraa hɔ no kaa sɛ, "Wo, saa nnuadewa yi squeeze no, squeeze no, squeeze no, me grandson Kwasaman.

Palm-tree squeeze no, squeeze no, palm-tree squeeze no. "

Ɛnna akuraa no mu Etire no kumm, na nkuraasefoɔ no nyinaa too dwom koro kyerɛɛ nnuadewa no sɛ ɛnkyere no. Na afei ne dɔfo no nso baeɛ, na ɔkae sɛ: "Hwan shadow na ɛda nsuo no so, te sɛ me dɔfo Akwasi Kwasaman deɛ no? "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ me, wo ɔdɔfo ankasa. "

Ɔkae sɛ: "Dɛn nti na nnuadewa dua no repiapia wo? "

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mekɔɔ sɛ merebɛbubu Palm fiber ama wo, na nnuadewa no bɛhyɛɛ me. "

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Palm-tree gyae no, gyae no, gyae no.

Palm-tree gyae me dɔfo, Akwasi Kwasaman.

Palm-tree gyae no, gyae no, gyae no, Palm-tree gyae no. "

Ɔkaa yei no, abotan no bueeɛ kakra, na ne dɔfoɔ no yafunu firi baeɛ kakra. Na ɔsan maa ne nne so bio sɛ:

"Hwan shadow na ɛda nsuo so, te sɛ me dɔfo Akwasi Kwasaman deɛ no? "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ me, wo ɔdɔfo ankasa. "

Ɔkae sɛ: "Dɛn nti na nnuadewa dua no repiapia wo? "

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mekɔɔ sɛ merebɛbubu Palm fiber ama wo, na nnuadewa no bɛhyɛɛ me. "

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Palm-tree gyae no, gyae no, gyae no.

Palm-tree gyae me dɔfo, Akwasi Kwasaman.

Palm-tree gyae no, gyae no, gyae no, Palm-tree gyae no. "

Na afei ne dɔfo no puei nyinaa prɛko pɛ, na ɔbaa bɛkyeree ne m'adɔfo bosom no, na ɛwosow kɔe; na ɛdane yɛɛ ngo. Na nnipa a wɔde ahopere kɔɔ hɔ kɔpraa bi wɔ wɔn anim no, wɔn na wɔn ho yɛ fɛ paa; nanso wɔn a wɔnnyaa bi no, wɔn na wɔn ho nyɛ fɛ. Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
