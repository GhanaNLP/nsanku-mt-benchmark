Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA EKUONA ABUSUAKUO NO BAA ABUSUAKUO MU

NA ƆBAA BI WƆ HƆ A WƆFRƐ NO Abena; bere a ne maame ne ne agya de no kɔhyɛɛ awareɛ no, ɔkae sɛ, Certainly not. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne agya no sɛ, On the da a mehu ɔbarima a medɔ no, on that da, when I come, and if you are taking asraba, I shall take your asraba-box and hit you (with it) on the top of your etire. Afei, aboa no, Ɛkoɔ no, tee saa abusuabɔ yi ho asɛm a, ɔsakra ɔbɛyɛɛ ne ho sɛ ɔbarima, na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ, na ɔbɛduruu hɔ kɔbaa ɔbaa no akuraa ase. Ɛnneɛ, Abena hunuu no no, ɔtutuu mmirika kɔɔ baabi a na ne agya rekɔfa asraba no. Ɔgyee asraba-box no firii ne nsam, na ɔde bɔɔ no etire. Na saa da no yɛ nea ɛyɛ anigyeɛ wɔ nkuraasefoɔ mu, a wɔka sɛ, Yɛn wura ba no ahunu nea ɔpɛ nasɛm. Ɛnna wɔkɔfa ɔbarima no baa fie. Na abaayewa no kaa sɛ, Wei ne nea mepɛ no. Na ɔbarima no nso pɛe saa, na ɔwaree no. Ɔdii hɔ nna mmiɛnsa, na afei ɔkaa sɛ ɔbɛkɔ ne akuraa, na ɔbaa no kaa sɛ ɔbɛkɔ ne no. Enti Ɔhemmaa no siesiee ne nneɛma nyinaa na ɔde nnipa aduonu nnan maa no sɛ ɔne wɔn nkɔ. Ɛnna wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ, na wɔduruu akuraase bi kuro akyi no, akokɔnini bi bɔɔ. Nnipa no kae sɛ, Yɛadu fie anaa? Na ɔbaa no kunu kae sɛ, M'aware yere foforo, na ɛsɛ sɛ medikan de no ba me fie, na wanya ne nyinaa wɔ ahoboa mu, ansa na wakɔ so afrɛ nnipa. Enti wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ na wɔkɔɔ so. Wɔduruu kwaeɛ mu petee bi. Bere a wɔduruu saa kwaeɛ mu petee no, ɔbarima no gyinaa nkyɛn, ɔkae sɛ, Abena, seesei berɛ aso sɛ meka nea meyɛ ankasa kyerɛ wo. Abena kae sɛ, Wo yɛ hwan? Ɔbarima no kae sɛ, Mɛyɛ pA! sɛ mɛtumi ayɛ ahabanmono! ; na ɔdanee ɛkoɔ. Nnipa no nyinaa kae sɛ, E! e, nea ɔka ne sɛ, ɔka sɛ ɔyɛ pA! sɛ ɔbɛtumi ayɛ ahabanmono! (Anka na wɔaka nsɛmfua yi sen) wɔn nyinaa danee nkoɔ. Na ɔbarima baako pɛ na aka a ɔtutuu kwan baa fie. Na ɔkɔbɔɔ akuraa so ɔbarima no nea wahu no. Ɔpanyin ne Ɔhemmaa no frɛɛ badwa bi, ɔno (nea na wakɔdwane) no maa wɔn amaneɛbɔ a ɛfa nea ɛkɔɔ so no ho bere a wɔrekɔ no, na bere a ɔduruu pɔ a ɔkae sɛ, He made pA! sɛ ɔbɛtumi ayɛ ahabanmono! no, ɔno nso, ɔdanee ɛkoɔ. Wɔn mu biara a ɔbɛka saa nsɛmfua yi bio no, ɔdanee ɛkoɔ. Nnipa a wɔwɔ akuraa hɔ no nyinaa danee nkoɔ, na ade a ɛda nkyɛn ne aberewa baako. Saa dwumadi yi akyi berɛ bi, odunsini bi baa akuraa hɔ. Ɔteɛɛm sɛ, Ah! kuropɔn a nnipa nni mu? Dinn! (Not a nnyigyei in mmuaeɛ save the nnomaa su of the babadua.) Ɔkasae bio. Ɛduruu tenten so no, aberewa no kaa sɛ, Me ni. Dunsini no kaa sɛ, Dɛn ne amaneɛbɔ no, na adɛn nti na wote akuraa yi mu nkutoo? Aberewa no nso buae sɛ, Woaba ha, (nanso mempɛ sɛ me ne wo nni hwee), kɔ na fa me ho to baabi. Dunsini no kaa sɛ, Woyɛ ɔbayifoɔ, wo na woadi nnipa a wɔwɔ akuraa yi nyinaa, enti gye sɛ wopue. eak, me bekum wo mprempren ara. Ɛnna aberewa no kae sɛ, Sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ me kasa a, (first) kɔ fa hama-creeper na kyekyere me, na fa me kyekyere me wɔ gyedua-dua no ho. Ɛnna dunsini no bae bɛkyekyeree no, na ɔde no kyekyeree no wɔ gyedua-dua no ho. Mprempren na aberewa no ka nea asi no nyinaa kyerɛɛ no, nanso bere a ɔduruu pɔ a na ɛsɛ sɛ ɔka no, Ɔkaa sɛ ɔbɛyɛ pA! sɛ ebia ɔbɛyɛ ahabanmono! Ná hama-creeper no apae; na aberewa no adane ɛkoɔ na afiri. Ɛnna dunsini no kaa sɛ, Afei, mehu sɛ ɛnyɛ aberewa no na okum nkuraasefoɔ yi nyinaa. Ɛnna ɔkɔpuee aduro (habannuro) na ɔhyɛɛ mu de hyɛɛ kɔbere prɛte mu, na ɔhwiee guu nsuo mu; na ɔfaa ne (magic) cow-tail no de hyɛɛ mu; afei ɔmaa ne adwumasuafoɔ faa pan no maa no. Ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ kwaeɛ. Ɔhunuu nkoɔ bi a ɛgyina hɔ ho, na ɔde ne dua guu aduro no mu, na ɔpete guu wɔn so, na wɔsan danee nnipa bio. Na ɛkaa hɔ nko ara, a na wɔyɛ ɛkoɔ ankasa. Na nnipa no san baa wɔn fie. Saa na Ekuona abusuakuo no baa abusuakuo no ntam; ɛfiri saa awareɛ a Abena hyɛɛ aseɛ no.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
