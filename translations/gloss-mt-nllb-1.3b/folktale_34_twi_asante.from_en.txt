Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ ONIPA BIAKO NKYERƐ MFITIASEƐ A ONIPA FOFORƆ FIRII MU BAEƐ NO

NA ɔbɔfoɔ bi wɔ hɔ pɛn bi. Bere a ɔsɔre no na ɔtaa kɔ nwura so kɔhwehwɛ agorɔ a ɔbɛku, na ebia ɔbɛnya bi adi, na ebinom nso atɔn. Afei da bi a ɔkɔɔ nwura no so no, ɔtee sɛ Kokotee, Nwura ɔPONKYERƐNI, refrɛ ne busuani sɛ, "Kokotee Asamoa e! " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Aane, onua, yiw. " Ɔ (ɔPONKYERƐNI a odi kan) kae sɛ, "Yɛn adwuma berɛ adu, enti yɛnkɔ blacksmith no forge no, ma ɔnhyehyɛ dadeɛ no na ɔmfa ano nkyerɛ (yɛn twitwa nnwinneɛ), na sɛ yɛbɛtwa nnua biara a, yɛbɛtumi atwa. . " Berɛ a Bush-pig no frɛɛ ne anuanom no, ɔbɔfoɔ no kotowee ehuntahunu, na ɔtee nkɔmmɔdie no nyinaa. Afei onuabarima a ɔwɔ Bush-pig (first) no bisaa ne onuabarima Bush-pig sɛ, "Na akuraa bɛn na yɛbɛkɔ, na yɛato dadeɛ no? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Yɛbɛkɔ akuraa a wɔfrɛ no Across-the-asuwa no. " Na ɔse, "Ɛda bɛn? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Monday. " Ɛduruu ɔbɔfoɔ no tee nhyehyɛeɛ yi nyinaa, na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔrekɔ fie. Afei, bere a ɔbaa fie no, ɔka kyerɛɛ Across-the-asuwa odikro no asɛm no, ɛne nea ɔteeɛ bere a na wakɔ nwura no mu no. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ akuraa panin no sɛ, Ma mmofra no nkɔ twitwa wɔn nnyina na wɔmfa mmra, na Ɛdwoada a Bush-pigs no bɛsesa wɔn ho (bɛyɛ nnipa) na wɔaba no, yɛbɛfa wɔn akyekyere wɔn nnyina no. Na mmofra no kɔ twitwa nnyina no, na wɔde bae. Akuraa no panin no kɔka kyerɛɛ akuraa tomfoɔ no sɛ wɔmmɔ no dade stapo. Na tomfoɔ no bisaa Ɔmanpanyin no sɛ, Na saa dade stapo a ɛdɔɔso yi nyinaa, a wose ɛsɛ sɛ mebɔ no, wode bɛyɛ dɛn? Na ɔka kyerɛɛ no amaneɛbɔ no, sɛnea ɔbɔfoɔ bi akɔ nwura mu, na ɔsan aba abɛbɔ amaneɛ sɛ Ɛdwoada mmoadoma bi bɛdane wɔn ho ayɛ wɔn nnipa, ahyɛdeɛ sɛ wɔmmra ne dɔnka so nni nnwinnadeɛ atoro. Na tomfoɔ no adwane adwane adi dade stapo no so nkonim ntɛm ara. Bere a ɔhene no de ne nsa too nnyina ne staplosa dade mma no so wieeɛ no, ɔmaa kuro no mu nteɛm bɔɔ dawuru sɛ, Ɛdwoada, sɛ ɛyɛ ɔbaa, anaa ɔbarima, obiara nkɔ baabiara. Ɛdwoada baeɛ, na anɔpa no, aberewa bi ka kyerɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no sɛ, Kɔ na ɛmo, ne nkyene, ne gyeene, ne baabi a mmoadoma bɛtu wɔn nwoma ahoroɔ ato hɔ, monyɛ yie. enti, berɛ a wokɔ hɔ no, fa ɛmo no bɔ so, na berɛ a mmoadoma no ba ha, na berɛ a yɛkyere wɔn, na ɛsɛ sɛ ebinom dwane kɔfa wɔn nwoma ahoroɔ, na wɔhyɛ, berɛ a ɛmo no bɔ wɔn, na wɔtu wɔn gu, na wɔbɛsan ayɛ nnipa. Ɔbɔɔfoɔ no kɔeɛ na ɔde ne ho sieeɛ wɔ hɔ. Na ɔtee sɛ wɔrefrɛ, Kokotee Asamoa, Ɛdwoada adu, momma yɛn nkɔ. Enti wɔn nyinaa baeɛ. Wɔyii wɔn nwoma ahoroɔ no, na wɔde sii hɔ. Afei, (Mprako no mu biako) a na ɔyɛ ɔyaresafoɔ wɔ habannuro ho no, na ɔwɔ wɔn mu, na ɔde ne wedeɛ no kɔtoo baabi foforɔ. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no hwɛ wɔn, na ɔma wɔkɔ. Ɔkɔfaa nwoma ahoroɔ no nyinaa, na ɔhyɛɛ ɛmo a wɔapete so no, na ɔde kɔtoo asuwa no mu, ma nsuo no de kɔeɛ. Nhabannuru ho ɔbenfo no deɛ, a na ɔde asi nkyɛn firi ahomegyeɛ no, ɔbɔfoɔ no anhu no. Ɔbɛfoɔ no baa fie, na Ɔhene no nso frɛɛ nnipa a wɔahyia wɔ dadeɛ dwumfoɔ no fononoo ho no maa wɔbaa ne fie. Berɛ a saa nnipa yi baa Ɔhene fie no, wɔbisaa Ɔhene no sɛ, Adɛn nti na wofrɛɛ yɛn? Ɔhene no kaa sɛ, Na woyɛ me mmarima, na wuguan kɔtenaa baabi foforɔ, ɛnnɛ da a wasan aba yi, ɛno ne sianteɛ a meka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ woba, ɛfiri sɛ meremma wo kwan sɛ wobɛkɔ bio. Ɛnna mmoadoma no ka kyerɛɛ Ɔhene no sɛ, Woaka no, yɛate; nanso yɛnim sɛ Ɔbɔadeɛ no huammɔdi. nter baa bɛka kyerɛɛ wo yɛn ho nsɛm nyinaa, nanso ɛno mfa ho, yɛn ne wo bɛtena aseɛ; nanso yɛnim sɛ nea woka no yɛ atoro; nanso, ɛno mfa ho; yɛda saa ɔbɔfoɔ no ase. Enti me ne wo bɛtena ase; nanso adeɛ baako na ɛwɔ hɔ a yɛbara, ɛne sɛ, monyi yɛn mfitiaseɛ adi; anaa sɛ mo adesuadeɛ biara nkyerɛ yɛn mfitiaseɛ; ɛsɛ sɛ ɛba saa a, yɛbɛtetew yɛn abusuakuo yi mu na yɛafi hɔ. Afei, berɛ a Ɔhene kɔfrɛɛ wɔn no, na dunsini no ne afoforɔ bi adwane. Na dunsini no kɔfa ne wedeɛ no guanee, nanso ahomegyeɛ no san nakyi. Na ɛyɛ sɛ wɔanhu wɔn nwoma ahoroɔ. Ɔhene no penee tebea a mmoadoma no de sii hɔ no so, na wɔne mmoadoma no tenaa ase anigyeɛ mu. Mprempren akuraa so mmarima no waree mmoadoma mmea no bi, na wɔwoo mmofra. Ɛbaa no da bi a na mmoadoma no mu baako ne nkuraasefoɔ no mu baako de wɔn nkuturu reko. Ɛnna akuraaseni no ka kyerɛɛ aboa kɛseɛ no sɛ, Wo ho mfi hɔ, aboa a wote sɛ wo, a wofiri Bush-pig kuo no mu. Ɛnkyɛreeɛ na mmoadoma no nyinaa aniwa so yɛɛ kɔkɔɔ, na wɔkɔɔ ɔhene fie kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Ɔkwan a ɛbɛtumi ayɛ yiye a yɛaka ho asɛm akyerɛ mo no, afei aba mu ampa, na dɛn na mobɛyɛ? Ɔhene no maa wɔkɔfrɛɛ nnipa no. na na ɔde mansotwe no aba. Ɔhene no hwɛɛ asɛm no mu yie na ɔhyɛɛ atɛmmuo sɛ mmoadoma no nnyae sɔboɔ no, ɛfiri sɛ (ɔkaa) sɛ ɛyɛ berɛ tenten na wɔaba na eyi nso ne dwumadie a ɛdi kan a obi aka biribi akyerɛ wɔn (wɔn mfitiaseɛ ho). Nanso mmoadoma no kaa sɛ, Yɛbɛpene so. Na Ɔhene no kaa sɛ, Worentie, na wosusu sɛ nea ɔbarima yi kae no yɛ atoro, wo nyɛ Bush-pigs ni? Na mmoadoma no kaa sɛ, Oh, yɛate. Ɛneɛ na mmoadoma no ne nsuo no so nnipa no reko. Mmoadoma no bɛsɛee akuraa no, ma ɛkame ayɛ sɛ nnipa du pɛ na wɔaka. Na wɔsrɛ mmɔborɔhunu na wɔka kyerɛɛ mmoadoma no sɛ wɔn (mmoadoma no) wɔ nea ɛfata wɔ wɔn nkyɛn. Mmoadoma no tiee, na wɔbɔɔ nnipa no amaneɛ sɛ, A asɛm bi a wɔaka dedaw no, ɛnyɛ den sɛ yɛbɛte aseɛ, na sɛ wo ne me bɛtena ase a, yɛto kasamfonin a ɛtwe adwene nyinaa gu yɛn mfitiaseɛ so. Sɛ wususuw ho anaa woka ho asɛm (again), ɛnneɛ (we will ask you) to point to us that very thick where we came to this baabi, that we may return ɛhɔ. Na nnipa no kaa sɛ, We can never do such a adeɛ again. Nea yɛayɛ yi ama yɛn abusuakuo no asɛe, yɛrenyɛ saa bio. Enti wɔyɛɛ amansan dawubɔ, bɔɔ dawuro kɔɔ nsunsuansoɔ sɛ, obiara nka nkyerɛ ɔfoforɔ baabi a ɔfiri, na nsɛm a wɔada no adi no ama kuro no asɛe. Ɛyi, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (ana) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi afa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a wode bɛkamfo me (asɛmka no.
