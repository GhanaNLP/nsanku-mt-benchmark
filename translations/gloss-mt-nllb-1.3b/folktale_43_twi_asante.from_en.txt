Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBƐBAA SƐ BERE A NWANSANA, NWANSENA NO, TENA KRAMAN, KRAMAN NO SO, ƆTEƐ NO SƐ, KAM! KAM!

Na Ɔbɔfoɔ bi wɔ nkraman nnan, baako din ne Sniff-sniff, foforɔ nso din ne Lick-lick, foforɔ nso din ne Tie-in-apɔ, na foforɔ nso din ne Mene kɛse-down. Na ɔde ne nkraman bɔ mpata. Daa, sɛ ɔkɔ nwura so a, na ɔne wɔn kɔ bɔ mu. Ɛda bi ɔkɔɔ nwura so na ɔhunuu kola-dua bi, na aduaba na ɛkata so wɔ ããã ahorow a ɛte sɛɛ mu. Afei ɔsan baa fie, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere amaneɛbɔ no sɛ, "Kola-tree bi a mehunuu no, wɔde aduaba akata so a ɛsɛn fam wɔ asaã ahorow a ɛte sɛɛ mu. " Ne yere no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na fa wɔn, efisɛ wunim sɛ yɛnni kola-nut baako mpo a yɛbɛwe na yɛntɔn. " Ɔbɔfoɔ no de nadwene kɔɔ ɔkwan so, nanso ɔgyaee nkraman no wɔ fie, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a kola-tree no wɔ. Afei ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔtwee kola-nuts no bi. Mprempren ɔtee wɔ baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ sɛ, "Twa! be! kukuru! kukuru! hu! pi! " Sasabonsam bi (stood) wɔ nanim. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Hwan na ɔyii me kola-nuts no? " Ɔɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Me. " Sasabonsam no kaa sɛ, "Mekum wo ntɛm ara wɔ adeɛ a woayɛ yi ho akawnto. " Ɔbɔɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wokum me a, ma menka mnkraman no ansa. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mfrɛ, nanso bere a woabɔ mmɔdenbɔ mprɛnsa sɛ wobɛfrɛ wɔn na wɔamma no, ɛnneɛ mekum wo. " Ɛnna ɔbɔfoɔ no maa ne nne so sɛ:

"Sniff-sniff, (come and) meet me, Lick-lick, (come and) meet me, Tie-in-apɔ, (come and) meet me, Mene kɛse fam, (come and) meet me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔwɔ akyiri ba!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔwɔ akyiri ba!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔwɔ akyiri ba!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔwɔ akyiri ba!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔwɔ akyiri ba!

Na nkraman a wɔwɔ fie hɔ no tee no dɛɛdɛɛ koraa, na wɔtutuu mmirika kɔɔ kuro no kuro akyi. Ɛneɛ na ɔbɔfoɔ no san maa ne nne so bio:

Sniff-sniff, (come and) meet me, Lick-lick, (come and) meet me, Tie-in-apɔ, (come and) meet me, Gulp-down, (come and) meet me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔwɔ akyiri ba!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔwɔ akyiri ba!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔwɔ akyiri ba!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔwɔ akyiri ba!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔwɔ akyiri ba!

Afei na nkraman no ate, wɔatu mmirika, prada! prada! Sasabonsam kae sɛ, Hwɛ bio, na ɛno wiee berɛ a ɛtɔ so mmiɛnsa. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no de nne kɛseɛ paa kaa sɛ:

Sniff-sniff, (come and) meet me, Lick-lick, (come and) meet me, Tie-in-apɔ, (come and) meet me, Gulp-down, (come and) meet me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔwɔ akyiri ba!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔwɔ akyiri ba!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔwɔ akyiri ba!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔwɔ akyiri ba!

Wai, wai, wai, ɛkyɛ na ɛreba! Na nano nsi hɔ koraa, berɛ a nkraman no wɔ nanim. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no reka sɛ, Sniff-sniff, ɛha ne Sasabonsam, sniff no. Na Sniff-sniff sniff no. Ɔkaa sɛ, Lick-lick, lick him over. Ɔkaa sɛ, Tie-in-apɔ, kyekyere no wɔ pɔ bi mu. Tie-in-apɔ kyekyeree ne menemmenem wɔ apɔ bi mu; na Sasabonsam no hwee aseɛ hɔ, kum! Ɛnna Mene kɛse no de no kɔɔ fam na wɔweɛwee no. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no ne nkraman no aboaboa kola-nuts no ano na wɔde aba fie. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no san baeɛ no, ɔyɛɛ ground-nut soup aduane a ɛyɛ ma, na ɔde siee hɔ maa nkraman no; ɔpete nsuo na ɔde siee hɔ, na ɔde kɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn sɛ ɔrekɔdi. Bere a odii dua wiei no, ɔkɔɔ abɔnten so kɔtenaa gyedua bi ase. Mprempren, ɔbɔmmɔfo no akoa, Nwansena, kɔhunuu aduane no (a wɔde ahyɛ ahoboa mu ma nkraman no), na ɔmaa so kɔtoo silin no so. Nkraman no firi abɔnten so baeɛ na na wɔn kɔm de wɔn. Lick-lick ka kyerɛɛ afoforɔ sɛ, "Mo ahunu nea yɛayɛ yɛn wura no, nanso ɛde besi nnɛ, wamma yɛn aduane mpo nni nti, yɛnkɔ kum no. " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Monni atoro, monni atoro. " Lick-lick firii adi kɔɔ abɔnten hɔ kɔdwee ɔbɔfoɔ no amu, na ɔne ɔbɔfoɔ no dii agorɔ; na Sniff-sniff nso baeɛ; na ɔhwee ɔbɔfoɔ no amu no; na ɔno nso kɔe. Ɛneɛ ɛmmereɛ a Tie-in apɔ bɛkyekyeree ne menem wɔ apɔ mu no, na Mene kɛse-down nso aba abɛdidi no. Mprempren nkraman no san baa fie; anka wɔrebɛhwɛ pato no mu, berɛ a wɔhunuu nkwan nsunsuansoɔ wɔ hɔ. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Fly, ebia na ɔbɔfoɔ no gyaee yɛn aduane na woadi anaa? " Fly kaa sɛ, "Me nhuu no wɔ baabiara. " Sniff-sniff no, berɛ a ɔmaa naniwa so no, ɔhunuu aduane a ɛda hɔ (wɔ silin no so). Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, father! yɛakum yɛn wura kwa, na ampa ɛwɔ hɔ aduane a na ɔde asie ama yɛn no, na ɛnyɛ baako, na ɛnyɛ mmienu, Nwansena na ɔde asie hɔ. " Nwansena no dwane firii hɔ kɔeɛ. Ɛno nti, sɛ kraman te hɔ a, na nwansena tena ne so a, na ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkyere no.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
