Ɔfaa aduane no bi de sii abofra no maame dan no silin so. Ɛnkyɛree biara, abofra no maame san fii ahohoro no bɔɔ no bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ɛhe na me abofra no wɔ? Ne yere a ɔyɛ nkabom no buae sɛ, Maka akyerɛ wo sɛ ɔne (ne) agya wɔ hɔ. Ɔbaa no wiee adidi no, na ɔkɔɔ ne kunu nkyɛn wɔ hɔ, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Mfa me abofra no mma me, efisɛ ɛnkyɛ na mehuu no da. Ɔbaa no san baa ne nkabom yere nkyɛn; ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Minnim want such-like; dɛn nti na woyɛ me agorɔ sɛ menyɛ abofra? " Ne nkabom yere no kae sɛ, "Ɛhe na wususuw sɛ mebɛnya sika afiri atɔ apataa a woaba abɛdi no? Wo abofra no deɛ, anɔpa awia hyerɛn wisiwis no mu no, mede no too mu; awia tuu wisiwis no, ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ menkɔyi no, nanso mehyɛɛ ano to mu; mehwehwɛɛ no a, na ɔdanee apataa, enti mefaa no na mede no yɛɛ aduane; ɛno ne amaneɛbɔ. " Abofra no maame kaa sɛ, "Meka akyiwadeɛ asɛm kɛseɛ sɛ, ɛfiri da yi kɔsi nnɛ, wo kanea wankasa fononoo na ma me nso memfa me hyew. " Ɛno nti na sɛ nkabom yernom baanu te fie biako a, biako wɔ ne nkuku, na biako nso wɔ ne nkuku.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
