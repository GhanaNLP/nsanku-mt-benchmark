Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

TWƐ HƆ NA WOBƐHU

NA ƐKƆƆ SO SƐ NA ɔbaa bi ne ne abofra te aseɛ. Bere a ɔresoma no (anywhere), na ɔka sɛ, I wont go. Na ne maame ka sɛ, Just wait there and you will see. Daa, bere a na ɔresoma no (anywhere), na ɔka sɛ, I wont go. Ɛnna na ne maame ka kyerɛ no sɛ, Just wait there and you will see. Ɛda koro bi na abofra no kaa sɛ, Daa, maame reka akyerɛ me sɛ, Just wait there and you will see. Ɛnnɛ ara na mefiri hɔ rekɔ hwehwɛ I-will-see. na mehwɛ. " Enti ofii hɔ. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na akutu dua bi a ɛsoa sika kɔkɔɔ a wɔabɔ no asinasin wɔ hɔ. Akutu no bisaa no sɛ, "Adɛn nti na wobaa ha bere a na obiara mma ha da? " Abofra no kae sɛ, "Me ne maame te hɔ daa; ɔkae sɛ, 'Twɛn hɔ na wobehu,' na mekaa sɛ mebɛba abɛhwehwɛ 'I-will-see', na mebɛhwɛ. " pea-tree no kae sɛ, "Meyɛ wɔn mu biako, nanso yɛn mu kɛse no wɔ akyiri. " Ɛnna abofra no san fii kwan so bio. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu Long-piece-of-nnyina a ɔreba abɛhyia no. Long-piece nnyina bisaa no sɛ, "Dɛn nti, bere a obiara mfaa saa kwan yi so da, afei woatam so? " Na abofra no buae sɛ, "Da biara na maame reka sɛ, 'Mebɛhu', enti mekae sɛ mereba 'I-will-see' hwehwɛ, na mebɛhwɛ. " Long-piece nnyina buae sɛ, "Meyɛ wɔn mu bi, nanso kɔ wanim. " Na abofra no san kɔɔ nakwan so bio. Na ɔrekɔ no, na Glowing-Sparks wɔ hɔ, na ɔda kuku a abubu sin bi so, na ɔreba ne nkyɛn. Ɔhyiaa no. Glowing-Sparks kae sɛ, Dɛn nti, bere a obiara mmɛba ha da no, woaba ha? Na abofra no buae sɛ, Bere a maame ne me te hɔ no, ɔse, Merebɛhu, enti mekae sɛ mebɛkɔ hwehwɛ I-will-see, wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ mebɛhwɛ so. Glowing-Sparks kae sɛ, Meyɛ wɔn mu biako, nanso kɔ wanim, efisɛ yɛn mu kɛse no da hɔ. Na abofra no san kɔɔ nakwan so bio. Na ɔrekɔ no, na Nkoo no de hwiirim nnyegyeeɛ reto fie bi so. Nkoo no kaa sɛ, Afei yɛbɛnya obi mogya de ato fie yi so. Afei bere a abofra no duu hɔ no, wɔkae sɛ, Adɛn nti, bere a obiara mmae ha da, wo ba ha nnɛ? Ɔbuaa sɛ, Me ne maame na me te daa; ɔkae sɛ, Montwɛn hɔ na mobehu, enti mekaeɛ sɛ mebɛkɔ hwehwɛ I-will-see, na mahwɛ so. Ɛneɛ na Nkoo no faa abofra no, na ɔtwaa ne ho. ad, na wɔgyee ne mogya guu kɔbere kuku mu; wɔgyee bi de too fie. Ɛneɛ na Nkoo no tu kɔtenaa dua tenten so, na ɛdii kan baeɛ na wɔde wɔn adua ahoroɔ guu mogya no mu, na wɔn adua ahoroɔ no yɛɛ kɔkɔɔ. Nkoo no nyinaa de wɔn adua ahoroɔ guu mu, bɛyɛɛ dwumadie no nkaedi ade. Ɛnneɛ, da bi, nkokɔ no mu baako kae sɛ ɔrekɔdi. Ɔkɔɔ kɔhyiaa abofra no maame. Na maame no bisaa Akoo no sɛ, Na me abofra a me ne no te no yɛ anihafoɔ te sɛ adeɛ biara. Sɛ na me sɔre a, anka mɛka sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔtwɛn hɔ na ɔbɛhu; ɔno deɛ, ɔte sɛ kwasea, excuse me ma me mfa asɛmfua no nka, ɔkae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ I-will-see no hwehwɛ mu, na ebia ɔbɛhwɛ. Ɛbɛyɛ da a ɛtɔ so nwɔtwe ni a na menhunu no, na me srɛ mo sɛ, sɛ moahu no a, momfa no mmra me nkyɛn. Akoo no kae sɛ, Oh, ɔbaa yɛn akuraa, na yɛn nso yɛkunkum no. Ne mogya no bi a yɛde kɔyɛɛ dwumadie no nkaeɛ no, ɛwɔ me dua yi so. Ɛno nti, sɛ wokɔ a, kɔka kyerɛ wo nnipa nyinaa sɛ, sɛ obi ka kyerɛ obi sɛ, Twɛn hɔ na wobehu a, na wato no nnome.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
