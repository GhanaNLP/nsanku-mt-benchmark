SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ ƆPƐTƐ NO DAA TE FI NTAM

NA Ɔpɛtɛ BI NE Kwaakwaadabi BI TENA HƆ DA BI, na Kwaakwaadabi no kae sɛ, Yɛnkɔ ntwitwa twene bi na yɛnni so agorɔ. Wɔhyɛɛ da kɔɔ nwura no mu, na wɔkɔtoo silk-cotton dua bi. Ɛduruu berɛ a Ɔpɛtɛ hunuu silk-cotton dua no, ɔkaa sɛ, Eyi yɛ me deɛ, na me deɛ, me ne ɔpanin, enti ɛsɛ sɛ metwa kɛseɛ yi. Kwaakwaadabi kaa sɛ, Me te. Kwaakwaadabi no nso, berɛ a ɔrehwɛ no, ɔhunuu Cedar dua bi a ɛsi hɔ no, ɔno nso kɔɔ hɔ kɔtwaa no. Wɔn mmienu nyinaa bɔɔ akyene. Bere a Ɔpɛtɛ bɔɔ ne twene no, na ɛnne no te sɛɛ ara:

Futuro! Futuro! Me nsa aka (me) ntɛm!

Ɔpɛtɛ Kwakye! Me nsa aka (me) ntɛm!

Kwaakwaadabi bɔɔ ne deɛ no, na ɛyɛ nne a ɛte sɛɛ:

Kwaakwaadabi, Kwa! Kwa!

Kwaakwaadabi, Kwa! Kwa!

Wɔkɔɔ so bɔɔ agorɔ no, na ɔsoro-nyame no tee, na ɔsomaa Akrɔma no sɛ ɔmmɛfrɛ wɔn; na Akrɔma no bae, na wɔgyee wɔn so sɛ "Berɛ a atwam! " Na wɔbuaa sɛ, "Ame! " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔsoro-nyame no ka sɛ ɔtee asa-nnwom bi wɔ ha, enti ɛsɛ sɛ wode ba na woto ma no te. " Na abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no de wɔn ani kyerɛɛ ɔsoro-nyame no anim, na wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ bɔɔ agorɔ, na wɔbɔɔ agorɔ, na wɔbɔɔ agorɔ, na ɔsoro-nyame no de wɔn ani kyerɛɛ ɔsoro-nyame no. aid, "I have heard that it is sweet; the two nnwom nnyigyei are sweet, therefore I present you (each) with something. " Ɛnna ɔsoro-nyame no kɔfaa nnaka mmienu no de sii hɔ, baako ɛho akyi yɛ fɛ paa sɛ obi bɛhu; dɔteɛ kɔsi sɛ ma sɛe te sɛɛ na ɛwɔ mu; foforɔ nso, akyideɛ paa; sika kɔkɔɔ kɔsi sɛ ma sɛe te sɛɛ na ɛwɔ mu. Ɔsoro-nyame no kasae, (ka sɛ), "Mo mmienu biara mmra na mofa. " Ɔpɛtɛ no kaa sɛ, "Me ne ɔpanin, enti ma me nkɔfa baako kan. " Bere a ɔkɔɔ no, ɔyii nea ɛyɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ paa, berɛ a Kwaakwaadabi nso faa nea ɛyɛ akyideɛ paa. Wɔmaa wɔn so kɔɔ wɔn akuraa. Bere a wɔrekɔ buee wɔn ano no, na Ɔpɛtɛ no, bere a ɔhwɛɛ ne mu no, atɛkyɛ fĩ. Kwaakwaadabi no mu no, berɛ a ɔhwɛɛ mu no, na sika kɔkɔɔ hyerɛn sɛ adeɛ biara. Ɛnkyɛree koraa na messenger bi baeɛ na ɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔpɛtɛ no sɛ, "Wɔsomaa me sɛ menka nkyerɛ sɛ wo maame mmara awu. " Ɔ (Ɔpɛtɛ) kaa sɛ, "Kwaakwaadabi, ma me sikakɔkɔɔ pow baako nkyekyere me basa no sɛ menkɔ ayie. " Ɔgyee sikakɔkɔɔ sin no kyekyeree no, na ɔkɔɔ ayie. Ɛduruu hɔ no, ɔkaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ ɔkɔhome ne ho, ɔhwee aseɛ, nanso berɛ a ɔrebɛsɔre no, ne sikakɔkɔɔ sin no hwee ase, na ɛyeraeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, Kwaakwaadabi agyapadeɛ no ayera, na ɛbɛyɛ dɛn na matumi asan aba fie? " Enti ɔhwee aseɛ kɔɔ fi no mu, na ɔhwehwɛɛ. Ɛno nti na wobɛhu Ɔpɛtɛ no, daa na ɔrebɔ ne ho, ɔrebɔ ne ho wɔ tiafi, efisɛ ɔrehwehwɛ Akraman no sika kɔkɔɔ de abrɛ no.

Ɛno nti na Mpanimfoɔ keka sɛ, "Anibereɛ nyɛ papa. "

Ɛyi, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (ana) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi afa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a wode bɛkamfo me (asɛmka no).
