Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

DƐN NTI NA APUPUO (NSUO A ƐYƐ FOFORO MUSSEL) A ƐMAA NE NSUO BƆƆ NO, ƐDA ASUBONTEN ANO

WƆKA SƐ Okawa, Opitire, 'Bonko, Mmɔtɔ, ne nsuo mu mmoa nyinaa (traa aseɛ) na ɔpɛ berɛ kɛseɛ bi bae na asubɔnten no nyinaa wee, na ɛmaa abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ yi amaneɛ kɛseɛ. Wɔda anwea so (wɔ asubonteɛ aseɛ). Ɛduruu tenten so no, wɔkae sɛ, "Saa mmusu a yɛahu yi, yɛnkɔ hɔ nkɔ Nyankopɔn, ɔsoro onyankopɔn nkyɛn, na yɛnsu frɛ no sɛ ɔmma yɛn osuo. " Ɛhɔ na abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no nyinaa kɔɔ so. Ɛnna Okawa maa ne nne so (na ɔtoo dwom):

" Ɛnyɛ me, Oka' Abena, na afei mekɔ ha, afei mekɔ hɔ?

Mehuruw na migyina me ananwɔwe so wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu frɛ Wo.

Metu aburobia ahweneɛ a ɛwɔ me nanpɔ so no.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔyɛ me yere, Abuya. "

Nanso osuo no anhwe ase. Na Opitire nso aba abɛto dwom:

" Ɛnyɛ me, Opitire Abena, na afei mekɔ ha, afei mekɔ hɔ?

Mehuruw na migyina me ananwɔwe so wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu frɛ Wo.

Metu aburobia ahweneɛ a ɛwɔ me nanpɔ so no.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔyɛ me yere, Abuya. "

Nanso osuo no anhwe ase. Na Bonko nso bae na ɔtoo dwom sɛ:

" Ɛnyɛ me, 'Bonko Abena, seisei mekɔ yi, seisei mekɔ hɔ?

Mehuruw na migyina me ananwɔwe so wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu frɛ Wo.

Metu aburobia ahweneɛ a ɛwɔ me nanpɔ so no.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔyɛ me yere, Abuya. "

Nanso osuo no anhwe ase. Na abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no nyinaa reba su, na Apupuo nko ara na na aka. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma me su kakra. " Wɔkae sɛ, " Firi adi, yɛn osu no anyɛ papa biara, na dɛn na wopɛ sɛ wode wankasa yɛ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma me su. " Nanso wɔbɔɔ no sɛ wɔaka dada akyerɛ no sɛ ɔnsu. Nanso Fresh-water mussel no kae sɛ, Mebɛsu. Enti wɔkae sɛ, Weep away. Na ɔde kɔɔ soro (the song):

" Ɛnyɛ me, Apupuo Abena, na afei mekɔ ha, afei mekɔ hɔ?

Mehuruw na migyina me ananwɔwe so wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu frɛ Wo.

Metu aburobia ahweneɛ a ɛwɔ me nanpɔ so no.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔyɛ me yere, Abuya. "

Na Ewiem-onyankopɔn bɔɔ naprannaa mu, koo! Na abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no nyinaa reka sɛ, "Kɔ so, kɔ so. " Na ɔsan teɛɛ mu bio, na Ewiem-onyankopɔn bɔɔ ne tirim dɛɛdɛɛ. Na afei abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no de no kɔɔ wɔn mmatiri so sɛ, "Kɔ so bio. " Na ɔsan nso maa saa ara. Na afei Ewiem-onyankopɔn yii nnyesoɔ no adi (osuo no), na ɛbɔɔ fam, ta! ta! ta! ta! na nsuka no nyinaa yiri faa so. Afei yi (now that) sɔreeeɛ, na ɛhyɛɛ twom! kɔɔ nsuo no mu (as they jumped in). Ɛyɛɛ abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no nyinaa na ɛkɔɔ mu. Apupuo (Fresh-water mussel), berɛ a na ɔbɛhyɛ nsuo no mu no, na wɔtutu no akyi (by the ahomegyeɛ)), na ɔda asuo no sikakorabea. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Apupuo, a ɛmaa ne asubɔnten guu, ɛda sikakorabea so. " Eyi, me abasɛm a meka ho asɛm yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
