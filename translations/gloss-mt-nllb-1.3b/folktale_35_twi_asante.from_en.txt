Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐNNƐ NTI NA MPANIMFOƆ KYERƐ SƐ YƐNNSAN NKYERƐ ANADWO MU AHOTOSOƆ

WƆKA SƐ (berɛ bi a atwam no) na Nyankopɔn Kwame, ɔsoro-nyame, asiesie afuo kɛseɛ bi, na wadua nkruma, gyeene, adua, turo-nnua, ɛmo, ne akutu. Turo no mu nwira bɛyɛɛ den na honhon nyin. Ewiem onyankopɔn no de dawurubɔ bɔɔ nsunsuansoɔ no wɔ gong-gong mu sɛ ne afuw no ayɛ nea nwira ahyɛ mu ma na obiara a ɔbɛtumi atetew no a ɔremmɔ ne ho akuturuku no mmra na ɔmfa ne babaa, Abena Nkroma (kyerɛ sɛ Abena yɛ ba a ɔtɔ so nkron) no nkɔ awareɛ mu. Ɔno deɛ, berɛ a ɔkɔeɛ (to try), ɔbɔɔ ne ho (where the honhon tickled), na wɔbɔɔ no ntwitwa; ɔno deɛ ɔkɔeɛ na wɔbɔɔ no ntwitwa; mmarima nyinaa kɔeɛ (and tried); wɔdii nkuguo. Mprempren Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Me deɛ, metumi. Mprempren na ɔsoro-Onyankopɔn afifideɛ no da kwan no nkyɛn, na saa kwan no na nnipa fa so kɔ dwaso wɔ Yawda biara. Mprempren Ananse no, esiane sɛ na ɔnim nokwasɛm yi nti, na ɔkɔpopa nwira no kɛkɛ wɔ Fida biara. Na berɛ a ɔrebue nano, na nnipa a wɔretwam no akyea no sɛ, Hail to you at your adwuma, Agya Ananse! no, na ɔbua sɛ, Thank you, Aku. Wɔkɔɔ so sɛ, Afuw bi a obiara antumi ansiesie, (wose woreweeding) no? Ananse no bebua sɛ, Ah, ɛyɛ abaayewa baako nti na mebrɛ me ho ase yi nyinaa. Ne basa baako te sɛɛ. " Na ɔbɛbɔ no na wapete ne basa no wɔ baabi a ɛrebɔ no, na sɛ ɔyɛ saa a, ɔbɛnya ahotɔ afiri abufuhyew no mu. Obi foforɔ nso bɛtwa mu bio na wagye no wɔ nadwuma ho; na ɔbɛfa ne nsa abɔ no wɔ baabi a ɛrebɔ no. Sɛ nhwɛsoɔ no, sɛ ne sere no, na ɔbɛka sɛ, "Saa abaayewa sigyafoɔ no, wɔka sɛ ne sere te sɛɛ" (slap and rubbing his own). Saa ɔkwan yi so na ɔwiee afuw no dwiri. Afei ɔkɔɔ sɛ ɔbɛka kyerɛɛ Ewiem-onyankopɔn sɛnea wawie ne afuo no dɔw. Ɔsoro-nyame bisaa messenger no sɛ, "Wowiei ampa? " messenger no kaa sɛ, "Aane. " Ɔsoro-nyame bisaa no sɛ, "Wowee wo ho anaa? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dabi, wowee wo ho. " Ɔsoro-nyame no faa Abena Nkroma de no maa Kwaku Ananse sɛ ɔnyɛ no awareɛ. Anadwo koro bi na Kwaku Ananse ne ne ayeforɔ no kɔdaeɛ, na ayeforɔ no bisaa no sɛ, "Nanso na ɛyɛ wo a wofiri nnipa nyinaa mu na wotumi yii agya afuw a nwira wɔ mu, afuw a obiara kɔɔ so, danee nakyi, nanso woatumi ayi yi? " Ɛnna Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Wofa no sɛ meyɛ kwasea anaa? Na mewe hohoro, na sɛ obi fa nkyɛn na obisa me sɛ, 'Ananse, woreyi afuo a obiara ntumi nyi no,' na afei me de me nsa bɔ baabi a me wedeɛ ho yɛ me yaw no, na mekyea no, na meka kyerɛ onipa no sɛ, sɛ nhwɛsoɔ no, na wo sere te sɛ ɛkoɔ sere, na ɛyɛ fɛ na ɛyɛ fɛ. Saa na ɛbaa sɛ metumi yii no. " Ɛnna Abena, ba a ɔtɔ so nkron no, kae sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ kɔsi ɔkyena mɛka akyerɛ agya sɛ woabɔ wo ho akuturuku. " Ananse no kasa kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛnsɛ sɛ woka ho asɛm, eyi yɛ sleep-mat confidence. " Abena, ba a ɔtɔ so nkron no, kae sɛ, "Minnim hwee koraa wɔ sleep-mat confidences ho, na mebɛka. " Abena Nkroma faa ne sle eping-mat kɔeɛ (ɛfiri Kwaku Ananse nkyɛn) na ɔkɔɔda dan no awieeɛ foforɔ. Mprempren Kwaku Ananse ani abere (a ɛyɛ awerɛhow), na ɔkɔfaa ne bentaa, ɔbɔɔ ahoma ahorow no na ɔtoo dwom sɛ:

"Abena, the ba a ɔtɔ so nkron, this is not a asɛm to quarrel about.

Momma yɛmfa no sɛ ahotoso a wɔde bɛda mpa so.

Ɔkae sɛ, 'Dabi.

Ɔwɔ asɛm bi (tia me), nanso obi foforɔ wɔ asɛm bi a ɛrekɔ kwan so dada. "

Ɛnna Ananse no sɔreeɛ, na ɔkɔɔ daeɛ. Bere a Kwaku Ananse daeɛ hɔ berɛ bi akyi no, afei ɔsɔreɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abena Nkroma"not a nnyigyei (save the dede of the babadua su) dinn! Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Me nsa aka wo. " Ɔfaa kora ketewa bi, na ɔde nsuo guu mu, na ɔhwieeɛ guu Abena Nkroma mpa so. Ɛneɛ Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ daeɛ. Bere a ɔdaa hɔ kakra akyi no, ɔkae sɛ, "Ko! Abena Nkroma, eyi deɛn? Woayɛ mpa so nsuo, wo abɔdeɛ a wunni aniwuo, akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho sɛ woyɛ fɛ koraa; sɛ nneɛma bɛda adi a, mebɛka akyerɛ obiara. Ɛyɛɛ nokware, ɛnneɛ (nea wɔn nyinaa kaeɛ) no, na obiara a ɔbɛkɔ saa afuw no mu no, na ɔka sɛ, 'A abaayewa a ɔnom nsuo!'. Menkɔ clear honhon afuo (for such a onipa). '" Ɛnna Abena ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Me srɛ wo, gyae, na ma asɛm no ntwa. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Me remfi hɔ, me asɛm na ɛdii kan; wokae sɛ wobɛka no kyerɛ wo agya; mekae sɛ, 'Gyae', wokae sɛ, 'Dabi'. Ɛno nti, me remfi asɛm no mu. " Na Abena, ba a ɔtɔ so nkron no, kaa sɛ, "Gyae me asɛm no, na wo asɛm no nso, meka ho asɛm. ok, mebɛgyae no; efisɛ sɛ woagyae me deɛ a, me aniwa bɛwu aniwu. Ɛnna Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Mewɔ aso sɛ, sɛ wopɛ saa a, ma ɛyɛ nna-mat confidence, enti asɛm no gyae hɔ. Saa na mpanimfoɔ no bae sɛ wɔbɛka sɛ Sleeping-mat confidences are not repeated.

Ɛyi, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (ana) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi afa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a wode bɛkamfo me (asɛmka no.
