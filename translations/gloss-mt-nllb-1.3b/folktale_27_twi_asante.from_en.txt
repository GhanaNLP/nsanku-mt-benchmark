Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

WƆ ABUSUAKUO MU NO, ADWENE ONIPA NEA BIRIBIARA NNI MU KORAA NNI HƆ

NA ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔwoo mmofra baanan. Wɔn mu panyin no yɛ kwasea a ne kɛse te sɛ biribiara. Na onnim sɛnea ɔbɛyɛ biribi baako pɛ, enti ne anuanom ne ne nuammaa kae sɛ, "Yɛbɛtumi atena ne nkyɛn, enti momma ɔmfi fie ha. " Entiexcuse me sɛ mefa asɛmfua (in the mbaeɛ of any of you elders) di dwuma) sɛ, saa kwasea no kɔsii atenaeɛ maa ne ho na ɔkɔtenaa hɔ. Ɛwɔ saa atenaeɛ no, na anwea nko ara na ɛwɔ hɔ. Ɔyɛɛ asensene na ɔde gyegye ne ho. Aberewa (i. e. the mother) ne ne mmofra no tenaa hɔ. Ɔsɔre kɔɔ afuw no mu da bi, na ɔhunuu sɛ abofra bi te bon mu wɔ dua bi mu. Ɔse, "Hwan na ɔwɔ hɔ? " Nanso abofra no mmua no. Ɔbaa no kɔɔ hɔ, na ɔnyaa hwɛbea pa wɔ abofra no ho, ɔfaa ne nsa, na ɔkae sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔ fie. " Na abofra no sɔre, na ɔdii nakyi, na ɔbɛduruu (wɔn) fie. Ɔbaa no ka kyerɛɛ ne mmofra no sɛ, Hwɛ abofra fɛfɛ a minyae no, ma me Sekkan na metwa ne ti nhwi tenten. Afei na ɔbaa no nnim sɛ abofra no yɛ nnipa nketewa no mu biako, na ɛno nti na na ne ti nwi no nni fɛ saa. Enti ɔgyee Sekkan no na ɔtwaa ne ti nwi a ɛwɔ etire no so, na ɔtoo guu hɔ. Bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi (i. e. anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ), berɛ a wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔnni, ɔkaa sɛ ɔrenni, na wɔbisaa no sɛ, "Ɛdɛn asɛm na ɛwɔ hɔ? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Sɛ woampɛ sɛ wode wo ho kɔhyɛ ɔhaw mu a, fa me etire ti nwi no san ba na fa bɔ me bio. " Ɔbaa no ne ne mmofra no kɔɔ kɔgyee amamfoɔ a ɛkamfam wɔ rubber-tree no mu, na wɔde hyɛɛ fairy no etire no so, na wɔgyee ne ti nwi no nso hyɛɛ no. Mmoatia no faa ne nsateaa ahodoɔ no de faa ne ti nwi mu, yii no, na ɔtwee guu hɔ. Bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, na ɛyɛ sɛ, "Momma me etire ti nwi no mmra. " Berɛ a na nneɛma nni hɔ bio no, na ɛyɛ sɛ, "Momma me etire ti nwi no mmra. " Na afei wɔn ho ayɛ mmerɛw na wɔayɛ nketewa paa (with adwendwen ne dadwen). Ɛda bi, abofra a na ɔyɛ kwasea no kae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhu ne maame. Ɛduruu baeɛ no, na (behold) ne maame ne ne anuanom ne ne nuammaa ayɛ dɛɛdɛɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛɛ dɛn na woayɛ mmerɛw saa? " Na maame no rebɛka neenti ankasa a enti na wɔn ho ayɛ mmerɛw no, bere a ne mmofra no kaa sɛ, "Nka nkyerɛ no, oyi ne kwasea no, bere bɛn na ɔnim adwene? " Na maame no kaa sɛ, "Mebɛkasa. " Bere a ɔka kyerɛɛ no no, ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, fa mmoatia no mmra na memfa no nkɔ me akuraa. " Na maame ne anuanom ne nuammaa no de mmoatia no kɔɔ d ne etire ti nwi na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔrekɔ kwasea akuraa. Bere a wɔduruu hɔ no, wɔ ne akuraa, ɔhyɛɛ mmoatia no sɛ, "Monkɔtena me asensene so na monnye mo ani kakra. " Ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔtenaa so, na ɔdwirii no, na ɔmaa ne dwom yi so:

"Maame woo me, abofra kwasea, Hurotom.

Agya woo me, abofra kwasea, Hurotom.

Maame woo me, a kwasea kɛkɛ, Hurotom. Maame woo me, a kwasea kɛkɛ, Hurotom.

Maame woo me, abofra kwasea, Hurotom.

Maame woo kwasea kɛkɛ, Hurotom. "

Mmoatia no bɔɔ agorɔ no kɔsii sɛ ɔbrɛɛ, afei abofra kwasea no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Yɛnkɔfa wo etire ti nwi. Ɔne Mmoatia no duu fie, ɔno (Mmoatia no) kaa sɛ, Fa me etire ti nwi no mmra na fa si ananmu na fa me kɔ. Na abofra kwasea no nso kaa sɛ, Wo nso, me anwea a wotutu no, ɛsɛ sɛ wode nsɛnkyerɛnne no nyinaa kata so ansa na me ne wo etire ti nwi no abɔ. Mmoatia no kɔɔ so ara. out the dwumadi; ntɛm ara na ɔde nsɛnkyerɛnne no mu biako kataa so (made by her anan) than, tiatia so, she made another. Now, try as she would, she couldnt erase all her anammɔntena. Ɔkaa sɛ, Agya, mempɛ manso biara, enti mema wo maame no kɔ, na me deɛ, fa me san kɔ me ara me ho, na ma me nkɔ. Na abofra a na ɔyɛ kwasea no kaa sɛ, Afei kɔ, na me nso mefa me maame (out of your tumi). Afei maame no baa bɛtoo nkotodwe kaa sɛ, Meda wo ase, na sesei (I realize that) adeɛ a ɛte sɛ kwasea nni abusuakuo no mu. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, sɛ wowɔ mmofra, mummua mo aniwa, na monhwɛ wɔn yie, na monnim sɛ daakye berɛ bi a ɔhaw da bɛba a, (one of them) bɛba abɛhwɛ mo.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
