SƐNEA KWATIMA, ODWENNINI NO, NYAA ODWANINI DIN

NA Odwennini bi, Kwatima, te hɔ, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Mekɔ hwehwɛ asase bi a mɛsi fie wɔ so. " Na Daboɔ no nso, ɛwɔ berɛ korɔ no ara mu, na ɔwɔ adwene korɔ no ara pɛpɛɛpɛ. Ɛnna Kwatima, Odwennini no, sii gyinaeɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwɛ asase a ɔpɛ no; na ɔtee, ahyɛdeɛ ne sɛ ɔbɛsi fie. Na Osebo, Daboɔ no, nso kɔeɛ (na bere a ɔduruu baabi a na Odwennini no ayi no), ɔkae sɛ, "Ayamye me! Saa akuraa a na mebɛsi no, nanso menyɛ ho adwuma da, na me nananom nsamanfo ahyɛ aseɛ dedaw. " Na ɔno nso, ɔyɛɛ petee ketewa na ɔkɔɔ. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma fii aseɛ pueeɛ (i.e. anɔpatutuutuutu) no, Odwennini no san kɔɔ bio. Bere a ɔduruu baabi no, ɔkae sɛ, "Ayamye me! Of a nokware my nananom nsamanfo rebɛboa me, (hwɛ) fam a wɔapopa no. " Na ɔno nso, wapopa nwura no kɔeɛ. Na Daboɔ no nso baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh! eyi yɛ asɛm bi; ɛkame ayɛ sɛ ɛyɛ me hu. " Na ɔno nso, ɔkɔɔ nwura no so; ɔtwaa adunsina na ɔde too hɔ (wɔ beaeɛ hɔ). Na Odwennini no nso baeɛ, na ɔno nso hwɛɛ kwan tenten wɔ ahodwiriw mu. Ɔno nso kɔɔ nwura no so, kɔtwaa nhahamama, na ɔtoo guu hɔ kɔeɛ. Na Daboɔ no san baeɛ. Ɔno nso hwɛɛ kyɛree (nkɔsoɔ a ɛbaa no), na ɔhyɛɛ fie hɔ kɔeɛ. Kwatima san baeɛ, na ɔtwaa akoturo no na ɔde adunsina no hyɛɛ mu, na ɔkɔɔeɛ. Na Daboɔ no san baeɛ, na ɔkyekyeree nnua a ɛtwam no, na ɔkɔɔeɛ. Na bio Kwatima baeɛ, na ɔkɔboaa sare no sii hɔ kɔeɛ. Na Daboɔ baeɛ bio, na ɔde atɛkyɛ no yɛɛ plaster no; na ɔkɔeɛ. Na bio Kwatima baeɛ na ɛdan atifi kata no, na ɔkɔeɛ. Na bio Daboɔ baeɛ, na ɛbɔɔ fam ahodoɔ no, na ɛkɔeɛ. Ɛnna Kwatima nso baeɛ, na ɔyii dɔte fitaa no yɛɛ plaster (afasuo no), na ɔkɔeɛ fie. Berɛ a ɔkɔeɛ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne abofra no sɛ, "Ɛnnɛ da a mewie fie no, na Akwasidae tɔ a, ɛwɔ (following) Monday no na yɛbɛyɛ ofie foforɔ hyɛ ase afahyɛ, enti monhwehwɛ nnipa a wɔwɔ atuo sɛ wɔkɔto dan no mu. " Ɛnneɛ na Daboɔ no nso baeɛ (na ɔhunuu fie a awie no). Ɔkaa sɛ, "Eh! Me nni hwee ka, gye sɛ mebɛkɔ akɔgye me agyapadeɛ na san ba. " Ɛnna ɔkɔɔ, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ ne abofra no sɛ, "Tu mmirika ntɛm ara sɛnea wubetumi na kɔka kyerɛ Honhom-Hunter no sɛ, Adae bɛtɔ Kwasiada a, Memeneda (a ɛdi hɔ) no na mɛdi ofie foforɔ hyɛ ase afahyɛ, enti ma ɔmmɛto tuo mma me. " Na ne abofra no kɔɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ no. Ɔ (the ghost-hunter) said, "I have heard. " Ɛneɛ da a ɛdi Adae no anim no, Kwatima boaboaa ne agyapadeɛ ano, na ɔde kɔtoo hɔ wɔ fie hɔ, na ɔsan baeɛ. Na Daboɔ no nso boaboaa ne deɛ ano, na ɔde kɔtoo hɔ. Berɛ a ɛduruu sɛ yɛbɛhunu nneɛma no, Akwasidae no so, Kwatima twaa ho hyiaeɛ kyerɛɛ ne nnipa a ɔne wɔn te no yie; na Daboɔ no nso yɛɛ ne nkrabew. Ɛdwoada no, berɛ a nneɛma pueeɛ (i.e. anɔpatutuutu) no, Kwatima ne ne abofra no kɔɔ (ofie foforɔ no mu); afei ɔkɔtoo tuo guu dan no mu. Ɛnna Daboɔ no nso bae; ɔtee tuo no, ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛnnɛ da no nyɛ da pa, ɛnnɛ da no ahonhom no rebɔ atuo. " Na ɔno nso bae; na Spirit-hunter no nso kɔɔ fie hɔ, na ɔmaa tuo no suanee sɛ, pomm! Daboɔ no bae na ɔhunuu Kwatima ne ne abofra no te hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo eguadwa ne deɛn? " Kwatima nso kaa sɛ, "Wo nso, wo eguadwa ne deɛn? " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Wo nni sianteɛ a, me nso me nni bi. " Na Kwatima kaa sɛ, "Sɛ saa na ɛteɛ a, ɛnneɛ yɛn mmienu nyinaa bɛtena ha. " Enti wɔtenaa hɔ. Da biara, sɛ Daboɔ sɔre a, na ɔkɔ ahayɔ, na ɔsan de nam ba. Na ɛte saa daa. Nanso Kwatima deɛ, na ɔmfa hwee mma. Ɛnna da koro bi a osurofoɔ no kɔɔ nwura no mu no, ɔbɔɔ ne ho mmɔden paa sɛ ɔbɛyi biribi, nanso ankosi hwee. Ɔsan baa fie, na ɔkaa sɛ, Kwatima, sɛ to-da woankɔ nwura no mu na woamfa nam bi ansan aba a, nea me ne wo bɛyɛ yi, worempɛ. Kwatima de nadwene sii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ nwura no mu, na ɔsan de nwura-buck bi baeɛ na ɔtoo hɔ. Daboɔ no hunuu aboa no a, ɛyɛɛ no nwanwa sɛdeɛ adeɛ biara nni hɔ. Berɛ a nneɛma pueeɛ (i.e. anɔpatutuutu) no, Kwatima kɔeɛ, na ɔsan de foforɔ (antelope) baeɛ. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "E! " Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne abofra no sɛ, "Hwɛ, me rekɔ nwura no so, na me nso me ne Kwatima rekɔ, na ɔtwa ba a mede kɔmaa wo da a ɛtwaa mu no, sɛ yɛakɔ a, frɛ Kwatima ba no na bisa no sɛ, 'Sɛ wo agya kɔ nwura so kɔyi nam a, kyerɛ yɛn wɔ ɔtwee yi so sɛnea ɔyɛ.' " Na nnipa no (Osebo ne Kwatima) de wɔn ho kɔeɛ. Na akɔre ba no kaa sɛ, "Kwatima ba, bra na yɛnni agorɔ. " Ɔbaa no, ɔkae sɛ, "Sɛ agya kɔ nwura so a, saa na ɔkyere agorɔ. " Na ɔhwee aseɛ, na ɔkyeree ɔtwa ba no kyeree ne menem. Kwatima ba no kaa sɛ, "Wo kɔ fa ɔbotan. " Akraman ba no kɔfaa baako. Ɔde sii hɔ. Kwatima ba no twee ne ho fii ho na ɔbaa abɛto ɔboɔ no etire atifi, pim! na ɛbubuu nsinnsin. Tiger ba no kaa sɛ, "Ah. " Na wɔn agyanom firi nwura no mu bae, na Tiger ba no kaa nea asi no kyerɛɛ ne agya, na Kwatima ba no nso kaa nea asi no kyerɛɛ ne agya. Ɛhɔ na na wɔwɔ, berɛ a da koro bi biribi bɛkaa Kwatima nan ho, na ɔrebɛpopa no, nanso Daboɔ no susui sɛ ɔsan nakyi wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ ɔbɛkum no. Ntɛm ara na Daboɔ no de ahyɛdeɛ tuu mmirika baa Kwatima so sɛ wɔrebɛkyere no. Na Kwatima dwanee kiri, kiri, kiri. Ɔkɔɔ asuwea bi a ɛda hɔ a na Ɔnanka bi wɔ mu. Kwatima kaa sɛ, "Agya Ɔnanka, meguan kɔ wo nkyɛn sɛ mepɛ bammɔ na Daboɔ ankyere me. " Ɛhɔ na Ɔnanka kaa sɛ, "Wo, yɔnkoɔ, woadi hokwan (ba me nkyɛn), enti fa me nsa. " Kwatima bɔɔ mmɔden de ne nsa bɔɔ no. Saa na Kwatima bɛgyee Odwa-nini din (lit. Shake nsa with the Ɔnanka). Ɛfiri ne nsamusukyea a ɔne Ɔnanka bɔeɛ no.

Ɛyi, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (ana) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi afa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a wode bɛkamfo me (asɛmka no.
