Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBƐBAA SƐ KWAKU ANANSE, A ƐYƐ ANANSE NO, KƆƆ SORO WƆ APRANNAA NO SO

ANANSE O! ɔwaree Nsia babaa, Ewiem-onyankopɔn maame, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ Niamey (Ewiem-onyankopɔn) sɛ, "Ɛda a wo maame Nsia bɛwu no, bra bɛka ɛda a wopaw ma ayi amanneɛ no kyerɛ me. "

Ɛbɛyɛ nnansa akyi na wɔbaa bɛka kyerɛɛ no sɛ Nsia, Ewiem onyankopɔn maame no, awu. Mprempren Kwaku Ananse mogya-abusua ne Berekuo, King Jay, Ɔkwakuo, Kwaadu ne Odwan, Odwan. Ɛnna Kwaku Ananse bɛkasa kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, Wɔba abɛka ayi amanneɛ bi kyerɛ me, enti ɛsɛ sɛ mo ne me kɔ. Enti Kwaku Ananse maa wɔde nkra kɔmaa Ewiem onyankopɔn sɛ ɔreba dwumadie no ase. Ɛnna ɔsoro-nyame no maa ne adesuadeɛ nyinaa boaboaa ano, te sɛ Omanhene asɛmbli. Na Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Sɛ anwummere dwo a, mɛba. " Ananse no yɛɛ kotokuo, na emu na ɔde Berekuo, King Jay, Ɔkwakuo, Kwaadu, ne Odwan, Odwan no sii hɔ, na ɔde kotokuo no too nabatiri so, na ɔkyekyeree ne ntoma no so. Afei ɔkasa kyerɛɛ abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no sɛ, "Sɛ meduru ɔsoro-nyame nkyɛn a, ɛsɛ sɛ mo su. " Berɛ a ɔduruu ɔsoro-nyame anim no, Berekuo no maa ne nne so sɛ:

"Kurukusu, kuw! kuw! kuw!

Kurukusu, kuw! kuw! kuw! nI. "

Na Kwaadu de ne nne kɔɔ soro sɛ:

"Kakum! "

Na Odwan de ne nne kɔɔ soro sɛ:

"Me ne no! "

Na eyi ne Kwaku Ananse awerɛhow. Na nnipa no nyinaa reka sɛ, "Woayɛ yie, ha na ɔbarima bi a ɔtumi su na ɔdi awerɛhoɔ wɔ akwan ahodoɔ mmiɛnsa so. " Bere a owiee (ne awerɛhow) no, ɔsoro-nyame no hwehwɛɛ ofie fɛfɛ bi sɛ ɔbɛtena mu. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔsoro-nyame no noa aduan pa na ɔde maa Kwaku Ananse. Bere a Kwaku Ananse huu saa aduane yi, ɔkae sɛ, "Me remfa biara mma 'Berekuo, me remfa biara mma Ɔkwakuo, me remfa biara mma Odwan, efisɛ me mmuadadi berɛ tenten. " Enti Kwaku Ananse dii, wamma ne mogya-abusua no biara. Ɛnneɛ, ɔne wɔn kɔɔ so kɔɔ so, na ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Yɛbɛyɛ da no ayiyɛ. " Nanso Odwan kaa (kyerɛ Kwaku Ananse) sɛ), "Ɛkɔm rekum me, menkɔ;" Ɔkwakuo ne "Berekuo kaa adeɛ koro. Afei ɔsoro-nyame no kɔɔtenaa nnipa a wɔahyia no ntam, na ɔmaa wɔn kɔbɔɔ Kwaku Ananse amaneɛ sɛ, fɛfɛɛfɛ adeɛ a ɔyɛɛ no da a ɛtwaa mu no, ɔbɛtumi ayɛ no bio, na ɔne ne mpanimfoɔ no aboa ano. Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "Me ho nyɛ me dɛ. " Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, ɔsoro nyame no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, "Woama maniwa ato me aniwu. " Na Berekuo, ne Ɔkwakuo, ne Odwan kae sɛ, Yɛn nso, woama yɛn kɔm, enti yɛn ne wo bɛtetew mu afiri abusuakuw no ho. Enti Ɔkwakuo ne Berekuo kɔɔ nwura mu, nanso Odwan, Odwan no, kɔɔ so traa fie; na Ananse huruw hwee aprannaa no so; ɛno na ɛyɛ sɛ na ɔfɛre ade.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi san ba me nkyɛn.
