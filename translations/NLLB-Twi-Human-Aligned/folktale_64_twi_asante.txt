ANANSE O! had married Nyankonpon's son Nsia, and he cried out to God, "As long as your son Nsia dies, then bless me". And three days later he said Nyankonpon's son Nsia died. And Ananse's family was Berekuo, Kwakuo, and Odwan. And Ananse said he cried yen, "Y'aba has blessed me, so your sons won't let me go". And Ananse gave him his voice to see Nyankonpon saying he has blessed me. And Nyankonpon cried on his face to say, "Ananse has blessed me". Ananse said, "Anansi has blessed me." Ananse cried and he said, "Berekuo, Kwakuo, and Odem", and he burned it, and he threw his clothes on his face. He caught the animals and said, "When I reach Nyankonpon, water". When he reached Nyankonpon, Berekuo said:

"Kurukusu, die! die! die!

Kurukusu, die! die! die! here.

Kwakuo added:

"Kakum!"

And Odwan added:

"Me!"

Kwaku Ananse cried. People stood and said, "Your father; 'people's father is three. " Finally, Nyankonpon gave him 'home so he could live.' The next day Nyankonpon gave him good food to Ananse. Kwaku Ananse cried, "My mother, my mother Kwakuo, my mother Kwakuo, my mother Odwan, and I'm old enough to be arrested". And Ananse cried, giving his family the food. And when he heard about it, Nyankonpon said, "We've never eaten before. " And Odwan said, "Okomkom e'ku me, my mother. " Kwaku Ananse said, "Berekuo sa sa sa". Ananse said, "I am sick." Adeekyeye Nyankonpon said to Ananse, "Thank you in my face". And 'Berekuo, Kwakuo, and Odwan said, "We're the reason you've been drinking yen, so he's your father abused you".
