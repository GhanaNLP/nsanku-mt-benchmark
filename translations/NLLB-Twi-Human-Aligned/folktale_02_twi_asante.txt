MPITRIPI AND MPATRAPA

MPITIRIPI and Mpatrapa, and Anyankama Kyerebi, and Kyerebi-Koso-Adawurampon, worshiped thirty women.

Ananse says, My side is better? And say, Your side says, Mpitiripi and Mpatrapa and Anyankama Kyerebi and Kyerebi-Koso-Adawurampon bow before women thirty, and says, Nobody touches sahuro sahuro

That is to say, when you see Ananse and go to the palace, the soul that walks on the ground is called Koto.

In my own words, say yes, say no, just take some and give some to me.
