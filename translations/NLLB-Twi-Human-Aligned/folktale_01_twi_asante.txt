SEYOYE A OBONTO YA NAMAM KWA

WE say that Grandmother is rich, and her things are beautiful, and she has given water to drink and drink. When Okoto is there, he says, A, thank you our faces, so that your brother will arrest you and your brother will cry and say: come and take your bed, and we will stand and weep, and he will not cry for her, she is the one who cried. He has called the father to come; Okoto cries, weep, weep; Mobyonse will cry; Okawa will cry; Okawa will cry; Oyoyo will cry; Otidie will cry; Obonko will cry; Akorese will cry; Opitire will cry; Okobo will cry; water service will not stand and cry. And later Obonto Yabo cried, you have not cried, you have not cried, and Obonto Yabo cried, and he also cried: Obonto Yabo cried and he cried. And Grandma the Elder said, Me tweet you. And the animals stood and said, Yennie is drunk, and you have lowered our faces. And we kissed her.

Ene says that when the children fight, we will wake up and Obonto Ya will walk here and here.

In my own words, say yes, say no, just take some and give some to me.
