AGYA ANANSE is the founder of Afudotwedotwe, Nyiwan-konfwea, Tikonokono. Ananse said to the yen, "You are the only ones who can feed me when I eat vegetables." These plants grow up; eat meat, meat, meat, and fight against some of the vegetables, and the plants crumble. When the child of the tree heard, he said, How is it? The crow said, Aa. And he cried and cried; and he asked us again, How is it? The crow said, Bring your head down and cry. He nodded, and cried, and his head spoke to the ground, kill!

He laughed, "What is it?

He laughed, "What is it?

That is why the elders say, "If someone falls down, kiss them, and laugh.
