OBOFUO Kwaku and his wife, Buruwa, were poor and had nothing to eat. When Farfar Kwaku's uncle fought in the field, he killed him. When he came home, he said, Oyi believes he did not believe him. One day, he killed him. His wife saw the money, and asked him, And you don't give money, you don't give meat to children, and you did better? He said, " Tell me, when you die, I will die. " One day he killed a farmer, and the house was on the road, and he went back to his house, he killed him, and he told him he had put the money in his house, and he drank it. He said to his wife. " He said, " Ya Ekudonto. " He said, " Where are you taking me now? " He said, " Get your mind off. " " Anger was not in your head. " On the other hand, on the following day, his wife, Kofufou, came to visit him; he said, " Madwo o! " He said, " Ya Buru. " Buru said, " Where are you taking me now? " Obo said, " Get your anger off. " Ene said ebeba 'manm' said, " Ya Kuronto," and " Ya Buru ".
