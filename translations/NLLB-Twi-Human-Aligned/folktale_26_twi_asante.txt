We say, say no

APUPUO A OBOO NASUO, DA NSUKAKYE

WE say Okawa, Opitire, Bonko and the water is not enough to keep the animals alive and water is not enough to keep the animals from suffering. We sleep on the sand. Then we say, Amane that we have found, let us not forget about Nyankonpon and let us forget about the salt to give us water. The water is not enough to keep the animals alive. Okawa added:

My name is Oka Abena, apimpim-a-ko-ha, apimpim-aba-ha?

I hurry to stand on my feet, I pray for you.

I have heard my grandmother's voice.

Aberefi Sempa my wife, Abuya.

It was not raining. And when Opitire came, he said:

My Opitire Abena, apimpim-a-ko-ha, apimpim-aba-ha?

I hurry to stand on my feet, I pray for you.

I have heard my grandmother's voice.

Aberefi Sempa my wife, Abuya.

It was not raining. When Bonko came, he said:

Who is my Bonko Abena, apimpim-a-ko-ha, apimpim-aba-ha?

I hurry to stand on my feet, I pray for you. I hear my grandmother's voice.

Aberefi Sempa my wife, Abuya.

It was not raining. The animals did not stop crying, and the sound came to Apuuo. He said, Please let me cry some. And he said, Didn't cry well, and your stomach was better? He said, Please let me cry. And we urged her to hold her tightly so she won't cry. And the Apuuo said, Mesu. And he said, Cry! And he said:Cry!

My name is Apupuo Abena, apimpim-a-ko-ha, apimpim-aba-ha?

I hurry to stand on my feet, I pray for you.

I have heard my grandmother's voice.

Aberefi Sempa my wife, Abuya.
