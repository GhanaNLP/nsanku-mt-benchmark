Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA WƆRETEƐTEƐ NSƐM A WƆDE BƐKƆ TRIBE no mu

WƆKA sɛ ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ pɔtee bi (pɛnkoro) kɔɔ wuram; ɔbaa amena bi mu bɛhwɛɛ mu, na ɛmu na na ɔgyinagyina 'Nipa, onipa, Osebo, Ɔkraman, Owo, Ɔkraman, ne Ɔkraman. Afei berɛ a saa nnipa yi hunuu Hunter no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya Hunter, mommra mmɛyi me adi." ɛnna saa ɔbaako no kaa sɛ, "Agya mmra mmɛyi me Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Merensiane ɛfiri sɛ adeɛ kyeeɛ mpo na sɛ megye wo nkwa na woduru me nkyɛn a, ɛdeɛn na wobɛyɛ me na menim sɛ ɛnsɛ sɛ mema me ho hia me." Oyi kae sɛ, “Mantumi (anyɛ saa).” Ɛno na ɔkaa sɛ, “Mantumi (anyɛ saa). Hunter no kaa sɛ, “Sɛ saa na ɛteɛ ampa ara mɛyi wo afiri hɔ.” na nokorɛ bi mu na ɔyii nnipa no nyinaa firii hɔ na ɔfirii adi kɔɔ n'akuraa ase Da koro bi, bere a na Hunter no wɔ hɔ no, Osebo a ɔyɛ Leopard no de nam pii bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya Hunter, yei yɛ aseda a wɔde ma wɔ ayamyɛ a woyɛɛ me no ho, meda wo ase, Apiafo." Ɔde nam nketewa dumienu brɛɛ ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no. Ɔbɔmmɔfo no daa no ase na Leopard no kɔe. Bere foforo nso, na Hunter no wɔ hɔ, na Okusie a ɔyɛ Rat no bae. Ɔkae sɛ, “Agya Hunter, so wobɛpɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma bi?” Hunter no kae sɛ, “Ho! Adɛn ntia, ɛno ne adeɛ a ɛma mmarima brɛ pa ara. Rat no sii mu kɔɔ baabi a Nyankonton a ɔyɛ ewiem nyame no de ne sika mfutuma siei no. ɔkɔe na ɔtutuu amena wɔ ɛdan no ase na ɔkɔɔ ɛdan no mu na ɔkɔfaa ewiem nyankopɔn mfuturo nyinaa na ɔde brɛɛ ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ Hunter no kaa sɛ, “Meda wo ase, Aku.” Na Rat no fii hɔ kɔe. ɛda foforɔ nso na ɔwɔ hɔ na ɔtee sɛ obi rekyia no sɛ “Ago” Ɔbɔmmɔfo no buae sɛ, “Ame” (bɛ mu). Ɔbarima no bae, na ɔkae sɛ, “Agya Hunter, ma wo anɔpa.” Ɔno (Ɔbɔ Hunter) buae sɛ, “Yiw, obusuani.” na ɔkaa sɛ yei yɛ abɛn ketewa bi a mede aba sɛ merebɛda wo ase Paapa Hunter kaa sɛ, "Mempɛ sɛ meyɛ saa nso na mo deɛ, onipa, ɛyɛ sɛdeɛ mekaa sɛ ɛbɛyɛ yi, berɛ a mepaee mu kaa sɛ merenyi mo mfiri mu no, hwɛ deɛ Leopard de brɛɛ me na hwɛ deɛ Rat no de brɛɛ me no." onipa yii ne nsa firi hɔ na ɔfirii hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ ewiem nyame no sɛ wo sika mfuturo a ayera sɛ ampa ara ɔbɛba na ɔbɛfaa no na ɔfirii hɔ kɔmaa ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ Wim-nyame no kae sɛ, “So ɛte saa?” Ɔkae sɛ, “Grandsire, ɛyɛ nokware a ɛda adi pefee.” Nyame a ɔwɔ wim no somaa abɔfo sɛ wɔnkɔ nkɔfa Hunter no, na wɔde hama kyekyeree n’asen. Wɔhwee no denneennen. Afei berɛ a wɔduruu Hunter no nkyɛn no, ɔkaa sɛ, “Nkwakoraa ne mmerewa, mepɛ sɛ mekɔ na mebrɛ me ho ase.” na abɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ dabi owifoɔ na mobɛdwane ɔkaa sɛ dabi, merenguan na sɛ wopɛ a fa hama no kyekyere m'asene na fa kyekyere dua bi ho na ma menkɔ na abɔfoɔ no tiee no na wɔmaa no kɔe ɛhɔ na na ɔwɔ no ɔmaa ne ho dwoo no na abɔdeɛ a ɔwowareeɛ no baeɛ na ɔkaa sɛ hwɛ m'adamfo yi wɔ ha na gye aduroyɛ ketewa yi na fa ka wo ho na sɛ wokɔ a wɔgyina w'anim na wɔgyina asafo no anim na ɛba sɛ wɔbɛku wo na ɔhene salomo kɔka ne yere ba a ɔpɛ n'asɛm pa ara na sɛ meka no a wohunu sɛ obiara sɔresɔre na wo nso woka sɛ mewɔ aduro na sɛ wode di dwuma wɔ abɔfra no so a ɔbɛsa no yadeɛ Hunter no kaa sɛ, “Meda wo ase, Aku.” na abɔfoɔ no faa ɔbɔnefoɔ no na wɔkɔgyinagyinaa ewiem nyame no anim wɔn anim wɔn anim ne wɔn agyinatufoɔ a wɔwɔ Kontire ne aaron ne aaron ne aaron ne aaron ne aaron ne aaron ne aaron ne wɔn ahemfo nyinaa Hunter no kaa n'asɛm no, na bere a Ɔkasamafo no sɔree sɛ ɔrebɛbu atɛn no, wɔtee dede bi a ɛkyerɛ sɛ wɔredi awerɛhow, pui! pui! akyirikyiri wɔ adehye asareman mu. Wɔkae sɛ, “Dɛn ne saa?” Wɔkaa sɛ, “Ɔwɔ bi aka ɔba a ɔyɛ ɔbarima a ɔpɛ ɔsoro nyame no ba.” Wɔn mu biara fii hɔ kɔe. Afei na Hunter no gyina hɔ; ɔkaa sɛ, “Sɛ menyɛ ɔdeduani a, ɛnde ebia menya aduro (aduru a wɔde ko tia awuduru no). Ɔyɔnko bi tee (no) na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Kɔ na fa ɔdeduani no bra ha." Afei Hunter no bae. na ɔsoro nyankopɔn kaa sɛ sɛ wotumi hwɛ yei a m'akoa na wanwu na mede wo tiri bɛkyerɛ wo Hunter no kae sɛ, “Mate, Kind-one.” Na Hunter no kaa bio sɛ, "Grandsire, deɛ mɛkyerɛ wo, sɛ wobɛtumi anya bi a, ɛnneɛ mɛtumi ahwɛ wo ba no." Wɔkae sɛ, “Dɛn na ɛrekɔ so?” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Mogya a ɛwɔ atetesɛm mu a wɔde bɛfra aduro a wɔayam no.” Wɔkae sɛ, “Ɛhe na yebenya mogya a ɛyɛ tell-tale?” na adiyifoɔ no mu baako a na ɔyɛ onyansafoɔ no kaa sɛ ɛdeɛn na yɛn yɔnko a ɔbaeɛ na ɔka kyerɛɛ ewiem nyame no sɛ ɔbɔnefoɔ no aba na ɔwiaa ewiem nyankopɔn sika no ɛhe na ɔwɔ Wɔkae sɛ, "Wonyɛ mfomso, ɛnyɛ mfomso." Wɔkɔe na wɔde ne yɔnko no bae. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Yɛbue ne nsa wɔ nkabom no mu, na fa ne mogya ma me." Ntɛm ara na wobubui. Ɔbɔfoɔ no faa mogya no sɛ ɔrekɔhwɛ aduro no na ɔde guu ɔhene ba no nan ase. Ntɛm ara na ɔsɔree. Wɔkae sɛ, “Wɔto ɔbɔmmɔfo ayeyi dwom.” Wɔteɛɛm sɛ, “Eee!” na ɔsoro nyankopɔn kaa sɛ mefa wo tiri so na mede makyerɛ wo na ma wɔnku no na wɔntwitwa ne honam na wɔmfa nto afasuo nyinaa so na wɔkum no na wɔtwitwaa ne nam nketenkete na wɔtotoo ne nyinaa guu mu Saa na nsɛm pii baa abusuakuo no mu.

Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
