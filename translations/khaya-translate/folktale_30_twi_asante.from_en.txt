Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA NKYERƐKYERƐMU BA TRIBE no mu

Bere bi na ɔbarima bi a wɔfrɛ no Hate-to-be-contracted wɔ hɔ, na esiane ɛno nti, ɔkɔe na osii atrae ketewaa bi (ne nyinaa nkutoo) na ɔkɔtraa mu. na abɔdeɛ a wɔfrɛ no yudahene yehoiakim kɔɔ ne nkyɛn na ɔne no kɔtenaa abɛ dua bi ase Na nkateɛ no bi guguu fam. Duykerfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Agya tan-kɔ-kɔtɔ, wo nnuaba abere." Wɔkae sɛ, "Ɛno ne mmɛ no su; sɛ ɛbere a, mmɛtem mmiɛnsa na ɛbere, na metwa, na sɛ menom de yi fangoo no a, wɔyɛ ntokuru mmiɛnsa a wɔde ngo ayɛ mu ma; na mede ngo no kɔ Akase kɔtɔ ɔbaa bi a wadi mfeɛ; Akase a wadi mfeɛ no ba bɛwo me nanabea a ɔsoa me maame a ɔno nso soa me no, sɛ ɛna no soa me a, megyina hɔ dada." Duyker no kaa sɛ, "Ne nyinaa deɛ, wodi atoro." na hate-to-be-contracted faa poma bɔɔ Duyker wɔ ne tiri so kumm no Afei Abedee antelope ketewa no bae. na ɔfirii hɔ kɔtenaa mmɛ dua no ase na saa ara na ɛbaa na ɛnam so na ɛyɛ mmoa nyinaa Afei, Kwaku Ananse a ɔyɛ Spider no kɔfaa ne ntama, na ɔfaa ne bag, de sii ne mmati so, na ofii hɔ kɔɔ Hate-to-be-contracted akuraa ase. Ɔkyeaa no sɛ, "Agya, ma wo kyɛw." Hate-to-be-contracted buae sɛ, "Y'Aku, na ɛhe na worekɔ?" Obuae sɛ, "Mereba wo nkyɛn." na ɔfaa n'asɛnnua na ɔde sii mmɛ dua no ase Hate-to-be-contracted kae sɛ, "Kɔtɔ aduan ma Spider no di." na ɛrenoa aduane no na ananse ne ɔtan a wɔde ahyɛ wɔn nsa no tenatenaa mmɛ dua no ase Na nkateɛ no bi guguu fam. na ananse faa wɔn de wɔn guu ne kotokuo mu na ɔkɔɔ so yɛɛ saa ara kɔsii sɛ ne kotokuo no yɛɛ ma Wɔde aduan no bae, na Ananse dii. Ɔdii aduane wieeɛ no, mmɛ no bi tɔɔ fam, na Ananse kaa sɛ, "Agya Hate-to-wo, wo nnuaba abere." Wɔn a wɔn ho nni asɛm no se, "Ɛyɛ wɔn su sɛ abere bɛyɛ saa; sɛ ɛbere a, ɛbere abiɛsa na ɛbere prɛko pɛ; sɛ ɛbere a, metwa, na sɛ menom ma wɔtwa ngo no, wɔyɛ nkuruwa mmiɛnsa a wɔde ngo ayɛ mu ma, na mefa ngo no kɔ Akase, kɔtɔ ɔbaa bi a ne mfe akɔ anim; Akase aberewa no ba bɛwo me nanabea a ɔwo me maame no, na ɔno nso bɛsoa me. Sɛ ɛna soa me a, megyina hɔ dedaw." Spider no kae sɛ, "Wonni atoro, nea woka no yɛ nokware; ɛdefa me fam no, mewɔ okros bi a egyina m'afuo mu, na sɛ ɛbere a, mede me ho hyɛ nnua atenten aduɔson ason a wɔde wɔn ho ahyɛ mu (de duru wɔn nkyɛn kɔtwe wɔn kɔ fam), nanso ɛno mpo no wɔantumi ankɔ, enti meda m'akyi, na mitumi de me barima di dwuma de tu wɔn." Hate-to-be-contracted kaa sɛ, "Oh, mete aseɛ; ɔkyena mɛba abɛhwɛ." Spider no kae sɛ, "Akyinnye biara nni ho." Berɛ a na Spider no rekɔ (fie) no, ɔwee nnuaba a na waboaboa ano no, na ɔtetee wɔn guu kwan no so. Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a wofii ase huu nneɛma no, wɔde wɔn ho too wɔn so sɛ wɔrekɔ Spider akuraa ase. Afei berɛ a Spider no dii kan duruu hɔ (da a ɛdi kan ansa na ɛreba no) na ɔkɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ ne mma sɛ, "Onipa bi bɛba ha, na ɔtan sɛ wɔbɛbɔ ne ho abira, na sɛ ɔduru hɔ kɔbisa m'akyiri a, ɛsɛ sɛ woka kyerɛ no sɛ nnora na maka sɛ merefiri baabi, berɛ a m'akyi berɛmo bubuu mmeaeɛ nson, na na ɛsɛ sɛ mede kɔma ɔtomfoɔ bi sɛ ɔnsiesie, na ɛsiane sɛ wantumi anwie saa berɛ no nti, ɔse wakɔ sɛ ɔrekɔwie adwuma no." Ɛno akyi bere tiaa bi no, Hate-to-be-contracted kaa ho. Ɔkae sɛ, “Ɛhe na wo papa akɔ?” na wɔbua see no sɛ awurade papa kɔɔ baabi nnɛra na ne barima bubuu mmeaeɛ ahodoɔ nson na ɔde kɔɔ ɔtomfoɔ nkyɛn nanso wantumi anwie adwuma no saa berɛ no na agya kɔ ma wɔwieeɛ na (wo, agya, woanhunu mogya no wɔ kwan no so anaa?" Ntawntawdi a ɛne sɛ wɔbɛbɔ wɔn sobo no kae sɛ, “Mihui.” Wɔbɔɔ no sobo sɛ, “Na wo maame wɔ he?” (The Spider’s ba buae sɛ:) “Maame nso, nnora ɔkɔɔ asuten no mu, na sɛ wammɔ ne ho ban amfi saa a ɔbɛyɛ denam ne ho a ɔkyeree no wɔ bere ano kɛkɛ so a, anka ne nsu-tumi bɛhwe ase na abubu, nanso wanwie koraa sɛ obegye afi ahwease, na wasan aba da biara sɛ ɔrebɛyɛ saa.” Ɔtan a wɔde bɔ nkorɔfoɔ no anka hwee. Afei de, Ananse duu hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, “Kɔtɔ aduane bi a ɛbɛtumi adi akɔnnɔ bɔne a wɔde bɔ nkorɔfoɔ ho ban.” Bere a na mmofra no renoa aduan no, na wɔde pɛnkoro pɛ na edi dwuma, na mmom na wɔde mako pii; wɔmaa nkrute no yɛɛ hyew yiye. Bere a wɔawie no, wɔde too fam ansa na wɔrebɔ wɔn sobo. Adidi a wɔtan sɛ wɔbɛbɔ wɔn ho ban. Afei mako no yɛɛ no yaw, na ɔpɛ sɛ owu. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ntikuma (Spider’s ba) nom nsuo ma me na mennom.” Ntikuma kɔɔ baabi a nsuo wɔ no, wamfa nsuo no amma da. Wɔkae sɛ, “Ntikuma, ɛhe na saa nsuo no wɔ?” Ntikuma kaa sɛ, “Ah, nsuo a yɛwɔ wɔ ha (a ɛwɔ yɛn nsuo nturo mu) no gu ahodoɔ mmiɛnsa; sɛ agya dea yɛ deɛ ɛdi kan, me maame yere mfɛfoɔ yere deɛ no wɔ mfimfini, na sɛ ɛyɛ m'ankasa me maame dea a, ɛwɔ aseɛ (a ɛwɔ kukuo no mu); ne ɛsiane sɛ mepɛ sɛ menom nsuo a ɛyɛ m'ankasa me maame dea nko ara ma wo nti, sɛ manhwɛ yie kɛseɛ berɛ a meretwe a, ɛde mmusuakuo mu akasakasa bɛba.” Ɔtan a wɔde bɔ nkorɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Woyɛ brat ketewa, wodi atoro.” Ɔkwan teaa so no, Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɔbɛn no na wawu." Ɔtan a wɔde bɔɔ wɔn no kaa sɛ, “Dɛn nti na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔboro me na matumi awu?” Spider no kaa sɛ, "Wose wompɛ sɛ wɔne wo bɔ abira, nanso woakasa atia ebi nti na mese ɛsɛ sɛ wɔboro wo na ama woawuwu." ɛnna wɔbobɔɔ wɔn a wɔato wɔn mogya mu na ɔno deɛ ɔwuiɛ Afei Ananse twitwaa ne nam nketenkete bɔ petee so. ɛno nti na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔhunu nnipa bebree a wɔmpɛ sɛ wɔne wɔn ho bɔ abira no wɔ abusuakuo no mu

Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
