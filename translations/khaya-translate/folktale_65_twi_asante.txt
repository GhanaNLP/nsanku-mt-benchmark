We don’t say, she shouldn’t say

SE YOYE A KEMPTOMS

ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS and Ananse are the ones we want to plant. We had only two cornstalks. And Ananse said, "I will plant my food." Kwame said to Akweteire, “I’m going to go get myself some wine.” Ananse fought for her food. Kwame put his lunch on a fire. It rained for a day, and it rained. Ananse went to the farm’; she ate and drank ache. "Don't keep my eyes closed, and you'll keep my eyes closed,” he said. Ananse says that she has caught her dog; her dog said, "Don't blame me, but I did it." If he disrespected the dog, Aba will say, "Don't respect me, and your dog will bite me." Ananse came home to report the case. He laughed and slept 'groom'. It is because of the heat of the dry season.

What I have said is that, if it is not for him, you can’t forgive him, and you can’t blame him.
