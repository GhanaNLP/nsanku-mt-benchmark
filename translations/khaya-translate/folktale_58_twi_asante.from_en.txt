Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

WƆ PATH biara so no, baabi a WOHUu SO OSEBO, LEOPARD, AKWANKYERƐ, ƐHƆ na ADANKO, THE ARRE, ASARE (ALREADY) PASSED

WƆKA sɛ ɔbarima a ɔyɛ ɔbaa ne ɔbarima a ɔyɛ ɔbarima, ne ɔbarima Hare ne ɔbea Hare, bere bi na wɔte akuraa ketewaa bi ase, na ɔkɔm bi bae. Afei ɔbaa Leopard kaa sɛ ɔrekɔ asuten no mu. Bere a ɔrekɔ (ohyiaa) ɔbea a ɔretu atoko no. Ɔbaa Leopard kaa sɛ, "Ah, maame, ɛhe na wonyaa biribi a ɛte saa?" Ɔbaa Hare buaa no sɛ, "Ɔkwan bi wɔ hɔ a ɛtrɛ wɔ ha, na na me kunu redi akyire akɔhwehwɛ wuram bayerɛ, na ɔkɔ kɔfaa saa aburoo yi, enti fa wei (na) kɔ na kɔ na kɔwe." Obuae sɛ, “Meda mo ase, Aku.” Afei ɔbaa panin no yii no firii fie kɔtoo gya. na ne kunu baa ne nkyɛn na ɔbisaa no sɛ ɛhe na wanya borɔdɔma no wɔ na ɔkaa sɛ ɔbaa hare de ama me na ɔkaa sɛ ɔkwan bi a ɛtrɛ da hɔ na ne kunu faa na ɔde bae na ɔde bae Ɔbarima a wɔfrɛ no Leopard no kaa sɛ, “Afei adeɛ kyeeɛ no mɛkɔ hɔ anɔpatutuutu pa ara.” ɛnna ɔfirii hɔ kɔeɛ Bere a ɔkɔe no, na aburow dɔɔso. Afei ɔfaa ne sekan twaa bi, na ɔyɛɛ kɛntɛn a wɔde bɛtɛ ayɛ, boaboaa ano (atokoɔ no), na ɔde sii mu. Afei na Adanko a ɔyɛ ɔbarima Hare wɔ ne fie, na ɔhunuu sɛ ɔbarima Leopard akɔ afuom. Ntɛm ara na ɔno nso fii adi. na ɔduruu kuro no awieeɛ na ɔdanee dɔteɛ Ná ne fã aporɔw; na fã nnya nporɔw. na ɔkɔɔ akyirikyiri na ɔdanee oyuo (antelope) Ná ne fã aporɔw; na emu fã nnya nporɔw. na ɔtoaa n'anim na ɔdanee ne ho kɔɔ babel Ná ne fã aporɔw; na emu fã nnya nporɔw. na ɔtoaa so na ɔdanee buffalo Ná ne nyinaa yɛ foforo. Afei na Leopard no de kɛntɛn ne atoko reba ne tiri so. Ɔbaa hɔ bɛhyiaa Buffalo. Ɔkwatani no kaa sɛ, "Ɛkɔm bɛba, nanso ɛnnya nyɛɛ kɛse, nanso saa mmoa yi rewuwu." na ɔtwam kɔe na ɔbaa abraham nkyɛn na ɔfaa mu na ɔbaa yosua nkyɛn na ɔtwaa mu kɔe na ɔbaa yuu nkyɛn bɛdii n'adeɛ na ɔbaa yudahene yehoiakim nkyɛn Afei ɔkaa sɛ, “Oo, saa mmoa yi dɔɔso ampa nti mɛkɔ akɔboaboa wɔn ano.” Ɔtow kɛntɛn ne atoko guu fam, na osii gyinae sɛ ɔrekɔfa mmoa no, na ofii nea edi kan no ase. Bere a ɔkɔe no, na wonni hɔ bio. Afei ɔsane baeɛ, nanso kɛntɛn ne aburoo no nso kɔe. Ofii fie, na ɔyɛɛ komm traa ase. Ɔbea a wɔfrɛ no Leopard no bisaa no sɛ, “Ɛhe na aburow no wɔ?” Ɔkae sɛ, “Mekɔɔ (ɛhɔ), nanso na mintumi ntɔ hwee.” Afei ɔbea a ɔyɛ ɔbarima korɔn no fii adi kɔɔ asuten no mu, na ɛhɔ na ohyiaa ɔbea Hare. Ɔbaa a wɔfrɛ no Leopard no kaa sɛ, "Deɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meka ara ne sɛ ɛnnɛra me kunu kaa sɛ ɔkɔeɛ no, wannya aburoo biara antɔ. Ɔbaa Hare buaa no sɛ, "Ɔredaadaa wo nnora, berɛ a ɔkɔeɛ no, me kunu boroo no na ɔmaa no ho tɔɔ no." Ɔbea a ɔyɛ ɔbarima a ɔyɛ ɔbea no fii fie. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne kunu sɛ, "Woayɛ yiye! yɛ yiye! yɛ yiye! na wokaa sɛ kɔ na woannya atokoɔ biara nso ɛyɛ hare na ɔbobɔɔ wo na ɔyi firii wo nsam Ɔkwatani no kaa sɛ, “Hena ne Hare no? ɛde bɛsi awieeɛ no mɛkɔ akɔko ne no akɔko.” Ɔbarima Hare tenaa fie na ɔtee (eyi). ɔkɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere sɛ adeɛ bɛkye na ɔbarima leopard bɛba ha na ɔne me abɛko enti mɛkɔ makɔhyia no wɔ kwan so ɛfiri sɛ ebia wobɛsuro Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma daa adi no, Hare no fii ase. Ɔduruu kuro no akyi, na ɔdanee Adowa antelope, na ɔfee dɔte fitaa, bidie ne mogya. Ɔkɔɔ n'anim, na ɔdanee Bush-buck, na ɔfee saa ara. Ɔkɔɔ n'anim, na ɔdanee Buffalo. Ná Leopard no reba. Adowa antelope no kaa sɛ, “Agya Lepard, boa me, ɛfiri sɛ merewu.” Leopard no kaa sɛ, “Dɛn ne asɛm no?” Adowa antelope no kaa sɛ, “Me ne Hare kɔsiesiee atokoɔ ho akasakasa bi, na ɔboroo me, na merewu.” Leopard no kaa sɛ, "Ma memfa me ho nhyɛ mu." Ɔtoaa so, na ohyiaa Bush-buck no. Bush-buck no kaa sɛ, "Leopard, boa me, ɛfiri sɛ Hare nyɛ obi a ɛsɛ sɛ ɔne no nya atokoɔ akasakasa." Leopard no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn ne asɛm no?" Bush-buck no kae sɛ, "Mekɔe na ɔboroo me, ɛno nti na merewu." Leopard no kaa sɛ, "Ma memfa me ho nhyɛ mu." Ɔtoaa so, na ɔbɛhyiaa Buffalo. Buffalo se, "Agya Osebo boa me, ɛfiri sɛ merewu." Leopard no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛrekɔ so?" Buffalo se, "Mekɔe na me ne Hare nyaa akasakasa wɔ atokoɔ ho, na ɔboroo me, na merewu." Ɛno akyi no, Leopard no susuw ho kyɛe (na ne tenten ka kyerɛɛ ne ho), "E! Mɛsan m'akyi, efisɛ ɛyɛ atoko a ɛte saa ara a ɛsɛ sɛ me ne no tra, na misusuw sɛ ɔboroo me..." Na Leopard no fii ase guanee, kiri! Kiri! Ntɛm ara na Hare nso sɔree. Ɔtaa no, na Ɔkwatani no teɛɛm denneennen sɛ, "Leopard a ɔyɛ ɔbarima no, guan fi hɔ o!" Leopard duruu ne fie, na ɔyerae kɔɔ ne dan mu, na ɔfaa ne ti bɔɔ fasu no biako mu na ɔfaa mu, nanso Hare no kuraa ne nan abien a ɛwɔ n’akyi no mu. na leopard kaa sɛ agya hare ma menkɔ Hare kaa sɛ, "Meremma wo nkɔ, mɛboro wo." Leopard se, "Mesrɛ wo, nhwe me, na mmom ma me anyame no nnom na wɔnka ntam sɛ ɔkwan biara a wobɛfa so no, mɛba mabɛdane." Ɛno nti, wofa kwan biara so koraa, na wohwɛ hunu sɛ ɔkwan no afiri hɔ, (wonim) Hare no atwam saa.

Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
