SƐNEA AKOKONINI, COCK, GOT A PINTINI HO NSƐM

Na PATEANANE, Spider, ne ne mma Afudotweotwe (a wɔte sɛ Bely-to-burst), Nyiwankonfwea (Thin-shanks), (ne) Tikonokono (Big-ti). ɛnna akɔre no ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ monkɔ nkɔhwehwɛ borɔdɔma na menni ɛnna saa mmɔfra yi siane kɔeɛ na wɔnantee na wɔnantee na wɔbɛhyiaa borɔdɔma dua bi na borɔdɔma no abere na mmɔfra no kɔɔ ase (dua no). Afudotwewe (Bely-like-to-burst) ka kyerɛɛ Nyiwankonfwea (Thin-shanks), "Climb na puck emu bi." Ɔkae sɛ, "O, sɛ meforo me nan a, ɛbɛbubu." Tikonokono (Big-ti) kaa sɛ, "Wo, Afudotwedotwe, so woforo foro kɔ soro." Ɔkae sɛ, "O, sɛ meforo a, me yafunu bɛpae, woforo wo ho." So Tikonokono (a ɔyɛ kɛse) foro kɔɔ soro. Ɔpaapaee Fig-tree no mma binom mu na ɔkɔɔ so tuu mmirika ne mmirika, afei ɔkae sɛ, "So ɛno bɛyɛ saa?" Wɔn nkaeɛ no kaa sɛ, "A, (yɛbɛyɛ dɛn na yɛahunu)." Enti ɔkɔɔ so twetwee wɔn; afei obisaa wɔn bio sɛ, "So ɛno bɛyɛ saa?" na wɔbua see no sɛ hwɛ wo tiri so na hwɛ na ɔrebɛsi ne tiri ase sɛ ɔrebɛhwɛ na ne tiri bɔɔ fam kaa sɛ (asɛmfua bi te sɛ) "Kum!" Bere a anka Belly-to-burst serew no, ne yafunu mu paee, ta! ɛnna dawid dwanedwane kɔka kyerɛɛ n'agya sɛ ne nan bubui na ɔtenaa ban no akyi na kohat hohoroo ne kra ho na ɔfiri adi kɔguu ɛserɛ so ɛserɛ so ɛserɛ so na ɔhunuu ɛserɛ soɔ sɛ ahwe ase na ne nan abubu na ɔgyaa no wɔ ban no akyi Bere a na ɔrebɛserew no, n'ano bɛyɛɛ nea ɛkyerɛ sɛ:

"Bere a na ɔrebɛserew no, n'ano bɛyɛɛ nea ɛkyerɛ sɛ ɛyɛ nokware."

Bere a na ɔrebɛserew no, n'ano bɛyɛɛ nea wɔde kyerɛ."

Ɛno nti na mpanimfoɔ se, "Sɛ obi hwe ase a, kɔ na kɔhwɛ no, na nsuro." Afei, ɛdefa Cock ho no, ɔyɛɛ n'ade te sɛɛ, na ɔserewee, enti n'ano mu yɛɛ den; kan no na ɔwɔ ano a ɛyɛ fɛ na ɛyɛ fɛ.

Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
