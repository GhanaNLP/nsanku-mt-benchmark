KOKOKYINAKA, TURACOU A WƆDE BLEE NO ANSI, ƆSƐNKYERƐMUFO, AKWANKYERƐ AKWANKYERƐ AKWANKYERƐ AKWANKYERƐ AKWANKYERƐ AKWANKYERƐ AKWANKYERƐ AKWANKYERƐ AKWANKYERƐ AKWANKYERƐMU

Bere bi akyi no, Kokokyinaka anomaa no kɔɔ Ananse, Spider, nkyɛn kɔhwehwɛɛ bosea a ɛne sɛ ɔbɛbɔ asuanu sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (£4). Afei, sɛdeɛ na Ananse ankasa nni bi no, ɔkɔɔ n'adamfo Ntontom, Mosquito nkyɛn kɔgyee bi de kɔmaa n'adamfo, Kokokyina anomaa no. Da a ɛtwaam a na Mosquito asiesie (a wɔde betua bosea no), na Mosquito ka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, “Kɔ na kɔgye me sika kɔkɔɔ mfuturo ma me.” Ananse buaa sɛ, “Kokokyina anomaa no te saa Silk-cotton-dua no atifi, enti kɔ na kɔgye wo sika kɔkɔɔ mfuturo.” Mosquito no bisaa sɛ, “Mɛyɛ dɛn atumi anya bi; abɔde no tumi tu.” Spider no bua see no sɛ kɔ na kɔforo dua no na sɛ wohunu dua no a ɛbɛbubu na woakyere Kokokyinina anomaa no na woasane anya wo sika mfuturo Mosquito no kɔforoo dua no. Berɛ a na ɛrebɛbubu no, Kokokyaka anomaa no firii hɔ kɔeɛ, pa bu bu bu! Afei Mosquito bae na ɔkaa sɛ, “Anansesɛm, Ananse.” Ɛno nti na yɛwɔ asɛm bi a ɛreka sɛ, “Abɔdeɛ a wɔfrɛ no Kokokyina anomaa no amfɛm amfiri Anansefoɔ hɔ sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ Mosquito kɔ akɔforo Silk-cotton-tree no.” Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
