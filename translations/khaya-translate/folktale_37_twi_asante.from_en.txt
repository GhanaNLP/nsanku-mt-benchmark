Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐDƐN NTI NA ƐBƐYƐ ASƐM, ƆSƐMFO, BERƐ A ƆWƆ ƆWASUASE

Kwaku AnaNSE, Spider, kɔka kyerɛɛ Okraman, Ɔkraman no sɛ, “Mepɛ sɛ me ne wo kɔ si akuraa ketewa bi a ɛsɛ sɛ wote mu.” Ɔkraman no kae sɛ, “Me nso, mɛpɛ.” Ananse kaa sɛ, “Sɛ Kwasiada-Adae hwe ase Dwowda a edi hɔ no a, kɔpɛ hama a wɔde hyɛ mu; Me nso, mɛnya hama a wɔde hyɛ mu na yɛayi adi. saa ara nso na mɛfa ɛferɛ na mede nsuo ahyɛ mu ma na wo nso woapete ebinom na ebinom nso akɔ baabi a yɛrekɔ na nsuo biara nni hɔ Ananse petee nsuo bi mu; Ɔkraman no nso nyaa ɛwo a ɔde bɛhyɛ (ne borɔdɔma) mu. na wɔrekɔ wɔn nkyɛn na wɔakɔduru ɛserɛ no so na wɔabrɛ Ananse kaa sɛ, “Momma yɛnnom yɛn nsuo no bi.” Ɔkraman no kae sɛ, “Mewɔ bi nom.” Ananse faa bi, na ɔde ne nsa kaa n'ano, ɔkaa sɛ ɛhe na yei firi baeɛ? Ɔkraman no kae sɛ, “Ɛwɔ me yafunu mu.” Spider no kae sɛ, “Yiw!” Bere a wɔte hɔ sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wɔn ho bɛtɔ wɔn no, Ananse kae sɛ, “Dog, momma yɛmfa yɛn ho nhyɛ agodie mu.” Ɔkraman no kae sɛ, “Agorudi bɛn?” Spider no buae sɛ, “Agorɔ a ɛkyekyere me; wodi kan kyekyere me, afei me nso, mɛkyekyere wo, nanso sɛ wote sɛ meka sɛ, ‘Wui’! afei wofiri hɔ kɔ.” Ɔkraman no kae sɛ, “Dabi, di kan kyekyere me.” Spider no kaa sɛ, “Fa wo ho kɔ, sɛ wo panyin reka biribi akyerɛ wo a, so wugye akyinnye wɔ ho?” Ɔkraman no kae sɛ, “Wiɛ, ma ɛntra hɔ (sɛnea wopɛ). Spider no kae sɛ, “Bra bɛkyekyere me.” Ɔkraman no faa ne hama a ɔde hyɛ mu no kyekyeree Spider no. na ɔde no kɔɔ ne tiri so kaa sɛ ɔrekɔtɔn no na wanya biribi adi Bere a Ananse tee saa no, ɔtee awerɛhow bi na ɔkae sɛ:

“Me, Ananse Akuamoa, mennyaa aboa biara mpo a ɔbɛtɔn me.”

Ɔkraman kwasea yi nkutoo na ɔbɛtɔn me.

Hena na metumi agye no nkwa?

O, Ɔkwan, gye me nkwa!”

Ananse tumi sui kɛkɛ. Ɔkraman no soaa no kosii sɛ oduu nsuten bi ho. Afei, Odenkyem a ɔyɛ Crocodile no tee sɛ Spider no resu. Bere a ɔbaa hɔ no, obisaa ɔkraman no asɛm no. Ɔkraman no tow Ananse gui, na oguan, na Crocodile no brɛɛ Ananse ase. na ananse kaa sɛ agya adeɛ bɛn na mede bɛma woɔ yi kyerɛ sɛdeɛ m'ani sɔ Crocodile no kaa sɛ, “Yɛmpɛ biribiara koraa.” Ananse kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wowɔ mma a ɛnneɛ adeɛ kyeeɛ no mɛba mabɛsiesie wɔn tirinwi mama wɔn yie pa ara." Crocodile no kae sɛ, "Mate." Ananse baa fie na ɔkaa sɛ, "Aso, monkɔ nkɔhwehwɛ abɛtwa ne ayɛyɛdeɛ, ɛfiri sɛ adeɛ bɛkye na mede dɛnkyɛm bɛsane aba a ebia yɛbɛnya nkrantɛ ama yɛadi." Aso kɔe na ɔnyaa bi, na Spider no sew ne sekan ano na ɔde kɔɔ asuten no mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya Crocodile, nsuo-mmoa, mereba abɛtua wo ka." na ɔfaa mmɛtem a wɔde ahyɛ mu no de guu nsuo no mu Crocodile no bae, na na ɔrebɛfa bayerɛ a wɔabɔ no akuturuku no adidi, bere a Spider no faa ne sekan twaa no, gya! nanso wamfa no amma sɛdeɛ ɛfata no, na ɔfirii hɔ kɔeɛ. Ananse san baa fie. Aso bisaa no sɛ, "Ɛhe na saa aboa no wɔ?" Ananse kaa sɛ, "Fa wo ho firi hɔ; obi firi adi reba, na ɔnnyaa ne home mpo, na wofiri aseɛ de nsɛmmisa haw no." Aso kaa sɛ, "Mahu sɛ woannya no." Ananse anka hwee. Ade kyee anɔpa no, Aso kae sɛ ɔrekɔ asubɔnten no ho ntɛm paa. Ɔduruu hɔ no, na Crocodile no da asubɔnten no ano, na na nwansena bruu no rebɔ no akuturuku. Aso san baa fie bɛka kyerɛɛ ne kunu. Ananse kaa sɛ, "Afei mohunu aduro pɔtee bi a mewɔ, nneyɛeɛ; sɛ mekum nam (da koro), ɛnyɛ sɛ nneɛma bɛda adi a, da a edi hɔ no, so mekɔ kɔfa, nanso ne nyinaa mu no, mema mo akwaaba sɛ mahu." Ananse twitwaa poma na ɔde no kɔɔ asuo a ɛwɔ hɔ no mu na ɛhɔ na ɔbɛhyiaa crocodile no Ɔde dua no bɔɔ so kae sɛ, "Kroto, so woawu?" Crocodile, so woawu?" na ɔdanee no kɔe na crocodile no da hɔ da so ara wɔ hɔ nanso na ɔnwui. Bere a Ananse rebɛfa ne nsa de ne nsa aka no, Crocodile no bɔɔ no mpofirim ara, kua! Ananse dii aperepere, peterere! peteree! na ɔnyaa kwan dwaneeɛ Ɛno nti, sɛ Spider no fa nsuo no mu a, ɔtu mmirika; osuro sɛ Crocodile no bɛsan aba bio.

Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
