KƐHA HE NI AKƐ AKƐTSƆƆ HE NI AKƐTSƆƆ HE NI AKƐTSƆƆ HE

Ghanabii kpakpai lɛ fɔ oshiki yɛ Afuabe 2020 akɛ amɛbaahiɛ H.E. Maŋhiɛnyiɛlɔ lɛ, Nana Addo Dankwa Akuffo-Addo yɛ be kroko mli koni etsa nitsumɔ kpakpa lɛ nɔ ni etsu nii babaoo. Hekɛnɔfɔɔ ni wɔnanemɛi Ghanabii lɛ kɛha yɛ nɔyeli nɛɛ mli lɛ ji nɔ ko ni wɔbuuu akɛ nɔ ko bibioo.

2021 Budget lɛ ji shika he gbɛjianɔtoo ko ni sa kadimɔ waa ni haa wɔ maŋ shika lɛ feɔ nɔ ni buɔ wala he, ni ehaa jarayeli nitsumɔi ni haoɔ mɔ lɛ feɔ nɔ ni he hiaa waa, ni wɔkɛ wɔjwɛŋmɔ maa Ghana tsumaa ekoŋŋ nɔ, yɛ COVID-19 sɛɛ. Shika lɛ damɔ ninaa ni tsakeɔ maŋ nɛɛ nɔ, ni ehaa shika helɛtemɔ yaa nɔ.

Yɛ klɛŋklɛŋ be lɛ fã ko mli lɛ, wɔkɛ shika helɛtemɔ lɛ eku sɛɛ eba, kɛtsɔ gbɛjianɔtoi ni ato he gbɛjianɔ jogbaŋŋ ni kɔɔ shika helɛtemɔ mli tsakemɔi ahe lɛ nɔ, wɔkɛ shika ni anáa lɛ he gbɛjianɔtoo ba, wɔkɛ nibii ni akɛwoɔ mli lɛ tsu nii, wɔye wɔbua aŋkroaŋkroi nitsumɔhei lɛ, ni wɔkɛ gbɔmɛi ateŋ nifeemɔi ni akɛwoɔ mli lɛ tsu nii. Yɛ afi 2019 naagbee lɛ, wɔyɛ gbɛ ni shika helɛtemɔ tsakemɔ tsɔɔ nɔ yɛ shika helɛtemɔ he okadii ni yɔɔ naakpɛɛ kɛ majimaji ateŋ sɛɛfimɔ akɛ maŋ ni miiya hiɛ, beni COVID-19 hela lɛ tswa wɔ lɛ. Jeŋ maji fɛɛ ni yɔɔ jeŋ fɛɛ, ni Ghana fata he lɛ nu nɔ ni nɛkɛ hela nɛɛ egba mɔ naa waa lɛ he.

Covid-19 hela lɛ ni je shishi lɛ kɛ naagbai babaoo ni ena wɔ nitsumɔi kɛ daa gbi shihilɛ nɔ hewalɛ eha. Shi kɛlɛ, nɔyeli lɛ kɛ haomɔ babaoo ekadi bɔ ni hela ni gbɛɔ eshwaa wɔ lɛ saa wɔhe akɛ gbɔmɛi, ni ebɔi mlai ni baaye abua ni agbele jarayeli nitsumɔi anaa ekoŋŋ ni wɔkɛ wɔwala aku sɛɛ aba bɔ ni sa. Hiɛnɔkamɔ fɛɛ elaaje ejaakɛ wɔna gbɛi heei ni wɔɔtsɔ nɔ wɔfee nibii ni bɛ naagbee. Nɔyeli lɛ etswa efai shi akɛ ebaakwɛ ni maŋbii lɛ aná gbɔmɔtsoŋ hewalɛ kpakpa, ni no hewɔ lɛ, eto gbɛjianɔ koni awo Ghanabii tsofa koni wɔfɛɛ wɔhe awa yɛ Ghana ni wɔmiitao ni wɔna lɛ mli.

Wɔkɛ bo efee ekome yɛ naagbai amli po akɛ wɔ hiɛyaa gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ fã, ni wɔkɛ ekãa etsu nii koni wɔkɛ wɔshika helɛtemɔ lɛ aku sɛɛ aya hiɛ. Amrɔ nɛɛ, Ghanabii aaafee akpekpei 1.2 etee nɔtsamɔ skul ni hi jogbaŋŋ ni hi jogbaŋŋ kɛha amɛwala, wɔmiisaa shikatoohe lɛ ni mɛi ni heɔ nii lɛ ateŋ mɛi babaoo ená amɛshika kɛjɛ shikatoohei ni egbee shi lɛ amli. Kɛfata he lɛ, ama tsɔne ko ni akɛbuaa tsɔnei anaa ni wɔna tsɔnei ni abua naa lɛ ateŋ klɛŋklɛŋ nɔ yɛ wɔ jarayeli kɛ gbɛjegbɛi anɔ.

Wɔkpɛlɛɔ nɔ yɛ anɔkwale mli akɛ wɔkɛ naagbai komɛi kpe yɛ be mli ni wɔbɔɔ mɔdɛŋ ni wɔwo wɔ hiɛyaa otii lɛ ahe hewalɛ lɛ. Nɔyeli lɛ kɛ gbɛjianɔtoo miitsu nii jogbaŋŋ koni akɛtsu naagbai nɛɛ ahe nii, ni ekɛ yelikɛbuamɔ aba jarayeli nitsumɔi amli. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔ nɔ lɛ, wɔkɛ GH¢750 akpekpei CAP-BuSS Program lɛ tsu nii yɛ May 2020 ni enɛ eye ebua Micro, Small kɛ Medium-Sized Enterprises (MSMEs) kɛ ekrokomɛi babaoo.

Tamɔ bɔ ni tsutsu ko lɛ, atsɛɔ wɔ koni wɔkɛ wɔhe awo wɔdɛŋ ni wɔha wɔmaŋ lɛ aya hiɛ kɛtsɔ nibii ni yɔɔ ekãa ni akɛtsuɔ nii koni anyɛ ashɛ hiɛyaa he aha mɛi fɛɛ lɛ nɔ. Fɛɛ sɛɛ lɛ, wɔwo wɔhe shi akɛ wɔkɛ wɔhewalɛ fɛɛ kɛ wɔtsui muu lɛ fɛɛ baatsu nii yɛ Ghana sɔɔmɔ lɛ mli.

Nyɔŋmɔ ajɔɔ wɔ fɛɛ.

INTR NYƆŊMƆI NI AKƐTSƆƆ HE

1. 2021 Citizens Budget lɛ ji nɔ ni Nɔyeli lɛ ná yɛ 2020 kɛ bɔ ni ekpɛ eyiŋ akɛ ebaabua shika naa koni ekɛ mlai kɛ gbɛjianɔtoi atsu nii yɛ 2021 mli. 2021 Shika Helɛtemɔ lɛ saneyitso ji Nibii ni Akɛtsuɔ Nii, Nibii ni Akɛwoɔ Mli, kɛ Nibii ni Akɛwoɔ Mli. Anɔkwale ni eji akɛ yɛ afii ni eho lɛ amli lɛ, Budget hee fɛɛ Budget hee hiɛ nitsumɔi heei babaoo ni abaaje shishi yɛ nitsumɔi ni yaa nɔ lɛ ahe nyɔmɔwoo mli. Jeee akɛ enɛ kɛ nɔnyɛɛ baa wɔshika nɔ kɛkɛ, shi moŋ ehaa awoɔ nyɔmɔ hu yɛ yinɔsane mli, ni awooo nitsumɔ mli nitsulɔi babaoo nyɔmɔ yɛ be ni sa mli kɛha nitsumɔ. Enɛ haa nitsumɔi ahe nyɔmɔwoo fɛɛ yaa hiɛ be mli ni nyɔmɔwoo ni akɛtoɔ sɛɛ lɛ gbalaa shika ni akɛwoɔ mli lɛ.

2. Yɛ afi 2021 lɛ, Nɔyeli lɛ eto gbɛjianɔ koni etsɔɔ gbɛ hee ko nɔ. Oti ni ma hiɛ ji ni akɛ nitsumɔi ni agbeko naa lɛ ahe kusum aba naagbee kɛtsɔ shiwooi ni akɛbaatsu nii koni akɛgbe nitsumɔi ni yɔɔ momo lɛ anaa lɛ nɔ.

7. Shika ni akɛwoɔ nyɔmɔ tamɔ nɔ ni yɔɔ naagbee 2020 lɛ damɔ GH¢29 (U.S. dɔlai akpekpei toi akpei 50.8), ni damɔ shi kɛha GDP mlijaa 76.1. • Shia nyɔmɔwoo: GH¢149.8 billion (U.S. dɔlai akpekpei toi akpei 26.1) ni damɔ shi kɛha nyɔmɔwoo fɛɛ mlijaa 51.4.

Glossary (Budget Wiemɔ)

1D1F (One District One Factory): Nitsumɔhei kɛ nitsumɔhei ni, yɛ no najiaŋ lɛ, amɛbaajie maŋ lɛ kɛya nitsumɔhei wuji amli. Eyiŋtoo ji ni eha Ghanabii lɛ aná nitsumɔi babaoo yɛ maŋ lɛ mli fɛɛ.

Akɔntaabuu: Enɛ ji be mli ni Nɔyeli lɛ baanyɛ aha emaŋbii lɛ ahetoo kɛtsɔ enifeemɔi ni eeeha ale kɛ nɔ ni jɛɔ nifeemɔi ni tamɔ nɛkɛ mli baa lɛ kpojiemɔ nɔ. Nɔ ni fata enɛ he ji shika loo nibii krokomɛi ni Nɔyeli lɛ kɛ ehe fɔ̃ɔ nɔ lɛ ahe gbɛnaa nii.

Nɔ ni akɛhaa: Gbɛ ni atsɔɔ nɔ ajaa loo ajaa nibii ni yɔɔ maŋ nɔyeli nitsumɔhei, nitsumɔhei, kɛ nitsumɔhei (MDAs).

Nɔmimaa: Enɛ ji gbɛ ni atsɔɔ nɔ ahaa naagba ko mli waaa tsɔ.

BSTEM (Shishijee jeŋ nilee, ŋaalee, tsɔne he nilee, kɛ Akuntaabuu): wiemɔ ko ni akɛbuaa nikasemɔ he nikasemɔi nɛɛ anaa. Akɛ wiemɔ nɛɛ tsuɔ nii titri kɛ́ akɛ tsɔsemɔ he mlai kɛ nikasemɔ he gbɛjianɔtoo ni ahalaa yɛ skul lɛ miitsu nii koni akɛha akaŋshii ni yaa hiɛ yɛ jeŋ nilee kɛ ŋaalee mli lɛ aya hiɛ.

Shika he gbɛjianɔtoo: Shika he gbɛjianɔtoo ji gbɛjianɔtoo ko ni tsɔɔ he ni esa akɛ aná shika kɛjɛ kɛ nɔ ni esa akɛ afite. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔ nɔ lɛ, weku ko baanyɛ afee shika he gbɛjianɔtoo ko ni baatsɔɔ shika abɔ ni yɔɔ kɛ bɔ ni esa akɛ akɛtsu nii dani afite shika (akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, shĩa nyɔmɔwoo, shika ni akɛwoɔ shĩa mli kɛ niyenii). Kɛ shika babaoo he miihia lɛ, ehe baahia ni weku lɛ asusu shika babaoo ni amɛaaná loo shika ni amɛfaa lɛ he.

Shika ni faaa lɛ he nyɔmɔwoo: Enɛ baa mli kɛ́ Nɔyeli lɛ shika ni efiteɔ lɛ fe shika ni enáa lɛ.

Shika ni akɛwoɔ nitsumɔ mli: Enɛ ji shika ni akɛtsuɔ nibii wuji tamɔ gbɛjegbɛi, skul, helatsamɔhei, blɔji, gbɛfaa, nu gbɛjianɔtoi, tsei kɛ tsɔnei ahe nii.

Nitsulɔi ni awoɔ amɛ nyɔmɔ: Enɛ ji nyɔmɔwoo kɛ nyɔmɔwoo kɛ nyɔmɔwoo ni kɔɔ nyɔmɔwoo he tamɔ gbɔmɛi ashihilɛ he shweshweeshwefeemɔ, shika ni akɛhaa mɛi kɛ pɛnshɛn ni awoɔ maŋ nitsulɔi lɛ.

Nɔyaa Hefatalɔi (DPs): Enɛ tsɔɔ maji kɛloo gbɛjianɔtoi ni kɛ maji ni eyaa hiɛ lɛ feɔ ekome kɛtsuɔ nii koni amɛshɛ amɛ hiɛyaa otii lɛ ahe. DPs baanyɛ afee nɔ ni yɔɔ srɔtoi, nɔkwɛmɔnɔ. Nibii ni akɛhaa: Enɛɛmɛi ji shika he yelikɛbuamɔ henɔi ni mɛi ni kɛ amɛhe woɔ hiɛyaa mli lɛ kɛhaa Nɔyeli lɛ, ni ehe ehiaaa ni awo Nɔyeli lɛ nyɔmɔ ekoŋŋ.

Nibii ni akɛhaa Nɔyelii Akui krokomɛi: Enɛɛmɛi ji shika ni akɛwoɔ shika mli ni mla biɔ ni akɛwo shika ni ato he gbɛjianɔ tamɔ Kpokpaa nɔ KpeeiMaŋ Shika (DACF), Gbɛjegbɛ nɔ Shika, Ghana Tsɔsemɔ He Shika (GETFUND), Petroleum Shika kɛ Maŋ Hewalɛ Shika.

Gross Domestic Product (GDP): Enɛ tsɔɔ naagbee nibii fɛɛ ni ataoɔ kɛ nitsumɔi ni afeɔ yɛ maŋ lɛ mli yɛ be pɔtɛɛ ko mli lɛ jara fɛɛ.

Gross International Reserves: Enɛ haa maŋ ko maŋsɛɛ shika nyɛɔ eheɔ nibii kɛ nitsumɔi kɛjɛɔ maŋsɛɛ maji amli fe ekome .
