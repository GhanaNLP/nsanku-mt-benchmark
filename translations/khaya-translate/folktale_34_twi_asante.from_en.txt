Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐKA SƐ OBIARA A ƆWƆ ADAMFO NSƐE ƆMANFO FOFORO A ƆWƆ SPRANG HO NSƐM

Bere bi na ɔbɔmmɔfo bi wɔ hɔ. na ɔsɔreeɛ no na ɔkɔ wuram kɔhwehwɛ agorɔ kum na ɔnya bi didi na ebinom nso tɔn Afei da bi ɔkɔɔ wuram; ɔtee sɛ Kokotee, Bush-pig, refrɛ n'abusuafoɔ sɛ, "Kokotee Asamoa e!" Obuae sɛ, "Yiw, onua, yiw." Ɔno (mprako a odi kan) kae sɛ, "Bere a yɛde bɛyɛ adwuma (wɔ yɛn mfuw mu) adu, enti momma yɛnkɔ adwumfo no atoro no ase, na ɔno na ɔyɛɛ dade no na ɔde ano to (yɛn nnwinnade a yɛde twa) so, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a, sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ yetwa nnua biara a, ebia yebetumi atwa." Berɛ a Bush-pig frɛɛ ne nuanom no, abɔmmɔfoɔ no de ne ho sieeɛ, na ɔtee nkɔmmɔdie no nyinaa. Afei Bush-pig (nea edi kan) no nuabarima bisaa ne nua Bush-pig sɛ, "Na akuraa bɛn na yɛbɛkɔ, sɛ yɛbɛma wɔabɔ dade no?" Obuae sɛ, "Yɛbɛkɔ akuraa a wɔfrɛ no Across-the-stream no ase." ɛnna ɔkaa sɛ da bɛn na wobɛyɛ? Obuae sɛ, "Memeneda." Abɔmmɔfo no tee nhyehyɛe yi nyinaa, na ofii hɔ kɔɔ fie. na afei ɔbaa fie na ɔkaa asɛm yi kyerɛɛ ɔpanin a ɔfiri apueeɛ fam no sɛ ɛyɛ deɛ ɔteeɛ berɛ a ɔkɔɔ wiram no na abɔmmɔfoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ akuraa no ase panin no sɛ momma mmɔfra no nkɔtwa nnua na wɔmfa mmra na wɔmfa mmra na sɛ ɛduru memeneda a ɛnneɛ bus mmɛdane wɔn ho na yɛmfa nkɔ nnipa mu na yɛmmɛkyekyere nnua no ho Mmofra no kɔe na wɔtwaa nnua no, na wɔde bae. Ɔpanyin a ɔhwɛ akode so no kɔka kyerɛɛ akuraa no ase ɔtomfoɔ no sɛ ɔmmɔ dade pon mma no. na ɔtomfoɔ no bisaa ɔbarima a ɔdi hene no sɛ ɛnneɛ dadeɛ nnoɔma bebree yi nyinaa na moka sɛ ɛtwa sɛ meboro wɔn sɛ ɛdeɛn na mode bɛyɛ wɔn? na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɛyɛɛ dɛn na ɔbɔfoɔ bi kɔɔ wiram na ɔsane ba bɛbɔɔ no amanneɛ sɛ ɛdwoada no mmoa bi bɛdane wɔn ho ama nnipa na wɔayɛ wɔn bɔne sɛ wɔde nnwinnadeɛ a ɛyɛ hu bɛba Odumgyafoɔ no tuu mmirika sɛ ɔrekɔboro dadeɛ ahyɛnsodeɛ no ntɛm so. Bere a ɔpanyin no wiee ne nsa a ɔde guu nnua ne nneɛma atitiriw no so ara pɛ no, ɔmaa kurow no mu nsɛmmɔnedifo no boroo dade pon no, na ɔkae sɛ, “Dwoda, sɛ́ ɛyɛ ɔbea, anaasɛ ɛyɛ ɔbarima no, ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara kɔ baabiara.” na dawid duruu hɔ na adeɛ kyee anɔpa no akɔkoraa bi ka kyerɛɛ ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no sɛ kɔ na kɔ na kɔgya mako ne nkyene ne nkyene na faako a mmoa no bɛyiyi wɔn honam na wɔto gu fam no wo nso wo na sɛ wokɔ hɔ a ɛnneɛ fa mako no kɔ na fa petepete ɛserɛ no so na sɛ mmoa no ba ha na sɛ yɛgye wɔn na yɛdwane kɔ kɔfa wɔn nwoma na yɛde gugu hɔ na sɛ mako no yɛ wɔn ya na wɔto wɔn twene a ɛnneɛ wɔbɛyɛ nnipa foforɔ bio Abɔmmɔfo no fii hɔ kɔhintaw hɔ. na ɔtee sɛ wɔsu frɛɛ kokotee esau dawid aba ma yɛnkɔ ɛnna wɔn nyinaa baeɛ na wɔyiyii wɔn nwoma mu na wɔde guu hɔ Afei, (Mprako no mu baako) a na ɔyɛ nhabamma ho dɔkota no ka ho, na ɔfaa ne honam de too hɔ baabi foforo. Abɔmmɔfo no hwɛɛ wɔn, na wogyaee wɔn kɔe. na ɔkɔfaa nwoma no nyinaa na ɔde petepetee mako a wɔde agu wɔn soɔ no na ɔfae na ɔde guu asuo no mu na ɔmaa nsuo no kɔe na ɔbɔfoɔ no deɛ ɔyii no firii nkaeɛ no mu na ɔbɔfoɔ no anhunu na ɔbɔfoɔ no baa fie na ɔsafohene no nso frɛɛ nkurɔfoɔ no boaboaa wɔn ho ano twaa ɔtomfoɔ no nsusuiɛ no ho hyiae na ɔmaa wɔbaa ne fie na saa nkurɔfoɔ yi baa awurade fie na wɔbisaa awurade na wɔkaa sɛ adɛn nti na woafrɛ yɛn Ɔpanin no kaa sɛ, "Na moyɛ me mmarima, na modwanedwane kɔtenaa baabi foforɔ; ɛnnɛ da yi deɛ moasane aba, ɛno nti na mese ɛsɛ sɛ moba, ɛfiri sɛ meremma mo kwan bio." Mmoa no see ɔpanin no sɛ deɛ woaka yi yɛateɛ nanso yɛnim sɛ ɔbɔadeɛ no abɔfoɔ no ba bɛkaa yɛn ho nsɛm nyinaa kyerɛɛ mo nanso ɛnyɛ hwee sɛ yɛne mo nyinaa bɛtena abom nanso yɛnim sɛ deɛ moka no yɛ atoro; ne nyinaa mu no, ɛno mfa ho koraa; yɛda saa ɔbɔmmɔfo no ase. Enti yɛne mo nyinaa bɛbom atra; nanso adeɛ baako wɔ hɔ a yɛbɔ no akyiwadeɛ, a ɛne sɛ mobɛda yɛn mfitiaseɛ adi; anaasɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mo manfoɔ no mu biara da yɛn mfitiaseɛ adi; sɛ ɛba saa a, yɛbɛbubu yeinom, mo abusuakuo no, na yɛafiri hɔ.” Afei, berɛ a Ɔpanin no kɔɔ wɔn nkyɛn kɔfrɛɛ wɔn no, dɔkota no ne ebinom dwaneeɛ. na aduroyɛfoɔ no kɔɔ sɛ ɔrekɔfa ne honam na ɔdwanee nanso wɔn a wɔaka no sanee wɔn akyi Ɛno firi sɛ wɔantumi anhu wɔn nwoma. na ɔsafohene no penee tebea a mmoa no de too hɔ no so na wɔne mmoa no boom tenaa ase anigyeɛ mu Afei mmarima a wɔwɔ akuraa no ase waree mmoa a wɔyɛ mmoa no bi; wɔwoo mma. afei da bi na mmoa no mu baako ne akuraa no asefoɔ no mu baako de wɔn nsateaa reko ɛno akyiri no, akuraa no aseni no ka kyerɛɛ aboa no sɛ, “Fa wo ho firi hɔ, aboa bi a ɔte sɛ wo a ɔyɛ Bush-pig fekuo no dea.” na ntɛm ara na ɔkaa saa sɛ mmoa nyinaa aniwa bɛyɛɛ kɔkɔɔ na wɔkɔɔ ɔsafohene fie kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɛdeɛn na yɛka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ afei deɛ ɛreba yi deɛ ɛdeɛn na wobɛyɛ na ɔsafohene no de wɔn kɔe na ɔfrɛɛ wɔn a wɔde akasakasa yi baeɛ no Ɔpanin no hwɛɛ asɛm no mu yie na ɔbuu atɛn sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mmoa no gyae soboɔ no, ɛfiri sɛ (ɔkaa sɛ) ɛyɛ akyɛreɛ firi berɛ a wɔbaa hɔ na yei ne deɛ ɛdi kan a obiara kaa bi kyerɛɛ wɔn pɛn (ɛfa wɔn mfitiaseɛ ho). Nanso mmoa no kaa sɛ, “Yɛnnye ntom.” na ɔpanin no kaa sɛ morentie na modwene sɛ deɛ ɔbarima yi kaeɛ no yɛ atorɔ anaa na monyɛ bush-pigs na mmoa no kaa sɛ o awurade yɛate Ɛno akyi no, mmoa no ne nnipa a wɔwɔ nsuo no mu nyinaa koeɛ. Mmoa no sɛee akuraa no ase, kosii sɛ nnipa bɛyɛ du pɛ na ɛkaa hɔ. na wɔsrɛɛ mmɔborɔhunu na wɔka kyerɛɛ mmoa no sɛ wɔn na wɔwɔ wɔn afa Mmoa no tieeɛ, na wɔbɔɔ ɔmanfoɔ no amanneɛ sɛ, "Asɛm bi a yɛaka dada no nyɛ den sɛ yɛbɛte aseɛ, na sɛ yɛne wo bɛtena faako a, yɛtwe yɛn ho firi nsɛm a yɛaka afa yɛn mfitiaseɛ ho no ho. Sɛ wususuw ho anaasɛ woka ho asɛm bi bio a, ɛnde (yɛbɛsrɛ wo) kyerɛ yɛn saa beaeɛ a yɛbaa ha no, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a yɛbɛsane akɔ akyire.” Na nkurɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Yɛrentumi nyɛ biribi a ɛte saa bio.” Nea yɛayɛ ama yɛn abusuakuw no asɛe, yɛrenyɛ saa bio.” ɛnna wɔmaa badwam mpaemuka baa wɔn nkyɛn sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a wɔrenka ɔfoforɔ ho asɛm biara nkyerɛ ɔfoforɔ, anyɛ saa a anka nsɛm a wɔbɛda no adi no bɛsɛe kuro no. Eyi, m'asɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom betumi afa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no, wubetumi akamfo me (sɛ woka ho asɛm a).
