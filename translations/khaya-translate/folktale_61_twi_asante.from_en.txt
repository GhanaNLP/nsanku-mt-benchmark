Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ OBI BƐKƆ ROOM SƐ WƆKYERƐKYERƐMU A, Ɛyɛ NKWASƐM

WƆN nso kaa sɛ, Kunturunku, Odds, ne Kuntunamoa, a wɔfrɛ wɔn Ends, firi mpomma mu bɛhwee fam. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Wɔn a wɔn mfe akɔ anim-asase, woyɛ petee dodo." Maame Earth kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woka sɛ me nso meyɛ petee?" na wɔbua see wɔn sɛ ɛfiri sɛ moantutu amoa bi amma yɛn nna mu Nyini-asase danee kɔɔ Prae, Broom, na ɔkaa sɛ, "W'ano (wɔakata so) nnomaa a wɔtow wɔn agu." Obuae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woyɛ me basabasa?" Nkwakoraa ne mmerewa-asase kae sɛ, "Sɛ mayɛ wo ayayade a, mayɛ wo ayayade; sɛ woante nea enti a ɛte saa ase a, gyae bisa." Broom danee kɔɔ Abena, The-strip-of-bark (a wɔprapra pow kɔ mu), na wɔkae sɛ, "Wofa (nneɛma) na kɔtow gu." Strip-of-bark kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woyɛ me basabasa?" Broom kaa sɛ, "(Sɛnea mɛyɛ biara) mayɛ saa." Strip-of-bark nso kasa kyerɛɛ Sumina, Midden-heap, sɛ, "Woakora nea wunya so." Midden-heap kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woyɛ me basabasa?" Ɔno (Strip-of-bark) buae sɛ, "Mayɛ wo ayayade, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a ɛte saa a, ɛnde gyae bisa." Midden-heap nso kasa kyerɛɛ Abe, Palm-tree, sɛ, "Wo kɔn dɔɔso dodo wɔ nwura mu." Palm-tree kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woyɛ me basabasa?" Midden-heap buae sɛ, "Mayɛ wo ayayade, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a ɛte saa a, ɛnde gyae nsɛmmisa." Saa ara nso na paulo nso danee ne ho kyerɛɛ tetea, na ɔkaa sɛ, "W'aso yɛ tuntum." Nnipa kakraa bi a wɔyɛ tuntum kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woyɛ me basabasa?" Palm-tree buae sɛ, "Mayɛ wo ayayade, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a ɛte saa a, gyae bisa." Ɔno nso a na ɔyɛ tuntum kakraa bi no kɔɔ Esono, Asono a ɔwɔ hɔ no nkyɛn, na ɔkɔɔ ne nan ase; na ɔtwitwaa Asono no mu. Bere a ɔno (Ɔsono) rema ne nan so no, ɔde ne nan sii Tamariwa aniwa no ase pɛɛ, ɔde ne nsa bɔɔ Tuntum, na ɔde ne nsa fii ase nsu, na ɔpopaa nsu no wɔfan ahaban (ɛrenyin) a ɛbɛn hɔ no so. Afei, aduane a ɔbaapanin ne nana di ne efan dua, na ɛhɔ na na Tamariwa a wɔfrɛ no Tuntum no apopa nsuo no afiri n'ani so. Na Kraman, Ɔkraman no retwam, na ɔno (Ɔkraman-Nana) frɛɛ Ɔkraman no na ɔfaa nnua (ɔsoa) na ɔmaa Ɔkraman no soaa no ne tiri so. Bere a Ɔkraman rekɔ no, onyaa kola-nut, na ɔpaapaee mu abien, na ɔtow biako guu Okagyafo, Squirrel no tokuru no ano. Ɔno nso faa kola-nut no bɔɔ Onyina, Silk-cotton-dua no. Silk-cotton-dua no nso, abufuw baa ne nofo mu. na ɔhunuu sɛ abɔfra kumaa bi nante na ɔsunsuanee ne nkorabata mu na ɔbɔɔ no aku no Afei, na saa abofra kumaa no yɛ Nyankonton, wim-nyame no ba. Ɛhɔ ne afei wogyee Silk-cotton-dua no, na wɔde kɔɔ Wim-nyame anim. Wobisaa no (Silk-cotton-tree), na wɔkae sɛ, "Ne nyinaa fa ho dɛn?" Silk-cotton-tree no kaa sɛ, "Na megyina hɔ wɔ m'ankasa me nkyɛn baabi, bere a saa Squirrel yi—akokoaa a wɔwoo no—(a wɔkyerɛ no) sɛ ɔbɛma kola-nut so na ɔde abɔ me, enti me deɛ, berɛ a saa abɔfra yi retwam no, mesusuu sɛ ɔyɛ Okagya, Squirrel, na meyii me nkorabata no mu baako bɔɔ no, na hwɛ na ɛyɛ wo ba." Nyame a ɔwɔ wim no kaa sɛ, "Fa no kyekyere no kyekyere dua bi ho, na ɔfrɛɛ Okagya a ɔyɛ Squirrel no sɛ ɔmmra." Okagya bae; ɔkaa sɛ, "Owura wonim sɛ me taboo ne kola-nut, nanso Okraman, Ɔkraman no kɔe na ɔnyaa bi, na ɔtoo guu me tokuru no ano, na bere a mereto agu no, ɔkɔbɔɔ Old-man-silk-cotton-tree." Ɔsoro nyankopɔn no kaa sɛ, "Fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho na frɛ ɔkraman no sɛ ɔmmra." Wɔkɔfrɛɛ ɔkraman no sɛ ɔmmra. Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Owura, na menam m'ankasa m'afuw mu baabi —seesei wohunu sɛdeɛ (adɔeɛ) mewɔ hɔ —bere a na Ɔpanin-ɔbaa-nana faa ne nnua a wɔaboaboa ano de guu me tirim no; seesei Okagya, Squirrel, ɔno na ɔda ne tokuru mu wɔ afuo no mu sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛnya biribi adi, nanso ɛyɛ me na nana-nanana na ɔsoaa ne gya; Me nso, mefaa kola-nut na ɔtow guu ne tokuru no ano." Ɔsoro nyankopɔn kaa sɛ, "Fa no kyekyere dua bi ho na frɛ nana panyin na ɔmmra." Ɔbea panyin nanabea bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ah, me nananom, hwɛ m'anum, me a merentumi nwe nam,fan ahaban yɛ deɛ medi; afei, meguare Bosompra; me taboo ne Tamariwa, Black-nail; Ɔkraman no nko ara na na na onim eyi, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ Tamariwa, na ɔmaa oyii nsu fii n'anom, na ɔde guufan ahaban a mete so no mu. ɛno nti na memaa no me nnua sɛ ɔnsoa no Ɔsoro nyankopɔn se, "Oh, fa no kyekyere dua bi ho na kɔ na fa Tamariwa a ɔyɛ Tuntum no ba." Bɔwerɛw a ɛyɛ tuntum no bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Owura, na me nko ara mewɔ baabi, hwɛ, ɔsono kɛseɛ kɛseɛ bi a wɔfrɛ no trod wɔ beaeɛ hɔ wɔ m'ani ase pɛɛ, na mekɔfaa ɔkraman bi (ɔkraman) popaa no. Ɛno ara ne nea minim fa ho." Onyankopɔn a ɔwɔ wim no kaa sɛ, "Fa no kyekyere no kyekyere dua bi ho, na fa Esono a ɔyɛ Asono ba." Wɔkɔe kɔfaa Esono. Asono no kaa sɛ, "Bɛyɛ nnansa a atwam ni na na mete hɔ, na me honam ani fii ase teɛteɛɛm, na bere a mehwɛɛ beae hɔ no—ɛnam asɛmfua a mede dii dwuma no so —saa nnaadaa yi na Little-black-ant no akɔ mu (me honam ani) sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛhwehwɛ nam a ɔbɛwe, na ebia anyin kɛse; na bere a meretwe me nan no, ɛkɔɔ so trod wɔ Tamariwa, Black-nail so." Onyankopɔn a ɔwɔ wim no kaa sɛ, "Fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na fa Tetea a ɔyɛ Tuntum Ketewa no bra." Tea baeɛ no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Owura, Palm-dua bi wɔ hɔ; sɛ ɛsoa a, (ne ntaa) yɛ tuntum; sɛ me, Kakra a ɔyɛ tuntum no nantew wɔn so a, ɛma bere kɔ soro. na adeɛ kyeeɛ no mewɔ hɔ na palm-doma no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ m'aso yɛ tuntum na ɛsiane sɛ meyɛ ketewa nti mesusuu sɛ mɛfiri adi makɔ asono a ɛwɔ hɔ no nkyɛn akɔhwehwɛ nam adi na manyɛ kɛseɛ na ɔsoro nyankopɔn kaa sɛ momfa no na wɔmfa no nkyekyere dua bi mu na monkɔ na monkɔfrɛ no mmɛbɔ mmra Abe a ɔyɛ Palm-dua no bae; ɔkaa sɛ, “Owura, sei na ɛsɛ sɛ meka: Me ne Sumina, Midden-heap, bom tra, na sɛ nwura a wɔatow agu no so no dɔɔso dodo, na ɔrentumi nnsoa bio a, megye bi na mede gu me kɔn ho; nanso afei ɔyɛɛ me ayayade, na ɔkae sɛ mapow me kɔn. Ɛno nti, me deɛ, m'aniwa yɛɛ kɔkɔɔ, na me nso meyɛɛ Little-tuntum no basabasa.” na ɔsoro nyankopɔn kaa sɛ momfa no nkɔkyekyere no na monkɔfrɛ sumina a ɔyɛ midden-heap na ɔmmra Midden-heap na ɔbaeɛ; ɔkaa sɛ, "Owura, Abena, Strip-of-bark, a sɛ wɔprapra nwura no mu wɔ dan bi mu a, wɔma no sɛ ɔno nso betumi de aba me nkyɛn." na adeɛ kyeeɛ no ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ sie deɛ wo nsa aka yi yɛ adifudepɛfo); mekaa sɛ, 'a', na sɛnea na Palm-dua no bɛn no, me nso, meyɛɛ no ayayade." na ɔsoro nyankopɔn kaa sɛ momfa no nkyekyere no mmra nkyɛn na monfrɛ no Abena na ɔmmra Strip-of-bark bae; ɔkaa sɛ, “Owura Prae, Broom, popa dan bi mu, sɛ wɔamma me (n'aprannaa), sɛ mframa no bɔ a, nwura no nyinaa san ba bio; afei, ade kyee no, ɛwom sɛ manyɛ no hwee de, nanso ɔkae sɛ, ‘Wofa nneɛma de tow gu nkutoo.’ Ɛno nti, me nso m'ani bɛyɛɛ kɔkɔɔ, na mekɔyɛɛ Sumina, Midden-heap no ayayade. Ɔsoro nyankopɔn kaa sɛ, "Fa no kyekyere dua bi ho na frɛ Prae a ɔyɛ Broom no sɛ ɔmmra ha." Draw no bae; ɔkaa sɛ, “Owura, nkokɔ-na-asase da hɔ ma ɛyɛ tratraa, ne soro yɛ fĩ, enti sɛ mesɔre a, mepra efĩ no nyinaa gu ne so na mema ne ho yɛ fɛ; nanso ade kyee no, ɛna-asase kasa kyerɛɛ me sɛ, “Nnomaa a wɔtow gu (ahyɛ w’anom ma,’ ne me nufu nso mu ma, na mekɔyɛɛ Abena, Strip-of-bark ayayade.” na ɔsoro nyankopɔn kaa sɛ momfa no mmɛkyekyere no na monfrɛ ɛna ne asaase mmra Bere a ɛna-asase bae no, ɔkae sɛ, "Owura, dada wɔ hɔ, wɔtiatia me so, menka hwee da; wɔyɛ nsɛm ahodoɔ nyinaa gu me so, nanso menka hwee da. Afei da a edi hɔ a mekɔɔ hɔ no, Kuntukrunku ne Kuntunamoa, Odds and Ends, hwee me so kaa sɛ, 'O!' asɛm bɛn na ɛrekɔ so? na wɔkaa sɛ monntu amena a yɛbɛtumi adi atorɔ mu na mo ne mo tenabea nyinaa nni hɔ mma obiara Ɛno nti, me nso meyɛɛ Dwoa no basabasa.” Onyankopɔn a ɔwɔ wim no kaa sɛ, "Fa no kyekyere dua bi ho na kɔ na kɔgye Odds and Ends." Odds ne Ends bae. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Ná yɛwɔ mpomma mu na na yɛredi agorɔ, na yɛhwee ase na yɛpiraa yɛn ho, ɛsiane sɛ yɛyɛɛ ɛna-asase ayayadeɛ nti, yɛkaa sɛ ɛyɛ petee." na ewiem nyankopɔn kaa sɛ awurade yɛate asɛm yi nyinaa enti momma yɛnsɔre na yɛnkɔ ɛdan no mu na yɛnhwɛ adanseɛ na yɛmmra mmɛbu atɛn ɛnna wɔsɔre kɔɔ ɛdan bi mu na wɔtoo pono no mu Ɛno nti, sɛ wokɔ dan bi mu anadwo a,—abibifo—wim nyame ne n’Aban Asoɛe no resusuw ho wɔ hɔ.

Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
