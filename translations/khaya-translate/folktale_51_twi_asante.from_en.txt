Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA ƐNO MU NSƐM MU NSƐM, NKYERƐKYERƐMU A ƐFA ƆBƆFO HO, NA MA AKOKO, ƆHENE NO MMRA HO

AkoKO, Hen, ne Aturukuku, Turtle-dove no fi agya biako mu na efi ɛna biako hɔ. Afei ɔhene no ka kyerɛɛ Turtle-dove sɛ, "Firi adi kɔ Tall-grass man no mu, na me nso mɛkɔ ɔman a ɛso yɛ den-kwae mu, na sɛ wo nsa ka aduane a ɛnneɛ wobɛba abɛka akyerɛ me." Afei wɔtetew mu. Aturuku de mfe abiɛsa anhu ne nuabea. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne kunu sɛ, “Accompany me, ɛfiri sɛ mɛhwehwɛ me nuabaa no.” ɛnna ne kunu kaa sɛ sɛ wokasa a mate na wofiri wo nkyɛn kɔ na ma me ne wo nkɔ na ɔne no sii mu kɔduruu kuro bi a ne nuabaa te mu na wɔhunuu ɛserɛ so bepɔ bi wɔ hɔ na ɛhɔ na ɔne ne nuabaa hyiaeɛ ɛnna ɔpanin no bisaa Turtle-dove no sɛ, "Wɔkɔɔ Tall-grass-man no mu no, wonyaa aduane dii anaa?" Obuae sɛ, "Baabi a mekɔeɛ no, na guinea-grain nko ara na ɛwɔ hɔ; na bere a mebɔɔ me mma no, bɛyɛ asia, na wɔde wɔn ho bataa me ho, na bere a wɔyɛɛ akɛse te sɛ yei (a ɛkyerɛ wɔn kɛse) no, memaa wɔbɛn me, na ɔbɔmmɔfo bi too ne tuo, pom! na ɔkumm me mma na sɛ mente aseɛ a anka wɔn nso wɔbɛkum me na ɔwiee kasa no ɔkaa sɛ, "Baabi a mekɔeɛ no, amonom hɔ ara mebɛyɛɛ obi a ne ho hia pa ara." Aturuku bisaa no sɛ, “Ka nea worepɛ akyerɛ ankasa kyerɛ me. ɛnna ɔpanin no kaa sɛ sɛ nhwɛsoɔ ne deɛ ɛkyerɛ ne sɛ, sɛ me wura tete kɛtɛ so a ɛnneɛ mekɔda so na me hweɛ hwe aseɛ na sɛ me wura fa nam hyɛ nkrantɛ ano a ɛnneɛ meyi deɛ mepɛ na me wura bɛdi aduane biara a me wura bɛdi akɔfa bi na sɛ medi saa a me wura ntumi nnye ne tuo anaa ne poma na ɔnhwe me Ɛno nti na meka sɛ meyɛ obi a me ho hia no ankasa.” Ɔkaa sɛ, "Baabi a woteɛ wɔ hɔ no, ɛnyɛ baabi papa nti Memeneda, Kwasida ansa na Adae reba no, fa wo mma ne wo kunu bra, na ma wɔn nyinaa ntra yɛn fie." Aturuku se, “Mate, mɛkɔ, na Memeneda a ɛdi Kwasiada Adae anim no, mɛba.” afei ɔsɔfoɔ bi baa yerusalem akuraa ase na ɔwosoo ne dɔn nketewa no Wɔbisaa no sɛ, “Nana, ɛyɛ dɛn?” Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dente se woama no kwan sɛ ɔkɔm de no mfeɛ mmienu ni enti ɛtwa sɛ wokɔ ɔpanin no nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛ no sɛ fa nkosua dummienu brɛ." Afei Ɔpanyin no kɔe na ɔkyeree nnomaa baanan de nkosua dumien bae na ɔfaa eto na ɔbɔɔ mu na ɔkɔɔ onyame Dente nkyɛn wɔ ne ntweneduro afɔrebukyia no ase, na ɔtwitwaa nnomaa baanan no ti na ɔde wɔn sii hɔ, na ɔfaa wɔn ntakra ne wɔn ti ne wɔn nan na ɔde wɔn bɔɔ atɛkyɛ afɔrebukyia no nkyɛn; na ɔsan nso bataa eto a ɛyɛ yaw no ho. Afei, da a edi Adae anim no, na Aturuku rekɔhyia ne nuabea. na ɔrekɔ no ɔhunuu sɛ afɔrebukyia no so ayɛyɛdeɛ kronkron ma dente ne nuabaa agyanom ne ne nantin ne ne ntaban ne ne mmerɛboɔ ne ne nsono mu Aturuku kaa sɛ, “ Yei nyinaa akyiri no, mintumi nkɔ hɔ bio.” Na akyekyedeɛ-dodoɔ tu firii hɔ bɛtɛɛ dua bi a wɔde sirikyi ayɛ so, na wɔkaa sɛ: "Mesu me nuabea o, Sorrow o.

Wo a na woyɛ obi a ne ho hia kakra no, so eyi yɛ wo ti?

Wo a na woyɛ obi a ne ho hia bi no, so eyinom yɛ wo ntaban?

Wo a na woyɛ obi a ne ho hia kakra no, wo nan ase na ɛteɛ anaa?

Wo a na woyɛ obi a ne ho hia kakra no, so eyi ne wo brɛboɔ?

Wo a na woyɛ obi a ne ho hia wo pa ara ne wo dwonsɔtoa no?

Na Aturuku kaa sɛ, "Merenkɔ kwaeɛ man no mu bio, ɛfiri sɛ kwaeɛ mu nnipa nyɛ papa." Saa na Aturuku bɛtenaa Tall-grass ɔman no mu, berɛ a Ɔhemmaa no kɔɔ so tenaa fie hɔ no.

Eyi, m'asɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom betumi afa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no, wubetumi akamfo me (sɛ woka ho asɛm a).
