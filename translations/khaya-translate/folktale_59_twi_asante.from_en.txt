Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SAA 'KRAMAN, DOG, WƆrentumi NHUHYƐ HISƐM (AKWANKYERƐ) ho NSƐM DA.

Bere bi na ɔkraman bi wɔ hɔ, na esiane saa su yi nti, na ontumi nnya ɔbea (wɔ n'akuraa ase) nware no. ɛnna ɔfirii adi kɔɔ kuro bi mu kɔ kɔfaa ɔbaa bi ne no kɔe (Ɔwaree, na ɔne no san kɔɔ n'akuraa ase.) na ɔne ne yere tenaa hɔ da koro na ɔfirii adi na ɔsane kɔɔ n'akuraa ase ɛberɛ bi akyiri no ne kunu no de asɛm kɔmaa no sɛ ɔreba hɔ N'asew no faa ne tuo na ɔkɔɔ wuram sɛ ɔrekɔkum antelope de asiesie ne ho ama Ɔkraman no ba. bɛyɛ nnansa akyiri no ɔkraman no kɔɔ hɔ na ɔkɔe na ɔkɔhunuu sɛ antelope nam da ogya no mu wɔ adiwo hɔ anadwo duruiɛ no ɔne ne yere kɔgyee wɔn ahome Ná ɔkraman no rekɔ abɔnten akɔyɛ nsu, bere a ohui sɛ nam no a srade no rebɔ kɔ ogya no mu no. Ɔkraman no san bae na ɔkɔtoo fam na ɔde ne ti too sumii no so (na ɔdaa hɔ) na ɔhwɛɛ nam no ware. Afei na nam no hua adu ne so, ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛsoa, nanso wantumi. Oo! ɔforo kɔɔ faako a nam no wɔ no ɔwee ne nyinaa na ɔsane kɔtoo fam Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu, bere a nneɛma daa adi, bere a ne yere rebɛprapra no, ɔhwehwɛɛ nam no, na ɛnyɛ biako mpo na ɛda hɔ. Ne yere kaa sɛ, "Oo, maame! o, ɛna! Ebinom awe nam no nyinaa." Bere a Ɔkraman no tee eyi no, n'ani wui (i.e.) na n'ani wuiɛ na ɔfaa ne ntoma kataa ne tiri so na ɔyɛɛ komm wɔ hɔ ɛnna amonom hɔ ara ɔsɔree na ɔfirii adi faa ɔkwan no so na ɔdwane kɔɔ n'akuraa ase na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ ɛyɛɛ dɛn na ɔkɔeɛ na ɔwee n'ase nam ne sɛdeɛ n'ani wuwuiɛ ɛnam aniwuo nti na ɛbaa sɛ ɛno nti ɔrekɔhwehwɛ obi a ɔwɔ aduro a wɔde ko tia adwennini nti ebia wɔbɛma no bi Nkorɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Adanko, Hare, wɔ bi pa ara, ɛwɔ tumi pa ara (aduruyɛ)." Ɔkraman no fii adi, na ɔde ahoɔhare duu Hare akuraa no ase. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya Hare, metu mmirika kɔ wo nkyɛn kɔhwehwɛ mmoa meyɛ owifoɔ, owifoɔ, na wɔka sɛ wowɔ aduro (ɛno yɛ aduro) ma ɛno nti mesrɛ wo, ma me bi, na nea ɛbɛyɛ biara no, mɛtua." Hare buae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nokware sɛ mewɔ bi, enti kɔ na fa mprako nam brɛ me."

Ɔkraman no sii mu kɔɔ akuraa bi ase na onyaa akyi nan biako maa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma mu duru (i.e.) 3s. 6d.) na ɔde bae na hare no noae na ɔtwitwaa mu na ɔteateaa mu wɔ nnua nketenkete so na ɔde mako ne nkyene ne nkyene guguu mu na ɔde guu nam no mu na ɔde guu ogya no mu Bere a ogya no tɔɔ mu no, bere bi akyi no, ɔno (Ɔkraman no) tee sɛ ɛrehuruw, e.e. Ɔkraman no hwɛɛ no kyɛe. Afei Hare kaa sɛ, "Dog, wokɔda adan no baako mu na me nso mɛda baako mu na adeɛ kyeeɛ no mɛyi saa nam yi afiri hɔ (ogya no) na mede ayɛ aduro ama wo." Ɔkraman no fii adi kɔdae. Hare no nso fii adi kɔdae. Afei ɔkraman no da so na ɔda (nanso wantumi anna) na nam no huam baa ne so wɔ ne hwenem nanso wankasa. Afei ɔsan kaa ho asɛm bio. Ɔkae sɛ, "Agya Hare." Hare no buae sɛ, "Yiw!" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mprako wɔ w'akuraa ase, enti mɛfa nam no, na mede ato me nkyɛn." Hare no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔgye; me deɛ deɛ medwene ho ara ne sɛ mede me nsa bɛto soɔ akɔsi owuo mu na mayɛ no aduro ama wo." Enti ɔkraman no kɔe kɔfae. Afei Ɔkraman no da hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, "Agya Hare." Obuae sɛ, "Yiw!" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mmire no bɛwe, enti mede bɛhyɛ me sumii ase, na mada." Hare no kaa sɛ, "Nea medwene ho ara ne sɛ, sɛ ɛkɔsi awieeɛ a nneɛma mu da hɔ a, mede bɛyɛ aduro ama wo." Ɔkraman no da hɔ bio; bɔ mmɔden sɛnea ɔpɛ, na ontumi nsiw ne ho kwan. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya hare, saa mmire yi aba me sumiiɛ ase nti mɛfa nam no na mede ato me tirim na ɛhɔ na megyina kɔsi sɛ nneɛma bɛda adi." Hare no buae sɛ, "Oo, mekɔ so ka sɛ, deɛ ɛhaw me ara ne sɛ menya (nam no) akɔsi awieeɛ de ayɛ nnuru ama wo." Ɔkraman no faa nam no de sii ne tiri so na ɛhɔ na ogyinae. Afei nkonimdifo no mu kakra daadaa n'anim, na ɔtaree. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oo, agya Hare, ɛnam yi nti, saa mmire no aforo agu me so, na wɔaba me tirim, enti mɛkura (nam no) wɔ m'anom." Hare no buae sɛ, "Oho o! Mekɔ so ka sɛ ade kyee a, sɛ nneɛma da adi a, mede bɛyɛ aduro ama wo." Afei na ɛyɛ mmuadadi wɔ n’anom, na ɔkyinkyin twaa dan no ho hyiae; ɔde ne dua (a ɔde nnyigyei a ɛsesa) bɔɔ ne tadua ho ban! awerɛhoɔ! awerɛhoɔ! Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya hare, ma menka wo ɛnam yi ho ka." Hare no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na wopɛ sɛ wode ka fa ho?" Ɔkraman no buae sɛ, "Mekyerɛ sɛ, sɛ anka ɛsɛ sɛ saa mmire yi di no, ma minnya wo ho akatua." Hare no kae sɛ, "Oh dabi! ma ɔkyena ahemadakye mmra Oo! Nanso seesei deɛ Ɔkraman no maa (nam no) kɔɔ fam. Afei ɔhwee ase dae. Ade kyee anɔpa, anɔpatutuutu koraa no, Hare kaa sɛ, "Dog, fa saa nam no bra." Ɔkraman no bua see no sɛ, "O, agya Hare, adɛn nti na woante asɛm a meka mekyerɛɛ wo nnɛra yi sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meka wo ka (ma nam no)?" Hare no buae sɛ, "Enti woadi no?" Ɔkraman no kae sɛ, "Yiw! Midii, efisɛ metɔɔ no." Hare no kaa sɛ, "Wo, Ɔkraman, korɔnobɔ a wɔfrɛ no 'theft' no rentwam da."

Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
