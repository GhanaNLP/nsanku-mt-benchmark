Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA NKYERƐKYERƐMU BA TRIBE no mu

Ɔbɔmmɔfo bi a ɔda so ara wɔ hɔ a na wɔfrɛ no Kwasi Gyaba, na bere bi ne yere kɔtraa nsraban bi mu sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛbɔ abɔmmɔ. Afei, Kwasi Gyaba kɔɔ wuram no, wamfa nam biara amma. Wamfa nam biara amma bio.

Afei da bi ne yere bisabisaa no nsɛm na ɔkaa sɛ "Megye di sɛ sɛ wokum nam a wotɔn no na wodi (deɛ wonya firi mu) anaasɛ wode ma akyɛdeɛ nti sɛ wokɔ kwaeɛ mu na woamfa nam ansane amfiri me maame nkyɛn bio ɛfiri sɛ meyare mpɔtorɔ ne mpataa ne nwa ne wɔn a wɔte saa." Ɔbɔmmɔfo no, Kwasi Gyaba, fii adi kɔɔ wuram. Ohui sɛ obi a ɔne no bɔ nkɔmmɔ retwam. Ɔkaa sɛ (ɔno ankasa), "Duyker, baabi a woda no, ɛhɔ na mɛgye m'ahome wɔ wo nkyɛn." Duyker no kɔgyinaa dua bi ase. Ɔno (ɔbɔfoɔ no) maa tuo no so, paulo! Python bi—baabi a efi papayɛ mu nkutoo na onim—daa Duykerfo so, na Leopard bi nso huruw guu so. Abɔmmɔfo no kɔɔ so yɛɛ komm. Ɔkae (kyerɛɛ ne ho), "Alas! Atoyerɛnkyɛm a mahu." Python no kaa sɛ, "Agya abɔmmɔfoɔ, bra, deɛ ɔredwene, ti foforɔ nso ate." ɛtɔ da a, abɔmmɔfoɔ, bra ha, na momma yɛnkɔ yɛn akuraa ase, na momma yɛnkyɛ duyker yi, ɛfiri sɛ yɛrenyɛ mo bɔne biara." Abɔmmɔfo no faa ne tuo dii wɔn akyi. Wɔkɔe na wɔkyekyɛɛ nam no. Python kaa sɛ, "Agya abɔmmɔfoɔ mepɛ w'asɛm enti meda ha da biara da na mede aduro bɛma wo na afei aduro yi na wode ahyɛ w'aso mu a wobɛte nhaban ne nnua ne nsuwansuwa nyinaa ne mmoa nyinaa anomu asɛm Yei nti anadwo yi kɔda, na mmom ahome." Abɔmmɔfo no dae, ogyaee ahome. Python no faa aduro de guu n'asom. Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma daa adi no, abɔmmɔfo no tee ade nyinaa kasa ase. ɔfirii adi na ɔbaa n'akuraa ase ɛnna dawid baeɛ no ɔtee sɛ n'asew barima aba ne babaa no nkyɛn fie Afei na n'ase ani afura wɔ n'ani nifa so. Abɔmmɔfo no kɔtraa dan no mu. Afei, ɔkasa ne ne benkum so nsɛm (i.e. me werɛ fii sɛ mɛka ho asɛm), ɔkaa sɛ berɛ a ɔhyiaa n'ase no, na ɔrebɔ aburoo. Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no tenaa hɔ komm, na nnomaa baanu a na wɔwɔ hɔ (fii ase kasae); obiako bisaa ɔfoforo no sɛ, "Wobɛyɛ dɛn atumi anya aburoo no, bere a mekɔ (kɔfa no) no, wɔtaa me kɔ?" Ɔfoforɔ no buaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wonhunuu sɛ ɔyɛ anifuraefoɔ wɔ n'ani nifa so, enti berɛ a (wopɛ) sɛ wofa aburoo no, sɛ wofa deɛ asɛe no mu a, ɔnhunu wo?" Kwasi Gyaba tee (eyi); ɔtow ne ho guu n'akyi serewee sɛ, "Yɛn ara!" Twe! Twe! n'ase na ɔkaa sɛ awurade nyankopɔn asɛm nie adɛn nti na woresere me saa yi? Nanso Kwasi Gyaba da fam serewee, na ɔkɔɔ so serewee. ɛhɔ na n'asew kaa ewiem nyankopɔn asɛm a wɔabara no guu ne so kaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ Kwasi Gyaba da ne serew adi ansa na ewiem nyame no anim Wim nyame no somaa abɔfo sɛ wɔmmɛfa Kwasi Gyaba mmra. Bere a oduu kurow no awiei no, ɔmaa ne dwom so sɛ:

"Seesei mɛkasa na mawu, Kwasi Gyaba e!

na mɛkasa na mawu owuo ɛba sɛ yei bɛba a ɛnsɛ sɛ wosu me Kwasi Mpuroko Gyaba, ɔrekɔ, Ɛba saa a ɛnsɛ sɛ wusu me.

Wim-nyame mpanyimfo kaa sɛ, “Tie ɔdeduani no dwom.” Wɔde Kwasi Gyaba kɔɔ wim-nyame anim. Okyeame (asɛmfua) sɔree na ɔkae sɛ, “Kwasi Gyaba, so woserew w’ase ankasa, anaasɛ ɔredi atoro?” Kwasi Gyaba buaa sɛ, "Owura, ɛsiane aduroyɛ bi nti, wɔ nkɛntɛnsoɔ a mewɔ aseɛ no, sɛ meka deɛ enti a (me sereɛ) mɛwu." na ɔsoro nyankopɔn kaa sɛ ɛsiane sɛ moyɛ m'abɔmmɔfoɔ nti meremma monnwu na n'asew kaa sɛ owura ɔredi atorɔ na ɔresere me kɛkɛ enti m'ani bɛgye biribi foforɔ biara ho sɛ nkyerɛkyerɛmu Ɔkasamafo no kae sɛ, “Kwasi Gyaba, woate?” Kwasi Gyaba buaa sɛ, "Afei mesrɛsrɛ ɔdamoa, na sɛ mawu a, fa me to mu." Ankyɛ koraa na n'ano gyinaa hɔ gyinaa hɔ, na ɛte sɛ kok ne ɔkokodurufo bi na ɔbaa hɔ; na Cock no ka kyerɛɛ Hen no sɛ, “Gyina hɔ na gyaa wo.” Ɔhene no kaa sɛ, “Dɛn, wɔ saa nkurɔfoɔ yi anim?” Cock kaa sɛ, "Sɛ woannyina hɔ a, mɛka asɛm a wɔabara no sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wubetumi ayi wo ho." Afei Kwasi Gyaba tee saa asɛm no. ntɛm ara ɔkaa sɛ, "Owura wim nyankopɔn ma wɔnnyae ɔdamoa bi a wɔtu fam, ɛfiri sɛ mewɔ asɛm bi ka." Wim-nyame no kae sɛ, “Kasa.” Kwasi Gyaba kaa sɛ, “Meka asɛm a wɔabara yi sɛ ebia m’asefoɔ bɛgye ne ba no afiri ne nsam.” Mpanimfoɔ se, "Ɛyɛ nokorɛ, ɛyɛ nokorɛ saa." ɛhɔ na ewiem nyame no hyɛɛ mmara bi sɛ sɛ ɔbaako mpo w'adamfo no haw wo a, ka asɛm a wɔabara no gu ne so anaasɛ ɛyɛ wo yere na ɔyɛ saa na ka asɛm a wɔabara no na gyae no.” ɛnna ɛbaa sɛ awaregyaeɛ baa abusuakuo no mu Ɛyɛ Cock a yɛyɛɛ ho mfonini no.

Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
