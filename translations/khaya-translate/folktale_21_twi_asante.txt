SEDIE OWE, ABOSOM-ACOBOD AND OPATUO

OWEA, Kwaduampon Kyereifo, Opokuo, and Abosom-Akotere, Odomankoma-wu, Afreyia-boa, Anoma-time-to-war Africa is yours. If we were to be poor, we would not be poor. and Adobe will sell it to us. If Adobe has gone to bed, she has gone to worship them; if she has gone to bed, she has to go there to worship them; if she has to, she has to. One day when he got to Odoankoma-death, he wanted to see Adobe. War broke out in Oye and Opoku. And we said, "Come and call him." Odoman-death:

" Adobe o! Adobe o!"

The sun said:

"Save! Don't let go! fight, come, come, give!

Opposuo said:

"If we drive this car, m! What a waste of time! m!"

And if you take three of these animals as yours, they will become yours.

If this is the case with me, if it is not the case with me, just take one and give it to me.
