5 Ghana Aban - 2022 Ɔman mma Sikasɛm

“Ɔman a Ɛkɔ So Daa a Wɔbɛkyekye: Asomdwoe a Ɛyɛ Paara ne Adwuma a Wɔbɔ”

NKYERƐKYERƐMU

Sikasɛm nyinaa te nka sɛ COVID-19 yareɛ no anya nsunsuansoɔ bɔne wɔ ɔman yi mu, a Ghana ka ho. Ɔhaw a Ghanafoɔ hyia wɔ wɔn nkwa, wɔn asetena ne nnwuma a wɔhwere mu no, aban no nhwere. Ɛyɛ me ahosɛpɛ sɛ meka sɛ sɛnea yɛyɛɛ yɛn ade wɔ nkɛntɛnso a ɔyaredɔm no anya no ho dwuma no yɛ akokoduru na ɛyɛ nea ɛyɛ den na nkurɔfo ani gye ho wɔ wiase nyinaa. Aban nso hyɛɛ nhyehyɛeɛ bi ase sɛ ɛbɛboa ama nnwuma asan akanyan wɔn na ama yɛn asetena asan akɔ tebea a ɛfata mu.

Bio nso, wɔ ahunahuna ne hokwan ahodoɔ a ɛfiri ɔyaredɔm no mu baeɛ ho mmuaema mu no, aban, wɔ H.E. akannifoɔ a wɔtumi di wɔn anim no ase. Ɔmanpanin Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo, yɛɛ GH100 billion COVID-19 Approvation and Recurisation of Enterprises nhyehyɛeɛ, GhanaCARES “Obaatan Pa” dwumadie no. Yei ama yɛn nkurɔfoɔ ho atɔ wɔn ntɛm so pa ara, na ɛresan ahyɛ yɛn man no den bio.

Yɛn ani sɔ gyidie a wo ne Aban wɔ no berɛ mpo a yɛrebɔ mmɔden sɛ yɛbɛnya ahoɔden kɛseɛ afiri ɔyaredɔm no mu. Wɔ saa berɛ yi mu no, wɔasɔ yɛn ahoɔden a yɛde bɛnya nkwa sɛ ɔman no ahwɛ. Bible no de akwankyerɛ a ehia ma bere nyinaa wɔ mmere a ɛtete saa mu. Titiriw no, sɛnea yɛaka wɔ Yesaia 9:10 no, “Bilkisi no ahwe ase, nanso yɛde aboɔ a wɔasiesie bɛsi; wɔatwa sycamore no, nanso yɛde kyeneduru bɛsi wɔn ananmu”. Saa ara nso na aban asi ne bo sɛ ɛbɛdi ne bɔhyɛ ahodoɔ so. Yei nti, yɛbɛkɔ so ara de nhyehyɛeɛ ne nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ a wɔayɛ sɛ wɔde bɛsan de nneɛma a ayera nyinaa aba na ama ɔmanfoɔ asetena atu mpɔn.

Afe 2022 Budget no botaeɛ titire baako, na ampa ara mfeɛ mmiɛnsa a ɛdi hɔ no mu no, ne sɛ wɔbɛkyekyere Ɔman a ɛbɛtena hɔ daa a ɛtumi de sikasɛm mu nkɔsoɔ a ɛyɛ den, ɛbɛtena hɔ daa, na ɛka obiara ho de ayɛ adwuma pa, titire Mmabunu. Enti, aban nhyehyɛe ahorow bɛyɛ nea wɔde bɛboa mmabun ma wɔde wɔn talente adi dwuma na wɔayi wɔn tumi a wɔde bɛma obiara anya yiyedi denam hokwan a ɛyɛ pɛ so no afi hɔ.

Sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a mmabunu no bɛnya wɔn mu ahotɔsoɔ na wɔakyekyere wɔn adwumayɛ honhom no, aban no bɛboa ama wɔatumi de YouStart nhyehyɛeɛ no adi dwuma wɔ afe 2022 mu sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a wɔbɛnya anuonyam ama mmabunu a wɔwɔ nnwuma mu no sɛ wɔbɛtete anaa wɔbɛtrɛ mu.

Wɔ afe 2022 sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ no mu no, yɛaka nhyehyɛeɛ a wɔayɛ sɛ wɔbɛkɔ so adi sikasɛm mu nsɛnnennen a ɛrekɔ so ntɛm ara no ho dwuma na wɔada wɔn ho adi denneennen wɔ ɔyaredɔm no akyi berɛmo mu. Aban no de ne ho ama sɛ wɔbɛyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛkɔ akyiri na wɔayɛ no biako de ama ahokokwaa na ama mmabunu no adwumayɛ anya nkɔsoɔ. yɛredi ɔmanpanin no akyi

3. 3. Ɔde ne nsa kyerɛɛ ne so. Bere tiaa mu nsɛm a ɛfa sikasɛm no adwumayɛ ho wɔ afe 2021 mu no te sɛ nea edidi so yi:

• Sɛ yɛka ne nyinaa bom a, GDP ankasa yɛ ɔha biara mu nkyem 3.5 wɔ afe 2021 fã a edi kan no mu a ɛko tia nkɔanim ɔha biara mu nkyem 0.7 wɔ bere koro no ara mu wɔ afe 2020 mu;

• GDP ankasa a ɛnyɛ Oil no nyaa nkɔanim ɔha biara mu nkyem 4.8 wɔ afe 2021 fã a edi kan no mu bere a wɔde toto ɔha biara mu nkyem 1.1 wɔ saa bere koro no ara mu wɔ afe 2020 mu no;

• Nkɔsoɔ sɛdeɛ ɛteɛ wɔ September awieeɛ no yɛ ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 10.6, sɛ wɔde toto ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 10.4 berɛ korɔ no ara ho wɔ afe 2020 mu a;

• Sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ mu sika a wɔsɛe no nyinaa (sika) a ɛyɛ GDP ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 7.7 a wɔde tia GDP ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 7.4;

• Sika a ɛkari pɛ titiriw no kyerɛe sɛ GDP ɔha biara mu nkyem 2.0 na ɛho ka yɛ den, sɛ wɔde toto sika a wɔde asi wɔn ani so sɛ wɔbɛsɛe no ho a, ɔha biara mu 1.7.;

• Ɔmanfoɔ ka (sɛ GDP ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu) no yɛ ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 77.7 wɔ September 2021 awieeɛ, ɛboroo ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 76.1 wɔ December 2020 awieeɛ;

• Gross International Reserves wɔ August 2021 awieeɛ no yɛ US$11.44 billion, a ɛdɔɔso sɛ ɛbɛtua asram 5.2 a wɔde firi amannɔne ba no ho ka, sɛ wɔde toto US$8.62 ɔpepepem a ɛne asram 4.1 a wɔde fi amannɔne baeɛ a wɔkyerɛw too hɔ wɔ December 2020 awieeɛ no yɛ pɛ a; ne

• Nsesaeɛ dodoɔ kɔɔ so yɛɛ den wɔ amanaman ntam sika akɛseɛ no nyinaa mu maa afe 2021 bosome mmiɛnsa no. Ne titire mu no, Ghana sidi no boaboaa ano ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 1.7 ano tiaa US Dɔla no wɔ Ɛbɔ bosome no awieeɛ wɔ afe 2021 mu, sɛ wɔde toto ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 2.9 a na ɛrekɔ fam wɔ saa berɛ korɔ no ara mu wɔ afe 2020 mu no ho a.

Nsɛmfua Nkyerɛase (Budget Nsɛmfua) .

1D1F (Ɔmantam Baako, Mfiridwuma Baako): Adwumayɛbea ne nnwuma a wɔbɛhyehyɛ a ɛno nso bɛma ɔman no akɔ mfiridwuma mu nkɔso kɛse. Wɔde asi wɔn ani so sɛ wɔbɛma Ghanafo anya nnwuma pii wɔ ɔman no tenten ne ne trɛw mu.

Akontaabu: Wei ne berɛ a Aban no bua ne manfoɔ denam ne dwumadie a ɔma wɔhunu na ɔda nea ɛfiri nneyɛeɛ a ɛte saa mu baeɛ adi. Eyi betumi ayɛ sika anaa agyapade afoforo a Aban no wɔ wɔ ahotoso mu ho asodi.

Nkyekyɛmu: Ɔkwan a wɔfa so kyɛ anaa kyekyɛ nneɛma a wɔde boa aban nnwumakuo ne nnwumakuo.

Nneɛma a wɔtew so: Eyi ne adeyɛ a ɛma ɔhaw bi mu yɛ den kɛse.

Budget: Sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ yɛ nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛkyerɛ baabi a wɔbɛnya sika afiri ne deɛ wɔde bɛyɛ ho adwuma. Sɛ nhwɛso no, abusua bi betumi ayɛ sikasɛm nhyehyɛe a ɛbɛkyerɛ sika dodow a wobetumi anya ne sɛnea ɛsɛ sɛ wɔde di kan wɔ sika a wɔsɛe no ho (sɛ nhwɛso no, dan ka, nneɛma a wɔde di dwuma ne aduan). Sɛ sika pii ho hia a, ɛsɛ sɛ abusua no susuw ho sɛ wobenya sika pii anaasɛ wobegye bosea.

Budget a wɔsɛe no: Eyi ba bere a Aban no sɛe sika sen sika a wonya no.

Kapital a Wɔsɛe no: Eyi yɛ sika a wɔsɛe no wɔ nnwuma akɛse te sɛ akwan, sukuu ahorow, ayaresabea ahorow, abɔntenban, akwantu, nsu ho nhyehyɛe, afifide ne mfiri ne nea ɛkeka ho ho.

Adwumayɛfoɔ Akatua: Eyi yɛ akatua ne sika a wɔde ma wɔ akatua ho te sɛ asetena mu ahobanbɔ, adesua ne pɛnhyen sika a wɔtua ma ɔman adwumayɛfoɔ.

Nkɔsoɔ Partners (DPs): Eyi kyerɛ aman ne/anaasɛ ahyehyɛdeɛ a wɔne aman a afei na wɔrenya nkɔsoɔ yɛ adwuma bom de du wɔn nkɔsoɔ botaeɛ ho. DP ahorow betumi ayɛ aman pii, s.e. Amanaman Ntam Sikakorabea (IMF), Wiase Sikakorabea, Afrika Nkɔso Sikakorabea (AfDB) anaa aman abien a aman ankorankoro te sɛ Germany, United Kingdom ne United States of America (USA).

Afe awiei Nkɔso: Nneɛma bo a ɛkɔɔ soro dodow a wonyae wɔ December mu wɔ afe biako mu a wosusuw ho.

Sika a wɔsɛe no boro so: Eyi ba bere a Aban sika a wɔsɛe no boro Budget sika dodow a Mmarahyɛ Bagua no apene so no.

FREE SHS: Free SHS (Free Senior High School) nhyehyɛeɛ no yɛ Ghana Aban nhyehyɛeɛ a Ɔmanpanin Nana Akuffo-Addo adwumayɛkuo no de baeɛ wɔ September 2017 mu a ɛma kwan ma wɔnya SHS nwomasua kwa ama JHS suani biara a ɔfata adesua mu.

Mmoa: Yeinom yɛ sikasɛm mu mmoa ahodoɔ a nkɔsoɔ ahokafoɔ de ma Aban na ɛho nhia sɛ wɔtua Aban ka.

Mmoa a wɔde ma Aban Nkabom afoforo: Eyinom yɛ sikatua a mmara te sɛ District Assemblies’ Common Fund (DACF), Road Fund, Ghana Education Trust Fund (GETFUND), Petroleum Related Fund ne National Health Fund hwehwɛ.

Gross Domestic Product (GDP): Eyi kyerɛ mfaso a ɛwɔ nneɛma ne nnwuma a etwa to a wɔhwehwɛ nyinaa so wɔ ɔman no mu wɔ bere pɔtee bi mu.

Gross International Reserves: Eyi susuw ɔman bi amannɔne sika a etumi tɔ nneɛma ne nnwuma fi amannɔne aman hɔ wɔ bere bi mu; ɛsan nso boa ahoɔden a ɛwɔ mpɔtam hɔ sika mu wɔ aman afoforo sika ho.

Inflation: Nneɛma ne nnwuma bo a ɛkɔ soro wɔ ɔkwan a ɛkɔ akyiri so wɔ bere bi mu.

IPEP (Infrastructure for Ohia Bere Nhyehyɛe): Infrastructure for Ohia Eradication Program (IPEP) yɛ NPP Aban no nhyehyɛe titiriw no mu biako a ne botae ne sɛ ebetu ohia ase na ama pɛyɛ a enni hɔ no ayɛ ketewaa bi, titiriw wɔ nkuraase na ama mpɔtam hɔfo ahwere wɔn ho denam nnwuma atitiriw a wɔde bɛma so.

Macrosikasɛm mu botae ahorow: Eyinom yɛ susudua a wɔde di dwuma de nya sikasɛm mu nkɔso. Nhwɛsoɔ bi ne sikasɛm mu nkɔsoɔ, nneɛma boɔ a ɛkɔ soro, nsakraeɛ a ɛba adwumayɛ mu, adwumayɛ mu nkɔsoɔ a wɔne aman foforɔ di, nkonimdie kakra anaa aban sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ a entumi nyɛ yie ne gyinaesie a Ghana Sikakorabea no asi a ɛka sika a wɔhwehwɛ ne nneɛma a wɔde ma wɔ sikasɛm mu.

MSME (Micro And Small Buisness Development Programme): Wɔtaa kyerɛkyerɛ micro-enterprise (anaasɛ microenterprise) mu sɛ adwuma ketewaa bi a wɔde nnipa dodow a ɛba fam koraa di dwuma, na wɔwɔ sikasɛm ho akontaabu anaa sika a wɔsakra no a ennu sika pɔtee bi

NABCo (Nation Builders Corps): NABCO sane frɛ no NABCO yɛ nhyehyɛeɛ a Ghana Aban de aba sɛ wɔde bɛma wɔn a wɔawie sukuupɔn a wɔnni adwuma no nnwuma de adi asetena mu haw ahodoɔ a ɛwɔ ankorɛankorɛ ne aban nnwuma nyinaa mu no ho dwuma na wɔasisi wɔn nimdeɛ a wɔde bɛhyɛ sikasɛm mu nkɔsoɔ ho nkuran wɔ ɔman no mu.

NBSSI (Ɔman Boayikuo a ɛhwɛ Nnwuma nketewa ne Medium Scale Nnwuma so): Ɔman Boayikuo a ɛhwɛ Nnwuma nketewa so (NBSSI) yɛ ɔman adwumayɛkuo ahyehyɛdeɛ a ɛnyɛ mfasoɔ a ɛhyɛ Aguadi ne Nnwuma Dwumadibea ase, na wɔahyɛ no sɛ ɛnhyɛ Micro ne Nnwuma nketewa (MSE) nkɔsoɔ ne nkɔsoɔ ho nkuran.

GDP a ɛnyɛ Oil-GDP: Ɛyɛ sikasɛm mu nsunsuansoɔ nyinaa na ɛyi dwumadie a ɛwɔ fangoo adwuma no mu, a ɛsusu nneɛma boɔ a ɛkɔ soro no ho.

Towtua a Wɔmfa Ntua: Aban sika a wɔmfa mfi towtua mu, nhwɛso ahorow yɛ sika a wɔbɔ wɔ tumi krataa anaa tumi krataa a wɔde ma, wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no ho ka ne nneɛma afoforo a wɔbɔ ho.

Asɛdeɛ a Ɛda Nsow: Asɛdeɛ a ɛda so wɔ hɔ no yɛ MDA bɔhyɛ a wɔne Sikasɛm Dwumadibea no ahyɛ ne sika a wɔtuaeɛ wɔ afe 2016 mu a ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ ma Mmara kwan so Sika.

Primary balance : Eyi ne nsonsonoe a ɛda Aban sika a wonya ne ne sika a wɔsɛe no a ɛnyɛ mfɛntom (a ɛka a wɔde tua ka ho). Wobetumi asusuw eyi sɛ ɔha biara mu nkyem 100 wɔ GDP Gross Domestic Product (GDP) no mu.

Projected Growth Rate: Sɛnea Ghana sikasɛm bɛkɔ soro wɔ afe biako mu.

Sika a wonya: Eyi ne sika dodow a Aban no nya fi ne dwumadi ahorow a efi ɔman no mu ne akyi nyinaa mu.

RFJ (Aduan ne Nnwuma a Wɔbɛsan Ayɛ): bɛma mmoayɛn adwuma a akansi wom na etu mpɔn, a ɛbɛma afie mu nneɛma a wɔyɛ no akɔ soro, atew mmoa a wɔde ba amannɔne so, aboa ma wɔanya adwuma, na ama mmoayɛn bo a wɔde di dwuma no asetra atu mpɔn”.
