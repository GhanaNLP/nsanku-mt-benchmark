Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBA SƐ NKYERƐKYERƐMU A ƐFA BI HO, NA AFOFORO NYƐ AKWANKYERƐ pa

WƆKA sɛ ɛhɔ (pɛnkoro) na ɔyɛ ɔbaa, na ɔkɔɔ Palm-dua bi a ɛgyina hɔ wɔ nsuo no mu a ebia ɔbɛhwɛ afa abofra a ɔbɛwo ho. na ɔkɔɔ hɔ no paulo kaa sɛ mede deɛ wopɛ bɛma woɔ nanso abɔfra a mede no bɛkyerɛ woɔ no sɛ ɔsɔre a ɔrenyɛ adwuma biara da Ɔkae sɛ, “Mepene so (ma ɛno). na ɛnyɛ nnanu na ɛnyɛ nnansa na ɔnyinsɛnee na ɔwoo Te sɛ mmofra a na wɔwɔ Spider-stours no, na onnyinii kyɛe no, wɔtetee no prɛko pɛ. ɛnna ne maame ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ sɛɛ na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɛyɛ abusuakuo no akyiwadeɛ no nyinaa mu no, obiara nni hɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ wohwɛ sɛ ɔgye paulo dua no nkwa a, ɛnsɛ sɛ woforo baako da.” Ɔkae sɛ, “Maame mate.” Afei, da bi, ɛna no gyaw no hɔ kɔɔ afuom. ɔkɔfrɛɛ n'adɔfo na ɔne no dii agorɔ na ɔkyekyeree n'asene ahwedeɛ na wɔbubui na wɔhwee ase na ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "Asiane-nkwansosɛm a woabubu yi, kɔ na fa mpaboa bi a wɔfrɛ no palm-tree brɛ me na memfa nkaeɛ Abarimaa no fii hɔ kɔtɔɔ bi de bae. Abaayewa no kaa sɛ, "Mempɛ saa asɛm no, ebinom pɛ na woahwehwɛ afiri Palm-Dua a ɛgyina nsuo no mu hɔ." Ɛno nti abɔfra no sii ne bo sɛ ɔrekɔhwehwɛ bi. Bere a ɔkɔe no, na palm-dua bi gyina nsu no mu. Ɔforo kɔɔ soro,kra! kra! kra! kɔsii sɛ ɔduruu dua no kɔn mu na ɔyii ne sekan no na ɔde ne nsa kaa no ntɛm ara na palm-dua no mu paapaee yɛɛ mmienu na abarimaa no yafunu kɔɔ mu na palm-doma no san too mu bio Ɛhɔ na na ɔwɔ, na ɛrehwehwɛ no. Bere a ne maame bae akyi bere tiaa bi no, oduu nsu no ho no, ohuu sunsuma bi wɔ so, na ɔkae sɛ:

“Hena ne sunsuma yi a ɛda nsuo a ɛte sɛ m'akoa Akwasi Kwasaman deɛ no?”

Ɔkae sɛ, “Maame, ɛyɛ me.”

Ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na Palm-tree no rehwehwɛ wo?"

Ɔkaa sɛ, “Mekɔɔ sɛ merekɔyi Palm fiber akɔma me dɔfoɔ ne Palm-dua a wɔamia me no.”

Ɛna no kaa sɛ, "Wo, Palm-tree yi, piapia no, piapia no, me ba Kwasaman.

Palm-tree pia no, piapia no, Palm-tree piapia no.”

na ɛna no firii hɔ kɔe na agya no nso bae na ɔse:

sunsuma bɛn na ɛda nsuo yi so te sɛ m'akoa Kwasaman deɛ

Ɔkae sɛ, “Agya, ɛyɛ me.”

Ɔno (agya no) kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na Palm-dua no rehwehwɛ wo?"

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mekɔɔ sɛ merekɔyi Palm fiber akɔma me dɔfoɔ ne Palm-dua a wɔamia me no."

Ɔno (agya no) kaa sɛ, "Wo, Palm-tree yi, piapia no, Me ba Kwasaman.

Palm-tree pia no, piapia no, Palm-tree piapia no."

na agya no firii adi kɔɔ fie na ɔka kyerɛɛ akuraa no aseɛ panin no na akuraa no ase panin no nso frɛɛ ɔman no nyinaa na wɔbaa paulo dua no ase na akuraa no ase panyin no maa ne nne so kaa sɛ

"Hena ne sunsuma na ɛda nsuo a ɛte sɛ me nana Akwasi Kwasaman deɛ so?"

Ɔkae sɛ, "Nana, ɛyɛ me."

Ɔno (akuraa no ase panyin) kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na Palm-tree no rehwehwɛ wo?"

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mekɔɔ sɛ merekɔyi Palm fiber akɔma me dɔfoɔ ne Palm-dua a wɔamia me no."

Akuraa no ase panyin no kae sɛ, "Wo, Palm-tree yi, piapia no, piapia no, me nana Kwasaman.

Palm-tree pia no, piapia no, Palm-tree piapia no."

na akuraa no ase panin no firii adi na akuraa no asefoɔ nyinaa too dwom korɔ no ara a ɛka kyerɛɛ paulo dua no sɛ wɔmpam no na afei ne dɔfoɔ no bae na ɔkaa sɛ hwɛ sunsuma bɛn na ɛda nsuo no soɔ sɛ me dɔfoɔ Akwasi Kwasaman deɛ

Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ me, wo dɔfo ankasa."

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na Palm-tree no rehwehwɛ wo?"

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mekɔɔ sɛ merekɔbubu Palm fiber ama wo, na Palm-dua no piapiaa me."

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Palm-tree gyae no, gyae no, gyae no.

Palm-tree yi me dɔfo Akwasi Kwasaman adi.

Palm-tree gyae no, gyae no, Palm-tree gyae no."

na ɔkasaa saa na paulo buee kakra na ne dɔfoɔ yafunu firii adi kakra na ɔmaa ne nne so bio na ɔkaa sɛ:

sunsuma bɛn na ɛda nsuo no so sɛdeɛ me dɔfoɔ Akwasi Kwasaman deɛ no

Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ me, wo dɔfo ankasa."

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na Palm-tree no rehwehwɛ wo?"

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mekɔɔ sɛ merekɔbubu Palm fiber ama wo, na Palm-dua no piapiaa me."

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Palm-tree gyae no, gyae no, gyae no.

Palm-tree yi me dɔfo Akwasi Kwasaman adi.

Palm-tree gyae no, gyae no, Palm-tree gyae no."

na afei ne dɔfoɔ nyinaa firii adi prɛko pɛ na ɔbae na ɔde ne dɔfoɔ kokom na wɔsunsuanee na wɔdanee ngo na wɔn a wɔkɔɔ hɔ no yɛɛ ntɛm na wɔde wɔn anim bɔɔ wɔn mu bi anaa na wɔn ho yɛ fɛ nanso wɔn deɛ wɔannya bi na wɔyɛ wɔn a wɔn ho nyɛ fɛ Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
