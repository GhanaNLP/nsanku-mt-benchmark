SƐNEA ANENE NO NECK, ROW, BECAME WƆ NE HERƐKYERƐMU BƆNE BLACK

Ɔbaa bi a ɔda so te ase, a wɔfrɛ no Ata (Twi no), ne ne ba no fii adi kɔpetepetee mpataa. Bere a woduu asuten no ho no, ne ba no kae sɛ, "Momma yɛnforo nsuo no." Nanso awuraa no kaa sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔ fam, ɛfiri sɛ ɛhɔ na nkawa nam nketewa no wɔ." Ne ba no kaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, merekɔ soro, ɛfiri sɛ ɛhɔ na nkawa nam nketewa no wɔ." Enti ne maame kɔɔ fam. na wɔtenaa hɔ kyɛree na n'abɔfra no nyaa bi na ɔbae na ɔtwam kɔhwehwɛɛ ne na Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na ne maame calabash a ɛsensɛn nsu no anim no wɔ hɔ. Abofra no kae sɛ, "Ah! Ɛnyɛ adeɛ baako, ɛnyɛ mmienu (nneɛma), ɛyɛ maame dea." na ɔmaa ne nne so sui na ɔfrɛɛ ne maame na ɔkaa sɛ:

na mekaa saa na moantie na mekaa saa mekyerɛɛ mo na moantie

Mekaa sɛ, 'Maame, momma yɛnkɔ soro, ɛfiri sɛ ɛhɔ na nkawa nam nketewa no te';

Na ɔkaa sɛ ɔrekɔ asubɔnten no ase, ɛfiri sɛ baabi a nkwaboa nketewa no nam no teɛ.

Na dɛn na menyɛ? Alas me maame, Ata, a wɔahwie agu!

na hawk bae na ɔkaa sɛ wo ba dwom yɛ dɛ na to dwom na mɛyi wo maame afiri adi ama wo Na abofra no maa soro (ne nne):

na mekaa saa na moantie na mekaa saa mekyerɛɛ mo na moantie

Mekaa sɛ, 'Maame, momma yɛnkɔ soro, ɛfiri sɛ ɛhɔ na nkawa nam nketewa no te';

Na ɔkaa sɛ ɔrekɔ asubɔnten no ase, ɛfiri sɛ baabi a nkwaboa nketewa no nam no teɛ.

Na dɛn na menyɛ? Alas me maame, Ata, a wɔahwie agu!

na hawk faa ne (magic) aboa dua-swit na ɔbɔɔ nsuo no anim nanso nsuo no gyinaa faako a ɛgyinaeɛ Na ɔno (Hawk no) kɔɔ so. na koseakyi a wɔfrɛ no ayuporebea no bae na ɔno nso bɛkaa sɛ monto saa dwom no mma me nte na menyi wo maame mfiri adi mma wo na abɔfra no too dwom no sɛ:

na mekaa saa na moantie na mekaa saa mekyerɛɛ mo na moantie

Mekaa sɛ, 'Maame, momma yɛnkɔ soro, ɛfiri sɛ ɛhɔ na nkawa nam nketewa no te';

Na ɔkaa sɛ ɔrekɔ asubɔnten no ase, ɛfiri sɛ baabi a nkwaboa nketewa no nam no teɛ.

Na dɛn na menyɛ? Alas me maame, Ata, a wɔahwie agu!

na ɔfaa ne dua nso na ɔbobɔɔ asubɔnten no anim nanso biribiara ansi na ɔno nso kɔɔ so ara Na (mprempren) Anene a ɔyɛ Crow no nso bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Akwadaa, adɛn nti na wosu saa? su na ma me nte sɛ ebia mɛyi wo maame ama wo na abɔfra no too dwom no sɛ:

na mekaa saa na moantie na mekaa saa mekyerɛɛ mo na moantie

Mekaa sɛ, 'Maame, momma yɛnkɔ soro, ɛfiri sɛ ɛhɔ na nkawa nam nketewa no te';

Na ɔkaa sɛ ɔrekɔ asubɔnten no ase, ɛfiri sɛ baabi a nkwaboa nketewa no nam no teɛ.

Na dɛn na menyɛ? Alas me maame, Ata, a wɔahwie agu!

Krow no faa aduru bi a wɔde yɛ nnuro guu nsuo no mu na ɔfaa ne nkonyaayi dua na ɔbɔɔ nsuo no anim na nsuo no mu paepaee mmienu na ɔkaa sɛ, "Dwom no to" na abɔfra no too dwom. na ɔhunuu ne maame tiri nwi na ɔserewee kakra. Ɔkae sɛ, "Wɔto san," na ɔtoo dwom. Na ne maame puei kɔɔ asɛnnibea hɔ. Crowfo no kae sɛ, "Kɔto dwom." na abɔfra no too dwom na ne na puee kɔduruu ne nansoaa Ɔkae sɛ, "Wɔto no bio"; na ɔtoo dwom. Ne maame hurii prɛko pɛ na ogyinaa sikakorabea no so. Ata, Twin no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meda mo ase?" Obuae sɛ, "Ɛdefa me ho no, mempɛ biribiara koraa, kɔ kɔtɔ ntama tuntum ne fitaa kɛkɛ." Ata tuu mmirika kɔtɔɔ bi, na ɔde baa Crow no nkyɛn, na Crow no de sum-blue kataa ne ho, na fitaa a ɔkyekyeree no no kataa ne kɔn ho. Ɛno nti na wobɛhunu Crow no, n'akyi kɔla yɛ tuntum deɛ, nanso ne kɔn nyinaa yɛ fitaa. Wɔyɛ akyɛdeɛ a ɔnya firii Ata a ɔyɛ Twin no hɔ.

Eyi, m'asɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom betumi afa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no, wubetumi akamfo me (sɛ woka ho asɛm a).
