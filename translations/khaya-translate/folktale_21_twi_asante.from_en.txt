SƐNEA OWEA (THE TREE-PEAR), ABOSOM-AKOTERE (CHMELEON), NE OPATUO (OWL) CAME SƐ WƆN NSƐM A WƆDE DI DWUMA HO NSƐM

OWEA, Dua-bear, (a ɔno nso wɔ abodin) Kwaduampɔn Kyerefo, Opatuo, Owl, ne Abosom-Akotere, Chameleon, (a wɔn abodin ne), “Ɔbɔadeɛ no wu”, “Afe biara-kɔ-biribiara-bɔ-adebɔ”, “Ɔno-nantew brɛoo-nantew-nanso-ɔpɛ-a-ɛkɔ-asase-a-wɔ-fitaa-men-akyirikyiri”, (mmoa yi nyinaa) boom traa ase bere bi. Afei, na wɔn mu biara nni yere. Na Adobe-dua no tɔn ne ho maa wɔn. Da biara, bere a Adobe-tree sɔree no, ɔkɔtwɛn eyi; ade kyee no ɔtwɛnee wɔn mu foforo; na ade kyee no, ɔfa foforo so. Da bi, bere a Ɔbɔadeɛ no wui (sɛ ɔbɛhyɛ Adobe nnwuma no so) na ɔhwehwɛɛ Adobe-dua no, ɛhe na obetumi ahu no? na ɔfirii hɔ kɔkaa nsɛm yi kyerɛɛ wɔn a wɔso dua ne wɔn a wɔka nsɛm no na wɔkaa sɛ kɔ na kɔfrɛ no Na Ɔbɔadeɛ no wu maa ne nne so sɛ: “Adobe o! Adobe o!

Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
