WƆSETEXT FAIR 2026:

Miishɛɛ ji mɛi ni hiɛ kã amɛmumɔŋ hiamɔ nii anɔ.

MATT. 5:3.

Ábɔ adesai kɛ shishijee hiamɔ nii komɛi. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, bɔni afee ni wɔnyɛ wɔhi shi daa lɛ, niyenii, atadei, kɛ wɔɔhe he miihia wɔ. Kɛji akɛ wɔbɛ hiamɔ nibii nɛɛ eko lɛ, be kukuoo po lɛ, shihilɛ bafeɔ kaa. Kɛfata nakai nibii lɛ ahe hiamɔ nii ni wɔɔfee lɛ he lɛ, Yehowa hu ebɔ wɔ kɛ mumɔŋ hiamɔ nii. (Kanemɔ Mateo 5:3.) Kɛ́ wɔmiisumɔ ni wɔná miishɛɛ diɛŋtsɛ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔhiɛ ahi wɔhe nɔ yɛ nakai hiamɔ nii lɛ ahe, ni no sɛɛ lɛ wɔya nɔ wɔha amɛmii ashɛ amɛhe.

2 Mɛni ji jwɛŋmɔ ni yɔɔ wɔ mumɔŋ hiamɔ nii lɛ he? Wiemɔ shishijee lɛ tsɔɔ susumɔ ni ji akɛ mɔ ko ji mɔ ko ni biɔ mumɔ lɛ. Nakai wiemɔ kuku lɛ feɔ jwɛŋmɔŋ mfoniri ni yɔɔ hewalɛ. Susumɔ mɔ ko ni woɔ atade ni haa mɔ tsui nyɔɔ emli yɛ maŋ lɛ gbɛjegbɛ ko nɔ lɛ he okwɛ. Ebɛ niyenii kpakpa, etse ehe yɛ ehiɛ su lɛ hewɔ, ni ekɛ shwane dɔlɛ kɛ fɛ̃i ni naa wa kpe gbɛkɛ. Mɔ ni biɔ nii lɛ le jogbaŋŋ akɛ ehe baahia ni eye ebua lɛ kɛ́ eba lɛ akɛ ebaaha eshihilɛ lɛ aya hiɛ yɛ shihilɛ mli. Nakai nɔŋŋ hu mɔ ko ni hiɛ kã shi yɛ mumɔŋ hiamɔ nii ahe lɛni ji mɔ ko ni biɔ mumɔ lɛesa akɛ eye ebua ni eha eshihilɛ lɛ aya hiɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, eesumɔ waa ni ekɛ mumɔŋ nibii babaoo ni Yehowa kɛhaa mɛi ni sumɔɔ Lɛ lɛ atsu nii.

3. Mɛni wɔbaawie he yɛ sane nɛɛ mli?

4 Be ko lɛ, Siria yoo ko tsi ebɛŋkɛ Yesu. Ebiyoo lɛ ji daimonio ko ni ehe gbɛi waa. (Matt. 15:21-28) Yoo lɛ gbee shi ni ekpa Yesu fai koni eye ebua lɛ. Yoo nɛɛ jie sui ni sa kadimɔ waa kpo be mli ni ebɛŋkɛ Yesu lɛ. Susumɔ enɛɛmɛi ekomɛi ahe okwɛ.

5 Malaria yoo lɛ jie heshibaa diɛŋtsɛ kpo. Mɛni hewɔ wɔbaanyɛ wɔkɛɛ nakai? Akɛni efeee efɔŋ ko hewɔ lɛ, beni Yesu kɛ mfoniri ko ni tamɔ nɔ ni ekɛto gbee bibioo ko he lɛ tsu nii lɛ, ekolɛ kooloo ni akɛto akɛ weku kooloo yɛ Jeŋmajiaŋnyo ko shia lɛ. Te ooofee tɛɛ oha Yesu wiemɔi kɛ enifeemɔi lɛ ahetoo? Ani obaanu he akɛ ofeee bo niseniianii ni otaooo eyelikɛbuamɔ? Jeee nakai ji bɔ ni Etiopia yoo lɛ fee enii eha. Jeee heshibaa pɛ ejie lɛ kpo, shi moŋ etee nɔ efee nakai hu. Enɛ jeɔ kpo faŋŋ yɛ gbɛ ni etee nɔ ekpa Yesu fai koni eye ebua lɛ lɛ nɔ. Mɛni tsirɛ lɛ ni efee nakai? Hemɔkɛyeli ni eyɔɔ yɛ Yesu mli lɛ. Yɛ anɔkwale mli lɛ, yoo nɛɛ hemɔkɛyeli lɛ tsirɛ Yesu waa aahu akɛ efee nɔ ko ni sa kadimɔ waa. Eyɛ mli akɛ ekɛɛ pɛŋ akɛ atsu lɛ koni eyaye ebua Israel shia lɛ mli tooi ni elaaje lɛ pɛ moŋ, shi Yesu jie mumɔ ni he tseee lɛ kɛjɛ nɛkɛ Jeŋmajiaŋnyo yoo nɛɛ biyoo lɛ dɛŋ.

Esa akɛ wɔ hu wɔfee nakai nɔŋŋ. Esa akɛ wɔba wɔhe shi, wɔya nɔ wɔye, ni wɔná hemɔkɛyeli ni mli wa. Mɔ ko ni baa ehe shi pɛ baakpa Nyɔŋmɔ fai be fɛɛ be koni eye ebua lɛ. Esa akɛ wɔná hemɔkɛyeli ni mli wa hu yɛ Kristo Yesu mli ni wɔkɛ wɔhiɛ afɔ mɛi ni ekɛtsuɔ nii kɛtsɔɔ ekaselɔi lɛ gbɛ lɛ anɔ. (Matt. 24:45-47) Yehowa kɛ ebi lɛ amii shɛ amɛhe waa akɛ amɛtsu mɛi ni jieɔ sui nɛɛ akpo lɛ amumɔŋ hiamɔ nii ahe nii. (Kɛ James 1:5-7 ato he.) Agbɛnɛ nyɛhaa wɔpɛia bɔ ni Yehowa kɛ mumɔŋ niyenii, atadei, kɛ wɔɔhe haa wɔ lɛ mli wɔkwɛa. Wɔbaakwɛ nɔ ni esa akɛ wɔfee koni wɔnine ashɛ nɛkɛ hiamɔ nii nɛɛ anɔ kɛtsɔ nɔkwɛmɔnii ni bɔfoi Peter kɛ Paul kɛ Maŋtsɛ David kɛ Maŋtsɛ David fee lɛ kwɛmɔ nɔ.

7 Susumɔ bɔfo Petro nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ he okwɛ. Eji klɛŋklɛŋ Yudafoi ni yɔse akɛ Yesu ji Mesia lɛnɔ ni Yehowa kɛtsuɔ nii kɛyeɔ emaŋ lɛ niyenii lɛ ateŋ mɔ kome. (John 6:66-68) Dani Yesu ni atee lɛ shi lɛ baaya ŋwɛi lɛ, ekɛ nitsumɔ nɛɛ ha Peter: Fee mitooi bibii lɛ. (John 21:17) Peter kɛ anɔkwayeli tsu nakai nitsumɔ lɛ he nii, ni Yehowa po kɛ lɛ ŋma woji enyɔ ni batsɔ Biblia lɛ fã. Shi ehe bahia ni Peter kɛ mumɔŋ niyenii awo ehe hewalɛ hu. Ákɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, ekase woji ni mumɔ tsirɛ lɛ ni bɔfo Paulo ŋma tsutsu lɛ. Peter kpɛlɛ nɔ akɛ nibii komɛi ni Paulo ŋma yɛ he lɛ shishinumɔ wa. (2 Pet. 3:15, 16) Shi eka shi faŋŋ akɛ Peter tee nɔ, ni eyɛ hemɔkɛyeli akɛ Yehowa baaye abua lɛ ni ekpa, loo enu shishi ni ekɛtsu nii, ni ji niyenii ni akɛfee ekome ni akɛ mumɔ tsirɛ Paulo ni esɔmɔ yɛ ewolo lɛ mli lɛ.Kanemɔ Kaselɔi 5:146:1.

8 Petro ma nɔ mi akɛ eyɛ hemɔkɛyeli yɛ Yehowa mli kɛtsɔ gbɛtsɔɔmɔ ni enine shɛ nɔ lɛ toiboo nɔ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔ nɔ lɛ, beni Peter yɔɔ ŋshɔkpɔ lɛ naa maŋ lɛ mli lɛ, ena ninaa ko ni Nyɔŋmɔ najiaŋdamɔlɔ lɛ kɛɛ lɛ akɛ egbe kooloi ni eye kooloi ni, taakɛ Mosaic Mla lɛ tsɔɔ lɛ, amɛhe tseee. Kɛha Yudafoi lɛ, kulɛ nakai gbɛ lɛ baafee nɔ ni haa mɔ naa kpɛɔ ehe. Shishijee lɛ, Peter ha hetoo akɛ: Daabi, Nuŋtsɔ, ejaakɛ miyeko nɔ ko ni awo he muji ni he tse pɛŋ. No sɛɛ lɛ akɛɛ lɛ akɛ: Kpaa tsɛmɔ ni akɛwo nibii ni Nyɔŋmɔ etsumɔ lɛ ahe muji. (Mlai 10:9-15) Peter shɛ sane lɛ he. Te le tɛŋŋ? Beni enine shɛ ninaa lɛ nɔ etsɛɛɛ lɛ, hii etɛ ni Jeŋmajiaŋnyo Cornelius tsu amɛ kɛba ni amɛfɔ̃ Petro nine koni eba ni ebawie etsɔɔ amɛnuŋtsɔ lɛ. Bei pii lɛ, kulɛ Peter ekpɛlɛŋ nɔ kɔkɔɔkɔ akɛ ebaabote Jeŋmajiaŋnyo ko shia mli. Yudafoi lɛ bu Jeŋmajiaŋbii lɛ akɛ amɛhe tse. (Mlai 10:28, 29) Shi amrɔ nɔŋŋ lɛ, Peter kpɛlɛ la hee ni Yehowa jie lɛ kpo etsɔɔ lɛ lɛ nɔ. (Prov. 4:18) Ejaje etsɔɔ Cornelius kɛ eshia lɛ, ni ená miishɛɛ akɛ ena amɛ ni amɛkpɛlɛ anɔkwale lɛ nɔ, amɛná mumɔ krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ, ni abaptisi amɛ.Mlai 10:44-48.

Esa akɛ wɔto be lɛ he gbɛjianɔ ni wɔkɛ wɔhe awo mɔdɛŋ ni abɔɔ koni anu Nyɔŋmɔ Wiemɔ lɛ shishi jogbaŋŋ lɛ mli, shi ehe yɛ sɛɛnamɔ. Mɛni hewɔ? Kɛ hoo lɛ, wɔnáa gbɛi enyɔ nɔ ŋɔɔmɔ: Ekome lɛ, kaseɔ nibii ni kɔɔ Yehowa he ni haa wɔsuɔmɔ kɛ bulɛ ni wɔyɔɔ kɛha lɛ lɛ mli waa; ni enyɔ lɛ, wɔnuɔ he akɛ ekanyaa wɔ po koni wɔkɛ mɛi krokomɛi agba wɔ Ŋwɛi Tsɛ ni wɔsumɔɔ waa lɛ he sane. (shi. 11:33; Kwɛmɔ. 4:11) Peter nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ tsɔɔ wɔ nikasemɔ kroko: Kɛ́ wɔnine shɛ tsakemɔi anɔ yɛ shishinumɔ ni wɔyɔɔ yɛ Nyɔŋmɔ Wiemɔ lɛ he lɛ mli lɛ, ehe miihia ni wɔfee oya ni wɔtsake wɔsusumɔi kɛ wɔnifeemɔi. No sɛɛ pɛ kɛkɛ lɛ, wɔbaahi shi jogbaŋŋ yɛ mumɔŋ ni amɛhe baaba sɛɛnamɔ aha Yehowa.

10 Bɔni afee ni Esaa Nyɔŋmɔ hiɛ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔkɛ gbɛjianɔtoo kroko ni kɔɔ mumɔŋ atadei ahe lɛ atsu nii. Mɛni wɔtsɔɔ? bɔfo Paulo ŋma akɛ esa akɛ ajie subaŋ momo lɛ ni wɔkɛ su hee lɛ awula wɔhe. (Kanemɔ Kolosebii 3:8-10.) Wɔhe ni wɔɔfee yɛ gbɛ ni tamɔ nɛkɛ nɔ lɛ baanyɛ afee kaa ni yaa nɔ. Susumɔ Paul diɛŋtsɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ he okwɛ. Kɛjɛ egbekɛbiiashi tɔ̃ɔ lɛ, ebɔ mɔdɛŋ waa akɛ enine aaashɛ Nyɔŋmɔ nɔ. (Gal. 1:14; Phil. 3:4, 5) Shi ebɛ Nyɔŋmɔ yiŋtoo he nilee ni ja ni no hewɔ lɛ eyɛ mumɔŋ shihilɛ ni ehiii mli. Bɔ ni eleee Kristo tsɔɔmɔi lɛ, kɛ henɔwomɔ lɛ ha efee nuu ko ni bɛ bulɛ ni woɔ atadei yɛ subaŋ gbonyo mli.1 Tim. 1:13.

12 Paul nyɛ eye omanye yɛ esubaŋi ni ehiii lɛ jiemɔ mli, ni no sɛɛ lɛ ekɛ gbɔmɔtso hee lɛ wo ehe atade ejaakɛ ekɛ ehe fɔ̃ɔɔɔɔ lɛ diɛŋtsɛ ehewalɛ nɔ. (Kwɛmɔ kuku 18)

Wɔmlifu ni wɔnuɔ loo wɔwieɔ wɔhaa mɔ ko lɛ he ehiaaa wɔ akɛ wɔji mɛi ni yeee omanye. Moŋ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔya nɔ wɔkɛfee oti ni ma wɔhiɛ akɛ wɔtsake bɔ ni wɔsusuɔ nii ahe wɔhaa lɛ. (shi. 12:1, 2; Tsɔɔmɔ. 4:24) Shi kɛlɛ, taakɛ wɔfeɔ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔkai anɔkwale ko ni he hiaa waa: Nɔ ni tamɔɔɔ atadei diɛŋtsɛ ni asaa he koni ekɛ wɔ akpã gbee lɛ, esa akɛ wɔtsake wɔhe koni wɔkɛ mumɔŋ atadei ni Yehowa kɛhaa lɛ akpã gbee. Nilee yɛ mli akɛ wɔtsake mɛi ni wɔji bɔni afee ni wɔsusu Nyɔŋmɔ taomɔ nii lɛ ahe lɛ pɛ.

(Isa. 32:1, 2) Kɛ́ wɔkpe yɛ ekomefeemɔ mli lɛ, bei pii lɛ, akaiɔ wɔ yɛ gbɛi pɔtɛɛ komɛi ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔtsɔ nɔ wɔtsu he nii lɛ ahe

Wiemɔ Anɔkwale lɛ Mli ka shi faŋŋ

Yehowa, Anɔkwale Nyɔŋmɔ lɛ.PS. 31:5.

Kɛji wɔkɛ enaanyo ko kpe klɛŋklɛŋ kwraa lɛ, sanebimɔi ni wɔfɔ̃ɔ amɛhe bimɔ lɛ ateŋ ekome ji, Te okase anɔkwale lɛ oha tɛŋŋ? Mɛi komɛi haa nakai sanebimɔ lɛ hetoo kɛtsɔ wiemɔ ni amɛwieɔ akɛ amɛhere amɛ atuu yɛ anɔkwale lɛ mli lɛ nɔ. Mɛi krokomɛi baanyɛ akɛɛ akɛ amɛbote anɔkwale lɛ mli nyɛsɛɛ nɛɛ. Ehaa hetoo yɛ gbɛ̀i ni tamɔ nɛkɛ nɔ ejaakɛ Nyɔŋmɔ Wiemɔ lɛ mli anɔkwale lɛ náa wɔshihilɛ gbɛ fɛɛ nɔ hewalɛ. Mɛni hewɔ? Ejaakɛ wɔle akɛ kɛ́ wɔtsɔɔ akɛ wɔsumɔɔ anɔkwale lɛ ni wɔhiɔ shi yɛ naa lɛ, no pɛ kɛkɛ wɔbaanyɛ amɛfata Yehowa weku lɛ he. Nɔ ni fata enɛ he ji anɔkwayeli ni wɔyɔɔ yɛ wɔwiemɔi kɛ wɔnifeemɔi amli.Ps. 15:1-3.

Saji kɛha Yehowa. Susumɔ nɔ ni ba yɛ Yesu sɔɔmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ mli yɛ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ lɛ he okwɛ. (Kanemɔ Mark 3:11, kɛwaje Beni Yesu shiɛɔ yɛ Galilea Ŋshɔ lɛ masɛi lɛ, asafo babaoo bua amɛhe naa amɛbɔle ehe. Shishijee lɛ, daimonioi lɛ wie anɔkwale lɛ, shi yiŋtoi ni tsirɛɔ amɛ lɛ ji pɛsɛmkunya. Amɛlakaaa Yesu loo amɛhaaa ehiɛ afee lɛ yaa. Yɛ anɔkwale mli lɛ, Yesu fã daimonioi lɛ akɛ amɛka shiɛ ehe sane.

Biblia lɛ diɛŋtsɛ kɛ anɔkwalei ni yɔɔ mli lɛ toɔ klante ni naa wa ni baanyɛ aja misusuma kɛ mumɔŋ mli lɛ he. Yɛ gbɛ kroko nɔ lɛ, ebaanyɛ ejie henumɔi kɛ yiŋtoi diɛŋtsɛ ni wɔyɔɔ lɛ kpo. (Heb. 4:12) Shi kɛji wɔkɛ Biblia lɛ tsuuu nii jogbaŋŋ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔkɛ lɛ atsu nii

kɛ efɔŋfeemɔ ni he ehiaaa kɛ béi ni he ehiaaa baa. Te ebaafee tɛɛ eba lɛ nakai?

Kɛtsɔ Biblia lɛ nɔ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔtsɔɔ nuu lɛ bɔ ni eji kwashiai asane akɛ asɔle aha amagai ni wala bɛ mli lɛ, ni wɔtsɔɔ lɛ akɛ Blonya kɛ Easter ji wɔŋjalɔi ahe gbijurɔyelii. (Isa. 44:14-20; 2 Kuu. 6:14-17) Kɛji wɔfee nakai yɛ wɔ klɛŋklɛŋ sanegbaa ni wɔkɛ lɛ gba lɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaakɛɛ anɔkwale lɛ, shi wɔkɛ Nyɔŋmɔ Wiemɔ lɛ he ŋaa tsuuu nii.

Te ooofee tɛɛ otsɔɔ anɔkwale lɛ nii jogbaŋŋ?

(Kwɛmɔ kukuji 11-12)

Nyiɛmɔ Yesu nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ sɛɛ yɛ be mli ni akpɛɔ yiŋ yɛ be ni esa akɛ awie anɔkwale kɛ be abɔ ni esa akɛ awie (Kwɛmɔ kuku 15)

Awo wɔ hewalɛ ni wɔkɛ wiemɔ ni ji sea-bied lɛ atsu nii koni wɔkɛ anɔkwale lɛ aha yɛ gbɛ ni baaha toibolɔ lɛ miishɛɛ lɛ aná he miishɛɛ nɔ. (Job 12:11) Nifeemɔ ni baanyɛ afee kaa. Yɛ niyenii diɛŋtsɛ he lɛ, wɔ su baanyɛ afee ni wɔsusu akɛ mɛi krokomɛi miisumɔ amɛ niyenii lɛ be fɛɛ be ni wɔsumɔɔ. Nakai nɔŋŋ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔsusu akɛ gbɛ ni wɔtsɔɔ nɔ wɔwieɔ lɛ ji nɔ ni mɛi fɛɛ sumɔɔ waa. Shi jeee nakai eji. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔ nɔ lɛ, mɛi ni jɛ kusumii komɛi amli lɛ baanyɛ aná gbɛ tɛ̃ɛ ni amɛtsɔɔ nɔ amɛjieɔ amɛsusumɔ kpo, kɛ́ amɛkɛ mɛi ni edara fe amɛ po miiwie. Mɛi ni jɛ efɔŋfeemɔi krokomɛi amli lɛ baanyɛ ana akɛ akpɛlɛɛɛ nakai kuu lɛ nɔ, ni eji nɔ ni yɔɔ gbeyei po. Paul kɛɛ esa akɛ wɔle bɔ ni wɔɔfee wɔha mɔ fɛɛ mɔ hetoo. Enɛ tsɔɔ akɛ esa akɛ wɔwie nɔ ni wɔwieɔ lɛ he, jeee yɛ bɔ ni wɔsumɔɔ loo wɔ kusum nifeemɔi wɔhaa lɛ naa, shi moŋ taakɛ wɔtoibolɔi lɛ sumɔɔ lɛ naa.

TIP

Biblia He Akɔntaabuu ni Anaaa

Yinɔsane mli saji babaoo ni tsɔɔ nikasemɔi ni he hiaa yɛ Biblia lɛ mli. Kɛtsɔ nɛkɛ akaunti nɛɛ mli jwɛŋmɔŋ mfonirii ni wɔɔfee lɛ nɔ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔnu nɔ ni wɔkane lɛ shishi jogbaŋŋ ni wɔná he sɛɛ babaoo. Susumɔ ŋaawoi komɛi ni baanyɛ aye abua bo ni okwɛ Biblia mli saji amli lɛ ahe okwɛ.

• Kanemɔ waa. Kɛ́ okane Biblia lɛ waa lɛ, ebaanyɛ eye ebua bo ni osusu nibii ni yaa nɔ lɛ ahe jogbaŋŋ. Kɛji okane Biblia lɛ akɛ weku lɛ, obaanyɛ oha akaunt lɛ aba wala mli kɛtsɔ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni oooha lɛ subaŋ kroko ni ebaakane lɛ nɔ.

• Mfoniri lɛ he mfoniri. Bɔɔ mɔdɛŋ ni emɔ susumɔi kɛ henumɔi ni mɛi ni yɔɔ mli lɛ yɔɔ lɛ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔ nɔ lɛ, bi ohe akɛ: Mɛni hewɔ mɔ lɛ wie loo efee nɔ ni efee lɛ? Kulɛ mɛni masusu he loo manu he kɛji miyɛ nakai shihilɛ lɛ mli lo?

• Gbalamɔ loo mfoniri. Biblia mli nibii ni ajieɔ lɛ kpo lɛ ni ooofee lɛ baanyɛ aye abua bo ni okwɛ saji lɛ jogbaŋŋ, kɛji akɛ o-nibii ni ogbe naa lɛ jeee nitɛŋmɔ nitsumɔ po.
