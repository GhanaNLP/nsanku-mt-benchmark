Susumɔi kɛ henumɔi ni ejaaa. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔwu wɔshi fɔyeli ni akpaaa gbɛ, gbeyeishemɔ ni naa wa, loo haomɔ ni esaaa. Satan, Mɔ ni bɛ hewalɛ lɛ, kɛ henumɔi ni tamɔ nɛkɛ tsuɔ nii, ni ebɔɔ mɔdɛŋ ni eha wɔkpa nitsumɔ. (Matt. 4:3; 6:13) Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ eha wɔ mumɔŋ niyenii ni afee koni ekɛha wɔ nɔmimaa yɛ Yehowa eshaifaa lɛ he, koni ekɛha wɔhiɛ ahi wɔhe nɔ yɛ ehiŋmɛii amli, ni eha hekɛnɔfɔɔ ni wɔyɔɔ yɛ shiwoo ni ewo akɛ ebaakwɛ wɔhiamɔ nii anɔ lɛ mli awa. (Ps. 86:5; Matt. 10:29-31; Luke 12:28; Heb. 13:5) Ani obaanyɛ okai nɔ ko yɛ wɔ woji lɛ eko mli ni taa otsui he ni eha osusu nii ahe, aŋma aha mi? Kaimɔ akɛ Yehowa kɛ saji ni tamɔ nɛkɛ ni baanyɛ abu wɔhe yɛ mumɔŋ lɛ haa yɛ be ni sa mli.

16 Yehowa buɔ ewebii lɛ ahe akɛ kuu ni yeɔ amɛ henyɛlɔi lɛ awui yɛ gbɔmɔtsoŋ. Shi kɛlɛ, bei komɛi lɛ, Yehowa eŋmɛ gbɛ ni mɛi aŋkroaŋkroi ni jaa lɛ kɛ yiwaa ekpe ni amɛgboi po yɛ mɛi ni teɔ shi woɔ lɛ adɛŋ bɔni afee ni amɛtsɔɔ akɛ Satan ji amalelɔ. (Tsɔɔmɔ. 2:10; 6:9) Shi Satan kɛ gbelei fioo nɛɛ bɛ tsakpaa ko kwraa. Akɛni enijiaŋ eje wui hewɔ lɛ, ebaasumɔ ni ena akɛ shitee-kɛ-wolɔi lɛ kpataa Nyɔŋmɔ anɔkwafoi lɛ fɛɛ ahiɛ. (Matt. 24:9; Kwɛmɔ. 12:9-12) Shi kɛlɛ, no baaa mli kɔkɔɔkɔ! Wɔbaanyɛ wɔna nɔmimaa akɛ Yehowa kɛ ebɔfoi lɛ miitsu nii kɛbuɔ ewebii lɛ ahe akɛ kuu bɔni afee ni jamɔ krɔŋŋ lɛ akafee nɔ ko yɛ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ. 1:7, 14.

17 Yehowa buuu wɔhe akɛ aŋkroaŋkroi kɛjɛ gbɔmɔtsoŋ naagbai fɛɛ ahe. Kɛji wɔhi shi yɛ eshishitoo mlai kɛ eshishitoo mlai anaa lɛ, abuɔ wɔhe kɛjɛɔ nifeemɔi kɛ shihilɛi babaoo ni baanyɛ aye wɔ awui yɛ gbɔmɔtsoŋ lɛ ahe. Bɔ ni afeɔ anáa "Tsui ni ekumɔ"

"Ha otsulɔ lɛ tsui anyɔ emli." "-1 KI. 3:9.

Mɛi babaoo boɔ mɛi ni yɔɔ hegbɛ lɛ toi yɛ yiŋtoi srɔtoi ahewɔ. Mɛi babaoo baanyɛ afee nakai kɛji akɛ akwɛ amɛ loo amɛmiisumɔ ni amɛtsi amɛhe kɛjɛ toigbalamɔ he pɛ. Kɛji eba lɛ akɛ abo Nyɔŋmɔ toi lɛ, mɛi komɛi feɔ toiboo koni amɛná gbɛi kpakpa, taakɛ jamɔ mli hiɛnyiɛlɔi ni yɔɔ Yesu gbi lɛ nɔ lɛ fee lɛ. (Matt. 23:5) Nɔ ni tamɔɔɔ enɛ lɛ, wɔbo Yehowa toi ejaakɛ wɔnaa sɛɛnamɔ ni yɔɔ egbɛtsɔɔmɔi lɛ ahe. (Isa. 48:17, 18) No ji yiŋtoo kpakpa ni tsirɛɔ wɔ koni wɔjie toiboo kpo, shi wɔyɛ yiŋtoo ko ni he hiaa fe no po ni baaha wɔbo lɛ toi.

2 Yiŋtoo titri hewɔ ni wɔbo Yehowa toi ji akɛ wɔsumɔɔ lɛ waa. (Matt. 22:37, 38) Wɔsuɔmɔ lɛ tsirɛɔ wɔ koni wɔfee "toiboo kɛjɛ tsui mli." (shi. 6:17) Suɔmɔ kɛha Yehowa kɛ "tsui ni boɔ toi" kanyaa wɔ koni wɔfee bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni wɔɔfee ni wɔye efamɔi lɛ anɔ jeee be mli ni mɛi krokomɛi miikwɛ loo be mli ni wɔnaa sɛɛnamɔi ni jɛɔ nakai feemɔ mli baa amrɔ nɔŋŋ shi moŋ be fɛɛ be. Eji anɔkwale akɛ, wɔtsui lɛ yeɔ anɔkwa, no hewɔ lɛ bei komɛi lɛ, su kpakpa ni wɔhiɛɔ mli lɛ baanyɛ afee kaa. Yɛ sane nɛɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaakwɛ Biblia mli saji enyɔ ni baanyɛ aye abua wɔ kɛna tsui ni boɔ toi. Klɛŋklɛŋ lɛ, wɔbaasusu be ni Yehowa here Israelbii lɛ kɛjɛ Mizraim lɛ he. No sɛɛ lɛ, wɔbaakwɛ Yuda Maŋtsɛ Asa lɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ kpakpa lɛ.

Nikasemɔ niŋmaa kɛha Alemle 30-December 6 26 Bulɔi ni Teɔ shi woɔ "Je lɛŋ Mumɔ lɛ"

kɛwaje PHRASE

"Ha Kristo Mla lɛ Toiŋjɔlɛ yɛ Otsuii Amli"

Kpaŋmɔ 3:15 lɛ tsĩ " Kristo toiŋjɔlɛ lɛ tã." Mɛni ji " Kristo toiŋjɔlɛ"? Ekɔɔ mligbɛ dioofeemɔ ni mɔ ko baanyɛ aná akɛ Kristo kaselɔ lɛ he. Nakai toiŋjɔlɛ lɛ jɛɔ nilee ni aleɔ akɛ Yehowa kɛ e-binuu lɛ sumɔɔ wɔ lɛ mli.-John 14:23, 27.

bɔfo Paulo wo Kristofoi ni yɔɔ asafo ni mli ewo muji lɛ mli lɛ hewalɛ koni amɛha toiŋjɔlɛ nɔyeli nɛɛ awo amɛtsui mli, loo amɛmli gbɔmɔ lɛ mli. Te efeɔ tɛŋŋ? Hela wiemɔ ni Paulo kɛtsu nii yɛ biɛ kɛha "nɔyeli" lɛ kɛ wiemɔ ni ji ummpire yɛ akaŋshii nifeemɔ ko mli lɛ yɔɔ tsakpaa. Ani enɛ etsɔɔɔ akɛ "esa akɛ Kristo toiŋjɔlɛ" lɛ aná naagbee wiemɔ yɛ wɔtsui mli?

Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔ nɔ lɛ, kɛ́ gbeekpamɔ ko te shi yɛ enaanyo hemɔkɛyeli ko he lɛ, esa akɛ wɔha " Kristo toiŋjɔlɛ" lɛ afee "shishiumɔ." Ebaaye ebua wɔ ni wɔkpɛ wɔyiŋ yɛ nɔ ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔfee koni toiŋjɔlɛ kɛ ekomefeemɔ ahi shi lɛ he. Kɛji wɔnyiɛ nakai gbɛ lɛ sɛɛ lɛ, kɛji akɛ etsɔɔ akɛ esa akɛ wɔkɛ nɔ ni wɔsumɔɔ loo nɔ ni wɔnuɔ he lɛ ato he po lɛ, wɔtsɔɔ akɛ wɔha " Kristo toiŋjɔlɛ" nɔyeli lɛ miiha wɔ tsuii amli. -Kɔl. 3:12-14.

Jeee ahɔ̃ɔmɔ wolo nɛɛ. Akɛhaa akɛ jeŋ muu fɛɛ Biblia kasemɔ nitsumɔ ko ni oniatsumɔi ni jɛɔ amɛsuɔmɔ mli amɛhaa lɛ fiɔ sɛɛ lɛ fã. Kɛ ootao ni okɛ onia aha lɛ, ofainɛ yaa shikatoohe.jw.org.

Kɛ atsɔɔɔ gbɛ kroko nɔ lɛ, Ŋmalɛ mli wiemɔi ni atsɛ yisɛɛ lɛ jɛ ŋmɛnɛŋmɛnɛ beaŋ Jeŋ Muu Fɛɛ Shishitsɔɔmɔ Hee ni kɔɔ Septuagint Krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ he lɛ mli.
