5 Ghana Nɔyeli lɛ - Maŋbii 2022 Shika Helɛtemɔ

Maŋ ni Hiɔ Shi Daa lɛ Tsumaa: Shika ni Akɛwoɔ Mli kɛ Nitsumɔ Adebɔɔ

NOWƆD

Shika kui fɛɛ ni Ghana fata he lɛ nu nɔ ni COVID-19 hela lɛ egba mɛi anaa waa lɛ he. Naagbai ni Ghanabii kɛkpeɔ yɛ amɛwala, amɛshihilɛ kɛ jarayeli nitsumɔi ni amɛlaaje lɛ mli lɛ elaajeee Nɔyeli lɛ. Mikɛ miishɛɛ wieɔ akɛ wɔ hetoo ni wɔha kɛha hela ni gbɛ eshwa lɛ he nitsumɔ lɛ yɛ ekãa ni etsɔɔ nɔ ni baaba, ni mɛi ni yɔɔ je lɛŋ he fɛɛ he lɛ sumɔɔ waa. Nɔyeli lɛ hu je mlai shishi koni eye ebua ni ate shi ekoŋŋ yɛ jarayeli nitsumɔi amli ni ekɛ wɔshihilɛ aku sɛɛ aba bɔ ni sa.

Agbɛnɛ hu, yɛ gbeyeishemɔ kɛ hegbɛi ni jɛɔ hela ni gbɛ eshwa lɛ mli baa lɛ hetoohamɔ mli lɛ, Nɔyeli lɛ, yɛ H.E hiɛnyiɛmɔ shishi. Maŋhiɛnyiɛlɔ Nana Addo Dankwa Akuffo-Addo, ni afee GH¢100 akpekpei toi akpei enyɔ kɛ nyɔŋmai-enyɔ kɛ enyɔ, ni ekɛ nɔyaa kɛ nɔyaa ba nitsumɔhei lɛ agbɛjianɔtoo lɛ mli, ( GhanaCARES Obama Pa gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ). Enɛ eha wɔmaŋbii lɛ ená hejɔɔmɔ amrɔ nɔŋŋ ni ehe hiaa waa, ni eji nɔ ko ni he hiaa waa yɛ wɔmaŋ lɛ mli.

Wɔhiɛ sɔɔ hemɔkɛyeli ni okɛ Nɔyeli lɛ yɔɔ beni wɔtsuɔ nii koni wɔná hewalɛ kɛjɛ hela ni gbɛɔ eshwaa mɛi lɛ mli lɛ Yɛ nɛkɛ be nɛɛ mli fɛɛ lɛ, aka mɔdɛŋ ni wɔbɔɔ koni wɔhi shi akɛ maŋ lɛ mli. Biblia lɛ kɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔ ni he hiaa lɛ haa be fɛɛ be yɛ bei ni tamɔ nɛkɛ mli. Yɛ gbɛ titri ko nɔ lɛ, taakɛ awie yɛ Biblia 9:10 lɛ mli lɛ, blɔkii lɛ egbee shi, shi wɔkɛ tɛi ni ewo muji baama; afo ŋmeii lɛ amli, shi wɔkɛ amɛhe baawo mli. Yɛ nakai gbɛ nɔ nɔŋŋ nɔ lɛ, nɔyeli lɛ hu etswa efai shi akɛ ebaaha eshiwoi lɛ aba mli pɛpɛɛpɛ. Yɛ yiŋtoo nɛɛ hewɔ lɛ, wɔbaatsa nɔ wɔkɛ mlai kɛ gbɛjianɔtoi ni kɔɔ yiŋtoi fɛɛ ni elaaje lɛ ahe lɛ atsu nii ni wɔha maŋbii lɛ ashihilɛ aya hiɛ.

Otii titrii ni yɔɔ 2022 Budget lɛ mli lɛ ateŋ ekome, kɛ anɔkwale kɛha afii etɛ (3) ni nyiɛ sɛɛ lɛ, ji ni ama Entrepreneurial State ni yaa nɔ daa ni baanyɛ ekɛ shika helɛtemɔ mli hiɛyaa ni wa, ni yaa nɔ daa, ni ekɛ nitsumɔ ni sa aha mɛi fɛɛ, titri lɛ Oblahii kɛ Oblayei. No hewɔ lɛ, nɔyeli lɛ mlai lɛ baafee nɔ ni baaye abua oblahii kɛ oblayei lɛ koni amɛkɛ amɛhenumɔi atsu nii ni amɛjie nyɛmɔ ni amɛyɔɔ ni amɛkɛbaakwɛ koni shweremɔ aya hiɛ kɛtsɔ hegbɛ ni tamɔ amɛhe kɛha mɛi fɛɛ lɛ kpo.

Bɔni afee ni hekɛnɔfɔɔ ni oblahii kɛ oblayei lɛ yɔɔ lɛ aya hiɛ ni amɛma amɛ nitsumɔ he mumɔ lɛ, Nɔyeli lɛ baaha YouStart gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ aya hiɛ yɛ afi 2022 mli koni ekɛ shika aha oblahii kɛ oblayei ni tsuɔ nii yɛ jarayeli nitsumɔi amli lɛ koni amɛto amɛnitsumɔ lɛ shishi loo amɛha amɛgbɛ amɛshwã.

Yɛ afi 2022 Budget lɛ mli lɛ, wɔtsɔɔ gbɛjianɔi ni ato koni aya nɔ atsu shika helɛtemɔ he naagbai lɛ ahe nii amrɔ nɔŋŋ ni ebaje kpo waa yɛ afi 2022 sɛɛ shika helɛtemɔ be lɛ mli. Nɔyeli lɛ kɛ ehe eha nifeemɔi ni mli kwɔ ni ato he gbɛjianɔ koni ekɛ ŋaa aha ni ekɛwo nitsumɔ mli yɛ oblahii kɛ oblayei ateŋ. Beni wɔtiuɔ Nɔyeli lɛ sɛɛ lɛ

8 Ghana Nɔyeli lɛ - Maŋbii 2022 Shika Helɛtemɔ

ECONOMIC PƐRMANƆ HE TSƆƆMƆ 2021

Macroe shika helɛtemɔ nitsumɔ

3. Be kukuoo mli saji ni kɔɔ shika helɛtemɔ nitsumɔ he yɛ 2021 ji nɔ ni nyiɛ sɛɛ nɛɛ:

• Kɛ aja mli lɛ, GDP diɛŋtsɛ mlijaa 3.5 yɛ 2021 klɛŋklɛŋ fã lɛ mli ni teɔ shi ewoɔ 0.7 oha mlijaa dalɛ yɛ be kome mli 2020;

• GDP ni jeee Oil diɛŋtsɛ lɛ tee hiɛ kɛ aja mli oha mlijaa 4.8 yɛ 2021 klɛŋklɛŋ afi lɛ mli kɛ akɛto oha mlijaa 1.1 he yɛ nakai be lɛ nɔŋŋ mli 2020;

• Wekukpaa ni yɔɔ naagbee-September lɛ ji oha mlijaa 10.6, kɛ akɛto oha mlijaa 10.4 be kome too lɛ nɔŋŋ he yɛ 2020;

• Yɛ shika helɛtemɔ he nyɔmɔwoo (shika) ni ji GDP mlijaa 7.7 lɛ fɛɛ mli lɛ, amɛte shi amɛwo GDP oha mlijaa 7.4;

• Shishijee shika ni eshwɛ lɛ ŋma GDP lɛ mlijaa 2.0 ni efite lɛ he saji, kɛ akɛto shika ni afiteɔ ni ji oha mlijaa 1.7 lɛ he;

• Maŋ nyɔmɔwoo (ni ji GDP mlijaa oha mlijaa) lɛ damɔ shi oha mlijaa 77.7 yɛ September 2021 naagbee gbɛ, kɛjɛ oha mlijaa 76.1 yɛ December 2020 naagbee gbɛ;

• Gross International Reserves yɛ Manyawale 2021 ji US$11.44 billion, ni fa bɔ ni sa ni akɛbaatsu nyɔji 5.2 ni akɛbaajɛ maŋsɛɛ aba lɛ he nii, kɛ akɛto US$8.62 billion ni tamɔ nyɔji 4.1 ni akɛbaajɛ maŋsɛɛ aba ni aŋmala ashwie shi yɛ naagbee 2020 lɛ he; kɛ

• Shika tsakemɔ he nyɔmɔwoo tee nɔ ehi shi yɛ majimaji ateŋ shika wuji lɛ amli kɛha 2021 mlijaa etɛ lɛ. Yɛ gbɛ pɔtɛɛ ko nɔ lɛ, Ghana oha mlijaa 1.7 te shi amɛwo US Dollar lɛ yɛ Gbo 2021 naagbee gbɛ, kɛ akɛto oha mlijaa 2.9 ni ajie yɛ nakai be lɛ mli yɛ 2020 lɛ he.

Glossary (Budget Wiemɔ)

1D1F (One District, One Factory): Nitsumɔhei kɛ nitsumɔhei ni, yɛ no najiaŋ lɛ, amɛbaajie maŋ lɛ kɛya nitsumɔhei wuji amli. Eyiŋtoo ji ni eha Ghanabii lɛ aná nitsumɔi babaoo yɛ maŋ lɛ mli fɛɛ.

Akɔntaabuu: Enɛ ji be mli ni Nɔyeli lɛ baanyɛ aha emaŋbii lɛ ahetoo kɛtsɔ enifeemɔi ni eeeha ale kɛ nɔ ni jɛɔ nifeemɔi ni tamɔ nɛkɛ mli baa lɛ kpojiemɔ nɔ. Nɔ ni fata enɛ he ji shika loo nibii krokomɛi ni Nɔyeli lɛ kɛ ehe fɔ̃ɔ nɔ lɛ ahe gbɛnaa nii.

Nɔ ni akɛhaa: Gbɛ ni atsɔɔ nɔ ajaa loo ajaa nibii ni yɔɔ nɔyeli nitsumɔhei kɛ nitsumɔhei lɛ.

Nɔmimaa: Enɛ ji gbɛ ni atsɔɔ nɔ ahaa naagba ko mli waaa tsɔ.

Shika he gbɛjianɔtoo: Shika he gbɛjianɔtoo ji gbɛjianɔtoo ko ni tsɔɔ he ni esa akɛ aná shika kɛjɛ kɛ nɔ ni esa akɛ afite. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔ nɔ lɛ, weku ko baanyɛ afee shika he gbɛjianɔtoo ko ni baatsɔɔ shika abɔ ni yɔɔ kɛ bɔ ni esa akɛ akɛtsu nii dani afite shika (akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, shĩa nyɔmɔwoo, shika ni akɛwoɔ shĩa mli kɛ niyenii). Kɛ shika babaoo he miihia lɛ, ehe baahia ni weku lɛ asusu shika babaoo ni amɛaaná loo shika ni amɛfaa lɛ he.

Shika ni afiteɔ lɛ he nyɔmɔwoo: Enɛ baa mli kɛ́ Nɔyeli lɛ shika ni efiteɔ lɛ fe shika ni enáa lɛ.

Shika ni akɛwoɔ nitsumɔ mli: Enɛ ji shika ni akɛtsuɔ nibii wuji tamɔ gbɛjegbɛi, skul, helatsamɔhei, blɔji, gbɛfaa, nu gbɛjianɔtoi, tsei kɛ tsɔnei kɛ ekrokomɛi ahe nii.

Nitsulɔi ni awoɔ amɛ nyɔmɔ: Enɛ ji nyɔmɔwoo kɛ nyɔmɔwoo ni kɔɔ shihilɛ mli shweshweeshwefeemɔ, shika ni akɛhaa mɛi kɛ pɛnshɛn ni awoɔ maŋ nitsulɔi lɛ he.

Nɔyaa Hefatalɔi (DPs): Enɛ tsɔɔ maji kɛloo gbɛjianɔtoi ni kɛ maji ni eyaa hiɛ lɛ feɔ ekome kɛtsuɔ nii koni amɛshɛ amɛ hiɛyaa otii lɛ ahe. DPs baanyɛ afee nɔ ni yɔɔ srɔtoi, nɔkwɛmɔnɔ. Majimaji Ateŋ Shika ni Akɛhaa (IMF), Jeŋ Sikatoohe, Afrika Yiwalɛ Shikatoohe (AfDB) loo maŋ enyɔ ni maji aŋkroaŋkroi tamɔ Germany, United Kingdom kɛ United States of America (USA) yɔɔ mli.

Naagbee Afi lɛ mli Nibii ni Akɛwoɔ Mli: Bɔ ni nibii ahe jara yaa hiɛ ehaa lɛ ná niiashikpamɔ yɛ December afi kome mli beni asusuɔ he lɛ.

36 - Maŋbii 2022 Shika Helɛtemɔ

Maŋ ni Hiɔ Shi Daa lɛ Tsumaa: Shika ni Akɛwoɔ Mli kɛ Nitsumɔ Adebɔɔ

Expenditure Overrun: Enɛ baa mli kɛ́ Nɔyeli lɛ shika ni efiteɔ lɛ fe shika ni Parliament lɛ kpɛlɛɔ nɔ lɛ.

FREE SHS: Free SHS (Skul Kpeteŋkpele ni Yeɔ Ehe) mla lɛ ji Ghana Nɔyeli lɛ shishitoo mla ko ni Maŋtsɛ Nana Akuffo-Addo nɔyeli lɛ kɛba yɛ September 2017 ni haa mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni he esa yɛ nikasemɔ mli lɛ baanyɛ aya SHS nikasemɔ mli ni aheee nɔ ko. JHS nikaselɔ.

Nibii ni akɛhaa: Enɛɛmɛi ji shika he yelikɛbuamɔ henɔi ni mɛi ni kɛ amɛhe woɔ hiɛyaa mli lɛ kɛhaa Nɔyeli lɛ, ni ehe ehiaaa ni awo Nɔyeli lɛ nyɔmɔ ekoŋŋ.

Nibii ni akɛhaa Nɔyelii Akui krokomɛi: Enɛɛmɛi ji nyɔmɔwoo ni mla biɔ tamɔ Kpokpaa nɔ KpeeiMaŋ Shika (DACF), Gbɛjegbɛ nɔ Shika, Ghana Tsɔsemɔ He Shika (GETFUND), Petroleum Shika kɛ Maŋ Hewalɛ Shika.

Gross Domestic Product (GDP): Enɛ tsɔɔ naagbee nibii fɛɛ ni ataoɔ kɛ nitsumɔi ni afeɔ yɛ maŋ lɛ mli yɛ be pɔtɛɛ ko mli lɛ jara fɛɛ.

Gross International Reserves: Enɛ haa maŋ ko maŋsɛɛ shika nyɛɔ eheɔ nibii kɛ nitsumɔi kɛjɛɔ maŋsɛɛ maji amli yɛ be ko mli; eyeɔ ebuaa maŋ lɛ shika lɛ hewalɛ hu yɛ maji krokomɛi ashika he.

Inflations: Nibii kɛ nitsumɔi ni jara wa lɛ ayibɔ ni yaa hiɛ yɛ be ko mli.

IPEP (Nibii ni akɛtsuɔ nii kɛhaa Ohia He Saji Ahe Gbɛjianɔtoo): Nibii ni akɛtsuɔ nii kɛhaa Ohia He Saji Ahe Gbɛjianɔtoo (IPEP) lɛ ji NPP Nɔyeli lɛ gbɛjianɔtoi ni he hiaa fe fɛɛ lɛ ateŋ ekome ni yiŋtoo ji ni etsɔɔ ohia kɛ ekomefeemɔ ni baa shi, titri lɛ yɛ akrowai amli ni ajie akutsei kɛtsɔ shishijee nibii ni akɛtsuɔ nii lɛ ahe gbɛjianɔtoo nɔ.

Macroeconomic otii: Enɛɛmɛi ji susumɔi ni akɛtsuɔ nii koni akɛna shika helɛtemɔ nitsumɔ lɛ. Nɔkwɛmɔnii ni fata he ji shika helɛtemɔ mli hiɛyaa, nibii ahe jara ni yaa hiɛ, tsakemɔi ni baa yɛ nitsumɔ mli, jarayeli nitsumɔ kɛ maji krokomɛi, nɔyeli shika helɛtemɔ he mlai ni yeɔ omanye loo ni fiteɔ kɛ yiŋkpɛɛi ni Bank of Ghana lɛ feɔ ni saa shika ni ataoɔ kɛ shika ni akɛhaa yɛ shika helɛtemɔ mli lɛ he.

MSME (Micro kɛ Small Buisness Nɔyaa He Gbɛjianɔtoo): Atsɔɔ micro-enterprise (loo microenterprise) shishi akɛ nitsumɔ bibioo ko ni kɛ gbɔmɛi ayibɔ bibioo tsuɔ nii, kɛ shika ni eshwɛ loo shika ni baa shi fe shika ko .

NBSSSI (Maŋ Nitsumɔhe Nine kɛha Smalls bibii kɛ Medium Nitsumɔhe lɛ): Maŋ Nitsumɔhe Nine ni Kwɛɔ Smallet Industries (NBSI) lɛ ji maŋ nitsumɔhe ko ni tsuuu nii kɛtaooo shika yɛ Jarayeli kɛ Nitsumɔhe Nitsumɔhe lɛ shishi, ni aha lɛ nitsumɔ akɛ ewo Micro kɛ Nitsumɔhe Bibii (MSEs) lɛ adalɛ kɛ amɛhiɛyaa he hewalɛ.

Nɔ ni jeee Oil Real GDP: Ani nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni jɛɔ shika helɛtemɔ mli baa ni jieɔ nitsumɔi ni yɔɔ mu nitsumɔ lɛ mli lɛ kɛjɛɔ mli, ni no susuɔ bɔ ni nibii ahe jara wa ha lɛ he.

Jeee Tooyeli He Shika: Nɔyeli lɛ shika ni jɛɔ tooyeli mli baa, nɔkwɛmɔnii ji nyɔmɔwoo kɛha gbɛŋmɛɛ wolo loo gbɛŋmɛɛ woji, mɛi ni kɛtsuɔ nii lɛ nyɔmɔwoo kɛ nyɔmɔwoo krokomɛi.

Gbɛnaa nii ni Fe Fɛɛ: Gbɛnaa nii ni sa kadimɔ waa lɛ ji MDA shiwooi ni ekɛ Shika He Nitsumɔhe lɛ kɛ agbɛnɛ hu 2016 nyɔmɔwoo ni sa kadimɔ waa kɛha Mla He Shika.

Nɔ titri ni akɛtoɔ he : Enɛ ji srɔtofeemɔ ni yɔɔ Nɔyeli lɛ shika ni enáa lɛ kɛ shika ni ekɛhaaa lɛ teŋ (ni nyɔmɔwoo nitsumɔ nyɔmɔwoo fataaa he). Abaanyɛ asusu enɛ he akɛ eji Gross Domestic Product (GDP) lɛ mlijaa oha mli ekome.

Dalɛ ni agbe naa: Bɔ ni Ghana shika helɛtemɔ baaya hiɛ yɛ afi mli.

Shika ni anáa: Enɛ ji shika fɛɛ ni Nɔyeli lɛ nine shɛɔ nɔ yɛ enitsumɔi ahe kɛjɛɔ maŋ lɛ mli kɛ maŋsɛɛ fɛɛ.

RFJ (Rearing For Food and Jobs): ebaaha kooloi ahe akaŋshii ni tsuɔ nii jogbaŋŋ aba, ni baaha shia mli nibii aya hiɛ, ebaaha kooloi ni akɛbaajɛ maŋsɛɛ aba lɛ aba shi, ebaaye ebua kɛha nitsumɔ, ni ebaaha kooloi ahe jara he nitsulɔi lɛ ashihilɛ aya hiɛ.
