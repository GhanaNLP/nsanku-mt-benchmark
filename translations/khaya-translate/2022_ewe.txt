In addition to this, everyone should engage in the financial services sector to get jobs done, especially the youth. This is why Government programs will support the youth to demonstrate their talents and skills by providing equal access to everyone, to satisfy the issues of activity for the general public.

In the Fiscal System of 2022, we have planned to continue to work on the financial financial problems that are urgently needed with the objective of financial resources being stable after the COVID-19 pandemic. The government has committed itself to implementing a variety of policies that will develop the youth's skills and promote the issues of self-service among the youth. As we strive to protect the Presidential objective of 'Ghana No Need for Support', I want to encourage all the citizens to participate in the development of these visions as well as develop innovations.
