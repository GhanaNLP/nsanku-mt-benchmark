Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐNEA SKOHYƐ-GOD NO HO NSƐM BA SƐ WƆBƐHYƐ SƐ "NKYERƐKYERƐMU" ho asɛm.

Kwaku AnaNSE, Spider no, kɔɔ Nyankonton, Wim-nyame no nkyɛn sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛtɔ Wim-nyame no nsɛm. Wim-nyame no kae sɛ, "So wubetumi atɔ wɔn?" Spider no kae sɛ, "Mmom no, mɛtumi." Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Nkuro akɛseɛ a ɛwɔ tumi te sɛ kokofuo, Bekwai, Asumengya aba nanso wɔantumi antɔ wɔn na wo deɛ woyɛ ɔbarima a wonim nyansa kɛkɛ na woka sɛ wobɛtumi." Spider no kae sɛ, "Bo bɛn na (asɛm no) bo yɛ?" Wim-nyame no kae sɛ, "Wɔrentumi ntɔ wɔn mma biribiara gye Onini abɔde a wɔfrɛ no Python no; Osebo, Leopard no; Mmoatia, Fairy no; (ne) Mmoboro, Hornets." The Spider se, "Mede saa nneɛma yi nyinaa bi bɛba, na (nea ɛboro saa), mede m'aberewa, Nsia (Abarimaa a ɔto so Nsia) bɛka ho." Wim-nyame no kae sɛ, "Kɔ na fa wɔn bra afei." Spider no bae, na ɔkaa ho asɛm kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ metɔ Wim-nyame no ho nsɛm, na Wim-nyame no se ɛsɛ sɛ mede Onini, Python no ba; Osebo, Leopard no; Mmoatia, Fairy bi; ne Mmoboro, Hornets, na mekae sɛ mede wo bɛka lot no ho na mekɔ na mede Sky-nyame no bɛma." Afei Spider no bisabisaa ne yere Aso nsɛm, na ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ na yɛatumi anya Onini, Python no?" na aaron ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ momfiri adi nkɔtwa mmɛ dua nkorabata na montwitwa ntwamutam bi nso na momfa mmrɛ wɔn na abɔsoɔ no sane baa wɔn nkyɛn ɛnna Aso kaa sɛ, "Fa wɔn kɔ asuo no mu." Na Ananse faa wɔn; sɛnea na ɔrekɔ no ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛware kyɛ sen sɛnea ɔte no, ɛnyɛ bere tenten te sɛ ɔno; modi atoro, ɛware sen no." Spider no kae sɛ, "Ɔwɔ hɔ a, ɔda akyirikyiri." Python (a na wate saa nkɔmmɔbɔ a wɔde wɔn adwene bu yi) kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛfa ho nyinaa?" The Spider se, "So ɛnyɛ (me yere) Aso, hena na ɔne me regye akyinnye sɛ saa abɛ yi kyɛ sen wo, na mese ɔyɛ ɔtorofo." Na Onini a ɔyɛ Python no kaa sɛ, "Fa bra na bra bɛsusu me." Ɔfaa abɛn no de guu Python amu no ho. Ɔkae sɛ, "Trɛw wo ho mu." Na Python no tenee ne ho firii adi, na Ananse faa hama-kyekyere no pirapiraa no (na na ɛnne a wɔde kyekyere nsɛm no yɛ ntɛm! nwenene! nwenene! kɔsii sɛ ɔbaa ne tiri so Ananse a ɔyɛ Spider no kaa sɛ, "Fool, mede wo bɛkɔ Wim-nyame no nkyɛn na manya Wim-nyame no anansesɛm (de sesa)." Ananse de no kɔɔ Nyame (Wim Nyame). Wim-nyame no kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka (i.e. Me nsa aka no, nea aka no da so ara wɔ hɔ." Spider no sane bae na ɔbɛkaa deɛ asi no kyerɛɛ ne yere na ɔkaa sɛ, "Hornetfoɔ no da so ara wɔ hɔ." na ne yere kaa sɛ hwɛ gourd na fa nsuo hyɛ mu ma na firi hɔ kɔ Spider no faa kwae no mu, bere a ohui sɛ Hornets (akuwakuw bi) sɛn hɔ no, ɔhwiee nsu no bi guu Hornetfo no so. Afei Spider no hwiee nkaeɛ no guu ne ho so na ɔtwitwaa dua no ahaban na ɔde kataa ne tiri so. na afei ɔkasa kyerɛɛ hornetsefoɔ no sɛ sɛdeɛ osuo retɔ nie na sɛ woammra yei mu a ɛnneɛ m'abantenten na osuo rentɔ mmɔ wo na woanhunu sɛ mafa ɛserɛ so ahaban sɛ mɛkata me ho anaa? Afei Hornet abusua no kaa sɛ, "Yɛda mo ase sɛ Aku, yɛda mo ase Aku." Hornetfo no nyinaa tu faa ɔkwan no mu, na ɛyerae kɔɔ ɛferɛ no mu! Paapa Spider kataa ano, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Aduane, manya wo, na mede wo rekɔgye Wim-nyame (de sesa) ho anansesɛm." na ɔde ɛserɛ so kɔɔ ewiem nyankopɔn nkyɛn Wim-nyame no kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka, deɛ aka (da so ara) da so ara wɔ hɔ." Spider no san bae bio, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere, na ɔkae sɛ, "Osebo aka, Leopard." Aso kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na tu amena." Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛno ara dɔɔso, mete aseɛ." Afei Spider no fii adi kɔhwehwɛɛ Leopard no akwan, na (wahu wɔn) otutuu amena bi a emu dɔ yiye, na ɔkataa so, na ɔsan baa fie. Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu, bere a nneɛma fii ase daa adi no, Spider no kae sɛ obefi hɔ, na bere a ɔkɔe no, (lo) Leopard bi da amena no mu. Ananse kaa sɛ: "Agya ba ketewa, ɛna kumaa ba, maka makyerɛ wo sɛ nnom nsa, na afei sɛdeɛ obi bɛhwɛ wo kwan no, saa ara na woaboro nsa, na ɛno nti na woahwe ase amena mu na sɛ meka sɛ anka mɛyi wo afiri adi a adeɛ kyeeɛ no wohunu me anaa saa ara nso na me mma mu biara bɛkɔ akɔkyere wɔn." Leopard no kaa sɛ, "O! Mantumi anyɛ biribi a ɛte saa." Ananse kɔtwaa nnua mmienu de baako sii ha, na baako nso wɔ hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa wo nan baako to ha, na wo nan baako nso wɔ ha." na leopard de wɔn sii hɔ (baabi a wɔka kyerɛɛ no). Bere a ɔreforo no, Ananse maa ne sekan so, na ɔde kanea bi sii ne ti, gao! (na ɛyɛ nnyigyei a wɔde yɛɛ no). Ɔdamoa no nsa kaa ɛda (ne) ɛda no! (na ɛte sɛ nea ɔhwee ase no). Ananse nyaa antweri bi a ɔde bɛsian akɔ amena no mu akɔgye nnipa no afiri adi. Ɔnyaa Leopard firii adi na ɔde baeɛ; ɔkaa sɛ, "Fool, mede wo rekɔ sesa ewiem-nyame no ho nsɛm." Ɔmaa nnipa no so sɛ wɔnkɔma Nyame a ɔyɛ wim nyame no. Wim-nyame no kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka, deɛ aka no da so ara wɔ hɔ." Spider no bae, na odii Akua ba bi (dua doll tuntum a n’anim yɛ tratraa), na ɔkyekyeree latex bi a ɛyɛ nnam (a efi dua bi mu) na ɔde plaster guu doll no amu so, na ɔbɔɔ eto (mashed bayerɛ), na ɔde bi guu doll no nsa mu, na ɔbɔɔ mu bio na ɔde guu kɔbere asubɔnten bi mu; ɔkyekyeree ahama twaa doll no ase, na ɔde kaa ho de sii odum-dua bi ase, beae a Faiies no ba bedi agoru no. Na fairy bi bae. Ɔkae sɛ, “Akua, ebia medi mash yi mu kakra?” Ananse de ne nsa kyerɛɛ ahama no so, na doll no bɔɔ ne ti. Fairy no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea biako sɛ, "Ɔka sɛ ebia medi bi." Ɔkae sɛ, “Enti kum bi.” Ɔdiidiiɛ wieeɛ no, ɔdaa no ase (doll no). Nanso bere a ɔdaa no ase no, wamma ho mmuae. na fair see ne nuabaa no sɛ sɛ meda no ase a ɔmmua Fairy nuabea (nea edi kan) kae sɛ, “Su ne baabi a ɔresu.” Na ɔbɔɔ mu, pa! Na ne nsa daa hɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no sɛ, “Me nsa aka hɔ.” Ɔkae sɛ, “Fa nea aka no na bɔ no akuturuku bio.” Na ɔfae na ɔbɔɔ no paulo! na yei nso kɔɔ so yɛɛ ntɛmntɛm. Na fair no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no sɛ, "Me nsa mmienu akɔgyina ntɛmntɛm." Ɔkae sɛ, “Fa wo yafunu hyɛ mu.” Ɔpiaa no, na ne yafunu no bataa ho. na ananse ba bɛkyekyeree no na ɔkaa sɛ fa wo nsa maka wo mede wo bɛkɔ ɔsoro nyankopɔn nkyɛn ama n'asɛm na ɔne no kɔɔ fie na Ananse ka kyerɛɛ ne maame yasia a ɔyɛ yosia ba a ɔtɔ so nsia no sɛ sɔre ma yɛnkɔ ɛfiri sɛ mede wo ne farisifoɔ rekɔ akɔma ɔsoro nyankopɔn no wɔ n'asɛm no ho ɔmaa wɔn so kɔe na ɔfirii hɔ kɔɔ faako a ewiem nyame no wɔ na ɔkaa sɛ ɛha yi onyame bi wɔ ha na m'afenaa a mekaa ne ho asɛm no wɔ ha yi ɔno nso wɔ hɔ Afei ewiem-nyame no frɛɛ (ne mpanyimfo), Kontire ne Akwam ahemfo, Adonten (asraafoɔ kuo kɛseɛ no kannifoɔ), Gyase (major-domo), Oyoko, Ankobea, ne Kyidom (akyi-wɛmfo panyin). Na ɔde asɛm no too wɔn anim kaa sɛ, "Ahemfo akɛseɛ pa ara aba, na wɔantumi antɔ Wim-nyame no ho nsɛm, nanso Kwaku Ananse, Spider no, atumi atua boɔ no; manya afiri ne hɔ Mmoboro, Hornetfoɔ; manya ne hɔ Mmoatia, Fairy, nsa aka bi afi ne hɔ Osebo a ɔyɛ Leopard no hɔ; manya Onini a ɔyɛ Python no hɔ, na ɔno ara apam ne sɛ, Ananse de ne maame aka ho; saa nneɛma yi nyinaa da ha.” Ɔkae sɛ, “Yɛto n'ayeyie dwom.” “Eee!” (wɔteɛɛm). Onyankopɔn a ɔwɔ wim no kaa sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse, ɛfiri da biara da na ɛrekɔ so daa, mefa me wim-nyame nsɛm na mede makyerɛ wo, kose! kose! kose! me nhyira nhyira ne nhyira Yɛremfrɛ wɔn ewiem nyame no ho nsɛm bio, na mmom yɛbɛfrɛ wɔn Spider-stories."

Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
