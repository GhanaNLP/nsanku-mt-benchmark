Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐnea na ɛte wɔ ɔkwan a ɛfata so no, CRICket, GOT HIS Kenkanfo a wɔwoo no

AKETEKYIRE, Kriket, ne Ananse, Spider, fii ase kae sɛ wɔrekɔhwehwɛ aburoo sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wobedua. na wɔnyaa atokoɔ mmienu pɛ ɛnna Ananse kaa sɛ, "Mefiri adi kɔdua me nnɔbaeɛ." Na Kriket no kaa sɛ, "Mɛtutu me nan sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a mɛwe." Ananse kɔduaa no. Ɔno nso kɔfaa no de guu ogya no mu. Ná ɛware sɛ ɔbɛnoa aduan, na ɔtraa hɔ (ɛka ho). Ɔno nso, ɔkɔɔ afuw no mu; na ɔrebɛhyɛ kotoku a ɛkata aburo no so no ase. Atokoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Mfa wo nsateaa nkɔ m'ani so, anyɛ saa a wo nsateaa bɛkɔ m'ani so." Na Ananse redan n'ani ahwɛ ne kraman; ne kraman kaa sɛ, "Ɛho nhia sɛ wohwɛ me, ɛnyɛ me na meyɛeɛ." Na Ananse rebebubu dua bi de aboro Ɔkraman no, bere a dua no kae sɛ, "Mmubu me, efisɛ (sɛ woyɛ a) wo kraman bɛka me." Ananse baa fie bɛkaa ho asɛm kyerɛɛ kriket no nyinaa. Bere a na ɔreserew no, ogya tɔɔ mu. Saa kwan no so na Kriket no se hyewee. Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
