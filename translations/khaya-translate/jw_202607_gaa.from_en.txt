Saamɔ nɔ ni efite lɛ oya!

Ani nyɛmi nuu loo nyɛmi yoo ko pila bo aahu akɛ amrɔ nɛɛ efite wekukpaa ni kã oshihilɛ mli lɛ? Bɔni afee ni osaa nibii ni efite lɛ ni ofee nanemɛi lɛ, esa akɛ okɛ yiŋtoo kpakpa atsu nii oya. *

Kɛha nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, esa akɛ atsa wu ni mli ewo wu koni enyɛ etsu nii jogbaŋŋ ni piŋmɔ ko bɛ he. Shi piŋmɔ lɛ baaya nɔ kɛji akɛ ajieee jwɛŋmɔ kɛjɛɛɛ nifeemɔ lɛ nɔ. Nɔ ni ehiii fe fɛɛ lɛ, wu lɛ baanyɛ afee nɔ ni ehiii kwraa ni enyɛɛɛ etsu nii jogbaŋŋ.

Tamɔ wu ni mli ewo wu lɛ, esa akɛ atsa wekukpaa ni efite ni kã mɔ ko kɛ enaanyo Kristofonyo teŋ lɛ oya. Kɛ jeee nakai lɛ, piŋmɔ ni jɛɔ naanyobɔɔ ni ekumɔ mli baa lɛ baaya nɔ. Nuhɔmɔ baanyɛ ewa, ni ekolɛ onaanyo lɛ baaŋmɛɛ bo. Yesu kɛɛ: Feemɔ oya kɛtsu saji ahe nii. (Matt. 5:25) No hewɔ lɛ, yaa onaanyo lɛ ŋɔɔ ni okɛ lɛ agba sane yɛ mlihilɛ mli shi yɛ faŋŋ mli yɛ sane lɛ he. Mɛni hewɔ ebaanyɛ efee nɔ ni wa lɛ? Susumɔ yiŋtoi enyɔ ahe.

Klɛŋklɛŋ lɛ, ekolɛ abaaka bo koni oku ohiɛ oshwie shihilɛ lɛ nɔ ni otsuuu he nii, ekolɛ ejaakɛ ohe ejɔɔɔ bo yɛ naagbai ahe.# Susumɔ nyɛmimɛi yei Kristofoi ni etamɔ nɔ ni amɛnyɛŋ amɛtsu gbeekpamɔ ko he nii lɛ ahe okwɛ. Ekolɛ amɛ naagba lɛ tee nɔ, ejaakɛ bɔfo Paulo, ni yɔɔ Roma shɔŋŋ lɛ po nu he. Ewo amɛ hewalɛ ni amɛfee nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni he hiaa koni ekomefeemɔ ahi amɛteŋ. (Philɛn. 4:2) Kɛtsɔ heshibaa, sanegbaa kpakpa, kɛ anɔkwa eshaifaa nɔ lɛ, amɛnaanyobɔɔ lɛ baanyɛ aya hiɛ ekoŋŋ.

Nɔ ni ji enyɔ lɛ, obaanyɛ okpa gbɛ akɛ mɔ kroko lɛ baakpɛlɛ etɔmɔ lɛ nɔ. Shi ootao ni onine ashɛ onaanyo lɛ nɔ ekoŋŋ, kaaje tɔmɔ lɛ shishi. Datrɛfonyo ni kwɛɔ wu ni ekumɔ lɛ nɔ lɛ kɛ ejwɛŋmɔ baama tsamɔ nɔ, shi jeee nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni kɛ mligbalamɔ lɛ ba lɛ mlitaomɔ. Kɛ́ okɛ onaanyo lɛ miiwie lɛ, kaakɛ ojwɛŋmɔ ama mɔ ni sa akɛ ashwa yɛ nɔ ni efite lɛ hewɔ lɛ nɔ. Moŋ lɛ, kaimɔ bɔ ni ohe hiaa ha yɛ naanyobɔɔ lɛ mli, ni okɛ ojwɛŋmɔ ama nɔ ni esa akɛ ofee kɛsaa lɛ nɔ.

Mɛni hewɔ okɛ onyɛmi nuu loo onyɛmi yoo lɛ egbaaa sane ŋmɛnɛ, ni eyiŋtoo ji ni esaa nibii ni efite lɛ ni okɛ naanyobɔɔ lɛ aku sɛɛ aba? Kaaha hulu lɛ aka shi be mli ni omli efu lolo lɛ. (Eph. 4:26) Kɛji akɛ ofee nɔ ko ni akɛsaa nɔ ni efite lɛ oya lɛ, ebaaye ebua ni ebu wɔ toiŋjɔlɛ tsakpaa lɛ he.Eph. 4:1-3.
