Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SAA ANANSƐM, ƆSƐNKYERƐKYERƐFO, NO MU NSƐM HO NSƐM

ANANSE O! ɔwaree Nsia babaa a na ɔyɛ Wim-nyame maame no na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ Nyame (Wim-nyame) sɛ "Da a wo maame Nsia wui no, bra bɛka da a wɔapaw ama ayie amanne no kyerɛ me."

bɛyɛ nnansa akyiri no wɔbaa ne nkyɛn bɛka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔsoro nyankopɔn maame awu Afei na Ananse mogya abusuabɔ ne 'Berekuo, Ɔhene Jay, Kwakuo, Bonsu, ne Odwan, Odwan. Afei Ananse kasa kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Wɔaba abɛka ayie bi ho asɛm akyerɛ me, enti ɛsɛ sɛ woka me ho." enti ananse maa wɔde nkra kɔmaa ewiem nyame sɛ ɔreba afahyɛ no ase Afei ewiem nyame maa ne manfoɔ nyinaa boaboaa wɔn ho ano, sɛdeɛ Omanhene bi hyiaeɛ no. ɛnna Ananse kaa sɛ anwummerɛ ɛdwo na mɛba Spider no yɛɛ bag, na emu no ɔde 'Berekuo, Ɔhene Jay, Kwakuo, Bonsu, ne Odwan, Nguan, ne ɔde bag no guu ne mmati so, na ɔde ne ntama kyekyeree so. Afei ɔkasa kyerɛɛ abɔdeɛ no sɛ, "Sɛ meduru wim-nyame no nkyɛn a monsu." Bere a oduu Wim-nyame anim no, 'Berekuo no maa soro (ne nne):')

"Kurukusu, ku! ku! ku!

Kukusu, ku! ku! ku! ni."

Na Bonsu no maa soro (ne nne):

"Kakum!"

Na Nguan no maa so (ne nne):

"Me!"

Ná eyi yɛ Kwaku Ananse awerɛhowdi. ɔman no nyinaa kaa sɛ moayɛ no yie ɔbarima baako nie na ɔbɛtumi asu na wadi awerɛhoɔ wɔ akwan ahodoɔ mmiɛnsa so Bere a owiee (n'abooboodi no) no, ewiem nyame no hwɛɛ ofie bi a ɛyɛ fɛ sɛ ɔbɛtra mu. Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma daa adi no, onyame a ɔwɔ wim no noaa aduan pa de maa Ananse. Kwaku Ananse hunuu saa aduane yi no ɔkaa sɛ, "Meremfa biribiara mma Berekuo, memma Kwakuo biara, meremfa biribiara mma Odwan, ɛfiri sɛ madi mmuada akyɛ." ɛnna ananse diiɛ na wamfa ne mogya ammrɛ no Afei, ɔne wɔn kɔɔ so ara kɔɔ so, na Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Yɛbɛyɛ nnawɔtwe ayie afahyɛ no." Nanso Nguan no kaa sɛ (kyerɛ Ananse), "Ɔkɔm rekum me, menkɔ"; Kwakuo ne 'Berekuo nso kaa saa asɛm korɔ no ara. afei ewiem nyankopɔn kɔtenaa nkurɔfoɔ a wɔahyia wɔn no mu na ɔmaa wɔka kyerɛɛ ananse sɛ adepa a ɔyɛɛ no da a ɛdi akyire no ɔnyɛ bio ɛfiri sɛ ɔne ne mpanimfoɔ boaboaa wɔn ano sɛ wɔrebɛtie Ananse buae sɛ, "Me ho ntɔ me." Ade kyee no, Wim-nyame no ka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, "Woama m'ani so atɔ fam esiane aniwu nti." na berɛsosɛm ne yakobo ne isak na ɔkaa sɛ yɛn nso woama ɛkɔm aba yɛn soɔ enti yɛne mo nyinaa bɛtetew mo ho afiri abusuakuo no ho So Kwakuo ne 'Berekuo fii adi kɔɔ wuram, nanso Odwan a ɔyɛ Nguan no kɔɔ so traa fie; na Spider no huruw na ɔde petee nnua no so; ɛno ne sɛ na n’ani awu.

Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
