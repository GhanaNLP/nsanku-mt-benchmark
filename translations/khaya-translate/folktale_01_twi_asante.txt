SE’YOYE A OBONTO AND NATION’KWA

It is good for Nana Aberua to have her own, and her goods are lost, and she has destroyed the water and the animal that is paining. When it comes to life, he says, "A', you've humiliated us, so what we are going to see is that: Go and fetch a sheaf and let's stand and fetch it, and he who can't draw it out is the one who can’t draw it and it is the one who can’t.” She brought the pain; Okoto cried for me; we have waited for me; Mɔbonse will die; Okawa will die; Oyoyo will die; Otedie will die; Okobɔ will die; Okore will die; Okobo will die; water will not stop. And later on, Obonɔ Yaa will also get well, and he will not be ashamed, and we will be afraid saying: "Way Ya and take. Asamoah said, "I don't like you." Animals did not stop saying, "We are going to beat him up, and he has scolded us." And we hit him. After we knocked him out, Nana Abewa said, “I completely abandoned you, you just don’t want someone to make you laugh.”
