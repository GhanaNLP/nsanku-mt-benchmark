Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Ɛsɛ sɛ Ɔbarima biara a ɔyɛ ɔbaabun no de ne ho hyɛ ɔbarima ne ɔbea nna mu wɔ Agumadi mu

Bere bi na abaawa bi a wɔfrɛ no Kwaboaso wɔ hɔ. Obiara a wɔde no maa no sɛ ɔnware no ɔkae sɛ, "Mempɛ no." Wɔde no maa ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ bi, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Ampa ara! Saa ɔbarima yi ahwɛ ne so, mempɛ no."

Da bi ɔkɔɔ mfuw no mu, na ɔkae sɛ ɔrekɔtwa plantains. Ɔfaa sekan bɔɔ pɔnkɔ no so, kɔɔ! na hwɛ na nkurɔfoɔ nketewa no te borɔdɔma no soɔ na wɔsii fam na wɔbae na wɔbɛkyeree isak na wɔkaa sɛ wo na wɔde wo bɛma obiara na wowoso wo tiri a ɛyɛ pɛsɛɛ! pusu!" na adwammaa no kyeree no na wɔkaa sɛ mommra mma yɛmpam no

Yɛpia no, O!

Yɛpia Kwaboaso.

Bra, momma yɛmpia no.

Yɛpia no, O!"

Afei, saa ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ a wɔmaa Kwaboaso no tee Kwaboaso nne no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Merebɛhwɛ asɛm no, ɛfiri sɛ yɛmfa biribi bɔne ntua biribi bɔne." Ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, na Kwaboaso wɔ hɔ, na guasodeyɛ ahorow no hwehwɛɛ no. Afei ɔbɔmmɔfo no tow tuo guu guasodeyɛ ahorow no so, na biako hwee ase. Ɔpanyin a ɔwɔ guasodeyɛ ahorow no mu kae (kyerɛ afoforo no), "Wanom nsa, na wabow nsa; fa no si hɔ saa bere yi, (na afei kɔ so hwehwɛ no mu." Afei ɔbɔmmɔfo no tow tuo, na ɔhwee ase bio. Ɔpanyin a ɔwɔ guasodeyɛ ahorow no mu kae sɛ, "Ɔyɔnko akokodurufo no anom nsafufuw na wɔadi so nkonim, de no akɔto hɔ." Abɔmmɔfo no kunkum guasodeyɛ no nyinaa gye ɔpanyin no nkutoo. Ɔpanyin no frɛɛ ɔbɔmmɔfo no sɛ, "Bra." Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Meremma." Ɔpanyin a ɔwɔ guasodeyɛ ahorow no mu kae sɛ, "O, bra, merenyɛ wo hwee." Ɔbɔmmɔfo no kɔe. Ɔpanyin a ɔwɔ guasodeyɛ no mu kae sɛ, "Hwɛ me dan mu wɔ hɔ, na wubehu aduru no ama tuo no (i.e.). otuotofoɔ no ne atuo a woatow agu no nyinaa, fa deɛ ɛyɛ wo dea na fa Kwaboaso nso, nanso ansa na wobɛkɔ no, kɔ na kɔtwitwa kwadu, na berɛ a worekɔ no, to wɔn twene, na sɛ guasodeɛ a aka no sɔre na wɔba bɛkyere wo a ɛnneɛ ɛberɛ a wɔagyae sɛ wɔrebɛfa wɔn no, mmiako mmiako, woakyɛ, akyɛ, akyɛ." na ɛno akyiri no ɔkɔtwaa kwadu na ɔfaa isak nso na ɔduruu kwan no soɔ no ɔtoo kwadu baako kɔe Ɔkɔɔ so yɛɛ saa wɔ fie nyinaa. na ɛberɛ no mu kakraa bi pɛ na aka ansa na wɔadu fie na hwɛ adwammaa no redi wɔn akyi na ɔtotoo kwadu baako pɛ a aka no gui na adwammaa no dii akyire na awieeɛ koraa no ɔdanee ne ho na ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no sane sii mu kɔɔ ne mogya so Afei abɔmmɔfo no fii adi kɔɔ n'ankasa fie. afei na abɔfoɔ no te hɔ na ɔhunuu abɔfoɔ sɛ wɔadu ne nkyɛn na ɔkaa sɛ ɛdeɛn ne asɛm yi? abɔfoɔ no se, "Kwaboaso se waka akyerɛ akuraa no ti no sɛ ɔmmɛsrɛ ama no na ɔkaa sɛ afei deɛ ɔwɔ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔbɛware wo." Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Meda mo ase sɛ nsɛm a ɛfiri akuraa no ase panin anomu nanso merentumi nware ɔbaa no, ɛfiri sɛ me nsa da so ara wɔ me nipadua so." ɛno nti na mpanimfoɔ see wɔn sɛ sɛ wɔgye mo awadeɛ mu na wɔde mo ma awadeɛ mu awareɛ mu a monware no ɛfiri sɛ monnim na mmom ɛda bi a mo ho bɛhia no no ɔbɛgye mo nkwa

Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
