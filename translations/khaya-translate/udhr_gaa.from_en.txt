Jeŋ Muu Fɛɛ Adesai Ahegbɛi Ahe Jajemɔ

Hiɛkpamɔ

Yɛ be mli ni bulɛ ni jɛ fɔmɔ mli kɛ hegbɛi ni adesai aweku lɛ mli bii fɛɛ yɔɔ ni anyɛɛɛ ajie kɛjɛ mli lɛ he kpɛlɛmɔ ji heyeli, jalɛsaneyeli kɛ toiŋjɔlɛ shishitoo yɛ je lɛŋ lɛ,

Yɛ be mli ni bulɛ ni abuuu kɛ bulɛ ni abuuu adesai ahegbɛi lɛ kɛ yiwalɛ nifeemɔi ni eha adesai a henilee mli efu lɛ eba lɛ, kɛ jeŋ ko ni adesai baaná wiemɔ kɛ hemɔkɛyeli kɛ heyeli kɛjɛ gbeyeishemɔ kɛ shwelɛ mli lɛ ni eba lɛ ji nɔ ni atswa he adafi akɛ eji nɔ ni mɛi fɛɛ sumɔɔ fe fɛɛ . gbɔmɛi,

Yɛ be mli ni ehe hiaa lɛ, kɛji akɛ esaaa akɛ anyɛɔ gbɔmɔ nɔ koni etsu nii, akɛ naagbee gbɛ lɛ, atuatsemɔ ni teɔ shi ewoɔ yiwalɛ kɛ nɔnyɛɛ, akɛ esa akɛ mla abu adesai ahegbɛi ahe . ,

Yɛ be mli ni ehe hiaa ni awo naanyobɔɔ wekukpaa ni yɔɔ maji ateŋ lɛ he hewalɛ lɛ,

Yɛ be mli ni Jeŋmaji Ekomefeemɔ lɛ mli bii lɛ ema amɛhemɔkɛyeli yɛ adesai ahegbɛi ni he hiaa waa lɛ amli lɛ nɔ mi ekoŋŋ lɛ, yɛ bulɛ kɛ adesa gbɔmɔtso lɛ jara kɛ hii kɛ yei ahegbɛi ni tamɔ amɛhe lɛ mli, ni amɛkpɛ amɛyiŋ akɛ amɛbaawo shihilɛ mli hiɛyaa kɛ shihilɛ mli shishitoo mlai ni hi jogbaŋŋ lɛ ahe hewalɛ yɛ heyeli wulu mli . ,

Yɛ be mli ni Maji ni yɔɔ mli lɛ ewo shi akɛ amɛkɛ Jeŋmaji Ekomefeemɔ lɛ baafee ekome kɛtsu nii lɛ, jeŋ muu fɛɛ bulɛ ni akɛhaa adesai ahegbɛi kɛ heyeli ni he hiaa waa lɛ he hiɛyaa,

Yɛ be mli ni hegbɛi kɛ heyeli nɛɛ shishinumɔ ni afɔɔ namɔ he hiaa fe fɛɛ kɛha shiwoo nɛɛ mlibaa lɛ,

Agbɛnɛ, no hewɔ lɛ,

Kpee Kpeteŋkpele lɛ

Nɛkɛ Jeŋ Muu Fɛɛ Adesai Ahegbɛi Ahe Jajemɔ nɛɛ ji nɔ ni afɔɔ feemɔ kɛhaa gbɔmɛi fɛɛ kɛ maji fɛɛ, kɛyashiɔ naagbee akɛ aŋkroaŋkro fɛɛ aŋkroaŋkro kɛ maŋ muu lɛ fɛɛ kuu, ni baaha Jajemɔ nɛɛ ahi jwɛŋmɔ mli be fɛɛ be lɛ, baabɔ mɔdɛŋ kɛtsɔ tsɔɔmɔ kɛ tsɔsemɔ nɔ koni ebu hegbɛi kɛ heyeli nɛɛ ahe kɛ kɛtsɔ gbɛjianɔtoi ni yaa hiɛ, maŋ kɛ majimaji ateŋ nɔ, koni amɛná amɛ jeŋ muu fɛɛ kpɛlɛmɔ kɛ odaseyeli ni mɔɔ shi jogbaŋŋ, yɛ Maji ni yɔɔ mli lɛ diɛŋtsɛ kɛ shikpɔŋkukuji ni yɔɔ amɛhegbɛ shishi lɛ amli bii lɛ ateŋ.

Niŋmaa 1

Afɔ adesai fɛɛ akɛ mɛi ni yeɔ amɛhe ni amɛyeɔ egbɔ yɛ bulɛ kɛ hegbɛi amli. Ahaa amɛ yiŋtoo kɛ henilee ni esa akɛ amɛfee amɛhe nii yɛ nyɛmifeemɔ mumɔ mli.

Niŋmaa 2

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛi kɛ heyeli fɛɛ ni ato he gbɛjianɔ yɛ Jajemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ hegbɛ, ni srɔtofeemɔ ko bɛ he, tamɔ hewolonɔ su, hewolonɔ su, bɔlɛnamɔ, wiemɔ, jamɔ, maŋkwramɔŋ loo susumɔi krokomɛi, maŋ loo shihilɛ mli, nibii, fɔmɔ loo gbɛhe kroko.

Kɛfata he lɛ, srɔtofeemɔ ko bɛ maŋ loo shikpɔŋkuku ni mɔ ko yɔɔ mli lɛ maŋkwramɔŋ, hegbɛ loo majimaji ateŋ gbɛhe, kɛji eji eheyeli, hekɛnɔfɔɔ, mɔ ni jeeehe-kɛ-afɔleshaa loo nɔyeli he husu kroko shishi.

Niŋmaa 3

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ehiɔ shi, heyeli kɛ gbɔmɔ shweshweeshwe shihilɛ.

Niŋmaa 4

Mɔ ko mɔ ko wooo nyɔŋyeli loo nyɔŋyeli mli; abaagu nyɔŋyeli kɛ nyɔŋyeli jarayeli yɛ amɛgbɛi fɛɛ amli.

Niŋmaa 5

Mɔ ko mɔ ko kɛ piŋmɔ loo yiwalɛ, adesayeli loo toigbalamɔ ni baa shi baakpe.

Niŋmaa 6

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ekpɛlɛɔ nɔ yɛ he fɛɛ he akɛ mɔ ko yɛ mla hiɛ.

Niŋmaa 7

Fɛɛ yeɔ egbɔ yɛ mla hiɛ ni eyɛ hegbɛ ni ekɛ hiɛaŋkwɛmɔ ko bɛ mli ni buɔ mla lɛ he yɛ gbɛ ni tamɔ nakai nɔŋŋ nɔ. Amɛ fɛɛ amɛyɛ hegbɛ ni amɛbuɔ amɛhe kɛjɛɔ mɛi ahe jwɛŋmɔ ni ejaaa ni akuɔ Jajemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ he, ni amɛteɔ shi amɛwoɔ kanyamɔ fɛɛ ni akɛhaa mɛi ahe jwɛŋmɔ ni ejaaa ni tamɔ nɛkɛ lɛ.

Niŋmaa 8

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ maŋ kojomɔhei ni he esa lɛ baatsu he nii jogbaŋŋ yɛ nifeemɔi ni kuɔ hegbɛi titrii ni maŋ mla loo mla kɛhaa lɛ lɛ mli lɛ hewɔ.

Niŋmaa 9

Mɔ ko mɔ ko tsuŋ nii yɛ gbɛ ni ejaaa nɔ, awo lɛ tsuŋ loo aŋɔ lɛ nom.

Niŋmaa 10

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ekɛ kojomɔ he ko ni yɔɔ eheyeli ni kwɛɛɛ hiɛaŋ lɛ yeɔ egbɔ yɛ jalɛsaneyeli kɛ maŋ saneyeli mli kwraa, yɛ hegbɛi kɛ gbɛnaa nii ni kã enɔ kɛ efɔŋfeemɔ fɛɛ ni akɛwoɔ enɔ lɛ he yiŋkpɛɛ mli.

Niŋmaa 11

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni awo lɛ fɔ akɛ efee efɔŋ lɛ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ esusuɔ akɛ efeko nɔ ko kɛyashi be mli ni abaatsɔɔ akɛ eye fɔ yɛ maŋ kojomɔ ko ni ekɛ nɔmimaa fɛɛ ni he hiaa kɛha ehebuu lɛ ewo mli lɛ mli.

Mɔ ko mɔ ko yeee fɔ yɛ toigbalamɔ ko he yɛ nifeemɔ ko loo nɔ ko ni ajie kɛjɛ mli ni jeee efɔŋfeemɔ he toigbalamɔ, yɛ maŋ loo majimaji ateŋ mla shishi lɛ hewɔ, yɛ be mli ni afee lɛ. Ni toigbalamɔ ni tsii hu baafee nɔ ni tsii fe nɔ ni akɛtsu nii yɛ be mli ni afee toigbalamɔ lɛ.

Niŋmaa 12

Mɔ ko mɔ ko kɛ ehe wooo eteemɔŋ saji, eweku, eshia loo woji amli yɛ gbɛ ni ejaaa nɔ, ni etutuaa ehiɛ nyam kɛ egbɛi kpakpa lɛ nɔ. Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ebuɔ mla lɛ he kɛjɛɔ nɛkɛ he ni akɛwoɔ mli loo tutuamɔi nɛɛ ahe.

Niŋmaa 13

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ efãa gbɛ ni ehiɔ Maŋ fɛɛ Maŋ husui lɛ amli.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ eshiɔ maŋ fɛɛ maŋ, ni lɛ diɛŋtsɛ enɔ̃ fata he, ni ekuɔ esɛɛ kɛyaa emaŋ.

Niŋmaa 14

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ etaoɔ abobaahe yɛ maji krokomɛi anɔ kɛjɛ yiwaa mli.

Anyɛɛɛ atsɛ hegbɛ nɛɛ yɛ kojomɔi ni jɛɔ awuiyeli ni jeee maŋkwramɔŋ saji amli loo nifeemɔi ni teɔ shi woɔ Jeŋmaji Ekomefeemɔ lɛ yiŋtoi kɛ eshishitoo mlai amli lɛ mli diɛŋtsɛ.

Niŋmaa 15

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ enáa maŋ.

Mɔ ko mɔ ko kɛ ehe wooo maŋ ni ejɛ mli lɛ mli yɛ gbɛ ni ejaaa nɔ, ni ahaaa lɛ hegbɛ ni etsake maŋ ni ejɛ mli lɛ.

Nikasemɔ 16

Hii kɛ yei ni eye afii muu lɛ fɛɛ, ni husu ko bɛ he yɛ hewolonɔ su, maŋ nɔ ni amɛjɛ loo amɛjamɔ hewɔ lɛ, yɛ hegbɛ akɛ amɛboteɔ gbalashihilɛ mli ni amɛnáa weku. Amɛyɛ hegbɛi ni tamɔ amɛhe yɛ gbalashihilɛ mli, yɛ gbalashihilɛ mli kɛ be mli ni amɛfiteɔ lɛ.

baabote gbalashihilɛ mli kɛtsɔ hefatalɔi ni yiŋtoo ji lɛ akpɛlɛmɔ ni yeɔ emuu ni yeɔ emuu lɛ pɛ nɔ.

Weku lɛ ji adebɔɔ mli kuu ni he hiaa waa, ni eyɛ hegbɛ akɛ maŋ kɛ maŋ lɛ buɔ amɛhe.

Niŋmaa 17

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ lɛ pɛ eyɔɔ nibii, ni ekɛ mɛi krokomɛi hu feɔ ekome.

Mɔ ko mɔ ko kɛ ehe wooo nibii amli yɛ gbɛ ni ejaaa nɔ.

Niŋmaa 18

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ esusuɔ nii ahe, henilee kɛ jamɔ he; hegbɛ nɛɛ mli heyeli ni ekɛtsakeɔ ejamɔ loo ehemɔkɛyeli, kɛ heyeli, lɛ pɛ loo yɛ akutso mli kɛ mɛi krokomɛi kɛ teemɔŋ, koni ejie ejamɔ loo ehemɔkɛyeli kpo yɛ tsɔɔmɔ, nifeemɔ, jamɔ kɛ gbijurɔyeli mli lɛ fata he.

Niŋmaa 19

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ esusuɔ nii ahe ni ejieɔ ejwɛŋmɔ kpo; hegbɛ nɛɛ mli heyeli ni akɛhiɛɔ susumɔi amli ni mɔ ko kɛ ehe wooo mli, ni akɛtaoɔ saji kɛ susumɔi ni akɛhaa kɛtsɔɔ adafitswaa woji fɛɛ anɔ kɛ husui ni kɔɔɔ he eko lɛ fata he.

Niŋmaa 20

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ enáa heyeli yɛ toiŋjɔlɛ kɛ ekomefeemɔ mli.

Anyɛŋ mɔ ko mɔ ko nɔ akɛ efata kuu ko he.

Niŋmaa 21

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ekɛ ehe woɔ emaŋ nɔyeli lɛ mli, tɛ̃ɛ loo kɛtsɔ najiaŋdamɔlɔi ni ahala amɛ ni amɛheee nɔ ko lɛ anɔ.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ekɛ ehe woɔ maŋ nitsumɔ mli yɛ emaŋ lɛ mli.

Gbɔmɛi lɛ asuɔmɔnaa nii baafee nɔyeli hegbɛ lɛ shishitoo nɔ; abaajie enɛ kpo yɛ oshikifɔɔ ni afeɔ yɛ be kɛ bei amli kɛ anɔkwa mli ni jeŋ muu fɛɛ kɛ oshikifɔɔ hegbɛ ni yeɔ egbɔ lɛ baafee nɔ ni yɔɔ mli lɛ mli, ni abaafee kɛtsɔ teemɔŋ oshikifɔɔ nɔ loo kɛtsɔ oshikifɔɔ gbɛjianɔtoi ni tamɔ nakai nɔ.

Nikasemɔ 22

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ, akɛ maŋ lɛ mlinyo lɛ, yɛ hegbɛ akɛ enáa shihilɛ mli shweshweeshwefeemɔ, ni eyɛ hegbɛ akɛ enaa, kɛtsɔ maŋ mɔdɛŋbɔɔ kɛ majimaji ateŋ nitsumɔ nɔ kɛ gbɛjianɔtoo kɛ nibii ni yɔɔ Maŋ fɛɛ Maŋ mli lɛ, shika helɛtemɔ, shihilɛ kɛ kusum hegbɛi ni he hiaa waa kɛha ebulɛ kɛ esubaŋ ni yaa hiɛ ni aheee nɔ ko lɛ nɔ.

Niŋmaa 23

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ etsuɔ nii, ehalaa nitsumɔ ni aheee nɔ ko, ekɛ nitsumɔ mli shihilɛi ni ja ni esumɔɔ waa lɛ tsuɔ nii, ni ebuɔ ehe kɛjɛɔ nitsumɔ ni anaaa lɛ he.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ, ni mɛi ahe jwɛŋmɔ ni ejaaa bɛ mli lɛ, yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ewoɔ nyɔmɔ ni tamɔ nakai nɔŋŋ kɛha nitsumɔ ni yeɔ egbɔ.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni tsuɔ nii lɛ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ekwɛɔ koni lɛ diɛŋtsɛ kɛ eweku lɛ aná shihilɛ ni sa adesa bulɛ, ni ekɛfata he, kɛ ehe miihia lɛ, kɛtsɔ gbɛi krokomɛi ni atsɔɔ nɔ abuɔ gbɔmɛi ahe lɛ nɔ.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ efeɔ ni ekɛ ehe woɔ nitsulɔi akui amli kɛha nibii ni esumɔɔ lɛ ahebuu.

Niŋmaa 24

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ekɛ ehe jɔɔ ehe ni ekɛ ehe jɔɔ ehe, ni nɔ ni fata he ji nitsumɔ be kɛ gbijurɔyelii ni afeɔ yɛ be kɛ bei amli ni awoɔ nyɔmɔ lɛ ahe husu ni sa.

Niŋmaa 25

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ehiɔ shi bɔ ni sa kɛha lɛ diɛŋtsɛ kɛ eweku lɛ hewalɛnamɔ kɛ amɛhilɛ-kɛhamɔ, ni niyenii, atadei, shĩa kɛ helatsamɔ kɛ gbɔmɛi ateŋ nitsumɔi ni he hiaa fata he, kɛ hegbɛ ni eyɔɔ akɛ ebaafee shweshweeshwe kɛji nitsumɔ ni anaaa, hela, gbɔlɛ, okulafo, afii ni eho loo nibii krokomɛi ni bɛ shihilɛ mli yɛ shihilɛi ni fe nɔ ni ekwɛɔ nɔ lɛ mli.

Nyɛmɛi kɛ gbekɛbii yɛ hegbɛ akɛ amɛkwɛɔ amɛbii jogbaŋŋ ni amɛyeɔ amɛbuaa amɛ. Gbekɛbii fɛɛ, kɛji akɛ afɔ amɛ yɛ gbalashihilɛ mli loo amɛbote gbalashihilɛ mli lɛ, baaná shihilɛ mli hebuu kome too lɛ nɔŋŋ.

Niŋmaa 26

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ekaseɔ nii. Kɛ hoo lɛ, nikasemɔ baafee nɔ ni aheee nɔ ko, yɛ shishijee kɛ shishijee mli. Esa akɛ mɔ ko akase nii yɛ skul. Akɛ ŋaa kɛ nitsumɔ he nikasemɔ baafee nɔ ni mɛi fɛɛ baanyɛ aná yɛ sɛɛnamɔ ni yɔɔ he lɛ nɔ.

Abaatsɔɔ nikasemɔ kɛha adesa su lɛ fɛɛ kɛ bulɛ ni ayɔɔ kɛha adesai ahegbɛi kɛ heyeli ni he hiaa waa lɛ mli hewalɛwoo. Ebaaha shishinumɔ, tsuishitoo kɛ naanyobɔɔ aya hiɛ yɛ maji fɛɛ, hewolonɔ su loo jamɔ kui ateŋ, ni ebaaha Jeŋmaji Ekomefeemɔ lɛ nitsumɔi aya hiɛ koni toiŋjɔlɛ ahi shi.

Fɔlɔi yɛ hegbɛ dani amɛhalaa nikasemɔ ni amɛbaaha amɛbii lɛ.

Niŋmaa 27

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ekɛ ehe woɔ akutso lɛ kusum shihilɛ mli, ni enáa nitsumɔi lɛ ahe miishɛɛ ni ekɛ ehe woɔ jeŋ nilee mli hiɛyaa kɛ sɛɛnamɔi ni jɛɔ mli baa lɛ amli.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ebuɔ jeŋba kɛ heloonaa nibii ahe ni jɛɔ jeŋ nilee, woji loo nitɛŋmɔ nitsumɔ fɛɛ ni lɛ ji mɔ ni ŋma lɛ mli baa.

Niŋmaa 28

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ enáa shihilɛ kɛ majimaji ateŋ gbɛjianɔtoo ko ni hegbɛi kɛ heyeli ni ato he gbɛjianɔ yɛ Jajemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ baanyɛ aba mli kwraa.

Niŋmaa 29

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ nitsumɔ yɛ akutso lɛ mli, ni lɛ pɛ ji mɔ ni baanyɛ afee esubaŋ lɛ ni aheee nɔ ko ni yeɔ emuu.

Yɛ hegbɛi kɛ heyeli ni ekɛtsuɔ nii lɛ mli lɛ, mɔ fɛɛ mɔ baafee nɔ ni yɔɔ husui ni mla tsɔɔ lɛ pɛ shishi kɛha yiŋtoo akɛ ebaabu mɛi krokomɛi ahegbɛi kɛ heyeli ni sa lɛ he ni eshɛ jeŋba kpakpa, maŋ gbɛjianɔtoo kɛ shweremɔ he taomɔ nii ni ja lɛ ahe yɛ demokrase maŋ mli.

Anyɛɛɛ akɛ hegbɛi kɛ heyeli nɛɛ atsu nii yɛ gbɛ ko nɔ ni teɔ shi ewoɔ yiŋtoi kɛ shishitoo mlai ni yɔɔ Jeŋmaji Ekomefeemɔ lɛ mli lɛ.

Niŋmaa 30

Abaanyɛ atsɔɔ nɔ ko nɔ ko ni yɔɔ Jajemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ shishi akɛ etsɔɔ akɛ Maŋ, kuu loo mɔ ko yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ekɛ ehe woɔ nifeemɔ ko mli loo efee nɔ ko ni yiŋtoo ji ni akpata hegbɛi kɛ heyeli ni ato he gbɛjianɔ yɛ biɛ lɛ eko fɛɛ eko hiɛ.
