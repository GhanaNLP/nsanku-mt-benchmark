Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA NE HO NSƐM A ƐFA MMOFRA A WƆWƆ HO (FIRST) HO NSƐM

WƆKA sɛ (pɛnkoro pɛ) ɔkɔm kɛse bi bae, na Paapa Ananse, Spider, ne ne yere Aso, ne ne mma, Ntikuma, Nyiwankonfwea (Thin-shanks), Afudotwetotwe (a ɛte sɛ Bely-to-burst), ne Tikonokono (Big-ti) sii atrae kakra na wɔtraa mu. Da biara na Spider no kɔ de nnuane, wuram bayerɛ ba, na na wɔanoa adi. Afei da bi, Agya Ananse kɔɔ wuram; ohui sɛ aduan fɛfɛ bi gyina hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, “Saa aduan yi yɛ fɛ.” Aduan no kae sɛ, “Me din nyɛ fɛ” Spider no kae sɛ, “Dɛn na wɔfrɛ wo?” Ɛbuaa sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ me sɛ, 'Fill-up-and-diats'." Spider no kae sɛ, “Ma ebinom nhu.” Aduane a wɔde ngo nkwan ahyɛ mu ma no, na Ananse nso dii ne nyinaa. Ɔwieeɛ no ɔbisaa aduane no sɛ, "Dɛn ne wo taboo no?" Aduan no buae sɛ, “Metan atuo a wɔasen, ne kuruwa ketewaa bi.” Ná Spider no de aduan no kɔ fie, na ɔkɔe na ɔde sii mpomma no so. Afei Ananse siane kɔɔ wuram na ɔde aduane baeɛ na Aso deɛ ɔwiee aduane na ɔfrɛɛ no Ananse, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Oo, wo deɛ ɛhia me pa ara, me deɛ meyɛ akwakoraa, ɛdeɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ wɔ aduane ho, wo deɛ ne wo mma yi deɛ yɛ adeɛ a ɛho hia pa ara; sɛ wodi ho fɛw a, ɛnneɛ m'aso ayɛ komm (ɛfiri w'awerɛhoɔ nnyigyeiɛ mu). Bere a wodiidii wiei no, Spider no twaam wɔ apata no akyi, na ɔkɔɔ mpomma no so faako a na aduan no wɔ no. Ɔkae sɛ, “Saa aduan yi yɛ fɛ.” Ɛbuaa sɛ, “Me din nyɛ fɛ” Ɔkae sɛ, “Wo din de sɛn?” Ɛkaa sɛ, “Wɔfrɛ me sɛ ɛyɛ adeɛ a ɛyɛ frɔmfrɔm na ɛyɛ aduane.” Ananse kaa sɛ, “Boaboa bi ano ma menhwɛ.” na ɛhyɛɛ mprɛte bi a wɔde nnuane a ɛyɛ frɔmfrɔm ahyɛ mu ma na ananse dii Da biara a ɔbɛsɔre no, na ɛte saa. Afei, Ntikuma hunuu sɛ ne papa nyinii teateaa, ɛmfa ho sɛ na wɔne ɔno nso nnidi nnidi, na Ntikuma kɔɔ so ara hwɛɛ n'agya so na ɔhunuu deɛ agya no anya. Bere a agya no fii adi kɔɔ wuram no, Ntikuma foro kɔɔ soro (ɛwɔ mpomma no ano) na ohuu aduan no. ɔfrɛɛ ne na ne ne nuanom na wɔforo kɔɔ soro Ntikuma kaa sɛ, “Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ.” Ɛkae sɛ, “Wɔmfrɛ me “ɛyɛ fɛ”.” Ɔkae sɛ, “Afei ɛdeɛn na yɛfrɛ wo?” Ɛkaa sɛ, “Me din de “Fill-up-and-diat”.” Ɔkae sɛ, “Ma menhu kakra.” na wɔde ngo nkwan hyɛɛ aduane no ma de bɛhyɛɛ mu ma na ntonto ne ne na ne ne nuanom didi kɔsii sɛ ne nyinaa wieeɛ Na afei Ntikuma bisaa aduane no sɛ, “Dɛn na wotaa yɛ?” Aduane no kaa sɛ, “Metan atuo a wɔasen ne borɔdɔma ketewaa bi.” Ntikuma ka kyerɛɛ Afudotwotwe (a ɛte sɛ nea ɛyɛ papa) sɛ, “Kɔ na fa bi brɛ me.” na ɔde wɔn bae na ɔfaa tuo no kɔkaa no (aduane no) ne kuruwa ketewa no na ɔde ne nsa kaa aduane no. Afei wɔn nyinaa sii fam. Na na Paapa Spider (mean-time) asan aba (a ɔne wuram yam asan aba). Aso wiee noa. Wɔfrɛɛ Ananse; ɔbuaa sɛ ebia woante asɛm a mekaeɛ no, mekaa sɛ ɛfiri da koro rekɔ no, sɛ mede aduane ba fie a mobɛdi bi, ɛfiri sɛ mo ara na mohia mmoa Aso ne ne mma didii. Paapa Spider hohoroo ne ho; ɔforoo mpomma mu; ɔkae sɛ, “Aduan yi yɛ fɛ.” Kommyɛ a edi mũ! “Saa aduan yi yɛ fɛ.” Kommyɛ a edi mũ! Paapa Spider kae sɛ, “Ah! Ɛsɛ sɛ ɛfiri saa ntoma yi mu na ɛnyɛ fɛ, mɛkɔ akɔfa Oyoko abusua no mu baako tartan aba na mede ato hɔ. Na ɔfirii aseɛ kɔfaa Oyoko abusua no tartan ntoma sɛ wɔnhyɛ. Ɔhyɛɛ ne mpaboa; ɔforoo kɔɔ hɔ bio. Ɔkae sɛ, “Saa aduan yi yɛ fɛ.” Kommyɛ a edi mũ! “Saa aduan yi yɛ fɛ.” Kommyɛ a edi mũ! Ɔhwɛɛ ɛdan no mu nyinaa na ɔhunuu sɛ tuo awe na kuruwa ketewa bi wɔ mu. Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ adeɛ baako, ɛnyɛ adeɛ mmienu (i.e. Akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ ɛyɛ Ntikuma.” Ananse bubuu aduane no, na ɔbaa fam; oyii Oyoko tartan ntama no de too fam, na ofii hɔ kɔɔ wuram. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ohui sɛ mpire no fɛfɛ bi a wɔfrɛ no Mapere sɛn hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, “Oo, ɛyɛ nwonwa! Saa adeɛ yi yɛ fɛ sene deɛ ɛtwa toɔ no, saa mpire yi yɛ fɛ pa ara. Wɔkae sɛ, “Wɔmfrɛ me “ɛyɛ fɛ”.” Ɔno (Spider no) kae sɛ, “Ɛnde ɛdeɛn na yɛfrɛ wo?” Ɛkaa sɛ, “Wɔfrɛ me “Abiridiabrada” (kyerɛ sɛ, “Swish-and-raise-weals”). na ɔkaa sɛ monnom kakra mma me nhunu na mpire no tɔɔ ne so sɛ nsuo a ɛyɛ mmerɛ na ɛyɛ dɛnkyɛm Paapa Spider sui sɛ, “Pui! pui! Anomaa bi (a ɔte bɛn hɔ) ka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, “Say Adwobere” (a ɛkyerɛ, cool-and-easy-now). Ɛnna Ananse kaa sɛ, “Adwobere.” na mpire no gyaee hwe no na Ananse de saa mpire yi baa fie na ɔkɔe na ɔde sii mpomma mu. Aso wiee aduane no, ɔkaa sɛ, “Anansefoɔ mommra mmɛdi.” ɔbua see no sɛ ɛsiane sɛ woda so wɔ ha (asaase so) nti ebia wowɔ amena biara wɔ w'aso mu (na woante deɛ mekaeɛ no nti merenni Ananse foro kɔɔ soro kɔtenaa ase komm. Afei ɔsan baa fam bio; afei ɔkɔɔ bio na ɔde ne ho hintaw baabi. Afei Ntikuma foro kɔɔ soro. Ɔkae sɛ, "Oo, saa papa (a ofi me) no de biribi aba bio." Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Maame, Nyiwankonfwea, Afudotwotwe, bra ha, ɛfiri sɛ, deɛ agya de aba (saa berɛ yi) na ɛboro deɛ ɛtwa toɔ no so koraa." Afei wɔn nyinaa foro foroo mpomma no. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Adeɛ yi yɛ fɛ." Ɛbuae sɛ, "Wɔmfrɛ me 'ɛyɛ fɛ'." Ɔkae sɛ, "Wo din de dɛn?" Ɛkaa sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ me 'Swish-and-raise-weals'." Ɔkae sɛ, "Kakra ma menhwɛ." na saa mpire no siane baa ne so bɛhwee wɔn denneennen. Ananse gyinaa nkyɛn, na ɔteɛɛm sɛ, "Da so ara wɔ so, de to so, titiriw wɔ Ntikuma so, de to no so." Afei berɛ a Ananse hwɛɛ na ɔhunuu sɛ wɔhwee wɔn yie no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Adwobere" (Cool-and-easy-now). na ananse ba bɛfaa mpire no na ɔtwitwaa mu nketenkete na ɔpetee wɔn ho Ɛno na ɛmaa mpire no baa abusuakuo no mu. ɛnna ɛbaa sɛ wokaa asɛm bi kyerɛɛ w'akoa na ɔrentie wo a yɛbɔ no mpire

Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
