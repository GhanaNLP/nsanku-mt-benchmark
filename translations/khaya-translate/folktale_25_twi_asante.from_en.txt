Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐ OBI BƐYƐ SƐ OBI BƐFA WO (akwanhyia) A, NKWAE MA ƐNYƐ NSƐM

WƆKA sɛ bere bi na mmanoma a wɔn maame awuwu na wogyaw wɔn (wɔn nkutoo). Na wɔredi afahyɛ bi, na na mmanoma a wɔn mfe akɔ anim (Ata’Panin) faa nhwiren bi a ne bo yɛ den ne sika kɔkɔɔ nkotokuo bi de sɛn atare kumaa no kɔn mu, Ata Kuma kɔn. Ntasu kumaa no ne ne nnamfonom kae sɛ wɔrekɔ asuten no mu akɔguare. Bere a wɔreguare no, ahama a wɔde nhama atwa Ata Kuma kɔn ho ahyia no bubui, na agude no guu nsu no mu. Sɛnea ɔbɛyɛ no, na ontumi nhu. Ɔsan baa fie bɛbɔɔ ne nuabea amanneɛ, na ɔkae sɛ, "Sika kɔkɔɔ nneɛma no ayera." Ata 'Panin (ɔpanyin twin) kae sɛ, "Minnye nni koraa sɛ wɔyera, na sɛ wɔwɔ hɔ mpo a, biribi wɔ hɔ a mɛka afi wo hɔ." Ata Kuma (nwansena kumaa no) kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na wobɛka afiri me hɔ?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mɛhwehwɛ wo hɔ sɛ asono nwɔtwe, Aserewa anomaa ketewa no bunu, Nsuo a ɛhyerɛn pɔnponponsona, ahaban a ɛno nko ara ne (wɔ dua a wotu firii mu no so), ne Asono Ɔhemmaa Maame dua. Ata Kuma kae sɛ, "Mate." Ata Kuma sii mu (ɔrekɔhwehwɛ saa nneɛma yi), na ɔbaa beaeɛ pɔtee bi a na dua baako gyinagyina hɔ. Bere a dua no huu no no, ɔkae sɛ, "Ata Kuma (a nkurɔfo ka ne ho asɛm) na ɔda ne ho adi wɔ kuro no akyi te sɛ osu a ɛtɔ a ɛma ahoɔden." (Ata Kuma) kae sɛ, "Ade biako pɛ nti a maba ha ne sɛ agya ba ne Ata 'Panin biako, na ɛna ba yɛ Ata 'Panin biako, na ɔde nhwiren a ɛsono emu biara bɔɔ mu na ɔde sɛn me kɔn ho, na wɔbubuu mu na wɔyerae." (Ɛha na Ata Kuma bubuu dwom):

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

ɔrehwehwɛ me nhaban bi afiri dua bi mu a ɛsoa gye ahaban baako pɛ

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔreka sɛ ahaban bi fi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔreka sɛ Aserewa anomaa no buw.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ sɛ wɔde nsu, a ɛredi akɔneaba wɔ mpɔtam hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ. Ɔrekyerɛ sɛ ɔyɛ Agya Lepard mmaranimfo.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrekyerɛ sɛ Ɔhemmaa a ɔwɔ Asono no mu no dua, kosi ne ntini mu.

Na dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ, agya, na manya bi?

Ɔka sɛ (ha)abofra kumaa bi, Akwasi Toboro Kojo babea.

Kojo Nsa na ɔkyerɛwee.

Agya Asono, na ɛsɛ sɛ wuyi wo ho fi hɔ, na ɛsɛ sɛ wuyi wo ho fi hɔ.

Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ yei nti monsu mommra mmɛgye biribi Na ata Kuma kɔgyee (Aserewa anomaa no bunu), na ɔdaa no ase. na ɔfirii adi na ɔhyiaa nsuo a na ɛredi akɔneaba wɔ ponoponsona mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ata Kuma wɔ hɔ a, sɛ ɔpue wɔ akuraa no ase abɔntene awieeɛ a, nkurɔfoɔ ma no akwaaba te sɛ osuo a ɛtɔ a ɛma ahoɔden.” Ata Kuma kaa asɛm a ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn a wɔaka no dedaw no kyerɛɛ no. na nsuo a ɛtɔ gugu ne soɔ maa no bi (n'asorɔkyeɛ nsuo no bi) na ɔfaeɛ na ɔtoaa so kɔeɛ. Afei ɔne Leopard hyiae. Berɛ a Leopard hunuu no no ɔkaa sɛ, “Ata Kuma wɔ hɔ, na ɔkɔpuee abɔntene no awieeɛ a, ɔte sɛ nsuo a ɛretɔ.” Ata Kuma ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔnyɛ adwuma. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mommra mmɛgye biribi," na ɔmaa ne nsensaneeɛ no mu baako. Afei Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, “Agya Leopard, ɛdeɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ na manya Ɔhemmaa a ɔda Asono ano no dua?” Ɔkaa sɛ, "Aa, worekɔ no, mmoa no nyinaa bɛkɔ fam, na Asonohemmaa no bɛda mfimfini, na worekɔ no, tim wɔn so denneennen, na sɛ woduru Asonohemmaa no nkyɛn a, twitwa ne dua, nanso sɛ wode ahwɛyie tim wɔn so a, wɔbɛkum wo." Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, "Meda wo ase." na ɔrekɔ no ɔhunuu saa mmoa yi; ɔtiatiaa wɔn so denneennen, na ɔduruu Ɔhemmaa no nkyɛn maame nkyɛn. Otwaa ne dua, na ɔsan n’akyi. Bere a ɔrekɔ no ɔtɔɔ ahwehwɛ bi a ɛte sɛɛ, na ɔde kɔɔ fie. Na oduu fie. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panin, nsɛm a wokaa sɛ wobɛgye afiri me nkyɛn nie." Ata 'Panin kaa sɛ, "Me nsa aka wɔn." Afei, da koro na wɔbom tena, na Ata Kuma faa ahwehwɛ no hwɛɛ mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Saa ahwehwɛ yi, sɛ obi hwɛ mu a, ohu ɛna a ɔwɔ Ahonhommɔne asase so." Ata 'Panin kaa sɛ, "So ɛte saa, fa bra, na ma menhwɛ." Ɔde maa no. na ɔrebɛhwɛ mu no ɛdwane firii ne nsam bubui Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, "Na yei deɛ wonya firi me hɔ no, mɛhwehwɛ afiri wo hɔ." Afei Ata Panin kɔfaa nneɛma a Ata Kuma de brɛɛ no no. Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, "Mempɛ (eyinom), deɛ mepɛ ne sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa foforɔ aba me so." Ata 'Panin fii adi, na ɔkɔhyiaa dua bi a ahaban baako na ɛwɔ mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panin wɔ hɔ a sɛ ɔpue akuraa no ase abɔntene awieeɛ a, wɔma no akwaaba te sɛ osuo asuo." Ata 'Panin buaa sɛ, "Mayera' wo maame! na w'agya dawid deɛ ma me w'ahaban baako na ma me kwan (Dua no ne) ahaban a ɛyɛ ankonam no kaa sɛ, "Obi wɔ hɔ a, deɛ ɔnim (sɛdeɛ ɔne wo bɛdi) wɔ hɔ." Ɔtoaa so, na ohyiaa Aserewa anomaa bi. Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panin na ɔpue wɔ akuraa no ase abɔntene no awieeɛ a na ɔrema ahoɔden te sɛ asuo a ɛretɔ." Ata 'Panin kaa sɛ, "Sɛ me ne wo maame ne w'agya wɔ hɔ a, deɛ mepɛ ara ne wo buo mu baako, na ma menkɔ." Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, "Pass, deɛ ɔnim (sɛdeɛ ɔne wo bɛdi) wɔ hɔ." Afei ohyiaa Gurgling-su. Gurgling-nsuo kaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panin wɔ ha; sɛ ɔpue wɔ kuro no awieeɛ a, ɔte sɛ asuo a ɛma ahoɔden." Ata 'Panin kaa sɛ, "Sɛ meba a, w'agya yi w'agya ho adi ma me wo nsuo no bi na ma me kwan." Gurgling-nsuo kaa sɛ, "Ɔwɔ anim no, deɛ ɔnim (sɛdeɛ ɔne wo bɛdi) wɔ hɔ." Afei ohyiaa Leopard no. Leopard kaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panin wɔ hɔ, a sɛ ɔpue abɔntene awieeɛ a, ɔma n'ahoɔden te sɛ osuo asuo." Ata 'Panin buae sɛ, "Mete aseɛ' wo maame! ne wo papa! na me deɛ ma me wo nsateaa no bi na ma me kwan Leopard kaa sɛ, "Deɛ ɔnim (sɛdeɛ ɔne wo bɛdi) wɔ hɔ." Ofii adi, na ohyiaa Asono akuw bi, na Asono no Hemmaa da mfinimfini. Afei ɔkɔɔ so twaa ne dua. Asono mmoakuo no nyinaa kumm no na wɔtiatiaa no so na ɔwui Ata Kuma hwehwɛɛ ne nuabea wɔ ɔkwan no so. Wamma. ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ sɛ wo ne wo mogya mu bi tena hɔ na sɛ wɔn mu baako sɛe biribiara a ɛyɛ wo dea a, ntua ka pɛpɛɛpɛ ɛfiri sɛ ata ‘Panin yɛɛ saa na ɔkɔeɛ na ɔwuiɛ.’

Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
