Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ABOSOM, LESSER-GODS, CAME kɔ TRIBE no so

Bere bi na ɔbɔmmɔfo bi a ɔte hɔ dada ne ne yere te abɔmmɔfo nsraban mu. na wɔyɛ ahiafoɔ na wɔtenaa hɔ a na wɔreko ama wɔn nkwa. Ná ɔbɔmmɔfo no sɔre kɔ kwae no mu kwa. na ɔbaa fie na ɔtoo mpataa wɔ asubɔnten no mu na ɔfaa mpataa ne asuogya nohoa na ɔde wɔn brɛɛ ne yere na afei na wɔwɔ hɔ na ne yere bisabisaa no nsɛm na ɔkaa sɛ sɛ mokum nam a mode ma no anaasɛ motɔn ma no didi ɛfiri sɛ ɛfiri sɛ ɛnnɛ deɛ mokɔ wuram na momfa nam biara mmra na mɛsane akɔ m'akuraa ase Abɔmmɔfo no fii adi kɔɔ kwae no mu. Ɔnantee na ɔnantee na wanhu aboa biara. Ɔkae sɛ, “Oo, mahu asiane.” Afei, berɛ a na ɔrehwɛ no, ɔhunuu Duyker bi sɛ ɔnam akyirikyiri. ɔkaa sɛ ɛdeɛn na wobɛyɛ sɛ Duyker na mɛkum woɔ Ɔbɔmmɔfo no dii akyi, Kyiri! kyiri! kyiri! (na ne nan nnyigyei). Duykerfo no guan kɔɔ dua bi ntini a ɛyɛ nam no ntam. Ɔno (Dɔyker no) kaa sɛ, “Nana Tano, meguan ba mo nkyɛn sɛ wɔmmɔ me ho ban, na ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no agyaw me.” na abɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ hwɛ mennim deɛ wɔfrɛ no atano yi na fa wo kɔ na kɔ na kɔma me yere Tano gyinaa hɔ, na ɔbuee kasa mu kaa sɛ, "Hunter, firi Duyker kɔ fie, ɛfiri sɛ mfasoɔ pii bɛba so ama wo wɔ me mu." Abɔmmɔfo no gyinaa hɔ, na ɔwosoo biribiri. Ɔfaa no (Tano) de no too ne tiri so. Bere a ɔde no rekɔ no, ohyiaa Owu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔbɔfoɔ, ɛhe na worefa me *kyeame* (ɔkasamafoɔ)?" ɔbɔfoɔ no bua see no sɛ awurade m'aniwa yɛ kɔkɔɔ na merehwehwɛ nam akɔma me yere na mekɔhunuu duyker no na ɔkaa sɛ wadwane akɔbɔ saa asɛm yi ho ban na mekaa sɛ mennim ho hwee Saa asɛm yi (saa bere no) kae sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mede kɔ fie, efisɛ na mfaso pii wɔ so." Owuo bisabisaa Tano nsɛm, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Worekɔ fie hɔ na wonim sɛ sɛ obi yare a, mefa me hammock, na meba ne nkyɛn a, ɛnsɛ sɛ obi de ne ho gye mu, enti sɛ wokɔ fie a, deɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meka kyerɛ wo ne sɛ yei ne sɛ: sɛ obi yare na meremfa no nkɔ a, mɛbɔ wo amanneɛ, na woanya biribi de atua me ka, na saa akatua no deɛ mogya nko ara na mepɛ, na ebia wowɔ nam." Onyankopɔn ketewa no ka kyerɛɛ owu sɛ, "Grandsire, mate, awurade." Abɔmmɔfo no faa Tano, na ɔne no baa fie. Bere a Tano gyinaa hɔ no, ɔtoo dwom bi, na wɔde wɔn ho bɔɔ nnwontofo kuw no ho. Ade kyee no, nneɛma bɛdaa adi, na obi yaree no, wɔde no kɔɔ Tano. Ɔno (Tano) kaa sɛ, "Wobɛwuo." Ná ɛyɛ nokware sɛ owui. (Ɔfoforɔ) na ɔkaa sɛ, "Worenwuo nti momfa yei ne ɛno mmra." Bere a obi faa saa nneɛma yi no, ɔno (nyame ketewa no) kaa sɛ, "Mekɔsrɛ nkwa ama wo." Na ɛsiane sɛ wode (saa afɔrebɔ yi) maa Tano nti, nokorɛ bi mu na wo ho tɔɔ wo. Ɛno na yɛhunu sɛ anyame nketewa no yɛ papa; enti nnipa kɔ hɔ kɔ Tano kɔgye ne mma no bi firi ne nkyɛn.

Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
