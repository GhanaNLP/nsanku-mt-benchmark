SƐNEA ƐBAA ƐNO HO NSƐM HO NA ƐWƆ TRIBE no mu

WƆKA sɛ Kwaku a ɔyɛ ɔsɛefoɔ no wɔ hɔ na ɔpra nimdeɛ nyinaa boaboaa ano wɔ baabi baako na ɔde guu ɛferɛ mu ɛnna ɔpaee mu kaa sɛ ɔbɛforo dua na ɔkɔ sɛn soɔ na nyansa a ɛwɔ asaase soɔ nyinaa awie

ɛnna ɔfaa soɔ de kɔeɛ na ɔde ne nsa kɔduruu dua a ɔrekɔ sɛn soɔ no ase na ɔfaa hama bi kyekyeree ɛferɛ no ho na ɔde sɛn n'anim na ɔde ne ho sii hɔ sɛ ɔreforo dua no Ɔforoo kɔɔ soro, na ɔforoo soro; kwa. Ɔsane bɔɔ mmɔden bio, na ɔsan yɛɛ sɛ ɔbɛforo, na ɔforoo, na ɔforoo; kwa.

Afei, ne ba Ntikuma a na ɔgyina hɔ no kaa sɛ, "O, w'aniwa awuwu ɛsiane aniwuo nti), anka ɛnyɛ papa sɛ wodanee ɛferɛ no na wode guu w'akyi, ɛnneɛ akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho sɛ anka wobɛtumi aforo." Ɔno (Spider no) kae sɛ, "Tew so, wo ne wo nsɛm a woaka dedaw no." Afei ɔdanee ne ho foroo bio sɛnea na ɛte kan no, nanso ankyɛ koraa. ɛnna ɔsusuu ho kyɛreeɛ na awieeɛ koraa no ɔfaa ɛferɛ no de too n'akyi Afei ɔde ne ho sii hɔ sɛ ɔbɛforo, na ɔtraa so ntɛmntɛm, Kra! kra! kra! (na ɛyɛ nnyigyei a ɛwɔ ne foro mu); ɛhɔ na ɔkɔ. Oduu baabi a baa dwumadibea ahorow no fii ase trɛwee fii asubɔnten no mu, na ɔkae (kyerɛɛ ne ho), "Me, Kwaku Ananse, onyame kumaa, Atio! Ebia me nso mawu, me ba a ɔyɛ ketewa, ketewaa saa—na mewɔ hɔ, meboaboaa nyansa nyinaa ano (enti misusuwii sɛ) wɔ beae biako, nanso ebinom kɔɔ so kae a manhu mpo, na mihui! me ba, akokoaa a ɔda so ara rehwehwɛ no, ada no adi akyerɛ me." Afei ɔfaa saa ɛferɛ no, na na ɛnne bi rehyia, tintini! na ɔtoo no twenee na ɛnne bi petepetee ɛserɛ so Saa kwan no so na obiara nyaa nyansa; na obiara a wankɔ hɔ ntɛm (sɛ mɛfa bi) yɛ ɔkwasea.

Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
