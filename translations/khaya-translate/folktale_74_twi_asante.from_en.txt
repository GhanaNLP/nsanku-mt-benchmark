Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Kwamena, NKYERƐKYERƐMU A ƐYƐ ANT-HILL, ƐNO kɔɔ so wɔ NKYERƐKYERƐMU ROOTS Ahorow no mu

WƆKA sɛ bere bi na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna bi babea wɔ hɔ, na ɛmfa ho onii ko a wɔde maa no wɔ aware mu no, ɔbɛka sɛ, na ɔmpɛ no. Nanso (awiei koraa no), Ahonhommɔne Hene, Adu Gyanfi biako, ba bɛwaree saa Ɔhemmaa Ɛna bi ba no. Ɔhemmaa ɛna no ba ka kyerɛɛ ne kunu sɛ me ne wo bɛkɔ wo kuro mu Ɔbarima no buae sɛ, "Ma menkɔ (kan), na sɛ Adae hwe ase Kwasida bi a, Memeneda a edi hɔ no mɛba abɛfa wo." Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "Oo dabi, me ne wo bɛbom akɔ." ɛnna wɔfirii hɔ kɔeɛ na wɔrekɔ no wɔhunuu kuro kɛseɛ bi a dadeɛ pono wɔ soɔ no ano na dadeɛ atwa kuro no nyinaa ho ahyia na afei kuro yi mu na sɛ mogya a ɛnni mogya mu no anwu na mogyinaa ɛpono no akyi na mosu frɛɛ sɛ obiara mmua mo Afei ɛdefa Ɔhemmaa Maame ba ho no, na ne busuani biara nwui da. Ɔkasa ne ne benkum so nsɛm (i.e. me werɛ fii sɛ mɛka saa) berɛ a Adu Gyanfi duruu dadeɛ pono no ano no, ɔkɔɔ ne fie; wankasa bio ne Ɔhemmaa Maame babea no. na ɔhemmaa maame babaa ne ne nkoa abeawa Kra kɔɔ so traa hɔ na wɔgyinaa dadeɛ pono no akyi na wɔannya kwan ankɔ fie Na Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no maa ne nne so (na ɔtoo dwom):

"Adodowa, bue ma me, ɛyɛ me na maba, Adodwa Kwasi Kampire, a wɔabue ama me, Adodwa, a wɔabue ama me o."

Nanso Adodwa ammue ankyerɛ no. Ɔno nso, na ontumi nhu ɔkwan a ɔbɛfa so asan akɔ n'ankasa kuro mu. Afei ɔne Kra bɔɔ bɔɔl abien. na ɔhemmaa abɔfra no faa kɔbere mfrafraeɛ na ɔtenaa so na ɔfaa foforɔ na ɔkataa ne tiri so na Kra tenaa ne nkyɛn Ɛno nti, sɛ wokɔ baabi a nantwinini no ntini (gyina) a, ɛhɔ na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba ne ne mpena-akoa Kra; wɔadan ntɛtea a wɔfrɛ no "Kwamena" a ɛsɔre tiafi wɔ tier so.

Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
