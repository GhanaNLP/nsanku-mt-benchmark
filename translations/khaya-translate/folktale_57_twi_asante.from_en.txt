MMOFRA-KWASI-GYINAMOA

Ɛhɔ na na abɔfra bi ne Kwasi Gyinamoa, na na ɔwɔ nuabeanom baawɔtwe, na wɔn mu baako mpo nni ɔhokafoɔ, enti wɔfirii adi kaa sɛ wɔrekɔ baabi akɔhwehwɛ okununom. abɔfra-kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ me ne wo na yɛbɛkɔ na wɔbua see no sɛ yɛne wo nkɔ Wɔn mu panyin no kae sɛ, “Ma ɔne yɛn nkɔ, na sɛ yɛduru kwan no ho yie, na nsɔeɛ bi tumi tutu yɛn a, ɔbɛtumi atwe ama yɛn.” Wde wn sii h, na wne wn kɔeε. Wɔduruu asubɔnten pɔtee bi ho, na ne fã bi sen te sɛ dɔte fitaa; na ne fã bi sen (te sɛ) dɔte kɔkɔɔ; na ne fã bi sen fango tuntum (te sɛ). Wɔteɛɛm sɛ, "Eh, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ na yɛatumi atwa saa asuten yi?" Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa ne (magic) nantwi-dua-nwansena, bɔɔ mu (nsuo no), na ɔmaa nsuo no mu kyekyɛɛ mu mmienu, na wɔfaa mu kɔeɛ. Wbaa kuro bi mu; na εyε kuro kεseε pa ara. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, “Kurow no yɛ dinn (i.e. kommyɛ gye sɛ ɛyɛ sikada a wɔde bɔ nkurɔfoɔ ho ban), so obiara nni mu?” Afei anomaa bi kasae na ɔkae sɛ, “Obi wɔ fie hɔ a, kɔ.” na wɔkɔtoaa so na aberewa bi wɔ hɔ na ne ti wɔ ne nan so na ɔhwehwɛɛ tumi wɔ mu Ɔhunuu Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, ɔmaa ne ti so na ɔde sii ananmu; ɔkaa sɛ, "Me nananom, adɛn nti na obiara nni ha a woaba ha yi?" Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buaa sɛ, "Me nuabeanom yɛ nnwɔtwe, na wɔnnyaa okununom a wɔne wɔn bɛware, na wɔkaa sɛ wɔbɛba abɛhwehwɛ okununom a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔware wɔn." na ɔbaa nana nana kaa sɛ o awurade nyankopɔn asɛm nie ɛfiri sɛ me nso mewɔ mma baawɔtwe Ɔbea panyin nanabea somaa ne banana sɛ ɔmmɛfrɛ ne mma. na wɔbaeɛ no ɔbaako yi kaa sɛ me kunu nie na ɔbaako kaa sɛ me kunu nie Bere a wɔn nyinaa awie a wɔde wɔn ho abɔ mu no, ɔbea panyin no kɔe na onyaa afɛfɛde bi a wɔfrɛ no _dokum_, na ɔkyekyeree no wɔ ne mma kɔn ho. Afei wɔn mu biara ne ne yere kɔdae. na abɔfra-kwasi ghana nso kɔgyee n'ahome ɔbaa a na wabɔ akora no anadwo no ɔbaa akɔkoraa no sɔɔ ne se yɛɛ kɔkɔɔ na ɔfirii adi kɔkumm mmabaa no kaa wɔn ho Abofra-Kawesi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, “E! e! e! Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ, “Ɛyɛ dɛn?” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buaa no sɛ, "Sɛ mewɔ m'akuraa ase na sɛ mekɔgye m'ahome nanso me nna ntumi nna a, ɛna no twitwa bayerɛ asinasini bi de gu ogya mu, na ɔfa korafa a wɔatwa no tiaa na ɔkɔpetepete nsuo mu ma menom." Ɔbaa nanabea a ne mfe akɔ anim sii kwan so kɔɔ asuten no mu, na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kɔe na ɔde afɛfɛde a wɔfrɛ no _dokum_ no fii ɔbea panyin no mma kɔn mu, na ɔfaa wɔn sɛn ne nuabeanom. Aberewa posoposo no sane firii nsuo no mu; ɔkaa sɛ, “Mebɔɔ mmɔden sɛ mɛpete nsuo no mu, nanso mantumi ampete.” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, “Kɔ na kɔfa atɛkyɛ, na fa nhaban na fa gu (ɔhaw no) mu, na fa petepete.” na aberewa no kɔe na ɔkɔyɛɛ saa na ɔde nsuo petepetee mu na ne nyinaa ye Ɔde bae na ɔyii bayerɛ no firii ogya no mu na ɔde maa abɔfra-kwasi-Gyinaberɛ sɛ ɔnni na ɔno nso nom nsuo no bi. Afei ɔhwee ase dae. Ɔbea panyin ne nana sɔree; ɔkae sɛ, “Ɛyɛ me.” Ɔsɔɔ ne sẽ, (kosii sɛ ɛhyerɛn) kɔkɔɔ, na ɔne wɔn fii adi kɔhyɛn dan no mu; ɔtee nka sɛ ɔde ne nsa atwa mmofra no kɔn ho ahyia, na nea ɛwɔ ne kɔn so no _dokum_ charm no anni atoro no, ɔde ne sẽ hyewee. Saa kwan yi so na okum mmɔfra baawɔtwe no. na ɔnnim sɛ ɛyɛ n'ankasa mma a ɔkumm wɔn Berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɛyɛɛ dɛn ara a, na ɔbaa ne nana a ɔdii kan kɔɔ mfuo no mu no asi wɔn ani so sɛ ɔrekɔ afuom. na abɔfra-kwasi ginaia nso kɔe na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nua mmaa no na ɔmaa wɔdwane firii hɔ Ɔbaa nanabarima a ne mfe akɔ anim sane firii mfuo no mu baeɛ. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, “Ɔpanyin ne ɔbea nana e! Ɔbea panyin-nanabea e! sɛ wonyɛ ɔkwasea a anka wobɛkum w'ankasa wo mma Me deɛ, me ne me nuabeanom rekɔ.” Ɔbaa nanabea a ne mfe akɔ anim kaa sɛ, “Ɛnyɛ biribiara na ɛbɛfiri mu aba, ɔno deɛ ɔnim (sɛdeɛ yɛne wo bɛdi no) wɔ akyirikyiri.” ɔfirii hɔ kɔe na ɔbaa nana nso danee ne ho ababaa a ne ho yɛ fɛ na ɔkuraa ɛferɛ na ɔfirii adi kɔɔ mmɔfra-kwasi-gyegyentwie kuro mu Ɔkaa sɛ, “Onipa a ɔbɛtumi agye agyan de atutu ɛferɛ yi mu no, mɛware no.” Nnipa a na wɔwɔ akuraa no ase nyinaa (a wɔde wɔn kɔɔ hɔ) wuraa mu, nanso wɔantumi. Mmofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa wuwui. Ɔkae sɛ, "Maware wo." Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Me deɛ merenware wo." Enti ne wɔfa waree no. ɔfirii hɔ kɔdaa ne nkyɛn nanso ɔsunsuanee n'ani mmienu mu na ɔne wɔn firii adi Afei abeawa kumaa no kae sɛ, "Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa wɔ hɔ! mayi wo wɔfa aniwa mmienu na me ne wɔn rekɔ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, "Fa wɔn na firi adi, ɛfiri sɛ deɛ ɔnim (sɛdeɛ ɔne wo bɛdi) no wɔ akyirikyiri." Adeɛ kyee anɔpa no, wɔhunuu nneɛma no, wɔkaa sɛ, "Akwadaa, kɔ na sane bra wo wɔfa nkyɛn. Ɔkae sɛ, "Merenkɔ." na wɔhyɛɛ no na wɔhyɛɛ no na awieeɛ koraa no ɔfirii adi Afei, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nim sɛ ɔbea panyin ne ne nana nanabea yɛ _encente_, enti ɔno nso, onyaa calabash na ɔbɔɔ no nsam wɔ ne yafunu so, na ɔkyekyeree ne ntama wɔ so, na ofii adi kɔɔ Nana-nanana-nana akuraa ase. Bere a oduu kurow no awiei no, osui. Ɔbea panyin-nana kae sɛ, "Dɛn ne asɛm no?" na ɔkaa sɛ me ne me kunu reba na ɔduruu bepɔ no atifi no ɔhwee fam na ɛno maa n'ani mmienu firii adi hwee fam Ɔbea panyin-nana kae sɛ, "Ɛte saa koraa, benkum so ni na ɛsan woo rascal's, Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa's, ne papa nua barima, ne ha nso yɛ n'ani nifa. Sɛ wokɔ (baabi a wo kunu a n'ani afura gyina) a, ɛsɛ sɛ woka sɛ, 'M'ani huruw kɔ soro, m'ani so da hɔ.' Ankyɛ koraa na abofra-kwasi-Gyinamoa nsa kaa wɔn, na ɔkae sɛ, "Wo, wo nana a woyɛ ɔbea panyin, yeinom ne me papa nua barima ani a woteɛteɛɛ mu, na me ne wɔn rekɔ." Ɔbaa panyin-nana kae sɛ, "Ɛno mfa ho, nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi) no wɔ akyirikyiri." na mmɔfra a wɔfrɛ wɔn keila ne ginaia baa fie na ɔfaa aniwa sii wɔn ananmu wɔ ne wɔfa nkyɛnkyɛn Ɔkae sɛ, "M'ani so pagyapagya, m'ani so da hɔ," na ne papa nua barima aniwa buee yiye. Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma daa adi no, mmofra no nyinaa (wɔ akuraa no ase) kɔɔ so guaree wɔ asuten no mu. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Merenware." Wɔkae sɛ, "Ebia wowɔ nkwaboaa dua?" na wɔkaa saa no wɔsunsuanee ne mpokua mu na ɔdwaree Ankyɛ koraa na ohuruw kɔɔ nsu no mu, sen sɛnea nana panyin no gyee no no, na ɛyɛ abien! na ɔkaa sɛ ɔbabarima a ɔyɛ kalebni ginaia mma me na manya woɔ na ɔne no kɔɔ n'akuraa ase ɛnna ɔbaa nana nana no kɔeɛ na ɔnyaa kɛntɛn na ɔkataa no so na ɔnyaa mmɛ na ɔde wɔn guu ogya mu Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mefiri adi rekɔ onion abusuakuo no mu, merekɔ nnuhuam abusuakuo no mu, na afei mɛba mabɛkum Mmofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa." Ɔfirii hɔ, na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa boaboaa abotan ano na ɔfirii aseɛ bubuu wɔn, pupru! pupru! Ɔbea panyin-nana nanabea kae sɛ, "Dɛn na worewe?" Ɔkae sɛ, "Tiger-nuts." Ɔkae sɛ, "Ma me bi nwe." Obuae sɛ, "Da me adi." Ɔkae sɛ, "Wobɛkɔ." Obuae sɛ, "Dabi, merenkɔ." Ɔdaa no adi, na ɔdaa ne ho adi; na ɔbea panyin no banana no rebɔ mmɛ no. Ɔbaa-Kwasi-Ginamoa faa nnuane a wɔde ngo ayɛ no na ɔbɔɔ ɔbaa nana nana nanabea na ɔkumm no. Afei ɔyii ne nana nana nana no honam, na ɔhyɛɛ ne ho atadeɛ. Ɔpanyin ne nanabea san bae; ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na mo nyinaa ho ntew te sɛ eyi?" Obuae sɛ, "Makum Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa; Mede no ato ogya no mu." Na saa asɛm yi yɛ dɛ ma ɔbea-nana, na ɔsaw na ɔmaa ne nne so (na ɔtoo dwom): "Abofra, woakum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, Da bi wobɛyɛ obi."

Ɔnoaa aduane no wieeɛ no, wɔtenaa hɔ didii. Ɔbea panyin-nana de de dede a ano yɛ den petepetee nkwan no mu (de guu n'anom), nwene! fweo! Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Worepete nkwan (wura w'anom) a wɔde wo nana ti na ɛyɛeɛ." Ɔbaa nanabea a ne mfe akɔ anim kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na woka?" Obuae sɛ, "Na mereka sɛ nkyene biara nni soup-stew yi mu." Ɔbea panyin-nana kae sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔfa bi." ɛnna ɔkɔeɛ na ɔnyaa bi. Wɔdii aduane wieeɛ no, na ɔbaa panyin ne nana kaa sɛ, "Afei deɛ makum abɔfra-kwasi-Gyinamoa, bra ha, me nana." Ɔbaa hɔ no, ɔfaa sika kɔkɔɔ nkotokuo na ɔsiesiee ne ho yie. Afei, na ne nana panyin de n'ani kyerɛɛ n'anim kakra. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ah! Abofra yi anim ma mekae mmofra-kwasi-Gyinamoa deɛ." Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Nana, adɛn nti na wonim sɛ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa ba bɛkunkum m'abusuafoɔ awotwe no nyinaa (woka saa), ɛfiri sɛ woma me werɛ how." Ɔbea panyin-nana kae sɛ, "W'ano." Afei, abofra no de ne ho too fie hɔ. Nanabea no yii fii hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ma menkɔtow agu midden-heap so." Ɛno nti na mmɔfra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa de kɔeɛ. na ɔduruu middle-head no ɔyii sika kɔkɔɔ ademudeɛ a ɔbaa panin no de asiesie no no nyinaa na ɔkyekyeree wɔn ɛnna ɔkaa sɛ ɛhɔ na ɔbaa panyin ne nana ho wɔ hɔ a ɔyɛ ɔbaa panyin ne nana me deɛ meyɛ ɔno ne ɔbaa-kwasi-Gyinamoa seesei ne mprenu so na maba mabɛkum wo nanabea na sɛ ɛnyɛ me a mayi wo sika kɔkɔɔ agudeɛ no a." Ɔbea panyin-nanabea buae sɛ, "Mekaa sɛ w'anim te sɛ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa de; afei baabiara a wobɛkɔ no, me ne wo kɔ." Afei wɔn baanu nyinaa dwanee, kiri! Kiri! Kiri! na mmɔfra a wɔfrɛ wɔn keila ne ginaia kɔduruu n'akuraa ase na ɔde sika kɔkɔɔ nkotokuo no maa ne mogya mu abusudeɛ na ɔkɔɔ ɔkwan no so na ɔde ne ho siee n'afa na nana nso baa hɔ Ɛno nti, sɛ wonam ɔkwan bi so a, wobɛte nnyigyei a ano yɛ den, “pe”. Ɛno ne ɔbaa a ne nana ne ɔbarima a ɔredi Kwasi-Gyinamoa akyi.

Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
