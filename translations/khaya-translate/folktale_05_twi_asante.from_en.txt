SƐNEA ƐBAA SAA NKWASƐM A ƐFA AKWANKYERƐ HO NKYERƐKYERƐMU A ƐWƆ NKWASƐM MU

Ɛhɔ na abɛtwafoɔ ne Kɔtɔkorɔ bi tenaa ase, na Crow kaa sɛ, “Momma yɛnkɔ nkɔtwa sankuo na yɛmmɔ so agorɔ.” na wɔfirii adi kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu na wɔhunuu sirikyie dua bi Bere a ahuhude no huu sirikyi dua no ara pɛ na ɔkae sɛ, “Oyi yɛ me dea, ɛfiri sɛ me deɛ meyɛ ɔpanin enti mɛtwa kɛseɛ yi.” Crowfo no kae sɛ, “Mate.” Afei nso, berɛ a na ɔrehwɛ no no, ɔhunuu “Cedar” dua bi a ɛgyina hɔ no, ɔno nso kɔeɛ na ɔtwaa mu. Wɔn baanu nyinaa yɛɛ tweneduro. Bere a Vulture no bɔɔ ne sanku no ɛte sɛɛ ara pɛ:

Futuru! Futuru! Manya (pane) ntɛm ara!

Nkonimdi Kwakye! Manya (pane) ntɛm ara!

Bere a Crowfo no boroo no no, na ɛte sɛɛ:

Crow, Kwa! Kwa!

Crow, Kwa! Kwa!

Wɔkɔɔ so dii agoru, na Wim-nyame no tee, na ɔsomaa Hawk (sɛ ɔmmɛfrɛ wɔn); na Hawk bae, (na ɔkamfoo wɔn) "Ago!" na wɔbuaa sɛ "Ame!" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Onyankopɔn a ɔwɔ wim no aka sɛ wate asaw-dwom bi wɔ ha ma enti ɛsɛ sɛ wode ba na wobɔ no ma ɔte." Abɔde no fii adi kɔɔ Wim-nyame no anim; na wɔbɔɔ nnwom, na wɔbɔɔ nnwom, na Wim-nyame no kae sɛ, "Mate sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ; nnwom abien no yɛ dɛ, enti mede biribi kyerɛ wo (biara)." Afei Wim-nyame no kɔfaa nnaka mmienu de sii hɔ, na baako akyi yɛ fɛ yie pa ara sɛ yɛbɛhunu; efĩ a ɛbɛhyɛ emu ma; baako nso yɛ deɛ ɛyɛ abofono koraa; sika kɔkɔɔ kɔsi saa mframa a ɛwɔ mu yi so. Wim-nyame no kasae, (ka sɛ), "Mo mu biara mommra mmɛfa." Vulture no kaa sɛ, "Meyɛ ɔpanin enti ma menkɔ na menkɔdi kan." Bere a ɔkɔe no, ɔpaw nea ɛyɛ fɛ yiye, bere a Crow no nso faa nea ɛyɛ abofono koraa no. na wɔmaa wɔn so na wɔfirii hɔ kɔɔ wɔn akuraa ase Bere a wɔkɔe sɛ wɔrekɔbue wɔn ano no, na ɔhwɛ ne ntade a ɛho nte a ɛho ntew no mu. Wɔ Crow no mu no, bere a ɔhwɛɛ mu no, sika kɔkɔɔ hyerɛn kɔkɔɔ te sɛ biribiara. Ɛno akyi ankyɛ koraa na ɔbɔfoɔ bi baeɛ na ɔka kyerɛɛ Vulture (ka sɛ), "Wɔsoma me sɛ memmɛka sɛ w'ase awu." Ɔno (Vulture no) kaa sɛ, "Kɔbere, ma me sika kɔkɔɔ paanoo baako a mede bɛkyekyere me nsa a ebia mɛkɔ ayie no ase." Ɔde ne nsa kyekyeree no, na ofii adi kɔɔ ayie no ase. Bere a oduu hɔ no, ɔkae sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ ɔkɔ brɛ ne ho ase, nanso bere a na ɔrebɛsɔre no, paanoo no hwee ase, na ɛyerae. Ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, Crow no agyapade ayera, na mɛyɛ dɛn atumi asan aba fie?" Enti ohuruw kɔɔ efĩ no mu hwehwɛɛ paanoo no mu. Ɛno nti na wobɛhunu Vaeba, berɛ biara a ɔrehwehwɛ, ɔrehwehwɛ Crow no paanoo no, ɔde reba ne nkyɛn.

Ɛno nti na mpanimfoɔ se, "Awerɛhoɔ nyɛ papa."

Eyi, m'asɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom betumi afa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no, wubetumi akamfo me (wɔ ho asɛm a wɔka kyerɛ no ho).
