SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ NANSANA HO NSƐM, NKYERƐKYERƐMU, KRAMAN, DOG, Ɔhyɛ IT, KAM ho nkɔm! KAM!

Ná CERSTIN abɔmmɔfo bi wɔ akraman baanan; na wɔfrɛ biako Sniff-sniff; na wɔfrɛ foforo Lick-lick; na wɔfrɛ foforo Tie-in-knots; na wɔfrɛ foforo nso Gulp-down. Ná ɔtaa de ne akraman hwehwɛ wɔn. Bere biara a ɔkɔɔ wuram no, ɔne wɔn boom kɔe. Da bi ɔkɔɔ wuram kɔhunuu kola-dua bi; na nnuaba a wɔde abɔ akuwakuw te sɛɛ akata so. Afei ɔsan kɔɔ fie na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere sɛ, "Kola-dua bi a mahunu no, wɔde nnuaba a wɔde abɔ akuwakuw te sɛɛ akata so." Ne yere se, "Kɔ na fa wɔn, ɛfiri sɛ monim sɛ yɛnni kola-nut baako mpo a yɛbɛwe na yɛntɔn bi." Abɔmmɔfoɔ no sii kwan no so, nanso ɔgyaa akraman no kwan wɔ fie, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a kola-dua no wɔ. Afei ɔforoo, na ɔfaa kola-nut no bi. Afei ɔtee fii baabi a ɛwɔ akyirikyiri sɛ, "Twa! yɛ! kukuru! kukuru! hu! pi!" Sasabonsam bi (gyinaa n'anim) wɔ n'anim. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Hena na wabɔ me kola-nut?" Abɔmmɔfo no kae sɛ, "Me." Sasabonsam kaa sɛ ntɛm ara mɛku wo ɛfiri sɛ adeɛ a woayɛ yi nti ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ sɛ wobɛku me a ma menkɔfrɛ me kraman kane ɔkaa sɛ momfrɛ nanso sɛ moabɔ mmɔden mprɛnsa sɛ mobɛfrɛ wɔn na wɔmmra a ɛnneɛ mɛku mo Afei ɔbɔmmɔfo no maa soro (ne nne) :

"Sniff-sniff, (ɔba na) hyia me, Lick-lick, (ɔba na) hyia me, Tie-in-knots, (ɔba na) hyia me, Gulp-down, (ɔba na) hyia me."

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ sɛ wɔbɛba! na n'ano nnyinaa n'anim na nkraman no wɔ n'anim Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Sniff-sniff, Sasabonsam bi nie, ɔde ne nsa bɛka no." Na Sniff-niff nso huu no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Lick-lick, tare no." Ɔkae sɛ, "Wɔn a wonnim akenkan ne akyerɛw, kyekyere no wɔ nhama mu." Tie-in-knots de nhama kyekyeree ne menewam; na Sassabonsam no hwee ase wɔ hɔ, kum! Afei Gulp-down de no kɔe na wɔde no too fam. na abɔfoɔ ne nkraman no boaboaa kola nnuane no ano na wɔde wɔn baa fie na abɔmmɔfoɔ no sane bae na ɔbaa hɔ bɛnoaa aduane a ɛyɛ asaase so nkwan ahyɛ mu ma na ɔde too hɔ maa nkraman no ɔpetepetee nsuo mu de guu hɔ na ɔfirii hɔ kɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn kɔdiidii Bere a odii aduan wiei no, ofii adi kɔɔ abɔnten so na ɔtraa gideda (dua) ase. Afei ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no akoa, Fly, kɔe na ɔkɔhunuu aduane no (a wɔasiesie ama akraman), na ɔmaa so de sii mpomma mu. na nkraman no firi mmorɔno so baeɛ na ɛkɔm de wɔn Lick-lick see wɔn a aka no sɛ, "Woahunu deɛ yɛayɛ ama yɛn wura yi nanso ɛde besi nnɛ no ɔmfaa aduane biara mmaa yɛn mpo sɛ yɛnni, enti momma yɛnkɔ nkɔkum no." Wɔbuae sɛ, "Wonni atoro, wonni atoro." Lick-lick fii adi kɔɔ abɔnten so na ɔtaree abɔmmɔfo no amu, na ɔne abɔmmɔfo no dii agoru; na Sniff-sniff nso bae; na ɔkaa ɔbɔmmɔfo no amu ho asɛm; na ɔno nso kɔe. Afei ankyɛ koraa na wɔn a wɔnnim no baeɛ bɛkyekyeree ne menewa mu, sene Gulp-down, nso, ɔbaa hɔ bɛbɔɔ no hwee fam. Afei akraman no sane baa fie hɔ; na wɔrebɛhwɛ pato no mu, na wɔhunuu nkwan a ɛtɔ gu fam wɔ hɔ. na wɔkaa sɛ ɛyɛ nokorɛ sɛ ebia ɔbɔfoɔ no gyaa yɛn aduane na woadi? Fly kae sɛ, "Minhuu no wɔ baabiara." Sniff-sniff, bere a ɔmaa n'ani so no, ohuu aduan a wɔde asi hɔ (a ɛwɔ mpomma mu) no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, papa! yɛakum yɛn awurade kwa hwee na ampa ara aduane a ɔde ato hɔ ama yɛn no wɔ hɔ na ɛnyɛ baako anaa mmienu na ɛyɛ ɔbaa a ɔfaeɛ na ɔde sii hɔ Fly no guane fii hɔ kɔe. ɛno nti na sɛ ɔkraman te hɔ na ɔkraman kɔtena ne soɔ no ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkyere no
