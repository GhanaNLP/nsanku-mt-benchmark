Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA (SALUTATIONs) "KURONTO" NE "BURU" CAME A WƆBƐYƐ BƆNE

Ɛhɔ na na ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ bi (a wɔfrɛ no Kwaku, ne ne yere (a wɔfrɛ no Buruwa) ne ne yere berɛ a na wɔdi hia no, wɔsii kwan sɛ wɔrekɔtena baabi foforɔ. Kwaku a ɔyɛ ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no firii adi kɔɔ wuram na ɔkumm eku bonsu bi nanso ɔde sane baa fie no ɔkaa sɛ, "Eyi nyɛ papa (ma aduane." ɛnna ɔkɔeɛ na ɔtɔneeɛ Bere biara a obekum biribi no, na ɛte saa. nanso ne yere no anhunu sika no da na ɔbisaa no sɛ, "Woamfa sika ammrɛ wo na woamfa nam amma, na ɛdeɛn ne nyinaa kyerɛ?" Obuae sɛ, "Sɛ mekɔ a, na nkurɔfoɔ ntɔ (nam no), na ɛbɛyɛ porɔwee, meto gu." da bi ɔkumm biribi na ɔde sii ɔkwan no so na ne yere dii n'akyi hwɛɛ no Afei ɔhunuu sɛ ɔtɔn saa bonsu no, ɔfaa sika no, na ɔtɔɔ nsafufuo bi (a wɔfrɛ no Dɔnto) nomeeɛ. Afei ne yere san kɔɔ fie. ɛno akyi bere tiaa bi no, Kwaku a ɔyɛ ɔbɔmmɔfo no nso bae. Bere a oduu fie hɔ ara pɛ na ɔkae sɛ, "Anɔpa pa, Buruwa." Obuae sɛ, "Yiw, Bonsu ma nsafufuo kukuo." Ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wubua me saa?" Obuae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuae a ɛfata ma nea ɛwɔ wo tirim." Ɔbɔmmɔfo no anka hwee. Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma daa adi no, ɔkɔɔ afuw no mu kɔsan bae; ɔkae sɛ, "Eye anwummere." Obuae sɛ, "Ya Buru." Buruwa kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wubua (me nkyia) wɔ saa kwan yi so?" Abɔmmɔfoɔ no bua se, "Ɛyɛ mmuaeɛ a ɛfata ma deɛ ɛwɔ wo tirim." Saa kwan no so na ɛbɛyɛɛ amanne wɔ abusuakuw no mu sɛ wɔde nkyia "Ya Kuronto" ne "Ya Buru" bedi dwuma. na wɔde wɔn firi ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ a wɔfrɛ no isak ne ne yere buruba Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
