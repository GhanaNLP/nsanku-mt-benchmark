Nsɛmsɛm a Efi Akenkanfo Hɔ 31

BIBLE AFOFORSƐM

Ahayɛ Ntonton a nna ɔwɔ hɔ wɔ ber a wɔrekyerɛw nsɛm no mu

Ebia Bible akenkanfo bɛkae dɛ na Yesu akyidzifo no bi yɛ apofofo. (Mart 1:16-20) Sɛ wɔreyi nam wɔ Galilea Po no do a, wɔdze asaw dzi dwuma.

Wɔdze kɔkɔbɔ na ɔyɛɛ ndzɛmba anaa nwera ho haban dze bɔɔ mose. Mose no kɛse gyina nam a wɔpɛ dɛ wɔkyer no do. Mpɛn pii no, na asaw kumaa bi na odzi kan anaa ɔbo mu duru dze tɔ famu, ber a na ndzɛw akɛse wɔ ndzɛmba a ɔwɔ famu na dua anaa nkyɛnsee tsentsen wɔ atifi.

Nna asaw a woyii no bo yɛ dzen. Ɔbo a ɔwɔ po no ho no botum asɛɛ hɔn anaa apataa kɛse bi a ɔwɔ hɔ no mu duru bɛsɛɛ hɔn. (Lakke 5:6) Bible ka James na John ho asɛm dɛ, wɔdze paane na nwera tsenae retwetwe hɔn atsenae. (Mat. Afarfo 4:21) Ebia wobesiesie dɛm nsiesie yi wɔ ber biara a wɔdze bedzi dwuma ekyir. Luka so kyerɛkyerɛ apofofo a wɔhohor hɔn asaw mu dɛ wobeyi nsunsuando anaa efi. Ɔno ekyir no, na apofofo no sensen hɔn asaw ma ɔwor—ɔyɛ dɛm a ɔbɔboa ma wɔaporɔw.—Ezek. 47:10

Yesu too nsa frɛɛ mbanyin a wɔtaa dze asaw dzi dwuma no dɛ wɔmbɛyɛ “nyimpa a woyi hɔn”

Wɔnntɔn nhoma yi. Wɔdze ma dɛ wiadze nyinara Bible nkyerɛkyerɛ edwuma a wofi ɔpɛ mu dze ntoboa boa. Sɛ epɛ dɛ iyi ntoboa a, yɛsrɛ wo, kɔ adɔnkɔ wɔ intanɛt do.

Sɛ yɛankyerɛ dɛ enyɛ dɛm a, Kyerɛw nsɛm a ɔfa nsɛm pii ho no fi Kyerɛw Kronkron Wiase Fofor Nkyerɛase no mu.
