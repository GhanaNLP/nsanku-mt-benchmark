Paul kɛ Sarah to he, yoo ni yeɔ ehe ni fɔ lɛ kɛtsɔ shiwoo ko nɔ. (Gal. 4:23) Nakai shiwoo lɛ ji Abraham kpaŋmɔ lɛ. (Gal. 3:29; 4:28, 30) Afo mli kuku yɛ 1 Mose 22:18: O [10] seshi lɛ nɔ lɛ, shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ maji fɛɛ baaná jɔɔmɔ amɛha amɛhe ejaakɛ obo amɛgbee toi.

Nakai kpaŋmɔ ni akɛ Abraham fee lɛ kɛ shiwoo ni Yehowa wo yɛ Eden lɛ fata he. (Gen. 3:15) Ejie lɛ kpo akɛ seshi ni agba afɔ shi lɛ baafee Abraham seshinyo. Paulo tsɔɔ mli akɛ enɛ gbele lɛ kɔɔ Yesu Kristo he titri. Shi mɛi ni ni ji Kristo nɔ lɛ, gboshiniyelɔi aweku ni kɛ Yesu baaye nɔ yɛ e-Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ mli lɛ fata nɛkɛ mfonirifeemɔŋ seshibii nɛɛ ahe. (Gal. 3:16, 29; Kwɛmɔ. 14:1-3) Nyɔŋmɔ shiwoo ni ewo Abraham lɛ mlibaa baaha mɛi ni kpɛlɛɔ Yesu nɔ ni amɛboɔ lɛ toi lɛ aná jɔɔmɔi babaoo.

Mɛni hewɔ Paulo kɛ nɛkɛ kwashiai asane nɛɛ tsu nii lɛ? Etsɔɔ mli akɛ, kɛ́ Yudafoi ni yɔɔ ebeaŋ lɛ ma nɔ mi akɛ amɛye Mla lɛ nɔ lɛ, no lɛ amɛbaahi nyɔŋyeli mli. Shi kɛlɛ, kɛji amɛkpɛlɛ Abraham Abraham seshi lɛ nɔ lɛ, amɛ, tamɔ Sarah lɛ, amɛbaaye amɛhe. Amɛbaaná heyeli diɛŋtsɛ kɛjɛ esha kɛ gbele mli. (John 8:32-34) Kɛji amɛkpɛlɛɛɛ Yesu nɔ lɛ, amɛbaafite yiŋtoo titri ni yɔɔ Mla kpaŋmɔ lɛ sɛɛ, ni ji, ni akɛ amɛ aya Kristo ŋɔɔ lɛ.

Fishing Nets yɛ Biblia Be mli

Biblia kanelɔi baanyɛ akai akɛ Yesu sɛɛnyiɛlɔi lɛ ateŋ mɛi komɛi ji wuolɛɛmɛi. (Mark 1:16-20) Amɛkɛ net baatsu nii kɛ́ amɛmiiya wuo yɛ Galilea Ŋshɔ lɛ naa.

Akɛ bai ni ahɔɔ nii yɛ mli loo mama ni akɛkpɛɔ nii yɛ mli lɛ fee basket nets. Meŋ lɛ dalɛ damɔ loo ni ataoɔ lɛ nɔ. Bei pii lɛ, net bibii lɛ yɛ hiɛnyiɛlɔi loo tɛi ni tsii koni amɛha amɛho, yɛ be mli ni tsɔnei wuji ni akɛwoɔ nu mli lɛ yɛ shwiili yɛ shishigbɛ kɛ tso loo cork flots ni yɔɔ ŋwɛi lɛ nɔ.

Woyaa jara wa Tɛi baanyɛ efite amɛ yɛ ŋshɔ lɛ naa loo loo babaoo ni yɔɔ ŋshɔ lɛ naa lɛ tsiimɔ baanyɛ afite amɛ. (Gulɛ 5:6) Biblia lɛ wieɔ James kɛ Yohane he amɛnet lɛ tsakemɔni tamɔ nɔ ni amɛkɛ ŋmei kɛ mamai thread tsuɔ nii. (Matt. 4:21) Ekolɛ Wuolɛilɛimɛi baafee nibii nɛɛ yɛ be fɛɛ mli ni akɛtsuɔ nii lɛ sɛɛ. Luke hu tsɔɔ bɔ ni wuolɛɛmɛi ejieɔ amɛŋm lɛ koni ejie slit loo sũ ni efite lɛ. (Naagbee lɛ 5:2) No sɛɛ lɛ, wuolɛɛmɛi lɛ baatswa amɛ tɛlifoŋ lɛ koni egbietamɔ nɔ ni no baatsi amɛnaa koni amɛkafee nɔ ko.Ezek. 47:10.

Yesu fɔ nine etsɛ hii ni le bɔ ni akɛ net tsuɔ nii koni amɛbatsɔmɔ hii ahe gbeyeishemɔ lɛ.
