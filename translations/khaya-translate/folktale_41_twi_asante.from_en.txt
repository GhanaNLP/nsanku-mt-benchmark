Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐ WOYƐ AKWANKYERƐ A, SƐ WO KINSMAN KA SƐ ƆBƐMA WO NKWASƐM A, WƆN NE WƆN NYINAA HO

Bere bi na ɔbea bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔwoo mma baasa. na wɔn mu akumaa no rehu amane wɔ yaw ho Anuanom mpanyimfo no ka kyerɛɛ wɔn maame sɛ ɔmma wɔnnya sika mfuturo na ebia wɔbɛkɔ adwadi. na wɔn mu akumaa no nso kaa sɛ ɔno nso bɛpɛ sɛ ɔkɔ Wɔpaee mu kae sɛ ɛnsɛ sɛ ɔne wɔn kɔ. Ɛna no kae sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wɔne no kɔ. Afei wɔn maame de sika kɔkɔɔ mfuturo a ɛyɛ £5, ne sika kɔkɔɔ mfuturo maa mmɔfra nkumaa no. Bere a nneɛma daa adi no, wofii adi. Mpanimfoɔ no kɔeɛ (wɔ anim) na wɔgyaa abɔfra no kwan kɔdii n'akyi. Abofra no bae nkakrankakra. Mpanimfoɔ no hyiaa ɔbarima bi a na ɔde mpataa reba. Ɔka kyerɛɛ mpanyimfo no sɛ, "Tɔ!" na wɔbua see wɔn sɛ kɔ na wobɛhyia abɔfra bi ma no ntɔ na sɛ ɔka sɛ ɔrentɔ na ɔhwe no na ɔma no tɔ na sɛ ɔda so ara po sɛ ɔbɛtɔ a fa no na ɔne no sane kɔ ne na nkyɛn Ɔyɔnko no kɔɔ so, na ogye dii sɛ ɛdɔɔso sɛ ohyiaa abɔfra no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Mpanyimfo bi a mihyiaa wɔn no kae sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wotɔ saa mpataa yi." Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Momma metɔ adeɛ, berɛ a wɔn a wɔyɛ me mpanimfoɔ no antɔ?" na ne yɔnko no kaa sɛ wɔse sɛ worentɔ a ɛtwa sɛ meboro wo na ɛtwa sɛ wɔsane de wo kɔma wo na Abofra no kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ ahe?" Ɔyɔnko no kaa sɛ, "Osua mu duru yɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfuturo (i.e.) £2." Abofra no tuaa ho ka, na ogyee. Ɔtoaa so, na ɔboroo mpanyimfo a wɔwɔ akuraa pɔtee bi ase no so. Wɔanoa atosodeɛ, nanso wɔnni nam biara a wɔne wɔn bɛkɔ. Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Pataa bi wɔ hɔ a metɔeɛ." Wɔde guu soup-stew no mu. Bere a wɔawie (kɔtɔn) no, mpanyimfo no maa no apataa no ti. Na abɔfra no rebɛbubu mu no, ɔhunuu sika kɔkɔɔ wɔ mu, na ɔkyekyeree no n'atadeɛ ano. na adeɛ kyeeɛ no ɛberɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no mpanimfoɔ no sii mu kɔeɛ na wɔrekɔ no wɔhyiaa ɔbarima bi na akokɔ bi de ne ho too nneɛma a na ɔsoa no so Bere a ɔne mpanyimfo no bae ara pɛ na ɔkae sɛ, "Tɔ saa akokɔ yi." na wɔbua see no sɛ kɔ na wobɛhyia abɔfra bi ma no ntɔ na sɛ wantɔ a boro no Nokwasɛm bi mu no ohyiaa abofra no. ɔkaa sɛ, "W'abusuafoɔ mpanimfoɔ kaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wotɔ saa akokɔ yi na sɛ woantɔ a ɛnneɛ mɛboro wo na mede wo akɔ na woakɔ wo na nkyɛn." Obuae sɛ, "Ɛha yi, fa nea mewɔ." Ɔyɔnko no de akokɔ no maa no. Woduu akuraa pɔtee bi ase. Wɔ akuraa no ase nyinaa no, na nkokɔ nni hɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔbɔ. Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma daa adi no, na akokɔ no yɛ abofra no dea, adwumayɛfo. Ɔpanin a ɔwɔ akuraa no ase no kaa sɛ, "Akwadaa momfa kosua no mmra na mentɔ bi." Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Boɔ no yɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfuturo ne adeɛ a ɛmu yɛ duru (i.e.) £3." Ɔpanyin no tuaa sika no. Ade kyee no, bere a nneɛma mu da hɔ no, wofii ase bio. Bere a na wɔrekɔ no, mpanyimfo no hyiaa ɔbarima bi a okura ɔkraman. Ɔkae sɛ, "Tɔ saa ɔkra yi." Mpanimfoɔ se, "Fa kɔ, na wobɛhyia abɔfra bi; fa na ma no ntɔ. Sɛ ɔka sɛ ɔrentɔ a, ɔbɛhwe no, na ɔde no asan akɔ ne maame nkyɛn." Ne yɔnko no twaam, na ohyiaa abofra no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo mpanimfoɔ se ɛsɛ sɛ wotɔ ɔkra yi, na sɛ woantɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ meboro wo na mede wo san kɔ wo maame nkyɛn." Abofra no kae sɛ, “Neahe?” Ɔyɔnko no kaa sɛ, “Osua ne suro a ɛyɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfuturo” (i.e. £3 na ɛwɔ hɔ. na abɔfra no tuaa boɔ no na ɔkɔtoaa so na ɔne ne mpanimfoɔ sɔree wɔ akuraa bi ase Seesei deɛ, na mmire no tumi twi mmire wɔ hɔ berɛ biara a ɔbɛtumi ada. na abɔfra no faa agyinamoa no kɔɔ hɔ no ɔkɔɔ awurade fie na ɛkyeree mmire a na ɛwɔ hɔ no Ɔpanin no kaa sɛ, “Mɛtɔ saa ɔkra yi afiri onipa a ɛyɛ ne dea no hɔ.” Abofra no kae sɛ, “Ɛyɛ me dea.” Ɔbarima panyin no kae sɛ, “Neahe?” Abɔfra no kaa sɛ, “An osua ne suro a ɛyɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfuturo” (i.e. £3 na ɛwɔ hɔ. na ɔbarima tiri soɔ no faa sika mfuturo no na ɔde guu ne nsam Ade kyee no, bere a nneɛma daa adi no, wofii adi. Ná mpanyimfo no nenam; wohyiaa akorɔmfo a na wɔawia ɔpanyin bi funu no. Akorɔmfo no kae sɛ, “Ɛsɛ sɛ wotɔ amu yi.” na mpanimfoɔ no kaa sɛ momfa nkɔ na monkɔ nkɔhyia abɔfra bi na momfa mma no na montɔ na sɛ ɔka sɛ ɔrentɔ na ɔhwe no na ɔgye no na ɔsane de no ma ne na Akorɔmfo no kɔɔ so, na wohyiaa abɔfra no, wɔkae sɛ, “Mpanyimfo no se ɛsɛ sɛ wotɔ amu yi.” Abofra no kae sɛ, “Eh! ɛdeɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ metɔ funu de gye me, mantumi ansoa; biribiara a mede bɛyɛ no, ɛrenyɛ papa mma me.” na akorɔmfoɔ no kaa sɛ mo mpanimfoɔ kaa sɛ sɛ moantɔ a ɛnneɛ ɛtwa sɛ yɛhwe wo na yɛgye wo na yɛsane de wo ma wo na Abofra no kae sɛ, “Neahe?” na wɔkaa sɛ osua ne adeɛ a ɛyɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfuturo Abofra no tuaa bo no, ɔfaa funu no, na ɔde guu wuram. ɛnna ɔfirii hɔ kɔɔ akuraa bi ase na ɔkɔɔ efie bi mu kɔsrɛɛ aduane na awurade ka kyerɛɛ saulo sɛ obiara nni hɔ Abofra no kae sɛ, “Nana, mesrɛ wo.” na awurade ka kyerɛɛ saulo sɛ obiara nni hɔ Abofra no kae sɛ, “Nana, mesrɛ wo.” Owura a ɔda fie no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na saa abofra yi haw me saa; yɛn panin yi awu, yɛadi mmuada; yei ne da a ɛtɔ so nwɔtwe; mmom yɛhwehwɛ sɛdeɛ yɛbɛtumi biara, yɛnhunu ne funu." Ná aberewa bi te bɛn hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ, “Ma no bi.” Ɔbea no maa no aduan ne nam. na abɔfra no wiee n'adedie no ɔkaa sɛ awurade fie wura mahunu ɔsafohene amu no ɛfiri sɛ nnora reba no akorɔmfoɔ binom maa metɔɔ maa osua ne sika kɔkɔɔ mfuturo Ɔbea no tuu mmirika, yiridi, yiridi, yiridi! (na ɛyɛ ne mmirikatu no nnyigyei) na ɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ mpanyimfo (akuraa) no. na wɔfaa abɔfra no kɔe na ɔkɔkyerɛɛ wɔn amu no Wɔkɔe na wɔde bae, na wosiee no sɛnea ɛsɛ. Wɔkae sɛ, “Afei wubedi yiye ama Ɔpanyin no.” ɛnna abɔfra no bɛyɛɛ ɔpanin ɛnna ne nuanom mpanimfoɔ tee ne ho asɛm no wɔbaeɛ na afei wɔkaa sɛ mogya na wɔne no wɔ Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ebia wobɛda wo ho adi, mennim wo, enti firi adi." na ɔmaa ne nkoa pamm wɔn kɔe ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ sɛ worekɔ baabiara na sɛ wo nua kumaa se ɔne wo bɛkɔ a ɔne no nkɔ

Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
