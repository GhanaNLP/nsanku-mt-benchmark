SƐ OBI BƐYƐ WO A, Kyerɛ WO HANKYERƐ SƐNEA WƆBƐYƐ NE NKYERƐKYERƐMU

WƆKA sɛ na ɛhɔ (pɛnkoro) yɛ Ɔkɔre, na wɔ ne kyinhyia mu no ɔkɔhyiaa aberewa bi a na ɔwɔ akuru wɔ ne nan ho. Na Ɔkɔre no kae sɛ, "Me ho yɛ fɛwdi! Eyi yɛ kuro wɔ adesuakuw bi ankasa mu; nea ɛte saa no, ɛmfa ho sɛnea wobɔ mmɔden biara no, so wutumi nantew? Ɔbea no akwakoraa no kae sɛ, "O, kakraa bi pɛ." The Eagle kaa sɛ, "Mo, nnɛ, sɛ meyɛ biribi pa ma mo (ɛnnɛ) a, ɔkyena anka wobɛfa biribi bɔne de ada me ase." Ɔbaa akwakoraa no kaa sɛ, "Oh, mentumi nyɛ saa (ɛno)." Ɔkɔre no kae sɛ, "Sɛ wuntumi nyɛ w'ade saa a, mate." Ɔkae sɛ, "W'ani so na bue." na ɔbaa panin no buee n'ani na ɔbuee wɔn ano Ɔkɔre no kae sɛ, "Hwɛ wo kuro no." Na ɔbaa a ne mfe akɔ anim no bɛhwɛɛ no —ɛnyɛ ne nkaeɛ. Afei ɔmaa no bɛnee (n'ani) bio; obuee wɔn, na ohui sɛ wɔayi asase no nyinaa afi hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, "Wɔbɛsan akata wɔn so bio." ɔtoo wɔn mu na ɔbuee wɔn na afie gyinagyinaa hɔ pintinn na ɔmaa no bɛnee wɔn bio Obuee wɔn ano sɛ wɔrekɔhwɛ kurow kɛse yi; ɛhɔ na na ɛrekɔ soro. Ɔkɔre no kae sɛ, "Ɔbea panyin, ɛyɛ wo dea (lit. wɔ wo nsam) . Ɔbaa akwakoraa no kaa sɛ, "Aseda na mede ma wo." Afei ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meda wo ase?" Ɔkɔre no kae sɛ, "Mempɛ ade a ɛyɛ mmiɛnsa mpo, sɛnea ɛte wɔ me fam no, (ne nyinaa) mepɛ ne saa Silk-cotton-tree yi a egyina hɔ." Ɔbaapanin no kaa sɛ, "Saa asɛm yi (wobisa) fa no sɛn, fa no." Afei Ɔkɔre no tu firii hɔ, na ɔteɛɛm guu so, na ɔwosoo buo, na ɔtoo nkosua mmienu, na ɔde guu mu. na ɔbobɔɔ nkosua mmienu no na ɔfirii hɔ kɔhwehwɛɛ biribi maa ne mma didiiɛ Ɔbea no banana a na ɔne no te no fii ase yɛɛ basaa sɛ, "Ehe! Ehe!" Ɔbea no akwakoraa no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛhaw adwene?" Ɔkae sɛ, "Ma menwe Eagle bi mma." Ɔbaa akwakoraa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛhe na menya Ɔkɔre mma?" Nana ketewa no san fii ase bio, na ɔpɛ sɛ ɔyɛ saa! ɔpɛfoɔ! Ɔbea no akwakoraa no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛhaw adwene?" Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ma menwe Eagle mma, ɛfiri sɛ sɛ mannya bi annom a mɛwu." Ɔbea a ne mfe akɔ anim no kae sɛ, "Ah! So eyi, ɛsɛ sɛ me nana wu esiane sɛ Ɔkɔre mma mpɛ sɛ wɔwe nti? sɔre fa agyan na kɔ na kɔbɔ sirikyi-twene na fa Ɔkɔre mma brɛ me Akuraa no asefo no kɔɔ hɔ, (wɔtee nsɔe no), Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! na ɛte sɛ nea dua no rekɔ fam na Ɔkɔre mma no mu mpanin hururii na ɔba bɛgyinaa bunu no ano na ɔmaa ɛsu frɛɛ ɛna no na ɔkaa sɛ:

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, anomaa no, Ɔkɔre no ba!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔ didi a, san bra!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, o! o!

Ɛna no tee sɛ ne ba no resu; ɔsɔree, (na ne ntaban nnyigyei bɔɔ mu yɛ,) Ɔbaa hɔ; ɔkaa sɛ, "Sanguri," na saa dua no (a ɛkaa kakraa bi na wɔtwaeɛ) sane baa mu bio, na wɔmee nnipa a na wɔrebɔ no nyinaa. ɛnna ɔfaa aduane a ɔde baeɛ no de maa ne mma Afei ɔbɔɔ wɔn hu yiye, na ɔkae sɛ, "Sɛ aberewa no ba bɛgye wo a, ma ɔmfa wo nkɔ." na aberewa no kaa sɛ monkɔ nkɔbɔ dua no na momfa abɔdeɛ no mmra mma me nana mmɛwe Na wɔkɔɔ hɔ ne mprenu so, Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! na ɛte sɛ nea dua no bɛkɔ asaase soɔ na elam ba no firii adi bɛgyinaa ɛserɛ ano na ɔfrɛɛ ne na kaa sɛ

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, anomaa no, Ɔkɔre no ba!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔ didi a, san bra!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, o! o!

Ɔfrɛɛ ne maame na ɔfrɛɛ no na ɔfrɛɛ mmuae biara —na afei dua no kasae bere a ɛbɔɔ fam sɛ “Brim” no. (ɛkae). na wɔyiyii Ɔkɔre no mma no wɔde baako maa ɔbaa akɔkoraa no deɛ nanso deɛ ɛkaa no kɔeɛ na ɛsosɔeɛ wɔ wawa-dua bi so Ɔbaako, wɔtoo no twenee na wɔde maa ɔbaa panin no nana de kaa dua a wɔatwa no ho (na ɔredidi). Ɛno akyi bere tiaa bi no, Ɔkɔre no bae. na ɔduruu dua a wɔhweeɛ no ho no ɔhunuu ne mma no mu baako sɛ ɔte hɔ na ɔbisaa no sɛ ɛdeɛn na ɛyɛeɛ na ɔkaa nsɛm yi nyinaa kyerɛɛ no Ɔkɔre no fii ase kɔɔ ɔbea no akuraa a akyɛ no ase. Bere a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, na ɔbea panyin no banana redi ne mma no mu biako. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔbea panyin, mema wo akwaaba." ɔfirii adi firii aberewa no fie na ɔfirii aseɛ (ne nkonyaayifoɔ) wɔ kuro no akyi Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sanguri," na onipa biara yerae, na ɔsan kaa sɛ, "Sanguri," na ofie biara bubuu mu prɛko pɛ na ɛnyɛ dan. Akuraa no ase sane bɛyɛɛ kwae no. "Sanguri," na ɔbea panyin no kuro no san bae. na ɔkaa sɛ ɔbaa dada woahunu ɛno nti na mpanimfoɔ se sɛ ebinom yɛ wo papa a ɛnneɛ meda no ase sɛ woayɛ no papa na woamfa bɔne ankyerɛ no Eyi, m'asɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom betumi afa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no, wubetumi akamfo me (wɔ ho asɛm a wɔka kyerɛ no ho).
