SƐnea Babaduca no maa nneɛma a ɛwom no kɔɔ soro (WƆ ITS STALK SO) .

Ɔbaa bi a ɔda so te ase (bere bi) nyinsɛnee, na ofii hɔ kɔɔ anyame nketewa no mu biako nkyɛn kɔhwehwɛɛ mu (ɛfa abofra no ho). Ɛnna onyame no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛbɛyɛ sɛ sɛ wowo abɔfra no a ɛbɛwuo, nanso mɛyɛ deɛ mɛtumi biara sɛ mede bɛma wo, nanso asɛm a ɛsɛ sɛ meka kyerɛ wo ne sɛ, abɔfra no yɛ (nokwarem no) ɔhemmaa ɛna bi ba wɔ hɔ a ɔne Nyame, Wim-nyame no wɔ hɔ, na sɛ woso a, mma no nyɛ hwee, na mmom sɛ wosɔre a, ɛsɛ sɛ woguare, fa ngo, na fa gu asɛnka agua so." Nokwasɛm bi mu no, ogye toom. (Te sɛ ne nyinaa) mmofra a wɔwɔ Spider anansesɛm mu no, ɛnkyɛ koraa na anyin. Wɔfrɛɛ abɔfra Akosua Denta. na sɛdeɛ onyame kumaa no hyɛɛ no no saa ara na ɛna no nso sɔree na ɔhohoroo no ngo na ɔnoaa biribi maa no didiiɛ na ɔfirii hɔ kɔɔ n'afuo mu Afei, da bi a na ɔnni berɛ a ɔde bɛnoa aduane biara a ɔbɛdi no, ɔde ahyɛdeɛ maa n'afenaa, Kra sɛ, "Merekɔ afuom, enti noa biribi na ɔde ma Akosua Denta sɛ ɔnni." ɛnna ɛna no sii mu kɔeɛ. Afei, berɛ a Kra wiee aduane no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Akosua Denta, bra ha bɛbɔ saa "fufu" yi, wo ne wo Queen-Maame asɛm." Ɔkae sɛ, "Merenba." Kra kaa sɛ, "Wo ne w'ahantan bɛba." Na Akosua Denta sɔre kɔe; ɔkɔgyee pestle no; ɔbɔɔ _fufu_ no. na ɔwieeɛ ara pɛ na ɔkɔdaa dan no mu na ɔfaa ne ntoma kataa ne tiri so Kra kɔɔ (ɔno akyi), ɔkaa sɛ, "Denta"; mmuae biara nni ho. Oyii ntoma no fii ne tirim; Akosua Denta awu; ɔde ntoma no sii n'ananmu. Ɛno akyi bere tiaa bi no, ɛna no fii afuom bae. Ɔbaa hɔ no, wanhwehwɛ ne ba no prɛko pɛ, na mmom ɔkɔɔ ogya no ano sɛ ɔrekɔyɛ aduane awie. Afei ɔkaa sɛ, "Kra, ɛhe na Denta wɔ?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Nana, mpo n'aduane a menoaeɛ no mede sii hɔ no, ɛhɔ na ɛda so ara gyina." Ɛna no kae sɛ, "Mate." Afei maame no sii mu kɔɔ dan no mu sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛfa ntoma (sɛ ɔbɛhyɛ) na ɔfiri abɔntene kɔhwehwɛɛ ne ba no. Bere a ɔkɔɔ dan no mu no, na biribi a ɛyɛ sum da hɔ. Ɔnyanee ntoma no, na ɔhunuu sɛ Akosua Denta awu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Kra, woka sɛ Denta afiri abɔntene, na seesei ha deɛ, wawu?" Kra kae sɛ, "Nanabea nhuu bi da." Afei ɔbaa no kyekyeree ntoma bɛlt kyekyeree n'asen na ɔfaa abɔfra (a wawu) wɔ n'akyi na ɔne no puee abɔntene so na ɔsui na akuraa no ase panyin no bae na ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa wo ba (wufiri w'akyi) na ma yɛnkɔ na yɛnkɔsie." Ɔbea no buae sɛ:

na me ne me ba Akosua Denta na meyɛɛ no nyinsɛneeɛ na me ne no kɔhwehwɛɛ nsuo a ɛwɔ nsuo mu no mu

Wɔka sɛ, 'Ɛyɛ Ɔhemmaa maame ba.'

Mepɛ sɛ obi kɔka sɛ, Ɔhemmaa no ba, Siankwa Adamowa ba, Yei ne me ba, merensie no wɔ ha."

na ɔfirii adi na ɔkɔɔ akuraa bi ase na ɔkɔduruu hɔ na akuraa no aseɛ panin no kaa sɛ yi abɔfra no firi hɔ (w'akyi) na bra ma mensie no ɛfiri sɛ yɛtaboo (adeɛ a ɛte saa) Nanso ɔbea no kae sɛ:

na me ne me ba Akosua Denta na meyɛɛ no nyinsɛneeɛ na me ne no kɔhwehwɛɛ nsuo a ɛwɔ nsuo mu no mu

Wɔka sɛ, 'Ɛyɛ Ɔhemmaa maame ba.'

Mepɛ sɛ obi kɔka sɛ, Ɔhemmaa no ba, Siankwa Adamowa ba, Yei ne me ba, merensie no wɔ ha."

Afei ɔsan kɔe bio. Ɔnantee, na ɔnantee, faa kwae bi a emu dɔ mu, na ebeduu saa bere yi no, abofra no aporɔw, na oyii fii hɔ (ne nkyɛn) na ɔtow gui, na ɔsan baa fie. na akuraa no ase panin no bisaa no sɛ ɛhe na abɔfra no wɔ Ɔkae sɛ, "Mefaa no na metow no gui." Akuraa no ase panyin no kae sɛ, "W'ani awu, sɛ womaa ho kwan wɔ ha a anka ɛsɛ sɛ yesie no wɔ ahonhommɔne a anka ɛbɛfata no mu." Yei nti mpanimfoɔ se, "Sɛ wo ba wu a, ɛnneɛ di awerɛhoɔ wɔ ɔkwan a ɛfata so; mma ɛnyɛ wo yaw. ɛfiri sɛ saa ɔbaa no yɛɛ n'adeɛ saa, na ɔtoo ne ba no guiɛ berɛ a ɛho nhia sɛ ɔyɛ saa no." Na afei ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn nti na Kra yi deɛ, fa no na to no twenee atɛkyɛ mu na ɔde no bɔɔ no maa me." Afei akuraa no asefo no kɔɔ hɔ (beae a na Kra wɔ, na na wɔte nnyigyei bi a wɔde bɔ Pu! Pu! Pu! na wɔbobɔɔ no kɔsii sɛ ne ho yɛɛ mmrɛ pa ara. Na afei deɛ na ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ sɛ "nea ɔwɔ ayamye"; bere a wɔbɔɔ no no, na ɛyɛ den, na bere a wɔbɔɔ no no, ehuruw kɔbataa Babadua demmire no ho. Ɛno nti na Babadua demmire no wɔ nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn (wɔ n’akyi), a kan no na ɛyɛ fom, kyerɛ sɛ, ɛyɛ mmerɛ koraa.

Eyi, m'asɛm a meka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom betumi afa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no, wubetumi akamfo me (sɛ woka ho asɛm a).
