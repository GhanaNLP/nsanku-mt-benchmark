Shikpɔŋ lɛ shishigbɛ niŋmaa ni yɔɔ Yeremia 7:25 lɛ tsɔɔ akɛ Yehowa tsɔɔ ehe akɛ eji mɔ ko ni miiya hiɛ mra. March 15, 2011, sane ni ji The ekpɔtɔ lɛ kɛɛ: Taakɛ almond tso lɛ afo mra lɛ, no hewɔ lɛ Yehowa ji yɛ mfonirifeemɔŋ eteɔ shi mra . . . ni ebɔ ewebii lɛ kɔkɔ yɛ nɔ ni jɛɔ toigbele mli baa lɛ he. . . . Ni ekɛ ehe wooo mliebaaya nɔ ete shi kɛyashi be mli ni abaatsu gbalɛ wiemɔ lɛ he nii. 1:11, 12.

Akɛ almond tso lɛ he ŋmɔlɔŋmɔlɔ lɛ hu tsuɔ nii yɛ Biblia lɛ mli kɛtsɔɔ bɔ ni afii ni mɔ ko eye lɛ ji yitso yɛŋ lɛ mli. (Ecl. 12:5) Ni ekolɛ yɛ amɛhefɛo ni yɔɔ mlɛo lɛ hewɔ lɛ, tso lɛ fɔfɔii lɛ ji nɔ ni akɛtsɔɔ kɔɔpoi ni yɔɔ kpee lɛ niji lɛ anɔ lɛ mli.Ex. 37:17-20.

Jeee ahɔ̃ɔmɔ wolo nɛɛ. Akɛhaa akɛ jeŋ muu fɛɛ Biblia kasemɔ nitsumɔ ko ni oniatsumɔi ni jɛɔ amɛsuɔmɔ mli amɛhaa lɛ fiɔ sɛɛ lɛ fã. Kɛ ootao ni okɛ onia aha lɛ, ofainɛ yaa shikatoohe.jw.org.

Kɛ atsɔɔɔ gbɛ kroko nɔ lɛ, Ŋmalɛ mli wiemɔi ni atsɛ yisɛɛ lɛ jɛ ŋmɛnɛŋmɛnɛ beaŋ Jeŋ Muu Fɛɛ Shishitsɔɔmɔ Hee ni kɔɔ Septuagint Krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ he lɛ mli.
