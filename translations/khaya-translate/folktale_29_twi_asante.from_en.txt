Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA OBI BƐHWEHWƐ WIFE A ƐWƆ AKWANKYERƐ (NE NSƐM A ƐSO SO) HISENA BIASE A ƐWƆ NKYERƐKYERƐMU A ƐFA NKYERƐKYERƐMU A WƆDE BƐMA NSƐM A WƆDE BƐMA

Ɛhɔ na na mmarima baasa te na na wɔpɛ sɛ wɔyɛ adwuma. Wɔkɔɔ Takyekye ɔman no mu kɔtɔɔ nkyekye ntoma ne nneɛma foforɔ. (Wɔresan aba Asante) wɔmaa wɔn wɔfase soaa nneɛma no bi, na wɔkɔɔ so tɔn wiei. Wɔkɔɔ kurow kɛse bi mu. Wɔ kuro no mu no, na ɔhemmaa maame babaa biara a wɔde no bɛma no aware no, wampene. Afei, saa ahɔhoɔ yi (adwadifoɔ no) kɔɔ hɔ (ne fie), nanso na Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no akɔ "sɛ ɔrekɔboro "hohoro" no. Nanso Ɔhemmaa no maame tɔɔ ntoma mmienu de too nkyɛn maa ne babaa no. Bere a ne ba no san bae no ohuu mmarima baasa no. ɔka kyerɛɛ n'akɔkoraa sɛ ɛde bɛsi nnɛ mahunu deɛ mepɛ no enti kum odwan na ma memfa mma wɔn Wɔwiee aduane no noa no, ababaa kumaa no kɔfaa asono sare, kotokuo, kola nnuane, ne asase mu nnuane, na wɔde sii aduane no atifi sɛ wɔrekɔkyɛ ama mmarima no. na ɔkaa sɛ deɛ ɔbɛtumi akyerɛkyerɛ wɔn asekyerɛ mu no ɔno na mɛware no na wɔde aduane no kɔe Mmarima no hunuu no, na wɔkaa sɛ, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ Ɔhemmaa no ba na ɔde saa aduane yi baeɛ a, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛnya nsɛm bi bisa no." mmarima no wɔfase no se, "Sɛ mansuro wo a, anka mɛkyerɛ wo nea saa nsɛm no kyerɛ." Wɔkae sɛ, "Kyerɛkyerɛ mu." Abofra no bua se, "Abeawa no se wayɛ n'adwene fitaa te sɛ kotokuo no, na sɛ ɔbɛtumi anya yɛn mu baako a waba ha a, ɛnsɛ sɛ ɔware? Deɛ asono sare kyerɛ ne sɛ, da koro ɔbɛyera; kola nut no nso kyerɛ sɛ ɛda bi bɛba a ɔbɛma no (ne kunu) awe kola (i.e. awerɛhoɔ. Fam nwa kyerɛ sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ wodua asase mu nnuaba ma no, na afe biara, sɛ ɔkɔhwɛ ntini no a, ɔbɛnya bi atwe akɔ soro." ɛnna ɔhemmaa maame ba no waree mmarima no wɔfaase Ɔwaree no, Ɔhemmaa no ba bisaa no sɛ, "Wo din de sɛn?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ me Kwaku Susufuo," kyerɛ sɛ, Kwaku-a ɔde ɔhaw ba adwene mu. Abeawa no buae sɛ, "Me nso, mefrɛ me Akua-a ɔfa ɔhaw a ɛsɛ sɛ wosusuw ho." Ɔwaree no maa guasodeyɛ a ɛfata. Ade kyee anɔpa no, wɔn papa nua baasa no fii adi kɔɔ wɔn ankasa kurom. Kwaku-a ɔfaa ɔhaw a ɛne sɛ obesusuw nneɛma ho no kɔɔ so traa ne yere akuraa ase. Ɔtraa hɔ afe, na ɔsɔree anɔpa no, na ɔne ne nnamfonom tena ase di agorɔ wɔ borɔferɛ so. Afei, da bi a na ɔredi agorɔ saa no, ɔbɔɔ ntɛtɛ mmienu tuo baako, nanso nnipa a ɔne wɔn redi agorɔ no kaa sɛ wɔremfa mma no, na wɔpɛ sɛ wɔhwe no mpo. na ɔyii n'ani kyerɛɛ n'akyi na hwɛ na abɔfra bi gyinagyina hɔ na ɔfaa ɔboɔ de maa abɔfra no sɛ ɔnkɔ na ɔmfa mma ne yere Afei ne yere nsa kaa ɔboɔ no na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Me kunu pɛ sɛ ɔsane ba ne kuro mu ɛfiri sɛ ɔka sɛ sɛ anomaa kyɛ dodo wɔ fam a, ɛgye ɔboɔ." ɛnna ɔhemmaa no maame kaa sɛ, "Ɔkɔ na ɛsɛ sɛ wo ne no kɔ." Nokwasɛm bi mu no, ɔne no fii hɔ kɔe. na wɔhyiaa isak a ɔde ɔhaw brɛ wɔn wɔfa Afei, mmerɛ bi akyi no, wɔsee no sɛ, "Kwaku a ɔde ɔhaw ba ne tirim sɛ ɔbɛdwene nneɛma ho no, nya hama bi a yɛbɛtumi akɔ wuram sɛ yɛrekɔto afiri ama yɛakyere mmorɔnoma ama ahɔhoyɛ ama wo yere a ɔyɛ ɔhɔhoɔ no." Ɛnna wɔsii mu kɔeɛ, nanso na saa mpanimfoɔ yi apene so sɛ wɔbɛkum no ankasa. Wɔne Kwaku-a wɔfaa ɔhaw a ɛne sɛ wobesusuw nneɛma ho no kɔe; wɔduruu (beaeɛ a ɛyɛ ankonam) wɔ kwan no so; wɔkyekyeree no kyekyeree no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Yɛbɛkum wo.” ɛnna ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ mekɔɔ me yere akuraa aseɛ no mehunuu sika bebree na sɛ moamma me kwan sɛ menkyerɛ mo faako a ɛwɔ na mewuo a morenhunu Wɔbisaa no sɛ, “Sika no da he?” Obuae sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ me yere nkyɛn kɔbɔ no amanneɛ sɛ matɔ nam, na (wɔn a wɔtɔn nneɛma no) se ne bo yɛ peredwan du (£80), na ɛsɛ sɛ ɔhwehwɛ wɔ bag no mu a wɔde nguan nhoma a wɔde abɔ mu ayɛ; sɛ ɔhwɛ hɔ na wanhu a, ma ɔnhwɛ Krampan kotoku no mu, sɛ wanhunu wɔ hɔ a, ma ɔnhwɛ bɛlt no mu a sekan wɔ mu.” ɛnna wɔbaa ne yere nkyɛn bɛkaa nsɛm yi kyerɛɛ no na ɔno nso kaa sɛ nnoɔma yi wɔ akuraa no ase panin fie enti momma yɛnkɔ nkɔfa wɔn Ɔne wɔn kɔɔ ɔpanin no fie; na ɔbaa no kaa asɛm yi kyerɛɛ tirinkwa no sɛ, "Ntampeɛ kotokuo a wɔde nguan ayɛ no kyerɛ sɛ wɔakyekyere no; Krampanfoɔ kyerɛ sɛ abɔsoɔ no rehwehwɛ sɛ ɔbɛdi ne nam; abɔsoɔ a ɛkura sekan no kyerɛ sɛ wɔrebɛtwa ne menewam. Saa berɛ no ara na ɔpanin no bɔɔ gong-gong, sɛ wɔnkɔ hɔ nkɔgye Kwaku-a ɔhaw biara nni ne tirim sɛ ɔbɛdwene ho no nkwa. na wɔne no sane bae Ɔmaa wokunkum mpanyimfo baasa no. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, “Ɛnam nsɛnkyerɛnne asekyerɛ so na obi nyaa ɔyere na ɔno nso gyee ne nkwa.” Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ɛma ebinom san ba me nkyɛn.
