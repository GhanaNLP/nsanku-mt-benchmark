HEBAAKƐ AGO

Be mli gbɛi ni Nyɔŋmɔ baatsɔ nɔ ekɛ gbɛi aha ehe. Nyɔŋmɔ baaná mɔ ko ni baaye odase yɛ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ,' taakɛ The Watch Tower of January 1, 1926 lɛ kɛɛ lɛ.

Beni afi 1926 lɛ yaa nɔ lɛ, Yehowakɛtsɔ asafo lɛ yitso nɔ, Kristoha efee faŋŋ akɛ esa akɛ shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ afee nɔ titri ni Kristofonyo ko shihilɛ kɛ ejwɛŋmɔ maa nɔ. Kɛha nakai feemɔ lɛ, nyɔŋ anɔkwafo kɛ nilelɔ lɛ kɛ woji heei ha Nyɔŋmɔ tsuji lɛ, amɛfee tsakemɔi yɛ kpeei lɛ amli, ni amɛha anu gbɛnaa nii ni kã Kristofonyo ko nɔ lɛ shishi jogbaŋŋ. (Matt. 24:45) Kristo kwɛ koni amɛná tsui kɛ nibii ni he hiaa amɛ koni amɛtsu shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ.

Kain KƐ HE NI AKƐTSƆƆ HE NYƆŊMƆI

Yɛ April mli lɛ, Shika Be lɛ (ni amrɔ nɛɛ atsɛɔ lɛ Awake!) Atswa adafi akɛ abaajie wolo hee ko ni ji musuŋtsɔmɔ lɛ kpo. Wolo nɛɛ baaye Nikasemɔi ni yɔɔ Septuagint lɛ najiaŋ akɛ wolo titri ni akɛtsuɔ nii yɛ sɔɔmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ mli. Bɔni afee ni atsu nɔ ni akpaa gbɛ lɛ he nii lɛ, nyɛmimɛi ni yɔɔ Malaysia lɛ he adafitswaa wolo hee ko ni baanyɛ akala woji 22 yɛ minitii kome mli. Biblia Nikaselɔi lɛ kɛ wolo hee lɛ ha yɛ hegbɛ fɛɛ hegbɛ mli.

Afii babaoo nɛ ni no mli lɛ Biblia Nikaselɔi lɛ esusu bɔ ni Nikasemɔi babaoo ni yɔɔ Septuagint lɛ mli lɛ fa ha yɛ amɛkpeei lɛ ashishi lɛ he. Shi kɛjɛ May mli kɛbaa nɛɛ, amɛbɔi saji ni jɛ The Watch Tower lɛ mli lɛ ahe susumɔ. Mɛni ji nɔ ni jɛ mli ba?

Bunker Hill, Illinois escclesia [wiemɔ] fioo lɛ kɛ ŋamɔi majeɔ bo, ŋma asafo kome yɛ wolo ko ni eŋma Brother Rutherford lɛ mli. Yɛ Hɔgbaa lɛ, wɔkaseɔ [niŋmaa] ni Kuu lɛ hala kɛha nakai gbii lɛ. Wɔheɔ wɔyeɔ waa akɛ wɔ fɛɛ wɔbaanyɛ wɔna kane hee lɛ bɔ ni sa.

Kane hee lɛ, loo shishinumɔ ni mli ka shi faŋŋ lɛ ye ebua Biblia Nikaselɔi lɛ ni amɛha shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ tee nɔ ekɛ amɛjwɛŋmɔ ma nɔ. Nɔkwɛmɔnɔ, niŋmaa ni ji Hewalɛ loo KpaŋmɔMɛni? tsɔɔ mli akɛ hiɛyaa, ni kɛ ejwɛŋmɔ maa bulɛ kɛ mlihilɛ subaŋ pɛ nɔ lɛ ha mɛi komɛi ba anɔkwa Kristofoi anitsumɔ diɛŋtsɛ lɛ shi. Niŋmaa lɛ wo ŋaa akɛ: Esa akɛ mɔ ni sumɔɔ Nuŋtsɔ lɛ amrɔ nɛɛ. . . Ye efamɔi lɛ anɔ. . . . Yehowa kɛɛ: "Minyɛ ji mi odasefonyo akɛ miji Nyɔŋmɔ. . . . Amrɔ nɛɛ Kristofonyo lɛ baasumɔ waa ni eye odase akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ odasefonyo krɔŋkrɔŋ.

Niŋmaa kroko, Wroa kɛ Sɔɔmɔ, ma nɔ mi akɛ anɔkwa Kristofoi jieɔ suɔmɔ ni amɛyɔɔ kɛha Yehowa lɛ kpo kɛtsɔ anɔkwayeli sɔɔmɔ ni amɛkɛhaa lɛ lɛ nɔ. Niŋmaa lɛ wo Biblia Nikaselɔi fɛɛ hewalɛ koni amɛkɛ amɛhe awo Nuŋtsɔ lɛ sɔɔmɔ lɛ mli kɛ ekãa, hiɛdɔɔ, kɛ ekãa ni amɛfee Nuŋtsɔ lɛ odasefoi ni amɛjaje eshɛɛ sane lɛ amɛtsɔɔ maji kɛ gbɔmɛi ni yɔɔ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ lɛ.Isa. 43:10,00 Matt. 24:14.

NI AKƐTSƆƆ HE NI AKƐTSƆƆ HE NYƆŊMƆI

Yɛ May naagbee lɛ, Biblia Nikaselɔi babaoo bua amɛhe naa yɛ kpee ko ni afee yɛ London, England lɛ shishi. Nitsumɔ he nitsumɔ ko ni tee nɔ yɛ Britain lɛ ha ewa akɛ abaahe jarayeli woji, no hewɔ lɛ nyɛmimɛi hii ni yɔɔ Brooklyn lɛ tsu nii fe nine koni amɛkɛ wolo bibioo ko ni ji The Standard for the People lɛ ekomɛi 117,000 aha kpee lɛ.

Yɛ Hɔgbaa, May 29 lɛ, akɛ nakai woji bibii lɛ tsu nii jogbaŋŋ. Kpee lɛ he gbɛjianɔtoo kɛha nakai gbi lɛ wo hewalɛ akɛ: A fɔ̃ɔ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni baa kpee lɛ shishi lɛ nine koni ekɛ ehe awo Maŋtsɛ lɛ kɛ Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ adafitswaa mli. Na Alexandra Maŋtsɛwe lɛ mra bɔ ni eeenyɛ, ni obɔ amaniɛ kɛha sɔɔmɔ yɛ Sɔɔmɔ Nitsumɔhe lɛ.

Aja woji ni fa fe 100,000. Watch Tower ni je kpo yɛ July 15, 1926 lɛ bɔ amaniɛ akɛ: nanemɛi lɛ kɛ miishɛɛ miitswa amɛhe. Amɛnu he akɛ amɛbɔ mɔdɛŋ fɛɛ ni amɛaanyɛ koni amɛbo Yehowa famɔ lɛ toi: Mi odasefoi ji mi akɛ miji Nyɔŋmɔ.

Enɔ jetsɛremɔ lɛ, yɛ Royal Albert Hall lɛ, Brother Rutherford wie saneyitso ni ji Nɔ Hewɔ ni Jeŋ Hewalɛi lɛ Miitswa Tsamɔ lɛ He. Yɛ minitii fioo komɛi pɛ sɛɛ beni agbele shinaai lɛ anaa lɛ, mɛi 13,000 eyi asa lɛ mli obɔ. Mɛi babaoo boɔ gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ toi jogbaŋŋ kɛtsɔɔ wiemɔ tsɔnei ni akɛwoɔ kpo lɛ nɔ.

Yɛ ewiemɔ lɛ mli lɛ, Brother Rutherford tsɔɔ mli akɛ je lɛŋ hewalɛi ni gbee shi lɛ jɛ amɛkpoomɔ ni amɛkpoo Yehowa kɛ e-Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ mli. Brother Rutherford tee nɔ ebu Jeŋmaji Akpaŋmɔ lɛ fɔ ni egba akɛ ebaakpa shihilɛ.

Yaa nɔ ohi Gbɛ ni Ja lɛ nɔ

Ŋɔɔ lɛ akɛ obote bɔs ko ni baafã gbɛ kilomitai ohai abɔ kɛya he ko ni yɔɔ fɛo lɛ mli. Yɛ nakai gbɛfaa kakadaŋŋ lɛ mli lɛ, ekolɛ obaanu he waa, ni no haa omii shɛɔ ohe waa yɛ anɔkwale ni eji akɛ oyɛ gbɛfaa lɛ mli lɛ hewɔ.

Shi be mli ni oyaa gbɛfaa nɛɛ mli lɛ, ekolɛ nibii komɛi yɛ ni baaha edɔ boekolɛ sanegbaa ni naa wa ni tee nɔ yɛ gbɛfalɔi enyɔ ateŋ loo tsɔne kudɔlɔ lɛ he hiɛgbele po. Shi nakai tsakemɔ lɛ eko kwraa tsakeee anɔkwale ni eji akɛ oyɛ bɔs ni sa mli lɛ, ni esa akɛ oshɛ he ni oyaa lɛ.

Yɛ wɔ gbɛfaa kɛmiiya Nyɔŋmɔ jeŋ hee lɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaaná henumɔi srɔtoini amɛteŋ babaoo ji miishɛɛ yɛ hegbɛ ni wɔyɔɔ akɛ wɔfeɔ Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ fã lɛ he. Shi bei komɛi lɛ, ekolɛ mɔ ko ni ehe gbɛi waa lɛ mli baafu loo mɔ ko ni wɔkwɛɔ lɛ efɔŋ lɛ baaha wɔhe afee wɔ yaa po. Shi nakai tsakemɔ lɛ eko kwraa tsakeee anɔkwale ni eji akɛ wɔyɛ bɔs ni sa mli lɛ, bɔni afee ni wɔwie lɛ. Kristowa

Yehowa Nyɔmɔwoo Hemɔkɛyeli

Emma tsuɔ nii yɛ hotɛl gbii etɛ yɛ otsi mli, ŋmɛlɛtswai nɛɛhu daa gbi. Ebi enitsumɔ nɔkwɛlɔ lɛ gbi kome bɔni afee ni eya ekpokpaa wulu nɔ kpee lɛ. Yɛ nɔ najiaŋ ni nitsumɔ nɔkwɛlɔ lɛ baaha ebimɔ lɛ, etsake egbɛjianɔtoo lɛ ewo gbii enumɔ yɛ otsi mli ni ekɛ ultimatum ha lɛ: ekpɛlɛ gbɛjianɔtoo hee lɛ nɔ loo eshi nitsumɔ lɛ. Akɛni Emma kɛ ehe fɔ̃ɔ Yehowa nɔ hewɔ lɛ, ehala akɛ ebaashi nitsumɔ lɛ ni eya kpee lɛ.

Beni Emma tee ni ekɛ enitsumɔ lɛ baaha lɛ, no mli lɛ hotɛl lɛ nɔkwɛlɔ nukpa lɛ yɛ jɛmɛ. Be ni ekase sane lɛ he nii lɛ, ewo shi akɛ ebaakwɛ sane lɛ nɔ. Shi, yɛ otsii enyɔ sɛɛ lɛ, akɛɛ Emma akɛ ashwie lɛ. Ekɛ nitsumɔ nɔkwɛlɔ nukpa lɛ kpe ekoŋŋ, ni emli fu akɛ aku hiɛ ashwie egbɛtsɔɔmɔi lɛ anɔ. Etsu sane lɛ he nii, ni aha Emma gbɛjianɔtoo hee: gbii enumɔ yɛ otsi mli, ŋmɛlɛtswai ejwɛ daa gbi. Ebafee nɔ ni hi fe bɔ ni ato he gbɛjianɔ aha shishijee lɛ po; ekɛ emaŋkwramɔŋ nitsumɔi lɛ tsu nii! Yehowa kwɛ nibii ni he hiaa lɛ lɛ anɔ yɛ gbɛ ni fe nɔ ni ekpaa gbɛ lɛ nɔ.

Jeee ahɔ̃ɔmɔ wolo nɛɛ. Akɛhaa akɛ jeŋ muu fɛɛ Biblia kasemɔ nitsumɔ ko ni oniatsumɔi ni jɛɔ amɛsuɔmɔ mli amɛhaa lɛ fiɔ sɛɛ lɛ fã. Kɛ ootao ni okɛ onia aha lɛ, ofainɛ yaa shikatoohe.jw.org.

Kɛ atsɔɔɔ gbɛ kroko nɔ lɛ, Ŋmalɛ mli wiemɔi ni atsɛ yisɛɛ lɛ jɛ ŋmɛnɛŋmɛnɛ beaŋ Jeŋ Muu Fɛɛ Shishitsɔɔmɔ Hee ni kɔɔ Septuagint Krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ he lɛ mli.
