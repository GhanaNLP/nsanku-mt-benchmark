Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ASƐM BƐN NA AKETEKYIRE, KRAKET no HYƐN N'ANOM

AKETEKYIRE, Nkran no, ne Ananse, Nkran no, fii ase kae sɛ wɔrekɔhwehwɛ aburoo akɔdua. Na wɔn nsa kaa aburoo nkran abien pɛ. Na Ananse kae sɛ, "Merekɔdua me de". Na Nkran no kae sɛ, "Merekɔtɔ me de no. " Ananse kɔɔ kɔdua n'ade. Nkran no nso faa n'ade, na ɔde too gya no so. Na ɛyɛ bere tenten na wɔretoto no, na ɔtenaa hɔ (ne nkyɛn). Nkran no nso, ɔkɔɔ afuw no mu; na ɔrebɛhwɛ aburoo no ase. Korɔn no kae sɛ, "Nhwɛ m'aniwa mu, anyɛ saa a wo nsateaa bɛkɔ m'aniwa mu". Ananse retwe n'ani ahwɛ ne Nkraman no; ne Nkraman no kae sɛ, "Ɛho nhia sɛ wohwɛ me, ɛnyɛ me na meyɛe". Na Ananse rebɛtetew dua bi a ɔde rebɛbɔ Nkraman no, na dua no kae sɛ, "Nsɛe me, efisɛ (sɛ woyɛ saa a) wo Nkraman no bɛkum me". Ananse baa fie bɛkaa ne ho asɛm nyinaa kyerɛɛ Nkraman no. Bere a na ɔreserew no, ɔkɔhwee ogya no mu. Saa kwan yi so na Nkraman no hyew ne se. Eyi ne m'asɛm, a maka ho asɛm, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
