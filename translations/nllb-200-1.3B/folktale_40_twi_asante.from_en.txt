Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Twɛn Wɔ Hɔ Na Wubehu

Ɔbea bi ne ne babea bi tenaa ase, na bere a ɔma no kwan no, na ɔka sɛ: "Merenkɔ". Na ne maame ka sɛ: "Twɛn na wobehu". Na bere biara a ɔma no kwan no, na ɔka sɛ: "Merenkɔ". Na ne maame ka kyerɛ no sɛ: "Twɛn na wobehu". Akutu no bisaa no sɛ: "Ɛdɛn nti na bere a obi mmae ha da no, afei woaba ha?" Abofra no kae sɛ: "Me ne me maame te hɔ daa; ɔkae sɛ: ' Twɛn wɔ hɔ na wubehu,' na mekae sɛ mɛba abɛhwehwɛ ' nea mɛhu' no, na mahwɛ". Akutu no kae sɛ: "Me nso meyɛ wɔn mu biako, nanso yɛn mu kɛse no wɔ akyirikyiri". Abofra no san fii hɔ kɔe. Bere a na ɔrekɔ no, ohui sɛ nkyenenkyene reba ne nkyɛn. Nkyenenkyene no bisaa no sɛ: "Dɛn nti na wunam ɔkwan yi so, bere a obiara ntwaa saa kwan yi da no? " Abofra no buae sɛ, "Me maame kae daa sɛ, 'Mehwɛ,' enti mekae sɛ merebɛhwehwɛ 'Mehwɛ' na mahwɛ. Glowing-Sparks kae sɛ, Dɛn nti, bere a obiara mma ha da no, na woaba ha? Abofra no buae sɛ, Bere a me ne me maame te hɔ no, ɔka sɛ, Mɛhwɛ, enti mekae sɛ mɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ I-will-see, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a metumi ahwɛ no. Glowing-Sparks kae sɛ, Meyɛ Wɔn mu biako, nanso kɔ w'anim, efisɛ wɔn mu kɛse no wɔ hɔ. Abofra no fii hɔ bio. Bere a ɔkɔe no, (na) Nkramofo no rekyekyere fie bi. Nkramofo no kae sɛ, Afei yɛbɛhwehwɛ obi mogya de agu fie yi so. Afei bere a abofra no duu hɔ no, wɔbisaa no sɛ, Adɛn nti, bere a obiara mmaa ha da no, afei na woaba ha? Ɔbuaa sɛ, Me ne me maame te hɔ daa; ɔkae sɛ, Twɛn wɔ hɔ na wubehu, enti mekae sɛ mɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ Mɛhwɛ, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a metumi ahwɛ.Afei Nkramofo no faa abofra no, na wotwaa ne ti, na wɔfaa ne mogya no hwie guu kɔbere anwenne mu; wɔfaa bi de guu fie no so. Afei Nkramofo no tu fii hɔ, na wɔtraa dua tenten bi so, na wɔbaa biako akyi biako, na wɔde wɔn duaw guu mogya mu, na wɔn duaw no yɛɛ kɔkɔɔ. Nkramofo no nyinaa faa wɔn duaw no, na wɔde wɔn duaw guu mu, sɛ adeyɛ no ho nkae. Afei, da koro, Nkramofo no mu biako kae sɛ ɔrebɛdidi. Ɔkɔhyiaa abofra no maame. Na ne maame bisaa Nkramofo no sɛ, "Me ba a me ne no tenae no yɛ ɔkwadwo te sɛ biribiara. Sɛ mesɔre a, na meka sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔtwɛn wɔ hɔ na obehu; no, te sɛ ɔkwasea, kyɛ me sɛ mede asɛm no di dwuma no, ɔkae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ I-will-see, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛhwɛ no. Nnawɔtwe atwam nnɛ na manhu no, na mesrɛ wo, sɛ woahu no a, fa no brɛ me. Parrot no kae sɛ, Oh, ɔbae yɛn akuraa no ase, na yɛn nso, yekum no. Ne mogya bi, a yɛfaa no de kae asɛm no, wɔ me dua yi so. Enti, sɛ wokɔ a, kɔ na kɔka kyerɛ wo manfoɔ nyinaa sɛ, sɛ obi ka kyerɛ obi sɛ, 'Twɛn wɔ hɔ na wobɛhunu' a, ɛnneɛ na wabɔ no nnome.

Eyi, m'asɛm no, a maka ho asɛm no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
