Freshwater

THERE were three young men together; we stood beside the bride. And these three young men came out of Bontuku to meet Odwa, and he said to them, Let us fight. And we came to Odwa, and he fought with a sword. And he gave the sword, and he took medicine, and touched the sword, and breathed. And he said, Let us kill the bride, and he ate the sword. And he said, Let our bride die. One said, I will give her our medicine, and we will fight with Bontuku. And another said, Let us take your medicine, and we will kill her. And another said, Let us take your medicine, and we will kill her. Then he stretched out his hand, took some vinegar and put it on the man's armpit. Then he said, "Wake up, O dead man! " And he woke up. "Which of these three was healed?"
