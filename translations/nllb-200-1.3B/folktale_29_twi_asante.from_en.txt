Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ASƐM A ƐDA SO Ɔbarima Bi Nyaa Ɔyere (Na Ɔsan Gyee Ne Nkwa)

Ɛberɛ bi, na mmarima baasa bi wɔ hɔ, na na wɔpɛ adwuma. Wɔkɔ kɔtɔɔ nkyekye ntade ne nneɛma afoforo wɔ asase tenten no so. (Wɔsan kɔɔ Ashanti no, wɔmaa wɔn nuabarima no soaa nneɛma no bi, na wɔkɔɔ so tɔn maa wɔn. Wɔkɔɔ kurow kɛse bi mu. Na Ɔhemmaa Maame babea bi wɔ kurow no mu a ɔpoo nea wɔde no maa no sɛ ɔnware no. Afei ahɔho yi (adwumayɛfo no) kɔɔ hɔ (ne fie), nanso Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no kɔɔ hɔ sɛ "ɔrekɔ" ahohoro nneɛma. Ɔhemmaa Maame no tɔɔ ntade abien na ɔde guu nkyɛn maa ne babea. Bere a ne ba no san bae no, ohuu mmarima baasa no. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere panyin no sɛ: "Hwɛ, mahu obi a m'ani gye ne ho, enti kum oguan bi na memfa nkɔma wɔn". Wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: Kyerɛ me ase. Ɔbaabun no buaa sɛ: Ababaa no se, wayɛ ne ho fitaa te sɛ atoko, na sɛ onya yɛn a yɛaba ha yi mu bi a, ɛnsɛ sɛ ɔware? Asono nwura no kyerɛ sɛ, da bi bɛba a ɔbɛyera; kola dua no nso kyerɛ sɛ da bi bɛba a ɔbɛma no (ne kunu) adi kola (ɛne sɛ, ɔbɛdi awerɛhoɔ). Asono dua no kyerɛ sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ wodua asono dua ma no, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a afe biara, sɛ ɔkɔ kɔhwɛ ntini no a, obenya bi a ɔbɛtwe". Enti Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no waree mmarima no wɔfase. Bere a ɔwaree no no, Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no bisaa no sɛ, "Wo din de sɛn?" Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Me din de Susu Kwakufuo," a ɛkyerɛ sɛ, Kwaku-nea ɔgye-haw-to-adwene. Abaayewa no buae sɛ, "Me nso, wɔfrɛ me Akua-nea-fa-nhaw-ne-adwene". Ɔwaree no wɔ ayeforohyia a ɛfata mu. Ade kyee anɔpa no, na n'asomfo baasa no rekɔ wɔn kurom. Kwaku-nea-fa-nhaw-ne-adwene tenaa ne yere akuraa no ase. Ɔtenaa hɔ afe, na bere a ɔsɔree (adekyee) no, ɔne ne nnamfonom tenaa ase na wɔbɔɔ nteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛteɛ Ɔsan hwɛɛ n'akyi, na ohuu abofra bi sɛ ogyina hɔ, na ɔfaa ɔbo de maa abofra no sɛ ɔmfa nkɔma ne yere. Afei bere a ne yere nsa kaa ɔbo no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Me kunu pɛ sɛ ɔsan kɔ ne kurom, efisɛ ɔka sɛ sɛ anomaa tena fam kyɛ dodo a, onya ɔbo". Na Ɔhemmaa Maame kae sɛ, "Sɛ ɔkɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ wo nso wo ne no kɔ". Na ɛno akyi no, wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: Kwaku, wo a w'adwene yɛ wo basabasa, fa hama bi ma yɛn, na yɛnkɔ nnua no mu, na yɛnkɔkyere mmorɔnoma, na yɛmmɛhwɛ wo yere a ɔyɛ ɔhɔho no. Na wɔkɔe, nanso mpanyimfo no yɛɛ wɔn adwene sɛ wobekum no. Na wɔne Kwaku-ɔno-a-gye-nsɛm-di no kɔɔ so, kɔsii sɛ wɔduruu (kwan no so baabi a ɛhɔ yɛ anika) no, wɔkyekyeree no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Yebɛkum wo". Afei ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ: "Bere a mekɔɔ me yere akuraa no ase no, mihuu sika pii, na sɛ woamma mankyerɛ wo baabi a ɛwɔ, na miwu a, worenhunu". Wɔbisaa no sɛ: "Ɛhe na sika no wɔ?" Obuae sɛ: "Ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ me yere nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛ no sɛ matɔ nam, na (adwumayɛfoɔ no ka sɛ ne bo yɛ £80), na sɛ ɔhwehwɛ a, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔhwehwɛ kotoku a wɔde nnwan ntonto ayɛ no mu; Na ɔno nso kae sɛ, Nneɛma no wɔ akuraa no mu panyin fie, enti momma yɛnkɔfa. Nneɛma no kɔɔ akuraa no mu panyin fie; na ɔbea no kaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ akuraa no mu panyin no sɛ, Nneɛma no a wɔde nnwennini ayɛ no kyerɛ sɛ wɔakyekyere no; Krampan no kyerɛ sɛ asukɔnkɔn pɛ sɛ wɔwe ne nam; abɔso a ekura sekan no kyerɛ sɛ wɔrebebubu ne kɔn. Ɛhɔ ara na ɔpanin no bɔɔ gong-gong, sɛ wɔnkɔ hɔ nkɔgye Kwaku-nea-fa-haw-a-dwen. Na wɔne no san bae. Ɔmaa wɔkum mpanimfoɔ baasa no. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, Ɛnam agyiraehyɛde asekyerɛ so no obi nyaa ɔyere na (ɔgyee ne nkwa nso) Eyi ne m'asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
