Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛ Obi Sɛe Biribi a Ɛyɛ Wo De a, (Ɛnyɛ Nhwɛso) Ɛnsɛ sɛ Wugye Sika Fi Obi Hɔ

WƆKA sɛ bere bi a atwam no, adwennini bi tenaa faako a wɔn maame wui gyaw wɔn (ako) no. Ná wɔreyɛ afahyɛ bi, na adwennini panyin no (Ata 'Panin) faa nhwiren a ne bo yɛ den ne sika nketewa bi de bobɔɔ Ata Kuma, adwennini kumaa no kɔn ho. Adwennini kumaa no ne ne nnamfo no kae sɛ wɔrebɛkɔ asubɔnten no ho akɔhohoro wɔn ho. Bere a na wɔrehohoro wɔn ho no, ahama a wɔde sika nketewa no akyekyere Ata Kuma kɔn ho no bubui, na adwennini no guguu nsu no mu. Ɔhwehwɛɛ sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ biara no, wantumi anhu. Ɔsan baa fie bɛbɔɔ ne nuabea amanneɛ sɛ, "Sika nneɛma no ayera". Ata 'Panin (twin panyin no) kae sɛ, "Minnye nni sɛ ayera, na sɛ ayera mpo a, biribi wɔ hɔ a mɛhwehwɛ afi wo hɔ". Ata Kuma (twin ketewa no) kae sɛ, "Dɛn na wobɛhwehwɛ afi me hɔ?" Ata Kuma kae sɛ: "Metee". Ata Kuma fii hɔ kɔhwehwɛɛ nneɛma yi, na oduu baabi a na dua biako pɛ wɔ hɔ. Bere a dua no huu no no, ɔkae sɛ: "Hwɛ, Ata Kuma ni, (nea wɔka ne ho asɛm no) ɔresan afi kurow no akyi te sɛ osu a ɛretɔ no". (Ata Kuma kae sɛ: "Nea enti a mebaa ha ara ne sɛ agya no ba yɛ Ata 'Panin, na ɛna no ba yɛ Ata 'Panin, na ɔkyekyeree nhwiren ahorow de bɔɔ me kɔn ho, na ɛpaapaee na ɔyerae". (Hwɛ Ata Kuma wɔ dwom yi mu):

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ nhaban bi afi dua bi a ne nhwiren yɛ biako pɛ no mu.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ nkratafa bi afi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ nnomaa a wɔfrɛ wɔn Aserewa adaka no.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ Nsu, na ɔrebɔ ponponponponsa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ asono Ɔhemmaa Maame no dua, kosi ne ntini so.

Na dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ, agya, na manya bi?

Ɔka sɛ abofra ketewa bi, Akwasi Toboro Kojo babea.

Kojo Nsa.

Agya Elephant, ɛbɛyɛ papa sɛ wubeyi wo ho afi mu, ɛbɛyɛ papa sɛ wufi hɔ.

Kojo Nsa a a.

Na dua a Nkorabere wɔ dua so no kae sɛ, "Eyi nti na woresu, bra na nya biribi". Na Ata Kuma kɔɔ kɔfaa (dua a na ne ho wɔ nkratafa biako pɛ) na ɔdaa no ase (dua no). Na ɔkɔɔ so, na ohyiaa Aserewa nnomaa. Na Aserewa anomaa no kae sɛ, Hwɛ, Ata Kuma ni, a sɛ oyi ne ho adi wɔ kurow no mfinimfini a, obiara ka sɛ ɔte sɛ osu a ɛrebɛtɔ. Ata Kuma buae sɛ, Ade biako pɛ nti a mebaa ha ne sɛ agya no ba yɛ Ata Panin, na ɛna no ba yɛ Ata Panin, na ɔde nhwiren ahorow a ɛyɛ fɛ bobɔɔ me ho, na ɛpaapaee na ɔyerae. (Na ha na Ata Kuma fii ase too dwom):

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ nhaban bi afi dua bi a ne nhwiren yɛ biako pɛ no mu.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ nnomaa a wɔfrɛ wɔn Aserewa adaka no.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ Nsu, na ɔrebɔ ponponponponsa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ Agya Leopard nsa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ asono Ɔhemmaa Maame no dua, kosi ne ntini so.

Na dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ, agya, na manya bi?

Ɔka sɛ abofra ketewa bi, Akwasi Toboro Kojo babea.

Kojo Nsa.

Agya Elephant, ɛbɛyɛ papa sɛ wubeyi wo ho afi mu, ɛbɛyɛ papa sɛ wufi hɔ.

Kojo Nsa a a.

Aserewa anomaa no kae sɛ, Ɛno nti na wusu, bra na gye biribi. Na Ata Kuma kɔɔ na ogyee (Aserewa anomaa no afuru), na ɔdaa no ase. Na osiim kɔe, na ohyiaa Nsu, ponponponponsa a ɔrekasa. Ɔkae sɛ, Hwɛ Ata Kuma a, sɛ oyi ne ho adi wɔ akuraa no awiei a, nkurɔfo gye no fɛw so te sɛ osu a ɛresɛe no. Ata Kuma kaa asɛm a na waka akyerɛ afoforo no kyerɛɛ no. Na Gurgling-water maa no bi (ne nsu a ɛrekasa no), na ogyee, na ɔkɔɔ so. Akyiri no, ohyiaa Leopard no. Bere a Akraman no huu no no, ɔkae sɛ, Hwɛ, Ata Kuma ni; bere a oyii ne ho adi wɔ akuraa no kwan ano no, na ɔte sɛ osu a ɛresosɔ no. Ata Kuma ka n'adwuma kyerɛɛ no. Ɔkae sɛ, Bra na gye biribi, (na ɔde ne nsateaa no biako maa no). Afei Ata Kuma kae sɛ, "Agya Akwatafo, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ na ama manya Elephant Queen Maame no dua?" Ɔkae sɛ, "Ah, bere a worekɔ no, mmoa no nyinaa akɔda, na Elephant Queen Maame no akɔda mfinimfini hɔ, na bere a worekɔ no, di wɔn so pintinn, na sɛ wokɔ Elephant Queen Maame no nkyɛn a, twa ne dua no, nanso sɛ wudi wɔn so pintinn a, wobekum wo". Ata Kuma kae sɛ, "Meda wo ase". Na bere a ɔrekɔ no, ohuu mmoa yi; ɔtiatiaa wɔn so pintinn, na oduu Queen Mother no nkyɛn. Ɔtwaa ne dua no, na ɔdan n'ani. Na ɔtɔɔ ahwehwɛ bi, na ɔfaa no. Na oduu fie no, ɔkae sɛ: Ata 'Panin, eyinom ne nneɛma a wokae sɛ wubegye afi me nsam no. Ata 'Panin kae sɛ: Me nsa na aka wɔn. Na da bi a wɔte faako no, Ata Kuma faa ahwehwɛ no, na ɔhwɛɛ mu. Ata Kuma kae sɛ: "Ɛdefa eyi ho no, nea wunya fii me hɔ no, mɛhwehwɛ afi wo hɔ". Afei Ata 'Panin kɔe kɔfaa nneɛma a Ata Kuma de brɛɛ no no. Ata Kuma kae sɛ: "Mempɛ (eyinom), nea mepɛ ne sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa foforo aba ama me". Ata 'Panin fii hɔ, na ɔkɔe na ohyiaa dua bi a ne nhwiren yɛ biako. Ɔkae sɛ: "Hwɛ, Ata 'Panin ni, sɛ oyi ne ho adi wɔ akuraa no awiei a, ogye no fɛw sɛ osu". Ata 'Panin buae sɛ: "M'ayi me ho adi akyerɛ wo maame! Na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: Kɔ, hwɛ, obi wɔ hɔ a onim nea ɛbɛyɛ wo yiye. Na ɔkɔe, na ɔhunuu anomaa bi. Aserewa nnomaa no kae sɛ, "Hwɛ Ata 'Panin; bere a oyii ne ho adi wɔ akuraa no kwan ano no, na ne ho yɛ dɛ te sɛ osu". Ata 'Panin kae sɛ, "Bere a 'meyi me ho adi' wɔ wo maame ne w'agya anim no, nea mepɛ ara ne mo adididaka no mu biako, na ma menkɔ". Gurgling-water kae sɛ: "Hwɛ Ata 'Panin; bere a oyii ne ho adi wɔ kurow no ano no, ɔte sɛ osu a ɛresosɔ". Ata 'Panin kae sɛ: "Bere a 'meyi me ho adi' wɔ wo maame anim, bere a 'meyi me ho adi' wɔ w'agya anim no, me de, ma me wo nsu no bi na ma menkɔ". Gurgling-water kae sɛ: "Twiw kɔ anim, nea onim (nea ɛsɛ sɛ ɔne wo di) no wɔ hɔ". Afei ɔne Akraman no hyiae. Akraman no kae sɛ: "Hwɛ Ata 'Panin, bere a oyii ne ho adi wɔ abɔnten no ano no, ɔte sɛ osu a ɛresosɔ". Ata 'Panin buae sɛ: "Meyi me ho adi wɔ wo maame ne w'agya anim! Me deɛ, fa wo nsateaa no bi ma me, na ma menkɔ". Akwata no kae sɛ: "Nea onim [sɛnea ɔne wo bedi dwuma] no wɔ hɔ". Na osiim kɔe, na ohyiaa asono kuw bi, na Asono Ɔhemmaa no da mfinimfini hɔ. Afei ɔkɔɔ so twaa ne dua. Asono kuw no nyinaa kyeree no, na wotwitwaa no so, na owui. Ata Kuma hwehwɛɛ ne nuabea no wɔ kwan no so. Wamma. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ: "Sɛ wo ne w'abusuafo bi te hɔ, na sɛ wɔn mu bi sisi biribi a ɛyɛ wo dea a, ɛnsɛ sɛ wotua ka, efisɛ 'Panin na ɔyɛɛ saa, na Ata Kuma kɔe na owui".

Eyi, m'asɛm no, a maka ho asɛm no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
