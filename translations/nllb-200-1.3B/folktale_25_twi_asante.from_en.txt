Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛ Obi Sɛe Biribi a Ɛyɛ Wo De a, (Ɛnyɛ Nhwɛso) Ɛnsɛ sɛ Wugye Sika Fi Obi Hɔ

WƆKA sɛ bere bi a atwam no, na awaregyafo a wɔn maame awu gyaw wɔn (ako) te faako, na na wɔreyɛ afahyɛ bi, na awaregyafo panyin (Ata 'Panin) no faa sika kɔkɔɔ ne nhwiren a ɛsom bo bi de kyekyeree akumaa no, Ata Kuma, kɔn ho. Bere a na wɔreguare no, ahama a wɔde sika kɔkɔɔ ahyɛn Ata Kuma kɔn mu no mu paee, na ahonyade no guu nsu no mu. Ɔhwehwɛɛ mu ara na wantumi anhu. Ɔsan baa fie bɛbɔɔ ne nuabea amanneɛ sɛ, "Sika nneɛma no ayera". Ata 'Panin (twin panyin no) kae sɛ, "Minnye nni sɛ ayera, na sɛ ayera mpo a, biribi wɔ hɔ a mɛhwehwɛ afi wo hɔ". Ata Kuma (twin ketewa no) kae sɛ, "Dɛn na wobɛhwehwɛ afi me hɔ?" Ata Kuma kae sɛ: "Mate". Ata Kuma fii ase (hwehwɛɛ nneɛma yi), na oduu baabi pɔtee bi a na dua biako pɛ wɔ hɔ. Bere a dua no huu no no, ɔkae sɛ: "Hwɛ, Ata Kuma ni, (nea wɔka ne ho asɛm sɛ) ɔde ne ho adi wɔ kurow no akyi te sɛ osu a ɛretɔ". (Ata Kuma) kae sɛ: "Nea enti a mebaa ha ara ne sɛ agya no ba yɛ Ata 'Panin, na ɛna no ba yɛ Ata 'Panin, na ɔkyekyeree nhwiren ahorow de bɔɔ me kɔn ho, na ɛpaapaee na ɔyerae". g) Nea edidi so yi:

Ɔrehwehwɛ nhaban bi afi dua bi a ne nhwiren yɛ biako pɛ no mu.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ nkratafa bi afi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ nnomaa a wɔfrɛ wɔn Aserewa adaka no.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ Nsu, na ɔrebɔ ponponponponsa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ asono Ɔhemmaa Maame no dua, kosi ne ntini so.

Na dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ, agya, na manya bi?

Ɔka sɛ abofra ketewa bi, Akwasi Toboro Kojo babea.

Kojo Nsa.

Agya Elephant, ɛbɛyɛ papa sɛ wubeyi wo ho afi mu, ɛbɛyɛ papa sɛ wufi hɔ.

Kojo Nsa a a.

Na dua a Nkorabere wɔ dua so no kae sɛ, "Eyi nti na woresu, bra na nya biribi". Na Ata Kuma kɔɔ kɔfaa (dua a na ne ho wɔ nkratafa biako pɛ) na ɔdaa no ase (dua no). Na ɔkɔɔ so, na ohyiaa Aserewa nnomaa. Na Aserewa anomaa no kae sɛ, Hwɛ, Ata Kuma ni, a sɛ oyi ne ho adi wɔ kurow no mfinimfini a, obiara ka sɛ ɔte sɛ osu a ɛrebɛtɔ. Ata Kuma buae sɛ, Ade biako pɛ nti a mebaa ha ne sɛ agya no ba yɛ Ata Panin, na ɛna no ba yɛ Ata Panin, na ɔkyekyeree nhwiren ahorow de bɔɔ me ho hyiae, na sɛee na ɔyerae. wɔ dwom mu):

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ nhaban bi afi dua bi a ne nhwiren yɛ biako pɛ no mu.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ nnomaa a wɔfrɛ wɔn Aserewa adaka no.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ Nsu, na ɔrebɔ ponponponponsa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ Agya Leopard nsa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Na dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ, agya, na manya bi?

Ɔka sɛ abofra ketewa bi, Akwasi Toboro Kojo babea.

Kojo Nsa.

Agya Elephant, ɛbɛyɛ papa sɛ wubeyi wo ho afi mu, ɛbɛyɛ papa sɛ wufi hɔ.

Kojo Nsa a a.

Aserewa anomaa no kae sɛ, Ɛno nti na wusu, bra na gye biribi. Na Ata Kuma kɔɔ na ogyee (Aserewa anomaa no ntini), na ɔdaa no ase. Na ofii hɔ, na ohyiaa Nsu, ponponponponsa a ɔrekasa. Ɔkaa sɛ, Hwɛ Ata Kuma, a sɛ oyi ne ho adi wɔ akuraa no kwan ano a, nkurɔfo gye no fɛw so te sɛ osu a ɛrebɛtɔ. Ata Kuma kaa asɛm a na waka akyerɛ afoforo no kyerɛɛ no. Na Gurgling-water maa no bi (ne gurgling nsu no bi), na ɔfaa no kɔɔ so. Ɛno akyi no, ohyiaa Leopard no. Bere a Akraman no huu no no, ɔkae sɛ, Hwɛ, Ata Kuma ni; bere a oyii ne ho adi wɔ akuraa no kwan ano no, na ɔte sɛ osu a ɛresosɔ no. Ata Kuma ka n'adwuma kyerɛɛ no. Ɔkae sɛ, Bra na gye biribi, (na ɔde ne nsateaa no biako maa no). Afei Ata Kuma kae sɛ, "Agya Akwatafo, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ na ama manya Elephant Queen Maame no dua?" Ɔkae sɛ, "Ah, bere a worekɔ no, mmoa no nyinaa akɔda, na Elephant Queen Maame no akɔda mfinimfini, na bere a worekɔ no, di wɔn so den, na sɛ wokɔ Elephant Queen Maame no nkyɛn a, twa ne dua no, nanso sɛ wudi wɔn so yiye a, wɔbɛsɛe wɔn. Ata Kuma kae sɛ: "Meda wo ase". Na bere a ɔrekɔ no, ohuu mmoa yi, na ɔkunkumm wɔn denneennen, na oduu Ɔhemmaa Maame no nkyɛn. Ɔtew ne dua, na ɔsan n'akyi. Ɔkwan no mu no ɔtɔɔ ahwehwɛ bi, na ɔde kɔɔ (fie). Na oduu fie. Ɔkae sɛ: "Ata 'Panin, eyinom ne nneɛma a wokae sɛ wubegye afi me nsam no". Ata 'Panin kae sɛ: "Me nsa aka wɔn". Da koro a na wɔtete faako no, Ata Kuma faa ahwehwɛ no hwɛɛ mu. Ɔkae sɛ: "Saa ahwehwɛ yi, sɛ obi hwɛ mu a, ohu ɛna a ɔwɔ Ahonhom asase so". Ata 'Panin kae sɛ: "Ɛyɛ saa, fa brɛ me, na ma menhwɛ". Ɔde maa no. Bere a na ɔrebɛhwɛ mu no, egyaa mu fii ne nsam, na ɛpaee. Ata Kuma kae sɛ: "Ɛdefa eyi ho no, nea wugyee fii me hɔ no, mɛhwehwɛ afi wo hɔ". Afei Ata 'Panin kɔɔ kɔfaa nneɛma a Ata Kuma de brɛɛ no no. Ata Kuma kae sɛ: "Mempɛ (eyinom), nea mepɛ ne sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa foforo ama me". Ata 'Panin fii hɔ, na ɔkɔe kohyiaa dua bi a ne nhwiren yɛ biako. Ɔkaa sɛ: "Ɛha na Ata 'Panin wɔ, na sɛ ɔba wɔ akuraa no kwan ano a, wɔgye no fɛw so te sɛ osu". Ata 'Panin buae sɛ: "Manya 'ayi me ho adi' akyerɛ wo maame! ne w'agya! Aserewa nnomaa no kae sɛ, "Hwɛ Ata 'Panin; bere a oyii ne ho adi wɔ akuraa no kwan ano no, na ne ho yɛ dɛ te sɛ osu". Ata 'Panin kae sɛ, "Bere a 'meyi me ho adi' wɔ wo maame ne w'agya anim no, nea mepɛ ara ne mo adididaka no mu biako, na ma menkɔ". Gurgling-water kae sɛ: "Hwɛ Ata 'Panin; bere a oyii ne ho adi wɔ kurow no ano no, ɔte sɛ osu a ɛretɔ". Ata 'Panin kae sɛ: "Sɛ meyi me ho adi kyerɛ wo maame, sɛ meyi me ho adi kyerɛ w'agya a, fa wo nsu no bi ma me na ma menkɔ". Gurgling-water kae sɛ: "Kɔ anim, nea onim (sɛ ɛbɛyɛ dɛn ne wo adi) no wɔ hɔ". Akraman no kae sɛ, "Ɛha na Ata 'Panin wɔ, na sɛ oyi ne ho adi wɔ abɔnten no awiei a, ne ho yɛ dɛ te sɛ osu". Ata 'Panin buae sɛ, "Me 'yi me ho adi' sɛ wo maame! ne w'agya! Me deɛ, fa wo nsateaa no bi ma me na ma menkɔ". Akraman no kae sɛ: "Nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi no) wɔ hɔ". Ofii hɔ no, ohyiaa asono kuw bi, na Asono Ɔhemmaa no da mfinimfini hɔ. Afei ɔkɔɔ so twaa ne dua. Asono kuw no nyinaa kyeree no, na wotwaa no so, na owui. Ata Kuma hwehwɛɛ ne nuabea no wɔ kwan no so. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Sɛ wo ne w'abusuafo bi te hɔ, na wɔn mu bi sɛe biribi a ɛyɛ wo dea a, ɛnsɛ sɛ wotua ka, efisɛ Ata 'Panin na ɔyɛɛ saa, na owui".

Eyi, m'asɛm no, a maka ho asɛm no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
