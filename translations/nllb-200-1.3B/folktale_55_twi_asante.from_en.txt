ASƐM A ƐKYERƐKYERƐ A Ɛde Kɔɔ Abusuakuw no Mu

Ɔkɔm kɛse bi bae, na Ntikuma fii hɔ, kae sɛ ɔrebɛhwehwɛ aduan de akɔma n'agya, Agya Ananse, Nkyekye no. Ofii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ akuraa bi ase; (ɔhɔ na ohyiaa Akwakoraa-ɔbea-nananom. Ɔbea panyin no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Me ba, sɛ obiara ntumi mma ha a, adɛn nti na woaba ha?" Ntikuma buae sɛ, "Ɔkɔm nti, na merehwehwɛ biribi a mɛkɔ adi". Ɔbea panyin nana kae sɛ, "Kɔ me afuw mu wɔ hɔ, nanso yam a ɛbɛka sɛ, Pulk me, pluck me, don't pluck it, nanso yam a ɛbɛka sɛ, Don't pluck me, don't pluck me, pluck it and bring it. Ntikuma kɔe. Ntikuma ka kyerɛɛ wɔn a wɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Mommfa mo nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa nnka me nsa". Ntikuma kae sɛ, Mawie, nanso ma me nam a mede bɛhyɛ mu. Ɔbea panyin nana kae sɛ, Kɔ na kɔka kyerɛ Nkwakuo, Apapafo a wɔwɔ dua no so no sɛ Ɔbea panyin nana se, mo nyinaa, mo maamenom! Apapafo no kae sɛ, Sɛ ɛnyɛ Ɔbea panyin nana nti a, anka ɛsɛ sɛ yekum mo. Ɔbea panyin nana kae sɛ, Nea ɛfa me ho no, ɛnyɛ m'asɛm, na mo de, mommra mmɛhwe no. Mmoa no baa fam sɛ wɔrebɛhwe no. Akwakoraa-ɔbea-nananom no kae sɛ, Me-Sharp-mataa, twa saa mmoa yi ti ma me. Afei The-sharp-one twaa mmoa no ti. Ntikuma boaboaa mmoa no ti nyinaa ano, na oyii yams bi kaa ho, na ɔde wɔn nyinaa siim kɔɔ ne kurom. Agya Spider huu (ne ba) Ntikuma sɛ ogyina n'anim, na ɔkae sɛ, Mema wo akwaaba, ant-hill breaker (i.e. abakan). Ɔtoaa so sɛ, Ma mensoa wo wɔ m'akyi. Ntikuma kɔɔ hɔ kɔsɛnkyee hɔ. Afei Ananse kɔe kɔfaa yam biako; ɔkae sɛ, Bere a menya asram foforo no, mɛdi eyi. (Ne yere) Aso kae sɛ, Efi bere a Ɔbɔadeɛ no bɔɔ nneɛma no, so woahu ɔbarima bi a wanya asram foforo? Ananse ne ne yere, ne ne mma no wiee yam no di, na nea aka ara ne nea na ɛyɛ Papa Sekan dea no. Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma no daa adi no, Ananse kae sɛ, Manya asram foforo no. Na ɔkɔtraa fie no akyi, na ɔnoa yam no, na odidii kosii sɛ (ne nyinaa) wiee. Ntikuma kae sɛ, "Mɛsan akɔ". Ananse kae sɛ, "Me ne wo bɛkɔ". Ntikuma kae sɛ, "Me ne wo renkɔ. " Bere a Ntikuma kɔdae no, Ananse kɔfaa aboa nhoma kotoku bi, na ɔde mpomma nketewa hyehyɛɛ mu, na ɔde nsõ hyɛɛ mu, na ɔde guu Ntikuma ntama no ano. Bere a nneɛma no daa adi no, Ntikuma fii kwan no so ntɛm ara. Bere a ohui wɔn no, odii wɔn akyi, na na ɔrekɔ no, bere a, na Ntikuma ne Ɔpanyin-Ɔbea-Nananom te hɔ no. Ananse kae sɛ, "Wugye di sɛ meyɛ ɔkwasea, mpo me ba Ntikuma bae, na woama no bi". Ananse kae sɛ, "Kɔ afuw no so wɔ hɔ; nkyenenkyene a wɔka sɛ, 'Twe me, twe me, twe wɔn' no, mfa wɔn, na mmom nea wɔka sɛ, 'Mma me, twe me, twe wɔn no". nea nkwaseasɛm yɛ ne sɛ wo ka kyerɛ me, sɛ obi ka sɛ ɔdɔ obi a, wo hyɛ me sɛ menka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mekyi no, mentee biribi a ɛte saa da? Nanso Spider no kɔɔ afuw no so. Bere a oduu hɔ no, yams no bi kae sɛ, Twe me, twe me, afoforo nso kae bio sɛ, Mfa me, ntwe me. Na ɔtwee wɔn a wɔkae sɛ, Twe me, twe me, na ɔne wɔn baa fie. Ɔbae na owiee wɔn ntwe; Ɔbea panyin-nananom kae sɛ, Kɔ na tow aduan pa no kyene, na fa mpopaho no, de gu kotoku no mu, na fa gu gya no so. Ananse buae sɛ, Ah, asɛm bɔne bɛn na woka kyerɛ me yi; esiane sɛ Ɔbɔadeɛ no na ɔbɔɔ nneɛma nti, so yɛtow aduan pa no kyene, na yɛde mpopaho no sie? Bere a wɔhyew aduan no, oyii, na ne nyinaa dan abo, nanso aboa nhoma biako a ɔde too so no dan aduan pa. Agya N'abranka kae sɛ: "Hwɛ! Ɔdebɔneyɛfo yi a wɔsan no baa nkwa mu no nni atoro (nyinaa akyi no)". Na N'abranka no kɔkataa aboa nhoma no so de brɛɛ no, na ɔde guu gya no so. Bere a wɔhyew aduan no, oyii no fii ogya no mu, na na ayɛ yams a wɔhyew no yiye sɛnea w'ani begye ho sɛ wubehu no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔbea panyin nana, eyi nyinaa ye yiye, nanso afei ɛsɛ sɛ woma me nam de ka ho". Ɔbea panyin nana kae sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔhwɛ dua no so, Aberewa wɔ so; sɛ wokɔ a, wobɛka sɛ, 'Moyɛ mmoa.'" Nnomaa no kae sɛ, "Ɔbea yi! Me ne Aberewa no nni asɛm, na dɛn nti na ɛsɛ sɛ mekɔ na midi wɔn nya saa kwan yi so?" Ɔbea panyin nana kae sɛ, "Kɔ, na sɛ ɔhaw ba a, ɛno ne m'asɛm". Nnomaa no fii hɔ kɔe. Ɔkae sɛ: "Mo a mowɔ nnua yi so no, Nanabea no ka sɛ moyɛ mmoa". Mmoa no kae sɛ: "Sɛ ɛnyɛ Nanabea no nti a, anka yebekum mo". Nanabea no kae sɛ: "Ɛnne mommra mmekum no".

Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɔbea panyin, eyi nyɛ ade pa". Afei mmoa no bɛhwee no so na wɔbaa bɛhwee Ananse. Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ, "Me peaw The-sharp-one, twa wɔn ti". Tintin! (ne nne a peaw no yɛe bere a na ɛreyɛ n'adwuma no). Ɔbea panyin nana no kae sɛ, "Afei de, yɛ dinn". Nanso N'abran no ante (nea ɔkae no). Afei, N'abran no boaboaa nam no ano na ɔsan baa fie hɔ, na Ɔbea panyin no twee ne ho kakra. Afei Ananse kɔwiaa ne peaw no de kɔhyɛɛ ne kotoku mu, na otuu mmirika san baa ne kurom. Bere a ɔsan kɔɔ ne kurom no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne babarima no sɛ: "Ntikuma, esiane sekan a mede me nsa ato so yi nti, ma yɛnkɔ kwae no mu". Enti wɔkɔe, na wɔkɔe no, wohuu wuram mprako bi. Ananse kae sɛ: "Ntikuma, kɔ na kɔhaw wɔn. Ntikuma kɔɔ hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Bush-pigs, wo ho yɛ fĩ". Bush-pigs no buae sɛ, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ sɛ woyɛ Kwaku Ananse a, anka ɛsɛ sɛ yɛba bɛhwe wo". Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Bra bɛhwe no wɔ biribiara mu, ɔyɛ onipa bɔne, ne animguase no nyɛ anigyede". Bush-pigs no rebɛhwe no, bere a Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Old woman's Knife, The-sharp-one, cut off their heads for me". Oh! na Knife no bɛhwee wɔn so, na otwaa mmoa no nyinaa ti. Afei ofii ase twaa nnua no. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Hwɛ! dɛn na woreyɛ yi? Meka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ wɔntwa mmoa no ti, na wowiee nea woreyɛ no, na afei na eyi nyinaa yɛ dɛn?" Afei Ntikuma hwɛɛ no kakra, na afei ɔkae sɛ, "Afei de, yɛ komm". Kwaku Ananse antie no, na Nkrante no baa fam, na Ananse kɔɔ kɔfaa no. Wɔsan kɔɔ fie. Ade kyee, bere a nneɛma no daa adi no, Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ wɔbɛkɔ ɔsoro-nyame a ɔwɔ hɔ no nkyɛn. Ntikuma kae sɛ, "Agya, sɛ ɛyɛ afoa yi nti a, nkɔ". Ananse kae sɛ, "Fi hɔ kɔ". Ntikuma kae sɛ, "Kɔ w'ankasa kwan so". Ananse fii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ɔsoro-nyame a ɔwɔ hɔ no nkyɛn, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Owura, ɛnnɛ, sɛ obi afom wo a, me nko ara, me, Kwaku Ananse, metwa wɔn nyinaa ti da koro na mede bɛma wo". Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Aseda afɔre wɔ hɔ ma obiara a obetumi ayɛ saa, efisɛ atamfo dɔm kɛse bi gyina kurow no akyi, enti kɔ". Ananse kae sɛ, "Ma wɔn nsa ne oguan baako". Ananse fii kwan no so; Ananse bisaa atamfo dɔm no sɛ, Mo na moka sɛ mo ne Ɔsoro-nyame no rebɛko? Wɔbuaa sɛ, Yiw, ɛno na yɛka. Afei Ɔsoro-nyame no dɔm gyina Ananse akyi. Afei Kwaku Ananse ka kyerɛɛ Ɔbaabun s Knife, The-sharp-one, sɛ, Twa atamfo dɔm yi ti ma me. Na Nkrante no twaa wɔn ti, tan! tan! na wiee. Ananse kae sɛ, Ɛyɛ papa, gyae. Nanso Nkrante no tow guu Ɔsoro-nyame no dɔm so na na ɛretwa (wɔn ti). Ananse kae sɛ, Oh, ɛno ne Ɔsoro-nyame no dɔm, enti gyae. Nanso etwaa so. Ananse kae sɛ, Sɛ wo kokom ayɛ den a, ma no nyɛ frɔmfrɔm. Oh! Ɔsoro-anyame dɔm no nyinaa yerae. Afei Nkrante no twaa wim; ɛbaa baabi a Kwaku Ananse wɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, Hwɛ, wunnim me anaa? Wunnim me anaa? Mene Kwaku Ananse, me na mede wo fi Ɔbea Panyin no nkyɛn baa hɔ. Oh! afei Kwaku Ananse ti hwehwee a, tan! Na ɔkɔɔ so kɔɔ Nkrante no so; na baabi a ɔgyinaa hɔ no dan nwura bi, a yɛfrɛ no Etene (nkrante-nwura); n'atifi yɛ hyerɛn paa, paa, paa.

Eyi, m'asɛm no, a maka ho asɛm no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
