Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ASƐM A ƐKYERƐKYERƐ A Ɛde Kɔɔ Abusuakuw no Mu

Ɔkɔm kɛse bi bae, na Ntikuma fii hɔ, kae sɛ ɔrebɛhwehwɛ aduan de akɔma n'agya, Agya Ananse, Nkyekye no. Ofii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ akuraa bi ase; (ɔhɔ na ohyiaa Akwakoraa-ɔbea-nananom. Ɔbea panyin no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Me ba, sɛ obiara ntumi mma ha a, adɛn nti na woaba ha?" Ntikuma buae sɛ, "Ɔkɔm nti, na merehwehwɛ biribi a mɛkɔ adi". Ɔbea panyin no nanabea kae sɛ, "Kɔ me afuw mu wɔ hɔ, nanso yam a ɛbɛka sɛ, Pulk me, pluck me, don't pluck it, nanso yam a ɛbɛka sɛ, Don't pluck me, don't pluck me, pluck it and bring it. Ntikuma kɔe. Yams no bi kae sɛ, Pulk me, pluck me; afoforo kae sɛ, Don't pluck me, don't pluck me. Afei Ntikuma pam wɔn a wɔkae sɛ, Don't pluck me, don't pluck me. Ɔde wɔn baa fie; ɔtetee wɔn. Afei Ɔbaabun no kae sɛ: "Montow aduan no fã a ɛyɛ nokware no nkyene, na momfa mpopaho no ngu paneɛ no mu na momfa nto gya no so". Ntikuma pam wɔn, na ɔfaa mpopaho no de guu ogya no so. Bere a wɔsiesiee wɔn no, oyii wɔn fii ogya no so, na ɔtotow paneɛ no mu nsɛm no mu, na biribiara (a ɛwɔ mu no) dan aduan pa. Ntikuma kae sɛ, Mawie, nanso ma me nam a mede bɛhyɛ mu. Ɔbea panyin nana kae sɛ, Kɔ na kɔka kyerɛ Nkwakuo, Apapafo a wɔwɔ dua no so no sɛ Ɔbea panyin nana se, mo nyinaa, mo maamenom! Apapafo no kae sɛ, Sɛ ɛnyɛ Ɔbea panyin nana nti a, anka ɛsɛ sɛ yekum mo. Ɔbea panyin nana kae sɛ, Nea ɛfa me ho no, ɛnyɛ m'asɛm, na mo m Ntikuma boaboaa mmoa no nyinaa ano, na ɔsan kyekyeree yams no bi, na ɔde wɔn nyinaa kɔɔ ne kurom. Agya Spider huu (ne babarima) Ntikuma sɛ ogyina n'anim, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Mema wo akwaaba, ant-hill breaker (i.e. abakan). Ɔtoaa so sɛ, Ma mensoa wo wɔ m'akyi. Ntikuma kɔɔ hɔ kɔkyekyeree hɔ. Afei Ananse kɔfaa yam biako; ɔkae sɛ, Bere a me ho bɛtɔ me bosome biara no, mɛdi eyi. (Ne yere) Aso kae sɛ, Efi bere a Ɔbɔadeɛ no bɔɔ nneɛma no, so woahu ɔbarima bi a wanya bosome biara? Ananse ne ne yere, ne ne mma, wiee yam no di, na nea aka ara ne nea na ɛyɛ Agya Spider dea no. Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma no daa adi no, Ananse kae sɛ, Manya m'asram. Na ofii hɔ kɔtraa fie no akyi, na ɔnoaa yam no, na odidii kosii sɛ ne nyinaa baa awiei. Ntikuma kae sɛ, "Mɛsan akɔ". Ananse kae sɛ, "Me ne wo bɛkɔ". Ntikuma kae sɛ, "Me ne wo renkɔ". Bere a Ntikuma kɔdae no, Ananse kɔfaa aboa nhoma kotoku bi, na ɔde mpompono nketewa hyehyɛɛ mu, na ɔde nsõ hyɛɛ mu, na ɔde hyɛɛ Ntikuma atade ano. Bere a nneɛma fii ase daa adi no, Ntikuma fii akwantu no ase ntɛmntɛm. Bere a [sum a na ɛkata nneɛma so no] bɔɔ n'anim no, Ananse hwehwɛɛ Ntikuma sɛnea ɔpɛ, na wanhu no, enti ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔhwehwɛɛ (baabi a nsõ no (adeda) no. Bere a ohuu wɔn no, odii n'akyi kɔɔ n'akyi, na na ɔrekɔ no, na Ntikuma ne ne nanabea panyin no te hɔ. Ananse buae sɛ, "Ɔkɔm de me, enti ma me biribi nni". Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɛhe na menkɔpɛ biribi?" Ananse buae sɛ, "Wugye di sɛ meyɛ ɔkwasea, mpo me ba, Ntikuma, bae, na woama no bi". Ananse kae sɛ, "Kɔ turo no so; yams a ɛka sɛ, "Twe me, twe me". mmfa wɔn mmma soro, nanso wɔn a wɔka sɛ, mmfa me mmma soro, mmfa me mmma soro, mfa wɔn mmma soro no. Abɔnsam no (buae), Ah! nkwaseafo adwuma bɛn na eyi yɛ a woka kyerɛ me sɛ, sɛ obi ka sɛ ɔdɔ obi a, wohyɛ me sɛ menka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mekyi no, mentee biribi a ɛte saa da? Nanso Abɔnsam no kɔɔ afuw no so. Bere a oduu hɔ no, nnomaa no bi kae sɛ, "Twe me, twe me", na afoforo nso kae sɛ, "Ntwe me, twe me". Na ɔtwee wɔn a wɔkae sɛ, "Twe me, twe me" no, na ɔne wɔn kɔɔ fie. Ananse buae sɛ, Ah, asɛm bɔne bɛn na woka kyerɛ me yi; esiane sɛ Ɔbɔadeɛ no na ɔbɔɔ nneɛma nti, so ɛsɛ sɛ yɛtow aduan pa no gu, na yɛkora ntɛtɛ no so? Ɔfaa aboa nhoma no fã bi de guu so. Bere a wɔtetee aduan no, oyii, na ne nyinaa dan abo, nanso aboa nhoma no fã biako a ɔde guu so no dan aduan pa. Agya Spider kae sɛ, "Eh! saa ɔkorɔmfo yi a wɔsan no baa nkwa mu no nni atoro (ne nyinaa akyi). " Na N'adamfo no kɔkataa aboa nhoma no mu na ɔde brɛɛ no, na ɔde guu gya no so. Bere a wɔhyewee no, oyii aduan no fii ogya no mu, na na ayɛ nkyenenkyene a w'ani begye ho sɛ wubehu no. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Ɔbaabun, eyi nyinaa ye, nanso ɛsɛ sɛ wode nam ka ho ma me". Ɔbaabun no kae sɛ: "Kɔ na kɔhwɛ dua no, ɛwɔ so no, Aberewa wɔ so; sɛ wokɔ a, wobɛka sɛ, 'Woyɛ mmoa.' " Abɔnsam no kae sɛ: "Ɔbea yi! Me ne Apataa no nni nea enti a ɛsɛ sɛ yɛko, na dɛn nti na ɛsɛ sɛ mede saa kwan yi so di wɔn nyaatwom?" Ɔbea panyin nana kae sɛ, "Kɔ, na sɛ ɔhaw ba a, ɛno ne m'asɛm". Ɔkae sɛ: "Mo a mowɔ nnua yi so no, Nanabea no ka sɛ moyɛ mmoa". Mmoa no kae sɛ: "Sɛ ɛnyɛ Nanabea no nti a, anka yebekum mo". Nanabea no kae sɛ: "Ɛnne mommra mmekum no".

Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɔbea panyin, eyi nyɛ ade pa". Afei mmoa no bɛhwee no so na wɔbaa bɛhwee Ananse. Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ, "Me peaw The-sharp-one, twa wɔn ti". Afei, Nkyekye no boaboaa nam no ano na ɔsan baa fie, na Ɔbea Panyin no gyaw ne ho kakra. Afei Ananse kɔwiaa ne peaw no de kɔhyɛɛ ne kotoku mu, na otuu mmirika san baa ne kurom. Bere a ɔsan kɔɔ ne kurom no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne babarima no sɛ: "Ntikuma, esiane sekan a mede me nsa ato so yi nti, ma yɛnkɔ kwae no mu". Enti wɔkɔe, na wɔkɔe no, wohuu wuram mprako bi. Ananse kae sɛ: "Ntikuma, kɔ na kɔhaw wɔn. Ntikuma kɔɔ hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Bush-pigs, wo ho yɛ fĩ". Bush-pigs no buae sɛ, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ wo ne Kwaku Ananse, Spider babarima no a, anka ɛsɛ sɛ yɛba bɛhwe wo". Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Bra bɛhwe no wɔ ɔkwan biara so, ɔyɛ onipa bɔne, ne animguase no nyɛ anigyede". Na Bush-pigs no rebɛhwe no, bere a Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Old woman's Knife, The-sharp-one, twa wɔn ti ma me". O Afei Kwaku Ananse ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Ɛdeɛn na woreyɛ yi? Meka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ wɔntwa mmoa no ti, na wowiee nea woreyɛ no, na afei na eyi nyinaa yɛ dɛn?" Afei Ntikuma hwɛɛ no kakra, na afei ɔkae sɛ, "Afei de, yɛ komm". Kwaku Ananse antie no, na Nkrante no baa fam, na Ananse kɔɔ kɔfaa no. Wɔsan kɔɔ fie. Ade kyee, bere a nneɛma no daa adi no, Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ wɔbɛkɔ ɔsoro-nyame a ɔwɔ hɔ no nkyɛn. Ntikuma kae sɛ, "Agya, sɛ ɛyɛ saa sekan yi nti a, nkɔ. Ananse kae sɛ, Fi hɔ. Ntikuma kae sɛ, Kɔ w'ankasa kwan so. Ananse fii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ɔsoro-nyame a ɔwɔ hɔ no nkyɛn, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Owura, ɛnnɛ, sɛ obi afom wo a, me nko ara, me, Kwaku Ananse, metwa wɔn nyinaa ti da koro na mede bɛma wo. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Aseda afɔre wɔ hɔ ma obiara a Ananse kaa sɛ: "Ma wɔnkɔfa nsã ne odwan baako". Ntɛm ara na wɔde wɔn bae. Ananse kɔɔ ɛkwan so. Ananse bisaa ɔsraani no sɛ: "Wo na woka sɛ worekɔko atia Ɔsoro nyame no?" Wɔbuaa sɛ: "Yɛnka saa. Na Ɔsoro nyame no dɔm gyina Ananse akyi. Afei Kwaku Ananse kasa kyerɛɛ Ɔbaa Akwakoraa s Knife, The-sharp-one, ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Twa saa atamfo dɔm yi ti ma me. Na Knife no twaa wɔn ti, tan! tan! na owiee. Ananse kae sɛ, Ɛyɛ papa, gyae. Nanso Knife no bɛhwee Ɔsoro-nyame no dɔm so na na ɔretwa (wɔn ti). Ananse kae sɛ, Oh, ɛno ne Ɔsoro-nyame no dɔm, enti gyae. Nanso etwaa so. Ananse kae sɛ, Sɛ wo nufu mu ayɛ den a, ma ɛnhyew. Oh! Wɔsɛee ɔsoro anyame dɔm no nyinaa. Afei Nkrante no twaa wim; ɔbaa baabi a Kwaku Ananse wɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Hwɛ, wunnim me anaa? Wunnim me anaa? Mene Kwaku Ananse, mede wo fi Nkwakoraa no nkyɛn baa hɔ. Oh! afei Kwaku Ananse ti hwehwee, tan! Na sekan no kɔɔ so, na baabi a esii no dan sare bi, a yɛfrɛ no Etene (abantenten); n'atifi yɛ nnam yiye.

Eyi, m'asɛm no, a maka ho asɛm no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
