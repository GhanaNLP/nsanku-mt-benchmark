Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ASƐM A ƐKYERƐKYERƐ MU ASƐM A ƐKYERƐKYERƐ

WƆKA sɛ oguanhwɛfo bi (bere bi) kɔɔ wuram; ɔbaa na ɔhwɛɛ amoa bi mu, na na Nipa, Onipa no, Osebo, Akraman no, Owo, ɔwɔ no, ne Kusie, mmoadabi no gyina mu. Afei bere a nnipa yi huu Ɔdɔmmarima no, ɔfoforo no kae sɛ, "Agya Ɔdɔmmarima, bra na fa me kɔ". Ɔfoforo no nso kae sɛ, "Agya, bra na fa me kɔ". Ɔdɔmmarima no kae sɛ, "Meremmra, efisɛ ɔkyena mpo, sɛ mibegye wo, na wuba me nkyɛn a, nea wobɛyɛ me no (me nim) remma me ho nhia". Ɔfoforo no kae sɛ, "Mentumi". Ɔfoforo no nso kae sɛ, "Mentumi". Ɔdɔmmarima no kae sɛ, "Sɛ saa na ɛte ankasa a, mɛfa wo akɔ". Da koro, bere a Okyerefo no wɔ hɔ no, Osebo, Okyerefo no, de nam pii brɛɛ no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Agya Okyerefo, eyi yɛ aseda de ama ayamye a woayɛ ama me no, meda wo ase, Apiafo". Okyerefo no de nam nkɛntɛn dumien brɛɛ Okyerefo no. Okyerefo no daa no ase, na Okyerefo no fii hɔ kɔe. Wɔ bere foforo mu no, Okyerefo no wɔ hɔ, na Okusie, Okyerefo no, bae. Okyerefo no kae sɛ, "Agya Okyerefo, wopɛ sika mfrafra bi? " Okyerefo no kae sɛ, "Hwee! adɛn nti na wompɛ, eyi ne ade a nnipa abrɛ ho. " Okyerefo no fii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a Nyankonpon, Ɔsoro-nyame no, de ne sika mfrafra no siee no. Ɔkɔɔ kɔtutuu amena wɔ dan no ase, na ɔkɔɔ dan no mu, na ɔkɔfaa Ɔsoro-anyame sika mfutuma no nyinaa, na ɔde brɛɛ Ɔhwefo no. Ɔhwefo no kae sɛ, "Meda wo ase, Aku". Na Oguammaa no fii hɔ. Da foforo, na ɔwɔ hɔ, na ɔtee sɛ obi rekyia no, Ago. Ɔhwefo no buae sɛ, Ame (ba mu). Ɔbarima no bae; ɔkae sɛ, Agya Ɔhwehwɛfo, anɔpa pa. Ɔ (Ɔhwehwɛfo no) buae sɛ, Yiw, onua. Ɔkae sɛ, Eyi yɛ nkuku ketewa bi a nkyenam bobesa wom a mede rebɛda wo ase. Agya Ɔhwehwɛfo no kae sɛ, Mempɛ; Wo de, Onipa, ɛte sɛ nea mekae sɛ ɛbɛba, bere a mekae sɛ meremfa wo nkɔ no; hwɛ nea Akraman no de brɛɛ me, hwɛ nea Akwan no de brɛɛ me no. Ɔbarima no faa ne nkyenam bobesa no fii hɔ kɔɔ hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔsoro-nyame no sɛ, Wo sika mfutuma a ayera no, Akwan no bae na akyinnye biara nni ho no, ɔfaa no de kɔmaa Ɔhwehwɛfo no. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Ɛyɛ saa? Ɔkae sɛ, Ɔsorosoro nsɛ, ɛyɛ nokware tee. Ɔsoro nyame no somaa abɔfo sɛ wɔnkɔkyere ɔtweaseɛ no, na wɔkyekyeree n'asen, na wɔhwee no denneennen. Afei bere a wɔduruu ɔtweaseɛ no kwan so no, ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ: Anuanom, mepɛ sɛ mekɔyɛ biribi. Asomafoɔ no kae sɛ: Dabi, ɔkorɔmfo, wobɛdwane. Ɔkae sɛ: Dabi, merentumi nnwane, na sɛ wopɛ a, fa hama no, kyekyere no me asen ho, kyekyere no dua ho, na ma menkɔ. Asomafoɔ no tiee no, na wɔmaa no kɔe. Ɛhɔ na ɔtee, na ɔtee nka sɛ n'ani agye, na bere a aboa a ne tenten ne ne teɛ no bae no. Ɔkaa sɛ, Hwɛ ha, m'adamfo, gye aduro ketewa yi, na fa kɔ wo nkyɛn, na sɛ wokɔ, na wogyina wo anim wɔ bagua no anim, na ɛba sɛ wɔbɛkum wo a, mɛkɔ ɔhene no baguam, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a metumi aka ne yere a ɔdɔ no no ba no; sɛ matumi aka no a, wubehu sɛ obiara anyin, na wo nso wobɛkasa, na woka sɛ, Mewɔ aduro, na sɛ wode bɛhyɛ abofra no so a, ɔbɛsa. Odɔmfo no kae sɛ, Meda wo ase, Aku. Asomafoɔ no faa Odɔmfo no kɔgyinaa Ɔsoro-nyame no anim, (n'anim ne n'Agyinatufo no anim) Kontire, Akwum, Adonten, Gyase, ne Kyidom atitiriw no. Ɔpanin no kaa ne asɛm no, na bere a Ɔkasafo no sɔre sii hɔ sɛ ɔrebu atɛn no, wɔtee awerɛhow nne bi, pui! pui! wɔ ahemfie hɔ akyirikyiri. Wɔkae sɛ, Ɛdɛn ne saa? Wɔkae sɛ, Ɔwɔ no aka Ɔsoro-nyame no yere a ɔpɛ n'asɛm no ba. Wɔn nyinaa kɔe. Afei Ɔpanin no gyina hɔ; ɔkae sɛ, Sɛ menyɛ ɔdeduani a, anka ebia mewɔ aduru (adefua bi a ɛko tia awuduru no). Onipa bi tee no, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, Monkɔfa ɔdeduani no mmra ha. Afei Ɔpanin no bae. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Sɛ wubetumi ahwɛ eyi, me ba, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔrenwu a, mede wo ti bɛma wo. Ɔhwefo no kae sɛ, Mate, Ɔdɔfo. Na Ɔhwefo no kae bio sɛ, Me nana, nea merebɛkyerɛ wo, sɛ wubetumi anya bi a, ɛno ansa na metumi ahwɛ wo ba no. Wɔkae sɛ, Dɛn asɛm ne no? Ɔkae sɛ, Tell-tale mogya a wɔde bɛfrafra aduru a wɔabɔ no. Wɔkae sɛ, Ɛhe na yebenya Tell-tale mogya? Ɔdefo no mu biako, a na ɔyɛ Ɔhwefo no, kae sɛ, Ɛdeɛn na ɛbae wɔ nea ɔbae na ɔwiaa Ɔsoro-nyame no ho - sɛ ɔbarima no aba na Ɔsoro-nyame no sika no, ɛhe na ɔwɔ? Wɔkae sɛ, Wunni mfomso, wunni mfomso. Wɔkɔɔ de onua no brɛɛ no. Ɔhwefo no kae sɛ, Bobu ne nsa no wɔ ntokuru no ho, na fa ne mogya ma me. Ntɛm ara na wɔbuee mu. Ɔhwefo no faa mogya no de no fraa aduru no, de kɔhyɛɛ Ɔhene no ba no nan ho. Amonom hɔ ara ɔsɔree. Wɔkae sɛ: "Montow nnwan no ayeyi nnwom". Wɔteateaam sɛ: "Hee!" Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ: "Megye w'atifi na mede bɛma wo, na deɛ ɛfa Tell-tale ho no, ma wonkum no, na twa n'afunu mu, na tow kyene mmaa nyinaa". Wɔkum no, na wɔtwaa ne nam mu nketewa nketewa, na wɔtow kyene mmaa nyinaa. Saa na anansesɛm pii fi bae wɔ abusuakuw no mu.

Eyi, m'asɛm no, a maka ho asɛm no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
