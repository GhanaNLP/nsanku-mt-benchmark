Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ASƐM A ƐDA SO Sɛ Obi Kɔ Adan Bi Mu Anadwo a, Ɛyɛ Sum

WƆKA sɛ Kuntukurunku, Odds, ne Kuntunamoa, Ends, fi ɔdan no atifi bɛhwee fam. Wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Agya maame asase, woyɛ ɔmansin". Na Maame Asase kae sɛ: "Adɛn nti na woka sɛ meyɛ ɔmansin?" Wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Esiane sɛ woantutu ɔbodan amma yɛn sɛ yɛmmɛda mu nti". Agya maame asase danee n'ani kyerɛɛ Prae a ɔyɛ Nkyene no, na ɔkae sɛ: "Nkyene a efi nnomaa mu ayɛ w'ano". Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Adɛn nti na wohaw me?" Agya maame asase kae sɛ: "Sɛ mayɛ wo bɔne a, mayɛ wo bɔne; sɛ wunhu nea enti a ɛte saa a, gyae". Na Nkyene no danee n'ani kyerɛɛ Abena a ɔyɛ Nkyene no, na ɔkae sɛ: "Kɔfa na tow kyene". Nkyene no kae sɛ: "Adɛn nti na woreyɛ saa? Broom kae sɛ, "(Anyhow) mayɛ saa". Strip-of-bark nso kaa Sumina, Midden-heap ho asɛm, na ɔkae sɛ, "Tie nea wunya". Midden-heap kae sɛ, "Adɛn nti na wotaa me?" Obuae sɛ, "Mayɛ wo ayayade, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a ɛte saa a, ɛnde gyae bisa". Midden-heap nso ka kyerɛɛ Abe, Palma dua no sɛ, "Wo kɔn nyinaa ayɛ nwura". Palma dua no kae sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woreyɛ me ayayade?" Midden-heap buae sɛ, "Mayɛ wo ayayade, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a ɛte saa a, ɛnde dan w'ani". Tete-palma dua no nso gyae bisabisae, na ɔkae sɛ, "Wo aso atew". Nkokoaa tuntum no kae sɛ: "Adɛn nti na wohaw me?" Nkokoaa dua no buae sɛ: "Mayɛ wo ayayade, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a ɛte saa a, gyae bisa". Nkokoaa tuntum no nso kɔɔ Esono, ɔsono a ɔwɔ hɔ no nkyɛn, na ɔkɔɔ ne nan so; na ɔkyekyeree ɔsono no. Bere a na (Ɔsono no) repagyaw ne nan so no, afei ɔde ne nan sii Tamiriwa, Nsono tuntum no, aniwa no ase pɛɛ, na Nsono tuntum no aniwa fii ase nomee, na ɔpopaa nsu no guu efan (a ɛrenyin) nhwiren bi a ɛbɛn hɔ no so. (Seesei), aduane a Ɔbaa Akwakoraa-Nananom di ne efan dua no, na na ɛyɛ saa na Tamiriwa, Ɔbaabunu no, apopa nsu no afi n'ani so. 'Kraman, Akraman no, retwam, na ɔno (Ɔbaa Akwakoraa-Nananom no) frɛɛ Akraman no na ɔfaa nnyina no (na ɔsoɛ no) na ɔmaa Akraman no soaa no wɔ ne ti so. Bere a Akraman no rekɔ no, ɔnyaa kola-nut, na ɔpaee biako mu abien, na ɔtow biako guu Okagya, Squirrel, no anom. Okagya, ɔno nso, faa kola-nut no na ɔde bɔɔ Onyina, Silk-cotton-tree no. Silk-cotton-tree no nso, n'atifi hyɛɛ n'abufuw mu. Afei, na abofra ketewa no yɛ Nyankonpon, Ɔsoro-nyame no ba. Ɛhɔ ara na wɔkyeree Silk-cotton-tree no, na wɔde no kɔɔ Ɔsoro-nyame no anim. Wɔbisaa no sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na ɛrekɔ so yi?" "Me ara na na migyina m'ankasa me fie baabi, na saa Nkwatia yi a ɔyɛ akokoaa no (((() yɛɛ n'adwene sɛ ɔbɛma me kola-nut na ɔde abɔ me, enti me deɛ, bere a abofra yi retwam no, na misusuw sɛ ɛyɛ Okagya, Nkwatia no, na meyii me mman no baako na mede bɔɔ no, na hwɛ, na ɛyɛ wo ba". Nyame, Ɔsoro-nyame no, kae sɛ, "Fa no na kyekyere no dua bi ho, na frɛ Okagya, Nkwatia no, sɛ ɔmmra". Okaman bae; ɔkae sɛ, "Sagya, wunim sɛ me tabu ne kola-nut no, nanso Okraman, Akraman no, na ɔkɔe. "Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Fa no, na kyekyere no dua bi ho, na frɛ Ɔkraman no ma ɔmmra". Wɔkɔfrɛɛ Ɔkraman no sɛ ɔmmra. Ɔkraman no kae sɛ, "Me wura, na merennantew me ho baabi afei wuhu sɛnea me ho yɛ den bere a Ɔbea Panyin nana faa nnyina no de guu me ti so no; afei Okagya, Squirrel no, ɔno na ɔda ne dan mu wɔ afuw no mu a Ɔbea Panyin nana ayɛ sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obenya biribi adi, nanso ɛyɛ me a Ɔbea Panyin nana maa no faa nnyina no; me nso, mefaa kola-nut bi tow guu ne dan no ano. " Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Fa no, kyekyere no nnua mu, na frɛ Ɔbea Panyin-naa ma ɔmmra". Ɔbea Panyin nana bae. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ah, me mmanana, monhwɛ m'anom, me a mintumi nnidi nam, efan nnua na mididi; ne nyinaa akyi no, mehohoro Bosompra; m'atade a ɛho ntew ne Tamiriwa, Black-sneak no; na Nkraman no nko ara na onim eyi, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ Tamiriwa, na ɔmaa no faa nsu fii n'anom, na ɔde kɔhyɛɛ efan nnua a mete so no mu. Ɛno nti na mede me nnyina maa no sɛ ɔnsoa". Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, "Oh, fa no na kyekyere no nnua ho, na kɔfa Tamiriwa, Black-sneak no bra". Black-sneak no bae. Ɔkae sɛ, "Owura, na me nko ara mewɔ baabi, na hwɛ, asono kɛse bi nantew baabi a na me aniwa anim hɔ ara, na mekɔɔ kɔfaa efan (ahaban) sɛ memfa mpopa ho. Ɛno ara na minim wɔ ho". Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Fa no na kyekyere no dua ho, na fa Esono, asono no brɛ me". Wɔkɔfaa Esono. Nkwan no kae sɛ: "Nnansa a atwam no na mete hɔ, na m'anim fii ase howee, na bere a mehwɛe baabi a pa m'asɛm no ɔkwasea yi a wɔfrɛ no Anantwi ketewa no de ne ho kɔhyɛɛ m'anim sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ nam a ɔde bɛwe, na ɔreyɛ kɛse no. Na bere a mereforo me nan no, ɛkɔɔ Tamiriwa, anantwi ketewa no so". Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ: "Fa no na kyekyere no dua ho, na fa Tetea, anantwi ketewa no bra". Bere a Tetea bae no, ɔkae sɛ: "Owura, borɔdɔma dua bi wɔ hɔ; bere a ɛsow aba no, (ne ntini) yɛ tuntum kan; bere a me, Anantwi ketewa no, nam so no, ɛma enyin. Da bi na mewɔ hɔ, bere a Abedua-dua no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ m'aso yɛ tuntum no, na esiane sɛ meyɛ ketewa nti, na misusuw sɛ mɛkɔ Elephant no nkyɛn hɔ akɔhwehwɛ nam a mɛdi na ama manya ahoɔden kɛse. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Fa no na kyekyere no dua ho, na kɔfrɛ Abedua-dua no ma ɔmmra. Abe, Abedua-dua no, bae; ɔkae sɛ, Owura, eyi ne nea ɛsɛ sɛ meka: Me ne Sumina, Midden-porow no, te faako, na sɛ nneɛma a wɔtow gu no so no dɔɔso dodo a, na ontumi nnyina bio a, migye bi de kyekyere me kɔn; nanso afei, ɔpaa me kyɛw, kae sɛ mayɛ m' kɔn ho hwee. Esiane eyi nti, me de, m'ani so yɛɛ kɔkɔɔ, na me nso, me ne Aberewa Ketewa no dii nsɛmmɔne. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Fa no na kyekyere no dua ho, na frɛ Sumina a ɔyɛ Midden-heap no, sɛ ɔmmra. Midden-heap no bae; ɔkae sɛ, Owura, Abena, Strip-of-bark, a sɛ wɔprapraw sum ase wɔ dan bi mu a, wɔde ma no sɛ ɔno nso mfa mmrɛ me. Da bi a atwam no, a na menhaw no biara, ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, Wode nea wunya no sie (w.e. me nso, me ne no anni no yiye. Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, Fa no na kyekyere no dua bi ho, na frɛ Abena, a ne din de Atutena, na ɔmmra. Atutena bae; ɔkae sɛ, Owura, bere a Prae, Atutena no, twe dan mu, sɛ wɔamma me (n'atutena no), bere a mframa bɔ a, atutena no nyinaa bɛsan aba; afei, da bi, ɛwom sɛ manyɛ hwee amma no de, ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Woafa nneɛma bi de tow gu. Eyi nti, me nso me ho yeraw me, na mekɔɔ Sumina, a ɔyɛ Atutena-Mfinimfini no so. Ɔsoro nyame no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Fa no, na kyekyere no dua bi ho, na ka n'ani. I Prae, the Broom, to come here. The Broom came; she said, Sire, Old-mother-earth lies there so flat, her surface gets dirty, so whenever I rise up, I sweep up all the dirt on her and make her beautiful; yet the other day, Mother-earth addressed me, saying, W'ano ayɛ nnomaa atade, na me nso m'awoɔ, na me kɔdii Abena, the Strip-of-bark no ayayade. The Sky-god said, Fa no na kyekyere no to a log, na frɛ Mother-earth sɛ ɔmmra. Bere a Mother-earth bae no, ɔkae sɛ, Sire, ɛhɔ na meda; wɔtiatia me so; menka hwee da; wɔyɛ nneɛma ahorow nyinaa. Mewɔ hɔ no, Kuntukurunku ne Kuntunamoa, Odds ne Ends, bɛhwee me so, na mekae sɛ, O! ɛdeɛn na ɛrekɔ so? Na wɔkaa sɛ, Woantutu amena a yɛbɛtumi ada mu, na wo ne w'ahoɔfɛ nyinaa ho nni mfaso biara. Esiane saa nti, me nso, mefaa Prae, Brom no ho. Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, Fa no na kyekyere no dua ho, na kɔfa Odds ne Ends. Odds ne Ends bae. Wɔkae sɛ, "Owura, na yɛwɔ ɔdan no so, na yɛredi agoru, na yɛhwehwee ase, na yɛhaw yɛn ho; ɛno nti na yɛfaa asase Maame ho, kae sɛ ɔte fam". Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Agyinatufo, yɛate asɛm yi nyinaa, enti momma yɛnsɔre na yɛnkɔ dan no mu nsusuw adanse no ho na yɛnkɔbu atɛn". Enti wɔsɔre kɔɔ dan bi mu, na wɔtoo ɔpon no mu. Ɛno nti, sɛ wokɔ dan bi mu anadwo a, abira Ɔsoro-nyame no ne n'Agyinatufo no kɔbɔ wɔn ho amanneɛ wɔ hɔ.

Eyi, m'asɛm no, a maka ho asɛm no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
