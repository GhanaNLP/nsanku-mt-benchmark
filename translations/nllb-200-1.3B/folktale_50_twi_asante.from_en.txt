ASƐM BƐN NA ABOSOM, NYANKOPƆN a Wɔnyɛ Nyamesomfo no, Baa Wiase

BERE bi na ɔbea bi wɔ hɔ a ɔwoo mma duako; na da biara bere a ɔsɔre siesie aduan no, na mmofra no di ne nyinaa. Afei osusuw ho pii, na ɔkɔɔ afuw no ho, na ɔka kyerɛɛ Silk-cotton dua no sɛ, "Mɛsoma me mma duako sɛ wɔmmra wo ase ha na wɔmmetu mmorɔnoma; na sɛ wɔba a, twa wo mman duako na kum me mma no". Silk-cotton dua no kae sɛ, "Mate, na mɛyɛ ama wo". Maame no tu fii hɔ kɔɔ fie, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne mma no sɛ, "Mo mus Wɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ: "Monkɔ mfuw no so, wɔ Silk-cotton dua no ase, na monkɔfa aburoo, na mommra". Na wɔkɔe, na wokoduu Silk-cotton dua no ho. Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ, "Number One, gyina hɔ; Nea ɛto so abien, gyina hɔ; Nea ɛto so abiɛsa, gyina hɔ; Nea ɛto so anan, gyina hɔ; Nea ɛto so anum, gyina hɔ; Nea ɛto so asia, gyina hɔ; Nea ɛto so ason, gyina hɔ; Nea ɛto so awotwe, gyina hɔ; Nea ɛto so akron, gyina hɔ; Nea ɛto so du, gyina hɔ; na me ankasa, Nea ɛto so dubaako, migyina hɔ". Afei Nea ɛto so dubaako no ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Munnim asɛm biako pɛ no?" Eason, adɛn nti na ne maame ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ yɛnkɔ nkosua?" Ne nuanom no kae sɛ, "Dabi". Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɔka kyerɛɛ Silk-cotton dua yi sɛ, sɛ yɛkɔ hɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ esiw ne mman na ɛbɔ yɛn; enti mo nyinaa momfa nnua, na momfa nto Silk-cotton dua yi so". Wɔtwaa nnua no, na wɔde guu Silk-cotton dua no so, pim! pene! pim! pene! (ne nne a wɔyɛɛ no). " Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma fii ase daa adi no, ɔkɔɔ ɔsoro-nyame no hɔ kɔkaa ne nyinaa kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Minyaa mmofra bi, na bere a mepɛ sɛ midi no, mintumi nnya biribi nni (efisɛ wodidi ntɛm na wodi pii); ɔkɔm rekum me; enti mesrɛ wo, ma mma no mmra Ɔbea no buae sɛ: Me ne obi a n'atifi yɛ fitaa kasa. Na ɔsomaa abɔfo, na wɔkɔtwaa amoa, na wɔde nkuku guu mu, na ɔkɔfaa ɔwɔ ne akekantwɛre, na ɔde guu amoa no mu, na ɔkataa so. Na afei ɔsɛmpakafo no kɔfrɛɛ mmofra no. Wɔduruu baabi a na amoa no da no ara pɛ na Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Number One, gyina hɔ; Number Two, gyina hɔ; Number Three, gyina hɔ; Number Four, gyina hɔ; Number Five, gyina hɔ; Number Six, gyina hɔ; Number Seven, gyina hɔ; Number Eight, gyina hɔ; Number Nine, gyina hɔ; Number Ten, gyina hɔ; na me ara, ketewa Number Eleven, makɔ so; ɛsɛ sɛ wo nso kɔ. Ɛha na; ɛnsɛ sɛ motwam hɔ". Ne nuanom kae sɛ: "Dɛn nti, bere a ɔkwan a ɛtrɛw wɔ hɔ no, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛfa kwae no mu?" Afei, bere a wɔrekɔ no, na wɔn nyinaa kura nnua. Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ: "Tow nnua yi mu biako gu kwan yi so". Wɔtow no guu hɔ, na ɛhwehwee mu (kɔ amoa no mu), yiridi (na nehwe no nne). Na wɔn nyinaa faa ɔkwan bi so kɔɔ ɔsoro nyame no nkyɛn. Na ɔsoro nyame no ma wotutuu amoa, na wɔde nkongua too wɔn so, na sɛ mmofra no bɛtena so a, wɔkɔhwe amoa no mu. Na mmofra no baa ɔsoro nyame no anim. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Wɔahyehyɛ nkongua wɔ hɔ, wubetumi akɔ akɔtena so". Afei Ɔpɛn dubaako kae sɛ, "Hwan ne yɛn a yɛbɛtumi atena nkongua a ɛyɛ fɛ saa no so; enti, Owura, yɛrebɛtena nkyɛn wɔ ha". Ɛno akyi no, Ɔsoro-nyame no hwɛɛ mmofra no; ɔkae sɛ, "Mɛsoma mmofra yi akɔ Owu akuraa no ase". Ade kyee a nneɛma no daa adi no, ɔfrɛɛ mmofra no; ɔkae sɛ, Ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ Owu nkyɛn (a ɔte) wɔ hɔ, na kɔ na gye sika kɔkɔɔ kuruwa, sika kɔkɔɔ ntɛtɛbo, sika kɔkɔɔ ntɛtɛbo, sika kɔkɔɔ ntɛtɛbo, ne sika kɔkɔɔ ntɛtɛbo". Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ, "Wone yɛn wura, baabiara a wobɛsoma yɛn no, yɛbɛkɔ". Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Gyae". Mmofra no fii hɔ kɔɔ Owu akuraa no ase. Na wɔduruu hɔ no, owuo no bisaa no sɛ: Dɛn nti na wobaa ha bere a ɛnsɛ sɛ onipa biara ba ha yi? Wɔn nyinaa buae sɛ: Yɛn de, na yɛredi akɔneaba, na yɛbaa ha. Bere a esum duruu no, Owuo maa ne se hyerɛn (kosii sɛ ɛhyerɛn), sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obetumi ne wɔn anya Owuo. Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ: "Owu, mennae". Owu kae sɛ: "Da bɛn na wobɛda?" Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ: "Sɛ wode sika kɔkɔɔ kuruwa bi ma me na menom kakra a, ebia mɛda". Na Owu de maa no. Bere tiaa bi akyi no, Owu san hwiee ne se, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛkɔ akɔfa nea ɔto so dubaako no aka ho. Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ, "Owu, menna". Owu kae sɛ, "Da bɛn na wobɛda?" Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ, "Sɛ wode sika kɔkɔɔ adaka brɛ me a, ebia mɛda". Na Owu de brɛɛ no. Bio, ankyɛ na Owu bɛfaa nea ɔto so dubaako no. Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ: "Mentɔ nko". Owu kae sɛ: "Da bɛn na wobɛda?" Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ: "Sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa sika kɔkɔɔ a mede bɛmono ade ama me sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mɛmono no kakra, na afei makɔda". Owu de no bae. Bere tiaa bi twaam, na na Owu rebɛkyere no. Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ, "Agyabea, mennya nnae". Owu kae sɛ, "Ɛnne, da bɛn na wobɛda?" Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ, "Agyabea, sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa sika kɔkɔɔ ɔbo brɛ me a, anka metumi ada". Na Owu kɔɔ hɔ de no bae. Bio, ankyɛ na Owu sɔre fii hɔ bio. Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ, "Oh, Nana, mekae sɛ mennya nnae". Owu kae sɛ, "Na da bɛn na wobɛda?" Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ, "Sɛ wokɔfa bokiti a mpomma wɔ mu na wode nsu gu mu, na wotua aduan ma midi a, ebia mɛda". Owu maa ne nsa so na ɔkɔɔ asuten no ho. Na bere a ɔde nsu no hwie guu mu no, nsunsuan no mu mporoporowa no maa nsu no twae. Na dubaako no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuanom sɛ: Sɔre na guan! Na wɔsɔre guan. Na dubaako no kɔ twaa aduaba, na ɔde guu baabi a ne nuanom dedae, na ɔfaa ntama de kataa wɔn so. Na Owu wɔ asubɔnten no ho; na ɔretetew nsu. Afei Owu Barima no frɛɛ Owu Barima no sɛ: "Hwɛ, Owu". Ɔbea no buae sɛ: "Adwo. " Ɔkae sɛ: "Dɛn na woreyɛ?" Ɔbea no kae sɛ: "Yiw, ɛnyɛ abofra ketewa bi a mewɔ no? Bere a merebɛkyere no no, ɔka sɛ, 'Mentumi nna bio'; ɔfa me nneɛma nyinaa, na afei ɔka sɛ: Ɛsɛ sɛ mefa mpopaho na mefa nsu". Owu Barima no kae sɛ: "Ah, woyɛ abofra ketewa; afei, sɛ wutwa ahaban na wusiw no a, Ɔbea Wu no kae sɛ, "Oh, ɛyɛ nokware". Oyii nhaban, de guu mu, na ɔde nsu no petee, na ofii hɔ kɔe. Ɔbea Wu no kae sɛ, "Owu, woaba dedaw? Bere a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, wansusuw wɔn ho, na ɔno ankasa kum ne mma du no nyinaa. Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu, bere a nneɛma no daa adi no, Owu sɔre tenaa ogya no ho. Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ, "Agyabea, nsukɔm bi te wo kokom". Owu kae sɛ, "Fa nsukɔm no a ɛda hɔ no, na kum no ma me". Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ, "Onyankopɔn adom me; onipa bi a ɔte sɛ wo, sɛ nsukɔm bi tena wo so, na sika kɔkɔɔ nsukɔm bi da hɔ a (wode saa ade dedaw yi bedi dwuma no), ma menkɔfa no mmra mmekum no". Owu kae sɛ, "Kɔfa no fi abɔnten". "Number Eleven kɔɔ hɔ de brɛɛ no. Ɔbɔɔ ne tirim pam nnomaa no; wankum no. Number Eleven kae sɛ, "Oh, ɛnnɛ, baabi a saa nnomaa yi bɛhome no, ɛhɔ na me ne no bɛhome". Afei Number Eleven kɔɔ dan no mu kɔfaa ne kotoku a sika mpoma no wɔ mu no, ne nneɛma a aka no nyinaa. Ɔkae sɛ, "Agyabea Owu, biribiara nni hɔ a ɛbɛso me gye sɛ mefa tsetse nnomaa no, mede gu saa kotoku yi mu, na mede brɛ wo". Na bere a oduu kurow no ano no, ɔkae sɛ: "Owu, sɛ ɛnyɛ wo na woyɛ ɔkwasea a, anka mɛma woade wo ho, na anka me nuanom a me ne wɔn bae no benya ɔkwan aguan, na anka mɛma woakum wo mma du no nyinaa anaa? Na me de, merefi hɔ". Owu kae sɛ, "Wo, abofra a ɔte sɛɛ; baabiara a wubeguan no, ɛhɔ ara na mɛhome". Nea ɔto so dubaako no siim, yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! na Owu nso dii n'akyi. Bere a Nea ɔto so dubaako no rekɔ no, ohyiaa ne nuanom, na na wɔte kwan no so. Na wɔreyɛ nnomaa afiri. Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ, "So wunnya nkɔ? Owu reba, enti momma yɛnhwehwɛ ɔkwan bi a yɛbɛfa so aguan". Afei Owu baa wɔn so. Nea Ɔtɔ so dubaako no faa aduru na ɔhwie guu ne nuanom so, na wɔforo kɔɔ sare dua bi atifi. Na Owu gyinaa Silk-cotton dua no ase. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛha ara, mihuu mmofra yi, na ɛhe na wɔakɔ?" Nea ɔto so dubaako no te atifi hɔ; ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuanom sɛ, "Merebɛma no nsu". Ne nuanom kae sɛ, "Hwɛ, ɔrehwehwɛ yɛn sɛ obeso yɛn mu, na yɛaguan na yɛaba abɛtena ha, nanso woka sɛ, 'Merebɛma no nsu.' " Nea ɔto so dubaako no antie, na ɔmaa Owu nsu. Owu kae sɛ, "Ah, woaba ha, nnɛ woahu amane". Owu kae sɛ, "Wo, abofra a wote hɔ no, 'Kyere-he-ne, Kyere-he-ne.' " Na afei mmofra no mu biako hwee fam. "Kyere-he-ne", nea ɔto so abien no hwehwee ase. Afei de, nea na aka ara ne Ɔkannifo no. Owu kae sɛ, "Ɔba, 'kyere-he-ne', " na Ɔkannifo no bɔɔ ne ho ase sii fam, kirim! Na Owu nso kɔɔ Silk-cotton dua no ho. Nea Ɔtɔ so dubaako no kae sɛ, "Wo, ɔbaa kɛse kɛse, wo nso, 'kyere-he-ne". Na Owu nso sian bae, tum! na wawu. Nea Ɔtɔ so dubaako no kɔɔ kɔsesaw aduru, na ɔkyekyeree no wɔ ne nsam, na ohwie guu ne nuanom so, na wɔsɔree. Nea Ɔtɔso dubaako no rebɛtow aduru no akyene, na na emu bi agu Owu so, na Owu nyanee. Ɔbea no kae sɛ: "Woakum me, na woanyan me nso; ɛnnɛ, me ne wo bedi asɛm". Afei wɔn nyinaa fii ase guanee prɛko pɛ, kiri! kiri! kiri! Na owu dii wɔn akyi. Bere a wɔrekɔ no, na asubɔnten kɛse bi abutuw. Bere a Ɔkannifo no ne ne nuanom no koduu hɔ no, na wonim sɛnea wɔbɔ nsu, na woguanee. Ɔkannifo no nkutoo na na onnim sɛnea wɔbɔ nsu. Na mmofra no gyina ɔfã foforo no. Wɔteɛm, na wɔteɛm, na wɔteɛm, na wɔn ano yɛɛ mmoroso. Na Ɔkannifo dubaako no de, ɔdan ɔbo. Owu duu asubɔnten no mu. Ɔka sɛ, "Ao, mmofra yi, munnyina hɔ, ma memfa ɔbo ntow na memfa nto wɔn ano a ayɛ wusiwusi no". Owu no, bere a ɔhwɛɛ fam no, ohuu ɔbo bi sɛ ɛda hɔ; ɔfaa no tow no; bere a ɔbo no nam no, ɔkae sɛ, "Ahonhom, fa me kɔfa me kɔfa me kɔfa me kɔfa ɔfã foforo no". Ogyaa fam wɔ ɔfã foforo no. Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ, "Mini". Owu kae sɛ, "Ah, abofra yi! Biribiara nni hɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ meka ho asɛm kyerɛ wo, na nea mewɔ ka kyerɛ wo ara ne sɛ: Kɔ na kɔtena wo fie, na yɛ wo ho sɛ anyame nketewa no mu biako, na sɛ obi a mepɛ sɛ mefa no no ba wo nkyɛn a, ka kyerɛ me. Sɛ mepɛ sɛ meyɛ saa a, mɛma no kwan, na mede no ayɛ akyɛde ama wo; nanso nea mepɛ no, ɛsɛ sɛ wugye ma me". Wɔyɛ abofra ketewa a ne din de Numero 11 no asefo.

Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebia mubegye adi sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nkae no de, mubedwinnwen me ho.
