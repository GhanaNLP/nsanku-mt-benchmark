Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ASƐM BƐN NA ABOSOM, NYANKOPƆN a Wɔnyɛ Nyamesomfo no Baa Wiase

BERE bi na ɔbea bi wɔ hɔ a ɔwoo mma duako; na da biara bere a ɔsɔre siesie aduan no, na mmofra no di ne nyinaa. Afei odwinnwen ho bere tenten, na ɔkɔe kɔkyerɛɛ Silk-cotton dua no, sɛ: "Mɛsoma me mma duako sɛ wɔmmra wo ase ha bɛtwitwa mmorɔnoma; na sɛ wɔba a, twa wo mman duako na kum me mma no". Silk-cotton dua no kae sɛ: "Matie, na mɛyɛ ama wo". Maame no fii hɔ kɔɔ fie; ɔka kyerɛɛ ne mma no sɛ: "Ɛsɛ sɛ mokɔ afuw no ase, wɔ Silk-cotton dua no ase; mmorɔnoma wɔ hɔ, monkɔ nkoyi na mommra". Mmofra no fii hɔ. Woduu Silk-cotton dua no ho. "Number Eleven kae sɛ, "Number One, gyina hɔ komm; Number Two, gyina hɔ komm; Number Three, gyina hɔ komm; Number Four, gyina hɔ komm; Number Five, gyina hɔ komm; Number Six, gyina hɔ komm; Number Seven, gyina hɔ komm; Number Eight, gyina hɔ komm; Number Nine, gyina hɔ komm; Number Ten, gyina hɔ komm; na me ankasa, Number Eleven, manyɛ komm". Wɔtwaa nnua no, na wɔtow guu nnua no so. Na nnua no susuwii sɛ mmofra no aba, na otwaa ne mman duako, na ɔgyaa wɔn guu fam. Na nketewa no kae sɛ: Hwɛ, sɛ yɛkɔɔ hɔ a, anka nnua no bekum yɛn. Na wɔtasee mmɛn no de kɔmaa wɔn na ɔnoa wɔn, na hwɛ, na wɔn mma no nyinaa adi, na wɔn na kae sɛ: Ah! Ɛdefa saa asɛm yi ho no, mintumi nnyina so, enti mɛfa mmofra yi na mede ama Ɔsoro-nyame no. " Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma no daa adi no, ɔkɔɔ ɔsoro-nyame no hɔ kɔkaa ne nyinaa kyerɛɛ no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Manya mmofra bi a mewoo wɔn, na sɛ mepɛ sɛ midi a, mintumi nnya biribi nni (efisɛ wodidi ntɛm na wodidi pii); ɔkɔm rekum me; enti mesrɛ wo, ma wɔmfa mmofra no mmra na wonkum wɔn, na manya biribi adi". Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ saa ampa?" Ɔbea no kae sɛ, "Mewɔ ti bi a n'atifi yɛ fitaa". Ɔsoro-nyame no paw messen. Na wɔn nyinaa kɔeɛ, na wɔtutuu amoa kɛseɛ, na wɔde nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn guu mu, na ɔkɔɔ kɔfaa ɔwɔ ne akɔdeɛ, na ɔde guu amoa no mu, na ɔkataa so. Na afei ɔsomafoɔ no kɔɔ sɛ ɔrebɛfrɛ mmammarima no. Bere a woduu baabi a amoa no da no ara pɛ na Nea Ɔpɛn dubaako kae sɛ, "Number One, gyina hɔ; Nea Ɛto so abien, gyina hɔ; Nea Ɛto so Mmiɛnsa, gyina hɔ; Nea Ɛto so Anan, gyina hɔ; Nea Ɛto so anum, gyina hɔ; Nea Ɛto so Nsia, gyina hɔ; Nea Ɛto so Asɔn, gyina hɔ; Nea Ɛto so Awotwe, gyina hɔ; Nea Ɛto so Akron, gyina hɔ; Nea Ɛto so Du, gyina hɔ; na me ankasa, ketewa Nea Ɛto so Duako, gyina hɔ; ɛsɛ sɛ motwam ha; ɛnsɛ sɛ motwam hɔ". Na wɔn mu biako kae sɛ: Bɔ nnua yi mu biako kyene kwan yi so! Na wɔtow no kyenee hɔ, na ɛhwehwee mu, yiridi. Na wɔn mu biako kae sɛ: Hwɛ, sɛ yɛfaa hɔ a, anka yɛn nyinaa bɛwuwu. Na wɔfaa ɔkwan bi so, na wɔkɔɔ soro nyame no nkyɛn. Na ɔsoro nyame no ama wɔatutu amoa, na wɔde nkongua asi wɔn so, na sɛ mmofra no ba bɛtena so a, wɔkɔhwehwee amoa no mu. Na mmofra no baa ɔsoro nyame anim. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ: "Wɔahyehyɛ nkongua wɔ hɔ, wubetumi akɔ akɔtena so". Afei Ɔkannifo dubaako kae sɛ: "Hena ne yɛn, sɛ yebetumi akɔtena nkongua fɛfɛ a ɛte saa so; enti Owura, yɛbɛtena faako". Ɛno akyi no, Ɔsoro-nyame no hwɛɛ mmofra no, na ɔkae sɛ: "Mɛsoma mmofra yi akɔ Owu akuraa no ase". Ade kyee a nneɛma no daa adi no, ɔfrɛɛ mmofra no, na ɔkae sɛ: "Ɛsɛ sɛ mokɔ Owu (nea ɔte hɔ) hɔ, na monkɔfa sika kɔkɔɔ bi, sika kɔkɔɔ dua, sika kɔkɔɔ ntafoa, sika kɔkɔɔ ntafoa, ne sika kɔkɔɔ nnomaa". Ɔkannifo dubaako kae sɛ: "Wo Owu kae sɛ: "Ɛyɛ yɛn wura, baabiara a wobɛsoma yɛn no, yɛbɛkɔ". Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ: "Fi hɔ kɔ". Mmofra no siim kɔɔ Owu akuraa no ase. Woduu hɔ no, Owu kae sɛ: "Dɛn nti na ɛsɛ sɛ obiara ba ha, na wo nso woba ha?" Wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Ná yɛretu kwan, na yɛbaa ha (aane). " Owu kae sɛ: "Oh, ɛrenyɛ yiye". Na Owu wɔ mma du, na ɔde ne ho kaa ho no, na wɔn nyinaa yɛ dubaako. Bere a nneɛma fii ase sɛ ɛyerae no, Owu kyekyɛɛ mmofra no mu biako maa ne mma no mu biara biako, na ɔno ne Ɔbaako no kɔhomee. Bere a esum duruu no, Owuo hyerɛn n'ano (nkosii sɛ ɛhyerɛn) sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔde wɔn bɛkyere Ɔdɔm Baako no. Ɔdɔm Baako no kae sɛ: "Owuo, mennya nnae". Owuo kae sɛ: "Da bɛn na wobɛda?" Ɔdɔm Baako no kae sɛ: "Sɛ wode sika mpoma ma me na menom kakra a, ebia mɛda". Na Owuo kyeree no maa no. Bere tiaa bi akyi no, Owuo hyerɛn n'ano bio sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔde wɔn bɛkɔ akɔkyere Ɔdɔm Baako no. Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ: "Owu, meda so meda". Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ: "Da bɛn na wobɛda?" Owu kae sɛ, "Ɛnne, da bɛn na wobɛda?" Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ, "Agyabea, sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa sika kɔkɔɔ ɔbo brɛ me a, anka metumi ada". Na Owu kɔɔ hɔ de no bae. Bio, ankyɛ na Owu sɔre fii hɔ bio. Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ, "O Nana, mekae sɛ mennya nnae". Owu kae sɛ, "Na da bɛn na wobɛda?" Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ, "Sɛ wokɔfa nkuku bi a adan mu ayɛ no ma kɔsɔ mu nsu, na wokɔnoa aduan bi ma midi a, ebia mɛda". Owu maa ne nsa so de no kɔɔ asuten no ho. Bere a ɔde nsu no guu mu no, na nkuku a ɛwɔ nkuku no mu no ama no afa mu. Na nea ɔto so dubaako no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuanom sɛ: Sɔre na guan! Na wɔsɔre guan; na nea ɔto so dubaako no kɔ twaa aduaba, na ɔde guu baabi a ne nuanom dedae, na ɔfaa ntama de kataa wɔn so. Na Owu wɔ asubɔnten no ho; na ɔretetew nsu. Afei Owuo no frɛɛ Owuo no se, "Hwɛ, Owuo. " Owuo no buae sɛ, "Adwo. " Owuo no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na woreyɛ?" Owuo no kae sɛ, "Yiw, ɛnyɛ abofra ketewa bi a mewɔ no; sɛ merebɛkyere no a, ɔka sɛ, 'Mentumi nna bio'; ɔfa me nneɛma nyinaa, na afei ose ɛsɛ sɛ mefa paneɛ na mepra nsu". Owuo no kae sɛ, "Ah, woyɛ abofra ketewa; afei, sɛ wutwa nhaban no mu ne ne ntini, na afei wutwa nsu a, ɛrenyɛ papa anaa?" Owuo no kae sɛ, "Oh, sɛnea ɛteɛ". Owuo no twaa paneɛ no, de guu ne mu, na ɔbɔɔ nsu no, na ofii hɔ kɔe. Owu kae sɛ: "Owu, woaba dedaw? Bɔ aduan no". Owu no boaboaa aduan no ano; ɔde ne se kyekyee ne mu sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obekum Owu nua mmarima no na wakɔtɔ aduan ama wɔn. Bere a ɔkɔe no, wanhwɛ wɔn yiye, na ɔno ankasa kum ne mma du no nyinaa. Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu, bere a nneɛma no daa adi no, Owu sɔre tenaa gya no ho. Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ, "Agyabea, nsukɔm bi te wo kokom". Owu kae sɛ, "Kyere nsukɔm no a ɛda hɔ no, na kum no ma me". Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ, "Onyankopɔn adom me; onipa bi a ɔte sɛ wo, bere a nsukɔm bi te wo so, na nsukɔm bi a ɛyɛ sikakɔkɔɔ da hɔ (wode ade dedaw yi bedi dwuma no), ma memfa no mmra mmekum no". Owu kae sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔfa no fi dan no mu". Nea ɔto so dubaako no kɔe kɔfaa no. Ɔbɔɔ mmɔden pam nsukɔm no; wankum no. Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ, "Oh, ɛnnɛ, baabi a ntutummɛ yi bɛhome no, ɛhɔ na me ne no bɛhome". Afei nea ɔto so dubaako no kɔɔ dan no mu kɔfaa ne kotoku a sika ntutummɛ no wɔ mu no, ne nneɛma a aka no nyinaa. Ɔkae sɛ, "Nananom Owu, biribiara nni hɔ a ɛbɛso me gye sɛ me nsa bɛka ntutummɛ no, de no ahyɛ kotoku yi mu, na mede no abrɛ wo". Nea ɔto so dubaako no fii ase, yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! Oduu kurow no ano no, ɔkae sɛ: "Ao, Owu Nana, fa m'asɛm a mekae no kyɛ me, nanso sɛ wo nyɛ ɔkwasea koraa a, anka metumi agye w'ade nyinaa afi wo nsam; anka me nuanom a me ne wɔn bae no betumi anya ɔkwan bi de aguan; na anka metumi ama wo nso akum wo mma du no nyinaa anaa? Me de, merefi hɔ". Owu kae sɛ: "Wo a woyɛ abofra a ɛte sɛɛ yi; baabiara a wobɛhome no, ɛhɔ ara na me nso mɛhome". Na Nea Ɔtɔ so Du bɔɔ mmirika kɔe, yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! Na Owu nso dii n'akyi. Bere a Nea Ɔtɔ so Du rekɔ no, ohyiaa ne nuanom no, na na wɔte kwan no so. Na wɔreyɛ nnomaa afiri. Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ, "Worennyaw hɔ? Owu reba, enti momma yɛnhwehwɛ ɔkwan bi a yɛbɛfa so aguan". Na afei Owu baa wɔn so. Nea ɔto so dubaako no faa aduru na ohwie guu ne nuanom so, na wɔforo kɔɔ ntɛtɛ dua bi atifi. Na Owu gyinaa ntɛtɛ dua no ase. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛha ara, mihuu mmofra yi, na ɛhe na wɔakɔ?" Nea ɔto so dubaako no te atifi hɔ; ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuanom sɛ, "Mɛkɔ akɔtɔ nsu agu no so". Ne nuanom no kae sɛ, "Hwɛ, ɔrehwehwɛ yɛn sɛ obeso yɛn mu, na yɛaguan aba abɛtra ha, nanso woka sɛ, 'Mɛkɔ akɔtɔ nsu agu no so.' " Nea ɔto so dubaako no antie, na ɔtɔ nsu agu Owu so. Owu kae sɛ, "Aha, wo ni, nnɛ woahu amane". Owu kae sɛ, "Wo, abofra, a wote atifi hɔ no, 'Kyere-he-ne, Kyere-he-ne.' Na mmofra no mu biako hwee ase. 'Kyere-he-ne,' nea ɔto so abien no hwee ase. Afei na nea aka ara ne Ɔkannifo dubaako. Owu kae sɛ, "Ɔba, 'kyere-he-ne'," na Ɔkannifo dubaako bɔɔ mmirika sii fam, kirim! Na Owu nso kɔɔ Silk-cotton dua no so. Ɔkannifo dubaako kae sɛ, "Wo, ɔbaa kɛse, wo nso, 'kyere-he-ne". Na Owu nso sianee, tum! na na wawu. Ɔkannifo dubaako kɔɔ kɔsesaw aduru, na ɔkyɛɛ mu wɔ ne nsam, na ɔde petee ne nuanom so, na wɔsɔree. Ɔkannifo dubaako yɛɛ n'adwene sɛ ɔbɛtow aduru no akyene, bere a na emu bi agu Owu so, na Owu nyanee. Ɔbea no kae sɛ: "Woakum me, na woanyan me nso; ɛnnɛ, wo ne me nyinaa benya asɛm". Afei wɔn nyinaa fii ase guanee prɛko pɛ, kiri! kiri! kiri! Na owu dii wɔn akyi. Bere a wɔrekɔ no, nsubɔnten kɛse bi bobɔɔ mu. Bere a Numero Dumienu ne ne nuanom no beduu hɔ no, na wonim sɛnea woguare, na woguare. Numero Dumienu nkutoo na na onnim sɛnea woguare. Mmofra no gyinaa ɔfã foforo no so, na wɔteɛteɛɛm, na wɔteɛteɛɛm, na wɔteɛteɛɛm, na wɔn ano buei. Nea ɛfa Numero Dumienu ho no, ɔdan ɔbo. Owu duu asubɔnten no ho. Ɔka sɛ, "Ao, mmofra yi, munnyina hɔ, ma memfa ɔbo ntow na memfa nto wɔn ano a ayɛ wusiwusi no". Owu no, bere a ɔhwɛɛ fam no, ohuu ɔbo bi sɛ ɛda hɔ; ɔfaa no tow no; bere a ɔbo no nam no, ɔkae sɛ, "Ahonhom, fa me kɔfa me kɔfa me kɔfa ɔfã foforo no". Oduu ɔfã foforo no. Nea ɔto so dubaako no kae sɛ, "Mini". Owu kae sɛ, "Ah, abofra yi! Biribiara nni hɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ meka ho asɛm kyerɛ wo, na nea ɛsɛ sɛ meka kyerɛ wo ara ne sɛ: Kɔ na kɔtena wo fie, na dan wo ho sɛ anyame nketewa no mu biako, na sɛ obi a mepɛ sɛ mefa no no ba wo nkyɛn a, ka kyerɛ me; sɛ mepɛ a, megyaw no na mede no ayɛ akyɛde ama wo; nanso nea mepɛ (sɛ́ ɛyɛ afɔrebɔde) no, ɛsɛ sɛ wugye ma me". (Ɛne sɛ, afɔrebɔde.) Saa na Abosom (anyame nketewa) baa wiase. Wɔfi abofra ketewa No. 11 mu.

Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebia mubegye adi sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nkae no de, mubedwinnwen me ho.
