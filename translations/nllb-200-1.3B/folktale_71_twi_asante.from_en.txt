Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Woyɛ Nwonwafo te sɛ Ananse, N'asekyerɛ

ANANSE, Nnomaa no, traa hɔ bere bi, bere a Nsia, Ɔsoro-nyame no maame yaree no. Wɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔfaa ɔyaresafo bi sɛ ɔmmɛhwɛ no. Bere a ɔbaa hɔ no, ɔbisaa sika mfrafraa a wɔde sika kɔkɔɔ ayɛ no pereduan (ɛte sɛ, £8) sɛ ɔde bɛhwɛ ne yare no. Afei, Ananse kɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔsoro-nyame no sɛ, "Saa nipa yi refa wo sika mfutuma kwa; ontumi nhwɛ no, enti ma menhwɛ no mma wo, na sɛ manhwɛ no a, wobetumi ayi me ti". Ananse faa sika mfutuma a ɛsom bo te sɛ asuanu (w. (£4), na ɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔsoro-nyame no sɛ, "Sɛ merentumi nyi yare yi ano a, mede gyata a ɔte ase bɛma wo". Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma no daa adi no, na Nsia, Nyankonpon, Ɔsoro-nyame no maame afi hɔ. Wɔkae sɛ, "Ananse, dɛn na ɛrekɔ so?" Ananse fii hɔ; oguan. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ mmoadabi no sɛ: Yei ne nea mahu, na afei ɛsɛ sɛ woboa me. Mmoadabi no buae sɛ: Ɛdɛn mu na metumi aboa wo? Ɔkae sɛ: Mepɛ sɛ wokɔto ɔkwan kɔsi sɛ ebedu abantenten a ɔsoro nyame no te so no ase. Mmoadabi no kae sɛ: Mɛyɛ ama wo. Enti ɔyɛɛ. Abɛn no bae na ɔka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, "Mawie". Ananse kɔfrɛɛ ne babarima, Ntikuma, na ɔtwaa abɛn maa no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔnkɔ abankɛse no mu na ɔntena ase wɔ abantenten a wɔama so no ase, na, bere a ohuu sɛ Ɔsoro-nyame no te abantenten a wɔama so no so no, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔbɔ abɛn no na ɔka sɛ:

"Sɛ wukum Ananse a, abusuakuw no bɛsɛe!

Sɛ mode Ananse kyɛ no a, abusua no bɛwosow!"

Ntikuma kɔ kɔtenaa ase wɔ baabi a wɔgye ahɔho no, na Ananse fii hɔ, kae sɛ ɔrekɔ Nyankonpon, Ɔsoro-nyame no nkyɛn wɔ hɔ. Bere a ɔkɔe no, na Ɔsoro-nyame no awudifo kyeree no; wɔfaa no kɔɔ Ɔsoro-nyame no anim. Afei ɔkasafo no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Wo, Kwaku Ananse, woka kyerɛɛ Nyankonpon, Ɔsoro-nyame no sɛ, sɛ ne maame Nsia wu a, obetumi akum wo; enti esiane sɛ Nsia afi hɔ nti, yebekum wo". Ntikuma teɛɛm sɛ: "Sɛ wukum Ananse a, abusuakuw no bɛsɛe!

Sɛ mode Ananse kyɛ no a, abusua no bɛwosow!"

Wɔkae sɛ: "Montie, montie!" Na torobɛnto no san bɔnee bio:

"Sɛ wukum Ananse a, abusuakuw no bɛsɛe!

Sɛ mode Ananse kyɛ no a, abusua no bɛwosow!"

Afei Kontirehene panyin no sɔre ka kyerɛɛ Ɔsoro-nyame no sɛ: "Abusuakuw yi yɛ wo dea, Ɔsoro-nyame, ne Ya, Agyanom-Mma-Asase de. Ɛnnɛ woafa Ananse na worebɛkum no. Ya, Agyanom-Mma-Asase, se, 'Ma no nkɔ,' enti sɛ woma no kɔ a, ɛbɛyɛ yiye". Afei Ɔsoro-nyame no maa awudifo no gyaa no. Ɛno nti na wɔka sɛ, "Woyɛ nwonwa te sɛ Ananse".

Eyi, m'asɛm no, a maka ho asɛm no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
