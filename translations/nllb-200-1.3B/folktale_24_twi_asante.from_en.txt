Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ASƐM A ƐDA SO AHOHWƐFO Yɛ Ahiafo

ƆKYERƐKYERƐ ne ne yere ne ne ba kumaa no kɔɔ wuram sɛ wɔrekokum nam. Na osii wuram dan, na ɔne ne yere ne ne ba no traa mu. Bere a nneɛma no yii ne ho adi no, ɔkɔɔ wuram hɔ. Bere a ohuu aboa bi na ɔde ne tuo too no so no, ɔtwee n'ani. Da biara na ɛte saa, bɛyɛ mprenu nnawɔtwe. Na ne yere kae sɛ, "Esiane sɛ minni nam biara a mɛdi nti, mɛkɔ fie". Afei me werɛ fii sɛ mɛka sɛ saa ɔhwehwɛni no din de Gyekye, na ne yere din nso de Boniaye-kae-dabi (a ɛkyerɛ sɛ, Da bi-a-nni-asedafo-obi-bɛ-kae). Bere a na ɔhwehwɛni no redi akɔneaba no, na (ɔduruu baabi) wɔ odum dua bi ase, na na ɛhɔ yɛ beae fɛfɛ. Ɛhɔ na ɔhwehwɛfo no susuw ho wɔ ne tirim, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ, Sɛ mekɔ fie a, wɔbɛhwehwɛ sika mfrafrae no de abɛyɛ mfrafrae, na minni bi, enti mɛfa tuo yi akum me ho wɔ me kɔn mu sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mewu. Ɛhɔ ara na Biribi frɛɛ no sɛ, Ɔbɔadeɛ no ɔhwehwɛfo. Na ɔhwehwɛfo no kae sɛ, Agya. Na Biribi no kae sɛ, Dɛn nti na w'aniwa kɔkɔɔ saa? Na afei, sɛ deɛ wɔfrɛ no Go-and-spoil-the-home no ankɔ wo fie a, anka mede biribi bɛma wo. Afei ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Biribiara nni fie. Biribi kaa sɛ, Esiane sɛ biribiara nni fie nti, kɔ odum dua no ntini mu, na adawuru gong mmienu da hɔ, enti fa brɛ me. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔ kɔde brɛɛ me. Na Biribi kaa sɛ: Kɔ na twa odwen nnua mmienu, na twa w'ani, na tu mmirika kɔfa baako na fa to hɔ, na fa baako no na dan w'ani na fa to hɔ. Bere a ɔbae no, ɔkae sɛ: Mawie. Biribi kaa sɛ: Fa dawuru (gong) kɛse no na bɔ no. Na ɔbɔɔ no, na nwura no bɛyɛɛ akuraa bi a ɛtrɛw wɔ nnua mmienu no ntam. Na odum dua no gyinaa ne mfinimfini. Na saa Biribi no daa no adi sɛ ɛyɛ ɔkɔre. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ okunafo no sɛ sɛ asɛm bi a ɛho hia sɔre a, ɛsɛ sɛ wɔte wɔ odum dua no ase. Okunafo no kae sɛ, "Hoo, mate". Bere a ɔkɔre no kaa eyi kyerɛɛ no no, onyaa yerenom, afotufo, ne asomfo. Saa bere no mu no, ne yere (a wɔfrɛ no) Da bi-nea-nnye-nni-obi-bɛkae-no huu sɛ wabɛyɛ ɔkannifo, enti ɔtɔɔ borɔdɔma bobesa, na ɔde kɔɔ ɔkannifo no akuraa no ase, na na ne ba no da n'akyi. Na ɔfaa nsã no, na ɔmaa wɔn nyinaa nom. Na ɔfaa ɔbaa no de no maa Gyasehene no sɛ ɔmmɛfa no nkɔ ne fie. Na adeɛ saeɛ no, ɔbaa no hohoroo ne ho, na ɔkɔɔ ɔsahene no dan mu. Ɔbea no ka kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no sɛ: "Wo ne me anya osu ne owia, ɛnnɛ woanya baabi a wobɛda, na anka ɛnyɛ papa sɛ wobɛsoma akɔfrɛ me?" Na bere a ɔrekasa saa (sɛnea nnipa ne wɔn nkwaseasɛm te) no, wanhu asɛm biara a ɔbɛka. Na ɛhɔ ara na ɔkyeree no, na ɔne no dae. Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu no, ɔyii no firii gyasefoɔ fie hɔ de no kɔɔ gyasefoɔ fie. Ɛhɔ na ɔtenaa kɔsii sɛ akasakasa kɛseɛ bi sii. Na gyasefoɔ ne ne mpanimfoɔ nyinaa kɔtenaa odum dua no ase. Ɔbaa no ba no gyina n'agya anim, na ɔhunuu ɔkɔre no ba no wɔ odum dua no atifi, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya, mepɛ no". Na n'agya no bɔɔ n'akyi, na ɔtwee n'atifi, na ɔmaa no kɔɔ fie. Na ɔbaa no mma a aka no mu baako nso ba bɛgyinaa n'anim. Ɔsan huu Ɔbɔfo no ba no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Agya, mepɛ no". Na ɔbɔɔ n'akyi no saa ara, na ɔde n'atifi too n'akyi, ma ɔhwehwee ase, na bere a ɔrekɔ no, osui. Na ne maame kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woresu?" Na ɔkae sɛ, "Agya boroo me". Na ne maame kae sɛ, "O! Adɛn nti na ɛnsɛ sɛ ɔhwe wo, sɛ ɛyɛ Some-day-the-ungrateful-one-will-remember ba no a, anka ɔrenhwe no. Na Some-day-the-ungrateful-one-will-remember kae sɛ: "Me de, manyɛ wo hwee a ɛsɛ sɛ woyɛ me saa". Bere a ɔkaa saa no ara pɛ na wɔn baanu nyinaa tutuu mmirika kɔko. Na asomfoɔ no kaa sɛ: Ɛsiane saa adeɛ yi nti a ɛwɔ dua yi atifi no, yɛn agya yerenom reko; yɛbɛtwa no. Amonom hɔ ara wɔn nsa kaa wɔn ntontom, pimpen! pimpen! (ɛno ne ɛnne a wɔyɛɛ no.) Saa berɛ no na ɔkɔre no kɔ akɔneaba na ɔfaa ne mma na ɔgyaa wɔn wɔ Nkwatia ne Nkwatia ho. Bere a nkurɔfo twaa dua no na na ɛredwiriw agu no, Sekyi no teɛɛm sɛ, "Pimpeao!" Na dua no boaa wɔn ho ano. Na wɔsan baa ne nkyɛn bio. Na Sekyi no ka kyerɛɛ Akwakoraa no sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔhwehwɛ Ɔbɔfo no sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛba abɛhwɛ ne mma, efisɛ me deɛ, owu aba me so". Amonom hɔ ara na nkurɔfo no kɔfaa wɔn atuo no kɔtoo no kum no. Na Old-Hawk tu fii hɔ sɛ ɔrebɛfrɛ Ɔbɔfo no. Bere a ɔrekɔ no nyinaa na ɔteɛm sɛ:

"Agyabea ne wo mma o!

Nana e! wo mma o!"

Bere a ɔteɛteɛɛm saa na ɔkɔre no antie no, ɔmaa ne nne so teɛteɛɛm sɛ:

"Sankur'e, Nnomaa no e, sankur'e, Nnomaa no, Ɔbɔfo no.

Sankur'e, Nnomaa e, sankur'e, sɛ woakɔ adidi a, san bra".

Na Eagle tee. Na ɔbaa hɔ, na ɔbisaa sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na ɛrekɔ so yi?" Na Akwakoraa no kaa sɛ, "Wɔatwa dua no ato fam sɛ wɔbɛfa wo mma, na nea ɛfa Akwakoraa ho no, wɔato tuo ato no so akum no". Amonom hɔ ara Akwakoraa no sɔre, wa (ne ntaban no nne ne no), na ɔforo kɔɔ dua bi so wɔ kuropɔn no kwan ano, na ɔmaa n'ani so, na ɛyɛ nokware sɛ na wɔretwa dua no. Ɔkaa hwee. Na afei bere a dua no hwee ase no, ɔkɔre no de mmirika kɔɔ hɔ, ho (ne ntaban nne), na ɔfaa ne mma no. Na ɔfaa wɔn de wɔn guu dua no so, baabi a osii fam no. Na afei osian baa fam, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ho (wɔ ne ti mu) sɛ: "Ah, nnipa te sɛɛ anaa?" Na ɛmaa ne werɛ howee. Na osui, na ɔkae sɛ:

"Ade ka tuo no ho, ade ka ɔwɔ no ho".

Na oyii n'akyi, na onyaa mmea mpintin abien, na ɔbɔɔ wɔn, na ɔsomaa saa nkrasɛm yi:

"Momma mmoa a wɔwɔ ntaban nyinaa mmra, [kosi sɛ] ɔsoro bɛyɛ tuntum tuntum".

Na nnomaa a wɔwɔ ntaban no nyinaa bae. Na ɔkaa nea esii nyinaa kyerɛɛ wɔn, na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wɔne no kɔ na wɔkɔda Gyekye ase, na ɛsɛ sɛ obiara a onim sɛnea wosu nyinaa su. Anomaa a wɔfrɛ no Apitie na odii kan fii ase sui:

"Mɛwu mpofirim!

Ɛrenkyɛ mɛwu!"

Apatupere anomaa no teɛɛm sɛ:

"Mempɛ akasakasa!

Mempɛ akasakasa!

M'ani nnye Kosante mmofra atade no ho".

Akokofwedee, abɛn a ne nan yɛ kɔkɔɔ no, teɛɛm sɛ:

"Owuraye's ho, ho, O!

Owuraye no ho, ho, O!

Owuraye ho, ho, ho, O!" Atewa nnomaa ketewa no teɛteɛɛm sɛ:

"Doye!

Doye!

Afua Anwea ne (ne kunu) Ano Kofi, Sɛ abofra nantew prɛko pɛ a, ɛnnyɛ ade pa, Kwaku Amoaten (din).

Doye!

Doye!

Odiawuo ne ne Ano Mmirikisie a ogyaa Elephant tusso no, Ogyaw no, Ogyaw no.

Agya Ɔdɔmfoɔ, sɔre na kɔ na kɔhwehwɛ apataa a ne nhwi yɛ nketewa no na woatumi ayɛ kuruwa na woanom, (bere a ɔdɔmfoɔ no yere kasa saa no,)

Abɔde biara (a ɔte sɛ ɔreka saa) remma dua no ase (baabi a wɔwɔ) no".

Opodee anomaa no teɛteɛɛm sɛ:

"Kyakyakya! nkawurubi!

(Hwɛ) saa abofra ketewa no tuo tuntum no, ɔde retwe n'ani asi me so, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛka me ne ne nkyenenkyene a wɔakyekyere no ho".

Opatuo, Owl no, teɛɛm sɛ:

"E! Mm!

E! Mm!"

Onwam, Hornbill no teɛteɛɛm sɛ:

" ((Hunter) Akuaferi-Saka, kɔkɔɔ ketewa no!"

Hornbill yere ka sɛ:

"Ɔne no nni hɔ".

The Hornbill ka sɛ:

"Aso (ne yere), didi na ma yɛnkɔ".

Na Saka tow ne tuo too no, papa! Na ne (Hornbill) yere tu fii hɔ kae sɛ:

"Ɔwɔ hɔ!

Ɔwɔ hɔ!"

Na Aserewa nnomaa ketewa no teɛɛm sɛ:

"Obeko Asamoa, sɛ ɔkwasea bɔ ne borɔdɔma dua a, anɔpatutuutu no, nkurɔfo kɔ anim ne akyi kɔ hɔ!"

Aburuburo, Aborɔnoma no, teɛɛm sɛ:

"Bere a na me maame rewu no, ogyaw me ɔfasu bi a wɔakyekyere mu;

Ɛberɛ a aduaba no wuwuiɛ no, ɛgyaa me nnuaba dua no so.

Ɛberɛ a aduaba nhwiren no wuwuiɛ no, ɛgyaa me nsa wɔ dua a wɔakyekyere ho no so;

Bere a baa a wɔakyekyere no nyaa me no, ɔde me dii dwuma basabasa, basabasa, basabasa!"

Akyenkyena anomaa no teɛɛm sɛ:

Ɛyɛ me, ɛyɛ me, ɛyɛ me". Na afei Oguammaa no ankasa bae, na ɔteɛɛm sɛ:

"Nkensen nkensen, nnomaa a wɔwɔ ntaban ya ya!

Meda Gyekye ase!

Nkensen nkensen, feathered creatures ya ya!"

(Na bere a ɔteɛɛm saa no, kuro no mu nnipa nyinaa dwane firii hɔ, na Gyekye anhu baabi a mmarima, nnwan, mmaa, mmaa, ne nnomaa anaa biribiara kɔeɛ. Ɛkaa ɔno ne ne yere ne ne ba nko ara, wɔ odum dua no ntini ho. Na ɔkɔre no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: Efi nnɛ no, ɔhwehwɛni biara renyɛ ɔdefo da. Eyi ne m'asɛm a meka kyerɛ wo yi. Sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi yɛ nokware, na fa nea aka no ma me.
