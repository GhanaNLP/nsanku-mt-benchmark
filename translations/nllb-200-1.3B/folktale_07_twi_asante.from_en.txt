"Wo a w'ano yɛ fĩ no, wopɛ yɛn anigyesɛm?" Wosiim kɔe; wɔkɔɔ kwan so no, Akraman no kae sɛ: "Hwɛ! de mpa dedaw no brɛ me na fa sika nketewa yi na siesie wo ho, sɛ ɛyɛ mpa dedaw no nti na mmaayewa no dɔ wo a, mɛhwɛ".
