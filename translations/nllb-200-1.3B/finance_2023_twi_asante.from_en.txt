ASƐM A ƐDA SO

Akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ afe yi bɛyɛ yɛn man no abakɔsɛm mu afe a ɛyɛ den paa no mu biako. Te sɛ wiase nyinaa no, yɛn nso COVID-19 yare no kaa yɛn afe 2020 mfinimfini a edi kan no mu, a ɛmaa sikasɛm mu dwumadi ano brɛɛ ase, na ɛmaa yɛn ahonyade so tewee ne sika a yɛde dii dwuma wɔ ɔyaredɔm no ano.

Bere a na yesusuw sɛ yɛreyɛ nkɔso kɛse wɔ yɛn ahotɔ mu afi ɔhaw a ɔyare no de aba no mu bere a yɛfii ase de yɛn nsakrae nhyehyɛe no fii ase no, nneɛma pii sisii yɛn so a ɛmaa sikasɛm mu nkonimdi a yenyae wɔ afe 2017 kosi 2019 mu no sɛee. Nea edi kan ne Russia ne Ukraine ko no, na afei yɛsan twentwɛn so wɔ sika ho nhyehyɛe atitiriw a wɔyɛe wɔ 2022 bosea no mu, na ɛno na ɛmaa credit rating no so tewee. Mpofirim ara, mfe du a atwam ni no, wɔtotoo International Capital Market no mu maa yɛn, na ɔman no mu sikasɛm mu tebea yɛɛ den, na ɛka a wɔde tuaa ka no yɛɛ kɛse, na ɛmaa ɛka a wɔde tuae no ano yɛɛ den, na cedi no so tew ntɛmntɛm.

Enti bere a sikasɛm mu sikasɛm mu mmoa a efi amannɔne aguadi mu ne yɛn amannɔne nkontaabu a ɛbaa fam mpo no, yɛn asɛyɛde a ɛsen biara a na yɛwɔ sɛ ɔman, ne yɛn ka a na ɛreyɛ ayɛ kɛse a yɛde tua ka no, maa yɛn sika no so yɛɛ den. Eyi ama yɛn nyinaa, sɛ́ aban ne ankorankoro, anya asetena ho ahiade kɛse. Mmura ne ngo a ɛredɔɔso no ama nneɛma ne ɔsom ahorow dodow no ara so atew, na ɛreyɛ adu 40.4% wɔ October 2022 mu.

Wɔasɔ yɛn ahwɛ ankasa sɛ ɔman, na aban no wɔ mo ho tema kɛse wɔ nhyɛso a ɛmfata a eyi de aba no ho.
