Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ASƐM A ƐDA SO 'KRAMAN, Ɔkraman no, Wontumi Nyare N'akorɔn (Ɔkwan) Da

Ɔbarima bi tenaa kurow bi mu bere tiaa bi, na ɔbea no nso kɔtenaa kurow no mu bere tiaa bi, na bere tiaa bi akyi no, ne kunu no soma kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Me kunu no reba wo nkyɛn". N'ase no faa ne tuo no, na ɔkɔɔ kwae no mu sɛ ɔrekɔkum anantwinini bi de asiesie ne ho ama Ɔkraman no ba. Bɛyɛ nna abiɛsa akyi no, Ɔkraman no kɔɔ hɔ, na bere a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, ohuu sɛ anantwinini bi nam wɔ ogya so wɔ adiwo hɔ. Bere a anadwo no dui no, ɔne ne yere kɔhomee. Ná Ɔkraman no rebɛkɔ abɔnten akɔsaw nsu, bere a ohui sɛ nam no ne srade no retetew gya no mu no. Ɔkraman no san kɔdaa hɔ, na ɔde ne ti too mpaboa no so (na ɔdaa hɔ) na ɔhwɛɛ nam no mu kyɛe. Afei nam no huam duu no so; ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ obegyina ano, nanso wantumi. O! ɔsɔre kɔɔ baabi a na nam no wɔ no; ɔwewee ne nyinaa, na ɔsan kɔdaa hɔ. Ade kyee anɔpatutuutuutu no, bere a nneɛma no daa adi, bere a na ne yere rebɛpra no no, ɔhwehwɛɛ nam no, na na emu biako mpo nni hɔ. Ne yere kae sɛ: "Ao, maame! Ao, maame! Obi adi nam no nyinaa". Bere a Ɔkraman no tee eyi no, n'ani wui (ɛne sɛ, ɔfɛree), na ɔfaa n'atade kataa ne ti, na ɔdaa hɔ komm. Afei prɛko pɛ na ɔsɔre sii kwan so; oguan kɔɔ ne kurom, na ɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ no, sɛnea ɔkɔ kɔweaa n'ase no nam, ne sɛnea n'ani wui wɔ aniwu mu, ne sɛnea, esiane saa nti, na ɔrekɔhwehwɛ obi a ɔwɔ ɔkwae bi wɔ ɔkorɔmfo ho, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wɔde bi bɛma no. Nkurɔfo no kae sɛ, "Adanko, Ɔkraman no, wɔ tumi kɛse bi". Ɔkraman no fii hɔ, na ɔde ahoɔhare duu Ɔkraman no akuraa no ase. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Agya Agyata, mede me ho rehwehwɛ wo; meyɛ ɔkorɔmfo, ɔkorɔmfo, ɔkorɔmfo, na wɔka sɛ wowɔ ano aduru, enti mesrɛ wo sɛ fa bi ma me, na biribiara a ɛho behia no, metua". Agyata no kae sɛ: "Ɛyɛ nokware sɛ mewɔ bi, enti kɔ na kɔfa mprako nam brɛ me".

Akraman no fii hɔ kɔɔ akuraa bi ase, na ɔde sika mfrafrae a emu duru yɛ domafa biako (a ɛne sɛ 3s. 6d.) kɔfaa ne nan akyi biako bae. Okyekyeree no, na ɔtwaa mu, na ɔde ntasu nketenkete a wɔde nnua ayɛ no so, na ɔtwee pɛprɛ, nkyene, ne ntweaa, na ɔde guu nam no mu, na ɔde too ogya no so. Ɛhɔ ara na aboa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Kraman, kɔda dan baako mu, na me nso mɛda ɔfã baako mu, na ɔkyena anɔpa mɛyi nam yi afi gya no mu na mede ayɛ aduru ama wo". Na ɔkraman no kɔdaeɛ, na aboa no nso kɔdaeɛ. Na Ɔkraman no da so da so da so da so da so, nanso na ontumi nna, na nam no huam faa ne hwene mu, nanso wanka hwee. Na afei ɔhomee bio. Ɔkaa sɛ: "Agya Aberewa". Aberewa no buae sɛ: "Yiw!" Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Nkɔre wɔ wo akuraa no ase, enti mɛkɔ akɔfa nam no, na mede no ato me nkyɛn". Aberewa no kae sɛ: "Kɔ na kɔfa no. Me deɛ, nea ehia me ara ne sɛ mede me nsa begu so anɔpa, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a metumi de no ayɛ aduru ama wo". Enti ɔkraman no kɔɔ kɔfaa no. Afei na ɔkraman no da hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ: "Agya Aberewa". Obuae sɛ: "Yiw!" Obuae sɛ: "Nkɔre no bɛwe no, enti mede bɛhyɛ me mpa ase, na makɔda". Aberewa no kae sɛ: "Nea ɛhaw me ara ne sɛ, ɔkyena, sɛ nneɛma mu da hɔ a, mede no bɛyɛ aduru ama wo". Ɔkae sɛ, "Agya Aberewa, mmoadabi yi aba me mpa ase, enti mɛfa nam no de agu me ti so, na megyina hɔ akosi sɛ nneɛma no bɛda adi". Aberewa no buae sɛ, "Oh, meka ara sɛ, nea ɛhaw me ara ne sɛ mɛkɔ akosi sɛ mɛkɔ akosi sɛ mɛyɛ aduru ama wo". Na ɔkraman no faa nam no de guu ne ti so, na ɔgyinaa hɔ. Na wɔmaa no aduane no bi, na wɔmaa no aduane no bi, na wɔmaa no nso aduane no bi, na wɔmaa no aduane no bi, na wɔmaa no nso aduane no bi, na wɔmaa no nso aduane no bi, na wɔmaa no aduane no bi, na wɔmaa no nso aduane no bi, na wɔmaa no nso aduane no bi, na wɔmaa no nso aduane no bi, na wɔmaa no nso aduane no bi, na wɔmaa no nso aduane no bi, na wɔmaa no nso aduane no bi, na wɔmaa no nso aduane no bi, na wɔmaa no nso aduane no bi, na wɔmaa no nso aduane no bi, na wɔmaa no nso aduane no bi, na wɔmaa no nso aduane no bi, na wɔmaa no nso aduane no bi. Mɛkɔ so aka sɛ ɔkyena, bere a nneɛma bɛda adi no, me ne no bɛyɛ aduru ama wo". Seesei na ɛreyɛ ayɛ den wɔ n'anom, na otwaa ne nan ho hyiae wɔ dan no mu wɔ hɔ, na ɔde n'akyi twaa n'akyi (wɔ nne a ɛretwam), fre! fre! fre! Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Agya, me wura, me ne wo wɔ ka wɔ saa nam yi ho". Ɔkeka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Dɛn nti na wode me ka wɔ ho?" Ɔkeka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Me ne wo wɔ ka, na ɛnyɛ mmoadabi no na wɔbɛdidi". Ɔkeka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Dabi, ma ade nkye". Na afei ɔkekae nam no, na ɔhwehwee ase dae. Ade kyee anɔpatutuutuutu no, Aberewa no kae sɛ, "Ɔkraman, de nam no brɛ me". Aberewa no buae sɛ, "Oh, Agya Aberewa, adɛn nti na woante nea meka kyerɛɛ wo anwummere no, sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ midi wo ka (wɔ nam no ho) no?" Aberewa no buae sɛ, "Enti woadi?" Aberewa no kae sɛ, "Yiw! Mididii, efisɛ metɔɔ". Aberewa no kae sɛ, "Wo, Akraman, korɔnbɔ a wɔfrɛ no 'korɔnbɔ' no remma wo ho da".

Eyi, m'asɛm no, a maka ho asɛm no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
