Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ASƐM A ƐDA SO Nea Ɛmaa Asono Nkyɛn

BERE bi, ɔkɔm kɛse bi bae, na Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ Elephant, Esono nkyɛn, na ɔkae sɛ, Agya Elephant, ma yɛnkɔ (na yɛnkɔhwehwɛ) wuram yams. Elephant no kae sɛ, Bra, ma yɛnkɔ. ɔne Kwaku, Spider no fii hɔ kɔe. Seesei de besi saa bere no, na Elephant no wɔ n'akyi kɛse; na bere a ɔne Kwaku Ananse rebetwam hɔ no, na Elephant no akyi no bɛkyekyere nnomaa no mu, ma enti na wontumi ne wɔn ntena. Na Kwaku Ananse hwɛɛ no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔsono no sɛ: Ma menkyekyere w'akyi kakra, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ na yɛatumi ne no adi nkitaho ntɛm. Ɔsono no kae sɛ: Kyekyere na yɛnhwɛ (sɛ mfaso bi wɔ so anaa). Enti Nkyekyere no kyekyere nam, nkɛntɛn abiɛsa a wɔde nam ahyɛ no ma, fii n'akyi. Ananse de guu n'atifi. Ɔka kyerɛɛ Elephant no sɛ, Megye eyi na mede begu me fie na masan aba. Ananse baa fie; ɔkae sɛ, Aso (ne yere), matwa Elephant bi a ɔyɛ nkwaseafo panyin no nam nyinaa afi n'akyi. Aso buae sɛ, Nhyira, Apeafo. Ɛhɔ ara na Ananse ne ne mma ne ne yere too pon no mu, na Ananse maa Ntikuma (ne babarima) dono drum biako, Tikonokono biako, ne Ankyɛ na obi bɔɔ Ananse pon mu, pan! pan! pan! Ananse ne ne mma no maa wɔn nne so na wɔtoo dwom sɛ:

Hena na ɔrebɔ me pon mu?

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den!

Afei Elephant ba no (a n'agya asoma no) toaa so sɛ:

So ɛnyɛ me, Elephant no ba?

Ɛnyɛ me, Elephant ba no?

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den!

Na Ananse bɔɔ mpae sɛ:

Dɛn na wopɛ?

Dɛn na wopɛ?

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den!

Na ɔsono ba no teɛɛm sɛ:

Mereba sɛ merebɛtɔ nam a ɛwɔ me papa n'akyi no bi !

Mereba sɛ merebɛtɔ nam a ɛwɔ me papa n'akyi no bi !

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den!

Na Ananse teɛɛm sɛ:

W'agya nam no yɛ dɛ sen oguan nam !

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den!

Metwaa bi, sɛ merememem!

Metwaa bi, sɛ mede bɛma!

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den!



Na ɔsono ba no bɔɔ asaw, bɔɔ asaw, bɔɔ asaw, na osiim kɔe. Bere a ɔkɔɔ (fie) no, n'agya bisaa no sɛ, "Ɛhe na nam no wɔ?" Obuae sɛ, "Ananse nni hɔ bere a mekɔɔ no". Bere a wɔkɔɔ hɔ na wɔsan bae no, wɔn agya bisaa wɔn ho asɛm; wɔkae sɛ, Bere a mekɔɔ hɔ no, na onni hɔ. Nanso nea esii ankasa ne sɛ, bere a Ananse fii asaw nnwom ase ara pɛ na Elephant mma no dii asaw, dii asaw, dii asaw, na wɔn werɛ fii adwuma a wɔn agya somaa wɔn sɛ wɔnyɛ no nyinaa. Afei ɔkɛse no kaa sɛ, Me ara mɛkɔ akɔfa nea ɛyɛ me dea. Ɔduruu Ananse ɛpono no anim, na ɔkyekyeree ne ti de bɔɔ so, pan ! pan ! pan ! na Nkyekye no faa so (nkyekyere no):

Hena na ɔrebɔ me pon mu?

Hena na ɔrebɔ me pon mu?

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den!

Ɛhɔ ara na ɔsono teɛɛm sɛ:

So ɛnyɛ me, Agya Elephant?

Ɛnyɛ me, Agya Elephant?

Na Ananse teɛɛm sɛ:

Dɛn na wopɛ ?

Dɛn na wopɛ?

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den!

Na ɔsono teɛɛm sɛ:

Mereba sɛ merebeyi nam afi m'apampam mu !

Merekɔhwehwɛ nam a ɛwɔ m'akyi no !

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den! Na Ananse teɛɛm (ne nne):

"W'akyi nam yɛ dɛ sen nguanten nam!

Mitwaa bi de dii!

Mitwaa bi de kyɛɛ afoforo!

Kon! kon! kon! kon! kon!"

Agya Elephant bɔɔ asa, na ɔbɔɔ asa, na ɔbɔɔ asa. Afei ɔkae sɛ, "Nana, bra ha". Nanan no kae sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ meba sɛ wobɛtiatia me so akum me anaa? Nkwan no kae sɛ, "Sɛ woba a, merenyɛ wo hwee, na sɛ biribi a ɛte saa betumi ayɛ yiye a, yebesusuw sɛ mede m'akyi na atɔ asaw yi". Nkwan no fii adi bɔɔ, bɔɔ, bɔɔ, bɔɔ, na ɔmaa Nkwan no bɔɔ asaw, bɔɔ asaw, bɔɔ asaw; na afei Nkwan no fii hɔ kɔe. Ɛno nti na Elephant akyi yɛ ketewa saa; ɛyɛ asaw a ɔtɔeɛ no nti; bere bi a atwam no na ɛnte saa.

Eyi, m'asɛm no, a maka ho asɛm no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
