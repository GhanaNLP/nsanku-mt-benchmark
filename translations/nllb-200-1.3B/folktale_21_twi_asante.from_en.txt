Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ASƐM A ƐKYERƐW OWEA (Nkramoa), ABOSOM-AKOTERE (KAMELEON), NE OPATUO (ƆKƆWƆWƆWƆ)

OWEA, Dua-bear, (a ɔwɔ) Kwaduampon Kyerefo, Opatuo, Owl, ne Abosom-Akotere, the Chameleon, (a wɔn din ne), Ɔbɔadeɛ no wu, The take-a-year-to-do-anything-creature, He-walks-very-slowly-but-he-will-reach-the-land-of-white-men-far-away, (abɔde yi nyinaa) traa faako bere bi. Da bi, bere a ɛyɛ Ɔbɔadeɛ Owuo (sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛhyɛ Adehyeɛ no som no) ne ne berɛ no, ɔhwehwɛɛ Adehyeɛ dua no, ɛhe na na ɔbɛhunu no? Na ɔkɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ Dua-Bear ne Owl. Na wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Kɔfrɛ no. Na Ɔbɔadeɛ Owuo maa ne nne so sɛ: Adobe o! Adobe o!

Dua-bear no teɛteɛɛm sɛ:

Bere a ɔkɔe no, wansan amma; ɔkɔɔ, wansan amma, wansan amma, wansan amma!

Na Owl frɛɛ sɛ:

"Yɛasusuw [sika mfutuma] no na anka yɛbɛtow agu, m!

Na ɛno ne nneɛma abiɛsa yi su fi.

Eyi, m'asɛm no, a maka ho asɛm no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
