Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ASƐM A ƐKYERƐW OWEA (Nkramoa), ABOSOM-AKOTERE (KAMELEON), NE OPATUO (ƆKƆWƆWƆWƆ)

OWEA, Dua-bear, (a ɔwɔ din nso sɛ) Kwaduampon Kyerefo, Opatuo, Owl, ne Abosom-Akotere, Chameleon, (a wɔn din ne), Ɔbɔadeɛ no wu, Abɔde a ɔde afe biako yɛ biribiara, Ɔnantew-akyɛ-nanso-na-obedu-aborɔfo-asase-a-wɔ akyirikyiri, (mmoa yi nyinaa) traa faako bere bi. Afei, na wɔn mu biara nni ɔyere. Na Adobe-dua no tɔn ne ho maa wɔn. Da biara, sɛ Adobe-dua no sɔre a, ɔkɔ kɔhwɛ saa dua yi; da a edi hɔ no, ɔhwɛ wɔn mu biako so; na da a edi hɔ no, ɔhwɛ ɔfoforo so. Da bi, bere a na ɛyɛ Ɔbɔadeɛ no wu (sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obedi Adobe-dua no dwuma) ho dwuma no, ɔhwehwɛɛ Adobe-dua no, baabi a obetumi ahu no no. Na ɔkɔe sɛ ɔrekɔka asɛm no akyerɛ Tree-bear ne Owl. Na wɔkae sɛ: "Kɔfrɛ no". Na Owu-Bɔfo no maa ne nne so sɛ: "Adobe o! Adobe o!"

Dua-bear no teɛteɛɛm sɛ:

Bere a ɔkɔe no, wansan amma; ɔkɔɔ, wansan amma, wansan amma, wansan amma!

Na Owl frɛɛ sɛ:

"Yɛasusuw [sika mfutuma] no na anka yɛbɛtow agu, m!

Na ɛno ne nneɛma abiɛsa yi su fi.

Eyi, m'asɛm no, a maka ho asɛm no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
