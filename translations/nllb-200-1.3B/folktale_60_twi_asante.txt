OBOFUO Kwaku and his wife Buruwa were in need of yen and we went out and tied him up. Obofuo Kwaku went out in the wild and killed the mouse. When the owner came home, he said, "this is the one that won't take it". When he killed the mouse, he said, "this is the one that won't take it". His wife saw the money, and asked him, "Why don't you give me the gold, why don't you give me the meat, and what's better?" He said, "Tell me, if we didn't sing, then I'd have lost my money". One day he killed a mouse, and he built a house on the road, and his wife followed him, killed him, and then he found out that the owner had put the money in his hand, and he ate and drank again. And his daughter came back. He said, "O Ekudonto". He said, "Why do you mock me?" He said, "It is your own mind". 'Do not be angry.' 'Ono, so early in the morning, his wife was in the field.' 'Ono, so early in the morning.' He said, 'Ooh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh!'
