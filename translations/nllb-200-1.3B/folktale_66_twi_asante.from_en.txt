BERE bi a atwam no, Kokokyinaka anomaa no kɔɔ Ananse, Nnomaa no nkyɛn kɔpɛɛ sika mfrafraa a emu duru yɛ asuanus (£4). Afei, esiane sɛ na Ananse ankasa nni bi nti, ɔkɔɔ n'adamfo Ntontom, Mosquito no nkyɛn kɔhwehwɛɛ bi de brɛɛ n'adamfo Kokokyinaka anomaa no. Da a mmoadabi no ahyɛ no twaam, na mmoadabi no ka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔfa me sika mfrafra". Ananse buae sɛ, "Kokokyinaka nnomaa no te Silk-cotton dua no atifi, enti kɔfa wo sika mfrafra". Mmoadabi no bisae sɛ, "Mɛyɛ dɛn akɔfa; aboa no tumi tu". Bere a na ɛrebobɔ mu no, Kokokyinaka anomaa no tu fii hɔ, pa bu bu bu! Afei Mosquito no bae, na ɔkae sɛ, M'ani agye, Ananse. Ɛno nti na yɛwɔ asɛm bi a ɛka sɛ, Abɔde a wɔfrɛ no Kokokyinaka anomaa no amfa amfi Ananse hɔ sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ Mosquito no kɔ na otwa Silk-cotton-tree no.
