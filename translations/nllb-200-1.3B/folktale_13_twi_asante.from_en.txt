ASƐM BƐN NA AKOKONINI, Ɔkokɔ no, Nyaa N'ano

NA PAPA ANANSE, N'aborɔ no, ne ne mma Afudotwedotwe (Belly-like-to-burst), Nyiwankonfwea (Thin-shanks), (na) Tikonokono (Big-big-head) te hɔ. Na N'aborɔ no ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ mokɔhwehwɛ borɔdɔma ma midi". Enti mmofra yi fii hɔ; wɔkɔɔ so nantewee, na wɔkɔɔ so nantewee, na wohyiaa borɔdɔma bi, na (borɔdɔma no) ayɛ frɔmfrɔm, na mmofra no kɔɔ ase (dua no ase). Afudotwedotwe (Belly-like-to-burst) ka kyerɛɛ Nyiwankonfwea (Thin-shanks) sɛ, "Kɔ soro na yi bi". Ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, sɛ mekɔ soro a, me nan bɛbubu". Tikonokono (Big-big-head) kae sɛ, "Wo, Afudotwedotwe, kɔ soro". Ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, sɛ mekɔ soro a, me yafunu bɛpae; wo ara kɔ soro". Enti Tikonokono (Big-big-head) foro. Na ɔfaa borɔdɔma nnua no bi, na ɔkɔɔ so kekaa no, na ɔkae sɛ: "Ɛbɛyɛ dɛn?" Na wɔn a aka no kae sɛ: "Ah! (yɛyɛ dɛn na yenim?) " Enti ɔkɔɔ so twee wɔn, na afei obisaa wɔn bio sɛ: "Ɛbɛyɛ dɛn?" Wɔkae sɛ: "Mommrɛ mo ti ase na monhwɛ". Na bere a na ɔrebɛbrɛ ne ti ase ahwɛ no, ne ti bɔɔ fam, na ɔkae sɛ (asɛm bi te sɛ) "Kum!" Bere a na Belly-like-to-burst aserew no, ne yafunu paee kɛse, ta! Thin-legs, bere a na ɔretu mmirika akɔka akyerɛ ne papa no, ne nan bubui na ɔtraa ɔfasu no akyi. Na akokɔ no rehohoro ne ho, na ɔrebɛtow ne nyɛmmoa no. Na ɔhunuu sɛ ne nan abutu, na ne nan abubu, na wɔagyaw no ɔfasuo no akyi, na ɔrebɛserew no, n'ano yɛɛ den.

"Bere a na ɔrebɛserew no, N'ano yɛɛ den.

Bere a na ɔrebɛserew no, N'ano yɛɛ den".

Ɛno nti na mpanimfoɔ no ka sɛ, "Sɛ obi hwe ase a, kɔ na kɔhwɛ no, na nserew". Na akokɔ no nso yɛ saa, na ɔserew, enti n'ano yɛɛ ntontom; kan no na n'ano yɛ fɛ, na n'ano yɛ fɛ.

Eyi, m'asɛm no, a maka ho asɛm no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
