1926

MFE ƆHA A EDI NNIPA

"BERE a Onyankopɔn bɛyɛ ne din ho adansefo wɔ asase so no rebɛn", sɛnea January 1, 1926, Ɔwɛn-Aban no kae no.

Bere a afe 1926 rekɔ so no, Kristo nam asafo no ti so maa emu daa hɔ pefee sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ asɛnka adwuma no yɛ nea ehia paa wɔ Kristofo asetena mu. Ɛno nti, "akoa nokwafo ne ɔbadwemma" no de nhoma foforo maa Onyankopɔn asomfo, ɔyɛɛ nsakrae wɔ asafo nhyiam ahorow mu, na ɔmaa wɔtee Kristofo asɛyɛde ahorow ase yiye. (Mat. 24:45) Kristo maa wonyaa adan ne nnwinnade a wohia na ama wɔatumi ayɛ asɛnka adwuma no.

Nhoma Foforo ne Nhyehyɛe Foforo a Wɔde Ama Nhyiam Ahorow

Wɔ April mu no, The Golden Age (seesei wɔfrɛ no Nyan!) no bɔɔ amanneɛ sɛ wɔbɛyi nhoma foforo bi a wɔato din Deliverance adi. Ná saa nhoma yi besi Studies in the Scriptures no ananmu sɛ nhoma titiriw a yɛde kɔ asɛnka.

Mfe pii na Bible Asuafo no de nhoma a wɔato din Studies in the Scriptures no suaa ade wɔ wɔn nhyiam ahorow ase, nanso efi May no, wofii ase kenkan Ɔwɛn-Aban mu nsɛm.

Bunker Hill, Illinois ecclesia [asafo] ketewa no kyia mo, kyerɛw asafo bi wɔ krataa bi a wɔde kɔmaa Onua Rutherford mu. Yɛn ani sɔ paa sɛ yɛn nyinaa tumi hu hann foforo no bere a ɛreba.

Hann foforo, anaa ntease a emu da hɔ, boaa Bible Asuafo no ma wɔde wɔn adwene sii asɛnka adwuma no so. Sɛ nhwɛso no, asɛm Suban Anaa Apam Nea? kyerɛkyerɛɛ mu sɛ suban a wɔde siesie, a wɔde wɔn adwene si abrabɔ pa ne ayamye so nkutoo no ma ebinom bu adwuma a nokware Kristofo yɛ no adewa. Asɛm no tu fo sɛ: Nea ɔdɔ Awurade no . . . ɛsɛ sɛ odi n'ahyɛde so. . . . Yehowa ka sɛ: Mone m'adansefo sɛ mene Onyankopɔn. . .

Asɛm no hyɛɛ Bible Asuafo no nyinaa nkuran sɛ wɔmfa wɔn ho nhyɛ Awurade som adwuma no mu denneennen, wɔmfa nsi ne ahokeka, na wɔnyɛ Awurade adansefo na wɔnka n'asɛm nkyerɛ asase so aman ne nnipa nyinaa. - Yes. 43:10, 12; Mat. 24:14.

2 ƆWƐN-ABAN

Nsakrae yi nyaa nkɛntɛnso ntɛmntɛm wɔ asafo ahorow no mu. Nnawɔtwe kakraa bi akyi no, July 15, 1926, Ɔwɛn-Aban no tintim krataa a edi so yi: Nsɛm a ɛbaa nnansa yi, Ɔsono Anaa Apam? ne Abɔde ne Som, yɛ nkuranhyɛ ne awerɛkyekye kɛse. Yentumi nkenkan yɛn Ɔwɛn-Aban no kɛkɛ seesei, ɛsɛ sɛ yesua. Krataa no baa awiei sɛ Ɛte sɛ nea obiara wɔ ɔpɛ kɛse dedaw sɛ ɔbɛkyerɛ ne nsusuwii mu pefee wɔ adesua ahorow ase, na ɛreboa yɛn ecclesia no yiye.

Nhyiam Ahorow a Ɛma Asɛnka Adwuma no Ho Hia

Wɔ May awiei no, Bible Asuafo pii hyiaam wɔ nhyiam bi ase wɔ London, England. Adwumayɛfo a na wɔwɔ Britain no yɛɛ adwuma a na ɛyɛ den sɛ wɔbɛfa nhoma tintimfo, enti anuanom a wɔwɔ Brooklyn Betel no yɛɛ adwuma bere tiaa mu de nhomawa foforo 117,000 a wɔato din The Standard for the People maa nhyiamfo no.

Memeneda, May 29 no, wɔde saa nhomawa no dii dwuma yiye. Wɔhyɛɛ nhyiam no ho nkuran saa da no sɛ: Wɔto nsa frɛ wɔn a wɔahyira wɔn ho so a wɔbɛkɔ nhyiam no nyinaa sɛ wɔmfa wɔn ho nhyɛ Ɔhene no ne n'Ahenni no ho dawuru mu.

Wɔkyekyɛɛ nhoma bɛboro 100,000. July 15, 1926, Ɔwɛn-Aban no bɔɔ amanneɛ sɛ: Ná nnamfo no ani gye paa. Wɔtee nka sɛ wɔayɛ nea wobetumi biara de adi Yehowa ahyɛde yi so: Mone m'adansefo sɛ mene Onyankopɔn.

Ade kyee no, wɔ Royal Albert Hall no, Onua Rutherford maa ɔkasa a n'asɛmti ne Why World Powers Are TotteringThe Remedy. Bere a wobuee dan no apon no akyi simma kakraa bi no, na nnipa 13,000 ahyɛ asa no so ma.

Onua Rutherford kyerɛkyerɛɛ mu wɔ ne kasa no mu sɛ wiase tumidi ahorow no ahwe ase esiane sɛ wɔapo Yehowa ne N'ahenni no nti.

NEW YORK Kurow Nhyiam

Ɔmantam nhyiam kɛse a etwa to a wɔyɛe wɔ saa afe no mu no, wɔyɛɛ no October 14-18, 1926 wɔ New York City. Atesɛm nkrataa a ɛwɔ hɔ no penee so sɛ wɔbɛbɔ Onua Rutherford kasa a ɛne "Asase So Ɔko Kɛse No Abɛn" no ho dawuru.

Onua R. J. Martin yii kratawa foforo bi a n'asɛmti ne "Against the People" (Nnipa Ho Ɔhaw) adi wɔ Fida, October 15, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wɔbɛda nsɛnnennen a ɛwɔ atesɛm nkrataa mu ne Onua Rutherford kasa no adi. Saa anwummere no, na wɔakyekyɛ nhoma 300,000.

Wɔ dapɛn awiei no, wɔkyekyɛɛ nkratawa bɛyɛ ɔpepem biako!

Fa Wo Gyidi Si Ntini a Emu Yɛ Den So

BERE a wo ne Yehowa Adansefo suaa Bible no, dɛn na ɛma wugye dii sɛ woahu gyidi biako pɛ no? ɔsom kwan a ɛsɔ Onyankopɔn ani no? (Efe. 4:4-6) Ɔdɔ a wuhui wɔ Yehowa nkurɔfo mu nti na w'ani begyee wɔn ho anaa?

Na sɛ wo yɔnko gyidini bi yɛ bɔne a anibere wom, te sɛ nea ɛtoo onua bi wɔ afeha a edi kan no mu wɔ Korinto asafo no mu no nso ɛ? (1 Kor. 5:1) Sɛ wo gyidi gyina sɛnea afoforo nneyɛe ma wote nka nkutoo so a, ɛkyerɛ sɛ wo gyidi gyina fapem a ɛyɛ mmerɛw so.

Gyidi a Ɛwɔ N'ase Pintinn

Sɛ wode Bible mu nokware a wusuae no mu gyidi a edi mũ hyɛ wo gyidi den a, ebetumi ama wo gyidi ayɛ den.

Bere bi, Yesu asuafo pii gyaw no hɔ, efisɛ asɛm a ɔkae sɛ wɔbɛdi ne honam na wɔanom ne mogya no maa wɔn werɛ howee, na wɔkaa sɛ: "Asɛm yi yɛ hu". Yesu asɛm no haw wɔn araa ma wogyaee n'akyi di. - Yohane 6:56-60, 66.

Ɛno akyi no, Yesu bisaa n'asomafo no sɛ: "Mo nso mopɛ sɛ mokɔ anaa?" Petro buaa no sɛ: "Awurade, hena nkyɛn na yɛbɛkɔ? Wo na wowɔ daa nkwa nsɛm; na yɛagye adi, na yɛahu sɛ wone Nyankopɔn kronkronni no". - Yohane 6:67-69.

Petro kae sɛ: "Yɛagye adi na yɛahu sɛ wone Onyankopɔn Kronkronni no". Ná saa anokwafo yi nim nea wogye di ne nea enti a wogye di. Bere a wɔde Yesu nsɛm ne ne nneyɛe totoo nea wonim wɔ Kyerɛwnsɛm mu ho no, wogye dii sɛ Yesu ne Mesia no.

Nea enti a ɛte saa ne sɛ, Bible nkyerɛkyerɛ - a nokware a yɛasua afa Onyankopɔn din, n'atirimpɔw, ne agyede no ho no ka ho - nsesa da.

Sɛ yɛyɛ saa a, yɛn gyidi bɛgyina fapem a ɛyɛ den so, na afoforo nsɛm anaa wɔn nneyɛe renhaw yɛn. - Dw. 16:8; hwɛ adaka a wɔato din Tena Ɔkwan a Ɛteɛ no So.

Kɔ So Fa Ɔkwan a Ɛteɛ no So

Fa no sɛ worekɔ bɔs bi mu a worekɔ baabi a ɛyɛ fɛ kɔfa kilomita ɔhaha pii. Saa akwantu tenten no mu no, ebia wobɛte nka pii - nea ɛsen ne nyinaa no, w'ani begye sɛ worekɔ baabi.

Nanso, bere a wowɔ akwantu yi mu no, nneɛma bi betumi ama wo bo afuw - ebia nkɔmmɔ a emu yɛ den a ɛkɔ so wɔ akwantufo baanu ntam anaa ofirikafo no animguase mpo - nanso emu biara ntumi nsesa nokwasɛm a ɛne sɛ wowɔ bɔs a ɛfata mu, na ehia sɛ wotena so ansa na woadu baabi a worekɔ no.

Bere a yɛretu kwan akɔ Onyankopɔn wiase foforo no mu no, yebenya nkate ahorow pii - titiriw anigye a yenya wɔ hokwan a yɛanya sɛ yɛyɛ Onyankopɔn ahyehyɛde no fã no ho. Nanso, ɛtɔ mmere bi a, ebia yɛn yɔnko gyidini bi bɛhaw yɛn anaa obi a na yɛn ani gye ne ho bɔne a ɔyɛ no bɛma yɛn ho adwiriw yɛn.
