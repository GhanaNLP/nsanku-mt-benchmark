ASƐM A ƐDA SO

COVID-19 asorɔkye no anya nkɛntɛnso bɔne wɔ aman nyinaa mu, a Ghana ka ho. Ɔhaw a Ghanafoɔ afa mu denam nnipa asetena, wɔn asetena ne wɔn nnwuma a wɔhweree so no nni aban no akyi. Ɛyɛ me anigye sɛ mɛka sɛ yɛn anoyie a yɛde dii asorɔkye no nkɛntɛnso ho dwuma no yɛ akokoɔduru na emu yɛ den, na w'ani agye ho wɔ wiase afanan nyinaa.

Bio nso, sɛ́ mmuaeɛ ama asiane ne hokwan ahorow a ɛresisi wɔ ɔyaredɔm no mu no, Ɔmanpanyin Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo nanmusini ase no, aban no yɛɛ GH¢100 ɔpepepem COVID-19 Ahobammɔ ne Nhyehyɛe a Ɛreboa Nnwumakuw (GhanaCARES Obaatan Pa) no ntɛmntɛm. Eyi ama yɛn nkurɔfo anya ahotɔ ntɛmntɛm na ɛrehyɛ yɛn man no den.

Yɛn ani sɔ mo gyidi a mowɔ wɔ aban no mu bere a yɛreyɛ adwuma de asiesie yɛn ho yiye wɔ ɔyaredɔm no ho no. Wɔ mmere yi mu no, yɛn ahoɔden a yɛwɔ sɛ ɔman no ayɛ sɔhwɛ ama yɛn. Bible ma yɛn akwankyerɛ bere nyinaa wɔ mmere a ɛte saa mu. Esiane eyi nti, yɛbɛkɔ so de nhyehyɛe ne nhyehyɛe ahorow a ɛhwehwɛ sɛ wɔsan de asase a wɔagyaw no nyinaa si hɔ, na wɔama nnipa asetena mu ayɛ yiye no ayɛ adwuma.

Ade titiriw biako a ɛwɔ 2022 Ntoboa Nhyehyɛe no mu ne sɛ, mfe abiɛsa a edi hɔ no mu no, yɛde yɛn adwene besi adwuma a ɛbɛkɔ so wɔ wiase nyinaa no so. Ade titiriw a ɛwɔ Ntoboa Nhyehyɛe no mu ne sɛ, yɛde yɛn adwene besi adwuma a ɛbɛkɔ so wɔ wiase nyinaa no so, na yɛde yɛn adwene asi adwuma a ɛbɛkɔ so wɔ wiase nyinaa no so.

Nea ɛbɛyɛ na ama mmerante ne mmabaa anya ahotoso na wɔatumi ayɛ wɔn adwuma no, aban no bɛhyɛ YouStart dwumadi no mu den wɔ afe 2022 mu de ama mmerante ne mmabaa a wɔyɛ adwuma no anya sika a wɔde bɛhyɛ wɔn adwuma ase anaa wɔde bɛtrɛw mu.

Wɔ 2022 Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛe no mu no, yɛahyehyɛ nhyehyɛe a ɛbɛma yɛakɔ so adi sikasɛm mu nsɛnnennen a ɛda yɛn anim no ho dwuma na yɛakɔ so ayɛ den wɔ ɔyare no akyi bere no.

35 Ghana Aban - Afe 2022 Ɔmanfo Sikasɛm

Aman a Wɔyɛ Adwumayɛ Ho Adwuma a Ɛbɛkɔ So: Sika a Wɔde Si Hɔ ne Adwumayɛbea Ho Adwuma a Wɔbɛbɔ

Nsɛmfua Nhyehyɛe (Budget Terminology)

1D1F (One District, One Factory): Adwumayɛbea ne mfiridwuma a wɔde besi hɔ, a ɛno nso bɛma ɔman no atu akɔ adwadie a ɛreyɛ kɛse mu. Ɛho botae ne sɛ ɛbɛma Ghanafoɔ anya nnwuma pii wɔ ɔman no mu nyinaa.

Asɛyɛde: Eyi ne bere a aban wɔ asɛyɛde wɔ ne manfo anim denam ne dwumadi ahorow a ɔma wohu ne nneyɛe a ɛtete saa ho nkontaabu so. Eyi betumi aka sika anaa agyapade afoforo a aban no de ahyɛ ne nsa no ho asɛyɛde.

Adwumayɛ: Adeyɛ a ɛne sɛ wɔbɛkyekyɛ anaa wɔbɛkyekyɛ nneɛma mu wɔ aban adwumayɛbea ne dwumadibea ahorow mu.

Awerɛkyekye: Eyi ne ɔkwan a wɔfa so ma ɔhaw no so tew.

Sikasɛm: Sikasɛm yɛ nhyehyɛe a ɛkyerɛ baabi a wobenya sika afi ne nneɛma a wɔde bɛsɛe sika no. Sɛ nhwɛso no, abusua bi betumi ayɛ sikasɛm ho nhyehyɛe a ɛbɛkyerɛ sika dodow a wɔwɔ ne sɛnea ɛsɛ sɛ wɔde sika no di kan de di dwuma (te sɛ, danta, nneɛma a wɔde yɛ adwuma ne aduan).

Sikasɛm mu nsunsuanso: Ɛba bere a aban no tua sika sen nea wonya no.

Sika a Wɔde Tua Ho Ka: Eyi ne sika a wɔde yɛ nnwuma atitiriw te sɛ akwan, sukuu, ayaresabea, ntenten, akwantu, nsu ho nhyehyɛe, nnua ne mfiri, ne nea ɛkeka ho.

Adwumayɛfo akatua: Eyinom ne akatua ne akatua a ɛne akatua wɔ abusuabɔ te sɛ ahobammɔ, sika ne pɛnhyen a wɔde ma aban adwumayɛfo.

Nkɔanim Afoforo (DPs): Ɛne aman ne/anaa ahyehyɛde ahorow a wɔne aman a wɔrenya nkɔso no bom yɛ adwuma de di wɔn nkɔso ho botae ahorow ho dwuma. Nkɔanim Afoforo (DPs) betumi ayɛ aman pii, te sɛ International Monetary Fund (IMF), World Bank, African Development Bank (AfDB) anaa aman abien a ɛka aman bi te sɛ Germany, United Kingdom ne United States of America (USA) bom.

Afe Awiei Dodow: Dodow a ɛkɔɔ so wɔ December mu wɔ afe a wɔresusuw ho no mu.

Aman a Wɔyɛ Adwumayɛ Ho Adwuma a Ɛbɛkɔ So: Sika a Wɔde Si Hɔ ne Adwumayɛbea Ho Adwuma a Wɔbɛbɔ

Sika a Wɔde Di Dodow: Ɛba saa bere a aban no sika a wɔde di dwuma no boro sika a wɔagye atom wɔ Ɔman no mu no so.

FREE SHS: Free SHS (Free Senior High School) nhyehyɛeɛ no yɛ Ghana aban nhyehyɛeɛ a Ɔmampanyin Nana Akuffo-Addo aban no de too dwa wɔ September 2017 mu a ɛma JHS sukuuni biara a ɔwɔ nhomanim a ɔbɛtumi akɔ SHS adesua kwa.

Ntoboa: Eyinom yɛ sika a aban nya fi wɔn a wɔboa ma nkɔso no hɔ, na ɛho nhia sɛ wɔde tua aban no ka.

Ntoboa a wɔde ma aban akuw afoforo: Eyinom yɛ ntoboa a mmara hwehwɛ sɛ wɔde ma te sɛ Ɔmantam Nhyiamu Common Fund (DACF), Road Fund, Ghana Education Trust Fund (GETFUND), Petroleum Related Fund ne National Health Fund.

Domestic Product (GDP): Eyi gyina hɔ ma nneɛma ne nnwuma a wɔyɛ wɔ ɔman no mu wɔ bere pɔtee bi mu.

Amanaman Ntam Sika Ahorow (Gross International Reserves): Eyi yɛ ɔman bi tumi wɔ amannɔne sika mu a ɔde bɛtɔ nneɛma ne nnwuma afi amannɔne aman mu wɔ bere bi mu; ɛsan nso boa sɛnea amannɔne sika mu yɛ den wɔ amannɔne sika ho.

Ahonyade ne ɔsom ho nneɛma a ɛho hia no: Ɛyɛ nneɛma ne ɔsom ho nneɛma a ɛho hia no.

IPEP (Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme): Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme (IPEP) no yɛ NPP aban no nhyehyɛe titiriw no mu biako a n'atirimpɔw ne sɛ ɛbɛtu ohia ase na ama nsonsonoe a ɛwɔ mmusua a wɔwɔ nkuraase ne wɔn a wɔdi hia mu no so atew.

Sikasɛm mu botae: Saa botae ahorow yi yɛ ade a wɔde di dwuma de hwehwɛ sikasɛm mu nkontaabu. Nhwɛso bi ne sikasɛm mu nkɔanim, sikasɛm mu nkɔanim, adwuma mu nsakrae, nsisi ne aman foforo, sikasɛm mu nhyehyɛɛ a aban no ayɛ no mu nkontaabu anaa nkontaabu, ne gyinaesi a Aban a ɛwɔ Ghana de di dwuma wɔ sikasɛm mu sikasɛm mu nkontaabu ne sikasɛm mu nkontaabu ho.

MSME (Micro And Small Buisness Developmet Programme): Wɔtaa kyerɛ micro-enterprise (anaa microenterprise) ase sɛ adwuma ketewaa bi a nnipa kakraa bi na wɔyɛ, na wɔn akontaabu anaa wɔn adwuma a wɔyɛ no nnu sika pɔtee bi.

NABCo (Nation Builders Corps): a wɔsan frɛ no NABCO no yɛ nhyehyɛeɛ a Ghana aban de ama sukuufo a wɔnni adwuma a wɔresane akɔ sukuupɔn mu ama wɔatumi adi asetenam nsɛnnennen ahodoɔ ho dwuma wɔ aban ne aban nyinaa mu, na wɔama wɔn nimdeɛ a wɔde bɛboa ɔman no sikasɛm mu.

NBSSI (National Board For Small And Medium Scale Industry): National Board for Small Scale Industries (NBSSI) yɛ aban dwumadibea bi a mfaso nni so a ɛwɔ Adwumayɛ ne Adwumam Adwumayɛbea no ase, na wɔde ahyɛ ne nsa sɛ ɔnhyɛ Micro ne Small Enterprises (MSEs) nkɔsoɔ ne wɔn nkɔsoɔ ho nkuran.

Nea Ɛmfa Ngo Ho: Ɛyɛ adwuma a wɔyɛ wɔ sikasɛm mu a wɔayi adwuma a wɔyɛ wɔ ngo mu afi mu, na wɔde sikasɛm mu ahokyere a ɛrekɔ so no di dwuma.

Sika a Enni Toɔ: Sika a aban nya fi tow mu, te sɛ sika a wɔde gye tumi krataa anaa tumi krataa, sika a wɔde di dwuma ne nneɛma foforo.

Nneɛma a ɛda so wɔ hɔ: Nneɛma a ɛda so wɔ hɔ no bi ne nneɛma a MDA no ahyɛ ho bɔ no a wɔde ama aban no ne sika ho dwumadibea no ne nneɛma a wɔde ama aban no wɔ afe 2016 mu no.

Primary balance: Eyi ne nsonsonoe a ɛda sika a aban no nya ne ne sika a wɔde tua ho ka (a ɛka ɛka ɛka a wɔde tua ho ka no).

Growth Rate a Wɔhwɛ Kwan: Sɛnea Ghana sikasɛm bɛnyini wɔ afe biako mu.

Akatua: Eyi ne sika dodow a aban no nya fi amannɔne ne amannɔne nyinaa de yɛ n'adwuma.

RFJ (Rearing For Food and Jobs): ɛbɛboa ma mmoa ho adwuma no ayɛ yiye, na ama ɔman no mu adwuma no akɔ anim, ama mmoa ho nneɛma a wɔde ba ɔman no mu no so atew, ama wɔatumi anya adwuma, na ama wɔn a wodi mmoa ho dwuma no asetena atu mpɔn.
