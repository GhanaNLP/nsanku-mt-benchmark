Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Wɔ ɔkwan biara so, baabiara a wubehu sɛ Osebo, Ɔsebɔ no, ayɛ ade, ɛhɔ na Adanko, Ɔsebɔ no, ayɛ ade dedaw

Wɔka sɛ bere bi a atwam no, na akekantwɛre barima bi ne akekantwɛre bea bi, akekantwɛre barima bi ne akekantwɛre bea bi te akuraa ketewaa bi ase, na ɔkɔm kɛse bi bae. Na akekantwɛre bea no kae sɛ ɔrekɔ asubɔnten no ho. Na ɔrekɔ no, ohyiaa akekantwɛre bea bi a ɔresesa awi. Ɔbea no kae sɛ, "Ah, maame, ɛhe na wunyae biribi a ɛte saa?" Ɔbea no buae sɛ, "Ɔkwan a ɛtrɛw wɔ ha, na na me kunu di akyi rekɔhwehwɛ yams wuram, na ɔkɔɔ kɔfaa awi yi, enti fa yi (na) kɔhyew na kɔwe. Ɔbea no buae sɛ, Meda wo ase, Aku. Afei ɔbaa no faa no kɔɔ fie, kɔhyew no. Na ne kunu bae, na obisaa no. Na ɔkae sɛ: Ɔbea Hare no de maa me, na ɔkae sɛ ɔkwan a ɛtrɛw da n'akyi, na ne kunu faa so, na ɔde (aduane bi) brɛɛ no. Ɔbarima Leopard no kae sɛ: Ɔkyena mɛkɔ hɔ anɔpatutuutu. Enti ofii adi. Bere a ɔkɔe no, na aduane pii wɔ hɔ. Afei ɔfaa ne peaw na otwaa bi, na ɔyɛɛ aduaba, na ɔboaboaa (aduane no) ano, na ɔde guu mu. Na Adanko, ɔbarima Hare no, wɔ ne fie, na ohuu sɛ ɔbarima Leopard no atwa afuw no ho. Amonom hɔ ara, ɔno nso tu fii hɔ. Oduu kurow no ano no, ɔbɛyɛɛ Duyker. Na ne fã no aporɔw, na ne fã no nnya mporɔw. Na ɔkɔɔ akyiri, na ɔbɛyɛɛ Oyuo (antelope). Na ne fã no aporɔw, na ne fã no nnya mporɔw. Na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔbɛyɛɛ Bongo. Akraman no ka kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ: "Aduankɔm bɛba, nanso ennya mmae (ɛdɔɔso), nanso mmoa yi rewuwu". Na ɔtwaa ho hyiae, na ohyiaa Bongo; na ɔtwaa ho hyiae, na ohyiaa Oyuo; na ɔtwaa ho hyiae, na ohyiaa Duyker. Na ɔkae sɛ: "Nokwarem, mmoa yi dɔɔso, enti mɛkɔ akɔboaboa wɔn ano". Agyatabea no bisaa no sɛ, Ɛhe na aburow no wɔ? Obuae sɛ, Mekɔɔ (ɔhɔ), nanso mannya bi ankɔtɔ. Afei, Agyatabea no kɔɔ asuten no ho, na (ɔhɔ na ohyiaa Aberewa no. Aberewa no kae sɛ, Nea ɛsɛ sɛ meka ara ne sɛ, ɛnnɛra me kunu kae sɛ, bere a ɔkɔe no, wannya aburow biara ankɔtɔ. Aberewa no buae sɛ, Ɔredaadaa wo, efisɛ, ɛnnɛra, bere a ɔkɔe kɔhwehwɛɛ (aburow no bi no, me kunu hwee no na ogyee no fii mu. Aberewa no fii fie. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne kunu sɛ, Moayɛ no yiye! Moayɛ no yiye! Moayɛ no yiye! wo kae sɛ wokɔɔ na woannya awi biara, bere a na ɛyɛ Hare no na ɔhwee wo na ogyee fii wo nsam. Akraman no kae sɛ, Hena ne Akraman no? Ɔkyena mɛkɔ ne no akɔko nnua. Akraman barima no tenaa fie na ɔtee (eyi). Ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere sɛ, Ɔkyena, Akraman barima no bɛba ha ne me akɔko, enti mɛkɔ akɔhyia no wɔ kwan so, efisɛ ebia wobɛsuro. Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma no daa adi no, Akraman no fii ase. Ɔduruu kuro no akyi no, na ɔdan Adwa anantweri, na na ɔretwa dɔte fitaa, nnua, ne mogya agu. Ɔkɔɔ anim, na ɔdan Bush-buck, na na ɔretwa ntasu saa ara. Ɔkɔɔ anim, na ɔdan Buffalo. Agyata no na ɛreba. Adwa anantweri no kae sɛ, Agya Leopard, boa me, na mereyɛ awu. Agyata no kae sɛ, Dɛn na asi? Adwa anantweri no kae sɛ, Me ne Hare no kɔdii awi ho akasakasa, na ɔhwee me, na mereyɛ awu. Agyata no kae sɛ, "Ma menkɔ". Ɔkɔɔ so, na ohyiaa Bush-buck no. Bush-buck no kae sɛ, "Leopard, boa me, efisɛ Hare no nyɛ obi a ɔne no bɛko wɔ awi ho". Leopard no kae sɛ, "Dɛn asɛm ne no?" Bush-buck no kae sɛ, "Mekɔɔ na ɔhwee me, ɛno nti na merewu". Leopard no kae sɛ, "Ma menkɔ so". Ɔkɔɔ so, na ohyiaa Buffalo no. Buffalo no kae sɛ, "Agya Osebo, boa me, efisɛ merewu". Leopard no kae sɛ, "Dɛn asɛm ne no?" Buffalo no kae sɛ, "Mekɔɔ na me ne Hare no gyee awi ho akyinnye, na ɔhwee me, na merewu". Mɛsan akɔ, na ɛyɛ awi ho asɛm a ɛte saa ara na ɛsɛ sɛ me ne no siesie, na fa no sɛ ɔboroa me"... Na Akraman no fii ase guanee, kiri! kiri! Amonom hɔ ara, Akraman no nso sɔree. Odii n'akyi, na Akraman no teɛɛm denneennen sɛ, "Ɔbea Akraman, guan kɔ!" Akraman no duu ne fie, na ɔyerae wɔ ne dan mu, na ogyee ne ti de bɔɔ ɔfasu no biako, na otwaa mu, nanso Akraman no kyeree ne nan abien no. Na Sakraman no kae sɛ, "Agya Aberewa, ma menkɔ". Sakraman no kae sɛ, "Meremma wo nkɔ, mɛhwe wo". Sakraman no kae sɛ, "Mesrɛ wo, mma me, na mmom ma me nsã nnom, na ka ntam wɔ anyame no so sɛ ɔkwan biara a wobɛfa so no, mɛba abɛtew ho". Ɛno nti, bere biara a wubefa kwan biara so, na wubehu sɛ Sakraman no apira hɔ no, (wonim) sɛ Sakraman no afa saa kwan no so.

Eyi, m'asɛm no, a maka ho asɛm no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
