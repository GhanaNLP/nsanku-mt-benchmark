SƐNEA OKOTO, KRAB no, Nyaa N'ahoma, NA ABEREWA, Ɔbaabun no, Nyaa N'abogyesɛ

Ɔbea bi nyinsɛnee, na bere a ɔmaa no kwan no, ɔkae sɛ: Merenkɔ. Na ɔkae sɛ: Wo ne w'akwantufo nkɔ. Na bere biara a ɔbɛma no kwan no, ɔkae sɛ: Merenkɔ. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: Kɔ na kɔsaw nsu na kɔfa brɛ me. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: Merenkɔ. Na ɔhwee no, na ɔkae sɛ: Twɛn, na hwɛ! Oduu akuraa bi ase no, ohuu akwakoraa bi sɛ ɔte hɔ, na bere a ohuu no no, na ɔpɛ sɛ ɔsan n'akyi. Ɔkaa sɛ: "Me maame ne me na yɛtena faako; da biara a ɔbɛsɔre no, ɔka sɛ: 'Twɛn na woahunu.' Na ɔhwee me, enti mekae sɛ: 'Mɛba abɛhwehwɛ na mahunu.' Na ɔbaa no kaa sɛ: 'Ɛyɛ me.' Na ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ: 'Kɔ efie no akyi na kɔtwaa yams na fa brɛ me. Sɛ wokɔ hɔ a, nea ɔbɛka no bɛka sɛ: 'Twa me, twa me.' na ɔbaa no buaa sɛ, "Hwɛ!" Na ɔbaa no kɔɔ hɔ; na nnwan no mu bi ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Hwɛ!" Na ebinom nso kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ!" Na ebinom nso kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ!" Na wɔn a wɔkaa sɛ, "Hwɛ!" no, wɔn ara na ɔhwee wɔn. Na ɔbaa no ne wɔn kɔeɛ, na ɔbaa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Hwɛ!" Na ɔbaa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Hwɛ!" Na ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ!" Na ɔbaa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Hwɛ!" Na ɔbaa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Hwɛ!" Na ɔbaa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Hwɛ!" Na ɔbaa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Hwɛ!" Na ɔbaa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Hwɛ!" Na ɔbaa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Hwɛ!" Na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ: Monkɔ kurow no akyi, na monkɔhwɛ adaka a nam wɔ so, na monhwɛ mu, na monhwehwɛ nea mopɛ. Na ɔfaa bi, na ɔbaa hɔ de guu gya no so, na ɔtoo brodo; na owiei no, ɔde maa ɔbaa no. Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ, "Wowiee?" Obuaa sɛ, "Yiw, Nana". Ɔkae sɛ, "Bra na kɔhwee fufu no". Na ɔbaa panyin no kɔɔ kɔdaa ne ho so; ɔkae sɛ, "Fa yams a wɔatoto no, hwie gu me hwene mu, na kɔhwee no". Abofra no kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nwonwa, sɛ yɛhwee fufu wɔ obi hwene mu; eyi ne nea na ne maame ka ho asɛm". Ɔkaa eyi nyinaa wɔ n'adwenem. Ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔhwee; nanso bere a ɔkɔhwee no, ɔhwee no brɛoo. Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ, "Hwee no den, sɛ wunhwee no den a, wo koko bɛpae. " Na ɔhwee no, pi! pi! pi! na bere a owiee no, ohwiee sop no guu so, na ɔde no sii hɔ maa ɔbaa panyin no. Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ: "Me babarima, bɔ me din na fa na didi". Na ɔkae sɛ: "Wɔfrɛ wo Ɔbaa panyin nana". Ɔkae sɛ: "Hwɛ! Onnya nhui, sɛ obetumi adi". Na ɔbaa panyin no dii ne nyinaa. Nnawɔtwe na ɔne no dii saa daa. Na da koro abofra no kɔɔ asuten no ho kɔhwee nsu. Nkran no huu no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na obi ba abrɛ sɛɛ?" Ɔbea no kae sɛ, "Me ne Nkranbea bi te, na bere a mereyɛ aduan na mede ma no no, ɔka sɛ, 'Ka me din na kɔdidi'; meka sɛ, 'Wɔfrɛ wo Nkranbea-Nkranbea,' na ɔka sɛ, 'Ee! wonhuu ɔkwan a wɔfa so didi,' na afei odi; ɛno nti na mayɛ mmerɛw". Nkran no kae sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ no Nkranbea-Mosono". Aborɔ no kae sɛ: "Mma wo werɛ mfi sɛ worekɔ". Abofra no fii hɔ; bere nyinaa a na ɔreba no, na ɔtaa ka sɛ, "Old-woman-Mosono, Mosono, Mosono". Bere a oduu kurow no mfinimfini no, ne werɛ fii, na ɔhwehwee ase na n'asuhina no bubui, na ɔsan kɔɔ fie. Ɔbea panyin no bisae sɛ: "Ɛhe na nsu kuruwa no wɔ?" Ɔbea no kae sɛ: "Bere a mereba no, mehintiw, na ebubui". Ɔbea no kae sɛ: "Fa biako na kɔ". Na ɔfaa no kɔe, na apataa no kae sɛ: "Woasan aba dedaw?" Ɔbea no kae sɛ: "Saa din a woka kyerɛɛ me no, makɔ na me werɛ afi, na maba abisa no bio". Apataa no kae sɛ: "Wɔfrɛ no Ɔbea Panyin Mosono". Na ɔbea no kae sɛ: "Ho". Ɔkɔɔ so kaa saa asɛm no wɔ kwan no nyinaa so, na bere a oduu fie no, ɔde [adudu] no too fam. Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ: "Kɔ na kɔtutu yams na kɔfa". Ɔyɛɛ biribiara te sɛ kan no, na ɔde aduan no brɛɛ no, na ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ: "Ka me din na di". Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Wɔfrɛ wo Ɔbaa panyin nana". Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Me kyerɛ me din ankasa". Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Wo din ankasa ne dɛn?" Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ: "Yiw". Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Wɔfrɛ wo Ɔbaa panyin Mosono". Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ: "Fa na didi". Abofra no gyee dii. Ɔbea panyin no fii kwan no so, na ɔkae sɛ: "Merekɔhwehwɛ onipa a ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ me din de Mosono no". Na ɔkɔɔ asuten no ho, na ohyiaa Obonto Ya, a ne din de minnow no, (na ɔkaa no anim ase, na ɔkae sɛ): "Wo 'Bonto, wo maame! wo 'Bonto, wo papa! woaka akyerɛ me mmanana ketewa no sɛ wɔfrɛ me Mosono?" Ɔkae sɛ: "Ɛnyɛ me". Ɔbea panyin no huu sɛ Adwen' (apataa no) gyina hɔ; ɔtan no, na ɔkae sɛ: "Wo Adwen, wo maame! wo maame! wo Adwen, wo papa! wo papa! wo papa! Woka kyerɛɛ me mmanana sɛ wɔfrɛ me Mosono?" Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ me". Ohuu sɛ Aborɔfoɔ no gyina hɔ. Ɔdomee no, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo Aborɔfoɔ, wo maame! Maame! Wo Aborɔfoɔ, w'agya! Agya! Agya! Wo na woka kyerɛɛ me mmanana sɛ mete Accra, mewɔ Ahennie mogya, na wɔfrɛ me Mosono?" Aborɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Yiw, me na meka kyerɛɛ no, na adɛn nti na woma obi ba nyini saa?" Ɛno nti na abɛn no wɔ mpompono wɔ n'akyi no. Bere tenteenten a atwam no, na abɛn no akyi no nni mpompono biara. Na abɛn no nso faa dɔte fitaa, na ɔtow guu ɔbaa no ti so. Ɛno nti na akwakoraa no ti so nhwi yɛ fitaa.

Eyi ne m'asɛm a mekae yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebia mubegye adi sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nkae no de, mubedwinnwen me ho.
