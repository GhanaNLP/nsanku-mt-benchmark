Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ASƐM A ƐDA SO Wɔbɛfrɛɛ Ɔsoro-nyame no Ho Atosɛm "Nkyinkyim Atosɛm"

KWAKU ANANSE, Nnomaa no, kɔɔ Nyankonpon, Ɔsoro-nyame no nkyɛn bere bi de kɔtɔɔ Ɔsoro-nyame no nsɛm. Ɔsoro nyame no bisae sɛ: "Wubetumi atɔ wɔn anaa?" Mpoano no kae sɛ: "Dabi, metumi". Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ: "Nkurow akɛse a emu yɛ den te sɛ Kokofu, Bekwai, Asumengya aba, nanso wontumi ntɔ wɔn, na wo a woyɛ onipa a wunni owura koraa no, (wo se) wobɛtumi?" Mpoano no bisae sɛ: "Dɛn na (asɛm no) bo yɛ?" Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ: "Wontumi mfa biribiara ntɔ wɔn. gye Onini abɔde a wɔfrɛ no Python; Osebo, Leopard; Mmoatia, Fairy; (ne) Mmoboro, a wɔfrɛ no Hornets no". Mpoano no kae sɛ, "Mede nneɛma yi nyinaa bɛba, na (nea ɛsen saa no), mɛma m'aberewa Nsia (Ɔba a Ɔtɔ so Nsia) aka ho". Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔfa wɔn". Mpoano no bae, na ɔkaa ne maame ho asɛm nyinaa kyerɛɛ no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ megye Ɔsoro-nyame no ne Sk no ho nsɛm. Y-nyame ka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mede Onini, Python no; Osebo, Leopard no; Mmoatia, a Fairy; ne Mmoboro, Hornets no; na mekae sɛ mɛma wo pii na makɔ ama Sky-nyame no. " Afei Spider no ne ne yere Aso dii nkitaho, kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ na ama yɛanya Onini, Python no?" Na Aso ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Wo kɔ na twa borɔdɔma dua ho baa bi, na twa nsensanee bi nso, na fa brɛ wɔn". Spider ne wɔn san bae. Na Aso kae sɛ, "Fa wɔn kɔ asuwa no ho". Na Ananse faa wɔn; bere a na ɔrekɔ no ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ tenten sen no, ɛnyɛ tenten te sɛ no; woadi atoro, ɛyɛ tenten sen no". na meka sɛ ɔyɛ ɔtorofo". Na Onini, Python no, kae sɛ, "Fa no, na bra na susuw me". Ɔfaa borɔdɔma dua no, na ɔde too Python no nipadua no ho. Ɔkae sɛ, "Trɛ wo ho mu". Na Python no tenee ne ho mu, na Ananse faa ahama no na ɔbobɔɔ no (na nea wɔkyekyeree no no nne yɛ nwenene! nwenene! nwenene! kosii sɛ oduu ne ti ho. Ananse a ɔyɛ nwansena no kae sɛ: "Ɔkwasea, mede wo bɛkɔ ɔsoro nyame no nkyɛn na manya ɔsoro nyame no nsɛm (sɛ́ anka wobegye no). " Ananse de no kɔɔ Nyame (ɔsoro nyame) nkyɛn. Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ: "Me nsa aka (i.e. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere sɛ: "Hwɛ kɔbere bi, na fa nsuo hyɛ mu, na fa kɔ". Na kɔbere no nam nnua no mu, na ɔhunuu kɔbere pii sɛ wɔsɛn hɔ, na ohwiee nsuo no bi guu kɔbere no so. Afei Nkyekye no hwiee nea aka no guu ne ho so, na otwaa plantain nhaban bi na ɔde kataa ne ti so. Na afei ɔka kyerɛɛ Hornet no sɛ, "Bere a osu no aba no, so ɛnyɛ papa sɛ wobɛba abɛhyɛn ha, me kurobo no, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a osu no remmɔ wo; wunhu sɛ magye banane ahaban bi de akata me ho?" Afei Hornet no kae sɛ, "Yɛda wo ase Aku, yɛda wo ase Aku". Hornet no nyinaa tu twaa wim, na wɔyerae wɔ kurobo no mu, fom! Agya Spider kataa n'ano, na ɔkae sɛ, "Nkwaseafo, me nsa aka mo, na mefa mo sɛ munnya Ɔsoro-nyame no ho nsɛm no (sɛ nkyɛmu). " Na ɔde Asorɔkye no kɔmaa Ɔsoro-nyame no. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka, nea aka no (da so ara) aka". Mpoano no san bae bio, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Osebo, Leopard no, aka". Aso kae sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔto ɔbodan". Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɛdɔɔso, mate ase". Afei Mpoano no kɔ kɔhwehwɛɛ Leopard no anammɔn, na (na wahu wɔn) ɔtwaa amoa a emu dɔ, na ɔkata so, na ɔsan baa fie. Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu, bere a nneɛma no fii ase hyerɛn no, Sekyi no kae sɛ ɔbɛpae, na bere a ɔkɔe no, na Akraman bi da amoa no mu. Ananse kae sɛ: "Agya ba kumaa, ɛna ba kumaa, maka akyerɛ wo sɛ nnom nsa, na afei, sɛnea obi betumi ahwɛ kwan wɔ wo ho no, woabobow, na ɛno nti na woahwe amoa no mu; sɛ meka sɛ mɛma wo afi amoa no mu a, ade kye a, sɛ wuhu me anaa me mma no bi a, wobɛkɔ akɔkyere wɔn". Ananse kɔ twaa nnua abien, na ɔde biako sii ha, na ɔde biako sii ha. Na Akraman no de wɔn sii hɔ. Na ɔrebɛforo no, Ananse maa ne peaw so, na prɛko pɛ no, ɛbaa ne ti so, gao! (ne nne no ne no). Na amoa no gyee Akraman no (na) fom! Ananse nyaa mpaboa bi de kɔɔ amoa no mu kɔyii Leopard no. Ɔyii Leopard no na ɔde no bae; ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔkwasea, mede wo rebɛsesa Ɔsoro-nyame no ho nsɛm". Ɔmaa Leopard no so kɔɔ Nyame, Ɔsoro-nyame no hɔ. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka, nea aka no da so ara". Mpofirim no bae, na otwaa Akua ba bi (abata a wɔde nnua ayɛ), na ɔfaa latex bi a ɛyɛ kɔkɔɔ (fi dua bi so) na ɔde bɔɔ mpopaho no nipadua mu, na ɔde totow eto (mashed yams), na ɔde bi hyɛɛ mpopaho no nsam, na ɔde totow bi kaa ho, na ɔde kɔkɔɔ nkuku mu; ɔde hama kyekyeree mpopaho no benkum ho, na ɔde kɔtoo odum dua bi ase, baabi a Fairies no kɔ di agorɔ. Na Fairies bi bae. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Akua, metumi adi eyi kakra? Ananse twea ahama no, na bonko no bobɔɔ ne ti. Fairies no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabeanom no biako sɛ, Ɔka sɛ metumi adi kakra. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Didi kakra. Na owiee didi, na ɔdaa no ase (bonko no). Nanso bere a ɔdaa no ase no, wammua no. Na Fae no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no sɛ, "Bere a meda no ase no, ɔmmua no". Na (kan) Fae no nuabea no kae sɛ, "Bɔ ne su-bea". Na ɔbɔɔ no, paapa! Na ne nsa traa hɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no sɛ, "Me nsa traa hɔ". Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Fa nea aka no na bɔ ne su-bea no bio". Na ɔfaa no de bɔɔ no, paapa! Na Ananse baa hɔ kyekyeree no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔkwasea, manya wo, mede wo bɛkɔ ɔsoro-nyame no nkyɛn (sɛ́ wɔde ne nsɛm no rebɛsesa". Na ɔne no kɔɔ fie. Na Ananse ka kyerɛɛ ne maame, Ya Nsia (ɔba a ɔtɔ so nsia no), sɛ, Sɔre, ma yɛnkɔ, ɛfiri sɛ merebɛfa wo ne Fairy no akɔ ama Ɔsoro-nyame no (sɛ yɛde bɛsesa n'asɛm no). Ɔmaa wɔn so, na ɔfirii hɔ kɔɔ baabi a Ɔsoro-nyame no wɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, Ɔsoro-nyame, hwɛ Fairy, na me nanabea a mekaa ne ho asɛm no nso wɔ ha. Afei Ɔsoro-nyame no frɛɛ (ne mpanyimfo), Kontire ne Akwam atitiriw, Adonten (asafo no mu panyin), Gyase (domo panyin); Oyoko, Ankobea, ne Kyidom (asogyafo no mu panyin). Na ɔde asɛm no too wɔn anim, kae sɛ, Ahemfo akɛse pii aba, na wontumi ntɔ ɔsoro anyame no abakɔsɛm, nanso Kwaku Ananse, Nkyekye no, atumi atua ne bo; minya fii ne hɔ Mmoboro, Nkyekye no; minya fii ne hɔ Mmoatia, Nkyekye no; minya fii ne hɔ Osebo, Ɔsebɔ no; minya fii ne hɔ Onini, Nkyekye no; na ne pɛ mu no, Ananse de bi aka ho Ɔkaa sɛ, To n'ayeyi. Eee! (wɔteateaam). Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Kwaku Ananse, efi nnɛ ne daa nyinaa, mefa me Soro-anyame no abakɔsɛm na mede ma mo, kose! kose! kose! me nhyira, nhyira, nhyira. Yɛrenfrɛ wɔn Soro-nyame no abakɔsɛm bio, na mmom yɛbɛfrɛ wɔn Nsa-nsa.

Eyi, m'asɛm no, a maka ho asɛm no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
