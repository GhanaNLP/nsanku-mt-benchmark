ASƐM A ƐDA SO Ɛyɛɛ Dɛn na Nyansa Baa Abusuakuw no Mu?

WƆKA sɛ Kwaku, Nkyekye no, na ɔwɔ hɔ, na ɔkyekyeree nimdeɛ nyinaa, boaboaa ano wɔ baabi biako, na ɔde koguu bokiti mu. Afei ɔkae sɛ ɔbɛforo dua bi so akɔsɛn so, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a nyansa a ɛwɔ asase so nyinaa bewie.

Enti ɔfaa no de no kɔe, na bere a oduu dua no ase baabi a na ɔbɛsɛn no no, ɔfaa hama de kyekyeree dua no ho, na ɔsɛn no n'anim, na osii ne ho sɛ ɔbɛforo dua no. Ɔforo kɔɔ soro, na ɔforo kɔɔ soro, na ɔforo kɔɔ soro, nanso anyɛ hwee. Ɔbɔɔ mmɔden bio, na ɔforo kɔɔ soro, na ɔforo kɔɔ soro, na ɔforo kɔɔ soro, nanso anyɛ hwee.

Na ne ba, Ntikuma a ɔgyina hɔ no, kae sɛ: Hwɛ, w'ani awu, na sɛ wotwa kɔbere no na wode gu w'akyi a, anka woatumi aforo no anaa? Na ɔkae sɛ: Monkɔ, mo ne mo nsɛm dedaw no. Na ɔsan kɔɔ anim bio sɛ ɔrekɔ, na ɔsan kɔɔ anim bio, na ɔhwɛɛ akyiri, na ɔfaa kɔbere no, na ɔde too n'akyi, na osii ne ho sɛ ɔrekɔ, na ɔkae sɛ: Kra! kra! kra! Ɔduruu baabi a mman no fii ase trɛwee no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ, "Me, Kwaku Ananse, wɔ onyame ketewa, Afio! anka mewu, me ba a ɔyɛ ketewa, ketewa, ketewa na mewɔ hɔ no, meboaboaa nyansa nyinaa ano (sɛnea misusuwii no) wɔ baabi koro, nanso nkae bi kaa ho a me mpo manhu, na hwɛ! me ba a ɔda so ara yɛ akokoaa yi, ada no adi akyerɛ me. " Afei ɔkyeree kuruwa no, na ɛnne bi teetee, tintini! na ɔtow kyenee, na ɛnne bi teetee, hwɛ! Saa kwan yi so na obiara nya nyansa; na obiara a wankɔ hɔ bere ano (anka ɔmmɛfa bi) no yɛ ɔkwasea.

Eyi, m'asɛm no, a maka ho asɛm no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
