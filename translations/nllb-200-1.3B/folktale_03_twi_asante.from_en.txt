ASƐM A ƐDA SO Ɛyɛɛ Dɛn na Nyansa Baa Abusuakuw no Mu?

WƆKA sɛ Kwaku, Nkyekye no, na ɔwɔ hɔ, na ɔkyekyeree nimdeɛ nyinaa, boaboaa ano wɔ baabi biako, na ɔde koguu bokiti mu, na afei ɔkaa sɛ ɔbɛforo dua bi so akɔsɛn so, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a nyansa a ɛwɔ asase so nyinaa bewie.

Enti ɔfaa no de no kɔe, na bere a oduu dua no ase baabi a na ɔbɛsɛn no no, ɔfaa hama, na ɔde kyekyeree dua no ho, na ɔde too n'anim, na osii ne ho sɛ ɔbɛforo dua no. Ɔforo kɔɔ soro, na ɔforo kɔɔ soro, na ɔforo kɔɔ soro, nanso anyɛ hwee. Ɔbɔɔ mmɔden bio, na ɔforo kɔɔ soro, na ɔforo kɔɔ soro, na ɔforo kɔɔ soro, nanso anyɛ hwee.

Na ne ba, Ntikuma a ɔgyina hɔ no, kae sɛ: Hwɛ, w'ani awu [fi aniwu mu]. Sɛ wotwa kɔbere no ho na wode gu w'akyi a, anka ɛrenyɛ papa anaa? Afei ɔtwɛnee kakra, na (awiei koraa no) ɔfaa bokiti no, na ɔde too n'akyi. Afei osii n'ani sɛ ɔbɛforo, na ɔde mmirika foro so, kra! kra! (ne nantew nne ne no). Ɛhɔ na ɔrekɔ. Oduu baabi a mman no fii ase trɛw fii dua no mu no, ɔkae sɛ (ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ), "Me, Kwaku Ananse, mehyɛ onyame ketewa, Afio! Anka mewu, me ba a ɔyɛ ketewa, ketewa, ketewa, na mewɔ hɔ no, meboaboaa nyansa nyinaa ano (sɛnea na misusuw no) wɔ baabi biako, nanso ebi kaa hɔ a me mpo manhu, na hwɛ! me ba, abofra a ɔda so nufu yi, ada no adi akyerɛ me. " Afei ɔfaa kuruwa no, na ɛnne bi teetee, tintini! na ɔtow kyenee, na ɛnne bi teetee, teese! Saa kwan yi so na obiara nya nyansa; na obiara a wankɔ hɔ bere ano (anka ɔmmɛfa bi) no yɛ ɔkwasea.

Eyi, m'asɛm no, a maka ho asɛm no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
