Amegbetɔ Ƒe Gomenɔamesiwo Ŋuti Nyatakaka

Ŋgɔdonya

Esi wònye be bubudede amegbetɔƒomea me tɔ ɖe sia ɖe ƒe bubu kple eƒe gomenɔamesi siwo sɔ eye womate ŋu aɖe asi le wo ŋu o la nye ablɔɖe, dzɔdzɔenyenye kple ŋutifafa ƒe gɔmeɖoanyi le xexeame ta la,

Esi wònye be bubumademade amegbetɔ ƒe gomenɔamesiwo ŋu kple ɖekematsɔleme ɖe wo ŋu he ŋutasẽnuwɔna siwo do dziku na ameƒomea ƒe dzitsinya vɛ, eye wogblɔ be xexe si me amegbetɔwo akpɔ gome le le nuƒoƒo kple dzixɔse ƒe ablɔɖe me, eye vɔvɔ̃ kple hiã manɔ anyi o la ƒe vavae nye didi gãtɔ kekeake na ame tsɛwo ta la,

Esi wònye be ele vevie be se ƒe dziɖuɖu nakpɔ amegbetɔ ƒe gomenɔamesiwo ta hafi amegbetɔ nagado go aglãdzedze ɖe ŋutasesẽ kple ameteteɖeanyi ŋu abe mɔnu mamlɛtɔ ene o ta la,

Esi wònye be ele vevie be woade dzi ƒo na dukɔwo dome xɔlɔ̃wɔwɔ ƒe kadodowo tutuɖo,

Esi Dukɔ Ƒoƒuawo ƒe Dukɔwo ɖo kpe woƒe xɔse le amegbetɔ ƒe gomenɔamesi veviwo, amegbetɔ ƒe bubu kple asixɔxɔ kpakple ŋutsu kple nyɔnu ƒe gomenɔamesi sɔsɔe me le Dukɔ Ƒoƒuawo ƒe Ðoɖowɔɖi me, eye woɖoe kplikpaa be yewoade ŋgɔyiyi le hadomegbenɔnɔ me kple agbenɔnɔ ƒe dzidzenu si nyo ɖe edzi le ablɔɖe geɖe wu me la dzi,

Esi wònye be Dukɔ Ƒoƒuawo ɖe adzɔgbe na wo ɖokui be yewoawɔ dɔ aduadu kple Dukɔ Ƒoƒuawo atsɔ ade bubu amegbetɔ ƒe gomenɔamesiwo kple ablɔɖe veviwo ŋu le xexeame katã ahalé wo me ɖe asi la,

Esi wònye be nugɔmesese ɖeka ɖe gomenɔamesi kple ablɔɖe siawo ŋu le vevie na ŋugbedodo sia me vava bliboe ta la,

Eya ta azɔ la,

Takpekpe Gã la

Ðe gbeƒã Amegbetɔ ƒe Gomenɔamesiwo Ŋuti Nyatakaka sia be enye nusi dukɔwo katã kple dukɔwo katã ate ŋu awɔ, ale be amesiame kple hadomegbenɔnɔ ƒe dɔwɔha ɖe sia ɖe nanɔ ŋku ɖom Nyatakaka sia dzi ɣesiaɣi, eye wòadze agbagba to nufiafia kple hehenana me be yeade bubu gomenɔamesi kple ablɔɖe siawo ŋu, eye to afɔɖeɖe siwo le edzi yim le dukɔa me kple dukɔwo dome dzi la, yeakpɔ egbɔ be dukɔ siwo le Dukɔ Ƒoƒuawo ŋutɔ me kple dukɔ siwo le nuto siwo te ɖe wo ŋu la me tɔwo siaa axɔ wo dzi ase ahalé wo me ɖe asi ŋutɔŋutɔ.

Nyatakakawo

Wodzi amegbetɔwo katã ablɔɖeviwoe eye bubu kple gomenɔamesi le wo si sɔsɔe. Susu kple dzitsinya le wo si, eye ele be woawɔ nu ɖe wo nɔewo ŋu le nɔvilɔlɔ̃ ƒe gbɔgbɔ me.

Nyatakakawo

Amesiame kpɔ mɔ akpɔ gome le gomenɔamesi kple ablɔɖe siwo katã ŋu woƒo nu tsoe le Nyatakaka sia me la me, vovototo aɖeke manɔ eme o, eɖanye le ŋutigbalẽ, ŋutigbalẽ ƒe amadede, gbɔdɔdɔ, gbegbɔgblɔ, subɔsubɔha, dunyahehe alo nukpɔsusu bubuwo, dukɔmevi alo hadomegbenɔnɔ ƒe dzɔtsoƒe, nunɔamesi, dzidzi alo nɔnɔme bubu aɖeke me o.

Gakpe ɖe eŋu la, womawɔ vovototo aɖeke le dukɔ alo nuto si me ame aɖe le la ƒe dunyahehe, ʋɔnudrɔ̃nya alo dukɔwo dome ɖoƒe, eɖanye ɖokuisinɔnɔ, dziɖulanyenye, dziɖulanyenye si mele eɖokui si o alo dziɖulanyenye ƒe seɖoƒe bubu aɖeke te o, dzi o.

Nyatakakawo

Agbe, ablɔɖe kple dedienɔnɔ ŋuti gomenɔamesi le amesiame si.

Nyatakakawo

Womawɔ ame aɖeke kluvi alo kluvi o; woaɖe asi le kluvinyenye kple kluvisitsatsa ŋu le eƒe nɔnɔme ɖesiaɖe me.

Nyatakakawo

Womate ŋu awɔ funyafunya ame aɖeke alo awɔ nu madzɔmadzɔ ɖe eŋu alo ahe to nɛ o.

Nyatakakawo

Gomenɔamesi le amesiame si be woade bubu eŋu le afisiafi be enye ame le se la ŋkume.

Nyatakakawo

Amesiame sɔna le se la ŋkume, eye gome le esi be woakpɔ ta nɛ le se la nu sɔsɔe, vovototo aɖeke manɔ eme o. Amesiame kpɔ mɔ akpɔ ta nɛ le vovototo ɖesiaɖe si tsi tre ɖe Nyatakaka sia ŋu kple vovototo ɖesiaɖe si ʋãa amewo be woawɔ vovototo ma ƒomevi la ta.

Nyatakakawo

Gomenɔamesi le amesiame si be woahe to na eya ŋutɔ le ʋɔnudrɔ̃ƒe siwo le dukɔa me le nuwɔna siwo gblẽa gomenɔamesi vevi siwo ŋu seɖoɖoa alo se ɖo la me.

Nyatakakawo

Womate ŋu alé ame aɖeke le mɔ si sɔ nu o nu, alé ame aɖe le gaxɔ me alo aɖe ame aɖe le dukɔa me o.

Nyatakakawo

Gomenɔamesi le amesiame si be woadrɔ̃ ʋɔnui le dutoƒo le ɖekawɔwɔ blibo me le ʋɔnudrɔ̃ƒe si le eɖokui si eye medea akpa aɖeke dzi o ŋkume le eƒe gomenɔamesiwo kple agbanɔamedziwo kpakple nutsotso ɖe sia ɖe si wotsɔ ɖe eŋu me.

Nyatakakawo

Woate ŋu abu fɔ ame sia ame si wotsɔ nya ɖe eŋu be mewɔ nu gbegblẽ o la be meɖi fɔ o va se ɖe esime wotso afia nɛ be ewɔ nu vɔ̃ le ʋɔnudrɔ̃ɖoɖo aɖe si me wokpɔ mɔ akpɔ eɖokui ta la me.

Womate ŋu abu ame aɖeke fɔ ɖe nu vlo wɔwɔ aɖe ta le nuwɔna alo nu madzɔmadzɔ wɔwɔ aɖe si menye nu vlo wɔwɔ le dukɔa alo dukɔwo dome se nu le ɣeyiɣi si me wowɔe o ta o, eye womate ŋu ahe tohehe sesẽ wu esi nɔ anyi le ɣeyiɣi si me wowɔ nu vlo wɔwɔa o la hã ɖe eŋu o.

Nyatakakawo

Amesiame mekpɔa mɔ be woagblẽ eya ŋutɔ ƒe dedienɔnɔ, eƒe ƒomea, eƒe aƒe alo eƒe lɛtaa me o, eye womagblẽ eƒe bubu kple bubu hã ŋu o. Amesiame kpɔ mɔ be se nakpɔ eta tso nugbegblẽ kple nugbegblẽ sia ƒomevi me.

Nyatakakawo

Gomenɔamesi le amesiame si be wòazɔ kple nɔƒe le eƒe dukɔ me faa.

Amesiame kpɔ mɔ adzo le dukɔ sia dukɔ me, eɖanye eya ŋutɔ tɔ o, eye wòagatrɔ ayi eƒe dukɔ me.

Nyatakakawo

Gomenɔamesi le amesiame si be wòadi sitsoƒe le yometiti ta le dukɔ bubuwo me eye wòakpɔ gome le eme.

Womate ŋu aɖe mɔ ɖe gomenɔamesi sia ŋu le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ siwo ku ɖe nu vlo wɔwɔ siwo menye dunyahehe o alo nuwɔna siwo tsi tre ɖe Dukɔ Ƒoƒuawo ƒe taɖodzinuwo kple gɔmeɖosewo ŋu la me o.

Nyatakakawo

Amesiame kpɔ mɔ anye dukɔmevi.

Womate ŋu aɖe eƒe dukɔmevinyenye le ame aɖeke si le eya ŋutɔ ƒe didi nu o, eye womate ŋu axe mɔ nɛ be wòatrɔ eƒe dukɔmevinyenye o.

Nyatakakawo

Ŋutsu kple nyɔnu siwo xɔ ƒe geɖe, siwo womede se le ŋutigbalẽ, dukɔmevinyenye alo mawusubɔsubɔ ta o, kpɔ mɔ awɔ srɔ̃ɖeɖe ahaɖo ƒome anyi. Gomenɔamesi ɖeka tɔgbi le wo si le srɔ̃ɖeɖe me, le srɔ̃ɖeɖe me kple srɔ̃ɖeɖe me.

Srɔ̃tɔwo ƒe lɔlɔ̃nu faa kple woƒe lɔlɔ̃nu faa ko hafi woate ŋu aɖe wo nɔewo.

Ƒomea nye hadome ƒe ƒuƒoƒo si le dzɔdzɔme nu, si nye nu vevitɔ, eye gome le eŋu be hadome kple Dukɔa nakpɔ eta.

Nyatakakawo

Amesiame kpɔ mɔ anye nunɔamesi na eɖokui kple ame bubuwo siaa.

Womate ŋu aɖe eƒe nunɔamesiwo le ame aɖeke si le mɔ aɖe nu o.

Nyatakakawo

Gomenɔamesi le amesiame si be wòanɔ tamebubu, dzitsinya kple mawusubɔsubɔ ƒe ablɔɖe me; ablɔɖe sia lɔ ablɔɖe ɖe eme be wòate ŋu atrɔ eƒe mawusubɔsubɔ alo dzixɔse, kple ablɔɖe, le eɖokui si alo le ha me kple ame bubuwo, le dutoƒo alo le ame ɖokui si, be wòaɖe eƒe mawusubɔsubɔ alo dzixɔse afia to nufiafia, tadedeagu, tadedeagu kple nuwɔna me.

Nyatakakawo

Gomenɔamesi le amesiame si be wòade bubu nukpɔsusu kple nuƒoƒo faa ƒe ablɔɖe ŋu; ablɔɖe sia lɔ ablɔɖe ɖe eme be wòate ŋu agblɔ eƒe nukpɔsusuwo le mɔ aɖeke nu o, eye be wòate ŋu adi nyatakaka kple nukpɔsusuwo, axɔ wo, ahana wo to mɔ ɖesiaɖe dzi, eye eɖanye dukɔ ka ke me tɔe o.

Nyatakakawo

Amesiame kpɔ mɔ aƒo ƒu ahanɔ ha dem le ŋutifafa me.

Womate ŋu azi ame aɖeke dzi be wòanɔ habɔbɔ aɖe me o.

Nyatakakawo

Amesiame kpɔ mɔ akpɔ gome le eƒe dukɔ ƒe dziɖuɖu me tẽe loo alo to ame siwo wotia le ablɔɖe me la dzi.

Amesiame kpɔ mɔ awɔ dɔ le dziɖuɖudɔwɔƒewo le eya ŋutɔ ƒe dukɔa me le mɔ ɖeka nu.

Dukɔa ƒe lɔlɔ̃nue nye dziɖuɖu ƒe ŋusẽ ƒe gɔmeɖoanyi; woɖea lɔlɔ̃nu sia fiana le ɣeyiɣiwo me le tiatia vavãtɔ siwo wowɔna to xexeame katã ƒe tiatiawɔblɔɖe si le ɖekawɔwɔ me dzi, eye wobua wo le adzame alo to tiatiawɔblɔɖe si sɔ kple ema me.

Nyatakakawo

Amesiame, abe hadome me tɔ ene, kpɔ mɔ na hadomegbenɔnɔ ƒe dedienɔnɔ, eye wòakpɔ gome le ganyawo, hadomegbenɔnɔ kple dekɔnuwo me gomenɔamesi siwo le vevie na eƒe bubu kple eƒe amenyenye ƒe tsitsi faa la gbɔ kpɔkpɔ to dukɔmeviwo ƒe agbagbadzedze kple dukɔwo dome hadede me, le ɖekawɔwɔ me kple dukɔ ɖe sia ɖe ƒe ɖoɖowo kple nunɔamesiwo.

Nyatakakawo

Amesiame kpɔ mɔ awɔ dɔ, akpɔ gome le dɔwɔɖui tiatia me faa, akpɔ gome le dɔwɔɖui ƒe nɔnɔme siwo sɔ eye wonyo ŋu, eye akpɔ mɔ ɖe dɔmakpɔwɔmawɔ nu.

Amesiame, si me vovototo aɖeke mele o la, kpɔ mɔ axɔ fetu sɔsɔe ɖe dɔ sɔsɔe ta.

Amesiame si wɔa dɔ la kpɔ mɔ axɔ fetu si sɔ eye wòsɔ, si akpɔ egbɔ be eya ŋutɔ kple eƒe ƒomea anɔ agbe si ade bubu amegbetɔ ŋu, eye ne ehiã la, woakpɔ egbɔ be wotsɔ ganyawo ƒe dedienɔnɔ ƒe mɔnu bubuwo kpe ɖe eŋu.

Gomenɔamesi le amesiame si be wòaɖo dɔwɔhatiwo ahawɔ ɖeka kpli wo hena eƒe nyonyo ta kpɔkpɔ.

Nyatakakawo

Amesiame kpɔ mɔ na ɖiɖiɖeme kple modzakaɖeɖe, si me dɔwɔŋkekewo ƒe seɖoƒe si sɔ kple gbɔɖemeɖoɖo siwo me fetu le hã le.

Nyatakakawo

Amesiame kpɔ mɔ akpɔ agbenyuinɔnɔ ƒe dzidzenu si sɔ na eya ŋutɔ kple eƒe ƒomea ƒe lãmesẽ kple dedienɔnɔ, si me nuɖuɖu, nudodo, aƒe kple atikewɔnyawo kpakple hadomegbenɔnɔ ŋuti dɔwɔna siwo hiã la le, kple gadzraɖoƒe le dɔmawɔmawɔ, dɔléle, gbɔdzɔgbɔdzɔ, ahosi, amegãkuku alo agbemenuhiahiã bubuwo ƒe anyimanɔmanɔ le nɔnɔme siwo me mate ŋu akpɔ ŋusẽ ɖe edzi o me.

Amedzidɔ kple ɖevimenɔɣi le se nu be woakpɔ wo dzi eye woakpe ɖe wo ŋu le mɔ tɔxɛ nu. Ðevi siwo katã wodzi le srɔ̃ɖeɖe me loo alo womedzi le srɔ̃ɖeɖe me o la kpɔa gome le hadomegakpɔkpɔ ma ke me.

Nyatakakawo

Amesiame kpɔ mɔ asrɔ̃ nu. Sukudede anye femaxee, le gɔmedzedze kple gɔmeɖoanyi ƒe ɖoƒewo ya teti me. Sukudede gɔmeɖoanyi nye nudidi. Woana mɔ̃ɖaŋununya kple dɔwɔŋutete ƒe hehe amesiame, eye amesiame ate ŋu asrɔ̃ nu le suku kɔkɔwo me le ɖekawɔwɔ me le woƒe ŋutetewo ta.

Taɖodzinu si le sukudede ŋu enye be wòana amegbetɔ ƒe amenyenye nade blibo eye wòado ŋusẽ bubudede amegbetɔ ƒe gomenɔamesiwo kple ablɔɖe veviwo ŋu. Taɖodzinu siae nye be wòade dzi ƒo na dukɔwo, ŋutigbalẽ kple subɔsubɔhawo katã ƒe nugɔmesese, dzidodo kple xɔlɔ̃wɔwɔ, eye wòado Dukɔ Ƒoƒuawo ƒe dɔwɔnawo ɖe ŋgɔ le ŋutifafa me léle ɖe asi me.

Dzilawo kpɔ mɔ atia hehe si woana wo viwo.

Nyatakakawo

Gomenɔamesi le amesiame si be wòakpɔ gome le nutoa me ƒe dekɔnuwɔnawo me faa, wòakpɔ gome le aɖaŋunuwo wɔwɔ me, eye wòakpɔ gome le dzɔdzɔmeŋutinunya ƒe ŋgɔyiyi kple viɖe siwo dona tso eme la me.

Amesiame kpɔ mɔ akpɔ eƒe agbenɔnɔ kple nunɔamesiwo ta le dzɔdzɔmeŋutinunya, agbalẽŋɔŋlɔ alo aɖaŋudɔwo me dɔ ɖesiaɖe si wòwɔ me.

Nyatakakawo

Amesiame kpɔ mɔ akpɔ hadomegbenɔnɔ kple dukɔwo dome ɖoɖo si me woate ŋu awɔ gomenɔamesi kple ablɔɖe siwo le Nyatakaka sia me la ŋu dɔ bliboe le.

Nyatakakawo

Agba le amesiame dzi le hadome si me ko eƒe amenyenye ƒe tsitsi bliboe kple ablɔɖe ate ŋu asu esi.

Ne ame aɖe le eƒe gomenɔamesi kple ablɔɖewo ŋu dɔ wɔm la, mɔxeɖenu siwo ŋu se ɖo la koe ate ŋu akpɔ ŋusẽ ɖe edzi, si nye be woakpɔ ame bubuwo ƒe gomenɔamesi kple ablɔɖewo dzi nyuie ahade bubu wo ŋu, eye be woanɔ agbe ɖe agbenyuinɔnɔ, hadomegbenɔnɔ kple hadomegbenɔnɔ ƒe nudidi dzɔdzɔewo nu le dukɔmevinyenye me.

Womate ŋu azã gomenɔamesi kple ablɔɖe siawo le mɔ aɖeke nu le mɔ si tsi tre ɖe Dukɔ Ƒoƒuawo ƒe taɖodzinuwo kple gɔmeɖosewo ŋu nu o.

Nyatakakawo

Womate ŋu aɖe Nubabla sia me nyawo gɔme be afia be gome le Dukɔ, ƒuƒoƒo alo ame aɖe si be wòawɔ dɔ alo awɔ nu sia nu si ana woatsrɔ̃ gomenɔamesi kple ablɔɖe siwo le eme la dometɔ aɖeke o.
