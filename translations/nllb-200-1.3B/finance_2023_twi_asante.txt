The Answer of the Disciple

This year will undoubtedly be one of the worst economic downturns in the country's history. As in other parts of the world, we were hit by the COVID-19 pandemic in the first half of 2020, which directly impacted the economy and directly impacted the country's gross domestic product, as well as incurring unexpected debts to combat the pandemic.

At the start of the Transformative Partnership, we thought we were trying to recover from the effects of the pandemic, but there were other problems, which led to the collapse of all the economic fundamentals from 2017 to 2019. The first was the war between Russia and Ukraine, and the second was the failure to meet the criteria for financing the 2022 fiscal year, which led the international community to declare that we were not eligible to borrow. Then, about ten years ago, all of a sudden, the Universal Creditors refused to lend us any money, and the country's borrowing situation became so bad that interest rates soared and, as a result, the borrowing burden soared, causing our money to go down the drain.

This means that while foreign exchange earnings from the global credit market and exports of goods are declining, and because of our high demand for foreign goods, exports of raw materials are also increasing, while foreign debt is increasing, and the weight of our money is also increasing. This has made life difficult for both the Government and the private sector. As a result of rising currency prices, commodity prices have risen to 40 percent in October 2022 and 40 percent in October 2022.
