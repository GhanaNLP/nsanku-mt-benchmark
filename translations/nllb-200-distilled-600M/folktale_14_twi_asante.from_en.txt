ASƐM A ƐDA SO EFUO, Ahempa a Ɔyɛ Black Colobus, N'afiri bɛyɛɛ fitaa

WƆKA sɛ na Esono, Elephant, Kwakuo, Monkey, Anyinhima, the Putty-Nose Monkey, ne Efuo, the Black-Colobus Monkey te hɔ, na wɔkae sɛ wɔbɛkɔ akuraa bi ase akɔhwehwɛ mmea aware, na wofii hɔ kɔe; na bere a wɔrekɔ no, wohuu ɔtare bi a ɛbɔ dɔte. Osee, "Yɛn akwantu yi mu hena na ne ho yɛ fɛ?" Osee, "Ɛyɛ Efuo a ɔyɛ Black-Colobus Monkey no". Kwakuo kae sɛ, "Sɛ saa na ɛte a, momma yɛmpira no nkɔbɔ no asorɔkye mu". Afei Colobus Monkey sɔre dii wɔn akyi nkakrankakra, na ɔkɔɔ akuraa no ase kɔtenaa hɔ. Na nnipa a aka no duu akuraa no ase no, wohuu mmabaawa bi a wɔahyiam wɔ gyedua dua ase. Na ababaa bi fii afuw no mu bae, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne na sɛ: Sɛ mmerante no ba a, paw biako na fa to hɔ ma me. Na ɔsan fii afuw no mu bae no, obisaa ne na sɛ: Me de wɔ he? ." Na ɔtwee nnuaba bi, na na ɔrebɛtow nnuaba no agu mfinimfini no so.

Afei, Colobus Monkey no baa hɔ na ɔtenaa ɔbotan no mfinimfini. Abeawa no rebɛtow nsakyiamoa no agu so, bere a Colobus Monkey no kae sɛ, Montow no me so. Abeawa no kae sɛ, Ɛyɛ hena? Ɔkaa sɛ, Ɛyɛ me. Abeawa no kae sɛ, Ɛdeɛn na asi? Ɔkaa sɛ, Me ne m'adamfo a me ne wɔn bɔ agoru no rebɛware (mo) mmabaa, bere a wɔboroo me, na wɔmaa me nipadua nyinaa ho yeraw, na ɛno nti na mebaa ha sɛ merebɛtena. Ɔkaa sɛ, Me fam de, medɔ wo, enti bra na yɛnkɔ fie. Ɔbea no ne no kɔe, na ɔfaa nsu kɔhohoroo no. Bere a ɔfaa nsonsonoe bi de kaa ne honam ho kakra no, ohui sɛ ne honam no hyerɛn fɛfɛɛfɛ paa. Abeawa no kae sɛ, "Ɔbea, bra bɛhwɛ, efisɛ saa ahoɔfɛ yi nyɛ ade a ɛsɛ sɛ wode di agorɔ". Na ɛna no kae sɛ, "Gyae hohoroo no". Na ɔwiee hohoroo no, na ɔtoo no ngo, na ɔfaa ne mpaboa, na ɔfaa ne sankuo (nwom bow), na ɔkɔtenaa Gyedua dua ase, na ɔtoo nnwom. Afei mmabaa a na wɔaware dedaw no, bere a wohuu no no, biako no kae sɛ, Monnɔ me kunu bio, biako no kae sɛ, Monnɔ me kunu bio, ɛyɛ Black-Colobus Monkey a medɔ no. Black-Colobus Monkey no kae sɛ, Ná mewɔ no, medɔ no. Afei mmoa no nyinaa twaa wɔn ho hyiae kae sɛ wɔrebɛfa wɔn nneɛma asan aba. Na bere a mmoa no kɔe no, wɔkɔɔ na wɔtenaa ɔkwan no so, na wɔtwɛne sɛ Black-Colobus Monkey no bɛboro no akum no bere a ɔbaa hɔ no. Black-Colobus Monkey no nso twaa ho hyiae kae sɛ, Me nso, mɛfa me nneɛma asan aba. Na ofii hɔ. Na bere a ɔbaa ɔkwan no so no, mmoa no huu no, na wɔkae sɛ: "Hwɛ, Abɔnsam Apɔnkɔ no wɔ hɔ, na Abɔnsam Apɔnkɔ no nso wɔ hɔ". Na Abɔnsam Apɔnkɔ no soo dua bi mu, na kra! kra! kra! kra! Bere tenteenten a atwam no, na Black-Colobus Monkey no dua ne ne nantin yɛ tuntum.

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
