Ɛhɔ na akekantwɛre no ka kyerɛɛ akekantwɛre no sɛ: "Momma yɛnkɔhwehwɛ mmea mma wɔnware". Akekantwɛre no kae sɛ: "Hwɛ!" Na akekantwɛre no hohoroo ne ho, na ɔde ne sikakɔkɔɔ ahosiesie hyɛɛ ne ho fɛfɛɛfɛ, na ɔkyekyeree ne mpaa dedaw no de maa akekantwɛre no sɛ ɔnsoa ne ti. Na wosiim kɔe, na woduu akuraa bi ase. Na ɔbaa no kae sɛ: "Mo mmammarima ne mo agyanom, mede anwummere nkyia ma mo". Wɔkae sɛ: "Yɛn kunu". 'Wansana, Nnomaa no kae sɛ, "Agyanom ne ɛnanom nyinaa, mede anwummere nkyea makyea mo". Wɔbuaa no saa ara.

Ɔkaa sɛ: "Wo a wowɔ ano bɔne yi, wopɛ sɛ wosan kyia yɛn anwummere?" Na wosiim kɔe; na wɔkɔɔ kwan no so, na akraman no kae sɛ: "Hwɛ! Fa sika kɔbere yi brɛ me, na fa sika kɔbere yi, na kɔhyɛ wo ho fɛw, sɛ ɛyɛ kɔbere dedaw no nti na mmabaa no dɔ wo a, mɛhwɛ". Na (Akraman no) gyee (ahyɛde no) na ɔhyehyɛɛ ne ho fɛw, na wofii hɔ koduu akuraa a edi hɔ no ase. Ná Leopard no wɔ n'anim, na na ɔhyɛ atade dedaw bi wɔ ne ti so; na ɔkyiaa (kuraafo no). Wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Nkyia yɛn, wo a w'anom yɛ fĩ, na ɛyɛ wo sɛ wode mpaa dedaw yi". Ɛnna ɔbaa hɔ no, ɔkae sɛ: "Mo mmammarima ne mo agyanom nyinaa, mede anwummere nkyiakyia makyiakyia mo". Bere a woduu kwan no so ara no, Leopard kae sɛ, "Hwɛ! adamfo, gyina hɔ pintinn, yi me sikakɔkɔɔ ntade no fi hɔ na ma me ntɛm, ntɛm, ntɛm. Ɔkaa sɛ, Mɛkyekyere wo, na dɛn na wowɔ ka? Na Nnomaa no kae sɛ, Menni hwee ka. Leopard no toaa so sɛ, Me ne wo nnya nnuu baabi a yɛrekɔ mpo . . . (na ɛwom sɛ mmea no dɔ wo dedaw). Afei Leopard no kyeree no kyekyeree no dua bi ho. Kenki fã no kae sɛ: "Metee, mɛsan ama wo". Ɔkɔɔ hɔ, na ɔsan maa no kwan. Bere a owiee saa no, mpofirim ara Leopard no bae. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Adɛn nti na woayi me nipa no?" Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Mayi no, na monyɛ nea mopɛ". Ɔkaa sɛ: "Me ne mo rentumi nyɛ hwee, gye me ne mo nko ara na yɛde yɛn nsa bɛko". na ɔsan bae". Na ɔkae sɛ: "Momma yɛnhyew no". Na akekantwɛre no hyewee no. Na bere koro no ara wɔde wɔn ho bɔɔ akono, na wɔteɛɛm sɛ: Yiri! Yiri! Yiri! Na akekantwɛre no bubuu akekantwɛre no, na ɔtow no kyenee, bam! Kenki fã kaa sɛ, "Nea ɛte saa nyɛ hwee), obi gye biribi a ɛte saa na ɔde kɔhohoro baabi a ne yere kɔhohoro". Na wohyiaa wɔn ho bio. Na Kenki fã nso ayi Leopard afi ne nsam, bam! Na Leopard no kae sɛ, "Me nso mefa biribi a ɛte saa kɔhohoro me yere sanho, (na) wɔ yɛn nhyiam a edi hɔ no mu no, sɛ obi tow ne yɔnko fi ne ho a, ɛno ne ade a etwa to no". Na wɔde wɔn ho bɛkaa ho (ko no, na ɛte sɛ) Kikiri! Kikiri! Kikiri! Na Half-a-ball-of-Kenki maa Leopard no so na ɔtow no too ogya no mu. Leopard kae sɛ, "Me firim, me firim, me firim_" (mefi hɔ, mefi hɔ, mefi hɔ. Ɛno na ɛmaa Leopard teɛɛm sɛ "Me firim_"). (Afei) wohuu sɛ ne nipadua te sɛ eyi, kurukyire, kurukyire, kurukyire (a ɛkyerɛ sɛ, nyinaa wɔ hɔ); baabi a fitaa no wɔ hɔ no, ɛhɔ na mfutuma no kaa no; na baabi a tuntum no wɔ hɔ no, ɛhɔ nso na kotoku tuntum no kaa no. Na sɛ worekɔ hɔ a, wubehu sɛ afifide no da hɔ a wɔakyekyere ne ho no, wubehu sɛ mpakye pii redɔɔso wɔ so, na ɛnyɛ biribi foforo biara ntia, na wɔda no ase.

Eyi ne m'asɛm a maka akyerɛ mo, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, mobɛfa bi sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no de, mobɛkamfo me.
