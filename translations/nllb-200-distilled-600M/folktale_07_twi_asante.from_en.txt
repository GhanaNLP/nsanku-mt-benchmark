ASƐM A ƐDA SO Sɛnea Wohuu Leopard no Afunu

Ɛhɔ na akekantwɛre no ka kyerɛɛ akekantwɛre no sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔhwehwɛ mmea mma wɔnware". Akekantwɛre no kae sɛ, "Hwɛ!" Na akekantwɛre no hohoroo ne nsa na ɔde ne sikakɔkɔɔ ahosiesie hyɛɛ ne ho fɛfɛɛfɛ, na ɔkyekyeree ne mpaa dedaw no de maa akekantwɛre no sɛ ɔnsoa ne ti. Na wɔkɔe, na woduu akuraa bi ase. Na ɔbaa no kae sɛ: "Mo mmaa ne mo agyanom nyinaa, mede anwummere asie mo". Wɔkae sɛ: "Yɛn kunu". Na ɔbaa no nso duu hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ: "Me mmaa ne mmaa ne me agyanom, mede anwummere asie mo". Wɔkae sɛ: "Wo a wode w'ano a ɛho ntew di yɛn anim sɛ wode anwummere asie yɛn?" Na wosiim kɔe, na woduu akuraa a edi hɔ no ase. Wansana, ɔbaa no kae sɛ: "Me mmaa ne me agyanom, mede anwummere asie mo".

Ɔkaa sɛ: "Wo a wowɔ ano bɔne, wopɛ sɛ wosan kyia yɛn anwummere anaa?" Na wɔkɔe, na wɔkɔɔ kwan no so, na akraman no kae sɛ: "Hwɛ! Fa sika kɔbere no brɛ me, na wofa sika kɔbere yi, na wohyɛ wo ho fɛw, sɛ ɛyɛ kɔbere dedaw no nti na mmabaa no dɔ wo a, mɛhwɛ". Na (Akraman no) gyee (akraman no) na ɔhyehyɛɛ ne ho fɛw, na wofii hɔ koduu akuraa a edi hɔ no ase. Na akraman no wɔ n'anim, na kɔbere dedaw no wɔ ne ti so; na ɔkyiaa (akraman no). Wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Nkyia yɛn, wo a w'anom yɛ fĩ, na ɛyɛ wo sɛ wode mpaa yi". Na ɔbaa hɔ no, ɔkae sɛ: "Mo mmaa ne mo agyanom, mede anwummerɛ kyea mo". Na wɔkae sɛ: "Me dɔfo! Hwɛ, ababaa bi wɔ ha a ɛsɛ sɛ wohwɛ no, sika kɔkɔɔ ntade, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wobɛyɛ wo; sɛ wɔankum yɛn a, anka yɛne wo bɛkɔ kwan so". Na wɔkɔe, na bere a woduu kwan no so no, akekantwɛre no kae sɛ: "Hwɛ! Adamfo, gyina hɔ ara, yi me sikakɔkɔɔ ntade no fi hɔ na ma me ntɛm, ntɛm, ntɛm. Bere a Apɔnkɔ no wiee sɛ ɔyi ntade no fi hɔ na ɔde maa no no, Leopard no hwɛɛ kwan no so na ɔtwee ntade bi a ɔde bɛkyekyere Apɔnkɔ no. Ɔkae sɛ, Mɛkyekyere wo, na dɛn na wowɔ ka? Na Apɔnkɔ no kae sɛ, minni hwee a mɛka. Leopard no toaa so sɛ, Me ne wo nnya nnui yɛn baabi a yɛrekɔ mpo . . . (na ɛwom sɛ mmea no dɔ wo dedaw). Afei Leopard no kyeree no kyekyeree no dua bi ho. Na ɔno nso kɔɔ baabi foforo kɔhintawee. Ankyɛ koraa na Nantwi no twaa kwan so kɔɔ gua so, na ohuu no no, ɔkae sɛ:

Hena na ɔda hɔ a ɔyɛ tuntum saa?

Hena na ɔda hɔ a ne ho yɛ hyerɛn saa?

(The Fly) kae sɛ:

Ɛyɛ me, Twi, Kwao Boakye.

Adenen!

Gyahene Kofi Amo, Adenen!

Ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ memmra na yɛnkɔhwehwɛ mmabaa.

Adenen!

Mmabaa no ka sɛ wɔnnɔ no.

Adenen!

Ɔkyekyeree me.

Adenen!

Ɔkyekyeree me koraa.

Adenen!

Gyefo, gye me nkwa.

Adenen!

Bere a woagye me nkwa no, fa ɔkwan no na kɔ.

Adenen! 6Nanso abusuani no ka kyerɛɛ no se, "Ɔkwatani no asesa wo, enti merentumi nyi wo mfi mu". Na ofii hɔ kɔe. Amankani, Coco-yam no bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Aburo, Nnua no bae (na wɔkɔe) saa ara; Atwibo, Yam no bae (na wɔkɔe) saa ara; Adua, Bean no bae (na wɔkɔe) saa ara; Akam, Potato no bae (na wɔkɔe) saa ara; Akapinkye, Red-roasted plantain no bae (na wɔkɔe) saa ara; Bankye, Cassava no bae (na wɔkɔe) saa ara; Bere, Paw-paw no bae (na wɔkɔe) saa ara; Emo, Apete, bae (na wɔkɔe) saa ara; Efrere, Pumpkin no bae (na wɔkɔe) saa ara; Habere, Bush-yam no bae (na wɔkɔe) saa ara; Badu, Kwadu, Kwadu a, bae (na kɔe) saa ara; Kontomere, Coco-yam nhaban, bae (na kɔe) saa ara; Krobona, Bean, bae (na kɔe) saa ara; Mako, red-pepper, bae (na kɔe) saa ara; Nkatee, Ground-nut, bae (na kɔe) saa ara; Nwiadowa, Garden-egg, bae (na kɔe) saa ara; Nkuruma, Okro, bae (na kɔe) saa ara; na afei Dokon'fa, Half-a-ball-of-Kenki (Boiled-pounded-corn), baa abɛka sɛ:

"Hena na ɔda hɔ a ɔyɛ tuntum saa?

Hena na ɔda hɔ a ne ho hyerɛn yiye saa?"

(The Fly) kae sɛ:

"Ɛyɛ me, Fly, Kwao Boakye, Adenen!

Gyahene Kofi Amo, Adenen!

Ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ memmra na yɛnkɔhwehwɛ mmabaa.

Adenen!

Mmabaa no ka sɛ wɔnnɔ no.

Adenen!

Ɔkyekyeree me.

Adenen!

Ɔkyekyeree me koraa.

Adenen!

Gyefo, gye me nkwa.

Adenen!

Bere a woagye me nkwa no, fa ɔkwan no na kɔ.

Adenen!"

Ɔkaa sɛ: "Mate, mɛma mo ho atɔ mo". Ɔkɔɔ hɔ, na ɔtwee ne ho; na bere a ɔyɛɛ saa no, hwɛ, akekantwɛre no yii ne ho adi, na ɔkae sɛ: "Adɛn nti na moatwe me onipa no?" Ɔkaa sɛ: "Matwe ne ho, na nea mobɛyɛ no, monyɛ". Ɔkaa sɛ: "Me ne mo de, merentumi nyɛ hwee, gye me ne mo nko ara na yɛde yɛn nsa bɛko". Na ɔkae sɛ: "Momma yɛnhyew no". Na akekantwɛre no hyee no. Na bere koro no ara wɔde wɔn ho bɔɔ ɔko, (na nne no te sɛ) Yiri! Yiri! Yiri! Na akekantwɛre no apow akekantwɛre no, na ɔtow no kyenee, bam! Na akraman no kae sɛ, "Me nso mefa biribi a ɛte saa kɔhohoro me yere sanho, (na) wɔ yɛn nhyiam a edi hɔ no mu, sɛ obi tow ɔfoforo no fi ne ho a, ɛno ne ade a etwa to no". Na wɔde wɔn ho bɛkaa ho (ko, na na ɛte sɛ) Kikiri! Kikiri! Kikiri! Na Half-a-ball-of-Kenki maa akraman no so na ɔtow no too ogya mu. Akraman no kae sɛ, "_Me firim, me firim, me firim_" (mefi, mefi, mefi, mefi. Saa na akraman no teɛɛm "_Me firim_"). Na wohuu sɛ ne honam te sɛ nea ɛteɛ yi; kurukyire, kurukyire, kurukyire; baabi a fitaa wɔ no, ɛhɔ na mfuturo no kaa no; na baabi a tuntum wɔ no, ɛhɔ nso na kotoku no kaa no; na sɛ wufi adi a, wubehu sɛ afifide no da hɔ a wɔakyekyere no a, wubehu nhwiren pii a wɔatwa ne ho ahyia; na eyi nyɛ biribi foforo biara, gye eyi nko ara, na wɔda no ase.

Eyi ne me ho asɛm a maka akyerɛ mo no. Sɛ ɛyɛ fɛ anaa ɛnyɛ fɛ a, mobɛfa bi sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no nso, mobɛkamfo me.
