Nea yɛreka yi nyɛ nokware.

ASƐM A ƐDA SO KRAMAN, Ɔkraman no, Wontumi Nnya Ne Dodow No Ho Ayaresa Da (Ɔkwan)

NA ƆKURU bi wɔ hɔ a na ɔrentumi mfa ne yere mmɛware no. Enti ofii hɔ kɔɔ kurow bi mu kɔfaa ɔbaa bi, na ɔne ne yere tenaa hɔ kakra, na ofii hɔ kɔɔ ne kurom, na bere bi akyi no, ne kunu somaa no sɛ ɔmmra hɔ. N'asefo faa n'akode kɔɔ wuram sɛ ɔrekɔkum mmoadabi bi de asiesie ne ho ama Ɔkraman no ba. Nnansa akyi no, Ɔkraman no kɔɔ hɔ, na bere a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, ohui sɛ mmoadabi bi nam wɔ ogya mu wɔ adiwo hɔ. Ná Ɔkraman no rebɛkɔ akɔbɔ nsu, na ohuu nam no sɛ ɛretɔ gya no mu. Ɔkraman no san kɔdaa hɔ, na ɔde ne ti too ɔpon no so (na ɔdaa hɔ) na ɔhwɛɛ nam no hwɛe. Afei na nam no huam duu ne so; ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ obegyina ano, nanso wantumi. Oh! ɔsɔre kɔɔ baabi a na nam no wɔ hɔ; ɔkyekyeree ne nyinaa, na ɔsan kɔdaa hɔ. Da a edi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma no fii ase sɛ ɛreba no, bere a ne yere rekasa no, ɔhwehwɛɛ nam no, na emu biako mpo nni hɔ. Ne yere kae sɛ: "O maame! O maame! Obi ate nam no nyinaa". Bere a Ɔkraman no tee eyi no, ne ani wui, na ɔfaa n'atade kataa ne ti so, na ɔdaa hɔ komm. Ɛhɔ ara, ɔsɔre kɔɔ ne kurom kɔkaa sɛnea ɔkɔe kɔkyekyeree n'ase no nam, ne sɛnea n'ani wui wɔ aniwu mu, ne sɛnea na ɔrehwehwɛ obi a ɔwɔ ɔkorɔmfo ho aduru a ɔde bɛtwe n'aso. Nkurɔfo no kae sɛ: "Adanko, Hare no wɔ aduru a tumi wom paa". Ɔkraman no fii hɔ, na ntɛm ara odii Hare no akuraa no ase. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Agya Hare, merehwehwɛ wo mmoa; meyɛ ɔkorɔmfo, ɔkorɔmfo, ɔkorɔmfo, na wɔka sɛ: 'Wowɔ eyi ho aduru,' enti mesrɛ wo, fa bi ma me, na biribiara a ɛho behia no, mɛtua". Hare no kae sɛ: "Mewɔ bi, enti kɔ na kɔfa m'afum nam".

Ɔpɔnkɔ no fii hɔ kɔɔ akuraa bi ase, na ɔmaa ne nan akyi baako a ne duru yɛ domfa sikakɔkɔɔ dɔte (a ɛkyerɛ 3s. 6d) na ɔde bae. Ɔpɔnkɔ no pɔɔ no, twaa no, na ɔtwee no guu nnua nketewa so, na ɔtwee aburoo, nkyene, ne nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn, na ɔtwee no nam mu, na ɔde too ogya so. Bere a ogya no de ne nsa kaa no no, bere bi akyi no, ɔtee sɛ ɛrehyew, hyee, eee! Ɔkraman no hwɛɛ no bere tenten. Afei ɔkraman no kae sɛ: "Ɔkraman, kɔ na kɔda ɔdan biako mu, na me nso mɛkɔ na makɔda ɔdan foforo mu, na ɔkyena anɔpa, mɛyi nam yi afi hɔ na mede ayɛ aduru ama wo". Ɔkraman no kɔdae. Ɔkraman no nso kɔdae. Na Ɔkraman no da hɔ daa hɔ, nanso na ontumi nna, na nam no huam a efi n'ano bae no kaa no, nanso na ɔnkasa. Na afei ɔtee nka bio. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Agya Hare". Hare no kae sɛ: "Yiw". Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Mo kurom wɔ mmoadabi, enti merekɔ akɔfa nam no, na mede ahyɛ me nkyɛn". Hare no kae sɛ: "Kɔ na kɔfa; na me de, nea ehia me ara ne sɛ mede me nsa bɛto so ɔkyena, na mayɛ nnuru ama mo". Enti Ɔkraman no kɔɔ kɔfaa no, na ɔkraman no da hɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Agya Hare". Ɔkae sɛ: "Yiw!" Ɔkaa sɛ: "Abofra bɛka, enti mɛhyɛ no me dua ase, na makɔda". Hare no kae sɛ: "Nea ehia me ara ne sɛ ɔkyena, bere a nneɛma befi hɔ no, mɛma wo nnuru". Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Agya Hare, mpakye yi aba me dua ase, enti mɛfa nam no de agu me ti so, na mɛgyina hɔ akosi sɛ nneɛma bɛda adi". Hare no kae sɛ: "Aane, nea ɛhaw me ara ne sɛ mɛkɔ (n'anum no) hɔ ɔkyena akɔyɛ aduru ama wo". Ɔkraman no faa nam no de guu ne ti so, na ɛhɔ na ɔgyinae. Afei ɔtwee nnuhuam no bi guu n'anim, na ɔtwee no. Ɔkae sɛ: "Agya Hare, saa nam yi ho de, mpakye no foro baa me so, na wɔbaa me ti so, na mɛhyɛ (aduan no) m'anom". Hare no kae sɛ: "O! Meka kyerɛ mo sɛ ɔkyena, bere a nneɛma bɛda adi no, mede bɛyɛ aduru ama mo". Saa bere no na ɛyɛ den wɔ n'anom, na ɔtwaa ne ho hyiae wɔ dan no mu; ɔtwee n'afiri (a ne ho yɛ den), fre! fre! fre! Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Agya Hare, ma menyɛ wo honam yi ho ka". Hare no kae sɛ: "Ɛdeɛn nti na wopɛ sɛ woyɛ me honam yi ho ka?" Ɔkraman no kae sɛ: "Mede, mmom sen sɛ saa mpakye yi bedi no, ma mentua ka no mma wo". Hare no kae sɛ: "Dabi, ma ɔkyena anɔpa mmra". Ade kyee anɔpa no, Apɔnkɔ no kae sɛ: "Akraman, fa saa nam no ma me". Apɔnkɔ no buae sɛ: "Ho, Agya Apɔnkɔ, adɛn nti na woantie nea meka kyerɛɛ wo da a edi kan no, sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mede wo ka (nanso nam no) no?" Apɔnkɔ no buae sɛ: "Enti wodii no?" Apɔnkɔ no kae sɛ: "Aane! midii no, efisɛ matɔ no". Apɔnkɔ no kae sɛ: "Wo, Apɔnkɔ, korɔn a wɔfrɛ no 'korɔn' no renka wo ho da".

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
