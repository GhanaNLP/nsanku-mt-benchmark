Nea yɛreka yi nyɛ nokware.

ASƐM A ƐDA SO KRAMAN, Ɔkraman no, Wontumi Nnya Ne Dodow No Ho Ayaresa Da (Ɔkwan)

BERE bi na ɔkraman bi wɔ hɔ, na esiane sɛ na ɔyɛ ɔkorɔmfo nti, wantumi anware no. Enti ofii hɔ kɔɔ kurow bi mu kɔfaa ɔbaa bi a ɔne no bɛwaree. (Ɔwaree, na ɔne no san kɔɔ ne kurom.) Ɔne ne yere tenaa hɔ (bere bi), na ofii hɔ san kɔɔ ne kurom. Bere bi akyi no, ne kunu somaa no sɛ ɔmmra hɔ. Nnansa akyi no, Akoa no kɔɔ hɔ, na bere a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, ohui sɛ antelope nam wɔ ogya no so wɔ adiwo hɔ. Anadwo no, ɔne ne yere kɔhomee. Akoa no rebɛkɔ akɔbɔ nsu, na ohui sɛ nam no ne dɔte no agu gya no mu. Akoa no san kɔe kɔdaa hɔ, na ɔde ne ti too ɔpon no so (na ɔdaa hɔ), na ɔhwɛɛ nam no hwɛe. Afei na nam no huam duu no so; ɔpɛɛ sɛ ɔso no, nanso wantumi. Oh! ɔsɔre kɔɔ baabi a na nam no wɔ hɔ; ɔkyekyeree ne nyinaa, na ɔsan kɔdaa hɔ. Da a edi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma no fii ase sɛ ɛreba no, bere a ne yere rekasa no, ɔhwehwɛɛ nam no, na emu biako mpo nni hɔ. Ne yere kae sɛ: "O maame! O maame! Obi ate nam no nyinaa". Bere a Ɔkraman no tee eyi no, ne ani wui, na ɔfaa n'atade kataa ne ti so, na ɔdaa hɔ komm. Afei ntɛm ara na ɔsɔre fii kwan no so; oguan kɔɔ ne kurom, na ɔkaa asɛm no, sɛnea ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔkyekyeree n'asefo nam, ne sɛnea n'ani wui wɔ aniwu mu, ne sɛnea, esiane eyi nti, na ɔrehwehwɛ obi a ɔwɔ akorɔmfo ho mmoawammoawa bi a wɔde bɛma no. Nkurɔfo kae sɛ: "Adanko, Hare no wɔ nnuru bi a tumi wom paa". Ɔkraman no fii hɔ, na ntɛm ara odii Hare no kurom. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Agya Hare, merehwehwɛ wo mmoa; meyɛ ɔkorɔmfo, ɔkorɔmfo, ɔkorɔmfo, na wɔka sɛ: Wowɔ eyi ho aduru, enti mesrɛ wo, ma me bi, na biribiara a ɛho behia no, mɛtua". Hare no kae sɛ: "Mewɔ bi, enti kɔ na kɔfa m'afum nam".

Ɔpɔnkɔ no fii hɔ kɔɔ akuraa bi ase, na ɔfaa ne nan akyi baako a ne duru yɛ domfa sika dɔte (a ɛkyerɛ 3s. 6d) na ɔde bae. Ɔpɔnkɔ no pɔɔ no, twaa no, na ɔtwee no guu nnua nketewa so, na ɔtwee aburoo, nkyene, ne nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn, na ɔtwee no nam mu, na ɔde no too ogya no so. Bere a ogya no kaa no, bere bi akyi no, ɔtee sɛ ɛrehyew, hyee, eee! Ɔpɔnkɔ no hwɛɛ no bere tenten. Na ɔkraman no kae sɛ: Ɔkraman, kɔda ɔdan biako mu, na me nso mɛkɔ akɔda ɔdan foforo mu, na ɔkyena anɔpatutu a, mɛyi nam yi afi hɔ, na mayɛ nnuru ama wo. Na ɔkraman no kɔdae, na ɔkraman no nso kɔdae, na ɔkraman no kɔdaa hɔ, na ɔdaa hɔ, na ne hwene baa ne hwene so, na wanka hwee; na afei ofii ase kasae bio. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Agya Hare". Hare no kae sɛ: "Yiw". Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Mo kurom wɔ mmoadabi, enti merekɔ akɔfa nam no, na mede ato me nkyɛn". Hare no kae sɛ: "Kɔ na kɔfa; na me de, nea ehia me ara ne sɛ mede me nsa bɛgu so ɔkyena, na mayɛ nnuru ama mo". Enti Ɔkraman no kɔɔ kɔfaa no, na ɔkraman no da hɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Agya Hare". Ɔkae sɛ: "Yiw". Ɔkae sɛ: "Abofra no bɛka, enti mɛto no me dua ase, na makɔda". Hare no kae sɛ: "Nea ehia me ara ne sɛ ɔkyena, bere a nneɛma befi hɔ no, mɛma wo nnuru". Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Agya Hare, mpakye yi aba m'afunumu ase, enti mɛfa nam no de agu me ti so, na mɛgyina hɔ akosi sɛ nneɛma bɛda adi". Hare no kae sɛ: "Aane, nea ɛhaw me ara ne sɛ mɛkɔ so anya (aduan no) ɔkyena na mayɛ aduru ama wo". Meka kyerɛ wo sɛ ɔkyena, bere a nneɛma bɛda adi no, mɛma wo nnuru". Afei na ɛyɛ ntɛm wɔ n'anom, na ɔtwaa ne ho hyiae wɔ dan no mu hɔ; ɔbobɔɔ n'afiri (a na ɛnne a ɛresesa no) fre! fre! fre! ɔkae sɛ, "Agya Hare, ma me ka wo nam yi ho". Hare no kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wopɛ sɛ wode ka no?" Dog no buae sɛ, "Me kyerɛ sɛ, sen sɛ mpakye yi bedi no a, ma memma wo sika no". Hare no kae sɛ, "Oh, ɛnte saa! ma ɔkyena anɔpa mmra". Oh! nanso afei Dog no gyaee (ɔnam no) kɔ (ne kɔn mu). Afei ɔhwehwee na ɔdae. Ade kyee anɔpa no, Apɔnkɔ no kae sɛ: "Akraman, fa saa nam no ma me". Apɔnkɔ no buae sɛ: "Ho, Agya Apɔnkɔ, adɛn nti na woantie nea meka kyerɛɛ wo da a edi kan no, sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mede wo ka (aduan no) no?" Apɔnkɔ no buae sɛ: "Enti wodii no?" Apɔnkɔ no kae sɛ: "Aane! midii no, efisɛ matɔ no". Apɔnkɔ no kae sɛ: "Wo, Apɔnkɔ, korɔn a wɔfrɛ no 'korɔn' no renka wo ho da".

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
