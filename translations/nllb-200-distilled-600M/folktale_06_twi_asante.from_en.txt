Sɛ Obi Yɛ Wo Biribi Pa a, Ma W'ani Nnye Sɛ Wode Biribi Pa Ayɛ No

Wɔkae sɛ: "Ná ɔkɔre bi wɔ hɔ, na bere a ɔretu kwan no, ohyiaa ɔbaa panyin bi a ne nan mu yɛ den, na ɔkɔre no kae sɛ: 'Onyankopɔn adom me! Eyi yɛ ɔyare wɔ sukuu mu nko ara; sɛ woyɛ saa a, ɛmfa ho sɛ wobɔ mmɔden sɛ wobɛtumi nantew anaa?" Ɔbea no kae sɛ, "Aah, kakraa bi pɛ". Owifo no kae sɛ, "Mo nkurɔfo, ɛnnɛ, sɛ meyɛ biribi pa ma mo (nnɛ), ɔkyena mobɛfa biribi bɔne abrɛ me ase". Owifo no kae sɛ, "Aah, mintumi nyɛ (ɛno)". Owifo no kae sɛ, "Sɛ muntumi nyɛ mo ho saa a, mate. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Kata w'ani na bue". Na ɔbaa no too n'ani na obuee wɔn. Na ɔkɔre no kae sɛ: "Hwɛ wo yare no". Na bere a ɔbaa no bae sɛ ɔrebɛhwɛ no no, wanhu ho ade biara. Ɛnna ɔtotoo n'ani bio, na obuee wɔn, na ohui sɛ asaase no nyinaa afi hɔ. Na ɔkae sɛ: "Kata wɔn bio". Na ɔtotoo wɔn, na obuee wɔn, na afie no gyinaa hɔ pintinn. Na ɔtwee wɔn ano bio, na obuee wɔn ano, na ohuu kurow bi a ne kɛse te sɛ saa kurow no. Na ɔkɔre no kae sɛ: Ɔbea, ɛyɛ wo dea. Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Meda wo ase". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Dɛn na memfa mfa mma wo?" Ɛhɔ na ɔkɔre no kae sɛ: "Mempɛ ade ketewaa bi mpo, na me de, nea mepɛ ara ne owu dua a egyina hɔ yi". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Dɛn na ɛwɔ mu yi, gye". Ɛnna ɔkɔre no tu fii hɔ, na osii ne so, na ɔtwee ntɛtɛw, na ɔtoo nnomaa abien, na ɔde siee. Ɔbea panyin no kae se, "Dɛn na ɛrekɔ so?" Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ, "Ma menkyekyere ɔkɔre mma". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ, "Ɛhe na menya ɔkɔre mma?" Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ, "Ɛhe na menya ɔkɔre mma?" Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ, "Ɛhe na menya ɔkɔre mma?" Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Dɛn na asi?" Na abofra no kae sɛ: "Ma menka Eagle mma, na sɛ mannya bi a mɛka a, mɛwu". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Ah! ɛsɛ sɛ eyi, me babarima babarima wu esiane sɛ Eagle mma nni hɔ a ɔbɛka nti? Sɔre, fa nkotodwe na kɔbɔ atweaatam dua no na fa nkotodwe mma no ma me. " Akuraa no mufo kɔɔ hɔ, (nkotodwe no bɔe), Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! Ɛte sɛ nea dua no retɔ fam, bere a ɔkɔre mma panyin no twitwaa ne ho, na ɔbaa hɔ bɛgyinaa wuram no ano, na ɔteɛɛm; ɔfrɛɛ ɛna no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ:

Sango, anomaa no!

Sango, anomaa no, ɔkɔre no ba!

Sango, anomaa no!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔ adidi a, san bra!

Sango, anomaa no!

Sango, oh oh!

Ɔbea no tee sɛ ne ba no resu, na ɔsɔre (na ne ntaban apirapira) no, ɔbaa hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ: Sanguri! Na dua no (a ɛbɛn sɛ wotwa no) san bae, na nnipa a wɔbobɔɔ no no nyinaa bɛmenee; na ɔfaa aduan a ɔde bae no de maa ne mma. Afei ɔkraa wɔn kae sɛ, "Merekɔ, sɛ ɔbea panyin no ba bɛfa wo a, ma ɔmfa wo". Na ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔbɔ dua no na kɔfa mmoa no mmra mma me banana no mmɛkyekyere". Na wɔkɔɔ hɔ ne mprenu so, Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! Ná ɛte sɛ nea dua no bɛkɔ asase so, bere a ɔkɔre no ba no fii adi, na ɔbaa hɔ bɛgyinaa wuram no ano, na ɔfrɛɛ ne maame ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ:

Sango, anomaa no!

Sango, anomaa no, ɔkɔre no ba!

Sango, anomaa no!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔ adidi a, san bra!

Sango, anomaa no!

Sango, oh oh!

Ɔfrɛɛ ne maame, na ɔfrɛɛ no, na ɔfrɛɛ no, na ɔfrɛɛ no, na ɔfrɛɛ no mmuaeɛ. Na afei dua no kasae bere a ɛhwe fam no, "Brim!" (ɛkae). Wɔfaa ɔkɔre mma no, na wɔde baako maa ɔbaawa no, na nea aka no tu fii hɔ kɔtenaa Wawa dua so. Bere tiaa bi akyi no, ɔkɔre no bae. Bere a oduu dua a wɔatwa no no so no, ohuu ne mma no mu biako sɛ ɔte hɔ; obisaa no nea asi, na ɔkaa asɛm no nyinaa kyerɛɛ no. ɔkɔre no ofii hɔ kɔɔ ɔbaa no akuraa ase. Bere a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, na ɔbaa no babarima no redidi ne mma no mu biako. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔbaawa, meda wo ase". Ɔfiri ɔbaawa no fie baeɛ na ɔfirii (nʼanwanwadeɛ) ase wɔ kuro no akyi. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sanguri", na nnipa nyinaa yeraeɛ, na ɔsan kaa sɛ, "Sanguri", na afie nyinaa sɛee prɛko pɛ na afie baako mpo annyaeɛ. "Sanguri", na akuraa no san bɛyɛɛ kwaeɛ. "Sanguri", na ɔbaawa no yareɛ saneeɛ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: Ɔbea, woahu sɛ mpanyimfo no ka sɛ: Sɛ obi yɛ wo papa a, yi no ayɛ ma no, na mfa bɔne nka ho mma no. Eyi ne me asɛm a meka kyerɛɛ wo, sɛ ɛyɛ fɛ anaa ɛnyɛ fɛ a, fa bi yɛ nokware, na nea aka no nso, kamfo me.
