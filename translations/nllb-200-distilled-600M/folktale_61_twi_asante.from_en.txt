ASƐM A ƐDA SO Sɛ Obi Kɔ Ɔdan Mu Anadwo a, Ɛyɛ Sum

WƆKA sɛ Kuntukurunku, Odds, ne Kuntunamoa, Ends, fii ɔdan no atifi bɛhwee fam. Wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Otumfoɔ asase, woyɛ ɔtreneeni". Otumfoɔ asase kae sɛ: "Adɛn nti na woka sɛ meyɛ ɔtreneeni?" Wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Esiane sɛ woantutu ɔbodan bi amma yɛn ankɔda mu". Otumfoɔ asase danee n'ani kyerɛɛ Prae, a ɔyɛ ɔtreneeni no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Wo anom da nnomaa nsonsonoe so". Otumfoɔ no kae sɛ: "Adɛn nti na wobɔ me ahohora?" Otumfoɔ asase kae sɛ: "Sɛ mayɛ wo ahohora a, me nso mayɛ wo ahohora; na sɛ wunnim nea enti a, gyae". Otumfoɔ no danee n'ani kyerɛɛ Abena, ɔtreneeni no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Fa nneɛma no kɔ na tow kyene". Broom kae sɛ, " (((Ɛwɔ mu nyinaa) mayɛ saa". Strip-of-bark nso ka kyerɛɛ Sumina, Midden-heap sɛ, "Wode nea wo nsa ka no sie". Midden-heap kae sɛ, "Adɛn nti na wotaa me?" (Strip-of-bark) buae sɛ, "Mayɛ wo ayayade, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a, ɛnde gyae bisa". Ck." Little black-ant kae sɛ, "Adɛn nti na wotaa me?" Palm-tree buae sɛ, "Mayɛ wo mmonnaa, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a, gyae bisa". Little black-ant, ɔno nso kɔɔ Esono, Elephant hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ne nan so; na ɔkyekyeree Elephant no. Bere a na (Elephant no) rebɔ ne nan so no, afei ɔbaa Tamiriwa, Black-sneak no ani ase, na Black-sneak no ani fii ase de nsu guu hɔ, na ɔtwee nsu no fii efan dua bi a ɛbɛn hɔ no so. (Seesei), aduan a Ɔbaa a Ɔbenfo-Nanan di no yɛ efan afifide no, na ɛno ara ne bere a Tamiriwa a wɔfrɛ no Black-Snail no apopa nsu no fii n'ani mu no. 'Kraman a wɔfrɛ no Dog, na ɔfrɛɛ Dog no, na ɔfaa nnyina no de maa Dog no de no tenaa ne ti so. Bere a Dog no rekɔ no, onyaa kola-akwaa, na obubuu biako mu abien, na ɔtow biako guu Okagya a wɔfrɛ no Squirrel no ano. Ok, ɔno nso faa kola-akwaa no de bɔɔ Onyina a wɔfrɛ no Silk-cotton-tree no. Silk-cotton-tree no nso, ne bo fuwii. Ɔhunuu sɛ abofra ketewa bi retwam, na ɔtwee n'abaa, na ɔhwee no kum no. Afei, saa abofra ketewa no yɛ Nyankonpon, ɔsoro-nyame no ba. Ɛhɔ ara na wɔkyeree Silk-cotton dua no, na wɔde no kɔɔ ɔsoro-nyame no anim. Wɔbisaa no (Silk-cotton-tree) sɛ: "Ɛdeɛn na ɛrekɔ so yi?" Silk-cotton-tree no kaa sɛ: "Na me gyina hɔ wɔ m'ankasa baabi, berɛ a Squirrel yi a ɔyɛ abofra yi (ɔteɛ) sɛ ɔfa kola-nut na ɔde bɛhwe me, na me deɛ, berɛ a abofra yi retwam no, misusuwii sɛ ɔyɛ Okagya, Squirrel no, na meyii m'abaa no mu baako de bɔɔ no, na hwɛ, na ɛyɛ wo ba no". Nyame, Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ: "Fa no kyekyere no dua so, na frɛ Okagya, Squirrel no, sɛ ɔmmra". Okagya bae; ɔkae sɛ: "Owura, wunim sɛ me mmara no yɛ kola-nut no, nanso Okraman, Dog-nut no, na ɔkɔe, na ɔbaa o ne, na ɔtow no guu m'abobow ano, na bere a merebɛtow no akyene no, ɛbaa Old-Man-Silk-cotton-tree. "Ɔsoro-nyame kae sɛ, "Fa no, na kyekyere no dua mu, na frɛ Dog no mma". Ɔkraman no kae sɛ: "Owura, na menam m'ankasa dan mu baabi baabi seesei woahunu sɛnea me ho yɛ den (a ɛyɛ den) bere a Ɔbaa Kunini Nana no faa ne nnyina no na ɔde too me ti so no; afei Okagya, Nkokoaa no, ɔno na ɔda n'abura mu wɔ afuw no mu a Ɔbaa Kunini Nana no ayɛ sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obenya biribi adi, nanso ɔno na Ɔbaa Kunini Nana no maa me so de ne nnyina no; me nso, mefaa kola-akwaa na mede guu ne abura no ano". Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Fa no, kyekyere no dua so, na frɛ Ɔbaa Kunini Nana na ɔmmra". Ɔbaa Kunini no bae. Ɔkae sɛ: "Aah, me mmanana, monhwɛ m'anom, me a mintumi nkeka nam, efan afifide ne nea midi; bio nso, mehohoro Bosompra; m'atemmusɛm ne Tamiriwa, Black-snail; na Ɔkraman no nko ara na onim eyi, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ Tamiriwa, na ɔmaa no nsa fii n'anom, na ɔde guu efan afifide a mete so no mu. Ɛno nti na mede me nnyina maa no sɛ ɔnsoa". Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ: "O, fa no kyekyere no dua so, na kɔfa Tamiriwa, Black-snail no mmra". Black-snail no bae. Ɔkae sɛ: "Me wura, na mewɔ m'ankasa baabi, hwɛ, ɔsono kɛse kɛse bi twaa baabi a ɛwɔ m'ani ase pɛɛ no so, na mekɔfaa efan (afua) sɛ mepopae. Ɛno nko ara ne nea minim". Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ: "Fa no kyekyere no dua bi so, na momfa Esono, ɔsono no mmra". Enti wɔkɔfaa Esono. Agyenkwa no kae sɛ: "Nnansa a atwam no, na mete hɔ, na me honam fii ase tewee, na bere a mihuu baabi a paee me sɛ mede asɛm yi paee me sɛ saa ɔkwasea yi a ne ho yɛ den no kɔɔ (me honam mu) sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obenya nam a ɔbɛka, na bere a na merete me nan so no, ɛbaa ne so, na ɛbɔɔ Tamiriwa, a ɔyɛ Black-Snail no". Ɔsoro-anyame no kae sɛ: "Fa no kyekyere dua bi so, na fa Tetea, a ɔyɛ Little-black-ant no". Bere a Tetea bae no, ɔkae sɛ: "Owura, dua bi wɔ hɔ; bere a ɛsow aba no, (ne ntasu) yɛ tuntum kan; bere a me, a meyɛ Black-ant no, menamfa wɔn so no, ɛma wɔn nyin. Da bi a atwam no na mewɔ hɔ, bere a Atɔe dua no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ m'aso yɛ tuntum, na esiane sɛ meyɛ ketewa nti, misusuw sɛ mɛkɔ Elephant hɔ akɔhwehwɛ nam a mɛdidi na mayɛ kɛse no. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na kɔfrɛ Atɔe dua no sɛ ɔmmra. Abe, Atɔe dua no, bae; ɔkae sɛ, Sire, eyi ne nea ɛsɛ sɛ meka: Me ne Sumina, Midden-heap, te faako, na sɛ abo a wɔtow gu ne so no dɔɔso dodo, na ontumi nnyina bio a, me gye bi na mede gu me kɔn ho; nanso afei de, ɔtoo me mmonnaa ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ magyae me kɔn ho. Esiane eyi nti, me de, m'ani dwirii, na me nso, meyɛɛ Little-black-ant no mmonnaa. Ɔsoro-nyame kae sɛ, fa no na kyekyere no dua mu, na frɛ Sumina, Midden-heap, sɛ ɔmmra. Midden-heap bae; ɔkae sɛ, Sire, Abena, Strip-of-bark, a, sɛ wɔtotow nwura mu wɔ dan bi mu a, wɔde ma no sɛ ɔmfa mmra me nkyɛn. Da a edi hɔ no, a onni asɛm biara a ɛbɛhaw no, ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, Wode nea wo nsa ka no sie (i.e. Wɔyɛ adifudepɛfo), na mekae sɛ, a, na bere a Palm-dua no bɛn no, me nso meyɛɛ no bɔne. Ɔsoro-nyame kae sɛ, fa no kyekyere no dua bi ho, na frɛ Abena, Bark-of-bark, ma ɔmmra. Bark-of-bark bae; ɔkae sɛ, Sire, bere a Prae, Brom no, tow dan bi mu, sɛ wɔamma me (n'ahohwi) a, sɛ mframa bɔ a, afifide no nyinaa ba bio; afei, da a edi hɔ yi, ɛwom sɛ manyɛɛ no hwee de, ɔkae sɛ, Wobegye nneɛma de agu. Eyi nti, me nso m'ani dwirii, na mekɔyɛɛ Sumina, Midden-heap no bɔne. Ɔsoro-nyame kae sɛ, fa no, na kyekyere no dua bi so, na ɔtow no nkyene. Prae, Brom no, baa ha. Brom no bae; ɔkae sɛ, Sire, Old-Mother-earth da hɔ dinn, n'anim yɛ mprɛte, enti bere biara a mesɔre no, mepopa ne ho fĩ nyinaa na meyɛ no fɛ; nanso da foforo no, Maame-Asase ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, Wo anom ayɛ nnomaa nsonsonoe, na me nufu nso ayɛ den, na mekɔɔ ne nkyɛn kɔyɛɛ Abena, Strip-of-k no bɔne. Ɔsoro-anyame no kae sɛ, Fa no kyekyere no dua so, na frɛ Maame-asase sɛ ɔmmra. Bere a Maame-asase bae no, ɔkae sɛ, Sire, ɛhɔ na mete; wɔto me so; menka hwee da; wɔyɛ nneɛma a ɛtete saa nyinaa. M'ani so, nanso menka hwee da. Afei da bi a na mewɔ hɔ no, Kuntukurunku ne Kuntunamoa, Odds and Ends, bɛhwee me so, na mekae sɛ, O! dɛn na ɛrekɔ so? na wɔkae sɛ, Wonnwene amoa a yɛbɛda mu, na wo ne wo flatness nyinaa nyɛ mfaso biara (ma obiara). Eyi nti, me nso, meyɛɛ Prae, the Broom ho ayayade. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Fa no kyekyere no dua mu, na kɔfa Odds and Ends. Odds and Ends bae. Wɔkae sɛ, Sire, na yɛwɔ ɔdan no atifi, na na yɛredi agorɔ, na yɛhwehwee ase, na yetwaa yɛn ho yaw; eyi nti na yɛkaa sɛ Maame Asase yɛ fɛ, na ɔkae sɛ ɔteɛ. Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn kae sɛ, Asomfo, yɛate asɛm yi nyinaa, enti momma yɛnsɔre nkɔ dan no mu nkɔhwehwɛ adanse no mu na yɛmmra mmɛbu atɛn. Enti wɔsɔre kɔɔ dan bi mu, na wɔtoo pon no mu. Ɛno nti, sɛ wokɔ dan bi mu anadwo a, soro Nyame ne n'Asomfo resusuw ho wɔ hɔ.

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
