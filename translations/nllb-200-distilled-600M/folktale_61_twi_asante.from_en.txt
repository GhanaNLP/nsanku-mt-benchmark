ASƐM A ƐDA SO Sɛ Obi Kɔ Ɔdan Mu Anadwo a, Ɛyɛ Sum

WƆKA sɛ Kuntukurunku, Odds, ne Kuntunamoa, Ends, fii ɔdan no atifi bɛhwee fam. Wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Otumfoɔ asase, wo ho yɛ fɛ". Otumfoɔ asase kae sɛ: "Adɛn nti na woka sɛ me ho yɛ fɛ?" Wɔkae sɛ: "Esiane sɛ woankɔto ɔbodan bi amma yɛn ankɔda mu". Otumfoɔ asase dan n'ani kyerɛɛ Prae, a ɔyɛ Apete no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "W'ano ayɛ nnomaa nsonsonoe". Otumfoɔ no kae sɛ: "Adɛn nti na wobɔ me ahohora?" Otumfoɔ asase kae sɛ: "Sɛ mayɛ wo ahohora a, me nso mayɛ wo ahohora; na sɛ woanyɛ saa a, me nso mayɛ wo ahohora". Bere a Brom huu nea enti a ɔyɛɛ saa no, na ɔnsan mmisa no bio". Brom dan ne ho kyerɛɛ Abena, The-strip-of-bark (a wɔde tow hyɛ mu no), na ɔkae sɛ: "Fa (nneɛma) kɔtow kyene". Brom kae sɛ: "Adɛn nti na wobɔ me ahohora?" Brom kae sɛ: "Yei nyinaa akyi no) mayɛ saa". Brom nso de ne ho kɔmaa Sumina, Midden-heap kae sɛ: "Fa nea wo nsa aka no". Midden-heap kae sɛ: "Adɛn nti na wobɔ me ahohora?" He (Strip-of-bark) r Ɔkaa sɛ: "Mayɛ wo ayayade, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a woyɛ saa a, ɛnde gyae bisa". Midden-heap nso buae sɛ: "Wo kɔn nyinaa abobow". Palm-tree kae sɛ: "Adɛn nti na woayɛ me ayayade?" Midden-heap buae sɛ: "Mayɛ wo ayayade, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a woyɛ saa a, ɛnde gyae bisa". Palm-tree nso danee ne ho kɔ Tetea, Little-black-ant, na ɔkae sɛ: "Wo aso yɛ fitaa". Ck. " Little black-ant kae sɛ, "Adɛn nti na wotia me?" Palm-tree buae sɛ, "Mayɛ wo mmonnaa, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a, gyae bisa". Little black-ant, ɔno nso kɔɔ Esono, Elephant wɔ hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ne nan so; na ɔkyekyeree Elephant no. Bere a na ɔretrɛw ne nan so no, ɔtwee ne nan ase wɔ Tamiriwa aniwa ase pɛɛ, na ne aniwa fii ase sɛ ɛretrɛw, na ɔtwee nsu no fii efan dua bi a ɛbɛn hɔ no so. (Seesei), aduan a Nananom-Nananom di no ne efan dua no, na ɛhɔ na Tamiriwa, Black-Snail no, twee nsu no fii ne aniwa mu. 'Kraman, Ɔkraman no, na ɔretwam, na ɔfrɛɛ Ɔkraman no, na ɔfaa nnyina no de maa Ɔkraman no de no too ne ti so. Bere a Ɔkraman no rekɔ no, ɔfaa kola dua bi, na ɔkyɛɛ emu baako mu mmienu, na ɔtow emu baako guu Okagya, Squirrel, abura no ano. Okagya nso faa kola dua no de bɔɔ Onyina, Silk-cotton dua no. Afei, saa abofra ketewa no yɛ Nyankonpon, Ɔsoro-nyame no ba. Ɛhɔ na wɔkyeree Sɛnte-nyame no, na wɔde no kɔɔ Ɔsoro-nyame no anim. Wɔbisaa no (Silk-cotton-tree) sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na ɛrekɔ so yi?" Silk-cotton-tree no kaa sɛ, "Na me gyina hɔ wɔ m'ankasa baabi, bere a Squirrel yi a ɔyɛ abofra no (gyinaa ano) sɛ ɔbɛma so kola-nut na ɔde abɔ me, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a me, bere a saa abofra yi retwam no, misusuwii sɛ ɔyɛ Okagya, Squirrel no, na miyii m'abaa no mu biako de bɔɔ no, na hwɛ, na ɛyɛ wo ba". Nyame, Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Fa no na kyekyere no dua bi so, na frɛ Okagya, Nkwatani no, ma ɔmmra". Okagya bae; ɔkae sɛ, "Owura, wunim sɛ me mmara no yɛ kola-nut, nanso Okraman, Ɔkraman, Ɔkraman no kɔfaa biako, na ɔtow guu m'atenae no ano, na bere a na mitu no no, ɔkɔhwee Oldman-Silk-cotton-tree". Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Fa no, na kyekyere no dua bi so, na frɛ Ɔkraman no mma ɔmmra". T Hey kɔfrɛɛ Ɔkraman no sɛ ɔmmra. Ɔkraman no kae sɛ: "Owura, na menam m'ankasa me dan mu baabi, afei woahu sɛnea me ho yɛ den, bere a Ɔpanyin-Abea-Nokware no faa ne nnyina no na ɔde maa me ti so no; afei Okagya, Nkwatani no, ɔno na ɔda ne bon mu wɔ afuw no mu a Ɔpanyin-Abea-Nokware no ayɛ de anya biribi adi, nanso ɔno na Ɔpanyin-Abea-Nokware no maa me so de ne nnyina no; me nso, mefaa kola-nut bi Ɔbɔfo no kae sɛ: "Fa no, kyekyere no dua mu, na to nsa frɛ Ɔbaayewa Nana". Ɔbaayewa Nana baa hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ: "Aah, me mmanana, monhwɛ m'anom, me a mintumi nkeka nam, efan afifide ne nea midi; bio nso, mehohoro Bosompra; m'atemmusɛm ne Tamiriwa, Black-snail; Dog nkutoo na onim eyi, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ Tamiriwa, na ɔmaa no nsa fii n'anom, na ɔde guu efan afifide a mete so no mu. Ɛno nti na mede me nnyina maa no sɛ ɔmfa". Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "O, fa no na kyekyere no dua mu, na kɔfa Tamiriwa, Black-Snail no mmra". Black-Snail no bae. Ɔkae sɛ, "Me wura, na mewɔ m'ankasa baabi, hwɛ, ɔsono kɛse bi twaa baabi a ɛwɔ m'ani ase pɛɛ no so, na mekɔɔ kɔfaa efan (afum) sɛ mɛpopa. Ɛno nko ara na minim". Ɔsoro-anyame no kae sɛ, "Fa no kyekyere no dua so, na fa Esono, Elephant no ba". Enti wɔkɔfaa Esono. Agyenkwa no kae sɛ: "Nnansa a atwam no na mete hɔ, na me honam fii ase tewee, na bere a mihuu baabi a mede asɛmfua excuse me de dii dwuma no, saa ɔkwasea yi a ɔyɛ black ant no baa (me honam) mu sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obenya nam a ɔbɛka, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛdɔɔso; na bere a na mitu me nan so no, ɛkɔɔ so bɔɔ Tamiriwa, black-snail no so". Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Fa no kyekyere no dua so, na fa no bra. Tetea, Nhwiren ketewa no". Bere a Tetea bae no, ɔkae sɛ: "Me wura, hwɛ dua bi wɔ hɔ a, sɛ ɛsow a, ɛyɛ tuntum, na me a meyɛ nhwiren ketewa no twa wɔn so a, ɛma wɔn ho yɛ den. Da bi a atwam no na mewɔ hɔ, bere a nnua no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ m'aso yɛ tuntum, na esiane sɛ meyɛ ketewa nti, misusuw sɛ mɛkɔ Elephant hɔ akɔhwehwɛ nam a mɛdidi na mayɛ kɛse. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na kɔfrɛ nnua no na ɔmmra. Abe, nnua no bae; ɔkae sɛ, Sire, eyi ne nea ɛsɛ sɛ meka: Me ne Sumina, Midden-heap, te ase t Afei nso, bere a asaman a wɔtow gu ne so no aboro so, na ɔrentumi nnyina ano bio no, migye bi de hyɛ me kɔn mu; nanso afei de, ɔtoo me mmonnaa ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, me kɔn mu ayɛ me sɛ asaman. Esiane eyi nti, me de, m'ani kɔkɔɔ, na me nso, meyɛɛ Little-black-ant no bɔne. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Fa no na kyekyere no dua mu, na frɛ Sumina, Midden-heap, sɛ ɔmmra. Midden-heap bae; ɔkae sɛ, Sire, Abena, Strip-of-bark, a, sɛ wɔtotow nwura wɔ dan bi mu a, wɔde ma no sɛ ɔmfa mma me. Da bi a atwam no, bere a na onni biribi a ɛbɛhaw me no, ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, Wo de nea wunya no sie (a.e. Wɔyɛ adifudepɛfo); mekae sɛ, a, na bere a Palm-dua no bɛn hɔ no, me nso meyɛɛ no basabasa. Ɔsoro-nyame kae sɛ, fa no na kyekyere no dua so, na frɛ Abena, Bark-of-bark, na ɔmmra. Bark-of-bark bae; ɔkae sɛ, Sire, bere a Prae, Brom no, tow dan bi mu, sɛ wɔamma me (ne sweepings) a, bere a mframa bɔ no, afifide no nyinaa san ba; afei, da a edi hɔ no, ɛwom sɛ manyɛɛ no hwee de, nanso ɔno ara na ɔyɛɛ saa. Ɔkae sɛ, Wofa nneɛma de tow wɔn gu. Eyi nti, me nso m'ani dwirii, na mekɔɔ sumina, Midden-heap no ho. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Fa no, na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na frɛ Prae, Brom no, na ɔmmra ha. Brom no bae; ɔkae sɛ, Sire, Old-Mother-earth wɔ hɔ a, n'anim yɛ petee, enti bere biara a mesɔre no, mepopa ne ho fi nyinaa na memfa no nyɛ ɔkwammɔne Wo anom ayɛ (yɛ) nnomaa nsonsonoe, na m'atifi nso ayɛ ahome, na mekɔɔ ne nkyɛn kɔyɛɛ Abena, a ɔyɛ Bark-of-Stripe no ayayade. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Fa no kyekyere no dua mu, na frɛ Maame-asase sɛ ɔmmra. Bere a Maame-asase bae no, ɔkae sɛ, Sire, ɛhɔ na meda; wɔto me so; menka hwee da; wɔyɛ nneɛma ahorow nyinaa Afei da bi a na mewɔ hɔ no, Kuntukurunku ne Kuntunamoa, Odds and Ends, bɛhwee me so, na mekae sɛ, O! Ɛdɛn na ɛrekɔ so? na wɔkaa sɛ, Woantutu ɔbodan bi a yɛbɛda mu, na wo ne w'abobɔde nyinaa nni mfasoɔ biara. Esiane saa nti, me nso meyɛɛ Prae, Brom no bɔne. Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, Fa no kyekyere no dua so, na kɔfa Odds ne Ends. Odds ne Ends bae. Wɔkae sɛ, Me wura, na yɛwɔ ɔdan no atifi, na na yɛredi agorɔ, na yɛhwehwee ase, na yɛapirapira yɛn ho; eyi nti na yɛkaa sɛ Maame Asase yɛ fɛ, na ɔkae sɛ ɔteɛ. Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn kae sɛ, Asomfo, yɛate asɛm yi nyinaa, enti momma yɛnsɔre nkɔ ɔdan no mu nkɔhwehwɛ adanse no mu na yɛmmɛbu atɛn. Enti wɔsɔre kɔɔ ɔdan bi mu, na wɔtoo pon no mu. Ɛno nti, sɛ wokɔ ɔdan bi mu anadwo a, soro soro nyame ne n'Agyenkwa no resusuw ho wɔ hɔ.

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
