ASƐM A ƐDA SO OKOTO, KRAB no nyaa nhina, ne ABEREWA, Ɔbaa a Ɔyɛ Ɔpanyin no nyaa nhwi fitaa

Ɔbea bi woo ne ba. Bere a ɔsomaa no no, ɔkae sɛ: "Merennkɔ". Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Montena hɔ, wo ne w'akwan". Bere biara a ɔsomaa no no, ɔkae sɛ: "Merennkɔ". abofra no kasae na ɔkae sɛ: "Mɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ 'Mɛhwɛ' sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mɛhwɛ (sɛnea ɛte). " Na ofii hɔ kɔɔ ɔkwan no so, na ɔde ntɛmntɛm kɔe, (na ne nan ase dede te sɛ) yiridi, yiridi, yiridi. Awiei koraa no, ɔkɔɔ akuraa bi ase, na ohuu ɔbea panyin bi a ɔte hɔ; na ohuu no no, na ɔrepɛ atwe ne ho afi hɔ. Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Me ba, nsesa w'ani, efisɛ mahu wo". Na abofra no twaa n'anim, na ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ: "Adɛn nti na nsu a efi w'aniwa mu agu wo saa?" Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Ɛyɛ me". Afei ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Kɔ fie no akyi hɔ na kɔtutu yams na kɔfa wɔn; na sɛ wokɔ hɔ a, nea ɔbɛka sɛ, 'Tutu me, tutu me, tutu me,' mma woantutu no; na nea ɔbɛka sɛ, 'Ntu me, ntutu me,' nea ɔka sɛ, 'Tutu me.'" Ɔbuaa no sɛ: "Ho!" Nokwarem no, ɔkɔɔ hɔ; yams no bi ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Tutu me". na ɔbaa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Hwɛ wɔn so na fa wɔn hyɛ gya no so". Na ɔtwee wɔn so na ɔde wɔn hyehyɛɛ gya no so, na bere a woguan no, ɔtwee wɔn guu prɛte so, na ɔfaa wɔn de wɔn kɔtoo ɔbaa no anim. Na ɔkae sɛ: Monkɔ kurow no akyiri, na monhwɛ ɔdan bi a nam da so, na monhwɛ nea mo ani gye ho. Ɛnna abofra no faa bi, na ɔbaa hɔ, na ɔde guu ogya so, na ɔyɛɛ apontow; na wiei no, ɔde maa ɔbaa no. Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ, "Woawie anaa?" Ɔbea panyin no buae sɛ, "Yiw, Nana". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ, "Bra na kɔbɔ fufu no". Na ɔbaa panyin no kɔdaa ne akyi, na ɔkae sɛ, "Kɔboaboa yams a wɔasi no ano, hwie gu me moma mu, na kɔbɔ no". Abofra no kae sɛ, "Hwɛ biribi a ɛyɛ nwonwa wɔ ha, sɛ yɛbobom fufu wɔ obi moma mu; eyi ne nea maame kaa ho asɛm". Ɔkaa eyi nyinaa wɔ ne tirim. Ɔkɔɔ na ɔbobɔɔ, nanso bere a ɔbobɔɔ no no, ɔbobɔɔ no brɛoo. Ɔbea no kae sɛ, "Bobɔ no brɛoo, sɛ wombobɔ no brɛoo a, wo kokoram bɛpae". Na ɔbobɔɔ no, pi! pi! pi! Na bere a owiee no, ɔhwiee sapɔw no guu so, na ɔde too hɔ wɔ ɔbaa no anim. Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Me babea, kae me din, na kɔ na didi". Na ɔkae sɛ: "Wɔfrɛ wo Ɔbaabun Ɔbaabun". Na ɔkae sɛ: "Ɔbaabun no anhu, na wantumi anidi". Na ɔbaa panyin no dii nyinaa, na ɔyɛɛ no saa nnawɔtwe; na da koro abofra no kɔɔ abura no ho sɛ ɔrekohwie nsu. Kram no huu no, na obisaa no sɛ, "Adɛn nti na obi ba no ayɛ basaa saa?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ná me ne ɔbaa nanabea bi te, na sɛ mehyew aduan ma no a, ɔka sɛ, 'Ka me din na kɔdidi'; meka sɛ, 'Wɔfrɛ wo nanabaa nanabea,' na ɔka sɛ, 'Ɛyɛ! Ɔkaa sɛ: "Ɔbaa no din de Ɔbaa a Ɔyɛ Ɔpanyin-Mosono". Na abofra no kae sɛ: "Ho". Na ɔde ne nsu kuruwa no too ne ti so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mma wo werɛ mmfi bere a worekɔ". Abofra no fii hɔ; bere nyinaa a ɔreba no, na ɔtoa so ka sɛ, "Ɔbea a ne ho akokwaw-Mosono, Mosono, Mosono". Bere a okoduu kurow no hyeɛ so no, ne werɛ fii, na ɔhintaw, na ne nsubo no bubuu, na ɔkɔɔ fie. Ɔbea panyin no bisae sɛ: "Nsu kuruwa no wɔ he?" Ɔbea panyin no buae sɛ: "Bere a mereba no, mihintiw, na ewiee". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Fa foforo kɔ". Na ɔfaa no kɔe; na ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ: "Woasan aba dedaw?" Na ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ: "Saa din no a woka kyerɛɛ me no, makɔ, na me werɛ afi, na maba sɛ merebisa no bio". Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ: "Wɔfrɛ no Ɔbaabun Mosono". Na ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ: "Ho". Ɔkɔɔ so kae saa bere no nyinaa. Ɔkɔɔ fie, na bere a ɔkɔɔ fie no, ɔtwee ne ho. Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Kɔ kɔtutu yams na kɔfa". Ɔyɛɛ sɛnea ɔyɛe kan no, na ɔde aduan no brɛɛ ɔbaa panyin no, na ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ: "Ka me din na didi". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Wɔfrɛ wo Ɔbea Panyin-Nanan". Ɔkae sɛ: "Mese me din ankasa". Ɔkae sɛ: "Wo din ankasa?" Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Aane". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Wɔfrɛ wo Ɔbea Panyin-Mosono". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Wɔfrɛ wo Ɔbea Panyin-Mosono". Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Fa na didi". Na abofra no faa na odidii. Na ɔbaa no fii adi kɔɔ ɔkwan no so, na ɔkae sɛ: "Merekɔhwehwɛ onipa a ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ me din de Mosono no". Na ɔkɔɔ asubɔnten no ho, na ɔhyiaa Obonto Ya, minnow no (na ɔtoo no mmonnaa, kae sɛ): "Wo 'Bonto, wo maame! wo 'Bonto, wo papa! Woka kyerɛɛ me nananom sɛ wɔfrɛ me Mosono?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ me". Ɔbaa no huu sɛ Adwen' (apataa no) gyina nkyɛn baabi; ɔdomee no kae sɛ, "Wo Adwen, wo maame! wo maame! wo maame! wo Adwen, wo papa! wo papa! wo papa! woaka akyerɛ me nananom sɛ wɔfrɛ me Mosono?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ me". Ohui sɛ Crab no gyina hɔ. Ɔdomee no kae sɛ: "Wo Krab, wo maame! wo maame! wo maame! wo Krab, wo papa! wo papa! wo papa! Wo na woka kyerɛɛ me nananom sɛ mete Accra, meyɛ ahemmanni, na wɔfrɛ me Mosono?" Crab no kae sɛ, "Yiw, ɛyɛ me na meka kyerɛɛ no, na adɛn nti na woma obi ba nyaa ahoɔden saa?" Bere tenten bi a atwam no, na nkotodwe biara nni akyi no. Na Nkotodwe no nso faa dɔte fitaa, na ɔtow guu ɔbea panyin no ti so. Ɛno nti na tete nkotodwe no nhwi fitaa wɔ wɔn ti so.

Eyi ne me ho asɛm a maka akyerɛ mo no. Sɛ ɛyɛ fɛ anaa ɛnyɛ fɛ a, mubetumi agye emu bi atom sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no nso, mobɛkamfo me.
