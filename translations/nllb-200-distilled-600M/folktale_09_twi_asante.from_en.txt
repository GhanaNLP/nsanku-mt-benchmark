ASƐM A ƐDA SO OKOTO, KRAB no nyaa nhina, ne ABEREWA, Ɔbaa a Ɔyɛ Ɔpanyin no nyaa nhwi fitaa

Ɔbea bi woo ne ba. Bere a ɔsomaa no no, ɔkae sɛ: "Merenkɔ". Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Montena hɔ, wo ne w'akwan". Bere biara a ɔsomaa no no, ɔkae sɛ: "Merenkɔ". Na ɔkae sɛ: "Kɔ na kɔbɔ nsu na kɔfa bi brɛ me". Ɔkae sɛ: "Merenkɔ". Na ɔbaa no bɔɔ no mmonnaa kae sɛ: "Twɛn na wubehu". Na abofra no kasae sɛ: "Merekɔhwehwɛ 'Merekɔhwɛ' sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mɛhwɛ". Iridi, yiridi, yiridi. Awiei koraa no, oduu akuraa bi ase, na ohui sɛ ɔbaa panyin bi te hɔ. Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Me ba, nsesa w'ani, na mahu wo". Na abofra no kɔɔ n'anim, na ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ: "Adɛn nti na nsu a efi w'aniwa mu agu wo saa?" Ɔkae sɛ: "Me maame ne me tenaa faako da biara a ɔsɔre no, ɔkae sɛ: 'Twɛn na wubehu,' na ɔbɔɔ me, na mekae sɛ: 'Mɛba makɔhwehwɛ, na mahu sɛnea ɛte.' Na ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ: 'Ɛyɛ me.' Ɛnna ɔkae sɛ: 'Kɔ fie a ɛwɔ hɔ no akyi, na kɔto nnua, na kɔfa wɔn; na sɛ wokɔ hɔ a, nea ɔbɛka sɛ: 'Monka me no bɛka me ho.' Ɔbea no buae sɛ: "Ho!" Ampa ara, ɔkɔɔ hɔ; yams no bi ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Hwe me, hwe me, hwe me", na afoforo nso kae sɛ: "Hwe me, nhwe me, nhwe me, nhwe me". Ɔbea no kae sɛ: "Monkɔ kurow no akyi nkɔhwɛ ɔdan bi a nam da so, na monhwɛ nea mo ani gye ho". Na abofra no faa bi, na ɔbaa hɔ de guu ogya no so, na ɔyɛɛ apontow, na wiei no, ɔde maa ɔbaa no. Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ, "Woawie anaa?" Ɔbea panyin no buae sɛ, "Yiw, Nana". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ, "Bra na kɔbɔ fufu no". Na ɔbaa panyin no kɔdaa ne akyi, na ɔkae sɛ, "Hwɛ, kɔboaboa yams a wɔasi no ano no ano, na kɔbɔ no". Abofra no kae sɛ, "Hwɛ, ɛyɛ nwonwa sɛ yɛbobom fufu wɔ obi hwene mu; eyi ne nea na maame reka ho asɛm". Eyi nyinaa no, ɔkaa wɔ ne tirim. Ɔbaa hɔ na ɔbobom, nanso bere a ɔbobom no, ɔbɔɔ no yiye. Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ, "Bobɔ no den, sɛ wonto no den a, wo koko bɛpae". Na ɔbobom no, pi! pi! pi! pi! Na bere a owiee no, ɔhwiee apiti no guu apiti no so, na ɔde no too ɔpanyin no anim. Na ɔpanyin no kae sɛ: Me ba, ka me din ho asɛm, na kɔdidi. Na ɔkae sɛ: Wɔfrɛ wo Ɔpanyin Nana. Na ɔkae sɛ: Yiw, onhuu, na wantumi anidi. Na ɔpanyin no didii ne nyinaa, na ɔyɛɛ no saa daa no nnawɔtwe. Na da koro abofra no kɔɔ asu no ho sɛ ɔrekɔsaw nsu. Kɔnkɔn no huu no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Adɛn nti na obi ba no ahwe ase saa?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ná Ɔbaabun-Abea bi wɔ hɔ a me ne no te, na sɛ mepɔn aduan ma no a, ɔka sɛ, 'Ka me din na kɔdidi'; meka sɛ, 'Wɔfrɛ wo Ɔbaabun-Abea-Abea,' na ɔka sɛ, 'Ɛyɛ! woanhu ɔkwan a wobɛfa so adi,' na afei odi; ɛno nti na me ho yɛ hare". Kɔnkɔn no kae sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ no Ɔbaabun-Mosono". Ɔba no kae sɛ, "Ho." Na ɔde nsubo no too ne ti so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mma wo werɛ mmfi bere a worekɔ". Abofra no fii hɔ; bere nyinaa a ɔreba no, na ɔtoa so ka sɛ, "Akansabea-Mosono, Mosono, Mosono". Bere a okoduu kurow no akyi no, ne werɛ fii, na ɔhwehwee, na ne nsu kuruwa no bubuu, na ɔkɔɔ fie. Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Nsu kuruwa no wɔ he?" Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Bere a mereba no, mihintiw, na ewiee". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Fa foforo kɔ". Na ɔfaa no kɔe; na Kamewa no kae sɛ: "Woasan aba dedaw?" Na ɔkae sɛ: "Saa din a woka kyerɛɛ me no, makɔ, na me werɛ afi, na maba sɛ merebisa no bio". Kamewa no kae sɛ: "Wɔfrɛ no Nkwakoraa Mosono". Na ɔkae sɛ: "Ho". Ɔkɔɔ so kae saa ɔkwan no so kosii fie, na bere a ɔbaa fie no, ɔde (kamewa no) too fam. Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Kɔ kɔtutu yams na kɔfa". Ɔyɛɛ sɛnea ɔyɛe kan no ara, na ɔde aduan no brɛɛ ɔbaa panyin no, na ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ: "Ka me din na didi". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Wɔfrɛ wo Ɔbea Panyin-Nanan". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Mese me din ankasa". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Wo din ankasa?" Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Yiw". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Wɔfrɛ wo Ɔbea Panyin-Mosono". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Fa na didi". Na abofra no gyee na odidii. Ɔbaa no fii adi kɔɔ ɔkwan no so, na ɔkae sɛ, "Merekɔhwehwɛ onipa a ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ me din de Mosono no". Na ɔkɔɔ asubɔnten no ho, na ohyiaa Obonto Ya, minnow no, (na ɔtoo no mmonnaa, kae sɛ), "Wo 'Bonto, wo maame! wo 'Bonto, wo papa! woaka akyerɛ me nananom sɛ wɔfrɛ me Mosono anaa?" Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ me". Ɔbaa no huu sɛ Adwen' (nsu) gyina nkyɛn baabi; ɔtwee no, kae sɛ, "Wo Adwen, wo maame! wo maame! wo maame! wo maame! wo Adwen, wo papa! wo papa! wo papa! Woka kyerɛɛ me nananom sɛ wɔfrɛ me Mosono?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ me". Ɔhunuu sɛ Crab no gyina hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo Crab, wo maame! wo maame! wo maame! wo Crab, wo papa! wo papa! wo papa! wo na woka kyerɛɛ me nananom sɛ mete Accra, meyɛ ahemman ba, na wɔfrɛ me Mosono?" Crab no kae sɛ, "Yiw, me na meka kyerɛɛ no, na adɛn nti na woma obi ba no nyinii saa?" Ɛno nti na Crab no wɔ nkotodwe wɔ n'akyi. Efi bere tenten a atwam no, na nkotodwe biara nni hɔ wɔ Crab no akyi. Na Crab no nso faa dɔte fitaa, na ɔtow guu ɔbea panyin no ti so. Ɛno nti na nkotodwe a ɛwɔ mmea mpanyimfo ti no atifi no wɔ nwi fitaa.

Eyi ne me ho asɛm a maka akyerɛ mo no. Sɛ ɛyɛ fɛ anaa ɛnyɛ fɛ a, ebia mobɛfa bi sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no nso, mobɛkamfo me.
