Nea Enti a Mpanyimfo Ka Sɛ Ɛnsɛ sɛ Yɛma Yɛn Ani Gye Nna Ho Bio

WƆKA sɛ (bere bi) Nyankonpon Kwame, ɔsoro-nyame no, sɛee afuw kɛse bi, na ɔtetee okros, nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn, aburoo, turom, abɔnsam. Ɛno akyi no, ɔsoro-nyame no maa gong-gong kae sɛ afifide a ɔyɛe no ayɛ nwura bɔne, na obiara a obetumi afifide no a ɔrenka ne ho no betumi aba abɛfa ne babea Abena Nkroma (nea ɛkyerɛ sɛ Abena ne ba a ɔto so akron) aware. Saa bere no ara nso na obi a ɔde n'adwene nyinaa si n'ani so sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛbɔ. Esiane sɛ Spider no nim saa nokwasɛm yi nti, da biara Fida na ɔkɔ kɔpam nwura bɔne no. Efisɛ bere a na ɔretwe ne ho, na nnipa a wɔretwam no taa kyia no sɛ, Hello to you at your work, Father Spider!, na ɔtaa bua sɛ, Meda wo ase, Aku. Wɔtoaa so sɛ, A plantation, a obiara ntumi ntew ho, (wopɛ sɛ woka sɛ) woreyɛ no nwura? Spider no buae sɛ, Ah, ɛyɛ ɔbea biako nti na mehyɛ me ho so saa. Ne nsa koro no ara te sɛ eyi". Na ɔbobɔɔ ne nsa na ɔhwehwɛɛ baabi a na ɛreyɛ no, na bere a ɔyɛɛ saa no, na obenya ahogono afi abufuw no mu. Obi foforo bɛsan atwa ne ho ahyia aka no wɔ n'adwuma mu; na na ɔbobɔɔ ne nsa na ɔbobɔɔ baabi a na ɛreyɛ no. Sɛ nhwɛso no, sɛ n'afurum no ba a, na ɔbɛka sɛ: "Ɔbea a onni kunu no, wɔka sɛ n'afurum no te sɛ eyi" (sɛ wɔbobɔ n'afurum na wɔhwehwe ne ho). Ɔsoro-nyame bisaa ɔsomafoɔ no sɛ, "Enti wawie?" Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Aane". Ɔsoro-nyame no bisaa no sɛ, "Wopɛ sɛ ɔtwe ne ho anaa?" Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Dabi, ɔmpɛ sɛ ɔtwe ne ho". Ɔsoro-nyame no faa Abena Nkroma de no maa Ananse sɛ awareɛ. Ananse ne ne yere kɔɔ fie anadwo bi, na ɔbaa no bisaa no sɛ: Ɛyɛɛ dɛn na wutumi tew me papa afuw no ho, afuw bi a obiara a ɔkɔ so kɔduruu so no dan n'akyi, na mo nso motumi tew no ho? Mekaa sɛ, 'Ananse, woreyi saa afuw yi afi hɔ a obiara ntumi nyi ho mfi hɔ da?' Ɛno akyi no, mede me nsa kaa baabiara wɔ m'atifi a na ɛrebɔ me, na mesɛee no, na meka kyerɛɛ onipa no sɛ, sɛ nhwɛso no, wo nante te sɛ afurum nante, na na ɛyɛ fɛ na ɛyɛ fɛ. Ɛno na ɛma mitumi twaa no". Ɛno akyi no, Abena, abofra a ɔto so akron no kae sɛ, "Afei ɔkyena mɛka akyerɛ papa sɛ woahwere wo ho". Spider no ne no kasae, kae sɛ, "Ɛnsɛ sɛ woka ho asɛm, eyi yɛ nnaa-abrabɔ ho ahotoso". Abena, abofra a ɔto so akron no kae sɛ, "Minnim biribiara wɔ nnaa-abrabɔ ho, na mɛka". Abena Nkroma faa ne nsa. Afei Ananse ani yɛɛ kɔkɔɔ (a ɛkyerɛ awerɛhow), na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔfaa ne nnwom bow, na ɔbobɔɔ nnwom no too dwom sɛ:

"Abena, ɔba a ɔto so akron no, eyi nyɛ asɛm a ɛsɛ sɛ yɛne no ko.

Ma yensusuw ho sɛ obi a ɔde ne ho to no so.

Ɔka sɛ: 'Dabi.

Ɔwɔ asɛm bi wɔ ne ho, na obi foforo nso wɔ asɛm bi wɔ ne ho a ɔda so ara nam kwan so".

Afei Spider no sɔre kɔdaa hɔ. Bere a Ananse da hɔ kakra no, ɔsɔre. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abena Nkroma" ɛnne biara nni hɔ (sɛnea cicada no bɔ no nko ara) adidi! Ananse kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka wo". Ɔfaa kuruwa kuruwa ketewa bi na ɔde nsu buu so, na ohwiee no guu Abena Nkroma mpaboa so. Afei Ananse kɔdaa hɔ. Bere a ɔdaa hɔ kakra no, ɔkae sɛ: "O Abena Nkroma, ɛdeɛn na eyi yɛ, wo a woabɔ ahohora, woayɛ adepa biara; na sɛ nneɛma kɔ yiye a, mɛka makyerɛ obiara, na sɛ obi kɔ afuw no mu a, ɔka sɛ: 'Ɔbabaa a ɔhyew! Afei Abena ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Mesrɛ wo, gyae, na ma asɛm no nkɔ". Spider no kae sɛ: "Merennyae, me asɛm no na edi kan bae; wokae sɛ wobɛka akyerɛ w'agya; mekae sɛ, 'Gyae'; wokae sɛ, 'Dabi.' Ɛno nti merennyae asɛm no". Ok, mɛtow kyene; efisɛ sɛ wunnyaw me de a, m'ani bewu wɔ aniwu mu. Afei Ananse kae sɛ, Metee sɛ, sɛ wopɛ saa a, ma ɛnyɛ nna mu awerɛhyem, enti asɛm no wie wɔ hɔ. Eyi nti na mpanyimfo no kae sɛ Deda mu awerɛhyem ntwentwɛn wɔn nan ase.

Eyi ne me ho asɛm a maka akyerɛ mo no. Sɛ ɛyɛ fɛ anaa ɛnyɛ fɛ a, mubetumi agye emu bi atom sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no nso, mobɛkamfo me.
