ASƐM A ƐDA SO ASƐM A ƐDA SO Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no, Ɛyɛɛ Dɛn na Wɔbɛfrɛɛ Wɔn "Afefefɛsɛm"

KWAKU ANANSE, Spider no, kɔɔ Nyankonpon, ɔsoro nyame no nkyɛn bere bi sɛ ɔrekɔtɔ ɔsoro nyame no ho nsɛm. Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ: "Wubetumi atɔ wɔn anaa?" Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ: "Ɛnte saa". Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ: "Asase akɛse ne ahoɔdenfo te sɛ Kokofu, Bekwai, Asumengya aba, nanso wɔantumi ankɔtɔ wɔn, na wo a woyɛ onipa a onni tumi no, wobɛtumi". Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ: "Ɛdeɛn ne [nsɛm no] bo?" Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ: "Wontumi ntɔ wɔn, gye Onini aboa a wɔfrɛ no Python; Osebo, Leopard; Mmoatia, Fairy; Mmoboro, Hornets". Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ: "Mede eyinom nyinaa bɛbrɛ me, na (deɛn na ɛsen saa) mɛma me dedaw no aka ho". Ɔbea, Nsia (Ɔba a Ɔtɔ so Nsia), kɔ nkonimbo no mu". Ɔsorosoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔfa wɔn mmra". Spider no bae, na ɔkaa ne maame ho asɛm nyinaa kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ metɔ soro-nyame no ho nsɛm, na Sky-nyame no ka sɛ memfa Onini, Python no mmra; Osebo, Leopard no; Mmoatia, Fairy; ne Mmoboro, Hornets; na mekae sɛ mede wo bɛkɔ nkonimbo no mu na memfa no mma Sky-nyame no". Afei Spider no bisaa ne yere Aso sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ na ama yɛanya Onini, Python no?" Na Aso ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔtwa nnua dua no nhama, na kɔtwa srade ntɛtɛw bi nso, na kɔfa wɔn". Na Ananse faa wɔn, na bere a ɔrekɔ no ɔkae sɛ: "Ɛyɛ tenten sen no, ɛnyɛ tenten sen no; wudi atoro, ɛyɛ tenten sen no". Spider no kae sɛ: "Ɔwɔ hɔ, ɔda hɔ". Python no (a na wate saa nkɔmmɔbɔ yi) kae sɛ: "Ɛdeɛn na ɛrekɔ so?" Spider no kae sɛ: "Ɛnyɛ (me yere) Aso na ɔne me rekasa sɛ dua yi yɛ tenten sen wo, na meka sɛ ɔyɛ ɔtorofo". Na Onini, Python no kae sɛ: "Fa no, na bra na susuw me". Ɔfaa dua no de kaa Python no ho. Ɔka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ: "Trɛw wo ho". Na abɔnsam no trɛw ne ho, na Ananse faa ahama no na ɔtwee ne ho. (Na ahama no kasae yiye kosii sɛ ɔbaa ne ti so.) Ananse, Spider no kae sɛ: "Ɔkwasea, mɛfa wo akɔ ɔsoro nyame nkyɛn na matie ɔsoro nyame no nsɛm". Ananse de no kɔɔ Nyame (Ɔsoro nyame) hɔ, na ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ: "M'ankasa me nsa aka. Enti ɔbaa no kae sɛ: "Hwehwɛ dua bi, na fa nsu hyɛ mu, na wo ne no nkɔ". Enti ɔbaa no faa sare no so, na ohui sɛ nnua pii reteetee hɔ, na ɔhwiee nsu no bi guu nnua no so, na ɔhwiee nea aka no guu nnua no so, na ɔhwiee nea aka no guu nnua no so. Afei ɔhwiee nea aka no guu n'ankasa so, na ɔtwee dua no bi de kataa ne ti so. Na afei ɔka kyerɛɛ nnomaa no sɛ: Sɛ osu no ba a, anka monkɔ mu, me kukuw, na nsu no mmɛbɔ mo; monhu sɛ mefaa dua dua dua bi sɛ mede bɛkata me ho? Na nnomaa no kae sɛ: Yɛda wo ase Aku, yɛda wo ase Aku. Na nnomaa no nyinaa tu yerae wɔ kukuw no mu, na hwɛ! agya Spider kataa n'ano, na ɔkae sɛ: Mo nkwaseafo, mewɔ mo, na mede mo kɔ ɔsoro nyame no nsɛm mu. Na ɔde nnomaa no kɔɔ ɔsoro nyame no nkyɛn. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ: "Me nsa aka, nea aka no (da so ara) wɔ hɔ". Spider no san bae, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere no, na ɔkae sɛ: "Osebo, Leopard no da so wɔ hɔ". Aso kae sɛ: "Kɔ na kɔtutu ɔbodan". Ananse kae sɛ: "Ɛno ara dɔɔso, mete ase". Afei Spider no fii ase hwehwɛɛ Leopard no agyapade no, na (ɔhunuu wɔn) ɔtwee ɔbodan kɛse bi, na ɔkataa so, na ɔsan baa fie. Ade kyee no, bere a nneɛma fii ase yerae no, Spider no kae sɛ obetu, na bere a ɔkɔe no, Leopard bi da ɔbodan no mu. Ananse kae sɛ: "Agya ba ketewa, ɛna ba ketewa, meka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ mma woannom nsa, na afei, sɛnea obi bɛhwɛ kwan afi wo hɔ no, woabobow, na ɛno nti na woahwe ase agu amoa mu; sɛ meka sɛ mɛma woafi adi a, da a edi hɔ no, sɛ wuhu me anaa me mma no bi a, wobɛkɔ akɔkyere wɔn". Ananse kae sɛ: "O! mintumi nyɛ saa ade no". Ananse kɔe kɔtwee nnua abien, de biako sii ha, na biako nso sii hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ: "Fa wo nan mu biako si ha, na biako nso si ha". Na Ananse de wɔn too hɔ ( baabi a wɔka kyerɛɛ no no). Bere a na ɔrebɛforo no, Ananse ma ne mpire no so, na prɛko pɛ na ɛbaa ne ti so, gao! (na ɛyɛ nne a na ɛrebɔ no). Amoa no gyee Leopard (na) fom! (na ɛyɛ ne hwehwee nne no). Ananse nyaa asennua sɛ ɔmmra amoa no mu nkɔfa Leopard no. Ɔfaa Leopard no fii hɔ na ɔde bae; ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔkwasea, mede wo rebɛhwehwɛ ɔsoro-nyame no ho nsɛm". Ɔma Leopard no so kɔmaa Nyame, ɔsoro-nyame no. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ: "Me nsa aka, nea aka no da so ara wɔ hɔ". Spider no bae, na ɔtwee Akua ba (dua dua bi a ne ti yɛ tuntum a ne ti yɛ fɛ), na ɔtwee latex bi a ɛkyekyere (fi dua bi mu) na ɔde kaa abodin no nipadua ho, na ɔbobɔɔ eto (mashed yams), na ɔde bi kɔtoo abodin no nsa mu, na ɔbobɔɔ bi kaa ho, na ɔde bi kɔtoo kɔbere kotoku mu; ɔkyekyeree abodin no kɔn mu, na ɔne no kɔe, na ɔde no too odum dua ase, baabi a Fairifo ba bɛdii agorɔ no. Na Fairi bi bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, Akua, mɛtumi adidi kakra afiri yi mu? Ananse twee ntadeɛ no, na abeawa no bɔɔ ne ti. Fawa no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabaa no mu baako sɛ, Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Ɔka sɛ mɛtumi adidi kakra. Ɔkaa sɛ, Didi kakra, na afei. Na ɔwiee adidi no, na ɔdaa no ase. Nanso bere a ɔdaa no ase no, wannyae. Na Fawa no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabaa no sɛ, Sɛ meda no ase a, ɔmmae. Fawa no nuabaa no kae sɛ, Popa ne suiɛ-bea. Na ɔbobɔɔ no, papa! Na ne nsa da hɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea sɛ, Me nsa aka hɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Fa nea aka no na kɔkyekyere ne suibea no bio. Na ɔfaa no, na ɔkyeree no, papa! na eyi nso, ɔkyeree no ntɛmntɛm. Na Fairy no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea sɛ, Me nsa abien no aka ntɛmntɛm. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Push it with your stomach. Ɔkyeree no, na ne yafunu aka ho. Na Ananse bae bɛkyekyeree no, na ɔkae sɛ, Fool, me nsa aka wo, mɛfa wo akɔ Ɔsoro-nyame no nkyɛn (sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ ne nsɛm no ananmu). Na ɔne no siim kɔɔ fie. Ananse ka kyerɛɛ ne maame Ya Nsia (ɔba a ɔto so asia) sɛ: "Sɔre, momma yɛnkɔ, na mede wo ne Nkɔmhyɛni no bɛkɔ akɔma Ɔsorosoro nyame (sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obegye n'anansesɛm no). Ɔfaa wɔn, na ofii hɔ kɔɔ baabi a Ɔsorosoro nyame no wɔ no. Ɔkae sɛ: "Ɔsorosoro nyame, hwɛ Nkɔmhyɛni bi wɔ ha, na m'aberewa a mekaa ne ho asɛm no nso wɔ ha". Na ɔde asɛm no too wɔn anim sɛ: Ahemfo akɛse pii aba, na wɔantumi annya ɔsoro nyame no ho nsɛm, nanso Kwaku Ananse, Spider no, atumi atua ne bo; minyaa ne nsam Mmoboro, Hornets; minyaa ne nsam Mmoatia, Fairy; minyaa ne nsam Osebo, Leopard; minyaa ne nsam Onini, Python; na ne pɛ mu, Ananse de ne maame aka agyapade no ho; eyinom nyinaa wɔ ha. Ɔkae sɛ: to ne ayeyi nnwom. Eee! (wɔn nyinaa teɛteɛɛm). Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Kwaku Ananse, efi nnɛ ne daa nyinaa, mefa m'asoro-nyame no ho nsɛm na mede ma wo, kose! kose! kose! me nhyira, nhyira, nhyira.

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
