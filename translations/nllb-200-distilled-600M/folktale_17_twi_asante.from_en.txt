ASƐM A ƐDA SO ASƐM A ƐDA SO Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no, Ɛyɛɛ Dɛn na Wɔbɛfrɛɛ Wɔn "Afefefɛsɛm"

KWAKU ANANSE, Spider no, kɔɔ Nyankonpon, ɔsoro nyame no nkyɛn bere bi sɛ ɔrekɔtɔ ɔsoro nyame no ho nsɛm. Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn kae sɛ: Wubetumi atɔ wɔn anaa? Nhwiren no kae sɛ: Dabi, mɛtumi. Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn kae sɛ: Nkoa akɛse a wɔwɔ ahoɔden te sɛ Kokofu ne Bekwai ne Asumengya aba, nanso wɔantumi atɔ wɔn, na wo a woyɛ onipa a onni tumi no, wobɛtumi atɔ wɔn anaa? Nhwiren no kae sɛ: Dɛn ne (asɛm no) akatua? Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn kae sɛ: Wɔrentumi ntɔ wɔn hwee. gye Onini abɔde a wɔfrɛ no Python; Osebo a ɔyɛ Leopard; Mmoatia a ɔyɛ Fee; (ne) Mmoboro a ɔyɛ Hornets no". Spider no kae sɛ, "Mede nneɛma yi nyinaa bi bɛbrɛ me, na (ne nea ɛkyɛn saa) mɛma me yere Nsia (Ɔba a ɔto so asia no) aba". Sky-nyame no kae sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔfa wɔn". Spider no bae, na ɔkaa ne maame ho asɛm nyinaa kyerɛɛ no, kae sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ metɔ soro-nyame, ne Sk-nyame no ho nsɛm". Y-nyame se ɛsɛ sɛ mede Onini, Python no; Osebo, Leopard no; Mmoatia, Fairy no; ne Mmoboro, Hornets no; na mekae sɛ mede wo bɛkɔ agyapade no mu na mede ama Sky-nyame no. Afei Spider no kɔɔ ne yere Aso nkyɛn kɔbisaa no sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ na ama yɛanya Onini, Python no?" Spider no ne wɔn san bae. Na Aso kae sɛ, "Fa wɔn kɔ asuten no so". Na Ananse faa wɔn; bere a na ɔrekɔ no ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ tenten sen no, ɛnyɛ tenten sɛ no; woadi atoro, ɛyɛ tenten sen no". Spider kae sɛ, "Ɔwɔ hɔ, ɔda hɔ". Python (nea na wate saa nkɔmmɔbɔ yi ho asɛm) kae sɛ, "Dɛn ho asɛm na ɛwom?" Spider kae sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ (me yere) Aso na ɔne me rekasa sɛ nnuaba yi yɛ me dea? Na Onini, abusuakuw no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Fa ma me na susuw me". Na ɔfaa dua no de too abusuakuw no mu, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Trɛw wo ho". Na abusuakuw no trɛw ne ho, na Ananse faa hama no na ɔtwee no. Na wɔkyekyeree no too ne ti so. Ananse, Spider no kae sɛ: "Ɔkwasea, mɛfa wo akɔ ɔsoro nyame no nkyɛn na magye ɔsoro nyame no ho anansesɛm". Ananse de no kɔɔ Nyame (Ɔsoro nyame no) hɔ. Na ɔbaa no kae sɛ: "Hwehwɛ dua bi, na hyɛ no nsu, na wo ne no nkɔ". Na ɔbaa no faa sare no so, na ohui sɛ abɔnsam pii reteetee hɔ, na ɔhwiee nsu no bi guu abɔnsam no so. Afei Spider no hwiee nea aka no guu ne ho so, na ɔtwee dua no ho krataa de kataa ne ti so. Na afei ɔka kyerɛɛ nnomaa no sɛ: "Sɛ osu no reba a, anka monkɔ mu, me kurobɔ, na osu no mmɛbɔ mo; monhu sɛ mefaa nnomaa dua bi sɛ mede bɛkata me ho?" Na nnomaa no kae sɛ: "Yɛda wo ase, Aku, yɛda wo ase". Agya Spider kataa n'ano, na ɔkae sɛ: "Akwasea, mewɔ mo, na mede mo kɔfa ɔsoro nyame no ho anansesɛm". Na ɔde Apɔnkɔ no kɔɔ ɔsoro nyame no nkyɛn. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ: "Me nsa aka, nea aka no (da so ara) wɔ hɔ". Spider no san bae, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere, na ɔkae sɛ: "Osebo, Leopard no da so wɔ hɔ". Aso kae sɛ: "Kɔ kɔtutu ɔbodan". Ananse kae sɛ: "Ɛno ara dɔɔso, mete ase". Ade kyee no, bere a nneɛma fii ase yerae no, Spider no kae sɛ ɔrebɛbɔ, na bere a ɔkɔe no, Leopard bi da amoa no mu. Ananse kae sɛ: "Agya ba ketewa, ɛna ba ketewa, meka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ mma wunnwe nsa, na afei, sɛnea obi betumi ahwɛ kwan sɛ wobɛnom wo no, woabobow, na ɛno nti na woahwe ase akɔ amoa no mu; sɛ meka sɛ mɛma woapue a, da a edi hɔ no, sɛ wuhu me anaa me mma no bi a, wobɛkɔ akyere wɔn". Leopard no kae sɛ: "O! Ananse kɔtwee nnua mmienu de baako sii ha ne baako sii hɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Fa wo nan mu baako si ha, na wo nan mu baako nso si ha". Na Leopard de no too hɔ ( baabi a wɔka kyerɛɛ no no). Bere a ɔrebɛforo no, Ananse maa n'afurum no so, na prɛko pɛ na ɛbaa ne ti so, gao! (sɛnea na ɛteɛ). Ananse twaa nnyinaso sɛ ɔrekɔfa Leopard no. Ɔde Leopard no bae, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɔkwasea, mede wo rekɔbɔ ɔsoro-nyame no ho amanneɛ". Ɔmaa Leopard no so kɔmaa Nyame, Ɔsoro-nyame no. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ: "Me nsa aka, nea aka no da so ara wɔ hɔ". Spider no bae, na ɔtwee Akua ba (dua dua bi a aniwa yɛ fitaa), na ɔtwee latex bi a ɛkyekyere (fi dua bi mu) na ɔde kaa abodin no nipadua ho, na ɔbobɔɔ eto (mashed yams), na ɔde bi hyɛɛ abodin no nsa, na ɔbobɔɔ pii de kaa no kɔkɔɔ kotoku mu; ɔkyekyeree abodin no kɔn mu, Ɔkɔɔ ne nkyɛn de no kɔtoo odum dua bi ase, baabi a Nkɔmhyɛfo ba bɛdii agorɔ no. Na Nkɔmhyɛni bi bae. Ɔkae sɛ: Akua, so metumi adi mprɛte yi bi? Ananse twee hama no, na ne babea no kekaa ne ti. Nkɔmhyɛni no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea biako sɛ: Ɔka sɛ metumi adi bi. Ɔkae sɛ: Didi bi, afei. Na ɔwiee aduan no di, na ɔdaa no ase. Nanso bere a ɔdaa no ase no, wammua no. Na Ɔbɔfo no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no sɛ, Sɛ meda no ase a, ɔmmua no. Ɔbɔfo no nuabea ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Popa ne suibea no. Na ɔpopae no, pa! Na ne nsa da hɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no sɛ, Me nsa da hɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Fa nea aka no na kɔbɔ no suibea no bio. Na ɔfaa no, na ɔpopae no, pa! Afei Ananse baa ne nkyɛn bɛkyekyeree no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɔkwasea, manya wo, na mɛfa wo akɔ ɔsoro nyame nkyɛn". Na ɔne no kɔɔ fie. Na Ananse ka kyerɛɛ ne maame, Ya Nsia (ɔba a ɔto so asia no), sɛ, "Sɔre, momma yɛnkɔ, na mede wo ne Nkɔmhyɛni no bɛkɔ akɔma Ɔsorosoro nyame (sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛma n'anansesɛm)". Ɔfaa wɔn, na ofii hɔ kɔɔ baabi a Ɔsorosoro nyame wɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Sky-god, hwɛ Fairy, na m'awuraa a maka ne ho asɛm no nso wɔ ha. Afei Sky-god frɛɛ (ne mpanimfoɔ), Kontire ne Akwam mpanyimfo, Adonten (asraafo no akannifo), Gyase (major-domo); Oyoko, Ankobea, ne Kyidom (ahennifo no kannifo). Na ɔde asɛm no too wɔn anim sɛ: Ahene akɛse pii aba, na wɔantumi annya ɔsoro nyame no ho nsɛm, na Kwaku Ananse, Spider no de, watumi atua ne bo; me nsa aka mmoboro, Hornets no; me nsa aka Mmoatia, Fae no; me nsa aka Osebo, Leopard no; me nsa aka Onini, Python no; na n'ankasa pɛ mu, Ananse de kaa ho sɛ: Ɔkaa sɛ, "Munyi n'ayeyi nnwom". (wɔn teɛteɛɛm). Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse, efi nnɛ rekɔ daa nyinaa, mɛfa m'asoro-nyame ho nsɛm na mede makyerɛ wo, kose! kose! kose! kose! m'aseda, nhyira, nhyira. Yɛrenfrɛ wɔn Ɔsoro-nyame ho nsɛm bio, na mmom yɛbɛfrɛ wɔn Spider-nsɛm.

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
