Nea yɛreka yi nyɛ nokware.

ASƐM A ƐDA SO Sɛnea Abusuakuw no Bɔɔ Nkɔmmɔ

WƆKA sɛ odwotwafo bi kɔɔ wuram bere bi; ɔbaa hɔ na ɔhwɛɛ amoa bi mu, na na Nipa, Onipa, Osebo, Leopard, Owo, Ɔwɔ, ne Kusie, Mmoadabi no gyina hɔ wɔ mu. Afei bere a nnipa yi huu Hunter no, obi ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Father Hunter, bra bɛyi me. Saa obi no kae sɛ, Father, bra bɛyi me. Hunter no kae sɛ, Merenkɔ, ɛfiri sɛ ɔkyena mpo, sɛ mɛgye wo nkwa, na wobɛto me so a, deɛ wobɛyɛ ama me no, (minim) ɛnsɛ sɛ ɛhaw me. Saa obi no kae sɛ, Mentumi (yɛ saa). Saa obi no kae sɛ, Mentumi (yɛ saa). Hunter no kae sɛ, Mentumi (yɛ saa). Sɛ saa na ɛte a, mɛyi wo. Na nokwarem no, ɔfaa nnipa no nyinaa fii hɔ kɔɔ (ne) akuraa ase. Da bi, bere a Ɔpɔnkɔfo no wɔ hɔ no, Osebo, Leopard no de nam bebree brɛɛ no. Ɔkae sɛ, Father Hunter, eyi yɛ aseda afɔre a woayɛ ama me, meda wo ase, Apiafo. Ɔde nam nkotoku nkotoku dumien brɛɛ Ɔpɔnkɔfo no. Ɔhwefo no daa no ase, na Leopard no kɔe. Bere foforo bi nso, Ɔhwefo no wɔ hɔ, na Okusie a ɔyɛ Rat no bae. Obisaa no sɛ, Father Hunter, wopɛ sika kɔkɔɔ dɔte bi? Ɔhwefo no kae sɛ, Ho! adɛn nti na ɛnyɛ, ɛno ne ade a nnipa hwehwɛ a ɛbrɛ wɔn. Ɔhwefo no fii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a Nyankonpon, ɔsoro-nyame no de ne sika kɔkɔɔ kɔkɔɔ no too hɔ. Ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔtɔɔ ɔbodan bi wɔ ɔdan no ase, na ɔkɔɔ ɔdan no mu kɔfaa ɔsoro nyame sika mfuturo no nyinaa de brɛɛ Ɔpɔnkɔ no. Ɔpɔnkɔ no kae sɛ, Meda wo ase, Aku. Na Ɔpɔnkɔ no fii hɔ. Da foforo, na ɔwɔ hɔ, na ɔtee sɛ obi kyia no, Ago. Ɔpɔnkɔ no buae sɛ, Ame (ba mu). Ɔbarima no bae, na ɔkae sɛ, Father Hunter, good morning. Ɔ (Hunter) buae sɛ, Yiw, abusuani. Ɔkaa sɛ, Eyi yɛ nsã ketewaa bi a mede abrɛ wo ase. Father Hunter kae sɛ, Mempɛ; sɛ ɛfa wo ho a, Man, ɛte sɛ nea mekae sɛ ɛbɛyɛ, bere a mekae sɛ meremmfa wo; hwɛ nea Leopard de brɛɛ me, hwɛ nea Rat de brɛɛ me. Onipa no gyee ne nsã. Ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔbɔɔ ɔsoro nyame no amanneɛ sɛ, "Wo sika mfuturo a woahweree no, na Rat no aba, na ɔfaa no de maa Ɔpɔnkɔ no". Ɔpɔnkɔ no kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ saa anaa?" Ɔpɔnkɔ no kae sɛ, "Ɔpɔnkɔ, ɛyɛ nokware". Ɔpɔnkɔ no somaa abɔfo sɛ wɔnkɔkyere Ɔpɔnkɔ no, na wɔkyekyeree no wɔ ne kɔn mu, na wɔhwehwɛɛ no denneennen. Na bere a wɔbaa ɔkwan no so no, ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ: "Akannifo, mepɛ sɛ mekɔ na mesan me ho". Asomafoɔ no kae sɛ: "Dabi, ɔkorɔmfoɔ, wobɛdwane". Ɔkaa sɛ: "Dabi, merennwane, na sɛ mopɛ a, momfa hama no, momfa nhyɛ me abɔso mu, na monkyekyere no nnua so, na momma menkɔ". Asomafoɔ no tiee no, na wɔmaa no kɔe. Ɛhɔ na na ɔte, na ɔde ne ho kyekyee bere a aboa a ne ho yɛ hare no bae no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Hwɛ ha, me adamfo, gye nnuru yi, na fa no ka wo ho, na sɛ wokɔ na wɔgyina w'anim wɔ nhyiam no ase, na ɛba sɛ wobekum wo a, mɛkɔ Ɔhene Harem, na makɔka ne yere a ɔdɔ no no ba no ho; na sɛ makɔka ne ho a, wubehu sɛ obiara asɔre, na wo nso wobɛka, na wobɛka sɛ, 'Mewɔ nnuru,' na sɛ Ɔpɔnkɔ no kae sɛ, Meda wo ase, Aku. Asomafoɔ no faa Ɔpɔnkɔ no, na wɔkɔgyinaa Ɔpɔnkɔ no anim, (wɔ n'anim ne ne mpanimfoɔ) Kontire, Akwum, Adonten, Gyase, ne Kyidom mpanimfoɔ no anim. Ɔpɔnkɔ no kaa n'asɛm, na berɛ a Ɔpɔnkɔ no sɔre gyinaa hɔ sɛ ɔrebɛbu atɛn no, wɔtee nteɛteɛm bi, pui! pui! Wɔkɔɔ ahemfie hɔ wɔ akyirikyiri. Wɔbisaa no sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn ni?" Wɔbuaa no sɛ, "Ɔwɔ ate ɔsoro nyame no yere a ɔdɔ no no babarima". Na wɔn nyinaa tu kɔeɛ. Ná Ɔpɔnkɔfo no gyina hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ, Sɛ manyɛ ɔdeduani a, anka mɛtumi anya aduru. Obi tee ne nka, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, Kɔfa ɔdeduani no mmra ha. Afei Ɔpɔnkɔfo no bae. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Sɛ wutumi hwɛ eyi a, me ba, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔrenwu a, mede wo ti bɛma wo. Ɔpɔnkɔ no kae sɛ, Metee, Ɔpɔnkɔ. Na ɔpɔnkɔ no kae sɛ, Nananom, nea mɛkyerɛ wo no, sɛ wubetumi anya bi a, ɛnde mɛtumi ahwɛ wo ba no so. Wobisae sɛ, Dɛn ne saa? Ɔkae sɛ, Tell-tale mogya a wɔde bɛhyiaa ɔyaresafo a wɔapirapira no ine. Wobisae sɛ, Ɛhe na yebenya obi a ɔka ho asɛm no mogya? Ɔkasafo no mu baako a ɔyɛ onyansafo kae sɛ, Na saa ɔbarima no nso ɛ? Ɔkaa sɛ, Ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ ɔsoro-nyame sɛ Ɔhwefo no aba abɛwia ɔsoro-nyame no sika, na ɔwɔ he? Wɔkae sɛ, Wonnni mfomso, wonni mfomso. Wɔkɔɔ kɔfaa saa ɔbarima no bae. Ɔhwefo no kae sɛ, Kyekyere ne nsa wɔ ɔfã no ho, na fa ne mogya ma me. Ɛhɔ ara na wɔkyɛɛ no. Ɔhwefo no faa mogya no sɛ ɔde bɛtotow nnuru no, na ɔde guu ɔhene no ba no nan so. Ɛhɔ ara na ɔsɔre. Wɔka kyerɛɛ no se, "Monto awudifo no ayeyi nnwom". Wɔteɛɛm se, "Yiw!" Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no kae se, "Mede wo ti no bɛma wo, na nea ɛwɔ Anansesɛm no mu no, ma wonkunkum no, na wonkunkum ne honam, na wonkunkum no wɔ afanan nyinaa". Wɔkunkumm no, na wɔkekaa ne honam mu nketewa nketewa, na wɔkunkum no nyinaa. Saa ara na Anansesɛm no dɔɔso wɔ abusuakuw no mu.

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
