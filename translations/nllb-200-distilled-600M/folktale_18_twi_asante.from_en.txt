ASƐM A ƐDA SO Sɛnea Abusuakuw no Bɔɔ Nkɔmmɔ

WƆKA sɛ odwotwafo bi kɔɔ wuram bere bi; ɔbaa hɔ na ɔhwɛɛ amoa bi mu, na na Nipa, Onipa, Osebo, Leopard, Owo, Ɔwɔ, ne Kusie, Mmoadabi no gyina hɔ wɔ mu. Afei bere a nnipa yi huu Hunter no, obi ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Father Hunter, bra bɛyi me. Saa obi no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Father, bra bɛyi me. Hunter no kae sɛ, Merenkɔ, efisɛ ɔkyena mpo, sɛ miyi wo a, na ɛsɛ sɛ wokum me a, nea wobɛyɛ ama me (minim) a, memfa ho nhia me. Saa obi no kae sɛ, Mentumi (yɛ saa). Saa obi no kae sɛ, Mentumi (yɛ saa). Hunter no kae sɛ, Sɛ saa na ɛte a, mɛyi wo. Na nokwarem no, ɔfaa nnipa no nyinaa fii hɔ kɔɔ (ne) akuraa no ase. Da koro bi, bere a Ɔpɛnkɔfo no wɔ hɔ no, Osebo, Leopard no de nam bebree brɛɛ no. Ɔkae sɛ, Father Hunter, eyi yɛ aseda afɔre a woayɛ ama me, meda wo ase, Apiafo. Ɔde nam nkotoku dumien brɛɛ Ɔpɛnkɔfo no. Ɔpɛnkɔfo no daa no ase, na Leopard no kɔe. Bere foforo nso, Ɔpɛnkɔfo no wɔ hɔ, na Okusie, Agyenkwa no bae. Ɔkae sɛ, Father Hunter, wopɛ sika kɔkɔɔ? Ɔpɛnkɔfo no kae sɛ, Ho! dɛn nti na ɛnyɛ, ɛno ne ade a nnipa hwehwɛ a ɛbrɛ wɔn. Ɔpɛnkɔfo no fii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a Nyanpon, ɔsoro nyame-konkɔn, de ne sika kɔkɔɔ no siee. Ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔtɔɔ ɔbodan wɔ dan no ase kɔhyɛn dan no mu kɔfaa ɔsoro nyame sika mfuturo no nyinaa de brɛɛ Ɔpɔnkɔfo no. Ɔpɔnkɔfo no kae sɛ, Meda wo ase, Aku. Na Ɔpɔnkɔ no fii hɔ. Da foforo, na ɔwɔ hɔ, na ɔtee sɛ obi kyia no, Ago. Ɔpɔnkɔfo no buae sɛ, Ame (ba mu). Ɔbarima no bae, ɔkae sɛ, Father Hunter, good morning. Ɔ (Hunter) buae sɛ, Yes, kinsman. Ɔkaa sɛ, This is a small pot of palm-wine that I have brought to thank you. Father Hunter said, I don't want it; as for you, Man, it's just as I said it would be, when I declared I wouldn't take you out; hwɛ nea Leopard no de maa me, hwɛ nea Rat no de maa me. Ɔbarima no yii ne palm-wine no fii hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔsoro nyame no sɛ, Wo sika mfuturo a ɛyerae no, saa Rat no bae na ɔfaa no fii hɔ de maa Hunter. Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, Ɛyɛ saa ampa? Ɔkaa sɛ, Gran Onyame no kae sɛ, Ɔbɔfo no kae sɛ: "Dabi, ɔkorɔmfo, wubeguan". Ɔbɔfo no kae sɛ: "Dabi, merentumi nguan, na sɛ wopɛ a, fa hama no kyekyere me kɔn mu, kyekyere no dua so, na ma me kwan". Ɔkae sɛ, Hwɛ ha, me adamfo, gye aduru ketewaa yi, na fa no ka wo ho, na sɛ wokɔ a, na wogyina w'anim wɔ hyiadan no anim, na ɛba sɛ wobekum wo a, mɛkɔ Ɔhene no harem, na makɔka ne yere a ɔdɔ no no ba; sɛ mesɛ no a, wubehu sɛ obiara asɔre, na wo nso wobɛka, na wobɛka sɛ, Mewɔ aduru, na sɛ wode di abofra no so a, obenya ahoɔden. Ɔpɔnkɔfo no kae sɛ, Meda wo ase, Aku. Asomafoɔ no faa Ɔpɔnkɔfo no, na wɔkɔgyinaa ɔsoro nyame no anim, (n'anim ne ne mpanimfoɔ) Kontire, Akwum, Adonten, Gyase, ne Kyidom mpanyimfo no. Ɔpɔnkɔ no kaa n'asɛm, na bere a Ɔsɛnkafo no sɔre gyinae sɛ obebu atɛn no, wɔtee nteɛteɛm bi, pui! pui! akyirikyiri wɔ ahemman no mu. Wɔkae sɛ, Ɛdeɛn ni? Wɔkae sɛ, Ɔpɔnkɔ atɔ ɔsoro nyame no yere a ɔdɔ no no ba. Obiara fii hɔ kɔe. Afei Ɔpɔnkɔ no gyinaa hɔ; ɔkae sɛ, Sɛ manyɛ ɔdeduani a, anka mɛtumi anya aduru (aduro a ɛkyekyere no). Ɔbarima bi tee (him) na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, Kɔ na kɔfa ɔdeduani no mmra ha. Afei ɔpɔnkɔ no bae. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Sɛ wutumi hwɛ eyi a, me ba, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔrenwu a, mede wo ti bɛma wo. Ɔpɔnkɔ no kae sɛ, Mate, Ɔpɔnkɔ. Na Ɔpɔnkɔ no kae sɛ, Ɔpɔnkɔ, nea mɛkyerɛ wo, sɛ wubetumi anya bi a, ɛno na mɛtumi ahwɛ wo ba no so. Wobisae sɛ, Dɛn ne saa? Ɔpɔnkɔ no kae sɛ, Ɔpɔnkɔ no mogya ne nnuru a wɔapɛtɛw no mmɔ. Wobisae sɛ, Ɛhe na yebenya Ɔpɔnkɔ no mogya? Ɔpɔnkɔ no mu biako a na ɔyɛ onyansafo no kae sɛ, Ɔpɔnkɔ no ho asɛm na ɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔsoro-nyame no sɛ Ɔpɔnkɔ no aba abɛwia. Wɔka kyerɛɛ no se, "Kyekyere ne nsa na fa ne mogya ma me". Amonom hɔ ara, wɔkyɛɛ no. Ɔkunkum no de mogya no maa ɔhene no na ɔde maa ne nan so. Amonom hɔ ara, ɔsɔre. Wɔka kyerɛɛ no se, "Monto Ɔwɛmfo no ayeyi dwom". Wɔteɛɛm se, "Aane!" Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no kae se, "Mede wo ti na mede bɛma wo, na nea ɛwɔ Anansesɛm no mu no, ma wonkunkum no, na wonkunkum ne honam, na wonkunkum no wɔ afanan nyinaa". Wɔkunkumm no, na wɔkekaa ne honam mu nketewa nketewa, na wɔkunkum no nyinaa. Saa ara na Anansesɛm no dɔɔso wɔ abusuakuw no mu.

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
