ASƐM A ƐDA SO OSEBO, LEOPARD, ne ADOWA, ANTELOPE, bɛyɛɛ nnamfo

OKUROTWIAMANSA (Nnipa a wosuro ɔman no) te hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ obi a ɔmpɛ no (na ɔnnsuro sɛ ɔbɛka akyerɛ no saa). Afei, Adowa Antelope ketewa no te hɔ; ɔkae sɛ: "Agya Leopard, dɛn na woka fa me ho, na wobɛyɛ m'atamfo?" Osebo, Leopard no kae sɛ: "Ɛno ara na mepɛ". Enti wɔbɛyɛɛ m'atamfo. Anadwo a na awɔw wom no, Leopard no kae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwɛ n'agyapade. Leopard kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ wo na woka sɛ me ne wo nyinaa bɛyɛ atamfo; enti, mpo Esono, Elephant no, rentumi nyɛ m'atamfo a me ne no bɛbom ayɛ apam no. Akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ woahyia nsɛnnennen". Leopard no fii hɔ kɔtwee nnuaba, na ɔtwee nnuaba, na ɔde Antelope no too mu; na ɔfaa no, na ɔsoaa no wɔ ne ti so. Bere a ɔrebɛn no, bere a ohyiaa ɔkwan no fã no, Antelope no ho yeraw no. Ohui sɛ Leopard no de no rekɔ. Antelope no hwɛɛ soro, na na liana bi ahanyan wɔ hɔ. Adowa antelope no kyeree no. Afei kasa ne ne mmaransɛm (a ɛkyerɛ sɛ manka biribi) no, antelope no mu nsonsonoe bi wɔ nkotoku no mu. Ná Leopard nnim nea ɛrekɔ so; na ɔfaa no (sɛnea na osusuw) kɔɔ fie. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ: "Ɔkwasea panyin a ɔyɛ Antelope no kae sɛ me ne no nyɛ atamfo, enti mede no bae, enti fa nnɔbaamoa gu gya mu". Agyenkwa no kae sɛ: "Me maame, watwa, na ne nsonsonoe no da hɔ; momma yɛnkɔfa nkɔhyɛ nsupɔw mu". Enti wɔde kɔhyɛɛ nsupɔw no mu, na wowiee aduan no nyinaa, na wɔtenaa ase. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Kwadwo, na eyi yɛ n'anantwie, na ɛnyɛ ne honam dɛdɛ; anka ɛyɛ dɛ sen nnantwi, enti baabiara a ɔbɛhome no, momfa no mmra". Na ɔkɔfaa n'atutenabea; na ɔrekɔ no, na Adowa no da so ara abobow. Leopard kae sɛ, "Oh, bere a edi kan a meyɛɛ me ho ɔkwasea no, afei de, mɛkɔ fie akɔfa adaka bi na makɔ". Leopard no fii fie, na ɔfaa n'atade nyinaa (fi ne adaka no mu), na ɔde guu hɔ, na ɔde adaka no ne nsafe a ɛda dua no so no kɔe. Ɔfaa Antelope no, ɔde no too adaka no mu, kyekyeree no, ɔfaa no, na ɔde no kɔɔ ne ti so. Bere a ɔbaa no duu fie no, ɔkae sɛ: "Me maame, mede no aba; bue ɔpon no na memfa no nkɔ mu". Afei Leopard no ka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Merekɔ sare tenten no so akɔhwehwɛ nananom, nkyene, ne nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn a wɔatwa, na memfa mma wɔn, na yɛmfa Adowa Antelope no mfa nyɛ aduan a yɛbɛdi". Leopard no fii n'akwantu no ase. Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma no fii ase hyerɛn no, na onuawa ketewa no rebɔ nsɔe, na bere a Antelope no de n'ani kyerɛɛ dua bi mu no, ohuu onuawa no. Duyker kae sɛ: "Nokwarebea-nananom, wo ho bɛyɛ wo mmɔbɔ; sɛ wobue ma me a, mɛbɔ wo". Ɔbea no kae sɛ: "Wo, Duyker, wubeguan". Duyker buae sɛ: "Momma Aserampon, Ta' Kese, Dwomo, Apeya, Konkroma, Ta' Kora, ne Krakye Dente nkum me, sɛ miguan bere a wobue ma me no. Sɛ wopɛ mpo a, to apon no nyinaa mu". Ɔbaa no kae sɛ, "Metee". Ɔbaa no too apon no mu, na obuee (ɔdan a na Duyker no wɔ mu no pon no. Duyker no baa hɔ, na ɔkyeree no, na ɔbobɔɔ nsɔe no. Ɔbaa no bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ obetwa biribi afi fam, bere a Duyker no faa nsɔe no, na ɔbobɔɔ ne ti akyi no, pe! na owui. Akyiri yi, Kwadwo bae, na Duyker kae sɛ: "Adɛn nti na woatwentwɛn wo nan ase saa? Mehyɛɛ (pono) maa Duyker na mekum no, hwɛ ne honam wɔ ogya no mu". Leopard kae sɛ, "Apeafo, woayɛ no yiye". Ɔno nso faa nneɛma a ɔde bae no de hyɛɛ soup-stew no mu. Ná wɔawie apɔwdi; bere a wɔkɔtenaa ase didii no, Leopard no faa nnompe bi. Duyker no hwɛɛ n'ano kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ wo maame nnompe a worekum". Leopard no kae sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na woka yi?" Duyker no buae sɛ, "Mekae sɛ Duyker yi adi mmerewa; ne nnompe yɛ den". Leopard no kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ saa, na me nso mentumi mmu wɔn". Afei Leopard no hwiee nsã no guu n'anom, yɛɛ dede te sɛ feao! feao! Duyker no kae sɛ, "Ah, ɛyɛ apiti a wo maame ti so na wode rema no". Leopard no kae sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na woka?" Duyker no buae sɛ, "Mekae sɛ ne nsonsonoe (yɛsusu sɛ ɛyɛ fɛ) mpo, sɛ ɛfa ne nam ho a, woahunu sɛ ɛyɛ fɛ anaa?" Leopard no kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ papa, papa, ɛho nhia sɛ woka saa mpo". Afei, na wɔadi awie. . Duyker kɔfaa fie hɔ; ɔka kyerɛɛ akraman no sɛ, "Merebɛtow akraman no agu mfinimfini abotan no so". Akraman no kae sɛ, "Kɔ, afei, dɛn na mayɛ wɔ ho, meyɛ ɔbea anaa?" Duyker no duu mfinimfini abotan no so. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ho there, Leopard! Ho there, Leopard! Sɛ wonyɛ ɔkwasea a, sɛ wonyɛ aboa a, anka mɛkum wo maame na mayɛ no nkyene a wobedidi?" Leopard no kae sɛ, "Mehwehwɛ wo (sɛ mekɔ wo nkyɛn a, na meda hɔ ara) anaa?" Duyker no kae sɛ, "Wobɛfa me mpo". Leopard no tuu mmirika, yiridi! yiridi! (ɛnee ne nan ase dede), na Duyker no nso fii ase tuu mmirika, prada! prada! prada! Duyker no duu ne fie, na ɔyerae wɔ ne dan mu. Bere a osii (dan no) no, na wanyɛ ɔkwan biara a ɔbɛfa mu, gye ɔkwan biako a osii no. Bere a Duyker no baa mu no, ɔtoo n'apon, bam! Afei, hwɛ sɛnea Leopard no bɛyɛ no, na onhu ɔkwan biara a na Duyker no afa so akɔ mu. Leopard no fii hɔ kɔfrɛɛ mmoa no nyinaa ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Mommra mma yɛnkɔkyere Duyker no mma me, ɛfiri sɛ wayɛ me ɔkwasea, enti merentumi nka nsɛm nyinaa nkyerɛ mo kosi sɛ mobɛkyere no ama me". Bere a Duyker no tee sɛ mmoa no aba ne fie hɔ abɛkyere no no, ɔfaa nsukɔm kɔkɔɔ, nkyene, ne nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn, na ɔtwee wɔn, na ɔde guu hɔ. Afei Kusie a ɔyɛ Ɔtweaseɛ no kae sɛ, Kwakuo a ɔyɛ Ɔtweaseɛ no wɔ hɔ, na ɔwɔ atɛkyɛ tenten, enti ma ɔnkɔ soro kan, na yɛmfa ne atɛkyɛ nkɔ fie. Kwakuo a ɔyɛ Ɔtweaseɛ no siesiee ne ho, kra! kra! kra! na ɔkɔɔ soro. Ɔtenaa fie no atifi, nanso ankyɛ koraa na ɔkotow n'ani hwehwɛɛ Duyker no, ansa na Duyker no de abɔnsam a na wasesa no no hyɛɛ n'ano, na ɔtwee guu Apia no ani so. Afei Apia no gyaee na ɔhwee fam. Mmoa no bae na wohui sɛ biribi wɔ n'ani mu. Obi de ne nsa kaa bi, na ɔdomee ne nsateaa. Ɔkae sɛ, Oh, nnamfo, mommra mmɛhwɛ nea Kwakuo akɔfa aba. Mmoa no nyinaa bae. Wɔbɛkyekyeree, na wɔkyekyeree, na wɔkyekyeree. Ɛno nti na Kwakuo, Apɔnkɔ no wɔ ani a emu dɔ saa. Afei wɔkae sɛ, Kokotee (Bush-pig), wo de, daa wugu mu, nuntun! nuntun! Enti wobɛtumi akɔ fie hɔ. Bere a Duyker tee saa no, ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔtasee n'afurum no, na ɔde ano a ano yɛ nnam paa too so. Bush-pig no kɔtwee mu kɔtwee mu, na ne hwene puee fie hɔ. Afei Duyker no faa n'afurum no de too Bush-pig no ano, na na ɔretwa. Bush-pig no kae sɛ, Oh, Bush-pig Asamoa, ɔretwa m'ano. Mmoa a na wogyina n'akyi (na wontumi nte nea ɔkae no pefee) kae sɛ, Yɛyɛ yiye, Apeafo; hyɛ w'ani so, na fa no ba. Kokotee, Bush-pig no, twee ne ti; na ɔne n'anom yɛ mogya. Bere a mmoa no huu sɛ wɔapirapira Kokotee Asamoa, Bush-pig no ano no, wofii hɔ, na yii! (ɛno ne wɔn mmirika no nne). Saa na Bush-pig no nyaa nsem a emu yɛ den no. Afei, bere a mmoa no guanee no, Osebo, Leopard no, anhu nea obetumi ayɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ɛnyɛ baabiara na mɛkɔ; mɛtena ha daa, kosi sɛ mɛwu, gye sɛ mewu Adowa, Duyker no. Bere a Adowa tee saa no, ɔfaa (Leopards) maame no honam ani, na ɔbobɔɔ ne nipadua mu, na ɔforo kɔɔ fie no mu, na ɔbaa akyi no. Afei ɔkɔɔ Leopard no nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Oh, Kwadwo, dɛn na ama wo ho ayɛ wo den saa; adɛn nti na worehaw wo ho wɔ Duyker ho? Momma yɛnkɔ fie na yɛmfa yɛn dan nsiesie, na yɛnsan mmra. Wɔkɔɔ fie. Duyker ka kyerɛɛ akraman no sɛ, Fa w'agyapade nyinaa kɔma me, na mɛkora so ama wo. Akraman no kae sɛ, Ɛnyɛ adwene bɔne. Afei ɔyii ne nneɛma nyinaa de maa Duyker. Bere a Duyker de ne nsa hyɛɛ wɔn so na ɔde wɔn kɔe no, ɔkɔɔ baabi kɔgyinaa hɔ kae sɛ: Ho there, Leopard! Ɔbuaa no sɛ: Yiw, onua, yiw! Ɔbuaa no sɛ: Sɛ ɛnyɛ ɔkwasea, sɛ ɛnyɛ aboa a, anka metumi akum wo maame ama wayɛ soup-stew ama wo adi? Afei nso mahwe wo aso na magye w'agyapade nyinaa. Leopard no kae sɛ, Mehyia wo wɔ hɔ (sɛ meba a)? Duyker no kae sɛ, Wobɛfa me mpo. Afei Leopard no twaa ne ho hyiae, kiri! kiri! kiri! nanso Duyker no nso fii hɔ kɔe. Ɔkɔɔ ne fie kɔtoo pon no, bam! Afei Leopard no gyinaa hɔ, na n'atifi yɛɛ den, na n'afiri no twaa ne ho fii baabi kɔɔ baabi (a ɛte sɛ) fre! fre! fre! na wantumi annya asɛm anka. Akyiri yi ɔka kyerɛɛ Duyker sɛ: "Meka Dwoda din a wɔabara no, sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ moba, na yɛbɛyɛ nnamfo". Duyker no kae sɛ: "Mremma, dodow a mopɛ biara, gye sɛ mohwɛ ma yɛnom anyame no". Leopard no kae sɛ: "Megye tom, ɛno nti merentumi nyɛ mo hwee da". Duyker no ne Leopard no bae sɛ wɔrebɛnom anyame no. Duyker ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Nkyere me da; sɛ wuhyia me a, na sɛ wɔkyere me afiri mu a, gye me". Leopard kae sɛ, "Metee". Na Leopard nso kaa sɛ, "Ɛwɔ ɔkwan a wobɛfa so no so mpo, mɛfa me nsa, na magye so, na woahu sɛ me ne wo nni akasakasa biara". Ɛno nti, bere a Leopard biara wɔ hɔ a, ɔkyere no. Afei nso, bere a afiri bi abɛkyere Duyker no, ɔno (Leopard no) kɔ ma ogye no.

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
