ASƐM A ƐDA SO AKOKONINI, COCK no, Nyaa N'ano a Ɛkyerɛw

Agya ANANSE, Spider no ne ne mma Afudotwedotwe (Belly-like-to-burst), Nyiwankonfwea (Thin-shanks), (na) Tikonokono (Big-big-head) te hɔ. Na Spider no ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ mokɔ kɔhwehwɛ borɔdɔma ma midi". Enti saa mma yi fii hɔ; wɔkɔɔ so nantewee, nantewee, nantewee, na wohyiaa borɔdɔma dua bi, na (borɔdɔma no) nyinii, na mmofra no kɔɔ (dua no ase). Afudotwedotwe (Belly-like-to-burst) ka kyerɛɛ Nyiwankonfwea (Thin-shanks) sɛ, "Sɔre na bɔ wɔn mu bi". Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, sɛ meforo a, me nan bɛbubu". Tikonokono (Big-big-head) kaa sɛ, "Wo, Afudotwedotwe, worekɔ soro". Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, sɛ meforo a, me yafunu bɛpae; wo ara wobɛforo". Enti Tikonokono (Big-big-head) foro. Ɔfaa borɔdɔma no mma no bi, na ɔkɔɔ so twee ne ho, na ɔkae sɛ: "Ɛyɛ saa anaa?" Na wɔn a aka no kae sɛ: "Ah, (yɛyɛyɛ dɛn hu?) " Enti ɔkɔɔ so twee wɔn; na ɔsan bisaa wɔn bio sɛ: "Ɛyɛ saa anaa?" Na wɔkae sɛ: "Momma mo ti so nhwɛ". Na bere a ɔrebɛbɔ ne ti ase ahwɛ no, ne ti bɔɔ fam kae (asɛm bi te sɛ) "Kum!" Na Kokɔ no rehohoro ne kra, na ɔrebefi adi akɔtow nnua no mu nkotoku (a wɔde bɔ afɔre no), bere a ohui sɛ Thin-Shanks ahwe ase, na ne nan ase abubu, na wɔagyaw no wɔ fasu no fã foforo so no. Bere a ɔrebɛserew no, n'ano yɛɛ den:

"Bere a na ɔrebɛserew no, n'ano yɛɛ den.

Bere a na ɔrebɛserew no, ne ano yɛɛ den".

Ɛno nti na Mpanyimfo no ka sɛ, "Sɛ obi hwe ase a, kɔhwɛ no, na nserew". Na Kokotwa no de, ɔyɛɛ n'ade saa ara, na oserew, enti n'ano yɛɛ den; kan no na n'ano yɛ fɛ, na ɛyɛ fɛ.

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
