ASƐM A ƐDA SO AKOKONINI, COCK no, Nyaa N'ano a Ɛkyerɛw

PAA ANANSE, a wɔfrɛ no Spider, ne ne mma Afudotwedotwe (Belly-like-to-burst), Nyiwankonfwea (Thin-shanks), (ne) Tikonokono (Big-big-head) na na wɔte hɔ. Na ɔbaa no ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ: Monkɔhwehwɛ borɔdɔma mma me nnidi. Ɛnna wɔkɔe, na wɔkɔe, na wɔkɔe, na wohuu borɔdɔma dua bi, na na ayɛ frɔmfrɔm, na mmofra no kɔɔ ase. Afudotwedotwe (Belly-like-to-burst) ka kyerɛɛ Nyiwankonfwea (Thin-shanks) sɛ, "Sɔre na bɔ wɔn mu bi". Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, sɛ meforo a, me nan bɛbubu". Tikonokono (Big-big-head) kaa sɛ, "Wo, Afudotwedotwe, worekɔ soro". Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, sɛ meforo a, me yafunu bɛpae; wo ara wobɛforo". Enti Tikonokono (Big-big-head) foro. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Momfa mo ti nto fam na monhwɛ". Na bere a ɔrebɛbɔ ne ti ase ahwɛ no, ne ti bɔɔ fam kae sɛ, "Kum!" E-to-burst bɛserew, ne yafunu ahwe ase, ta! Thin-shanks, bere a na ɔreguan akɔka akyerɛ n'agya no, ne nan ase bubuu na ɔtenaa ɔpon no akyi. Bere a na ɔrebɛserew no, n'ano sii fam sɛ:

"Bere a na ɔrebɛserew no, n'ano yɛɛ den.

Bere a na ɔrebɛserew no, ne ano yɛɛ den".

Ɛno nti na Mpanyimfo no ka sɛ, "Sɛ obi hwe ase a, kɔhwɛ no, na nserew". Na Kokotwa no de, ɔyɛɛ n'ade saa ara, na oserew, enti n'ano yɛɛ den; kan no, na n'ano yɛ fɛ, na ɛyɛ fɛ.

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
