ASƐM A ƐDA SO ABUSOM, NYAMANKOPƆN A Wonni Biara, Baa Wiase

BERE bi na ɔbea bi wɔ hɔ a ɔwoo mma dubaako; na da biara a ɔsɔre yɛ aduan no, mmofra no di nyinaa; na wɔn maame nni bi nni. Afei ɔdwennwenee ho pii, na ɔkɔɔ afuw no mu kɔkaa Silk-cotton dua no ho asɛm kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Mɛsoma me mma dubaako aba wo nkyɛn ha sɛ wɔmmɛpɛtɛw mmorɔnoma; na sɛ wɔba a, mompɛtɛw mo mman dubaako na monkum me mma no". Silk-cotton dua no kae sɛ, "Metee, na mɛyɛ ama mo". Maame no fii hɔ kɔɔ fie; ɔka kyerɛɛ ne mma no sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ moyɛ saa". Mommra nnua no ase, monkɔ nnua no ase, monkɔkyekyere nnua no". Mma no fii hɔ kɔduruu nnua no ase. Ɛka sɛ, "Nomoro biako, gyina hɔ; Nomoro abien, gyina hɔ; Nomoro abiɛsa, gyina hɔ; Nomoro anan, gyina hɔ; Nomoro anum, gyina hɔ; Nomoro nsia, gyina hɔ; Nomoro ason, gyina hɔ; Nomoro awotwe, gyina hɔ; Nomoro du, gyina hɔ; na me ankasa, Nomoro dubiako, migyina hɔ". Ɛnneɛ, adɛn nti na ne maame kae sɛ yɛnkɔkyekyere mmorɔnoma?" Ne nuanom kae sɛ, "Dabi". Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɔka kyerɛɛ Silk-cotton dua yi sɛ, sɛ yɛkɔ hɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ ɛtwe nnubaa na ɛbɔ yɛn; enti mo nyinaa twa nnua, na montow wɔn ngu Silk-cotton dua yi so". Wɔtwee nnua no, na wɔtow wɔn guu Silk-cotton dua no so, pim! pen! pim! pen! (yɛ nne a wɔtee no). Na atoko dua no kae sɛ mma no aba, na oyii ne mman dubaako, na ɔmaa wɔn nyinaa hwee fam. Na akumaa dubaako kae sɛ: Woahu sɛ, sɛ yɛkɔ hɔ a, atoko dua no bɛkum yɛn. Na wɔfaa atoko no de maa wɔn maame, na ɔhyew; na ntɛm ara na mma no dii. Na wɔn na kae sɛ: Ah! Sɛ ɛba asɛm yi ho a, merentumi nnyina ano, enti mɛfa mmofra yi na mede ama ɔsoro-nyame". Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ adi pefee no, ɔkɔɔ soro-nyame no nkyɛn kɔkaa ne ho asɛm kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "M'awo mma no bi wɔ hɔ a, sɛ mepɛ sɛ midi a, mintumi nnya biribi nni (efisɛ wodi ntɛm na wodi pii); ɔkɔm rekum me; enti mesrɛ wo, ma mmofra no nyɛ frɔmfrɔm. Ɔbɔfo no kae sɛ: "Meka ti bi a emu yɛ fitaa ho asɛm". Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no yii abɔfo no, na wɔkɔtwee abura kɛse bi a wɔtoo ahina mu, na ɔbaa hɔ no, ɔfaa ɔwɔ ne abɔnkɔ de wɔn koguu abura no mu, na ɔkataa so. Afei ɔsomafo no kɔfrɛɛ mmofra no. Bere a wɔduruu baabi a ɔbodan no da hɔ ara no, Ɛkan Baako kae sɛ, "Nomoro Baako, gyina hɔ ara; Nomoro Baako, gyina hɔ ara; Nomoro Baako, gyina hɔ ara; Nomoro Baako, gyina hɔ ara; Nomoro Baako, gyina hɔ ara; Nomoro Nsia, gyina hɔ ara; Nomoro Nsia, gyina hɔ ara; Nomoro Baawɔtwe, gyina hɔ ara; Nomoro Baako, gyina hɔ ara; na me ara, Nomoro Baako, gyina hɔ ara; ɛsɛ sɛ wugyina hɔ ara. Na ne nuanom kae sɛ: Ɛdɛn nti na, sɛ ɛkwan trɛw wɔ hɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛfa dua no mu? Na wɔrekɔ no, na wɔn nyinaa de nnua atwa ho ahyia. Na ɔkan no kae sɛ: Fa nnua yi mu biako gu saa kwan yi so. Na wɔtow no kyenee hɔ, na ɔhwehwee mu (wɔ amoa no mu), yiridi (na ɛyɛ ne sɛe nne). Ɔkaa sɛ: "Hwɛ, sɛ yɛtwa ho a anka yɛn nyinaa bɛwu". Enti wɔfaa kwan bi so kɔɔ ɔsoro nyame no nkyɛn, na ɔtwee amoa, na ɔde mpomma sii wɔn so, na sɛ mmofra no ba bɛtenaa wɔn so a, na wɔhwe ase, na mmofra no duu amoa no anim. Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn kae sɛ: "Adan a wɔde asi hɔ, monkɔ na montena so". Afei Eleven no kae sɛ: "Hwan ne yɛn a yɛbɛtumi atena so a ɛyɛ fɛ; enti, Owura, yɛbɛtena nkyɛn". Ɛnna Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn hwɛɛ mmofra no hwɛe, na ɔkae sɛ: "Mɛsoma mmofra yi akɔ owu kurom". Ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ Owu (nea ɔte hɔ) hɔ, na kɔ na gye sika kɔkɔɔ bi fi ne nkyɛn, sika kɔkɔɔ a wotwa, sika kɔkɔɔ a wotwa, sika kɔkɔɔ whet-stone, ne sika kɔkɔɔ a wotwa". Nambala Eleven kae sɛ, "Wone yɛn wura, baabiara a wobɛsoma yɛn akɔ no, yɛbɛkɔ". Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Fi hɔ". Mma no fii hɔ kɔɔ Owu akuraa no ase. Woduu hɔ no, Owu kae sɛ: "Adɛn nti na woaba ha bere a ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara ba ha da?" Wɔbuaa sɛ: "Na yɛretu mmirika na yɛbaa ha (ɛnyɛ biribi a ɛte saa). " Owu kae sɛ: "Afei de, eye". Afei Owu woo mma du; ne ho kaa ho no, na wɔn dodow yɛ dubaako. Bere a nneɛma fii ase yerae (i.e. Bere a esum duruu no, Owu kyɛɛ mmofra no mmiako mmiako, na ɔde biako maa ne mma no mu biara, bere a ɔno ankasa ne Ɔkannifo Baako no kɔe sɛ wɔrekɔda no. Bere a esum duruu no, Owu hyerɛn maa ne se yɛɛ kɔkɔɔ (kɔɔ kosii sɛ ɛhyerɛn) sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obenya Ɔkannifo Baako no bi. Ɛka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Owu, menna". Ɛka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Ɛka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Sɛ wode sika kɔkɔɔ bi rema me sigaret kakra a, ebia mɛda". Na owu kɔfaa no bae. Ɛka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Owu, menna". Ɛka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Ɛka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Sɛ wode sika kɔkɔɔ bi a wɔde ma me a, ebia mɛda". Ɛka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Owu na ɔde maa no". Ɛka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Sɛ wokɔ kɔfa sika kɔbere bi a mede bɛtɔ mu kakra a, ebia mɛdeda". Owu kɔfaa no, na bere tiaa bi twaa mu, na na owu rebɛkyere no. Ɔpanyin dubaako kae sɛ: "Nananom, minnya nna". Owu kae sɛ: "Ɛnneɛ, bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Ɔpanyin dubaako kae sɛ: "Nananom, sɛ wokɔ kɔfa sika kɔkɔɔ bi a, anka mɛda". Na Owu kɔfaa no bae. Bio, ankyɛ na Owu no nyanee bio. Na owusɔre kae sɛ: "Na da bɛn na wobɛda?" Na owusɔre kae sɛ: "Sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa bobesa bi a abura ahyɛ mu mã, na woakɔhwie nsu agu mu, na woakɔhyew aduan bi ama me sɛ mennidi a, ebia mɛda". Owu no maa ne nsa so na ɔkɔɔ asuten no ho. Na bere a ɔtwee nsu no mu no, abura a ɛwɔ mpoma no mu no maa no twaa mu. Afei Eleven ka kyerɛɛ ne nuanom no sɛ: Monsɔre, na monkɔ! Na wɔsɔre guanee. Na Eleven kɔtwee nnuaban, na ɔde wɔn kɔtoo baabi a ne nuanom da no, na ɔfaa ntade kataa wɔn so. Na owu wɔ abura no ho, na ɔtwee nsu. Ɔbea no buae sɛ, "Adwo". Ɔbarima no kae sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na woreyɛ yi?" Ɔbea no kae sɛ, "Aah, ɛnyɛ abofra ketewa bi na mewɔ; bere a merebɛkyere no no, ɔka sɛ, 'Menni ada'; ɔfaa me nneɛma nyinaa, na afei ɔka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mefa nsu a ɛkyekyere ade na mehu nsu". Ɔbea no kae sɛ: "Oh, hwɛ sɛnea ɛyɛ nokware". Ɔtwee afifide, na ɔde guu mu, na ɔtwee nsu no guu mu, na ofii hɔ kɔe. Nhoma dubaako kae sɛ: "Owu, woaba dedaw? Bere a ɔkɔe no, wanhwehwɛ wɔn mu yiye, na ɔno ankasa kum ne mma du no nyinaa. Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu, bere a nneɛma no daa adi no, Owu sɔre gyinaa hɔ, na ɔtenaa ogya no ho. Nambala dubaako kae sɛ: "Nananom, tsetse mmoadabi bi te wo kokom". Owuo kae sɛ: "Boa mmoadabi a ɔda hɔ no, na kum no ma me". Nambala dubaako kae sɛ: "Me ho yɛ fɛ; obi a ofi wo mu, bere a tsetse mmoadabi bi si wo so, na sikakɔkɔɔ mmoadabi bi da hɔ (wobɛfa saa ade dedaw yi) no, ma memfa mmrɛ no mmra mmɛkum no". Owuo kae sɛ: "Kɔ na kɔfa no mmrɛ no". Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnnɛ, baabi a tsetse yi bɛhome no, ɛhɔ na me ne no bɛhome". Afei ɔbaa dan no mu, na ɔfaa ne kotoku a sikakɔkɔɔ dua no wɔ mu no, ne nneɛma a aka nyinaa. Ɔkae sɛ, "Nanan Owu, biribiara nni hɔ a ɛbɛdɔɔso gye sɛ mefa tsetse nwewee no, mede to saa kotoku yi mu, na mede brɛ wo". Oduu kurow no awiei no, ɔkae sɛ: "O Nana Owu, mesrɛ wo, fa kyɛ me asɛm yi, na sɛ ɛnyɛ wo na woyɛ ɔkwasea a, anka mɛma wo nneɛma nyinaa afi wo nsam, na me nuanom a me ne wɔn bae no anya ahofadi, na mama wo nso woawu wo mma du no nyinaa? Na me de, merekɔ". Owu kae sɛ: "Wo, abofra a ɔte saa; baabiara a wobɛhome no, ɛhɔ ara na me nso mɛhome". Nambala dubaako no tu fii hɔ, yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! Na Owu nso dii n'akyi. Bere a Nambala dubaako no rekɔ no, ohyiaa ne nuanom, na na wɔte ɔkwan no so, na wɔreto nnomaa afiri. Nambala dubaako no kae sɛ: "Wunnyaankɔ? Owu reba, enti momma yɛnhwehwɛ ɔkwan bi a yɛbɛfa so aguan". Afei Owu baa wɔn so. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Hayi, mihuu mmofra yi, na wɔakɔ he?" Afa a ɛto so dubiako no tenaa soro, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuanom sɛ: "Mɛto nsu ama no". Ne nuanom kae sɛ: "Ɛyɛ, ɔrehwehwɛ yɛn sɛ obegye yɛn, na yɛadwane aba ha, na yɛabɛtena ha, nanso woka sɛ, 'Mɛto nsu ama no.' " Afa a ɛto so dubiako no antie, na ɔmaa nsu maa owu. Owu kae sɛ: "Ah, ha na wowɔ, ɛnnɛ woahu ɔhaw". Owu kae sɛ: "Wo, abofra, a wote hɔ no, 'Kyere-he-ne, Kyere-he-ne.' " Ɛhɔ ara na mmofra no mu biako hwehwee ase. "Kyere-he-ne", nea ɛto so abien no hwehwee ase. Afei ade biako pɛ na ɛkae. Owu kae sɛ: "Abofra, 'kyere-he-ne,'" na Number Eleven bɔɔ kirisi sian kɔɔ fam, kirim! Na owuo nso kɔɔ Silk-cotton dua no so. Numeral Eleven kae sɛ, "Wo, ɔbea kɛse kɛse, wo nso, 'kyere-he-ne.' " Na owuo nso baa fam, tum! na wawu. Numeral Eleven kɔfaa nnuru no, na ɔbobɔɔ no ne nsa ano, na ɔhwiehwiehwiehwiehwiee guu ne nuanom so, na wɔsɔre. Numeral Eleven kɔɔ sɛ ɔtow nnuru no gu, na emu bi twaa owuo so, na owuo nyan. Ɔbea no kae sɛ: "Woakum me, na woanyan me nso; ɛnnɛ wo ne me nyinaa benya asɛm". Afei wɔn nyinaa fii ase dwanee prɛko pɛ, kiri! kiri! kiri! Afei owu dii wɔn akyi. Bere a wɔrekɔ no, na asubɔnten kɛse bi abrɛw. Bere a Eleven ne ne nuanom duu hɔ no, na wonim sɛnea wɔbɔ san kɔfaa. Eleven nko ara na na onnim sɛnea wɔbɔ san kɔfaa. Na mma no gyinagyina faako no, na wɔteɛteɛɛm, na wɔteɛteɛɛm, na wɔn ano hinhimii. Na Elefante bɛyɛɛ ɔbo, na owu duu asu no ano. Ɔkae sɛ: "O, mmofra yi, munnyina hɔ, momma menkɔfa ɔbo bi mmɛtow nkɔbɔ wɔn ano a aporɔw no". Owuo, bere a ɔhwɛɛ fam no, ohui sɛ ɔbo bi da hɔ; ɔfaa no tow kyenee; bere a ɔbo no nam kwan so no, ɔkae sɛ: "Mframa, fa me na fa me kɔ ɔfã foforo so". Ɛbaa fam wɔ ɔfã foforo no so. Ɔkaa sɛ: "Mewɔ ha". Owuo kae sɛ: "Ah, abofra yi! M'asɛm a meka kyerɛ mo ara ne sɛ: Monkɔ na montena fie, na monkɔsom anyame nketewa no mu biako, na sɛ obi a mepɛ sɛ mefa no ba wo nkyɛn a, monka me ho asɛm nkyerɛ me; na sɛ mepɛ a, megyaw no hɔ, na mayɛ n'akyɛde ama wo; na nea mepɛ no, gye ma me". Wɔfiri mmɔfra ketewa a wɔfrɛ no Eleven no mu na ɛbaeɛ.

Eyi ne me ho asɛm a maka akyerɛ mo no. Sɛ ɛyɛ fɛ anaa ɛnyɛ fɛ a, mubetumi agye emu bi atom sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no nso, mobɛkamfo me.
