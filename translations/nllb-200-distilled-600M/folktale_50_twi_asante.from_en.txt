ASƐM A ƐDA SO ABUSOM, NYAMANKOPƆN A Wonni Biara, Baa Wiase

BERE bi na ɔbea bi wɔ hɔ a ɔwoo mma dubaako; na da biara a ɔsɔre yɛ aduan no, mmofra no di nyinaa; na wɔn maame nni bi nni. Afei ɔdwennwenee ho pii, na ɔkɔɔ afuw no mu kɔkaa Silk-cotton dua no ho asɛm kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Mɛsoma me mma dubaako aba wo nkyɛn ha sɛ wɔmmɛpɛtɛw mmorɔnoma; na sɛ wɔba a, wo mmorɔnoma dubaako aba wo nkyɛn na kum me mma no". Silk-cotton dua no kae sɛ, "Metee, na mɛyɛ ama wo". Maame no fii hɔ kɔɔ fie; ɔka kyerɛɛ ne mma no sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ mokɔ afuw no ase, Silk-cotton dua no ase; mmorɔnoma wɔ hɔ, monkɔ na momfa mmorɔnoma no mmra". Mma no fii hɔ kɔduu Silk-cotton dua no so. 11 kae sɛ, "Number One, gyina hɔ; Number Two, gyina hɔ; Number Three, gyina hɔ; Number Four, gyina hɔ; Number Five, gyina hɔ; Number Six, gyina hɔ; Number Seven, gyina hɔ; Number Eight, gyina hɔ; Number Nine, gyina hɔ; Number Ten, gyina hɔ; na me ankasa, Number Eleven, migyinaa hɔ". Afei Number Eleven buae wɔn sɛ, "Munnim nea enti a Maame kae sɛ yɛnkɔkyekyere mmorɔnoma?" Ne nuanom kae sɛ, "Dabi". Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔka kyerɛɛ Silk-cotton dua yi sɛ, sɛ yɛkɔ hɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ ɛtwe nnuan na ɛboro yɛn; enti yɛn nyinaa. Wɔtwee nnua no, na wɔtow guu nnua no so. Wɔtwee nnua no tow guu nnua no so, pim! pen! pim! pen! (ɛno ne nne a wɔtee no). Nnua no kae sɛ mmofra no aba. Ɔtwee ne mman dubaako, na ɔmaa wɔn nyinaa hwee fam. Nhoma Baako kaa sɛ: "Woahu sɛ, sɛ yɛkɔ hɔ a, anka nnua no bɛkum yɛn". Wɔtwee mmorɔnoma no de maa wɔn maame. Ɔtoo wɔn; oh! ntɛm ara na mmofra no dii (ne nyinaa). Wɔn maame kae sɛ: "Ah! Sɛ ɛba asɛm yi ho a, merentumi nnyina ano, enti mɛfa mmofra yi na mede ama ɔsoro-nyame". Ade kyee anɔpa a nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ adi no, ɔkɔɔ ɔsoro-nyame no hɔ kɔkaa ne ho asɛm kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "M'awo mma bi wɔ hɔ a, sɛ mepɛ sɛ midi a, mintumi nnya biribi nni (efisɛ wodi ntɛm na wodi pii); ɔkɔm rekum me; enti mesrɛ wo, ma wɔmfa mma no mmra mmekum wɔn, na manya biribi nni". Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ saa ampa?" Ɔbea no kae sɛ, "Mede ti a emu yɛ fitaa na ɛkasa". Ɔsoro-nyame no yii mesen. na wɔkɔtwee abura kɛse bi, na wɔtotoo nsuhina wɔ mu. Na ɔbaa hɔ kɔfaa ɔwɔ ne akekantwɛre de wɔn koguu abura no mu, na ɔkataa so. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔfrɛɛ mma no. Bere a wɔduruu baabi a ɔbodan no da hɔ ara pɛ na Ɔman Baako no kae sɛ: "Ɔman Baako, gyina hɔ ara; Ɔman Baako, gyina hɔ ara; Ɔman Baako, gyina hɔ ara; Ɔman Baanan, gyina hɔ ara; Ɔman Baako, gyina hɔ ara; Ɔman Baako, gyina hɔ ara; Ɔman Baako, gyina hɔ ara; Ɔman Baako, gyina hɔ ara; Ɔman Baako, gyina hɔ ara; Ɔman Baako, gyina hɔ ara". Ne nuanom kae sɛ: "Adɛn nti na, bere a ɔkwan atenten wɔ hɔ no, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛfa dua no mu?" Na mmarima dubaako no kae sɛ: Montow nnua yi mu biako ngu ɔkwan yi so! Na wɔtow no kyenee hɔ, na ɔhwee ase, yiridi. Na mmarima dubaako no kae sɛ: Hwɛ, sɛ yɛatwa ho a, anka yɛn nyinaa bɛwu. Na wɔfaa ɔkwan bi fii hɔ kɔɔ ɔsoro Nyankopɔn nkyɛn; na ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no yɛɛ amoa, na ɔde mpomma sii wɔn so, na sɛ mmofra no ba bɛtenaa wɔn so a, wɔhwehwee amoa no mu; na mmofra no duu ɔsoro Nyankopɔn anim. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ: "Wɔasiesie nkongua wɔ hɔ, monkɔ na monkɔtena so". Afei Nhoma dubaako no kae sɛ: "Hwan ne yɛn, sɛ yɛbɛtumi atena nkongua a ɛyɛ fɛ saa so; enti, Owura, yɛbɛtena nkyɛn ha". Ɛno akyi no, Ɔsoro-nyame no hwɛɛ mmofra no hwɛe; ɔkae sɛ: "Mɛsoma mmofra yi akɔ Owu kurow no mu". Ade kyee anɔpa a nneɛma no fii hɔ no, ɔfrɛɛ mmofra no ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ: "Ɛsɛ sɛ mokɔ Owu (a ɔte) hɔ, na monkɔ na momfa sika kɔkɔɔ bi mfi ne nsam, sika kɔbere, sika kɔbere, sika kɔbere, sika kɔbere, ne sika kɔbere, ne sika kɔbere". Nhoma dubaako no kae sɛ: "Mo de, monkɔ na munnye sika kɔbere". Woyɛ yɛn wura, baabiara a wobɛsoma yɛn no, yɛbɛkɔ". Ɔsorosoro nyame no kae sɛ, "Monkɔ". Mma no fii hɔ kɔɔ Owu akuraa no ase. Woduu hɔ; Owu kae sɛ, "Adɛn nti na bere a ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara ba ha a, moaba ha?" Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Yɛnenam na yɛbaa ha (sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ biara). " Owu kae sɛ, "Afei de, eye". Afei Owu nyaa mma du; ne ho kaa ho no, na wɔn dodow yɛ dubaako. Bere a nneɛma fii ase yerae (a ɛkyerɛ sɛ esum duruu no), Owu kyekyɛɛ mma no mmiako mmiako na ɔde biako maa ne mma no mu biara, bere a ɔne Eleven kɔɔ ɔhome. Na bere a esum beduu no, Owu hyerɛn maa ne se kɔkɔɔ sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔde wɔn bɛkyekyere Numeral dubiako no. Numeral dubiako no kae sɛ: "Owu, minnya nna". Owu kae sɛ: "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Owu kae sɛ: "Sɛ wode sikakɔkɔɔ ahina bi bɛma me a, mɛda". Na Owu bae, na bere tiaa bi akyi no, Owu hyerɛn maa ne se kɔkyeree Numeral dubiako no. Na owuo de no brɛɛ no bio, na ankyɛ na owuo de no bɛkyeree no. Na owuo kae sɛ: Wobɛda bere bɛn? Na owuo kae sɛ: Wobɛda bere bɛn? Na owuo de no brɛɛ no. Na owuo de no brɛɛ no bio, na ankyɛ na owuo de no bɛkyeree no. Na owuo kae sɛ: Minnya nna. Na owuo kae sɛ: Wobɛda bere bɛn? Na owuo kae sɛ: Wobɛda bere bɛn? Na owuo kae sɛ: Sɛ wokɔ kɔfa sika kɔbere bi a mede bɛkyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyekyeky Ɔpanyin dubaako kae sɛ: "Nananom, minnya nna". Owu kae sɛ: "Ɛnneɛ, bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Ɔpanyin dubaako kae sɛ: "Nananom, sɛ wokɔ kɔfa sika kɔkɔɔ bi a, anka mɛda". Na Owu kɔfaa no bae. Bio, ankyɛ na Owu no nyanee bio. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sɛ wokɔfa ɔkorow bi a abura ahyɛ mu, na wokɔto nsu gu mu, na woyɛ aduan bi ma me adidi a, ebia mɛda". Na owuo no faa ɔkorow bi de kɔɔ asuten no ho, na bere a ɔtwee nsu no mu no, ɔkorow no mu abura no maa ɛbaa mu. Afei, Elefante no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuanom no se, "Monsan nkɔ!" Na wɔsɔre guanee. Elefante no kɔtwaa nnuaba, de wɔn kɔtoo baabi a na ne nuanom da no, na ɔfaa ntade kataa wɔn so. Saa bere no, na Owu wɔ asubɔnten no ho, na ɔretwa nsu. Ɔbea no buae sɛ, "Adwo". Ɔbea no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na woreyɛ?" Ɔbea no kae sɛ, "Aah, ɛnyɛ abofra ketewa bi na mewɔ; bere a merebɛkyere no no, ɔka sɛ, 'Menni ada'; ɔfaa me nneɛma nyinaa, na afei ɔka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mefa nsu a ɛresɛe no". Ɔkaa sɛ: "Owu, woaba dedaw? mommoaboa aduan no ano". Owu yɛɛ aduan no; ɔhyew ne se sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obekum Owu nuanom na wama wɔn aduan. Bere a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, wansusuw wɔn ho yiye, na ɔno ankasa kum ne mma du no nyinaa. Ade kyee no, ahemadakye, bere a nneɛma fii ase yerae no, Owu sɔre gyinaa hɔ tenaa ogya no ho. Nambala dubaako kae sɛ, "Nananom, tsetse mmoadabi bi te wo kokom". Owuo kae sɛ, "Kɔhwehwɛ mmoadabi a ɛda hɔ no, na kum no ma me". Nambala dubaako kae sɛ, "Me ho yɛ fɛ; obi a ɔte sɛ wo, bere a tsetse mmoadabi bi si wo so, na sikakɔkɔɔ mmoadabi bi da hɔ (wobetumi de saa ade dedaw yi ayɛ adwuma), ma memfa mmrɛ no mmra mmɛkum no". Owuo kae sɛ, "Kɔhwehwɛ mmrɛ no mfi dan no mu". Nambala dubaako kɔfaa no bae. Ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛpam mmoadabi no; wankum no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Nnɛ, faako a tsetse nhwiren yi bɛhome no, ɛhɔ na me ne no bɛhome". Afei ɔbaa dan no mu, na ɔfaa ne kotoku a sikakɔkɔɔ dua no wɔ mu no, ne nneɛma a aka no nyinaa. Ɔkae sɛ, "Nananom Owu, biribiara renso da gye sɛ menya tsetse nhwiren no, mede no gu kotoku yi mu, na mede no brɛ wo". Ɔkaa sɛ, "Yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! Oduu kurow no awiei; ɔkae sɛ: "Awoo, Nana Owu, fa kyɛ me sɛ meka saa, nanso sɛ wonyɛ ɔkwasea pa a, anka metumi agye wo afi w'ahode nyinaa mu; anka me nuanom a me ne wɔn bae no betumi afa ɔkwan bi so aguan; na anka metumi ama woakum wo mma du no nyinaa nso anaa? Me de, merekɔ". Owu kae sɛ: "Wo, abofra a ɔte saa; baabiara a wobɛhome no, ɛhɔ ara na mɛhome". Nambala Eleven no tu fii hɔ, yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! na Owu nso dii n'akyi. Bere a Nambala Eleven rekɔ no, ohyiaa ne nuanom, na wɔte ɔkwan no so. Na wɔreyɛ nnomaa afiri. Afei owuo baa wɔn so. Afei owuo no faa nnuru de guu ne nuanom so, na wɔkɔɔ atweaatam dua bi so. Na owuo no gyinaa atweaatam dua no ase. Ɔkaa sɛ: "Ɛha nko ara na mihuu mmofra yi, na wɔakɔ he?" Nambala dubaako no te soro, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuanom sɛ: "Mɛto nsuo ama no". Ne nuanom kae sɛ: "E, ɔhwehwɛ yɛn sɛ ɔkyere yɛn, na yɛadwane aba ha na yɛabɛtena ha, nanso woka sɛ: 'Mɛto nsuo ama no.' " Nambala dubaako no antie, na ɔtoo nsuo maa owu. Owuo kae sɛ: "Ah, ha na wowɔ, ɛnnɛ woahu amane". Owuo kae sɛ: "Wo, abofra a wote hɔ, 'Kyere-he-ne, Kyere-he-ne.' " Ɛhɔ na mmofra no mu biako hwee fam. "Kyere-he-ne", nea ɛto so abien no hwee fam. Na owuo kae sɛ, "Abofra, 'kyere-he-ne,'" na owuo nso kɔɔ Silk-cotton dua no so. Na owuo nso kae sɛ, "Wo, ɔbaa kɛse, wo nso, 'kyere-he-ne.'" Na owuo nso baa fam, tum! na wawu. Na owuo nso kɔɔ kɔfaa nnuru no, na ɔbobɔɔ no ne nsa ano, na ɔhwiee guu ne nuanom so, na wɔsɔre. Na owuo nso kae sɛ, 'Wo, ɔbaa kɛse, wo nso, 'kyere-he-ne.' Na owuo nso baa fam, tum! na wawu. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Woakum me, na wo nso woanyan me; ɛnnɛ wo ne me nyinaa benya asɛm". Afei wɔn nyinaa fii ase dwanee prɛko pɛ, kiri! kiri! kiri! Afei owu dii wɔn akyi. Bere a wɔrekɔ no, na nsubɔnten kɛse bi wɔ hɔ a asukɔ wɔ mu. Bere a Eleven ne ne nuanom duu hɔ no, na wonim sɛnea wɔbɛnantew, na wotwaa mu. Eleven nkutoo na na onnim sɛnea wɔbɛnantew. Mmofra no gyinaa ɔfã foforo no so, na wɔsu, na wɔsu, na wɔsu; wɔn anom buebue. Na Eleven de, ɔdan ɔbo. Owu duu asu no so. Ɔkae sɛ: "O, mmofra yi, munnyina hɔ, momma menkɔfa ɔbo bi mmɛtow nkɔbɔ wɔn ano a apɔw no". Owuo, bere a ɔhwɛɛ fam no, ohui sɛ ɔbo bi da hɔ; ɔfaa no tow kyenee; bere a ɔbo no nam kwan so no, ɔkae sɛ: "Mframa, momfa me na momfa me nkɔ ɔfã foforo no so". Ɛbaa fam wɔ ɔfã foforo no so. Ɔkaa sɛ: "Mewɔ ha". Owuo kae sɛ: "Ah, abofra yi! Me de, minni hwee ka kyerɛ mo bio, nanso meka kyerɛ mo sɛ: Monkɔ na montena fie, na monkɔsom anyame nketewa no mu biako, na sɛ obi a mepɛ sɛ mefa no ba wo nkyɛn a, monka me ho asɛm nkyerɛ me, na sɛ mepɛ a, megyaw no hɔ, na mayɛ n'akyɛde ama mo, na nea mepɛ no, momfa mma me".

Eyi ne me ho asɛm a maka akyerɛ mo no. Sɛ ɛyɛ fɛ anaa ɛnyɛ fɛ a, ebia mobɛfa bi sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no nso, mobɛkamfo me.
