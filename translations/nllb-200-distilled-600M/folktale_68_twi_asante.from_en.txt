ASƐM A ƐDA SO Sɛnea Ananse, Ahemfo, Fii Ase Yɛɛ Ohia

KWAKU ANANSE, Spider no fii hɔ kɔɔ Nyankonpon, ɔsoro-nyame no nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Ma me awifua baako na memfa nkuraa a nnipa wɔ mu nyinaa mma wo. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Wontumi. Spider no buae sɛ, Sɛ mentumi a, mehyɛ wo maame Nsia bɔ sɛ, mede me ti bɛtua me ntam a midii no so ka. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Gye (aburow no) na kɔ. Spider no fii hɔ kɔɔ nkuraa bi so. Ɔkaa asɛm a edi hɔ yi kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no. Ɔsoro nyame asoma me; ɔka sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ mefa aburow koro yi na me ne no kɔ baabi, na sɛ meba ( baabiara) a, ɛsɛ sɛ mema aburow koro yi ne nnomaa no kɔda. Ɔkannifo no kae sɛ, E! bere a nnomaa ne aburow no kɔda faako a, ɛbɛyɛ yiye? Ananse buae sɛ, Nea ɛsɛ sɛ woyɛ ara ne sɛ wobɛma wɔda faako. Ɔkannifo no kae sɛ, Kɔ na kɔto hɔ. Ade kyee anɔpa no, Ananse kae sɛ ɔrebɛhwehwɛ, nanso (aburow koro no) nni hɔ bio. Spider no kae sɛ, Head-man, nnomaa no adi ɔsoro-nyame no aburow. Head-man no kae sɛ, Me ntumi nnye akyinnye biara wɔ eyi ho, enti fa nnomaa no na kɔ. Ananse kɔfaa nnomaa kɛse bi, na ɔne no fii hɔ kɔɔ akuraa foforo ase. Bere a oduu hɔ no, ɔkaa asɛm bi kyerɛɛ ɔsafohene no sɛ, Ɔsoro-nyame no somaa me, na mɛda ha, na nnomaa yi a mehyɛ mu yi ne nguan no bɛda. Head-man no kae sɛ, Fa no bra na fa no hɔ. Ananse kɔɔ nguan kraal no nkyɛn anadwo no, na okum nnomaa no. Ɔfaa nnomaa no amoa na ɔkyekyeree wɔn wɔ oguan kɛse bi mmɛn so. Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu no, ɔkɔɔ ɔpanyin no nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Ma me nnomaa no". Bere a wɔkɔɔ kɔfaa no no, hwɛ, na nnomaa no awu. Ananse kae sɛ: "Ah! ɛno ne ɔsoro nyame nnomaa a nguan no akum no no". Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ: "Mentumi nnye akyinnye biara wɔ ho, enti fa nguan no na kɔ". Ananse faa nguan no fii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ akuraa foforo bi ase. Ɔkɔɔ ɔpanyin no fie. Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ: "Asɛm bɛn?" Spider no buae sɛ: "Ɔpanyin Nyankopɔn asoma me, na mikura nguan no; ɔno ne anantwi no bɛda faako". Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ: "Ho! Ɔkaa sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔto no wɔ hɔ". Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔkɔ na kɔto no wɔ hɔ". Ananse kɔkum nnwan no anadwo, na ɔde ne mogya guu anantwi no mu baako ti so. Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu no, Ananse kae sɛ, "Headman, ma me me me nnwan no". Wɔkɔɔ kraal no nkyɛn; na nnwan no awu. Ananse kae sɛ: "Onipa, ɛyɛ ɔsoro Nyankopɔn nnwan a wɔakum no". Ɔnipa no kae sɛ: "Minni ho kwan sɛ me ne wo bɛkasa wɔ asɛm yi ho, enti fa anantwi a wokum no no kɔ". Ananse faa anantwi no, na ofii hɔ kɔɔ akuraa foforo bi ase, na ɔfaa anantwi no, na ɔkyekyeree no, na ɔdae. Ɔkannifo no kae sɛ: "Ɛsɛ sɛ wode kɔtow no kyene, efisɛ minni adaka". Spider no kae sɛ: "Me de, mepɛ, efisɛ ɔsoro-nyame no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ sɛ minya afunu bi a wɔbɛtow ato hɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ mesesa no ayɛ anantwi". Akuraa no mufo kae sɛ: "Dabi". (Nanso) Ananse (gyinaa so kae sɛ: "Ɛsɛ sɛ wode ba me nkyɛn, na sɛ wokɔtɔn anantwi no a, wubenya sika kɔkɔɔ dɔte a ɛsom asuanu (a ɛkyerɛ sɛ £4) na wubetumi de (saa dodow no) atɔ akoa foforo". Nkurɔfo no kae sɛ: "Ɛyɛ nokware wɔ mu". Enti Ananse yɛɛ asesae no. Ɔfaa afunu no, na ofii hɔ kɔɔ akuraa foforo so. Na bere a oduu hɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no sɛ: Ma me ba yi nkɔ ne wo mma mmɛtena wo fie ha; na hwɛ, abofra yi nyɛ fɛ, na ɔtaa bɔ mframa anadwo, na sɛ ɔbɔ mframa a, mma mma mma mmɛbɔ no. Na ɔbarima no awu nnansa ni, na mmofra no ne no da hɔ, na afei wohui sɛ ɛyɛ hu, na wɔkae sɛ: Saa abofra yi apira mframa, momma yɛmmɔ no. Na wɔhwehwee no so, titim! titim! titim! Ade kyee anɔpa no, Ananse kɔɔ ne ba no nkyɛn kɔfrɛɛ no. Ananse kae sɛ: "Ɔkannifo, wo mma akum ɔsoro-nyame no bɔfo a na ɔne me wɔ hɔ no". Ɔkannifo no kae sɛ: "Mentumi nnye asɛm yi ho akyinnye biara, enti wubetumi de mmofra no akɔ". Spider no buae sɛ: "Mentumi nnye ntom) gye sɛ wo, ɔkannifo, ne nnipa a wɔwɔ kurow yi mu nyinaa kɔ ɔsoro-nyame no anim kɔka wo asɛm no". Ɔkannifo no bɔe 'dawuro gong, kae sɛ: "Ɛsɛ sɛ mo nyinaa kɔ ɔsoro-nyame no anim wɔ hɔ kɔka yɛn asɛm no". Spider no dii n'anim; nnipa no nyinaa dii n'akyi. Bere a Ananse duu kurow no akyi no, ɔsomaa ɔbɔfo sɛ ɔnkɔka nkyerɛ ɔsoro Nyankopɔn sɛ, "Me ne nnipa a wɔwɔ kurow bi mu reba", na ɔfrɛɛ n'asraafo no, na ɔkɔtenaa hɔ.

Ananse, awerɛhosɛm, awerɛhosɛm, mede nnomaa no kɔfaa oguan.

Mefaa nguan no, na mekɔfaa nantwi bi, mefaa nantwi no, na mekɔfaa afunu bi, mefaa afunu no, na mefaa nnipa a wɔwɔ kurow bi mu".

Obiara kae sɛ, "Wote nnwom a Ananse reto no anaa?" Ananse maa ne dwom no so bio:

"Ananse, ah, ah, mewɔ aduaba biako pɛ a ɛsɛ sɛ mekɔfa so nya nnomaa.

Ananse, awerɛhosɛm, awerɛhosɛm, mede nnomaa no kɔfaa oguan.

Mefaa nguan no, na mekɔfaa nantwi bi, mefaa nantwi no, na mekɔfaa afunu bi, mefaa afunu no, na mefaa nnipa a wɔwɔ kurow bi mu".

Ananse koduu ɔsoro Nyankopɔn anim, na ɔsoro Nyankopɔn bisaa Ananse asɛm no. Ananse kae sɛ: Me nsa aka awifua biako, na mekae sɛ, mɛba kurow bi mu, na kurow bi mu nnipa gyina hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ: Kamfo no. Ɔkaa sɛ: "Dɛn na memfa mma wo sɛ aseda?" Saa asɛm yi (m'asɛm) no, wubewu ahia mu, wo a wokɔɔ de nneɛma pii bae, nanso woka sɛ wunni hwee akyi". Ɛno nti na ohia remma Ananse so da.

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
