ASƐM A ƐDA SO Sɛnea Ananse, Ahemfo, Fii Ase Yɛɛ Ohia

KWAKU ANANSE, Spider no fii hɔ kɔɔ Nyankonpon, Ɔsoro-nyame no nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Ma me awifua baako na memfa nkuraa a nnipa wom nyinaa mmra mma wo. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Wontumi. Spider no buae sɛ, Sɛ mentumi a, mehyɛ wo maame Nsia bɔ sɛ, mede me ti bɛtua me ntam a midii no so ka. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, fa (akwansode no) na kɔyɛ Spider no fii hɔ kɔɔ akuraa bi ase. Ɔkaa asɛm a edidi so yi kyerɛɛ ɔsahene no. Ɔsoro-nyame no somaa me; ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ mede aburow koro yi kɔ baabi, na sɛ meba ( baabiara) a, ɛsɛ sɛ mema aburow koro yi ne nnomaa no kɔda. Ɔsahene no kae sɛ, E! Ananse buae sɛ, Nea ɛsɛ sɛ woyɛ ara ne sɛ wobɛma wɔn nyinaa ada. Ɔkannifo no kae sɛ, Kɔ na fa to hɔ. Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu no, Ananse kae sɛ ɔrebɛhwehwɛ no, nanso (aburow koro) no nni hɔ bio. Spider no kae sɛ, Head-man, nnomaa no adi Sky-gods awi. Head-man no kae sɛ, Me ntumi nnye akyinnye biara wɔ eyi ho, enti fa nnomaa no kɔ. Ananse kɔfaa nnomaa kɛse biako, na ɔne no fii hɔ kɔɔ akuraa foforo ase. Bere a oduu hɔ no, ɔkaa asɛm bi kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no sɛ, Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn somaa me sɛ memmɛda ha, na nnomaa a mehyɛ mu yi ne nguan no bɛda. Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, Momfa no mmra na momfa no mmra hɔ. Da no anadwo no, Ananse kɔɔ nnwan kraal hɔ kɔkum nnomaa no. Ɔfaa nnomaa no amoa na ɔtotoo no nnwan kɛse bi mmɛn so. Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu no, ɔkɔɔ ɔpanyin no nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Fa nnomaa no ma me". Bere a wɔrekɔ kɔfaa no no, hwɛ, na nnomaa no awu. Ananse kae sɛ, "Ah! ɛno ne ɔsoro nyame nnomaa a nguan no akum no". Ɔkɔɔ ɔpanyin no fie, na ɔpanyin no kae sɛ: "Asɛm bɛn?" Spider no kae sɛ: "Ɔsoro nyame asoma me, na me na mikura nnwan no; ɛne anantwi no bɛdeda faako". ɔpanyin no kae sɛ: "Ho! Ɔkaa sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔto no wɔ hɔ". Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔkɔ na ɔto no wɔ hɔ". Ananse kɔkum nnwan no anadwo, na ɔde ne mogya hwiee anantwi no mu baako ti. Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu no, Ananse kae sɛ: "Owura, ma me me nguan". Wɔkɔɔ kraal no hɔ; na nguan no awu. Ananse kae sɛ: "Owura, ɛyɛ ɔsoro Nyankopɔn nguan a anantwi no akum no". Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ: "Mentumi ne wo nkasa wɔ asɛm yi ho, enti fa anantwi a okum no no no kɔ". Ananse faa anantwi no, na ofii hɔ kɔɔ akuraa foforo ase. Na ɔfaa anantwi no de kyekyeree no, na ɔdaa hɔ. Na ɔdaa hɔ no, wɔbɛka kyerɛɛ ɔtemmufoɔ no sɛ ne nkoa no mu baako awu. Ɔkannifo no kae sɛ: "Momfa no nkyene, na minni adaka". Spider no kae sɛ: "Me de, mepɛ, efisɛ ɔsoro-nyame no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, sɛ minya afunu a wɔrebɛtow akyene no a, ɛsɛ sɛ mede sika kɔtɔn". Nkuraasefo no kae sɛ: "Dabi". (Nanso) Ananse sii so dua sɛ: "Momfa no mmra me nkyɛn, na sɛ mokɔtɔn anantwi no a, wubenya sika kɔkɔɔ mfuturo a ɛsom asuanu. (Ɔsɛnkafo no kae sɛ: "Ɛyɛ nokware wɔ mu". Enti wotwaa amu no. Ananse faa amu no kɔe, na ofii hɔ kɔɔ akuraa foforo bi ase. Na bere a ɔbaa hɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no sɛ: "Ma me ba yi ne wo mma no nkɔda wɔ fie ha; na saa abofra yi nyɛ fɛ, na ɔtaa twa mframa anadwo, na sɛ mframa twa a, mma mma mma no mpire no". Na ɔbarima no awu nnansa, na ɔne ne mma no da hɔ. Afei wohui sɛ biribi yɛ hu, na wɔkae sɛ: "Abofra no anya mframa, momma yɛmmɛbɔ no". Wɔhwehwee no so, titim! titim! titim! (sɛ wɔhwehwee no) Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu no, Ananse kɔɔ ne ba no nkyɛn kɔfrɛɛ no. Bere a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, ohuu no sɛ wawu. Ananse kae sɛ: "Onipa panyin, wo mma kunkum ɔsoro Nyankopɔn bɔfo a ɔne me wɔ hɔ". Ɔnipa panyin no kae sɛ: "Minni ho kwan sɛ migyina asɛm yi ho, enti fa wɔn kɔ". Spider kae sɛ: "Minni ho kwan, gye sɛ wo ne kurom hafo nyinaa kɔ ɔsoro Nyankopɔn anim kɔkyerɛ wo asɛm". Enti bere a Ananse duu kurow no akyi no, ɔsomaa ɔbɔfo sɛ ɔnkɔka nkyerɛ Ananse sɛ ɔne nkurow no mu nnipa reba, na ɔfrɛɛ ne nkurɔfo nyinaa baa ne nkyɛn bɛtenaa hɔ. Bere a Ananse kɔɔ hɔ no, ɔmaa ne dwom no so sɛ: "Ananse, ah, ah, mewɔ awifua biako a ɛsɛ sɛ mekɔ kɔfa nnomaa bi.

Ananse, awerɛhosɛm, awerɛhosɛm, mede nnomaa no kɔfaa oguan.

Mefaa nguan no, na mekɔfaa anantwi bi, mefaa anantwi no, na mekɔfaa afunu bi, mefaa afunu no, na mekɔfaa kurow bi mu nnipa".

Obiara kae sɛ, "Wote nnwom a Ananse reto no anaa?" Ananse maa ne dwom no so bio:

"Ananse, ah, ah, mewɔ aduaba biako pɛ a ɛsɛ sɛ mekɔfa so nya nnomaa.

Ananse, awerɛhosɛm, awerɛhosɛm, mede nnomaa no kɔfaa oguan.

Mefaa nguan no, na mekɔfaa anantwi bi, mefaa anantwi no, na mekɔfaa afunu bi, mefaa afunu no, na mekɔfaa kurow bi mu nnipa".

Ananse kɔɔ ɔsoro Nyankopɔn anim, na ɔbisaa Ananse asɛm no. Ananse kae sɛ: "Minyaa awifua biako, na mekae sɛ me ne nkurow bi mufo bɛba, na nkurow no mufo gyina hɔ". Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ: Dɛn na memfa mma wo sɛ aseda afɔrebɔ? Nhwiren no kae sɛ: Mempɛ hwee; (bea) wɔ ɔdan no so nko ara na mepɛ. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ: Oh, oh, saa nipa yi (me de) yi, wubewu hia mu, wo a wokɔɔ na wode nneɛma pii bae, nanso woka sɛ wompɛ hwee. Ɛno nti ohia remfa Ananse ho da.

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
