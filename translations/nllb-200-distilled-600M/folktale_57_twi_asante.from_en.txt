CHILD-KWASI-GYINAMOA

BERE bi na abofra bi wɔ hɔ a wɔfrɛ no Kwasi Gyinamoa, na na ɔwɔ anuanom mmea baawɔtwe, na wɔn mu biara nni hɔ a ɔwɔ okunu, enti wofii adi kae sɛ wɔrebɛkɔ baabi akɔhwehwɛ okununom. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ: Me ne wo bɛkɔ. Wobuae sɛ: Yɛn ne wo nkɔ. Wɔn mu panyin no kae sɛ: Ma ɔne yɛn nkɔ, na sɛ yɛkɔ (kwan no so yiye) na nsɔe bi twa yɛn mu a, obetumi ayi ama yɛn. Wofii adi, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa ne wɔn kɔe. Na wɔbaa asubɔnten bi so, na emu bi te sɛ dɔte fitaa, na emu bi te sɛ dɔte kɔkɔɔ, na emu bi nso te sɛ dɔte tuntum, na wɔkae sɛ: Ɛdɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ na yɛatwa mu? Wɔkɔɔ so, na na ɔbea panyin bi wɔ hɔ a ne ti da ne nan so, na na ɔhwehwɛ nsɔe wɔ mu. Bere a ohui sɛ Ɔba-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, ɔmaa ne ti so na ɔsan de no kaa ho; ɔkae sɛ, Me mmanana, sɛ obiara mma ha a, adɛn nti na moaba ha? Ɔba-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, Me nuabeanom yɛ baawɔtwe, na wonni kunu a wɔde bɛware wɔn, enti wɔkae sɛ wɔbɛba abɛhwehwɛ okununom a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔware wɔn. Ɔbaabun panyin no kae sɛ, Oh, ɛyɛ papa, efisɛ me nso mewɔ mma nwɔtwe. Ɔbaabun panyin no somaa ne babea panyin sɛ ɔnkɔfrɛ ne mma. Bere a wɔn baanu no bae no, ɔfoforo no kae sɛ, "Me kunu ni", na ɔfoforo no kae sɛ, "Me kunu ni". Bere a wɔn nyinaa twaa wɔn ho hyiae wiei no, ɔbaa panyin no kɔfaa fɛfɛ bi a wɔfrɛ no _dokum_, na ɔde kyekyeree ne mma no kɔn mu. Afei wɔn mu biara kɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn kɔdae. Na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔɔ ɔbaa panyin no nkyɛn kɔhome. Bere a ade sae no, ɔbaa panyin no hyee n'ano a ɛyɛ kɔkɔɔ kɔkɔɔ kɔkum mmabaa no. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, E! e! Ɔbea no kae sɛ, Ɛdeɛn ne saa? Ɔbea no buaa sɛ, Sɛ mewɔ me kurom, na mekɔhome nanso mintumi nna a, ɛnneɛ ɛna no twaa yam bi na ɔde kɔtoo ogya mu, na ɔfaa afoa bi a wɔatutu na ɔkɔhwie nsuo guu mu de brɛɛ me sɛ mennom. Ɔbea no nanabaa no fii hɔ kɔɔ asubɔnten no so, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kɔtwee fɛfɛ bi a wɔfrɛ no _dokum_ fii ɔbaa no mma no kɔn mu, na ɔfaa no de no sɛn ne nuabeanom so. Ɔbaa no san fii nsu no mu bae; ɔkae sɛ, Mebɔɔ mmɔden sɛ mɛpopa nsu (wɔ ɔtare no mu), nanso mantumi. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, Kɔ na kɔfa dɔte bi, na kɔtotow nhaban na kɔto mu (wɔ ɔtare no mu), na kɔfa na kɔpopa. Ɔbaa no kɔe na ɔyɛɛ saa, na ɔpopae nsu wɔ mu, na ɛyɛɛ yiye. Ɔfaa no, na ɔfaa yam no fii ogya no mu, na ɔde maa Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa sɛ ɔnni, na ɔno nso nom nsu no. Afei ɔtow ne ho hwee fam na ɔdae. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ɛyɛ me. Ɔmaa ne nsa hyerɛn, (kosii sɛ wɔn hyerɛn) kɔkɔɔ, na ɔne wɔn kɔe kɔhyɛn dan no mu; ɔde ne nsa kaa mmofra no kɔn ho, na nea _dokum_ nsɛso nni ne kɔn mu no, ɔde ne nsa hyee ne nsa. Ɔnam saa kwan yi so kunkum mmofra baawɔtwe no. Onnim sɛ ɛyɛ ne mma ankasa a ɔkum wɔn no. Bere a nneɛma no fii ase hyerɛn no, ͻyɛ, ͻyɛ, ͻyɛ, ͻyɛ, ͻyɛ, ͻyɛ, ͻyɛ, ͻhyɛe sɛ ͻbɛkɔ afuw no mu. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuanom na ɔmaa wɔn guanee. Ná me ne me nuabeanom rekɔ. Ná onii a onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi dwuma) no wɔ hɔ. Ɔkɔeɛ, na Ná onii no nso yɛɛ ne ho sɛ ababaa fɛfɛ, na ɔkyeree kurobɔ, na ɔkɔɔ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa akuraa ase. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Onipa a ɔbɛtumi de agyan akyekyere yi, mɛware no". Nnipa a wɔwɔ kuro no mu no nyinaa bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ wɔbɛkɔ akyekyere no, nanso wɔantumi. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Maware wo". Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, merenware wo". Enti ne wɔfa waree no. Ɔkɔɔ ne nkyɛn kɔdae, nanso ɔtwee n'ani mmienu no mu, na ɔne wɔn kɔe. Afei ababaa no kae sɛ, "Maware wo!" Na meyii w'adamfo no ani abien, na me ne no rekɔ". Ɔkaa sɛ: "Fa na kɔ, na nea onim no wɔ hɔ". Na ade kyee no, bere a nneɛma no fii adi no, wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Me ba, kɔ na kɔfa w'adamfo no ani mmra". Na ɔkae sɛ: "Merentumi nkɔ". Afei, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nim sɛ Nananom-Nananom babea no yɛ _enceinte_, enti ɔno nso, ɔfaa bi kaa ne mpa so, na ɔkyekyeree n'atade so, na ofii ase kɔɔ Nananom-Nananom akuraa no ase. Bere a oduu kurow no awiei no, osui. Nananom no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na asi?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me ne me kunu reba, na bere a odii bepɔw no atifi no, ɔhwehwee ase, na ɛno maa n'ani abien no fii adi na ɛhwehwee ase". Nananom no kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ papa, nea ɛwɔ hɔ ne nea ɛwɔ benkum ani a onifuraefo a wɔwoo no foforo no, Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, n'adamfo no ani, na nea ɛwɔ hɔ nso ne nea ɛwɔ nifa ani. Sɛ wokɔ hɔ a, ka sɛ, 'M'ani gye, na m'ani da hɔ.' " Bere a abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nsa kaa wɔn no, ntɛm ara na ɔkae sɛ, "Wo, Ɔbabaa-Nokware, eyinom ne m'adamfo aniwa a wofaa mu, na me ne wɔn rekɔ". Ɔbabaa-Nokware no kae sɛ, "Ɛmfa ho, nea onim (sɛnea ɛsɛ sɛ ɔne wo di) no wɔ hɔ". Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu no, mmofra no nyinaa (wɔ akuraa no mu) kɔɔ asu no mu kɔhohoroo. Na abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ: "Menni hohoroo". Wɔkae sɛ: "Ebetumi aba sɛ wowɔ amu dua bi?" Bere a wɔkaa saa no, ne nufu yɛɛ den, na ɔhohoroo. Bere a ɔtu kɔɔ nsu no mu no, Ɔpanyin-Abea-Nokuru no kyeree no, mmienu. Ɔkae sɛ: "Ɔpanyin-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, me nsa aka wo", na ɔne no kɔɔ ne kurom. Ɛnna Ɔpanyin-Abea-Nokuru no ne no kɔe kɔfaa kotoku bi, na ɔkataa no so, na ɔkataa no so, na ɔkyekyeree nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn, na ɔhyew wɔn. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Merekɔ mmonnua abusuakuo no mu, merekɔ nnuhwam abusuakuo no mu, na afei mɛba abɛkum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa". Ɔfirii hɔ, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa boaboaa aboɔ no ano, na ɔfirii ase sɛresɛe wɔn, pupru! pupru! Ɔtoo aduan no wiei, na wɔtenaa ase wɔ nkyɛn, na wɔdidii. Ɔbea no nanabea no hwiee apiti no (wɔ n'ano no mu wɔ dede kɛse mu), fweo! fweo! Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ: "Wo hwiee apiti no (wɔ w'anom) mu". Ɔbea no nanabaa no kae sɛ: "Dɛn na woka?" Ɔbuaa sɛ: "Mekae sɛ nkyene biara nni apiti no mu". Ɔbea no nanabaa no kae sɛ: "Kɔ na kɔfa bi". Enti ɔkɔe kɔfaa bi. Wɔdidii wiei no, Ɔbea Kunini-Nanan kae sɛ: "Afei a makum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, bra ha, me banana". Bere a ɔbae no, ɔfaa sika kɔkɔɔ nketewa na ɔhyehyɛɛ no fɛfɛɛfɛ. Afei, Ɔbea Kunini-Nanan hwɛɛ n'anim kakra. Ɔkae sɛ: "Ah! abofra yi anim kaee me sɛ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa". Abofra no kae sɛ: "Nananom, sɛ wunim sɛ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa bae na okum m'abusuafo baawɔtwe no nyinaa (nea woka no saa a), efisɛ wohaw me". Ɔbea Kunini-Nanan kae sɛ: "Munyi w'ano". Afei abofra no kyekyee ne ho wɔ fie hɔ. Ɔbea no yii no fii hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ: "Ma menkɔ na menkɔtow no ngu mfinimfini". Enti Kwasi-Gyinamoa de no kɔe, na bere a oduu mfinimfini no, oyii sika kɔkɔɔ a ɔbaa no de bɔɔ ne ho ban no nyinaa fii hɔ, na ɔkyekyeree wɔn. Afei ɔkae sɛ, "Ho there, Old-Woman-Grandmother, ho there, Old-Woman-Grandmother, me ne no, Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, afei ne mprenu so, maba abɛkum wo babarima, na ɛno akyi no, makɔfa wo sika ahosiesie". Old-Woman-Grandmother buae sɛ, "Mekae sɛ w'anim te sɛ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa; afei baabiara a wobɛkɔ no, me ne wo bɛkɔ". Afei wɔn baanu nyinaa guanee, kiri! kiri! Kiri! Child! Kiri! Kwasi-Gyinamoa duu ne kurom, na ɔde sika kɔkɔɔ no maa ne mogya-mmusuakuw, na ɔkɔɔ ɔkwan no so, na ɔde ne ho siee, na Old-Woman-Grandmother nso baa hɔ. Ɛno nti, sɛ wokɔ ɔkwan bi so a, wobɛte nne bi a ɛte sɛ, "pe". Ɛno ne Ɔbea a Ɔyɛ Ɔpanyin-Nanan a ɔretutu Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa.
