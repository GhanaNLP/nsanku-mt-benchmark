Nea yɛreka yi nyɛ nokware.

CHILD-KWASI-GYINAMOA

BERE bi na abofra bi wɔ hɔ a wɔfrɛ no Kwasi Gyinamoa, na na ɔwɔ anuanom mmea baawɔtwe, na wɔn mu biara nni hɔ a ɔwɔ ɔhokafo, enti wofii ase kae sɛ wɔrekɔ baabi akɔhwehwɛ okununom. Ɔkaa sɛ: "Me ne mo bɛkɔ". Wɔbuaa sɛ: "Yɛn ne mo rentumi nkɔ". Wɔn mu panyin no kaa sɛ: "Ma ɔne yɛn nkɔ, na sɛ yɛkɔ kwan so a, na otwa bi twa yɛn a, ɔbɛtwe". Enti wɔkɔe, na ɔbaa no ne wɔn kɔe. Na wɔbaa asubɔnten bi so, na emu bi te sɛ dɔte fitaa, na emu bi te sɛ dɔte kɔkɔɔ, na emu bi te sɛ ɔtankɔkɔɔ, na wɔkae sɛ: Ɛdɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ na yɛatwa mu? Wɔbaa kurow bi mu; na ɛyɛ kurow kɛse paa. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, Kurow no yɛ dinn (i.e. ɛhɔ yɛ dinn gye cicada a ɛrebɔ no nti), so onipa biara nni hɔ wɔ mu? Afei nnomaa bi kasae sɛ, Obi wɔ fie hɔ, kɔ so. Wɔkɔɔ so, na ɔbea panyin bi a ne ti da ne nan so wɔ hɔ, na ɔhwehwɛɛ nwene wɔ mu. Bere a ohui sɛ Ɔba-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, ɔmaa ne ti so na ɔsan de no kaa ho; ɔkae sɛ, Me mmanana, adɛn nti na, bere a obiara mma ha, moaba ha? Ɔba-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, Me nuabeanom yɛ awotwe, na wonni kunu a wɔde bɛware wɔn, enti wɔkae sɛ wɔbɛba abɛhwehwɛ okununom a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔware wɔn. Ɔbaabun panyin no kae sɛ, Oh, saa ara na ɛte. Me nso mewɔ mma baawɔtwe. Ɔbea a ne ho akokwaw somaa ne babea sɛ ɔnkɔfrɛ ne mma. Bere a wɔbaa hɔ no, biako kae sɛ, Me kunu ni, biako kae sɛ, Me kunu ni. Bere a wɔn nyinaa wiei sɛ wɔhyehyɛ no, ɔbaa a ne ho akokwaw no kɔfaa fɛfɛɛfɛ bi a wɔfrɛ no _dokum_, na ɔde kyekyeree ne mma no kɔn mu. Afei wɔn mu biara kɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn kɔdae. Na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔɔ akwakoraa no nkyɛn kɔhomee. Bere a ade sae no, ɔbaa no hyee n'ano, kɔkɔɔ kɔkɔɔ kɔkɔɔ, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obekum mmabaa no. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, E! e! e! Ɔbea no bisae sɛ, Ɛdeɛn ne saa? Ɔbea no buaa sɛ, Ɛberɛ a mewɔ me kurom, na mekɔhome nanso mintumi nna no, ɛnneɛ ɛna no twaa yam baako na ɔde kɔtoo ogya mu, na ɔfaa ɔtadeɛ bi a wɔatutu na ɔkɔhwe nsuo mu de brɛɛ me sɛ mennom. Ɔbea no nanabaa no fii hɔ kɔɔ asuten no ho, na Ɔba no kɔɔ hɔ na ɔtwee fɛfɛ bi a wɔfrɛ no _dokdok na ɔfaa wɔn de bɛsɛn ne nuabeanom so. Ɔbea no san fii nsu no mu bae; ɔkae sɛ, Mebɔɔ mmɔden sɛ mɛpopa nsu (wɔ saa abura no mu), nanso mantumi. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, Kɔ na kɔfa dɔte bi, na kɔtotow nnua na kɔto (abura no mu), na kɔfa na kɔpopa. Ɔbea no kɔe na ɔyɛɛ saa, na ɔpopa nsu wɔ mu, na na eye. Na ɔde brɛɛ no, na oyii yam no fii ogya no mu de maa Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa sɛ ɔnni, na ɔno nso nom nsu no. Na ɔtow ne ho hwee fam kɔdae. Ɔbea a ne ho akokwaw no sɔre kae sɛ, Ɛyɛ me. Ɔmaa ne nsa hyerɛn, (kosii sɛ wɔn hyerɛn) kɔkɔɔ, na ɔne wɔn kɔe kɔhyɛn dan no mu; ɔde ne nsa kaa mmofra no kɔn ho, na nea _dokum_ nsonsonoe nni ne kɔn so no, ɔde ne nsa hyew. Saa kwan yi so na okunkum mmofra baawɔtwe no. Onnim sɛ ne mma ankasa na okunkum wɔn. Bere a nneɛma fii ase hyerɛn no, Ɔbaa Nkwakoraa-Nananom fii ase kɔɔ afuw no mu. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuabeanom na ɔma woguanee. Ɔbaa Nkwakoraa-Nananom fii afuw no mu bae. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, Ɔbaa Nkwakoraa-Nananom e! Ɔbaa Nkwakoraa-Nananom e! sɛ monnyɛ nkwaseafo a, anka mobekum mo mma? Me de, me ne me nuabeanom rebɛkɔ. Ɔbea a ne ho akokwaw no kae sɛ, Ɛmfa ho hwee, nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi dwuma) no wɔ hɔ. Ɔkɔeɛ, na Ɔbea a ne ho akokwaw no nso yɛɛ ne ho sɛ ababaa fɛfɛ bi, na ɔkyeree kurobɛnka, na ɔkɔɔ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa akuraa ase. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Onipa a ɔbɛtumi de agyan akyekyere yi, mɛware no". Nnipa a wɔwɔ kuro no mu no nyinaa bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ wɔbɛkɔ akyekyere no, nanso wɔantumi. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Maware wo". Ɔkaa sɛ, "Maware wo". Child-Kwasi-Gynamoa kae sɛ, "Me deɛ, merenware wo". Enti n'adamfo waree no. Ɔkɔɔ ne nkyɛn kɔdedae, nanso ɔtwee n'ani mmienu no fii hɔ, na ɔtwee ne ho fii hɔ. Na meyii w'adamfo no ani abien, na me ne no rekɔ". Ɔkaa sɛ: "Fa na kɔ, na nea onim no wɔ hɔ". Na ade kyee no, bere a nneɛma no fii adi no, wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Me ba, kɔ na kɔfa w'adamfo no ani mmra". Na ɔkae sɛ: "Merentumi nkɔ". Afei, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nim sɛ Nananom-Nananom babea no yɛ _enceinte_, enti ɔno nso, ɔfaa bi kaa ne mpa so, na ɔkyekyeree ne ntama so, na ofii ase kɔɔ Nananom-Nananom nkuraa ase. Bere a oduu kurow no awiei no, osui. Nananom no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na asi?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me ne me kunu reba, na bere a odii bepɔw no atifi no, ɔhwehwee ase, na ɛno maa n'ani abien no puee na ɛhwehwee ase". Nananom no kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ papa, nea ɛwɔ hɔ ne nea ɛwɔ benkum ani a onifuraefo a wɔwoo no foforo no, Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, n'adamfo no ani, ne nea ɛwɔ nifa ani nso. Na bere a abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nsa kaa wɔn no, ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ: "Me nana, eyinom ne m'adamfo aniwa a wofaa mu, na me ne wɔn rekɔ". -Gyinamoa baa fie, na ɔfaa n'ani na ɔsan de hyɛɛ ne wɔfa so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "M'ani bu bɔɔ mu, m'ani tew", na n'ani buee pefee. Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma fii ase yerae no, mmofra no nyinaa (wɔ akuraa no ase) kɔbɔɔ wɔn ho ban wɔ asuten no mu. Kwasi-Gyinamoa ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Merenhohoro". Wɔkae sɛ: "Ebetumi aba sɛ wowɔ abosom dua bi". Bere a wɔkaa saa no, ne nufu yɛɛ den, na ɔhohoroo. Bere a ɔkɔɔ nsu no mu no, onuawa a ne ho akokwaw no kyeree no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Kwasi-Gyinamoa, me nsa aka wo", na ɔne no kɔɔ ne kurom. Na ɔbaa nanabaa no kɔɔ, na ɔfaa kotoku bi, na ɔkataa no so, na ɔfaa nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn, na ɔde guu ogya so. Na ɔkae sɛ: Mɛkɔ akɔbere abusuakuw no mu, mɛkɔbere abusuakuw no mu, na afei mɛba abɛkum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa. Na ofii hɔ, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa boaboaa aboɔ ano, na ofii ase sɛ ɔresɛe wɔn, pupru! pupru! Nananom babea no babea bisae sɛ: "Ɛdeɛn na worekum yi?" Na ɔkae sɛ: "Nadweaa". Na ɔkae sɛ: "Momma me bi nkum". Na ɔkae sɛ: "Kyerɛw me". Na ɔkae sɛ: "Wobɛkɔ". Na ɔkae sɛ: "Dabi, merenkɔ". Na ɔkyerɛw no, na ofii adi. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa aduaba no, na ɔhwee Ɔbabaa Nkwakoraa no nanabea, na okum no. Afei ɔtwee ne nanabea no honam fii ne ho, na ɔhyehyɛɛ ne ho. Nananom no san bae; obisaa no sɛ: "Adɛn nti na mo nyinaa ho agu fi saa?" Na obuae sɛ: "Mekum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa; mede no asi gya mu". Asɛm yi yɛɛ fɛ maa Nananom-Nananom, na osii dan de ne nne so (na ɔtoo dwom): "Ɔba, wokum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, da bi wobɛyɛ obi".

Ɔtoo aduan no wiei, na wɔtenaa ase, na wodidii. Mmofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ: "Woretew sop (wɔ w'anom) a wɔayɛ afi wo ba no ti mu". Ɔbea panyin-nananom no kae sɛ: "Dɛn na woka?" Ɔbuaa sɛ: "Mekae sɛ nkyene biara nni sopii yi mu". Ɔbea panyin-nananom no kae sɛ: "Kɔ na kɔfa bi". Enti ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔfaa bi. Bere a wowiee adidi no, onuawa no nanabea kae sɛ: "Afei a makum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, bra ha, me banana". Bere a ɔbaa hɔ no, ɔfaa sika kɔkɔɔ nkatabo na ɔhyehyɛɛ no fɛfɛɛfɛ. Afei onuawa no nanabea no hwɛɛ n'anim kakra. Ɔkae sɛ: "Ah! abofra yi anim kaee me sɛ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa". Abofra no kae sɛ, "Nananom, sɛ wunim sɛ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa baa na okum m'abusuafo baawɔtwe no nyinaa a (woka saa a), ɛma m'ani gye". Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: Ma menkɔ na menkɔtow no nkyene". Enti Kwasi-Ginamoa de no kɔe, na bere a oduu nkyene no, oyii sika kɔkɔɔ a ɔbaa no de bɔɔ ne ho ban no nyinaa, na ɔkyekyeree wɔn. Afei ɔkae sɛ, "Ho there, Old-woman-nananom, ho there, Old-woman-nananom, me ne no, Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, afei ne mprenu so, maba abɛkum wo babarima, na ɛno akyi no, mefaa wo sika ahosiesie". Old-woman-nananom buae sɛ, "Mekae sɛ w'anim te sɛ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa; afei baabiara a wobɛkɔ no, me ne wo bɛkɔ". Afei wɔn baanu nyinaa guanee, kiri! kiri! Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa duu ne nkuraa ase, na ɔde sikakɔkɔɔ nnwinnade no maa ne mogya-abusuafo, na ɔkɔɔ ɔkwan no so, na ɔde ne ho hintaw wɔ n'anim, na Ɔbea a Ɔyɛ Ɔpanyin-Nanan nso baa hɔ. Ɛno nti, bere a wufi ɔkwan bi so rekɔ no, wobɛte dede a ɛte sɛ "pe". Ɛno ne Ɔbea a Ɔyɛ Ɔpanyin-Nanan a ɔretu Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa akyidi.

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
