Bere a afe 1926 reba no, Yehowa nam Kristo a ɔyɛ asafo no ti no so daa no adi pefee sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ asɛmpaka adwuma no yɛ ade titiriw a ɛsɛ sɛ Kristoni de si n'ani so wɔ n'asetena mu.

Nhoma Foforo Ne Nhyehyɛe a Wɔde Kɔ Nhyiam Ahorow Ase

Wɔ April mu no, The Golden Age (seesei wɔfrɛ no Nyan!) bɔɔ amanneɛ sɛ nhoma foforo bi a wɔato din Agyenkwa no bɛba. Ná nhoma yi besi Studies in the Scriptures ananmu, na ɛno ne nhoma titiriw a wɔde yɛ asɛnka adwuma no. Nea ɛbɛyɛ na anuanom a wɔwɔ Betel no atumi anya sika a wɔde tintim nhoma foforo a wobetumi atintim nhoma 22 da biara no. Bible Asuafo no de nhoma foforo no mae bere biara a wobetumi.

Bere a na Bible Asuafo no rekasa wɔ wɔn nhyiam ahorow ase mfe pii no, wofii ase suaa Ɔwɛn-Aban mu nsɛm no. Dɛn na efii mu bae?

Bunker Hill, Illinois ecclesia [asafo] nketewa no kyia mo, asafo biako kyerɛw kɔmaa Onua Rutherford wɔ krataa bi mu. Kwasida biara yesua [nsɛm] a Asafo no de asi hɔ ama saa bere no. Yɛda no ase sɛ yɛn nyinaa betumi ahu hann foforo no bere a ɛsɛ mu.

Hann foforo, anaa ntease foforo boaa Bible Asuafo no ma wɔde asɛnka adwuma no sii wɔn ani so. Sɛ nhwɛso no, asɛm a wɔato din Character or CovenantWhich? no kyerɛkyerɛɛ mu sɛ character development, a wɔde wɔn adwene sii ɔfɛyɛ ne ayamye so nkutoo so no maa ebinom buu nokware Kristofo adwuma no so. Asɛm no bɔɔ kɔkɔ sɛ: Deɛ ɔdɔ Awurade no . . . ɛsɛ sɛ odi n'ahyɛde so seesei. . . . Yehowa ka sɛ: Mone m'adansefo sɛ mene Onyankopɔn. . . .

Asɛm foforo a wɔato din Sacrifice and Service, sii so dua sɛ nokware Kristofo de nokwaredi som Yehowa de kyerɛ sɛ wɔdɔ no. Asɛm no hyɛɛ Bible Asuafo nyinaa nkuran sɛ wɔmfa nsi ne ahokeka nyɛ Awurade som adwuma no, na 'wɔnnyɛ adansefo mma Awurade, na wɔnka n'asɛm nkyerɛ amanaman ne nnipa a wɔwɔ asase so.' - Yes. 43:10, 12; Mat. 24:14.

2 ƆWƐN-ABAN

Nsakrae yi nyaa nsunsuanso ntɛm ara wɔ asafo ahorow no mu. Nnawɔtwe kakraa bi akyi no, Ɔwɛn-Aban a ɛbae July 15, 1926 no tintimii krataa yi: Nsɛm a wɔatintim nnansa yi, Karakter or CovenantWhich? ne Sacrifice and Service, yɛ nkuranhyɛ ne awerɛkyekye kɛse. Ɛnsɛ sɛ yɛkenkan yɛn Ɔwɛn-Aban kɛkɛ seesei, ɛsɛ sɛ yesua wɔn. Krataa no wiee sɛ daa adi sɛ obiara pɛ sɛ ɔda ne nsusuwii adi pefee wɔ adesua no mu dedaw, na sɛ ɛnte saa a, ɛreyɛ adwuma pa ara wɔ yɛn asɔre no so.

ASƐM A ƐDA SO ASƐM A Yɛde Ka Asɛm No

Wɔ May awiei no, Bible Asuafo pii hyiae wɔ nhyiam bi ase wɔ London, England. Adwumayɛfo atuatew bi a wɔyɛe wɔ Britain no maa ɛyɛɛ den sɛ wobegye aguadifo a wotintim nhoma no adwuma, enti anuanom a wɔwɔ Brooklyn Betel no yɛɛ adwumaden de nhomawa bi a wɔato din The Standard for the People no maa nhyiam no.

Wɔ Memeneda, May 29 no, wɔde saa nhomawa no dii dwuma yiye. Nhyiam no dwumadi no hyɛɛ nkuran sɛ: Wɔto nsa frɛ wɔn a wɔahyira wɔn ho so nyinaa sɛ wɔmmɛka Ɔhene no ne Ahenni no ho dawuru.

Wɔkyekyɛɛ nhoma bɛboro 100,000. Ɔwɛn-Aban a ɛbae July 15, 1926, no bɔɔ amanneɛ sɛ: Nnamfo no ani gyei paa. Wɔtee nka sɛ wɔayɛ nea wobetumi biara sɛ wobedi Yehowa ahyɛde so: Mone m'adansefo sɛ mene Onyankopɔn.

Ade kyee no, Onua Rutherford kasae wɔ Royal Albert Hall wɔ asɛmti a ɛne Why World Powers Are SwingingThe Remedy no so. Bere a wobuee apon no akyi simma kakraa bi no, nnipa 13,000 hyɛɛ asa no ma.

Onua Rutherford kyerɛkyerɛɛ mu wɔ ne kasa no mu sɛ, esiane sɛ wiase atumfoɔ no poo Yehowa ne N'Ahenni no nti, wɔsɛee wiase atumfoɔ no. Onua Rutherford kae sɛ Amanaman Nkabom no nni hɔ bio.

Yehowa pɛ sɛ anuanom a wɔde wɔn ho to wɔn so a wonim sɛnea wɔde Bible nnyinasosɛm bɔ wɔn bra di n'ahyehyɛde no anim. (Deut. 1:13-15) Sɛ wode da no asɛm yɛ wo som adwuma a, ɛbɛma woahu saa nnyinasosɛm no na woasiesie wo ama dwumadi foforo.

Fa Wo Gyidi Si Nnyinaso Pintinn So

BERE a wo ne Yehowa Adansefo suaa Bible no, dɛn na ɛma wuhui sɛ woanya gyidi biako, ɔsom a ɛsɔ Onyankopɔn ani no? (Efe. 4:4-6) Esiane ɔdɔ a wohuu wɔ wɔn mu nti, Yehowa nkurɔfo twee wo baa wɔn nkyɛn anaa?

EDWƐKƐ BƐN A ƐMA YƐYƐ GYIDI

Sɛ wugye nokware a wusua fi Bible mu no di yiye a, wubetumi ahyɛ wo gyidi den.

Bere bi, Yesu asuafo no mu pii gyaw no hɔ kɔe. Ná wɔabotow n'asɛm a ɔkae sɛ wonni ne honam na wɔnnom ne mogya no so. Wɔkae sɛ: "Nsɛm yi yɛ nwonwa". Yesu asɛm no haw wɔn paa ma wofii ase dii n'akyi. - Yohane 6:56-60, 66.

Akyiri yi, Yesu bisaa n'asomafo no sɛ: "Mo nso mopɛ sɛ mokɔ anaa?" Petro buae sɛ: "Awurade, hena nkyɛn na yɛbɛkɔ? Wo na wowɔ daa nkwa nsɛm; na yɛagye adi, na yɛahu sɛ wone Onyankopɔn Ɔkronkronni no". - Yohane 6:67-69.

Dɛn na ɛmaa Petro ne asomafo a aka no yɛɛ soronko wɔ wɔn a wɔhwehwee no ho? Ná wɔn gyidi gyina biribi a ɛyɛ den sen wɔn nkate so. Petro kae sɛ: "Yegye di na yenim sɛ wone Onyankopɔn Kronkronni no".

Dɛn na saa kyerɛwtohɔ no kyerɛkyerɛ yɛn? Nea yenim no betumi ayɛ kɛse asen sɛnea yɛte nka no. Dɛn ntia? Efisɛ nokware a yɛasua afa Onyankopɔn din, n'atirimpɔw, ne agyede no ho no ka Bible nkyerɛkyerɛ ho.
