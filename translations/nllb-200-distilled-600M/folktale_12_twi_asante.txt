KOKU ANANANSE had four sons, Tikonokono, Afudotwedotwe, and Nyiwankonfwea, and we lived with Kotoko in the house. Kotoko "planted a field, and Kwaku Ananse smiled and asked the uncle not to drink. He gave her some. He said, "This is what I have found, tomorrow morning I will come to my work". So he moved, moved, moved, and the eagle fought; he fought his fields. The eagle "didn't know how good it was to stand, and he cried out, "Ginzaworowa, Kotoko sairowa, Ginzaworowa, Kotoko sairowa, Ginzaworowa".
