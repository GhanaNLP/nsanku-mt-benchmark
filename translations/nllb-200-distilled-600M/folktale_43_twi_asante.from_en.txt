ASƐM A ƐDA SO Bere a NWANSANA, Afoforo a Wɔetu Kwan no, Sii KRAMAN, Ɔkraman, Ɔkraman no So no, Ɛyɛɛ Dɛn na Ɔkaa Asɛm No?

NA ƆKUNTUFO bi wɔ nkraman anan; na wɔfrɛ no Sniff-sniff; na wɔfrɛ no Lick-lick; na wɔfrɛ no Tie-in-knots; na wɔfrɛ no Gulp-down. Afei ɔsan kɔɔ fie kɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ ne yere sɛ, "Kola dua bi a mahunu no wɔ hɔ a ɛsow nnua bebree". Ne yere ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔboaboa ano, ɛfiri sɛ wunim sɛ yɛnni kola dua baako mpo a yɛbɛka na yɛntɔn". Ɔpɔnkɔ no fii adi kɔɔ kwan so, nanso ogyaw nkraman no wɔ fie, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a kola dua no wɔ no. Afei ɔforo kɔɔ hɔ kɔfaa kola dua no bi. Afei ɔtee sɛ efi baabi no, "Twa! be! kukuru! kukuru! hu! pi!" Abonsam Sasa (gyina) n'anim. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Hwan na ɔtwee m'abɔnsam nnua no?" Ɔbɔfo no kae sɛ: "Me ara". Ɔbɔfo no kae sɛ: "Mebekum wo esiane nea woayɛ yi nti". Ɔbɔfo no kae sɛ: "Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wokum me a, ma memfrɛ me nkraman no". Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Momfrɛ, na sɛ mofrɛ wɔn mprɛnsa na wɔmmra a, mɛkum mo". Ɛnna ɔbɔfo no teɛɛm sɛ:

"Sniff-sniff, (ba na) hyia me, Lick-lick, (ba na) hyia me, Tie-in-knots, (ba na) hyia me, Gulp-down, (ba na) hyia me.

Na akraman a wɔwɔ fie no tee no no, woguan kɔɔ kurow no akyi. Afei ɔtowfo no san teɛɛm bio se,

Sniff-sniff, (ba na) kohyia me, Lick-lick, (ba na) kohyia me, Tie-in-knots, (ba na) kohyia me, Gulp-down, (ba na) kohyia me.

Afei nkraman no tee; wotuu mmirika, prada! prada! Sasabonsam kae sɛ, Frɛ bio, na eyi wiee ne mprɛnsa so. Ɔwodifo no teɛɛm (ne nne) dennen:

Sniff-sniff, (ba na) kohyia me, Lick-lick, (ba na) kohyia me, Tie-in-knots, (ba na) kohyia me, Gulp-down, (ba na) kohyia me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔrebɛba akyɛ! Bere a nkraman no wɔ n'anim no, na n'anom nnya nnhyinaa hɔ. Ɔpɔnkɔ no kae sɛ: "Sniff-sniff, hwɛ Sasabonsam, hu no". Na Sniff-sniff hwɛɛ no. Ɔkae sɛ: "Lick-lick, hohoro no". Ɔkae sɛ: "Tie-in-knots, kyekyere no". Tie-in-knots kyekyeree ne kɔn mu; na Sasabonsam hwee fam wɔ hɔ, kum! Afei Gulp-down faa no kɔe kɔkyeree no. Ɔpɔnkɔ no ne nkraman no boaboaa kola-nuts no ano de wɔn baa fie. Bere a ɔkɔmfo no san bae no, ɔboaboaa ɔfã bi a ne nsa ahyɛ mu ma, na ɔde too hɔ maa nkraman no. Afei ɔtwee nsu guu hɔ, na ɔde too hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn sɛ ɔrekɔdidi. Bere a owiee aduan didi no, ɔkɔɔ mmɔnten so kɔtenaa gyedua (dua) ase. Afei ɔkɔmfo no akoa Fly kɔɔ hɔ kɔhwɛɛ aduan no (a wɔasiesie ama nkraman no), na ɔtwee no de no too ɔdan no so. Nkraman no fii mmɔnten no mu bae, na na ɔkɔm de wɔn. Lick-lick ka kyerɛɛ wɔn a aka no sɛ, "Moahunu deɛ yɛayɛ ama yɛn wura no, nanso ɛnnɛ de, wamma yɛn aduane mpo a yɛbɛdi, enti momma yɛnkɔkum no". Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Wonnni atorɔ, wonni atorɔ". Lick-lick firii adi kɔkyekyeree awudifoɔ no amu, na ɔne awudifoɔ no dii agorɔ; na Sniff-sniff nso baa hɔ; na ɔtwee awudifoɔ no amu no ano; na ɔno nso firii hɔ kɔeɛ. Na apɔnkɔ no san kɔɔ fie; na wɔrebɛhwɛ pato no mu no, wohuu susukora bi wɔ hɔ, na wɔkae sɛ: Twi, ebia ɔkwafo no agyaw yɛn aduan, na mo nso moadi bi? Twi kae sɛ: Minhuu bi wɔ baabiara. Twi, bere a ɔmaa n'ani so no, ohuu aduan a ɛda hɔ no. Ɔkae sɛ: O! agya, yɛakum yɛn wura kwa, efisɛ aduan a ɔde asi hɔ ama yɛn no wɔ hɔ, na emu biako anaa abien biara nni hɔ, mmom Twi no na ɔfaa no de too hɔ. Twi fii hɔ kɔe. Ɛno nti, sɛ ɔkraman bi te hɔ na ɔkɔre si no so a, ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkyere no.

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
