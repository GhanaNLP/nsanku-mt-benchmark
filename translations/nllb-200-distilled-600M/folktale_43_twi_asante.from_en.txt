ASƐM A ƐDA SO Bere a NWANSANA, Afoforo no, de ne ho too KRAMAN, Ɔkraman no so no, ɔno nso de ne ho too no so, KAM! KAM!

NA ƆKUNTUFO bi wɔ nkraman anan; na wɔfrɛ no Sniff-sniff; na wɔfrɛ no Lick-lick; na wɔfrɛ no Tie-in-knots; na wɔfrɛ no Gulp-down. Afei ɔsan baa fie, na ɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ ne yere, kae sɛ, "Kola dua bi a mahu no, ɛsow aba pii a ɛsɛn nkotodwe te sɛ eyi". Ne yere kae sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔfa bi, efisɛ wunim sɛ yɛnni kokora dua biako mpo a yɛbɛka na yɛntɔn bi". Ɔpɔnkɔ no fii hɔ kɔɔ kwan so, nanso ogyaw nkraman no wɔ fie, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a kokora dua no wɔ no. Afei ɔforo fii hɔ, na ɔfaa kola-nuts no bi. Afei ɔtee wɔ hɔ sɛ, "Twa! be! kukuru! kukuru! hu! pi!" Sasabonsam (gyinaa n'anim). Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Hena na ɔtwee m'abɔnsam nnua no?" Ɔbɔfo no kae sɛ: "Me". Ɔbɔfo no kae sɛ: "Mebekum wo esiane nea woayɛ yi nti". Ɔbɔfo no kae sɛ: "Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wokum me a, ma memfrɛ me nkraman". Ɔkae sɛ: "Momfrɛ, na sɛ woabɔ wɔn mpɛn abiɛsa na wɔmmra a, mɛkum wo". Ɛnna ɔbɔfo no teɛɛm sɛ:

"Sniff-sniff, (ba na) hyia me, Lick-lick, (ba na) hyia me, Tie-in-knots, (ba na) hyia me, Gulp-down, (ba na) hyia me.

Wai, wai, wai, wɔrebɛba akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, wɔrebɛba akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, wɔrebɛba akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, wɔde bere pii reba!

Na akraman a wɔwɔ fie no tee no no, woguan kɔɔ kurow no akyi. Afei ɔtowfo no teɛɛm bio se,

Sniff-sniff, (ba na) kohyia me, Lick-lick, (ba na) kohyia me, Tie-in-knots, (ba na) kohyia me, Gulp-down, (ba na) kohyia me.

Wai, wai, wai, wɔrebɛba akyɛ!

Afei nkraman no tee; wotuu mmirika, prada! prada! Sasabonsam kae sɛ, Frɛ bio, na ɛno wiee ne mprɛnsa so. Ɔwodifo no teɛɛm (ne nne) dennen:

Sniff-sniff, (ba na) kohyia me, Lick-lick, (ba na) kohyia me, Tie-in-knots, (ba na) kohyia me, Gulp-down, (ba na) kohyia me.

Wai, wai, wai, wɔrebɛba akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, wɔrebɛba akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔrebɛba akyɛ! Na n'anom nnya nnwudwo, bere a nkraman no wɔ n'anim no. Ɔpɔnkɔ no kae sɛ: "Sniff-sniff, hwɛ Sasabonsam, hu no". Na Sniff-sniff hwuduu no. Ɔkae sɛ: "Lick-lick, lick him over". Ɔkae sɛ: "Tie-in-knot, tie him in a knot". Tie-in-knot kyekyeree ne kɔn mu; na Sasabonsam hwee fam wɔ hɔ, kum! Afei Gulp-down de no kɔe, na ɔdɔɔ no. Ɔpiapuni no ne nkraman no boaboaa kokoram no ano de no baa fie. Bere a ɔpiapuni no san bae no, ɔde kokoram no yɛɛ aduan a ne nyinaa yɛ kokoram, na ɔde maa nkraman no; ɔtwee nsu guu hɔ de maa hɔ, na ofii hɔ kɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn kɔdidii. Bere a owiee aduan no, ofii adi kɔɔ abɔnten kɔtenaa gyedua (dua) ase. Afei, odwotwafo no akoa Fly kɔɔ hɔ kɔhwɛɛ aduan a wɔasiesie ama nkraman no. Na ɔtwee no de no too ɔdan no atifi. Na nkraman no fi abɔnten bae na na ɔkɔm de wɔn. Lick-lick ka kyerɛɛ wɔn a aka no sɛ, "Moahunu deɛ yɛayɛ ama yɛn wura no, nanso ɛsi ɛnnɛ yi, wamma yɛn aduane mpo a yɛbɛdi, enti momma yɛnkɔkum no". Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Wonnni atorɔ, wonni atorɔ". Lick-lick fii adi kɔɔ abɔnten kɔkyekyeree awudifo no amu, na ɔne awudifo no dii agorɔ; na Sniff-sniff nso bae; na ɔtwee awudifo no amu no ano; na ɔno nso fii hɔ kɔe. Afei ntɛm ara Tie-in-knots ba bɛkyekyeree ne kɔn mu wɔ knots, na Gulp-down, nso, bae na bɛmenee no. Afei nkraman no san kɔɔ fie; na wɔrebɛhwɛ pato no mu, bere a wohuu nsõ nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn wɔ hɔ no. Wɔkae sɛ: "Mmofra, ebia odwotwafo no agyaw yɛn aduan, na mo nso moadi". Mmofra kae sɛ: "Mannya bi da". Yɛakum yɛn wura no kwa, efisɛ aduan a ɔde asi hɔ ama yɛn no wɔ hɔ ampa, na emu biako anaa abien biara nni hɔ, na mmom Nnomaa no na ɔfaa no de no kɔtoo hɔ". Nnomaa no guan fii hɔ kɔe. Enti sɛ ɔkraman bi te hɔ na nnomaa bi te ne so a, ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkyere no.

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
