WOYƐ NEA ƐYƐ nwonwa te sɛ Ananse, Ahemfo

ANANSE, Spider, tenaa hɔ bere bi, bere a Nsia, ɔsoro-nyame no maame yaree. Wɔkɔɔ kɔfaa ɔyaresafo bi sɛ ɔmmɛhwɛ no. Bere a ɔbae no, ɔkae sɛ ɔde sika kɔkɔɔ dɔte (a.e. £8) bi bɛma no. Afei, Ananse kɔka kyerɛɛ ɔsoro-nyame no sɛ, "Onipa yi gye wo sika kɔkɔɔ dɔte no kwa; ontumi nhwɛ no, enti ma menhwɛ no mma wo, na sɛ manhwɛ no a, me ti betumi ahwere". Ananse faa sika kɔkɔɔ dɔte (a.e. 4), na ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔsoro-nyame no sɛ, "Sɛ mantumi asa yare yi a, mɛma wo gyata a ɔte ase". Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma no baa mu no, Nyankonpon, ɔsoro-nyame no maame Nsia fii hɔ. Wɔbisaa no sɛ, "Ananse, ɛdeɛn na ɛrekɔ so?" Ananse fii hɔ; woguanee. Ɔkaa nea esii no kyerɛɛ Ɔteasefo no kae sɛ: "Yei ara na mahu, enti afei ɛsɛ sɛ woboa me". Ɔteasefo no kae sɛ: "Mɛyɛ dɛn atumi aboa wo?" Ɔteasefo no kae sɛ: "Mepɛ sɛ wokɔ kɔto fononoo bi kosi sɛ ɛbɛkɔ abotan a ɔsoro Nyankopɔn te so no ase". Ɔteasefo no kae sɛ: "Mɛyɛ ama wo". Enti ɔyɛɛ no. Na Ananse kɔfrɛɛ ne babarima Ntikuma, na ɔtwaa mmɛn maa no. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔnkɔ amoa no mu na ɔnkɔtena abantenten no ase, na sɛ ohu sɛ ɔsoro nyame no atena abantenten no so a, na ɛsɛ sɛ ɔbɔ mmɛn no ka sɛ:

"Sɛ wokum Ananse a, abusua no bɛsɛe!

Sɛ wode Ananse kyɛ no a, abusuakuw no de nne bɛbobom!"

Ntikuma kɔtenaa ɔpon no ase, na Ananse fii hɔ kae sɛ ɔrebɛkɔ Nyankonpon, ɔsoro-nyame no hɔ, wɔ n'ankasa pɛ mu. Bere a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, ɔsoro-nyame no atuatewfo no kyeree no; wɔde no kɔɔ ɔsoro-nyame no anim. Afei ɔkasafo no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Wo, Kwaku Ananse, woka kyerɛɛ Nyankonpon, ɔsoro-nyame no sɛ, sɛ ne maame Nsia wu a, obetumi akum wo; enti esiane sɛ Nsia afi hɔ nti, yebekum wo". Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ woma me kwan ma menka m'asɛm". Nanso atuatewfo no kyeree no sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wɔbɛkɔ akum no. Ntikuma kae sɛ: "Sɛ wokum Ananse a, abusua no bɛsɛe!

Sɛ wode Ananse kyɛ no a, abusuakuw no de nne bɛbobom!"

Wɔkae sɛ: "Muntie, muntie!" Na torobɛnto no bɔe bio:

"Sɛ wokum Ananse a, abusua no bɛsɛe!

Sɛ wode Ananse kyɛ no a, abusuakuw no de nne bɛbobom!"

Afei Kontirehene panyin no sɔre ka kyerɛɛ ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ: "Ɔsoro nyame yi yɛ wo dea, ne Ya a ɔyɛ Asase dada no dea, ɛnnɛ wo nsa aka Ananse na wopɛ sɛ wokum no. Ya a ɔyɛ Asase dada no ka sɛ: 'Gyaa no kɔ,' na sɛ wogyaa no a, ɛbɛyɛ yiye". Ɛno nti ɔsoro nyame no maa wɔn a wokum no no gyaa no. Eyi nti na wɔka sɛ: "Woyɛ sɛnea Ananse yɛ nwonwa".

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
