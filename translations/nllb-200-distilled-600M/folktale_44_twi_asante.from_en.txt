Nea yɛreka yi nyɛ nokware.

ASƐM A ƐDA SO Nea Ɛyɛe a Abɔfra Nsu Kɔkɔɔ

WƆKA sɛ bere bi na Ɔhemmaa Maame bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔwoo ababaa fɛfɛ bi. Ɛhɔ na na ɔwɔ; nanso bere a wɔde no maa obi waree no, wampene sɛ ɔbɛware. Bere biara a nkurɔfo kɔ ne nkyɛn kɔkasae (aware ho) no, ɔkae sɛ, Akyinnye biara nni ho. Na Python da hɔ, na ɔkae (wɔ ne tirim) sɛ, Mɛdan me ho ababaa pa, na makɔware Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no. Nokwasɛm ne sɛ, Python dan ne ho ababaa; sɛ obi hwɛ no tenten a, na ontumi nyɛ. Ɔrekɔ akuraa no ase. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na ɔrekeka kola-nuts. Bere a ɔbaa kurow no ano no, Ɔhemmaa Maame ba a ɔkɔe sɛ ɔrebɛhohoro aguade no ba no san bae, na ɔretrɛw ntade no mu ma apue. Python no hwiee, na ɛhwee ɔhemmaa Maame Maame ba no so. Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no bisaa no sɛ, Ɛdɛn nti na wohintiw me ho? Ɔhemmaa no kae sɛ, Wo na woteɛ (wɔ saa asɛm no mu), Amu. Ɔhemmaa no kae sɛ, Me ba no nkyia nyɛ Amu. Ɔhemmaa no buae sɛ, Wo na woteɛ (wɔ saa asɛm no mu), Eson. Ɔhemmaa no kae sɛ, Wo na woteɛ (wɔ saa asɛm no mu), me yere. Ɔhemmaa no kae sɛ, Afei meware wo. Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no ne Python no fii hɔ kɔɔ (ne) fie. Ɛhɔ ara na Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no kɔɔ ne maame nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Eh! eh! eh! (ɔde anigye rekasa), mahu nea mepɛ no. Ɔhemmaa Maame kae sɛ, Akyire yi! me babarima no wɔ he? Ɔkaa sɛ, Mewɔ ha, nana. Ɔhemmaa Maame de ne nsa kaa Python no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Ma wo ne wo yere ntena ase anigye mu. Bere a ade sae no, ɔne ne yere kɔdaa hɔ. Ɛyɛɛ saa nnawɔtwe. Akyiri yi ɔkae sɛ, Me yere, merekɔ me kurom nanso mɛsan aba. Ne yere kae sɛ, Afei me ne wo bɛkɔ (bom). Python kae sɛ, Dabi. Python kasae na ɔkasae (na ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛka akyerɛ no sɛ ɔnnkɔ), nanso Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no antie (n'asɛm). Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no bisaa no sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na woreyɛ, ɔkwan yi na ɛrekɔ so yi?" Ɔhemmaa no kaa sɛ, "Wo de, to wo ano na di m'akyi". Ɔhemmaa no ne ne ba no siim, na wɔkɔɔ so, na (ahyɛase no) ɔhemmaa no yerae ɔbodan bi mu. Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no nso kɔɔ mu. Amonom hɔ ara, Python no sesaa ne ho yɛɛ aboa; ɛno akyi no, ɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no sɛ, Eyi ne nea meyɛ ankasa, efisɛ mesesaa me ho sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mɛware wo, efisɛ na wote wo maame ne w'agya mmati so na woka sɛ, Biribiara nni hɔ (nsuro) ; mɛkum wo; mɛpɛ sɛ miyi me ti na me nsa ka nkotodwe; sɛ minnya bi a, na woadi aduan awie. Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no te ne nkyɛn, na ɔtwee nkotodwe, na ɔhwehwɛɛ nkotodwe. Afei, na wɔwɔ hɔ, bere a Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no huu sɛ Parrot bi retwam no. Ná Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no rebɛbue n'ano aka sɛ, "Agya Papaya", bere a Papaya no de ne nsa guu n'ano no. Papaya no faa n'akode, siesiee biribiara, na ɔde (n'afurum) too ne kotoku so. Papaya no kae sɛ, "Hwee me ti". Papaya no kɔɔ ɔbodan no mu kɔfrɛɛ Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no ne Python no de wɔn kɔɔ fie sɛ wɔnka sɛnea ɛbae sɛ ohuu Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no ne Python no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn ɔkwan a ɔfaa so kunkum Python no. Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no nso kaa ne nsɛm no nyinaa kyerɛɛ no. Ɔhemmaa Maame kae sɛ, "Parrot, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mefa de da wo ase?" Papaya no kae sɛ, "Nananom, mempɛ hwee, me nkutoo na mepɛ nnuaba mu ngo". Ɔhemmaa Maame no kɔe kɔfaa nnuaba mu ngo. Ɔkae sɛ: "Nananom, mɛfa me yoma no de agu dua yi mu, na mede ayɛ sɛnkyerɛnne". Saa ara na papaya yi yoma kɔkɔɔ. Eyi ne m'asɛm a maka ho asɛm, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom mmra me nkyɛn.
