Nea yɛreka yi nyɛ nokware.

ASƐM A ƐDA SO Nea Ɛyɛe a Abɔfra Nsu Kɔkɔɔ

WƆKA sɛ bere bi na Ɔhemmaa Maame bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔwoo ababaa fɛfɛ bi. Ɛhɔ na na ɔwɔ; nanso bere a wɔde no maa obi waree no, wampene sɛ ɔbɛware. Bere biara a nkurɔfo kɔ ne nkyɛn kɔkasa kyerɛ no (aware ho) no, ɔkae sɛ, Akyinnye biara nni ho. Da bi a na ɔwɔ hɔ no, ɔtee sɛ awudifo bi retwam, na na wɔrekasa afa ababaa fɛfɛ no ho, sɛnea bere a wɔde no ama obi aware no, ɔkae sɛ ɔmpɛ no. Agyenkwa no da hɔ, na ɔkae (wɔ ne tirim) sɛ, Mɛdan me ho ayɛ aberante pa, na makɔware Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba. Nokwasɛm ne sɛ, Agyenkwa no dan ne ho ayɛ aberante; na ɛnyɛ mmerɛw sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ no akyɛ. Ɔkɔɔ akuraa no ase. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na ɔrekeka kola-akwaa. Bere a ɔbaa kurow no ano no, Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba a ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔhohoroa nneɛma no san bae, na na ɔretrɛw ntade no mu ma apue. Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no bisae sɛ, Dɛn nti na wohintiw me ho? Python no kae sɛ, Wo na woteɛ (wɔ saa asɛm no mu), Amu. Ɔkae sɛ, Me nton nkyia nyɛ Amu. Ɔbuaa sɛ, Wo na woteɛ (wɔ saa asɛm no mu), Eson. Ɔbuaa sɛ, Me nton nkyia nyɛ Eson. Ɔbuaa sɛ, Wo na woteɛ (wɔ saa asɛm no mu), me yere. Ɔbuaa sɛ, Afei meware wo. Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba ne Python no de o Ɛhɔ ara na Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no kɔɔ ne maame nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛyɛ me pɛ sɛ mihu no". Ɔhemmaa Maame no kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ me pɛ sɛ mihu no". Ɛhe na me barima no wɔ? Ɔbarima no kae sɛ, "Mewɔ ha, Nana". Ɔhemmaa no de ne nsa kekaa aboa no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ma wo ne wo yere ntena ase asomdwoe mu". Awiei koraa no ɔkae sɛ, Me yere, merekɔ me kurom nanso mɛsan aba. Ne yere kae sɛ, Afei me ne wo bɛkɔ (bom). Python kae sɛ, Dabi. Python kasae na ɔkasae (na ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛka akyerɛ no sɛ ɔnnkɔ), nanso Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no antie (n'asɛm). Bere a wɔrekɔ no, na ɔtweaseɛ no atetew (afi kwan no so) akɔ nnuaba no mu. Ɔhemmaa Maame no babea bisaa no sɛ, "Dɛn na woreyɛ, ɔkwan yi ne dɛn?" Ɔhweaseɛ no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Wo de, to w'ano na di m'akyi". Ɔne no fii hɔ, na wɔkɔɔ so, na (ɛno akyi no) ɔhweaseɛ no yerae ɔbodan bi mu. Ɔhemmaa Maame no babea no nso kɔɔ mu. Amonom hɔ ara, Python no sesaa ne ho yɛɛ aboa; ɛno akyi no, ɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no sɛ, "Eyi ne nea meyɛ ankasa, efisɛ mesesaa me ho sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mɛware wo, efisɛ na wote wo maame ne w'agya mmati so ka sɛ, Biribiara nni hɔ (nsuro) ; mɛkum wo; me ti so na minkyekyere nsɔe; sɛ wunnya bi a, woawie Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no tenaa ne nkyɛn a na ɔresere na ɔrehwehwɛ nsɔe. Afei, wɔbaa hɔ, bere a Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no hui sɛ Papaa bi retwam. Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no rebɛbue n'ano aka sɛ, "Papaa Papaa", bere a Papaa no de ne nsa guu n'ano no. Papaa no faa n'akode, siesiee biribiara, na ɔde (n'afurum) too ne kotoku so. Python kae sɛ, "Hwe me ti". Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "M'abam abu; sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wokum me a, wu me". Python no rebɛma ne ti so sɛ ɔrekɔkyere Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no, bere a Papaya no de tuo no teɛɛm sɛ, pomm! Papaya no kɔɔ ɔbodan no mu kɔmaa Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no ne Python no de wɔn kɔɔ fie sɛ wɔnka sɛnea ɛbae sɛ ohuu Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no ne Python no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn ɔkwan a ɔfaa so kunkum Python no. Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no nso kaa nea ɔyɛe no nyinaa kyerɛɛ wɔn. Ɔhemmaa Maame kae sɛ: "Parrot, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mede ma wo de da wo ase?" Papaya no kae sɛ: "Nananom, mempɛ hwee, me ho de, mepɛ nsã kɛkɛ". Ɔhemmaa Maame kɔfaa nsã de bae. Ɔkae sɛ: "Nananom, mɛfa m'afiri na magugu mu wɔ nsã yi mu, na ɛno na ɛbɛma ayɛ sɛnkyerɛnne". Saa ara na papaya no afiri ayɛ kɔkɔɔ. Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
