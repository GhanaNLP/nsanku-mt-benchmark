Nea yɛreka yi nyɛ nokware.

ASƐM A ƐDA SO Nea Ɛtoo Ananse, Ahemfo a Wɔfrɛ No Spider no, Bɛkɔɔ Ahemfo no So

ANANSE O! ɔwaree Nsia babea a ɔyɛ ɔsoro-nyame no maame, na ɔkɔɔ Nyame (ɔsoro-nyame no) hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Da a wo maame Nsia bewu no, bra bɛka da a wɔayi no ama ɔboda no ho amanne no akyerɛ me".

Nnansa akyi no, wɔbaa ne nkyɛn bɛka kyerɛɛ no sɛ Nsia, ɔsoro nyame no maame no awu. Afei Ananse abusuafo yɛ 'Berekuo, Ɔhene Jay, Kwakuo, Apam no, ne Odwan, Nnwan no. Afei Ananse ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ: "Wɔaba abɛka funeral amanne bi ho asɛm akyerɛ me, enti ɛsɛ sɛ wo ne me kɔ". Enti Ananse maa wɔde (asɛm) kɔmaa ɔsoro nyame no sɛ ɔrebɛba afahyɛ no ase. Afei ɔsoro-nyame no maa ne manfo nyinaa hyiae, te sɛ Omanhene. Na Ananse kae sɛ, "Sɛ anwummere kɔtɔ a, mɛba". Spider no yɛɛ kotoku, na ɔde 'Berekuo, Ɔhene Jay, Kwakuo, Apam, ne Odwan, Nnwan no hyɛɛ mu, na ɔtow kotoku no too ne nan so, na ɔbobɔɔ n'atade guu so. Afei ɔka kyerɛɛ abɔde no sɛ: "Sɛ mekɔ ɔsoro Nyankopɔn nkyɛn a, monsu".

"Kurukusu, ku! ku! ku!

Kurukusu, ku! ku! ku! ni".

Na abeawa no teɛɛm sɛ:

"Kakum!"

Na nguan no teɛɛm sɛ:

"Me!"

Eyi ne Kwaku Ananse awerɛhoɔ. Nkurɔfoɔ no nyinaa kaa sɛ, "Woayɛ no yie, hwɛ ɔbarima bi a ɔtumi su na ɔdi awerɛhoɔ wɔ akwan mmiɛnsa so". Bere a ɔwiee (ne awerɛhoɔ) no, Ɔsoro-nyame no hwehwɛɛ fie pa a ɔbɛtena mu. Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma no kɔeɛ no, Ɔsoro-nyame no yɛɛ aduane pa maa Ananse. Bere a Kwaku Ananse huu saa aduan yi no, ɔkae (wɔ ne tirim) sɛ: "Merennye Berekuo biara, merennye Kwakuo biara, merennye Odwan biara, efisɛ mayɛ mmuada akyɛ". Enti Ananse dii, na wanka ne mogya-abusuafo ho asɛm biara. Afei, ɔno ne wɔn kɔɔ so tenaa hɔ, na ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Yɛbɛyɛ awudi afahyɛ a edi nnawɔtwe". Nanso Oguan no kae sɛ, "Ɔkɔm rekum me, merenkɔ"; Kwakuo ne 'Berekuo nso kae saa ara. Na ɔsoro nyame no kɔtenaa nkurɔfokuw no mu, na ɔmaa wɔn ka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ adepa a ɔyɛe da bi no, ɔbɛsan ayɛ bio; na ɔne ne mpanimfoɔ hyiae sɛ wobetie no. Ananse buae sɛ, "Mente me ho yiye". Ade kyee no, ɔsoro-nyame no ka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, "Woama m'ani agu ase". Na 'Berekuo, Kwakuo, ne Odwan kae sɛ, "Yɛn nso, woama yɛn ɔkɔm de yɛn, enti yɛne wo bɛtetew yɛn ho afi abusua no ho". Enti Kwakuo ne 'Berekuo fii hɔ kɔɔ wuram, nanso Odwan, Nnwan no, kɔɔ fie; na Spider no bɔɔ kirididi, na ɔhwee ne ho wɔ abantenten no so. Esiane sɛ na ne ho agu no nti.

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
