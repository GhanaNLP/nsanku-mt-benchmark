Nea yɛreka yi nyɛ nokware.

ASƐM A ƐDA SO Ɔkwan a Obi Fa So Kyerɛkyerɛ Abɔde Mu Maa Ne Yere Fii Ɔbea Hɔ (NA ƆGYE N'Agya Nkwa)

BERE bi a atwam no, na mmarima baasa bi wɔ hɔ a na wɔpɛ adwuma. Wɔkɔɔ soro asase so kɔtɔ nwera ne nneɛma foforo. (Sɛ wɔsan kɔ Ashanti a) wɔmaa wɔn wɔfase no de nneɛma no bi kɔtɔn. Wɔbaa kurow kɛse bi mu. Ná ɔhemmaa bi babea wɔ kurow no mu a wampene obiara a wɔde no bɛma no aware. Bere a ahɔho no kɔɔ fie no, ɔhemmaa no babarima kɔhohoroo nsu no. Ɔhemmaa no tɔɔ ntade abien de maa ne babea. Bere a ne babea no san bae no, ohuu mmarima baasa no. Ɔka kyerɛɛ n'aberewa no sɛ: "Ɛnnɛ mahu obi a mepɛ no, enti kum oguan biako na memfa mma wɔn". Bere a wowiee aduan no sie no, ababaawa no kɔfaa asono nhwiren, koton, kokora, ne adua a wɔatetew, na ɔde hyɛɛ aduan no so de maa mmarima no. Na ɔkae sɛ: Nea obetumi akyerɛkyerɛ wɔn ase no, ɔno na mɛware no. Na wɔne aduan no kɔe. Mmarima no yii no adi, na wɔkae sɛ: Sɛ ɛnyɛ Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no na ɔde aduan yi bae a, anka ɛsɛ sɛ yebisa no nsɛm bi. Mmarima no babarima kae sɛ: Sɛ manhu mo suro a, anka mɛkyerɛ mo nea eyinom kyerɛ. Wɔkae sɛ: Kyerɛkyerɛ. Ɛha no, ɛnsɛ sɛ ɔware anaa? Afahyɛ no kyerɛ sɛ, da bi bɛba a ɔbɛyera; kola nut no nso kyerɛ sɛ da bi bɛba a ɔbɛma (ne kunu) adi kola (i.e. ne werɛ ahow). Enti, Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no waree mmarima no babarima. Bere a ɔwaree no no, Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no bisaa no sɛ, "Wo din de dɛn?" Ɔbarima no kae sɛ, "Me din de Kwaku Susufuo", kyerɛ sɛ, Kwaku-nea-gye-nko-nko-nko. Abaayewa no buae sɛ, "Me nso wɔfrɛ me Akua a ɔgye akyinnyegye". Ɔwaree no. Ade kyee anɔpa no, wɔn nananom baasa no fii hɔ kɔɔ wɔn kurom. Kwaku a ogye akyinnyegye kɔtenaa ne yere akuraa ase. Ɔtenaa hɔ afe baako, na bere a ɔsɔre (adekyee) no, ɔne ne nnamfonom kɔtenaa ase dii agoru. Afei da koro a na ɔrebɔ ne ho agorɔ yi, ɔtwee mmirika abien wɔ akuturuku biako mu, nanso nnipa a ɔne wɔn redi agorɔ no kae sɛ wɔremfa mma no, na wɔpɛe mpo sɛ wɔboro no. Ɔhwɛɛ n'akyi, na ohuu abofra bi a ogyina hɔ; ɔfaa ɔbo de maa abofra no sɛ ɔmfa nkɔma ne yere. Bere a ne yere nsa kaa ɔbo no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Me kunu pɛ sɛ ɔsan kɔ ne kurom, ɛfiri sɛ ɔka sɛ, sɛ anomaa tena asase so akyɛ dodo a, ɔbɛnya ɔbo". Na Ɔhemmaa Maame kae sɛ, "Sɛ ɔkɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ wo ne no kɔ". Afei, bere bi akyi no, wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Kaku, obi a ne ho akyere no, kɔfa hama bi na yɛnkɔto nnua no mu nkɔkyere mmorɔnoma, na yɛmfa nnye wo yere a ɔyɛ ɔhɔho no ahɔho". Enti wɔkɔe, nanso mpanyimfo no yɛɛ adwene sɛ wobekum no. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ: "Bere a mekɔɔ me yere nkuraa ase no, mihuu sika pii, na sɛ moamma me kwan sɛ menkyerɛ mo faako a ɛwɔ, na mewu a, morenhu". Wobisaa no sɛ: "Ɛhe na sika no wɔ?" Kɔ me yere nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛ no sɛ matɔ nam, na (adwumayɛfo no) ka sɛ ne bo yɛ peredwan du (£80), na ɛsɛ sɛ ɔhwehwɛ kotoku a wɔde nnwan nhama ayɛ mu; sɛ ɔhwehwɛ hɔ na ɔanhu a, ma ɔnhwehwɛ Krampan kotoku no mu, na sɛ wanhu a, ma ɔnhwehwɛ atade a nkrante wɔ mu no mu. Enti wɔbaa ne yere nkyɛn kɔmaa no nkra no. Na ɔno nso kae sɛ, Nneɛma no wɔ akuraa no so panyin fie, enti momma yɛnkɔfa wɔn. Ɔno ne wɔn kɔɔ ɔhwɛfo no fie; na ɔbea no kaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ ɔhwɛfo no, na ɔkyerɛkyerɛɛ mu sɛ, Ɛno kyerɛ sɛ wɔkyekyeree no; Krampan kyerɛ sɛ aporɔw no rebɔ mmɔden sɛ wobedidi ne nam; atade a wɔde nkrante no mu no kyerɛ sɛ wɔrepɛ sɛ wotwa n'ano. Amonom hɔ ara, ɔpanyin no bɔɔ gong-gong sɛ wɔnkɔ hɔ nkɔgye Kwaku a ɔgye akyinnye no. Na wɔsan baa ne nkyɛn, na ɔmaa wɔn kum mpanyimfo baasa no. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, Ɛnam nsɛnkyerɛnne nkyerɛase so obi nyaa ɔyere na (ɔsan) gyee ne nkwa. Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka ho asɛm no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
