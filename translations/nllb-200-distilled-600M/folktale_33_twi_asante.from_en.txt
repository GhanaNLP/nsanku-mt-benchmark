Nea yɛreka yi nyɛ nokware.

Wɔka sɛ, sɛ wokɔ baabi foforo a, san bra fie ntɛm ara

BERE bi na abaayewa bi a wɔfrɛ no Abena wɔ hɔ, na n'abusuafo waree no. Ofii hɔ kɔɔ ne kunu akuraa ase, na ne werɛ fii ne ankasa abusua; na ɔmpɛ sɛ ɔsan kɔ ne kurom. Ɛhɔ na na ɔwɔ (wɔ n'ankasa fie), na ne maame wui; bio, ne papa wui; bio, ne nanabea wui; ɔsan n'akyi ankɔ wɔn ayi biara mu. Ná ɛbɛyɛ mfe du, nanso wamma fie da. Afei, da koro, Abena wɔ hɔ, bere a anomaa bi bae begyinaa fie hɔ kae sɛ:

"Abena maame awu, papa awu, nanaa awu!

Akode no rebɔ, pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom!

Mpoma no rebɔ, kun! kun!

Mmea no resu, y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y!

Abena maame awu, papa awu, nana nso awu!"

Abena kae sɛ: "Dɛn asɛm ni?" Bere a ne kunu bae akyi bere tiaa bi no, ɔkaa nea asi no kyerɛɛ no; na nnomaa no sui bio. Ɔbarima no kae sɛ: "Mente ase koraa, enti mɛkɔfrɛ Hare no". Ɔfrɛɛ no, na ɔmaa no bae; na nnomaa no san teɛɛm bio.

"Abena maame awu, papa awu, nanaa awu!

Akode no rebɔ, pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom!

Mpoma no rebɔ, kun! kun!

Mmea no resu, y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y!

Abena maame awu, papa awu, nana nso awu!"

Hare no kae sɛ: "M'aso biako ate, na biako da so ara wɔ hɔ". Na anomaa no teɛteɛɛm sɛ:

"Abena maame awu, papa awu, nanaa awu!

Akode no rebɔ, pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom!

Mpoma no rebɔ, kun! kun!

Mmea no resu, y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y!

Abena maame awu, papa awu, nana nso awu!"

Hare no kae sɛ: "O! O! ɔka sɛ wo maame awu, wo papa awu, wo nana nso awu". Afei Abena de ne nsa guu ne ti so kae sɛ: "Pue! oh maame! oh nana! oh agya!" Ofii hɔ kɔɔ ne kurom; nanso ne fie yɛ amamfõ, pasa! Ɛno nti na wɔka sɛ: "Sɛ wode wo ho kɔ kwan so a, twa". Efisɛ Abena yɛɛ sɛnea wɔaka no, na ɔmaa n'abusuafo nyinaa wui wɔ ne bere a na onni hɔ no mu.

Eyi ne me ho asɛm a maka akyerɛ mo no. Sɛ ɛyɛ fɛ anaa ɛnyɛ fɛ a, mubetumi agye emu bi atom sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no nso, mobɛkamfo me.
