Nea yɛreka yi nyɛ nokware.

Wɔka sɛ, sɛ wokɔ baabi foforo a, san bra fie ntɛm ara

BERE bi na abaayewa bi wɔ hɔ a wɔfrɛ no Abena, na n'abusuafo waree no. Ofii hɔ kɔɔ ne kunu akuraa ase, na ne werɛ fii n'ankasa mogya ho abusuabɔ nyinaa; na ɔmpɛ sɛ ɔsan ba ne kurom. Obi foforo akuraa no yɛ papa ma no. Ɛhɔ na na ɔwɔ (wɔ ne fie ho baabi), na ne maame wui; bio nso, ne papa wui; bio nso, ne nanabea wui; wanyan mmaako mmaako biara (wɔn ayi no mu). Ná ɛbɛyɛ mfe du, nanso wanyan aba fie da. Afei, da bi, Abena wɔ hɔ, bere a anomaa bi baa fie hɔ begyinaa hɔ kae sɛ:

"Abena maame awu, ne papa awu, ne nanabea awu!

Akode no rebɔ, pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom!

Mpoma no rebɔ, kun! kun!

Mmea no resu, y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y!

Abena maame awu, papa awu, nana nso awu!"

Abena kae sɛ: "Dɛn asɛm ni?" Bere a ne kunu bae no, ankyɛ koraa na ɔkaa nea asi no kyerɛɛ no. Na anomaa no san teɛteɛɛm bio. Ɔbarima no kae sɛ: "Mennim, enti mɛkɔfrɛ Hare no". Ɔfrɛɛ no, na ɔmaa no bae; na anomaa no san teɛɛm bio.

"Abena maame awu, ne papa awu, ne nanabea awu!

Akode no rebɔ, pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom!

Mpoma no rebɔ, kun! kun!

Mmea no resu, y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y!

Abena maame awu, papa awu, nana nso awu!"

Hare no kae sɛ: "M'aso biako ate, na biako da so ara wɔ hɔ". Na anomaa no teɛteɛɛm sɛ:

"Abena maame awu, ne papa awu, ne nanabea awu!

Akode no rebɔ, pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom! pom!

Mpoma no rebɔ, kun! kun!

Mmea no resu, y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y! y!

Abena maame awu, papa awu, nana nso awu!"

Ɔkraman no kae sɛ: "O! O! ɔka sɛ wo maame awu, wo papa awu, wo nana nso awu". Afei Abena de ne nsa guu ne ti so kae sɛ: "Pue! oh maame! oh nana! oh agya!" Ɔfirii hɔ baa ne kurom; nanso ne fie yɛ amamfõ, pasa! Ɛno nti na wɔka sɛ: "Sɛ wokɔ kwan so a, twa".

Eyi ne me ho asɛm a maka akyerɛ mo no. Sɛ ɛyɛ fɛ anaa ɛnyɛ fɛ a, ebia mobɛfa bi sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no nso, mobɛkamfo me.
