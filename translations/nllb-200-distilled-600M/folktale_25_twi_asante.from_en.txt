Sɛ Obi Gye Biribi Fi Wo Hɔ a, Mfa Ntua Ho Ka

WƆKA sɛ bere bi na mmɔfra a wɔn maame wui na wogyaw wɔn nkutoo no tenaa ase. Ná wɔreyɛ afahyɛ bi, na mmɔfra panyin (Ata 'Panin) no faa nhene a ɛsom bo ne sika kɔkɔɔ bi kyekyeree mmɔfra kumaa, Ata Kuma, kɔn ho. Bere a na wɔrehohoro nsu no, ahama a wɔde sika kɔkɔɔ ayɛ Ata Kuma kɔn mu no bubuu, na ahyɛnsode no hwee nsu no mu. Ɔsan kɔɔ fie kɔbɔɔ ne nuabea amanneɛ sɛ: "Wɔn sikakɔkɔɔ nneɛma no ayera". Ata 'Panin (mpanin panyin) kae sɛ: "Minim sɛ wɔayera, na sɛ na ayera mpo a, biribi wɔ hɔ a mɛhwehwɛ afi wo hɔ". Ata Kuma (mpanin kumaa) kae sɛ: "Ɛdeɛn na wobɛhwehwɛ afi me hɔ?" Ɔkae sɛ: "Mehwehwɛ afi wo hɔ nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn ho, Aserewa anomaa ketewaa no atenae, Nsu a ɛkyekyere". Gles ponponponponsa, dua a ɛyɛ biako (wɔ dua a wubeyi no afi mu) ne ɔse a ɛwɔ Elephant Queen Mother no so. Ata Kuma kae sɛ: "Metee". Ata Kuma fii ase (hwehwɛɛ nneɛma no), na okoduu baabi a na dua biako wɔ hɔ. Bere a dua no huu no no, ɔkae sɛ: "Hwɛ Ata Kuma, (nea nkurɔfo ka ne ho asɛm) ɔda adi wɔ kurow no hyeɛ so te sɛ osu a ɛredɛw". (Ata Kuma) kae sɛ: "Nea enti a mebaa ha ne sɛ agya no ba yɛ Ata 'Panin, na ɛna no ba yɛ Ata 'Panin, na ɔkyekyeree nhene ahorow pii kyekyeree me kɔn ho, na wɔbobɔɔ na wɔyerae". g):

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ me sɛ memfa dua bi a ɛso bi nni bio no nhini mmra.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ nhoma bi afi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ nnomaa a wɔfrɛ no Aserewa no atenae.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ Nsu, na ɔrekeka ponponponponsa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ sɛ ɔde ne nsa bɛka Elephant Queen Mother no atade.

Na dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ, O Agya, na manya bi?

Ɔka sɛ (ɛha) ɔyɛ abofra ketewa bi a ɔyɛ Akwasi Toboro Kojo babea.

Kojo Nsa.

Agya Elephant, eye ma wo sɛ wubeyi wo ho, eye ma wo sɛ wufi hɔ.

Kojo Nsa a.

Na dua biako no kae sɛ: "Eyi nti na wusu, mommra mmɛpɛ biribi". Na Ata Kuma kɔfaa bi, na ɔdaa no ase, na ɔkɔɔ so kohyiaa anomaa bi. Na Aserewa nnomaa no kae sɛ, Ɛha na Ata Kuma wɔ, bere a ɔbaa kurow no akyi no, obiara ka sɛ ɛte sɛ osu a ɛredɛw. Ata Kuma buae sɛ, Deɛ enti a mebaa ha ne sɛ agya ba no yɛ Ata Panin, na ɛna ba no yɛ Ata Panin, na ɔtwee nhwiren ne nhwiren pii kyekyeree me ho, na wɔbobɔɔ na wɔyerae. (Na ha na Ata Kuma bɛbobɔɔ. wɔ dwom mu):

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ me sɛ memfa dua bi a ɛso bi nni bio no nhini mmra.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ me nhoma.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ nnomaa a wɔfrɛ no Aserewa no atenae.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ Nsu, na ɔrekeka ponponponponsa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ Agya Leopard nsateaa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ sɛ ɔde ne nsa bɛka Elephant Queen Mother no atade.

Na dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ, O Agya, na manya bi?

Ɔka sɛ (ɛha) ɔyɛ abofra ketewa bi a ɔyɛ Akwasi Toboro Kojo babea.

Kojo Nsa.

Agya Elephant, eye ma wo sɛ wubeyi wo ho, eye ma wo sɛ wufi hɔ.

Kojo Nsa a.

Aserewa nnomaa no kae sɛ, Ɛno nti na wusu, bra na gye biribi. Na Ata Kuma kɔfaa (Aserewa nnomaa atenae no), na ɔdaa no ase. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ɛha yɛ Ata Kuma a, sɛ ɔfiri akuraa no ase ba a, nkurɔfo gye no fɛfɛ te sɛ osu. Ata Kuma kaa nsɛm a ɔka kyerɛɛ afoforo no kyerɛɛ no. Na Gurgling-water maa no bi (ne nsu a ɛregyigye no), na ɔfaa no kɔɔ so. Afei ɔne Leopard no hyiae. Bere a Leopard no huu no no, ɔkae sɛ, Ɛha na Ata Kuma wɔ; bere a ɔdaa adi wɔ akuraa no abɔnten ano no, ɔte sɛ osu a ɛredɛw. Ata Kuma ka kyerɛɛ no n'adwuma. Ɔkae sɛ, Bra na gye biribi, (na ɔmaa ne nsateaa no mu biako). Ɛnna Ata Kuma kae sɛ, Father Leopard, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ na manya asono Queen Mother of the Elephants no ahwe?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ah, bere a worebɛkɔ no, mmoa no nyinaa bɛda hɔ, na Asono Queen Mother no bɛda hɔ wɔ mfinimfini, na bere a worebɛkɔ no, nante wɔn so denneennen, na bere a worebɛkɔ Asono Queen Mother no so no, twa ne ahwe, na sɛ wode ahwɛyiye nante wɔn so a, Ata Kuma kae sɛ, "Meda wo ase". Na bere a ɔrekɔ no, ohui mmoa yi; ɔtwaa wɔn so denneennen, na ohyiaa Ɔhemmaa Maame. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panin, nneɛma a wokae sɛ wobɛfa afiri me hɔ ni". Ata 'Panin kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka wɔn". Afei, da bi na wɔte hɔ, na Ata Kuma faa ahwehwɛ no hwɛɛ mu. Ɔkae sɛ, "Saa ahwehwɛ yi, sɛ obi hwɛ mu a, ohu ɛna a ɔwɔ ahonhom asase so". Ata 'Panin kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ saa a, momfa mmɛhwɛ". Ɔmaa no. Bere a ɔrebɛhwɛ mu no, efii ne nsa mu, na ɛbubuwee. Ata Kuma kae sɛ: "Eyi de, nea wo nsa aka afi me hɔ no, mɛhwehwɛ afi wo hɔ". Ɛnna Ata 'Panin kɔfaa nneɛma a Ata Kuma de brɛɛ no no bae. Ata Kuma kae sɛ: "Mempɛ, nea mepɛ ara ne sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa foforo aba me nkyɛn". Ata 'Panin fii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kohyiaa dua bi a nhaban baako pɛ wom. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Hwɛ Ata 'Panin a sɛ ɔba abɔnten awiei a, ɔte sɛ osu". Ata 'Panin buae sɛ: "Mahunu wo maame! na wo papa! Sɛ ɛbɛyɛ me yiye a, ma me wo nhoma biako na ma menkɔ". Dua no kae sɛ: "Kɔ, nea onim (nea ɛsɛ sɛ woyɛ) no wɔ hɔ". Ɔkɔɔ so, na ohyiaa Aserewa anomaa. Aserewa nnomaa no kae sɛ: "Hwɛ Ata 'Panin; bere a ɔbaa kurow no abɔnten awiei no, ɔyɛ ahotɔfo te sɛ osu". Ata 'Panin kae sɛ: "Sɛ meba wo maame ne w'agya nkyɛn a, nea mepɛ ara ne wo guan biako, na ma menkɔ". Aserewa nnomaa no kae sɛ: "Fa no kɔ, nea onim (sɛnea ɛsɛ sɛ wo ne no di) wɔ hɔ". Afei ɔbaa Gurgling-water no so. Gurgling-water kae sɛ, "Hayi Ata 'Panin; sɛ ɔfiri kuro no awieeɛ a, ɔte sɛ osu a ɛredɛw". Ata 'Panin kae sɛ, "Sɛ meba wo maame nkyɛn a, sɛ meba wo papa nkyɛn a, ma me wo nsuo kakra na ma menkɔ". Gurgling-water kae sɛ, "Kɔ so kɔ anim, nea onim (sɛnea ɛsɛ sɛ ɔne wo di) wɔ hɔ". Afei ɔhyiaa Leopard no. Leopard no kae sɛ, "Hwɛ Ata 'Panin a, sɛ ɔsan ba abɔnten awiei a, ɔsan yɛ frɔmfrɔm te sɛ osu". Ata 'Panin buae sɛ, "Mehyɛ wo maame ne wo papa! Na me de, fa wo nsateaa bi ma me na ma menkɔ". Leopard kae sɛ: "Nea onim (sɛnea ɛsɛ sɛ ɔne wo di no) wɔ hɔ". Ɔsɔre fii hɔ, na ohyiaa asono kuw bi, na asono Queen Mother da mfinimfini no. Afei ɔkɔɔ so twaa n'afiri. Asono kuw no nyinaa kyeree no, na wotwaa no so, na owui. Ata Kuma hwehwɛɛ ne nuabea wɔ ɔkwan no so. Ɛno nti na yɛka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Sɛ wo ne wo abusuafo bi te hɔ na wɔn mu bi gye wo biribi a, mommmɔ ho ka, ɛfiri sɛ Ata 'Panin na ɔyɛɛ saa, na ɔkɔeɛ na ɔwuiɛ".

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
