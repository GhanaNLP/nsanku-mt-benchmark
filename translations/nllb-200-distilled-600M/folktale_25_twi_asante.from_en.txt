Sɛ Obi Gye Biribi Fi Wo Hɔ a, Mfa Ntua Ho Ka

WƆKA sɛ bere bi na mmɔfra a wɔn maame awu na wogyaw wɔn nkutoo no te faako. Ná wɔreyɛ afahyɛ bi, na mmɔfra panin (Ata 'Panin) no faa nhene a ɛsom bo ne sika kɔkɔɔ bi kyekyeree mmɔfra panin, Ata Kuma, kɔn ho. Mmɔfra panin no ne ne nnamfonom kae sɛ wɔrekɔ nsubɔnten no so akɔhohoro. Bere a wɔrehohoro wɔn ho no, mpokyerɛ a wɔde sika kɔkɔɔ kɔkɔɔ kɔkɔɔ no kyekyeree Ata Kuma kɔn ho no bubuu, na ahosiesie no hwee nsu no mu. Ɔhwehwɛɛ sɛnea ɔpɛ biara no, wanhu wɔn. Ɔsan kɔɔ fie kɔbɔɔ ne nuabea amanneɛ sɛ: "Wɔn sika kɔkɔɔ no ayera". Ata 'Panin (mpanin panyin no) kae sɛ: "Minim sɛ wɔayera, na sɛ na ɛte mpo a, biribi wɔ hɔ a mɛhwehwɛ afi wo hɔ". Ata Kuma (mpanin ketewa no) kae sɛ: "Ɛdeɛn na wobɛhwehwɛ afi me hɔ?" Ɔkae sɛ, "Mehwehwɛ afi wo hɔ mpataku, Aserewa anomaa ketewaa no atenae, Nsu a ɛkyekyere ponponponponsa, dua a ɛyɛ baako pɛ (wɔ dua a wofa mu no), ne Ɔhemmaa Elephantfo Maame no dua. Ata Kuma kae sɛ: "Metee". Ata Kuma fii adi (sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ nneɛma yi), na okoduu baabi a dua biako si hɔ. Bere a dua no huu no no, ɔkae sɛ: "Hwɛ Ata Kuma, (nea nkurɔfo ka ne ho asɛm) ɔbaa kurow no hyeɛ so te sɛ osu a ɛredɛw". (Ata Kuma) kae sɛ: "Ade biako pɛ nti na mebaa ha ne sɛ agya no ba yɛ Ata 'Panin, na ɛna no ba yɛ Ata 'Panin, na ɔkyekyeree nhene ahorow pii kyekyeree me kɔn ho, na wɔtetew na wɔyerae".

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ afi me hɔ dua bi nhaban, na ɛso bi nni bio.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ nhoma bi afi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ nnomaa a wɔfrɛ no Aserewa no atenae.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ Nsu, na ɔrekeka ponponponponsa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ sɛ ɔde ne nsa bɛka Elephant Queen Mother no atade.

Na dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ, O Agya, na manya bi?

Ɔka sɛ (ɛha) ɔyɛ abofra ketewa bi a ɔyɛ Akwasi Toboro Kojo babea.

Kojo Nsa.

Agya Elephant, eye ma wo sɛ wubeyi wo ho, eye ma wo sɛ wufi hɔ.

Kojo Nsa a.

Na dua biako no kae sɛ: "Eyi nti na wusu, mommra mmɛpɛ biribi". Na Ata Kuma kɔfaa no, na ɔdaa no ase, na ɔkɔɔ so kohyiaa anomaa bi. Na Aserewa nnomaa no kae sɛ, Ɛha na Ata Kuma wɔ, bere a ɔbaa kurow no akyi no, obiara ka sɛ ɛte sɛ osu a ɛredɛw. Ata Kuma buae sɛ, Deɛ enti a mebaa ha ne sɛ agya ba no yɛ Ata Panin, na ɛna ba no yɛ Ata Panin, na ɔtwee nhwiren a ɛsono wɔn mu na ɔsɛn me ho, na wɔbobɔɔ na wɔyerae. (Na ɛha na Ata Kuma bɔɔ dwom):

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ afi me hɔ dua bi nhaban, na ɛso bi nni bio.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ m'afã bi afi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ nnomaa a wɔfrɛ no Aserewa no atenae.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ Nsu, na ɔrekeka ponponponponsa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ Agya Leopard nsateaa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ sɛ ɔde ne nsa bɛka Elephant Queen Mother no atade.

Na dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ, O Agya, na manya bi?

Ɔka sɛ (ɛha) ɔyɛ abofra ketewa bi a ɔyɛ Akwasi Toboro Kojo babea.

Kojo Nsa.

Agya Elephant, eye ma wo sɛ wubeyi wo ho, eye ma wo sɛ wufi hɔ.

Kojo Nsa a.

Aserewa nnomaa no kae sɛ, Ɛno nti na wusu, bra na gye biribi. Na Ata Kuma kɔɔ na ogyee (Aserewa nnomaa atɔ) no, na ɔdaa no ase. Na ofii hɔ, na ohyiaa Water, a ɔretwe ponponponponsa. Ɔkae sɛ, Ɛha yɛ Ata Kuma, a, bere a ɔfiri akuraa no kwan ano ba a, nkurɔfo gye no fɛfɛɛfɛ te sɛ osu. Ata Kuma ka kyerɛɛ no nsɛm a ɔka kyerɛɛ afoforo no kyerɛɛ no. Na Gurgling-water maa no bi (ne nsu a ɛretwe no), na ɔfaa no kɔɔ so. Ɔhyiaa Leopard. Bere a Leopard no huu no no, ɔkae sɛ, Ɛha na Ata Kuma wɔ; bere a ɔdaa adi wɔ akuraa no abɔnten ano no, ɔte sɛ osu a ɛredɛw. Ata Kuma ka kyerɛɛ no n'adwuma. Ɔkae sɛ, Bra na gye biribi, (na ɔmaa ne nsateaa no mu biako). Afei Ata Kuma kae sɛ, Father Leopard, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ na manya Ɔhemmaa Elephant Maame no dua?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ah, bere a worekɔ no, mmoa no nyinaa akɔda, na Ɔhemmaa Elephant Maame no bɛda mfinimfini, na bere a worekɔ no, bɔ wɔn so denneennen, na sɛ worekɔ Ɔhemmaa Elephant Maame no nkyɛn a, twa ne dua, nanso sɛ wode ahwɛyiye bɔ wɔn so a, wobekum wo". Ata Kuma kae sɛ, "Meda wo ase". Na bere a ɔrekɔ no, ohuu saa mmoa yi; ɔtwaa wɔn so denneennen, na ɔbaa Ɔhemmaa Maame no so. Ɔtwaa ne dua, na ɔsan n'akyi. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔtɔɔ ahwehwɛ, na ɔfaa no (fi fie). Na ɔbaa fie no, ɔkae sɛ: "Ata 'Panin, nneɛma a wokae sɛ wobɛfa afi me nkyɛn yi ni". Ata 'Panin kae sɛ: "Me nsa aka wɔn". Afei, da koro bi na wɔte hɔ, na Ata Kuma faa ahwehwɛ na ɔhwɛɛ mu. Ɔkae sɛ: "Saa ahwehwɛ yi, sɛ obi hwɛ mu a, ohu ɛna a ɔwɔ ahonhom asase so". Ata 'Panin kae sɛ: "Ɛyɛ saa, momfa mmrɛ me nhwɛ". Ɔde maa no. Bere a ɔrebɛhwɛ mu no, efii ne nsa mu, na ɛbubuwee. Ata Kuma kae sɛ: "Eyi de, nea wo nsa aka fi me nkyɛn no, mɛhwehwɛ afi wo hɔ". Ɛnna Ata 'Panin kɔfaa nneɛma a Ata Kuma de brɛɛ no no. Ata Kuma kae sɛ: "Mempɛ (eyinom), nea mepɛ ara ne sɛ wobɛkɔ na wode foforo aba me nkyɛn". Ata 'Panin fii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kohyiaa dua bi a ne nhaban baako pɛ wom. Ɔkae sɛ: "Hwɛ Ata 'Panin a, sɛ ɔsan ba abɔnten awiei a, ne ho yɛ fɛ sɛ osu". Ata 'Panin buae sɛ: "Mayi me ho adi akyerɛ wo maame! Na w'agya de, ma me wo nhoma biako, na ma menkɔ". Aserewa nnomaa no kae sɛ: "Hwɛ Ata 'Panin; bere a ɔbaa kurow no abɔnten awiei no, ɔyɛ ahotɔfo te sɛ osu". Ata 'Panin kae sɛ: "Sɛ meba wo maame ne w'agya nkyɛn a, nea mepɛ ara ne wo guan biako, na ma menkɔ". Aserewa nnomaa no kae sɛ: "Fa no kɔ, nea onim sɛnea ɛsɛ sɛ ɔne wo di no wɔ hɔ". Gurgling-water kae sɛ: "Hwɛ Ata 'Panin; bere a oyii ne ho adi wɔ kurow no awiei no, ɔte sɛ osu a ɛsan tɔ". Ata 'Panin kae sɛ: "Sɛ mihu wo maame, sɛ mihu wo papa a, fa wo nsu no bi ma me na ma menkɔ". Gurgling-water kae sɛ: "Kɔ so kɔ anim, nea onim (sɛnea ɛsɛ sɛ ɔne wo di) wɔ hɔ". Ɛno akyi no, ohyiaa Leopard no. Leopard no kae sɛ: "Hwɛ Ata 'Panin a, bere a oyii ne ho adi wɔ kwankyɛn no awiei no, ɔte sɛ osu a ɛsan tɔ". Ata 'Panin buae sɛ: "Mihu wo maame ne w'agya! Na me de, fa wo nsateaa bi ma me na ma menkɔ". Akraman no kae sɛ: "Nea onim (nea ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ wo ho) no wɔ hɔ". Ɔkɔɔ hɔ, na ohyiaa asono kuw bi, na asono no babea a ɔyɛ ɔhemmaa no da mfinimfini hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ so twaa n'afiri. Asono kuw no nyinaa kyeree no, na wotwaa no so, na owui. Ata Kuma hwehwɛɛ ne nuabea no wɔ ɔkwan no so, na wanni hɔ. Ɛno nti yɛka sɛ: "Sɛ wo ne wo mogya mu biara te hɔ, na wɔn mu biara sɛe wo biribi a, mfa hwee mma no, efisɛ Ata 'Panin yɛɛ saa, na ɔkɔe, na owui".

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
