ASƐM A ƐDA SO Nea Ɛyɛe a Wɔkyeree Mmofra (Nea Edi Kan)

WƆKA sɛ (bere bi) ɔkɔm kɛse baa, na Agya Ananse, Spider, ne ne yere Aso, ne ne mma, Ntikuma, Nyiwankonfwea (Thin-Shanks), Afudotwedotwe (Belly-like-to-burst), ne Tikonokono (Big-Big-head), sii akuraa ketewa bi na wɔtraa mu. Da biara Spider kɔfaa aduan, wuram yams, na wɔtɔɔ wɔn dii. Ɔkaa sɛ, Dua yi yɛ fɛ. Dua no kae sɛ, Me din nyɛ beautiful. Spider no kae sɛ, Wo din de sɛn? Ɛbuaa sɛ, Me din de, Fill-up-some-and-eat. Spider no kae sɛ, Fa bi hyɛ ma menhwɛ. Dua no hyɛɛ borɔdɔma nsuhina ma, na Ananse dii nyinaa. Bere a ɔwiee no, obisaa didi no sɛ, Wo mmara no ne dɛn? The dish replied, I hate a gun wad, and a little gourd cup. Spider no faa didi no kɔɔ fie, na ɔkɔɔ na ɔde no too ɔdan no so. Afei Ananse kɔɔ wuram kɔfaa aduan, na Aso, bere a otwaa aduan wiei no, ɔfrɛɛ Ananse, na ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, wo na wuhia ankasa, na me de, meyɛ ɔpanyin, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mede aduan yɛ, wo de ne mmofra yi na ɛyɛ ade a ɛho hia ankasa; sɛ wohyɛ wo ma a, na m'aso ayɛ komm (fi w'awerɛhow nne no mu). Bere a wowiei adidi no, Spider no twaa ɔdan no akyi, na ɔkɔɔ (na ɔtenaa) ɔdan no atifi. Ɔkaa sɛ, Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. Ɛbuaa sɛ, Me din nyɛ beautiful. Ɔbisaa sɛ, Wo din de sɛn? Ɛbuaa sɛ, Me din de Fill-up-some-and-eat. Ananse kaa sɛ, Fa bi hyɛ ma me na menhwɛ. Na ɛhyɛɛ prɛte bi a ɛhyɛ aduhuam so ma, na Ananse dii. Da biara a ɔsɔre no, na ɛte saa. Afei, Ntikuma hui sɛ n'agya annya ahoɔden, ɛmfa ho sɛ na ɔne no nnidi no, na Ntikuma hwɛ n'agya na ɔhwɛ nea n'agya nsa aka no. Bere a n'agya kɔɔ wuram no, Ntikuma foro kɔɔ (ɔdan no so) na ɔhuu prɛte no. Ɔfrɛɛ ne na ne ne nuanom, na wɔkɔɔ soro. Ntikuma kae sɛ: Saa aduan yi yɛ fɛ. Ɛkae sɛ: Me din de beautiful. Ɔkaa sɛ: Ɛnneɛ, wo din de dɛn? Ɛkae sɛ: Me din de Fill-up-some-and-eat. Ɔkaa sɛ: Fa kakra hyɛ ma menhwɛ. Na prɛte no de nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn gu so. Ntikuma ne ne na ne ne ne nuanom didii kosii sɛ biribiara wiee. Afei Ntikuma bisaa aduan no sɛ, Ɛdeɛn na w'ani gye ho? Ɛnde aduane no kaa sɛ, Mikyi nneduaba ne kurukuru kuruwa ketewa bi. Ntikuma ka kyerɛɛ Afudotwedotwe (Belly-like-to-burst) sɛ, Kɔ na kɔfa bi mma me. Na ɔde maa wɔn, na ɔfaa nneduaba kuruwa no de kaa (aduan no), ne kurukuru kuruwa ketewa no nso de kaa (aduan no). Afei wɔn nyinaa baa fam. Wɔfrɛɛ Ananse; obuae sɛ, Ɛyɛ sɛ moantie nea mekae no; mekae sɛ, efi nnɛ, sɛ meba fie a mede aduan ba a, mobɛtumi adi, efisɛ mo na ehia mo. Aso ne ne mma didii. Agya Spider hohoroo; ɔkɔɔ ɔdan no so; ɔkae sɛ, Saa aduan yi yɛ fɛ. Saa aduan yi yɛ fɛ. Saa aduan yi yɛ fɛ. Saa dinn yi yɛ fɛ. Saa dinn yi yɛ fɛ. Papa Spider kae sɛ, Ah! ɛbɛyɛ sɛ saa ntama yi nyɛ fɛ; mɛkɔ akɔfa nea ɛwɔ Oyoko abusua tartan no mu na memfa no. Na ɔkɔɔ fam kɔfaa nea ɛwɔ Oyoko abusua tartan no mu no. Ɔde ne mpaboa hyɛe; ɔsan foro kɔɔ hɔ (san). ɔkae sɛ: Dua yi yɛ fɛ. Mmɔborɔhunu a edi mũ! Dua yi yɛ fɛ. Mmɔborɔhunu a edi mũ. Ɔhwɛɛ dan no ho hyiae na ohui sɛ akode bi wɔ hɔ, na kuroboa kuruwa ketewa bi wɔ mu. Ananse kae sɛ: Ɛnyɛ ade biako, ɛnyɛ nneɛma abien (a ɛkyerɛ sɛ akyinnye biara nni ho), ɛyɛ Ntikuma. Ananse bubuu ade no, na ɔbaa fam; oyii Oyoko tartan nwera no de too fam, na ɔkɔɔ wuram. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ohui sɛ Mpere a ne ho yɛ fɛ paa (nea wɔfrɛ no) mpire no sɛn hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ: Oh, ɛyɛ nwonwa! Eyi yɛ fɛ sen nea edi kan no; saa mpire yi yɛ fɛ. Mpire no kae sɛ, Me din de beautiful. Ɔ (Spider) kae sɛ, Ɛnneɛ wo din de dɛn? Ɛkae sɛ, Me din de Abiridiabrada (a ɛkyerɛ sɛ, Swish-and-raise-weals). Na ɔkae sɛ, Swish kakra ma menhu. Na mpire no hwee ne so, Swish, swish, swish. Papa Spider teɛteɛɛm sɛ, Pui! pui! Nnomaa bi (a ɔte nkyɛn) ka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, Say Adwobere (a ɛkyerɛ sɛ, seesei-na-easy-now). Na Ananse kae sɛ, Adwobere. Na mpire no gyaee sɛ ɛbɛn no. Na Ananse de mpire yi brɛɛ fie; na ɔkɔɔ hɔ de no too ɔdan no so. Aso wiee aduan no siee, na ɔkae sɛ: Ananse, bra didi. Obuae sɛ: Esiane sɛ woda so wɔ ha (asase so) nti ebia wunni ɔhɔ wɔ w'aso mu (na wontie nea meka no), merenni. Ananse foro kɔɔ soro kɔtenaa ase dinn. Afei ɔsan baa fam bio; na ɔsan kɔɔ baabi kɔhintawee. Afei Ntikuma foro kɔɔ soro. Ntikuma kae sɛ: "Me maame, Nyiwankonfwea, Afudotwedotwe, bra ha, na nea agya de bae (saa bere yi) sen nea otwa to koraa". Na wɔn nyinaa foro kɔɔ (ɔdan no so). Ananse sɔre gyinaa nkyɛn, na ɔteɛɛm sɛ, "Fa hyɛ, fa hyɛ, titiriw wɔ Ntikuma so, fa hyɛ no so". Afei bere a Ananse hwɛɛ na ohui sɛ wɔhwee wɔn yiye no, ɔkae sɛ, "Adwobere" (Kye-na-ease-se-se-se-se). Ananse bae bɛfaa mpire no, na ɔtwee no nketewa nketewa na ɔhwetee. Eyi na ɛmaa mpire no baa abusuakuw no mu. Enti ɛba sɛ sɛ woka biribi kyerɛ wo ba, na ɔrentie wo a, yɛhwee no.

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
