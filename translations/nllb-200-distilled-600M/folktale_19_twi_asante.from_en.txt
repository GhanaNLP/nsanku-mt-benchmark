ASƐM A ƐDA SO Nea Ɛyɛe a Wɔkyeree Mmofra (Nea Edi Kan)

WƆKA sɛ (bere bi) ɔkɔm kɛse baa, na Agya Ananse, Spider no, ne ne yere Aso, ne ne mma, Ntikuma, Nyiwankonfwea (Thin-Shanks), Afudotwedotwe (Belly-like-to-burst), ne Tikonokono (Big-Big-head), sii akuraa ketewa bi tenaa mu. Afei da koro, Paapa Ananse kɔɔ wuram hɔ, na ohui sɛ afuw fɛfɛ bi gyina hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, Saa afuw yi yɛ fɛfɛ. Afuw no kae sɛ, Me din de fɛfɛfɛ. Spider kae sɛ, Wo din de dɛn? Ɛbuaa sɛ, Me din de, Fill-up-some-and-eat. Spider kae sɛ, Fill some so that I may see. Afuw no hyɛɛ palmi-nsuo ma, na Ananse dii nyinaa. Bere a owiee no, obisaa aduan no sɛ, "Dɛn ne wo mmara?" Dwumadi no buae sɛ, "Mikyi akodeɛ, ne kuruwa ketewaa bi". Spider no faa aduan no kɔɔ fie, na ɔkɔtenaa ɔdan no atifi. Afei Ananse kɔɔ wuram kɔfaa aduan, na Aso, bere a otwaa aduan wiei no, ɔfrɛɛ Ananse, na ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, wo na wuhia ankasa, na me de, meyɛ ɔpanyin, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mede aduan yɛ, wo de ne mmofra yi na wuhia ankasa; sɛ woawie a, na m'aso ayɛ komm (wɔ w'awerɛhow nne no mu). Bere a wowiei adidi no, na Spider pa Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me din de sɛn?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me din de Fill-up-some-and-eat". Ananse kae sɛ, "Fill up some for me to see". Na ɔhyɛɛ abodoo bi mu nsã a ɛyɛ nsã, na Ananse dii. Da biara a ɔsɔre no, na ɛte saa. Afei, Ntikuma hui sɛ n'agya anhinhim, ɛmfa ho sɛ na ɔne no nnidi no, na Ntikuma hwɛɛ n'agya so na ohuu nea n'agya nsa aka. Bere a n'agya fii hɔ kɔɔ wuram no, Ntikuma foro kɔɔ atifi (ɔdan no atifi) na ohuu prɛte no. Ɔfrɛɛ ne maame ne ne nuanom, na wɔkɔɔ soro. Ntikuma kae sɛ, Saa aduan yi yɛ fɛ. Ɛkae sɛ, Me din de beautiful. Ɔkae sɛ, Ɛnneɛ wo din de dɛn? Ɛkae sɛ, Me din de Fill-up-some-and-eat. Ɔkae sɛ, Fa kakra hyɛ ma menhwɛ. Na wɔde nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn nsã hyɛɛ prɛte no ma. Ntikuma ne ne na ne ne ne nuanom didii kosii sɛ biribiara wiee. Na afei Ntikuma bisaa aduane no sɛ, Ɛdeɛn na w'ani gye ho? Ɛnneɛ aduane no kaa sɛ, Mikyi afurum kuruwa ne kuruwa ketewa bi. Ntikuma ka kyerɛɛ Afudotwedotwe (Belly-like-to-burst) sɛ, Kɔ na kɔfa bi mma me. Na ɔde wɔn bae, na ɔfaa afurum kuruwa no de kaa ho (aduane no), na kuruwa kuruwa kuruwa ketewa no nso de kaa ho (aduane no). Afei wɔn nyinaa sianee. Na Paapa Spider (bere a etwa to no) bae (fi nnuaba no mu). Aso wiee wɔn a wodii wɔn aduan no. Wɔfrɛɛ Ananse, na obuae sɛ: "Ebetumi aba sɛ montee nea mekae no; mekae sɛ, efi nnɛ, sɛ mede aduan ba fie a, mobɛtumi adi, efisɛ mo na ehia mo. Aso ne ne mma didii. Agya Spider hohoroo; ɔforo kɔɔ ɔdan no so; ɔkae sɛ, Saa aduan yi yɛ fɛ. Mmɔborɔhunu nyinaa! Saa aduan yi yɛ fɛ. Mmɔborɔhunu nyinaa! Agya Spider kae sɛ, Ah! ɛbɛyɛ sɛ saa ntama yi nyɛ fɛ; mɛkɔ akɔfa nea ɛwɔ Oyoko abusua tartan no mu na memfa no. Na ɔkɔɔ fam akɔfa Oyoko abusua tartan ntama no a wɔbɛhyɛ. Ɔde ne mpaboa hyɛe; ɔforo kɔɔ hɔ bio. Ɔkae sɛ, Dua yi yɛ fɛ. Dua yi yɛ fɛ. Dua yi yɛ fɛ. Dua yi yɛ fɛ. Dua yi yɛ fɛ. Dua yi yɛ fɛ. Dua yi yɛ fɛ. Akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ), ɛyɛ Ntikuma. Ananse bubuu prɛte no, na ɔbaa fam; oyii Oyoko tartan nwera no de too hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ wuram. Eyi yɛ fɛ sen nea edi kan no; saa mpire yi yɛ fɛ. Mpire no kae sɛ, Me din de beautiful. Ɔ (Spider) kae sɛ, Ɛnneɛ wo din de dɛn? Ɛkae sɛ, Me din de Abiridiabrada (a ɛkyerɛ sɛ, Swish-and-raise-weals). Na ɔkae sɛ, Swish kakra ma menhu. Na mpire no hwee ne so, Swish, swish, swish. Agya Spider teɛteɛɛm sɛ, Pui! pui! Anomaa bi (a ɔte nkyɛn baabi) ka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, Ka Adwobere (a ɛkyerɛ, awɔw-ne-mmoa-se-se-se). Ananse kae sɛ, Adwobere. Na mpire no gyaee sɛ ɛrebɔ no. Na Ananse de mpire yi brɛɛ fie; na ɔkɔe de no too ɔdan no so. Aso wiee aduan no, ɔkae sɛ: "Ananse, bra didi". Obuae sɛ: "Esiane sɛ wowɔ ha (asase so) nti ebia worenhu po wɔ w'aso mu (na wontie nea mekae no), enti merennidi. Ananse foro kɔɔ soro kɔtenaa ase komm, na ɔsan kɔɔ fam bio, na ɔsan kɔɔ baabi kɔhintawee. Afei Ntikuma foro kɔɔ soro. Ɔkae sɛ: "Ao, m'agya de biribi aba bio". Ntikuma kae sɛ: "Me maame, Nyiwankonfwea, Afudotwedotwe, bra ha, na nea agya de bae (saa bere yi) sen nea otwa to koraa". Ntikuma kae sɛ: "Eyi yɛ fɛ", na ɔkae sɛ: "Wɔamfrɛ me fɛfɛ". Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Wo din de dɛn?" Na ɔkae sɛ: "Wɔfrɛ me fɛfɛfo". Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Ma menhwɛ kakra". Ananse sɔre gyinaa nkyɛn, na ɔteɛɛm sɛ, "Fa hyɛ, fa hyɛ, titiriw wɔ Ntikuma so, fa hyɛ no so". Afei bere a Ananse hwɛɛ na ohui sɛ wɔhwe wɔn yiye no, ɔkae sɛ, "Adwobere" (Kye na ɛyɛ mmerɛw-se-se-se-se). Enti sɛ woka biribi kyerɛ wo ba na ɔmpɛ sɛ otie wo a, yɛto no mpire.

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
