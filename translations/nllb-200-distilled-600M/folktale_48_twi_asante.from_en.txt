BERE bi na ɔkɔm kɛse aba, na Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ Esono, Elephant no nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Father Elephant, ma yɛnkɔ (na yɛnhwehwɛ) wuram yams. Elephant no kae sɛ, Bra, ma yɛnkɔ. Ɔno ne Kwaku, Spider no fii hɔ. Saa bere no, na Elephant no wɔ akyirikyiri paa; na bere a ɔne Kwaku Ananse rebɛfa hɔ no, na Elephant no mfimfini abɛda wɔn ho mu, enti na wontumi ntew wɔn ho da. Afei Kwaku Ananse hwɛɛ no twaa n'ani hwɛɛ no, na ɔfrɛɛ Elephant no kae sɛ, Ma meyi wo akyi no bi ntwa sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a yɛne wo bɛtena yiye ntɛm. Elephant no kae sɛ, Ma yɛnhwɛ (sɛ ɛyɛ papa anaa). Enti Spider no twaa nam, nkɛntɛn abiɛsa a nam ahyɛ mu ma, fii n'akyi. Ananse de too ne ti so. Ɔka kyerɛɛ Asono no sɛ, Mede eyi bɛto me fie na maba. Ananse baa fie; ɔkae sɛ, Aso (ne yere) adaadaa Asono a ɔyɛ nkwaseafo mu panyin no, na makyi nam nyinaa afi ne mfem. Aso buae sɛ, Nhyira nka wo, Apeafo.
