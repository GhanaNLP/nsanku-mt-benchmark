ASƐM A ƐDA SO Ɔman no Mu Ɔsɔretia Fii Ase

Ná ɔbarima bi wɔ hɔ a wɔfrɛ no Nea Ɔkyi Nea Ɔkyi, na esiane eyi nti ɔkɔe na osii kurow ketewa bi, na ɔkɔtraa mu. Na aboa bi a wɔfrɛ no Duyker baa ne nkyɛn, na ɔne no kɔe kɔtenaa dua bi ase, na dua no mu nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn bi hwehwee ase. Ɔbarima no kae sɛ: "Agya a ɔmpɛ sɛ wɔtwe wo ho fi wo ho no, wo nsɔe no ayɛ frɔmfrɔm". Nea ɔmpɛ sɛ wɔtwe wo ho fi wo ho no kae sɛ: "Saa ara na nsɔe no yɛ. Sɛ ɛyɛ frɔmfrɔm a, ɛyɛ frɔmfrɔm abiɛsa, na sɛ ɛyɛ frɔmfrɔm a, mikum no, na sɛ meyɛ frɔmfrɔm no a, ɛyɛ nkuku abiɛsa a ngo ahyɛ mu ma. me nanabea a ɔwo me maame a ɔwo me no, ɔno nso na ɔwo me; na sɛ me maame a ɔwo me a, na mabegyina hɔ". Duyker no kae sɛ, "Eyi nyinaa akyi no, woadi atoro". Na ɔtan a wɔmpɛ sɛ wɔkasa tia no faa poma de bɔɔ Duyker no ti, na okum no. Afei Abedee antilope ketewa no bae. Na ɔne no kɔe kɔtenaa dua no ase, na asɛm koro no ara na esii. Na saa ara na na ɛte wɔ mmoa nyinaa ho. Afei, Kwaku Ananse, Spider no kɔfaa ne ntama no, na ɔfaa ne kotoku no, na ɔde too n'ani so, na ɔkɔɔ Ɔtan-to-Be-Contradicted akuraa no ase. Ohyiaa no sɛ, "Agya, ahemadakye". Ɔtan-to-be-be-contradicted no buae sɛ, "Y'Aku, na ɛhe na worekɔ?" Osee sɛ, "Merekɔ wo nkyɛn". Na ɔfaa ne dan no de no sii dua no ase. Na wɔn a wɔtan wɔn ho no kae sɛ: Fa aduan ma Spider no nni! Na bere a wɔreyɛ aduan no, Ananse ne Ananse a wɔtan wɔn ho no tenaa dua no ase, na nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn no mu bi hwehwee ase, na Ananse faa wɔn de guu ne kotoku mu, na ɔyɛɛ saa kosii sɛ ne kotoku no hyɛɛ ma, na wɔde aduan no brɛɛ no, na Ananse dii. Na bere a owiee aduan no, nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn no mu bi hwehwee ase, na Ananse kae sɛ: "Agya, w'ani nnye akyinnyegye ho, na wo nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn no apue". Na nea ɔtan akyinnyegye ho no kae sɛ: "Ɛyɛ wɔn su sɛ wɔbɛpue saa ara; na sɛ wɔpue a, wɔpue nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn abiɛsa; na sɛ wɔpue a, mitwa wɔn, na sɛ meboaboa ano no a, wɔyɛ nkuku abiɛsa a ngo ayɛ ma, na mede ngo no kɔ Akas". E, sɛ mɛtɔ Akase ɔbaa panyin bi; Akase ɔbaa panyin no ba bɛwo me nanabea a ɔwo me maame no, na ɔno nso awo me. Ɔkaa sɛ: "Monnni atoro, na mo asɛm no yɛ nokware; na me de, mewɔ okros bi a wogyina m'afuw mu, na bere a wɔanyinyin no, mede nnua aduɔson ason a wɔakyekyere (sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mɛtwe wɔn akɔ fam) bom, nanso saa bere no mpo, wɔantumi ankɔ, enti mekɔdaa m'akyi, na metumi de me mmaransɛm atwe wɔn". D; ɔkyena mɛba abɛhwɛ". Spider no kae sɛ, "Akyinnye biara nni ho". Bere a Spider no rekɔ fie no, ɔkyekyeree nkotodwe a ɔboaboaa ano no, na ɔtwee wɔn guu kwan no so. Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma fii ase yerae no, ɔtanfo no fii ase kɔɔ Spider no akuraa ase. Afei bere a Spider no dii kan bae (da a edi hɔ) no, ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ ne mma no sɛ, "Ɔbarima bi bɛba ha, na ɔmpɛ sɛ wɔkasa tia no, na sɛ ɔba na obisa me ho nsɛm a, ɛsɛ sɛ woka kyerɛ no sɛ, da a edi hɔ no, meka kyerɛɛ no sɛ merekɔ baabi, bere a m'afurum bubuu m'anim wɔ mmeae nson, na ɛsɛ sɛ mede no kɔdansifo bi nkyɛn kɔsiesie no, na esiane sɛ wantumi anwie no wɔ adwumam nti. Bere bi akyi no, ɔkae sɛ wakɔyɛ adwuma no wie. Ankyɛ koraa na ɔtan a wɔmpɛ sɛ wɔkasa tia no bae. Ɔbisaa sɛ, Wo papa akɔ he? Wobuae sɛ, Aah, papa kɔɔ baabi ɛnnɛ, na ne penis no bubuu wɔ mmeae ahorow nson, na ɔfaa no kɔmaa dwetimfoɔ bi, nanso wantumi anwie adwuma no saa bere no, na papa kɔmaa no wiee, na (wo,) Agya, wunhuu mogya no wɔ kwan no so anaa? Ɔtan a ɛne no nhyia kae sɛ, Mihuu no. Ɔtan a ɛne no nhyia bisaa sɛ, Na (The Spider's child replied,) Mother, too, yesterday she went to the stream, and her waterpot would have fallen and broken had she didn't save it from doing so by just catching it in time, but she didn't quite finish saving it from falling, and has returned today to do so. Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted no anka hwee. Afei Ananse baa hɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Pɔw aduan bi ma wɔn a wɔtan wɔn ho no nni". Bere a na mmofra no reyɛ aduan no, na wɔde sika kɔkɔɔ baako pɛ pɛ na ɛyɛ, nanso na ɛyɛ pii; na wɔhyew apiti no paa. Bere a wowiee no, wɔde kɔkɔɔ no maa nea ɔtan wɔn ho no. Na nea ɔtan wɔn ho no dii. Afei apiti no yɛɛ no yaw, na ɔpɛe sɛ owu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ntikuma (Spider) ba no, ma me nsu mmrɛ me mmɛnom". Ntikuma kɔɔ baabi a nsu wɔ, nanso wamfa nsu no amma. Ntikuma kae sɛ, Ah, nsu a yɛwɔ wɔ ha (wɔ yɛn asuti mu) no yɛ nneɛma abiɛsa; nea ɛyɛ agya dea no di kan, nea ɛyɛ me maame yere dea no wɔ mfinimfini, na nea ɛyɛ me maame dea no wɔ asuti no ase; na esiane sɛ mepɛ sɛ mema wo nsa ka nsu a ɛyɛ me maame dea no nko ara nti, sɛ menyɛ saa a, monhwɛ yiye bere a mobɛtwe no a, ɛde abusua mu akasakasa bɛba. Ɔtan a wɔkasa tia no kae sɛ, Wo ba ketewa, wudi atoro. Ɛhɔ ara, Ananse kae sɛ, Bɔ no na onwu. Ɔtan a wɔkasa tia no kae sɛ, Dɛn nti na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔboro me na mewu? Spider kae sɛ, Woka sɛ wokyi sɛ obi bɛkasa tia wo, nanso woakasa tia obi, ɛno nti na meka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wɔboro wo sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wubekum wo Ɛno nti na nnipa pii a wɔmpɛ sɛ wɔsɔre tia no no wɔ abusuakuw no mu.

Eyi, me ho asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
