Na wɔhunuu ɔsebɔ no nipadua

NA NWANSENA NE ƆSEBƆ BI TE WƆ HƆ, NA ƆSEBƆ NO KA KYERƐƐ NWANSENA NO SƐ, "Yɛnkɔ nkɔhwehwɛ mmaa mma nware. " Nwansena no kae sɛ, "Ho. " Ɛnna ɔsebɔ no dwareɛ, na ɔde ngo siesiee ne ho, na ɔde ne sika nneɛma siesiee ne ho, na ɔkyekyeree ne mpa a ɔda so dedaw no maa no sɛ ɔmfa nkɔ ne ti so. Na wɔtu kwan, wɔbaa akuraa bi ase. Ɛno na Nwansena no kae sɛ, "Maame nyinaa ne papa nyinaa, meka mo anwummere nkra. " Wɔn nso buae sɛ, "Yɛn kunu pa. " Leopard no nso bɛduruu hɔ, ɔkae sɛ, "Maame nyinaa ne papa nyinaa, meka mo anwummere nkra. " Wɔbuae sɛ, "Wode ano bɔne bɛbɛma yɛn anwummere nkra? " Wɔtuu kwan, na wɔduruu akuraa a ɛdi hɔ no. Wansana, the Fly, kae sɛ, "Mma nyinaa ne Agya nyinaa, mehyɛ mo anwummere nkwa. " Wɔbuaa saa ara. Leopard no nso duu hɔ na ɔhyɛɛ wɔn nkwa na wɔbuae.

Wɔn a wode w'ano bɔ bɔne no, wopɛ anwummere nkyea? Wɔde wɔn ho sii hɔ, wɔkɔɔ kwan no so, Leopard no kae sɛ, "Hwɛ ha! Fa mat dedaw no ba me na fa sika kɔkɔɔ nneɛma yi siesie wo ho, sɛ mat dedaw no nti na mmeawa no dɔ wo a, me bɛhu. " Na (Fly) no gye nneɛma no, siesiee ne ho, na wɔde wɔn ho kɔɔ akuraa a ɛdi hɔ no ase. Na Leopard no wɔ anim, na ntama dedaw a ɛwɔ ne ti so no, ɔde ne nsa kyerɛɛ nkurasefoɔ no. Wɔkae sɛ, "Mma yɛn nni nsa, wo a wode ano bɔne no, ɛfata sɛ wosoa saa mpa dada yi. " Ɛnna Fly no baa hɔ, ɔkae sɛ, "Mma nyinaa ne Agya nyinaa, me ma mo anwummere nkra. " Na wɔbuae sɛ, "Owo dɔfo, ɛha ara na ɛwɔ aberanteɛ bi a ɔfata sɛ yɛhwɛ no, sika kɔkɔɔ nneɛma, sɛnea wɔyɛ wo, sɛ yɛnni nkonim a yɛbɛnya no, anka yɛbɛka wo ho wɔ wo. akwantu." Na wɔkɔɔ so. Bere a woduu kwan no so no, Leopard no kae sɛ, "Hey! adamfo, gyina hɔ, yi me sika kɔkɔɔ nneɛma no ntɛm ntɛm ntɛm. " Bere a Fly no asa ayi agude no ama no no, Leopard no hwɛɛ kwan no so na ɔde vine kyekyeree Fly no. Ɔkae sɛ, “Mɛkyekyere wo, na wanya asɛm bi ka? ” Na Fly no kae sɛ, “Me nni hwee ka. ” Leopard no kɔɔ so kae sɛ, “Me ne wo nnya nnu baabi a yɛrekɔ no, nanso mmea no de wo ho akɔ wɔn ani so. ” Afei Leopard no kuraa no na ɔde no kyekyeree dua bi ho. Na ɔsan nso kɔɔ baabi foforo. Ɛnkyɛreeɛ bi, Plantain no twaa kwan kɔɔ gua so, na berɛ a ɔhunuu no (Fly no) ɔkaa sɛ:

“Hwan na ɔsɛn wɔ hɔ a ɔyɛ tuntum koraa?

Hwan na ɔsɛn wɔ hɔ a ɔhyerɛn paa?

(Nwansena) kae:

Me ne Fly a mepɛ Kwao Boakye.

Adenen!

Gyahene Kofi Amo, Aden, w'ani nnye!

Ɔkaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meba, na yɛatumi akɔhwehwɛ mmabaa.

Adenen!

Mmeawa no ka sɛ wɔmpɛ no.

Adenen!

Ɔkyekyeree me ankasa.

Adenen!

Ɔakyekyere me koraa.

Adenen!

Ɔgye, nnye me.

Adenen!

Berɛ a wogye me no, fa kwan no nkɔ.

Adenen!” Nanso Plantain no kae sɛ, “Leopard no akyekyere wo, enti mentumi nnya wo”; na ɔkɔɔ. Amankani, Coco-yam no, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Aburo no, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Atwibo no, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Adua no, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Aka no, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Akapinkye, Red-roasted plantain no, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Bankye, Cassava no, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Brofere no, Paw-paw no, bae (Na kɔɔ saa ara; Emo, aburow no, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Efrere, akutu no, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Habayere, kuro-yam no, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Kwadu, borɔde no, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Kontomere, koko-yam nhaban no, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Krobona, bankye no, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Mako, kɛntɛn kɔkɔɔ no, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara) na Nkatee, asase mu-nnuaba, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Nwiadowa, turo-akokora, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Nkuruma, okro, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; na seesei Dokon’fa, Half-a-ball-of-Kenki (Boiled-pounded-corn), bae bɛkaa sɛ:

Hwan na ɔsɛn hɔ a ɔyɛ tuntum koraa?

Hwan na ɔsɛn wɔ hɔ a ɔhyerɛn paa?

(Nwansena) kae:

Me ne nwansena no, Kwao Boakye, Adenen!

Gyahene Kofi Amo, Aden, w'ani nnye!

Ɔkaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meba, na yɛatumi akɔhwehwɛ mmabaa.

Adenen!

Mmeawa no ka sɛ wɔmpɛ no.

Adenen!

Ɔkyekyeree me ankasa.

Adenen!

Ɔakyekyere me koraa.

Adenen!

Ɔgye, nnye me.

Adenen!

Berɛ a wogye me no, fa kwan no nkɔ.

Adenen!"

Bɔɔl fã bi a ɛyɛ Kenkii kae sɛ, "Meate, mebɛgya wo. " Ɔkɔɔ hɔ na ɔgyaa no. Bere a wayɛ saa no, mpofirim ara na Leopard no puee. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wama me barima no adwo no? " Ɔ (Half-a-ball-of-Kenki) kaa sɛ, "Meama no adwo, na nea wobɛyɛ biara, yɛ. " Ɔ (Leopard) kaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, me ne wo rennyɛ hwee koraa, me ne wo nko ara na yɛde yɛn nsa bɛko. " Half-a-ball-of-Kenki kaa sɛ, "Sɛ yɛbɛko a, yɛnkɔ kwaeɛ na yɛnntwa nnua nsi gya. " Enti wɔkɔtwaa nnua. na ɔsan bɛdii. Na ɔkaa sɛ, "Let's set fire to it. " Na leopard no de ogya too so. Saa bere koro no ara na wɔbɔɔ ko, (and the sound of it was like) Yiri! Yiri! Yiri! Na leopard no apaapae no (from him) no, na atow no, bam! Half-a-ball-of-Kenki kae sɛ, "(adeɛ a ɛte sɛɛ nnyɛ hwee), obi fa adeɛ a ɛte sɛɛ de hohoro baabi a ɔyere hohoro." Na wɔhyiaa bio. Na Half-a-ball-of-Kenki nso atow Leopard no afiri ne ho, bam! Na Leopard no kae sɛ, "Me nso de adeɛ a ɛte sɛɛ bɛhohoro me yere fie, (nanso) wɔ yɛn nhyiamu a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a obi bɛtow ɔfoforo no agu no, ɛnneɛ ɛno ne tow a ɛtwa toɔ. " Na wɔde wɔn ho bɔɔ mu (akodi no, nnyigyei no yɛɛ te sɛ) Kikiri! Kikiri! Kikiri! Na Half-a-ball-of-Kenki maa Leopard no na wɔtoo no guu ogya mu. Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "_Me firim, me firim, me firim_". Saa na ɔkraman no nyaa ne nteɛm no "_Me firim_". Ɛnna wɔhunuu sɛ ne nipadua no te sɛɛ, kurukyire, kurukyire, kurukyire nyinaa (a ɛkyerɛ sɛ ne nyinaa yɛ nsensanee). baabi a na aborɔfoɔ no da hɔ no, ɛhɔ na nsaman no aka no; na baabi a na kɔkɔɔ no da hɔ no, ɛhɔ nso na na kɔkɔɔ nnuadewa no aka no. Na sɛ worenantew a, na wuhu ahaban no a ɛda hɔ a wɔde Half-a-ball-of-Kenki (akyekyere) no mu, wobɛhu nwansena pii a wɔrebu no; na ɛno nso nnyɛ nti biara (save only this), wɔrehyɛ no ayɛ.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebia wobɛtumi afa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no nso wobɛtumi nkamfo me.
