Na Babadua sare no nyaa nkwaa (joints) wɔ ne dua no so.

Ɔbaa bi hyɛɛ yafunu na ɔkɔɔ anyame nketewa bi nkyɛn kɔbisaa no asɛm. Na ɔnyame no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛbɛyɛ sɛ bere a wode abofra no bɛwo no, ɛbɛwu, nanso mebɛyɛ nea metumi biara de ama wo, nanso ade a ɛsɛ sɛ meka kyerɛ wo ne sɛ, abofra no yɛ (ankasa) ɔhemmaa maame ba a ɔne Nyame, ɔsoro-nyame no wɔ hɔ, na bere a wode no bɛwo no, mma no nnyɛ hwee, nanso bere a wosɔre no, ɛsɛ sɛ woha no, wode ngo gu no, na wode no si akonnwa so. " Of a true N’abofra no na woo no. Te sɛ mmofra a wɔwɔ akɔre nsɛm mu no nyinaa, ɛnkyɛ na ɛnyin. Wɔfrɛɛ abofra no Akosua Denta. Sɛnea ɔnyame ketewa no kyerɛɛ kwan no, na maame no, bere a ɔsɔreɛ, hohoroo no, de ngo guu no, na noa aduane bi maa no, na afei ɔkɔɔ n’afuo mu. Mprempren, da bi a na onni bere a ɔde bɛnoa biribi mma no, ɔde ahyɛde maa ne nkoa ba, Kra, sɛ, "Merehyɛn afuo no, enti noa biribi mma Akosua Denta nni. " Na ɛna no tuu kwan. Ɛnneɛ, bere a Kra awie noa plantain no, ɔkae sɛ, "Akosua Denta, bra ha na bɔ saa _fufu_ yi mu, wo ne wo Ɔhemmaa no asɛm no. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me mmra. " Kra kae sɛ, "Bra, wo nso, wo ho ahantan. " Na Akosua Denta sɔre kɔe, ɔkɔgyee pestle no, ɔbɔɔ _fufu_ no. Bere a na wawie no, ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔdae wɔ dan no mu, ɔde n’atadeɛ na ɔkataa ne ti. Kra kɔɔ n’akyi, ɔkae sɛ, "Denta"; mmuae biara nni hɔ. Ɔyii atadeɛ no firi ne ti so; na Akosua Denta awu; ɔde atadeɛ no san kɔɔ ne so. Ɛnkyɛree no, maame no san bae firi afuo mu. Bere a ɔbae no, ɔanhwehwɛ ne ba ntɛm, na mmom ɔkɔɔ ogya no ho kɔdii aduane. Ɛnna ɔkae sɛ, "Kra, Dɛn na Denta wɔ? " Ɔkae sɛ, "nananom, ne aduane a me noa no de too hɔ no mpo, ɛhɔ na ɛda so ara gyina. " Maame no kae sɛ, "mete. " Ɛnna maame no kɔɔ dan no mu sɛ ɔbɛfa ntama bi a ɔbɛhyɛ akɔ abontene akɔhwehwɛ ne ba. Bere a ɔkɔɔ dan no mu no, na adeɛ bi a ɛyɛ sum da hɔ wɔ nkotodwe mu. Ɔmaa ntama no so, na ɔhunuu sɛ Akosua Denta awu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Kra, wose Denta akɔ abontene, na afei ɔwɔ ha awu? " Kra kaa sɛ, "nananom mehuu. " Ɛnna ɔbaa no kyekyeree ntama abɔso ne kɔn mu na ɔde ne ba (awu) no kɔɔ n'akyi, na ɔne no puee abontene, na ɔteɛm; na akuraa no panyin bae, na ɔkae sɛ, "Fa wo ba no wo akyi na yɛnkɔsie no. " Ɔbaa no buae sɛ:

Na me ne me ba Akosua Denta, Na mehyɛɛ no nyinsɛn, me ne no kɔɔ nsuo-hwɛfoɔ nkyɛn.

Wɔka sɛ, 'Ɛyɛ ɔhemmaa ba.'

Mepɛ sɛ obi kɔka kyerɛ hɔ sɛ, Ɔhemmaa ma ba, Siankwa Adamowa ba no, eyi, me ba, me nsie no wɔ ha.

Na ɔde ne ho kɔɔ akuraa bi ase, na ɔduruu hɔ no, akuraa no panyin kae sɛ, "Yi wo ba no fi wo akyi na wo mmɛto mu, efisɛ yɛbɔ ho taboo. " Nanso ɔbaa no kae sɛ:

Na me ne me ba Akosua Denta, Na mehyɛɛ no nyinsɛn, me ne no kɔɔ nsuo-hwɛfoɔ nkyɛn.

Wɔka sɛ, 'Ɛyɛ ɔhemmaa ba.'

Mepɛ sɛ obi kɔka kyerɛ hɔ sɛ, Ɔhemmaa ma ba, Siankwa Adamowa ba no, eyi, me ba, me nsie no wɔ ha.

Na ɔsan fii aseɛ kɔɔ. Ɔnam kwaeɛ bi a emu dɔ mu, na ɛnkyɛre no, abofra no abɔ, na ɔde ne ho kɔtwee ne ho kɔtoo fam, na ɔsan baa fie. Na ahenkuro no panyin bisaa no sɛ, "Ɛhe na abofra no wɔ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "me de no tuu no kɔɔ. " Ahenkuro no panyin no kaa sɛ, "W'animguaseɛ ne sɛ, sɛ woama kwan wɔ ha a, anka ɛsɛ sɛ yɛbɛsie no wɔ nsaman yi mu, anka ɛbɛyɛ nea ɛfata. " Saa ara na mpanimfoɔ no ka sɛ, "Sɛ wo ba bi wuwu a, ɛnneɛ di awerɛhoɔ wɔ moderation mu, mma no nka wo. Esiane sɛ saa ɔbaa no yɛɛ saa, na ɔtow ne ba no guu no bere a na ɛnsɛ sɛ ɔyɛ saa." Na afei ɔbaa no kae sɛ, "Kra yi deɛ, fa no na tow no gu akukuo mu na bɔ no mma me. " Afei akuraafoɔ no kɔɔ hɔ (baabi a Kra wɔ no, na na ɛyɛ nnyigyei a wɔde bɔ) Pu! Pu! Pu! na wɔbɔɔ no kɔsii sɛ ɔyɛɛ mmerɛw koraa. Na afei na aka "ɔdomfoɔ no"; bere a wɔbɔɔ no no, na ɛyɛ den, na bere a wɔbɔɔ no no, ɛhuruw kɔbɔɔ Babadua sare no ho. Ɛno nti na Babadua sare no wɔ akwansereɛ, tete no na ɛyɛ trom, trom, ɛno ne ɛyɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebia wobɛtumi afa no sɛ nokware, na nea aka no wobɛtumi akamfo me sɛ meka kyerɛɛ no.
