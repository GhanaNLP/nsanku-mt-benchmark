Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA OBI NYAA YERE (NA ƆSAN NSO GYEE NE NKWA) DENAM NSƐNKYERƐNNEDE AHOROW A ƆKYERƐƐ ASE SO

Ɛbɛyɛɛ bere bi a na mmarima mmiɛnsa bi te hɔ na wɔpɛɛ adwuma. Wɔkɔɔ Tall-grass-country sɛ wɔbɛtɔ ntama nkyekye ne nneɛma foforɔ. Wɔn a wɔsan baa Ashanti no, wɔmaa wɔn nua ba no de nneɛma no bi kɔeɛ, na wɔkɔɔ so tɔn. Wɔbaeɛ kuro kɛseɛ bi ase. Ɛwɔ kuro no mu no, na ɔhemmaa ba baa bi a ɔpoo sɛ wɔbɛma no obiara awareɛ. Ɛnneɛ amanfoɔ yi (aguadifoɔ no) kɔɔ hɔ nanso Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba akɔbɔ nsuo no. Nanso Ɔhemmaa Maame no tɔɔ ntama mmienu de siee hɔ maa ne ba. Bere a ne ba no san bae no, ɔhunuu mmarima mmiɛnsa no. Ɔkae kyerɛ ne nananom sɛ, "Ɛnnɛ mehuu nea mepɛ no nti kum ɔpo bi mfa no nkyerɛ wɔn. " Bere a wɔawie aduane no no, abaayewa no kɔfa asono sare, cotton, kola nut, ne nnuaba a wɔayam kɔde guu aduane no so ma ɛkɔkyerɛ mmarima no. Na ɔkae sɛ, "Nea obetumi akyerɛkyerɛ wɔn nteaseɛ mu no, me ne no bɛware. " Na wɔde aduane no kɔe. Mmarima no bɛdaa no adi, na wɔkae sɛ, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ Ɔhemmaa no ba a ɔde aduane yi bae a, anka yɛbɛka nsɛm bi de abisa no. " Mmarima no nua barima no kae sɛ, "Sɛ me nni wo ho suro a, anka metumi aka saa nneɛma no nteaseɛ. " Wɔkae sɛ, "Monkyerɛkyerɛ mu. " Abofra no buae sɛ, "Abaayewa no ka sɛ ɔayɛ n’adwene abɔ te sɛ cotton no, na sɛ ɔbɛtumi anya yɛn mu biako a yɛaba yi. Ɛha, ɛnsɛ sɛ ɔware na? Anantwi sare no nteaseɛ ne sɛ, da bi bɛba a ɔbɛyera; kola nut no nso nteaseɛ ne sɛ da bi bɛba a ɔbɛma no akye. Asase mu nnuaba kyerɛ sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ wo dua asase mu nnuaba no na ama afe biara, bere a ɔkɔhwɛ ne ntini no, ɔbɛnya bi atutu." Enti Ɔhemmaa no ba waree mmarima no babarima. Bere a ɔwaree no no, na Ɔhemmaa no ba bisa no sɛ, "Wo din dɛn ne? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me din de Kwaku Susufuo," ɛno ne sɛ, Kwaku-a-ɔhaw-dwen. Abaayewa no buaa no sɛ, "Me nso, wɔfrɛ me Akua-a-ɔbɔ-mmɔden-susuw. " Ɔde amanneɛ a ɛfata ware no. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, mmusua mmiɛnsa no tu kwan kɔɔ wɔn kurom. Kwaku-a-ɔbɔ-mmɔden-susuw no tenaa ne yere akuraa ase. Ɔtenaa hɔ afe baako, na bere a ɔsɔreɛ, na ɔne ne nnamfoɔ tena ase di agorɔ wɔ nte marbles ho. Ɛnneɛ, da bi a na ɔredi agorɔ yi, ɔbɔɔ bɔɔl abien wɔ shot bi mu, nanso nnipa a na wɔne no redi agorɔ no kae sɛ wɔremma no, na mmom wɔpɛ sɛ wɔbɔ no. Ɔde n’ani kyerɛɛ n’akyi, na ɔhunuu abofra bi a ɔgyina hɔ, ɔde boɔ bi maa abofra no sɛ ɔmfa nkɔma ne yere. Afei bere a ne yere gyee aboɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "M’aware pɛ sɛ ɔsan kɔ ne kuro mu, efisɛ ɔka sɛ bere a nnomaa bi tra fam kyɛ dodo no, ɔnya aboɔ. " Na Ɔhemmaa no kae sɛ, "Sɛ ɔkɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ wo ne no kɔ. " Nokwarem no, ɔne no kɔɔ akɔnkyɛn. Na wɔne Kwaku-who-takes-trouble-to-think nua mmarima no hyiaa. Mprempren, ɛnkane yi, wɔkae kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Kwaku-a-ɔfa-ɔhaw-susuwii no, fa nkyɛn bi a yɛbɛkɔ kwae hɔ akɔto afiri a yɛakyere pigeons a yɛde ahɔhoyɛ ama wo yere a ɔyɛ ɔhɔhoɔ no nya. " Enti wɔtuu kwan, nanso mpanimfoɔ yi agye atom sɛ wɔbɛkum no. Wɔn ne Kwaku-a ɔde ne ho bɔ ne ho adwene no kɔɔ so; wɔduruu akwansin bi a ɛyɛ ankonam wɔ kwan no so; wɔkyekyeree no kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Yɛbɛkum wo. ” Afei ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, “Bere a mekɔɔ me yere akuraa ase no, mehuu sika kɛseɛ bi, na sɛ woamma me kwan mma me nkyerɛ wo baabi a ɛwɔ, na mewu a, morenhu. ” Wɔbisaa no sɛ, “Sika no wɔ he? ” Ɔbuaeɛ sɛ, “ɛsɛ sɛ mo kae. kɔ me yere fa me kyerɛ no sɛ matɔ nam, na (vendors no) ka sɛ ne bo yɛ peredwan du (£80), na ɛsɛ sɛ ɔhwehwɛ bag a wɔde nwanwa nwoma ayɛ no mu; sɛ ɔhwɛ hɔ na wannya no a, ɔnhwehwɛ Krampan bag no mu, sɛ ɔnhunu no wɔ hɔ a, ɔnhwehwɛ belt a nkrante wɔ mu no mu.” Enti wɔde ne nkra no kɔmaa ne yere. Na ɔno nso kae sɛ, “Nneɛma no wɔ akuraa no panyin fie, enti momma yɛnkɔfa. ” Ɔne wɔn kɔɔ panyin fie; na ɔbaa no ka kyerɛɛ panyin no asɛm no kyerɛkyerɛɛ mu sɛ, “Ntamadan a wɔde nanteɛ nwura ayɛ no kyerɛ sɛ wɔde no akyekyere no; Krampan no kyerɛ sɛ akraman no rebɔ mmɔden sɛ wɔbɛdidi ne nam; Abɛntenten a ɛkura nkrante no kyerɛ sɛ wɔrebɛtwa ne kɔn. ” Sɛ ɔpɛn pɛ a ɔpanyin no bɔɔ gong-gong no sɛ wɔnkɔ hɔ nnye Kwaku-a-ɔyɛ-ɔhaw-susuw. Na wɔne no san baeɛ. Ɔmaa wɔkum no mpanyin mmiɛnsa no. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, “By means of interpreting symbols some one got a wife and (also) saved his life. ” Wei, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi san ba me nkyɛn.
