Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA NO AKOKORƆ NO DUA NO BƐYƐƐ KƆKƆƆ

WƆka sɛ bere bi na na ɔhemmaa maame bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔwoo abaayewa bi a ɔyɛ fɛ. Ɛhɔ na na ɔwɔ, nanso bere a wɔde no bɛhyɛɛ awareɛ ne obiara no, ɔpoo sɛ ɔbɛware. Bere nyinaa na n’akyi nkurɔfo kɔkasa kyerɛ no, na ɔka sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ da. " Mprempren abɔdeɛ no, Onini (Python), wɔ kwaeɛ bi mu, na ɔte abodan kɛseɛ bi mu. Ɛda bi a na ɔwɔ ne abodan mu no, ɔtee abɔmmɔfoɔ bi a wɔretwam, na wɔreka asɛm a ɛfa ababaawa fɛfɛɛfɛ no ho asɛm, sɛnea bere a wɔde no ma obiara awareɛ no, ɔkae sɛ ɔmpɛ no. Ɔnanka no daa hɔ, na ɔkae wɔ ne tirim sɛ, “Me bɛsesa me ho ayɛ me abarimaa fɛfɛɛfɛ, na mɛkɔware Ɔhemmaa Hemma ba no. ” Nokwarem no, ɔwɔ de ne ho danee bɛyɛɛ aberanteɛ, na ɛyɛ nea ɛtumi nna n’ani nkyɛ. Ɔtuu kɔɔ akuraa ase. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na ɔrewe kola-nuts. Berɛ a ɔduruu kuro no ano no, na ɔhemmaa ba a wadi kan akɔbɔ nsuo no asan aba na ɔrekyekyere ntadeɛ no ama ayow. Python no peree, na ɛhwee Queen Mother ba no so nnyigyei. Ɔhemmaa maame no ba no kae sɛ, “Dɛn nti na wode w’ano bɔ m’akutu? ” Python no kae sɛ, “Woteɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ, Amu. ” Ɔkae sɛ, “Me nton nkyia no nyɛ Amu. ” Ɔbuae sɛ, “Woteɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ, Eson. ” Ɔkae sɛ, “Me nton nkyia no nyɛ Eson. ” Ɔbuae sɛ, “Woteɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ, me yere. ” Ɔkae sɛ, “Ɛnneɛ me aware wo. ” Ɔhemmaa maame no ba no ne Python no set o ff ma ne fie. Ɔhemmaa Panyin no ba biara ntɛm ara na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔɔ ne maame nkyɛn na ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛ! Ɛ! Ɛ! (ne ho dwiriw no) reka Mahunu nea mepɛ no. " Ɔhemmaa Panyin no kae sɛ, "At last! Ɛhe na me kunu ba? Ɔkae sɛ, “Me ha na nanana. ” Ne Python no bɔɔ nsam, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Mma wo ne wo yere tra ase anigye so. ” Bere a anwummere bɛyɛɛ dwoo no, ɔne ne yere kɔɔ home. Ɛte sɛɛ na nna nwɔtwe. Akyiri yi ɔkae sɛ, “Me yere, me ne me nkuraase me kɔ nanso merebɛsan aba. ” Ne yere kae sɛ, “Ɛnneɛ me ne wo bɛkɔ abom. ” Python no kae sɛ, “No. ” Python no kasaa na ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛma no kwan sɛ ɔnkɔ, nanso Ɔhemmaa no ba no ntie. Bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔne ne kunu kɔɔ. Wɔkɔɔ so no, Python no bɔɔ nkorabata wɔ kwan no so kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu. Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na woyɛ, wei ne kwan no? " Python no kaa sɛ, "Wɔn deɛ, mo nne mu na di m'akyi. " Ɔne no tuu kwan, na wɔkɔɔ so, na awieeɛ no Python no kɔhyɛɛ abodan bi mu. Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no nso hyɛɛ mu. Mprempren ara, Python danee ne ho aboa; afei ɔkasae kyerɛɛ Ɔhemmaa ba no sɛ, “Eyi ne nea me yɛ ankasa, efisɛ mesakraa me ho sɛ mebɛware wo, efisɛ na wote wo maame ne wo papa kwan so na wokae sɛ, ‘Biribiara nni baabiara (me suro)’, me bɛkum wo seesei; Bɔ me ti na fa kyɛw no; sɛ woannya bi a, woyɛ awie. Wɔ aduan ho.” Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no tenaa ne nkyɛn a ɔrekyerɛ ne nea ɔrehwehwɛ no ntontom. Mprempren na wɔwɔ hɔ bere a Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no huu kɔkɔɔ kɔkɔɔ bi retwam. Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no buee n’ano bɛkae sɛ, "Papa kɔkɔɔ," bere a kɔkɔɔ no de ne nsa too n’ano so. Kɔkɔɔ no faa ne tuo, ɔyɛɛ ne nyinaa krado, na ɔde akyi no too ne moma so. Python no kae sɛ, "Me bɛbɔ me ti. " Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no kae sɛ, "Me brɛ sɛ me bɛbɔ me ti, sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wukum me a, kum me. " Python no sɔre ne ti de kɔpue Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no, bere a akraman no bɔɔ tuo maa ɛteɛ, pom! Akokora no kɔɔ abodan no mu sɛ ɔbɛfa Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no ne Python no de wɔn akɔ wɔn fie akɔbɔ amanneɛ sɛnea ɛbaa no, na wahu Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no ne Python no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn ɔkwan a ɔfa so kum Python no. Ɔhemmaa Maame no ba no nso maa n’asɛm no nyinaa. Ɔhemmaa no kae sɛ, "Ɔkɔkɔɔ, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ me de kɔma wo? " Ɔkɔkɔɔ no kae sɛ, "Me mpemmaa, me mpɛ biribiara, me ara me pɛ nko ara nnuadewa ngo. " Ɔhemmaa no kɔ kɔɔ nnuadewa ngo. Ɔkae sɛ, "Me mpemmaa, me de me dua rehyɛ me nnuadewa ngo yi mu, na saa kwan yi so maa no yɛɛ agyiraehyɛde (of this adventure). " Saa kwan yi so na ɔkɔkɔɔ dua no bɛyɛɛ kɔkɔɔ. Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
