Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

Na Ekuona abusuakuo no baeɛ wɔ abusuakuo no mu

Ɛbɛyɛɛ bere bi a na ɔbaa bi a wɔfrɛ no Abena wɔ hɔ; na ne maame ne ne papa de no kɔmaa awareɛ (obiara) bere a ɔkae sɛ, “Ɛnyɛ saa. ” Na ɔkyerɛɛ ne papa sɛ, “Ɛda a mehu ɔbarima a mepɛ no, saa da no, meba no, na sɛ worefa snuff a, mɛfa wo snuff-box no abɔ wo wo ti atifi. ” Ɛnneɛ, aboa no, ɔpɛn no, tee asɛm yi, ɔtwaa mu. Ɔde ne ho yɛɛ ne ho, na ɔde ne ho kɔɔ, na ɔbaa hɔ kɔɔ ɔbaa no akuraa ase. Mprempren, bere a Abena hunuu no no, ɔde ne ho tuu mmirika rekɔ baabi a ne papa regye nsukoradeɛ. Ɔde ne nsukoradeɛ no kɔgyee no, na ɔde bɔɔ no ne ti. Na saa da no yɛ anigyeɛ a ɛwɔ akuraafoɔ no mu, a wɔkae sɛ, “Yɛn wura ba no ahunu nea ɔpɛ no. ” Ɛnneɛ wɔkɔɔ de ɔbarima no bɛdii fie. Na abaayewa no kae sɛ, “Eyi ne nea mepɛ no. ” Ɔbarima no nso pɛɛ saa ara, na ɔwaree no. Ɔde nna mmiɛnsa kɔɔ hɔ, na afei ɔkae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ ne nkuraase, na ɔbaa no nso kae sɛ ɔne no bɛkɔ. Saa ara na ɔhemmaa no siesiee ne nneɛma nyinaa maa nnipa aduonu-nan sɛ wɔne wɔn nkɔ. Ɛnna wɔtuu kwan kɔduruu akuraa bi akyi, na akokora bɔɔ. Nkurɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, “Yɛadu fie anaa? ” Ɔbaa no kunu kae sɛ, “Mayɛ me yere foforɔ, na ɛsɛ sɛ me di kan de no kɔ me fie, na wanya ne nyinaa ayɛ krado ansa na ɔbɛkyinkyin akɔsrɛ nkurɔfoɔ. ” Saa ara na wɔtuu kwan kɔɔ hɔ. Wɔduruu kwaeɛ bi mu. Berɛ a wɔduruu saa kwaeɛ mu asasetifi no, ɔbarima no gyinaa nkyɛn bi; ɔkaa sɛ, “Abena, seesei bere no adu a ɛsɛ sɛ meka wo nea me yɛ ankasa. ” Abena kaa sɛ, “Wo yɛ dɛn? ” Ɔbarima no kaa sɛ, “‘I make ‘pa!’ that I may make ‘gyiren!’;’” na wadan abɛntu. Nnipa no nyinaa kaa sɛ, “E! e, dɛn na ɔka, ɔka sɛ ɔyɛ ‘pa!’ sɛ ɔbɛtumi ayɛ ‘gyiren!’ Wɔka saa nsɛm yi pɛɛ na wɔn nyinaa danee anantwi. Na ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ ɔbarima bi a ɔdwanee na ɔbaa fie. Na ɔkɔɔ so ka kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no nea ɔahu. Ɔhene Panyin ne Ɔhemmaa Panyin frɛɛ badwa bi, ɔno a ɔdwane kɔe no bɔɔ wɔn amanneɛ wɔ nea ɛkɔɔ so no ho, na bere a ɔkɔduruu baabi a ɔkae sɛ, “He made ‘pa!’ that he might make ‘gyiren!’,” ɔno nso danee adwane. Bere biara a wɔn mu biako bɛsan aka saa nsɛm yi, ɔdanee adwane. Nnipa a wɔwɔ akuraa no ase nyinaa danee nantwi gye ɔbaa panyin biako. Bere bi a nsɛm yi kɔɔ so no, aduruyɛfo bi baa akuraa no ase. Ɔteɛm sɛ, "Ah! Kuro kɛse bi, na nnipa nni mu? " Dinn! Ɔsan kaa asɛm bi. Ɛbɛdii akyiri koraa no, ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, "Me wɔ ha. " Aduruyɛfoɔ no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ nsɛm no, na adɛn nti na wote nko ara wɔ akuraa yi mu? " Ɔbaa panyin no buaa no sɛ, "Wobɛtumi aba ha, nanso me mpɛ sɛ me ne wo nni asɛm biara, kɔ nnyane na nnyane mma no ntena n'ankasa baabi. " Aduruyɛfoɔ no kae sɛ, "Wo yɛ ɔbayifoɔ, wo na woadi akuraa yi mufoɔ nyinaa, enti, gye sɛ wopɛ. Ena na me bɛkum wo ntɛm ara.” Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, “Sɛ mebɛkasa a, fa akohyɛn-kɔkɔɔ na fa kyekyere me, na fa me kyekyere me wɔ gyedua-dua no ho. ” Aduruyɛfo no bae na ɔkyekyeree no, na ɔde no kyekyeree gyedua-dua no ho. Mprempren ɔbaa panyin no ka kyerɛɛ no nea asi nyinaa, nanso bere a ɔduruu baabi a ɛsɛ sɛ ɔkae sɛ, “Ɔkae sɛ ɔbɛyɛ ‘pa!’ sɛ ebia ɔbɛyɛ gyiren,” na ɔhama-kɔmfo no bɔɔ mu; ɔbaa panyin no dan ɔsono na ɔkɔe. Ɛnna aduruyɛfoɔ no kae sɛ, “Seesei, mehu sɛ ɛnyɛ ɔbaa panyin no na ɔkunkum akuraafoɔ yi nyinaa. ” Afei ɔkɔtwaa nnuru na ɔbɔɔ no mu de guu kɔbere kɛntɛn mu, na ɔde nsuo guu mu; na ɔde n’akokorɛ kɔɔ mu; afei ɔyɛɛ ne nteɛsofoɔ no kɔɔ so. Ɔde ne ho kɔɔ kwaeɛ mu. Ɔhunuu anantwi a wɔregyina hɔ bɛyɛ aduanan, na ɔde ne dua no guu aduro no mu, na ɔde petepete wɔn so, na wɔn ho san bɛyɛɛ nnipa. Na ɛhɔ nko ara na ɛtraa a wɔyɛ anantwi ankasa. Na nnipa no san baa home. Saa na Ekuona abusuakuo no baa abusuakuo no mu, ɛfiri saa awareɛ a Abena hyɛɛ aseɛ no.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
