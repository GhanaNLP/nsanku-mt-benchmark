Na ɔsɔre a wɔfrɛ no Kwatima bae sɛ ɔbɛnya Odwanini din.

Na ɔkraman bi, Kwatima, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Me rekɔpɛ asase bi a ɔbɛsi fie. " Na ɔgya no nso, wɔ saa bere koro no ara mu, na ɔwɔ adwene koro no ara. Ɛnna Kwatima, ɔkraman no, de n’ani kɔɔ hɔ sɛ ɔbɛkɔhwɛ asase a ɔpɛ no, na ɔtew mu sɛ ɔbɛsi fie. Na Osebo, Leopard no nso kɔɔ so, na bere a ɔduruu nea Ram no apam no no, ɔkae sɛ, "Gracious me! Me nkurase a na mebɛsi, nanso mennyaa ho adwuma biara nni so, me honhom agyanom ahyɛ aseɛ wɔ so. " Na ɔno nso yɛɛ akwan nketenkete na ɔkɔɔ so. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma hyɛɛ aseɛ bɛdaa adi wɔ anɔpa no, Ram no san kɔɔ so. Bere a ɔduruu beaeɛ no, ɔkae sɛ, "Gracious me! Ɛwɔ mu sɛ me honhom nananom rebɛboa me, (hwɛ) asase a wɔaprapra no. Na ɔno nso bɔɔ nkurase na ɔkɔɔ. Na Leopard no nso bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh! wei yɛ asɛm bi; me bɛyɛ hu wɔ ho. " Na ɔno nso kɔɔ kwae no mu; ɔtwaa nnua na ɔde too hɔ. Na Ram no nso bae, na ɔno nso hwehwɛɛ mu yɛɛ nwanwa. Ɔsan nso kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu kɔtwaa nhama a ɛkɔkɔɔ, na ɔtoo no hɔ kɔeɛ. Na bio Ɔsebɔ baeɛ. Ɔno nso de n’ani kɔɔ akyiri wɔ nkɔsoɔ a ɛwɔ hɔ no, na ɔbɔɔ no na ɔkɔeɛ. Bio Kwatima baeɛ, na ɔtutu ntokuro de nnua no sii mu, na ɔkɔeɛ. Na bio Ɔsebɔ baeɛ, na ɔde mmeamudua nnua no bɔɔ mu kɔeɛ. Na bio Kwatima baeɛ, na ɔkɔgyee sare no de sii hɔ kɔɔ hɔ. Na ɔsebɔ no nso san baeɛ, na ɔde atɛkyɛ no bɔɔ plastering, na ɔkɔeɛ. Na bio Kwatima baeɛ na ɔyɛɛ roofing no kɔɔ hɔ. Na bio ɔsebɔ no baeɛ, na ɔbɔɔ fam no kɔɔ hɔ. Ɛnna Kwatima nso baeɛ, na ɔbɛyii dɔteɛ fitaa bɔɔ plastering wɔ fie. Bere a ɔkɔe no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ba sɛ, "Ɛnnɛ meawie fie no, na bere a Sunday Adae bɛtɔ no, wɔ Dwoada a ɛdi hɔ no, yɛbɛyɛ fie foforo no ho adagyaw, nti hwɛ sɛ nnipa a wɔwɔ atuo bɛkɔ akɔto ɔdan no mu. " Mprempren Leopard nso bae (na ɔhunuu fie a wɔawie no). Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛ! " Manka hwee nanso mekae sɛ mebɛkɔ akɔfa me nneɛma asan aba." Ɛnna ɔkɔɔ n'anim, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ ne ba sɛ, "Tu mmirika ntɛm ara sɛnea wotumi na ka kyerɛ ahonyadeɛ hwehwɛfoɔ no sɛ, bere a Adae tɔ wɔ Kwasida no, Monday a ɛdi hɔ no, mebɛdi fie foforo no ho afahyɛ, enti ɔmmra mma no nto tuo mma me. " Na ne ba no kɔɔ n'anim na ɔkasa kyerɛɛ no. Ghost-hunter no kae sɛ, "Meateɛ. " Afei da a ɛdi Adae no anim no, Kwatima boaboaa ne nneɛma ano, na ɔde kɔtoo hɔ wɔ fie no mu, na ɔsan bae. Na ɔsebɔ no nso boaboaa ne ano, na ɔde kɔtoo hɔ. Ɛduruu da bi a ɛda adi sɛ yɛbɛtumi ahu nneɛma no, wɔ Adae Kwasida no, Kwatima kɔɔ nkyinkyin de n’akyi kyerɛɛ nnipa a ɔne wɔn teɛ no; na Leopard no nso yɛɛ n’akyi. Ɛwɔ Dwoada, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi, Kwatima ne ne ba no kɔɔ fie foforo no mu; afei wɔkɔɔ so too tuo guu dan no mu. Na Leopard no nso bae, ɔtee atuo, ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnnɛ nnyɛ da pa, ɛnnɛ ahonhom no retoto atuo. " Na ɔno nso bae, na Ghost-hunter no nso kɔɔ fie hɔ, na ɔmaa atuo no bɔɔ so, pomm! Leopard no bae na ɔhunuu Kwatima ne ne ba te hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo dwadie bɛn? " Kwatima nso kaa sɛ, "Wo nso, wo ho yɛ dɛn? " Leopard no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wo ho nni nti a, me nso me ho nni nti. " Na Kwatima kaa sɛ, "Sɛ saa a, na yɛn baanu nyinaa bɛtena ha. " Enti wɔtenaa hɔ. Da biara, bere a Leopard no sɔre no, ɔkɔɔ abɔmmɔ, na ɔde nam bae. Bere nyinaa na ɛte saa ara. Nanso Kwatima deɛ, ɔmfa hwee mmra. Ɛnna da bi a hufoɔ no kɔɔkɔɔ kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu, ɔbɔɔ mmɔden paa sɛ ɔbɛkyere adeɛ bi nanso ɛnyɛeɛ. Ɔsan baa fie na ɔkaa sɛ, "Kwatima, sɛ wɔda yi wunni kwaeɛ no mu na woanhyɛ nam bi a, nea mebɛyɛ wo, worennye ntom. " Kwatima kɔɔ kwan, na ɔkɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu, na ɔde akantuo bi baeɛ, na ɔtow guu hɔ. Bere a Leopard no huu aboa no, ɛmaa ne ho dwiriw no sɛ nea ɛso biara. Bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi anɔpa no, Kwatima kɔɔ so, na ɔsan de foforo bae. Ɔbɔmmɔ kae sɛ, "E! " Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ba sɛ, "Hwɛ ha, me ne kwae rekɔ, na me de Kwatima nso kɔ, na me ne ne duker baa a me de wo bae wɔ da a atwam no, bere a yɛakɔ no, nfrɛ Kwatima ba no na nmmisa no sɛ, 'Sɛ w'agya kɔ kwae kɔfa nam a, kyerɛ yɛn wɔ saa duker yi so sɛnea ɔyɛ no. '" Na nkurɔfoɔ no (Osebo ne Kwatima) tuu kwan. Na Leopard ba no kae sɛ, "Kwatima ba, ba ha na yɛnni agorɔ. " Ɔbaa no, ɔkae sɛ, "Agya kɔ kwae a, saa na ɔkyere aboa. " Na ɔtuu mmirika, na ɔkyeree duker ba no na ɔbɔɔ n'akono. Kwatima ba no kae sɛ, "Wose fa fa kɔfa ɔbotan kɛseɛ. " Leopard ba no kɔɔ hɔ kɔgyee baako. Ɔde sii fam hɔ. Kwatima ba no danee n'akyi fii ho bae na ɔde ne ti so bɔɔ abo no, pim! na ɛbubuu mu. Leopard ba no kae sɛ, "Ah. " Na wɔn agyanom fii kwae mu bae, na Leopard ba no kae kyerɛɛ ne papa nea asi no, na Kwatima ba no nso kae kyerɛɛ ne papa nea asi no. Ɛnneɛ na wɔwɔ hɔ no, da bi a adeɛ bi bɛbɔɔ Kwatima nan ho, na ɔrebɛdwiri no, nanso Leopard no dwene sɛ ɔregyina akyi sɛ ɔbɛku no. Ntɛm ara na Leopard no tuu mmirika bɛtoo Kwatima so sɛ ɔbɛkyere no. Na Kwatima dwanee kiri, kiri, kiri. Ɔkɔɔ asuo bi a ɛda hɔ a Python bi wɔ mu. Kwatima kae sɛ, "Father Python, medwane kɔ wo nkyɛn banbɔ sɛ Leopard no ankɔkyere me. " Ɛhɔ na Python no kae sɛ, "Wo, wo yɔnko, woayɛ nea ɛfata, enti wofa me nsa. " Kwatima bɔɔ mmɔden na wo ne no nsa kaa. Saa kwan yi so na Kwatima baa bɛgyee Odwa-nini din a ɛkyerɛ sɛ wo ne Python no nsa ka. Ɛfii ne nsa a ɔne Python no bɔe no mu.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebia wobɛtumi afa no sɛ nokware, na nea aka no wobɛtumi akamfo me sɛ meka kyerɛɛ no.
