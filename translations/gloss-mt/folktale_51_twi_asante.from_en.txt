Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBƐYƐ NA ATURUKUKU, AKOKORA-AKOKORA, KƆƆ KWAEƐ NO MU NA AKOKO, AKOKORA, BƐBAA FIE

Akoko, the Hen, ne Aturukuku, Turtle-dove, firi agya baako ne ɛna baako. Mprempren Hen no ka kyerɛɛ Turtle-dove no sɛ, "Kɔ nkɔ Tall-grass-country no, na me nso me bɛkɔ kwaeɛ a ɛyɛ den mu, na sɛ wunya aduane a, na wo mmɛka me asɛm. " Ɛnna wɔn tetew wɔn ho. Mfeɛ mmiɛnsa ni na Aturukuku anhu ne nuabea. Ɔkae kyerɛ ne kunu sɛ, “Mmoa me, me rebɛhwehwɛ me nuabea.” Ɛnna ne kunu kae sɛ, “Waka a, m’ate, fi wo nkyɛn, na ma meka wo ho. ” Na ɔne no tuu kwan kɔduruu kuro bi akyi. Wɔhunuu conical mound bi wɔ hɔ, na ɛhɔ na ɔne ne nuabea no hyiaa. Na Hen no bisaa akokora-kɔkɔɔ no sɛ, “Bere a wokɔɔ sare-tenten-man no, wunyaa aduan bi a wubedi? ” Ɔbuae sɛ, “Baabi a me kɔe no, na guinea-grain nko ara wɔ hɔ; na bere a me nkwadaa bɛyɛ nsia na wɔde wɔn ho bɛbɔ me ho no, na bere a wɔayɛ kɛseɛ saa no, me kuraa wɔn ho bɛn me ho, na ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ bi too ne tuo, pom! ” Na ɔkunkum me mma, na sɛ anka mennim nteaseɛ a, anka wɔkunkum me nso.” Bere a wakasa wiee no, Hen no kae sɛ, “Baabi a me kɔe no, mebɛyɛɛ obi a ɔho hia ankasa. ” Aturukuku bisaa no sɛ, “Ka kyerɛ me nea wose ‘person of real importance’ ho. ” Afei Hen no kae sɛ, “nea ɛkyerɛ wɔ ‘person of real importance’ ho ne sɛ, sɛ nhwɛso no, sɛ me wura trɛw ntama pa mu a, t Sɛ me kɔda so a, na me nsuo bɛtɔ so, na bere a me wura de nam kɔhyɛ soup-stew no mu no, ɛnna me yi nea me pɛ; aduane biara a me wura bɛdi no, me kɔfa bi, na sɛ me di saa a, me wura ntumi mfa ne tuo anaa ɔpɛn bɔ me. Ɛno na ɛyɛ me nea enti meka sɛ me yɛ obi a m’ho hia paa.” Hen no kaa sɛ, “Baabi a wote hɔ no, ɛnyɛ baabi pa nti, wɔ Memeneda bi a ɛdi Kwasida Adae anim no, fa wo mma ba, ne wo kunu, na wɔn nyinaa ntra yɛn fie. ” Aturukuku kaa sɛ, “Me ate, me bɛkɔ, na me bɛba Memeneda a ɛdi Kwasida Adae anim no. ” Mprempren ɔsɔfoɔ bi baa Hen fie hɔ, shak bɔ ne sɔn nketenkete. Wɔbisaa ne no sɛ, “Papa, dɛn na ɛyɛ? ” Ɔkae sɛ, “Dente ka sɛ woama no kwan ma ɔkɔm mfe abien ni, enti ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ ɔhene no nkyɛn na woka kyerɛ no sɛ ɔmfa akokora ne nkesua dumien mmra. ” Afei ɔhene no kɔgyee akokora baanan, de nkesua dumien bae, de bɔɔ eto kɔmaa nyame Dente wɔ ne dɔte afɔrempe a ɛyɛ cone no so, na ɔtwaa akokora baanan no ti. Wɔde wɔn kɔtoo hɔ, na wɔde wɔn ntakra ne wɔn ti, ne wɔn nan, na wɔde plaster bɔɔ atɛkyɛ no ho; na ɔno nso de mashed eto no bɔɔ so. Mprempren, da a edi Adae anim no, na Aturukuku rehyia ne nuabea. Bere a ɔkɔɔ so no, ɔhunuu asɔree kronkron a ɛwɔ Dente, ne nuabea agyanom, ne ti, ne nan, ne ntaban, ne mmerɛbo, ne ne dwensɔtwaa. Aturukuku kae sɛ, “Eyi nyinaa akyi no, mentumi nkɔ hɔ bio. ” Na akokora-kɔkɔɔ tu kɔɔ silk-cotton dua bi so, na ɔkae sɛ: "I weep for my sister o, Sorrow o.

Woyɛ Hen a na woyɛ obi a ɔho hia no, na ɛyɛ wo ti yi?

Wo, Hen, a na woyɛ obi a ɔho hia, ne wo ntaban yi?

Woyɛ Hen a na woyɛ obi a ɔho hia na ɛyɛ wo nan yi?

Wo, Hen, a na woyɛ obi a ɔho hia, na ɛyɛ wo mmerɛbo anaa?

Wo, Hen, a na woyɛ obi a ɔho hia, ne wo dwensɔtwaa yi? "

Na Aturukuku kaa sɛ, "Me renkɔ kwaeɛ man mu bio, ɛfiri sɛ kwaeɛ no mufoɔ nnyɛ papa. " Saa ara na Aturukuku baa bɛtenaa asase a sare tenten wɔ mu, bere a Hen no tenaa fie.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebia wobɛtumi afa no sɛ nokware, na nea aka no wobɛtumi akamfo me sɛ meka kyerɛɛ no.
