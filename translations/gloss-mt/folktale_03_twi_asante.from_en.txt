SƐNEA ɛBAA NO NYANSA BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU

WƆKA SƐ NA KWAKU, ƆPRAPRA NO, WƆ HƆ, NA ƆBƐBƆƆ NIMDEƐ NYINAA BOABOAA ANO WƆ BAABIAKO NA ƆDE GUU AKUTU KƐNTƐN MU. Afei ɔde ne nne kyerɛɛ sɛ ɔbɛforo dua bi so akɔsɛn no sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a nyansa a ɛwɔ asase so nyinaa bɛsa.

Ɛnna ɔde kɔɔ so kɔne no, na bere a ɔduruu dua no ase baabi a ɔde bɛsɛn no, ɔde hama bi kyekyeree no kɔɔkɔɔ no ho, na ɔde too n’anim, na ɔde ne ho sii hɔ sɛ ɔbɛforo dua no. Ɔforo, na ɔforo, na ɔforo kɛkɛ. Ɔbɔɔ mmɔden bio, na ɔsan yɛɛ sɛ ɔbɛforo, na ɔforo kɛkɛ.

Afei, ne ba Ntikuma a na ɔregyina hɔ no kae sɛ, "Owu, w’ani abɔ so, anka ɛrenyɛ yiye sɛ wobɛdane akutu no akɔto w’akyi, akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ wubetumi afa so? " Ɔno kae sɛ, "Yi wo ho, wo ne wo kasa dedaw no. " Afei ɔdane ne ho sɛ ɔbɛforo bio sɛnea na ɔyɛ da no, nanso ɔsan yɛɛ saa bio kwa. Ɛnna ɔdwen ho tenten, na awieeɛ no ɔde akutu no kɔtoo n’akyi. Ɛnna ɔde ne ho sii hɔ sɛ ɔbɛforo, na ɔforoe ntɛm ara, Kra! kra! kra! (na ɛyɛ ne foro nnyigyei) ɛhɔ na ɔkɔ. Ɔduruu baabi a nkorabata no fii aseɛ trɛw firi dua no mu, na ɔkae sɛ, "Me, Kwaku Ananse, abosom ketewa no, Afio! Ɛbɛyɛ sɛ me nso me wu, me ba a ɔyɛ ketewa paa, ketewa paa, ketewa paa—na me wɔ hɔ, na meaboaboa nyansa nyinaa wɔ baabiako, nanso ebi kaa a mpo menhunuu no, na ɛha! me ba, abofra a ɔda so nsuo no, akyerɛ me no." Ɛnna ɔkyeree saa borɔdeɛ no, na ɛyɛ nteɛteɛm, tintini! na ɔtow guu no, na ɛyɛ nteɛteɛm, tesee! Saa ara na obiara nyaa nyansa; na obiara a ɔnkɔɔ hɔ wɔ bere ano (sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔfa bi) mfa kwan mma me, ɔyɛ ɔkwasea.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
