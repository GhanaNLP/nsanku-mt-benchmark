Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ASONO NO BAE BƐKƆƆ SƐ WƆBƐKƆ SƐ SARE TENTEN MAN NO MU

Ɛbɛyɛɛ bere bi a na asono bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔkaa sɛ ɔpɛ obi a ɔne no di agodie a wɔfrɛ no "butting heads". Mmoa no nyinaa kae sɛ wɔantumi anni saa agodie yi ne asono no. Mprempren na Kwaku Ananse akɔre no wɔ hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ, "Me deɛ, mɛtumi ayɛ, nanso tebea a me ne mo de bɛhyɛ aseɛ ne sɛ, sɛ wotumi kum me a, ɛsɛ sɛ wosee me wɔ baabi foforo a ɛnyɛ abotan mu. " Ɔsono no kae sɛ, "Mamee, na mebɛba abɛbɔ wo nna nson, na wodeɛ bɛba abɛbɔ me nna nson. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ tuu borɔdɔma, na ɔ- Ɔde wɔn too ogya no mu, sɛ wɔnkyekye wɔn, na ɔde kwan bi ho akɔyɛ latrine. Mprempren, bere biara a Ananse sɔree no, na ɔde yam bi kura ne nsa mu, na ɔde ne ho akɔtenatena latrine no so. Ɔhuu Adowa antelope ketewa no a ɔretwam. Mprempren nsɛm a wɔn nsa aka akyi (i. e. me werɛ fi sɛ meka biribi) na ɔkɔm kɛse bi aba (saa bere no). Bere a ɔpanyin no twam no, Spider no tow borɔdeɛ no guu kwan no so. Ntɛm ara Adowa no faa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Father Spider, amanehunu kɛseɛ a ɛte sɛɛ na aba, na ɛno na wode di dwuma de wo ho to wo ho? " Ananse nso buaeɛ sɛ, "Ɔhaw biara nni hɔ wɔ me fie wɔ aduane ho. " Adowa no kaa sɛ, "Me ne wo bɛkɔ na yɛanya bi. " Ananse ne no bɛduruu fie hɔ, ɔka kyerɛɛ Adowa no sɛ, "Ɔbarima bi wɔ hɔ, na ɛyɛ ɔno na ɔde aduane ba me anadwo; enti kɔda hɔ wɔ dɔrwa no. na bere a ɔba no ɔbɛka sɛ, ‘Asikoto!’ (na wobɛkye wo ti) na woabua sɛ, ‘Me de me ti bɛgye,’ na waka saa no ara pɛ, ɔbɛfa aduane no bi akɔto hɔ ama wo. Adowa no kaa sɛ, "Me da wo ase, Aku; wo deɛ, Papa Ananse, me nnya nhunu ɔbarima bi a ɔte sɛ wo. " Adowa no kɔtoo fam wɔ abura no ano. Ɛnkyɛreeɛ no, ɔsono no baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Asikoto. " Adowa antelope no buaeɛ sɛ, "I take my head to receive it," na ɔsono no bɔɔ no pan! na Adowa antelope no awu. Ɔsono no tuu kwan. Anɔpahema bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, akɔre no de Adowa kɔmaa Aso (ne yere) sɛ ɔnyɛ no soup-stew. Ananse seesei kɔɔ ɔsono no nkyɛn sɛ ɔbɛma no anɔpa nkra. Ɔsono no kae sɛ, "Wo ho teɛ yiye anaa? " Ananse nso buae sɛ, "Me ho teɛ yiye. " Ananse tuu kwan na ɔbaa ne fie. Ɔfaa yam foforo bi, na ɔsan kɔɔ nsuo mu. Ɔhunuu Otwe, Duyker a ɔretwam no, na ɔtow yam no gui. Duyker kae sɛ, "O, Papa Ananse, ɔkɔm a ɛte sɛɛ aba, na eyi ne nea wode kɔ bere a wode kɔ nsuo mu? " Ananse buaa no sɛ, "Sɛ wopɛ bi a, ba, yɛnkɔ me fie sɛ mɛkɔ ama wo bi. " Duyker ne Ananse kɔɔ kwan. Ananse kae sɛ, "Woda hɔ wɔ agoprama no ano, na anwummerɛ yi me nkoa bɛba de aduane, na bere a wadu hɔ no, ɔka sɛ, 'Asikoto'; wo nso ka sɛ, 'Megyina m'anim gye'. " Duker no kae sɛ, "Meda wo ase, Aku, wo nko ara na wotumi siesie nneɛma a ɛte sɛɛ. " Duker no kɔɔ hɔ sɛ ɔbɛda. Wɔ anwummere no, ɔsono no bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Asikoto. " Duyker no kaa sɛ, "I take my head to receive it. " Na ɔbɔɔ Duyker no, na ɔhwee ase, na ne hwee ase no nnyigyei yɛ kum! Ɛnna ɔsono no kɔɔ hɔ. Anɔpahema bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɛnna Ananse de Duyker no kɔmaa Aso, na ɔde kwan kɔɔ ɔsono fie sɛ ɔbɛbɔ no anɔpa. Ɔsono no kae sɛ, "Okuamoa na ɔwɔ hɔ, wo ho yɛ wo yiye? " Okuamoa buae sɛ, "Perfectly yɛ. " Afei wɔdii nkɔmmɔ kɔɔ so, na Ananse kɔhyɛɛ da sɛ ɔbɛkɔ, na ɔsan kɔɔ ne fie. Na afei Ananse yɛɛ ade koro no ara ne Oyuo, duker tuntum no; Wansane, bush-buck; Kokote-Asamoa, bush-pig; Otromo, bɛnkyi; ne Oko, roan no. Afei Ananse nso, ɔbɛkɔ akɔbɔ ɔsono no. Afei ɔkɔɔ adwumfo bi fie kɔyɛɛ dade a wɔde yɛ wedge, na ɔyɛɛ dade hama a ɛne no bɛka ho. Anwummere no, ɔkɔɔ Asono fie. Ananse kae sɛ, "Asikoto. " Asono no kae sɛ, "I take my head to receive it. " Ananse de dade wedge no kɔtoo asono no ti atifi, na ɔde dade hama no bɔɔ no pan! pan! pan! Na ɛno (the wedge) yerae kɔɔ ɔsono no ti mu. Afei Anansi baa fie. Anɔpa bi a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔsono no kae kyerɛɛ ne yere sɛ, "Me ti yɛ me yaw," na ne yere kae sɛ, "Nka saa, ɛsɛ sɛ woyi wo ho fi wo ho; Ananse mpo a wopiraa no nna nson no nnyaa tiyɛ na onwu, na woka saa; mprempren kɔ nkɔma Ananse anɔpa pa sɛnea na ɔyɛ daa no." Ɔsono no bɔɔ n’ani mu (kyerɛ sɛ. bɔɔ mmɔden kɛse, na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔduu baabi a Ananse teɛ no. Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "Ɔmanpanin, woayɛ yiye paa? " Ɔsono no buaa no sɛ, "Oh, me yɛ yiye. " Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "ankasa. " Ananse kɔyɛɛ wedge foforɔ. Anadwo no tɔɔ no dwo a, ɔde ne ho kɔ Ɔsono hɔ. "Asikoto," ɔkaa no. Ɔsono no buae sɛ, "Mefa me ti kɔgye no. " Ananse faa wedge no de sii hɔ, na ɔde dade bɔɔ no, pan! pan! pan! Ananse san baa ne fie. Ɛnneɛ na ɔwɔ hɔ, bere a ɔmanpanyin bi baa hɔ bɛkaa sɛ, "Ananse, Asono nyinaa nana ka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meba bɛbɔ wo amanneɛ sɛ wo adamfo, Asono no, bere a ɔdaeɛ no, wannya so bio; enti ba bue abotan bi mu fa no kɔto no mu. " Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ koraa, ɛno na ɛnyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ a yɛyɛeɛ no. Yɛkaa sɛ sɛ obi wu a, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔfoforo no bɔ no ma ɔwe. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔɔ bɔɔ dawuro maa ɔsono no mogya abusuafoɔ. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Wofa no na fa no ba no prɛko. " Ɔbɔfoɔ no baa hɔ bɛn Spider no hɔ na ɔkaa sɛ, "Wɔse wo bɛba prɛko. " Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ wotwɛn me. " Spider no kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu na ɔtwaa dua bi, na ɔyɛɛ no nnua-barima, na ɔtwaa atuo bi, na ɔde hyɛɛ ne nsa, na ɔde maa Aso sɛ, "Fa no, kɔ anim, fa no to hɔ wɔ baabi a wɔse. Ɛsɛ sɛ mekyekyere bɔntɔn no, na w’nso bɛgyina hɔ, na meba no, wobɛfrɛ me." Aso kuraa dua-mfonini no na ɔkɔe. Spider no nso ne amanfoɔ no tuu kwan. Ananse bɛduruu akokosika no anim, na wɔde dɔm, ne akɔre-pɛn, ne akono ne sika, na wɔde hyɛɛ ne nsam. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Monyɛ so. " Afei wɔde Spider no kɔɔ baabi a na ɛwɔ hɔ abotan kɛseɛ bi. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Paapae mu, to amena, na wode Asono no hyɛ mu. " Spider kɔɔ ne ho, pe! pe! pe! (ɔbɔɔ ne nnwinnadeɛ nnyigyei). Ɛhɔ na na Ananse wɔ, bere a ɔde ntɛm frɛɛ no sɛ, "Aane. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Asononono, mo teɛ, (ɛte sɛ nea) obi frɛ me? " Nantwinini no kae sɛ, " Mfi, wopɛ kwan sɛ wubeguan. " Ananse kae sɛ, " Metee. " Ɔkɔɔ so yɛɛ n’adwuma, pɛ! pɛ! pɛ! Afei nso ɔteɛm sɛ, " Aane. " Ɔkae sɛ, " Obi frɛ me. " Nkurɔfo no kae sɛ, " Yɛremma wo kwan. " Saa ara, saa ara, mpɛn nson. Akyiri yi wɔkae sɛ, "Monkɔtie (ɔno ne hwan) na monnsan nsan mmra ntɛm ara, na sɛ wuguan a, yɛbɛnya wo. " Ananse kɔɔ baabi a na Akua ba wɔ. Mprempren Ananse tuu mmirika san, kiri! kiri! kiri! na ɛyɛ ne nan nnyigyei. Elephants no kae sɛ, "Ananse, dɛn na ɛyɛ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, ɛsɛ sɛ wofiri hɔ, efisɛ ɔbɔfoɔ bi gyina hɔ, na ɔse ɛsɛ sɛ me twe me ho kakra kɔ nkyɛn ma ɔbɔ ne tuo bɛto wo. Ɛno nti na ɔafrɛ me, na ɔafrɛ me sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ me gyina nkyɛn. " Bere a Elephants no rebɛyɛ nhwehwɛmu wɔ hɔ wɔ kwaeɛ mu no, nokwarem no wɔhunuu sɛ obi bi gyina hɔ, na wɔdwanee. Ananse kae sɛ, "Papa abɔmmɔfoɔ, kum wɔn mma me. " Elephants no dwanee kɔɔ sare-tenten-man no mu, na wogyaw asono a wɔawuwu no wɔ hɔ, na Ananse faa no kɔdii no. Saa na asono no baa sɛ wɔrekɔ sare-tenten-man no mu, efisɛ bere bi na asono (nko) na wɔte kwae no mu.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebia wobɛtumi afa no sɛ nokware, na nea aka no wobɛtumi akamfo me sɛ meka kyerɛɛ no.
