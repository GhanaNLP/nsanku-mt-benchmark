Wɔka sɛ tɔnnua bi na ɔwɔ n’akwantu mu no, ɔkɔhyiaa ɔbaa panyin bi a ɔwɔ akisikuru wɔ ne nan so. Na tɔnnua no kae sɛ, "Gracious me! Ɛno ne akuru a ɛwɔ adesuakuw bi mu nko ara; nanso sɛ wopɛ a, wobɛtumi atutuw kwan? Ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, "O, kakraa bi pɛ. " Ɔkɔmhyɛn no kaa sɛ, "Mo nkurɔfoɔ, ɛnnɛ yi, sɛ mebɛyɛ mo biribi pa ɔkyena, ɔkyena no mobɛfa biribi bɔ me ase. " Ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, "Onyɛe. " Ɔkɔmhyɛn no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wuntumi nni nneyɛɛ saa a, metee. Ɔkaa sɛ, "To n’ani na bue. " Na ɔbaa panyin no too n’ani mu na ɔbuee no. Ɔkɔmden no kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ wo akuru no. " Na bere a ɔbaa panyin no bɛhwɛ no, ɛnyɛ nkae biara. Ɛnna ɔmaa ɔtoo n’ani mu bio, ɔbuee no, na ɔhunuu sɛ asase no nyinaa ayɛ kusuu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "To n’ani mu bio. " Ɔtoo mu, na ɔbuee no, na afie no gyina hɔ den. Na ɔsan bɛtoo mu ara. Ɔbuee mu maa ɔhunuu kuro a ɛte sɛɛ, ɛhɔ na ɛwɔ, ɛyɛ kɛse. Eagle no kaa sɛ, "Aberewa, ɛyɛ wo de. Wɔ wo nsam). Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, "Asemeda mede ma wo. " Afei ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meya me ho ma wo? " Ɔkɔre no kae sɛ, "Mempɛ nketenkete mpo, me deɛ, me pɛ ne saa silk-cotton-tree a ɛgyina hɔ no. " Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, "ade yi (wose) fa no. " Afei ɔkɔre no tuu kwan, na ɔtoo so, na ɔbɔɔ abɔnten, na ɔtoo nkesua mmienu, nneɛma a wɔde sie. wɔde no hyɛɛ mu no. Na ɔbɔɔ nkesua mmienu no, na ɔkɔɔ hwehwɛɛ biribi ama ne mma adidi. Ɔbaa panyin no nana ba a ɔne no teɛ no hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔrewe, "Ehe! Ehe! " Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ asɛm? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Momma me nwe ɔkɔre ba. " Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, "Ɛhe na metumi nya ɔkɔre ba? " Nana ba ketewa no san hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, whimper! whimper! Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ asɛm no? " Abofra no kae sɛ, "Nwe ɔkɔkɔɔ ba, sɛ me nnya bi nwe a, mɛwu. " Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, "Ah! ɛsɛ sɛ me mmanana no wuwu esiane ɔkɔkɔɔ mma a wɔn nwe nti? Wɔn so na wunya, fa nnwene kɔbɔ akokora-kɔkɔɔ-dua no na fa ɔkɔkɔɔ mma no ba me. " Akuraa asefo no kɔɔ hɔ, (nnwene no bɔɔ), Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! Ɛyɛɛ te sɛ nea dua no reba fam, bere a akokora no mma panyin no tuu soro bɛbae bɛgyinaa akokora no ano, na ɔbɔɔ dawuru; ɔfrɛɛ maame no sɛ:

Sango, anomaa no ɛ!

Sango anomaa no, ɔkɔmhyɛn ba!

Sango, anomaa no ɛ!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔdidi a, san ba!

Sango, anomaa no ɛ!

Ɛna maame no tee sɛ ne ba no rehu nyan; ɔsɔre, (ne ntaban nnyigyei no) baa no, ɔkae sɛ, "Sanguri," na saa dua no (a na ɛbɛtwa no) bɛbɔɔ mu bio, na nnipa a na wɔrebɔ no nyinaa, wɔdɔm. Ɛnna ɔde aduane a ɔde bae no maa ne mma no. Ɛnna ɔde n’ano bɔɔ wɔn kwan sɛ, "Me rekɔ, sɛ ɔpanyin no bɛba abɛfa wo a, momma no fa wo. " Na ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, "Kɔto dua no so na fa mmoa no ba ma me nana no ka. " Na wɔkɔɔ hɔ ne mprenu so, Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! Ɛkame ayɛ sɛ dua no bɛkɔ fam, bere a (Eagle) no ba bae bɛgyinaa akokora no ano, na ɔfrɛɛ ne maame sɛ:

Sango, anomaa no ɛ!

Sango anomaa no, ɔkɔmhyɛn ba!

Sango, anomaa no ɛ!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔdidi a, san ba!

Sango, anomaa no ɛ!

Ɔfrɛɛ ne maame, na ɔsan frɛɛ, na ɔsan frɛɛ, na ɔsan frɛɛ—mmuae biara—na afei dua no kasae bere a ɛbɔɔ fam, "Brim! " (ɔkae). Wɔde akokora no mma kɔe; wɔde baako maa ɔbaa panyin no, nanso baako a aka no tu kɔe na ɛbɛtoo wawa dua bi so. Baako a wɔayam no, na wɔde maa ɔbaa panyin no mmanana a ɔde kaa plantain a wɔayam no ho. Ɛnkyɛreeɛ bi, ɔkɔmhyɛn no baeɛ. Bere a ɔduruu dua a wɔatwa no, ɔhunuu ne ba no mu baako te hɔ; ɔbisaa no nea ɛkɔɔ so, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no nsɛm no nyinaa. Ɔkɔmhyɛn no tuu kwan kɔɔ aberewa no akuraa ase. Bere a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, na aberewa no nana ne ba no mu baako redidi. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Aberewa, mma wo nsa nka wo. " Ɔfiri aberewa no fie hɔ na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ yɛɛ nkonyaayi wɔ kuro no akyi. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sanguri," na nipa biara yeraeɛ, na bio ɔkaa sɛ, "Sanguri," na ofie biara bɔɔ mu prɛko pɛ na ɛnkaeɛ biara nni hɔ. "Sanguri," akuraa no san bɛyɛɛ kwaeɛ. "Sanguri," na aberewa no akuru san baeɛ. Na ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔbaa, woahu, ɛno nti na mpanimfoɔ no ka sɛ, sɛ obi yɛ wo adepa a, mma no na woanhyɛ no amane.
