Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ANANSE NTAMFO KAE SƐ ƆREKƆPƐ ƆKWASEA BERE TENTEN A NA ƆNO ANKASA YƐ ƆKWASEA

Na Ananse akɔre no abɛtena hɔ da bi, na ɔkae sɛ na ɔpɛ ɔkwasea bɛka no akɔto mpata adaka. Ɔkyinii kuro no nyinaa, nanso wannya baako. Ɛnna ɔhyiaa Osansa, akɔre no. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Yɛnkɔ nkɔto mpata adaka. ” Hawk no buae sɛ, “Me wɔ nam pii. ” Akyire yi ɔne Anene, Crow no, kɔto mpata adaka. Crow no kaa sɛ, “Me ate. ” Mprempren Hawk no ka kyerɛɛ Crow no sɛ, “Saa mpata adaka a wone Ananse rekɔ yi, nkɔ, efisɛ Ananse ka sɛ ɔrekyerɛ ɔkwasea, bere a ɔne ɔkwasea no bɛto mpata adaka no. na ɔbɛnya sika no nyinaa.” Crow no buae sɛ, “Ɛnsɛ sɛ wose pii, efisɛ mebɛhwɛ sɛ mɛnya sika no nyinaa. ” Crow ne Spider no tu kwan kɔ kwae mu kɔtwa mmɛdua nkorabata. Abɛn kae sɛ, "Ma me sekan na mebɛakɔtwa, na wo adwuma bɛyɛ sɛ wubɛfa ɔbrɛ a ɛfiri me mmɔdenbɔ mu. " Akɔre no buae sɛ, "Wobu me sɛ nkwasea; ma menya twa no bere a wobɛfa brɛ no. " Na Ananse retwa (mmɛn) bere a Anene, abɛn no, tenaa hɔ nwiinwii. Bere a wawie atwa no no, wɔkyekyeree wɔn. Krak no kaa sɛ, “Spider, mefa mfa wɔn, bere a wode ne brɛ no. ” Spider no buaa no sɛ, “firi hɔ, wofa me yɛ me nkwasea. ” Na ɔfa wɔn de too ne ti so. Krak no dii n’akyi na na ɔregrue. Berɛ a wɔduruu fie no, Crow no kae sɛ, "Menyɛ mpataa no na wofa me mmɔdenbɔ no ho ɔbrɛ. " Ananse buaa no sɛ, "Da biara, menya wɔn na wofa ɔbrɛ no. " Ɛnna Crow no kɔɔ so kɔtoo n'akyi na ɔrenwiinwii. Ananse kae sɛ, "ɔkwasea, wose wunya adwene nanso wuwu. " Akɔre no wiee mpataa no. Abɛn kae sɛ, "Mfa mfa mpata adaka no nkɔ nsuo no mu, na wofa ne brɛ. " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Dabi, me ne wɔn bɛkɔ, na wofa ne brɛ. " Wɔduruu nsuo no ho, Abɛn no kae sɛ, "Papa Ananse, aboa bi te nsuo no mu, enti momma me nnyina nsuo no mu mfa mpata adaka no nsi hɔ, sɛ aboa no ka me a, wobɛfa ne wu. " Ananse kae sɛ, "Ah, wode me di ɔkwasea ankasa; me ara me bɛhyehyɛ akwan no, na sɛ ɔka me a, wode owuo no bɛfa. " Ananse faa mpataa no de sii wɔ nsuo no mu, na wɔsan baa fie. Anɔpa foforo no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, wɔkɔɔ sɛ wɔbɛhwɛ akwan no mu, na wɔakyere mpataa mmienu. Crow no kaa sɛ, "Spider, wofa mmienu yi, na daakye yɛba, na (traps no) akyere anan no, me nso mebɛfa wɔn. " Spider no buae sɛ, "Ɔbarima yi! Wofa me yɛ me nkwasea a ɔfata; wofa mmienu yi, na daakye no, me nso mebɛfa anan no. " Crow no faa wɔn. Anɔpahema no, wɔsan kɔɔ traps no nkyɛn, na wɔnyaa mpataa anan. Abɛn kae sɛ, "Wofa nnanan yi, na ɔkyena yɛbɛkyere nwɔtwe, me nso me bɛfa wɔn. " Spider no buae sɛ, "Wofa me yɛ me nkwasea; wofa yi nnanan, na ɔkyena me nso me bɛfa nwɔtwe. " Anɔpa foforo no, wɔkɔɔ sɛ wɔbɛkɔ akɔhwɛ traps no na wɔyii nwɔtwe. Abɛn kae sɛ, "Fa (wɔn), na ɔkyena me nso me bɛfa du-nsia no. " Akɔre no kae sɛ, "Wofa me yɛ me nkwasea; wofa awotwe no, na ɔkyena me bɛnya du-nsia no. " Ɛde besi ha no mpataa no abrɛ, na Abɛn no kyerɛɛ Akɔre no sɛ, "Kɔfa mpataa yi a ɛporɔw no, na bere a obi tɔn no, obi bɛnya ntanu. (£16) ma biara kɛkɛ (trap), enti wo nso, Father Spider, fa mpataa yi, fa mpataa a ɛporɔw na fa mma me.” Ananse no kae sɛ, “Dabi, wofa mpataa no na wotɔn, na mefa mpataa a ɛporɔw no na mefa kɔ. ” Wɔde (wɔn nneɛma) kɔɔ dwadibea. Ntɛm ara na krakra no adi ne nneɛma nyinaa afi hɔ; ɔnyaa sika kɔkɔɔ dɔteɛ pii. Nanso ananse no aka nneɛma. Ɛnna no, Krak kae kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Fa saa mpataa a ɛporɔw yi bɔ no kurukuruwa na wɔntɔ. " Ananse no maa no so, ɔkae sɛ, "Me tɔn mpataa a ɛporɔw! " Mprempren ahenni no tee (no), na ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛhɔ na ɔkwaa yi fi, kɔfrɛ no mmra. " Ananse no kɔe. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, “Wosusuw sɛ yɛn nkurase yi yɛ nkwaseasɛmfo; seesei wo yɔnko no aba abɛtɔn mpataa, w’anhu no, na mmom wode mpataa a ɛporɔw bɛto yɛn so? ” Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, “bɔ no. ” Enti wɔbɔɔ no. Ɛnna crow no kae sɛ, “Spider, woahu, wo ani abere wo; wosee sɛ woresrɛ kwasea bi bɛdi wo dwuma, na woahu sɛ wo ara ne kwasea no. ”

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
