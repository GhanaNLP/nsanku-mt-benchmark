Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBƐYƐ NA OBIAKO NKYERƐ NE FIBEA A OBI FIRI MU BAEƐ

Ɛbɛyɛ bere bi a na ɔbɔmmɔfo bi wɔ hɔ. Sɛ ɔsɔre a, na ɔkɔ kwaeɛ mu kɔhwehwɛ mmoa kɔkum, sɛ ɔbɛnya bi adi, na bi nso atɔn. Ɛnɛ da bi na ɔkɔɔ kwaeɛ mu; ɔtee Kokotee, kwaeɛ-pɔn no, frɛɛ ne nua sɛ, "Kokotee Asamoa e! " Ɔbuaeɛ sɛ, "aane, nuabarima, aane. " Ɔno (pɔn a ɔdi kan no) kae sɛ, "Adwuma bere (wɔ yɛn afuo mu) adu, enti yɛnkɔ dadeɛ-dwumayɛbea, ma ɔnkyekyere dadeɛ no na ɔmfa nkyɛn (yɛn nsaano), na sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ yɛtwa nnua biara a, yɛbɛtumi atwa. ." Bere a Bush-pig frɛɛ ne nuanom no, na ɔhwehwɛfo no akoto ahintaw, na ɔtee nkɔmmɔ no nyinaa. Mprempren Bush-pig nua a ɔdi kan no bisaa ne nua Bush-pig sɛ, "Na kuraa bɛn na yɛbɛkɔ akɔbɔ dadeɛ? " Ɔbuaeɛ sɛ, "Yɛbɛkɔ kuraa a wɔfrɛ no Across-the-stream. " Na ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "bɛn da? " Ɔbuaeɛ sɛ, "Monday. " Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no tee saa nhyehyɛeɛ yi nyinaa, na ɔtuu kwan kɔɔ fie. Afei, bere a ɔbaa fie no, ɔka kyerɛɛ Asuom-kwan no so panyin nsɛm no, ne sɛ, nea ɔtee bere a ɔkɔɔ kwae no. Na ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa kyerɛɛ agya-panyin no sɛ, "Momma mmofra no nkɔtwa nnua na wɔmfa mmra, na dwoada no, bush-pigs no bɛsesa wɔn ho bɛba, yɛbɛfa wɔn de akyekyere wɔn ho wɔ nnua no ho. " Mmofra no kɔtwaa nnua no, na wɔde bae. Agya-panyin no kɔka kyerɛɛ kɔkɔɔ-dwamfoɔ no sɛ ɔntwa dade mma no. Na adwumfoɔ no bisaa ɔhene no sɛ, “Na saa dadeɛ a ɛdɔɔso yi nyinaa a woseɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mebɔ no, wode bɛyɛ dɛn? ” Na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, sɛnea ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ bi akɔ kwaeɛ mu, na wasan abɔ amanneɛ sɛ wɔ Dwoada no, mmoa bi bɛdane wɔn ho ayɛ nnipa sɛ wɔbɛba n’adwumadwamu abɛyɛ nneɛma. Ɔbɔfo tuu mmirika kɔbɔɔ dade a wɔde bɔ nneɛma ntɛm ara. Bere a ɔpanyin no de ne nsa aka nnuadewa ne staples no ho awie no, ɔmaa kuro-bɔfo no bɔɔ iron gong no, na ɔkae sɛ, “Ɛdwoada no, be it woman, or be it man, ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara kɔ baabi. ” Ɛduruu Dwoada no, anɔpahema no, ɔbaa panyin bi kae kyerɛɛ ɔbɔmmɔfo no sɛ, “Kɔyam pepper, ne salt, ne onions, ne baabi a mmoa no bɛbɔ wɔn honam ani na wɔatoto wɔn ho; woayɛ dɛn? Ɛnneɛ, sɛ woka hɔ a, fa peppers no bɔ so, na mmoa no bɛba ha, na yɛbɛkyere wɔn, na ebia bi bɛdwane akɔfa wɔn honam ani, na wɔde abɔ so, bere a peppers no bɛpira wɔn, na wɔbɛtow wɔn agu, wɔbɛyɛ nkurɔfoɔ bio. Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kɔɔ hɔ na ɔde ne ho siee hɔ. Na ɔtee sɛ wɔrefrɛ no sɛ, “Kokotee Asamoa, Monday adu, momma yɛnkɔ. ” Ɛnneɛ wɔn nyinaa bae. Wɔpaa wɔn aboa nhoma no, na wɔde sii hɔ. Seesei, (Pigs no,) a na ɔyɛ nhabannuru ho ɔbenfo no ka wɔn ho, na ɔde ne nhoma no kɔtoo baabi foforo. Ɔbɔfo no hwɛɛ wɔn, na ɔmaa wɔkɔe. Ɔkɔfaa nhoma no nyinaa de pepper a wɔapae no bɔɔ ho, na ɔde kɔtoo nsuo no mu maa nsuo no faa kɔe. Nanso Herbalist no deɛ a na ɔde ato nkyɛn no, ɔhwehwɛfo no anhu. Ɔhwehwɛfo no baa fie, na Ɔhene no nso frɛɛ nkurɔfo a wɔahyia wɔ nkyerɛwfo no dɔnno ho bɛkaa ne fie. Bere a saa nkurɔfoɔ yi baa ɔhene no fie no, wɔbisaa ɔhene no sɛ, “Dɛn nti na woafrɛ yɛn? ” Ɔhene no kaa sɛ, “Na woyɛ me mmarima, na woadwane akɔtena baabi foforɔ; ɛnnɛ woasan aba, ɛno nti na meka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ woba, efisɛ meremma kwan mma wo nkɔ bio. ” Mmoa no ka kyerɛɛ ɔhene no sɛ, “Nea woaka no, yɛate; nanso yɛnim sɛ Ɔdomankoma no hu. Nta bae ka yɛn ho asɛm kyerɛɛ mo nyinaa, nanso ɛno mfa ho, yɛn ne mo bɛtena abom; nanso yɛnim sɛ nea wose no, ɛyɛ atoro; ɛmfa ho, ɛno mfa ho; yɛda saa ɔbɔfoɔ no ase. Ɛnneɛ yɛn ne mo bɛtena abom; nanso ɛwɔ hɔ adeɛ baako a yɛbɔ ho taboo ne sɛ, wobɛda yɛn mfiase adi; anaa sɛ wo manfoɔ biara bɛda yɛn mfiase adi; sɛ ɛba saa a, yɛbɛpae yɛn mu, wo abusuakuo yi, na yɛakɔ.” Ɛnneɛ, bere a Ɔhene kɔfrɛɛ wɔn no, ɔdɔmfoɔ ne afoforɔ bi dwanee. Na aduruyɛfoɔ no kɔgyee ne honam ani na ɔdwanee, nanso nea aka no danee akyi. Ɛno nti na wɔantumi anhu wɔn honam ani. Ɔhene no penee tebea a wɔde too hɔ no so, na wɔn ne mmoa no tenaa ase anigye so. Mprempren akuraa no mmarima no waree mmoa no mmaa bi, wɔwoo mma. Mprempren, da bi na mmoa no mu biako ne akuraa no mu biako de wɔn nsam reko. Ɛno akyi no, ɔkuraa no kae aboa no sɛ, “Fa wo ho fi hɔ, aboa a wote sɛ wo, woyɛ Bush-pig company no muni. ” Anka ɔka saa no ara na mmoa no nyinaa ani beree no, na wɔkɔɔ ɔpanyin no fie sɛ wɔrebɛka akyerɛ no sɛ, “Akwanya a yɛka kyerɛɛ wo no abɛba mu, enti wone bɛyɛ dɛn? ” Ɔpanyin no maa wɔn kɔfrɛɛ nkurɔfo no. a ɔde ɔhaw no bae no. Ɔpanyin no hwɛɛ asɛm no mu yiye na obuu atɛn sɛ mmoa no nnyae sobo no. efisɛ (ɔkae) na ɛyɛ bere tenten a wɔaba na eyi nso na ɛyɛ bere a edi kan a obiara aka biribiara akyerɛ wɔn. Nanso mmoa no kae sɛ, “Yɛnnye ntom. ” Na Ɔhene no kae sɛ, “Worente, na wosusuw sɛ saa ɔbarima yi kae no yɛ atoro, wonyɛ Bush-pigs? ” Mmoa no kae sɛ, “Oh, yɛate. ” Ɛno nti, mmoa no ne nsuo no so nkurɔfo no dii ako. Mmoa no sɛee akuraa no kɔsii sɛ nnipa bɛyɛ du pɛ na wɔaka. Na wɔsrɛɛ ayamhye na wɔka kyerɛɛ mmoa no sɛ wɔwɔ hokwan wɔ wɔn ho. Mmoa no tiee, na wɔbɔɔ amanneɛ kyerɛɛ nkurɔfo no sɛ, “Asɛm bi a ɛda adi dada no, ɛnyɛ den sɛ wote ase, na sɛ wo ne yɛn bɛtena ase a, yɛbɛtaboo yɛn mfiase ho nsɛm nyinaa. ” Sɛ wosusuw anaa wose no bio a, ɛnneɛ yɛbɛbisa wo sɛ wonyi saa kwae a ɛyɛ den paa no nkyerɛ yɛn sɛ yɛfiri baa ha sɛ yɛbɛsan akɔ hɔ.” Na nkurɔfo no kae sɛ, “Yɛrentumi nnyɛ saa ade no bio da. Nea yɛayɛ yi asɛe yɛn abusuakuo no. Yɛrennyɛ saa bio. Enti wɔmaa amansan dawurubɔ bi bae denam odawuro gongs no bɔ a wɔbɔe no so sɛ obiara nka obiara mmaninyɛ, na amansan no nkyerɛkyerɛ no nnya nkuro no nsɛe. Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom betumi afa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no nso betumi nkamfo me sɛ meka kyerɛɛ no.
