Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

BIRIBIARA NNI HƆ (SƐ YƐSURO)

Wɔka sɛ na ɔgya kɛseɛ bi ne ne yere te hɔ, na wɔwɔ mma mmienu. Mprempren ɛna ne papa a wɔwɔ saa mma yi kɔ kwaeɛ mu da biara da kɔkyere nam ba wɔn nkyɛn. Ɛda bi, wɔn maame ne wɔn papa kae kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Sɛ wɔsɔre a, ɛsɛ sɛ wɔteɛm kae sɛ, 'Ɛnni baabiara a yɛsuro, ɛnni baabiara a yɛsuro. '" Ɛnɛ wɔkɔɔ kwaeɛ kɔɔ, na wogyaw wɔn mma hɔ. Afei Dankwanta, anadwo-kɔkɔɔ no baeɛ, na mma no teɛm.

Biribiara nni hɔ wɔ baabiara a yɛsuro no, Biribiara nni hɔ wɔ baabiara a yɛsuro no!

Anadwo-akora no kae sɛ, "Hwan na ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ wimpaa asɛm a ɛte sɛɛ? " Wɔbuae sɛ, "papa ne maame na wɔkaa no. " Anadwo-akora no kae sɛ, "Wɔnni fɔ; ɛsɛ sɛ wimpaa sɛ, 'Mmerɛ bɛsesa, mmerɛ bɛsesa. " Sesei, saa nkokoaa no, wɔn maame ne wɔn papa san bae, na wɔtee wɔn mma no nteɛm.

"Mmerɛ no nkyɛ nsesa, Mmerɛ no nkyɛ nsesa a!"

Wɔkaa sɛ, "Hwan na waka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ wopae asɛm a ɛte sɛɛ? " Wɔbuae sɛ, "Ɔbarima bi ka kyerɛɛ yɛn. " Afei wɔn maame ne wɔn papa kae sɛ, "Ɛnsɛ sɛ wopae no sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ wopae, 'Ɛnni baabiara a yɛsuro, ɛnni baabiara a yɛsuro. '" Wɔde nkokoaa no nam maa wɔn na wɔkɔe. Anadwo-akokora no san bae, na na ɔayɛ akofena mmienu. Mprempren wɔ beaeɛ a nkokoaa no da no, na nnua mmienu bi gyina hɔ. Bere a Night-jar no baeɛ no, ɔbɔɔ nnua mmienu no, na ɔde abɛn no bɔɔ abɛn no. Ɔka kyerɛɛ nkokoaa no sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ wosrɛ sɛ, 'Mmerɛ bɛsesa, mmerɛ bɛsesa', na bere a wo maame ne wo papa san ba no, ɛsɛ sɛ woka sɛ ɛyɛ me na meka kyerɛɛ mo. " Night-jar no tuu kwan kɔtoo abɛn no so abɛn biako. Ɛnkyɛree no, nkokoaa no maame ne wɔn papa san bae. Wɔkae sɛ, "Hena na ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ wimpae? " Night-jar no buae sɛ, "Me (yɛe). " Ɔkraman barima no tuu mmirika sɛ ɔbɛkyere no, bere a Night-jar no tuu kwan na ɔkraman no de ne ho bɔɔ peaw no ho. Night-jar no tuu kwan wɔ peaw a ɛto so abien no so. Bere a anka ɔkraman bea no bɛtu mmirika akɔkyere no no, ɔno nso na ɔde ne ho bɔɔ peaw no ho. Night-jar no kae kyerɛ mmofra no sɛ, “Seesei, wuhu sɛ worennya nam bio. Ɛno ne nea mehyɛɛ da kyerɛɛ mo sɛ, ‘mmerɛ no bɛsesa ntɛm ara.’”

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
