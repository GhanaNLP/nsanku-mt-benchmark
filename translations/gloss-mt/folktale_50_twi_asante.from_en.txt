Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

Na Abosom a wɔyɛ adagyaw no bae wiase mu

Ɛbɛyɛɛ bere bi a na ɔbaa bi wɔ mma dubaako; na da biara bere a ɔsɔre na ɔnoa aduane no, mma no nyinaa di. Wɔn maame annya bi a wɔbɛdi. Afei ɔdwen ho paa, na ɔkɔɔ afuo no ase na ɔka kyerɛɛ Silk-cotton dua no sɛ, "me mma du-baako no, mɛsoma wɔn sɛ wɔmmra wo ase ha na wɔntwa wo nkorabata du-baako no, na wɔnkum wɔn mma no mma. " Silk-cotton dua no kae sɛ, "MATE, na mɛyɛ no ama wo. " Ɛna no tuu kwan kɔɔ fie, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne mma sɛ, "Ɛnsɛ sɛ woyɛ. Wokɔ afuo no ase, Silk-cotton dua no ase; akutu bi wɔ hɔ, kɔfa ba. Mmofra no tuu kwan. Wɔkɔduruu Silk-cotton dua no ase. Number Eleven kae sɛ, "Number a ɛto so baako, gyina pintinn; Number a ɛto so abien, gyina pintinn; Number a ɛto so abiɛsa, gyina pintinn; Number a ɛto so anan, gyina pintinn; Number a ɛto so anum, gyina pintinn; Number a ɛto so asia, gyina pintinn; Number a ɛto so ason, gyina pintinn; Number a ɛto so awotwe, gyina pintinn; Number a ɛto so akron, gyina pintinn; Number du, gyina pintinn; na me ankasa, Number Eleven, megyina pintinn. " Ɛnna Number du-baako kasa kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Wunnim r no nkutoo. Nea enti a maame kae sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ yɛkɔfa akutu anaa? Ne nuanom mmarima no kae sɛ, "Dabi. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔka kyerɛɛ Silk-cotton dua yi sɛ, sɛ yɛkɔ hɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ ɛbɛbɔ nkorabata na ɛbɔ yɛn; enti mmomma nyinaa twa nkorabata na wɔtow gu Silk-cotton dua yi so. " Wɔtwaa nkorabata no, na wɔtow guu Silk-cotton dua no so, pim! pen! pim! pen! (was the sound they made). Silk-cotton dua no susui sɛ mmofra no aba. Ɔtwaa ne nkorabata no mu du-baako, na ɔde too fam. Little Number Eleven kae sɛ, "You have seen, had we gone on there, the Silk-cotton tree would have killed us. " Wɔgyee akutu no na wɔde kɔɔ wɔn maame nkyɛn. Ɔnoa wɔn, oh! prɛko pɛ na mmofra no nyinaa adidi. Asɛm yi deɛ, metumi nnyina ano, enti mebɛfa mmofra yi akɔma wɔn Ɔsoro-nyame. Anɔpa foforo no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔkɔkaa asɛm no nyinaa kyerɛɛ Ɔsoro-nyame no sɛ, "Mmofra bi a mewoo wɔn no, bere a mepɛ sɛ me-di aduan no, metumi nnya biribi nni (efisɛ wɔdi ntɛm na wɔdi dodo); ɔkɔm reku me; enti me srɛ mo, wɔmfa mmofra no mmra. na ɔkumm no, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mɛnya biribi a mɛbɛdi." Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Ɛno yɛ nokware ankasa? " Ɔbaa no kae sɛ, "Me ne ne ti rekasa a ne mu yɛ fitaa. " Ɔsoro-nyame no paw nkrakyerɛfoɔ, na wɔkɔtutu amena kɛseɛ bi a wɔde nkotokuo (abubu) na ɔkɔgyee ɔwɔ bi ne ɔwɔ bi de guu amena no mu na ɔkataa so. Na afei ɔmanpanyin no kɔfrɛɛ mmofra no. Ansa na wɔadu baabi a amena no da no, Number Eleven kae sɛ, "Number One, gyina hɔ; Number Two, gyina hɔ; Number Three, gyina hɔ; Number Four, gyina hɔ; Number Five, gyina hɔ; Number Six, gyina hɔ; Number Seven, gyina hɔ; Number Eight, gyina hɔ; Number Nine, gyina hɔ; Number Ten, gyina hɔ; na me ara, Number Eleven ketewa no, megyina hɔ; ɛsɛ sɛ wopa" Ɛwɔ ha, ɛnsɛ sɛ wofa hɔ. Ne nuanom mmarima no kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na bere a kwan kɛseɛ bi da hɔ no, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛfa kwaeɛ no mu? " Seesei, bere a wɔrekɔ kwan so no, na wɔn nyinaa akura akuturuku. Namba du-baako no kae sɛ, "To nnua yi mu baako gu wɔ kwan yi so. " Wɔtoo no guu hɔ, na ɛhwee mu yiridi (yɛ nnyigyei a ɛbɔeɛ). Namba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Ɛhɔ na woteɛ, wuhu sɛ, anka yɛbɛfa hɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛn nyinaa wu. " Enti wɔfaa akwanhyia bi kɔɔ hɔ kɔɔ Sky-god no nkyɛn. Sky-god no maa wɔtutuu amena, kataa so, na wɔde nkongua hyɛɛ so sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mmofra no bɛtenaa so no, wɔbɛkɔtoo amena no mu. Mmofra no bɛduruu Sky-god no anim. Ɔsoro-nyame kae sɛ, "Wɔde nkongua asi hɔ, mobɛtumi akɔtena so. " Ɛnna Namba Dubiako no kae sɛ, "Hena na yɛn ne yɛn ho sɛ yɛbɛtumi atena nkongua a ɛyɛ fɛ saa so; enti, Sire, yɛrebɛtena nkyɛn ha. " Ɛhɔ na Ɔsoro-nyame no de n'ani kyerɛɛ mmofra no; ɔkae sɛ, "Me de mmofra yi bɛkɔ Owuo akuraa ase. " Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a ɛdaa adi no, ɔfrɛɛ mmofra no; ɔkae sɛ, " Ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ Owuo (a ɔte hɔ) na wode sika-kwan, sika-kwan a wɔde kae, sika-kwan a wɔde snuff, sika-kwan a wɔde whet, ne sika-kwan a wɔde fly-switch kɔgye no. Number Eleven kae sɛ, "Wo ne yɛn wura, baabiara a wobɛma yɛn akɔ no, yɛbɛkɔ. " Asahen-nyame no kae sɛ, "Wɔn nni hɔ. " Mmofra no tuu kwan kɔɔ Owuo akuraa ase. Wɔduruu hɔ na Owuo kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara ba ha da, w'aba ha? " Wɔbuae sɛ, "Na yɛrekyinkyin na yɛba ha wɔ akwanhyia mu. " Owuo kae sɛ, "O, ɛnneɛ ɛyɛ. " Mprempren na Owuo wɔ mmofra du, ne no nso, wɔyɛɛ no du-baako. Bere a nneɛma hyɛɛ aseɛ yerae (i. e. Bere a ɛyɛ sum no, Owu kyekyɛɛ mmofra no baako baako na ɔde baako maa ne mma biara, bere a ɔno ankasa na ɔne nɔma du-baako kɔɔ ahome. Bere a ɛyɛɛ sum no, ɛnna Owu de n’asomdwoeɛ kɔɔ kɔkɔɔ kɔsii sɛ ɛhyerɛn sɛ ɔbɛkyere nɔma du-baako no ɔne no. Namba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Owuo, mennya nna." Owuo kae sɛ, "Dɛn bere na wobɛda?" Namba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Sɛ wode sika kɔkɔɔ paipu bi bɛma wo wim a, ɛnneɛ metumi ada." Na Owuo kɔgyee no maa no. Bere tiaa bi akyi no, Owuo san sii n’aniwa sɛ ɔbɛfa ne ho akɔfa namba dubiako no. Namba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Owuo, mennya nna." Owuo kae sɛ, "Wobɛda da? " Namba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Sɛ wode sika kɔkɔɔ snuff-box ba me nkyɛn a, mɛda." Na Owuo de no baa no. Bio, ɛnkyɛeɛ no, Owuo rebɛkyere Namba Dubiako. Nnipa du-baako kae sɛ, "Mennya nna." Owuo kae sɛ, "Wobɛda da bɛn? " Nnipa du-baako kae sɛ, "Sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa sika kɔkɔɔ kwan na meada no kakra a. " Owuo de no maa no. Bere tiaa bi twaam no, na Owuo rekyere no. Namba Dubiako kae sɛ, "nananom, mennya nna. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ, wobɛda da bɛn? " Namba Du-baako kae sɛ, "nananom, sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa sika kɔkɔɔ abotiri ama me a, ɛnneɛ menna me da. " Na Owuo kɔɔ de no bae. Bio, ɛnkyɛeɛ no, Owuo sɔre bio. Namba Dubiako kae sɛ, "O, nananom, mekae sɛ mennya nnaa." Owuo kae sɛ, "Na da bɛn na wobɛda? " Namba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa calabash a ntokuro ahyɛ mu ma na woakɔgu nsuo mu, na woaforo aduane bi ama me, na metumi ada." Owuo maa calabash no so na ɔkɔɔ asubɔnten no so. Bere a ɔde nsuo no guu mu no, nsuo-kwan no mu ntokuro no maa no twaa mu. Mprempren nɔma du-baako kae sɛ, "Monyane ntu nkɔ. " Na wɔsɔree kɔɔ; na namba du-baako twaa plantain nkorabata, na ɔde too baabi a ne nuanom dae, na ɔde ntama kataa so. Mprempren na owuo no wɔ nsuo no ho, na ɔde nsuo no regu. Mprempren Owuo Barima frɛɛ Owuo Ɔbea sɛ, "Ho there, Death. " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Adwo. " Ɔkae sɛ, "What are you doing? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Alas, is it not some small child whom I have got; when I am about to catch him, he says, 'I am not yet asleep'; he has taken all my things, and now says I must take a strainer and splash water. " Owuo Barima no kaa sɛ, "Ah, woyɛ abofra ketewa; seesei, sɛ wotutu nhaban na woka a. Wɔ akutu no mu, na afei petepete nsuo, ɛrenyɛ hwee anaa? Ɔbaa owu kae sɛ, "Oh, ɛyɛ nokware. " Ɔtwaa nhaban no de guu mu na ɔpetepete nsuo no na ɔkɔɔ so. Nɔma Dubiako kae sɛ, "Owui, woaba dedaw? Noa aduane no. " Owui noa aduane no, ɔhyew n’asom sɛ ɔbɛkum Nɔma Dubiako nuanom na wanoa no aduane. Bere a ɔkɔe no, ɔanhwehwɛ wɔn yie, na ɔno ankasa kunkum ne mma du no nyinaa. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, ntɛm ntɛm bere a nneɛma daa adi no, Owuo sɔre, na ɔtenaa ogya no nkyɛn. Du-baako kae sɛ, "Nana, tsetse nwansena bi rete wo nufu so." Owuo kae sɛ, "Fa nwansena-nsakrae a ɛda hɔ no, na kum no mma me." Namba du-baako kae sɛ, "Meyɛ wo adom; wo nipa a woyɛ nipa kɛse, sɛ tsetse nwansena bi te wo so, na sika kɔkɔɔ nwansena-nsakrae da hɔ (wode saa tete yi bedi dwuma a), ma mefa no na meba abɛkum no." Owuo kae sɛ, "Fa no fi ruma no mu." Ɛnneɛ Namba Dubiako kɔɔ no de bae. Ɔhyɛɛ da tuu nwansena no kɔe, ɔankum no. Namba Du-baako kae sɛ, "O, ɛnnɛ, baabi a tsetse nwansena yi bɛda no, ɛhɔ na me ne no bɛda. " Afei Namba Dubiako kɔɔ dan no mu na ɔfaa ne bag a na sika kɔkɔɔ nsuo ne nneɛma no nyinaa da mu no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Grandmother Death, nothing will suffice save that I get the tsetse fly, put it in this bag, and bring it to you. " Number Eleven tuu kwan, yiri! yiri! yiri! Ɔduruu kuro no awiei, na ɔkae sɛ, "Ho, ɛhɔ, Nana Owu, kyɛ me bɔne sɛ meka saa, nanso sɛ wanyɛ kwasea a ɔyɛ pɛ no, anka metumi agya wo nneɛma nyinaa; anka me nuanom a meka wɔn no anya ɔkwan afiri hɔ; na anka metumi ama wo nso akum wo mma du no nyinaa? Me deɛ, mebɛkɔ akɔnkyɛn. Owuo kae sɛ, "Wo, abofra a ɔte sɛɛ; baabiara a wotena no, me bɛda hɔ. " Number Eleven no tuu ne ho, yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! Na Owuo nso dii ne ho mmirika. Bere a Number Eleven no rekɔ no, ɔtwaa ne nuanom anim, na wɔte kwan no so. Na wɔreyɛ bird-trap. Number Eleven no kae sɛ, "Woankɔ nso ankɔ? Owuo reba nti, momma yɛnni kwan bi a yɛbɛfa so agye yɛn ho. Mprempren Owuo no baa wɔn so. Number Eleven no faa aduro hwie guu ne nuanom so, na wɔkɔɔ silk-cotton dua no so. Na Owuo no gyinaa silk-cotton dua no ase. Ɔkae sɛ, "Wɔ ha ara, mehuu mmofra yi, na wɔakɔ he? " Number Eleven no na ɛte hɔ atifi, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuanom sɛ, "Me bɛyɛ nsuo wɔ ne so. " Ne nuanom no kae sɛ, "E, ɔrehwehwɛ yɛn sɛ yɛbɛkyere yɛn, na yɛadwane aba atena ha, nanso wose, 'Me bɛyɛ nsuo wɔ ne so. " Number Eleven no annye antie, na ɔyɛɛ nsuo wɔ Death so. Owuo kae sɛ, "Ɛw, wɔ ha, ɛnnɛ woahu ɔhaw. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Wo, abofra, a wote hɔ no, 'Kyere-he-ne, Kyere-he-ne. " Ɛno akyi no, abofra no mu biako hwee ase. "Kyere-he-ne", nea ɛtɔ so mmienu no hwee ase. Mprempren na Number Eleven nko ara ka hɔ. Owuo kae sɛ, "Abofra, 'kyere-he-ne '," na Number Eleven huruw kɔɔ asase no so, kirim! Na Owuo nso kɔɔ so kɔɔ Silk-cotton dua no so. Number Eleven no kae sɛ, "Wo, ɔbaa kɛseɛ paa, wo nso, 'kyere-he-ne'. " Na Owuo nso baa fam, tum! Na wauwu. Number Eleven no kɔgyee aduro, bɔɔ ne nsam, na ɔpete guu ne nuanom no so, na wɔsɔre. Na Number Eleven no rekɔto aduro no agu, bere a ebi tɔɔ Owuo so, na Owuo sɔree. Ɔkae sɛ, "Woakum me, na woakanyan me nso; ɛnnɛ wo ne me bɛdi asɛm. " Ɛnna wɔn nyinaa hyɛɛ aseɛ tu mmirika prɛko pɛ, kiri! kiri! kiri! Afei na Death redi wɔn mmirika. Wɔrekɔ no, na asuo kɛseɛ bi da hɔ wɔ nsuyiri mu. Bere a Number Eleven ne n’uanom duu no no, na wonim sɛnea wɔbɛtumi atutu nsuo no. Number Eleven nko ara annim sɛnea wɔbɛtumi atutu nsuo no. Mmofra no gyinaa wɔ afa foforo no so, wɔkeka su, na wɔkeka su, na wɔkeka su, wɔn ano hyehyeɛ. Number Eleven no, ɔdane bɔɔ ɔbo. Owuo bɛduruu asubɔnten no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, mmofra yi, mo gyina hɔ, momfa abotan nkɔto wɔn ano a ɛho ahyehye wɔn. " Owuo, bere a ɔhwɛɛ fam no, ɔhunuu abotan bi a ɛda hɔ, ɔgyee no bɔɔ no, bere a abotan no retu kwan no, ɛkaa sɛ, "Mframa, fa me kɔto me wɔ afa foforo no. " Ɛbɛdii afa foforo no so. Namba Dubiako kaa sɛ, "Me na me wɔ ha. " Owuo kaa sɛ, "Ah, abofra yi! Me nni asɛm biara a mebɛka akyerɛ mo bio, nanso nea meka kyerɛ mo nyinaa ne sɛ: monkɔ nkɔtena fie, na nsakra wo ho nkɔ abosom nketewa bi mu, na sɛ obiara, a mepɛ sɛ mefa no, ba wo nkyɛn a, wobɛbɔ me amanneɛ; sɛ mepɛ saa a, mebɛgya no na meama wo no akyɛdeɛ; nanso nea mepɛ no, ɛsɛ sɛ wogye no ma me." (Ɛne sɛ, nkyɛkyɛ.) Saa ara na Abosom nketewa no. -Anyame baa wiase. Wɔfiri abofra ketewa a ɔto so du-baako.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebia wobɛtumi afa no sɛ nokware, na nea aka no wobɛtumi akamfo me sɛ meka kyerɛɛ no.
