Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

OWEA (a ɔyɛ nnua), ABOSOM-AKOTERE (a ɔyɛ chameleon), NE OPATUO (a ɔyɛ ɔkɔre) BAE SƐ WƆBƐNYA WƆN NNYIGYEI A ƐDA NSOW

OWEA, dua-bɛa, (a ɔwɔ abodin) Kwaduampon Kyerefo, Opatuo, akɔre, ne Abosom-Akotere, chameleon, (a wɔn abodin ne) “Ɔbɔfo no wu”, “Ɔbɔfo a ɔde afe biara yɛ abɔde”, “Ɔnam brɛoo paa nanso ɔbɛduru aborɔfo asase a ɛwɔ akyirikyiri”, (saa mmoa yi nyinaa) tenaa bɔ mu pɛnkoro. Mprempren, na wɔn mu biako nni yere. Na Adobe-dua no tɔn ne ho maa wɔn. Da biara, bere a Adobe-tree sɔree no, ɔkɔɔ sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ yi kwan; ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, ɔhwɛɛ wɔn mu bi; na ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no nso wɔ foforo so. Da koro bi, bere a na ɛyɛ Ɔdomankoma-wuo (sɛ ɔbɛhyɛ Adobe no som) na ɔhwehwɛɛ Adobe-dua no, baabi a obetumi ahu no? Na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔka Dua-aboa ne akɔre no asɛm no kyerɛɛ. Na wɔkae sɛ, “Monkɔfrɛ no. ” Na Ɔbɔfoɔ wu-owuo nso maa ne nne sɛ, “Adobe o! Adobe o! ”

Duan-aboa no teɛɛ sɛ:

“Bere a ɔkɔe sɛ ɔansan amma, ɔkɔe sɛ ɔansan amma, ɔkɔe sɛ ɔansan amma! ”

Na ɔkɔre frɛɛ sɛ:

“Yɛbuu ho akontaa sɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma na anka yɛbɛtɔ no, M! Yɛbuu ho akontaa sɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma na anka yɛbɛtɔ no, M! M!”

Na ɛno ne abɔdeɛ mmiɛnsa yi nnyigyei mfiase a ɛfiri mu baeɛ.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
