SƐNEA ƐBƐYƐE SƐ OBONTO YA, MPATAWA NO, RE-NANTEW KWA WƆ NSUO NO MU.

Wɔka sɛ na agya-nananom no wɔ hɔ, na n’agyapadeɛ ayera. Ɔkaa sɛ ɛyɛ "the water creatures" na ɛde nneɛma no kɔeɛ. Na krak no te hɔ, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Ah, wo ama yɛn ani wui, na nea yɛbɛhyɛ sɛ yɛbɛyɛ de akyerɛ wo nea ɛwɔ yɛn tirim ne (eyi): kɔfa kɔbere kɛntɛn na yɛn nyinaa de nusu nhyɛ mu, na nea ɔrentumi nsua ahyɛ mu no, ɛno na ɔwɔ (nneɛma no). "

Ɔde kɔbere kɛntɛn no bae. Okoto, krak no, suu hyɛɛ mu ma, wɔyii mu. Mmobonse bae na ɔsuu. Okawa bae na ɔsuu. Oyoyo, ɔkwan no, bae na ɔsuu. Otidi bae na ɔsuu. Obonko, kɔkɔɔ no, bae na ɔsuu. Akorese bae na ɔsuu. Opitiri bae na ɔsuu. Okobo bae na ɔsuu. Nsuo mu mmoa no nyinaa bae na wɔsuu. Obonto Ya a ɔtwa toɔ no, Minnow no, baeɛ sɛ ɔbɛsu; ɔannya nsuo mfi n’ani mu, enti na wɔhunuu sɛ Obonto Ya afa nneɛma no.

Nanso, ɔbaa-nananom kae sɛ, "Me renyɛ hwee mma wo. " Ɛno akyi no, abɔdeɛ no nyinaa kae sɛ, "Yɛn deɛ, yɛbɛbɔ no, efisɛ ɔde yɛn ayɛ aniwu. " Enti wɔbɔɔ no.

Bere a wɔawie abɔ no no, nananom kae sɛ, "Megyaw mo (daa) sɛ mobɛkyinkyin kwa; obiara renpɛ sɛ ɔne mo bɛyɛ biribiara. "

Ɛno ne nea enti a, bere a mmea kɔ nsuo mu no, wɔhu OBONTO YA rekyinkyin (seesei) wɔ hɔ.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
