Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

WOTE SƐ ANANSE A ƆYƐ NWANWA

Bere bi na Ananse a ɔyɛ Akwansere no te hɔ, bere a Nsia, ɔsoro-nyame no maame, yaree. Wɔkɔɔ so kɔgyee aduro-yɛfo bi sɛ ɔmmɛhwɛ no. Ɔbaa no, ɔkae sɛ perduan no sika kɔkɔɔ dust (i. e. £8) sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ no yare no. Afei, Ananse kɔɔ kɔkaa asɛm bi kyerɛɛ Wɔsoro-nyame no sɛ, "Ɔkwaa yi de wo sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no kɔ kwa; Ɔntumi nhwɛ no so, mma mehwɛ no mma wo, na sɛ mehwɛ no so a, m’abusuabɔ bɛtumi ayera. " Ananse faa asuanu sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. e. £4), na ɔkae sɛ, "Sɛ menntumi nsa saa yareɛ yi a, mɛma wo gyata a ɔte ase. " Anɔpa foforo no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, Nyankonpon, wim-nyame no maame, Nsia, afiri hɔ. Wɔkae sɛ, "Ananse, ɛyɛ dɛn? " Ananse firi saa beaeɛ no, ɔadwane. Mprempren, ɔkasa ne n'akyi kyerɛ sɛ me werɛ fi sɛ mekae sɛ, Ananse adamfo kɛseɛ ne Okusie, ɔkraman no. Ɔka kyerɛɛ Rat no nea ɛkɔɔ so no sɛ, "Ɛno na mahunu, enti afei ɛsɛ sɛ wo boa me. " Rat no buae sɛ, "Ɛkwan bɛn so betumi aboa wo? " Ananse buae sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ wokɔ kwan no mu atu kɔsi sɛ ɛbɛduru abotan a wɔama so no ase a ɔsoro-nyame no te so no. " Rat no kae sɛ, "Mebɛyɛ no ama wo. " Enti ɔyɛɛ no. Rat no baeɛ na ɔbɔɔ Ananse amanneɛ sɛ, "Meawie. " Ananse kɔɔ ne ba Ntikuma frɛɛ no; na ɔtwaa abɛn maa no; na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔnkɔ abantenten a ɛso no ase na ɔnkɔtena, na ɔhunuu sɛ ɔsoro-nyame de ne ho asi abantenten a ɛso no so, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔbɔ n’abɛn na ɔka sɛ:

Sɛ wokum Ananse a, abusuakuo no bɛba abɛsɛe!

Sɛ wobɛkyɛ Ananse a, abusuakuo no bɛma nne hinhim!

Ntikuma kɔtenaa ase wɔ ahɔhoyɛ nkongua a ɛwɔ hɔ no ase, na Ananse de ne ho sii hɔ sɛ ɔrekɔ Nyankonpon, soro-nyame no, ɛhɔ ara ne n’ankasa pɛ. Bere a ɔkɔe no, ɛnna soro-nyame no awerɛhowfo kyeree no; wɔde no kɔɔ soro-nyame no anim. Ɛnna ɔkasafoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Wo, Kwaku Ananse, wosee kyerɛɛ Nyankonpon, Wɔsoro-nyame no, sɛ ne maame Nsia wui a, obetumi akum wo; enti Nsia kɔeɛ nti, yɛbɛkum wo. " Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ wogyae me kwan ma meka me asɛm. " Nanso atemmufoɔ no kuraa no sɛ wɔbɛkɔ akum no. Ntikuma de ne nne kɔɔ soro sɛ: "Sɛ wokum Ananse a, abusuakuo no bɛba abɛsɛe!

Sɛ wobɛkyɛ Ananse a, abusuakuo no bɛma nne hinhim!

Wɔkaa sɛ, "Tie bio, tie! " Na bio abɛn no bɔɔ.

Sɛ wokum Ananse a, abusuakuo no bɛba abɛsɛe!

Sɛ wobɛkyɛ Ananse a, abusuakuo no bɛma nne hinhim!

Ɛnna Kontirehene hene no sɔre bɔɔ amanneɛ kyerɛɛ ɔsoro-nyame no sɛ, "Abusuakuo yi yɛ wo dea, ɔsoro-nyame, na wo ne Ya, Asase-nananom. Ɛnnɛ woanya Ananse na wode no bɛtu no kum. Ya, Asase-nananom, ka sɛ, 'Momma no nkɔ,' enti sɛ wogya no kwan a, ɛbɛyɛ yiye. Ɛnna ɔsoro-nyame no maa akwansrafoɔ no gyaa no kwan. Ɛno nti na ɛwɔ asɛm bi a ɛne sɛ, "Wo ne Ananse yɛ nwanwa sɛ. "

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
