Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA NO, ƆSORO-NYAME NSƐM NO BƐYƐƐ SƐ "SPIDER-STORIES"

Ananse, ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no, kɔɔ Nyankonpon, ɔsoro nyame no, sɛ ɔbɛtɔ ɔsoro nyame no nsɛm. Ɔsoro Nyame kae sɛ, "Wobɛtumi atɔ wɔn? " Akɔre no kae sɛ, "Mmom metumi. " Ɔsoro Nyame kae sɛ, "Nkurow akɛseɛ ne nnam te sɛ Kokofu, Bekwai, Asumengya aba, nanso wɔantumi antɔ wɔn, na wone a woyɛ ɔbarima kɛkɛ a wunni wura no, (wose) wobɛtumi? " Akɔre no kae sɛ, "Nsɛm no bo yɛ dɛn? " Ɔsoro Nyame kae sɛ, "Wɔntumi ntɔn hwee. " Gye Onini abɔdeɛ Python no; Osebo, Leopard; Mmoatia, Fairy; (ne) Mmoboro, Hornets." Akɔre no kae sɛ, "Mebɛfa nneɛma yi nyinaa bi aba, na (nea ɛsen saa no) mede me nananom, Nsia (Abofra a ɔto so nsia) bɛka akwammɔ no ho." Wim Nyame no kae sɛ, "Mokɔmfa wɔn afei." Akɔre no bae na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame asɛm no nyinaa sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ metɔ wim Nyame no anansesɛm ne Sk Y-God kae sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mede Onini, Python no ba; Osebo, Leopard no; Mmoatia, Fairy no; ne Mmoboro, Hornets no. na mekae sɛ mɛka mo ho akɔma wim yɛ no. Mprempren Spider no abisa ne yere Aso asɛm sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔyɛ na yɛnnya Onini, Python no? " Na Aso kae kyerɛ no sɛ, "Wokɔtwa palm-tree no baabi bi, na wotwa string creeper bi nso, na wode wɔn ba. " Na th e Spider bɛsan bae wɔn. Na Aso kae sɛ, "Fa wɔn kɔ nsuo no ase. " Na Ananse faa wɔn, bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛware sen no, ɛnyɛ tenten te sɛ no, wɔdaadaa, ɛware sen no. " Akɔre no kae sɛ, "Ɛhɔ na ɔwɔ, wɔdaadaa wɔ akyirikyiri. " Python (a ɔtee saa nkɔmmɔ a ɔde n’ani yɛɛ no no) kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛwɔ mu nyinaa? " Akɔre no kae sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ (me yere) Aso a ɔne me regye akyinnye sɛ saa nnuadewa yi yɛ l. Ɔsen wo, na me kae sɛ ɔyɛ nkontomfo." Na Onini, Python no kae sɛ, "Fa no ba, na wo mmɛsusuw me. " Ɔfaa mmɛdua no de too Python no nipadua so. Ɔkae sɛ, "Wotrɛw wo ho. " Na Python no trɛw ne ho, na Ananse faa nhama-kɔkɔɔ no kyekyeree no (na nteɛteɛ no yɛ nwenene! nwenene! nwenene! kosi sɛ ɔbaa ti no so). Ananse, akɔre no kae sɛ, "ɔkwasea, me de wo bɛkɔ ɔsoro nyame nkyɛn na mayɛanya ɔsoro nyame no nsɛm (de asesa). " Ananse de no kɔɔ Nyankopɔn (ɔsoro nyame). Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka kyerɛ sɛ. Me nsa kaa no, na ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ a ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ." Spider no san baeɛ na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere nea asi sɛ, "Hornets no aka." Ne yere no kae sɛ, "Hwɛ abɛn, fa nsuo ahyɛ mu, na fa kɔ. " Spider no kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu, bere a ɔhunuu Hornets a wɔsɛn hɔ, na ɔde nsuo no bi guu mu na ɔpetepete wɔn so. Afei akɔre no hwiee nea aka no guu ne ho so na ɔtwaa afifide no ahaban bi de kataa ne ti so. Na afei ɔkasa kyerɛɛ Hornets no sɛ, "Sɛnea osuo no aba no, anka ɛnsɛ sɛ woba na wura aba mu, me borɔdɔma, na osuo no anntu wo; wunhu sɛ mayi plantain ahaban bi akata me ho? " Ɛnna Hornet no kae sɛ, "Yɛda mo ase Aku, yɛda mo ase Aku. " Hornet no nyinaa tuu kwan kɔyeraa wɔ borɔdɔma no mu. Papa Spidana de n’ano kataa ano, na ɔkae sɛ, "Nkwasea, m’anya mo, na merefa mo akɔgye ɔsoro-nyame (exchange) no nsɛm. " Na ɔde Hornets no kɔɔ ɔsoro-nyame. Wim-nyame kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka, nea aka no da so ara. " Akɔnno no san bae pɛnkoro, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere, na ɔkae sɛ, "Osebo, ɔpɛn no da so ara. " Aso kae sɛ, "Kɔtu amena. " Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɛno nko ara, me te ase. " Afei akɔnno no kɔhwɛɛ ɔpɛn no anammɔn, na ɔtuu amena a ɛmu dɔ paa, na ɔkataa so, na ɔsan baa fie. Adekyeeɛ da no, nneɛma hyɛɛ aseɛ bɛdaa adi, na Spider no kaa sɛ ɔbɛkɔ, na ɔkɔeɛ no, na (lo) Leopard bi da amena no mu. Anansesɛm no kae sɛ: "Papa ba baa, maame ba baa, maka wo sɛ wunnom nsa, na afei, sɛnea wɔhwɛ kwan fi wo so no, woayɛ nsa, na ɛno nti na woahwe ase amena no mu; sɛ mekae wo a, da a edi hɔ no, sɛ wuhu me a, anaa saa ara na me mma biara, wobɛkɔ akɔkyere wɔn." Ɔpɔnkɔ no kae sɛ, "O! Meantumi anyɛ saa ade yi." Ananse kɔtwaa nnua mmienu, de baako sii ha, na baako nso sii baabi. Ɔkaa sɛ, "fa wo nsa baako ha, na wo nsa foforo nso ha. " Na Leopard no de wɔn sii hɔ, baabi a wɔhyɛɛ no. Bere a ɔrebɛforo no, Ananse maa ne sekan no so, na ɔtɔɔ no ntɛm ara bɔɔ ne ti, gao! (na ɛyɛ nnyigyei a ɛyɛ gao!) Amena no gyee Leopard no ne fom! Na ɛyɛ ne hwe ase a ɛrehwe ase no nnyigyei. Ananse nyaa odumgya de kɔɔ amena no mu kɔgyee ɔgya no. Ɔde ɔgya no yii no adi na ɔne no bae. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔkwasea, merefa wo akɔsesa wo ɔsoro-nyame anansesɛm. " Ɔmaa ɔgya no so kɔmaa Nyame, ɔsoro-nyame. Wim-nyame kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka, nea aka no da so ara da so. " Akɔre no bae, na ɔbɔɔ Akua ba a ɛyɛ nnua tuntum a n’anim yɛ tratraa, na ɔbɔɔ latex bi a ɛyɛ nnam fii dua bi so, na ɔde kataa doll no nipadua so, na ɔbɔɔ eto, na ɔde bi guu doll no nsa, na ɔbɔɔ bi bio na ɔde guu kɔbere basin bi mu, na ɔde nhama kyekyeree doll no ase. Na ɔde kɔɔ so de sii odum dua ase, baabi a Fairies no ba abɛdi agoru no ase. Na Fairy bi bɛba. Ɔkae sɛ, “Akua, menni mash yi bi? ” Ananse twee nhama no, na dɔla no bɔɔ ne ti. Fairy no kaa kyerɛɛ ne nuabea bi sɛ, “Ɔkae sɛ metumi di bi. ” Ɔkae sɛ, “Di bi afei. ” Na ɔwiee adidi no, na ɔdaa no ase. Nanso bere a ɔdaa no ase no, ɔmmua mmuaeɛ. Na Fairy no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea sɛ, “Me da no ase a, ɔmmua. ” Fairy no nuabea a ɔdi kan no kaa sɛ, “Slap her crying-place. ” Na ɔbɔɔ no pa! Na ne nsa bɛkaa hɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea sɛ, “Me nsa aka hɔ. ” Ɔkae sɛ, “Take the one that remains and slap her crying-place again. ” Na ɔde no bɔɔ no. Na eyi nso bɛgyinaa. Na Fairy no kyerɛɛ ne nuabea sɛ, “Me nsa mmienu yi akɔhyɛ mu. ” Ɔkae sɛ, “Fa w’akyi piapia no. ” Ɔde no bɔɔ mu, na ne yafunu bɛbɔɔ no mu. Na Ananse bae bɛkyekyeree no, na ɔkae sɛ, “Ɔkwase, me nsa aka wo, mebɛfa wo akɔ Sorosom Nyame nkyɛn nsesɛm. ” Na ɔne no kɔɔ fie. Na Ananse ka kyerɛɛ ne maame, Ya Nsia a ɔyɛ abofra a ɔtɔ so nsia no, sɛ, “Sɔre, momma yɛnkɔ, me de wo ne Fairy no bɛkɔ ama ɔsoro nyame no n’asɛm. ” Ɔmaa wɔn so, na ɔkɔɔ hɔ baabi a ɔsoro nyame no wɔ no. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Sorom-nyame, ɛha na abayifoɔ wɔ, na me nananom a mekae wɔn ho asɛm no, wɔ ha nso. ” Mprempren Sorom-nyame frɛɛ n’akannifoɔ, Kontire ne Akwam ahemfo, Adonten (asraafoɔ dɔm no mu panyin), Gyase (major-domo); Oyoko, Ankobea ne Kyidom (akyi-dɔm no mu panyin). Na ɔde asɛm no too wɔn anim sɛ, “Ahene akɛseɛ paa aba, na wɔantumi antɔ Sorosom-nyame no nsɛm, nanso Kwaku Ananse, akɔre no, atumi atua ka; meanya afiri ne nkyɛn Mmoboro, anantwi; meanya afiri ne nkyɛn Mmoatia, abofra; meanya afiri ne nkyɛn Osebo, ɔsebɔ; meanya afiri ne nkyɛn Onini, ɔnanka; na n’ankasa nso, Ananse aka ho. ntu n'ɛna kɔɔ lot no mu; saa nneɛma yi nyinaa da hɔ.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Mema no nkamfo. ” “Eee! ” (wɔteɛm). Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, “Kwaku Ananse, ɛfiri nnɛ kɔsi daakye nyinaa, meya me soro-nyame nsɛm de kɔma wo, kose, kose, kose! Me nhyira, nhyira, nhyira. Ɛnka bio yɛbɛfrɛ no Ɔsoro-nyame nsɛm, na mmom yɛbɛfrɛ no Spider-stories. ”

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
