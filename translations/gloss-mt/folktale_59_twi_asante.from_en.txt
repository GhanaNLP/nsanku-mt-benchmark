Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA NO SƐ 'KRAMAN, ƆKRAMAN NO, RENTUMI NSA N’ADWOWTWA (AKWAN) NO DA.

Ɛbɛyɛɛ bere bi a, na ɔkraman bi wɔ hɔ a, na n’akraman su nti, wankɔtumi annya ɔbaa bi a ɔfiri n’akuraa ase bɛware no. Enti ɔde n’akwan kɔɔ kuro bi ase sɛ ɔbɛkɔfa ɔbaa bi ma wɔn ho abɔ. Ɔwaree no, na ɔne no san baa n’akuraa ase. Ɔne ne yere tenaa hɔ bere a ɔde n’akwan kɔe, na ɔsan kɔɔ n’akuraa ase no. Ɛbɛyɛɛ bere bi a, ne kunu de nkra kɔmaa no sɛ ɔreba hɔ. N’agya de ne tuo kɔɔ kwaeɛ mu sɛ ɔbɛkum ɔwansane bi de siesiee ne ho maa ɔkraman no baeɛ. Bɛyɛ nnansa akyi no, ɔkraman no kɔɔ hɔ, na bere a ɔkɔeɛ no, ɔhunuu sɛ ɔwansane nam wɔ ogya so wɔ abɔnten. Anadwo no baeɛ no, ɔne ne yere kɔɔ home nsam. Ɔkraman no bɛyɛ sɛ ɔbɛhyɛ abɔnten akɔfa nsuo, bere a ɔhunuu nam no a sradeɛ a ɛretu gu ogya no mu. Ɔkraman no san bae na ɔkɔdae, na ɔde ne ti too pillow no so atɛkyɛ nam no. Mprempren nam no ho huam no adu no; ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛkura mu, nanso ɔantumi. Oh! ɛsoro ɔsɔre kɔɔ baabi a nam no wɔ no, ɔwe no nyinaa, na ɔsan kɔdae. Anɔpa kɔtɔn, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, bere a ne yere rekɔprapra no, ɔhwehwɛɛ nam no, na ɛnyɛ biako mpo na ɛda hɔ. Ne yere kae sɛ, "O, maame! o, maame! obi akye nam no nyinaa. " Bere a Dog no tee eyi, n'ani wui, na ɔde n'atadeɛ no kataa ne ti so, na ɔda hɔ komm. Afei prɛko pɛ na ɔsɔre kɔɔ kwan no so, ɔdwanee kɔɔ n’akuraa ase, na ɔka kyerɛɛ nsɛm no sɛnea ɔkɔdidi ne papa-nua nam, na n’ani awu fɛre, na ɛno nti, ɔrekɔhwehwɛ obi a ɔwɔ aduru a wɔde siw korɔnbɔ ano sɛ ɔbɛma no bi. Nnipa no kaa sɛ, "Adanko, Hare no, wɔ aduro a ɛyɛ den paa. " Ɔkraman no tuu kwan, na ɔde mmirika bɛduruu Hare no akuraa ase. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Father Hare, me dwane kɔ wo nkyɛn mɛsrɛ mmoa; me yɛ korɔmfoɔ, korɔmfoɔ, korɔmfoɔ, na wɔka sɛ wo wɔ aduro a ɛyɛ ayaresa ma saa nti me srɛ wo, fa me bi ma me, na nea ɛbɛyɛ biara me bɛtua. " Hare no buae sɛ, "Ɛwom sɛ me wɔ bi nti kɔfa me mprako nam. "

Dog no fii aseɛ kɔɔ akuraa bi ase na ɔnyaa akyi faako baako wɔ sika mfutuma mu duru (3s. 6d.) na ɔde baeɛ. Hare no noa no, na ɔtwaa no mu, na ɔtow no guu nnua nketenkete so, na ɔde ɛmo, nkyene, ne ayɛyɛde petepete nam no ho, na ɔde too ogya no so. Bere a ogya no kaa no no, bere bi akyi no, ɔtee sɛɛ, hyee, e e e! Ɔkraman no hwɛɛ no ne n’ani kɔɔ so. Seesei Hare no kae sɛ, "Dog, wone wo ho nkɔda dan bi mu, na me nso me bɛkɔda foforo mu, na ɔkyena ntɛm no, me bɛyi saa nam yi afiri ogya no so de ayɛ aduro ama wo. " Ɔkraman no kɔɔ bɛda. Hare no nso kɔɔ bɛda. Mprempren ɔkraman no da na ɔda (nanso ɔntumi nna), na nam no hua no baa ne hwene mu, nanso ɔnkasae. Mprempren ɔsan teɛ no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Papa Hare. " Hare no buae sɛ, "Yiw! " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mmoawa bi wɔ wo akuraa ase, enti mekɔfa nam no, na meka ne ho. " Hare no kaa sɛ, "Kɔfa no, me deɛ, nea mepɛ ne sɛ mebɛto me nsa so wɔ ɔkyena sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mɛyɛ no aduro ama wo. " Enti ɔkraman no kɔfae. Mprempren ɔkraman no da hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Papa Hare." Ɔbuae sɛ, "Yiw!" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mice no bɛwe no nti, mebɛto no me ntama ase na meakɔda. " Hare no kaa sɛ, "Nea mepɛ ne sɛ, ɔkyena sɛ nneɛma mu da hɔ a, mɛyɛ no aduro ama wo. " Dog no sii fam bio, bɔ mmɔden biara a, na ontumi nni ne ho so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Father Hare, me mpɔtorɔ yi aba me kotonom ase, enti mɛfa nam no mɛto me ti so, na ɛhɔ na mebɛgyina akosi sɛ nneɛma bɛda adi. " Hare no buaa no sɛ, "Ɛwom, me da me ka sɛ nea ɛfa me ho nyinaa ne sɛ menya (nam no) ɔkyena de ayɛ wo aduro. " Ɔkraman no faa nam no nam kɔtoo ne ti so, na ɔgyinaa hɔ. Afei gravy ananmusini no kakra bi hwie guu n’anim, na ɔpɛɛ no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, Papa Hare, ne nam yi deɛ, akokora no asɔre me so, na aba me ti so, enti mebɛkura (nam) no m’anom. " Hare no buae, "Oho o! Meka ɛnkyɛree, bere a nneɛma bɛda adi no, mede no bɛyɛ aduro ama wo." Mprempren na ɛyɛ ntɛm wɔ n’anomu, na ɔrekyinkyin dan no mu, ɔde ne dua bɔɔ nnyigyei, fre! fre! fre! Ɔkaa sɛ, "Papa Hare, ma me ka wo ho ka wɔ saa nam yi ho. " Hare no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na wopɛ sɛ woka ho ka? " Ɔkraman no buae sɛ, "Me kyerɛ sɛ, sen sɛ saa mmoawa yi bɛdi no, ma me tua wo ka. " Hare no kae sɛ, "Oh da! ma ɔkyena adekye no ba. " Oh! nanso seesei ɔkraman no maa nam no kɔɔ ne menemu. Afei ɔhwee ase na ɔdae. Anɔpa kyeɛ no, ntɛm pa ara, Hare no kaa sɛ, "Ɔkraman, fa saa nam no ba. " Ɔkraman no buae sɛ, "Ho, Agya Hare, adɛn nti na wante nea meka kyerɛɛ wo nnɛra sɛ menka wo ka (wɔ nam no ho)? " Hare no buae sɛ, "So woadi no? " Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Yiw! Me dii no, efisɛ me tɔɔ no. " Hare no kaa sɛ, "Wo, Ɔkraman, korɔm a wɔfrɛ no 'korɔm' no renka wo ho da. "

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
