Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ɛBAA NO NYAREWA PII BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU

MPREMPREN, na Kwaku Ananse te Nyankonpon, ɔsoro-nyame no, na ɔkɔɔ hɔ na ɔkae sɛ, "Panyin, fa wo nwan a wɔfrɛ no Kra Kwame (i. e. nea wode kura bɔ afɔre ma wo kra wɔ Memeneda) no kɔ na mfa mma me nkum na menni no, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mɛkɔ akɔfa wo ababaawa fɛfɛɛfɛ (wɔ exchange). " Ɔsoro-nyame no maa no nwan no, na Ananse de ne ho kɔɔ n’akuraa ase na ɔkunkum nwan no na ɔdii no. Afei Spider no kɔɔ akuraa bi ase—wɔ saa akuraa no ase na ɔbarima baako mpo nni hɔ; wɔn nyinaa yɛ mmaa. Ananse waree wɔn nyinaa na ɔne wɔn tenatenae wɔ hɔ. Da bi, ɔbɔfo bi baa hɔ. Bere a ɔbɔmmɔfo no kɔe no, ɔkɔɔ so ka kyerɛɛ ɔsoro nyame no sɛ, "Ananse deɛ, wo nwanan no a ɔgyee no no, wakum no ama mmea bi adi na (afei) waware wɔn. " Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, "Ɛno yɛ nokware? " Ɔbɔmmɔfo no kae sɛ, "nana, ɛyɛ nokware. " Ɔsoro nyame no somaa nkrakyerɛfo kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ wɔnkɔ saa akuraa no na wɔmfa mmea a wɔwɔ hɔ no nyinaa mmra. Nkrasɛmfoɔ no kɔɔ so hyiaa mmaa no, na wɔde wɔn nyinaa kɔɔ ɔsoro-nyame no gye baako pɛ a ɔyareɛ. Ananse kaa sɛ, "Wo a waka no, dɛn na metumi ayɛ mo, wontumi nyɛ hwee mma me? " Ɔbaa a ɔyareɛ no kae sɛ, "Kɔfa abɛn me nkyɛn. " Ananse kɔɔ ne abɛn-kɔɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wohohoro me na fa nsuo a wode dii dwuma no fa gu kurukuruwa yi mu. " Ananse hohoroo ne nipadua na ɔde nsuo a ɔde dii dwuma no guu kurukuruwa no mu. Ɔbaa yi yɛɛ fɛ paa, na ɔbaa biara nni hɔ a ɔte sɛ no wɔ abusuakuo no mu. Ananse ware nea na ɛyɛ ne dea dedaw. Mprempren ɔbɔfoɔ no san bae, na ɔhunuu ɔbaa yi. Ɔkɔɔ so bɔɔ amanneɛ kyerɛɛ ɔsoro Nyankopɔn sɛ, "Ananse ayɛ wo ayɛwo, ɔde mmaa a wɔn ho nyɛ fɛ no baa wo nkyɛn na wakora nea ɔyɛ fɛ no. " Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn no somaa nkrakyerɛfoɔ maa wɔkɔɔ hɔ baabi a na Akɔre no wɔ sɛ wɔmfa saa ɔbaa no mmra no nkyɛn. Wɔde saa nkra yi kɔmaa Ananse. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔrenpɛ me nso anaa? " Amanneɛbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Sky-god no ka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ yɛde ɔbaa no kɔ ne nkyɛn. " Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛno ne no a ɔte hɔ no, fa no kɔ. " Mprempren, Ananse kɔgyee abɛn a yareɛ a ɔde afiri ɔbaa no mu nyinaa guu mu no, na ɔde aboa nhoma bi so, na ɔde aboa abɛn foforɔ bi nso so, na ɔde maa ne ba Ntikuma. na Ananse bɔɔ sankuo a wayɛ no so na ɔtoo nnwom.

"Y'odende dende den, ɛyɛ odende den.

Aso Ya e!

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

W'aniwa berɛ kwa!

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

Wowu wɔ bandy akode!

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

Ɛno na ɛyɛ Aso Ya?

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

Wo ho adwo wo!

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

W'anom yɛ akisikuru wɔ w'anim!

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

W'anan yɛ akuturuku te sɛ nkoa deɛ!

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

W'abusuapanyin ne anantwi pɛ.

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den na ɔdende sɛ ɔyɛ den.

Ntikuma (bɔɔ nnwom na ɔtoo):

Abaayewa fɛfɛɛfɛ, Abaayewa fɛfɛɛfɛ!

Na Afudotwedotwe (Belly-like-to-burst) ne Nyiwankonfwea (Spindle-shanks) bɔɔ asaw. Anene no de ntɛm tuu mmirika na ɔka kyerɛɛ Sorosomanyankopɔn no sɛ, “Ananse wɔ asaw bi a ɛfata wo nanso ɛnyɛ ma Spider. ” Ntɛm ara na Sorosomanyankopɔn no somaa nkrakyerɛfoɔ hɔ kɔɔ Ananse sɛ wɔnkɔfa ne asaw no mma. Ananse kae sɛ, “Me asaw yi, yɛdi agorɔ no harem no mu nko ara, na sɛ ɔsoro-nyame no pene a, ɛnneɛ mebɛka no abɛ. ” Wɔkɔɔ bɛka kyerɛɛ ɔsoro-nyame no. Ɔkae sɛ, “Ɛno nnyɛ hwee, mfa mma. ” Ananse de sankuo kɔɔ harem no mu, na ɔsoro-nyame no bae bɛsaw, na ne yerenom nyinaa saw. Mprempren, na ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ ne nea na ɔyare. Bere a ɔhunuu sɛ Ananse de aboa bi abɔ ntɛkyɛ a na ne nyarewa nyinaa wɔ mu no, ɛno nti, ɔkae sɛ ɔrensa. Na afei ɔsoro-nyame no hyɛɛ no, na ɔbae; na bere a ɔrebɛsa, Ananse maa ntɛkyɛ no so de bɔɔ ɔbaa no, na nyarewa no peteɛ (nnyigyei a ɛte sɛ), tese! Saa ara na akisikuru, yafunu-yare, ti-yare, akwammɔne, guinea-worm, akwansere, yaws, fits, asikreyare, adammɔne baa abusua no mu. Ɛbɛyɛɛ bere bi a na yareɛ biara nni nnipa mu, nanso Ɔsoro-nyame na ɔde yareɛ baa abusua no mu.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
