Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐ OBI SƐE ADEƐ A ƐYƐ WO DEA WƆ AKWANHYIA MU A, MFA WO NSA NNYE AKATUA.

Wɔkaa sɛ da bi a na ɛbaa no, na mmananom bi te hɔ a ne maame awu agyaw wɔn. Wɔreyɛ afahyɛ bi, na mmananom panyin no de nhwiren a ɛsom bo ne sika kɔkɔɔ nneɛma bi sɛn wɔ mmananom ketewa no, Ata Kuma, kɔn mu. Nna ketewa no ne n’ayɔnkofoɔ kae sɛ wɔrekɔ asubɔnten no so kɔhohoro. Bere a wɔreguare no, nhama a wɔde sika kɔkɔɔ nneɛma ahyɛ no wɔ Ata Kuma kɔn mu no bɔɔ, na agude no hwee nsu no mu. Sɛnea ɔpɛ sɛ ɔhwehwɛ no, wantumi anhu no. Ɔsan baa fie bɛbɔɔ ne nuabea amanneɛ sɛ, "Sika kɔkɔɔ nneɛma ayera. " Ata Panin (twin) kae sɛ, "Minnye nni sɛ wɔayera, na sɛ wɔayera mpo a, biribi wɔ hɔ a mebɛka afi wo hɔ. " Ata Kuma (twin) kae sɛ, "Dɛn na wobɛka afi me hɔ? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Mebɛka afi wo hɔ ɔgya pɛn, Aserewa nketewa awo dan, nsu a gur. gles ponponponponsa, nhaban bi a ɛyɛ baako pɛ wɔ dua a wotwitwa no so, ne Asanompona Ɔhemmaa no dua. Ata Kuma kae sɛ, "Meateɛ. " Ata Kuma tuu kwan sɛ ɔbɛhwehwɛ saa nneɛma yi, na ɔbaa beae bi a na dua koro bi gyina hɔ. Bere a dua no huu no no, ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛha na ɛwɔ Ata Kuma, a nkurɔfoɔ ka ne ho asɛm no, ɔda adi wɔ kuro no akyi te sɛ osu a ɛtɔ foforɔ. " (Ata Kuma) kae sɛ, "Nea enti a maba ha ne sɛ papa ba yɛ Ata 'Panin' baako, na maame ba nso yɛ Ata 'Panin' baako, na ɔkyekyeree nhwiren a ɛsono emu biara na ɔde guu me kɔn mu, na ɛbɔe na ɛyerae. Kojo Nsa.

Agya asono, ɛsɛ sɛ wotwe wo ho, ɛsɛ sɛ wofi hɔ.

Kojo Nsa a ɔkae sɛ.

Na nkorabata baako a ɛwɔ dua no so kae sɛ, “Na ɛno nti na woresu, ba bɛgye biribi. ” Na Ata Kuma kɔgyee ahaban baako a na ɛwɔ dua bi so, na ɔdaa no ase (dua no). Na ɔkɔɔ so kɔhyiaa Aserewa nnomaa bi. Na Aserewa anomaa no kae sɛ, “Ɛno ne Ata Kuma, a bere a ɔpue wɔ kuro no akyi no, obiara ka sɛ ɔte sɛ osu a ɛtɔ foforo. ” Ata Kuma buaa no sɛ, “Nea enti a maba ha nko ara ne sɛ papa ba yɛ Ata ’Panin’ baako, na maame ba nso yɛ Ata ’Panin’ baako, na ɔkyekyeree nhwiren a ɛsono emu biara na ɔde hyɛɛ me ho, na ɛbɔɔ mu yerae. ” (Na ɛha Ata Kuma bɔɔ mu) kɔɔ nnwom mu)

Ɔse sɛ ɔregye biribi afiri me hɔ.

Ɔkyerɛ Ɔhemmaa Ɛna a ɔwɔ asono no dua kosi ntini no ase.

Na ɛsɛ sɛ me yɛ, agya, na meanya bi.

Ɔse ɔba ketekete bi a ɔyɛ Akwasi Toboro Kojo ba.

Kojo Nsa.

Agya asono, ɛsɛ sɛ wotwe wo ho, ɛsɛ sɛ wofi hɔ.

Kojo Nsa a ɔkae sɛ.

Aserewa anomaa no kae sɛ, “Ɛno nti na wosuro, bra na mgye biribi. ” Na Ata Kuma kɔgyee Aserewa anomaa no adaka no, na ɔdaa no ase. Na ɔde ne ho kɔe, na ɔhyiaa nsuo a ɛyɛ nsuo nsuo nsuo nsuo. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ata Kuma na ɛno a, bere a ɔda adi wɔ akuraa no kwan no awiei no, nkurɔfoɔ ma no akwaaba te sɛ osu a ɛtɔ foforɔ. ” Ata Kuma ka kyerɛɛ nsɛm no a ɔka kyerɛɛ afoforo no dedaw. Na Gurgling-water no maa no bi, na ɔde kɔɔ so. Afei ɔne Leopard no hyiaa. Bere a Leopard no huu no no, ɔkae sɛ, “Ata Kuma na ɔwɔ hɔ; bere a ɔreba wɔ akuraa no kwan no awiei no, ɔte sɛ osu a ɛtɔ a ɛma ahoɔden. ” Ata Kuma ka kyerɛɛ no n’asɛmpatrɛw. Ɔkae sɛ, “Mba mfa biribi, na ɔde ne ntaban bi mae. ” Ɛnna Ata Kuma kae sɛ, “Papa Leopard, ɛsɛ sɛ wɔyɛ dɛn na menya asono Ɔhemmaa no dua? ” Ɔkae sɛ, “Ah, bere a woretu kwan no, mmoa no nyinaa ada, na asono Ɔhemmaa no da mfinimfini, na woretu kwan no, fa wo nan so si wɔn so denneennen, na bere a wodu asono Ɔhemmaa no nkyɛn no, twa n’asɛn, nanso sɛ wofa wo nan so si wɔn so yiye a, wobɛ- Ɔbɛku wo no." Ata Kuma kae sɛ, "Meda mo ase." Na sɛnea ɔkɔɔ so no, ɔhunuu saa mmoa yi, ɔbɔɔ wɔn so den paa, na ɔduruu Ɔhemmaa no. Ɔtwaa ne dua no, na ɔdanee n'akyi. Wɔ kwan a ɔtɔɔ ahwehwɛ-ahwehwɛ, na ɔde kɔe no. Na ɔduruu fie. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panin' nso, ɛhɔ na woaka sɛ wobɛfa nneɛma no afiri me nkyɛn. " Ata 'Panin' kaa sɛ, "Me nsa aka no. " Mprempren, da bi a na wɔte hɔ bom, Ata Kuma faa ahwehwɛ no mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "ahwehwɛ yi, sɛ obi hwɛ mu a, obi hu maame a ɔwɔ Ahonhom kuro no mu. " Ata 'Panin' kaa sɛ, "Ɛte saa a, fa no ba, na ma mehwɛ. " Ɔde no maa no. Bere a ɔrebɛhwɛ mu no, ɛfiri ne nsam bɔɔ mu. Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, "Wei deɛ, nea wunyaa me hɔ no, mebɛsrɛ wo. " Ɛnna Ata 'Panin kɔgyee nneɛma no a Ata Kuma de baa no. Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, "Me mpɛ (yi), nea mepɛ ne sɛ mokɔfa foforo ba me. " Ata 'Panin de ne ho kɔe, na ɔkɔhyiaa dua bi a ɛwɔ ahaban baako pɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛha na Ata 'Panin, a ɔda adi wɔ akuraa no kwan ano awiei no, ne nea osu tɔ no yɛ pɛ. " Ata 'Panin buae sɛ, "Me da me ho adi wo maame! ne wo papa! Me deɛ, ma me wo ahaban koro no na ma me kwan. " Nnua no a ɛwɔ ahaban koro no kaa sɛ, "Fa kɔ, nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi) no wɔ hɔ. " Ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔne Aserewa nnomaa bi hyiaa. Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛno ne Ata 'Panin, bere a ɔpue wɔ akuraa no kwan no awiei no, ne ho yɛ dɛ te sɛ osuo. " Ata Panin kaa sɛ, "Bere a me 'appear' wo maame ne wo papa no, me deɛ me pɛ ne sɛ mepɛ wo ntamadan biako na menkɔ. " Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, "Pass on, nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bɛdi) no wɔ hɔ. " Ɛnna ɔhunuu nsuo a ɛgu. Nsuo a ɛgu-gu kae sɛ, "Ata 'Panin' na ɛwɔ ha; bere a ɔpue wɔ kuro no awiei no, ɔte sɛ osu a ɛtɔ a ɛyɛ anigye. " Ata 'Panin' kae sɛ, "bere a me 'appear' wo maame no, bere a me 'appear' wo papa no, me deɛ, fa nsuo no bi ma me mfa mma kwan. " Nsuo a ɛgu-gu no kae sɛ, "Fa kɔ anim, nea onim sɛ ɔne wo bɛdi no wɔ hɔ. " Afei ɔhyiaa ɔsebɔ. Leopard no kae sɛ, "Ɛno ne Ata 'Panin, a ɔda adi wɔ abontene no awiei no yɛ fɛ te sɛ osu a ɛtɔ. " Ata 'Panin buae sɛ, "Me 'appear' wo maame! ne wo papa! Me deɛ, fa me nsa bi na fa wo nsa bi mma me kwan. Leopard no kae sɛ, "Nea ɔnim wo ho no wɔ hɔ. " Ɔtuu kwan, na ɔne asasefoɔ dɔm bi hyiaa, na Elefɛ Queen Mother no da mfinimfini. Ɛnna ɔkɔɔ so twaa ne dua. Asasefoɔ dɔm no nyinaa kuraa no mu, na wɔbɔɔ no so maa no wuiɛ. Ata Kuma hwehwɛɛ ne nuabea wɔ kwan no so. Ɔmmra paa. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Sɛ wo ne wo mogya biara te hɔ a, na sɛ wɔn mu biako sɛe wo deɛ biara a, mfa sika mma, efisɛ Ata 'Panin' yɛɛ saa, na ɔkɔwuiɛ. "

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
