Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ABOFRA-KWASIGINAMOA

Ɛbɛyɛɛ bere bi a na abofra bi a wɔfrɛ no Kwasi Gyinamoa ne nuanom mmea baawɔtwe na ɔwɔ, na wɔn mu biara nni hokafo, enti wɔkɔɔ sɛ wɔrekɔ baabi akɔhwehwɛ awarefo. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, “Me ne wo na yɛbɛkɔ. ” Wɔn buae sɛ, “Yɛn ne wo renkɔ. ” Wɔn mu panin no kae sɛ, “Ɔne yɛn nkɔ, na yɛadu kwan no so, na abusuapɛn bi apira yɛn, ɔbɛtumi atwe ama yɛn. ” Wɔtuu kwan, na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kɔɔ hɔ. Wɔbaa asubɔnten bi ase, na emu bi sen dɔte fitaa; emu bi guu dɔte kɔkɔɔ; na emu bi sen fitaa nkyene. Wɔteɛɛm sɛ, “Eh, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ sɛ yɛbɛtwa asubɔnten yi? ” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa ne nantwi-dɔte bɔɔ nsuo no mu yɛɛ mmienu na wɔnam mu kɔɔ so. Wɔbaa kuro bi ase, na ɛyɛ kuro kɛseɛ paa. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaeɛ sɛ, “Kuro no dinn (i. e. silent save for the chirping of cicada), nipa nni mu anaa? ” Mprempren abɛɛ bi kasaeɛ sɛ, “Obi wɔ fie hɔ, kɔ so. ” Wɔkɔɔ so, na ɔbaa panyin bi a ne ti da ne nan so, na ɔhwehwɛ mu ntontom. Bere a ɔhunuu Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, ɔmaa ne ti so na ɔsesaa no; ɔkae sɛ, “Me mmanana, adɛn nti na obiara renba ha? Mo aba ha? ” Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, “Me nuabeanom yɛ nwɔtwe, na wɔnni mmarima a wɔbɛware wɔn, na wɔkae sɛ wɔabɛhwehwɛ mmarima a wɔnware wɔn. ” Ɔbaa-nananom kae sɛ, “Oh, ɛno ne nyinaa yɛ kwan. na me nso wɔ mma nwɔtwe. Agya baa de ne mmanana kɔe sɛ ɔnkɔfrɛ ne mma. Wɔn bae no, eyi kae sɛ, “Me kunu ne no,” ɛno kae sɛ, “Me kunu ne no. ” Bere a wɔn nyinaa wiee wɔn ho bɔɔ mu no, na abayewa no kɔgyee abosono bi a wɔfrɛ no _dokum_, na ɔde kyekyeree ne mma kɔn mu. Ɛnna wɔn mu biara kɔɔ ne ne yere dae. Na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔɔ home kɔgya ne ɔbaa panyin no. Anadwo bae no, ɔbaa panyin no sii n’ani fitaa kɔkɔɔ kɛse sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔkum mmeawa no de wɔn. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, “E! e! e!” Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, “Dɛn na ɛyɛ? ” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, “Bere a me wɔ me ara akuraa ase, na bere a me kɔhome nanso metumi nna, ɛnna na maame twitwa yam bi gu ogya mu, na ɔde calabash a ɛwɔ ntokuro no kɔ na ɔde nsuo bɔ me. ” Ɔbaa panyin nana no tu kwan kɔ asubɔnten no so, na abofra no kɔgyaee akɔmfuo a wɔfrɛ no _dok no. um_ fii ɔbaa panyin no mma kɔn mu, faa wɔn de too ne nuabeanom so. Ɔbaa panyin no san fii nsuo no mu bae, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Me bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ mebɛbɔ nsuo (wɔ strainer no mu), nanso menntumi annyɛ no. ” Abofra Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, “Kɔfa atɛkyɛ bi, na fa nhaban na fa gu mu, na fa bɔ nsuo no. ” Ɔbaa panyin no kɔyɛɛ saa, na ɔbɔɔ nsuo no mu, na ne nyinaa yɛɛ yiye. Ɔno na ɔde bae, na ɔde yam no fii ogya no so, na ɔde maa Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa sɛ ɔnni, na ɔnom nsu no nso. Afei ɔtoo ne ho ase na ɔdae. Ɔbaa-nananom sɔreɛ, ɔkae sɛ, “Me na me yɛ me. ” Ɔhyɛɛ n’asom kɔsii sɛ ɛhyerɛn kɔkɔɔ, na ɔde wɔn kɔeɛ kɔhyɛɛ dan no mu; ɔde ne nsa kyinkyin mmofra no kɔn, na nea na _dokum_ nsamu nni ne kɔn no, ɔde n’asom hyeɛ no. Saa kwan yi so na ɔkunkum mmofra baawɔtwe no. Na onnim sɛ ɛyɛ ne mma ankasa a ɔkunkum wɔn. Bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ntɛm ara na ɔgyaw-nananom kɔɔ afuo no mu. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔkaa ne nuabeanom na ɔmaa wɔdwanee. Ɔgyaw-nananom bɛsan bae firi afuo no mu. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, “Ɔgyaw-nananom e! Ɔgyaw-nananom e! sɛ wo-nnyɛ nkwasea a, anka wobekum wo ankasa wo mma? Me deɛ, me ne me nuanom rekɔ. Agya-nananom kae sɛ, “Ɛnyɛ hwee, nea onim sɛnea wɔbɛdi wo ho dwuma no wɔ akyiri. ” Ɔkɔɔ, na agya-nananom nso danee ne ho bɛyɛɛ abaayewa fɛfɛɛfɛ, na ɔkuraa akutu-sankɔ, kɔɔ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa akuraa ase. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Nipa a ɔbɛtumi atwi akwan-kwan yi, mebɛware no. ” Nnipa a na wɔwɔ akuraa no ase bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ wɔbɛbɔ no, nanso wɔantumi anbɔ no. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa bɔɔ no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Meaware mo. " Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, me nware mo. " Enti ne wofa waree no. Ɔkɔɔ so dae ne no, nanso ɔtwee n’ani mmienu no, na ɔne wɔn kɔe. Ɛnna ababaawa no kae sɛ, "Abofra Kwasi-Gyinamoa hɔ! Meapae wo papa nua no n’ani mmienu no na me ne wɔn rekɔ. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa mmuaeɛ ne sɛ, "Mo fa kɔ na mfi hɔ, efisɛ nea onim wo ho no wɔ akyiri. " Anɔpahema, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, wɔkae sɛ, "Abofra, fa kɔsan siesie na fa wo papa nua no ani no ba. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me nkɔ. " Wɔhyɛɛ no den, na awieeɛ no ɔde ne ho kɔe. Mprempren, abofra Kwasi-Gyinamoa nimii sɛ ɔpanyin-nananom ba baa no wɔ yafunu, enti ɔno nso nyaa calabash na ɔbɔɔ ne yafunu so, na ɔkyekyeree ne ntama no so, na ɔde ne ho kɔɔ ɔpanyin-nananom akuraa ase. Bere a ɔduruu kuro no awiei no, ɔsuu. Ɔpanyin-nananom kae sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ asɛm no? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me ne me kunu reba no, bere a ɔduruu bepɔw no atifi no, ɔhwee ase na ɛno maa n’ani mmienu no puee ase. " Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, "Ɛno nyinaa teɛ, ɛha na ɛyɛ saa nkonyaayi nkonyaayi no ani benkum, wofa, na ɛha nso ne n’ani nifa. Bere a wokɔ (baabi a w'aniwa nnafoɔ gyina hɔ) no, ɛsɛ sɛ meka sɛ, 'M'aniwa tu me ho, m'aniwa mu da hɔ.' Ansa na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa de ne nsa bɛka wɔn no, ɔkae sɛ, "Wo a wote hɔ no, nananom, eyinom yɛ m'onua aniwa a woyii no, na me ne wɔn rekɔ. " Nananom kaa sɛ, "Ɛno mfa ho, nea onim sɛnea ɔne wo bɛdi no wɔ akyiri. " Abofra Kwasi -Gyinamoa baa fie, na ɔde n’aniwa kɔhyɛɛ n’onua no aniwa mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "My eyes jump about, my eyes are clear," na ne onua no aniwa buee pefee. Anɔpa a edi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, mmofra no nyinaa kɔhohoroo wɔ asutene no mu. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, "Me nsuo nni me ho. " Wɔkae sɛ, "Ebia wowɔ awotwaa embryo? " Bere a wɔkae saa no, ne nufu yɛɛ no dɛɛdɛɛ, na ɔhohoroo ne ho. Ɔtuu nsuo no mu no, ɔgyaw-nananom kyeree no, twim! Ɔkae sɛ, "Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, me ne wo anya," na ɔne no kɔɔ n’akuraa ase. Ɛnna agya-nananom kɔgyee kɛntɛn bɛkataa no so, na ɔgyee nnuadewa, na ɔde too ogya no so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me fi kɔ onion abusuakuo no so, me fi kɔ spice abusuakuo no so, na me bɛba abɛkum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa. " Ɔtuu kwan, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa boaboaa abotan ano hyɛɛ aseɛ bɔɔ no pupru! pupru! Ɔbaa-nananom ba baawa kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ wode ka? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Tiger-nuts. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Fa me bi ka. " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Yi me ano. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wobɛkɔ. " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Dabi me nkɔ. " Ɔyi no ano na ɔpuee, ɔbaa nananom no ba baawa no bɔ nut. Abofra Kwasi-Gyinomaa faa nnuadewa no bɔɔ Old-Woman-Grandmother nananom ba ba no na okum no. Afei ɔpaa ne nananom ba ba no honam ani na ɔde hyɛɛ ne ho. Ɔpanyin-nananom san bae, na ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na mo nyinaa yɛ fĩ saa? " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Makum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa; mede no ato ogya no so. " Saa asɛm yi yɛɛ dɛ ma Ɔpanyin-nananom, na ɔsaw na ɔmaa ne nne so bɔɔ dwom sɛ: "Ba, woakum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, da bi wobɛyɛ obi. "

Ɔwiee aduane no no, wɔtenaa n’akyi, na wɔdii. Ɔbaa-nananom petee nsuo no wɔ nnyigyei kɛseɛ, fweo! fweo! Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, "Worepetepete nkwan a wode wo nana ba ti ayɛ. " Ɔbaa-nananom kae sɛ, "Ɛdɛn na wo ka? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Na me reka sɛ nkyene biara nni hɔ wɔ nkwan-kwan yi mu. " Ɔbaa-nananom kae sɛ, "kɔ nso bi. " Enti ɔkɔɔ kɔgyee bi. Wɔwiee aduane no, na ɔgyawammaa no kae sɛ, "Mprempren meakum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nti, ba ha, me mmanana. " Ɔbaa no, ɔde sika kɔkɔɔ nneɛma siesiee no fɛfɛɛfɛ. Mprempren, ɔgyawammaa no hwɛɛ n'anim bere bi. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ah! Abofra yi anim ma mekae nea ɔte sɛ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa. Abofra no kae sɛ, "Nana, adɛn nti na bere a wunim sɛ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa bae bɛbɔɔ me nua mmiɛnsa-nwɔtwe no, woka asɛm a ɛte sɛɛ, woyɛ me awerɛhow. " Ɔbaa-nananom kae sɛ, "to wo ano. " Seesei abofra no de ne ho tɔɔ fam wɔ fie hɔ. Nana no de no kɔe. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Momma me nkɔ nkɔtow no gu midden-heap no so. " Enti abofra Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa no kɔe. Bere a oduu midden-heap no so no, ɔyii sika kɔkɔɔ agude a na ɔbaa no de asiesie no nyinaa na ɔkyekyeree no. Ɛnna ɔkae sɛ, "Hɔ hɔ, mmea-nananom, ho hɔ, mmea-nananom, me ne no, Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, mprempren me ne mpren a ɛtɔ so mmienu no, meaba abɛkum wo nana, na akyiri yi nso meafa wo sika nneɛma. " Mmea-nananom no buae sɛ, "Meaka sɛ wo anim te sɛ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa; mprempren baabiara a wopɛ kɔ no, me ne wo kɔ. " Ɛnna wɔn baanu nyinaa tuu mmirika, kiri! kiri! kiri! Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa duu n'akuraa ase, na ɔde sika kɔkɔɔ nneɛma no maa n'abusuafoɔ, na ɔkɔɔ kwan no so, na ɔde ne ho siee ne nkyɛn, na ɔgyaw-nananom nso baa hɔ. Ɛno nti, sɛ wo nam kwan bi so a, wobɛte nnyigyei a ɛyɛ nnyansaa, "pe. " Ɛno ne ɔgyaw-nananom retaa Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
