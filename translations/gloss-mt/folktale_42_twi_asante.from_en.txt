Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA NO, ƆSEBƆ NO NE ƆPƐ NO BƐYƐƐ NNAMFO.

OKUROTWIAMANSA (Ɔman no mu surofoɔ) na ɔte hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ obi a ɔmpɛ no (na ɔnsuro sɛ ɔbɛka no saa anim). Wɔ mmoa no nyinaa mu no, obiara antumi anyɛ saa. Mprempren, na Adowa Antelope ketewa no rete hɔ; ɔkae sɛ, "Father Leopard, dɛn na wose me ne wo bɛyɛ atamfo? " Osebo, Leopard no, kae sɛ, "Ɛno ara na mepɛ. " Enti wɔbɛyɛɛ atamfo. Leopard no bɔɔ nsafua-bɔɔl. Antelope no kɔɔ, nom nsafua-bɔɔl no, ma ɔbɛyɛɛ nsa, ɔrentumi nnyina, na ɔda nsafua no ase. Wɔ anwummere berɛ a ɛyɛ dwoo no, Leopard no kae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwɛ n’agyapadeɛ. Bere a ɔkɔe no, na Adowa no nsa aka n’aburo na ɔda hɔ. Leopard no kae sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ, wo na wose wone me bɛyɛ atamfo a wɔda adi pefee; adɛn nti na Esono mpo a ɔyɛ asono no antumi anyɛ me atamfo a wɔda adi pefee, akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ wode wo ho bɛto asɛm mu. " Leopard no tuu mmirika na ɔtwaa nnuadewa nhaban, na ɔyɛɛ nnuadewa nhaban kɛntɛn, na ɔde antelope no guu mu; na ɔde no kɔɔ ne ti so. Bere a ɔreba na wakɔ kwan no fã no, Antelope no yɛɛ dwoodwoo. Ɔtee nka sɛ Leopard no rekura no mu. Antelope no kɔɔ soro, na liana bi da hɔ. Adowa antelope no kyeree no. Seesei ɔkasa ne ne nneɛma a ɔgyaee no (i. e. mehyɛɛ no nkyɛn sɛ mebɛka biribi), antelope no nsuo bi wɔ nsõ-kɛntɛn no mu. Na Leopard nim hwee wɔ nea ɛrekɔ so no ho; na ɔde no kɔɔ fie (sɛnea osusuwii no). Ɔkae kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Saa nkwaseasɛm panyin a ɔyɛ ɔwansane no, ɔkae sɛ ɔne me nyɛ atɛmfoɔ a yɛgye tom ma waba, fa palm-nuts no to ogya no so. " Ɔwansane maame no kae kyerɛɛ ne ba sɛ, "Soup no ayɛ krado, enti fa no ba sɛ mɛkum no. " Bere a ɔwansane no hwɛɛ kɛntɛn no mu no, na ɛmu nni hwee. Na waka kɔ, na nea aka no nyinaa yɛ ne droppings. Leopard no kaa sɛ, "Maame, ɔakɔ, na aka ne nsuo no, momma yɛnfa nkɔhyɛ soup no mu. " Saa ara na wɔde guu aduane no nyinaa mu, na wɔwiee no, na wɔtenaa ase. Ɔbaa panyin ketewa no de ne nsateaa kɔhyɛɛ soup no mu na ɔpae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, Kwadwo. (Kwadwo awoda no yɛ Dwoada), na eyi yɛ ne nsuo nko ara, dɛn na ne honam ani nkae; na ɛyɛ dɛ sen tiger-nuts, enti baabiara a ɔbɛda no, bɔ mmɔden na ɔde no aba. " Na ɔgya no tuu kwan kɔɔ n’abɛɛfo dua no ase. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na Adowa nso na ɔsan ayɛ ayɛfo. Leopard no kaa sɛ, "Ogyaa, nea ɛdi kan no sɛ mebɛyɛɛ me ho nkwasea a, seesei mebɛkɔ fie akɔfa adaka abɛsan aba. " Leopard no kɔɔ fie, ɔde n’atadeɛ nyinaa guu hɔ, na ɔde adaka ne nkyerɛwdeɛ no kɔɔ palm-tree no ho. Ɔde Antelope no guu adaka no mu, bɔɔ no mu, gyee no, na ɔde no kɔɔ ne ti so. Berɛ a ɔduruu fie no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Maame, me de no aba; bue dampon no mu sɛ me mfa no nhyɛ mu. " Ɔbaa panyin ketewa no buee mu, na ɔde no hyɛɛ mu. Afei Leopard no kae kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Mefiri hɔ rekɔ sare tenten man mu akɔhwehwɛ onions, ne salt, ne ground ginger, na meba, na yɛmfa Adowa Antelope no nni stew. " Leopard no tuu n'akwantu. Anɔpa kyeɛ no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, na ɔbaa panyin ketewa no rebɔ ne nsa ka nnuadewa, na bere a Antelope de n’ani kɔtoo amena bi mu no, ɔhunuu ɔbaa panyin no. Duker kae sɛ, "Aberewa-nananom, wɔbɛbu wo amane; sɛ wobue wo ano ma me a, anka mebɛbɔ wo. " Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, "Wo, Duker no, wobɛdwane. " Duker no buaa no sɛ, May (anyame nkumaa) Aserampon, ne Ta’ Kese, ne Dwomo, ne Apeya, ne Konkroma, ne Ta’ Kora, ne Krakye Dente, nkum me sɛ mebɛdwane a, woabue wo ano ama me. Sɛ wopɛ a, to apon no nyinaa mu." Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, "Metee." Ɔtoo apon no mu, na obuee dan a na Duyker no wɔ mu no. Duyker no bae, ɔkuraa pestle no, na ɔbɔɔ palm-nuts no. Ɔbaa panyin ketewa no bɛbɔɔ ase sɛ ɔbɛfa biribi afi fam bere a Duyker no faa pestle no bɔɔ ne ti akyi, na owui. Duyker paa ne honam ani, na ɔde kataa ne ho so, na ɔtwaa ne nam de too ogya no so. Ɛnkyɛree koraa na Kwadwo bae. Duyker kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wotwee bere tenten saa? Meabue kwan no na meakunkum no, ɛha mpo ne honam a ɛwɔ ogya so. Leopard no kaa sɛ, "Ayekyere yiye, Apeafo. " Ɔno nso faa nneɛma a ɔde bae no, na ɔde guu nkwan mu. Na wɔawie aduane bere a wɔkɔtenaa ase sɛ wɔbɛdi no, Leopard no faa bone bi. Bere a ɔbɔɔ no, na ɛyɛɛ pu! pu! no. Duyker kekaee wɔ n’anammɔn mu sɛ, "Ɛno na ɛyɛ wo maame dompe a worebubu no. " Ɔkraman kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛno wose? " Duyker buae sɛ, "Me nko ara kae sɛ Duyker yi yɛ onipa a wadi mfe pii; ne dompe yɛ den. " Ɔkraman kae sɛ, "ɛte saa, me mpo ntumi nsɛe wɔn. " Mprempren Ɔkraman no de nkwan a wɔnoa no guu n’ano, na ɛyɛɛ nnyigyei a ɛte sɛ feao! feao! Duyker kae sɛ, "Ɛh, ɛno yɛ soup-stew a wode wo maame ti a worebɔ no. " Leopard kae sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na wo ka? " Duyker buae sɛ, "Me werɛ refrɛ me sɛ, ne nsuo mpo a na yɛdwen sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ no, ne nam deɛ, woahunu sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ? " Leopard kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ koraa, koraa, koraa sɛ ɛnhia sɛ woka saa. " Seesei, na wɔawie. . Duker no bɔɔ fie hɔ ho ban; ɔka kyerɛɛ Leopard no sɛ, "I am going to throw the sweepings on to the midden-heap. " Leopard no kae sɛ, "Go, then, what have I to do with it, am I a woman? " Duker no duu midden-heap no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ho there, Leopard! Ho there, Leopard! Sɛ wonyɛ ɔkwasea a, sɛ wonyɛ aboa a, anka mɛkum wo maame na meayɛ no nkwan-nkwan ama wo? Leopard no kae sɛ, "Mɛhwehwɛ wo (sɛ woda so ara gyina hɔ, sɛ meba wo nkyɛn a)? " Duyker no kae sɛ, "Wobɛtwa me mpo. " Leopard no tuu mmirika, yiridi! yiridi! (ne ne nan nnyigyei), na Duyker no nso hyɛɛ aseɛ tuu mmirika, prada! prada! prada! Duker duu ne fie hɔ, na ɔyerae wɔ ne dan mu. Bere a ɔkyekyeree fie no, na wannya kwan biara a ɔfa so kɔ mu gye kwan baako a ɔasi. Duker no wuraa, ɔtoo n'apon mu, bam! Mprempren, hwɛ sɛnea Leopard no bɛyɛe no, ɔnhunuu kwan biara a ɔfa so kɔɔ mu. Leopard no tuu kwan frɛɛ mmoa no nyinaa, na ɔkae sɛ, "Mbra, na yɛnkɔ nkyere Duyker no mma me, sɛ me ayɛ ɔkwasea a ɔte sɛɛ, sɛ me rentumi nka asɛm no nyinaa nkyerɛ mo kosi sɛ wobɛkyere no mma me. ” Mmoa no nyinaa baa hɔ bɛbaa Duyker fie hɔ. Bere a Duyker tee sɛ mmoa no aba ne fie hɔ sɛ wɔrebɛkyere no no, ɔfaa pepper kɔkɔɔ, nkyene, ne onions, na ɔde no guu hɔ. Mprempren na mmoa no nyinaa gyina hɔhɔhɔ. Eyi hwɛɛ saa no anim. Afei Kusi, ɔkraman no, kae sɛ, “Kwakuo, ɔkraman no wɔ hɔ, na ɔwɔ dua tenten, enti ɔnforo no kan, na yɛatumi de ne dua no akɔ fie hɔ. ” Kwakuo, ɔkraman no, hyɛɛ aseɛ kra! kra! na ɔforoɔ no. Ɔtenaa fie no atifi, nanso ankyɛ na ɔde n’ani kyerɛɛ Duyker no no, Duyker de peppers a ɔhweee no guu n’ano na ɔtow guu wɔ ananse no ani mu. Afei ɔkraman no gyaee na ɔhwee ase. Mmoa no bae na wɔhunuu sɛ biribi wɔ n’ani mu. Baako kaa ne mu bi, na ɔpaa ne nsateaa. Ɔkaa sɛ, “O, nnamfo, monba (na moahwɛ) nea Kwakuo akɔfa aba. ” Mmoa no nyinaa bae. Wɔpae, na wɔpae, na wɔpae. Ɛno nti na Kwakuo, ɔkraman no, ani da mu saa. Mprempren wɔkae sɛ, “Kokotee (Bush-pig), wo deɛ, woda daa na wotutu, nuntun! nuntun! Enti wobɛtumi akɔ fie hɔ.” Bere a Duyker tee no, ɔkɔtwaa n’akofena de n’akofena a ɛyɛ nnam paa sii hɔ. Bush-pig no tutuu na ɔtutuu, na n’ano puei wɔ fie hɔ mu. Afei Duyker de n’akofena no too Bush-pig no ano, na ɔretwa. Bush-pig no kaa sɛ, “Ɛwoi, Bush-pig Asamoa, ɔretwitwa me ano. ” Mmoa no a na wɔgyina n’akyi (na wɔantumi ante nea ɔkae no yie) kae sɛ, “Yɛn yɛn Apeafo, monhyɛ mo ani, na woayi no adi. ” Kokotee, wura-pɔn no, de ne ti san n’akyi, na ɔne n’ano yɛ mogya. Bere a mmoa no huu sɛ Kokotee Asamoa, Bush-pig no, atwa n’ano, wɔfii hɔ, na ɛyɛɛ nnyigyei! Saa kwan yi so na Bush-pig no nyaa n’ano kɛseɛ. Seesei, bere a mmoa no adwane no, Osebo, ɔpɛpɔn no, anhu nea ɔbɛtumi ayɛ. Ɔkae sɛ, “Ɛmfa ho no, me nkɔ baabi foforo, me te ha daa, kosi sɛ me bɛwu, gye sɛ me kum Adowa, Duyker no. ” Adowa tee saa no, ɔde n’ɛmaame no honam ani kyekyeree ne ho kɔforoɔ fie no soro kɔne fam wɔ akyi. Afei ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔtoo ɔsebɔ no. Ɔkae sɛ, “O, Kwadwo, dɛn na ɛma wo ho ayɛ wo te sɛɛ, adɛn nti na woredwiri wo ho wɔ Duyker ho? ” Yɛnkɔ fie na yɛmfa nkyekyere yɛn fie hɔ, na yɛnsan mmra.” Wɔtuu kwan kɔ fie. Duyker no kae kyerɛɛ Leopard no sɛ, “Yi wo nneɛma nyinaa fa ma me, na mefa sie ma wo. ” Leopard no kae sɛ, “Ɛnyɛ adwene bɔne. ” Ɛnna ɔyii ne nneɛma nyinaa de kɔmaa Duyker no. Ansa na Duyker nsa bɛkaa wɔn, na ɔde wɔn kɔtoo hɔ wɔ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ den so, ansa na ɔkɔgyinaa baabi. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Hɔ hɔ, Leopard! ” Ɔbuae sɛ, “aane, nuabarima, aane! ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Sɛ wone no nni ɔkwasea a, sɛ wone no nni aboa a, metumi akum wo maame ayɛ no aduan a wubedi? ” Afei bio mmeɛ mo mmeɛ mo agyapadeɛ nyinaa.” Ɔsebɔ no kae sɛ, “Mɛbɛhyia mo wɔ hɔ (sɛ meba a)? ” Duyker no kae sɛ, “Mɛbɛboro mo mpo. ” Ɛnna ɔsebɔ no tuu ne ho kiri! kiri! kiri! nanso Duyker no nso tuu mmirika. Ɔkɔɔ mu kɔhyɛɛ ne fie na ɔtoo ɔpono no mu, bam! Mprempren Leopard no gyinaa hɔ, ne bo yɛɛ no dɛɛdɛɛ; na ne dua no kɔɔ nkyɛn ne nkyɛn a ɛyɛ nnyigyei te sɛ fre! fre! fre! Na ontumi nnyaa asɛm bi a ɔbɛka. Akyiri yi ɔkae kyerɛɛ Duyker sɛ, "Meka din a wɔabara no, Thursday, ɛsɛ sɛ wobɛba na yɛbɛyɛ nnamfo. " Duyker kae sɛ, "merenba sɛ wopɛ pii, gye sɛ woyɛ nhyehyɛe ma yɛnom anyame no. " Leopard no kae sɛ, "Mepene so, ɛnneɛ merentumi nnyɛ hwee mma wo. " Duyker bae sɛ ɔne Leopard no bɛnom anyame no. Duker kae no sɛ, "Minkyere me da; sɛ wuhunu me a, na sɛ wɔkyere me trap a, gya me kwan. " Leopard kae sɛ, "Mate. " Na Leopard nso kasae sɛ, "Wɔ kwan a wobɛfa so mpo, mebɛfa me nsa na meabɔ so, na woahu sɛ me ne wo nni ntɔkwa biara wɔ yɛn ntam. " Ɛno nti, bere a Leopard biara rebɛn no no, ɔnkyere. Duyker no. Bio nso, sɛ ɔtɔn Duyker a, ɛkɔgye no.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
