Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA (NKRA) "KURONTO" NE "BURU" NKRA NA BAE BƐDII DWUMA

Ɛdii mmere bi a ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ bi wɔ hɔ a wɔfrɛ no Kwaku, na ne yere a wɔfrɛ no Buruwa, na wɔyɛ ahiafoɔ nti, wɔtu kwan sɛ wɔbɛhwehwɛ baabi foforɔ. Kwaku, ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no, bere a ɔkɔ kwaeɛ mu no, ɔkumm eku anoonam, nanso bere a ɔde baa fie no, ɔkae sɛ, "Eyi nyɛ aduane pa. " Enti ɔkɔtɔn no. Bere biara a ɔkumm biribi no, na ɛte saa. Nanso ne yere anhu sika no da, na ɔbisaa no sɛ, "Wonsan mma sika, na wonsan mma nam, na dɛn na ɛyɛ 'nea ɛkyerɛ'? " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Sɛ me kɔ a, na nkurɔfo rɛntɔ nam no, na ɛbɛporɔw, me to gu. " Da bi, okunkum biribi, na ɔde kɔtoo kwan no so, na ne yere no dii n'akyi bɛhwɛɛ no. Ɛnna ɔhunuu sɛ ɔtɔn saa ɔkraman no, ɔgyee sika no, na ɔtɔɔ mmɛw ngo (a wɔfrɛ no dɔnto) kɛntɛn bi na ɔnom. Ɛnna ne yere san baa fie. Ɛnkyɛreeɛ no, Kwaku, ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no nso baeɛ. Bere a oduruu fie hɔ ntɛm ara no, ɔkae sɛ, "Good evening, Buruwa. " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Ya Ekudonto (Yes, Monkey for pot of palm wine). " Ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wobua me saa? " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuae a ɛfata nea ɛwɔ w'adwene mu. " Ɔbɔfo no anka hwee. Anɔpa kyeɛ no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔkɔɔ afuo no mu na ɔsan bae, ɔkaa sɛ, "Anwummere pa. " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Ya Buru. " Buruwa kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wubua me nsafoɔ yi? " Ɔbɔfoɔ no buae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuae a ɛfata nea ɛwɔ w’adwene mu. " Saa ara na ɛbɛyɛɛ amanneɛ wɔ abusuakuo no mu sɛ wɔde nsafoɔ "Ya Kuronto" ne "Ya Buru" di dwuma. Wɔn awofoɔ de ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ Kwaku ne ne yere Buruwa. Wei me asɛm a mekaeɛ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mma me.
