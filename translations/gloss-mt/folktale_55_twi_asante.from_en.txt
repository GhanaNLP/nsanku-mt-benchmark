Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

Na akode-sare bae abusuakuw no mu

Ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bi baeɛ na Ntikuma tuu kwan sɛ ɔrekɔpɛ aduane de bɛma ne papa, Ananse, akɔre. Ɔtuu kwan kɔɔ akuraa bi ase, ɛhɔ na ɔhyiaa ɔbaa-nananom. Ɔbaa panyin no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Me mmanana, dɛn nti na obiara ntumi mma ha, w’aba ha? ” Ntikuma buae sɛ, “Ɛyɛ kɔm no nti, meaba sɛ mebɛhwehwɛ biribi a mebɛkɔ adidi. ” Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, “Kɔ me afuo mu hɔ, nanso yam a ɛbɛka sɛ, ‘Mmu me, mmu me’, mmu no na mmɛba. Na ɛ.” Ntikuma kɔɔ abɔnten. Yam bi kae sɛ, “twitwa me, twitwa me”; afoforo nso ka sɛ, “ka. ” Ɛnna Ntikuma twaa nea wɔkae sɛ, “Nnya me, nnya me. ” Ɔde wɔn baa fie, na ɔpae wɔn. Ɛnna agya-nananom kae sɛ, “Twa ne fã ankasa mfa nni aduane, na fa ne honam gu kuku no mu na fa to ogya so. ” Ntikuma yii ne honam no na ɔde ne honam no guu ogya no so. Bere a wɔasiesie wɔn ho no, ɔyii wɔn fii ogya no so, ɔdanee kuku no mu nneɛma nyinaa, na ne nyinaa danee aduane pa. Ntikuma kae sɛ, “Meawie, nanso ma me nam mfa nsie. ” Agya-nananom kae sɛ, “Kɔ kyerɛ Nkwakuo, Kwakuakuo a wɔwɔ dua no so sɛ ‘Agya-nananom ka sɛ, wɔn nyinaa, mo maame——! ’ ” Kwakuakuo no kae sɛ, “Sɛ ɛnyɛ agya-nananom nti a, anka yɛbɛbɔ mo. ” Agya-nananom no kae sɛ, “Me deɛ, ɛnyɛ me asɛm, na mo m- mma ɔmmɔ no no. Mmoa no twaa aseɛ nnidiso nnidiso sɛ wɔbɛbɔ no. Ɔbaa panyin kae sɛ, "Me Sharp-knife, twa mmoa yi ti mma me. " Ɛnna Sharp-no twaa mmoa no ti. Ntikuma boaboaa mmoa no ti nyinaa ano, na ɔtwaa yam bi nso de ne nyinaa kɔɔ ne nkuraase. Papa huu ne ba Ntikuma sɛ ɔregyina n’anim, na ɔkae sɛ, “Mpaeɛ wɔ wo ba a waba no, ɔbɔfoɔ a ɔbɔ ant-hill no. ” Ɔtoaa so sɛ, “Ma me de wo bɛsoa me akyi. ” Ntikuma kɔɔ hɔ kɔsɛn wɔ hɔ. Mprempren Ananse kɔɔ so yii yam baako; ɔkaa sɛ, “Me nna bosome biara a me nna no, me bɛdi saa baako yi. ” (Ne yere) Aso kae sɛ, “Efi bere a Ɔbɔfoɔ no yɛɛ nneɛma no, woahu ɔbarima bi a ɔwɔ nna bosome biara? ” Ananse ne ne yere ne ne mma dii yam no wiee, na aka ɛno baako pɛ a ɛyɛ Father Spider dea. Anɔpa kyeɛ no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, Ananse kaa sɛ, “Meanya me bosobɔ. ” Na ɔkɔɔ akɔtena fie no akyi, noa yam no, na ɔdii no kɔsii sɛ ɛwiee. Ntikuma kae sɛ, “Me bɛsan akɔ. ” Ananse kaa sɛ, “Me ne wo bɛkɔ abom. ” Ntikuma kae sɛ, “Me ne wo nkɔ abom. ” Bere a Ntikuma kɔdae no, na Ananse kɔgyee ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ aboa, de ntokuro nketenkete ahyɛ mu, de nsõ ahyɛ mu, na ɔde akyekyere wɔ Ntikuma ntama no ano. Bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi koraa ntɛm ara no, Ntikuma tuu kwan. Bere a esum a ɛrekyere nneɛma anim no atwa mu no, Ananse nhwehwɛ Ntikuma sɛnea ɔpɛ, wannya no, ɔkɔɔ bɛhwehwɛɛ baabi a nsõ no atɔ. Bere a ɔhunuu wɔn no, ɔdii wɔn akyi kɔɔ so kɔɔ so. Na ɔkɔɔ so no, bere a, na Ntikuma ne ɔgyaw-nananom te hɔ. Ɔpanyin mpanim kae sɛ, “Papa barima, dɛn na woyɛ dwadie? ” Ananse buae sɛ, “Me kɔm abɔ me, enti ma me ade a mebɛdi. ” Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, “Ɛhe na mebɛnya ‘ade’ firi hɔ? ” Ananse buae sɛ, “Wosusuw sɛ me yɛ ɔkwasea, me ba mpo, Ntikuma, bae na woama no bi. ” Ɔpanyin mpanim no kae sɛ, “Kɔ afuo no hɔ, yam a ɛka sɛ, ‘twe me so, twe me so. ” ,’ wontwe mma no so, nanso wɔn a wɔka sɛ, ‘Don’t pull me up, don’t pull me up,’ twe saa bi. ” Spider no buae, "Ah! Ɛyɛ dɛn na ɛyɛ nkwaseasɛm a wose kyerɛ me, bere a obi ka sɛ ɔdɔ obi, woka kyerɛ me sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mepɛ no tan, menntie da? " Nanso Spider no kɔɔ afuo no mu. Bere a ɔduruu hɔ no, yam no bi kae sɛ, “twe me so, twe me so,” ebinom nso kae sɛ, “Ntwe me so, ntwe me so. ” Na ɔtwee nea ɛkae sɛ, “twe me so, twe me so,” no so, na ɔne wɔn baa fie. Ɔbaeɛ na ɔde ne ho kɔtwaa wɔn honam ani; Nanananom kae sɛ, “Monkɔto aduane pa no fã bi, na fa aboa no to akora no so gu ogya so. ” Ananse buaa no sɛ, “Ah, nsɛm bɔne a ɛporɔw yi na wose kyerɛ me; Ɔdomankoma no yɛɛ nneɛma firi bere a yɛtow aduane pa no gu, na yɛkora? ” Akɔre no faa aduane no fã pa no, na ɔde too ogya so, nanso ɔ Ɔde honam ani fua bi kɔtoo soro. Bere a wɔnoa aduane no, ɔyii no, na ne nyinaa adane abotan, nanso honam ani fua baako pɛ a ɔde too soro no adane aduane pa. Father Spider kaa sɛ, "Eh! saa ɔdeduutu a ɔsan ba bio yi nni nkontompo ankasa. " Na Spider no kɔprapraa ne honam ani fua no bae de too ogya no so. Bere a wɔnoa aduane no, ɔde firi ogya so, na adane ayɛ yam a wɔanoa no yiye sɛnea wubetumi apɛ sɛ wuhu. Ɔkae sɛ, "ɔbaa-nananom, eyi nyinaa yɛ yiye paa, nanso seesei ɛsɛ sɛ wode nam ma me de ka ho. " Ɔbaa-nananom no kae sɛ, "nkɔ akɔhwɛ dua no so, kɔkɔɔ wɔ so; bere a wode bɛkɔ no, wubɛka sɛ, 'Woyɛ mmoa. '" Spider no kae sɛ, "Ɔbaa yi! Me ne Munkuntuntunkum nni ntɔkwa ho asɛm, na dɛn nti na me bɛkɔ abɔ wɔn abɔne saa kwan yi so? Nananom kae sɛ, "Kɔ, na sɛ ɔhaw ba a, ɛno ne me asɛm. " Ananse tuu kwan. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mmoa, mo a wɔwɔ nnua yi so no, nananom kae sɛ mo yɛ mmoa. " Mmoa no kae sɛ, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ nananom nti a, anka yɛbɛbɔ mo. " Nananom no kae sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ monya mmra. "

Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɔbaa panyin, ɛno nteɛ. " Afei mmoa no hwee ase bɛtoo ne so bɛbɔɔ Ananse. Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, "Me Se-akode-kete no twa wɔn ti. " Tintin! ne nnyigyei a Se-akode no yɛe bere a ɔrehyɛ aseɛ reyɛ n’adwuma no. Ɔbaa panyin nana no kae sɛ, "Mprempren, dwo na ɛnyɛ den. " Nanso akɔre no ntee (ne kae no). Mprempren, Ananse no boaboaa nam no na ɔsan baa fie, na ɔbaa pɛɛ. Afei Ananse wiaa ne sekan no, na ɔde kɔhyɛɛ ne bag mu, na ɔtuu mmirika baa ne nkuraase. Berɛ a ɔsan baa n’akuraa ase no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ba sɛ, "Ntikuma, esiane sekan a ɔde ne nsa ato so yi nti, momma yɛnkɔ kwaeɛ. " Ɛneɛ wɔtuu kwan, na wɔkɔhwɛɛ wild pigs bi. Ananse kae sɛ, "Ntikuma, kɔto wɔn so. Ntikuma kɔɔ so kae sɛ, "Bush-nkoko, mo ho fĩ. " Bush-nkoko no buae sɛ, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ sɛ woyɛ Kwaku Ananse, akɔre ba a, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛba bɔ mo. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Mo ba mmɛbɔ no wɔ ɔkwan biara so, ɔyɛ bɔne koraa, n’anibiannaso nni aseresɛm. " Bush-nkoko no rebɛbɔ no bere a Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "ɔbaa panyin sekan, nea ɛyɛ nnam no, ntwa wɔn ti mma me. " O h! ne Knife no tɔɔ wɔn so, na ɛtwaa mmoa no ti nyinaa. Ɛnna ɛhyɛɛ aseɛ twaa nnua no. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Hai! dɛn na woyɛ yi? Me kae sɛ wotwa mmoa no ti, na wowiee no a, na afei dɛn na ɛyɛ eyi nyinaa? Mprempren Ntikuma hwɛɛ hɔ kakra, na ɔkae sɛ, "Softly and easy now. " Kwaku Ananse ntee no, na Seakɔm no baa fam, na Ananse kɔgyee no. Wɔsan baa fie. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ wɔbɛkɔ ɔsoro-nyame hɔ. Ntikuma kae sɛ, "Papa, nkɔ sɛ ɛyɛ sekan yi nti a." Ananse kae sɛ, "Fi hɔ." Ntikuma kae sɛ, "Fa wo kwan so." Ananse tuu kwan kɔɔ wimyanomhenni bi nkyɛn wɔ hɔ, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Onyankopɔn, ɛnnɛ, sɛ obiara ayɛ wo bɔne a, me nko ara me, Kwaku Ananse, me bɛtwa ne ti nyinaa da koro pɛ na me ama wo." Wimyanomhenni no kae sɛ, "Ɛwɔ aseda ma obiara a Sɛ ɔbɛtumi ayɛ saa a, ɔtamfo dɔm kɛseɛ bi agyina wɔ kuro no akyi, enti wofa wo ho kɔ.” Ananse kaeɛ, “Wɔmfa bɔɔl ne nguan baako mma. ” Ntɛm ara na wɔde wɔn bae. Ananse kɔɔ kwan no so; Ananse bisaa ɔtamfo dɔm no sɛ, “Ɛyɛ wo na wose wone Osoro-nyame no bɛko? ” Wɔbuaeɛ sɛ, “Aane, ɛno na yɛka. ” Mprempren Osoro-nyame no dɔm no agyina Ananse akyi. Ɛnna Kwaku Ananse kyerɛɛ ɔbaa no sekan, nea ɛyɛ nnam no, sɛ, “Twa saa ɔko dɔm a ɛyɛ atuatew yi ti. ” Na sekan no twaa wɔn ti tan! tan! na ɛwiee. Ananse kae sɛ, “Ɛno ye deɛ, gyae. ” Nanso sekan no hwee ase bɛtoo ɔsoro-nyame no asraafo no so na na ɛretwa wɔn ti. Ananse kae sɛ, “Ɛwoi, ɛno yɛ ɔsoro-nyame no asraafo, enti gyae. ” Nanso ɛtwaa so. Ananse kae sɛ, “Sɛ wo nufu mu yɛ wo dɛ a, ma ɛyɛ dwoo. ” Ɛwom! soro-nyame asraafoɔ no nyinaa, wɔapopa. Mprempren akofena no tuu kwan wɔ wim, ɛbaa baabi a na ɔwɔ Kwaku Ananse. Ɔkae sɛ, “Hwɛ, wonnim me anaa? Wonnim me anaa? Me ne Kwaku Ananse, mefiri ɔbaa dada no nkyɛn de wo bae. ” Ɛwom! seesei Kwaku Ananse ne ne ti a, tan no bɔɔ fam! Na ɛkɔɔ so kɔɔ so akofena no; na baabi a ɛgyinaa no danee sare bi a yɛfrɛ no “Etene” (akofena-sare); ne ano yɛ nnam paa.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
