Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐSƐ SƐ ƆBABAA BIARA WARE NE A WƆDE NO MA NO AWAREƐ NO

Ɛbɛyɛɛ bere bi a na ɔbaabunu bi a wɔfrɛ no Kwaboaso. Wɔn a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔware no, wɔde no maa no sɛ, "Mempɛ no. " Wɔde no maa ɔhwehwɛfo bi, na ɔkae sɛ, "Ugh! ɔbarima yi wɔ ticks wɔ ne ho, mempɛ no. "

Ɛda bi na ɔkɔɔ afuo no mu sɛ ɔrebɛtwa plantain. Ɔde sekan bɔɔ plantain no so kɛkɛ! bere a ɛhɔ na abofra nketenkete no te plantain no so; wɔsii fam, bae, na wɔkyeree Kwaboaso; wɔkae sɛ, "Wo ne nea, bere a wɔde kɔma obiara no, wosɛe wo ti, pusu! "pusu!" Na anantwi no kyeree no na wɔkae sɛ, "Ba, momma yɛnnhyɛ no.

Yɛkyere no, O!

Yɛtwitwa Kwaboaso.

Mɛnka mma kyere. Mma yɛn kyerɛ sɛ yɛbɛbɔ no. Mma yɛn kyerɛ sɛ yɛbɛbɔ no. Mma yɛn kyerɛ sɛ yɛbɛbɔ no.

Yɛkyere no, O!

Afei, bere a saa ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ a na wɔde Kwaboaso ama no no tee Kwaboaso nne no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Me bɛkɔ akɔhwɛ sɛ dɛn na ɛyɛ asɛm no, ɛfiri sɛ yɛmfa biribi bɔne ntua biribi bɔne. " Ɔkɔeɛ no, na Kwaboaso wɔ hɔ, na na anantwi no rekyere no. Afei ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no bɔɔ tuo maa anantwi no, na baako sii fam. Abayifoɔ no mu panin no kae sɛ ɔka kyerɛɛ afoforo no sɛ, "Ɔnom mmɛw ngo, na wanya nsa; fa no to hɔ bere a ɔrekyere no. " Bio ɔbɔmmɔfo no bɔɔ, na foforo bi hwee ase. Abayifoɔ no mu panin no kae sɛ, "Ɔkofoɔ no nom mmɛw ngo na wanya nsa, fa no to hɔ. " Ɔbɔmmɔfo no kumm abayifo no nyinaa gye panin no nkutoo. Ɔpanyin no frɛɛ abɔmmɔfo no sɛ, "Ɔmmra." Ɔbɔmmɔfo no kae sɛ, "Me renba." Abayifo no mu panyin no kae sɛ, "Oh, hwɛ me dan mu hɔ, na woahunu akode no aduro. " Ɔbɔmmɔfo no kɔɔ. Abayifo no mu panyin no kae sɛ, "Hwɛ me dan mu hɔ, na woahunu akode no aduro. " na atuduro ne atuo a woato no nyinaa, fa nea ɛyɛ wo deɛ, na fa Kwaboaso nso, nanso ansa na woakɔ, kɔtwa banana, na wode gu, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a abayifoɔ foforo no bɛsɔre, na wɔabɛkyere wo, ɛnneɛ bere a wɔagyae apɛ, baako baako, woakɔ akyiri, akyiri, akyiri." Na saa ara na ɔkɔtwaa banana, na ɔde K Waboaso nso, na bere a ɔduruu kwan no so no, ɔtow banana baako kɔe. Ɔkɔɔ so yɛɛ saa ara kɔɔ fie nyinaa. Na bere a na aka bere tiawa bi ansa na wɔbɛduru fie no, ɛbɛn fairies no retaa wɔn so. Na ɔtow banana baako a aka no guu fam, na fairies no dii no akyi na awiei koraa no wɔdanee wɔn ho san bae. Na ɔbɔfo no san de Kwaboaso baa ne mmusuafo. Afei ɔbɔmmɔfo no kɔɔ n’ankasa fie. Mprempren, na ɔbɔmmɔfo no te hɔ, bere a ɔhunuu sɛ amanneɛbɔfo aduru n’anim, na ɔkae sɛ, "Asɛm no ne dɛn? " Amanneɛbɔfo no kae sɛ, "Kwaboaso ka sɛ ɔsrɛɛ agya akuraa no panyin sɛ ɔmmɛgye ntam mma no, sɛ seesei ɔwɔ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔbɛware wo. " Ɔbɔmmɔfo no kae sɛ, "Me da wo ase wɔ nsɛm a efi akuraa no panyin ano no ho, nanso me ntumi nware ɔbaa no, efisɛ me da so ara wɔ t aky me ho wɔ. Ɛno nti na Mpanimfoɔ no ka sɛ, "Sɛ wɔde wo kɔma wo awareɛ biara, ware no, efisɛ wunnim nanso da bi a wuhia, ɔbɛgye wo. "

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
