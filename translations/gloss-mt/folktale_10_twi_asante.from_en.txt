Na Anene kɔn yɛɛ fitaa na n’akyi bɛyɛɛ tuntum

Ɔbaa bi a ne din de Ata (Twin) no, ne ne ba kɔɔ kɔbɔɔ nsuo no mu. Bere a wɔduruu asubɔnten no so no, ne ba no kae sɛ, "Yɛnkɔ nsuo no soro. " Nanso ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, "Yɛnkɔ nsuo no ase, efiri sɛ nsuo no ase na nkawa nketenkete no wɔ. " Ne ba no kae sɛ, "Me deɛ, mereko nsuo no soro, efiri sɛ nsuo no soro na nkawa nketenkete no wɔ. " Enti ne maame kɔɔ nsuo no ase. Wɔtenaa nkyɛn bere tenten, na ne ba no nyaa bi, na ɔbaeɛ bɛtwaa kwan kɔhwehwɛɛ ne maame. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na ne maame calabash no resensɛn wɔ nsuo no anim. Abofra no kae sɛ, "A! ɛnyɛ baako, ɛnyɛ mmienu, ɛyɛ maame calabash no. " Na ɔde ne nne so, suu na ɔfrɛɛ ne maame sɛ:

Mekae saa, na worente, mekae saa, na worente.

Meka kyerɛɛ sɛ, ‘Maame, yɛnkɔ nsuo no soro, efisɛ nsuo no soro na nkawa nketenkete no te.

Na ɔkae sɛ ɔrekɔ stream no ase, efisɛ stream no ase na nkawa mpata nketewa no te.

Na ɛyɛ dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ bi? Awerɛhow me maame, Ata, mhm!

Na Hawk no baa so; ɔkaa sɛ, "Abofra, wo dwom no yɛ dɛ, bɔ no na meanya wo ma wo. " Na abofra no maa ne nne so.

Mekae saa, na worente, mekae saa, na worente.

Meka kyerɛɛ sɛ, ‘Maame, yɛnkɔ nsuo no soro, efisɛ nsuo no soro na nkawa nketenkete no te.

Na ɔkae sɛ ɔrekɔ stream no ase, efisɛ stream no ase na nkawa mpata nketewa no te.

Na ɛyɛ dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ bi? Awerɛhow me maame, Ata, mhm!

Na Hawk no faa n’aboa akyi-kwan no bɔɔ nsuo no anim, nanso nsuo no gyinaa baabi a ɛgyinaa no. Na ɔno nso kɔɔ so. Na Kokosakyi, Vulture no, baa hɔ, na ɔno nso kae sɛ, "Mo-to saa dwom no mma me ntie, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mɛnya wo ma wo. " Na abofra no too dwom no.

Mekae saa, na worente, mekae saa, na worente.

Meka kyerɛɛ sɛ, ‘Maame, yɛnkɔ nsuo no soro, efisɛ nsuo no soro na nkawa nketenkete no te.

Na ɔkae sɛ ɔrekɔ stream no ase, efisɛ stream no ase na nkawa mpata nketewa no te.

Na ɛyɛ dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ bi? Awerɛhow me maame, Ata, mhm!

Na ɔsan nso de ne ntaban bɔɔ nsuo no anim, nanso hwee nsii. Na ɔno nso kɔɔ so. Na Anene, ɔkɔkɔɔ no nso baa hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abofra, dɛn nti na wosuro yi? Suro na ma me nte sɛ ebia mɛnya wo ma wo. Na abofra no bɔɔ dwom.

Mekae saa, na worente, mekae saa, na worente.

Meka kyerɛɛ sɛ, ‘Maame, yɛnkɔ nsuo no soro, efisɛ nsuo no soro na nkawa nketenkete no te.

Na ɔkae sɛ ɔrekɔ stream no ase, efisɛ stream no ase na nkawa mpata nketewa no te.

Na ɛyɛ dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ bi? Awerɛhow me maame, Ata, mhm!

Crow no faa aduro kɔn bi de too nsuo no mu, na ɔde n’akyi a ɛyɛ nkonyaayie no bɔɔ nsuo no anim maa nsuo no mu yɛɛ mmienu; na ɔkae sɛ, "Mo to dwom no"; na abofra no too dwom. Na ɔhunuu ne maame ti nhwi; na ɔserewee kakra. Ɔkae sɛ, "Mo to dwom bio," na ɔtoo dwom. Na ne maame puee kɔɔ ne kɔn mu. Abɛn kae sɛ, "kɔ so bɔ wɔ". Na abofra no bɔɔ dwom, na ne maame puei kɔɔ ne nan ase. Ɔkae sɛ, "Bɔ no bio"; na ɔbɔɔ dwom. Ne maame puee prɛko pɛ na ɔgyinaa bank no so. Ata, mmaninyi no kae sɛ, "Abɛɛma, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mefa de ma wo? " Ɔbuae sɛ, "me deɛ, me mpɛ hwee koraa, kɔtɔ kɔkɔɔ tuntum ne fitaa ntama. " Ata tuu mmirika tɔɔ bi, na ɔde baa kyerɛɛ Abɛɛma no, na Abɛɛma no de kɔkɔɔ tuntum no kataa so, na ɔde fitaa a ɔde kyekyeree ne kɔn no. Ɛno nti na wobɛhu sɛ Abɛɛma no, n'akyi yɛ tuntum, nanso ne kɔn ho yɛ fitaa. Saa yɛ akyɛdeɛ a ɔnyaeɛ firi Ata, Twin no hɔ.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebia wobɛtumi afa no sɛ nokware, na nea aka no wobɛtumi akamfo me sɛ meka kyerɛɛ no.
