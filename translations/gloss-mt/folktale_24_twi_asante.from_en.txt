Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA NO, NA ABƆMMƆFOƆ NO HO YƐ DEN

ƆBƆMMƆFOƆ BI KƆTWAA AKODEƐ ADURO NE KƆBERE NTAM MMIENU WƆ SIKA KWAN SO, NA ƆNE NE YERE NE NE BA KETEWA KƆƆ KWAEƐ MU SƐ WƆBƐKUNKUM NAM. Na ɔkyekyeree ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ dan na ɛmu na ɔne ne yere ne ne ba teɛ. Bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔkɔɔ kwaeɛ mu. Bere a ɔhunuu aboa bi na ɔtoo n’akodeɛ guu no so no, ɔantumi annye. Ɛda biara na ɛte saa, bɛyɛ mprenu nnawɔtwe pɛ. Na ne yere kae sɛ, “Me nnyaa nam biara nni hɔ sɛ mebɛkɔ fie. ” Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a ɔbɔmmɔfo no kɔɔ kwae mu no, ne yere ne ne ba nso kɔɔ wɔn akuraa ase. Mprempren na atuo koro pɛ aka. Mprempren me werɛ fi sɛ mekae sɛ saa ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no din ne Gyekye, na afei nso ne yere deɛ ne Boniaye-kae-dabi (kyerɛ sɛ, da bi ɔnni aseda bɛkae). Bere a na ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no rekyinkyin no, na ɔadu odum-dua bi ase, na ɛhɔ na ɛyɛ beaeɛ a ɛyɛ fɛ. Ɛhɔ na ɔhwehwɛfoɔ no de n’adwene sii so kaeɛ (kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ), “Sɛ me kɔ fie a wɔbɛfa sika dɔteɛ no ama powder no, na me annya bi, enti mɛfa saa tuo yi atow me ho atuo wɔ me menew so sɛ menwu. ” Prɛko pɛ Adeɛ bi frɛɛ no sɛ, “Ɔbɔfoɔ no abɔmmɔfoɔ. ” Na ɔhwehwɛfoɔ no kaeɛ sɛ, “Papa. ” Na Adeɛ no kaeɛ sɛ, “Dɛn nti na w’ani abere saa? Ɛnneɛ na ɔwɔ, a wɔfrɛ no ‘Go-and-spoil-the-home’ no nni wo fie, anka mebɛma wo biribi. Afei ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Biribiara nni fie. ” Biribi kae sɛ, “biribiara nni fie nti, kɔ odum-dua no dua mu kwan mu, na adawuru gongs mmienu da hɔ, enti fa ba. ” Na ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kɔɔ de baa. Na biribi kae sɛ, “Monyi cut peg abien, na mmu w’ani, na mutu mmirika fa baako si yonder, na fa baako no nso tu mmirika fa si yonder. ” Bere a ɔbae no, ɔkae sɛ, “Mayɛ. ” Biribi kae sɛ, “Fa dawuru kɛse no bɔ no. ” Na ɔbɔɔ no maa kuro no bɛyɛɛ akuraa a ɛtrɛw wɔ peg abien no ntam. Na odum-dua no gyina wɔ mfinimfini a ɛda no. Na saa adeɛ no daa ne ho adi sɛ ɛyɛ ɔkɔre. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no sɛ, sɛ asɛm biara a ɛho hia bɛba nteɛsoɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ wɔte no wɔ odum-dua no ase. Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, “Ho, meate. ” Bere a ɔkɔre no aka eyi akyerɛ no no, ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no nyaa yerenom, agyinatufoɔ, ne asomfoɔ. Ɛduruu saa bere no, ne yere a wɔfrɛ no “Daakye-ɔdɔm-ɔdɔm-bɛkae” no nyaa ho hu sɛ na ɔrebɛyɛ ɔhene, enti ɔtɔɔ nsa-bɔɔl na ɔde kɔɔ ɔhene no akuraa ase, na ne ba da n’akyi. Berɛ a ɔduruu hɔ no, ɔbɔɔ nea enti a ɔba no adi, na ɔkae sɛ, “Wo ba no ka sɛ ɔde nsa ba sɛ ɔnhwɛ wo anim. ” Na ɔgyee nsa no too mu, na ɔmaa wɔn a wɔwɔ hɔ no mu biara nnom. Na ɔde ɔbaa no kɔtoo Gyasehene nsa sɛ ɔmfa no nkɔ ne fie. Berɛ a anwummere bɛdwoeɛ no, ɔbaa no dwaree na ɔkɔɔ baabi a ɔhene no wɔ ne dan mu. Ɔbaa no ka kyerɛɛ ɔhene no sɛ, “Wo ne me anya nsuo ne owia, ɛnnɛ woanya baabi a wobɛtena, na anka ɛnyɛ sɛ wobɛsrɛ me? ” Mprempren bere a na ɔrekasa yi te sɛ mmarima ne wɔn nkwaseasɛm, na ɔtumi nnya hwee nka. Ɛnna na ɛhɔ ara na ɔkyeree ɔbaa no na ɔne no dae. Anɔpahema bi yii no fii ne dan mu wɔ Gyase Panyin fie na ɔde no kɔɔ ɔhene no harem. Ɛhɔ na ɔtenaa kosi sɛ akyinnyeɛ kɛseɛ bi baeɛ. Ɔhene no ne n’akwankyerɛfoɔ nyinaa kɔtenaa odum-dua no ase. Ɔbaa no ba gyinaa ne papa anim, na ɔhunuu ɔkɔmhyɛn no ba wɔ odum-dua no atifi, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Papa, mepɛ no. ” Na ne papa bɔɔ ne ase, na ɔtoo ne ti akyi ma no kɔɔ fie. Na mmea no mu baako foforo nso baa n’anim, na ɔnso gyinaa n’anim. Ɔsan nso huu Ɔkɔmhyɛn ba no, na ɔkae sɛ, “Papa, mepɛ no. ” Na ɔbɔɔ n’ase saa ara na ɔde ne ti akyi bɔɔ no ma ɛhwee ase, na bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔsuu. Na ne maame kae sɛ, “Dɛn na woresu? ” Na ɔkae sɛ, “Papa bɔ me. ” Na ne maame kae sɛ, “O! Dɛn nti na ɛsɛ sɛ ɔmmɛbɔ wo, sɛ ɛyɛ abofra a ɔda-ase-ɔ-nni-aseda a, anka wammɔ no. Na ɔda bi ɔda-ase-ɔ-nni-aseda no kae sɛ, "Mea ɛte sɛɛ no, mennyɛɛ wo hwee sɛ woyɛ saa. " Anka ɔka saa no ara na wɔn baanu nyinaa de wɔn ho bɔɔ mu bɔɔ ɔko. Na atiefoɔ no kae sɛ, “Saa ade pɔtee yi a ɛwɔ dua yi atifi nti, yɛn agya yere reko no, yɛbɛtutu no. ” Ntɛm ara na wɔnyaa wɔn akofena, pimpen! pimpen! (na ɛyɛ nnyigyei a wɔyɛe). Na aserewa nkokoaa no teɛ sɛ:

"Obeko Asamoa, Berɛ a ɔkwasea kɛkɛ bɔ ne nsa-bɔ nnuadewa no no, anɔpa hene nkurɔfoɔ rekɔ akyiri ne anim kɔ hɔ!"

Aburuburo teɛm sɛ ɔka.

Bere a na ɛna rewu no, ɔgyaee me nkorabata a ɛyɛ abien.

Bere a nkorabata a ɛwɔ nkorabata no wui no, ɛgyee me akyi berɛmo a ɛwɔ mmɛw nhaban mu no so.

Bere a mmɛw nkorabata no wui no, ɛgyaa me wɔ nkorabata a ɛkotow so;

Bere a nkorabata a ɛkɔntɔn no kaa me no, ɛdii me bɛyɛ bɛyɛ bɛyɛ!

Akenkena nnomaa no teɛɛ sɛ:

Me yɛ me deɛ me deɛ me deɛ." Na seesei Ɔkɔre no ankasa baeɛ, na ɔteɛm:

"Nkensen nkensen, aboa a wɔwɔ ntakra ya ya!

Meda Gike ase!

Nkensen nkensen, feathered creatures ya ya!"

Na bere a ɔteɛm saa no, kuro no mufoɔ nyinaa tu firii mu; Na Gyekye anhu sɛ mmarima, nguan, mmea, yerenom, ne nnomaa anaa biribiara kɔe. Ɔno nko ara ne ne yere ne ne ba na ɔtraa odumdua no ntɛm no ho wɔ nkyɛn. Na Ɔkɔmhyɛn kae kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Efi da yi, ɔbɔfoɔ biara renya ɔmanfoɔ da. " Wei, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaasɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi wobɛtumi agye no di, na nea aka no nkamfo me sɛ meka kyerɛɛ no.
