Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware.

SƐNEA ƐBƐDAA NO SƐ YƐBƐHUNU OKRA, ƆKƆKƆ, DA BERE NYINAA WƆ VELVET AKONWA SO, BERE A 'KRAMAN, ƆKƆKƆ, BƐDA ADUANNOA FIE GYA NO NSÕ MU

Wɔka sɛ ɔbaa bi a n’akyi bɛyɛɛ no dɛ a ɔwo ba biara no. Enti ɔde ne ho kɔɔ abosom nketewa no mu biako bisaa no asɛm na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔpɛ ba. Abosom ketewa no kae sɛ, "Me bɛma wo biako, nanso ɔba no deɛ, nnwuma a ɔbɛyɛ biara ne sɛ ɔbɛma wo aka ka, nanso da bi ɔbɛtua wo ka. " Ɛnyɛ nna mmienu, na mmom ɛnyɛ nna mmiɛnsa bere a wosusuw abosom ketewa no ho no, ɔbaa no yɛɛ awotwaa. Ɔwoo no abosom-abakɔsɛm ba, ɛnyɛ bere tenten na ɔrenyin. Akokoaa no nnyinii bɛyɛɛ aberante a ne ho yɛ fɛ. Ɛda bi a na ɔne ne maame wɔ hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ, "Maame, ma me sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma, sɛ mebɛkɔ mpoano man no so akɔtɔ nkyene. " Maame no kae sɛ, "Sika dodow dɛn? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Asuanu" (i. e. £4). Na maame no de no ma no, na ɔde kwan no kɔe. Mprempren, bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔne ɔbarima bi ne ne kraman a ɔwɔ nsensanee no hyiaa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa no ba sɛ mebɛtumi atɔ no. " Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Worentumi antɔ no. " Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ɛbɛn dɛn? " Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Asuanu kɔkɔɔ mfutuma a ɛyɛ £4. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛno bɛn me? fa kɔkɔɔ mfutuma a ɛyɛ £4 yi. " Ɔgyee ɔkraman no bɛdii fie. Bere a ɔsan bae no, ne maame kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woantumi ankɔ baabi a wopɛ sɛ wotwa kwan no? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me de sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no tɔɔ ɔkraman bi. " Ne maame kae sɛ, "Ho! " Mprempren na wɔte hɔ, ɛbɛyɛ asram baako. Ɔkae sɛ, "Maame, ma me sika dɔteɛ a mɛtumi akɔdwadie. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wɔn deɛ, sɛnea w’akwan kyerɛ no, wobɛfa sika dɔteɛ no nko ara na woatow agu bio, nanso wopɛ dodow bɛn? " Ɔbuae sɛ, "sika dɔteɛ a ɛyɛ Asuanu ne suru. " Ɔkae sɛ, "fa no a. " Enti ɔfii aseɛ kɔɔ dwadie kwan no so. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa ɔbarima bi a ɔde ɔkraman bi kɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔbarima, fa saa aboa no a ɛtaa to n’anammɔn so no ba sɛ mebɛtumi atɔ no. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Sɛ me da me dan mu a, mmoawa no bɔ me nan, ɛno nti na me tɔɔ no. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me srɛ mo sɛ monnye mma me. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wontumi ntɔ no. " Abofra no kae sɛ, "Bɛn na wobɛfa ama no? " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Sika kɔkɔɔ a ɛyɛ Asuanu ne suru. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛno nti na wose me ntumi ntɔ no. Ɛha, fa wo no. Abofra no nyaa ɔkraman no, na ɔne no kɔɔ fie. Bere a ɔduruu fie no, ɔkae sɛ, "Maame, monhwɛ nea me de aba yi. " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Ah, ɛno ara na wɔkae no. " Abofra no traa hɔ. Ɛbɛyɛ nna aduanan akyi no, bere a ɔkasa kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Ma me sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma na metumi akɔdwadie. " Maame no kaa sɛ, "Sika a me wɔ me ho no nyinaa asɛe, gye Asuasa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. e. £6); mehyɛ wo yi a, na woakɔ, na woannkɔtɔ nneɛma nni mu a, ɛno ne adwuma yi awiei." Abofra no kae sɛ, "Meateɛ." Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔde ne bag no kɔe, pa! Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔne Ashanti barima bi hyiaa, na ɔde pigeon bi kɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, "Adamfo, fa wo mmoa no ba sɛ wobɛtumi atɔ no. " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Menntɔn no, efisɛ mede no di me ho fɛw. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me bɛtɔ no. " Anomaa no wura kae sɛ, "Merentɔn no, efisɛ minim nea ebetumi ayɛ ama me. " Abofra no kae sɛ, "woi fa no ma me. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wobɛtumi atɔ no? " Abofra no kae sɛ, "Sika bɛn? " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Asuasa sika a ɛyɛ dust a ɛyɛ £6. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wosusuw sɛ ɛno nti mɛntɔ saa, na ɛno ne sika no. " Ɔde anomaa no baa fie. Ne maame kae sɛ, "Eyi nnyaa nnyɛ yiye nsen kan, enti eyi na wode aba? " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Nanso, eyi na me de aba. " Mprempren abofra no te hɔ, bere a Pigeon no frɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɔmmra. " Bere a ɔforo kɔɔ no no, Pigeon no bɔɔ no amanneɛ sɛ, "Me ara me yɛ ɔhene wɔ me kuro mu, na me rebɛkɔ akwantu bi, bere a ɔpanyin bi c me ne no kuraa me mu, bere a wone no, wofiri w’adɔeɛ mu, wo nso, wotɔɔ me, na seesei me srɛ wo, sɛ wode me bɛsan akɔ me kuro mu a, na wɔbɛda wo ase kɛse." Abofra no kae sɛ, "Woreka me atoro, wobɛdwane akɔ." Pigeon no kae sɛ, "Sɛ w’antumi anhu wo kwan a wobɛfa so ayɛ sɛnea mebisa no a, fa nkyɛn, na fa kyekyere me nan ho, na fa me kɔ." Abofra no faa nkyɛn na ɔkyekyeree. Ɛkɔɔ ne nan ho, na ɔdii n'akyi nkakrankakra kɔsii sɛ wɔduruu Pigeon kuro no mu. Bere a wɔduruu kuro no akyire no, na mmofra no redi nte marbles. Bere a wɔhunuu no no, wɔkae sɛ, "Chief no wɔ ha! Ɛno ne Ɔhene no! Wɔn mu baako tuu mmirika kɔka kyerɛɛ Korenti hene no, nanso wɔkyeree no, na wɔtwaa ne kɔn sɛ, "Worema yɛnkae yɛn awerɛhow a ɛtwaa mu no. " Nanso foforo bi nso san kɔe de nsɛm koro no ara. Na afei Akwamu hene no kae sɛ, "Wo de Gyase hene, wo ara na woakɔhwɛ nea eyi nyinaa fa ho. " Ɔkɔhwɛɛ na ɔsan bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "O, ɛyɛ nokware. " Afei wɔnyaa hammock ne regalia no kɔɔ de Ɔhene no baa ne fie. Wɔka kyerɛɛ abusuakuo no nyinaa asɛm no sɛnea ɔrehyɛ akwantu, ne sɛnea ɔpanyin bi kyeree no, ne sɛnea abofra yi ne ayamye nti atɔ no, ne sɛnea ɛnnɛ ɔde no baa fie. Mpanimfoɔ ne mmeranteɛ nyinaa sɔre na wɔdaa abofra no ase. Ɔhemmaa Panyin no de nsuo-kuku bi a sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma ahyɛ mu ma bae; mpanyimfo no nyinaa, kuku biara a sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma ahyɛ mu ma. Ɔhene no ankasa de ne nsa kyerɛɛ, na ɔde ring bi kɔɔ n’aniwa so, na ɔde maa no. Na ɔkae sɛ, "Fa ring yi, na nea wopɛ biara, saa ring yi bɛma wo. " Ɔkae sɛ, "mete. " Na ɔde ring no kɔɔ n'akuraa ase; na ɔde sika dɔteɛ no ne ring no kyerɛɛ ne maame. Ɛnna na maame no kae sɛ, "Welcome Aku, welcome Aku. " Kan no, bere a ɔhyew n’sika-kɔɔ no, na na ɔfiri n’akwantu ba, bere a ɔde n’ani kyerɛ ne maame no, na ɔmmua no. Na ɔde n’asɛm no kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Woahu sika-kɔɔ yi ne ring yi; Mɛkɔsi akuraa kɛse bi ama yɛn sɛ yɛntena mu. " Maame no kae sɛ, "Press your eyes hard (i.e. Bɔ wo mmɔden sɛ woyɛ saa). Abofra no kɔɔ kwan gyinaa kwae mu; Ɔde ring no hwee ase na ɔde too fam; Ɔkae sɛ, "Ring, nnyi kwae ne kwae yi nyinaa afi hɔ mma me. " Na saa beae no nyinaa yɛɛ fitaa. Ɔkae sɛ, "Nboaboa nea woayi no nyinaa ayɛ no nkotodwe ama ɔhyew. " Na ɛyɛɛ saa. Ɔkae sɛ, "Nsi adan. " Na ɛsii adan pii. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ring, momma nkurɔfoɔ mma wɔmmra mma wɔnni saa afie yi mu. " Na nkurɔfoɔ baa. Ɔmaa ne maame yɛɛ ɔhemaa, ɔno nso bɛyɛɛ ɔhene. Mprempren, na me werɛ fi sɛ me nkae sɛ Ananse, ɔkɔkɔɔ no, ne n'adamfo paa. Da bi, na ɔte hɔ wɔ ne nkuraa foforo no mu, bere a Kwaku Ananse de n'adwene sii hɔ sɛ ɔbɛba ababaawa yi ase. (Bere a ɔduruu hɔ no,) ɔkaa sɛ, "O, maame ba ketewa, papa ba ketewa, woanya nneɛma na wo ho akye wo, na wunni me ho kora a worenkora me so, nanso dɛn na ɛde eyi nyinaa bae? " Ɛnna abofra no kaa nsɛm no nyinaa kyerɛɛ no. Akɔre kae sɛ, "Mebɛkɔ me nkuraase akɔfa biribi na measan aba. " Ananse kɔɔ n’akuraa ase, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuabaa sɛ, "Mebɛma wo akɔ me yɔnko adamfo nkyɛn, na wode kɔ white wine fitaa yi bɛma no na w’ani agyina hɔ na woyɛ nea ɔhyɛ wo na ɛsɛ sɛ wode ahintasɛm bɔ mmɔden sɛ wode wo nsa bɛka saa ring no. " Ɔbaabun no tuu kwan, na ɔkɔɔ ɔbarima no akuraa ase. Ɔbarima no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛno deɛ, mebɛhwɛ sɛ wunni nkɔ akyiri bio, ɛsɛ sɛ wode nna mmiɛnsa tena me nkyɛn ansa na woasan aba. " Ɔbaabun no kae sɛ, "Mame. " Mprempren na ɔne ɔbaabun no na wɔte hɔ bom, na ɔbarima no kɔdware, na ɔde ring no kɔtoo pon bi so, na ɔbaabun no faa no, na ɔde kɔɔ ne wofa. Bere a Ananse de ne nsa kyerɛɛ so no, ɔde abɔso no yɛɛ kuro kɛseɛ. Mprempren, ɔbarima no, bere a ɔbɛhwehwɛ n’abɔso no no, wannya no. Ɔbɛtee sɛ akɔre no akyekye kuro kɛseɛ sen ne deɛ. Afei ɔkɔɔ so kɔbɔɔ nkɔmmɔ wɔ abosom nketewa no mu biako wɔ akyirikyiri. Lesser-god no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ananse nuaba a ɔbaa hɔ bɛtoo wo no, afa akɔma ne wofa. " Spider no nso kɔɔ akɔkɔɔ sɛ wɔde Okra, Cat, ne Okraman, Dog no bɛkɔ abɔ wɔn ho ban, enti ɔkɔɔ so nyaa aduro a ɔde bɛsa nam de too kwan no so sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mmoa no rekɔ, sɛ wɔfa bi di a, wɔrentumi nsan nkɔ baabiara. Ɔbarima no a na wayera n’ananseni no baa fie, na ɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔkraman ne Ɔkraman no sɛ, "Berɛ no adu sɛ meka kyerɛ mo nea enti a metɔɔ mo, (na ɛyɛ eyi); me deɛ bi ayera, na wɔka sɛ ɛwɔ Spider no nsam, nanso ɛda adaka bi a ɛwɔ nneɛma no nyinaa ntam. " Na bio, wɔka sɛ ɔde aduro akɔfa de afra ɔpono nam na ɔde ato kwan no so, enti sɛ wodu hɔ a, nnye no na munhuru so." Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Cat, woate, wo na wobɛwe no." Cat no kaa sɛ, "firi hɔ, wo a bere biara wotwe w'ano na wotwe w'ano." Ɛnna wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kasaeɛ pii, na wɔn wura no kaa sɛ, "Ɛno nyinaa yɛ kwan, yɛ. Ɛhɔ na mmoa mmienu no hyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ so. Mprempren na ɛka berɛ tiawa bi pɛ na wɔbɛduru baabi a nam no wɔ no, bere a Dog no "tee" nam no hua. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Cat, me yaw wɔ me yafunu mu, na mentumi nkɔ so. " Cat no kaa sɛ, "Momma me nkɔ, adwuma a ɛwɔ hɔ no yɛ nea ɛho hia. " Dog no kaa sɛ, "Cat, me ntumi. " Cat no kɔɔ so. Ɔkraman no kɔɔ baabi a nam no wɔ, ɔbɔɔ nam no nyinaa, hɔ na ɔdaeɛ, ɔrentumi nkɔ bio. Na ɔkraman no duu Ananse akuraa ase, na ɔdaeɛ wɔ Ananse mpa no dan mu, wɔ ɔdan no atifi. Bere a ɔda hɔ no, ɔhunuu mmoawa bi a ɔretwam. Squeak! bere a ɔtoo ne ti so no. (Mmoawa no) kae sɛ, "Wonkyere me, asɛm no ne dɛn? " Ɔkraman no buae sɛ, "Me wura no ring no ayera, na wɔse ɛda Ananse adaka no mu, a ɛda n'adaka no nyinaa mfinimfini, na sɛ wotumi kɔfa ba me nkyɛn a, anka mebɛma wo kwan. " Mmoawa no kae sɛ, "Metumi. " Ɔkraman no kae sɛ, "Sɛ me gyae wo kwan a, na wofiri hɔ nkɔ na woamfa amba, mmom woguan? " Mmoawa no Ɔkae sɛ, "Sɛ wopɛ a, fa nhama no twitwiw me asen. " Ɔkraman no faa nhama bi de kyekyeree ne asen ho. Afei mmoawa no kɔɔ ɔkɔmhyɛn no dan mu na ɔbɔɔ amena no mu tokuru a na ɛda nneɛma a aka no nyinaa mfinimfini no. Na ɔyɛ nkakrankakra na ɔbɔɔ ɔkwan bi, na ɛbɛyɛɛ kɛse. Ɔfaa mu kɔgyee ring no kɔmaa ɔkraman no. Bere a ɔkraman no de ne nsa ka so no, ɔtuu mmirika kɔe na ɔbaa ɔkraman no nkyɛn na ɔda baabi a ɔde no too no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Woda so ara da ha, na ɛhe na saa nam no wɔ? " Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Oh, me mmu (nea ɛbɛyɛe no), ebia nnipa a na ɛyɛ wɔn dea no bae bɛgyee no; nanso ring no wɔ he? " Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Ɛha na ɛwɔ hɔ. " Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Wɔse sɛ asubɔnten a ɛda kwan no so no yɛ nsuyiri, na sɛnea woyɛ, wotwa nsuo no mu, wode wo nan nam fam no, ɛbɛyɛ sɛ (ring no) bɛtɔ fam nti. Ma me, esiane sɛ me deɛ, wunim, mebu nsuo no so. Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Ɛte saa, fa (no). " Woduu asubɔnten no so, na ɔkraman no huruw mu. Saa ara na ɔkraman no yɛe. Prɛko pɛ na ɔkraman no twaa mu. Ɔkraman no nso duu asubɔnten no mfinimfini na ɔbrɛɛ ne ho, na bere a ɔrebɛgye n’ahome no, ring no tɔɔ (n’anom) nsuo no mu. Ɔtwaa kwan baeɛ baabi a na Cat no wɔ. Cat no kaa sɛ, "Ɛhe na saa ring no wɔ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛhwee nsuo no mu (me n’ano). " Cat no tuu mmirika kɔhyɛɛ nsuo no mu, ɔhunuu mpataa kɛseɛ bi a ɛretwam. Na Cat no kyeree no. Mpataa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛdɛn na ɛyɛ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me ring no akɔhwe nsuo yi mu, enti sɛ wopɛ ɔhaw a, ma me nea ɛyɛ me ntɛm ara. Sɛ woamfa no amma me a, mebɛku wo ntɛm ara. Fish no kae sɛ, "Yɛnkɔ asubɔnten no ano na mɛma wo nea ɛyɛ wo. " Wɔn baanu nyinaa duu bank no, Fish no yii no, na ring no bae. Ɔkraman no faa no bae kyerɛɛ Dog no. Dog no kae sɛ, "Papa, me srɛ wo, sɛ wobɛkɔ a, nka nea asi. " Ɔkraman no de n’ano siee. Wɔduruu fie hɔ, na Ɔkraman no ka nea ɛkɔɔ so no nyinaa kyerɛɛ ne wura sɛ, "Dog no nam no kae a ɔkae no bere a yɛrekɔ no nti, wantumi ankɔ akyiri. Na ɛsan nso, mɛkɔ anya ring no na measan de bae, Dog no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, bere a ɔtwaa asuo bi so no, ɛsɛ sɛ mede ring no ma no. Mede no maa no, na ɔtow no guu asuo no mu. Na ɛyɛ bere kakra pɛ na me nsa ka no bio." Nnipa a na wɔwɔ hɔ no nyinaa kae sɛ, "Praise be to the Cat. " Na wɔteɛm sɛ, "E! " Afei ɔhene no kae sɛ, "Wo, Cat, aduane biara a meedi no, me bɛhwɛ sɛ me bɛpa bi na meato mu w'akutu ketewa no mu. Mat biara me da so nso me da so nko sɛ woda so bi. Wɔ wo nso, ɔkraman, wobɛda ogya a ɛrekyɛn no so wɔ anadwo a ɛyɛ nwunu bere a anadwo a ɛyɛ nwunu no ba no. Flogging nko ara na ɔmanfoɔ bɛbɔ wo. Ɛno nti, nna nyinaa wobɛhu ɔkraman no, baabiara nni a ɔda gye ɔkɛntɛn a ɛyɛ papa. Afei nso, sɛ wode aduane bi gu fam ma no a, ɔrenni gye sɛ ɛfiri prɛte so. Nanso Dog no deɛ, daa na yɛbɛhu no sɛ ɔda asɛnnibea hɔ wɔ nsononoo a ɛwuiɛ no nsõ so. Afei nso, wobɛhu no sɛ wɔrebɔ no, ɔbɛteɛ, "Kao! " Ɛno nyinaa fi bere a wɔde wɔn kɔeɛ wɔ ring yi ho.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebia wobɛtumi afa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no nso wobɛtumi nkamfo me.
