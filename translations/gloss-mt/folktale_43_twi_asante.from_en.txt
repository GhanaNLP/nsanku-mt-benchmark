Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA NO BERE A NWANSENA, FLY NO, BƐTENAA KRAMAN, DOG NO SO, ƆBƆ NO, KAM! KAM!

Ɔbɔfoɔ bi na na ɔwɔ akraman nnan; biako na wɔfrɛ no Sniff-sniff; foforɔ na wɔfrɛ no Lick-lick; foforɔ na wɔfrɛ no Tie-in-knots; ne foforɔ nso na wɔfrɛ no Gulp-down. Ɔde ne akraman no bɔɔ abɔmmɔ. Bere nyinaa, bere a ɔkɔ kwaeɛ mu no, ɔne wɔn kɔ baako. Da bi, ɔkɔɔ kwaeɛ mu na ɔhunuu kola dua bi a nnuaba a ɛsensɛn hɔ a ɛte sɛɛ. Ɛnna ɔsan baa fie, na ɔka kyerɛɛ n’yere asɛm no sɛ, "Kola dua bi a meahu no, ɛso aba a ɛsensɛn hɔ te sɛɛ. " Ne yere no kae sɛ, "Kɔfa, efisɛ wunim sɛ yɛnni kola-nut koro mpo a yɛbɛwe na yɛnni biara a yɛbɛtɔn. " Ɔbɔfo no de ne ho kɔɔ kwan no so, nanso ɔgyaa akraman no wɔ fie, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a kola dua no wɔ. Ɛnna ɔforo so, na ɔpaw kola-nnu bi. Mprempren ɔtee nneɛma a ɛwɔ akyirikyiri, "Twa! be! kukuru! kukuru! hu! pi! " Sasabonsam gyinaa ne anim. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Hwan na ɔtew me kola-nnu? " Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no nso kaa sɛ, "me nko. " Sasabonsam no kaa sɛ, "Mebekum wo ntɛm ara wɔ adeɛ a woyɛeɛ yi nti. " Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wobɛkum me a, momma mdi kan frɛ me nkra. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Meka, nanso sɛ wanya mɔ mmɔden mmiɛnsa a frɛ wɔn na wɔmmra a, ɛnneɛ mebekum wo. " Ɛnneɛ ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no de ne nne so.

Mprempren akraman no ate; wɔtu mmirika, prada! prada! Sasabonsam kae sɛ, “Call again, and that completes the third time. ” Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no maa ne nne so kɛse paa.

Wai, wai, wai, wai, Wɔn ho nkyɛn nkyɛn nkyɛn nkyɛn. N'ano nnyinaa hɔ bere a akraman no na wɔwɔ n'anim. Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Sniff-sniff, ɛha ha Sasabonsam bi, fa no hwɛ. " Na Sniff-sniff hwe no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Lick-lick, lick him over. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Tie-in-knots, tie him in a knot. " Tie-in-knots no kyekyeree ne menewa no mu, na Sasabonsam no hwee ase wɔ hɔ, kum! Afei Gulp-down de no kɔɔ de no guu mu. Ɔbɔmmɔfo no ne nkraman no boaboaa kola-nuts no ano de baa fie. Bere a ɔbɔmmɔfo no san bae no, ɔbaa hɔ bɛyɛɛ nnuan a wɔayam no a ɛyɛ ma, na ɔde too hɔ maa nkraman no. Bere a wawie adidi no, ɔkɔɔ abontene na ɔtenaa gyedua bi ase. Seesei ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no nkoa Fly kɔɔ hɔ kɔhwɛɛ aduane no a wɔde siesiee ama akraman no, na ɔmaa no so kɔɔ soro. Akraman no firi abontene bae na wɔn kɔm de wɔn. Lick-lick ka kyerɛɛ afoforo no sɛ, "Moahu nea yɛayɛ ama yɛn wura, nanso ɛde besi ha no, ɔmfa aduan biara mma yɛn nti momma yɛnkɔ kum no. " Wɔn buae sɛ, "Wonni nkontompo, wonni nkontompo. " Lick-lick fii abɔnten kɔɔ kwan so kɔtoo ɔbɔmmɔfo no ho, na ɔne ɔbɔmmɔfo no dii agorɔ; na Sniff-sniff nso bae; na ɔhweehwee ɔbɔmmɔfo no ho; na ɔno nso kɔe. Afei Tie-in-knots no bae na ɔkyekyeree ne menewa mu, Gulp-down no nso bae na ɔde no guu fam. Mprempren akraman no san kɔɔ fie, na wɔrebɛhwɛ patu no mu bere a wohuu soup nsensanee wɔ hɔ. Wɔkae sɛ, "Fly, ebia ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no gya yɛn aduane na woadi no? " Fly kae sɛ, "Manhu no baabiara. " Nteɛteɛteɛ, bere a ɔma n'ani so no, ɔhunuu aduane a ɛda hɔ (wɔ ɔdan no atifi). Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛw, papa! Yɛakum yɛn wura no kwa, ɛfiri sɛ aduane a na ɔde ahyɛ hɔ ma yɛn no, ɛnyɛ baako, ɛnyɛ mmienu, ɛyɛ Fly no na ɔde kɔtoo hɔ. Fly no guanee firi hɔ kɔɔ wim. Ɛno nti, bere a kraman bi te hɔ, na fly bi te ne so no, ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkyere no.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
