Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐYƐ DƐN NA TALE-BEARING BAE ABUSUAKUW NO MU

WƆKA SƐ ƆBƆMMƆFOƆ BI KƆƆ KWAEƐ MU, ƆBAA BƐHWƐƐ AMENA BI MU, NA ƐMU NA ’Nipa, Ɔbarima, Osebo, Ɔsebɔ, Owo, Ɔwɔ ne ’Kusie, Ɔkɔm gyina hɔ. Mprempren bere a saa nkurɔfoɔ yi huu Hunter no, eyi kae sɛ, “Papa Hunter, mma me mfi hɔ. ” Ɛno kae sɛ, “Agya, mma me mfi hɔ. ” Hunter no kae sɛ, “Mererenba, efisɛ ɛkɔsi ɔkyena mpo, sɛ mebɛgye wo a, na woanya me so a, nea wobɛyɛ me no (me nim) ɛnsɛ me ho. ” Eyi kae sɛ, “meantumi nyɛɛ saa. ” Ɛno kae sɛ, “meantumi nyɛɛ saa. ” Hunter no kae , “Sɛ ɛno ne nokware a, mebɛyi wo.” Na nokwarem no, ɔyii nkurɔfo no nyinaa na ɔkɔɔ n’akuraa ase. Ɛda bi, bere a Ɔbɔmmɔfo no wɔ hɔ no, Osebo, ɔkraman no, de nam kɛse bi bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Papa Hunter, eyi yɛ aseda wɔ ayamye a woyɛɛ me no, me da wo ase, Apiafo. ” Ɔde nnuadewa du-mmienu a ɛyɛ nnuadewa a nam wom kɔmaa Ɔbɔmmɔfo no. Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no daa no ase na ɔgya no kɔeɛ. Wɔ bere foforɔ bi mu no, na Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no wɔ hɔ na Okusie, Rat no, baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Father Hunter, wobɛpɛ sika kɔkɔɔ dust bi? ” Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Ho! adɛn nti na ɛnyɛ, ɛno ne adeɛ a mmarima bɛhwehwɛ no abere. ” Rat no kɔɔ n’akwan mu, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a Nyankonpon, ɔsoro Nyame no, de ne sika kɔkɔɔ dust sieeɛ. Ɔkɔdii tokuro bi wɔ dan no ase, na ɔkɔhyɛɛ dan no mu, na ɔkɔgyee Sky-god sika dɔte no nyinaa, na ɔde baa Hunter no nkyɛn. Hunter no kaa sɛ, “Meda wo ase, Aku. ” Na Rat no kɔɔ. Ɛda foforo no, na ɔwɔ hɔ, na ɔtee sɛ obi rehyɛ no ayɛ, “Ago. ” Hunter no buae sɛ, “Ame. ” Ɔbarima no bae na ɔkae sɛ, “Papa Hunter, anɔpa pa. ” Ɔno ne busuani. Ɔkae sɛ, “Eyi yɛ mmɛw ngo ketewa bi a me de aba de ayɛ wo aseda. ” Papa Hunter kae sɛ, “Mempɛ wo deɛ, ɛte sɛ nea mekae no ara sɛ mebɛfa wo akɔ, monhwɛ nea Leopard no de baa me, monhwɛ nea Rat no de baa me. ” Ɔbarima no de ne mmɛw ngo no kɔe. na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ ɔsoro nyame no sɛ, “Wo sika-dwetɛ a ɛyerae no, saa Rat no akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ ɔbae faa no kɔe de maa Hunter no. ” Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, “Ɛte saa? ” Ɔkae sɛ, “nananom, ɛyɛ nokware pefee. ” Ɔsoro nyame no somaa nkranteɛ sɛ wɔnkɔkyere Hunter no, na wɔde hama kyekyeree ne kwaso. Wɔbɔɔ no akuturuku kɛse. Afei bere a wɔne Hunter no akɔ kwan no so no, ɔkae sɛ, “Mpanimfoɔ, mehia sɛ mebɛkɔ akɔdwo me ho. ” Amanneɛbɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, “Dabi, ɔkorɔmfoɔ, wobɛdwane. ” Ɔkae sɛ, “Dabi, me-ren-dwane, na sɛ wopɛ a, fa rope no kyekyere no, kyekyere no bata dua no ho, na ma me kwan. ” Amanneɛbɔfoɔ no tiee no, na wɔmaa no kwan. Ɛhɔ na ɔde ne ho toɔ, bere a aboa a ɔyɛ tenten ne ɔyɛ tratraa no baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Hwɛ ha, me yɔnko, gye aduro ketewa yi, na fa kɔ, na bere a wode kɔ, na wɔbɛgyina wo anim wɔ asɛnka agua no so, na ɛba no sɛ wɔbɛkum wo, mebɛkɔ ɔhene no harem, sɛ me bɛka ne yere dɔfo no ba; bere a me aka no, wubehu sɛ obiara asɔre, na wo nso bɛkasa, na woaka sɛ, ‘Me wɔ aduro,’ na sɛ wobɛka me wɔ aduro a. Wɔde no bɛdi dwuma wɔ abofra no so na ɔbɛsan anya ahoɔden.” Ɔbɔmmɔfo no kae sɛ, “Meda mo ase, Aku. ” Amanneɛbɔfo no de Abɔmmɔfo no kɔɔ anim a ɔsoro-nyame, (ne ne mmarahyɛ baguafo) Kontire, Akwum, Adonten, Gyase ne Kyidom atitire. Ɔbɔmmɔfo no kaa n’asɛm, na bere a Ɔkasafo no sɔree sɛ ɔbɛbu atɛn no, wɔtee ahweho nnyigyei, pui! pui! Wɔ ahemfie a ɛwɔ akyirikyiri. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛdɛn ne ɛno? " Wɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔwɔfoɔ ba akyerɛ-nyame no akɔka. " Obiara kɔɔ akyiri. Mprempren na Hunter no gyina hɔ; ɔkae sɛ, “Sɛ mennyɛ nneduafoɔ a, anka me bɛnya aduro a ɛbɛsa me awuduru no. ” Ɔbarima bi tee nka na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Mommra nneduafoɔ no nkɔ ha. " Ɛnna ɔbɔfoɔ no bae. Ɔsoro-nyame kae sɛ, “Sɛ wotumi hwɛ eyi a, me ba, sɛ ɔnwu a, mɛma wo de wo ti akyerɛ wo. ” Ɔbɔfo no kae sɛ, “mete Kind-baako. ” Na Ɔbɔfo no kae bio sɛ, “Nanabarima, nea mebɛkyerɛ wo no, sɛ wotumi nya bi a, ɛnneɛ mɛtumi ahwɛ wo ba so. ” Wɔkae sɛ, “Ɛnyɛ dɛn na ɛyɛ? ” Ɔkae sɛ, “Tell-tale mogya a ɛne aduro a wɔabɔ no fra. ” ine. Wɔkae sɛ, “Ɛhe na yɛbɛnya Tell-tale mogya? ” Ɔkasafoɔ no mu baako a na ɔyɛ ɔnyansafoɔ kae sɛ, “Ɔbarima a ɔba bɛka kyerɛɛ soro-nyame sɛ Hunter aba na wawia soro-nyame sika no, ɛhe na ɔwɔ? ” Wɔkae sɛ, “Wo nni mfomso, mo nni mfomso. ” Wɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔgyee ɔbarima no. Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Twa ne nsa mu wɔ ntamadan no mu, na fa ne mogya ma me. ” Wɔn ara ntɛm ara bɔɔ no. Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no de mogya no kɔɔ kɔdii aduro no ho dwuma na ɔde guu ɔhene ba no nan ase. Ntɛm ara ɔsɔre. Wɔkaa sɛ, “Nto ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no nkamfo. ” Wɔbɔɔ dawuro sɛ, “Eee! ” Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, “Mefa wo ti kyerɛ wo, na Tell-tale no nso ɔnwu, na ɔntwitwa ne nam na ɔmfa nkyekyere afã nyinaa. ” Wɔkum no, na wɔtwaa ne nam mu nketenkete, na wɔde ne nyinaa guu afra. Saa na Tell-tale pii baa abusuakuo no mu.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
