NNIANIM ASƐM A WƆAKYERƐW

Akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ afe yi bɛkɔ so ayɛ nea ɛyɛ den sen biara wɔ yɛn man no mu sikasɛm abakɔsɛm mu no mu biako. Te sɛ wiase no mufoɔ fã a aka no, wɔde Covid-19 ɔyaredɔm no too yɛn so wɔ afe a ɛtɔ so mmiɛnsa a ɛdi kan no mu, na ɛno maa sikasɛm mu dwumadiɛ so tew, na ɛmaa yɛn sika a yɛnya no so tew na ɛde ɛka foforɔ a wɔanhyɛ da ayɛ a ɛbata ɔyaredɔm no ho bae.

Bere a yɛresusuw sɛ yɛrenya nkɔso kɛse wɔ yare no nkɛntɛnso a ɛsɛe no mu bere a yɛde yɛn nsakrae nhyehyɛe no rehyɛ ase no, wɔde nsɛm ahorow aba a ɛboro so no, na ɛsɛee sikasɛm mu nkɔso a yɛanya fi afe 2017 kosi 2019 no. Nea edi kan no, ɛyɛɛ Russia ne Ukraine ɔko no, na afei sika a wonya ho nhyehyɛe atitiriw a wɔkyɛe wɔ 2022 sikasɛm nhyehyɛe no mu, na ɛmaa wɔtew boseabɔ so. Mpofirim, nea edi kan wɔ mfe du mu no, wɔato amanaman ntam kapital gua no mu akɔ yɛn so, ɔman no mu sikasɛm tebea ayɛ den na boseabɔ ho ka akɔ soro, na ɛno na ɛde ɛka a ɛkɔ soro ne CD no bo a ɛkɔ fam ntɛmntɛm aba.

Enti, bere a amannɔne sika a wɔde boa sika kɛse no ne amannɔne sika a yɛnya no so atew no, sika a yɛsɛe no wɔ nneɛma a yɛde fi amannɔne ba ho no akɔ soro afi yɛn ɔman no akɔnnɔ a enni awiei a yɛwɔ sɛ yɛde nneɛma fi amannɔne ba no, ne ɛka a yɛbɔ no so atew no asɛe yɛn sika no bo kɛse. Eyi ama yɛn nyinaa asetra mu ka abɔ. Petrol bo a ɛkɔ soro no ama nneɛma ne nnwuma dodow no ara bo akɔ soro, na nneɛma bo a ɛkɔ soro no duu ɔha biara mu aduanan anan sen biara wɔ October 2022 mu no.

Wɔasɔ yɛn ahwɛ sɛ ɔmanfoɔ na aban no wɔ tema kɛseɛ ma mo wɔ nhyɛsoɔ a ɛnsɛ a eyi de aba no ho. Yɛpɛ sɛ yɛda yɛn anisɔ adi sɛ Aban no ma yɛn a wɔdi dwuma ahodoɔ a Mmarahyɛ Bagua, Adwumawuranom Fekuo, Adwumayɛfoɔ Fekuo, Ɔmanfoɔ, Nyamesom Fekuo, Ghana Nnwumakuo Fekuo ka ho.

Non-Oil Real GDP Growth: Ɛyɛ ade a ɛkyerɛ afe afe sikasɛm mu nkɔsoɔ ankasa wɔ nnwuma nyinaa mu gye ngo.

Sika a ɛnyɛ towtua: Aban sika a wonnya mfi tow mu, sɛ nhwɛso no, ɛne sika a wotua wɔ tumi krataa anaa tumi krataa a wɔde ma ho, sika a wɔde di dwuma ne sika afoforo a wɔbɔ.

Asɛdeɛ a ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ no ne MDA bɔhyɛ a ɛne Sikasɛm Dwumadibea no yɛ ne 2016 sikatua a ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ ma mmara sika.

Outturn: Ɛyɛ adeyɛ anaa nsɛm a ɛtoatoa so.

Primary balance : Eyi ne nsonsonoe a ɛda aban sika a wonya ne sika a wɔsɛe no a ɛnyɛ mfɛntom (a ɛnka ɛka ho ka ho) ntam. Wobetumi asusuw eyi ho sɛ GDP ɔha biara mu nkyem 100.

Ɛkyerɛ sika a wonya fi mu titiriw (sika a ɛho ka) a ɛka sikasɛm mu sika a wonya (sika a ɛho ka) ho, a ɛnka mfɛntom a wotua wɔ aban ka ho no ho.

Nnwuma nketewa ne akɛse (SMEs): Wɔyɛ nnwumakuw a wɔnyɛ adwumakuw a wɔhyɛ wɔn ase, wɔde wɔn ho a wɔfa adwumayɛfo a wonnu dodow bi.
