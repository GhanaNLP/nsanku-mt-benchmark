Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA NO MMOFRA BƐTOO NSA

WƆkaa sɛ bere bi na ɔkɔm kɛseɛ bi baeɛ, na Ananse, Ɔkɔm-kɔm, ne ne yere Aso, ne ne mma Ntikuma, Nyiwankonfwea (Thin-shanks), Afudotwedotwe (Belly-like-to-burst), ne Tikonokono (Big-big-head), sii atutuo ketewa bi de tenaa mu. Da biara na Ɔkɔm-kɔm no taa kɔ fa aduane, wɔn a wɔde yɛ aduan, na wɔnoa na wɔdi. Ɛnɛ da bi, Ananse Papa kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu, ɔhunuu aduane a ɛyɛ fɛ regyina hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. ” Aduane no kaa sɛ, “Me din nnyɛ fɛ. ” Akɔre no no kae sɛ, “Wofrɛ wo? ” Ɔbuae sɛ, “Mefrɛ me, ‘Fill-up-some-and-eat’. ” Akɔre no kae sɛ, “Fa nnuadewa-ngo bi hyɛ mu sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mebɛhu. ” Aduane no de nnuadewa-ngo-ngo hyɛɛ mu, na Ananse dii no nyinaa. Bere a wawie no, ɔbisaa aduan no sɛ, “Dɛn na ɛyɛ wo taboo? ” Aduan no buaa no sɛ, “Me nka atuo wad ne gourd cup ketewa. ” Spider no de aduan no kɔɔ fie na ɔde kɔɔ soro. Ɛnna Ananse kɔɔ kwaeɛ mu de aduane baeɛ, na Aso nso, bere a ɔno nso aduane no wiee no, ɔfrɛɛ Ananse, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Oh, wo deɛ hia ankasa, me deɛ me yɛ ɔpanyin, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ me ne aduane di, wo ne mmofra yi deɛ hia ankasa; sɛ woyɛ ma a, m’aso ayɛ dwoo (w’anibereɛ nnyigyei). ” Bere a wɔwiee adidi no, na ɛnna Spider no pa Wɔdan no akyi, na ɔtenaa ɔdan no atifi a aduane no wɔ no. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Aduane yi yɛ fɛ. ” Ɛbuae sɛ, “me din nnyɛ fɛ. ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Wo din bɛn? ” Ɛkaa sɛ, “me frɛ no, ‘Fill-up-some-and-eat’. ” Ananse kaa sɛ, “fa bi ma me ma mehu. ” Na ɛhyɛɛ prɛte bi a ɛyɛ anane aduane ma, na Ananse dii. Da biara a ɔsɔre no, na ɛte saa. Mprempren, Ntikuma huu sɛ ne papa nnyinii, ɛmfa ho sɛ ɔne no nni aduane bom, na Ntikuma kuraa ne papa no so na ɔhunuu nea papa no kuraa no. Bere a papa no kɔɔ kwae mu no, Ntikuma foroo soro na ɔhunuu aduan no. Ɔfrɛɛ ne maame ne ne nuanom, na wɔkɔɔ soro. Ntikuma kae sɛ, “Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. ” Ɔkae sɛ, “Mefrɛ no fɛfɛɛfɛ. ” Ɛnneɛ wɔfrɛ wo? Ɔkae sɛ, “Me din ne ‘Fill-up-some-and-eat’. ” Ɔkae sɛ, “Fa no ma kakra sɛ mebɛhu. ” Na aduane no hyɛɛ mu ma nnuadewa ngo. Ntikuma ne ne maame ne ne nuanom dii kosi sɛ wɔn nyinaa wiee. Na afei Ntikuma bisaa aduan no sɛ, “Dɛn na wotaboo? ” Aduan no kae sɛ, “Me tan gun wad ne gourd cup ketewa. ” Ntikuma ka kyerɛɛ Afudotwedotwe (Belly-like-to-burst) sɛ, “Kɔfa bi mma me. ” Na ɔde wɔn bae, na ɔde gun wad no kaa no, ne gourd cup ketewa no kaa no. Ɛnna wɔn nyinaa baa fam. Na Agya Spider (mmerɛ a) san bae a ɔne kwae mu yams no. Aso wiee wɔn aduane no. Wɔfrɛɛ Ananse; ɔbuae sɛ, “Ebia wante nea mekae no; mekae sɛ, efi nnɛ, bere a meba fie ne aduane no, wobɛtumi adi bi, efisɛ wo na wodi hia. ” Aso ne ne mma no dii. Father Spider hohoroo na ɔforo soro no so; ɔkae sɛ, “Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. ” Koraa kommyɛ! “Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. ” Koraa kommyɛ! Father Spider kae sɛ, “Ah! Ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ ntama yi nti na ɛnyɛ fɛ; me bɛkɔ akɔfa Oyoko clan no tartan no mu baako abɛto so. ” Na ɔforo fam kɔfa Oyoko clan no ntama bɛhyɛ. Ɔhyɛɛ ne mpaboa, ɔforoɔ soro hɔ (bio). Ɔkaa sɛ, “Aduane yi yɛ fɛ. ” Dwoeɛ koraa! “Aduane yi yɛ fɛ. ” Dwoeɛ koraa! Ɔhwɛɛ dan no mu na ɔhunuu sɛ akodeɛ wad ne kɔkɔɔ kɔpo ketewa bi wɔ mu. Ananse kaa sɛ, “Ɛnyɛ adeɛ baako, ɛnyɛ nneɛma mmienu (kyerɛ sɛ. Akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ, ɛyɛ Ntikuma. Ananse bɔɔ aduane no, na ɔbaa fam; ɔyii Oyoko tartan ntama no de too hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ kwaeɛ. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu Mpere a ɛyɛ fɛ paa (adeɛ a wɔfrɛ no Mpere) no da hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “O, anwanwasɛm! Saa ade yi yɛ fɛ sen nea etwa to no, aboa no yɛ fɛ. Aboa no kaa sɛ, “Me nkae no ‘fɛ’. ” Ɔno kae sɛ, “Ɛnneɛ wofrɛ no dɛn? ” Ɛkaa sɛ, “mefrɛ no ‘Abiridiabrada’ kyerɛ sɛ Swish-and-raise-weals. ” Na ɔkaa sɛ, “Swish kakra mma me nhu. ” Na aboa no bɔɔ no ase sɛ, “Swish, swish, swish. ” Papa aboa no teɛɛm sɛ, “Pui! Pui! Anomaa bi a ɔte nkyɛn ka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, “Kyerɛ Adwobere” (a ɛkyerɛ sɛ, dɛn na ɛyɛ mmerɛw ne ntɛm). Na Ananse kae sɛ, “Adwobere. ” Na abirekyi no gyaee no bɔɔ no. Na Ananse de saa abirekyi yi baa fie; na ɔde kɔtoo soro. Aso wiee aduane a ɔnoa no, ɔkae sɛ, “Ananse, mma nni. ” Ɔbuae sɛ, “Wɔda so ara wɔ ha, ebia woasɛn wo aso mu biara nni, merendi. ” Ananse foroɔ soro na ɔkɔtenaa ase dwoodwoo. Afei ɔsan baa fam bio; na afei ɔsan kɔhyɛɛ ne ho wɔ baabi. Afei Ntikuma foroɔ soro. Ɔkaa sɛ, "O, saa agya no de adeɛ bi aba bio. " Ntikuma kae sɛ, "Maame, Nyiwankonfwea, Afudotwedotwe, ba ha, efisɛ nea agya de aba no sen nea etwa to no koraa. " Ɛnneɛ wɔn nyinaa foro soro wɔ ɔdan no atifi. Ntikuma kae sɛ, "Saa ade yi yɛ fɛ. " Ɛbuae sɛ, "mefrɛ no 'fɛ'. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wo din bɛn? " Ɛkae sɛ, "mefrɛ no 'Swish-and-raise-weals'. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Swish kakra ma mehu. " Na saa abere no tui ne so bɔɔ wɔn denneennen. Ananse gyinaa nkyɛn, na ɔbɔɔ ose sɛ, "fa no so, fa no so, titiriw fa no so wɔ Ntikuma, fa no so wɔ no. " Mprempren bere a Ananse ahwɛ na wahu sɛ wɔde abobɔ no sɛnea ɛfata no, ɔkae sɛ, "Adwobere" (Cool-and-easy-now). Ananse bae na ɔde abobɔ no twitwaa no mu nketenkete pɛɛ no wɔ abɔnten. Ɛno na ɛmaa abobɔ no baa abusuakuo no mu. Ɛnneɛ ɛba no, bere a woka wo ba asɛm bi no, na ɔrentie wo, yɛbɔ no abɔmmɔ.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
