Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐ KOKOSAKYI AKRAMAN NO REKASA WƆ MFATOHO MU KYERƐ BONEKYEREFO AKRAMAN NO A, ƆTE NO ASE.

BONEKYEREFO a ɔyɛ Hyena no, na ɔte hɔ, na wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ne maame dedaw no awu. Hyena no susuu, na ɔsusuu, na ɔsusuu, na ɔde ne ho kɔsrɛɛ Kokosakyi, Vulture no; Akoko, Hen no; Odwan, Sheep no; Okra, Cat no; ne Kraman, Dog no, sɛ wɔmmɛka no nkɔ n’awo no ase. Mmoa no nyinaa hyɛɛ aseɛ, na wɔn ne no kɔeɛ. Berɛ a wɔduruu hɔ no, wɔwiee amannee no, na ɛno nko ara na aka sɛ wɔbɛhohoro wɔn ho. Wɔkɔɔ so kyerɛɛ akokoraa no sɛ, "Wohohoro, yɛn nso yɛbɛtumi ahohoro yɛn ho. " Akokoraa no buaa no sɛ, "Me deɛ, me maame panyin da asase ase, na me renwetew me ho da kosi sɛ nna aduanan ayi afahyɛ no bɛkɔ so. " N'ayɔnkofoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Eyi yɛ asɛm kɛseɛ a yɛahunu," (na ɛkyerɛ sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wɔn nso yɛ mmuadadi nna aduanan). Da biara na wɔsɔre, na wɔn yafunu mu yɛ wɔn yaw, nanso Hyena no de, ɔkura ahoɔden nyinaa. Ɛnɛ, da bi a Vulture no kae sɛ, "Ah, saa Hyena no, me nim no; wɔ saa bere yi ara mu na wanya so akɔdidi ne maame funu. " Anwummere a ɛdwo dwo no, Kokosakyi, Vulture no, tuu kwan (na ne ntɛn no nnyigyei) fa, na ɔsii dua tenten bi a ɛwɔ asaman no mu, na ɛhɔ na ɔhunuu Hyena no retu ntutuutu. Na ɔbaa no, na ɔkɔɔ ɔdamu-kɔn no so bɔɔ bɔɔ na ɔtwee ne maame funu no kɔdii no. Vulture bɛtenaa hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Hyena, congratulations, yɛde aseda ma wo, nanso ɛyɛ ahosɛpɛsɛm aseda. " Hyena no kaa sɛ, "Me srɛ mo, ɛno deɛ, monka nkyerɛ obiara, nea wobɛka kyerɛ me no, mebɛte wo nteaseɛ ase na meayɛ ho ade ntɛm ara. " Vulture no tuu kwan kɔɔ soro, fa nnyigyei, na ɔbaa fie. Ɛnkyɛreeɛ bi, Hyena no baeɛ. Ɔkɔmfoɔ no kae kyerɛɛ ne nnamfoɔ sɛ, "Ɛnnɛ, sɛ ɛbɛyɛ den sɛ ɛbɛtɔ ɔboɔ a, a yɛbɛdidi; ɛno nkyɛn no, yɛbɛkɔ yɛn akuraa ase. " Mmoa no kae sɛ, "Really? " Ɔkɔmfoɔ no buae sɛ, "Wobɛpɛ sɛ mebɛhyehyɛ no. " Ɔkɔmfoɔ no kae sɛ, "Ba, yɛnkɔ. " Wɔde wɔn kwan so na wɔkɔɔ Hyena fie. Vulture no kae sɛ, "Hyena, tie ntie na ma meka nkyerɛ wo, ɛnnɛ yɛpɛ sɛ yɛdi, na wode nso wode bɛdi; ɛno akyi no yɛbɛkɔ yɛn akuraa ase ɛnnɛ, na wanya hwee aka? " Hyena no kae sɛ, "Me pene so, me bɛdi, ɛwom sɛ wode nso bɛtumi adi. " Ntɛm ara na Hyena no kɔgyee nguan mmiɛnsa de kyekyeree wɔn kɔm, na ɔde wɔn maa n’ayɔnkofo sɛ wɔnni, na ɔno nso nyaa s’afã. Ɛnna ne nnamfo no tuu kwan san baa fie. Ɛno nti na yɛwɔ asɛm bi a ɛne sɛ, "When the Vulture speaks in allegories to the Hyena, he understands its hidden meaning. "

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
