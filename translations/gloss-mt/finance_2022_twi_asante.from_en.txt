5 | Ghana Aban no - 2022 Ɔmanfoɔ Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ

“Ɔman a Wɔbɛkyekyere no: Sikasɛm mu nhyehyɛe a wɔbɛma ayɛ den ne adwumayɛ a wɔbɛma aba”

NNIANIM ASƐM A WƆAKYERƐW

Sikasɛm nyinaa ate nkɛntɛnso bɔne a Covid-19 ɔyaredɔm no de aba no ase, a Ghana ka ho. Amaneɛ a Ghanafoɔ hyia denam asetena, asetena ne nnwuma a wɔhwere so no nni abusuabɔ. Mewɔ nidi sɛ meka sɛ yɛn mmuae a yɛde di ɔyaredɔm no nkɛntɛnso ho dwuma no yɛ akokoduru ne ahokokwaw na wɔkamfo no wɔ wiase nyinaa. Aban nso hyɛɛ aseɛ yɛɛ nhyehyɛeɛ bi a ɛbɛboa ma nnwuma asan akanyan na yɛde yɛn asetena aba bio.

Bio nso, yɛɛ ho biribi wɔ ahunahuna ne hokwan ahorow a efi ɔyaredɔm no mu bae no ho, aban no, wɔ H.E. President Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo akanni ase, ntɛm ara yɛɛ GH¢100 ɔpepepem COVID-19 Alleviation and Revitalisation of Enterprises nhyehyɛe (GhanaCARES “Obaatan Pa” nhyehyɛe). Eyi de ahotɔ a ɛba ntɛm ne nea ɛho hia aba yɛn nkurɔfoɔ na ɛrekanyan yɛn man.

Yɛn ani sɔ wɔn ahotoso wɔ aban no mu, na yɛreyɛ adwuma de ahwɛ ahu sɛ yɛbɛsan anya ahoɔden afi amanehunu no mu. Saa bere yi mu no, asɔ yɛn ahoɔden a yɛde gyina hɔ sɛ ɔman no bɛtra ase no ahwɛ. Bible no taa de akwankyerɛ a ɛho hia ma wɔ mmere a ɛte sɛɛ mu. Titiriw no, sɛnea wɔaka wɔ Yesaia 9:10 no, “The bricks have fallen, but we will build with dressed stones; the sycamores have been cut down, but we will put cedars in their place”. Wɔ ɔkwan koro no ara so no, aban no asi ne bo saa ara sɛ ɔbɛma ne bɔhyɛ ahorow so na ɔbɛpa. Esiane eyi nti, yɛbɛkɔ so de nhyehyɛe ne nhyehyɛe ahorow a wɔde bɛsan de asase a ayera nyinaa aba na ama ɔmanfoɔ asetena atu mpɔn adi dwuma.

Botaeɛ titire a ɛwɔ 2022 sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ no mu ne mfeɛ mmiɛnsa (3) a ɛdi hɔ no ne sɛ wɔbɛkyekyere adwumayɛkuo a ɛbɛtena hɔ daa a ɛbɛtumi de sikasɛm mu nkɔsoɔ a ɛyɛ den, ɛbɛtena hɔ daa na ɛka obiara ho aba na ama obiara anya adwuma pa, titire mmeranteɛ ne mmabaa. Enti aban nhyehyɛe ahorow bɛboa mmabun ma wɔde wɔn talente adi dwuma na ama wɔatumi de anya yiyedi denam hokwan a ɛyɛ pɛ a obiara benya so.

Sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɛbɛma mmabun anya wɔn ho mu ahotoso na wɔanya wɔn adwumayɛ ho nkuran no, aban no bɛhyɛ YouStart nhyehyɛeɛ no mu den wɔ afe 2022 mu na ama wɔanya bosea a wɔde bɛma mmeranteɛ a wɔyɛ adwuma no sɛ wɔasiesie wɔn adwuma anaa wɔatrɛ mu.

Wɔ afe 2022 sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ mu no, yɛayɛ nhyehyɛeɛ sɛ yɛbɛkɔ so adi sikasɛm mu nsɛnnennen a ɛwɔ hɔ ntɛm ara no ho dwuma na yɛayɛ den wɔ ɔyaredɔm akyi. Aban ahyɛ bɔ sɛ ɔde nhyehyɛe ahorow a ɛkɔ akyiri na ɛyɛ biako bɛma ahokokwaw na ahyɛ adwumayɛ ho nkuran wɔ mmabun mu. Bere a yɛredi Ɔmampanyin no akyi no

Nsɛsoɔ (Sikasɛm mu nsɛmfua)

1D1F (One District, One Factory): Mfiridwuma ne nnwuma a wɔde besi hɔ a ɛbɛma ɔman no akɔ mfiridwuma mu nkɔso kɛse. Ɛde asi n’ani so sɛ ɛbɛma Ghanafoɔ adwumayɛ hokwan pii wɔ ɔman no mu.

Akontaabu: Eyi ne bere a aban no bu akontaa ma ne manfo denam ne dwumadi ahorow a ɛda no adi na ɛda nea efi nneyɛe a ɛtete saa mu ba no adi. Eyi betumi aka asɛyɛde a ɛwɔ sika anaa agyapade afoforo a aban no de asie ho.

Nkyekyɛmu: Adeyɛ a ɛfa nneɛma a wɔkyɛ anaa wɔkyekyɛ wɔ aban adwumayɛbea ne dwumadibea ahorow ntam ho.

Alleviation: Eyi ne ɔkwan a wɔfa so tew ɔhaw bi so.

Budget: Budget yɛ nhyehyɛe a ɛkyerɛ baabi a wobenya sika ne nea wɔde bɛyɛ. Sɛ nhwɛso no, abusua bi betumi ayɛ sikasɛm nhyehyɛe a ɛkyerɛ sika dodow a ɛwɔ hɔ ne sɛnea ɛsɛ sɛ wɔde di kan de di dwuma (sɛ nhwɛso no, dan ka, nneɛma a wɔde di dwuma ne aduan). Sɛ sika pii ho hia a, ɛsɛ sɛ abusua no susuw ho sɛ wobenya sika pii anaasɛ wɔbɛbɔ bosea.

Budget deficit: Eyi ba bere a aban sika a wɔsɛe no boro sika a wonya no so no.

Capital Expenditure: Eyi yɛ sika a wɔsɛe no wɔ nnwuma akɛse a ɛfa nnwuma ho te sɛ akwan, sukuu, ayaresabea, abɔntenban, akwantu, nsu nhyehyɛe, adwumayɛbea ne mfiri ne nea ɛkeka ho.

Adwumayɛfoɔ akatua: Wɔde akatua ne akatua ho sika te sɛ asetena mu ahobanbɔ, gratuities ne pensions a wɔtua ma ɔman adwumayɛfoɔ ka bom.

Nkɔsoɔ ahokafoɔ (DPs): Eyi kyerɛ aman ne/anaasɛ ahyehyɛdeɛ a wɔne aman a afei na wɔrenya nkɔsoɔ no yɛ adwuma bom de adu wɔn nkɔsoɔ botaeɛ ho. DP ahorow no betumi ayɛ aman pii, sɛ nhwɛso no Amanaman Ntam Sikakorabea (IMF), Wiase Sikakorabea, Afrika Nkɔso Sikakorabea (AfDB) anaa aman abien a ɛka aman ankorankoro te sɛ Germany, United Kingdom ne United States of America (USA) ho.

Afe awiei nneɛma bo a ɛkɔ soro: Nneɛma bo a ɛkɔ soro a wɔde bu akontaa no nyae wɔ December mu.

Mmoa ahorow: Eyinom yɛ sikasɛm mu mmoa a nkɔso ahokafo de ma aban no a ɛnsɛ sɛ aban no tua ho ka.

Mmoa a wɔde ma aban dwumadibea afoforo yɛ sikatua a mmara hwehwɛ te sɛ Ɔmantam Nhyiamu Sikakorabea a Wɔbom Yɛ (DACF), Akwan Sikasɛm, Ghana Nhomasua Ahotosoɔ Sikakorabea (GETFUND), Petroleum Sikakorabea ne Ɔman Akwahosan Sikakorabea.

Gross Domestic Product (GDP): Eyi kyerɛ nneɛma ne nnwuma a wɔhwehwɛ awiei koraa a wɔyɛ wɔ ɔman no mu nyinaa bo nyinaa wɔ bere pɔtee bi mu.

Amanaman ntam sika a wɔde asie nyinaa: Eyi susuw ɔman bi amannɔne sika tumi a ɛde tɔ nneɛma ne nnwuma a efi amannɔne wɔ bere bi mu; Ɛsan nso boa ɔman no mu sika no ahoɔden wɔ aman afoforo sika ho.

Inflation: Nneɛma boɔ nyinaa a ɛkɔ soro wɔ berɛ bi mu.

IPEP (Nnwuma a Wɔde Yi Ohia Fi Hɔ Dwumadie): Nnwuma a Wɔde Yi Ohia Fi Hɔ Dwumadie (IPEP) yɛ NPP Aban no nhyehyeɛ titire no mu baako a ne botaeɛ ne sɛ ɛbɛtumi ayi ohia afiri hɔ na atew pɛyɛ a enni hɔ so, titire wɔ nkuraaseɛ ne mpɔtam a wɔn ho nni asɛm denam nnwuma titire a wɔde bɛma so.

Sikasɛm mu botae akɛse: Eyinom yɛ susudua ahorow a wɔde susuw sikasɛm mu nkɔso. Nhwɛsoɔ no bi ne sikasɛm mu nkɔsoɔ, nneɛma boɔ a ɛkɔ soro, nsakraeɛ a ɛba wɔ adwumayɛ mu, aguadi mu nkɔsoɔ, aban sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛfa ho a ɛdi yie anaa ɛdi nkoguo ne gyinaesie a Bank of Ghana sisi a ɛka sika a wɔhwehwɛ ne nea wɔde ma wɔ sikasɛm mu.

MSME (Micro and Small Business Development Program): Wɔtaa kyerɛ adwumayɛbea ketewa (anaa adwumayɛbea ketewa) ase sɛ adwuma ketewa a ɛfa nnipa kakraa bi ho na ɛwɔ sikasɛm ho akontaabu anaa sika a wonya no nnu sika pɔtee bi.

NABCO (Nation Builders Corps) a wɔsan frɛ no NABCO no yɛ nhyehyɛeɛ bi a Ghana Aban de baeɛ sɛ ɛbɛma sukuupɔn mu adesuafoɔ a wɔnni adwuma anya adwuma de adi asetena mu haw ahodoɔ ho dwuma wɔ ankorankoro ne ɔman adwumayɛbea nyinaa mu na wɔakyekyere wɔn nimdeɛ de ahyɛ ɔman no sikasɛm mu nkɔsoɔ ho nkuran.

NBSSI (National Board for Small and Medium Scale Industries): Ɔman Boayikuw a ɛhwɛ Nnwuma nketewa ne Nnwuma akɛse so (NBSSI) yɛ ɔman adwumayɛkuw a ɛnyɛ nea wɔhwehwɛ mfaso a ɛhyɛ Nnwuma ne Nsɛm Ho Dwumadibea no ase na wɔama wɔn tumi sɛ wɔnhyɛ nnwuma nketewa ne nketewa nkɔso ne nkɔso ho nkuran.

Non-Oil Real GDP: yɛ sikasɛm mu nneɛma nyinaa a ɛnyɛ ngo adwuma no na ɛfa nneɛma bo a ɛkɔ soro ho.

Sika a ɛnyɛ towtua: Aban sika a wonnya mfi tow mu, sɛ nhwɛso no, ɛne sika a wotua wɔ tumi krataa anaa tumi krataa a wɔde ma ho, sika a wɔde di dwuma ne sika afoforo a wɔbɔ.

Asɛdeɛ a ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ no ne MDA bɔhyɛ a ɛne Sikasɛm Dwumadibea no yɛ ne 2016 sikatua a ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ ma mmara sika.

Primary balance : Eyi ne nsonsonoe a ɛda aban sika a wonya ne sika a wɔsɛe no a ɛnyɛ mfɛntom (a ɛnka ɛka ho ka ho) ntam. Wobetumi asusuw eyi ho sɛ GDP ɔha biara mu nkyem 100.

Nkɔsoɔ dodoɔ a wɔhwɛ kwan: Sɛdeɛ Ghana sikasɛm bɛnya nkɔsoɔ wɔ afe baako mu.

Revenue: Eyi ne sika dodow a aban nya wɔ ne dwumadi ahorow ho fi ɔman no mu ne amannɔne nyinaa.

RFJ (Rearing For Food and Jobs): bɛhyehyɛ akansi ne adwumayɛ a etu mpɔn kɛse wɔ mmoayɛn adwuma mu, a ɛbɛma ɔman no mu nneɛma a wɔyɛ no akɔ soro, atew mmoa nneɛma a wɔde fi amannɔne ba no so, adwumayɛ hokwan ahorow aba na ama mmoayɛn ho adwumayɛfo asetra atu mpɔn.
