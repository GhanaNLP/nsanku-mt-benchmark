Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐYƐ DƐN NA MPANIMFOƆ NO KAE SƐ ƐNSƐ SƐ YƐSAN KA KƐTƐ SO AHINTASƐM

Wɔka sɛ bere bi na Nyankonpon Kwame, wim-nyame no, na atew aduanbea kɛseɛ bi, na ɔduaa okro, onion, beans, garden-eggs, pepper ne pumpkin. Nhabannuru a ɛwɔ turo no mu no yɛɛ den na nhabannuru no nyin. Afei wim-nyame de gong-gong bɔɔ amanneɛ sɛ n’afuw no ayɛ nwura na obiara a obetumi atutu no a ɔmfa ne ho nhaw betumi akɔ n’anim akɔgye ne babea, Abena Nkroma (i.e. Abena ba a ɔto so akron) wɔ aware mu. Ɛno deɛ, bere a ɔkɔɔ sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden no, ɔbɛbɔɔ ne ho baabi a ntɛtea no bɔɔ no, na wɔbɔɔ no ninkunu; ɛno nso kɔɔ so bɔɔ no ninkunu. Mmarima no nyinaa kɔɔ so bɔɔ mmɔden. Wɔdii nkogu. Mprempren Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, “Me deɛ, metumi. ” Mprempren na soro-nyame no n’afuo no wɔ kwan no nkyɛn, na saa kwan no na nkurɔfoɔ fa so kɔ gua so wɔ Fida biara. Mprempren Spider no, esiane sɛ na onim saa nokwasɛm yi nti, ɔde kɔn ara na ɛkɔsiesie nwura no Fida biara. Ɛduruu bere a na ɔrehoro no, na nnipa a na wɔretwam no de wɔn ho bɛhyɛɛ no nkwa sɛ, “Hail to you wɔ wo adwuma mu, Agya Spider! ” na ɔbua no sɛ, “Thank you, Aku. ” Wɔkɔɔ so sɛ, “Afuo bi a obiara ntumi nni ho kwan, wose worewe no? ” Spider no bɛbua sɛ, “Ah, ɛyɛ ɔbaa bi nti na mebɛhyɛ me ho saa. Ne nsa biako te sɛ eyi." Na ɔbɔ ne nsa na ɔprapra ne nsa baabi a ɛyɛ no yaw, na bere a ɔyɛɛ saa no, ɔnya ahotɔ fi abufuw no mu. Obi foforo san twa mu na ɔma no akwaaba wɔ n’adwuma mu; na ɔfa ne nsa na ɔbɔ baabi a ɛyɛ no yaw. Sɛ nhwɛso no, sɛ ne sere a, ɔbɛka sɛ, "saa ɔbaa baako no, wɔka sɛ ne sere te sɛɛ" (ɔbɔ ne ho na ɔprapra). Saa kwan yi so na ɔtwaa afuo no mu de wiee. Afei ɔkɔɔ abɛka kyerɛɛ ɔsoro-nyame sɛnea ɔwiee ne afuo no mu adwuma no. Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn bisaa ɔmanpanyin no sɛ, "Wawie no ankasa? " Ɔmanpanyin no kae sɛ, "Yiw. " Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn bisaa no sɛ, "Ɔbɔɔ ne ho? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Dabi, ɔmmɛbɔɔ ne ho. " Ɔsoro Nyankopɔn faa Abena Nkroma na ɔde no maa Ananse awareɛ. Anadwo bi na Ananse ne ne yere kɔɔ home kɔgya, na yere no bisaa no sɛ, "Nanso ɛyɛ dɛn na wo nyinaa mu wo tumi yii aduan mfuo a wura no de asi hɔ no fi hɔ, sɛ obiara a ɔkɔɔ so no san bae, nanso wotumi yii no fi hɔ? " Ɛnna no, Ananse no kae sɛ, "Wosusuw sɛ me yɛ ɔkwasea? " Ná mebɔ, na obiara bɛtwam na abisa me sɛ, ‘Ananse, woyi afuw yi a obiara ntumi nnyi?’, ɛnna medii me nsa bɔɔ me honam ani baabiara a na ɛkanyan me no, na mebɔɔ no, na meka kyerɛɛ onipa no sɛ, sɛ nhwɛso no, wo nan ase te sɛ anantwinini nan ase, na ɛyɛ fɛ na ɛyɛ polished. Saa ara na ɛbaa no, metumi twaa no. Ɛnna no, ɔba a ɔtɔ so nkron Abena kae sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ ɔkyena mekae papa sɛ woaprapra wo ho (awiei). " Spider no kasa kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛnsɛ sɛ woka no ho asɛm, eyi yɛ sleeping-mat confidence. " Abena, ɔba a ɔtɔ so nkron no kae sɛ, "Mekum hwee wɔ sleeping-mat confidences ho, na mebɛka. " Abena Nkroma faa ne sle Ananse nkyɛn na ɔkɔɔ kɔtoo dan no awiei foforo. Mprempren Ananse ani beree no, na ɔkɔgyee ne nnwinnadeɛ, na ɔbɔɔ no na ɔtoo nnwom.

Abena ba a ɔtɔ so nkron yi, ɛnyɛ asɛm a ɛfata sɛ yɛgye ntɔkwa.

Yɛnbu no sɛ sleeping mat ahotoso.

'Dabi, ɔse no.

Ɔwɔ asɛm a ɛwɔ me so, nanso obi foforo wɔ asɛm a ɔnam kwan so dedaw.

Ɛnna akɔre no sɔree, na ɔkɔɔ so dae. Bere a Ananse daa hɔ bɛyɛɛ bere bi no, ɔsɔree. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abena Nkroma" ɛnyɛ nnyigyei a ɛwɔ cicada chirping no mu. Ananse kaa sɛ, "Me ne wo. " Ɔde kɔkɔɔ keteɛ ketewa bi guu so nsu no nyinaa, na ɔde guu Abena Nkroma nna-kɛteɛ no so. Ɛnna Ananse kɔɔ so dae. Bere a na ɔda n’akyi kakra no, ɔkae sɛ, "Koo! Abena Nkroma, dɛn na ɛyɛ yi? Woho guu wo so, wo mmaninyɛni, akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ wo ho nni fɛ koraa; bere a nneɛma bɛda adi no, me bɛka kyerɛ obiara. Na ɛyɛ nokware, ɛnneɛ nea wɔn nyinaa kae no sɛ bere a obiara kɔɔ saa afuo no mu no, na ɔtaa ka sɛ, 'Ababaawa a ɔbɔ nsuo...! Me renyii akutu afuo a ɛte sɛɛ me ne saa nipa no. "Afei Abena ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, 'Me srɛ wo, gyae, na ma asɛm no gu. " Spider no kae sɛ, "Me nnyae no, me asɛm no na ɛdi kan; wose wobɛka wo papa asɛm; me kae sɛ, 'Gyae'; wose, 'Dabi. ' Ɛno nti me rennyae asɛm no. Na Abena, ba a ɔtɔ so nkron, kae sɛ, "Gyae me asɛm no, na wo asɛm no nso a mekae sɛ, " oke, mebɛgya no efisɛ sɛ woanfiri me deɛ mu a, m’ani bɛwu aniwuo.” Ɛnna Ananse kae sɛ, “Meate, wopɛ no saa nti, nnyɛ kɛtɛ so ahotoso, na asɛm no awie wɔ hɔ. ” Saa na mpanyimfo no bae sɛ “Sleeping-mat confidences no nni hɔ bio. ”

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebia wobɛtumi afa no sɛ nokware, na nea aka no wobɛtumi akamfo me sɛ meka kyerɛɛ no.
