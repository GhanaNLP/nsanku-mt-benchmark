Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware.

Na Ananse a ɔyɛ akɔre no bɛyɛɛ ohiani.

KWAKU ANANSE, Akɔre no, kɔɔ hɔ kɔɔ Nyankonpon, ɔsoro-nyame no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Ma me aburo baako na fa me mma wo nkurɔfoɔ akuraa mũ (wɔ nsakraeɛ mu). " Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Wuntumi nyɛ saa. " Akɔre no buaa no sɛ, "Sɛ metumi nyɛ saa a, me nso mepa wo maame ntam, ɛnneɛ me ne me ti na mebɛtua me ntam a meabu so no ka. " Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Gye no na fa. no off.” Spider no tuu kwan na ɔkɔɔ akuraa bi ase. Ɔkae asɛm yi sɛ head-man no. “Wim-nyame asoma me; ɔse, ɛsɛ sɛ me-fa atoko baako yi kɔ baabi, na meba (baabiara) mɛma atoko yi atoto nnomaa no. ” Head-man no kae sɛ, “E! Sɛ nkokoaa ne aburow da bom a, ɛbɛyɛ yiye? Ananse buaa no sɛ, "Nea ɛsɛ sɛ woyɛ nyinaa ne sɛ wubɛma wɔn da bom. " Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, "kɔ fa no si hɔ. " Anɔpa kɔkɔɔ no, Ananse kae sɛ ɔrebɛhwehwɛ no, nanso na aburow no nni hɔ bio. Spider no kaa sɛ, “Ɔhene, nkokɔ no adidi ɔsoro-nyame no aburow no. ” Ɔhene no kaa sɛ, “Me ntumi nni eyi ho akyinnye, enti fa nkokɔ no kɔ. ” Ananse kɔgyee nkokɔ kɛseɛ bi, na ɔde kɔɔ akuraa foforɔ bi nkyɛn. Bere a ɔduruu hɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no asɛm bi sɛ, “Ɔsoro-nyame no na ɔsomaa me, na mebɛda ha, na ɛsɛ sɛ saa akokora a mekura yi, ɛne nguankuw no da. ” Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, “fa no ba na fa no to hɔ. ” Anadwo no, Ananse kɔɔ nguankuw fie na ɔkumm akokora no. Ɔfaa akokora no kɔkɔɔ na ɔde ɔponko kɛse bi abɛntenten bɔɔ. Anɔpa kɔkɔɔ no, ɔkɔɔ ɔhene panyin a ɔwɔ hɔ no nkyɛn na ɔkae sɛ, "Ma me akokora no. " Bere a anka wɔbɛkɔ akɔfa no ama no no, ɛhɔ na akokora no awu. Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ! ɛno ne ɔsoro-nyame akokora a ɔbɔɔ no akokora no. " Ɔhene panyin no kae sɛ, "Me ntumi nni ho akyinnye biara, enti fa ɔkraman bi kɔ. " Ananse faa ɔkraman no na otutuu kwan. Ɔde akuraa foforo bi kɔduruu. Ɔkɔɔ ɔpanyin fie. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Asɛm bi? " Spider no buaa no sɛ, "Asramanyankopɔn asoma me, na mekurakura nguan; ɛne anantwie no bɛda abom. " Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Hoo! Ma no nnye ahome ne nguan no." Akɔre no kae sɛ, "Dabi, efisɛ eyi ne ɔsoro nyame no nguan titiriw no, ɔne anantwi no da." Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, "kɔ fa no si hɔ afei." Akɔre no kɔɔ fa no kɔsii hɔ. Anadwo no, Ananse kɔkumm nguan no, na ɔde ne mogya guu anantwi bi ti so. Anɔpa kɔkɔɔ no, Ananse kae sɛ, "Opanin, fa me nwan ma me. " Wɔkɔɔ kuraal hɔ, na nwan no awu. Ananse kae sɛ, "Opanin, ɛyɛ Osoro-nyame nwan a anantwie akunkum no. " Opanin no kae sɛ, "Menti rentumi ne wo nni ntwitwansi wɔ asɛm yi ho, enti fa ɔpɔn a ɔkunkum no kɔ. " Ananse faa ɔpɔn no kɔɔ n'akwan mu, na ɔbaa akuraa foforo bi ase. Ɔfaa nantwinini no de kɔkyekyeree no wɔ hɔ, na ɔdaeɛ. Bere a ɔda wɔ hɔ no, wɔbɛka kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no sɛ ne nkoa no mu bi awu. Ɔhene no kae sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ wotow no gu, efisɛ me nni adaka. " Spider no kae sɛ, "Me deɛ, mepɛ no, efisɛ ɔsoro-nyame kyerɛɛ me sɛ sɛ me nya funu funu a wɔde bɛtow agu a, ɛsɛ sɛ me dane no ma ɔpɛ. " Akuraafoɔ no kae sɛ, "Dabi. " Nanso Ananse sii pi sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ wode ba me nkyɛn, na sɛ wokɔtɔn ɔpɛ no a, wubenya asuanu sika-dwetiri. £4), na wotumi fa (saa sika yi) na wo tɔ nkoa foforo." Nnipa no kaa sɛ, "Nokware wɔ mu. " Enti wɔyɛɛ nsesaeɛ no. Ananse faa funu no na ɔde ne ho kɔɔ akuraa foforo bi ase. Bere a ɔduruu hɔ no, ɔkasa kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no sɛ, "Wɔn nnyae me ba yi nkɔda ne wo mma wɔ pato (open veranda room) yi mu; seesei (ɛsɛ sɛ meka kyerɛ wo sɛ) abofra yi nni nteɛ, ɔtaa bɔ mframa anadwo, enti bere a ɔbɔ mframa no mma mma foforo no bɔ no. " Seesei na ɔbarima no awu nna mmiɛnsa. Mmofra no ne ɔno da. Mprempren wɔtee nneɛma bi a ɛyɛ huam. Wɔkae sɛ, "Saa abofra no abubu ne ho, momma yɛnko nni no. " Wɔtoo no so, titim! titim! titim! Anɔpahema, Ananse kɔɔ sɛ ɔbɛfrɛ ne ba. Ɔkɔe no, ɛhɔ na ɔwui. Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɔpanyin, mo mma akum ɔsoro-nyame nkyerɛkyerɛni a na ɔne me wɔ hɔ no. " Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, "Me nni akyinnye biara wɔ asɛm yi ho, enti wobɛtumi de mma no akɔ. " Akɔre no buaa no sɛ, "(Me ntumi nnye ntom) gye sɛ wo, ɔpanyin no, ne nnipa a wɔwɔ kuro yi mu nyinaa kɔ ɔsoro-nyame anim kɔka wo asɛm. " Ɔpanyin no bɔɔ dawuro no, na ɔkae sɛ, "nyinaa Ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ ɔsoro Nyame anim kɔka yɛn asɛm. Akɔre no dii anim, na nkurɔfo no nyinaa dii n'akyi. Bere a Ananse duu kuro no ahyeɛ no, ɔsomaa ɔmanpanyin bi kɔka kyerɛɛ ɔsoro Nyame sɛ ɔreba ne kuro bi mufo. Ɔsoro Nyame frɛɛ n'asafo no, na ɔtenaa ase wɔ n'abasafo ntam. Bere a Ananse baa mu no, ɔmaa ne dwom: "Ananse, alas, alas, I got a single grain of corn to go and gain a fowl.

Ananse, alas, alas, mede akokora kɔgyee nguan.

Mefa nguan na mɛkɔfa nantwi, mefa nantwi no na mɛkɔfa funu, mefa funu no na mɛkɔfa kuro bi mufoɔ.

Obiara kae sɛ, "Wote dwom a Ananse reto no? " Ananse maa ne dwom no so pɛnkoro bio.

"Ananse, alas, alas, me nyae aburo baako pɛ sɛ mebɛkɔ akɔfa akokoraa bi.

Ananse, alas, alas, mede akokora kɔgyee nguan.

Mefa nguan na mɛkɔfa nantwi, mefa nantwi no na mɛkɔfa funu, mefa funu no na mɛkɔfa kuro bi mufoɔ.

Ananse duu ɔsoro-nyame no anim ansa na ɔsoro-nyame no bisaa Ananse ho asɛm. Ananse kae sɛ, "Aburo baako na menyae sɛ mebɛba kurow bi mufoɔ nkyɛn; kurow bi mufoɔ wɔ hɔ a wɔgyina hɔ. " Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Nkamfo no. " (Wɔteɛm) "E! e! e!" Sorosom Nyankopɔn kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mebɔ wo aseda? " Akɔre no kae sɛ, "Me nni pɛ biara; mmeaeɛ a ɛwɔ abantenten no so no, ɛno nko ara na mepɛ. " Sorosom Nyankopɔn no kae sɛ, "Oh, oh! me nipa yi, wo bɛwu wɔ ohia mu, wo a wopuee na wode nneɛma pii bae no, nanso wose wopɛ biara. " Ɛno nti na ohia renyɛ Ananse kwan da.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
