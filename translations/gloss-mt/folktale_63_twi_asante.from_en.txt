Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

Na Abosom a wɔyɛ anyame nketewa no bae abusuakuw no mu.

Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ bi ne ne yere tenaa abɔmmɔfoɔ nsraban bi mu. Na wɔyɛ ahiafoɔ, na wɔte hɔ ko ara wɔn ho. Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no, na ɔteɛ kɔ kwaeɛ mu kɛkɛ. Bere a ɔbaa fie na ɔde mpataa sii asuo no mu, na ɔde mpataa ne mpataa no baa ne yere nkyɛn no. Ɛnneɛ na ɛbaa no, na ne yere bisaa no sɛ, “Me gye di sɛ sɛ wodi nam a, wode kɔma obi, anaasɛ wotɔn no na wodi, enti ɛnnɛ sɛ wode kɔkɔɔnam kɔkɔɔnam na woamfa nam biara amma, mɛsan akɔ me nkuraase. ” Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no tuu kwan kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu. Ɔnam n’anammɔn so, na ɔnhunuu aboa biara. Ɔkaa ne ho sɛ, “Oh, mehuu amaneɛ. ” Mprempren, bere a na ɔrekyinkyin no, ɔhunuu Duyker bi a ɔnam kwan so rekɔ nkyɛn. Ɔkae sɛ, “Wɔ wo ho no, Duyker, mebekum wo. ” Ɔbɔfoɔ no dii akyi, kyiri! kyiri! kyiri! Na ɛyɛ ne nan nnyigyei. Duyker no guanee na ɔkɔɔ nnua no ntini ntam. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Grandfather Tano, me dwane kɔ wo nkyɛn kɔbɔ me ho ban sɛ ɔbɔmmɔfo no bɛgya me. ” Ɔbɔmmɔfo no kaa sɛ, “me deɛ, me nnyim ade a wɔfrɛ no Tano no, enti fa kwan sɛ mebɛfa wo akɔma me yere. ” Tano gyinaa hɔ, na ɔbɔɔ ne nne mu kaa sɛ, "Ɔbɔmmɔfo no, gyae Duyker no na fa me kɔ fie, efisɛ mfaso bɛdɔɔso ama wo wɔ me mu. " Ɔbɔmmɔfo no gyinaa hɔ. Na ɔwosow biribiri. Ɔyii no so (Tano) na ɔde no too ne ti so. Bere a ɔde no reba no, ɔhyiaa Owuo. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ, worefa me okyeame no kɔ he? " Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no buae sɛ, "Papa, m’ani abere me; na merehwehwɛ nam sɛ mɛkɔ akɔma me yere, bere a mekɔɔ hɔ huu Duyker no, na bere a na merebɛkum no, ɔkae sɛ waguan akɔbɔ ne ho ban akɔ saa adeɛ yi so; mekae sɛ mennim hwee wɔ ho. Saa adeɛ yi na ɛkaeɛ sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ me de kɔ fie, sɛ na ɛkaeɛ sɛ mfasoɔ pii wɔ mu." Owuo bisaa Tano sɛ, "Wokɔ fie, na wunim sɛ obiara yareɛ, na me de me ntamadan, meba, me de to ne nkyɛn, ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara de ne ho hyɛ mu, enti woka kɔ fie no, nea mebɛka akyerɛ wo ne sɛ, obiara yareɛ, na meankɔfa no, mebɛbɔ wo amanneɛ. u, na wobɛnya me akatua, na saa akatua no deɛ, mogya nko ara ne nea me pɛ, na wobɛtumi anya honam no. Abosom ketewa no kae kyerɛɛ Owuo sɛ, "Nanabarima, me ate, wura. " Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no de Tano kɔɔ, na ɔne no baa fie. Bere a Tano gyina hɔ no, ɔtoo dwom bi, na wɔkaa ho. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛdaa adi, na bere a obi yareɛ no, wɔde no kɔɔ Tano. Ɔno na ɔkaa sɛ, "Wobɛwu. " Ɛyɛ nokware, ɔwui. Ɔne foforo nso kae sɛ, "Wunwu, enti fa eyi ne ɛno ba. " Bere a obi de nneɛma no kɔe no, ɔwɛmme no kae sɛ, "Mekɔsrɛ mo nkwa. " Na esiane sɛ wode abosom no ama Tano nti, nokwarem no wo ho yɛɛ yie. Saa ara na yɛte sɛ abosom nketewa no yɛ adɔe, nti nkurɔfo kɔ Tano hɔ na wɔanya ne mma no bi.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
