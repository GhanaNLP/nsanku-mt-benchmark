Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA NO, NKURƆFOƆ BI WƆN HO YƐ FƐ NA AFOFORƆ NSO WƆN HO NYƐ FƐ

Wɔka sɛ na ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ (pɛn) na ɔkɔɔ Palm-dua bi a ɛgyina hɔ wɔ nsuo no mu sɛ ɔbɛtumi asusu ho wɔ awo ho. Na ɔkɔeɛ no, mmɛdua no kaeɛ sɛ, “Mɛma wo nea wopɛ, nanso abofra a mebɛde wo bɛba no, bere a ɔsɔreɛ no, ɔrenyɛ adwuma biara. ” Ɔkaeɛ sɛ, “Mepene so saa. ” Ɛnyɛ nna mmienu, na ɛnyɛ nna mmiɛnsa, bere a ɔyɛɛ afuru na ɔwoo no. Te sɛ mmofra a wɔwɔ Spider-stories mu no, na ɛnkyɛ na ɔrenyin, ɔnyinii pɛnkoro. Na ne maame ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Abusuakuo no nyinaa mu ntaban, ɛnni hɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ wuhwɛ gye nnua-dua no, ɛnsɛ sɛ wopere bi da. ” Ɔkae sɛ, “Maame, me ate. ” Ɛnneɛ, da bi, na maame no kɔɔ hɔ ma no kɔɔ afuo mu. Ɔkɔfrɛɛ n’adɔfo no, na ɔne no hyɛɛ aseɛ dii agorɔ, na ɔkyeree no kɔn-ntɛn na ɛbɔɔ guu fam. Na ɔbaa no kae sɛ, “Waasɛm-beads a woabubu yi, kɔfa me nnuadewa bi mma menntetew no. ” Abarimaa no kɔɔ hɔ kɔtɔɔ bi na ɔde bae. Ɔbaa no kae sɛ, “Mempɛ ɛno biara a wopɛɛ no firi nnuadewa bi a ɛgyina nsuo mu. ” Nti, abofra no kɔɔ hɔ kɔhwehwɛɛ bi. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na nnuadewa bi a ɛgyina nsuo mu wɔ hɔ. Eduruu sɛ ɔforoɔ no, kra! kra! kra! na ɔkɔduruu dua no kɔn mu. Ɔde ne sekan yii, na sɛ ɔde ne nsa kae no a, mmɛdua no bɔɔ mmienu, na abofra no yafunu kɔɔ mu, na mmɛdua no bɛto mu bio. Ɛhɔ na na ɛrekyere no. Ɛnkyɛree ne maame bae no, bere a ɔduruu nsuo no ho no, ɔhunuu sunsuma bi wɔ so, na ɔkaa sɛ:

Hene ne sunsuma na ɛda nsuo no so te sɛ me ba Akwasi Kwasaman no.

Ɔkae sɛ, “Maame, ɛyɛ me. ”

Ɔkaa sɛ, “Dɛn nti na mmɛw aban no kyere wo? ”

Ɔkaa sɛ, "mekɔɔ kɔtwaa mmɛw nhama sɛ mebɛma me dɔfo na mmɛdua no bɛhyɛɛ me. "

Ɛna maame no kae sɛ, “Wo, nnuadewa yi kye no, kye no, kye no, me ba Kwasaman.

Nnua dua kyere no, kyere no, kyere no, Nnua dua kyere no.

Na maame no kɔ so. Na papa no nso baa, na ɔkaa sɛ:

“Mea ne sunsuma na ɛda nsu yi so te sɛ me ba Kwasaman deɛ?”

Ɔkae sɛ, “Papa, ɛyɛ me. ”

Ɔpapa no kae sɛ, "Adaka no kyere wo adɛn? "

Ɔkaa sɛ, "mekɔɔ kɔtwaa mmɛw nhama sɛ mebɛma me dɔfo na mmɛdua no bɛhyɛɛ me. "

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo, palm-tree yi twitwa no, twitwa no, me ba Kwasaman.

Nnua dua kyere no, kyere no, Nnua dua kyere no.

Na agya no kɔɔ fie, kɔka kyerɛɛ Ahenkuro no Panyin, na Ahenkuro no Panyin nso frɛɛ nkurɔfoɔ no nyinaa; na wɔbaa nnua no ase, na Ahenkuro no Panyin maa ne nne so sɛ:

Hene ne sunsuma a ɛda nsuo no so te sɛ me mmanana Akwasi Kwasaman deɛ?

Ɔkae sɛ, "Nana, ɛyɛ me. "

Ɔkuraa no panyin kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na Palm-tree no na ɛkyere wo? "

Ɔkaa sɛ, "mekɔɔ kɔtwaa mmɛw nhama sɛ mebɛma me dɔfo na mmɛdua no bɛhyɛɛ me. "

Akuraa no mu panyin kae sɛ, "Wo, nnuadewa yi twitwa no, twitwa no, twitwa no, me mmanana Kwasaman.

Nnua dua kyere no, kyere no, Nnua dua kyere no.

Na ahenkuro no panyin kɔɔ n’akyi, na akuraafoɔ no nyinaa bɔɔ dwom koro kyerɛɛ nnua no sɛ ɔnkyere no. Na afei n’adɔfo no baa hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ: "Hene na ɛyɛ mfonini a ɛda nsuo no so sɛ saa ara na me dɔfo Akwasi Kwasaman deɛ? "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Me ne me, wo dɔfo ankasa. "

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na mmɛw aban no kyere wo? "

Ɔkaa sɛ, "mekɔɔ kɔbɔɔ mmɛw nhama no maa wo, na mmɛw dua no bɔɔ me. "

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Palm-tree release him, release him, release him.

Nnua yi me dɔfo, Akwasi Kwasaman.

Nnua gya no, gya no, gya no, Nnua gya no. Nnua ma ne ho tɔ no. Nnua pae no, pae no, pae no. Nnua gya no, gya no, gya no, Nnua gya no.

Bere a ɔkasae saa no, mmɛdua no buee mu kakra, na n’adɔfo no yafunu puei kakra. Na ɔmaa ne nne so bio a ɔkae sɛ:

Hene ne sunsuma a ɛda nsuo no so te sɛ me dɔfo Akwasi Kwasaman deɛ?

Ɔkae sɛ, "Me ne me, wo dɔfo ankasa. "

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na mmɛw aban no kyere wo? "

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Palm-tree release him, release him, release him.

Nnua yi me dɔfo, Akwasi Kwasaman.

Na afei n'adɔfo no baa adi prɛko pɛ, na ɔbaa bɛbɔɔ n'adɔfo no bo mu, na wɔpɔn; na wɔdan kɔɔ ngo mu. Na nnipa a wɔkɔɔ hɔ ntɛm ara na wɔde bi bɔɔ wɔn anim, wɔn na wɔyɛ fɛ paa; nanso wɔn a wɔannya bi no, wɔn na wɔn ho nna hɔ. Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
