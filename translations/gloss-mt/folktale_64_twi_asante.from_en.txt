Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBƐYƐ NA ANANSE, ƆKƆMHYIA NO, KƆƆ SORO WƆ ABANTENTEN NO SO

ANANSE OGYA! ɔwaree Nsia, ɔsoro-nyame no maame ba, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ Nyame (ɔsoro-nyame no), "Wo maame Nsia wu da a wɔapaw no ama ayi no. "

Ɛbɛyɛ nnansa akyi no, wɔbae ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ Nsia, ɔsoro-nyame no maame, awu. Mprempren Ananse mogyafoɔ yɛ ‘Berekuo, hene Jay, Kwakuo, ɔkraman ne Odwan, odwan. Afei Ananse de n’ano kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Wɔaba ka akyerɛ me ayie amanneɛ, enti ɛsɛ sɛ woka me ho. " Enti Ananse maa amanneɛ kɔmaa ɔsoro-nyame no sɛ ɔreba amanneɛ no ase. Ɛnna ɔsoro nyame no hyɛɛ n'asɛmfoɔ nyinaa hyiaa te sɛ Omanhene nhyiamu. Na Ananse kae sɛ, "Anwummere bɛtɔ dɛ a, me bɛba. " Akɔre no yɛɛ bag bi, na ɔde 'Berekuo, ɔhene Jay, Kwakuo, ɔkraman, ɔkraman ne odwan' guu mu, na ɔde bag no guu ne mmati so, na ɔde ntama no kyekyeree so. Ɛnna ɔkasa kyerɛɛ abɔdeɛ no sɛ, "Me du ɔsoro-nyame no hɔ no, ɛsɛ sɛ wo su. " Bere a ɔreba ɔsoro-nyame anim no, na wɔmaa no so nne.

"Kurukusu, ku! ku! ku!

Kurukusu, ku! ku! ku! ni.

Na ɔkraman no maa ne nne.

"Kukum!"

Na Nwanwa no maa ne nne so.

Me ho yɛ me nwonwa!

Ɛno na ɛyɛɛ Kwaku Ananse deɛ. Nnipa no nyinaa kaa sɛ, "Woayɛ adeɛ, ɔbarima baako wɔ hɔ a ɔtumi su di awerɛhow wɔ akwan horow mmiɛnsa so. " Bere a na wawie n’awerɛhow no no, Ɔsoro-nyame hwɛ kwan sɛ ɔbɛnya fie pa a ɔbɛtena mu. Anɔpa foforo no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, Ɔsoro-nyame yɛɛ aduane a ɛyɛ dɛ de kɔmaa Ananse. Bere a Kwaku Ananse hunuu aduane yi no, ɔkaa ne ne ho sɛ, "Mɛnnya biara mma 'Berekuo, mɛnkyɛ biara mma Kwakuo, mɛnnya biara mma Odwan, efisɛ m'afiri bere tenten. " Ɛneɛ Ananse ate, wamma ne mogyafoɔ biara. Afei, ɔne wɔn kɔɔ so pɛpɛɛpɛ, na ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Yɛbɛkura nna nwɔtwe ayiyɔ afahyɛ no. " Nanso Ɔbɔɔfo no kae kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, "Ɛkɔm rekum me, me nkɔ"; Kwakuo ne Berekuo kaa saa asɛm no ara. Mprempren na Wim-nyame no kɔtenaa nnipa a wɔahyia no mu, na ɔmaa wɔbɔɔ Ananse amanneɛ sɛ adeɛ pa a ɔyɛeɛ wɔ da a ɛtwaa mu no, ɔbɛtumi ayɛ no bio, ɛfiri sɛ ɔne ne mpanimfoɔ ahyia (sɛ wɔbɛtie no). Ananse buae sɛ, "Me ho nte me dɛ. " Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, wim-nyame no ka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, "Woama m'ani agu fɔ. " Na 'Berekuo, ne Kwakuo, ne Odwan ka sɛ, "Yɛn nso, woama yɛn kɔm nti, yɛn ne mo bɛtew afiri abusuakuo no ho. " Nti, Kwakuo ne 'Berekuo kɔɔ kwae mu, nanso Odwan, Nguan no, tenaa fie; na Ananse no tuu ne ho bɛbɔɔ ne ho abantenten no ho; ɛno Ɛfiri sɛ na ɔfɛre ne ho.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
