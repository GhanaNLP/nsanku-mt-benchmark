Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

MPANIMFOƆ KA SƐ, "YƐNNYƐ WO BUSUANI, ANAASƐ W'AWOFOƆ, ANAASƐ OBIARA A ƆWƆ ADWUMA YƐ, SƐ ƆBISA WO A, ƆMMOA NO"

ANANSE, akɔre no, ka kyerɛɛ Aso (ne yere) sɛ, "Yɛnkɔ nnyɛ turo. " Aso kae sɛ, "Renkɔ. " Ananse kɔɔ sɛ ɔbɛtew fam. Bere a ɔsan bae no, ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nokware ankasa sɛ worenkɔ mmoa me? " Aso buaa no sɛ, "Ɔbaa taboo a ɔbɛtwa asase no so. " Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɔkyena yɛnkɔ nkɔhyew turo mu nwura. " Aso kae sɛ, "Ɔbaa taboo sɛ ɔbɛhyew turo mu nwura, na me deɛ, me so bɛkɔ akɔhyew me? " Enti Ananse kɔɔ so san bae. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛdaa adi, ɔkae sɛ, "Aso, me srɛ wo, momma yɛnkɔ bom ntu nneɛma. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me kae sɛ, menkɔ. " Ananse kae sɛ, "Me ahu amaneɛ. " Ɛnna ɔkɔtoo nneɛma nyinaa fɛɛfɛɛ. Mprempren na ɛwɔ hɔ, na nnɔbae no bae. Ɛda bi Aso kɔɔ afuo hɔ. Ɔbaa sɛ, " Kwaku, nnɔbae no aba wɔ turo no mu, na ɛnka bere bɛn na yɛbɛkɔ hɔ? " Ananse kae sɛ, " Ho! Mohammedan ɔpanyin no kaeɛ no, me nte aseɛ koraa." Aso kaeɛ sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ dɛn kasa sɛ mebɛte wo ase." Ananse kaeɛ sɛ, "mekɔ na me-san-ba. " Ananse kɔɔ hɔ kɔɔ turo no mu, na ɛhɔ na ɔgyinaa hɔ tenten, na ɔkaeɛ (wɔ ne ho sɛ), "Me, Kwaku ɔakɔre no, me nso me te ha, na mebɛtena aseɛ ama ɔbaa kɛkɛ yi ayɛ me nnaadaa? Ananse faa sekan, akofena, ne dɔte, na ɔtutu amena kɛseɛ bi. Ɔde kuadwuma no mu nneɛma nyinaa kɔhyɛɛ mu, na ɔdane n’akyi kɔɔ fie. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Aso, ba ha sɛ mebɛka wo asɛm no. " Aso bae. Ɔkae sɛ, "Mekɔɔ akɔbisaa Mohammedan bi wɔ me nkrabea ho; ɔka sɛ me nna awotwe na aka me bɛwu, enti merennya kɔ afuo mu bio, na worenkɔ hɔ bio, na me wu a, wobɛkɔ abɛsie me. Meatutu amena kɛseɛ bi wɔ hɔ, na mu wobɛsie me. " Nna awotwe no kɔɔ so, Ananse yɛɛ ne ho sɛ owui. Nnipa no maa Spider no so, na wɔde no kɔsiee no wɔ hɔ wɔ ne turo mu. Wɔsan baa fie na wɔyɛɛ ayiyie amanneɛ a ɛyɛ fɛ paa. Aso nso yɛɛ ɔkunafoɔ amanneɛ. Bere a wɔredi da a ɛtɔ so nwɔtwe ayiyie no, Aso kae sɛ, "Ah, ma me kɔhwɛ me kunu turo mu. " Aso kɔe no, na turo no da hɔ dadwa! Aso kae sɛ, "O, turo a me kunu yɛeɛ a me ani da so no, nkurɔfoɔ bi aba abɛdidi nea ɛwɔ mu no nyinaa. " Afei ɔsuu na ɔbɔɔ nsew sɛ, "mefrɛ Kwaku Ananse honhom, wo afuo mu nnɔbaeɛ nyinaa a wogyaw me no, nkurɔfoɔ aba abɛdidi. "

Kwaku Ananse maa ne nne so na ɔtoo dwom.

Wogyae nwiinwii ara! Me nsa aka me nko ara!

Gyae nwiinwii, Aso taboos a wotutu nwura.

Gyae nwiinwii a, me nko ara na meadi me deɛ.

Gyae (nwiinwii), Aso taboos rehyew turo mu nwura. Gyae (nwiinwii), medi me deɛ nko ara!

Wogyae nwiinwii mu, Aso taboo dua.

Gyae nea wode nteɛteɛ no, meadi nea me deɛ nko!

Ɛno nti, Aso gyinaa hɔ, na ɔsuro. Ɔkɔɔ fie bɛfrɛɛ Adanko, Ɔkraman no, sɛ ɔmmra sɛ ɔbɛte nsɛm bi a ɛwɔ ne kunu afuo mu hɔ. Ɔkraman no ne no kɔɔ kwan. Aso duu afuo hɔ, na ɔsan maa ne nne so bɔɔ so.

Me kunu ne Kwaku, Ananse, na ɔwuiɛ na ɔgyaa me n'afuo no, na nkurɔfoɔ baeɛ na wɔdii aduane a ɛwɔ mu no.

Kwaku ahonhom, Ananse, me koto wo, me srɛ wo, kum wɔn mma me.

Kwaku Ananse de ne nne sii hɔ.

Gyae nwiinwii a, me madi me deɛ me nko ara.

Gyae nwiinwii, Aso taboos a wotutu nwura.

Gyae nwiinwii a, me nko ara na meadi me deɛ.

Wogyae nwiinwii, na Aso taboos rehyew turo mu nwura no.

Gyae nwiinwii, me nko ara meadidi me deɛ!

Wogyae nwiinwii mu, Aso taboo dua.

Wogyae nteɛteɛ, meadi me nko ara! Wogyae nteɛteɛ!

Efi bere a Ɔdomankoma yɛɛ mmea, woahu ɔbaa a ɔmmoa ne kunu?

Wogyae nteɛteɛ, meadi me de nko, wogyae nteɛteɛ nko!

Ɔkraman no kae sɛ, "Me awie atie, yɛnkɔ fie sɛ mebɛka mo nea ɛwɔ mu nyinaa. " Ɔne Aso na ɛkɔɔ fie. Hare kae sɛ, "Anansesɛm kae sɛ, ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ, 'Yɛnkɔ tew turo'; wo kae sɛ wo renni nkɔ; 'Yɛnkɔ hyew' (nnua a ɛyɛ kusuu); na wo kae sɛ wo renni nkɔ; 'Yɛnkɔ nhyehyɛ'; na wo kae sɛ wo renni nkɔ; enti wubetumi agyae su, efisɛ ɔredi nea ɛyɛ n’ankasa de, efisɛ, efi bere a ɔbɔfo no bɔɔ no, ɔnnyaa ɔbaa bi a ɔmmoa ne kunu. " Ɔnkae sɛ, efisɛ wadu baabi a wadi ne de nyinaa pɛ. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "obiara wɔ adwuma biara ho na ɔsrɛ wo, boa no. "

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
