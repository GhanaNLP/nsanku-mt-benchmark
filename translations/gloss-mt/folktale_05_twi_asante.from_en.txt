SƐNEA ƐBAA NO, AKRAMAN TAA TE WƆ EFĨ MU.

Ɛbɛyɛɛ bere bi a na akraman ne krak baako te, na krak no kae sɛ, “Yɛnkɔ nkɔtwa sanku bi nkɔbɔ so. ” Wɔde kwan kɔɔ kwae no mu, na wɔbɛtoo silk-cotton dua bi. Bere a Vulture no hunuu silk-cotton dua no, ɔkaa sɛ, “Eyi yɛ me dea, efisɛ me de, me ne ɔpanyin, enti mebɛtwa kɛseɛ yi. ” Krak no kaa sɛ, “mete. ” Krak no nso, bere a ɔrehwɛ n’ani so no, ɔhunuu “Cedar” dua bi a ɛgyina hɔ, ɔno nso kɔtwaa no. Wɔn baanu nyinaa bɔɔ sankuo. Bere a Vulture no bɔɔ ne sankuo no, ɛyɛɛ saa ara.

Futuru! Futur no! Meanya me deɛ!

Vulture Kwakye! Me nsa aka me deɛ ntɛm ara!

Bere a akokoraa no bɔɔ ne ho no, na ɛyɛ sɛɛ.

Akraman, Kwakwa!

Akraman, Kwakwa!

Wɔkɔɔ so dii agorɔ, na ɔsoro-nyame no tee, na ɔsomaa Hawk no sɛ ɔmmɛfrɛ wɔn; na Hawk no bae, (na ɔbɔɔ wɔn nsamu) "Ago! " Na wɔbuae, "Ame! " Ɔkae sɛ, "ɔsoro-nyame no ka sɛ wate asaw-nnwom bi wɔ ha na ɛno nti ɛsɛ sɛ wode kɔ na ɔmmɛte. " Abɔdeɛ no kɔɔ ɔsoro-nyame no anim; na wɔsan bɔɔ, na wɔsan bɔɔ, na wɔsan bɔɔ, na ɔsoro-nyame no s mmoa, "Meate sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ; nnwom mmienu no yɛ dɛ, enti mede mo mu biara adeɛ bi ma. " Afei ɔsoro-nyame no kɔfa adaka mmienu bi kɔto hɔ, baako no akyi yɛ fɛ paa, emu yɛ fĩ a ɛdɔɔso saa, baako foforo nso, nea ɛyɛ tan paa, sika kɔkɔɔ a ɛdɔɔso saa wɔ mu. Ɔsoro-nyame kasae sɛ, "Mo mmienu biara mmra na mo nnye. " Vulture no kaa sɛ, "Me ne ɔpanyin, enti ma me kɔfa bi kan. " Bere a ɔkɔe no, ɔyii nea ɔyɛ fɛ paa, bere a kɔkɔɔ no nso faa nea ɛyɛ tan paa. Wɔmaa wɔn so kɔɔ wɔn akuraa ase. Bere a wɔkɔbuee wɔn no, vulture no, bere a ɔhwɛɛ ne mu no, filthy slime. Wɔ Crow’s no, bere a ɔhwɛɛ mu no, sika kɔkɔɔ a ɛhyerɛn kɔkɔɔ sɛ biribiara. Ɛnkyɛree koraa na ɔbɔfo bi baa hɔ bɛka kyerɛɛ Vulture no sɛ, "Me wɔsoma me sɛ meka kyerɛ sɛ wo mma mma no awu. " Ɔno (Vulture no) kae sɛ, "Crow, ma me sika kɔkɔɔ baako de nkyekyere me nsa ho sɛ mɛkɔ ayie no ase. " Ɔnyaa nugget no na ɔde nkyekyeree no ho, na ɔkɔɔ ayie no ase. Ɔduruu hɔ no, ɔkaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ ɔkɔ fa ne ho tɔ no, ɔkɔtoo fam, nanso bere a ɔrebɛsɔre no, nugget no tɔeɛ na ɛyeraeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, Crow no agyapadeɛ ayera, na ɛbɛyɛ dɛn na metumi asan akɔ fie? " Ɛneɛ ɔtu bɛdɔteɛ no mu na ɔhwehwɛɛ mu. Ɛno nti na wobɛhu Vulture no koraa ɔrekyinkyin, ɔrekyinkyin, wɔ nsuo no mu, sɛ ɔrehwɛ kɔkɔɔ no nugget bɛma no aba.

Ɛno nti, mpanimfoɔ ka sɛ, "Greed nnyɛ pa. "

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebia wobɛtumi afa no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no nso wobɛtumi nkamfo me.
