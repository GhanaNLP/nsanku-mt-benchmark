Na Okoto kɔkɔɔ no nyaa akoradeɛ, na aberewa, ɔpanyin no, nyaa ti nhwi fitaa.

Ɔbaa bi woo ne ba; bere a ɔde no kɔe no, ɔkae sɛ, "merenkɔ. " Ɔno (maame no) kae sɛ, "ntena hɔ, wo ne wo nyansa akwan no. " Daa bere a ɔde no kɔe no, ɔkae sɛ, "merenkɔ. " Ɛnna bi ɔkae sɛ, "kɔ nsuo nkyiri na fa mma me. " Ɔkae sɛ, "merenkɔ. " Na ɔbaa panyin no bɔɔ no na ɔkae sɛ, "Wo nko ara tena hɔ, na wo bɛhu. " Na t Abofra no kasae na ɔkae sɛ, "Me bɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ 'I will see' sɛ mebɛhu sɛnea ɛteɛ. " Na ɔde ne ho kɔɔ kwan no so, na ɔkɔɔ so ntɛm paa, (na ne nan a ɔde tu mmirika no nnyigyei te sɛ) yiridi, yiridi, yiridi. Akyiri yi ɔduruu akuraa bi ase, na ɔhunuu ɔbaa panyin bi a ɔte hɔ. Bere a ɔhunuu no no, na ɔrebɛtwe ne ho. Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, "Me mmanana, wo ntwetwe wo ho sɛ meahu wo. " Na abofra no kɔɔ anim; na ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na nsu a ɛfiri w'aniwa mu no abɔ wo ho adagyaw yi? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me maame ne me na yɛte hɔ, da biara a ɔsɔre no, ɔka sɛ, 'Wotwɛn hɔ nko na wubehu,' na ɔbɔ me, enti mekae sɛ, mebɛhwehwɛ 'mebɛhu' sɛ mebɛtumi ahu (dɛn na ɛbɛtumi ahu). Ɛte sɛ nea ɔbaa no a ɔyɛ ɔpanyin kae sɛ, "Me ne no. " Afei ɔkaa sɛ, "Kɔ fie no akyi hɔ tu yam ba; bere a wobɛkɔ adu hɔ no, nea ɔbɛka sɛ, 'tu me mmu, tu me mmu, tu me mmu,' nntu no; nanso nea ɔbɛka sɛ, 'nntu me mmu, nntu me mmu,' ɔno deɛ, tu no. " Ɔbuae ho! Nokwarem no ɔkɔɔ hɔ ɔkwan bi so yam bi ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Tu me u p, dig me up, dig me up;" afoforo nso kae sɛ, "Ntu me ase, ntu me ase, ntu me ase. " Wɔn a wɔkae sɛ, "Ntu me ase," wɔn na ɔtutu. Na ɔne wɔn bae; na ɔpanyin no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Skin them and put them on the fire. " Ɔde wɔn kɔɔ ogya no so, na bere a wɔayɛ no no, ɔde wɔn guu prɛte so, na ɔde kɔtoo ɔpanyin no anim. Na ɔkaa sɛ, "Kɔ kuro no akyi, na hwɛ rack bi a nam da so, hwe nam no mu na fa nea wopɛ. " Na abofra no fa bi ba, na ɔde too ogya no so yɛɛ soup. Bere a ɔwiee no, ɔde too ɔbaa no anim. Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, "Woawie? " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Yiw, Nana. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Mbra na bɛbɔ fufu no. " Na ɔbaa panyin no kɔtoo n'akyi, ɔkae sɛ, "Boaboa yam a wɔanoa no ano, hwie gu me hwene mu, na bɔ no. " Abofra no kae sɛ, "Wei yɛ nwanwa sɛ yɛbɔ fufu wɔ nipa hwene mu, ɛno na ɛna ka ho asɛm. " Wei nyinaa na ɔkae wɔ n'anim. Ɔkɔɔ bɔɔ ne ho adwene, nanso bere a ɔbɔɔ ne ho adwene no, ɔbɔɔ no dwoodwoo paa. Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, "Mobɔ no den, sɛ wunbɔ no den a, wo moma bɛpae. " Na ɔbɔɔ no, pi! pi! pi! na bere a ɔwiee no, ɔde soup no guu so, de sii hɔ wɔ ɔbaa panyin no anim. Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, "Me nana ba, fa me din ka na fa nni. " Na ɔkae sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ wo Ɔbaa panyin nana. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Ee! ɔnhunuu sɛ ɔbɛdi. " Na ɔbaa panyin no dii ne nyinaa. Nna nwɔtwe na ɔyɛɛ no saa daa. Na da bi, abofra no kɔɔ asuo no mu kɔbɔɔ nsuo. Krak huu no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na obi ba ayɛ te sɛɛ? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Nana-baa-baa bi wɔ hɔ a me ne no te, na bere a me noa aduane na me de ma no no, ɔka sɛ, 'Ka me din na fa di'; Meka sɛ, 'Wɔfrɛ wo Nana-baa-baa,' na ɔka sɛ, 'Ee! Na woanhu ɔkwan a wofa so di aduan, na afei ɔdidi, ɛno nti na me ho yɛ me hare." Krak no kae sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ no ɔbaa panyin Mosono." Abofra no kae sɛ, "Ho." Na ɔde ne nsuo-kuku too ne ti so. Krak kae sɛ, "wo nwerɛ wo werɛ fi bere a woresan rekɔ. " Abofra no tuu kwan, ne bere nyinaa a na ɔreba no, na ɔkɔ so kae sɛ, "Old-woman-Mosono, Mosono, Mosono. " Bere a ɔduruu kuro no akyi no, ɔwerɛ fi, na ɔhwe aseɛ na ne nsuo-kuku no bubui, na ɔkɔɔ fie. Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "Nsuo-kuku no wɔ he? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me reba no, me hwee ase, na ɛbɔeɛ. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa foforɔ bi na wokɔ. " Na ɔde kɔeɛ, na Crab no kaa sɛ, "Woasan aba ansa? " Na ɔkaa sɛ, "Saa din a woka kyerɛɛ me no, meafiri hɔ afira no, na meaba abisa no bio. " Crab no kaa sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ no Old-woman-Mosono. " Na ɔkaa sɛ, "Ho. " Ɔkɔɔ so kaa no nyinaa. Ɔkwan a ɔfa so kɔ fie, na bere a ɔduruu fie no, ɔde (pot) no sii hɔ. Ɔbaa papahwekwa no kae sɛ, "Kɔtu yam no na fa ba. " Ɔyɛɛ ne nyinaa sɛ nea na ɔyɛ daa no, na ɔde aduane no bae, na ɔde too ɔbaa no anim, na ɔbaa no kae sɛ, "Mention my name na wo nso didi. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ wo Old-woman-grandmother, wo din na wo nso didi. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mekyerɛ me din ankasa. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo din ankasa anaa? " Ɔbaa papahwekwa no kae sɛ, "Yiw. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ wo wo din Old-woman-Mosono. " Ɔbaa papahwekwa no kae sɛ, " Ɔkae sɛ, "Fa na wodi. " Abofra no de ne nsa kae na ɔdii. Ɔbaa panyin no fii kwan no so na ɔkae sɛ, "Merehwehwɛ nipa ankasa a ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ me din ne Mosono. " Na ɔkɔɔ asutene no so na ɔne Obonto Ya, minnow no hyiaa, na ɔbɔɔ no bɔne sɛ, "Wo 'Bonto, wo maame! wo 'Bonto, wo papa! So woka kyerɛɛ me mmanana ba sɛ wɔfrɛ me Mosono? Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ me. " Ɔpanyin no huu sɛ Adwen' a ɔte hɔ no gyina nkyɛn, na ɔtoo no sobo sɛ, "Wo Adwen', wo maame! mother! mother! wose Adwen', wo papa! father! father! So woka kyerɛɛ me mmanana ba no sɛ wɔfrɛ no Mosono? Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ me. " Ɔhuu sɛ Crab no gyina hɔ. Ɔbɔɔ no so sɛ, "Wo Krak, wo maame! maame! maame! Wo Krak, wo papa! papa! papa! Ɛyɛ wo na woka kyerɛɛ me mmanana ba sɛ me te Accra, me yɛ ahemfo mogyahura, na wɔfrɛ me Mosono? Crab no kae sɛ, "Yiw, me na meka kyerɛɛ no, na adɛn nti na wama obi ba ayɛ teateaa saa? " Na ɔbaa no bɛyɛɛ abufuw, na ne calabash a ɔkura no, ɔmaa so na ɔtow guu no bɔɔ crab no. Ɛno nti na crab no wɔ n’akyi akono. Ɛbɛdaa no wɔ Crab no akyi na akoradeɛ biara nni hɔ. Na Crab no nso faa dɔteɛ fitaa, na ɔde guu ɔbaa no ti so. Ɛno nti na ɔbaa no ti so yɛ fitaa.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebia wobɛtumi afa no sɛ nokware, na nea aka no wobɛtumi akamfo me sɛ meka kyerɛɛ no.
