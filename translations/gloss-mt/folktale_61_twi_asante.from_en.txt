Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea ɛbaa no, bere a obi kɔ dan mu anadwo no, ɛyɛ sum.

Wɔka sɛ Kuntukurunku, Odds ne Kuntunamoa, Ends hwee fii ɔdan no atifi so baa fam. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Tete-ɛna-asase, woyɛ treatifi. " Ɛna-asase kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woka sɛ me yɛ treatifi? " Wɔbuae sɛ, "Efisɛ woantu amena mma yɛn nna mu. " Tete-ɛna-asase no dan kɔɔ Prae, Broom no nkyɛn na ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo ano yɛ nnomaa nsu. " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wodi me ayayade? " Tete-ɛna-asase no kae sɛ, "Sɛ m’adi wo ayayade a, m’adi wo ayayade; sɛ woan-yɛ. Wote nea enti a ɛte saa ase nti gyae bisa. Bɔɔdɔm no dan kɔɔ Abena, akyi-ntama (a wɔtow nwura gu mu no) kae sɛ, "Wofa (nneɛma) kɔtow gu. " Strip-of-bark no kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wobɔ me ahohora? " Broom no kae sɛ, "(Anyhow) mayɛ saa. " Strip-of-bark no nso kasa kyerɛɛ Sumina, Midden-heap, sɛ, "wo nsa ka na wode sie. " Midden-heap no kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wobɔ me ahohora? " Ɔ (Strip-of-bark) r Ebed, "Mea mewe wo, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a, gyae bisa." Midden-heap nso kasa kyerɛɛ Abed, "Wo kɔn no nyinaa ayɛ nwura." Nnua dua kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woyɛ me ayayade? " Midden-heap buae sɛ, "Mewee wo, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a, ɛnde gyae bisa." Palm-tree nso danee n’akyi kyerɛɛ Teta, antammum tuntum ketewa no na ɔkae sɛ, "Wo aso yɛ bla ck. "Ant-black-small kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wopira me? " Palm tree no buae sɛ, "Me ayɛ wo ayayade na sɛ wunnim nea enti a, gyae bisa. " Ant-black-small, ɔno nso kɔɔ Esono, asono a ɔwɔ hɔ no, na ɔkɔɔ ne nan ase; na ɔbɔɔ asono no serew. Bere a ɔrekyekyere ne nan so no, ɔtutu Tamiriwa, Black-snail no, ne Black-snail no aniwa ase, na Black-snail no aniwa hyɛɛ aseɛ nsuo guu no, na ɔde ne nsa popaa no wɔ efan afifideɛ bi a ɛbɛn hɔ so. Mprempren, aduane a ɔpanyin-nananom di no, efan afifideɛ na ɛwɔ soro a na Tamiriwa, Black-snail no, apopa n’aniwa so nsuo no. Kraman, ɔkraman, twaa, na ɔ (ɔpanyin-nananom) frɛɛ ɔkraman no de nnyinaa (a ɔde kɔe no) kɔe na ɔmaa ɔkraman no de kɔɔ ne ti so. Bere a ɔkraman rekɔ no, ɔnyaa kola-nut, na ɔkyekyɛɛ biako mu abien, na ɔtow biako guu Okagya deɛ, Squirrel no amena no mu ano. Okagya, ɔno nso, faa kola-nut no de bɔɔ Onyina, Silk-cotton-dua no. Silk-cotton-tree no nso nyaa ne bo fuw no. Ɔhunuu sɛ abofra ketewa bi retwa mu, na ɔtwaa ne nkorabata no bɔɔ no kum no. Mprempren, na abofra ketewa no na ɔyɛ Nyankonpon, Sorosom ba. Ɛhɔ na wɔde Silk-cotton-tree no kɔɔ Sorosom anim. Wɔbisaa no (Silk-cotton-dua no) sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ ne nyinaa? " Silk-cotton-dua no kaa sɛ, "Na me gyina hɔ wɔ me ankasa me baabi, bere a Squirrel yi—abofra a ɔyɛ no—(dared) bɛgyee kola-nut bɛbɔɔ me, me deɛ, bere a abofra yi retwam no, na me dwene sɛ ɔyɛ Okagya, Squirrel no, na meayi me nkorabɛn bi abɔ no, na ɛda adi sɛ ɛyɛ wo ba. " Nyame, Wim-nyame kae sɛ, "Fa no kyekyere no kɔ dua no ho, na frɛ Okagya, Squirrel no, mmra. " Okagya bae, ɔkae sɛ, "Sire, wonim sɛ me taboo ne kola-nut no, nanso Okraman, Dog no, kɔgyee bi, na ɔtow no kɔɔ me amena no ano, na bere a mebɛtow no kɔe no, ɛkɔbɔɔ Old-man-silk-cotton-dua no. " Wim-nyame no kae sɛ, "Fa no, kyekyere no kɔ dua no ho, na frɛ Dog no mmra. " Ɛho kɔɔ frɛɛ ɔkraman no sɛ ɔmmra. Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Agya, na mekyinkyin me ankasa me baabi—seesei wote sɛnea me yɛ (delicate) no—bere a ɔgyaw-nananom de ne nnua a wɔaboaboa ano too me ti so no; seesei Okagya, Squirrel no, ɛyɛ ɔno a ɔda n’amena wɔ afuo a ɔgyaw-nananom ayɛ no sɛ ɔbɛnya biribi adi, nanso na me na ɔgyaw-nananom maa ɔde ne nnua no kɔe; Me nso, me de kola-nut a" na ɔtow no kɔɔ n’amena no ano. "Wanyankopɔn no kae sɛ, "Fa no, kyekyere no dua, na frɛ ɔbaa panyin no sɛ ɔmmra. " Ɔbaa panyin bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ah, me mmanana, monhwehwɛ me n’ano mu, me a metumi nwe nam no, efan nhaban na meye; afei nso, mehohoro Bosompra; me taboo ne (ɛno nti) Tamiriwa, Black-snail no; Dog no nko ara na onim eyi, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ Tamiriwa, na ɔmaa no faa nsuo fii n’ano mu, na ɔde guu efan nhaban a me tena so no so. " Ɛno nti na mehyɛɛ me nnua ma ɔde kɔeɛ." Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Oh, fa no kyekyere no kɔfa no dua bi ho, na kɔfa Tamiriwa, ɔkraman tuntum no. " Ɔkraman tuntum no bae. Ɔkae sɛ, "Sire, na me wɔ me deɛ bi so, hwɛ, ason kɛseɛ bi bɛhyɛɛ me ani ase, na me kɔfa efan (nhaban) bɛhohoro no. " Ɛno nko ara ne nea menim fa ho." Wɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "fa no na kyekyere no wɔ dua so, na fa Esono, ɔsono no, bɛba. " Wɔkɔɔ kɔgyee Esono. Ɔsono kae sɛ, "Nnansa bi a atwam no, na me te hɔ, na me honam ani hyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ so, na bere a mehwɛɛ beaeɛ no—me srɛ sɛ mepae asɛm no—ɔkwasea kɔkɔɔ-kɔkɔɔ nsenia ketewa yi anya me honam mu sɛ ɔrebɛhwehwɛ nam na wanyin kɛse. Na bere a merebɔ me nan no, ɛkɔtoo Tamiriwa, Black-snail no so. " Sorosom Nyankopɔn aka sɛ, "Fa no kyekyere no wɔ dua so, na fa no ba. Tetea, ant tuntum ketewa no. "Sɛ Tetea bae a, ɔkae sɛ, "Agya, palm tree bi wɔ hɔ; bere a ɛso no, ɛyɛ tuntum nea edi kan; bere a me, ant tuntum ketewa no, nam so no, ɛma ɛnyin. Ɛda a ɛtwaa toɔ no na me wɔ hɔ, bere a Palm-tree no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ me aso yɛ tuntum, na me nketenkete no nyinaa me dwen sɛ mebɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ asono hɔ nam so adidi sɛ mebɛnyin ayɛ kɛse.” Wim-nyame no kae sɛ, “Fa no kyekyere no, na kɔfrɛ Palm-tree no sɛ ɔmmra. ” Abe, Palm-tree no, bae; ɔkae sɛ, “Sire, saa na meka: Me ne Sumina, Midden-heap no, te ase. Sɛ wɔka bom a, na nwura a wɔtow gu ne so no dɔɔso dodo na ontumi nnyina ano bio a, mepene bi so na mekyekyere no wɔ me kɔn mu; nanso ɔde me dii dwuma bɔne sɛ mehyɛ nwura ho. Ɛno nti, me deɛ, m’ani bɛyɛɛ me kɔkɔɔ, na me nso mfa Little-black-ant no bɔɔ abufuo. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, “Fa no kyekyere no, na frɛ Sumina, Midden-heap, sɛ ɔmmra. ” Midden-heap no bae, ɔkae sɛ, “Sire, Abena, Strip-of-bark, hwan bere a wɔrekyekyere nwura wɔ dan bi mu no, wɔde no ma no sɛ ɔmmɛfa no mmra me. Ɛda a ɛtwaa toɔ no, a na ɔnni bɔne biara, ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, ‘Wode nea wunya no sie’ (i. e. Na wɔyɛ anibereɛ kɛseɛ); me kae sɛ, ‘a’, na bere a mmɛdua no abɛn no, me nso me bɔɔ no ahohoro. Wim-nyame no kae sɛ, “Fa no fa no kyekyere no dua, na monfrɛ Abena, Strip-of-bark, sɛ ɔmmra. ” Nnua-a-wɔ-ayiyi no bae, ɔkae sɛ, “Sire, bere a Prae, Broom, bɔ dan bi mu no, sɛ wɔamfa (sweepings) no amma me a, bere a mframa bɔ no, nneɛma a wɔtow gu no nyinaa san ba bio; seesei, da a ɛtwaa toɔ no, ɛwom sɛ menyɛɛ no hwee de, ɔ- kae sɛ, ‘Wofa nneɛma nko de mo to hɔ.’ Ɛno nti, me nso me bo fuwii, na me kɔbɔɔ Sumina, a ɔyɛ Midden-heap no, abusuu. Wim-nyame kae sɛ, “Fa no, na mokyekyere no log ho, na monfrɛ Prae, Broom no, sɛ ɔmmra ha. ” Broom no bae, ɔkae sɛ, “Agya, tete-ɛna-asase no da hɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ, ne soro yɛ fĩ, enti bere biara a me sɔre, me bɔ fĩ no nyinaa ho ma no yɛ. fɛfɛɛfɛ; nanso da a ɛtwaa toɔ no, Asase-ɛna kasaeɛ me sɛ, ‘Wo ano yɛ nkokora,’ na me nufu nso bɛyɛɛ me dɛɛdɛɛ, na me kɔɔ so bɛbɔɔ Abena, Strip-of-bark no. Asa-nyame no kaeɛ sɛ, “Fa no kyekyere no ho, na frɛ Asase-ɛna sɛ ɔmmra. ” Asase-ɛna baeɛ no, ɔkaeɛ sɛ, “Sire, ɛhɔ na me da; wɔrebu me; me nka hwee da; wɔyɛ nneɛma nyinaa. Sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a me so no, nanso menka hwee da. Mprempren ɛda a ɛtwaa toɔ no a na me wɔ hɔ no, Kuntukurunku ne Kuntunamoa, Odds and Ends, bɛtoo me so, na mekae sɛ, ‘O! Asɛm no ne dɛn? na wɔkae sɛ, ‘Moanbɔ amena a yɛbɛtumi de atena ase, na mo ne mo dɔteɛ nyinaa nni mfasoɔ mma obiara. ’ Ɛno nti, me nso mebɔɔ Prae, a ɔyɛ Broom no abɔne. ” Sky-god kae sɛ, “Fa no na fa no kyekyere no wɔ dua bi ho, na kɔfa Odds and Ends. ” Odds and Ends bae. Wɔkae sɛ, “Ogya, na yɛwɔ abantenten no so, na yɛredi agorɔ, na yɛhwee ase na yɛyɛɛ yɛn ho; ɛno nti yɛbɔɔ Asase-maame ahohora sɛ ɔyɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ. ” Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, “Councillors, yɛate asɛm yi nyinaa, enti yɛnsɔre nkɔ dan mu nkɔhwɛ adanseɛ no na yɛmmra mfa atemmuo mma. ” Enti wɔsɔreɛ kɔɔ dan bi mu, na wɔtoo mu. Ɛno nti, bere a wokɔ dan mu anadwo no,—kusuu, Ɔsoro-nyame ne n’Agyinatufoɔ rehyia wɔ hɔ.

Ɛno, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan mma me.
