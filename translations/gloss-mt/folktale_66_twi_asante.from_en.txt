Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

KOKOKYINAKA, TURACU A NE TI YƐ BRUU NO, ANKƆ ANANSE AKƆRE NO NKYƐN SƐ ƆBƐBƆ BOSEA.

BERE BI NA KOKOKYINAKA NNOMAA NO KƆƆ ANANSE, SPIDER NO NKYƐN KƆHWEHWƐƐ SIKA KƆKƆƆ MFUTUMA A ƐYƐ ASUANU MU DURU (£4). Mprempren, sɛnea Ananse ankasa nni biara no, kɔkɔɔ n'adamfo, nwansena Ntontom, kɔgyee bi kɔmaa n'adamfo, Kokokyinaka nnomaa no. Ɛda a Mosquito no atwa mu na na Mosquito no de atua bosea no, na Mosquito no ka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, “Fa me sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no ba. ” Ananse buae sɛ, “Kokokyinaka nnomaa no te Silk-cotton-tree no atifi nti, fa wo sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no nsa ka. ” Mosquito no bisaa sɛ, “Ɛbɛyɛ dɛn na mɛfa so anya no; abɔdeɛ no tumi tu kwan. ” Spider no mmuae ne sɛ, “Fa nnua no kɔ, na wuhu. , dua no bɛbu, na woakyere Kokokyinaka nnomaa no, na woanya wo sika dɔteɛ.” Mosquito no kɔɔ kɔtwaa dua no. Bere a na ɛrebɛbu no, Kokokyinaka nnomaa no firi hɔ kɔeɛ, pa bu bu bu! Ɛnna Nwansena no baa, na ɔkae sɛ, “Congratulations, Anansesɛm. ” Ɛno nti na yɛwɔ asɛm bi a ɛkɔ so sɛ, “Abɔdeɛ no a wɔfrɛ no Kokokyinaka nnomaa no amfa boro Anansesɛm ho sɛ ɔnkɔkɔ ntwa Silk-cotton-tree no. ” Wei, me asɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi nsan mma me.
