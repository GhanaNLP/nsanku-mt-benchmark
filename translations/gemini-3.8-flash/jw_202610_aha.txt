SUNDAY'S PROGRAM ENCOURAGED GOD'S PEOPLE TO FOCUS ON THE PREACHING WORK

In late May 1926, the Bible Students gathered at a convention in London, England. At that time, all the newspaper printers in Britain went on strike, so it was difficult to find people to print our publications. Therefore, the brothers at Brooklyn Bethel worked long hours to print 117,000 copies of the booklet entitled The Standard for the People, which was distributed at the convention.

On Friday, May 29, the brothers and sisters began to work that morning. The activity that took place during the session that day encouraged all the brothers: “All Bible Students are invited to serve as volunteers for Jesus and the Kingdom. They should go directly to Alexandra Palace, and the brothers in the Service Department will show them what to do.”

Over 100,000 booklets were distributed on that day. The July 15, 1926 Watchtower reported: “The brothers were very happy. Because, they felt that they had obeyed Jehovah's command to preach the word in the morning.”

That day, Brother Rutherford gave a discourse at the Royal Albert Hall, and he spoke about what would befall the world's rulers. When the meeting place was opened, it was not long before about 13,000 people filled the hall. And those who did not get the opportunity to enter the hall listened to the program outside through the loudspeakers that had been mounted outside.

In this speech, Brother Rutherford explained that the world rulers would fall because they had rejected Jehovah and his Kingdom. Brother Rutherford spoke against the League of Nations arrangement, and foretold that it would come to an end.

MEETING AT 117 ADAMS STREET

PSALM 94

We Delight in God's Word

Base Your Faith on a Sure Foundation

When you studied the Bible with Jehovah’s Witnesses, what convinced you that you had found the “one faith” or the only true way God wants us to worship him? (Eph. 4:4-6) Was it the love that exists among Jehovah’s people that drew you to become a Jehovah’s Witness? Was it their honesty and good conduct that touched your heart? Real love and peace within Jehovah’s organization are what attract many people to become Jehovah’s Witnesses. And this is understandable, because love and fine conduct are what identify true Christians.—John 13:35; 1 Pet. 2:12.

But what if a brother does something against us or does not treat us well? Or what if a brother commits a serious sin like what happened in the Corinthian congregation about which Paul spoke? (1 Cor. 5:1) If our faith can be strong, it should not depend only on our brothers' actions. How can you know that your faith stands on a solid foundation?

How can you build your faith on a solid foundation?

If we can base our faith on a good foundation, we must continue to strengthen the confidence we have in the basic teachings of the Bible. How can this be done? Consider this.

One day, Jesus said something: that they must eat his flesh and drink his blood. But this statement offended many of his disciples, so they stopped following Jesus. They said: “This teaching is hard.” Because what Jesus said offended them so much, they no longer followed him.—John 6:56-60, 66.

At this, Jesus asked his apostles: “You do not want to go also, do you?” Peter answered him: “Lord, to whom shall we go? You have the words of eternal life. We have come to believe and to know that you are the Holy One of God.”—John 6:67-69.

Why did Jesus’ words not stumble Peter and the other apostles? Because their faith was based on a solid foundation and not on their feelings. Peter said: “We have believed and have come to know that you are the Holy One of God.” Therefore, these faithful apostles knew what they believed, and the reason why they believed it. They were fully convinced that Jesus was the Messiah, because they compared what Jesus said and what he did with what they had learned in the Scriptures. The strong conviction they gained through Bible study made their faith firm, and when other people stumbled over what Jesus said, they did not stumble.

What can we learn from this example? What we know has far greater power than how we feel. Why? Because the Bible truth we have learned, such as God’s name, his purpose, and the truth, has not changed at all, but the actions of other people can change how we feel.

Therefore, if we have faith in the Bible and have confidence in the truth that Jehovah's organization teaches us, no matter what other people do, we will never stop serving Jehovah.

Indeed, the love and good conduct shown by our brothers and sisters strengthen us. And that is also how Jehovah wants us to feel. But even if the actions of some cause our feelings to change, continuing to have faith in the truth we have learned from the Bible will enable us to remain firm in the truth. This gives us confidence that we belong to the only organization that worships Jehovah in truth. Therefore, this truthful message will not change when someone does something against us.

When we have a good study routine, it strengthens our confidence in the truth we have learned from God’s Word. And this will help us to continue having great confidence that we are following the only way God wants us to worship him. If so, our faith will rest on a solid foundation, and we will not be easily shaken by whatever other people say or do.—Ps. 16:8; see the section entitled “Support Jehovah’s Organization.”

If our faith is based only on the actions of other people, then it does not stand on a good foundation.

Support Jehovah's Organization

Imagine that you are traveling by bus to a very distant place. When the bus starts moving, there is no need for you to be anxious.

However, while you are in the bus, some things that happen in the bus might annoy you. It could happen that some passengers in the bus make noise, or the driver might even speak harshly to you. But all these things should not make you think that you took the wrong bus. You only need to remain in the bus until you reach your destination.

So to speak, all of us have embarked on a journey to Jehovah’s new world. And we are very glad that we belong to Jehovah’s organization. However, no matter what others do, we have only one organization that is leading us into God’s new world. Therefore, if we have very great confidence in the truth we have learned from the Bible and have confidence in the organization that teaches us that truth, we will continue on our journey. And if it is so, our faith will be strong.

Jehovah Helps Those Who Have Faith in Him

Sister Emma worked three days a week at a hotel. Each day, she worked for eight hours. She asked the person the hotel manager had appointed to supervise the work to allow her to take one of her workdays off to attend a regional convention. Instead of the supervisor granting Emma's request, he did not do so. Rather, he told her that from that time on, she had to work five days every week. And he added that if she could not, then she should quit the job. Emma still wanted to go to the convention, because she had strong faith that even if she lost her job, Jehovah would bless her.

When Emma submitted her resignation letter at work, the hotel manager was there. When the hotel manager heard the matter, he promised Emma that he would look into the situation. However, two weeks later, the supervisor dismissed Emma. It was unexpectedly that Emma met the hotel manager somewhere. When the hotel manager heard that the supervisor had not followed his instructions, he became very angry. Then he dealt with the situation, reinstated Emma to her job, and made a new arrangement for her. Every week, she was allowed to work for five days. And each day, she was allowed to work for six hours. This new arrangement was very good for her, because it allowed her to have time to do her ministry work! Jehovah resolved Emma's situation in a way she did not expect at all.
