The Bible contains many ancient events that actually happened and are true. When we visualize these events in our minds, it will help us to understand what we read and also benefit from it. Consider how these things will help you to visualize what it was like in Bible times.

• Read aloud. Reading aloud will help you to visualize the account in your mind. When you and your family read the Bible together, you can assign roles to each person to read, which will help you better understand the account.

• Visualize what is happening. As you read, pause and think about how the people felt. For example, ask yourself: ‘Why did the person speak or act that way? If I were in that situation, what would I think or how would I feel?’

• Draw the scene. If you draw the Bible events, it will help you visualize the scenes well and remember what you have read, even if you do not know how to draw well.
