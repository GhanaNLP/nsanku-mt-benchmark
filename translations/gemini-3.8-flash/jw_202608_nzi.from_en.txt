Kpo Seetan Menle Nwo Ngyegyelɛ Ne Mɔ—Fa Wɔ Rɛle Wula Gyihova Nee Gyisɛse Anu

EDWENE 55 Mmmasulo Bɛ!

MA Ɛ NYE ƐLO Mmaamaa Seetan kpole mɔɔ ɔbɔ kɛ ɔbamaa Gyihova Nyamenle nee Gyisɛse Kelaese anyunlu asɛkye la bɛlɛbela wɔ.

“Bɛdwazo bɛtia Abɔnsam na ɔbanriandi bɛ.”—JAS. 4:7.

ƆVI mekɛ mɔɔ yɛsi kpɔkɛ kɛ yɛsonle Gyihova la, Seetan nee ye sunsum ɛtane ne mɔ kakyi yɛ agbɔvolɛ kpole mɔɔ ɛnlie menle a. Noko Seetan ze kɛ ɔnrɛhola yɛ saa Gyihova boa yɛ a. Ɔti Abɔnsam bɔ mɔdenle kɛ ɔsɛkye moalɛ zɔhane ɔlua kɛ ɔbamaa yɛakpo Gyihova la azo. Adenle ko mɔɔ ɔdua zo ɔyɛ ɛhye la a le kɛ ɔbɔ mɔdenle kɛ ɔbamaa yɛanyia adwenledwenle kɛ Gyihova nee ɔ Ra ne amgba gyi yɛ afoa.—2 Kɔl. 11:14; Gye. 1:6.

2 Wɔ konle nu, ɔwɔ kɛ sɔgyanema nyia bɛ hyehyɛfo ne mɔ anu anwodozo. Saa bɛminli anwodozo zɔhane a, bɛbahola bɛayɛ debie mɔɔ bɛ kulo a. Ɛhye bahola amaa bɛaminli bɛ ngoane. Zɔhane ala a, yɛle sɔgyanema wɔ sunsum nu konle mɔɔ yɛ nee “sunsum ɛtane feku ne mɔ” le nu la anu a. (Ɛfɛ. 6:12) Seetan Abɔnsam ze kɛ saa yɛminli Gyihova Nyamenle nee Gyisɛse Kelaese anu anwodozo a, ɔda ali kɛ yɛbakpo bɛ adehilelɛ nee bɛ moalɛ. Yemɔti ɔyɛ mɔɔ ɔbahola biala amaa yɛ anwodozo mɔɔ yɛlɛ wɔ bɛ nu la azo adwe. Wɔ edwɛkɛbɔlɛ ɛhye anu, yɛbanwu kɛzi ɔvale adalɛ adwenle zɔhane ɔlile gyima wɔ tete ne nee kɛzi ɔtɛfa ɔtɛdi gyima ɛnɛ la. Akee yɛbanwu kɛzi yɛbahola yɛagyinla ye nwole edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ɔbɔ la anloa la. Wɔ edwɛkɛbɔlɛ mɔɔ doa zo la anu, yɛbanwu adalɛ adwenle nwiɔ bieko mɔɔ Seetan fa di gyima wɔ yɛ sunsum nu konle ne anu la.*

* Tɛ ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ yɛyia la amuala a Seetan a bɔ zo ɔfa ɔba a. Noko ɔdaye a ɔwɔ “ewiade ye sunsum ne” anzi a, yɛɛ ɔka dwazotiama ɔmaa bɛyɛ Nyamenle menli ne amaneɛ a. Ɔti ɔlua adenle kpalɛ zo o anzɛɛ adenle fofɔle zo o, Seetan a wɔ ngyegyelɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ yɛyia bɛ wɔ yɛ sunsum nu konle ne anu la anzi a.—1 Kɔl. 2:12; 1 Dwɔn 5:19.

1. Adenle boni a Seetan dua zo kpondɛ kɛ ɔsɛkye Nyamenle azonvolɛ nɔhalɛlilɛ a?

2. Duzu ati a nwolɛ hyia kɛ yɛnyia Gyihova Nyamenle nee Gyisɛse Kelaese anu anwodozo a?

2

SEETAN HAHILE GYIHOWA NEE GYISƐSE ANWO EDWƐKƐ ƐTANE

3 Mɔoolua alesama mekɛ ne mɔlebɛbo la, Seetan bɔle mɔdenle kɛ ɔbamaa Adam nee Yive ahwe bɛ nwo avi Gyihova anwo. Seetan bɛlbɛlanle Yive maanle ɔliele ɔlile kɛ ye Bɔvolɛ ne ɛngulo ye kpalɛ. Seetan bizale ye kɛ: “Nɔhalɛ nu Nyamenle ɛha kɛ bɛmmadi tɔkɛ ne azo baka biala ma ɔ?” (Mɔl. 3:1-5) Seetan maanle Yive liele lilɛ kɛ Gyihova ɛlɛfa mɛla mɔɔ ɛnle kpalɛ la ado ɔ nee ɔ hu anwo zo. Mɔɔ yɛ alɔbɔlɛ la, Yive yɛɛ nzinlii Adam lile kpɔvolɛ Seetan adwenle ne anzi. Bɛhakyile bɛ kɔme bɛvile Gyihova—Ahenle mɔɔ vale ninyɛne kɔsiiti maanle bɛ la anwo.—Mɔl. 3:6.

4 Ɔvi Adam nee Yive mekɛ ne azo, Seetan ɛhɔ zo ɛzɛkye Gyihova duma. Kɛ neazo la, Seetan hanle Dwobo agɔnwolɛma mɔɔ ɛnle nɔhalɛma la kɛ bɛmaa Dwobo adwenle ɛyɛ ye kɛ Gyihova ɛnkulo ye na ɔ nye ɛnlie ye nɔhalɛlilɛ ne anwo. (Dwobo 22:1-3) Abɔnsam dwenlenle kɛ ngyegyeleɛ mɔɔ dwule Dwobo anwo zo la bamaa yeapo Gyihova. Ɔwɔ nuhua kɛ Seetan ngyehyɛleɛ ne maanle Dwobo hɔle amaneɛ kpole ɛdeɛ, noko nɔhalɛ nrenyia ne hɔle zo lile nɔhalɛ, na yeammee ye agɔnwolɛvalɛ mɔɔ kɔ zo wɔ ye nee Nyamenle avinli la ɛlɛ.—Dwobo 27:1, 5.

5 Ɛvɔlɛ dɔɔnwo anzi mɔɔ Gyisɛse rale azɛlɛ ye azo la, Seetan bɔle mɔdenle kɛ ɔnrɛmaa menli ɛnrɛdo Gyisɛse azo. Adenle ko mɔɔ ɔluale zo ɔyɛle ɛhye la a le kɛ ɔmaanle ɛzonlenlɛ nu aduakpole mɔɔ kpi kpole nee menli gyɛne hanle Gyisɛse anwo adalɛ edwɛkɛ. Yɛɛ awieleɛ bɔbɔ la, bɛhunle ye kɛ sonla mɔɔ ɛyɛ basabasa la.—Kenga

Mateyu 11:19; 27:27-31; Maake 14:55, 56; Dwɔn 8:41-45.

6 Gyisɛse ewule ne anzi, Seetan hakyile ye adwenle hɔle Gyisɛse ɛdoavolɛma ne mɔ gyima ne azo. Mɔlebɛbo ne, Abɔnsam bɔle mɔdenle kɛ ɔkɛma bɛkanyia adwenlele kɛ Gyisɛse amwɔ zo. Wɔ Seetan sunsum ne abo, adalɛ edwɛkɛ dɔle kɛ Gyisɛse ɛdoavolɛma ne ɛva ye funli ne ɛhɔ. (Mat. 28:11-15) Akee kenle mɔɔ doale zo la azo, Seetan bɔle mɔdenle kɛ ɔdua kpɔdekpɔdelɛ zo yeamaa asafo ne asulo. Seetan kpondɛle kɛ ɔmaa gyima mɔɔ bɛ Menle ne ɛva ɛwula bɛ sa nu la yɛ se somaa kɛ bɛnrɛhola bɛnrɛyɛ.—Gyi. 7:57–8:1; 12:1-3; 28:22.

7 Ɔwɔ nuhua kɛ tete Keleseɛnema ne bie mɔ amba adonle zɔhane la ati begyakyile edwɛkpa ne ɛhanlɛ ɛdeɛ, noko dɔɔnwo ne ala gyinlanle kpundii na bɛkpole kɛ bɛbadili koonwu. Bɛnwunle kɛ Seetan mini sunsum ɛtane ne mɔ a le bɛ kpɔvolɛma kpalɛ a. (Gyi. 4:18-20; 1 Pita 5:8) Na ɛnɛ noko ɛ? Mayɛlɛ boni mɔ a Seetan ɛlɛfa ayɛ gyima amaa yeabɔ mɔdenle kɛ ɔbamaa yɛ sa anu ado Gyihova ɛzonlenlɛ nu a?

SEETAN KƆ ZO SƐE GYIHOVA NE GYISƐSE ADWENLE

8 Ɛnɛ, Seetan foa titili ko a le kɛ ɔbamaa nɔhalɛ ne mɔɔ fale Gyihova mini Gyisɛse anwo la ayɛ nwɔho. Kɛmɔ ɔle “amalɛkɛselɛ selɛ” la ati, Abɔnsam fa adalɛ ɛzonlenlenlɛ bɔ adalɛ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ fale bɛ nwo la egu. (Dwɔn 8:44) Kɛ neazo la, yemɔ a yemaa menli ɛnnwu mɔɔ Gyihova mini ɔ Ra ne amgba de la a. AUGUSTE 2026 3

Satan ewiade ne maa yɛnwu ninyɛne dɔɔnwo mɔɔ sɛkye Nyamenle ngyinlazo ne mɔ (Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 11)

kile kɛ Selɛ ne nee Ralɛ ne sɛ. Ɛhye ati, menli bu wɔ Selɛ ne anwo, mɔɔ bɛnze ye duma la, na bɛsonle Ralɛ ne. Gyihova nee Gyisɛse amuala ɛnlie ɛzonlenlɛ zɛhae ɛndo nu. (Mat. 4:10; Dwɔn 4:23) Saa bɛwula menli anye zo wɔ adenle zɛhae mɔ azo a, ɔbahola yeayɛ se kɛ bɛbadie adwenle anzɛɛ bɛbahola bɛadwazo bɛatia yɛ ɛzonlenlɛ gyima ne bɔbɔ.

9 Saa yɛlɛbɔ agɔnwolɛvalɛ bie abo a, ninyɛne mɔɔ lumua mɔɔ yɛyɛ la ko a le kɛ yɛbaze ahenle duma. Seetan kpondɛ kɛ ɔsi menli adenle na bannyia agɔnwolɛvalɛ kpalɛ bɛangye Gyihova ɔlua kɛ ɔmmaa bɛnze Ye duma bɔbɔ la azo. Yemɔti, Seetan ɛva adalɛ ɛzonlenlɛ ɛli gyima mekɛ tendenle amaa menli arɛle avi Nyamenle duma. (Kenga Gyɛlɛmaya 23:26, 27.) Kpɔvolɛ ne ɛyɛ ye bɔbɔ kɛ bɛbaye Nyamenle duma—mɔɔ Baebolo ne fa toto aban kpundii mɔɔ bɔ ahenle nwo bane nwo la—bɛavi Baebolo ngilebɛbo dɔɔnwo anu. (Mrɛ. 18:10) Abɔnsam noko a de adalɛ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ le kɛ Gyihova a fa amaneɛnwunlɛ ba la anzi a. Ɔlua ɛhye ati, ɔbahola yeyɛ se kɛ menli mɔɔ lɛ ahonle kpalɛ la banwu Gyihova kɛ Agɔnwo mɔɔ bɛbahola bɛabikye ye a. (Gye. 4:8) Noko, Seetan eza kpondɛ kɛ ɔsɛkye Gyihova menli diedi.

9. Ɔlua Seetan amozii zo, duzu a ɛzonlenlɛ kɔnwo ɛmmaa menli ɛnnwu ye a? (Gyɛlɛmaya 23:26, 27)

4 ƐZINZANLƐ ARANEA NE

10 Mekɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛyia tɛnlenelilɛ ngakyile la, Seetan kulo kɛ yɛdie yɛdi kɛ Gyihova ɛndwenle yɛ nwo. Seetan anye la kɛ saa Gyihova maa yɛ nyanedwonle ne kɔ zo a, yɛva ɛya. (Mrɛ. 19:3) Menli mɔɔ te nganeɛ kɛhye la bahola abiza kɛ: ‘Nzonle a vi Gyihova mɛla nee ye ngyinlazo mɔɔ mebali zo la anu bara a? Ɔngyɛ kɛ ɔnyia nvasoɛ biala!’ Adwenle ɛhye bahola amaa yɛagyinla Seetan adwenle zo.

11 Seetan fa ninyɛne le kɛ abɔnsam ekyi ne mɔ dua zo kɛ ɔbamaa menli anyia adwenlenlu wɔ Gyihova ngyinlazo nwo. Wɔ ɛleka ɛhye mɔ, menli dɔɔnwo kile adwenle kɛ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ ngyinlazo mɔɔ wɔ Baebolo ne anu la ɛhɔ yɛ nvonle nu na ɔwɔ kɛ awie biala nyia adenle si ɔ ti anwo mɛla. Nɔhalɛ nu, “ewiade tumivolɛ ne” ze kɛzi ɔbamaa ngakyile mɔɔ wɔ kpalɛ nee ɛtane avinli la ayɛ basabasa kpalɛ.—Dwɔn 14:30; Aye. 5:20; 1 Tim. 4:1, 2.

12 Saa yietie Seetan adwelie a, ɔbahola yeamaa yɛahwe yɛ nwo yɛavi Gyihova anwo. Adiema raalɛ kakula bie mɔɔ yɛbavɛlɛ ye Shannon la ka kɛ: “Ɔliele mekɛ na meanwu kɛ subane mɔɔ bɛhilele me wɔ sukulu nee menli mɔɔ me nee bɛ bɔle la ɛlɛsɛkye kɛzi menwu debie mɔɔ le kpalɛ nee mɔɔ le ɛtane la. Mekɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ mevale meziekɛle intanɛte ne azo la, zɔhane ala a mehulole kɛ mesukoa menli mɔɔ me nee bɛ lile ngitanwo wɔ ɛkɛ la nyɛleɛ a. Ngyikyingyikyi mebɔle ɔ bo kɛ mehwe me nwo meavi Gyihova, me abusua, nee me mediema mrenyia nee mraalɛ nwo.” Nzinlii, Shannon ziale rale ye anwuma Selɛ ne anwo na ɔvinde vile Abɔnsam ɛdane ne anu. Duzu a boale ye a?

13 Shannon nwunle kɛ ɔhyia kɛ ɔbɔ mɔden na yeahɔ zo yeayɛ Gyihova nee Gyisɛse agɔnwo. Ɔhanle kɛ: “Meyɛle asɔne na menyele me nvonlehyee kɛ mɛbɔ mɔden meayɛ neɛnleaninu wɔ yɛ mbuluku ne mɔ anu. Vidio mɔɔ se Young People Ask—How Can I Make Real Friends? la boale me kpole.* Wɔ drama zɔhane anu, mediema raalɛ kakula bie hɔle ngyegyelɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ le kɛ me ɛdeɛ ne la anu. Ɔbɛlɛle ɔ nwo aze ɔhɔle ye awovolɛ ɛkɛ na ɔbipale moalɛ. Menyɛle zɔhane ala bie. Me awovolɛ, mgbanyima ne mɔ, nee Kilisiene mraalɛ mɔɔ ɛyɛ kpanyinli wɔ asafo ne anu la boale me ma mengyakyile agɔnwolɛ ɛtane ne mɔ. Menwunle kɛ nɔhalɛ ne ɛngile debie ala kɛ mɛkɔ debiezukoalɛ na meamaa me awovolɛ anye alie, emomu ɔkile kɛ mebamaa nɔhalɛ ne ayɛ me ɛdeɛ na me nee Gyihova agɔnwolvalɛ ne anu amia kpalɛ.”

BAABOLO EZUKOALƐ MƆƆ ƐNTUA HWEE

Yɛko yɛtia Seetan mɔdenlebɔlɛ ne mɔ kɛ yɛbadwenledwenle kɛzi yɛbahola yɛamaa agɔnwolɛvalɛ kpalɛ kɛha yɛ mini Gyihova avinli la azo (Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 14)

liele ninyɛne mɔɔ nuhua nɔhalɛ ɛtɛyɛle pɛpɛ anzɛɛ le adalɛ kpole la lile. Noko saa yɛsukoa Baebolo ne mini yɛ mbuluku ne mɔ na yɛbɔ Kilisienenma mɔɔ ɛbdelɛ la anwo dahuu a, awie ɛnrɛhola ɛnrɛbɛlɛbɛla yɛ mgbane. Saa yɛnwu nɔhalɛ ne kpalɛ a, ɔnrɛyɛ se kɛ yɛbapo Seetan mɔdenlebɔlɛ mɔɔ ɔbɔ kɛ ɔsɛkye yɛ diedi la.—Mrɛ. 13:20; Gyi. 17:11.

15 Saa yɛneɛnlea Baebolo ne anu kpalɛ a, yɛbanyia anwodozo kɛ Gyihova nee Gyisɛse gyi yɛ afoa nu. (Mrɛ. 22:19)

Saa yɛdwenle ninyɛne mɔɔ kile kɛzi Gyihova nee Gyisɛse dwenle alesama anwo kpalɛ la anwo a, ɔbaha yɛ kɛ yɛbava yɛ nwo yɛado bɛ nwo zo kɛ agɔnwolɛma mɔɔ fɛta. Baebolo ne anu pɛlepɛle kɛ Gyihova nee Gyisɛse kulo yɛ edwɛkɛ. Kɛ neazo la, Gyihova hilehilele nganeɛ mɔɔ ɔte ye wɔ ye menli nwo la anu kɛ ɔlɛ anyebolo kpole ɔtɛla mɔɔ maame mɔɔ lili ama kɛnyia kɛmaa ye kakula la. (Aye. 49:15) Ɔbu awie biala mɔɔ kulo ye duma ne la kɛ “egyapadɛ mɔɔ sonle bolɛ.” (Mal. 3:16, 17, ɔbd.) Eza Baebolo ne da kɛzi Gyisɛse ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ ɔlɛ ɔmaa yɛ la dɔɔnwo la ali ɔlua nganeɛ mɔɔ ɔdele wɔ ye afɔlebɔlɛ ne anwo la mɔɔ ɔhɛlɛle la azo.—Dwɔn 15:12, 13.

15. Kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na yɛanyia anwodozo kpole kɛ Gyihova nee Gyisɛse wɔ yɛ afoa nu ɛ?

6 ƐZINZALE ARANE NE

16 Saa yɛsuzu moalɛ mɔɔ Gyihova nee Gyisɛse fa maa yɛ ɛnɛ la anwo a, ɔbahola yeamaa yɛanyia anwodozo kpalɛ kɛ bɛkulo yɛ. Yɛbahola yɛahakye mekɛ mɔɔ yɛdele nganeɛ kɛ Gyihova bikye yɛ la, na yɛanyia anwosesebɛ yɛavi ninyɛne zɔhane mɔ mɔɔ yɛhakye la anu. Eza yɛbahola yɛabiza yɛ mediema mrenyia nee mraalɛ kɛzi bɛnnyianle moalɛ ɛhye bie la. (Mrɛ. 22:17) Saa yɛyɛ zɔ a, ɔbamaa yɛanyia anwodozo kɛ ngyegyelɛ biala mɔɔ yɛbayia la, Gyihova nee Gyisɛse wɔ yɛ afoa.—Kenga Edwɛndolɛ 118:5, 6.

17 Yɛ muala yɛkulo kɛ yɛkɔ zo yɛyɛ Gyihova Nyamenle nee Gyisɛse Kelaese agɔnwolɛma kpalɛ. Noko kɔkpula kɛ bɛbazɛkye Seetan nee ye sunsum ɛtane ne mɔ dahuu la, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛko yɛtia mɔdenle mɔɔ bɛbɔ kɛ bɛbamaa yɛ anwodozo mɔɔ yɛlɛ ye wɔ Gyihova nee Gyisɛse anu la ayɛ bɛtɛɛ la. (Yek. 20:1-3, 10) Amaa yɛahola yɛagyinla yɛ kpɔvolɛma ne mɔ mayɛlɛ nwanwane ne mɔ anwo zo la, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛ nee Gyihova fa agɔnwolɛvalɛ mɔɔ anu yɛ se na yɛkɔ zo yɛnyia anwodozo kɛ ɔdaye nee ɔ Ra kpole ne kulo yɛ na yɛ sonla nu yɛ se kpalɛ wɔ bɛ nye zo. Akee, kɛ mɔɔ Gyisɛse hanle la, awie biala ɛnrɛhola ‘ɛnrɛye [bɛ] ye Selɛ ne asa anu.’—Kenga Dwɔn 10:29; Gye. 4:7.

YƐBAHOLA YƐALI KONEA WƆ KONO NE ANU!

16 Baebolo ne maa yɛnyia anwodozo kɛ yɛbahola yɛali sunsum ɛtane mgbole ne mɔ anwo zo konim. (Jem. 4:7; 1 Dwɔn 5:4, 5) Mɛyɛ yɛlɛko yɛatiatia bɛ la, Nyamenle Edwɛkɛ ne ka kɛ diedi a bamaa yɛali konim a. (Ɛfɛ. 6:16) Ɔti kɔ zo nyia diedi kpole wɔ Gyihova Nyamenle nee Gyisɛse Kelaese anu. Maa diedi zɔhane ɛha wɔ na kɔ zo kulo wɔ mediema mrenyia nee mraalɛ ne mɔ na bɛbɔ nu bɛdɛnla ko.

17 Ɔnle kɛ yɛmaa debie biala mɔɔ Abɔnsam babɔ mɔdenle kɛ ɔkɛva yeakyekye yɛ nu na yeamaa yɛ sa nu ado wɔ kenle mɔɔ ɛlɛba la anu la kɔ yɛ nzi ɛlɛ. Gyihova nee Gyisɛse ɛbɔ ɛwɔkɛ kɛ bɛbadɛnla yɛ afoa saa yɛbika bɛ a. Yɛ mediema mrenyia nee mraalɛ kɔ zo yia yɛ nwo ɛkɛ wɔ yɛ kpɔvolɛ titili, Seetan, anwo konle ne anu. Mɔɔ fale bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛsɔre bɛtia yɛ la anwo la, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛbɔ mɔdenle biala yɛdi Gyisɛse folɛdulɛ mɔɔ se yɛye atiakunlukɛnlɛma yɛhile bɛ la azo. (Luku 6:27, 28) Ɔti kɔ zo dwazo tia Seetan mayɛlɛ ne mɔ, na nyia anwodozo kɛ ɛbali konim wɔ ɛ konle mɔɔ ɛlɛko awonreumgbɔne mɔɔ bɛle sunsum nu mgbanyima la anwo la anu.

Ɛdendɛlɛ Mɔɔ Gyi Ɛlɔlɛ Zo

“Medua ɛlɔlɛ zo mesɛlɛ wɔ bɔbɔ.”—FILEM. 9.

ANZODWOLƐ mɔɔ yɛ nye die nwo wɔ Gyihova menli avinli la sonle bolɛ kpalɛ maa yɛ. Anzodwolɛ ɛhye maa yɛnwu kɛzi ɛbɛlabɔlɛ bayɛ fɛ wɔ Paladaese la. (Aye. 32:17, 18) Ɔwɔ nuhua kɛ Gyihova a maa yɛ anzodwolɛ ɛhye ɛdeɛ, noko ɔle yɛ nwozonle gyima kɛ yɛbamaa yɛ anwo adwo yɛ na yɛabɔ nwolɛ bane. (Wlo. 12:18) Kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na yɛamaa anzodwolɛ adɛlɛ asafo ne anu mekɛ mɔɔ yɛ nee awie mɔ bɔ nu yɛ ninyɛne la? Saa yɛfomɛ awie noko ɛ? Saa awie fomɛ yɛ noko ɛ? Yɛbanyia mualɛ ne mɔ wɔ nzonhɛlɛ mɔɔ bɛgyinlanle ɛlɔlɛ zo bɛyɛle la anu. Nzonhɛlɛ ɛhye fale Baebolo nu menli nsa anwo: ɛzoanvo Pɔɔlo, Faelemon, nee Ɔnɛsemɔse.

Ɔlɛ akɛlɛ bie mɔɔ ɛnle Kilisieni mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Onɛsimɔso, mɔɔ ɔgyakyiye na ɔnriandile ɔhɔle Wulomu, bie a ɔwule ye ninyɛne bie ansa na yeahɔ. (Filem. 18; nea adesua nwo edwɛkɛ.) (Kɔl. 4:9) Noko kɛ mɔɔ Wulomuma mɛla ne kile la, ɔwɔ kɛ Onɛsimɔso sia kɔ ye menle nwo, yemɔti Pɔɔlo somanle ye hɔle Filemon ɛkɛ bieko.—Filem. 12, 13; nea adesua nwo edwɛkɛ mɔɔ fane “Melasɔma ye meamaa wɔ bieko” anwo.

3 Kɛzi Faelimɔn bayɛ ye nyɛleɛ wɔ Ɔnɛsemɔse sia kɔ la anwo ɛ? Mɛla nu la, ɛnee ɔlɛ adenle kɛ ɔtwe Ɔnɛsemɔse aso kpole kpalɛ. Asoo ɛnee ɔbali mɛla adenle zɔhane azo? Kɛzi asafo ne bayɛ bɛ nyɛleɛ ɛ? Yemɔti ɔhɛlɛle kɛlata ɔhɔmanle Faelimɔn na ɔmanle Tekekɔse vale hɔle. (Ɛfɛ. 6:21, 22; Kɔl. 4:7-9; nea Kolosema 4:7 anwo nzukoalɛ edwɛkɛ ne.) ƐZUKOA MƆƆ YƐBAHƆLA YƐANYA YƐAVI PƆƆLO NEAZO NE ANU

4 Duzu a yɛbahola yɛazukoa ye wɔ Pɔɔlo neazo ne anu a? Pɔɔlo nee menli lile wɔ adenle mɔɔ maa anzodwolɛ ba la azo. Ye neazo ne kile kɛzi yɛbahola yɛamaa anzodwolɛ adɛnla asafo ne anu wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛ nee awie mɔ bɔ nu la.

5 Pɔɔlo bulenle Faelemon kɛ agɔnwolɛ. (Filem. 17) Pɔɔlo andwenlene kɛ ɔbahola yeamaa Faelemon mɛla, kɛ asolo ɔle ye menle la. (2 Kɔl. 1:24) Pɔɔlo nleanle Faelemon kɛ kɔpate nwo gyimayɛvɔlɛ na ɔhakenle ye bɛ agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne anwo edwɛkɛ. Neazo kpalɛ boni a Pɔɔlo yɛle hilele Faelemon wɔ kɛzi ɔwɔ kɛ ɔne Ɔnɛsemo, ye adiema fofolɛ ne di la anwo a! Ɔyɛ neazo kpalɛ noko ɔmaa yɛ muala, titili bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛsonle kɛ mgbanyima wɔ asafo ne anu la. Mgbanyima mɔɔ bu awie mɔ kɛ agɔnwolɛma la boa maa anzodwolɛ ba. Kɛ mboaneneavɔlema mɔɔ lɛ ɛlɔlɛ la, bɛnli mboane ne mɔ anwo “tumililɛ.” (1 Pita 5:1-3) Emomu, bɛnea bɛdie dɔnehwele bɛboka Nyamenle mboane ne mɔ anwo, bɛtie bɛ, bɛbu bɛ, na bɛboa bɛ wɔ ngyegyelɛ mekɛ nu. Kɛmɔ Pɔɔlo yɛle la, mgbanyima mɔɔ bu awie mɔ kɛ agɔnwolɛma la yɛ neazo kpalɛ maa mboane ne na bɛboa bɛmaa anzodwolɛ tɛnla ɛleka mɔɔ bɛwɔ la.

6 Pɔɔlo hanle Filemɔn anwo edwēkē kpalē. (File. 4, 5, 7) Yɛ muala yɛhyia nganeɛdelɛ kpalɛ nwo. Duzu ati ɔ? Debie ko a le kɛ, yɛ nuhua dɔɔnwo te nganeɛ kɛ yɛnli munli. Bie a yɛbadwenledwenle yɛ nyunlu kɛzi ɔde la anwo, kɛ bie a awie mɔ kulo yɛ edwɛkɛ anzɛɛ yɛbaminli sonvolɛ nwo nwolɛ adenle kpole bie. Nganeɛdelɛ ɛhye mɔ le kpalɛ. Noko akee, ɔtɛnla a ɔmaa yɛdi nyane, yɛ nyunlu kɛzi ɔte la yɛ se, yɛbɔ yɛ nwo ban, anzɛɛ yɛsi akanli—subane mɔɔ baha yɛ mɔdenlebɔlɛ mɔɔ yɛbɔ kɛ yɛkpondɛ anzodwolɛ la azo. Saa awie te nganeɛdelɛ kpalɛ a, ɔbahola yeagyinla nganeɛ mɔɔ ɔte kɛ ɔnli munli la anloa kpalɛ. (Mrɛ. 12:25) Ɔmaa ɔnwu kɛ bɛkulo ye na bɛ nye sɔ ye. Ɛkulo kɛ ɛnyia sunsum zɛhae wɔ awie mɔ anwo ɔ? Saa ɔle zɔ a, kpondɛ nwolɛ adenle maa kanvo awie mɔ kpalɛ. Mmakanvo awie mɔ wɔ nyianle kpalɛ mɔɔ bɛbanyia la ala anwo, emomu bɔ mɔdenle mɔɔ bɛbɔ la noko anwo. Kɛ mɔɔ ɔde la, saa ɛnwu debie kpalɛ a, ka edwɛkɛ kpalɛ.

7 Pɔɔlo hanle edwɛkpa vile Ɔnɛsemɔse anwo. (Kenga Faylemon 10-12.) Kɛzi Pɔɔlo edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ɔhanle ye kɛnlɛma mɔɔ fane Ɔnɛsemɔse anwo la bahola aha Faylemon ɛ? Asoo ɔhanle kɛzi Faylemon dele nganeɛ, dendele hilele, na ɔyɛle Ɔnɛsemɔse la? Ɛhɛe amgba! Zɔhane ala a, edwɛkɛ mɔɔ yɛka yɛfane

8 Dahuu Pɔɔlo yɛle Gyihova mo wɔ Filemɔn subane kpalɛ ne mɔ anwo. (File. 4, 5) Ɔyɛle zɔhane ala noko ɔmaanle menli dɔɔnwo bie mɔ. Yɛɛ ɛnee ɔle ye subane kɛ ɔkayɛ Gyihova mo wɔ agɔnwolɛma ɛhye mɔ anwo. 8. Duzu a bahola aboa yɛ maayɛadwenle na yɛaha edwɛkɛ kpalɛ yɛagwɔzonlenlɛma nwo a? (Nea nvoninli ne mɔ noko.)

9 Ɔnva nwo kɛ Paul yiale tɛnlabelɛ mɔɔ anu yɛ se la, ɔlale atiakunlukɛnlɛma ali kilele Philemon. Ɛvɛle anzɛɛ ɛlɛnwu amaneɛ na wɔ nye fu kɛlɛ aleyɛle bie ɛlɛ ɔ? Saa yɛlɛyia tɛnlabelɛ mɔɔ anu yɛ se a, ɔkula ɔyɛ sɔbelɛ kɛ yɛbava yɛ abufole yɛagua awie mɔ anwo zo. Wɔ yemɔ azo la, suzu Paul tɛnlabelɛ ne mɔɔ ɛnee ɔwɔ nu mekɛ mɔɔ ɔhɛlɛle ngɛlata hɔnenle Philemon la anwo. Ɛvolɛ ekyi bie mɔ pɛle nu la, bɛvale ɛtane kpole bɛdole Paul anwo zo na bɛdule ɔ nwo bɛdole efiade mɔɔ ɔmfɛta. Bɛdule ɔ nwo bɛdole efiade wɔ Jerusalem nee Caesarea. Akee, bɛvale ye bɛhɔle Rome noko bɛ sukunle sɔlɔle wɔ adenle ne azo. Na kɛkala mɔɔ ɔlɛkɛlɛ Philemon la, ɛnee Paul tɛde “kpɔkɛvonoa nu sonla,” mɔɔ bɛdu ye nwo bɛto sua nu wɔ Rome la. (Philem. 1) Noko asoo Paul manle ye anwubielɛ mɔɔ gyegye adwenle la hanle adenle mɔɔ ɔlua zo ɔdendɛle ɔhilele Philemon la ɔ? Kyɛkyɛ. Paul kɛlata mɔɔ ɔhɛlɛle Philemon la lale atiakunlukɛnlɛma nee ndelebɛbo ali. Ɛzukoalɛdeɛ ne? Yɛbahola yɛaboa amaa anzodwolɛ ara ɔlua mɔdenle mɔɔ yɛbɔ kɛ yɛbala atiakunlukɛnlɛma ali yɛahile awie mɔ bɔbɔ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛvɛ, yɛ adwenle engula yɛ anzɛɛ yɛlɛgyinla sɔnea nwanwane nwo la azo. Akee, anzodwolɛ tɛnlabelɛ mɔɔ yɛmaa ɔba la boa yɛ maa yɛgyinla yɛ ngyegyelɛ nwo.

ƐZUKOALƐ MƆƆ YƐBAHƆLA YƐANWUNII WƆ ƆNISIMƆSƐ NEAZOƐ NE ANU

10 Yɛ muala yɛyɛ yɛɛ yɛka ninyɛne mɔɔ gyegye awie mɔ a. Ɔnɛsimɔse neazo ne kilehile yɛ mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ na anzodwolɛ ara mekɛ mɔɔ zɔhane asi la. Ɔyɛle debie bie mɔɔ ɔzɔho kɛ ɔnyɛ se noko fane dɔɔnwo ne ala ɔyɛ se kɛ awie bayɛ la. Ɔliele ye nvonleɛ ne ɔdole nu na ɔyɛle mɔɔ ɔbahola la amuala amaa yeanzikɛ bɛ nwo kpalɛ yeagye Filemɔn. Nɔhalɛ nu, ɛnee Ɔnɛsimɔse ze kɛ Wulomu mɛla ne kile kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ ɔsia ɔkɔ ye menle ne ɛkɛ. Ɔti ɔdule adenle mɔɔ gye ezukɔ dɔɔnwo na ɛwɔlɔ kpole wɔ nu mɔɔ bo kilemitan 1,400 (maele 900) la kɛ ɔsia yeagɔ Filemɔn ɛkɛ. Ɛnee ɔwɔ kɛ ɔdie ɔto nu kɛ ɔkpanve ye fanwodi ne na bie a yeaziezie ɔ nwo yeamaa nyianezu gyɛne mɔɔ ɔbanyia wɔ mɔɔ ɔyɛle la ati. Ɛnee ɛhye ɛnle debie mɔɔ bɛsi ye kpɔkɛ mgbane a. Na ninyɛne noko mɔɔ ɔdwenlenle kɛ ɔle kpalɛ kɛ ɔbanriandi ɔavi Filemɔn anwo ɛ? Saa ɔdele nganeɛ zɔhane bɔbɔ a, yeammaa yeangpɔti ye kɛ ɔsia yeagɔ ye menle ne ɛkɛ na yeayɛ mɔɔ ɔbahola la yeamaa anzodwolɛ arɛla bɛ avinli kɛ sunsum nu mɛnma.

11 Saa wɔyɛ nvonleha wɔtia awie a, fa adwenle kɛ ɛbamaa anzodwole ara. (Mat. 5:23-25) Nɔhalɛ nu, ɛhye bahola ayɛ se, noko anzodwole mɔɔ bavi nu ara la hyia kpalɛ. Bɔ ɔ bo wɔ ahonle muala anu kɛ ɛkɛzɛlɛ fakyɛ. Nyia ɛhulolɛ kɛ ɛbatie ahenle na wɔade ye nganeɛdelɛ bo. Die ɛ nvonleha to nu. Mmasɔkpa wɔ ninyɛne mɔɔ ɛyɛle la anzɛɛ mmatoto fɔlɛ wɔbɔ awie gyɛne. Saa ɛyɛ zɔ a, ɔbamaa fakyɛselɛ ne ayɛ mgbane, ɔkɛhwe ahedwolɛ ne azo, anzɛɛ ɔbamaa ninyɛne azɛkye bɔbɔ. “DUZU a kakula he bayɛ a?” (Luku 1:57-66) Ɛnee bɛ gɔnwo mɔ nee bɛ mbusuanli kpondɛ kɛ bɛnwu ninyɛne dɔɔnwo wɔ Dwɔn anwo, ralɛ ne mɔɔ bɛvale anwonyia adenle zo bɛwole ye bɛmaanle ɛsɔfo Zepalaya mɔɔ ɛyɛ kpanyinli nee ye aye Elizabeth mɔɔ ɛyɛ kpanyinli mɔɔ ɛnee le sonle la. Gyihova luale Zepalaya anwo zo buale kpuya zɔhane. Ɔhilele nu kɛ Mɛzaya ne mɔɔ bɛhendɛle ye mekɛ tendenle la bara ɔnrɛhyɛ yɛɛ Dwɔn baziezie menli mɔɔ ɛnu bɛ nwo la amaa bɛanwu ahenle zɔhane na bɛali ye anzi. (Emɔso 3:7; Luku 1:67-79) Noko Zepalaya anyɛ mɔɔ ɔbaha debie mɔɔ ɔ ra Dwɔn bayɛ la ngome.* Kɛ mɔɔ yɛbanwu ye wɔ edwɛkɛ he anu la, Zepalaya edwɛkɛ ne ɛnee le ngalɛbɛra na ɔka yɛdayɛ noko ɛnɛ. Yɛbanwu ninyɛne nsa mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛbu bɛ kpalɛ la wɔ ye edwɛkɛ ne anu: (1) ngoanelielɛ ahyɛlɛdeɛ ne, (2) ɛzonlenlɛ nwuanzanwuanza adenlewɔlɛ ne, nee (3) edwɛkɛhanlɛ ɛzukoalɛdeɛ ne.

2. (a) Kɛ mɔɔ Luku 1:67-73 kile la, ahyɛlɛdeɛ boni a bɔle Nyamenle menli nwo ɛwɔkɛ a? (b) Nwane a le “agyelielɛ kpole” mɔɔ baha nwolɛ edwɛkɛ wɔ Luku 1:69 la?

20

menli akyɛdeɛ kɛnlɛma: ngoanelielɛ. Noko nwane a balie bɛ ngoane a? Zɛkalaya hanle kɛ: “[Gyihova] ɛdwazo ngoanelielɛ kpolɛ kpalɛ ɛmaa yɛ wɔ ye sonvolɛ Devidi sua ne anu.” (Luku 1:69; nea nzonlɛhideɛ.) Zɔhane “ngoanelielɛ kpolɛ kpalɛ” ne a le Mɛzaya ne. Dyuuma mɔɔ wɔ Zɛkalaya mekɛ zo la ɛnee ɛlɛkpondɛ Mɛzaya ne mɔɔ bɛbɔle ye nwo ɛwɔkɛ la kɛkala na bɛze kɛ ɔbavi Ebileham abo wɔ Devidi belemgbunlililɛ ehyia ne anu. (Mɔl. 22:17, 18; Mat. 22:42) Ɛnee kɛmɔti a Gyihova hanle Zɛkalaya kɛ ɔbɔ zɔhane “ngoanelielɛ kpolɛ kpalɛ” ne ɛralɛ nolo a? Ɔluakɛ na Mɛzaya ne ɛralɛ ɛbikye kpalɛ tɛla dahuu! Siane nsia anu, bɛlɛra bie mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Mɛle la bawo Gyisɛse, mɔɔ kɛmɔ anwumabɔvolɛ Geebliɛl hanle la, bɛbavɛlɛ ye “Nyamenle Ara” na ɔbali Belemgbunli wɔ “ye selɛ Devidi belemgbunli ebia ne azo.” (Luku 1:30-35) Bɛdwazole “ngoanelielɛ kpolɛ kpalɛ” ne wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛzɔnenle Gyisɛse mɔɔ ɛnee yeɛli ɛvolɛ 30 la. Zɔhane mekɛ ne, Gyihova vale sunsum nwuanzanwuanza kpokpale ye kɛ Mɛzaya ne mɔɔ bɛbɔle ye nwo ɛwɔkɛ la. (Mat. 3:16, 17) Amgba edwɛkɛ mɔɔ yɛ anyelielɛ kpalɛ!

3 Ɛnee ɔwɔ kɛ bɛdie ɛvoya mɔɔ limoa la anu Dwuuma ne. Noko kɛvi nwane anrɛɛ duzu afoa nu? Zɛkalaya buale kɛ: “Ngoanelielɛ kɛvi yɛ kpɔvolɛma asa anu nee menli mɔɔ kyi yɛ la amuala asa anu.” (Luku 1:71) Ɛnee ɛhye kile kɛ Gyisɛse badie Dwuuma ne avi Wuloma tumivolɛma mɔɔ sisi bɛ la asa anu ɔ? Kyɛkyɛ. Emomu, ɛnee ɔwɔ kɛ bɛdie Dwuuma ne bɛvi amaneɛnwunle ɛvolɛ kpole ne, Seetan, asa anu. (Hib. 2:14, 15) Seetan a ɛmaalɛ alesama, mɔɔ tete Dwuuma ne boka nwo la, rale ɛtane nee ewule ngɛkɛlɛzonlenlɛ nu a. Bieko, ɛnee Dwuuma ne

bɛanrihɔ bɛanli Mosisi Mɛla ne azo kɛmɔ ɔwɔ la pɛpɛɛpɛ. Ɔti, munzule dɔle bɛ nwo zo mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛlie bɛ bɛfi nu a. (Wlo. 3:20; Gal. 3:10, 13) Ɔti, ɛnee bɛhyia mɔɔ Gyihova luale Zɛkalaya anwo zo bɔle ɛwɔkɛ kɛ ɔbayɛ la anwo—bɛbanyia “ngoane . . . bɛdua bɛ ɛtane ɛtanekyɛlɛ ne azo.”—Luku 1:77.

4 Agbɔvolɛ boni mɔ a ɔhyia kɛ bɛdie yɛ bɛfi bɛ sa nu ɛnɛ a? Kɛ mɔɔ Djuufoɔ ne mɔ mɔɔ wɔ Gyisɛse mekɛ zo la de la, yɛle ɛtane nee ewule ngɛkɛlɛ. (Wlo. 5:12) Bieko, Seetan nee sunsum ɛtane ne mɔ sisi yɛ. Bɛbɔ mɔdenle kɛ bɛbelɛ yɛ, bɛkpondɛ kɛ bɛtwe yɛ bɛfi nɔhalɛ ɛzonlenlɛ ne anwo. Bɛnwu menli nee eku ahyehyɛdeɛ mɔɔ bie mɔ boka “bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛkyi yɛ” la anwo zo ambele na bɛadwazo bɛatia yɛ ɛzonlenlɛ ne. Ɔhyia kɛ bɛdie yɛ bɛfi agbɔvolɛ zɛhae mɔ asa nu.

5 Kɛzi yɛ Ngoledolɛnli ne die yɛ fi agbɔvolɛ ɛhye mɔ asa anu ɛ? Suzu kɛzi Gyisɛse die yɛ fi ɛtane nu la anwo. Mekɛ mɔɔ ɔwɔ azɛlɛ ye azo la, Gyisɛse hilehilele ye ɛdoavolɛma kɛzi bɛkɛzonle Gyihova wɔ adenle mɔɔ ɔdie ɔto nu la azo. Akee ɔvale ye ngoane ɔdole ɛkɛ kɛ ɛkpɔnenledeɛ, “amaa awie biala mɔɔ gile ye nu diedi la anminli, emomu yeanyia dahuu ngoane.” (Dwɔn 3:16; Mat. 20:28) Yɛbɔ mɔdenle yɛdi ninyɛne mɔɔ Gyisɛse hilehilele mɔɔ fale kɛzi bɛkɛzonle Gyihova wɔ adenle mɔɔ ɔdie ɔto nu la azo. Noko yɛtɛdi munli, yemɔti yɛsɛlɛ Gyihova kɛ ɔva yɛ ɛtane ɔhyɛ yɛ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛtɛla ye ngyinlazo mɔ la. Akee ɔlua ɛkpɔnenledeɛ ne azo, Gyihova fa ɛlɔlɛ die yɛ ɛzonlenlɛ to nu. (1 Dwɔn 2:1, 2) Wɔ ndenle ɛhye mɔ azo, yɛ Ngoledolɛnli Gyisɛse die yɛ fi ɛtane folɛdulɛ nu.

MƆƆ menle sukunli la, me nye anlie sukulu nwo, yɛɛ me makese bɔle aze. Ɛnee mekpondɛ kɛ meyɛ ninyɛne ngome na awie biala annwu me. Ɛnee mennle debie biala mɔɔ mebahola mangyinla zolɛ meadwenle kɛ Nyamenle banwu me a. Kɛmɔti a?

Menyinyale wɔ Paris, France, ɛleka mɔɔ menli ɛnde Nyamenle edwɛkɛ na bɛnlie Nyamenle bɛnli la, meandie Nyamenle meanli. Emomu, menliele adwenebɔdelɛ ngilehilelɛ ne mendonle nu. Me awovolɛ amba asɔre nyianle adwenle kpalɛ. Bɛdwenlenle kɛ saa Nyamenle wɔ ɛkɛ a, anrɛɛ amaneɛnwunlɛ nee nzisi ɛnrɛyɛ ewiade tɛkɛtɛkɛ. Yɛɛ kɛmɔ menwunle asɔredendenle dɔɔnwo kpole la ati, me noko mendwenlenle kɛ ngɛbanyilɛ a le kɛ awie badie Nyamenle mɔɔ lɛ ɛlɔlɛ la adi a. Kɛkala ɛdeɛ, mele adwenle kpole kɛ tɛ Bɔvolɛ ne ngome a wɔ ɛkɛ ne a, emomu ɔdwenle me nwo noko, ɔnva nwo me sinlidɔlɛ mɔɔ le dɔɔnwo la. Maa mengaha debie mɔɔ hakyile me adwenle nee kɛzi Diedi zɔhane hakyile me ɛbɛlabɔlɛ la mengile wɔ.

Mezonle kɛ maangyebakyi zo neavolɛ wɔ New Caledonia

Noko, me sa nu dole ɔluakɛ dahuu mentɔ me nwo zo ndonlɛ mɔɔ ɛnle fɔle la anu. (Nolo. 7:16) Ɛnee ɔwɔ kɛ mesukoa kɛ Gyihova le Selɛ mɔɔ lɛ ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ ɛngpondɛ kɛ yɛyɛ pɛpɛpɛ la, emomu ɔ nye die mɔdenle kpalɛ biala mɔɔ yɛbɔ la anwo.

Wɔ Paris, bɛhanle kɛ melie maangyebakyi zo neavolɛ agyakɛ nu. Mendenrɛ kɛ mele kakula dodo yɛɛ menyɛ ɛhye bie ɛlɛ. Noko menwunle kɛ Gyihova sunsum nwuanzanwuanza ne boa yɛ maa yɛyɛ dɔɔnwo tɛla mɔɔ yɛngome yɛbahola yɛayɛ la.

Meyiale ngyegyelɛ fofolɛ noko. Ɔvi me kakulɛ, menyianle ewule mɔɔ maa awie fifi dɔɔnwo la, mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye hyperhidrosis la. Ewule ɛhye ka me sa kpole kpalɛ. Ɔyɛ kpole wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ me nwo kye me la, le kɛ mekɛ mɔɔ melɛtendɛ meamaa menli wɔ maanyɛlɛ anzɛɛ nyianu kpole bo la. Noko mebahola meamaa me nwonane ne ayɛ kɛzi ɔwɔ kɛ ɔyɛ la ɔlua Gyihova mɔɔ mebaye ye adua kɛ ɔmaa me adwenle anu adwo me la azo. Ngyegyelɛ ne ɛtɛwiele, noko saa mefa me nwo meto Gyihova anwo kpole a, me nwo tɔ me dɔɔnwo na menfifie dɔɔnwo.

Wɔ 2011 ne anu, Gyihova lale atootole ali hilele yɛ na ɔyɛle mɔɔ yɛ ahonle ɛdu kɛyɛ mekɛ tendenle la ɔmanle yɛ. Yɛbɔle edwɛkpa ɛhanlɛ gyima bo wɔ New Caledonia, lona nsua bie mɔɔ wɔ southwest Pacific, mɔɔ kɔ dɔɔnwo tɛla 16,000 kilometers (10,000 mi) vile Paris la azo. Gyima ɛhye noko vale ngyegyelɛ bie mɔ rale. Ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkakyi yɛ nwo yɛfa aleɛ fofolɛ bie mɔ, le kɛ bankye, taro, nee bayerɛ. Bieko, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛsukoa kɛ yɛbadɛnla akɔnoma mɔɔ kpoloma nee kɛlete mɔɔ tete mɔɔ sɛkye yɛ nwoalɛ wɔ ngɛnlema kpalɛ la anwo. Ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkakyi yɛ nwo yɛfa adenle fofolɛ mɔɔ bɛdua zo bɛtendɛ la noko. Fane dɔɔnwo ne ala menli ne ɛmbua edwɛkɛ mɔɔ bɛkaha la, na emomu bɛtu bɛ nyunlu konle bɛkile.

Gyihova eza ɛboa yɛ ma yɛdɛlɛ yɛ nwo yɛdo teokelase gyima fofolɛ nu. Yɛzonlenle kɛ ɛzonlenlɛ gyima nu edwɛkpatɛlɛvolɛma na yɛlile mekɛ ekyi kɛ Bɛtɛlema. Yɛhɔle Belemgbunlililɛ Edwɛkpatɛlɛvolɛma Sukulu ne, na kɛkala yɛwɔ maangyebakyi gyima ne anu. Yɛnwu kɛzi Gyihova dua ye sunsum nwuanzanwuanza ne azo boa yɛ wɔ gyima biala anu la. Yehilehile me kɛ awie sinlidɔlɛ ɛngile kɛ ɔnrɛhola ɔnrɛzonle ye kpalɛ. Ɔti mesɛlɛ dahuu kɛ ɔboa me ma menyɛ mɔɔ hyia la. Ɔwɔ nuhua kɛ mekɛ mɔɔ megyinla menli nyunlu la ɛzulolɛ tɔ me nwo ɛdeɛ, noko Nyamenle moalɛ zo, mehola mema asafo mɔɔ yɛkɔ yɛkpɔla bɛ la anwosesebɛ na meye ndenle mehile bɛ.

Wɔ nzenzaleɛ ɛhye mɔ amuala anu, Claudine azonle me dahuu. Ɔtɛla ye abotane wɔ me sinlidɔlɛ nwo kpalɛ. Ɔboa me wɔ gyima mɔɔ yɛbɔ nu yɛyɛ la anu, yɛɛ ɔziezie ɔ nwo dahuu kɛ ɔbadi dasele kpalɛ. Ɔnnwiinwii wɔ ewɔle kpole ne nee ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ wɔ maangyebakyi gyima ne anu la anwo.

Duzu ninyɛne a ɛhɔle zo wɔ suakpole kponle nloa wɔ Baebolo mekɛ nu a?

SUAKPO anlenkɛ ne mɔ ɛnle ɛleka mɔɔ bɛdua ala bɛkɔ bɛ ba. Kɛmɔ menli dɔɔnwo dua ɛkɛ ne la ati, ɛnee ɔle ɛleka mɔɔ menli dɔɔnwo yia wɔ alehyenlɛ biala a. Baebolo ne ka suakpo anlenkɛ ne mɔ anwo edwɛkɛ tɛla fane 50, yɛɛ ɔka ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛyɛle bɛ wɔ ɛkɛ ne la anwo edwɛkɛ.

Menli yialee wɔ suakpole ne anlenkɛ ne mɔ anloa. Hibulu edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ mɔɔ bɛhilele ɔ bo “anlenkɛ” la ɛngile suakpole bie anlenkɛ kɛkɛ. Ɔkile ɛleka mɔɔ bɛsisia azua wɔ suakpole anzɛɛ suane bie anlenkɛ nu lɔ la. Wɔ ɛleka bie mɔ, ɛnee agbɔ mɔɔ menli dɔɔnwo kola yia nu la wɔ anlenkɛ ne mɔ anzi.—Nɛh. 8:1.

Guadwulenvoma kpalye gua wɔ suazo anlenkye ne mɔ anwo lɔ. Ɔbayɛ kɛ bɛvale ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛtɔne ye bikye anlenkye ɛhye mɔ la a bɛdole Gyɛlusalɛm anlenkye bie mɔ aluma a, le kɛ Fɛlɛ Anlenkye ne nee Mboane Anlenkye ne la.—Zɛf. 1:10; Dwɔn 5:2.

Menli nienleenle gualilɛ nee bɛ ngakyile ninyɛne nwo ngyegyeleɛ nwo debie wɔ suakpole ne anlenkɛ ne mɔ anloa. Abraham nee Boaze lile adwelie mɔɔ bɛyɛ ye amara zo wɔ alasevolɛ nyunlu wɔ suakpole anlenkɛ ne mɔ anloa. (Gyn. 23:10-15, 17, 18; Lute 4:1, 10) Saa awie amvɛlɛ adendulɛma bie mɔ anwɔ ye sua nu a, bɛda eyanlue ne mɔɔ bikye suakpole ne anlenkɛ ne mɔ la azo nɔɔzo. (Kpe. 19:15-21) Eza menli kɔ suakpole ne anlenkɛ ne mɔ anloa kɔdie edwɛkɛ fofolɛ mɔɔ ɛdo la.—2 Sam. 18:24, 25.

Mɛlatotolɛma di ndɛne wɔ suazonle nlenkɛ ne mɔ anwo lɔ. Bɛdi edwɛkɛ mɔɔ menli mɔɔ wɔ lɔ la buke bɛ nzo tie daseledilɛ ne nee edwɛkɛ ne awieleɛ. (Mɛla 21:18-20) Ɛleka ɛhye mɔɔ menli dɔɔnwo wɔ la maa mɛlatotolɛma ne di pɛlepɛle wɔ bɛ ndɛnedilɛ nu. Saa mɛlatotolɛma ne di ndɛne ne wie a, mgbanyima ne mɔ di bɛ kpɔkɛzilɛ ne azo fofɔle nu. (Mɛla 25:2) Wɔ suazonle nlenkɛ ne mɔ anwo lɔ,

Suazo anlenkɛ ne mɔ dɛnla ye ɛleka mɔɔ menli dɔɔnwo yia wɔ alehyenlɛ biala ɛkɛ ne

ɛnee bɛkpondɛ kɛ ndɛnebualɛma maa pɛlepɛlelilɛ mini yɛɛ bɛbɔ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ kpalɛ nolo.—Dɛt. 16:18-20; Dwobu 29:7, 11-17.

Mrenyia nɔhalɛvoma hanle hilele Yizilayɛma mɔɔ wɔ suakpole ne anlenkɛ nloa la kɛ bɛnyia Gyihova anu diedi. Ngapezoma yɛɛ awie mɔ dɔɔnwo dendɛle wɔ anlenkɛ ne mɔ anloa ɔluakɛ ɛnee bɛze kɛ menli hyia ɛkɛ. (1 Ki. 22:10; Jer. 17:19, 20) Kɛ neazo la, Yehu bɔle nolo wɔ menli ne amuala anyunlu kɛ Gyihova ɛli Ye edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ɔhanle ɔtiale Ehaba sua ne la azo. (2 Ki. 10:8-10) Belemgbunli Hɛzikaya boɔboale ye ɛlɔne asafohene anloa wɔ suakpole ne anlenkɛ nloa amaa yeamaa bɛ anwosesebɛ kɛ bɛva bɛ nwo bɛdo Gyihova moalɛ zo.—2 Chron. 32:6-8.

Baebolo ne ka kɛ “nrɛlɛbɛ kpalɛ” tea nu yɛɛ ɔtua ɔ ngye kpalɛ wɔ “suakpole ne anlenkɛ ne mɔ anloa.” (Mrɛ. 1:20, 21) Mɔɔ ɛnee bɛwɔ tete Yizilayɛ la, suakpole ne anlenkɛ ne mɔ anloa a nrenyia mɔɔ di nɔhalɛ la tua folɛ kpalɛ yɛɛ bɛbua ndɛne pɛpɛpɛ a. Ɛnɛ, bɛhɛlɛ Gyihova nrɛlɛbɛ ne wɔ Baebolo ne anu na ye azonvolɛ mɔɔ wɔ ɛleka biala la ɛlɛboa menli kɛ bɛhakyi bɛ nyunlu bɛwula nrɛlɛbɛ kpalɛ ɛhye mɔɔ vi ye ɛkɛ la anu.

BAEBOLO NE AZUANLEDEƐ Baka Mɔɔ Sɔ Te Ndɛ

Almond baka ne boka mbaka kɛyɛ 30 mɔɔ baha nwolɛ edwɛkɛ wɔ Baebolo ne anu la anwo. Ɔboka mbaka mɔɔ limoa fufule wɔ ɛwɔlɛ mekɛ ne anzi la anwo. Bɛnwu ye folawa kɛnlɛma mɔɔ le pink anzɛɛ fufule la wɔ January awieleɛ kɛkala. Ɔfɛta kɛ, Hibulubunli duma mɔɔ bɛva bɛdo almond baka ne la kile pɔpɔɔpɔ kɛ “awie mɔɔ ɛdwazo.” Asoo almond baka zɔhane nee ye Bɔvolɛ ne le ko ɔ?

Ɔwɔkɛ ngyehyɛnu mɔɔ wɔ Gyɛlɛmaya 7:25 kile kɛ Gyihova hanle kɛ ɔdwazo nwonlomɔ fɛlɛfɛlɛ. March 15, 2011, Ɛzinzalɛ Arane ne hanle kɛ: “Kɛ mɔɔ alimonde baka ne ‘dwazo’ nwonlomɔ la, zɔhane ala a Gyihova noko ninyɛne mɔɔ kile la anu ‘dwazole nwonlomɔ fɛlɛfɛlɛ’ . . . kɛ ɔbɔ ye menli ne kɔkɔ wɔ nzinloa mɔɔ vi anzosesebɛyɛlɛ nu ba la anwo a. . . . Eza ɔnlie ye menle—ɔbahɔ zo ‘yeade’—kɔkpula kɛ ye ngapezonlema edwɛkɛ ne bara nu.”—Gyɛ. 1:11, 12.

Baebolo ne eza fa amande baka ne felawule fufule ne kile banyinlile atipɛne fufule. (Nol. 12:5) Yɛɛ ɔda ali kɛ kɛmɔ baka ne felawule ne anye yɛ fɛ kpalɛ la ati, bɛvale bɛzole ɛzonlenlɛ sua ne anyinlazo kɛnlɛma ne nrenyia ne mɔ anwo kɔpo ne mɔ.—Adon. 37:17-20.
