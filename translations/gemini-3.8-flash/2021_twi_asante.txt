THE MINISTER'S STATEMENT

In the month of December in 2020, Ghanaians voted again for President Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo to continue the good work he was doing and to add more good deeds to what he was doing. We do not at all underestimate the confidence that Ghanaians have had in this government.

This 2021 Budget is a unique budget because it will manage our economy well to help protect the citizens, stimulate struggling businesses, and further help us harness our strength to put Ghana back on the path of development after the peak of the COVID-19 pandemic. This budget is based on our vision to transform this nation into a financially self-reliant country.

In the first four years, we made plans through various means so that this nation would stand on its feet again economically. These various policies included those that would create jobs in abundance, the ways we raise revenue for the national purse, various steps that have improved businesses, support for private businesses, and social intervention support that we gave to the vulnerable. By the end of 2019, Ghana was on a good path of economic development, as evidenced by all the macroeconomic indicators, and the world had also recognized us as a nation on the path of progress, and it was in the midst of this that this COVID-19 pandemic struck us. Nations of the world, including Ghana, have all suffered some of the negative effects of this pandemic.

This Covid-19 pandemic has brought various hardships upon us and has fought against our businesses and daily lives. The government has seen the negative impact this pandemic has brought upon the citizens; therefore, the government has made various plans to support businesses so that we can return to our normal state. There is hope because we have identified new ways of operating that will bring many opportunities. The government is determined to ensure that the citizens attain good health; for this reason, it has made plans for every Ghanaian to be vaccinated against this pandemic so that we will all have the health to work to elevate the nation of Ghana to the higher level we all desire.

We have worked together with you through all the difficulties in our ongoing journey of development, and we have taken bold steps to put the nation's economy back on the path of progress. Currently, about 1.2 million Ghanaians have had the opportunity to attend Senior High School, which will transform their lives; we have also fixed the bad situation in the banking sector and the vast majority of people have received their money that was locked up in the collapsed banks. Furthermore, we have established automotive assembly companies in our country and we have seen the first vehicles produced by these companies in car dealerships and on our roads.

The truth is, we acknowledge that we have encountered various challenges while continuing our efforts to take steps toward our development goals. The government is very serious about making plans to find solutions to these challenges so as to bring relief to businesses. An example is that, in the month of May 2020, we supported micro, small, medium, and large enterprises and others with seven hundred and fifty million Ghana cedis (GH¢750 million) under the programme dubbed the “CAP-BuSS” Programme.

Now, it is more crucial than ever that we join hands and unite to continue taking courageous steps that will bring about the development we desire for ourselves. At the very least, we have promised in the National Pledge to serve Ghana with all our strength and all our hearts.

May God bless us all.

Long live Ghana!

Ken Ofori-Atta, Minister of Finance

INTRODUCTION

1. This 2021 Citizens' Budget is a summary of all the government's performance and the amount of revenue the government intends to mobilize to implement its policies and programmes in 2021. The theme for this Budget is "Completion, Consolidation and Continuation to Revitalize the Economy". The Budget bears this theme because in past times, each year's budget contained many new projects the government wanted to implement, while there were many ongoing projects that had not yet been fully completed. When this happens, not only are our finances stretched thin, but we also incur heavy debts to the extent that we are unable to pay contractors who have completed their work on time. This leads to an excessive increase in project costs due to interest payments made on delayed project payments.

2. In this year 2021, the Government has decided to chart a new path. The objective of this new direction the Government wants to take is to bring an end to the situation that has almost become a culture where projects are started and then abandoned midway, and for that reason, the Government is determined to complete all unfinished projects.

HOW THE NATIONAL ECONOMY PERFORMED IN 2020

The state of the nation's economic development foundations

3. The following is how the country's economy was in the year 2020:

• In the first nine months, the country's overall economic growth progressed by about zero point two percent (0.2 percent). However, by the end of the year, it was expected to reach zero point nine percent (0.9 percent);

• Non-oil revenue also increased in the first nine months to 0.4 percent, and is expected to reach 1.6 percent by the end of the year;

Alleviation: This is a way through which the effects of a problem are reduced.

Rearing For Jobs - RFJ: This is a new development initiative introduced by the Government of Ghana, through which poultry farming will be modernized and made profitable, so that poultry meat will become abundant for citizens to consume, drastically reduce the importation of poultry meat into the country, create abundant jobs for citizens to undertake, and improve the living standards of all actors in the poultry value chain.

Social Benefits: This is the money that the Government pays on behalf of the citizens to water, petrol, and electricity companies to relieve the burden on Ghanaians.

Subsidy: This is a financial support that the Government provides to citizens to ease some of their burden and grant them relief.

Teaching And Learning Materials -TLM: These are living and non-living things or people or other creatures that a teacher uses in teaching and learning so that it will help to successfully achieve the teaching and learning objectives.
