Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐ WOREKƆ BAABIARA A, SƐ WO NUA KA SƐ ƆNE WO BƐKƆ A, ƐNDE WO NE NO NKƆ

ƐBAA bi na ɔwɔ hɔ a ɔwo mma baasa. Wɔn mu akumaa no na gyepɔ abu no. Nuabarima panin no ka kyerɛɛ wɔn maame sɛ ɔmma wɔn sika futuro mma wɔnkɔ dwa. Wɔn mu akumaa no nso kaa sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkɔ bi. Wɔkae sɛ ɔnkɔ bi. Ɛna no kaa sɛ wɔne no nyinaa nkɔ. Ɛnna wɔn maame de sikakɔkɔɔ sika a ne boɔ yɛ £5 maa mpaninfoɔ no, na ɔde sikakɔkɔɔ a ne boɔ yɛ £2 nso maa kumaa no. Adeɛ kyeeɛ no, wɔfirii mu kɔe. Mpaninfoɔ no dii kan kɔe gyaa abofra no ma ɔdii wɔn akyi. Abofra no nante bɔkɔɔ kɔe. Mpaninfoɔ no hyiaa ɔbarima bi a na ɔde nsuomnam reba. Ɔka kyerɛɛ mpanimfoɔ no sɛ, "Montɔ!" Wɔbuae sɛ, "Kɔ w’anim, na wobɛhyia abofra bi; ma ɔntɔ, na sɛ ɔka sɛ ɔrentɔ a, boro no na ma ɔntɔ; na sɛ ɔpo sɛ ɔbɛtɔ ara a, fa no na fa no san kɔ ne maame nkyɛn." Ɔbarima no kɔɔ n’anim, na ampa ara ɔhyiaa abofra no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mpanimfoɔ bi a mehyiaa wɔn no kaa sɛ tɔ saa nsuomnam yi." Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ metɔ, berɛ a mpanimfoɔ a wɔkyɛn me antɔ no?" Barima no kaa sɛ, "Wɔkaa sɛ sɛ woantɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ mebɔ wo, na ɛsɛ sɛ mede wo san kɔma wo maame." Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ ahe?" Barima no kaa sɛ, "Sika kɔkɔɔ dodoɔ a ɛyɛ osua (kyerɛ sɛ £2)." Abofra no tuaa ho ka na ɔgyeeɛ. Ɔkɔɔ n'anim, na ɔbɛtoo mpaninfoɔ no wɔ akuraa bi ase. Wɔanoa afan, nanso wɔnni nam biara a wɔde bɛka ho awe. Abɔfra no kaa sɛ, "Nsuomnam a metɔeɛ nie." Wɔde guu nkwan no mu. Wowieeɛ no, mpaninfoɔ no de nam no ti maa no. Berɛ a abɔfra no rebɛbubu mu no, ɔhunuu sika kɔkɔɔ wɔ mu, na ɔde kyekyeree ne ntoma ano. Adekyeeɛ no, berɛ a adeɛ bɛgyee hann no, mpanimfoɔ no sii mu, na berɛ a wɔrenam kwan so no, wɔhyiaa ɔbarima bi, na akokɔnini bi te nneɛma a ɔsoa no atifi. Ɛhɔ ara a ɔbɛduruu mpanimfoɔ no nkyɛn no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Mompɛ akokɔnini yi nni." Wɔbuae sɛ, "Kɔ w'anim, wobɛhyia abofra bi; ma ɔntɔ, na sɛ wampɛ a, bɔ no." Ampa ara ɔkɔhyiaa abofra no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "W'abusuafo mpaninfoɔ kaa sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ wotɔ akokɔnini yi, na sɛ woantɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ mebɔ wo, na mede wo kɔ wo maame nkyɛn." Ɔno nso buaa sɛ, "Gye deɛ mewɔ yi." Ɔbarima no nso de akokɔnini no maa no. Wɔduruu akuraa bi ase. Akuraa no nyinaa, na akokɔnini biara nni hɔ a ɔbɔn. Adeɛ kyeeɛ a adeɛ saeɛ no, akokɔnini a ɔyɛ abɔfra no dea no bɔnee. Akuraa no so panyin no kaa sɛ, "Abaayewa/Abarimaa, fa akokɔnini no bra na mentɔ." Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ne boɔ yɛ sika futuro osua ne suru (kyerɛ sɛ £3)." Panyin no tuaa sika no. Adekyeeɛ a adeɛ mu baeɛ no, wɔfirii aseɛ kɔeɛ bio. Berɛ a wɔrekɔ no, mpanimfoɔ no hyiaa ɔbarima bi a ɔkura agyinamoa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Tɔ kurotwiammansa yi." Mpaninfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Fa kɔ, na wobɛhyia abofra bi; fa ma no ntɔ. Sɛ ɔka sɛ ɔrentɔ a, bɔ no, na fa no sane kɔma ne maame." Ɔbarima no kɔɔ n'anim, na ɔhyiaa abofra no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "W'akumafoɔ se tɔ kurotwiammansa (akraa) yi, na sɛ woantɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ mebɔ wo na mede wo kɔma wo maame bio." Abofra no bisaa sɛ, “Sɛn?” Ɔbarima no kaa sɛ, “Sika dɔteɛ osua ne suru mu duru” (kyerɛ sɛ £3). Abofra no tuaa sika no; na ɔtoaa so kɔe, na ɔkɔtoo n'akumafoɔ wɔ akuraa bi ase. Afei na nkura no taa wewe hɔhene no nan bere biara a ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛda. Bere a abofra no faa agyinamoa no kɔɔ hɔ no, ɔkɔɔ ɔhene no fie na ɔkyeree nkura a na wɔwɔ hɔ no. Odikro no kaa sɛ, “Mɛtɔ kraman yi afiri obi a ɔwɔ no no nkyɛn.” Abofra no kaa sɛ, “Meyɛ me dea.” Odikro no kaa sɛ, “Sɛn?” Abofra no kaa sɛ, “Osua ne suru sikakɔkɔɔ dodoɔ” (kyerɛ sɛ £3). Odikro no faa sikakɔkɔɔ no de hyɛɛ ne nsam. Adekyeeɛ no, berɛ a adeɛ bɛyɛɛ hann no, wɔtuu kwan kɔeɛ. Mpaninfoɔ no kɔɔ wɔn anim; wɔhyiaa akorɔmfoɔ a na wɔawia ɔhene bi funu. Akorɔmfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ motɔ saa funu yi." Mpaninfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Monfa, na monkɔ mo anim, na mobɛhyia abofra bi; monfa nkɔma no na ɔntɔ. Sɛ ɔka sɛ ɔrentɔ a, mommɔ no, na monfa no nkɔma ne maame bio.” Akorɔmfoɔ no kɔɔ wɔn anim, na wɔkɔhyiaa abofra no; wɔkaa sɛ, “Mpaninfoɔ no se tɔ efunu yi.” Abofra no kaa sɛ, “Eei! ɛdeɛn nti na ɛsɛ sɛ metɔ efunu, merentumi nso nsoa; biribiara a mɛyɛ de ayɛ no mpo, ɛrenyɛ me mfasoɔ biara.” Akorɔmfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “W’apaninfoɔ kaa sɛ sɛ woantɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛboro wo, na yɛfa wo, na yɛde wo kɔma wo maame bio.” Abɔfra no bisaa sɛ, “Sɛn?” Wɔkaa sɛ, “Sika futuro a ne dodoɔ yɛ osua ne suru.” Abɔfra no tuaa ɛboɔ no, ɔfaa efunu no, na ɔde no kɔtoo kwaeɛ no mu. Enti ɔtuu kwan, na ɔbaa akuraa bi ase, na ɔkɔɔ fie bi mu kɔsrɛɛ aduane. Ofiewura no kaa sɛ, “Biara nni hɔ.” Abofra no kaa sɛ, “Nana, mepa wo kyɛw.” Ofiewura no kaa sɛ, “Biara nni hɔ.” Abofra no kaa sɛ, “Nana, mepa wo kyɛw.” Ofiewura no kaa sɛ, “Adɛn nti na saa abofra yi ha me saa; yɛn Hene awu, yɛredi mmuada; ɛnnɛ yɛ da a ɛtɔ so nwɔtwe; nanso sɛdeɛ yɛhwehwɛ biara no, yɛnhunu n’amu no.” Akwakoraa bi te hɔ bɛn hɔ; ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma no bi." Ɔbaa no maa no aduane ne nam. Berɛ a abɔfra no wiee aduane no di no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Ofie wura, mahunu Ɔhene no amu; ɛfiri sɛ nnɛra berɛ a mereka ba no, akorɔmfoɔ bi maa metɔeɛ maa osua ne suru sika futuro." Ɔbaa no tuu mmirika kɔeɛ, yiridi, yiridi, yiridi! (na ɛyɛ ne mmirikatuo no nnyegyeeɛ) na ɔkɔbɔɔ (akrowa no mu) mpaninfoɔ no amanneɛ. Wɔfaa abɔfra no, na ɔkɔkyerɛɛ wɔn amu no. Wɔkɔfaa no bae, na wosiee no yiye. Wɔkaa sɛ, “Afei wobɛdi Ɔhene no adeɛ.” Enti abɔfra no bɛyɛɛ Ɔhene. Afei, berɛ a ne nuanom mpanimfoɔ tee ne nka no, wɔbaa hɔ, na afei wɔkaa sɛ wɔne no yɛ mogya korɔ. Abɔfra no kaa sɛ, “Monfirii ha; mennim mo, enti momfiri ha nkɔ.” Na ɔmaa ne nkoa pamoo wɔn kɔe. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, “Sɛ worekɔ baabiara, na sɛ wo nua kumaa ka sɛ ɔne wo bɛkɔ a, fa no kɔ.”

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
