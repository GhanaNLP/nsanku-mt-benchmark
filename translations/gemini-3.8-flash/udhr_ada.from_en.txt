Je Kpɛ He Nimli A He Blɔhi A He Jemi

Hemi nya munyu

Akɛnɛ nɔmlɔ tsuo blɔhi kɛ e he nya jãmi nɛ a fɔ e kɛ ba je nɛ ɔ, nɛ nɔ kake hɛɛ blɔhi nɛ a be nyɛe maa je kɛ je e dɛ nɔ ɔ, e ji he simi kɛ dami kɛ tue bumi fãji kuku nɛ ngɛ je nɛ ɔ mi heje ɔ,

Akɛnɛ nɔmlɔ he blɔhiɛ nɛ a yi kɛ he bami nɛ a jɛ e he a gbe kɔtɔkɔtɔ ninyɛhi kɛ ba, nɛ enɛ ɔ pue nɔmlɔ adesa tsuo he tsui nɔ hwo, nɛ akɛnɛ a fia je ehe ko nɛ adesa tsuo ma ná tui munyu kɛ he hemi kɛ he blɔ kɛ je gbeye yemi kɛ hiami he blɔ mi kɛ ba a he gbɛfa akɛ lɔ ɔ ji nɔ fɛɛ nɔ suɔmi nɛ nɔ kuɔ pe kulaa heje ɔ,

Kɛji a sume nɛ adesaa nɛ bɔ mɔde kaa e maa gbla e he kɛ je yi mi wami kɛ nɔmlɔ he wami kpami he kɛ gu atua tumi nɔ ɔ, e he hia wawɛɛ nɛ mlaa nɛ po adesa he blɔhi a he piɛ.

Akɛnɛ e he hia babauu nɛ a wo huɛbɔmi kpakpa nɛ ngɛ ma amɛ a kpɛti ɔ he wami heje,

Kɛji a susu kaa United Nations ma kpo ɔmɛ ma nɔ mi kɛ gu Mlaa Womi ɔ nɔ kaa a ngɛ hemi kɛ yemi ngɛ adita he blɔ titli ɔmɛ a mi, ngɛ nimli a hɛ mi nyami kɛ a nɔmlɔtɔ mi, kɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi a pɛpɛɛpɛ he blɔhi a mi, nɛ a fia a yi mi kpɔ kaa a maa wo ma si himi nya kɛ ya hie kɛ je mi bami nɛ hi pe nɛ ɔ ngɛ he yeli nɛ kle pe nɛ ɔ mi,

Akɛ Nɔyeli he bimɔhi a si wo kɛ wo a he si kaa a kɛ Je Kɛ Wɛ Nimeli Kpe ɔ maa bɔ kɛ wo buomi kɛ yi nami kɛ ha adesahi a heblɔhi kɛ a he si womihi a nɔ yami he wami nɛ je kɛ wɛ nɛ le,

Akɛ nɔ kake nɛ a kɛ he tɔ kɛ kɔ he blɔhi nɛ ɔmɛ kɛ heyeli nɛ ɔmɛ a he ɔ he hia tso kɛ ha si womi nɛ ɔ he tsuwomi kɛ ya si nyagbe,

Lɔɔ heje ɔ, amlɔ nɛ ɔ,

Kpe Kpeteŋkpele ɔ

Fie Je Kɛ Wɛ Sɔmɛ A Nimli A He Blɔhi A He Jemi nɛ ɔ kaa kɔmɔtɔ he blɔ nɔ blɔ nya nɛ e sa kaa ma amɛ tsuo kɛ nimli amɛ tsuo nɛ a su he, nɛ enɛ ɔ he ɔ, nɔ fɛɛ nɔ kɛ wami he gbogbo fɛɛ gbogbo, nɛ a hɛ kɛ Fiemi nɛ ɔ nɔ be fɛɛ be ɔ, maa bɔ mɔde kɛ gu tsɔsemi kɛ he blɔ kaseemi nɔ kɛ wo bu nɛ a ma ná kɛ ha he blɔ nɛ ɔmɛ kɛ he yehemi nɛ ɔmɛ a he wami, nɛ kɛ gu nɔ ya blɔ nya gbɛji a nɔ, ma mi kɛ je kɛ wɛ tsuo, nɛ a ná jemi tsuo kɛ nitsɛ nitsɛ lemi kɛ yi nɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ, ngɛ Ma Nɛ A Kpa Gba he Nimli amɛ a nitsɛmɛ a kpɛti kɛ ma he nɛ ngɛ a blɔ nya kɛ a wami nya a a kpɛti lɔlɔɔle.

Gba 1

Adetɔmɛ tsuo nɛ a fɔ mɛ ɔ, a ngɛ heyeli, nɛ a tsuo sɔ ngɛ nɔmlɔtɔmi kɛ heblɔhi a mi. A ngɛ juɛmi kɛ he nilee, nɛ e sa kaa a kɛ a he nɛ hi si kaa nyɛmimɛ.

Kuku 2

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ kɛ ha heblɔhi kɛ henyemihi tsuo nɛ a fia he si ngɛ jami nɛ ɔ mi, nɛ kpojiemi ko be, kaa wɛtso, hewomisu, nyumu loo yo, gbi, jamɔ, ma nya he su loo susumi kpa ko, jehe loo wɛtso nɔmlɔ, ninɛnɔ, fɔmi loo blɔ nya kpa ko heje.

Jehanɛ se hu, a be slɔɔto ko kɛ ma mɛnɔ nɔmlɔ ji nɔ ɔ he ma loo zugba a ma-nɔ-yemi, kojomi loo je kɛ wɛ sihimɛ he, ke zugba a ye e he, a kɛ hwo nɔ kpa dɛ mi, e be he-yeli nɛ e nitsɛ kɛ ye e he nɔ, aloo he-yeli gbɔjɔmi kpa ko ngɛ e nɔ jio.

Buka 3

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ kɛ ha wami, he yemi kɛ e nɔmlɔtso he buami.

Kuku 4

A ko be nɔ ko nyɔgu yee loo nɔ ko kɛ lɛ nɛ pee nyɔgu; a tsi nyɔguyemi kɛ nyɔgujua blɔ kɛ je he fɛɛ he kɛ e blɔ nya slɔɔtohi tsuo a nɔ.

Kuku 5

A ko ko tloo yi nɔ hemi aloo nɔ́ ko nɛ e mi wa, nɛ e hɛ mi wami be mi aloo gbagbami aloo hɛ mi si womi tutɔmi.

Kuku 6

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ kaa a kpa le he kaa nɔ ko ngɛ he fɛɛ he ngɛ mlaa hɛ mi.

Kuku 7

Mlaa a hɛ mi ɔ, nɔ tsuaa nɔ sɔ, nɛ nɔ tsuaa nɔ ngɛ he blɔ kaa mlaa a buɔ e he kaa bɔ nɛ e buɔ nɔ kpa he ɔ, nɛ hɛ mi kpo nɔ jemi ko be mi. Nɔ tsuaa nɔ ngɛ he blɔ nɛ a buɔ e he ngɛ hɛ mi kpo nɔ jemi tsuaa nɔ jemi nɛ yaa si Nɔmlɔ He Blɔhi A He Munyu Nɛ Ɔ kɛ gbɛ nya nɛ a guɔ nɔ kɛ tswaa hɛ mi kpo nɔ jemi nɛ ɔ he hewɔ.

Kuku 8

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ blɔ nya kaa e ná kpo nɔ jemi kpakpa ngɛ kɔɔtuhi nɛ a ngɛ he blɔ ngɛ ma a mi ɔ a dɛ mi, kɛ ha níhi nɛ a pee kɛ tɔ̃ e he blɔ titlihi nɛ mlaa kpoo loo ma a mlaa a ha lɛ ɔ a nɔ.

Gba nya 9

A ko hwu nɔ ko kɛ gu yaka no nɔ, aloo a ko wo lɛ tsu, aloo a ko fia lɛ kɛ je ma a mi.

Fakahi 10

Nihi tsuo ngɛ he blɔ kɛ sɔsɔɔsɔ kaa kojolɔhi nɛ a te shi nɛ a tɛ̃ nɛ a yi nɔ́ ko nɔ́ a bu a sane tue ngɛ ma hɛ mi, kɛ fã a blɔhi kɛ a tsuomihi a he, aloo ní yaya ko nɛ a tsɔ̃ a nɔ ngɛ mlaa nya a he.

Kuku 11

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ nɛ a po e he nya ngɛ tɔmi nɛ a gblaa nɔ tɔmi ngɛ he ko he ɔ ngɛ heblɛm nɛ a bu lɛ kaa nɔ nɛ e tɔ we hluu kɛ ya si be nɛ a ma kɛ mlaa ma nyɛ maa tsɔɔ kaa e tɔ ngɛ ma nya sani yemi nɛ e ná blɔ nya fɛɛ blɔ nya nɛ he hia kɛ ha e he jemi kɛ je fɔ yemi ɔ mi.

A be no ko tue gblami nɔ ngɛ tɔmi ko he ngɛ nɔ́ ko nɛ e pee loo e pee we he, ke jamɛ a be ɔ mi nɛ e pee jã a, e pi tɔmi nɛ a fiaa tue ngɛ he ngɛ ma loo je kɛ wɛ mlaa nya. Jã kɛkɛ nɛ a be nɔ ko tue gbla nɛ e mi maa wa pe bɔ nɛ e sa kaa a gbla e tue ngɛ be nɛ a to mlaa a nɔ kɛ tɔ̃ ɔ mi.

Gba 12

Nɔ ko be he blɔ nɛ e maa fĩ nɔ kpa la blɔ nɛ dɛ he kɛ wo e lahemi nina, weku, we mi aloo sɛgbee womihi a mi, aloo e he biɛ kɛ e bumi nina. Nɔ tsuaa nɔ ngɛ he blɔ kɛ ha mlaa nina a buhemi kɛ si basabasa fifĩi aloo tutuhi nɛ ɔmɛ.

Nitsumitɛso 13

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ nɛ e ma nyɛ maa hi kɛ kpe nɛ e ma nyɛ maa hi he nɛ e suɔ ngɛ ma fɛɛ ma he husu ɔmɛ a mi.

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ nɛ e kɛ ma kpo ngɛ ma fɛɛ ma mi, kɛ e nitsɛ e ma mi nouu hu, nɛ e ma nyɛ ma kpale kɛ ya e ma a mi.

Gba 14

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ nɛ e ma nyɛ e hla kɛ ná wekemi ngɛ ma kpahi a nɔ kɛ je yiwalɛ dɛmi.

A be he blɔ nɛ a kɛ blɔ nya nɔ semi nɛ ɔ ma tsu ní ngɛ kɔɔto yaami nɛ je tɔmi nɛ tsa pi ma nya ní tsumi níhi a he loo ní peepeehi nɛ teɔ si kɛ woɔ United Nations yi mi tomihi kɛ nɔmlɔ he blɔhi a he.

Kuku 15

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ ngɛ ma nɔ bimɔ he.

A ko koo kpo nɔ ko ma mi bimi ngɛ e dɛ gu, nɛ a ko koo tsi e nya kɛ je he blɔ nɛ e ngɛ kaa e ma tsake e ma mi bimi ɔ he.

Gba 16

Nyumuhi kɛ yihi nɛ a su jeha nɛ a kɛ sɛ gba si mi ɔ, ke ma, weku aloo jami sisi tsimi ko be a nɔ ɔ, a ngɛ he blɔ nɛ a sɛ gba si mi nɛ a fɔ bimɛ kɛ to weku sisi. A ngɛ he blɔ kake ngɛ gba si himi ɔ mi, gba si himi ɔ be mi, kɛ be nɛ gba a hule si po.

Ke gba si himi nɔ kɛ gu nihi nɛ a to kaa a maa sɛ gba si himi mi ɔ a he pɔ nɛ a je a tsui kpo nɛ a kplɛ nɔ pɛ nɔ.

Weku ji ma zugba nɔ kake peemi kɛ nina nɛ ma hehia kɛha ma a, nɛ e sa kaa ma a kɛ ma nɔyeli ɔ nɛ a bɔ he ba ha lɔɔ.

Fimi 17

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ nɛ e ma nyɛ maa na níhi a nɔ heblɔ kɛ ha e he pɛ aloo kɛ piɛɛ ni kpahi a he.

A ko nɛ a ko kpɔ̃ e níhi a he blɔ ngɛ e dɛ gu kɛkɛ.

Gba 18

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ ngɛ juɛmi, he nilee kɛ jamí heblɔ mi; heblɔ nɛ ɔ biɔ nɔ́ nɛ ji heblɔ nɛ a kɛ tsakeɔ a jamí aloo a hemi kɛ yemi, kɛ heblɔ nɛ a kɛ tsɔɔ a jamí aloo a hemi kɛ yemi kpo ngɛ nitsɔɔmi, nítsumi, jamí kɛ kusumi nɔ yemi mi, ke e ji nɔ kake pɛ aloo ngɛ ni kpomí mi kɛ ha ma nɔ aloo ngɛ laami mi.

Kuku 19

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ ngɛ e susu kɛ gbi jemi he; heblɔ nɛ ɔ biɔ heblɔ kɛ ha susumahi nɛ a mɔɔ a mi nɛ basabasa yemi ko be mi, kɛ nɔ́ nɛ kɔɔ nilee kɛ susumahi a hlahemi, a hemi kɛ a gbami kɛ gu blɔ fɛɛ blɔ nɔ nɛ huse ko be mi he.

Kuku 20

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ ngɛ hejɔmi kpe kɛ he bimɔ kpe mi.

A be nɔ ko nyɛe nɛ a ná heblɛ nya kaa e ya piɛɛ kuuli a he.

Kuku 21

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ he blɔ nɛ e kɛ e he ma wo e ma a nɔ yemi mi, kɛ gu e nitsɛ e he nɔ, aloo kɛ gu ma nimeli nɛ a hla kɛ he jɔmi mi ɔmɛ a nɔ.

Nɔ tsuaa nɔ ngɛ he blɔ kɛ ha ma sɔmɔmi ní tsumihi ngɛ e ma a mi nɛ slɔɔto be mi.

Ma nya suɔmi nɛ maa pee nɔyemi hewami ɔ nɔ daami nɔ; a maa je nɛ kɛ ma nya suɔmi nɛ ɔ kpo ngɛ dumi be kɛ be mli kɛ dumi nitsɛmɛ a mli, nɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ nɛ gba nɔ kɛ su kake nya, nɛ a maa pee ngɛ laami mli dumi aloo dumi blɔ nɔ nɛ kake pɛ nɛ ngɛ he je.

Fã 22

Nimli tsuo, kɛji a ji ma nɔbii ɔ, ngɛ heblɔ ngɛ ma mi bɔɔbɔɔ nina mi, nɛ a ngɛ heblɔ nɛ a ná, kɛ gu ma a mɔde bɔmi kɛ jeŋ maamɛ a kpaami nina mi, nɛ e kɔ kɛ ya he nɔ tomi kɛ ma he wami nɛ ma tsuaa ma ngɛ ɔ he, nɛ a ná heblɔ ngɛ sika, ma nina, kɛ kusii he kɛ ha a hemi kɛ a buami, kɛ e nɔmlɔtso mi nɔ yami nɛ he jɔmi ngɛ mi.

Gba 23

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ nɛ e ma tsu ní, nɛ e ma hla nítsumi nɛ e suɔ kɛ ha e he, nítsumi he blɔ nya tomihi nɛ da nɛ a hi, kɛ nítsumi he buemi ngɛ be mi nɛ nítsumi be.

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ, nɛ dlami ko be mi, ngɛ heblɔ nɛ a wo lɛ nyɔhiɔ kake ngɛ ní tsumi kake he.

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ nɛ tsuɔ ní ɔ ngɛ he blɔ kɛ ha hiɔ womi nɛ da kɛ nɔ nɛ sa, nɛ maa ha lɛ kɛ e weku ɔ wami he nitsuní nɛ sa adesa hɛ mi nyami, nɛ ke e hia a, a kɛ he buemi blɔ kpa komɛ ma piɛɛ he kɛ ha lɛ.

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ kaa e toɔ nitsumikui a hekpetimi kpehi kɛ yaa e mi koni e kɛ bu e he kɛ e hiemi nɔ.

Kuku 24

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɛ nɛ e kɛ ma he e hɛ mi kɛ hejɔɔmi, heblɛ nɛ kɔɔ nitsumi be nɛ sa he, kɛ hejɔɔmi behi nɛ a woɔ he hiɔ.

Gba 25

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ ngɛ si himi kpakpa nɛ da he ha lɛ kɛ e weku ɔ a he wami kɛ a si himi kpakpa, nɛ niye nii, tade womi, we kɛ he wami kpɔmi kɛ he wami dɔmi yemi kɛ buami nɛ he hia ngɛ mi, kɛ heblɔ ngɛ he bumi he ke e ji kaa ní tsumi be, hiɔ kami, hegbanyami, kualɔ yemi, bimɔ aloo níhi kpa nɛ hɛɛ wami nɛ be nɛ pi lɛ e lɔɔ.

Bi kɛ nyɛ nɛ fɔ lɛ sa nɛ a ná lami kɛ yelikɛbuami titli. Bimɛ tsuo, ke a fɔ mɛ ngɛ gba si jemi mi jio, loo a fɔ mɛ kɛ je kpo jio, a tsuo sa nɛ a ná ma nya he bami kake ɔ nɔuu.

Gba 26

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ ngɛ sukuu yaami he. Sukuu yaami maa pee mahuɔ, titli ngɛ we dukɔ kɛ he nɛ a jeɔ sisi ngɛ sukuu yaami mi ɔ. We dukɔ sukuu yaami maa pee nɔ́ nɛ e sa kaa a ya nɛ gbeye tsɛ̃e. Nitsumi kɛ dade he nile sukuu yaami maa pee nɔ́ nɛ ma ya nɔ ha nɔ fɛɛ nɔ nɛ sukuu he kpakpa yaami hu maa pee nɔ́ nɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ nɛ sa ma nyɛ ma ya ngɛ blɔ nɛ da nɔ.

Nikasemi maa kɔ kɛ ya nɔmlɔ su nɛ hi pɛpɛɛpɛ hami nɔ, kɛ nɔmlɔ heblɔhi kɛ he yehemi agbo ɔmɛ a buhemi nɔ womi nɔ. E maa ha nɛ sisi numi, kɛ tsui si himi, kɛ huɛbɔmi maa hi ma amɛ tsuo a kpɛti, kɛ jami loo wetso kpahi a kpɛti, nɛ e maa ya United Nations nítsumi ɔmɛ nɛ kɔɔ tue gbli himi he ɔ a hɛ mi.

Fɔli ngɛ he blɔ kɛ je kekle jeha a mi kaa a ma hla nɔ́ he nitsɔɔmi nɛ e sa kaa a kɛ ha a bimɛ.

Kuku 27

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ nɛ e kɛ e he nɛ wo ma a kusumi nítsumihi a mi ngɛ he yemi mi, nɛ e bua nɛ jɔ ga níhi a he, nɛ e ná ní lemi he kpe yami kɛ e mi se namihi a mi gbɛfa.

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ ngɛ jehanɔ kɛ helo nya nimli a hiemi kɛ ha níhi nɛ je jeŋ nile, womi ngma kɛ adebɔ níhi a mi nɛ e je e tsui mi nɛ e bɔ ɔ he.

Blɛfo 28

Nihi tsuo ngɛ he blɔ kɛ ha je blɔ nya tomi kɛ ma nimeli a blɔ nya tomi nɛ ha nɛ he blɔhi kɛ heyeli nɛ a kale ngɛ mlaa nɛ ɔ mi ɔ ma ba mi ngɛ e blɔ nya tutuutu.

Kuku 29

Nɔ tsuaa nɔ ngɛ blɔ nya ní tsumi kɛ ha ma a he nɛ lɛ nɔuu nɛ nɔ ko ma nyɛ maa ná he blɔ kɛ je e tsui mi nɛ e jehumi nɛ hi nɛ e nyɛ nɛ e wa ngɛ tsui nɔ.

Ke nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ e he blɔhi kɛ e he yemihi kɛ ngɛ tsuɔ ní ɔ, nɔmlɔhi kɛ hyehyɛɛhi nɛ mlaa kɛ to pɛ nɛ maa ba e nɔ, konɛ a kɛ ná nɔ yeli kɛ bumi kɛ ha ni kpahi a he blɔhi kɛ a he yemihi, kɛ kɛ ha je mi bami kpakpa, ma mi tue gblii kɛ ma ha ma kɛ e he nami he hia níhi nɛ da ngɛ ma nɛ ngɛ he yemi kɛ blɔhi a mi.

A he blɔhi kɛ heyeli nɛ ɔmɛ nɛ a ná a, a be nyɛe maa kɛ tsu ní ngɛ blɔ ko blɔ ko nɔ nɛ e maa te si kɛ wo United Nations ɔ yi numi kɛ e sisi tomi mlaahi.

Gba 30

Nɔ́ ko nɛ ngɛ Hɛmi Kɛ Ngɔmi Se Enɛ ɔ mi ɔ, a be nyɛe maa kɔ kɛ ha ma ko, kuu ko, aloo nɔ ko kaa e ngɛ he blɔ nɛ e ma kɛ tsu ní ko, aloo e pee nɔ́ ko nɛ ma kpata he blɔhi kɛ he yehemihi nɛ a fia ngɛ he blɔ kɛ he yehemi he munyu nɛ ɔ mi ɔ kɛ ya hɛ mi ɔ a he.
