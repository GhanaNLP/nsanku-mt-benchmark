Nomlo He Gbɔnaahi A Kpokplonto Nɔ Gbi Kɛ Sɔle

Kekle Munyu

Akɛnɛ nɔmlɔ tsuo nɛ a fɔ lɛ ɔ hɛ mi nyami kɛ e he blɔhi nɛ a kɛ fɔ lɛ nɛ mɔ ko be nyɛe maa je e dɛ nɛ a ma ná le he kpakpa ji he yemi, dami kɛ tue gbli nami he simi kuku ngɛ je ɔ mi he je ɔ,

Kɛji a bui adesahi a he blɔhi nɛ a kɛ he kuɔ si ɔ ha nɛ níhi nɛ a hɛ mi ngɛ nyagbahi nɛ a dɔ adesahi a tsui nitsɛ ba, nɛ a fa kɛ fia je ɔ mi tsuo ke nimli tsuo ma ná he blɔ kɛ tu munyu kɛ he ye, nɛ gbeye kɛ hiami be a nɔ yee hu ɔ ji nɔ́ nɛ adesahi tsuo ngɛ hloe nitsɛ,

Akɛnɛ e he hia babauu kaa mlaa nɛ baa adita heblimahi a he kɛkɛ nɛ a be nɔ ko nyɛe nɛ e kɛ tso kɛ to guɔ yemi kɛ nɔ nitsumi nɔ nya heje ɔ,

Akɛnɛ e he hia babauu nɛ a wo huɛbɔmi kpakpa nɛ ngɛ je ma amɛ a kpɛti ɔ he wami heje ɔ,

Kɛjɛ bɔnɛ Je Hanɔ Maji ɔmɛ wo a he si ngɛ mlaa womi ɔ mi kɛ kɔ hegbɛ nɛ amɛ ngɛ ngɛ adesa hegbɛ nɛ hiɛ kɛ ya tɔ he, adesa he su kɛ e he jãmi kɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi a hegbɛ nɛ sɔ he, nɛ a fia a pɛɛ si kɛ ha jami kɛ he si himi kpakpa ngɛ he yeli nɛ mi gba mi ɔ heje ɔ,

Akɛ nɔ́ nɛ Jeŋmahi nɛ a ngɛ kpe ɔ mi ɔ wo si kaa a kɛ United Nations ɔ ma pee kake kɛ dla nimli tsuo a bumi kɛ nɔ́ nɛ a le ngɛ adita he blɔhi kɛ he jemi he blɔ kpɛti nɛ he hia pe kulaa a nɔ,

Kaa bɔ nɛ heblɛmahi kɛ yei nɛ he si kami nɛ ɔmɛ a sisi numi kake ji nɔ́ nɛ he hia pe kulaa kɛ ha si womi nɛ ɔ mi nya himi nitsɛ nitsɛ,

Jehanɔ nɛ ɔ, looo

Kpe Ngua A

E fĩa adafi kɛ kɔ Je Kɛ Wɛ Nimli A He Blɔhi A He Fami Nɛ Ɔ he kaa yi mi tomi nɛ ma hi si kɛ ha maamli kɛ jehi tsuo, konɛ nɔ tsuaa nɔ kɛ kpehi tsuo nɛ ngɛ ma a mi, kɛ nina nɛ ngɛ kã nɛ ɔ nɛ a kɛ ma a tsui mi be fɛɛ be ɔ, nɛ a bɔ mɔde kɛ gu tsɔɔmi kɛ heblɔ nɛ a kɛ ha nɛ a wo blɔhi kɛ heblɔhi nɛ ɔmɛ a hɛ mi nyami, kɛ gu blɔhi nɛ ma ya a hɛ mi nɛ a kpa ngɛ ma a kɛ je kɛ wɛ mi, konɛ a na nɛ a le kɛ a nɔ yami nɛ tsu ní ngɛ je kɛ wɛ tsuo, ngɛ ma nimli nɛ a ngɛ Maami Nɛ Kpe ɔmɛ a mi ɔmɛ a kpo mi kɛ ngɛ nimli nɛ a ngɛ kpokpo nɛ ngɛ a nɔ yemi sisi ɔmɛ a kpo mi tsuo.

Kuku 1

Adesahi tsuo a fɔ mɛ he yemi kɛ nɔ yemi kɛ blɔhi a mi nyɛmimii. A ha mɛ juɛmi kɛ he nilee nɛ e sa kaa a kɛ a he nɛ hi si kaa nyɛmimɛ.

Baafa 2

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ ngɛ heyeli kɛ he blɔhi tsuo nɛ a fɔ he munyu ngɛ Munyu nɛ ɔ mi ɔ he, nɛ slɔɔto ko be ngɛ he kɛ ha nɔ ko, kaa kɛ ji nɔ kuku, he wami, nyumu aloo yo, gbi, jamɔ, ma nya munyu aloo susumii kpa ko, jehe aloo sihimɛ he, nihi nɛ a ngɛ, fɔmi aloo nɔ́ kpa ko.

Jehanemɔ se ɔ, a be slɔɔto ko kpae ngɛ ma aloo zugba nɛ nɔ ko je mli ɔ ma nya sisi tomi, e mlaa blɔ nya he blɔhi, aloo je kɛ wɛ wekukpami he, ke zugba a ye e he, e ngɛ nɔ yemi ko sisi, e be nɔ yemi nɛ e yeɔ e he nɔ, aloo e ngɛ blɔ nya kpatami kpa ko sisi ngɛ he blɔ nɛ e ngɛ kɛ yeɔ e he nɔ ɔ he.

Kuku 3

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ ngɛ wami, he yemi kɛ nɔmlɔtso he bami he.

Fã 4

A ko be nɔ ko nyɔgu gbee loo koli mi wue; a ko tsu nyɔgu yemi kɛ nyɔgu jua he ní blɔ nya fɛɛ blɔ nya nɛ e maa gu nɔ.

Kuku 5

A ko ko dukɔ nɔ ko, loo a ko ye nɔ ko ayilɔ, a ko pee nɔ ko níhi nɛ tsoo kaa a bui lɛ nɔmlɔ, loo a gbla nɔ ko tue nɛ maa dɔ lɛ wawɛɛ nɛ e ha e tsui nɔ na loo e he nɛ ba si.

Fimii 6

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ he blɔ kaa a le lɛ he fɛɛ he kaa nɔ ko ngɛ mlaa a hɛ mi.

Kuku 7

Nimli tsuo sɔ ngɛ mlaa a hɛ mi nɛ a sa nɛ a ná mlaa a he buumi nɛ sɔ kɛ je mlami fɛɛ mlami mi. Nimli tsuo sa nɛ a ná he buumi nɛ sɔ kɛ si mlami fɛɛ mlami nɛ kɛ Sɔlemi Nɔ́ Nɛ O he nɔ́ ko tɔ̃ɔ, kɛ si tutumi fɛɛ tutumi kɛ ya nina nɛ ɔ he.

Kuku 8

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ kaa ma a kojolɔhi nɛ a ngɛ he wami ɔ maa kojo e sane nɛ a ye ha lɛ ke a tɔ̃ heblɔhi nɛ ma a mlaa amɛ kɛ ha lɛ ɔ nɔ.

Kuku 9

A ko ko pɛ fia a ko nu lɛ kɛ ya wo tsu mi, aloo nɛ a fie lɛ kɛ je ma a mi e he guɔ nɛ dɔmɔ be mi.

Fã 10

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɛ nɛ kɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ ngɛ kojoɔ nya nɛ a nu e nya kɛje ma fɛɛ ma hɛ mi kɛ gu sane kojoli nɛ a be nɔ ko fã kɛ nɔ nɛ he ye mi, kɛ fã e heblɛhi kɛ e blɔ nya níhi a mi, kɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ nɛ a ti e he tɔmi kɛ wo e nɔ.

Kuku 11

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ nɛ a po e he fɔ ngɛ mlaa tɔmi ko he ɔ ngɛ he blɔ kaa a bu lɛ kaa e he tsɔ kɛ yaa si be nɛ a ma tsɔɔ kaa e gbe fɔ ngɛ mlaa nya ngɛ ma hɛ mi kojo ko nɛ a ha lɛ he blɔ fɛɛ he blɔ nɛ he hia kɛ ha e he buli ngɛ mi ɔ mi.

A be nɔ ko nɔ fɔ buue kɛ ha yayami ko nɛ e pee aloo nɛ e gbo nya, nɛ be nɛ e pee jã a, tɔmi ko be mi ngɛ ma aloo ma amɛ a he mlaa nya. Jã kɛnɛ nɛ a be nɔ ko gblave gbee nɛ mi wa pe nɔ́ nɛ mlaa kɛ to be nɛ a pee yayami ɔ.

Kuku 12

Nɔ ko nɔ ko be he blɔ nɛ e kɛ he nɛ fia nɔ ko laami ninyɛɛmihi, e weku, e we mi, aloo e sɛgbehi a mi kɛ yayami, loo e gbe e he guɛ kɛ e biɛ kɛ fia si. Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ blɔ nya nɛ mlaa maa po e he piɛ kɛ si laami ninyɛɛmihi loo he guɛ gbee nɛ ɔmɛ.

Fiba 13

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ nɛ e ma nyɛ maa hi he fɛɛ he kɛ ya he fɛɛ he ngɛ ma fɛɛ ma mi.

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ he blɔ kaa e maa je ma fɛɛ ma mi, kɛ e ma nitsɛ hu mi, nɛ e maa kpale kɛ ya e ma a nɔ.

Kuku 14

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ nɛ e kɛ hla hegbɛ kɛ je yiwalɛ dɛ he kɛ ya hi ma kpa nɔ.

A be he blɔ nɛ a kɛ blɔ nya nɔ semi nɛ ɔ ma tsu ní ngɛ sane yemihi nɛ a je tɔmihi nɛ pi ma nya sanehi aloo nifeemihi nɛ te si kɛ wo Je Sɔlemi Kpe ɔ (United Nations) yi mi tomihi kɛ e sisi tomi mlaahi a he mi.

Gba 15

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ he blɔ kɛ ha ma mli binyɛ.

A be nɔ ko nɔ tɔɔ kɛ je e ma mi bimi mi basabasa, aloo a be lɛ he blɔ ngɔɔ nɛ e kɛ ma kake e ma mi bimi nɔ́ ɔ kpa.

Fã 16

Nyumuhi kɛ yihi nɛ a sɛ kpe si, nɛ jãmi, jehanɛ se he womi aloo ma nya gbami ko be a nɔ ɔ, a ngɛ he blɔ kaa a gbaa kɛ to weku. A ngɛ he blɔ kake ngɛ gba si himi mi, gba si himi ɔ be mi, kɛ be nɛ a gbaa gba si himi ɔ mi.

Gba si himi baa nyɛ̃ maa hi si ngɛ he ja a pɛ kɛ gu suɔmi nɛ je yi blɔ kɛ bɔ nɛ hi kɛ kɔ nihi nɛ a to a he blɔ nya kaa a ma gba a a kpɛti.

Weku ji adesahi a kpe he nɔ́ ko nɛ ngɛ gbɔɔ kɛ titi, nɛ e sa kaa ma a kɛ Ma No ɔ nɛ a bu e he.

Gba 17

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ he blɔ kaa e ná nɔ́ e kɛ peee nɔ́, jã kɛ̃ nɔ́ nɛ e kɛ ni kpa amɛ kpe kɛ ná.

A kɔ we nɔ ko nɔ́ ngɛ e dɛ mi yaka gu kɛtsɔ gbɔjɔɔ nɔ.

Kpɔ 18

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ he blɔ ngɛ jwɛmi, he nilee kɛ jami he; he blɔ nɛ ɔ biɔ he blɔ nɛ a kɛ tsakeɔ nɔ jami loo hemi kɛ yemi, kɛ he blɔ nɛ a kɛ jeɔ nɔ jami loo hemi kɛ yemi ɔ kpo ngɛ tsɔsemi, nitsumi, jami kɛ kusumihi anɔ kɛpee kɛtsɔ nɔ kake peemi loo ni kpahi a he, ngɛ ma se loo laami mi.

Fã 19

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ ngɛ juɛmi kɛ he susumi he; heblɔ nɛ ɔ biɔ nɛ nɔ ko bɔɔ he juɛmi nɛ nɔ ko be lɛ gigie nɛ e hla, e he, kɛ e gba sanehi kɛ susumihi kɛ gu blɔ fɛɛ blɔ nɔ nɛ husu ko be he kpee.

Fã 20

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ kɛ ha tuegbee kpeemi kɛ heblɔ kɛ ha kpeemi akuahi a mi.

A ko nyɛ we nɔ ko nɔ kaa e ya piɛɛ kuu ko he.

Bake 21

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ nɛ e ma fĩ e ma a nɔ yemi ɔ mi, e kɛtsɔ e nitsɛ e he nɔ jã loo kɛtsɔ nɔ́tseli nɛ a hla mɛ kɛ he jɔmi nɔ.

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ he blɔ kɛ ha ma ní tsumi ngɛ e ma a mi pɛpɛɛpɛ kɛ ji nɔ kpa amɛ.

Ma nya suɔmi nɛ maa pee nɔyeli he wami ɔ nya; a maa je nɛkɛ suɔmi nɛ ɔ kpo ngɛ kɔne womi nɛ a maa pee be fɛɛ be nɛ a be lakpa yee ngɛ mi ɔ mi, nɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ nɛ su ma nɔ maa ya nɛ a tsuo a he maa su kɛ kɔne womi ɔ, nɛ a maa gu laami mi kɔne womi aloo he je blɔ nɔ kɔne womi kɛ da kpa ko nɔ kɛ pee.

Gba 22

Nimli tsuo, kɛji e ji ma a mi no ko o, ngɛ heblɛ ngɛ ma a mi bami he, nɛ e sa kaa e na, kɛ gu ma a mi mɔde bɔmi kɛ je ma nya kake peemi nɔ, nɛ e kɛ ma tsuaa ma tomi kɛ e ninɔ ngɔmi sa, hiɛ ko nitsɛ e we jami, ma a mi kɛ kusii nɔ heblɛhi nɛ he hia ha e bubu kɛ e he nya he nɔ yami ngɛ he yeji mi.

Kuku 23

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɛ nɛ e ma tsu ní, nɛ e ma hla nítsumi nɛ suɔ, nítsumi he blɔ nya tɔ̃ɔhi nɛ da nɛ a he ngɛ fɛu, kɛ he bulɛ kɛ si nítsumi he he blɔ nya nɛ be.

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ, nɛ hɛ mi kpo nɔ jemi ko be mi, ngɛ he blɔ kɛ ha hiɔ nɛ sɔ kɛ ha ní tsumi nɛ sɔ.

Nihi tsuo nɛ a tsuɔ ní ɔ ngɛ he blɔ kɛ ha hiɔ nɛ da nɛ hi nɛ maa ha lɔɔ nɛ e weku ɔ maa ná si himi nɛ sa nɔmlɔ he womi, nɛ ke e hia a, a kɛ he buemi kpahi nɛ kɔɔ si himi he nɛ piɛɛ he.

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ nɛ e ma bɔ aloo e ma piɛɛ nitsumitsɛmɛi a kpehi a he konɛ e kɛ fua e yi mi tomihi a he.

Fã 24

Nihi tsuo ngɛ he blɔ kɛ ha hejɔɔmi kɛ bua jɔmi, nɛ enɛ ɔ biɔ nɛ a kase nitsumi behi a nya bɔ nɛ sa, kɛ behi nɛ a kɛ ma jɔɔ a he nɛ a ma he hiɔ.

Kuku 25

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ ngɛ si himi kpakpa nɛ da he ha lɔ̃ kɛ e weku ɔ he wami kɛ a nami, nɛ lɔɔ biɔ niye ní, tade womi, we kɛ tsofa yemi kɛ jemi kɛ blɔ nya tomihi nɛ he hia, kɛ heblɔ ngɛ he buami he ke e be ní tsumi, ke e be he wami, ke gbɔjɔmi ba e nɔ, yalɔyo si himi, nɔkɔtɔmahi a si himi aloo hiami kpa ko ngɛ si himi nɛ be e dɛ mi.

Nyɛmimɔ kɛ jokuɛmimɔ ngɛ he blɔ kɛ ha lami kɛ yemi kɛ buami he kpo nɛ a to kɛ fɔ si. Jokuɛwi tsuo, ke a fɔ mɛ ngɛ gba si aloo gba se jio, ma na he buami kake nɔuu ngɛ ma a mi.

Gba 26

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ kɛ ha womi kami. Womi kami maa hi nya gu, titli ɔ ngɛ jã kami kɛ dade nya womi kami hehi. Jã kami womi kami maa pee nɔ́ nɛ e sa nɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ nɛ e kase ngɛ nɔnyɛɛ nya. Dɛhi a kɔni nitsumi kɛ nikasemi kpatoli a he womi kami maa hi ngɛ blɔ kɛ ha nɔ fɛɛ nɔ, nɛ womi kami kpekpɛpɛ maa hi kɛ ha nɔ fɛɛ nɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ kɛ fã he nɛ nɔ ɔ he maa su.

Ní kaseemi maa kɛ e juɛmi maa fɔ adesa he nami nɛ e maa ba he wami saminya kɛ adesa he blɔhi kɛ he blɔ titlihi nɛ a ngɛ ɔ nɛ a buɔ kɛ ha nɔmlɔ ɔ nɔ womi nɔ. E maa ha nɛ sisi nunɔ, tsui kpeemi kɛ huɛ bɔmi maa ba je ma amɛ tsuo a kpɛti, wɛtso loo jami kuuhi a kpɛti, nɛ e maa ya nɔ kɛ wo Je Kpa Si Gbami Kutsu (United Nations) ɔ ní tsumihi a he wami kɛ ha he jɔmi he buami.

Fɔli ngɛ he blɔ kɛ je kekleje nɛ a kɛ ma hla nɔ́ he tsɔsemi nɛ a kɛ ma ha a bimɛ.

Kuku 27

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ nɛ e kɛ he simi nɛ gba ma a kusumi womi he kɛ bɔ nɛ e suɔ, nɛ e gba gajelɔhi a nítsumihi a he, nɛ e ná jã kɛ je jehe nílé kɛ e he se namihi a mi.

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ heblɔ nɛ a bu jehanu kɛ helo nya nina nɛ jeɔ jeŋ kpo sɔumi, womi ngmaami aloo ga nihi nɛ kɔɔ he nɛ lɛ nitsɛ e pee ɔ he.

Fia 28

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ blɔ kɛ ha ma kɛ je kɛ wɛ mlɛ kplɛkii nɛ hehi nɛ a kɛ he blɔhi kɛ he yelimihi nɛ a tsɔɔ he munyu ngɛ Munyu Kɛ Fami Nɛ Ɔ mi ɔ ma nyɛ maa tsu ní kɛ e he wami tsuo ngɛ mi.

Kuku 29

Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ blɔ nya níhi kɛ ha ma a he nɛ nɔmlɔ he blɔhi a he kplɔkɔtɔ kɛ a mi nɛ maa hi he kɛkɛ nɛ ma nyɛ maa ba.

Ke nɔ ko ngɛ e he blɔhi kɛ e he yemihi kɛ tsuɔ ní ɔ, blɔ nya tomihi nɛ mlaa kɛ to pɛ nɛ e maa hi sisi, konɛ a kɛ ha nɛ nɔ tsuaa nɔ nɛ le nɛ e bu ni kpahi a he blɔhi kɛ a he yemihi, kɛ kɛ ha nɛ jehanɛ se hu a nyɛ nɛ a tsu nimli a jehemi kpakpa he hia níhi, ma mi tue gbee, kɛ ma kɛ ma mi bi tsuo a he jɔmi he níhi a he ní ngɛ ma nɛ ma mi bi lɛ a yeɔ a he nɔ mi.

A he blɔhi kɛ heyeli nɛ ɔmɛ nɛ a ko kɛ tsu ní gblegbleegble konɛ e kɔni kɛ te si kɛ wo United Nations ɔ yi mi tomihi kɛ e sisi tomi mlaahi.

Fimɔ 30

A ko tsɔɔ nɔ ko nɛ ngɛ Kita Nɛ Ɔ mi se kɛ tsɔɔ kaa e ha Ma, kuu, loo nɔ ko heblii nɛ e kɛ tsu níhi loo nɛ e pee nɔ́ ko nɛ ma kpa heblii kɛ blɔhi nɛ a fĩ se ngɛ hiɛ ɔ a kpɛti kake tutua ko si.
