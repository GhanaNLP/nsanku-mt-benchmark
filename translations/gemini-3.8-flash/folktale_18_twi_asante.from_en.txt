Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA KƆNTƆNRƆKYE BAAYƐ ABUSUAKUO NO MU

Wɔse ɔbɔfofoɔ bi kɔɔ kwaem pɛn; ɔbaa bɛhwɛɛ amena bi mu, na nkurɔfoɔ a na wɔwɔ mu gyina hɔ ne ’Nipa, Onipa no, Osebo, Ɔsebɔ no, Owo, Ɔwɔ no, ne ’Kusie, Okusie no. Afei berɛ a saa nkorɔfoɔ yi hunuu Ɔbɔfoɔ no, deɛ ɔwɔ ha yi kaa sɛ, “Agya Bɔfoɔ, bra bɛyi me firi ha.” Deɛ ɔwɔ hɔ no nso kaa sɛ, “Agya, bra bɛyi me firi ha.” Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Meremma, efisɛ ɔkyena koraa mpo, sɛ megye wo na wo nsa ka me a, deɛ wobɛyɛ me no (menim) merensuro ho hwee.” Deɛ ɔwɔ ha yi kaa sɛ, “Merentumi (nyɛ saa da).” Deɛ ɔwɔ hɔ no nso kaa sɛ, “Merentumi (nyɛ saa da).” Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ , “Sɛ ɛte saa ampa a, mɛyi mo afi mu.” Na nokwarem no, ɔyii nnipa no nyinaa fii mu kɔɔ (ne) kuro mu. Da koro bi, berɛ a Ɔbɔfoɔ no wɔ hɔ no, Ɔsebɔ de nam pii bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Agya Bɔfoɔ, yei yɛ aseda a mede ma wo wɔ papayɛ a woyɛɛ me no ho, meda wo ase, Apiafo.” Ɔde nam kɛntɛn dumien kɔmaa Ɔbɔfoɔ no. Ɔbɔfoɔ no daa no ase na Ɔsebɔ no kɔe. Da foforɔ bi, na Ɔbɔfoɔ no te hɔ, na Okusie bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Agya Bɔfoɔ, wobɛpɛ sikakɔkɔɔ mfuturo bi anaa?” Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Hoo! adɛn nti na mempɛ, ɛno na nnipa pɛ a wɔbrɛ no.” Okusie sɔree, na ɔkɔɔ faako a Nyankopɔn de ne sikakɔkɔɔ mfuturo siee no. Ɔkɔe kɔtuee amena wɔ dan no ase, na ɔwuraa dan no mu, na ɔkɔfaa Nyankopɔn sika futuro no nyinaa, de brɛɛ Ɔbɔfoɔ no. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Meda wo ase, Aku.” Na Okusie no kɔe. Da foforɔ bi, na ɔte hɔ, na ɔtee sɛ obi rekyea no sɛ, “Ago.” Ɔbɔfoɔ no gyee so sɛ, “Ame.” Onipa no bae; ɔkaa sɛ, “Agya Ɔbɔfoɔ, maakye.” Ɔno (Ɔbɔfoɔ no) nso gyee so sɛ, “Yoo, abusuafoɔ.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Nsafufuo kuku ketewa bi nie a mede reba abɛda wo ase.” Agya Ɔbɔfoɔ kaa sɛ, “Memmpɛ; wo deɛ, Onipa, sɛdeɛ mekaeɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ no na aba no, berɛ a mekaa sɛ merenyi wo no; hwɛ deɛ Ɔsebɔ de brɛɛ me, hwɛ deɛ Okusie de brɛɛ me.” Onipa de ne nsafufuo no kɔe na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ Nyankopɔn sɛ, “Wo sika futuro a ɛyerae no, saa Okusie no na ampa ara ɔbɛfae de kɔmaa Ɔbɔfoɔ no.” Nyankopɔn kaa sɛ, “Ɛte saa?” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Nana, ɛyɛ nokware turodoo.” Nyankopɔn somaa abɔfoɔ sɛ wɔnkɔkye Ɔbɔfoɔ no, na wɔde ahoma kyekyeree n'asene. Wɔbɔɔ no basabasa. Afei berɛ a wɔne Ɔbɔfoɔ no duruu kwan no so no, ɔkaa sɛ, “Mpaninfoɔ, merepɛ akɔ agya nan so.” Asofoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Dabi, owifoɔ, wobɛdwane.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Dabi, merendwane, na sɛ mopɛ a, momfa ahoma no nkyekyere me sena mu, na montehye dukuu bi ho, na momma me kwan ma menkɔ.” Asofoɔ no tiee no, na wɔmaa no kwan ma ɔkɔe. Ɔwɔ hɔ no, ɔtenaa ase bɔkɔɔ, berɛ a abɔdeɛ tenten a ne ho yɛ fefeefefe no baeɛ. Ɔkae sɛ, “Hwɛ ha, me nnamfo, gye saa aduro kakra a ɛyɛ sɛ dɔte kɔn yi, na fa ka wo ho, na sɛ wokɔ na wɔde wo gyina asafo no anim, na ɛba sɛ wɔbɛkum wo a, mɛkɔ Ɔhene no mpena dan mu, na makɔka ne yere a ɔdɔ no paa no ba; sɛ meka no a, wobɛhunu sɛ obiara asɔre, na wo nso wobɛkasa aka sɛ, ‘Mewɔ aduro,’ na sɛ wode bɛka abofra no ho a, ne ho bɛtɔ no.” Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Meda wo ase, Aku.” Asofoɔ no faa Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔgyinaa Nyankopɔn anim, (n’anim ne n’Aginafoɔ) Kontire, Akwum’, Adonten, Gyaase, ne Kyidom ahemfo anim. Ɔbɔfoɔ no maa n’asɛm so, na berɛ a Ɔkyeame no sɔre sɛ ɔrebu atɛn no, wɔtee osuo nnyigyei bi, pui! pui! wɔ ahemfie mman mu akyirikyiri kɔɔ e. Wɔbisaa sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na asie?" Wɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔwɔ aka Nyankopɔn yere dɔfoɔ ba no." Obiara firii hɔ kɔe. Afei na Ɔbɔfoɔ no gyina hɔ; ɔkaa sɛ, “Sɛ manyɛ deduani a, anka mɛnya aduro (aduro a ɛko tia bɔre no).” Ɔbarima foforɔ bi tee (n’asɛm no) na ɔkɔbɔɔ wɔn amanneɛ sɛ, “Monkɔ na mommɛfa deduani no mmra ha.” Afei Ɔbɔfoɔ no baeɛ. Nyankopɔn se, “Sɛ wotumi hwɛ me ba yi na wanwu a, mede wo ti bɛkyɛ wo.” Ɔbɔfoɔ no se, “M'ate, Adɔfo.” Na Ɔbɔfoɔ no se (bio), “Nana, deɛ merebɛkyerɛ woɔ no, sɛ wobenya bi a, ɛnde mɛtumi ahwɛ wo ba no.” Wɔse, “Ɛyɛ ade bɛn?” Ɔse, “Nkɔnkɔnsafoɔ mogya a wɔde bɛfra aduro a wɔayam no mu no.” Ɛnna wɔkae sɛ, “Ɛhe na yɛbɛnya Otiefoɔ mogya no?” Akyeame no mu baako a ɔyɛ onyansafoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Ɔbarima a ɔbɛbɔɔ Nyankopɔn amanneɛ sɛ Ɔbɔfoɔ no abɛwia Nyankopɔn sika no nso ɛ, ɔwɔ he?” Wɔkae sɛ, “Woante fɔm, woante fɔm.” Wɔkɔe kɔfaa ɔbarima no bae. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Bubu ne nsa firi n’akwanmu hɔ, na fa ne mogya ma me.” Ntɛm ara wɔbubu gui. Ɔbɔfoɔ no faa mogya no de yam aduro no na ɔde sraa Ɔhene ba no nan so. Ntɛm ara ɔsɔre gyinaa hɔ. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Monto Ɔbɔfoɔ no ayɛyie dwom." Wɔteɛɛm sɛ, "Eee!" Onyankopɔn kaa sɛ, "Mede wo ti kyɛ wo, na Ɔbɔfo-kɔtɔkɔ no deɛ, wɔnkum no, na wontwitwa ne nam, na wɔmpete ngu afa nyinaa." Wɔkumm no, na wɔtwitwaa ne nam nketenkete, na wɔpetee guu afa nyinaa. Ɛno nti na Kɔkɔsɛm-bɔfoɔ dɔɔso wɔ abusuakuo no mu no.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
