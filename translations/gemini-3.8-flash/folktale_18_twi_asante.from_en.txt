Yɛnni aseɛ koraa, yɛnni aseɛ koraa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokorɛ)

SƐDEƐ NKOFIKOFIE BAAYƐ ABUSUA NO MU

Wɔse ɔbɔfoɔ bi kɔɔ kwaem pɛn; ɔbaa bɛhwɛɛ amena bi mu, na na ’Nipa, Osebo, Ɔwɔ, ne ’Kusie nyinaa gyinagyina mu. Afei berɛ a saa nkurɔfoɔ yi hunuu Ɔbɔfoɔ no, wei kaa sɛ, “Agya Bɔfoɔ, bra bɛyi me.” Ɔno nso kaa sɛ, “Agya, bra bɛyi me.” Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Meremma, efisɛ ɔkyena mpo, sɛ meyi mo, na mo nsa ka me a, deɛ mobɛyɛ me no (menim sɛ) m’ani rennye ho.” Wei kaa sɛ, “Merentumi (nyɛ saa).” Ɔno nso kaa sɛ, “Merentumi (nyɛ saa).” Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Sɛ ɛte saa ampa a, mɛyi mo.” Na ampa ara ɔyii nkurɔfoɔ no nyinaa kɔɔ (ne) kuro mu. Da koro bi, berɛ a Ɔbɔfoɔ no te hɔ no, Osebo de nam bebree bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Agya Bɔfoɔ, ayɛyie mfa adɔeɛ a woyɛɛ me no nie, meda wo ase, Apiafo.” Ɔde nam kɛntɛn dumien brɛɛ Ɔbɔfoɔ no. Ɔbɔfoɔ no daa no ase na Osebo kɔe. Berɛ foforɔ bi, Ɔbɔfoɔ no te hɔ, na Okusie bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Agya Bɔfoɔ, wobɛpɛ sika futuro bi?” Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Ho! adɛn a, ɛno na nnipa pɛ a wɔbrɛ no.” Okusie sɔree, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a Nyankopɔn de ne sika futuro sieeɛ. Ɔkɔtuu amena wɔ dan no ase, na ɔwuraa dan no mu, na ɔkɔfaa Nyankopɔn sika futuro nyinaa, na ɔde brɛɛ Ɔbɔfoɔ no. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Meda wo ase, Aku.” Na Okusie kɔe. Da foforɔ bi, ɔte hɔ, na ɔtee sɛ obi rebɔ no agu, “Ago.” Ɔbɔfoɔ no gyee so sɛ, “Ame” (bra mu). Onipa no bae; ɔkaa sɛ, “Agya Bɔfoɔ, maakye.” Ɔno (Ɔbɔfoɔ no) gyee so sɛ, “Yaa, nua.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Nsafufuo ahina ketewa bi a mede rebɛda wo ase nie.” Agya Bɔfoɔ kaa sɛ, “Memmpɛ; na wo, Onipa, ɛte sɛ deɛ mekaeɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ berɛ a mekaa sɛ merennyi wo no; hwɛ deɛ Osebo brɛɛ me, hwɛ deɛ Okusie brɛɛ me.” Onipa faa ne nsafufuo no na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ Nyankopɔn sɛ, “Wo sika futuro a ɛyeraeɛ no, Okusie no na ɔbaa bɛfa kɔmaa Ɔbɔfoɔ no.” Nyankopɔn kaa sɛ, “Ɛte saa?” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Nana, ɛyɛ nokorɛ turodoo.” Nyankopɔn somafoɔ ma wɔkɔkyeree Ɔbɔfoɔ no, na wɔde ahoma kyekyeree n’asene. Wɔboroo no denneennen. Afei berɛ a wɔne Ɔbɔfoɔ no duruu kwan no so no, ɔkaa sɛ, “Mpaninfoɔ, merepɛ akɔ agya me nan so.” Asomafoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Dabi, korɔmfoɔ, wobɛdwane.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Dabi, merendwane, na sɛ mopɛ a, momfa hama no nkyekyere me sena mu, na momfa nkyekyere dunsiri ho, na momma me nkɔ.” Asomafoɔ no yee no aso, na wɔmaa no kwan ma ɔkɔe. Na ɔwɔ hɔ, berɛ a ɔredwudwo ne ho no, abɔdeɛ tenten-na-wateateaa no bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Hwɛ ha, m’adamfo, gye aduro kakra a ɛyɛ sɛ dɔteɛ kurobɛta yi, na fa kɔ, na sɛ wokɔ, na wɔde wo bɛgyina asafo no anim, na ɛba sɛ wɔrebɛkum wo a, mɛkɔ Ɔhene no mmaa fie, na makɔka ne yere a ɔdɔ no paa no ba; sɛ meka no pɛ a, wobɛhunu sɛ obiara asɔre, na wo nso wobɛkasa, aka sɛ, ‘Mewɔ aduro,’ na sɛ wode yɛ abofra no a ɔbɛnya ahoɔden.” Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Meda wo ase, Aku.” Asomafoɔ no faa Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔgyinaa Nyankopɔn anim, (n’anim ne n’Afotufoɔ anim) Kontire, Akwamu, Adonten, Gyaase, ne Kyidom ahemfo. Ɔbɔfoɔ no maa n’asɛm, na berɛ a Ɔkyeame no sɔree sɛ ɔrebu atɛn no, wɔtee osuo nnyegyeeɛ, pui! pui! akyirikyiri wɔ ahemfie mmaa fie hɔ. Wɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛdeɛn na ɛkɔ so?” Wɔkaa sɛ, “Ɔwɔ aka Nyankopɔn yere a ɔdɔ no paa no ba.” Obiara dwae kɔe. Afei na Ɔbɔfoɔ no gyina hɔ; ɔkaa sɛ, “Sɛ manyɛ deduafoɔ a, anka mɛnya aduro.” Ɔbarima bi tee (ne nka) na ɔkɔbɔɔ wɔn amanneɛ, sɛ, “Monkɔfa deduafoɔ no mmra ha.” Afei Ɔbɔfoɔ no bae. Nyankopɔn kaa sɛ, “Sɛ wobɛtumi ahwɛ me ba yi, ma wannyera a, mede wo ti bɛkyɛ wo.” Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Matee, Ɔdɔfoɔ.” Na Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa (bio) sɛ, “Nana, deɛ merebɛkyerɛ wo yi, sɛ wobɛnya bi a, ɛneɛ mɛtumi ahwɛ wo ba no.” Wɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛyɛ biribi bɛn?” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Kɔkɔsɛm-kafoɔ mogya a wɔde bɛfraw aduro a wɔayam no mu.” Wɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛhe na yɛbɛnya Kɔkɔsɛm-kafoɔ mogya?” Akyeame no mu baako a ɔyɛ onyansafoɔ kaa sɛ, “Ɔbarima a ɔbɛbɔɔ Nyankopɔn amanneɛ sɛ Ɔbɔfoɔ no aba abɛwia Nyankopɔn sika no nso ɛ, ɔwɔ he?” Wɔkaa sɛ, “Wontɔe, wontɔe koraa.” Wɔkɔfaa ɔbarima no bae. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Mombu ne nsa firi n’akwanmu, na monnye ne mogya mma me.” Ntɛm ara na wɔbui. Ɔbɔfoɔ no faa mogya no de fraa aduro no yamii na ɔde sraa Ɔhene ba no nan so. Ɛhɔ ara na ɔsɔre gyinaa hɔ. Wɔkaa sɛ, “Monto Ɔbɔfoɔ no ayeyie nnwom.” Wɔteɛɛm sɛ, “Eee!” Nyankopɔn kaa sɛ, “Mede wo ti kyɛ wo, na Ɔbɔsomfoɔ no deɛ, wɔnkum no, na wɔntwitwa ne honam mu, na wɔmpete mfa mfa mmaa nyinaa.” Wɔkum no, na wɔtwitwaa ne honam nketenkete, na wɔpetee no baabiara. Ɛno nti na Abɔsomfoɔ dɔɔso wɔ abusuakuo no mu no.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
