IF IT IS DONE, PEOPLE AND EVIL BEASTS AWAKEN

It is said that Death and Sleep are the same thing. And one day Death asked, "Who can take something from my hands?" The Cock was sitting there; he said, "Kokuro! I can." The Coucal (Oberekuo) said, "Du du, du du du, I can!" The Bulbul (Apatupere) said, "Twetwero, twetwero, I can." Death said, "Do you know that human life is the soul, and when a person sleeps, I take their soul out of them, and what do they become; do you know that they become a corpse?" The three creatures said, "Even so, we will wake them." Death said, "If you are able to, I will make you elders." Night fell, and all the people went to sleep. Two midnight watches passed, and the Coucal raised its voice, "Du du, du du du!" And the Antelope suddenly woke up; he said, "Well! It is not yet daybreak, let me go and eat a little; the hunter has not yet come." The Antelope went under a tree; he was eating. Then the Cock raised his voice, "Day has broken, have you seen? Day has broken, have you seen?" And the hunter suddenly woke up; he lifted his gun, went into the bush; he came to see the Antelope standing right there; he raised his gun, bang! he killed him. Then the Bulbul raised its voice, "Rise up! Rise up!" And all the people stretched themselves, and they woke up. Then the creatures went to Death and gave him the message, saying, "Have you seen that we have awakened all the people?" Death said, "Oh! It is a wonder to me; from this day forth, you are the elders of the world." This spider tale (anansesem) of mine that I have told, whether it is sweet or whether it is not sweet, take some away, and bring some back.
