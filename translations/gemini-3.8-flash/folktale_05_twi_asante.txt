The vulture's habit of always sitting on feces

OPETE and Anene were there, and Anene said, “Let us go and cut a drum and drum on it.” They set off into the forest, and they came upon a silk cotton tree. Opete saw the silk cotton tree and said, “This is mine, and since I am the elder, I will cut the big one.” Anene said, “I have heard.” Anene also looked, and he saw a cordia tree standing there, and he also went and cut it. Both of them carved drums.

The vulture says he will beat his drum, and that is to say:

Dust! Dust! I have not been early!

Vulture Kwakye! I am in a hurry!

Anene also added his part, which is:

Crow caw! Caw! Crow caw! Caw!

They played, played, and played, and God heard it, and He sent the Hawk, who came and said, "Knock, knock!" They said, "Come in!" He said, "God says He has heard some playing here, and so you should bring it and come and play for Him to hear." The animals set out and went before God, and they played, played, and played, and God said, "I have heard it and it is sweet; both plays are sweet, and so I will give you a gift." Then God went and took two boxes and placed them there; one was very beautiful, filled to the very brim with feces, and the other was very ugly, filled to the very brim with gold. God said, "Both of you, come and take." The Vulture said, "I am the elder, so let me go and choose first." When he went, he chose the very beautiful one, and the Crow also chose the very ugly one. They lifted them up, and they took them to their town. When they went and opened them, the Vulture, looking at his, saw it was full of feces! The Crow also, looking at his, saw it was completely full of gold! Not long after, a messenger came, and he told the Vulture, "They say your father-in-law is dead." He said, "Crow, give me one gold ring to put on my hand so that I may go to the funeral." He put on the ring, and he went to the funeral. He went to the latrine, squatted over it, and when he went to get up, the ring slipped off and fell into the latrine. He said, "Alas! The Crow's property is lost, so how do I go home?" He jumped into the latrine and searched for it.

That is, you will never see the Vulture scratching around there, seeking to mate with the Crow.

That is why the elders say greed is not good.

This tale of mine that I have told, whether it is sweet or not, take some and eat, and take some and approve of me.
