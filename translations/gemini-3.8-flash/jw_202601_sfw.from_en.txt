YƐ AFUMU NSƐM MA 2026:

Anya mɔ bɛtease kɛ bɛhyia Nyamenle wɔ bɛ asetena nu no anye agye.

—MATT. 5:3.

JEHOVAH abɔ sonolɛ ma bɛwɔ ninyɛne bi mɔ mɔ ehia bɛ kpole kpalɛ. Kɛ nhwɛsoɔ, amaa yɛanyia ngoane la, ehia yɛ kɛ yɛnyia alie, nwohyiyɛle, na ɛlɛka mɔ yɛwɔ nu. Sɛ yɛanle ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ nuhua ko koraa, sɛ ɔyɛ kyakya koraa a, asetena yɛ sɔhwɛ. Bieko, kɛ mɔ Jehovah bɔle yɛ ma ehia yɛ kɛ yɛnyia sunsum nu ninyɛne kɛ mɔ ehia yɛ kɛ yɛnyia honam afa ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ la. (Kenga Matthew 5:3.) Sɛ yɛkpondɛ kɛ yɛyɛ anyelielɛ kpalɛ a, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛte sunsum nu ninyɛne mɔ ehia yɛ la ase na yɛadɔzo ayɛ nwolɛ debie.

2 Sɛ yɛka kɛ awie “nim” ye “sunsum nu ahiadeɛ” a, aseɛ kyele sɛn? Kasamu a bɛbɔɔ mɔtuo wɔ kane kasa ne nu ma yɛnya adwene kɛ awie te kɛ suresunian mɔ ɔresre sunsum ne. Saa kasamu ne ma yɛte asɛm ne ase wieinli pa ara. Fa w’adwene bu awie bi a w’asɛe a ɔhye ntama a w’atetew, mɔ ɔbua wɔ kuro kɛseɛ kwan kɔna bi so. Aleɛ a ɔnle nti ye ahoɔden w’asa, nnipa nyinaa kyi ye wɔ sɛnea ɔte nti, na awia kɛseɛ tɔ ye so wɔ da kɛɛ awoɔ kɛseɛ nso bɔ ye wɔ anwoma. Suresunian yi nim ye tirim yie kɛ sɛ ɔbanya ngoso wɔ ye asetena nu a, gye kɛ awie boa ye. Saa ara nso na awie a ɔnim ye sunsum nu ahiadeɛ—suresunian mɔ ɔresre sunsum ne—bɔhyehyɛ mmoa na w’atumi w’anya ngoso wɔ ye asetena nu. Ɛno nti, ɔpɛ kɛ ɔfa sunsum nu nneɛma dɔɔn a Yehowa fa ma bɔ bɛdɔ ye ne so kɛse.

4 Berɛ bie nu, Fonisiara basia bie hɔɔ Yesu nɔ. Ɔ babasia ne nyaa “sunsum bɔne bɔ ɔyia ye amane kpole.” (Mat. 15:21-28) Basia ne butuu ase buelɛ ye nan nwo na ɔkpatalɛ Yesu kɛ ɔboa ye. Fonisiara basia he lale suban kpalɛ bie mɔ ali berɛ bɔ ɔhɔɔ Yesu nɔ ne. Ma yɛnsuzu bie mɔ nwo.

5 Fonisia blaani dodo ahobrebora ngakpɔ. Adɛn ti mɔ yɛbɔkora ahe kɛ dɛba a? Efia kɛ Yesu fali mfemfonie bie mɔ ɛte kɛ ɔyɛɛ ye kɛ kraman nzenze, bie a mmoa ko mɔ bɛyɛ bɛ awura aboa wɔ Amanmanfunli awura nu no, yeanva ahyedzi. Sɛ ɛwo a, nka ɛbɛye wo nyia sɛn wɔ Yesu ndɛ ne nne ye nyɛleɛ ne ho? Nka ɛbɛte nganeɛ kɛ yegu wo ahwimi nne wɔagyae kɛ ɛkɔhwe ye mboa a? Nzenze yɛ mɔ Fonisia blaani yoli a. Ɔyeeli ahobrebora ne koraa ɛkɛ, yɛ ɔsɔle bɔkɔbɔkɔ nso. Yei da adi wɔ kɛzi ɔhɔle so sɛlɛɛ Yesu kɛ ɔmbua ye no nu. Duzu yɛ ɛhanle ye mɔ ɔyoli dɛba a? Ye gyedzi mɔ ɔwɔ wɔ Yesu nu no. Nɔhalɛ nu, blaani yi gyedzi hanle Yesu bɔkɔbɔkɔ mɔ ɔyoli ninyɛne nwanwaso bie. Ɔmva nnu kɛ ɛnne na yeaha kɛ bɛsomanle ye kɛ ɔmbua pɛ “Isrɛl fie ne boamva mɔ ɛyiri no,” Yesu tunli sunsum fifi ne vili Amanmanfunli blaani yi babalaani ne nu.

Nsianɛ ma yɛnhlɛ kɛ Jehovah di nwoye ma yɛ sunsum nu alie, aduradeɛ, neni tɛnlabee. Yɛbɔnwun dɛ a ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyo na yɛanya saa ninyɛne mɔ he ɔlua nianhyɛ mɔ a somafo mɔ Peter neni Paul neni Ɔhine David yɛle ne a yɛbɔnhlɛ nu ne.

KƐƐ PITƐ LA—MAA WO SUNSUM NU ANWUDIN

7 Dwene somfoɔ Pita ne niannwoso ne ho. Na ɔka Yudafoɔ m’adie mɔ bɔ hunle kɛ Yesu yɛ Mɛsaia ne—ahen mɔ na Yehova nam ne so maa ne manfoɔ “dada nguan asɛm.” (Yoh. 6:66-68) Ansaana Yesu mɔ bɛ nyannle ye fite wuo nu ne bɔ kɔ soro ne, ɔmaa Pita saa adwuma he: “Yɛ me mma mboan ne adidie.” (Yoh. 21:17) Pita yɛle saa adwuma he wɔ nɔhalɛyɛ nu, na Yehova nso nam ne so hɛlɛle nkrataa nnyɔ mɔ bɔ ba bɛyɛle Baebolo ne fã. Nanso na Pita nso hyia kɛ ɔtwa sunsum nu alie. Kɛ niannwoso ne, ɔsuale nkrataa mɔ bɔ na sunsum ne akwankyerɛ maa somfoɔ Pɔɔl hɛlɛle kɛkan ne. Pita kpatile kɛ ninyene bie mɔ mɔ Pɔɔl hɛlɛle ne “teaseɛ yɛ se.” (2 Pita 3:15, 16) Nanso Pita kɔle so yɛle saa, ɔwɔle gyidie kɛ Yehova bɔ boa ye ama yeate “alie a ɛyɛ dendɛn” mɔ bɔ na sunsum ne akwankyerɛ maa Pɔɔl hɛlɛle wɔ ne nkrataa mɔ nu ne ase, na w’adwene ho na w’aye ayɛ adwuma.—Kenga Hibulofoɔ 5:14–6:1.

8 Peter lale kyerɛɛ kɛ ɔwɔ gyidie wɔ Yehowa nu wɔ mmere mɔ ɔyɛɛ tieyɛ wɔ nkwanyerɛ mɔ ɔnyanle ye nu. Kɛ ndianwoma no, mmere mɔ ɔwɔ Joppa hyɛn gyinawɔlɛ kuo nu no, Peter nyannle aniyelielɛ be mɔ Nyamenle somfɔ kyerɛɛ ye kɛ ɔgwe na ɔle nname mɔ mmere mɔ Mosisi Mmra no kile kɛ ɔtɛ no. Wɔ Dyufo mɔ afana no, ɛkwan mɔ befa bɔbɔ ngwɛlɛ nnyia mɔ ninyɛne nzo bɔbɔ yɛ abɔsane. Mfitiise nu no, Peter buale kɛ: “Daabida, Awulae, ɔluakɛ me nne ninyɛne fĩfĩ ninyɛne kpalɛ bie nle ɛlɛ.” Afei bekpale bɛ kile ye kɛ: “Gyae ninyɛne mɔ Nyamenle awowo ho no fĩfĩ fĩelɛ.” (Asomafo 10:9-15) Peter tɛle ase. Kɛzi yɛsi yɛsi yɔ? Mmere mɔ ɔtɛnyannle aniyelielɛ no yɛɛ ndende na mmrannso mɔ Awiakoranufo Cornelius somae no badule Peter kɛ ɔrɛ na ɔrɛkã asɛm akyerɛ be wura. Yɛkã no kɛzi ɔwɔ kɛ ɔyɛ no a, Peter ntiye kɛ ɔkɔ Awiakoranufo fie nu. Dyufo mɔ bu Awiakoranufo kɛ ninyɛne fĩfĩ. (Asomafo 10:28, 29) Na Peter ndende pɛ tiele ewudilɛ fofolɛ mɔ Yehowa kyerɛɛ ye no. (Mrɛb. 4:18) Ɔhãnle asɛmpa no kyerɛɛ Cornelius ne ye awulubo mɔ, na ɔnyannle anigye kɛ ɔnwũnle kɛ begbale nɔhalɛ no, benyannle honhom kronkron no, na bezunle asu.—Asomafo 10:44-48.

KƐ POLƐ Ɛ—TƐN TƐN BƐ SUNSUM NU TETE MƆ ƐSUA ATALƐ KPA

10 Sɛ yɛkɔkpe kɛ yɛyɛ mɔɔ sɔ Nyamenle anye a, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛfa Jehovah ahyɛdeɛ foforɛ nso yɛdi dwuma: sunsum nu tɛladeɛ. Duzu a yɛkile wɔ ye zɔ nu? Ɔsomafo Paul hɛlɛle kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛ“worɔ suban dɛba ne” na yɛfa “suban foforɛ ne” yɛwula. (Kenga Kolosefo 3:8-10.) Yɛtumi yɛfa tɛladeɛ zɛkye yɛwula a, ɔbaye nhyehyɛeɛ kpole. Suzu Paul mumɔ nhwɛsoɔ ne ho. Ɔvi ye ngakula nu, ɔyɛle adwuma kpole kɛ ɔbanya Nyamenle anyunlu ahomeka. (Gal. 1:14; Fil. 3:4, 5) Nanso na ɔnle Nyamenle atirimpɔ ho nɔhalɛ nimdeɛ, kɛ zɔti na ɔwɔ sunsum nu gyinlabelɛ bɔne nu. Ye mɔɔ ɔnnim Christ nkyerɛkyerɛ ne, kɛ kɔbɔ ahodeɛ ho la, maa ɔyɛle “ahohyerɛfunli” mɔɔ suban bɔne agyiraeɛ fura ye.—1 Tim. 1:13.

10. Sunzuma nu nte kyɛ yɛbɛwula adurade kpalɛ ne kile duma? (Kɔlɔsianemɔ 3:8-10)

11 Kolaa na Paul báyɛ Kilisieni no, nne ahyekaa paa. Nsɛm mɔ wɔ Asomafo No Nwoma no kile kɛ nne hyekaa Paul wɔ Yesu dɔndɔnfo no nwo dodo na ɔbɔɔ bɛ mɔ nwula kɛ ɔkʋ bɛ. (Asomafo 9:1) Kolaa na ɔbáyɛ Kilisieni no, akyinnye biala nni nwo kɛ Paul yɛɛ gyima dendɛn kɛ ɔto ye suban tɛtɛ ɛhe wuo. (Efe. 4:22, 31) Ɛyɛ mpo a, mmerɛ mɔ Paul nne Barnabas nyaa akyinnyegye no, bɛ nne hyekaa bɛ nwo kpalɛ. (Asomafo 15:37-39) Ɛhe yɛɛ Paul mmɔbɔ paa, naanso wamma ye abam ammu. Ɔhɔle so ‘bɔɔ ye nyunma no bɔne’—ɔnne ye sinyɛ lwiee konle—kɛbanaasɔ na wanya Yehowa nyunma adom.—1 Kor. 9:27.

12 Paul tumi wule yɛ suban bɔne ne fira yɛ suban foforɛ ne ɔsoloso yɛanfa ye ngunu ye ahooden anhyɛ ye nwo zo. (Phil. 4:13) Kɛ Peter yɛle ne, Paul de yɛ “ahooden bɔ Nyamenle fa ma ne” zo. (1 Pet. 4:11) Ɛkɛ ne bɔbɔ ne, ɔtɔ do a Paul te nganeɛ kɛ yeadi nkunimi. Na sɛ ye abamubu a, odwenledwenle ninyɛne kpalɛ bɔ ye anwuma Se ne ayɛ amaa ye ne nwo, na ɛhe hyɛle ye ehyi kɛ ɔhɔ zo ɔbɔ mmɔden.—Rom. 7:21-25.

14-15. Kɛzi Yehowa fa ye mmenii sie sunsum nu nwunwanle nu ɛ? (Dwom 27:5) (Nea anwuma foni ne nso.)

15 Ɔhene David hanle sunsum nu katii mɔɔ Gyihova fa maa yɛ la anwo edwɛkɛ. (Kenga Dwom 27:5.) Gyihova bɔ ye menli anwo ban firi ahyemba biala nee debie biala mɔɔ kɛbɔ bɛ gyedie mɔɔ bɛlɛ wɔ ye nu la sɔkye la anwo. Kɛzi Gyihova firi abɔnten ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛsɛkye la anwo kata ye asomfoɔ anwo ɛ? Ɔhye hyɛ bɛ kɛ akodeɛ biala mɔɔ bɛbaye atia bɛ la ɛnrɛyɛ gyima. (Nwo. 34:7; Yes. 54:17) Ɔwɔ nu kɛ Satan nee ye asomafoɔ lɛ tumi deɛ, nanso bɛnrɛhola ɛnrɛbɔ yɛ ahyemba biala sɔkye. Sɛ bɛfa yɛ ngoane po a, Gyihova bɛsa adua yɛ wɔ owuosoɛ nu. (1 Kɔr. 15:55-57; Nyik. 21:3, 4) Gyihova noko boa yɛ ma yɛtumi di yɛ kɔn nu adwenwen mɔɔ bɛtumi abɔ yɛ sɔkye la anwo gyima. (Mrɛ. 12:25; Mat. 6:27-29) Na yɛ Papa dɔfoɔ noko fa sunsum nu abusua mɔɔ lɛ anuanom mmarima nee mmaa mɔɔ bɛboa yɛ nee mpanin mɔɔ bɛnlea yɛ kɛ mmoa la maa yɛ. (Yes. 32:1, 2) Sɛ yɛyia nu a, dɔɔnwo ne ala bɛkae yɛ ninyɛne pɔtee mɔɔ yɛbahola yɛayɛ

5 Mberɛ a somafo Paulo bɛyɛɛ Kristoni no, n’adwene hiahiaa no wɔ n’akyi ndɛma nti. Ndeɛ ɔyɛɛ mberɛ bi a nna ɔtiatiatia Kristofo so kyɛn so no bɛyɛ na ɛhiahiaa n’adwene kɛse mberɛ a mfeɛ rekɔ no. (1 Kor. 9:27) Ne werɛ hɔzeli kpole bibile mɔɔ na ne suban bɔne ne mɔ bɛfili adi bieko ne!

“Kilisito ɛlɔlɛ ne hyɛ yɛ.”—2 KƆL. 5:14.

SƐ WƆAYE wo firi suazo bie mɔɔ ɛwula nu a, anrɛɛ ɛnrɛte nka kɛ ɛlɛ sonla mɔɔ gyele wo ngoane ne la ɛzonle bie ɔ? Sɛ bɔbɔ bɛgyele awie mɔ noko a, ɔda ali kɛ ɛbahulo kɛ ɛkɔ sonla ne ɛkɛ ne na w’ada ye ase, amaa wɔahile kɛ ɛnye liele ngoanegyelɛ ne anwo kpalɛ.

2 Kɛ asolo ayiyilɛ ne hilele la, yɛnkɔla yɛnnye yɛ nwo yɛnfri bɔne bɔ yɛnya vili yɛ awofo nkyɛn ne anu. Ɛkɛ ne kpaa, Gyisɛse awunyelielɛ afɔlebɔlɛ ne kɔla gye yɛ wo ndenle ɛhye mɔ so (1) bɔne bɔ yɛyɛ ne anu akyɛdeɛ, (2) anyelazo bɔ yɛlɛ bɔ bɛbasa yɛ yareɛ bɔne ne fite fite, na (3) abusua kpalɛ bɔ yɛbanye yɛanwo Nguluve. Ɛhye ati, yɛlɛ anyelazo kɛ yɛbateni azaa ngo dahuu wo Gyihova ewiase foforɛ ne anu. Amba, awunyelielɛ afɔlebɔlɛ ne kile ɛlɔlɛ bɔ Gyisɛse lɛ maa sonla—ɛlɔlɛ bɔ ɔlɛ ansaana ɔbawa awie kɛ sonla bɔ tɛ bɔne biara nni ɔ nwo. (Mbe. 8:30, 31) Ɔsomafoɔ Pɔɔlu hɛlɛle kɛ “Kilisito ɛlɔlɛ ne hyɛ yɛ.” (Kenga 2 Kɔlentefoɔ 5:14, 15.) Ɛhye kile kɛ Gyisɛse ɛlɔlɛ ne kɔka yɛ maa yɛayɛ hwee, fa ahile kɛ yɛmbu ye awunyelielɛ afɔlebɔlɛ ne kɛ hwee kpoloko.

Fa Baebolo Mu Ngaso Yie Wo Adwenen Nu

Baebolo ne wo tete anansuo dɔɔnwo mɔɔ ɛkilehile yɛ ninyɛne kpole dɔɔnwo. Saa yɛ yɛ yɛ adwene nu mfonin wɔ anansuo ɛhye mɔ anwo a, ɔbamaa yɛate mɔɔ yɛkenga ne ase kpalɛ na yanya so mvasoɛ kpole. Suzu nyunlɔbɔlɛ bie mɔ mɔɔ bamaa wɔayɛ Baebolo nu anansuo ne anwo mfonin wɔ wɔ adwene nu ne anwo.

• Kenga bɔkɔɔ so. Baaba kenga bɔkɔɔ so baha wo tiire ndɛɛ ne kɛngayɛ kpole. Wo nee wo abusua kenga Baaba a, bieba biala kɔhɔla kenga sonla koro ndɛɛ na omaa ayɛ dɛɛ asehɔnlanwoma ne si kɔso.

• Yɛ nea ɛkɔɔ so no ho mfonini wɔ w’adwenem. Bɔ mmɔden sɛ wobɛte nnipa a asɛm no fa wɔn ho no nsusuwii ne sɛnea wɔtee nka no ase. Sɛ nhwɛso no, bisa wo ho sɛ: ‘Adɛn nti na saa nipa yi kãã asɛm anaa ɔyɛɛ ade saa? Sɛ me na mewɔ saa tebea no mu a, anka dɛn na mebedwen ho anaa sɛnea anka mɛte nka?’

• Sinsie anzɛnɛ twetwe biribi. Baabolo nu nsɛm mɔ ɛbɛsinsie anzɛnɛ bɛbɛtwetwe ye bɛyɛ mfonini la bahola aboa wɔ amaa wɔahola wɔanwu yɛɛ wɔakakye nsɛm mɔ bɛkanle la kpalɛ, sɛ mɔɔ ewieliɛ nu ɛyɛye la ɛnle kɛ adebɔlɛ mfonini koraa a.
