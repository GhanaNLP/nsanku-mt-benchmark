Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka yi yɛ nokware)

ANANSE KAKRAKA KAA SƐ ƆRƐKƆ PƐ KWASEA, ƐBERƐ A ƐBERƐ NYINAA MU NO ƆNO ARA NSO YƐ KWASEA

ANANSE, Ananse no, na ɔte hɔ bere bi, na ɔkaa sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ kwasea bi a ɔne no bɛkɔ akɔto nsuomu mfiri. Ɔkyinkyin baabiara, nanso wannya baako koraa. Ɛnna ɔhyiaa Osansa, Akorɔma no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma yɛnkɔbɔ nsɔ." Akorɔma buaa sɛ, "Mewɔ nam (pii) a mɛwe." Ɛno akyi no, ɔhyiaa Anene, Kwaakwaadabi no, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma yɛnkɔbɔ nsɔ." Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Mate." Afei, Akorɔma ka kyerɛɛ Kwaakwaadabi no sɛ, "Saa nsɔbɔ akwantu a wo ne Ananse rekɔ yi, nkɔ, efisɛ Ananse se ɔrehwehwɛ kwasea bi, na sɛ ɔne saa kwasea no kɔbɔ nsɔ s, ɔbɛnya sika no nyinaa.” Kwaakwaadabi nso buaa sɛ, “Ɛho nhia sɛ wokasa bio, efisɛ mɛhwɛ ama manya sika no nyinaa.” Kwaakwaadabi ne Ananse sii mu kɔɔ kwaem sɛ wɔrekɔtwa mmɛ mpaa. Kwaakwaadabi ka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, “Fa sekan no ma me na menkɔtwa, na w’adwuma bɛyɛ sɛ wobɛfa brɛ (a efi m’adwumaden mu no).” Ananse buaa sɛ, “Wobu me sɛ kwasea anaa; ma mentwa, na wo nso wobɛfa brɛ no.” Ananse retwa (abɛ no) bere a Anene, Kwaakwaadabi no, te hɔ regyina. Bere a ɔtwatwa wieeɛ no, wɔkyekyeree no (bɔbɔɔ so). Ɔkwaakwaadabi kaa sɛ, “Ananse, ma memfa nsoa, na wo nso fa emu brɛ.” Ananse buaa sɛ, “Firi hɔ kɔ, wobu me kwasea?” Na ɔfaa soaeɛ. Ɔkwaakwaadabi dii n’akyi na na ɔresi apin. Bere a wɔduruu fie no, Kwaakwaadabi kaa sɛ, "Ma memmɔ nsɔwa no na gye m’adwuma no mu brɛ." Ananse buaa sɛ, "Dabi da; ma memmɔ na wo nso gye brɛ no." Afei Kwaakwaadabi kɔbɔɔ ne mu dedae, na na ɔresisie. Ananse kaa sɛ, "Okwasea, wose wowɔ nyansa, nanso worewuo yi." Ananse wiee nsɔwa no bɔ. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Ma mensoa asau no nkɔ nsuo no mu, na wo nso gye ɛho abrɛbrɛ no." Ananse kaa sɛ, "Dabi, me na mede bɛkɔ, na wo nso gye ɛho abrɛbrɛ no." Wɔduruu nsuo no ho; Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Agya Ananse, aboa bi te nsuo no mu, enti ma mengyina nsuo no mu na mensum asau no, na sɛ aboa no ka me a, wo nso agye ɛho owuo no." Ananse kaa sɛ, "Aane, mobu me kwaseaboni; me ara na mɛsum mfiri no, na sɛ ɔka me a, mobɛfa owuo no." Ananse faa mfiri no kɔsum wɔ nsuo no mu, na wɔsan baa fie. Adekyeeɛ a adeɛ bɛgyee no, wɔkɔhwɛɛ mfiri no, na na aso nsunsuani mmienu. Kwaakwaadabi see sɛ, "Ananse, fa mmienu yi, na sɛ ɔkyena yɛba na (mfidie no) ayi nan a, me nso mɛfa." Ananse buaa sɛ, "Nipa yi! Wobu me kwasea pa ara; fa mmienu yi, na ɔkyena, me nso mɛfa ɛnan no." Kwaakwaadabi faaeɛ. Adeɛ kyeeɛ no wɔkɔeɛ (kɔhwɛɛ mfidie no biom), na wɔyii (nam) nan. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Fa nnan yi, na ɔkyena sɛ yɛba na yɛanya nwɔtwe a, me nso mɛfa." Ananse buaa sɛ, "Wobu me kwasea; fa nnan yi, na ɔkyena me nso mɛfa nwɔtwe no." Ade kyee no, wɔkɔe (kɔhwɛɛ mfiri no) na wɔyii nwɔtwe. Kwaakwaadabi see, "Fa, na ɔkyena me nso mɛfa dunsia no." Ananse see, "Wobu me kwasea; wo fa nwɔtwe no, na ɔkyena menya dunsia no." Ɛnnɛ nso na nsɔnka no asɛe, na Kwaakwaadabi ka kyerɛɛ Ananse see, "Kɔkɔfa nsɔnka porɔporɔwee yi ba, na sɛ wɔkɔtɔn a, wɔbɛnya ntanu (kyerɛ sɛ (£16) ma firi baako biara, enti wo, Agya Ananse, fa nsuomnam yi, na fa mfiri a aporɔ no ma me.” Ananse see sɛ, “Dabi, wo fa nsuomnam no kɔtɔn, na me nso mɛfa mfiri a aporɔ no na magye (sika no).” Wɔsoaa (wɔn nneɛma) kɔɔ dwa so. Ɛhɔ ara na Kwaakwaadabi tɔn ne nneɛma nyinaa wiei; ɔnyaa sika futuro pii. Nanso aka Ananse dea. Ɛnna Kwaakwaadabi ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Fa nsoroma a abɔre yi kɔkyinkyin na wɔbɛtɔ.” Ananse maa so; ɔkaa sɛ, “Meretɔn nsoroma a abɔre!” Afei akuraa no so panin tee (ne nne), na ɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛhe na saa nipa yi firi; monkɔfrɛ no ma ɔmmra.” Ananse kɔe. Ɔpanin no kaa sɛ, “Wosusu sɛ yɛn a yɛwɔ akuraa yi ase ha yɛ nkwaseafoɔ; afei wo yɔnko bae bɛtɔn nsuomnam (nyaa mfasoɔ), wonhunuu no, na mmom wobɛtɔn nsuminam a aporɔ ama yɛn?” Ɔpanin no kaa sɛ, “Mompempem no.” Enti wɔboroo no. Ɛnna Kwaakwaadabi kaa sɛ, “Ananse, woahu, aniwuo ama w’ani awu; wokaa sɛ worekɔhwehwɛ kwasea bi ama wo ne no ayɛ adwuma, na (seesei) woahu sɛ wo ara na na woyɛ kwasea no.”

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
