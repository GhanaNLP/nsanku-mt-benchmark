Yɛnni aseɛ koraa, yɛnni aseɛ koraa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokorɛ)

ANANSE, ANANSE NO, KAE SƐ ƆRƐKƆPƐ KWASIAMARIFOƆ, BERƐ A NA ƆNO ARA MMOM NYƐ KWASIAMARIFOƆ

ANANSE te hɔ bi, na ɔkae sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ kwasea bi a ɔne no bɛkɔ akɔto fidie. Ɔkyinkyin mmaa nyinaa, nanso wannya baako. Afei ɔkɔhyiaa Osansa, Akorɔma no. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Ma yɛnkɔ nkɔto fidie.” Akorɔma no buaa sɛ, “Mewɔ nam (pii) a medi.” Ɛno akyi no, ɔkɔhyiaa Anene, Kwaakwaadabi no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Ma yɛnkɔ nkɔto fidie.” Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, “Mate.” Afei Akorɔma no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaakwaadabi no sɛ, “Fidieto a wo ne Ananse rekɔ yi, nkɔ, efisɛ Ananse se ɔrehwehwɛ kwasea bi, na sɛ ɔne kwasea no to fidie no a, ɔno na ɔbɛgye sika no nyinaa.” Kwaakwaadabi no buaa sɛ, “Ɛnsɛ sɛ wokasa bio, efisɛ mɛhwɛ sɛ me na mɛnya sika no nyinaa.” Kwaakwaadabi no ne Ananse tuu kwan kɔɔ wuram kɔtwitwaa mbebaa. Kwaakwaadabi no ka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, “Fa sekan no ma me na menkɔtwitwa, na wo nso w'adwuma ne sɛ wobɛfa ɔbrɛ no.” Ananse buaa sɛ, “Wosusu sɛ meyɛ kwasea anaa; ma mentwitwa na wo nso fa ɔbrɛ no.” Ananse retwitwa mbebaa no nna Anene, Kwaakwaadabi no, te hɔ regye ahome teetee mu. Ɔwiee twitwa no, wɔkyekyeree no dɔm. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, “Ananse, ma memfa nsoa, na wo nso fa ɔbrɛ no.” Ananse buaa sɛ, “Firi me so kɔ, wosusu sɛ meyɛ kwasea?” Na ɔfaa soaeɛ. Kwaakwaadabi no dii n'akyi na ɔregye ahome teetee mu. Wɔduruu fie no, Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, “Ma mennwo fidie no na wo nso fa m'adwuma no brɛ.” Ananse buaa sɛ, “Daabi da; ma mennwo na wo nso fa ɔbrɛ no.” Ɛnna Kwaakwaadabi no kɔbutuu fam na ɔregye ahome teetee mu. Ananse kaa sɛ, “Kwasea, wose wowɔ nyansa, nanso worewuo yi.” Ananse wiee fidie no nwo. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, “Ma mensoa fidie no nkɔ nsuo no ho, na wo nso fa ɔbrɛ no.” Ananse kaa sɛ, “Daabi, me ara na mede bɛkɔ, na wo fa ɔbrɛ no.” Wɔduruu nsuo no ho; Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, “Agya Ananse, aboa bi te nsuo yi mu, enti ma mensi nsuo no mu na mento fidie no, na sɛ aboa no ka me a, wo na wobɛfa owuo no.” Ananse kaa sɛ, “Ah, wofa me sɛ kwasea ankasa; me ara mɛto fidie no, na sɛ ɔka me a, wo na wobɛfa owuo no.” Ananse faa fidie no kɔtoo nsuo no mu, na wɔsan baa fie. Adekyee a adehyee no, wɔkɔhwɛɛ fidie no, na na ayi mpataa mmienu. Kwaakwaadabi see sɛ, "Ananse, fa mmienu yi, na sɛ yɛba ɔkyena na (mfidie no) kyere nnan a, me nso mɛfa." Ananse buaa sɛ, "Onipa yi! Woboo me sɛ me yɛ kwasea ampa; fa mmienu yi, na ɔkyena, me nso mɛfa nnan no." Kwaakwaadabi fae. Adeɛ kyeeɛ no wɔkɔe (kɔhwɛɛ mfidie no biom), na wɔnyaa nnan (apataa nnan). Kwaakwaadabi see sɛ, "Fa nnan yi, na sɛ yɛba ɔkyena na yɛkyere nwɔtwe a, me nso mɛfa." Ananse buaa sɛ, "Woboo me kwasea; fa nnan yi, na ɔkyena me nso mɛfa nwɔtwe no." Adeɛ kyeeɛ no wɔkɔe (kɔhwɛɛ mfidie no) na wɔnyaa nwɔtwe. Kwaakwaadabi see sɛ, "Fa (wɔn), na ɔkyena me nso mɛfa dunsia no." Ananse see sɛ, "Woboo me kwasea; wo nko fa nwɔtwe no, na ɔkyena me nso mɛnya dunsia no." Afei deɛ na asau no asɛe, na Kwaakwaadabi kasa kyerɛɛ Ananse see sɛ, "Kɔfa asau a aporɔ yi bra, na sɛ wɔkɔtɔn a, wɔbɛnya ntanu (i. e. (£16) wɔ baako biara ho, enti wo, Agya Ananse, fa apataa yi, na fa asau a aporɔ yi ma me." Ananse see sɛ, "Dabi, wo fa apataa no kɔtɔn, na me nso mɛfa asau a aporɔ yi akɔfa (ne sika no)." Wɔsoaa (wɔn nnoɔma) kɔɔ dwaso. Ɛhɔ ara na Kwaakwaadabi tɔn ne nnoɔma nyinaa wieeɛ; ɔnyaa sika futuro bebree. Nanso na aka Ananse deɛ (nnoɔma). Ɛnna Kwaakwaadabi see no sɛ, "Fa asau a aporɔ yi kɔbɔ so dwaso na wɔbɛtɔ." Ananse maae so; ɔsee sɛ, "Metɔn asau a aporɔ!" Afei akuraa no so panyin tee (ne nne), na ɔsee sɛ, "Ɛhe na onipa yi firi bae; monkɔfrɛ no mmra." Ananse kɔe. Panyin no see sɛ, "Wosusu sɛ yɛn a yɛwɔ akuraa yi ase ha yɛ nkwaseafoɔ; afei wo yɔnko (yi) baa ha bɛtɔnn apataa (nyaa mfasoɔ), wanhwɛ no, na wo deɛ wobɛtɔn asau a aporɔ akyerɛ yɛn?" Panyin no see sɛ, "Mommɔ no mmaa." Enti wɔboroo no. Ɛnna Kwaakwaadabi see sɛ, "Ananse, woahunu, w'ani awu fɛreɛ nti; wose worekɔhwehwɛ kwasea bi afa no ayɔnkofoɔ, na (seesei) woahunu sɛ wo ara na woyɛ kwasea no."

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
