NEA ƐYƐE A YƐNGYAA ABƆFRA WƆ OFIE HU MU

Wɔse ɔbɔfoɔ bi ne ne ba kɔsii akuraa ketewa bi, na wɔtenaa hɔ. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no akum mmoa, wakum, wakum, wakum. Mmoa ahodoɔ nyinaa bi kɔɔ mu. Afei, sɛ ɔku mmoa yi a, na ɔwe nam no, na watwa atifi no agu hɔ; na damboma mu koro pɛ ayɛ mmoa tiri ma, ama te sɛ sei. Afei asɛm bi tumi ka akyi (kyerɛ sɛ biribi wɔ hɔ a me werɛ afi sɛ mɛka); saa bɔfoɔ no, sɛ ɔkɔ wuram a, na ɔgya ne ba no wɔ fie daa. Da koro bi, berɛ a abofra no te hɔ no, ɔhunuu aboa Fitaa (a ne ho asunsun) no; na ɛyɛɛ mpofirim koraa. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Me nana, maadwo.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Wo papa wɔ hefa?” Ɔno (abofra no) kaa sɛ, “Agya kɔ kwaeɛ mu.” Ɔsebɔ no, berɛ a na ɔrehwɛhwɛ fam no, ɔhunuu mmoa no atire, na na ɛdɔɔso pa ara. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Aane! w’asɛm ayɛ kɛse.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Fa mmoa no atire nyinaa brɛ me ma menhwɛ.” Na nea ɔyɛ hu no gyinaa hɔ hihim ne dua (a ɛyɛɛ nnyegyeɛ kɛse te sɛ) f’re! f’re! f’re! Na abɔfra no faa mmoa no ti guu hɔ kɔɔ akyirikyiri sɛ efi ha kɔsi baabi no. Na Ɔsebo kae sɛ, “Bobɔ wɔn din kyerɛ me na mente.” Na ɔgyinaa abɔfra no kɔn ho tee, na ɔkae sɛ, “Bɔ (wɔn din); oyi, aboa bɛn koraa na ɔte saa?”

Ɔno (abofra no) kaa sɛ:

"Yei ne adowa ketewa no, Adowa, Adowa, Adowa, Kwadwo Berefi Adowa, Agya na okum saa Adowa yi, etuo no twa a, mebɔ Agya aboso, damirifa!" (Due).

Aboa bɛn koraa ni?

Yei ne Otwe, Otwe, Otwe, Otwe a ne suban ne, osi-akwan-mu-a-obɔfoɔ-na-ɔyɛ-bɔne. Agya na ɔkum saa Otwe yi, etuo bɔ a, mekamfo Agya, damirifa!

Aboa bɛn koraa ni?

Yeinom ne ɔtwa tuntum no, Oyuo, Oyuo, Oyuo, Oyuo Kwabi, Agya kum saa Oyuo yi, sɛ etuo no bɔ a, mebɔ Agya abaso, damirifa!

Aboa bɛn koraa ni?

“Wɔfrɛ eyi ɔtwee, Abedie, Abedie, Abedie, Agya na ɔkumm saa Abedie yi, sɛ etuo no to a, mekamfo Agya, damirifa!”

Aboa bɛn koraa ni?

“Woi ne Ɔtromo, Ɔbongo, Ɔbongo, Ɔbongo, Sakwa Ɔbongo no, Agya na ɔkum saa Bongo yi, sɛ etuo no mu pa a, mehyira Agya, damirifa!”

Aboa bɛn koraa ni?

Wansane nie, Ɔwansane, Ɔwansane, Ɔwansane, Mmomire Ɔwansane, Agya na ɔkumm wansane yi, etuo bɔ a, mebɔ Agya aboso, damirifa!

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

Wɔfrɛ no Kotoko, Kotoko, Kotoko, Gyanbibi Kotoko, Agya na okum Kotoko yi, tuo bɔ a, mekamfo Agya, damirifa!

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

Yei ne Ɛkuo, Ɛkuo, Ɛkuo, Asɛmpi, Ɛkuo, Agya na okum saa Ɛkuo yi, etuo to a, mebɔ Agya din, damirifa!

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

Yei ne Oforotie, Ɔwansane, Ɔwansane, Ɔwansane, Pantan, Ɔwansane, Agya kum Ɔwansane yi, etuo to a, mebɔ Agya abodin, damirifa!

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

Yei ne Osonson, Ɔtɔmpran, Ɔtɔmpran, Ɔtɔmpran, Ofuntum Kɔkɔɔ, Ɔtɔmpran, Agya na ɔkumm saa Ɔtɔmpran yi, otuo bɔ a, mekamfo Agya, damirifa!

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

Yei ne Oko, Ɔkoo no, Ɔkoo no, Ɔkoo no, Agya kum Ɔkoo yi, sɛ etuo no tow a, mebɔ Agya aboso, damirifa!

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

“ Yei ne Aboa Kɛseɛ, Aboa Kɛseɛ no, Aboa Kɛseɛ no, Aboa Kɛseɛ no, ’Sai, ɛserɛ so hene, Agya kum Aboa Kɛseɛ yi, sɛ etuo no gyigye a, mebɔ Agya abodin, damirifa! ”

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

Yei ne Esono, Ɛsono, Ɛsono, Ɛsono, Ɔtweeduampon a ɔnante ne koro, ɔno a ɔbubu nnamfoɔ, Agya na ɔkumm saa Ɛsono yi, sɛ etuo no mu twa a, mebɔ Agya abodin, damirifa!

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

Yei ne Susono, Susono no, Susono no, Susono no, Agya kum Susono yi, sɛ tuo bɔ a, mekamfo Agya, damirifa!

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

Ebie nie, Kwakuo no, Kwakuo no, Kwakuo no, Agya na okum saa Kwakuo yi, etuo to a, mebɔ Agya abodin, damirifa!

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

" Yeinom ne Kokotee, wuram prako no, wuram prako no, wuram prako no, Asamoa, wuram prako no, Agya na okum saa wuram prako yi, sɛ etuo no to a, mebɔ Agya abodin, damirifa! " "Abowa bɛn koraa nie?"

Yei ne Ofosuo, Ofosuo, Ofosuo, Agya kum Ofosuo yi, otuo to a, mebɔ Agya abaso, damirifa!

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

Eyi ne Kwaduo, Ɔtromo, Ɔtromo, Ɔtromo, Boapampa, Agya kum saa Ɔtromo yi, sɛ etuo to a, mebɔ Agya abodin, _damirifa!_

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

Yei ne Apese, Apesedie, Apesedie, Apesedie, Agya na okum Apese yei, etuo reto a, mebɔ Agya abodin, _damirifa!_

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

Ebuo ne ye, Efoo no, Efoo no, Efoo no, Ya, Efoo no, Agya kum Efoo yi, sɛ etuo no te a, mebɔ Agya abodin, damirifa!

Na afei ɔduruu faako a Ntokoroma Fitaa no ti da no, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Na yei nso anaa?" Ɔno (abofra no) kaa sɛ:

Ma Agya mmra ansa (na mato ne din), efisɛ eyi yɛ hu.

Ɔseboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Mate; merekɔ; ɔkyena mɛba bio, na sɛ wo papa ba fie a, bisa no ne din." Papa no firi wuram baeɛ, na ɔno (abofra no) kaa sɛ: "Ammaneɛ kɛseɛ na mahunu." Papa no kaa sɛ: "Ɛdeɛn na asie?" Ɔno (abofra no) kaa sɛ: "Wogyaa me ha kɔɔ wuram, na Ɔseboɔ no baeɛ, na ɔmaa mede mmoa no nyinaa tiri totohyɛɛ nkyɛnkyɛn na m bobɔɔ wɔn din mmaako mmaako, kosii sɛ (yɛ)duruu ne busuani deɛ no so, na ɔkaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mebɔ ne din, nanso mekaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ ɔma wodi kan ba, efisɛ na yei yɛ adeɛ a ɛyɛ hu." Agya no kaa sɛ: "O, ɛnyɛ hwee, yɛbɛhunu deɛ ɛbɛsi bio ɔkyena." Na ampa ara, bio berɛ a adeɛ hyɛɛ aseɛ firi baeɛ (kyerɛ sɛ anɔpahema) Agya no kɔɔ kwaem. Ɔsɛbɔ no san bae bio maa no (abofra no) de atifi no guu hɔ sɛ nhyehyɛe bio, na ɔmaa no bɔɔ mmoa no atifi din mmaako mmaako. Bere a ɔsan duruu Ɔsɛbɔ no tiri so ne mprɛnsa so bio no, ɔkaa sɛ:

Ma Agya mmra ansa (na mato ne din), efisɛ eyi yɛ hu.

Osebo no kaa sɛ: "Ɛyɛ, nanso ɔkyena sɛ meba na sɛ woanka ankyerɛ me a, mɛtwa wo nso wo ti." Abofra no kaa sɛ: "M'ate!" Anwummere dwoo yiye, na Agya no san baa fie na (abofra no) kaa sɛ: "Ɛnneɛ ɛnnɛ deɛ—ɔkyena sɛ woannyɛ nnam annhwɛ nea wobɛyɛ afa ho a, ɛnde mɛkɔ agyaw wo ha wɔ akuraa yi ase." Agya no kaa sɛ: "Ɛdeɛn na asɛm no bio?" Abofra no kaa sɛ: "Osebɔ no baa ne mprɛnu so, na ɔka sɛ sɛ ɔkyena mankasa a, deɛ ɔbɛyɛ me no bɛboro deɛ mɛtumi adi so." Agya no kaa sɛ: "Yoo, mate." Afei ɔkɔkyini pɛɛ nhahan ne nnuru nhini de guare ne tuo no. Ɔhyee mu, na ɔde sii hɔ. Ɛduruu berɛ a nnoɔma bɛyɛɛ hann no, ɔkɔhuntaa baabi. Ɛnkyɛe koraa na ɔhwee ase hunuu Ɔsebo sɛ ɔreba ntɛmntɛm.

Ɛbɛyɛɛ sɛ Ɔsɛbɔ no duruu fie pɛ, ɔkasa kyerɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no ba no sɛ, “Ɛnnɛ deɛ, deɛ ɛbɛba biara bɛba.” Abofra no kaa sɛ: “Aane, ɛnam sɛ wonnim (biribiara pɛpɛɛpɛ) nti a.” Ɔsɛbɔ no kaa sɛ: “Ɛdeɛn na woreka no, mante?” Abofra no kaa sɛ: “Woka kyerɛɛ me sɛ mentoto (ti no) nnidisoɔ, na ɛdeɛn na metumi aka (wɔ ho)?” Ɔsɛbɔ no kaa sɛ: “Toa (adwuma no) so.” Abofra no nso fa mmoa no ti na to nhoma gu fam, na ɔkaa (brɛoo kyerɛɛ n’Agya) sɛ, “Ɛhe na memfa (yei) ngu?” Ɔsebo no bisae sɛ: “Ɛdeɛn na woreka no?” Akwadaa no kaa sɛ: “Na merekae me ho aboa yi din kɛkɛ.” Ɔsebo no kɔgyinaa akwadaa no kɔn akyi tee na ɔkaa sɛ, “Toa so, na yei nso nso ɛ, aboa bɛn na ɔte saa?”

Wɔi ne Ɔtwe no, Ɔtwe no, Ɔtwe no, Ɔtwe no, Agya na ɔkumm saa Ɔtwe yi, sɛ etuo no gyigye a, mebɔ Agya aboso, damirifa!

Na ɔbɔɔ mmoa no nyinaa ti din saa ara, na ɔduruu Ɔsebɔ deɛ no so. Ɔno (Ɔsebɔ no) kaa sɛ: "Na yei nso ɛ?" Ɔno (abofra no) kaa sɛ: "Yei ne Etwie, Ɔsebɔ, Ɔsebɔ, Ɔsebɔ, Agya Ɔsebɔ, Agya na ɔkumm saa Ɔsebɔ yi, sɛ etuo no bɔ a, mebɔ Agya abodin, damirifa!"

Ɔsebɔ no kaa sɛ: “O, nanso woahunu amane; m’aso mu baako ate, aka baako no.” Na ɔsane kɔe kɔbisaa no bio, na abofra no sane kaa bio, na ɔno (Ɔsebɔ no) kaa sɛ: “M’aso mmienu no nyinaa ate seesei, aka me kora kumaa a me bosom a mede asi m’anim no wom no nko ara.” Afei Agya no hyɛɛ abobroboma, na ɔfrɛɛ ne ba no na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Twe wo ho nkyɛn,” na abofra no hurui kɔgyinaa nkyɛn. Na Ɔsɛbɔ no kaa sɛ: “Ɛhe na worekɔ?” Na abofra no kaa sɛ: “Na meretwe me ho kakra akɔ nkyɛn ara kwa.” Ɔsɛbɔ no kaa sɛ: “Me nana, wowɔ nyansa.” Na ɔno (Ɔsɛbɔ no) kaa sɛ: “Na yei nso, ne din de sɛn?” Ɔno (abofra no) kaa sɛ:

Etwie ni, Ɔsebɔ, Ɔsebɔ, Ɔsebɔ, Agya Ɔsebɔ, Agya na okum Ɔsebɔ yi, otuo to a, mebɔ Agya din, damirifa!

N'ano kyerɛe, na na Ɔsebɔ no rebɛhuruhuru akyere no no, na abofra no Papa maa otuo no suee sɛ, Tomm! Na Ɔsebɔ no kɔdaa hɔ, na ne hweaseɛ nnyegyeeɛ yɛɛ sɛ, Tim! Na Papa bae bɛtwitwaa Ɔsebɔ no nam petee baabiara.

Enti ɛno nti na mpanimfoɔ se ɛnsɛ sɛ yɛgya abɔfra nkutoo wɔ fie, ɛfiri sɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no yɛɛ saa, na aka ketee na ɔsebɔ no akyere ne ba no.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
