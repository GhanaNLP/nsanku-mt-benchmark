NEA ƐYƐE A YƐNGYA ABƆFRA WƆ OFIE HUNU MU

WƆSE ɔbɔfoɔ bi ne ne ba kɔsii akuraa ketewa bi, na wɔtenaa hɔ. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no akum mmoa, wakum, wakum, wakum. Mmoa ahodoɔ nyinaa bi wɔ mu. Na sɛ ɔkum mmoa yi a, na ɔwe nam no, na ɔtwa atifi no gu hɔ; dan mũ koro pɛ yɛɛ mmoa ti mã, mã sɛ sɛɛ ara. Afei asɛm bi na aka akyi (kyerɛ sɛ biribi wɔ hɔ a me werɛ afi sɛ mɛka); saa ɔbɔfoɔ no, sɛ ɔrekɔ kwaem a, ɔgya ne ba no wɔ fie daa. Da koro bi, berɛ a abofra no te hɔ no, ɔhunuu aboa Fitaa (nkronkroma) no; na ɛyɛɛ mpofirim koraa. Ɔsee sɛ, “Me nana, mema wo adwo.” Ɔbisae sɛ, “Wo papa wɔ he?” Ɔno (abofra no) see sɛ, “Papa akɔ kwaem.” Ɔsebɔ no, berɛ a ɔrehwɛhwɛ faako no, ɔhunuu mmoa no ti no, na ɛdɔɔso pa ara. Ɔsee sɛ, “Aha! woanya asɛm.” Ɔsee sɛ, “Fa mmoa no ti nyinaa brɛ me na menhwɛ.” Na obuo no gyinaa hɔ hiahiaa ne dua mu (a ɛyɛɛ nnyegyeɛ sɛ) f’re! f’re! f’re! Na abofra no faa mmoa no ti no hyehyɛɛ hɔ ntentensoɔ tenten a ɛfiri ha kɔsi kɔpem faako kɔɔ akyiri noho. Na Ɔsebɔ no see sɛ, “Bɔ wɔn din kyerɛ me na mente.” Na ɔgyinaa abofra no kɔn akyi tee, na ɔsee sɛ, “Bɔ (wɔn din); yei, ɛyɛ aboa bɛn?”

Ɔno (abofra no) kaa sɛ:

“Yeinom ne adowa ketewa no, Adowa, Adowa, Adowa, Kwadwo Berefi Adowa, Agya na ɔkumm Adowa yi, sɛ tuo no te a, mebɔ Agya abodin, damirifa!” (Due).

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

Otwe nie, Ɔtwe, Ɔtwe, Ɔtwe a ne sabran ne ɔ-kɔ-adɔfo-yɛ-na-asɛm-wɔ-ɔbɔfoɔ. Agya na ɔkum otwe yi, etuo bɔ a, mebɔ Agya abodin, damirifa!

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

“Woi ne ɔtwe tuntum no, Oyuo, Oyuo, Oyuo, Oyuo Kwabi, Agya kum saa Oyuo yi, sɛ etuo no mu pae a, mebɔ Agya abodin, damirifa!”

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

“Yeinom ne ɔtwee kɔkɔɔ no, Abedie, Abedie, Abedie, Agya na ɔkumm saa Abedie yi, sɛ etuo no mu pae a, mebɔ Agya abaso, damirifa!”

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

“Otromo ni, Bongo ni, Bongo ni, Bongo ni, Sakwa Bongo ni, Agya na okum Bongo yi, etuo to a, mebɔ Agya abaso, damirifa!”

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

Wansane ni, Ɔwansane, Ɔwansane, Ɔwansane, Mmomire Ɔwansane, Agya na ɔkumm wansane yi, etuo bɔ a, mehyira Agya, damirifa!

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

Kotoko ni, Kɔtɔkɔ, Kɔtɔkɔ, Kɔtɔkɔ, Gyanbibi, Kɔtɔkɔ, Agya kum Kɔtɔkɔ yi, tuo gyigye a, mehyira Agya, damirifa!

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

Woi ne Ɛkuo, Ɛkuo no, Ɛkuo no, Asempi, Ɛkuo no, Agya na okum Ɛkuo yi, etuo bɔ a, mekamfo Agya, damirifa!

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

“ Woi ne Oforotie, Ɔwansane no, Ɔwansane no, Ɔwansane no, Pantan, Ɔwansane no, Agya na okum saa Ɔwansane yi, sɛ etuo no bɔ a, mekamfo Agya, damirifa! ”

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

“ Oyi ne Osonson, Otrom, Otrom, Otrom, Ofuntum Kwoa, Otrom, Agya kumm Otrom yi, etuo bɔ a, mekamfo Agya, damirifa! ”

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

Oko nie, Ɔkankane no, Ɔkankane no, Ɔkankane no, Agya kumm Ɔkankane yi, sɛ etuo no bɔ a, mekamfo Agya, damirifa!

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

“ Yei ne Aboa Kɛseɛ, Aboa Kɛseɛ no, Aboa Kɛseɛ no, Aboa Kɛseɛ no, ’Sai, Sareso-manhene, Agya na ɔkumm saa Aboa Kɛseɛ yi, sɛ etuo no mu pa a, mehyira Agya, damirifa! ”

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

Wei ne Esono, Esono, Esono, Esono, Odenden a ɔkɔ n'anko, ɔno a ɔbubu nnam, Agya na okum Esono yi, sɛ otuo no tow a, mekamfo Agya, damirifa!

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

Yei ne Susono, Susono, Susono, Susono, Agya kum saa Susono yi, sɛ tuo no bɔ a, mekamfo Agya, damirifa!

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

“ Ebie ni, Kokote, Kokote, Kokote, Agya akum Kokote yi, etuo te a, mebɔ Agya aboso, damirifa!”

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

" Woi ne Kokote, Kwaebiafoɔ no, Kwaebiafoɔ no, Kwaebiafoɔ no, Asamoa, Kwaebiafoɔ no, Agya na ɔkumm saa Kwaebiafoɔ yi, sɛ etuo no mu pae a, mebɔ Agya abodin, damirifa! " "Aboa bɛn koraa ni?"

Yei ne Ofosuo, Ofosuo no, Ofosuo no, Ofosuo no, Agya kum Ofosuo yi, sɛ etuo no bɔ a, mekamfo Agya, _damirifa!_

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

Yei ne Kwaduo, Ɔkwaduo, Ɔkwaduo, Ɔkwaduo, Boapampa, Agya na okum saa Ɔkwaduo yi, etuo to a, mebɔ Agya abodin, _damirifa!_

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

Wɔfrɛ eyi Apeseɛ, Apeseɛ, Apeseɛ, Agya na ɔkum saa Apeseɛ yi, sɛ etuo no te a, mekoto Agya, _damirifa!_

Abowa bɛn koraa ni?

Ebuo nie, Ɔfoɔ no, Ɔfoɔ no, Ɔfoɔ no, Ya, Ɔfoɔ no, Agya na okum Ɔfoɔ yi, otuo no to a, mebɔ Agya abaso, damirifa!

Na afei ɔduruu baabi a Fitaa-(nsonsono)-ni no ti daeɛ no, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Na yei nso anaa?" Ɔno (abofra no) kaa sɛ:

"Momma Agya mmra ansa (na mato ne din), efisɛ yei yɛ hu."

Ɔsebɔ no kaa sɛ, "Mate; merekɔ; ɔkyena mɛsan aba, na sɛ wo Papa ba fie a, bisa no ne din." Papa no firi kwaeɛm baeɛ, na ɔno (abofra no) kaa sɛ: "Amaneɛ na mahunu yi." Papa no kaa sɛ: "Ɛdeɛn na asi?" Ɔno (abofra no) kaa sɛ: "Wogyaa me ha kɔɔ kwaeɛm, na Ɔsebɔ no baeɛ, na ɔmaa mebobɔɔ mmoa no nyinaa ti guu hɔ didi so na mebɔɔ wɔn din mmaako mmaako, kɔsii sɛ (yɛ)duruu ne busuani deɛ no so, na ɔkaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mebɔ ne din, nanso mekaa sɛ ɔmma wo mma ansa, ɛfiri sɛ yei deɛ ɛyɛ adeɛ a ɛyɛ hu." Papa no kaa sɛ: "O, ɛnyɛ hwee, yɛbɛhunu deɛ ɛbɛsi bio ɔkyena." Na ampa ara, bio berɛ a adeɛ kye maa adeɛ nyinaa daa adi no (kyerɛ sɛ anɔpahema) Papa no kɔɔ kwaeɛm. Ɔsebɔ no san baeɛ na ɔmaa ɔno (abofra no) bobɔɔ ti no guu hɔ didi so bio, na ɔmaa no bɔɔ mmoa no ti din mmaako mmaako. Berɛ a bio, ne mprɛnu so no, ɔduruu Ɔsebɔ no ti so no, ɔkaa sɛ:

"Momma Agya mmra ansa (na mato ne din), efisɛ yei yɛ hu."

Ɔsɛboɔ no kaa sɛ: "Ɛyɛ, nanso ɔkyena sɛ meba na sɛ woanka ankyerɛ me a, mɛtwa wo nso wo ti." Abofra no kaa sɛ: "Mate!" Anwummerɛ dwoeɛ no, Agya no san baa fie na (abofra no) kaa sɛ: "Ɛnneɛ deɛ—ɔkyena sɛ woanso w'ani annhwɛ deɛ wobɛyɛ afa ho a, ɛnneɛ mereka m'akyi na magya wo wɔ kuro mmaako yi mu." Agya no kaa sɛ: "Ɛdeɛn asɛm bio?" Abofra no kaa sɛ: "Ɔsɛboɔ no baa ne mprenu so, na ɔse sɛ ɔkyena mankasa a, deɛ ɔbɛyɛ me no bɛboro m'ano." Agya no kaa sɛ: "Ɛyɛ, mate." Afei ɔkɔkyeakyee kɔpɛɛ nhahan ne nnuru ntini de gguaree n'etuo no. Ɔhyɛɛ mu gyae, na ɔde sii hɔ. Berɛ a adeɛ bɛyɛɛ hann pefee pɛ no, ɔkɔhintaa baabi. Ankyɛ koraa na ɔhunuu sɛ Ɔsɛboɔ no reba ntɛmntɛm.

Ɔsebɔ no duruu fie pɛ, ɔkasa kyerɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no ba no sɛ, “Ɛnɛ deɛ, deɛ ɛbɛsi biara bɛsi.” Akwadaa no kaa sɛ: “Aane, ɛfiri sɛ wonnim (biribiara pɔtee).” Ɔsebɔ no kaa sɛ: “Ɛdeɛn na woreka no, mante?” Akwadaa no kaa sɛ: “Wokaa sɛ menhyehyɛ (ti no) nnidisoɔ, na ɛdeɛn na anka mɛtumi aka (wɔ ho)?” Ɔsebɔ no kaa sɛ: “Kɔ so (yɛ adwuma no).” Akwadaa no faa mmoa no ti no de guu fam hyehyɛɛ no didisoɔ, na ɔkaa (bɔɔ ne Papa kɔkɔ wɔ fam) sɛ, “Ɛhe na memfa (yei) ngu?” Ɔsebɔ no kaa sɛ: “Ɛdeɛn na woreka no?” Akwadaa no kaa sɛ: “Na merekae me ho aboa yi din kɛkɛ.” Ɔsebɔ no kɔgyinaa akwadaa no kɔn akyi pɛɛ na ɔkaa sɛ, “Kɔ so, na yei nso, aboa bɛn koraa nie?”

Yei ne Otwe no, Otwe no, Otwe no, Otwe no, Agya na okum saa Otwe yi, sɛ etuo no mu pae a, mebɔ Agya aboso, damirifa!

Na ɔbɔɔ mmoa no nyinaa ti din saa ara, kɔsii sɛ ɔduruu Ɔsebɔ deɛ no so. Ɔno (Ɔsebɔ no) kaa sɛ: “Na yei nso nso, ɛ?” Ɔno (abɔfra no) kaa sɛ: “Yei ne Etwie, Ɔsebɔ, Ɔsebɔ, Ɔsebɔ, Agya Ɔsebɔ, Agya na ɔkumm saa Ɔsebɔ yi, sɛ etuo no tow a, mebɔ Agya abodin, damirifa!”

Ɔsebɔ no kaa sɛ: “O, nanso woahu amane; m’aso abien no mu baako ate, aka baako no.” Na ɔkɔɔ n'akyi kɔbisaa no bio, na abofra no san kaa bio, na ɔno (Ɔsebɔ no) kaa sɛ: “Afei deɛ m'aso mmienu no nyinaa ate, aka me kora ketewa a me bosom a mede asi m'anim no gu mu no nko ara.” Afei Agya no hyɛɛ ntoroma, na ɔfrɛɛ ne ba no na ɔkaa kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Twe wo ho kɔ nkyɛn,” na abofra no hurui kɔgyinaa nkyɛn. Na Ɔsebɔ no kaa sɛ: “Ɛhe na worekɔ?” Na abofra no kaa sɛ: “Na mereguan me ho akɔ nkyɛn kakra bi pɛ.” Ɔsebɔ no kaa sɛ: “Me nana, wowɔ adwene.” Na ɔno (Ɔsebɔ no) kaa sɛ: “Na yei nso nso ɛ, ne din de sɛn?” Ɔno (abofra no) kaa sɛ:

"Etwie nie, Ɔsebɔ, Ɔsebɔ, Ɔsebɔ, Agya Ɔsebɔ, Agya na okum Ɔsebɔ yi, otuo to a, mebɔ Agya din, damirifa!"

N'ano gyinaa hɔ dinn, na na Ɔsebɔ no reyɛ ahuri akye no pɛpɛɛpɛ, berɛ a abɔfra no Papa maa tuo no gyee so sɛ, Tomm! Na Ɔsebɔ no daa hɔ, na ne hweaseɛ nnyegyeeɛ yɛɛ sɛ, Tim! Na Papa bae bɛtwitwaa Ɔsebɔ no nam petee baabiara.

Ɛno nti na mpanimfoɔ ka sɛ yɛnnyaw abɔfra nkutoo wɔ fie, ɛfiri sɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no yɛɛ saa, na aka kakra ma ɔsebo kyee ne ba no.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
