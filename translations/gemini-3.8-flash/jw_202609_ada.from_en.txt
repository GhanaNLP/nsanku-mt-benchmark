19 E he biako nɛ waa ye gbeye kɛ ha je yayami nɛ ɔ hɛ mi kpatami nɛ maa ba ngɛ Harmagedon ta a mi ɔ. Mɛni he je? Wɔɔ ngɔ wɔ he kɛ fɔ Yehowa nɔ kɛ pee wa we nane tso kɛ wa mo nɛ ma he shi kpeii. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, wa ma nyɛ ma ná nɔ mami kukuuku ngɛ enɛ ɔ mi: Ke Yesu kɛ e hiɔwe tabilɔ ɔmɛ tsu Mawu kojomi ɔ he ní ngɛ Harmagedon ɔ, Mawu he nyɛli ɔmɛ a ti nɔ kake po be si fimi. (Yer. 25:33; Zak. 14:12) Se ngɛ jamɛ a ta a mi ɔ, Mawu to nɛ a he jua wa a ti nɔ kake po be gboo ngɛ e he nyɛli—nimli jio, mumi yayamihi jio—a dɛ mi.—Luka 21:28; 2 Pet. 2:9.

20 Ngɛ Harmagedon ta a be mi ɔ, la fiami munyu nɛ kɔɔ lalatsɛ ɔ he ɔ ma nyɛ maa ba mi pepeepe ke: “Nihi akpe kake maa gbo ngɛ o kpe he, nɛ nihi akpe nyɔngma maa gbo ngɛ o hio nɔ he, se e be mo he sue gblegbleegle. O kɛ o hɛngmɛ pɛ kɛkɛ maa hyɛ be nɛ o na yayami peeli a gblave yemi. Akɛnɛ o de ke: ‘Yehowa ji ye wehe hewɔ ɔ,’ o ha nɛ Ohiɛ Pɛtsɛ ɔ plɛ o hihe.” (La 91:7-9) Ke Harmagedon je sisi pɛ, wa ma nyɛ maa hyɛ blɔ kaa Yehowa maa bu e webi a he ngɛ nɔmlɔtso mi, henumi mi, kɛ mumi mi tsuo kɛ pi kpehi kɛkɛ a he se nɔ fɛɛ nɔ gbagbaata hulɔ. Akɛnɛ o de ke: “Yehowa ji ye wehe hewɔ ɔ,” Yehowa wo si ke: “E be mo he sue gblegbleegle.” Lɔɔ he ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ wa kɛ he kɛ nɔ fɔmi nɛ kpe hwooo se be ɔ, nɛ waa le kaa wa ma nyɛ maa fɔ Yehowa nɔ ngɛ buhemi he tsuo.

LA 36 Wa Baabu Wa Tsui He

HA O JUƐMI NƆ Bɔ nɛ wa ma plɛ kɛ bu Yehowa tue kɛ je wa tsui mi.

Bɔ Nɛ O Ma Plɛ Kɛ Ná “Tsui Nɛ Bue Nɔ́”

"Mo ha o tsɔlɔ ɔ tsui nɛ buɔ nɔ tue."—1 MAŊ. 3:9.

NÍMIHI bueɔ nihi nɛ ngɛ blɔ nya a he ngɛ nɔ́ he jehi fuu a he je. E biɛ nɛ nihi fuu maa pee jã kɛkɛ ke a ngɛ a hɛ jae loo a ngɛ hlae nɛ a tu a he kɛ je gblaveyeli he. Ke e ba Mawu bueblɔ he ɔ, komɛ peeɔ bueli ngɛ anɔkuale mi konɛ a ná hɛ mi nyami, kaa bɔ nɛ jamɔ hɛmi nyɛɛli nɛ ngɛ Yesu be ɔ mi ɔ pee ɔ. (Mat. 23:5) Se ngɛ nɔ́ nɛ e sɛhlɛɛ mi ɔ, wɔɔ lɛɛ wa bueɔ Yehowa ejakaa wa naa se nami nɛ ngɛ e blɔtsɔɔmi ɔ he. (Yes. 48:17, 18) Nɔ́ he je kpakpa nɛ konɛ a kɛ bue e blɔ, se wa ngɛ nɔ́ he je nɛ he hia pe kulaa nɛ wa kɛ maa bue lɛ.

2 Yi mi titli he je nɛ wa buɔ Yehowa tue ji kaa wa suɔ lɛ wawɛː. (Mat. 22:37, 38) Suɔmi nɛ wa ngɛ kɛ ha lɛ ɔ haa nɛ waa “yeɔ tue kɛ jeɔ wa tsui mi.” (Rom. 6:17) Suɔmi nɛ wa ngɛ kɛ ha Yehowa kɛ “tue bumi tsui” tsɔɔ wa nya nɛ waa bɔ mɔde fɛɛ nɛ wa maa bɔ kɛ ye e kita amɛ a nɔ, pi be nɛ ni kpahi ngɛ wɔ hyɛe pɛ, aloo be nɛ wa na he se nɛ wa ma ná amlɔ nɔuu ɔ kɛkɛ, se be fɛɛ be. Níhi a mi kpa ko ji kaa, wa tsui lakaa nɔ, enɛ ɔ he ɔ, be komɛ ɔ, e he wami kaa wa ma ná juɛmi nɛ da. Ngɛ munyu nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, wa ma susu Baiblo mi sanehi enyɔ nɛ ma nyɛ maa ye bua wɔ nɛ waa ná tue bumi tsui he. Kekleekle ɔ, wa ma susu be nɛ Yehowa kpɔ̃ Israelbii ɔmɛ kɛ je Ejipt ɔ he. Lɔ ɔ se ɔ, wa ma hyɛ Yuda Matsɛ Yehoshafat nɔhyɛnɔ kpakpa a.

KƐ KƐLE TUE BƆƆ NƐ PEE SE Ɔ ATUA TSƆMI

3 Loko Yehowa ma he Israelbime ɔmɛ a yi wami kɛ je Egipt ɔ, e ha mɛ mlaa ko nɛ ka a tue bumi.

1. Mɛni heje nimli ma nyɛ maa bu nihi nɛ ngɛ he blɔ nya a?

2. Mɛni ji nɔ titli he je nɛ wa buɔ Yehowa tue ɔ?

3. Kɛ mɛni blɔ nɔ nɛ Yehowa fãmi nɛ kɔɔ muɔ kɛ sinɔmi ngɛ sinyami tso ɔmɛ a he ɔ ka Israelbi ɔmɛ a tue gblami kɛɛ? (Hyɛ fɔto ɔ hulɔ.)

8

Israelbii ɔmɛ bu Yehowa gbi, keji Egiptbii ɔmɛ maa pee nɔ́ ko po

(Hyɛ kuku 3)

Konɛ a kpa gblamii nyɔngɔne kɛ nyagbe nɔ ɔ he je ɔ, e biɔ nɛ a sã gwenɔ loo aple binyumu kɛ sã afɔle nɛ a kɛ e muɔ ɔ nɛ fia a wehi a sinya tsohi a nɔ. (2 Mose 12:5, 7) Yehowa maa be hehi nɛ a kɛ muɔ ɔ to he kadi ɔ a nɔ nɛ e ma baa nɔ fɛɛ nɔ we mi klɛngbina bihi, nimli kɛ lohwehi tsuo a yi. Se kɛ̃ ɔ, e ko ba kɛ̃ nɛ a ka Israelbii ɔmɛ nɛ a gbo tue. Mɛni he je? Eko ɔ, a maa ye gbeye kaa Egiptbii ɔmɛ maa tɔ a nɔ bɔbi, ejakaa a buɔ gwenɔhi kaa nɔ́ klɔuklɔu. (2 Mose 8:25-27) Se kɛ̃ ɔ, Israelbii ɔmɛ na kaa tue bumi he hia wawɛɛ nɛ a “pee bɔ nɛ Yehowa fã ɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ.” (2 Mose 12:28) Yehowa gbaa mɛ kɛ gu a klɛngbina bihi a yi nɛ e baa a nɔ.

4 Jamɛ a nyo ɔ nɔuu nɛ Israelbii ɔmɛ je Egipt. Kekleekle ɔ, Yehowa tsɔɔ mɛ blɔ kɛ ya Wo Tsu ɔ he yɔ kɔkɔɔyɛ je gbɛ, se lɔ ɔ se ɔ, e ha mɛ blɔtsɔɔmi komɛ nɛ adesahi nɛ mɛ nɔ́ nɛ he hia nɛ a maa pee tlɔ. E de mɛ ke a plɛ kɛ ya kɔkɔɔyɛ je kɛ ya to asafo wo ɔ kɛ yɔhi a kpɛti. Ngɛ he nɛ a ya bɔ asafo ngɛ ɔ, e ngɛ kaa Farao tabhi nɛ a ngɛ a se nyɛɛe ɔ kɛ Wo Tsu ɔ ma tsi a blɔ. Se kɛ̃ ɔ, Israelbii ɔmɛ pee nɔ́ nɛ Yehowa fã mɛ kɛ gu e tsɔlɔ Mose nɔ kɛ kɛ ya nɔ ekoko. (2 Mose 14:1-4) Se benɛ a na Egiptbii ɔmɛ nɛ a ngɛ bae ɔ, “a tsui po” nɛ a fã Mose po nɛ e ya kpata a hɛ mi! (2 Mose 14:5-11) Mɛni heje nɛ a fɔ̃li a buɛmi kaa a bu tue ɔ? Nɛ kɛ Yehowa na a subai ɔ ha kɛɛ?

5 Baiblo ɔ tsɔɔ kaa be mi nɛ Israelbi ɔmɛ ngɛ Wo Tshu ɔ he ɔ, a “nui [Yehowa] nyakpɛ nitsumi ɔmɛ” nɛ e tsu ngɛ Mizraim ɔ sisi loo a “kai” e suɔmi nɛ se pui ɔ. (Kane La 106:7 kɛ sisi ningma ɔ.) A na bɔ nɛ Yehowa po a bitɛhi a he piɛ ngɛ haomi nɛ ji nyɔngma a mi. Se a nui Yehowa suɔmi nɛ e ngɛ kɛ ha mɛ ɔ sisi kulaa loo a hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔ e nɔ kaa e ma he a yi wami ekko hu. Akɛnɛ Yehowa le bɔ nɛ a tsui ngɛ ha heje ɔ, e de kaa nimli ɔmɛ “tɔ̃ . . . ngɛ Wo Tshu ɔ nya.” E ngɛ mi kaa a nyɛɛ Mose se kɛ gu Wo Tshu ɔ mi mohu lɛɛ, se a tsakee we a susumi gblegbleegble. Nyagbenyagbe ɔ, Yehowa kua jamɛatsɛmɛ ɔmɛ tsuo pɛpɛɛpɛ

4. Mɛni blɔtsɔɔmi nɛ se be nɛ Yehowa kɛ ha Israelbi ɔmɛ benɛ a je kɛ je Egipt ɔ?

5. Mɛni Israelbi ɔmɛ pee we ngɛ Wo Tsu ɔ nya? (La 106:7 kɛ sisi ningma)

KASE NÍ NƆ NƐ ISRAELBI ƆMƐ PE E PE KƆKƆBƆ NƆ HYƐMI NƆ́ Ɔ HE

6 Klɛɛle Munyu ɔ tsɔɔ kaa e sa nɛ waa hyɛ nɔ́ he je nɛ wa bueɔ Yehowa kɛ nihi nɛ e hla konɛ a nyɛɛ e webi a hɛ mi ɔ he mi nɛ wa le. Anɛ ke wa na nɔ́ kpakpa ko kɛ je mi amlɔ nɔuu pɛ ji be nɛ wa bueɔ lo? Ekoɛ ɔ, Israelbii ɔmɛ ekomɛ kpiti jijɔ muɔ kɛ fɔ sinyami tso ɔmɛ a nɔ konɛ a bitɛtehi nɛ a ko gbo pɛ kɛkɛ. Jehanɛ se hu, anɛ nɔ́ nɛ ni kpaamɛ peeɔ ɔ nɔ nɛ wa tue bumi daa lo? Hɛ Kɛ Hɔ Nyɔɔ ɔ nɔ ɔ, Yehowa ha nɛ “kpootsɛmɛ ɔmɛ na mɛ mɔbɔ.” (2 Mose 12:36) Se benɛ kpootsɛmɛ ɔmɛ kpa mɛ nya huɛɛpee se ɔ, Israelbii ɔmɛ kpa Yehowa hɛkɛnɔfɔmi nɛ a tso atua. (2 Mose 14:10-12) Ngɛ slɔɔto kɛ je subai nɛ nɛ a je kpo ɔ he ɔ, ke wa ngɛ tue bumi tsui ɔ, e maa wo wɔ he wami nɛ waa fɔ Yehowa kɛ nihi nɛ e hla konɛ a nyɛɛ wa hɛ mi ɔ a nɔ hɛkɛ. (Kane Yeremia 23:4.) Wa maa nyɛɛ blɔtsɔɔmi nɛ a kɛ haa wɔ ɔ se, ke wa nui nɔ́ he je tutuutu nɛ a de ke wa pee jã a sisi hulɔ, aloo ke jã nɛ wa maa pee ɔ maa wo nihi nɛ a sume Yehowa a mi mi la. Mɛni blɔ nɔ wa ma nyɛ maa gu kɛ tsi wa he kɛ je tɔmi nɛ ɔmɛ nɛ Israelbii ɔmɛ tɔ ɔ he, nɛ waa na tue bumi tsui?

7 Ha nɛ suɔmi nɛ o ngɛ kɛ ha Yehowa a mi nɛ wa. Ke wa nɛ Mawu blɔtsɔɔmi se nami amrɔ nɔuu aloo ke wa kpe kɛ nyagbahi a nya ngɛ wa tuebɔmi he je ɔ, e ma nyɛ maa ka wa tuebɔmi. Ke jã

nɔ́ nɛ ba mɛ, e sa nɛ waa kai kaa nɔ́ heje titli nɛ wa buɔ Yehowa tue ji kaa wa suɔ lɛ nɛ wa ngɛ hemi kɛ yemi ngɛ e mi. (1 Yohane 5:3) Suɔmi nɛ wa ngɛ kɛ ha lɛ ɔ yeɔ bua wɔ nɛ wa toɔ wa tsui si kɛ mlɛɔ lɛ, nɛ e haa nɛ wa kplɛɛɔ nɔ kaa eko ɔ, be ko maa be loko wa maa na nyɔmwo kpakpa nɛ tue nɛ wa buɔ ɔ he je nɛ wa náa.—Fiɛlɔ 7:8; Mika 7:7.

8 Ngɛ wa be nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, Mawu we bi pee nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ kpakpa ngɛ tue bami mi, ke a nɛ́ he se nɛ ngɛ mi amlɔ nɔuu po. Kake he nɔ hyɛmi ji, benɛ COVID-19 hiɛ ɔ, a tsɔɔ asafohi blɔ kaa a nɛ ngɔ videokonferensi kɛ pee a kpehi. Asafo mi nɔkɔtɔma ko ngɛ Peru kai ke: “Kekleekle ɔ, i susu kaa blɔtsɔɔmi ɔ be he se nami ngɛ wa he ɔ.” Ngɛ e tsui nɛ po ɔ se ɔ, e fia e pɛɛ si kaa e maa bu Mawu tue nɛ e maa ngɔ e hɛ kɛ fɔ e nɔ. (Abɛ 3:5, 6) Kɛ pee kɛ ba kɛɛ? E ke: “I na bɔ nɛ Yehowa jɔɔ tue bami nɔ ha. Wa safo ɔ mi wa nɛ kake peemi ba wa kpɛti pe tsɔ.” Mo susu nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ kpa he nɛ o hyɛ. Be ko nɛ be ngɛ jeha 2020 mi ɔ, blɔ nya tomi ɔ tsake blɔtsɔɔmihi nɛ kɔɔ ma se gbi asafohi kɛ kuuhi a he. Benɛ nyɛminyɛyo ko ngɛ Japan le kaa a maa kpa kuu nɛ e ngɛ sɔmɔe ngɛ mi ɔ mi kpeemi ɔ, e ke: “I suɔ nɛ ma bu tsakemi ɔ tue nɛ ma ko tu he munɔ yayami. I ngɛ blɔ hyɛe kaa ma na se namihi nɛ ngɛ mi.”

10 HWƐƆMI MƆ Ɔ

Mawu Matsɛ Yemi ɔ he sane kpakpa a fiɛɛmi biɔ nɛ waa de ni kpahi kaa e be kɛe nɛ yi wu matsɛ yemi nɛ ɔ ma ba nyagbe. Wa maa ya nɔ maa fiɛɛ sɛgbi nɛ ɔ kɛ ya su be nɛ je nɛ ɔ ma ba nyagbe ngɛ Harmagedon ɔ nya pɛɛ. (Mat. 24:14) Jehanɛ se hu ɔ, ngɛ haomi ngua a be ɔ mi ɔ, ekeko sɛgbi nɛ wa maa fiɛɛ ɔ maa pee nɛ he wa nɛ e tɛ̃ɛ be he nɛ maa ha nɛ nihi nɛ a kpe a yi mi kpɔ kaa a maa kplɛɛ Matsɛ Yemi ɔ nɔ aloo a be kplɛɛe. Kɛ ha nihi nɛ a nyɛ Yehowa a, ekoɔ wa sɛgbi ɔ maa pee kaa gbenɔ hiɔ nɛ “nya wa tso.”* (Kpo. 16:21) E be he jajei ko kɔɔ he kaa a mi mi ma fu, kaa bɔ nɛ e ba ngɛ Farao blɔ faa ngɛ Ŋshí Tsutsu ɔ he ɔ. Waa kɛ bɔ nɛ wa maa bu tue kɛ ha fiɛɛmi fãmi ɔ amlɔ nɛ ɔ be nɛ nihi susu nɔ́ kpa ko ngɛ wa he po ɔ, ma nyɛ maa dla wa he kɛ ha tutuami nɛ maa ba hwɔɔ se.

KASE MATSƐ YEHOSHAFAT LƐ E TUE BULI LƐ

10. Kɛ Matsɛ Yehoshafat nu he ha kɛɛ benɛ ma kpahi ba konɛ a kɛ lɛ nɛ ba hwu ɔ?

Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, Matsɛ Yehoshafat kɛ je ma etɛ nɛ a ba bɔɔ he kɛ ma nɛ a kɛ Yuda ma a nɛ ba hwu ta kpe. E sa nɛ waa nu sisi kaa, gbeyei nɔ Yehoshafat kɛ e webi ɔmɛ. (2 Kron. 20:1-3) Se matsɛ ɔ ha we nɛ gbeyei nɛ ha nɛ e kpa nɔ́ ko peemi; e pee nɔ́ ko he nɔ́. Nɔ́ nɛ Yehoshafat pee lɔ ɔ se ɔ nɛ wa maa kase ɔ ma nyɛ maa ye bua wɔ nɛ waa ná tsui nɛ buɔ nɔ tue.

11 Tswaa o yi mi kaa o maa hla Yehowa se, nɛ o maa bu e tue. Benɛ Jehoshaphat ye gbeye ɔ, “e tswaa e yi mi kaa e maa hla Yehowa se.” (2 Kron. 20:3) Jehoshaphat fia e pɛɛ kɛ je sisije kɛ kɔ Yehowa se hlaami kɛ ha blɔtsɔɔmi he. (2 Kron. 19:3) Se ngɛ si fɔfɔɛ nɛ mi wa nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, e “tswaa e yi mi,” aloo e wo e he wami nɛ kɔ e pɛɛ nɛ e fia kaa e maa hla Yehowa se kɛ bu blɔtsɔɔmi tsuaa blɔtsɔɔmi nɛ E kɛ maa ha e tue. Kɛkɛ nɛ Jehoshaphat kɛ tsui tsuo sɔle. (Kane 2 Kronika 20:5, 6, 10-12.) Ke wa ngɛ haomihi a mi kɛ guee ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ wa kase e nɔhyɛwe nɛ ɔ. Wa ma nyɛ maa tswaa wa yi mi kaa wa maa bu Yehowa tue, nɛ wa maa hla e blɔtsɔɔmi se

YEMI KƐ BUAMI! Adesahi tsuo hia lɛ kɛ̃, jio a le loo a li. Ngɛ anɔkwale mi ɔ, he nɛ a hia yemi kɛ buami nɛ ɔ he wa wawɛː. Mɛni heje? Munyu kake kɛkɛ, he yayami. Akɛnɛ a ba adesahi tsuo kɛ he yayami heje ɔ, a gba a kɛ Mawu kpe mi. (Efe. 2:12; Kol. 1:21) Ngɛ blɔ ko nɔ ɔ, he yayami haa nɛ adesahi kɛ Mawu a kpɛti tsɔɔ, nɛ adesa nyɛ we nɛ e fo muɔ nɛ ɔ lolo ngɛ lɛ nitsɛ e he mi. Ke yemi kɛ buami be ɔ, wa be nyɛe maa na si himi nitsɛnitsɛ sisi gblegbleegble. Wa be hɛ nɔ kami ko náe kɛ ha hwɔɔ se. Mɛni yemi kɛ buami Yehowa kɛ ha?

2 Bɔ nɛ pee nɛ a kpɔ̃ wɔ kɛ je wa yayami si himi ɔ mi heje ɔ, Yehowa to blɔ nya nɛ yɔɔ nyakpɛ ko—lɛ ji osɔfo yemi blɔ nya tomi ɔ nɛ. E pee jã kɛ ha blema Israel ma a, nɛ pee se ɔ, e pee jã kɛ ha Kristofohi a ma a. Jamɛ a blɔ nya tomi ɔ ngɛ nitsumi nɔ kɛ ba si himɛ nɔɔ. Ngɛ munyu nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, wa ma susu bɔ nɛ osɔfo yemi blɔ nya tomi ɔ ba pee jɔɔmi kɛ ha nyumuhi kɛ yihi ngɛ blema a he. Wa maa kase bɔ nɛ wa ma nyɛ ma ná osɔfo yemi blɔ nya tomi nɛ ngɛ nitsumi nɔ amlɔ nɛ ɔ he se ha, kɛ bɔ nɛ wa maa ya nɔ nɛ wa maa pee jã ngɛ hwɔɔ se hu. Nɛ ngɛ nyagbe ɔ, wa maa na bɔ nɛ osɔfo yemi ɔ jeɔ suɔmi gbagbatee nɛ Yehowa ngɛ kɛ ha wɔ ɔ kpo ha.

Ha Kristo He Jɔmi Ɔ Nɛ Ye Nyɛ Tsuihi A Nɔ

Kolosebi 3:15 tuɔ “Kristo he jɔmi” he munyu. Mɛni ji “Kristo he jɔmi”? E biɔ mi he jɔmi nɛ nɔ ko ma nyɛ ma ná kɛ gu Kristo kaselɔ nɛ e ji he. Jamɛatsɛ he jɔmi nɛ ɔ jeɔ nilee nɛ waa le kaa Yehowa kɛ e Bi ɔ suɔ wɔ ɔ mi.—Yohane 14:23, 27.

Bɔfo Paulo wo Kristofohi nɛ ngɛ Kolose asafo ɔ mi ɔ he wami kaa a ha he jɔmi nɛ ɔ nɛ ye a tsuihi, aloo a mi nɔmlɔ ɔ nɔ. Kɛ nɛ ɔ ji ha kɛɛ? Helabii a munyu nɛ Paulo kɛ tsu ní ngɛ hiɛ ɔ kɛ ha “ye nɔ” ɔ kɛ munyu nɛ a kɛ tsuɔ ní kɛ ha hɛmi nyɛɛlɔ ngɛ kami ní tsumihi a mi ɔ ngɛ tsakpa. Anɛ enɛ ɔ tsɔɔwe kaa e sa nɛ “Kristo ɔ he jɔmi ɔ” nɛ ye nyagbe munyu ɔ ngɛ wa tsuihi a mi lo?

Kɛ̃kɛ, ke nɔ́ ko ha ní kakehemi be wa kɛ nyɛminyɛ hemi kɛ yemi nɔ́ ko kpɛti ɔ, e sa nɛ waa ngmɛ́ "Kristo tue gbaami ɔ" blɔ konɛ e pee "kojoalo." E maa ye bua wɔ nɛ waa le nɔ́ nɛ wa maa pee konɛ tue gbaami kɛ kake peemi nɛ hi si. Ke wa nyɛɛ gbɛ nɛ ɔ nɔ, ke e biɔ po ke waa je wa suɔmi níhi aloo wa he blɔhi a he níhi a nɔ ɔ, wa tsɔɔ kaa wa ngɛ "Kristo tue gbaami ɔ" blɔ ngmɛ́e konɛ e ye matsɛ ngɛ wa tsuihi a mi. -Kol. 3:12-14.
