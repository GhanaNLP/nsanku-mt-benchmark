It is said that there were once twins whose mother died and left them. During a festival, Ata Panin took beads and gold to adorn Ata Kuma. Ata Kuma and his peers said they were going to bathe in the river. As they bathed, the gold that adorned Ata Kuma broke and fell into the water. He searched and searched, but could not find it. He returned home and told his brother, "The gold is lost." Ata Panin said, "I don't care if it is lost, but if it is indeed lost, I will demand of you something difficult." Ata Kuma said, "What will you demand of me?" He said, "I demand of you a leopard's claw, an Aserewa's cartridge belt, Ponponponponsa, a single leaf, and the tail of the Queen Elephant." Ata Kuma said, "I have heard." Ata Kuma set out and arrived at a place where a solitary tree stood. When the tree saw him, it said, "Here is Ata Kuma; he has set off so early that the skies have gathered clouds." He said, "It is for no ordinary reason that I have reached here, but my father's child is Ata Panin, and my mother's child is Ata Panin, and he bought beads to adorn me, and they broke and were lost."

Aserewa said, “Is this why you are crying? Come and take some.” And Ata Kuma went and took some, and thanked him. And he set off and went on, and he came upon Ponponponponsa. He said, “Here is Ata Kuma, he has pricked the back of his foot and made the rain gather clouds.” And he related the mission in the very same way. And Ponponponponsa gave him some, and he took it away. Then he came upon Etwie (Leopard). When Etwie saw him, he said, “Here is Ata Kuma, he has pricked the back of his foot and made the rain gather clouds.” Ata Kuma related his mission to him. He said, “Come and take some.” And then Ata Kuma said, “Father Leopard, how can I get the tail of the Queen Elephant?” He said, “Oh! When you go, all the beasts will be asleep, and the Queen Elephant will be lying in the middle. When you go, tread heavily upon them, and when you reach the Queen Elephant, cut off her tail; but if you tread softly upon them, they will kill you.” Ata Kuma said, “Thank you.” He went and saw these beasts; he trod very heavily upon them, and reached the Queen Elephant. He cut off her tail and returned. And he found a looking-glass on the way and brought it along. And he arrived home. He said, “Ata Panin, the things you said you would take from me, here they are.” Ata Panin said, “I have received them.” One day, as they were sitting there, Ata Kuma took the looking-glass and looked into it. He said, “This looking-glass, when you look into it, you see your mother who is in the underworld.” Ata Panin said, “Truly? Give it to me so I may look.” He gave it to him. As he went to look into it, it slipped from his hand and broke. Ata Kuma said, “As for this, what you demanded of me, I too demand of you.” Then Ata Panin went and took what Ata Kuma had brought and brought it to him. Ata Kuma said, “I do not want this, unless you go and fetch a new one for me.” Ata Panin set off and came upon a single leaf. It said, “Here is Ata Panin, he has pricked the back of his foot and made the rain gather clouds.” Ata Panin said, “I have pricked your mother and your father! As for me, give me some of your leaves so I may go.” The single leaf said, “Go forward to where someone who knows you is.” He came upon Aserewa. Aserewa said, “Here is Ata Panin, he has pricked the back of his foot and made the rain gather clouds.” Ata Panin said, “I have pricked your mother and your father! As for me, give me some of your nest so I may go.” Aserewa said, “Go to where someone who knows you is.” Then he came upon Ponponponponsa. Ponponponponsa said, “Here is Ata Panin, he has pricked the back of his foot and made the rain gather clouds.” Ata Panin said, “I have pricked your mother, I have pricked your father! As for me, give me some of your water so I may go.” Ponponponponsa said, “Go forward to where someone who knows you is.” Then he came upon Etwie. Etwie said, “Here is Ata Panin, he has pricked the back of his foot and made the rain gather clouds.” Ata Panin said, “I have pricked your mother and your father! As for me, give me some of your claws so I may go.” Etwie said, “Someone who knows you is ahead.” He set off and went and came upon the herd of elephants, and the Queen Elephant was lying in the middle. Then he went and cut her tail. The whole herd of elephants seized him, trampled upon him, and he died. Ata Kuma waited for his brother. He did not come. That is why it is said, “If you live with your own blood, and he spoils your thing, do not exact compensation from him, for Ata Panin did so and went to his death.”
