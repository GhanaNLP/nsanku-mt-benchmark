MARRIAGE ARRANGEMENT

10. The government has made various marriage arrangements in the 2021 Budget Statement that will enable us to achieve rapid progress and address the challenges brought about by the Coronavirus Disease. These arrangements are as follows:

• COVID-19 Health Levy of one percent to be placed on the National Health Insurance Levy, and a one percent increase in the Value Added Tax (VAT);

• The Sanitation and Pollution Levy, which is ten pesewas levied on every liter of petrol. This is a health measure that will protect our health from various diseases.

• 20 pesewas should be added to each liter of oil or petrol. This will help reduce the existing electricity bill;

• A 5 percent tax has been imposed on the profits made by the banks. This will be reviewed and amended in the year 2024;

THE IMPACT OF THE CORONAVIRUS PANDEMIC IN THIS COUNTRY AND THE MEASURES TAKEN BY THE GOVERNMENT THROUGH THE GHANA CARES PROGRAMME (OBAATAN PA) TO PROVIDE RELIEF

12. In this Section, we will look at the challenges of the coronavirus pandemic on the world economy, including Ghana. We will also look at the measures taken by the government to help address the challenges.

The Symptoms of the Coronavirus Disease

13. The Corona Virus Pandemic that hit the whole world in the year 2019 brought tremendous trouble upon all the people of the world. It destroyed many businesses, especially in the economy. People lost their wages and the world's economic situation was completely ruined. This caused 2.6 million people to die.

14. In Ghana, the disease that previously affected eleven people every week increased, and in the last week of July in the year 2020, the disease affected seven hundred and eighty-one people. The disease decreased in December in the year 2020. By the end of that month, one hundred and four people had contracted the disease. In the year 2021, the disease has increased again. In January, ninety-nine people contracted the disease. By August 5, 880 people had contracted the disease.

15. In order to reduce the disease, the government introduced many measures in the country. This brought difficulties to many jobs. Some of the jobs that were mostly affected include hospitality industries, food service businesses, entertainment, health, tourism, manufacturing companies, construction businesses, and schooling.

16. Difficulties affected transport businesses. Drivers and their mates, as well as self-employed workers who did not receive any government assistance, were in great distress. The policy was necessary, but it brought hardship to many workers. It brought about a decline in trade.
