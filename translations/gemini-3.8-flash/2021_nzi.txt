THE SPOKESPERSON'S WORDS

In December 2020, Ghanaians voted again to elect President Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo to lead the nation for another four years and continue to do many things. The trust that Ghanaians have placed in this Government is something that must not be taken for granted at all.

The 2021 Budget is a significant Budget that will structure our country's economy to protect the lives of the citizens, revitalize struggling businesses, and look forward to rebuilding this nation of Ghana so that it recovers well in the aftermath of the Coronavirus pandemic. The hope of this Budget is to transform this country into a self-reliant nation.

In the first four years of our administration, we did many things to improve our economic situation. The economic policies we carefully put in place brought about many revenue-generating initiatives. This brought support that made various businesses progress, helped self-employed workers, and showcased many social intervention programs. By the end of 2019, our economy was completely sound. Indicators showing that our economic situation was strong became evident, and the international community saw that our nation was progressing. Not long after, the Coronavirus pandemic arrived here. The difficulties that this pandemic brought have affected every country in the world, including Ghana.

The Coronavirus pandemic brought many problems into our living conditions and into our various jobs. The government has seen the challenges that the pandemic has brought upon us, so we have made various arrangements that will help businesses stand on their feet again and ease our burden in our living conditions once more. Hope is not lost, because we have seen new ways through which we do different things to make them go well. The government is ensuring that we achieve good health, so we have made plans to vaccinate all Ghanaians so that we may have strength and build the nation of Ghana that we want to see.

We worked together with you during difficult times, even in the midst of economic challenges. We made sacrifices and bold decisions to put our economic situation in a place that would bring us progress. About 1.2 million Ghanaians are able to access quality secondary school education. We restructured the arrangements of the many collapsed banks and ensured that the majority of those whose money was locked up received their money. We have also opened a car manufacturing industry in this country, which has made cars made here in Ghana available on the market for purchase.

We truly admit that challenges arose in our development project goals. The Government is putting measures in place that will bring complete relief to the various businesses. For example, in May in the year 2020, the Government used funds of GH¢750 million Ghana Cedis to support small and large businesses to keep them going.

We are calling on everyone to unite as one in this nation's development effort. That means we will continue to work hard to support the progress of the nation. It all concludes with us dedicating ourselves to coming together, uniting, and using our strength and good hearts to build the nation of Ghana.

May God bless us all.

1. The 2021 National Budget is a review of what the Government was able to achieve in the year 2020 and the revenue expected to be obtained in the year 2021 to be used to undertake various projects. 10. Government has made various economic initiatives in the 2021 Budget Statement that will accelerate progress and address the challenges brought about by the Corona Virus Disease. These initiatives are:

• Pollution and Environmental Degradation tax of ten pesewas levied on every gallon of diesel or petrol. This is a health measure that will protect our health from various diseases.

• Add 20 pesewas to each bottle of oil or petrol. This will help to reduce the electricity bill that is there;

• A 5 percent tax has been imposed on the profits earned by the banks. This will be reviewed and amended in the year 2024;

THE CHALLENGES OF THE CORONAVIRUS IN THIS COUNTRY AND THE MEASURES THAT THE GOVERNMENT HAS UNDERTAKEN THROUGH THE GHANA CARES PROGRAMME (ƐRƐLADANE KPALƐ) TO ASSIST

12. In this section, we will look at the challenges of the coronavirus pandemic on the world economy, which includes Ghana. We will also examine the measures that the government took to help address these challenges.

Problems/Symptoms of the Coronavirus Disease

13. The Corona Virus Pandemic that hit the whole world in the year 2019 brought immense hardship upon all the people of the world. It destroyed many businesses and jobs, especially in the economy. People lost their livelihood and the world's economic situation was completely ruined. This resulted in the death of 2.6 million people.

14. In Ghana, the disease which had been infecting eleven people every week increased, and in the last week of June 2020, the disease infected seven hundred and eighty-one people. The disease decreased in December 2020. By the end of that month, one hundred and four people had contracted the disease. In the year 2021, the disease increased again. In January, ninety-nine people contracted the disease. By the fifth day of August, 880 people had contracted the disease.

15. In order to reduce the disease, the government introduced many measures in the country. This brought hardship to many businesses. Some of the businesses that were greatly affected include the hotel industry, catering businesses, transport, health, travel, manufacturing companies, construction businesses, and schooling.

16. Difficulties affected transport operations. Drivers and their mates, as well as informal workers who received no government assistance, suffered greatly. Although the measures were necessary, they caused great hardship for many workers. It led to a decline in business.

GLOSSARY

One District, One Factory: Factories and various manufacturing centers opened in every single district across the country to advance the nation's industrialization. This will bring numerous jobs to Ghanaians everywhere in the country.

Accountability: This means that the Government has a responsibility and an explanation to the citizens regarding the various works it has undertaken and the outcomes that resulted from them. It also includes an accounting of the various funds and resources in the hands of the Government.

Budgeting: This refers to the manner in which the country's money is shared among the various Government civil service works, various departments, and various aid agencies.

Troubleshooting: This is a way by which problems are reduced.

Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics: This is an umbrella term used to group these academic disciplines together. Most often, it is used to describe an educational policy and academic curriculum in schools to improve interest and competitiveness in science and technological development.

Financial Budget: This is a plan that shows how money was obtained or spent. For example, a family can make a financial budget that shows the amount of money it has and how it must take care of things that are most needed (such as rent, electricity and water bills, and food).
