Yɛnni aseɛ koraa, yɛnni aseɛ koraa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokorɛ)

ƆBABAABUN BIAARA ƐSƐ SƐ ƆWARE NEA WƆDE NO MA NO WƆ AWAREƐ MU NO

ƐBERƐ bi na abaayewa bi wɔ hɔ a wɔfrɛ no Kwaboaso. Obiara a wɔde no maa no sɛ ɔnwre no no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Mempe no." Wɔde no maa ɔbɔfoɔ bi, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Twea! saa nipa yi wɔ nkontuma, mempe no."

Da koro bi ɔkɔɔ afuo mu, sɛ ɔrekɔtwa bɔrɔdeɛ. Ɔfaa sekan de twaa bɔrɔdeɛ no, to! hwɛ na mmoatia no tete bɔrɔdeɛ no so; wɔsian bae, bɛkyeree Kwaboaso; wɔkaa sɛ, "Wone deɛ sɛ wɔde wo rema obiara a, wowoso wo ti, pusu! pusu!" Na mmoatia no kyeree no kaa sɛ: "Bra na yɛnkyi no."

Yɛmia no o!

Yɛmia Kwaboaso.

Bra, ma yɛnkyi no.

Yɛmia no o!

Afei, berɛ a ɔbɔfoɔ a wɔde Kwaboaso maa no no tee Kwaboaso nne no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Merekɔhwɛ nea ɛrekɔ so, efisɛ yɛmfa bɔne nntua bɔne so ka." Berɛ a ɔkɔeɛ no, na Kwaboaso da hɔ, na mmoatia no rekyi no. Afei ɔbɔfoɔ no totoo tuo guu mmoatia no so, na baako hwee fam. Mmoatia no mu panyin kaa kyerɛɛ (afoforo no) sɛ, "Wanom nsafufuo, na waboro; momfa no nto hɔ ansa, (na montoaso nkyi no)." Ɔbɔfoɔ no san totoo tuo bio, na baako nso hwee fam. Mmoatia no mu panyin kaa sɛ, "Ɔkofoɔ no anom nsafufuo na aboro no, momfa no nkɔto nkyɛn wɔ hɔ." Ɔbɔfoɔ no kunkum mmoatia no nyinaa gye ɔpanyin no nko ara. Ɔpanyin no frɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no sɛ, "Bra." Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Meremma." Mmoatia no mu panyin no kaa sɛ, "O, bra, merenyɛ wo hwee." Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔeɛ. Mmoatia no mu panyin no kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ me dan mu hɔ, na wobɛhunu tuo aduro (kyerɛ sɛ atuduro) no ne otuo-mma a wototoyɛ no nyinaa, fa nea ɛyɛ wo deɛ, na fa Kwaboaso nso ka ho, nanso ansa na wobɛkɔ no, kɔtwa kwadu, na berɛ a worerekɔ no, to gu kwan so, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a berɛ a mmoatia a aka no bɛnyan, na wɔaba sɛ wɔrebɛkye wo no, ɛkɔba sɛ wɔgyina hɔ sesei baako baako a, na woakɔ akyirikyiri akyirikyiri dedaw." Enti ɔkɔtwitwaa kwadu, na ɔfaa Kwaboaso nso kaa ho, na berɛ a ɔduruu kwan no so no, ɔtoo kwadu baako guu fam. Ɔyɛɛ saa ara kosii sɛ ɔkɔduruu fie. Na berɛ a ɛkaa kakraa bi pɛ na wɔaduru fie no, hwɛ na mmoatia no reti wɔn. Na ɔtoo kwadu a aka no nyinaa guu fam, na mmoatia no kɔhwehwɛɛ no na (awieeɛ koraa no wɔsanee) kɔeɛ. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no de Kwaboaso kɔmaa ne mbusuafoɔ. Afei ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔɔ ne fie. Afei, na ɔbɔfoɔ no te hɔ, berɛ a ɔhunuu sɛ abɔfoɔ aduru ne nkyɛn, na ɔbisaa sɛ, "Amanneɛbɔ bɛn na ɛwɔ hɔ?" Abɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Kwaboaso se wasrɛ nkuroti Panyin no sɛ ɔmmɛsrɛ mma no, sɛ seesei deɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔware wo." Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Meda mo ase wɔ asɛm a ɛfiri nkuroti Panyin no anom no ho, nanso mentumi nware ababaawa no, efisɛ mprɛmprɛn mpo ntontom da so wɔ me ho." Ɛno nti na Mpanyimfoɔ se, "Sɛ wɔde wo rema obi aware a, ware no, efisɛ wonnim, ebia da koro bi a woba ahokyere mu no, ɔno na ɔbɛgye wo nkwa."

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
