Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka yi yɛ nokware)

ƐWƆ SƐ ABEAWA BIARA WARE NEA WƆDE NO MA NO WAREFOƆ NO

NA abaayewa bi wɔ hɔ a na wɔfrɛ no Kwaboaso. Obiara a wɔde no ma no sɛ ɔware no no, ɔka sɛ, "Mempe no." Wɔde no maa ɔbɔfoɔ bi, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Puu! saa barima yi ho wɔ ntontom, mempɛ no."

Da bi ɔkɔɔ afum sɛ ɔrekɔtwa bɔrɔdeɛ. Ɔfaa sekan de twaa bɔrɔdeɛ no, to! na hwɛ mmoatia no tete bɔrɔdeɛ no so; wɔsianee, na wɔbaa bɛkyeree Kwaboaso; wɔkaa sɛ, "Wo na sɛ wɔfa wo ma obi a, wowoso wo ti, pusu! pusu!" Na mmoatia no soo ne mu kae sɛ: "Bra, ma yɛnkyi no.

Yɛmia no o!

Yɛmia Kwaboaso.

Bra, ma yɛnkyi no.

Yɛmia no o!

Afei, berɛ a obɔfoɔ a wɔde Kwaboaso maa no no tee Kwaboaso nne no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Merekɔhwɛ deɛ ɛrekɔ so, ɛfiri sɛ yɛmfa bɔne nsi bɔne ananmu." Berɛ a ɔkɔeɛ no, na Kwaboaso da hɔ, na mmoatia no remia no. Ɛnna obɔfoɔ no too tuo guu mmoatia no so, na baako hwee fam. Mmoatia no mu panin no ka kyerɛɛ (nkae no) sɛ, "Wanom nsāfufuo, na wabo; monfa no nto hɔ ansa, (na afei monkɔ so nsiw no)." Ɔbɔfoɔ no totoo tuo bio, na baako nso hwee fam. Mmoatia no mu panin no kaa sɛ, "Ɔbarima katakyie no anom nsāfufuo na watɔ bā, monfa no nkɔto nkyɛn wɔ hɔ." Ɔbɔfoɔ no kunkum mmoatia no nyinaa gye panin no nko ara pɛ. Panyin no frɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no sɛ, "Bra." Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Meremma." Mmoatia no mu panyin no kaa sɛ, "O, bra, merenyɛ wo hwee." Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔeɛ. Mmoatia no mu panyin no kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ me dan mu hɔ, na wobɛhunu etuo aduro no (kyerɛ sɛ atoduro no) ne atoprɛ a woatow no nyinaa, fa nea ɛyɛ wo de, na fa Kwaboaso nso ka ho, nanso ansa na wobɛkɔ no, kɔ twa kwadu, na sɛ worekɔ a, tow gu, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a sɛ mmoatia a aka no nyan na wɔba sɛ wɔrebɛkyere wo a, ɛnde ansa na wɔbɛgyina asese no mmaako mmaako no, na woakɔ akyɛkyɛkyɛ pɛn." Enti ɔkɔe kɔtwaa kwadu, na ɔfaa K waboaso nso, na berɛ a ɔduruu kwan no so no, ɔtooo kwadu baako twenee. Ɔkɔɔ so yɛɛ saa ara kɔsii fie. Na berɛ a aka kakraa bi na wɔaduru fie no, hwɛ, mmoatia no reti wɔn. Na ɔtooo kwadu baako pɛ a aka no guu fam, na mmoatia no kɔdii akyi na (awieeɛ koraa no wɔsanee) kɔɔ wɔn akyi. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no de Kwaboaso kɔmaa ne mmarimafoɔ. Ɛnna ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔɔ ne fie. Afei, na ɔbɔfoɔ no te hɔ, ɛnna ɔhunuu sɛ abɔfoɔ aba ne nkyɛn, na ɔbisaa sɛ, "Amanneɛ bɛn na ɛwɔ hɔ?" Abɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Kwaboaso se ɔsrɛɛ kuro no mu Panyin sɛ ɔmmɛpa kyɛw mma no, na ɔse afei deɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔware wo." Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Meda mo ase wɔ nsɛm a ɛfiri kuro no mu Panyin anom no ho, nanso merentumi nware ababaawa no, efisɛ me da so wɔ t ntutu me nipadua no so." Ɛno nti na Mpaninfoɔ no ka sɛ, "Sɛ wɔfa wo ma wo kɔware obi a, ware no, efisɛ wonnim na da koro bi a wobiaba ahohia mu no, ɔbɛgye wo nkwa."

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
