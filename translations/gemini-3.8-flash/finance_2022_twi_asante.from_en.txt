5 | Ghana Aban - 2022 Ɔmanmma Sikakora Nhyehyɛe

“Adwumayɛfoɔ Ɔman a Ɛbɛtumi Agyina Ne Nan So Fapem Siesie: Sikasɛm Mu Nkɔsoɔ Ahyɛdeɛ ne Nnwuma Bɔ”

NNIANIM ADWENE

COVID-19 ɔyaredɔm no sɛeɛ a ɛde baeɛ no aka sikasɛm nyinaa, a Ghana nso ka ho. Amanehunu a Ghanafoɔ faa mu denam nkwa a ɛfiri mu, asetena ne nnwuma a ɛsɛee so no, Aban no nte aseɛ yie. Me ho keka me sɛ mereka sɛ yɛn anammɔntu a yɛde ko tiaa ɔyaredɔm no mu nsɛnnennen no yɛ kɛseɛ na ɛda adi pefee, na ewiase nyinaa kamfoo no kɛse. Afei nso, Aban no yɛɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ a ɛbɛboa ama nnwuma asan asɔre bio na ama yɛn asetena asan aba ne sibea pɛpɛɛpɛ.

Bio nso, deɛ ɛbɛyɛ na yɛatumi adi nsɛnnennen ne nneɛma pa a ɛfiri yarebɔne no mu baeɛ no ho dwuma no, Aban, wɔ Ɔtitenani H.E. Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo akannifoɔ pa ase no, yɛɛ COVID-19 Alleviation and Revitalisation of Enterprises nhyehyɛeɛ (GhanaCARES “Obaatan Pa” nhyehyɛeɛ) a ne boɔ yɛ GH¢100 billion ntɛm ara. Yei de ahotɔ a ɛba ntɛm na ɛso aba yɛn manfoɔ so, na ɛrenyane yɛn man nso bio.

Yɛda mo ase sɛ mokɔ so nya Aban no mu gyidie berɛ a yɛreyɛ adwuma sɛ yɛbɛnya ahotɔ a emu yɛ den afiri owudɔ yareɛ no mu no. Saa berɛ yi mu no, yɛn ahoɔden sɛ yɛbɛtumi atena ase sɛ ɔman no asɔ yɛn ahwɛ. Twerɛ Kronkron no ma yɛn akwankyerɛ a ɛho hia berɛ nyinaa wɔ mmere a ɛte sɛ yei mu. Ne titire koraa no, sɛdeɛ wɔaka wɔ Yesaia 9:10 mu no, “Ntayaa no abubu, nanso yɛde aboɔ a wɔasen bɛsi; sikamɔɔ nua no, wɔatwa, nanso yɛde nsedo bɛsi ananmu”. Saa kwan yi nso so no, Aban no nso asi ne bo sɛ ɛbɛdi ne bɔhyɛ ahodoɔ no so. Ɛno nti, yɛbɛkɔ so adi nhyehyɛeɛ ne dwumadie ahodoɔ a ɛbɛma yɛanya nea yɛahwere nyinaa bio na afei nso ama ɔmanfoɔ asetena atu mpɔn no akyi.

2022 Sikakora Nhyehyɛe no botae titiriw no mu baako, na nokwarem no wɔ mfe mmiɛnsa (3) a ɛreba no mu, ne sɛ yɛbɛsi Adwumayɛfo Man a ɛbɛtumi atim na ama sikasɛm mu nkɔso a emu yɛ den, ɛbɛtena hɔ daa, na ɛka obiara ho na ama obiara, titiriw Mmabun no, anya adwuma pa a ɛfata ayɛ. Enti, Aban nhyehyɛe ahorow bɛsi so dua sɛ ɛbɛboa mmabun no na wɔde wɔn akyɛde adi dwuma na wɔayi wɔn tumi adi na ama obiara anya hokwan pɛpɛɛpɛ de anya asetenam yiyedi.

Sɛnea ɛbɛhyɛ mmabun no awerɛhyem den na ahyɛ wɔn dwumadi ho akwankyerɛ mu den no, Aban bɛhyɛ YouStart nhyehyɛe no ase wɔ 2022 mu de ama adwumayɛfo nketewa anya boseabɔ kwan de ahyɛ wɔn dwumadi ase anaa wɔatrɛ mu.

Wɔ afe 2022 Sikakora Nhyehyɛe no mu no, yɛahyehyɛ nhyehyɛe ahorow a yɛde bɛtoa so adi sikasɛm mu ahokyere a ɛwɔ hɔ seesei no ho dwuma, na yɛasan nso afi ɔyaredɔm yi akyi aba a yɛn ho yɛ den kɛse. Aban no asi ne bo sɛ ɔbɛyɛ nhyehyɛe a ɛkɔ akyiri na ɛkɔ so pɛpɛɛpɛ de ama mmabun no anya nimdeɛ na watoto adwumayɛ ho nkuran. Bere a yɛrekɔ so adi Ɔmampanyin no

7 | Ghana Aban - 2022 Ɔmanmma Pɛsɛmenkominya Sikakora Nhyehyɛe

NNHIANIM

1. 2022 Citizen’s Budget no yɛ nea Aban no tumi yɛe wɔ 2021 mu ne sika a Aban no pɛ sɛ ɔboaboa ano wɔ towgyina mu de di ne nhyehyɛe ahorow so wɔ 2022 ne mfe kakra a ɛreba no mu no mfonin tiawa.

2. Sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a yɛbɛboa asetena-sikasɛm mu nsan-ntenten mmɔdenbɔ ne ɔman mpuntuo botaeɛ ahorow no, 2022 Sikakɔkɔɔ Nhyɛyeɛ (Budget) no si Adwumayɛfoɔ Ɔman a Ɛbɛtumi Agyina Pientee a Ɛnam Sikasɛm Mu Nkabom ne Nnwuma Mmɔbɔ So Si no so dua.

SIKASƐM HO NKƆSOƆ WƆ 2021 MU

Sikasɛm Mu Nsunsuanso Kɛseɛ

3. Nsɛm a ɛfa sikasɛm no kɔ anim kwan ho wɔ afe 2021 mu a yɛnyae mfiase no nie:

• Ne nyinaa mu no, real GDP nyaa nkyekyɛmu 3.5 percent wɔ 2021 fa a ɛdi kan no mu, sɛ yɛde toto 0.7 percent nkɔsoɔ a ɛbaa saa berɛ korɔ no ara mu wɔ 2020 ho a;

• Non-Oil real GDP nyinii bɛyɛ 4.8 percent wɔ afe 2021 fa a ɛdi kan no mu sɛ yɛde toto 1.1 percent a ɛkɔɔ so wɔ saa berɛ korɔ no ara mu wɔ 2020 ho a;

• Nneɛma boɔ akorɔn a ɛwɔ hɔ kɔpem Ɛbɔ awieeɛ no yɛ ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 10.6, sɛ yɛde toto 2020 berɛ korɔ no ara deɛ a ɛyɛ ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 10.4 no ho a;

• Budget deficit nyinaa (cash) a ɛyɛ GDP ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 7.7 berɛ a na wɔn botaeɛ yɛ GDP ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 7.4;

• Primary balance no nyaa deficit a ɛyɛ GDP no ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 2.0, sɛ yɛde toto deficit a na wɔahyɛ da ayɛ a ɛyɛ ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 1.7 no ho a;

• Ɔman no aka (sɛdeɛ ɛteɛ wɔ GDP ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu mu) gyinaa 77.7 ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu wɔ September 2021 awieeɛ, a ɛkɔɔ soro firii 76.1 ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu wɔ December 2020 awieeɛ;

• Gross International Reserves wɔ August 2021 awieeɛ no kɔduruu US$11.44 billion, a ɛdɔɔso sɛ ɛbɛtumi atua nnoɔma a yɛde ba ɔman mu no ho ka abosome 5.2, sɛ yɛde toto US$8.62 billion a ɛne nnoɔma a yɛde ba ɔman mu no ho ka abosome 4.1 yɛ pɛ a ɛbaa December 2020 awieeɛ no ho a; na

• Sika sesa boɔ no kɔɔ so gyinaa pintinn wɔ amanaman ntam sika akɛseɛ no ntam wɔ afe 2021 nkyekyɛmu mmiɛnsa a edi kan no mu. Ne titire no, Ghana cedi no boɔ so tee dodoɔ a ɛyɛ 1.7 percent wɔ US Dollar ho kɔsi Ɛbɔ 2021 awieeɛ, sɛ yɛde toto 2.9 percent a ɛso tee wɔ saa berɛ koro no ara mu wɔ afe 2020 ho a.

35 | Ghana Aban - 2022 Mmanfoɔ Boosɛt (Citizens' Budget)

“Adwumayɛfoɔ Ɔman a Ɛbɛtumi Agyina Ne Nan So Fapem Siesie: Sikasɛm Mu Nkɔsoɔ Ahyɛdeɛ ne Nnwuma Bɔ”

Nkyerɛaseɛ Nhoma (Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ Nsɛmfua)

1D1F (One District, One Factory): Mfididwumayɛbea ahodoɔ a wɔbɛhyehyɛ a ɛno nso bɛma ɔman no akɔ anim kɛseɛ wɔ mfididwuma mu. Ne botaeɛ ne sɛ ɛbɛbɔ adwuma pii ama Ghanafoɔ wɔ ɔman no afanan nyinaa.

Akontabuo: Wei ne berɛ a Aban bu n'akontaa kyerɛ ne manfoɔ denam ne dwumadie a ɔda no adi ne nsunsuansoɔ a efiri saa nneyɛeɛ no mu ba a ɔpa ho ntoma no so. Wei betumi aka asɛdeɛ a ɛfa sika anaa nnoɔma foforɔ a Aban kura mu ma ɔmanfoɔ no ho.

Nkyekyɛmu: Ɔkwan a wɔfa so kyɛ nneɛma mu ma aban adwumakuo ne asoɛeɛ ahodoɔ.

Ahobrɛaseɛ/Teetee: Yei ne kwan a wɔfa so ma asɛm bi ano brɛ ase anaa ɛso tew.

Budget: Budget yɛ nhyehyɛe a ɛkyerɛ baabi a wobɛnya sika afiri ne deɛ wobɛsɛe no wɔ so. Sɛ ebia, abusua bi bɛtumi ayɛ budget a ɛbɛkyerɛ sika dodoɔ a ɛwɔ hɔ ne sɛnea ɛsɛ sɛ wɔde di kan yɛ nnoɔma ho adwuma (sɛ ebia, ofie ka, nsuo ne anyinam ahoɔden ka, ne aduane). Sɛ sika foforɔ ho behia a, abusua no bɛhia sɛ wɔdwene sika foforɔ a wɔbɛnya anaa bosea a wɔbɛbɔ ho.

Budget deficit: Yei si berɛ a Aban sika a ɔsɛe no dɔɔso sene sika a ɔnya no.

Capital Expenditure: Yei ne sika a wɔsɛe no mpuntudwuma akɛseɛ te sɛ akwan, sukuu, ayaresie, kwan-ntwene, akwantuo, nsuo nhyehyɛeɛ, afidie ne nnoɔma a wɔde yɛ adwuma ne deɛ ɛkeka ho so.

Adwumayɛfoɔ Akatua: Wei yɛ akatua ne mfasoɔ foforɔ a ɛbata akatua ho te sɛ asetenamu banbɔ (social security), anigyedeɛ sika (gratuities) ne ahomegyesika (pensions) a wɔtua ma aban adwumayɛfoɔ.

Development Partners (DPs): Wɔn ne aman ne/anaa ahyehyɛdeɛ a wɔne aman a wɔn nsa aka nkyɛn bom yɛ adwuma sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a wɔbɛtumi adu wɔn mpuntuo botaeɛ ho. DPs bɛtumi ayɛ multilateral, e.g. International Monetary Fund (IMF), World Bank, African Development Bank (AfDB) anaa bilateral a ɛyɛ aman mmaako mmaako te sɛ Germany, United Kingdom ne United States of America (USA).

Afe Awiee Nneɛma Bo Kɔsoro: Nneɛma bo kɔsoro gyinapɛn a ɛba wɔ afe a yɛrehwɛ no Ɔpɛnimba bosome no mu.

36 | Ghana Aban - 2022 Ɔmanmma Sika Kɔkɔɔkɔ nhyehyɛe

“Adwumayɛfoɔ Ɔman a Ɛbɛtumi Agyina Ne Nan So Fapem Siesie: Sikasɛm Mu Nkɔsoɔ Ahyɛdeɛ ne Nnwuma Bɔ”

Sika a Wɔbɔ Boro So: Yei si berɛ a Aban sika a ɔsɛeɛ boro Sika Nhyehyɛeɛ a Mmarahyɛbadwafoɔ agye atom no so.

FREE SHS: Free SHS (Free Senior High School) nhyehyɛeɛ no yɛ Ghana Aban nhyehyɛeɛ a Ɔmanpanyin Nana Akufo-Addo amammuo no de baeɛ wɔ Ɛbɔ 2017 a ɛma JHS suani biara a ne nyansapɛ firi mu kwan ma ɔtumi kɔ SHS adesua a wontua hwee.

Grants (Mmoa sika): Yei yɛ sika mmoa a nkɔsoɔ nkabomfoɔ de ma Aban a ɛnhia sɛ Aban no tua ka no biara bio.

Mmoa a Wɔde Ma Aban Nkorabata Afoforo: Eyinom yɛ akatua a mmara hwehwɛ sɛ wɔtua te sɛ District Assemblies’ Common Fund (DACF), Road Fund, Ghana Education Trust Fund (GETFUND), Petroleum Related Fund ne National Health Fund.

Gross Domestic Product (GDP): Eyi kyerɛ nneɛma ne nnwuma a wɔayɛ wɔ ɔman no mu nyinaa boɔ a wɔaboaboa ano wɔ berɛ pɔtee bi mu.

Gross International Reserves: Weyi susuw ɔman bi amannɔne sika tumi a ɛtumi de tɔ nnoɔma ne ɔsom a ɛfiri amannɔne aman so wɔ berɛ bi mu; ɛsan nso boa ma ɔman no ankasa sika no yɛ den berɛ a wɔde toto aman foforɔ sika ho no.

Inflation: Nnoɔma ne ɔsom a wɔde ma ho bo a ɛkɔ soro mpofirim wɔ berɛ pɔtee bi mu.

IPEP (Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme): Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme (IPEP) yɛ NPP Aban no dwumadie atitire no mu baako a ne botaeɛ ne sɛ ɛbɛtu ohia ase na ama nsononsoɔ a ɛda nkurɔfoɔ ntam no so ate, titire no wɔ nkuraase ne mmeammea a wonni bi no mu denam nkɔsoɔ nneɛma atitire a wɔde bɛma no so.

Macroeconomic botaeɛ ahodoɔ: Yei nom yɛ nsusuho a yɛde hwɛ sɛdeɛ sikasɛm no rekɔ so yie anaa ɛnkɔ yie. Nhwɛsoɔ bi ne sikasɛm mu nkɔsoɔ, nnoɔma boɔ kɔ soro (inflation), nsakraeɛ a ɛba adwumayɛ gyinabea mu, amannɔnefoɔ ntam dwadie gyinabea, aban sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ nkonimdi anaa nkogu, ne nsiho a Bank of Ghana yɛ a ɛka sika pɛ ne ne dwetiri a ɛwɔ sikasɛm no mu.

37 | Ghana Aban - 2022 Manfoɔ Boosɛt

“Adwumayɛfoɔ Ɔman a Ɛbɛtumi Agyina Ne Nan So Fapem Siesie: Sikasɛm Mu Nkɔsoɔ Ahyɛdeɛ ne Nnwuma Bɔ”

MSME (Micro And Small Buisness Developmet Programme): Mpɛn pii no, wɔkyerɛ adwuma ketewa ase sɛ adwuma ketewa bi a ɛfa nnipa kakraa bi pɛ adwumamu, na ɛwɔ sika a ɛkɔ ba a ɛnnu baabi a wɔahyɛ da akyerɛ

NABCo (Nation Builders Corps): a wɔsan nso frɛ no NABCO yɛ nhyehyɛe bi a Ghana Aban de bae sɛ wɔde bɛma suapɔn mu agyidifo a wonni adwuma no adwuma de adi asetena mu nsɛnnennen ahorow ho dwuma wɔ ankorankoro ne aban nnwuma mu, na afei nso wɔasi wɔn nimdeɛ so dua de ama sikasɛm mu nkɔso aba ɔman no mu.

NBSSI (National Board For Small And Medium Scale Industry): National Board for Small Scale Industries (NBSSI) yɛ aban adwumakuo a ɛnhwehwɛ mfasoɔ a ɛhyɛ Ministry of Trade and Industry ase, na wɔama wɔn tumi sɛ wɔnhwɛ mma Nketewa ne Nketewa Kɛseɛ Nnwuma (MSEs) nnya nkɔsoɔ na ɛntrɛ.

Non-Oil Real GDP: Yɛ ade nyinaa a sikasɛm no tumi yɛ a ngo dwumadie nka ho, a wɔasiesie no sɛdeɛ ɛbɛfa nneɛma boɔ a ɛkɔ soro no ho.

Sika a Ɛmfa Toɔ So Mba: Aban sika a ɛmfiri toɔ mu, ebi ne sika a wotua de gye tumi krataa, dwumadie ho akatua ne ɛho aka foforɔ biara.

Asɛdeɛ a Aka a Wɔntuaeɛ: Asɛdeɛ a aka a wɔntuaeɛ no ka MDA nkabom a wɔne Ministry of Finance wɔ no ho, ne afe 2016 akatua a aka a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔtua ma Statutory Funds nso ho.

Primary balance : Yei ne nsonsonoeɛ a ɛda Aban sika a ɛkɔ mu ne ne sika a ɛfiri mu a ɛnka mfasoo ho (ɛnka ka a wɔtua de gye wɔn ho no ho). Yebitumi asusu yei sɛ Gross Domestic Product (GDP) ɔha nkyekyɛmu biako.

Mpuntuo A Wɔahyehyɛ Sɛ Ɛbɛba: Sɛnea Ghana sikasɛm bɛkɔ anim wɔ afe baako mu.

Sika a ɛba: Yei ne sika nyinaa a Aban nsa ka de yɛ n’adwuma a ɛfiri ɔman no mu ne amannɔne nyinaa.

RFJ (Rearing For Food and Jobs): bɛma mmoayɛn adwuma a akansie wom na etu mpɔn kɛseɛ anya nkɔsoɔ, a ɛbɛma ɔman no mu nnoɔma a wɔyɛ anya nkɔanim, ate mmoayɛn mu nnoɔma a wɔde firi amannɔne ba no so, aboa ama adwumayɛ anya nkɔanim, na ahyɛ mmoayɛn nhyehyɛeɛ mu nnwumayɛfoɔ abrabɔ den”.

38 | Ghana Aban - 2022 Ɔmanmma Pɛnkyɛn Bi / Nkɔsoɔ Pɛnkyɛn

Asetena mu Mfasoɔ: Aban tua mmoa sika ma anyinam ahoɔden ne nsuo nnwumakuo ne ngo nneɛma so ma ɔmanfoɔ.
