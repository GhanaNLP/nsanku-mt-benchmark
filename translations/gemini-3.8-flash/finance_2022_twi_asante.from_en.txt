ANIMUONYAM NSƐM

COVID-19 yarebɔne no sɛeɛ a ɛde baeɛ no aka sikasɛm nyinaa, a Ghana nso ka ho. Ɔhaw a Ghanafoɔ faa mu denam nkwa a ɛfiri mu, nneyɛeɛ a ɛbɔeɛ ne adwumayɛbea ahodoɔ a ɛguieɛ no nyɛ biribi a Aban no werɛ afiri. Ɛyɛ me ahantan sɛ mɛka sɛ yɛn anammɔntu a yɛde dii yarebɔne yi nsunsuansoɔ ho dwuma no yɛɛ kɛseɛ na ɛmu yɛ den, na ewiase nyinaa ayi no ayɛ. Aban no nso hyɛɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ ase de boaa adwumayɛbea ahodoɔ ma ɛsɔre bio na ɛde yɛn abrabɔ san kɔɔ baabi a na ɛwɔ kan no.

Bio nso, deɛ ɛbɛyɛ na yɛatumi adi asiane ne akwanya ahodoɔ a ɛfirii yareɛ kɛseɛ no mu baeɛ no ho dwuma no, Aban, wɔ Ɔbenfo H.E. Ɔmampanyin Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo akwankyerɛ pa ase, yɛɛ COVID-19 Alleviation and Revitalisation of Enterprises nhyehyɛeɛ a ne boɔ yɛ GH¢100 billion ntɛm pa ara, (a ɛne GhanaCARES “Obaatan Pa” nhyehyɛeɛ no). Yei de ahotɔ a ɛba ntɛm na ɛso aba yɛn manfoɔ so na ɛresan ahyɛ yɛn man den biom.

Yɛda mo ase sɛ moakɔ so anya gyidi wɔ Aban mu bere mpo a yɛreyɛ adwuma sɛ yɛbɛma ahotɔ a emu yɛ den aba bere a yarebɔn no twaa mu yi. Saa bere yi nyinaa mu no, yɛn ahoɔden sɛ yɛbɛtumi atena ase sɛ ɔman no anya sɔhwɛ. Bere nyinaa Bible ma akwankyerɛ a ɛho hia wɔ mmere a ɛte sɛ eyi mu. Ne titiriw no, sɛdeɛ wɔaka wɔ Yesaia 9:10 no, “Ntayaa no abubu, nanso yɛde aboɔ a wɔasen bɛsi; wɔatwa adɔtema no agu, nanso yɛde nsedo bɛsi ananmu”. Saa kwan no ara so no, Aban nso si ne bo sɛ ɛbɛhyɛ ne bɔhyɛ ahorow no ma. Ɛno nti, yɛbɛkɔ so adi nhyehyɛe ne dwumadi ahorow a ɛbɛsan de nneɛma a ahwere nyinaa aba na ama ɔmanfoɔ asetena mu ayɛ yie no so.

Botae titiriw a ɛwɔ 2022 Sikakora Nhyehyɛe no mu baako, na nokwarem no ma mfe mmiɛnsa (3) a ɛreba no, ne sɛ yɛbɛsi Adwumayɛfo Ɔman a ɛbɛtumi atim na atumi ama sikasɛm mu nkɔso a ɛyɛ den, a ɛbɛtumi atim, na ɛka obiara ho na ama adwuma pa aba ama obiara, titiriw Mmabun no. Enti, Aban nhyehyɛe ahorow bɛfa kwan a ɛbɛboa mmabun no ma wɔde wɔn akyɛde adi dwuma na wɔapue wɔn ahoɔden adi de ahwɛ ahu sɛ yiedie bɛba denam hokwan pɛpɛɛpɛ a wɔbɛnya ama obiara so.

Sɛnea ɛbɛhyɛ mmerante ne mmabaa no nkuran na ahyɛ wɔn adwumayɛ honhom den no, Aban bɛhyɛ YouStart dwumadi no ase wɔ 2022 mu de ama mmerante ne mmabaa a wɔyɛ nnwuma anya sika bɔbone de atim anaa atrɛw wɔn nnwuma mu.

Wɔ afe 2022 Sikakora Nhyehyɛeɛ no mu no, yɛatwerɛ nhyehyɛeɛ a yɛde bɛkɔ so adi sikasɛm mu ahokyere a ɛwɔ hɔ seesei ara no ho dwuma, na yɛasan agyina pintinn wɔ yarebɔn no akyi berɛ yi mu. Aban de n'adwene asi nneyɛe a ɛkɔ akyiri na ɛbɔ mu yiye so sɛ ɔbɛma mmabun nsa ano nimdeɛ na watoto nnwuma a wɔn ankasa bɛbɔ ho kwan ama wɔn. Berɛ a yɛredi Ɔmampanyin no

NKYERƐKYERƐMU AHYƐASEƐ

1. 2022 Citizen's Budget no yɛ nea Aban no nyae wɔ 2021 mu ne sika dodow a Aban no bɔ ne tirim sɛ obenya afi tow ne nneɛma foforo mu de adi ne nhyehyɛe ahorow so wɔ 2022 ne mfe kakra a edi hɔ no mu no ntwaho.

2. Sɛ yɛbɛboa asetra-adze nyinaa yiedie san-hyɛase mmɔdemmɔ ne ɔman mpuntuo botaeɛ ahorow no nti, afe 2022 Sikakyerɛ no si Dwumadie a Ɛkɔ so Daa Ɔman a Adwumayɛfoɔ Wɔ Mu sii so denam Sika Nhyehyɛeɛ a Ɛtim Hɔ ne Nnwuma Bɔ so.

SIKASƐM HO NKƆSOƆ WƆ 2021 MU

Sikasɛm Mu Nsunsuansoɔ Kɛseɛ

3. Nsɛm a ɛfa sikasɛm no nkɔsoɔ ho wɔ afe 2021 mu a yɛnyaeɛ no na ɛdidi soɔ yi:

• Ne nyinaa mu no, real GDP kɔɔ soro bɛyɛɛ ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 3.5 wɔ afe 2021 fa a ɛdi kan no mu sɛ yɛde toto ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 0.7 nkɔsoɔ a ɛbaa saa berɛ korɔ no ara mu wɔ afe 2020 ho a;

• Non-Oil real GDP nyinii bɛyɛ 4.8 percent wɔ afe 2021 fa a ɛdi kan no mu sɛ yɛde toto 1.1 percent a ɛbaa saa berɛ korɔ no ara mu wɔ 2020 ho a;

• Nnoɔma boɔ kɔ soro wɔ Ɛbɔ awieeɛ no kɔduruu ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 10.6, sɛ yɛde toto ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 10.4 a ɛbaa saa berɛ pɔtee no ara mu wɔ afe 2020 mu no ho a;

• Budget deficit (cash) nyinaa yɛ 7.7 percent wɔ GDP mu a ɛne botaeɛ a ɛyɛ 7.4 percent wɔ GDP mu no bɔ abira;

• Primary balance kyerɛɛ deficit a ɛyɛ GDP ɔha mu nkyekyem 2.0, sɛ yɛde toto deficit a na wɔahyɛ da ayɛ a ɛyɛ ɔha mu nkyekyem 1.7 ho a;

• Ɔman no aka a ɛda hɔ no (sɛ yɛde toto GDP ho a) kɔduruu ɔha mu nkyekyem 77.7 wɔ Ɛbɔ 2021 awieeɛ, a ɛkɔɔ soro firii ɔha mu nkyekyem 76.1 wɔ Ɔpɛnimba 2020 awieeɛ;

• Gross International Reserves wɔ Ɔsanaa 2021 awieeɛ no yɛ US$11.44 billion a ɛbɛtumi atua nnoɔma a yɛde ba ɔman no mu asram 5.2 ka, sɛ yɛde toto US$8.62 billion a ɛne nnoɔma a yɛde ba ɔman no mu asram 4.1 yɛ pɛ a ɛbaa Ɔpɛnimba 2020 awieeɛ no ho a; na

• Sika nsesaeɛ gyinapɛn kɔɔ so timii wɔ amanaman ntam sika akɛseɛ no nyinaa mu wɔ afe 2021 nkyem anan mu mmiɛnsa no mu. Ne titire koraa no, Ghana cedi so tee dodoɔ a ɛyɛ ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 1.7 tiaa US Dollar kɔsii Ɛbɔ 2021 awieeɛ, sɛ yɛde toto ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 2.9 a ɛtwee fam wɔ saa berɛ koro no ara mu wɔ 2020 ho a.

Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛe Botae a Ɛwɔ Hɔ Ma 2022

9. Wɔahyehyɛ sikasɛm mu botaeɛ a ɛdidi soɔ yi ama afe 2022:

• Real GDP nyin nyinaa bɛyɛ ɔha biara mu nkyekyɛmu 5.8;

• Non-Oil Real GDP nkɔsoɔ a ɛyɛ 5.9 percent;

• Ɔpɛnima awieeɛ nneɛma boɔ kɔ soro bɛyɛ ɔha nkyekyɛmu 8;

• Sikakorabea kɛseɛ no aka a ɛtɔ so 7.4 percent wɔ GDP mu;

• GDP mu ɔha biara nkyekyɛm 0.1 mfaso a ɛdi kan; na

• Gross International Reserves a ɛbɛtumi atua nnoɔma a yɛde ba ɔman yi mu no bɛboro abosome 4.

Nneɛma a Wɔboaboa Ano ma 2022

10. Sika a Wɔboaboa Ano ne Mmoa Nyinaa ma 2022 no, wɔbu akontaa sɛ ɛbɛyɛ GH¢100.52 billion (GDP no mu ɔha nkyekyɛm 20.0). Ɔman no Mu Sika a Wɔboaboa Ano no, wɔbu akontaa sɛ ɛbɛyɛ GH¢99.55 billion. Ɔman no Mu Sika a Wɔboaboa Ano nyinaa no mu no, Tow Sika a Ɛmfiri Ango Mu yɛ ɔha nkyekyɛm 77.5 na ɛsi GH¢77.136 million. Wɔhwɛ kwan sɛ nneɛma a wɔde boaboa sika ano ma 2022 no bɛnya nkɛntɛnsoɔ afiri tow a wɔtua no yie kɛse, nsakraeɛ a wɔde ba sika a wɔboaboa ano sohwɛ mu, ne tow nhyehyɛeɛ akwan ahodoɔ nso mu.

Adwumayɛfoɔ Ɔman a Ɛbɛtumi Agyina Ne Nan So Fomfom Si: Sikasɛm Mu Nhyehyɛe Pa ne Adwumayɛ Ntetee Si

Nsɛmfua Asekyerɛ (Sikasɛm Nsɛmfua)

1D1F (One District, One Factory): Nnwumakuo akɛseɛ ne nketewa a wɔresi a ɛbɛma ɔman no anya nkɔsoɔ kɛseɛ wɔ mfididwuma mu. Ne botaeɛ ne sɛ ɛbɛbɔ adwuma pii ama Ghanafoɔ wɔ ɔman no afanan nyinaa.

Akontaabuo: Eyi ne berɛ a Aban no bɔ ne manfoɔ amanneɛ fa ne nneyɛeɛ ho na ɔda nsunsuansoɔ a efiri saa nneyɛeɛ no mu ba adi kyerɛ wɔn. Eyi bi ne asɛdeɛ a ɛda ne so fa sika anaa nneɛma foforɔ a Aban no kura mu ma manfoɔ no ho.

Allocation: Ɔkwan a wɔfa so kyɛ nneɛma ma aban asoɛe ne mpanimfoɔ adwumayɛbea ahodoɔ.

Ahotɔ: Yei ne kwan a wɔfa so ma asɛm bi so te anaasɛ ɛnyɛ den dodo.

Budget: Budget yɛ nhyehyɛe a ɛkyerɛ baabi a sika bɛfiri ne deɛ yɛde bɛyɛ. Sɛ ebia, abusua bi bɛtumi ayɛ nhyehyɛe a ɛbɛkyerɛ sika dodow a ɛwɔ hɔ ne sɛnea ɛsɛ sɛ wɔde sika no di nneɛma ho dwuma sɛnea ɛho hia teɛ (sɛ ebia, ofie gyeɛ, nsuo ne anyinam kanea ho ka, ne aduane). Sɛ sika pii ho behia a, ɛbɛhia sɛ abusua no dwen akwan a wɔbɛfa so anya sika foforo anaa wɔbɛbɔ bosea ho.

Budget deficit: Yei ba berɛ a Aban sika a ɔsɛe no dɔɔso sene sika a ɔnya no.

Agyapadeɛ Ho Ka (Capital Expenditure): Yei yɛ sika a wɔsɛe no wɔ mpuntuo nnwuma akɛseɛ ho te sɛ akwan, sukuu ahoroɔ, ayaresabea ahoroɔ, twene ahoroɔ, akwantu, nsuo nhyehyɛeɛ, afidie ne nneɛma a wɔde yɛ adwuma ne deɛ ɛkeka ho.

Adwumayɛfoɔ Akatua: Wei gyina hɔ ma akatua ne mfasoɔ ahodoɔ a ɛbata akatua ho te sɛ social security, gratuities ne ahomegyekatua a wɔtua ma aban adwumayɛfoɔ.

Nkɔsoɔ Akyitaafoɔ (DPs): Yei kyerɛ aman ne/anaa ahyehyɛdeɛ a wɔne aman a wɔn nkɔsoɔ kɔ akyiri yɛ adwuma bɔ mu sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wɔbɛtumi adu wɔn nkɔsoɔ botaeɛ ho. DPs bɛtumi ayɛ ekuo a aman dodoɔ bi wom, te sɛ International Monetary Fund (IMF), World Bank, African Development Bank (AfDB) anaa aman mmienu ntam ayɔnkofa a ɛyɛ aman mmaako mmaako te sɛ Germany, United Kingdom ne United States of America (USA).

Afe Awieeɛ Nneɛma Boɔ Kɔsoro: Nneɛma boɔ kɔsoro a ɛsii wɔ afe a yɛreka ho asɛm no Ɔpɛnimaa bosome no mu.

Sika A Wɔsɛee No Boro So: Yei si berɛ a Aban sika a ɔsɛee no boro sika dodoɔ a Mmarahyɛbadwafoɔ no penee so wɔ Budget no mu.

FREE SHS: Free SHS (Free Senior High School) nhyehyɛeɛ no yɛ Ghana Aban nhyehyɛeɛ a Ɔmanpanyin Nana Akuffo-Addo aban de baeɛ wɔ Ɛbɔ 2017 a ɛma JHS osuani biara a ne nyansapɛ fata kwan sɛ ɔkɔ SHS adesua a wontua hwee.

Grants (Mmoa sika): Yei yɛ sika fam mmoa ahodoɔ a nkɔsoɔ akwankyerɛfoɔ de ma Aban a ɛnhia sɛ Aban no tua ka no bio.

Mmoa a Wɔde Ma Aban Nkorabata Nkae: Wei yɛ akatua ahodoɔ a mmara hwehwɛ sɛ wɔtua te sɛ District Assemblies’ Common Fund (DACF), Road Fund, Ghana Education Trust Fund (GETFUND), Petroleum Related Fund ne National Health Fund.

Gross Domestic Product (GDP): Eyi kyerɛ nneɛma ne nnwuma a wɔayɛ wɔ ɔman no mu nyinaa boɔ a wɔaboaboa ano wɔ berɛ pɔtee bi mu.

Gross International Reserves: Yei susuw ɔman bi amannɔne sika tumi a ɛtumi de tɔ nnoɔma ne ɔsom a efi amannɔne aman mu wɔ berɛ bi mu; ɛsan nso boa ma mpɔtam hɔ sika no tumi yɛ den wɔ aman foforɔ sika ho.

Nnoɔma boɔ kɔ soro (Inflation): Sɛdeɛ nnoɔma ne ɔsom a wɔde ma boɔ kɔ soro berɛ bi mu.

IPEP (Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme): Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme (IPEP) yɛ NPP Aban no dwumadie akɛseɛ no mu baako a ne botaeɛ ne sɛ ɛbɛtutu ohia ase na ate ntɔkwaw ne nsonsohyeɛ so, titire wɔ nkuraaseɛ ne mpɔtam a ahokyere wom denam nnoɔma titire a ɛho hia a wɔde bɛma so.

Macroeconomic targets: Yeinom yɛ nsusuiɛ a wɔde hwɛ sɛnea sikasɛm no rekɔ so. Ebi ne sikasɛm mu nkɔsoɔ, nneɛma boɔ a ɛkɔ soro (inflation), nsakraeɛ a ɛba adwumayɛ gyinabea mu, dwadie a ɛkɔ so wɔ aman foforɔ ntam, aban no sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛdi nkunim anaa ɛtɔ sin, ne gyinaeɛ a Bank of Ghana si a ɛka sika a wɔhwehwɛ ne nea ɛwɔ hɔ wɔ sikasɛm no mu.

NBSSI (National Board For Small And Medium Scale Industry): The National Board for Small Scale Industries (NBSSI) yɛ aban adwumakuo a ɛnhwehwɛ mfasoɔ a ɛhyɛ Ministry of Trade and Industry ase, na wɔama wɔn tumi sɛ wɔnhwɛ mma Nketewa ne Nketenkete Nnwuma (MSEs) nnyini na ɛntu mpɔn.

Non-Oil Real GDP: Ɛyɛ sika kɛseɛ a sikasɛm no nyinaa nya a ngo dwumadie nka ho, a wɔasiesie no sɛdeɛ ɛbɛfa nneɛma boɔ a ɛkɔ soro no ho.

Non Tax Revenue: Aban sika a ɛmfiri toɔgyeeɛ mu, nhwɛsoɔ ne sika a wɔtua de gye tumikrataa anaa laeseense, sika a obi tua de yɛ adeɛ bi ne tow afoforɔ.

Asodi a Aka: Asodi a aka no bi ne MDA nhyehyɛe a ɛne Ministry of Finance wɔ hɔ ne 2016 akatua a aka a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔtua kɔ Statutory Funds mu no.

Primary balance : Woi ne nsonsonoeɛ a ɛda Aban sika a ɛkɔ mu ne ne sika a ɛfiri mu a ɛnka nsiho ho (a ɛnyɛ ɛka a wɔtua de gye wɔn ho firi ka mu). Yɛtumi suso woi sɛ ɔha nkyekyɛmu wɔ Gross Domestic Product (GDP) mu.

Mpuntuo a Wɔhwɛ Kwan Sɛ Ɛbɛba: Sɛnea Ghana sikasɛm bɛkɔ anim wɔ afe baako mu.

Sika a Ɛba: Yei ne sika dodoɔ a Aban nsa ka firi ɔman no mu ne amannɔne nyinaa de yɛ n'adwuma.

RFJ (Rearing For Food and Jobs): bɛma mmoayɛn adwuma a akansi wom na etu mpɔn kɛse aba, a ɛbɛma ɔman no mu nsohwehwɛ adɔɔso, ate mmoa nnoɔma a yɛde firi amannɔne ba no so, aboa ama adwumayɛ aba, na ama mmoayɛn nhyehyɛeɛ mu nnwumayɛfoɔ asetena atu mpɔn”.
