Daabiligu Tiŋgbani Din Mali Kpabsigu Meebu: Laɣ'gbubsi Kpaŋsibu mini Tumakpaŋsibu Nucheche

TOONI YƐLTƆƔA

COVID-19 dɔro ŋɔ barina din gari yɛlimaŋli maa daa gbaaya tiŋgbana zaa daabiligu, kpalinkpaa Ghana. Nyɛvuli kalinli din daa bɔrigi, biɛhigu n-ti pahi daabiligu din daa saɣim daa chɛmi ka Ghana nima nya biɛrim pam, ka gɔmnanti mi lala yɛla ŋɔ zaa viɛnyɛla. N nyɛvuli pɛla pam ni n yɛli ni ti daa kpaŋ ti maŋa kpe lala dɔro maa barina maa ni ni yɛda din mali yaa, ka andunia zaa gba daa ti ti paɣibu lala ŋɔ zuɣu. Gɔmnanti daa lahi kpa zalisi ni di sɔŋsi labisi daabiligu zali ka ti biɛhigu labina di zaashee.

Pahi pahi, domin dabiɛhi mini laɣimtum din daa yina doro maa polo maa zuɣu, Gɔmnanti, H.E. Tingbani Zuɣulan Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo toontali suŋ maa puuni, daa yom n-kpa GH¢100 billion COVID-19 Alleviation and Revitalisation of Enterprises bɔmma yɛligu lahabali, (GhanaCARES “Obaatan Pa” bɔmma yɛligu). Di tahi kpaŋmaŋ mini tiɛhi din bara n-ti ti niriba ka kpaŋsiri ti tingbani maa.

Ti paɣiya ya ni yi ni zali yɛmbahiga ni Gɔmnanti pam n-ti pahi ti tuma din nyɛ ni ti kpaŋmaŋa kpaŋsi ti bɛhigu labina polo doro ŋɔ nyaaŋa. Di saha ŋɔ puuni, ti kpaŋmaŋa din yɛn chɛ ka ti tiligi ka nyɛ tiŋgbani nyɛla bɛ ni dahim shɛli. Baabulu saha kam tiri la soli din tuhi lala saha ŋɔ. Di bahi bahindi, kamani bɛ ni buɣisi shɛli Isaiah 9:10 puuni, “Yɛnshiŋa ŋɔ lu ya mi, amaa ti ni labi m-mɛ ni kuɣu viɛla; bɛ ŋmaai la do' viɛla n-luhi, amaa ti ni zaŋ seeda tihi n-zali di zaani”. Lala n-nyɛ li, Gɔmnanti gba kuli mali la niya din kpeeni ni o pali o daalikauli nima. Di zuɣu, ti ni tuɣi n-kpaŋsi soya mini tuma nima din kpaŋsiri labi mali binshɛɣu kam din bɔrgi, ka lahi chɛ ka tiŋgbani bihi ʒii bɛhigu viɛla.

2022 Budget ŋɔ nia kpani yini, ka lahi nyɛ yuma ata (3) din kani na maa dini, nyɛmi ni ti me daabiligu tiŋgbani din na yɛn kuuka n-tooi tahi daabiligu kpaŋmaŋa na din mali yaa, din yɛn kuuka, ka kpiɣi sokam pahi n-nam tuma suŋ ti sokam, di bahi bahindi bipola. Dinzuɣu, gɔmnanti sodoligu yɛn dalimla bipola sɔŋsim polo ka bɛ tooi zaŋ bɛ baŋsim tum tuma ka bahi bɛ kpaŋmaŋa palo ni di kpaŋsihili doli zaɣisuma din saɣi sokam zaa puuni.

Zaŋ chaŋ bi’pola kpaŋmaŋa kpaŋsibu mini bɛ daabilim kpaŋmaŋa kpaŋsibu polo, Gɔmnanti ni kpa YouStart tuunzurigu yuuni 2022 n-ti bi’pola ban be daabilim puuni samli soli ni bɛ tooi kpa bee n-yaligi bɛ daabilim.

Yuuni 2022 Liɣiri Salinli puuni, ti teela so’shɛŋa ti ni yɛn doli n-tuɣi tuhi tɔɣano nolini yɛlimuɣisira zaŋ chaŋ daabiligu polo, ka lan yi di puuni n-kpɛŋ dɔli nyaaŋa dor’bambɔɣu maa nyaaŋa. Gɔmnanti tahi o hankali zaa n-ti kpaŋmaŋa din paai polo ka lahi doya viɛnyɛla ni di ti baŋsim ka lahi kpaŋsi daabiligu binyɛra bipola puuni. Saha shɛli ti ni doli Tiŋgbani Zuɣulana maa

Daabiligu tuma yɛligu zaŋ ti 2021

Macroeconomic Tum Tuma

3. Saha ŋɔ lahabali din wuhiri ariziki daabiligu tuma yuuni 2021 puuni n-nyɛ ŋɔ:

• Di zaa liɣiri daa (real GDP) daa paai vaabu 3.5 kɔbigi puuni 2021 pirigili tuuli maa ni, ka di nyɛla vaabu 0.7 kɔbigi puuni n-daa nyɛ di biɛhigu lala saha maa 2020 puuni;

• Non-Oil real GDP daa zoomi n-chaŋ average 4.8 percent yuuni 2021 pirigili tuuli maa ni n-zaŋ maɣisi 1.1 percent lala saha maa yuuni 2020;

• Nima daa duri di ni daa niŋ ka Silimin-gɔli September bahigu daa nyɛla 10.6 kɔbigu puuni, n-zaŋ maɣisi 10.4 kɔbigu puuni saha yini maa yaha yuuni 2020 puuni;

• Yɛlimuɣisirili din be liɣiri ku n-zani tariga (laɣ'kpalinkpaa) puuni nyɛla 7.7 kɔbigu puuni zaŋ chaŋ GDP polo ka di zani ti nini ni daa kpa shɛli din nyɛ 7.4 kɔbigu puuni zaŋ chaŋ GDP;

• Primary balance daa nya la deficit din yiɣisi vaabu 2.0 kɔbigi puuni zaŋ chaŋ GDP polo, ka di lee daa ŋmali deficit din daa bɔri ni di nya la din daa nyɛ vaabu 1.7 kɔbigi puuni;

• Salo samli maa (GDP kɔbigu puuni vaabu) daa paai la vaabu 77.7 kɔbigu puuni silimin gɔli September 2021 bahigu, ka di daa du n-yiri vaabu 76.1 kɔbigu puuni silimin gɔli December 2020 bahigu;

• Gross International Reserves August 2021 bahigu daa nyɛla US$11.44 billion, ka daa ni tooi lihi n-ti chira 5.2 binyɛra nambu n-kpe tiŋa ŋɔ ni, zaŋ maɣisi US$8.62 billion din daa zani n-ti chira 4.1 binyɛra nambu n-kpe tiŋa ŋɔ ni ka bɛ daa kpaŋsi li December 2020 bahigu; mini

• Liɣiri taɣibu dariza daa zani viɛnyɛla andunia liɣiri nima pam puuni yuuni 2021 pirigili dibaa ata maa ni. Di mini di kuli niŋ lala maa zaa yoli, Ghana cedi daa sigi tiŋgbani ni kɔbigi puuni vaabu 1.7 zaŋ chaŋ US Dollar polo silimin gɔli September 2021 bahigu, ka di lee nyɛla kɔbigi puuni vaabu 2.9 n-daa sigi tiŋgbani ni lala saha maa yaha yuuni 2020 puuni.

LIIƔARI ZALISI SOYA N-TI YUUNI 2022 LAƔIRI LAƔINGU KUBU

Yɔri Deebu Zalisi Naba Zani

14. Gɔmnanti ni zali so' shɛŋa din yɛn labi kpaŋsi tuma duri ka chɛ ka bɛ nɛma mali kpaɣiri bɔmma ti maŋmaŋa ni tiŋduya dahi puuni. Din zuɣu, di pa nyɛla din kpa talahi pam ni bɛ niŋ zalisi taɣibu ŋɔ:

• Dini yɛn kpaŋsi nyɔri din paai ka yaligi tuma tabibi daabilim ŋɔ puuni, Gɔmnanti ni ti soli n-kpaŋsi Vat Relief din daa zaŋ kpa African textile nima polo n-ti textile maankpamba yuuni 2019 la pahi yuma ayi;

• Yuma ayi ni pirigili tuma nyaaŋa, sahazi-ŋɔ benchmark (filimbu) zaligu zaŋ chaŋ dapaale kpemina polo din daa yina ni di tum saha biela tuma la yɛn labi mi vihi viɛnyɛla domin di paagi di niya n-gari lala;

• Withholding tax kalinli baligibu zaŋ ti salima din na ʒi kohibu n-ti ban gbiri salima bɛla bɛla ban nyɛ ban yori lala farigu ŋɔ yi vaabu 3 percent n-kpa vaabu 1.5 percent;

Gɔmnanti tuma yɛlikpana polo zaŋ ti salo (Accountability): Dini n-nyɛ Gɔmnanti yi nyɛ ŋun tumdi ka buɣisiri o tuma n-wuhiri o tiŋgbani bihi ka baŋsiri ba o tuun-tuma maa ni yi doli shɛm. Pirigibu (Allocation): Di nyɛla sɔhibi' shɛli bɛ ni doli n-tɔhiri bee n-tariti aʒia nima ti gɔmnanti yaɣa mini o tuma du' zuɣuri.

Baligibu: Di nyɛla yɛlli biɛɣu baligibu soli.

Liɣi kooto: Liɣi kooto nyɛla laasabu shɛli din wuhiri luɣishɛli a ni yɛn nya laɣifu mini a ni yɛn zaŋ li n-niŋ shɛli. Kamani, dɔɣiriba ni tooi maali laasabu din yɛn wuhi laɣi shɛli din be bɛ nuu mini bɛ ni yɛn zaŋ li ku binshɛŋa zuɣu tuuli (kamani, duu liyili, konyurim mini buɣim, n-ti pahi bindirigu). Di yi niŋ ka laɣi bɔbigu n-kpaŋsi, dɔɣiriba maa ni bɔ soli n-laɣisi laɣi paala bee n-deei samli.

Liɣi kalinli kalinsi: Di niŋdila gomnanti ninsalisi yɔbu yi gari o laɣa kpeeri.

Daabiligu Laɣidi Dihigu: Ŋɔ nyɛla laɣ'shɛli bɛ ni dihi n-zali tuun' kara kamani palli mɛbu, shikuriti, ashibitinima, kobilisi, binkpɛra, kom soya, tihi mini maashinnima n-ti pahi n-gari lala.

Tumtumdiba Yɔri: Di nyɛla yɔri mini yɔri-yɛlitɔɣa sɔŋsim kɔŋkɔba kamani social security, gratuities n-ti pahi pensions shɛŋa bɛ ni yɔri gɔmnanti tumtumdiba.

Leebiŋsim Zoodoli nima (DPs): Ŋɔ wuhirila tiŋgbana mini/bee laɣinsi din laɣindi tumanima ni tiŋgbana ban na bɔri leebiŋsim ni bɛ paai bɛ leebiŋsim nia nima. DPs ni tooi nyɛ laɣinsi din gbaai tiŋgbana pam, kamani International Monetary Fund (IMF), World Bank, African Development Bank (AfDB) bee tiŋgbana ayi sunsuuni din nyɛ tiŋgbani yini yini kamani Germany, United Kingdom n-ti pahi United States of America (USA).

Yuuni Kpalinkpaa Dɔgiti Tibaazi: Dɔgiti tibaazi tariga din daa nyɛli Disɛmba goli puuni lala yuuni maa polo.

Liɣiri Dibu Din Gari Yiko: Ŋɔ niŋdami yi ti niŋ ka Gɔmnanti liɣiri dibu gari Liɣiri Tarisi shɛli Parliament ni saɣi ti.

FREE SHS: Free SHS (Free Senior High School) zaligu nyɛla Ghana Gɔmnanti noli kpeeni din daa pili tiŋgbani zuɣulana Nana Akuffo-Addo gɔmnanti tali saha Silimin gɔli September 2017 puuni din tiri sokam soli ka o bɔhimdi SHS baŋsim yoli, di yi niŋ ka bia maa nyɛla ŋun paasi o JHS viɛnyɛla.

Sɔŋsim laɣifu: Ŋɔ nyɛla sɔŋsim laɣi balishɛli lɛbiginsim kpalinkpaaba ni tiri gɔmnanti ka di pala gɔmnanti yɛn yo li mi n-labisi.

Sɔŋsim n-ti Gɔmnanti Tumaduri Zaɣ’ shɛŋa: Ŋɔ nim n-nyɛ yɔri shɛŋa zaligu ni bɔri shɛli kamani District Assemblies’ Common Fund (DACF), Road Fund, Ghana Education Trust Fund (GETFUND), Petroleum Related Fund mini National Health Fund.

Gross Domestic Product (GDP): Ŋɔ wuhirila tiŋgbani maa ni nɛma mini tuma din zaa pili ka naai daachiɛɣu kɔligu zaa yi laɣim taba saha shɛli din gahim puuni.

Gross International Reserves: Dinŋɔ buɣisirila tiŋgbani gɔmnanti laɣi-gɔhi yiko ni bɛ da nɛma mini bɔmma tiŋgbana din pahi ni saha shɛli puuni; di lahi kpaŋsirila tiŋgbani maa maŋmaŋa laɣi-baligu yaa n-zaŋ ŋmahim tiŋgbana din pahi laɣi-balisa.

Inflation: Nima mini tuma daa durilim tariga saha shɛli puuni.

IPEP (Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme): The Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme (IPEP) nyɛla NPP gɔmnanti maa tuuntumsa kpani yini din nia nyɛ ni di tuhi kari fara ka baligi fukumsi mini nambɔzɔrigu, di bahi bahindi tiŋkpaŋsi mini yaɣi shɛŋa din bi nya tɔɣisi puuni doli la bɛ ni yɛn kpaŋsi bɛhigu tɔɣisibu bɔmma n-ti ba.

Macroeconomic targets: Ŋɔŋɔ nyɛla binshɛŋa bɛ ni mali n-zahindi daabiligu polo zoobu mini di biɛhigu. Di shɛhira n-nyɛ economic growth, inflation, tuma kpuɣibu kalinli taɣibu, daabiligu ni tiŋduya, gɔmnanti daabiligu zalisi naba zani bee di chandi kalinsi, n-ti pahi Bank of Ghana shawara gbaabu shɛŋa din tahiri laɣ' bɔri mini laɣ' tahi gbaŋgbani na daabiligu puuni.

MSME (Micro And Small Buisness Developmet Programme): Daabiligu bila nyɛla daabiligu shɛli bɛ ni mi ka di mali tumtumdiba bɛla, ka di laɣidiligu bee laɣimmaŋa pɔri gari laɣ'shɛli din zali.

NABCo (Nation Builders Corps): ka bɛ lahi mi li NABCO nyɛla kpaŋmaŋ' soya din yi Ghana Gɔmnanti sani na ni di ti karinzɔŋ kara shikuru bihi ban naai ka ka tuma tumanima, domin nyɛ yɛlimuɣisira balibu din be ʒingama mini gɔmnanti tuma duri puuni maligu, ka lahi kpaŋsi bɛ baŋsim din yɛn duhi tiŋgbani maa daabiligu n-chaŋ tooni.

NBSSI (National Board For Small And Medium Scale Industry): The National Board for Small Scale Industries (NBSSI) nyɛla gomnanti tuma du’ shɛli din ka nyɔri bɔbu n-ti gɔmnanti ka be Ministry of Trade and Industry sulinsi ni, ka bɛ yiko nyɛla ni bɛ kpaŋsi Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) zoobu mini bɛ tooni chandi.

Non-Oil Real GDP: Nyɛla daabiligu puuni binshɛŋa zaa bɛ ni yihi na din bi kpa kpam yaɣili polo, ka lahi zaŋ daa dabilim biɛhigu n-lasi li.

Liɣi shɛli din pala Farigu: Gɔmnanti liɣi shɛli din bi yiri farigu puuni na, shɛhira n-nyɛ soli chɛbisi bee lisinisi nima yɔri, ban mali binshɛli n-kuri bukaata yɔri mini yɔri shɛŋa gba.

Samani Din Na Kpalim: Samani din na kpalim ŋɔ nyɛla MDA alikawlenima din be Ministry of Finance sani n-ti pahi yuuni 2016 samani din na kpalim ka bi yo n-ti Statutory Funds.

Primary balance : Di nyɛla waliginsim din be Gɔmnanti laɣi nyɛbu mini o laɣi di din bi pahi kɔbigu puuni (ka chɛ Samli yoobu tuma). Di ni tooi zaŋ n-ŋmahim ka di nyɛ kɔbigi puuni vaabu zaŋ chaŋ Gross Domestic Product (GDP).

Tɛhigu zaŋ chaŋ zoobu tariga: Ghana daabilim ni yɛn zoori shɛm yuuni puuni.

Revenue: Ŋɔ nyɛla liɣiri zaa kalinli gbaŋ din kpe gɔmnanti noli ni n-ti o tuma, ka di nyɛla din yina tiŋgbani maa puuni mini tiŋgbani duu polo zaa.

RFJ (Rearing For Food and Jobs): ni kpaŋsi binkɔb’ gulibo daabilim din mali kpaŋmaŋa ka tirisi tooni, din yɛn kpaŋsi ti maŋmaŋa binkɔb’ gulibo, ka baligi binkɔbiri mini bɛ binbɔra zaŋ kpe ti tiŋgbani ni na, ka sɔŋsi n-nam tuma, ka lahi tooni tiŋgbani binkɔb’ daabilim puuni nima biɛhigu.
