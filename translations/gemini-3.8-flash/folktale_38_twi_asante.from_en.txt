Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka yi yɛ nokware)

MPANYINFO SE, "SƐ ƐYƐ WO BUSUANI OO, ANAA WO KUNU OO, ANAA OBIARA KORAA A ƆWƆ ADWUMA BI YƐ, SƐ ƆSRƐ WO A, BOA NO"

ANANSE kyerɛɛ Aso (ne yere) sɛ, "Ma yɛnkɔyɛ afuo." Aso nso see sɛ, "Merenkɔ." Ananse kɔe kɔdɔɔ afuo no. Bere a ɔsan baeɛ no, ɔkaa kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nokorɛ ampa sɛ worenhɔ (ammoboa me)?" Aso buae sɛ, "Ɔbaa kyiri asaase do." Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɔkyena ma yɛnkɔhyew afuo mu nwura no." Aso kaa sɛ, "Ɔbaa kyiri afuo mu nwura hyew, na me deɛ, menkɔ na ogya nkɔhye me anaa?" Enti Ananse kɔeɛ na ɔsan baeɛ. Adekyeeɛ a adeɛ bɛyɛɛ hann no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Aso, mepa wo kyɛw, ma yɛnkɔ na yɛnkɔdua nnoɔma." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mekaae sɛ merenkɔ." Ananse kaa sɛ, "Mahu mmusuo." Afei ɔkɔe kɔduaa biribiara kama. Na wɔwɔ hɔ no, nnɔbaeɛ no bae. Da koro bi Aso kɔɔ afuom hɔ. Ɔbae bɛka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, "Kwaku, afifide no anyin wɔ afuom hɔ, na da bɛn na yɛbɛkɔ hɔ (akɔtwa)? " Ananse see sɛ, "Ho! deɛ Nkramoni barima no kaeɛ no, mente aseɛ koraa." Aso kaa sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn ne no; kasa ma me nte wo aseɛ." Ananse kaa sɛ, "Merekɔ na masan aba." Ananse kɔɔ afuo no mu hɔ, na ɔgyinaa hɔ akyɛ, na ɔkaa (kyerɛɛ ne ho) sɛ, "Me, Kwaku Ananse, medeɛ mawie, na mɛtena ase ama saa obi a ɔyɛ ɔbaa kɛkɛ yi agye me agorɔ anaa? Ananse faa sekan, akuma, ne asɔ, na ɔtutu amena kɛse pa ara. Ɔfaa afifide a ɛwɔ afum hɔ nyinaa de guu mu, na ɔsane kɔɔ fie. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Aso, bra ha, na menkã asɛm no nkyerɛ wo." Aso bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mekɔɔ Kramoni bi nkyɛn kɔbisaa m'adesua; ɔse aka nnaawɔtwe pɛ na mawu, enti merenkɔ afum bio, na wo nso worekɔ hɔ bio, na sɛ mewu a, ɛhɔ na mobɛkɔ akɔsie me. Matu amoa kɛseɛ bi wɔ hɔ, na emu na mobɛsie me." Nnaawɔtwe no duruu soɔ no; Ananse yɛɛ ne ho sɛ wawu. Nnipa no maa Ananse so kɔsiee no wɔ n'afuo no mu hɔ. Wɔsane baa fie bɛyɛɛ ayie fɛfɛɛfɛ bi. Aso nso yɛɛ kunayɛ ho amanneɛ no nyinaa. Berɛ a wɔreyɛ nnawɔtwe ayi no, Aso kaa sɛ, "A, ma menkɔhwɛ me kunu afuo no mu." Aso kɔeɛ; na afuo no da hɔ dadwa! Aso kaa sɛ, "O, afuo a me kunu yɛeɛ a na m'ani da so (sɛ ɛbɛma me aduane) no, nkurɔfoɔ bi aba abɛwe adeɛ a ɛwɔ mu nyinaa." Ɛnna ɔsu mee na ɔdomee wɔn, kaa sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse saman, w'afumudeɛ a wogyaw maa me nyinaa, nkurɔfoɔ aba abɛwe."

Kwaku Ananse maa ne nne so toe:

Gyaa (wiinwiin no)! Me ara me deɛ na madi no!

Gyae (nwinwi), Aso kyiri wura do.

Gyae (nwiinwii no), medeɛ pɛ na madi!

Gyae (nwunwuna), Aso kyiri (afifide a ɛwɔ turo no mu) hyeɛ. Gyae (nwunwuna), medeɛ na madi no pɛ!

Gyae (nwiinwii), Aso kyiri dua.

Gyae (nwinwi), medeɛ na madiɛ ara pɛ!

Ɛnna Aso gyinaa hɔ dinn, na ne ho popoeɛ. Ɔkɔɔ fie kɔfrɛɛ Adanko sɛ ɔmmra mmɛtie nsɛm bi a ɛwɔ ne kunu afuom hɔ. Ɔne Adanko tuu kwan. Aso duruu afuom hɔ no, ɔmaa ne nne so bio suee sɛ:

Me kunu ne Kwaku Ananse, na ɔwuiɛ gyaa n'afuo maa me, na nkorɔfoɔ baeɛ bɛdii emu aduane no nyinaa.

Kwaku Ananse Suman, mebɔ me nkotodwe ma wo, mesrɛ wo, Kum wɔn ma me."

Kwaku Ananse ma ne nne so:

Gyae (nwunwuna), deɛ ɛyɛ me dea nko ara na madi!

Gyae (nwinwi), Aso kyiri wura do.

Gyae (nwiinwii no), medeɛ pɛ na madi!

Gyae (nwiinwii), Aso kyiri (afifide a ɛwɔ afuo mu no) hyeɛ.

Gyae, me deɛ pɛ na madi!

Gyae (nwiinwii), Aso kyiri dua.

Gyae, medi m'ara me deɛ; gyae!

Ɛfiri sɛ Ɔbɔadeɛ bɔɔ adeɛ no, woahunu ɔbaa a ɔnmoa ne kunu pɛn?

Gyae (nwiinwii), m'ara me deɛ na madi; gyae (nwiinwii)!"

Adanko no kaa sɛ, "Mawie atie, ma yɛnkɔ fie na menka nea ɛfa ho nyinaa nkyerɛ wo." Ɔno ne Aso kɔɔ fie. Adanko kaa sɛ, "Ananse kaa sɛ, ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ, 'Yɛnkɔ nkɔdɔ afum'; wo kae sɛ wonkɔ; 'Yɛnkɔ nkɔhye (wira a awo no)'; na wo kae sɛ wonkɔ; 'Yɛnkɔ nkɔdua adeɛ'; na wo kae sɛ wonkɔ; enti gyae osuo, efisɛ ɔredie deɛ ɛyɛ ne dea ara pɛ, efisɛ, ɛfiri berɛ a Ɔbɔadeɛ bɔɔ adeɛ, ɔnhuu ɔbaa biara a wammoa ne kunu da; enti gyae awerɛhowdi ng (ɔse), efisɛ wadi nea ɛyɛ ne dea nyinaa pɛ." Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Sɛ obiara biara yɛ adwuma bi na sɛ ɔbisa wo mmoa a, boa no."

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
