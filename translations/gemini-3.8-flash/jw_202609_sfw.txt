"Let the Peace of Christ Rule in Your Hearts"

Colossians 3:15 speaks about the “peace of Christ.” When it says the “peace of Christ,” what does it mean? When someone becomes a disciple of Jesus and comes to realize that Jehovah and his Son love him, it gives him inner calm. That is what is called the “peace of Christ.”—John 14:23, 27.

Paul told the Colossians to let that peace rule in their lives. What did he mean by that? In Greek, when they say someone rules over something, it is often used to refer to someone who acts as a referee in sports. When people play football, it is the referee who directs how the game should proceed. Likewise, in that same way, we must let the "peace of Christ" direct how we live our lives.

Consider this scenario. Suppose a problem arises between you and a brother. What will you do to let the “peace of the Christ” act as an “umpire” or direct you in what you do? You might not do what you yourself want, but rather you will do something that shows that you love peace. If you are able to do that, you will take your mind off the pain you are suffering, and focus your mind on the peace that will exist between you. Acting this way shows that you let the “peace of the Christ” control your life.—Col. 3:12-14.
