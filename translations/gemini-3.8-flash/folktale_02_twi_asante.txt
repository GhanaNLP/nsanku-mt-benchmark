Confusion and Disorder

MPITIRIPI and Mpatrapa, and Anyankama Kyerebi, and Kyerebi-Koso-Adawurampon, went and found thirty palm trees. And they said, “No one should touch the wine-dregs to lick them.” But Father Spider went and touched the wine-dregs and licked them, and they said, “Take and touch it.”

Ananse said, "What debt do I owe?" And they said, "You owe a debt because Mpitiripi and Mpatrapa and Anyankama Kyerebi and Kyerebi-Koso-Adawurampon went and tapped thirty palm trees, and they said nobody should taste the dregs of the palm wine, but you tasted the dregs of the palm wine, and that is why they say, 'Pay the debt'." And they fell upon Ananse, and they beat him, and he jumped, and entered into the ceiling kado!

So it is that, the day you will see Spider sitting in a boat, left to him, he would have been walking on the ground like Crab.

This tale of mine that I have told, whether it is sweet or not sweet, take some away and bring some back to me.
