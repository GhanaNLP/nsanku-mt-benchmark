Kigbinge nna e lii bee ba wese nɛ e kpenya kawol kii nɛ e bi nyiŋe anyi kaye kigbɔŋwule dondo nna ere to. Nɛ e bee lii dumu nɛ kaye kamana gba bee lii na, COVID-19 nlowi kumo nna ka kulu anyi nɛ kigbinge 2020 kaseŋtɔ kapeletɔ to, nɛ kumo na kpee nɛ kigbɔŋwule kumu kusowe pɛyɛ, nɛ kumo ya kpee nɛ anyi kigbɔŋ to alimbia kumo tɔr, nɛ anyi ya kpe alimbia wulo mɔ nɛ anyi maa so kumo kashito to gba bee lii nlowi kumo nna e lii e kanɛ to.

Kamo kumo lara kumo mɔ Russia-Ukraine war, nɛ a-nyɛ delay nɛ bumu ba pasa key revenue measures nɛ 2022 budget tɔ, nɛ kumo lara credit rating downgrades. Firifiri, nɛ afi adu tɔ, International Capital Market e-shishi anɛ so, domestic financing conditions e-wɔ nɛ a-lɛɛ ashi borrowing kuso, kumo tɔ debt service e-durɔ nɛ cedi e-lile nɛ fɛfɛɛ.
