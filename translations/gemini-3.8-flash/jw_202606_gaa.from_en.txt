JWƐŊMƆ HE KPO Wɔbaanyɛ wɔye Yehowa anɔkwa kɛmɔ shi ekɔɔɔ he eko hekaai ni kã wɔhemɔkɛyeli lɛ hiɛ ni wɔbaakpe kɛ amɛ.

“Nuŋtsɔ, namɔ ŋɔɔ wɔbaaya? Bo lɛ oyɛ naanɔ wala wiemɔi.”—YOHANE 6:68.

"Wiemɔ nɛɛ wa tsɔ; namɔ baanyɛ abo toi?" (Yohane 6:60) Bɔ ni Yesu kaselɔi lɛ ateŋ mɛi pii fee amɛnii yɛ wiemɔi ni Yesu wie beni etsɔɔ nɔɔ yɛ Kapernaum lɛ he nɛ. Yesu wie akɛ: "Kɛji nyɛyeee Gbɔmɔ Bi lɛ heloo ni nyɛnuuu elá lɛ, nyɛbɛ wala yɛ nyɛmli." Kɛkɛ ni etee nɔ akɛ: "Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni yeɔ miheloo ni enuɔ milá lɛ yɛ naanɔ wala, ni matee lɛ shi yɛ naagbee gbi lɛ nɔ."—Yohane 6:53, 54.

2 Mɛi ni Yesu wiemɔi lɛ tɔ̃ amɛhe aahu lɛ kpa enɔ nyiɛmɔ. Ani amɛbɛ hemɔkɛyeli ko kwraa yɛ emli? Yɛ anɔkwale mli lɛ, dani enɛ baaba lɛ, mɛi komɛi—ni Yesu kaselɔi lɛ fata he—na nɔ ni Yesu fee lɛ he miishɛɛ aahu akɛ amɛkɛɛ: “Lɛ ji Gbalɔ lɛ ni baaba je lɛ mli lɛ lɛɛlɛŋ.” (Yohane 6:14) No hewɔ lɛ, eyɛ faŋŋ akɛ nakai kaselɔi lɛ yɛ hemɔkɛyeli fioo ko yɛ Yesu mli. Shi beni aka amɛhemɔkɛyeli lɛ akwɛ lɛ, amɛyeee lɛ anɔkwa dɔŋŋ.

3 Bɔfoi lɛ susu Yesu wiemɔi lɛ ahe yɛ gbɛ srɔto nɔ. Beni Yesu bi amɛ kɛji amɛ hu amɛmiisumɔ ni amɛshi lɛ lɛ, Petro ha hetoo akɛ: “Nuŋtsɔ, namɔ ŋɔɔ wɔbaaya?

Wɔhe wɔye ni wɔbana hu akɛ, bo ji Nyɔŋmɔ Krɔŋkrɔŋ Nɔ lɛ.” (Yohane 6:68, 69) Kadimɔ akɛ Petro ewieee akɛ lɛ kɛ bɔfoi krokomɛi lɛ nu nɔ ni Yesu wie be ni ekɛɛ amɛ akɛ ehe miihia ni amɛye eheloo ni amɛnu elá lɛ shishi kɛmɔ shi. Bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni amɛshishinumɔ yɔɔ nakai be lɛ mli lɛ, amɛhala akɛ amɛbaaya nɔ amɛye Yesu anɔkwa. Mɛni hewɔ? Ejaakɛ nɔ ni anaa pɔtɛɛ kɛ nɔkpɛlɛmɔ ko he amɛhehemɔkɛyeli lɛ damɔ—Yesu ji “Nyɔŋmɔ Kristo lɛ.”​—Luka 9:20, 35.

⁴ Abaanyɛ aka wɔ-anɔkwayeli kɛha Yehowa lɛ akwɛ yɛ gbɛi pii anɔ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, te wɔfeɔ wɔnii wɔhãa tɛŋŋ kɛ́ wɔnu akɛ atsɔɔ Biblia mli ŋmalo ko mli yɛ gbɛ kroko nɔ, aloo kɛ́ atsake gbɛ nɔ ni ato kaselɔfeemɔ nitsumɔ lɛ he gbɛjianɔ ahã lɛ? Aloo te wɔfeɔ wɔnii wɔhãa tɛŋŋ kɛ́ ewa kɛhã wɔ akɛ wɔbaanu nɔ hewɔ ni Yehowa ŋmɛɔ gbɛ ni amanehulu ko baa wɔnɔ lɛ shishi? Agbɛnɛ kɛ́ Kristofonyo naanyo kpaakpa ko tɔ̃ wɔnɔ, aloo mɔ ko ni wɔbuaa lɛ he lɛ kɛ wɔ ye yɛ gbɛ ni ejaaa nɔ lɛ, ebaanyɛ eka wɔ-hemɔkɛyeli lɛ ekwɛ.

⁵ Bɔni afee ni wɔna nɔ hewɔ ni ehe hiaa ni wɔhemɔkɛyeli lɛ ahiɛ shishitoo kpeteŋkpele lɛ, feemɔ tsu ko ni ama yɛ he ko ni shikpɔŋ nɔkpokpɔmɔ fɔɔ nɔfeemɔ yɛ jɛmɛ lɛ he mfoniri. Ebiɔ ni tsu nɛkɛ ahiɛ shishitoo kpeteŋkpele koni ekpa fite. Nakai nɔŋŋ kɛji wɔmiisumɔ ni wɔnyɛ wɔdamɔ hemɔkɛyeli he kaai tamɔ nɔ ni atsɔɔ mli yɛ kuku ni tsɔ hiɛ lɛ mli lɛ naa lɛ, ehe miihia ni wɔhemɔkɛyeli lɛ ahiɛ shishitoo ni mli wa. Kɛji wɔtee nɔ wɔwo wɔhemɔkɛyeli lɛ shishitoo lɛ mli hewalɛ lɛ, wɔbaaya nɔ wɔma shi shiŋŋ kɛtsɔ anɔkwale ni wɔbaaye wɔha Yehowa yɛ kaai ahe nɔ.​—1 Kor. 15:58; kanemɔ Kolosebii 1:23.

ye Yesu anɔkwa beni mɛi krokomɛi babaoo kwa lɛ? Bɔfoi lɛ ná nɔmima akɛ Yehowa sa jamɔ ejaakɛ lɛ ji nibii fɛɛ a-Bɔlɔ; akɛ Yesu ji Nyɔŋmɔ Bi lɛ, mɔ ni yɔɔ “nɔŋkpamɔ wiemɔi ni kɛ mɔ yaa naanɔ wala mli”; kɛ agbɛnɛ akɛ Yehowa fiɔ Yesu sɛɛ kɛmɔɔ shi. Amrɔ nɛɛ, yɛ nɔ najiaŋ ni wɔbaasusu gbɛi pɔtɛi babaoo ni abaanyɛ atsɔ nɔ aka wɔhemɔkɛyeli lɛ he lɛ, nyɛhaa wɔkɛ wɔjwɛŋmɔ ama anɔkwalei etɛ ni baanyɛ aye abua wɔ koni wɔdamɔ shi shiŋŋ kɛlɛkɛlɛ kaa fɛɛ kaa ni wɔbaakpe kɛkpɛ he nɔ. Amɛji: (1) Yehowa ji nibii fɛɛ a-Bɔlɔ. (2) Yehowa tsɔ emumu lɛ nɔ eha aŋma Biblia lɛ. (3) Yehowa miifi ewebii lɛ asɛɛ ŋmɛnɛ. Kɛ́ wɔná nɔmima ni mli wa yɛ nɛkɛ anɔkwalei etɛ nɛɛ ahe lɛ, wɔbaaye anɔkwa kɛ́ wɔkpe hemɔkɛyeli kaai ahe.

YEHOWA JI BƆLƆ LƐ

7 Kanemɔ Kpojiemɔ 4:11. Kuku nɛɛ kaiɔ wɔ akɛ Yehowa bɔ nibii fɛɛ. Enɛ pɛ hewɔ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔjá lɛ. Kɛ́ wɔjwɛŋ ehewalɛ ni yɔɔ gbeyei lɛ nɔ lɛ, wɔheɔ wɔyeɔ kwraa akɛ ebaanyɛ ehere wɔ kɛjɛ wɔkaai fɛɛ amli. Kɛbɛ nakai lɛ, Satan eha lakapai eyi je nɛɛ mli tɔ koni gbɔmɛi ahe akaaye akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ yɛ. Saainslɛlɔi po baanyɛ akɛɛ akɛ wala ba kɛjɛ ehe pɛ kɛkɛ. Kɛ́ wɔheeee wɔyeee kwraa akɛ Yehowa ji Bɔlɔ lɛ ni esumɔɔ wɔ lɛ, ekolɛ wɔbaaje shishi wɔkɛkpe kɛji akɛ eyɛ, kɛ agbɛnɛ hu kɛji ebaaye ebua wɔ be ni wɔkɛ hemɔkɛyeli he kaai kpeɔ.—Heb. 11:6.

8 Kɛji obɔi nɔ he nifeemɔ akɛ Yehowa ji Bɔlɔ lɛ, fee nɔ ko amrɔ nɔŋŋ. Taomɔ saji babaoo yɛ wɔwoji lɛ amli kɛhã odase nɔŋŋ ni yɔɔ faŋŋ akɛ Yehowa bɔ nibii fɛɛ lɛ he sane. Obaanyɛ ona nibii pii ni woɔ hemɔkɛyeli hewalɛ yɛ sane nɛɛ he yɛ woji tɛtrɛbii ni ji Was Life Created? kɛ The Origin of Life—Five Questions

Ekolɛ okane wolo bibioo ni ji Ani Abɔ Wala? lɛ momo. Mɛni hewɔ okaneeko ekoŋŋ? (Kwɛmɔ kuku 8)

Esa akɛ Abi. Agbɛnɛ hu, awie odaseyeli krokomɛi ni fiɔ enɛ sɛɛ he yɛ saji ni akoloo naa ni ji “Ani Abɔ Lo?” ni yɔɔ jw.org nɔ lɛ mli. Jessica, nyɛminuu ko ni ji gbekɛ yoo yɛ United States lɛ kɛɛ: “Beni mikane Origin of Life wolo bibioo lɛ sɛɛ lɛ, miná nɔmimaa akɛ anɔkwalei lɛ tsɔɔ akɛ Bɔlɔ ni yɔɔ nilee yɛ. Mile enɛ momo, shi beni mikwɛ susumɔi ni yɔɔ wolo bibioo lɛ mli lɛ nɔ ekoŋŋ lɛ, miye mihe nɔŋŋ odase kɛteke nɔ ni amale ko bɛ mli.” Agbɛnɛ hu, mɛi pii ená sɛɛnamɔ kɛjɛ wolo ni ji Is There a Creator Who Cares About You? lɛ nɔkwɛmɔ mli. Ekolɛ okane nibii nɛɛ momo. Shi mɛni hewɔ osusuuu he akɛ obákane ekoŋŋ?

⁹ Kɛ́ oomihe ousuŋmɔ kaseemɔ nɔ kpokpo ni baanyiɛ sɛɛ lɛ, obaanyɛ osusu anɔkwalei ni tsɔɔ akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ yɛ lɛ ahe. Niiamlɛitaomɔ nɛɛ jeee nɔ ni baaye abua bo kɛkɛ ni owaje ohemɔkɛyeli yɛ Yehowa shihilɛ mli, shi moŋ ebaaha ole esui lɛ agbɛfaŋ nibii pii hu. (Rom. 1:20) Kɛfata he lɛ, obaanyɛ okɛ nɔ ni okase lɛ aye abua mɛi krokomɛi ni kɛ́ eba Nyɔŋmɔ shihilɛ he lɛ amɛyɛ ŋwɛi kɛ shikpɔŋ. 13 Kɛji eba akɛ wɔjei he koŋkɔɔ yɛ kɛji Yehowa Odasefoi ji gbɔmɛi ni Nyɔŋmɔ miifi sɛɛ ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, esaaa akɛ wɔŋmɛɔ gbɛ ni nakai hekoŋkɔɔ lɛ shweshweɔ yɛ wɔmli. Moŋ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔtsa nakai hekoŋkɔɔ lɛ kɛtsɔ sanebimɔi tamɔ nɛkɛ ni wɔbaabi wɔhe lɛ nɔ: ‘Namɛi leelee bɔɔ mɔdɛŋ fɛɛ ni amɛbaanyɛ koni amɛhi shi yɛ Biblia lɛ shishitoo mlai lɛ anaa? Namɛi buɔ Biblia lɛ akɛ nɔ ni yɔɔ hegbɛ yɛ saji fɛɛ amli?’ (2 Tim. 4:3, 4) ‘Namɛi miifee Yehowa suɔmɔnaa nii ŋmɛnɛ?’—Mat. 7:20, 21.

14 Kɛ́ akɛto Nyɔŋmɔ suɔmɔnaanii nifeemɔ he ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔbi wɔhe akɛ, ‘Namɛi feɔ shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ ni Yesu gba fɔ shi akɛ abaatsu yɛ naagbee gbii nɛɛ amli lɛ?’ (Mat. 24:14) Hetoo lɛ yɛ faŋŋ tsɔ. Nyɔŋmɔ miikɛ emɛi—Yehowa Odasefoi—miitsu shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ nɛɛ. Wɔ pɛ wɔshiɛɔ Nyɔŋmɔ Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ he sane kpakpa lɛ yɛ shikpɔŋmɛi ni fa fe 240 anɔ. Wɔmiitsu nitsumɔ nɛɛ fe afii 100 yɛ Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ nɔyeli shishi. Hɛɛ, wɔya nɔ wɔkɛ wɔjwɛŋmɔ ma wɔshiɛmɔ kɛ nitsɔɔmɔ nitsumɔ ni he hiaa fe fɛɛ lɛ nɔ.—Mat. 28:19, 20.

Wɔji jálɔi aweku ni sumɔɔ Yehowa kɛ Yesu lɛ fã, ni suɔmɔ nɛɛ baa wɔ kpɔŋ. (Yohane 13:35) Shi nakai Kristofoi anaanyobɔi lɛ ebaaa kɛkɛ. Amɛbiɔ mɔdɛŋbɔɔ. Ni tamɔ naanyobɔɔ fɛɛ naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ gbɔmɛi ni yeee emuu ateŋ lɛ, naanyobɔi nɛɛ baanyɛ akɛ naagbai aba.

4 Bɔni afee ni wɔwaje Kristofoi awekutsoo wekukpaa lɛ, esa akɛ wɔtao nɔ ni hi yɛ mɛi krokomɛi ahe. Be ni wɔkɛ wɔnyɛmimɛi hii kɛ yei baagba lɛ baanyɛ akai wɔ amɛsuban kpakpai lɛ. Bɔfo Paulo ná shidaamɔ ni ehiɛ kɛba e-nyɛmimɛi hii kɛ yei lɛ anɔ. Eŋma etee Tesalonikabii lɛ akɛ: “Wɔbɔ mɔdɛŋ fɛɛ bɔ ni wɔbaanyɛ koni wɔna nyɛ hiɛ kɛ hiɛ.” (1 Tes. 2:17) Mɛi pii ena akɛ nɔ ko nɔ ko bɛ ni tamɔ hiɛ kɛ hiɛ sanegbaa kɛha amɛnaanyobɔɔ lɛ wajɛmɔ. Wɔyɛ hegbɛi pii ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔtsɔ nɔ wɔfee enɛ be ni wɔyaa shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ mli kɛ be ni wɔboteɔ asafoŋ kpeei, kpokpaa nɔ kpeei, kɛ kpeei wuji amli feemɔ mli. Shi wɔbaanyɛ wɔna akɛ bɔ ni wɔkɛ wɔhe bɔɔ kɛkɛ lɛ tsiŋ naagbee kɛ ha naanyobɔɔ lɛ sanebimɔi ahe gbɛ.

5 Nyɔŋmɔ suɔmɔ sui baanyɛ aye abua wɔ koni wɔkɛbá naanyobɔi ni yɔɔ oshara mli lɛ yi. (Kanemɔ Kolosebii 3:12.) Kɛ́ wɔdɔle heshiba, toishitoo, mlihilɛ, kɛ mɔbɔnalɛ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔye naanyobɔi ni aahiɛ lɛ he naagbai lɛ anɔ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, tamɔ bɔ ni fɔ nu haa lɔle mli latsa tsuɔ nii jogbaŋŋ lɛ, nakai nɔŋŋ Nyɔŋmɔ suɔmɔ sui baatsi tawuu fɛɛ tawuu ni baanyɛ aba wɔ wekukpitiŋ lɛ naa. Agbɛnɛ, nyɛhaa wɔsusua naagbai etɛ ni baanyɛ afite naanyobɔi ni yɔɔ wɔ kɛ nanemɛi heyelilɔi ateŋ lɛ he. Be ni wɔfeɔ nakai lɛ, wɔbaagba bɔ ni Yesu heshiba, toishitoo, mlihilɛ, kɛ mɔbɔnalɛ ni wɔbaakase lɛ baaha wɔya nɔ wɔtsɔ anɔkwa naanyobɔi kɛha mɛi krokomɛi lɛ he sane.

10 Mɛni hewɔ ewa nakai? Ekolɛ ewa kɛha wɔ akɛ wɔ kɛ nyɛmimɛi hii kɛ yei ni amɛsuban kɛ wɔ nɔ etɔko lɛ baabɔ naanyo kpekpeemeh. Ekolɛ mɛi komɛi yɛ su ko ni tɔ̃ɔ wɔtsui nɔ. Ekolɛ mɛi krokomɛi leee bɔ ni akɛ mɛi bɔɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ, ejaakɛ aye amɛ awui yɛ be ko ni eho lɛ mli, loo ejaakɛ amɛjɛ kusum ko ni mɛi ni yɔɔ mli lɛ buɔ amɛhe nɔ, loo amɛkɛ amɛhe woɔ nibii amli tsɔ lɛ mli. Aloo ekolɛ wɔsuban kɛ bɔ ni wɔkɛ mɛi bɔɔ lɛ ekaaa kwraa—ekolɛ wɔ lɛ wɔkɛ mɛi bɔɔ jogbaŋŋ, shi amɛ lɛ amɛhiɛ gboɔ, loo ekolɛ wɔhiɛ gboɔ, shi amɛ lɛ amɛkɛ mɛi bɔɔ jogbaŋŋ.

11 Kaseɔ nii kɛjɛ Yesu toishitoo lɛ mli. Eyaa nɔ ehiɛɔ naanyobɔɔ kɛ mɛi ni amɛsuban srɔto kwraa yɛ ehe lɛ teŋ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, bɔfoi Yakobo kɛ Yohane jie hejɔɔmɔ su kpo beni amɛbi hegbɛi ni nɔ kwɔ yɛ Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ mli lɛ. (Mar. 10:35-37) Yɛ enɛ he lɛ, Yesu kɛ heshiba shi eŋwɛi hegbɛ kpele kɛ hegbɛi ni nɔ kwɔ lɛ kɛba shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ. (Fil. 2:5-8) Susumɔ bɔ ni amɛsu srɔto kɛteke nɔ lɛ he okwɛ. Kɛbɔ ni eji lɛ fɛɛ sɛɛ lɛ, Yesu to etsui shi eha Yakobo kɛ Yohane kɛ mɛi krokomɛi hu.

12 Yesu le nɔ ni kpaa gbɛ yɛ enanemɛi lɛ ahe. Ele akɛ Yakobo kɛ Yohane bimi ni he hiaa amɛ lɛ tsɔɔ mɛi pii ni yɔɔ kaselɔi lɛ ateŋ lɛ a-kɛ-mɛi-kpeemɔ mumɔ lɛ. (Marko 9:34) Yesu da yɛ jamɔ he nifeemɔ kome lɛ nɔŋŋ mli tamɔ amɛ. Ewo hegbɛ kɛ gbɛhe he nyam waa, no hewɔ lɛ enu amɛjwɛŋmɔ shishi. 13 Mɛni obaanyɛ ofee koni naanyobɔi ni okɛ mɛi yɔɔ lɛ ahi shi daa? Tsaa nɔ otsɔse toishitoo kɛtsɔ bɔ ni obaanyɛ ofee fɛɛ koni okɛ mɛi krokomɛi asui ni edɔɔ bo lɛ ahi shi lɛ nɔ. Ehe miihia hu ni wɔsusu nɔ ni yɔɔ mli kɛmɔ shi kɛtsɔ mɛi krokomɛi ahe nɔmima ni wɔbaaha lɛ nɔ. Nɔ kome mli lɛ, susumɔ mɔdɛŋbɔɔ ni ebiɔ dani mɔ ko ni feɔ dioo kɛ hiɛgbele lɛ kɛ mɛi krokomɛi abɔ ni ekɛ amɛ agba sane lɛ he. Nɔ kroko mli lɛ, bɔɔ mɔdɛŋ koni otsui anaa kpa mɔ ko ni he yɔɔ kãmɔ ni wieɔ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ faŋŋ lɛ he. Wɔnyɛŋ wɔkpa gbɛ akɛ wɔbaatsɔmɔ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ naanyo kpaakpa, shi ehe miihia ni wɔha ehi wɔjwɛŋmɔ mli akɛ Kristofoi ni amɛsui yɔɔ srɔto kwraa tete baanyɛ atsu nii kɛ hegbɛ kpakpa yɛ ekomefeemɔ mli.

Nyɛbaa Nyɛhe Nii Yɛ Bɔ Ni Nyɛboɔ Toi Lɛ He

“Nyɛkwɛa bɔ ni nyɛboɔ toi lɛ jogbaŋŋ.”—LUKA 8:18.

ŊMƐNƐ nɛɛ, Yehowa miiha wɔ mumɔŋ niyenii babaoo. Wɔbaanyɛ wɔkpɛlɛ nɔ̃ ni David lala lɛ nɔ jogbaŋŋ, nɔ̃ ni ekɛɛ: “Yehowa ji mi-Kwalɔ. Nɔ ko nɔ ko efeŋ mi.”—Kanemɔ Lala 23:1, 2, 5.

2 Gbɛ kome nɔ ni Yehowa kɛ mumɔŋ niyenii haa wɔ ŋmɛnɛ ji asafoŋ kpeei lɛ anɔ. Yɛ kpeei nɛɛ ashishi lɛ, bei pii lɛ wɔtiɔ nibii ni wɔle momo lɛ amli. Eyɛ mli akɛ “wɔtsɔ̃ hewale kɛte shi yɛ anɔkwale lɛ mli” moŋ, shi wɔhe miihia kaimɔi nɛɛ. (2 Pet. 1:12) Kɛbina lɛ, kɛ́ mumɔŋ nitsumɔi lɛ biɔ nibii ni akɛtsɔɔ nɔ shii abɔ lɛ, ani efeɔ gbɔhɔɔ kɛhaa bo akɛ obanuu toi jogbaŋŋ? Nikasemɔ nɛɛ baanyɛ aye abua bo. Wɔbaasusu nɔ hewɔ ni ehe hiaa ni wɔbo toi jogbaŋŋ yɛ kpeei ashishi lɛ he. Wɔbaana hu bɔ ni mɛi ni tsɔɔ nii yɛ asafo lɛ mli lɛ baanyɛ aye abua mɛi krokomɛi koni amɛbo toi. Ni wɔbaasusu shishipɔŋi komɛi ni baanyɛ aye abua wɔ koni wɔtsɔmɔ toiboɔlɔi kpakpai lɛ he. Shi klɛŋklɛŋ lɛ, nyɛhaa wɔsusua mɔ ni kɛ wɔ wieɔ lɛ he.

Kɛji mafee anɔkwale lɛ, shishijee lɛ, Betel sɔɔmɔ lɛ eŋɔɔɔ minaa kwraa. Mibɔ mɔdɛŋ kɛtsɔ afii pii amli yɛ gbɛfãa nitsumɔ lɛ mli aahu akɛ mijwɛŋmɔ kɛ mitsui fɛɛ yɛ shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ he. Ewa kɛha mi akɛ matara okpɔlɔ sɛɛ. Shi minanemɛi Betelbii lɛ kɛ titri lɛ miŋa Emilia, ye amɛbua mi ni matsake mijwɛŋmɔ. Be ni beee shishi lɛ, miná mi-miishɛɛ lɛ ekoŋŋ, ni amrɔ nɛɛ misɔmɔɔ kɛ miishɛɛ yɛ Betel.

Mi kɛ Emilia wɔbɔi gbɔlɔmɔ. Shi kɛ̃lɛ, kɛ́ wɔjwɛŋ nyɛmimɛi hii kɛ yei fɛɛ ni wɔná hegbɛ kɛle amɛ kɛ amɛ sɔmɔ afii babaoo nɛɛ ahe lɛ, ehaa wɔmii shɛɔ wɔhe naakpa. Ehaa wɔmii shɛɔ wɔhe titri kɛ́ wɔna akɛ wɔbii lɛ kɛ wɔnanabiimɛi lɛ miisɔmɔ Yehowa. Wɔnuɔ he tamɔ bɔ ni bɔfo Yohane ni ebɔi gbɔlɔmɔ lɛ nu he beni ekɛɛ: “Mináaa miishɛɛ ko ni fe nɛɛ: akɛ manu akɛ mibii lɛ miitee nɔ miinyiɛ yɛ anɔkwale lɛ mli.”​—3 Yohane 4.

Amrɔ nɛɛ kɛjɛ be ni wɔbɔi sɔɔmɔ yɛ Betel aahu kɛbɛkɛ afii 30 lɛ, mi kɛ Emilia bɔɔ mɔdɛŋ fɛɛ ni wɔbaanyɛ koni wɔtsu wɔgbɛnaa nii he nii eyakee nɔ fɛɛ nɔ gbi kansa kɛ gbɔlɛ kɛba wɔnɔ lɛ. Yɛ afii ni wɔkɛsɔmɔ Yehowa lɛ amli lɛ, wɔkɛ naagbeehi babaoo kpe, shi wɔmii shɛɔ ejaakɛ wɔnyɛ wɔsɔmɔ "miishɛɛ Nyɔŋmɔ" lɛ aahu kɛbɛkɛ afii 70 yɛ ŋshɔkpɔ nɛɛ nɔ yɛ Cuba!—1 Tim. 1:11; Lala 97:1.

SANEMƆJII KƐJƐ KANEELƆI ADƐŊ

Taakɛ akɛŋmɛɛ yɛ Galatabii 4:24 lɛ, kpaŋmɔi enyɔ nɛɛgbɛ he bɔfo Paulo wie?

◼ Be bɔfo Paulo wie Abraham kɛ Sara kɛ Hagar teŋ wekukpɔŋ lɛ he egbe naa lɛ, ekɛɛ: “Nibii nɛɛ ji nɔmɔ ko; ejaakɛ yei nɛɛ ji kpaŋmɔi enyɔ.” (Gal. 4:22-24) Tsutsu lɛ, anuɔ shishi akɛ kpaŋmɔi enyɔ nɛɛ ji Mla kpaŋmɔ lɛ kɛ kpaŋmɔ hee lɛ. Shi kɛlɛ, be ni akwɛ mli jogbaŋŋ ekoŋŋ lɛ, wɔheɔ wɔyeɔ akɛ ehe miihia ni atsake nɔ ko. Efeɔ nɔ ni Paulo wieɔ Abraham kpaŋmɔ lɛ, shi jeee kpaŋmɔ hee lɛ he. Hã wɔsusu saji komɛi ahe pɛpɛɛpɛ.*

Paulo kɛ mabilɔ yoo Hagar to Sinai Gɔŋ lɛ he. (Gal. 4:25) Hagar damɔ shi kɛha Mla kpaŋmɔ ni akɛha yɛ Sinai Gɔŋ lɛ nɔ yɛ afi 1513 B.C.E. lɛ. (2 Mo. 19:5, 6) Gbɔmɔ fɔŋ ko fɔŋ nyɛɛɛ aye nakai Mla lɛ nɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ, no hewɔ lɛ, ekae Yudafoi lɛ akɛ amɛji esha nyɔji. Nakai kpaŋmɔ lɛ baanyɛ aye abua Israel maŋ lɛ ni amɛyɔse Mesia lɛ, gbɔmɔ kome pɛ ni baanyɛ aye Mla lɛ nɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ lɛ. Be ni Mesia lɛ kɛ e-shihilɛ ni ewan ko bɛ he lɛ shã afɔle lɛ, ehã ebafeɔ nɔ ni baahi kɛha gbɔmɛi fɔji akɛ abaajie amɛ kɛjɛ esha kɛ gbele mli. (Gal. 3:19, 24, 25) Be ni Yesu tsu Nyɔŋmɔ yiŋtoo lɛ he nii lɛ, ehe ehiaaa Mla lɛ dɔŋŋ.—Rom. 10:4.

Paulo kɛ Hagar to Sara he, mɔ ni ji “yoo hefatalɔ” ni fɔ “kɛtsɔ shiwoo nɔ lɛ.” (Gal. 4:23) Shiwoo nɛɛ ji Abraham kpanŋmɔ lɛ. (Gal. 3:29; 4:28, 30) Akɛtsɔɔ mli kuku nɛɛ yɛ 1 Mose 22:18: “Oseshi lɛ [Abraham nɔ] nɔ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ maji fɛɛ baana jɔɔmɔ kɛha amɛhe, ejaakɛ obo mi gbee toi.”

* Enɛ tsɔɔ bɔ ni sa ni anaa nɔ ni akɛfɔ̃ The Watchtower, March 15, 2006, pp. 10-12 lɛ he gbɛ.

Kpaŋmɔ nɛɛ ni akɛ Abraham fee lɛ bafata shiwoo ni Yehowa kɛha yɛ Eden lɛ he. (1 Mo. 3:15) Etsɔɔ akɛ “seshi” ni agba efa lɛ baatsɔ Abraham seshinyo. Paulo tsɔɔ mli akɛ “seshi” nɛɛ kɔɔ Yesu Kristo he titri. Shi mɛi ni “yeɔ Kristo nɔ” lɛ, 144,000 gboshiniyelɔi ni kɛ Yesu baaye nɔ yɛ e-Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ mli lɛ, fata mfoniri feemɔ seshi nɛɛ he. (Gal. 3:16, 29; Kpo. 14:1-3) Nyɔŋmɔ shiwoo ni ekɛha Abraham lɛ mlibaa baaha jɔɔmɔi babaoo aba mɛi ni kpɛlɛɔ Yesu nɔ ni amɛboɔ lɛ toi lɛ anɔ.

Mɛni hewɔ Paulo kɛ nɛkɛ “nɔkwɛmɔnɔ nina” nɛɛ tsu nii? Etsɔɔ akɛ, kɛ́ Yudafoi ni yɔɔ e-beiaŋ lɛ mɔ Mla lɛ mli kpɛŋŋ lɛ, amɛbaaya nɔ amɛhi nyɔŋyeli mli tamɔ Hagar nɔŋŋ. Shi kɛ́ amɛkpɛlɛ Abraham “seshi” titri lɛ, Yesu Kristo nɔ lɛ, amɛbaaye amɛhe tamɔ Sara nɔŋŋ. Amɛbaaná heyeli krɔŋŋ kɛjɛ esha kɛ gbele mli. (Yohane 8:32-34) Kɛ́ amɛkpɛlɛɛɛ Yesu nɔ lɛ, amɛtɔ̃ Mla kpaŋmɔ lɛ oti titri lɛ he, nɔ ji, ni ekpãa amɛ kɛya Kristo lɛ ŋɔɔ.

Galatabii pii ni Paulo ŋma amɛ lɛ jeee Yudafoi ni amɛyeee Mla lɛ nɔ dã. Yudafoi komɛi kɛɛ kɛ̃ akɛ esa akɛ Jeŋmajiianbii ni batsɔmɔ Kristofoi lɛ aye Mla lɛ nɔ, ni kitsofeemɔ he mla lɛ fata he. Paulo susu akɛ ebaafeɛ bulɛ kwraa kɛji amɛkpɛlɛ Mla lɛ “nyɔŋyeli” lɛ nɔ, ejaakɛ amɛna “heyeli” ni yɔɔ Kristo mli lɛ momo.—Gal. 5:1, 10-14.

BIBLIA MLI ANEHOKUMO

Loofɔlɔi Anyaa yɛ Biblia Beiaŋ

Biblia kanelɔi baanyɛ akai akɛ Yesu sɛɛnyiɛlɔi lɛ ekomɛi ji wolei. (Marko 1:16-20) Kɛ́ amɛmiiya woyaa yɛ Galilea Ŋshɔ lɛ nɔ lɛ, amɛkɛ asɔbai tsuɔ nii.

Akɛ kpãa loo kpoji kɛbá afii kɛfee yaa lɛ mli hɔ̃ɔji lɛ kɛfee yaaji. Hɔ̃ɔji lɛ amli agbo kɛ bibioo damɔ loo nɔ́ nitsumɔ ni ahe ehia lɛ nɔ. Yaai bibii lɛ fɔɔ sumii loo tɛi ahe nitsumɔ bɔni afee ni amɛkpeleke shi, be ni yaai wujiwuji lɛ hu yɔɔ nibii ni tsii yɛ amɛshishi kɛ tso loo korukoo he nibii ni hielɔɔ nɔ yɛ amɛyiteŋ.

Wɔliayai jara wa. Tɛi ni yɔɔ ŋshɔ lɛ shishi lɛ baanyɛ afite amɛ, aloo loo babaoo kpele ko ni amɛmɔ lɛ baanyɛ atserɛ mli. (Luka 5:6) Biblia lɛ wie Yakobo kɛ Yohane he akɛ “amɛmiikpɛ amɛyai lɛ”—ekolɛ amɛkɛ abui kɛ kpaa ni akɛ kpoku kpaa efee lɛ tsu nii. (Mat. 4:21) Ekolɛ wɔlɔi lɛ kpɛɔ amɛyai lɛ be fɛɛ be ni amɛkɛbaatsu nii amɛgbe naa. Luka gba wɔlɔi ni “miifɔ amɛyai lɛ ahe” koni amɛjie nshɔshishi mɔlɔ loo nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni eyawo mli lɛ he sane hu. (Luka 5:2) No sɛɛ lɛ, wɔlɔi lɛ sɛŋɔ amɛyai lɛ koni amɛgbi—ekolɛ bɔ ni afee ni amɛkashwo.—Ezek. 47:10.

Yesu fɔ̃ hii ni ele yaa kɛtsuɔ nii lɛ nine koni amɛtsɔmɔ “gbɔmɛi ahe loolɔi.”

Akɛ wolo nɛɛ haaa koni ahɔɔ. Eji jeŋ muu fɛɛ Biblia kasemɔ nitsumɔ ni afeɔ kɛtsɔɔ suɔmɔ mli onai anɔ lɛ fã kome. Kɛ́ oomiisumɔ ni ofee onai lɛ, ofainɛ yaa donate.jw.org.
