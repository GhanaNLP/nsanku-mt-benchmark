EDWENE 97

Ngoane Gyina Nyamenle Edwɛkɛ Ne Zo

FA W’ADWENE SIE ƆZƆHUNU ZO

Kɛzi yɛbahola yɛahɔ zo yɛanyia sunsum nu aleɛ, tɛladeɛ nee dabidilɛ mɔɔ Gyihova fa maa yɛ la azo nvasoɛ la.

Kɔ Zo Ma Ɛ Nzonle Ɛlie Wɔ “Sunsum Nu Ngyianle” Ne Anwo

YƐ ƐVOLƐ 2026 NGYƐNU NE:

Anyelielɛ a le menli mɔɔ ze kɛ bɛhyia sunsum nu ninyɛne nwo la

—MAT. 5:3.

GYIHOVA ebɔ sonla kɛ ɔnyia ninyɛne ngyenlazo bie mɔ anwo hyia. Kɛ neazo la, amaa yɛali ngoane la, yɛhyia aleɛ, tɛladeɛ, nee ɛleka mɔɔ yɛbadɛnla a. Saa ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ anu ko bɔbɔ ka yɛ, bɔbɔ wɔ mekɛ ekyi anu a, ɛbɛlabɔlɛ yɛ ngyegyeleɛ. Mɔɔ boka nwonane afoa nu ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ mɔɔ yɛhyia nwo mɔɔ Gyihova bɔle yɛ la anwo la, Gyihova eza bɔle yɛ kɛ yɛnyia sunsum nu ninyɛne nwo hyia. (Kenga Mateyu 5:3.) Saa yɛkulo kɛ yɛkɔ zo yɛyɛ anyelielɛ kpalɛ a, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛnwu hyia mɔɔ yɛhyia nwolɛ la na akee yɛkɔ zo yɛdi nwolɛ gyima.

2 Kɛzi ɔkile kɛ yɛbanwu yɛ “sunsum nu ngyianle” ɛ? Mɔlebɛbo aneɛ nu edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ ne kile sonla mɔɔ kpondɛ sunsum ne kɛ sɛlɛvolɛ la. Zɔhane edwɛkɛ ne maa yɛnwu nvoninli kpole bie wɔ yɛ adwenle nu. Fa ye kɛ ɛlɛnwu sonla bie mɔɔ bɔ nvutuke mɔɔ pɛtɛpɛtɛ nu tɛladeɛ gua ɔ nwo zo la kɛ ɔde suakpole bie anlenkɛ nwo lɔ. Aleɛ ɛnle ɛkɛ ne maa ɔyɛ mɛlɛbela, ɔ subane ti menli twe bɛ nwo kɔfi ɔ nwo, yɛɛ ewia mɔɔ kye kpole la sɔ ye wɔ dibilɛ yɛɛ awɛle kpole noko kɔ ye nɔsolɛ. Sɛlɛvolɛ ne ze ye kpole kɛ ɔhyia moalɛ amaa yeanyia anyhɔlɛ wɔ ye ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nu. Zɔhane ala a ahenle mɔɔ nwu ye sunsum nu ngyianle la—sɛlɛvolɛ mɔɔ kpondɛ sunsum ne la—bahyia moalɛ amaa yeanyia anyhɔlɛ a. Ɔti, ɔkulo kɛ ɔkɔ zo ɔkpondɛ sunsum nu ninyɛne dɔɔnwo mɔɔ Gyihova fa maa menli mɔɔ kulo ye la kpalɛ.

3 Wɔ edwɛkɛpimile ɛhye anu, yɛbalimoa yɛanlea mɔɔ yɛbahola yɛazukoa la

1. Ninyɛne titili boni mɔ a Gyihova bɔle yɛ la a yɛhyia nwo a? (Mateyu 5:3)

2. Kɛzi ɛbahilehile ninyɛne mɔɔ bamaa yɛanwu mɔɔ ɔkile kɛ yɛbanwu yɛ “sunsum nu ngyianle” la anu ɛ?

3. Duzu a yɛbazuzu nwo wɔ edwɛkɛ ɛhye anu a?

2

Ɛnee Fɔneshyanirale ne hyia mɛlɛbɛnwoaze, abotane, nee diedi na yeanyia moalɛ mɔɔ ɔkpondɛ la

(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 5)

vi Finisiara raalɛ bie mɔɔ sɔrele Gyisɛse kɛ ɔboa ye la ɛkɛ. Kɛzi edwɛkɛ ne si kɔ la si subane nsa mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ menli mɔɔ ze kɛ bɛhyia sunsum nu ninyɛne nwo la nyia la azo. Akee yɛbazuzu neazo mɔɔ ɛzoanvolɛ Pita nee Pɔɔl yɛɛ Belemgbunli Devidi mɔɔ bɛ kɔsɔɔti bɛle sunsum nu menli la yɛle la anwo.

4 Mekɛ bie, Fonisia raalɛ bie rale Gyisɛse anwo lɔ. Ɛnee sunsum ɛtane bie “ɛlɛbɔ ye ra raalɛ ne amaneɛ kpole.” (Mat. 15:21-28) Raalɛ ne kotole aze na ɔbɔle Gyisɛse mpae kɛ ɔboa ye. Mɔɔ Fonisia raalɛ ɛhye rale Gyisɛse anwo lɔ la, ɔlale subane ngakyile mɔɔ yɛbahola yɛazukoa debie yɛavi nu la ali. Bɛmaa yɛzuzu nuhua bie mɔ anwo.

5 Finisiara raalɛ ne lale mɛlɛbɛnwoaze kpole ali. Duzu ati a yɛbahola yɛaha zɔ a? Ɔluakɛ Gyisɛse vale ndonwo mɔɔ zɔho kɛ ɔfa ye ɔtoto kpolikpoli nwonane nwo la lile gyima la, yeambva ye nyane, bie a kpolikpoli mɔɔ bɛtete ye wɔ Maanle maanle sonla sua nu la. Anrɛɛ kɛzi ɛbayɛ wɔ nyɛleɛ wɔ Gyisɛse edwɛkɛ mini yɛɛ ye nyɛleɛ ne anwo ɛ? Anrɛɛ ɛbade nganeɛ kɛ yegua wɔ nyunlu aze na wɔagyakyi kɛ ɛkpondɛ ye moalɛ ɔ? Finisiara raalɛ ne anyɛ ye zɔ. Ɔlale mɛlɛbɛnwoaze ngomekye ali, emomu ɔlale abotane noko ali. Ɛhye da ali wɔ kɛzi ɔhɔle zolɛ ɔzɛlɛle Gyisɛse moalɛ la anu. Duzu a hanle ye kɛ ɔyɛ zɔ a? Ye diedi mɔɔ ɔlɛ wɔ Gyisɛse anu la. Nɔhalɛ nu, raalɛ ɛhye diedi ne hanle Gyisɛse kpalɛ kɛ ɔyɛle debie mɔɔ yɛ azibɛnwo la. Ɔnva nwo kɛ ɛnee yebɔ mɔdenle yɛaha kɛ bɛzoanle ye kɛ ɔraboka “Yizilayɛ sua nu mboane mɔɔ ɛminli la ngomekye,” Gyisɛse dule sunsum ɛtane ne vile Maanle maanle raalɛ ɛhye ara raalɛ ne anu.

Ɔwɔ kɛ yɛnyia diedi kpole kpalɛ wɔ Kelaese Gyisɛse anu yɛɛ yɛfa yɛ nwo yɛto menli mɔɔ ɔlɛfa bɛ yeali ye ɛdoavolɛma nzonlɛ la azo. (Mat. 24:45-47) Ɔyɛ Gyɛhova mini ye Ra ne fɛ kɛ bɛbamaa menli mɔɔ da subane ɛhye mɔ ali la asa aha sunsum nu ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛhyia nwo la. (Fa toto Gyemise 1:5-7 anwo.) Kɛkala maa yɛzuzu adenle mɔɔ Gyɛhova dua zo maa yɛnyia sunsum nu aleɛ, tɛladeɛ, nee ɛleka mɔɔ yɛsie yɛ ti la anwo. Yɛbanwu mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ na yɛasa aha ninyɛne mɔɔ hyia la kɛ yɛnea neazo mɔɔ ɛzoanvolɛma Pita nee Pɔɔlo kɛye Bie la Belemgbunli Devidi yɛle la.

KƐ PITA LA—TƐNLA AZE KƐ AKELE NWOONWU SUNSUM NU

7 Suzu ɛzoanvo Pita neazo ne anwo. Ɛnee ɔboka Dwuuma mɔɔ lumua nvonle kɛ Gyisɛse a le Mɛzaya ne—ahenle mɔɔ Gyihova ɛlɛdua ɔ nwo zo amaa ye menli ne “dahuu ngoane edwɛkɛ” la anwo. (Dwɔn 6:66-68) Kolaa na Gyisɛse mɔɔ bɛdwazo ye la ahɔ anwuma la, ɔmanle Pita gyima ɛhye: “Nea me mboane nwanwa ne mɔ azo.” (Dwɔn 21:17) Pita vale nɔhalɛlilɛ liile zɔhane gyima ne azo, na Gyihova bɔbɔ luale ɔ nwo zo hɛlɛle ngɛlata nwiɔ mɔɔ bɛrayɛle Baebolo ne foa la. Noko ɛnee ɔhyia kɛ Pita noko fa sunsum nu aleɛ nea ɔ nwo. Kɛ neazo la, ɔsukanle sunsum nu ngɛlata mɔɔ ɛzoanvo Pɔɔlo lumuanle hɛlɛle la. Pita liele dole nu kɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ Pɔɔlo hɛlɛle nwolɛ edwɛkɛ la bie mɔ “anwo edwɛkɛ ɛdelɛ yɛ se.” (2 Pita 3:15, 16) Noko ɔda ali kɛ Pita hɔle zo yɛle zɔ, na ɔnyianle diedi kɛ Gyihova baboa ye amaa yeade na yeava “aleɛ mɔɔ ɛyɛ se” mɔɔ sunsum ne hanle Pɔɔlo manle ɔhɛlɛle wɔ ye ngɛlata ne mɔ anu la yeali gyima.—Kenga Hibuluma 5:14–6:1.

8 Pita lale ye ali kɛ ɔlɛ Gyihova anu diedi ɔlua adehilelɛ mɔɔ ɔnyianle la mɔɔ ɔliile zolɛ la azo. Kɛ neazo la, mɔɔ Pita wɔ meli gyinlawoke zo azuamgbole mɔɔ le Dgyɔpa anu la, ɔnwunle ɛnyelɛzodeɛ mɔɔ Nyamenle sonvolɛ bie zele ye kɛ ɔhu na ɔli nane mɔɔ kɛ mɔɔ Mɔyisi Mɛla ne kile la, anwo ɛnde la. Wɔ Dyuuma afoa nu, anrɛɛ adehilelɛ zɔhane bayɛ anyezelɛ. Mɔɔ lumua la, Pita buale kɛ: “Awulae, kyɛkyɛ kpalɛ, ɔluakɛ mentɛlinle debie mɔɔ anwo ɛnde anzɛɛ ɛnle kpalɛ la ɛlɛ.” Akee bɛzele ye kɛ: “Mmagyegye ninyɛne mɔɔ Nyamenle ɛde nwo la anwo.” (Gyi. 10:9-15) Pita dele edwɛkɛ ne abo. Kɛzi yɛsi yɛnwu ye ɛ? Yeanyɛ boɛ na yeanyia ɛnyelɛzodeɛ ne la, mrenyia nsa mɔɔ Maanle Maanle nuhua sonla Kɔnileɛse zoanle bɛ la rale na bɛdole ɛsalɛ bɛfɛle Pita kɛ ɔrɛla na ɔradendɛ ɔgile bɛ menle ne. Kɛ mɔɔ ɔde fɛlɛfɛle la, anrɛɛ Pita ɛnrɛlie ɛnrɛdo nu ɛlɛ kɛ ɔkɔ Maanle Maanle nuhua sonla sua nu. Ɛnee Dyuuma bu Maanle Maanle nu amra kɛ bɛ nwo ɛnde. (Gyi. 10:28, 29) Noko Pita liele wulirɛ fofolɛ ne mɔɔ Gyihova ɛhile ye la dole nu ndɛndɛ. (Mrɛ. 4:18) Ɔhɔhɛlɛle edwɛkɛ ne ɔgilele Kɔnileɛse nee ye sua nu amra na ɔnyianle anyelielɛ mɔɔ le kɛ ɔnwunle kɛ bɛliele nɔhalɛ ne, bɛnyianle sunsum nwuanzanwuanza ne, na bɛzɔnenle bɛ la.—Gyi. 10:44-48.

7. Gyima boni a Pita nyianle a, noko duzu a noko a ɔwɔ kɛ ɔyɛ a? Kilehile nu. (Hibuluma 5:14–6:1)

4 ƐZINZANLI NE

sunsum nu aleɛ kpole—nɔhalɛ mɔɔ bie a ɔnla aze kɛ bɛte ɔ bo la. Ɔwɔ kɛ yɛnyia mekɛ na yɛkulo kɛ yɛbɔ mɔdenle kɛ yɛbade Nyamenle Edwɛkɛ ne abo kpalɛ, noko ɔfɛta. Kɛmɔti ɛ? Yɛnyia adenle nwiɔ bie azo nvasoɛ: Mɔɔ limoa, yɛsukoa ninyɛne mɔɔ fane Gyihova anwo la mɔɔ maa yɛ kulo yɛɛ ɛbulɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛ maa ye la yɛ kpole; na mɔɔ tɔ zo nwiɔ, yɛte nganeɛ kɛ yɛlɛ ngeseebɛ kɛ yɛbaha yɛ anwuma Selɛ mɔɔ yɛ anyelielɛ la anwo edwɛkɛ yɛahile awie mɔ. (Wul. 11:33; Yek. 4:11) Pita neazo ne kilehile yɛ ɛzukoalɛdeɛ fofolɛ bie: Saa yɛsa ka nzenzaleɛ mɔɔ bɛyɛ wɔ kɛzi yɛte Nyamenle Edwɛkɛ ne abo la anu a, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ ndɛ yɛkakyi yɛ adwenle nee yɛ nyɛleɛ. Ɛhye ala a bamaa yɛahɔ zo yɛali sunsum nu aleɛ kpalɛ na yɛazonle nvasoɛ yɛamaa Gyihova a.

KƐMƆ PƆƆLU YƐLE LA—TƐNLA SUNSUM NU ƐDANLƐ KPALƐ

10 Amaa yɛazɔ Nyamenle anye la, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛdua Gyihova nziezielɛ fofolɛ bie noko azo: sunsum nu tɛladeɛ. Duzu a yɛkile a? Ɛzoanvolɛ Pɔɔlo hɛlɛle kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛye “subane dɛba ne yɛto ɛkɛ” na yɛwula “subane fofolɛ ne.” (Kenga Kɔlɔsaema 3:8-10.) Subane zɛhae a yɛbawula la bahola ayɛ gyima mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkɔ zo yɛyɛ dahuu. Suzu Pɔɔlo mumua ne neazo ne anwo. Ɔvi ye ngakula nu, ɔbɔle mɔdenle kpole kɛ ɔbanyia Nyamenle anyezɔlɛ. (Gal. 1:14; Fel. 3:4, 5) Noko ɛnee ɔnze Nyamenle bodane ne kpalɛ, yemɔti ɛnee ɔ sunsum nu gyinlabelɛ ɛnle kpalɛ. Kɛmɔ ɛnee ɔnze Kelaese ngilehilelɛ kpalɛ yɛɛ ɛnee ɔle memgbɔlanli la ati, ɔhakyile “sonla mɔɔ le amumuyɛ” mɔɔ wula subane mɔɔ ɛnle kpalɛ la.—1 Tim. 1:13.

10. Duzu a sunsum nu tɛlade kpalɛ ɛwulale kile a? (Kɔlɔsaema 3:8-10)

11 Kolaa na Pɔɔlo akɛra Kilisienenli la, ɛnee ɔfa ɛya mɔdenle. Edwɛkɛ bie mɔɔ wɔ Gyima ne mɔ anu la ka kɛ ɛnee Pɔɔlo ɛya ɛva Gyisɛse ɛdoavolɛma ne kpole kpalɛ yemɔti ɛnee ɔlɛhɔ ‘amaa yeawula bɛ ɛzulolɛ na yeaho bɛ.’ (Gyima. 9:1) Mɔɔ Pɔɔlo rale Kilisieneyɛlɛ nu la, nɔhalɛ nu, ɔbɔle mɔdenle kpole kɛ ɔbaye ye subane dɛba ne foa ɛhye yeavi ɛkɛ. (Ɛfɛ. 4:22, 31) Noko bɔbɔ la, mekɛ mɔɔ akɛnrasesebɛdwe rale Pɔɔlo nee Baanabase avinli la, “bɛva ɛya kpole kpalɛ.” (Gyima. 15:37-39) Ɛhye vale anwubielɛ rale Pɔɔlo anwo zo, noko yeangyakyi. Ɔhɔle zo ‘yevelele ye sonlabaka ne’—yeanzo ye sinlidɔlɛ ne azo—amaa yeanyia Gyihova anyezɔlɛ.—1 Kɔl. 9:27.

12 Pɔɔlo holle adua wulole ye subane ɛtane ne mɔ na akee ɔva subane fofolɛ ne ɔwula ɔboalekɛ yeanva ɔ nwo yeando ɔdaye mumua ne ye anwosesebɛ zo. (Fel. 4:13) Kɛmɔ Pita yɛle la, Pɔɔlo zɔho “anwosesebɛ mɔɔ Nyamenle fa maa la” azo. (1 Pita 4:11) Noko, ɔyɛ a Pɔɔlo te nganeɛ kɛ yebɔ mgbane. Noko mekɛ mɔɔ ɔ sa nu do la, ɔdwenledwenle ninyɛne kpalɛ mɔɔ ye anwuma Selɛ ne ɛyɛ ɛmaa ye la anwo, na ɛhye maanle ye kpɔkɛdelɛ kɛ ɔbahɔ zo yeabɔ mɔdenle la anu miarenle.—Wlo. 7:21-25.

Ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkɔ zo yɛdi sunsum nu aleɛ, adɔleɛ nee dabida mɔɔ yɛhyia nwo la anwo gyima

(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 18)

yɛ ɛya mɔɔ bamaa yɛaho anzɛɛ yɛaha edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ɛnle fɛ yɛahile awie la—ɔnle kɛ yɛbu yɛ nwo kɛ yɛbɔ yɛ bra. Emomu, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkɔ zo yɛbɔ mɔdenle kɛ yɛbahakyi yɛ adwenle nee yɛ nyɛleɛ. (Wlo. 12:1, 2; Ɛfɛ. 4:24) Noko mekɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛyɛ zɔ la, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkakye ninyɛne bieko mɔɔ anwo hyia: Ɔnle kɛ nwonane nu tɛladeɛ mɔɔ bɛpamua bɛmaa ɔfɛta yɛ la, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkakyi yɛ nwo amaa sunsum nu tɛladeɛ mɔɔ Gyihova fa maa la afɛta yɛ. Ɔfɛta kɛ yɛkakyi sonla ko mɔɔ yɛde la amaa yɛali Nyamenle ngyinlazo nwo gyima.

KƐ DEEBIDI LA—TƐNLA SUNSUM NU ƐRƐNE KPALƐ

14 Amaa yɛanyia anyelielɛ kpalɛ la, yɛhyia ninyɛne mɔɔ tɛ sunsum nu aleɛ nee sunsum nu adɔleɛ ngome a. Yɛhyia sunsum nu ɛleka mɔɔ yɛbanrɛlɛ noko. Duzu a le zɔhane, na kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na yɛanwu kɛ yɛbɔ yɛ nwo bane wɔ ɔ bo dahuu ɛ?

14-15. Adenle boni azo a Gyihova fa sunsum nu ɛvealekan maa ye menli a? (Edwɛne 27:5) (Nea mukelɛ ne azo nvoninli ne noko.)

15 Belemgbunli Devidi hanle sunsum nu banebɔlɛ mɔɔ Gyihova fa maa la anwo edwɛkɛ. (Kenga Edwɛndolɛ 27:5.) Gyihova bɔ ye menli nwo bane ɔvi awie biala nee debie biala mɔɔ bahola azɛkye bɛ diedi mɔɔ bɛlɛ ye wɔ Ye nu la bɔkɔɔ la asa. Kɛzi Gyihova bɔ ye azonvolɛ nwo bane fi ekyi ngyegyelɛ nwo ɛ? Ɔbɔ ɛwɔkɛ kɛ konle debie biala mɔɔ bɛbayɛ bɛatia bɛ la ɛnrɛwie awielie kpalɛ. (Edw. 34:7; Aye. 54:17) Ɔwɔ nuhua kɛ Seetan nee ye gyima-yɛvoma lɛ tumi ɛdeɛ, noko bɛnrɛhola bɛnrɛyɛ yɛ nyane biala mɔɔ badɛnla ɛkɛ dahuu la. Saa bɛku yɛ bɔbɔ a, Gyihova bamaa yɛanyia ngoane bieko wɔ ewudwazo ne anu. Mɔɔ boka nganeɛdelɛ mɔɔ ɛnee ɔlɛko ɔtia ye wɔ ɔ nu ɛhye anwo la, ɛnee ɔyɛ a Pɔɔlo te nganeɛ ɛtane wɔ mɔɔ ɔyɛle ye wɔ tete ne la anwo nee ngyegyelɛ bie mɔɔ ɛnee ɛnrɛvi ye nwo la anwo.

4 Ninyɛne mɔɔ ɔyɛle ye wɔ tetedwɛkɛ nu la. Kolaa na Paul mɔɔ ɛnee bɛfɛlɛ ye Saul la araye Kilisienenli la, ɔyɛle ninyɛne mɔɔ nzinlii ɔnzonlenle ye la. 5 Mɔɔ sɔfo Pɔɔlo rayɛle Kilisienenle la, ninyɛne mɔɔ ɔyɛle ye wɔ tete ne la hanle ye adwenle wɔ mekɛ bie mɔ anu. Kɛ neazo la, mɔɔ ɔhɛlɛle ye kɛlata mɔɔ limoa kɔmaale Kɔlentema wɔ kɛyɛ 55 Y.M. anu la, ɔhanle kɛ: “Menfɛta kɛ bɛfɛlɛ me ɛzoanvolɛ, ɔluakɛ mekpɔle Nyamenle asafo ne ati mehendele bɛ.” (1 Kɔl. 15:9) Kɛyɛ ɛvolɛ nnu nzi, wɔ ye kɛlata mɔɔ ɔhɛlɛle kɔmaale Ɛfɛsɛsoma la anu, ɔhanle ɔ nwo edwɛkɛ kɛ ɔle “nwomenle kpalɛ tɛla nwonlomane amuala mɔɔ ɛha la.” (Ɛfɛ. 3:8) Mɔɔ ɔhɛlɛle kɔmaale Timote la, Pɔɔlo hanle ɔ nwo edwɛkɛ kɛ limoa ne ɛnee ɔle “Nyamenletendɛvolɛ nee menlinzonzonlevolɛ nee debie kpole nwo ndelebɛbovonle.” (1 Tim. 1:13) Ɛbahola wɔapɛ kɛzi Pɔɔlo dele nganeɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɔhɔle asafo bie anu na ɔyiale menli mɔɔ ɔkpɔle bɛ ati ɔhendele bɛ anzɛɛ bɛ mbusuafo la anwo nvoninli ɔ?

7 Ye sintilī nwo ninyɛne. Pɔɔlo ho nwonle ye sintilī nwo ninyɛne. (Kenga Wulomuma 7:18, 19.) Bieko, mɔdenle mɔɔ yɛbɔ kɛ yɛbanlea azɛlɛ ne azo na yɛadɛnla anzodwole nu la ɛnrɛyɛ mgbane bieko na ɔbawie boɛ wɔ Ahenle mɔɔ kpɔnenle yɛ, “Anzodwole Belemgbunli,” Gyisɛse Kelaese belemgbunlililɛ ne abo.—Aye. 9:6, 7.

14 Ɔvi mekɛ mɔɔ Adam nee Yivi yɛle ɛtane la, menli ɛvi Nyamenle anwo, anzɛɛ bɛtetɛ bɛ nwo bɛvi ɔ nwo. Baebolo ne bɔbɔ ka kɛ sonla amuala ɛvi Ɔbɔvolɛ ne anwo. (Wlo. 8:7, 8; Kɔl. 1:21) Duzu ati ɔ? Ɔluakɛ Gyihova ngyinlazo mɔɔ di munli la ɛmmaa ɔmbu ɛtane kɛ ɛhwee. Baebolo ne ka Gyihova anwo kɛ: “Wɔ nye anwo tɛ kpalɛ kɛ ɔnle kɛ ɔnwu ɛtane, yɛɛ ɛntɛla nvonleɛ.” (Hab. 1:13) Ɔti ɛtane ɛmaa ɛtetɛlɛ ɛra Nyamenle nee sonla avinli. 15 Baebolo ne ka kɛ Gyisɛse le “yɛ ɛtane ne mɔ anwo mpɔtane afɔle.” (1 Gyɔn 2:2) Giliki edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ mɔɔ bɛgile ɔ bo “mpɔtane afɔle” la kile “debie mɔɔ bɛfa bɛtutu ɛya.” Kɛzi ɛkpɔnelɛ afɔle ne tutule Gyihova ɛya ɛ? Ɔnle kɛ yɛdwenle kɛ ye Ra ne ewule ne manle ɔ rɛle kyekyele. Emomu, ɛkpɔnelɛ afɔle ne tutule Gyihova ɛya, anzɛɛ ɔmanle Gyihova pɛlepɛlelilɛ ngyinlazo ne yɛle gyima. Kɛkala na ɔlɛ debie mɔɔ ɔkegyina zo yeaziezie agɔnwolɛvalɛ mɔɔ wɔ ɔdaye nee sonla avinli la. (Wlo. 3:23-26) Ɔbahola eza yeabu menli mɔɔ zonlenle ye nɔhalɛ nu kolaa na Kelaese awu la tenlene. (Mɔl. 15:1, 6, ɔ bo ɛkɛ ne edwɛkɛ.) Kɛzi ɔkɛyɛ ɛhye ɛ? Ɔlua kenlebie ɛkpɔnelɛ afɔle ne ati. Gyihova nwunle ye kpole kɛ ye Ra Gyisɛse bamaa afɔle zɔhane. (Aye. 46:10) Ɛkpɔnelɛ afɔle ne buele adenle manle sonla kɛ ɔbaziezie ɔ gɔnwolɛvalɛ mɔɔ wɔ ɔdaye nee Nyamenle avinli la.

Kɛ anrɛɛ yɛbabuo ɛkpɔnenle ninyɛne ne kɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ bamaa yɛnyia nvasoɛ ala la, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkakye kɛ ɔle adenle mɔɔ Gyihova dua zo bualɛ kpokɛ mɔɔ Seetan dule wɔ Yidɛn tɛnla ne anu la. (Gyn. 3:1-5, 15) Ɛkpɔnenle ninyɛne ne dua zo, Gyihova tɛla ye duma ne anwo, mɔɔ ɔye nzonlɛ mɔɔ bɛbɔ ye la kɔsɔɔti ɔfi ɛkɛ a. Ɔdie yɛ noko ɔfi ɛtane nee ewule nu, mɔɔ ɔkile kɛ ɔle ɛlɔlɛ Nyamenle a. Yɛɛ wɔ ye ahyɛledeɛ kpole ne anu, Gyihova maa yɛ—ɔnva nwo yɛ ɛtane tɛnlabelɛ ne—nyia nwolɛ adenle bua Seetan kpokɛdule ne. Kɛzi Ɛbayɛ Wɔ Ninyɛne Wɔ Ɛkpɔnelɛ Ne Anwo Ɛ?

“Kelaese ɛlɔlɛ ne piapia yɛ.”—2 KƆL. 5:14.

SAA bɛliele wɔ bɔba nu wɔ azua bie mɔɔ ɛbubu la anu a, asoo ɛnrɛde nganeɛ kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ ɛda ahenle mɔɔ liele wɔ ngoane la ase? Saa bɛliele awie mɔ noko a, bie a ɛbahulo kɛ ɛkɔ ahenle ɛkɛ ɛda ye ase wɔ wɔ ngomekye wɔ nwo ti, mɔɔ ɛkile kɛ ɛbu mɔɔ bɛyɛle bɛmaanle wɔ la.

2 Kɛ mɔɔ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ limoa la hilehilele nu la, yɛnrɛhola yɛnrɛlie yɛ nwo yɛnrɛvi ɛtane mɔɔ yɛnyianle yɛvile yɛ awovolɛ ɛkɛ la nzonlɛlielɛ nu. Noko, Gyisɛse ɛkpɔnenle afɔlebulɛ ne kola die yɛ ɔlua (1) ɛtane mɔɔ yɛyɛ la anwo fakyɛ, (2) anyelazo kɛ yɛbanwu yɛ nwo bɔkɔɔ yɛavi yɛ ɛtane tɛnlabelɛ ne anu, nee (3) Nyamenle mɔɔ yɛ nee ye bazi nsa mɔɔ ɔmaa la azo. Ɔlua ɛhye ati, yɛlɛ dahuu ngoane anyelazo wɔ Gyihova ewiade fofolɛ ne anu. Nɔhalɛ nu, ɛkpɔnenle afɔlebulɛ ne maa Gyisɛse ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ ɔlɛ ɔmaa menli la da ali kpole—ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ ɛnee ɔlɛ zɔhane kenle pɔkɔɔ kolaa na bɛawo ye kɛ sonla mɔɔ di munli. (Mrɛ. 8:30, 31) Ɛzoanvolɛ Pɔɔlo hɛlɛle kɛ “Kelaese ne ɛlɔlɛ ne piapia yɛ.” (Kenga 2 Kɔlentema 5:14, 15.) Ɛhye kile kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ Gyisɛse ɛlɔlɛ ne ka yɛ ma yɛyɛ debie, amaa yɛahile kɛ yɛmbu ye ɛkpɔnenle afɔlebulɛ ne mgbane.

Kɛ neazo la, fa ye kɛ menli nsa ɛlɛkɔ ɛleka ko, noko bɛ nuhua ko biala vi suakpole ngakyile zo a bɔle ɔ bo a. Adenle mɔɔ bɛbalua zo la bayɛ ngakyile. 4 Saa ɛlɛsukoa Baebolo ne a, suzu ɛhye anwo: Kɛzi ɛyɛle wɔ nyɛleɛ wɔ edwɛkpa ne anwo la kile kɛ ɛboka menli mɔɔ Gyihova ɛlɛtwe bɛ ara ɔ nwo lɔ la anwo. Ka Nɔhalɛ Ne Wɔ Ɛlɔlɛ Nu

“Gyihova, nɔhalɛ Nyamenle ne.”—EDW. 31:5.

MEKƐ mɔɔ yɛbanlea yɛ gɔnwo Gyihova sonvolɛ bie anyi anu la, kpuya mɔɔ yɛta yɛbiza yɛ nwo la ko a le kɛ, “Kɛ ɔyɛle mɔɔ ɛnwunle nɔhalɛ ne a?” Bie mɔ bua kpuya zɔhane kɛ bɛtetele bɛ “wɔ nɔhalɛ ne anu.” Bie mɔ noko kɛhanle kɛ bɛnrɛhyɛle mɔɔ bɛralɛ “nɔhalɛ ne anu a.” Yɛbua wɔ adenle zɛhae mɔ azo ɔluakɛ Nyamenle Edwɛkɛ ne anu nɔhalɛ ne ka yɛ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ kɔsɔɔti. Duzu ati ɔ? Ɔluakɛ yɛze kɛ yɛbahola yɛara Gyihova abusua ne anu saa yɛda ye ali kɛ yɛkulo nɔhalɛ ne na yɛfa yɛbɔ yɛ ɛbɛla a ngome. Ɛhye kile kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛdi nɔhalɛ wɔ yɛ edwɛkɛhanlɛ nee yɛ nyɛleɛ nu.—Edw. 15:1-3.

² Dahuu Gyisɛse hanle nɔhalɛ ne. Ye agbɔvolɛ bɔbɔ liele dole nu kɛ ɔkaha nɔhalɛ ne ɔnva nwo kɛ menli anye ɛnlie nwo la. (Mat. 22:16) Mɔɔ fale nsunsuanlilɛ mɔɔ nɔhalɛ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ɔkilehilele la kɛnyia la anwo la, Gyisɛse hanle kɛ: “Memgale kɛ mebamaa menli avile nu, nrenyia nee ye papa avinli kɛdo, raalɛ nee ye mame avinli kɛdo, na aye nee ɔ ze ye bɔbɔ avinli kɛdo.” (Mat. 10:35) Ɛnee Gyisɛse ɛngulo kɛ menli twe bɛ nwo fi edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ɔdaye nee ye ɛdoavolɛma bɛka la anwo, noko ɛnee ɔze kɛ zɔhane a ɔkaba a. (Mat. 23:37) Ɛnee ɔze kɛ ye edwɛkɛ ne bamaa menli ahyɛ eku nwiɔ—bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛkulo Baebolo nu nɔhalɛ ne nee bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛngulo la.—2 Tɛs. 2:9-11.

1. Duzu a ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ na yɛayɛ Gyihova abusua ne anu amra a?

2. (a) Duzu duma a ɛnee Gyisɛse lɛ a? (b) Kɛzi nɔhalɛ ne mɔ mɔɔ Gyisɛse hilehilele la baha menli ɛ?

3 Kɛ mɔɔ Gyisɛse yɛle la, yɛbɔ mɔdenle kɛ yɛbayɛ nɔhalɛma, yɛaha nɔhalɛ ne dahuu, ɔwɔ nuhua kɛ saa yɛyɛ zɔ a menli nzonle ɛnrɛdo yɛ ɛdeɛ. Eza yɛka Baebolo nɔhalɛ ne na yɛkilehile nuhua, ɔwɔ nuhua kɛ mɔɔ yɛka la bahola agyegye bie mɔ ɛdeɛ. Asoo ɛhye kile kɛ ɔnle kɛ yɛsuzu kɛzi na mekɛ mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛka nɔhalɛ ne la anwo ɔ? Kyɛkyɛ fee! Wɔ edwɛkɛbɔlɛ ɛhye anu, lumua yɛbabua kpuya titiliu ɛhye: Nienwu a yɛbahola yɛanwu nɔhalɛ ne a? Akee yɛbabua kpuya bie mɔɔ fane ɛhye anwo la: Duzu ati a ɔwɔ kɛ yɛka nɔhalɛ ne a, kɛzi a ɔwɔ kɛ yɛka a, na mekɛ boni a ɔwɔ kɛ yɛka a? Mualɛ mɔɔ yɛbanyia la baboa yɛ amaa yɛali nɔhalɛ ne anwo edwɛkɛ mɛlɛbɛnwoaze nu, kile kɛ, nwonlomua nu na wɔ mekɛ kpalɛ nu.

NII LA YƐBAHƆLA YƐANWUN NƆHALƐ NE A?

4 Gyihova a le nɔhalɛ ne Ɛvileka a. Debie biala mɔɔ ɔka la le nɔhalɛ. Kɛ neazo la, edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ɔka ye wɔ mɔɔ le kpalɛ nee mɔɔ le ɛtane nwo la le nɔhalɛ. (Edw. 19:9; 119:142, 151) Debie biala mɔɔ ɔka ye wɔ kenlema nwo la ba nu. (Aye. 55:10, 11) Ɔmbubu ɛwɔkɛ ɛlɛ. (Dɔn. 23:19) Nɔhalɛ nu, ɔnrɛhɔle zo kɛ Gyihova bɔ adalɛ ɛlɛ! (Hib. 6:18) Ɔlua adwenle kpalɛ zɔhane ati, bɛze Gyihova kɛ “nɔhalɛ Nyamenle ne.”—Edw. 31:5.

5 Ɔnva nwo mɔɔ menli bie mɔ ka la, ɔnle se kɛ yɛbanwu Gyihova, “nɔhalɛ Nyamenle ne.” Daselɛ mɔɔ kile kɛ ɔwɔ ɛkɛ la ɛyia yɛ nwo ɛtane. (Wlo. 1:20) Mɔɔ ɛzoanvolɛ Pɔɔlo ɛlɛtendɛ ahile Gilikima mɔɔ ze nwoma kpalɛ wɔ Atɛnse la, ɔhanle kɛ Nyamenle kpondɛ kɛ yɛnwu “ye” na “ɔnle moa wɔ yɛ nuhua biala anwo.” (Kenga Gyima ne 17:27.) Nɔhalɛ nu, Gyihova twe mɛtɛnema mɔɔ ɛlɛkpondɛ nɔhalɛ ne la bika ɔ nwo.—Dwɔn 6:44.

6 Adenle ngoko mɔɔ yɛbahola yɛalua zo yɛanwu Gyihova la a le kɛ yɛbazukoa Baebolo ne. Menli mɔɔ hɛlɛle buluku zɔhane la, Nyamenle sunsum nwuanzanwuanza ne a hanle bɛ a. (2 Pita 1:20, 21) Ɔti edwɛkɛ mɔɔ wɔ Baebolo ne anu la amuala le nɔhalɛ na ɔfɛta kɛ yɛnyia nu anwodozo. Kɛ neazo la, yɛbahola yɛalie mɔɔ ɔka ye wɔ ewiade ne nee ngoane mɔɔ wɔ azɛlɛ ye azo la amɔbɔbɔle nwo la yɛali. (Mɔb. 1:1, 26) Yɛbahola yɛalie mɔɔ ɔka ye wɔ deɛmɔti yɛ muala yɛyɛ ɛtane nee deɛmɔti amaneɛnwunlɛ nee ewule dɔɔnwo wɔ ɛkɛ la yɛali. (Wlo. 5:12; 6:23) Yɛbahola yɛanyia nu anwodozo kpalɛ kɛ Gyihova balua ɔ Ra ne azo azɛkye ninyɛndane mɔɔ Seetan, mɔɔ le “nwolɛka fɔlɛ” la ɛyɛ la amuala. (Dwɔn 8:44; Wlo. 16:20) Yɛɛ yɛbahola yɛanyia Baebolo ne ɛwɔkɛzilɛ mɔɔ kile kɛ Gyisɛse bazɛkye amumuyɛma, badwazo mowuamra, bayɛ azɛlɛ ne kpalɛ, na yeaboa yɛ ma yɛali munli la anu anwodozo. (Dwɔn 11:25, 26; 1 Dwɔn 3:8) Nwolɛ adenle kpole boni a Gyihova ɛva ɛmaa yɛ a! Yehilehile yɛ nɔhalɛ ne, na yemaa yɛ adenle kɛ yɛhilehile awie mɔ nɔhalɛ ne.—Mat. 28:19, 20.

Saa ɛkilehile nɔhalɛ ne a, nwane a ɛtwe adwenle ɛkɔ ɔ nwo a? (Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 7-8)

hyia Gyihova. Suzu mɔɔ seli wɔ Gyisɛse azɛlɛ ye azo ɛzonlenlɛ gyima ne anu la anwo. (Kenga Maake 3:11, 12.) Mɔɔ Gyisɛse lale edwɛkpa ne wɔ Galeli Azule ne anloa la, menli dɔɔnwo rale ɔ nwo lɔ. Ɛnee sunsum ɛtane wɔ bɛ nuhua bie mɔ anwo mɔɔ bɛdole bɛ nwo aze wɔ Gyisɛse anyunlu na bɛdeanle nu bɛzele ye kɛ: “Wɔmɔ a ɛle Nyamenle Ara ne a.” Duzu ati a sunsum ɛtane ne mɔ hanle nɔhalɛ mɔɔ fane Gyisɛse anwo la a? Bie a ɛnee bɛ kpondɛ kɛ menli mɔɔ tie bɛ la nyia bɛ nu anwodozo na akee bɛhwe bɛ bɛvi Gyisɛse ɛzonlenlɛ nwo. Mɔlebɛbo ne sunsum ɛtane ne mɔ hanle nɔhalɛ edwɛkɛ, noko ɛnee bɛ bodane le bɛ ngomekye bɛ nvasoɛ. Bɛtɛbale Gyisɛse yɛɛ bɛanyɛ debie mɔɔ sɔle ɔ nye a. Nɔhalɛ nu, Gyisɛse bɔle sunsum ɛtane ne mɔ kɔkɔ kɛ bɛmmaka ɔ nwo edwɛkɛ.

8 Ɛzukoalɛ boni a yɛbahola yɛanwu ye wɔ ghanlɛ ɛhye anu a? Bodane mɔɔ yɛlɛ na yɛka nɔhalɛ ne la anwo hyia Gyihova kpole. 9 Suzu tɛnlabelɛ bieko mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkoatia nwolɛwɔlɛ kponle mɔɔ yɛbakpondɛ yɛamaa yɛ nwo la anwo. Fa ye kɛ adiema nrenyia bie mɔɔ lɛ ɛzonlelilɛ la vale edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛfeze nwolɛ la wulale yɛ sa na akee yɛhahanle yɛahile awie mɔ. Saa menli mɔɔ bɛdiele yɛ la nwu ye nzinlii kɛ mɔɔ yɛhanle la le nɔhalɛ a, ɔbahola yeha bɛ ahonle kɛ yɛze ninyɛne dɔɔnwo na bɛasu kɛ bɛfa edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛfeze nwolɛ dɔɔnwo bɛwula yɛ sa. Ɛhye bahola amaa bɛabu yɛ kpole, noko ɔnrɛmaa Nyamenle anwunle mɔɔ ɔlɛ ye wɔ yɛ nwo la ɛnrɛhɔ ɔ nyunlu. (Mrɛ. 11:13) Kɛmɔti ɛ? Ɔluakɛ tɛ kɛ yɛhanle edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛfeze nwolɛ la ala, emomu yɛhanle nɔhalɛ ne wɔ adwenle mɔɔ ɛntɛnla azo la ati.

Kɛzi ɛbahola wɔahilehile nɔhalɛ ne mɔɔ ɛbazi ye kpalɛ ɛ?

(Nea ɛkpunli 11-12)

Kpondɛ mekɛ mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ ɛka nɔhalɛ ne nee deɛ mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ ɛka la kɛ mɔɔ Gyisɛse yɛle la (Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 15)

tu yɛ nwo kɛ yɛdua edwɛkɛ mɔɔ “nkyene wɔ nu” la azo amaa yɛaha nɔhalɛ ne wɔ adenle mɔɔ kɛyɛatievonyia ne “fɛ” la azo. (Dwobu 12:11) Ɛhye ɛyɛlɛ bahola ayɛ se. Wɔ nwonane afoa nu aleɛ nwo, bie a yɛkɛdwenle kɛ awie mɔ kpondɛ kɛ bɛdua nkyene mɔɔ yɛkulo la azo bɛnziezie bɛ aleɛ. Zɔhane ala a, bie a yɛkɛdwenle kɛ kɛzi yɛkaha edwɛkɛ la kɛyɛ awie biala fɛ a. Noko tɛ zɔ a ɔde a. Kɛ neazo la, menli mɔɔ vi amamerɛ bie mɔ anu la kɛhola kɛha bɛ adwenle dahuu nwiinwii, bɔbɔ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛlɛtendɛ bɛahile menli mɔɔ bɛle mgbanyima bɛtɛla bɛ la. Menli mɔɔ vi amamerɛ gyɛne nu la bahola abu kɛ edwɛkɛhanlɛ zɔhane ɛnle kpalɛ, bie bɔbɔ a ɔkɛzi nvonleɛ. Pɔɔlo hanle kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛnwu kɛzi ɔwɔ kɛ “yɛbua awie biala” la. Ɛhye kile kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛbua edwɛkɛ mɔɔ nkyene wɔ nu, tɛ kɛ mɔɔ yɛdayɛ yɛkulo anzɛɛ yɛ amamerɛ kile la azo, na kɛ mɔɔ yɛ tievonyia ne kulo la azo.

EZUKOALƐ NGƐLATA WƆ ADWENE NU

Nea Baebolo Nu Edwɛkɛ Ne Mɔ Wo Adwenle Nu

Baebolo ne lɛ tete edwɛkɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ kilehile debie mɔɔ hyia la. Saa yɛsusu edwɛkɛ ɛhye mɔ anwo kpalɛ yɛyɛ ye nvoninli wɔ yɛ adwenle nu a, ɔbamaa yɛade mɔɔ yɛkengye la abo kpalɛ na yɛanyia zolɛ nvasoɛ kpole. Suzu edwɛkɛ bie mɔ mɔɔ bahola aboa wɔ amaa wɔayɛ Baebolo nu edwɛkɛ nvoninli wɔ wɔ adwenle nu la anwo.

• Kenga ye dɔnkɛ. Baebolo ne mɔɔ bɛkenga ye dɔnkɛ la baha bɛ amaa bɛanwu ninyɛne mɔɔ sisile la kpalɛ. Saa bɛlɛkenga Baebolo ne bɛ abusua ne a, bɛbahola bɛamaa ninyɛne ne mɔ ayɛ kɛ mɔɔ bɛlɛsi kɛkala la ɔlua sonla ko biala mɔɔ bɛbamaa ye awienemoi gyĩnlaleɛ amaa yeagenga la azo.

• Maa nea ninyɛne mɔɔ sisile la kɛ asolo wɔ nye zo. Bɔ mɔdenle kɛ ɛbade adwenle nee nganeɛdelɛ mɔɔ menli mɔɔ wɔ nu la lale ye ali la abo. Kɛ neazo la, biza wɔ nwo kɛ: ‘Duzu ati a ahenle dendɛle anzɛɛ yɛle ninyɛne zɔhane a? Saa akee medame a mewɔ tɛnlabelɛ zɔhane anu a, anrɛɛ kɛ mebadwenle anzɛɛ nganeɛ boni a anrɛɛ mede a?’

• Senla anzɛɛ kɛlɛ nvoninli. Baebolo nu edwɛkɛ mɔɔ bɛyɛ nvoninli bɛsie bɛ nye zo la bahola aboa wɔ amaa wɔanwu ye kpalɛ na wɔahakye edwɛkɛ ne mɔ kpalɛ, saa bɔbɔ mɔɔ ɛyɛle la ɛnle nvoninli kpole mɔɔ ye wie kpalɛ a.
