Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SE NEA ABOSOM BAA WIASE MAA WOYEE ABOSOM NKETEA

ƐBERƐ bi na ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ; ɔwooo mma dubaako; na da biara sɛ ɔsɔre noaa aduane a, mmofra no wee ne nyinaa. Wɔn maame annya bi anni. Afei ɔdwen dwenii berɛ tenten, na ɔkɔɔ afum hɔ kɔbɔɔ Onyina no amanneɛ sɛ, "Mɛsoma me mma dubaako no ma wɔabɛba wo ase ha abɛtetɛ akorantoɔ; na sɛ wɔba a, bu wo mman dubaako gu wɔn so na kum me mma no." Onyina no kaa sɛ, "Matee, na mɛyɛ ama wo." Ɛna no tuu kwan kɔɔ fie; ɔka kyerɛɛ ne mma no sɛ, "Ɛwɔ sɛ mo nkɔ afuom hɔ, wɔ Onyina no ase; asafuntum wɔ hɔ, monkɔte na mommra." Mmofra no tuu kwan. Wɔkɔe kɔduruu Onyina no ase. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Number One, gyina hɔ dinn; Number Two, gyina hɔ dinn; Number Three, gyina hɔ dinn; Number Four, gyina hɔ dinn; Number Five, gyina hɔ dinn; Number Six, gyina hɔ dinn; Number Seven, gyina hɔ dinn; Number Eight, gyina hɔ dinn; Number Nine, gyina hɔ dinn; Number Ten, gyina hɔ dinn; na me ara, Number Eleven, magyina hɔ dinn." Afei Number Eleven kasa kyerɛɛ wɔn, sɛ, "Monnim ade korɔ a nti a ɛma maame kaa sɛ yɛnkɔte asankum no?" Ne nuanom no kaa sɛ, "Dabi." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Waka akyerɛ saa Onyina yi sɛ, sɛ yɛkɔ hɔ a, ɛntete mmabaa na ɛmfa mmɔ yɛn; enti mo nyinaa ntwa mmaa, na montow ngu saa Onyina yi ho." Wɔtwaa mmaa no, na wɔtotow guu Onyina no ho, pim! pen! pim! pen! (na ɛyɛ nnyegyei a ɛyɛe). Onyina no susu sɛ mmofra no aba. Ɔtwee ne mman du-baako, na ɔmaa ɛhwee fam. Ɔketewa Nɔma Du-baako no kaa sɛ, "Moahunu, sɛ yɛkɔɔ hɔ a, anka Onyina no bekum yɛn." Wɔsesaa asafuntum no na wɔde kɔmaa wɔn maame. Ɔnoaa; o! ɛhɔ ara na mmofra no wiee die (nyinaa). Wɔn maame kaa sɛ, "Ah! na asɛm yi deɛ, mentumi nnya m'ani so, enti mɛfa mmofra yi akɔma Nyankopɔn." Adeɛ kyeeɛ a adeɛ saeɛ no, ɔkɔbɔɔ Nyankopɔn amanneɛ nyinaa, sɛ, "Mmofra bi a mawo wɔn, sɛ mepɛ sɛ medidi a, menya biribi nni (ɛfiri sɛ wɔdidi ntɛmntɛm na wɔdidi dodo); kɔm rekum me; enti mesrɛ wo, ma wɔmfa mmofra no mmra kyeree no kunkum no, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mɛnya biribi adi." Nyankopɔn see sɛ, "Ampa na ɛte saa?" Ɔbaa no see sɛ, "Mekasa a me ti mu fitaa." Nyankopɔn paw asomafo, na wɔkɔtutu amoa kɛse bi, na wɔde nkutooguow (bɔtolo abubuwa) guu mu; ɔkɔfaa ɔwɔ ne ɔsebo de wɔn guu amoa no mu, na ɔde nneɛma kataa so. Afei ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔfrɛɛ mmofra no. Wɔduruu baabi a amena no daa pɛ, na Namba Eleven kaa sɛ, "Namba One, gyina hɔ dinn; Namba Two, gyina hɔ dinn; Namba Three, gyina hɔ dinn; Namba Four, gyina hɔ dinn; Namba Five, gyina hɔ dinn; Namba Six, gyina hɔ dinn; Namba Seven, gyina hɔ dinn; Namba Eight, gyina hɔ dinn; Namba Nine, gyina hɔ dinn; Namba Ten, gyina hɔ dinn; na me ara, Namba Eleven ketewa no, megyina hɔ dinn; ɛsɛ sɛ motwa wɔ ha; ɛnsɛ sɛ motwene hɔ." Ne nuanom kaa sɛ, "Adɛn, berɛ a ɛkwan trɔtrɔɔ da hɔ yi, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛfa wura no mu?" Afei, berɛ a wɔrekɔ no, na wɔn nyinaa kura nkuntuma. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Monto nua mmaa yi baako ngu kwan yi so." Wɔto gui hɔ, na ɛkɔtɔɔ mu (amena no mu), yiridi (ne nnyigyei berɛ a ɛrehwe aseɛ no). Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ, moahunu, sɛ yɛfaa hɔ a, anka yɛn nyinaa awuwu." Enti wɔfaa kwan foforo so kɔɔ Onyankopɔn nkyɛn wɔ hɔ. Na Onyankopɔn ama wɔatutu amena, (akata so,) na wɔde nkonnwa asisi so, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a mmofra no ba bɛtena so a, wɔbɛkɔ akɔhwe amena no mu. Mmofra no duruu Onyankopɔn anim. Nyankopɔn se, "Nkonnwa gu hɔ, monkɔtena so." Ɛnna Number Eleven se, "Yɛyɛ ananmusifoɔ bɛn na yɛbɛtumi atena nkonnwa a ɛyɛ fɛ sei so; enti, Me Wura, yɛrekɔtena nkyɛn ha." Ɛnna Nyankopɔn hwɛɛ mmofra no dinn; ɔsee sɛ, "Mɛsoma mmofra yi akɔ Owuo kuro mu." Adekyeeɛ a adeɛ bɛyɛɛ hann no, ɔfrɛɛ mmofra no; ɔsee sɛ, " Ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ Owuo (a ɔte) hɔ no nkyɛn, na wokɔgye sika kɔkɔɔ taasen, sika kɔkɔɔ tweapea, sika kɔkɔɔ asraborɔ, sika kɔkɔɔ syɛba, ne sika kɔkɔɔ bodua firi ne nkyɛn." Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Wone yɛn wura, baabiara a wobɛsoma yɛn no, yɛbɛkɔ." Nyankopɔn kaa sɛ, "Monkɔ." Mmofra no tuu kwan kɔɔ Owuo akuraa ase. Wɔduruu hɔ no; Owuo kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na, bere a obiara nni hɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ ɔba ha koraa no, moaba ha?" Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Yɛretutu yɛn ananmu na yɛbɛpuee ha ara kwa." Owuo kaa sɛ, "O, ɛnde ɛyɛ." Afei na Owuo wɔ mma du; ɔno ara kaa ho a, wɔyɛ dubaako. Bere a nneɛma hyɛɛ aseɛ yerae no (kyerɛ sɛ, bere a esum duriɛ no), Owuo kyekyɛɛ mmofra no mmaako mmaako de obiara maa ne mma no mu baako, bere a ɔno ara ne Number Eleven kɔhomeeɛ. Bere a esum duriɛ no, ɛnna Owuo maa ne se hyerɛnee (kɔsii sɛ ɛyɛɛ) kɔkɔɔ, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a ɔde bɛtumi akye Number Eleven. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Owu, mennaeeɛ." Owu kaa sɛ, "Da bɛn na wobɛda?" Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wode sika kɔkɔɔ taasen bɛma me ma menomi kakra a, ɛnde ebia mɛtumi ada." Na Owu kɔfaa brɛɛ no. Ɛkyɛɛ kakraabi no, Owu sɔɔ ne se mu bio, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a ɔde bɛkɔ akɔkyere Number Eleven. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Owu, mennaeeɛ." Owu kaa sɛ, "Ɛdɛn berɛ na wobɛda?" Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wode sika kɔkɔɔ asraneeɛ brɛ me a, ebia mɛda." Na Owu de brɛɛ no. Bio nso, ɛnkyɛeɛ koraa na Owu kɔkyeree Number Eleven. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Mendaee." Owuo kaa sɛ, "Da bɛn na wobɛda?" Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wokɔfa sika kɔkɔɔ tweape bɛma me na metwe kakra a, ɛbɛtumi ama mada." Owuo kɔfaa maa no. Ɛnkyɛree koraa na Owuo reyɛ akyere no. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Nana, mennaeɛ ɛ." Owuo kaa sɛ, "Ɛndeɛ, da bɛn na wobɛda?" Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Nana, sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa sika kɔkɔɔ sɛbebrɛ abrɛ me a, ɛndeɛ ebia mɛda." Na Owuo kɔe kɔfaa baeɛ. Bio, ɛno akyi kakra no, Owuo sɔre bio. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "O, Nana, mekaa sɛ mennaee." Owuo kaa sɛ, "Na da bɛn na wobɛda?" Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wokɔfa kora a ntokuro ahyɛ mu ma na wokɔsa nsuo gu mu, na wonoa aduane bi ma medi a, ɛno deɛ ebia mɛda." Owuo maa sɔneeɛ so kɔɔ asubonten no ho. Bere a ɔhwiee nsuo no (guu mu) no, nsɔneeɛ no mu tokuro maa ɛtɔreeɛ. Afei Number Eleven ka kyerɛɛ ne nuanom sɛ, "Monsɔre na mondwane nkɔ." Na wɔsɔree na wɔdwaneeɛ; na Number Eleven kɔtwitwaa borɔdeɛ dua, na ɔde kɔtwerɛɛ faako a ne nuanom dedaeɛ no, na ɔfaa ntoma de kataa wɔn so. Afei na Owuo wɔ asubɔnten no ho; na ɔrehwie nsuo. Afei Owuo Barima frɛɛ Owuo Bedia sɛ, "Hee Owuo." Ɔno nso gyee so sɛ, "Adwo." Ɔbisaa no sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na woreyɛ?" Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "Agyei, ɛnyɛ akwadaa bi na manya no yi; sɛ mereka sɛ mereka no a, ɔka sɛ, 'Mendaee'; wagye me nneɛma nyinaa, na seesei ɔse memfa sohyire na menpete nsuo." Owuo Barima kaa sɛ, "A, woyɛ akwadaa ketewa anaa; afei, sɛ wotete nhahan na wote hyɛ asɔnono no mu, na afei petepete nsuo no mu a, ɛrenyɛ yie anaa?" Owuo Bediafoɔ no kaa sɛ, "O, ɛyɛ nokorɛ paa." Ɔtetee nhahan, de hyɛɛ mu, na ɔpetee nsuo no mu kɔe. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Owuo, woaba dada? Noa aduane no." Owuo noaa aduane no; ɔhyerɛn ne se na watumi akum Number Eleven nuanom no de wɔn ayɛ aduane anoa. Berɛ a ɔkɔeɛ no, wanhwɛ wɔn yiye, na ɔno ara kunkum ne mma du no nyinaa. Adekyeeɛ no, anɔpahechehe berɛ a adeɛ bɛyɛɛ hann no, Owuo sɔre kɔtenaa ogya no ho wɔ hɔ. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Nana, hweroma bi te wo nufoɔ so." Owuo kaa sɛ, "Kɔfa prapra a ɛda hɔ no bra, na fa kum no ma me." Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Ao me Nyankopɔn; nipa kɛseɛ te sɛ wo, sɛ hweroma tena wo so a, na sikakɔkɔɔ prapra da hɔ (wode saa nnoɔma dada yi bɛyɛ adwuma), ma me nkɔfa mmra na me mmɛkum no." Owuo kaa sɛ, "Kɔfa firi r dan no mu." Number Eleven kɔe kɔfaa bae. Ɔhyɛɛ da pam huruiɛ no; wankum no. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "O, ɛnnɛ deɛ, baabi a saa huruiɛ yi bɛhome no, ɛhɔ ara na me ne no bɛhome." Afei Number Eleven kɔɔ dan no mu kɔfaa ne bɔtɔ a sikakɔkɔɔ taasenan no ne nneɛma no nyinaa da mu no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Nana Owuo, hwee rentumi nsi ananmu gye sɛ menya huruiɛ no, de no gu kotokuo yi mu, na mede brɛ wo." Number Eleven tuu kwan, yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! Ɔduruu kuro no ano; ɔkaa sɛ, "Hoo, ɛhɔ, Nana Owuo, sɛ mereka saa a firi me, nanso sɛ woanyɛ kwasea koraa a, anka mɛtumi agye wo nneɛma nyinaa afiri wo nsam; anka me nuanom a me ne wɔn baeɛ no bɛtumi anya kwan adwane; na anka mɛtumi ama wo nso akum wo mma du no nyinaa anaa? Me deɛ, mereko." Owuo kaa sɛ, "Wo, abofra a wote sɛɛ; baabiara a wobɛgye w'ahome no, ɛhɔ ara na me nso mɛgye m'ahome." Number Eleven huruii de kɔe, yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! na Owuo nso tii no. Berɛ a Number Eleven kɔe no, ɔtoaa ne nuanom mmarima no, na wɔte kwan no so. Na wɔreyɛ anomaa fidie. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Monkɔeɛ ɛ?" Owu reba, enti momma yɛnhwehwɛ kwan bi a yɛbɛfa so aguan." Afei Owu bɛtoo wɔn. Number Eleven gyee aduro no bi guu ne nuanom no so, na wɔkɔɔ onyina bi atifi. Na Owu nso gyinaa onyina no ase. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ha ara na mehunuu mmofra yi, na wɔakɔ hefa?" Ɔbaako-baako te soro; ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuanom sɛ, "Mererekɔgu nsuo agu ne so." Ne nuanom kaa sɛ, "E, ɔrehwehwɛ yɛn sɛ ɔbɛkye yɛn, na yɛadwane aba abɛtena ha, na nso woreka sɛ, 'Merekɔgu nsuo agu ne so.' " Ɔbaako-baako wantie, na ɔguguu nsuo guu Owuo so. Owu kae sɛ, "Aha, wowɔ ha yi, ɛnnɛ deɛ woahunu amane." Owu kae sɛ, "Wo, abofra a wote soro hɔ no, 'Kyere-he-ne, Kyere-he-ne'." Ɛhɔ ara na mmofra no mu baako hwee fam. "Kyere-he-ne", nea ɔtɔ so mmienu no nso hwee fam. Afei na aka Number Eleven nko ara. Owu kae sɛ, "Abofra, 'kyere-he-ne'," ɛnna Number Eleven huri sii fam, kirim! Na Owuo nso kɔɔ Onyina no so. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Wo, ɔbaa kɛseɛ pa ara, wo nso, 'kyere-he-ne'." Na Owuo nso bɔ fam, tum! na wawu. Number Eleven kɔtetee aduro, kyimii no ne nsam, na ɔpetee guu ne nuanom no so, na wɔsɔrebae. Number Eleven rebɛto aduro no atwene no, na ebi tɔɔ Owuo so, na Owuo nyanie. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Moakum me, na moanyan me nso; ɛnnɛ me ne mo bɛdi asɛm." Ɛnna wɔn nyinaa bɔɔ mu guanee prɛko pɛ, kiri! kiri! kiri! Afei Owuo tii wɔn. Berɛ a wɔrekɔ no, asubɔnten kɛseɛ bi a ayiri da hɔ. Berɛ a Number Eleven ne ne nuanom duruu hɔ no, na wɔnim asubɔ, enti wɔboroo asu no twaa kɔɔ baabi foforɔ no. Number Eleven nko ara na na onnim asubɔ. Mmofra no gyinaa faako baako no; wɔsuiee, na wɔsuiee, na wɔsuiee; wɔn anomu honoe. Na Number Eleven deɛ, ɔdanee ɔboɔ. Owuo duruu asutene no ho. Ɔkaa sɛ, "O, mmofra yi, monnyina hɔ, momma menkɔfa ɔboɔ mmɛto mmɔ wɔn anofafa no." Owuo, berɛ a ɔhwɛɛ fam no, ɔhunuu sɛ ɔboɔ bi da hɔ; ɔfaa no toee; berɛ a ɔboɔ no retu kɔ no, ɛkaa sɛ, "Mframa, fa me kɔsi faako no." Ɛkɔsii faako no. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Meni o." Owuo kaa sɛ, "A, abɔfra yi! Menni asɛm foforɔ biara a mede bɛka akyerɛ wo, na mmom deɛ mewɔ ka kyerɛ wo ne sɛ: kɔ na tena fie, na sakyera bɛyɛ abosom no baako, na sɛ obiara a mepɛ sɛ megye no ba baabi a wowɔ hɔ a, wobɛbɔ me amanneɛ; sɛ mepɛ a, megyaa no ama wo de no akyɛ wo; nanso deɛ mepɛ (sɛ ananmu) no, ɛsɛ sɛ wogye ma me." (Kyerɛ sɛ, afɔrebɔdeɛ.) Ɛno ne kwan a Abosom (lesser -anyame) baa wiase. Wɔyɛ abofra ketewa Number Eleven no asefo.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, ebi nkɔ na ebi mmra.
