Yɛnni aseɛ koraa, yɛnni aseɛ koraa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokorɛ)

SƐNEA ABOSOM BAWIE WI ASE

Ɔbaa bi na ɔwɔ hɔ; ɔwoo mma dubaako; na da biara sɛ ɔsɔre noaa aduane a, mmofra no we nyinaa. Na wɔn maame nnya bi nni. Afei ɔdwenee ho kɔɔ akyiri, na ɔkɔɔ afum na ɔkɔbɔɔ Onyina amanneɛ sɛ, "Mɛsoma me mma dubaako no sɛ wɔmmra w'ase ha mmɛtetew afa; na sɛ wɔba a, bu wo mman dubaako na fa kum me mma no." Onyina no kaa sɛ, "Matee, na mɛyɛ ama wo." Maame no tuu kwan kɔɔ fie; ɔka kyerɛɛ ne mma no sɛ, "Monkɔ afum, Onyina no ase; afa bi wɔ hɔ, monkɔtetew na mommra." Mmofra no tuu kwan. Wɔkɔe kɔduruu Onyina no hɔ. Dubaako kaa sɛ, "Baako, gyina hɔ dinn; Mmienu, gyina hɔ dinn; Mmiɛnsa, gyina hɔ dinn; Ɛnan, gyina hɔ dinn; Enum, gyina hɔ dinn; Nsia, gyina hɔ dinn; Nson, gyina hɔ dinn; Nwɔtwe, gyina hɔ dinn; Nkron, gyina hɔ dinn; Edu, gyina hɔ dinn; na me ara, Dubaako, magyina hɔ dinn." Afei Dubaako kasa kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Monnim ade titiriw nti a maame see yɛnkɔtetew afa no?" Ne nuanom no kaa sɛ, "Dabi." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Waka akyerɛ Onyina yi sɛ, sɛ yɛkɔ hɔ a, ɛmmu mman na ɛmfa mmɔ yɛn; enti mo nyinaa montwitwa mmaa, na mompento ngu Onyina yi ho." Wɔtwitwaa mmaa no, na wɔto guu Onyina no ho, pim! pen! pim! pen! Onyina no dwenee sɛ mmofra no aba. Ɔbui ne mman dubaako, na ɔmaa ɛhwee fam. Dubaako ketewa no kaa sɛ, "Moahunu, sɛ yɛkɔɔ hɔ a, anka Onyina no akum yɛn." Wɔsesaa afa no na wɔde kɔmaa wɔn maame. Ɔnoae; o! prɛko pɛ na mmofra no awe (nyinaa). Wɔn maame kaa sɛ, "Aane! asɛm yi deɛ, mintumi nnyina ano, enti mɛfa mmofra yi akɔma Nyankopɔn." Adeɛ kyeeɛ a adeɛ saeɛ no, ɔkɔbɔɔ Nyankopɔn amanneɛ nyinaa, sɛ, "Mmofra bi a mawo wɔn no, sɛ mepɛ sɛ medidi a, minnya biribi nni; ɛkɔm rekum me; enti mesrɛ wo, ma wɔmmɛfa mmofra no nkɔkum wɔn, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a mɛnya biribi adi." Nyankopɔn see sɛ, "Ampa ara na ɛte saa?" Ɔbaa no see sɛ, "Merekasa a m'adwene mu da hɔ fann." Nyankopɔn yii asomafoɔ, na wɔkɔtutu amena kɛseɛ bi, na wɔde mprɛte (abubuwa) guu mu; ɔkɔfaa ɔwɔ ne ɔsebɔ nso de wɔn guu amena no mu, na ɔkataa so. Afei somafoɔ no kɔfrɛɛ mmofra no. Wɔduruu faako a amena no da no ara pɛ, na Number Eleven see sɛ, "Number One, gyina hɔ dinn; Number Two, gyina hɔ dinn; Number Three, gyina hɔ dinn; Number Four, gyina hɔ dinn; Number Five, gyina hɔ dinn; Number Six, gyina hɔ dinn; Number Seven, gyina hɔ dinn; Number Eight, gyina hɔ dinn; Number Nine, gyina hɔ dinn; Number Ten, gyina hɔ dinn; na me nso, Number Eleven ketewa yi, magyina hɔ dinn; ɛsɛ sɛ motwa ha; ɛnsɛ sɛ motwa ha." Ne nuanom see sɛ, "Adɛn, berɛ a ɛkwan tɛtrɛɛtɛ da ha no, yɛnkɔfa kwaeɛ mu?" Na berɛ a wɔrenam rekɔ no, na wɔn nyinaa kura mporanporan. Number Eleven see sɛ, "Monto nkoran yi baako ngu ɛkwan yi so." Wɔto gui hɔ no, na ɛhwee ase kɔɔ mu (amena no mu), yiridi (ne gyeɛ a ɛgyeeɛ berɛ a ɛrehwe ase no). Number Eleven see sɛ, "Hwɛ, moahu, sɛ yɛfaa ha a, anka yɛn nyinaa awuwu." Enti wɔfaa mfifikwan so kɔɔ Nyankopɔn nkyɛn wɔ hɔ. Na Nyankopɔn ama wɔatutu amena, (akakata so,) na wɔde nkonnwa asisi so, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a sɛ mmofra no ba bɛtena so a, wɔbɛkɔ akɔhwe amena no mu. Mmofra no baa Nyankopɔn anim. Onyankopɔn kaa sɛ, "Nkonnwa gu hɔ no, monkɔtena so." Ɛnna Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Yɛyɛ ananmusifoɔ bɛn na yɛbɛtumi atena nkonnwa a ɛyɛ fɛ sei so; enti, Nana, yɛrekɔtena nkyɛn ha." Ɛhɔ ara na Onyankopɔn hwɛɛ mmofra no dinn; ɔkaa sɛ, "Mɛsoma mmofra yi akɔ Owuo kuro mu." Adeɛ kyeeɛ no, ɔfrɛɛ mmofra no; ɔkaa sɛ, "Monkɔ Owuo a ɔwɔ faako no nkyɛn, na monkɔgye sika tawa, sika tweapea, sika asrabaadaka, sika sennɛ, ne sika bɔddua mfi ne nsam." Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Wone yɛn wura, baabiara a wobɛsoma yɛn no, yɛbɛkɔ." Onyankopɔn kaa sɛ, "Monkɔ." Mmofra no tuu kwan kɔɔ Owuo kuro mu. Wɔduruu hɔ no; Owuo kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na, aberɛ a obiara nni hɔ a ɔfata sɛ ɔba ha no, moaba ha?" Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Yɛrenantem na yɛbaa ha kɛkɛ." Owuo kaa sɛ, "O, ɛnde ɛyɛ." Afei na Owuo wɔ mma du; ɔno ara kaa ho a, na wɔyɛ dubaako. Adeɛ saeɛ no, Owuo kyɛɛ mmofra no baakobaako maa ne mma no nyinaa, na ɔno ankasa ne Number Eleven kɔdaeɛ. Adeɛ duruii pɛ, na Owuo ma ne se hyerɛnee kɔkɔɔ sɛdeɛ ɔbɛtumi de akyere Number Eleven. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Owuo, mennaeeɛ." Owuo kaa sɛ, "Da bɛn na wobɛda?" Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Sɛ woma me sika tawa ma mennom kakra a, anka mɛtumi ada." Na Owuo kɔfaa maa no. Kakra akyi no, Owuo san ma ne se hyerɛnee bio, sɛdeɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔkyere Number Eleven. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Owuo, mennaeeɛ." Owuo kaa sɛ, "Da bɛn na wobɛda?" Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wode sika asrabaadaka bɛbrɛ me a, anka mɛtumi ada." Na Owuo de brɛɛ no. Bio, ɛnkyɛeɛ koraa na Owuo kɔɔ sɛ ɔrekɔkyere Number Eleven. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Mennaeeɛ." Owuo kaa sɛ, "Da bɛn na wobɛda?" Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wokɔfa sika tweapea brɛ me ma mente kakra a, anka mɛtumi ada." Owuo kɔfaa maa no. Ɛberɛ kakra bi twaam no, na Owuo rebɛkyere no. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Nana, mennaeeɛ." Owuo kaa sɛ, "Ɛnde, da bɛn na wobɛda?" Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Nana, sɛ wokɔfa sika sennɛ brɛ me a, anka mɛtumi ada." Na Owuo kɔe kɔfaa baeɛ. Bio, ɛnkyɛeɛ koraa na Owuo sɔre bio. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "O, Nana, mekaa sɛ mennaeɛ." Owuo kaa sɛ, "Na da bɛn na wobɛtɔ nko?" Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wokɔfa kora a ntokuro ahyɛ mu ma na wokɔsa nsuo gu mu, na wonoo aduane bi ma me di a, ɛnneɛ ebia mɛda." Owuo maa sɔneeɛ so na ɔkɔɔ asubɔnten no ho. Berɛ a ɔsaa nsuo no (guu mu no), sɔneeɛ no mu ntokuro no maa ɛsene faa mu kɔe. Afei Number Eleven ka kyerɛɛ ne nuanom sɛ, "Monsɔre na monnwane nkɔ." Na wɔsɔreeɛ na wɔdwaneeɛ; na Number Eleven kɔtwitwaa bɔrɔdeɛ dua, na ɔde kɔguu faako a ne nuanom dedaeɛ no, na ɔfaa ntoma de kataa wɔn so. Afei Owuo wɔ asubɔnten no ho; na ɔresa nsuo. Afei Owuo Barima frɛɛ Owuo Baa sɛ, "Hee, Owuo." Ɔgyee so sɛ, "Adwo." Ɔbisaa sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na woreyɛ?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ao, ɛnyɛ abofra ketewa bi na manya no yi; sɛ mereka sɛ mereka ne mu a, ɔse, 'Mennaeɛ'; wagye me nneɛma nyinaa, na afei nso ɔse menfa sɔneeɛ mmɛsa nsuo." Owuo Barima kaa sɛ, "Ah, woyɛ abofra ketewa anaa; afei, sɛ wotete nhahan na wode gu sɔneeɛ no mu, na wosa nsuo no a, ɛrenyɛ yie anaa?" Owuo Baa kaa sɛ, "O, nokware mpo." Ɔtetee nhahan, de guu mu, na ɔsaa nsuo no na ɔkɔe. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Owuo, woaba dada? Noo aduane no." Owuo noo aduane no; ɔhyerɛn ne se sɛ ɔrekɔku Number Eleven nuanom de wɔn ayɛ aduane. Berɛ a ɔkɔeɛ no, wanhwɛ wɔn yie, na ɔno ara kunkum ne mma du no nyinaa. Adekyeeɛ no, anɔpahechehe berɛ a adeɛ bɛgyee hann no, Owuo sɔreeɛ, na ɔbɛtenaa gya no ho. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Nana, huri te wo nufoɔ so." Owuo kaa sɛ, "Kɔfa baha a ɛda hɔ no bra, na ku no ma me." Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Agyei me Nyankopɔn; onipa kɛseɛ te sɛ wo, sɛ huri tena wo so a, na sika mfaateɛ da hɔ a, (wode baha dedaw yi na ɛbɛyɛ), ma menkɔfa mmra na membɛku no." Owuo kaa sɛ, "Kɔfa firi pia no mu bra." Number Eleven kɔfaa baeɛ. Ɔhyɛɛ da pamoo huri no; wanku no. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "O, ɛnnɛ deɛ, faako a saa huri yi bɛkɔ akɔgye n'ahome no, ɛhɔ ara na me ne no bɛgye yɛn ahome." Afei Number Eleven kɔɔ pia no mu na ɔkɔfaa ne kotokuo a sika taasen kɛseɛ no ne nneɛma no nyinaa gu mu no. Ɔsee sɛ, "Nana Owuo, biribiara remmɔ me kɔn gye sɛ menya hurui no, mede gu bɔtɔ yi mu, na mede brɛ wo." Number Eleven tuu mmirika, yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! Ɔduruu kuro no ano; ɔsee sɛ, "Hee, Nana Owuo, sekan me kwan, na sɛ wonyɛ kwaseabrone a, anka mɛtumi agye wo nneɛma nyinaa; anka me nuanom a me ne wɔn bae no bɛnya baabi adwane; na anka mɛtumi ama wo nso akum wo mma du no nyinaa? Me deɛ, mereko." Owuo see sɛ, "Wo, abofra a wote sei; baabiara a wobɛgye w'ahome no, ɛhɔ ara na mɛgye m'ahome." Number Eleven hurui guu mu, yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! na Owuo nso tii no. Bere a Number Eleven rekɔ no, ɔtoo ne nuanom, na na wɔtete kwan no so. Na wɔreyɛ anoma fidie. Number Eleven see sɛ, "Monkɔɔe ara ni? Owuo reba, enti momma yɛnhwehwɛ kwan bi nwane." Afei Owuo bɛduruu wɔn so. Number Eleven faa aduro petee ne nuanom so, na wɔkɔɔ onyaadua bi atifi. Na Owuo gyinaa onyaadua no ase. Ɔsee sɛ, "Ɛha pɛɛ na mehunuu mmofra yi, na ɛhe na wɔakɔ?" Na Number Eleven te soro hɔ; ɔsee ne nuanom sɛ, "Merekɔdwanso agu ne so." Ne nuanom see sɛ, "E, ɔrehwehwɛ yɛn sɛ ɔbɛkye yɛn, na yɛadwane abɛtena ha yi, na wo nso woresee sɛ, 'Merekɔdwanso agu ne so.' " Number Eleven antie, na ɔdwansoo guu Owuo so. Owuo see sɛ, "Aha, mowɔ ha yi, ɛnnɛ deɛ moahunu amane." Owuo see sɛ, "Wo, abofra a wote soro hɔ no, 'Kyere-he-ne, Kyere-he-ne'." Ɛhɔ ara na mmofra no mu baako hwee fam. "Kyere-he-ne", nea ɔtɔ so mmienu no nso hwee fam. Afei aka Number Eleven nko ara. Owuo see sɛ, "Abofra, 'kyere-he-ne'," na Number Eleven hurui sii fam, kirim! Na Owuo nso kɔɔ onyaadua no so. Number Eleven see sɛ, "Wo, ɔbaa kɛseɛ kɛseɛ yi, wo nso, 'kyere-he-ne'." Na Owuo nso bɛhwee fam, tum! na wawu. Number Eleven kɔtetee aduro, kyimii wɔ ne nsam, na ɔpetee ne nuanom so, na wɔsɔreɛ. Number Eleven rekɔto aduro no agu no, ɛbi bɔ guu Owuo so, na Owuo nyanee. Ɔsee sɛ, "Woakum me, na woanyan me nso; ɛnnɛ me ne wo bɛdi asɛm." Ɛnna wɔn nyinaa bɔɔ mu tuu mmirika prɛko pɛ, kiri! kiri! kiri! Afei na Owuo reti wɔn. Bere a wɔrekɔ no, asubɔnten kɛseɛ bi a ayiri da hɔ. Bere a Number Eleven ne ne nuanom duruu hɔ no, na wɔnim asuguare, enti wɔguaree faa asu no kɔɔ baabi foforo no. Number Eleven nko ara na na onnim asuguare. Mmofra no gyinaa faako a ɛwɔ baabi foforo no; wɔsuiee, na wɔsuiee, na wɔsuiee; wɔn anomu hohonii. Number Eleven deɛ, ɔdanee ɔboɔ. Owuo duruu asubɔnten no ho. Ɔkaa sɛ, "O, mmofra yi, mogyina hɔ, ma menya ɔboɔ bi mmɔ ngu wɔn anom a ahohono no so." Owuo, bere a ɔhwɛɛ fam no, ɔhunuu sɛ ɔboɔ bi da hɔ; ɔfaa so na ɔtowee; bere a ɔboɔ no retu kɔ no, ɛkaa sɛ, "Mframa, fa me na kɔde me si baabi foforo no." Ɛkɔsii baabi foforo no. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Me nie." Owuo kaa sɛ, "A, abofra yi! Menni asɛm biara bio a me ne wo bɛkasa ho, na mmom deɛ mewɔ ka kyerɛ wo ne sɛ: kɔ na kɔtena fie, na dan wo ho bɛyɛ bosom baako, na sɛ obi a mepɛ sɛ mefa no ba faako a wowɔ a, wobɛbɔ me amanneɛ; sɛ mepɛ a, megyaa no na mede no ama wo sɛ akyɛdeɛ; nanso deɛ mepɛ (sɛ nsesaamu) no, ɛsɛ sɛ wogye ma me." (Kyerɛ sɛ, akyɛdeɛ a wɔbɔ afɔdeɛ.) Saa na ɛbaa sɛ Abosom baa wiase. Wɔfiri abofra kumaa Number Eleven ase.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, ebi nkɔ na ebi mmra.
