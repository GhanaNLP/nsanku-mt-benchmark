WƆ-AFII ŊMƐNƆ ŊMALA KƐHA 2026:

Ajɔɔ mɛi ni le amɛmumɔŋ hiamɔ nii.

—MAT. 5:3.

YEOVA ebɔ gbɔmɛi kɛ nibii komɛi ni he hiaa amɛ lɛɛlɛŋ. Ákɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, koni wɔya nɔ wɔhiɛ kã lɛ, wɔhe miihia niyenii, atade, kɛ shĩa. Kɛ́ wɔbɔle nibii nɛɛ ateŋ ekome po, be kukuoo ko po lɛ, shihilɛ batsɔɔ kaahii. Kɛfata bɔ ni ebɔ wɔ kɛ nibii nɛɛ ahe hiamɔ lɛ he lɛ, Yeova ebɔ wɔ kɛ mumɔŋ hiamɔ nɔ ko hu. (Kanemɔ Mateo 5:3.) Kɛ́ wɔmiisumɔ ni wɔná miishɛɛ diɛŋtsɛ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔle nakai hiamɔ nɔ lɛ he nii ni no sɛɛ lɛ wɔya nɔ wɔha enɛ ahe gbɛfaŋ kɛha wɔhe.

2 Mɛni efeɔ akɛ wɔbaale akɛ wɔbana “sunsumŋɔɔ nibii ahe miishɛɛ”? Wiemɔ ni kɛ enɛ je kpo yɛ wiemɔ ko mli nɛɛ tsɔɔ akɛ mɔ ko baa ehe shi akɛ lumɔlɔ kɛha sunsum lɛ. Nɛkɛ wiemɔ nɛɛ haa mɔ susuɔ nɔ ko ni yɔɔ gbeyei he. Feemɔ he mfoniri akɛ gbɔmɔ ko ewo atade foji, ni ebu shi yɛ maŋ kpo ko nɔ. Ehe eba shi yɛ niyenii ni ebɛ hewɔ, mɛi nyɛɔ lɛ yɛ egbɔmɔtsoŋ hewɔ, ni hulu kɛ fɛi ni mli wa miiye lɛ. Lumɔlɔ nɛɛ le he pɛpɛɛpɛ akɛ ehe miiahia yelikɛbuamɔ bɔni afee ni eshihilɛ awo hi. Nakai nɔŋŋ mɔ ko ni le nibii ahe miishɛɛ yɛ sunsumŋɔɔ gbɛfaŋ—lumɔlɔ kɛha sunsum lɛ—baahiɛ yelikɛbuamɔ bɔni afee ni eshihilɛ awo hi. No hewɔ lɛ, efeɔ oya kɛkaseɔ nibii babaoo ni Yehowa kɛhãa mɛi ni sumɔɔ Lɛ lɛ.

3 Yɛ nikasemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ, klɛŋklɛŋ lɛ, wɔbaasusu nɔ ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔkase lɛ he

1. Mɛni ji nibii titri ni he hiaa wɔ ni Yehowa bɔ wɔ kɛwo wɔmli? (Mateo 5:3)

2. Mɛni nɔkwɛmɔnɔ obaakɛtsɔɔ nɔ ni kɛ́ akɛ mɔ ko “le” akɛ “mumɔŋ nibii ahe miihia lɛ” lɛ tsɔɔ?

3. Mɛni he wɔbaagba yɛ nɛkɛ sane nɛɛ mli?

4 Be ko lɛ, Foinike yoo ko tee Yesu ŋɔɔ. Efɔŋ mumɔ ko eye ebi yoo lɛ awui waa. (Mat. 15:21-28) Yoo lɛ kula shi yɛ Yesu hiɛ ni ekpa lɛ fai ni eye ebua lɛ. Foinike yoo nɛɛ jie sui kpakpai kpo be ni etee Yesu ŋɔɔ lɛ. Nyɛhaa wɔsusua enɛɛmɛi ekomɛi ahe.

5 Foinikia yoo lɛ jie heshiba diɛŋtsɛ kpo. Mɛni hewɔ wɔbaanyɛ wɔkɛɛ nakai? Ejaakɛ ewerɔ hooo beni Yesu kɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ ko tsu nii ni tamɔ nɔ ni ekɛ lɛ to gbee bibioo he, ekolɛ kooloo he ko ni Jeŋmajiaŋnyo lɛ hiɛɔ lɛ yɛ shĩa tamɔ weku kooloo lɛ. Te obaafee onii oyaha tɛŋŋ yɛ Yesu wiemɔi kɛ enifeemɔi lɛ ahe? Ani obaanu he akɛ aje ohe kɛkɛ ni okpa eyelikɛbuamɔ taomɔ? Jeee nakai Foinikia yoo lɛ fee enii. Ejie jeee heshiba pɛ kpo, shi moŋ tswaa ni ebaa emɔ shi hu. Enɛ jeɔ kpo faŋŋ yɛ bɔ ni etee nɔ ekpa Yesu fai koni eye ebua lɛ lɛ mli. Mɛni tsirɛ lɛ ni efee nakai? Ehe ni ehe Yesu eye lɛ. Anɔkwale mli lɛ, yoo nɛɛ hemɔkɛyeli tsirɛ Yesu aahu akɛ efee nɔ ko ni yɔɔ naakpɛɛ. Eyɛ mli akɛ etsɔ hiɛ ekɛɛ akɛ “Israel we lɛ mli toi ni elaaje lɛ” pɛ hewɔ atsu lɛ moŋ, shi Yesu jie mumɔ fɔŋ lɛ kɛje Jeŋmaji anɔ yoo nɛɛ biyoo lɛ mli.

TAMƆ PETRO—HÃ O MUMƆŊ NIYENII ANYA AJÕ BO KWA

7 Susumɔ bɔfo Petro nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ he. Eji Yudafoi lɛ ateŋ mɔ ko ni tsɔ hiɛ eyɔse akɛ Yesu ji Mesia lɛ—mɔ ni Yehowa kɛmiitsu nii kɛmiilɛ E-webii lɛ kɛ “naanɔ wala wiemɔi.” (Yoh. 6:66-68) Dani Yesu ni atee lɛ shi lɛ baaku ehiɛ kɛya ŋwɛi lɛ, ekɛ nɛkɛ gbɛnaa nii nɛɛ wo Petro dɛŋ: “Lɛɛmɔ mi-tooi bibii lɛ.” (Yoh. 21:17) Petro tsu nakai gbɛnaa nii lɛ he nii kɛ anɔkwayeli, ni Yehowa tsu ehe nii po kɛŋma woji enyɔ ni batsɔmɔ Biblia lɛ fã. Shi Petro hu he hiaa ni elɛ ehe kɛ mumɔŋ niyenii. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, ekase woji ni akɛ mumɔ tsirɛ bɔfo Paulo ni eŋma tsutsu ko lɛ. Petro kpɛlɛ nɔ akɛ Paulo nibii ni eŋma lɛ ekomɛi ahe “wa akɛ aaashishii.” (2 Pet. 3:15, 16) Shi eka shi faŋŋ akɛ Petro ya nɔ etsu he nii, ni eyɛ hemɔkɛyeli akɛ Yehowa baaye abua lɛ ni enu “niyenii ni wa” ni akɛ mumɔ tsirɛ Paulo kɛfã efɔ̃ shi yɛ ewoji lɛ amli lɛ shishi ni ekɛtsu nii.—Kanemɔ Hebribii 5:14–6:1.

8 Petro tsɔɔ akɛ eyɛ hemɔkɛyeli yɛ Yehowa mli kɛtsɔ gbɛtsɔɔmɔ ni enine shɛ nɔ lɛ ni ebo toi lɛ nɔ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, be ni Petro yɔɔ ŋshɔnaa maŋ ni ji Yopa mli lɛ, ena ninaa ko ni mli lɛ Nyɔŋmɔ bɔfo ko kɛɛ lɛ akɛ egbe kooloi ni Mose Mla lɛ tsɔɔ akɛ amɛhe bɛ nɔŋŋ lɛ eye. Kɛ́ Yudafoi nu gbɛtsɔɔmɔ nɛɛ, ebaagba amɛnaa waa. Klɛŋklɛŋ kwraa lɛ, Petro kɛɛ: “Dabi, Nuŋtsɔ, ejaakɛ miyeko nɔ ko nɔ ko ni he bɛ nɔŋŋ kɛ nɔ ko ni etswalɔ pɛŋ.” Kɛkɛ ni akɛɛ lɛ akɛ: “Kpa nibii ni Nyɔŋmɔ etsɔɔ amɛ he nɔŋŋ lɛ amli fite ni otsɛɔ amɛ nakai.” (Bɔfoi 10:9-15) Petro nu shishi. Mɛni tsɔɔ nakai? Ninaa lɛ sɛɛ etsɛɛɛ nɔŋŋ ni hii etɛ ni maŋsɛɛnyo Kornelio tsu lɛ bashɛ, ni amɛfɔ̃ Petro nine koni eba ebagba amɛnuŋtsɔ lɛ nɔ ko. Kɛ́ eji tsutsu lɛ, gbi ko gbi ko Petro kpɛlɛŋ akɛ ebaateŋ maŋsɛɛnyo shĩa mli. Ejaakɛ Yudafoi buɔ maŋsɛɛbii akɛ mɛi ni he bɛ nɔŋŋ. (Bɔfoi 10:28, 29) Shi Petro kpɛlɛ la hee ni Yehowa kɛhã lɛ lɛ nɔ amrɔ nɔŋŋ. (Abɛi 4:18) Eshiɛ ehã Kornelio kɛ eshĩabii lɛ, ni ena miishɛɛ akɛ ena akɛ amɛkpɛlɛ anɔkwale lɛ nɔ, amɛnine shɛ mumɔ krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ nɔ, ni abaptisi amɛ.—Bɔfoi 10:44-48.

9 Taakɛ Petro lɛ, esa akɛ wɔyeɔ Nyɔŋmɔ Wiemɔ lɛ mli fufɔ, aloo anɔkwalei ninaa lɛ daa. Esa akɛ wɔhã ŋɔɔmɔ hu ba wɔmii kɛhã

7. Mɛni nitsumɔ akɛwo Petro dɛŋ, shi mɛni he hiaa ni efee hu? Tsɔɔ mli. (Hebribii 5:14–6:1)

(Rom. 11:33; Kpo. 4:11) Petro nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ tsɔɔ wɔ nɔkroko hu: Kɛ́ wɔná tsakemɔi yɛ Nyɔŋmɔ Wiemɔ lɛ shishinumɔ mli lɛ, ehe miihia ni wɔtsake wɔ susumɔ kɛ wɔ nifeemɔi amrɔ nɔŋŋ. Kɛkɛ pɛ wɔbaaya nɔ wɔná mumɔŋ niyenii jogbaŋŋ ni wɔfee nɔ ni he yɔɔ sɛɛ kɛha Yehowa.

TAMƆ PAULO—YAA NƆ NI OWO MUMƆŊ ATADEE NI SA OHE PƆPƆƆPƆ

10 Kɛkɛ ni wɔɔtsɔ kɛsa Nyɔŋmɔ hiɛ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔkɛ Yehowa gbɛjianɔtoo kroko atsu nii: mumɔŋ atade. Mɛni wɔtsɔɔ kɛkɛ ni wɔwie nɛkɛ? Bɔfo Paulo ŋma akɛ esa akɛ “wɔje su gbɛtsɛ lɛ” kɛwo he, ni wɔkɛ “su hee lɛ” awo wɔhe. (Kanemɔ Kolosebii 3:8-10.) Su nɛɛ kɛhewoo baanyɛ afee nɔ ko ni mli wawa daa. Susumɔ Paulo diɛŋtsɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ he okwɛ. Kɛjɛ egbekɛbiiashi tɔ̃ɔ lɛ, ebɔ mɔdɛŋ kɛkpo Nyɔŋmɔ he miishɛɛ. (Gal. 1:14; Fil. 3:4, 5) Shi ebɛ Nyɔŋmɔ yiŋtoo lɛ he nilee krɔŋŋ, no hewɔ lɛ, eyɛ mumɔŋ shihilɛ ni ehiii kwraa mli. Klɛŋklɛŋ kɛ Kristo tsɔɔmɔi lɛ ahe nilee ni ebɛ, ni hefɛo kɛfa mli lɛ, ha efee “mɔ ko ni bɛ bulɛ kwraa” ni ewo su gbohii ahe atade.—1 Tim. 1:13.

10. Mɛni mumɔŋ atade kpakpa woo biɔ? (Kolosebii 3:8-10)

11 Dani Paul baatsɔ Kristofonyo lɛ, emlifuɔ mra. Saneyelii ko ni yɔɔ Bɔfoi lɛ Asaji lɛ amli lɛ tsɔɔ akɛ, Paul mlifu Yesu kaselɔi lɛ ahe aahu akɛ, e-“tswá emli shi akɛ ebaawa amɛ yi ni egbe amɛ.” (Bɔf. 9:1) Be ni Paul batsɔ Kristofonyo sɛɛ lɛ, ekã shi faŋŋ akɛ ebɔ mɔdɛŋ kpele koni eje su gbonyo nɛɛ eba emli. (Efe. 4:22, 31) Kɛ̃lɛ, be ni sane ba Paul kɛ Barnaba teŋ lɛ, emli “wo la naakpa.” (Bɔf. 15:37-39) Enɛ ji tɔtɔfeemɔ kɛha Paul, shi ekpaaa pai. Eya nɔ ‘eŋmɛ etsuishi kɛha egbɔmɔtso lɛ’—e kɛ ehegbɔjɔmɔi lɛ wu—koni eya nɔ ená Yehowa kpatshimɔ.—1 Kor. 9:27.

12 Paulo nyɛ eye efee nibii kpakpai kɛtsɔ sui gbohii ni ewie he lɛ ajeemɔ kɛ sui hee lɛ awoomɔ nɔ, ejaakɛ ekɛ ehe efɔ̃ɔɔ lɛ diɛŋtsɛ ehewalɛ nɔ. (Fil. 4:13) Tamɔ Petro nɔŋŋ lɛ, Paulo kɛ ehe fɔ̃ “hewalɛ ni Nyɔŋmɔ kɛhaa” lɛ nɔ. (1 Pet. 4:11) Kɛbashi amrɔ nɛɛ, be komɛi yɛ ni Paulo nuɔ he akɛ eye nkɔmɔ. Shi kɛ́ enijiaŋ je wui lɛ, ejwɛŋɔ nibii kpakpai ni eŋwɛi Tsɛ lɛ efee kɛha lɛ lɛ anɔ, ni enɛ wo lɛ hewalɛ koni eya nɔ ebɔ mɔdɛŋ.—Rom. 7:21-25.

13 Wɔbaanyɛ wɔkase Paulo kɛtsɔ le ni wɔbaale akɛ bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni wɔsɔmɔ Yehowa kɛjɛ shishi gbii kɛbaa nɛɛ, ehe miihia ni wɔya nɔ wɔbɔ mɔdɛŋ wɔje gbɔmɔtso momo lɛ wɔfɔ shi ni wɔwo gbɔmɔtso hee lɛ—mumɔŋ atade ni Nyɔŋmɔ kɛhaa lɛ. Kɛ́ wɔkɛ nɔ ko ni gblaoɔ wɔ sɛɛ kpe—tamɔ

(Kwɛmɔ kuku 18)

wɔmi mli fu loo wɔkɛ mɔ ko wie yɛ gbɛ ni ehiii nɔ—ehiaaa ni wɔbu wɔhe akɛ mɛi ni efite. Moŋ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔya nɔ wɔtiu wɔoti ni ji ni wɔtsake bɔ ni wɔsusuɔ nii ahe kɛ bɔ ni wɔfeɔ nii lɛ sɛɛ. (Rom. 12:1, 2; Efe. 4:24) Shi bɛ kɛlɛ, beni wɔfeɔ nakai lɛ, esa akɛ wɔkai anɔkwale ko ni he hiaa waa: Ekãaa shi akɛ diɛŋtsɛ atadei ni atsaa mli koni esa wɔ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔtsake wɔhe bɔ ni wɔbaasa mumɔŋ tadei ni Yehowa kɛhaa lɛ mli. Nilee yɛ mli kɛkɛ akɛ wɔtsake gbɔmɔ ni wɔji lɛ koni wɔnyɛ wɔba wɔjeŋ yɛ Nyɔŋmɔ taomɔ nii lɛ anaa.

TAMƆ DAVID—HIƐ MUMƆƆ ŊSƆƆ LEEMƆ SHISHI NI ASHWERƆ

14 Kɛkɛ nɛɛ, bɔ ni afee ni wɔná miishɛɛ diɛŋtsɛ lɛ, ehe miihia ni wɔná nɔ ko ni fe mumɔŋ niyenii kɛ mumɔŋ atadei. Ehe miihia ni wɔná mumɔŋ shia hu. Mɛni ji enɛ, ni mɛni wɔbaanyɛ wɔfee koni wɔyana nɔ wɔhi eshishi?

14-15. Mɛni gbɛ nɔ Yehowa yeɔ ebuaa etsuji koni amɛbɛŋkɛ lɛ kpaakpa? (Lala 27:5) (Kwɛmɔ yitso he mfoniri lɛ hu.)

15 Maŋtsɛ David wie mumɔŋ hebuu he nɔ ni Yehowa kɛhãa lɛ he. (Kanemɔ Lala 27:5.) Yehowa buɔ etsuji ahe kɛjɛɔ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ kɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni baanyɛ afite ahemɔkɛyeli ni yɔɔ mli kɛhã lɛ lɛ kwraa lɛ he. Mɛni Yehowa feɔ kɛbuɔ etsuji ahe kɛjɛɔ naagbai ni baanyɛ ajɛ kpo aba amɛnɔ lɛ he? Ewo shi akɛ tawuu nɔ ko nɔ ko ni akɛbaawu kɛshi amɛ lɛ eŋɔɔŋ mli. (Lala 34:7; Yes. 54:17) Eyɛ mli akɛ Satan kɛ ewebii lɛ yɛ hewalɛ lolo moŋ, shi amɛnyɛŋ amɛhã wɔhe ahira wɔ kɛya naanɔ. Kɛ́ amɛgbe wɔ po lɛ, Yehowa baahã wɔshihilɛ lɛ ekoŋŋ yɛ gbohiiashitee lɛ mli. (1 Kor. 15:55-57; Kpo. 21:3, 4) Yehowa yeɔ ebuaa wɔ hu koni wɔnyɛ wɔdamɔ tsuiyeli ni yɔɔ wɔmli ni baanyɛ afite wɔ kɛya naanɔ lɛ naa. (Abɛi 12:25; Mat. 6:27-29) Kɛkpe he lɛ, wɔ-Tsɛ ni sumɔɔ wɔ lɛ hãa wɔ mumɔŋ weku ni ji nyɛmimɛi hii kɛ yei ni fiɔ wɔsɛɛ kɛ asafoŋ onukpai ni kwɛɔ wɔ nɔ lɛ. “Gbɔmɔ kɔbɔi ji mi!”—ROM. 7:24.

MƐNI baa ojwɛŋmɔ mli kɛ́ osusu bɔfo Paulo he? Ani osusuɔ maŋsɛɛ sanekpakpa jajelɔ ni yɔɔ ekãa, tsɔɔlɔ ni he esa, aloo mɔ ko ni ŋma Biblia lɛ fã kpoo ko he? Nakai tsɔɔmɔi lɛ ja pɛpɛɛpɛ. Kɛbɛ nakai kwraa lɛ, Paulo ji mɔ ko ni yɔɔ henumɔi ni mli kwɔ hu. Tamɔ wɔteŋ mɛi pii lɛ, be komɛi yɛ ni ekɛ henumɔi gbohii wuu ta.

² Kanemɔ Romabii 7:21-24. Yɛ Paulo wolo ni eŋma kɛyahe Romabii lɛ mli lɛ, etsɔɔ henumɔi komɛi anɔ, ni wɔteŋ mɛi pii nuɔ he nakai nɔŋŋ. Eyɛ mli akɛ Kristofonyo ni yeɔ anɔkwa ji lɛ moŋ, shi ehiɛ kpe yɛ emli—ta ni kã eshwelɛ ni yɔɔ mli kpaakpa akɛ ebaafee Nyɔŋmɔ suɔmɔnaanii lɛ kɛ suɔmɔ ni eyɔɔ akɛ ebaanyiɛ shwelɛi gbohii asɛɛ lɛ ateŋ. Kɛfata mlita nɛɛ he lɛ, Paulo kɛ henumɔi gbohii ni kɔɔ enɔtsɛ̃lemɔ kɛ naagba ni mli ewa ni kã ehiɛ lɛ he hu wu yɛ bei komɛi amli.

³ Eyabɔ amanehulu ni Paul kɛkpe yɛ emli lɛ fɛɛ sɛɛ lɛ, eŋmɛɛɛ gbɛ ni esusumɔi gbohii* lɛ aye enɔ. Yɛ sane nɛɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaasusu sanebimɔi nɛɛ ahe: Mɛni ha Paul nu he yɛ bei komɛi amli akɛ “mɔ ko ni awulaŋhoo lɛlɛŋ”? Te efee tɛŋŋ ekpe susumɔi gbohii lɛ anaa? Kɛ agbɛnɛ te wɔbaafee tɛŋŋ wɔtsɔ wɔ-diɛŋtsɛ wɔ-susumɔi gbohii lɛ anɔ kɛ omanyeyeli?

* WIEMƆ LƐ SHISHITSƆƆMƆ: Yɛ nikasemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ, wiemɔ ni ji “jwɛŋmɔi fɔji” lɛ tsɔɔ awerɛhoyeli kɛ jwɛŋmɔ ni tsakeɔ be kɛ be ni baanyɛ aba wɔnɔ lɛ. Etsɔɔɔ tsuiŋkpo ni mɔ ko kɛhiɔ shi be babaoo, ni ji hela ni naa wa kɛha dɔketa lɛ.

4 Nibii ni efee yɛ be ni eho lɛ mli. Dani Paulo, ni tsutsu ko lɛ atsɛɔ lɛ Saulo lɛ baatsɔ Kristofonyo lɛ, efee nibii komɛi ni sɛɛ mli lɛ etsɔi egbɔ yɛ he. 5 Be ni bɔfo Paulo batsɔ Kristofonyo sɛɛ lɛ, bei komɛi lɛ nibii ni efee yɛ e-shihilɛ ni eho lɛ mli lɛ gbaa enáa. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, be ni eŋma ewolo klɛŋklɛŋ lɛ kɛya Korintobii lɛ aŋɔɔ yɛ aaafee 55 Ŋ.B. lɛ, ekɛɛ: “Misaaa akɛ atsɛɔ mi bɔfo, ejaakɛ miwa Nyɔŋmɔ asafo lɛ yi.” (1 Kor. 15:9) Aaafee afii enumɔ sɛɛ lɛ, yɛ ewolo ni eŋma kɛya Efesobii lɛ aŋɔɔ lɛ mli lɛ, etsɔɔ mɔ ni eji akɛ mɔ ko ni “feɔ mɔ ni baa shi fe mɛi krɔŋkrɔŋi lɛ fɛɛ.” (Efe. 3:8) Be ni eŋma Timoteo lɛ, Paulo wie ehe akɛ blema lɛ, eji “musubɔlɔ kɛ yiwatɔ kɛ he-nɔwolɔ.” (1 Tim. 1:13) Ani obaanyɛ osusu bɔ ni Paulo nu he eha be ni eyasara asafo ko ni eyakpe mɛi ni ewa amɛ yi aloo mɛi ni ewa amɛ yi lɛ awekumɛi lɛ he?

7 E-hewalɛnamɔi. Paulo kɛ ehewalɛnamɔi lɛ kpe hewalɛnamɔ. (Kanemɔ Romabii 7:18, 19.) Eyɛ mli akɛ eesumɔ ni efee nɔ ni ja moŋ, shi ehewalɛnamɔ helo lɛ fite emlifai kɛha nɔ ni ja feemɔ lɛ. Ekpɛlɛ nɔ akɛ ta kpee nɔ ni kã e-heloonaa kɔŋkɔŋi kɛ nɔ ni ja feemɔ shweemɔ lɛ ateŋ. Kɛbɔ fɛɛ kɛbɔ lɛ, Paulo bɔ mɔdɛŋ waa ni ehi. (1 Kor. 9:27) Bo nɛ eha enu he akɛ eje mli nɔ fɛɛ nɔ be ni esui fɔji lɛ ba ekonn!

Feemɔ Biblia Mli Sanebimɔi Lɛ He Mfoniri Yɛ Ojwɛŋmɔ Mli

Biblia lɛ hiɛ blema saji pii ni tsɔɔ wɔ nikasemɔi ni he hiaa. Kɛ́ wɔsusu amaniɛbɔi nɛɛ ahe yɛ wɔjwɛŋmɔ mli lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔnu nɔ ni wɔkaneɔ lɛ shishi jogbaŋŋ, ni wɔná he sɛɛ kpaakpa. Susumɔ ŋaawoi komɛi ni baanyɛ aye abua bo koni onyɛ osusu Biblia mli amaniɛbɔi lɛ ahe yɛ ojwɛŋmɔ mli lɛ ahe.

• Kanemɔ ewa. Biblia lɛ kanemɔ ewa baanyɛ aye abua bo ni oha saji lɛ afee bo faŋŋ jogbaŋŋ yɛ ojwɛŋmɔ mli. Kɛ́ nyɛmiikane Biblia lɛ akɛ weku lɛ, nyɛbaanyɛ nyɛha amaniɛbɔɔ lɛ afee henumɔ nɔ kɛtsɔ gbɔmɔ fɛɛ gbɔmɔ ni aaaha ekane mɔ ko he sane kpotookpo nɔ.

• Feemɔ bɔ ni nibii lɛ yaa nɔ ha lɛ he mfoniri yɛ ojwenmɔ mli. Bɔɔ mɔdɛŋ ni onu nibii ni mɛi ni he sane lɛ kɔɔ he lɛ susuɔ kɛ bɔ ni amɛnuɔ he amɛha lɛ shishi. Ákɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, bi ohe akɛ: ‘Mɛni hewɔ mɔ lɛ wie loo efee nɔ ko nakai? Mɛni makɛŋ loo bɔ ni manu he mahã kɛ́ mikɛ shihilɛ nakai kpe?’

• Tsiŋ mfoniri loo fee he mfoniri fioo. Mfoniri ni atsiŋ yɛ Biblia mli sanebimɔi ahe lɛ baanyɛ aye abua bo ni ona ni okai sanebimɔi lɛ kpakpa, kɛji po nɔ ni otsohi lɛ jeee nikanemaa kpele ko.
