SƐNEA AKOKƆNINI NYAA ANO A ƐSO YƐ NNAAM

AGYA ANANSE, Ananse no, ne ne mma Afudotwedotwe (Afu a ɛrebɛpaepae), Nyiwankonfwea (Nankoroma nsensanee), (ne) Tikonokono (Ti-kɛse-kɛse) na ɛte hɔ. Na Ananse ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ mokɔpɛ borɔdɔma brɛ me ma medi." Enti mmofra yi sɔre kɔe; wɔnamm, wɔnamm, wɔnamm, na wɔkɔtoo borɔdɔmadua bi, na (borɔdɔma no) abere, na mmofra no kɔɔ (dua no) ase. Afudotwedotwe (Belly-like-to-burst) ka kyerɛɛ Nyiwankonfwea (Thin-shanks) sɛ, "Fofɔr kɔte bi." Ɔkaa sɛ, "O, sɛ meforo a me nan bɛbu." Tikonokono (Big-big-head) kaa sɛ, "Wo, Afudotwedotwe, foro kɔ soro." Ɔkaa sɛ, "O, sɛ meforo kɔ soro a, me yam bɛpae; wo ara foro." Enti Tikonokono (Big-big-head) foroeɛ. Ɔtew borɔdɔma mma no bi, na ɔkɔɔ so tewee ara tewee, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛbɛyɛ anaa?" Wɔn a aka no kaa sɛ, "Ah, (yɛbɛyɛ dɛn ahunu?)." Enti ɔkɔɔ so twee wɔn; afei ɔbisaa wɔn bio sɛ, "Ɛbɛyɛ anaa?" Na wɔgyee so sɛ, "Brɛ wo ti ase na hwɛ." Na berɛ a ɔrebɛbrɛ ne ti ase sɛ ɔrehwɛ no, ɛnna ne ti bɔɔ fam, kaa (asɛm bi te sɛ) "Kum!" Berɛ a Yafunu-pɛ Nea-obepae anka oresere no, ne yam paee ntehwee, ta! Nankrɔn, bere a anka ɔretu mmirika akɔka akyerɛ ne papa no, ne nan bubui kae wɔ ban no akyi. Akokɔnini no reguare ne kra, na ɔrekɔ tow nkesua-nwonono (a wɔde abɔ afɔre no) agu no, na ɔhwee Nankrɔn sɛ wahwe fam na ne nan abubu, agyaw wɔ ban no afa baako. Bere a ɔrebɛsereɛ no, n’ano yɛɛ tsentsen:

Bere a ɔrebɛsereɛ no, n’ano yɛɛ tɛntɛm.

Bere a na ɔrebɛsere no, N’ano bɛyɛɛ sɔnsɔn."

Ɛno nti na Mpanyinfoɔ se, "Sɛ obi hwe ase a, kɔ na kɔhwɛ no, na nsere no." Afei Akokɔnini deɛ, ɔyɛɛ saa pɛpɛɛpɛ, na ɔsereeɛ, enti n’ano bɛyɛɛ tsiritsiri; kane no na ɔwɔ ano fɛfɛɛfɛ, fɛfɛɛfɛ.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
