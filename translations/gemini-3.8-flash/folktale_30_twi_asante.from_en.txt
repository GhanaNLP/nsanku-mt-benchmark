Yɛnni aseɛ koraa, yɛnni aseɛ koraa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokorɛ)

SƐNEA NTƆKWA BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU

Ná ɔbarima bi wɔ hɔ a wɔfrɛ no Mepɛ-asɛm-nso-mmpɛ-akyinnyeɛ, na ɛno nti, ɔkɔsii akuraa ketewa bi (ɔno nko ara) kɔtenaa mu. Ɔkwaaduo nso kɔsraa no, na ɔne no kɔtenaa abɛ ase. Na abɛbɛ no bi hwee fam. Ɔkwaaduo see sɛ, "Agya Mepɛ-asɛm-nso-mmpɛ-akyinnyeɛ, w'abɛ no abere." Mepɛ-asɛm-nso-mmpɛ-akyinnyeɛ see sɛ, "Saa na abɛbɛ teɛ; sɛ ɛbere a, mbetia mmiɛnsa na ɛberɛ prɛko pɛ; sɛ ɛbere a, metwa, na sɛ menoanoa de yi ngo a, ɛma ngo ahina mmiɛnsa ma; na mede ngo no kɔ Akase kɔtɔ Akase aberewa bi; Akase aberewa no ba bɛwo me nanabea, na ɔwo me maame, na ɔno nso wo me; sɛ me maame wo me a, na megyina hɔ dada." Ɔkwaaduo see sɛ, "Ne nyinaa mu deɛ, wotwa nkontompo." Na Mepɛ-asɛm-nso-mmpɛ-akyinnyeɛ faa poma bi bɔɔ Ɔkwaaduo ti so, kumm no. Ɛno akyi no, adowa Abedee ketewa no nso baeɛ. Na ɔne no kɔtenaa abɛ no ase, na asɛm korɔ no ara na ɛsiiɛ. Na mmoa no nyinaa nso kɔɔ so saa ara. Afei, Kwaku Ananse kɔfaa ne ntoma, na ɔfaa ne kotokuo, senn no n'abatire mu, kɔɔ Mepɛ-asɛm-nso-mmpɛ-akyinnyeɛ kuro mu. Ɔkyea no sɛ, "Agya, maakye." Mepɛ-asɛm-nso-mmpɛ-akyinnyeɛ gyee so sɛ, "Yaa Aku, na worekɔ he?" Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Merenom ba wo nkyɛn." Na ɔfaa n'adwa de sii abɛ no ase. Mepɛ-asɛm-nso-mmpɛ-akyinnyeɛ see sɛ, "Monnoa aduane mma Ananse nni." Na berɛ a ɛrenoaso no, Ananse ne Mepɛ-asɛm-nso-mmpɛ-akyinnyeɛ tenaa abɛ no ase. Na abɛbɛ no bi hwee fam. Na Ananse sesaeɛ de guu ne kotokuo mu. Na ɔkɔɔ so yɛɛ saa ara kɔsii sɛ ne kotokuo yɛɛ ma. Wɔde aduane no baeɛ, na Ananse didiiɛ. Berɛ a ɔdidi weeɛ no, abɛbɛ no bi bio hwee fam, na Ananse see sɛ, "Agya Mepɛ-asɛm-nso-mmpɛ-akyinnyeɛ, w'abɛ no abere." Mepɛ-asɛm-nso-mmpɛ-akyinnyeɛ see sɛ, "Saa na ɛbereɛ; sɛ ɛbere a, mbetia mmiɛnsa na ɛberɛ prɛko pɛ; sɛ ɛbere a, metwa, na sɛ menoanoa de yi ngo a, ɛma ngo ahina mmiɛnsa ma, na mede ngo no kɔ Akase, kɔtɔ Akase aberewa bi; Akase aberewa no ba bɛwo me nanabea, na ɔwo me maame, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a ɔno nso bɛwo me. Berɛ a me maame wo me no, na me gyina hɔ dedaw." Ananse kaa sɛ, "Wontwa nkontompo, nea woreka no yɛ nokware; me deɛ, mewɔ nkruma bi a ɛwɔ m'afuo mu, na sɛ ɛbere a, mebɔ nkɔntɔndua tenteenten aduoson nson bom (sɛ mede bɛtwe aba fam), nanso ɛmpo a ɛnnuru hɔ, enti mebutuw m'akyi, na metumi de me kɔte tew." Okyir-Akyinnyeɛ kaa sɛ, "O, mete aseɛ; ɔkyena mɛba abɛhwɛ." Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ampa." Berɛ a Ananse rekɔ (fie) no, ɔwe abe a ɔboaboaa ano no, na ɔtetew guu kwan no so. Adekyee a adeɛ resan abue no, Okyir-Akyinnyeɛ tuu kwan sɛ ɔrekɔ Ananse kuro mu. Afei berɛ a Ananse dii kan duu fie (ɛnora no), ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ ne mma sɛ, "Ɔbarima bi bɛba ha, na ɔkyiri akyinnyeɛ, na sɛ ɔba na ɔbisa me a, monka nkyerɛ no sɛ ɛnora mekaa sɛ mereka baabi akɔ, na me kɔte bubuu mprɛnson, na ɛsɛ sɛ mede kɔma otomfoɔ ma ɔyɛ, na esiane sɛ wantumi anwie saa berɛ no nti, ɔse wakɔ hɔ bio ama wɔanwie adwuma no." Ɛnkyɛree koraa na Okyir-Akyinnyeɛ baeɛ. Ɔbisaa sɛ, "Moyɛn papa akɔ he?" Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Aka, papa kɔɔ baabi ɛnora, na ne kɔte bubuu mprɛnson, na ɔde kɔmaa otomfoɔ, nanso wantumi anwie adwuma no saa berɛ no, enti papa akɔ hɔ bio ma wɔanwie, na (wo,) Papa, woanhu mogya no wɔ kwan no so anaa?" Okyir-Akyinnyeɛ kaa sɛ, "Mehunuie." Okyir-Akyinnyeɛ bisaa sɛ, "Na mo maame nso wɔ he?" (Ananse ba no buaa sɛ,) "Maame nso, ɛnora ɔkɔɔ asu, na n'ahina anka ɛbɛtɔ fam abubu sɛ wantwe anso mu ntɛm a, nanso wantumi anwie so mu yie, enti wasan akɔ ɛnnɛ sɛ ɔrekɔwie so mu." Okyir-Akyinnyeɛ anka hwee. Afei Ananse baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Monnoa aduane mma Okyir-Akyinnyeɛ nni." Berɛ a mmofra no renoa aduane no, wɔde apataa baako kɛkɛ na ɛyɛe nanso mako no dɔɔso bebree; wɔmaa nkwan no yɛɛ hye paa. Berɛ a wɔwiei no, wɔde sii Okyir-Akyinnyeɛ anim. Okyir-Akyinnyeɛ diiɛ. Afei mako no yɛɛ no ya; na ɔrepɛ awu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ntikuma (Ananse ba), kɔsa nsuo brɛ me ma mennom." Ntikuma kɔɔ asuo no ho; wammfa nsuo no amma da. Mempɛ-asɛnkyerɛfoɔ kaa sɛ, “Ntikuma, nsuo no wɔ he?” Ntikuma kaa sɛ, “Aane, nsuo a yɛwɔ ha (wɔ yɛn ahina mu) no yɛ ahodoɔ mmiɛnsa; papa dea na ɛwɔ soro, me maame kora dea na ɛwɔ mfinimfini, na me maame ankasa dea na ɛwɔ aseɛ hɔ (wɔ ahina no ase); na esiane sɛ mepɛ sɛ mesa me maame ankasa dea ma wo nti, sɛ manhwɛ yie ansa na meresa a, ɛbɛkɔ akɔfa abusuakuo akasakasa aba.” Mempɛ-asɛnkyerɛfoɔ kaa sɛ, “Akwadaa ba, woredi atoro.” Ɛhɔ ara pɛ, Ananse kaa sɛ, “Mompempem no mma no nwuo.” Mempɛ-asɛnkyerɛfoɔ kaa sɛ, “Adɛn nti na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔpempem me ma mewuo?” Ananse kaa sɛ, “Wose wompɛ asɛnkyerɛ, nanso woapo obi asɛm, ɛno nti na mese wɔmpempem wo mma wonwuo no.” Enti wɔpempem Mempɛ-asɛnkyerɛfoɔ maa no wuiɛ. Afei Ananse twitwaa ne nam nketenkete na ɔbɔ hwetee kɔɔ mmaa nyinaa. Ɛno nti na nkurɔfoɔ bebree a wɔmpɛ asɛnkyerɛ wɔ abusuakuo no mu no.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
