Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka yi yɛ nokware)

NEA ƐYƐE A AKOO DUA BƐYƐƐ KƆKƆƆ

Wɔka sɛ bere bi na Ɔhemmaa bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔwooo abaayewa bi a ne ho yɛ fɛ pa ara. Ɔte hɔ; nanso sɛ wɔde no ma obiara sɛ ɔnware a, ɔmpɛ sɛ ɔware. Daa bere a ne nkorɔfo kɔkasa kyerɛ no (fa aware ho) no, ɔka sɛ, “Dabi koraa.” Na abɔde, Onini, no nso wɔ kwae no mu baabi, na ɔte ɔbodan kɛse bi mu. Da koro bi a na ɔwɔ hɔ (ne bɔn mu) no, ɔtee sɛ abɔfo bi retwam, na na wɔresusuw ababaawa fɛfɛ no ho asɛm, sɛnea obiara a wɔde no ma no sɛ ɔnware no no, ɔka sɛ ɔmpɛ no no. Ɔnanka no daa hɔ, na ɔkaa (kyerɛɛ ne ho) sɛ, “Mɛsakra me ho ayɛ aberante fɛfɛɛfɛ bi, na makɔware Ɔhemaa no ba no.” Nokorɛm no, Ɔnanka no sakraa ne ho yɛɛ aberanteɛ; a obi rentumi nhwɛ no kɔ akyiri. Ɔtuu kwan kɔɔ akuraa no ase. Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, na ɔretwe bise. Berɛ a ɔduruu kuro no ano no, Ɔhemaa no ba a ɔkɔhoroo nnoɔma no baeɛ na ɔresam nnoɔma no ama awo. Ɔnwanmanka no tesei guu fam, na ɛtɔɔ Ɔhemaa no ba no dɔteɛkɔkɔɔ so. Ɔhemaa ba no kaa sɛ, “Adɛn nti na wote ntasu gu me kente akyi?” Ɔnanka no kaa sɛ, “Woka nokorɛ, Amu.” Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, “Me nton agyegye soɔ nyɛ Amu.” Ɔbuae sɛ, “Woka nokorɛ, Eson.” Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, “Me nton agyegye soɔ nyɛ Eson.” Ɔbuae sɛ, “Woka nokorɛ, me yere.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛnde m’aware wo.” Ɔhemaa ba no ne Ɔnanka no firi aseɛ kɔ ff ne fie. Ntɛm ara na Ɔhemaa no ba kɔɔ ne maame nkyɛn kɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛh! ɛh! ɛh! (anigye nti na ɔretoto n’anom), mahunu deɛ me pɛ no.” Ɔhemaa no kaa sɛ, “Afei deɛ! me asew no wɔ he?” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Mewɔ ha, nana.” Ɔhemmaa no ne Ɔnanka no kyeaa nsa, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Wo ne wo yere ntena asomdwoeɛ mu.” Berɛ a anwummere dwoeɛ no, ɔkɔhomee ne ne yere. Ɛbaa saa kɔsii nnanwɔtwe. Akyire yi ɔkaa sɛ, “Me yere, merekɔ me kuro mu nanso mɛsan aba.” Ne yere kaa sɛ, “Ɛnde me ne wo bɛkɔ.” Ɔnini no kaa sɛ, “Dabi.” Ɔnini no kasae, kasae nanso Ɔhemaa no ba no antie. Adeɛ kyeeɛ no, ɔne ne kunu no tuu kwan. Bere a wɔrekɔ no, Ɔnanka no manee kɔwuraa kwaeɛ mu. Ɔhemaa no ba no kaa sɛ, “Ɛdeɛn na woreyɛ yi, ɛha na kwan no da?” Ɔnanka no kaa sɛ, “Wo deɛ, mu w’ano na di m’akyi.” Ɔno ne no tuu kwan, na wɔkɔe kɔeɛ, na afei deɛ Ɔnanka no yera kɔɔ ɔboda bi mu. Ɔhemaa no ba no nso wuraa mu. Ntɛm ara na Ɔnini no danee ne ho yɛɛ aboa kɛseɛ; afei ɔkasa kyerɛɛ Ɔhemmaa no ba no sɛ, “Sɛdeɛ meteɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ nie, ɛfiri sɛ medanee me ho sɛdeɛ mɛtumi aware woɔ, ɛfiri sɛ na wotaa tena wo maame ne wo papa serɛ so ka sɛ, ‘Biribiara nni baabiara (a mesuro)’; (mprempren) merekɔkum wo; fiti me ti mu na kyere nseno ma me; sɛ woannya bi a, na w’awieeɛ aba ne aduane." Ɔhemaa ba no te ne nkyɛn, retitititi ne ho na ɔrehwehwɛ nsesaa. Afei, na wɔwɔ hɔ no, na Ɔhemaa ba no hunuu sɛ Akoo bi retwam. Ɔhemaa ba no rebue n'ano aka sɛ, "Agya Akoo," na Akoo no de ne nsa too n'ano. Akoo no faa ne tuo, siesiee ne nyinaa yie, na ɔde too ne kɔn mu. Ɔnini no kaa sɛ, "Sa me ti." Ɔhemmaa no ba no kaa sɛ, "Mabrɛ ne sa; sɛ wobɛku me a, ɛndeɛ ku me." Na Ɔnini no rebɛma ne ti so sɛ ɔrekye Ɔhemmaa no ba no pɛ, na Akoo maa tuo no suueɛ sɛ, pomm! Akoo kɔɔ ɔbodan no mu kɔfaa Ɔhemmaa ba no ne Ɔnanka no de wɔn kɔɔ fie kɔbɔɔ amanneɛ sɛdeɛ ɛyɛeɛ a ɔhunuu Ɔhemmaa ba no ne Ɔnanka no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn kwan a ɔfaa so kumm Ɔnanka no. Ɔhemmaa ba no nso bɔɔ n'amanneɛ nyinaa. Ɛna Hemmaa no kaa sɛ, "Akoo, dɛn na memfa nna wo ase?" Akoo no kaa sɛ, "Nana, mempɛ hwee, me deɛ merekɔpɛ ngo kɛkɛ." Ɛna Hemmaa no kɔeɛ kɔfaa ngo baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Nana, mede me dua rebɔ ngo yi mu, na ayɛ sɛnkyerɛnne (ama asɛm yi)." Ɛno nti na Akoo dua bɛyɛɛ kɔkɔɔ no. M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, momfa bi nkɔ baabi, na momfa bi nsan mmrɛ me.
