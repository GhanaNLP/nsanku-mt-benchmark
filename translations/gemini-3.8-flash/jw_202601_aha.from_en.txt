Kɛ bɔ yɛbahola yɛadɔ do yɛanya mvasoɛ wɔ sunsum nu aleɛ, aduradeɛ, nee atenbɔ bɔ Gyihova fa maa yɛ la anu.

YƐ AFE NGYIKYILƐ MA 2026:

Anigyeɛ wɔ bɛnli mɔɔ bɛze kɛ bɛhyia sunsum nu ninyɛne nwo la anwo.

—MATT. 5:3.

JEHOVAH abɔ menle kɛ bɛnyia ninyɛne nsunsu bie mɔ anwo hyia. Kɛ neazo la, amaa yɛali ngoane la, yɛhyia aleɛ, atadeɛ, nee tenleka anwo. Saa yɛnnyia ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ anu ko bɔbɔ wɔ mekɛ ekyi bie anu a, ngoane yɛ sɔhwɛ. Bieko, kɛ mɔɔ ɔbɔle yɛ kɛ yɛhyia honam afoa nu ninyɛne zɔhane mɔ anwo la, Jehovah ɛbɔ yɛ kɛ yɛnyia sunsum nu afoa nu hyia noko. (Kenga Matthew 5:3.) Saa yɛkulo kɛ yɛnyia anyelielɛ kpole a, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛnwu hyia zɔhane na akee yɛkɔ zo yɛdi nwolɛ gyima.

2 Sɛ yɛka kɛ awie “nim” ye “sunsum nu ahiadeɛ” a, edzin kɛse? Kasa mɔ bɔbɔ bɛvale bɛhɛlɛle edwɛkɛ ɛhye nu la kile kɛ awie bayɛ ebisrafo kɛ ɔbanya sunsum ne. Edwɛkɛ ɛhye kile nvoninli kpole fɛlɛko bie wɔ adwenle nu. Fa ye kɛ ɛlɛnwu awie bie mɔ bɔbɔ woyikula nu fura nsalra mɔ gyi suakpole nu adenle kɔmɔ mɔɔ do. Aleɛ ɛnle ye akunlu ti yelua, ye anyidɔ kɛti nti amanfo tu ye to ahye, yɛ awia yiriba ne tɔ ye nwo yɛ awɔbale ne mɔɔ tɔ nɔe la noko ku ye. Ebisrafo ne nim kpalɛ kɛ, sɛ ye asetena bayɛ kpalɛ a, kɛyia kɛ ɔnya mmoa. Zɔhane ala noko a, awie mɔ ɔnim ye sunsum nu ahiadeɛ—ebisrafo mɔ kpondɛ sunsum ne—bayia mmoa na amaa ye asetena ayɛ kpalɛ. Ɔti, ɔtua ye kɛ ɔbade sunsum nu ninyɛne dɔɔnwo mɔ Jehovah fua maa bɛdabɛ mɔ bɛkulo Ye la azo kpama.

3. Duzu a yɛbaaka nwolɛ edwɛkɛ wɔ edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ ɛhye anu a?

Akee yɛbazuzu neazo mɔɔ ɛzoanvolɛ Peter nee Paul vomee Edwomikɛlɛvolɛ Devidi mɔɔ bɛmuala bɛle sunsum nu menli la yɛle la anwo.

4 Mekɛ bie, Fonisia bɛla bie hɔle Gyisɛse ɛkɛ. Ɛnee sunsum bɔne ɛsɛkye ɔ ba bɛla ne “kpalɛ.” (Mat. 15:21-28) Bɛla ne wule aze kɔtole Gyisɛse anyunlu na ɔbɔle ye mpaɛ kɛ ɔboa ye. Mɔɔ Fonisia bɛla ɛhye hɔle Gyisɛse ɛkɛ ne, ɔyilile suhonle kpalɛ bie mɔ ali. Yɛsuzu ɛhye mɔ bie anwo.

5 Foneka raa ne lale ahobrebese kpole ali. Duazu a yɛbahola yaaha nwole edwɛkɛ ɛleka zɛhe a? Ɔluakɛ mekɛ mɔɔ Gyisɛse fale ndonwo bie mɔɔ ɛnee te kɛ ɔte kɛ kpolikpoli mɔɔ bɛte kɛ aboa mɔɔ Amanhaale bie anye gye nwole wɔ ye sua nu la, ɔnyale abufuwa. Saa ɛdawɔ a, kɛ ɛbayɛ Gyisɛse edwɛkɛ nee ye nyɛleɛ ne ɛ? Ɛbade nganeɛ kɛ yɛgu w’adze na w’agyae ye mmoa kpondɛ? Ɛleka ɛzɛhi mɔɔ Foneka raa ne yɛle a. Ɔnla ahobrebese ngome ali na ɔbɔle mmɔden noko. Ɛhe la ali wɔ kɛ mɔɔ ɔzɔle Gyisɛse nwole zo kɛ ɔboa ye la anu. Nzu a manle ɔyɛle zɔhane a? Ye diedi mɔɔ ɔwɔ Gyisɛse anu la. Nɔhalɛ nu, raa ɛhe ye diedi hanle Gyisɛse ahonle kpole kpalɛ manle ɔyɛle nwanwane debie. Ɔwɔ nuhua kɛ ɛnee yɛha fofolɛ ne ala kɛ bɛzomanle ye kɛ ɔraboa “Isrɛle sua ne mboane mɔɔ ɛminli la ngome,” noko Gyisɛse dule sunsum ɛtane ne vile Amanhaale raa ɛhe ye ra raa ne anu.

Sesei yɛnlea adenle mɔɔ Gyɛhova dua do maa yɛ sunsum nu aleɛ, aduradeɛ, nee ɛleka a yɛbada la. Yɛbanwu mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ na yɛanyia ninyɛne he mɔɔ yɛhyia nwo la wɔ nɔhalɛyɛlɛ mɔɔ asɔfo Pita nee Pɔɔlu nee Ɔhene Devidi yɛle la nu.

KƐ PITƐ LA—KƆ DO NYA SUNSUM NU ALIDƆLƐ WƆ PƆƆPƆƆ NU

7 Dwen somafo Pita nneɛso ne nwo. Ɔle Dyufo mɔɔ lumua nwunle kɛ Gyisɛs a le Mesaya ne—ahenle mɔɔ Gyihova ɛlɛfa ye adua ye menli ne “dahuu ngoane nwonle.” (Dwɔn 6:66-68) Kolaa na Gyisɛs mɔɔ bɛdwazo ye la ahɔ anwuma no, ɔmanle Pita gyima ɛhye: “Nea me mboane nwanwa ne mɔ ayile.” (Dwɔn 21:17) Pita lile gyima zɔhane nwo nɔhalɛ, na Gyihova bɔbɔ lile ye nzonlɛ manle ɔhɛlɛle ngɛlata nwiɔ mɔɔ rabale Bayibolo ne anu a. Noko ɛnee ɔwɔ kɛ Pita noko fa sunsum nu aleɛ dua ɔ nwo. Kɛ neazo la, ɔsukoale sunsum nu ngɛlata mɔɔ somafo Pɔɔlu hɛlɛle ye dɛba la. Pita liele dole nu kɛ ninyɛne bie mɔ mɔɔ Pɔɔlu hɛlɛle nwo edwɛkɛ la “yɛ se kɛ bɛte ase.” (2 Pita 3:15, 16) Noko Pita doale zolɛ kpole, na ɔliele ɔ lile kɛ Gyihova baboa ye amaa yeade na yeava “aleɛ mɔɔ anu yɛ se” mɔɔ bɛmanle Pɔɔlu sunsum hɛlɛle ye ngɛlata ne mɔ anu la yeali gyima.—Kenga Hibuluma 5:14–6:1.

8 Pita lale ye ali kɛ ɔle gyedie wɔ Gyɛhova nu ɔlua kɛ ɔdieli ngilenu mɔ ɔnyanne na zɔ. Kɛ ne ndonwo mɔ, mɔɔ Pita wɔ po nloa azua Joppa nu la, ɔnyanne anyezolole mɔɔ Nyamenle somfoma bikanle hilele ye kɛ ɔgwu na ɔli mmoa mɔɔ Mosis Mmla ne kile kɛ benle kpole la. Wɔ Dyuuma anyunu la, ngilenu he le mɔɔ gyegye adwenle kpole. Wɔ mɔlebɛbo ne, Pita bualele kɛ: “Da, Awurade, ɔluakɛ mengyuli debie biala mɔɔ ɛnle kpole anzɛɛ ɛnsonzɔ la li.” Bikanle hilele ye ekko kɛ: “Gya kɛ ɛbavɛlɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ Nyamenle yewowola nwole la kɛ ɛnsonzɔ.” (Asomafo ne mɔ 10:9-15) Pita dele aseɛ. Kɛ ɔyɛle mɔɔ yɛsi la? Mɔɔ ɔnwunle anyezolole he mɔ yɛkyɛle la, mrenya nsa mɔɔ Amanrɛlefiynye Kɔniliɔso somane bɛ la rale na bɛdɔle Pita ɛsalɛ kɛ ɔrɛla na ɔbika edwɛkɛ ɔgile bɛ wura. Wɔ gyinlabelɛ biala nu la, anrɛɛ Pita ɛnrɛlie ɛnrɛdo nu kɛ ɔbakɔ Amanrɛlefiynye sua nu. Dyuuma buale Amanrɛlefoɔ kɛ benle kpole. (Asomafo ne mɔ 10:28, 29) Na Pita liele nwɔlaba fofolɛ mɔɔ Gyɛhova yili hilele ye la dole nu ndɛndɛ. (Edw. 4:18) Ɔhanle edwɛkɛ ne ɔhilele Kɔniliɔso nee ye sua nu amra na ɔnyanne anyelielɛ kɛ ɔnwunle kɛ bɛliele nɔhalɛ ne bɛdole nu, bɛnyanne sunsum nwɔnwanla ne, na bɛyɛle asɔne.—Asomafo ne mɔ 10:44-48.

4 ƆWƐNWƐNSINLAI NE

sunsum nu edidia a ɛyɛ den—nɔhalɛ mɔɔ ɔyɛ den kɛ yɛbade ase. Ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ yɛfa mekɛ na yɛyɛ mɔden kɛ yɛbade Nyamenle Edwɛkɛ ne ase kpalɛ, na ɔwɔ nvasoɔ. Nzu ati a? Yɛnya nvasoɔ wɔ adenle nwiɔ anwo zo pɛɛ: Kɛnlan, yɛsukoa ninyɛne wɔ Gyihova anwo mɔɔ maa yɛ ɛlɔlɛ nee yɛ enlidolɛ kɔ anim; na nwiɔ, yɛte nganeɛ kpalɛ kɛ yɛbaha yɛ anwuma Selɛ kpole ne anwo edwɛkɛ yɛahile awie mɔ. (Rom. 11:33; Rev. 4:11) Pita neazo ne kilehile yɛ ɛzukoalɛdeɛ fofolɛ: Mekɛ mɔɔ yɛsa ka nzenzaleɛ wɔ Nyamenle Edwɛkɛ ne mɔɔ yɛte ase anwo la, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ ndɛ yɛsesa yɛ adwenle nee yɛ nyɛleɛ. Ɛhye ala a bamaa yɛali sunsum nu aleɛ kpalɛ na yɛayɛ mvasoɔ amaa Gyihova a.

KƐƐ PƆƆLƐ LA—KƆƆ SO TƐ SUNSUM NU ADURADE PAA

10 Sɛ yɛkpondɛ kɛ yɛyɛ mɔɔ sɔ Nyamenle anye a, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛfa ninyɛne bieko mɔɔ Gyihova ɛva ɛmaa yɛ la yɛdi gyima: sunsum nu adwalie. Ɛhye kile duzu? Ɛzoanvo Pɔɔlo hɛlɛle kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛye “subane dɛba ne” yɛto ɛkɛ na yɛva “subane fofolɛ ne” yɛwula. (Kenga Kɔlɔsaema 3:8-10.) Adenle ɛhye azo mɔɔ yɛbalua yɛawula yɛ nwo adwalie la bahola ayɛ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ anu yɛ se dahuu. Nea Pɔɔlo mumua ne ye neazo ne. Ɔvi ye ngakula nu, ɔbɔle mɔdenle kɛ ɔkpondɛ Nyamenle anyunlu ɛlolɛ. (Gal. 1:14; Fil. 3:4, 5) Noko ɛnee ɔnze Nyamenle bodane ne kpalɛ, na yemɔ ti ɛnee ɔwɔ sunsum nu gyinlabelɛ ɛtane nu. Kɛ mɔɔ ɛnee ɔnze Kelaese ngilehilelɛ ne yɛɛ ahonlehyelɛ mɔɔ ɛnee ɔlɛ la maa ɔyɛle “sonla mɔɔ ɛnyɛ nidi,” mɔɔ ɛnee ɔva subane ɛtane ngakyile yewula ye nwo adwalie a.—1 Tim. 1:13.

10. Sunsum nu adulɛ kpalɛ nwu kɛlɛ si duolɛ nwolɛ edwɛkɛ kile dɛbɛn? (Kolosefo 3:8-10)

11 Kolaa nna Paul kɛrayɛ Kristonyi no, nna ɔwɔ eya kpole. Asomafo no ngilenu kile kɛ nna Paul eya ne anye do dodo wɔ Gyisɛse eduandi no do a ɔmaa “ɔbɔɔ bɔbɔ kɛ ɔkoku bɛ.” (Asomafo 9:1) Kolaa nna ɔbayɛ Kristonyi no, anwoho akyinnye biala ɛnle nu kɛ Paul bɔɔ mmoden kpole kɛ ɔbato su kɛhɛ a ɔwɔ suban dada no nu ato aze. (Efe. 4:22, 31) Ɛhye mɔɔ kɔ do no, mekɛ mɔɔ akate kpole bale Paul nza Barnabas avinli no, eya hyew “kpole tɔɔ bɛ nu.” (Asomafo 15:37-39) Nna ɛhye yɛ anwubaye maa Paul, na noko yeando ne sa nu. Ɔhɔle do ‘nwunwunle ye nwonane’—ɔvale ye sintɔ mɔ ho konle—kɛbaayɛ a ɔkɛhɔ do anyia Gyihova adwemmɔvoa.—1 Kɔr. 9:27.

12 Paul hɔle do bɔle mɔden yili ye suban ɛtane nwole na ɔhyele suban fofolɛ ne ɔluakɛ wanza ye ahoodin nwo. (Fil. 4:13) Kɛmɔ Pita yɛle la, Paul noko zale “ahoodin mɔɔ Nyamenle fa maa la” nwo. (1 Pita 4:11) Ɛhye noko aze, dɔkɔnwo Paul dele nganeɛ kɛ ɔnyia ngɔso. Na sɛ ye abama bu a, ɔdwene ninyɛne kpalɛ mɔɔ ye anwuma Selɛ ne yɛ maa ye la nwo, na ɛhye hyɛle ye ahoodin kɛ ɔbɔ mɔden kɛ ɔkɔ do ayɛ mɔɔ ɔkɔhola la.—Wlo. 7:21-25.

13 Yɛbahola yɛasuasua Pɔɔlo wɔ kwan a yɛbanyia bɛyɛ titili ninyɛne nwolɛ mɔɔ kile kɛ ɔnle mɔɔ yɛava mɛlɛbɛla yɛazonle Gyihova tɛla biala, yɛhyia kɛ yɛbɔ mɔdenle kɛ yɛbaye subane dɛba ne yɛado gua na yɛawula subane fofolɛ ne—sunsum nu ninyɛne mɔɔ Nyamenle fa maa la. Saa yɛnyia ahyirandane—kɛ mɔɔ

(Neɛ kɔntua 18 ne anu)

yɛ ani bo tɔ anzɛnsɛ yɛkã edwɛkɛ mɔɔ mmenle edwɛkɛ fufule kile obiara la—ɔnsɛ kɛ yɛbu yɛ nwo kɛ yɛambɔ mɔdenle. Mmom, ɔsɛ kɛ yɛkɔ so yɛbɔ mɔdenle kɛ yɛbahakyi yɛ nzunsuando mini yɛ ndɛnebɔle. (Rom. 12:1, 2; Eph. 4:24) Noko akee, kɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛyɛ zɔ la, ɔsɛ kɛ yɛkakye ninyɛne kpole bie: Ɔnte kɛ nwonane nu tɛladɛ mɔɔ bɛyɛ bɛto yɛ nwo la, ɔsɛ kɛ yɛyɛ yɛ nwo kɛ mɔɔ sunsum nu tɛladɛ mɔɔ Gyihova fa maa yɛ la de la. Ɔle fɛlɛko kɛ yɛbahakyi kɛzi yɛde la amaa yɛali Nyamenle ninyɛne mɔɔ ɔkpondɛ la azo.

KƐ DAVID—TENA SUNSUM NU ANWUMA FƐFƐƐFƐ

14 Sɛ yɛkɛnya anyigye kpole a, yɛhyia ninyɛne mɔɔ tɛ sunsum nu aleɛ nee sunsum nu adɔkɔnla angomekye a. Yɛhyia sunsum nu ɛbolɔkɛ noko. Duzu a le ɛhye, na kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na yɛanwu kɛ yɛwɔ ye banbɔlɛ ne abo dahuu ɛ?

14-15. Asɛm boni nu a Gyihova bɔ ye menle nwo ban wɔ sunsum nu a? (Edwene 27:5) (Nea anwuma foni ne noko.)

15 Ɔhe David hanle sunsum nu katabui mɔɔ Gyihova fa maa la anwo edwɛkɛ. (Kenga Edwɛndolɛ 27:5.) Gyihova bɔ ye menli nwo bane fi awie biala nee debie biala mɔɔ kɛbɔ bɛ diedi mɔɔ bɛlɛ ye wɔ Ye nu la aze dahuu la anwo. Kɛzi Gyihova kata ye azonvolɛ nwo fi ninyɛne mɔɔ bavi ɛkɛ ne ara la anwo ɛ? Ɔbɔ ɛwɔkɛ kɛ akodeɛ biala mɔɔ bɛbaye bɛatĩa bɛ la ɛnrɛyɛ ye gyima. (Edw. 34:7; Aye. 54:17) Ɔwɔ nuhua kɛ Seetan nee ye menli lɛ tumi ɛdeɛ, noko bɛnrɛhola bɛnrɛzɛkye yɛ bɔkɔɔ. Saa bɛku yɛ bɔbɔ a, Gyihova badwazo yɛ ara ngoane nu bieko wɔ ewudwazo ne anu. (1 Kɔl. 15:55-57; Yek. 21:3, 4) Gyihova noko boa yɛ maa yɛgyinla adwenlenyane mɔɔ wɔ yɛ nu mɔɔ bahola azɛkye yɛ dahuu la anloa. (Mrɛ. 12:25; Mat. 6:27-29) Eza yɛ Selɛ mɔɔ lɛ ɛlɔlɛ la fa sunsum nu abusua mɔɔ bɛle mediema mrenyia nee mraalɛ mɔɔ bɛboa yɛ nee mgbanyima mɔɔ bɛnlea yɛ kpalɛ la maa yɛ. (Aye. 32:1, 2) Saa yɛhyia nu a, fane dɔɔnwo ne ala bɛkakye yɛ adenle pɔtee mɔɔ yɛbahola yɛalua zo

6 ƐWƆNƆSIANƐ NE

Duzu ati ɔ? Ɔluakɛ yɛze kɛ saa yɛbrayɛ Gyihova abusua ne anu amra a, kɛ mɔɔ yɛkɛla ye ali kɛ yɛkulo nɔhalɛ ne na yɛbɔ yɛ ɛbɛla kɛ mɔɔ ɔkile la ala a. Fa w’adwen bu Baebolo nu ndɛ nea

Baebolo ne lɛ abakɔsɛm fowɔlɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ kilehile edwɛkɛ mɔɔ anwo hyia la. Saa yɛdwene ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ anwo kpalɛ a, yebahola yɛade mɔɔ yɛkenga la abo kpalɛ na yɛanyia zolɛ nvasoɛ dɔɔnwo. Nea nyɛleɛ bie mɔ mɔɔ bahola aboa wɔ amaa wɔadwene Baebolo nu edwɛkɛ ne mɔ anwo la.

• Kenga nna ɛ nne do. Baabaale ne mɔ ɛkenga nna ɛ nne do la bahola aboa wɔ ma waahunu ninyɛne ne mɔ kɛnlɛma kpalɛ. Saa bɛyɛ abusua nna bɛkenga Baabaale ne a, bɛkola bɛmaa asɛm ne yɛ kɛ agya nwanwa debie nwo na bɛamaa awozonle ne biala ayɛ sonla foforɛ adwuma wɔ ahengale ne nu.

• Bo mfonin no mian w’adwen nu. Bo mmoden kɛ ɛbɛhwe menma nwo adwen nɛ bɛ ahunluntɔkelɛ nu. Kɛ ndianwoma, bisa w’ati kɛ: ‘Nzu ti a ahenia hanze anzɛɛ yɛle kɛmɔ ɔyɛle nu? Nzu a anka mede mendwen anzɛɛ bɛbabu me sa kɛ menwo ala wɔ suaholɛ kɛ ɛmɔ nu a?’

• Yɛ mfonin anaa sensan biribi. Baebolo nu ndwuma a ɛbɛsensan bɛbɔ mfonin kɔ boka wɔ maa ahe ahyia anzɛɛ ahɔ ahyɛ wɔ adwen nu na akayi abawosɛm ne kpalɛ, sɛ mfonin a ɛyɛle ne ɛnle mfonin kpalɛ koraa a.
