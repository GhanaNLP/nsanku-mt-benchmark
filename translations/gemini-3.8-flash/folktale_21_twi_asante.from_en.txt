Yɛnni aseɛ koraa, yɛnni aseɛ koraa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokorɛ)

SƐNEA ƆWEA, ABOSOM-AKOTERE, NE OPATUO YƐE A WƆNYAA WƆN SUPRƆMPRƆM A ƐDA NSO

OWEA, a ɔsan nso wɔ abodin sɛ Kwaduampɔn Kyerɛfoɔ, Opatuo, ne Abosom-Akotere, a ne mmorane ne, “Ɔbɔadeɛ wuo”, “Abɔdeɛ a ɔfa afe ansa na wayɛ biribiara”, “Ɔnanten brɛoo nanso ɔbɛduru aborɔkyiri a ɛwɔ akyirikyiri no”, mmoa yi nyinaa kɔtenaa faako pɛn. Na afei, wɔn mu biara nni yere. Na Adobe dua no tɔn ne ho maa wɔn. Da biara, sɛ Adobe dua no sɔre a, ɔkɔsom wei; adeɛ kye a na wasom wɔn mu foforɔ; na adeɛ kye bio a na wasom foforɔ. Da koro bi, berɛ a ɛduruu Ɔbɔadeɛ-wuo so sɛ ɔbɛnya Adobe no adwuma na ɔhwehwɛɛ Adobe dua no, ɛhe na ɔbɛtumi ahunu no? Ɛnna ɔkɔbɔɔ Owea ne Opatuo amanneɛ. Na wɔkaa sɛ, “Kɔfrɛ no.” Na Ɔbɔadeɛ-wuo maa ne nne so sɛ: “Adobe o! Adobe o!”

Owea suee sɛ:

Berɛ a ɔkɔeɛ no, wansane amma; ɔkɔeɛ, wansane amma, wansane amma, wansane amma!

Na Patuo no frɛe sɛ:

Yɛakari (sika futuro no) agu ara kwa, m! Yɛakari (sika futuro no) agu ara kwa, m! m!

Na ɛno na ɛmaa mmoa mmiɛnsa yi suu saa.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
