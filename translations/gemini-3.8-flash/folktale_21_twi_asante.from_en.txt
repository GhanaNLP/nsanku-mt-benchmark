Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐDEA ƆWEA, ABOSOM-AKOTERE, NE OPATUO YƐE A WƆNYAA WƆN NNE SORONKO NO

OWEA, a ɔne Kwaduampɔn Kyerɛfoɔ, Opatuo, ne Abosom-Akoterɛ, a wɔfrɛ no, “Ɔbɔadeɛ wuo”, “Afe-na-mayɛ-abɔdeɛ”, “Ɔnante brɛoo nanso ɔbɛduru aborɔkyiriman mu a ɛwɔ akyirikyiri no”, mmoa yi nyinaa tenaa faako pɛn. Afei, wɔn mu biara nni yere. Na Adɔbe-dua no tɔn ne ho maa wɔn. Da biara, sɛ Adobe-tree sɔre a, ɔkɔsom wei; ade kyee no ɔsom obi foforo wɔ wɔn mu; na ade kyee no ɔsom foforo nso. Da koro bi, berɛ a ɛduruu Creator’s-death so (sɛ ɔnhyɛ Adobe no sɛ ɔnsom no) na ɔhwehwɛɛ Adobe-tree no, ɛhe na anka ɔbɛhunu no? Na ɔkɔe kɔbɔɔ Tree-bear ne Owl amanneɛ. Na wɔkaa sɛ, “Kɔfrɛ no.” Na Ɔbɔadeɛ-wuo no ma ne nne so sɛ: “Adobe o! Adobe o!”

Owea no sui sɛ:

“Berɛ a ɔkɔeɛ no, wansane amma; ɔkɔeɛ no, wansane amma, wansane amma, wansane amma!”

Na Patuo no frɛe sɛ:

Yɛakari (sika futuro no) agu, m! Yɛakari (sika futuro no) agu, m! m!

Na ɛno na ɛmaa mmoa mmiɛnsa yi suu saa.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
