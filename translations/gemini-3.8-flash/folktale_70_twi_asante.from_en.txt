Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka yi yɛ nokware)

ABISADEE

Mmeranteɛ baasa bi na wɔwɔ hɔ; wɔn nyinaa dɔɔ abaayewa baako. Afei mmeranteɛ baasa yi firi Bontuku kɔɔ Cape Coast, sɛ wɔrekɔtɔ nneɛma. Berɛ a wɔduruu Cape Coast no, wɔn mu baako kura ahwehwɛ. Ɔfaa ahwehwɛ no, na ɔtew nhahan bi guu ahwehwɛ no so, na ɔhwɛɛ mu. Ɔhunuu sɛ wɔn dɔfo no awu, na ɔda abansoro dan kɛseɛ a ɛhɔ da hɔ no mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Nnamfonom, yɛn dɔfo awu." Wɔn mu baako kaa sɛ, "Mɛma yɛadu Bontuku seesei ara sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a yɛbɛkɔ akɔsie no." Nea ɔtɔ so mmienu no nso kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wobɛtumi ama yɛadu hɔ a, mɛma wanyan seesei ara." Afei baako kɔtetee aduro, de guu wɔn nkotodwe so, na wɔfirii aseɛ tuu kwan. Ɛhɔ ara na wɔduruu hɔ. Foforɔ no nso kɔgyee aduro de guu yaawa mu; na ɔfaa ne nantwie dua de bɔɔ aduro no mu, na ɔde petee efunu no so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Efunu, sɔre; efunu, sɔre!" na ɛsɔreeɛ. Afei nnipa baasa yi mu, wɔn mu hwan na ɔyɛɛ adeɛ a ɛyɛ paa?
