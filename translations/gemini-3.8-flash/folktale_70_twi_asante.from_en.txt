Yɛnni aseɛ koraa, yɛnni aseɛ koraa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokorɛ)

ABISADEE

NA mmeranteɛ baasa bi wɔ hɔ; na wɔn nyinaa dɔ abaayewa baako. Na saa mmeranteɛ baasa yi firi Bontuku kɔɔ Cape Coast, sɛ wɔrekɔtɔ nneɛma. Berɛ a wɔduruu Cape Coast no, na wɔn mu baako kura ahwehwɛ. Ɔfaa ahwehwɛ no, na ɔtettee nhaban bi guu ahwehwɛ no so, na ɔhwɛɛ mu. Ɔhunuu sɛ wɔn dɔfo no awu, na ɔda patio so dan mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Nnamfonom, yɛn dɔfo no awu." Wɔn mu baako kaa sɛ, "Mɛma yɛaduru Bontuku seesei ara ama yɛakɔsie no." Nea ɔtɔ so mmienu no nso kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wotumi ma yɛduru hɔ a, mɛma wanyane seesei ara." Afei baako kɔtettee aduro, na ɔde petee wɔn nkotodwe so, na wɔsii mu. Ɛhɔ ara na wɔduruu hɔ. Ɔfoforɔ no nso kɔfaa aduro guu yaawa mu; na ɔfaa ne nantwie dua de bɔɔ aduro no mu, na ɔde petee efunu no so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Efunu, nyane; efunu, nyane!" na ɔnyanee. Afei saa nnipa baasa yi mu no, wɔn mu hena na ɔyɛɛ adeɛ a ɛyɛ kyɛn so?
