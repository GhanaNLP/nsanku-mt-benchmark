Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SEDEƐ A AWARGUO BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU

Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ bi a na wɔfrɛ no Kwasi Gyaba ne ne yere kɔtenaa nkurotia baabi sɛ wɔrekɔbɔ famuro. Afei, sɛ Kwasi Gyaba kɔ wuram a, na ɔmfa nam biara mma. Wamma nam biara da.

Afei, da koro bi ne yere bisaa no sɛ, "Megye di sɛ, sɛ wokum aboa a, wotɔn, na wodi sika no anaa sɛ wode kyɛkyɛ nnipa, enti ɛnnɛ, sɛ wokɔ kwae mu na woamfa nam amma a, mɛkɔ me maame nkyɛn wɔ hedei, efisɛ mateetee sɛ mede nkura, ne mpataa, ne nwa, ne deɛ ɛtete saa betena ase." Ɔbɔfoɔ no, Kwasi Gyaba, tuu kwan kɔɔ kwae mu. Ɔhunuu ɔtwee bi sɛ ɔretwa mu rekɔ. Ɔkaa (ne tirim) sɛ, "Ɔtwee, baabi a wobɛda no, ɛhɔ ara na me ne wo bɛhome." Ɔtwee no kɔgyinaa dua bi ase. Ɔno (ɔbɔfoɔ no) maa tuo no so, pam! Ɔnini bi—baabi a ɔfiri firi baeɛ Nyame nko ara na ɔnim—bɛtɔɔ Ɔtwee no so, na Ɔsebo nso huruu guu ne so. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔɔ so hintaa hɔ yɛɛ komm. Ɔkaa (ne tirim) sɛ, "Awiɛ! amusuo bɛn na mahunu yi." Ɔnini no see sɛ, "Agya bɔfoɔ, bra, nea eti baako dwen no, ɛtɔ da a eti foforɔ nso tee, enti, bɔfoɔ, bra ha, na yɛnkɔ yɛn akuraa, na yɛnkye ɔtwee yi, ɛfiri sɛ yɛrenyɛ wo bɔne biara." Ɔbɔfoɔ no faa n'etuo na ɔdii wɔn akyi. Wɔkɔeɛ na wɔkyɛɛ nam no. Ɔnini no kae sɛ, "Agya ɔbɔfoɔ, m'ani gye wo ho, enti da ha nnɛ, na mɛma wo aduro; afei saa aduro yi, sɛ wode gu w'aso mu a, wobɛte nhahan, nnua, ne nsubɔnten nne, ne mmoa nyinaa kasa. Enti anadwo yi kɔkɔda, nanso nnhome." Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔdae; ɔgyae ahomegyeɛ. Ɔnini no faa aduro no de guu n'aso mu. Adeɛ kyee anɔpa a adeɛ nyinaa daa adi no, ɔbɔfoɔ no tee adeɛ nyinaa kasa ase. Ɔtuu kwan, na ɔbaa ne kuro mu. Afei, berɛ a ɔbaa no, ɔtee sɛ n'asebaa aba ne babaa no hɔ (fie). Afei na n'asebaa no nifa ani afira. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔtenaa dan no mu. Afei, kasa ne n'akaakyire (kyerɛ sɛ Mewerɛ fii sɛ mɛka), kaa sɛ berɛ a ɔhyiaa ne sewaseɛ no, na ɔresi aburoo. Ɔbɔfoɔ no tenaa hɔ komm, na nkokɔ mmienu bi a na wɔwɔ hɔ no (hyɛɛ aseɛ rekasa); baako bisaa baako sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dɛn na wotumi nya aburoo no, nso sɛ meba (sɛ merebɛfa bi a), ɔpam me?" Baako no buaa sɛ, "Adɛn, wonhunuu sɛ n’ani nifa afira, enti sɛ (wopɛ sɛ) wofa aburoo no a, sɛ wofa deɛ asɛe no afa a, ɔrentumi nhunu wo?" Kwasi Gyaba tee (yei); Ɔbutuu ne benkum so na ɔseree, twe! twe! twe! Ne nsew kãa sɛ, "Ao, me nsew, adɛn nti na woresere me saa yi?" Nanso Kwasi Gyaba daa fam hɔ resere, na ɔkɔɔ so seree. Ɛhɔ ara pɛ na ne nsew bɔɔ Nyankopɔn ntam guu no so, sɛ Kwasi Gyaba nkyerɛ nea ɛmaa ɔseree no wɔ Nyankopɔn anim. Nyankopɔn somaa abɔfoɔ sɛ wɔnkɔfa Kwasi Gyaba mmra. Ɔduruu kuro no ano no, ɔmaa ne dwom so:

Seesei mereka na mawu, Kwasi Gyaba e!

Merekɔkasa awu, sɛ yei si a, monnsu mma me. Kwasi Mpuroko Gyaba, ɔrekɔ, sɛ yei si a monnsu mma me.”

Onyankopɔn mpanimfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Montie deduani no dwom." Wɔde Kwasi Gyaba kɔɔ Onyankopɔn anim. Ɔkyeame no sɔre kãe sɛ, “Kwasi Gyaba, ampa ara woserew w’ase, anaasɛ ɔredi atoro?” Kwasi Gyaba buae sɛ, “Me wura, esiane aduru bi a mehyɛ ase nti, sɛ meka deɛ enti a meserewee no a, mɛwuo.” Onyankopɔn kãe sɛ, “Esiane sɛ woyɛ me bɔfoɔ nti, meremma wonwuo.” N’ase no kãe sɛ, “Me wura, ɔredi atoro, na ɔreserew kɛkɛ rew me, enti mepɛ sɛ woyɛ nkyerɛkyerɛmu mpo na ɛbɛma m'ani agye.” Ɔkyeame no kaa sɛ, “Kwasi Gyaba, woate?” Kwasi Gyaba buaa sɛ, “Ɛnde mesrɛ sɛ montu damena mma me, na sɛ mowu a, momfa me ngu mu.” Bere a n'ano gyaee kasa ara pɛ no, na Akokɔnini ne Akokɔbedeɛ bi reba; na Akokɔnini no ka kyerɛɛ Akokɔbedeɛ no sɛ, “Gyina hɔ din na memforo wo.” Akokɔbedeɛ no kaa sɛ, “Dɛn, wɔ nnipa yi nyinaa anim?” Akokɔnini no kaa sɛ, “Sɛ woannyina hɔ dinn a, mɛka asɛm a wɔabara no a ɛkyerɛ sɛ wotumi kɔ baabiara a wopɛ.” Afei deɛ Kwasi Gyaba tee saa asɛm no. Ntɛm ara ɔkaa sɛ, “Me wura, Nyankopɔn, ma wɔngyae da no tu, na mewɔ asɛm bi bɛka.” Nyankopɔn kaa sɛ, “Kasa.” Kwasi Gyaba kaa sɛ, “Meka ntam kɛseɛ no sɛ, me nsewama mfa ne ba no nkɔ.” Mpanimfoɔ no nyinaa kaa sɛ, “Ɛyɛ nokorɛ, ɛyɛ nokorɛ, asɛm no asa.” Ɛhɔ ara na Nyankopɔn yɛɛ mmara sɛ, “obiara, sɛ ɔyɛ w’adamfo mpo a, sɛ ɔha wo a, s ka asɛm a wɔabara no gu no so, anaa sɛ ɛyɛ wo yere na ɔyɛ saa a, ka asɛm a wɔabara no na gyae no awareɛ.” Ɛnam saa so na awaregyaeɛ baa abusuakuo no mu. Akokohene na yɛsuasuaa no.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
