HIƐ DƆƆ NƆ Wa ma nyɛ maa ya nɔ maa ye Yehowa anɔkuale kɛ̃ ke he kaamihi nɛ baa wa hemi kɛ yemi nɔ ɔ te si kɛ wo wɔ po.

“Nyɔmtsɛ, mɛnɔ ngɔ wa maa ya? Mo nɛ o ngɛ neneeto wami munyuhi.”—YOHANE 6:68.

"Munyu nɛ ɔ yɛ gbegbeepe; mɛnɔ ma nyɛ ma bu tue?" (Yohane 6:60) Bɔ nɛ Yesu kaselihi fuu pee nɔ́ nɛ Yesu de benɛ e ngɛ ní tsɔɔe ngɛ Kapernaum ɔ nɛ. Yesu de ke: "Kaa nyɛ yi nɔmlɔ Bi ɔ he lo nɛ nyɛ nui e muɔ ɔ, nyɛ be wami ngɛ nyɛ he." Nɛ e ya nɔ ke: "Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ nɛ yeɔ ye he lo nɛ e nuɔ ye muɔ ɔ, e ngɛ neneene wami, nɛ ma tle lɛ si ngɛ nyagbe ligbi ɔ nɔ."—Yohane 6:53, 54.

2 Nihi nɛ Yesu munyu ɔmɛ dɔ mɛ wawɛɛ ɔ kpa lɛ se nyɛɛmi. Anɛ a be hemi kɛ yemi ko kɛkɛɛkɛ ngɛ e mi lo? Ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ, lɔɔ se ɔ, ni komɛ—nɛ Yesu kaseli ɔmɛ hu piɛɛ he—bua jɔ nɔ́ nɛ Yesu pee ɔ he babauu nɛ a de ke: “Gbalɔ nɛ e sa nɛ e ba je ɔ mi ɔ ji nɛ ɔ nɛ miimii.” (Yohane 6:14) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e je kpo heii kaa jamɛ a kaseli ɔmɛ ngɛ hemi kɛ yemi bɔ ko ngɛ Yesu mi. Se benɛ a ka a hemi kɛ yemi ɔ, a yɛ nɔ nɛ a ye lɛ anɔkuale.

Bɔfo ɔmɛ ná nɔ mi mami kaa Yehowa sa nɛ a ja lɛ ejakaa lɛ ji níhi tsuo a Bɔlɔ; kaa Yesu ji Mawu Bi nɛ ngɛ “neneene wami munyuhi”; kɛ jehã se ɔ, Yesu ngɛ Yehowa fĩsemi kpele. Amlɔ nɛ ɔ, kɛ pe nɛ wa ma susu blɔhi pɔtɛɛ nɛ a ma nyɛ maa gu nɔ kɛ ka wa hemi kɛ yemi ɔ a he ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ wa hɛ mi nɛ kpa anɔkuale munyuhi etɛ nɛ ma nyɛ maa ye bua wɔ nɛ wa da si kpindii ke ka tsuaa ka ba wa nɔ ɔ nɔ. Lɛɛ ji: (1) Yehowa ji níhi tsuo a Bɔlɔ. (2) Yehowa nɛ e ha nɛ a ngma Baiblo ɔ. (3) Yehowa ngɛ e webi fĩe mwɔnɛ ɔ. Ke wa ná anɔkuale munyuhi etɛ nɛ ɔmɛ a mi nɔ mi mami kpele ɔ, wa maa ye anɔkuale ke hemi kɛ yemi kamihi ba wa nɔ.

7 Kane Kpo Jemi 4:11. Ngmami nɛ ɔ kaiɔ wɔ kaa Yehowa bɔ níhi tsuo. Enɛ ɔ kɛkɛ ha nɛ e sa nɛ waa ja lɛ. Nɛ ke wa he he nɛ wa susu e hewalu nɛ ngɛ gbeye ɔ he ɔ, wa náa nɔ mami kaa e ma nyɛ ma je wɔ kɛ je wa kaa amɛ tsuo a mi. Se kɛ̃ ɔ, Satan ha nɛ lakpa munɔhi hyi je nɛ ɔ mi kɛ fia kɛ bɔ nɛ a to nɛ e ha adesahi nɛ a je Mawu ngɛmi ɔ he jaje. E ma nyɛ ba po kaa je mi kpɔtsɔli ma de ke wami ba e he nɔ kɛkɛ. Ke wa be nɔ mami kpataku kaa Yehowa ji Bɔlɔ ɔ nɛ e suɔ wɔ ɔ, eko ɔ, wa ma bɔni he jaje jemi kaa e ngɛ nɛ e ma ba ye bua wɔ ke wa kpe kɛ hemi kɛ yemi kaahi.—Heb. 11:6.

8 Ke o je sisi nɛ o ngɛ yi mi kpɔ nyɛɛe kaa Yehowa ji Bɔlɔ ɔ, mo kpale o he nɔuu. Hla níhi a mi tso ngɛ wa womi ɔmɛ a mi kɛ ha odasehi nɛ tsɔɔ faa kaa Yehowa bɔ níhi tsuo. O ma nyɛ ma ná níhi fuu nɛ ma wo o hemi kɛ yemi ɔ he wami ngɛ sane nɛ ɔ he ngɛ womiyo nɛ ji, Anɛ A Bɔ Níhi Kɛ Wami Lo? kɛ The Origin of Life—Five Questions ɔ mi.

⁹ Kɛ ha o kasemi nítsumi nɛ nyɛɛ se ɔ, o ma nyɛ maa susu anɔkuale níhi nɛ tsɔɔ kaa Mawu ngɛ ɔ he. Níhi nɛ o maa kase ɔ be tsɔɔe kɛkɛ kaa o hemi kɛ yemi nɛ ngɛ kaa Yehowa ngɛ ɔ mi ma wa, se e maa ha nɛ o le e suhi a he ní babauu. ¹⁰ Kane 2 Timoteo 3:16, 17. Baiblo ɔ ji sɛgbe ko nɛ je Yehowa ngɔ kɛ ba adesahi a ngɔ. E haa si ngbami kɛ haa adesahi a sane bimi heemi nitsɛmɛ ɔmɛ nɛ e tsɔɔ wɔ nɔ́ nɛ ji Yehowa suɔmi nya nɛ kɛ nɔ́ nɛ e suɔ kaa wa pee. 10. Kɛ wa buɔ Baiblo ɔ ha kɛɛ? (2 Timoteo 3:16, 17)

¹¹ Mɛni he je nɛ wa ná nɔmí nɔ́ kaa Yehowa nɛ e ngɔ e mumi ɔ kɛ ha nɛ a ngma Baiblo ɔ? Yi mi tomihi fuu a he je. Mawu Munyu ɔ ji anɔkuale ngɛ blema sanehi a blɔ fa mi. (Luka 3:1, 2) E ji anɔkuale hulɔ ke e ngɛ je nɛ wa ngɛ mi ɔ he munyu tue. (Hiob 26:7) Baiblo ɔ ji womi nɛ a gba e mi fuu pe womi fɛɛ womi nɛ a ngma hyɛ, ngɛ mɔde nɛ Satan bɔ kɛ behi fuu konɛ a kpata e hɛ mi ɔ tsuo se. Nyumuhi kaa 40 sɔuu nɛ a ngma Baiblo ɔ ngɛ jehahi kaa 1,600 a mi; se kɛ̃ ɔ, nɔ́ nɛ a ngma a kpa gbee. Ga womihi nɛ be be nɛ a ma nyɛ ma je ngɛ mi ɔ ngɛ mi nɛ ye bua nimli ayɔhi abɔ ngɛ a daa ligbi si himi mi. (Abɛ 13:20; 15:21; Mat. 7:12) Nɛ Baiblo gbamihi fuu ba mi, nɛ lɔɔ ji odase kpa nɛ tsɔɔ kaa a ma nyɛ ma ngɔ a hɛ kɛ fɔ Baiblo ɔ nɔ. (Yos. 23:14) Ee, Mawu mumi ɔ nɛ e ngɔ kɛ ha nɛ a ngma Baiblo ɔ kɛɛtsɔɔ!

¹² Mɛni e sa nɛ waa pee ke wa je sisi nɛ wa ngɛ juɛmi enyɔenyɔ pee ngɛ Baiblo ɔ he kaa e je Mawu mumi mi? E sa nɛ waa sɔle ngɛ he, nɛ waa pee nɔ́ ko ngɛ wa sɔlemi ɔ nya. Mo kadi nɔ́ nɛ nyɛminyumu jokuɛ nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Jordan, nɛ a tsɔse lɛ kaa Odasefo no ɔ de: “I he we nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ nɛ a deɔ mi ɔ yi kɛkɛ. E sa nɛ ma susu níhi a he saminya ngɛ i nitsɛ ye tsui mi. Ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ, be komɛ ɔ i náa juɛmi enyɔenyɔ lɔɔ kɛbɔɔ. Se ke e ba jã a, i bɔɔ mɔde nɛ i peeɔ níhi a mi hluami ngɛ wa womihi a mi bɔ nɛ pee nɛ ma ná sane bimihi nɛ i ngɛ ɔ a he heto kɛ tsu ye juɛmi enyɔenyɔ ɔ he ní.” Be mi nɛ wa yaa nɔ nɛ wa ngɛ hemi kɛ yemi nɛ wa ngɛ kaa Yehowa nɛ e ha nɛ a ngma Baiblo ɔ he wami womi ɔ, wa ma wo hemi kɛ yemi nɛ wa ngɛ ɔ sisi tomi ɔ he wami.—1 Kor. 3:12, 13.

Bɔ Nɛ O Ma Plɛ Kɛ Hɛɛ Kristofohi A Huɛbɔmi Mi Ha

“Nyɛɛ wo mobɔnalo peemi, mi mi jami, he si bami, kpoo peemi, kɛ tsui si hemi he tade.”—KOL. 3:12.

“KE WA ngɛ si himi nɛ mi ngɛ nyagbahi nɛ ɔmɛ a mi hyɛe ngɛ nyagbe ligbi nɛ ɔmɛ a mi ɔ, e maa pee nɔ kake pɛ tso ko. Yehowa le kaa wa hia huɛmɛ nɛ a maa ye bua wɔ ngɛ blɔ ɔ nɔ.” Anɛ o le la nɛ a tsɔɔ sisi kɛ je la nɛ a to nɛ ji “Huɛmɛ Nitsɛmɛ” ɔ nya nɛ ɔ lo? Gbaa nɛ e ji kɛ ha wɔ kaa wa na huɛmɛ nitsɛmɛ ngɛ Yehowa we bi a kpeti! (La 119:63) Wɔ ji suɔmi nɛ fĩ jami weku ko nya kɛ ba kake ɔ fã.

2 Huɛbɔmi nɛ wa hlaa kaa waa kɛ wa nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi nɛ ná a pi huɛbɔmi kpa ko kɛkɛ. Mohu ɔ, e mi kuɔ nɛ se nami ngɛ he. Wá kɛ jami weku nɛ suɔ Yehowa kɛ Yesu ɔ mi bimɛ ji wɔ, nɛ suɔmi nɛ ɔ peeɔ wɔ kake. (Yohane 13:35) Se jamɛatsɛmɛm Kristofohi a huɛbɔmi nɛ ɔ bɛ nɔ kɛkɛ. E biɔ bɔdimi. Nɛ kaa bɔ nɛ huɛbɔmi fɛɛ huɛbɔmi nɛ ngɛ nimli nɛ yi mluku a kpɛti ɔ hii ɔ, jamɛatsɛmɛm huɛbɔmi nɛ ɔmɛ hu ma nyɛ ma ná haomihi.

Se eko ɔ, e maa pee wa ha wɔ kaa waa kɛ nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ nyɛmimɛ yi kpahi maa ya nɔ kɛ huɛ bɔmi nɛ Kristofohi kɛ bɔɔ ɔ ya hɛ mi. 4 Bɔ nɛ ma ha nɛ huɛ bɔmi nɛ ngɛ Kristofohi a kpɛti ɔ mi maa wa ji kaa, waa hyɛ nɔ́ kpakpa nɛ ngɛ ni kpahi a he. Be nɛ wa maa sɛɛ kɛ ha wa nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi ɔ ma nyɛ maa kai wɔ su kpakpahi nɛ a ngɛ. Bɔfo Paulo hɛdɔ ji kaa e kɛ e nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi nɛ hi si. E ngma ha Tesalonikabi ɔmɛ ke: “Wa bɔ mɔde nɛ wa le tso kaa wa maa na nyɛ.” (1 Tes. 2:17) Nihi fuu na kaa nɔ́ ko be nɛ daa si kɛ ha hɛ mi kɛ hɛ mi nitsɛ nɛ a kɛ a he ma gba sɛgbee kɛ ha nɛ a huɛ bɔmi ɔ mi nɛ wa. Wa ngɛ he blɔhi fuu kaa wa maa pee jã be nɛ wa ngɛ fiɛɛmi ní tsumi ɔ pee kɛ be nɛ wa yaa kpehi, kpokpo nɔ kpehi, kɛ kpe wuhi a nɔ kɛ kpe ɔ. Seeko wa ma na kaa kɛ̃ nɛ wa kɛ wa hehi ngɛ bɔɔ ɔ pɛ be haae nɛ haomihi nɛ ma nyɛ ma puɛ wa huɛ bɔmi ɔ ko ba.

5 Mawu suhi ma nyɛ maa ye bua wɔ nɛ waa ba huɛ bɔmi nɛ ngɛ oslaa mi ɔ yi. (Kane Kolosebi 3:12.) Ke wa je he si bami, kpoo nɛ a toɔ, mi mi himi, kɛ mobe nali kpo ɔ, wa ma nyɛ maa ye haomihi nɛ tsiɔ huɛ bɔmi nya a nɔ. Kɛ̃ nɛ nu haa nɛ lɔle mɔtɔ tsuɔ ní saminya a, jã kɛ̃ nɛ Mawu suhi ma nyɛ maa tsi pɛ kɛ heblɛ nɛ baa huɛ bɔmi mi ɔ nya. Amlɔ nɛ ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ wa susu haomihi etɛ nɛ kɛ wɔ kɛ wa nyɛmimi heyelilɔhi a kpeti huɛ bɔmi ɔ ma nyɛ maa kpe ɔ he. Be nɛ wa ngɛ lɔ ɔ pee ɔ, wa maa susu bɔ nɛ Yesu he si bami, kpoo nɛ e toɔ, mi mi himi, kɛ mobe nali nɛ wa maa kase ɔ maa ye bua wɔ ha nɛ waa ya nɔ nɛ waa pee huɛ nitsɛmɛ kɛ ha ni kpahi ɔ he.

6 Mɛni heje nɛ eji haomi? Ke be tsua be nɛ sɔlemi fia a, nɔ fiaa nɔ ma nyɛ ma he ma ye kaa e susumi ji nɔ́ nɛ da. Mo susu nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi enyɔ nɛ a kpa kpa he nɛ a ma tsu ní ngɛ a fiɛɛmi kuu ɔ mi ɔ he nɛ a kpa we gbi he nɛ o hyɛ. Nɔ fiaa nɔ ngɛ he se kaa e blɔ nɔ nɛ a gu ɔ nɛ hi pe kulaa. Anɛ o na bɔ nɛ a susumihi nɛ kpa gbi we ɔ ma nyɛ ma puɛ a kpa si himi ɔ ha lo? Eko ɔ, a ma nyɛ ma ha nɛ he nɔ womi ma ná a nɔ he wami. Ke a sɛɛ we sane ɔ he ní nɛ a dla we ɔ, a ma nyɛ ma bɔni abui kɛ mli fumi numi. Be mi nɛ be ngɛ tsɔɔ ɔ, eko ɔ, a ma gba a he kɛ ya tsɔ, nɛ nyagbenyagbe ɔ, a kpa huili he peemi—nɛ enɛ ɔ tsuo baa ngɛ nɔ́ nyafii ko heje.

7 Kase Yesu he si bami ɔ. E tsɔɔ e kaseli ɔmɛ nɔ́ nɛ he hia konɛ a nyɛ nɛ a ye mimi tomihi a nɔ—he si bami. Be kɛkɛ nɛ kaseli ɔmɛ daa si kɛ ngɛ nya demi nɛ a ngɛ he sɔle kɛ ngɛ nɔ nɛ pe nɔ́ ngɛ a kpɛti ɔ he. Bɔ nɛ pee nɛ Yesu nɛ kpo su fɔ̃ɔ nɛ gba nya nɛ ngɛ a mi ɔ, e tsɔɔ mɛ kaa e sa nɛ a bu ni kpahi pe a he. (Mat. 20:25-28) Ee, e ngɛ mɛ tsɔɔe bɔ nɛ a ma plɛ kɛ ba a he si ha. ⁸ Kɛ o ma plɛ kɛ ba o huɛbɔmi mi kɛ kpo ha kɛɛ? He si bami ma nyɛ maa ye bua mo nɛ o be munyu kpitihi a nya nɛ o kɛ kɛ ngɔ pa bimɛ he kɛ tɔ. (Kane Kolosebi 3:13.) KE SUBAN SLƆƆTƆ KPA

10 Mɛni he je nɛ e ji haomi? E ma nyiɛ nɛ e he maa wa ha wɔ kaa wa maa ya nɔ nɛ huɛ bɔmi kpekpɛpɛ nɛ hi wa kɛ nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi nɛ a subai je ekpa kɛ je wa nɔ́ ɔ he ɔ a kpɛti. Eko ɔ, ni komɛ ngɛ subai nɛ wa buɔ nɔ́ nɛ dɔɔ nɔ. Ni komɛ hu eko ɔ, a li bɔ nɛ a kɛ nihi hiɔ si ha pɔtɛɛ akɛnɛ a ye mɛ niseni ngɛ blema a he je aloo akɛnɛ a je kusuhi nɛ e he je nɛ eko ɔ, a bua jɔɛ munyu tumi he aloo a bua jɔ munyu tumi he nitsɛtsɛ a mi he je. Aloo eko ɔ, wa suhi ngɛ ekpa pɛpɛɛpɛ ngɛ bɔ nɛ wa kɛ nihi bɔɔ ha a blɔ faa mi—eko ɔ, wɔ lɛɛ wa kɛ nihi bɔɔ saminya be mi nɛ mɛɛ lɛɛ a ngɛ hiɔhiɔ aloo bɔ nɛ e ngɛ kpaako ɔ.

11 Kase Yesu kpo mi jemi ɔ. E ya nɔ nɛ e kɛ nihi nɛ a subai je e nɔ́ ɔ he ɔ bɔ huɛ. Kɛ̃ ɔ, bɔfo Yakobo kɛ Yohane je a he nɔ womi he su kpo benɛ a bi he blɔ hejihi ngɛ Matsɛ Yemi ɔ mi ɔ. (Mar. 10:35-37) Se ngɛ enɛ ɔ se ɔ, Yesu kɛ he si bami si e hiwe blɔ nya kpekpɛpɛ ɔ kɛ he blɔhi nɛ e ngɛ ɔ kɛ ba zugba a nɔ. (Fil. 2:5-8) Mo susu bɔ nɛ a juɛmihi je a he ngɛ ha a he nɛ o hyɛ. Kɛ̃ ɔ, Yesu to e tsui si kɛ ha Yakobo kɛ Yohane kɛ ni kpahi hu.

12 Yesu le níhi a he si nɛ e ma nyɛ maa hyɛ blɔ kɛ je e huɛmɛ ɔmɛ a ngɔ. E le kaa nɔ́ nɛ Yakobo kɛ Yohane bi ɔ tsɔɔ kaa kaseli ɔmɛ ekomɛ ngɛ heto he nya fiami hemi nyami ngɛ a he. (Marko 9:34) Jami mi nɛ a tsɔse mɛ ngɛ ɔ, jamɛatsɛ kɛ̃ mi nɛ a tsɔse Yesu ngɛ. Jami nɛ ɔ buɔ he nitsumi kɛ blɔ nya pomi babauu, lɔɔ he ɔ, e nu susumi nɛ a ngɛ ɔ sisi. Se kɛ̃ ɔ, e to e tsui si kɛ ha mɛ, nɛ e ha mɛ be konɛ a kɛ pee níhi a he nitsumi kɛ je he nɔ womi kɛ he sɔmi hemi nyami nɛ a ná kɛ ba mɛ nitsɛmɛ a he ahe ɔ.—Marko 10:42-45.

13 Mɛni o ma nyɛ maa pee konɛ o huɛbɔmi ɔ nɛ hi si daa? Ya nɔ nɛ o ná tsui kɛ gu bɔ nɛ o ma nyɛ maa pee nɛ o kɛ ni kpahi a subai nɛ dɔɔ mo ɔ ma to o tsui si ɔ nɔ. (Abɛ 14:29) Wɔ tsuo wa ngɛ subai pɔtɛɛ komɛ, nɛ e hiɛhiɛɛ nɛ tɔmihi ji mɛ we, se mohu bɔ nɛ a bɔ wɔ kɛkɛ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, ke nihi to a tsui si kɛ ha wɔ ɔ, e dɔɔ wa he wawɛɛ. (Kane Efesobi 4:2.) Jehanɛ se hu ɔ, e he hia nɛ waa le nɔmlɔtso nya gbɔjɔmihi nɛ wa kɛ ha ni kpahi. Ngɛ gbɛ kake nɔ ɔ, mo susu mɔde nɛ nɔ ko nɛ e he jɔ nɛ e yeee kɛkɛ bɔɔ kɛ piɛɛ nihi a he nɛ e kɛ mɛ tuɔ munyu ɔ he nɛ o hyɛ. Ngɛ gbɛ kpa nɔ ɔ, bɔɔ mɔde nɛ o ko kpa tsui nini kɛ ha nɔ ko nɛ e he be nɛ e tuɔ munyu babauu. Wa be nyɛe maa hyɛ blɔ kaa nɔ tsuaa nɔ maa pee wa huɛ kpakpa, se e sa nɛ waa kai kaa Kristofohi nɛ a subai sle kɔkɔɔkɔ po ma nyɛ maa pee kake kɛ tsu ní saminya.

BE NƐ WA NYƐMIMƐ NYUMUHI KƐ YOHI NGƐ AMANEHƐLUHI NGƆƆ PƐE

16 Mɛni heje nɛ enɛ ɔ ji haomi? Ke wa nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi ngɛ nɔ yeli kɛ hewamize nya nɛ a ngɛ nɔ yeli ngɛ susumi nya hu, eko ɔ, a maa pee nɔ́ ko nɛ e he maa wa ha wɔ kaa wa maa kpee nya. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, eko ɔ, a maa je a he ngɛ nɔ he, a mi maa fu mlamla, aloo eko ɔ, a be pee nɔ́ nɛ a le kaa a maa pee. Eko ɔ, a ma de aloo a maa pee níhi nɛ maa wa wɔ. (Hiob 6:2, 3) Akɛnɛ eko ɔ, wa li haomihi nɛ wa nyɛmimɛ heyeli ɔmɛ ngɛ kpee nya heje ɔ, eko ɔ, wa ma kpa babauu he blɔ ngɛ a dɛ.

17 Kase kɛ je Yesu mi mi himi kɛ e mɔbɔ nami ɔ he. E je mi mi himi kɛ mɔbɔ nami kpo kɛ tsɔɔ nihi nɛ a ngɛ haomi mi, po nihi nɛ e kɛ mɛ kpe we hyɛ gblegbleegble. Asafo ɔ mi bi komɛ de Bartimeo kaa e fĩ e nya. Se e ngɛ hlae wawɛɛ nɛ Yesu nɛ tsa lɛ, heje ɔ e ya nɔ nɛ e kpa ngmlaa pe kekleekle ɔ po. Ná Fiɛɛmi Kpe Ku Ɔ He Se

“Ma je Yehowa yi kɛ ye tsui tsuo ngɛ dali a kpe he ɔ.”​—LA 111:1.

ANƐ o kaiɔ ligbi nɛ a kplɛɛ o nɔ kaa sane kpakpa a fiɛɛlɔ ɔ lo? Ngɛ jamɛ a bua jɔmi ligbi ɔ nɔ ɔ, o ba piɛɛ jálɔhi babauu nɛ a ngɛ Yehowa biɛ ɔ jeje ngɛ he ɔ a he. (La 148:1, 2, 12, 13) Se kɛ̃ ɔ, ekeko ɔ, ngɛ jamɛ a ligbi ɔ nɔ nɔuu ɔ, o ba pee kuu nyafii ko nɛ jálɔhi bɔɔ pɛ ngɛ mi ɔ hu fã—fiɛɛmi kuu ko. Fiɛɛmi kuuhi yeɔ bua wɔ ngɛ blɔ titli nɔ konɛ wa nyɛ nɛ wa tsu he blɔ nɛ a kɛ ha wɔ kaa wa fiɛɛ Matsɛ Yemi ɔ he sane kpakpa a ngɛ “je ɔ tsuo mi” ɔ he ní.—Mat. 24:14.

2 Kɛ o nu he ha kɛɛ ngɛ fiɛɛmi kuu he blɔ nya tomi ɔ he? Nyɛminyɛ ko nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Wendy* de ke: “Blɔ nya tomi nɛ ɔ ye bua mi nɛ i ba le fiɛɛmi kɛ tsɔɔmi blɔhi fuu, e ye bua mi nɛ i to mumi mi otihi nɛ i su he, nɛ i kɛ nihi ba pee huɛ tsikpihi—nɛ enɛ ɔmɛ tsuo je suɔmi mi blɔ nya tomi nɛ ɔ mi.” Nyɛminyumu ko nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Rich hu de ke: “I buu e he kaa hegbɛ ngua nɛ i ná nɛ i ngɛ sɔmɔe kaa kuu nɔ hyɛlɔ. Be mi nɛ fiɛɛli komɛ náa hegbɛ nɛ a yeɔ bua nɔ ko nɛ e yaa nɔ kɛ ya suɔ nɛ a baptisi lɛ ɔ, kuu nɔ hyɛlɔ lɛ náa blɔ nya hegbɛ kpatalɔ nɛ e kɛ yeɔ bua jamɛatsɛ ɔ nɛ e yaa nɔ ngɛ mumi mi kɛ yaa jehahi nɛ ma a amɛ a mi.” Nyɛminyɛ ko nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Dinah de ke: “Fiɛɛmi kuu he blɔ nya tomi ɔ ngɛ fɛu saminya! I buu ye kuu ɔ kaa ye weku.”

BƆ NƐ GUSTAVO JOSEPH GBĀ

A fɔ mi ngɛ jeha 1947 mi ngɛ Cuba kpɔ nɛ he ngɛ fɛu nɛ ngɛ Caribbean ɔ nɔ. E ngɛ he nɛ Caribbean Wo ɔ he nɛ hiɛ ngɛ koomhi nɔ kɛ Atlantic Wo ɔ kpe ngɛ ɔ. Benɛ a fɔ mi se ɔ, ye fɔli fɔ ye nyɛmimɛnyiɛmɛ enyɔ. Wɔ nitsɛmɛ ni enuɔ wa hi Esmeralda kɔpe ɔ mi.

I kai kaa si himi ngɛ wa kɔpe wayoo ɔ mi ɔ ngɛ he jɔmi. Wa kɛ wa wekuli komɛ, nɛ a kpɛti nihi ji nyɛmimɛ yihi, nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi, kɛ nɛmɛhi kɛ nanihi hi si ngɛ he kake. Wa ná niba heii nɛ wa ye nɛ wa ná bua jɔmi si himi.

Benɛ i ye maa e je jeha kake lɔɔ, ye fɔli bɔni Baiblo ɔ kasemi kɛ piɛɛ Walton Jones he. E ji sane kpakpa fiɛɛlɔ nɛ kã ngɛ e he nɛ e nyɛɛɔ ngmlmli maa e nyɔngma kɛkɛ konɛ e ba su wɔ wa kɔpu ɔ mi. Be fɛɛ be nɛ e ma a, ye wekuli fuu buaa a he nya ngɛ ye nɛmɛ kɛ ye nani a we ɔ mi ngmlmlihi abɔ konɛ a kɛ lɛ nɛ susu Baiblo ɔ he. Ye fɔli, kɛ piɛɛ ye nyɛminyumu Pedro kɛ ye nyɛminyumu Ela a he ɔ, a bua jɔ nɔ́ nɛ a kaseɔ ɔ he nɛ e kɛ we nɛ a baptisi mɛ kaa Yehowa Odasefohi. Ela be kɛe nɛ e ma ye jeha 100 nɛ e ngɛ sɔmɔe kɛ mwoo kaa gbɛgbalɔ ngɛ Cuba.

Jamɛ a be ɔ mi ɔ, Yehowa Odasefohi ma nyɛ maa ja ngɛ Cuba nɛ nɔ ko tɔ̃we a he. Nihi le wɔ saminya ngɛ we mi kɛ ya we mi fiɛɛmi ɔ he, nɛ wa hɛɛɔ dɔtehi kɛ alɔtɔhi nɛ Baiblo womihi nɛ jua wa hyi mɛ tɔ ngɛ mi. Nɛ wa nyɛɛɔ si wawɛɛ nitsɛ! I kaiɔ Yehowa sɔmɔmi ngɛ jamɛ a “be nɛ da” a mi ngɛ ye jokuɛwi si himi mi ɔ kɛ bua jɔmi. Se “behi nɛ yeli wa” ya ba pee se.—2 Tim. 4:2.

BAIBLO MI ANƆKWALƐ

Lo Hɛmi Yaahi Ngɛ Baiblo Be O Mi

Baiblo kaneli ma nyɛ maa kai kaa Yesu se nyɛɛli ɔmɛ ekomɛ ji woleli. (Marko 1:16-20) Kɛ́ a ya wo huɛ ngɛ Galilea Wo ɔ nɔ ɔ, a kɛ ya tsuɔ ní.

A fĩɔ kpo aloo lolo kpa kɛ peeo woo ya nɛ a kɛ fĩɔ kpohi a mi. Bɔ nɛ woo ya a mi kpo ɔmɛ kle ha a daa si ngɛ lo nɛ a suɔ nɛ a gbe ɔ nɔ. Ya bibii ɔmɛ behi fuu ɔ, a woɔ sumpii aloo tɛ kɛ fĩɔ a he konɛ a nyɛ nɛ a nɔ́, se ya kpahi nɛ a gblaa a, a woɔ nɔ́ nitsɛmɛ kɛ fĩɔ a sisi kɛ tsohi aloo cork nɛ teɔ si kɛ fĩɔ a yiti je.

Gblɔ nya wa. Tɛhi nɛ ngɛ wo ɔ sisi ɔ ma nyɛ maa puɛ mɛ, loo loo nɛ hiɛ babauu nɛ a nu ɔ jiimi ma nyɛ maa hira mɛ. (Luka 5:6) Baiblo ɔ tu Yakobo kɛ Yohane he munyu kaa a ngɛ “a gblɔhi kpale”—eko ɔ, a kɛ abui kɛ kpo he kpa nɛ a kɛ ngɛ pee. (Mat. 4:21) Eko ɔ, loo nulɔhi kpalɛɔ gblɔhi nɛ ɔmɛ be fɛɛ be nɛ a kɛ ma tsu ní lɔ ɔ se. Luka kpale tsɔɔ he munyu kaa loo nulɔhi ngɛ “a gblɔhi fɔe” konɛ a jie zua loo ninahi nɛ ngɛ mi. (Luka 5:2) Lɔ ɔ se ɔ, loo nulɔhi maa sɛ a gblɔhi nɛ a gbli—eko ɔ, konɛ e koo fɔ.—Ezek. 47:10.

Yesu fɔ nyumuhi nɛ a belemɔ yitehi a kɛ tsuɔ ní ɔ nine konɛ a ba pee “nimli a hɛlɔli.”

A jua we womi nɛ ɔ. E ji je kɛ wɛ Baiblo tsɔsemí ní tsumi nɛ a kɛ suɔmi yemi kɛ buami fĩɔ se ɔ fã kake. Ke o suɔ nɛ o ya ha nɔ́ ko kɛ fĩ se ɔ, mo ya donate.jw.org.

Ke a tsɔɔ we nɔ́ kpa ko ɔ, Ngmami munhi nɛ a fã ke a wo mi ɔ je Je He Baabli He Ngmami Klɔuklɔu ɔ mi.
