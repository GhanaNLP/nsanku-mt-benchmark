If they do it, the parrot's tail will become red.

IT is said that there was once a Queen Mother, and she had given birth to a very beautiful young woman. When she gave birth to her, whenever she was offered to anyone, she said she would not marry. Every day when people went to ask for her hand, she said, "No." Now, there was a beast, Python, somewhere in the forest, and he lived in a huge cave. One day while he was lying there, he heard some hunters passing by, and they were talking about that young woman, saying that whenever she was offered to anyone, she said she did not want him. Python lay there and said, "I will turn myself into a handsome young man, and go and marry the Queen Mother's daughter." Truly, Python transformed himself into a young man; if you looked at him, you could not look away. He set off to the town. As he walked, he chewed kola nut. When he reached the outskirts of the town, the Queen Mother’s daughter had just returned from washing clothes and was spreading them out to dry. Python spat, and the spit landed on the young woman's waistcloth. The Queen Mother’s daughter said, "Why have you spat on my waistcloth?" Python said, "You are right, Amu." She said, "My name is not 'Amu'." He said, "You are right, Eson." She said, "My name is not 'Eson'." He said, "You are right, my wife." She said, "Then I will marry you." The Queen Mother’s daughter and Python set off for home. Presently, the daughter went to her mother and said, "Eh! Eh! Eh! I have found the one I want." The Queen Mother said, "Indeed! Where is my son-in-law?" He said, "Here I am, Nana." The Queen Mother welcomed Python and told him, "Today, sit down peacefully with your wife." When evening fell, he went and slept with his wife. It went on like this for a week. After that, he said, "My wife, I am going to my hometown, but I will return." His wife said, "Then I will go with you." Python said, "No." Python pleaded and pleaded, but the Queen Mother’s daughter would not listen. When day broke, she set off with her husband. As they walked, Python branched off into the deep forest. The Queen Mother’s daughter said, "Why, is this the path?" Python said, "Just keep your mouth shut and follow me." She walked on with him, and Python entered a cave. The Queen Mother’s daughter also entered. Immediately, Python turned back into a beast; and he said to the Queen Mother’s daughter, "This is who I really am; I only transformed myself to come and marry you. When you lived in the comfort of your mother and father, you thought 'Nothing bad exists anywhere.' I will kill you; search my head and catch lice for me; if you do not find any, that is the end of your food." The Queen Mother’s daughter sat there, parting his hair to look for lice. After a while, the Queen Mother’s daughter looked up and saw Parrot passing by. As she opened her mouth to say, "Father Parrot," Parrot put his claw to his mouth to signal silence. Parrot prepared his gun carefully and aimed it at Python's chest. Python said, "Search my head." The Queen Mother’s daughter said, "I will not search it; I am exhausted. If you will kill me, kill me." As Python stretched out his head toward the Queen Mother’s daughter, Parrot fired the gun, bang! Parrot entered the cave, took the Queen Mother’s daughter and Python, and went home to give an account of how he found the young woman and Python, and how he had killed Python. The Queen Mother’s daughter also told her entire story. The Queen Mother said, "Parrot, what can I give you to thank you?" Parrot said, "Nana, I want nothing; as for me, just go and bring red clay." The Queen Mother went, fetched the red clay, and brought it. Parrot said, "Nana, I will dip my tail into this red clay to serve as a mark." And that is how Parrot's tail came to be red. This folktale of mine that I have told, whether it is sweet or not sweet, take some of it away, and bring some back.
