5 | Ghana Aban - 2022 Emaa Kanyinshaɛ Gbegbe

KAFO-AWULA KASHOPO

COVID-19 lɔŋ-kpalwurɛ nɛ a tɔ kuto eka eka kikɛ to, nɛ Ghana gba ba bɔ to. Wahala nɛ Ghana-biya nya kabo bɛɛ wuu bɛɛ la aniyeshe, lala-chɛ nɛ la-kpanasa kpakpa man bɔ gɔmina nɛ kɛyɛ to. Ma kiti lara nɛ ma ka lara bɔ kɛnyɛ nɛ anikupe nɛ a fa pɔkɔ nɛ a dɛ COVID-19 kuto nɛ a yɛlɛ chɛmuru nɛ kashishiŋ, nɛ kaye kikɛ e chɛ anasa. Gɔmina gba nya ndara koto to a tina la-kpanasa nɛ aniyeshe kudurwɛ nɛ a la anɛ pɔshɛ ba eka nɛ a kuli ba kabo nɛ a dɛ nya koo.

Nɛ mɔ kɛkyɛŋ, nɛ kumo bee ba nɛ bee giligili nɛ bɔmbɔre mɔ nɛ bee laŋ lii dɔn-kpe-kpe mɔ dɔ, Gɔmɛnti, nɛ H.E. President Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo bee nyɛ kanyɛ-nyu dɔ kumo dɔ, baa mɔla lii GH¢100 billion COVID-19 Alleviation and Revitalisation of Enterprises programme, (GhanaCARES “Obaatan Pa” programme mɔ). Kumo yaŋ nyaa bee tɔgɛ bɔrɔkɔlɛ nɛ kanyi-churito kii anyi anyebɔ nɛ kumo bee kpara anyi maŋ mɔ deere.

Annyin dɔŋkɔ mɔ ere n kpenani gbee ti Gomɛnti mɔ, nɛ annyin duma kpa pɛpɛɛpɛ n ya kɔ kumu mɔ so lafaŋa kanyi ere n lii mɔ. Kumu ere pɔpe nite, anyi kashashi nɛ anyi be anyishi n la kii maasa n bɔ mɛ kpaa. Baebul mɔ sa anyi be kanyishi n la wura n ba kumu kɔ ere to. Kanyi mɔ, tɛtɛɛ Isaiah 9:10 to mɔ n lii, “Anyi be kabishi mɔ n lii, ama anyi be bu ti bu-nyankpane; anyi be nyankpa mɔ n lii, ama anyi be bɔ anyi be kanyi ti nɛ nyi kumu to”. Mɔ ere to, Gomɛnti kpa anyishi n la wura n sa kumu nɛ o pɔpe n ya bɔ anyi mɔ. Mɔ ere so, anyi be ti ka kpa n ya ma n bɔ mpɔla nɛ amɔ be kumu nɛ e be kɔ anyi be kanyi n lii mɔ, n kpenani anyi be kanyishi to nɛ e kanyi be lii mɔ ti ashe.

Maa kumo kodo kede 2022 Budget nɛ e bɔ nɛ kumo e la, amo nɛ mbe m-fa nɛ ashe asa (3) nɛ e ba nɛ kumo e nunumo nɛ la, e la kɛ kuli nɛ a nu Goman nɛ e ba sa kɛ gbaalɛ nɛ kumo e bɔ nɛ kumo ma kɛ nya chinchinka nɛ kumo kaŋ, nɛ kumo la kumo nɛ kumo ba n-te kumu kumu, nɛ kumo kɛ kuli bɛyiyi nɛ e tiya nɛ kumo sa kɛ gbaalɛ nɛ kumo gba nɛ ba sa anye pɛyɛ, kachenchɛn e la Mbelanto. N-kpalato e mɛ, Goman e kacheso nɛ e kɛ ba la kɛ jeso mbelanto nɛ ba nya n-tiya ba alubɛre nɛ ba nya n-wɔ ba kɛbaa nɛ kumo sa kɛ taa n-so bunyɛ nya nɛ ba bɛyiyi nya kalwɛ nɛ e pɛyɛ kɛkpɛ.

E kpe bee baa firi mmbiyipɔ be laryo nɛ m pɔr ba be jisi be ngelso be adwuma lariyɛ, Gɔmnati bee kuro YouStart kpaŋkpaŋ nɛ m bɔ kumo kpa 2022 nɛ m ba tiri mmbiyipɔ adwumanyɔso be suli nɛ m laa pɔsa ba be adwuma akpa m koro kumo bee m pɔr kumo e nya kumu.

2022 Budget nɛ e-ko, anɩ tɔgɛ m-bɔrɔ nɛ anɩ la kpa nɛ anɩ kpenge ashe-kpanashe nɛ e kpe anɩ kanyɛ-so-kama nɛ anɩ ba nna nɛ anɩ daŋ so kumo tɔ nɛ kufu-kpae nɛ e pɔrɩ na e-ko. Gomnati de kumo n-gbon so nɛ ashe-kpa pɛi nɛ kufu-kpa nɛ ku ba nna nɛ ku sa mbia nɛ ba kɔrɔ kanyi-she nɛ ba la kpa kanyi-lɛ mbia-bi nɛ ba tɔ. Nɛ anɩ wɔrɔ Kasowulebi nɛ

Gbambɔ-ŋwaasa (Kɔbiliŋ-kparawura Asheli)

1D1F (One District, One Factory): Fabriki nɛ industiri ana na kparɛ nungso nɛ bi kuli lara kuso to, kɛ ba lara maŋso nɛ kigbɛŋso to lara industiri ana nungso to. Kɛ gbiŋɛ kɛ ba ba lara agyima gbagba to kɛ ha Ghana-biye maŋso nɛ kikɛ to.

Lara kumo nna: Amo mɔ e du nɛ Gɔmnanti kpe laa lara mbia nɛ kpe ebi nɛ ba nyi kumu nɛ o lara nɛ o laŋ ba kumo kii nɛ e fɔr. Mɔ tɔ be laŋ nɛ Gɔmnanti be laa lara ligbi mɔ nkpɛ kpa lala nɛ o baa kɛtɛ to nɛ mbia mɔ to.

Kagbana: Kebɔ nɛ ba te gbaŋ nɛ ba daa kasɔwelɛ kutoŋ bantoŋ kɔmɛti ashe nɛ e-ofisi-ana to.

Kufiito: Nɛŋɛ e kpa bɔrɔ kɛlɛɛ maŋ maŋ k'e maŋ to nulo.

Bajɛti: Bajɛti lile bee nyaŋe kute kikɛ ne e bee lere kapo ne ba bee nya kowɔ ne mɔ ne ba bee kpe kowɔ e di. Nfono, kowurabi bee nyaŋe kute bajɛti ne e bee sule kowɔ ne e wule na anyi ne ba bee pɛrɛ n gba kowɔ n kpe di (nfono, lere kule, anyi nchu nna kanji nne kidi). Nde ba bɔ kowɔ n kpe di to, kowurabi bee bɔ nɛ ba lere saŋa ne ba bee nya kowɔ kusuŋ bee ba nya kowɔ kalolo.

Gomna lile kpe a dumo: Ene e ba e ya ne Gomna kumo nyo n bee dulo nyi a ti n fiti kumo nyo n bee da kpa.

Kekonɛ Nsaŋi: Emo nɛ e baa nsaŋi kɛkpakpa nɛ a gbaayi nɛ a wɔ kɛrɛbɔ kɛnaŋ to nɛ ere bee ba: abal, asukulu, asipiti, akparo, moto-kpanɛ, nchu ashen, mashini-ana nɛ abishi.

Ebunɛ ebi mɔ kpa nɛ: Nɛmɔ kpa nɛ a bo asara kpeŋkpelɛ nɛ asara koso fufalɛ ashiŋso pɛnɛ social security, gratuities nɛ pensions mɔ nɛ ba ya tɔŋ ebi nɛ ba te kpa afuli kumu ebi nɛ bɔ.

Kanyishi m-bɔrɔ-ana (DPs): Ene kpa bumo nɛ be ti e-kini lanto-ana anaŋ/ana kanyintɔ-ana nɛ bumo nɛ lanto nɛ bumo e-kumo e-fɛɛ kanyishi m-bɔrɔ to e-kumo bumo-kpa nɛ bumo maŋ-yiri bumo kanyishi be kpa-ana. DPs e-wɔ kumo nɛ kumo e-kini lanto-ana be gbansha, kumpam International Monetary Fund (IMF), World Bank, African Development Bank (AfDB) ana kumo nɛ kumo e-kini lanto-ana anyɔ-anyɔ nɛ kumo e-ti lanto m-bɔrɔ ana nɛ e-fɛɛ Germany, United Kingdom nɛ United States of America (USA).

Kufɔrŋwɛ bi Kutuŋkpa Gbayibu: Kutuŋkpa gbayibu nɛ ba wu Desɛmba nupɔ kapeŋ kɔ e ba kra nɛ nunpo.

IPEP (Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme): Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme (IPEP) anyi ere e sa NPP gɔbina nɛ dɛn nɛ gbagba nɛ kpeŋkpeŋ nɛ e baa lara ba boɔ a lara ohia saŋa na a laŋ tinti kalwɛlɛ nɛ, lere nɛ nteŋ teŋ nɛ kusubi bi so nɛ anyi nɛ ba maŋ wu anyi kpeŋkpeŋ a laŋ nɔg kpeŋkpeŋ anyi baŋ kpaŋ a tɛ ba.

Macroeconomic targets: Mba nna nye gɛmbilɛ ne ba kparɩ ba baŋ eburi nɛ aniteŋ so na e kii keŋkeŋ.
