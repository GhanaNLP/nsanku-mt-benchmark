5 | Ghana Gɔbina - 2022 Malɛ-ana Bɔjet

Kanyiti Kaso Kumoŋ Gbaŋbaŋ Kitoŋ Kumoŋ: Kebia Kitoŋ Kasɔwɔ nɛ Kusun Kaso

KAFULWULE KASHA

COVID-19 lumbire kɔrɛkɔrɛ nɛ be ashi nyi dɔ eŋu anyi nyɔmbɔlɛ pɛyɛ, nɛ Ghana mɔ ba dɔ. Kishi nɛ Ghana mambanase be ashi dɔ eŋu nɛ be kumo mɔ e na anyi be anyinɛ, kashinto nɛ kusuwɔ bee, Gɔbina mɔ e nyi kumo pɛyɛ. Me bee yelɛ nyi dɔ eŋu nɛ me bee ti nyi kɛba nɛ anyi kɔrɔ so nɛ anyi da mɔlɛ lumbire nɛ be ashi nyi dɔ eŋu nɛ e ba nɛ keshito nɛ kabaliso, nɛ kashishi bee pɛyɛ dɔ e be fɛ kumo so. Gɔbina mɔ dɛ kɔrɔ kusuwɔ kɛkɛ so e kishi nɛ kusuwɔ koso dɛ nɛ e tɔrɔ anyi be anyinɛ kulo gbelɛng dɔ.

Nɛ mɔ nɛɛ, kɛbaŋ nɛ kɛyiliŋ nɛ lara nɛ a na kumo e-yi nɛ kɛdɔ kuloŋkuloŋ bee nishi, Gɔfɛmɛnti, kɛyili nɛ Nyiniko Pɛresidɛnti Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo bɛ kɔman nɛ a wɔ kpaŋkpaŋ bee tɔ, kpalbi baŋ kpara GH¢100 billion COVID-19 Alleviation and Revitalisation of Enterprises programme, (GhanaCARES “Obaatan Pa” programme nɛɛ). Mɔ mɔ baŋ fa anyi anyishebi kɛfɔlbi nɛ a ba dɛdɛ nɛ a wɔ kɛkpe nɛ kumo lara anyi kɛbiishi tɔ.

Aniku kile fanɛ manɛ bee kpiini naŋkpa nɛ Gomnanti, fɛnɛ aniku bee kpaa kusuŋ fanɛ anikpanji kɔkɔ bee lara nɛ laabii loloŋ ebi nɛɛ to. N kanyi ebi nɛɛ to, aniku kusuŋ nɛ anyi bee laŋ nɛ anyi fo n kpaa nɛ aŋkpe fanɛ ebɔra a tɛɛ nɛ lala. Nawule-bee kpaa mɔwɔ n keni kpa n keni kpa nɛ aniku bee kpaa ebi fɛnɛ nɛɛ to. N kile fanɛ a bee saŋ Isaiah 9:10 to, “Abiligi e lu, ama anyi bee laŋ abuye n lii a kpaa; agowari bee chɔ, ama anyi bee lii a-cedars n ba n kpaa bumo naasɛ to”. N kile fɛnɛ nɛɛ, Gomnanti kpe kɔkɔ fanɛ e bee kpaa kusuŋ nɛ e laŋ kuso nɛ e gbaŋ na. N kusuŋ nɛɛ to, aniku bee laŋ kusuŋ n kpaa policies nɛ programmes nɛ a bee lara nɛ anyi kili kuso nɛ a fena kpakpa n lii n ba, n kpaa ashi ebi to n sa kpakpa-na.

2022 Budget nɛ e-kpena kpeŋkpaŋ nɛ mɔ nɛ e-bige kutoŋ sa anaasa (3) nɛ e-ba nɛ nini akulo kumo ko e-la ni a kpa e-State nɛ aniyaso nɛ a tɔ kpa a lara n-sa e-growth nɛ a na kpeŋ, a na tina nɛ a tɔ pini bebi n-ti tɔ e-gyama nɛ a na lara a-kutoŋ nɛ a kpeŋ n-sa bebi kakama, kpaŋkpaŋ bebi nɛ a la e-Youth. E-kikpala be policies nɛ a na ti, e-la nɛ a ba la n-sa e-youth nɛ ba pini ba-talents nɛ ba-potential a tina a sa e-prosperity nɛ anasɛ kakama e-nya a-gbana be opportunity.

Kuma n kute mbilito kiti n be kpa bunɔ kumo pɛi n sa bamo ekuutu bee kpaasaŋ, Gomɛnti bɛ kɔ YouStart bɔlɛ pɛi n lara 2022 n kute n dɔŋ mbilito n ba kpa bunɔ n kɔ mbulu e bɔ lara bee e kpa bamo bunɔ n lara.
