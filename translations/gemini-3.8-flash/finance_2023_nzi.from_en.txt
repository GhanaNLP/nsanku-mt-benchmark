ANYUNLUMU EDWƐKƐ

Ɛvolɛ ɛhye baha yɛ adwenle nu kɛ ɔle yɛ maanle ne ezukoa gyima mekɛ mɔɔ anu yɛ se kpalɛ la ko amgba. Kɛ mɔɔ ɔdole ewiade amuala la, COVID-19 ewule kpole ne dole yɛ wɔ 2020 kɛlata ne mɔɔ lumua la anu, mɔɔ manle ezukoa gyima ne mɔ hɔle aze, na ɔmanle ezukoa mɔɔ yɛnyia la zo dele yɛɛ yɛzɛkyele ezukoa dɔɔnwo mɔɔ yɛtɛyɛle nwolɛ ngyehyɛleɛ la wɔ ewule kpole ne anwo konle mɔɔ yɛhole la anu.

Mekɛ mɔɔ yɛdwenle kɛ yɛlɛbɔ mɔdenle kpole wɔ yɛ anyunluhɔle nu mɔɔ kile kɛ yɛlɛphe vi ewule kpole ne anyebolo nyanelilɛ ne mɔ anu ɔlua yɛ nzenzaleɛ ngyehyɛleɛ ne mɔ mɔɔ yɛbɔle ɔ bo kɛ yɛbadi zo la, ninyɛne dɔɔnwo bie mɔ rale mɔɔ wɔ mekɛ ne anu la, sɛkyele maekroikɔnɔmiki nyanelilɛ kpalɛ mɔɔ yɛnyianle ye vi 2017 kɔdwu 2019 la. Mɔɔ limoa la a le Russia nee Ukraine konle ne, akee eza ezukoa mɔɔ bɛgye mɔɔ anwo hyia wɔ 2022 ezukoa kɛlata ne anu mɔɔ ɔhyɛle na bɛpɛ zo la, mɔɔ manle credit rating dwenledwenlenlɛ dɔwunle aze la. Arɛlevilenu, wɔ ɛvolɛ bulu anu mɔɔ limoa la, bɛdole International Capital Market ne anloa bɛtiale yɛ, maanle ne anu ezukoalilɛ ngyehyɛleɛ ne mɔ yɛle se na kakɛ mɔɔ bɛfa la anye yɛle se, mɔɔ boale manle kakɛ ɛtualɛ yɛle kpole na cedi ne nvasoɛ dwule aze ndɛndɛ kpole la.

Zɔhane ati, mekɛ mɔɔ ezukoa mɔɔ vi maanyɛ mɔɔ bɛyɛ adwelie wɔ ezukoa gua nu nee yɛ maanyɛ ezukoa mɔɔ yɛnyia la dele aze la, ninyɛne mɔɔ yɛkɔkpɔsa yɛvi maanle gyɛne nu la ezukoa mɔɔ fole la vi kɛzi yɛ maanle ne kpondɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ vi maanle gyɛne nu la kpole, mɔɔ bɛva bɛboka kakɛ mɔɔ yɛkɛdua la ezukoa mɔɔ ɛlɛfo la anwo, bɔle yɛ ezukoa ne anwosesebɛ aze kpole kpalɛ. Ɛhye ɛmaa ɛbɛlabɔle ɛyɛ se ɛmaa yɛ muala, kɛ Aban nee ngakula muala. Angalɛ bolɛ mɔɔ ɛlɛfo la ɛmaa ninyɛne dɔɔnwo nee gyima bolɛ ɛfo, na mekɛ mɔɔ ninyɛne bolɛ fole kpole kpalɛ la dwule 40.4% wɔ October 2022.

Yɛnwu amane amgba kɛ menli, na Aban ne te bɛ nyanelielɛ ne abo kpalɛ wɔ ngyegyelɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ ɛhye ɛva ɛra la anwo. Kɛ Aban la, yɛkulo kɛ yɛda yɛ anyezɔlɛ ali yɛkile yɛ adwumayɛvɔlɛma ngakyile ne mɔ, mɔɔ Parliament, Employers’ Associations, Labour Unions, Civil Society, Faith Based Organizations, Association of Ghana Industries, Ghana Union of boka nwo

MUKYIA KYENLE

1. 2023 Maanmaale Boane Ne le mɔɔ Aban ne holē ye wɔ 2022 nee ezukoa dodo mɔɔ ɔkpondɛ kɛ ɔgye na yeava yeali gyima amaa yeali ye ngyehyɛleɛ nee ye gyimayɛlɛ nwo gyima la anwo edwɛkɛ sikalɛ. 2023 boane ne adwenlenzuzule titilii ne a le "Restoring and Sustaining Macroeconomic Stability and Resilience for Inclusive Growth & Value Addition”.

2. Wɔ ɛvolɛ dɔɔnwo anu, ezukoa ngyehyɛleɛ fofolɛ biala kile gyima fofolɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛbɔ ɔ bo la wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛkpo gyima mɔɔ bɛlɛyɛ bɛ la. Ɛhye ɛngyi ezukoa mɔɔ yɛlɛ la noko aze ala, emomu ɔva kakɛ mɔɔ bɛtɛtuale la noko ɔba, mɔɔ maa gyima ne mɔ anwo kakɛ kɔ ɔ nyunlu kɛmɔ kakɛ mɔɔ bɛtɛtuale la twe nwolɛ ɛzukoazo la.

3. Wɔ 2023 nu, Aban bɛli mɛla fofolɛ nee gyima fofolɛ nzonlɛ, noko eza ɔbamaa subane mɔɔ kile kɛ bɛwie gyima kɔsɔɔti mɔɔ bɛyɛ la anu anyeamia.
