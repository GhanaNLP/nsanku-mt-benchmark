MUKELƐ NYUNLU EDE Kwame

Ɛvolɛ ɛhye bahɔ do ngakyile nu kɛ ɔle yɛ maanle ne ezukoa nwo tete edwɛkɛ nu ɛvolɛ mɔɔ yɛ se kpalɛ la ko. Kɛmɔ ewiade amuala noko nwunle amaneɛ la, COVID-19 ewule kpole ne doale yɛ wɔ 2020 kɛnkɛnlimanle siane nsa ne anu, mɔɔ manle ezukoa nwo gyimalilɛ yɛle bɛtɛɛ, mɔɔ ɔmanle yɛ ezukoa mɔɔ yɛnyia la doale aze na ɔvale kakɛ fofolɛ mɔɔ yɛtɛyɛle nwolɛ ngyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ bamaa yɛaho ewule kpole ne anwo la rale.

Mekɛ mɔɔ yɛdwenle kɛ yɛlɛbɔ mɔdenle kpole wɔ yɛ anyunluhɔle nu wɔ ewule mɔɔ bɔle la nzinloo ngyegyelɛ ne mɔ anwo amaa yɛali zolɛ konim la ɔlua yɛ ngakyile ngyehyɛleɛ ne mɔɔ yɛbɔle ɔ bo kɛ yɛdi nwolɛ gyima la ati la, ninyɛne dɔɔnwo bie mɔ doole yɛ mɔɔ wɔ mekɛ ne anu, eza ɔzɛkyele yɛ maekroikɔnɔmiki nkunimlilɛ mɔɔ yɛnyianle ye vi 2017 kɔdwu 2019 la. Mɔɔ limoa la a le Russia-Ukraine konle ne, akee ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ rale wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛlɛpɛ ngyehyɛleɛ kpole mɔɔ kɛva ezukoa kɛra la azo wɔ 2022 ngyehyɛleɛ ne anu, mɔɔ ɔmanle kiretiti mɔɔ bɛfa bɛkile gyinlabelɛ ne dɔle aze la. Arɛlevilɛ nu, wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ limoa wɔ ɛvolɛ bulu anu, bɛdole International Capital Market ne anloa bɛtiale yɛ, maanle ne anu ezukoa anyunlu ɛzɛkyelɛ nvonleɛ yɛle kpole na ezukoakɛlɛ bolɛ zɔle, mɔɔ manle kakɛ ɛtualɛ yɛle kpole na cedi ne noko dwule aze ndɛndɛ kpole la.

Yemɔti, kɛmɔ maanle ngakyile ezukoa mɔɔ vi ebiama ekyi ne anu nee yɛ maanle gyɛne ezukoa mɔɔ yɛnyia la dele aze la, ninyɛne mɔɔ yɛfa yɛvi maanle gyɛne nu yɛba yɛ maanle ye anu mɔɔ bo zo kpole la, mɔɔ boka yɛ kakɛ mɔɔ yɛtua la anwo, zɛkyele yɛ ezukoa ne anwosesebɛ kpole kpalɛ. Ɛhye ɛmaa asetɛnla ɛyɛ se kpalɛ ɛmaa yɛ muala, kɛ Arane nee yɛ nwomenle mɔɔ yɛde maanle ye anu la. Angalɛ bolɛ mɔɔ ɛyɛ kpole la ɛmaa ninyɛne dɔɔnwo bolɛ ɛdo zo kpalɛ, mɔɔ ɛhye ɛmaa ninyɛne bolɛ ɛyɛ kpole kpalɛ mɔɔ yɛ 40.4% wɔ Ɔkotoba 2022 anu.

Bɛsɔle yɛ mɔɔ yɛle menli la yɛ nrelehe kpole kpalɛ, yɛɛ Aban ne nee bɛ di nyane kpole wɔ nyanelilɛ mɔɔ ɛhye ɛva ɛra la anwo. Yɛkpondɛ kɛ yɛda yɛ anyezɔlɛ ali kɛ Aban kɔmaa yɛ nsafoalɛma dɔɔnwo ne, mɔɔ Parliament, Employers’ Associations, Labour Unions, Civil Society, Faith Based Organizations, Association of Ghana Industries, Ghana Union of boka nwo la

MUKELƐBƐLA

1. 2023 Maanmaanyɛlɛ Ezukoa Nhyehyɛlɛ ne le mɔɔ Aban ne yɛle ye wɔ 2022 la anu ezinradonle, yɛɛ ezukoa dodo mɔɔ ɔkpondɛ kɛ ɔboɔboa ano na yeava yeali gyima amaa yeali ye ngyinlazo nee ye gyimayɛlɛ nwo nhyehyɛlɛ nzonle la. 2023 ezukoa nhyehyɛlɛ ne adwenleteleɛ a le "Restoring and Sustaining Macroeconomic Stability and Resilience for Inclusive Growth & Value Addition”.

2. Wɔ ɛvolɛ dɔɔnwo anu, kakɛ fofolɛ biala anu akee gyima fofolɛ dɔɔnwo a bɛbɔ ɔ bo a ɔtiati gyima mɔɔ ɛlɛkɔ zo la azo a. Ɛhye ɛngyi yɛ ezukoa nwo arɛle ala, na eza wɔ tete ne ɔlua bɔne mɔɔ bɛtua la ati ɔmaa gyima ne mɔ ezukoa mɔɔ bɛbɔ la kɔ ɔ nyunlu ɔluakɛ ezukoa mɔɔ bɛdɛnla aze bɛtua la fa nvasoɛ ba zo.

3. Wɔ 2023 ne anu, Aban ne bazi ngyinlazo nee gyimayɛlɛ ngyehyɛleɛ fofolɛ bo, noko eza ɔbamaa gyima biala mɔɔ bɔle ɔ bo la awieleɛ ɛyɛlɛ subane anyi.

Gyimalilɛ ɛhye mɔ ɛla ali wɔ sika kakye kɛlɛta ɛzuzulɛ mɔɔ dɔle aze ndɛndɛ la, ninyɛne bolɛ mɔɔ doale zo kɔle anwuma la, kakɛ ɛtualɛ adesoa mɔɔ yɛnrɛhola yɛnrɛzoa la, maanyɛlɛ sika ngyegyelɛ nee amanyɔnwenle afoa nu ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ rale la anu ɔnva nwo sika nee maanyɛlɛ nwo ngyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ Aban ne yɛle la. Mekɛ ekyi maanyɛlɛ gyimalilɛ mɔɔ vi Jan-Sept 2022 la ala ali kɛ mɔɔ doadoa zo la:

i. Real GDP anyunluhelenle dodo yɛle kɛyɛ 4.0 pesɛnte ɛvolɛ ko biala anu wɔ 2022 foa mɔɔ lumua ne anu, wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɛnee ɔle 3.9 pesɛnte wɔ zɔhane mekɛ kokye ne ala anu wɔ 2021;

ii. Ninyɛne boɛ ɛdelɛ mɔɔ kɔ anwuma la hɔle anwuma kɔkpula 40.4 pesɛnte wɔ Ɔkɔtoba 2022 mɔɔ vi 37.2 pesɛnte wɔ Sɛptɛmba nee 33.9 pesɛnte wɔ Ɔgɔso la, mɔɔ aleɛ nee ninyɛne mɔɔ ɛnle aleɛ la aboɛ mɔɔ hɔle anwuma la a hanle ye a;
