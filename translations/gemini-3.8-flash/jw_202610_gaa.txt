THESE PAST 100 YEARS

IT WAS reported in the January 1, 1926 Watchtower: “Soon, God will glorify his name. He will have people who will bear witness to him.”

Throughout 1926, Jehovah, through Jesus Christ the head of the congregation, helped Christians understand that preaching should be the most important work in their lives. Therefore, “the faithful and discreet slave” produced new literature, reorganized congregation meetings, and clearly explained things so that all Christians would know what they needed to do. (Matt. 24:45) Jesus Christ helped them acquire buildings and printing presses that would help them preach.

A NEW PUBLICATION HAS BEEN PRODUCED, AND THE WAY WE CONDUCT CONGREGATION MEETINGS HAS BEEN ADJUSTED

In April, an announcement was made in The Golden Age (now called Awake!) that a new book called Deliverance had been produced. At that time, the primary publication used in the preaching work was Studies in the Scriptures, but this new book replaced it. In order to print many copies of this new book, the brothers at Bethel bought a new printing press. This machine could print 22 books in just one minute. The Bible Students distributed this book to all the people they preached to.

In past years, the Bible Students studied the book Studies in the Scriptures at their congregation meetings. But from May onward, they studied certain articles found in The Watchtower. What fruitage did this change bear?

One congregation wrote to Brother Rutherford: ‘The little Bunker Hill congregation in Illinois greets you. Every Sunday, we study the subject that the congregation has recommended for study that Sunday. We are pleased that we understand the new information given to us so quickly.’

New information or a new understanding given to them at that time helped them to preach with seriousness. For example, an article in The Watchtower explained that our effort to develop a good personality, and also to treat people well and show respect to them, is not the most important thing. It was explained in that article that Jehovah has commanded Christians to bear witness about him. Christians love Jehovah, therefore, each of them must obey this command and be happy to preach.

Another article in The Watchtower also explained that the way true Christians show that they love Jehovah is by serving him well. In that article, all Bible Students were encouraged to preach as vigorously as they could.—Isa. 43:10, 12; Matt. 24:14.

The Bible Students made these adjustments right away. A few weeks after these Watchtower articles appeared, a congregation wrote a letter to Bethel. Here is some of what was written: “The thoughts brought out in some of the recent Watchtowers have comforted us greatly and strengthened

CONVENTIONS THAT EMPOWERED GOD'S PEOPLE TO PREACH ZEALOUSLY

At the end of May, a convention was held in London, England, and many Bible Students attended. At that time, all the printing establishments in Britain were shut down. Therefore, the brothers at Brooklyn Bethel worked hard so that they could print a brochure about God's direction for his people. This brochure was distributed to those who attended the convention.

On Saturday, May 29, the brothers began using the brochure. The assembly program for that day stated: “Every Bible Student here present is invited to advertise Jesus and the Kingdom. Go to the Alexandra Palace early in the morning, and the brethren there will tell you what to do.”

Over 100,000 books were distributed. The July 15, 1926 Watchtower reported: “The brethren rejoiced greatly. They felt that they had heeded Jehovah’s command to witness about him.”

The next morning, Brother Rutherford gave a talk at the Royal Albert Hall, and he spoke about what would happen to the world's powerful governments. Just a few minutes after the doors were opened, 13,000 people filled the hall to capacity. Those who did not get inside the hall listened at the entrance.

In Brother Rutherford's speech, he stated that mankind is suffering because the governments have rejected the one whom Jehovah has anointed as King and his Kingdom. Brother Rutherford spoke against the League of Nations and said that it would be destroyed.

PILGRIMS LEAD THE WAY IN THE PREACHING WORK

From about 1894, the society sent out some representatives to meet with various groups of Bible Students. In July 1926, the work done by these representatives, who were called pilgrims, changed. The work of the pilgrims was just like the work circuit overseers do today. At that time, they visited the groups for one day, but from that month onward, they would visit the groups for two days, and it was required that, at the very least, they spend 100 hours in the preaching work each month.

Brother George Draper, who was a pilgrim, wrote a letter to the congregation. He said that all the brothers, including the pilgrims, should be prepared to warn people. What would help them to do so was to study the Watchtower. Brother Draper was an anointed Christian, and he died in 1938.

THE BIG MEETING HELD IN NEW YORK CITY

The final convention held in that year took place on October 14-18, 1926, in New York City. The city’s newspapers agreed to advertise the talk entitled “Soon Will Be Fought the Greatest Battle on Earth,” which Brother Rutherford was to give. But when the time drew very near, some of the major newspapers said they would no longer advertise the convention.

The brothers decided to let everyone know what the news organizations had done. Therefore, on Friday, October 15, Brother R. J. Martin released a new tract entitled Against the People. This tract was produced to inform people of the bad things the news organizations had done, and also to advertise Brother Rutherford’s talk. Brother Martin said, “We have started printing our own literature, and our brothers will work hard until 1,250,000 copies of this tract are printed. After that, we will ask the brothers who attended the convention to distribute them to the people.” When the morning session ended, the brothers began distributing the tract. And by evening, they had distributed 300,000 tracts.

Over the weekend, the brothers distributed more tracts than they did on Friday. Therefore, in all, they distributed more than one million tracts! In addition, these zealous publishers distributed 3,783 Deliverance books and 35,140 booklets.

A NEW BUILDING WAS BUILT ON ADAMS STREET

At that time, many people wanted our publications, and therefore, the responsible brothers in Brooklyn saw the need to have a larger place to print the publications. In the past years, they rented the building on Concord Street, but they realized that this building was no longer big enough for the work. Therefore, the brothers bought a piece of land on Adams Street, which was not far from the building on Concord Street. They began building an eight-story building on the land they had bought. This building would provide them with a much larger space to print the publications.

The building on Adams Street was completed in 1927, and it greatly helped the brothers. From 1927 onward, many people joined the Bible Students, and further changes were made in the congregation, which led to them becoming fit to adopt the name Jehovah's Witnesses in 1931.

What Is Your Faith Based On?

When Jehovah’s Witnesses studied with you, what convinced you that you had found the “one faith,” which is the only way God wants us to worship him? (Eph. 4:4-6) Did you associate with Jehovah’s people because you saw love among them? Were you pleased with them because of their good conduct? Many people like to associate with Jehovah’s people because Jehovah’s people make them feel safe and loved. That is as it should be because what identifies true Christians is love and fine conduct.—John 13:35; 1 Pet. 2:12.

If you joined Jehovah's people mainly because of how they made you feel, then if a fellow Witness does something that upsets you, or does something unloving, or commits a very serious sin like the man Paul spoke of in the ancient Corinthian congregation, will you continue to associate with Jehovah's people? (1 Cor. 5:1) If how people make you feel is the main basis of your faith, then there is a problem. If so, what should your faith be based on?

HOW CAN YOU STRENGTHEN YOUR FAITH?

If your faith is based on the truths found in the Bible, your faith will grow stronger as you continue to learn more about them. Consider an example that illustrates this.

At one time, many of Jesus’ disciples stopped following him. What happened? At that time, Jesus said that they had to eat his flesh and drink his blood before they would have life, and they did not like what he said. They said: “This speech is shocking.” What Jesus said upset them so much that they stopped following him.—John 6:56-60, 66.

Later, Jesus asked his apostles: “Do you also want to go?” Peter answered him: “Lord, to whom shall we go? You have the sayings of everlasting life. We have believed and have come to know that you are the Holy One of God.”—John 6:67-69.

Why did Peter and the other apostles not get offended and stop following Jesus? The reason is, their faith was based on something good, not just on how they felt. Peter said: “We have believed and have come to know that you are the Holy One of God.” They knew what they believed and why they believed it. They compared Jesus’ words and actions with what they learned from the Scriptures, and they were convinced that Jesus was the Messiah. For this reason, their faith was strong, and so when the people stopped following Jesus, they did not do so.

What do we learn from this account? What we know is far more powerful than how we feel. Why? Because the things we learn from the Bible, such as God’s name, his promises, and the ransom, do not change. People's words
