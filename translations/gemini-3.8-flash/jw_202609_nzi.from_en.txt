(Wlo. 9:22) Noko akee, ye abotane mɔɔ ɔlɛfa yeazie zɔhane nzisi ne la ɛlɛba ye awieleɛ. Wɔ mekɛ ko ne ala anu, ɔbaboa yɛ maayɛagyinla sɔnea biala mɔɔ yɛbayia la anu amaa yɛahola yɛabɔ yɛ nee ye agɔnwolɛvalɛ kpalɛ ne anwo bane.—1 Kɔl. 10:13; 1 Pita 5:10.

(Yiz. 38:1, 2, 10-16) Ɛhye bamaa Amagɛdɔn konle ne ara, wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ Gyihova bamaa ye ɛwulalɛ ɛlɔnneɛ—Gyisɛse Kelaese nee ye anwuma dɔm ne ayɛ gyima la. Bɛbayɛ debie bɛatiaha Nyamenle agbɔvolɛ. Ɔti yɛbahola yɛanyia anwodozo bɔkɔɔ wɔ ɛhye anu: Saa Gyisɛse nee ye anwumabɔfoɔ ɛlɔnɛ ne bua Nyamenle ndɛne wɔ Amagɛdɔn a, Nyamenle agbɔvolɛ ne mɔ anu biala ɛnrɛdɛnla aze. (Gyɛ. 25:33; Zɛk. 14:12) Noko wɔ konle ne anu, Nyamenle mboane mɔɔ sonle bolɛ la anu biala ɛnrɛwu wɔ ye agbɔvolɛ—menli anzɛɛ sunsum ɛtane—asa anu.—Luku 21:28; 2 Pita 2:9.

Ɔluakɛ ɛhanle kɛ: ‘Gyihova a le me nveanleo a,’ ɛva Anwuma-Anwumanli ne wɔyɛ wɔ dɛnraka.” (Edw. 91:7-9) Mekɛ mɔɔ Amagɛdɔn babɔ ɔ bo la, yɛbahola yɛanyia anyelazo kɛ Gyihova babɔ ye menli nwo bane bɔkɔɔ wɔ nwonane, adwenle nee sunsum nu, tɛ kɛ ekpunli ala na kɛ ngoko ngoko noko. Ɔluakɛ ɛka kɛ: “Gyihova a le me nveanleo a,” Gyihova bɔ ɛwɔkɛ kɛ: “Ɔnrɛbikye wɔ.” Ɛhye ati, bɛmaa yɛva anwodozo yɛnea kenlema, ɔluakɛ yɛze kɛ yɛbahola yɛava yɛ nwo bɔkɔɔ yɛado Gyihova anwo zo kɛ ɔbabɔ yɛ nwo bane.

EDWENE 36 Yɛbɔ Yɛ Ahonle Nwo Bane

ADWENE Kɛzi yɛbahola yɛavi yɛ ahonle nu yɛalie Gyihova yɛadeli la.

Kɛzi Ɛbanyia “Ahonle Mɔɔ Tie Debie” La

“Maa ɛ sonvolɛ noko ahonle kpalɛ mɔɔ tie edwɛkɛ la.”—1 AR. 3:9.

MENLE tie menle mɔɔ lɛ tumi la debie kpalɛ ngakyile ti. Menle dɔɔnwo bayɛ zɔ kɛ eza menle ɛlɛnea bɛ anzɛɛ kɛ bɛkpondɛ kɛ bɛkpo anrɛɛ ndetelɛ a. Saa ɔfane Nyamenle mɛla mɔɔ bɛbali zo nwo a, bie mɔ yɛ kɛ bɛlɛdi zo amaa bɛanyia enyimanle, kɛmɔ ɛzonlenlenlɛ nzoanvolɛma mɔɔ wɔ Gyisɛse mekɛ zo yɛle la. (Mat. 23:5) Noko yɛdayɛ yɛtie Gyihova debie ɔluakye yɛnwu nvasoɛ mɔɔ wɔ ye adehilelɛ zo la. (Aye. 48:17, 18) Ɛhye le debie kpalɛ mɔɔ bamaa yɛayɛ tieyɛ a, noko yɛlɛ ndelebɛbo kpole kpalɛ mɔɔ bamaa yɛalie ye yɛali a.

2 Ɛhye ati a yɛtie Gyihova a le kɛ yɛkulo ye kpalɛ. (Mat. 22:37, 38) Yɛ ɛlɔlɛ ne ka yɛ maa yɛfi "yɛ ahonle nu yɛdi ye mɛla zo." (Wlo. 6:17) Ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛ maa Gyihova nee "ahonle mɔɔ tie debie" ka yɛ maa yɛbɔ mɔdenle kpalɛ yɛdi ye mɛla ne mɔ zo, tɛ mekɛ mɔɔ awie mɔ ɛlɛnwu yɛ ngome anrɛɛ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛnwu nvasoɛ mɔɔ wɔ zo fofɔle la a, na emomu dahuu. Nɔhalɛ nu, yɛ ahonle le kɛlɛbana, yemɔti subane kpalɛ mɔɔ yɛbalie yɛado nu dahuu la bahola ayɛ sɔnea wɔ mekɛ bie mɔ anu. Wɔ edwɛkɛ ekyi ɛhye anu, yɛbanwu Baebolo nu edwɛkɛ nwiɔ mɔɔ baboa yɛ amaa yɛanyia ahonle mɔɔ tie debie la. Mɔɔ limoa, yɛbazuzu mekɛ mɔɔ Gyihova liele Yizilayɛma vile Yigyibiti la anwo. Akee, yɛbanea Yuda Belemgbunli Dwɛhɔyiafate neazo kpalɛ ne.

ALIMOE NDIESE NAAKƆNE ATUAWOLƐNLI

3 Kolaa na Gyihova alie Yizilayɛlema yeavi Yigyibiti la, ɔmanle bɛ mɛla mɔɔ zɔle bɛ tieyɛ nzonle.

1. Duzu ati a menli bahola adie bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛlɛ tumi la ɛ?

3. Kɛzi Gyihova mɛla ne mɔɔ se bɛpɛ mogya bɛgua anlenkɛ ne mɔ kɛlata ne mɔ anwo la sɔle Yizilayɛma tieyɛ nzonle ɛ? (Nea nvoninli ne noko.)

8

Yizilayelnma ne mɔ diele Gyihova ɛzonlenle ɔnva nwo kɛzi Yigyibitinma ne mɔ bayɛ bɛ ninyɛne la

(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 3)

Amaamɔ bɛalie bɛ nwo bɛavi ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ tɔ zo bulu yɛɛ ɔli awieleɛ ne anwo la, ɛnee ɔwɔ kɛ bɛbɔ mboane nyinli anzɛɛ abirekyi nyinli afɔle yɛɛ bɛfofo ɔ mogya ne bɛto bɛ azua ne mɔ anlenkɛ tɛkɛ ne mɔ anwo. (Ex. 12:5, 7) Gyihova bapɛ sua biala mɔɔ mogya ne wɔ zolɛ la azo yɛɛ ɔbavuanle sonla nee abɔnsai faneva nwo wɔ sua biala anu. Noko akee, anrɛɛ Yizilayɛma bahola anyia edwɛkɛ anzɛɛ subane kɛ bɛanlie bɛanli. Nzrɔti ɔ? Bie a ɛnee bɛsulo kɛ Yigyibitima, mɔɔ bɛbu mboane nyinli kɛ ɔle nwuanzanwuanza la, bahola abu bɛ nyunlu. (Ex. 8:25-27) Noko akee, Yizilayɛma nwunle ye kɛ ɔhyia kɛ bɛyɛ tieyɛ ndɛndɛ na “bɛyɛle kɛmɔ Gyihova hanle la pɛpɛɛpɛ.” (Ex. 12:28) Gyihova yilale bɛ ɔlua bɛ faneva mɔɔ ɔvuanle bɛ nwo la azo.

4 Zɔhane nɔe ne ala, Yizilayɛlema vi Yigyibiti hɔle. Gyihova limoale hanle hwenle bɛ hɔle Nyevile Kɔkɔlɛ ne anwuma lɔ afoa nu, noko akee ɔmanle bɛ adehilelɛ mɔɔ dasanve adwenle ɛngola ɛnde bo kpalɛ a. Ɔhanle kɛ bɛhakyi bɛ nye bɛhɔ adɔleɛ afoa nu na bɛbɔ azua wɔ nyevile ne mini avinli. Wɔ ɛleka mɔɔ bɛbɔle azua ne, ɛnee ɔzɔho kɛ Falo ɛlɔne mɔɔ ɛlɛti bɛ la nee Nyevile Kɔkɔlɛ ne kɛdo bɛ avinli. Noko akee, Yizilayɛlema yɛle mɔɔ Gyihova luale ye sonvolɛ Mosisi anwo zo hanle la bieko. (Ex. 14:1-4) Noko mɔɔ bɛnwunle kɛ Yigyibitima ne ɛlɛba bɛ nwo lɔ la, “ɛzulolɛ kpole hanle bɛ” na bɛdoale Mosisi bɔbɔ kɛ yeva bɛ yera ye bawu! (Ex. 14:5-11) Duzu ati a bɛ nwo hyele bɛ wɔ kpɔkɛ mɔɔ bɛbɔle kɛ bɛbadie debie la anwo a? Na kɛzi Gyihova nwunle bɛ subane ne ɛ?

5 Baebolo ne ka kɛ wɔ Nyevile Kɔkɔlɛ ne anloa, Yizilayɛma “anrɛlɛ anyezɔlɛ angile wɔ [Gyihova] nwanwane gyima ne mɔ” mɔɔ ɔyɛle wɔ Yigyibiti la anrɛɛ bɛanle “anrɛlɛ kakyelɛ” ye nɔhalɛ ɛlɔlɛ ne anwo. (Kenga Edwɛne 106:7 nee ɔ bo ɛkɛ ne edwɛkɛ.) Bɛnwunle kɛzi Gyihova bɔle bɛ abakanlema anwo bane wɔ ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ tɔ zo bulu ne anu la. Noko bɛande Gyihova ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ ɔlɛ ɔmaa bɛ la abo bɔkɔɔ yɛɛ bɛannyɛ anyelazo kɛ ɔbalie bɛ bieko. Gyihova nwunle bɛ ahonle nu tebea ne, yɛɛ ɔhanle kɛ menli ne “dele atua . . . wɔ Nyevile Kɔkɔlɛ ne anloa.” Ɔnva nwo kɛ bɛdole Mosisi anzi bɛpɛle Nyevile Kɔkɔlɛ ne la, bɛanyɛ bɛ subane ne kpalɛ fee. SUA DEBILE VI YISILAYELEMABƐ KƆKƆBƆLƐ NEANLEZII NE ANWO

6 Edwɛkɛ mɔɔ wɔ Adendulɛ buluku ne anu la kile kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛneɛnlea deɛmɔti mɔɔ yɛtie Gyihova nee menli mɔɔ yekpa bɛ kɛ bɛli ye menli ne anyunlu la anu. Asoo yɛtie edwɛkɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛnwunle kɛ yɛbanyia nvasoɛ ndɛndɛ la ala a? Bie a Yizilayɛma ne bie mɔ hanle boane nrenyia ne mogya ne anwunvnehyire ne anwo amaa bɛadeli bɛ bamaanyia ala ati a. Eza, asoo yɛ tieyɛ gyi mɔɔ awie mɔ yɛ la azo? Wɔ Akpabɔdeɛ nɔe ne, Gyihova manle Yizilayɛma ne “nyianle anyunlunyia wɔ Yigyibitima ne anyunlu.” (Adon. 12:36) Noko akee mɔɔ nzinlii Yigyibitima ne hakyile tiale bɛ la, Yizilayɛma ne minnli anwodozo mɔɔ bɛlɛ wɔ Gyihova anu la na bɛdele atua. (Adon. 14:10-12) Wɔ bɛ subane ne amunli, saa yɛlɛ ahonle mɔɔ tie edwɛkɛ a, ɔbaha yɛ maa yɛanyia anwodozo wɔ Gyihova nee menli mɔɔ yedua bɛ kɛ bɛli anyunlu la anu. (Kenga Gyɛlɛmaya 23:4.) Yɛbali adehilelɛ mɔɔ yɛnyia la azo ɔnva nwo kɛ yɛnde ngɛlɛlera mɔɔ fa gyi zo la amuala abo fɔɔnwo anzɛɛ saa yɛyɛ zɔ a ɔbamaa menli mɔɔ kyi Gyihova la asa aze a. Kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na yɛanva nzisile ko ne ala mɔɔ Yizilayɛma ne vale la na yɛanyia ahonle mɔɔ tie edwɛkɛ ɛ?

7 Maa ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ ɛlɛ maa Gyihova la anu ɛmia. Yɛ tieyɛlɛ bahola ayɛ sɔnea wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛnnwu anyelazo nvoninli mɔɔ fane Nyamenle hyehyɛleɛ nwo ndɛndɛ anzɛɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛyia ngyegyelɛ kɛmɔ yɛyɛ tieyɛ la ati. Saa zɔhane

esi la, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkakye kɛ titili ne yɛtie Gyihova edwɛkɛ ɔluakɛ yɛkulo ye yɛɛ yɛfa yɛ nwo yɛto ɔ nwo zo. (1 Dwɔn 5:3) Ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛ la boa yɛ maanle yɛtɛnla aze yɛkendɛ ye yɛɛ yɛdie yɛto nu kɛ bie a ɔbagyegye mekɛ na yɛanwu nyilalɛ mɔɔ vi yɛ tieyɛlilɛ nu ba la.—Nol. 7:8; Mae. 7:7.

8 Wɔ yɛ mekɛ ye anu, Nyamenle menli ɛyɛ neazo kpalɛ wɔ tieyɛlilɛ nu ɔnva nwo kɛ mekɛ bie mɔ la bɛnnwu nvasoɛ mɔɔ bɛbanyia ye ɛkɛ ne ala la. Kɛ neazo la, wɔ COVID-19 ewule ne mekɛ nu, bɛhanle bɛhilele asafo ngakyile ne mɔ kɛ bɛva vidio kɔnferɛnsenyeli bɛyɛ bɛ nyianu ne mɔ. Asafo nu kpanyinli bie mɔɔ wɔ Peru la kankɛ kɛ: “Mɔlebɛbo ne, menganle kɛ adehilelɛ ne ɛnle kpalɛ ɛmmaa yɛ ɛleka ne.” Ɔnva nwo kɛ ɛnee ye ahonle ɛndɔ ye azule nu la, ɔbɔle kpɔkɛ kɛ ɔbadie Nyamenle na yeava ye nwo yeado ɔ nwo zo. (Mrɛ. 3:5, 6) Ninyɛne hɔle ye sɛkɛlɛ? Ɔhanle kɛ: “Menwunle kɛzi Gyihova yila tieyɛlilɛ zo la. Yɛ asafo ne anwosesebɛ yɛle kpole na yɛyɛle kɔnko kpalɛ.” Suzu neazo fofolɛ bie anwo. Nzinlii wɔ ɛvolɛ 2020 anu, ahyehyɛdeɛ ne hakyile adehilelɛ mɔɔ fale maanle gyɛne aneɛ asafo ngakyile nee ekpunli ngakyile nwo la. Mekɛ mɔɔ adiema raalɛ bie mɔɔ wɔ Japan la dele kɛ ekpunli mɔɔ ɔsonle wɔ nu la bɛbapɛ nu la, ɔhanle kɛ: “Mekpondɛ kɛ medi nzenzaleɛ ne azo na maanle meanga edwɛkɛ ɛtane meandianwo zo. Mekpondɛ kɛ menwu nvasoɛ mɔɔ bavi nu ara la.”

10 ƐZINLÎ NE

Nyamenle Belemgbunlililɛ ne anwo edwɛkpa ne mɔɔ yɛbɔ nolo la kile kɛ yɛkaha yɛahile awie mɔ kɛ ewiade ɛtane ɛhye bara awieleɛ ɔnrɛhyɛ. Yɛbahɔ zo yɛabɔ zɔhane edwɛkpa ne nolo kɔkpula mɔɔ ewiade ɛhye bara awieleɛ wɔ Amagɛdɔn la. (Mat. 24:14) Bieko, wɔ anwunvnehyelɛ kpole ne anu, edwɛkpa mɔɔ yɛbɔ nolo la bahola ayɛ kpole na amaa menli azie kɛ asoo bɛbalie Belemgbunlililɛ ne anzo anzɛɛ bɛnrɛlie. Wɔ bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛtanlo Gyihova la afoa nu, yɛ edwɛkɛ ne bayɛ kɛ nyane kpole “mɔɔ tɛ kpolera” la.* (Yek. 16:21) Ɔda ali kɛ, bɛ ɛya bava, kɛ mɔɔ Falo yɛle wɔ Nyevile Kɔkɔlɛ ne anloa la. Yɛbahola yɛaziezie yɛ nwo yɛamaa dwazotia mɔɔ bara kenlebie la kɛ yɛbali mɛla mɔɔ yɛse yɛbɔ edwɛkpa ne nolo la azo kɛkala ɔnva nwo kɛzi menli bɛabu yɛ la.

Nea Boɛ Wɔ “Ewiade Ne Sunsum Ne” Nwo

“Debie biala mɔɔ wɔ ewiade ne anu la . . . ɛnvi Selɛ ne.”—1 DWƆN 2:16.

YƐDE ewiade mɔɔ Seetan a di zo tumi la anu. (Dwɔn 14:30; 1 Dwɔn 5:19) Ɔko kpole kpalɛ la Kelaese ɛdoavolɛma anwo zo. (Yek. 12:12, 13, 17) Ɛhye ɛngile kɛ Seetan wɔ ɛleka biala mekɛ biala. Nɔhalɛ nu, Baebolo ne ɛngile kɛ abɔnsam anzɛɛ anwumabɔvolɛ kpalɛ biala lɛ tumi zɔhane. Noko Seetan lɛ anwumabɔvolɛ ɛtane dɔɔnwo mɔɔ wɔ ye afoa a. (Yek. 12:9) Eza ewiade ne sunsum mɔɔ Seetan maa ɔtɛla la wɔ ɛleka biala. Menli dɔɔnwo maa ɔnyia bɛ nwo zo tumi, saa bɛze o, saa bɛnze o. (Kenga 1 Dwɔn 2:15, 16.) Saa menli ɛnlie ɛnli kɛ Seetan wɔ ɛkɛ bɔbɔ a, saa bɛkɛyɛ ye kɛmɔ ewiade ne sunsum kile la a, bɛsɔ ye anye. Wɔ adenle fofolɛ zo la, yehyɛ bɛ ngɛkɛlɛ kɛ bɛyɛ ye ɛhulolɛdeɛ.—2 Tim. 2:26.

6 Baebolo ne hanle dole ɛkɛ kɛ kenlenzile ne mɔ anu, menli bayɛ “mgbanyianwomenevuanlɛma, memgbambanliinli,” yɛɛ bɛnrɛdie “ngilenu biala.” (2 Tim. 3:1-3) Ɛnɛ, fane dɔɔnwo ne ala menli da subane ɛhye mɔ ali wɔ bɛ edwɛkɛhanlɛ nu. Kɛ neazo la, amaneɛbɔlɛ kile kɛ anyebolo edwɛkɛhanlɛ maa basabasayɛlɛ ba. Edwɛkɛ ɛtane hanlɛ noko ɛbu wɔ awuke dɔɔnwo anu. Zɔhane ala a edwɛkɛ mgbane mgbane hanlɛ de a, mɔɔ bɛte kɛ mgbanyima nee ngakula kɔsɔɔti ɛlɛka a. (Ɛfɛ. 5:3, 4) Edwɛkɛhanlɛ ɛhye da ewiade ne sunsum ne ali kpalɛ.

7 Kɛ Kilisienenma la, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ neazo wɔ yɛ edwɛkɛhanlɛ nu, sɛ yɛ nee awie lumua anye a yɛtendɛ o, anzɛɛ yɛfa abɛlɛte ninyɛne zo a yɛtendɛ o. (Jem. 1:26, 27) Saa yɛneɛnlea yɛ edwɛkɛhanlɛ nu a, yɛbahola yɛabiza yɛ nwo kɛ: ‘Asoo meka edwɛkɛ ɛtane wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ awie ɛye m’abufua la, bie a wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ me nee menli mɔɔ ɛnze kɛ mele Gyihova Alasevolɛ mɔɔ ko la ɛlɛtendɛ? Asoo me nee me gɔnwo mɔ diedima adwelielilɛ ta yɛ edwɛkɛ ɛtane, nzonisɛlɛ edwɛkɛ, anzɛɛ ahendele edwɛkɛ bɔbɔ ɔ? Asoo meka edwɛkɛ ɛsesebɛ mekile me abusua ne anu amra? Duzu a bɛbaha wɔ me edwɛkɛhanlɛ nwo a?’

BAEBOLO EDWƐKƐTI

Bɛmaa Kelaese Ne Anzonudwolɛ Ne Ɛli Bɛ Ahonle Nu Tumi

Kɔlɔsaema 3:15 ka “Kelaese anzodwolɛ ne” anwo edwɛkɛ. Duzu a le “Kelaese anzodwolɛ ne”? Ɔboka nuhua anzodwolɛ mɔɔ ahenle kɔsɔɔti banyia kɛ Kelaese ɛdoavolɛ la anwo. Anzodwolɛ zɔhane vi nɔhalɛ mɔɔ yɛze kɛ Gyihova nee ye Ara ne kulo yɛ la anu ba.—Dwɔn 14:23, 27.

Somafoɔ Pɔɔlo dule Kilisienema mɔɔ wɔ Kɔlɔsea asafo ne anu la folɛ kɛ bɛmaa anzodwole ɛhye ɛli bɛ ahonle anzɛɛ bɛ sonla nu gyima. Kɛzi ɔde zɔ bɔbɔ ɛ? Giliki edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ mɔɔ Pɔɔlo vale lile gyima wɔ ɛke maa "bɛli be ahonle gyima" anzɛɛ "di tumi" la nee edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ mɔɔ bɛfa bɛfɛlɛ bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛdi agɔnwolɛlilɛ nu kpolerazulɛ nwo gyima (ampaya) la le ko. Asoo ɛhye ɛngile kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ "Kilisene anzodwole ne" pɛ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ li awieleɛ la wɔ yɛ ahonle nu ɔ?

Kɛ neazo la, saa yɛ nee yɛ gɔnwo diedinli bie le adwenle nwiɔ a, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛmaa "Kelaese anzodwɔle ne" yɛ "kpolerabɔvolɛ." Ɔbaboa yɛ yeamaa yɛazi kpɔkɛ wɔ mɔɔ yɛbahola yɛayɛ na yɛalie anzodwɔle nee koyɛlɛ yɛadɔ nu la anwo. Saa yɛkɔ zo yɛyɛ zɔ a, saa ɔwɔ kɛ yɛgyakyi mɔɔ yɛkulo la anzɛɛ yɛ mɛyɛlɛ bie bɔbɔ to ɛkɛ a, yɛda ye ali kɛ yɛlɛmaa "Kelaese anzodwɔle ne" di tumi wɔ yɛ ahonle nu. -Kɔl. 3:12-14.

Bɛndɔne buluku ɛhye. Bɛyɛ bɛmaa ewiade amuala Baebolo ɛhwehyɛle gyima ne mɔɔ bɛfa ndonwo ahyɛlɛdeɛ bɛyɛ la. Saa ɛkulo kɛ ɛye ahyɛlɛdeɛ a, yɛsɛlɛ wɔ kɔ donate.jw.org.
