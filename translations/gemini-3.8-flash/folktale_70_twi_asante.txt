EXCESS

There were three young men; they all shared one lover. These three young men left Bontuku and went to the Coast (Odwa) to buy goods. When they arrived at the Coast, one of them had a mirror. He lifted the mirror, dropped some medicine onto it, and looked into it. He saw that their lover had died and was lying on a bier. He said, "Friends, our lover is dead." Another one said, "I will make us reach Bontuku right now so that we can go and bury her." The third one also said, "If you can make us reach there, I will resurrect her immediately." Then the first one went and fetched medicine; he applied it to their knees, and they set off. Instantly, they arrived there. Then the other one also took medicine, put it into a brass basin, took his whisk, dipped it into the medicine, and sprinkled it over the corpse. He said, "Awake, corpse! Awake, corpse!" And she revived. Now, of these three men, who did the greatest deed?
