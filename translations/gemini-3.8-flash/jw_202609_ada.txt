A BIBLE TOPIC

"Let the Peace of Christ Rule in Your Hearts"

Colossians 3:15 speaks about "the peace of Christ." What is "the peace of Christ"? It is the peace that a person gets because he becomes a disciple of Christ. We get this peace because we know that Jehovah and his Son, Jesus, love us very much.—John 14:23, 27.

The apostle Paul encouraged the Christians in the Colossian congregation to let this peace rule in their hearts, or within their very person. In what way? The Greek word that Paul used for “rule” in this scripture is associated with a referee, who is someone who directs a game or ensures that the rules of a game are applied. This means that, just as that person is the one who makes decisions in the game, so also the “peace of Christ” should control how we conduct ourselves in life.

For example, if we have a disagreement with a brother, or we do not agree with him about something, we should let the “peace of Christ” control us like a referee. It will help us to know what we should do to preserve peace. But before we can do that, it will require that we sacrifice some things we like, and freely forgive the mistakes of others. If we do that, it means we have let the “peace of Christ” rule in our hearts.—Col. 3:12-14.
