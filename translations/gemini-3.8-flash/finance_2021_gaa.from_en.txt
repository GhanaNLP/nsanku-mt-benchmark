SHIKASHITSƆƆMNƆKWƐLƆ LƐ SANEYELI SHISHIJIEE

Ghana maŋbii kpakpai lɛ fɔ̃ nɔ yɛ December 2020 kɛtsɔ amɛgbɛjianɔtoo lɛ nɔ koni amɛhiɛ H.E. Maŋtsɛ, Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo nɔ kɛya nɔ yɛ be kroko mli koni eya nɔ efee nitsumɔ kpakpa lɛ kɛ babaoo hu. Hemɔkɛyeli ni wɔ-Ghanabii anyɛmimei lɛ kɛfɔ̃ Nɔyeli nɛɛ nɔ lɛ ji nɔ ko ni wɔkɛ feee fɛo kwraa.

Afí 2021 Shika-tsɔɔmɔ wolo lɛ ji shika-tsɔɔmɔ wolo kenteŋkrɔŋ ni toɔ wɔ maŋ shika nitsumɔi ahe gbɛ ekoŋŋ koni ebu gbɔmɛi awala he, ewo jarayeli nitsumɔi ni yɔɔ naagbee mli lɛ ahewalɛ ekoŋŋ, ni wɔkɛ wɔhewalɛ fɛɛ ama Ghana ni hi jogbaŋŋ maŋmɔ nɔ kɛjɛ COVID-19 sɛɛ. Shika-tsɔɔmɔ wolo nɛɛ damɔ wɔ ninaa ni kɔɔ maŋ nɛɛ tsakemɔ kɛ shikpɔŋ-kpalɛ he shika-yeli nɔmamɔ maŋmɔ nɔ.

Yɛ be fɛɛ mli kɛha klɛŋklɛŋ afii ejwɛ lɛ mli fã kpatuu lɛ, wɔsaa shikpɔŋ lɛ he nitsumɔi lɛ ekoŋŋ, kɛtsɔ nitsumɔi srɔtoi ni ato he gbɛjianɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ kɛha shika he nibii ahe saawatoo, shika kpatamɔ nibii ni afee lɛ nɔ, shishijee kɛha shwelɛ kɛ hewalɛwoo nitsumɔi, wɔye wɔbua private sector lɛ, kɛ maŋbii ahebuu nitsumɔi ni akɛba. Kɛyashi afi 2019 naagbee lɛ, nɔ ko kwraa bɛ ni miiha wɔkpoo shikpɔŋ lɛ shika he nitsumɔi atsakee lɛ nɔ kɛjɛ nɔyaa kpoji kpakpai ni anaa kɛ jeŋ muu fɛɛ kpɛlɛmɔ ni akɛba akɛ maŋ ni miiya hiɛ, beni COVID-19 hela gbohii nɛɛ ba wɔnɔ. Nɛkɛ hela nɛɛ gbeyeishemɔ hewalɛ lɛ, jeŋ muu fɛɛ he shikpɔŋ nitsumɔi kɛ shika he nibii fɛɛ ena, ni Ghana hu fata he.

Covid-19 hela babaoo nɛɛ kɛ naagbai babaoo ba ni eha wɔjarayeli kɛ wɔdabiishihilɛ fɛɛ fite. Kɛkɛ ni, nɔyeli lɛ ena bɔ ni hela nɛɛ eba wɔnɔ kɛ hejaramɔ babaoo, ni ebɔi gbɛjianɔtoi komɛi fɛɛ nitsumɔ koni ekɛba jarayeli lɛ ekoŋŋ kɛ shihilɛ lɛ kɛba bɔ ni etsɔ hiɛ eji lɛ nɔ. Hiɛnɔkamɔ fɛɛ etãko ejaakɛ wɔna gbɛi hee komɛi ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔtsɔ nɔ wɔfee nibii babaoo. Nɔyeli lɛ efee ejuŋmɔ akɛ ebaakwɛ ni maŋbii lɛ fɛɛ ahi shi kɛ hewalɛ, ni no hewɔ lɛ eto gbɛjianɔ koni awo Ghanabii tsofani koni wɔ fɛɛ wɔhe awa kɛ ma Ghana ni wɔtaoɔ ni wɔna lɛ.

Wɔkɛ nyɛ efee ekome kɛtsɔ haomɔi amli po tamɔ wɔ shweshweeshwefeemɔ gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ fã ko, ni wɔtsɔ ekãa nɔ wɔfee nibii bɔni afee ni wɔ shika kɛ nibii anɔkpɔɔ lɛ aku sɛɛ etee shweshweeshwefeemɔ gbɛ lɛ nɔ. Kɛjɛ ŋmɛnɛ kɛya lɛ, Ghana bii aaafee 1.2 million baanyɛ aná sekɛndri kaseemɔ kpakpa kɛha gbenaa, wɔtsuu gbɛjianɔtoo ni kwɛɔ shika toohii anɔ lɛ he, ni shikatolɔi babaoo ená amɛ shikatooi kɛjɛ shika toohii ni fite lɛ amli. Kɛfata he lɛ, ama lɔle tsurɔɔ he ko, ni wɔna klɛŋklɛŋ lɔlei ni atsu yɛ jɛmɛ lɛ yɛ wɔ jara nɔ kɛ wɔ gbɛi anɔ.

Wɔkpɛlɛɔ nɔ yɛ anɔkwale mli akɛ wɔkpe naagbai komɛi be ni wɔbɔ mɔdɛŋ akɛ wɔbaaha wɔshikpɔŋ lɛ shwelɛ nibii lɛ aya hiɛ. Nɔyeli lɛ miikɛ ekãa eto gbɛjianɔi koni akɛtsu naagbai nɛɛ ahe nii ni akɛ hejɔlɛ aba nitsumɔi lɛ amli. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, wɔje GH¢750 million CAP-BuSS Gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ shishi yɛ May 2020 ni enɛ eha yelikɛbuamɔ eba kɛha Nitsumɔ Bibii, Nitsumɔ Kuku Bibii kɛ Nitsumɔ Agboi (MSMEs) kɛ mɛi krokomɛi babaoo.

Tamɔ bɔ ni ebaaa dã lɛ, atsɛ́ wɔ koni wɔkpe wɔdɛŋsrɔi amli kɛtswa wɔmaŋ lɛ, kɛtsɔ gbɛtsɔɔmɔi wuji lɛ anɔfonyɔmɔ nɔ bɔni afee ni shikpɔŋ nɔ gbɛfaŋnɔ baaha mɔ fɛɛ mɔ. Yɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ sɛɛ lɛ, wɔwo shi akɛ wɔbaasɔmɔ Ghana kɛ wɔhewalɛ fɛɛ kɛ wɔtsui fɛɛ.

Nyɔŋmɔ ajɔɔ wɔ fɛɛ.

Ghana ahi shi daa!

Ken Ofori-Atta Shika nɔŋtsɔ

KƐJIE SHISHI

1. Afi 2021 Citizen's Budget lɛ ji nɔ ni Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ nyɛ etsu yɛ afi 2020 kɛ shika abɔ ni eto eyiŋ akɛ ebaná kɛjɛ tooke mli kɛtsu mlai kɛ nitsumɔi ahe nii yɛ afi 2021 mli lɛ he kuku. Afi 2021 Budget lɛ saneyitso ji “Egbelie, Emaashiŋ kɛ Eyafeko”. Saneyitso nɛɛ damɔ anɔkwale ni yɔɔ mli akɛ yɛ afii babaoo amli lɛ Budget hee fɛɛ Budget kɛ nitsumɔi heei babaoo baa ni abatsutsu he nii ni ashiɔ nitsumɔi ni yaa nɔ amrɔ nɛɛ ahe. Enɛ efeee nɔ ni kɛ nɔnyɛɛ baa wɔshika nɔ kɛkɛ, shi moŋ kɛjɛ blema lɛ, ebaa akɛ anyɛɛɛ atutu nyɔmɔi, ni akɛ hewalɛ babaoo ewooo contractors lii adɛŋ yɛ be ni sa mli yɛ nitsumɔ ni amɛtsu lɛ he. Enɛ haa nitsumɔi lɛ a-nyɔmɔ yaa nɔ efeɔ kpele ejaakɛ nyɔmɔ ni akɛtsɛɛ lɛ kɛ he-nɔfatalɔ shika (interest) baa.

2. Yɛ afi 2021 mli lɛ, Aban lɛ to gbɛjianɔ akɛ ebaatsɔ gbɛ hee nɔ. Yiŋtoo lɛ ji ni akɛba nitsumɔi ni afooo naa lɛ ashihilii lɛ naagbee kɛtsɔ shiwoo kɛha nitsumɔi ni yaa nɔ amrɔ nɛɛ agbeenaafoo mli.

Maŋ hegbɛ shishitsuulɔi a-nitsumɔ

3. Yisɔɔ mli sane nɔŋŋ yɛ bɔ ni hegbɛjianɔtoo lɛ tsu nii eha yɛ afi 2020 mli lɛ ji nɔ ni nyiɛ sɛɛ nɛɛ:

• Afi lɛ mli nyɔji nɛɛhu klɛŋklɛŋ lii lɛ na GDP kɛteke nɔ lɛ fɛɛ hewalɛwoo nɔyaa kɛyashɛ 0.2 percent. Akpaa gbɛ akɛ GDP nɔyaa lɛ baashɛ 0.9 percent kɛyashi afi lɛ naagbee;

• Non-Oil real GDP ná shishijee nyɔji nɛɛhu lɛ mli shwɛshwɛɛshwɛ yɛ mli 0.4 percent hewalɛyaa, ni akpaa gbɛ akɛ afi lɛ baaba naagbee yɛ 1.6 percent nɔ;

• December naagbee nibii anyɔɔmɔ mli kpeemɔ lɛ bafee 10.4 percent kɛ́ akɛto bɔ ni akpa gbɛ ni ato he gbɛjianɔ ekoŋŋ ni ji 11.0 percent lɛ he;

• Shikpɔŋ kɛ shikpɔŋ nɔ gbɛjianɔtoo nina fɛɛ nɔ shika nina nɔ ji GDP lɛ mli 11.7 percent yɛ gbi jurɔ kɛha hegbɛ hewalɛ nina ni ji GDP lɛ mli 11.4 percent lɛ he;

• Primary balance lɛ fee deficit ni ji GDP lɛ mli jaŋji 5.3 yɛ bɔ ni aato akɛ ebaafee deficit ni ji GDP lɛ mli jaŋji 4.6 lɛ hewɔ; kɛ

• Gross International Reserves lɛ ji US$8.6 billion, ni ekɛ nyɔji 4.1 nibii ni akɛbaatoŋ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ he nyɔmɔwoo yɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ.

Maŋ Nyɛmɔ Nyɔmɔ Lɛ Nɔyaai Kɛha 2020

7. Nyɛmɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni yɔɔ nɔ yɛ December 2020 naagbee lɛ bafee GH¢291.6 billion (US$50.8 billion), ni enɛ feɔ GDP lɛ mli jaŋtsɛmɔ 76.1. Shika nɛɛ mli fata shika ni Aban lɛ na koni ekɛwo shika he nitsumɔi lɛ amɛ nyɛmɔi ni awo kɛ ha amɛ lɛ kɛ la nitsumɔhe Independent Power Purchase (IPPs) lɛ he nyɛmɔi lɛ. Kɛji akɛ shika he nitsumɔi lɛ ahe waamɔ shika lɛ bɛ mli lɛ, 2020 nyɛmɔ lɛ baafee GDP lɛ mli jaŋtsɛmɔ 71.5. Bɔ ni nyɛmɔ lɛ gbá kɛteŋ nɛ:

• Kpɔ̃sɛɛ nyɔmɔwoo: GH¢141.7 billion (US$24.7 billion) ni damɔ shi kɛha nyɔmɔwoo fɛɛ mli jaa 48.6 percent; kɛ

• Maŋmli nyɔmɔ: GH¢149.8 billion (US$26.1 billion) ni ji nyɔmɔ fɛɛ nɔŋ kɛteke lɛ mli nɔ 51.4 percent.

2021 GAIKPEE KƐ SHIKASHLIŊTSƆƆ LƐ

Macroeconomic Otii kɛha 2021

8. Aban lε shika nitsumɔ gbεjianɔtoi kε nifeemɔi (tamɔ toogbanaamɔ kε yiŋkpɔi yε shika fiteemɔ nɔ) baanyε asa shika kε heloonaa nitsumɔi ahe yε be kukuoo nliŋ. 2021 Shikatsoo wolo lε damɔ maŋ heloonaa yitsootiitii loo naakpɔi nɛɛ anɔ:

• Real GDP mli shweremɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni ji 5.0 percent;

• Nu kpakpa kɛɛŋɔɔ Real GDP hewalɛyaa ni ji 6.7 percent;

• December naagbee nibii anyɔɔmɔ mli kpelemo ni ji 8.0 percent;

Bikpamɔ hetsɛŋ-feemɔ: Enɛ ji be ni Maŋnɔyelɔi lɛ haa amɛ-manbii lɛ leelei ni fa ni yeɔ be he nɔ ni amɛfeɔ lɛ.

TLM (Teaching And Learning Materials): ji nibii fɛɛ nibii kpee, ni nibii ni yɔɔ wala kɛ nibii ni bɛ wala, kɛ adesai kɛ jeee adesai ahe nitsumɔ nibii fata he, ni tsɔɔlɔ ko baanyɛ kɛtsu nii yɛ nitsɔɔmɔ kɛ nikasemɔ shihilemɔi amli kɛye bua koni anyɛ anine ashɛ nikasemɔ oti kɛ nibii ni akpaa gbɛ akɛ abaana lɛ nɔ.

Nibii Kɛ Sɔɔmɔi Anitsumɔ: Shika nɔ ni Aban tsuɔ kɛtsɔɔ enitsumɔi anɔ kɛ sɔɔmɔi ahanuu kɛhaa maŋbii lɛ.

Value Added Tax (VAT): Too ni awoɔ yɛ bɔ ni hewalɛ kɛ nibii ahe sɛɛnamɔ yaa hiɛ yɛ nibii kɛ sɔɔmɔ nitsumɔi ahe yɛ amɛfeemɔ gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ fɛɛ mli.
