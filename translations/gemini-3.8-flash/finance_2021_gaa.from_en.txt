SHIKPƆŊ KƐ SHIKA NƆKWALƐNYƐƆ LƐ SHISHIJEE SANE

Ghana mɛi kpakpai lɛ hala yɛ December 2020 mli akɛ amɛbaaha H.E. Maŋtsɛ, Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo afi kroko koni eya nɔ etsu nitsumɔ kpakpa lɛ kɛkpo nɔ. Hekɛnɔfɔɔ ni wɔ-Ghanabii nanemɛi lɛ kɛfɔ nɔyeli nɛɛ nɔ lɛ ji nɔ ko ni wɔkɛ feo gbɛkɛ.

Afí 2021 Shika-tsɔɔmɔ wolo lɛ ji shika-tsɔɔmɔ wolo titri ni toɔ wɔ maŋ shika lɛ he gbɛjianɔ ekonn bɔ ni afee ni ebaŋkɛ gbɔmɛi awala, ewo hewalɛ he eha jaramɔ nibii ahe nitsumɔi ni yɔɔ haomɔ mli, ni efee gbɛjianɔ ni kɔɔ wɔ hewalɛ kɛjwɛŋmɔ ni wɔkɛbaamã Ghana ni hi fe tsɔu, yɛ COVID-19 sɛɛ. Shika-tsɔɔmɔ wolo nɛɛ damɔ wɔ ninaa ni kɔɔ maŋ nɛɛ tsakemɔ kɛ shika-yeli hegbɛ ni damɔɔ lɛ diɛŋtsɛ enane nɔ mamɔ he.

Kɛha klɛŋklɛŋ be lɛ fã babaoo lɛ, wɔto shika kɛ nibii ahe nitsumɔ lɛ he gbɛjianɔ ekonn, kɛtsɔ gbɛjianɔtoo nɔŋŋ ni akwɛ nɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ kɛto shika kɛ nibii ahe nitsumɔi anɔ, wɔtsɔɔ nibii ni he hiaa kɛha shika babaoo nanemɔ, wɔfee nibii ni hani nitsumɔi lɛ dara, wɔye wɔbua hefatalɔi krokomɛi ni jeee maŋ nitsumɔi, ni wɔtsɔɔ nibii ni yeɔ buaa gbɔmɛi. Kɛyashi afi 2019 naagbee lɛ, wɔyɛ he ni maŋ lɛ shika kɛ nibii ahe nitsumɔ lɛ yaa nɔ jogbaŋŋ yɛ kadimɔ nii kpakpai kɛ jeŋ muu fɛɛ yelikɛbuamɔ akɛ maŋ ni yaa nɔ nɔ, be ni COVID-19 hela gblamɔ lɛ ba wɔnɔ lɛ. Hela nɛɛ he nɔnyɛɛ ni yɔɔ gbeyei lɛ eje kpo yɛ maŋ shika kɛ nibii ahe nitsumɔi fɛɛ mli yɛ jeŋ muu fɛɛ, ni Ghana hu fata he.

Covid-19 Hela kpeŋkpele nɛɛ kɛ naagbai pii ba, ni ebahe nɔ bɔ ni wɔfeɔ wɔjarayeli kɛ wɔdabiigbɛ shihilɛ eha. Shi kɛlɛ, Amralo lɛ ena bɔ ni hela nɛɛ ebahe nɔ eha wɔ akɛ maŋ, ni eto gbɛjianɔi kɛ mlai amli bɔ ni afee ni ewa kɛwo jarayeli hewalɛ ekoŋŋ ni wɔshihilɛ aba nɔ ni eji momo lɛ mli. Hiɛnɔkamɔ fɛɛ eteko, ejaakɛ wɔna gbɛi hee kɛha nibii afeemɔ kɛ hewalɛi ni bɛ naagbee. Amralo lɛ kɛ emli fɛɛ ewo mli akɛ ebaafee nɔ fɛɛ nɔ koni maŋbii lɛ anaa gbɔmɔtsoŋ hewalɛ kpakpa, ni enɛ hewɔ lɛ, eto gbɛjianɔ koni akɛ tsofa atswia Ghanabii lɛ koni wɔ fɛɛ wɔhiɛ awa kɛma Ghana ni wɔsumɔɔ ni wɔna lɛ.

Wɔkɛ nyɛ efee ekome kɛtsɔ haomɔi amli po tamɔ wɔ shishijee kɛyaa nɔyaa mli kɛ hewalɛwoo nifeemɔi kɛba koni wɔba wɔ shika he nitsumɔ lɛ ekoŋŋ kɛya nɔyaa mli. Kɛbɛ nɛɛ, Ghanabii aaafee 1.2 million baanyɛ ana nikasemɔ kpakpa kɛ hewalɛwoo kɛha kɔleji mli nikasemɔ kɛha wala, wɔtsiu shika he nitsumɔ lɛ he kɛjɛ egbɔmɔtso mli ni mɛi pii ni kɛ amɛshika to lɛ enine eshɛ amɛshika lɛ nɔ kɛjɛ shikatoli he ni efite lɛ. Kɛfata he lɛ, ama tsɔne feemɔ he ko ni wɔna klɛŋklɛŋ tsɔnei ni afee yɛ jɛmɛ lɛ yɛ wɔ jara nɔ kɛ wɔ gbɛi anɔ.

Wɔkpɛlɛɔ anɔkwale nɔ akɛ wɔkpe naagbee komɛi be mli ni wɔbɔ mɔdɛŋ akɛ wɔha wɔ shwɛshweeshwefeemɔ otii lɛ aya hiɛ lɛ. Aban lɛ miibɔ mɔdɛŋ fɛɛ kɛgbɛjianɔtooi ni baatsa naagbei nɛɛ koni ehe afee bɔbɔɔ kɛha jatselɔi. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, wɔbɔi GH¢750 million CAP-BuSS Gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ nitsumɔ yɛ May 2020 mli, ni enɛ eha yelikɛbuamɔ eba kɛha Micro, Small and Medium-Sized Enterprises (MSMEs) kɛ mɛi krokomɛi babaoo.

Tamɔ bɔ ni ebako pɛŋ lɛ, atsɛ́ wɔ ni wɔkpe wɔdɛŋhiɛ kɛfee ekome koni wɔtsɔ gbɛtsɔɔmɔi kɛ yiŋkpɔi kpele kɛ ekãa nɛɛ nɔtsaa nɔ wɔhã wɔ maŋ lɛ aya hiɛ, bɔ ni afee ni shwelɛ kɛ hewalɛbaa aba ahã mɔ fɛɛ mɔ. Ejaakɛ kwraa lɛ, wɔwo shi akɛ wɔkɛ wɔhewalɛ fɛɛ kɛ wɔtsui fɛɛ baasɔmɔ Ghana maŋ lɛ.

Nyɔŋmɔ ajɔɔ wɔ fɛɛ.

Ghana ayi anɔkpua!

Ken Ofori-Atta Shika nɔkwɛlɔ

SHISHIJEE MƆ

1. Afi 2021 Maŋbii Ashika Kpɔŋ lɛ ji kukuwoo he nɔ ni Aban tsu yɛ afi 2020 kɛ shika abɔ ni eto etsuiŋ akɛ ebaafee yɛ tooke mli kɛwo hewale kɛtsɔ gbalamɔ kɛ nitsumɔi anɔ yɛ afi 2021 mli. Afi 2021 Shika Kpɔŋ lɛ mli sane ji "Gbegbɛmɔ, Heŋmɛi-kɛ-nɔ kɛ Yaanɔ". Nɛkɛ sane nɛɛ damɔ nɔ akɛ afii ni eho nɛɛ mli lɛ, daa Shika Kpɔŋ hee mli lɛ nitsumɔi heei babaoo baa ni ajeɔ shishi ni aŋmɛɛɔ nitsumɔi ni yaanɔ lɛ ahe. Jeee akɛ nɛkɛ nifeemɔ nɛɛ kɛ nɔnyɛɛ baa wɔshika nɔ kɛkɛ, shi moŋ kɛtsɔ blema nifeemɔi anɔ lɛ, ehãa nyɔmɔwoo sɛɛsɛɛkpeemɔ baa, ni awooo nitsulɔi babaoo anyɔmɔ yɛ be mli kɛhã nitsumɔ ni amɛtsu lɛ. Enɛ wóɔ nitsumɔi lɛ ajra nɔ akɛni nyɔmɔwoo ni asishi kpaŋ lɛ kɛ shika-nɔ-wóo he nyɔmɔ baa hewɔ.

2. Yɛ afi 2021 mli lɛ, Nɔyeli lɛ to gbɛjianɔ akɛ ebaatsɔ gbɛ hee nɔ. Yiŋtoo lɛ ji ni akɛba kusum ni ji nitsumɔi ni agbeee naa lɛ naagbee kɛtsɔ shiwoo ni akɛ agbe nitsumɔi ni yɔɔ amrɔ nɛɛ naa lɛ hanii nɔ.

3. Be mli nikanemɔi ni kɔɔ bɔ ni heatalɛ lɛ nyɛ efee nibii eha yɛ afi 2020 mli lɛ ji nɔ ni nyiɛ sɛɛ nɛɛ:

• Afi lɛ mli nyɔji nɛɛhu klɛŋklɛŋ lɛ mli lɛ, akɛna akɛ dade fɛɛ dade mli GDP shishijee nɔyee lɛ ji 0.2 percent. Akpaa gbɛ akɛ kɛbaafee afi lɛ naagbee lɛ, GDP nɔyee lɛ baashɛ 0.9 percent;

• Non-Oil real GDP ná shishijee nɔ́ ni ji 0.4 percent hewalɛyaa yɛ afi lɛ nyɔji nɛɛhu klɛŋklɛŋ lɛ mli, ni akpáa gbɛ akɛ ebaagbe afi lɛ naa yɛ 1.6 percent;

• December naagbee nibii ayiŋwalɛ mli kɛmiishɛ 10.4 percent kɛ akɛto oti ni asaa ato ni ji 11.0 percent he lɛ;

• Budget nɔɔ mli ŋtɔɔ hewalɛ kɛtsɔ shika anɔkwale nɔ lɛ bafe GDP 11.7 percent yɛ be ni ato oti ni asaŋ atoto he ekonn lɛ akɛ ebaafe GDP 11.4 percent;

• Primary balance lɛ fee tɔɔlɛ ni ji GDP mli jaaŋmɔi 5.3 yɛ bɔ ni atotɔ akɛ ebaafe tɔɔlɛ ni ji GDP mli jaaŋmɔi 4.6 lɛ he; kɛ

• Jeŋ Muu Fɛɛ Shika Kɛŋtɛŋ lɛ ji US$8.6 billion, ni ji nɔ ni baanyɛ ahe nibii kɛba maŋ lɛ mli kɛya nyɔji 4.1.

3 | Ghana Amralo - 2021 Manbii A-Budget

Yitso: “Shika kɛ nitsumɔi ahewalɛwoo kɛtsɔ egbelemɔ, emliwoo kɛ enɔya nɔ”

Maŋ Nyɛmɔ He Nyaa Shishitswaa yɛ 2020 mli

7. Nyɛmɔ nɔ ko ni yɔɔ nɔ yɛ December 2020 naagbee lɛ damɔ GH¢291.6 billion (US$50.8 billion) nɔ, ni enɛ ji GDP lɛ mli jaŋtsɛ 76.1. Akɔntaabu nɛɛ fata shika ni Aban lɛ kɛba koni ekɛwo shika he nitsumɔi a-bailout lɛ kɛ energy sector Independent Power Purchase (IPPs) capacity charges lɛ he nyɛmɔi lɛ ahe. Kɛji akɛ shika he nitsumɔi a-bailout lɛ bɛ mli lɛ, 2020 nyɛmɔ nɔ ko lɛ ji GDP lɛ mli jaŋtsɛ 71.5. Bɔ ni nyɛmɔ nɔ ko lɛ mli gbɛ́ ji nɛɛ:

• Kpoo nɔ nyɔmɔ: GH¢141.7 billion (US$24.7 billion) ni ji nyɔmɔ fɛɛ nɔ mli 48.6 percent; kɛ

• Maŋmli nyɔmɔ: GH¢149.8 billion (US$26.1 billion) ni ji nyɔmɔ fɛɛ nɔŋ kɛteke nɔ 51.4 percent.

2021 SHIKANƆ HE GBƐJIANƆTOO KƐ HEE-HIƆ SHIHILƐ LƐ

Maŋ shika yeli he otii kɛha afi 2021

8. Nɔyeli lɛ shika he mlaai kɛ nifeemɔi (tamɔ too kɛ yiŋkpɔi yɛ shika ninaa kɛ egbɛɛmɔ he) náa hewalɛ yɛ hefatalɔi ahewalɛ kɛ yarɔfeemɔ nɔ yɛ be kukuoo mli. 2021 Shika Tɛŋmɔ lɛ damɔ macroeconomic otii loo kadii nɛɛ anɔ:

• Fɛɛ mli Real GDP shweremɔ ni ji 5.0 percent;

• Non-Oil Real GDP shweshweeshwe nɔyaa ni ji 6.7 percent;

• Afi 8.0 mli nibii ayiŋteke yɛ December naagbee;

• Shika hefatɔɔ ni ji 9.5 percent kɛjɛ GDP mli;

• GDP mli gbɛtsɔɔmɔ nɔ hewalɛ fioo ni ji 1.3 percent; kɛ

Nibii kɛ Sɔɔmɔi anitsumɔ: Shika gbɛdzee nɔ ni Aban tsuɔ kɛtsuɔ enitsumɔi ahe nii kɛ kɛhãa maŋbii lɛ sɔɔmɔi.

Value Added Tax (VAT): Tooi ni awoɔ yɛ be mli ni anaa nɔkɔ yɛ nibii kɛ sɔɔmɔ nibii ahe yɛ nitsumɔ lɛ he fɛɛ he.
