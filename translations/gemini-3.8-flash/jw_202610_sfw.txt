1926

HOW IT HAS BEEN OVER A HUNDRED YEARS

The Watch Tower of January 1, 1926, stated: “Soon God will make everyone know his name. Therefore it is necessary that he has people to bear witness about him.”

Jesus, who is the head of the congregation, was used by Jehovah in 1926 to help the brothers understand something. That is, as for Christians, what should be most important to them is the preaching work. Therefore, the "faithful and discreet slave" produced new literature that they would use in preaching. (Matt. 24:45) Now, the brothers were made to clearly understand what a Christian must truly do. The way meetings were conducted was also changed. And as for a place to print literature, along with the machines to be used for printing, Jesus also helped them to acquire some.

THEY MADE A NEW BOOK, AND AS THEY CONTINUED THE STUDIES, THEY CHANGED IT

The book that the brothers used in the field ministry was called Studies in the Scriptures. But in April 1926, The Golden Age, which is now called Awake!, announced that a new book would be produced, and that was what would be used in the field ministry. Its title was Deliverance. To print it quickly, the brothers at Bethel bought a new printing press. This one could print 22 books every minute. After it was printed and released, the brothers were also very eager to distribute it.

The Bible Students were studying the Studies in the Scriptures, and this continued for many years. But in May, they began using The Watchtower for study at the Kingdom Hall. When they did this, how did it go?

The brothers were very happy about it, and in a certain congregation, they wrote a letter thanking Brother Rutherford. They said: “Every Sunday we study The Watchtower, just as the whole congregation used to do. Because of this, the way we understand some matters in the Bible has changed, enabling us to understand it quickly.”

The new understanding that they received helped the brothers realize that the preaching work was not something to be neglected. One study that appeared in a certain Watchtower was entitled “Good Conduct and the Work of Jehovah—Which Is More Important?” It stated: “There are some who think that the most important thing is to have good conduct and to help people. Therefore, the preaching work that a Christian is supposed to do does not seem very important to them. But the work that Jehovah has given us is to bear witness about him. Therefore, anyone who loves Jehovah must obey that word and exert himself to take part in the preaching work.”

In a certain Watchtower study article, the theme was: “Yield Yourself and Serve Jehovah.” It showed that if a Christian truly loves Jehovah, he must do something. That is, he will serve Jehovah from his heart. Therefore, the preaching work should not be taken lightly by the brothers. When that happens, people will get to know about Jehovah, and they will also hear about the things he will do.—Isa. 43:10, 12; Matt. 24:14.

THE ASSEMBLY THEY HELD HELPED THE BROTHERS AND SISTERS EXERT THEMSELVES IN THE PREACHING WORK

At the end of the month of May, a convention was held in London, England, and many brothers and sisters attended. A book called The Standard for the People had been prepared, and it was to be distributed to the brothers and sisters at that convention. However, they did not get the required quantity to take to that convention, because at that time, workers had gone on strike in Britain, so the printing presses were not operating. When that happened, the brothers at Brooklyn Bethel gave of themselves. They stood on their feet and printed 117,000 copies of the book.

On Friday, May 29, the brothers received a letter at the convention. The letter read: "Everyone here should come so that we can preach about the king and our kingdom. Early tomorrow morning, let us meet at Alexandra Palace and hold a preaching meeting."

The books they distributed exceeded 100,000. The Watch Tower of July 15, 1926, reported concerning this: “The brothers rejoiced greatly, because they saw that the work Jehovah had given them, they were willing to do it.”

That evening, Brother Rutherford gave a talk at the Royal Albert Hall. Its title was, “Why Do the Rulers Oppose Jehovah?” Before they opened the door, about 13,000 people were already there to listen to it. They came so that the place was filled up completely. Therefore, they placed loudspeakers outside, and for that reason, those who could not enter the hall also listened to it.

In Brother Rutherford’s talk, he showed the reason why the rulers were unable to make things go well. And that was because they took Jehovah and his Kingdom as nothing. He also explained that the League of Nations that existed at the time, they claimed would bring peace, but it would not succeed; rather, it would fail.

During the Second World War, the way Jehovah's Witnesses were persecuted was terrible. But even at that time, many people came into the congregation. It was very surprising. One German soldier said: "As for Jehovah's Witnesses, the more they are persecuted, the more people join them."

Something similar happened in the Netherlands. The 1986 Yearbook reported concerning it: "As the brothers were persecuted, others were arrested. Some were imprisoned, and some were killed. Yet through all this, many people became Jehovah's Witnesses. In 1941, they numbered 642, but two years later, they reached 1,379. Others were also added.... Indeed, they were persecuted, but by Jehovah's grace they grew even more."

If one is a disciple of Jesus, he must know that the way he chooses will not be easy, and he may even be killed. Jesus also said that his disciples would be taken to court and made to stand before rulers. (Matt. 10:17, 18) He also said that on account of serving Jehovah, the religious leaders would hate them. (John 16:2) And the relationship between them and their family members could be severed.—Matt. 10:34-36.

6 Just as Jesus had said, the Christians were severely persecuted. Not long after his death, the Jewish priests turned their attention against the Christians because they wanted them to stop the preaching work. So they arrested them and brought them before their high court called the Sanhedrin. The priests decided that ‘they would kill them,’ but a Pharisee named Gamaliel spoke some words to them. He said: “Do not do anything against these men; let them alone. For if this plan or this work is of men, it will be overthrown. But if it is from God, you will not be able to overthrow them, and you may even be found fighting against God.” (Acts 5:33, 34, 38, 39) After this, they released them, but even so, they continued to persecute them. Yet Christianity did not end; rather, many people became Christians. (Acts 6:7) This shows us that Gamaliel's words were true, because what they did came to nothing. How did these people who were so persecuted become so numerous? Because God was with them.—Acts 1:8.

Now That You Know the Truth, How Will You Hold On to It?

It has been a long time since you began studying the Bible. What made you realize that Jehovah's Witnesses speak the truth? (Eph. 4:4-6) Was it the love that exists among them? Or was it their good conduct? For many people, these two things drew them to the congregation. Because Jehovah's organization has helped them see that if they follow it, their worship is beneficial. This is not surprising, because this is what identifies true Christians.—John 13:35; 1 Pet. 2:12.

However, suppose a brother or a sister does not treat you well, or has done something to show that he or she does not like you. Or a brother or a sister has committed a sin, and that sin is unbecoming of a Christian. It is like a man who took his father's wife in the Corinthian congregation. (1 Cor. 5:1) If someone's behavior alone is what you look at to serve Jehovah, if he does not treat you well, you will abandon the congregation. What will you do so that something like this does not happen to you?

Let Your Faith Be Strong

There are many things you have learned from the Bible. If you accept that it is true, every truth in it will make your faith strong as time goes on. If it happens that way, it will help you very much. As for you, look at what we are saying.

At one time Jesus said something that his disciples did not understand. He said that unless they ate his flesh and drank his blood. Some of the disciples said: “This saying is shocking.” Therefore many of them no longer followed him.—John 6:56-60, 66.

Jesus asked his 12 disciples: “Do you also want to go?” Peter replied: “Lord, to whom shall we go? You have the words of eternal life; and we have believed, and we know that you are the Holy One of God.”—John 6:67-69.

Why did the 12 disciples not go away? They truly believed that Jesus was the Messiah. Do you remember what Peter said? He said: "We have believed and have come to know that you are the Holy One of God." What gave them such faith? They had seen Jesus' character and had also listened to his teachings. When they compared what they had learned in the Bible with it, it gave them evidence showing that Jesus was the Messiah. That is what gave them faith in him. Therefore, if he said something and they did not understand it, that is why they did not turn away from following him.

For example, the Bible says God's name is Jehovah, and that Jesus died and will give us Paradise. That word which has been spoken will never change at all. Therefore, if someone in the congregation does something bad, it is that person whose behavior has changed. That does not mean that the words learned from the Bible are no longer the truth. For this reason, if you believe what the Bible says and you accept that it is Jehovah's congregation that teaches that truth, you will not say that because of a brother's behavior, you will leave the congregation.
