Sua Fi Gibeonfo Ne Hɔ

Gibeonfo ne Yizilfo duma nzo na be te be nu.”—JOSH. 10:1.

Afe ne le 1473 B.C.E. Israel man ne ebɔ n’abɔ kɛ ɔkɔfa Ɛwɔkɛ Azɛlɛ ne. Israelfo ne mɔ anyia konimdie soronko wɔ Yɛriko nee Ai azua ne mɔ anwo. Na akyire, debie mɔɔ bɛanzo nnya sili. Akwantufo eku bie rale bɛ nwo, bɛhãle kɛ bɛvi azɛlɛ mɔɔ wɔ akyi ba na bɛkpondɛ kɛ bɛ nee Nyamenle menli ne yɛ asomdwole ngyegyenu.

2 Mbanantubawolɛ nɛ anwo a le Gibiyɔnfowolɛ nɛ a. Ɛkɛ a le ahyile kɛ bɛbɔ Gibiyɔnfowolɛ nɛ anwo adwenle wɔ Baebolo nɛ nu, nza kɛ nna a ɛnle ahyile mɔɔ kuraa a. Baebolo nu anwuma belemgbunlililɛ dɔɔnwo le kɛ Gibiyɔnfowolɛ nɛ nee Yizilayɛlifowolɛ nɛ le bɔbɔ mɔɔ bikye bɛ nwo wɔ mfe mɔɔ li anzie nɛ nu amuala. Saa anyiɛ bɔbɔ belemgbunlililɛ nɛ nu a, yɛbanwu nzunzonilɛ mɔɔ sonle bolɛ maa yɛ, na ɔbamaa yɛanwu Gyihova Nyamenle nwo ninyɛne dɔɔnwo.

Gibeonfoɔ ne bɛbɔɔ Yɔsuwa asisie maa ɔne bɛ yɛɛ asomdwoe (Nea ɔwɔ nkyekyɛm 4 nu).

Israel fo bɔbɔ ɛnle bɔnwun. Bɛnwunnlɛ kɛ Gyɛhova ɛlɛkwo amaa ye menli ne, na bɛnwunnlɛ kɛ yehyɛ bɔ kɛ ɔbato Kenaanmanma ne mɔ yeavi azɛlɛ ne azo. (Ex. 34:11; Josh. 9:24) Ɛhye ati mɔɔ Yizilayama lile Gyɛleko nee Ayi anzo konim la, Gibiyɔnma soman ananmusima kɔle Dwɔhyuwa ɛkɛ wɔ Gilgal kɛ bɛkɔpɛ anzodwolɛ.

⁴ Kenga Joshua 9:8-13. Gibeonfoɛ ne bɛyɛle bɛ nwo kɛ bɛvi asolo moa kpalɛ. Bɛgyenle nu kɛ Gyihova lile boka wɔ Yizilayɛfoɛ nkunimdilɛ ne mɔ mɔɔ bɛnyianle ye wɔ Yigyibiti nee Amɔlafoɛ Belemgbunli Sihon nee Og anwo zo la anu. Noko wɔ ngɛlɛbɛ nu, bɛamva mɔɔ dole Gyɛleko nee Ayi la bɛanlole nu,

ɔluakɛ ndɛ fofolɛ ɛhye amrɛ ɛngola ɛndwu bɛ “asase mɔɔ wɔ akyi fɔɔnwo” ne azo. Bɛamviza Gyihova dɛba, Yizilayɛ ambafo ne mɔ liele daselɛ mɔɔ Gibeɔnfo ne mɔ vale rale ne do na bɛ nee bɛ yɛle anzondwolɛ ngyekyenu. (Gis. 9:14, 15) Yemɔ akyi ekyi bie la, bɛnwunle Gibeɔnfo ne mɔ adalɛ ne, naaso Yizilayɛfo ne mɔ lile anzondwolɛ ngyekyenu mɔɔ bɛ nee Gibeɔnfo ne mɔ yɛle ne azo ɔluakɛ bɛva “Gyihova duma bɛka ndane bɛmaa bɛ.” (Gis. 9:16-19) Bɛvale ɛzonlɛlilɛ mɔɔ baha aze bɛmaanle Gibeɔnfo ne mɔ kɛ bɛyɛ “gyima mɔɔ le kɛ bɛkɔkpɛ nyane na bɛahɔsa nzule bɛamaa asɔne ne nee Gyihova afɔlemokyea ne” dahuu.—Gis. 9:27.

Gibeon-amofo hianle mboa vile Joshua ɛkɛ. (Josh. 10:3-7) Joshua hanle Israel-amofo do kɔle kɛ bɛkɔgye bɛ, na bɛ mmɔdembɔ nwo yiale nyilalɛ. Jehovah bɔbɔ tole asukɔtew ngpole guale atamfo Amorite-amofo asrafo ne mɔ anwo zo na ɔnam ahyɛnzomanzo zo ɔmanle ewia nwolɛ hanle kɛ bɛkoli konle ne. (Josh. 10:9-14) Kɛ mɔɔ bɛhwele anzodwolɛ ngyegyenu bɛbɔle nu bɛvile Israel-amofo ɛkɛ na bɛhianle mboa vile Joshua ɛkɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛhunlule bɛ ne, Gibeon-amofo ne lale diedi ali wɔ Jehovah nu—diedi wɔ ye tumi nu kɛ ɔkɔyɛ mɔɔ yehɛnla nuhua na ɔbagye bɛ.

6 Nde ɛhye kile yɛ debie boni wɔ Jehovah nwo? Jehovah bɛlɛ ye nwo aze yɛɛ ɔle ahunlunbɔfo. Ɛnee yeha ahyɛ kɛ Israel bɔ “bɛtu kɛra nwo dwulɔfo amuala,” na ɛnee Gibionfo mɔ noko boka nwo. (Num. 33:51, 52) Noko kɛ mɔɔ Israel nee bɛ yɛle anzodwolɛ ngyegyenu ne ti, Jehovah hilele ahunlunbɔkɛ nwolɛ adenle manle Gibionfo mɔ kɛ bɛdɛnla ngoane nu. Jehovah yɛle ɛhye ɔnva nwo kɛ Israel mɔ anbizale ye debie kɛ bɛkɔyɛ ngyegyenu ne. Akee ɔboale Israel manle bɛli bɛ ngyegyenu ne azo yɛɛ ɔnamle anwanwadeɛ zo ɔliele Gibionfo mɔ.—Josh. 9:26; 11:19.

7 Kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na yɛasuasua Gibeonfɔ ne a? Ɔlua gyidie kpole a yɛkɛnya wɔ Gyihova nu ne zolɛ. Kɛmɔ yɛze Gyihova nwo ninyɛne dɔɔnwo yɛtɛla Gibeonfɔ ne mɔ wɔ mekɛ zɔhane ne ati, yɛlɛ ngilenu dɔɔnwo a yɛkɛva yɛkɛfa yɛ nwo yɛkɛto Gyihova zolɛ bɔkɔɔ. (Edw. 40:4, 5) Bieko, yɛbahola yɛasuasua Gibeonfɔ ne mɔ ɔlua mɛlɛbɛnwoaze gyima a yɛbaye yɛ nwo kɛ yɛkɛyɛ wɔ Gyihova ɛzonlenlɛ nu ne anwo. (Dwɔh. 9:23, 27) Adiema kakula nrenyia bie a bɛfɛlɛ ye Luke arɛle ɛnrɛvi mekɛ mɔɔ ɔnwunle kɛ adiema kpanyinli bie a wɔ Bɛtɛle abusua ne anu yɛle kɛ zɔhane pɛpɛɛpɛ la kɔlɛ. Ɔwɔ nuhua kɛ zɔhane Bɛtɛlenyi ne neɛnlea gyima kpole bie mɔɔ anwunvɔne nwohyia la azo ɛdeɛ, noko ɔbale ye nwo aze ɔyɛle gyima wɔ nɔɔzo kɛ ɔkɛwɛ Fanu Ahendzi sua ne a bɛlɛto la azo. Luke ka kɛ: “Mesukonle kɛ mɛlɛbɛnwoaze ɛnle nganeɛdelɛ ala. Ɔda ali wɔ debie mɔɔ yɛkpa kɛ yɛkɛyɛ anzɛɛ yɛnrɛyɛ la anu.” Ɔrɛla ye kɛ yɛkɛda yɛ gyidie a yɛlɛ wɔ Gyihova nu ne ali dahuu ɔlua ɔ nwo a yɛbava yɛado zolɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛkɛyia ngyegyelɛ la anu, na yɛali mɛlɛbɛnwoaze nu yɛayɛ Nyamenle gyima biala mɔɔ bɛkɛva bɛkɛwula yɛ sa nu la.

4 NƐNƐANLE ABAN NE

Suasua Gibeonfo no mɔ na pɛ kɛ ɛbɛyɛ edwuma mɔɔ bɛbu no kɛ ɔnle kɛzi (Nea ɛdendɛnsilɛ 7)

ɛvolɛ nsa ɛhɔne. Mɔɔ David bizale ɛhɔne ne anwo debie la, Gyihova hanle hilele ye kɛ Saul nee ye sua nu amra le mogya anwo fɔfɔlɛ ɔluakɛ bɛyɛle Gibiɔnma ne abɔne ɛvolɛ bie mɔ mɔɔ ɛze ɛhɔ la.—2 Sam. 21:1.

10 Kenga 2 Samuel 21:3-6. David nli Gibeonfoɛ ne ahendle belemunli kɛ ɔbɔ mɔden kɛ ɔkɛhwe kɛ ɔbɔ yɛ bɛbɔ Saul yɛɛ mɛla kɔne bɔ ɔbɔ yɛɛ bɛ ne anwo. Asɛɛ bɛbɔ mɔden kɛ bɛkɛyɛ anyundɔfo kɛ bɛbama David kɛhua kakɛ bɔ ɔanyɛ adwenle kɛ ɔkɛboa bɛ ndɛ ne ɔ? Gibeonfoɛ ne bua kɛ ɔkpa kɛ ɔnle “dwetɛ fufule anaa dwetɛ kɔkɔɔ nwo edwɛkɛ,” ɔluakɛ mɔɔ Mɛla ne kɛle la, bɛnlie agyedeɛ bie bɔ ewufo kukufoɛ anwo. (Num. 35:30, 31) Bɛbɔ ye nzonlɛ noko kɛ bɛntumi bɛnkuko nrenyia bɔ mɛla ɛmma bɛ kwan. Awielei ne, David ma kwan ma bɛkuko Saul mrenyia mra nsuu bɔ bie a bɛvale bɛ nwo bɛwulale Saul amamgbon ewu mɔdenbɔ ne anu. Mekɛ bie anzidɛ, amian ne ba, ma ɔgyale ɛhɔne ne na ɔkile kɛ bɛdua ɛtane ne anwo kakɛ ma ɔsɔle Jehovah anye.—2 Sam. 21:9, 10, 14.

11 Ahyɛnsisidɛ ɛhye kile yɛ duma wo Gyɛhova nwo? Yɛnwu adanse fann dɛ Gyɛhova le Nyamenle mɔɔ tenrɛ. (Edw. 37:28) Ɔkulo kɛ bɛyɛ menli mɔɔ nwohyee biala nni nu fɛɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ. Ɛhye boka bɔbɔ menli mɔɔ bɛyɛ bɛ nyeyeyilɛ, kɛ mɔɔ ɔta ɔto amanaamfo nee ekpunli nketewa la. Wo ye mekɛ mɔɔ fɛta nu la, Gyɛhova bazi ndɛne tenrɛ amaa edwɛkɛ mɔɔ nwohyee biala nni nu mɔɔ bɛyɛle ye asɔrefonli la fɛɛ. Yɛsukoa noko dɛ Gyɛhova kpondɛ kɛ yɛdi yɛ ɛwɔkɛ nzonle, kɛ mɔɔ ɔkpondɛle dɛ Yizilayɛma ɛli bɛ anzodwolɛ ɛwɔkɛ mɔɔ bɛbɔle Gibiyɔnma la nzonle la.—Fa toto Emɔso 1:9 nwo.

13 Bɛyɛ ɛvolɛ 500 wɔ Ɔhye David mekɛ ne anzo la, Gibeonfo ne mɔ lale ali bieko wɔ Baebolo kɛlata ne anu. Ɛvolɛ 70 Babilɔn ngɛlɛbaye ne rale awieleɛ, na wɔ 537 B.C.E. nu la, Dyufo ngɛlɛba mɔɔ lumuaale la hɔle Gyɛlusalɛm bɔbɔ Gamado Zerubbabel. (Ezra 2:1, 2, 58) Wɔ 468 B.C.E. nu la, ekpunli fofolɛ rale bɔbɔ kɛlatakɛlɛvo Ezra. (Ezra 7:1-7) Ekpunli nwiɔ ɛhye mɔ mɔɔ ɛnle kpole la mɔɔ bɛziale bɛ rale la bie a le “Nethinim” abusua ne mɔ. (Nea ahyɛse edwɛkɛ mɔɔ wɔ Ezra 2:58 nee 7:7 la.) Nwane mɔ a le Nethinim ne mɔ? Ɛnee bɛle “asonrɔfo mɔɔ ɛnle Yizilayɛfo mɔɔ bɛsonle wɔ asɔredan nu,” na bɛ nuhua dɔɔnwo le Gibeonfo ne mɔ mma.—Nea Edwɛkɛ Nu Ndenlɛ, “Nethinim.”

14 Kenga 1 Chronicles 9:2 nza ahyɛseɛ asɛm ne. “Ɛzonlenu azonvolɛ ne mɔ” boka ngedwaliɛ mɔɔ limoe ziale rale Israel la anwo. Ɛhye anye zo ɔluakɛ nnyɛ Djuufoɔ mɔɔ wɔ ngedwale nu la amuala a ziale rale a. Ɔda ali kɛ Djuufoɔ dɔɔnwo anya bɛ ti anwo wɔ Babilɔn, na amgbodzo bɛamkpondɛ kɛ bɛbɔ afɔleɛ mɔɔ hyia na bɛazia na bɛabuka bɛazie bɛ man mɔɔ asɛkye la bieko. Na ngedwaliɛ mɔɔ di nɔhalɛ, mɔɔ boka bie mɔ mɔɔ ɔbayɛ kɛ bɛle Gibeonfoɔ ne mɔ anwo mma la, anye lale kɛ bɛbanwu kɛ bɛzia bɛadua nɔhalɛ ɛzonlenlɛ bo wɔ ɛzonlenu sua ne anu wɔ Gyirusɛlɛm, na bɛbɔle mɔdenle biala kɛ bɛbazia. Nnyɛ kɛ Djuufoɔ ne mɔ la, Gibeonfoɔ ne mɔ anwo mma annya asase agyapadeɛ biala wɔ Israel. Noko, bɛziale rale kɛ bɛbadie bɛ ɛzonlenu sua ne anu gyima nwo na bɛabuka bɛazie Gyirusɛlɛm banyinli ne mɔ bieko tɛtɛ.—Neh. 3:26.

15 Nzo yɛ asɛm ɛhye kile yɛ wɔ Gyihova nwo a? Yɛsukoa kɛ yɛ Nyamenle anye sɔ ye azonvo mɔɔ di nɔhalɛ la kpole kpalɛ yɛɛ dahuu ɔnea bɛ boɛ. Mekɛ mɔɔ Babilɔn ngɛlɛbaye ne rale awieleɛ la, ɛnee kɛyɛ ɛvolɛ 1,000 ɛpɛ nu vi mekɛ mɔɔ Gyihova liele Gibeyɔnma ne wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ Yizilayama dele Ɛwɔkɛ Azɛlɛ ne la. Noko, ɔhɔle zo ɔhyirale bɛ. Gibeyɔnma ne abozoama dele ngoane wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛsɛkyele Dwuda nee Gyɛlusalɛm wɔ 607 B.C.E. la. Ngɛlɛbaye ne anzi, bɛhɔle zo bɛvale bɛ nwo bɛwulale nɔhalɛ ɛzonlenlɛ nu kpalɛ, na bɛzonlenle bɛbokale Livaema ne anwo wɔ ɛzonlenlɛ sua ne anu. Ɔda ali kɛ bɛ nuhua bie mɔ dɛnlanle ɛzonlenlɛ sua ne anwo bikyele. (Ɛzɛla 2:70; Nih. 11:21) Yɛɛ bɛmanle bɛ adenle kɛ bɛmmatu tofe, adwule, nee ezukoa biala wɔ bɛ gyima mɔɔ bɛyɛ wɔ ɛzonlenlɛ sua ne anu la ati.—Ɛzɛla 7:24.

Ɛrehu Gyihova kpalɛ kpalɛ ɔ?

"Hu kɛle ɛ papa Nyamenle ne na fa ahonle mɔɔ le munli sonle ye."—1 KRON. 28:9.

Ɛsusuw Gyɛhova ninyedzi ndu, ye nyansaa dɛlɛ, anzeɛ ye ninyɛyɛ kpalɛ nwo ɛlɛ a? Sɛ yɛsusuw yɛ Bɔvolɛ ne nwo a, yɛbɔ yɛ nwo aze. Bie a ɛte nka kɛ mɔɔ asɔfo Pɔɔlo yɛle ye la, mɔɔ ɔhɛlɛle kɛ: “O Nyamenle ye anyunnyam, ye nyansaa, nye ye ninyedzi ne ndu tɛla ebie biala! Ye ndendoma nwo ɛnla ɛkɛ, na ye adenle nzoninli ɛnla ɛkɛ!”—Rom. 11:33, 34.

2 Ɔwɔ nuhɔ kɛ yɛnrɛhɔla yɛnrɛnwun duma biala mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛnwun ye wɔ Gyɛhova nwo la, noko ɔda aze kɛ, wɔ ndenle fofolɛ do, yɛbahɔla yɛanwun kɛzi ɔte la. Belemgbunli Devidi hanle hilele ye ra Salɔmɔn kɛ: “Nwun ɛ ze Nyamenle ne na fa ahonle mɔɔ tɛnla biala ɛnle nu la sonle ye . . . Saa ɛkpondɛ ye a, ɔbamaa wɔanwun ye.” (1 Edw. 28:9) Kɛ mɔɔ ɛzoanvolɛ Dwɔn hakyele ye Kilisienenma mɔɔ wɔ hɔ la ɛvɔlɛ dɔɔnwo anzi la, yɛlɛ “adwenle amaa yɛanwun ahenle mɔɔ le nɔhalɛ la.” (1 Dwɔn 5:20) Yɛɛ Gyisɛse mumkɛ hanle kɛ Gyɛhova anwo nɔhalɛ nwunlɛ wɔ ɛkɛ maa “awie biala mɔɔ Ɔralɛ ne kulo kɛ ɔda ye ali ɔkile ye la.”—Mat. 11:27.

3 Bɛbɔ bɛva bɛ nwo bɛma Gyihova no kɔsɔɔti anwu ninyɛne dɔɔnwo wɔ ɔ nwo dedee. Noko, yɛngyaa Gyihova “nwonle.” (Dwɔn 17:3; nea adesua ahyɛnzi edwɛkɛ no.)

1. Kɛ yɛdwene Gyihova ndelebɛbo, nyansa, nneɛma mɔɔ yeyɛ nwo a, kɛ ɔte yɛ nwo ɛ?

2. Duzu a kile kɛ yɛbahɔla yɛanwu Gyihova a?

Mma yɛzuzu kpuya nsa mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛ muala yɛsuzu nwolɛ ɔnva nwo mekɛ tendenle mɔɔ yɛva yɛzonle Gyihova la anwo: Kɛzi Gyihova anwo ndelebɛbo mɔɔ yɛnyia la kile duzu? 4 Yɛsukua mɔɔ kile kɛ yɛbɔkɔ nsa ayɛzi Yehowa la wɔ mɔɔ ɔhanle ɔfalale Dyuufu mɔɔ bɛkɔ nsa bɛavi nnomɛ nu bɛara la anwo la nu. Yehowa hyele bɔ kɛ: “Mebamaa bɛ ahonle mɔɔ bɛbɔfa bɛaze me, kɛ emi a le Yehowa.” (Jer. 24:7) Ɔda ali kɛ menli mɔɔ vi Babɛlɔn rale la noko ɛnee ze ninyɛne bie mɔ mɔɔ kile Yehowa la. Na mɔɔ bɛbɔyɛ na bɛaze Yehowa la kɛhyia kɛ bɛbɔfa bɛ ahonle, bɛ adwenle nee bɛ ndelebɛbo noko bɛaka ho. Buluku bie ka kɛ: “Ngakyile kpole wɔ nwonle kɛ ɛbaze Nyamenle anwo edwɛkɛ nee kɛ ɛbaze Nyamenle bɔbɔ la anu. Ɔle kɛ ngakyile mɔɔ wɔ nwonle kɛ ɛbɔgengaso ɛlɔlɛ anwo edwɛkɛ nee kɛ ɛbɔhɔ ɛlɔlɛ nu la.” Ɔwɔ kɛ yɛnwu ninyɛne dɔɔnwo yɛtɛla kɛ Yehowa lɛ duma yɛɛ ɔle Nyamenle. Yɛkulo kɛ yɛnwu Ahenle mɔɔ wɔ duma ne anzi la.

5 Kɛ yɛsukua Gyihova nwo debie la, yɛnwu debie mɔɔ maa o nye die yɛɛ mɔɔ ɔngulo la. Kɛ neazo la, dwenle Belemgbunli Dzosaya neazo ne anwo. Baebolo ne ka kɛ: “Wɔ ye belemgbunlililɛ ne anu ɛvolɛ mɔɔ tɔ zo 8 ne anu, mekɛ mɔɔ ɛnee ɔle kakula kɛkɛ la, ɔbɔle ɔ bo kɛ

kpondɛ ɔ manin David Nyamenle ne.” (2 Chron. 34:3) Na Josiah anyɛ mɔɔ ɔkpondɛ bie ala. Ɔyɛle ninyɛne mɔɔ ɔnwunle ye la. Ɔlua ngapezoni Jeremiah do, Gyihova hanle kɛ Josiah “lile tenlene nee pɛlepɛlelilɛ nzi” na ɔbokale anyunlunyiavolɛma nee ehyiavolɛma. Akee Gyihova dɔhole zo: “Ɛhye ɛnle mɔɔ ɔkile kɛ ɛkɛze me a?”—Jer. 22:15, 16.

6 Kenga Edwene 9:10. Saa yɛnwʋ Gyɛhova kpalɛ a, yɛbanyia ye nwo anwodoaso kpole. Dɛba ngyikyi ngyikyi nrenyia ɛhyia nwonlɛ Pɔɔlo nwo edwɛkɛ yɛ nɔhalɛ. Ye efiade nwo nrenyia a le awieeɛ nwonlɛ nee dɔkɔ a onli ekyi ansaana yeawu la, ɔhɛlɛle kɛ: “Me nyunlu engua anyiemgbole. Ɔbolekɛ menze Ahenle mɔɔ medie me di ye la.” (2 Tim. 1:12) Ninyɛne mɔɔ Pɔɔlo zukoale nee mɔɔ ɔnwunle ye wɔ ye ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nu la hanle ye maanyia ye Nyamenle nwo anwodoaso kpole. Kɛ mɔɔ adesua nwo edwɛkɛ mɔɔ wɔ ngyekyenu ɛhye nwo kile la: “Pɔɔlo . . . nyianle anwodoaso kɛ Gyɛhova balelɛ ye nɔhalɛlilɛ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ kɔkpula ‘kenle kpole ne’ mɔɔ Ɔbadwazo ye la.”

Baebolo ne so kile yɛ ninyɛne dɔngunwo wɔ Ye Kɛlɛvo ne nwo. (Dw. 25:8-10) Ɔngyia kɛ yɛ fa yɛ adwenle yɛ si kɛzi Baebolo ne fɛlɛko boe yɛ anzɛɛ awie mɔ anwo kɛzi ɔ bɔ yɛ bo kɛ yɛ biisa yɛ nwo kɛ, ‘Duzu a eyi kile me wɔ Gyihova Nyamenle nwo a?’*

8 Mɔ yɛnwu Gyihova kpalɛ la, yɛnɛ yɩ agɔnwolɛvalɛ nu bahye kpole. Kɛ neaso la, sɛ ɛkenga Baebolo ne dahuu, ɛnea siane biala dwumadzi ne, anaa ɛkɔ asambule anaa amansan nhyiamu wie a, kɛzi ɛte nwonle wɔ Gyihova nwo a? Dahuu yɛte nwonle kɛ yɛbikyeye yɛ anwuma Selɛ ne. Nzuotamandi? Iluwa kɛ yɛnwunle yɩ nwo ninyɛne dɔɔnwo la ti. Ɔzɔti, ɔka yɛ ma yɛyi yɩ ayɛlɛ na yɛda yɩ ase. Yɛte nwonle kɛ ndwomtowunle ne mɔɔ dwennwenne kɛzi Gyihova nianle yɩ nwo duma na ɔhanle kɛ: “Nyamenle yɛ me ahonle nu bodwe nɛɛ me kyɛfa afebɔɔ” la. (Kenga Dwom 73:24-28.) Mmɔden biala mɔɔ yɛbɔ kɛ yɛbanwu Gyihova kpalɛ la ma yɛ anwonyia mɔɔ yɛwɔ ye agɔnwolɛvalɛ mɔɔ sonle bolɛ he nwo la yɛ kpole kpalɛ.

9 Saa yɛ “dwen” Gyihova nwo—yɛkakye mɔɔ yɛzukoa mɔɔ fale ɔ nwo na yɛdwenye kɛzi yɛ nyɛleɛ ka ye nwo a—yɛbazi kpɔkɛ kpalɛ. (Mrɛ. 3:5, 6) Kɛ ndonwo: Mɛnli dɔɔnwo fa bɛ ye anzɛɛ bɛ hu fotu sie bɛ tɛkese zo, bɛ kɔtɔku nu anzɛɛ bɛ founu zo. Ɛhye boa bɛ ma bɛdwenye bɛ ye anzɛɛ bɛ hu nwo kpalɛ fane dɔɔnwo, bɛsi kpɔkɛ mɔɔ bɛze kɛ ɔbazɔ ye anye, na bɛtwe bɛ nwo bɛfi debie biala mɔɔ bahola aha ye nyane la nwo. Zɔhane ala a, saa yɛdwenye Gyihova nwo fane dɔɔnwo a, yɛbazi kpɔkɛ mɔɔ bazɔ ye anye kpalɛ a.—Edw. 19:13, 14.

10 Yɛbɔ yɛ wɔ Gyihova zunzuole nu, na ɛhye maa yɛnyia ngole yɛda ye suban ali. (Gen. 1:26) Na saa yɛsukoa Gyihova suban nwo edwɛkɛ dɔɔnwo a, ɔbamaa yɛade kɛzi yɛbazukoa ye la abo kpalɛ. Gyisɛse hwenle mɛla kilehilevoma nee Falesifo ne mɔ aso ɔluakɛ bɛande Nyamenle suban bɛanli. Ɔwɔ nuhua kɛ bɛdwenlenle Mɛla ne anu edwɛkɛ nketewa nwo kpalɛ deɛ, noko bɛkpɔle “Mɛla ne anu edwɛkɛ kpole kpalɛ ne mɔ, mɔɔ le pɛlepɛlelilɛ nee ahunlunyele nee nɔhalɛlilɛ la.” (Matt. 23:23) Yɛnzɔhonle ɛzonlenlɛ nu adwulofoma zɔhane mɔ, yɛmɔ yɛbɔ mɔdenle yɛde kɛzi Gyihova suban da ali wɔ ye mɛla nee ye ngyinlazo nu la abo kpalɛ. Saa yɛyɛ zɔ a, ɔbamaa yɛada suban zɔhane mɔ kpalɛ ali.—1 John 4:8, 11.

12-13. Duzu a yɛbahola yɛazuko yɛavĩ Baebolo ne mɔɔ yɛkenga ne anwo wɔ Gyɛhova anwo a? Fa fɛntolua bie ma. (Jeremiah 39:15-18) (Nea kate nwo mfonin ne noko.)

“Ɛhe kile ngoane mɔɔ ɩnnkɛwie, kɛ bɛkanwo wɔ, Nyamenle fɔnkyɛ mɔɔ ɛle nɔhɔlɛ la.”—JOHN 17:3.

KƐZI ɛte nganeɛ fɛlɛɛ wɔ berɛ mɔɔ Baebolo suani bie kɔ ye asafo ayia mɔɔ limoa, ɔbɔ ɔ bo kɛ ɔbua kpɔkɛ, ɔka ye diedi ne anwo edwɛkɛ ɔkile awie mɔ, na akee ɔsi kpɔkɛ kɛ ɔbatu ɔ nwo yemaa Gyihova Nyamenle a? Ɔda ali kɛ ɛ nye die kpole! Nɔhalɛ nu, Gyihova a ɔfɛta kɛ ɔnyia ngɛlɛbɛra wɔ sunsum nu anyuhɔlɛ mɔɔ yɛ Baebolo suani ne nyia la anwo a. 2 Nyamenle pɛ a le kɛ “menli ngakyile kɔsɔɔti” kɛnya “ngoane na bɛanwu nɔhalɛ ne kpalɛ.” (Kenga 1 Timote 2:3, 4.) Gyihova kpondɛ kɛ menli banwu ɔdaye nee ye bodane ne kpalɛ kɛmɔ bɛbahola la. (Gyɛr. 9:24) Ɛhye kile kɛ bɛbanwu nɔhalɛ ne kpalɛ. Alagye aneɛ nu edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ mɔɔ bɛhile ɔ bo kɛ “nɔhalɛ ne kpalɛ” la ɛngile edwɛkɛ mɔɔ awie suzu nwo dɛba la ala, emomu ɔle nɔhalɛ mɔɔ “ka ahenle kpalɛ,” kɛmɔ Baebolo nwunlonwunlilenli bie hanle la. Saa Baebolo sukunli bie banwu Gyihova a, zɔhane nɔhalɛ ne ka kɛzi ɔbɔ ye ɛbɛla la. Na ɔbua adenle ɔmaa ye kɛ ɔbanya “dahuu ngoane.”—Dwɔn 17:3.

JAPAN BATA NE YE GYIMA EDWUMA

Mbrɛ mberɛ kɔlɛ no, Miwako anyinli nwiɔ mɔɔ ɛnee wɔ Japan no nyianle anyandolɛ kpole. Ɔti bɛyɛle adwenepa bɛmaanle yɛhɔle Japan braintsi no bɔbɔ amaa yɛahola yɛabikye bɛ na yɛanlea bɛ. Yɛhɔle ɛkɛ ne wɔ 1999 nu.

Bɛ neɛnazo kpalɛ ne hyɛle me ngodzo maa mebrɛle me nwo aze me ne biewɔ mɔ yɛle adwuma, na mebule me kakula kyɛfa mɔɔ menva meboka Gyihova bodane kpole ne anwo la anwea.

Nansie, amandi nna nyɛleɛ ngakyirɛ ti, dɔbere wie mɔ wɔ hɔ a ɛyɛɛ se maa me kɛ mebede deɛmɔti bɛyɛɛ ninyɛne bie mɔ wɔ ɔkwan pɔtee bie do la ase. Ɔyɛɛ me hia kɛ mesuko abotore na meannzi me nwo do. Mekɛ kɔɔ do la, mesakyire me adwenle na mehole ye do kɛ Gyihova kɔhola hyira gyinaesi biala do mɔɔ duma kɛ yɛbɛbrɛ yɛ nwo aze yɛali adehilelɛ mɔɔ ɔdua ye ahyehyɛdeɛ ne do ɔfa ɔmaa yɛ la do. Adiema bie anzɛnɛ adiemaba bie ɛyɛ wɔ mɔbɔ mɔɔ kɛkala bɛ nriɔnyia nu ɛzɛkye a? Amaa wɔaziezie ngyegyelɛ ne na bɛadɛnla kɛ agɔnwolɛma la, ɔwɔ kɛ ɛyɛ ninyɛne ndɛndɛ na ɛfa adwenle kpalɛ a.*

Sɛ yɛbanya nhwɛso a, sɛ biew bu a, ɔsɛ kɛ yɛyɛ ho ayile ama akyiri no wayɛ gyima kpalɛ na awoya biara anwu nu. Na sɛ amba kɛ wɔanyɛ biew mɔɔ ebu no ho ayile ndɛ a, awoya no bɔhɔ zo ara. Mɔɔ yɛ ɛtane kpalɛ a le kɛ, biew no bɛtumi agyina asolo kpalɛ na ɔnrɛtumi ɛnrɛyɛ gyima kɛ mɔɔ ɔsɛ.

Kɛ mɔwuwu mɔɔ abu la, awɔzeɛ mɔɔ asɛkye wɔ wɔ nɛɛ Kilisieni bie avinli la fɛti kɛ bɛyɛ nwolɛ edwɛkɛ ndɛndɛ. Saa bɛanyɛ zɔ a, awɔzeɛ mɔɔ asɛkye la nyane ne bahɔ zo. Ɛya bahola anyi, na ɛbahola wɔaminli wɔ gɔnwo ne. Gyisɛse hanle kɛ: “Yɛ ndɛ na siesie edwɛkɛ.” (Mat. 5:25) Ɔti kɔ wɔ gɔnwo ne anwo lɔ na fa atiakunlukɛnlɛma kɛndɛ nwolɛ edwɛkɛ pefee. Kɛmɔti a ɛhye bahola ayɛ se a? Suzu ninyɛne nwiɔ anwo.

Muka, ebahola asisile kɛ ɛbapɛ dɩ ɛnganyi asɛm ne anu, bie a ɔluakɛ ɛmpɛ kɛ ɛkã ngyegyelɛ nwo edwɛkɛ.# Dwenle Yuwodiya nɩɩ Sintikɛ nwo, bɛdɩ Keleseɛne mediama mraalɛ mɔɔ ɛhye dɩ kɛ bantam bɛangiangia bɛ asɛm ne anu. Bɛ ngyegyelɛ ne yɛle kyɛnkyɛn, ɔluakɛ somafo Pɔɔlu mɔɔ nɩ ɔwɔ Lom mɔɔ ɔwɔ akyirika nɩ bɔbɔ dele nwo edwɛkɛ. Ɔhɔle bɛ ehyile kɛ bɛyɛ debie biala mɔɔ bɛbahola bɛayɛ dɩkɛ bɛahola bɛabɔ nu bikye bieko. (Fil. 4:2) Nwolilɛ, adwelielie kpalɛ, nɩɩ nvatotolɛ kpatanle nu, bɛ agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne bahola ayɛ kpalɛ bieko.

Ne nwiɛ̃ne, ebile ɛkpondɛ kɛ sonla fofolɛ ne kɛlie ye nvonle ne kɛdo nu. Na kɛkpondɛ kɛ ɛbanyia wɔ gɔnwo ne bio, ɛnle kɛ ɛbua ndɛnle. Dɔketa mɔɔ te wulowulo mɔɔ ebubu ye afoa a, ɔfa ye adwenle ɔsie kɛ ɔbasa ɔ nwo, na nwole ɛnle kɛ ɔkpondɛ ninyɛne dɔɔnwo mɔɔ manle wulowulo ne bubule. Saa ɛ nee wɔ gɔnwo ne kpendɛ a, mmafa wɔ adwenle ɛsie nvonle mɔɔ kɔlaa bɔle azele ne azo. Mmom, kaka kɛzi wɔ agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne sonle bolɛ manle wɔ, na fa wɔ adwenle sie mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ ɛyɛ na wɔaziezie nuhua.

Duzu a nti a ɛnkã ɛkile ɛ diema banyin anzɛɛ ɛ diema basia ɛnɛ, amaa woadzie asɛm ne na woadzie bɛ agɔnwolɛvalɛ boɛ a? “Mmaamaa ewia tɔ wɔ berɛ mɔɔ ɛ ya ɛtɛtee a.” (Eph. 4:26) Sɛ ɛtu agyalɛ ndɛndɛ amaa woadzie asɛm ne a, ɛbaboa woamaa wɔabɔ yɛ “asomdwoeɛ nkabom” ne anwo bane.—Eph. 4:1-3.
