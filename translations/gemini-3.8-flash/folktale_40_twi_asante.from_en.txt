Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka yi yɛ nokware)

TENA HƆ NA WOBƐHU

Bere bi na ɔbaa bi ne ne ba te faako. Sɛ ɔsoma no (baabiara) a, ɔka sɛ, "Merenkɔ." Na ne maame taa ka kyerɛ no sɛ, "Twɛn hɔ na wobɛhunu." Daa, sɛ ɔsoma no (baabiara) a, ɔka sɛ, "Merenkɔ." Ɛnna ne maame taa ka kyerɛ no sɛ, "Twɛn hɔ na wobɛhunu." Da koro bi abofra no kaa sɛ, "Daa, maame ka kyerɛ me sɛ, 'Twɛn hɔ na wobɛhunu'; ɛnnɛ da yi ara mereka me ho akɔhwehwɛ 'mɛhunu' no, sɛnea mɛtumi ahwɛ so." Enti ɔsii mu kɔe. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, (na) Asea-dua bi a ɛsow sika kɔkɔɔ abo wɔ hɔ. Asea-dua no bisaa no sɛ, "Adɛn nti na obiara mmaa ha da, nanso seesei woaba ha yi? Abofra no kae sɛ, "Me ne me maame te hɔ daa; ɔkae sɛ, 'Twɛn hɔ na wobɛhunu,' na me nso mekae sɛ mɛba abɛhwehwɛ 'Mɛhunu' akyi kwan, na matumi ahwɛ no." Pea-dua no kae sɛ, "Meka Wɔn ho, nanso Nea ɔso kyɛn Yɛn no da anim kɔ akyiri." Abofra no san sii mu bio. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu Nnyansin-tenten sɛ ɔreba abɛhyia no. Nnyentia-tenten bisaa no sɛ, “Adɛn, berɛ a obiara mfaa saa kwan yi so da no, afei wo afa so?” Abofra no buaa sɛ, “Daa, na maame kyerɛ sɛ, ‘Mɛhunu,’ enti mese mereba abɛhwehwɛ ‘Mɛhunu’ no, na mahwɛ no.” Nnyentia-tenten buaa sɛ, “Meyɛ wɔn mu bi, nanso kɔ w’anim.” Na abofra no sii so bio. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, (na) Nsramma-Agya wɔ hɔ, na ɔda dadesɛn a abubu so, na ɔreba (ne nkyɛn). Ɔhyiaa no. Glowing-Sparks kaa sɛ, “Adɛn, aberɛ a obiara mmaa ha da no, woaba ha?” Abɔfra no buaa sɛ, “Aberɛ a me ne me maame tete hɔ no, ɔkaa sɛ, ‘Mɛhunu,’ enti me nso mekaa sɛ mɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ ‘Mɛhunu’, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a mɛtumi ahwɛ no.” Glowing-Sparks kaa sɛ, “Meka Wɔn ho, nanso kɔ w’anim, ɛfiri sɛ deɛ ɔso paa (wɔ Yɛn mu) no wɔ hɔ.” Abɔfra no sɔre kɔɔ bio. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, Akoo no na wɔresuo dɔte agu fie bi ho. Akoo no kaa sɛ, “Afei yɛbɛnya obi mogya de agu saa fie yi ho.” Afei berɛ a abofra no duu hɔ no, wɔkaa sɛ, “Adɛn, berɛ a obiara mmaa ha da no, enti wo nso woaba ha?” Ɔno nso buaa sɛ, “Mete me maame nkyɛn berɛ nyinaa; ɔkaa sɛ, ‘Twɛn hɔ na wobɛhunu,’ enti mekaa sɛ mɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ ‘Mɛhunu’ no, na mahwɛ.” Ɛhɔ ara na Akoo no kyee abofra no, na wɔtwaa ne ti na wɔfaa ne mogya no guu kɔbere kuku mu; wɔfaa bi petee dan no so. Ɛnna Akoo no tutui kɔtenaa dua tenten bi so, na wɔbaa mmaako mmaako bɔɔ wɔn dua mu wɔ mogya no mu, na wɔn dua no yɛɛ kɔkɔɔ. Akoo no nyinaa faa wɔn dua de bɔɔ mu sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ nkaedum ama saa asɛm no. Afei, da koro bi, Akoo no mu baako kae sɛ ɔrekɔdidi. Ɔkɔe kɔtoo abofra no maame. Ɛnna maame no bisaa Akoo no sɛ, “Me ba a me ne no teɛ no yɛɛ onyannyarefoɔ pii. Sɛ me sɔre a, na mereka sɛ, ɔntwɛn hɔ na ɔbɛhunu; ɔno nso, sɛdeɛ kwasea teɛ, mpaapaakyɛw sɛ mede asɛmfua no redi dwuma, kaa sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ ‘Mɛhunu’, na watumi ahwɛ.” Ɛnnɛ yɛ nnawɔtwe pɛpɛɛpɛ a menhuu no, na mesrɛ wo sɛ, sɛ woahu no a, fa no brɛ me.” Akoo no kaa sɛ, “O, ɔbaa yɛn akuraa ase, na yɛn nso, yɛkumm no. Ne mogya no bi a yɛfaa de yɛɛ nkaedum maa asɛm no gu me dua yi so.” Enti, sɛ wokɔ (w’akwan so) a, kɔ na kɔka kyerɛ wo nkurɔfoɔ nyinaa sɛ, sɛ obi ka kyerɛ obi sɛ, ‘Twɛn hɔ na wobɛhunu’ a, ɛnneɛ wato ne so nsew.”

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
