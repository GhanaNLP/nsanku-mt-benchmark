In the same way, if we are not careful, we might think that the way we speak will please everyone. But that is not always the case. In some lands, when someone is expressing their view on a matter, they can speak very bluntly, regardless of whether they are speaking with an elderly person. Paul said that we should know ‘the right answer to give to everyone.’ What this means is that when we speak to someone, we should speak in a manner that will appeal to the person. How It Will Help You Study This Material

When you read a Bible account, imagine yourself in the situation, at that time

The Bible tells many true stories that happened. And we can learn many things from those stories. If we read those stories and imagine that we were there when it happened, it will really help us understand them well. Let us look at some things that will help us to do so.

• Sometimes read it aloud. When you read the Bible aloud, it really helps you to focus your mind on what was happening at that time. If you and your family are reading a portion of the Bible about the life of a certain person, you can do it this way: Assign parts to yourselves, and let each of you read the portion of one character. That will really make the story touch your hearts.

• Focus your attention on the one you are reading about. When you read about someone who did something, try to understand the motive behind what they did. You could ask yourself: What truly caused the person to speak in that manner, or to act that way? If this had happened to me, how would I have felt, and how would I have acted?

• Draw a picture of what you have read. Even if you don’t know how to draw well, try to draw a small something about the account you read from the Bible. If you do this, it will make it seem as though you were there when the event happened, and this will help it stay in your mind.
