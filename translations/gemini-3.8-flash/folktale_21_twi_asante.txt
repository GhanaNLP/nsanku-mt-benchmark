HOW THE TREE HYRAX, THE CHAMELEON, AND THE OWL CRY

OWEA, Kwaduampon Kyerefo, Opatuo, and Abosom-Akotere, Odomankoma-wuo, Aferehyia'boa, and Onam-bere-bere-be-ko Aburokyire were there. None of them had any clothes. Then Adobe came and sold herself to them. When Adobe woke up, she went to serve this one; the next day she went to serve that one; the next day she went to serve another one. One day it was the turn of Odoankoma-wuo; wherever he looked for Adobe, could he find her? Then he went and reported the matter to Owea and Opatuo. And they said, "Go and call her." Odomankoma-wuo set off:

"Adobe oh! Adobe oh!"

The hyrax said:

He preserves! He does not give! He fights, he does not give, he does not give, he does not give!

And Owl said:

When it is weighed and poured out like this, mm! When it is weighed and poured out like this, mm! mm!

And these are the natures and meanings of these three animals.

This is my folktale that I have told, whether it is sweet or not sweet, take some away and bring some back.
