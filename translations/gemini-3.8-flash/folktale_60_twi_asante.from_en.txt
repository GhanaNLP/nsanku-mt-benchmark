Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐYƐE A WƆDE (NKYEABƆ) "KURONTO" NE "BURU" FII ASE DI DWUMA

Ɛberɛ bi na ɔbɔfuo bi wɔ hɔ a wɔfrɛ no Kwaku, ne ne yere a wɔfrɛ no Buruwa, na esiane sɛ na wɔyɛ ahiafoɔ nti, wɔtu kɔtenaa faako foforɔ. Kwaku a ɔyɛ ɔbɔfuo no, berɛ a ɔkɔɔ kwaeɛ mu no, ɔkukum ekuo, nanso berɛ a ɔde baa fie no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Yei nyɛ (adie a yɛdi)." Enti ɔkɔtɔnii. Daa sɛ ɔkum biribi a, na ɛte saa ara. Nanso ne yere anhunu sika no da, enti ɔbisaa no sɛ, "Womfa sika mma, na womfa nam nso mma, na ɛno kyerɛ sɛn?" Ɔbuae sɛ, "Sɛ mekɔ na nkorɔfoɔ antɔ (nam no), na ɛporɔ a, meto twene." Da koro bi ɔkumm biribi, na ɔde too kwan no so, na ne yere dii n'akyi sɛ ɔrekɔhwɛ no. Ɛnna ɔhunuu sɛ ɔtɔn saa kwakuo no, gyee sika no, na ɔtɔɔ nsafufuo ahina (a wɔfrɛ no dɔnto) nomeeɛ. Ɛnna ne yere sanee n'akyi kɔɔ fie. Ɛno akyi baabiara no, Kwaku a ɔyɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no nso baeɛ. Ntɛm ara a ɔduruu fie pɛ na ɔkaa sɛ, "Mema wo adwo, Buruwa." Ɔno nso gyee so sɛ, "Ya Ekudonto (Aane, Kwakuo de kɔbɔ nsafufuo kuku)." Ɔbisaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na wogye me so saa kwan yi so?" Ɔno nso buaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuaeɛ a ɛfata deɛ ɛwɔ w'adwene mu no." Ɔbɔfoɔ no anka hwee. Adeɛ kyee adeɛ yee no, ɔkɔɔ afum bae; ɔkaa sɛ, "Memema wo anwummere." Ɔno nso gyee so sɛ, "Ya Buru." Buruwa bisaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na wogye me so saa kwan yi so?" Ɔbɔfoɔ no buaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuaeɛ a ɛfata deɛ ɛwɔ wo tirim no." Saa kwan yi so na ɛbɛyɛɛ amanneɛ wɔ abusuakuo no mu sɛ wɔde nkyia "Ya Kuronto" ne "Ya Buru" di dwuma. Wɔn fapem firi ɔbɔfoɔ Kwaku ne ne yere Buruwa hɔ. M'anansesɛm a metoeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na ma bi nso nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
