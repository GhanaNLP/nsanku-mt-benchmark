Yɛnni aseɛ koraa, yɛnni aseɛ koraa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokorɛ)

SƐNEA (NKRAMA) "KURONTO" NE "BURU" BƐYƐE A YƐDE DI DWUMA

Bere bi na ɔbɔfoɔ bi wɔ hɔ (a wɔfrɛ no) Kwaku, ne ne yere (a wɔfrɛ no) Buruwa, na esiane sɛ na wɔyɛ ahiafoɔ nti, wɔtu kɔtenaa faako foforo. Kwaku, ɔbɔfoɔ no, bere a ɔkɔɔ wuram no, ɔkunkum eku (adwene), nanso bere a ɔde baa fie no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Wei nyɛ aduane." Enti ɔkɔtɔnii. Dabiara a ɔbɛkum biribi no, saa pɛpɛɛpɛ na na ɔyɛ. Nanso ne yere no anhunu sika no da, na ɔbisaa no sɛ, "Womfa sika mma, na womfa nam nso mma, na ɛno kyerɛ sɛn?" Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Sɛ mekɔ, na nkorɔfoɔ antɔ (nam no), na ɛporɔ a, meto gu." Da koro bi ɔkunkum biribi, na ɔde too ɛkwan no so, na ne yere dii n'akyi sɛ ɔrekɔhwɛ no. Ɛnna ɔhunui sɛ ɔtɔn saa eku no, gyee sika no, na ɔde tɔɔ nsafufuo kutu (a wɔfrɛ no dɔnto) nomee. Ɛnna ne yere no sanee n'akyi kɔɔ fie. Ɛnkyɛree koraa na Kwaku, ɔbɔfoɔ no, nso bae. Bere a ɔduruu fie pɛ no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Memema wo adwo o, Buruwa." Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Yaa Ekudɔnto." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na wobua me saa kwan yi so?" Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuaeɛ a ɛfata deɛ ɛwɔ wo tirim no." Ɔbɔfoɔ no anka hwee. Adekyeeɛ a adeɛ sae no, ɔkɔɔ afum na ɔsane bae; ɔkaa sɛ, "Memema wo adwo o." Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Yaa Buru." Buruwa kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na wobua (me nkyea) saa kwan yi so?" Ɔbɔfoɔ no buaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuaeɛ a ɛfata deɛ ɛwɔ wo tirim no." Ɛkwan a ɛfa so bɛyɛɛ amammerɛ wɔ abusuakuo no mu sɛ wɔde nkyea mmuaeɛ "Yaa Kuronto" ne "Yaa Buru" di dwuma no nie. Ɛfiri ɔbɔfoɔ Kwaku ne ne yere Buruwa hɔ na ɛbae. M'anansɛm a metoeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, momfa bi nkɔ baabi, na momma bi nsane mmra me nkyɛn.
