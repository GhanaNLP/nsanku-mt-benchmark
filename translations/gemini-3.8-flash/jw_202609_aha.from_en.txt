19 Ɔnsɛ kɛɛ yɛsuro amonɛ mbelasɔ ekyi mɔɔ ɛlɛba mɔɔ bɔsɛkye ewiade ɛtane ɛhe wɔ Amagɛdɔn kɔnle nu la. Kɛmɔti ɛ? Yɛfa yɛ nwo yɛto Gyihova anwo zo kɛ yɛ agyanezoa nee yɛ efiadado. Ɔti yɛbahola yɛanyia anwodozo bɔkɔɔ kɛɛ: Mɔɔ Gyisɛse nee ye sɔfolɛ mɔɔ bɔwɔ anwuma la bali Nyamenle ndɛne wɔ Amagɛdɔn la, Nyamenle kpɔvolɛma kpalɛ ko bɔbɔ ɛnrɛgyinla. (Gyɛr. 25:33; Sɛk. 14:12) Noko wɔ kɔnle zɔhane nu, Nyamenle mboane kpalɛ mɔɔ sonle bolɛ la ko bɔbɔ ɛnrɛwu wɔ ye kpɔvolɛma—sonla anzɛɛ sunsum ɛtane—sa nu.—Luku 21:28; 2 Pita 2:9.

20 Wɔ Amagɛdɔn kɔn ne fo nu no, ndwomtoni ne edwɛkɛ mɔɔ sunsum ne hanle hilele ye no bahola ayɛ nɔhalɛ kpalɛ: “Apem bagua wɔ kɛnla na apem du noko bagua wɔ sa nniɛma nu, noko ɔnrɛba wɔ nwo bɔbɔ. Wɔ anye ngome a ɛbava wɔanwunle ye wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɛbanwu amumuyɛma ndenle no a. Ɔluakɛ ɛhanle kɛ: ‘Gyihova a le me nveanleka a,’ enti wɔva Nyamenle mɔɔ Korɔ Ewiade Amuala la wɔyɛ wɔ dabilikɛ.” (Edw. 91:7-9) Saa Amagɛdɔn bɔ ɔ bo ala a, yɛbahola yɛazelɛ kɛ Gyihova balie ye menli ne ngoane kpalɛ wɔ nwonane, adwenle, nee sunsum nu, tɛ kɛ kuw ala, na kɛ ngoko ngoko noko. Ɔluakɛ ɛka kɛ: “Gyihova a le me nveanleka a,” Gyihova bɔ ɛwɔkɛ kɛ: “Ɔnrɛba wɔ nwo bɔbɔ.” Enti, bɛmaa yɛva ahotosoɔ yɛnea kenlema, mɔɔ yɛze kɛ yɛbahola yɛava yɛ nwo yɛato Gyihova azo kpalɛ kɛ ɔbabɔ yɛ nwo bane.

EDWENE 36 Yɛbɔ Yɛ Ahonle Nwo Ban

ENYENLE Ke ye bahola yeeti Jehovah ahyi wɔ ye ahonle nu.

Kɛzi Ɛkɛyɛ Na Anyia "Ahonle Mɔɔ Tie Aseɛ"

"Maa wɔ sonvolɛ ahonle mɔɔ tie edwɛkɛ."-1 KI. 3:9.

MENOMA tii mbanyimma ano wɔ ninyɛ dɔɔnwa ti. Menoma dɔɔnwa bɔ ye zɔ akyi sɛ be nwu bɛ anzɛɛ sɛ be kulo kɛ be nyia asotwe a. Sɛ ɔba Nyamenle ye etiedi nuhua a, bie mɔ yɛ kɛ be tie ano amaa be anyia duma, kɛ mɔɔ ɛzonlenle mgbanyima yɛle wɔ Gyisɛse ye berɛ zo la. (Matt. 23:5) Noko yɛmɛ, yɛ tie Gyihova ano ɔluakɛ yɛ nwu ye nkyile nwo mvasoɔ. (Isa. 48:17, 18) Ɛhye le adwenle kpalɛ bɔ yɛ gyina zo tie ye ano, noko yɛ lɛ ngilenu titile bɔ ɔ boro ɛhye bɔ yɛ gyina zo tie ye ano.

2 Debie titiri mɔ ti mɔ yɛtie Gyihova azo a le kɛ yɛkulo ye kpalɛ. (Matt. 22:37, 38) Yɛ ɛlɔlɛ ne ka yɛ ma yɛye "tiezo fvi ahonle nu." (Rom. 6:17) Ɛlɔlɛ mɔ yɛlɛ ma Gyihova ngyikyi "ahonle mɔ tiezo" ka yɛ ma yɛyɛ yɛ mɔdenlebɔlɛ kɔsɔɔti kɛ yɛbalie ye mɛla azo, nnyɛ kɛ awie mɔ ɛnwu yɛ ala anzɛɛ yɛnwu mvasoɛ mɔ wɔ nu sesei ala, na mmɔm dahuu. Yɛdie to nu kɛ yɛ ahonle yɛ sesebɛ, kɛti asunsum kpalɛ mɔ yɛbalɛ la bahola ayɛ sɔhwɛ wɔ mekɛ bie mɔ nu. Wɔ adawubɔlɛ ɛhye nu, yɛbahwɛ Baebolo nu ndɛbɔsoma nwiɔ mɔ baboa yɛ ma yɛanyia ahonle mɔ tiezo. Mɔlimoa, yɛbazuzu mekɛ mɔ Gyihova liele Yizilayɛmma fvile Igyibiti la azo. Afei, yɛbahwɛ Anyianyi kpalɛ mɔ Famwunli Yehɔsafate mɔ fvi Dwuda yɛle la.

(Nea nkyekyɛmu 3)

Ama bɛahwɛ bɛahwe amonye bulu nli mɔɔ li awieeliɛ ne anwo a, nna ɔwɔ kɛ bɛbɔ odwambani anzɛɛ apɔnkye nrenyia afɔle na bɛpete ɔ mogya ne wɔ bɛ azua ne anwomenle nwo. (Ex. 12:5, 7) Gyihova bapɛ amodinli mɔɔ mogya ne wɔ nwolɛ ne azo na yealie sonla mini ne awunle kɛnlɛma mɔɔ li bombo yɛɛ bɛ mowa mɔɔ li bombo ngoane. Noko akee, nna Yizilayɛmma ne bahola anwo atete kɛ bɛmbadie. Duzu ati ɔ? Nna bɛbahola bɛazulo kɛ Yigyibitiyama ne bahola azɛkye bɛ, ɔluakɛ nna Yigyibitiyama ne bu odwambani kɛ debie kpole. (Ex. 8:25-27) Noko akee, Yizilayɛmma ne nwunle kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛtie edwɛkɛ ndɛndɛ na “bɛyɛle kɛ mɔɔ Gyihova hanle la pɛpɛɛpɛ.” (Ex. 12:28) Gyihova yilale bɛ ɔlua bɛ mowa mɔɔ li bombo mɔɔ ɔlielie bɛ ngoane ne ati.

4 Nɔkɔwunli nɔ nɔ, Yizilayili-amun vi Igipiti. Dumɔ nɔ, Dwihova lele bɛ kɔle Boka Kɔkɔlɛ nɔ anli anyunzonzolɛ afoa nɔ anyi, na akyi nɔ ɔmanle bɛ adehilelɛ mɔɔ sonla adwenli ɛntu bo. Ɔzele bɛ kɛ bɛhakyi bɛ anyi bɛkɔ anyunvumɔ na bɛbɔ asoanle wɔ anyunkun nɔ nee boka-amun nɔ avinli. Wɔ asoanle nɔ anyi, nna ɔzɔho kɛ bɛbalua Fɛlo asafo nɔ mɔɔ nna bɛɛti bɛ nee Boka Kɔkɔlɛ nɔ avinli. Ɛhye anyi amuala, Yizilayili-amun yɛle mɔɔ Dwihova luale ye sonvo Mosisi anwo zole manle mɛla nɔ bieko. (Efi. 14:1-4) Na mɔɔ bɛnwunle kɛ Igipiti-amun kɔkpɛ bɛ anwo nɔ, “sulɛ kpole hanle bɛ” na bɛbɔle Mosisi sombo kɛ ɔlele bɛ kɔle ewule nu mɔɔn! (Efi. 14:5-11) Duzu ti a bɛbɔle bo kɛ bɛ adwenli kɛhyi bɛ kpɔkɛ mɔɔ bɛbɔle kɛ bɛbedie asɔle nɔ anwo a? Na kɛzi Dwihova bule bɛ suban nɔ ɛ?

5 Baebolo ne ka kɛ wɔ Bɔkɛ Kɔkɔlɛ ne anloa ne, Yizilayɛmma ne “anyi anlie [Gyihova] ɛyɛyɛlɛ kpole ne mɔ” mɔɔ ɔyɛle wɔ Yigyibiti ne azo anzɛɛ bɛan-“gakyii” ye nɔhalɛ ɛlɔlɛ ne. (Kenga Edwɛndolɛ 106:7 nee ɔ bo ɛkɛ ne edwɛkɛ ne.) Bɛnwunle kɛzi Gyihova bobɔle bɛ baanyinli mɔ anwo ban wɔ sɔhyia mɔɔ tɔ zo bulu ne anu la. Noko bɛande Gyihova ɛlɔlɛ kpole mɔɔ ɔlɛ maa bɛ la abo kpalɛ anzɛɛ bɛannye bɛanli kɛ ɔbagye bɛ bieko. Mɔɔ Gyihova nwunle kɛzi bɛ ahonle de la, ɔhanle kɛ menli ne “dele atua . . . wɔ Bɔkɛ Kɔkɔlɛ ne anloa.” Ɔwɔ nuhua kɛ bɛli Mosisi anzi bɛpɛle Bɔkɛ Kɔkɔlɛ ne anu ɛdeɛ, noko bɛangakyi bɛ suban ne ɛlɛ. SUA BƆNƐ MAAHO BƆ YƐVƆ YISILAYƐLIMBA NƐ

6 Adwoso nhoma ne kyerɛ kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛhwe yɛ ahenu nu fa kɛzi yɛtie Gyihova nee mrenya mɔɔ ɔfa bɛ ɔto hɔ kɛ bɛli ye menli ne anyunlu ne. Asoo yɛtie ye wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛbanya nyilalɛ mɔɔ ɛkɛ ne ala bie pɛ la a? Ɔbayɛ kɛ Yizilayɛmma ne bie mɔ fualɛ boane mogya ne guale anlenu mgbolɛ ne mɔ anwo kɛ mɔɔ ɔbaye bɛ nwo kɛ ɔbamaa bɛ mmalɛ banyinli ahɔ zo adɛnla aze pɛ ala. Bieko, asoo yɛ setie ne gyi mɔɔ awie mɔ yɛ la azo? Wɔ Akpohɔle ne anɔkue ne, Gyihova manle Yizilayɛmma ne “adwomaye wɔ Yigyibitima ne anyunlu.” (Adwo. 12:36) Noko mekɛ mɔɔ akee Yigyibitima ne dwazolɛ bɛ nwo ekyi ne, Yizilayɛmma ne minli anwodozo mɔɔ bɛlɛ wɔ Gyihova nu la na bɛdele atua. (Adwo. 14:10-12) Wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛ subunle ne le ngakyile la, saa yɛlɛ ahonle mɔɔ yɛtie debie a, ɔbamaa yɛanyia anwodozo wɔ Gyihova nu nee mrenya mɔɔ yedua bɛ kɛ bɛli anyunlu la nu. (Kenga Gyɛlɛmaya 23:4.) Yɛbali adehilelɛ mɔɔ yɛbanyia la azo ɔnva nwo kɛ yɛnde ngɛlɛlera mɔɔ ɔwɔ nwolɛ la kɔsɔɔti abo bɔbɔ anzɛɛ kɛzi yɛbayɛ la bamaa bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛkye Gyihova la asa aze fa la. Kɛzi yɛbahola yɛahoati nvonleɛ mɔɔ Yizilayɛmma ne yɛle la na yɛanyia ahonle mɔɔ yɛtie debie ɛ?

7 Ma ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ ɛwɔ ma Gyɛhova no nu ɛyia. Yɛ edwiekɛtie no baha asɔhwɛ nu wɔ aberɛ mɔɔ yɛnnwu anyunlɔ mɔɔ Nyamenle asafo no adwulilɛ fa ba no ndɛndɛ anzɛɛ aberɛ mɔɔ yɛ edwiekɛtie ti yɛyia ngyegyelɛ no. Saa zɔ

sisi a, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkae kɛ bɔ yɛtie Gyihova mɔtue a ne titilii nye kɛ yɛkulo ye na yɛwɔ ye nu anyelazo. (1 Dwɔn 5:3) Yɛ ɛlɔlɛ ne boka yɛ ma yɛtua ye abotore nu na yɛdie yɛto nu kɛ bie a yɛkɛhyia mekɛ ansaana yɛanwu anyunlunyia bɔ wɔ yɛ tieyɛ ne nu.—Asɛnk. 7:8; Maeka 7:7.

8 Yɛ berɛ he anwo, Nyamenle menli yɛ suakpɔlɛ kpalɛ wɔ tiezonlɛ nu, amomɔ berɛ mɔɔ bɛanwu nvasoɔ mɔɔ wɔ nu prɛko ne la po. Kɛ neaso ne, wɔ COVID-19 yelɛ ne berɛ zo, bɛkilele asafo mɔ kɛ bɛva videoconferencing bɛyɛ bɛ nhyiamu mɔ. Asafo nu kpanyinli bie mɔɔ wɔ Peru la kae kɛ: “Wɔ mɔlebɛbo ne, membele kɛ ngilenu ne ɛntumi ɛnyɛ gyima wɔ yɛ ɛkɛ.” Amomɔ ɔhwenle ɔ nzi la, ɔbɔle kpɔkɛ kɛ ɔbatie Nyamenle na yeava ye nwo yeato ɔ nwo zo. (Mrɛ. 3:5, 6) Nzu a vi nu rale? “Mennwunle kɛzi Gyihova yila menli mɔɔ tie ɔ nzonlɛ la,” ɔka zɔ. “Yɛ asafo ne nyianle ahoɔdenle kpalɛ na bɛyɛle kɔnkyelɛ kpalɛ.” Bieko, dwenle neaso fofolɛ nwo. Mɔɔ ɔhanle ekyi wɔ 2020 nu la, ahyehyɛdeɛ ne hakyili ngilenu mɔɔ fane asafo mɔ nee eku mɔɔ ka anwuma aneɛ nwo la. Mɔɔ adiema raalɛ bie mɔɔ wɔ Japan la nwenle kɛ bɛbapɛ eku mɔɔ ɔwɔ nu la bɛali la, ɔhanle kɛ: “Metua kɛ mebayɛ tievoolɛ wɔ nhyɛleɛ fofolɛ ne anwo na menyɛ bɔbɔ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ɛnle kpalɛ wɔ nwolɛ. Menwɔ anyelazo kɛ mebanwu nvasoɔ mɔɔ wɔ nu la.”

10 ƐZINZANLƐ NE

Nyamenle Belemgbunlilɛ ne anwo edwɛkpa ne mɔɔ yɛka la kile noko kɛ yɛbaha yɛahile awie mɔ kɛ ɔnrɛhyɛ biala ninyɛne ɛtane ekyi ɛhye bara awieleɛ. Yɛbahɔ zo yɛaha edwɛkɛ zɔhane kɔkpula kɛ ninyɛne ekyi ɛhye bara awieleɛ wɔ Amagɛdɔn anu pɛpɛɛpɛ. (Mat. 24:14) Bieko, wɔ anwongenga kpole ne anu, edwɛkɛ mɔɔ yɛka la bahola ayɛ kpole kpalɛ na yeala ali pɔkɛ, mɔɔ bamaa menli ayɛ kɛ asoo bɛbalie Belemgbunlilɛ ne bɛado nu anzɛɛ bɛnrɛlie. Wɔ bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛkyi Gyihova la afoa nu, yɛ edwɛkɛ ne bayɛ kɛ asoo ɔle kpɔkɛ kpole mɔɔ “ɛnle kɛ mɔɔ bɛze la.”* (Yek. 16:21) Ɔda ali kɛ, bɛva ɛya kpole kɛmɔ Fɛlo yɛle wɔ Nyevile Kɔkɔlɛ ne anloa la. Yɛbahola yɛaziezie yɛ nwo yɛamaa dwazotia mɔɔ bara kenlebie la ɔlua mɛnla mɔɔ yɛbalie mɛla mɔɔ se yɛha edwɛkɛ ne la yɛali kɛkala, ɔnva nwo kɛzi awie mɔ bu yɛ la.

SUA ƆHENE JEHOSHAPHAT ƐSETSIELE NE NYU

10 Baebolo ne noko kile menle mɔɔ bɛyɛle asetie ne bɛ nwo neazo kpalɛ.

* Nea “Ngisikpɔ Neɛ Nkanbivolɛ Neɛ Bɔbivoe” wɔ August 2025 The Watchtower nu.

10. Kɛ amansan mɔɔ bɛlɛba bɛado bɛ nwo do bɔle Ɔhene Jehoshaphat ɛzule la, kɛzi ɔdele nganeɛ ɛ?

Kɛ nzinwoma, Belemgbunli Jehoshaphat hwenle maanle nsa mɔɔ bɛhanle bɛ nwo bɔ nu kɛ bɛba bado Yuda anwo zolɛ kɔne la. Ɔle fɛlɛkyi kɛ, Jehoshaphat nee ye menli ne hulole. (2 Chron. 20:1-3) Na belemgbunli ne ammaa ɛzulɔlɛ ne amba ye nwo; ɔyɛle debie. Jehoshaphat ninyɛne mɔɔ ɔyɛle nzinli la a banwoma yɛ ma yɛanyia ahonle mɔɔ yie tie debie.

11 Si wo tirin kɛ ɛkɛpɛ Jehovah na ɛkayɛ ye nzonilɛ. Mɔɔ ezunle hanle Jehoshaphat la, “ɔzinle ye tirin kɛ ɔkɛpɛ Jehovah.” (2 Abak. 20:3) Ɛnee Jehoshaphat tɛnla kɛ ɔnea Jehovah nwɔhanle kɛ ɔkɛhwe ye adwenle. (2 Abak. 19:3) Noko wɔ ngyegyele ɛhye anu, ɔzinle ye tirin kɛ ɔkɛpɛ Jehovah na ɔkayɛ debie biala mɔɔ Ɔkɛha kɛ ɔyɛ la. Jehoshaphat lale ɛhye ali wɔ asɔneyɛlɛ mɔɔ vi ye ahonle nu la anu. (Kenga 2 Abakronika 20:5, 6, 10-12.) Sɛ yɛwɔ ngyegyele kpole nu a, bɛma yɛli ye nzonilɛ zɔ. Yɛbahola yɛazi yɛ tirin kɛ yɛkayɛ Jehovah nzonilɛ na yɛapɛ ye adwenle

dwuma wɔ yɛn asetsena fã koraa kɛseɛ nu anu, a yɛn asɔreyɛ nso boka ho. Ɛkɛ koraa na, ninyɛne dɔɔnwo a ɔwɔ Intanɛt nu tu ewiase yi sunsum kɔ anim. Kɛ nhwɛso no, intanɛt do aguamambɔ ahyɛ baabiara ma na mpanimfoɔ n’abofra nyinara kɔla nya ho kwan asann. Sexting—a ɔyɛ kɛ obi bɛyɛ bɛmana bɔnsosɛm mfonini, vidio, anaa asɛm—agye baabiara, titiriw no wɔ mmabun nu. Intanɛt do atosɛm dɔɔnwo nso wɔ hɔ a ɔsɛe nokware na ɔma menmen dɔɔnwo fɔm kwan. Ninyɛne a menmen gya wɔ kɔmputa ntanɛt do dɔɔnwo kyerɛ ninyɛne kɛkɛ a ɔkɔ yie wɔ bɛ asetsena nu. Mfonini, nsɛm a bɛka, na vidio tian kɛbɔ kɔla ma yɛte nka kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛsɛe berɛ dɔɔnwo wɔ agodie n’akwantu nu, a ɔba bɔbɔ kɛ ɔbɛsɛe yɛn asɔreyɛ mpo.—Heb. 10:24, 25.

Maa Krayst ne anwundwolɛ ne eli be ahonle nu tumi

Kolosaefo 3:15 ka "Kilisito anzodwulɛ" nwo edwɛkɛ. Duzu a le "Kilisito anzodwulɛ" ne? Ɔka ahonle nu anzodwulɛ mɔɔ ahenle kola nyia ye kɛ Kilisito ɛdoavolɛ la anwo edwɛkɛ. Nzodwulɛ zɔhane vi nɔhalɛ mɔɔ yɛze kɛ Gyihova nee ye Ra ne kulo yɛ la anu.-Dwɔn 14:23, 27.

Somafo Paul hyehyɛɛ Kristofo mɔɔ wɔ Kɔlose asɔre ne anu la kɛ bɛmaa asomdwoe zɛhae nwo bie ɛli tumi wɔ bɛ ahonle nu, anzɛɛ bɛ sonla nu. Kɛzi ɔti ɛ? Giliki edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ mɔɔ Paul lale ye ali wɔ ɛke mɔɔ kile “ɛli tumi” la fane asoaso mɔɔ di agoru nwo edwɛkɛ la nwo. Ɛhye ɛngile kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ “Kilisito ne asomdwoe” ne nyia edwɛkɛ nwo pɛlepɛlelilɛ mɔɔ li awieleɛ la wɔ yɛ ahonle nu ɔ?

Kɛ nhwɛso no, sɛ asɛm foforɔ bi sɔre yɛn nye yɛn diema gyidini bi avin a, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛma "Kilisito asomdwoe" no yɛ "kwanhyɛnii." Ɔbaboka yɛn ama yaazi kpae wɔ ninyɛne mɔ yɛbahola yɛayɛ ama asomdwoe nye koroyɛ adɛn no nwo. Sɛ yɛfa kwan zɔhane a, sɛ ɔkile kɛ yɛgya yɛn awozonye pɛ anaa yɛn asɛm to nkyɛn mpo a, yɛda ali kɛ yɛlɛma "Kilisito asomdwoe" no di tumi wɔ yɛn ahonle nu. -Kɔl. 3:12-14.

Bulumu ɛhe ɛnla ɛtɔne nu. Bɛva bɛmaa kɛ ɔboka ewiade amuala Baebolo ngyehyele edwuma ne mɔɔ bɛfa ahonle kpalɛ ahyɛdeɛ bɛdi nwolɛ gyima la anwo. Saa ɛkulo kɛ ɛyɛ ahyɛdeɛ a, yɛsɛlɛ wɔ kɔ donate.jw.org.
