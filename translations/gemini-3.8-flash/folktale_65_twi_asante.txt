We are not alike, not alike at all

Whether it turned out well in the end, whether it burned

Cricket and Ananse set out to look for corn to plant. And they got only two ears of corn. Then Ananse said, "I am going to plant mine." And Cricket said, "I am going to roast mine and eat it." Ananse went and planted his. Cricket also put his on the fire. It had already dried up, yet it remained there. Ananse also went to the farm; he went to uncover it and look. The corn said, "Do not uncover me, or your hand will pierce my eye." When Ananse turned his eyes to look at his Dog, his Dog said, "Do not look at me, for it was not I who did it." When Ananse went to break a stick to beat the Dog, the Stick said, "Do not break me, or your Dog will bite me." Ananse came home and told Cricket the news. As he was laughing, he fell into the fire. That is why Cricket's teeth are completely burnt.

This story of mine that I have told, whether it is sweet or not sweet, take some away, and bring some back.
