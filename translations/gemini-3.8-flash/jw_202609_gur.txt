BIBLE GOOD NEWS

Let the peace of Christ rule in your hearts

Colossians 3:15 speaks about “the peace of Christ.” What is “the peace of Christ”? It is the peace that a person can have if he is a follower of Christ. This peace lets us know that Jehovah and his Son, Jesus, love us.—John 14:23, 27.

Paul advised the Colossian brethren to let the peace that Christ gives rule their hearts. How so? The Greek word for "rule" that Paul used is like what a referee says in a football match that no one can change. This makes us realize that like the referee, we too should let the peace that Christ gives help us make good decisions in our lives.

For example, if we have an issue with our brother, we should let “the peace of the Christ” act as an “umpire.” This will help us know what to do to maintain peace and unity between ourselves and our brothers. For us to be able to do this, we may sometimes have to forgo our personal preferences in order to maintain unity. This will show that we let “the peace of the Christ” rule in our hearts.—Col. 3:12-14.
