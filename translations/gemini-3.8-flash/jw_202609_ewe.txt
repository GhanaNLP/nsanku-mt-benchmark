BIBLE EXPRESSION

Let the Peace of Christ Rule in Your Hearts

Colossians 3:15 talks about “the peace of Christ.” What is “the peace of Christ”? It is the inner calm that a person experiences because of being a disciple of Christ. We experience this peace when we know that Jehovah and his Son love us.—John 14:23, 27.

The apostle Paul encouraged the Christians in the Colossian congregation to let this peace rule in their hearts, that is, in the person they are on the inside. How could they do this? The Greek word translated “rule” that Paul used here is related to the word used for an umpire (referee or arbitre) in athletic contests. This means that just as that umpire makes decisions regarding how the game is played, in the same way we should let the “peace of Christ” direct how we should live our lives.

For example, if a disagreement arises between us and a fellow Christian, we must let “the peace of the Christ” act as “an umpire” for us. This figurative umpire will help us know what to do so that peace and unity may prevail. If we do what it directs us to do and give up our own preferences or freely forgive for the sake of peace, it means we are allowing “the peace of the Christ” to rule in our hearts.—Col. 3:12-14.
