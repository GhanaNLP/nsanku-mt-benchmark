1926

Mfeɛ ɛha baako mɔ pɛ nu

“BEBERƐ neɛ ebia mɔ Nyame bama ɔbɛnya duma kɛseɛ. Nyame bama ebie abɛyɛ ne dansefoɛ wɔ asee ha,” kɛ The Watch Tower mɔ efii adi January 1, 1926 hanleɛ.

Kɛ afe 1926 kɔlɛ ne, Gyihova—nam Kristo mɔɔ ɔle asɔne ne ti ne so—manle ɔ lale ali kpalɛ kɛ asɛnkɛnga gyima ne a ɔsɛ kɛ ɔyɛ debie mɔɔ hyia kpalɛ wɔ Kilisienyi ngoane nu a. Ɔti ne, “akɛnganvo mɔɔ di nɔhalɛ na ɔle anyunlumuanyunlumu ne” vale nwoma fofolɛ manle Nyamenle sonvolɛma ne, ɔsiesiele debiezulɛ ne mɔ, na ɔmanle bɛdele Kilisienyi debiezulɛ ngakyile ne mɔ abo kpalɛ. (Mat. 24:45) Kristo manle bɛ nsa hale azua nee zangwanzilɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛhyia na bɛava bɛayɛ asɛnkɛnga gyima ne.

NWOMA FOFOƆ NE NSAKRAMU ƆWƆ NHYIAMUBOƆ MU

Wɔ April bosoom ne nu, The Golden Age (mɔ afei bɛfrɛ ye Awake!) bɔɔ nwuma foforɔ bie mɔ bɛfrɛ ye Deliverance amanie kɛ bɛyiyi adi. Saa nwuma ɛhe ti mɔ ɛbahyɛ Studies in the Scriptures anu kɛ asɛmpa biala ne mɔ bɛtɔ beedi gyima kɛse wɔ asɛmpakpa ɛbɔ ne nu. Na banya saa nwuma ne ama ninyɛne dodo mɔ bɛhwehwɛ, anuanom mɔ bɛwɔ Bethel tɔɔ afidie foforɔ mɔ bɛfa tin nwuma mɔ ebia otumi tin nwuma 22 wɔ miniti baako nu. Baebolo Asuafo ne mɔ faa saa nwuma foforɔ ne maa wɔ berɛ biala mɔ bɛbanya kwan.

Mfe pɛnli dɔɔnwo nu, Baebolo Sukuuma ne mɔ dwenlenle Studies in the Scriptures ne mɔ buluku nzonle ne mɔ anwo wɔ bɛ ayia ninyɛne nu. Noko ɔbɔle ɔ bo wɔ May nu la, bɛbɔle ɔ bo kɛ bɛdwenle The Watch Tower nu edwɛkɛ nzonle nzonle nwo. Duzu a vile nu rale a?

“Asafo ketewa mɔɔ wɔ Bunker Hill, Illinois kyea wo,” asafo bie hɛlɛle ye wɔ kɛlata mɔɔ ɔhɛlɛle nwolemʋnli Rutherford nu. “Kwasiada biala yɛsua [nwoma mɔɔ] Asafo ne ayi amaa dahuu ne mɔ. Yɛda ase kpole kɛ yɛmuala yɛkola yenwu ewia fofolɛ ne kɛ mɔɔ ɔfɛta la.”

“Hannwun foforɛ” nna, anaa ndɛkye aseɛ kɛlɛkɛlɛ, boale Baebolo Asuafoɛ no ma bɛvale bɛ adwene bɛtɛle asɛnkapɔ adwuma no so. Kɛ mfasoɛ no, edwene “Su Anaa Alahyumu—Benini A?” kyerɛkyerɛɛ mu kɛ “su yɛye,” mɔ ɛfa kɔmɔ ne nyɛmɔ pa ngomekye so no, maa bie mɔ bule nokorɛ Kristofoɛ adwuma pa no amba bwee. Edwene no bole kɔkɔ kɛ: “Mɔ ɔkulo Awurade no kɛseɛ kɛseɛ . . . di ne mmla so. . . . Yehowa se: ‘Bɛyɛ me adansefoɛ kɛ meye Nyamenle.’ . . . Kristoni no bɔ mɔden kɛ ɔdi adanse kɛ Nyamenle adansefoɛ kronkron.”

Adesua fofolɛ bie mɔɔ yɛse, “Afɔlebɔlɛ Nee Ɛzonlenlɛ,” yilehilele nu kɛ nɔhalɛ Kilisienenma kile bɛ ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ bɛlɛ maa Gyihova la wɔ adenle mɔɔ bɛbalua zolɛ bɛazonle ye nɔhalɛ nu la azo. Adesua ne hanle hilele Baebolo Sukuluma kɔsɔɔti kɛ bɛva mɔdenlebɔlɛ, bɛ ahonle kɔsɔɔti nee bɛ seseebɔlɛ bɛli Awulae ɛzonlenlɛ gyima ne amrahyee na “bɛyɛ Awulae adansezhima na bɛbɔ ye edwɛkɛ ne nolo bɛahile aleɛabo maanle maanle nee menli ne.”—Isa. 43:10, 12; Mat. 24:14.

AMANSAN NHYIAMU NZELEKƐ ASƐMPAKANƐ GYIMA NE SO DUDU

May awieyɛ ne, Baebolo Asuafo dɔɔn yiale nhyiamu kɛse bi ase wɔ London, England. Adwumayɛfo atuatew bi a ɔkɔle so wɔ Britain maa ɛyɛle den kɛse kɛ bɛbanya adwumakuw a bɛtsetse nwoma, enti anuanom mmarima a bɛwɔ Brooklyn Bɛtɛl yɛle adwuma dodo kɛ bɛtsitsie nwomaba a bɛfɛr ye The Standard for the People ne bi 117,000 fofor bɛmanle nhyiamu kɛse ne.

Memendee, May 29, bɛvale ngɛlata nwaa bɛyɛle egyima kpale. Saa kɛnɛ ne asafo titiri ninyɛne nhyehyɛe ne huale adwenle kɛ: “Bɛto nsa bɛfɛlɛ awie biala mɔɔ yebua ye nwo afɔle mɔɔ ɔwɔ asafo titiri ne azo kɛ ɔva ye nwo ɔwula Belemgbunli ne mini Belemgbunlililɛ ne noloelilɛ nu. Bɛhɔ Alexandra Palace ndɛndɛ kɛ mɔɔ bɛbahola biala, na bɛbɔ bɛ nwo amaneɛ wɔ Service Department ne kɛ bɛba egyimalilɛ ne.”

Bɔ bo literature mɔɔ bɔ kyɛle no bo dodo bo 100,000 so. July 15, 1926 Watch Tower no bɔle amaniɛ kɛ: “Anuanwoman no anye gyee kpole kpaa. Bɛdele nka kɛ bɛyɛ bɛ ahekele kɛ bɛbali Jehovah ahyɛdeɛ no so: ‘Bɛle me dasefow kɛ mele Nyamenle.’ ”

Enviɛ nna, wɔ Royal Albert Hall, Anuanin Rutherford hanle debie fɛle “Azua Kpole Kpole Mɔ Wɔ Ewiase Ye Ɛsusu—Sanipe.” Ɛha mɔ bɛbulele anuanin ne pɛɛ, menle 13,000 yile asoayianlɛ sua ne anu. Menle dɔɔnwo mɔ ɛnnyale bie tena ne tilenle dwumalilɛ ne aso kpole wɔ afidie mɔ bɛyɛle wɔ aze ne azo.

Wɔ ne nkɔmmɔbɔ ne anu, Onua Rutherford kyerɛkyerɛɛ mu kyerɛɛ kɛ ewiase tumidi mɔɔ bɔhwe ase ne vi kɛ bampɛ Gyihova nee Ne Famiɛyɛle ne anwo edwɛkɛ. Onua Rutherford kɔle zo bɔɔ League of Nations ne anwo gua yɛɛ ɔhanle kɛ ɔbawie mɔɔ ɔnnyɛ hwee bieko.

“Bɛliele asɛm ne . . . , na bɛhwehwɛle Ngɛlɛlera ne anu kpalɛ daa.”—ASOMAƆFOƆ 17:11.

Buluku sɔ kpolan kɛlɛtɛ bie na afei ndelebɛbo mɔɔ vi Zion’s Watch Tower mu mɔɔ nuanom mrenyia na mmaa bahola akenkan da biara wɔ afoɛ no mu. Yehowa nmenvuama vale zɔ buluku kunkun no lili gyuma kɔtoole 1927 berɛ mɔɔ bɛbɔle ɔbo kɛ bɛyiyi kɛlɛtɛ no na ndelebɛbo no adi wɔ Year Book no mu.* Afei yɛholale yɛsuannle Baebolo kɛlɛtɛ dɔɔnwo na afei ndelebɛbo mɔɔ nyunlu tew mɔɔ vi The Watch Tower mu.

4 Yɛn tim ngaso kyerɛ Yudafo kuo bi suban a ɔsomafo Paul kɛkaa asɛmpa kyerɛɛ bɛ wɔ Makedonia kuropɔn Beroea mu no. (Kenga Asomafo 17:10, 11.) Saa Beroeafo no ante Paul asɛm no nkyerɛɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ pɛ. Baebor no kyerɛ bɛ dɛ nna wɔwɔ “suban pa” na “wɔgyee asɛm no” a na ɔreka no. Ber a wɔde deɛ Paul kae no totoo deɛ na wɔkenga wɔ Kyerɛwnsɛm no mu no ho no, wɔhunuu dɛ Paul rekyerɛkyerɛ nokwar no. Yɛyɛ biribi a ɛte saa ber a yɛretie ɔkasa bi wɔ asafo nhyiam ase anaasɛ nhyiam kɛse bi ase. Yɛkɔ so di ekyi ber a ɔkasafo no kenga Nyamen Asɛm kyerɛ yɛn no. Ɛnam saaso nti, yɛbɛtumi anya ahotɔsɔ dɛ ɔkasafo no reka nokwar akyerɛ yɛn.

5 “[Beroyaa amʋ] dɔɔngwaa gyee diili.” (Acts 17:12) Naaso aninyiane dwole. Dwuu amʋ bɔ firi Tɛsaloniika bala Beroyaa “kɔ bɛtɔ mɔ ahenkole yi” kɔ bɛbula ngile fofolɛ yi atia. (Acts 17:13) Naaso, Kristofoo fofolɛ amʋ anzo ansulo. Bɛ dahuu Ngayilelɛ yi sukulu mɔ kilele bɛ kɛ bɛnya nɔhalɛ yi, na akyinnyegye biara nni nwo kɛ bɛgyinani tintin wɔ gyidi yi nu. Yɛ dahuu Nyame Asɛm yi su dwenle mɔ kɔkɔla kɛhyɛ yɛ nso dengye mmerɛ mɔ yɛyia ewieyi aninyiane amʋ.

Baebolo Nu Nhyɛso Ɔfa Da Biara Ɛzonlɛ Nwo

● Mosese Mmla ne manlɛ kɛ bɛyɛ afɔlebɔlɛ dahuu.—Ex. 29:38-42; 30:7, 8.

● Yehowa hanle hakyire Joshua kɛ ɔ genga Mmla ne na ɔ dwene ho “dawia na anwɔhe.”—Josh. 1:8, ftn.

● Yehowa hyɛɛ Yizilayil ahemfo kɛ bɛhɛlɛ Nyamenle Mmla ne bie mma bɛ nwo na bɛkenga ye dahuu.—Dit. 17:18-20.

● Nwoma hyelefo ne hanle kɛ yɛnyã anyigye wɔ Onyankopɔn mbra ne kenkan nu “nnwoma ne nnɔe.”—Dw. 1:1-3.

● Daniel yɛɛ asɔre mprɛnsa da biara.—Dan. 6:10.

● Adiyisɛm buluku ne kile kɛ nnipaku kpole ne son Nyamenle “dawia ne kɔngwɛ.”—Ady. 7:15.

na yɛ asɔreeyɛ so. Kɛ ɔte yɛ Nyamenle la ati, ɔfata kɛ yɛfa yɛ adwenle kɔ Yehowa so na yɛsɔre ye. Ɔkulo yɛ na ɔkulo kɛ yɛnya ngoane mɔɔ ɛnnwunle awieeɛ. (Dwɔɔn 3:16) Baebolo nu neazo ma yɛnwunle kɛ sɛ yɛsɔre Yehowa dahuu a ɔye ne anye. 7 Yɛkenga da biara kɛlata ne boabu afise ɔte kɛ yɛde yɛ berɛ yɛ adwuma pa. (Kenga Efesofo 5:15, 16.) Eden adwuma foforɔ kpo a yɛbɔtumi de yɛ berɛ ayɛ a ɔtɔ bo nsen kɛ da biara yɛbɔgye sima kakra asusu yɛ Sorɔ Kɛse Awurade a ɔdɔ yɛ no nsusuyɛ ho?

8 Yɛkenga da biara kyerɛwsɛm ne efĩ sɛ ɔhyɛ yɛ anwodeɛ na yɛatumi agyina da biara haw ninyeni ano. Gyisɛse hãã yɛanwʋ sɛ da biara wɔ “yɛn da haw.” (Mat. 6:34) Kɛ ɔhãã yɛanwʋ pɛpɛɛpɛ no, yɛhyia ninyeni a ɛyɛ dzen da biara. Nanso saa Baebol mfonini yi ara nu, Gyisɛse mãã yɛ anwodohosɛm sɛ Gyihova nim ninyeni a yɛrekɔ nu na ninyeni a yehia koraa. (Mat. 6:25-32) Yɛkenga kyerɛwsɛm ne a, yɛyi yɛn adwene fi yɛn adwendwen ne su kɔ mmoa a Gyihova fa mã yɛ no su. (Dw. 68:19; 1 Pet. 5:6, 7) Kɛ nhwɛso no, mmabun nuani baa bi nwunnwunee sɛ wayɛ mfomso bi a atwam na obisaa ne ho sɛ Gyihova afa akyɛ ye anaa. Ɔbɔɔ mpae bisaa Gyihova sɛ ɔmmoa ye na ɔnte nka sɛ wafa akyɛ ye ampa. Ɛno akyi no, ɔkengaa da no kyerɛwsɛm ne. Ɛyɛ Dwom 86:5 a ɛka sɛ: “Wo, O Gyihova, woyɛ papa na wo ho yɛ hare sɛ wobɛfa akyɛ; wo dɔ a enni huam no dɔɔso mã bɛn a wɔbɔ wo din nyinaa.” Ɔkãã sɛ: “Ɛntɔɔ koraa me tirim dwoo me na medaa Gyihova ase kɛse sɛ ɔmaa me awurakyekye a mehia no. Saa osuahu yi hyɛ me nkuran sɛ da biara mɛkenga kyerɛwsɛm ne.”

9 Daa kɛ daa ngilasoɛ ne taa faka yɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ boka yɛ ma yɛ “wɔ Yehowa nu awolezo” wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛkɔ ahotɔ nu ne. (Mye. 3:5, 6) Kɛ ndanwo ne, daa kɛ mɔɔ konne ne mɔɔ wɔ Russia nee Ukraine afina ne bole aze ne, ngilasoɛ ne vi Ayezaya 30:15, mɔɔ se: “Amɔ anwosesebɛ bawɔ amɔ asoo mɔɔ amɔto nu na amwala awolezo ali nu.” Ngilasoɛ kɛhyɛ yɛ mmenma mrenya nee mmaa mɔɔ bɔwɔ Ukraine ne ketekete. Kɛ ndanwo ne, adiema nrenya be mɔɔ bɛfrɛ ye Eduard hanle kɛ: “Ngilasoɛ zɔ ne yɛle kɛ ahome foforɔ be. Me nee me ye kanle bɔbɔ anwuma kɛhyia zɔ ne. Na yɛzarele Yehowa yewie ne, omaanle yɛ anwosesebɛ mɔɔ yɛhyia ye mɔɔ ma yɛte yɛ adwene nwonlomɔ.” Adiema raa be mɔɔ bɛfrɛ ye Svitlana hanle kɛ: “Ayezaya 30:15 bayɛle sonbolɛ kpale maanle yɛ. Yɛkɔle zolɛ yɛdwenlenle zolɛ na yɛsusu ye ngilasoɛ ne anwo wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ konne kpole ne kɔ zo wɔ yɛ nwo aze ne.”

10 Ye kenga daa ngilaso ne ahyia efie ɛhyɛ yɛ bɔbɔ anzɛɛ yɛ adwene den maa yɛtena pɛpɛɛpɛ. Yehia akokoduro maa yeayɛ foforɔ wɔ ye sukuufoɔ ne yɛ adwumayɔfoɔ mu, yɛka Baebolo nokorɛ kyerɛ afoforɔ, na yɛtena pɛpɛɛpɛ berɛ a yehyia anitehwee. Berɛ a wɔyɛɛ Joshua maa ɔhwɛɛ Israel man no so no, Yehowa ka kyerɛɛ no mprɛnsa kɛseɛ kɛ ɔnyɛ akokodurofoɔ na ɔnyɛ den. Sɛnea Joshua akokoduro bɛyɛ den no, Yehowa nso kyerɛɛ no kɛ ɔkenga Mmara buluku no daa. (Kenga Joshua 1:6-9.) Daa ngilaso ne ahyia kenga, bɔka yɛ daa Baebolo kenga ho no, bɔboa yɛ maa yɛayɛ den na yeatena pɛpɛɛpɛ.

daawaa tɛkisi ne, ɔma yɛ ahabaso kɛ yɛdwene kɛzi yɛbanyin kɛ honhom mu nyinla la nwo. Honhom mu nyinla le awie mɔɔ osuahu Yehowa adwenle na ɔbɔ mmɔden kɛ ɔbaye Ɔ nye na watena ase mɔɔ ɔkɔ n’ahyɛdeɛ zolɛ la.—Rom. 8:5, 6.

12 Yɛteɛteɛ daa kɛlɛkɛlɛ kyerɛwunlɛ ne anu ɔluakɛ ɔkɛboa yɛ ma yɛkɛyɛ siezie yɛ nwo maa Teokrasi adwuma he mɔɔ bɔbɔ yɛkɛyɛ kenlebie. Wɔ Yehowa ahyehyɛdeɛ ne anu, yɛhyia mrenya ne mraa mɔɔ bɛnyi wɔ sunsum nu mɔɔ bɛkɛboa adwuma ne mɔɔ ɛlɛyɛ kpole daa ne. Yɛhyia anuanmrenya mrenya ne mraalɛ mɔɔ bɛkɔyɛ akwampaefoɔ ne asɛmpakpafoɔ, ɛkɛ bɔbɔ noko wɔ Betɛl, adanlusili adwuma mɔ, ne asaase mɔɔ bɛkyeakyie mɔɔ bɛkilekile anyunlu asoeɛ he mɔ. Yɛhyia mrenya mbadwa mɔɔ bɛbɔ bɛ nwo mɔdɛn na bɛbɛlɛ bɛ nwo aze mɔɔ bɛkɔyɛ nsonnwamvuɔ, mpaninmu, ne amansin sohyɛfoɔ. Yehowa pɛ anuanmrenya mrenya mɔɔ bɛte nokorɛ na bɛtumi fa bɛ nwo to bɛ so, mrenya mɔɔ bɛte Baebolo nnyinasosɛm mɔɔ yɛfa yɛbɔ yɛ bra ase kpaa, na bɛli anyunlu wɔ ye ahyehyɛdeɛ ne anu. (Deut. 1:13-15) Mɔɔ ɛkɛdwenledwenle daa kɛlɛkɛlɛ kyerɛwunlɛ ne anwo kɛ wo daa sunsum nneyɛe ne bɔkile wo saa nnyinasosɛm he mɔ na ɔkɛboa wo ma wo kɛyɛ siezie wo nwo maa adwuma foforɛ noko.

13 Kɛkɛ a ɛbahola weanwua da biala ngɛlɛlera ne anu ɛ? Bɛtɛl abusua biala mɔɔ wɔ ewiase nyinaa bɔ bɛ gyima bo da biala kɛ bɛbɔ mpayɛ tsiawa noko ɛhyɛ a fɛ bɛfa bɛkɔ Gyihova ɛkɛ, na bɛ wie a bɛadwenlenwua da ne ngɛlɛlera ne anu. Wɔ noko ɛbahola wabɔ bo kɛ ɛbɔ mpayɛ tsiawa, kɛ ɛbisa Gyihova kɛ ɔboa wɔ ma dza ɛkɔkenga ne agye wɔ mfasoɔ. (Yem. 1:5) Afei kenga ngɛlɛlera ne nee dza bɛahɛlɛ bɛaha ho ne. Na amaa wie a, bisa wɔho kposo kɛ: 'Kɛkɛ a ngɛlɛlera ɛhe bahola ahyɛ me diedi gyinli ɛ? Kɛkɛ a kɛ medwenlenwua ho a ɔbahola yeamaa me abikye Gyihova kpalɛ ɛ? Kɛkɛ a ɔbahola yaboa me amaa meagyinli nnɛ ngyegyeleɛ ne anrwo ɛ?' Sɛ ɛnee abusua anaa anyɛnkoyɛ na ɛnee bedwenlenwua ngɛlɛlera ne anu a, nzɔne ti a ɛmfa ɛndzi ngyinlatie kɛ ɛfa ɛbɔ bɛ kokroko

14 Ndenle biala woɔ na sɛ ɛfa so a ɛkonyia afotu kɛseɛ wɔ daa ahyɛnsɛm no nu? Sɔ nzuehyɛ ndɛ nga nu ko anaa bie mɔ nlea: (1) Hwe adwenpɔ mɔ m’ɔwɔ Watchtower afotu no nu no ase na dwene ho kɔ ndɔ nu. (2) Kenga ngelɛsera mɔ b’ahwe b’ase wɔ afotu no nu no. (3) Kenga Baebolo ti m’ahyɛnsɛm no wɔ nu kɔsɔɔkɔ no. (4) Fa Jehovah Adansefo Nhwehwɛmu Akwankyerɛ anaa adesua ahyɛnsɛm mɔ m’ɔwɔ Baebolo no nu no yɛ nhwehwɛmu fa sua ninyɛne kɛseɛ. (5) Kenga adesua adesua m’afotu no firi nu no bie. Sɛ ɛde foonu anaa kɔmputa na ɛresua a, sɛ ɛkɔ afotu no awieɛ pɛ a, ɛbɔ adasua adesua no botae so a ɔkɔfa wo kɔ adesua adesua no nu tɛɛ.*

15 Berɛ a Mackenzie a ɔle adiyelɛba a ɔli mfe 19 sɔnle adwenle wɔ da biala tɛkese ne nwo ne, ɔhanle kɛ, “Sɛ migye berɛ fofolɛ kakra, sɛ ɔle emuniti nnu pɛ koraa, na mito me nwo ase mikenga na midwenle Ngɛlɛlera tɛkese ne nee nwolɛ edwɛkɛ ne nwo a, metumi mikakye edwɛkɛ ne kpalɛ.”

16 Berɛ beni na ɔsɛ kɛ musuaso muna firi ahyensianikɔ nwo? Mosi berɛ bie mɔɔ bɔkɔ bo mɛ abusua berɛnhyehyɛɛ nwo kpalɛ. Kɛ nhwɛsoɔ no, bie mɔ yɔ ye ansaana yeali anzɛɛ yeli anwuma aleɛ awie anzɛɛ ansaana yeava bɛ mma akɔ sukuu. Kɛ ahyensiani ma yɛyɔ ye so no, bɛsie Examining the Scriptures Daily bie wɔ kaba no so. Ewie bie a, berɛ mɔɔ akyikyiye bi ba no, ɔsɛ kɛ musesa mɛ berɛnhyehyɛɛ no.

17 Kɛnzɛ mɔti a ɔle kpalɛ kɛ yɛbazi dɔnhe fɔte mɔɔ yɛbaze daa ngɛlɛlera ne a? Mɔti mɔɔ yɛte ye zɔ la yɛle kɛ, sɛ anzi dɔnhe a, ebie a ɛnlikenga koraa. Anuanzɛ banyinli bie mɔɔ yeli mfe 16 mɔɔ bɛfrɛ ye John hanle kɛ: “Sɛ amfa daa ngɛlɛlera ne kengalɛ anzie w’anyunlu a, ebie a ɛnrɛyɛ ye da.” Sierra, anuanzɛ raalɛ bie mɔɔ yeli mfe 25 hanle kɛ: “Mebɔ mɔdenle kɛ menganhye mɔɔ kɛbaho nwiɔle la, mekenga ngɛlɛlera ne pɛpɛɛpɛ mɔɔ menya tɛyɛle ninyɛne mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ meyɛ la.” Na Rogelio, anuanzɛ banyinli bie mɔɔ yeli mfe 21 noko hanle kɛ: “Dɔnhe fɔte mɔɔ mezi ye me fonu ne azo la boa me ma mebɔ mɔdenle meyɛ ye daa.”

18 Ma duma a kenga be w’apangayɛ nu, na bo mmɔden sika n'ati pɛpɛɛpɛ dɛ mɔkɔhula biara. Yɛ kɔ n'a hwee angye w'adwene. Isabelle, yera bi a ɔle mfe 19, hanle kɛ: “Ka kile wo ngunmin kɛ kɔngɔhula kɔnyɛ hwee anzɛɛ kɔnnea hwee wɔ wo foonu su kɔpula kɛ wakennga duma a. Sɛ wo foonu n'a ngome a wobɔnea su tu w'adwene a, nya Da Biara Nwoma Su Nlɛlɛbɔ a bɛahyehyɛ nu no, na kennga duma a ansaana woaso wo foonu nu bɔbɔ.” Yera bi a ɔle mfe 19 a bɛfɛrɛ ye Olivia nso hanle kɛ: “Ewiase kuku a ɔboa le kɛ, wone afoforɛ, dɛ abusua anzɛɛ anyamfo kɛnnga duma a. Sɛ wunya bie a ɔbakakye wo anzɛɛ wo ne ye bɔkɔngɔ a, ɔyɛ mmerɛ kɛ wobɔkɔ su dahuu.”

BOA MMBA NGO NNE PƐHI NGOMSANELƐ NE ANU DA BIARA

19 Awoleɛ, sɛ mobɔ mmɔden kenga da biara kɛlɛta ne kile mo abusua ne da biara a, suban ɛhe baho ado mo mma ne kɛbaaba kɛyɛ mpanimfuo. Sɛ mo mma ne le nkuma na bɛngola bɛnte da biara kɛlɛta ne ase kpalɛ koraa po a, suban ɛhe bayɛ bɛ mfaso daakye. Kɛ mɔ baka fofolɛ kakraba gola nyin yɛ baka kpole ɔlua kɛ ɔgwe azule wɔ dɔteɛ ne anu da biara ne, yɛ mma nkuma ne nso bagola anyin ayɛ mpanimfuo kpalɛ wɔ sunsum nu sɛ yɛfa sima kakraba bi da biara suzu Ngɛlɛlera kɛlɛta ne ne emu edwɛkɛ ne ho a.—Kenga Filipifo 1:9, 10.

19. Nzu ti ye ɔ somi bo kɛ mgbanyinman bɛyɛ bɛ suban kɛ bɛkenga da kɔ da baabolo ngyehyɛɛ ne a? Ma nfakanyi. (Filipifo 1:9, 10)

DWEN 94 Yɛda Nyame Ase Wɔ Ye Asɛm Ne Ti

Fa Wo Gyidi No Si Fapɛm Kpole Zo

BERƐ a woni Jehovah Adansefo suaa Baebolo ne, kɛzi woyɛɛ na wonyianle awulielɛ kɛ woanyi “diedie kʋn”—ayeyeli kpanngɔkʋn biekele mɔɔ Nyamenle gye to nu ne? (Eph. 4:4-6) Asoo Jehovah mmenii ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ wonwunle ye wɔ bɛ afona ne yɛɛ ɔmaale wo hɔle bɛ nkyɛn a? Asoo bɛ suban kpa ne maa w’ani gyeli bɛ nwo kpalɛ a? Mmenii dɔɔnwo ba nɔhalɛ ne nu ɔluakɛ Nyamenle ahyehyɛdeɛ ne maa bɛte nka kɛ bɛwɔ banbɔbɔ nu na bɛkulo bɛ nɔhalɛ nu, na ɛhye yɛ nteaseɛ. Ɛlɔlɛ ne suban kpa yɛ ahyɛnse mɔɔ bɛfa bɛsi nɔhalɛ ayeyeli nwoma.—John 13:35; 1 Pet. 2:12.

Nansɔ sɛ wo nua banyin anzɛɛ wo nua baa bie yɛ wo bɔne, anzɛɛ ɔnyɛ ndɛma bie mɔ a ɔkile kɛ ɔdɔ wo ɛ? Anzɛɛ sɛ wo nua bie kɔyɛ bɔne kpole bie—a ɔkɛha ahyi bɔbɔ kɛ mɔ ɛyiaa banyin bie nwo wɔ baka bieken asafo ne mɔ mu wɔ Kɔlɛnte ne ɛ? (1 Kɔr. 5:1) Sɛ wo gyedie gyina dodo ninyɛne a awie mɔ yɛ ne nzonlɛ a ɔfa ba wo nwo zo pɛ pɛkye a, ɛnee ɔgyina fapɛm a ɛnsi pi zo. Asɛ fapɛm foforɛ a ɛsi pi wɔ hɔ a wo gyedie kɔhɔla kɛgyina zo a?

GYEDI ASERƐBO ƐDAN KEKELE

Ɛbɛkɔla awie awie wo gyidi ne tiase mpii sɛ wodi nɔhalɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ woazukoayei mɔɔ wo Bible ne anu mɔɔ woadi nwo nɔhalɛ kpole la ti. Kɛzi ɛhe bahɔla aboa wo ɛ? Bue nhwɛsoɔ fɛlɛ.

Kɛ bɛyɛle nzonlɛ ko bie la, Yesu esuafuo dɔɔnwo guale ye amvoo. Bɛbale nwoma wɔ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ɔhanle kɛ bɛli ye sonlabaka ne na bɛnwlo ye mogya ne la anwo. Bɛhanle kɛ: “Edwɛkɛ ɛhye yɛ hu.” Mɔɔ Yesu hanle la hanle bɛ ahonle kpole kpalɛ kɛ bɛgyakile ye ɛdoadi.—Jɔn 6:56-60, 66.

Ye akyi no, Yesu bizale ye asomafo no kɛ: “Bɛnso bɛkulo kɛ bɛkɔ bi, anzɛn?” Pita buale kɛ: “Awurade, hwan nkyɛn na yɛbɛkɔ? Wɔwɔ nguan a ɛnni awiee nsɛm. Yɛagye yɛadi na yɛanwu nso kɛ wɔne Nyame Krongronnii no.”—Yohane 6:67-69.

Nzu yɛ ɔmaan Peter ne asomafo mɔɔ ɛha bɔɔ nzonle wɔ bɛ mɔɔ bɛsunzule la anwo a? Bɛ gyedie gyina debie mɔɔ hyire tɛla bɛ anwondɔlɛ zoolɛ. Peter hanle kɛ: “Yɛhye yɛlie yɛli na yɛnwu kɛ wɔyɛ Nyamekpɔn ninyene kɔnwoma Ne.” Gyedima ɛhye mɔ nwu dza bɛlie bɛli ne dza ti mɔɔ bɛlie bɛli la. Bɛtete Jesus ngyelelera ne ne ye ayɛleɛ ne bɛtoto ninyene mɔɔ bɛnwu ye wɔ Ngyelelera ne anu la anwo la, bɛnwu kɛ Jesus kpaakyia a le Messiah ne. Bɛ gyedie kpole mɔɔ wɔ nɔhalɛ ɛhye anu la maan bɛ gyedie ne timle dza mɛla mɔɔ afoforɛ sunzule dza Jesus hanle la anwo la.

Duzu yɛ saa asɛm ne kile yɛ a? Duzu mɔɔ yɛze la kola nyia yɛ so tumi tra mɔɔ yɛte nka la. Nzuati? Ɔluakɛ Baebolo ne mɔɔ kilehile la—mɔɔ nɔhalɛ mɔɔ yɛzukoa ye wɔ Nyamenle duma, ye atiridie, nna agyedeɛ ne anwo la boka ho—tɛnla ɛkɛ dahuu. Yɛ adwene mɔɔ te nka la nwo tɔkɔ, mɔɔ menle yɛ anzɛɛ bɛka la ɛnsɛkye Baebolo nɔhalɛ mɔɔ wɔ nu la.

30 ƐWƐNSIN BOA FOƆ

Ɛhe ti, sɛ yɛdie Baebolo nu nɔhɔlɛ ne di kɛyɛ yɛ te a, na yɛfa nɔhɔlɛ hene yɛbɔ atikya mɔɔ Gyihova fa di dwuma ne anwo a, awie foforɛ nneyɛe ɛnrɛhola ɛnrɛma yɛnrɛto nsu nun.

Nɔhɔlɛ, anuanzɛ ne anwo dɔlɛ ne ma yɛ komyɛ tɔ yɛ yamla. Na yɛ gɔnwo mɔ diedifo ne mɔ suban kpale ne nso hyɛ yɛ anwosesebɛ. Na sɛ bie a afofolo nyɛleɛ ti suban ɛhye mɔ bɔ i bo kɛ ɔkɔyɛ kpolozo a, akee nɔhɔlɛ mɔɔ wɔ Baebolo ne anu la anwo ahotoso mɔɔ yɛwɔ la bahola amaa yɛ gyinli kpundii wɔ yɛ diedi ne anu. Ɔbabuka yɛ ma yɛanwu kɛ yɛwɔ ahyehyɛdeɛ kpale ne anu—mɔɔ ahyɛnso ngɛnlema ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛyɛ la ɛnvale ho.

Sɛ yɛbɔ mmoden suaa Baabolo ne fie a, ɔma yɛ gye nɔhalɛ biala mɔɔ yɛasua wɔ Nyamenle Wɔlɛ ne anu la di kpalɛ. Na nɔhalɛ mɔɔ yegye yeli kpalɛ zɔ la, bama yɛalie yɛali kɛ yɛanwu Nyamenle duma sonwomɔ mɔɔ sɔ n’anyi la. Sɛ ɔba ye zɔ a, yɛ gyediehye bama agyinla kanyinkanyi na awie biala anwo ngyegyeleɛ anzɛɛ ndendemu bama atumi ahwe yɛ agyinlatutu.—Dw. 16:8; nea adaka mɔɔ se “Tena Ɔkwan Foforɛ Ne Anu.”

Tena Atin Kpa Kpɔkɔ Ne Zo

Susu kɛ woaworo lɔre be mɔ ɔkɔ bika bɔ ɔte pɛn dɔɔn kɔ ebie bɔ ɔyɛ fɛ kpalɛ. Wɔ kwan tenten nhe anu, ebie a worekɔnya ngelentene dɔɔn—kpalɛ kpalɛ anyelielɛ kɛ woanyan kwan kɔ worekɔ nhe.

Naaso wɔ kwan ye azo no, biribi kɔhɔla bama wɔ abɔfu ba—ebiye a akwantufo nnyɔ bɛ ndɛkɛlɛ bobea anzɛɛ drayvani ne ndwuman duman. Naaso yei biala nzakyi nɔhalɛ ne kɛ ewo bɔsi kpa ne anu na ɔwɔ kɛ etena nu kɔkpula kɛ edwu bebia kɛ ɛkɔ ne.

Yeɛ kwan neɛ so kor Nyame ewiem fofolɛ ne anu no, yebete ninyɛne dɔɔnwo nka—titili no ɛnyeliyelɛ kɛ yɛka Nyame ahyehyɛdeɛ ne ho. Nsoso ɛyɛ a, ebiemɔ bɛyɛfo gyidini ko bie beyi yɛ ahi na amonle kɛ yɛ gyo bie kɛyɛ ninyɛne bɔnɛ a yɛboti yɛ nwo. Nso ɛhe biala nsesaa nɔhalɛ ne kɛ yewɔ bɔɔso kpakpa ne anu, kɛ mɔɔ yɛbahola yɛaha no. Yewɔ ahyehyɛdeɛ kpakpa ne anu. Sɛ yɛwɔ Baebolo nɔhalɛ ninyɛne ntiɛtiɛ ne mɔ anu nimdeɛ kpole—na yegye deeka ne bɔɔ yɛsukwankwene nɔhalɛ ninyɛne he mɔ yefri nu no to nu a—yɛbatenla kwan kpakpa ne so. Yeɛ gyidi ne batenla ase piŋŋ.

Sɛ yɛn gyidi gyina pɛpɛɛpɛ kɛkɛ wɔ sɛnea afoforo nyeyɛe ma yɛte nka no so a, ɛnde na ɛgyina fapem a ɛnhyɛ denden so

Emma yɛɛ gyima wɔ hotɛl biala dapɛn bieara nna nsa, na dɔnhwerew ngwɔnne duma da koro. Ɔbisaa ne sohyɛfo kɛ ɔpɛ da koro akyi ama w’akɔ ne mantam nhyiamu. Mmom kɛ obegye ne bisadeɛ no ato mu no, sohyɛfo no sɛkyɛɛ ne nhyehyɛe no kɔɔ nna nnum dapɛn bieara na ɔmaa ye asɛm tiawa: Gye nhyehyɛe foforɛ no to mu anaa gyae gyima no. Ɔfa awerɛhyɛmu guu Jehovah so, Emma paw kɛ ɔbegyae gyima no na w’akɔ nhyiamu no.

Mekɛ mɔɔ Emma hɔle kɛ ɔkɔfa ye gyimayelalɛ kɛlata ne yemɔ kɔma la, ɔyeli kɛ hɔtɛle ne kpaninbɔle kpole ne wɔ ɛkɛ. Mekɛ mɔɔ ɔdele asɛm ne anwo edwɛkɛ la, ɔbɔle ɛwɔkɛ kɛ ɔbadi asɛm ne anwo gyima. Noko akee, dapɛn nwiɔ anzi, bɛhanle bɛhilele Emma kɛ bɛyia ye wɔ gyima ne anu. Ɔyeli kɛ ɔnwunle kpaninbɔle kpole ne bieko, mɔɔ ye ɛya ɛha ɔluakɛ bɛamfa ye edwɛkɛ ne anyɛ gyima la. Ɔziesiele asɛm ne, na bɛmanle Emma gyimayɛlɛ mekɛ fofolɛ: dapɛn ko anu dahuu nnawɔtwe ngolo nnu, dahuu dɔnhwene nna. Ɔhɔle ye boɛ dɔɔnwo kɛ mɔɔ ye mekɛ mɔɔ limoa la ɛdeɛ ne de la; ɔboale ye kpalɛ wɔ ye sunsum nu gyima ne mɔ anu! Gyihova lile ye ahyɛdeɛ nwo gyima wɔ adenle mɔɔ bo mɔɔ ɛnee ɔze la azo.
