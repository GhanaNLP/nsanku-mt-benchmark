1926

ƐVOLƐ ƐYA KOKO YE AZE

“MEKƐ ne ɛlɛbikye mɔɔ Nyamenle bayɛ duma amaa ɔ nwo a. Nyamenle banyia bie mɔ mɔɔ bayɛ ye alasevolɛ wɔ azɛlɛ ne azo a,” zɔhane a The Watch Tower mɔɔ bapele ye wɔ January 1, 1926 la hanle a.

Mekɛ mɔɔ ɛvolɛ 1926 bɔle ɔ bo la, Gyihova—mɔɔ lua asafo ne ati, Kelaese azo la—maanle ɔ lale ali kpalɛ kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ edwɛkɛhanlɛ gyima ne yɛ debie titili mɔɔ Kilisienenli kɛva ye ngoane kɛyɛ a. Yemɔti, “akɛlɛ nɔhalɛfoɔ mɔɔ ze nrɛlɛbɛ la” vale mbuluku fofolɛ maanle Nyamenle azonvolɛ, yɛle nzenzaleɛ wɔ ayia ne mɔ anu, na ɔmaanle bɛdele Kilisienenli gyimayɛleɛ bo kpalɛ. (Mat. 24:45) Kelaese maanle bɛnyianle azua nee ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛhyia nwo na bɛava bɛayɛ edwɛkɛhanlɛ gyima ne la.

BULUKU FOFOLE NDELE BEBAYE YE NDOLE YELE ANZELENZELENLE WƆ AYIA NGƐBA NE MƆ ANU

Wɔ Ɛbɔlba ne anu, The Golden Age (mɔɔ kɛkala bɛfɛlɛ ye Awake! la) bɔle buluku fofolɛ mɔɔ bɛto ye duma kɛ Deliverance la anwo amaneɛ. Buluku ɛhye a baraye Studies in the Scriptures kɛ edwɛkɛhanlɛ gyima ne anu buluku titili mɔɔ bɛfa bɛdi gyima a. Kɛmɔ bɛnwunle kɛ nuhua hyia kpole la ati, mediema mrenyia mɔɔ wɔ Bɛtɛle la dɔle masin fofolɛ mɔɔ kɛhola kɛbɔ mbuluku 22 wɔ miti ko anu a. Baebolo Sukuluvo ne mɔ vale buluku fofolɛ ne maanle wɔ mekɛ biala mɔɔ banyia adenle la.

Ɛvolɛ dɔɔnwo la, Baebolo Sukuuma ne mɔ zukoale buluku mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Studies in the Scriptures la anu edwɛkɛ wɔ bɛ debiezukoalɛ bo. Noko ɔbɔle ɔ bo wɔ May la, bɛbɔle ɔ bo kɛ bɛsukoa edwɛkɛ mɔɔ wɔ The Watch Tower ne anu la. Duazu a vi nu rale?

“Bunker Hill, Illinois asafo [asafo] ekyi ne kɛlɛ kɛ ɔto mo atuu,” zɔhane a asafo bie hɛlɛle wɔ kɛlɛtokɛlɛ bie mɔɔ ɔhɛlɛle Adiema Rutherford la anu a. “Wɔ Molɛ nwo yɛsukoa [edwɛkɛ mɔɔ bɛhɛlɛ] mɔɔ Eku ne ɛziezielɛ mɔɔ fɛta zɔhane mekɛ ne la. Yɛte nganeɛ kɛ yɛ nye die kpole kɛ yɛ muala yɛnwu wulomunli fofolɛ ne kɛmɔ ɔbara mekɛ mɔɔ fɛta la.”

“Wɔkɛ fofolɛ ne,” anzɛɛ ndelebɛbo kpalɛ ne, boale Baebolo Sukuluvooma ne maanle bɛvale bɛ adwenle bɛziele edwɛkpa ɛhanlɛ gyima ne azo. Kɛ neazo la, edwɛkɛbikye “Subane anzɛɛ Ngyekyeleɛ—Mɔɔ Boni A?” hilale nu kɛ “subane kpalɛ ɛnyelɛ,” mɔɔ bɛvale bɛ adwenle bɛziele subane kpalɛ nee ayamgba pɛ azo la, maanle bie mɔ ambo nɔhalɛ Kilisienema nɔhalɛ gyima ne anwo mgbonda. Edwɛkɛbikye ne dule folɛ kɛ: “Ahenle mɔɔ kulo Awulae la kɛkala kɔsɔɔti . . . kɛli ye mɛla nzonlɛ. . . . Gyihova ka kɛ: ‘Bɛle me alasevolɛ kɛ mele Nyamenle.’ . . . Kɛkala Kilisienenli ne kɛnyia ɛhulolɛ kɛ ɔkɛdi alase kɛ Nyamenle sonla nwuanzanwuanza dasevolɛ.”

Edwɛkɛ fofolɛ mɔɔ se, “Afɔlebɔlɛ Nee Ɛzonlenlɛ,” si zo kɛ nɔhalɛ Kilisienema kile kɛ bɛkulo Gyihova ɔlua bɛ nɔhalɛlilɛ ɛzonlenlɛ mɔɔ bɛbaye bɛamaa ye la azo. Edwɛkɛ ne hanle hilele Baebolo Sukuuma kɔsɔɔti kɛ bɛva seseebilɛ, ahonle mualɛ, nee mɔdenrebɔlɛ bɛyɛ Awulae gyima ne na “bɛyɛ Awulae alasevolɛ na bɛbɔ ye edwɛkɛ ne nolo bɛahile ewiade maanyianu nee menli ne kɔsɔɔti.”—Aye. 43:10, 12; Mat. 24:14.

2 ƐZINZALƐ ARANE NE

Nzenzale ɛhye mɔ nyianle asafo ne mɔ azo sunsum ngyikyi bie ala. Dapɛne ekyi pɛ la, July 15, 1926, The Watch Tower yɛle kɛlata ɛhye anwo amanneɛ: “Edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ɛbue ngyikyi bie mɔ, ‘Subane Anzɛɛ Ngyekyeleɛ—Mɔɔ Boni?’ nee ‘Afɔlebɔlɛ Nee Ɛzonlenlɛ,’ maa anwosesebɛ kpole kpalɛ yɛɛ bɛkyekye mɔdenle. Yɛnrɛhola yɛnrɛgenga yɛ Towers ne mɔ ala kɛkala, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛsukoa bɛ.” Kɛlata ne ruale kɛ “dɛba ne yɛkile kɛ kɛkala bie a ɛhulolɛ kpole wɔ menli kɔsɔɔti anwo kɛ bɛbaha bɛ adwenle kpalɛ wɔ debiezukoalɛ ne mɔ azo, yɛɛ ɛhye lɛ nvasoɛ kpole kpalɛ wɔ yɛ asafo ne azo.”

NYIANU NE MA EDWÈKPAHANLÈ GYIMA NE ANWO HYIA

Mɔɔ May awieeɛ dwule la, Baebolo Sukuluvooma dɔɔnwo hyiale wɔ nyianu bie mɔɔ bɛyɛle ye wɔ London, England la abo. Adwumayɛvoma mɔɔ yɛle adwumadenle wɔ Britain la maanle ɔyɛle se kɛ bɛbanyia bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛpɛnte kɛlata bɛbamaa bɛ la, kɛmɔ ɔte la ati mediemma mɔɔ wɔ Brooklyn Bɛtɛle la yɛle gyima dɔɔnwo maanle bɛpɛntele kɛlata mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye The Standard for the People la foa 117,000 dɔɔnwo bɛhɔle nyianu ne abo.

Folɛ, May 29 ne azo, bɛvale ngɛlata nzinli ne mɔ bɛlile gyima kpalɛ. Nyianu ngyehyɛleɛ ne mɔɔ bɛyɛle ye kenle ne la hanle kɛ: “Bɛto ɛsalɛ bɛfɛlɛ awozonle biala mɔɔ ɛra nyianu ne bie la kɛ ɔlie edwɛkɛ mɔɔ fale Belemgbunli ne nee Belemgbunlililɛ ne anwo la ɔdo gua. Dwulɛ Alexandra Palace ne ndɛndɛ kɛmɔ ɛbahola la, na kɔyɛ gyima woamaa Ɛzonlenlɛ Gyimayɛleɛ ne.”

Bɛhyehyɛle mbuluku dɔɔnwo mɔɔ bo 100,000. July 15, 1926, The Watch Tower bɔle amanieɛ kɛ: “Mediemma ne mɔ anye liele kpalɛ. Bɛdele nganeɛ kɛ bɛyɛ bɛ mɔdenlebɔlɛ biala bɛlie Gyihova mɛla ne bɛdo nu: ‘Bɛle me alasevolɛ kɛ mele Nyamenle.’ ”

Alehyenlɛ ne mɔɔ doalɛ zo la, wɔ Royal Albert Hall ne anu, Adiema Rutherford dendale edwɛkɛtile mɔɔ se “Deɛmɔti Ewiade Tumililɛma Ɛlɛseso la—Ayezile Ne” la azo. Mekɛ mɔɔ bɛbuale anlenkɛ ne mɔ la, yeangyɛ biefee na menli 13,000 rale sua kpole ne anu. Menli dɔoonwo mɔɔ bɛannyia ɛleka bɛande la dendenle aze bɛhakyile bɛ nye bɛdiele dwumadli ne wɔ magnɛte mɔɔ bɛsisile bɛ wɔ abɔnten la azo.

Wɔ Adiema Rutherford ɛdendɛlɛ ne anu, ɔhilehyele kɛ ewiade tumivolɛma ne mɔ ɛwɔlɛ vi kɛ bɛkpole Gyihova nee ye Belemgbunlililɛ ne la. Adiema Rutherford hɔle zo dendɛle tianle Maanle Maanle Kɔngɛleese ne na ɔbɔle amanieɛ kɛ ɔnrɛhɔ zo ɔnrɛdɛnla ɛkɛ bieko.

Bɛhwenle bɛ siazo ne mɔ anu vi kenle ko kɔdwu kenle nwiɔ, yɛɛ ɛnee ɔwɔ kɛ bɛdi dɔnehwele 100 siane biala wɔ daselɛlilɛ gyima ne anu.

Eza ninyɛne kɛlata ne mɔ liele dole nu kɛ bɛbaye Adiema Rutherford edwɛkɛ mɔɔ se “Earth’s Greatest Conflict Near” la bɛagua gua zo. Noko akee, mekɛ ekyi mɔɔ ɛha la, ninyɛne kɛlata kpole dɔɔnwo kpole kɛ bɛbaye nyianu ne bɛagua gua zo.

SUA MƆƆ WƆ 117 ADAMS STREET LA

Kɛmɔ menli dɔɔnwo kpondɛle mbuluku la ati, mediema mrenyia mɔɔ di gyima la wɔ Brooklyn la nwunle kɛ ɔhyia kɛ bɛtrɛ bɛ mbulukupɛlɛle gyima ne anu. Ɛvolɛ dɔɔnwo anu, bɛbɔle azua mɔɔ wɔ 18 Concord Street la fɛlɛ, noko sua ɛhye angyia kɛ ɔbalie kɛtrɛlɛ mɔɔ hyia la. Mediema ne mɔ dɔle azɛlɛ bie wɔ Adams Street, mɔɔ bikye sua mɔɔ ɛnee wɔ ɛkɛ la kpalɛ. Bɛbɔle ɔ bo kɛ bɛsi sementi sua mɔɔ lɛ ɛkpɔlɛ mɔtwɛ la, mɔɔ hɔle zolɛ fane nwiɔ wɔ kɛzi ɛnee sua ne anu la anwo.

Sua ne mɔɔ wɔ 117 Adams Street la, bɛwulale nu wɔ 1927, na ɛhye maanle bɛnyianle anyuhɔle kpole kpalɛ. Ɛvolɛ mɔɔ li ɛnreze la maa anyuhɔle kpole rale na bɛhɔle zo bɛdelale ahyehyɛdeɛ ne anwo, mɔɔ sieziele Nyamenle menli ne kɛ bɛfɛta kɛ bɛlie duma ne mɔɔ le Gyihova Alasevolɛ la bɛdo nu a.

SUZU kɛzi ɛ nye gbalie kɛ ɛbagyinla Nyevile Kɔkɔlɛ ne anloa na wɔanwu kɛzi Gyihova hyehyɛle azule ne anu, maanle ye menli ne adenle kɛ bɛnriandi bɛvi Yigyibiti sɔlɔma ne anwo la anwo. Eza dwenle anyelielɛ mɔɔ ɛbanyia ye kɛ ɛbanwu kɛ Gyisɛse ɛlɛyɛ menli mɔɔ ɛnde kpɔkɛ la ayile yɛɛ ɔlɛdwazo mɔwuamɔ bɔbɔ la anwo nea! Ɛhye mɔ le nwanwane ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛhɛlɛ wɔ Baebolo ne anu la ekyi bie ala. Ahenle biala mɔɔ lɛ ahonle kpalɛ mɔɔ nwunle nwanwane ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ la adenle biala ɛnle ɛkɛ mɔɔ ɔbali nwolɛ adwenle kɛ Nyamenle a wule ninyɛne zɔhane mɔ anzi a. 2 Bɛbahola bɛahilehile nwanwane debie abo kɛ ɔle debie mɔɔ sonla ɛntowolo anrɛɛ ɔngola ɔnde ɔ bo kpalɛ bɔbɔ. Ɔle debie mɔɔ Gyihova dua ye tumi ne azo yɛ a. Wɔ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ yɛbazukoa ye la anu, yɛbanwu nwanwane ninyɛne nsa mɔɔ Gyihova ɛlɛyɛ ye wɔ ye ahyehyɛdeɛ ne anu ɛnɛ la. Yɛbahola yɛavɛlɛ bɛ nwanwane ninyɛne ɔluakɛ Gyihova angome kɛzi ɔkɛyɛ mɔɔ bɛbasi a. Wɔ ko biala anu, yɛbanwu kɛzi yɛbahola yɛayɛ Gyihova “gɔnwo mɔ gyimayɛvɔma” wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɔlɛyɛ nwanwane ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ la.—Kenga 1 Kɔlentema 3:9.

9 Ngapezonli Maeka hanle nwanwane ninyɛne bie mɔɔ yɛnwu ye wɔ yɛ mekɛ ye anu la anwo edwɛkɛ doanle—Nyamenle menli koyɛlɛ kpole ne. Wɔ sunsum ne adehilelɛ bo, Maeka hanle mrenya nee mraalɛ mɔɔ Gyihova babea bɛ nwo aze na bɛadɛnla ko la anwo edwɛkɛ. (Kenga Maeka 2:12.) Wɔ ngapezonli zɔhane mɔɔ rale nuhua la anu, Nyamenle menli mɔɔ wɔ ɛkɛ ne ɛnɛ la de ko ɔnva nwo subane ngakyile mɔɔ bɛlɛ anzɛɛ leka mɔɔ bɛvi la. Bɛnee edwɛndolɛnli Devidi yɛ adwenle kɔ, mɔɔ hɛlɛle kɛ: “Nea! Kɛzi ɔle kpalɛ na ɔyɛ fɛ kɛ adiema mrenya badɛnla ko la!”—Edw. 133:1.

10 Ɛnɛ, yɛ koyɛlɛ ne da ali wɔ ndenle dɔɔnwo azo. Kɛ neazo la, ezia ebie biala mɔɔ yɛde la, yɛnyia sunsum nu ninyɛne ko ne ala wɔ debiezukoalɛ, maangyebakyi nyianu, yɛɛ maanle maanle nyianu bo. Alasevolɛ mɔɔ vi ɛleka ngakyile yɛɛ maanle ngakyile nu la bɔ nu yɛ gyima wɔ ɛleka mɔɔ bɛsi azua la yɛɛ Bɛtɛle azua nu wɔ ewiade amuala. Yɛ agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne anu mia.

Amanebɛnle, ezukoa gyinlabelɛ, anzɛɛ maanyɛlɛ nwo diedi ɛmgbakye yɛ nu. Dwenle nwo nea: Ɛleka biala mɔɔ yɛbahɔ la, yɛ abusua mɔɔ lɛ ɛlɔlɛ la wɔ ɛkɛ ne maa yɛ—wɔ nziezielɛ nu kɛ bɛbamaa yɛ sunsum nu nee nwonane nu moa. Wɔ tete ne mɔ amuala anu, menli dɔɔnwo mɔɔ vi maanle nee amanebɛnle dɔɔnwo anu la ɛtɛyɛle kɔnwonle wɔ adwenle nee nyɛleɛ nu zɛhae ɛlɛ.

11 Yɛ koyɛlɛ ne yɛ azibɛnwo ɔluakɛ ɔle ngakyile kpalɛ wɔ ewiade ne mɔɔ ɛkpɔkɛkpɔkɛ la anwo. Wɔ ewiade amuala, menli ɛhyehyɛ nu kpole. Edwɛkɛbɔlɛ ne yɛ tɛkɛɛ wɔ konle, atuadelɛ, basabasayɛlɛ, atuadelɛ nee konle nwo—ɔda mgbɔlɔgansilɛ nee konle ali kpalɛ. Kɛ mɔɔ Baebolo ne hɛlɛle la, menli lɛ “bɛnle nɔhalɛvooma, . . . bɛmbu adwelielilɛ, mgbɔsobɔvolɛma, bɛnlɛ bɛ nwo zo tumi, atisesebɛma, bɛngulo mɔɔ le kpalɛ la, bɛyie bɛ gɔnwo mɔ bɛma, bɛ ti yɛ se, bɛmemaa bɛ nwo zo.” (2 Tim. 3:2-4) Ɔnva nwo kɛ “ewiade amuala la amumuyɛnli ne” nee ye sunsum ɛtane ne mɔ tumi bo la, yɛkɔ zo yɛbɔ ebiye kɛ menli mɔɔ bɛlɛ koyɛlɛ la. (1 Dwɔn 5:19; Yek. 12:9) Nɔhalɛ nu, ɛhye yɛ azibɛnwo kpalɛ!

12 Yɛbahola yɛaboa yɛamaa kɔyɛlɛ ayɛ kpole wɔ Gyihova ahyehyɛdeɛ ne anu. Kɛzi yɛbahola yɛayɛ zɔ ɛ? Ɔlua Gyihova ngilehilelɛ ne mɔ mɔɔ yɛbadie yɛamaa ɔadwu yɛ ahonle nu na yɛali zo la azo. Ye Edwɛkɛ ne anu ngilehilelɛ boa yɛ maa yɛnyia abotane, yɛfa yɛkyɛ, yɛkpo akɛsakɛsa yɛɛ yɛyɛ atiakunlukɛnlɛma kile awie mɔ. (Ɛfɛ. 4:2, 31, 32) Mekɛ biala mɔɔ yɛbabu yɛ nye yɛagua yɛ mediema diedima sinlidɔlɛ zo, mekɛ biala mɔɔ yɛbahye yɛ nwo yɛazi awie mɔ anwo edwɛkɛ mɔɔ yɛkɛha la azo, na mekɛ biala mɔɔ yɛbava yɛakyɛ la—ɛnee yɛlɛbɔ nu yɛayɛ gyima yɛagye Gyihova nwo mekɛ mɔɔ ɔlɛyɛ nwanwane ninyɛne kɛkala wɔ ye ahyehyɛdeɛ ne anu la.

13 Gyisɛse hanle kɛ ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ ye ɛdoavolɛma lɛ maa bɛ nwo ngoko la a bɛbava bɛanwu bɛ a. (Kenga Dwɔn 13:35.) Ɛlɔlɛ zɛhae a ɛnee anyelazo wɔ Kilisienlema mɔɔ dɛnlanle aze wɔ ɛvoya mɔɔ limoa la ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nu a. Kɛ neazo la, bɛvale bɛ ninyɛne bɛkyɛle bɛdabɛ mɔɔ ɛnee bɛlɛ ngyianle la. (Gyi. 2:44, 45) Bɛboale bɛdabɛ mɔɔ ɛnee bɛlɛkpɔ bɛ la. (Gyi. 12:5) Bɛnleanle anyonhuanle nee kunvolema. (Gye. 1:27) Yɛɛ ɔnva nwo bɛ ngomekye bɛ ehyia la, bɛboale Kilisienlema gyɛne. (2 Kɔl. 8:1-4) Wɔ ɛvoya mɔɔ tɔ zo nwiɔ la anu, kɛlɛvo Tertullian hanle mɔɔ awie mɔ hanle vile tete Kilisienlema nwo la: “Nea . . . kɛzi bɛkulo bɛ nwo ngoko [yɛɛ] kɛzi bɛziezie bɛ nwo kɛ bɛbawu bɛamaa bɛ nwo bɔbɔ la.”

Yɛda ɛlɔlɛ zɔhane yɛali noko yɛmaa menli mɔɔ bɛnle asafo ne anu la ɔlua yɛ edwɛkɛhanlɛ gyima ne azo. Amaa yɛaboa gyima zɔhane la, yɛye ezukoa yɛto nu. (Luku 22:28; Dwɔn 15:15) Agɔnwolɛ kpalɛ a le ahenle mɔɔ bikye wɔ tɛ mekɛ mɔɔ debie biala kɔ boɛ la ala a, na eza bikye wɔ wɔ “amaneɛnwunlɛ mekɛ nu.” (Mrɛ. 17:17) Nolɛvo 4:9-12* da ye ali kɛ agɔnwolɛma kpalɛ bahola amia yɛ na bɛakyekye yɛ rɛle. Noko wɔ Baebolo ngyɛnu kɔsɔɔti ɛhye anu, Gyisɛse maa yɛ anwodoɔsow kɛ Gyihova ze mɔɔ yɛlɛyia ye la kpalɛ yɛɛ mɔɔ yɛhyia nwo la. (Mat. 6:25-32) Saa yɛsukoa ngɛlɛlera ne a, yɛyi yɛ adwenle wɔ yɛ adwenleadwenle ne mɔ azo yɛfa yɛto moalɛ mɔɔ Gyihova fa maa la azo. (Edw. 68:19; 1 Pita 5:6, 7) Kɛ neazo la, adiema raalɛ kakula bie dele nganeɛ kɛ ɔlɛ fɔlea ɔluakɛ ɔyɛle nvonleɛ bie wɔ tete ne yɛɛ ɔnwunle kɛ asoo Gyihova ɛva ɛhyɛ ye. Wɔ asɔneyɛlɛ nu, ɔbikanle kɛ Gyihova ɛboa ye na ɔlie ye ali kɛ yeva yɛhyɛ ye. Akee ɔgengale kenle ne ngɛlɛlera ne. Ɛnee ɔle Edwɛndolɛ 86:5, mɔɔ ka kɛ: “O Gyihova, ɛle kpalɛ yɛɛ ɛziezie wɔ nwo kɛ ɛfa nvonleɛ wɔahyɛ; wɔ nɔhalɛlile ɛlɔlɛ ne zonle kpalɛ maa bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛsɔne wɔ duma ne la kɔsɔɔti.” Ɔhanle kɛ: “Ndɛndɛ ne ala menyianle anzondwole yɛɛ meyɛle Gyihova mo kpole kɛ ɔmanle me arɛlekyekyelɛ mɔɔ mehyia nwo la. Ngalɛ nu edwɛkɛ zɔhane maa me anwosesebɛ kɛ mensukoa ngɛlɛlera ne kenle biala.”

9 Kenle ko ngɛlɛlera ne taa kilehile yɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ boka yɛ ma yɛ“fa yɛ nwo yɛto Gyihova anwo zo” wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛkɛyia ngoane nu ngyegyelɛ la. (Mrɛ. 3:5, 6) Kɛ neazo la, kenle mɔɔ konle mɔɔ wɔ Russia nee Ukraine avinli la bɔle ɔ bo la, ɛnee ngɛlɛlera ne vi Ayezaya 30:15, mɔɔ ka kɛ: “Bɛ anwosesebɛ kɛvi bɛ nwo mɔɔ bɛbamaa yeado bɛ nu nee diedi mɔɔ bɛbanyia la anu.” Zɔhane ngɛlɛlera ne hanle yɛ mediema mrenyia nee mraalɛ mɔɔ wɔ Ukraine la arɛle. Kɛ neazo la, adiema nrenyia bie mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Eduard la hanle kɛ: “Ngɛlɛlera zɔhane hanle yɛ ahonle kpole. Me nee me ye gualɛ nwolɛ dɛba kenle ne alehyenlɛ nu. Yɛzɛlɛle yɛwiele la, ɔmaanle yɛ anwosesebɛ mɔɔ yɛhyia nwo na yɛahola yɛagyinla yɛ adwenle mɔɔ hanle la anloa.” Adiema raalɛ bie mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Svitlana la hanle kɛ: “Ayezaya 30:15 rayɛle debie mɔɔ sonle bolɛ kpole kpalɛ maa yɛ. Ɛnee yɛkɔ zo yɛkakye na yɛdwenledwenle nwolɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɛnee konle kpole ne ɛlɛkɔ zo wɔ yɛ nwo lɔ la.”

10 Yɛsukoa dahuu ngɛlɛlera ne ɔluakɛ ɔmaa yɛ kpɔkɛdelɛ kɛ yɛbali nɔhalɛ la anu yɛ se. Ɔhyia kɛ yɛnyia akɛnrasesebɛ amaa yɛayɛ ngakyile yɛavi yɛ gɔnwo mɔ sukuluma nee yɛ gɔnwo mɔ gyimayɛvuma anwo, yɛaha Baebolo ne anu nɔhalɛ ne yɛahile awie mɔ, yɛɛ yɛali nɔhalɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛyɛ yɛ kpɔdekpɔde la. Mɔɔ bɛkpa Gyehyuwa kɛ Yizilayɛ maanwo ne azo neavolɛ la, Gyihova hanle hilele ye fane nsa kɛ ɔnyia akɛnrasesebɛ na ɔyɛ kpole. Amaa Gyehyuwa akɛnrasesebɛ ne anu ayɛ se la, Gyihova eza hilele ye kɛ ɔgenga Mɛla buluku ne dahuu. (Kenga Gyehyuwa 1:6-9.) Dahuu ngɛlɛlera ne mɔɔ yɛbagenga la, yɛɛ dahuu Baebolo ɛgengalɛ ne mɔɔ yɛbava yɛaboka nwo la, baboa yɛ amaa yɛayɛ kpole na yɛali nɔhalɛ.

11 Yɛsuzu kenle biala ngɛlɛlera ne anwo ɔluakɛ ɔboa yɛ maana yɛnyi sunsum nu. Pɔɔlo hanle hilele kpavɔle Timote kɛ ɔhɔ zo ɔnyi wɔ mgbɔkka mɔɔ le kɛ ɛdendɛlɛ, ɛbɛlabɔlɛ, ɛlɔlɛ, diedi, nee ninyɛne mɔɔ nwo te la anu. (1 Tim. 4:12, 15) Saa yɛsuzu

7-8. Nzonlehilehilelɛ fofolɛ boni a yɛlɛ kɛ yɛbabɔ mɔdenle yɛadwenledwenle kenle ko biala ngɛlɛlera ne anwo a? (Ɛfɛsɛsembunli 5:15, 16)

9. Kɛzi kenle ko ngyekyeleɛ ne boale yɛ mediema mrenyia nee mraalɛ mɔɔ wɔ Ukraine la ɛ?

10. Duzu ati a yɛhyia akɛnrasesebɛ a, na kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na yɛamaa yɛ akɛnrasesebɛ ne ayɛ kpole e? (Gyɔhyuwa 1:6-9) (Nea nvoninli ne mɔ noko.)

11. Kɛzi ngɛlɛlera ne mɔɔ yɛbadwenledwenle nwolye la baboa yɛ amaa yɛanyia sunsum nu anyuhɔlɛ ɛ?

26 ƐZINZANLƐ ARANE NE

Yɛdua Ngɛlɛlera ne mɔ dahuu ɛgengalɛ zo yɛmaa yɛ akɛnrasesebɛ yɛ kpole

(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 10)

dahuu kɛlata ne, ɔmaa yɛnyia nwolɛ adenle kɛ yɛbadwenledwenle kɛzi yɛbahola yɛanyi kɛ sunsum nu sonla la anwo. Sunsum nu sonla a le ahenle mɔɔ sukoa Gyihova adwenle na ɔbɔ mɔdenle kɛ ɔsɔ ɔ nye na yeabɔ ye ɛbɛla kɛmɔ Ye ngyinlazo ne mɔ kile la.—Wlo. 8:5, 6.

12 Yɛsuzu kɛlata ne mɔɔ yɛkenga ye dahuu la anwo ɔluakɛ ɔbaboa yɛ yeamaa yɛaziezie yɛ nwo yɛamaa teokelase gyima mɔɔ yɛbanyia ye kenlebie la. Wɔ Gyihova ahyehyɛdeɛ ne anu, yɛhyia mrenya nee mraalɛ mɔɔ bɛnli sunsum nu la anwo mɔɔ baboa wɔ gyima ne mɔɔ ɛlɛkɔ zo anyunlu dahuu la anu. Yɛhyia mediema mrenya nee mraalɛ mɔɔ babola ayɛ adekpakyelɛma yɛɛ edwɛkpakɛlɛma kɛ mɔɔ bɛbayɛ gyima wɔ Bɛtɛle, sua si gyima nu, yɛɛ ɛleka mɔɔ bɛkile edwɛkɛ bo la. Yɛhyia mgbavunli mɔɔ lɛ anwosesebɛ yɛɛ bɛbɛlɛ bɛ nwo aze la mɔɔ babola azonle kɛ azonlenyi, mgbanyima, yɛɛ maangyebakyi zo neavoma. Gyihova kpondɛ mediema mrenya mɔɔ bɛdi nɔhalɛ na bɛkola bɛfa bɛ nwo bɛto bɛ nwo zo, mrenya mɔɔ bɛte Baebolo ngyinlazo bo na bɛfa bɛbɔ bɛ bra, amaa bɛali aduane wɔ ye ahyehyɛdeɛ ne anu. (Dɛt. 1:13-15) Kɛ ɛbasuzu kɛlata ne anwo dahuu kɛ ɛ sunsum nu ninyɛne ne mɔ anu ko la bamaa wɔazukoa zɔhane ngyinlazo ne mɔ na yeaboa yeamaa wɔaziezie wɔ nwo wɔamaa gyima gyɛne mɔɔ bɛbava bɛawula wɔ sa la.

KƐZI ƐBAHOLA YEANWU EDWƐKƐ NE Ɛ?

13 Kɛzi ɛbahola wɔanwu dahuu ngɛlɛlera ne ɛ? Bɛtɛle mbusua mɔɔ wɔ ewiade amuala la fa asɔneyɛlɛ sikalɛ noko awozonle gua zolɛ bɔ bɛ gyima bo dahuu, na akee bɛsuzu kenle ne ngɛlɛlera ne anwo. Ɔwɔ kɛ ɛdaye noko ɛkpa kɛ ɛbava asɔneyɛlɛ sikalɛ wɔabɔ ɔ bo, na wɔazelɛ Gyihova kɛ ɔboa wɔ ɔmaa mvasoɛ mɔɔ wɔ mɔɔ ɛkɛgenga la azo kɛra wɔ nwo zo. (Gye. 1:5) Akee kenga ngɛlɛlera ne nee ɛdendɛlɛ ne. Ɛwie a, biza wɔ nwo kpuya kɛ: 'Kɛzi ngɛlɛlera ɛhye bahola amia me diedi ɛ? Kɛzi nwolɛ dwenledwenlenlɛ bamaa meabikye Gyihova ɛ? Kɛzi ɔbahola yeaboa me yeamaa meagyinla ɛnɛ ngyegyelɛ ngyegyelɛ ne mɔ anwo ɛ?' Saa ɛlɛsuzu ngɛlɛlera ne anwo ɛ nee abusua anzɛɛ agɔnwolɛma a, kɛmɔti a ɛnva ɛnli gyima kɛ agyakɛlilɛ kpalɛ mɔɔ maa anwosesebɛ

12. Mekɛ boni a bɛhyia mrenyia nee mraalɛ mɔɔ bɛ nye lie sunsum nu la anwo a, na duzu a bahola aboa yɛ amaa yɛaziezie yɛ nwo yɛamaa nzonlɛ ne mɔ a?

13. Adenle ko boni a yɛbahola yɛalua zo yɛadwenle edwɛkɛ ne anwo a?

BƆLBƆLE 2026 27

Duzu a baboa wɔ na wɔadwe wɔ nwo wɔvi ninyɛne mɔɔ bamaa wɔ adwenle adenrɛ la anwo mekɛ mɔɔ ɛlɛkenga kenle ko biala ngɛlɛlera ne la?

(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 18)

adwelielilɛ? (Wlo. 1:12) Bie mɔ nwu kɛ ɔboa saa bɛbɔ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ wɔ ngyehyɛnu ne anu la anloa ezinra nu wɔ edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ nsa anzɛɛ nna anu amaa bɛahola bɛakakye ye wɔ kenle ne amuala anu a.

14 Adenle mɔɔ ɛbahola wɔalua zo wɔanyia dɛba ne ngyɛnu ne azo nvasoɛ kpole la bie a le boni? Bɔ mɔdenle fa adwenyelɛye mɔɔ dooa zo la ko anzɛɛ mɔɔ bo zɔhane la yɛ gyima: (1) Hwe adwenlikpole mɔɔ wɔ *Ɛzinzalɛ Arane* ne edwɛkɛ ne anu la abo na dwenledwenle nwolɛ. (2) Kenga ngɛlɛlera mɔɔ bɛhwe bɛ adwele bɛhɔ zo wɔ edwɛkɛ ne anu la. (3) Kenga Baebolo tile mɔɔ ngyɛnu ne wɔ nu la amuala. (4) Nea *Kpondɛwolɛ Buluku Mɔɔ Gyihova Alasevolɛ Kpondɛ Ninyɛne Nu La* anzɛɛ nvonkayɛlɛ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ wɔ Baebolo ne anu la anu amaa wɔanwu nwolɛ edwɛkɛ dɔɔnwo. (5) Kenga ɛzukoalɛ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ bɛyele edwɛkɛ ne bɛvile nu la foa bie mɔ. Saa ɛlɛfa ɛlɛtɔlɔneke buluku wɔali gyima a, ɛbahola wɔamia ngyinlazo mɔɔ wɔ edwɛkɛ ne awieleɛ la azo wɔahɔ ɛzukoalɛ edwɛkɛ ne anu.*

15 Mɔɔ fale mekɛ mɔɔ adiema raalɛ Mackenzie mɔɔ ɛli ɛvolɛ 19 gyinla zo suzu kenle ko ngɛlɛlera ne anwo la anwo la, ɔhanle kɛ, “Saa megyinla ekyi, bɔbɔ mitutu minyɛleɛ kɛyɛ miti nnu bieko, na megua aze mekenga ngɛlɛlera ne nee edwɛkɛ mɔɔ bɛha bɛguazo la na medwenledwenle nwo a, metee kɛ mebahola meahakye edwɛkɛ ne kpalɛ.”

16 Mekɛ boni a ɔwɔ kɛ bɛsuzu ngyɛnu ne anwo a? Kpa mekɛ mɔɔ kɛyɛ kpalɛ kɛmaa wɔ abusua ne ngyehyɛleɛ la. Kɛ neazo la, bie mɔ yɛ ye kolaa na bɛali anzɛɛ bɛli ngilenu bɛwie anzɛɛ kolaa na bɛava bɛ mra bɛahɔ sukulu. Kɛ kakyelɛ kɛ bɛyɛ zɔ la, bɛfa *Neɛnlea Ngɛlɛlera Ne Anu Dahuu* buluku ne bɛsie bɛ kponle ne azo. Nɔhalɛ nu, saa ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛ nye enle kɛ ɔkɛzi la si a, ɔbahia kɛ ɛsakyi wɔ ngyehyɛleɛ ne bie.

17 Nzuati a ɔle kpalɛ kɛ ɛbazie mekɛ titili kɛ ɛkengadɛ ngyɛnu ne a? Ɔluakɛ saa wɔanla a, bie a ɛnrɛgenga ngyɛnu ne fee. Adiema nrenyia bie mɔɔ le ɛvolɛ 16 mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye John la hanle kɛ: “Saa wammaa ngyɛnu ne ɛgengalɛ anla ɛ nzonle zo a, bie a ɛnrɛyɛ fee.” Sierra, adiema raalɛ bie mɔɔ le ɛvolɛ 25 la hanle kɛ: “Mebɔ mɔdenle kɛ mekenga ngyɛnu ne ngɛba nwonlomɔ kolaa na kenle ne azo gyima bɔ ɔ bo.” Yɛɛ Rogelio, adiema nrenyia bie mɔɔ le ɛvolɛ 21 la hanle kɛ: “Mekɛ mɔɔ mezie wɔ me fonu ne azo la boa me maa meyɛ ye dahuu.”

* Ɛleka mɔɔ bɛvale edwɛkɛ ne bɛvile la kile ɛvolɛ nee siane mɔɔ bɛyele Ɛnzanrenlɛ Arane ne, mukelɛ nwo nɔmba(mɔ), nee ɛdendɛkpunli nwo nɔmba(mɔ) mɔɔ bɛvale nzonlɛyɛlɛ ne mɔ bɛvile nu la.

14-15. Duzu bieko a ɔwɔ kɛ yɛsuzu nwo a, yɛɛ duzu a bahola aboa yɛ amaa yɛahakye ngyekyenu ne a?

16. Mekɛ boni a ɔwɔ kɛ ɛdwenledwenle edwɛkɛ ne anwo a?

17-18. Duzu ati a ɔle kpalɛ kɛ yɛbayɛ ngyehyɛleɛ kɛ yɛbakenga ngɛlɛlera ne dahuu wɔ mekɛ fɔɔnwo bie anu a, yɛɛ duzu a baboa yɛ amaa yɛahɔ zo yɛayɛ zɔ a? (Nea nvoninli ne mɔ noko.)

28 ƐZINLÎ NE

18 Maa ngyɛnu ne ɛgengalɛ ɛyɛ wɔ ngyehyɛleɛ ne foa ko, na bɔ mɔdenne sɔ nu kpalɛ kɛ mɔɔ ɛbahola la. Ɛmmaa biribiara ɛntwe wɔ adwenle. Isabelle, adiema raalɛ ko mɔɔ ɛli ɛvolɛ 19 la bɔle adwenle kɛ: “Ka kile wɔ nwo kɛ ɛnrɛyɛ debie biala anzɛɛ ɛnrɛnea debie biala wɔ wɔ s Phone ne azo kɔkpula kɛ ɛbagenga ngyɛnu ne. Saa Phone ne mɔɔ ɛbanea la ala bɔbɔ twe wɔ adwenle a, fa *Kɛlɛtokɛ Lulɛ Mɔɔ Ɛbagenga Ngɛlɛlera Ne Dahuu* ne mɔɔ bɛyɛ ye kɛlata azo la na kenga ngyɛnu ne kolaa na wɔazɔ wɔ Phone ne azo.” Adiema raalɛ bie mɔɔ ɛli ɛvolɛ 19 mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Olivia la hanle kɛ: “Debie ko mɔɔ boa la a le kɛ ɛ nee awie mɔ, kɛ mɔɔ le wɔ abusua anzɛɛ wɔ gɔnwo mɔ la bagenga ngyɛnu ne. Saa ɛlɛ awie mɔɔ bakae wɔ anzɛɛ ɛ nee ye bagenga a, ɔnyɛ se kɛ ɛbayɛ ye dahuu.”

BOA NGOANE MA MENLE NE AMỌ BA NE AYE NGELENLE NE AMỌ AHYEHYE NIKYE DUUDUU

19 Awovolɛ, saa bɛnyia subane kɛ dahuu bɛ nee bɛ abusua ne babɔ nu agenga dahuu Ngɛlɛlera ne a, bie a subane zɔhane bahɔ zo adɛnla bɛ mra ne mɔ anwo kɛpɛ bie mɔɔ bɛanyinli la. Ɔnva nwo kɛ bɛ mra ne mɔ le ngakula na bɛnrɛha ndelebɛbo kɔsauti mɔɔ wɔ kenle ko biala ngɛlɛlera ne anu la, debie mɔɔ bɛyɛ ye dahuu la banyia bɛ nwo zo nvasoɛ kenlebie. Kɛ mɔɔ baka mbuleka nyianla nzule fi azɛlɛ ne anu kenle biala na bɛyɛ mbaka mgpole la, zɔhane ala a yɛbahola yɛaboa yɛ ngakula ne mɔ bamaa bɛanyinli bɛayɛ sunsum nu mrenya nee mraalɛ mɔɔ bɛgyinla kpundii la, saa yɛdua mekɛ ekyi biala anu kenle biala yɛsuzu ngɛlɛlera ne nee ɛdendɛlɛ ne anwo a.—Kenga Felepaema 1:9, 10.

20 Gyisɛse hanle kɛ: “Tɛ kponkpo ngome a sonla dua zo tɛnla asee a, na emomu edwɛkɛ mɔɔ vi Gyihova anloa la amuala a ɔdua zo ɔtɛnla asee a.” (Mat. 4:4) Ɔnrɛyɛ kɛ yɛbamaa kenle mumwa pɛlɛ yɛ anwo zo mɔɔ yɛtɛlinle nwonane afoa nu aleɛ a. Ɛnee kɛzi ɔhyia kpalɛ kɛ yɛbanyia sunsum nu aleɛ mɔɔ maa ngoane la ɔlua Ngɛlɛlera ne mɔ mɔɔ yɛbaneɛnlea nu dahuu la azo la!

19. Duzu ati a ɔhyia kɛ ngakula nyian subye kɛ bɛbabɔ nu bɛagenga dahuu ngɛlɛlera ne a? Maa neazo. (Felepaema 1:9, 10)

20. Kɛzi sunsum nu aleɛ kɛlɛ kɛzi nwolɛ hyia la de ɛ?

KƐZI ƐBABUA YE Ɛ?

■ Nzu atiakunlukɛlɛ nti a ɔwɔ kɛ yɛdwenledwenle kenle ko ngɛlɛlera ne anwo a?

■ Kɛzi yɛbahola yɛanyia dahuu ngɛlɛlera ne anu anyunlunyia kpalɛ ɛ?

■ Nzuzule boni mɔ a wɔ edwɛkɛ ɛhye anu a baboa wɔ amaa wɔadwenledwenle ngɛlɛlera ne anwo dahuu a?

EDWENE 94 Yɛkɛlata Nyamenle Edwɛkɛ Ne Anwo

Maa Wɔ Diedi Ɛgyinla Fane Kpundii Zo

MEKƐ mɔɔ ɛ nee Gyihova Alasevolɛ zukoale Baebolo ne la, duzu a maanle ɛliele ɛlile kɛ wɔnwu “diedi ko” ne—ɛzonlenlɛ nɔhalɛ ko ne mɔɔ Nyamenle die to nu la? (Ɛfɛ. 4:4-6) Asoo ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ ɛnwunle ye wɔ Gyihova menli avinli la a hwele wɔ rɛle bɛ nwo a? Asoo bɛ subane kpalɛ ne lile daselɛ kpalɛ hilele wɔ? Menli dɔɔnwo a nɔhalɛ ne twe bɛ ɔluakɛ Nyamenle ahyehyɛdeɛ ne maa bɛte nganeɛ kɛ bɛbɔ bɛ nwo bane na bɛkulo bɛ kpalɛ, yɛɛ ɔtɛla zɔ. Ɛlɔlɛ nee subane kpalɛ le nzonlɛdeɛ mɔɔ bɛfa bɛnwu nɔhalɛ ɛzonlenlɛ a.—Dwɔn 13:35; 1 Pita 2:12.

Noko na saa adiema Kilisienenle bie foa wɔ anzɛɛ ɔnla ɛlɔlɛ ali ɔngile wɔ noko ɛ? Anzɛɛ saa adiema Kilisienenle bie di ɛtane kpole—wɔ debie ɛtane kpole bie mɔɔ yɛ anyunluwula kɛ mɔɔ dahuu rale debie kɛ zɔ la azo wɔ asafo ne mɔɔ ɛnee wɔ Kɔlentɩ la anu wɔ ɛvoya mɔɔ limoa la anu? (1 Kɔl. 5:1) Saa wɔ diedi gyi kɛzi awie mɔ nyɛleɛ maa ɛte nwohɔle la ala azo a, ɛnee ɔgyinla fane mɔɔ ɛngyina kpundii la azo. Asoo fane mɔɔ gyina kpundii dɔɔnwo kpole bie la wɔ ɛkɛ mɔɔ wɔ diedi kola gyinla zolɛ a?

DIDIDIKƐLƐ MƆƆ GYINLA KPƆKƐ MAA DIDILƐ

Ɛbahola wɔamaa wɔ diedi anyia fawula kpole kpalɛ ɔlua nɔhalɛ titilii mɔɔ wɔzukoa wɔ Baebolo ne anu la mɔɔ ɛbamaa wɔ diedi anu amia la azo. Kɛzi ɛhye bahola aboa ɛ? Suzu neazo bie anwo.

Mekɛ bie, Gyisɛse ɛdoavolɛma dɔɔnwo gyakyile ye. Ɛdendɛlɛ mɔɔ ɔhanle mɔɔ fane ye nwonane mɔɔ bɛbali nee ye mogya mɔɔ bɛano nwo la hanle bɛ adwenle. Bɛhanle kɛ: “Ɛdendɛlɛ ɛhye yɛ ɛzulolɛ.” Mɔɔ Gyisɛse hanle la hanle bɛ adwenle kpole kpaleti mɔɔ ɔlua zo bɛgyakyile ye ɛdoalɛ.—Dwɔn 6:56-60, 66.

Yemɔ anzi, Gyisɛse bizale ye ɛzoanvolɛ ne mɔ kɛ: “Bɛdaye mɔ noko bɛkulo kɛ bɛkɔ ɔ?” Pita buale kɛ: “Awurade, nwane ɛkɛ a yɛkɔ a? Ɛlɛ dahuza ngoane edwɛkɛ. Yɛlie yɛli na yɛnwu kɛ ɛdaye a le Nyamenle Sonla Nwuanzanwuanza ne.”—Dwɔn 6:67-69.

Duzu a manle Pita nee sɛso ɛzoanvolɛma ne yɛle ngakyile wɔ bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛdole aze la anwo a? Ɛnee bɛ diedi gyi debie mɔɔ anu yɛ se tɛla bɛ nganeɛdelɛ la azo. Pita hanle kɛ: “Yɛdie yɛdi yɛɛ yɛze kɛ ɛle Nyamenle Krotobɔnema ne.” Nɔhalɛvolɛma ɛhye mɔ ze mɔɔ bɛdie bɛdi la yɛɛ deɛmɔti bɛdie bɛdi la. Kɛmɔ bɛtoto Gyisɛse edwɛkɛ nee ye nyɛleɛ nwo bɛto mɔɔ bɛze wɔ Ngɛlɛlera ne anu la anwo la ati, ɛnee bɛlɛ nɔhalɛlilɛ mɔɔ anu yɛ se kɛ Gyisɛse a le Mɛzaya ne. Nɔhalɛlilɛ kpole mɔɔ bɛlɛ ye wɔ nɔhalɛ titili ɛhye anu la manle bɛ diedi gyinlanle kpundii wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ awie mɔ dole aze wɔ mɔɔ Gyisɛse hanle la anwo la.

Duzu a edwɛkɛ zɔhane kilehile yɛ a? Mɔɔ yɛze la bahola anyia tumi kpole adɛla kɛzi yɛte nganeɛ la. Dɛnla a le zɔ? Ɔluakɛ Baebolo ngilehilelɛ—mɔɔ nɔhalɛ mɔɔ yɛzukoa ye wɔ Nyamenle duma, ye bodane, nee ɛkpɔnedeɛ ne anwo la boka nwo—ɛnzeke. Ɔnzɔho kɛzi yɛte nganeɛ la, mɔɔ menli yɛ anzɛɛ bɛka la ɛnzɛkye Baebolo nɔhalɛ ne mɔ.

30 ƐZINLII NE

Yemɔti, saa yɛdie Baebolo ne anu nɔhalɛ ne yɛdi na yɛfa nɔhalɛ ɛhye yɛbɔ mɔdenle mɔɔ Gyihova ɛlɛfa ali gyima la anwo a, yɛbahola yɛazi nwolɛ adenle kɛ awie mɔ subane ɛnrɛdɔ yɛ aze.

Nɔhalɛ nu, adiema mɔɔ wɔ anwuma ne la ɛlɔlɛ ne maa yɛ anwosesebɛ. Yɛɛ yɛ mediema mɔɔ bɛdie bɛdi la subane kpalɛ ne noko maa yɛ nye die. Noko saa zɔhane nganeɛdelɛ ne bɔ ɔ bo kɛ ɔtotɔ aze wɔ awie mɔ nyɛleɛ ti a, ɛnee diedi mɔɔ yɛlɛ wɔ Baebolo nɔhalɛ ne mɔ anu la bahola amaa yɛ diedi ne agyinla kpundii. Ɔbaboa yɛ yeamaa yɛanwu kɛ yɛwɔ ahyehyɛdeɛ kpalɛ ne anu—ɔnva nwo mɛtɛnlenu ekyi bie mɔ nyɛleɛ.

Saa yɛbɔ mɔden yɛsukoa Baebolo ne kpalɛ dahuu a, nɔhalɛ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ yɛzukoa yɛvi Nyamenle Edwɛkɛ ne anu la banwu yɛ kɛnlɛma. Yɛ nɔhalɛlilɛ zɔhane noko baboa yɛ amaa yɛalie yɛali kɛ yɛnwu sonlolilɛ adenle kɔnwonle kpalɛ mɔɔ Nyamenle die to nu la. Ɛhye bamaa yɛ diedi agyina fɛlɛfɛlɛ zo kpalɛ na awie mɔ edwɛkɛ anzɛɛ nyɛleɛ ɛnrehyi yɛ.—Edw. 16:8; nea ɛlɛka mɔɔ se “Dɛnla Adenle Kpalɛ Ne Zo.”

Dɛnla Adenle Kpalɛ Ne Azo

Fa ye kɛ wɔvo lɔre mɔɔ bado mayɛle dɔɔnwo ahɔ ɛleka kɛnlɛma bie la. Wɔ adenrɛ tendenle zɔhane anu, ɔda ali kɛ ɛbade nganeɛ ngakyile dɔɔnwo—titili ne anyelielɛ mɔɔ vi kɛ ɛwɔ adenrɛ ne azo la anu.

Noko akee, mekɛ mɔɔ ɛwɔ adenle ɛhye azo la, ninyɛne bie mɔ bahola amaa wɔ sa nu ado—bie a menli nwiɔ mɔɔ ɛlɛtendɛ kpole anzɛɛ kɛzi draeva ne nwo ɛnyɛ se kpalɛ la. Noko zɔhane ninyɛne ne mɔ anu mɔɔ biala ɛnle bɔbɔ mɔɔ bahola ahakyi nɔhalɛ mɔɔ ɛle kɛ ɛwɔ bɔse mɔɔ fɛta la azo na ɔhyia kɛ ɛtena zo amaa wɔadwu ɛleka mɔɔ ɛlɛkɔ la.

Wɔ yɛ adendulɛ kɔ Nyamenle ewiade fofolɛ ne anu la, yɛbanyia nganeɛdelɛ dɔɔnwo—titili anyelielɛ kɛ yɛboka Nyamenle ahyehyɛdeɛ ne anwo. Noko akee, ɔdwu mekɛ ne bie a, mediema Kilisienenle bie kola di yɛ nyane anzɛɛ nvonleɛ mɔɔ ahenle mɔɔ yɛbu ye la bayɛ la kola bɔ yɛ nwanwane. Noko ɛhye mɔ amuala ɛngakyi nɔhalɛ mɔɔ ɛwɔ nu kɛ yɛde bɔse kpalɛ ne anu la. Yɛwɔ ahyehyɛdeɛ kpalɛ ne anu. Saa yɛlɛ Baebolo ne anu nɔhalɛ titili ne mɔ anwo nrɛlɛbɛ kpalɛ—yɛɛ yɛkile anyezɔlɛ wɔ ɛleka mɔɔ yɛzukwile nɔhalɛ ɛhye mɔ la anwo a—yɛbadɛnla adenle kpalɛ ne azo. Yɛ diedi bagyinla kpundii.

Saa yɛ diedi gyi kɛzi awie mɔ nyɛleɛ maa yɛte nganeɛ la ngomekye azo a, ɛnee ɔgyinla fane mɔɔ ɛnli munli la azo

ANWONWONDELE

Gyihova Maa Diedi Kakɛ

Emma yɛle gyima wɔ hotɛle bie dabɛnla nsa dapɛne biala, dɔnehwele ngɔnla kenle ko. Ɔzɛlɛle ye kpanyinli kɛ ɔmaa ye kenle ko menlekonle amaa yeahɔ ye maangyebakyi nyianu ne. Kɛ anrɛbee ɔbalie ye nzɛlɛlɛ ne la, kpanyinli ne hakyile ye gyimalilɛ ngyehyɛleɛ ne yɛle ye dabɛnla nnu dapɛne biala na ɔzele ye kɛ: Lie gyimalilɛ ngyehyɛleɛ fofolɛ ne to nu anzɛɛ gyakyi gyima ne. Ɔlua Gyihova anu mɔɔ Emma nyianle diedi la ati, ɔkpɔlɛle kɛ ɔbagyakyi gyima ne na yeahɔ nyianu ne.

Mekɛ mɔɔ Emma hɔle kɛ ɔkɔva ye gyimayɛlɛ ekyi kɛlata ne yeamaa la, ɛnee hotɛle ne azo kpanyinli kpole ne wɔ ɛkɛ. Mɔɔ ɔdele edwɛkɛ ne anwo edwɛkɛ la, ɔbɔle ɛwɔkɛ kɛ ɔbali edwɛkɛ ne anwo gyima. Noko akee, dapɛne nwiɔ pɛle nu la, bɛhanle bɛhilele Emma kɛ bɛpɛ ye gyima nu. Ɔdole hotɛle ne azo kpanyinli kpole ne bieko, na ɔvale ɛya kɛ bɛanli ye adehilelɛ zo la. Ɔziesiele edwɛkɛ ne, na bɛmanle Emma mekɛ fofolɛ mɔɔ ɔbava yeayɛ gyima la: dapɛne ko biala kenle nnu, dɔnehwele nna kenle ko biala. Ɛnee ɛhye le kpalɛ tɛla ye dahuu mekɛ ne bɔbɔ; ɔboale ye kpalɛ wɔ ye sunsum nu gyimalilɛ nwo! Gyihova lile ye ngyianlɛ nwo gyima wɔ adenle mɔɔ tɛla mɔɔ ɛnee ɔkpondɛ la azo.

Bɛndɔne buluku ɛhye. Bɛyɛ bɛmaa ewiade amuala Baebolo ɛhwehyɛle gyima ne mɔɔ bɛfa ndonwo ahyɛlɛdeɛ bɛyɛ la. Saa ɛkulo kɛ ɛye ahyɛlɛdeɛ a, yɛsɛlɛ wɔ kɔ donate.jw.org.
