Yɛnni aseɛ koraa, yɛnni aseɛ koraa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokorɛ)

SƐNEA AKETEKYIRE GYAE NYAA NE SE A AHYE NO

AKETEKYIRE ne Ananse kɔe sɛ wɔrekɔpɛ aburoo adua. Ɛnna wɔnyaa aburoo aburow mmienu pɛ. Ɛnna Ananse see sɛ, "Merekɔdua me deɛ." Ɛnna Aketekyire nso see sɛ, "Merekɔtoto me deɛ awe." Ananse kɔduaa ne deɛ. Aketekyire nso faa ne deɛ tow too gya no so. Ɛkyɛe ansa na ɛreben, na ɔtenaa hɔ (ho). Ananse nso kɔɔ afum hɔ; ɔrekɔhwɛ aburoo no ahahan no ase. Aburoo no see sɛ, "Mma nhwɛ m'ani so, na w'nsateaa ankɔwɔ m'ani." Ananse dan n'ani sɛ ɔrehwɛ ne Kraman; ne Kraman no see sɛ, "Ɛnhia sɛ wohwɛ me, ɛnyɛ me na meyɛe." Ananse rebubu abaa abɔ Kraman no, na abaa no see sɛ, "Mmu me, anyɛ saa a wo Kraman no bɛka me." Ananse baa fie bɛkaa ne nyinaa kyerɛɛ Aketekyire. Berɛ a ɔresere no, ɔhwee gya no mu. Ɛno nti na Aketekyire se hyee no. M'anansesɛm a metoe yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na ma bi nso nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
