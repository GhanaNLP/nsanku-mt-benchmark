Yɛnni aseɛ koraa, yɛnni aseɛ koraa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokorɛ)

SƐ OBI SƐE W’ADEE BI (A ƐNYƐ N’APƐDEƐ A), NGYE HO AKATUA

Wɔse ntaafoɔ bi tenaa ase a na wɔn maame awu agya wɔn (nkutoo). Na wɔredi afahyɛ bi, na Ata 'Panin faa ahweneɛ pa ne sika agudeɛ bi de guu Ata Kuma kɔn mu. Ata Kuma ne ne nnamfonom kɔkae sɛ wɔrekɔ asubɔnten no mu akɔware. Berɛ a na wɔreware no, ahoma a na sika agudeɛ no hyehyɛ so a ɛwɔ Ata Kuma kɔn mu no tei, na agudeɛ no hwee nsuo no mu. Ɔhwehwɛɛ ara nanso wanhunu. Ɔbaa fie bɛbɔɔ ne nuabaa no amanneɛ sɛ, "Sika agudeɛ no ayera." Ata 'Panin kaa sɛ, "Mentumi nnye nni sɛ ayera, na sɛ ayera mpo a, ɛdeɛn bi na mɛgye afiri wo nsam." Ata Kuma nso bisaa sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na wobɛgye afiri me nsam?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mɛgye ɔsebo bɔwerɛ afiri wo nsam, aserewa ketewa no pirebuo, nsuo a ɛyɛ ponponponponsa, ahaban a ɛno nko ara pɛ na ɛsensɛn dua no a wobɛtetɛ so no so, ne Ɛsono Hemmaa dua." Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, "Mate." Ata Kuma kɔe (sɛ ɔrekɔhwehwɛ nnoɔma yi), na ɔbaa faako a dua koro bi sisi hɔ. Berɛ a dua no hunuu no no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Ata Kuma a wɔka ne ho asɛm sɛ ɔpue kuro no ano te sɛ nsuo a ɛretɔ dwoodwoo no nie." (Ata Kuma) kaa sɛ, "Deɛ enti a mebaa ha ara ne sɛ agya ba ne Ata 'Panin bi, na ɛna ba ne Ata 'Panin bi, na ɔnwonoo ahweneɛ a ɛho agye guu me kɔn mu, na ɛtetee yerae." (Ɛha na Ata Kuma hyɛɛ aseɛ too dwom):

Ɔse ɔregye biribi afiri me nsam.

Ɔrehwehwɛ dua a ɛsow ahaban baako pɛ no ahaban firi me nkyɛn.

Ɔse ɔregye biribi afiri me nsam.

Ɔregye ahaban bi afiri me nkyɛn.

Ɔse ɔregye biribi afiri me nsam.

Ɔregye aserewa no berebuo no.

Ɔse ɔregye biribi afiri me nsam.

Ɔrese Nsuo, a ɛreworo ponponponponsa.

Ɔse ɔregye biribi afiri me nkyɛn. Ɔregye Agya Ɔsebo mmɔwerɛ.

Ɔse ɔregye biribi afiri me nsam.

Ɔregye Ɛsono Ɔhemmaa no dua no kɔsi ase pɛɛ.

Na ɛdeɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ, O agya, na anya bi?

Ɔse (nie) abofra ketewa bi a ɔyɛ ketewa pa ara, Akwasi Toboro Kojo babaa.

Kojo Nsa.

Agya Ɛsono, ɛbɛyɛ yie sɛ wokɔ, ɛbɛyɛ yie sɛ wofiri ha kɔ.

Kojo Nsa a a.”

Na Dua-baako-nhahan no kaa sɛ, “Na ɛno nti na woresu yi, bra bɛgye biribi.” Na Ata Kuma kɔe kɔgyee (nhahan no firii dua a na nhahan baako pɛ na ɛwɔ so no hɔ), na ɔdaa no (dua no) ase. Na ɔtoaa n’akwantu no so, na ɔhyiaa anomaa Aserewa. Na anomaa Aserewa no kaa sɛ, “Ata Kuma a ɔpue kurotia a, obiara ka sɛ ɔte sɛ nsuo tɔbrɛ a ɛma ahomtɔ nie.” Ata Kuma buaa sɛ, “Deɛ enti a maba ha ara ne sɛ, agya ba ne Ata ’Panin baako, na ɛna ba ne Ata ’Panin baako, na ɔnwenee ahwenepa hyehyɛɛ me, na ɛtetew yerae.” (Na ɛha na Ata Kuma hyɛɛ aseɛ too dwom):

Ɔse ɔregye biribi afiri me nsam.

Ɔrehwehwɛ dua a ɛsow ahaban baako pɛ no ahaban firi me nkyɛn.

Ɔse ɔregye biribi afiri me nsam.

Ɔrebisa me ahaban.

Ɔse ɔregye biribi afiri me nsam.

Ɔregye aserewa no berebuo no.

Ɔse ɔregye biribi afiri me nsam.

Ɔrese Nsuo, a ɛreworo ponponponponsa.

Ɔse ɔregye biribi afiri me nsam.

Ɔrese Agya Ɔsebo awerew no yɛ ne dea.

Ɔse ɔregye biribi afiri me nsam.

Ɔregye Ɛsono Ɔhemmaa no dua no kɔsi ase pɛɛ.

Na ɛdeɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ, O agya, na anya bi?

Ɔse (nie) abofra ketewa bi a ɔyɛ ketewa pa ara, Akwasi Toboro Kojo babaa.

Kojo Nsa.

Agya Ɛsono, ɛbɛyɛ yie sɛ wokɔ, ɛbɛyɛ yie sɛ wofiri ha kɔ.

Kojo Nsa a a.”

Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, “Ɛno nti na woresu yi, bra bɛgye biribi.” Na Ata Kuma kɔgyee (Aserewa no pirebuo no), na ɔdaa no ase. Na ɔtuu kwan, na ɔkɔhyiaa Nsuo a ɛrewoso ponponponponsa. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ata Kuma ni, deɛ sɛ ɔpue kurotia a, nkurɔfoɔ gye no fɛfɛɛfɛ sɛ osuo a ɛtɔ ma fam dwoɔ no.” Ata Kuma bɔɔ no amanneɛ a wabɔ afoforɔ no dedaw no. Na Nsuo-a-ɛrewoso no maa no bi (ne nsuo a ɛrewoso no bi), na ɔgyeeɛ na ɔkɔɔ n'anim. Ɛno akyi no ɔkɔhyiaa Ɔsebɔ. Ɛberɛ a Ɔsebɔ hunuu no no, ɔkaa sɛ, “Ata Kuma ni; sɛ ɔpue kurotia a, ɔte sɛ osuo a ɛtɔ ma fam dwoɔ no.” Ata Kuma bɔɔ no n'asɛm. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Bra bɛgye biribi, (na ɔmaa no ne bwerɛ baako). Ɛnna Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, “Agya Ɔsebɔ, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ na manya Ɛsono Ɔhemmaa no dua?” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Aane, sɛ wokɔ a, mmoa no nyinaa bɛda fam, na Ɛsono Ɔhemmaa no bɛda mfimfini, na sɛ worekɔ a, tiatia wɔn so denneennen kɔ, na sɛ woduru Ɛsono Ɔhemmaa no nkyɛn a, twa ne dua no, nanso sɛ wotiatia wɔn so brɛoo a, wɔbɛkum wo.” Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, “Meda wo ase.” Na ɛberɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu saa mmoa yi; ɔtiatiaa wɔn so denneennen kɔeɛ, na ɔduruu Ɔhemmaa no nkyɛn. Ɔtwaa ne dua no, na ɔsanee n'akyi. Ɔkwan so no ɔtɔɔ ahwehwɛ, na ɔde kɔe (fie). Na ɔduruu fie. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ata 'Panin, nnoɔma a wokaa sɛ wobɛgye afiri me nsam no ni.” Ata 'Panin kaa sɛ, “Me nsa aka.” Afei, da koro bi na wɔte hɔ baako, na Ata Kuma faa ahwehwɛ no na ɔhwɛɛ mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ahwehwɛ yi, sɛ obi hwɛ mu a, ɔhunu maame a ɔwɔ Asaman no.” Ata 'Panin kaa sɛ, “Ɛte saa, fa bra na menhwɛ.” Ɔde maa no. Ɛberɛ a ɔrebɛhwɛ mu no, ɛfiri ne nsa hwee fam, na ɛbɔeɛ. Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, “Ɛnneɛ yei deɛ, deɛ wogyeeɛ firi me nsam no, mɛgye afiri wo nsam.” Ɛnna Ata 'Panin kɔfaa nnoɔma a Ata Kuma de brɛɛ no no baeɛ. Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, “Mempe (yeinom), deɛ mepɛ ne sɛ wokɔ na wode foforɔ bɛbrɛ me.” Ata 'Panin tuu kwan, na ɔkɔhyiaa dua bi a ɛwɔ ahaban baako pɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ata 'Panin ni, deɛ sɛ ɔpue kurotia a, ɔyɛ fɛ sɛ osuo a ɛtɔ ma fam dwoɔ no.” Ata 'Panin gyee so sɛ, “Mepuee wo maame!” ne w’agya!, me deɛ, ma me wo baaha baako no na menkɔ." (Dua a) ɛwɔ baaha koro no kaa sɛ, "Fa mu kɔ, deɛ ɔnim (wo ho asɛm) wɔ hɔ." Ɔkɔɔ n’anim, na ɔhyiaa Aserewa anomaa. Aserewa no kaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panin nie; sɛ ɔpue wɔ kuro no abɔntene ano a, ɔte sɛ nsuo tɔbrɛ a ɛyɛ dwo." Ata 'Panin kaa sɛ, "Sɛ 'mepue' wo maame ne w’agya, me deɛ, deɛ mepɛ ara ne wo pirebuo baako pɛ, na menkɔ." Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, "Fa mu kɔ, deɛ ɔnim (wo ho asɛm) wɔ hɔ." Ɛno akyi no, ɔkɔhyiaa Nsuo a Ɛhuro. Nsuo a Ɛhuro no kaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panin nie; sɛ ɔpue wɔ kuro no ano a, ɔte sɛ nsuo tɔbrɛ a ɛyɛ dwo." Ata 'Panin kaa sɛ, "Sɛ 'mepue' wo maame, sɛ 'mepue' w’agya, me deɛ, ma me wo nsuo no bi na menfa mu nkɔ." Nsuo a Ɛhuro no kaa sɛ, "Fa mu kɔ anim, deɛ ɔnim (wo ho asɛm) wɔ hɔ." Afei ɔhyiaa Ɔsebo. Ɔsebo no kaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panin nie, deɛ sɛ ɔpue wɔ abɔntene ano a, ɔte sɛ nsuo tɔbrɛ a ɛyɛ dwo." Ata 'Panin buaa sɛ, "Me 'pue' wo maame! ne w’agya! me deɛ, ma me wo mmɔwere no bi na menfa mu nkɔ." Ɔsebo no kaa sɛ, "Deɛ ɔnim (wo ho asɛm) wɔ hɔ." Ɔfirii hɔ kɔeɛ, na ɔkɔhyiaa Ɛsono dɔm, na Ɛsono Ɔhemmaa no da mfinimfini. Afei ɔkɔe sɛ ɔrekɔtwa ne dua. Ɛsono dɔm no nyinaa sɔɔ ne mu, na wɔtiatiaa ne so, na ɔwuiɛ. Ata Kuma hwɛɛ ne nua baa no kwan so. Wamma. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Sɛ wo ne wo mogya mu nipa bi te baabi a, na sɛ wɔn mu baako sɛe wo biribi a, nnye akatua, efisɛ Ata 'Panin yɛɛ saa, na ɔkɔeɛ na ɔwuiɛ."

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
