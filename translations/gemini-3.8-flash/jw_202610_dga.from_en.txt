A saŋa be la kpeɛŋaa naŋ na baŋ ka Naaŋmene na maale o meŋɛ yuori. Naaŋmene na nyɛ la noba ka ba e o seɛredembe a teŋɛzuŋ,” a The Watch Tower naŋ yi a January 1, 1926 da yeli la a lɛ.

A yuoni 1926 naŋ da wa gaa nentoo, Yehowa—tu ne a asɔmblee zu, Kristo eŋɛ—da wuli la veɛlee gaali a pɔɔleŋ ka a moɔloo toma seŋ ka a e a boma kpoŋ Kristabiye nyɔvore poɔ. GAŊ-PAALAA KAŊA NE LEƐ-MAALOO LANNE LAŊ-KPEREGHO POƆ

Gyenoŋ poɔ, The Golden Age (ba naŋ boɔlɔ pampana Awake!) da maaleŋ yeli la gane paalaa kaŋa naŋ yie na yɛlɛ ka Deliverance. A gane ŋa da na leɛre la Studies in the Scriptures ka a e a gane kpoŋ na ba naŋ maŋ de tonɔ ne a yelnoore tontonmaaloŋ. Ka ba baŋ nyɛ a noba zaa naŋ boɔrɔ a gane, a yideme naŋ be Bethel da da la a maasĩ paalaa naŋ taa fanteɛ na ŋmɛ gaba 22 minta kpoŋ zaa poɔ. A Bible Students da de la a gane paalaa ŋa kora a noba saŋa zaa ba naŋ nyɛ sori.

Yumo yaga poɔ, a Baabole Zannereba da maŋ kannɛɛ a Studies in the Scriptures gbaŋgbɛlɛ a ba lantaapɔgeŋ. Kyɛ a piili May poɔ, ba da piilee gbaŋgannuu a The Watch Tower gbaŋgbɛlɛ poɔŋ. Boŋ da la a poɔ-yeli?

“Bunker Hill, Illinois lantaapuobilɛ na [asɛmbele na] baree fo puoruu,” la ka asɛmbele kaŋa da sɛge gane poɔ ko Mba Rutherford. “Ladaare zaa te maŋ zanna la a [zannoozie] na a Lantaapuoree naŋ iri ko a bebie na. Te pɛleɛ yaga zaa ka te zaa tõɔ nyɛ a kpeɛõ paaloŋ a naŋ wa te ta a saŋa na.”

A “kannaa paalaa,” bee wulluu naŋ wa laŋ, da soŋ la a Baabule zannerebɔ ba na eŋ a moɔloo toma ba sũurɛ poɔ. Aseŋ, a dɛmmo yɛlɛ na “Character or Covenant—Which?” da manne la ka “subeɛ maaloŋ,” naŋ da de ba teɛroŋ zaa eŋ eŋɛ nensaala maaloŋ ane sũ-yoma poɔ yoŋ, da vɛŋ la ka kpoŋ kaŋa fẽẽ a Kristabiiri yelmeŋa toma kpoŋ. A dɛmmo da ŋmaa yeli: “Neɛ naŋ nɔŋ a Teŋgane-soba seŋ ka o pampana . . . tu o noɔre yɛlɛ. . . . Yehowa yeli: ‘Yɛ e la n shɛrebiiri ka n e la Naaŋmen.’ . . . A Kristabie pampana na moɔ la ka o di shɛre aseŋ Naaŋmen shɛrebie soŋ.”

Gbaŋ-peɛle kpoŋ kanga, “Malaabu Ane Tontonne,” da wuli la a peɛle kpoŋ ka Kiristo-biiri sɔhibe wulirɔ la ba nɔmmo ko Jehoova a yi ba naŋ maŋ de ba meŋɛ kpɛ o tontonne poɔŋ a taa dɛmmo. A gbaŋ-peɛle ŋa da kpeɛrɛɛ a Baabolo Sakuuri-biiri zaa faŋa ka ba gaa nimie, tona ne yɛŋ-zaa, ane kpeɛo a Teŋgane-Daan tontonne poɔŋ, ka ba la “e seere-dire ko a Teŋgane-Daan ka ba la mɔɔle o duoroo ko a teŋɛ zannoo zaa ane a teŋgane noba zaa.”—Isa. 43:10, 12; Mat. 24:14.

YENG-KPEƐRE MAŊ TEƐRE LA A MOƆLOƆ TOMA YƐL-NYƆGERAA

May baaraa poɔ, Baabole Zannereba yaga da laŋ la taa London, England tigri kaŋa poɔ. Tuma bareba zɔɔre kaŋa naŋ da be Britain poɔ da vɛŋ la ka a da tuo ko ba ka ba nyɛ sēēreba naŋ na sēē a gafiti mine, lere a yizie poɔ la ka a yideme naŋ be Brooklyn Bethel tona tuma gaa tɔɔre a ko a tigri 117,000 gampɛlɛ naŋ na paale a booklet naŋ taa a yuori The Standard for the People.

O yeli-manne poɔ, Saama Rutherford da manneŋ ka a teŋgane noba kpambee lɔɔbo da yi la ba naŋ da zagere a Naaŋmene Jehoova ane o Naaloŋ. Saama Rutherford da la maaleŋ gaa la nimipɛle a zage League of Nations a la faŋŋe yeli ka o na wa baare la.
