Yɛnni aseɛ koraa, yɛnni aseɛ koraa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokorɛ)

DEƐ ENTI A MPANYINFOƆ KA SƐ ƐNSƐ SƐ YƐTIE KƐTƐ SO ASƐM GU ABƆNTEN SO NO

Wɔse (tete no bi) Nyankopɔn Kwame dɔɔ afum kɛse bi, na ɔduaduaa nkuruma, gyeene, adua, nyaadewa, mako, ne asikirekuku wom. Wira bɔɔ turo no mu na ahyehyew-nsaa fifii wom. Ɛnna Nyankopɔn bɔɔ dawuro sɛ ne turo no mu ayɛ wira dodo, na obiara a obetumi adɔ na wankeka ne ho no, ɔmmra mmɛfa ne babaa, Abena Nkroma (kyerɛ sɛ Abena a ɔto so nkron) nware. Oyi kɔe (sɛ ɔrekɔyɛ bi) no, ɔkekaa ne ho (faako a ahyehyew-nsaa no hyee no no), na wɔhurii no; ɔno nso kɔe na wɔhurii no (saa ara); mmarima nyinaa kɔe (kɔyɛɛ bi); wantumi. Afei Kwaku Ananse see sɛ, "Me deɛ, metumi." Na Nyankopɔn turo no da ɛkwan ho, na ɛkwan no na nkurɔfo fa so kɔ dwaso Fida biara. Na Ananse, esiane sɛ na ɔnim asɛm yi nti, ɔkɔdɔ wira no Fida biara nko ara. Na sɛ ɔredɔ, na nkurɔfo a wɔretwam kyea no sɛ, "Ayekoo, Agya Ananse!", ɔno nso gye so sɛ, "Yaa, Aku." Wɔtoa so sɛ, "Afum a obiara antumi annɔ yi, (wopɛ akyerɛ sɛ) wo na woredɔ yi?" Ananse bua sɛ, "Aane, abaayewa koro yi nti na merebrɛ me ho sei. Ne nsa baako te sɛ sei." Na ɔbɔɔ ne nsa so na ɔtwitwii faako a ɛrehyew no no, na sɛ ɔyɛ saa a, ɛnya ahodwiriw ma no. Obi foforo betwam bio akyea no wɔ n’adwuma ho; na ɔde ne nsa abɔ faako a ɛreyɛ no heneheene no. Sɛ ebia ne srɛ so a, ɔbɛka sɛ, "Abaayewa koro no, wɔse ne srɛ te sɛ sei" (na wabɔ na watwitwi ne deɛ no so). Saa kwan yi so na ɔfae dɔɔ afum no wiei. Ɛnna ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ Nyankopɔn sɛ wawie n'afum no dɔ. Nyankopɔn bisaa bɔfo no sɛ, "Wawie ampa?" Bɔfo no see sɛ, "Aane." Nyankopɔn bisaa no sɛ, "Ɔkekaa ne ho?" Ɔsee sɛ, "Dabi, wankeka ne ho." Nyankopɔn faa Abena Nkroma de no maa Ananse awareɛ. Anadwo bi Ananse ne ne yere kɔdae, na ayeforɔ no bisaa no sɛ, "Ɛyɛɛ dɛn na wo mpo wotumi dɔɔ m'agya afum no mu wira, afum a obiara a ɔkɔɔ so no sanee n'akyi, nanso wo deɛ wotumi dɔe?" Ɛnna Ananse see sɛ, "Wosusu sɛ meyɛ kwasea?" Me dɔe no, na sɛ obi retwa mu na obisa me sɛ, ‘Ananse, wo na worefow afum yi a obiara ntumi mfowee da yi anaa,’ ɛnna deɛ me ho keka me biara mebɔ me nsa wɔ so na mesunsun ho, na maka akyerɛ onipa no sɛ, sɛ ebia wo srɛ te sɛ ɛkoɔ srɛ, na ɛyɛ fɛ na ɛhyerɛn. Ɛno na ɛmaa metumi dɔe no.” Ɛnna Abena Nkroma kaa sɛ, “Ɛnde ɔkyena mɛka akyerɛ agya sɛ wosunsun wo ho.” Ananse kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Mma mmɔ din, kɛtɛsoasɛm a ɛte saa.” Abena Nkroma kaa sɛ, “Mennim kɛtɛsoasɛm biara, na mɛkasa.” Abena Nkroma yii ne kɛtɛ (firi Ananse nkyɛn) kɔdano wɔ dan no fam baabi. Afei Ananse ani bɛberee kɔɔ, na ɔkɔfaa ne bɛntɛntoɔ, na ɔbɔe na ɔtoo dwom sɛ:

Abena, ba a ɔtɔ so nkron no, yei nyɛ asɛm a ɛsɛ sɛ yɛko ho.

Momma yɛmfa nyɛ kɛtɛ so asɛm a ɛyɛ ahintasɛm.

'Daabi,' ɔse.

Ɔwɔ asɛm (tia me), nanso obi foforo wɔ asɛm a seesei koraa ɛnenam kwan no so."

Ɛnna Ananse sɔreeɛ, na ɔkɔe kɔdaeɛ. Berɛ a Ananse daa hɔ kakra no, ɛnna ɔsɔre gyinaeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abena Nkroma"—anomaa bi mpo nsu (gyene nkukua nnaasoɔ) dinn! Ananse kaa sɛ, "Manya wo." Ɔfaa koraa ketewa bi de nsuo hyɛɛ mu ma, na ɔhwie guu Abena Nkroma kɛtɛ so. Ɛnna Ananse kɔe kɔdaeɛ. Ɔdaa hɔ kakra no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Ko! Abena Nkroma, ɛdeɛn koraa nie? Woagugu kɛtɛ so, obi a ɔnni animtiatia, nokorɛm wo nyɛ koraa; adeɛ kye a, mɛka akyerɛ obiara. Na ɛyɛ nokorɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ (deɛ na wɔn nyinaa reka no), sɛ obiara a ɔkɔ saa afuom hɔ no, ɔtaa ka sɛ, 'Abaayewa a ɔgugu...! Merenkɔbɔ ahwenneɛ afuo (mma onii a ɔte saa).'" Ɛnna Abena ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Mesrɛ wo, gyae, na ma asɛm no nsa." Ananse kaa sɛ, "Merengyae, m'asɛm na edi kan; wokaa sɛ wobɛka akyerɛ wo papa; mekaa sɛ, 'Gyae'; wokaa sɛ, 'Dabi.' Ɛno nti merengyae asɛm no." Na Abena, ɔba a ɔtɔ so nkron no kaa sɛ, "Gyae m'asɛm no, na w'asɛm a mekaa ho asɛm no nso, mɛgyae; efisɛ sɛ woangyae me deɛ no a, fɛreɛ bɛku me." Ɛnna Ananse kaa sɛ, "Mate, sɛdeɛ wopɛ no, ma ɛnyɛ kɛtɛ so asɛm, enti ma asɛm no nsi hɔ." Ɛno nti na mpanyimfoɔ kae sɛ "Kɛtɛ so asɛm, yɛnka no abɔnten".

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, ebi nkɔ na ebi mmra.
