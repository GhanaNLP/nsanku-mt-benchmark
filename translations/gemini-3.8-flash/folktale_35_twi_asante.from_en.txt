Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka yi yɛ nokware)

DEƐNTI A MPANYINFOƆ SE YƐNNKĀ MPĀ SO ASƐM

Wɔse (tete no bi) na Nyankopɔn Kwame dɔɔ afum kɛse bi, na ɔdwudwoo nkruma, gyene, asɛ (aboboe), nyaadewa, mako, ne asikyerɛbɔfra (akadeɛ) wɔ mu. Wira no bɛfɛfɛɛ afum hɔ kɛse na ahahan a ɛhye nso fifii mu. Nyankopɔn bɔɔ dawuro sɛ ne mfuo ayɛ wira kɛse, na obiara a ɔbɛtumi adɔ a ɔrennwɔ ne ho no, ɔntwe ne ho mmra anim mmɛfa ne babaa, Abena Nkroma (kyerɛ sɛ Abena ba a ɔtɔ so nkron) nware. Oyi kɔeɛ no, ɔtwee ne ho, na wɔbɔɔ no hyire; oyie nso kɔeɛ na wɔbɔɔ no hyire; mmarima nyinaa kɔeɛ; wɔantumi. Afei Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, “Me deɛ, mɛtumi.” Na Nyankopɔn afuom no da kwan no nkyɛn, na saa kwan no na nnipa fa so kɔ dwa da biara Fida. Afei Ananse nso, esiane sɛ na onim saa asɛm yi nti, na ɔkɔ do wura no Fida biara pɛ. Na berɛ a ɔredɔ no, na nnipa a wɔretwamu no taa kyea no sɛ, “Mo ne adwuma, Agya Ananse!”, ɔtaa gye so sɛ, “Meda mo ase, Aku.” Wɔtoa so sɛ, “Afuo a obiara ntumi nnɔeɛ no, (woreka akyerɛ yɛn sɛ) woredo?” Ananse nso bɛbua sɛ, “Aane, abaayewa koro pɛ nti na merebrɛ me ho sei nyinaa. Ne basa baako pɛ no te sɛ sei." Na ɔbɛbɔ ne nsa na watwi ne basa baabi a na ɛrehene no no, na sɛ ɔyɛ saa a, na ne ho adwo no afiri ahehene no ho. Obi foforɔ nso bɛsen bio na wakyea no wɔ n'adwuma mu; na ɔde ne nsa bɛbɔ baabi a na ɛrehene no no. Sɛ ebia, sɛ ɛyɛ n’asre a, ɔbɛka sɛ, "Abaayewa biako pɛ no, wɔse n’asre te sɛ yei" (ɔbobɔ n’asre no mu na ɔtwitwi so). Ɔkwan yei so na ɔfaa so wiee afuo no dɔ. Afei ɔkɔeɛ sɛ ɔrekɔbɔ Nyankopɔ amanneɛ sɛ wawie n’afuo no dɔ. Nyankopɔn bisaa somafoɔ no sɛ, "Wawie ampa anaa?" Somafoɔ no see sɛ, "Aane." Nyankopɔn bisaa no sɛ, "Ɔtwee ne ho anaa?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Daabi, wantwe ne ho." Nyankopɔn faa Abena Nkroma de no maa Ananse sɛ ɔmware no. Anadwo baako bi, Ananse ne ne yere kɔdaeɛ, na ne yere no bisaa no sɛ, "Ɛyɛɛ dɛn na wo ankasa wo tumi dɔɔ agya afuo no so wira, afuo a obiara a ɔkɔɔ so no sanee n'akyi, nanso wo deɛ wo tumi dɔeɛ?" Ɛnna Ananse see sɛ, "Wosusu sɛ meyɛ kwasea anaa? Na metow dɔw, na sɛ obi biara retwam, na ɔbisa me sɛ, 'Ananse, afum a obiara ntumi ndɔw da yi na worebɔ yi anaa,' ɛnna mɛde me nsa abɔ me honam faako a ɛrekeka me no, na matitɔ so, na maka akyerɛ nipa no sɛ, ebi mpo a, wo srɛ te sɛ ɛkoɔ srɛ, na ɛyɛ fɛ na ɛhyerɛn. Ɛno na ɛmaa metumi dɔee." Ɛnna Abena Nkroma, ba a ɔtɔ so nkron no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛnde ɔkyena mɛka akyerɛ agya sɛ wokekaa wo ho." Ɛnna Ananse ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Mma nka, eyi yɛ kɛtɛ so asɛm." Abena Nkroma, ba a ɔtɔ so nkron no kaa sɛ, "Menni kɛtɛ so asɛm biara ho asɛm, na mɛka." Abena Nkroma faa ne kɛ kɛtɛ no firii (Ananse nkyɛn) kɔdaa dan no afa baako. Afei Ananse ani beree (kyerɛ sɛ, n'ani kumee), na ɔkɔfaa ne sankuo no, na ɔbɔɔ (ahoma no) too dwom sɛ:

Abena, ba a ɔtɔ so nkron no, yei nyɛ asɛm a ɛsɛ sɛ yɛko ho.

Momma yɛmfa nyɛ kɛtɛ so ahintasɛm.

'Daabi,' ɔse.

Ɔwɔ asɛm (tia me), nanso obi foforo wɔ asɛm a seesei koraa ɛrekɔ kwan so."

Ɛnna Ananse sɔre kɔdae. Bere a Ananse daa hɔ kakra no, ɛnna ɔsɔree. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abena Nkroma"—hwee annye (gai pɛpɛɛpɛ a ɛretwɛ twɛ nko ara) dinn! Ananse kaa sɛ, "Manya wo." Ɔfaa kora ketewa bi de nsuo hyɛɛ mu ma pɛpɛɛpɛ, na ɔde hwiee Abena Nkroma kɛtɛ a ɔda soɔ no so. Ɛnna Ananse kɔdae. Berɛ a ɔdae kakra no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Ko! Abena Nkroma, ɛdeɛn koraa ni? Woagugu kɛtɛ no so, wo a wonni aniwuo, ampa ara wonye koraa; adeɛ kye a, mɛka akyerɛ obiara. Ɛnneɛ na ɛyɛ nokware (deɛ na wɔn nyinaa reka no), sɛ obiara kɔ saa haban no mu a, na ɔtaa ka sɛ, 'Abaayewa a ɔgugu...! Merenkɔyɛ honhon afuo mma (onipa a ɔte saa).'" Ɛnna Abena ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Mesrɛ wo, gyae na ma asɛm no nto fam." Ananse kaa sɛ, "Merengyae, m'asɛm na edi kan; wokaa sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔka akyerɛ wo papa; mekaa sɛ, 'Gyae'; wokaa sɛ, 'Dabi.' Ɛno nti merengyae asɛm no." Na Abena, ba a ɔtɔ so nkron no kaa sɛ, "Gyae m'asɛm no, na w'asɛm nso a meka yoo, mede bɛto hɔ; efisɛ sɛ woangyae me deɛ a, m’ani bɛwu aniwuo nti.” Ɛnna Ananse see sɛ, “Mate, ɛfiri sɛ wo pɛ no saa no, ma ɛnyɛ kɛtɛ so asɛm, enti asɛm no to atwa wɔ hɔ.” Ɛno na ɛmaa mpaninfoɔ kãe sɛ, “Kɛtɛ so asɛm, yɛntĩ mu”.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, ebi nkɔ na ebi mmra.
