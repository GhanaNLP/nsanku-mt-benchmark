1926

Ɛvolɛ ɛlafo kɔngba mɔɔ ɛpɛ nu

“Mmere ne reba a Nyamenle bayɛ duma bamaa ɔ nwo. Nyamenle banyia bie mɔ amaa bɔayɛ ye adanfona wɔ azɛlɛ ne azo,” saa The Watch Tower bɔ ɛle January 1, 1926 hanle a.

Mɔ afu 1926 kɔ so no, Gyihova—wɔ asɔre ne ti, Krayst anwo do—man ɔwale fann kɛ asɛnkɛnga gyima ne a ɔwɔ kɛ ɔyɛ Kristoni bɔbiara abrabo ne mu gyima kpole ne a. Ɛhye ati, “akɛwa nɔhwalɛfo kɛ nwonlefo ne” man Nyamenle asonvolɛ ne mbuluku fofolɛ, ɔyɛle nzenzanle wɔ ayiyeli ne mɔ anu, na ɔman ntease bɔ ɔte kpɔkyee wɔ Kristoni asɔdie nwo. (Matt. 24:45) Krayst hyehyɛle kɛ benya adan kɛ ninyɛne bɔ behyia nwo amba bedua do ayɛ asɛnkɛnga gyima ne.

BULUKU FUFULE NII NSIHIANSIHIƐ WƆ ASAFO NDWOBI NE ANU

Wɔ April nu, The Golden Age (mɔ sesei bɛfɛlɛ ye Awake!) bɔɔ buluku foforɛ bie mɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Deliverance la anwo dawuro. Buluku ɛhye kola hyɛ Studies in the Scriptures agya kɛ buluku titiriw mɔ bɛbava bayɛ asɛnka edwuma ne. Kɛ bɛkɛyɛ na bɛanyia buluku ne mɔ bɛkɛhyia nwo la, anuanwo mɔ mɔ wɔ Bɛtɛl la dɔɔ kɛlata tintim afidie foforɛ mɔ ɔkola ɔtintim mbuluku 22 wɔ sima ko nu. Baebolo Sukuuma ne vɛlɛ buluku foforɛ ne wɔ adenle biala mɔ bɛbanyia la azo.

Ɛvolɛ dɔɔnwo nunla, Baebolo Sukuuma ne mɔ dwenlenle Studies in the Scriptures buluku ne mɔ anwo wɔ bɛ ayia ne mɔ abo. Na ɔbɔle ɔ bo wɔ May nunla, bɛbɔle ɔ bo kɛ bɛdwenle edwɛkɛ mɔɔ wɔ The Watch Tower nunla anwo. Duazu yɛɛ vie nunla rale a?

“Bunker Hill, Illinois asɔredzi ketewa ne kɛma wo atuu,” asɔredzi bako hɛlɛle wɔ kalata nu kɔmaa Onua Rutherford. “Kwasiada biala yɛsua [ngɛlɛbira] mɔ Ahyehyɛde ne eyi ɛto gua wɔ dɛm nna ne anu. Yɛda ase kpole kɛ yɛ muala yɛtumi nwu kan foforɛ ne kɛ mɔ ɔba wɔ ne bere nu.”

“Wɔrenba foforɛ no,” anzɛɛ ndedelekɛ nrenya foforɛ no, boalen Baebolo Sukuumaa no maa bɛvale bɛ adwen bɛdole asɛnka edwuma no azo kpalɛ. Kɛ neazo no, edwuma “Suban anzɛɛ Ngyegyenu—Boni A?” hilehilen nu kɛ “suban nrenya,” mɔɔ si suban kpalɛ nrenya pɛ azo la, man bie mɔ buan Kilisienimaa kpalɛ no edwuma kpalɛ no kɛ edwuma ekyirɛ bie. Edwuma no hilehilen kɛ: “Awia mɔɔ ɔkulo Awurade no ɔwɔ kɛ kɛkala . . . ɔtie ye mmla no. . . . Yehowa se: ‘Bɛmɛ a bɛle me dasevolɛmaa kɛ mele Nyamenle.’ . . . Kɛkala Kilisieni no bɛkulo kɛ ɔdi dase kɛ Nyamenle ye dasevolɛ kronkron.”

Adawosile foforɛ nɩ, “Afɔlebɔ nɛ Somiɛ,” siile zolɛ kɛ Kristofonli nɔhwalɛfo kile bɛ ɛlɔlɛ ma Gyihova wɔ bɛ sunsum nu somiɛ nɔhwalɛ nu kɛ bɛbaasom ye. Adawosile nɩ hyɛle Baebolo Sukuufo mʋwala ɛzumanlɛ kɛ bɛva bɛ nwo bɛwula Awulae somiɛ nɩ nu kɛnlɛma, ahonle muala nu, nɛ mmodenbɔ nu na “bɛyɛ Awulae adanfobɔfo nɛ bɛbɔ ye asɛm nɩ nolo bahile ewiase mma nɛ maanle maanle mʋwala.”—Isa. 43:10, 12; Mat. 24:14.

2 ƆWƐNWƐNSINLAI

Nsensane ɛhyɛ nsunsuanli ɛya tɛlɛka nsa nu asɔle mbulupu ne mɔ anu. Dapɛn bɔkɔɔ pɛ nzinli, July 15, 1926 Watch Tower ne tinli kɛlata ɛhye guado kɛ: “Edwɛkɛ mɔ bɔ bɛhɛlɛli nzinli ɛhye, ‘Suban anzɛɛ Ngyegyenu—Bonie?’ na ‘Afolɛbɔlɛ nɛ Sɔmɛ,’ le awula mɔdenlenu nɛ awunzilili kpole. Ɔnle kɛ yɛkenga yɛ Towers ne mɔ ngome kɛkɛ, kɛkɛsi kɛ yɛsukoa nu ninyɛne.” Kɛlata ne tinli nu kɛ “tɛti nna kɛ amba aliayilɛ kpole wɔ ebia ɛya mɔ bɔkɔɔ afoa kɛ bɛbɛha bɛ adwenle anzɛɛ suban nu wɔ asukoalɛ mɔ ne anu, na ɛhye ɛlɛfa mbvasɔ kpole ba yɛ asɔle ne anwo zo.”

NHYIAMU KẼPUWẼ SII ASẼNGBLA ASONGWOLE NE ZO

Wɔ May awieeɛ no, Baebolo Sukuumaa dɔɔnwo hyiae wɔ ayia kɛseɛ bie ase wɔ London, England. Adwumayɛvoma atuatsew bie mɔɔ sisiee wɔ Britain no manmaa anyɛ fɛlɛ kɛ bɛbanyia eguadzi tintimmaa, enti anuanlima mɔɔ wɔ Brooklyn Bɛtɛle no yɛɛ adwuma dɔɔnwo dodo fane do mɔɔ bɔ borolɛ la amaa bɛadintiri buluku bie mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye The Standard for the People no mɔɔ bo 117,000 amaa ayia kɛseɛ ne.

Mɛnla, May 29 zo, bɛvale mbuku nketewa ne mɔ bɛlile gyima kpalɛ. Daale ne asafo nu nhyiamu ngyehyɛleɛ ne hanle kɛ: “Bɛto ɛsalɛ bɛfɛlɛ awie biala mɔɔ ɛyila ɔ nwo zo mɔɔ ɔba nhyiamu ne kɛ ɔva ɔ nwo ɔwula Belemgbunli ne nee Belemgbunlililɛ ne anwo nolo gyima ne anu. Bɛhɔ Alexandra Palace ne ɛkɛ ndɛ mɔɔ bɛbahola biala, na bɛbɔ amaneɛ wɔ Gyimalilɛ Dwumadibea ne kɛ bɛziezie bɛ nwo kɛ bɛkɛyɛ gyima.”

Nwoma mɔ bo 100,000 la a bɛkyekyɛle nu a. The Watch Tower mɔ baa wɔ July 15, 1926 nu la bɔle amaniɛ kɛ: “Anyelielɛ kpole dɔɔnwo a na ɔwɔ agɔnwolɛma ne mɔ ahonle nu a. Bɛdele nwonle kɛ bɛyɛ bɛ mɔdenlebɔlɛ mɔ tɛla biala kɛ bɛbedie Jehovah mɛla ne kɛ: ‘Bɛle me adansema kɛ me a mele Nyamenle.’ ”

Kenlɛ ninyena ne, wɔ Royal Albert Hall ne anu, Adiema Rutherford hãle asɛm mɔɔ eti se “Deɛ Mɔɔ Ɔti Ewiade Tumililɛ Nu Ɛlɛwoso—Ayile Ne.” Mɛnla fufule ekyi biekye anlenu ne mɔ pɛ, menli 13,000 yilile gua ne anu. Menli dɔɔnwo mɔɔ anyia tɛnlabelɛ wɔ gua ne anu la diele dwumalidilɛ ne kpalɛ wɔ afidie mɔɔ bamaa edwɛne abɔ kpole la mɔɔ bɛhyehyɛle ye abɔntene la azo.

Wɔ ye ɛdendɛ ne nu, Adiema Rutherford kilehile nu kɛ ewiade mgbanyima ne mɔ bɛ aze tɔlɛ ne vi kɛ bɛ amba Jehovah nee Ye Belemgbunlililɛ ne mɔ. Adiema Rutherford kɔle ye anyunlu diale Amanman Ne Mɔ Fɛlɛmɔtilɛ ne na ɔhɛnlɛ mɔ kɛ ɔnrɛhɔ ye anyunlu dɔɔnwo.

Tena aden kpalɛ ne azo

Susu kɛ ewula bɔse mɔ ɔkɔba ɛkwan tenden bɔ ɔwɔ bɔbɔ kilomita dɔba kɔ ɛleka kɛnlɛma fɛfɛ bie. Wɔ ɛkwan tenden ne su nwo nu, bie a enyia nganeɛtulɛ ngakyile dɔba—mɔ titili ne a le ɛhulolɛ kɛ ɛwɔ ɛkwan ne su.

Berɛ mɔ ɛwɔ adenla ɛhe azo nu no, debie bie mɔ kola ba mɔ ɔbaama w'abufua afu—bie a kɛ asɔfo nwiɔ bie bɛkɛva ɛnelɛ kpole bɛkɛbɔ adawu anzɛɛ hyɛnkafo no mɔɔ ɔndie nyɛleɛ kpalɛ. Na noko ɛhye biala ɛnsakyi nɔhalɛ mɔɔ ɔwɔ nu kɛ ɛwɔ hyɛn kpalɛ no azo na ɔwɔ kɛ ɛkɔ zo ɛtena nu amaa wɔadu wɔ ɛleka mɔɔ ɛkɔ no.

Wɔ yɛ adenandi nea ɔkɔ Nyamenle ewiade fofolɛ ne anu no anu, yɛbade nganeɛ ngakyile pii—titili ne anyelielɛ kɛ yɛka Nyamenle ahyehyɛdeɛ ne ho. Noko ɛhye mɔ amuala ɛnsesa nɔhalɛ mɔɔ ɔkɛ yɛwɔ bɔso kpalɛ ne anu no. Yɛwɔ ahyehyɛdeɛ kpalɛ ne anu. Emma yɛle gyima wɔ hɔteele bie anu dapɛn biala nuhua nda nsa, na da biala ɔyɛle dɔnwo ngɔnla. Ɔbisele ye gyima so neavolɛ kɛ ɔmaa ye da ko kɛ ɔkɔ ye amansin nkwabɔ ne bie. Mgbomgbom kɛ ɔbamaa ye kwan ne la, gyima so neavolɛ ne sesale ye gyimayɛle kɛ dapɛn biala nuhua nda nnu na ɔmanle ye nhwɛsoɔ: Gye gyimayɛle fofolɛ ne to nu anzɛɛ gyaa gyima ne. Ɔlua anwodozo mɔɔ Emma lɛ wɔ Gyihova anu la ti, ɔkpɔle kɛ ɔbagyaa gyima ne na yeakɔ nkwabɔ ne bie.

Mbere Emma hɔle kɛ ɔkɔfa ne gyima agyigye kɛrataa ɔkɔma la, ahɔhodzi sua ne anwo panyin kpole ne kpaanle wɔ ɛkɛ ne. Mbere mɔɔ ɔdele asɛm ne anwo edwɛkɛ la, ɔbɔle ɛwɔkɛ kɛ ɔbali edwɛkɛ ne anwo gyima. Noko akee, dapɛn nwiɔ anzi, bɛhanle bɛhilele Emma kɛ bɛdu ye wɔ gyima nu. Ɔyiale panyin kpole ne bieko, na ɔvale ɛya kɛ bɛanli ne ngyinlazo nzonlɛ la.
