Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka yi yɛ nokware)

NEA ENTII NA ATURUKUKU, ABURUBURU NO, KƆƆ WIRAM, NA ƆMAA AKOKƆ NO BAA EFIE

AKOKO ne Aturukuku firi agya baako ne ɛna baako. Afei Akoko ka kyerɛɛ Aturukuku sɛ, “Kɔ Sare-so-man mu, na me nso mɛkɔ Kwae-brentuo-mu-man mu, na sɛ wonya aduane a, ɛnneɛ bɛbɔ me amanneɛ.” Afei wɔtetee mu. Mfeɛ mmiɛnsa na Aturukuku anhunu ne nuabaa no. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne kunu sɛ, “Ka me ho kɔ, efisɛ merekɔhwehwɛ me nuabaa.” Ɛnna ne kunu kaa sɛ, “Sɛ woaka a, mate, kɔ na ma me nka wo ho.” Na ɔne no tuu kwan kɔduruu kuro bi akyi (faako a ne nuabaa te). Wɔhunuu dɔteɛ bi a wɔaboa ano sɛ bepɔw wɔ hɔ, na ɛhɔ na ɔne ne nuabaa hyiae. Na Akokɔbedie no bisaa Aborɔnoma no sɛ, “Bere a wokɔɔ Sare-so-man no mu no, wonyaa aduane bi diiɛ?” Ɔno nso buei sɛ, “Baabi a mekɔeɛ no, na efomwisa nko ara na ɛwɔ hɔ; na bere a mewoo me mma, bɛyɛ nsia, na wɔbataa me ho, na bere a wɔsoosoo sɛ sei (ɔde kyerɛɛ sɛdeɛ wɔn kɛseɛ teɛ) no, mede wɔn bɛn me, na ɔbɔfoɔ bi totow n'atuo, pom! na wɔkunkum me mma, na sɛ anka manya nyansa a, anka wɔakum me nso.” Bere a ɔkasa wiei no, Akokɔbere no see sɛ, “Baabi a mekɔe no, amonom hɔ ara na mebɛyɛɛ obi a ɔho hia paa.” Aturukuku bisaa no sɛ, “Kyerɛ me nea wopɛ sɛ wuka wɔ wo ‘obi a ɔho hia paa’ no ho.” Ɛnna Akokɔbere no see sɛ, “Nea ‘obi a ɔho hia paa’ kyerɛ ne sɛ, sɛ ebia, sɛ me wura sɛw kɛtɛ fɛfɛ bi a, t ɛnna mekɔda so, na me bae gu so, na sɛ me wura fa nam sɛ ɔde regu nkwan no mu a, ɛnna meyi deɛ mepɛ; aduane biara a me wura bɛdi no, mekɔfa bi, na sɛ medi no saa a, me wura ntumi mfa n’etuo anaa poma mmɔ me. Ɛno nti na mese meyɛ nipa a me ho hia ampa.” Akokɔ no kaa sɛ, “Faako a wote hɔ no, ɛnyɛ baabi pa, enti Memeneda a ɛbɛdi Kwasida Adae anim no, fa wo mma, ne wo kunu bra, na ma wɔn nyinaa mmɛtena yɛn fie.” Aturukuku kaa sɛ, “Mate, mɛkɔ, na Memeneda a ɛbɛdi Kwasida Adae anim no, na maba.” Afei ɔsɔfo bi baa Akokɔ no kuro mu, rehoso bɔ ne dɔn nketewa no. Wɔbisaa no sɛ, “Nana, ɛdeɛn na asie?” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Dente se moama kɔm ade no mfeɛ mmienu, enti monhɔ nka nkyerɛ ɔhene no sɛ ɔmfa nkokɔ ne nkesua dummienu mmra.” Afei Ɔhene no kɔkyeree nkokɔ nnan, de nkesua dummienu baeɛ, faa ɛtoɔ yɛeɛ, na ɔkɔde maa abosom Dente wɔ ne dɔteɛ afɔrebukyia kɔntɔnkrɔn no so, twitwaa nkokɔ nnan no ti na p de wɔn guu hɔ, na ɔfaa wɔn ntɛkrɛma ne wɔn ti, ne wɔn nan, de taree dɔte afɔrebukyia no nkyɛn; na ɔde ɛtɔ a wɔayam no nso taree ho. Afei, da a edi Adae anim no, na Aturukuku rekɔhyia ne nuabea. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhwee Dente afɔrebukyia kronkron no ho, na ɔhunuu ne nuabea ntɛkrɛma, ne ne ti, ne nan, ne ntaban, ne bɔbrɛ, ne ne nsono. Aturukuku kaa sɛ, "Nea aba yi nyinaa akyi no, merentumi nkɔ hɔ bio da." Ɛnna Aburuburo tu kɔe na ɔkɔsii onyaa dua bi so, na ɔkaa (too dwom) sɛ: "Mesu me nuabea o, Awerɛhoo o.

Wo, Akokɔbedeɛ, a na woyɛ obi a ɔwɔ dibea bi, yei ne wo ti?

Wo, Akokɔbedeɛ, a na woyɛ obi a ɔwɔ dibea bi, yeinom ne wo ntaban no?

Wo, Akokɔbedeɛ, a na woyɛ nipa kɛseɛ no, yeinom ne wo nan no?

Wo, Akokɔbere, a na woyɛ obi a wosom boɔ, wei ne w'akoma anaa?

Wo, Akokɔbedeɛ, a na woyɛ obi a ɔwɔ dibea bi, yeinom ne wo nsono? "

Na Aturukuku kaa sɛ, "Merenkɔ Kwaeɛ asase so bio da, efisɛ kwaeɛ mu nkorɔfoɔ no nyɛ." Ɛno nti na Aturukuku kɔtenaa Serɛm asase so, berɛ a Akokɔ no nso tenaa fie no.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, ebi nkɔ na ebi mmra.
