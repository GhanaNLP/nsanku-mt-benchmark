Yɛnni aseɛ koraa, yɛnni aseɛ koraa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokorɛ)

DEƐ ƐYƐEƐ A ATURUKUKU, ABORƆNOMA NO, KƆƆ WIRAM, NA ƆMAA AKƆKO BAA EFIE

AKOKO, Akokɔ no, ne Aturukuku, firi agya baako na wɔfiri ɛna baako. Afei Akokɔ ka kyerɛɛ Aturukuku sɛ, “Kɔ Sare-so-man mu, na me nso, mɛkɔ Kwae-birentuo-man mu, na sɛ wonya aduane a, ɛnde bra bɛka kyerɛ me.” Ɛnna wɔtetew mu. Mfeɛ mmiɛnsa Aturukuku anhunu ne nuabaa. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne kunu sɛ, “Ka me ho, efisɛ merekɔhwehwɛ me nuabaa.” Ɛnna ne kunu kaa sɛ, “Sɛ woakasa a, mate, kɔ na ma me nka wo ho.” Na ɔno ne no tuu kwan kɔduruu kuro no ano (faako a ne nuabaa teɛ). Wɔhunuu dɔteɛ puee bi wɔ hɔ, na ɛhɔ na ɔno ne ne nuabaa hyiaeɛ. Na Akokɔ bisaa Aturukuku sɛ, “Berɛ a wokɔɔ Sare-so-man mu no, wonyaa aduane diiɛ?” Ɔbuaa sɛ, “Faako a mekɔeɛ no, na efomwisa nko ara na ɛwɔ hɔ; na berɛ a memaa me mma pɔtee, bɛyɛ nsia, na wɔbataa me ho, na berɛ a wɔyɛɛ akɛseɛ sɛ sei (ɔkyerɛɛ sɛdeɛ wɔn kɛseyɛ teɛ), medaa wɔn mfaŋŋ, na ɔbɔfoɔ bi tuo tuo, pom! na ɔkumm me mma, na sɛ me nso mannim nyansa a, anka wɔbɛku me nso.” Berɛ a ɔwiee kasa no, Akokɔ kaa sɛ, “Faako a mekɔeɛ no, mebɛyɛɛ obi a ɔwɔ dibea kɛse ampa ara prɛko pɛ.” Aturukuku bisaa no sɛ, “Ka deɛ wopɛ sɛ wukyerɛ wɔ wo ‘obi a ɔwɔ dibea kɛse’ no mu kyerɛ me.” Ɛnna Akokɔ kaa sɛ, “Nea ‘obi a ɔwɔ dibea kɛse’ kyerɛ ne sɛ, sɛ ebia me wura sɛw kɛtɛ pa bi a, ɛnde mekɔda so, na me dɔeɛ tɔ so, na sɛ me wura fa nam sɛ ɔde regu nkwan-nkwan mu a, ɛnde meyi deɛ mepɛ; aduane biara a me wura bɛdi no, mekɔfa bi, na sɛ medi saa a, me wura ntumi mfa ne tuo anaa poma mmɔ me. Ɛno nti na mereka sɛ meyɛ obi a ɔwɔ dibea kɛse no.” Akokɔ kaa sɛ, “Faako a wote hɔ no, ɛnyɛ faako a ɛyɛ, enti Memeneda a ɛdi Kwasida Adae anim no, fa wo mma, ne wo kunu bra, na ma wɔn nyinaa mmɛtena yɛn fie.” Aturukuku kaa sɛ, “Mate, mɛkɔ, na Memeneda a ɛdi Kwasida Adae anim no, mɛba.” Afei ɔkɔmfoɔ bi baa Akokɔ akuraa hɔ, a na ɔrewoso ne nfidie nkumaa. Wɔbisaa no sɛ, “Nana, ɛdeɛn na ɛrekɔ so?” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Dente se moama kɔm ade no mfeɛ mmienu, enti monkɔ Ɔhene nkyɛn nkɔka nkyerɛ no sɛ ɔmfa nkokɔ ne nkesua dummienu mmra.” Afei Ɔhene kɔkyeree nkokɔ nnan, de nkesua dummienu baeɛ, ɔfaa ɛtoɔ wuiɛ, na ɔkɔde maa abosom Dente wɔ ne dɔteɛ afɔrebukyia kurukuruwa no so, twitwaa nkokɔ nnan no ti de guu hɔ, na ɔfaa wɔn ntɛkrɛma ne wɔn ti, ne wɔn nan, de nɔkɔɔ dɔteɛ afɔrebukyia no nkyɛn; na ɔde ɛtoɔ a wanya no nso taree ho. Afei, da a ɛdi Adae anim no, na Aturukuku rekɔhyia ne nuabaa. Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu ne nuabaa ntɛkrɛma, ne ne ti, ne ne nan, ne ne ntaban, ne ne brɛboɔ, ne ne nsono wɔ afɔrebukyia a wɔde ama Dente no so. Aturukuku kaa sɛ, “Yeinom nyinaa akyi no, merentumi nkɔ hɔ bio koraa.” Na Aburuburo tu kɔtenaa onyina so, na ɔkaa (too dwom) sɛ: "Mesu me nuabaa o, Awerɛhoɔ o."

Wo, Akɔmfɛm/Akoko (Hen = Akoko), a na woyɛ obi a ɔwɔ tumi bi no, w'akoma/w'asepatere (liver = bɔbrɛ) ni? Hen = Akokɔberɛ / Akokɔ. "You, Hen, who were a person of some importance, is this your liver?" "Wo, Akokɔ, a na woyɛ obi a ɔsom bo/obi kɛseɛ no, wo bɔbrɛ nie?" Liver in Twi = bɔbrɛ. Hen = Akokɔ (or Akokɔberɛ). You, Hen = Wo, Akokɔ, who were a person of some importance = a na woyɛ nipa kɛseɛ bi / obi a ɔsom bo bi, is this your liver? = wo bɔbrɛ nie? / yei ne wo bɔbrɛ?Wo, Akokɔ, a na woyɛ obi a ɔsom bo no, wo bɔbrɛ nie?

Wo, Akoko, a na woyɛ obi a ɔho hia no, w’ayamdeɛ nie? "

Na Aturukuku kaa sɛ, "Merenkɔ Kwaeɛ asase so bio da, efisɛ kwaeɛ mu nkorɔfoɔ nyɛ." Ɛno nti na Aturukuku kɔtenaa Ɛserɛ-tenten-asase so, berɛ a Akɔkɔ nso tenaa fie no.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, ebi nkɔ na ebi mmra.
