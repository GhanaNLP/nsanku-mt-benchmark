‘Let the Peace That Jesus Christ Gives Rule in Your Hearts’

Colossians 3:15 speaks about 'the peace that Jesus Christ gives us.' What is 'the peace that Jesus Christ gives us'? It is a peace that someone gains when they become a follower of Christ. We gain that peace when we come to know that Jehovah and his Son love us.—John 14:23, 27.

Apostle Paul encouraged the Christians who were in the Colossian congregation to let that peace rule in their hearts or within them. In what way? The Greek word "rule" that Paul used there was applied to someone who acted as an umpire or referee in a contest. Just as a referee has authority over a game, so also we must let ‘the peace that Jesus Christ gives us’ have control over our lives.

For example, if a disagreement arises between us and a fellow Christian, we must let ‘the peace of Christ’ rule over us. If we do so, it will help us to know what to do so that peace and unity will prevail between us and our brothers. To be able to do this, sometimes we have to sacrifice our preferences or forgive from the heart. This will show that we have let ‘the peace of Christ’ rule in our hearts.—Col. 3:12-14.
