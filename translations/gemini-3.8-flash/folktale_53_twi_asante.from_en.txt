Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐDEƐ EKUONA ABUSUA NO YƐƐ DE BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU

ƐBERƐ bi na ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ a wɔfrɛ no Abena; sɛ ne maame ne ne papa fa no sɛ wɔde no mereka awareɛ (ama obiara) a, ɔka sɛ, “Dabi koraa.” Na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne papa sɛ, “Da a mɛhunu ɔbarima a medɔ no no, saa da no, sɛ meba na sɛ woretwe asra a, mɛfa w’asratoa ade wo (ano) wɔ wo ti atifi.” Afei, aboa no, Ɛkoɔ no, tee asɛm yi, ɔdan danee ne ho onipa, na ɔtuu kwan, na ɔbaa ɛhɔ wɔ ɔbaa no akuraa ase. Afei, berɛ a Abena hunuu no no, ɔtuu mmirika, kɔɔ baabi a ne papa rete asrannaa. Ɔgyee asrannaatoa no fii ne nsam, na ɔde bɔɔ ne ti so. Na saa da no yɛ anigyeɛ da maa akuraafoɔ no, na wɔkaa sɛ, “Yɛn wura ba no ahunu deɛ ne kɔn dɔ no.” Ɛnna wɔkɔfaa ɔbarima no baa fie hɔ. Na ababaawa no kaa sɛ, “Oyi ne deɛ mepɛ no.” Ɔbarima no nso pɛɛ no saa ara, na ɔwaree no. Ɔdii nna mmiɛnsa wɔ hɔ, na afei ɔkaa sɛ ɔrekɔ ne kuro mu, na ɔbaa no nso kaa sɛ ɔne no bɛkɔ. Enti Ɔhemaa no siesiee ne nneɛma nyinaa (maa akwantuo no) na (ɔmaa no) nnipa aduonu nan sɛ ɔne wɔn nkɔ. Afei wɔsii mu, na wɔduruu akuraa bi kwan so, na akokɔbini suii. Nnipa no kaa sɛ, "Yɛaduru fie anaa?" Ɔbea no kunu kaa sɛ, "Maware yere foforɔ, na ɛsɛ sɛ medi kan de no kɔ me fie, na wasiesie ade nyinaa ansa na wakɔkyia nnipa nyinaa." Enti wɔsii mu kɔe. Wɔduruu kwaeɛ mu petee bi so. Ɛberɛ a wɔduruu kwaeɛ no mu petee no mu no, ɔbarima no gyinaa nkyɛn kwan bi; ɔkaa sɛ, “Abena, seesei mmerɛ no aduru a ɛsɛ sɛ meka nea meteɛ pa ara kyerɛ wo.” Abena bisaa sɛ, “Wote sɛn?” Ɔbarima no kaa sɛ, “‘Meyɛ ‘pa!’ na manya ayɛ ‘gyiren!’;’” na wadane ayɛ ɛkoɔ. Nnipa no nyinaa kaa sɛ, “E! e, ɔse dɛn, ɔse ɔnyɛ ‘pa!’ na ɔnyɛ ‘gyiren!’” (Wowiee saa nsɛm yi keka pɛ na) wɔn nyinaa dan bɛyɛɛ ɛkoɔ. Ɔbarima baako na ɔkaa hɔ a ɔdwanee kɔɔ fie. Na ɔkɔɔ kuraa no so Panyin nkyɛn kɔkaakaa deɛ wahunu nyinaa kyerɛɛ no. Ohene ne Ɔhemmaa frɔɔ ahyiaeɛ; ɔno (deɛ ɔdwaneeɛ no) bɔɔ wɔn deɛ ɛsiiɛ wɔ wɔn kwan so no ho amanneɛ, na berɛ a ɔduruu faako a ɔkaa sɛ, “Ɔyɛɛ ‘pa!’ sɛ ɔbɛyɛ ‘gyiren!’,” no, ɔno nso danee ɛkoɔ. Berɛ biara a wɔn mu baako bɛpɛ sɛ ɔka saa nsɛm yi bio no, na wadane ɛkoɔ. Nkurɔfoɔ a wɔwɔ akuraa no ase nyinaa danee ɛkoɔ, gye aberewa baako pɛ. Bere bi a nneɛma yi sii akyi no, oduyɛfoɔ bi baa akuraa no ase. Ɔteɛɛm sɛ, “Aane! kuro kɛseɛ a nkurɔfoɔ biara nni mu?” Dinn! (Hwee annye so gye kɔtɛbɛtɛbɛ nko ara nne.) Ɔkasae bio. Akyire yi aberewa no kaa sɛ, “Mewɔ ha.” Ɔdunsini no bisaa sɛ, “Amanneɛ bɛn; na adɛn nti na wowɔ kuro yi mu nkutoo?” Aberewa no buaa sɛ, “Ebia na woaba ha deɛ, (nanso mempɛ sɛ me ne wo nya biribiara yɛ), kɔ na gyae me ma mentena me baabi kɛkɛ.” Ɔdunsini no kaa sɛ, “Woyɛ ɔbayifoɔ; ɛyɛ wo na woawe kuro yi mufoɔ nyinaa, enti, gye sɛ wok kasa a, mekum wo ntɛm ara.” Aberewa no kaa sɛ, “Sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mekasa a, (ne kwan so no) kɔfa ahahamono bɛkyekyere me, na fa me kɔkyekyere gyedua no ho.” Ɔdunsini no bae bɛkyekyeree no, na ɔde no kɔkyekyeree gyedua no ho. Afei aberewa no kaa nea esii nyinaa kyerɛɛ no, nanso bere a ɔduruu faako a ɛsɛ sɛ ɔka sɛ, "Ɔkaa sɛ ɔbɛyɛ 'pa!' na ama wayɛ gyiren!'" no, ahoma no tei; aberewa no adan ɛkoɔ na watwa ne ho afiri hɔ kɔ. Ɛnna ɔdunsini no kaa sɛ, “Afei deɛ, mahunu sɛ ɛnyɛ aberewa no na ɔkunkum nkurofoɔ yi nyinaa.” Afei ɔkɔtetee aduro (ahaban) guu yaawa bi mu potɔeɛ, na ɔhwee nsuo guu mu; na ɔfaa ne bodoa bɔɔ mu; afei ɔmaa n’asuafoɔ no soaa (yaawa no) maa no. Ɔtuu kwan kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu. Ɔhunuu ɛkoɔ pii sɛ wɔwogyinagyina hɔ, na ɔde ne dua kɔdɔɔ aduro no mu, na ɔpete guu wɔn so, na wɔsesaa bɛyɛɛ nnipa bio. Na ɛkaa wɔn a wɔyɛ ɛkoɔ ampa no nko ara wɔ hɔ. Na nnipa no san baa fie. Ɛno na ɛmaa Ekuona abusua no baa abusuakuo no mu; ɛfiri saa awareɛ a Abena kɔwareeɛ no mu na ɛfiri baeɛ.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
