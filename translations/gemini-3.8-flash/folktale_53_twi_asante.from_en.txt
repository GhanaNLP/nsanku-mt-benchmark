Yɛnni aseɛ koraa, yɛnni aseɛ koraa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokorɛ)

SƐNEA EKUONA ABUSUA NO BƐBAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU

ƐBERƐ bi na ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ a wɔfrɛ no Abena; sɛ ne maame ne ne papa fa no sɛ wɔde no bɛma awareɛ (ama obiara) a, ɔka sɛ, “Ɛrenkɔso da.” Na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne papa sɛ, “Da a mɛhunu barima a medɔ no no, saa da no, sɛ meba na sɛ woretwe asrɛ a, mɛfa w’asrowa na mede abɔ wo tipɛn so.” Afei aboa Ɛkoɔ tee asɛm yi, ɔdanee ne ho barima, na ɔtuu kwan, baa ɔbaa no kuro mu hɔ. Afei, berɛ a Abena hunuu no no, ɔtuu mmirika, kɔɔ baabi a ne papa retwe asrɛ no. Ɔgyee asrowa no firii ne nsam, na ɔde bɔɔ ne ti so. Na saa da no yɛ anigyeɛ da wɔ nkurofoɔ no ntam, a wɔkaa sɛ, “Yɛn wura ba ahunu deɛ ɔpɛ no.” Ɛnna wɔkɔe kɔfaa barima no baa fie hɔ. Na abaayewa no kaa sɛ, “Oyi na mepɛ no.” Barima no nso pɛɛ saa, na ɔwaree no. Ɔdii nnafua mmiɛnsa wɔ hɔ, na afei ɔkaa sɛ ɔrekɔ ne kuro mu, na ɔbaa no nso kaa sɛ ɔne no bɛkɔ. Enti Ɔhemaa no siesiee ne nnoɔma nyinaa (maa akwantuo no) na (ɔmaa no) nnipa aduonu nan sɛ ɔne wɔn nkɔ. Ɛnna wɔtuu kwan, na wɔduruu kuro bi kurotia, na akokɔbrenyane suee. Nnipa no kaa sɛ, “Yɛaduru fie anaa?” Ɔbaa no kunu kaa sɛ, “Maware yere foforɔ, na ɛsɛ sɛ medi kan de no kɔ me fie, ama wasiesie biribiara pɛpɛɛpɛ, ansa na wakɔkyia nnipa nyinaa.” Enti wɔtuu kwan na wɔkɔe. Wɔduruu kwaeɛ mu petee bi so. Berɛ a wɔduruu saa kwaeɛ mu petee no so no, barima no gyinaa nkyɛn; ɔkaa sɛ, “Abena, mmerɛ no aduru seesei a ɛsɛ sɛ meka deɛ meyɛ ankasa kyerɛ wo.” Abena kaa sɛ, “Sɛ woyɛ deɛn?” Barima no kaa sɛ, “‘Meyɛ ‘pa!’ ama mayɛ ‘gyiren!’;’” na wadane ɛkoɔ. Nnipa no nyinaa kaa sɛ, “E! e, ɛdeɛn na ɔreka, ɔse ɔyɛ ‘pa!’ ama wayɛ ‘gyiren!’” (Wɔanka saa nsɛm yi anwie koraa na) wɔn nyinaa danee ɛkoɔ. Ɛkaa barima baako a ɔtuu mmirika baa fie. Na ɔkɔ kaa deɛ wahunu kyerɛɛ kuro no mu Panyin. Kuro no mu Panyin ne Ɔhemaa no frɛɛ nhyiamu; ɔno (deɛ ɔdwanee no) bɔɔ wɔn amanneɛ wɔ deɛ ɛsii berɛ a wɔrekɔ no, na berɛ a ɔduruu faako a ɔkaa sɛ, “Ɔyɛɛ ‘pa!’ ama wayɛ ‘gyiren!’” no, ɔno nso danee ɛkoɔ. Berɛ biara a wɔn mu baako bɛyɛ sɛ ɔbɛtĩ saa nsɛm yi mu no, na wadane ɛkoɔ. Kuro no mufoɔ nyinaa danee ɛkoɔ, gye ɔbaa panyin kɔkɔɔ baako pɛ. Bere bi akyi wɔ nnoɔma yi akyi no, ɔdunsinni bi baa kuro no mu. Ɔtwee n’adwene kaa sɛ, “Aane! kuro kɛseɛ a nkurɔfoɔ biara nni mu yi?” Dinn! (Asɛm biara ankyere no gye sɛ kɔtɛbire nne na ɛteɛ.) Ɔkasaa bio. Akyire yi akwakoraa no kaa sɛ, “Mewɔ ha.” Ɔdunsinni no bisaa sɛ, “Amanneɛ bɛn; na adɛn nti na wo nko ara te kuro yi mu?” Ɔbaa bɔnefoɔ no buaa sɛ, “Ebia na woaba ha deɛ, (nanso me ne wo nni asɛm biara), kɔ na gyae me ma mentena me baabi.” Ɔdunsinni no kaa sɛ, “Woyɛ ɔbayifoɔ; ɛyɛ wo na woadi nkurɔfoɔ a wɔwɔ kuro yi mu nyinaa, enti, gye sɛ woka, mɛku wo amonom hɔ ara.” Ɔbaa kɔkoraa no kaa sɛ, “Sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meka asɛm a, (dikan) kɔkɔfa ahoma kɔbɔ me, na fa me bɔ gyedua no ho.” Ɔdunsinni no baeɛ bɛkyekyere no, na ɔde no bɔɔ gyedua no ho. Afei ɔbaa kɔkoraa no kyerɛɛ no deɛ ɛsisiiɛ nyinaa, nanso bere a ɔduruu baabi a ɛsɛ sɛ ɔka sɛ, “Ɔkaa sɛ ɔbɛyɛ ‘pa!’ na ama wayɛ ‘gyiren!’” ahoma no tetee; ɔbaa kɔkoraa no adane ɛkoɔ na watwa kɔ. Afei ɔdunsinni no kaa sɛ, “Afei medeɛ, mahunu sɛ ɛnyɛ ɔbaa kɔkoraa no na ɔkum kuro mma yi nyinaa.” Afei ɔkɔtetee aduro (nhahan) na ɔkyiyii guu yaawa mu, na ɔguu nsuo guu mu; na ɔfaa ne dua (suman) bɔɔ mu; afei ɔmaa ne nkoa soaeɛ. Ɔtuu kwan kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu. Ɔhunuu ɛkoɔ pii sɛ wɔsisagyina hɔ, na ɔde ne dua no bɔɔ aduro no mu, na ɔpetee wɔn so, na wɔdane nkurɔfoɔ bio. Na deɛ wɔkaa hɔ no ara ne ɛkoɔ ankasa no. Na nkurɔfoɔ no san baa fie. Saa na ɛkwan a Ekuona abusuakuo no faa so baa kuro no mu; ɛfiri awareɛ a Abena kɔɔ mu no mu na ɛfiri baeɛ.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
