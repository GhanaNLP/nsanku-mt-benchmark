“Gibeon noba da maale la nooreyeni ne Israel a kyɛ la be ba sʋŋ.”—JOSH. 10:1.

Jehoova e la a lɛ baaba a Israel deme naŋ da ba bɔ o noɔre sɛre ba naŋ mɛ a kootaa na. O leɛ soŋ la a Israel deme ka ba tɔgle ba noɔre kpeɛbo kyɛ de kpeɛo faa a Gibeon deme.—Josh. 9:26; 11:19.

ZANNOO NYƆRE Maale Nembɛrɛ Yeli-mannebie

Te lantaapuori nembannuu poɔ, te taa la kpeɛoŋ ka te de te noore yeli eŋ te yɔɔmine mine faŋa. (Heb. 10:24, 25) Wala ka te na baŋ maale noore yeli naŋ na maŋ tɔɔ eŋ noba mine faŋa velaa?

• Puori Jehoova. Sɔre o ka o soŋ bo ka foo leɛre yeli ka a wulu o tɛgɛŋ kyɛ meŋ kpeɛrɛ nembɔ.—Ym. 35:18.

• Maale fo beeŋe ka fo baŋ e yaga gaŋ a gane na kannoo yoŋ. Ka fo wa leɛre a yɛlɛ fo menga noɔre poɔ, noba mine na nyɛ la tɔnɔ saŋa na ba naŋ woŋ fo sagediibu yelbie.

• Vɛŋ ka fooŋ na manne neŋ a yele ta zore. Mana naŋ e kpeɛŋaa ane ŋmaaŋmaa la maŋ sonno yaga ka te maŋ nyɛ dɛmo ka lɛ meŋ vɛŋɛɛ ka noba yaga tõɔ manne a laŋkpeɛbo poɔŋ.—Prov. 10:19.

• Vɛŋ ka foo noɔreyeeloŋ e soŋ. Ta de foo noɔreyeeloŋ a ku saglaa bee a ŋmɛ kpeɛoŋ ko a lantaapuuro bee Kristabie tɔ.

Hali ba yaa ba boɔlɔ fo ka fo leɛre gba, fo na baŋ maaleŋ soŋ la a mine nyɔvore. Wuli tɛgɛteŋɛ, kyɛ kaara ba leɛroŋ poɔ sonnoŋ.—Prov. 10:21.

A gane ŋa ba koɔrɔ. Ba maale o la ka o soŋ a teŋɛ zaa Baabole zannoo toma naŋ tona ne nembɔnne yɔɔrɔ. Ka fo bɔkɔ ka fo soŋ, kyɛ gaa donate.jw.org.

Ka ba ba yeli ka a yi zie bamaamine poɔ, a Baabole yelyaga zaa yi la a pampana kɔkɔre New World Translation of the Holy Scriptures poɔŋ.
