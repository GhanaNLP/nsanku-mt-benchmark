5 | Ghana Nɔyeli - 2022 Ma Bi Ɔmɛ A Ga Womi

Ma nɛ ma hi si kɛ ya nɛ jua yeli lɛ e nɔ: Sika he blɔ nya tomi kɛ ní tsumi he blɔhi a hami

HƐMI MUNYU

COVID-19 hio gbalami nɛ kpata níhi a hɛ mi ɔ he ha nɛ hiɔ nɔ́ nu he ngɛ mahi tsuo a sikasɛtsɛm he, kɛ Ghana ma a hu piɛɛ he. Nɔ́ nɛ Ghana bihi nu he kɛ gu wamihi, wami nina níhi kɛ jatsɛ yemihi a hɛ mi kpami mi ɔ, nɔyemi ɔ li nɛ ɔ tsuo he munyu. E ngɔɔ i he nɛ ma de ke wa blɔ nɛ wa gu nɔ kɛ tsu hio gbalami ɔ nya níhi a he ní ɔ da wawɛɛ nɛ e kɔ kpelekepe nɛ je ɔ tsuo ngɛ e he ngmlaa jee. Nɔyemi ɔ hu to mlaahi kɛ fĩ jatsɛ yemihi a se konɛ a kpale ba e he nɔ, nɛ e ngɔ wa wami kpale ba si himi kpakpa nɛ da mi ekko.

Kɛ fĩ se ɔ, kɛ ya ha nyagbahi kɛ he blɔhi nɛ je hiɔ kpe ɔ mi kɛ ba a he blɔ nya a, Nɔyeli ɔ, ngɛ H.E. President Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo yi jemi nitsumnɔ hewahi a sisi ɔ, mla kɛ to GH¢100 billion COVID-19 Alleviation and Revitalisation of Enterprises ga womi nitsumi, (GhanaCARES “Obaatan Pa” ga womi nitsumi ɔ) he blɔ. Enɛ ɔ ha amlɔ nɔuu kɛ he jɔmi ngua kɛ ha wa ma bi ɔmɛ nɛ e ngɛ wa ma a hɛ mi nyɛɛmi wami he kpe kpoe ekɔ.

Wa buɔ hemi kɛ yemi nɛ nyɛ ngɛ ngɛ Nɔyemi ɔ mi ɔ he akɔnta, benɛ wa ngɛ ní tsue konɛ wa he nɛ wa si ekko ngɛ gbalahi nɛ gbeɔ nimli nɛ ɔ mi. Be nɛ ɔ tsuo mi ɔ, wa nyɛmi nɛ wa kɛ ma nyɛ ma hi si kɛ ya nɛ kpe se kaa ma a kpe kahemi. Be fɛɛ be ɔ, Baiblo ɔ haa blɔtsɔɔmi nɛ he hia ngɛ behi kaa nɛ ɔmɛ a mi. Titli ɔ, kaa bɔ nɛ a tsɔɔ ngɛ Yesaya 9:10 mi ɔ ke, “A ku tɛ hiɔhi ɔmɛ, se wa maa ma kɛ tɛ nɛ a kpa hehi; a gbo sikamoo tso ɔmɛ a nɔ, se wa maa to seda tsohi a nane mi”. Ngɛ jamɛ a blɔ nɔ nɔuu ɔ, Nɔyemi ɔ hu fia e pɛɛ si kaa e maa pee nɔ nɛ e wo he si ɔ. Enɛ ɔ he je ɔ, wa maa ya nɔ maa pee mlaahi kɛ nitsumiji nɛ ma ha nɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ nɛ e laa a nɛ kpale kɛ ba, kɛ wami si himi nɛ da ha ma a mi bi tsuo.

Yi mi tomi titihi nɛ ngɛ 2022 Hwɔɔse Yisɔ nɔ hyɛmi ní ɔ mi, nɛ ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ kɛ ha jeha etɛ (3) nɛ ma a, ji kaa a ma ma Matsɛ Yemi nɛ ngɛ he wami nɛ kɔɔ Tsɔsemi he nɛ ma nyɛ ma ha sikasitsɛmí he hewami nɛ ma he shi, nɛ ma hi si daa, nɛ ma kpa he wulaa konɛ e ha ní tsumi kpakpa kɛ ha nɔ fɛɛ nɔ, titli ɔ Nihewi kɛ Yihewi ɔmɛ. Lɔ ɔ he ɔ, nɔ yemi mlaahi maa da si kɛ fĩ nihewi kɛ yihewi ɔmɛ a se konɛ a kɛ a ní lemihi nɛ tsu ní nɛ a je a he wami kpo kɛ ha helo nya kɛ mumi mi he jɔɔmi kɛ gu he blɔ nɛ sɔ kɛ ha nɔ fɛɛ nɔ mi.

Kɛ ha nɛ a ma wo nihewi kɛ yihewi a bua nɛ a ma tsu jula tsumi he nile he ní ɔ, Nɔyeli ɔ maa tsu YouStart blɔ nya tomi ɔ he ní ngɛ jeha 2022 mi kɛ ha nihewi kɛ yihewi nɛ a tsuɔ jula nɛ a ná sika nɛ a kɛ to jula alo a kɛ hɛɛ jula nɛ a ngɛ tsuɔ kɛ ya hɛ mi.

Ngɛ jeha 2022 Sika Tsumi Gbɛjianɔto ɔ mi ɔ, wa to ga kɛ ha bɔ nɛ wa ma plɛ kɛ ya nɔ kɛ tsu si himi kpakpa he haomi nɛ ma nɔ amlɔ nɛ ɔ he ní, kɛ bɔ nɛ wa ma plɛ kɛ da he wami nɔ ngɛ hiɔ nɛ gba nɔ ɔ se. Nɔ yeli ɔ kɛ e he ha womi kɛ ga koli kpata nɛ a kpa gba he nɛ kɛ ga womi ma ha nɛ a wo nihewi kɛ yihewi he wami kɛ ha daka he ní tsumi. E ngɛ mi nɛ wa ngɛ Nɔyelɔ ɔ

• Sika tsake semihi he sɔuu ngɛ je kɛ wɛ sika dɔngɔma amɛ a blɔfa kɛ ha 2021 nyɔhiɔ nɛɛ a kpo etɛ amɛ a mi. Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa, Ghana cedi ɔ kplɛ si kɛ ya su 1.7 percent ngɛ US Dollar ɔ he kɛ ya su September 2021 nyagbe, ngɛ be mi nɛ e kplɛ si 2.9 percent ngɛ jamɛ a be ɔ nɔuu mi ngɛ 2020.

Subsidy: Enɛ ji hejɔmee nɛ guɔ nɔ nɛ nɔyeli ɔ kɛ haa ma bi nɛ e kɛ tsoo tɔmi nɛ ngɛ a nɔ ɔ nɔ.

Bulɔmi: Sane ko a he hɛ kɛ fɔmi blɛuu aloo kɛ juɛmi.

Hɛ mi kpɔ: Enɛ ji be nɛ nɔyeli ɔ haa e ma bi ɔmɛ sane bimɛ nɛ kɔɔ nɔ́ nɛ e ngɛ pee he nɛ hi kɛ nɛ su be nɔ.

Níhi Kɛ Sɔlemi Níhi A Ní Tsumi: Sika hiɛ nɛ Ma nɔ yeli ɔ woɔ kɛ tsuɔ e ní tsumihi a he ní nɛ e kɛ tsuɔ sɔlemi níhi kɛ haa ma nɔ bi ɔmɛ.

Value Added Tax (VAT): Too nɛ a tsoo ngɛ nɔ́ nɛ a kɛ piɛɛ níhi kɛ sɔlemihi a he ngɛ níhi a womi blɔ nya tsuo nɔ.
