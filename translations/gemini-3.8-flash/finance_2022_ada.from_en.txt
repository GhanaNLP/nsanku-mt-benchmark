5 | Ghana Nɔyeli - 2022 Ma Bi Ɔmɛ A Sika Todi He Mlaa

“Nitsumisilɔhi A Ma Nɛ Ma Hi Si Kɛ Ya Neneene Mamuɔmi: Sika Lɛ Nɔ Nɛ A Ma Mɛ Kɛ Nitsumi Hiami”

HƐMI NƆ MUNYU

COVID-19 gblamigbɔ hiɔ nɛ e hɛ mi ngɛ gbeye ɔ he hiahiami nitsɛ ba nɔ ngɛ sikasɛm nya he wamihi tsuo a nɔ, nɛ Ghana hu piɛɛ he. Haomahi nɛ Ghana bi kɛ kpe kɛ gu a wamihi, a he wami nitsumihi kɛ a jua yemi nitsumihi nɛ a hɛ je a dɛ ɔ he nitsuní gɔɔwe nɔ yemi ɔ he. I ngɛ he ngua kpee kɛ dee ke blɔ nɛ wa to kɛ fia hiɔ gblamigbɔ nɛ ɔ nya kɛ tsu e he hiahiami he ní ɔ pee nɔ kpe kɛ kã nɛ e ha nɛ je ɔ tsuo je wa yi. Jehanɛ hu, nɔ yemi ɔ to mlaahi kɛ ye bua nɛ jua yemi nitsumihi ba a he nɔ ekko nɛ wa wami ba si himi kpakpa nɛ da mi ekko.

Jehanɛ se hu, kɛ ya ha gbeye womi kɛ he blɔhi nɛ je kpo ngɛ gbalahi nɛ e ba a mi heje ɔ, Nɔ yemi ɔ, ngɛ H.E. President Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo yi nami nitsɛ nɛ nɔ ngɛ mi ɔ sisi ɔ, dla GH¢100 billion COVID-19 Alleviation and Revitalisation of Enterprises blɔ nya tomi ɔ, (GhanaCARES “Obaatan Pa” blɔ nya tomi ɔ) mla. Enɛ ɔ ha nɛ wa ma bi ɔmɛ ná he jɔɔmi amlɔ nɔuu kɛ gbɔjɔɔ nɛ e ngɛ ma a hu kpɔ̃e kɛ ba he wami he ekoko.

Wa buɔ hemi kɛ yemi nɛ nyɛ ngɛ ngɛ Nɔyeli ɔ mi ɔ he akɔnɔ beni wa ngɛ ní tsue konɛ wa da si he wami ngɛ gbalahi nɛ dɔmɔ ɔ kɛ ba a mi. Ngɛ be nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, wa tsui si nɔ semi nɛ ma hɛɛ wɔ kɛ gu kaa ma a, a ka hyɛ. Baiblo ɔ haa blɔ tsɔɔmi nɛ sa be fɛɛ be ngɛ behi kaa nɛ ɔmɛ a mi. Titli ɔ, kaa bɔ nɛ a tsɔɔ ngɛ Yesaya 9:10 mi ɔ, “Brikisi ɔmɛ huasa, se wa maa ma kɛ tɛhi nɛ a kpa; sikamɔ tso ɔmɛ a po, se wa maa ngɔ sida tsohi kɛ to a nane mi”. Ngɛ nɛ ɔ nɔuu mi ɔ, Nɔyeli ɔ hu fia e pɛɛ si kaa e maa pe nɔ́ nɛ e wo he si ɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, wa maa ya nɔ maa to mlaahi kɛ nitsumɔhi a blɔ nya nɛ ma ha nɛ wa na níhi tsuo nɛ dɔ wɔ ɔ ekɔ, nɛ e ma wo ma bi ɔmɛ a si himi nɔ.

Yi mi tomi titli nɛ ngɛ 2022 Ga Bomi Munyu ɔ mi, nɛ ngɛ anɔkwale mi ɔ ngɛ jeha etɛ (3) nɛ ma a mi kɛ ya a kake ji, nɛ a ma Tsɔsemi Kɛ Nitsumi Ma nɛ nɔ bami ngɛ he nɛ ma nyɛ ma ha sikasɛtsumi mi hewami nɛ he wa, nɛ nɔ bami ngɛ he, nɛ kɔɔ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ he kɛ ha nitsumi kpakpa kɛ ha nɔ fɛɛ nɔ, titli ɔ Nihewi Kɛ Yihewi. Lɔ ɔ he ɔ, nɔyeli ɔ heblamahi maa kɔ he nɛ a kɛ maa fĩ nihewi kɛ yihewi a se nɛ a kɛ mɛ nitsɛmɛ a ga si himi nɛ tsu ní nɛ a je a hewami kpo kɛ ha he lo nya bami kɛ gu heblɛ kake nɔ nɛ a kɛ ma ha nɔ fɛɛ nɔ.

Kɛ ba ha nɛ a wo nihewi kɛ yihewi a hɛ mi nyami nɛ a tsa a tsui kɛ ba dadi jumi he ɔ, Nɔyeli ɔ ma je YouStart blɔ nya tomi ɔ sisi ngɛ 2022 mi konɛ a na sika kɛ ha nihewi kɛ yihewi nɛ a tsuɔ jumi nɛ a kɛ to jumi hehi aloo a kɛ bli a jumi ɔ nya.

Ngɛ jeha 2022 Sikatsɔmi Mlaa a mi ɔ, wa tsɔɔ blɔ nya tomihi nɛ wa to kɛ ma ya nɔ kɛ tsu si himi mi haomihi nɛ kɔɔ he wami nɛ ma a hiɔ kɛ tsuɔ ní ɔ he kɛ ha amlɔ nɛ ɔ a he ní nɛ wa ma je kpo kɛ he wami ngɛ be nɛ gbenɔ hiamɔ hiɔ ɔ se po ɔ. Nɔ yeli ɔ to e tsui si kɛ ha blɔ nya tomihi nɛ mi gba kɛ nɔ nɛ a to nɛ a ma kɛ ha nihi ga womi kɛ wo nihewi kɛ yihewi he wami ngɛ daka nya ní tsumi mi. Benɛ wa yaa nɔ kɛ Ma Nɔtsɛ ɔ

Mahe Si Himi Sikanane He Nitsumi

3. Be kpa si hiami he he nina kɛ ha sikasɛtsumi ní tsumi ɔ he sanehi ngɛ 2021 mi ji nɛ:

• Jeha 2021 nyɔhi ɔmɛ a fã kekleekle ɔ mi ɔ, real GDP tsoo nɔ́ nɛ ji 3.5 percent kɛ tsi 0.7 percent nɛ e pɔ̃ ngɛ jamɛ a be ɔ mi nɔ́u nɔ́ ngɛ jeha 2020 ɔ nɔ;

• Non-Oil real GDP ɔ hiɛ kɛ ya hami nya subɔ 4.8 percent ngɛ 2021 jeha a fa kekleekle ɔ mi kɛ to 1.1 percent he ngɛ 2020 be kake ɔ nɔɔ mi;

• Níhi a he jami kɛ ya si September nyagbe ji 10.6 percent, kɛ kɔ 10.4 percent he ngɛ be kake ɔ nɔuu mi ngɛ 2020 mi;

• Ga tsuo sika he hiami (kɛɛsi) nɛ ji 7.7 pesɛnt ngɛ GDP mi nɛ te si kɛ wo bɔ nɛ a hla nɛ ji 7.4 pesɛnt ngɛ GDP mi ɔ he;

• Sisije tutuutu nó ɔ ná hyehe jemi nɛ ji GDP ɔ nɔ je he kɛ yi 2.0 percent, nɛ e kɛ si tomi hyehe jemi nɛ ji 1.7. percent ɔ kpa we gbi;

• Ma a hiɔhiɛ (kɛ ya si GDP nɔ kpu) ba su 77.7 percent ngɛ September 2021 nyagbe, nɛ e kpa si kɛ je 76.1 percent ngɛ December 2020 nyagbe;

• Je Kɛ Wɛ Sika To He Níhi Nɛ A To Kɛ Wo Si Ngɛ August 2021 Nyagbe ɔ ji US$11.44 billion, nɛ e maa hi nyɔhiɔ 5.2 níhi nɛ a kɛ jeɔ kpo kɛ baa ɔ he, ngɛ nɔ nɛ a ngma kɛ fɔ si ngɛ December 2020 nyagbe nɛ ji US$8.62 billion nɛ kɛ nyɔhiɔ 4.1 níhi nɛ a kɛ jeɔ kpo kɛ baa ɔ sɔ ɔ he; kɛ

• Sika tsake hemihi maa nɔ nɛ a nɔ́ yi pe kulaa ngɛ je kɛ wɛ sika wamihi a he kɛ ha 2021 ligbi nyɔhiɔ etɛ kakehi a mi. Titli ɔ, Ghana cedi nɔ́ si ngɛ nyɔhiɔ kpanaku 2021 nyagbe ɔ mi nɔ́ nɛ su 1.7 percent ngɛ US Dollar he, ke a kɛ to 2.9 percent si nɔ́mi nɛ ba ngɛ be kake ɔ nɔuu mi ngɛ 2020 he.

RFJ (Rearing For Food and Jobs): maa pee lohwe hyɛmi he nitsumi nɛ he wa nɛ e tsuɔ ní saminya, nɛ maa ha ma mi ní tsumi maa ya hiɛ, maa kɛ lohwe he níhi nɛ a kɛ jeɔ kpo ba a maa kpa si, maa pee ní tsumi kɛ ha nihi, nɛ e maa pee lohwe hyɛmi he nitsulahi a he wami kpakpa”.

38 | Ghana Nɔyeli - 2022 Ma Bi Ɔmɛ A Ga Womi Sika Tɔmi

“Nitsumisilɔhi A Ma Nɛ Ma Hi Si Kɛ Ya Neneene Mamuɔmi: Sika Lɛ Nɔ Nɛ A Ma Mɛ Kɛ Nitsumi Hiami”

Ma Nɔ Sɛhlɛmihi: Nɔyeli ɔ woo jé kɛ we yemi he dɔlahi kɛ kpetrol níhi a he lili kɛ haa ma níhi a nane mi.

Subsidy: Enɛ ji sika yemi kɛ buami nɛ nɔyeli ɔ kɛ haa ma bi nɛ e tsɔɔ tlukaa nɛ ngɛ a nɔ ɔ nɔ.

Hwesɔumi: Blɔ nɔ nɛ a guɔ kɛ kpaago aloo nilee kɛ hyɛɛ sane ko mi nɛ a leɔ si.

Nɔ́ nɛ a laa we nɔ: Enɛ ɔ ji be nɛ Nɔyeli ɔ haa e ma bi ɔmɛ sane bimi nɛ hi kɛ nɛ be kpa we nɔ kɛ kɔ nɔ́ nɛ e ngɛ pee ɔ he.

Níhi Kɛ Sɔlemihi Ní Tsu Ní: Sika Ní Abɔ nɛ Nɔyeli ɔ kɛ tsuɔ e nítsumihi a he ní kɛ kɛ haa ma nimeli ɔmɛ sɔlemi.

Value Added Tax (VAT): To nɛ a tsɔɔ kɛ fɔɔ níhi kɛ sɔlemihi nɔ benɛ a ngɛ a he jã kɛ yaa nɔ ngɛ níhi a peemi he tsuaa he.
