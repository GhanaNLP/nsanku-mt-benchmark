5 | Ghana Aban - 2022 Maanma Ne Ezukoa Ngyehyɛleɛ

Maanle Mɔɔ Gyinla Ɔ Nwo Azo Mɔɔ Ɛlɛnyia Ngyikhyeranu Gyima: Sɔkɔlɛzo Ninyɛne Ngyehyɛleɛ nee Gyimabɔlɛ

MUKELƐ NYUNLU EDE Kwame

COVID-19 ewule kpole ne anyebolo ngyegyelɛ ne hanle maandoo ngakyile kɔsɔɔti ezukoa agyakɛ, mɔɔ Ghana boka nwo la. Ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ Ghana amra nwunle ye wɔ ngoane, gyima nee adwelielie mɔɔ minli la anu la ɛnle debie mɔɔ Aban ne nwo ɛvi a. Me nye die kɛ meka kɛ mgbondabulɛ mɔɔ yɛvale yɛyɛle gyima wɔ ewule kpole ne anwo la yɛle kpole na ɔlile munli, na ewiade amuala anye liele nwo. Aban ne eza dwenlenle ndinli mɔɔ bɔbɔboa yeamaa adwelielie gyima agyina bɛ gyakɛ azo bieko na yɛ ngoane azia araa kɛ mɔɔ ɛnee ɔde la anwo.

Bikye ɛhye, kɛ mɔɔ yɛbanlea amaneɛbɔlɛ nee nwolɛ adenle ngakyile mɔɔ vi ewule kpole ne anu rale la, Arane, wɔ Y.G. Maanyɛvolɛ Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo adwenle kpalɛ ne abo, nzenzale nu hyehyɛle GH¢100 billion COVID-19 Alleviation and Revitalisation of Enterprises gyimalilɛ ne, (GhanaCARES “Obaatan Pa” gyimalilɛ ne). Ɛhye ɛva ndɛndɛ nee ahyɛlɛde kpole ɛbrɛ yɛ menli ne yɛɛ ɔlɛmaa yɛ maanle ne anwosesebɛ fofolɛ.

Yɛ nye sɔ kɛ bɛlɛ diedi wɔ arane ne anu wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛbɔ mɔdenle kɛ yɛbanyia kpɔkɛdelɛ kpalɛ yɛavi ewule kpole ne anu la. Wɔ mekɛ ɛhye anu, yɛ abotane mɔɔ yɛlɛ kɛ maanle la ɛnyia sɔnea. Baebolo ne fa adehilelɛ mɔɔ nwolɛ hyia la maa dahuu wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ zɛhae la anu. Titili, kɛmɔ bɛhɛlɛ ye wɔ Ayezaya 9:10 la, “Brike ne mɔ ɛbubu ɛgua, noko yɛbava abo mɔɔ bɛyɛ nwolɛ gyima la yɛazi bieko; bɛpɛ kɔkɔbɛtɛ baka ne mɔ bɛgua, noko yɛbava sidare baka yɛazie bɛ agyakɛ anu”. Zɔhane ala a, arane ne noko ɛbɔ kpɔkɛ kɛ ɔbali ye ɛwɔkɛ mɔɔ yebɔ la azo a. Ɔlua ɛhye ati, yɛbahɔ zo yɛali ngyehyɛleɛ nee gyima mɔɔ baboa amaa yɛanyia ninyɛne kɔsɔɔti mɔɔ yɛminli la bieko na yɛamaa menli ne ɛbɛlabɔlɛ ayɛ kpalɛ la azo.

Bodiɛ titilii mɔɔ wɔ 2022 Ezukoa Neleazo ne anu, na amgba noko kɔ la ye anyunlu ɛvolɛ nsa (3) mɔɔ bara la anu ko a le kɛ bɛbadua Esuanwunlɛ/Gyalalilɛ Maanle mɔɔ gyina kpundii na ɔbahola yeamaa maanyɛlɛ nu nyilɛ mɔɔ anu yɛ se, tɛla aze, yɛɛ ɔka awie biala la ara amaa gyima kpalɛ ara amaa awie biala, titili Mgbavolɛ nee Mbɛlema. Ɔti, Aban nhyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ bara la barali gyima kɛ bɛbaboa mgbavolɛ nee mbɛlema ne mɔ amaa bɛava bɛ ahyɛlɛdeɛ bɛali gyima na bɛahola bɛaye ninyɛne kpole mɔɔ wɔ bɛ nu la bɛali amaa adenrɛ fɔɔnwo mɔɔ awie biala banyia la alua zo amaa anyuhɔlɛ ara amaa awie biala.

Ama bɛamaa mgbavolɛ nee mbɛlra arɛle abɔ bɛ nwo zo na bɛanyia gyimayɛlɛ sunsum la, Aban kɛbɔ YouStart gyimayilalɛ ne azo wɔ 2022 amaa yeabuke ɛzonlenlɛ nyianlɛ adenle yeamaa mgbavolɛ nee mbɛlra gyimayɛvoma amaa bɛadɛla anzɛɛ bɛatrɛ bɛ gyima ne anu.

Wɔ 2022 Ezukoa Ne Anwo Ngyehyɛleɛ ne anu, yɛhyehyɛ ngyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ yɛbalua zo yɛahɔ zo yɛali ezukoa gyima ne anwo ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ ɛlɛbɔ yɛ nuhua kɛkala la anwo gyima na yɛavi ewule kpole ne anzi yɛara kɛ yɛnde kpole kpalɛ. Aban ne ɛyɛ kɛzi kɛ ɔbalua ngyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ anu te na bɛbɔ nu bɛyɛ la azo yeamaa ngakula ne mɔ anyia ndetelɛ na yeaboa mgbanyinli mɔɔ kulo kɛ bɛbɔ gyima bo la. Mɔɔ yɛlɛdi Maanyɛvolɛ Kpole ne

EZUKOA GYIMAƐYƐLƐ WO 2021 NE ANU

Maakro-ekɔnɔme Gyimalilɛ

3. Edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ɛnle fɔɔnwo mɔɔ fane kɛzi maangyebakye nwo gyima hɔle la nwo wɔ 2021 anu a le ɛhye:

• GDP ambaamgba mɔɔ lumua la yɛle bɛhyɛle nu 3.5 pesɛnte wɔ 2021 fɛlɛko ne anu mɔɔ ɛnee anyrɛlɛ ne le 0.7 pesɛnte wɔ mekɛ ko ne ala anu wɔ 2020;

• Nzao ɛnle nu real GDP nyianle anyuhɔlɛ kɛyɛ 4.8 percent wɔ afe 2021 fafe ne mɔɔ lumua ne anu mɔɔ yɛfa yɛtoto 1.1 percent anwo wɔ zɔhane mekɛ ne ala anu wɔ 2020;

• Ezukoa anwosesebɛ ɛdelɛ kɔkpula Ɛkɛbɛ ne awieleɛ la yɛle 10.6 kɛbele, kɛ yɛfa yɛtoto 10.4 kɛbele ne mɔɔ ɛnee wɔ ɛvolɛ 2020 mekɛ ko ne ala anu la anwo;

• Ezukoa mɔɔ bɛlɛ ye nzenzaleɛ mɔɔ luwole aze (ezukoa kpotue) 7.7 wɔ ɛvoya nu wɔ GDP nu mɔɔ di fofolɛ kɔ 7.4 wɔ ɛvoya nu wɔ GDP mɔɔ bɛvale bɛziele bɛ nye zo la anwo;

• Mɔlebɛbo ezukoa ngyekyenu ne lale ahyi mɔɔ kɔdwu GDP ne anu ngyɛnu 2.0 percent, mɔɔ yɛfa yɛtoto ahyi mɔɔ bɛbodone kɛ ɔbadwu ngyɛnu 1.7. percent ne anwo a;

• Maanle ne kakye (kɛ mɔɔ GDP pesɛnte kile la) dwule pesɛnte 77.7 wɔ September 2021 awieleɛ, mɔɔ ɛvande pesɛnte 76.1 anwo wɔ December 2020 awieleɛ la;

• Maanle Maanle Ezukoa Mɔɔ Bɛbɔ Nu Bɛzie Mɔɔ Bɔ Nu Kɔsɔɔti wɔ Ɔgɔse 2021 awieleɛ la yɛle US$11.44 bilion, mɔɔ fɛta kɛ ɔdua ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛfa bɛba maanle nu la siane 5.2, mɔɔ bɛfa bɛtoto US$8.62 bilion mɔɔ fɛta ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛfa bɛba siane 4.1 mɔɔ bɛhɛlɛle ye wɔ Dɛsemba 2020 awieleɛ la anwo; na

• Ezukoa kakelɛ boɛ mɔɔ gyi ewiade amuala ezukoa kpole ne mɔ anwo la dɛnlanle kpalɛ wɔ 2021 melembele nsa ne anu. Titili ne, Ghana cedi ne dolezulɛ bɔbɔle nu kɔdwule 1.7 percent wɔ US Dollar ne anwo wɔ September 2021 awieleɛ, mɔɔ yɛfa yɛtotɔ 2.9 percent dolezulɛ nwo wɔ melembele ko ne ala anu wɔ 2020.

1D1F (One District, One Factory): Fabriki nee ndwuma mgbole mgbole ɛtetelɛ mɔɔ bamaa maanle ne anyi kɔ ngelata ndwuma mgbole yɛlɛ nu kpalɛ. Ɔbodane a le kɛ ɔbamaa Ghanama anyia gyima dɔɔnwo wɔ maanle ne amuala anu.

Ngilenu: Ɛhye a le adenle mɔɔ bɛdua zo bɛmaa ngyegyelɛ bie anu te a.

Ezukoa ngyehyɛleɛ: Ezukoa ngyehyɛleɛ le ngyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ kile ɛleka mɔɔ bɛbanyia ezukoa bɛavi nee mɔɔ bɛbava bɛayɛ a. Saa bɛhyia ezukoa dɔɔnwo nwo a, ɔwɔ kɛ abusua ne dwenle kɛzi bɛbanyia ezukoa dɔɔnwo anzɛɛ bɛbavɛlɛ bosea la anwo.

Budget deficit: Ɛhye si mekɛ mɔɔ Aban ezukoa mɔɔ ɔsɛye la dɔɔnwo tɛla ezukoa mɔɔ ɔnyia la.

Anyunluɛholɛ Agɔnwolɛma (DPs): Ɛhye kile maanle anzɛɛ ahyehyɛdeɛ mɔɔ bɛ nee maanle mɔɔ ɛlɛbɔ mɔdenle kɛ bɛanyia anyunluɛholɛ la yia bɔ nu di gyima kɛbɔkɛ bɛanyia bɛ anyunluɛholɛ bodane mɔ. FREE SHS: Free SHS (Free Senior High School) ne mɛla ne le Ghana Aban ne anyiemgbolenyia mɔɔ Maanepɛnle Nana Akuffo-Addo amraado ne vale rale wɔ September 2017 mɔɔ maa adenle kɛ JHS suakɛlɛma kɔsɔɔti mɔɔ fɛte la bahɔ SHS sukulu mɔɔ bɛndua ɛhwee a.

Ngonlɛ: Ɛhye mɔ le ezukoa moalɛ mɔɔ mpɔndekpɔn nu agɔnwolɛma fa maa Aban mɔɔ ɔngyia kɛ Aban tua bieko a.

Mgbonda Mɔɔ Bɛbɔ Bɛmaa Aban Yunit Gyɛne: Ɛhye mɔ a le ahatualɛ mɔɔ mɛla kpondɛ kɛ bɛyɛ la, le kɛ District Assemblies’ Common Fund (DACF), Road Fund, Ghana Education Trust Fund (GETFUND), Petroleum Related Fund nee National Health Fund.

Gross Domestic Product (GDP): Ɛhye kile ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛyɛ na sonla dɔɔnwo hyia nwo nee gyima kɔsɔɔti mɔɔ bɛyɛ wɔ maanle ne anu wɔ mekɛ pɔkɛbie anu la amuala abo mɔɔ bɛbɔ la.

Gross International Reserves: Ɛhye tendɛ maanle bie maanle gyɛne ezukoa anwosesebɛ mɔɔ ɔkɛnyia mɔɔ ɔbava yeadɔ ninyɛne nee somanlɛ ngakyile yeavi maanle gyɛne nu wɔ mekɛ bie anu la anwo; eza ɔboa ɔmaa maanle ne amra ezukoa ne anwosesebɛ yɛ kpole wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛfa bɛtoto maanle gyɛne ezukoa nwo la.

Ninyɛne bo ɛdelɛ kpole: Mekɛ pɔkɛ bie anu ninyɛne nee nzonlɛlielɛ bo kɔ anwuma kpole.

IPEP (Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme): Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme (IPEP) le NPP Aban ne ehyianlɛ gyima kpole ne mɔ ko mɔɔ kpondɛ kɛ ɔtu ehyia na ɔpɛ amumuyɛlɛ zo, titili wɔ kpalɛzo nee azule mɔɔ bɛndwu bɛ nwo kpalɛ la anu ɔlua gyima nvoninli kpole mɔɔ bɛhyia nwo la anwo nziezielɛ zo.

Macroeconomic targets: Ɛhye mɔ le nzenzenlenlɛ mɔɔ bɛfa bɛneɛnlea kɛzi maanle ne ezukoa gyima kɔ la anu. Neazo bie mɔ a le ezukoa kɛnlema anyuhɔlɛ, ninyɛne bo ɛdelɛ, kakilalɛ mɔɔ ba gyimayɛlɛ nuhua, kɛzi bɛ nee maanle gyɛne di gua la, kɛzi aban ne ezukoa gyima ngyehyɛleɛ mɔ yɛ boɛ anzɛɛ ɛnla kɛnlema la, nee kpɔkɛbɔlɛ mɔɔ Bank of Ghana yɛ mɔɔ ka ezukoa ɛkpondɛlɛ nee ye ɛwulalɛ wɔ maanle ne ezukoa gyima nu la.

MSME (Micro And Small Buisness Developmet Programme): Gyele gyima ekyi kpole (anzɛɛ microenterprise) le gyima ekyi mɔɔ sonla dɔɔnwo ɛnle nu, yɛɛ ye ezukoa mɔɔ ɔfa ɔyɛ gyima anzɛɛ ɔnyia ye wɔ ɛvolɛ biala anu la ɛndwu dodo bie mɔɔ bɛhyehyɛ la

NABCo (Nation Builders Corps): mɔɔ eza bɛfɛlɛ ye NABCO la le ngyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ Ghana Abanvale rale kɛ ɔbamaa yunivɛsiti mɔɔ bɛwie mɔɔ bɛnlɛ gyima la anya gyima amaa bɛali asetɛnlamu ngyegyelɛ dɔɔnwo anwo gyima wɔ anwuma nee ananze gyimayɛlɛleka kɔsɔɔti na bɛamaa bɛ nyianu anwo ndelebɛbo ayɛ kpole amaa maanle ne sikadɔnwoma anyia anyuhɔlɛ.

NBSSI (National Board For Small And Medium Scale Industry): The National Board for Small Scale Industries (NBSSI) le eku mɔɔ ɛnle maanle ne ɛdeɛ na ɛnnyia mfasoɔ mɔɔ wɔ Ministry of Trade and Industry abo la, na bɛmaa bɛ adenle kɛ bɛboa mɔɔ bɛyɛ ninyɛne nketewa nee ngakyile (MSEs) anyunluwɔlɛ nee bɛ nyilɛ.

Non-Oil Real GDP: Le ezukoa mɔɔ bɔ ye kpɔkɛ ne amuala wɔ maanyɛleɛ ne anu mɔɔ kpoke kpolera gyima ne mɔ fvi nu, mɔɔ bu ngelata mɔɔ kpundale ninyɛne kɛlata ne kpolera nwo nu.

Ezukoa Mɔɔ Ɛnvi Adwule Nu: Aban ezukoa mɔɔ nde adwule nu a vi a, kɛ neazo la fofolɛ mɔɔ bɛtua bɛfa bɛmaa adenle anzɛɛ laehɛnse, fofolɛ mɔɔ debie lilɛma tua nee ezukoa gyɛne mɔɔ bɛgye la.

Gyima mɔɔ Ɛtɛbɔle Bɛ Nzo: Gyima mɔɔ ɛtɛbɔle bɛ nzo ne mɔ kile MDA bɔwɔle mɔɔ bɛ nee Ministry of Finance bɔle la, ɛkɛ ne ala noko a le 2016 ezukoa mɔɔ ɛtɛbɔle nzo mɔɔ bɛfa bɛmaa Statutory Funds la.

Primary balance : Ɛhye a le ngakyile mɔɔ wɔ Aban ezukoa mɔɔ ɔnyia nee ye ezukoa mɔɔ ɔbɔ mɔɔ ɛnle ezukoa ahyɛze (mɔɔ ɛkɛnla ahyɛze Tua ɛnle nu) la avinli. Bɛkola bɛsusu ɛhye kɛ Gross Domestic Product (GDP) ne pesɛnte bie.

Anyunluwɔlɛ Nyilalɛ Kɛlɛla: Kɛ Ghana sikadwuma ne banyinli wɔ ɛvolɛ ko anu la.

Ezunle mɔɔ kɔ nu: Ɛhye a le ezunle dodo mɔɔ Aban nyia ye kɛ ɔfa ɔyɛ ye gyima mɔɔ ɔvi maanle ne anu nee maanle gyɛne nu a.

RFJ (Rearing For Food and Jobs): bayɛ nane neɛnlenlɛ gyima mɔɔ le kpalɛ na ɔlile kpole, mɔɔ bamaa maannu ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛyɛ la ayɛ kpole, ate nane ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛfa bɛvi maanle gyɛne nu bɛba la azo, aboa gyimayɛlɛ nwo adenle ɛlielɛ, na yeamaa menli mɔɔ wɔ nane neɛnlenlɛ nyianu la ɛbɛlabɔlɛ atu mpɔn”.

Mɛla Zo Nvasoɛ: Aban ne tua boate nu boa debie mɔɔ bɛfa bɛyɛ gyima ninyɛne kɔmpile mɔɔ bamaa maangyebakyi nu amra anyia nvasoɛ nee ngo ninyɛne nzonu nwo kakɛ maa menli dɔɔnwo ne.

Abodwonu ezukoa: Ɛhye le ezukoa moalɛ mɔɔ arane fɛlɛ fa maa maanema amaa bɛ nzonlɛ azo ade bɛ nwo zo.

Neanle/Nzinzane: Adenle mɔɔ bɛdua zo bɛfa ndelebɛbo anzɛɛ adwenle bɛnea edwɛkɛ bie anu la.

Nwo-nwomilielɛ: Ɛhye a le mekɛ mɔɔ Arane maa ɔ manma anye die edwɛkɛ mɔɔ fɛta na ɔdwu mekɛ nwo kpalɛ mɔɔ fane mɔɔ ɔlɛyɛ nwo la.

Ninyɛne Nee Ɛzonlenlɛ Gyima Nwo Dwulilɛ: Ezukoa mɔɔ Aban tua kɛ ɔfa yeayɛ ye gyima nee mɔɔ ɔfa ɔbayɛ gyima yeamaa menli dɔɔnwo la.

Value Added Tax (VAT): Ezukoa mɔɔ bɛyɛ ye adwule mɔɔ bɛdie wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛfa ninyɛne bie bɛboka ninyɛne nee nzonlɛlielɛ nwo wɔ yɛyɛlɛ ne foa biala anu la.
