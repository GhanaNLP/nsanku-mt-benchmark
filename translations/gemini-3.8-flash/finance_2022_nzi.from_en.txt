5 | Ghana Aban - 2022 Menli Ne Ezukoa Ngyehyɛleɛ

“Kɛzi Yɛbazi Yɛamaa Ninyɛne-Yɛvoma Maanle Ne Anyi Dahuu: Ezukoa Ngyehyɛleɛ Nu Ɛsesebɛyɛlɛ Nee Gyimayɛlɛ Nwo Ɛkponledolɛ”

ANYUNLUMU EDWƐKƐ

COVID-19 ewule kpole ne anyebolo nyane edwɛkɛ ne ɛha maangyebakyi kɔsɔɔti, mɔɔ Ghana boka nwo la. Amaneɛnwunlɛ mɔɔ Ghana amra nwunle ye wɔ ngoane, asetɛnla nee gualilɛ mɔɔ bɛminlinle la ɛtɛvile Aban ne adwenle nu. Me nye die kɛ meka kɛ yɛ nyianle akɛnrasesebɛ na yɛdule gyinlabelɛ kpalɛ yɛvale yɛyɛle nzonle wɔ ewule kpole ne nyane edwɛkɛ ne anwo mɔɔ ewiade amuala anye liele nwo la. Eza Aban ne yɛle nhyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ baboa amaa gualilɛ adua zo na yɛ asetɛnla azo ara ye kɛkala la.

Bieko, kɛ amualɛ kɔ mɔɔ ɛzulolɛ ne nee adenle mgbalɛ mɔɔ vi ewulensane ne anu rale la anwo la, Aban, wɔ H.E. President Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo adwelie kpalɛ ne abo, yɛle GH¢100 billion COVID-19 Alleviation and Revitalisation of Enterprises gyimalilɛ ne, (GhanaCARES “Obaatan Pa” gyimalilɛ ne) ndɛndɛ kpalɛ. Ɛhye ɛva ndɛndɛ nee fɛlɛralɛ kpole ɛrɛlɛ yɛ menli ne na ɔlɛmaa yɛ maanle ne anwosesebɛ fofolɛ.

Yɛda bɛ ase kɛ bɛlale diedi ali wɔ Aban ne anwo mekɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛyɛ gyima kɛ yɛbamaa ewulenzane ne anzo anyia anwosesebɛ kpole la. Mekɛ ɛhye anu, yɛ ngyinlazo mɔɔ bamaa yɛadɛnla aze kɛ maanle la anwo sɔnea rale. Baebolo ne dahuu maa adenle mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛfa zo wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ le kɛ ɛhye mɔ anu. Titili, kɛ mɔɔ bɛhɛlɛ ye wɔ Ayezaya 9:10 anu la, “Mɔɔbire ne mɔ ɛdɔ aze, noko yɛbava bolɛ mɔɔ bɛyɛ nwo gyima la yɛazi; bɛpɛ kɔkɔba baka ne mɔ bɛgua, noko yɛbava sidabaka yɛazie bɛ gyinlabelɛ nu”. Wɔ adenle ko ne ala azo, Aban ne noko ɛbɔ kpɔkɛ kɛ ɔbali ye ɛwɔkɛ mɔɔ ɔbɔle la azo. Ɛhye ati, yɛbahɔ zo yɛali ngyehyɛleɛ nee gyimayɛlɛ mɔɔ bamaa yɛanyia ninyɛne kɔsiniti mɔɔ ɛminli la bieko na yɛamaa menli ne ɛbɛlabɔle ayɛ kpalɛ la azo.

Bodane titilii ne mɔ mɔɔ wɔ 2022 Ezukoa Ne Anwo Ngyehyɛleɛ ne anu, na amgba kɛvɛlɛ mfeɛ nsa (3) mɔɔ bara la anu ko a le kɛ bɛbadua Gyimayɛlɛ Maanle mɔɔ gyina kpundii mɔɔ kola maa ezukoa kpole mɔɔ anu yɛ se, gyina kpundii, na ɔka menli kɔsɔɔti bɔbɔ la anyinli kpalɛ amaa bɛanyia gyima kpalɛ bɛamaa awie biala, titilii Kɛlɛntalɛ ne mɔ. Ɔti, arane ne ngyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ ɔbaye la kɔsɔɔti bamaa anyelazo ara kɛ bɛbaboa kɛlɛntalɛ ne mɔ amaa bɛava bɛ nyianenu ahyɛlɛdeɛ ne mɔ bɛali gyima na bɛahile bɛ tumi bɛamaa maanyelɛ ara ɔlua nwolɛ adenle mɔɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ mɔɔ bɛbamaa awie biala la azo.

Ama amgbavolɛ nee mbɛlɛra anye alie yɛɛ bɛanyia gyimayɛlɛ sunsum la, Aban ne bamaa YouStart gyimayɛlɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ne abɔ ɔ bo wɔ 2022 amaa yeaboa mgbavolɛ nee mbɛlɛra mɔɔ yɛ gyima la kɛ bɛbanyia ezukoa mɔɔ bɛfa bɛabɔ bɛ gyima bo anzɛɛ bɛadɛlɛ nu a.

Wɔ 2022 Ezukoa Ne Anwo Ngyehyɛleɛ ne anu, yɛhyehyɛ ngyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ yɛbalua zo yɛali ezukoadwuma ne anwo ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ ɛlɛsi kɛkala ne anwo gyima na yɛavi ewule kpole ne anzi yɛara kɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛ anwosesebɛ dɔɔnwo la. Arane ɛva ye adwenle kɔsɔɔti ɛdo ngyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ ɛlie debie biala na bɛyɛ bɛbɔ nu la azo amaa yeamaa mgbavolɛ nee mbɛra adehyelɛ na yeamaa adwumayɛlɛ anwo anyelielɛ kpole ara bɛ nu. EZUKO GYIMA YƐLƐ WƆ 2021 NU

Maakiroɛkɔnɔme Gyimalilɛ

3. Ndonwo edwɛkɛ mɔɔ fale kɛzi ezukoa gyima ne hɔle wɔ 2021 anu la a li ɛke:

• Amuala anu real GDP biala kɛyɛ 3.5 pesɛnte wɔ 2021 mekɛ ne foa mɔɔ limoa la anu mɔɔ ɛnee le 0.7 pesɛnte anyuhɔlɛ wɔ zɔhane mekɛ kokye ne ala anu wɔ 2020;

• Non-Oil real GDP yɛle kpole kɔle 4.8 percent wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɔbɔle 2021 ɛvolɛ ne anu la, mɔɔ yɛfa yɛtotɔ 1.1 percent mɔɔ yɛnyianle ye wɔ zɔhane mekɛ kokye ne ala anu wɔ 2020 la anwo;

• Ninyɛne bo ɛdelɛ wɔ Anyunu mekɛ ne awieleɛ rale 10.6 pesɛnte, na saa ɛfa ɛto 10.4 pesɛnte mɔɔ rale mekɛ ko ne ala anu wɔ 2020 la anwo a;

• Ezukoa dodo mɔɔ bɔle (ezukoa kpalɛ) mɔɔ le GDP ne anu 7.7 pesɛnte mɔɔ bɛvale bɛdole bodane 7.4 pesɛnte mɔɔ le GDP ne anu la anwo;

• Bulalɛ ngyikyi ngyehyɛleɛ ne hilele kɛ 2.0 pesɛnte ɛvile GDP nu ɛminli, yɛɛ mɔɔ bɛvale bɛziele bɛ nye zo kɛ ɔbaminli la a le 1.7. pesɛnte;

• Maanle ne kakɛ (kɛ mɔɔ ɔde wɔ GDP ɔbupɛ nu la) dwulɛ 77.7 ɔbupɛ wɔ September 2021 awieleɛ, mɔɔ ɔvale 76.1 ɔbupɛ nwo anwuma wɔ December 2020 awieleɛ la;

• Ezonlenlɛ Maŋgodo Ezukoa mɔɔ Bɛhyɛ Ye Sie la wɔ August 2021 awielie la yɛle US$11.44 billion, mɔɔ bɔkɔla abua siane 5.2 debie mɔɔ bɛfa bɛba maŋgodo nu la, mɔɔ bɛfa bɛtotolɛ US$8.62 billion mɔɔ sɛ siane 4.1 debie mɔɔ bɛfa bɛba maŋgodo nu mɔɔ bɛhɛlɛle ye wɔ December 2020 awielie la; na

• Ezukoa sesebɛyɛlɛ kɛlɛte ne mɔ gyinlanle kpalɛ wɔ ewiade amuala ezukoa kpole ne mɔ anu wɔ 2021 nuhua nsa ne mɔ anu. Titili ne, Ghana cedi ne dulale bɔbɔ kɔdwulale 1.7 pesɛnte wɔ US Dollar ne anwo wɔ September 2021 awieleɛ, noko ɔtɛyɛle kɛ 2.9 pesɛnte mɔɔ ɔdulale wɔ zɔhane mekɛ kokye ne ala anu wɔ 2020 la.

Mgbɔlabonle: Ɛhye kile kɛ Aban kɛbua ninyɛne nwo mualɛ kɛkile ɔ manma ɔlua ye gyimalilɛ mɔɔ ɔkɛda ali na yeaha ninyɛne mɔɔ vi nuhua rale la anwo edwɛkɛ. Ɛhye bahola alie ezukoa anzɛɛ ninyɛne gyɛne mɔɔ Aban lɛ mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ ɔnea zo kpalɛ la anwo mgbɔlabonle.

Mgbanezo: Ɛhye a le adenle mɔɔ bɛdua zo bɛmaa ngyegyelɛ bie anu te a.

Ezukoa ngyehyɛleɛ: Ezukoa ngyehyɛleɛ le ngyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ kile ɛleka mɔɔ banyia ezukoa avi nee mɔɔ bava bayɛ la. Afe Awiele Ninyɛne Boɛ Ɛzonlenlɛ: Ninyɛne boɛ ɛzonlenlɛ ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ bɛnwunle ye wɔ afe biala mɔɔ bɛlɛsuzu nwo la December siane ne anu la.

“Kɛzi Yɛbazi Yɛamaa Ninyɛne-Yɛvoma Maanle Ne Anyi Dahuu: Ezukoa Ngyehyɛleɛ Nu Ɛsesebɛyɛlɛ Nee Gyimayɛlɛ Nwo Ɛkponledolɛ”

Ezunle mɔɔ bo zo: Ɛhye si mekɛ mɔɔ Aban ne ezunle mɔɔ ɔsɛye la bo ezunle mɔɔ Begyapɛlɛ kpole ne liele doli gua la azo.

FREE SHS: Free SHS (Free Senior High School) ngyinlazo ne le Ghana Aban ne gyimalilɛ mɔɔ Maanepɛnle Nana Akuffo-Addo amandoɛlɛ ne vale rale wɔ September 2017 mɔɔ maa JHS suahuvo biala mɔɔ fɛta wɔ sukulu nwoadeɛ nu la nyia adenle kɔ SHS sukulu mɔɔ ɛtua ɛhwee a.

Ngyehyɛnu mɔɔ bɛtua mɔɔ bɛnzea bɛndua: Ɛhye mɔ le ezukoa moalɛ ngakyile mɔɔ anyunluhɔle adɔkɔnma fa maa Aban na ɔnle kɛ Aban tua fɔɔnla.

Mgbɔlɔkɛ mɔɔ bɛfa bɛmaa Aban Azua Gyɛne: Ɛhye mɔ a le ezukoa mɔɔ mɛla kile kɛ bɛdua la, kɛ mɔɔ le District Assemblies’ Common Fund (DACF), Road Fund, Ghana Education Trust Fund (GETFUND), Petroleum Related Fund nee National Health Fund la.

Gross Domestic Product (GDP): Ɛhye kile ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛyɛ na sonla biala kola fa di gyima nee ɛzonlenlɛ kɔsɔɔti mɔɔ bɛyɛ ye maanle ne anu wɔ mekɛ pɔtɛee bie anu la amuala aboɔ.

Gross International Reserves: Ɛhye kile maangye bie maanye ezukoa mɔɔ ɔlɛ mɔɔ ɔbahola yeava yeadɔ ninyɛne nee sonvolɛlilɛ wɔ maanle gyɛne nu wɔ mekɛ bie anu; eza ɔboa ɔmaa maanle ne ezukoa anwosesebɛ yɛ kpole wɔ maanle gyɛne ezukoa anwo.

Inflation: Mekɛ bie anu ninyɛne nee nzonlɛlielɛ nwonane mɔɔ kɔ ɔ nyunlu la anlenkɛ.

IPEP (Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme): The Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme (IPEP) le NPP Aban ne gyima titili ne mɔ ko mɔɔ kpondɛ kɛ ɔtu ehyia na ɔpɛ pɛpɛlɛlilɛ mɔɔ ɛnle nu ne azo, titili wɔ kɛnra mɔɔ ɛnwomoe ɛnle bɛ nu nee namule mɔɔ ɛhyia la anu lua maangyebakilɛ gyima titili mɔɔ bɛsi la azo.

Makroɛkɔnɔmiki botaeɛ mɔ: Ɛhye mɔ a le ndetenle mɔɔ bɛfa bɛneɛnlea maangye ne maanyɛlɛ nu a. Ɔ nwo neazo mɔ bie a le maangye ne anyunluhɔlɛ, ezukoa nu ɛsesebɛyɛlɛ, nzenzaleɛ mɔɔ ba gyimayɛlɛ dodo nu, gyima mɔɔ bɛ nee maanle gyɛne mɔ yɛ la anyunluhɔlɛ, aban ne maangye nhyehyɛeɛ mɔ anyunluhɔlɛ anzɛɛ anwubielɛ nee kpɔkɛbɔlɛ mɔɔ Bank of Ghana yɛ mɔɔ ka ezukoa ɛkpondɛlɛ nee ɛmanlɛ wɔ maangye ne anu la.

37 | Ghana Aban - 2022 Menli Ezukoa Ngyehyɛleɛ

“Kɛzi Yɛbazi Yɛamaa Ninyɛne-Yɛvoma Maanle Ne Anyi Dahuu: Ezukoa Ngyehyɛleɛ Nu Ɛsesebɛyɛlɛ Nee Gyimayɛlɛ Nwo Ɛkponledolɛ”

MSME (Micro And Small Buisness Developmet Programme): Fane dɔɔnwo ne ala, bɛkile micro-enterprise (anzɛɛ microenterprise) abo kɛ ɔle gyima ekyi mɔɔ sonla ekyi bie ala a yɛ gyima wɔ ɛkɛ ne a, na ye ezukoa mɔɔ ɔfa ɔdi gyima anzɛɛ ye nyianu ne ɛnle dɔɔnwo kɛ mɔɔ bɛhulo kɛ ɔyɛ la.

NABCo (Nation Builders Corps): mɔɔ eza bɛfɛlɛ ye NABCO la le ngyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ Ghana Arane vale rale kɛ ɔbamaa suahole kpɔnma mɔɔ ɛnle gyima la gyima amaa bɛali asetɛnla nu ngyegyelɛ ngakyile nwo dwuma wɔ arane gyima nee sonla ngomekye gyima nu, na eza bɛaziezie bɛ mɔdenlebɔlɛ mɔɔ bamaa maanyɛleɛ nu nwonlomɔ anyi wɔ maanle ne anu.

NBSSI (National Board For Small And Medium Scale Industry): National Board for Small Scale Industries (NBSSI) le eku mɔɔ ɛnnyia mfasoɔ mɔɔ wɔ arane ne ase wɔ Ministry of Trade and Industry abo, na bɛmaa bɛ adenle kɛ bɛmaa mbɔnkrabɔlɛ nee anyunhɔle ɛrɛla Ngyikyi nee Ngyikyi kpole Dwuuma (MSEs) anu.

Non-Oil Real GDP: Le ezukoa mɔɔ yɛnyia mɔɔ ngakyile biala ɛnle nu mɔɔ kpulie kpolera ehyiase gyimalilɛ ngakyile biala fi nu, mɔɔ bu ninyɛne boɛdelɛ nwo mgbonda.

Ezukoa Mɔɔ Ɛnle Adwule: Aban ezukoa mɔɔ nde adwule nu, kɛ neazo la a le ndwɛba mɔɔ bɛtua mɔɔ bɛfa bɛmaa adenle anzɛɛ laehɛnsee, debie mɔɔ bɛfa bɛdi gyima nwo kakɛ nee ezukoa gyɛne mɔɔ bɛtua.

Ɛzonlɛ mɔɔ Ɛbɔ Nwole Ɛkɛ ne Mɔɔ Ɛtɛtualɛ: Ɛzonlɛ mɔɔ ɛbɔ nwole ɛkɛ ne mɔɔ ɛtɛtualɛ ne bie a le MDA ɛwɔkɛ mɔɔ bɛbɔle bɛguale Ministry of Finance anyunlu nee 2016 ezukoa mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛtua bɛmaa Statutory Funds mɔɔ bɛtɛtualɛ.

Primary balance : Ɛhye a le ngakyile mɔɔ wɔ Aban ezukoa mɔɔ ɔnyia nee ye ezukoa mɔɔ ɔbɔ mɔɔ nzɔho ɛnle nwo (mɔɔ kakɛ ndua mɔɔ kɔ nwo la ɛnle nwo) la avinli. Bɛkola bɛkyele ɛhye kɛ Gross Domestic Product (GDP) ne ɔfehyɛ mɔɔ vi ɛvoya nu.

Anyunluɛvole Nyilenu Ndinli: Kɛzi Ghana ɛzonlenlɛ ninyɛne bahyi wɔ ɛvolɛ ko anu la.

Ezukoa mɔɔ banyia: Ɛhye a le ezukoa kɔsɔɔti mɔɔ Aban nyia ye wɔ maanle ne anu nee maanle gyɛne nu amaa yeava yayɛ ye gyima ngakyile a.

RFJ (Rearing For Food and Jobs): bamaa nane neɛnle ninyɛne agyakɛ nu anwosesebɛ na bamaa yeayɛ kpalɛ kpole kpalɛ, mɔɔ bamaa maanyelɛ nu nane ninyɛne ɛyɛlɛ azɔhe, apepe nane ninyɛne mɔɔ bavi maanle gyɛne nu bɛara la azo, aboa amaa gyima ɛyɛlɛ anyia anyuhɔle, na yeamaa menli mɔɔ bɛwɔ nane neɛnle ninyɛne ɛyɛlɛ ne anwo gyima kɔsɔɔti anu la ɛbɛlabɔlɛ ayɛ kpalɛ”.

“Kɛzi Yɛbazi Yɛamaa Ninyɛne-Yɛvoma Maanle Ne Anyi Dahuu: Ezukoa Ngyehyɛleɛ Nu Ɛsesebɛyɛlɛ Nee Gyimayɛlɛ Nwo Ɛkponledolɛ”

Menli Ngunazele: Aban tua debie kɔ utility kɔmpene mɔ nee ewɔkɛ ninyɛne nwo zo maa maanyinli ne.

Ezukoa moalɛ (Subsidy): Ɛhye le ezukoa moalɛ mɔɔ arɛlɛbɔlɛ fa maa maanema amaa yeade bɛ nzonlɛ nu nyanelielɛ ne azo.

Neesianlɛ: Mekɛ mɔɔ bɛfa nrɛlɛbɛ nee adwenle bɛneɛnlea edwɛkɛ bie anu la.

Pɛlepɛleyɛlɛ: Ɛhye a le mekɛ mɔɔ Arane maa ɔ maamɔ nɔhalɛ nwo kɛlata mɔɔ fɛta na ɔdwu mekɛ nwo mɔɔ fane ninyɛne mɔɔ ɔlɛyɛ la anwo.

Ninyɛne Nee Ɛzonlenlɛ Gyima Nwo Gyimalilɛ: Ezukoa dodo mɔɔ Aban tua kola yɛ ye gyima ngakyile na ɔfa ɔmaa menli dɔɔnwo ne ɛzonlenlɛ gyima.

Value Added Tax (VAT): Toole ne mɔɔ bɛyɛ bɛto ninyɛne nee nzonle nwo zo wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛlɛyɛ bɛ kɔ nyunlu wɔ debie biala mɔɔ bɛyɛ la anu.
