THE WATCHTOWER ANNOUNCING JEHOVAH’S KINGDOM

STUDY ARTICLES: DECEMBER 7, 2026–JANUARY 3, 2027

1926

A hundred years have passed

ON January 1, 1926, The Watchtower said: “It will not be long before God will make his name known to all. There will be people who will be his witnesses on the earth.”

From the year 1926 onwards, Jehovah, through the head of the congregation, Jesus Christ, helped Christians to see that the most important work in human life is the Kingdom preaching work. Therefore, for the Kingdom preaching work to make progress, “the faithful and discreet slave” had to help God’s worshippers obtain new publications, make changes regarding our meetings, and clarify matters so that it would especially help Christians understand how they ought to conduct their affairs. (Matt. 24:45) Jesus also provided them with all the help they needed. Among these were buildings and machines that would help the work to make progress.

A NEW BOOK AND SOME REVISIONS CONCERNING OUR MEETINGS

In the month of April, the magazine called The Golden Age (now called Awake!) announced that a new book had been released. The title of the book was Deliverance. This new book was to replace the Studies in the Scriptures in the preaching work. However, there was a need to produce many copies of this new book. Therefore, our brothers at Bethel purchased a printing machine. Every single minute, the machine could print 22 books. Our sisters bound these books by hand, so they made use of every opportunity they had to distribute the books to people in the preaching work.

The Bible Students spent many years learning many things from the book *Studies in the Scriptures* at their meetings. But from the month of May, they no longer studied this book at their meetings; it was the *Watch Tower* magazine that they now studied at the meetings. What benefit came from this change?

One congregation wrote to Brother Rutherford: “Every Sunday we study the Watchtower magazine. We are very happy because we always learn new things at the proper time. It is like a new dawn that is breaking.”

“That new view” showed that the Bible Students had understood matters very well, seeing that they should devote themselves to the Kingdom-preaching work, because it was the most important work. In that illustration, a study article in a Watchtower magazine stated that the most important thing was not just trying to be Christians with good conduct, being humble, and having respect for people. If they did only that, they would turn their minds away from the Kingdom-preaching work. The study article emphasized that Jehovah gave Christians a command to be his witnesses. Because they loved Jehovah, every Christian had to obey this command and be ready to do the Kingdom-preaching work.

One study article also had the title “Sacrifice and Service.” The study article showed that it is important for true Christians to worship Jehovah in truth and to show that they love him. It also stated that all Bible Students had to do their utmost to preach the good news of the Kingdom as much as they could, thus preaching the good news to many people as well.—Isa. 43:10, 12; Matt. 24:14.

It was not easy to make this change, but members in the congregations benefited. In some areas, a letter was published in the July 15, 1926 Watch Tower. The letter stated: “The study articles ‘Character or Covenant—Which?’ and ‘Sacrifice and Service’ helped us greatly. It truly touched our hearts and strengthened us. Now, we read and also study. Our brothers rejoice over these things. Because during the study, they have the opportunity to comment.”

THE LOVE STRENGTHENED GOD'S PEOPLE TO ZEALOUSLY CARRY OUT THE KINGDOM PREACHING WORK

Towards the end of May, Bible Students from all over attended a convention held in London, England. At that time, the places where books were usually printed were not working that day. Therefore, it was difficult for our sisters to find a place to print our literature. So, our brothers working at Brooklyn Bethel put in a lot of overtime to print 117,000 tracts. These tracts contained some advice for God’s people. The title of the publication was The Standard for the People.

On Saturday, May 29, our brothers and sisters began to use these tracts, going out and distributing the tracts to people. On that day, the meeting strengthened them very much. The resolution of the meeting urged them: “All Bible Students here, we are calling on all of you to put yourselves into the kingdom proclamation work, proclaiming our King Jesus and his Kingdom. Go immediately to Alexandra Palace; our brothers who are there will show you what you should do.”

Our brothers distributed over 100,000 tracts. The July 15, 1926 Watch Tower stated: “Our brothers and sisters had boundless joy. Truly, they saw that they had obeyed the voice of Jehovah. Jehovah said: ‘You are my witnesses, and I am your God.’”

The next morning, Brother Rutherford gave a talk at the gathering place, the Royal Albert Hall. The talk was about the problems that would affect the strong rulership of the world. The title of the talk was “Why World Powers Are Tottering—The Remedy.” Soon, the entire venue for the talk was filled with people. There were 13,000 people at the venue where the talk was being given, yet people kept coming. Therefore, some people stood outside the venue to listen to the talk, so large speakers were connected outside so that all the people could listen and hear the talk clearly.

From our Brother Rutherford's talk, he showed that the world rulers would struggle in vain, because they refused to accept Jehovah and His Kingdom. Our Brother Rutherford also showed what would happen to the League of Nations, saying that it would cease to exist.

SOME OF OUR BROTHERS WERE SENT TO HELP PROGRESS THE KINGDOM PREACHING WORK

From 1894 to 1926, the organization sent out brothers to strengthen the Bible Students. But from July 1926 onward, the organization made some changes regarding the brothers sent out to represent the organization. Their work became like that of regional overseers. Previously, they visited the brothers for one day, but from that time on, they spent two days visiting the brothers. In addition, they were required to spend 100 hours in the preaching work each month.

According to a letter in The Watch Tower, our Brother George Draper, who was one of the traveling overseers, said: “It is important that all our overseers and brothers prepare themselves to do the Kingdom preaching work, reading The Watch Tower and giving the appropriate encouragement to their fellow believers. [Isa. 56:10] For this reason, I am striving to make every effort to do so.” In 1938, Brother Draper died, finishing his earthly course. He was an anointed man, so we are confident that he is now in heaven.

A MAJOR ACCIDENT THAT HAPPENED IN NEW YORK CITY

Towards the end of the year 1926, a convention was held from October 14 to 18 in New York City. At that gathering, our Brother Rutherford delivered a powerful speech entitled “Earth’s Greatest Conflict Near.” Therefore, many of the country's newspaper agencies agreed to publish the speech in their newspapers so that many people could read it. However, when the gathering was about to begin, all the major newspaper agencies refused to do what they had said they would do.

These developments prompted our brothers to go out and tell people about what the news agencies had done. Therefore, on Friday, October 15, our Brother R. J. Martin released a tract entitled Against the People. This tract exposed the underhanded deeds of the news agencies, which had prevented many people from hearing the good news. The tract also contained some of Brother Rutherford's speeches for people to read. Brother Martin said: “Now we have a press that can print our literature. Our brothers at this Bethel branch will work hard day and night until we have printed 1,250,000 copies of the tract. When we finish printing, we will tell our brothers and sisters in this assembly to take the tracts out and distribute them to the people.” Therefore, when one session of the assembly ended, our brothers and sisters began distributing the tracts. By daybreak, they had distributed 300,000 tracts.

On Saturday and Sunday as well, our brothers and sisters distributed about 1,000,000 tracts. Also, because our brothers worked with zeal in the preaching work, they distributed 3,783 copies of the Deliverance book. In addition, other books distributed were 35,140 in number.

LIFE EXPERIENCES Jehovah Gives Us Strength When We Are Weak

Emma is a sister of ours who works in a hotel. She usually worked three shifts a week, nine hours each day. She told the man in charge of the hotel that she wanted to ask for just one day off to attend a convention of ours. The man refused, but told her that from that time on she would instead work five days every week. He also said that if she wanted, she could quit the job. Emma trusted in Jehovah and knew that he would provide for her needs. Therefore, she was prepared to quit the job and attend the convention.

Emma wrote a letter to give and show that she had resigned from the job. When she went to deliver the letter, the director of the workplace was present. When Emma explained everything to the director, he told her not to quit the job, but that he would arrange things to help her so she could continue working. However, two days later, Emma was told that she had been dismissed from the workplace. But here is the strange part of the matter: she met the director again and explained the situation to him. The director became very angry because he said that the man managing the workplace had not acted on his instructions. Therefore, he arranged the matter himself. He re-hired Emma at the workplace, but told her that she would work five days every week. On each day, she would work for only four hours. This arrangement was better for Emma than the previous one, because now she could get more time to do her pioneer work while still doing more things for Jehovah in her worship. Truly, Emma was not expecting such things, but if you look at this event in Emma's life, you will see that Jehovah was involved in her affairs and helped her in the appropriate way.
