NEƐMA A ƐYƐE A ƐMAA OPETE TENA NWURA MU DAADAATOƆ

Bere bi na Pɛtɛ ne Kwaakwaadabi te hɔ, ɛnna Kwaakwaadabi see sɛ, “Ma yɛnkɔ na yɛnkɔsen twene na yɛmbɔ.” Wɔsii mu kɔɔ kwaeɛ mu, na wɔkɔtoo ogya dua bi. Ɛhɔ ara a Pɛtɛ hunuu ogya dua no, ɔsee sɛ, “Yei deɛ me dea a, efisɛ me deɛ, me na meyɛ panyin, enti mɛtwa kɛseɛ yi.” Kwaakwaadabi see sɛ, “Mate.” Kwaakwaadabi nso, bere a ɔrehwehwɛ baabi no, ɔhunuu Dubini dua bi sɛ esi hɔ, ɔno nso kɔtwae. Wɔn baanu nyinaa senee atwene. Bere a Pɛtɛ bɔɔ ne twene no, na ɛte sɛ yei pɛpɛɛpɛ:

Futuru! Futuru! Menya (me deɛ) ntɛm!

Pɛtɛ Kwakye! Menya (me deɛ) ntɛm!

Berɛ a Kwaakwaadabi bɔɔ ne deɛ no, ɛgyee dɛ sɛm:

Kwaakwaadabi, Kwa! Kwa!

Kwaakwaadabi, Kwa! Kwa!

Wɔkɔɔ so bɔeɛ, na Onyankopɔn teeɛ, enti ɔsomaa Akroma (sɛ ɔnkɔfrɛ wɔn); na Akroma baeɛ, (na ɔbɔɔ wɔn pɔ) "Agooo!" Na wɔgyee so sɛ, "Amee!" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Nyankopɔn se ɔtee asaw sanku bi wɔ ha enti momfa mmra mmɛbɔ mma ɔntie." Mmoa no sɔre kɔɔ Nyankopɔn anim; na wɔbɔeɛ, bɔeɛ, bɔeɛ, na Onyankopɔn kaa sɛ, "Mate sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ; nnwom mmienu no nyinaa yɛ dɛ, enti mede biribi rekye mo (mu biara)." Ɛnna Nyankopɔn kɔfaa nnaka mmienu de bɛsii hɔ, baako akyi yɛ fɛ yie sɛ wobɛhwɛ; nanso fi ayɛ mu ma akɔsi ano; baako nso, ɛho yɛ tan pa ara; sika kɔkɔɔ ayɛ mu ma akɔsi ano. Nyankopɔn kae, (sɛ), "Mo baanu mu biara mmra mmɛfa." Pɛtɛ kaa sɛ, "Mene panin, enti ma menkɔfa baako kan." Ɔkɔeɛ no, ɔkɔyii nea ɛyɛ fɛ yie no, na Kwaakwaadabi nso faa nea ɛho yɛ tan yie no. Wɔmaa so kɔɔ wɔn akuraa. Wɔkɔbuei no, Pɛtɛ no, ɔhwɛɛ ne dea no mu no, afideɛ a ɛyɛ tan nko ara. Kwaakwaadabi dea no mu no, ɔhwɛɛ mu no, sika kɔkɔɔ a ɛrehyerɛn kɔkɔɔ sɛ biribi. Ankyɛ koraa na ɔbɔfoɔ bi ba bɛka kyerɛɛ Pɛtɛ (sɛ), "Wɔasoma me sɛ memmɛka sɛ w'asebaa awu." Ɔno (Pɛtɛ) kaa sɛ, "Kwaakwaadabi, ma me sika kɔkɔɔ pɔw baako na memfa mmɔ me nsa na menkɔ ayie no." Ɔnyaa sika pɔw no bɔɔ ne nsa, na ɔkɔɔ ayie no. Ɔduruu hɔ no, ɔkaa sɛ ɔrekɔ ne baabi; ɔsii fam tee ne ho, nanso bere a ɔrebɛsɔre no, sika pɔw no twitwiiɛ, na ɛyerae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "O, Kwaakwaadabi agyapadeɛ no ayera, na mɛyɛ dɛn na matumi akɔ fie?" Enti ɔhuruw kɔwuraa fi no mu hwehwɛɛ (sika pɔw no). Ɛno nti na wobɛhunu Pɛtɛ no, bere biara na ɔretutuo, tutuo adeɛ wɔ tiafi hɔ, efisɛ ɔrehwehwɛ Kwaakwaadabi sika pɔw no de akɔma no.

Ɛno nti na Mpanyinfoɔ se, "Anibereɛ nyɛ."

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, ebi nkɔ na ebi mmra.
