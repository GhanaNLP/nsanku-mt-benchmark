DEƐ ƐYƐEƐ A ƐKƆDURUU SƐ PƐTƐ TENA SUMINA SO DAADA

Pɛtɛ ne Kwaakwaadabi tenaa ase pɛn, na Kwaakwaadabi kaa sɛ, “Ma yɛnkɔ na yɛnkɔsen twene na yɛmbɔ.” Wɔsii mu kɔɔ kwae mu, na wɔkɔtoo onyina. Ɛbɛyɛɛ pɛ sɛ Pɛtɛ hunuu ogya dua no, ɔkaa sɛ, “Yei yɛ me dea, ɛfiri sɛ, me deɛ, mene panin no, enti mɛtwa kɛseɛ yi.” Kwaakwaadabi kaa sɛ, “Mate.” Kwaakwaadabi nso, berɛ a ɔrehwehwɛ baabi no, ɔhunuu Tweneboa dua bi sɛ ɛsi hɔ, ɔno nso kɔtwai. Wɔn baanu nyinaa sɛn akyene. Berɛ a Pɛtɛ bɔɔ ne twene no, na ɛte pɛpɛɛpɛ sɛ sei:

Futuru! Futuru! Nkyɛnkyɛn ara na manya (me deɛ)!

Kɔtɛkɔ Kwakye! Menya (medeɛ) ntɛm!

Bere a Kwaakwaadabi bɔɔ ne dea no, ɛgyee sɛ:

Kwaakwaadabi, Kwa! Kwa!

Kwaakwaadabi, Kwa! Kwa!

Wɔkɔɔ so bɔeɛ, na Onyankopɔn teeɛ, na ɔsomaa Akroma (sɛ ɔnkɔfrɛ wɔn); na Akroma baeɛ, (na ɔbɔɔ wɔn pɔ) "Agooo!" Na wɔgyee so sɛ, "Amee!" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Onyankopɔn se ɔate asaw-nnwom bi wɔ ha enti momfa mmra mmɛbɔ mma no ntie." Mmoa no tuu kwan kɔɔ Onyankopɔn anim; na wɔbɔeɛ, na wɔbɔeɛ, na wɔbɔeɛ, na Onyankopɔn s kaa sɛ, "Matee sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ; nnwom mmienu no yɛ dɛ, enti mede biribi rekyɛ mo (baako biara)." Ɛnna Nyankopɔn kɔfaa adaka mmienu de bɛsisii hɔ, baako no akyi yɛ fɛ yiye sɛ wode w'ani behu; efi ahyɛ mu ma kɔpem ano sɛɛ; baako no nso, na ɛyɛ nea ɛyɛ tan pa ara; sika kɔkɔɔ ahyɛ mu ma kɔpem ano sɛɛ. Nyankopɔn kae, (se), "Mo baanu no mu biara mmra mmɛfa." Pɛtɛ se, "Mene panin no, enti ma me nkɔfa baako kan." Ɔkɔe no, ɔyii deɛ ɛyɛ fɛ pa ara no, berɛ a Kwaakwaadabi nso faa deɛ ɛyɛ fi pa ara no. Wɔmaa so kɔɔ wɔn akuraa ase. Wɔkɔbuee ano no, Pɛtɛ, berɛ a ɔhwɛɛ ne deɛ no mu no, amɛteɛ a ɛyɛ fi. Kwaakwaadabi deɛ no mu no, berɛ a ɔhwɛɛ mu no, na sika kɔkɔɔ retwerɛm kɔkɔɔ te sɛ biribiara. Ankyɛ koraa na ɔsomafoɔ bi bae bɛka kyerɛɛ Pətəprow (sɛ), "Wɔasoma me sɛ memmɛka sɛ wo ksew awu." Ɔno (Pətəprow) kaa sɛ, "Kwaakwaadabi, ma me sika kɔkɔɔ pɔkɔ baako na menkyekyere ngu me nsa ho na menkɔ ayie no ase." Ɔnyaa sika pɔkɔ no de kyekyeree ne ho, na ɔkɔɔ ayie no ase. Ɔduruu hɔ no, ɔkaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ ɔkɔ ne ho so; ɔkɔtɔɔ fam, nanso berɛ a ɔrebetumi asɔre no, agudeɛ no firi ne ho tɔeɛ, na ɛyerae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "O, Kwaakwaadabi agyapadeɛ no ayera, na mɛyɛ dɛn akɔ fie?" Enti ɔhuruw kɔwuraa efi no mu kɔhwehwɛɛ (agudeɛ no). Ɛno nti na wobehu Pɛtɛ sɛ, dabiara ɔretutu, ɔretutu tiafi mu, efisɛ ɔrehwehwɛ Kwaakwaadabi sikakɔkɔɔ no de abrɛ no.

Ɛno nti na Mpanyimfoɔ ka sɛ, "Adurobon nyɛ."

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, ebi nkɔ na ebi mmra.
