Siw Satan No Nyansa Nye N’akwan Nyina Do Kwan—Nya Ahotoso Wɔ Jehovah Na Jesus Mu

NDWOM 55 Mmosuro Hɔn!

FA W’ADWEN SI DO Mma mma Satan nndaadaa wo dɛ obesiw kwan ma woantse Jehovah Nyankopɔn nye Jesus Christ ase yie.

“Mompaw Abɔnsam, na oboguan efi hom nkyɛn.”—JAS. 4:7.

EFI ber a yesi dɛ yɛbɔsom Jehovah pɔw no, Satan na n’abɔnsamhonam bɛyɛ hɛn atamfo a wonsosɔw mu da. Naaso Satan nyim dɛ nkotum edzi hɛn do konyim ber tsentsen a Jehovah bɔbɔ hɛn ekyir no. Dɛm ntsi Abɔnsam bɔ mbɔdzen dɛ obosɛɛ mboa a yedya no dze nam dɛ ɔbɛyɛ ma yɛatwe hɛnho efi Jehovah ho do. Kwan kor a ɔfa do yɛ iyi nye dɛ ɔbɔbɔ mbɔdzen ama yaagye akyinnye dɛ Jehovah nye ne Ba no wɔ hɛn afa ampa.—2 Cor. 11:14; Jas. 1:6.

2 Sɛ akofo no wotu sa a, ɔwɔ dɛ wonya awerɛhyɛmu wɔ hɔn akandifo mu. Sɛ wonyaa awerɛhyɛmu a ɔtse dɛm a, wobotum ayɛ ndzɛmba a hɔn ankasa pɛ. Iyi botum ama wɔahwer hɔn nkwa. Dɛmara so na yɛyɛ asraafo wɔ sunsum fam kwan do a yɛnye “sunsum bɔne dɔm” roko. (Eph. 6:12) Satan Ɔbɔnsam nyim dɛ sɛ yɛhwer awerɛhyɛmu a yɛwɔ wɔ Jehovah Nyankopɔn na Jesus Christ mu a, obotum ama yɛapo hɔn akwankyerɛ na hɔn mboa. Dɛm ntsi, ɔyɛ biribiara a obotum dze asɛe awerɛhyɛmu a yɛwɔ wɔ hɔn mu no. Wɔ asɛm yi mu no, yebesusu mbrɛ ɔdze dɛm kwan yi dzii dwuma wɔ tsetse mber mu na mbrɛ ɔda ho ara dze dzi dwuma ndɛ ho. Afei yebohu mbrɛ yebotum ayɛ etsia n’adwenbɔn no. Wɔ asɛm a odzi hɔ no mu no, yebosusu akwan ebien fofor a Satan dze dzi dwuma tsia hɛn wɔ hɛn sunsum fam ko no mu no ho.*

* Ɔnnyɛ sɔhwɛ nyinara a yehyia no na Satan dze ba tẽẽ. Naaso ɔno na ɔma “wiase sunsum no” kɔ do, a ɔkanyan atsɔrtsɔrfo ma wɔyɛ Nyankopɔn n’asomfo basabasa. Dɛm ntsi tẽẽ o, anaa kwan fofor do o, Satan na ɔwɔ nsɛndzendzen pii a yehyia wɔ hɛn sunsum afamu koadzi no ekyir.—1 Cor. 2:12; 1 John 5:19.

Satan Kaa Jehovah Na Jesus Ho Nsɛm A Ɔnntse Mpaa

3 Nyimpa abakɔsɛm ahyɛse no, Satan bɔɔ mbɔdzen dɛ ɔbɛma Adam na Eve efi Jehovah ho. Satan daadaa Eve ma ɔgyee dzii dɛ no Bɔfo no nndwen n’asodzi pa ho. Satan bisaa no dɛ: “Nyankopɔ aka ampa dɛ mma mmanndzidzi turo no mu dua biara do?” (Gen. 3:1-5) Satan ma Eve gyee dzii dɛ Jehovah rehyɛ ɔno na no kun do ahyɛdze a ntease nnyi mu. Awerɛhosɛm nye dɛ, Eve na ekyir no Adam dɔm ɔtamfo Satan. Wɔyɛɛ asoɔden tsiaa Jehovah—Nyia ɔmaa hɔn biribiara no.—Gen. 3:6.

4 Fitsi Adam na Hawa ber do no, Satan akɔ do asɛɛ Jehovah dzin. Dɛm ntsi, Satan kanyan Job n’anyɛnkofo atorfo no ma wɔmaa Job gyei akyinnye dɛ Jehovah dɔ no na N’ani sɔ n’anokwasɛm kwan no. (Job 22:1-3) Ɔbɔnsam susui dɛ amandze a ohyiae no bɛma Job epo Jehovah. Ɔwɔ mu dɛ na Satan n’apam no ma Job hyɛ nhyɛdo kɛse ase dze, nanso dɛm banyin nokwafo no kɔr do dzii no botae ho bem, a n’enyi emmfi n’adamfofa a ɔnye Nyankopɔ wɔ no do da.—Job 27:1, 5.

5 Mfehaha pii akyir ber a Jesus baa asaase do no, Satan bɔɔ mbɔdzen dɛ ɔbɔsow nyimpa kwan dɛ wɔnnkɔ do nndzi Jesus ekyir. Kwan kor a ɔfaa do yɛɛ iyi nye dɛ ɔkanyan asɔfo atsenaehemfo a wɔwɔ ekyirkyireyɛ nye afofor ma wɔhɛɛ nkontompo fir Jesus ho. Na ewiei koraa no, wɔkũũ no dɛ mbodmbodnkyerɛfo a wɔyɛ ebubunsɛm.—Kenkan

Matthew 11:19; 27:27-31; Mark 14:55, 56; John 8:41-45.

6 Jesus no wu ekyir no, Satan dween Jesus n’ekyirdzifo no edwuma no ho. Mfehyɛse no, Abɔnsam bɔɔ mbɔdzen dɛ ɔdze adwenbɔn bɛhyɛ nkorɔfo mu afa Jesus ne wusɔr no ho. Satan ne nhyɛdo ase no, atorsɛm bi trɛwee dɛ Jesus n’esuafo no ebowia ne nyimpadua no. (Matt. 28:11-15) Nna nda a odzii do no mu no, Satan bɔɔ mbɔdzen dɛ ɔnam ɔtaa do bɛhyɛ asafo no ahuranhyɛ. Satan pɛɛ dɛ ɔma edwuma a hɔn Wura no dze ahyɛ hɔn nsa no yɛ dzen pii ma hɔn dɛ wɔbɛyɛ.—Asomafo. 7:57–8:1; 12:1-3; 28:22.

7 Ɔwɔ mu dɛ tsetse Christianfo no binom gyaa asɛnkaw no osiandɛ nkyerɛwee no kɔr do ntsi, naaso hɔn mu dodow noara gyinaa pintsinn na wɔpoo dɛ wɔbɔyɛ tsinn. Wɔhyehyɛɛ no dɛ Satan nye adaemone no na wɔyɛ hɔn atamfo paapaa. (Asomafo 4:18-20; 1 Pet. 5:8) Na ndɛ nso ɛ? Akwanso horow bɛn na Satan nam do rebɔ mbɔdzen dɛ obesi hɛn kwan ma yaagyaa Jehovah som?

(Jas. 4:8) Naaso, Satan nso pɛ dɛ ɔsɛe Jehovah no nkorɔfo hɔn gyedzi.

BIBLE ADZESUA A WƆNTUA HWEE

Yɛko tsia Satan n’abɔmbɔdze dzenam susuhwɛ a yebosusu kwan a yebesi do akɔ do anya abusuabɔ kɛse a ɔbɛn Jehovah mu (Hwɛ nkyekyɛm 14)

gyee nsɛm a nkontompō kakra wɔ mu anaa ator a emu da hō fann no dzii. Naaso sɛ yɛsua Bible no na hɛn nwoma ahorow no na yɛnye Christianfo a wɔabir kɔ ehyiadzi daa a, yɛremmpow hɛnho mberɛwtsen. Sɛ yenhim nokwar no yie mpo a, ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yɛatumi asiw Satan ne mbɔdzembɔ a ɔrebɔ dɛ ɔbɛyɛ hɛn gyedzi mberɛw no kwan.—Mbe. 13:20; Ndw. 17:11.

15 Sɛ yɛhwehwɛ Bible no mu yie a, yebotum ahyɛ yɛn ahotoso no dzen dɛ Jehovah na Jesus wɔ yɛn afamu. (Prov. 22:19)

Sɛ yedwen kyerɛwtohɔ ahorow a ɔkyerɛ mbrɛ Jehovah na Jesus dwen adasamba ho dɔnkye no ho a, ɔbɛkã hɛn ma yɛade hɔn dɛ anyɛnkofo a yebotum dze hɛn werdam hɔn do. Ahotoso ahorow pii a ɔkyerɛ dɛ Jehovah na Jesus dɔ hɛn ahyɛ Bible no mã. Dɛ nhwɛdo no, Jehovah ka nkãtsenkyerɛ a ɔwɔ ma no nkorɔfo ho asɛm dɛ ɔyɛ dzen sen dzea maame a ɔroma no ba nufo wɔ ma no ba. (Isa. 49:15) Obiara a no dɔ gye n’ebir no, obu no dɛ “agyapade a ɔsom bo.” (Mal. 3:16, 17, ftn.) Na Bible no yiyi mbrɛ Jesus dɔ a ɔwɔ ma hɛn mu dɔ fa kyerɛ dzenam n’asɛm a ɔkãe faa n’afɔrbɔ ho a ɔkoraa do no do.—John 15:12, 13.

15. Yebesi dɛn etum ahyɛ hɛn gyedzi a yɛwɔ dɛ Jehovah na Jesus wɔ hɛn afamu no dzen?

(Nyan. 20:1-3, 10) Mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a yebotumi agyina hɛn atamfo no hɔn anofew ano no, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛkɔ do nya abusuabɔ kɛse a yɛnye Jehovah wɔ no, na yɛkɔ do gye dzi dɛ ɔno nye ne Ba a ɔdɔ no no dɔ hɛn na hɔn anyi sɔ hɛn. Ɔno a, dɛ mbrɛ Jesus kãe no, obiara runntum ‘ntwetwe [hɔn] mfi m’Egya nsamu.’—Kenkan John 10:29; Jas. 4:7.

Ibotum Edzi Konyim Wɔ Ɔko No Mu!

“Yɛnye mogya na honam nnyɛ aperper, na mbom . . . sunsum bɔne dɔm a wɔwɔ sorbea hɔ no.” —EF. 6:12.

DƐ JEHOVAH n’asomfo no, yɛnnkɔ honam fam akwambɔ. (Isa. 2:4; John 18:36) Nanso, Bible no ka akokoakoko bi a yɛn mu biara nntum nnguan ho asɛm—akokoakoko a yɛnye honhom bɔnefo roko. (Kan Ephesians 6:11, 12.) Yɛbɛyɛ dɛn atum adi atamfo kɛse a wɔtse dɛm no do nkonyim? Dza ɔsen biribiara no, ɔwɔ dɛ yɛdze yɛn ho to Jehovah n’aboa do. Nanso ɔwɔ dɛ yehu ndwuma bɔne na anofafa kwan a Satan na ne sunsum bɔnefo no dze yɛ yɛn na yɛn sunsum fam ebusua no dɔm so.

2 Wɔ adzesua a odzi kan no mu no, yɛhwɛɛ mbrɛ Abɔnsam bɔ mbɔdzen dɛ ɔbɛsɛe ahotoso a yɛwɔ wɔ Jehovah Nyankopɔn na Jesus Christ mu. Wɔ adzesua yi mu no, yebosusu akwan fofor ebien a Satan fa do kɔ do ko tsia hɛn no ho. Ɔbɔ mbɔdzen dɛ ɔbɛsɛe koroyɛ a ɔda hɛn na hɛn nuanom mbanyin na mbaa ntamu no. Na ɔkanyan ɔsɔretia so dze bɔ mbɔdzen dɛ obetwe hɛn adwen anaa obohuahun hɛn, a n'enyi da do dɛ obesiw hɛn kwan ma yɛannkɔ do annyɛ edwuma a Jehovah dze ama hɛn dɛ yɛnyɛ no.

Dza nna ɔkɔ do wɔ kurow no apon ano wɔ Bible mber mu?

KUROW no n’apon nnyɛ beebi a wɔfa hɔ kɔ kurow no mu kɛkɛ. Osian dɛ nkorɔfo pii faa hɔ nti, ɔbɛyɛɛ mbea a dwumadzi pii kɔ do da biara. Bible no ka kurow n’apon ho asɛm bɔbor mprɛn 50, na ɔka dwumadzi ahorow a nna wɔyɛ wɔ hɔ no ho asɛm.

Nkorɔfo boaboaa hɔn ho ano wɔ kurow n’apon ano. Hebri asɛmfua a wɔkyerɛ ase dɛ “pon” no kyerɛ biribi kɛse sen kurow no n’abow a ɔwɔ enyim ara kwa. Ɔkyerɛ beebi a woesisie nkyɛnkyɛn a ɔwɔ kurow bi n’anoboabow ano. Wɔ mbeambea binom no, na adwa akɛse a baguafo gu do wɔ apon no mu hɔ a nkorɔfo pii betum ehyia wɔ hɔ.—Neh. 8:1.

Eguadzifo yɛɛ eguadzi bea wɔ kurow no apon ahorow no ho. Jerusalem apon binom, tse dɛ Nsunam Pon no nye Nguambaa Pon no, bi a wonyaa hɔn dzin fii ndzɛmba a nna wɔtɔn wɔ hɔ no mu.—Zeph. 1:10; John 5:2.

Nkorɔfo dziiguatsir na hɔn ara hɔn asɛm ho dwuma wɔ kurow no apon ano. Abraham na Boaz yɛɛ amansan dwumadzi wɔ adansefo enyim wɔ kurow no apon ano. (Gen. 23:10-15, 17, 18; Ruth 4:1, 10) Akwantufo binom da baguam guadzibea a ɔbɛn kurow no apon ano no sɛ obi annye hɔn fɛw so a. (Atsɛm. 19:15-21) Nkorɔfo kɔ kurow no apon ano nso dɛ wɔrokɛtse amandzɛɛ fofor no.—2 Sam. 18:24, 25.

Atsɛmbuafo no buu atsɛn wɔ kurow no apon ano. Wobuu atsɛn wɔ adansifo a wotumi tee dza wodzi dase faa ho nye dza efii mu bae no nyina enyisido. (Deut. 21:18-20) Dɛm bea a obiara tumi kɔ yi hyɛɛ atsɛmbuafo no nkuran ma wodzii bem na woyɛɛ pɛrpɛr wɔ hɔn atsɛmbu mu. Sɛ atsɛmbuafo no si gyinae wie pɛ a, mpanyimfo no dze asotwe no yɛɛ edwuma amonom hɔ ara. (Deut. 25:2) Wɔ kurow no apon ano,

Kurow no apon ahorow no nna ɔyɛ mbea a dabiara asetsena mu ndwuma gu do kɔ do kɛse

Nna wɔhwɛ kwan dɛ atsembuafo bɔma pɛrpɛryɛ nye suban pa ahyeta.—Deut. 16:18-20; Job 29:7, 11-17.

Nokwafo mbanyin hyɛɛ Israelman a wɔwɔ kurow no apon ano no nkuran dɛ wɔmfa hɔn ho nto Jehovah do. Nkɔnhyɛfo na afofor kãa asɛm wɔ apon no ano osiandɛ nna wonyim dɛ nkorɔfo boaboa hɔn ho ano wɔ hɔ. (1 Ki. 22:10; Jer. 17:19, 20) Dɛ nhwɛdo no, Jehu kãa wɔ nkorɔfo no nyina enyim dɛ Jehovah ahyɛ N’asɛm a ɔfa Ahab fie ho no mã. (2 Ki. 10:8-10) Ɔhen Hezekiah boaboaa n’asraafo asafohene ano wɔ kurow pon ano dɛ ɔrehyɛ hɔn nkuran ma wɔdze hɔn ho ato Jehovah mboa do.—2 Chron. 32:6-8.

Bible no kã dɛ “nyansa pa” teamu na ɔma ne nze do “wɔ kurow n’apon ano.” (Mbe. 1:20, 21) Tsetse Israel no, kurow n’apon no ano na mbarimba anokwafo tuu fo a nyansa womu na wobuu atsen pɛr. Ndɛ, wɔakyerɛw Jehovah ne nyansa wɔ Bible no mu na n’asomfo a wɔwɔ mbea nyina roboa nkorɔfo ma wɔadan ekyir akɔ nyansa pa yi ne fibea hɔ.

March 15, 2011, The Watchtower no kã dɛ: “Dɛ mbra a almond dua no ‘nyan’ ntsɛm no, dɛmara na Jehovah nso wɔ mfatoho kwan do ‘soɛr ntsɛm’ . . . dɛ ɔrebɔ n’asefo kɔkɔ wɔ asoɔden ho sunsumadze ho. . . . Na ɔronngye n’ahome—ɔbɛkɔ do ‘awe’—kɛpem dɛ n’nkɔnhyɛ asɛm no bɛba mu.”—Jer. 1:11, 12.

Wɔdze almond dua no nhyiren fitaa no so dzi dwuma wɔ Bible mu dze kyerɛ nyin a obi enyin ma ne tsir nhwi efuw fitaa. (Nseb. 12:5) Na ebisa a, osian sɛnea n’ahoɔfɛ no tse ntsi, dua no nhyiren no bɛyɛɛ nhwɛdo maa nkuruwa a na ɛwɔ ntamadan no kɛndua no mman no ho no.—Ex. 37:17-20.
