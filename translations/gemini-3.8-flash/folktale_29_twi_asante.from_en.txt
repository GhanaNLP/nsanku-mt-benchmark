Yɛnni aseɛ koraa, yɛnni aseɛ koraa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokorɛ)

SƐNEA OBI NYA NKYERƐKYERƐMU A ƆDE MAA AGYIRAEHYƐDEƐ SO NYAA YERE (NA ƆSAN NYAA) NE NKWA

ƐBERƐ bi na mmarima baasa bi wɔ hɔ a na wɔpɛ adwuma ayɛ. Wɔkɔɔ Sare-so kɔtɔɔ nkyekye ntoma ne nnoɔma foforɔ. (Wɔresan aba Ashanti no) wɔmaa wɔn wɔfase soaa nnoɔma no bi, na wɔnam kwan so tɔntɔneeɛ. Wɔbaa kuro kɛseɛ bi so. Kuro no mu na Ɔhemaa bi babaa wɔ a obiara a wɔde no bɛma no awareɛ no, na ɔmpɛ. Na ahɔhoɔ yi (aguadifoɔ no) kɔɔ hɔ (ne fie), nanso Ɔhemaa no ba no na wakɔ "abɔ" ntoma hohoroɔ. (Nanso) Ɔhemaa no tɔɔ ntoma mmienu de sie maa ne babaa no. Berɛ a ne ba no baeɛ no, ɔhunuu mmarima baasa no. Ɔka kyerɛɛ n'aberewa sɛ, "Ɛnnɛ mahunu deɛ me kɔn dɔ no, enti kum odwan na memfa nkɔma wɔn." Berɛ a wɔwiee aduane no noa no, ababaawa no kɔfaa aheo dua, asaawa, bise, ne nkateɛ, de guu aduane no atifi sɛ ɔrekɔma mmarima no. Na ɔkaa sɛ, "Deɛ ɔbɛtumi akyerɛ aseɛ no, ɔno na mɛware no." Na wɔde aduane no kɔeɛ. Mmarima no yii so, na wɔkaa sɛ, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ Ɔhemaa no ba na ɔde aduane yi baeɛ a, anka yɛwɔ asɛm a yɛbɛbisa no." Mmarima no wɔfase kaa sɛ, "Sɛ mensuro mo a, anka mɛtumi akyerɛ mo saa nnoɔma no aseɛ." Wɔkaa sɛ, "Kyerɛ aseɛ." Abofra no buaa sɛ, "Abaayewa no se wama n'adwene mu ate sɛ asaawa no, na sɛ ɔbɛnya yɛn a yaba ha yi mu baako a, ɛnsɛ sɛ ɔware anaa? Aheo no asekyerɛ ne sɛ, da bi bɛba a ɔbɛyera; bise no nso asekyerɛ ne sɛ da bi bɛba a ɔbɛma no (ne kunu) awe bise (kyerɛ sɛ, ɔbɛdi awerɛhoɔ). Nkateɛ no kyerɛ sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ wodua nkateɛ ma no, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a afe biara, sɛ ɔkɔhwɛ ntini no a, ɔbɛnya bi atutu." Enti Ɔhemaa no ba no waree mmarima no wɔfase. Berɛ a ɔwaree no no, Ɔhemaa no ba no bisaa no sɛ, "Wo din de sɛn?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ me Kwaku Susufuo," kyerɛ sɛ, Kwaku-a-ɔdwene-ho-firi-akoma-mu. Abaayewa no buaa sɛ, "Me nso, wɔfrɛ me Akua-a-ɔdwene-ho-firi-akoma-mu." Ɔwaree no sɛdeɛ ɛsɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ. Adeɛ kyeeɛ no, wɔfanom baasa no tuu kwan kɔɔ wɔn ankasa kuro mu. Kwaku-a-ɔdwene-ho-firi-akoma-mu kuraa ne yere akuraa hɔ. Ɔtenaa hɔ afe baako, na sɛ ɔsɔre (anɔpa) a, ɔne ne nnamfonom kɔtena ase to nte. Afei, da koro bi a na ɔregoro saa no, ɔbɔɔ atere mmienu prɛko pɛ, nanso nnipa a na ɔne wɔn regoro no kae sɛ wɔremfa mma no, na na wɔpɛ mpo sɛ wɔbɔ no. Ɔdanee n'ani hwɛɛ n'akyi, na ɔhunuu abofra bi sɛ ɔgyina hɔ; ɔfaa ɔboɔ de maa abofra no sɛ ɔmfa nkɔma ne yere. Afei berɛ a ne yere nsa kaa ɔboɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Me kunu pɛ sɛ ɔsan kɔ ne kuro mu, efisɛ ɔka sɛ sɛ anomaa kyɛ fam dodo a, ɔtɔ aboɔ." Na Ɔhemmaa no kae sɛ, "Sɛ ɔrekɔ a, wo ne no nkɔ." Nokorɛm no, ɔne no kɔe. Na wɔhyiaa Kwaku-a-ɔdwene-ho-yie no wɔfanom. Afei, berɛ bi akyi no, wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Kwaku-a-ɔdwene-ho-yie, hwehwɛ ahoma bi na yɛnkɔ wuram nkɔsum afidie mfa nkyere mmorɔnoma na yɛmfa nnye wo yere a ɔyɛ ɔhɔhoɔ no hɔhoo.” Enti wɔtuu kwan, nanso na mpanimfoɔ yi agye atom sɛ wɔbɛku no. Wɔne Kwaku-a-ɔdwene-ho-yie kɔe; wɔduruu baabi a ɛhɔ yɛ dinn wɔ kwan no so; wɔkyekyeree no na wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Yɛbɛku wo.” Ɛnna ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, “Berɛ a mekɔɔ me yere kuro mu no, menyaa sika pii, na sɛ moamma mankyerɛ mo faako a ɛwɔ, na mewuo a, morenhunu.” Wɔbisaa no sɛ, “Ɛhefa na sika no da?” Ɔbuae sɛ, “Monkɔ me yere nkyɛn nkɔbɔ no amanneɛ sɛ matɔ nam, na wɔse ne boɔ yɛ peredwan edu (£80), na ɔnhwehwɛ kotokuo a wɔde adwammaa nwoma ntwitwie ayɛ no mu; sɛ ɔhwɛ hɔ na wanhunu a, ma no nhwɛ Krampan kotokuo no mu, sɛ wanhu no hɔ a, ma no nhwɛ asɛsɛ a nkranteɛ gu mu no mu.” Enti wɔbae bɛbɔɔ ne yere amanneɛ no. Na ɔno nso kae sɛ, “Nnoɔma no wɔ kurom panyin no fie, enti ma yɛnkɔ na yɛnkɔfa.” Ɔne wɔn kɔɔ kurom panyin no fie; na ɔbaa no bɔɔ kurom panyin no amanneɛ, kyerɛkyerɛɛ mu sɛ, “Ahoma kotokuo a wɔde adwammaa nwoma ayɛ no kyerɛ sɛ wɔakyekyere no; Krampan no kyerɛ sɛ akorɔma repɛ sɛ wɔwe ne nam; asɛsɛ a nkranteɛ gu mu no kyerɛ sɛ wɔrebɛtwa ne kɔn. Amonom hɔ ara na kurom panyin no bɔɔ dawuro, sɛ wɔnkɔ hɔ nkɔgye Kwaku-a-ɔdwene-ho-yie. Na wɔne no san bae. Ɔmaa wɔkum mpanimfoɔ baasa no. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Nsɛnkyerɛnne asekyerɛ so na obi nam nyaa yere na (bio nso) ɔde gyee ne nkwa." M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na ma bi nso nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
