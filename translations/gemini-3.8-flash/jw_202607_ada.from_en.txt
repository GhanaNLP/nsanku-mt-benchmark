Kase Ní Kɛ Je Gibeonbhi Ɔmɛ A Ngɔ

“Gibeon bi ɔmɛ kɛ Israel kpa tue, nɛ a kɛ mɛ ya nɔ nɛ a hi si.”—YOS. 10:1.

Jeha a ji 1473 B.C.E. Israel ma a bɔni Si Womi Zugba a nɔ yemi kɛkɛ. Israelbime ɔmɛ ye kunimihi nɛ ngɛ nyakpɛ ngɛ Yeriko kɛ Ai ma amɛ a nɔ. Se pee se ɔ, nɔ́ ko nɛ a hɛ kɛ nɔ́ jɛ we nɔ ba. Gbɛfalɔhi a kuu ko ba a ngɔ, nɛ a ba de ke a je zugba nɛ ngɛ se tsɔ nɔ, nɛ a ngɛ hlae nɛ a kɛ Mawu webi ɔmɛ nɛ so tue gbaami kpaŋmɔ.

2 Gbɛzɛli nɛ ɔmɛ ji Gibeon bi ɔmɛ. Nɛ ɔ ji kekleekle be nɛ a tsĩ Gibeon bi ɔmɛ a tã ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi, se pi nyagbe nɔ ji nɛ ɔ. Baiblo mi sanehi babauu tsɔɔ kaa Gibeon bi ɔmɛ kɛ Israel bi ɔmɛ bɔ kpakpa ngɛ jehahi nɛ nyɛɛ se ɔmɛ a mi. Sane nɛ ɔmɛ hɛɛ nɔ́ kpakpa nɛ wa ma nyɛ maa kase, nɛ a tsɔɔ níhi babauu kɛ kɔ Yehowa Mawu he.

Gibeonbi ɔmɛ kpa Yosua pɛɛ konɛ e ye bua mɛ. (Yos. 10:3-7) Yosua nyɛɛ Israelbi ɔmɛ a hɛ mi kɛ ya he mɛ, nɛ a mɔde bɔmi ɔ ná jɔɔmi. Yehowa nɛ gblegbleehi kɛ tɛ tɔtsɛmɛ babauu kɛ fɔ henyɛli Amoribi ɔmɛ a nɔ, nɛ e ha nɛ je ɔ tso kɛ sɔle ta a nya nyanyaanya hulɔ. (Yos. 10:9-14) Kɛtsɔ kpaŋmi kɛ ha tue bumi nɛ a hla kɛ je Israelbi ɔmɛ a dɛ, kɛ bɔ nɛ a kpa Yosua pɛɛ kɛ ha yemi kɛ buami benɛ a fia mɛ gbeye ɔ nɔ ɔ, Gibeonbi ɔmɛ tsɔɔ hemi kɛ yemi ngɛ Yehowa mi—hemi kɛ yemi ngɛ e hewami nɛ e ngɛ kɛ ha e munyu ɔ nya bimɔ kɛ a kpɔmi ɔ mi.

6 Mɛni sane nɛ ɔ tsɔɔ wɔ ngɛ Yehowa he? Yehowa baa e he si, nɛ e ngɛ mɔbɔnalemi. Kekle ɔ, e fã Israel ke a “fia nihi nɛ ngɛ zugba a nɔ ɔ tsuo kɛ je lejɛ ɔ,” nɛ Gibeonbi ɔmɛ hu piɛɛ he. (4 Mose 33:51, 52) Se kɛ̃ ɔ, akɛnɛ Israel kɛ mɛ so tue kpee he kpaako heje ɔ, Yehowa na Gibeonbi ɔmɛ mɔbɔ nɛ e ha nɛ a hi si. E ngɛ mi kaa Israelbi ɔmɛ bi we Yehowa lɔyeli loko a so kpaako nɛ ɔ mohu lɛɛ, se Yehowa pee jã. Lɔ ɔ se ɔ, e ye bua Israel kɛ ma nɔ nɛ a ye a somi ɔ nɔ, nɛ e gu nyakpɛ blɔ nɔ nɛ e he Gibeonbi ɔmɛ a yi wami.—Yos. 9:26; 11:19.

7 Mɛni wa ma nyɛ maa pee kɛ kase Gibeon bi ɔmɛ? Wa ma nyɛ maa pee jã kɛ gu hemi kɛ yemi nɛ mi wa nɛ wa maa na ngɛ Yehowa mi ɔ nɔ. Akɛnɛ wa le Yehowa he ní babauu pe Gibeon bi ɔmɛ jamɛ a be ɔ heje ɔ, wa ngɛ yi mi tomi babauu nɛ wa ma da nɔ kɛ fɛɛ wa hɛ kɛ fɔ Yehowa nɔ blɔblɔ. (La 40:4, 5) Jehanɛ se hu ɔ, wa ma nyɛ maa kase Gibeon bi ɔmɛ kɛ gu suɔmi nɛ wa ma na kɛ tsu ní hejɔɔhi ngɛ Yehowa sɔmɔmi mi ɔ nɔ. (Yosh. 9:23, 27) Nyɛminyumu oblahi kake nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ Luke ɔ be be nɛ e na nyɛminyumu nɔkɔtɔma kake nɛ e ngɛ Bɛtɛl weku ɔ mi nɛ e pee jã a hɛ kɛ jeee nɔ gblegbleegble. E ngɛ mi kɛ̃ kaa jamɛ a Bɛtɛl no ɔ tsuɔ ní tsumi nɛ he hia nɛ a le he ní babauu moŋ, se e fã e he ngɛ kpoo mi kɛ sɔle nɛ e kɛ tsu ní ngɛ gbɔɔ mi kɛ bu he nɛ a ngɛ Matsɛ Yemi Kpe He ɔ mae ngɛ ma a mi ɔ he. Luke de ke: “I kase kaa he si bami jeee nɔ́ ko nɛ a nuɔ he pɛ kɛkɛ. Nɔ́ nɛ wa hlaa kaa wa maa pee loo wa be pee ɔ lɛ tsɔɔ kaa wa baa wa he si.” Nyɛ ha nɛ waa je wa hemi kɛ yemi ngɛ Yehowa mi kpo daa ligbi kɛ gu e nɔ nɛ wa maa fɛɛ wa hɛ kɛ fɔ ke wa kɛ si himihi nɛ mi wa kpe, kɛ he si bami nɛ wa kɛ ma tsu teokrasi ní tsumi tsuaa ní tsumi he ní ɔ nɔ.

13 Maa e he jeha 500 se ngɛ Matsɛ David be ɔ se ɔ, a tu Gibeonbi ɔmɛ a he munyu ekeko ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi. Babilon nyɔguɔ yemi jeha 70 ɔ ba nyagbe, nɛ ngɛ jeha 537 B.C.E. mi ɔ, Yudafoi nɛ a ya nyɔguɔ yemi ɔ a kuku nɛ kekle ɔ kpale kɛ ba Yerusalem kɛ piɛɛ Amlalo Zerubbabel he. (Ezra 2:1, 2, 58) Ngɛ jeha 468 B.C.E. mi ɔ, kuku kpa ko hu kpale kɛ ba, se mɛɛ lɛɛ Ezra nɛ ji wolengmabi ɔ lɛ kɛ mɛ ba. Mɛ ji “nihi nɛ pi Israelbi nɛ a sɔmɔɔ ngɛ sɔlemi we ɔ, aloo sɔmɔli,” nɛ ekoɔ maa pee e hiɛ nɛ a ji Gibeonbi ɔmɛ a nina.—Hyɛ Munyuhi A Sisi Tsɔɔmi, “Netinim.”

14 Kane 1 Kronika 9:2 kɛ sisi ningma a. “Sɔlemi we ɔ mi tsunyɔli ɔmɛ” piɛɛ nyɔguɛhi nɛ kɛ mɛ kekle kpale kɛ ba Israel ɔ a he. Enɛ ɔ he hia wawɛɛ ejakaa pi Yuda bi nɛ a ya nyɔguɛyami mi ɔmɛ tsuo nɛ kpale kɛ ba. E be he tsɔmi kaa Yuda bi babauu ná a he si bɔ ko ngɛ Babilon, nɛ a sume nɛ a kɛ nɔ́ ko nɛ sá afɔle kɛ kpale kɛ ba konɛ a ya tsu a ma nɛ a kpata hɛ mi ɔ a he ní. Se nyɔguɛhi nɛ a ye anɔkwale, nɛ a kpɛti ni komɛ ji Gibeon bi a nina amɛ, kɛ ma nɔ mi hami tsui suɔ kaa a maa na nɛ anɔkwale jami ma nɔ koni ngɛ sɔlemi we ɔ nɛ ngɛ Yerusalem ɔ mi, nɛ a bɔ mɔde fɛɛ mɔde nɛ a kpale kɛ ba. Kɛ gu gbɔjɔnɔ kɛ je Yuda bi ɔmɛ a he ɔ, Gibeon bi ɔmɛ a nina amɛ be zugba gbosi ngɛ Israel. Kɛ̃ ɔ, a kpale kɛ ba konɛ a ya hyɛ a sɔlemi we blɔ nya nítsumihi a nɔ nɛ a ya ye bua po kɛ fia Yerusalem gbogbo ɔmɛ.—Neh. 3:26.

Gibeonbi ɔmɛ a ninaahi yi ná ngɛ Yuda kɛ Yerusalem hɛmi kpatami ɔ mi ngɛ jeha 607 B.C.E. E ngɛ ní tsumi kpakpa nɛ a woo lɛ hiɔ nɛ e he wa ngɛ nu tsumi ko mi. Se e suɔ nɛ e pee babauu ngɛ sɔmɔmi nitsumi ɔ mi kɛ ha Yehowa. Be fɛɛ be nɛ kpokpa nɔ hyɛlɔ ɔ ma e asafo ɔ mi ɔ, Alwin kpaa ní tsumi bɔ nɛ pee nɛ e kɛ e he nɛ wo slɔɔto peemi ɔ mi saminya nɛ e bua jɔɔ he kaa e maa pee babauu ngɛ fiɛɛmi ní tsumi ɔ mi. Benɛ be ko be nɛ e sɔle babauu se ɔ, Alwin hɛ e yi mi kpɔ kaa e ma kpa e ní tsumi ɔ nɛ e maa hla kpa nɛ ma ha nɛ e ma nyɛ maa sɔmɔ kaa daa blɔ gbalɔ. Jamɛatsɛ pee jã lɔɔ maa bi nɛ e kɛ helo nya nínɛ́mɛ kɛ sã afɔle. Se kɛ̃ ɔ, e su e oti ɔ he. E yo hu ba piɛɛ e he kɛ pee blɔ gbalɔ, nɛ kɛ kaa a tsuo ɔ, a nyɛ nɛ a ye bua nimli 21 nɛ a kase anɔkwa ɔ. Nikaa ko be he kaa, Yehowa bua jɔ mɔde nɛ gba si himi nɛ ɔ bɔ ɔ he wawɛɛ. Wɔ hu wa ma nyɛ maa ná nɔ mami kaa Yehowa bua jɔɔ afɔle fɛɛ afɔle nɛ wa sãã ngɛ e sɔmɔmi ɔ mi he, nɛ kɛ je enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e maa hyɛ wa hiami níhi a nɔ daa.—Mat. 6:33.

17 Baiblo munyuhi nɛ kɔɔ Gibeon bi ɔmɛ a he ɔ tsɔɔ su nɛ́ haa nɛ Yehowa he suɔmi waa kɛ yaa nɔ ɔ nya pue he. E he si bami ngɛ, e naa mɔbɔ, e yeɔ dami sane, nɛ e yeɔ anɔkuale. E gbaa e sɔmɔli anɔkualetsɛmɛ ɔmɛ be fɛɛ be. Baiblo mi sane nɛ ɔmɛ hu ngɛ ní kpahi nɛ a ma nyɛ maa kase. Kaa bɔ nɛ Gibeon bi ɔmɛ fee ɔ, wɔ hu wa suɔ nɛ hemi kɛ yemi nɛ mi wa nɛ wa ngɛ ngɛ Yehowa mi nɛ waa kɛ wa hɛ nɛ fɔ e nɔ ke waa kɛ ní komɛ nɛ a yeli gbeye kpe. Jamɛ a hemi kɛ yemi ɔ ma ha hu nɛ waa kɛ he si bami maa tsu teokrase ní tsumi fɛɛ ní tsumi nɛ a kɛ ma ha wɔ ɔ he ní. Wa suɔ nɛ waa to wa tsui si kɛ mɛ Yehowa, nɛ wa hɛ ma nyɛ maa hi nɔ kɛ pi si kaa e maa tsa sane nɛ a yi kɛ dami tsɔ ɔ tsuo ngɛ be nɛ sa mi. Nɛ wa kɛ kã kɛ anɔkualeyeli fĩɔ anɔkuale jami se. HƐ NYƐMI

Bɔ nɛ womi nɛ ji Yesaya a nɔ nɛ wa ma jwɛmi kɛ ya sɔ̃ɔ ma nyɛ ma wo wɔ he wami ke wa tsui he jɔ̃ aloo wa kɔ̃ita jɔ.

Na Bua Jemi Ngɛ Yesaya Womi Ɔ Mi

“Nɔ́ nɛ Yehowa de ji nɛ: . . . ‘Kaa bɔ nɛ nyɛ kɛ e bi binuo tɔɔ e tsui mi ɔ, jã nɔ́u nɛ imi hu ma ya nɔ kɛ tɔ nyɛ tsui mi.’”—YES. 66:12, 13.

MOO LƐ nɛ jokuɛ ko nɛ e kɔnɔ mi jɔ̃ fia fĩa a ngɛ ya fooe kɛ kpekpemi. Ekoɔ e weku ɔ ngɛ fiae kɛ yaa we he ko, nɛ lɔɔ tsɔɔ kaa e sa nɛ e si e huɛmɛ ngɛ se. Aloo ekoɔ tɔmi ko nɛ e pee ɔ ha nɛ e kɔnɔ mi jɔ̃ fia fĩa a. Ngɛ behi nɛ ɔmɛ a mi ɔ, mɛni nɔ́ bimɔnyɛ nɛ ngɛ suɔmi ma pee? E ma fua e bi ɔ ngɛ dɔ̃ɔ mi, e ma bu e ya foomi nya munyu ɔmɛ tue, nɛ e ma ha nɛ e le kaa e suɔ lɛ.

2 Ngɛ Baablu ɔ mi ɔ, Yehowa ngɔ e he kɛ to nyɛ nɔ́ nɛ e suɔmi mi wa nɛ e bɔɔ e bi he mɔde nɛ e woɔ lɛ he wami ɔ he. E de ke: “Kɛji nɛ nyɛ fɔlɔ woɔ e bi bua a, jã kɛ̃ nɛ i maa wo nyɛ bua.” (Isa. 66:13) Suɔmi nɛ Yehowa ngɛ kɛ ha wɔ ɔ kle pe fɔlɔ fɛɛ fɛɛ nɔ́. (Isa. 49:15) Kɛ gu e Munyu Baablu ɔ nɔ ɔ, Yehowa haa wɔ bua womi kɛ he wami nɛ he hia wɔ ke wa kɔni mi jɔ aloo ke wa ngɛ haomi mi.—Rom. 15:4.

3 Yesaya womi ɔ hyi tɔ kɛ he wami womi munhi nɛ je Yehowa ngɔ. (Yes. 40:1) Wa Tsɛ nɛ suɔ wɔ ɔ ha nɛ a ngma womi nɛ ɔ konɛ e kɛ wo e webi a bua ngɛ be nɛ mi wa ngɛ Yuda yi nɔ tomi mi. Wɔ hu wa ngɛ wami kpee ngɛ “behi nɛ yeyeeye ngɛ mi, nɛ a mi wa” mi heje ɔ, wa hia he wami womi. (2 Tim. 3:1) Nyɛ ha nɛ wa susu Yesaya womi ɔ mi ngmami komi nɛ ma nyɛ ma wo wɔ he wami nɛ a wo wa bua ke (1) nɔ́ ko nɛ wa hyɛ we blɔ tsake wa si himi mi he.

KE NÍHI TSƆƆKE NƐ A HWIƐƐ WE BLƆ

4 Si himi nɛ wa ngɛ mi ɔ ma nyɛ maa tsake trukaa. (Fiɛl. 9:11; 1 Kor. 7:31) Eko ɔ, wa he ma nyɛ maa fia trukaa, wa he ma nyɛ maa hiɛ ngɛ helo nya nina blɔ fa mi, ma kudɔmi tsakemihi ma nyɛ maa ba, aloo wa sɔmɔmi he ma nyɛ maa tsake. Tsakemihi nɛ ngɛ jã a ma nyɛ ma ha nɛ waa ye tsui aloo e ma ha nɛ wa hɛ ma ja. Enɛ ɔ haa nɛ e he ba hia nɛ waa bi ke: ‘Kɛ ma pee kɛ ya hɛ mi kɛ je hiɔ kɛ ya kɛɛ?’ Nyɛminyumu ko nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Joseph, nɛ e sɔmɔ ngɛ Betel maa pee jeha 17 nɛ lɔ ɔ se ɔ, a tso lɛ kɛ ya kpe nɔ ɔ de ke: “I susu kaa nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ puɛ kɛ ha mi. E ji haomi kpele wawɛɛ kaa ma bɔni si himi he ko bɔbiimi ngɛ jehahi fuu nɛ i kɛ hi Betel ɔ se. I li he nɛ ma je kɛ bɔni.” Ke tsakemi ko nɛ wa hyɛ we blɔ ba wa si himi mi ɔ, kɛ nɔ mami he blɔ nɛ Yehowa kɛ gu Yesaya nɔ kɛ ha wɔ kɛɛ?

5 Kane Yesaya 42:16. Yehowa wo si kaa e maa kpa nihi nɛ a hɛngmɛ yuu ngɛ mfoniri yemi blɔ nɔ ɔ kɛ gu blɔhi nɛ a li a nɔ, nɛ e maa tsɔɔ diblii kɛ pee la. Munyunguhi kaa “hɛngmɛ yuu,” “blɔhi nɛ a li,” “diblii,” kɛ “blɔ tɔtɔɔtɔhi” maa nyɛ maa tsɔɔ bɔ nɛ wa buɔ wa hwɔɔ se ha ke wa si fɔfɔɛ tsake nɛ wa hyɛ we blɔ. Eko ɔ, e maa pee wɔ kaa nɔ́ nɛ wa ngɛ nyɛɛe ngɛ ngɔbua gagaa nɔukpɔ ko mi, nɛ wa li blɔ nɛ ngɛ hɛ mi. Se kɛ̃ ɔ, Yehowa ha wɔ nɔmima kaa ke wa kɛ si fɔfɔɛ fɛɛ si fɔfɔɛ kpe po ɔ, e maa ha wɔ blɔtsɔɔmi nɛ wa hia a, nɛ e be wɔ kuae kɔkɔɔkɔ. E maa hɛɛ wa dɛ nɛ e kɛ wɔ maa gu diblii ɔ mi, nɛ e maa ha la kɛ tsɔɔ wa blɔ nɔ nɛ e maa pee blɔ tɔtɔɔtɔ ɔmɛ zɔzɔɔzɔ. Mɛni blɔ nɔ Yehowa guɔ kɛ peeɔ

4. Kɛ si fɔfɔɛ kake tɔ̃ɔ nɛ tsake kpo nya a ma nyɛ maa sa wa he ha kɛɛ?

5. Kɛ fɔ si ngɛ Yesaya 42:16 ɔ, mɛni nɔ mi mami Yehowa ha wɔ? (Hyɛ foni ɔ nɛ ngɛ fɛ̃ɛ ɔ hu.)

“Ngɛ blɔ nɛ a li nɔ . . . , I be [nyɛ] si he e”

enɛ ɔ? Kɛ gu nile ga womi nɛ e guo e Munyu ɔ kɛ e blɔ nya tomi ɔ nɔ kɛ haa wɔ ɔ nɔ. (La 119:105; Yes. 30:21) E ngɔɔ wa nyɛmimɛ hemikɛyeli bimɛ kɛ ni kpahi hu kɛ yeɔ bua wɔ ngɛ blɔhi nɛ da a nɔ. Ke blɔ nɛ ngɛ wa hɛ mi ɔ ngɛ tsolee nɛ e peeɔ kakeeshi po ɔ, wa ma nyɛ ma he ma ye kaa Yehowa maa kɛ hɛ kɔmi kɛ sisi numi tsoo wɔ blɔ.—Yes. 41:10; 1 Pet. 5:6, 7.

6 Joseph nɛ a tsɔɔ e he munyu kɛ sɛ hlami ɔ tsɔɔ bɔ nɛ e kɛ e yo ɔ na Yehowa suɔmi yemi kɛ buami ha benɛ a je Betel se. E de ke: “Wa na Yehowa suɔmi dɛ ɔ ngɛ be nɛ wa kɛ kpe tsakemihi a mi ɔ nɔuu. E ye bu wɔ kɛ gu nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi nɛ a he ngɛ fɛu a nɔ. E hyɛ wa helo nya kɛ hemi kɛ yemi nɔ he hia níhi a nɔ pɔtɛɛ ngɛ be nɛ sa mi, nɛ e kɛ mɔu yaa nɔ nɛ e peeɔ jã.” Ke o si himi tsake ngɛ blɔ nɛ o hyɛ we nɔ ɔ nɔ ɔ, mo hu o ma nyɛ ma ná nɔ mami kaa Yehowa maa ha mo hewame kɛ blɔtsɔɔmi nɛ o hia a, e kɛɛ kɔkɔɔkɔ bɔ nɛ tsakemi ɔ maa pee nɛ gbeye ngɛ he ha. (Yes. 54:10; 58:11) Joseph kɛ piɛɛ he ke: “Munyuhi nɛ ngɛ wa la a kake mi ɔ tsɔɔ nɔ́ nɛ ɔ saminya, ‘Nɔ́ nɛ hwɔɔ se maa ba a, i li, se i le kaa i be kpee nya ngɛ i kɛ ye nɔmlɔtso pɛ mi.’* Ee, ke níhi kɔpe we bɔ nɛ wa to he blɔ nya a po ɔ, nɔ́ kake pɛ nɛ nɔ mami ngɛ he ji: Yehowa kɛ wɔ maa hi si ngɛ nane he kake fɛɛ nane he kake nɛ wa maa fɔ̃ ɔ nɔ.”

7. Mɛni níhi a he nɛ ekoɔmɛ maa wua nɔ ngɛ tɔ̃tɔ̃ɛhi nɛ a pee be ko nɛ be ɔ heje?

hɛmi kɛ jɛ asafo mi nikɔtɔma amɛ a ngɔ, nɛ lɔ ɔ se ɔ, wa bɔɔ mɔde wawɛɛ konɛ wa ko pee yayami ɔ hu. (Abɛ 28:13; Yak. 5:14-16; 1 Yoh. 1:9) Se kɛ̃ ɔ, ke a na wɔ mɔbɔ po ɔ, eko ɔ, fɔ nɛ wa buɔ wa he ɔ ma ya nɔ kuɔ wɔ yetoo. Eko ɔ, wa ma susu ke, ‘Mɛni blɔ nɔ nɛ Yehowa ma kɛ nɔ́ nɛ i pee ɔ kɛ ke mi?’ (2 Kor. 2:7) Nɔ́ nɛ nu sisi ji kaa yayami nɛ wa pee blema a he nɔ́ ma nyɛ maa tsi wɔ wawɛɛ, nɛ e hɛ ko ji nɔ kpoma. Matsɛ David ngma ke: “He jɔmi ko he jɔmi ko be ye wui amɛ a mi ngɛ ye yayami ɔ he je.” “Ye he wui gbo, nɛ a fia mi nɔ́ gbagbanii.” (La 38:3, 8) Ke be komɛ ɔ o nuɔ he jã ngɛ tó nɛ o tɔ̃ blema a he je ɔ, kɛkɛ ɔ, Yesaya womi ɔ ma nyɛ maa wo mo bua.

8 Kane Yesaya 1:18. Yehowa kɛ nɔhyɛnɔ ko nɛ tsoɔ kpo heii kɛ tsɔɔ bɔ nɛ e kɛ wa he yayamihi kɛ keɔ wɔ mluku kɛ tsuo ha. E de ke ke wa he yayamihi “tsu lolo” po ɔ, e maa pee mɛ “pupuipui kaa snoo.” Bɔ nɛ tsuo lolo ɔ, e ji nɔ ko nɛ he wa nɛ e be nya galee kɛkɛ. Se Yehowa woɔ wɔ he do ke ke e kɛ ke wɔ ɔ, wa be jamɛatsɛmɛi yayamihi a lɔhɔ ɔ hii hiee kɛ yaa su wa si himi nyagbe.

9 Mo susu Matsɛ David nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ he nɛ o hyɛ. E ngɛ mi kaa e tɔ̃ nɛ e gba puɛ nɛ e gbe nɔ mohu lɔɔ, se Yehowa kɛ e he tɔ̃mi ɔmɛ ke lɛ nɛ pee se ɔ, e tsɔɔ kaa e ji nɔ ko nɛ e yeɔ anɔkuale. (2 Sam. 11:3, 4, 14, 15; 12:13; 1 Ma. 9:4, 5) Pi David tɔ̃tɔ̃ɛmɛ ɔmɛ nɛ Yehowa da nɔ kɛ kojo lɛ, se mohu e da anɔkuale nɛ e ye ɔ nɔ. Jã kɛ̃ nɛ ke wa tsake wa tsui ɔ, Yehowa kɛ suɔmi kɛɔ wɔ, nɛ e yeɔ bua wɔ konɛ waa ya nɔ kɛ henilee nɛ he tsɔ. Kɛ̃ɛ nɔmami kpa nɛ wa ma nyiɛ nɛ waa na ngɛ Yesaya womi ɔ mi ke pɔtɛɛ nɛ tɔ̃mi nya wa nɔ he ngɛ wɔ haoee?

8-9. Kaa bɔ nɛ a tsɔɔ he munyu ngɛ Yesaya 1:18 ɔ, kɛ Yehowa dɛ he kɛ keɔ tɔ̃mihi ha kɛ ya suɔ kɛɛ?

10 HWƐƆMI MƆ Ɔ

O sake ye tɔ̃mihi tsuo ngo fɔ o se mi

Yehowa hɛɛ we wa tɔmi nɛ wa pee be ko nɛ be ɔ kɛ kɔ̃ɔ wa he (Hyɛ kuku 11)

Ko ha o tɔ̃mɔ nɛ be ɔ nɛ puɛ o bua jɔmi nɛ ngɛ amlɔ nɛ ɔ gblegbleegble

10 Kane Yesaya 38:17 kɛ sisihi ngmami ɔ. Munyu nɛ ji “o sake ye he yayamihi tsuo ngo fɔ o se” ɔ maa nyɛ hu nɛ e tsɔɔ kaa “o je ye yayamihi tsuo kɛ je o hɛ mi.” Hiɛ ɔ a tsɔɔ Yehowa kaa e ngɔ nɔ nɛ e pia e he ɔ yayami ɔmɛ nɛ e sake mɛ ngo fɔ e se, he nɛ e be mɛ nae loo e be a he susue hu kɔkɔɔkɔ. Kaa bɔ nɛ womi ko nɛ tsɔɔ munyuhi a sisi tsɔɔ ɔ, a ma nyɛ maa kɛ munyu nɛ ɔ ma tsɔɔ kaa: “O pee [ye yayami ɔmɛ] kaa nɔ́ nɛ a pee we mɛ hyɛ.” Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, ke Yehowa ngɔ wa yayamihi kɛ ke wɔ ɔ, ngɛ e hɛ mi ɔ, e ngɛ kaa nɔ́ nɛ wa pee we yayami hyɛ gblegbleele. Ke Yehowa ngɔ kɛ ke wɔ ɔ, e be wa blema yayami ɔmɛ a he munyu tue hu gblegbleele kɛ po wɔ nya aloo kɛ gbla wa tue ekko. E peeɔ yayami nɛ ɔmɛ a he ngmami ɔ he nɔ́ nɛ laa kpenkpen. E wo si ke: “I be o yayami ɔmɛ a nɔ kae hu.” (Yes. 43:25) E kɛ bua jɔmi nɛ je mi kɛ ba ha kɛɛ! Ke Yehowa nɛ ji Kojolɔ Pe Kulaa a kɛ wa yayami ɔmɛ to we wa he hu ɔ, mɛnɔji wɔ nɛ wa maa kɛ fɔ wɔ nitsɛmɛ wa he?

11 Be fɔɔ he nɔ buumi he nuunuehi teɔ si ekoŋŋ ɔ, e sa nɛ waa kai Yehowa suɔmi he nɔkpeemihi. E faami ngɛ nɔuu nɛ e ye emu. E hɛ mi gbi ngɛ waa he ngɛ wa tɔmi nɛ behi a he je, nɛ e be kɛ̃ ngɛ kaa nimli nɛ yi emu nɛ a hɛɛɔ mumi yayami. (Yes. 57:16) Nɔ́ nɛ he hia Yehowa nitsɛ ji, pi nɔ nɛ wa ji be ko nɛ be ɔ, se mohu nɔ nɛ wa ji mwɔnɛ ɔ kɛ nɔ nɛ wa ngɛ mɔde bɔe kaa waa ba pee. (Yes. 55:7) Suɔmi kɛ kpekpemi Mawu nɛ wa ngɛ sɔmee nɛ! Koo ŋmɛ blɔ gblamli kɔkɔɔkɔ kaa wa tɔ̃tɔ̃li nɛ be ɔ nɛ puɛ wa amlɔ nɛ ɔ bua jɔmi aloo nɛ e kpo jehanyi jɔɔmihi kɛ je wa dɛ.

KE O YA NO SUƆLƆ KO GBEGBE

12 Ke nɔ ko nɛ wa suɔ e sane gbo ɔ, kɔmɔ nɛ wa yeɔ ɔ ma nyɛ maa hiɛ babauu nɛ dɔmɔ nɛ jeɔ e mi ɔ hu ma nyɛ maa se kɛ ya jami. Be komɛ ɔ, eko ma nyɛ maa pee wɔ yaa kaa mɛni he je nɛ Yehowa ngmɛ blɔ nɛ nɔ nɛ wa suɔ e sane ɔ gbo. Nyɛminyumu ko nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Michael ɔ fɔli gbo ngɛ la gbi ko mi. E de ke: “Ye tsui ku bibioobi, nɛ ye mi mi fu Yehowa he kaa e ngmɛ blɔ nɛ a gbo. I le kaa pi lɛ nɛ e gbe mɛ, se e tsi we hu nya.” Eko ɔ, mo hu o nu he jã hyɛ. Ngɛ aywilɛyeli be nɛ ɔmɛ a mi ɔ, mɛni bua jɛmi kɛ hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi nɛ Yehowa haa?

LALA 12 Yehowa, Mawu Kpeteŋkpele

HƐ MI NƆ Nɔ́ nɛ e tsɔɔ kaa a le Yehowa, nɔ́ he je nɛ e sa kaa waa suɔ kaa wa maa le lɛ kɛ ya hiɛ, kɛ bɔ nɛ wa ma plɛ kɛ pee jã.

Anɛ O Ngɛ Yehowa Leee Saminya Pe Sane O Lo?

“Lé o tsɛ Mawu ɔ nɛ o sɔmɔ lɛ kɛ tsui kpakpa.”—1 KRON. 28:9.

ANƐ o susu Yehowa nilee kpetekpleenyɛ ɔ, e juɛmi nɛ mi kuɔ, aloo níhi nɛ e pee ɔmɛ a he hyɛ lo? Ke wa susu wa Bɔlɔ ɔ he ɔ, e haa nɛ wa baa wa he si. Eko ɔ, o maa nu he kaa bɔ nɛ bɔfo Paulo nu he ɔ, nɛ e ngma ke: “Mawu niatsɛmi, kɛ e juɛmi, kɛ e nilee ɔ mi kuɔ nitsɛnitsɛ! Nɔ ko be nyɛe maa le e kojomihi a nya, nɛ nɔ ko be nyɛe maa le e blɔhi a mi kɔ!”—Rom. 11:33, 34.

2 Ke si kɛ̃ nɛ wa be nyɛe nɛ waa le nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ nɛ kɔɔ Yehowa he lɔɔ, wa ma nyɛ maa le suhi nɛ e ngɛ ɔ kɛ ya su he kɔɔti ko. Matsɛ David de e bi Salomo ke: “Lé o tsɛ Mawu ɔ, nɛ o sɔmɔ lɛ kɛ tsui kpaako . . . Ke o hla lɛ ɔ, e ma ha nɛ o na lɛ.” (1 Kron. 28:9) Kaa bɔ nɛ bɔfo Yohane kai e nyɛmimɛ Kristofohi jeha babauu se ɔ, wa ngɛ “nile konɛ waa le nɔ nɛ ji anɔkuale ɔ.” (1 Yoh. 5:20) Nɛ Yesu nitsɛ de ke nɔ fɛɛ nɔ nɛ “Bi ɔ suɔ kaa e ma kpa lɛ ngɔ ha lɛ ɔ” ma nyɛ maa le Yehowa.—Mat. 11:27.

3 Nihi tsuo nɛ a kɛ a he jɔɔ Yehowa nɔ ɔ kase ní kpehi fuu nɛ kɔɔ e he momo. Se kɛ̃ ɔ, wa kpa we Yehowa he ní nɛ wa ma “le.” (Yohane 17:3; hyɛ ní kasemi munyu ɔ.)

1. Kɛ waa susu Yehowa nilee, e juɛmi, kɛ níhi nɛ e pee ɔmɛ a he ɔ, kɛ wa nuɔ he ha kɛɛ?

2. Mɛni tsɔɔ kaa wa ma nyɛ maa le Yehowa?

3. Mɛni he wa ma susu ngɛ womi nɛ ɔ mi?

Nyɛ ha nɛ wa susu sane bimi etɛ nɛ e sa kaa wɔ tsuo waa susu he jio be abɔ nɛ wa sɔmɔ Yehowa a he: Mɛni lɔɔ tsɔɔ kaa a le Yehowa? Mɛni he je nɛ e sa kaa waa ya nɔ nɛ waa le lɛ saminya a? Kɛ wa ma plɛ kɛ le lɛ saminya kɛte nɔ ha kɛɛ?

MƐNI E TSƆƆ KAA O MA LE YEHOWA?

4 Ke wa kase nɔ́ nɛ Yehowa de kɛ kɔ Yuda bi nɛ a maa kpale kɛ je nyɔguɔ yemi mi ɔmɛ a he ɔ, wa maa na nɔ́ nɛ e biɔ koni nɔ ko nɛ le Yehowa. Yehowa wo si ke: “Ma ha mɛ tsui nɛ a kɛ maa le mi, kaa imi ji Yehowa.” (Yer. 24:7) E ngɛ heii kaa nihi nɛ kpale kɛ je Babilon ɔ le Yehowa he sanehi momo. Se e he hia nɛ a kɛ a tsui tsuo, nɛ tsɔɔ a henumɔhi kɛ a susumɔhi, nɛ kase Yehowa he nɔ́. Womi ko tsɔɔ nya kɛkɛ ke: “Slo Mawu he nɔ́ lemi ngɛ Mawu lemi he kulaa. E ngɛ kaa slo nɛ ngɛ suɔmi he nɔ́ kanemi kɛ suɔmi mi nɛ nɔ ko maa ya a he.” E sa nɛ wa le Yehowa pe kaa e ngɛ biɛ nɛ e ji Mawu kɛkɛ. Wa suɔ nɛ wa le Nɔ nɛ ngɛ biɛ ɔ se ɔ nitsɛ.

5 Be nɛ wa ba le Yehowa a, wa kaseɔ nɔ́ nɛ peeɔ lɛ bua jɔmi kɛ nɔ́ nɛ dɔɔ lɛ. Kɛ kɛ̃ nɔ́ nɛ ya nɔ ngɛ Matsɛ Yosia be ɔ mi ɔ tsɔɔ jã. Baiblo ɔ de ke: “Benɛ e ye matsɛ jeha 8, nɛ e kɛ fĩ jokuɛ kɛkɛ ɔ, e bɔni

e hla e nɛɛtsi David Mawu ɔ.” (2 Kron. 34:3) Se Yosia pee babauu pe kɛkɛ nɛ e maa kase ní. Nɔ́ nɛ e ngɛ kasee ɔ, e kɛ tsu ní. Yehowa gu gbalɔ Yeremiya nɔ kɛ de ke Yosia “kpo dami sane yemi kɛ dɔu pɛpɛɛpɛ nɔ” nɛ e fã nimli nɛ a ngɛ nɔ́ nae kɛ ohiatsɛmɛ a he. Kɛkɛ nɛ Yehowa kɛ piɛɛ he ke: “Anɛ lɔɔ ji nɔ́ nɛ tsɔɔ kaa a le mi lo?”—Yer. 22:15, 16.

6 Kane La 9:10. Ke wa le Yehowa saminya a, lɔɔ ma ha nɛ wa ma he lɛ ye wawɛɛ nitsɛ. Jamɛ a sisi tomi mami ɔ ba mi ngɛ bɔfo Paulo blɔ fa mi. Benɛ a wo lɛ tsu nyagbe peemi nɛ e sɛ hlami nɛ e ma gbo ɔ, e ngma ke: “Hiami biɛ wui mi; ejakaa i le nɔ nɛ i he lɛ ye ɔ.” (2 Tim. 1:12) Níhi nɛ Paulo kase kɛ níhi nɛ e kɛ kpe ngɛ e si himi mi ɔ ha nɛ e fia e kpa si kɛ he e Mawu ɔ ye kɛ e tsui tsuo. Kaa bɔ nɛ kaseemi munyu ko nɛ kɔɔ jamɛ a ngmami ɔ he tsɔɔ mi ɔ: “Paulo . . . ná nɔ mami kaa Yehowa maa kai e dali si himi blɔ ɔ kɛ ya si ‘jamɛ a ligbi ɔ’ nɛ E ma tle lɛ si.”

MƐNI HE JE E SA KAA O YA NƆ NƐ O KASE YEHOWA HE NÍ?

7 Be mi nɛ behi kɛ yaa a, ekoɔ eko ɔ, wa be mɔde bɔe wawɛɛ kaa bɔ nɛ wa pee sisije nɛ wa kase Yehowa he ní ɔ konɛ wa le lɛ saminya. Mɛni he je? Ke wa ngɛ Baiblo ɔ kane ngɛ wa dɛhe aloo ngɛ asafo mi kpehi a sisi kɛ piɛɛ ni kpaahi a he ɔ, ekoɔ eko ɔ, nɔ́ nɛ wa maa juɛ nɔ ji bɔ nɛ sane ɔ ma nyɛ maa ye bua wɔ nitsɛmɛ ngɛ blɔ nitsɛnitsɛ nɔ, aloo bɔ nɛ wa ma nyɛ maa kɛ ye bua ni kpaahi. Baiblo ɔ tsɔɔ nɔ nɛ ngma a he níhi babauu kɛ fɔ si. (La 25:8-10) E sɛ nɛ wa hao wa he kɛ kɔ bɔ nɛ Baiblo ɔ sɛgbe ɔ yeɔ bua wɔ aloo ni kpahi ha a he nɛ e ha wa he nɛ kpa sane nɛ ɔ bimi ke, ‘Mɛni enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ mi ngɛ Yehowa Mawu he?’*

8 Ke wa le Yehowa kpakpata a, huɛ bɔmi nɛ ngɛ wa kɛ lɛ wa kpɛti ɔ mi maa wa. Kɛ̃ɛtsɔ, kɛ o nuɔ he ha kɛɛ ngɛ Yehowa he ke o kane Baiblo ɔ daa ligbi, o hyɛ nyɔhiɔ nɛ ɔ mi broadcast ɔ, aloo o ya kpe kpo aloo kpe ngua a? Behi fuu ɔ, wa nuɔ he kaa wa hɛ kɛ suɔ wa hiɔwe Tsɛ ɔ he kpaata. Mɛni heje? Ejakaa wa kase níhi fuu ngɛ e he. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e tsuɔ wa tsui he nɛ wa jeɔ e yi nɛ wa daa lɛ si. Wa nuɔ he kaa la polɔ nɛ e jwɛŋ bɔ nɛ Yehowa hyɛ e nɔ ha a he nɛ e de ke: “Mawu ji ye tsui a tɛsa kɛ ye gbosi ní kɛ ya neneene.” (Kane La 73:24-28.) Mɔde biɛ nɛ wa bɔɔ kaa wa maa le Yehowa a nitsɔ haa nɛ wa bua jɔɔ huɛ bɔmi jua wa nɛ ngɛ wa kɛ lɛ wa kpɛti ɔ he nitsɛnitsɛ.

9 Ke wa “kai” Yehowa—ke wa kai nɔ́ nɛ wa kase ngɛ e he kɛ bɔ nɛ wa nímɔhi náa e nɔ he wami ha a—wa maa pee yi mi kpɔhi nɛ da pe kulaa. (Abɛ 3:5, 6) Kɛ ha nɔhyɛmi nɔ́: Nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi fuu kɛ a huno aloo a yo fɔto toɔ a kɔmpiuta kpɛ̃i nɔ, a kɛ woɔ a sika kotoku mi, aloo a tɛlifo nɔ. Enɛ ɔ yeɔ bua mɛ nɛ a susuɔ a he kɛ suɔmi be fɛɛ be, nɛ a peeɔ yi mi kpɔhi nɛ a le kaa e maa sa a huno aloo a yo ɔ hɛ mi, nɛ a kpa nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ nɛ maa dɔ lɔ ɔ peemi. Jã kɛkɛ nɛ ke wa susu Yehowa he be fɛɛ be ɔ, lɔ ɔ maa ye bua wɔ wawɛː nɛ wa maa pee haniemihi nɛ sa e hɛ mi.—La 19:13, 14.

10 A bɔ wɔ ngɛ Yehowa subai nya, lɔɔ ha nɛ waa kpe nyɛmi nɛ wa maa je e suhi kpo. (1 Mose 1:26) Nɛ ke wa kase Yehowa sui ɔmɛ a he nina babauu ɔ, lɔɔ maa ha nɛ wa maa nu bɔ nɛ wa maa kase lɛ ha a sisi kɛ hi pe nɛ ɔ. Yesu bu womi ngmallilɔ ɔmɛ kɛ Farisifobi ɔmɛ fɔ ngɛ bɔ nɛ a nyɛ we nɛ a je Mawu sui ɔmɛ kpo ha a heje. E ngɛ mi kɛ̃ kaa a bu Mlaa a nina tsɔwitsɔwi ɔmɛ tue saminya mohu, se a “kua níhi nɛ a he hia pe kulaa ngɛ Mlaa a mi ɔ, nɔ́ he ko ji, dami kɛ mɔbɔnalihami kɛ anɔkwayeli.” (Mat. 23:23) Kaa bɔ nɛ jamɛatsɛmɛ nɛ ɔmɛ pee we ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ wɔɔ lɔ waa bɔ mɔde kɛ nu bɔ nɛ Yehowa sui ɔmɛ je kpo ngɛ e mlaahi kɛ e sisi tomi mlaahi a mi ha a sisi. Ke wa pee jã a, lɔɔ maa ye bua wɔ nɛ waa je jamɛ a sui ɔmɛ nitsɛmɛi kpo.—1 Yoh. 4:8, 11.

12-13. Mɛni wa ma nyɛ maa kase ngɛ Yehowa he kɛ je Baiblo ɔ kanemi mi? Ha nɔhyɛnɔ kake. (Yeremiya 39:15-18) (Hyɛ kpo ɔ nɔ foni ɔ hulɔ.)

Anɛ o na Yehowa suhi nɛ a je kpo ngɛ sane nɛ ɔ mi lo? (Kane Yeremiya 39:15-18.) Kɛ̃ kɛ̃ɛ, ngmami kɔni te nɛɛ nɛ tsɔɔ kaa Yehowa buɔ nɔ he, e da, nɛ e yi nɔ kɔ lo? Yehowa su kpahi te nɛɛ nɛ o na ngɛ sane nɛ ɔ mi? Be fɛɛ be nɛ o maa kane sane ko ngɛ Mawu Munyu ɔ mi ɔ, hla Yehowa suhi nɛ je kpo ngɛ mi, nɛ o na lɛ si nɛ o je e yi ngɛ su nɛ ɔmɛ a he.

14 Nɔ́ nɛ ji enyɔ, wa ma nyɛ maa kase nɔ́ kpa nɛ kɔɔ Yehowa he kɛ gu níhi nɛ e bɔ ɔmɛ a nɔ he nɛ wa ma susu nɔ. (La 145:9, 10) Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ we kaa je nɛ ɔ nɛ wa maa hyɛ kɛkɛ ɔ hiɔ. Ye Bua Nihi Kɛ Ba Yehowa Le Mi

“Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ neneene wami, kaa a maa le mo, anɔkwa Mawu kake ɔ.”—YOHANE 17:3.

KƐ e ji kɛɛ ha mo ke Baabolo kaselo ko ba e kpe ko kekleekle, nɛ e bɔni hɛ mi nyami munyuhi tsumi, nɛ e kɛ ni kpahi gba e hemi kɛ yemi ɔ he sane, nɛ pee se ɔ e mwɔ e yi mi kpɔ kaa e maa jɔɔ e he nɔ ha Yehowa Mawu? E ngɛ heii kaa e dɔɔ mo wawɛɛ! Ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ, Yehowa ji nɔ nɛ sa kaa a je e yi ngɛ mumi mi hɛ mi yami nɛ wa Baabolo kaselo ɔ peeɔ ɔ he. Wa jeɔ Yehowa yi ke wa na bɔ nɛ e yeɔ bua nɔ ko nɛ e wa ngɛ mumi mi ha.—1 Kor. 3:5-9; 3 Yoh. 3, 4.

2 Mawu suɔmi nya ji kaa “nimli slɔɔtoslɔɔtoi tsuo nɛ a he a yi wami nɛ a ná anɔkuale ɔ he nilee nɛ da pɛpɛɛpɛ.” (Kane 1 Timoteo 2:3, 4.) Yehowa suɔ nɛ nihi nɛ a le lɛ kɛ e yi mi tomihi kɛ ya su he nɛ a ma nyɛ maa ya su kpaata. (Yer. 9:24) Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa a maa ná nilee nɛ da pɛpɛɛpɛ. Kaa bɔ nɛ Baiblo he ní lelɔ ko tsɔɔ nya a, Hebri kɛ Hela munyu nɛ a tsɔɔ sisi kaa “nilee nɛ da pɛpɛɛpɛ” ɔ tsoo we anɔkuale munyu komɛ kɛkɛ nɛ nɔ ko kaseɔ, se mohu nilee nɛ “náa nɔ ɔ nɔ hewalɛ kpaata.” Ke Baiblo kaselɔ ko ba le Yehowa a, nilee nɛ ɔ náa bɔ nɛ e hiɛ e si ha a nɔ hewalɛ. Nɛ e bliɔ blɔ kɛ haa lɛ konɛ e ná “neneene wami.”—Yohane 17:3.

3 Yehowa ha wɔ he blɔ nɛ e sa kaa waa ye bua ni kpahi konɛ a le lɛ. Ngɛ munyulɔ nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, wa ma susu sane bimi nɛ ɔmɛ a he: Mɛni he je nɛ e sa kaa Baiblo kaselɔhi nɛ a le Yehowa saminya a? Kɛ wa ma plɛ kɛ tsɔɔ mɛ bɔ nɛ Yehowa ngɛ ha kɛɛ? Nɛ kɛ wa ma plɛ kɛ ye bua mɛ kɛ ngɔ a

4 Yesu de ke mlaa nɛ kle pe kulaa ji nɛ waa suɔ Yehowa kɛ wa tsui tsuo, wa klaa tsuo, wa juɛmi tsuo, kɛ wa he wami tsuo. (Kane Marko 12:30.) Ja Baiblo kaselɔ ko ba le Yehowa lolo loko e ma nyɛ maa suɔ lɛ. Wa suɔ nɛ wa Baiblo kaselɔ ɔ nɛ jɔɔ e he nɔ—pi kɛ ha wɔ, pi kɛ ha tsɔɔmihi nɛ a to, pi kɛ ha asafo ko—se kɛ ha Yehowa. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, wa bɔti nɛ he hia pe kulaa ke wa ngɛ Baiblo mi nikasemi pee kɛ ngɛ nɔ ko kasee ji nɛ waa ye bua wa kaselɔ ɔ nɛ e ba le Yehowa nɛ e suɔ lɛ.

5 Suɔmi kɛ tue bɔmi tsɔɔ nɔ́ kake. Ke o Baablo kaselɔ ɔ ba le Yehowa nɛ e suɔ lɛ ɔ, lɔ ɔ maa wo e hɛ mi nɛ e maa bu e tue. Yesu de ke: “Konɛ je ɔ nɛ le kaa i suɔ Tsɛ ɔ, i ngɛ ní pee kaa bɔ nɛ Tsɛ ɔ fã mi ɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ.” (Yohane 14:31) Jã kɛ̃ nɛ Yehowa suɔmi nɛ e kpa nɔ pe nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ ɔ maa wo Baablo kaselɔ ɔ he wami nɛ e maa bu lɛ tue nɛ e maa kpatã e he.—1 Yohane 5:3.

6 Baiblo kaseli nɛ a ba le Yehowa saminya a maa nyɛ maa pee nile mi kpɔtsɛmɛ. Kɛkɛ nɛ nɔ ko ma nyɛ ma nɛ́ e tsɔɔ mi lɛ ji: Lɛlɔ nɛ kpee kɔɔhiɔ mi lɛ́ nɛ hwoɔ nihi kɛ yaa hehi nɛ a yaa a kasee lɛ́ kpomi kɛ guu kɔɔhiɔ mi lɛ́ kpomi mlaahi a kasesemi kɛ ka nɛ a kɛ kaa lɛ kɛkɛ nɔ. E sa nɛ e kpo lɛ́ ɔ ngɛ hiɔwe ngɛ ngmlɛfiahiahi fuu amli. Be bɔbɔ nɛ be fua a, e kaseɔ bɔ nɛ kɔɔhiɔ mi lɛ́ ɔ plɛɔ e he ha ngɛ hiɔwe he bɔbɔ nɛ kɔɔhiɔ ngɛ nɔ tsoe ha a kɛ bɔ nɛ kpɔtsɛmɛ nɛ e peeɔ kɔɔ kɛ fɔɔ lɛ́ ɔ kpomi nɔ ha. Jã kɛ̃ nɛ, Baiblo kaselɔ maa ba le Yehowa pi kɛ gu e nitsɛ e Baiblo mi ní kasemi kɛkɛ nɔ, mohu ɔ, kɛ gu bɔ nɛ e na nɔ he wami ngɛ be nɛ e kɛ Ngmami mi sisi mlaahi tsuɔ ní kɛ peeɔ kpɔtsɛmɛ a he. Bɔ nɛ e le Yehowa kɛ yaa nɔ nɛ e na bɔ nɛ e hiɔwe Tsɛ ɔ ye bua lɛ ha a, e maa nyɛ maa pee kpɔtsɛmɛ nɛ da ngɛ si fɔfɔɛ tsuo mi.

7 Yehowa he níhi a si nɛ Yosef le ɔ ye bu lɛ benɛ Potifar yo ɔ bɔ mɔde kaa e maa laka lɛ ɔ. Kɛ je Yosef jokuɛwi a si ɔ, he nɛ e ma ka ha kaa e nu Adam kɛ Hawa kɛ Abraham kɛ Sara a he níhi a he sane. (1 Mose 2:24; 20:3, 6) Yosef nyɛ nɛ e na kɛ je jamɛ a sanehi a mi bɔ nɛ Yehowa buɔ gba si himi kɛ bɔmi he nike ní ɔ ha. Yosef ba le Yehowa saminya nɛ e kua Potifar yo ɔ nya nɛ e de lɛ ke: “Kɛ ma plɛ kɛ pee yayami ngua nɛ ɔ, nɛ ma pee Mawu yayami ha kɛɛ?” (1 Mose 39:7-9) Yosef le nɔ́ nɛ da kɛ nɔ́ nɛ dɛ ngɛ Yehowa hɛ mi. E nu nɔ́ nɛ maa dɔ e Mawu ɔ sisi. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, Yosef pee yi mi kpo nɛ da. Ke wa ye bu wa Baiblo kaseli ɔmɛ konɛ a le Yehowa saminya nɛ a kase bɔ nɛ e ngɛ ha a, mɛ hu a ma pee níhi nɛ maa sa e hɛ mi.

Be ko benɛ i ngɛ ní tsume ngɛ mɔ ko nɛ a kɛ peeɔ womi he fɛu nɔ ɔ, i kɛ tɔmi puɛ womi he kpehi babauu. Womi kpami he ɔ bimi mi nɛ ma ba bɔ he amaniɛ. I tsɔɔ nɔ́ heje nɛ tɔmi ɔ ba pɛpɛɛpɛ, se nɔ́ kake ji nɔ́ nɛ be mi. Kɛ mi ngɔɔmi, nɔ hyɛlɔ ɔ kae mi ke, “E hi be fɛɛ be kaa o ma kpa fɛ̃ɛ.” Jamɛ a be ɔ mi nɔuu ɔ, i kase hefia nɛ ngɛ he kaa ma kplɛɛ ye tɔmihi a nɔ nɛ ma kɛɛ, “I kpa mo fɛ̃ɛ.”

Bɔ nɛ i naa ni kpahi a sɔmɔmi ha a ba pee kpakpa kɛ gu kã nɛ i kã Betel weku ɔ mi nikɔtɔmahi nɛ a yeɔ anɔkuale ɔ nɔ. Be ko ɔ, Nyɛminyumu Milton Henschel nɛ ji Gbɛtsɔɔmi Kɔmiti ɔ mi no ko hiɛ he nɛ wa ngɛ niye nina ye ngɛ haane nɔ. Jamɛ a ligbi ɔ, nihi babauu ba niyehe ɔ, nɛ nihi nɛ a tsoo niye ní ɔ ngɛ kpe tɔtsɔtsɔ konɛ a gba niye ní ɔ ha nihi. Wɔ, nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi nɛ wa wɛ ngɛ kplɔ̃ ɔ he ɔ, wa ngɛ guɔguɔe kpoo ngɛ bɔ nɛ sɔmɔmi ɔ ngɛ nya fĩe ha a he. Nyɛminyumu Henschel de we munyu kake po, se e te si nɛ e kɛ nihi nɛ a tsoo niye ní ɔ bɔni nyu kaahi kɛ abolo kɛ bɔtɔ plɛtihi tsɔɔmi. Sɔmɔmi he si bami nɔ́ nɛ ɔ maa hi ye juɛmi mi kɛ ya neneene. JAPAN NÍTSUMÍ HE Ɔ KƐ FIƐƐMI NÍTSUMÍ Ɔ

Benɛ behi kɛ yaa a, Miwako fɔli tsuo nɛ a ngɛ Japan a nu hiɔ wawɛɛ. Lɔɔ he ɔ, a kɛ mɔmobɔmi ngmɛ wɔ blɔ nɛ wa fia kɛ ya Japan nitsumi kɔba a konɛ wa nyɛ nɛ wa hi a he kɛ hyɛ a nɔ. Wa hia kɛ ya lejɛ ɔ ngɛ jeha 1999 mi.

E kɛ we nɛ Japan Bɛtɛl weku ɔ he kɛ bu wɔ, nɛ wa kɛ wa he kpee wa we he ɔ. Níhi nɛ ba a gba mi kɛ ya hie ligbi kɛ ligbi be nɛ i kase Japan nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi a su kpakpahi ɔ, kaa a nítsɔmi nɛ jɔ kɛ a nibimɛ he mi fiami nɛ ngɛ tɛtɛɛlɔ ɔ. Ke e ba nítsumi he ɔ, a peeɔ jiojio nɛ a juɛmi nɔ kpe ngɛ nɔ. A kpa we a he kɛ tsuɔ ní si kpakpa hu. Kɛ a peeɔ kɛɛ? Japan kusii kɛ hie gbamina mi nɛ a kɛ ni kpahi tsu ní ngɛ kakepeemi kɛ fiɛɛmi mi kɛ ha nihi tsuo a kpakpa pe nɛ a tsu ní ngɛ anɔkɛ kɛ ha nɔmlɔ he níhi. A kpee nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ kpakpa nɛ ɔ wo mi he wami nɛ ma kɛ he si bami kɛ ni kpahi nɛ tsu ní, nɛ ma bu bɔ nɛ ye fa suɔfiɛ ɔ yeɔ bua kɛ ha Yehowa yi mi tomi nɛ kle ɔ ha.

Kɛ̃ ɔ, ngɛ kusii kpa amɛ a heje ɔ, be komɛ ngɛ nɛ e he wa ha mi nɛ ma nu nɔ́ heje nɛ a tsu níhi a he ní ngɛ blɔ pɔtɛɛ ko nɔ ɔ sisi. E bi nɛ ma kase kpo nya jemi kɛ tsui si nɔmi, nɛ ma ba ye je nɛ i ko mɔ de tso. Be babauu se ɔ, i kpa si kɛ susu níhi a he saminya nɛ i ba na kaa Yehowa ma nyɛ maa gbaa yi mi kpomi tsuaa yi mi kpomi nɛ a ma mwɔ, ja pɛ nɛ wa ba wa he si ha blɔ tsɔɔmi nɛ e kɛ guɔ e we blɔ nya tomi ɔ nɔ kɛ haa wɔ ɔ. Nyagbenyagbe ɔ, Yehowa ji nɔ nɛ haa nɛ níhi yaa nɔ saminya.

Pla Puɔ Ɔ Mlamlaamla!

Anɛ nyɛminyumu aloo nyɛmiyo ko e tɔ̃ o nɔ babauu nɛ kɛ ya su piimi nɛ amlɔ nɛ ɔ huɛ bɔmi nɛ ngɛ nyɛ kɛteke nɛ ɔ puɛ lo? Bɔ nɛ pee nɛ o kɛ lɛ nɛ kpata nɛ nyɛ ya nɔ nɛ nyɛ pee huɛmɛ ɔ, e he hia nɛ o tsu he ní mlamlaamla kɛ susumɔ nɛ da.*

Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, ja a tsa wui nɛ kplɔ́ konɛ pee se ɔ, e nyɛ nɛ e tsu ní saminya nɛ pɔmi be mi. Se ke a tsaa wui nɛ kplɔ́ ɔ mɛla a, e maa ya nɔ maa kɔ́. Nɔ́ nɛ dɔ pe kulaa ji kaa, eko ɔ, wui ɔ be kpee he ekoekoohu nɛ e be nyɛe maa tsu ní saminya.

Kāa bɔ nɛ wuu nɛ ku laa kɛ tsɔ̃ɔ e he ɔ, huɛbɔmi nɛ puɛ ngɛ nyɛminyɛ Kristofonyo kɛ wa kpeeti ɔ hu sa nɛ a tsa mlamlaamla. Ke pi jã a, dɔmi nɛ huɛbɔmi nɛ puɛ ɔ kɛ baa a maa ya nɔ. Henyɛli kpaŋ maa kpa, nɛ o huɛ ɔ ma nyɛ maa je o dɛ. Yesu de ke: “Mo kpa mo kɛ o henyɛlɔ ɔ nya mlamlaamla.” (Mat. 5:25) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, yaa o huɛ ɔ ngɔ nɛ o kɛ lɛ nɛ tu munyu ngɛ dromi mi se kɛ fitsofitso yemi ngɛ sane ɔ he. Mɛni heje nɛ enɛ ɔ peemi ma nyɛ maa yee? Susu nɔ́ heje enyɔ komɛ a he.

Kekleekle lɛ, eeko pe kaa o maa ngɔ o hɛ kɛ fɔ sihimɛ ɔ nɔ nɛ o be dlae, ekoɔ pe kaa e he wa kɛ ha mo kaa o ma tu haomihi a he munyu.# Mo susu Euodia kɛ Sintike he nɛ o hyɛ, Kristofoli nyɛmimɛ yi nɛ e pee kaa a nyɛ we nɛ a dla gbaami ko nɛ ngɛ a kpɛti. Ekoɔ pe kaa a haomi ɔ se kɛ, ejakaa bɔfo Paulo po nɛ e ngɛ he ko nɛ kɛ tsɔ ngɛ Roma a nu he. E kpa mɛ pɛɛ kaa a pee nɔ fɛɛ nɔ nɛ a ma nyɛ bɔ nɛ pee nɛ a kake peemi ɔ nɛ ba ekko. (Filip. 4:2) Kɛ gu he si bami, munyu tumi kɛ nɔ́ nɛ a maa ye kɛ ha a he nɛ da, kɛ kpatsemi nitsɛ nɔ ɔ, a huɛ bɔmi ɔ ma nyɛ maa ya nɔ ekko.

Nɔ́ nɛ ji enyɔ nɛ, ekoɔ eko ɔ, o ma tso nya kaa nɔ kpa a nɛ kpɛlɛ e tɔmi ɔ nɔ. Se o ngɛ hlae nɛ o ná o huɛ ɔ ekohu, pi kaa o ma hla nɔ nɛ tɔ̃. Dɔkuta nɛ ngɛ wuo nɛ ku tsae ɔ ma tsu ní konɛ e tsa, se pi kaa e ma hla nɔ́ tututu nɛ ha nɛ wuo ɔ ku ɔ se kɔkɔɔkɔ. Ke o kɛ o huɛ ɔ ngɛ ní sɛɛe ɔ, koo ngɔ o juɛmi kɛ ma nɔ nɛ tɔ̃ nɛ nɔ́ ɔ ba a nɔ. Mohu ɔ, kai bɔ nɛ huɛbɔmi ɔ he hia mo ha, nɛ o ngɔ o juɛmi kɛ ma nɔ́ nɛ e sa nɛ o pee konɛ o kɛ tsa mi ekohu ɔ nɔ.

Mɛni heje nɛ o be o nyɛminyumu aloo o nyɛmiyo ɔ tuɔ munyu nɛ mwɔnɛ ɔ, konɛ o dla níhi a nya nɛ o to o kɛ lɛ a kpɛti huɛbɔmi ɔ nya ekko? “Nyɛ ko ha nɛ pu nɛ nɔ si be mi nɛ nyɛ ngɛ abɔi he.” (Efe. 4:26) Ke o tsu he ní mlamla konɛ o dla níhi a nya a, lɔɔ maa ye bua konɛ a po wɔ “kpe ngɛ hejɔmi mi.”—Efe. 4:1-3.
