A good deed that Obonto suffered walking in the water in vain

They say there was an Old Woman, and her things kept missing, and she said the water creatures had taken them. When the Crab heard this, he said, “Ah, you have disgraced us, so what we will do to show you so you will know our intentions is: go bring a brass basin, and let all of us weep tears into it, and whoever is unable to cry enough to fill it, that person is the one who took it.” She brought the brass basin; the Crab wept enough to fill it, and it was poured out; Mmobonse came and wept; Okawa came and wept; Oyoyo came and wept; Otidie came and wept; Obonko came and wept; Akorese came and wept; Opitire came and wept; Okobo came and wept; all the water creatures came and wept. And afterwards, Obonto Ya also came to weep, but she did not get any tears, and they realized that: Obonto Ya was the one who took it. Then the Old Woman said, “I will not do anything to you.” But all the creatures said, “As for us, we will beat her, for she has disgraced us.” And they beat her. When they finished beating her, the Old Woman said, “I leave you outdoors, you wander about in vain, no one wants you for anything.”

This means that when fighting breaks out, we will see Obonto Yaa walking around here and there.

This tale of mine that I have told, whether it is sweet or not sweet, take some away and bring some back to me.
