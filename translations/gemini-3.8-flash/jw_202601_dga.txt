THINGS THAT CAN HELP YOU IN YOUR STUDIES

Visualize Bible Accounts in Your Mind

The Bible contains ancient events that took place, from which we can learn important lessons. If we picture these events in our minds, it will make the reading enjoyable for us and help us benefit greatly from it. Let us see some things that can help us visualize the events in the Bible in our minds.

• Always read aloud. When you read the Bible aloud, it will be easy to visualize the events in your mind. If you are reading the Bible with your family members, you can assign the parts of all the people who spoke in that Bible reading portion to each of your family members. This will help them read the words so that you can visualize the events in your mind.

• Picture the events in your mind and see how things were happening. Picture the events in your mind and see whether the people you are reading about were angry or happy, or if they were being serious. For example, you can ask yourself, ‘Why did that person say or do this thing? If it were me, how would I have handled my situation?’

• You can draw the things. If you draw the things down, it will help you take a picture of the things in your mind. It will also make you remember the things very well, even if you did not draw the things very nicely.
