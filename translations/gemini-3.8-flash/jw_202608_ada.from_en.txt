LA 55 Koo Ye Mɛ Gbeye!

HƐ MI NƆ KPO O ko ha nɛ nɔ ko nɛ sisi mo ngɛ bɔ nɛ Satan bɔɔ mɔde kaa e maa puɛ Yehowa Mawu kɛ Yesu Kristo a he biɛ ɔ he.

“Nyɛɛ te si kɛ wo Abosonsi, nɛ e maa tu fo kɛ je nyɛ he.”—YAK. 4:7.

KƐ JE be mi nɛ wa mwɔ wa yi mi kpɔ kaa wa maa sɔmɔ Yehowa a, Satan kɛ e daimonio ɔmɛ batsɔ wa he nyɛli nɛ a kpa we si gblegbleegble. Se Satan le kaa e be nyɛe maa ye wa nɔ kekleke be nɛ wa ngɛ Yehowa yemi kɛ buami ɔ náe. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, Abosiami bɔɔ mɔde kaa e ma kpata yemi kɛ buami nɛ ɔ he kɛ gu nɛ e maa ha nɛ waa kpo wa he ngɛ Yehowa he ɔ nɔ. Blɔ kake nɛ e guɔ nɔ kɛ peeɔ jã ji kaa, e bɔɔ mɔde nɛ e ha nɛ wa ná juɛmi enyɔɔnyɔ kaa Yehowa kɛ e Bi ɔ ngɛ wa se nitsɛnitsɛ.—2 Kor. 11:14; Yak. 1:6.

2 Ke ta ngɛ nɔ yae ɔ, e he hia nɛ tabɔhi nɛ a ná a nyɔtɔtsɛmɛ a mi hemi kɛ yemi. Ke a je hemi kɛ yemi nɛ a ngɛ ngɛ a mi ɔ kɛ je a he ɔ, eko ɔ, a maa pee nɔ́ nɛ a suɔ. Enɛ ɔ ma nyɛ maa gbe mɛ. Jã kɛ̃ nɛ wɔ hu wa ji tabɔhi ngɛ mumi mi ta nɛ wa kɛ “mumi yaya nɛ ngɛ hiɔwe he ɔmɛ” ngɛ hwue ɔ mi. (Efe. 6:12) Satan Abosiami le kaa ke wa je hemi kɛ yemi nɛ wa ngɛ ngɛ Yehowa Mawu kɛ Yesu Kristo mi ɔ kɛ je a he ɔ, eko ɔ, wa be kplɛɛe ngɛ a blɔtsɔɔmi kɛ a yemi kɛ buami nɔ. Lɔ ɔ he ɔ, e bɔɔ mɔde fɛɛ nɛ e ma nyɛ nɛ e gbɔjɔ wa hemi kɛ yemi nɛ wa ngɛ ngɛ a mi ɔ. Ngɛ munyu nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, wa ma susu bɔ nɛ e kɛ ga nɛ ɔ tsu ní ha ngɛ blema, kɛ bɔ nɛ e kɛ ngɛ ní tsue mwɔnɛ ɔ hu he. Kɛkɛ ɔ, wa ma susu bɔ nɛ wa ma plɛ kɛ te si kɛ wo e ga nɛ ɔmɛ ha he. Ngɛ munyu nɛ nyɛɛ se ɔ mi ɔ, wa ma susu ga kpa enyɔ nɛ Satan kɛ tsuɔ ní kɛ siɔ wɔ ngɛ wa mumi mi ta ɔ mi ɔ he.*

* Pi Satan nɛ kɛ kaahi nɛ waa kpeɔ kɛ kpeɔ ɔ tsuo baa tɛ̃ɛ. Se lɛ nɛ e kɛ “je ɔ mumi ɔ” ba, nɛ enɛ ɔ haa nɛ nihi nɛ a teɔ si kɛ woɔ wɔ ɔ yi ma Mawu webi nɔ. Lɔ ɔ he ɔ, kɛ̃ tɛ̃ɛ jio, kɛ gu nɔ ko nɔ jio, Satan ngɛ kahamihi babauu nɛ waa kpeɔ kɛ kpeɔ ngɛ wa mumi mi ta nɔ womi ɔ mi ɔ se.—1 Kor. 2:12; 1 Yoh. 5:19.

SATAN GU YEHOWA KƐ YESU A HE NƆ DUƆ̌

3 Benɛ adesahi a yi nɔ to he kɛkɛ nɛ Satan bɔ mɔde kaa e maa je Adam kɛ Hawa a hɛ mi kɛ je Yehowa nɔ. Satan sisi Hawa nɛ e ha e he ye kaa Bɔlɔ ɔ susuu we e he kpakpa. Satan bi lɛ ke: “Anɔkuale nɛ Mawu de ke nyɛ ko ye abɔɔ ɔ mi tsohi tsuo a nɔ yiblii lo?” (1 Mose 3:1-5) Satan ha Hawa he ye kaa Yehowa ngɛ hyehi nɛ sɛ nɔ sue kɛ ngɛ lɛ kɛ e huno ɔ tsie. E dɔ wawɛɛ kaa Hawa kɛ pee se Adam fĩ henyɛlɔ Satan se. A kpa Yehowa—nɔ nɛ ha mɛ nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ ɔ nɔ fomi.—1 Mose 3:6.

4 Kɛ je Adam kɛ Hawa be ɔ mi nɛ Satan ya nɔ nɛ e guo Yehowa he guɛ. Kɛ̃kɛ nɛ Satan ha nɛ Hiob huɛmɛ lakatsɛmɛ ɔmɛ wo Hiob yi mi nɛ e pee nya kɛ ji Yehowa suɔ lɛ nɛ e buɔ anɔkuale nɛ e yeɔ ɔ he akɔtaa. (Hiob 22:1-3) Abosonsɛ bu akɛ haomihi nɛ ba e nɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ Hiob ma kua Yehowa. E ngɛ mi akɛ Satan ga womi ɔ ha nɛ Hiob kɛ nyagba ngua kpe mohu lɛɛ, se anɔkualetsɛ nɛ ɔ ya nɔ nɛ e ye anɔkuale, nɛ e hɛ ji e huɛ bɔmi kɛ Mawu kɔ nya gblegbleegble.—Hiob 27:1, 5.

5 Jeha lafa komɛ a se benɛ Yesu ba zugba a nɔ ɔ, Satan bɔ mɔde kaa e maa tsi nihi a nya konɛ a kpe Yesu se. Blɔ kake nɛ e gu nɔ kɛ pee enɛ ɔ ji kaa, e ha nɛ jami nya fɛɛli nɛ a nyɛɔ Yesu kɛ ni kpahi gba lakpahi kɛ kɔ Yesu he. Nɛ nyagbenyagbe ɔ, a gbe lɛ kaa nɔ ko nɛ pee yayami nitsɛnitsɛ.—Kane

Mateo 11:19; 27:27-31; Marko 14:55, 56; Yohane 8:41-45.

6 Benɛ Yesu gbo se ɔ, Satan hɛɛ e hɛ ngɛ Yesu seblɔli ɔmɛ a ní tsumi ɔ nɔ. Kekleekle ɔ, Abosami bɔ mɔde kaa e maa wo jeyi nami ngɛ Yesu si temi ɔ he. Ngɛ Satan hewami nya ɔ, lakpa sane pɛtle kaa Yesu kaseli ɔmɛ ba ju e gbɔyɔ ɔ. (Mat. 28:11-15) Lɔ ɔ se ɔ, ngɛ ligbihi nɛ nyiɛ se ɔmɛ a mi ɔ, Satan bɔ mɔde kaa e maa gu yi mi wami nɔ kɛ wo asafo ɔ he gbeye. Satan suɔ nɛ e ha nɛ ní tsumi nɛ a Nyɔmtsɛ ɔ kɛ wo a dɛ ɔ nɛ pee kɛha mɛ nɔ́ ko nɛ yeko kɛ tsu.—Bɔfo 7:57–8:1; 12:1-3; 28:22.

7 E ngɛ mi kaa blema Kristofohi ɔmɛ ekomɛ kpa fiɛɛmi ngɛ nɔnyɛmi nɛ ɔ heje mohu lɛɛ, se a kpɛti nihi babauu da si kpinii nɛ a kpo kaa a maa kpa munyatsɔmi. A yɔse kaa Satan kɛ mumi yaya amɛ ji a he nyɛli nitsɛmɛ. (Bɔfo 4:18-20; 1 Pet. 5:8) Nɛ mwɔnɛ ɔ hu nɛɛ? Mɛni nɔ́ Satan ngɛ kɛ tsuɔ ní konɛ e kɛ hao wɔ nɛ waa kpa Yehowa sɔmɔmi?

Abonsam je ɔ ngɛ níhi nɛ puɛɔ Mawu simitihi ɔ fiee ngɛ wa nɔ (Hyɛ kuku 11)

tsɔɔ kaa Tsɛ ɔ kɛ Bi ɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ. Enɛ ɔ ha nɛ nimli be tsui he nɛ a maa le Tsɛ ɔ nɛ a li e biɛ ɔ, nɛ a jaa Bi ɔ mohu. Yehowa jio, Yesu jio, a kpɛti nɔ ko nɔ ko kplɛɛ we jami nɛ ɔ nɔ. (Mat. 4:10; Yoh. 4:23) Ke a gba nimli a hɛngmɛi jã a, e he maa wa nɛ a kɛ mɛ maa susu níhi a he, aloo po mɛ nitsɛmɛ a maa te si kɛ wo wɔ nɛ a maa fia níhi nɛ wa tsuɔ ɔ nɔ.

9 Ke wa kɛ nɔ ko bɔi huɛ bɔmi ɔ, nɔ́ kekleekle nɛ wa peeɔ ji kaa wa leɔ e biɛ. Satan hlaa nɛ e tsi nihi a nya konɛ a kɛ Yehowa ko bɔ huɛ kpekpeme kɛ gu nɛ e ha we nɛ a le E biɛ po nɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, kɛ je blema tɔɔ nɛ Satan ngɛ lakpa jami kɛ ngɛ ní tsue konɛ e ha nɛ nihi a hɛ nɛ je Mawu biɛ ɔ nɔ. (Kane Yeremiya 23:26, 27.) Jamɛ a henyɛlo ɔ ha po nɛ Mawu biɛ ɔ—nɛ Baiblo ɔ kɛ to mɔ nɛ he wa nɛ buɔ nɔ he he ɔ—je Baiblo sisi tsɔɔmihi fuu a mi. (Abɛ 18:10) Abosiami nɔuu nɛ e ngɛ lakpa munyu nɛ se kaa Yehowa nɛ haa nɛ nihi naa nɔ́ ɔ. Enɛ ɔ ha nɛ e he wa ha anɔkualetsɛmɛ nɛ a naa Yehowa kaa Huɛ nɛ a ma nyɛ maa hɛ kɛ su e he. (Yak. 4:8) Se kɛ̃ ɔ, Satan hlaa nɛ e gba Yehowa webi a hemi kɛ yemi ɔ hu nya.

9. Mɛni lakpa jami ha we nɛ nihi nɛ a kase ngɛ Satan he wami nya? (Yeremiya 23:26, 27)

4 Hwɔɔmi Mɔ O

10 Ke wa kɛ haomihi nɛ kɔɔ wɔ nitsɛmɛ wa he ngɛ kpee ɔ, Satan suɔ nɛ waa he nɛ waa ye kaa Yehowa susuu we wa he. Satan ngɛ hɛ kɛ nɔ nɔ kaa wa mi ma mi si ke Yehowa ha nɛ wa nɔ́ nami ɔ ya nɔ. (Abɛ 19:3) Nihi nɛ a nɔ́ peeɔ mɛ jã a ma nyɛ ma bi a he po ke: ‘Mɛni heje nɛ ma ya nɔ nɛ ma nyɛɛ Yehowa mlaahi kɛ e sisi tomi mlaahi a se ɔ? E be kaa e he ngɛ sɛ nɛ e kɛ ma ha mi!’ Susuomi nɛ ɔ ma nyɛ ma ha nɛ Satan ná wa nɔ he wami.

11 Satan kɛ níhi kaa intanɛt nɔ nimli a kpe hehi ngɛ ní tsɔe konɛ e kɛ fɔ̃ nɔ juɛmi nɔ kaa Yehowa sisi tomi mlaahi dɛ. Ngɛ he nɛ ɔmɛ a nɔ ɔ, nihi babauu tsɔɔ kaa je mi bami he mlaahi nɛ ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi ɔ be ngɛ tsaatsɛ, nɛ e sa kaa nɔ tsuaa nɔ nɛ e to lɔɔ he mlaa kɛ ha e he. E ji anɔkuale kaa, “je nɛ ɔ nɔ yelɔ ɔ” le nɔ́ nɛ e maa pee kɛ tsɔɔ kaa nɔ́ kpakpa kɛ nɔ́ yayami a kpɛti be gbami.—Yoh. 14:30; Yes. 5:20; 1 Tim. 4:1, 2.

12 Satan lakpa munyi kɛ susumihi a tue nɛ a maa bu ɔ ma nyɛ maa laka wɔ kɛ je Yehowa he. Nyɛminyɛyo kpataku ko nɛ waa ma tsɛ lɛ Shannon de ke: “E he be nɛ i kɛ le kaa níhi nɛ a tsɔɔ mi ngɛ sukuu kɛ nihi nɛ i kɛ mɛ bɔɔ ɔ ngɛ nɔ́ nɛ da kɛ nɔ́ nɛ dɛ he juɛmi nɛ i ngɛ ɔ puɛe. Bɔ nɛ i fiaa be babauu ngɛ intanɛt nɔ ɔ, jã nɔuu kɛ̃ nɛ i suɔ nɛ ma kase nihi nɛ i kɛ mɛ kpeɔ ngɛ jeme ɔ a jeblɛɛhi. Bleuu pɛ nɛ i bɔni Yehowa, ye weku, kɛ ye nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi a he jemi.” Nyagbenyagbe ɔ, Shannon kpale kɛ ba e hiɔwe Tsɛ ɔ ngɔ nɛ e je Abosiami klɔ ɔ mi. Mɛni ye bua lɛ?

13 Shannon yɔse kaa e sa nɛ e bɔ mɔde nɛ e kɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu nɛ hi huɛ bɔe mi. E de ke: “I sɔle nɛ i tsu ní ngɛ gbi nɛ i sɔle ɔ he kɛ gu níhi a mi nɛ i hla ngɛ wa womihi a mi ɔ nɔ. Vidio nɛ ji *Young People Ask—How Can I Make Real Friends?* ɔ ye bu mi wawɛɛ.* Ngɛ vidio nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, nyɛminyumu yo ko kɛ haomihi nɛ kpaa ngɛ kaa ye nɔ́ ɔ kpe. E kɛ he si bami ya e fɔli a ngɔ nɛ e bi yemi kɛ buami. Imi hu i pee jã. Ye fɔli, nikɔtɔma amɛ, kɛ Kristofohi nɛ a sɛ ngɛ asafo ɔ mi nɛ a ji nyɛmimɛ yi ɔmɛ ye bu mi nɛ i gba ye he kɛ je huɛ bɔmi yaya he. I ba nu sisi kaa anɔkuale ɔ pi asafo mi kpehi a yaami kɛ ye fɔli a bua nɛ ma jɔɔ pɛ kɛkɛ, se mohu kaa ma ha nɛ anɔkuale ɔ nɛ pee imi nitsɛ ye nɔ́ nɛ ma kɛ Yehowa nɛ hi wekumi kpekpeme mi.”

Baiblo Kasehemi Ní A Hwɛ́ We Sika

Wa teɔ si kɛ woɔ Satan mɔdebliblicai ɔ kɛ guu blɔ nɔ nɛ wa susuɔ bɔ nɛ wa ma plɛ kɛ ya nɔ nɛ huɛ bɔmi kpakpa nɛ mi wa nɛ hi wa kɛ Yehowa kpatipɔɔ wa kɛle nɔ (Hyɛ kuku 14)

he níhi nɛ mi yijiemɔ ji lakpa loo níhi nɛ be anɔkwale kulaa a nɔ. Se ke wa kase Baiblo ɔ kɛ wa womihi nɛ wa bɔɔ kɛ Kristofoli nɛ a tsɛ kɛ ya hɛ mi daa a, e be he wao kaa a ma sisi wɔ. Ke wa le anɔkwale ɔ kɛ ya hɛ mi nitsɛ ɔ, e maa pee he wao ha Satan kaa e ma gba wa hemi kɛ yemi ɔ nya.—Abɛ 13:20; Bɔfo 17:11.

15 Ke wa kase Baiblo ɔ saminya a, wa hemi kɛ yemi nɛ wa ngɔ fɔ Yehowa kɛ Yesu nɔ kaa a ngɛ wa fã a ma wa. (Abɛ 22:19)

Ke wa susu níhi nɛ tsɔɔ bɔ nɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu susuɔ adesahi a he ha a he ɔ, e ma ha nɛ wa ma ná nɔmiami kaa a ji huɛmɛ nɛ a yeɔ anɔkuale. Baiblo ɔ hyi tɔ kɛ nɔmiami níhi nɛ tsɔɔ kaa Yehowa kɛ Yesu suɔ wɔ. Kaa nɔhyɛnɔ́ ɔ, Yehowa tsɔɔ kaa suɔmi nɛ e ngɛ kɛha e webi ɔ kle pe nɔ́ nɛ bi nyɛ nɛ ngɛ e bi nye ha a ngɛ kɛha e bi ɔ. (Yes. 49:15) E buɔ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ nɛ suɔ e biɛ ɔ kaa “jwetri nɛ he jua wa.” (Mal. 3:16, 17, sisi ningma.) Jehanɛ se hu, Baiblo ɔ ha nɛ wa na bɔ nɛ Yesu suɔmi nɛ e ngɛ kɛha wɔ ɔ mi kuɔ ha kɛ gu bɔ nɛ e de níhi ngɛ e sɔlemi afɔle sami ɔ he ɔ nɔ.—Yoh. 15:12, 13.

15. Mɛni wa maa pee konɛ wa ná nɔ mi mami kaa Yehowa kɛ Yesu ngɛ wa fã?

6 HWE TƆƆ NEƐ

16 Ke wa hɛ mi ngɛ nɔ kplɛɛe ngɛ ye nɔ buamɔ nɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu ngɛ wɔ hae mwɔnɛ ɔ nɔ ɔ, e ma nyɛ maa ye bua wɔ konɛ waa ná nɔ mi mami ngɛ suɔmi nɛ a ngɛ kɛ ha wɔ ɔ he. Wa ma nyɛ ma kai behi nɛ wa nu he kaa Yehowa kɛ wɔ ngɛ kpaata, nɛ kaimi nɛ ɔmɛ ma nyɛ ma ha wɔ he wami. Wa ma nyɛ ma bi wa nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi he nɛ a ná ye nɔ buamɔ nɛ ɔ ngɛ. (Abɛ 22:17) Ke wa pee jã a, e maa wo wa he nɔ fɔmi he wami kaa tɔxuu kahle nɛ wa kɛ maa kpe ɔ, Yehowa kɛ Yesu ngɛ wa se.—Kane La 118:5, 6.

17 Wa ti nɔ fɛɛ nɔ suɔ kaa e maa ya nɔ nɛ e pee Yehowa Mawu kɛ Yesu Kristo a huɛ kpakpa. Se kɛ yaasi be nɛ a ma kpata Satan kɛ e daimonio ɔmɛ a hɛ mi kɛ ya neneene ɔ, e sa nɛ waa kpe kɛ nina mɔbɔ nɛ a bɔɔ kaa a maa gblagbla hemi kɛ yemi nɛ wa ngɛ ngɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu mi ɔ. (Kpo. 20:1-3, 10) Bɔ nɛ pee nɛ wa nyɛ nɛ waa da henyɛli ɔmɛ a ga nyɛmi ɔmɛ a nya a, e sa nɛ waa kɛ Yehowa kpa ti huɛ bɔmi kpakpa nɛ wa nɔ nɛ mi masi kaa lɛ kɛ e Bi suɔlɔ ɔ suɔ wɔ nɛ wa jua wa ngɛ a hɛ mi. Kɛkɛ ɔ, kaa bɔ nɛ Yesu de ɔ, nɔ ko nɔ ko be nyɛe maa “gbla [nyɛ] kɛ je Tsɛ ɔ dɛ mi.”—Kane Yohane 10:29; Yak. 4:7.

18 Ngɛ munyu nɛ nyɛɛ se ɔ mi ɔ, wa ma susu ga slɔɔto enyɔ kpa nɛ Satan kɛ tsuɔ ní ngɛ wa he kɛ siɔ wɔ ngɛ wa mumi mi ta nɛ wa ngɛ hwuɔ ɔ mi ɔ he—bɔ nɛ e bɔɔ mɔde kaa e ma ba mi gbami kɛ ba asafo ɔ mi kɛ si nɛ a teɔ kɛ woɔ wɔ kɛ jeɔ kpo nɔ ɔ. Otshi komɛ a se ɔ, i je kpokpa a nɔ ngɛ French Guiana nɛ i kpale kɛ ho France ya. I na Matsɛ Yemi Kpe He ko nɛ i je ye Baiblo kasemi ɔ sisi ekko. E ngɛ mi kaa ye si himi ya nɔ hehi mohu lɛɛ, se bɔ nɛ i tsake ha a pee ye weku ɔ nyakpɛ. I kpa siga numi, dã dɛhi numi, aloo yami ngɛ hehi nɛ a fiaa gba ngɛ. A baptisi mi ngɛ 1996 mi. I sɔle kɛ ha Yehowa kaa ma nyɛ maa pee ma se gbɛgbalɔ kaa Michel. Enɛ ɔ pee kɛ ha mi kaa blɔ nɛ hi pe kulaa nɛ ma gu nɔ kɛ je ye si kpami kpo kɛ tsɔɔ Yehowa.

Se kɛ̃ ɔ, ye tsui jɔ̃ e bike tsɔ̃ he je nɛ i nyɛɛ we nɛ ma tsu ye hɛ nɔ kami nɛ hiɛ kpa ko ɔ he ní. (Fiɛl. 7:16) E he hia nɛ ma kase kaa Yehowa ji Tsɛ nɛ ngɛ suɔmi, nɛ e hwe we nɛ wa pee nɔ mluku, mohu ɔ, e buɔ mɔde nɛ wa bɔɔ ɔ he klilɛ.

Ngɛ Paris ɔ, a tsu mi kaa kpokpa nɔ hyɛlɔ nɛ e daa nɔ he. I nu he kaa i wa we nɛ i be níhi a si kpami kɛ ha blɔ nya ní tsumi nɛ ɔ. Se i kase kaa Yehowa mumi klɔuklɔu ɔ yeɔ bua wɔ nɛ wa tsuɔ níhi babauu pe nɔ́ nɛ wɔ nitsɛmɛ wa ma nyɛ maa tsu.

I kɛ nyagba kpa ko hu kpe. Kɛje ye jokuɛwiashi ɔ, I na nɔ ko nɛ a tsɛɛ ke hyperhidrosis, nɛ e haa nɛ nɔ fiaa lakpa fuu nitsɛ. Jamɛatsɛ hiɛ kpaako ye dɛhi a nɔ. E mi waa pe kulaa ke I ngɛ nɔ ko nɛ tsɔɔ dɔmɔ mi, kaa be nɛ I ngɛ munyu tue ngɛ kpɔ mi kpe aloo kpokpa nɔ kpe ko sisi. Se I nyɛɔ nɛ I gbaa nɔ nɛ ngɛ ye nɔmlɔtso ɔ nɔ nɛ ɔ nɔ kɛ gu Yehowa nɛ I sɔleɔ kɛ biɔ lɛ he jɔmi kɛ ha ye juɛmi ɔ nɔ. Nyagba a we se pɔtɛɛ, se ke I fia ye hɛ ngɛ Yehowa nɔ wawɛɛ ɔ, ye he jɔɔ mi pe nɛ I fiaa lakpa bɔɔ.

Jeha 2011 ɔ mi ɔ, Yehowa dloo wɔ ní nɛ wa tsui ngɛ hlae be gbagbɛɛgbɛ ɔ ngɛ mi mi jami mi. Wa bɔni ma se sɔmɔmi ní tsumi ngɛ New Caledonia, nɛ ji zugba kpekpemi ko ngɛ Pacific anigbɔ kɛ seje he ɔ, nɛ e kɛ Paris kɛ wehi pe kilomita 16,000 (maili 10,000). Jamɛ a ní tsumi ɔ hu ba kɛ haomahi ba. E bi nɛ waa kɛ wɔ nitsɛmɛ wa he nɛ ma nya ní hehi, kaa agbeli, amakani, kɛ bami a nɔ. Nɛ e bi nɛ waa kɛ kokonitihi a kuomi kɛ gbehi a gbomí nɛ puɛɔ wa mahe kpo motu hiɛ ɔ hu nɛ maa kpa. Jehanɛ se hu ɔ, e bi nɛ waa kɛ ní sɛɛmi blɔ he nɛ maa kpa. Ma a nɔ bi ɔmɛ pɔɔ heto bumi, pi kɛ munyahi, se kɛ a hɛngmɛ sɔhi nɛ a woɔ nɔ kɛkɛ.

Yehowa hu ye bua wɔ konɛ waa nyɛ nɛ waa da blɔ nya nitsumi heehi nya. Wɔ tsu ní kaa ma se sanekpakpa fiɛɛli nɛ wɔ tsu ní ngɛ Betel hu jeha bɔɔ ko. Wɔ ya Matsɛ Yemi Sanekpakpa Fiɛɛli A Sukuu ɔ, nɛ amlɔ nɛ ɔ, wɔ ngɛ kpokpa nɔ nitsumi ɔ tsue. Wɔ na Yehowa yemi kɛ buami kɛ gu e mumi klɔuklɔu ɔ nɔ ngɛ blɔ nya nitsumi nɛ ɔmɛ tsuo a mi. E tsɔɔ mi kaa tsɔ̃ he wami nɛ be nɔ mi ɔ tsi we nɔ nya konɛ e ko nyɛ kɛ tsu ní kɛ ha lɛ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, i sɔleɔ be fɛɛ be nɛ i kpaa pɛɛ wawɛɛ kɛ biɔ yemi kɛ buami konɛ ma nyɛ ma tsu nɔ́ nɛ he hia a. E ngɛ mi kaa i kɛ he jɔmi kpaako tu we munyu ngɛ tue buli a hɛ mi mohu lɛɛ, se kɛ gu Mawu yemi kɛ buami nɔ ɔ, i nyɛ nɛ i wo asafohi nɛ wɔ ya slaa mɛ ɔ he wami nɛ i tsɔɔ mɛ blɔ.

Ngɛ tsakemi nɛ ɔmɛ tsuo a mi ɔ, Claudine ye bua mi be fɛɛ be. E kɛ tsui kpaa mi ngɛ ye yiwamihi a he. E yeɔ bua ngɛ ní nɛ wa tsuɔ ɔ he, nɛ e peeɔ klaalo be fɛɛ be nɛ e maa fiɛɛ kɛ kã. E yi nyagba ngɛ pu nɛ nya wa kɛ kpokpa nɔ ní tsumi ɔ mi haomihi a he.

Ngɛ ye kpe ko nɛ e nɔ nya kekleekle ɔ mi ɔ, i kase kaa Yehowa, Bɔlɔ Kpatami ɔ, hyɛɔ e jálɔi a nɔ be fɛɛ be. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, i ná gbaamihi babauu. Yehowa ye bua mi nɛ i ná juɛmi nɛ da ngɛ nɔyeli he kɛ he nɛ i ba si. I kpa je ɔ mi huɛmɛ a he bɔmi bɔ nɛ pee nɛ ma nyɛ maa hi si ngɛ Yehowa blɔhi a nya, se Yehowa ha nɛ i ná huɛmɛ nitsɛmɛ babauu nɛ e he be si pe lɔɔ po. I ná yo kpakpa ko kɛ ba pee ye yi. Nɛ i ngɛ hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi kpetekpleenyɛ kaa ma ná Yehowa mi mi jami ɔ mi he bua jɔmi kɛ ya neneene.—Marko 10:29, 30.

ANƐ O LE LO?

Mɛni níhi a peeɔ ngɛ ma agbohi a nya ngɛ Baiblo behi a mi?

MAAGPƆ agbóhi pi hehi kɛkɛ nɛ a guɔ kɛ sɛɔ ma a mi. Akɛnɛ nihi fuu guɔ lejɛ ahi heje ɔ, a plɛ pee hehi nɛ nihi yaa daa ligbi kɛ tsuɔ ní fuu ngɛ. Baiblo ɔ tu ma agbóhi a he munyu sihi nɛ se 50, nɛ e tsɔɔ níhi nɛ a tsuɔ ngɛ lejɛ ahi.

Nihi buaa a he nya ngɛ ma a agbohi a nya. Hebri munyu nɛ a kɛ tsu ní kɛ ha “agbo” ɔ tsɔɔ babauu pe agbo nitsɛ nɛ ma kpaako a ya hyɛɛ ngɛ ma a hɛ mi ɔ kɛkɛ. E tsɔɔ he ko nɛ a to kaa ma ngɛ he nɛ a guɔ kɛ sɛɛ ma ko mi ɔ. Ngɛ he komɛ ɔ, kpo gbagbahi nɛ ma a nya nɔ bi babauu ma nyɛ ma bua a he nya ngɛ nɔ ɔ ngɛ agbohi ɔmɛ a se.—Neh. 8:1.

Julaahi toɔ jua hehi ngɛ ma agbohi a he. Eko ɔ, a biɛ Yerusalem agbohi ekomɛ kɛ ngɛ níhi nɛ a juaa ngɛ lejɛ ɔ he je, kaa Lo Agbo ɔ kɛ To Agbo ɔ.—Zef. 1:10; Yoh. 5:2.

Nihi tsuɔ jua yemi kɛ a nitsɛmɛ a sanehi a he ní ngɛ ma agbohi a nya. Abraham kɛ Boaz tsu níhi ngɛ mɛ nitsɛmɛ a he ngɛ odasefohi a hɛ mi ngɛ ma agbohi a nya. (1 Mo. 23:10-15, 17, 18; Rut 4:1, 10) Ke nɔ ko hɛɛ we blɔhialɔhi a nɔ ɔ, a kpɛti ni komɛ hwɔɔ kpeteŋkpele he kpeti ngɛ ma agbo ɔ nya. (Koj. 19:15-21) Nihi yaa ma agbohi a nya konɛ a ya nu sane hehi nɛ ya nɔ ɔ nɔuu.—2 Sam. 18:24, 25.

Kojolɔhi kojɔɔ nɔ he ngɛ ma agbóhi a nya. A kojoɔ sanehi ngɛ nimli nɛ a ngɛ sane nɛ a ngɛ kɛee kɛ nyagbenɔ ɔ nue ɔmɛ a hɛ mi. (I-Maa. 21:18-20) Ma nɔ kojomi nɛ ɔ wooo kojolɔ ɔmɛ he wami nɛ a pee dalo kɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ ngɛ a kojomihi a mi. Ke kojolɔ ɔmɛ kojo se ɔ, ma nitsulɔhi tsoo tomi nɔ amlɔ nɔuu. (I-Maa. 25:2) Ngɛ ma agbó ɔmɛ a nya a,

Ma a agbo ɔmɛ a nya ji hehi nɛ daa ligula si himi ɔ yaa nɔ ngɛ babauu

A kpa kojoli a blɔ kaa a wo dami kɛ jehanɛ se je mi bami kpakpa he wami.—Deut. 16:18-20; Hiob 29:7, 11-17.

Nyumu anɔkualetsɛmɛ wo Israelbii nɛ a ngɛ ma a agbo ɔ nya a he wami konɛ a kɛ a hɛ fɔ Yehowa nɔ. Gbalɔi kɛ ni kpahi tu munɔ ngɛ agbo ɔmɛ a nya ejakaa a le kaa nihi buaa a he nya ngɛ lejɛ ɔ. (1 Maŋ. 22:10; Yer. 17:19, 20) Kɛ kɔ he ɔ, Yehu jaje ngɛ ma a tsuo hɛ mi kaa Yehowa ha e munyahi ba mi kɛ si Ahab we ɔ. (2 Maŋ. 10:8-10) Matsɛ Hezekia bua e ta buli a hanyihi a nya ngɛ ma a agbo ɔ ko nya konɛ e wo mɛ he wami nɛ a ngɔ a hɛ kɛ fɔ Yehowa yemi kɛ buami nɔ.—2 Kpɔ. 32:6-8.

Baabulo ɔ de ke “nile nitsɛnitsɛ” ngɛ kpoe kɛ ngɛ e gbi nɔ wue ngɛ “ma a agbó ɔmɛ a nya.” (Abɛ 1:20, 21) Blema ngɛ Israel ɔ, ma a agbó ɔmɛ a nya ji he nɛ nyumuhi nɛ a yeɔ anɔkwale ɔ kɛ nile ga womi haa ngɛ kɛ tsoo sanehi a mi ngɛ dami nya a nɛ. Mwɔnɛ ɔ, a ngma Yehowa nile ɔ ngɛ Baabulo ɔ mi, nɛ e sɔmɔli ngɛ he tsuaa he ngɛ nimli ye bua kɛ ngɛ a hɛ mi tsɔɔe kɛ yaa nile nitsɛnitsɛ nɛ ɔ he.

BAIBLO MI ANƆKWALYE Tso Nɛ E Teɔ Si Mamlɛ

Amond tso ɔ ji tsohi maa pee 30 nɛ a tu a he munyu ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi ɔmɛ a kpɛti kake. E ngɛ tso kɛkɛlemi ɔmɛ nɛ fɔfɔɛ puɛɔ ngɛ a nɔ ngɛ fɛ̃ɛ be ɔ se ɔ a kpɛti. A ma nyɛ maa na e fɔfɔɛhi nɛ a he ngɛ fɛu nɛ a he ngɛ yɛlɔɔ jio, heblɔɔ jio ɔ kɛ je kɛ ba si Yanuari nyagbe lɔɔ po. Ní nɛ sɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ ji kaa, Hebri biɛ nɛ a kɛ tsɛɔ amond tso ɔ tsɔɔ nɔ́ ko nɛ “tleɔ si.” Anɛ kake peemi ko ngɛ tso nɛ ɔ kɛ e Bɔlɔ ɔ a kpɛti lo?

Sisi ningma nɛ ngɛ Yeremiya 7:25 tsɔɔ kaa Yehowa kale e he kaa nɔ ko nɛ teɔ si mɔtu hiɛ. March 15, 2011, Hwɔɔmi Mɔ ɔ de ke: “Kaa bɔ nɛ amɔnd tso ɔ ‘teɔ si’ mɔtu hiɛ ɔ, jã kɛ̃ nɛ ngɛ mfoniri yemi blɔ nɔ ɔ, Yehowa hu ngɛ ‘tee si mɔtu hiɛ’ . . . konɛ e bɔ e ma a kɔkɔ ngɛ tue gboemi he biɛmɛ ɔmɛ a he. . . . Nɛ e be e he jɔɔe—e ma ‘hi hɛ ja’—kɛ yaasi be nɛ e gbalɔ munyu ɔ maa ba mi.”—Yer. 1:11, 12.

A kɛ almond tso ɔ fɔfɔɛ hiɔhiɔ ɔmɛ hu tsu ní ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi kɛ kɔ bimɔ kɛ tsɔɔ bɔ nɛ yikpɔ hiɔ kɔɔ kɛ ha nimeli ha. (Fiɛl. 12:5) Nɛ eko ɔ, akɛnɛ tso ɔ fɔfɔɛ ɔmɛ he ngɛ fɛu nɛ a he ngɛ fɛuu heje ɔ, a kɛ tsu ní kɛ pee nɔhyɛnɔ ha kplu nɛ ngɛ bo tsu ɔ kane tso ɔ niji ɔmɛ a nɔ ɔ.—2 Mose 37:17-20.
