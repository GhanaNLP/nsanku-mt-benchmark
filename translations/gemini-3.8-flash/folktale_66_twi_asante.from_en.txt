Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka yi yɛ nokware)

KOKOKYINAKA ANKƆ ANANSE HƆ ANKƆBƆ BOFA

BERE bi a atwam no, anomaa Kokokyinaka kɔɔ Ananse nkyɛn kɔsrɛɛ no sɛ ɔmfa sikakɔkɔɔ mframa a ne kɛse te sɛ asuanu (£4) mbɔ no bosea. Afei, esiane sɛ na Ananse ankasa nni bi nti, ɔkɔɔ n’adamfo Ntontom nkyɛn kɔpɛɛ bi de bɛmaa n’adamfo, anomaa Kokokyinaka no. Da a Ntontom hyɛe (sɛ ɔmfa bosea no mma no) no twaam, na Ntontom ka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, “Kɔ kɔfa me sika futuro bra ma me.” Ananse buaa no sɛ, “Kokokyinaka no te Onyina no atifi hɔ, enti kɔfa wo sika futuro no.” Ntontom bisae sɛ, “Mɛyɛ dɛn na manya; abɔdeɛ no tumi tu.” Ananse buaa no sɛ, “Kɔ kɔtɔ dua no ananmu pɛn, na sɛ wohunu , dua no bɛbu, ɛnna woakyere Kokokyinaka anomaa no, na woanya wo sika futuro no bio.” Ntontom no kɔe kɔtoaa dua no ase. Ɛberɛ a na ɛrebɛbu no, Kokokyinaka anomaa no tu firii hɔ kɔe, pa bu bu bu! Ɛnna Ntontom baeɛ, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Mo ne adwuma, Ananse.” Ɛno nti na yɛwɔ abɛbusɛm bi a ɛkɔ so sɛ, “Abɔdeɛ a yɛfrɛ no Kokokyinaka anomaa no amfɛm hwee firii Ananse hɔ a ɛbɛma Ntontom akɔwatiri Onyina.” M’anansesɛm a mato yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na ma bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
