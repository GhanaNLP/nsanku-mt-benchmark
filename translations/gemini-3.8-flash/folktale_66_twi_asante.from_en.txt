Yɛnni aseɛ koraa, yɛnni aseɛ koraa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokorɛ)

KƆKƆKYINAKA ANKƆ ANANSE NKYƐN ANKƆFA EFƐM

BERE bi a atwam no, anomaa Kokokyinaka kɔɔ Ananse nkyɛn kɔhwehwɛɛ sɛ ɔbɛbɔ asuanu sika (£4) bosea. Afei, esiane sɛ na Ananse nso nni bi nti, ɔkɔɔ ne nnamfo Ntontom nkyɛn kɔgyee bi de bɛbrɛɛ ne nnamfo anomaa Kokokyinaka no. Da a Ntontom hyɛe (sɛ ɔntua bosea no) twamii, na Ntontom ka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, “Kɔgye me sika brɛ me.” Ananse buaa sɛ, “Anomaa Kokokyinaka no te Onyina no atifi hɔ, enti kɔgye wo sika.” Ntontom bisae sɛ, “Mɛyɛ dɛn na manya; abɔdeɛ no tumi tu.” Ananse buaa sɛ, “Kɔto dua no hwiti, na wohwɛ a, dua no bɛbu, ɛnna woaso anomaa Kokokyinaka no mu, na woanya wo sika aba.” Ntontom kɔtoe dua no hwiti. Ɛberɛ a ɛreyɛ abu no, anomaa Kokokyinaka no fii hɔ kɔe, pa bu bu bu! Ɛnna Ntontom bae, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Mmo ne yɔ, Ananse.” Ɛno nti na yɛwɔ bɛ bi a ɛka sɛ, “Abɔdeɛ a wɔfrɛ no anomaa Kokokyinaka amfɛm Ananse sika na Ntontom akɔto Onyina hwiti.” Me mpaninnsɛm a metoeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na ma bi nso nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
