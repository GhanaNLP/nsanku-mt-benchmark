Jeŋ Muu Fɛɛ Adesai Ahedoo He Kpaŋmɔ

Kaimɔ

Ejaakɛ adesai aweku lɛ mli bii fɛɛ anɔkwa gbɛnaa kɛ amɛ agboshimo kɛ hegbɛ ni akɛfɔ amɛ ni anyɛɛɛ aje kɛjɛ amɛdɛŋ lɛ yɔsemɔ lɛ, eji heyeli, jalɛ kɛ toiŋjɔlɛ ni yɔɔ je lɛ mli lɛ nɔshitoo nii,

Ejaakɛ adesai ahegbɛi anɔ kpeemɔ kɛ amɛnɔ gbeemɔ kɛ niseniianii ni gbaa gbɔmɔtsoŋ henilɛ kɛ amanehulu baa, kɛ jeŋ shishijee nɔ ni adesai baana wiemɔ kɛ hemɔkɛyeli hegbɛ kɛ gbeyeishemɔ kɛ hiamɔ mli heyeli lɛ ji gbɔmɛi fɛɛ akpeemɔ nɔ ni nɔ kwɔ fe fɛɛ,

Akɛnɛ ehe hiaa waa diɛŋtsɛ akɛ mla abu adebɔɔ hegbɛi anɔ, koni gbomɔ akayafee nɔ ni ebaanyɛ kɛte shi ewo yiwalɛ kɛ nɔnyɛɛ nɔyeli akɛ naagbee gbɛjianɔmɔ hewɔ lɛ,

Beni eba lɛ akɛ ehe miihia waa akɛ akanya naanyobɔɔ wekukpɔŋ ni ka jeŋmaji ateŋ lɛ nɔyaa,

Beni United Nations maŋbii lɛ emale kɛteŋŋ yɛ amɛ Kpitiŋgbɛi Lɛ mli yɛ adasa hegbɛi titrii lɛ amli, yɛ gbɔmɔ gbekɛ kɛ agbo hejɔlɛ kɛ hegbɛ ni eyɔɔ lɛ mli, kɛ yɛ hii kɛ yei ahegbɛi ni yeɔ egbɔ lɛ amli, kɛ beni amɛfa amɛtsuiŋ akɛ amɛbaaha maŋ mli ŋwɔlɛ kɛ shihilɛ kpakpa aya hiɛ yɛ heyeli ni da kɛmɔ shi mli lɛ,

Akɛni Jeŋmaji ni yɔɔ Kpee lɛ mli lɛ ewo shi akɛ amɛ kɛ Jeŋmaji Ekomefeemɔ lɛ baafee ekome kɛwo jeŋ fɛɛ bulɛ kɛ hegbɛi kɛ heyeli krɔŋkrɔŋ ni adebɔɔ kɛha adesai lɛ mli hewalɛ kɛ nɔkwɛmɔ,

Ejaakɛ hegbɛi kɛ heyeli nɛɛ ashishinumɔ ni tamɔ kome ji nɔ ni he hiaa fe fɛɛ kɛha shiwoo nɛɛ mlihenyɛmɔ kɛmɔ shi,

No hewɔ lɛ, agbɛnɛ,

Kpee Kpeteŋkpele Lɛ

Jajeɔ Nɔ ni Akpã shi nɛɛ ni kɔɔ Jeŋ Fɛɛ Gbɔmɛi Ahegbɛi Ahe nɛɛ akɛ nɔ ko ni maŋbii fɛɛ kɛ maji fɛɛ baakwɛ nɔ kɛtsɔ he gbɛjianɔtoo nɔ, koni gbɔmɔ fɛɛ gbɔmɔ kɛ gbɛjianɔtoo fɛɛ gbɛjianɔtoo ni yɔɔ maŋbii amli nɛɛ, kɛ nɔ ni akpã shi nɛɛ ma amɛjwɛŋmɔ mli be fɛɛ be, kɛtsɔ tsɔɔmɔ kɛ nikasemɔ nɔ koni ewo hegbɛi kɛ heyeli nɛɛ amli bulɛ he hewalɛ, kɛ kɛtsɔ gbɛjianɔtoi ni yaa nɔ, ni kɔɔ maŋ lolo kɛ jeŋ fɛɛ he nɛɛ nɔ, kɛha jeŋ fɛɛ kɛ nɔ ni yɔɔ mli ni baaye omanye ni akpɛlɛ nɔ ni ahe ahiɛ, yɛ Maŋbii ni ji Bimɛi nɛɛ kɛ amɛ diɛŋtsɛ amɛmaŋbii kɛ maŋbii ni yɔɔ hegbɛi ni yɔɔ amɛ nɔyeli shishi nɛɛ ateŋ fɛɛ.

Kuku 1

Adebɔ gbɔmɛi fɛɛ heyeli kɛ yei ni yɔɔ bulɛ kɛ hegbɛi amli. Akɛ jwɛŋmɔ kɛ henilɛ ehã amɛ, ni esa akɛ amɛfee amɛnii kɛhã amɛhe yɛ nyɛmimɛi asu mli.

Kukutoo 2

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ kɛ heyeli fɛɛ ni atsɔɔ mli yɛ Kpojiemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ hegbɛ, ni mɛi ateŋ mlikpamɔ ko kwraa bɛ, tamɔ gbɔmɔtsoŋ su, gbɔmɔtso he womi su, nuu loo yoo he su, wiemɔ, jamɔ, maŋkwramɔŋ loo jwɛŋmɔ kroko, maŋ loo weku ni ajɛ mli, ninɔ, fɔmɔ loo hegbɛ kroko.

Kɛfata he lɛ, akɛ mɔ ko maŋ loo shikpɔŋ nɔ ni ejɛ lɛ maŋkwramɔŋ, kojomɔŋ loo jeŋmaji ateŋ shihilɛ ko nɔ tɔŋŋ tswaaŋ mlikpotomɔ ko kɔŋŋ, kɛji maŋ lɛ yɛ ehe nɔ, akɛto mɔ ko dɛŋ, ebe hegbɛ ni eye ehe nɔ, loo hegbɛ ni eyɔɔ akɛ maŋ ni eye ehe nɔ lɛ yɛ hetoomɔ ko shishi.

Kuku 3

Gbɔmɔ fɛɛ gbɔmɔ yɛ hegbɛ kɛha wala, heyeli kɛ hebuu heha lɛ diɛŋtsɛ ehe.

Kuku 4

Akaŋmɛɛŋ mɔ ko mɔ ko gbɛ ni etsu nii akɛ nyɔŋloo loo nyɔŋteŋtsuulɔ; akpoo nyɔŋyeli kɛ nyɔŋhɔɔmɔ yɛ amɛ gbɛi fɛɛ gbɛi anɔ.

Kuku 5

Akafee mɔ ko mɔ ko nɔnyɛɛ loo yiwalɛ, gbɔmɔtsoŋ nɔ gbeyeishemɔ loo heguɔgbee nifeemɔ loo toigbalamɔ ko kwraa.

Kuku 6

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ akpɛlɔ lɛ nɔ akɛ gbɔmɔ yɛ mla hiɛ he fɛɛ he.

Kuku 7

Mɛi fɛɛ yeɔ egbɔ yɛ mlaa lɛ hiɛ, ni amɛyɛ hegbɛ ni akɛ mlaa lɛ abuɔ amɛ he kɛ egbɔyeli ni mlilɛgbɛko bɛ mli. Mɛi fɛɛ yɛ hegbɛ ni akɛbu amɛ he kɛ egbɔyeli kɛte shi awo mlilɛgbɛko fɛɛ nɔ ni kuɔ Kitã nɛɛ mli kɛ nyɛɛlɔɔ fɛɛ nɔ kɛha nakai mlilɛgbɛ lɛ.

Kuku 8

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ maŋ kojomɔhei ni sa lɛ abu efa kɛba mli ni awaje lɛ kɛtsɔ nitsumɔi ni kuɔ hegbɛi titrii ni maŋ mla wulu lɛ loo mla lɛ eha lɛ lɛ amli.

Kuku 9

Esaaa akɛ amɔɔ mɔ ko kɛyaaa tsuŋ, asaŋ esaaa akɛ awoɔ mɔ ko tsuŋ aloo ashwieɔ lɛ kɛjeɔ emaŋ nɔ yɛ nɔ ko nɔ ko gbɛfaŋ ni nɔ ko nɔ ko bɛ mli.

Kuku 10

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ akɛ kɔɔti ni te shi shiŋŋ ni ehiɛ ejaŋ kɛ gblabalatsɛmɔ ko bɛ mli abu lɛ fɔŋ loo sane yɛ maŋ mli kɛ hejɔlɛ mli, kɛtsɔ ehegbɛi kɛ gbɛnaanii ahe sanebimbɔmɔ mli, kɛ tɔmɔ ko ni aŋɔ fɔ e nɔ lɛ ahe sane yijiemɔ mli.

Kuku 11

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni afee enɔ sane yɛ mla kpamɔ ko he lɛ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ abu lɛ akɛ etɔ̃ko kɛyashi be mli ni anaa akɛ etɔ̃ lɛlɛŋ yɛ mla naa yɛ kojomɔ ni agba afɔ̃ faŋŋ ni afee aha lɛ hegbɛi fɛɛ ni he hiaa kɛha ehebuu lɛ mli.

Akɛŋ tɔmɔ ko ebaaa mɔ ko nɔ yɛ nifeemɔ ko loo nifeemɔ ko ni efeee hewɔ, kɛji akɛ nɔ ni efee loo nɔ ni efeee lɛ jeee tɔmɔ ni yɔɔ mla naa yɛ maŋ mla loo majimaji ateŋ mla naa yɛ be mli ni afee lɛ. Asaŋ abuŋ mɔ ko fɔ ni aheŋ toigbalamɔ ni yɔɔ hiɛdɔɔ fe nɔ ni kɔɔ tɔmɔ lɛ he yɛ be mli ni afee lɛ nɔ awoŋ e-nɔ.

Kuku 12

Mɔ ko mɔ ko bɛ ni abaakɛ ehenɔyeli afee basabasa yɛ e-teemɔŋ saji, eweku, eshĩa loo ewoloŋŋmaa he, loo ni afee basabasa kɛtsɔ e-hizoolɔ kɛ egbɛi he. Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ mla baabu ehe kɛjɛ nɛkɛ basabasafeemɔ loo tutumɔ nɛɛ ahe.

Kuku 13

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ efa gbɛ kɛ he kɛ he ni ehi he fɛɛ he ni eesumɔ yɛ maŋ ko maŋ ko husu lɛ mli.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ eeje maŋ fɛɛ maŋ mli, ni lɛ diɛŋtsɛ emaŋ hu fata he, ni eeku esɛɛ kɛya emaŋ.

Kuku 14

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ eetao kɛ agbɛnɛ ená hejɔlɛhe kɛjɛ yiwalɛ mli yɛ maji krokomɛi amli.

Agbɛnɛ hegbɛ nɛɛ nyɛŋ etsu nii yɛ kojomɔi ni jɛ efɔŋfeemɔi ni kɔɔɔ maŋsɛɛ sane he lɛ ahe, aloo nifeemɔi ni teɔ shi ewoɔ United Nations lɛ yiŋtoi kɛ shishitoo mlai lɛ ahe lɛ amli.

Kuku 15

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ efee maŋnyo.

Mɔ ko mɔ ko bɛ ni abaakpɔ̃ emanɔ̃nyeli yɛ gbɛ ni ejaaa nɔ loo aatsĩ lɛ kɛjɛ hegbɛ ni eyɔɔ akɛ eetsake emanɔ̃nyeli lɛ mli.

Kuku 16

Hii kɛ yei ni edashɛ, ni hegbɛ ko hegbɛ ko etsĩii amɛgbɛ yɛ amɛhenɔ, amɛmaŋ nɔ loo amɛjamɔ hewɔ lɛ, yɛ hegbɛ akɛ amɛgbaa ni amɛfee weku. Amɛyɛ hegbɛ ni kã shi pɛpɛɛpɛ yɛ gbaashihilɛ mli, gbaashihilɛ lɛ be mli kɛ be ni gbaashihilɛ lɛ fiteɔ lɛ hu.

Kɛji gbɔmɛi enyɔ miisumɔ ni amɛkpe lɛ, ja mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ekpɛlɛ nɔ kɛ ehiɛ kɛ etsui fɛɛ mli dani anyɛ akpɛlɛ gbalashihilɛ lɛ nɔ.

Weku ji maŋŋtsɛlɛ kɛ gbɛjianɔtoo ni ja ni yɔɔ maŋ fɛɛ mli, ni ebiɔ ni maŋ kɛ Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ abua he.

Kuku 17

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ebaná nibii lɛ diɛŋtsɛ enɔ aloo ekɛ mɛi krokomɛi abɔfeee.

Akaŋɔɔ mɔ ko mɔ ko ninɔŋ yɛ eŋɔɔ efɔŋtsɔɔ mli.

Kuku 18

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ kɛha jwɛŋmɔ, henilee kɛ jamɔ heheyeli; hegbɛ nɛɛ biɔ ni mɔ ko anyɛ atsake ejamɔ aloo eghemɔkɛnɔyelii, kɛ heyeli ni ebaanyɛ, ekome aloo kɛ ekɛ mɛi krokomɛi yɛ maŋbii ateŋ aloo yɛ teemɔŋ, ni eje kpo etsɔɔ ejamɔ aloo eghemɔkɛnɔyeli yɛ nitsɔɔmɔ, nifeemɔ, jamɔ kɛ nileenɔyeli mli.

Kuku 19

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ kɛha susumɔ kɛ wiemɔ heheyeli; hegbɛ nɛɛ biɔ heheyeli ni akɛhiɛ susumɔi ni mɔ ko efeee he nɔ ko, kɛ sanebimɔ, saneyeli, kɛ saji kɛ susumɔi agbɛɛmɔ kɛtsɔ gbɛ fɛɛ gbɛ nɔ, ni husui etsĩii enaa.

Kuku 20

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ kɛha hejɔlɛ mli kpeemɔ kɛ hekpatamɔ heheyeli.

Akɛnyɛɛɛ mɔ ko nɔ koni ebatsɔ kpee loo kuu ko mli bi.

Kuku 21

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ efeɔ ekome yɛ emaŋ nɔyeli lɛ mli, tɛ̃ɛ loo kɛtsɔ maŋsɛɛdamɔlɔi ni ahala amɛ yɛ heyeli mli lɛ anɔ.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ekɛ mɛi krokomɛi baanyɛ aye maŋ nitsumɔi ahe sɛɛ yɛ e-maŋ lɛ mli gbɛ kpleŋkpleŋ nɔ.

Maŋbii lɛ asuɔmɔ ji nɔ ni nɔyeli lɛ hewalɛ damɔ nɔ; abaajie nɛkɛ suɔmɔ nɛɛ kpo yɛ kɔi kɛ kɔi amli hala-hala ni nɔŋŋ yɔɔ kɛ anɔkwa gbɛ nɔ, ni mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni sa kɛ gbɛfaŋnɔ ni yeɔ egbɔ bɛ gbɛfaŋ kɛji abaahala, ni abaato he gbɛjianɔ yɛ teemɔŋ hala-hala gbɛ nɔ aloo hewalɛ-yeli ko kwraa bɛ mli gbɛ nɔ ni tamɔ nakai nɔŋŋ.

Kuku 22

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ, akɛ maŋbii aŋkro ko lɛ, yɛ hegbɛ kɛha maŋ shihilɛ mli hebuu, ni ebaanyɛ ena, kɛtsɔ maŋ mli mɔdɛŋbɔɔ kɛ jeŋ muu fɛɛ mli ekomefeemɔ nɔ, ni yɛ maŋ fɛɛ maŋ gbɛjianɔtoo kɛ nii anɔ, shika, maŋ shihilɛ kɛ kusum gbɛjianɔtoi ahe hegbɛi ni he hiaa kɛha e-wojiku kɛ e-gbɔmɔtsoŋ hewalɛ kɛ nibii fɛɛ a-shwereemɔ faŋŋ.

Kuku 23

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ etsu nii, ehala nitsumɔ ni esumɔɔ, ená nitsumɔ mli shihilɛ kpakpa kɛ hejɔlɛ, kɛ hebuu kɛjɛ nitsumɔhe gbɛjianɔtoo ni bɛ mli.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ, ni mlikwolɛ ko bɛ mli, enine ashɛ nyɔmɔwoo ni tamɔ nɔ kome nɔ yɛ nitsumɔ ni tamɔ nɔ kome he.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni tsuɔ nii lɛ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ awo lɛ nyɔmɔ ni ja ni sa, ni baaha lɛ kɛ eweku lɛ ahi shi ni kɛ gbɔmɔ gbijɔ sa, ni kɛji ebaahi lɛ, aná gbɛ krokomɛi ni akɛbuɔ gbɔmɛi ahe lɛ akɛfata he.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ efee kɛ agbɛnɛ hu ebɔfo nitsumɔ kuu kɛha ehebuu yɛ enɔŋ nɔ̃ mli.

Kuku 24

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hejɔɔmɔ kɛ hekpojiemɔ he gbɛnaa nii, ni enɛ biɔ ni atsi nitsumɔ be he kɛ gbɛjianɔtoo ni ja, kɛ hejɔɔmɔ be fɛɛ be ni awoɔ he nyɔmɔ.

Kuku 25

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ehi shi yɛ gbɛ ni baaha lɛ kɛ eweku lɛ aná hewalɛ kɛ shihilɛ kpakpa nɔ, ni niyi, atadei, shĩatsɛ̃mɔ kɛ hewalɛnamɔ nitsumɔi kɛ maŋ nɔ yelikɛbuamɔ nitsumɔi ni he hiaa lɛ fata he, kɛ hegbɛ ni kɔɔ shwelɛ he yɛ be ni nitsumɔ bɛ, helatsɛyeli, gbamɔ, egbayeli, gbɔlɛ aloo shihilɛ mli gbɛjianɔtooi krokomɛi ni kpaa yɛ e-nyɛmɔ naa lɛ ahe.

Nyɛmimɔ kɛ gbekɛbiiashi yɛ hegbɛ akɛ anaa amɛ he gbɛjianɔtoo krɛkrɛ kɛ yelikɛbuamɔ. Gbekɛbii fɛɛ, kɛji afo amɛ yɛ kpeemɔ mli loo kpeemɔ sɛɛ jio, baana maŋ mli bulee nɔ kome lɛ nɔŋŋ.

Kuku 26

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ kɛha nikasemɔ. Nikasemɔ baahi shi efɔŋ, titri lɛ yɛ shishijee kɛ heshibaajiiaŋ nikasemɔ mli. Shishijee nikasemɔ baafee nɔ ni anyɛɔ mɔ nɔ akɛ efee. Abaaha ninenaa nitsumɔ kɛ gbɛianɔtoo nitsumɔ nikasemɔ afee nɔ ni yɔɔ kɛha mɛi fɛɛ, kɛ kɔledji nikasemɔ hu afee nɔ ni mɔ fɛɛ mɔ baanyɛ aná bɔ ni fatɔɔ mɔ lɛ gbɛfaŋ hewalɛ kɛ hegbɛ yɔɔ lɛ he.

Akrowa kɔɔ gbɔmɔ su muu lɛ fɛɛ hewalɛwoo he kɛ gbɔmɔ hegbɛi kɛ shishijee heyeli nɔ buu hewalɛwoo he. Ebaaha nilee, tsuishiŋmɛɛ kɛ naanyobɔɔ ahi maŋ fɛɛ maŋ, wekui loo jamɔi ateŋ, ni ebaaya nɔ ewa United Nations nitsumɔi kɛha toiŋjɔlɛ hiɛkamɔ.

Fɔlɔi yɛ hegbɛ klɛŋklɛŋ kɛhã hegbɛ ni amɛyɔɔ akɛ amɛbaahala nɔŋ nikanemɔ kɛ tsɔsemɔ nɔ ni esa akɛ akɛhã amɛbii lɛ.

Kuku 27

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ eji ehiɛ kɛba maŋ lɛ kusum kusum nibii amli faŋŋ, ni ená hewalɛ kɛjɛ ŋaa-nii amli, kɛ agbɛnɛ hu akɛ efee ekome yɛ jeŋ nilee gbɛfaŋ heshwemo kɛ emli hewalɛi lɛ ahe.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ abua ehe kɛha susumɔ kɛ heloonaa nibii ahe sɛɛnamɔi ni jɛ jeŋ nilee, woloŋŋmaa loo amale nitsumɔ fɛɛ nitsumɔ ni lɛ diɛŋtsɛ efee lɛ mli.

Kuku 28

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ebaaná maŋ kɛ jeŋ muu fɛɛ gbɛjianɔtoo ni hegbɛi kɛ heyeli nibii ni awo he gbɛjianɔ yɛ Adafitswaa nɛɛ mli lɛ baanyɛ aje kpo kɛmɔ shi kpam.

Kuku 29

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ sɔ̃ɔ gbɛfaŋ kɛha maŋ lɛ, he ni lɛ pɛ nɔ ebaanyɛ ena hegbɛ kɛtsɔ hewalɛ kɛ mumɔŋ shwelɛ nɔ kɛshɛ gbɔmɔtso kɛ hekɛnɔfɔ fɛɛ mli.

Kɛji mɔ ko miitsu nii kɛmiitso e-gbalamɔi kɛ e-heyeli nii ahe lɛ, mlai pɛ ni akɛto koni akɛbu ni akɛwo mɛi krokomɛi agbalamɔi kɛ amɛheyeli nii ahiɛ nyam, kɛ nɔ ni he hiaa kɛha jeŋba kpakpa, maŋ toiŋjɔlɛ, kɛ maŋbii fɛɛ ahewalɛ kpakpa yɛ demokrasi maŋ mli lɛ pɛ nɔ ebaadamɔ.

Agbɛnɛ kɛ hegbɛ nɛɛ, akɛbaatsu nii yɛ gbɛ koo kɛ gbɛ ko nɔ koni ekpã United Nations yiŋtoo kɛ emlai ahe.

Kuku 30

Ekaaa akɛ nɔ ko nɔ ko ni yɔɔ Shiɛmɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ, aatsɔɔ mli akɛ eha Maŋ ko, kuu ko, aloo mɔ ko hegbɛ akɛ eeetuu ehe awo nifeemɔ ko mli aloo ebaaafee nɔ ko ni yiŋtoo ji ni ekpata hegbɛi kɛ heyeli nɛɛ ateŋ eko fɛɛ eko ni akɛfɔ shi yɛ biɛ nɛɛ hiɛ.
