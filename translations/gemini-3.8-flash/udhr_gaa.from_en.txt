Jeŋ Muu Fɛɛ Adesai Ahegbɛi Akpeemɔ Kɛ Ha Jeŋ Fɛɛ

Hiɛkpamɔ wiemɔi

Akɛnɛ adebɔɔ mli bulɛ kɛ adesawusumɛi fɛɛ a-gbojee kɛ hegbɛi ni anyɛɛɛ aje kɛjɛ amɛdɛŋ lɛ he kpatroo ji heyeli, jalɛsaneyeli kɛ hejɔle fãishiwoo yɛ je lɛ mli hewɔ lɛ,

Akɛnɛ adebɔɔ gbɛnaa nii anɔkwale nɔ ni ayeee kɛ bu ni abuuu lɛ eha nifeemɔi ni yɔɔ nyaŋegbɔ nɔ ni gbaa gbɔmɛi adesai anilɛlɛ naanyɔɔŋ ekɔle ba, kɛ agbɛnɛ hu jeŋ hee ni adesai baaná wiemɔ kɛ hejɔɔmɔ, kɛ gbeyeishemɔ kɛ ohia mli heyeli he gbɛfalɛ lɛ, atsɔɔ mli akɛ eji gbɔmɔ fɛɛ gbɔmɔ henumɔ ni kwɔ fe fɛɛ hewalɛ he shiwoomɔ hewɔ lɛ,

Ehe hia diɛŋtsɛ akɛ mla abu adebɔɔ hegbɛi ahe koni gbɔmɔ akagba lɛ diɛŋtsɛ ehe hewalɛ kɛte shi ewo amanehulu kɛ niniyeli, tamɔ nɔ ni ji e-naagbee gbɛjianɔtoo pɛ pɛ,

Ejaakɛ ehe miihia waa ni awo naanyobɔɔ kɛ hejɔlɛ gbalashihilɛ ni yɔɔ maji ateŋ lɛ hewalɛ,

Akɛnɛ United Nations maŋbii lɛ efee ekome yɛ kpaŋmɔ lɛ mli kɛma amɛhemɔkɛyeli nɔ mi yɛ adesai ahegbɛi titrii lɛ amli, yɛ gbɔmɔ gbeseŋgbɛ nɔnyeli kɛ e-tsɔɔmɔ mli, kɛ yɛ nyumuhi kɛ yei ahegbɛi ni yeɔ egbɔ lɛ mli, ni amɛtswa amɛfai shi akɛ amɛbaaha maŋ mli ŋkɔyeli kɛ shihilɛ kpakpa ahe aba mli yɛ heyeli ni da mli lɛ,

Akrɔ nɔ ni Maŋbii lɛ ewo shi akɛ amɛ kɛ Jeŋmaji Ekomefeemɔ lɛ aaabɔ kɔŋ kɛ baa kɛtsɔ adasa hegbɛi kɛ hefatalɔi ahe hegbɛi lɛ anɔkwale ni kɛ jeŋ muu fɛɛ bulɛ kɛ mlihilɛ awo mli lɛ hewɔ lɛ,

Akɛnɛ hegbɛi kɛ heyeli nɛɛ anɔkwa shishinumɔ ji nɔ ni he hiaa fe fɛɛ kɛha shiwoo nɛɛ mlihenyɛmɔ kɛmɔ shi pɛpɛɛpɛ hewɔ lɛ,

Agbeŋɛ kɛkɛ lɛ,

Kpee Kpeteŋkpele Lɛ

Jajeɔ Jeŋ Muu Fɛɛ Adesai Agbɛnaa He Jaje nɛɛ akɛ nɔ ni gbɔmɛi fɛɛ kɛ maŋbii fɛɛ afee ekome kɛtsu he nii, koni mɔ fɛɛ mɔ kɛ maŋ lɔɔ mli kuu fɛɛ kuu, ni amɛhiɛ kã nɛkɛ Jaje nɛɛ nɔ be fɛɛ be lɛ, abɔ mɔdɛŋ kɛtsɔ nitsɔɔmɔ kɛ hekpaamɔ nɔ kɛwo bulɛ kɛha nɛkɛ hegbɛi kɛ heyeli nɛɛ, kɛ kɛtsɔ gbɛtsɔɔmɔi ni yaa hiɛ nɔ, yɛ maŋ kɛ jeŋ muu fɛɛ mli, kɛha amɛhe kpɛlɛmɔ kɛ amɛnɔyeli ni baa be fɛɛ be kɛ mlihilɛ, yɛ Maŋbii ni ji Gbami mli bii lɛ a-teŋ kɛ maŋbii ni yɔɔ amɛnɔyeli shishi lɛ hu a-teŋ.

Kuku 1

Adesai fɛɛ kɛ heyeli kɛ hegbɛi kɛ gbɛnaanii ni yɔɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ ji nɔ ni afɔɔ amɛ kɛbaa je lɔɔ mli. Amɛyɛ jwɛŋmɔ kɛ henilɛ, ni ehe miihia ni amɛfee amɛnii yɛ nyɛmimɔ mumɔ mli kɛha amɛhe.

Kuku 2

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ kɛ hetsɛlɛ fɛɛ ni atsɔɔ mli yɛ Kpojiemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ he, ni mɛi ateŋ mlikpamɔ ko bɛ, tamɔ weku, hewolonɔ su, nyɛŋmɔ loo nuumɔ, wiemɔ, jamɔ, maŋkwramɔŋ loo jwɛŋmɔ kroko, maŋ loo gbɔmɛi ateŋ jɛmɔ, ninai, fɔmɔ loo hegbɛ kroko fɛɛ.

Kɛfata he lɛ, akɛ mɔ ko maŋloo shikpɔŋ nɔ ni ejɛ lɛ maŋkwramɔ, kojomɔ kɛ jeŋmaji ateŋ nɔyeli he gbɛnaa he mligblamɔ ko efeŋ, kɛji maŋ lɛ yɛ ehedoo, alo eji nɔ ni akwɛɔ nɔ, alo ebe ehedoo, loo hegbɛ ni eyɔɔ akɛ maŋ ni kɛ ehe eye lɛ yɛ gbɛ ko nɔ ni atsi naa.

Kuku 3

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ kɛha wala, heyeli, kɛ gbɔmɔtsoŋ shweshweeshwefeemɔ.

Kuku 4

Akɛ mɔ ko mɔ ko tsɔɔŋ nyɔŋ loo kpaa mli; akɛ nyɔŋyeli kɛ nyɔŋhɔ̃ɔ mli gbɛ̀i fɛɛ baafee nɔ ni atsi naa.

Kuku 5

Akafee mɔ ko mɔ ko nɔnyɛɛ loo nitsumɔ ni yɔɔ nyaŋgbɔ, ni gbɔmɔtso bɛ mli, loo ni kɛ heguɔgbee baa, loo toigbalamɔ ni tamɔ nakai.

Kuku 6

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ akpɛlɔ lɛ nɔ he fɛɛ he akɛ gbɔmɔ yɛ mla hiɛ.

Kuku 7

Mɛi fɛɛ yeɔ egbɔ yɛ mlaa hiɛ, ni amɛyɛ hegbɛ ni mlaa lɛ abu amɛ fɛɛ ahe egbɔ ni mlilaatsɛ̃mɔ ko kwraa bɛ mli. Mɛi fɛɛ yɛ hegbɛ ni abu amɛhe egbɔ kɛte shi amɛwo mlilaatsɛ̃mɔ ko kɛtee shi ewo Kita Nɛɛ, kɛ mlilaatsɛ̃mɔ nɛɛ he hewalɛwoo fɛɛ nɔ.

Kuku 8

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ maŋ kojolɔi ni sa lɛ akpata ehiɛ kɛha nibii ni efeɔ kɛtɔɔ e-hegbɛi titrii ni maŋ mla wolo loo mla kɛha lɛ lɛ nɔ.

Kuku 9

Esaaa akɛ amɔɔ mɔ ko, aloŋ awoɔ lɛ tsuŋ, aloo ashwieɔ lɛ kɛjeɔ emaŋ nɔ efolo kɛkɛ ni ebaaa nɔ ko he.

Kuku 10

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ ni kanyɔɔ nɔ kwraa akɛ akoja esane yɛ hegbɛ kpele kɛ gbɔmɛi fɛɛ ahiɛ kɛtsɔ kojomɔhe ni damɔ enɔteŋ ni efeee gbɛi kɔŋkɔŋ nɔ, yɛ ehegbɛi kɛ nɔ ni kã enɔ ni akpɛɔ mli, kɛ tɔmɔ mli sane fɛɛ sane ni akɛfɔ̃ɔ enɔ lɛ mli.

Kuku 11

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni awo lɛ fɔ nɔ ko ni mla guɔ he lɛ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ abu lɛ akɛ etɔko aahu kɛyashi mla kɛ maŋ saneyeli baatsɔɔ akɛ etɔ kɛtsɔ hegbɛi fɛɛ ni sa ni akɛbaa eha lɛ kɛha ehe buɔ nɔ.

Akaabu mɔ ko mɔ ko akɛ etɔ̃ tɔ̃mɔ ko nɔ ní afeɔ he kojoŋgbɛianɔ fɔbuu, yɛ nifeemɔ ko aloo nifeemɔ ko ní egbe naa hewɔ ní jeee tɔ̃mɔ ko ní feɔ kojoŋgbɛianɔ fɔbuu yɛ maŋ mli mlai aloo maŋsɛɛ mlai anaa yɛ be mli ní afee lɛ mli lɛ hewɔ. Agbɛnɛ hu, akato toigbalamɔ ko ní yɔɔ dɔlɛ fe nɔ ní sa yɛ be mli ní afee tɔ̃mɔ ní afeɔ he kojoŋgbɛianɔ fɔbuu lɛ mli lɛ nɔ aka mɔ ko nɔ.

Kuku 12

Akafee mɔ ko mɔ ko efɔŋ kɛtsɔ ehe saji ahe ni awoɔ dɛŋ, eweku, eshĩa loo ewoloŋŋmaa saji ahe gbɛ, loo etutuaa egbɛ́i kpakpa kɛ ehiɛnyam. Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ mla buɔ ehe kɛjɛɔ tutualɔi kɛ mɛi ni kɛ amɛhe woɔ saji ni tamɔ nɛkɛ mli lɛ ahe.

Kuku 13

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ efa gbɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni eesumɔ ni ehi he fɛɛ he ni eesumɔ yɛ maŋ fɛɛ maŋ husu lɛ mli.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ eeje maŋ fɛɛ maŋ mli, ni lɛ diɛŋtsɛ emaŋ hu fata he, ni eeku esɛɛ kɛya emaŋ.

Yitso 14

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ eetao kɛ agbɛnɛ eena hejɔɔmɔ kɛjɛ nɔnyɛɛ mli yɛ maŋ krokomɛi amli.

Abaanyɛ akɛ hegbɛ nɛɛ atsu nii kɛji akɛ nɔ ni afee lɛ jeee maŋsɛɛ sane loo nɔ ni efee lɛ teɔ shi ewoɔ United Nations oti kɛ shishitoo mlai lɛ.

Kuku 15

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ efee maŋnyɛŋtsɛ loo maŋnyɛ.

Mɔ ko mɔ ko bɛ ni abaakpa ehe gbɛ akɛ abaajie emaŋbiiyeli kɛje edɛŋ gbɛkpamɔ ko kwraa bɛmli, loo ni abaatsĩ enaa kɛjɛ ehegbɛ ni eyɔɔ akɛ ebaanyɛ etsake emaŋbiiyeli lɛ he.

Kuku 16

Hii kɛ yei ni edashɛ, ni hegbɛ ko hegbɛ bɛ ni tsiɔ amɛnaa yɛ hewalɛ, maji ahe gbɛjianɔtoo loo jamɔ hewɔ lɛ, yɛ hegbɛ akɛ amɛbote gbalashihilɛ mli ni amɛfĩ weku shishi. Amɛyɛ hegbɛ kome nɔŋŋ yɛ gbalashihilɛ mli, yɛ gbalashihilɛ lɛ be mli, kɛ be ni agbɛ gbala lɛ mli.

Gblamɔ kɛ gblashihilɛ mli boteemɔ baaya nɔ yɛ mɛi ni kɛ amɛhe baatsɔ gbla mli lɛ ateŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ lakamɔ kɛ suɔmɔ kɛ hejɔlɛ mli pɛ.

We kɛ wekuli ji adebɔɔ mli kɛ hefatalɔi akuu krɔŋkrɔŋ kɛha maŋbii, ni sa akɛ maŋbii kɛ Maŋ lɛ ebu amɛhe.

Kuku 17

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ebaná nináa lɛ diɛŋtsɛ egbɛfaŋ, kɛ agbɛnɛ hu ekɛ mɛi krokomɛi abɔɔ mli.

Atsɔŋ mɔ ko mɔ ko ninɔŋ fɔŋ kɛkɛ ni shishitoomɔ ko bɛ he.

Kuku 18

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ kɛha jwɛŋmɔ, henilee kɛ jamɔ he heyeli; hegbɛ nɛɛ kɔɔ heyeli ni ebaana kɛtsake e-jamɔ loo e-hemɔkɛyeli, kɛ heyeli hu, kɛji ekome loo kɛ mɛi krokomɛi yɛ maŋ mli loo teemɔŋ, ni eje e-jamɔ loo e-hemɔkɛyeli kpo yɛ nitsɔɔmɔ, nifeemɔ, jamɔ kɛ gbiŋjurɔyeli mli.

Kuku 19

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ kɛha susumɔ kɛ wiemɔ he heyeli; hegbɛ nɛɛ biɔ ni aná hegbɛ ni akɛsusu bɔ ni asumɔɔ ni mɔ ko etɔ̃ɔɔ nɔ, kɛ hegbɛ ni akɛtaoɔ, anaa, ni agbɛɔ saji kɛ susumɔi ashwãa kɛtsɔɔ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni akɛgbaa sane nɔ, ni husui komɛi efeŋ nɔ ko kɛha enɛ.

Kuku 20

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ kɛha hejɔlɛ mli kpeemɔ kɛ bɔfeemɔ heyeli.

Abaafee mɔ ko mɔ ko nyɛmɔ koni ebatsɔ kpeemɔku ko mli bi.

Kuku 21

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ efeɔ ekroŋŋ aloo eŋɔɔ gbɛ kɛtsɔ mɛi ni ahala amɛ faŋŋ lɛ anɔ kɛfata emaŋ nɔyeli lɛ he.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ekɛ mɛi krokomɛi anaa gbɛ kome nɔŋŋ kɛya maŋ sɔɔmɔ nitsumɔi amli yɛ emaŋ lɛ mli.

Maŋbii lɛ asuɔmɔnaa nɔ nɔɔ nɔyeli lɛ hewalɛ damɔ; abaajie nɛkɛ suɔmɔnaa nɛɛ kpo yɛ nɔyeli hewoloo kpakpa ni ayaa nɔ daa be kɛ be nɔ mli, ni ebaahi shi kɛtsɔ maŋbii fɛɛ anɔgbayeli kɛ ekomefeemɔ nɔ, ni abaatsɔ teemɔŋ woloo-woomɔ loo woloo-woomɔ gbɛ kroko ni yeɔ emuu ni he yɔɔ faŋŋ nɔ atsu he nii.

Kuku 22

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ, akɛ maŋbii ateŋ mɔ kome, yɛ hegbɛ kɛha nibii ahe buɔmɔ kɛ gbɔmɔtsoŋ hejɔlɛ, ni esa akɛ ena, kɛtsɔ maŋ lolo mli mɔdɛŋbɔɔ kɛ jeŋmaji ateŋ hegbɛjianɔtoo kɛ nɔyaye nɔ, ni yɛ maŋ fɛɛ maŋ hegbɛjianɔtoo kɛ ninai anaa, hegbɛi ni kɔɔ shika kɛ nibii ahe, maŋbii ahe, kɛ blema kusii ahe ni he hiaa kɛha e-hiɛnyam kɛ esusuu kɛ e-gbɔmɔtsoŋ shweremɔ ni he yɔɔ hewalɛ.

Kuku 23

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ etsu nii, ehala nitsumɔ ni esumɔɔ, enyɛ anɔkwale kɛ nibii kpakpai anɔ yɛ nitsumɔ mli, kɛ hegbɛ ni akɛbaabu ehe kɛjɛ nitsumɔ he hegbɛ kpatamɔ mli.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ, ni gbɔmɛi aŋkroŋkro-kwɛmɔ ko bɛ mli, yɛ hegbɛ akɛ anaa nyɔmɔwoo ni tamɔ nɔ ko nɔŋŋ kɛha nitsumɔ ni tamɔ nɔ ko nɔŋŋ.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni tsuɔ nii lɛ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ awoɔ lɛ nyɔmɔ ni ja ni sa, ni baaha lɛ kɛ eweku lɛ ahi shi ni kã he ni sa gbɔmɔ hegbɛ, ni kɛ́ ebaahi lɛ, aná gbɛ krokomɛi ni kɛ gbɔmɛi ahe buɔ akɛtsɔɔ eŋɔɔ efata he.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ eeetoo nitsumɔ kuu loo ebaanyɛ eyafata nitsumɔ kuu he kɛha ehiamɔ nii ahebuu.

Kuku 24

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hejɔɔmɔ kɛ hekpojiemɔ he gbɛnaa nii, kɛ nitsumɔ be ni agbɛi kɛ hejɔɔmɔ be fɛɛ be ni awoɔ he nyɔmɔ hu fata he.

Kuku 25

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ebaaná shihilɛ kpakpa ni sa kɛha lɛ diɛŋtsɛ kɛ eweku lɛ agbɔmɔtseiŋ kɛ hewalɛnamɔ, ni enɛ biɔ niyenii, atadewoo, shĩa, kɛ tsɔfemɔ he nibii, kɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni he hiaa kɛha gbɔmɔ shihilɛ, kɛ hegbɛ ni kɔɔ shwelɛ he kɛji nitsumɔ bɛ, helatsemɔ, gbɔmɔtsoŋ gbɔjɔmɔ, kunayeli, gboklɛntsiŋ, loo shihilɛ mli gbɔjɔmɔ kroko ni kɛ nibii ni fe ehemɔ ba.

Biɛfonyeli kɛ gbekɛbiiayeji sa kɛha hegbɛ krɛkrɛ nɛɛ kɛ yelikɛbuamɔ. Gbekɛbii fɛɛ, kɛji agbɔ amɛ yɛ gbaashihilɛ mli jio, loo gbaashihilɛ sɛɛ jio, baana hebuu kome nɔŋŋ kɛjɛ maŋbii ahe.

Kuku 26

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ kɛha nikasemɔ. Nikasemɔ baahi shi efɔŋ, titri lɛ yɛ shishijee kɛ heshiba nibii lɛ amli. Shishijee nikasemɔ baafee nɔ ni awo mɔ nyɔŋ nɔ. Abaaha nilee kɛ nitsumɔ he nikasemɔ ahi shi kɛha mɛi fɛɛ, ni nikasemɔ ni kɔɔ nɔ kwraa lɛ baafee nɔ ni mɔ fɛɛ mɔ baanyɛ aná yɛ mɔ lɛ nitsumɔ kɛ eŋwɛi hewalɛ naa.

Nilee kaseemɔ baatsɔ gbɛ nɔ etee adesa henumɔ kɛ egbɔmɔtsoŋ kɛ e-jwɛŋmɔ nɔyaa mli kɛyashi naagbee, ni ebaawa adesai ahegbɛi kɛ heyeli krɔŋkrɔŋ nɔ bulɛ mli. Ebaaha nuumɔ, tsuishiŋmɛɛ kɛ naanyobɔɔ ahi maŋtai fɛɛ, gbɔmɛi akui loo jamɔi ateŋ, ni ebaaha United Nations nitsumɔi ni kɔɔ toiŋjɔlɛ hiɛkɛnɔya he lɛ aya hiɛ.

Fɔlɔi yɛ hegbɛ klɛŋklɛŋ kɛhã hegbɛ ni amɛyɔɔ akɛ amɛhalaa tsɔsemɔ nɔɔi ni sa akɛ akɛhã amɛbii lɛ.

Kuku 27

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ ni eyaje kpo kɛba maŋ lɛ kusum nifeemɔi amli, ni ená amaniɛbɔɔ nifeemɔi lɛ ahe miishɛɛ, kɛ agbɛnɛ hu ni ená gbɛfaŋnɔ yɛ jeŋ nilee shishiloo shwɛshweeshwe kɛ enɔgbɔɔi lɛ ahe.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ahe ejeŋba kɛ nibii anɔkɔnyɛlɛ he sɛɛnamɔi ni jɛ jeŋ nilee, woloŋmaŋ, aloo ŋaamɔ nitsumɔ fɛɛ nitsumɔ ni lɛ diɛŋtsɛ efee lɛ mli lɛ ayi aha lɛ.

Kuku 28

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ebaná gbɛjianɔtoo ni yɔɔ maŋ kɛ jeŋ muu fɛɛ mli ni hegbɛi kɛ heyeli nibii ni awo he shi yɛ Adafitswaa nɛɛ mli lɛ baaba mli pɛpɛɛpɛ.

Kuku 29

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ gbɛnaanii yɛ maŋ ni emli pɛ mɔbɔɔgbɛ kɛ gbɔmɔtsoŋ shweremɔ ni yeɔ emuu lɛ baanyɛ aba mli lɛ mli.

Kɛji mɔ ko miitsu ehegbɛi kɛ heyeli lɛ ahe nii lɛ, nɔ pɛ ni baanyɛ atsi enaa ji nɔ ni mla etoo koni akɛbu ni akɛwo mɛi krokomɛi ahegbɛi kɛ amɛheyeli lɛ ahiɛnyam, kɛ agbɛnɛ hu koni anyɛ afee nɔ ni jeŋba kpakpa, maŋ mli toiŋjɔlɛ, kɛ maŋbii fɛɛ ahiɛkɛnɔya kpaa he gbɛ yɛ demokrasi maŋ mli.

Agbeɛnɛ lɛ, hegbɛi kɛ heyeli nɛɛ ehiii ni akɛtsu nii yɛ gbɛ ko kwraa nɔ ni ebaate shi ewo United Nations lɛ yiŋtoi kɛ eshishitoo mli wiemɔi lɛ.

Kuku 30

Nɔ ko nɔ ko bɛ Kpojiemɔ nɛɛ mli ni abaanyɛ ashishi akɛ ehaɔ Maŋ ko, kuu ko aloo mɔ ko hegbɛ akɛ efee nɔ ko aloo etsu nifeemɔ ko ni yɔɔ yiŋtoo akɛ ebaakpata hegbɛi kɛ heyeli kuku nɛɛ amli nɔ ko nɔ ko hiɛ.
