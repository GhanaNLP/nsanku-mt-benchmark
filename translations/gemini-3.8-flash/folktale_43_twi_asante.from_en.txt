Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐDEƐ ƐBƐYƐEƐ A NWANSANA SI ƆKRAMAN SO A, ƆKA NO, KAM! KAM!

Ɔbɔfoɔ bi wɔ hɔ a na ɔwɔ akraman nnan; na wɔfrɛ baako Hwahuam-hwahuam; na wɔfrɛ baako Tafere-tafere; na wɔfrɛ baako Kye-no-nsohwe; na wɔfrɛ baako nso Mene-koraa. Na ɔtaa de ne kraman yi kɔ bɔm. Dabiara a ɔbɛkɔ wuram no, na ɔne wɔn na ɛkɔ bom. Da koro bi ɔkɔɔ wuram na ɔhɔɔ bise dua bi; na aba ahyɛ so ma a asensɛn so akuwakuw sɛɛ. Afei ɔsan kɔɔ fie, na ɔbɔɔ ne yere amanneɛ sɛ, "Bese bi a mahunu no asow aba pii a ɛsɛn so amumɔamumɔ sei." Ne yere kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔte bi, efisɛ wonim sɛ yenni bese baako koraa a yɛbɛwe na yenni bi nso a yɛbɛtɔn." Ɔbɔfoɔ no sii kwan so, nanso ɔgyaa akraman no wɔ fie, na ɔkɔɔ faako a bese no si no. Ɛnna ɔforo kɔɔ so, na ɔtetew bise no bi. Afei ɔtee nnyigyei firi babi se, "Twa! be! kukuru! kukuru! hu! pi!" Sasabonsam bi gyina n'anim. Ɔbisae sɛ, "Hwan na watetew me bise no?" Ɔbɔfo no nso see sɛ, "Meme." Sasabonsam no see sɛ, "Ade a woayɛ yi nti, mereka wo abɔ fam seesei ara." Ɔbɔfo no see sɛ, "Sɛ worebekum me a, ma me mfrɛ me nkraman kan." Ɔno nso see sɛ, "Frɛ wɔn, nanso sɛ wofrɛ wɔn mpɛn mmiɛnsa na wɔamma a, ɛnde mɛkum wo." Ɛnna ɔbɔfo no maa ne nne so:

Hwamhwam, bra behyia me, Tafretafre, bra behyia me, Kyekyerepɔ, bra behyia me, Menemkese, bra behyia me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn ba no akyɛ dodo!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn ba no akyɛ dodo!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn ba no akyɛ dodo!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn ba no akyɛ dodo!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn rekyɛ pii ansa na wɔaba!”

Na nkraman a wɔwɔ fie no tee ne nka brɛoo koraa, na wɔtutuu mmirika kɔɔ kuro no ano. Ɛnna ɔbɔfoɔ no maa ne nne so bio:

Hwahwa-hwahwa, (bra bɛ)hyia me, Taa-taa, (bra bɛ)hyia me, Kyekyere-abɔpɔ, (bra bɛ)hyia me, Mene-mene, (bra bɛ)hyia me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn ba no akyɛ dodo!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn ba no akyɛ dodo!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn ba no akyɛ dodo!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn ba no akyɛ dodo!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn rekyɛ pii ansa na wɔaba!”

Afei akraman no teeɛ; wɔtuu mmirika, prada! prada! Sasabonsam no kaa sɛ, “Frɛ bio, na ɛno ma ne mprɛnsa so no wie.” Ɔbɔfoɔ no maa ne nne so kɛse pa ara:

Hwahwa-hwahwa, (bra bɛ)hyia me, Taa-taa, (bra bɛ)hyia me, Kyekyere-abɔpɔ, (bra bɛ)hyia me, Mene-mene, (bra bɛ)hyia me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn ba no akyɛ dodo!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn ba no akyɛ dodo!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn ba no akyɛ dodo!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn ba no akyɛ dodo!

"Wai, wai, wai, wɔnkyɛ ba!" N'ano nsiieɛ no, na akraman no si n'anim. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Hwamm-hwamm, Sasabonsam ni, hwa no." Na Hwamm-hwamm hwaa no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Tafre-tafre, tafre ne ho nyinaa." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Kyekyere-abɔpɔ, kyekyere no bɔpɔ." Kyekyere-abɔpɔ kyekyeree ne mene mu abɔpɔ; na Sasabonsam no hwee fam wɔ hɔ, kum! Ɛnna Gulp-down faa no kɔe na ɔmenee no. Ɔbɔfoɔ no ne nkraman no boaboaa bisee no ano de kɔɔ fie. Berɛ a ɔbɔfoɔ no sane baeɛ no, ɔbɛnoaa nkatenkwan dɔsɔɔso hyɛɛ asanka ma, na ɔde sii hɔ maa nkraman no; ɔpetee nsuo de sii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn wɔ hɔ kɔdii aduane. Berɛ a ɔdidi wieeɛ no, ɔfirii adi kɔɔ abɔntene so kɔtenaa gyedua ase. Afei ɔbɔfoɔ no akoa, Fly, kɔe kɔhwee aduane no (a wɔde asie ama akraman no), na ɔmaa so de sii dan no atifi. Akraman no firi abɔntene so baeɛ na ɛkɔm de wɔn. Lick-lick ka kyerɛɛ afoforɔ no sɛ, "Moahunu deɛ yɛayɛ ama yɛn wura, nanso kɔsi seesei mpo, ɔmmaa yɛn aduane biara sɛ yɛnni, enti momma yɛnkɔ nkunkum no." Wɔgyee so sɛ, "Wontwa nkontompo, wontwa nkontompo." Lick-lick pue kɔɔ abɔntene so kɔfɛfɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no ho, na ɔne ɔbɔfoɔ no dii agorɔ; na Sniff-sniff nso bae; na ɔhwamhwam ɔbɔfoɔ no ho; na ɔno nso kɔe. Ɛnna ɛnkyɛe koraa na Tie-in-knots bae bɛkyekyeree ne mene mu nso, na Gulp-down nso aba amene no. Afei akraman no san kɔɔ fie hɔ; na wɔrebɛhwɛ pato no mu pɛ, na wɔhunuu nkwan nsonsoɛ wɔ hɔ. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Nwansena, ebia ɔbɔfoɔ no gyaw aduane maa yɛn na woadi?" Nwansena kaa sɛ, "Menhunuu bi baabiara." Hwehwe-hwehwe, berɛ a ɔmaa n’ani so no, ɔhunuu aduane no sɛ ɛgu hɔ (wɔ dan no atifi). Ɔkaa sɛ, "O, agya! yɛakum yɛn wura no kwa, efisɛ nokwarem aduane a ɔde siee ma yɛn no nie, na ɛnyɛ baako, na ɛnyɛ mmienu nso, Ɛnwansena na ɔfaa kɔde sii hɔ." Ɛnwansena no dwanee firii hɔ kɔe. Ɛno nti na sɛ ɔkraman te hɔ, na nwansena si ne so a, ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkyere no no.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
