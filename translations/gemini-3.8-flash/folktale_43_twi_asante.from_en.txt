Yɛnni aseɛ koraa, yɛnni aseɛ koraa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokorɛ)

NEƐMA A ƐYƐE A ƐMAA SƐ NWANSANA TENAA KRAMAN SO A, ƆKA NO, KAM! KAM!

Ɔbɔfoɔ bi wɔ hɔ a na ɔwɔ akraman nnan; na wɔfrɛ baako Hwamm-hwamm; na wɔfrɛ baako Tafi-tafi; na wɔfrɛ baako Kyekyere-mpɔkɔ; na wɔfrɛ baako nso Mene-koraa. Na ɔne ne kraman yi taa kɔ ahayɔ. Dabiara a ɔbɛkɔ kwaeɛ mu no, ɔne wɔn na ɛkɔ bom. Da koro bi ɔkɔɔ kwaeɛ mu na ɔkɔhunuu bɛseɛ dua bi; na ɛaso aba bebree sɛntɛn so sei. Ɛnna ɔsanee kɔɔ fie, na ɔkɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ ne yere sɛ, "Bɛseɛ dua bi a mahunu no, ɛaso aba bebree sɛntɛn so sei." Ne yere kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔtete bi, efisɛ wonim sɛ yɛnni bɛseɛ baako mpo a yɛbɛwe na yɛnni bi nso a yɛbɛtɔn." Ɔbɔfoɔ no sii kwan so, nanso ɔgyaa akraman no wɔ fie, na ɔkɔɔ faako a bɛseɛ dua no si no. Ɛnna ɔforoo dua no, na ɔtetee bɛseɛ no bi. Afei ɔtee nnyegyeeɛ bi firii nkyɛn kɔɔ sɛ, "Twa! be! kukuru! kukuru! hu! pi!" Sasabonsam bi gyinaa n'anim. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Hwan na watete me bɛseɛ yi?" Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Meme." Sasabonsam no kaa sɛ, "Mɛku wo seesei ara esiane adeɛ yi a woayɛ yi nti." Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ worebɛku me a, ma me mfrɛ me kraman ansa." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Frɛ wɔn, nanso sɛ wofrɛ wɔn mprɛnsa na wɔamma a, ɛnde mɛku wo." Ɛnna ɔbɔfoɔ no maa ne nne so :

"Hwahwahwa, bɛto me, Taa-taa, bɛto me, Bobɔ-pɔ, bɛto me, Mennem-mennem, bɛto me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ ba paa!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ ba paa!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ ba paa!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ ba paa!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn rekyɛ dodo sɛ wɔbɛba!”

Na nkraman a wɔwɔ fie no tee ne nka brɛoo koraa, na wɔtuu mmirika kɔɔ kuro no ano. Ɛnna ɔbɔfoɔ no maa ne nne so bio:

Hwamm-hwamm, bɛhyia me, Taaferɛ-taaferɛ, bɛhyia me, Pɔw-bɔ-pɔw, bɛhyia me, Menedeɛ, bɛhyia me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ ba paa!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ ba paa!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ ba paa!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ ba paa!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn rekyɛ dodo sɛ wɔbɛba!”

Afei nkraman no ate; wotuu mmirika, prada! prada! Sasabonsam no kaa sɛ, “Frɛ bio, na ɛno ma ne mprɛnsa so no wie pɛyɛ.” Ɔbɔfoɔ no maa ne nne so kɛse pa ara:

Hwamm-hwamm, bɛhyia me, Taaferɛ-taaferɛ, bɛhyia me, Pɔw-bɔ-pɔw, bɛhyia me, Menedeɛ, bɛhyia me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ ba paa!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ ba paa!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ ba paa!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔakyɛ ba paa!

“Wai, wai, wai, wɔnkyɛ koraa!” N’ano nnya ntoo fam koraa na nkraman no agyina n’anim. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Hwahwa-hwahwa, Sasabonsam ni, hwahwa no.” Na Hwahwa-hwahwa hwahwaa no. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Tafatana, tafatana ne ho nyinaa.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Pɔ-mprɔpɔ, bɔ no pɔ.” Pɔ-mprɔpɔ bɔɔ ne mene pɔ; na Sasabonsam no hwee fam hɔ, kum! Ɛnna Mene-amene faa no menee no korɔgyen. Ɔbɔfoɔ no ne nkraman no boaboaa bise no ano de baa fie. Berɛ a ɔbɔfoɔ no baeɛ no, ɔbɛnoo nkatenkwan tasa ma, de sii hɔ maa nkraman no; ɔpetee nsuo guu hɔ de sii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn wɔ hɔ kɔdii aduane. Berɛ a ɔdii aduane wieeɛ no, ɔfiri kɔɔ abɔnten kɔtenaa gyedua ase. Afei ɔbɔfoɔ no nkoa, Huruo, kɔhunuu aduane no (a wɔde asie ama nkraman no), na ɔmaa so de kɔsii pampram no so. Nkraman no firi abɔnten bae na kɔm de wɔn. Tafatana ka kyerɛɛ afoforɔ no sɛ, “Moahunu deɛ yɛayɛ ama yɛn wura, nanso bɛsi seesei koraa, ɔmmaa yɛn aduane biara anyɛ mpo sɛ yɛbɛdi, enti momma yɛnkɔkunkum no.” Wɔbuae sɛ, “Wonni atoro, wonni atoro.” Tafatana firi kɔɔ abɔnten kɔtafataa ɔbɔfoɔ no ho, na ɔne ɔbɔfoɔ no goroe; na Hwahwa-hwahwa nso bae; na ɔhwahwaa ɔbɔfoɔ no ho; na ɔno nso kɔe. Afei berɛ pɔtee a Pɔ-mprɔpɔ baa bɛbɔɔ ne mene pɔ pɛ, na Mene-amene nso aba abɛmene no korɔgyen. Afei nkraman no san kɔɔ fie; wɔrebɛhwɛ pato no mu pɛ, na wɔhunuu nkwan nsɔnsɔnsoɔ wɔ hɔ. Wɔkaa sɛ, “Huruo, ebia na ɔbɔfoɔ no agya aduane ama yɛn na woadi?” Huruo kaa sɛ, “Menhunuiɛ wɔ baabiara.” Hwahwa-hwahwa, berɛ a ɔmaa n’ani so no, ɔhunuu aduane no a wɔde asi hɔ (wɔ pampram no so). Ɔkaa sɛ, “O, agya! yɛakum yɛn wura kwa, efirisɛ ampa ara aduane a ɔde sii hɔ maa yɛn no nie, na ɛnyɛ baako, na ɛnyɛ mmienu, ɛyɛ Huruo na ɔfaa de kɔsii hɔ.” Huruo dwanee firi hɔ kɔe. Ɛno nti na, sɛ kraman te hɔ, na huruo si ne so a, ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkyere no no.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
