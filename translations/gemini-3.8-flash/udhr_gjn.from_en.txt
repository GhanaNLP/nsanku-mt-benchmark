Kaso Kebia Bumo Nyaŋyidɛ to kede kumo jimbɔ

Mbɔntolbito

Gela nnyɛ anyi bee nyi ebi k'asa kekleke kpaa b'akore n'e-lɛ ba n'e-wura nna b'aleko nɛ a maa tula nna dɔ nɛ e-kɛ k'aso n'enyanashee, chereyɛ nɛ ashe kɛba duniya to,

Kekele nɛ b’ɛ kpa benyishi nɛ n nyi bumo nensa maŋ gbanan to malɛ su nyi bɔrɛ anyansapo gbagbarawulo mɔ nɛ ba lara ebumo kamangaso, nɛ kanyishe bɔrɛ kumpɔ nɛ banyishi bɛ la ba na fɔrɔkoma so bɔtɔ to nɛ be biya to, nɛ fɔrɔkoma so kpa n’e-kufu to nɛ e-kpe to alara nɛ b’a lara bunumo nenso kanyishi nensa kumpɔ kamayɛ to bɔkɔtɛ.

Nɛ e be fɛ kɛnyɛ ne bee lii nɛ anyi kuli maŋ ba lii nɛ anyi bɔlɛ asheŋ nɛ anyi la a lara nɛ amutɔ kɔŋkɔŋ kusu to, mbra bee bɔra nɛ e kɔ anyi gɛnso nɛ e daŋ eba to anyi gbangbanto.

Gbo nɛ e kɔ kpeŋ nɛ a kpa nso kɛkpalɛ lara kpa sa nɛ e tɔŋ e-bɛɛ-lɛ lara nɛ ama kpeŋ kɛkpalɛ a-nyin-gbe to,

Amɔkpaa nɛ United Nations nyebia be bɛɛra mɔ Charter mɔtɔ mɔ a kɔɔ lara bɛ kumo nyanaso be baŋgban mɔtɔ kaseebi be fɛrɛ mɔtɔ, kaseebi be dita nɛ kumo be dɛna mɔtɔ, kaseebi nyoorawolpo nɛ kaseebi nyentsɛ be nyanaso be fɛrɛ mɔtɔ, bɛ lara bɛ sɔɔ bɛ kpa bɛ yiri bɔna be dɔmbia be kasɔbɔ nɛ kawol be nyiŋga mɔtɔ fɛrɛ be dɛna mɔ be kumpawurɛ mɔtɔ,

Ema anyi a-sa nyoro awolɛ nna ma-kparaba United Nations nɛ, a mɔ nyi kɛ bumo bɛ kpa nyi ma-la nɛ kumu ba kpa gbonan so nɛ anyi be ashey ashey nɛ anyi be kɔnkɔn bunyɔ so,

Ntɔso bɔyaŋ kpeŋkpele nɛ ashibi-kamatɔ bee n’kpaa m-bɔna lɛɛ maŋ kpa k’deŋe k’bɔra k’kɔsɔ b’shuŋ k’fuluŋ nɛɛ nɛ.

Ntono, to,

Kikpɛɛ Kiti-kiti

E titi ebia be Asɔrebia be Kuntulo Koko nɛ e la Kasiyɛ be Kawol nɛ e la nnyɛ e kpa nɛ anyi be anyɛ nini kpekpe nɛ nche nini kpekpe na shili ebia be kumo, nɛ e lara nɛ anyɛ nini kpekpe nɛ anigbi be lile nini kpekpe, nɛ ba a choro Kasiyɛ be Kawol nɛ ebia be kumo n dɔ nɛ kumo mɔ be e kpa nɛ anyi bɔla, nɛ ba a wula nɛ ba a kɔle kumo n dɔ mɔ ba a nya n kani dumo be kasiyɛ nɛ kigbanyɛ nɛ mɔ n dɔ, nɛ ba a ba nɛ asɔrebia be ale nɛ asɔrebia gbagbara be kumo n dɔ, nɛ ba a nya n la dumo be kasiyɛ nɛ kigbanyɛ n dɔ kumo n bo n la kawol kpekpe nɛ anyi be anyɛ kpekpe be kumo n dɔ, nɛ ebia e bo asɔrebia be Anyɛ Kuntulo be lile n dɔ nɛ nche be lile nɛ e bo ba be kumu n dɔ.

Kigbeseŋ 1

Minyankpare pɛyɛ ba lii ba daa faalɛ bee ba ji ashe bee jigni nɛ bashepa kashashento. Ba wura nini bee efeɛnto, nɛ ba kɔ ba lala bashe kashashento nɛ m'ba kile minyankpare n'ba kpena.

Gingili 2

Mo-ama kɛrɛ de bumburi kɛrɛ nɛ b’a sa nɛ kumo lara bumburibee nɛ na bunɔ, amɔ fɛnsɛ ba ma ta bumburi duma nɛ giligili, kumo bee fɛɛ enyishi, fufol, kashinti nɛ eche, lankoo, e-gbiŋbo nɛ kumo lara amayanshe, amayanshe kɛrɛ duma, lere bee nɛ kaana bunɔ, agyibi, ebiwule bee nɛ bumburi bee duma.

Mba so nɛ, b’a bɛ kɛ fali fali ma nɛ eba kpa nɛ kawol to, kiti kpa to, bee dunya kpa to nɛ kuwɔl bee kuso nɛ ebi la kulu to na e kpa, koo ku kɔ baari, koo ku teŋ la a ji ku to, koo ku maŋ nyi ku to bee ku la nɛ laalɛ fali kpa kɔso.

Asowɔ 3

Kumu-kumu bee mɔ nya kusowule anaŋ ngowu, fanŋgama naŋ baasa kaanyɛ.

Kowashena 4

Baa maa na nyan kufuli bee nyankuu gba; nyan kufuli nɛ nyankuu chɛrɛŋ yaa gba tiŋe bee gboŋgboŋ kə kpa saŋ kpaa fɛɛfɛɛ kpoŋpoŋ.

Atekil 5

Mba pɔ mɛ pɛlɛ bɛɛ dɔŋɛ gbangban nɛ ashi mɔ, anaso kowulɛ bɛɛ kuso nɛ a tɔgɛ bɛɛ a bɔrba nyinipo jiri pɔ mɛ.

Kafalɛ 6

Esa kɛkɛ wɔ gbenka nɛ bɔɔ kɔ dumu kuso nɛ e ba dɛ e kpar e yɛ esa mpa kaseɛ ere a lara a so.

Atekere 7

Bɛsan-bɛsan gɛba bɛla baa kiti kashe nɛ, bɛ buko bo kishi nɛ bɛ bɔ bɛ bee kiti nɛ so kishi nɛ bee kiti nɛ so mbo bɛ kpa. Bɛsan-bɛsan bee kiti nɛ so kishi nɛ bɛ bɔ bɛ bee kiti nɛ so bɛ buko bo gbagba nɛ kiti gbagba nɛ so, nɛ bɛ buko bo gbagba nɛ kiti gbagba nɛ so mbo bɛ bee bɛkpa kashe.

Gbongbonto 8

Kagbɛŋ kpaasa-kpaasa be asheŋ nna nɛ kulo kama wora kusɔ nɛ kumo lara m baa kpa kiyil kɔtɔ nɛ kumo kraa kulo na be aleshilwura-ana be kakpa nɛ baa lara asheŋ nɛ a maŋ bɔra alɛ, nɛ a mɔrba kushenshu nɛ mbra nɛ bumu kishiŋ-kishiŋ be mbra nɛ e nyeshe mo na.

Leta 9

Bana bee nuŋ kusuŋ mo, kpeŋ mo, baa daŋ mo be eba kpaa so.

Leta 10

Bumɔ kɔkɔ debi pɛɛ wura dɔ kɛtɛtɛ nyanasa nɛ jito nɛ ku kpeŋ dɔ nɛ kashilɛ kpa so, nɛ bɛ bɔlɔ kashilɛ aso nɛ kumu ba kpa mbia nɛ kumu mɔ ba kpe nɛ bɛ fena mɔ asere nɛ ku du bɛlɛɛ so.

Atekol 11

Kenyiti soŋsoŋ na ba mɔ a lara bɔyila ere so n de kanyiti na b'a la bumo kaŋkare fo n ya wu kikɛ so na ba nya ale a la nna n sa kitiya na a wule asheeyi anyi nɛ kitiya pɛɛ kusuŋ nɛ, na ku sa mo asheyi kamasa nɛ ku dɔra bo n sa mo a kani mo mbɔrɛ.

Bɛ kpaan kuŋ pii lara nyulso baye pii bun nɛ e bee a ma kute baasa baye ma eŋ, nɛ a m'ba e pii bun nɛ e lara nyulso, nɛ e tɔŋ manaa nɛ gbɛɛ tɔŋ lara nyulso bee manaa kɛɛ eŋ, saŋa nɛ e bee wula a tɔŋ manaa. Bɛ ma kpaan kuŋ pii kpaŋkpaŋ lara fɔŋ nɛ e daa nɔɔ ma kpaŋkpaŋ lara nɛ a daa kute eŋ saŋa nɛ e tɔŋ lara nyulso baye ma.

Ngben 12

Baa be pɔrɛ nnyɛ fo nyinti kananan, fo mɔrɛ-ana, fo lere bee fo kagbanaŋŋi be kashen to bee baa tɔ fo be kɛlɛkɛlɛ bee fo be kagbanaŋŋi to kpaŋkpaŋ. Banto kɛkɛ wɔ nna be kigbenɛ be kanyishi nna kiti to be nnyɛ be kumo kashen bee kpaŋkpaŋ be mbia to.

Giligili 13

Asha pɛyɛ kɛ kiyii nɛ kɔso kɛbaa nɛ kɛyini kikɛ nɛ kigbɛŋbɛŋ pɛyɛ kumo nɛrɛto.

Esa kama wɔ mbite nɛ ba lii kawol kama to, egbana nɛ ere kawol, nɛ eba leŋ kuli e-kawol to.

Atikili 14

Kusoŋsoŋ bia pɛi pɛi de kpaa pɛi nɛ o kɔle o laŋ e kide kusoŋsoŋ mɔ kpe nɛ o laŋ lara nyaŋsa nɛ be kumo tɔ.

Kigbanyɛ nna maŋ ba nyɛ kitiŋ nɛ a pɛ kɛlɛ kuso nɛ ku kɔ na kumo nɛ ku maŋ gbaŋ m-ba pɔlitikis nɛ bee kumu nɛ ku maŋ lii m-ba United Nations nɛ bumo akpanasa nɛ bumo awula na nunu.

Kasɛɛti 15

Kanyi kama bɔra ebise nɛ e na la kumu fɔŋ kɔŋkɔ.

Koko mɔ maŋ yaa kii fɔrɛ fɔrɛ bɛɛ fɛɛ nɛ nyiti mo kalii loŋ bɛɛ nyiti mo ji fɛɛ mo mɔ lafa nɛ mo kɛrɛ mo kalii nɛ.

Atekul 16

Anyeŋ ne eche nsi n de mfe kuso, n na ma gba kuso n de eburo, kaye anaaso kanyi kuso, de ashitewoto be kuso n kpe efe nko koto ne n la kpe kuwo be lanto. Ba de ashitewoto be kuso n so koto be nuntokpaŋ, koto be kanyipoto ne n de n ba fɛɛ koto be kanyipoto.

Ebɔ k'ebaa nɛ enyɛ mɔ b'ayir b'eyur nɛ a gba nyaŋsɛ ba nɛ bee kɔlɛ ba kpa abɔ mɔ k'ebɔ e nya bee kpe n'ebɔ mɔ nɛ.

Awula nna alege ashiya kanyi nɛ e dumo na, mɔ e tinti nɛ alege ashiya nɛ kawol nyaŋasa bee lara nɛ e kɔ.

Kanan 17

Enyini be bumo nɛ fɛ n kɔ kigbɛɛ kpa fɔrɔ nɛ n kishi bee n kɔ lara nɛ biya.

Mo bɛlɛ ma ba nya kutɔ ba da fa mo bee kpa mo tɔ ebi fanɛ bumu ya nya sa bɔ.

Lɛbili 18

Kumo kumo bee de kelaŋ to so la kumo bee de enyingi, la kumo bee de ebu to so fantaŋ to la nyisoso to so; kelaŋ nyo be la kelaŋ kumo bee de fantaŋ to kumo tiri kumo nyisoso be kumo bee de enyingi, bee be fantaŋ, kumo gbaŋ bee be kumo kpenkpen bee be enyi to, kumo kumu to bee be kumo kpeto to, kumo bee tiri kumo nyisoso be kumo bee de enyingi be kasoso to, be kaputu to, be kasuraŋ to bee be kumo kitiŋ to.

Kagbɛɛ 19

Kumo kumo bee wora kaso lere nɛ nchɛnche nɛ mawa n'aso; lere kumo bee kpe kaso nɛ be fena mawa ma bo kumo bɔso nɛ aso ma tula, nɛ be fena, b'e nyo nɛ b'e nɛɛ nchɛnche nɛ nkoŋko n'eborɛ kalɛ pɛɛ bee ta ba nɛ amani ma lara gbere.

Kashɛ 20

Kusoana kumo bee de sule to na ba lara ba be kafo to na fo bee de kumo a naa de alafia be kpeena kusoana bee be aso to.

Kuso pɛyɛ ma kuli ba ma da bɔkɔ ereŋ nɛ ba ta kumo be kaba to.

Wɔlɛ 21

Koso-koso bee do kile mɔ lara mo asaŋ kuwɔto kute mɔ to, mo kumo to anee ba mɔ bee lara ba luluto keta-to ba pɛɛra to.

Kumuasa kumuasa bee keta to na bumo ba tii bee lara asa sa gbaŋgbam a wo bumo kaŋkaŋ to.

Asheebi kumo n'bɔso e yili a lara asheebi kakyerɛ mɔ kumo a wɔ kinyi; kumo n'bɔso mɔ e lara b'nyini kashintiri kiba ebo e bɔso bumo e bɔso kakyerɛ nɛ, kumo e yili nɛ asheebi fɛɛ na kanyɛ k'yiri k'yiri, na b'na bɔso kakyerɛ mɔ n'faase kumo a tili nɛ a sa asheebi e bɔso kakyerɛ nɛ kanyɛ fɛɛ.

Kaye 22

Kusobolobi ketee, kashinena neng kuwule keeshe kaman-na to, be kalenso neng e ba n nya kashikama ketolto neng kuwule e dunge bo neng e nya kumu n gba n do ebo kashinena kekalenso neng mboso to, n gba n do ebolpo be kubonishi neng eba be kashinena be dunge-dunge be kete to, n gba n koso ebolpo be kafonshu neng eba be ketonbipoto be kalenso to, neng kuwule e yele mboso to, neng kuwule e ba n nya aloriti, asikama neng akalenso be kadunto to be kalenso be kumu neng kuwule e be burowi kase kumu e gba n nya ebo neng e ba n nya ketowuto neng e be ketoto be kaman-na be kanyapoto be kubalepo.

Edɔmbɛ 23

Enyimbii kɛkɛ wɔ kashishe nɛ e lii n suŋ kushuŋ, nɛ e lara kushuŋ nɛ e lara nna, n suŋ kushuŋ nɛ ku dɔ nɛ ku bɛɛ de nteeso, nɛ n nya kidilge kufuŋ kashoŋso to.

Kumu saŋkpaŋ bee kusole kama b'eŋ, bumo pɛyɛ bumo bɛɛ kumu kpa nso kumo n'iŋ kumu kpakpa nɛ kumo nyaŋsa kpakpa.

Kumu so nna kuso nyanɛ bee nya kpaaso nyanɛ gbenya nɛ e bee sa nɛ e bee fo nɛ e bee kpaaso be chere nɛ be nna kumo sa bumo bupowu nɛ bee fo kumu nɛ ku bee baa kpa kuso, na kumo bee kpaaso, ba bee sa nɛ be bee nya ebelto kpaaso be chere nɛ bee kpaaso.

Kusowule bumo fɛrɛ ku nyi bumo nɛ mbara nɛ bumo e kpa nɛ bumo e ji adwuma agbaaso nɛ bumo e pɛ bumo nnyansapo so.

Atikil 24

Kusɔ kpaasa nɛ pɔrɔwulɛ dɔ bito kumo nɛ ekuri tɔ nɛ kumo be chɛ, kumo dɔ nɛ kumo be gbanŋ be lara nɛ kɔle nɛ kumo lara bee lila nɛ kɛyoo be ayi bee yaa nɛ fula nɛ asheyii be kakyerɛ nɛ bama lara lila.

Kashɛɛ 25

Kenyiti sa kumo baa de ketaaso nɛ e sa fɔfɔi nɛ ashi-kutu nɛ e n'e kumu nɛ e lafulɛ kumo nɛ e sa kumu, kumo nɛ liri kumu, kutu, nɛ eduruto, nɛ dɔkuta lari nɛ sooshal sevisis nɛ e baa tiri, nɛ e sa ketaaso nɛ kutu-kanɛ nɛ kumo bee nɛ baa tiri edwuma, kɔnɔ, alafia-kanɛ, kukpala-kanɛ, nimbiri-kutu, bee ashi-kumo nɛ e moni nɛ kumo lara e baa de ketaaso nɛ e kumo kumo.

Enyɛ-ana nɛ kumufuli-ana bɔkɔ kufu nɛ kumuŋyɛ kɛsaso. Bimbiesebi kɛkɛ, banɛ ba lii ba nɛ kashɛ kpo mɔ nɛ banɛ ba ma lii ba nɛ kashɛ kpo mɔ, bɛɛ na kumuŋyɛ nɛ kakumo kɔnkɔ kɛkoŋkoŋ.

Asɔlɛ 26

Kagbana ku nya dusi nẹ ba kẹẹ bɔɔlɔsukuru. Bɔɔlɔsukuru bee ya m-fufu, ketɛketɛ pɩɩla mbu nẹ e pɛ kpa nya bɔsukuru kpa nya kikɛkɛ la. Pɩɩla bɔsukuru kpa bee ya pɩrɩpɩrɩ nẹ e tii nti. Adegresukuru kpa nẹ afuli bɔsukuru kpa bee bɔlɛ sa kumo nẹ e wola nya, nẹ bɔsukuru bɔti kpa bee bɔlɛ pɛrɛpɛrɛ sa mbawura kikɛ mbu nẹ ba chɛrɛ la to.

Sukuru wɛkɛ mɔ kpe nɛ e bɔ kɛlaŋ nɛ k'fumu bɔ bimbim nɛ e kpenyɛ bɔ kɔɔle nɛ ashe kishi nɛ aluntua bɔ ashipo bɔ nankpa pɛɛ. E kpe nɛ e kpenyɛ aso bɔ k'fumu, kɔmɔtɔ nɛ kɛyumu nankpa pɛɛ bɔ ashipo, kɛlaŋ bɔ nɛ alɔŋ bɔ ashipo, nɛ e bɔ United Nations bɔ gbagba nɛ k'fulo mɔ nankpa lara.

Awura nɛ achɛ bɔ kaworɛ nɛ kigile kishi nɛ bɛ kishi e-kpar bɛ biya na.

Kanyee 27

Kumo gba kpe mɔrɔ kasasanto nɛ kumo bɛɛ la abɔrɔ fɔfɔ nyi e-kumo kawol nɛ kumo le kasasanto to, nɛ kumo nyi amena-nɛ-kumu-kpa to, nɛ kumo mɔrɔ kpa nya kɔntɔŋ nɛ kumo kpa ba adɛkpa nɛ sayensi kɔmɔ mɔ.

Kuso kama kpa toso nɛ ba la kii e-nyisi nɛ e-kuntuntun to bɔr-ganshe nɛ k'furi k'nyɛ abanshe k'nyinti, k'wootire k'nyinti bee amale k'furi k'nyinti to nɛ e kɔ kishi nɛ e kii k'mɔ nyuŋ.

Ashɛ 28

Kagbanyin kɔkɔlɛ bee nyi kabolgbewura nɛ kawol kumo a dɛɛ awol a kpa nsa be kumu nɛ m-pɛɛ a mɛ pɔr nɛ kasɛbi bee nna bɛŋa nɛ a wɔ Kasɛwura a gbaŋ dɛɛ a-kpa bee pɛ bɛɛ nya n-ti kumu kama.

Kanyɛ 29

Baaŋasa bee kuwɔlɛ e sa anyi kpeŋ so, amo koŋkpaŋ to nɛ baŋ tuutuu nɛ aniyi nini be kawula be kumuŋ be nyiŋso e yɔ.

E bɔkɔ ebi mɔ be ere nɛ e laawɔ a wola to, kuso kemaa bɛ yina ere-tɔ pɛi nɛ kashia be kowurama pɛ nɛ n ya wola, n pɔsɔ mbɔɔ kumo n ya wola fɛɛ e n kɔ mbɔɔ be ere nɛ bɛ laawɔ to, n gba kashia be chɛrɛ n gba koso pɛi nɛ kumo bɔkɔ bɛnyɛ fɛɛ e kɔ kumo n de n de fɛɛ laabɔ, anyi be nyeshe nɛ bɛkpa be ashibi be fula nɛ ashu e-tin to be kowura to.

Koto nɛ ashorɛ mɔ ma ta ba lara bɔyɛ nɛ ɛ gya United Nations kumo ashe nɛ kumo agbama mɔ mɔ.

Kagbana 30

Fɛyɛ nɛ kigbelɛ pɛmpɛ eba kii na, m bɛ bɛɛ fɛɛ maŋ, lonto mɔ nchu kumo bee kii nɛ e biishi kpa nɛ be lara kanyɔ-tɔ bee be kishi kumo nɛ ku bo nɛ e bishi pɛmpɛ nɛ kumo lara kanyɔ-tɔ to ku bɛ m bee nyiŋ fɛyɛ be lara kanyɔ-tɔ nɛ kumo bo nso na.
