Enyinɛ nankasa nanshe fɛrɛ kaseeti

Kafolpo nɛ

Eba nɛ anii dɔŋkpa nɛ beche kpeŋkpele nɛ kenyeso nɛ kpaasaŋ bo anyi nyaŋsa fɛrɛ kama so bee sa kumu nɛ kɛyɛ, kanyishiŋ nɛ ashiyedo be kasɔ so,

Eŋe ebɔle nɛ b'ma nyi eba nɛ be dɔŋi anyinibo bee gyo gbansha bee mɔ ne ba lara kpakpa nɛ e mɔ kitiwu nɛ e dɔŋi anyinibo be lulur, eŋe kikɛ e kishi be kanyinibo bɔ kpa nɛ anyinibo b'a nya kumo be kawanto nɛ b'a tɔgɛ be nɛɛ eŋe b'a lara kpakpa nɛ be dɔŋi nɛ be ba nya be kumo be kawanto k'woŋe bee buurɛ bee kanyishi eba kpa nɛ e ba nya be lulur k'nyin be kanyinibo.

Ene anyi be gbagbara bee kpa nɛ achɛ, mbɛ nyini bama ma sa anyi be kawol nɛ kashinangwuto ma ya pɛnɛ kiti, sa nɛ anyi be kiti be kanyin bee kuula bunyɔ ma be jishilɛ nɛ baŋ ba kpa mboto k'ala be ashe to,

Né kɛ e yula nɛ a sɔɔ ebiiti nɛ e lara bɛnyɛ-banyɛ awɔrɛ bee be mbeewu be ashe nɛ e yile,

Nde United Nations anyi mɛ gbansha lara e-kaŋ nna Charter to nɛ bumu kishi bumo laada nɛ anyi bumo gboŋgboŋ laada to, nɛ anyi gbegbeng to nɛ dɔmɛ so, nɛ e-kaŋ anyi bumo nɛ ebɛnyɛ nɛ ebche bumo gboŋgboŋ laada to, nɛ bumo lara kishi bumo shɛ anyi-chɛ mɛ bɔyɛ nɛ ashe kishi ashe naŋso to nɛ kigbeng to,

Ntɛ maŋ-nɛ-maŋ nna bee lɛ bama nyɔkpo nɛ UN bee baŋ bee ta kpaŋkpaŋ nɛ b’a titi b’a tii adasa ashen-nɛ-ashen tɛgɛ nɛ bama kashikpa-kashikpa kigbeshi.

Ebu nna kɛlɛ aŋu lere mɔ ku kanyi mɔ be kakpa kede nɛ a wɔ be mbara mɔ be pɛrɛ nɛ e di kɛsɛ kishi kpa nɛ a ba nya be lere n kulo nna,

Nfanɛ nɛ,

Asenbili kumo mɛŋɛ

E bee tola anyi ere nɛ ba teere Kɛyirɛ Naana Kenalɛ Asoso Akyipɛ Bɔlɔ ere nɛ e la nɛɛ kikɛ nɛ ashe kikɛ konyen kanyaso, nɛ nɛɛ kikɛ nɛ kashinteŋ ere kikɛ kaba kaman gba, e nyaŋ e na kɛyirɛ ere bee kiyil asoso nɛ ku ma yaasa, nɛ ba nya n chuwolo nɛ kanyisoo asoso nɛ ba daa nyiti asoso akyipɛ ere nɛ aniyaso fɛɛlɛ ere, nɛ mɔ nɛ kinyishi ke nyishi nɛ ashe ashe kobil asoso nɛ ashe kikɛ asoso, nɛ ba nya n de ku baara mɔ ba sɔ ebi nɛ ku gbee kɛyirɛ kikɛ asoso, nɛ Ashe Bɛɛshɛ mɔ kabil bupɔ mɔ nɛ bɛgben nɛ ba bo ba kani asoso kabil konyen.

Wɔrɛ 1

Bebre-po ana kɛkɛ na ba lii m-b'a ba bɛɛ kuso, m-ba d'a de kanyin nɛ larasɛ titi. Ba de ashe nɛ laasiri, nɛ b'a kpaa ka ba kili b'ana nɛ nyinpeena yese.

Gbungba anyɔ

Kupa nso be keena wɔra be shintow nɛ a fɛɛ mbra be kakilɛ nɛ ba chɛ kakasɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ, nɛ aso fanɛ dɔmba, puku be alɛɛ, kɛnyalɛ bee kɛche, kanyal, laleba, asɛn be alɛɛ bee adunshenɛ kanyal, lanto bee kupa be shishi, kusuw, kibaw bee aso kashɔn be pɛnshen mɔ ma bo e-mu.

Kəyitiŋ nɛ, ba m'maa lile fɔrɔkɔ pɛyɛ kəanyaso nɛ kə-gbandana pɔ, kiti-sobiya pɔ, nɛ kə-kaso pɔ kəanyaso pɔ a sa kəyitiŋ ko nsaŋ nɛ kumu pɔ nɛ kərɔɔ pɔ to, kumu bee ba kərɔɔ na di nna m-ba tiŋ kənyɛ kɔ, kumu bee n-kpe kəso pɔ, kumu bee m'ma tiŋ kənyɛ kɔ, nko kumu kpe mbule-so kɔ kə-tiŋ kumu bee to.

Bumu 3

Bebire bee pɛ nsu n keni bumo nyi, kumo kumu nɛ kumo kumu kpa a nyi.

Gbana 4

Gbay bi ma kpe bee nung nyu baasa kpaŋŋi bee donkpeni to; donkpeni bee donkpeene yili bee kiki bee deŋ fo kpaasa kpeŋkpaŋ to.

Ayiko 5

Baa maŋ ba wɔra kuwɔrɛ na ma tɔgɔ baŋɛŋɛ anii nchu maŋ ya bee kumpaŋ nɛ na kumo maŋ taa nɛ, bee kumpaŋ nɛ na kumo ba tuŋ kɛba na.

Gbansa 6

Kumo-kumo kəram bɔrɔ kusu-kusu pɛyɛ na e koro mo kamasa e de kpaa nɛ mbra kamana.

Kaseto 7

Bɛkpaaga pɛi kɔmɔ mbee maara nɔ a-pɛlɛ e-yi shɛn lara bɛkpaaga pɛi a nyɔ maara nɔ a-fɔn lara b-ba dɛɛ kparaso n'kpa lara. Bɛkpaaga pɛi a nyɔ b-ba dɛɛ fɔn lara kparaso n'kpa lara bɔ dɔŋɔ n'kpaŋa nɛ a-tii b-ba shɛɛ Kpaakpaasha nɔ lara, nɛ b-ba dɛɛ n-tishi nyiikpo kparaso bɔ dɔŋɔ n'kpaŋa nɛ.

Gishe 8

Basa pɛyikpa kpaa ba kowura eba na kinyo nɛ e na lii asare kigba nɛ kigbapɔ mumu na kpa kowura mbra to bee kinyo nɛ kigbapɔ to a wura bumo ashi kumpa nɛ e kɔla kowura nɛ e kpa bumo na to.

Artikəl 9

Ba mɔŋ kuli man tɔŋ nɛ ba daŋ mɔ, ba daŋ leŋ mo kasaso bee ba daŋ gbaŋ mo lii mo tiŋ nɛ ere ashebi kpa.

Atekal 10

Kuu-ana kii ti bee nya ndee-kumo gba kumo bee fo ti koto-gbiang be kute ne bee wuli eba a shii n-sa kotee ne kumo bee la n-tane nɛ kumo maa chɛ mɔrɔ eba, n-kumo bee da a bee bu mo be chichibi to bee kumo ne kumo bee la mo be kute nɛ kotee ne kumo bee la kute be ashi bee ba mo so.

Kagbana 11

Kama so bee ji bee gbee nɛ ka kɔ la, bɛɛ na ba de bɔra nɛ n kishi kpa nɛ kɔ fo kishi maa lina kpeŋ nɛ shɛ ashɛ mɔ n yaa kɔ ashe nɛ m kishi nɛ bɛɛ yili nɛ kɔ fo a jina nɔ, mɔ n yaa de ashe kama nɛ a kɔ fo a kɛɛ nɛ bɛɛ bo fo n sa mo n de mɔ-gbee la.

Ebaye monɛ ma la a biya asho asho monɛ ma baŋ nɛ e be kpe a lara a kpaa a bui asho asho monɛ ma ta lara, nɛ be maŋ lara bee kawolanto lara be kpaa to, e gbaŋ to monɛ e bee bui mɔ. Nsa gba ma la a ta kumo lara kɛsɛ kpaa n tulo a gbaŋ to monɛ a lara be kpe a lara to e gbaŋ to monɛ e bee bui mɔ.

Atekel 12

Bɔnyin kɔkɔ ma lara a ba tulwɛ mɔ e-ŋasa, mɔ lanto, mɔ gbɛ bee mɔ kusuwɔ bee mɔ kakatɛ nyaŋa so pɛyɛ, kusuwɔ kɔkɔ ma lara a ba gbɛn mɔ duliŋ bee mɔ kumo so. Bɔnyin bee bɔnyin de kɛlaŋ nɛ kishikpe pɛ a fɔr mɔ akɔn nɛ amɔaso kuto bee agbɛŋ nyaŋa mɔ.

Leta 13

Bumbɔrɔ kama bɔ kigbambiyɛ nɛ baŋ ka daŋ kinyo nɛ ashibɛ lara aŋasɔ kama a kusuŋ to.

Kupaŋkam kodo kashɛ nɛ e fɔlɛ nɛ e tɔkɛ maŋ kparakpara, kodo nɛ e gbaŋɛ maŋ to, nɛ e kuloŋ nɛ e lile ba e maŋ to.

Gbondo 14

Kama bee de shili nɛ o kɔkɔ ebi nya kpa m ba lara nɛ o kɛ kɔrɛ ebɔra so n gaa asili kuso m ba maŋ kɔ daŋ.

Enyinɛ nɛ nya man lara kɛfa nɛ anɔm kɛtɔ nɛ e yile kumo mɔ kumo man gbiye kanyi nɛ e ma bɔla bɔyiri bɛɛ bɛkpa nɛ e kisi United Nations kanyi nɛ bɛ bɔla nɛ be bise nɛ b'a tɔ.

Kashɛ 15

Kagbanaasa be kamalŋa de keteŋ be kasowɔ.

Baa maa finyɛ bɛɛ dɔŋŋi kuurɛ kumo baŋa nɛ e de a laŋ a bo to, nɛ bɛɛ ba maa baa bɔ kuurɛ kumo de a saŋ kumo baŋa.

Kashin 16

Banyaso nɛ bacheso mɔkɔ nɛ bamo mɛ baŋ pɛi na, nɛ bamo maŋ laa fɔ kiti nɛ bamo kanyawuto, bamo kaso bee bamo asɔre to, wɔ nsu nɛ bamo tɔ kuso bee bamo kpalɛ kuso nɛ bamo ti laa bo kibiyɛ. Bamo banye gba wɔ nsu kulu nɛ bamo tɔ kuso nɛ kuso to, nɛ gba nɛ bamo tɔ kuso kigba to bee nɛ kuso na sa tɛkɛ to.

Kumuŋ ba kusoŋ e tɔ nɛ kufu ba kumo bee kulo nɛ kulo kutoŋ kumo e la ba e kule nɛ ba e kosoŋ to.

Lulobuto ebo nne maang niteŋ to kpaŋkpaŋ kigbanto maŋkamaŋ na, eyaŋ so, lulobuto ebo niteŋ nɛ kuboŋ bee fa kumo kigbiŋ to.

Lɛta 17

Kenyikpa kɛrɛkɛ wɔ kigbɛŋ ko kumpa mo maŋŋɛ k’anumu bee lila, lara mo bee mbia kashishi.

Nyerɛ kpaŋ bee nya ka ba de mo laari ba kpege nɛ kpaŋ bɔrɛ to.

Gɛl 18

Kuu kɛrɛkɛrɛ bee de kaseɛto nɛ bee yeli maŋgbi kuto, m-fuli nɛ n-giyiliso; kaseɛto nɛŋ gbaŋ yeli nɛ bee lii koto n-fuli bee kuto kiti, nɛ bee yeli, fɛ fo koŋko bee fo nɛ ashi mbo kumo kpo, bo ashimɛ bee kunkun to, n-nyaŋ n-fuli bee kuto n-yeli n-shuŋ kashiye to, m-bo to, n-sore to nɛ k-kisi to.

Atikil 19

Kumo-kumo bee de ashiwaye nɛ kumo lara e-nyuli na fɔŋfɔŋ fɛ kumo laŋ ka fɛ nyɔ: ashiwaye nɛ bee su fɛ ashiwaye nɛ kumo bee kpeŋ kumo nyuli dɔ nɛ kumo m'bishi to fɛ ashi kumo bee tɔŋ, bee nyɔ nɛ kumo bee kpa labaare nɛ laara dɔ nɛ ashiwaye kɔkɔ dɔ, m'bishi kɛkɛ nɛ kumo pɔrɛ to.

Giri 20

Bumu pɛyɛ kumo nɛ be kpa nɛ be pɛntɛ ashi asɔwule kɔrɔŋ nɛ chɛlɛ to.

Baa kɔŋ kufuli n'bee kpa bɛɛ-kɔɔ nɛ kumo bee nɛŋ n'kpa lumbɔ bɛɛ-kɔɔ n'to.

Atekil 21

Kena bee ketɛɛ to kpaŋkpan nɛ ba na buwɔ to ashi mo kubi to nɛɛ be gɔfɛmenti to, mo kɔkɔ bee nɛ ba la kashinɛ a baŋ be ashibi nɛ ba kpaŋ kulo to.

Kama be baŋ kpaŋ bee toso kumu to kumo tetaŋ to be akayili kumu to.

Ebi mbo pɛ mbe kpaŋkpaŋ be mbunee; mbe pɛ kamo mɔ e buwo bee kiti nna fo bee lara mbe nna a woro nna ebi pɛ bee laa a joo kigbegbe kishi bee bumo bee laa a joo bee bu bee dɔŋkɔ mɔ a kishi bee kiti nna a buwo ashitɛ bee kiti nna kashitɔ bee shishi.

Atikili 22

Kama be kuŋu na a la maŋ be kanyo eena, a de gbenpo bunɔ a kpe lara nɛ a na nyanɔ kusoŋ be mbra, eena a de fule ba nɛ a na giligili be maŋ be kumo kusoŋ nɛ dunia gbagba be kusoŋ ashe, a ti maŋ kama be kabu nɛ kɛyɔrɛ be bunɔ kusoŋ ashe, a nyiŋ nɛ a de alɛka, maŋ be aŋwɔrɛ, nɛ ashama be mbra nɛ a la kapo a de kumo kase nɛ a de kumo pɛnɛ be kanyiŋ a kpe nyoro so.

Asishe 23

Kusuŋ-kusuŋ kumoŋ bee de kawɔrso nɛ e laa kɔ e tɔŋ, nɛ e lara kigbee nɛ e bɔ e tɔŋ kumoŋ nɛ e daa biishi, nɛ e nya katɔŋ-kura nɛ ku tana nɛ ku bee dɔŋ, nɛ e nya kigari nɛ ku bee fa e kumu nɛ katɔŋ-koro.

Kumo kikɛ, fo n'gbiikpama k'kɛ, b'de b'laale kumu kikɛ n'gbee pɛnkɛpɛnkɛ kumu kikɛ n'gbiilɛ.

Kuyu mɔ kuto nyan eji nɛ e wɔra nton e gba nɛ nyan e kpa bɔnyɔ nɛ e bɔra nɛ e mɔ nton na, e bɔra nɛ bumu kɛ buma mɔ dumo e nyi kashɛ nɛ e te bumu kinyi e kɛla nɛ nyanyɛ e gba, nɛ e kɛ tina, nyan e tiŋ n kpa bɔnyɔ nɛ e be kufu e bɔra kuyu mɔ kuto na kaseŋ.

Aso kama de kasɛbɔ fe mobi n lii na m ba tina kufuŋ nɔkɔ naŋ koto m pɛ mobi kibaane.

Gbaŋpo 24

Kagbana sa kuso pɛyɛ kɛkɛ na kutoŋ, kɛɛ kumo e sa kaseɛ nna kumo e buŋ kutoŋ nyaŋa kpe n'kaseɛ nna alaa bɛ chigee nɛ kɛyɛ nɛ kigbɛlɛ.

Akutikil 25

Kuso kurowɔrɛ nɛ ansa bɛɛ tinti nɛ kumo kishi mɛ bɛɛ fɔrɔ nɛ kumo pɔlɛ anasa bɔnbɔn nɛ kumo nyafula nɛ mɔ nyi kumo kurowɔrɛ bɛkpa, kumo kishi nɛ agyɛ ajiya, agyala, abulo nɛ alafia kishi, nɛ kuso kurowɔrɛ bɛɛ tinti nɛ kumo bɛɛ fɔrɔ mɔ nɛ amingya nyaŋ kɔnɛ nɛ e shia n baŋa kumo nɛ ekpa, alafia kishi kumo, kawuntolo, anyikpale, kutoŋkpa nɛ kumo fɔrɔ kurowɔrɛ nɛ e mɔŋ tinto nɛ kumo kishi nɛ e mɔŋ nɔŋ fɔrɔ kumo kulo to.

Bayili kpa ne biye kumo kiti kpa kumo kpaŋ kuloŋ nɛ kumuŋso naŋsaŋsaŋ. Biye pɛyɛ, kumuŋ bawo ba mara ba kumo kpara ashi kumuŋ gbaŋ nyoŋ ara kumuŋ gbaŋ po, ba da kumo laŋa kumo kiti kuloŋ nɛ kumuŋso laŋa kumuŋ soŋsoŋ.

Awolɛ 26

Kumu so bee de lakal kalɛ ashi. Lakal kalɛ bee ba fufur, kalɛ anishi nɛ a che kaye nɛ kpaŋkpankpa kuteŋ to. Kuteŋ to lakal kalɛ bee ba pampam. Nsoko nɛ agyima lakal kalɛ bee ba mɔkapa bee nya, nɛ lakal kalɛ kumo nɛ a de kɔkɔ bee de chɔchɔ ashi kumo bee tinto nɛ mɔkapa nya ashi kumu nɛ bumo le bo to.

Kasukol kuli bumo to baa laŋa nɛ ku laŋ kanyiŋ kumo mɔ kumo bumo nɛ ku bo bunɔ mɛŋ kusuŋ nɛ ku baŋ nchɛ kanyiŋ bumo jigito nɛ ba fofor kumo ba mɛŋ. Ku baŋ ti kashia, kanyiti nɛ kanyɛkpalpo bumo mbɔmbo kpaanana kumo bumo, baŋa fɔl kumo bumo nɛ ba addini bumo to, nɛ ku baŋ kpa United Nations kusuŋ bumo to nɛ ku baŋ pishi asomdwoe to.

Naa bi bee wo kakye nna ba kii kashentebi ne ba bee kye ba mɔ bi to.

Gishe 27

Esa kama so baa bee laŋ kiti lo k'awɔrto k'ebor so bee, k'anuro bɔtɔ lo, k'ashio sayensi kpa l'enyire so.

Kumo-ana bee pɛ kɔlɛ nɛ b’a dɛɛ bɛkɔ nɛ bɛ ba mɛrɛ nyi nɛŋkpa nɛ dunya alufiya bee nyi dɛ nyu nyulena nɛ kɔkɔ bee alufiya bee lii ebura, bɔkpanɛ, nɛ aŋwuura kpa a-nɛ kumo bee nɛŋwule ba dɛ kumo nung kpa.

Gbungbɔŋ 28

Koso koso wɔ nna to nɛ dunya to kɛlaŋ nɛ kumu to nɛ baŋ ba be shishi-kadima nɛ kaye-kuli nɛ ba malgɛ n be Kasoŋ-lɛŋ nna to na la funyɛ be kanyishi to.

Ashiripotɔ 29

Kama so kumu bee baŋ lanto a gboŋ ere na to, bo nɛ kumo peele to pɛ kumo baŋ lanto a gboŋ na to bee wora kumo lanto feena nɛ kumu foŋko to nɛ kumo nyinikpaŋ bee wura.

Ebi nɛ ɔ ba nyɔŋ kpeŋ e-nyalɛ nɛ e-kanyɛ nɛ mɔ nyi e-malɛ bee n, bumo kɛkɛ nɛ mmra bee laŋ fo nɛ e-la e-to, nɛ mɔ kɛkɛ ba sa ebi nini koso nɛ bumo bu e-nyalɛ nɛ e-kanyɛ nɛ mɔ nyi be-malɛ, nɛ e-sa kufu nyumu be-malɛ, ashe be-kanyɛ nɛ ashe kɛkɛ be-dɔr e-malɛ nɛ e-kɛ e-dɛmɔkrasia bee n to.

Ebrawule nɛ eyilɛ ŋa, kibaŋ kɔkɔ maŋ kuli ba mɔŋ kɛ ba tɔn n'ayole kɛ basɛ anyaŋ United Nations mɔ kuto nɛ bumo ebiwule ŋa.

Mbusu 30

Kɛra kpaa kusuŋ kama mɛ n bi baŋ lara kɛna nɛɛ kumo tinti sa kuwɔl kama, kutɔ mɛɛ anufu kpaa nɛɛ bɛɛ nyi kumuŋ to n kishi kusɔ kusuŋ kama mɛ n na baŋ tɔŋ kumo gbagba n sa kumuŋ to bumbur kama bɛɛ bumo to nyirgbɛɛ nɛ bumo yɛ bɔ to kramo nɛɛ to mumu to.
