Jeha nɛ ɔ maa ba si kaa jehahi nɛ haomi ngɛ mi pe kulaa ngɛ wa ma a hewɔ nimli a he si kpami yi mi kɛ he tɔtsɔɔ mi. Kaa bɔ nɛ e ba ngɛ je ɔ kpoo nɔ ɔ, COVID-19 gblamgblam hiɔ ɔ ba fia wɔ ngɛ jeha 2020 kekleekle nyɔhiɔ etɛ ɔmɛ a mi, nɛ lɔ ɔ ha nɛ jé sika yemi kɛ ní tsumihi ba si, nɛ e kɛ wa sika nɛ wa náa a mi nɔ́ gbli ba kɛ sika babauu nɛ a to we he blɔ nya nɛ a puɛ kɛ kpe hiɔ nɛ ɔ nya.

Benɛ wa susu kaa wa ngɛ nyɛɛe kɛ yaa hɛ mi wawɛɛ ngɛ gbali nɛ hio gbaligbali ɔ kɛ ba a he kɛ gu wa tsakemi he ga womi ɔ nɛ wa bɔni tsumi kɛ tsu ní ɔ he nɔ ɔ, nibɔhi babauu ba tɔtɔ wɔ nɛ lɔɔ puɛ ma a sikayeeli he blɔ nya tomi nɛ ya nɔ saminya kɛ je 2017 kɛ ya si 2019 ɔ. Kɛkɛ nɛ Russia-Ukraine ta a ba, lɔɔ se ɔ, yikomi tɔ babauu nɛ kɔɔ sikayeeli he blɔ nya tomi he ngɛ 2022 ga womi ɔ mi, nɛ lɔɔ ha nɛ wa nane kpami kɛ ha hiɔ he sika heblimɔ si kple. Truetruenu ɔ, kɛ je jeha nyɔngɔ nɛ be ɔ mi lɔɔ nɛ kɛ ma pɛ, International Capital Market tsi a sinyɔ nya ngɛ wa hɛ mi, ma a mi sika kɛ ha heblimɔ he blɔ nya tomihi wa nɛ hiɔ he sika heblimɔ tsɔ sika pɛi, nɛ lɔɔ ha nɛ hiɔ nɛ a kɛ ha nɔ ɔ hiɔ yeli kple nɛ cedi ɔ hu he wami nɛ ya si mlamlaamla.
