HƐMI NƆ MUNYU

Jeha nɛ ɔ maa pee jeha nɛ haomi hi mi pe kulaa ngɛ wa ma a he wami kɛ sikayelɔhi a blema jami mi, nɛ he jemi be he. Kɛ ji kɛ ha je ɔ tsuo ɔ, COVID-19 gbenɔ hiami hiɛmim nɛ ɔ ba fia wɔ ngɛ jeha 2020 klɛmtle kpokpu ɔ mi, nɛ enɛ ɔ ha nɛ sikahyɛmi he nítsumihi ba si, nɛ e ha nɛ wa níná nɛ ba si nɛ e kɛ he nɛ a to we he tsomihi nɛ a kɛ fia kɛ kpe gbenɔ hiami nɛ ɔ ba.

Be nɛ wa susu kaa wa ngɛ nyɛɛe kɛ yaa hɛ mi wawɛɛ ngɛ gbali nɛ hiɔ gbagbalii ɔ kɛ ba a he kɛ gu wa tsakemi blɔ nya tomi ɔ tsumi nini nɛ a je sisi ɔ mi ɔ, jamɛ a be ɔ nɔuu nɛ nɔ́ kpɔtihi ba nɔ nɛ e kɛ be ɔ mi ɔ, ba fite je kɛ wɛ sikasitsɛmi he nya nitsumi kpakpa nɛ wa tsu kɛ je 2017 kɛ ya si 2019 ɔ. Kekleekle ɔ, e ji Russia-Ukraine ta a, kɛ kɛkɛ nɛ kɛtsami nɛ ba ngɛ ga blɔ tomi wami nitsɛ nɛ a to kɛ ha sika nami ngɛ 2022 sika tomi blɔ nya tomi ɔ mi, nɛ kɛ ba nɛ a ba si wa kadi nɛ a kɛ haa kpa nyɛmi ɔ. Trukutruku ɔ, ngɛ kekleekle be mi ngɛ jeha nyɔngɔ mi ɔ, a ngmi International Capital Market ɔ nya ha wɔ, ma mi sika nami blɔhi ba wa nɛ kpa nyɛmi he hiahi ba hiɛ, nɛ lɔɔ ha nɛ hiɔ womi ba wa nɛ cedi ɔ nɔ́ si mlamlaamla.

Enɛ ɔ he je ɔ, benɛ ma se sika yemi kɛ buami nɛ jeɔ jua nɛ a ya juaa níhi nɛ he jua wa ngɛ kɛ wa ma se sika nɛ wa náa a nɔ si ɔ po ɔ, níhi nɛ wa kɛ jeɔ ma se kɛ baa a he hie nɛ kpaako nɛ ba si ngɛ bɔ nɛ e ngɛ heii kaa wa ngɔɔ ma se níhi babauu kɛ baa wa ma a mi kɛ wa nyɔmi nɛ kpaako nɛ ba si nɛ wa ngɛ woe ɔ kɛ piɛɛ he ɔ, ha nɛ wa sika a he jua kpale ba si wawɛɛ nitsɛ. Enɛ ɔ ha nɛ si himi ba pee he jua wa kɛ ha wɔ tsuo, kɛ ha Nɔ yemi ɔ kɛ ha nɔ he nya nɔ he nya tsuo. Kɔkɔɔti he jua nɛ ya nɔ ɔ ha nɛ níhi kɛ sɔmɔmihi fuu a he jua ya nɔ, nɛ níhi a he jua yemi nɛ ya nɔ ɔ ya su 40.4% kɛ ya nɔ nɛ e he be ngɛ October 2022 mi.

A ka wɔ wawɛɛ nitsɛ kaa ma, nɛ nɔyeli ɔ kɛ nyɛ na mi tsɔ kɛ ha nɔ nyɛmi nɛ sɛ nɛ enɛ ɔ kɛ ba a he. Wɔ kaa nɔyeli ɔ, wa ngla suɔe nɛ wa da wa nibi tsuo nɛ a ngɛ he gbɛfa nɔ kpa, nɛ Parliament, Employers’ Associations, Labour Unions, Civil Society, Faith Based Organizations, Association of Ghana Industries, Ghana Union of hu piɛɛ he

Jwalɔhi A Kpe, Kaseli Kpahi, Níleli A Kpe, kɛ Ghana bi kpakpa amɛ nɛ a ya nɔ nɛ a kɛ wɔ to a tsui si benɛ wa ngɛ be nɛ a yeli wa nɛ ɔmɛ a mi tsɔe ɔ.

Ngɛ H. E. Nana Addo Danquah Akuffo-Addo biɛ mi ɔ, I ngɛ hlae nɛ ma ha Ghana bi tsuo nɔmlo nɛ he nɛ e hi kaa Nɔyeli ɔ ngɛ blɔ tsuaa blɔ toɔ nɛ ma ha he blɔ nɛ ekoɔnɔmi ɔ maa ba slɔɔto kɛ macroeconomic bɔɔ nya jɔuumi maa kpale ba, nɛ a ma ná sika babauu, nɛ a ma to blɔ tsuaa blɔ kɛ ma nɔ nɔyeli he blɔtsɔɔmihi, a maa gba dɛkpamɔ kɛ hewɔ níhi a blɔhi a he kɛ ya nɔ, a maa gbla níhi nɛ he hia we kɛ ba ma mi ɔ a nya si, a maa wo níhi nɛ a jeɔ kɛ yaa ma se ɔ he wami, a maa gba ma mi nítsumihi nɛ a ngɛ pee kɛ tsake níhi nɛ a kɛ jeɔ ma se kɛ baa ɔ he kɛ ya nɔ, nɛ a maa bu he kɛ ha nihi nɛ a he be wami kɛ nihi nɛ a kpa ma blɔ kɛ je a he ɔ.

Níhi nɛ wa kase kaa ma ngɛ be nɛ ɔ mi ɔ ha nɛ e he ba hia wawɛɛ kaa wa ma to wa sikali kɛ hewolo hiami níhi a blɔ nya kɛ ya neneene, nɛ waa tsake lɛ, nɛ waa wo wa he hewami nɛ waa da si kpii. Wa maa pee e he ní kɛ ya hi pe hiɔ. Wa maa je nɔ́ nɛ ɔ mi kɛ hewami nɛ nya wa pe sa. Nyɔmtsa a maa hɛɛ wɔ nɛ e maa ha wɔ hewami kɛ pee enɛ ɔ.
