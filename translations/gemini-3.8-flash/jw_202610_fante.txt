1926

A CENTURY HAS PASSED

The January 1, 1926 Watchtower stated: “It will not be long before God will let everyone see that he is the Almighty and he will raise up a people to bear witness to him.”

In 1926, Jehovah, through Jesus Christ who is the head of the congregation, made it clear to Christians that the preaching work must come first in their lives. Therefore, the "faithful and discreet slave" produced new books, and they also made changes to the way Christian meetings were conducted. Aside from these, the brothers were helped to understand what they must do to show that they are true Christians. (Matt. 24:45) Christ ensured that they acquired buildings to work in and machines to print various books so that they could carry out the preaching work.

A NEW BOOK WAS RELEASED AND CHANGES WERE ALSO MADE TO THE WAY CONGREGATION MEETINGS ARE CONDUCTED

In the month of April, The Golden Age, now called Awake!, announced that a new book called Deliverance had been released. That was to replace the book titled Studies in the Scriptures, which was used in preaching. In order for the brothers to receive the number of books they would need for preaching, the brothers at Bethel bought a machine for printing books. That machine could print 22 books in one minute. Whenever possible, the Bible Students gave a copy of the book to anyone they witnessed to.

Before the year 1926, the Bible Students studied the book Studies in the Scriptures at their meetings. But from May 1926, they began to study The Watchtower. What benefit came from this?

There was a small congregation in Illinois called Bunker Hill. They wrote a letter to Brother Rutherford, saying: “The Bunker Hill congregation greets you. When Sunday comes, what Jehovah’s congregation has directed us to study is what we study. We are very glad that whenever any new light comes, all of us see it quickly in The Watchtower.”

“New light” or new changes that came helped the Bible Students to focus their attention on the preaching work. To illustrate this, one Watchtower study explained that although it is proper to show good character, treat people well, and respect them, we must not overlook the most important work. That study article stated: “If someone loves the Lord, he must . . . keep his commandments. . . . Jehovah says: ‘You are my witnesses and I am God.’ . . . If someone is a Christian, bearing witness about God must be a necessity to him.”

Another Watchtower study also emphasized that what true Christians do to show that they truly love Jehovah is to serve him with all their heart. That study article encouraged the Bible Students that come rain or shine, ‘they must bear witness to all the nations.’—Isa. 43:10, 12; Matt. 24:14.

It was not long before these changes that were made bore fruit. A few weeks later, a letter written by a congregation was published in The Watchtower of July 15, 1926. It stated:

MEETINGS WERE HELD TO ENCOURAGE THE BRETHREN TO ZEALOUSLY DO THE PREACHING WORK

At the end of May, many Bible Students attended a convention held in London, England. At that time, workers across Britain went on strike. Therefore, it was difficult to find a company to print books for them. Under those circumstances, the brothers at Brooklyn Bethel worked overtime, so they were able to print an additional 117,000 copies of the booklet titled The Standard for the People. This booklet was distributed to the brothers who attended the London convention.

On Saturday, May 29, they began using this booklet in preaching. That day, a statement printed on the convention program encouraged the brothers: “Everyone who has been baptized and is attending this convention ought to tell people about Jesus and his Kingdom. Tomorrow morning, let everyone endeavor to arrive early at Alexandra Palace, and the brothers there will show you what to do.”

Over 100,000 books were distributed. The Watch Tower of July 15, 1926, reported: “The brothers were very happy. They felt that they had listened to Jehovah and preached the good news to people to show that they are his witnesses.”

The next day, Brother Rutherford gave a talk about the fate of the world powers at the Royal Albert Hall. When the doors were opened, it was not long before 13,000 people crowded the hall to capacity. For those who did not get the chance to enter, large speakers were set up at the entrance so that they could also listen.

In Brother Rutherford’s talk, he explained that the reason why the rulers of the world were blundering was that they had turned their backs on Jehovah and his Kingdom. Then, he denounced the League of Nations and said that it would not be long before it passed away.

TRAVELING OVERSEERS LED THE PREACHING WORK

From about 1894 on, Jehovah’s organization sent representatives to visit the Bible Students, just as circuit overseers do today. In July 1926, the way their work was carried out changed. Previously, the work was done in one day, but at this time they were to spend two days visiting each group. In addition, they were required to spend 100 hours in preaching each month.

A brother named George Draper, who was a traveling overseer, wrote a letter to the congregation. He said: “Jehovah’s servants are like watchdogs in a house. Therefore, we must warn people so that they see that danger is coming. [Isa. 56:10] It is studying The Watchtower that will help us to do this, and that is what I have set as my goal.” It was in the year 1938 that Brother Draper finished his earthly service.

A MEETING WE HELD IN NEW YORK CITY

The last convention that we held that year was held on October 14-18, 1926, in New York City. Brother Rutherford was to give a talk entitled “The Approaching World War.” The advertising agencies agreed to advertise Brother Rutherford’s talk. But shortly before the time arrived, many of the prominent ones among them withdrew.

In response to what those companies did, the brothers decided to strike back. So on Friday, October 15, Brother R. J. Martin released a new tract entitled Against the People. Its main purpose was to expose those companies for everyone to see and to advertise Brother Rutherford’s talk. Brother Martin said: “We have started printing our books with our own machines. Our brothers at Bethel will work hard day and night until they have printed 1,250,000 tracts. We will give them to the brothers attending this convention to distribute to people.” When the convention closed on Friday, the brothers set out and distributed the tracts. By evening, they had distributed about 300,000 of the tracts.

On Saturday and Sunday, more were distributed in addition, and the total number distributed reached 1,000,000. Apart from that, the brothers exerted themselves and distributed 3,783 Deliverance books and also distributed 35,140 booklets.

A BUILDING WAS BUILT AT 117 ADAMS STREET

Because many people wanted our publications, the responsible brothers in Brooklyn saw that the place where we printed our publications needed to be expanded. Many years ago, a building had been rented at 18 Concord Street. That was where our publications were printed. But because the work was growing, the place was too small. So the brothers went and bought a piece of land on Adams Street. It was very close to where they already were, so they began to build an eight-story building on it. Now, they would have a large place to print their publications.

The building that was being constructed at 117 Adams Street was scheduled to begin operations in 1927. That building was going to help the brothers prepare for the growth that was to come. In 1927, many people entered the congregation, and Jehovah continued to prepare and purify them so that in 1931, they could be called Jehovah's Witnesses.

PSALM 94 We Value God’s Word

Base Your Faith On The Bible, And Not On Humans

WHEN you studied the Bible with Jehovah’s Witnesses, what convinced you that this was the truth, or what the Bible calls the “one faith”? (Eph. 4:4-6) Was it the love they had for one another? Or was it their good conduct that appealed to you? It was the love and peace within Jehovah’s congregation that drew many people to the truth. And rightly so, because it is our love and good conduct that identify us as Jehovah’s servants.—John 13:35; 1 Pet. 2:12.

Even as we say this, what if a brother offends you or fails to show you love? Or what if a brother commits a serious, disgraceful sin, like what happened in the Corinthian congregation? (1 Cor. 5:1) The point is, if your whole faith is based on how people act or how they make you feel, that is dangerous. So what should your faith really be based on?

Let Your Faith Stand Firm

One thing that can make your faith stand firm and strong is to check every day to see that what you are learning from the Bible is true. Why should you do so? Now let us look at an event that took place in the time of Jesus.

One day, Jesus said that his followers must eat his flesh and drink his blood. Many people did not understand what he said, so they stopped following him. They said: "This saying is shocking."—John 6:56-60, 66.

When they left, Jesus asked his apostles: "Do you also want to leave?" Peter answered: "Lord, to whom shall we go? You have the words of eternal life. We have believed and have come to know that you are the Holy One of God."—John 6:67-69.

As for Peter and the rest of the apostles, what Jesus said did not trouble them. Why? The reason is that their faith was not based on human beings or how they felt, but rather it was based on something solid. The apostle Peter said: "We have believed and have come to know that you are the Holy One of God." So the apostles knew what they believed and why they believed. They compared what Jesus said and did with what they knew from the Scriptures. Therefore, they truly believed that Jesus was the Messiah. That truth which they had come to know made their faith firm. So when what Jesus said troubled some people, as for them, they were not troubled.

What do we learn from this matter? If you know or realize that something is true, do not look at what someone is doing or saying. Why are we saying this? The very reason is that humans can change. But the truth in the Bible, such as the truth about God's name, what he wants to do, and the ransom, will never change. That truth, no matter what humans do or what humans say, will never change. Therefore, if we believe what the Bible teaches, and we are with the organization Jehovah is using

AN EXPERIENCE

If You Believe in Jehovah, He Will Take Care of You

Emma is a sister who works in a hotel. She goes to work three days a week, and each day she works for nine hours. She wanted to attend a regional convention, so she asked her supervisor for time off. Her supervisor did not agree, but rather told her to work five days, and if she would not, she should quit the job. Emma had strong trust in Jehovah, so she decided to quit the job and attend the convention.

When Emma was going to give the letter indicating that she had quit the job to her supervisor, she met the manager of the hotel there. When the manager heard what had happened, he promised Emma to set her mind at ease and that he would take care of it. But sadly, two weeks later, Emma was told that she had been dismissed. One day, she met the manager of the hotel again. When the manager heard that she had been dismissed, he was very angry because he had not said she should be dismissed. So the manager looked into the matter, settled it, and made everything right. He said she should work five days a week, and on each of those days, she should work for only four hours. Even with this, things had turned out completely in Emma's favor. This new arrangement could help her carry out her pioneer work. What Jehovah did for Emma was something she did not expect at all. He blessed her exceedingly.
