"Edendolɛ ɛhe yɛ ɛzulɔ; nwane a obohola atie a?" (John 6:60) Ɛhye a le kɛzi Gyisɛse ɛdoavolɛma dɔɔnwo yɛle bɛ ninyɛne wɔ edendolɛ mɔɔ Gyisɛse yɛle wɔ Kapanɔm la anwo. 2 Bɛdabɛ mɔɔ Gyisɛse edwɛkɛ ne hunle bɛ kpole kpalɛ la guale ye edzi mɔɔ bɛanli la azo. Asoo bɛnlɛ diedi kpalɛ wɔ ye nu ɔ? Nɔhalɛ nu, kolaa na ɛhye asi la, bie mɔ—mɔɔ Gyisɛse ɛdoavolɛma boka nwo la—anyelielie ninyɛne mɔɔ Gyisɛse yɛle la anwo kpalɛ kɛ bɛhanle kɛ: “Nɔhalɛ nu, ɛhye a le Ngapezolɛ mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ ɔba ewiade la.” (Dwɔn 6:14) Ɔti ɔda ali kɛ ɛdoavolɛma zɔhane mɔ lɛ diedi ekyi wɔ Gyisɛse nu. Noko mekɛ mɔɔ bɛsɔle bɛ diedi bɛnleanle la, bɛanli nɔhalɛ bɛamma ye.

Anzɛɛ kɛ yɛyɛ yɛ nyɛleɛ kɛzi wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɔyɛ se kɛ yɛbade deɛ mɔɔ ɔti Gyihova maa amaneɛnwunle to yɛ no abo no? Yɛ gyeledie noko bahola anwu sɔhwɛ sɛ Kilisienyi agɔnwo mɔɔ bikye yɛ pɛ yɛ nwo edwɛkɛ anzɛɛ sɛ awie mɔɔ yɛbu ye ne yɛ yɛ nyɛleɛ mɔɔ kɛyɛ kɛ ɔntɛnla nu a.

Zɔhane ala noko a, amaa yɛagyina ninyeni diele sɔnea mɔɔ yɛhã nwolɛ ɛdendɛlɛ wɔ ɛdendɛkpunli mɔɔ li ɛnla la anwo zo la anloa la, ɔhyia kɛ yɛ ninyeni diele anyi kɔ aze amba kpole. 7 Kenga Nyikileba 4:11. Kɛlata ɛhye kakye yɛ kɛ Gyihova yɛbɔle ninyɛne muala a. Ɛhye ngome maa ɔfɛta kɛ yɛzonle ye. Na saa yɛdwene ye tumi kpole ne anwo a, yɛnyia anwodozo kɛ ɔbahola yealie yɛ yeavi yɛ sɔnea muala anu. Noko, Seetan ɛva adalɛ edwɛkɛ ɛyia ewiade ɛhye anu kɛ ɔbamaa sonla anyia adwenle kɛ Nyamenle ɛnle ɛkɛ. Abɔdeɛ nwo nwomanynli bɔbɔ bahola aha kɛ ngoane rale ɔ ngomekye. Saa yɛɛnlie yɛnli kpalɛ kɛ Gyihova a le Ɔbɔvolɛ ne na ɔkulo yɛ a, yɛbahola yɛabɔ ɔ bo kɛ yɛadwenle kɛ ɔnle ɛkɛ na ɔnrɛrɛla ɔnrɛraboa yɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛkɛyia diedi sɔnea la.—Hib. 11:6.

8 Saa ɔbɔ ahyɛ aseɛ kɛ edwindwen kɛ Gyihova le Ɔbɔvolɛ a, yɛ ninyɛne nzo-nzo pɛɛ. Hwehwɛ yɛ mbuluku ne mɔ anu bɔkɔɔ kɛ ɛkonyia ninyɛne mɔɔ di adanse kpole kɛ Gyihova a ɔbɔle debie biala a. Ɛbahola wɔanyia ninyɛne dɔɔnwo mɔɔ bahyɛ wɔ gyedi zo wɔ edwɛkɛ ɛhye anwo wɔ brohyua mɔɔ le Was Life Created? nee The Origin of Life—Five Questions ne anu

Jessica, kakula raalɛ bie mɔɔ wo United States la ka kɛ: “Me gwenle buluku mɔɔ se Origin of Life la pɛ, me nyianle anwodozo kɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ wo ɛkɛ la kile kɛ Bɔvolɛ nyansaso bie a bɔle ninyɛne a. Ɛnee meze ɛhye dedeɛ, na mɔɔ me dwenlenle nzusunleɛ mɔɔ wo buluku ne anu la anwo la, me nwunle kɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ baka la le nɔhalɛ amgba.” Bieko, nwomenle dɔɔnwo anyia mvasoɛ wo buluku mɔɔ se Is There a Creator Who Cares About You? la anu. 10. Kɛzi yɛbu Baebolo ne ɛ? (2 Timote 3:16, 17)

¹¹ Kɛmɔti a yɛgye yɛdi kɛ Nyamenle a ɔmaan bɛhɛlɛle Baebolo ne a? Ninyɛne dɔɔnwo ati. Nyamenle Edwɛkɛ ne di munli wɔ tete edwɛkɛ nu. (Luke 3:1, 2) Eza ɔdi munli saa ɔreka abɔdeɛ nwo edwɛkɛ a. (Job 26:7) Baebolo ne a le buluku mɔɔ bɛahyehyɛ ye kpalɛ wɔ mekɛ muala anu a, ɔnva nwo kɛ Seetan ɛbɔ mɔdenle dɔɔnwo kɛ ɔbaye ye avi ɛkɛ ne la. Mrenya ngakyile kɛyɛ 40 a hɛlɛle Baebolo ne wɔ ɛvolɛ kɛyɛ 1,600 anu a; noko, bɛ edwɛkɛ ne yia. Ɔlɛ folɛdulɛ mɔɔ ɛmmaa mɔɔ ɛboa menle mgbe dɔɔnwo wɔ bɛ dahuu ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nu. (Prov. 13:20; 15:21; Matt. 7:12) Yɛɛ Baebolo ngapezii dɔɔnwo ɛra nu, mɔɔ kile kɛ yɛbahola yɛava yɛ rɛle yɛawula Baebolo ne anu kpalɛ a. (Josh. 23:14) Ɛhye, Nyamenle a sunsum ye amgba maan bɛhɛlɛle Baebolo ne a!

¹² Sɛ yɛbɔ aze kɛ yɛngye yɛndi kɛ Nyamenle a ɔmaan bɛhɛlɛle Baebolo ne a, duazu a ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ a? Ɔwɔ kɛ yɛfa yɛto asɔneyɛlɛ nu na yɛbɔ mɔdenle kɛ yɛbanyia mualɛ yɛamaa yɛ asɔneyɛlɛ ne. Yɛ nzonle edwɛkɛ mɔɔ Jordan mɔɔ le adiema kpanyinli kakula mɔɔ bɛtetele ye kɛ Dasevolɛ la hanle la: “Mende debie biala mɔɔ bɛka bɛkile me la medi amɛla amɛla. Ɔwɔ kɛ me nwo mumwa me dwenledwenle ninyɛne nwo. Menkile awozonle, me nye gyigyi siane siane. Noko saa ɔba ye zɔ a, mebɔ mɔdenle kɛ mebayɛ neɛnleanle wɔ yɛ mbuluku ne mɔ anu amaa meanyia me kpuya ne mɔ anwo mualɛ na meadua me nye mɔɔ ɛlɛgyigyi la azo.” Saa yɛkɔ zo yɛmaa anwodozo mɔɔ yɛlɛ kɛ Gyihova a ɔmaan bɛhɛlɛle Baebolo ne la anu mia a, yɛbamaa yɛ diedi ne anu amia kpalɛ.—1 Kɔl. 3:12, 13.

13 Se yɛbɔ ahyɛse kɛ yɛbanya adwennyew kɛ Jehovah Adanfoman a le menmenia Nyamenle te wɔ bɛ akyi ɛnɛ a, ɔnsɔ kɛ yɛma adwennyew ɛhye kɛ bɔ so. Mfomunli ɛhye a, yɛbahola yɛapɛ adwennyew ɛhye azo se yɛbisa yɛ nwoma ndiannwoma ɛhye mɔ a: ‘Nwan mɔ a amgba bɛlɛbɔ mmɔden kɛ bɛbamaa bɛ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ ali Baebolo ne ngyinlazo nzonlɛ a? Nwan mɔ a bɛbu Baebolo ne kɛ ɔle tumivolɛ wɔ debie biala anu a?’ (2 Tim. 4:3, 4) ‘Nwan mɔ a bɛlɛyɛ Jehovah apɛde ɛnɛ a?’—Matt. 7:20, 21.

14 Nrenyia a yɛlɛyɛ Nyamenle ɛhulolɛdeɛ ɛnɛ ne anwo, yebola yebiza yɛ nwo kɛ, ‘Nwane a ɛlɛyɛ asɛnkɛhanlɛ gyima mɔɔ Gyisɛse hanle kɛ bɛbayɛ ye wɔ awielɛ mekɛ ne anu la a?’ (Matt. 24:14) Mualɛ ne da ali fann. Nyamenle ɛlɛfa ye menli—Gyihova Alasevolɛ—ayɛ asɛnkɛhanlɛ gyima ne. Yɛ ngomekye a yɛlɛka Nyamenle Belemgbunlililɛ ne anwo edwɛkpa ne wɔ maanle mɔɔ bo 240 anu a. Yɛlɛyɛ gyima ɛhye yebo ɛvolɛ 100 wɔ Belemgbunlililɛ ne tumililɛ bo. Ɛhye, yeva yɛ adwenle yezie asɛnkɛhanlɛ nee ngilehilelɛ gyima kpole ne anwo tɛnla.—Matt. 28:19, 20.

15 Kenga Mateyu 6:9 nee Dwom 43:10. Bɛdua Nyamenle ye duma kpole, Gyɛhova, zoolɛ bɛnwunla yɛ, yɛɛ ɔyɛ yɛ fɛ kɛ bɛfɛlɛ yɛ Gyɛhova Alasevolɛ. Wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ewiade ne foa kpole ɛnnze duma ɛhye la, yɛva yɛzia yɛgua Baebolo ne anu wɔ ɛleka mɔɔ ɔfɛta ye la. Yɛnwunla ye dɔɔnwo kɛ bo 7,000 wɔ Baebolo mɔɔ le Ewiade Fofolɛ Ngilebɛbo ne ye kɛlata ne kpole anu.

MMA GYAA JEHOVAH ƐLƐ

17 Yɛwɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɛyɛ se mɔɔ ɛle seseɛ kɛ yɛbali nwo gyima nu, yemɔti nna nlehyee ɛhye mɔ bayɛ se amaa yɛ muala. (2 Tim. 3:1, 13) Yɛbahola yɛayia tebea nvonkyi gyɛne. Nyamenle ahyehyɛdeɛ ne bahola azie kpɔkɛ mɔɔ yɛnde ase kpalɛ. Anzɛɛ adiabaticadi bie anzɛɛ raaladi bie bahola amaa yɛade nganeɛ kɛ bɛnde yɛ ase anzɛɛ bɔbɔ yɛ nwo ayɛ yɛ ya. Yɛnrɛhola yɛnrɛnwu yɛ ngyegyelɛ ne anwo sɔwebɔ ndɛndɛ, anzɛɛ yɛnrɛnyianle mualɛ wɔ debie biala mɔɔ ɛha yɛ la anwo.

18 Ɔwɔ nuhwɛ kɛ ndenden ne ebie mɔ kɔhɔla kɔyɛ adwen nu basabasayɛlɛ dumaa, yɛkɔhɔla yɛkɔhɔ zolɛ yɛdi Jehovah nɔhɔlɛ sɛ yɛ gye di kɛ ɔdaye a ɔlɛ Bɔvolɛ ne, kɛ Baebolo ne lɛ ye Edwɛkɛ, na Jehovah Alasevolɛ noko lɛ ye menli. Kɛ mɔ Galatiabunli 4:24 kile la, sɔmfo Paul hanle ngyekyehyɛleɛ nwiɔ boni anwo edwɛkɛ?

◼ Abraham nee Sarah nee Hagar be ntamukulaa nzi no, sɔmafo Paul hanlɛ kɛ: “Eyi mɔ bɛbahola bɛava bɛayɛ nzunzonhwele; bɔbɔ mraalɛ eyi mɔ gyi ɛkɛ maa apam nwiɔ.” (Gal. 4:22-24) Mɔɔ limoa no, yɛdele ase kɛ apam nwiɔ ne a le Mmla apam ne nee apam fofolɛ ne. Noko akee, kɛ yɛneɛnleanle nu kpalɛ no, yɛdie yɛdi kɛ nzenzaleɛ nwo hyia. Ɔzɔho kɛ ɛnee Paul ɛnlɛkaha apam fofolɛ ne anwo edwɛkɛ, na emomu Abraham apam ne a. Fa ye adwenle kɔ ninyɛne bie mɔ azo.*

Pɔɔl vale aponkɛ baa Hagar totole Bepɔ Sainai anwo. (Gal. 4:25) Hagar gyinlanli Mmla ngyegyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ bɛvale bɛmanle wɔ Bepɔ Sainai anwo wɔ 1513 B.C.E. la agyakɛnu. (Ex. 19:5, 6) Dasanli mɔɔ ɛnli munli bie biala anla mɔɔ ɔbahola yeali Mmla zɔhane azo pɛpɛɛpɛ a, yemɔti ɔhakyele Dwuuma ne mɔ kɛ bɛle ɛtane nvonle. Ngyegyehyɛleɛ ɛhye bahola aboa Yizilayɛ maanlo ne amaa bɛanwu Mɛzaya ne, dasanli koonwu mɔɔ ɔbahola yeali Mmla ne azo pɛpɛɛpɛ la. Mɔɔ Mɛzaya ne vale ye ngoane mɔɔ di munli la bɔle afɔle la, ɔmaanle ɔyɛle bɔkɔbɔkɔ kɛ alesama mɔɔ ɛnli munli la bahola anya fanwodi avi ɛtane nee ewule nu. (Gal. 3:19, 24, 25) Mɔɔ Gyisɛse lile Nyamenle bodane nwo gyima wielɛ la, ɛnee Mmla ne ɛngyia bieko.—Rom. 10:4.

Paul lale Hagar le Sarah a mɔ, “baala nrenyia nu nli” mɔɔ wowole “wɔ anwobobole zo.” (Gal. 4:23) Saa anwobobole ne a le Abraham ngyegyenu ne. (Gal. 3:29; 4:28, 30) Wɔahyehyɛ nu wɔ Genesis 22:18 kɛ: “Wɔ [Abraham] asunma zo manman mɔɔ wɔ azɛlɛ ye azo amuala banyia anzonyehye amaa bɛ nwo ɔlua kɛ etie me neɛn nti.”

* Ɛhye tinli debie mɔɔ bɛyɛle ye wɔ Watchtower, March 15, 2006, mafa 10-12 nu la anwo.

Nkyekyemụ nọ bɔ bɛ nɛ Abraham yɛle la kɛhale anwoma nọ bɔ Jehovah yɛle wɔ Eden la anwo. (Gen. 3:15) Ɔhilele kɛ “asaso” nọ bɔ bɛhanle nwolɛ duma la kɛyɛ Abraham asaso. Paul hilehilele nu kɛ saa bɛka “asaso” ɛhye a, ɔfa Jesus Christ dodo. Na bɛdabɔ “bɛle Christ ɛdeɛ,” agyidifo 144,000 nɔ bɔ bɛ nɛ Jesus kɛli belemgbunli wɔ ye Belemgbunlililɛ nu la, boka mfɔnwoma asaso ɛhye anwo. (Gal. 3:16, 29; Rev. 14:1-3) Ninyɛne bɔ Nyamenle hyehyɛle kɛ ɔkɛyɛ kɛma Abraham la bamane kɛba nwolɛ nhyira dɔɔnwo kɛma bɛdabɔ bɛgye Jesus bɛto nu na bɛtie ye la.

Nzu ti a Paul vawale zɛnɛ “ayelɛ ne a ɛyɛ sɛnkyerɛnne”? Ɔkilele kɛ sɛ Yudafo mɔɔ wɔ ye mekɛ ne anu la kɔ zo fa Mela ne yɛ gyima a, bɛbadɛnla akɛlɛzonlenle nu kɛ Hagar la. Noko, sɛ bɛdie Abraham “awudilɛnli” kpole ne, Gyisɛse Kelaese bɛto nu a, bɛbanyia bɛ ti kɛ Sarah la. Bɛbanyia nɔhalɛ fanwodi bɛavi ɛtane mini ewule nu. (Dwɔn 8:32-34) Sɛ bɛandie Gyisɛse a, bɛnrɛnwu Mela ngyekyeleɛ ne bodane kpole ne, mɔɔ le kɛ ɔkɛhwe bɛ kɛhɔ Kelaese ɛkɛ la.

Galatiafo pii mɔɔ Pɔɔl kɛlɛɛ bɛ ngɛlata ne nwo a ntanle Yudafo, na bɛnla Mmla ne ase ɛlɛ. Yudafo bie mɔ mɔɔ bɛrayɛ Kilisienfo ne hanle kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ Amanrande Kilisienfo ne di Mmla ne azo, ninyɛne kɛ mɔɔ le twetia nwo mmla ne noko boka nwo. Pɔɔl hanle kɛ ɔyɛ ngodofo dwuma kɛ bɛba kɛ bɛbanyia Mmla ne “akɛlɛyɛlɛ” ne awolɛ, ɔluakɛ bɛva Kilisito “dimaa” ne bɛli dwuma kɛkɛ.—Gal. 5:1, 10-14.

NGYINGVIAMBO NGOBISABISA | 31

BAEBELE NƆHALƐDEƐ

Esenle ɛbɛla nwo asabu nwo edwɛkɛ

Baebolo ahengamvolɛ bele bɛti kɛ Gyisɛse nuhua ekyi a bɛdodo ye nuhua bie mɔ mɔɔ le amangyevolema. (Mak 1:16-20) Bɛde Galeli Bosopunu zoli tɛkɛ mɔɔ bɛvale asabu bɛlele anye.

Bɔbɔ ebowa nlanla nɛ, bɔbɔvowɔ asa anzɛɛ linen asanwa bɔbɔtu mbulu mbulu. Asanwa nɛ nu kpole gyi apate mɔ bɔbɔkpa kɛ bɔbɔye nɛ zoolɛ. Ebowa nkanra nɛ taa lɛ abobola anzɛɛ abo awolɛ kɛ bɔmaa bɔtɔ azule nɛ nu, berɛ mɔ ebowa akpole nɛ lɛ awolɛ wɔ ɔ bo na baka anzɛɛ kɔɔke ahunkpa wɔ ɔ ti zoolɛ.

Asawunɛ nli bɔbɔ sonle. Ɛbɔlɛ mɔ bɔ bɛwɔ po ne anye ne bɛkola bɛsɛi bɛ anzɛɛ enyiɛ kpole dɔɔnwo mɔ bɛhwe bɛ la anloa kola teketeke bɛ nu. (Luke 5:6) Baebolo ne ka kɛ James nee John lale “bɛsasie bɛ sawunɛ mɔ”—ɔbayɛ kɛ bɛvale panɛ nee linin asa la. (Matt. 4:21) Ɔda ali kɛ eponlekɛvoma ne bayɛ sasie eyi mɔ wɔ mekɛ mɔ bɛva bɛli gyima bieala la anzi. Luke eza kilehile nu kɛ eponlekɛvoma “kpulale bɛ sawunɛ mɔ nu” amaa bɛaye ngyikyi anzɛɛ ndɛtɛ mɔ bɔ bɛwɔ nu la bɛawi. (Luke 5:2) Ye anzi, eponlekɛvoma ne basɛnle bɛ sawunɛ ne mɔ amaa bɛawu—ɔbayɛ kɛ amaa bɛanvɔnvɔ.—Ezek. 47:10.

Gyisɛs vɛlɛle mbanyin mɔɔ bɛyɛle bɛ nzonlɛ kɛ bɛbava asau bɛali gyima la kɛ bɛrayɛ “menli kyevoe.”

Bulumu ɛhe ɛnla ɛtɔne nu. Bɛva bɛmaa kɛ ɔboka ewiade amuala Baebolo ngyehyele edwuma ne mɔɔ bɛfa ahonle kpalɛ ahyɛdeɛ bɛdi nwolɛ gyima la anwo. Saa ɛkulo kɛ ɛyɛ ahyɛdeɛ a, yɛsɛlɛ wɔ kɔ donate.jw.org.
