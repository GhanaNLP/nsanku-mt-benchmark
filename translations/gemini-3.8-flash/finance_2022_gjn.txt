MONDAY

The suffering brought about by the COVID-19 pandemic is something that has affected every country, including Ghana. The hardship it brought upon Ghanaians—causing the loss of lives, destruction of businesses, and the loss of jobs—is not something that the government took lightly. It gladdens my heart to say that the way we stood on our feet to fight this pandemic was a matter of courage and unity, which the whole world saw and praised. The government is pursuing new paths that will enable businesses to recover so that our economy can stand firm once again as it was before.

To wrap up, in order for us to find a lasting solution to this disease and the benefits that will come out of it, the Government led by H.E. President Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo has found a way to establish the GH₵ 100 billion COVID-19 Alleviation and Revitalization of Enterprises Support program, (GhanaCARES “Obaatan Pa” program). This brought emergency financial relief to support our people and helped our country stand back on its feet.

It is our hope that you will even support the Government to continue to overcome until we fully recover from the impact of this pandemic. Throughout this difficult time, our focus has been to ensure that our country emerges even stronger. As Isaiah said, “The bricks have fallen down, but we will rebuild with dressed stone; the fig trees have been felled, but we will replace them with cedars” (Isaiah 9:10)

It is the Government's responsibility to do everything that it has promised to do, so it will continue with its work, policies, and programs that will enable it to overcome the things that it was not able to do, in order to bring about a good life for all citizens.

The main theme of the 2022 World Youth Day, which marks the third edition, is creating spaces for employers that will be sustainable, provide access to youth throughout their lives, and offer opportunities to everyone, especially the youth. Government policies will be something that will allow them to include youth, recognize the importance of their names, and prepare all their energy to work, thereby supporting the creation of a good future that will bring benefits to everyone without anyone being left behind.

To enhance youth employment and access to capital in 2022, Government will roll out the YouStart programme to build capacity and provide funding for youth businesses to start or expand their businesses.
