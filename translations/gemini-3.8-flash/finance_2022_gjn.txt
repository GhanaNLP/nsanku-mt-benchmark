MONDAY

The severe hardship brought about by the COVID-19 pandemic is something that has affected every country, not just Ghana. The burden it has placed on Ghanaians, including the loss of lives, destruction of businesses, and job losses, is not something the government takes lightly. It has brought me deep pain, but I must say that the way we stood together and fought this pandemic was a matter of courage and unity that earned admiration and respect from the whole world. The government is taking new measures to help businesses resume so that our economy can stand strong as it did before.

I must add, in order for us to overcome this disease and the economic challenges emanating from it, the Government under H.E. President Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo has found a way to establish the GH₵ 100 billion COVID-19 Alleviation and Revitalisation of Enterprises Support programme, (GhanaCARES “Obaatan Pa” programme). This will bring about rapid economic transformation to empower our people and set the country on a path to sustained growth.

It has opened our eyes to how to manage government properly so that we can have power and come out of the poverty of this disease. In all this, our hope still stands that we can rebuild, because the nation is like something that fell into the mud. As Isaiah said, "The bricks have fallen, but we will rebuild with dressed stone; the sycamores have been cut down, but we will put cedars in their place" (Isaiah 9:10)

It is the Government's responsibility to do everything that it has promised to do, therefore it will continue with its work, policies and programs that will enable it to accomplish what it was unable to do, in order to bring good living conditions to all citizens.

One important thing that the 2022 World Youth Day, and indeed these three (3) days, signifies is to create a business environment that is supportive and capable of continuously bringing youth-oriented ideas and creating jobs from all fronts for everyone, especially young people. Government policy will be geared toward encouraging young people to break their mental limitations and commit all their energies to work to create a better future where everyone's livelihood is guaranteed and without poverty.

In order to support the youth in training and to obtain capital from employers, in the 2022 Budget, the Government will begin the YouStart program to pave the way for providing financial assistance for youth businesses so that they can start or expand their businesses.
