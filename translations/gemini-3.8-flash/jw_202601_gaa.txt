SOMETHING YOU CAN LEARN

Visualize the Bible Stories

The events spoken of in the Bible really happened, and you can learn a lot from them. If you visualize the stories you read in the Bible, it will help you understand them well and also benefit from them greatly. Here are some things that will help you visualize the accounts you read in the Bible.

• Read aloud. If you read the Bible aloud, it will help you better visualize what you are reading. When you are reading the Bible with your family, you can assign the characters in the account you are reading to each other. This will help you better visualize the story.

• Visualize the scene. Consider what might have been going on in the minds of the people you are reading about, and how they felt? For example, you might ask yourself: ‘Why did this person say what he said, or do what he did? If I were in this situation, what would be going on in my mind, and how would I feel?’

• Draw a picture of the story. Even if you do not know how to draw very well, but you try your best to draw a picture of how the events happened, it will help you visualize what took place, and you will remember the story well.
