iv | Ghana Aban - 2021 Manfo Sika Nhyehyɛe

Asɛmti: “Sikasɛm Nyan Nsoamfoɔ Denam Wieyɛ, Nkonsonkonsonmuhyɛ & Ntoasoɔ So”

NKRA A EFIRE SIKASƐM SOAFOƆ HƆ

Ghanafoɔ pa no too aba wɔ Ɔpɛnimba 2020 mu de maa Ɔmanpanyin, Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo bio sɛ ɔntena ahengua no so berɛ foforɔ bio mfa ntoa adwuma pa no so na ɔnyɛ pii nka ho. Ahotosoɔ a yɛn Ghanafoɔ nuanom de ahyɛ saa Aban yi mu no yɛ adeɛ a yɛmfa nni agorɔ koraa.

2021 Sikakora Nhyehyɛe no yɛ nhyehyɛe kɛse a ɛsesa yɛn man sika kwan sɛnea ɛbɛbɔ nkurɔfo nkwa ho ban, ama nnwuma a asɛe no anya nkwa bio, na ama yɛde yɛn ahoɔden nyinaa asi hɔ pefee sɛ yɛbɛkyekyere Ghana a ɛyɛ papa bio, wɔ COVID-19 akyi. Sikakora Nhyehyɛe yi gyina yɛn anisoadehunu a ɛne sɛ yɛbɛsesa ɔman yi na yɛasi sikasɛm a etumi gyina n'ankasa nan so.

Wɔ bere a ɛdi kan no fa kɛseɛ no mu no, yɛama sikasɛm no asan agyina ne nan so, ɛnam nhyehyɛeɛ a yɛayɛ no yiye a ɛfa sikasɛm a ɛkɔ akyiri nsakraeɛ ho, akwan a ɛma sika kɔ anim a yɛde baeɛ, nneyɛeɛ a ɛma nkɔsoɔ ba a yɛde yɛɛ adwuma, ankorankorɛ nnwuma a yɛboaa wɔn, ne asetra mu mmoa nhyehyɛeɛ a yɛde baeɛ no so. Eduu 2019 awieeɛ no, na yɛwɔ sikasɛm mu nsakraeɛ kwan so kɛseɛ esiane sikasɛm mu nsɛnkyerɛnne a ɛyɛ nwanwa ne amanaman ntam ahotosoɔ a ɛkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ ɔman a ɛrekɔ anim nti, bere a COVID-19 yarebɔne no bɛbɔɔ yɛn no. Saa yarebɔne yi nsɛnnennen kɛseɛ no, wiase sikasɛm nyinaa ate nka, a Ghana nso ka ho.

Covid-19 Ɔyaredɔm no baeɛ no de ɔhaw pii bae a ɛkaa yɛn nnwuma ne yɛn da biara asetena. Nanso, Aban ahunu sɛnea ɔyaredɔm yi aka yɛn sɛ ɔmanfoɔ no yie pa ara, na wayɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ a ɛbɛboa ama nnwuma asɔre bio na yɛn asetena asan aba tebea pa mu. Anidasoɔ nyinaa nsaee ɛfiri sɛ yɛanya akwan foforɔ a yɛfa so yɛ nnoɔma a ɛwɔ anidasoɔ a ɛnni ano. Aban no asi ne bo sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ ama yɛanya ɔmanfoɔ a wɔwɔ apɔmuden, enti wayɛ nhyehyɛeɛ sɛ Ghanani biara bɛnya paninbɔ aduro no bi ama yɛn nyinaa ayɛ den de akyekyere Ghana a yɛpɛ sɛ yɛhunu no.

Yɛne mo abɔ mu ayɛ adwuma mpo wɔ ɔhaw ahorow mu sɛ yɛn nkɔsoɔ nhyehyɛeɛ fa baako, na yɛatu anammɔn a ɛyɛ kɛseɛ de yɛn sikasɛm asan akɔ nkɔsoɔ kwan so. Ɛnnɛ, bɛyɛ Ghanafoɔ 1.2 million betumi anya ntoasoɔ sukuu adesua a ɛyɛ papa na ɛwɔ su pa ama wɔn abrabɔ, yɛasiesie sikakorabea dwumadibea no na adwumayɛfoɔ dodoɔ no ara anya wɔn sika a wɔde kɔtoo sikakorabea a ɛhwee ase no mu no. Bio nso, yɛasi beaeɛ a wɔpam kar na yɛahunu kar a wɔapam wɔ ha a ɛdi kan no wɔ yɛn dwa ne yɛn akwan so.

Yɛgye tom nokorɛm sɛ yɛhyiaa nsɛnnennen bi berɛ a yɛbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ yɛbɛma yɛn nkɔsoɔ botaeɛ anya nkɔanim no. Aban de ahokeka rehyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ahorow ase de asiesie saa haw ahorow yi na ama adwumakuo ahorow anya ahomeka. Sɛ nhwɛsoɔ no, yɛde GH¢750 million CAP-BuSS Dwumadie no too dwa wɔ Kɔtonnim 2020 mu na yei ama Nnwuma Nketewa, Nketenkete ne Emu Ntam Nnwuma (MSMEs) ne nnwuma afoforɔ bebree anya mmoa.

Sɛnea ɛnsii da no, wɔfrɛ yɛn sɛ yɛnka yɛn nsa mmɔ mu na yɛnyɛ yɛn man no yie denam nkabom ne akokoduru nhyehyɛe ahorow a yɛde bɛkɔ so adi dwuma no so na ama yɛanya nkɔso ama obiara. Ne nyinaa akyi no, yɛahyɛ yɛn ho bɔ sɛ yɛde yɛn ahoɔden nyinaa ne yɛn koma nyinaa bɛsom Ghana.

Onyankopɔn nhyira yɛn nyinaa.

Ghana nkwa so!

Ken Ofori-Atta Sikasɛm Soafoɔ

v | Ghana Aban - 2021 Ɔman Mma Budget

NKYERƐKYERƐMU AHYƐASEƐ

1. 2021 Manfrani Budget no yɛ nea Aban no nyae wɔ 2020 mu ne sika dodow a ɔpɛ sɛ ɔboaboa ano wɔ sika a ɛba mu de di nhyehyɛe ne dwumadi ahorow so wɔ 2021 mu ho tɔfatoa. 2021 Budget no asɛmti ne “Completion, Consolidation, and Continuation”. Saa asɛmti yi gyina asɛm no so sɛ mfe pii a atwam no mu no, Budget foforo biara kura adwuma foforo pii a ɛsɛ sɛ wofi ase a ɛsɛe nnwuma a ɛrekɔ so no. Ɛnyɛ sɛ eyi de nhyɛso ba yɛn sikasɛm so nko, na mmom abakɔsɛm mu no ama akyireka ba akatua mu, ma wɔantumi antua apatafo pii ka wɔ bere a ɛsɛ mu wɔ adwuma a wɔayɛ ho. Eyi ma nnwuma no nyinaa ka kɔ soro efisɛ akatua a wɔtwentwɛn so no ma mfaso ka ba so.

2. Wɔ afe 2021 mu no, Aban yɛ nhyehyɛe sɛ ɔbɛfa kwan foforo so. Botae no ne sɛ wɔbɛtwa dwumadi ahorow a wɔanwie ho su no aso denam bɔhyɛ ahorow a wɔbɛdi so de awie dwumadi ahorow a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw no so.

SIKASƐM HO NKƆSOƆ WƆ 2020 MU

Sikasɛm Mu Nsunsuansoɔ Kɛseɛ

3. Sikasɛm no dwumadie ho nsɛm a yɛanya aka abɔnten wɔ afe 2020 mu no te sɛ nea ɛdidisoɔ yi:

• Afe no mu abosome nkron a ɛdi kan no nyaa GDP nkɔsoɔ kɛseɛ a ɛyɛ 0.2 ɔha biara mu. Wɔhwɛ kwan sɛ GDP nkɔsoɔ bɛduru 0.9 ɔha biara mu ansa na afe no bɛba awieeɛ;

• Non-Oil real GDP nyaa nkɔsoɔ a ne kɛseɛ yɛ ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 0.4 wɔ afe no abosome nkron a ɛdi kan no mu, na wɔhwɛ kwan sɛ ɛbɛwie afe no wɔ ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 1.6;

• Ɔpɛnimaa awieeɛ no na ɛhɔnom nnoɔma boɔ a ɛkɔɔ soro no yɛ ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 10.4 sɛ yɛde toto botaeɛ a yɛsesaa no a ɛyɛ ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 11.0 no ho a;

• Sika a aka a ɛbɔɔ kɔ no nyinaa wɔ sika pɔtee a wɔde di dwuma so yɛɛ GDP no mu ɔha mu nkyekyem 11.7 berɛ a na wɔahyehyɛ sɛ ɛbɛyɛ GDP no mu ɔha mu nkyekyem 11.4;

• Primary balance no kyerɛɛ deficit a ɛyɛ GDP no mu ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 5.3 berɛ a na wɔahyehyɛ deficit a ɛyɛ GDP no mu ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 4.6; na

• Gross International Reserves yɛɛ US$8.6 billion, a ɛne nneɛma a yɛde ba ɔman mu no bosome 4.1 ka yɛ pɛ.

• Apɔmuden adwumayɛfoɔ dodoɔ a wɔfa wɔn adwumam a ɛkɔɔ soro, sika foforɔ a wɔde ma apɔmuden adwumayɛfoɔ a wɔwɔ anim, ne asraafoɔ mpanyimfoɔ ahyɛnsoroma mfeɛ a wɔde kɔ pɛnhyen a ɛkɔɔ soro wɔ Adwumayɛfoɔ Akatua ase;

• COVID-19 sika a wɔsɛee no wɔ Nneɛma ne Ɔsom a Wɔde Di Dwuma ase; ne

Ɔman Ka Nsakraeɛ Wɔ 2020 Mu

7. Ɛkame ayɛ sɛ Ɔpɛnimma 2020 awieeɛ no, na ɛka a ɛda hɔ no nyinaa yɛ GH¢291.6 billion (US$50.8 billion), a ɛyɛ GDP no mu ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 76.1. Saa nkyekyɛmu yi ka sika a Aban boaboaa ano de tuaa sikakorabea dwumadibea no siesie ne ahoɔden dwumadibea Independent Power Purchase (IPPs) ahoɔden ho ka no ho. Sɛ yɛyi sikakorabea dwumadibea no siesie sika no firi mu a, na 2020 ɛka no yɛ GDP no mu ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 71.5. Sɛnea yɛhyehyɛɛ ɛka no nyinaa nie:

• Abɔnten so ka: GH¢141.7 billion (US$24.7 billion) a ɛgyina hɔ ma ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 48.6 wɔ ɛka a ɛwɔ hɔ nyinaa mu; na

• Ɔman mu aka: GH¢149.8 billion (US$26.1 billion) a ɛyɛ aka nyinaa mu ɔha nkyekyem 51.4.

2021 SIKASƐM NHYEHYƐEƐ NE SIKASƐM NO

Sikasɛm Nsusuwii Akɛse a Wɔde Asi Wɔn Ani So Ma 2021

8. Aban no sikasɛm nhyehyɛe ne nneyɛe (sɛ ebia towtua ne gyinaesi a ɛfa sika a wɔsɛe ho) ka sikasɛm no nkɔso wɔ mfe kakra a ɛreba no mu. 2021 Sikakora Nhyehyɛe (Budget) no gyina sikasɛm mu botae ahorow anaa nsɛnkyerɛnne a edidi so yi so:

• Real GDP nyin nyinaa bɛyɛ 5.0 ɔha mu nkyekyem;

• Angwa a ɛnka ho Real GDP nyini a ɛyɛ ɔha biara mu nkyekyem 6.7;

• Ɔpɛnimma awieeɛ nneɛma boɔ akorɔn a ɛyɛ 8.0 percent;

• Sikakorabea kɛseɛ no ka a ɛyɛ 9.5 percent wɔ GDP mu;

• GDP mu ɔha nkyekyɛmu 1.3 mfitiaseɛ ka; ne

• Gross International Reserves a ɛbɛtumi atua nnoɔma ne ɔsom a yɛde firi amannɔne ba no bɔbor asram 4.0 ka.
