iv | Ghana Aban - 2021 Manfo Sika Nhyehyɛe

NKRA A EFIRI SIKA KƆKƆƆ SOAFOƆ HƆ

Ghanafoɔ pa no too aba wɔ December 2020 mu sɛ wɔde Ɔmampanyin, Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo bɛba bio mprɛnsa so ama watoa adwuma pa no so na wayɛ pii aka ho. Gyidi a yɛn nuanom Ghanafoɔ de ahyɛ Aban yi mu no yɛ adeɛ a yɛmfa nni agorɔ koraa.

2021 Sikakora Nhyehyɛe no yɛ nhyehyɛe soronko a ɛsiesie yɛn man sika kwan so de bɔ nkwa ho ban, ma nnwuma a asɛe no san ba nkwa mu, na ɛde yɛn ahoɔden nyinaa si Ghana a ɛyɛ papa sene kane no si bio so, wɔ COVID-19 akyi. Sikakora Nhyehyɛe no gyina yɛn anisoadehunu a ɛne sɛ yɛbɛsakra ɔman yi na yɛasi sikasɛm a ɛtumi gyina ne nan so no so.

Bere a ɛfa yɛn berɛ a edi kan no fa kɛseɛ no ho no, yɛama sikasɛm no asan agyina ne nan so, denam nhyehyɛe pɔtee a wɔayɛ no yie a ɛfa sikasɛm mu nsakraeɛ kɛseɛ ho no so, yɛde akwan foforɔ a ɛbɛma sika a yɛnya no akɔ anim aba, yɛde nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛbɛma nkɔsoɔ aba adi dwuma, yɛaboa ankorankoro adwumakuo ahorow, na yɛayɛ asetra mu mmoa dwumadie ahorow. Eduu 2019 awieeɛ no, na yɛwɔ sikasɛm mu nsakraeɛ kwan so denneennen esiane sikasɛm kɛseɛ agyinapɛn a ɛyɛ anigyeɛ ne amanaman ntam nkamfo sɛ ɔman a ɛrekɔ anim nti, berɛ a COVID-19 yarebɔne kɛseɛ no bɔɔ yɛn no. Saa yarebɔne yi nsɛnnennen kɛseɛ no aka sikasɛm nyinaa wɔ wiase afanan nyinaa, a Ghana ka ho.

Covid-19 Yareɛ kɛseɛ no baeɛ no de ɔhaw pii bae a ɛkaa yɛn nnwuma ne yɛn da biara asetena. Nanso, aban no de awerɛhoɔ kɛseɛ ahunu sɛnea yareɛ kɛseɛ no aka yɛn sɛ ɔmanfoɔ, na wayɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ a ɛbɛtumi aboa ama nnwuma asɔre bio na ama yɛn asetena asan aba tebea pa mu. Yɛn anidasoɔ nyinaa nsaee ɛfiri sɛ yɛanya akwan foforɔ a yɛbɛfa so ayɛ nneɛma a anidasoɔ pii wom. Aban no ayɛ n’adwene sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ ama ɔmanfoɔ anya apɔmuden, enti wayɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ama Ghanafoɔ anya aduro a wɔde bɔ wɔn ho ban no bi na ama yɛn nyinaa ayɛ den de akyekyere Ghana a yɛpɛ sɛ yɛhunu no.

Yɛne mo abɔ mu ayɛ adwuma mpo wɔ ɔhaw ahorow mu sɛ yɛn mpuntuo kwan no fa baako, na yɛatu anammɔn a akokoduru wom de yɛn sikasɛm asan akɔ mpuntuo kwan pa so. Seesei, bɛyɛ Ghanafoɔ 1.2 million betumi anya ntoaso sukuu adesua a ɛyɛ na ɛwɔ su pa de abɔ wɔn bra, yɛasiesie sikakorabea adwuma no mu ma adetɔfo dodoɔ no ara nsa aka wɔn sika a na ɛwɔ sikakorabea ahorow a ɛbubu guiɛ no mu. Bio nso, yɛasi beae a wɔhyehyɛ kar na yɛahunu kar a wɔahyehyɛ no mfitiaseɛ deɛ no wɔ yɛn dwam ne yɛn akwan so.

Yɛgye tom nokware mu sɛ yɛhyiaa nsɛnnennen bi berɛ a yɛbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ yɛbɛma yɛn nkɔsoɔ botaeɛ anya nkɔanim no. Aban no de ahokeka rehyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ahorow ase de asiesie saa ɔhaw ahodoɔ yi na adeɛ ayɛ mmerɛ ama nnwuma. Sɛ ebia, yɛde GH¢750 ɔpepepem CAP-BuSS Dwumadie no baeɛ wɔ Ɔbɛnem 2020 mu na yei ama Micro, Small and Medium-Sized Enterprises (MSMEs) ne afoforo pii anya mmoa.

Sɛdeɛ ɛnsii da no, wɔrefrɛ yɛn sɛ yɛnka yɛn ho mmɔ mu na yɛnyɛ yɛn man no yie denam nkabom ne nhyehyɛe akɛseɛ a yɛde bɛba ama obiara anya nkɔsoɔ no a yɛbɛtoa so ayɛ no so. Ne nyinaa akyi no, yɛahyɛ bɔ sɛ yɛde yɛn ahoɔden ne yɛn akoma nyinaa bɛsom Ghana.

Onyankopɔn nhyira yɛn nyinaa.

Nkwa tenten mma Ghana!

Ken Ofori-Atta Sikasɛm Soafoɔ

NNHIANIM

1. 2021 Ɔman Mma Budget no yɛ nea Aban no nyae wɔ 2020 ne sika dodoɔ a ɔpɛ sɛ ɔboaboa ano wɔ sika a ɔnya mu de di nhyehyɛeɛ ne dwumadie ahodoɔ so wɔ 2021 no ntotoho tiawa. 2021 Budget no asɛmti ne “Wieeɛ, Ahyɛden, ne Ntoasoɔ”. Saa asɛmti yi gyina asɛm nokorɛ a ɛne sɛ mfeɛ a atwam no mu no, Budget foforɔ biara kura adwuma foforɔ pii a wɔrebɛhyɛ aseɛ na agya adwuma a ɛrekɔ so no akyi. Ɛnyɛ sɛ eyi de nhyɛsoɔ gu yɛn sika so nko ara, na mmom ɛnam abakɔsɛm kwan so nso ama aka a ɛwɔ sɛ wɔtua no aka akyi, a ɛma adwumawuranom pii nnya wɔn sika wɔ berɛ ano mfa nsi adwuma a wɔayɛ no so. Eyi ma dwumadie ahodoɔ no nyinaa boɔ kɔ soro efisɛ akatua a wɔtwentwɛn soɔ no de mfɛntom ka ho ba.

2. Wɔ afe 2021 mu no, Aban yɛ nhyehyɛe sɛ ɔbɛfa kwan foforo so. Botae no ne sɛ wɔbɛtwa adwuma a wɔnwie ho amanne no aso denam bɔhyɛ a ɛne sɛ wɔbɛwie nnwuma a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw no so dua so.

Sikasɛm Mu Nsunsuansoɔ Wɔ Afe 2020 Mu

Sikasɛm Mu Nsunsuanso Kɛseɛ

3. Nnipa kakra bi nkontabuo a ɛfa sikasɛm no kɔ anim wɔ afe 2020 mu no te sɛnea ɛdidi so yi:

• Afe no nansoaa nkron a edi kan no kyerɛɛ GDP nyin mpaapaamu nyinaa a ɛyɛ ɔha biara mu nkyekyem 0.2. Wɔhwɛ kwan sɛ afe no awiei no, GDP nyin no bɛduru ɔha biara mu nkyekyem 0.9;

• Non-Oil real GDP nyaa nkɔsoɔ a ne kɛseɛ yɛ ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 0.4 wɔ afe no abosome nkron a ɛdi kan no mu, na wɔhwɛ kwan sɛ ɛbɛwie afe no wɔ ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 1.6;

• Ɔpɛnimma awieeɛ nneɛma boɔ kɔ soro yɛɛ 10.4 percent sɛ yɛde toto botaeɛ a wɔasesa a ɛyɛ 11.0 percent no ho a;

• Sika a ɛka baa sikasɛm nhyehyɛe no mu nyinaa wɔ sika a ɛkɔe ne nea ɛbaee so no yɛɛ GDP no mu ɔha nkyekyem 11.7 sen botae a wɔsiesiee no a na ɛyɛ GDP no mu ɔha nkyekyem 11.4;

• Primary balance no kyerɛɛ deficit a ɛyɛ GDP no mu ɔha nkyekyɛmu 5.3 berɛ a na wɔahyehyɛ deficit a ɛyɛ GDP no mu ɔha nkyekyɛmu 4.6; na

• Gross International Reserves yɛɛ US$8.6 billion, a ɛne abosome 4.1 a wɔde tɔ nnoɔma firi amannɔne sika yɛ pɛ.

5. Eduu Ɔpɛnimaa 2020 awieeɛ no, na Aban abɔ ka a ne nyinaa yɛ GH¢100.1 billion wɔ Nkaabɔ nyinaa mu (a aka a wɔtuaeɛ ka ho), a ɛne botaeɛ GH¢97.7 billion na ɛsii anana mu. Nsɔhudee ne Nkaabɔ afoforɔ kɔɔ so yie wɔ botaeɛ no mu. Nkaabɔ nkaeɛ nyinaa boro botaeɛ a wɔde sii hɔ maa afe 2020 no so, sɛdeɛ ɛda adi wɔ Figure 2 mu no. • COVID-19 ho sika a wɔsɛee no wɔ Nneɛma ne Ɔsom a Wɔde Di Dwuma ase; na

• COVID-19 nnoɔma a wɔasiesie a ɛfa ho adwuma a ɛwɔ Sika a Wɔde Yɛ Nnoɔma Akɛseɛ Ase.

Ɔman No Ka Nkɔsoɔ ma 2020

7. Ɛkɔpem Ɔpɛnimaa 2020 awieeɛ no, na ɛka a ɛda hɔ nyinaa yɛ GH¢291.6 ɔpepepem (US$50.8 ɔpepepem), a ɛgyina hɔ ma GDP mu nkyekyɛmu 76.1. Saa nkyekyɛmu yi ka sika a Aban boaboaa ano de tuaa sika afa mu mmoa (financial sector bailout) ne ahoɔden afa mu Independent Power Purchase (IPPs) ahoɔden ho ka ho. Sɛ yɛyi sika afa mu mmoa no firi mu a, na afe 2020 ɛka a ɛda hɔ no yɛ GDP mu nkyekyɛmu 71.5. Sɛdeɛ wɔahyehyɛ ɛka no teɛ nie:

• Abɔnten so ɛka: GH¢141.7 ɔpepepem (US$24.7 ɔpepepem) a ɛgyina hɔ ma ɔha nkyekyɛmu 48.6 wɔ ɛka a ɛwɔ hɔ nyinaa mu; na

• Ɔman mu bosea: GH¢149.8 billion (US$26.1 billion) a ɛgyina hɔ ma bosea nyinaa dodoɔ mu ɔha nkyekyɛmu 51.4.

2021 SIKASEM NE SIKASƐM TEBEA

Sikasɛm Mu Botaeɛ Akɛseɛ a Wɔde Asi Hɔ ma 2021

8. Aban no sikasɛm nhyehyɛe ne nneyɛe (a ɛne tow ne gyinaesi a ɛfa sika a wɔsɛe ho) ka sikasɛm nkɔso wɔ mfe kakra a ɛreba yi mu. 2021 Bɔnkete no gyina sikasɛm akɛse botae anaa nsɛnkyerɛnne a ɛdidiso yi so:

• Real GDP nyin nyinaa bɛyɛ 5.0 percent;

• Anguantɔ Real GDP nyini a ɛnyɛ ngo deɛ a ɛyɛ ɔha nkyekyɛmu 6.7;

• Ɔpɛnimma awieeɛ nneɛma boɔ korɔ kɔpem 8.0 ɔha nkyekyɛmu;

• Sikakorabea kɛseɛ no ka a ɛbɔɔeɛ yɛ GDP no mu ɔha nkyekyɛmu 9.5;

• GDP mu ɔha biara nkyekyem 1.3 mfifiee ka; ɛna

• Gross International Reserves a ɛbɛtumi atua nneɛma ne ɔsom a yɛde firi amannɔne ba no nyinaa ka a ɛnsua koraa nsen abosome 4.0.

Sika a Ɛba Ho Nhyehyɛe

10. Aban de sika a wɔboaboa ano nhyehyɛe pii aba wɔ afe 2021 Nkontaabu (Budget) no mu sɛdeɛ ɛbɛma nkɔsoɔ atoto ne kwan ntɛmntɛm na ama COVID-19 ɔyaredɔm no sunsum a ɛbɔɔ yɛn so no so ate. Saa sika a wɔboaboa ano nhyehyɛe ahodoɔ yi ne yeinom:

• COVID-19 Apɔmuden Toɔ a ɛyɛ ɔha biara nkyekyɛmu baako nkabom wɔ National Health Insurance Levy mu ne ɔha biara nkyekyɛmu baako nkabom wɔ VAT Flat Rate mu;
