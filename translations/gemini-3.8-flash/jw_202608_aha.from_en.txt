ADWE M’ENYI Mma mma kwan ma Satan ɛbɛbɔ ye mɔden kɛ ɔkyeakyea Gyihova Nyamenle nee Gyisɛse Kelaese anyi.

“Besisi Abɔnsam amũa, nɛ ɔkɔnri be nwo nri.”—JAS. 4:7.

EBƆ mɔ yɛsi yɛ ti anwo kɛ yɛkɔsom Gyihova la, Satan nɛɛ ye adebɔnsamfo noko bayɛ yɛ atamfofo mɔ bɛmbu mgbɔlɔka. Nza Satan ze kɛ ɔntumi yɛ nwo anwo mɛla berɛ mɔ Gyihova boa yɛ la. Ɔti Ɔbonsam bɔ mɔden kɛ ɔkɔsɛkye mboa nina na yamaa yava yɛ kɛzi yamaa Gyihova. Ɔdua aden kɔngɔ so bɔ mɔden kɛ ɔkɔma yɛanya adwen kɛ Gyihova nɛɛ ye Ra nina nni yɛ afoa.—2 Kɔr. 11:14; Gye. 1:6.

2 Ɔkõ kpalɛ ne anu, ɔwɔ kɛ asorfo kɔzo nya bɛ mpanyimfo nu awaredie. Saa bɛminli awaredie zɔho a, bɛkɔhɔla bɛkɛyɛ bɛ ti anwo debie. Ɛhye kɔhɔla kɛma bɛkɛminli bɛ ngoane. Zɔhane ala noko a yɛle asorfo wɔ sunsum nu kɔne kpalɛ nu tia “sunsum nu amumuyɛfo tumi ne mɔ.” (Eph. 6:12) Abonsam Satan ze kɛ saa yɛminli awaredie mɔɔ yɛwɔ ye wɔ Gyihova Nyamenle nee Gyisɛse Kilisito nu a, ɔda ali kɛ yɛkɛpo bɛ adehilelɛ nee bɛ mmoa. Ɔti ɔyɛ debie biala mɔɔ ɔkɔhɔla la amaa yɛ awaredie mɔɔ yɛwɔ ye wɔ bɛ nu la anye zo aho teketeke. Wɔ edwɛkɛ-edendɛ ɛhye anu, yɛkɛnwu kɛzi ɔvale adwenle ɛhye ɔlile gyima wɔ tete ne nee kɛzi ɔkɔzo ɔfa ɔdi gyima ɛnɛ la. Akee yɛkɛzuzu kɛzi yɛkɔhɔla yɛkɛsi ye nwo adenle la anwo. Wɔ edwɛkɛ-edendɛ mɔɔ kɛli ɛhye anzi la anu, yɛkɛnwu ninyɛne nwiɔ bieko mɔɔ Satan fa di gyima tia yɛ wɔ yɛ sunsum nu kɔne ne anu.*

* Ɔnsonsɔn nnyɛ sɔhane kɛ ɔfa sɔhwɛ mɔɔ yɛkɔ nu nwonkye nwonkye ba yɛ nwo do. Na ɔdaye a ɔwɔ “ewiade ye sunsum ne” mɔɔ ka atiafo kɛ bɛyɛ Nyamenle menli ne amaneɛ la akyi a. Ɛhye ati, sɛ ɔfa nwonkye nwonkye ba oo, anzɛɛ ɔnva nwonkye nwonkye amba oo, Ɔnsonsɔn a ɔwɔ sɔhwɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ yɛkɔ nu wɔ yɛ sunsum nu konle ne anu la akyi a.—1 Kɔl. 2:12; 1 Dwɔn 5:19.

SATAAN BƆLE GYIHOVA NEE GYISƐSE BƆLƐ

3 Dasani mɔ ne abakɔsɛm mɔ mɔbɔ mɔ, Satan yeli nkan dɛ ɔkɛtwɛ Adam nye Hawa kɔvi Gyihova nwo. Satan seseli Hawa man ɔliele lili dɛ yɛ Bɔvolɛ ne anlili ye kpalɛ mɔ nwo adwenle kpalɛ. Satan bizale ye dɛ: “Nyamenle ka dɛ bɛmmadi paka ne mɔ mɔ wɔ toka ne anu kɔsba mɔ ne bie biɛ?” (Gyn. 3:1-5) Satan man Hawa liele lili dɛ Gyihova ɛlɛfa mmla mɔ ɛnnli munli ato ɔ nye ye kunli ne nwo. Nyane sesei nu, Hawa nye akyi Adam valili bɛ kpolevolɛ Satan afoa. Bɛkpole Gyihova—Mɔ ɔvale debie biala ɔmanle bɛ ne.—Gyn. 3:6.

4 Ɔfiri Adam nee Hawa mekɛ zo, Abonsam akɔ zo asɛi Gyihova duma. Kɛ neazo, Abonsam hanle Hiob agɔnwolɛma mɔɔ ɛnle nɔhalɛfoɔ no hyɛle kɛ bɛmaa Hiob adwenle anye zo kɛ Gyihova kulo ye na ɔdi ye nɔhalɛlilɛ no anwo eni. (Hiob 22:1-3) Abonsam dwonle kɛ ɔti kɛ ninyɛne nwiɛnweɛ kɛdo Hiob no ɔbamaa Hiob akpo Gyihova. Ɔwɔ nuhua kɛ Hiob yiale ngyegyelɛ kpole firi Abonsam agyedzi nu ɛdeɛ, noko saa nɔhalɛnli he lile ye nɔhalɛlilɛ no eni, na wammaye ye agɔnwolɛvalɛ mɔɔ ɔnee Nyamenle le no anwo adwenle kɔkɔkɔ.—Hiob 27:1, 5.

5 Mfe ahahanli ndɛ̃ kɛ Jizɔs rale aze ne, Satan bɔle mɔdɛn kɛ ɔkɛkpata menle kɛ bɛmmali Jizɔs nzi. Adenle foko mɔɔ ɔluale zo ɔyɛle ɛhye la a le kɛ, ɔtutule asɔrenuma ne mɔ mɔɔ kyi ye ne nee menle gyɛne kɛ bɛha atɔfolɛ wɔ Jizɔs nwo. Na awieleɛ kolaa ne, bɛhunle ye kɛ awudifo bɔbɔ la.—Kenga

Mateo 11:19; 27:27-31; Maake 14:55, 56; Dwɔn 8:41-45.

6 Wɔ Gyisɛs ewule nzi no, Seetan hakyile ye adwene kɔle Gyisɛs aliɛfo mɔ gyima ne anwo. Mɔɔ limoa no, Abonsam bɔle mɔden kɛ ɔkɛma bɛkɛnye akyinnye wɔ Gyisɛs ɛwɔlɔsowale ne anwo. Wɔ Seetan ye nsunzuyan bo no, atotoo edwɛkɛ mɔɔ bɛhanle yɛle kɛ Gyisɛs ɛdoavolɛ mɔ ɛva ye fɔnwolɛ ne ɛhɔ. (Matt. 28:11-15) Akee wɔ kenle mɔɔ doole zɔ no anu, Seetan bɔle mɔden kɛ ɔdua anwosisi zo yeasesa asafo ne. Ɛnee Seetan kulo kɛ ɔma gyima mɔɔ bɛ Menle ne ɛva ɛhyɛ bɛ no yɛ se somaa ma bɛ kɛ bɛkɛli zo.—Acts 7:57–8:1; 12:1-3; 28:22.

7 Ɔwɔ nuhũ kɛ mɔfʋlɛ Kilisinyemɔ bie mɔ gyae asɛŋkpa ne ɛha fɛlɛfɛlɛ ɛyɛlɛ ne ti dɛɛ, mɔ dɔɔnwo gyinli bɛ ti nzi na bɛampɛ dɛ bɛkɛyɛ koonw. Bɛnwunli kɛ Sitan nɩɩ amunwuma mɔ a le bɛ kpɔfʋlɛ paa. (Asomafo 4:18-20; 1 Pita 5:8) Na nnɛ nlo? Sitan lɛfa ahonle nuhũ adwenle boni mɔ bedzi dwuma dɛ ɔkɛfa yeabʋ yɛ abawu na yaangonwʋ Gyihova?

Satan ye ewiade yi fa ninyɛne mɔɔ sɛkye Nyamenle ngyinlazo la tua yɛ do (Nea ɛdendɛmunli 11)

yɛ Selɛ nne Ba ne dɛ bɛsɛ. Ɛhye ti, menli ta kpondɛ kɛ bɛbu Selɛ ne anyi, mɔɔ bɛnze ye duma ne la, na bɛsɔnle Ba ne. Gyihova nne Gyisɛse amuala ɛnlie asɔrenle zɛhae ɛndo nu. (Matt. 4:10; John 4:23) Saa menli anyi fura wɔ adenle zɛhae azo a, ɔbaahola wayɛ se kɛ yɛbazo bɛ adwenle anzɛɛ bɛbahola bɛadwazo bɛatia yɛ asɛnka edwuma ne mɔɔkɔ.

9 Saa yɛhyɛ agɔnwalɛ bo a, deɛ mɔɔ yɛdi kan yɛ a le kɛ yɛbaze ahenle duma. Satan kpondɛ kɛ ɔsi menli adenle na bɛamma bɛ nye anlie duma kɛ bɛbanyia agɔnwalɛ kpalɛ bɛawo Gyihova, ɔlua mɔɔ ɔnsesa kɛ bɛnze ye duma noko koraa. Ɔti, Satan eva asɔre mɔɔ le amumuɛ ali gyima dɔɔnwo amaa menli ali Nyamenle duma awole. (Kenga Gyɛlemaya 23:26, 27.) Ɔkpɔvolɛ ne mɔɔ le ɛhye mɔ mɔɔ Baebolo ne fa toto aban bodane mɔɔ le bodane mɔɔ bɔ yɛ nwo ban nwo la, yeha ye kɛ bɛye Nyamenle duma noko bɛvi Baebolo ngakyile dɔɔnwo nu. (Mrɛ. 18:10) Abonsam a wɔ awoza nu mɔɔ ɔka kɛ Gyihova a ma amaneɛnwunlɛ ba a. Ye ahyiri ne, menli mɔɔ bɛ ahonle le kpalɛ la fannua mɔɔ bɛbanwu Gyihova kɛ Agɔnwa mɔɔ bɛbahola bɛabikye ye. (Gye. 4:8) Noko akee, Satan noko kpondɛ kɛ ɔsɛkye Gyihova menli didilɛ.

9. Wɔ Satan n’amozuma bo no, duzu a asɔre kyikye amɔ aama menle ansua a? (Jeremiah 23:26, 27)

4 NƐNƐANLE ABAN NE

10 Mekɛ mɔɔ yɛwɔ yɛ nsa nu ngyegyelɛ nu la, Satan kpondɛ kɛ yɛdie yɛdi kɛ Gyihova ɛndwenle yɛ nwo. Sa-tan kpondɛ kɛ yɛva ɛya saa Gyihova maa yɛ amaneɛnwunlɛ ne kɔ zo a. (Mrɛ. 19:3) Menli mɔɔ bɔ ɔ bo te nganeɛ zɛhae la bahola adwenle bɔbɔ kɛ: ‘Nzu a Gyihova mɛla nee ye ngyinlazo mɔɔ yɛbali zo la baboa yɛ a? Ɔngɛnla kɛ ɔbawie me boɛ!’ Adwenle ɛhye bahola amaa yɛagɔ Satan tumi ne abo.

11 Satan fa ninyɛ kɛ asolo intanɛt zolɛ ninyɛ gyima ma awie mɔ nyia adwen-tanta wɔ Gyihova nnyelaye nwo. Wɔ ɛleka ɛhye mɔ, menli dɔɔnwo ka kɛ subane pa nwo nnyelaye mɔɔ wɔ Baebolo ne anu la ɛnle kpalɛ bieko yɛɛ ɔwɔ kɛ awie biala nyia fanwodi kɛ ɔbaziezie ye ngyinlazo yeamaa ɔ nwo. Nɔhalɛ nu, “ewiade ne ahenle” ne ze kpalɛ kɛzi ɔbamaa ninyɛ kpalɛ nee ninyɛ ɛtane bazi ahyi la.—Dwɔn 14:30; Aye. 5:20; 1 Tim. 4:1, 2.

12 Sɛ yɛtie Satan n’atorokae a, ɔkɔhɔ ama yɛahwe asese wɔ Jeho-vah nwo. Akokoa banyenra bie mɔɔ yɛkɔfɛlɛ ye Shannon ka kɛ: “Ɔgye me mekɛ ansaana manwu kɛ suban mɔɔ bɛkile me wɔ sukuu nee menli mɔɔ me nee bɛ bɔ nu la ɛlɛsɛkye kɛzi menwu debie kpa nee debie bɔne la. Mekɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ mefa metena intanɛte zo la, ɛnee mekulo kɛ mesukoa ninyɛne mɔɔ menli mɔɔ menwu bɛ wɔ intanɛte zo la yɛ la kpalɛ. Mekɛ kɔ zo la, mebɔle ɔ bo kɛ mehwe meseze Jeho-vah, me busuanli, nee me mediema mrenya nee mraalɛ nwo.” Awieleɛ koraa la, Shannon nwunle kɛzi ɔbayɛ na yeazia yeahɔ ye anwuma Selɛ la anwo na ɔnwunle kɛzi ɔbahɔ ye ti afi Abɔnsam asabɛle nu la. Duzu a boale ye a?

Ɔbɛlɛle ɔ nwo aze ɔhɔle ɔ bama ɛkɛ na ɔbikanle kɛ bɛboa ye. Menyɛle zɔhane ala bie. Me bama, asafo nu mgbanyima, nee mediema mraalɛ mɔɔ bɛnyi sunsum nu wɔ asafo ne anu la boale me ma mengyakyile agɔnwolɛbɔlɛ ɛtane. (Aye. 49:15) Ɔbu awie biala mɔɔ bu ye duma ne la kɛ “agyapadeɛ kpalɛ.” (Mal. 3:16, 17, ɛkɛ ne edw.) Eza Baebolo ne kile kɛzi Gyisɛse ɛlɔlɛ ne mɔɔ ɔlɛ maa yɛ la dɔɔnwo la kɛ ɔhanle kɛzi ɔte nganeɛ wɔ ye afɔlebɔlɛ ne anwo la.—Dwɔn 15:12, 13.

6 THE WATCHTOWER (SIN-ABAN)

16 Ye sɛ yɛdwene mboa mɔ Gyɛhova nɛ Gyisɛse fa ma yɛ ɛnnɛ ne nwo a, ɔbahola yeaboa yɛ ma yeagye ali fitaa kɛ bɛdɔ yɛ. Yɛbahola yɛadwenledwene mekɛ mɔ yɛwule kɛ Gyɛhova bikye yɛ ne nwo, na yɛanya ahomeka wɔ saa ninyɛne mɔ yɛkae ne nu. Yɛbahola noko yeabiza yɛ mediema mrenya nɛ mmea sɛdeɛ bɛnoko bɛwule saa mboa he ne. (Mrɛ. 22:17) Sɛ yɛyɛ zɔ a, ɔbamaa yɛanya anwodozo kɛ nsɔhwɛ bieala mɔ yɛbahyia ye ne, Gyɛhova nɛ Gyisɛse wɔ yɛ afa.—Kenga Ndwom 118:5, 6.

17 Yɛ mɔ fɛɛ yɛ kpondɛ kɛ yɛ kɔ do yɛ yɛ Nyamenle Gyihova nee Gyisɛse Kelaese agɔnwolɛ kpalɛ. Noko kɔkpula kɛ bɛbazɛkye Seetan nee ye sunsum ɛtane ne mɔ dahuu la, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛ ko yɛ tia bɛ mɔdenlebɔlɛ kɛ bɛbamaa yɛ nwo do Gyihova nee Gyisɛse anu la ayɛ fɛtɛɛ. (Yek. 20:1-3, 10) Amaa yɛahola yɛagyinla yɛ kpɔvolɛma mɔ ayɛne nwo la, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛ nee Gyihova nya abusuabɔlɛ kpalɛ na yɛ die yɛ di kɛ ɔdaye nee ye Ra dɔkɔba ne kulo yɛ na bɛ bu yɛ. Ɔluakɛ, kɛ mɔɔ Gyisɛse hanle la, awie biala ɛnrɛhola ɛnrɛye “[bɛ] ɛnrɛvi Selɛ ne asa anu.”—Kenga Dwɔn 10:29; Gye. 4:7.

18 Wɔ adesua a ɔdo do yi nu, yɛbato yɛ nwan ayɛ ndɛnla nwiɛn foforɛ mɔɔ Satan fa ko yɛ nwo ɛko wɔ yɛ sunsum nu ɛkone nu la—yɛ botaeɛ kɛ ɔbama mpaepanu aba asɔne ne nu nɛɛ bɛ mɔɔ bɛte abɔnten la sɔre bɛtia yɛ. Kɛ ɔyɛ a yɛbahola yɛako yɛatia ɛhye mɔ yie ɛ? Yɛbanwu ye.

Dyuuma ne mɔ mɔɔ wɔ Zɛkaraya ye mmere nu ne ɛnee ɛlɛkyea anwoma Mɛzaya ne na bɛze kɛ ɔbadei Abraham ye azunza nu lua David ye hene abusua ne anwo. Siane nsia pɛ nu, bɛlegule bie mɔɔ bɛfrɛ ye Mary bawo Gyisɛs, mɔɔ kɛ anwumabɔvolɛ Gabriel hanle la, bɛbavɛlɛ ye “Nyamenle ye Ba” na ɔbali hene wɔ “ye papa David ye ahenegua zo.” (Luka 1:30-35) Bɛmanle zɔ “ngoane dieno kpole” ne zo wɔ mmere mɔɔ Gyisɛs lile mfe 30 mɔɔ ɔzɔnenle la. Zɔ mmere ne nu, Gyihova fa sunsum nwuanzanwuanza kpatuale ye kɛ anwoma Mɛzaya ne. (Mat. 3:16, 17) Ninyɛne fɛlɛko kpalɛ a ɛzɔ kɛzi mɔɔ zili a!

Mekɔle m'enyi wɔ me Baebolo esuanyɛ ne nu ne, mesiesiele me bɔbɔ me nwo. Mesile kpɔkɛ kɛ mebagya sikaa nee abɔnzwɛ nlonlo. Mekɛ mɔɔ mesile kpɔkɛ zɔhane ala la, mentole sikaa nee abɔnzwɛ mɔɔ ɛha la mengwule senle nu na mennlonle bie bio le. Mebɔle ɔ bo kɛ mekɔ ayia bie mɔ. Ayia ko bo ne, menzelɛ adiema nrenyia bie kɛ ɔndua me ti nwi sɔlɔ ne, mɔɔ ɔyɛle ye ɛkɛ ne ala wɔ Ahendzi Su ne anu a.

Dabaw fia nzi no, miguanli mɛla French Guiana na misali midoli France. Menyanli Ahendzi Asalo koon na migyili me Baebolo desua no zo. Ɔwɔ nu kɛ me bɔbɔ me bɔbɔ adawolɛ bo yɛli fɛ dɛba no dɛdeɛ, noko me mbusuafo akunlu yɛli bɛ kɛsɛ kɛ midukonli me nwo pɛpɛɛpɛ kɛba ye. Mianwe sega bio, miannzo bio, meamfa me nwo meangɔ akɛsakɛsa asaw nu bio. Noko, kɛ midoli mɔdimbɔ kɛ mibɔ bɛ asɛnga a kpalɛ mɔɔ menle ninyene nu tɛtɛtɛ no anlɛ bɛ fɛ. Akyi no menwunli kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ menyɛ ninyene bɛtɛɛ nu na midwenli bɛ adwenle nwo kpalɛ.

Mebɔle asu wɔ 1996 nu. Mebɔle mpae mezele Gyihova kɛ mepɛ kɛ meyɛ asɛmpakãvovoma kɛ Michel la. Ɛhye a ɔyɛle adenle kpalɛ mɔɔ menwunle kɛ mebahola mealua zo mealaye Gyihova ase a.

Menwunle kɛ ɛnee ɔlɛ mɔdenbɔ wɔ asɛnkɛnga edwuma ne anu, yɛɛ ye nyianu ayia ne mɔ abo kyerɛle me kɛ ɔdɔ Gyihova kpolekpole. Ɛnee mepɛ kɛ yɛyɛ anyelazo, noko ɛnee mepɛ kɛ menze tɛtɛ kɛ Claudine anye die misinari edwuma ne ho anzeɛ. Ɛnee ɔpɛ kpa! Yɛgyanle wɔ April 2000.

Afe nua li zɔ nu, yɛbɔle akwampae edwuma ne bo bɔbɔle nu. Mbere pɔtee bie anzi, yɛsusuwu kɛ yɛkosua Mandarin aneɛ ne amaa yɛaboa Chinese kuw bie wɔ asafo mɔɔ bika yɛ nwo la anu. Ɔyɛle me se kpole kɛ mekosua aneɛ ɛhe. Ɔwɔ kɛ metoto me adwenli bɔbɔle nu na meyɛ ahyɛnze wɔ asɛnkɛpa ne nwolɛ. Nɔhalɛ nu, meanzua Mandarin aneɛ ne kpaa kyikyi. Noko meyɛle mɔɔ mebahola biala, na yɛ nee Chinese anuanli mrenya nee mmaa ne diale mfe mɔɔ yɛnrɛla arɛle na ɔyɛ anyelielɛ kpole. Nza nu la, ɔnzɛ nhyira kpole ngome, noko eza ɔyɛle ntetee kpaa maa gyima mɔɔ yɛbali ye anzi la.

Wɔ ɛvolɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ ɛze nu la anu, menwunle kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ meko mehyia ngyegyelɛ dɔɔnwo anwo. Kɛ neazo la, mengyele menlinle kɛ mɔdenle mɔɔ merebɔ kɛ memaa Gyihova nee me mediema mrenyia nee mraalɛ debie kpalɛ kpalɛ la ɛnli munli ɛlɛ. Ɔti mendɛnnlanle aze dɔɔnwo mempɛle mɛla mɔɔ ɛngyia la anwo mezieziele me ngilehilelɛ gyima ne kpalɛ.

Ɔsom wɔ mantsi edwuma nu wɔ New Caledonia

Ɔyɛ amgba kɛ, me werɛ bolee ɔluakɛ mendetumi mennyian ninyɛne nzenzema mɔɔ mengyinlanli me ti anwo zolɛ kɛ mebayɛ la dahuu. (Eccl. 7:16) Ɔhyian kɛ menwu kɛ Gyɛhova le Selɛ mɔɔ lɛ ɔlɔlɛ, yemɔ menzomɔ kɛ yɛyɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ, emomu, ɔti yɛ mmɔdenbɔ mɔɔ fi ahonle nu la anwo anye.

Wɔ Paris nu, bɛyɛle me amansin sohwɛfo ananmusifo. Menele nwo kɛ me le kakra na menga menle anyunlu wɔ edwuma ɛhe nu. Na mesukwole kɛ Gyihova sunsum nwuanzanwuanza ne boa yɛ ma yɛyɛ debie kpole tɛla mɔɔ yɛngola yɛyɛ wɔ yɛ ngomekye yɛ anwosesebɛ nu.

Me nzo menwunle awokebane foforɛ nu amaneɛ. Ɔvi me ngakula nu, menwunle hyperhidrosis amaneɛ, noko alevelɛ dɔɔnwo titile. Ɔyɛ kɛ amaneɛnwunlɛ ɛhye kɔ me sa mɔ anu kpalɛ. Ɔkɔ anyunlu wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ adwenle nu hawule wɔ nu la, kɛ mɔɔ mekayɛ asalo kɛ meka edwɛkɛ mehile menli dɔɔnwo wɔ nyianu anzɛɛ maanyianu la. Noko mebahola meamaa nwonane nu suban ɛhye adwo ɔlua asɔneyɛlɛ mɔɔ mebɔ Gyihova mebize adwenle nu anzodwolɛ la azo. Ɔtɛwiele, noko mekɛ mɔɔ mefa me nwo meto Gyihova azo kpalɛ la, me nwo dwo kpalɛ yɛɛ me nwo ɛnfifi dɔɔnwo.

Wɔ 2011 nu, Gyihova fa ayamyɛ yɛle yɛ ahonle nu abebizela mɔɔ yɛva mekɛ tendenle yɛbiza la manle yɛ. Yɛbɔle asɛmpatukwɛlɛ gyima bo wɔ New Caledonia, supɔ ngyikyi ngyikyi ekpunli mɔɔ wɔ Pacific atɔe-anwɔne afa nu, mɔɔ bɔ borɔ 16,000 kilometers (10,000 mi) vi Paris. Gyima ɛhye noko manle yɛyiale ngyegyelɛ. Ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ yɛ nwo kɛ yɛlidie aleɛ fofolɛ, le kɛ bankye, amanye, nee bayɛre. Eza ɔwɔ kɛ yɛtaa yɛ nwo kɛ akɔba bɛkɔsu nee ngwle bɛkɔbɔ mɔɔ sɛkye yɛ nna wɔ ngelɛbɛla kpole. Eza ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ yɛ nwo kɛ yɛkɛdendɛ wɔ adenle fofolɛ zo. Ɛkɛ ne amra ta bua edwɛkɛ, tɛ edwɛkpemgbe ɔ, na mmom kɛ bɛkɔwula bɛ nnyikpo zo ala.

Gyihova noko aboa yɛ ma yɛatumi asesa yɛn nneɛma wɔ N’asom adwuma foforɔ a yɛnya no mu. Yɛyɛɛ asɛmpatrɛwfo wɔ asɛnka adwuma no mu na yɛyɛɛ adwuma nso wɔ Bɛtɛl mfeɛ kakra. Yɛkɔɔ Ahemman Asɛmpakawfo Sukuu no, na seesei yɛwɔ mansin adwuma no mu. Adwuma biala a yɛyɛɛ no, yɛhunye Gyihova mboa nam ne sunsum kronkron do. Wahwɛ akyerɛ me kɛ yɛn bɔkɔbɔkɔ anaa mmerɛwyɛ nkyerɛ kɛ yɛntumi nyɛ adwuma mma ye. Enti mebɔ mpae mpɛn pii na mepɛ mboa afi me koma mu kɛ me nsa bɛka deɛ ɛhia. Ɛwom kɛ me suro nso da so wɔ hɔ sɛ megyina anim rekasa a, Nyankopɔn mboa nti, matumi ama asafo ahorow a yɛkɔhwehwɛ bɔn hɔ no awerɛkyekyerɛ na makyerɛ bɔn kwan.

Wɔ nsakrae ɛhyeam muala nu, Claudine ɛyɛ mboa kpole m’ama me dabrabia. Ɔnya abotore kpole wɔ me sintɔ nu. Ɔboa me wɔ gyima mɔ yɛbɔ nu yɛ nu, na ɔyezie ɔnwo dabrabia kɛ ɔdi daselɛ wɔ ahunlundole nu. Ɔnnunwini wɔ ewia kpole ne anzɛɛ ninyɛne ngakyile mɔ yɛfa nu wɔ mansin gyima ne nu anwo.

Me asoho kpole foforɛ no bo, mesuko kɛ Gyihova, Bɔbɔfo Kpole no, nea ɔsomfo nɩ mɔ anyi dahuu. Ɔ nti, menyia nhyira dɔɔnwo. Gyihova abuka me ma menya adwenpa wɔ tumi nwo na meyɛ ahonlɛsika. Megyake ewiade anyengominlima agɔnwolɛvalɛ amaa meava Gyihova ngyinlawalɛ nɩ meali gyima, na mmom Gyihova amaa menya anyengominlima kpa dɔɔnwo awula ɔ gyakɛ nu kpole kpalɛ. Menyia ye kpa fɛlɛko. Na menyia anyelazo kɛnlɛma kɛ mebanya Gyihova yamnlɛnlɛ dahuu dahuu.—Mak 10:29, 30.

30 ƆWƆN-AWONZO SIN LI

Ɛnzi a?

Ninyɛnwun ndɛnla na bɛyɛle wɔ kurow nwo anlenkɛ kɛnla wɔ Baebolo mɛkɛ nu a?

KUROW anumbonli nɩ ɛnle edwɛkɛ mɔɔ kile kɛ nwolɛ le edwɛkɛ ala. Kɛmɔ menli dɔɔnwo fa ɛkɛ ne pɛ nu la ati, ɛkɛ ne yɛle ɛleka mɔɔ menli dɔɔnwo yɛ gyima wɔ kenle biala nu a. Baebolo ne ka kurow anumbonli nwo edwɛkɛ dɛla fane 50, na ɔka ninyɛne mɔɔ sisile ɛkɛ ne la anwo edwɛkɛ.

Menli hyianli kuro kponlɛ nuhwã nwo. Hebri edwɛkponle mɔɔ kile “kponlɛ” la ɛnle kuro bie kponlɛ ala. Ɔkile ɛleka mɔɔ bɛzisi azua wɔ kuro anzɛɛ suakpole bie anloa. Wɔ ɛleka bie mɔ la, ɛnyiannu kponle mɔɔ kɛzi mgbane mɔɔ menli dɔɔnwo kola hyia wɔ ɛkɛ la wɔ kponlɛ ne anwo.—Neh. 8:1.

Adwadifo guagualɛ gua bɔbɔ su arowa ninyia nu. Jerusalem anowulolɛ bie mɔ kɛ asɛ Kenren Anowulolɛ noko mɔ Buan Anowulolɛ lɛ, bɔbɔ benya bɛ eduma ngɛ ninyia bɔ bɛtɔn wɔ bɛ nzo.—Zeph. 1:10; John 5:2.

Menli di agyadi ne ahyiade ngakyile wɔ suw anwo anzoe a. Abraham ne Boaz lele adansefow anyun yɛle agyadi dwuma ngakyile wɔ suw anwo anzoe a. (Gen. 23:10-15, 17, 18; Ruth 4:1, 10) Sɛ obi angye afɔntunvow anlɔ ye fie a, bie mɔ da gua zo wɔ suw anwo anzoe a. (Judg. 19:15-21) Menli kɔ suw anwo anzoe kɛ bɛkɔtie amanebɔ fofolɛ noko.—2 Sam. 18:24, 25.

Atɛmbufo noko di asɛn wɔ suando n’anan anloa. Bɔ bu asɛn wɔ amanfole bɛ anruni wɔ duma noko bɛ kɔhɔla atie adasemaneli nɛ na bɛ ti asɛn n’awieɛ noko. (Deut. 21:18-20) Ngbando bɛ di asɛn wɔ ɛleka noko amanfole boanu nɛ maa atɛmbufo noko di asɛntenrɛn na bɛ nzi pɛpɛɛpɛ wɔ bɛ abuo nɛ nu. Sɛ atɛmbufo noko pɛ asɛn n’aso a, ɛkɛnla kɛnla alimabufo noko yo asotweɛ nɛ. (Deut. 25:2) Wɔ suando n’anan anloa,

Kuro ngyando ɛnee le ebiasia mɔ dwumadie dɔɔnwo kɔ do wɔ daa ngoane nu

ɛbɛlɛ kɛ ndɛnlibolɛnli noko bama bɛamaa pɛlepɛlelilɛ nee abrabɔ kpalɛ ɛnya anyunhɔlɛ.—Deut. 16:18-20; Job 29:7, 11-17.

Mrenyinli anɔfoboe hyehyɛle Yisrayɛlfoɔ mɔɔ wɔ suakpole ne anloa ano ne anwosesebɛ kɛ bɛnyia anwodozo wɔ Gyihova nu. Ngapezoma nee afoforɛ dɛndɛle wɔ ano ne anloa ɔluakɛ bɛze kɛ menli yia ɛkɛ. (1 Ki. 22:10; Jer. 17:19, 20) Kɛ neazo la, Gehu hanle wɔ menli ne amuala anyunlu kɛ Gyihova ɛli Ye edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ɔhanle ɔtiale Yehaba sua ne azo. (2 Ki. 10:8-10) Belemgbunli Hɛzikaya yiale ye sɔfofoɔ kpanyinli mɔɔ wɔ suakpole ano ne anloa na ɔhyehyɛle bɛ anwosesebɛ kɛ bɛva bɛ nwo bɛdo Gyihova moalɛ zo.—2 Chron. 32:6-8.

Baebolo ne ka kɛ “nwonle kpalɛ” ne tea nu na ɔma ne anwuma “wɔ ku ne anlenkɛ ne anwo ɛkɛ.” (Manny. 1:20, 21) Wɔ tete Yizilayɛle ne, ku ne anlenkɛ ne anwo ɛkɛ yɛɛ mrenya mɔɔ di nɔhalɛ ne tua ngelɛbɛ kpalɛ na bɛdi ndɛne fɛlɛfɛlɛ a. Ɛnɛ, bɛhɛlɛ Gyihova nwonle ne wɔ Baebolo ne anu, na ye azonvolɛ mɔɔ wɔ ɛleka biala ne ɛlɛboka menli amaa bɛahɔ nwonle kpalɛ ɛhye anwo ɛkɛ.

BAEBƆLO NƆHALƐ Baka Ne Mɔ Sɔre Nhyɛhe

Almond baka ne baka ngakyi 30 mɔ bɛbɔ bɛ dunke wɔ Baebolo ne anu ne boko. Ɔkyele baka mɔ bɛtete bɛyiri wɔ awɔlɛ anzɛɛ awunle berɛ nzi ne anu. Nnwu nrenrenye anzɛɛ fufule mɔ anyunle yɛ fɛ ne, bɛtumi banwu bɛ kɛ bɛyiri wɔ Sanda anzɛɛ January awieleɛ berɛ ne. Yemɔti, Hebri duma mɔ bɛfɛ almond baka ne fane titire kɛ “mɔ ɔtete ɔnwunle.” Asɛ kɛ baka ɛhye ngyinraba biko wɔ ye noko ye Bɔvolɛ ne avinla a?

Aseɛkyerɛ nea ɔwɔ Yerimiya 7:25 kile kɛ Yehowa ka ɔ nwo edwɛkɛ kɛ ɔsɔre nwonloma. March 15, 2011, The Watchtower ne ka kɛ: “Kɛ mɔ almond dua ne ‘sɔre’ nwonloma ne, zɔhane ala a na Yehowa noko wɔ sɛkɛlɛneɛ adenle zo ‘sɔre nwonloma’ . . . bɔ ɔ menle ne kɔkɔ wɔ asoɔdenleyɛlɛ nzonle nwo. . . . Na ɔnrɛlie ɔ menle—ɔkɔhɔ zo ‘ɔkɔwɔ nzonle’—kɔkpula kɛ ye ngapezonle edwɛkɛ ne bayɛ nɔhalɛ.”—Yer. 1:11, 12.

Almond dua ne nkyɛnkyɛn fufuo ne nso, Baebolo ne fa yɛ mfaso dɛ ɔfa kyerɛ nyekpɔn fufuo mɔ wɔ nyekpɔnle nu. (Eccl. 12:5) Na bie a ɔlua bɛ ahondzi fɛfɛ ne ati, dua ne nhwiren ne yɛɛ mfaso dɛ kɛpumu mɔ wɔ ɛhyɛn ne mɔ anwo wɔ asɔredan ne kendolo dua ne anwo.—Ex. 37:17-20.

Bulumu ɛhe ɛnla ɛtɔne nu. Bɛva bɛmaa kɛ ɔboka ewiade amuala Baebolo ngyehyele edwuma ne mɔɔ bɛfa ahonle kpalɛ ahyɛdeɛ bɛdi nwolɛ gyima la anwo. Saa ɛkulo kɛ ɛyɛ ahyɛdeɛ a, yɛsɛlɛ wɔ kɔ donate.jw.org.
