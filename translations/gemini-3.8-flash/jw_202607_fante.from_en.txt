Sua Bi Fi Gibeonfo No Hɔ

“Gibeonfo no nye Israelfo ayɛ asomdwee na wɔatena hɔn mu.”—JOSH. 10:1.

AFE no yɛ 1473 B.C.E. Israel man no ahyɛ ase fofor dɛ wɔroridzi Bɔhyɛ Asaase no do konyim. Israelmba no edzi nkunyim kɛse a ɔyɛ nwanwa wɔ Yeriko na Ai nkurow no do. Naaso ekyir no, biribi a wɔnhwɛ kwan kɔr do. Akwantufo bi baa hɔn nkyɛn, a wɔkãa dɛ wofi asaase a ɔwɔ ekyir kɛse do na wɔrohwehwɛ dɛ wɔnye Nyankopɔn nkorɔfo bɛyɛ asomdwee apam.

2 Dɛm akwantufo no yɛ Gibeonfo no. Iyi nye ber a odzi kan a wɔabɔ Gibeonfo no dzin wɔ Bible kyerɛwtohɔ no mu, naaso nnyɛ ber a otswa do no a. Bible kyerɛwtohɔ pii da no edzi dɛ Gibeonfo no nye Israelfo no bɔɔ mu bɔbɔɔ afa wɔ mfehaha a wodzidzi do no mu. Dɛm kyerɛwtohɔ ahorow yi kura asuadze a no ho hia ma hɛn, na wɔda no edzi pii fa Jehovah Nyankopɔn ho.

(Ex. 34:11; Josh. 9:24) Dɛm nti ber a Israelfo no dzii Jericho na Ai do konyim ekyir no, Gibeonfo no somaa ananmusifo kɔr Joshua nkyɛn wɔ Gilgal dɛ wɔrokɔhwehwɛ asomdwee.

⁴ Kenkan Joshua 9:8-13. Gibeonfo no yɛɛ hɔnho dɛ wɔtse asaase a ɔwɔ akyirikyiri do. Wogyee too mu dɛ nna Jehovah ka Israelfo ho ma wodzii konyim wɔ Egyptfo na Amorifo ahemfo Sihon na Og do. Na mbom wɔ nyansa mu no, wɔanbɔ dza osisii wɔ Jericho na Ai no ho asɛm,

osian dɛ dɛm amandzɛɛ fofor no nntum nndu hɔn asaase a wɔfrɛ no “ekyir kyir pii” no do ntsi. (Josh. 9:14, 15) Okyir kakra no, wohun Gibeonfo no hɔn adaagye no, naaso Israelfo no dzii hɔn asomdwee ahyɛmu a wɔnye Gibeonfo no yɛe no do osiandɛ na wɔaka “ntam ahyɛ hɔn dze nam Jehovah do.” (Josh. 9:16-19) Wɔdze dwumadzi a ɔbrɛ nyimpa ase maa Gibeonfo no dɛ wɔnsom afebɔɔ dɛ “enyankpaafo na nsuhorfo mma asafo no na Jehovah n’afɔrmbukyia no.”—Josh. 9:27.

⁵ Ber a Amorfo ahemfo enum no tee dɛ Gibeonfo no nye Israelfo ayɛ asomdwee no, wɔbɔɔ mu kɔwee dɛ wɔrekɔtow ahyɛ Gibeon do.

Gibeonfo no serɛɛ Joshua dɛ ɔmboa hɔn. (Josh. 10:3-7) Joshua dzii Israel enyim kɔgyee hɔn, na wohyiraa hɔn mbɔdzembɔ do. Jehovah mpo too asukɔtwadzee akɛse guu atamfo Amorfo no hɔn asraafo no do, na ɔmaa owia gyinaa faako ma wiadze kɔr do yɛɛ kɛnkyɛn wɔ ɔko no mu. (Josh. 10:9-14) Dɛ mbrɛ Gibeonfo no pɛɛ asomdwee ahyɛmu nye Israel, na osiandɛ wɔserɛɛ Joshua dɛ ɔmboa hɔn ber a hɔn asom dwee no, woyii gyedzi kyerɛɛ wɔ Jehovah mu—gyedzi a ɔwɔ n’ahoɔdzen a ɔdze bɛyɛ n’asɛm na ɔagye hɔn nkwa mu.

6 Ebɛn asuadze na iyi kyerɛ hɛn fa Jehovah ho? Jehovah brɛ noho adze na ɔwɔ mbɔbɔrhu. Ná odzii kan ahyɛ Israel dɛ “wontow asaase no do tsenafo nyina nkyen,” a nna Gibeonfo no ka ho. (Kankyerɛ 33:51, 52) Eso, osian asomdwee ahyɛmu a Israelfo nye hɔn yɛe ntsi, Jehovah dze mbɔbɔrhu maa kwan ma Gibeonfo no tsenaa ase. Jehovah yɛɛ dɛm ɔmfa ho dɛ Israelfo no ammfa hɔn adwen annhyɛ ne nsa ansaana wɔreyɛ dɛm ahyɛmu no. Afei ɔboaa Israel ma wokitsaa hɔn ahyɛmu no mu, na ɔnam anwanwadze kwan do gyee Gibeonfo no.—Josh. 9:26; 11:19.

7 Yebesi dɛn asuasua Gibeonfo no? Ɔnam Jehovah mu gyedzi a ɔyɛ dzen a yebenya do. Esiane dɛ yenyim Jehovah ho nsɛm pii dɔɔso sen dza nna Gibeonfo no nyim dɛm ber no ntsi, yɛwɔ biribiara a ɔbɛma yaatwer Jehovah koraa. (Dw. 40:4, 5) Yebotum so asuasua Gibeonfo no denam pɛ a yɛbɛpɛ dɛ yɛyɛ ahobrɛadze edwuma wɔ Jehovah som mu. (Josh. 9:23, 27) Aber bi a aberantsɛ bi a wɔfrɛ no Luke hun dɛ onua panyin bi a ɔwɔ Bethel abusua no mu reyɛ dɛmara no, orunntum mma n’enyi mfi da. Ɔwɔ mu dɛ nna Bethelnyi no wɔ asodzi kɛse bi a no mu yɛ dur dze, naaso ɔyɛɛ kom pɛɛ dɛ ɔbɛwɛn krow no mu Ahendzi Asa a nna worusi no dɛm anafua no. Luke ka dɛ: “Mibosũae dɛ ahobrɛadze nnyɛ nkãtsedzi kɛkɛ. Dza yɛpaw dɛ yɛbɛyɛ anaa yerunnyɛ na ɔkyerɛ dɛ yɛwɔ ahobrɛadze.” Ɔbra a yɛbɔ da biara no mma yɛnkyerɛ gyedzi a yɛwɔ wɔ Jehovah mu denam dɛ yɛbɛtwer no wɔ aber a yɛhyia tebea a no mu yɛ dzen mu, na yɛnam ahobrɛadze do ahwɛ Nyankopɔn som mu asodzi biara a yɛbɛnya do.

mfe ebiasa kɔm. Ber a David bisaa siantsir a kɔm no bae no, Jehovah see no dɛ Saul na n’ebusuafo dzi bɔgyafɔ osiandɛ nkan no nna wɔayɛ Gibeonfo no basabasa ntsi.—2 Sam. 21:1.

10 Ken 2 Samuel 21:3-6. David kãa asɛm kyerɛɛ Gibeonfo no dɛ ɔpɛ dɛ ohu dza wobotum ayɛ dze apata Saul n’ebusunnsɛm a ɔyɛɛ tsiaa hɔn no. Aso nna wɔbɔpɛ dɛ wonya hɔnho osiandɛ wɔbɛma David atua kaw osiandɛ wannyɛ biribi ntɛm ammba hɔn ntsi? Gibeonfo no buaa no fɛfɛɛfɛ dɛ nna “nnyɛ dwetɛ anaa sika asɛm” osiandɛ, dɛ mbra no kã no, nna wonntum nnye agyedze mma nyimpa a okum nyimpa. (Num. 35:30, 31) Wɔsan so hun dɛ mbra mma hɔn kwan dɛ wokum obi a mbra mmaa ho kwan. Awiei koraa no, David maa kwan ma wokunkum Saul n’asefo mbanyin esuon a bi a nna wɔwɔ mu bi wɔ Saul n’apɛdze dɛ ɔbɔtɔre ebusua mũ no nyinara ase no mu. Aber bi ekyir no, osu tɔree, dza ɔmaa ekom no baa ewiei na ɔkyerɛe dɛ wɔatua ndzeyɛee bɔn no ho kaw a ɔsɔ Jehovah n’enyi.—2 Sam. 21:9, 10, 14.

Iyi ka hɔn mpo a wɔyɛ hɔn nyiyimu, dɛ mbrɛ ɔtaa ba wɔ ahɔho na nyimpa kakraba a wɔwɔ kuw bi mu no ho. Wɔ ne ber a ɔsɛ mu no, Jehovah betsew atsɛntsen biara a n'asomfo ahyia no do. Yɛsan so sua dɛ Jehovah hwɛ kwan dɛ yebedzi yɛn ehyɛmu do, dɛ mbrɛ nna ɔhwɛ kwan dɛ Israelmba no bedzi asomdwee ehyɛmu a wɔnye Gibeonfo no yɛe no do no.—Fa toto Amos 1:9 ho.

13 Bɛyɛ mfe 500 wɔ Ɔhen David ber no ekyir no, Gibeonfo no puei bio wɔ Bible no mu. Babylonia nnommumfa a ɔtsenaa hɔ mfe 70 no baa ewiei, na wɔ afe 537 A.Y.B. mu no, Jewfo nnommum kuw a odzi kan no kɔr Jerusalem bio a Amrado Zerubbabel ka hɔn ho. (Ezra 2:1, 2, 58) Wɔ afe 468 A.Y.B. mu no, kuw fofor kɔe a nna Ezra a ɔyɛ kyerɛwfo no ka hɔn ho. (Ezra 7:1-7) Nnommum a wɔsan kɔe a na hɔn dodow nsuar kɛse yi nyinara mu no, nna “Netinimfo” no hɔn ebusuafo ka ho. (Hwɛ Ezra 2:58 na 7:7 ho asɛm a ɔwɔ famu no.) Woanafo na nna wɔyɛ Netinimfo no? Nna wɔyɛ “asɔrfi somfo, anaa asomfo a wɔnnyɛ Israeltsenfo,” na hɔn mu pii kɛse no nna wɔyɛ Gibeonfo no aseni.—Hwɛ Asɛm a Ɔkasa Ho Asɛm, “Netinimfo.”

14 Kenkan 1 Mbermunsɛm 9:2 na asoɛyɛ asɛm no. “Asofi asomfo” no ka nnommum a wodzii kan san baa Israel no ho. Iyi yɛ kɛse efisɛ nnyɛ nnommum a wɔwɔ Jewfo mu nyina na wɔsanee. Akyinnye biara nnyi ho dɛ Jewfo pii nyaa ahonyadze bi wɔ Babylon, na nna wɔmpɛ dɛ wɔbɔbɔ afɔr a ɔwɔ dɛ wɔbɔ dɛ wɔbɛsan akɔ na wɔaaboa ma wɔasiesie hɔn kurow a asɛɛ no. Naaso nnommum anokwafo, a bi mpo a Gibeonfo no aseni bi ka ho no, hɔn kɔn dɔɔ dɛ wobehu dɛ wɔasan dze nokwar som asi hɔ wɔ Jerusalem asofi hɔ, na wɔbɔɔ mbɔdzen biara dɛ wɔbɛsan akɔ. Wɔnnyɛ dɛ Jewfo no, nna Gibeonfo no aseni nnyi asaase egyapadze biara wɔ Israel. Naaso, wɔsanee dɛ wɔrokɔhwɛ hɔn asofi edwuma do na mpo wɔaboa ma wɔasiesie Jerusalem afasu no.—Neh. 3:26.

15 Ebɛn adze na asɛm yi kyerɛ hɛn fa Jehovah ho? Yehũ dɛ hɛn Nyankopɔn kyerɛ n’asomfo anokwafo no ho adɔw paa na ɔhwɛ hɔn aber nyina. Ber a Babylon nkowasom no baa ewiei no, nna mfe bɛyɛ 1,000 atwa mu fi ber a Jehovah gyee Gibeonfo no ber a Israel dzii Bɔhyɛ Asaase no do konyim no. Eso, ɔkɔr do hyiraa hɔn. Gibeonfo no ekyirdzifo no nyaa hɔn ti didzii mu wɔ aber a wɔsɛɛ Judah na Jerusalem wɔ 607 B.C.E. mu no. Nkowasom no ekyi no, wɔkɔr do dze hɔn ho hyɛɛ nokwar som no mu dendennden, a nna wɔnye Levifo no som wɔ asɔrfie hɔ. Bɛyɛ dɛ hɔn mu binom bɔɔ bra bɛn asɔrfie hɔ. (Ezra 2:70; Neh. 11:21) Na woyii hɔn fii tow, tow krɔnkrɔn, na kwan do tow tua mu osian hɔn asɔrfie edwuma no ntsi.—Ezra 7:24.

16 Yebesi dɛn etum asuasua Gibeonfo no? Hɛn so yɛpɛ dɛ yɛyɛ biribiara a yebotum dze aboa nokwar som. Yefi hɛn pɛ mu bɔ afɔr dze som Jehovah osiandɛ yɛdɔ no na yɛpɛ dɛ yɛyɛ dza ɔsɔ n’ani. Dɛm na nna ɔtse wɔ Alwin a ɔyɛ onua bi a ɔwɔ Philippines no famu. Nna ɔwɔ edwuma a akatua kɛse wom a ɔreyɛ ama ngo edwumakuw bi. Naaso nna ɔpɛ dɛ ɔyɛ pii wɔ ne som a ɔdze ma Jehovah no mu. Aber biara a mansin dohwɛfo no bɛsrasra n’asafo no, nna Alwin gye ahomefi wɔ edwuma mu dze boa nsrasra no na n’ani gyee ho dɛ obenya kyɛfa kɛse wɔ asɛnka edwuma no mu. Ber kakra ekyir na mpaebɔ pii akyi no, Alwin sii gyinae dɛ obegyaa n’edwuma no na wapehwe kor a oboma no kwan ma maasom dɛ daa akwampaeefo. Sɛ ɔyɛ dɛm a, nna obegyina hɔ ma sikasɛm mu afɔrbɔ ahorow. Naaso, oduu ne botae no do. Ne yer so bɛkaa no ho wɔ akwampae edwuma no mu, na dɛ awarefo no, woetum aboa nyimpa 21 ma woeasua nokwar no. Akyinbye biara nnyi ho dɛ, Jehovah bu mbɔdzen a dɛm awarefo yi bɔe no enyisaso kɛse. Hɛn so yebotum enya ahotoso dɛ Jehovah n’ani sɔ afɔr biara a yɛbɔ wɔ ne som mu, na iyi ntsi, ɔbɛhwɛ hɛn ehiadze ber nyina.—Matt. 6:33.

17 Bible kyerɛwtohɔ ahorow a ɔfa Gibeonfo no ho no ma yehu Jehovah no su a ɔyɛ fɛw no yiye. Ɔyɛ ahobrɛasefo, mmɔborɔhunufo, ɔtreneeni, na odzi nokwar. Bere nyina otua n’asɔfo anokwafo no kaw. Saa Bible kyerɛwtohɔ ahorow yi so kura afotu pa pii. Dɛ mbrɛ Gibeonfo no yɛe no, yɛpɛ dɛ yenya gyedzi a ɔyɛ dzen wɔ Jehovah mu, na yɛdze hɛn ho to no do bere a yehyia nsɛm a ɔyɛ hu. Gyedzi a ɔtse dɛm no so bɛkanyan hɛn ma yɛdze ahobrɛase ahwɛ teokrasi asɛndze biara a yebenya no do. Yɛpɛ dɛ yɛdze aboterɛ twɛn Jehovah, a yenya awerɛhyemu dɛ awiee koraa no obosiesie ndzɛmba a entsen nyina. Na yɛdze nsi na nokwardzi boa nokwar som no. Bere a yɛyɛ dɛm no, yɛbɛkyerɛ dɛ yeesua asuadze a nsombo wɔ mu efi Gibeonfo no hɔ.

BOTHO Dza ɔkyerɛ dɛ yebohu Jehovah, dza ntsi a ɔwɔ dɛ yɛpɛ dɛ yehu no yie kɛse, na kwan a yebotum afa do ayɛ dɛm.

Ikor Do Resua Jehovah Ho Nyansa Kɛse Anaa?

“Hu w’agya no Nyankopɔn na fa akoma a odzi mũ som no.”—1 CHRON. 28:9.

Aso edwen dɛ mbrɛ Jehovah n’adwen mu dɔ, n’enyansa dɔɔ sõ, anaa mbrɛ n’enyidado kɛse no tse pɛn a? Sɛ yedwen yɛn Bɔfo no ho a, yɛbrɛ yɛnho adze. Bi a ebɛtse nka dɛ mbrɛ ɔsomafo Paul yɛe no, nyia ɔkyerɛwee dɛ: “O Onyankopɔ n’ahonyade na n’enyansa na n’adwen mudɔ ara nyi! N’atsɛmbu mu yɛ dzen dɛ wobedwen ho papaapa, na n’akwan nso nnyɛ dza wobotum ahwehwɛ mu!”—Rom. 11:33, 34.

2 Ɔwɔ mu dɛ yerenntum nnhu biribiara a ɔfa Jehovah ho dɛm ara de, naaso yetum hu dza ɔtse dɛ no wɔ kwan bi do. Ɔhen David ka kyerɛɛ ne babanyin Solomon dɛ: “Hu w’egya no Nyankopɔn na fa akoma a odzi mu som no . . . Sɛ ihwehwɛ no a, ɔbɛma ehu no.” (1 Chron. 28:9) Dɛ mbrɛ mfe pii akyir no ɔsomafo John kaakaa n’anyɛnkofo Christianfo no, yɛwɔ “adwen a ɔbɛma yeenya nyimdzee a ɔfa nyia ɔyɛ nokwarfo no ho.” (1 John 5:20) Na Jesus ara koraa kaa dɛ Jehovah ho nyimdzee no bi wɔ hɔ ma “obiara a Ɔba no pɛ dɛ ɔda no edzi kyerɛ no.”—Matt. 11:27.
