Ewiade Amuala Sonla Nwo Nwolɛ Gyinlabelɛ Noloɛ

Mukenyele

Kɛmɔ sonla abusua ne anu amra amuala nwo eni nrenyia nee mraalɛ pɛpɛɛpɛlilɛ nee tumi mɔɔ bɛnrɛhola bɛnrɛlie bɛ sa nu la a le fawodi, pɛpɛɛpɛlilɛ nee anwunvnezelɛ mɔɔ wɔ ewiade la ahyɛse la ati,

Kɛmɔ sonla amatodwɛlɛ nwo mgbonyi nee ɛtane mɔɔ bɛyɛ la ɛva amumuyɛleɛ mɔɔ ɛzɛkye aleɛabo amra adwenle ɛra la ati, kɛmɔ eza ewiade mɔɔ sonla biala banyia adenle aha mɔɔ kpondɛ yɛɛ mɔɔ diedi, na eza bɛanyia ɛhulolɛ mɔɔ vi ɛzulolɛ nee ehyia nu la ɛhwe ameleba mɔɔ aleɛabo amra anye la azo la ati,

Kɛmɔ ɔhyia kpalɛ kɛ mɛla bamaa sonla adenle bɔbɔ nwo banebɔlɛ, amaa yeanrɛla ye kɛ sonla babɔ mɔdenle kɛ ɔtetɛ ɔ nwo azo ɔdua atuatebɔ zo ɔtia ti-anwosesebɛyɛlɛ nee amumuyɛlɛ la,

Kɛ mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛmaa agɔnwolɛlilɛ kpalɛ ba maanle maanle avinli la,

Kɛmɔ United Nations mende ne mɔ ɛla ye ali wɔ Bɛ Kpɔkɛzelɛ Kɛlata ne anu kɛ bɛnyia diedi kpole wɔ sonla ngoane ndetenlenye mɔɔ bɔbɔ fane sonla ngoane nwo la anu, wɔ sonla sonlabɔye anzɛɛ nidi yɛɛ ye sonlaleɛ kpole kpalɛ ne anu, yɛɛ mrenyia nee mraalɛ nzonlenyia pɛlepɛlelilɛ nu, na bɛbɔ kpɔkɛ kɛ bɛbamaa asetɛnla nu anyuhɔlɛ nee ɛbɛlabɔlɛ kpalɛ adenle kpole ɛhɔ ɔ nyunlu wɔ fanwodi kpole nu la,

Kɛmɔ Maanle mɔɔ boka nwo la ɛbɔ ɛwɔkɛ kɛ bɛbanyia Maanle Maanle Koyɛlɛ Eku ne anwo anyelazo, amaa bɛamaa sonla adenle nee ye ngoane nu fanwodi mɔɔ hyia kpalɛ la anyia nidi nee ɛzonlenlɛ kpalɛ wɔ ewiade amuala la,

Kɛmɔ adenle ɛhye mɔ nee edwɛkɛ mɔɔ bɛdie bɛto nu la ɛdelɛ kpalɛ nwo hyia kpole kpalɛ maa ɛwɔkɛ ɛhye ba nuhua fɔɔnwo la ati,

Yɛkɛ kɛkala,

Alemgbunli Kɛhyire ne

Bɔ Aleɛbɔ Munlata Ngyinlazo Ɛhye mɔɔ fane Sonla Ndemele Nwo la nolo kɛ ɔle ngyinlazo mɔɔ menli kɔsɔɔti nee maanle kɔsɔɔti baho adwu nwo, amaa akee sonla biala nee eku biala mɔɔ wɔ menli ne anu la, bamaa Nolo Ɛhye adɛnla bɛ adwenle nu dahuu, abɔ mɔdenle wɔ ngilehilelɛ nee ngitolɛ nu amaa bɛabu ndelebɛbo nee fanwodi ɛhye mɔ, na ɔlua ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛyɛ mɔɔ bɛkɔ arilɛ, maanle nu nee maanle maanle avinli la azo, bɛamaa bɛalie bɛado nu na bɛali bɛ nzi kpalɛ wɔ ewiade amuala, wɔ Maanle mɔɔ boka nwo la amra bɛ ngomekye bɛ avinli nee menli mɔɔ wɔ bɛ azilɛ mɔɔ bɛdi bɛ nzonle la azo la avinli.

Mbuluku 1

Alesama kɔsɔɔti bɛwo bɛ mɔɔ bɛdele bɛ nwo na bɛsɛ wɔ eniililɛ nee adenlezilɛ nu. Bɛlɛ adwenle nee ahonle mɔɔ bɛva bɛwula bɛ nu la, na ɔwɔ kɛ bɛyɛ bɛ nyɛleɛ kɛ menli mɔɔ bɛle mediema la.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 2

Bosome biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔnyia ndetenle nee fanwodi kɔsɔɔti mɔɔ bɔbɔ wɔ Nolo ɛhye anu la, mɔɔ ngakyile biala ɛnle nu, kɛ mɔɔ le menli debie, nwole nuhye, raalɛ anzɛɛ nrenyia, aneɛ, ɛzonlenlɛ, maanyɛlɛ anzɛɛ adwenle gyɛne biala, maanle anzɛɛ eku mɔɔ vi nu la, anwonyia, awole anzɛɛ dibilɛ gyɛne biala.

Bieko, ngakyile biala ́ngyina maanyɛlɛ, mɛla anzɛɛ ewiade amuala gyinlabelɛ mɔɔ manle anzɛɛ azɛlɛ mɔɔ ahenle vi nu la de nu la azo, saa ɔde ɔ ti anwo, ɔwɔ ninyɛne nzoanlɛ nu, ɔnli ɔ ti anwo anzɛɛ ɔwɔ tumililɛ nzinlodolɛ gyɛne biala abo o.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 3

Aweɛne biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔtɛnla aze, ɔnyia fanwodi yɛɛ ɔ banbɔ eku zo.

Gyima 4

Awie biala ɛnle kɛ ɔyɛ kɛnganvola anzɛɛ ɔtenla kɛ kɛnganvola nu; bɛbara kɛnganvolayɛlɛ nee kɛnganvoladɔlenɛ wɔ bɛ adenle biala azo.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 5

Bɛnrɛyɛ awie biala ayelɛ anzɛɛ bɛnrɛyɛ ye atisesebɛ, nyɛleɛ mɔɔ ɛnle nsonle anzɛɛ mɔɔ gua anyunlunyia aze, yɛɛ bɛnrɛdu ye folɛ kpalɛ biala le kɛ ɛhye la.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 6

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ bɛbu ye kɛ sonla wɔ mɛla anyunlu wɔ ɛleka biala.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 7

Bɛ muala bɛlɛ fealeraa mɛla anyunlu yɛɛ bɛfɛta kɛ bɛnyia mɛla ne anwo banbɔlɛ kokye mɔɔ nyeyelielɛ ɛnle nu a. Bɛ muala bɛfɛta kɛ bɛbɔ bɛ nwo bane wɔ nyeyelielɛ biala mɔɔ buale Ɛwɔkɛbɔlɛ ɛhye anwo anzɛɛ debie biala mɔɔ maa awie yɛ nyeyelielɛ zɔhane anwo.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 8

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔkɔ maanle ne kɔɔto mɔɔ fɛta la anyunlu kɛ ɔbanyia atenlene mɔɔ fɛta wɔ gyima biala mɔɔ bɔ ye ndenle kpalɛ mɔɔ mɛla titile anzɛɛ mɛla biala ɛva ɛmaa ye la abo.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 9

Awiē biala ēnle adenle kɛ ɔkyi awiē, ɔbado ye efiade anzɛɛ ɔbavuanle ye mgbane mgbane.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 10

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔsukoa pɛlepɛlelilɛ nu wɔ bagua nu asɔnudzi nu, mɔɔ kɔndɛma mɔɔ sonla biala nwo ɛnle nu na ɔti ɔ nwo la a die ɔ nzo a, wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛlɛbɔ ye adenle nee ninyɛne mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ ɔyɛ nolo, yɛɛ amozii kpole biala mɔɔ bɛbɔ ye la.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 11

Awie biala mɔɔ bɛdwe ye kɔ kɔɔto kɛ yeyɛ ɛtane la lɛ adenle kɛ bɛbu ye kɛ ɔnde ɛtane nwo kɔkpula kɛ mɛla kile kɛ ɔlile fɔlɛ wɔ bagua nu fɔlebualɛ mɔɔ bɛvale ninyɛne kɔsɔɔti mɔɔ hyia kɛ ɔfa ɔlie ɔ nwo la bɛmanle ye la anu.

Bɛnrɛbu awie biala fɔlɛ wɔ mɛla ɛtane biala anwo ɔlua debie mɔɔ ɔyɛle anzɛɛ yeanyɛ mɔɔ ɛnee ɛnle mɛla ɛtane wɔ maanle nu anzɛɛ maanle maanle avinli mɛla zo wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɔyɛle ye la. Eza bɛnrɛva asɛnzɛlɛ mɔɔ anu yɛ se kpalɛ bɛnrɛdo ɔ nwo tɛla mɔɔ ɛnee wɔ ɛkɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛyɛle mɛla ɛtane ne la.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 12

Awie biala ɩnlɛ adenle kɛ ɔfa anwo-adidilɛ kɔ awie fofolɛ anwo dɔɔnwo, ye abusua, ye sua nu anzɛɛ ye ngɛlata nu, anzɛɛ ɔsɛkye ye duma kpalɛ nee ye anyunlunyia. Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ mɛla bɔ ye nwo ban fvi anwo-adidilɛ anzɛɛ anyunlunyia-ɛsɛkyelɛ zɛhae anwo.

Ngɛlɛlera 13

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔkɔ ɛleka biala mɔɔ ɔkulo na ɔtɛnla maanle biala hye azo.

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔfi maanle biala anu, mɔɔ ɔdaye ye maanle kɔsɔɔti boka nwo la, yɛɛ ɔbahola yeazia yeara ye maanle nu.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 14

Awozonle nu sonla biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔkpondɛ yɛɛ ɔnwu ɛleka mɔɔ ɔkɛpɛ ɔ nwo zo kpole wɔ maanle gyɛne nu a debie ɛnrɛyɛ ye wɔ amaneɛnwunle ti.

Bɛnrɛla adenle ɛhye azo wɔ ndɛne biala mɔɔ vi amamuo nwo ɛtane ngakyile anzɛɛ nyɛleɛ mɔɔ kpo United Nations bodane nee ye ngyinlazo ngakyile la anu.

Gyɛkɛ 15

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔyɛ maanle bie amra.

Awiɛ kpalɛ biala ɛnlɛ adenle kɛ ɔfa mɛla nwo adenle bie anu ɔbaye awie mɔɔ le maninli la ɔbavi ye maanle nu anzɛɛ ɔbazi ye adenle kɛ ɔmmakakyi ye maanlenli nu gyinlabelɛ ne.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 16

Mrenyia nee mraalɛ mɔɔ ɛnyi la le adenle kɛ bɛgya na bɛtɛkye abusua mɔɔ kpole biala ɛnle nu wɔ bɛ menli, bɛ maanle anzɛɛ bɛ ɛzonlenlɛ nwo. Bɛlɛ adenle ko wɔ agyaleɛ nwo, wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛwɔ agyaleɛ nu la nee mekɛ mɔɔ agyaleɛ ne kɛzɛkye la.

Baa nee nrenyia mɔɔ kulo kɛ bɛgya la kɛhyia kɛ bɛbɔ nu kpalɛ bɛdie agyalɛ ne bɛto nu mɔɔ awie biala anhyɛ bɛ na bɛnli agyalɛ ne.

Abusua a le mɔɔ le maanle ne fane mɔɔ bɔbɔ ɛdeɛ ne la yɛɛ ɔle kyɛkyɛmgbɔnla nwo zo adwenle ne la, na ɔfɛta kɛ maanle ne nee arane ne bɔ ye nwo bane.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 17

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔtetɛ ɔ nwo anzɛɛ ɔ nee bie mɔ bɔ nu nyia agyapadeɛ.

Awiye biala ɛnle adenle kɛ ɔfa awiye fofolɛ agyapadeɛ wɔ adenle mɔɔ ɛntende zo.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 18

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔdwenle mɔɔ ɔkulo la anwo, ɔnea ye adwenle mɔɔ kile ye la azo, na ɔkɔ asɔne mɔɔ ɔkulo la; adenle ɛhye boka nwo kɛ ɔbahola yeakakyi ye asɔne anzɛɛ ye diedi, na ɔdaye ngome anzɛɛ ɔ nee menli gyɛne mɔɔ bɔ nu de la, bagua nu anzɛɛ fealera nu, kɛ ɔbahola yeala ye asɔne anzɛɛ ye diedi ali wɔ ngilehilelɛ, nyɛleɛ, ɛzonlenlɛ nee amanneɛyɛlɛ nu.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 19

Asonle biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔfa ye adwenle kpalɛ ɔto gua yɛɛ ɔka ye edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ɔkpondɛ la; adenle ɛhye boka nwo kɛ ɔkɛlie ye adwenle nu mɔɔ awie biala ɛnza nu, yɛɛ ɔkɛhwe, ɔkɛnyia na yeabɔ edwɛkɛ nee adwenle biala nolo wɔ adenle biala azo mɔɔ ɛzonlenlɛ biala ɛnle nu.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 20

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔfa anzodwɔlɛ adenle zo ɔboɔboa ɔ nwo ano na ɔboka eku biala anwo.

Bɛntnla awie biala kɛ ɔhɔle ɛku biala anu.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 21

Aweɛne biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔ boka ɔ maanle ne arane nwo, kɛ ɔdaye mumwa anzɛɛ ɔdua menli mɔɔ bɛva adenle fɔlɔ bɛkpa bɛ la anwo zo.

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔnwu kɛ bɛdua pɛlepɛle zo bɛye ye bɛkɔ manle gyima nu wɔ ye maanle ne anu.

Menli ne kulo a ɔbayɛ aban ne tumi gyinlabelɛ; bɛbalua abatɔnle kpalɛ mɔɔ bɛyɛ ye dahuu la azo bɛala kulo ɛhye ali, mɔɔ awie biala mɔɔ fɛta la babɔ bie mɔɔ bɛ nuhua biala lɛ tumi kɔsɔɔti, yɛɛ bɛbalua abatɔnle mɔɔ bɛfa bɛfeala anzɛɛ kɛzi bɛtoto nuhua kpalɛ mɔɔ bɛ kulo nwo la azo bɛayɛ.

Gyekeɛ 22

Bia biala, kɛ mɔɔ ɔle menli nu sonla la, lɛ adenle kɛ ɔsa kɛ ɔbanyia sonlabaka nu banebɔlɛ na ɔwɔ kɛ ɔdua maanle nu mɔdenlebɔlɛ nee ewiade amuala agɔnwolɛlilɛ zo, na ɔlua maanle biala nhyehyɛlee nee ye anwonyia nwo ninyɛne zo, ɔbanyia anyuhɔlɛ, asetɛnla nu nee amaamuo nu adenle mɔɔ anwo hyia kpalɛ maa ye anyunlunyia nee ye subane mɔɔ bɔkɔ zo anyia anyuhɔlɛ wɔ fanwodi nu la.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 23

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔyɛ gyima, kɛ ɔkpa gyima mɔɔ ɔkulo la kpalɛ, kɛ ɔnyia gyimayɛlɛ ninyɛne kpalɛ mɔɔ fɛta la, yɛɛ kɛ bɛbɔ ɔ nwo bane bɛvi gyimanyianlɛ nwo.

Awie biala, mɔɔ nyeyenu biala ɛnle nu la, lɛ ndelebɛbo kɛ ɔnyia kakatua ko wɔ gyima ko anwo.

Awie biala mɔɔ yɛ gyima la lɛ adenle kɛ ɔkɛnyia ahatualɛ kpalɛ mɔɔ fɛta na ɔkɛboa ɔdaye nee ye abusua kɛ bɛkɛdɛnla aze mɔɔ dasanba enyidɔlɛ wɔ nu, na saa ɔhyia noko a, bɛalua asetena nu banbɔlɛ ngyehyɛleɛ gyɛne zo bɛaboa ye.

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔboka nwo yɛɛ ɔtua gyimayɛvoma eku biala nwo amaa yeabɔ ye nzonle nwo bane.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 24

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔdie ɔ menle na ɔyɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ gye ye anye, mɔɔ gyima dɔnehwele mɔɔ fɛta nee mekɛ mɔɔ bɛfa bɛkɔ ɛnwomenlelielɛ mɔɔ bɛtua bɛ kakɛ boka nwo a.

Gyikiti 25

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔtɛnla asee wɔ adenle mɔɔ fɛta ye kpɔkɛdelɛ nee ye abusua ne anyunlunyia, mɔɔ aleɛ, adudoeɛ, ɛleka mɔɔ ɔkɛla nee ayileyelɛ kpalɛ nee anyunlunyia gyima mɔɔ hyia la boka nwo, yɛɛ ɔlɛ adenle kɛ ɔnyia banwodolɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ gyima ɛnle ɛkɛ, ewule, foflɛyelɛ, kunlu-mgbane, mgbanyinli anzɛɛ debie fofolɛ mɔɔ bamaa ye asetɛnla azɛkye mɔɔ ɔnvi ye ɛhulolɛ nu la kɛdo ye la.

Ɛnrenyia nee kakula le adenle kɛ bɛnea bɛ boɛ na bɛboa bɛ wɔ adenle titili zo. Ngakula kɔsɔɔti, bɛwo bɛ wɔ agyalɛ nu o anzɛɛ agyalɛ anzi o, lɛ adenle kɛ bɛnyia bɛ nwo banebɔlɛ ko ne ala wɔ menli nu.

Ngilenu 26

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔkɔ sukulu. Ɔwɔ kɛ sukulu ɛgɔlɛ yɛ mgbane, titili wɔ mɔlebɛbo sukulu ne anu. Ɔwɔ kɛ mɔlebɛbo sukulu ɛgɔlɛ yɛ kɔntelate. Ɔwɔ kɛ ninyɛne yɛlɛ nee gyima ngakyile ɛzukoalɛ ba kɛ awie biala banyia nwolɛ adenle, yɛɛ ɔwɔ kɛ sukulu kpole ɛgɔlɛ noko yɛ kɛ awie biala banyia nwolɛ adenle kɛmɔ ye adwenle nee ye anwosesebɛ de la.

Ɔwɔ kɛ sukulu ɛdelɛ boa kpalɛ maa sonla nyililɛ ba pɛpɛɛpɛ na ɔmaa sonla agbɔlɔbomaa fɛlɛtolɛ nee fanwodi kpalɛ mɔɔ sonla lɛ la anwo ɛbulɛ yɛ kpole. Ɔwɔ kɛ ɔmaa nteaseɛ, abotane nee agɔnwolɛvalɛ ba maanle maanle kɔsɔɔti, amangyinli anzɛɛ asɔne ɛzonlenlɛ nyianu ngakyile nu, na ɔboa United Nations gyimalilɛ mɔɔ ɔfa ɔkye anzodwolɛ la.

Awovolɛ lɛ adenle kɛ bɛkpa ngɛlɛlera ko mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛfa bɛmaa bɛ mra la lumua.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 27

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔfa ɔ nwo ɔboka maanle ne amaamuo nwo gyimayɛlɛ nwo mɔɔ afoa biala ɛnzi ye adenle a, kɛ ɔ nye balie nvoninli nee ndwɛne nwo, yɛɛ kɛ ɔbanyia abɔdeɛ nwo nzinlii anyunluhɔlɛ nee ye nvasoɛ ne bie.

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ bɛbɔ sunsum nu nee nwonane nu anyelielɛ mɔɔ vi abɔdeɛ nwo ninyɛne nwo ndelebɛbo, ehyilɛ anzɛɛ adwulendolɛ biala mɔɔ ɔdaye a ɔyɛle la anwo bane.

Mbuluku 28

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔnyia maanyɛlɛ nee ewiade amuala ngyehyɛleɛ kpalɛ mɔɔ adenletuto nee fanwodi mɔɔ bɔbɔle nwolɛ edwɛkɛ wɔ Edwɛkpa ɛhye anu la bahola ayɛ gyima kpalɛ wɔ nu la.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 29

Awie biala lɛ gyima mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ ɔyɛ ɔmaa menli mɔɔ bɔ nu de la, emɔti ala a ɔbahola yeanyunlu kpalɛ na yeade kɛ mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ ɔde la.

Wɔ ye nwolɛ adenle nee ye fanwodi nli ɛlilɛ nu, bɛbadendɛ awie biala anwo zo mɛla la ala kɛmɔ mɛla kile la, ɔ nwo debie kpalɛ biala ɛnle nu kɛ bɛbamaa bɛanwu bɛazie bɛ nye zo na bɛabu awie mɔɔ ɛha la noko bɛ nwolɛ adenle nee bɛ fanwodi nli, na eza bɛali subane kpalɛ nwo ninyɛne nzenzaleɛ nwo gyima, ameleba anzodwɔlɛ nee menli kɔsɔɔti boɛyɛlɛ wɔ menli fanwodi maanle nu.

Adenle biala ɛnle ɛkɛ mɔɔ bɛbahola bɛalua zo bɛava nwolɛ adenle nee fanwodi ɛhye mɔ bɛali gyima mɔɔ ɛnle kɛpɛ nu kɛ mɔɔ United Nations bodane nee ye ngyinlazo kile la.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 30

Debie biala ɛnle Bagua Nolo ɛhye anu mɔɔ bɛkɛva bɛkile kɛ Maanle biala, eku anzɛɛ ahenle biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔyɛ debie anzɛɛ ɔdi ninyɛne mɔɔ bɔbodane mɔdenle kɛ ɔbaziezie ndenle nee fanwodi mɔɔ bɛzolo bɛ nwo edwɛkɛ wɔ ɛke la azo a.
