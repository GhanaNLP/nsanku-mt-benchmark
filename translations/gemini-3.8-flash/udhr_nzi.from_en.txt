Ewiade Amuala Nolobɔlɛ Wɔ Sonla Ndilenu Nwo

Mɔlebɛbo edwɛkɛ

Kɛmɔ ahotuvolɛ wulirɛ ne mɔɔ wɔ sonla abusua ne amula asa nu nee bɛ pɛlepɛlelilɛ mɔɔ awie ɛngola ɛnlie ɛnvi bɛ sa la a le fanwodi, pɛlepɛlelilɛ nee anwunvnezelɛ mɔɔ wɔ ewiade la abo gyinlabelɛ la ati,

Kɛmɔ sonla adenlezhe mɔɔ bɛambu na bɛdole bɛ aze la ɛva atisesebɛyɛlɛ mɔɔ ɛzi awie biala adwenle ɛra la, na bɛbɔ ewiade fofolɛ mɔɔ sonla biala banyia fanwodi wɔ edwɛkahanlɛ nee diedi nu, kɛmɔ eza bɛanyia fanwodi bɛavi ɛzulolɛ nee ehyia nu la nolo kɛ ɔle debie mɔɔ sonla kɔsɔɔti kpondɛ kpalɛ la ati,

Kɛ mɔɔ ɔhyia kpalɛ kɛ, amaa sonla anrɛɛ amgba anyia adenle kɛ ɔfa awieleɛ debie kɛ ɔkpondɛ ngoane la ɔtɛtɛ atisesebɛyɛlɛ nee amumuyɛlɛ nzi la, ɔwɔ kɛ mɛla bɔ sonla adenlema nwo bane la,

Kɛmɔ ɔnwo hyia kɛ bɛbamaa agɔnwolɛlilɛ kpalɛ adenle wɔ maanle maanle avinli la ati,

Kɛmɔ United Nations mɛlɛbela ne mɔ ɛla ye ali wɔ bɛ Mɛla Kpole ne anu kɛ bɛdie sonla amomuo adenle kpole ne mɔ, sonla enyidelenle nee ye nvasoɛ, yɛɛ mrenyia nee mraalɛ pɛlepɛlelilɛ adenle ne mɔ bɛdi, yɛɛ bɛbɔ kpɔkɛ kɛ bɛbamaa menli adwenle ayɛ kpalɛ na bɛanyia ɛbɛlabɔlɛ kpalɛ wɔ fanwodi kpole nu la ati,

Kɛmɔ Maanmaan mɔɔ bɔ nu yɛ Maanmaan Kpole Kokye ne anu amra ɛbɔ ɛwɔkɛ kɛ bɛ nee Maanmaan Kpole Kokye ne babɔ nu ayɛ gyima amaa ewiade amuala abu yɛɛ yeali sonla anyunlunyia ngakyile nee fanwodi titili mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ sonla nyia la azo,

Kɛmɔ mɔɔ nwo hyia kpalɛ maa ɛwɔkɛ ɛhye mɔɔ bɛbɔ la bamaa yeali munli a le kɛ bɛte nwolɛ adenle nee fanwodi ɛhye mɔ abo kɛnlɛma la ati,

Yɛɛ kɛkala,

Alemgbunli Nyianu ne

Bɔ ewiade amuala Menli Ndinli kɛ ɔle bodane ko maa menli kɔsɔɔti nee maanle maanle kɔsɔɔti, kɛ noko awozonle biala nee eku biala mɔɔ wɔ maanle ne anu la, bamaa Yebele Ewiade Amuala Menli Ndinli Nwolɛ Edwɛkɛ ɛhye adɛnla bɛ adwenle nu dahuu, abɔ mɔdenle wɔ ngilehilelɛ nee ngitolɛ nu amaa bɛabu ndinli nee fanwodi ɛhye mɔ, na ɔlua ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛkɔ zo bɛyɛ wɔ maanle ne anu nee maanle maanle avinli la ati, bɛamaa menli kɔsɔɔti adelɛ nu na bɛali zolɛ pɛlepɛle, wɔ Maanle mɔɔ boka nwo la bɛ menli ne mɔ avinli nee menli mɔɔ wɔ maanle mɔɔ wɔ bɛ tumi bo la amuala avinli.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 1

Menli kɔsɔɔti bɛwo bɛ la bɛde bɛ nwo yɛɛ bɛsɛ wɔ enrinli nee ndelebɛbo nu. Bɛlɛ adwenle nee ahonle mɔɔ bua ndɛne la, yemɔti ɔwɔ kɛ bɛyɛ bɛ nwo ninyɛne kɛ adiema nee ɔ gɔnwo la.

Ngɛlɛlera 2

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔnyia ndetenlene nee fanwodi kɔsɔɔti mɔɔ bɔbɔ wɔ Nolo ɛhye anu la, mɔɔ ngakyile biala ɛnle nu, kɛ mɔɔ le amusu, nwolɛ nzonilɛ, nrenyia anzɛɛ raalɛ, aneɛ, asɔne, maanyɛlɛ anzɛɛ adwenle fofolɛ biala, maanle anzɛɛ maanyɛlɛ nu ɛzonlenlɛ, anwonyia, ewule anzɛɛ dibilɛ fofolɛ biala.

Bikye ɛhye noko, bɛnrɛyɛ ngakyile biala wɔ maanyɛlɛ, mɛla anzɛɛ ewiade amuala gyinlabelɛ mɔɔ kpalɛ anzɛɛ azule mɔɔ sonla ko vi zolɛ la lɛ la azo, kɛ ɔde ɔ ti anwo, ɔwɔ ninyɛne nwo tumi lilɛ bo, ɔtɛnyianle ɔ ti anwo tumi lilɛ anzɛɛ ɔwɔ tumi biala mɔɔ bɛkpɔkɛ zolɛ la bo.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 3

Awie biala lɛ ngoane, fanwodi nee sonla nwo banebɔlɛ nwo adenle.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 4

Awie biala ɛnle kɛ bɛyɛ ye akɛlɛ anzɛɛ sonla kpoloma; bɛdua akɛlɛyɛlɛ nee akɛlɛ ɛtɔlɛ nee ye ɛdɔnelɛ azo wɔ bɛ ninyɛne kɔsɔɔti anu.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 5

Awie biala ɛnle adenle kɛ ɔbaye awie nyane anzɛɛ ɔbamaa ye amaneɛnwunle, anzɛɛ ɔbayɛ ye atisesebɛ nu, ngakyile nu anzɛɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ bamaa ye anyunlu agu aze anzɛɛ duma ɛtane aze.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 6

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ bɛbu ye kɛ sonla wɔ mɛla anyunlu wɔ ɛleka biala.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 7

Awiɛne amuala le kɔsɔɔti wɔ mɛla ne anyunlu yɛɛ bɛfɛta kɛ mɛla ne bɔ bɛ nwo bane kɔsɔɔti mɔɔ nyeyelielɛ biala ɛnle nu a. Awiɛne amuala lɛ adenle kɛ bɛbɔ bɛ nwo bane kɔsɔɔti wɔ nyeyelielɛ biala mɔɔ bu Ezinzalɛ Edwɛkɛ ɛhye azo la anwo yɛɛ nyeyelielɛ zɛhae anyezonlenlɛ biala anwo.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 8

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔnyia ateletele kpalɛ wɔ kɔɔto mɔɔ fɛta la ɛkɛ amaa mɛla mɔɔ bubu ninyɛne mɔɔ fɛta ye kpalɛ mɔɔ kplɛmɛla anzɛɛ mɛla ɛva ɛmaa ye la azo.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 9

Awie biala ɛnle adenle kɛ ɔkye awie, ɔto ye efiade anzɛɛ ɔdu ye maanle nu mgbane mgbane.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 10

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ pɛpɛpɛ kɛ asolo asɔretia biala mɔɔ gyi ɔ nwo zo yɛɛ pɛlepɛlelilɛ nu la tie ye edwɛkɛ wɔ bagua nu, wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛlɛbɔ ye adenle nee ninyɛne mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ ɔyɛ la anwo kpɔkɛ, yɛɛ amumuyɛle biala mɔɔ bɛfa bɛbɔ ye somolɛ la anwo kpɔkɛ.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 11

Awie biala mɔɔ bɛdwe ye kɔ kɔɔto wɔ mɛla ɛtane bie anwo la lɛ adenle kɛ bɛbu ye kɛ ɔnde ɛtane kɔkpula kɛ bɛdua mɛla zo bɛkile ye fɔlɛ wɔ bagua nu asɔnudzi mɔɔ bɛva ndelebɛbo kɔsɔɔti mɔɔ hyia kɛ ɔfa ɔlie ɔ nwo la bɛmaa ye.

Bɛnrɛbu awie biala fɔlɛ wɔ nvonle kpole biala anwo ɔlua debie mɔɔ ɔyɛle anzɛɛ yeanyɛ la ati, mɔɔ zɔhane mekɛ ne mɔɔ ɔyɛle zɔ la, na ɔnle nvonle kpole biala wɔ maanle ne anzɛɛ maanle maanle mɛla nu. Eza bɛnrɛva ɛsɔlɔlɛ mɔɔ anu yɛ se kpalɛ bɛnrɛdoa ye bɛndɛla mɔɔ ɛnee bɛdua wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛyɛle nvonle ne la.

Gyima 12

Awie biala nde adenle kɛ ɔfa anwosesebɛ ɔdi awie nyunlu wɔ ye ngoane nu, ye abusua nu, ye sua nu anzɛɛ ye ngɛlata nu, yɛɛ awie biala ɛnle adenle kɛ ɔsɛkye ye duma anzɛɛ ɔgu ye nyunlu aze. Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ mɛla bɔ ye nwo bane fi ngɛbakyɛlɛ anzɛɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ zɛhae la anwo.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 13

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔkɔ ɛleka mɔɔ ɔkulo na ɔtɛnla maanle biala mɔɔ ɔwɔ nu la anu kɛmɔ ɔkulo la.

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔvi maanle biala anu, mɔɔ ɔdaye ye maanle noko boka nwo la, yɛɛ ɔkɔhɔ ye maanle nu bieko.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 14

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔkpondɛ na ɔnyia ɛvealekae wɔ maanle gyɛne nu ɔlua kpɔdekpɔdelɛ ti.

Ɔnwo adenle ɛhye ɛnva nwo wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛlɛbɔ afoa bɔbɔ mɔɔ vi amanyɛsɛm nwo amumuyɛle anzɛɛ nyɛleɛ mɔɔ kpo United Nations bodane nee ye ngyinlazo ne mɔ la anu.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 15

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔyɛ maanle bie amonibaa.

Bɛnrɛlie awie biala manle nwo adenle bɛnrɛvi ye asa nu mgbane mgbane, yɛɛ bɛnrɛzi ye adenle kɛ ɔkakyi ye manle nwo adenle.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 16

Mrenyia nee mraalɛ mɔɔ ɛyɛ mgbanyima la, mɔɔ bɛnzikpa bɛ nwo wɔ bɛ abusua, maanle anzɛɛ ɛzonlenlɛ nwo la, lɛ adenle kɛ bɛgya na bɛkye abusua. Bɛlɛ adenle kɛ bɛbanyia adenle ko wɔ agyaleɛ nwo, wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛwɔ agyaleɛ nu la nee mekɛ mɔɔ agyaleɛ ne kɛbɔ la.

Bia agyaleɛ nu wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ menli nwiɔ ne mɔɔ bɛkulo kɛ bɛgya la fi bɛ ahonle nu na bɛ nye lie nwole kpalɛ la ala.

Abusua a le ekyi nu na ninyɛne kpalɛ mɔɔ bɔ maanyɛlɛ bo na ɔwɔ kɛ maanyɛlɛ nee maanle ne bɔ ye nwo bane.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 17

Awiye biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔfa debie ɔyɛ ɔdaye ɔ ngomekye ɛdeɛ anzɛɛ ɔ nee bie mɔ abɔ nu ayɛ bɛ ɛdeɛ.

Awie biala ɛnlɛ adenle kɛ ɔfa awie fofolɛ agyapadeɛ mɔɔ ngilenu biala ɛnle nu.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 18

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔdwenle ninyɛne nwo kɛ mɔɔ ɔkulo la, ɔnea ye adwenle nu, na ɔkɔ ɛzonlenlɛ mɔɔ ɔkulo la; adenle ɛhye boka nwo kɛ ɔbahola yeahakyi ye ɛzonlenlɛ anzɛɛ mɔɔ ɔdie ɔdi la, na ɔlɛ adenle kɛ ɔngome anzɛɛ ɔ nee awie mɔ bɔ nu, wɔ bagua nu anzɛɛ fefele nu, bamaa ye ɛzonlenlɛ anzɛɛ mɔɔ ɔdie ɔdi la ala ali wɔ ngilehilelɛ, nyɛleɛ, ɛzonlenlɛ nee ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛyɛ la anu.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 19

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔte nganeɛ na ɔka ye adwenle kɛ mɔɔ ɔkpondɛ la; adenle ɛhye bie a le kɛ ahenle balie adwenle biala ado nu mɔɔ awie biala ɛntoto nu, na ɔbanyia, ɔbalie na yeaha ngelɛlera nee adwenledwenle ngakyile yealuakɛ adenle biala mɔɔ bɛdua zo bɛka edwɛkɛ la azo, mɔɔ maanle hɔhyelɛ biala ɛnzi ye adenle.

Gyɛne 20

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔyɛ anzɛɛ ɔkɔ anzodwolɛ ayia nee eku biala anu mɔɔ ɔkulo la.

Ɔnle kɛ bɛhyɛ awie biala kɛ ɔhɔle eku biala anu.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 21

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔfa ɔ nwo ɔwula ye maanle ne amulelilɛ nu, ɔdaye mumua ɔ nwo anzɛɛ ɔlua menli mɔɔ bɛkpa bɛ wɔ fanwodi nu la azo.

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔsa ɔsa ɔnyia maangyele ɛzonlenlɛ nwonlomɔ wɔ ye maanle ne anu.

Menli ne ɛhulolɛ a bayɛ mgbanyima ne mɔ tumi ne anwo gyinlabelɛ a; bɛbalua mekɛ mɔɔ bɛkpa na bɛdi nɔhalɛ wɔ abatɔne mɔɔ awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔto na yɛbɔkole, mɔɔ bɛtoa ye fealera nu anzɛɛ adenle fofolɛ mɔɔ ɔ nee ye sɛ mɔɔ bɛfa bɛto aba kɛmɔ bɛkulo la azo bɛala kɛzi menli ne kulo la ali.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 22

Awie biala, kɛ mɔɔ ɔle menli nu sonla la, lɛ adenle kɛ ɔnyia asetɛnla nu banbɔlɛ na ɔwɔ adenle kɛ, ɔlua maanle nu mɔdenlebɔlɛ nee maanle maanle avinli agɔnwolɛlilɛ zo, na ɔkɔ do kɛ mɔɔ Maanle biala ngyehyɛleɛ nee ye anwonyia de la, ɔnyia sɔkye, asetɛnla nu nee amaamuo nu adenle mɔɔ anwo hyia kpalɛ maa ye enililɛ nee ye sonlabaka nu anyuhɔlɛ mɔɔ eku biala ɛnle nu la.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 23

Awenle biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔyɛ gyima, ɔkpa gyima mɔɔ ɔkulo la, gyimayɛlɛ nwo ninyɛne kpalɛ mɔɔ fɛta la, yɛɛ banbɔlɛ mɔɔ teta gyimayeelɛ.

Awie biala, mɔɔ nyebolo biala ɛnle nu la, lɛ ndelebɛbo kɛ ɔnyia ahatualɛ ko wɔ gyima ko ti.

Awie biala mɔɔ yɛ gyima la lɛ adenle kɛ ɔkɛnyia ahatualɛ kpalɛ mɔɔ fɛta, mɔɔ bamaa ɔdaye nee ye abusua alie bɛ nwo ɛsane mɔɔ fɛta sonla ɛnrinli, na saa ɔhyia noko a, bɛalua ndenle gyɛne zɔhane azo bɛabɔ bɛ nwo bane wɔ asetɛnla nu.

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔkɛboɔboa gyimayɛvoma eku nwole yɛɛ ɔkɛhɔ nu ɔkɛboka nwo amaa yeabɔ ye ninyɛne nwo bane.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 24

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔdie ɔ menle yɛɛ ɔgye ɔ nwo, mɔɔ gyima dɔɔnwo mɔɔ bɛyɛ la anwo ndetelɛ mɔɔ fɛta yɛɛ ɛnwomenlelielɛ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛtua kakɛ la boka nwo.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 25

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔtɛnla asee wɔ adenle mɔɔ fɛta ye kpɔkɛdelɛ nee ye nee ye abusua ne anyelielɛ nwo, mɔɔ aleɛ, adilɛ, azua nee ayileliele nwo ngyehyɛleɛ nee asetɛnla nu nzonle mɔɔ hyia la boka nwo, na eza ɔlɛ adenle kɛ ɔbanyia bangebɔlɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ gyima ɛnle ɛkɛ, anwodolɛ, anwosunsumle, akunlenliayɛlɛ, mgbanyinliayɛlɛ anzɛɛ asetɛnla nu ninyɛne nwo ɛngyenlɛ mɔɔ vi tɛnlabelɛ mɔɔ ɔtumi ɔndɔle zo la anu.

Ɛnlonlile nee ngakula nwohyee fɛta nwozelɛ nee moalɛ titilii. Ngakula kɔsɔɔti, saa bɛwulale bɛ agyalɛ nu o anzɛɛ agyalɛ anzi o, banyia nwozelɛ ko ne ala wɔ eku ne anu.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 26

Awenle biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔkɔ sukulu. Sukululilɛ bayɛ mgbane, titili wɔ ahyɛseɛ nee ngilenu debiezukoalɛ nu. Ahyɛseɛ debiezukoalɛ bayɛ hyee kɛ awie biala kɔ. Bɛbayɛ ninyɛne nwo ndetelɛ nee gyimayɛlɛ nwo ndetelɛ bamaa sonla biala anyia nwolɛ adenle, yɛɛ sukulu kpole nu debiezukoalɛ noko bayɛ mɔɔ sonla biala banyia nwolɛ adenle pɛpɛɛpɛ kɛ mɔɔ ɔ nwo ndelebɛbo de la.

Sukulu ɛdelɛ bahɔ lae awie biala nwonane nu mini kpole nee kɛ ɔbamaa sonla adenle nee adenletile mɔɔ gyi hɔlɛ biala anwo ɛbulɛ ayɛ kpole. Ɔbamaa ndelebɛbo, abotane nee agɔnwolɛvalɛ ara maanle biala, eku mɔɔ bavi maanle ngakyile nu nee ɛzonlenlɛ nu avinli, eza ɔbamaa United Nations gyima mɔɔ le anzondwolɛ ɛbɔlɛ nwo gyima la ahɔ zo.

Awozonli lɛ adenle titili kɛ bɛkpa ngakyile ngakyile ngilenu nwo debiezukoalɛ mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛfa bɛmaa bɛ mra la.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 27

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔfa ɔ nwo ɔboka kɛrɛnwo nwo mgbɔlandane gyima biala anwo wɔ maanle ne anu, ɔ nye kpalye adwumanyianlɛ nwo, na ɔ nwo bie ala abɔdeɛ nwo nwomazukoalɛ anyuhɔlɛ nee nvasoɛ mɔɔ vi nu ba la anu.

Awie biala lɛ adenle kɛ bɛbɔ ye subane nee nwonane nu anyelielɛ mɔɔ vi abɔdeɛ nu nrɛlɛbɛ, ɛgengaleɛ anzɛɛ ndonwo gyima mɔɔ ɔdaye ɔyɛle la anwo bane.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 28

Awiye biala lɛ adenle kɛ ɔtɛnla eku mɔɔ yelielɛ nee maanle maanle agɔnwolɛlilɛ nu mɔɔ nwolɛ adenle nee fanwodi mɔɔ bɔbɔ mɛla ɛhye anu la bahola ayɛ gyima kpalɛ.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 29

Awie biala lɛ gyima mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ ɔdi ye wɔ maanle ne anu a, ɔluakɛ laleɛ ne ala a ɔbahola yeanyia ɛnyelɛzole kpalɛ mɔɔ di munli wɔ ye sonlabɔleɛ nu a.

Mɔɔ awozonle biala kɛlala ye adenle nee ye fanwodi ali la, ɔwɔ kɛ ɔdi mɛla mɔɔ bɔbodane mɔɔ wɔ zo la ala azo kɛmɔ bɛhyehyɛ ye mɛla nu kɛ ɔkɛyɛ mɔɔ ɔbamaa bɛanwu bɛahyehyɛ na bɛabu awie mɔ adenle nee bɛ fanwodi, na eza bɛali abrabɔ kpalɛ, maanle nu anzodwolɛ nee awie biala anwosesebɛyɛlɛ mɔɔ fɛta nwo dwuma wɔ demokrase eku nu.

Nwolɛ adenle nee fawodi ɛhye mɔ, ɔnle kɛ bɛdua debie biala azo bɛfa bɛdi gyima mɔɔ ɛnle United Nations bodane nee ye ngyinlazo mɔɔ ɔfa ɔyɛ gyima la anwo fɔlɛ.

Edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ 30

Ɛhye mɔ amuala mɔɔ wɔ Edwɛkpagbalɛ ɛhye anu la, bɛnrɛva bɛnrɛmaa Maanle biala, eku anzɛɛ ahenle biala adenle kɛ ɔyɛ debie anzɛɛ ɔdi gyima biala mɔɔ bamaa adenle nee fanwodi mɔɔ bɛha nwolɛ edwɛkɛ wɔ ɛke la azɛkye.
