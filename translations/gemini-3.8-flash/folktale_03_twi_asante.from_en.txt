SƐDEA ƐBƐYƐE A NYANSA BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU

Wɔse na Kwaku Ananse wɔ hɔ, na ɔpraa nyansa nyinaa boaboaa ano kɔbɔɔ mu baabiako, na ɔde guu koraa mu. Afei ɔkaa sɛ ɔbɛforo dua akɔsɛn so, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a nyansa a ɛwɔ asaase so nyinaa bɛsa.

Enti ɔfaa mu sɛ ɔne no rekɔ, na bere a ɔduruu dua a na ɔrekɔsɛn so no ase no, ɔfaa ahoma, na ɔde kyekyeree kora no ho, na ɔsɛn no n’anim, na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔreforo dua no. Ɔforoeɛ, na ɔforoeɛ, na ɔforoeɛ; nanso anyɛ yie. Ɔbɔɔ mmɔden bio, ɔyɛe bio sɛ ɔreforo, na ɔreforo, na ɔreforo; nanso anyɛ yie.

Afei ne ba Ntikuma a na ɔgyina hɔ no kaa sɛ, "O, w’ani awu paa, anka ɛnyɛ yie sɛ wobɛdane kora no ato w'akyi, na afei akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho sɛ anka wobɛtumi aforo?" Ɔno (Ananse) kaa sɛ, "Firi me so kɔ, wo ne w’abɛbusem dadaw no." Afei ɔdanee sɛ ɔrebɛforo bio sɛdeɛ na ɔyɛ no kan no, nanso bio, anyɛ yie. Ɛnna ɔdwenee ho kɔɔ akyiri, na (awieeɛ koraa no) ɔfaa kora no de guu n'akyi. Ɛnna ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔreforo, na ɔforoo no ntɛmntɛm, Kra! kra! kra! (ne nne a na ɔde reforo no); na ɔrekɔ no. Ɔduruu baabi a mman no hyɛɛ aseɛ trɛɛtee firi dua no mu no, na ɔkaa (kyerɛɛ ne ho) sɛ, "Me, Kwaku Ananse, mereka abosom nketewa no mu baako, Afio din! Anka mewu koraa na eye, me ba a osua seesei, osua seesei, osua seesei yi—me ara na meboaboa nyansa nyinaa ano (seesei na medwene) baabiako, nanso ebi kae a menyaa anhunu mpo, na hwe! me ba, akokoaa a oda so nom nufu yi, akyere me." Enti osoo kora no mu, na sunsuane nnyegye bae, tintini! na oto twenee, na petee nnyegye bae, tesee! Sɛnea obiara nyaa nyansa nie; na obiara a wankɔ hɔ ntɛm (kɔfaa bi no)—mepa kyɛw sɛ mereka saa—yɛ kwasea.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
