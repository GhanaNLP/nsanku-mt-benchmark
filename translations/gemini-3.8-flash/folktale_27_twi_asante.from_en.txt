Yɛnni aseɛ koraa, yɛnni aseɛ koraa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokorɛ)

EBUSUAKUO BIARA MU NO, OBIPƆTEE BIARA NNIM A ƆNYƐ NYANSAFOƆ KORAA

ƆBAA bi na ɔwɔ hɔ, na ɔwoo mma baanan. Nea ɔyɛ panyin wɔ wɔn mu no yɛɛ kwasea paa ara ma ɛtra so. Na ɔnnim biribiara yɛ, enti ne nuanom mmarima ne mmaa no kae sɛ, "Yɛntumi ne no ntena, enti ma ɔmfiri fie ha nkɔ." Enti—mepaw kyɛw sɛ mede asɛmfua yi redi dwuma (wɔ mo mpanyimfo anim)—saa kwasea no kɔsii atenaeɛ maa ne ho na ɔtenaa mu. Saa atenaeɛ no, na anwea nko ara na ɛwɔ hɔ. Na ɔyɛɛ akyɛdeɛ na ɔde gyigye ne ho ani. Ɔbaa panyin no (kyerɛ sɛ ɔna no) ne ne mma no kaa akyi. Ɔsɔree da koro sɛ ɔrekɔ afuo mu, na ɔhunuu sɛ abofra bi te dua tokuro bi mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Hwan na ɔwɔ hɔ?" Abofra no anyi no ano. Ɔbaa no kɔɔ hɔ, na ɔhwɛɛ abofra no yie, ɔsɔɔ ne nsa mu, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma yɛnkɔ fie." Na abofra no sɔree, na ɔdii n'akyi, na ɔbaa (wɔn) fie. Ɔbaa no kaa kyerɛɛ ne mma no sɛ, "Monhwɛ abofra fɛfɛɛfɛ a manya no, mommfa me sekwan mma me na mentwitwa ne ti nhwi tenten no ngu." Afei na ɔbaa no nnim sɛ abofra no yɛ mmoatia no mu baako, na ɛno nti na ne ti nhwi ayɛ sakasaka saa no. Enti ɔgyee sekwan no na ɔtwitwaa ne ti nhwi no guye. Adeɛ kyeeɛ no (kyerɛ sɛ adehyeeɛ da no), berɛ a wɔkae sɛ ɔnnidi no, ɔkaa sɛ ɔnnidi; na wɔbisaa no sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na asie?" Ɔbuae sɛ, "Sɛ moampɛ sɛ asɛm to mo a, monsan mmfa me ti nhwi mmɛtare so bio." Ɔbaa no ne ne mma no kɔe na wɔkɔpɛɛ amamfõ afidie bi, na wɔde sraa mmoatia no ti so, na wɔfaa ne ti nhwi no de taree so. Mmoatia no faa ne nsateaa de firaa ne ti nhwi no mu, twee firi so, na ɔtow guye. Adeɛ kye pɛ a, (na ɔse,) "Momfa me ti nhwi mmrɛ me." Adeɛ sa pɛ a, (na ɔse,) "Momfa me ti nhwi mmrɛ me." Na seesei wɔyɛɛ fɔn na wɔsuanee koraa (wɔ dadwene mu). Da koro bi, abofra a ɔyɛ kwasea no kaa sɛ ɔrekɔ akɔhwɛ ne maame. Berɛ a ɔbae no, (hwɛ) na ne maame ne ne nuanom mmarima ne mmaa no ayɛ fɔn yie. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛyɛɛ dɛn na moayɛ asisa sei?" Na maame no rebɛka asɛm a ɛwɔ mu pɔtee a ɛma wɔayɛ asisa akyerɛ no no, na ne mma no aka sɛ, "Mma nka nkyerɛ no, kwasea no na ɔnie, da bɛn na ɔbɛnya nyansa?" Maame no kaa sɛ, "Mɛka." Berɛ a ɔkaa kyerɛɛ no no, ɔkaa sɛ, "O, momfa mmoatia no mmra na memfa no nkɔ me kuro mu." Maame no ne ne nuanom mmarima ne mmaa faa mmoatia no ne ne tirinwi no kɔɔ kwasea no kuro mu. Berɛ a wɔduruu hɔ, wɔ ne kuro mu no, ɔhyɛɛ mmoatia no sɛ, "Kɔkɔtena m'asɔkye so na gye w'ani kakra." Ɔkɔtenaa soɔ, na ɔwoowoo no, na ɔtoo saa dwom yi:

Ɛna woo me, abofra kwasea, Hurotom.

Agya woo me, abofra kwasea, Hurotom.

Ɛna woo me, ogyimfo bi kɛkɛ, Hurotom. Ɛna woo me, ogyimfo bi kɛkɛ, Hurotom.

Ɛna woo me, abofra kwasea, Hurotom.

Ɛna woo kwasea bi, Hurotom."

Sasabonsam no goro kɔsii sɛ ɔbrɛeɛ, ɛnna abɔfra kwasea no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ma yɛnkɔgye wo tiri nwi." Ɔne Sasabonsam no duruu fie; ɔno (Sasabonsam no) kaa sɛ, "Fa me tiri nwi no brɛ me na fa si so na ma me nkɔ." Abɔfra kwasea no nso kaa sɛ, "Wo nso, m'anwea a woatiatia so no, kora anammɔn no nyinaa so ansa na mede wo tiri nwi no atoto so." Sasabonsam no kɔe sɛ ɔrekɔyɛ adwuma no; na ɔnkoraa anammɔn (a n'ananteɛ ayɛ) no baako so pɛ na, ɔtiatiaa so yɛɛ foforɔ. Afei, sɛdeɛ ɔbɛyɛ biara, wantumi ampopa n'anammɔn no nyinaa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya, mempɛ asɛm biara, enti mede wo maame regya wo, na me nso, fa me ma me, na ma me nkɔ." Abɔfra a ɔyɛ kwasea no kaa sɛ, "Ɛnde kɔ, na me nso magye me maame (afi wo nsam)." Afei ɛna no ba bɛkotɔeɛ, kaa sɛ, "Meda wo ase, na seesei (mahunu sɛ) kwasea biara nni abusua mu." Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, sɛ wowɔ mma a, bue w'ani kɛse, na hwɛ wɔn yiye, efisɛ wonnim na ebia daakye bi wɔ ahohiahia da mu no, (wɔn mu baako) bɛba abɛhwɛ wo.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
