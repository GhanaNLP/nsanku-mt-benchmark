Just as it is easy to commit evil in the dark, just as it is easy for children to play under the moonlight, just as it is easy to speak the truth in the daylight.

NYANKONPON had three children, who were Darkness (Esum), Moon (Osrane), and Sun (Owia). And when these three children grew up, Nyankonpon let them go to the village. This one went and built his village; this one also went and built his village; and this one also went and built his village. And as they were there, their father loved Owia. And while Nyankonpon was there, he had a stool carved and said to his court servants, "Who knows what is in my mind?" And Ananse said, "I am the one who knows." When he said, "I am the one who knows," Onyankonpon made all the servants stand up. Right then, Ananse also got up to go to the town of Nyankonpon's children. When Ananse reached the road, he asked himself, "I do not know what is in his mind, yet I said I know." Then he plucked some feathers from all the birds, stuck them all over himself, flew, and perched on a shady banyan tree in Nyankonpon's town. And when the people saw the bird, they all shouted, "Ye e e e!" And Nyankonpon came out of the house under the shady tree and said, "If Ananse were here, he would have known the name of this bird, for I have said that it is Owia whom I wish to make king, and I asked who knows what is in my mind, and Ananse said he does, and I have had a stool carved, and now I have gone to harvest a yam called 'kintinkyi', and whoever guesses the name to mention it, he is the one to whom I will give it. Therefore he has gone to bring my children, but if he were here, he would have known the name of this bird." And then the bird flew away, went and plucked off the feathers, and set off until he arrived at Esum's village and said, "Your father says I should bring you." And Esum said, "All right, I will go with you." And Ananse said, "I am going to get Osrane and Owia." And Esum told him, "Let me find something for you to eat." And Ananse said, "Oh!" Esum went and took roasted corn and gave it to Ananse. And when he finished chewing the corn, he set off for Osrane's town. When he reached 'Srane's town, he said, "Your Father says I should bring you." And 'Srane said, "All right, I will go." And Ananse said, "I am going to Owia's town to get him." And Osrane said, "Let me find something for you to eat." And Ananse said, "Oh!" And Osrane made boiled yam mash for him to eat. And Ananse set off for Owia's town. When he arrived at Owia's town, he told him, "Your father says I should bring you." And Owia said, "All right, I will go with you, but let me find something for you to eat." And Ananse said, "Oh!" And Owia went and caught a sheep. When he came, he told Ananse, "I wished that my father would come here, so that he would see what I am doing, whether it is good or not, he would see it, but since he did not come and you have come, it is as though father has come, so it is this sheep of mine that I am slaughtering for you to eat." And he slaughtered the sheep, prepared it very nicely, and gave it to Ananse to eat. And Ananse said, "Let us go into the tree." And when they went, Ananse told the Sun, "Your father has carved a certain stool there, and he wants you to be the one he will give the stool to, and he has gone to dig a certain yam there and if you know its name, he will give the stool to you; and it is 'Kintinkyi.' And so that you will not forget it, I have carved a short drum for you, and I have made a mpintin drum to go with it, so that when the drum and the mpintin drum are played, then you will never forget, and the short drum will say:

Get out of here!

Get out of here!

Then the mpintin drums say:

The very large drum!

Kintinkyi, strike!'"

And they set off to go to God's town. And then they arrived at the Moon's town there and took him, and they arrived at Darkness's place and took him. All along the way, they danced to the mpintini drums. And when they reached the outskirts of town, Ananse saw a certain person and sent him to go tell God that he was coming. Then God held a gathering, and Ananse came with them and went to greet people. Then Ananse gave the message to the Spokesman, saying, "The assignment the King sent me on, I have brought them." And God said, "My children, the reason why I had you fetched is that; I have carved this stool that is sitting here, and I have gone to dig up these yams that are lying here, and whoever can identify the name of this yam and speak it, I will give this stool to him. Therefore, my eldest child is Darkness, so let him call the name." And Darkness said, "It is 'Pona'." And all the people shouted, "Noooo!" And God said, "My second child is the Moon, so let him call the name." And the Moon said, "It is Asante yam." They shouted, "Noooo!" God said, "My child who is the third is the Sun, so let him call the name." And while he was speaking, he was strutting majestically, and the dancing was ongoing:

Unshakeable and mighty!

The great one strikes!"

Then Ananse felt ashamed, and the Sun rose and stood there, and held the yam and said, "Oh! as for this, when I used to walk with Father when I was small, he taught me its name, and I have not forgotten it; its name is 'Kintinkyi'." And the people assented to him three times, "Yes! Yes! Yes!" And his father arose and stood there and said, "You, Darkness, you are the eldest, but the words which I spoke to you, you have let yourself forget them, so you have not obeyed my word; therefore only wicked deeds shall be done in your time. And now also you, Moon, the words which I, when walking with you, spoke to you, you did not obey, so as for you, only children shall delight in you. And you also, Sun, when I tell you something, you do not forget; you have listened to my counsel, so as for you, you are a king; when anyone has a matter, he does not judge it under you, because only matters of the household are judged at night without harm. And the path which I have given you to walk upon, if the Moon wishes to walk upon it, let a halo encircle you so that he may not approach you; and also when rain clouds gather, let the rainbow appear, so that your children who are under you may know that when it rains, it will not flood and carry us away. And formerly also, these stories were called the stories of the Sky God, but because Ananse has found out the thoughts of my heart, let them be called Ananse stories."

This folktale that I have told, whether it is sweet or not, take some and eat, and take some and approve of me.
