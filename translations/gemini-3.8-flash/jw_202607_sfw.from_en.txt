DWENNE HO Hwe ninyɛne mɔɔ yɛbahola yɛazukoaye afi Gibeonfo ne mɔ ɛkɛ ne na afi kɛzi Gyihova nee bɛ lili ne anu.

Sua Fi Gibeonfoɛ No Ɛkɛ

“Gibeonfoɔ ne Israel ayɛ biakoyɛ na wɔte wɔn mu.”—JOSH. 10:1.

AFE ne le 1473 B.C.E. Israelfoɔ man ne bɔboɔ be anwondolalɛ anwo adwen wɔ Anwondoma Asaase ne anwo. Israelfoɔ ne anya nkunim kɛseɛ wuraa Jericho ne Ai nkurow ne anwo. Nanso akyiri yi, debie bie a wɔanwun anwo kɛ ɔbɛsi zolɛ sili. Akwantufoɔ eku bie bɛbaa be nkyɛn, bɔbɔ kɛ befi asaase a ɛwɔ akyirikyiri bie so na bepɛ kɛ be ne Nyame man ne kye ayɔnkofa kpa.

2 Sa anantewufoɔ no le Gibeonifoɔ no. Ɛkɛ ye le mɔhɔlɛ mɔɔ bɛhanle Gibeonifoɔ no ho ndɛ wɔ Baebolo ne anu le, na nzenze ɛkɛ ngyinla le mɔhɔlɛ mɔɔ li awieye. Baebolo ne anu asɛm dɔɔnwo kile kɛ Gibeonifoɔ no ne Yisiraɛlifoɔ no tɛnlae bɔbɔ wɔ mfe dɔɔnwo mɔɔ lile zolɛ no anu. Nsɛm ɛhye mɔ le edwianu mɔɔ anye kile ma yɛ, na ɔkile ninyɛne dɔɔnwo nso mɔɔ fa Gyihova Nyamenle ho.

Nna bɛboka Hivifoɔ ne anwo, bɛle Kenaan asee nson ne mɔɔ bazo yɛɛ “bɛ anwo yɛ den” kɛ Yizilayɛfoɔ ne nuanu ko. (Deut. 7:1) Ɔti Gibiyɔnfoɔ le menma mɔɔ bɛ anwo yɛ den wɔ ɛkɔn nwo. Bɛambisa Jehovah anzɛɛ bɛannye n’adwen nwo pɛ, Israel mbanimbani lile ninyeni mɔɔ Gibeonfoɛ ne bahilele bɛ la edzi na bɛnye bɛ yɛle anzondwolenye ahyɛmu. (Josh. 9:14, 15) Yɛmmaale kyɛle na bɛnwunnle Gibeonfoɛ ne bɛ ngososo ne, nza Israelifoɛ ne kɛle bɛ anzondwolenye ahyɛmu mɔɔ bɛnye Gibeonfoɛ yɛle la azo efise bɛvale “Jehovah duman yiale ndan maale bɛ.” (Josh. 9:16-19) Bɛmaanle Gibeonfoɛ ne juma mɔɔ ɔba aze kɛ bɛyɛ dahuu kɛ bɛbɛyɛ “egya-nwunvunvunvoɛ nye nsuo-safoɛ maa ehyiabo ne nye Jehovah afɔrebɔlatɛ ne.”—Josh. 9:27.

5. Aden kɛseɛ bɔ Gibeonfoɔ la lale gyidie ali wɔ Yehowa nu?

Gibeon-foɔ hɔ sɔre bie Joshua nza kɛ ɔboa bɛ. (Josh. 10:3-7) Joshua hyɛɛ Israel-foɔ anim hɔ kɔlie bɛ, na Nyame yiraa bɛ mmɔdenbɔ so. Jehovah mpo hyɛɛ kɔmputuo kpole guu bɛ kpolotuafoɔ Amorite-foɔ asraafofokuw so na ɔnam anwonwadeɛ so maawia daa so tenten maa koni kɔ. (Josh. 10:9-14) Kɛ mɔ bɛhwehwɛɛ asomdwee apam bɛne Israel ntam, na kɛ mɔ bɛsɔre bie Joshua nza kɛ ɔboa bɛ wɔ mmere mɔ na bɛ te asiane nu, Gibeon-foɔ hɔ daa gyedie adi wɔ Jehovah nu—gyedie wɔ ne tumi mɔ ɔtumi de di ne bɔhyɛ so na ɔlie bɛ.

6 Dzu ɛhe kile yɛ dze wɔ Yehowa nwo? Yehowa bɔ ne nwo ase na ɔwɔ anwunvɔnezelɛ. Ɔhakyilɛ ɔhanle ɔhilele Izraelfoɛ kɛ “bɛkponfo asaase ne azo amra kɔsɔɔti,” na ɛhe kɛhye Gibiyɔnfoɛ noko wo nu. (Nùm. 33:51, 52) Ɔbɔbɔ nwo ne amvula, dzu ne bɛkpatalɛ kɔngoyeli ne dze Izraelfoɛ nɛɛ bɛ yɛle, Yehowa dzu anwunvɔnezelɛ manle Gibiyɔnfoɛ ne dɛnla ngoane nu. Yehowa yɛle ɛhe ɔbɔbɔ nwo ne amvula Izraelfoɛ anye ɛndwa ne ansaana bɛyɛle kɔngoyeli ne. Ne akyi ɔboale Izraelfoɛ kɛ bɛli bɛ kɔngoyeli ne azo na ɔdɔle anyebolo kponle Gibiyɔnfoɛ ne.—Djos. 9:26; 11:19.

7 Kɛzi yɛbaye Gibeonfoɛ ne ayie ɛ? Ɔnam gyidie kpole a yɛbenya wɔ Gyihova nu ne so. Kɛmɔ yɛte Gyihova su bie kpalɛ nkyɛn Gibeonfoɛ ne a na bɔwɔ hɔ mmre ne nu ne ti, yɛwɔ ngaazo dodo a ɔwɔ kɛ yɛdua zo fa yɛ werɛ hyɛ Gyihova nu bɔbɔ. (Dw. 40:4, 5) Yɛbaye Gibeonfoɛ ne ayie nko ara nso ɔnam ahobroase dwumadie a yɛbaye yɛ nwo aze ayɛ wɔ Gyihova som nu ne so. (Gyo. 9:23, 27) Adiema kɛntɛnnua bie a yɛfrɛ ye Luke renwula mmre a ɔnwunnle kɛ adiema kpanyin bie a ɔwɔ Bɛtɛl abusua ne nu yɛle zɔhane la vi. Ɔwɔ mu kɛ na Bɛtɛlfoɛ zɔhane lɛ asɛdeɛ a ɛso na ɔda adi koraa deɛ, nanso ɔnam ahobroase so yele ye nwo aze kɛ ɔbɛwɛ Kingdom Hall adansi bea ne wɔ kɔnsɛ nu. Luke hanle kɛ: “Mesukulɛle kɛ ahobroase ɛnyɛ tebea bie kɛkɛ a ɛfa wo nwo to nu. Ɔda adi wɔ deɛ yɛfa yɛ adwene kɛ yɛbayɛ anzɛɛ yɛrenyɛ ne nu.” Ɔyɛ a anka yɛbada yɛ gyidie adi wɔ Gyihova nu dabiara ɔnam kɛ yɛbafa yɛ werɛ ahyɛ ye nu wɔ mmre a yɛhyia ninyɛne a ɛyɛ se nu na yɛnam ahobroase so abɔ mmɔden ayɛ teokrasi asɛdeɛ biara.

4 ƆWƐN-ABAN

Sũasũa Gibeonfoɔ no na pe kɛ ɛkɛyɛ gyima mɔ bɛbu no kɛ ɔnsɛ (Hwɛ ɛdɛndɛnmu 7)

mfe mɛnsa ɛkɔm. Mɔ David bisalɛ ɛkɔm ne anwo debie ne, Yehowa hanle hilele ye kɛ mɔ Saul nɛɛ ye awulubo ne nle mɔ bɛyɛle Gibiyɔnfo ne mɔ mfe kakra mɔ bɛpɛle nu ne ti, bɛbɔ mɔgwa fɔ.—2 Sam. 21:1.

10 Kenga 2 Samuel 21:3-6. David nɛ Gibiɔnfɔ ne bɔbɔɔ nkanɛ nyansua dwuma a ɔkɔtɔ a ɔbɛyɛ ama bɛata bɔn a Saulo yɛɛ tiaa bɛ ne duma. Bɛbɛbɔ mɔdɛn kɛ bɛbanyan dwɛtɛ kɛ bɛbama David atua bɔn a ɔanyɛ ndɛ amfa amboa bɛ ne duma? Gibiɔnfɔ ne buaa nɔhalɛ kɛ “ɛnyɛ dwɛtɛ fitaa anzɛnsɛ dwɛtɛ kɔkɔɔ ndwuma,” ɔluakɛ mmla ne se, bɛntwe ɛpɔn bima awudifo biara. (Num. 35:30, 31) Bɛnwunnɛ kɛ bɛntumi bɛnkua nwan biara a mmla ne mma kwan. Awieyɛ ne, David maa kwan ma bɛkunkun Saulo mmabarima mrenkwa nson a bie a bɛwɔ bie wɔ mɔdɛn a Saulo bɔɔ kɛ ɔkunkun kuo ne kɔsɔɔkɔ ne nu. Akyi berɛ bi nu, esu ne tɔɔe, na ɔmɛwiee ɛkɔm ne, ɔkyerɛ kɛ bɛadie bɔn a bɛyɛɛ ne bɔbɔɔ Yehowa kɔn dɔ.—2 Sam. 21:9, 10, 14.

11 Asɛm yi kile yɛ kɛ dɛn wɔ Gyihova ho? Yɛnwun adanse kpole kɛ Gyihova le Nyamenle mɔɔ tenrɛ. (Edw. 37:28) Ɔkulo kɛ bɛyɛ menli muala pɛpɛɛpɛ. Bie a bɛhyehyɛ menli nuhua, kɛ mɔɔ ɔtaa si wɔ ahɔho nee mgbane mgbane ho la. Wɔ ye mekɛ kpalɛ nu, Gyihova bayɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛanyɛ no tenrɛ mɔɔ ye asɔfonli agyina ɔ nloa la muala pɛpɛɛpɛ. Yɛsukoa noko kɛ Gyihova kpondɛ kɛ yɛdi yɛ bɔhyɛ nzo, kɛ mɔɔ ɔkpɔndɛle kɛ Yizilɛyɛma di bɔhyɛ mɔɔ bɛbɔle kɛ bɛnee Gibiyɔnma batena asomdwoe nu la nzo la.—Fa toto Emɔso 1:9 ho.

FA NƆNDƆLƐ BOA NYAMENLE ƐZƆMƐ KPATI

13 Bɛyɛ mfe 500 wɔ Ɔhene David berɛ akyi no, Gibeonfoɔ no pue bio wɔ Baebolo no mu. Ná mfe 70 a wɔde kɔɔ nnommumfa mu wɔ Babilon no aba awieeɛ, na wɔ afe 537 B.C.E. mu no, Yudafoɔ nnommumfoɔ kuo a ɛdi kan no sane kɔɔ Jerusalem kɔkaa Amrado Zerubbabel ho. (Ezra 2:1, 2, 58) Wɔ afe 468 B.C.E. mu no, ekuo foforɔ nso sane kɔe nanso ná wɔne Ezra a ɔyɛ kyerɛwfoɔ no na ɛkɔe. (Ezra 7:1-7) Saa nnommumfoɔ a wɔsane kɔe a wɔn dodoɔ nsua pii no nyinaa, ná “Netinimfoɔ” no mmusua ka wɔn ho. (Hwɛ Ezra 2:58 ne 7:7 ase hɔ nsɛm no.) Nanso nwanwa na na wɔyɛ Netinimfoɔ no? Ná wɔyɛ “asɔredan mu asomfoɔ a wɔnnyɛ Israelnom,” na ebia na wɔn mu dodoɔ no ara yɛ Gibeonfoɔ no asefoɔ.—Hwɛ Asɛmfua Asekyerɛ, “Netinimfoɔ.”

14 Keng 1 Beresosɛm 9:2 na asehɔ asɛm no. “Asɔredan no mu asomfo” no boka mbie bɛyɛɛ mvaebaso bɛhɔle Israel duma nu no ho. Ɛhye hyia bɔbɔ naaso nnyɛ Yudafo biala mɔɔ wɔ mvaeyo nu no a ɔhɔle. Yudafo dɔɔnwo nyaa anyunnyam bie wɔ Babilon, na amba bɛnlie amba bɛnyɛ afɔlebɔ mɔɔ behyia na bɛahɔ azuzue bɛ asaase mɔɔ asɛkye no bio. Naaso mvaeyofo anokwafo no, mɔɔ biebɔbɔ yɛ Gibeonfo aseni no, bɛ kunlu hɔle kɛ bɛkonwu kɛ bɛasiesie nɔhalɛ ɛzonlenlɛ bio wɔ asɔredan mɔɔ wɔ Yerusalem no nu, na bɛyɛle bɛ ahomeka biala na bɛahɔ. Saa bɛfa bɛtoto Yudafo no ho a, Gibeonfo aseni no annya asase agyapadɛ bie wɔ Israel. Naaso, bɛhɔle kɛ bɛkɔyɛ bɛ asɔredan mu asɛnzɛna na bɛboa maa bɛasiesie Yerusalem bane no mpo.—Neh. 3:26.

15 Asɛm yi kyerɛ yɛn deɛ fa Jehovah ho? Yɛsua kyerɛ kɛ yɛn Nyame bu yeasoafoma anokwafoɔ kpole kpalɛ na ɔhwɛ bɛ daa. Mmerɛ mɔ Babilon ngokadwom wiee nu no, na anyɛ kɛ mfe 1,000 asɛn abɔ mmerɛ mɔ Jehovah gyee Gibeonfoɔ ngo nna mɔ Israel gyea Anwoba Man no nu. Ɛlɛ nu koraa no, ɔkɔle so hyiraa bɛ. Gibeonfoɔ asefoɔ nyan bɛ ti fiti Juda nna Jerusalem sɛyɛlɛ nu wo afe 607 B.C.E. Ngokadwom no ekyi no, bɛkɔle so yɛɛ asɔre nokware no kpole, na bɛboka Levi-foɔ no wo tɛmpol no nu. Bie mɔ traa tɛmpol no agyakɛ nu. (Ezra 2:70; Neh. 11:21) Na bɛyɛɛ bɛ ahotwe fiti tow, amandze, nna etualɛ nu wo bɛ tɛmpol dwuma no ti.—Ezra 7:24.

6 ABƆN-KORƐ NI

16 Kɛkɛ yɛkɔyɛ sɛ Gibeonfo ne a? Yɛn nso yɛpɛ kɛ yɛyɛ debie biala mɔ yɛkɔhɔla de asuo nukpɛyɛ som ne akyi. Yɛfi yɛn pɛ mu yɛ afɔrebɔ de som Yehowa ɔluakɛ yɛdɔ ye na yɛpɛ kɛ yɛyɛ debie mɔ ɔbɔsɔ ye ani. Na yei le nɔhalɛ wɔ Alwin, brada biala mɔ ɔwɔ Philippines ne afa nu. Ɔlale adwuma biala mɔ betua ye kakatula kpole wɔ ngo adwumakuo biala mu. Naaso na ɔpɛ kɛ ɔyɛ dɔɔnzo wɔ Yehowa som nu. Berɛ biala mɔ mantam sohwɛfo ne kɔsra ye asafo ne, Alwin gyee berɛ wɔ ye adwuma nu de suoe nsrahwɛ ne akyi na ɔboale anyigye kɛ ɔbɔwɔ akyɛde kpole wɔ asɛnka edwuma ne mu. Berɛ kɛkɛ na asɔreyɛ dɔɔnzo ekyi no, Alwin zusu kɛ ɔbɔgyaa ye adwuma ne na ɔbɔhwehwɛ biala mɔ ɔbɔma wayɛ adwuma kɛ daa akwampaeefo. Sɛ ɔbɔyɛ zɔ a, na ɔbɔhia kɛ ɔbɔyɛ afɔrebɔ wɔ sika afa nu. Naaso, ɔduru ye botae ne ho. Ye yere nso bɔbɔɔ ye ho de yɛɛ akwampaeɛ edwuma ne, na kɛ abusua no, beholale beboale menma 21 de sukulale nɔhalɛ ne. Awenye biala nni ho kɛ, Yehowa bu afɔrebɔ mɔ abusua he yɛe ne. Buka Bikɔ Ma Bɛnwun Yehowa

“Nda ye ngoan tinnin mɔ ɛnwie ɛlɛ, kɛ bɛkɔhʋ̃ bɛanwo wɔ, Nyamepɔn kʋn kpilin ne.”—YƆHANE 17:3.

SE ETI wo de sɛ Baebolo suani bie kɔ asafo nhyiamu ne mɔteɛ fwee ne bo, oboli ase kɛ ɔbua nyianmu, ɔka ne gyidie ne ho adwene kile afoforɛ, na akyiri yi osi ne bo kɛ ɔbade ne ho ahyɛ Gyihova Nyankopɔn nsa? Akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho kɛ wo bo tu pɛɛ! Nanso, Gyihova pɛ na ɔfata ayeyie wo sunsum mu nkɔsoɔ a yɛ Baebolo suani ne nyia ne ho. Yɛyi Gyihova ayɛ se yɛhunu kɛ ɔboa obi ma ɔnyini sunsum mu a.—1 Kor. 3:5-9; 3 Yoh. 3, 4.

2 Nyamen pɛ ye kɛ “nnipa nyinaa ngoe ngoa na banyia nɔhalɛ ne ho nimdeɛ kpa.” (Kenga 1 Timoteo 2:3, 4.) Yehowa pɛ kɛ nnipa nyia ye ne ne nyaneyie ho nimdeɛ titili kpalɛ. (Yer. 9:24) Ɛyɛ kile kɛ bɛbanyia pɛpɛɛpɛ nimdeɛ. Bɔbɔ kasa ne mɔɔ bɛhɛlɛ kɛ “pɛpɛɛpɛ nimdeɛ” ne nkile ninyɛne mɔɔ obi suanzua dadeala pɛ, na mmom kɛ mɔɔ Baebolo nimdefoɔ bie hãle no, ɔkile nimdeɛ mɔɔ “kã sonla ne kpalɛ.” Sɛ Baebolo suani bie nyia Yehowa ho nimdeɛ a, saa nimdeɛ ne kã asetena mɔɔ ɔbɔ. Na ɔbue “dahuu ngoa” ho kwan ma ye.—Yohane 17:3.

3 Yehowa ama yɛ adom a yɛfa so boa afoforo ma wonya hu no. Wɔ asɛm yi mu no, yɛbɛkasa afa nsɛmmisa a edidi so yi ho: Adɛn nti na ɛho hia sɛ Baebolo asuafo hu Yehowa yiye? Yɛbɛyɛ dɛn atumi akyerɛkyerɛ wɔn sɛnea Yehowa te? Na yɛbɛyɛ dɛn aboa wɔn ma wɔde wɔn

4 Yesu hãã nga mbra kpole kpa ne lɛ kɛ yɛdɔ Yehowa yɛ ahonle muala, yɛ kra muala, yɛ adwene muala, nienyen yɛ ahomeka muala nu. (Kenga Mark 12:30.) Asuafo ne kɛyɛ na wahole Yehowa nzu meenzene kɛ ɔdɔ ye. Yɛkulo kɛ yɛ Baebolo suani ne hyira ye ngoane so—nnyɛ mma yɛ, nnyɛ mma nkyekyere bie, nnyɛ mma ahyehyɛdeɛ bie—na mmom mma Yehowa. Ti, yɛ adwene titire wɔ mberɛ a yɛne obi suaa Baebolo ne lɛ kɛ yɛboa yɛ suani ne maa ɔhu Yehowa nienyen ɔdɔ ye.

5 Ntebielɛ bie wo ɔlɛ n’asotie ntam. Sɛ wo Baabolo suani ne ba bɔnɔ nwu Yehowa na ɔdɔ ye a, ɔbɛyɛ asoatie ama ye. Yesu hange: “Na ama ewiase anwu kɛ medɔ me Papa ne ti, meyɛ deɛ me Papa ne ɛha me kɛ menyɛ ne pɛpɛɛpɛ.” (Yohanɛs 14:31) Bieko, ɔlɛ bɔ wo ma Yehowa—bɔ ɛtɛla biribiara ne—bɛkanyan Baabolo suani boma wayɛ asoatie ama ye na wazɔ ye nu nnamuu.—1 Yohanɛs 5:3.

6 Baebolo asuafoɔ mɔɔ bɛbanya Yehowa ho ntease kpa la bɛatumi ayɛ nsisie kpalɛ. Yɛfa ndianwo bie: Ewiemuhyɛnkafoɔ bie nsua ewiemuhyɛn ka sɛ ɔsukoa ewiemuhyɛnka mmra ngondolɛ anzɛɛ ɔyɛ sɔhwɛ ala. Ɔsɛ kɛ ɔfa dɔnhwele dodo bie tena ewiemuhyɛn ne anu. Mmerɛ rekɔ ne, ɔbahu kɛzi ewiemuhyɛn ne te wɔ tebea ngakyile nu na kɛzi ye nsisie bɔbɔ ka ewiemuhyɛn ne bɔbɔ. Saa ala nso a, Baebolo suani bie benya Yehowa ho ntease kpa nnyɛ ye adesua ngondolɛ nna mmom ɔbahu nso kɛzi ninyɛne kɔ ma ye mmerɛ mɔɔ ɔfa nsisie mɔɔ ne Ngɛlɛlera nnyinasosɛm kɔ la. Mmerɛ mɔɔ ɔrenya Yehowa ho ntease kpa na ɔhu kɛzi ye sɔlɔ Selɛ boa ye ne, ɔbanya ahoɔden ayɛ nsisie kpalɛ wɔ tebea biala nu.

7 Nimdeɛ mɔɔ na Joseph lɛ ye wɔ Jehovah ho no boaa ye mberɛ mɔɔ Potiphar ye yee bɔɔ mbɔden kɛ ɔbato ye no. Firi ye mmeranteberɛ mu no, akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho kɛ Joseph tii Adam ne Eve ne Abraham ne Sarah ho asɛm. (Gen. 2:24; 20:3, 6) Joseph hyehyɛɛ asɛm yia mɔ mu hunii sɛnea Jehovah bu agyadeɛ ne nna akyɛdeɛ no. Joseph hunii Jehovah kpaara mɔɔ ɔpoo Potiphar ye yee no na ɔsee ye kɛ: “Mɛyɛ dɛn na mayɛ amumuyɛ kɛseɛ yi na mayɛ bɔne atia Nyankopɔn?” (Gen. 39:7-9) Joseph nim deɛ ɛyɛ kpa ne deɛ ɛyɛ bɔne wɔ Jehovah ani so. Ɔtee deɛ ɛbɛyɛ ye Nyankopɔn yaw no ase. Ɛhye nti, Joseph yɛɛ adwenkpa. Sɛ yɛboa yɛn Baebolo asuafoɔ mɔɔ bɛhu Jehovah kpaara na bɛsua sɛnea ɔte no a, bɛnso bɛyɛ adwenkpa mɔɔ sɔ ye ani.

Wɔ 2024 mu no, yɛhunzile nsakyerayɛ kɛseɛ kpaa wɔ yɛ asetena mu. Wɔ bere mɔ yɛzonlele wɔ Bethel bɔborɛ mfeɛ 30 akyi no, bɛmaanle yɛhɔle asɛnkyerɛ edwuma ne mu. Seesei yɛresom kɛ akwampaefo titiriw wɔ supɔ Okinawa so na yɛnya kwan di adanse kyerɛ mmusua mɔ bɛte asraafo beedi mɔ bɛbɛn ɛkɛ ne no. Nteteɛ kpa mɔ yɛnyanle ye wɔ Bethel no boale yɛ kpaa maanle kwan mɔ yɛfale so kɔle asɛnkyerɛ edwuma ne mu no yɛle mmerɛw kpaa. Yɛbɔ mmɔden kɛ yɛbɛkɔ do anya nhyehyɛe kpa na yɛadeberɛ yɛ berɛ yiye denam yɛ adwene mɔ yɛde kɔ yɛ asɔnkyerɛ edwuma ne so na yɛyɛ adwuma denneennen no so. Yeayamuyie mu no, Yehowa hyirale yɛ so maanle yɛnyanle Baebolo asuafo mɔ afei bɛyɛ kɛ yɛ mmusuafo. Yɛrehu ye pefee kɛ ohia kpaa kɛ yɛdɔ nkorɔfo. Miwako na me da Yehowa ase da biara wɔ saa dwumadi fɛfɛɛfɛ yi ti.

SI YE BO BO BƆ JEHOVA KƆHƆ YE NYUNLO BƆ ƆKYƐN KƐ

Me abusua, ɛkɛ nna bɛntia me bio koraa, bɛtɛngɛ bɛnba bɛmboka me ho wɔ asɔredzi kpatakpata ne nu. Mmusua muala bɔ bɛwɔ ewiase ne muala nu bɔ baye me dɔ na babua me wɔ mfe pii nu kɛ bɛ tiara bɛ abusua ne, bɛsɔ dodo bɔ menkula menkakyɛ bɛ muala.

Siesie nzekyee ne ndɛndɛ!

Onua bianzoma anzɛɛ onua baa be ɛyɛ wo bɔne a ɔte sɛ yeama bepua awula bɛ afianze ɔ? Sɛ ɛkulo kɛ ɛsiezie na bɛhɔ so ayɛ anyankɔfo a, ɔwɔ kɛ ɛyɛ ninyɛne ndɛndɛ na ɛfa adwenpa nso so yɛ.*

Kɛ bɛyɛ nhwɛsoɔ no, sɛ ebie bɔ a, ɔsɛ kɛ bɛyɛ ho adwuma ama akyire yi yeatumi ayɛ adwuma kpa a ɛnnyɛ ya. Nanso sɛ bɛanbua anyɛ ebie a abɔ no ho adwuma ntɛm a, ɛya no bɛtɔ so adan. Deɛ ɛtɛ kɛ ɛbɛyɛ bɔne kɛseɛ koraa no, ebie no bɛtumi akyea akɔ fam afebɔɔ na ɛrentumi nyɛ adwuma sɛdeɛ ɛsɛ.

Kɛ mɔ kɔwu kɛ abubu la, sɛ ayo kɛ wɔne Kilisieneni fofolɛ afiangole zoli a, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ ye nzalɛ ndɛndɛ. Sɛ yɛanyɛ ye zɔ a, awoluboe mɔ kɛ wɔ afiangole mɔ kɛ azoli la bama ye kɛkɔ ye zɔ. Ɔbama zungolɛ kɛyi wɔ ahonle nu, na ebiasun wɔ afiangole ne. Yesu hanle kɛ: “Yɛ ndɛndɛ siesie asɛm.” (Mat. 5:25) Ɔti kɔ wɔ afiangole ne anwo na fali ahonledole ka asɛm ne anwo edwei fɔɔn. Duzu ati a kɛyɛ se kɛ ɛbayɛ ye zɔ? Suzue ndinyazo nwiɔ anwo.

Dumua, ebie a ɛbazo wo adwen dɛ mmasusu asɛm ne ho mma bɔbɔ kɛ nni ano, ebie a ɔluakɛ asɛm a ɛte kɛnde nsua ne su ne kã yɛ wo denden.# Dwene Yodiya nɛ Sentiki ho hwɛ, Kilisieni anyemimbiayia mma mɔ noko kɛ bɔbɔ annyuannu andi bɛ asiane ano a. Ɔda kɛ bɛ asɛm ne dii fɛm bere tentele, ɔluakɛ somafo Pɔɔlo mɔ ɔwɔ bomaa wɔ Rom man ne mu mpo tele ye ho asɛm. Ɔhyɛɛ bɛ nkuran dɛ bɛyɛ biribi biala mɔ ɛhia kɛ bɛyɛ na bɛ ayɔnkofa ne abazɔ ebie. (Fil. 4:2) Ahobrose, bɛ ne bɔhɔ adawubɔ kpa, nɛ patorɔ kpatie bamaa bɛ ayɔnkofa ne asan ayɛ kpa ebie.

Deɛ ɔtɔ so nnyu, ebia ɛkɛhye kɛ mɔɔ ɔka no bɛɛ gyi yɛ mɔɔ ɔyɛe no to nu. Nso ɛpɛ kɛ ɛnya wɔ nyɔnkɔ ne bieko, na nnyɛ kɛ ɛkɛhye mɔɔ ɔbɔɔ fɔlɛ. Dɔkɔta mɔɔ ɔresa bowunle mɔɔ abu la di bowunle ne sa kɛ ɔkɛyɛ kpalɛ, na nnyɛ kɛ ɔkɛpɛhye bɔbɔ mɔɔ ma bowunle ne bui la anzunu. Saa ɛkasa ɛkile wɔ nyɔnkɔ ne a, mmadi mɔɔ ɔbɔɔ fɔlɛ wɔ bɔbɔ mɔɔ asi la anzunu. Mmom, kaka kɛzi wɔ nyɔnkoafa ne sonle bolɛ maa wɔ la, na di mɔɔ ɔsɛ kɛ ɛyɛ na amaa asanla ayɛ kpalɛ la anzunu.

Kɛkire nti na ɛngɔ kɛ ne wo nua banyin anzɛɛ wo nua bla ninkɔ kɛmɔ ndɛ nnɛ, ama woasiesie bɔbɔ anzɛɛ woanwun wo bɔbɔ kpalɛ ne bio? “Mma mma wia ngya kɛ akyi nwo bo.” (Eph. 4:26) Sɛ wutu anamon ntsɛntsɛ kɛ wusiesie ndɛ ne a, wobaboka ama yɛabo “asomdwoe bɔ ɔkyekyere yɛn bɔ mu” ne ho ban.—Eph. 4:1-3.

Wɔ yɛ asafo nhyiamu ase no, yɛwɔ adwene kɛseɛ sɛ yɛnam yɛn mmuae so bɛhyɛ yɛn nuanom mmarima ne mmaa nkuran. (Heb. 10:24, 25) Kwan bɛn so na yebetumi asiesie mmuae a ɛbɛhyɛ afoforo den ampa?

• Bɔ Yehowa mpaeɛ. Bize ye kɛ ɔboka wɔ ma bua mmuaeɛ mɔ ɔbama waye ye ayɛ na yeahyɛ afoforɔ nkuran.—Dw. 35:18.

• Ma wo nyiano ɛnka ndɛ tenten. Nyiano tian na mɔ ɔkɔ bɔkɔɔ la taa hyɛ nyamefo awulenden na ɔma menma dɔɔnwo nya kwan ma bɛ nyiano wɔ nhyiamu ne ase.—Mɛb. 10:19.
