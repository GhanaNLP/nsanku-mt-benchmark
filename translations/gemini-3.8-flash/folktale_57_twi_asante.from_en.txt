Yɛnni aseɛ koraa, yɛnni aseɛ koraa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokorɛ)

ABOFRA-KWASI-GYINAMOA

Ɛberɛ bi na abofra bi wɔ hɔ a yɛfrɛ no Kwasi Gyinamoa, na na ɔwɔ nuammariman mmaa baawɔtwe, na wɔn mu biara nso nnyaa kunu, enti wɔtutuu wɔn nan sii so, kaa sɛ wɔrekɔ baabi akɔhwehwɛ mmarima aware. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, “Me ne mo bɛkɔ.” Wɔkaa sɛ, “Yɛne wo rennkɔ.” Wɔn mu panin no kaa sɛ, “Momma ɔne yɛn nkɔ, na sɛ yɛkɔ kwan no so, na nsɔe wɔ yɛn a, ɔbɛtumi atwe ama yɛn.” Wɔtutuu wɔn nan sii so, na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso ne wɔn kɔe. Wɔduruu asubɔnten bi ho, na ne fa sen te sɛ hyire; ne fa sen te sɛ twiton; na ne fa nso sen te sɛ bidie tuntum. Wɔbɔɔ tikwankwa sɛ, “Ɛh, dɛn na yɛbɛyɛ na yɛatumi atwa asuo yi?” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa ne bɔdua, de bɔɔ nsuo no mu, maa nsuo no mu paee mmienu, na wɔfaa mu kɔe. Wɔduruu kuro bi so; na ɛyɛ kuro kɛse pa ara. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, “Kuro yi ayɛ dinn, obiara nni mu anaa?” Ɛnna Anomaa bi kaa sɛ, “Obi wɔ fie hɔ, monkɔ anim.” Wɔkɔɔ anim, na akwakoraa aberewa bi te hɔ a n’atifi da ne nan so, na ɔrehwehwɛ n’atifi nsaa. Ɛberɛ a ɔhunuu Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, ɔmaa ne ti so na ɔsan de too hɔ; ɔkaa sɛ, “Me mmanana, adɛn nti na, ɛberɛ a obiara mma ha yi, moaba ha?” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buaa sɛ, “Me nuammariman mmaa yɛ baawɔtwe, na wonnyaa mmarima a wɔbɛware wɔn, enti wɔkaa sɛ wɔbɛba abɛhwehwɛ mmarima aware.” Aberewa-nana kaa sɛ, “Oo, ɛno deɛ ɛyɛ, efisɛ me nso mewɔ mma baawɔtwe.” Aberewa-nana somaa ne nana sɛ ɔnkɔfrɛ ne mma no mmra. Ɛberɛ a wɔbae no, oyi kaa sɛ, “Me kunu nie,” oyi nso kaa sɛ, “Me kunu nie.” Ɛberɛ a wɔn nyinaa wiee awaregudie no, ɛnna aberewa no kɔfaa suman bi a yɛfrɛ no dokum, de guu ne mma no kɔn mu. Afei obiara ne ne yere kɔdae. Na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔe sɛ ɔne aberewa no rekɔda. Ɛberɛ a anadwo duruu no, aberewa no maa ne se yɛɛ kɔɔ te sɛ gya, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a ɔde bɛkɔ akɔkum mmaa no. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, “E! e!” e!” Abrewa no see sɛ, “Ɛdeɛn na ɛkɔ so?” Akwadaa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buaa sɛ, “Sɛ mewɔ me kurom, na sɛ mereka masrɛ so nanso mantumi anna a, ɛnneɛ ɛna twitwa bayerɛ sin gu gya mu, na ɔfa kora a ɛmu abu kɔsa nsuo ba ma menom.” Abrewa-nananom sɔre kɔɔ asubɔnten no ho, na Akwadaa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kɔe kɔworɔɔ suman a wɔfrɛ no _dokum_ no firii abrewa no mma kɔn mu, na ɔde kɔhyehyɛɛ ne nua mmaa kɔn mu. Abrewa no firi nsuo no ho bae; ɔsee sɛ, “Mebɔɔ mmɔden sɛ mɛsa nsuo (wɔ dadesɛn no mu), nanso mantumi ansa.” Akwadaa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buaa sɛ, “Kɔfa dɔteɛ, na tew nhahan gu mu (dadesɛn no mu), na fa sa.” Abrewa no kɔyɛɛ saa, na ɔde saa nsuo no, na ɛyɛe. Ɔde bae, na ɔyii bayerɛ no firii gya no so, de maa Akwadaa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa ma ɔdiiɛ, na ɔno nso nomee nsuo no bi. Ɛnna ɔtoo ne ho too fam dae. Abrewa-nananom sɔre gyinae; ɔsee sɛ, “Ɛyɛ me.” Ɔhyerɛn ne se, (kɔsii sɛ ɛyɛɛ) kɔkɔɔ, na ɔde kɔɔ dan no mu; ɔde ne nsa sosɔɔ mmofra no kɔn ho, na deɛ _dokum_ suman no nni ne kɔn mu no, ɔde ne se hyee no pasaa. Ɔkwan yei so na ɔfaa so kum mmofra nwɔtwe no. Na ɔnnim sɛ ɔno ara ne mma na wakum wɔn no. Berɛ a ade kyei, anɔpatutuutu Abrewa-nananom tuu kwan sɛ ɔrekɔ afuom. Akwadaa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔka kyerɛɛ ne nua mmaa no ma wɔdwanee kɔe. Abrewa-nananom firi afuom bae. Akwadaa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa see sɛ, “Abrewa-nananom e! Abrewa-nananom e! sɛ woyɛ kwasea a, anka wobɛkum wo mma ara anaa? Me deɛ, me ne me nua mmaa rekɔ.” Abrewa-nananom see sɛ, “Ɛnyɛ hwee, deɛ ɔnim (sɛdeɛ ɔbɛyɛ wo) no wɔ iderɛ.” Ɔkɔe, na Abrewa-nananom nso danee ne ho yɛɛ abaayewa fɛfɛɛfɛ bi, na ɔkuraa torowa, kɔɔ Akwadaa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kuro mu. Ɔsee sɛ, “Nipa a ɔbɛtumi afa bɛmma apempem saa torowa yi, mɛware no.” Nnipa a wɔwɔ kuro no mu nyinaa (bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ) wɔbɛpempem, nanso wɔantumi. Akwadaa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa pempeem. Ɔsee sɛ, "Maware wo." Akwadaa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa see sɛ, "Me deɛ, merenware wo." Enti ne wɔfa waree no. Ɔkɔe sɛ ɔne no rekɔda, nanso ɔtutu ne fam ani mmienu no, na ɔde kɔe. Ɛnna abaayewa no kaa sɛ, "Abafra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa a wowɔ hɔ no! Matutu wo wɔfa aniwa mmienu no na mede rekɔ." Abafra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso buaa sɛ, "Fa kɔ, na nea onim (nea ɔbɛyɛ wo) no wɔ hɔnom." Adekyee no, berɛ a ade fee no, wɔkaa sɛ, "Abafra, kɔ kɔgye wo wɔfa aniwa no bra." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Merenkɔ." Wɔsrɛɛ no kɔkɔɔkɔ, na (awieeɛ koraa no) ɔtuu kwan. Na Abafra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nim sɛ Abrewa-nananom banana baa no nyinsɛn, enti ɔno nso kɔfaa kora de hyɛɛ ne yafunu so, na ɔde ne ntoma bɔɔ so kyekyeree ho, na ɔtuu kwan kɔɔ Abrewa-nananom kuro mu. Berɛ a ɔduruu kuro no ano no, ɔsuui. Abrewa-nananom bisaa sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na asie?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me ne me kunu na ɛreba, na berɛ a ɔduruu bepɔ no atifi no, ɔhwee ase na ɛno maa n'aniwa mmienu no nyinaa firi kɔtɔɔ fam." Abrewa-nananom kaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ hwee, ɔbɔfoɔ kɔbɔbrɛfoɔ, Abafra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa wɔfa benkum aniwa nie, na n'nifa aniwa nso nie. Sɛ wokɔ (baabi a wo kunu anifuraefoɔ no gyina no a) keka sɛ, 'M'aniwa huruhuru, m'aniwa te.'" Berɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ a Abafra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nsa kaa no pɛ, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo a wowɔ hɔ no, Abrewa-nananom, me wɔfa aniwa a motutui no nie, na mede rekɔ." Abrewa-nananom kaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ hwee, nea onim (nea ɔbɛyɛ wo) no wɔ hɔnom." Abafra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa baa fie, na ɔfaa aniwa no de hyehyɛɛ ne wɔfa anitɔkɔ mu bio. Ɔkaa sɛ, "M'aniwa huruhuru, m'aniwa te," na ne wɔfa aniwa buee fefee. Adekyee no, berɛ a ade fee no, mmofra a wɔwɔ kuro no mu nyinaa kɔdwaree asubɔnten no mu. Abafra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Merenware." Wɔkaa sɛ, "Ebia wowɔ duatĩa?" Berɛ a wɔkaa saa no, n'abofra bo fui, na ɔkɔdwaree. Berɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ a ɔhuruu guu nsuo no mu pɛ no, Abrewa-nananom sɔɔ ne mu, twom! Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abafra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, manya wo," na ɔde no kɔɔ ne kuro mu. Ɛnna Abrewa-nananom kɔfaa kɛntɛn de kataa ne so, na ɔfaa abɛ de siesiee gya no so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Merekɔ gyeene kuo no nkyɛn, merekɔ hyehyewa kuo no nkyɛn, na afei maba abɛkum Abafra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa." Ɔtuu kwan kɔe, na Abafra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa boaboa mmoaano ano na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ weei, pupru! pupru! Abrewa-nananom ba kae sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na woreweɛ?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Atadwe." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma me bi ma menwe." Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Yi me so." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wobɛdwane." Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Dabi, merendwane." Ɔyii ne so, na ɔpueeɛ; na aberewa no banana no rewɔ abɛ. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa abɛwɔma no, bɔɔ Abrewa-nananom banana baabaa no, na ɔkuu no. Afei ɔworɔɔ Abrewa-nananom banana baabaa no honam, na ɔfiraeɛ. Abrewa-nananom san bae; ɔkaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na wo ho afi sei?" Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Maku Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa; mede no ato gya so." Asɛm yi yɛɛ Abrewa-nananom dɛ, na ɔsaeɛ na ɔmaa ne nne so (too dwom): "Ba, woaku Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, Daakye wobɛyɛ nipa."

Ɔnoaa aduane no wieeɛ no, wɔtenaa ho, na wɔdiiɛ. Aberewa-nananom hwerɛɛ nkwan no (guu n'anom nnyegyeeɛ kɛseɛ mu), fweo! fweo! Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Worehwerɛ nkwan a wɔde wo nananom ti na ayɛ (agu w'anom)." Aberewa-nananom kaa sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na woreka no?" Ɔbuae sɛ, "Mekaae sɛ nkyene nni nkwan-frɔeɛ yi mu." Aberewa-nananom kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔfa bi bra." Enti ɔkɔfaa bi bae. Wɔdidi wieeɛ no, Aberewa-nananom kaa sɛ, "Afei a ma kum Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa yi, bra ha, me nananom." Berɛ a ɔbaeɛ no, ɔfaa sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ de siesiee no fɛfɛɛfɛ. Afei, Aberewa-nananom hwɛɛ n'anim kyɛree kakra. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Aane! abofra yi anim kura Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa deɛ no so." Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Nana, adɛn, berɛ a wonim sɛ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa bae bɛkumm me nuanom nwɔtwe no nyinaa no (na woreka saa), na worema me werɛ ahoɔ." Aberewa-nananom kaa sɛ, "Muna w'ano." Afei abofra no gyaa ne nan ho wɔ fie hɔ. Nana no hohoroo so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma menkɔto ngu sumina so." Enti Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa soaa kɔe. Berɛ a ɔduruu sumina no so no, ɔworɔɔ sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ a aberewa no de siesiee no no nyinaa na ɔkyekyereeɛ. Afei ɔkaa sɛ, "Hoo hɔ, Aberewa-nananom, hoo hɔ, Aberewa-nananom, mene no, Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, afei ne mprenu soɔ, mabɛkum wo nananom, na afei nso mafa wo sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ." Aberewa-nananom buae sɛ, "Mekaa sɛ w'anim sɛ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa deɛ no; afei baabiara a wobɛkɔ no, me ne wo na ɛbɛkɔ." Afei wɔn baanu nyinaa tuu mmirika, kiri! kiri! kiri! Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa duruu ne kuro mu, na ɔde sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ no maa n'abusuafoɔ, na ɔkɔɔ kwan no so, kɔhyehyɛɛ nkyɛn, na Aberewa-nananom nso baa hɔ. Ɛno nti na, sɛ worenante kwan so a, wobɛte nnyegyeeɛ bi sɛ, "pɛ." Ɛno ne Aberewa-nananom a ɔretaataa Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
