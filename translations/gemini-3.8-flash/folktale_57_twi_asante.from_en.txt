Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka yi yɛ nokware)

AKWADAATOƆ-KWASI-GYINAMOA

Bere bi na abofra bi wɔ hɔ a wɔfrɛ no Kwasi Gyinamoa, na ɔwɔ nuammariman baawɔtwe, na wɔn mu biara nni kunu, enti wɔtutuu kwan, kaa sɛ wɔrekɔ baabi akɔhwehwɛ akununom. Abaayewa Kwasi Gyinamoa see, "Me ne mo bɛkɔ." Wɔbuae sɛ, "Yɛne wo renwɔ." Wɔn mu panyin see, "Mma yɛne no nkɔ, na sɛ yɛduru kwan no mu, na nsɔe wɔ yɛn a, ɔbɛtumi atwe ama yɛn." Wɔkɔe, na Abaayewa Kwasi Gyinamoa ne wɔn kɔe. Wɔbaa asubɔnten bi ho, na ne fa sen sɛ hyire; ne fa nso sen sɛ ntwoma; na ne fa nso sen sɛ bikyire. Wɔkae sɛ, “Ɛɛ, dɛn na yɛnyɛ na yɛatumi atwa asubɔnten yi?” Akwadaa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa ne bɔddua, de bɔɔ nsuo no mu, maa nsuo no mu paee mmienu, na wɔfaam kɔe. Wɔduruu kuro bi so; na ɛyɛ kuro kɛseɛ pa ara. Akwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, “Kuro no ayɛ dinn, nanso obiara nni mu anaa?” Afei Anomaa bi kaa sɛ, “Obi wɔ fie hɔ, monkɔ.” Wɔkɔɔ anim, na aberewa bi te hɔ a ne ti da ne nan so, na ɔrehwehwɛ ne ti mu hwehwɛ mmoawa. Bere a ɔhunuu Akwadaa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, na ɔmaa ne ti so na ɔde sii fam био; ɔkaa sɛ, "Me nnananom, adɛn nti na, bere a obiara nni ho kwan sɛ ɔba ha no, moaba ha?" Akwadaa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buaa sɛ, "Me nuammarima yɛ baawɔtwe, na wɔnnya akunnom a wɔne wɔn bɛda, enti wɔkaa sɛ wɔbɛba abɛhwehwɛ akunnom a wɔbɛware wɔn." Abrewa-nananom kaa sɛ, "O, ɛnyɛ hwee t, ɛfiri sɛ me nso mewɔ mma baawɔtwe.” Abarwa-nanabea somaa ne banana sɛ ɔnkɔfrɛ ne mma no. Berɛ a wɔbaa no, oyi kaa sɛ, “Me kunu nie,” ɔno nso kaa sɛ, “Me kunu nie.” Berɛ a wɔn nyinaa wiee wɔn ho mpanindie no, ɛnna aberewa no kɔfaa suman bi a wɔfrɛ no _dokum_, de kyekyeree ne mma no kɔn mu. Ɛnna obiara kɔe sɛ ɔne ne yere rekɔda. Na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔɔ aberawa no nkyɛn kɔhomee. Ɛduruu anadwo no, aberawa no maa ne se mu hyerɛnee kɔɔ kɔkɔɔ biribiri, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a ɔde bɛkɔ akɔkuku mmabaawa no. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, “E! e! e!” Abrewa no bisae sɛ, “Ɛdeɛn na ɛrekɔ so?” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, “Sɛ mewɔ m’ankasa me kuro mu, na sɛ mekɔda na nna mma a, ɛnde na me maame twa bayerɛ sin de gu gya mu, na ɔfa kora a ɛhoteɛ kɔsa nsuo brɛ me ma menom.” Abrewa-nanabea sii mu kɔɔ asubɔnten no ho, na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kɔe kɔde suman a wɔfrɛ no _dok um_ firii aberewa no mma no kɔn mu, na ɔfaa wɔn de kɔhyɛɛ ne nuammarima mmaa no kɔn mu. Abaayewa no firi nsuo no mu baeɛ; ɔkaa sɛ, “Mebɔɔ mmɔden sɛ mɛhwie nsuo (agu sɔneeɛ no mu), nanso mantumi anhwie.” Akwasi-Gyinamoa-babaa nso buaa sɛ, “Kɔ kɔfa dɔteɛ bi, na tew nhahan gu mu (sɔneeɛ no mu), na fa bɔ nsuo no.” Abaayewa no kɔyɛɛ saa, na ɔhwiee nsuo guu mu, na ɛyɛe yie. Ɔde baeɛ, na ɔyii bayerɛ no firii gya no so, na ɔde maa Akwadaa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa ma ɔdiiɛ, na ɔfaa nsuo no nso nomeeɛ. Ɛnna ɔtoo ne ho too fam na ɔdaeɛ. Abaawa-aberewa no sɔre kɔɔ soro; ɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛyɛ me.” Ɔmaa ne se hyehyɛe kɔkɔɔ, na ɔde kɔe kɔwuraa dan no mu; ɔde ne nsa sosɔɔ mmofra no kɔn ho, na deɛ na _dokum_ suman no nni ne kɔn mu no, ɔde ne se hyee no pasaa. Ɛkwan wei so na ɔfaa do kum mmofra baawɔtwe no. Na ɔnnim sɛ ɔno ara ne mma na waku wɔn no. Adeɛ kyeeɛ no, anɔpahehedididi no ara na Abrewa-nana tuu kwan sɛ ɔrekɔ afuom. Abarima-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuammarima mmaa na ɔmaa wɔdwaneeɛ. Abrewa-nana firi afuom baeɛ. Abarima-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, “Abrewa-nana e! Abrewa-nana e! sɛ woanyɛ kwasea a, anka wobɛku wo ara wo mma anaa?” Me deɛ, me ne me nuammarima rekɔ.” Apan-aberewa se, “Ɛnyɛ hwee, deɛ ɔnim (ne kwan a ɔbɛfa so adi wo so) no wɔ hedei.” Ɔkɔeɛ, na Apan-aberewa nso danee ne ho baabaa fɛfɛɛfɛ bi, na ɔkuraa torowa, na ɔkɔɔ Agyanka-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kuro mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Nipa a ɔbɛtumi de agyan awɔ saa torowa yi, mɛware no." Nnipa a na wɔwɔ akuraa no ase nyinaa (bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ wɔbɛ)wɔ, nanso wɔantumi. Akwadaa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa wɔe. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Maware wo." Akwadaa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, merenware wo." Enti ne wɔfa waree no. Ɔkɔe sɛ ɔne no rekɔna, nanso ɔtutu ne fam ani mmienu no, na ɔde kɔe. Afei ababaawa no kaa sɛ, "Abaayewa Kwasi Gyinamoa a ɔwɔ hɔ no! Matutu wo wofa ani mmienu no na mede rekɔ." Aboa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso buaa sɛ, "Fa kɔ, na deɛ ɔnim (sɛdeɛ ɔbɛyɛ wo) no wɔ hɔ." Adeɛ kyee a adeɛ see no, wɔkaa sɛ, "Abaayewa/Akwadaa, kɔgye wo wofa ani no na fa brɛ yɛn. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Merenkɔ." Wɔsrɛɛ no, srɛɛ no, na (awieeɛ koraa no) ɔtuu kwan kɔeɛ. Afei, Akwadaa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa hunuu sɛ Abrewa-nananom banana no nyinsɛn, enti ɔno nso kɔfaa kora de hyɛɛ ne yafunu so, na ɔde ne ntoma bɔɔ so, na ɔtuu kwan kɔɔ Abrewa-nananom kuro mu. Ɔduruu kuro no ano pɛ, ɔsuuiɛ. Abrewa-nananom kaa sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na asie?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me ne me kunu na ɛreba, na berɛ a ɔduruu bepɔ no atifi no ɔhwee fam na ɛmaa n'ani mmienu no puei tɔɔ fam." Abrewa-nananom kaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ hwee, ɔbɔfoɔ a waba bio no, Ɔba-Kwasi-Gyinamoa wɔfa no benkum ani nie, na ne nifa ani nso nie. Sɛ wokɔ (baabi a wo kunu a n'ani afira no gyina hɔ a) ɛsɛ sɛ woka sɛ, 'M'ani ahuruhuruw, m'ani ate.'" Ɛnkyɛe koraa na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nsa kaa no pɛ, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo a wowɔ hɔ no, Abrewa-nanabea, eyinom ne me wɔfa ani a wotutui no, na mede rekɔ." Abrewa-nanabea kae sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ hwee, nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi no) wɔ faako hɔ." Abofra-Kwasi Gyinamoa baa fie, na ɔfaa aniwa no de hyehyɛɛ ne wɔfa ani tokuro mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "M'aniwa huruhuruw, m'aniwa da hɔ fann," na ne wɔfa ani buee fann. Ade kyee a ade sae no, mmɔfra no nyinaa kɔwaree wɔ asubɔnten no mu. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Merenguare." Wɔkaa sɛ, "Ebia wowɔ dua a ahyɛ aseɛ rebɔ?" Berɛ a wɔkaa saa no, ne bo hiahiae, na ɔguaree. Ɔhuru guu nsuo no mu pɛ, na Agyenkwa-nanabea sɔɔ ne mu, twom! Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, manya wo," na ɔne no kɔɔ ne kuro mu. Ɛnna Akyerewaa-nananom kɔgyee kɛntɛn de kataa ne so, na ɔkɔfaa abɛ, de guu gya so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Merekɔ gyeenefoɔ nkyɛn, merekɔ hyehyewafoɔ nkyɛn, na afei maba abɛku Akwasi-Gyinamoa-babaa." Ɔtuu kwan kɔeɛ, na Akwasi-Gyinamoa-babaa boaboaa mboba ano na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ weewee mu, pupru! pupru! Abaayewa nana no ba kae sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na woreweɛ no?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Atadwe." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma me bi ma menwe." Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Yi me so." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wobɛkɔ." Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Dabi, merenkɔ." Ɔyii ne so, na ɔpueeɛ; na aberewa no nana no resi abɛ. Abaayewa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa abɛwɔma no, de bɔɔ Abrewa-nananom nana baa no, na ɔkumm no. Afei ɔworɔɔ Abrewa-nananom nana baa no wedeɛ, na ɔhyɛeɛ. Aberewa-nana san bae; ɔkaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na mo nyinaa ho afi sɛɛ?" Ɔbuae sɛ, "Makum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa; mede no ato gya so." Asɛm yi yɛɛ Aberewa-nana dɛ, na ɔsae na ɔmaa ne nne so (too dwom sɛ): "Abaayewa/Abarima, woakum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, Daakye wobɛyɛ obi."

Ɔwiee aduane no noa no, wɔtenaa ho didiiɛ. Ɔbabanin-nananom kɔɔ nkwan no mu (guso n'anomu tee), fweo! fweo! Abaayewa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Worehuruhuru nkwan a wɔde wo nana tirim ayɛ (agu w'anom)." Abrewa-nanabea kaa sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na woreka no?" Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Meka sɛ nkyene nni nkwan-fomflaa yi mu." Abrewa-nanabea kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔfa bi bra." Enti ɔkɔeɛ kɔfaa bi baeɛ. Wowiee adidi no, na Nana-aberewa kaa sɛ, "Afei a makum Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa yi, bra ha, me nanankanso." Ɛberɛ a ɔbaa yɛ no, ɔfaa sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ de siesiee no fɛfɛɛfɛ. Afei, Nana-aberewa hwɛɛ n'anim kyɛɛ kakra. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Aane! abofra yi anim kae me Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa deɛ no." Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Nana, adɛn nti na wunim sɛ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa baeɛ bɛkumm me nua baawɔtwe no nyinaa nso (woka asɛm a ɛte saa), ɛfiri sɛ woma me werɛ how." Nana aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Mumu w'ano." Afei abofra no gyee ne nneɛma wɔ fie hɔ. Nana no sesaa guree. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma menkɔto ntwene wɔ sumina no so." Enti Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa soaa no kɔe. Berɛ a ɔduruu sumina no so no, ɔworɔɔ sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ a ɔbaapanin no de siesiee no no nyinaa guu hɔ na ɔkyekyeree mu. Ɛnna ɔkaa sɛ, "Hee, Akyerebaa Nana, hee, Akyerebaa Nana, me ara ne no, Akwasi-Gyinamoa-ba, afei ne mprɛnu so ni, maba abɛkum wo nana, na afei nso magye wo sikakɔkɔɔ adwinneɛ nyinaa." Akyerebaa Nana buaa sɛ, "Mekaae sɛ w'anim sɛ Akwasi-Gyinamoa-ba deɛ; afei baabiara a wobɔkɔ biara, me ne wo na ɛbɛkɔ." Ɛnna wɔn baanu nyinaa twee mmirika, kiri! kiri! kiri! Abaayewa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa duruu ne kuro mu, na ɔde sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ no maa n’abusuafoɔ, na ɔkɔɔ kwan no so, kɔhuntaa kwan no nkyɛn, na Aberewa-nana nso baa hɔ. Ɛno nti na sɛ worefa kwan so a, wobɛte nnyegyeeɛ bi sɛ, "pɛ." Ɛno ne Aberewa-nana a ɔretu mmirika adi Abaayewa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa akyi.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
