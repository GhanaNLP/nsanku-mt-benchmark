PREFACE

The hardships brought by the Coronavirus Pandemic have affected all nations across the world, including Ghana. The suffering that came upon Ghanaians, which resulted in deaths, ruined living conditions, and affected various jobs, became a challenge for the Government. I can proudly say that we faced the problem with courage and, if I may say so, wisdom. People all over the world have commended us for that step. The government also made arrangements that revived various jobs and improved our living conditions once again.

Furthermore, in order to address the Coronavirus challenges and take advantage of the opportunities that arose, the Government, through the Administration of President Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo, introduced an initiative that allocated 100 billion Ghana cedis to mitigate the hardship and revitalize struggling businesses; this is the Ghana CARES “Obaatanpa” programme. This brought quick relief to our people and is boosting our country.

We appreciate the trust you have had in the Government as we work to overcome the Coronavirus Pandemic. At the same time, our resilience as a nation has been put to the test. The Bible also reveals clear guidance in times like this, as it is revealed in Isaiah Chapter 9, Verse 10, saying, “The bricks have fallen down, but we will rebuild with dressed stone; the sycamore-fig trees have been cut down, but we will replace them with cedars.” At the same time, the Government has also made it a point to fulfill all the promises it made. Because of this, it will continue to implement policies and programs that will ensure all unfinished business is completed, thereby improving the living conditions of the people of Ghana.

One of the main objectives of the 2022 Budget Statement is that, in the next three (3) years, the Government will assist us to become an entrepreneurial nation that will enable us to achieve truly comprehensive development. Through this, many people, especially the youth, will obtain decent jobs that they are passionate about. The entire Government policy will help the youth utilize their entrepreneurial talents so that they can prosper, and equity will work for all people.

In order for young men and young women to have self-confidence and develop an entrepreneurial spirit in the year 2022, the Government will implement the YouStart Program.

PREPARATION OF THE 2022 FINANCIAL STATEMENT BUDGET ESTIMATES

Financial Planning

14. The Government shall make arrangements that will enable the country's various industries to grow and their products to be competitive in both domestic and international markets. For this reason, it is imperative that these arrangements be made:

• In order to protect our gains and expand the businesses, the government will extend the VAT relief on the manufacture of African prints, which was introduced in the 2019 budget, for a further two years;

• If we implement this policy within two and a half years, the reduced import duties will be revised to make it fully complete;

• Withholding tax on small scale miners will be reduced from 3% to 1.5%;

• From the month of January in the year 2022, Government through the Revenue Authority will assist the Government agencies to implement the Property Tax System so that property tax payment and its accounting will be complete;

• From the first day of January in the year 2022, the Government will make changes to the taxes collected when certain Government agencies operate. These changes will be made based on how the cost of goods is increasing;

• Once the Financial Comparison arrangements are accepted, no more taxes will be paid on Government Roads and Bridges annually in this Country.

• They will begin collecting taxes on money sent via mobile phones and bank channels. This will broaden the tax net in this country and ensure that all workers contribute their share. Some of the things that will be taxed include mobile money transfers, bank transfers, merchant payments, and both inward and outward remittances.

GLOSSARY

One District, One Factory: Various factories and manufacturing places established in each district across the country to advance industrialization. This will bring many jobs to Ghanaians (or citizens) everywhere in the country.

Accountability: This means that the Government has an obligation and an explanation to the citizens regarding the various works it has done and how they were carried out. It also includes accountability for the various funds and resources in the hands of the Government.

Public finance: This refers to the process through which the country's revenue is distributed among various Ministries, Departments, and Agencies.

Problem solving: This is the way through which problems are reduced.

Financial Budget: This is a plan that shows how money was obtained or used. For example, a married family can make its financial budget showing the amount of money it has and how it must take care of things that are most necessary for it (even house rent, lights and water, and food). If there is a need to look for more money to add to it, they see whether it is necessary to work more or if they should take a loan.

Excess Expenditure: This happens when the Government spends money beyond the money that is in the Budget approved by Parliament.

Expenditure on Key Things: This is the money that the Government uses to pay for the execution of certain important projects. Some of these include roads, school buildings, hospitals, bridges, transport, water supply, acquisition of heavy machinery, and many others.

Compensation of Employees: This refers to the money the government pays its employees as monthly salaries, their allowances, their retirement leave grants, and their social security contributions.

Development Partners: This stands for various countries and organizations that join forces to help us enable the country to achieve its development goals. Some of these partners are the United Nations, the International Monetary Fund (or World Bank)

Free Senior High School: The Free Senior High School Policy is a policy introduced by the Government through the Nana Akufo-Addo administration in September, 2017, which enables students who complete junior high school and pass to attend senior high school for free without paying any school fees.

Grant: This is financial assistance that the Government receives from other countries which it does not have to repay.

Inflation: This is the rate at which the prices of goods and services fall or rise over a certain period.
