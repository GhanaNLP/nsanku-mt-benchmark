INTRODUCTION

The difficulties brought by the Coronavirus Pandemic have affected countries all over the world, including Ghana. The suffering that came upon Ghanaians, which resulted in deaths, ruined their livelihoods, and affected businesses, was a challenge for the Government. I can proudly say that we stood up to the challenge with courage and, excuse me to say, wisdom. People all over the world have commended us for that step taken. The Government also put measures in place that revived businesses and improved our living conditions again.

Also, in order to address the coronavirus challenge and make use of the opportunity that came with it, the Government, through President Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo's government, made arrangements that allocated 100 million Ghana cedis to reduce the hardship and use part of it to revive businesses that had collapsed; this is the Ghana CARES “Obaatan Pa” program. This brought quick relief to our citizens and is building up our nation.

We appreciate the trust you have had in the Government as we work to overcome the Coronavirus Pandemic. At this very time, our resilience to stand as a nation has been tested. The Bible also provides guidance in such times, as revealed in Isaiah Chapter 9, Verse 10, which says, “The bricks have fallen down, but we will rebuild our houses with dressed stones. The sycamore-fig trees have been cut down, but we will put cedars in their place.” At the same time, the Government has also committed to fulfilling all the promises it made. Because of this, it will continue to implement policies and programs that will ensure all unfinished tasks are completed and improve the living conditions of the Ghanaian people.

One of the main objectives of the 2022 Budget Statement is that, within the next three (3) years, the Government will assist in creating a nation where entrepreneurship thrives to enable us to achieve comprehensive and sound development. Through this, many people, especially young men and young women, will be provided with decent jobs of their choice. All government policies will help the youth utilize their entrepreneurial skills to prosper, ensuring that fairness works for all people.

To enable young men and women to have self-confidence and develop an entrepreneurial spirit in the year 2022, the Government will implement the YouStart Programme.

PREPARATION OF THE 2022 FINANCIAL ESTIMATES BUDGET

Financial Planning

14. The Government shall make arrangements to promote industries in this country and ensure that their products are competitive in both domestic and international markets. For this reason, it is extremely important that the following arrangements are made:

• In order to protect the gains made and expand operations, the government will extend the VAT relief on the manufacture of African prints, which was introduced in 2019, for another two years;

• If we implement this plan within two and a half years, the reduced property rate will be reviewed to make it fully effective;

• The tax paid to the Government by small-scale mining operations will be reduced from 3 percent to 1.5 percent;

• From January 2022, the Government will, through the Ghana Revenue Authority, support the Metropolitan, Municipal, and District Assemblies (MMDAs) to implement a Common Property Rate Platform to ensure the efficient collection of Property Rates and their associated accounting;

• From January 1st, 2022, the Government will make changes to the taxes charged on the operations of certain Government agencies. These changes will be made by taking into consideration the rising cost of goods;

• If the Economic Partnership Agreement is accepted, road and bridge taxes will no longer be paid in this country.

• The collection of taxes on money sent via Mobile Phones and Banks will commence. This will increase the number of taxpayers in this Country and ensure all workers pay their share. Some of the things that will be taxed include Mobile Phone money transfers, Bank money transfers, Merchant payments, as well as International and Inward remittances.

GLOSSARY

One District, One Factory: Various factories and manufacturing centers established in every single district across the country to advance the nation's industrialization. This will bring numerous jobs to Ghanaians (or citizens) everywhere in the country.

Accountability: This means that the Government has a responsibility and an explanation to the citizens regarding the various works it has undertaken and how they were carried out. It also includes an accounting of the various monies and properties in the Government's hands.

Budget: This refers to the method by which the country's funds are distributed to various Ministries, Departments, and Agencies.

Troubleshooting: This is a way by which problems are reduced.

Financial Budget: This is a plan that shows how money was obtained or used. For example, a married family can make its financial budget showing the amount of money it has and how it should cater to its most important needs (even rent, light and water, and food). If there is a need to find additional money to add to it, they look into whether it is necessary to work more or if they should take a loan.

Over-expenditure: This occurs when the Government spends money in excess of the amount approved in the Budget Estimates by the Legislature.

Expenditure on Major Projects: This is the money that the Government uses to pay for the execution of certain major projects. Some of these include motor roads, school buildings, hospitals, bridges, transport, water supply, provision of large machinery, and many others.

Compensation of Employees: This refers to the money the government uses to pay its employees monthly salaries, their allowances, their severance or leave allowances, and social security contributions.

Development Partners: This stands for various countries and organizations that join forces to help us so that this country achieves its development goals. Some of these partners include the European Union, the World Bank

Year-End Inflation: The monthly inflation in Bologna which went down in the year under review.

Budget Deficit: This occurs when the Government spends money beyond the amount of money in the Financial Estimates approved by Parliament.

Free Senior High School: The Free Senior High School Policy is a policy introduced by the Government under President Nana Akufo-Addo's administration in September 2017, which enables students who complete junior high school and pass to attend senior high school for free without paying any school fees.

Grant: This is financial assistance that the Government receives from other countries which does not have to be repaid.

Government Statutory Funds: This is money that the law mandates the Government to provide to various agencies, such as the District Assemblies Common Fund, the Road Fund, the Education Trust Fund, the Minerals Development Fund, and the National Health Insurance Fund.

Gross Domestic Product (GDP): This refers to the total number of goods and services produced within the country and the money or revenue that the Government obtains within a specific period.

Gross Domestic Product: This refers to the total amount of money in a country that enables it to purchase domestic goods and also use part of it to pay for imports within a specific period; this amount also helps determine the strength of the country's currency relative to that of another country.

Inflation: This is the rate at which the prices of goods and services rise or go up over a period of time.
