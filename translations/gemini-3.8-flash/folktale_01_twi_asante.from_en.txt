NEA ƐYƆƆ A OBONTO YA, APATAPO NO, TAA "NANTE" BASABASA WƆ NSUO MU DAAN

Wɔse na Abrewa-nana wɔ hɔ, na ne nnoɔma yerae. Ɔkaa sɛ ɛyɛ "nsuom mmoa" na wɔfaa (nnoɔma no). Kɔtɔ no te hɔ, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Aane, moama yɛn ani akɔ fam (animguaseɛ mu), na deɛ yɛpɛ sɛ yɛyɛ de kyerɛ mo deɛ ɛwɔ yɛn tiri mu ne (sɛ): kɔkɔfa yaawa kɔkɔɔ bi mmra na yɛn nyinaa mfa (yɛn) nisuo nhyɛ mu mma no mmorosoɔ, na obiara a ɔntumi nsu mma no nhyɛ mu ma no, ɔno ne deɛ wagye (nnoɔma no)."

Ɔde yaawa no baeɛ. Ɔkɔtɔ suiɛ ma ɛyɛɛ ma; wɔhwie guye; Mmobonse baeɛ bɛsuiɛ; Ɔkawa baeɛ bɛsuiɛ; Oyoyo baeɛ bɛsuiɛ; Ɔtidi baeɛ bɛsuiɛ; Ɔbɔnko baeɛ bɛsuiɛ; Akorese baeɛ bɛsuiɛ; Ɔpitiri baeɛ bɛsuiɛ; Ɔkobo baeɛ bɛsuiɛ; nsuom mmoa nyinaa baeɛ bɛsuiɛ. Awiei koraa no Obonto Ya a ɔyɛ Asensensunoborobofoɔ no bae sɛ ɔrebɛsu; nanso wannya nsuo amfiri n'aniwa mu; ɛno nti wɔhunuu sɛ Obonto Ya na wagye (nneɛma no).

Nanso Abrewa-nananom kaa sɛ, "Merenyɛ wo hwee." Ɛnna abɔdeɛ no nyinaa kaa sɛ, "Yɛn deɛ, yɛbɛborɔ no, efisɛ wagu yɛn anim ase." Enti wɔboroo no.

Berɛ a wɔboroo no wieeɛ no, Awo-Nana-Kwakwa kaa sɛ, "Magya wo (afebɔɔ) sɛ kyinkyin kwa a wunnim faako a worekɔ; obiara rentumi mpɛ sɛ ɔne wo bɛyɛ biribiara da."

Eyi nti na, sɛ mmaa kɔ bɔ asu (pɛ mpataa) a, wɔhunu sɛ OBONTO YA retutu ne nan (seesei) ha ne ha no.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
