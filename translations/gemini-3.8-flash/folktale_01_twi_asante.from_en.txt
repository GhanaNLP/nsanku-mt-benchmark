SƐNEA ƐYƐE A OBONTO YA, APATAPO NO, TAA "NANTE" GU NSO MU KWA

Wɔse Abrewa-nananom bi na ɔwɔ hɔ, na ne nnoɔma yerae. Ɔkaa sɛ "nsuom mmoa" no na wɔafefa (nnoɔma no). Kɔtɔ te hɔ, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Aane, woama yɛn ani awu, na nea yɛpɛ sɛ yɛyɛ de kyerɛ wo nea ɛwɔ yɛn tiri mu ne (eyi): kɔkɔfa yaawa bi bbra na yɛn nyinaa mfa (yɛn) nisuo nhyɛ mu ma, na deɛ ɔntumi nsu mfa nhyɛ mu mma no, ɔno na wafa (nnoɔma no)."

Ɔde ayowa no bae. Okoto suiɛ ma ɛyɛɛ ma; wɔhwie gui; Mmobonse bae bɛsuiɛ; Okawa bae bɛsuiɛ; Oyoyo bae bɛsuiɛ; Otidi bae bɛsuiɛ; Obonko bae bɛsuiɛ; Akorese bae bɛsuiɛ; Opitiri bae bɛsuiɛ; Okobo bae bɛsuiɛ; nsuom mmoa nyinaa bae bɛsuiɛ. Deɛ ɔtwaa toɔ koraa no Obonto Ya bae bɛsuiɛ; wannya nsuo amfiri n'ani so; enti wɔhunuu sɛ Obonto Ya na wagye (nneɛma no).

Nanso Abrewa-nanabea kaa sɛ, "Merrenyɛ wo hwee." Ɛnna abɔdeɛ no nyinaa kaa sɛ, "Yɛn deɛ, yɛbɛbore no, efisɛ wagu yɛn anim ase." Enti wɔboroo no.

Berɛ a wɔboroo no wieeɛ no, Aso-Nana-aberewa kaa sɛ, "Megyaw wo (afebɔɔ) sɛ kyinkyin kwa a wunni baabiara a worekɔ; obiara rentaa mpɛ sɛ ɔne wo bɛnya biribiara yɛ da."

Eyi nti na, sɛ mmaa kɔ "asubɔ" (kɔpɛ mpataa) a, wɔhunu OBONTO YA sɛ ɔretutu akwan (seesei) ha ne ha no.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
