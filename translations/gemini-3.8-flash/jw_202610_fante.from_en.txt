MFIE OHANA A ABƆSƐN

“BER no rokɔ famu ber a Nyankopɔn bɛyɛ dzin ama no ho. Nyankopɔn benya obi a ɔbɛyɛ n’adansefo wɔ asaase do,” dɛm na January 1, 1926, The Watch Tower no kaa.

Ber a afe 1926 kɔr n’enyim no, Jehovah nam asafo no tsir, Christ do ma ɔbɛyɛɛ pefee kɛse dɛ asɛnka edwuma no na ɔwɔ dɛ ɔyɛ biribi tsitsir a Christianyi dze n’adwen si do wɔ n’abrabɔ mu. Dɛm ntsi, “akowaa nokwafo na onyansafo no” ma Onyankopɔn n’asomfo nhoma afofor, yɛɛ nsesa wɔ nhyiamu ahorow no mu, na ɔma wonyaa ntease a emu da hɔ fa ndwuma a ɔsɛ dɛ Christianyi yɛ ho. (Matt. 24:45) Christ hwɛɛ dɛ wobenya adan na mfirdwuma a wohia dze ayɛ asɛnka edwuma no.

NWOMA FOFOR NA NSAKRAHO A WƆAYƐ WƆ NHYIAMU AHOROW HO

Wɔ April mu no, The Golden Age (a sesei wɔfrɛ no Awake!) bɔɔ nhoma fofor bi a wɔato dzin Deliverance ho dawur. Nna dɛm nhoma yi bɛhyɛ Studies in the Scriptures ananmu dɛ nhoma titir a wɔdze yɛ asɛnka edwuma no. Mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a wobotum anya nhoma a dɔm no behia no, anuanom a wɔwɔ Bethel tɔɔ afir fofor a wotumi dze tintim nhoma 22 wɔ sima biara mu. Bible Esuafo no dze nhoma fofor no maa wɔ kwan biara a wobenya mu.

Mfe pii no, nna Bible Esuafo no kɔ do suso Studies in the Scriptures no mbuukuu no ho wɔ hɔn nhyiamu ase. Naaso ofi May mu no, wofii ase susoo adzesua a ɔwɔ The Watch Tower mu no ho. Ebɛn sunsum mu nsunsuando na onyae?

“Bunker Hill, Illinois asafo ketsewa no ma wo akye,” dɛm na asafo kor kyerɛwee wɔ krataa a wɔkɛmɛaa Onua Rutherford mu. “Kwesida biara yɛsua [nsɛm] a Fekuw no ahyehyɛ ama dɛm nna no. Yɛda ase kɛse dɛ hɛn nyina botum ehu kan fofor no ber a obodu.”

“Kanea fofor no,” anaa ntease a w’atsew no, boaa Bible Asuafo no ma wode asɛmpakã edwuma no sii hɔn enyi do. Dɛ nhwɛdo no, asɛm a wɔato dzin “Suban anaa Ahyɛmu—Hena Wɔ Mu?” kyerɛkyerɛɛ mu dɛ “suban nkɔdo,” a nna wosi do dua pɔtsee dɛ obi bɛda suban a obu na ayamye wom edzi kɛkɛ no, maa binom buu edwuma kankan a nokwar Christianfo yɛ no animtsia. Asɛm no kãa dɛ: “Nyia ɔdɔ Ewuradze no seseiara ɔsɛ dɛ . . . osetsie n’ahyɛdze ahorow no. . . . Jehovah kã dɛ: ‘Hom nye m’adansefo dɛ me nye Nyankopɔn.’ . . . Christiannyi no seseiara dze no ho bɛpɛ dɛ odzi dase dɛ Nyankopɔn no dasefo krɔnkrɔn.”

Adesua fofor bi a wɔato dzin, “Afɔrbɔ nye Som,” sii do dua dɛ nɔkwar Christianfo da ɔdɔ a wɔwɔ ma Jehovah edzi dɛ wɔbɔsom no nɔkwardzi mu. Adesua no hyɛɛ Bible Esuafo no nyinara nkuran dɛ wɔmfa nsi, akomamudzi, na nsi nyinara nsom Awurade na “wɔnyɛ adansefo mma Awurade na wɔnka no nkrasɛm kyerɛ asaase do amanaman nye nkorɔfo nyinara.”—Isa. 43:10, 12; Matt. 24:14.

EHYIADZI NO TSI ASI DƐ ASƐMPA KƆKĀ EDWUMA NO HO HIA

Wɔ May n'ewiei no, Bible Esuafo pii hyiae wɔ ehyiadzi bi ase wɔ London, England. Edwumayɛfo hɔn etuatew bi a ɔkɔr do wɔ Britain no maa ɔyɛɛ dzen dɛ wobenya adan a wotim nhoma ma edwuma, dɛm ntsi anuanom a wɔwɔ Brooklyn Bethel no yɛɛ edwuma bɔbor mber a wɔhyehyɛɛ dze maa nhomaba a wɔato dzin The Standard for the People no bɔbor bi 117,000 dze kɔmaa ehyiadzi no.

Memenda, May 29 no, wɔdze dɛm nhomawa no yɛɛ edwuma pa. Dɛm da no nhyiamu dwumadzi no hyɛɛ nkuran dɛ: “Wɔto nsa frɛ obiara a woehyira no do a ɔwɔ nhyiamu no ase dɛ ɔmfa no ho nhyɛ Ɔhen no na Ahendzi no ho dawurbɔ no mu. Kɔ Alexandra Palace no ntsɛm dɛ mbrɛ ibotum biara, na kɔbɔ wo ho amandzɛɛ ma edwuma wɔ Edwumayɛbea Hɔ.”

Wokor kyerɛwkyerɛw ahorow boro 100,000 kyekyɛe. July 15, 1926 Watch Tower no bɔɔ amandzɛɛ dɛ: “Nna enyigye ahyɛ anyɛnkofo no ma mboroso. Wɔtsee nka dɛ wɔayɛ dza wobotum nyina dze etsie Jehovah ne mbra a ɔse: ‘Hom yɛ m’adansefo dɛ emi nye Nyankopɔ’ no.”

Adekyee no, wɔ Royal Albert Hall no, Onua Rutherford kaa asɛmti a ɔfa “Dza Ntsi a Wiase Tum Ahorow No Rututu Ahyɛse—Ano Adur No” ho asɛm. Mber kakrabi pɛ nna wobuee apon no ekyir no, nyimpa 13,000 hyɛɛ asa no ma. Nyimpa bebree a wɔammba hɔn nsa no tsiee dwumadzi no yie dzenam akasaafidzi a wosiesiee wɔ abowadze no do.

Wɔ n’ankasa ne nkɔmbɔbɔ mu no, Onua Rutherford kyerɛkyerɛɛ mu dɛ wiase tumidzi ahorow no ahyɛse hwe ase no fi dɛ wɔpoo Jehovah nye N’ahendzi no. Onua Rutherford kɔr n’enyim keka tsiaa League of Nations no na ɔhyɛɛ nkɔm dɛ ɔbɔtow ahyɛ gu na ɔrennyɛ edwuma bio.

Emma yɛɛ edwuma wɔ ahɔhodan bi mu nna ebiasa dapon biara, nndɔnhwerew akron da biara. Ɔbisaa n’edwumasufo kwan dɛ ɔmma no da koro kwan mma ɔnkɔ n’amansin ehyiadzi ase. Mbom dɛ nkyɛ ɔbɛma no kwan no, edwumasufo no sesaa ne mber nna no kɔr nna enum dapon biara na ɔmaa no asɛm a obotum apaw mu baako: Gye mber fofor no to mu anaa gyaa edwuma no. Esiane dɛ na Emma dze ne werɛ ahyɛ Jehovah mu ntsi, ɔpaw dɛ obegyaa edwuma no na wakɔ ehyiadzi no ase.

Ber a Emma kɔree dɛ ɔrekɔdze n’edwuma a oegyaa no kɔma no, na hɔtɛl no panyin panyin no wɔ hɔ. Ber a ɔtsee asɛm no, ɔhyɛɛ bɔ dɛ ɔbɛhwɛ asɛm no do. Ɔyɛɛ asɛm no ho biribi, na wɔmaa Emma ber fofor a ɔdze bɛyɛ edwuma: nna enum dapɛn biara, dɔnhwer anan da biara. Ekyir no, ɔbɛyɛɛ papa mpo koraa sen ne ber a nkan no ɔdze yɛ edwuma no; na ɔma onya kwan dze yɛ n’abosonsom dze nkyɛn, nyamesom ndwuma!
