Tee Shi Abonsam Gbɛjianɔtoi Lɛ—Mo Ye Yehowa Kɛ Yesu Nɔ

LALA 55 Kaashe Amɛ Gbeyi!

JWƐŊMƆ HE KPO Kaaha Satan lakaa bo kɛtsɔ bɔ ni ebɔɔ mɔdɛŋ ni ekɛtsi Yehowa Nyɔŋmɔ kɛ Yesu Kristo he gbɛi fɔŋ lɛ nɔ.

“Nyɛtea shi nyɛwoa Abonsam, kɛkɛ ni ebaajo foi kɛjɛ nyɛŋɔɔ.”—YAK. 4:7.

KƐJƐ be ni wɔkpɛɔ wɔyiŋ akɛ wɔbaasɔmɔ Yehowa lɛ, Satan kɛ edaimonioi lɛ batsɔɔ wɔhenyɛlɔi ni feɔ basabasa. Shi Satan le akɛ enyɛŋ wɔ nɔ kɛji Yehowa fĩ wɔsɛɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, Abonsam bɔɔ mɔdɛŋ ni ekpata sɛɛfimɔ nɛɛ hiɛ kɛtsɔ bɔ ni eeebɔ mɔdɛŋ ni eha wɔku wɔsɛɛ kɛjɛ Yehowa he lɛ nɔ. Gbɛ kome ni etsɔɔ nɔ efeɔ enɛ ji, bɔ ni eeebɔ mɔdɛŋ ni eha wɔyiŋ afee wɔ kɔshikɔshi akɛ Yehowa kɛ e-Bi lɛ yɛ wɔfaŋ lɛɛlɛŋ.—2 Kor. 11:14; Yak. 1:6.

2 Kɛ́ asraafoi yɛ ta mli lɛ, ehe miihia ni amɛná nɔmimaa yɛ amɛhiɛnyiɛlɔi lɛ amli. Kɛ́ amɛlaaje nɔmimaa nɛɛ lɛ, ekolɛ amɛbaatsu nii yɛ amɛ diɛŋtsɛ amɛsusumɔ naa. Enɛ baanyɛ afee nɔ ni kɛ amɛwala baakpã. Nakai nɔŋŋ hu, wɔji asraafoi yɛ mumɔŋ ta mli ni wɔkɛ “mumɔi fɔji” miiwu. (Efe. 6:12) Satan Abonsam le akɛ, kɛ́ wɔlaaje nɔmimaa ni wɔyɔɔ yɛ Yehowa Nyɔŋmɔ kɛ Yesu Kristo mli lɛ, ewaŋ akɛ wɔbaakpoo amɛhiɛnyiɛmɔ kɛ amɛyelikɛbuamɔ. No hewɔ lɛ, ebɔɔ mɔdɛŋ fɛɛ ni ebaanyɛ koni egbe nɔmimaa ni wɔyɔɔ yɛ amɛmli lɛ hewalɛ. Yɛ nikasemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaasusu bɔ ni ekɛ gaŋtsɔxɔ nɛɛ tsu nii yɛ blema kɛ bɔ ni ekɛtsuɔ nii lolo ŋmɛnɛ lɛ he. Kɛkɛ lɛ wɔbaasusu bɔ ni wɔbaafee wɔtsĩ egãbii lɛ anaa lɛ he. Yɛ nikasemɔ ni nyiɛ sɛɛ lɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaasusu gaŋtsɔxɔi krokomɛi enyɔ ni Satan kɛtsuɔ nii yɛ wɔhe yɛ wɔmumɔŋ tawuu lɛ mli lɛ he.*

* Jeee Satan ji mɔ ni kɛ kaa fɛɛ kaa ni kɛ wɔ kpeɔ lɛ baa tɛ̃ɛ. Shi lɛ ji mɔ ni kɛ “je lɛ mumɔ lɛ” baa, ni enɛ kanyaa shitee-kɛ-wolɔi ni amɛwaa Nyɔŋmɔ webii lɛ ayi. No hewɔ lɛ, kɛ́ tɛ̃ɛ jio, kɛ́ tɛ̃ɛ jeee jio lɛ, Satan yɛ naagbai pii ni kɛ wɔ kpeɔ yɛ wɔ mumɔŋ ta lɛ mli lɛ sɛɛ.—1 Kor. 2:12; 1 Yoh. 5:19.

1. Mɛni gbɛjianɔtoo Satan kɛtsuɔ nii kɛkaɔ akɛ ebaafite Mawu tsuji lɛ anɔkwayeli?

2. Mɛni hewɔ ehe hiaa ni wɔhe Yehowa Nyɔŋmɔ kɛ Yesu Kristo nɔ wɔye lɛ?

2

SATAN KƐ AMALE FO YEHOWA KƐ YESU NƆ

3 Yɛ adesaa yinɔsane mli kpaako lɛ, Satan bɔ mɔdɛŋ ni ekpa Adam kɛ Hawa kɛjɛ Yehowa he. Satan laka Hawa koni ehe eye akɛ ebɔlɔ lɛ sumɔɔɔ eshihilɛ mli nibii kpakpai ha lɛ. Satan bi lɛ akɛ: “Ani anɔkwale ni akɛ, Nyɔŋmɔ kɛɛ nyɛkaye tso fɛɛ tso nɔ yibii yɛ abɔɔ lɛ mli?” (1 Mose 3:1-5) Satan ha Hawa he eye akɛ Yehowa kɛ kitai ni mli wawai miito ekɛ ewu lɛ fɛɛ ahe. Mɔbɔ sane ji akɛ, Hawa kɛ sɛɛ mli Adam fɛɛ banyiɛ henyɛlɔ Satan sɛɛ. Amɛtse amɛhe kɛjɛ Yehowa—Mɔ ni eha amɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ lɛ he.—1 Mose 3:6.

4 Kɛjɛ Adam kɛ Hawa beiaŋ tɔ̃ɔ lɛ, Satan etee nɔ efite Yehowa gbɛi. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, Satan kɛ Yob nanemei amaleŋfoi lɛ tsu nii koni eha Yob aye nɔ̃ he nɔ̃ akɛ Yehowa sumɔɔ lɛ kɛ akɛ ebuɔ e-anɔkwayeli gbɛ lɛ akɔnta. (Yob 22:1-3) Abonsam susuu akɛ hegbɛ nitsumɔi kɛ nɔnyɛɛi ni baa Yob nɔ lɛ baaha ekpoo Yehowa. Eyɛ mli akɛ nɔnyɛɛ kpele ba Yob nɔ kɛjɛ Satan gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ mli moŋ, shi nakai anɔkwafo lɛ hiɛ e-anɔkwayeli mli, ni ehaaa enanemɛi kɛ Nyɔŋmɔ mli afite kɔkɔɔkɔ.—Yob 27:1, 5.

5 Afii ohai komɛi asɛɛ be ni Yesu ba shikpɔŋ nɔ lɛ, Satan bɔ mɔdɛŋ ni ekpa gbɛi amia koni mɛi akanyiɛ Yesu sɛɛ. Gbɛ kome ni etsɔ nɔ efee enɛ ji akɛ, ewo jamɔŋ hiɛnyiɛlɔi kɛ mɛi krokomɛi ni nyɛɔ mɛi lɛ ahiɛ koni amɛgbɛ amale shi yɛ Yesu he. Kɛkɛ ni naagbee lɛ, amɛha agbe lɛ tamɔ efɔŋfeelɔ kpakpa ko.—Kanemɔ

Mateo 11:19; 27:27-31; Marko 14:55, 56; Yohane 8:41-45.

6 Yesu gbele sɛɛ lɛ, Satan kɛ ejwɛŋmɔ ma Yesu sɛɛnyiɛlɔi lɛ anitsumɔ lɛ nɔ. Klɛŋklɛŋ lɛ, Abonsam bɔ mɔdɛŋ ni ekɛ ŋwɛiŋwɛiyeli awo Yesu shitee lɛ mli. Yɛ Satan hewalɛ shishi lɛ, amale sane ko gbɛ eshwapo akɛ Yesu kaselɔi lɛ eba eyaju egbonyo lɛ. (Mat. 28:11-15) No sɛɛ yɛ gbii ni nyiɛ sɛɛ lɛ amli lɛ, Satan bɔ mɔdɛŋ ni ekɛ yiwaa awo asafo lɛ hegbeyei. Satan sumɔɔ ni efee nitsumɔ ni amɛ-Nuŋtsɔ lɛ kɛwo amɛdɛŋ lɛ he nɔ ko ni wa tsɔ akɛ amɛaatsu.—Bɔf. 7:57–8:1; 12:1-3; 28:22.

7 Eyayee akɛ klɛŋklɛŋ Kristofoi lɛ ekomɛi kpa shiɛmɔ yɛ ninyɛɛ nɛɛ hewɔ moŋ, shi amɛteŋ mɛi pii damɔ shi shiŋŋ ni amɛkpoo akɛ amɛbaafeo diŋŋ. Amɛyɔse akɛ Satan kɛ daimonioi lɛ ji amɛhenyɛlɔi diɛŋtsɛ lɛ. (Bɔf. 4:18-20; 1 Pet. 5:8) Ni ŋmɛnɛ hu? Mɛni gbɛi Satan tsɔɔ nɔ ebɔɔ mɔdɛŋ akɛ ebaatsi wɔnaa kɛjɛ Yehowa sɔɔmɔ he?

SATAN YA NO EEFO YEHOWA KƐ YESU AHE GUƆ

MANYAAWIE 2026 3

Satan je lẹ fɔɔ nibii ni fiteɔ Nyɔŋmɔ mlaai lẹ kɛ́fɔɔ wɔ nɔ (Kwɛmɔ kuku 11)

haa Tsɛ lɛ kɛ Bi lɛ feɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ. Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, mɛi kpaa Tsɛ lɛ he susumɔ, mɔ ni aleee egbɛi lɛ, ni amɛjáa Bi lɛ. Yehowa loo Yesu fɛɛ ekpɛɛɛ jamɔ nɛkɛ nɔ. (Mat. 4:10; Yoh. 4:23) Kɛ́ akpɔ́ mɛi ahiŋmɛii yɛ gbɛi nɛɛ anɔ lɛ, ebaanyɛ ewa akɛ akɛ amɛ baasusu nii ahe loo amɛbaanyɛ amɛte shi amɛwo wɔ shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ po.

9 Kɛ́ wɔbɔi naanyobɔɔ ko mli kpamɔ lɛ, nibii klɛŋklɛŋi ni wɔfeɔ lɛ ateŋ ekome ji akɛ wɔkaseɔ mɔ lɛ gbɛi. Satan bɔɔ mɔdɛŋ ni ekpa gbɔmɛi agbɛnaa kɛjɛ wekukpitiŋkpamɔ ni amɛkɛ Yehowa aaana lɛ he kɛtsɔ amɛtsĩi ni amɛkanu E-gbɛi po he lɛ nɔ. No hewɔ lɛ, kɛjɛ kɛŋkɛŋ lɛ Satan ekɛ jamɔ krɔŋkrɔŋbii etsu nii kɛha gbɔmɛi ehiɛ ekpa Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛi lɛ nɔ. (Kanemɔ Yeremia 23:26, 27.) Nakai henyɛlɔ lɛ efee he nɔmimaa po akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛi lɛ—nɔ ni Biblia lɛ kɛtoɔ mɔɔ kpele ni buɔ mɔ he lɛ he lɛ—ajeɔ kɛjɛɔ Biblia shishitsɔɔmɔi babaoo amli. (Abɛi 18:10) Abonsam mli gbɛtsiiŋ lɛ yɛ amale ni kɛɛ Yehowa ji mɔ ni haa amanehulu baa lɛ sɛɛ hu. Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, gbɔmɛi ni hiɛ tɔ̃ɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ lɛ nyɛŋ amɛna Yehowa akɛ Naanyo ni amɛbaanyɛ amɛbɛŋkɛ lɛ kpaakpa. (Yak. 4:8) Shi kɛlɛ, Satan bɔɔ mɔdɛŋ hu ni efite Yehowa webii lɛ ahemɔkɛyeli.

9. Yɛ Satan hewalɛwoo shishi lɛ, mɛni amale jamɔ etsi gbɔmɛi akɛ amɛkakashe? (Yeremia 23:26, 27)

4 BUULUƆ MƆƆ

10 Kɛ́ wɔkpeɔ wɔ-diɛŋtsɛ wɔ-nɔnyeli gbɛjianɔtoi ahe nitsumɔi anɔ lɛ, Satan sumɔɔ ni wɔhe wɔye akɛ Yehowa tsui efãaa wɔhe. Sa-tan kpaa gbɛ akɛ wɔmli baafu kɛ́ Yehowa ŋmɛ gbɛ ni wɔ-nɔnaa lɛ ya nɔ. (Abɛi 19:3) Mɛi ni jeɔ shishi amɛnuɔ he nakai lɛ baanyɛ po abi amɛhe akɛ: ‘Mɛni he ewɔ akɛ wɔbaaya nɔ wɔnyiɛ Ye-howa mla kɛ shishitoo mlai lɛ asɛɛ? Etsɔɔɔ akɛ ebaaye ebua mi!’ Nɛkɛ susumɔ nɛɛ baanyɛ ekɛ wɔ aya Satan hewalɛ shishi.

11 Satan kɛ nibii tamɔ intanɛti nɔ sanegbaa hei lɛ tsuɔ nii koni ekɛwo mɛi anɔkuyeli he jwɛŋmɔi yɛ Yehowa shishitoo mlai lɛ ahe. Yɛ nɛkɛ gbɛɛmɔhei nɛɛ anɔ lɛ, mɛi pii jieɔ susumɔ kpo akɛ jeŋba he mlai ni yɔɔ Biblia lɛ mli lɛ ebe eho, kɛ agbɛnɛ hu akɛ esa akɛ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ aye ehe kɛto lɛ diɛŋtsɛ eshishitoo mlai. Lɛɛlɛŋ, “je nɛŋ lumɔ lɛ” ele gbɛ ni etsɔɔ nɔ etutuaa naanyobɔlɛ ni yɔɔ nɔ ni hi kɛ nɔ ni ehiii teŋ lɛ jogbaŋŋ diɛŋtsɛ.—Yoh. 14:30; Yes. 5:20; 1 Tim. 4:1, 2.

12 Satan amale wiemɔi lɛ a toi ni wɔbaabo lɛ baanyɛ aha wɔgbee Yehowa he kɛya shɔŋŋ. Nyɛminuu ko ni wɔbaatsɛ lɛ Shannon lɛ gba akɛ: “Ehe mi be dani miyɔse akɛ jeŋba kpakpa he nɔkwɛmɔnɔi ni atsɔɔ mi yɛ skul lɛ kɛ mɛi ni mikɛ amɛ bɔɔ lɛ miifite bɔ ni minuɔ nɔ ni ja kɛ nɔ ni ejaaa shishi lɛ. Bɔ ni mibɔɔ mɔi ahe yɛ intanɛt nɔ lɛ, nakai nɔŋŋ misumɔɔ ni mikase mɛi ni mikɛ amɛ kpeɔ yɛ jɛmɛ lɛ ajeŋba. Blɛoo pɛ kɛkɛ ni mije Yehowa, mifami, kɛ minyɛmimɛi hii kɛ yei lɛ ahe kɛtee.” Nyɛkɛ nɔŋŋ lɛ, Shannon nyɛ eku esɛɛ kɛba eŋwɛi Tsɛ lɛ ŋɔɔ, ni eje Abonsam tsɔne lɛ mli. Mɛni ye ebua lɛ?

13 Shannon yɔse akɛ ehe miihia ni ebɔ mɔdɛŋ waa koni eya nɔ efee Yehowa kɛ Yesu naanyo. Ekɛɛ: “Misɔle ni mikɛ misɔlemɔi lɛ kpa gbee kɛtsɔ woji ni akala lɛ amli ni mikpa amli pɛi lɛ nɔ. Vidio ni ji Young People Ask—How Can I Make Real Friends? lɛ ye ebua mi waa.* Yɛ nakai sini lɛ mli lɛ, nyɛminuu ko ni ji gbekɛbii lɛ kɛ naagbai kpe tamɔ bɔ ni mikɛkpe lɛ nɔŋŋ. Ekɛ heshiba tee efɔlɔi aŋɔɔ ni ebi yelikɛbuamɔ. Mifee nakai nɔŋŋ. Mifɔlɔi, asafoŋ onukpai lɛ, kɛ nyɛmimɛi yei ni edara yɛ mumɔŋ yɛ asafo lɛ mli lɛ ye amɛbua mi koni mikpa naanyobɔi fɔji. Miná miyɔse akɛ anɔkwale lɛ jeee kpeei aya kɛ mifɔlɔi aniiashikpamɔ pɛ, shi moŋ hegbɛ ni anaa akɛ anɔkwale lɛ afee mɔ diɛŋtsɛ enɔ kɛ wekukpaa kpekpeemei ni mɔ kɛ Yehowa aná kpaakpa.”

HÉMƆ́ OYE AKƐ YEHOWA KƐ YESU JI ONANEMƐI

BIBLIA KASEMƆ NI AHEEE NƆKƆ-NƆKƆ

Kɛtsɔ bɔ ni wɔbaatee nɔ wɔbɛŋkɛ Yehowa kpaakpa he ni wɔjwɛŋɔ nɔ lɛ nɔ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔte shi wɔwo Satan (Kwɛmɔ kuku 14)

he nibii ni amɛhó amɛto ni amɛmli yɔɔ anɔkwale faŋŋ loo amale pɛɛ amɛye. Shi kɛ́ wɔkase Biblia lɛ kɛ wɔwoji lɛ, ni wɔkɛ Kristofoi ni edara bɔ daa nɛkɛ lɛ, aninŋ wɔhe nɔ kuku. Bɔ ni wɔleɔ anɔkwale lɛ jogbaŋŋ lɛ, nakai nɔŋŋ ebaawa kɛha wɔ akɛ wɔbaaye omanye yɛ mɔdɛŋ ni Satan bɔɔ koni etsɔ nɔ egblamɔ wɔhemɔkɛyeli lɛ mli lɛ kpoo mli.—Abɛi 13:20; Bɔf. 17:11.

15 Kɛ́ wɔkase Biblia lɛ mli jogbaŋŋ lɛ, ebaawaje wɔhemɔkɛyeli akɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu yɛ wɔfai aŋɔɔ. (Abɛi 22:19)

Kɛ́ wɔjwɛŋ saji ni tsɔɔ bɔ ni Yehowa kɛ Yesu susuɔ adesai ahe kpo lɛ anɔ lɛ, ebaatsirɛ wɔ ni wɔkɛ wɔhiɛ afɔ̃ amɛnɔ akɛ nanemɛi ni anyɛɔ akɛ he fɔ̃ɔ amɛnɔ. Biblia lɛ eyi kɛ hekɛnɔfɔɔ saji babaoo ni tsɔɔ akɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu sumɔɔ wɔ. Ákɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, Yehowa tsɔɔ bɔ ni enuɔ he ehaa ewebii lɛ akɛ ehe wa fe bɔ ni fufɔtsɛ nuɔ he ehaa ebiyoo ni enuɔ fufɔ lɛ. (Yes. 49:15) Ekɛ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni hiɛ sɔɔ egbɛi lɛ feɔ “jwetri ni jara wa.” (Mal. 3:16, 17, shishigbɛ niŋmaa.) Kɛfata he lɛ, Biblia lɛ jieɔ bɔ ni Yesu sumɔɔ wɔ kpo babaoo kɛtsɔ bɔ ni enu he yɛ e-afɔleshaa lɛ he ni aŋma ashwie shi lɛ nɔ.—Yoh. 15:12, 13.

15. Mɛni wɔbaafee koni wɔ ná nɔmima akɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu yɛ wɔsɛɛ?

6 BUU MƆƆ

16 Yisusɛm yɛ yelikɛbuamɔ ni Yehowa kɛ Yesu kɛhaa wɔ ŋmɛnɛ lɛ nɔ hu baanyɛ aye abua wɔ koni wɔná nɔmimaa yɛ suɔmɔ ni amɛyɔɔ kɛha wɔ lɛ he. Wɔbaanyɛ wɔkai bei ni wɔnu he akɛ Yehowa bɛŋkɛ wɔ kpaakpa lɛ, ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔná hewalɛ kɛjɛ nakai nɔkaiimɔi lɛ amli. Wɔbaanyɛ wɔbi wɔnyɛmimɛi hii kɛ yei lɛ hu bɔ ni amɛ hu ená nakai yelikɛbuamɔ lɛ he nɔkwɛmɔnɔ. (Rom. 12:18) Mɛni wɔbaanyɛ wɔfeɛ koni wɔha toiŋjɔlɛ ahi asafo lɛ mli kɛ́ wɔ kɛ mɛi krokomɛi miifee nibii? Kɛ́ wɔtɔ̃ mɔ ko nɔ? Kɛ́ mɔ ko tɔ̃ wɔ nɔ hu? Wɔbaana hetoo lɛ yɛ kpamɔ ko ni adamɔ suɔmɔ nɔ afeɛ lɛ mli. Nakai kpamɔ lɛ kɔɔ Biblia mli gbɔmɛi etɛ he: bɔfo Paulo, Filemon, kɛ Onesimo.

4 Mɛni wɔbaanyɛ wɔkase kɛjɛ Paulo nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ mli? Paulo kɛ mɛi krokomɛi ye yɛ gbɛ ni kɛ toiŋjɔlɛ baa nɔ. Enɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ tsɔɔ bɔ ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔye wɔbua kɛha toiŋjɔlɛ yɛ asafo lɛ mli kɛ́ wɔ kɛ mɛi krokomɛi miifee nibii.

5 Paulo bu Filemon tamɔ enaneko. (File. 17) Paulo esusuuu akɛ ebaanyɛ efã Filemon kɛkɛ, tamɔ nɔ ni ji enuntsɔ. (2 Kor. 1:24) Paulo bu Filemon akɛ enanemotsumɔlɔ kɛkai lɛ amɛnanekãa lɛ. Mɛni nɔkwɛmɔnɔ kpakpa po Paulo fĩi Filemon yɛ bɔ ni esa akɛ efee Onesimo, enyɛminuu hee lɛ he! Eji nɔkwɛmɔnɔ kpakpa hu kɛha wɔ fɛɛ, titri lɛ kɛha mɛi ni sɔmɔɔ akɛ asafoŋ onukpai yɛ asafo lɛ mli lɛ. Asafoŋ onukpai ni buɔ mɛi krokomɛi akɛ amɛnanemɛi lɛ kɛ toiŋjɔlɛ baa. Akɛ tookwɛlɔi ni yɔɔ suɔmɔ lɛ, amɛyeŋ “nɔtsɔmɔ nɔ” yɛ tooi lɛ anɔ. (1 Pet. 5:1-3) Yɛ no najiaŋ lɛ, amɛkɛ be shãa afɔle kɛha Nyɔŋmɔ gwantɛŋi lɛ, amɛboɔ amɛ toi, amɛbuɔ amɛ, kɛ amɛyeɔ amɛbuaa amɛ yɛ amanehulu beiaŋ. Tamɔ Paulo lɛ, asafoŋ onukpai ni buɔ mɛi krokomɛi akɛ amɛnanemɛi lɛ fĩɔ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ kpakpa kɛhaa tooi lɛ ni amɛkɛ amɛhe woɔ he ko ni toiŋjɔlɛ baanyɛ eshwere yɛ mli lɛ he.

6 Paulo jie Filemon yi. (Filem. 4, 5, 7) Wɔn fɛɛ wɔhe miihia yijiemɔ. Mɛni hewɔ? Klɛŋklɛŋ kwraa lɛ, wɔteŋ mɛi pii kɛ henamɔ he henyɛmɔ kpeɔ. Ekolɛ wɔtsui baaye yɛ bɔ ni wɔji he, kɛji mɛi asumɔ wɔ, aloo kɛji wɔbaalaaje hegbɛ kpele ko ni wɔyɔɔ yɛ sɔɔmɔ lɛ mli. Nii nɛɛ ji henumɔi ni yɔɔ bɔ ni eji. Kɛbɛ nakai lɛ, amɛbaanyɛ amɛha wɔtsui aye, wɔye henumɔi ahe tsɔ, wɔkpɛleee sane nɔ amrɔ nɔŋŋ, aloo po wɔte shi wɔwo mɛi—subanii ni baanyɛ afee gbagbalii kɛha wɔ mɔdɛŋbɔi ni wɔfeɔ koni wɔha toiŋjɔlɛ ahi shi lɛ. Mɔ ko baanyɛ adamɔ ehe ni eyeɔ tsui yɛ he lɛ naa jogbaŋŋ kɛji ajie eyi. (Abɛi 12:25) Ehaa enaa faŋŋ akɛ asumɔɔ lɛ ni ahiɛ sɔɔ lɛ. Ani obaasumɔ ni oná hewalɛ ni tamɔ nɛkɛ yɛ mɛi anɔ? Kɛ́ nakai ni lɛ, taomɔ hegbɛi koni ojie mɛi ayi kɛtsui muu fɛɛ. Jiemɔ mɛi ayi jeee yɛ nɔ kpakpa ni amɛnyɛ amɛtsu lɛ hewɔ kɛkɛ, shi moŋ yɛ mɔdɛŋ ni amɛbɔ lɛ hu hewɔ. Kɛ́ wɔkɛɛ lɛ kuku lɛ, kɛ́ ona nɔ kpakpa ko lɛ, wie nɔ kpakpa ko he.

NIKASEMƆI NI BAAJIE KƐJƐ ONESIMO NƆKWƐMƆNƆ LƐ MLI

10 Wɔnɔ fɛɛ wɔfeɔ nɔ ko kɛ wieɔ nibii ni gbaa mɛi ahe kɛ́ eba lɛ nakai. Onesimo nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ tsɔɔ wɔ bɔ ni wɔbaafee wɔsa toigbjɔlɛ kɛ́ eba lɛ nakai. Efee nɔ ko ni kɛ́ afee lɛ etamɔ nɔ ni waaa, shi bei pii lɛ efeemɔ wa. Ekpɛlɛ etɔmɔ lɛ nɔ, ni efee enɔgbɛ kɛ kpatɔ kɛ Filemon. Eka shi faŋŋ akɛ Onesimo le akɛ Roma mla biɔ ni eku esɛɛ kɛya enyɔŋtsɛ lɛ ŋɔɔ. No hewɔ lɛ, efee gbɛfãa ni he baahia shika babaoo ni tɔɔ mɔ aahu, ni efa fe kilomita 1,400 (mail 900) kɛku esɛɛ kɛtee Filemon ŋɔɔ. Ebaabi ni efee klalo akɛ ebaakpa ehewalɛ he, ni ekolɛ ekpɛlɛ nyɔmɔwoo krokomɛi ni jɛ enifeemɔi lɛ amli baa lɛ nɔ. Eŋkpaŋ akɛ no ji nɔ ko ni kɔɔmɔ waaa kwraa. Ni kɛ́ enu he akɛ ejeŋ kɛjɛ Filemon he lɛ eji nɔ ni ja hu? Kɛ́ enu he nakai tɔ̃ɔ po lɛ, eŋmɛɛɛ gbɛ ni no atsi etsui kɛjɛ eku ebaa enyɔŋtsɛ lɛ ŋɔɔ kɛ efeemɔ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni ebaanyɛ kɛwo toigbjɔlɛ hewalɛ yɛ amɛteŋ akɛ mumɔŋ nyɛmimɛi lɛ he.

11 Kɛ́ oto mɔ ko nɔ̃ lɛ, bɔɔ mɔdɛŋ koni okɛ lɛ akpata. (Mat. 5:23-25) Yɛ anɔkwale mli lɛ, ewa akɛ obaafee nakai, shi hejɔlɛ ni jɛɔ mli baa lɛ he yɛ sɛɛnamɔ kpoo. Je shishi kɛ kpoomɔ ni jɛ tsuiŋ. Feemɔ klalo akɛ obaafee toi kɛbo mɔ kroko lɛ toi koni onu bɔ ni enuɔ he eha lɛ shishi. Kpɛlɛmɔ otɔmɔ lɛ nɔ. Kpoo akɛ obaafee nibii ni ofee lɛ he sanebimɔ loo okɛ fɔ lɛ awo mɔ kroko nɔ. Kɛ́ ofee nakai lɛ, ebaanyɛ egbɔjɔ kpoomɔ lɛ hewalɛ, ebaatsĩ sane lɛ sɛɛ, loo ebaanyɛ eha sane lɛ afee nɔ ni ehiii kwraa po. Miku mihe kɛteŋ akrowai ni yɔɔ shɔŋŋ yɛ French Guiana lɛ ateŋ ekome mli he ni misɔmɔ yɛ asraafoi lɛ amli yɛ lɛ. Mitsuɔ nii otsi muu fɛɛ koni manyɛ maye ŋkpai yɛ otsi lɛ naagbee.

Mɔ ko ha mi Biblia yɛ nakai beiaŋ lɔɔ nɔŋŋ. Mikane emli nibii komɛi, ni mihiɛ kã nɔ akɛ mana ŋaawoo kpakpa ni baaye abua mi koni mikɛ miwala ahi shi jogbaŋŋ. Shi minuu nɔ ni mikaneɔ lɛ shishi.

Miná nanemɛi ni amɛhu amɛtaoɔ shihilɛ shishi, ni amɛfɔ̃ mi nine koni mibahi amɛŋɔɔ yɛ amɛ kpokpaa ko nɔ yɛ kwaa lɛ mli, he ko ni amɛtsɛɔ jɛmɛ Unity.

Yɛ koolooŋɔɔ lɛ mli lɛ, shihilɛ waaa kwraa, ni wɔnu he akɛ wɔye wɔhe. Wɔtsɔɔ wɔ diɛŋtsɛ wɔ be mli kɛfeɔ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni wɔjieee wɔhe naa wɔhãaa mɔ ko mɔ ko. Kɛ́ hulu te shi lɛ wɔfeteɔ shi, kɛ́ hulu nina shi lɛ wɔyatee shi, wɔduɔ wɔ niyenii, wɔtswaa lala, ni wɔshiaa weedi. Wɔkwɛɔ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ jogbaŋŋ ni wɔkɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni wɔyɔɔ lɛ jaa wɔhe. Kɛlɛ, minijie je wui ejaakɛ daa nɛɛ minyɛɛɛ matsɔ bɔ ni misusuɔ akɛ esa akɛ matsɔ ni kɛ shidaamɔ kuku kpaa we lɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ. (Jaj. 7:16) Ehe bahia ni mikase akɛ Yehowa ji Tsɛ ni yɔɔ suɔmɔ ni etaooo emuu-yeli kɛjɛɔ wɔŋɔɔ, shi moŋ ehiɛ sɔɔ bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔfee lɛ.

Yɛ Paris lɛ, ahala mi akɛ nɔyelɔ kpokpaa nɔkwɛlɔ nɔnajiemɔlɔ. Minu he akɛ migbɔ̃ tsɔ ni minyɛŋ mitsuu nakai nitsumɔ lɛ ejaakɛ minuu nitsumɔ lɛ he kɛjɛŋ. Shi mikase akɛ Yehowa mumɔ krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ yeɔ ebuaa wɔ ni wɔnyɛɔ wɔtsuo nibii babaoo fe nɔ ni wɔ pɛ wɔbaanyɛ wɔtsuu.

Mina naagbee kpoo ko hu. Kɛjɛ migbekɛbiiashi lɛ, miyeɔ hyperhidrosis hela lɛ, ni hãa mitsɔ̃ɔ tsɔ̃. Nɛkɛ hela nɛɛ yeɔ midaji ahe waa titri. Ewaa fe nine yɛ be mli ni miyeɔ hejarɛ, tamɔ be ni miwieɔ yɛ kpeemɔ wulu kpee ko shishi. Naagba nɛɛ bɛ mli akɛ eje kpo kwraa, shi kɛ́ mikɛ mihiɛ fɔ̃ Yehowa nɔ fe tsutsu lɛ, miyijiemɔ baa shi fe nine ni mitsɔ̃ɔ tsɔ̃ɔ kwraa bɔ ni tsɔ.

Wɔje maŋsɛɛ sɔɔmɔ nitsumɔ shishi yɛ New Caledonia, ni ji ŋshɔkpɔi akuu ko ni yɔɔ Pacific ŋshɔ lɛ wu-boka gbɛ, ni efa fe kilomitai 16,000 (maili 10,000) kɛjɛ Paris. Nɛkɛ nitsumɔ nɛɛ hu kɛ naagbai ba. Ebaahi ni wɔkɛ wɔhe afɔ̃ niyenii heehi amli, tamɔ duade, amankani, kɛ yelei. Ni ebaahi ni wɔfee wɔ nibii kɛfɔ̃ kokoroshie kukuurukuu kɛ gbeehi agbomɔ ni fiteɔ wɔwɔɔ leebi mra lɛ nɔ. Kɛfata he lɛ, ebaahi ni wɔtsake bɔ ni wɔkɛ mɛi gbaa sane lɛ he. Bei pii lɛ, maŋbii lɛ ehãaa hetoo kɛ wiemɔi, shi moŋ amɛholeɔ amɛhiŋmɛitsɔi nɔ kɛkɛ.

Wɔtee Maŋtsɛyeli Shiɛlɔi askul lɛ, ni amrɔ nɛɛ wɔyɛ kpokpaa nitsumɔ lɛ mli. Wɔna bɔ ni Yehowa kɛ emumu krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ eye ebua wɔ yɛ nitsumɔ fɛɛ nitsumɔ mli. Etsɔɔ mi akɛ gbalashihilɛ mli gbɔjɔmɔi haaa wɔtsɔmɔ nɔ ko ni he bɛ sɛɛnamɔ kɛha lɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, misɔleɔ be fɛɛ be kɛ mitsui fɛɛ kɛbiɔ yelikɛbuamɔ bɔ ni afee ni manye matsu nɔ ni he hiaa lɛ. Eyɛ mli akɛ kɛ́ midamɔ gbɔmɛi ahiɛ lɛ minuɔ he lolo akɛ mitsui enyɔɔ mimliii moŋ, shi Nyɔŋmɔ yelikɛbuamɔ nɔ lɛ, minye mikɛ hewalɛwoo kɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔ eha asafoi ni wɔyasaraa amɛ lɛ.

Yɛ tsakemɔi nɛɛ fɛɛ amli lɛ, Claudine ehiɛ kã mi he be fɛɛ be. Etoɔ etsui shi waa yɛ mifatɔi ahe. Efiɔ misee yɛ nitsumɔ ni wɔfeɔ feo lɛ mli, ni efeɔ klalo be fɛɛ be koni eye odase kɛ kã. Ehumɔɔɔ yɛ jenɔ hũu kɛ nɔnyɛɛi ni yɔɔ kpokpaa nitsumɔ lɛ mli lɛ he.

ANI OLE?

Mɛni nifeemɔi yaa nɔ yɛ maŋ agbóii anaa yɛ Biblia beiaŋ?

MAŊ agbói lɛ jeee he ni atsɔɔ kɛboteɔ maŋ lɛ mli kɛkɛ. Akɛnɛ gbɔmɛi babaoo tsɔɔ jɛmɛ hewɔ lɛ, ebatsɔ hei ni nitsumɔi babaoo yaa nɔ yɛ daa gbi shihilɛ mli. Biblia lɛ wie maŋ agbói ahe fe shii 50, ni etsɔɔ nibii ni yaa nɔ yɛ jɛmɛ.

Gbɔmɛi buaa amɛhe naa yɛ maŋ agbóhei lɛ anaa. Hebri wiemɔ ni atsɔɔ shishi “agbó” lɛ kɔɔ nɔ ko ni fa fe maŋ agbó lɛ kɛkɛ he. Etsɔɔ he ko ni atswia tsũi yɛ maŋ ko agbó lɛ he. Yɛ heti komɛi lɛ, kpoji kpelehei yɛ agbóhei lɛ amli he ni gbɔmɛi babaoo baanyɛ abua amɛhe naa yɛ.—Neh. 8:1.

Jaramɔlɔi toɔ jara nɔ yɛ maŋ agbóai lɛ ahe. Ekolɛ maŋ agbóai komɛi ni yɔɔ Yerusalem, tamɔ Loo Agbó lɛ kɛ Toi Agbó lɛ, ná amɛgbɛ́i lɛ kɛjɛ nibaanii ni ahɔ̃ɔ yɛ amɛhe lɛ mli.—Zef. 1:10; Yoh. 5:2.

Gbɛi tsuɔ jarayeli kɛ amɛ diɛŋtsɛ amɛsaneyeli ahe nii yɛ maŋ agbóji lɛ anaa. Abraham kɛ Boaz feɔ nitsumɔ he sanebimɔi yɛ odasefoi ahiɛ yɛ maŋ agbóji lɛ anaa. (1 Mose 23:10-15, 17, 18; Rut 4:1, 10) Gbɛfalɔi komɛi kpaa maŋ agbóji lɛ anaa maŋ kpo lɛ nɔ nyɔɔŋ kɛji akɛ mɔ ko ehereko amɛ atuu. (Koj. 19:15-21) Gbɛi hu yaa maŋ agbóji lɛ anaa kɛyaa sanekpakpai amli.—2 Sam. 18:24, 25.

Kojolɔi lɛ yeɔ sane yɛ maŋ lɛ agbó lɛ he. Amɛkwɛɔ sanehi amli yɛ gbɔmɛi ni damɔ shi ni baanyɛ anu odaseyeli lɛ kɛ fãmɔ lɛ fɛɛ hewalɔi amhiɛ. (5 Mose 21:18-20) Yɛ he ni amɛfeɔ nɛkɛ yɛ maŋ lɛ ahiɛ hewɔ lɛ, ewoɔ kojolɔi lɛ hewalɛ ni amɛye sane pɛpɛɛpɛ ni amɛhiɛ akakwɛ mɔ ko hiɛ yɛ amɛfãmɔi lɛ amli. Kɛ́ kojolɔi lɛ fã sane lɛ pɛ kɛkɛ ni maŋ onukpai lɛ tsu fãmɔ lɛ he nii nɔŋŋ. (5 Mose 25:2) Yɛ maŋ lɛ agbó lɛ he lɛ,

Maŋ agbói lɛ ji hehei ni gbɔmɛi feɔ nibii babaoo yɛ daa gbi shihilɛ mli

Atswɛɛ kojolɔi lɛ kɛkɛ ni amɛwo jalɛsane kɛ jeŋba kpakpa he hewalɛ.—5 Mose 16:18-20; Hiob 29:7, 11-17.

Hii anɔkwafoi wo Israelbii ni yɔɔ maŋ agboi lɛ anaa lɛ hewalɛ ni amɛkɛ amɛhiɛ afɔ̃ Yehowa nɔ. Gbalɔi kɛ mɛi krokomɛi wie yɛ agboi lɛ anaa ejaakɛ amɛle akɛ mɛi buaa amɛhe naa yɛ jɛmɛ. (1 Maŋ. 22:10; Yer. 17:19, 20) Ákɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, Yehu jaje yɛ maŋ lɛ fɛɛ hiɛ akɛ Yehowa eha ewiɛmɔ ni ekɛkɛ Ahab weku lɛ eba mli. (2 Maŋ. 10:8-10) Maŋtsɛ Hezekia bua etaatsɛmɛi lɛ anaa yɛ maŋ agboo ko naa bɔ ni afee ni ewo amɛ hewalɛ ni amɛkɛ amɛhiɛ afɔ̃ Yehowa yelikɛbuamɔ nɔ.—2 Kron. 32:6-8.

Biblia lɛ kɛɛ akɛ “anɔkwale nilee” bolɔɔ kɛ gbee wulu kɛkpaa heemɔ “yɛ maŋ agbóii lɛ anaa.” (Abɛi 1:20, 21) Blema yɛ Israel lɛ, maŋ agbóii lɛ anaa ji he ni nuumɛi ni yeɔ anɔkwale lɛ kɛ ŋaawoo kpakpa haa yɛ, ni amɛkojoɔ kojomɔ ni ja yɛ. Ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, aŋma Yehowa nilee lɛ yɛ Biblia lɛ mli, ni etsuji ni yɔɔ he fɛɛ he lɛ miaye amɛmiabua gbɛmɛi koni amɛku amɛsɛɛ kɛba anɔkwale nilee nɛɛ jɛhe.

BIBLIA MLI ANEWATSƆ Tso Ni Teɔ Shi Mra Lɛ

Almond tso lɛ ji tsei srɔtoi 30 ni atsĩ amɛtã yɛ Biblia lɛ mli lɛ ateŋ ekome. Eji tsei ni tsɔɔ fɔfɔi kɛji fɛĩ be lɛ eho nɔŋŋ lɛ ateŋ ekome. Abaanyɛ ana efɔfɔi ni yɔɔ fɛo ni yɔɔ kɔla ni ji pink aloo yɛnŋ lɛ mli tɔnn kɛjɛ January naagbee gbɛ. Esa jogbaŋŋ akɛ, Hebri gbɛi ni akɛtsɛɔ almond tso lɛ tsɔɔ “nɔ ni shĩɔ.” Ani kpaamɔ ko yɔɔ nakai tso lɛ kɛ e-Bɔlɔ lɛ teŋ?

Shishigbɛɛmɔ wiemɔ ni yɔɔ Yeremia 7:25 lɛ tsɔɔ akɛ Yehowa wie lɛ diɛŋtsɛ ehe akɛ eteɔ shi mra. March 15, 2011, *Buu-Mɔɔ* lɛ kɛɛ: “Tamɔ bɔ ni amondi tso lɛ ‘teɔ shi’ mra lɛ, nakai nɔŋŋ Yehowa ‘teɔ shi mra’ yɛ mfoniriŋ gbɛ nɔ . . . kɛbɔɔ emɛi lɛ kɔkɔ yɛ toigbele hewalɛi lɛ ahe. . . . Ni ejɔŋ ehe—‘ehiɛ aaakã’—kɛyashi egbalɛ wiemɔ lɛ baaba mli.”—Yer. 1:11, 12.

Akutu tso lɛ fɔfɔi yɛji lɛ hu aŋɔkɛfe nɔkwɛmɔnɔ yɛ Biblia lɛ mli kɛtsɔɔ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ kɛha yitso fufɔ nɔ ni gbalamɔ kɛbaa lɛ. (Jaj. 12:5) Ni ekolɛ akɛni amɛfɛo lɛ yɛ sɔrɔto hewɔ lɛ, tso lɛ fɔfɔi lɛ sɔmɔ akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ kɛha kɔɔpoi ni yɔɔ sɔlemɔ buu lɛ kanea tso lɛ niji lɛ anɔ lɛ.—2 Mo. 37:17-20.
