EDWƐNE ƐVI EZUKOANWOMA SOANVOLƐ NE ƐKƐ

Ghana menli kpalɛ ne mɔ wɔ December 2020 kpondole kɛ H.E. Maankpanyinli ne, Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo ɛdɛnla ekyi bieko amaa yeadoa gyima kpalɛ ne azo na yeayɛ dɔɔnwo bieko. Anwodoazo kpole mɔɔ yɛ gɔnwo mɔ Ghananli mɔ doale Arane ɛhye la le debie mɔɔ yɛmbu ye kɛ ɔle debie mgbane.

2021 Ezukoa Nhyehyɛleɛ ne le ezukoa nhyehyɛleɛ kpole mɔɔ siezie yɛ maanye ezukoa boɛ amaa yeabɔ ngoane nwo bane, yeamaa eguadene mɔɔ wɔ ngyegyeleɛ nu la anyia anwosesebɛ bieko, na yeadua yɛ adwenle kpalɛ wɔ Ghana mɔɔ le kpalɛ tɛla dɛba ne la fufule nwo, wɔ COVID-19 anzi. Ezukoa Nhyehyɛleɛ ne gyi yɛ ɛnyelɛzodeɛ mɔɔ le kɛ yɛkakyi maanle ɛhye na yɛazi maanye mɔɔ maa ɔ nwo sonle la azo.

Wɔ mekele ne fane dɔɔnwo mɔɔ li kanle la anu, yɛvale maangyebakyi ne yɛziele adwelielilɛ kpalɛ zo, yɛlua ngyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ bɛyɛle nwolɛ ngyehyɛleɛ kpalɛ mɔɔ bɛfa bɛyɛ maangyebakyi nzenzaleɛ zo, yɛvale ninyɛne mɔɔ maa ezukoa kɔ ɔ nyunlu la yɛlile gyima, yɛyɛle ninyɛne mɔɔ maa anyuhɔlɛ ba la, yɛboale anwuma gualilɛ nzenzaleɛ ne mɔ, na yɛvale menli ngyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ boa la yɛdole gua zo. Mɔɔ ɔbadwu 2019 awieleɛ la, ɛnee yɛwɔ maangyebakyi nzenzaleɛ adenle ne azo kpalɛ mɔɔ gyi maangyebakyi nzenzaleɛ nzonlenlɛ kpalɛ nee ewiade amuala diedi kɛ yɛle maanle mɔɔ ɛlɛkɔ ɔ nyunlu la azo, mekɛ mɔɔ COVID-19 ewule kpole ne rale yɛ nwo zo la. Ewule kpole ɛhye sɛkɛlɛ kpole ne, maangyebakyi kɔsɔɔti mɔɔ wɔ ewiade amuala la ɛde nwonle, mɔɔ Ghana boka nwo la.

Covid-19 Ewule kpole ne mɔɔ rale la vale ngyegyelɛ dɔɔnwo rale mɔɔ ɛzɛkye yɛ gyima nee yɛ alehyenlɛ biala ɛbɛlabɔlɛ. Noko, Aban nwu kɛzi ewule kpole ne ɛha yɛ kɛ menli la kpalɛ yɛɛ yebɔ mɛla ngakyile aba kɛ ɔfa yeaboa yeamaa gyima adɛnla kpɔkɛ bieko na yɛ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ azi pi kɛ mɔɔ ɛnee ɔde la. Anyelazo biala ɛnle aze ɔluakɛ yenyia ndenle fofolɛ mɔɔ yɛdua zo yɛyɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ lɛ anyelazo kpole. Aban lɛ ɛhulolɛ kɛ ɔbamaa yɛanyia menli mɔɔ le kpɔkɛ kpalɛ yɛɛ ɔti yeyɛ ngyehyɛleɛ kɛ bɛbamaa Ghanaamra ayile mɔɔ bɔ bɛ nwo bane la amaa yɛ muala yɛanwunvye yɛali munli yɛazi Ghana mɔɔ yɛkpondɛ kɛ yɛnwu ye la.

Yɛ nee bɛ ɛyɛ kɔnratɛ wɔ ngyegyelɛ nu kɛ yɛ anyuhɔle adenle ne foa bie, yɛɛ yɛli agyalɛ mɔɔ anu yɛ se la kɛ yɛbamaa yɛ maŋ ne sikadwuma ayɛ kpalɛ abia adenle kpalɛ zo kɛ ɔkɔ anyunlu. Kɛyɛ Ghana amra 1.2 million kɛkala bahola anyia ntoadoh sukulu ɛgengolɛ mɔɔ le kpalɛ na ɔze kpɔkɛ la amaa bɛ ngoane, yɛziezielɛ sikakpɔnle dwumayɛleɛ ne yɛɛ dwumadivo maando ɛnyia bɛ ezukoa mɔɔ bɛvale bɛguale sikakpɔnle mɔɔ zɛkyele la azo. Eza, bɛzisi kale yɛlɛ ɛleka na yɛnwu kale mɔɔ bɛyɛle bɛ limoa la wɔ yɛ gua ne mɔ azo nee yɛ adenle mɔ azo.

Yɛkpa yɛ nwo nzonlɛ kɛ yɛyiale ngyegyelɛ bie mɔ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛbɔle mɔdenle kɛ yɛmaa yɛ anyunluhɔle bodane ne mɔ ayɛ gyima la. Aban ne ɛlɛbɔ mɔdenle kpole kpalɛ ɛlɛye ngyegyelɛ ɛhye mɔ avi ɛkɛ na yeamaa adwelielilɛ ninyɛne anyia anwolseseɛ. Kɛ neazo la, yɛdoale GH¢750 million CAP-BuSS Gyimalilɛ ne azo wɔ May 2020 na ɛhye ɛboa Adwelielilɛ Ekyi, Ngalɛ Nu Nee Mɔɔ Boka Nwo (MSMEs) nee gyɛne dɔɔnwo.

Kɛmɔ yɛtɛyɛle bie ɛlɛ la, bɛfɛlɛ yɛ kɛ yɛva yɛ sa yɛdo nu na yɛbɔ nu yɛyɛ yɛ maanle ne kpalɛ kɛ yɛbadoa ninyɛne kpole kpole mɔɔ yɛbɔle ɔ bo kɛ yɛyɛ la azo amaa anyuhɔlɛ ara awie biala anwo zo. Ɔluakɛ amgba, yɛliele yɛ bɔ kɛ yɛbava yɛ anwosesebɛ kɔsaiti nee yɛ ahonle kɔsaiti yɛazonle Ghana.

Nyamenle ɛyila yɛ muala.

Ghana dɛnla kyɛ!

Ken Ofori-Atta Ezukoa Soafo

MUKYIA KYENLE

1. 2021 Manneɛma Budget ne le mɔɔ Aban ne yɛle wɔ 2020 nee ezukoa dodo mɔɔ ɔkpondɛ kɛ ɔgye amaa yeava yeali ngyehyɛleɛ nee gyima ngakyile nzonlɛ wɔ 2021 la mukuru. 2021 Budget ne edwɛkɛtile a le “Ewielie, Enlunlonlɛ, nee Ɔhɔzonlɛ”. Edwɛkɛtile ɛhye gyi nɔhalɛ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ kile kɛ wɔ ɛvolɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ ɛze ɛhɔ la anu, Budget fofolɛ biala kile gyima fofolɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛbɔ ɔ bo na bɛgyakyi mɔɔ bɛlɛyɛ la ɛkɛ. Ɛhye ɛndoto yɛ ezukoalilɛ ne ngyegyelɛ nu ala, na emomu ɔmaa bɛtɛtua ɛzonlenlɛ noko dɔɔnwo, mɔɔ bɛndua gyimayɛvoma dɔɔnwo wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ fɛta la anu wɔ gyima mɔɔ bɛyɛle la anwo. Ɛhye maa gyima ne mɔ anwo ezukoa ne kɔ ɔ nyunlu kɛmɔ ezukoa mɔɔ bɛtɛtuale la kɔ bɛ nyunlu kɛ anyebolo ezukoa la.

2. Wɔ 2021 anu, Aban yɛ ngyehyɛleɛ kɛ ɔdua adenle fofolɛ azo. Botaeleɛ ne a le kɛ bɛbaweɛ gyima mɔɔ bɛtɛwiele mɔɔ bɛyɛ la azo ɔlua ɛwɔkɛ mɔɔ bɛbɔle kɛ bɛbawe gyima mɔɔ wɔ ɛkɛ la mɔɔ bɛbali zo.

EZUKOA GYIMA YƐLƐ WƆ 2020

Maakiroɛkɔnɔme Gyimalilɛ

3. Maanzisile ndelebɛbo mɔɔ fane maangyebakye ne gyimalilɛ nwo wɔ 2020 la a le ɛhye:

• Afe ne siane ngone mɔɔ limoa la hyele GDP bɔbɔ nyunluhole ngyehyɛnu mɔɔ amuala kɔ la 0.2 pesɛnte. Bɛkɛlɛ anyelazo kɛ afe ne awieleɛ GDP nyunluhole ngyehyɛnu ne kɛdwu 0.9 pesɛnte;

• Anwonde-Angyinli amgba GDP anyunhɔlɛ kɛyɛ 0.4 pesɛnte wɔ aumeka bulu ne abo azo nwiɔ ne anu, yɛɛ bɛ nye la kɛ ɔbadwu 1.6 pesɛnte wɔ aumeka ne awieleɛ;

• Mɔkɔlɛbolɛ awieleɛ ninyɛne boɛ gyinlabelɛ mɔɔ yɛ kɔlɛ kpole la yɛle 10.4 pesɛnte mɔɔ yɛfa yɛtotɔ bodane mɔɔ bɛyɛle nu nzenzaleɛ kɛ ɔkɛyɛ 11.0 pesɛnte nwo la;

• Ezukoa mɔɔ bɛvale bɛlile gyima amuala mɔɔ ɛngula nwolɛ ezukoa la yɛle GDP ne anu pɛsɛnte 11.7 mɔɔ ɛnee bɛkpondɛ kɛ ɔyɛ GDP ne anu pɛsɛnte 11.4 la anzi;

• Ezukoa mɔɔ bɛvale bɛyɛle gyima la ɛnzenzɛlɛ hakyile hɔle aze 5.3 pesɛnte wɔ GDP nu mɔɔ ɛnee bɛkpondɛ kɛ ɔyɛ 4.6 pesɛnte wɔ GDP nu; na

• Ewiade Amunli Ezukoa Mɔɔ Bɛva Bɛsie La yɛle US$8.6 billion, mɔɔ kɛyɛ siane 4.1 ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛfa bɛfi maanle gyɛne nu bɛba la anwo kakɛ.

Ezunle Gyimalilɛ

5. Wɔ Mɔkɔ-Bulalɛ 2020 awieleɛ ne, Aban ne yɛle ezukoa mɔɔ kɔ bɔbɔ GH¢100.1 billion la anwo gyima wɔ ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛyɛle la anu (mɔɔ kakɛ mɔɔ bɛtua noko boka nwo la), mɔɔ ɛnee bɛkpondɛ kɛ bɛtwe GH¢97.7 billion la. Ezukoa mɔɔ bɛnyianle nwo nzɔlɛ nee ninyɛne gyɛne mɔɔ bɛyɛle la lile bodane ne azo. Ezukoa mɔɔ bɛyɛle nwolɛ gyima mɔɔ ɔha la amuala bɛ nwo dele vile 2020 bodane ne mɔ anwo, kɛ mɔɔ bɛnwu ye wɔ Nvoninli 2 la. Ezukoa mɔɔ bɛyɛle nwolɛ gyima kpole wɔ ninyɛne bie mɔ anu la vi mɔɔ dooa ye la, wɔ ninyɛne gyɛne anu:

• Kpɔkɛdelɛ gyimayɛvoma mɔɔ bɛfa bɛ dɔɔnwo la, kakɛ mɔɔ bɛtua bɛma frontline kpɔkɛdelɛ gyimayɛvoma la, nee sogyama mɔɔ bɛkpanyinli la ɛvolɛ mɔɔ bɛfa bɛpɛ gyima la mɔɔ bɛdɛle zolɛ wɔ Compensation of Employees abo la;

• COVID-19 nwolɛ ezukoa mɔɔ bɛzɛkyele wɔ Ninyɛne nee Ɛzonlenlɛ Gyima Mɔɔ Bɛfa Bɛli Gyima abo; nee

• COVID-19 gyimalilɛ nwo azua nee ninyɛne ngyehyɛleɛ gyima mɔɔ wɔ Capital Expenditure abo.

6. Ɔhyia kɛ yɛnwu kɛ Aban boale ezukoa GH¢44.9 billion doale ezukoa kɔsɔɔti mɔɔ yɛnyianle nee ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛvale bɛkyɛle mɔɔ yɛ GH¢55.1 billion la anwo amaa yeali ngyehyɛle mɔɔ ɔyɛle kɛ ɔbatu ezukoa yeazie la azo wɔ 2020.

Manzema Kakɛ Gyima Yɛlɛ Wɔ 2020

7. Kakɛ mɔɔ ɛnee wɔ ɛkɛ wɔ December 2020 awieleɛ la yɛle GH¢291.6 billion (US$50.8 billion), mɔɔ gyina hɔ GDP ngyɛnu 76.1 azo la. Dodo ɛhye boka ezukoa mɔɔ Aban boale kɛ ɔbava yeadua ezukoa gyima ngɛlɛbera ne mɔ ɛwɔlɛ nwo kakɛ nee anyezɔlɛ gyima ne mɔ Independent Power Purchase (IPPs) wulirera nwo kakɛ la anwo. Saa ezukoa gyima ngɛlɛbera ne mɔ ɛwɔlɛ nwo kakɛ ne ɛnle nu a, ɛnee 2020 kakɛ ne gyina hɔ GDP ngyɛnu 71.5 azo. Kɛzi bɛhyehyɛ kakɛ ne la a le ɛhye:

• Maanle gualilɛ kɛlɛtɛ: GH¢141.7 billion (US$24.7 billion) mɔɔ le 48.6 ekyi wɔ kɛlɛtɛ mɔɔ bɛliele amuala la anu; na

• Maanle nu kɛkɛ: GH¢149.8 billion (US$26.1 billion) mɔɔ kile kɛkɛ muala dodo ne anu 51.4 percent.

COVID-19 Nyanelilɛ Kpole Ne Anyunlu Ndɛlebɛbo

13. COVID-19 ewule kpole ne mɔɔ rale ewiade amuala wɔ 2019 awieleɛ ne la nyianle nganeɛdelɛ kpole wɔ abusua biala kɛyɛ, eguadeli, nee ewiade amuala sikadwuma nwo. Ɔsɛkyele sikadwuma gyimalilɛ ngakyile, mɔɔ manle bɛdule menli gyima nu, bɛminli bɛ kakɛ, na sikadwuma nwo anyelazo noko zɛkyele kpole. Bieko, bɛhɛlɛ ewule mɔɔ bo 2.6 million wɔ ewiade amuala.

14. Wɔ Ghana, ewule ne mɔɔ ɛlɛtɛlɛ la hɔle zɔhane dahuu dapɛne ko ko biala menli 11 kɔkpula kɛ ɔdrole 781 wɔ July 2020 dapɛne mɔɔ li awieleɛ la anu, na ɔdole aze kɔkpula nyane 104 wɔ December 2020 dapɛne mɔɔ li awieleɛ la anu. Wɔ 2021 anu, ewule ne mɔɔ ɛlɛtɛlɛ la rale bieko na yemɔ maa nuhua dɔɔnwo hɔle zɔhane mɔɔ vi 99 wɔ January dapɛne mɔɔ limoa la anu kɔkpula 880 kɔdwu dapɛne mɔɔ li 5th February la anu.

15. Wɔ mmɔdenbɔ mɔɔ bɛbɔle kɛ bɛsi ewulenzane ne adenle la anu, Aban ne dole mɛla bie mɔ guale Ghana amra kpalɛ ne mɔ anwo zo na ɛhye hanle gualilɛ wɔ maangyebakye ne kɔnɔmi ne afahye titilii ne mɔ anu, titilii ɛyvolɛlilɛ, ɛyvalɛ, kpɔkɛdelɛ, adendulɛ, ninyɛne yɛlɛ, azule nee azua, ezukoa gualilɛ, nee sukulu ɛzukoalɛ afahye ne mɔ.

16. Kalehyelilɛ nu gyimayɛvoma, kadelɛvoma nee "tro-tro" nuhua mɛteama, nee eku gyɛne mɔɔ bɔ wɔ abɔntene zo gyima nu mɔɔ bɛnnyia menli duma nu nyilalɛ biala la nwunle amaneɛ kpole wɔ ngyinlazo mɔɔ bɛvale bɛdole ɛkɛ la anwo. Ɔwɔ nuhua kɛ ɛhye mɔɔ bɛyɛle la hyianle kpalɛ na bɛahola bɛaziezie ewule ne ɛdeɛ, noko eza ɔmanle ninyɛne dɔɔnwo mɔɔ menli kpondɛ kɛ bɛtɔ nee azonlenyia dɔɔnwo mɔɔ bɛkpondɛ la dwule aze kpole kpalɛ.

17. Saa yɛfa yɛtoto yɛ gɔnwo mɔ mɔɔ bɛwɔ eku ne anu la anwo a, ninyɛne mɔɔ arɛle boka nwo mɔɔ Ghana Arane yɛle kɛ ɔfa yeazi ewule ne ɛhɔlɛ kpole ne azo la, Ewiade Amra Eku ne foa bie mɔ yele ye ayɛlɛ, mɔɔ bɛhanle kɛ yɛyɛ kpole yɛdɛla zɔ bɔbɔ.

21. Aban ne dole Ghana COVID-19 Alleviation and Revitalization of Enterprises Support (Ghana CARES) nhyehyɛleɛ ne gua wɔ 20th November 2020, amaa yeade ewulenzane ne ngyegyeleɛ zo na yeamaa adenle bɔ yɛbalua zo yɛanyia anyunhɔlɛ ndɛndɛ mɔɔ lɛ ezukoa gyinlabelɛ kpalɛ na ɔhyia. Nɔhalɛ nu, ɔle adenle kpole bɔ yɛbalua zo yɛahakyi Ghana ezukoa gyinlabelɛ ne yɛamaa yeayɛ mɔɔ ɛlɛkɔ anyunlu, mɔɔ gyima ngakyile wɔ nu, mɔɔ anyunhɔlɛ wɔ nu, na ɔhyia kpalɛ amaa yeadie Ghana amra kɔsɔɔti ngyianlɛ kpalɛ.

22. Ghana CARES gyinla kɛzi bamaa maangyebakye ayɛ kpole ndɛndɛ la azo amaa anwonyelɛ ara ndɛndɛ na badoa zo baha gualilɛ kpalɛ bazi kɛkɛ. Dua CARES azo, Aban le bodane kɛ maanyɛlɛ nzinilɛ nee anyuhɔlɛ gyinla maangyebakye zo na yebɔ gualilɛ fofolɛ mɔɔ bamaa ahatualɛ kpalɛ la anyi, titile wɔ anwumabɔvolɛma gyimayɛlɛ nu.

23. Gyima mɔɔ anwo hyia kpole nee nhyehyɛlee mɔɔ bɛbali nwolɛ gyima wɔ Ghana CARES Nhyehyɛlee ne abo la bie a le:

• gua-yɛlɛ ɛyazonlɛ nu ɛzonlenlɛ nee ngakula mɔɔ kɔ sukulu la ɛtweyɛlɛ kɔ gua-yɛlɛ ɛyazonlɛ nu;

• Ghana ninyɛne nsisile ekyi kyirale gyima ne mgbɔlabɔlɛ;

• mɔɔ bɛyɛ enginiringi/masin ninyɛne nee ICT/dɛgyetal ekɔnɔmi gyima ngakyile mɔɔ bɛbaye bɛazie la.

• ndɛndɛ mɔɔ bɛlɛyɛ dwinlinli nu debie;

• Ghana Azua & Azua Si Maanle Nu Gyima Ne Anyuhɔlɛ;

• Ghana mɔɔ bɛbayɛ ye Maandɔlɛ Nu Gyimalilɛleka Kpole;

• Development Bank of Ghana mɔɔ bɛbabɔ ɔ nloa amaa yeaboa ezukoa nu wɔ nyilenu nee anyuhɔlɛ gyima ne mɔɔ wɔ Ghana CARES Programme ne abo la anwo.
