NRƐLƐBƐBƆLƐ ƐWƆ EZUKOASUMUNLI NE ƐKƐ

Ghana menle kpalɛ ne mɔ dɔle aba wɔ December 2020 kɛ bɛkɛlie H.E. Maankpanyinli ne, Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo bɛkɛdo nu bieko kɛ ɔli gyima kpalɛ ne azo na yeayɛ dɔɔnwo. Anwodozo mɔɔ yɛ gɔnwo mɔ Ghananli mɔ arɛlɛ la le mɔɔ yɛmbu yɛ nye yɛngua zo.

2021 Ezukoa Nhyehyɛleɛ ne le nhyehyɛleɛ titilii mɔɔ kpondɛ kɛ ɔsiezie yɛ maanle ne ezukoa amaa yɛabɔ ngoane nwo bane, yɛawula gyima mɔɔ ɛlɛsɛkye la anwosesebɛ, na yɛava yɛ adwenle kɔsɔɔti yɛado kɛ yɛkɛzia yɛazi Ghana mɔɔ le kpalɛ la kpalɛ, wɔ COVID-19 nzi. Ezukoa Nhyehyɛleɛ ne gyi yɛ ɛnyelɛzodeɛ mɔɔ kile kɛ yɛbahakyi maanle ɛhye na yɛazi maanle mɔɔ kola nea ɔ nwo la azo.

Wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ li ɛkɛ ne anu la, yɛva maanyɛlɛ yɛzia yɛzie adenle kpalɛ zo, yɛlua ngyehyɛleɛ ngakyile mɔɔ bɛyɛle bɛkpondɛle maanyɛlɛ kpole mɔɔ bɛsiezie la azo, yɛva ndenle mɔɔ maa ezukoa kɔ ɛzonlɛ la yɛli gyima, yɛyɛle ninyɛne mɔɔ bamaa maanyɛlɛ anyi la, yɛboale anwondomba gyima ngakyile, yɛɛ yɛva menli asetɛnla boalɛ ngyehyɛleɛ yɛvuale. Wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ 2019 rale awieleɛ la, ɛnee yɛwɔ maanyɛlɛ kpole kpalɛ mɔɔ ɛlɛsesa la adenle zo kpalɛ wɔ kɛzi maanyɛlɛ mɔɔ bɛkile la si kɛ kpalɛ la ati, yɛɛ ewiade amuala liele yɛ dole nu kɛ maanle mɔɔ ɛlɛkɔ zolɛ la, kolaa na COVID-19 ewule kpole ne ara yɛ nwo zo. Nyane kpole mɔɔ ewule ɛhye vale rale la, ewiade maanyɛlɛ kɔsɔɔti ɛnwu ye, mɔɔ Ghana boka nwo la.

Covid-19 Ewule kpole ne mɔɔ rale la vale ngyegyelɛ dɔɔnwo rale mɔɔ ɛzɛkye yɛ gyima nee yɛ alehyenlɛ biala ɛbɛlabɔlɛ a. Noko akee, Aban ne ɛnwu kɛzi ewule kpole ne ɛha yɛ kɛ menli la kpalɛ na yebɔ ngyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ bamaa gyima gyinla ɔ nwo azo bieko na yɛ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ azia ara kɛmɔ ɔde la adwelie. Anyelazo biala ɛnzenzenle ɔluakɛ yenyia ndenle fofolɛ mɔɔ yɛbalua zo yɛayɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ ɔbamaa nwolɛ adenle dɔɔnwo ara a. Aban ne ɛzi kpɔkɛ kɛ ɔbamaa yɛanyia menli mɔɔ lɛ kpɔkɛdelɛ kpalɛ na ɔti yeyɛ ngyehyɛleɛ kɛ bɛbado Ghanama arɛle amaa yɛamuala yɛali munli yɛadua Ghana mɔɔ yɛkpondɛ kɛ yɛnwu ye la.

Yɛ nee bɛ ɛyɛ kɔnratɛ bɔbɔ wɔ ngyegyelɛ nu kɛ yɛ anyuhɔle ngyehyɛleɛ ne foa ko na yɛbɔ mɔdenle yɛlie yɛ sikadwuma ne yɛzi anyuhɔle adenle kpalɛ zo bieko. Kɛyɛ Ghananma mgbe 1.2 kɛkala kola nyia mbayelɛ sukuu ɛzukoalɛ kpalɛ mɔɔ tɛla biala maa bɛ ngoane, yɛziezie sikakponle dwumayɛleɛ ne na edwɛkɛvolɛma dɔɔnwo ɛnyia bɛ ezukoa mɔɔ bɛvale bɛguale sikakponle mɔɔ guale la azo bieko. Bieko, bɛzisi kaliye ngakyile ngilɛleɛ ɛleka na yɛnwu kaliye mɔɔ bɛlimoale bɛgyilale bɛ la wɔ yɛ gua nee yɛ adenle muala azo.

Yɛdie yɛto nu kpalɛ kɛ yɛyiale ngyegyelɛ bie mɔ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛbɔle mɔdenle kɛ yɛkɛpɛ yɛ anyuhɔle bodane ne mɔ anye la. Aban ne ɛlɛbɔ mɔdenle kpole kɛ ɔsiezie ngyegyelɛ ɛhye mɔ na yeamaa gyima ne mɔ anyia ahomegyeɛ. Kɛ neazo la, yɛdole GH¢750 million CAP-BuSS Dwumalilɛ ne azo wɔ May 2020 na ɛhye ɛboa Ngyikyi, Ngyikyi-Kpole Nee Kpole-Nlɛnle Gyima ne mɔ (MSMEs) nee gyɛne dɔɔnwo.

Kɛ mɔɔ ɔtɛsile ɛlɛ la, bɛfɛlɛ yɛ kɛ yɛbɔ nu yɛbɔ yɛ sa nu na yɛboka yɛ maanle ne anyidɛlɛ nwo ɔlua gyima ngakyile mɔɔ yɛva akɛnrasesebɛ yɛyɛ la mɔɔ yɛbado zo yɛayɛ na amaa yɛanyia anyidɛlɛ yɛamaa awie biala. Yeanyɛ boɛ biala, yɛbɔle ɛwɔkɛ kɛ yɛbasonle Ghana yɛava yɛ anwosesebɛ muala nee yɛ ahonle kɔsɔɔti.

Nyamenle hyira yɛ muala.

Ghana tɛnla aze dahuu!

Ken Ofori-Atta Ezukoa Soafo

MUKELƐBƐLA

1. 2021 Maninli Ne Ezukoa Ndwukɛlata Ne le mɔɔ Aban ne holɛ wɔ 2020 nu nee ezukoa dodo mɔɔ ɔkpondɛ kɛ ɔboɔboa ano ɔlua adwule zo amaa yeali nhyehyɛe nee gyima dɔɔnwo azo wɔ 2021 anu amuanli. 2021 Ezukoa Ndwukɛlata ne bodane a le “Awielielie, Mgbondabulɛ, nee Gyimadili”. Bodane ɛhye gyi nɔhalɛ ne mɔɔ kile kɛ wɔ ɛvolɛ dɔɔnwo anu, Ezukoa Ndwukɛlata fofolɛ biala kɔ zo kɛ nyianle gyima fofolɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛbɔ ɔ bo la, na ɔmaa gyima mɔɔ bɛlɛyɛ bɛwie la kpondɛ kɛ ɔminli. Tɛ ye ngome a ɔtwe yɛ ezukoa nwo la, emomu mekɛ dɔɔnwo anu ɔkɔwie kɛ bɛndua kakɛ, mɔɔ gyimayɛvoma dɔɔnwo ɛnnyia bɛ ezukoa wɔ mekɛ kpalɛ nu wɔ gyima mɔɔ bɛyɛ la anwo a. Ɛhye maa gyima ne mɔ anwo kakɛ yɛ kpole kpalɛ kɛmɔ kakɛ mɔɔ bɛndua ndɛ la twe mgbondabu ezukoa boka nwo la.

2. Wɔ 2021 anu, Aban yɛ ngyehyɛleɛ kɛ ɔfa adenle fofolɛ zo. Bodane ne a le kɛ ɔbawie gyima mɔɔ bɛtɛwiele bɛ subiane ne ɔlua ɛwɔkɛ mɔɔ bɛbɔle kɛ bɛbawie gyima mɔɔ wɔ ɛkɛ ne la mɔɔ bɛbali zo.

Maakro-ekɔnɔme Gyimalilɛ

3. Edwɛkɛ mɔɔ bɛlimoa bɛhyehyɛ ye wɔ ezukoa gyima ne mɔɔ kɔle zo wɔ 2020 anu la a le ɛhye mɔ:

• Afe ne anu siane ngonla mɔɔ limoa la hakyile GDP mɔɔ fane bɔbɔ la anyuhɔlɛ ndɛnla mɔɔ le ɔbɔbɔ nu 0.2 pesɛnte. Bɛlɛ anyelazo kɛ afe ne awieleɛ la GDP anyuhɔlɛ ndɛnla ne badwu 0.9 pesɛnte;

• Non-Oil real GDP nyianle anyuhɔlɛ mɔɔ bɔ nu kɛyɛ 0.4 pesɛnte wɔ kenlenzile bulu ne anwo anye nwiɔ mɔɔ limoa wɔ ɛvolɛ ne anu, na bɛkpondɛ kɛ ɔbawie ɛvolɛ ne wɔ 1.6 pesɛnte;

• Mɔkɔmɔkɔ azolɛ mɔɔ dɔle wɔ December awieleɛ la yɛle 10.4 pesɛnte saa ɛfa ɛto kɛzi bɛvale bɛzie la mɔɔ yɛ 11.0 pesɛnte la anwo;

• Ezukoa mɔɔ wɔ ɛkɛ ne mɔɔ bɛvale bɛyɛle ngyehyɛleɛ ne amuala ɛbazi ɛtane ne yɛle GDP ne anu pɛsɛnte 11.7 mɔɔ bɛvale bɛdole bɛ bodane fofolɛ mɔɔ le GDP ne anu pɛsɛnte 11.4 la anwo;

• Ezukoa kɛlata ne kilele kɛ bɛbɔle sin 5.3 pesɛnte wɔ GDP ne anu tialele bɛ nye kɛ ɔbabɔ sin 4.6 pesɛnte wɔ GDP ne anu; na

• Ewiade Amunli Ndoto Ezukoa mɔɔ bɛva bɛzie la yɛle US$8.6 billion, mɔɔ kɛyɛ nzelane mɔɔ bɛfa bɛba maanle nu la siane 4.1 a.

7. Kakɛ mɔɔ ɛnee wɔ ɛkɛ wɔ December 2020 awieleɛ la yɛle GH¢291.6 billion (US$50.8 billion), mɔɔ gyina ɛkɛ ne maa GDP ngyɛnu 76.1. Dodo ɛhye boka ezukoa mɔɔ Aban boale kɛ ɔbadea ezukoa gyima nwo ngyegyelɛ nee anwosesebɛ gyima nwo Independent Power Purchase (IPPs) kakɛ nwo la anwo.
