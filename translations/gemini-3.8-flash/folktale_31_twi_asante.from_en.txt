Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐYƐE A YƐBƐHU OKRA DAA SƐ ƆDA VELVET KUSIN SO, BERƐ A ƆKRAMAN NSO BƐDA MSO A ƐWƆ GYAADE GYAHENE MU NO

Wɔse, bere bi na ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ a (ne nkrabea yɛɛ mmɔbɔ araa ma) bere biara a ɔbɛwo ba no, na ɔba no wu. Enti ɔkɔɔ abosom no mu baako nkyɛn kɔbisaa n'asɛm, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔpɛ ba. Abosom no kaa sɛ, "Mɛma wo baako, nanso abofra no deɛ, adwuma biara a ɔbɛyɛ da biara ne sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhyɛ wo ka mu, nanso ɛmfa ho hwee, da bi bɛba a ɔbɛtua ama wo." Ankyɛ, nna mmienu anaa nna mmiɛnsa pɛ (a ɔkɔbisaa abosom no akyi) na ɔbaa no nyinsɛnee. Ɔwoeɛ—na ɔyɛ anansesɛm mu ba—ɛfiri sɛ ankyɛ koraa na ɔnyinii. Abofra no nyinii bɛyɛɛ aberanteɛ kɛseɛ a ne ho yɛ fɛ. Da bi na ɔne ne maame te hɔ, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Maame, ma me sika futuro, na me nkɔ Po-ano-man mu, na me nkɔtɔ nkyene." Ɛnna maame no bisaa no sɛ, "Sɛn?" Ɔkae sɛ, "Asuanu" (kyerɛ sɛ £4). Na maame no fa maa no, na ɔtuu kwan kɔe. Afei, berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa ɔbarima bi ne ne kraman a ne ho gye nsensanee. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa bra ma mentɔ." Kraman no wura kaa sɛ, "Wontumi ntɔ." Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ne boɔ yɛ ahe?" Kraman no wura kaa sɛ, "Asuanu sika dɔteɛ" (kyerɛ sɛ £4). Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛno deɛ ɛyɛ hwee (mma me), gye saa £4 sika dɔteɛ yi." Ɔgyee kraman no, na ɔde no baa fie. Bere a ɔsan baeɛ no, ne maame kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woamfiri hɔ ankorɔ faako a na worekɔ no?" Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Mede sika futuro no tɔɔ kraman." Ne maame kaa sɛ, "Ho!" Afei wɔtenaa hɔ bɛyɛ sɛ bosome baako pɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛna, ma me sika futuro na menkɔyɛ dwa." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo deɛ, sɛdeɛ ɛteɛ ara daa no, wobɛfa sika futuro no akɔto agu bio ara kwa, nanso dodoɔ sɛn na wopɛ?" Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Sika futuro Asuanu ne suru boɔ" (i. e. £5). Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ gye." Enti ɔde kwan no so sii so kɔɔ dwa-kwan no so. Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa ɔbarima bi a ɔkura agyinamoa. Ɔse, "Barima, fa aboa a ɔhwe ase a ne nan si fam daa no bra, ma mentɔ." Ɔno nso se, "Sɛ meda me dan mu a, nkura we me nan, ɛno nti na metɔe." Ɔse, "Mesrɛ wo ma menya bi." Ɔse, "Worentumi ntɔ." Abarimaa no se, "Sika ahe na wobɛgye wɔ ho?" Ɔbuae se, "Asuanu ne suru sika futuro." Ɔse, "Ɛno nti na wose merentumi ntɔ no; fa eyi." Abofra no gyee agyinamoa no, na ɔde no kɔɔ fie. Ɔduruu fie no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Maame, hwɛ nea mede aba ha." Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Aane, nea wɔkae (sɛ ɛbɛsi) pɛpɛɛpɛ nen." Abofra no tenaa hɔ (wɔ fie). Ɛbɛyɛ nnafua aduanan akyi no, ɔkasa kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Ma me sika futuro na menkɔyɛ batadie." Maame no kaa sɛ, "Sika a mewɔ no nyinaa asa, gye Asuasa sika futuro (kyerɛ sɛ £6); sɛ mede eyi ma wo, na wokɔ, na woamfa antɔ nneɛma a, na asɛm yi to atwa." Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Mate." Adekyee no, berɛ a adeɛ bɛfee no, ɔfaa ne bɔtɔ, na ɔsiimu kɔe, pa! Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa Asanteniba bi, na ɔkura aborɔnoma. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Adamfo, fa w’aboa no bra na mentɔ." Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Merentɔn, efisɛ mede gye m'ani." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mɛtɔ ara." Anomaa no wura kaa sɛ, "Merentɔn, efisɛ menim deɛ ɔbɛtumi ayɛ ama me." Akwadaa no kaa sɛ, "O, fa ma me." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wobɛtumi atɔ?" Akwadaa no kaa sɛ, "Sɛn?" Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Sika futuro Asuasa dodoɔ" (i.e. £6). Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wosusu sɛ ɛno nti merentɔ, sika no ni." Ɔde anomaa no baa fie. Ne maame kaa sɛ, "Yei anyɛ yie nsen kan no, enti yei na wode aba?" Ɔbuae sɛ, "Ɛmfa ho hwee, yei na mede aba." Afei (da koro bi) na abɔfra no te hɔ (fie), ɛnna Aburuburo no frɛɛ no sɛ, "Bra." Bere a ɔkɔɔ ne nkyɛn no, Aburuburo no bɔɔ no amanneɛ sɛ, "Me kuro mu no meyɛ ɔhene, na na mereka sɛ mereka akwantu bi, na obarima bi b baa bɛfaa me, berɛ a wo, nam w'ayamye so, wo nso tɔɔ me, na afei mesrɛ wo, sɛ wobɛtumi de me akɔ me kurom pɛ a, ɛnde wɔbɛda wo ase kɛse." Abɔfra no kaa sɛ, "Woresisi me, wobɛdwane." Aborɔnoma no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wuntumi nyɛ deɛ merebisa no a, ɛnde fa ahoma, na fa kyekyere me nan, na fa me kɔ." Abɔfra no faa ahoma de kyekyeree ɛde guu ne nan so, na ɔdii n'akyi brɛoo, kɔsii sɛ wɔduruu Aburuburo kuro mu. Berɛ a wɔduruu kuro no ano no, na mmofra no regoro nte. Berɛ a wɔhunuu no no, wɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔhene no nie! "Ɔhene no ni!" Wɔn mu baako tuu mmirika kɔbɔɔ Korentihene amanneɛ, nanso wɔkyeree no, na wɔtwaa ne mene, kaa sɛ, "Woma yɛkae yɛn awerɛhoɔ a etwaam no." Nanso foforɔ nso kɔe de saa amanneɛbɔ korɔ no ara kɔe. Afei Akwamuhene kaa sɛ, "Wo, Gyasehene, wo ara kɔ na kɔhwɛ deɛ ɛrekɔ soɔ no." Ɔkɔe kɔhwɛe, na ɔsane bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "O, ɛyɛ nokware." Ɛnna wɔkɔfaa apakan ne ahemfo nneɛma de kɔfaa Ɔhene no baa ne fie. Wɔbɔɔ abusuakuo no nyinaa amanneɛ, sɛdeɛ ɔtuu kwan, ne sɛdeɛ obi kyee no, ne sɛdeɛ abofra yi, nam ne yamyeɛ so tɔɔ no, ne sɛdeɛ ɛnnɛ wde no aba fie. Mpanyimfoɔ ne mmeranteɛ nyinaa sɔreeɛ na wɔdaa abofra no ase. Ɔhemaa no de ahina a sika futuro ahyɛ no ma baeɛ; mpanimfoɔ no nyinaa nso, obiara de ahina a sika futuro ahyɛ no ma baeɛ. Ɔhene no ankasa hwɛɛ ne nsa, na ɔworɔɔ kawa bi firii ne nsa, de maa no. Na ɔkaa sɛ, "Gye saa kawa yi, na biribiara a wopɛ no, saa kawa yi bɛma wo." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mate." Na ɔne kawa no kɔɔ ne kuro mu; na ɔde sika futuro no ne kawa no kyerɛɛ ne maame. Ɛnna maame no kae sɛ, "Akwaaba Aku, akwaaba Aku." Kane no, berɛ a na wahyehyɛ ne sikakɔkɔɔ mfuturo no, na sɛ ɔfiri n'akwantuo mu ba a, sɛ ɔkyea ne maame a, na ɔnnye no so. Na ɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Woahunu saa sikakɔkɔɔ mfuturo yi ne saa kawa yi; mɛkɔ akɔsi kuro kɛseɛ ama yɛatena mu." Maame no kae sɛ, "Mia w'ani (kyerɛ sɛ bɔ mmɔden sɛ wobɛyɛ saa)." Abofra no tuu kwan kɔgyinaa kwaeɛ no mu; ɔworɔɔ kawa no guu fam; ɔkaa sɛ, "Kawa, pow kwaeɛ ne wiram yi nyinaa ma me." Na ɛhɔ nyinaa bɛyɛɛ sɔnn. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Boa deɛ woapow nyinaa ano gu afaafa ma yɛnhye." Na ɛyɛɛ saa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sisi adan." Na ɛsisii adan bebree. Ɔkae sɛ, "Kawa, ma nkurɔfoɔ mmɛtena afie yi mu." Na nkurɔfoɔ bae. Ɔyɛɛ ne maame Ɔhemaa; ɔno nso bɛyɛɛ ɔhene. Afei (kyerɛ sɛ, na biribi wɔ hɔ a me werɛ firii sɛ mɛka), Ananse na na ɔyɛ neadamfo pa. Da koro bi na ɔte hɔ (wɔ n'akuraa foforɔ no mu) na Kwaku Ananse sii mu sɛ ɔreba aberanteɛ yi kuro mu. (Berɛ a ɔduruu hɔ no,) ɔkaa sɛ, "O, me nua kumaa, me papa ba kumaa, woanya nhyira ne nkunimdi, na wo mfa me ho hwee bio sɛ wobɛhwɛ me, nanso ɛdeɛn na asie a ɛde yei nyinaa baeɛ?" Ɛnna abofra no kaa asɛm no nyinaa kyerɛɛ no. Ananse kaa sɛ, "Mɛkɔ me kuro mu (akɔfa biribi) aba." Ananse kɔɔ ne kuro mu; ɔka kyerɛɛ ne wɔfase ba baa no sɛ, "Mɛsoma wo akɔ m'adamfo a ɔwɔ hɔ no nkyɛn, na sɛ wokɔ a, fa nsafufuo yi kɔma no na yɛ aso yɛ biribiara a ɔbɛhyɛ wo; na bɔ mmɔden fa kokoam nya saa kawa no." Abaayewa no tuu kwan, kɔɔ ɔbarima no kuro mu. Ɔbarima no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛyɛ eyi deɛ, mɛhwɛ so na woansan woakyi bio, efisɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wo ne me tena nna mmiɛnsa ansa na woasane." Abaayewa no kaa sɛ, "Mate." Afei ɔne abaayewa no tenaa hɔ bom, na ɔbarima no kɔdwareeɛ; na ɔworɔɔ kawa no de too ɛpono so; na abaayewa no faeɛ, na ɔde kɔmaa ne wɔfa, Kwaku Ananse. Ntɛm ara a Ananse nsa kaa no no, ɔde (kawa no) sii kuropɔn kɛseɛ bi. Afei, ɔbarima no baeɛ sɛ ɔrebɛhwehwɛ ne kawa no, wantumi anhunu. Ɔbɛtee sɛ Ananse asi kuropɔn kɛseɛ a ɛso sene ne deɛ no. Ɛnna ɔkɔeɛ sɛ ɔrekɔbisa abosom nkumaa a wɔwɔ nkyɛn no baako hɔ afutuo. Abosom no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ananse wofase baa a ɔbaa wo nkyɛn hɔ no, wafa de akɔma ne wɔfa." Ananse nso kɔbisaa abosom hɔ afotu na wɔdaa no adi kyerɛɛ no sɛ wɔbɛsoma Okra, Agyinamoa no, ne Okraman, Kraman no, sɛ wɔnkɔgye kawa no mbra, enti ɔkɔfaa aduro a ɔde bɛfra nam mu de ato ɔkwan no so, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mmoa no retwa mu rekɔ no, sɛ wɔfa bi di a, wɔrentumi nkɔ baabiara biom koraa. Ɔbarima no (a na ne kawa ayera no) baa fie, na ɔka kyerɛɛ Agyinamoa no ne Kraman no sɛ, "Mmerɛ no aduru seesei sɛ mereka deɛ enti a metɔɔ mo no akyerɛ mo, (na ɛne sɛ); me adeɛ bi ayera, na wɔse ɛwɔ Ananse nsam, nanso ɛda adaka a ɛwɔ nea aka no nyinaa mfinimfini mu. Bio nso, wɔse wafa aduro afra odwan nam no mu de ato kwan no so, enti sɛ moduru hɔ a, monnhwehwɛ nso monturi ntra." Kraman no kaa sɛ, "Agyinamoa, woate, wo na wobɔweɛ." Agyinamoa no kaa sɛ, "Firi hɔ kɔ, wo a kanea biara wode wo hwene hwehwehwɛ fam no." Ɛnna wɔfirii aseɛ kasaeɛ pii, na wɔn wura kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ, monyɛ kɔ." Mmoa mmienu no sii mu, na wɔrekɔ. Afei na aka kakraabi pɛ na wɔaduru faako a nam no wɔ no, na Kraman tee nam no hwa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agyinamoa, me yam reka me, na mentumi nkɔ bio." Agyinamoa no kaa sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔ, adwuma a ɛda yɛn anim yi ho hia." (Kraman no) kaa sɛ, "Agyinamoa, mentumi." Agyinamoa no toaa so kɔe. Kraman kɔɔ faako a na nam no gu no; ɔweee nam no nyinaa; ɔdaa hɔ; wantumi ankɔ n'anim bio. Na Agyinamoa nso duu Ananse akuraa, na ɔkɔdaa Ananse dabere, wɔ adasawa no so (dan no atifi). Bere a ɔda hɔ no, ɔhunuu akura bi sɛ ɔretwam. Kwi! bere a ɔbɔɔ ne ti so pɛ. (Akura no) kaa sɛ, "Mfa me, asɛm bɛn na asi?" Agyinamoa no buaa sɛ, "Me wura kawa ayera, na wɔse ɛda Ananse adaka a ɛsi ne nnaka a aka no nyinaa mfinimfini no mu, na sɛ wobɛtumi akɔ akɔfa abrɛ me a, ɛnde mɛma woakɔ." Akura no kaa sɛ, "Mɛtumi." Agyinamoa no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ megyaa wo na wokɔ a na woamfa ammɛma me, na mmom wodwanee nso ɛ?" Akura no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wopɛ a, kyekyere ahoma bɔ me sena." Agyinamoa no faa ahoma de kyekyeree ne sena. Ɛnna Akura no kɔɔ Ananse dan mu, na ɔkekaa adaka a ɛsi mfinimfini koraa no mu tokuro. Nkakrankakra ɔyɛɛ tokuro, na ɛbɛyɛɛ kɛse. Ɔfaa mu, sɛ ɔrekɔfa kawa no de abrɛ Agyinamoa no. Ɛnkyɛree koraa na Agyinamoa no de ne nsa kaa so pɛ, na ɔdwanee, kɔhyiaa Kraman no, na ɔda faako pɛpɛɛpɛ a ɔgyaa no hɔ ma ɔdae no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Woda so ara da ha, na nam no wɔ he?" Kraman no kaa sɛ, "O, manhu (deɛ ɛyɛɛ no), ebia nafoɔ no na wɔbɛfaa kɔeɛ; nanso kawa no wɔ he?" Agyinamoa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛnie." Kraman no kaa sɛ, "Wɔse asubɔnten a ɛda kwan no so no ayiri, na wo deɛ, sɛ woretwa nsuo a, wonante aseɛ nti, ebia na (kawa no) bɛhwe ase, enti fa ma me, efisɛ me deɛ, wonim sɛ menam nsuo no ani." Agyinamoa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛte saa, gye." Wɔduruu asubɔnten no ho, na Ɔkraman no hurui guu mu. Agyinamoa no nso yɛɛ saa ara. Ɛhɔ ara na Agyinamoa no twae kɔɔ faako no. Ɔkraman no nso duruu asubɔnten no mfimfini na ɔbrɛe, na berɛ a ɔrebɔ home kɛseɛ no, kawa no firii n'anom tɔɔ nsuo no mu. Ɔtwam kɔɔ baabi a Agyinamoa no wɔ no. Agyinamoa no bisae sɛ, "Ɛhe na kɔaa no wɔ?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛfii m'anom tɔɔ nsuo no mu." Agyinamoa no tuu mmirika kɔwuraa nsuo no mu; ɔhunuu nam kɛseɛ bi sɛ ɔretwam. Na Agyinamoa no kyee no. Nam no bisae sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na asie?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me kɔaa no ara pɛ na atɔ asubɔnten yi mu, enti gye sɛ wopɛ asɛm, fa deɛ ɛyɛ me dea no ma me seesei ara. Sɛ woamfa amma me a, mɛku wo seesei ara." Nsunam no kaa sɛ, "Ma yɛnkɔ asubɔnten no ano na memfa nea ɛyɛ wo deɛ no mma wo." Berɛ a wɔn baanu nyinaa duruu asubɔnten no ano no, Nsunam no fee, na kawa no firii mu bae. Agyinamoa no fae na ɔbɛkyerɛɛ Kraman no. Kraman no kaa sɛ, "Agya, mesrɛ wo, sɛ wokɔ a, mma nka nea asi yi ho asɛm." Agyinamoa no muaa n'ano. Wɔduruu fie, na Agyinamoa no ka kyerɛɛ ne wura nea esii nyinaa, sɛ, "Ɛnam nam a Ɔkraman no weei berɛ a yɛrekɔ no nti, wantumi ankɔ akyiri bio. Bio nso, mekɔfaa kawa no baeɛ no, Ɔkraman no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, berɛ a ɔretwa asubɔnten no ɔfaa ɛso, enti ɛsɛ sɛ mede kawa no ma no. Mede maa no, na ɔto twene kɔɔ asubɔnten no mu. Ɛbaa saa berɛ kakra akyi ansa na mede me nsa too so bio." Nnipa a na wɔwɔ hɔ no nyinaa kaa sɛ, "Ayeyie nka Agyinamoa no." Na wɔteɛɛm sɛ, "E!" Afei ɔhene no kaa sɛ, "Wo, Agyinamoa, aduane biara a mereweɛ no, mɛhwɛ sɛ mɛbubu bi de agu w’asanka ketewa no mu. Kɛtɛ biara a mɛda so no, (mɛda so) gye sɛ wo nso woada bi so. Wo nso, Kraman, sɛ anadwo nwunu no ba a, gya a adum no gyabiriw so nko ara na wobɛda. Nnipa no bɛboroboro wo mmoroso nko ara." Ɛno nti na da biara wubehu sɛ Agyinamoa nna baabiara gye sɛ kɛtɛ pa so. Afei nso, sɛ wugu aduan bi fam ma no a, ɔrenni (gye sɛ ɛda mprɛte so). Nanso kraman deɛ, bere biara yɛbɛhunu sɛ ɔda adiwo hɔ wɔ da no gya nso a adum no mu. Afei nso wobɛhunu sɛ wɔrebɔ no wɔ hɔ, na ɔbɛteɛm sɛ, "Kao!" Eyi nyinaa fi bere a wɔsomaa wɔn (wɔ kawa no ho asɛm yi mu) no.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, ebi nkɔ na ebi mmra.
