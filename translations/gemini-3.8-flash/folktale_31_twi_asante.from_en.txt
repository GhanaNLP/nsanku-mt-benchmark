Yɛnka nsi pi, yɛnka nsi pi (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka yi yɛ nokware)

SƐDEA ƐBƆƆ YƐE A ƐMA YƐBƐHU OKRA BƆBERƐ MA ƆDA VELVET KUSIN SO DAABAA, BERƐ A ƆKRAMAN DEƐ, ƆBƐDA GYA ADEFOƆ A ƐWƆ MUKASE GYA HO NO MU

Wɔse tete no, na ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ a, bere biara a ɔbɛwo ba no, na ɔwuo. Enti ɔtu kɔbisaa abosom no mu baako asɛm no ho asɛm, kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔpɛ ba. Ɔbosom no kae sɛ, "Mɛma wo baako, nanso abofra no deɛ, adwuma a ɔbɛyɛ nyinaa ne sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔbɔ wo ka, nanso da bi ɔbɛtua ama wo." Ankyɛ koraa, ankyɛ nna mmienu, ankyɛ nna mmiɛnsa (bere a ɔbisaa ɔbosom no akyi) na ɔbaa no nyinsɛnee. Ɔwoe—na ɛyɛ anansesɛm mu ba—ɛfiri sɛ ankyɛ koraa na ɔnyinii. Abofra no nyin bɛyɛɛ abarimaa fɛfɛɛfɛ bi. Da koro bi na ɔwɔ hɔ (ne ne maame), na ɔkae sɛ, "Maame, ma me sika futuro, na menkɔ Mpoano-man mu nkɔtɔ nkyene." Ɛna no bisaa sɛ, "Sɛn?" Ɔkae sɛ, "Asuanu" (kyerɛ sɛ £4). Na ɛna no fa maa no, na ɔtuu kwan no. Afei, bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa ɔbarima bi ne ne kraman a ne ho ayɛ nsensanee. Ɔkae sɛ, "Fa bra na mentɔ." Kraman no wura kae sɛ, "Wontumi ntɔ." Abofra no bisaa sɛ, "Ne boɔ yɛ sɛn?" Kraman no wura kae sɛ, "Sika futuro asuanu" (kyerɛ sɛ £4). Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛno deɛ ɛyɛ deɛn (ma me), gye sika futuro £4 yi." Ɔgyee kraman no, na ɔde no sane kɔɔ fie. Bere a ɔbaa fie no, ne maame kae sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woankɔ baabi a worekɔ no?" Ɔbuae sɛ, "Mede sika futuro no kɔtɔɔ kraman." Ne maame kae sɛ, "Oo!" Afei wɔtenaa hɔ, ɛbɛyɛ sɛ abosome baako. Ɔkae sɛ, "Maame, ma me sika futuro na menkɔ aguadi." Ɔkae sɛ, "Wo deɛ, sɛdeɛ woyɛ daa no, wobɛfa sika futuro no akɔto agu bio, nanso ahe na wopɛ?" Ɔbuae sɛ, "Sika futuro asuanu ne suru" (kyerɛ sɛ £5). Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛnde gye." Enti ɔfaa aguadifoɔ kwan no so kɔe. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa ɔbarima bi a ɔkura agyinamoa. Ɔkae sɛ, "Agya, fa aboa a ɔhwe ase a ɔde ne nan sisi fam daa no bra na mentɔ." Ɔkae sɛ, "Sɛ meda me dan mu a, nkura we me nan, ɛno nti na metɔe." Ɔkae sɛ, "Mepa wo kyɛw ma me mentɔ." Ɔkae sɛ, "Wontumi ntɔ." Abofra no bisaa sɛ, "Wobɛgye sɛn wɔ ho?" Ɔbuae sɛ, "Sika futuro asuanu ne suru." Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛno nti na wose, mentumi ntɔ no; gye yi." Abofra no gyee agyinamoa no, na ɔde no kɔɔ fie. Bere a ɔduruu fie no, ɔkae sɛ, "Maame, hwɛ deɛ mede aba nie." Ɔbuae sɛ, "Aane, pɛpɛɛpɛ deɛ wɔkae sɛ (ɛbɛsi) no ara pɛ nie." Abofra no tenaa hɔ (wɔ fie). Ɛbɛyɛ sɛ nna aduanan akyi no, ɔkasa kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Ma me sikakɔkɔɔ sika na menkɔtɔ aguadi." Ɛna no kaa sɛ, "Sika a mewɔ no nyinaa asa, gye Asuasa sika (i. e. £6); sɛ mede eyi ma wo, na wokɔ, na wamfa antɔ nneɛma a, afei deɛ asɛm no aba awieeɛ." Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Mate." Adeɛ kyeeɛ no, ɔfaa ne bɔtɔ, na ɔtuu kwan, pa! Ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa Asanteni bi, na ɔkura aborɔnoma. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Adamfo, fa w’aboa no brɛ me na mentɔ." Ɔno nso buaa sɛ, "Merentɔn, efisɛ mede gye m’ani." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mɛtɔ." Aboa no wura kaa sɛ, "Merentɔn, efisɛ menim deɛ ɔbɛtumi ayɛ ama me." Abofra no kaa sɛ, "O, fa ma me." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wobɛtumi atɔ?" Abofra no bisaa sɛ, "Sɛn?" Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Asuasa sika" (i.e. £6). Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wosusu sɛ ɛno nti merentɔ, sika no nie." Ɔde anomaa no baa fie. Ne maame kaa sɛ, "Eyi nso anyɛ yie te sɛ kane no ara, enti deɛ wode aba nie?" Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Ɛmfa ho hwee, deɛ mede aba nie." Afei (da koro bi) abofra no te hɔ (fie), na Aborɔnoma no frɛɛ no sɛ, "Bra." Ɔkɔɔ ne nkyɛn no, Aborɔnoma no bɔɔ no amanneɛ sɛ, "Me kuro mu no meyɛ ɔhene, na mereka sɛ merekɔ akwantu bi, na obi bɛkyeree me, na wo nso, wo papayɛ nti, wotɔɔ me, na afei mesrɛ wo, sɛ wobɛtumi de me akɔ me kuro mu ara a, wɔbɛda wo ase paa." Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Worededaadaa me, wobɛdwane." Aborɔnoma no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wunnye nni a, fa ahoma bɔ me nan na fa me kɔ." Abofra no faa ahoma de kyekyeree ne nan, na ɔdii n'akyi bɔkɔɔ, kɔsii sɛ wɔduruu Aborɔnoma no kuro mu. Wɔduruu kuro no ano no, na mmofra regoro nte. Wɔhunuu no no, wɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔhene no nie! Ɔhene no nie!" Wɔn mu baako tuu mmirika kɔbɔɔ Korentihene amanneɛ, nanso wɔkyeree no, na wɔtwaa ne mene, kaa sɛ, "Worema yɛakae yɛn awerɛhoɔ a ɛtwaam no." Nanso foforɔ nso kɔɔ bio kɔkaa asɛm koro no ara. Afei Akwamuhene kaa sɛ, "Wo, Gyasehene, wo ara kɔhwɛ deɛ asɛm yi kyerɛ." Ɔkɔhwɛeɛ, na ɔsane bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "O, ɛyɛ nokware." Ɛnna wɔkɔfaa apakan ne ahenkyɛm ne nneɛma a wɔde kɔfa Ɔhene baa ne fie. Wɔbɔɔ abusuakuo no nyinaa amanneɛ, sɛdeɛ na ɔretu kwan, ne sɛdeɛ obi kyeree no, ne sɛdeɛ saa abofra yi, nam n'ayamyeɛ so tɔɔ no, ne sɛdeɛ ɛnnɛ wde no aba fie. Mpanyimfoɔ ne mmabun nyinaa sɔre bɔɔ abofra no aba so. Ɔhemmaa no de ahina a sika futuro ahyɛ no ma bae; mpanimfoɔ no nyinaa nso, obiara de ahina a sika futuro ahyɛ no ma bae. Ɔhene no ara nso hwɛɛ ne nsa, na ɔworɔɔ kawa bi, na ɔde maa no. Na ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa saa kawa yi, na biribiara a wobɛpɛ no, saa kawa yi bɛma wo." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mate." Na ɔde kawa no kɔɔ ne kuro mma; na ɔde sika futuro no ne kawa no kyerɛɛ ne maame. Ɛnna ne maame no kaa sɛ, "Akwaaba Aku, akwaaba Aku." Kane no, berɛ a na "wahye" ne sika futuro no, na sɛ ɔfiri n'akwantuo mu ba a, sɛ ɔkyia ne maame a, na ɔnnye no so. Na ɔbɔɔ ne maame amanneɛ sɛ, "Woahunu saa sika futuro yi ne saa kawa yi; mereka akɔsi kuro kɛseɛ bi ama yɛatena mu." Ɛnna ne maame kaa sɛ, "Mia w'ani (kyerɛ sɛ bɔ wo ho mmɔden yɛ saa)." Abofra no tuu kwan kɔgyinaa kwaeɛ no mu; ɔworɔɔ kawa no na ɔde too fam; ɔkaa sɛ, "Kawa, siesie saa kwaeɛ ne wiram ha nyinaa ma me." Na ɛhɔ nyinaa bɛyɛɛ sɔnn. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Boa deɛ woasiesie no nyinaa ano gu afaafa ma yɛnhye." Na ɛyɛɛ saa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Si afie." Na ɛsii afie pii. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Kawa, ma nkurɔfoɔ mmra mmɛtena saa afie yi mu." Na nkurɔfoɔ bae. Ɔyɛɛ ne maame Ɔhemmaa; ɔno nso bɛyɛɛ ɔhene. Afei (kyerɛ sɛ na biribi wɔ hɔ a me werɛ afi sɛ mɛka), Ananse na na ɔyɛ n'adamfo kɛseɛ pa ara. Da koro bi na ɔte hɔ (wɔ ne kuro foforɔ no mu) berɛ a Kwaku Ananse tuu kwan sɛ ɔreba saa aberanteɛ yi kuro mu. (Berɛ a ɔduruu hɔ no,) ɔkaa sɛ, "O, maame ba kumaa, papa ba kumaa, woanya adeɛ na w'ani agye, na wo mpo wonsusu me ho bio sɛ wobɛhwɛ me, nanso ɛdeɛn na esii a ɛmaa wei nyinaa bae?" Ɛnna abofra no bɔɔ no amanneɛ no nyinaa. Ananse see, "Mɛkɔ m'akuraa (akɔfa biribi) aba." Ananse kɔɔ n'akuraa; ɔka kyerɛɛ ne wɔfase baa see, "Mɛsoma wo akɔ me nnamfo nkyɛn wɔ hɔ, na sɛ wokɔ a, fa nsafufuo yi kɔma no na yɛ aso yɛ biribiara a ɔbɛhyɛ wo; na bɔ mmɔden sɛ fa sumii bɛnya saa kawa no." Abaayewa no tuu kwan, kɔɔ ɔbarima no akuraa. Ɔbarima no see no, "Ɛnna yei deɛ, mɛhwɛ ama woansan woakyi bio, efisɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wo ne me tena nna mmiɛnsa ansa na woasan akɔ." Abaayewa no see, "Mate." Afei ɔne abaayewa no tenaa hɔ bomu, na ɔbarima no kɔguaree; na ɔworɔɔ kawa no de too ɛpono so; na abaayewa no fae, na ɔde kɔmaa ne wɔfa, Kwaku Ananse. Ɛberɛ a Ananse nyaa kawa no ara pɛ, ɔde yɛɛ adwuma de kyerɛɛ kuro kɛseɛ. Afei, ɔbarima no, ɛberɛ a ɔbɛhwehwɛɛ ne kawa no, wannya. Ɔbɛtee sɛ Ananse akyekyere kuro kɛseɛ a ɛso sene ne deɛ mpo. Afei ɔkɔbisaa abosom no baako wɔ hɔ. Ɔbosom no ka kyerɛɛ no see, "Ananse wɔfase baa a ɔbaa wo nkyɛn no, na ɔafa de akɔma ne wɔfa. Ananse nso kɔbisaa (ɔbosom no na wɔdaa no adi kyerɛɛ no) sɛ Okra ne Ɔkraman na wɔbɛsoma wɔn (akɔfa kawa no aba), enti ɔkɔfaa aduro de fraa nam mu, de too kwan no so, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a sɛ mmoa no nam kwan so rekɔ na sɛ wɔfa bi di a, wɔrentumi nkɔ baabiara bio koraa. Ɔbarima (a ne kawa ayera no) baa fie, na ɔka kyerɛɛ Okra ne Ɔkraman see, "Ɛberɛ aduru a ɛsɛ sɛ meka adeɛ a enti a metɔɔ mo kyerɛ mo, (na ɛne yei); me biribi ayera, na wɔse ɛwɔ Ananse nkyɛn, nanso ɛda adaka bi a ɛwɔ mfimfini koraa mu. Na afei nso, wɔse ɔde aduro afra odwan nam mu de ato kwan no so, enti sɛ moduru hɔ a, monhwɛ na monamfa na mmom mohuruw tra." Ɔkraman see, "Okra, woate, wo na wobɛwe." Okra see, "Firi hɔ kɔ, wo a kwan tiaa biara wode wo hwene hwehwehwɛ biribiara mu." Afei wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kasaa beberee, na wɔn wura see, "Ɛyɛ, monkɔ." Mmoa mmienu no tuu kwan, na wɔrekɔ. Afei aka kakraabi pɛ na wɔaduru baabi a nam no da no, ɛnna Ɔkraman "tee" nam no hua. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agyinamoa, me yafunu reyɛ me ya, na mentumi nkɔ anim." Agyinamoa no kaa sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔ anim, adwuma a ɛda yɛn anim yi ho hia." (Kraman no) kaa sɛ, "Agyinamoa, mentumi." Agyinamoa no toaa so kɔe. Kraman no kɔɔ faako a ɛnam no wɔe; ɔweem nam no nyinaa; ɔkɔdaa hɔ; wantumi ankɔ anim bio. Na Agyinamoa no duruu Ananse akuraa, na ɔkɔdaa Ananse dabere, wɔ dan atifi (wɔ dan no atifi). Bere a ɔda hɔ no, ɔhunuu akura bi sɛ ɔretwa mu kɔ. Krikri! bere a ɔtoo n'ani kɔɔ ne ti so. (Akura no) kaa sɛ, "Nkyere me, asɛm bɛn na asie?" Agyinamoa no buaa sɛ, "Me Wura kawa ayera, na wɔse ɛda Ananse adaka a ɛsi mfinimfini (wɔ n'adaka a aka nyinaa mu) no mu, na sɛ wubetumi akɔ akɔfa abrɛ me a, ɛnde mɛma woakɔ." Akura no kaa sɛ, "Metumi." Agyinamoa no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ mema wokɔ, na wokɔ na wamfa amma, na mmom woguan nso ɛ?" Akura no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wopɛ a, sɔ ahoma bɔ me sena mu." Agyinamoa no faa ahoma de kyekyeree ne sena mu. Ɛnna Akura no kɔɔ Ananse dan mu, na ɔkɔkekaa tokuro wɔ adaka a ɛsi mfinimfini wɔ nkae no nyinaa mu no ho. Nkakra nkakra ɔyɛɛ tokuro, na ɛbɛyɛɛ kɛse. Ɔfaa mu, sɛ ɔrekɔfa kawa no de abrɛ Agyinamoa no. Agyinamoa no nsa kaa kawa no pɛ, na ɔtetee mmirika kɔe, na ɔbɛtoo Kraman no, na ɔda faako a ɔgyaw no hɔ ma ɔdae no ara. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Woda hɔ ara, na nam no wɔ hefa?" Kraman no kaa sɛ, "O, manhu (deɛ ɛyɛɛ no), ebia na nnipa a wɔwɔ no bɛfaa kɔe; nanso kawa no wɔ he?" Agyinamoa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛnie." Kraman no kaa sɛ, "Wɔse asubɔnten a ɛda kwan no mu no ayiri, na wo deɛ, sɛ worefa nsuo mu a, wonante aseɛ pɔtee, ebia ɛbɛtɔ fam, enti fa ma me, efisɛ, me deɛ, wonim sɛ, metena nsuo no ani na metwa." Agyinamoa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛte saa, gye." Wɔduruu asubɔnten no ho, na Kraman no huri kɔɔ mu. Agyinamoa no nso kɔɔ bi. Amonom hɔ ara na Agyinamoa no twaa kɔɔ agya. Kraman no nso duruu asuonwini no mfinimfini na ɔberɛe, na bere a ɔrepɛ ahome kɛse no, kawa no firi (n'anom) tɔɔ nsuo no mu. Ɔtwaa nsuo no baa faako a Agyinamoa no wɔe. Agyinamoa no kaa sɛ, "Kawa no wɔ hefa?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛfiri (m'anom) tɔɔ nsuo no mu." Agyinamoa no tuu mmirika kɔwuraa nsuo no mu; ɔhunuu mpataa kɛse bi sɛ ɔretwa mu kɔ. Na Agyinamoa no kyeree no. Mpataa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na asie?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me kawa atɔ asubɔnten yi mu seesei ara, enti gye sɛ wopɛ asɛm, fa deɛ ɛyɛ me dea no ma me ntɛm ara. Sɛ woamfa amma me a, mereka ara meku wo seesei ara." Nam no kae sɛ, "Ma yɛnkɔ asubɔnten no ano na memfa nea ɛyɛ wo de mma wo." Berɛ a wɔn baanu nyinaa duruu mpoano hɔ no, Nam no feee, na kawa no firii mu bae. Agyinamoa no fae na ɔde ba bɛkyerɛɛ Kraman no. Kraman no kae sɛ, "Agya, mepa wo kyɛw, sɛ wokɔ a, mma nnka nea asi yi ho asɛm." Agyinamoa no muum n'ano. Wɔduruu fie, na Agyinamoa no kaa nea esii nyinaa kyerɛɛ ne wura sɛ, "Ɛnam nam a Kraman no wee berɛ a yɛrekɔ no nti, wantumi ankɔ n'anim bio. Afei nso, mekɔfaa kawa no bae no, Kraman no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, berɛ a ɔretwa asubɔnten no ɔnam nsuo no ani, enti ɛsɛ sɛ mede kawa no hyɛ ne nsa. Mede maa no, na ɔto twene kɔɔ asubɔnten no mu. Ɛyɛɛ kakra ansa na me nsa retumi aka bio." Nnipa a wɔwɔ hɔ nyinaa kae sɛ, "Nkamfoɔ nka Agyinamoa." Na wɔteam sɛ, "E!" Afei ɔhene no kae sɛ, "Wo, Agyinamoa, aduane biara a merewe no, mɛhwɛ sɛ mɛbubu bi de ahyɛ wo kyɛnsee ketewa no mu. Kɛtɛ biara a mɛda so no, (mɛda so ara) gye sɛ woada ne fa bi so. Wo nso, Kraman, sɛ anadwo nwunu ba a, nsramma a anya adum no so nko ara na wobɛda. Na mmorɔso nko ara, nkurɔfoɔ no bɛbɔ wo mmaa." Ɛno nti na da biara wobɛhunu Agyinamoa no, ɔnna baabiara gye sɛ kɛtɛ pa so. Afei nso, sɛ woto aduane bi twene fam ma no a, ɔrenwe (gye sɛ ɛda prɛte so). Nanso Kraman deɛ, daa yɛbɛhunu no sɛ ɔda adiwo wɔ nso a adum a ɛfiri da no gya mu no so. Afei nso wobɛhunu no hɔ sɛ wɔrebɔ no, na ɔbɛsu sɛ, "Kao!" Ɛnyinaa firi berɛ a wɔsomaa wɔn (kawa yi ho asɛm no nti).

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, ebi nkɔ na ebi mmra.
