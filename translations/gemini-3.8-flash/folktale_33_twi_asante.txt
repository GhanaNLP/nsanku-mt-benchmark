A certain child called Abena was given into marriage by her family. She set off to her husband's hometown and forgot her family; she did not want to come to her hometown, for someone's hometown had become pleasant to her. While she was there, her mother died; again her father died; again her grandparent also died; still, she did not come. It had been about ten years, and she had not come. Then one day, while Abena was there, a certain bird came and perched on the building and cried, saying:

Abena, her mother is dead, her father is dead, her grandparent is dead!

Guns fire bang! boom! bang! boom! bang! boom!

The drum sounds boom! boom! sounds boom! boom! sounds boom! boom!

Don't cry, yo! yo! cry, yo! yo! cry, yo! yo!

Abena's mother is dead, her father is dead, her grandparent is dead!

Abena said, "What is the matter?" Not long after, her husband arrived; she told him what had happened; and the bird cried again. The husband said, "I did not hear it well, so I am going to call Adanko." He went and called him to come; and the bird began again:

Abena, her mother is dead, her father is dead, her grandparent is dead!

Guns fire bang! boom! bang! boom! bang! boom!

The drum sounds kun! kun! sounds kun! kun! sounds kun! kun! Women are crying, yo! yo! crying yo! yo! crying yo! yo!

Abena, her mother is dead, her father is dead, her grandparent is dead!

Hare said, “Oh! Oh! He said, ‘Your mother is dead; your father is dead; your grandparent is also dead’.” Then Abena placed her hands on her head, “Alas! Mother! Grandparent! Father!” She set off and came to her hometown; and the house was completely destroyed! That is why it is said, “When you go on a journey, cut it short.” And Abena did that, causing all her people to die in her absence.
