Kaseɛ Nɔ Ko Kɛjɛ Gibeonbii Lɛ Adɛŋ

“Gibeonbii lɛ kɛ Israel efee hejɔlɛ, ni amɛyahi amɛteŋ.”—YOS. 10:1.

AFİ lɛ ji 1473 B.C.E. Israel maŋ lɛ ebɔi Shiwoo Shikpɔŋ lɛ ŋɔɔ yɛ be kukuoo nɛɛ mli. Israelbii lɛ eye kunim ni yɔɔ naakpɛɛ yɛ Yeriko kɛ Ai maji lɛ anɔ. Shi sɛɛ mli lɛ, nɔ ko ni akpaaa gbɛ ba. Gbɛfalɔi akuu ko ba amɛŋɔɔ, ni amɛkɛɛ amɛjɛ shikpɔŋ ko ni yɔɔ shɔŋŋ nɔ, ni amɛmiitao amɛkɛ Nyɔŋmɔ maŋ lɛ akpaŋmaŋ hejɔlɛ kpaŋmɔ.

2 Gbɛfalɔi nɛɛ ji Gibeonbii lɛ. Nɛkɛ ji klɛŋklɛŋ be ni atsĩ Gibeonbii lɛ atã yɛ Biblia lɛ mli, shi jeee naagbee be ni. Biblia mli sanebimɔi pii tsɔɔ akɛ Gibeonbii lɛ kɛ Israelbii lɛ bɔ kpaakpa yɛ afii babaoo ni nyiɛ sɛɛ lɛ amli. Nɛkɛ saneyeli nɛɛ hiɛ nikasemɔi ni jara wa kɛhã wɔ, ni amɛtsɔɔ Yehowa Nyɔŋmɔ he nibii pii.

Amɛji Hivibii lɛ ahe fã, ni amɛji Kanaan maji kpaawo ni “fa fe amɛ ni amɛhe yɛ wa fe” Israelbii lɛ a-mli ekome. (5 Mo. 7:1) No hewɔ lɛ, Gibeonbii lɛ ji mɛi ni yɛ hewalɛ kɛteke nɔ yɛ tawuu mli. (2 Mo. 34:11; Yosh. 9:24) No hewɔ lɛ, beni Israelbii lɛ ye Yeriko kɛ Ai nɔ kunim sɛɛ lɛ, Gibeonbii lɛ tsu mɛi komɛi* kɛtee Yoshua ŋɔɔ yɛ Gilgal koni amɛyatao toiŋjɔlɛ.

Gibeonbii lɛ kpa Yoshua fai koni ewa amɛ. (Yosh. 10:3-7) Yoshua nyiɛ Israel hiɛ kɛba koni ebaha amɛ kɛje amɛnyɛlɔi lɛ adɛŋ, ni ajɔɔ amɛ mɔdɛŋbɔɔ lɛ nɔ. Yehowa po tswia anyɔŋte agboi kɛgbee hefatalɔi Amorbii lɛ anɔ, ni ebɔ mɔdɛŋ kɛ kpoo hulu lɛ kɛ kpeetaa kɛha ta lɛ. (Yosh. 10:9-14) Kɛtsɔ hejɔlɛ kpaŋmɔ ni amɛkɛ Israel ta kɛ kpa fai ni amɛkpa Yoshua koni ewa amɛ beni awula amɛgbeyei lɛ nɔ lɛ, Gibeonbii lɛ jie hemɔkɛyeli kpo yɛ Yehowa mli—hemɔkɛyeli yɛ enyɛmɔ ni ekɛbaaye ewiemɔ nɔ kɛ ebaahee amɛyiwala lɛ mli.

6 Mɛni sane nɛɛ tsɔɔ wɔ yɛ Yehowa he? Yehowa baa ehe shi ni eyeɔ mɔbɔ. Kɛjɛ tsɔɔ nɔ lɛ, etsɔɔ Israel akɛ ‘amɛshwie shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ bii lɛ fɛɛ,’ ni Gibeonbii lɛ hu fata he. (4 Mo. 33:51, 52) Kɛlɛ, bɔni afee ni Yehowa afi hejɔlɛ kpaŋmɔ ni Israelbii lɛ kɛ amɛ kpa lɛ sɛɛ lɛ, eye mɔbɔ ni eŋmɛ Gibeonbii lɛ gbɛ ni amɛhi shi. Yehowa fee nakai eyɛ mli akɛ Israelbii lɛ bibiiikɔ lɛ danyi amɛkɛ amɛ kpa nakai kpaŋmɔ lɛ. Kɛkɛ ni eye ebua Israel ni amɛhi shi yɛ amɛkpaŋmɔ lɛ naa ni etsɔ naakpɛɛ gbɛ nɔ ehere Gibeonbii lɛ ayiwala.—Yos. 9:26; 11:19.

7 Mɛni wɔbaanyɛ wɔfee koni wɔkase Gibeonbii lɛ? Kɛtsɔ hemiyeli ni mli wa ni wɔbaana yɛ Yehowa mli lɛ nɔ. Akɛnɛ wɔle nibii pii kɛkpe Yehowa he fe bɔ ni nakai be lɛ mli Gibeonbii lɛ le hewɔ lɛ, wɔyɛ yiŋtoi babaoo hewɔ ni esa akɛ wɔkɛ wɔhiɛ afɔ̃ Yehowa nɔ kɛmɔ shi. (Lala 40:4, 5) Wɔbaanyɛ wɔkase Gibeonbii lɛ hu kɛtsɔ heshibaamɔ nitsumɔi ni wɔbaasumɔ ni wɔtsu yɛ Yehowa sɔɔmɔ lɛ mli lɛ nɔ. (Yos. 9:23, 27) Nyɛminuu oblaho ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Luke lɛ hiɛ kpaŋ be ko ni enaa nyɛminuu onukpa ko yɛ Betel weku lɛ mli ni miifee nakai pɛpɛɛpɛ lɛ nɔ kɔkɔɔkɔ. Eyɛ mli akɛ nakai Betelnyo lɛ tsuɔ nitsumɔ ko ni yɔɔ hiɛdɔɔ ni he hiaa feemɔ moŋ, shi eyajie ehe kpo kɛba heshibaamɔ mli koni eyabu he yɛ maŋ lɛ mli Matsɛyeli Kpo lɛ maamɔhe lɛ gbɛkɛ. Luke kɛɛ: “Mikase akɛ heshibaamɔ jeee henumɔ kɛkɛ. Ejeɔ kpo kɛtsɔ nɔ ni wɔhalaa akɛ wɔbapfee loo wɔfeŋ lɛ nɔ.” Ebaahi akɛ wɔbaajie wɔhemiyeli yɛ Yehowa mli lɛ kpo daa gbi kɛtsɔ e-nɔ ni wɔkɛ wɔhiɛ baafɔ̃ kɛji wɔkɛ shihilɛi ni jaraa kpe, kɛ heshibaamɔ mli ni wɔbaakwɛ teokrase nitsumɔ fɛɛ nitsumɔ nɔ.

Kaseemɔ Gibeonbii lɛ kɛtsɔ heshibaanii ni obaasumɔ ni otsu lɛ nɔ (Kwɛmɔ kuku 7)

afii etɛ hɔmɔ. Be ni David bi nɔ hewɔ ni hɔmɔ lɛ ba lɛ, Yehowa kɛɛ lɛ akɛ Saul kɛ eweku lɛ yɛ lá fɔŋ mli, ejaakɛ bɔ ni amɛkɛ Gibeonbii lɛ ye ha afii komɛi ni eho lɛ.—2 Sam. 21:1.

10 Kanemɔ 2 Samuel 21:3-6. David kɛ Gibeonbii lɛ wie kɛbɔ mɔdɛŋ ni ebaale nɔ ni anyɛɔ afee kɛkpata nɔ fɔŋ ni Saul fee amɛ lɛ he. Ani amɛbaabɔ mɔdɛŋ ni amɛtsɔ David nɔ amɛná ninamɔ kɛtsɔ hãmɔ ni amɛbaahã eto najiaŋ akɛ efeee nɔ ko kɛye bubu hã amɛ tsutsu lɛ hewɔ? Gibeonbii lɛ hã hetoo ni ja akɛ jeee “jwiɛtɛi loo shika he sane,” ejaakɛ Mla lɛ kɛɛ, anyɛɛɛ akpɛlɛ kpɔmɔnɔ ko nɔ ahã gbomɔgbeilɔ. (4 Mose 35:30, 31) Kɛlɛ, amɛna hu akɛ amɛnyɛŋ amɛgbe mɔ ko kɛji amɛnáaa mla naa hewalɛ kɛjɛɛɛ gbɛ. Yɛ naagbee lɛ, David hã agbe Saul seshishi hii kpawo ni ekolɛ amɛ hu amɛkpɛlɛ mɔdɛŋ ni Saul bɔ akɛ ebaakpata mɛi fɛɛ hiɛ lɛ nɔ. Be ko sɛɛ lɛ, nubu nɛ, ni no gbe hɔmɔ lɛ naa ni ejie lɛ kpo hu akɛ ahã nɔgbɔŋ nɔ lɛ hetotoo ni sa Yehowa hiɛ.—2 Sam. 21:9, 10, 14.

11 Mɛni sane nɛɛ tsɔɔ wɔ yɛ Yehowa he? Wɔnaa odaseyeli kpele ni tsɔɔ akɛ Yehowa ji jalɛ Nyɔŋmɔ. (Lala 37:28) Eesumɔ ni aye gbɔmɛi fɛɛ saneyeli pɛpɛɛpɛ atsɔɔ amɛ. Enɛ biɔ gbɛi kɛtsɔɔ mɛi po ni ekolɛ ajieɔ amɛ yɛ amɛsrɔtofeemɔ hewɔ, tamɔ bɔ ni eji yɛ maŋsɛɛbii kɛ kuu bibii ahe lɛ. Yɛ ebe ni sa mli lɛ, Yehowa baajaje nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni afee esha awo ejalɔi lɛ. Wɔkaseɔ hu akɛ Yehowa kpaa gbɛ akɛ wɔbaaye wɔ shiwoo he, tamɔ bɔ ni ekpa gbɛ kɛjɛ Israelbii lɛ adɛŋ akɛ amɛbaaye hejɔlɛ shi ni amɛwo Gibeonbii lɛ nɔ lɛ.—Kɛto Amos 1:9 he.

FI ANƆKWALE JAMƆ LƐ SƐƐ KƐ ANƆKWAYELI

13 Kɛkɛ ni beni Maŋtsɛ David be lɛ eba naagbee aafee afii 500 lɛ, agbɛnɛ Gibeonbii lɛ je kpo ekoŋŋ yɛ Biblia lɛ mli. Babylon nomŋɔɔ afii 70 lɛ ba naagbee, ni yɛ afi 537 B.C.E. mli lɛ, Yudafoi nomii akuu ni ji klɛŋklɛŋ lɛ kɛ Amralo Zerubbabel ku amɛsɛɛ kɛba Yerusalem. (Ezra 2:1, 2, 58) Yɛ afi 468 B.C.E. mli lɛ, kuu kroko ku amɛsɛɛ, shi be nɛɛ kɛ Ezra, ninmalɔ lɛ. (Ezra 7:1-7) Nomii akuuji enyɔ nɛɛ ni faaa tsɔ ni ku amɛsɛɛ kɛba lɛ, fɛɛ mli yɛ wekuji ni jɛ “Netinim-bii” lɛ ateŋ. (Kwɛ shishigbɛ niŋmai ni yɛ Ezra 2:58 kɛ 7:7 lɛ.) Namɛi ji Netinim-bii lɛ? Amɛji “gbɔmɛi ni jeee Israelbii ni ji sɔlemɔtsu lɛ mli tsuji, loo sɔɔlɔi,” ni eeenyɛ eba akɛ amɛteŋ mɛi pii ji Gibeonbii lɛ aseshibii.—Kwɛ Wiemɔi A-Shishitsɔɔmɔ, “Netinim-bii.”

14 Kanemɔ 1 Kronika 9:2 kɛ shishigbɛlɛtsɔɔmɔ lɛ. “Sɔlemɔwe lɛ mli tsuji” lɛ yɛ mɛi ni ji klɛŋklɛŋ nomii ni ku amɛsɛɛ kɛba Israel lɛ ateŋ. Enɛ he hiaa ejaakɛ jeee Yudafoi ni yɔɔ nomŋmee lɛ mli lɛ fɛɛ ku amɛsɛɛ. Ŋwanejee ko bɛ he akɛ Yudafoi pii ena heloonaa nibii babaoo yɛ Babilon, ni amɛsumɔɔɔ ni amɛkɛ nibii ni amɛyɔɔ lɛ asha afɔle koni amɛku amɛsɛɛ ni amɛyatao amɛ maŋ ni efite lɛ amɛma ekoŋŋ. Shi anɔkwa nomii lɛ, ni mɛi komɛi ni eka shi faŋŋ akɛ Gibeonbii lɛ aseshibii ji amɛ fata he lɛ, shwe waa ni amɛna akɛ akɛ anɔkwa jamɔ ema shi ekoŋŋ yɛ sɔlemɔwe lɛ yɛ Yerusalem, ni amɛbɔ mɔdɛŋ fɛɛ ni amɛbaanyɛ koni amɛku amɛsɛɛ. Tamɔ bɔ ni eji yɛ Yudafoi lɛ agbɛfaŋ lɛ, Gibeonbii lɛ aseshibii lɛ bɛ shikpɔŋ gbɛnaa nii ko yɛ Israel. Kɛbɔ ni eji shɔŋŋ lɛ, amɛku amɛsɛɛ koni amɛbatsu amɛ sɔlemɔwe nitsumɔi lɛ ni po amɛye amɛbua kɛto Yerusalem gbogboi lɛ he.—Neh. 3:26.

15 Mɛni sane nɛɛ tsɔɔ wɔ yɛ Yehowa he? Wɔkaseɔ akɛ wɔ-Nyɔŋmɔ lɛ hiɛ sɔɔ etsuji anɔkwafoi lɛ waa diɛŋtsɛ ni ekwɛɔ amɛ be fɛɛ be. Kɛyashi be ni nomŋɔɔ yɛ Babilon lɛ baakpa lɛ, afii kpaako 1,000 mli eto kɛjɛ be ni Yehowa kɛ Gibeonbii lɛ here yiwala yɛ be mli ni Israelbii lɛ yeɔ Shiwoo Shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ kunim lɛ. Kɛbɛ nɔ lɛ, etee nɔ ejɔɔ amɛ. Gibeonbii lɛ asɛɛbii lɛ ye omanye yɛ Yuda kɛ Yerusalem fiteemɔ lɛ mli yɛ 607 B.C.E. Nomŋɔɔ lɛ sɛɛ lɛ, amɛtee nɔ amɛkɛ anɔkwa jamɔ bɔ gbagbalii, amɛsɔmɔ kɛfata Lewibii lɛ ahe yɛ sɔlemɔtsu lɛ mli. Eka shi faŋŋ akɛ mɛi komɛi yahi sɔlemɔtsu lɛ he kpaŋŋ. (Ezra 2:70; Neh. 11:21) Kɛkpe he lɛ, aŋmɛ amɛ gbɛ ni amɛkawa too, gbɛdidi too, kɛ toigbɔlɔi anɔ too yɛ sɔlemɔtsu lɛ mli nitsumɔ lɛ hew.—Ezra 7:24.

Nakai hemɔkɛyeli lɛ baaha wɔkɛ heshiba atsu nitsumɔ fɛɛ nitsumɔ ni akɛbaaha wɔ yɛ asafo lɛ mli lɛ he nii. LALA 12 Yehowa, Nyɔŋmɔ Kpeteŋkpele Lɛ

OTI Nɔ ni Yehowa leetsɔɔ, nɔ hewɔ ni esa akɛ wɔhã wɔleemɔ kɛhã lɛ mli awa, kɛ bɔ ni wɔbaafee wɔfee nakai.

Ani Oole Yehowa Kpɔŋ?

“Le otsɛ Nyɔŋmɔ lɛ, ni ofi otsui muu fɛɛ mli osɔmɔ lɛ.”—1 KRON. 28:9.

ANI osusu bɔ ni Yehowa lele kpaa he, bɔ ni enilee mli kwɔ ha, loo nibii ni etsu lɛ he pɛŋ? Kɛ́ wɔsusu wɔ-Bɔlɔ lɛ he lɛ, wɔbaa wɔhe shi. Ekolɛ obaanu he tamɔ bɔ ni bɔfo Paulo nu he lɛ, beni eŋma akɛ: “Ao, Nyɔŋmɔ ninamɔ kɛ nilee kɛ lele mli kwɔ kɛkɛ! Bɔ ni anyɛŋ akojoŋ kojomɔi ni ekojoo lɛ, ni anyɛŋ akpa egbɛi lɛ ahe shi eha nɛ!”—Rom. 11:33, 34.

2 Eyɛ mli akɛ wɔnyɛŋ wɔle nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni kɔɔ Yehowa he kɛmɔ shi kokooko moŋ, shi wɔbaanyɛ wɔle bɔ ni eji kɛyashi he ko. Maŋtsɛ David kɛɛ ebinuu Salomo akɛ: “Le otsɛ Nyɔŋmɔ lɛ, ni ofi otsui muu fɛɛ mli osɔmɔ lɛ . . . Kɛ́ otao elee lɛ, ebaaha ona lɛ.” (1 Kron. 28:9) Bɔ ni bɔfo Yohane kaimɔ enanemei Kristofoi lɛ afii ohai komɛi asɛɛ lɛ, wɔyɛ “nilee koni wɔle mɔ ni ji anɔkwalo lɛ.” (1 Yoh. 5:20) Ni Yesu diɛŋtsɛ kɛɛ akɛ, “mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni Bi lɛ sumɔɔ ni ejie lɛ etsɔɔ lɛ” lɛ baanyɛ ale Yehowa.—Mat. 11:27.

3 Mɛi fɛɛ ni ejɔɔ amɛhe nɔ amɛhã Yehowa lɛ ekase nibii babaoo yɛ ehe momo. Kɛbɔ ni eji lɛ kɛŋmɔ, wɔkpaaa Yehowa “he nilee kasee” kɔkɔɔkɔ. (Yohane 17:3; kwɛmɔ nikaselɔi a-wolo lɛ.)

2. Mɛni tsɔɔ akɛ wɔbaanyɛ wɔle Yehowa?

3. Mɛni he wɔbaagba yɛ nikasemɔ nɛɛ mli?

MƐNI ETSƆƆ AKƐ AALE YEHOWA?

4 Wɔkaseɔ nɔ ni kɔɔ Yehowa lele he kɛtsɔ nɔ ni ewie yɛ Yudafoi ni baaku amɛsɛɛ kɛjɛ nomŋmee mli lɛ ahe lɛ nisusuuhilɛ nɔ. Yehowa wo shi akɛ: “Maha amɛ tsui ni amɛkɛle mi, akɛ miji Yehowa.” (Yer. 24:7) Ŋkɔŋkɔ lɛ mɛi ni ku amɛsɛɛ kɛjɛ Babilon lɛ le saji komɛi ni yɔɔ kpatashii yɛ Yehowa he dadaw. Shi ebiɔ ni amɛtsui afata mɔdɛŋ ni amɛbɔɔ koni amɛle Yehowa lɛ he, nɔ ni henumɔi kɛ yiŋtoi fata he. Wolo ko tsɔɔ mli akɛ: “Soro nɔ ni ji akɛ ooole Nyɔŋmɔ he nɔ ko, ni soro nɔ ni ji akɛ ooole Nyɔŋmɔ diɛŋtsɛ. Ebaanyɛ atɔ sinɔ kɛ hefɛo ni yɔɔ suɔmɔ he nikanemɔ kɛ suɔmɔ mli nintee teŋ.” Ehe miihia ni wɔle fe bɔ ni ji kɛkɛ akɛ Yehowa yɛ gbɛi kɛ akɛ lɛ ji Nyɔŋmɔ. Wɔmiisumɔ ni wɔle Gbɔmɔ ni yɔɔ gbɛi lɛ sɛɛ lɛ.

5 Be ni wɔkaseɔ Yehowa he nii lɛ, wɔnaa nɔ ni kɛ miishɛɛ baa eŋɔɔ kɛ nɔ ni edɔɔ lɛ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, susumɔ Maŋtsɛ Yosia nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ he okwɛ. Biblia lɛ kɛɛ: “Yɛ eyeeli afi ni ji 8 lɛ mli, be ni eji gbekɛŋtsɔ lolo lɛ, ebɔi

tao etsɛ David Nyɔŋmɔ lɛ sɛɛ.” (2 Kron. 34:3) Shi Yosia fee babaoo fe nɔ ni ekaseɔ lɛ kɛkɛ. Ekɛ nɔ ni ekaseɔ lɛ tsu nii. Kɛtsɔ gbalɔ Yeremia nɔ lɛ, Yehowa kɛɛ Yosia “tsu kojomɔ kɛ jalɛ nɔ nii” ni ehéra amanehulufoi kɛ ohiafoi lɛ agbɛnaa. Kɛkɛ ni Yehowa kɛfata he akɛ: “Ani jeee nɔ ni tsɔɔ akɛ ale mi nɛ?”—Yer. 22:15, 16.

6 Kanem Lala 9:10. Bɔ ni wɔkaseɔ Yehowa he nii babaoo lɛ, nakai nɔŋŋ wɔheɔ lɛ wɔyeɔ babaoo. Nakai shishitoo mla lɛ ba mli yɛ bɔfo Paulo gbɛfaŋ. Be ni awo lɛ tsuŋ yɛ naagbee kwraa kɛ be kukuoo dani ebaagbo lɛ, eŋma akɛ: “Hiɛ egbee ko mi. Ejaakɛ mile Mɔ ni mihe miye lɛ.” (2 Tim. 1:12) Nɔ ni Paulo kase kɛ nɔ ni ekɛkpe yɛ eshihilɛ mli lɛ ha ehe e-Nyɔŋmɔ lɛ eye kwraa. Bɔ ni nikasemɔ ŋmalɛ ni kɔɔ nakai kuku lɛ he lɛ tsɔɔ mli lɛ: “Paulo . . . yɛ nɔmimaa akɛ Yehowa baakai e-anɔkwayeli shihilɛ gbɛ lɛ kɛyashi ‘nakai gbi lɛ,’ be ni Ebaatee lɛ shi lɛ.”

8 Kɛ́ wɔle Yehowa jogbaŋŋ lɛ, naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ wɔ kɛ lɛ wɔteŋ lɛ mli baawa. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, te onuɔ he oyahaa tɛŋŋ yɛ Yehowa he kɛ́ okane Biblia lɛ daa gbi, okwɛ nyɔɔŋ nɔ nyɔɔŋ nɔ broadcast lɛ, loo oyasɔmɔ yɛ kpokpaa nɔ kpeemɔ loo kpokpaa wulu nɔ kpeemɔ shishi? Wɔnuɔ he tamɔ lalatsɛ ni jwe Yehowa bɔ ni ekwɛɔ ehe nɔ ehaa lɛ nɔ, ni ekɛɛ: “Nyɔŋmɔ ji mitsui lɛ tɛsaa kɛ migbɛfaŋnɔ kɛyaana naanɔ.” (Kanemɔ Lala 73:24-28.) 9 Kɛ́ wɔ-“kai” Yehowa—kɛ́ wɔkai nɔ ni wɔkase yɛ ehe kɛ bɔ ni wɔ-nifeemɔi saa ehe he nɔ—wɔbaakpɛ yiŋ kpakpai. Enɛ yeɔ bua amɛ ni amɛkaiɔ amɛ-hefatalɔi lɛ be fɛɛ be kɛ suɔmɔ, ni amɛkpɛɔ yiŋ ni amɛle akɛ ebaasa amɛ-hefatalɔi lɛ ahiɛ, kɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni baagba mɔ lɛ naa lɛ he tseemɔ. Nakai nɔŋŋ kɛ́ wɔkai Yehowa be fɛɛ be lɛ, ebaawa wɔ kɛ-ha yiŋkpɛi ni sa ehiɛ lɛ feemɔ.—Lala 19:13, 14.

10 Abɔ wɔ yɛ Yehowa subaŋ lɛ nɔ, ni enɛ haa wɔnyɛɔ wɔjeɔ esubai lɛ akpo. (1 Mose 1:26) Ni kɛ́ wɔkase Yehowa subai lɛ ahe nii babaoo lɛ, wɔbaanu bɔ ni wɔbaafee wɔkase lɛ lɛ shishi jogbaŋŋ. Yesu wie eshi woloŋmalɔi lɛ kɛ Farisifoɔ lɛ akɛni amɛnyɛɛɛ amɛje Nyɔŋmɔ subai akpo lɛ hewɔ. Eyake amɛkpaa Mla lɛ mli saji bibii lɛ ahe gbee jogbaŋŋ moŋ, shi amɛ “kwa Mla lɛ mli saji ni he hiaa fe fɛɛ lɛ, nɔ hewɔ ji, jalɛsane kɛ mɔbɔnalɛ kɛ anɔkwayeli.” (Mat. 23:23) Wɔ lɛ, ekaaa jamɔŋ hiɛnyielɔi nɛɛ, nyɛhaa wɔbɔa mɔdɛŋ fɛɛ ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔnu bɔ ni Yehowa subai lɛ jeɔ kpo yɛ emlaii kɛ eshishitoo mlaii lɛ amli lɛ shishi. Kɛ́ wɔfee nakai lɛ, ebaaye ebua wɔ ni wɔje nakai subai lɛ nɔŋŋ kpo.—1 Yoh. 4:8, 11.

Yeebua Mɛi Krokomɛi Ni Amɛle Yehowa

“Enɛ ji naanɔ wala lɛ, akɛ amɛbaale bo, anɔkwa Nyɔŋmɔ kome pɛ lɛ.”—YOHANE 17:3.

TE onuɔ he ohaa tɛŋŋ kɛ́ Biblia kaselɔ ko yabɔi asafoŋ kpee kɛtsɔ eklɛŋkɛlɛŋ nɔ ni eyaa lɛ nɔ, ebɔi hetoo hãmɔ, ejaje ehemɔkɛyeli lɛ etsɔɔ mɛi krokomɛi, ni yɛ be ko sɛɛ lɛ, ekpɛ eyiŋ akɛ eeejɔɔ ehe nɔ ehã Yehowa Nyɔŋmɔ? Ŋwanejee ko bɛ he akɛ omǐi shɛɔ ohe pam! Yɛ anɔkwale mɔɔ mli lɛ, Yehowa ji mɔ ni sa yijiemɔ yɛ mumɔŋ shwelii ni wɔ-Biblia kaselɔ lɛ feɔ lɛ he. Wɔjieɔ Yehowa yi kɛ́ wɔna bɔ ni eyeɔ ebuaa mɔ ko ni etee ehiɛ yɛ mumɔŋ.—1 Kor. 3:5-9; 3 Yoh. 3, 4.

2 Nyɔŋmɔ suɔmɔ ji ni “gbɔmɛi srɔtoi fɛɛ” “ahe ayiwala ni amɛba anɔkwale lɛ he nilee pɔtɛɛ mli.” (Kanemɔ 1 Timoteo 2:3, 4.) Yehowa miisumɔ ni gbɔmɛi ale lɛ kɛ eyiŋtoi lɛ aahu bɔ ni amɛbaanyɛ. (Yer. 9:24) Enɛ tsɔɔ nilee pɔtɛɛ nináa. Shishijee wiemɔ mli wiemɔ ni atsɔɔ shishi akɛ “nilee pɔtɛɛ” lɛ etsɔɔɔ anɔkwalei kɛkɛ ni mɔ ko kaseɔ, shi moŋ nilee ni “náa mɔ lɛ nɔ hewalɛ kwraa,” tamɔ bɔ ni Biblia he nilelɔ ko wie lɛ. Kɛ́ Biblia kaselɔ ko babale Yehowa lɛ, nakai nilee lɛ náa bɔ ni ehiɔ shi ehaa lɛ nɔ hewalɛ. Ni efeɔ hegbɛ kɛha lɛ koni ená “naanɔ wala.”—Yohane 17:3.

3 Yehowa ehã wɔ hegbɛ ni kã shi akɛ wɔye wɔbua mɛi krokomɛi koni amɛbale lɛ. Yɛ nikasemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaasusu sanebimɔi nɛɛ ahe: Mɛni hewɔ ehe miihia ni mɛi ni wɔkɛ amɛ kaseɔ Biblia lɛ abale Yehowa jogbaŋŋ? Mɛɛ gbɛ nɔ wɔbaanyɛ wɔtsɔ wɔtsɔɔ amɛ nɔ ni Yehowa ji? Kɛ agbɛnɛ hu, mɛɛ gbɛ nɔ wɔbaanyɛ wɔye wɔbua amɛ koni amɛkɛ amɛ

4 Yesu kɛɛ kitã ni fe fɛɛ ji ni wɔsumɔ Yehowa kɛ wɔtsui fɛɛ, wɔsusuma fɛɛ, wɔjwɛŋmɔ fɛɛ, kɛ wɔhewalɛ fɛɛ. (Kanemɔ Marko 12:30.) Esa akɛ Biblia kaselɔ ale Yehowa koni enyɛ esumɔ lɛ. Wɔmiisumɔ ni wɔ-Biblia kaselɔ ajɔɔ ehe nɔ—jeee eha wɔ, jeee eha tsɔsemɔi komɛi, jeee eha gbɛjianɔtoo ko—shi moŋ eha Yehowa. No hewɔ lɛ, oti titri ni ma wɔhiɛ kɛ́ wɔmiifee Biblia mli nikaselɔ kɛ mɔ ko ji ni wɔye wɔbua wɔkaselɔ lɛ ni ele Yehowa kɛ esumɔ lɛ.

5 Tsakpaa yɛ suɔmɔ kɛ toiboo teŋ. Kɛ́ obibia lε balelɔ Yehowa ni esumɔ lɛ pɛ, ebaafee klalo akɛ ebaabo lɛ toi. Yesu kɛɛ: “Koni je lɛ ale akɛ misumɔɔ Tsɛ lɛ, miifee tamɔ bɔ ni Tsɛ lɛ fã mi akɛ mafee lɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ.” (Yohane 14:31) Nakai nɔŋŋ hu, suɔmɔ kɛha Yehowa—fe nɔ fɛɛ nɔ—baatsirɛ bibia ko koni ebo lɛ toi ni ekpɛtɛ ehe.—1 Yohane 5:3.

6 Ebafeɔ mlɛo kɛha Biblia kaselɔi ni baale Yehowa jogbaŋŋ lɛ akɛ amɛbaakpɛ yiŋ ni nilee yɔɔ mli. Kɛ́ wɔbaaha nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ: Lɔle kpakpa kɔ̃ɔlɔ kaseee nɔ ni kɔ̃ɔmɔ biɔ kɛtsɔ lɔle kɔ̃ɔmɔ mlaiaŋ nikasemɔ loo kaamɔ ko feemɔ pɛ nɔ. Esa akɛ ekɛ ŋmɛlɛtswai babaoo akɔ̃ lɔle lɛ hu. Be ni beie shwieɔ mli lɛ, ekaseɔ bɔ ni lɔle lɛ feɔ eŋii yɛ shihilɛi srɔtoi amli, kɛ bɔ ni yiŋkpɛi ni ekpɛɔ lɛ saɔ lɔle lɛ kɔ̃ɔmɔ he. Nakai nɔŋŋ hu, Biblia kaselɔ ko baale Yehowa jeee kɛtsɔ enikasemɔ pɛ nɔ, shi kɛtsɔ bɔ ni nibii yaa nɔ ehaa lɛ be ni ekpɛɔ yiŋ ni kɛ Ŋmalɛ mli shishitoo mlai kpaa gbee lɛ nɔ hu. Be ni enilee yɛ Yehowa he lɛ yaa nɔ edaa, ni enaɔ bɔ ni eŋwɛi Tsɛ lɛ yeɔ ebuaa lɛ lɛ, ebaaná hewalɛ ni ekɛkpɛ yiŋ kpakpai yɛ shihilɛ fɛɛ shihilɛ mli.

7 Yehowa he nilee ye bua Yosef beni Potifar ŋa bɔ mɔdɛŋ ni ebaanyɔŋ lɔlɔ eŋɔɔ lɛ. Kɛjɛ egbekɛbiiashi lɔɔ, Yosef nu Adam kɛ Hawa kɛ Abraham kɛ Sara asane lɔɔ he nɔŋŋ ekãa. (1 Mo. 2:24; 20:3, 6) Yosef na kɛjɛ nakai saneji lɛ amli bɔ ni Yehowa buɔ gbalashihilɛ kɛ bɔlɛnamɔ he nikee lɛ. Yosef ba le Yehowa jogbaŋŋ aahu akɛ ekpoo Potifar ŋa lɛ bɔmɔi lɛ ni ekɛɛ lɛ: “Mɛɛba mabafee efɔŋ kpele nɛɛ ni matsɔ Nyɔŋmɔ nɔ esha kɛ?” (1 Mo. 39:7-9) Yosef le nɔ ni hi kɛ nɔ ni ehiii yɛ Yehowa hiɛ. Enu nɔ ni baagba e-Nyɔŋmɔ lɛ naa shishi. Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, Yosef fee yijiemɔ ni ja. Kɛ́ wɔye wɔbua wɔ-Biblia kaselɔi lɛ ni amɛle Yehowa jogbaŋŋ ni amɛkase bɔ ni eji lɛ, amɛ hu amɛbaahala nɔ ni sa ehiɛ.

*Nyɛsaa Nyɛhiɛ Kɛya Naano!* wolo lɛ hiɛ sanebimɔi babaoo ni tsɔɔ nikaselɔi lɛ Yehowa he nii kɛyaa hiɛ. Susumɔ nɔkwɛmɔnii fioo nɛɛ ahe:

“Te Yehowa nuɔ he ehaa tɛŋŋ yɛ mɛi ni shiɛɔ sane kpakpa lɛ ahe?” (nikasemo 21)

“Mɛni nɔ́ ni okase yɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu he lɛ tsɔɔ bo ni ehaa onaa faa akɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni amɛbaafee [yɛ Harmagedon lɛ] baahi kɛgbe he, ni ebaaja?” (nikasemo 33)

Te Yehowa nuɔ he ehaa tɛŋŋ kɛ́ oyɛ nilee ni obo lɛ toi? Mɛni hewɔ? (nikasemɔ 34)

“Te osusuɔ akɛ Yehowa nuɔ he ehaa tɛŋŋ kɛ́ wɔkɛ mɔ ko ni sumɔɔɔ lɛ bote gbalashihilɛ mli?” (nikasemɔ 42)

“Te Yehowa kɛfamɔ lɛ da kɛjɛ nɛɛbɔɔ?” (nikasemɔ 56)

Mɛni gbɛ nɔ bɔ ni Yehowa kɛ eshafeelɔi yeɔ ehaa lɛ tsɔɔ akɛ ehiɛ tɛ̃ɛ, enaŋmɔ kɛ suɔmɔ yɛ emli? (nikasemɔ 57)

Lɛ kɛtsɔ sanebimɔi a-nɔ—nɔ ni fe 50 sɔŋŋ! (Hiob 38:1–41:34) Wɔ hu wɔbaanyɛ wɔbi sanebimɔi. Ajie gbɛ afee sanebimɔi ni yɔɔ Na Ohiɛ Kɛkpe Wala Kɛya Naanɔ! wolo lɛ mli lɛ koni amɛye amɛbua nikaselɔ ko ni ebale Yehowa su kɛ ŋwɛi gbɛ. (Kwɛmɔ daka ni ji “Sanebimɔi Ni Yeɔ Buaa Wɔ Koni Wɔtsɔɔ Nii.”) No hewɔ lɛ, kɛ́ sanebimɔi nɛɛ atsu nii koni oye obua onikaselɔ lɛ ni ebale Lɛ.

9 Kɛji wɔbɔi mɔ ko Biblia kasemɔ kɛtsɔ wolo nɛɛ nɔ pɛ nɔŋŋ lɛ, wɔwoɔ lɛ hewalɛ ni ebɔi Biblia lɛ kanemɔ daa gbi.* Mɛni wɔbaanyɛ wɔfee kɛye wɔbua amɛ koni amɛkanemɔ lɛ ahe aba sɛɛnamɔ kɛha amɛ diɛŋtsɛ amɛhe? Wɔbaanyɛ wɔwo amɛ ŋaa akɛ be fɛɛ be ni amɛbaakane Biblia mli kuku ko lɛ, amɛsusu nɔ hewɔ ni Yehowa kɛwo e-Wiemɔ lɛ mli kɛ sui ni ejieɔ kpo yɛ ehe lɛ he. ATSIƐ AHE YƐ BROOKLYN BETEL

Beni mikɛ Wilson hi shi afii enyɔ sɛɛ lɛ, afɔ̃ mi nine kɛtee Brooklyn Betel yɛ afi 1985 mli. Tamɔ bɔ ni mitsĩ tã yɛ shishijee lɛ, akɛ mi yawo he ni apeeɔ woji yɛ lɛ (Bindery). Gbi ko beni miitsu nii yɛ tsɔne ni akɛŋmaa nii awoɔ woji ahewolo mli lɛ nɔ lɛ, mifite woji ahewoloi ohai abɔ yɛ gbɛ ni jeee gbɛ nɔ. Bindery Ɔfis lɛ bi ni maya bɔ amɛ amaniɛ yɛ nɔ ni eba lɛ he. Nyɛminuu Henschel wieee nɔ ko, ete shi ni ekɛ mɛi ni kɛ niyenii bahãa lɛ bɔi nu kpaai kɛ aboloo kɛ buter plɛtii kɛhãmɔ. Nɔmɔ heshiba sɔɔmɔ nɛɛ hi mijwɛŋmɔ mli kɛyaashee naanɔ. Saamɔ Fiteemɔ Lɛ Mra!

Ani nyɛminuu ko loo nyɛmiwoo ko efee bo nɔ ko ni edɔ bo aahu akɛ amrɔ nɛɛ nyɛteŋ efite? Bɔ ni afee ni asaa naanyobɔɔ ni efite lɛ kɛya nɔ lɛ, ehe miihia ni ofee oya nɔŋŋ kɛ susumɔ ni ja.*

Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, esa akɛ atsaa wui ni ekumɔ bɔni afee ni ebaanyɛ etsu nii kpakpa yɛ sɛɛ mli ni hepiŋmɔ bɛ mli. Shi hepiŋmɔ lɛ baaya nɔ kɛ́ atsaa wui ni ekumɔ lɛ amrɔ nɔŋŋ. Nɔ ni fe fɛɛ lɛ, ekolɛ wui lɛ etɔ̃ɔŋ dɔŋŋ kɛya naanɔ, ni enyɛŋ etsu nii bɔ ni sa dɔŋŋ.

Tamɔ wu ni ekumɔ nɛɛ, esa akɛ atsaa wekukpɔŋ ni efite yɛ okɛ Kristofonyo ko teŋ lɛ nɔŋŋ. Kɛ́ jeee nakɔ lɛ, naanyobɔɔ ni efite lɛ mli piŋmɔ lɛ baaya nɔ. Minilɛɛ baanyɛ ada, ni obaanyɛ olaaje onaanyo lɛ. Yesu kɛɛ: “Kɛ́ onaanyo kɛ bo yɛ sane lɛ, tsaa mɔ lɛ nɔŋŋ okɛ lɛ akpata.” (Mat. 5:25) No hewɔ lɛ, yaa onaanyo lɛ ŋɔɔ ni okɛ lɛ agba sane yɛ mlihilɛ mli, shi kɛ faŋŋfeemɔ yɛ sane lɛ he. Mɛni hewɔ enɛ baanyɛ afee nɔ ni wa? Susumɔ yiŋtoi enyɔ nɛɛ ahe.

Klɛŋklɛŋ kwraa lɛ, ebaanyɛ eba akɛ obaaŋmɛ otsui shi kɛkɛ ni otsuuu sane lɛ he nɔ ko, ekolɛ ejaakɛ efeɔ bo naagba akɛ obaagba naagba he sane.# Susumɔ Euodia kɛ Sintike he okwɛ, Kristofoi anyɛmimɛi yei ni eka shi faŋŋ akɛ amɛnyɛɛɛ amɛtsa naagba ko ni ba amɛteŋ lɛ nɔ. Eka shi faŋŋ akɛ amɛnaagba lɛ hi shi be kplaŋŋ, ejaakɛ bɔfo Paulo po, ni yɔɔ shɔŋŋ yɛ Roma lɛ, nu he sane. Ewo amɛ hewalɛ ni amɛfee nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni he hiaa kɛsaŋ amɛ ekomefeemɔ lɛ he. (Fil. 4:2) Kɛ heshiba, sanegbaa kpakpa, kɛ tɔmɔkɛke diɛŋtsɛ lɛ, amɛnaanyobɔɔ lɛ baanyɛ afee ekpakpa ekoŋŋ.

Nɔ ni ji enyɔ lɛ, ekolɛ obaakpa gbɛ akɛ mɔ kroko lɛ afee enii yɛ gbɛ ni tsɔɔ akɛ eyɔse etɔmɔ lɛ nɔ. Shi osumɔɔ ni ona onaanyo lɛ ekoŋŋ, jeee ni okɛ fɔmɔ atswi lɛ. Dɔkta ni tsaa wu ni ekumɔ lɛ kɛ ejwɛŋmɔ maa nɔ ni ebaaafee kɛtsa nɔ, jeee ni ebaatao nibii fɛɛ amli pɛpɛɛpɛ yɛ nɔ ni ha ekumɔ lɛ he. Kɛ́ okɛ onaanyo lɛ wieɔ lɛ, kaaha ojwɛŋmɔ yaa mɔ ni yɔɔ fɔmɔ yɛ naanyobɔɔ lɛ ni efite lɛ he. Shi moŋ, kaimɔ bɔ ni onaanyobɔɔ lɛ jara wa kɛha bo, ni okɛ ojwɛŋmɔ ama nɔ ni esa akɛ ofee koni osa hiɛ.

Mɛni hewɔ oyaaa nyɛminuu loo nyɛmiyei lɛ ŋɔɔ ŋmɛnɛ, koni nyɛyakpata naanyo bɔ ni afee ni naanyobɔɔ lɛ ba ekoŋŋ? “Nyɛkahaa hulu nyɔɔ nyɛmli kɛ miifu.” (Efe. 4:26) Kɛ́ ofee oya nɔŋŋ koni nyɛkpata naanyo lɛ, ebaaye ebua ni nyɛtoŋ “hejɔlɛ kpaa ni baa mɛi amli kpaŋŋ” lɛ he gbɛtsiibii.—Efe. 4:1-3.

Yɛ wɔn asafoŋ kpeehi lɛ ashishi lɛ, wɔyɛ hegbɛ akɛ wɔtsɔ wɔhanoomɔi lɛ anɔ wɔwo wɔnyɛmimɛi hii kɛ yei lɛ ahewalɛ. (Heb. 10:24, 25) Mɛni wɔbaanyɛ wɔfeɛ koni wɔsaa wɔhe kɛha hanoomɔi ni baawo mɛi krokomɛi ahewalɛ lɛɛlɛŋ?

• Sɔle kɛha Yehowa. Bi lɛ ni eye ebua bo ni ohã hetoo ni woɔ ehĩɛ nyam ni ewoɔ mɛi krokomɛi ahewalɛ.—Lala 35:18.

• Bɔ mɔdɛŋ ni ofee nɔ ko kɛfa he fe nɔ ni obaakane nɔ ni yɔɔ wolo lɛ mli lɛ kɛkɛ. Kɛ́ okɛ odiɛŋtsɛ owiemɔi ha hetoo lɛ, mɛi krokomɛi baaná he sɛɛ be ni amɛnuɔ bɔ ni otsɔɔ ohemɔkɛyeli lɛ mli lɛ.

• Ha ohetoo lɛ afee kukuuku. Bei pii lɛ, hetoi ni ji kukuuku ni yɔɔ kɛkɛ lɛ woɔ mɔ hewalɛ fe fɛɛ, ni ehaa mɛi babaoo náa hegbɛ kɛgbaa sane yɛ kpee lɛ shishi.—Abɛi 10:19.

• Hã ohetoo lɛ afee nɔ ni woɔ mɔ hewalɛ. Kaakɛ ohetoo lɛ wo asafo lɛ loo nyɛminyo Kristofonyo ko ŋaa loo kɛkã amɛ.

Kɛ̃kɛle atsɛɛɛ bo ni ohã hetoo po lɛ, obaanyɛ owo mɛi krokomɛi ahewalɛ lolo. Bo toi jogbaŋŋ, ni oda shi yɛ amɛhetoi lɛ ahe.—Abɛi 10:21.

Akɛ wolo nɛɛ haaa koni ahɔɔ. Eji jeŋ muu fɛɛ Biblia kasemɔ nitsumɔ ni afeɔ kɛtsɔɔ suɔmɔ mli onai anɔ lɛ fã kome. Kɛ́ oomiisumɔ ni ofee onai lɛ, ofainɛ yaa donate.jw.org.

Kɛji akɛtsɔɔɔ gbɛ kroko nɔ lɛ, Ŋmalɛi ni atsɛ yisɛɛ lɛ jɛ ŋmɛnɛŋmɛnɛ beaŋ wiemɔ New World Translation of the Holy Scriptures lɛ mli.
