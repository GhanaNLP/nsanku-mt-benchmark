1926

JEHA LAFA NƐ BE O

“BE ɔ suɔ si nɛ Mawu maa pee e he biɛ. Mawu ma ná nɔ ko nɛ e maa pee e se odasefohi ngɛ zugba a nɔ,” lɛ The Watch Tower nɛ a pee ngɛ January 1, 1926 ɔ de.

Benɛ jeha 1926 kpa si kɛ yaa a, Yehowa—kɛ gu asafo ɔ yi, Kristo nɔ—haa nɛ e he be kaa fiɛɛmi ní tsumi ɔ nɛ pee nɔ́ titli nɛ ngɛ Kristofo nɔ si himi mi. Kɛ ha lɔ ɔ heje ɔ, “tsɔlɔ anɔkweetsɛ kɛ nilelɔ ɔ” ha Mawu sɔmɔli ɔmɛ womihi hehi, e tsake níhi ngɛ kpe ɔmɛ a mi, nɛ e ha nɛ a nu Kristofo nɔ blɔ nya ní tsumihi a sisi kɛ e mi tɛ̃ɛ. (Mat. 24:45) Kristo hyɛ nɛ a ná tsuhi kɛ ní tsumi nɔ́hi nɛ a he hia kɛ ha fiɛɛmi ní tsumi ɔ.

WO HE KO KƐ KPEHI A HE TSƆƆMIHI

Ngɛ April mi ɔ, The Golden Age (nɛ amlɔ nɛ ɔ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Awake!) bɔ womi he ko nɛ a wo lɛ biɛ ke Deliverance ɔ he amaniɛ. Womi nɛ ɔ maa ye Studies in the Scriptures ɔ nane mi kɛ pee womi titli nɛ a maa kɛ tsu ní ngɛ fiɛɛmi nítsumi ɔ mi. Bɔ nɛ pee nɛ a nyɛ kɛ ba womi ɔmɛ nɛ a maa kpa mɛ kɛ po ɔ nya a, nyɛmimɛ nɛ ngɛ Betel ɔ he mɔtulɔ he ko nɛ ma nyɛ maa tɔ̃ womi 22 ngɛ mɛnɛti kake mi. Baiblo Kaselɔ ɔmɛ kɛ womi he ɔ tsu ní ngɛ he fɛɛ he nɛ blɔ ma bli kɛ ha mɛ.

Jeha babauu nɛ be ɔ, Baiblo Kaseli ɔmɛ kaseɔ womihi nɛ a tsɛɛ ke Studies in the Scriptures ɔ nɔ́ tsuaa nɔ́ ngɛ a kpehi a sisi. Se kɛ je May mi ɔ, a bɔni The Watch Tower womi tɛtlɛɛ ɔ mi munyahi kasemi. Mɛni je mi kpo?

“Bunker Hill, Illinois asafo kuku ɔ ngɛ mo ha he nɔ gbami doe,” jamɛ a asafo ɔ ngma ngɛ sɛwɔ bi ko mi kɛ ya ha Nyɛminyumu Rutherford. “Hɔgbanihi a nɔ ɔ, wa kaseɔ [munyuhi nɛ] Asafo ɔ to kɛ ha jamɛ a behi ɔ. E ngɔɔ wa he wawɛɛ nɛ wɔ tsuo wa nyɛɔ nɛ wa naa la he ɔ kaa bɔ nɛ e be suɔ.”

“La ehe ɔ,” aloo sisi numi nɛ a dla mli ɔ, ye bua Baiblo Kaseli ɔmɛ nɛ a ya nɔ nɛ a kɛ a hɛ fɔ fiɛɛmi ní tsumi ɔ nɔ. Kɛ̃kɛ nɛ, munyu nɛ ji “Character or Covenant—Which?” ɔ tsɔɔ kaa “jehanuɛ su kpakpa he nitsumi,” nɛ hɛ kɛ fɔ bɔ nɛ a maa pee a he su kpakpa kɛ dɔgblagblanyi nɔ kɛkɛ ɔ, ha nɛ ni komɛ bui Kristofohi nitsɛmɛ a ní tsumi nitsɛ ɔ nya. Munyu ɔ wo ngɔmami ke: “Nɔ nɛ suɔ Nyɔmtsa a lɛɛ e sa nɛ amlɔ nɛ ɔ . . . e bu e mlaahi a tue. . . . Yehowa de ke: ‘Nyɛ ji ye odasefli nɛ tsɔɔ kaa Mawu ji mi.’ . . . Kristofo no ɔ maa suɔ amlɔ nɛ ɔ wawɛɛ nɛ e ye odase kaa Mawu odasefo klɔuklɔu.”

Muni kpa ko nɛ ji, “Afɔle Sami Kɛ Sɔmɔmi,” ma nɔ mi kaa anɔkwa Kristofohi tsɔɔ a suɔmi kɛha Yehowa kɛ gu anɔkwayeli sɔmɔmi nɛ a kɛ haa lɛ ɔ nɔ. Muni ɔ wo Baiblo Kaseli tsuo he wami kaa a kɛ kpekpemi, nɔ́tsɛmi, kɛ kã nɛ pee Nyɔmtsa a sɔmɔmi nítsumi ɔ, koni a “pee odasefohi kɛha Nyɔmtsa a nɛ a fiɛɛ e sɛgbi ɔ kɛ ya ha je ma amɛ kɛ je ɔ nɔ bimɛ.”—Yes. 43:10, 12; Mat. 24:14.

2 HWƐƆMI MƆƆ

Tsakemi nɛ ɔmɛ a he wami ba kpe he ɔmɛ a nɔ mlamla. Otshi bɔɔ pɛ se ɔ, The Watch Tower nɛ a pee July 15, 1926 ɔ je sɛvɔ nɛ ɔ kpo: “Munyubɔ nɛ nyɛ minɔ nɛ ɔmɛ, ‘Su loo Somi—Kɛ Te nɛ e ji?’ kɛ ‘Afɔle Sami kɛ Sɔmɔmi,’ ji haomi kɛ buami kpeteŋkpele. Wa be nyɛe maa kane wa Towers ɔmɛ kɛkɛ amlɔ nɛ ɔ, ja wa kase mɛ.” Sɛvɔ ɔ nyagbe ke “amlɔ nɛ ɔ po ɔ, e ngɛ kɛe kaa suɔmi kpele ngɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ lɔ kpo kaa a maa tu a susuumi he munyu faa ngɛ nikasemi ɔmɛ a si, nɛ e ngɛ he wami kpakpa kpeteŋkpele pee ngɛ wa kpe he ɔ nɔ jio.”

KPÉHI TEE FIƐƐLIƐ NÍ TSUOMI Ɔ NƆ

Benɛ May nyahiɔ ɔ yaa nyagbe ɔ, Baiblo Kaseli babauu kpe ngɛ kpe ko nɛ a pee ngɛ London, England ɔ sisi. Nitsumi nɛ a kpa ngɛ Britain ɔ ha nɛ e he wa kaa a ma na womi fialo komɛ, enɛ ɔ he ɔ, nyɛmimɛ nɛ a ngɛ Brooklyn Bethel ɔ tsu ní be kɛ se konɛ a fia womiyo nɛ a tsɛɛ ke The Standard for the People ɔ he kɔpi 117,000 babauu kɛ fɔ kpe ɔ mi.

Nɔ Hɔ, May 29 ɔ, a kɛ womi tsowi nɛ ɔmɛ tsu ní wawɛː. Kpe wami he ní tsumi blɔ nya tomi ɔ kɛ ha jamɛ a ligbi ɔ wo a he wami ke: “A ngɛ nɔ tsuaa nɔ nɛ ejɔɔ e he nɔ nɛ e ba kpe ɔ fɔe ngɛ nɛ e ba piɛɛ he kɛ fia Matsɛ ɔ kɛ Matsɛ Yemi ɔ he fiɛɛli a he. Nyɛɛ ya Alexandra Palace mlamla bɔ nɛ nyɛ ma nyɛ, nɛ nyɛ ya bɔ amaniɛ ngɛ Sɔmɔmi He ɔ kɛ ha ní tsumi.”

A gba womihi nɛ hiɛ pe 100,000. July 15, 1926 Watch Tower ɔ bɔ amaniɛ ke: “Huɛmɛ ɔmɛ a bua jɔ wawɛɛ nitsɛ. A nu he kaa a bɔ mɔde nɛ a ye Yehowa fãmi nɛ se: ‘Nyɛ ji ye Odasefohi kaa I ji Mawu’ ɔ nɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ.”

E no jena a, ngɛ Royal Albert Hall ɔ, Nyɛminyumu Rutherford tu munyu kɛ kɔ hɛmi munyu nɛ ji, “Mɛni He Je Je O Nɔ Yemi Womihi Ngɛ Slikiti Ye E?—Tsɔ̃semi O” he. Be bɔoo kɛkɛ nɛ a bli sinyahi a se ɔ, nimli 13,000 hyi he nɛ a kpe ngɛ ɔ tɔ. Nimli babauu nɛ a nine su we tɔɔ mi ɔ bu gbihi a he fɔfɔɔkpa kɛ gu kpolomi dadehi nɛ a ma ngɛ kpo ɔ nɔ.

Nyɛminyumu Rutherford tsɔɔ blɔ nya ngɛ e munyu ɔ mi kaa je ɔ mi hewanyeli nɛ hlaa a kpee si ɔ je bɔ nɛ a kpo Yehowa kɛ e Matsɛ Yemi ɔ mi. Nyɛminyumu Rutherford kpa Jemahi A Kpee ɔ he gu nɛ e gba kɛ fɔ si kaa e be hie hu.

Se kɛ̃ ɔ, be nɛ kpe ɔ su si pɔɔ he ɔ, sane bɔmi womi tɛtlɛɛ ngua komɛ kpo kaa a maa fia kpe ɔ he adafi.

TSŬ NƐ NGƐ 117 ADAMS STREET

Akɛnɛ nihi ngɛ womihi hlae babauu heje ɔ, nyɛminyumuhi nɛ a ngɛ blɔ nya nitsumihi tsuo ngɛ Brooklyn ɔ na kaa e sa nɛ a gba he nɛ a fiaa womihi ngɛ ɔ mi. Kɛ je jehahi babauu nɛ be ɔ, a haya tsu nɛ ngɛ 18 Concord Street ɔ, se tsu nɛ ɔ be nyɛe maa su ha tsɔsemi nɛ a hia a. Nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ he zugba kpo ko ngɛ Adams Street, he nɛ kɛ je tsu nɛ a ngɛ mi amlɔ nɛ ɔ he kɛ ya lejɛ ɔ be tsɔe. A bɔni tsu nɛ ngɛ tsu hiɔwe kpo kpaanyɔ nɛ a kɛ amaga kɛ simɛnti ma mami, nɛ enɛ ɔ ha nɛ he nɛ a ma tsu nitsumi ngɛ ɔ he bɔ he kɛ ya bɔba enyɔ mi.

Tsu nɛ ɔ nɛ a ma ngɛ 117 Adams Street ɔ, a ya hi mi ngɛ 1927 mi, nɛ lɔ ɔ ha nɛ hwooli babauu ba. Jeha nɛ ma a mi ɔ, nɔyami babauu kɛ asafo ɔ mi he nɛ a ya nɔ nɛ a tsu ɔ maa ya nɔ, nɛ e maa dla Mawu we bi ɔmɛ kɛ to konɛ a sa nɛ a ngɔ biɛ nɛ ji Yehowa Odasefohi.

Nihi nɛ a hyɛɔ e nɔ ɔ a kpɛti nɔ kake de ke, “E ngɛ mi kaa e be he wami mohu lɛɛ, se John ya nɔ nɛ e pee nɔ ko nɛ ngɛ bua jɔmi kɛ kɔni nina babauu.” John níhi a si kpami ɔ tsɔɔ kaa sinami su haa nɛ nɔ náa bua jɔmi kɛ kɔni nina.

4 Sida nɛ a ná we ke a kpa si ɔ ngɛ hɛdɔ. Nihi nɛ a nɛ buɔ nɔ nɔ́ ɔ pɔɔ nɔ́ yayami kɛkɛ he susumi. Baiblo ɔ de ke: “Ni nɛmɛ nɛ a ngɛ nɔ́ nae ɔ a ligbihi tsuo yeɔ he yemi.” (Abɛ 15:15) Be komɛ ɔ, wɔ tsuo waa kpaa ka komɛ a he nɛ ma nyɛ ma ha nɛ waa jɔ̃. Waa nyɛ we be fɛɛ be nɛ wa ma tsi ka nɛ wa kpaa a he, se waa nyɛɔ nɛ wa tsuɔ bɔ nɛ wa ma pee wa ní ngɛ he ɔ he ní. Ke wa he nɛ wa ma jɔ̃ ɔ pɔɔ wa tsui he nɔ́ nɛ ngɛ wa si himi mi ɔ he susumi kɛkɛ nɔ ɔ, daa ligbi maa pee “yayami,” loo yimi. Juɛmi kɛ he numi kɛ nɔ́ kaa kikɛ nɛ ɔ jiɔ nɔ he bua jɔmi, ejakaa e haa nɛ nɔ hí nɛ e na ní kpakpahi nɛ ngɛ wa si himi mi.

4. Mɛni heje nɛ sinɔ he buɛ sinɔ yɔɔ oslaa?

5 Sinɔ nya siemi hemi kɛ su hi ha wɔ. Sinɔ nya siemi haa nɛ wa bua jɔɔ wawɛː. Abɛ 15:15 fã nɛ ji enyɔ ɔ de ke: “Nɔ nɛ e tsui jɔ e he ɔ yeɔ gbiɛ be fɛɛ be.” Nɔ nɛ ngɛ “tsui nɛ jɔ e he” ɔ pɛsɛpɛsɛɔ ní kpakpahi a mi—níhi nɛ e daa nɔ nɛ e sinaa ngɛ a he ɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, ní yajahi nɛ ngɛ e si himi mi ɔ jeɔ e he. Jamɛatsɛ ɔ sinɔ nya siemi kɛ juɛmi kpakpa nɛ e hɛɛ ɔ haa nɛ e bua jɔɔ pe kulaa, nɛ e nuɔ he kaa nɔ nɛ ngɛ “gbiɛ yeoe be fɛɛ be.”

Maa O Hemi Kɛ Yemi Ɔ Nɔ́ Kpua Nɔ

BENƐ o kɛ Yehowa Odasefohi kase Baiblo ɔ, mɛni ha nɛ o ná nɔmí nami kaa o na “hemi kɛ yemi kake” ɔ—jami kake pɛ nɛ da nɛ Mawu kplɛɛ nɔ? (Efe. 4:4-6) Anɛ suɔmi nɛ o na ngɛ Yehowa we bi a kpeti ɔ nɛ ha nɛ o hɛ kɛ su a he lo? Anɛ a je mi bami kpakpa a ha nɛ o bu mɛ lo? Nihi babauu baa anɔkuale ɔ mi ejakaa Mawu blɔ nya tomi kpe ɔ haa nɛ a nuɔ he kaa a ngɛ slɔkee nɛ a suɔ mɛ ngɛ anɔkuale mi, nɛ lɔɔ da. Suɔmi kɛ je mi bami kpakpa ji anɔkuale jami kadimí nɔhi.—Yoh. 13:35; 1 Pet. 2:12.

Se kɛ́ henemeli he yemi no ko tɔ̃ o nɔ, aloo e gu blɔ kpa ko nɔ kɛ kpo jemi nɔ́ kɛ tsɔ̃ɔ kaa e be suɔmi kɛ ha mo hu nɛɛ? Aloo kɛ́ henemeli he yemi no ko ya piɛɛ yayami ngua ko he—kɛ ya si po je gbaku je mi bami, kaa bɔ nɛ e ba ngɛ no ko he ngɛ kekleke biɛ asafo ɔ mi ngɛ Korinto ɔ nɛɛ? (1 Kor. 5:1) Kɛ́ o hemi kɛ yemi ɔ da pɛ kɛ kpe bɔ nɛ ni kpahi a ní peepeehi haa nɛ o nuɔ he ha a nɔ ɔ, e ma ngɛ sipi nɛ ma be e he nɔ. Anɛ sipi ko ngɛ nɛ ma ngɛ he kɛ ha o hemi kɛ yemi ɔ pe jã lo?

HEMIKƐYELI SISI TOHI NƐ MA SHI KPO NƐ MA KPELEKPELE

O ma nyɛ maa ma o hemi kɛ yemi ɔ nɔ si kɛ gu nɔ́ nɛ o heɔ ye kɛ ma a nɔ womi nɛ o maa kase kɛ je Baiblo ɔ mi anɔkwale nɛ a he hia nɛ o kase ɔ mi. Kɛ lɔ ɔ ma nyɛ maa ye bua ha kɛɛ? Mo susu nɔhyɛnɔ ko he nɛ o hyɛ.

Be ko ɔ, Yesu kaselihi fuu kua lɛ. E munyi nɛ kɔɔ e helo nɛ a maa ye kɛ e muɔ nɛ a maa nu he ɔ tɔ a nane. A de ke: “Munyi nɛ ɔ gbisiɔ nɔ.” Nɔ́ nɛ Yesu de ɔ dɔ mɛ babauu nɛ e ha nɛ a kpa lɛ nyɛɛmi.—Yohane 6:56-60, 66.

Enɛ ɔ se ɔ, Yesu bi e bɔfo ɔmɛ ke: “Nyɛ hu nyɛ sume kaa nyɛ ma ya aloo?” Petro ha lɛ heto ke: “Nyɔmtsɛ, mɛnɔ ngɔ wa ma ya? Mo nɛ o ngɛ neneene wami munyu ɔmɛ. Wa he ye, nɛ wa ba le hu kaa mo ji Mawu Klɔuklɔu no ɔ.”—Yohane 6:67-69.

Mɛni ha nɛ Petro kɛ bɔfo kpa amɛ e kpa kɛ je nihi nɛ a nane tɔ̃ ɔ a he? A hemi kɛ yemi ɔ ma si ngɛ nɔ́ ko nɛ he wa pe a henumihi a nɔ. Petro de ke: “Wɔɔ lɛɛ wa he ye, nɛ wa ba le kaa mo ji Mawu Klɔuklɔu Nɔ ɔ.” Anɔkputsɛmɛ nɛ ɔmɛ le nɔ́ nɛ a he ye kɛ nɔ́ heje nɛ a he ye. Benɛ a kɛ Yesu munyahi kɛ e nínɛɛhi kɛ Ngmami ɔ to he ɔ, a ná nɔmi mami kaa Yesu ji Mesia a. A nɔmi mami kpekpɛɛkpe ngɛ anɔkuale titli nɛ ɔ mi ɔ ha nɛ a hemi kɛ yemi ɔ ma si kpindii benɛ ni kpahi a nane tɔ̃ ngɛ nɔ́ nɛ Yesu de ɔ he.

Mɛni nɔ́ sane nɛ ɔ tsɔɔ wɔ? Nɔ́ nɛ wa le ɔ ma nyɛ maa pee hewamitsɛ pe bɔ nɛ waa nuɔ he ha. Mɛni heje? Ejakaa Baiblo mi tsɔɔmihi—kɛ anɔkualehi nɛ wa kase kɛ kɔ Mawu biɛ ɔ, e yi mi tomi ɔ, kɛ kpɔmi nɔ́ ɔ he ɔ—tsakee we gblegbleegle. E tsɔɔ we kaa wa henumɔhi ɔ, nɔ́ nɛ nihi peeɔ aloo nɔ́ nɛ a deɔ ɔ sɛɛ we Baiblo mi anɔkualehi a he.

Hi Blɔ Kpakpa a Nɔ

Moo susu kaa o ya hi bɔsu ko nɛ ma kɛ mo ma ya he ko nɛ he ngɛ fɛu nɛ kɛ je he nɛ o ngɛ ɔ kɛ wehi babauu nɔ. Blɔ hluu nɛ o ma hia a mi ɔ, eko ɔ, o ma ná hɛnɔ nyɛmiba slɔɔtoslɔɔtohi babauu—titli ɔ, bua jɔmi kaa o ngɛ blɔ ɔ hiae.

Be nɛ o ngɛ blɔ hluilɔ nɛ ɔ nɔ ɔ, nibɔhi ma nyɛ maa ya nɔ nɛ a ma hao mo—ekoɔ lɛɛ blɔhilitɔhi enyɔ komɛ nɛ a ngɛ munyu tue gbagbagba aloo blɔlɔ ɔ po nɛ be bumi. Se lɔ ɔ ekoeko tsakee we anɔkuale nɛ e ngɛ mi kaa o ngɛ bɔs nɛ da a mi, nɛ e sa kaa o hi mi konɛ o nyɛ nɛ o ya su he nɛ o yaa a.

Yehowa Gbaa Hemi Kɛ Yemi Nɔ́

Emma tsuɔ ní ngɛ gbɔldalɔhi a tsu kpekpemi ko mi ligbi etɛ ngɛ otsi mi, ngmlɛwa nɛɛ ngɛ ligbi kake mi. E bi e nitsumlɔ tsɔɔlɔ ɔ ligbi kake gbɔjɔɔ konɛ e nyɛ nɛ e ya kpe ngua a. Mohu nɛ e nitsumlɔ tsɔɔlɔ ɔ maa kplɛɛ e bimi ɔ nɔ ɔ, e tsake e nitsumi be ɔ kɛ ya ligbi lɔɔwe ngɛ otsi mi nɛ e gba le nya: Kplɛɛ nitsumi be he ɔ nɔ jio, koo kpa nitsumi ɔ jio. Akɛnɛ Emma he Yehowa nɔ e yee heje ɔ, e hla kaa e maa kpa nitsumi ɔ nɛ e ya kpe ɔ.

Ben Emma ya nɛ e ya ngɔ e he jemi womi ɔ kɛ ha a, gbɔtodoli a we ɔ nɔ hyɛlɔ ngua a ngɛ lejɛ ɔ. Benɛ e le níhi a he sane ɔ, e wo si kaa e maa tsu sane ɔ he ní. Se kɛ̃ ɔ, otsi enyɔ se ɔ, a de Emma kaa a fia lɛ gbi. E kɛ nɔ hyɛlɔ ngua a kpe ekko, nɛ e mi mi fu kaa a gbe e blɔ tsɔɔmi ɔmɛ a guɔ. E tsu sane ɔ he ní, nɛ a ha Emma be he: daa otsi ligbi enumɔ, daa ligbi ngmlɛfĩa nɛɛkɛ eywiɛ. Enɛ ɔ ba pee kpakpa pe nɛ e ngɛ kekleekle ɔ po; e ha nɛ e nyɛ tsu e Mawu jami nitsumi ɔmɛ!
