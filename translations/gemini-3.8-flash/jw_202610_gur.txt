A century ago

The January 1, 1926 Watch Tower said, “it will not be long before God will cause everyone to know his name. He will have witnesses on this earth.”

Starting from the year 1926, Jehovah, through Christ the head of the congregation, has been helping Christians realize that they must give priority to the Kingdom preaching work. They adjust how we conduct our studies and also teach Christians how they should conduct themselves. (Mt. 24:45) Christ has also helped them to acquire sound cars and houses to use in the Kingdom preaching work.

THEY PUBLISHED A NEW BOOK AND CHANGED HOW OUR TEACHING IS DONE

In the month of April, The Golden Age magazine (now called Awake!) stated that they would produce a new book entitled Deliverance. Therefore, they would no longer use the book Studies in the Scriptures in the Kingdom preaching. They would now use the Deliverance book to teach. Because they wanted to print many copies of the book, Bethel bought a machine that could print 22 books in one minute. The Bible Students would then give the book to everyone they preached to.

The Bible Students used the Studies in the Scriptures books for congregation study for many years. But beginning in the month of May, they started using study articles in The Watch Tower for study in the congregations. How did they benefit?

One group wrote a letter to thank Brother Rutherford. They said, “every week we study at home the materials the congregation gives us. We are very glad that it has helped us to understand things better.”

As the Bible Students came to a clearer understanding of matters, it helped them to be zealous in preaching about God’s Kingdom. For example, the booklet “Character or Covenant—Which?” stated that “it is important to strive to have good character, to do good to people and to respect people. But what is most important is for us to do the things that show that we are true Christians.” The booklet further stated: “Jehovah gave Christians the command to be his witnesses. Therefore, if Christians love Jehovah, they will obey this command of his and joyfully preach the good news.”

One tract entitled “Sacrifice and Service” stated that if Christians love Jehovah, they will worship him in truth. The tract also urged the Bible Students to dedicate themselves to preaching the good news to everyone.—Isa. 43:10, 12; Mt. 24:14.

The radio made the announcement about the books immediately. A few weeks later, The Watch Tower of July 15, 1926, was released. It said: “The title of the book is ‘Cha-

THEY ENCOURAGED THE BRETHREN TO GATHER TO PREACH THE GOOD NEWS

At the end of May, many Bible Students attended a convention in London, England. At that time, the printers in Britain were on strike. On Saturday, May 29, the brothers began using the two leaflets in the work. On that day, the congregation encouraged the brothers, saying: “Every Bible student here is a preacher of Jesus and the Kingdom. Go from here to Alexandra Palace, and the brothers there will show you what you should do.”

Over 100,000 tracts were distributed. The Watch Tower of July 15, 1926, said: “All the brethren were deeply rejoiced, realizing they were Jehovah’s mouthpieces in publishing the good news.”

One day, Brother Rutherford gave a speech at the Royal Albert Hall and spoke about what would happen to those who have power in this world. A few minutes after the doors of the building were opened, up to 13,000 people came and filled the meeting place. For those who could not enter the building, speakers were placed outside for them to listen to the meeting.

In Brother Rutherford’s speech, he showed that the governments cannot succeed because they have rejected Jehovah and his Kingdom. Brother Rutherford also said that the League of Nations would cease to exist.
