Xexeame Katã ƒe Amegbetɔ ƒe Gomenɔamesiwo Ŋu Nyamedegblɔɖi

Ŋgɔdonya

Esi wònye be amegbetɔ ƒomea me tɔwo katã ƒe gomekpɔkpɔ le bubu, gome sɔsɔ kple gome makpemakpe me nye ablɔɖe, nu dzɔdzɔe wɔwɔ kple ŋutifafa ƒe gɔmeɖoanyi le xexeame ta la,

Esi wònye be amegbetɔ ƒe gomenɔamesiwo gbɔ mã kple wo ŋu vlododo he ŋutasesẽwɔwɔ siwo do dziku na amegbetɔwo katã ƒe dzitsinya vɛ, eye wònye be xexeame si me amegbetɔwo ase vivi na nuƒoƒo kple dzixɔse ƒe ablɔɖe, kpakple vɔvɔ̃ kple hiã me ablɔɖe va zu didi kɔkɔtɔ kekeake na amesiame ta la,

Esi wòhiã be se nanɔ anyi ahe amegbetɔ ƒe gomekpɔkpɔwo ta bene amegbetɔ maganɔ teƒe aɖeke aɖe mɔ kplikpaa be wòadze aglã ɖe ŋutasesẽ kple anyidzidzi ŋuti o ŋuti la,

Esi wòle vevie be woado xɔlɔ̃wɔwɔ ɖe ŋgɔ le dukɔwo dome la,

Elabena Xexeame Dukɔ Ƒoƒuawo me tɔwo gbugbɔ woƒe xɔse ɖo kpe edzi le Se Gã la me le amegbetɔ ƒe gomenɔamesi veviwo ŋu, le amegbetɔ ƒe bubu kple asixɔxɔ ŋu, kple ŋutsu kple nyɔnu ƒe gomenɔamesi sɔsɔwo ŋu, eye woɖoe kplikpaa be yewoado hadomeŋgɔyiyi kple agbenɔnɔ ƒe ɖoƒe nyuitɔwo ɖe ŋgɔ le ablɔɖe gãtɔ me,

Esi Dukɔ siwo le Xexeame Dukɔ Ƒoƒuawo me la do ŋugbe bena, yewoaɖo ɖekawɔwɔ kple Xexeame Dukɔ Ƒoƒuawo be amegbetɔ ƒe gomenɔamesiwo kple ablɔɖe veviwo nanɔ te ɖe anyi bliboe la ta,

Esi wònye be gɔmesese ɖeka kple sidzedze ablɔɖevinyenye kple ablɔɖemenɔnɔ siawo nye nu vevi aɖe na ŋugbedodo sia gbɔ ɖoɖo bliboe la ŋuti,

Eyata, fifia la,

Dukɔ Ƒoƒuawo Ƒe Takpekpe Gã

ɖe gbeƒã Amegbetɔ ƒe Gomekpɔkpɔwo Ƒe Xexeme Kpeɖodzinya sia be enye taɖodzinu ɖeka si dukɔwo katã kple amewo katã aɖo gbɔ, kple susu be amesiame kple habɔbɔ ɖesiaɖe si le ame dome, siwo aɖo Kpeɖodzinya sia ɖe dzi me ɣesiaɣi la, nado vevie nu to nufiafia kple hehenana me be woabu gomekpɔkpɔ kple ablɔɖe siawo, eye to afɔɖeɖe siwo ayi ŋgɔ le dukɔwo me kple xexeame katã me la, woakpɔ egbɔ be xexeme katã nanya wo hena wo dzi pɛpɛpɛ, le Dukɔ siwo nye Habɔbɔa me tɔwo ƒe amewo ŋutɔwo dome kple anyigba siwo le woƒe dziɖuɖu te la ƒe amewo dome hã.

Kplɔ̃ɖoɖo 1

Amegbetɔwo katã le ablɔɖe me vana, eye wosɔ le bubu kple gomekpɔkpɔ me. Susu kple dzitsinya le wo si, eye wòle be woawɔ nu ɖe wo nɔewo gbɔ le nɔviwɔwɔ ƒe gbɔgbɔ me.

Kplɔɖoɖo 2

Amesiame kpɔ mɔ kple ablɔɖe siwo katã ŋu wowo nu tsoe le Gbeƒãɖeɖe sia me la ƒe gome, tovoɖeɖe aɖeke manɔmee le to, ŋutigbale ƒe amadede, ŋutsu alo nyɔnu nyenye, gbegbɔgblɔ, subɔsubɔ, dunyahehe alo susuwɔwɔ bubu, dukɔ alo hadome dzɔtsoƒe, kesinɔnu, dzidzi alo nɔnɔme bubu ɖeke gome o.

Azɔ hã la, womawɔ vovototo aɖeke le dunyaheha, ʋɔnudrɔ̃ƒe alo dukɔwo dome gomenɔamesi si le dukɔ alo anyigbamama si me tɔ amea nye ŋu o, eɖanye dukɔ si le eɖokui si, esi le ametapepɛ te, esi mele eɖokui dzi ɖum o, alo esi dziɖulanyenye le seɖoƒe aɖe te o.

Kpukpui 3

Amesiame kpɔ gbeɖiɖi be yeanɔ agbe, aɖe dzi ɖe le, eye yeanɔ dedie le ye ɖokui me.

Kpukpui 4

Womadzi kluvi alo subɔvi ɖe ame aɖeke dzi o; woade se ɖe kluvisisi kple kluwitsatsa nu le woƒe nɔnɔmewo katã me.

Kplɔ̃ɖoɖo 5

Wometɔ amedzidzenu aɖeke dzi awɔ funyafunya ame alo awɔ ŋlɔmi, ŋutasesẽ kple amedomebɔbɔ ƒe nuwɔna alo ahe to na ame alea o.

Kpukpui 6

Amesiame kpɔ mɔ be woabu ye le afisiafi abe amesi dze le se la ŋkume ene.

Kpukpui 7

Amewo katã sɔ le se la ŋkume, eye vovototodedeameme aɖeke manɔmee la, se la ɖe mɔ na wo katã be wòakpɔ wo ta le mɔ sɔsɔ nu. Ame sia ame kpɔ mɔ be woakpɔ ye ta tso vovototodedeameme ɖe sia ɖe si nye gbegblẽ le Nyagbɔgblɔ sia ŋu gbɔ, kple ŋusẽdoame ɖe vovototodedeameme ma tɔgbi ŋu la me.

Kpukpui 8

Amesiame kpɔ gome be dukɔa ƒe ʋɔnudrɔ̃ƒe siwo dze la nakpɔ nya siwo gblẽ gomenɔamesi vevi siwo dukɔa ƒe dukplɔse alo se bubu aɖe na ye la gbɔ nɛ nyuie.

Kpukpui 9

Womeɖe mɔ be woalé ame aɖeke, ade eya ɖe gaxɔ me alo anyae le dukɔ me le amedokui tɔƒe o.

Kpukpui 10

Amesiame kpɔ mɔ kple gomeblamanyi blibo be ʋɔnudrɔ̃ƒe si le eɖoksi hedrɔ̃a ʋɔnu le dzoɖiɖi kple dzɔdzɔenyenye me la nadrɔ̃ yeƒe nya le dutoƒo le dzɔdzɔenyenye me, le yeƒe gomenɔamesiwo kple dɔdeasiwo gbɔkpɔkpɔ me, eye le hlɔ̃dodome nya ɖesiaɖe si woatsɔ ɖe eŋuti la me.

Kpukpui 11

Ame sia ame si wotsɔ nya ɖe eŋu be ewɔ nu vɔ̃ aɖe la, gome le esi be woabu eŋu be menye fɔɖitɔ o vaseɖe esime woaɖo kpe edzi be enye fɔɖitɔ le se nu le ʋɔnutɔtrɔ le dutoƒo me, afisi woana kpeɖodzi siwo katã hiã hena eƒe taɖeɖe lae le.

Womekpɔ ame aɖeke be enye fɔbula le vodada aɖe si wòwɔ ta, ne vodada ma menye fɔɖiɖi le dukɔa alo dukɔwo dome se nu le ɣeyiɣi si wòwɔe me o. Nenema ke womado tohehee sesẽ aɖe nɛ wu esi woate ŋu ado nɛ le ɣeyiɣi si me wòwɔ vodada la me o.

Kpodɔkpo 12

Mèɖe tɔ kura o be woa do alɔ ame aɖeke ƒe nya me le vome o, alo a do alɔ eƒe ƒome, eƒe aƒe alo eƒe lɛtawo me o, eye wo ma gblẽ eƒe bubu kple ŋkɔ hã me o. Gome le amesiame si be se na kpɔ eta tso alɔdodo alo ŋukpenana siawo me.

Kpukpui 13

Amesiame kpɔ gome le ablɔɖe me nɔnɔ kple mɔzɔzɔ me le dukɔ ɖesiaɖe ƒe liƒowo dome.

Amesiame kpɔ gbeɖuɖu be yeadzo le dukɔ ɖesiaɖe me, eya ŋutɔ tɔ hã le eme, eye wòatrɔ agbɔ va eƒe dukɔ me.

Kpukpui 14

Amesiame kpɔ gome be wòadi sitsoƒe eye wòakpɔ dzidzeme le sitsoƒe ma me le dukɔ bubuwo me ne woti eyome le funyafunyawɔwɔ ta.

Gomekpɔkpɔ sia maganyo na ame si wole yometi tom le nuvɔ̃ siwo mesɔ kple dunyahenunya o alo nuwɔna siwo tsi tre ɖe Xexeame Kɔtɔ Dukɔ Ƒoƒuawo ƒe tameɖoɖowo kple gɔmeɖosewo ŋu la me o.

Kpukpui 15

Amesiame kpɔ gome le dukɔmevinyenye ŋu.

Womanaɖe dukɔmenɔnɔ gome le ame aɖeke si kpoo o, eye womagbe gomekpɔkpɔ le dukɔmenɔnɔ tɔtrɔ me nɛ hã o.

Kpukpui 16

Ŋutsu kple nyɔnu siwo tsi ɖe edzi, to vovo na tɔtrɔ aɖeke le to, dukɔmevinyenye alo subɔsubɔha gome o, kpɔ gblɔe be yewoade srɔ̃ eye yewoatu ƒome ɖo. Gomenɔamesi sɔsɔe le wo si le srɔ̃ɖeɖe me, le srɔ̃ɖeɖe me nɔnɔ me, kple ne srɔ̃ɖeɖe la gblẽ.

Srɔ̃ɖeɖe ate ŋu adze egɔme le ame siwo le eɖe ge la ƒe lɔlɔ̃nu faa kple woƒe didi blibo ko dzi.

Ƒome la nye hadome ƒe dzɔdzɔme kple gɔmeɖokpe ƒuƒoƒo, eye dɔ le hadome kple Dukɔa dzi be woakpɔ eta.

Kpukpui 17

Ame sia ame kpɔ gome be wòatɔ ŋutɔ ƒe aɖaŋuɖoɖo alo nunɔamesi nanɔ esi ɖeka alo eya kple ame bubuwo siaa.

Womatsɔ ame aɖeke ƒe nunɔamesiwo le esi le mɔ maɖɔŋu dzi o.

Kpukpui 18

Amesiame kpɔ gome le susuŋudɔwɔwɔ, dzitsinya kple subɔsubɔ ƒe ablɔɖe me; gomekpɔkpɔ sia lɔ eƒe subɔsubɔ alo dzixɔse trɔtrɔ ɖe eme, kple ablɔɖe be wòate ŋu aɖe eƒe subɔsubɔ alo dzixɔse afia le nufiafia, nuwɔna, subɔsubɔ kple kɔnuwo wɔwɔ me, eɖeɖe alo le amewo dome, le gaglãgbe alo le adzame.

Kpukpui 19

Amesiame kpɔ gome le susuwɔwɔ kple egbɔgblɔ ƒe ablɔɖe me; gomekpɔkpɔ sia lɔ ablɔɖe be woanɔ susuwɔwɔ me manɔmee kple xexedome, kpakple nyatakakawo kple susuwo didi, xɔxɔ kple gbɔgblɔ to mɔnu ɖesiaɖe dzi kple liƒowo ƒe manyamanya me.

Kplɔ̃ɖoɖo 20

Amesiame kpɔ gome le ablɔɖe me be wòakpɔ gome le takpekpe kple habɔbɔ siwo me ŋutifafa le la me.

Womate ŋu azi ame aɖeke dzi be wòanye habɔbɔ aɖe me tɔ o.

Kpukpui 21

Amesiame kpɔ gome be wòakpɔ gome le yeƒe dukɔ ƒe dziɖuɖu me, tẽ alo to amesiwo woti faa teƒenɔlawo dzi.

Amesiame kpɔ gome sɔsɔe be wòakpɔ gome le dutoƒodɔwɔwɔwo me le eƒe dukɔ me.

Dukɔa ƒe lɔlɔ̃nu ye anye dziɖuɖu ƒe ŋusẽ ƒe gɔmeɖoanyi; woaɖe lɔlɔ̃nu sia fia to tiatia siwo woawɔ le ɣeyiɣi ɖoɖiwo dzi eye wòanye nyateƒetɔ me, si anye to amesiame ƒe gomekpɔkpɔ kple sɔsɔme me, eye woawɔe to akɔdada le adzame alo to tiatiawɔwɔ faa ƒe mɔnu siwo sɔ kplii me.

Kpukpui 22

Amesiame, abe hadomegotɔ ene la, gome le esi be hadomedekadodo nanɔ anyi na ye, eye wòle be woawɔe be gaŋgɔyiyi, hadomegbenɔnɔ kple dekɔnuwo ƒe gomenɔamesi siwo hiã vevie na eƒe bubu kple eƒe amenyenye ƒe ablɔɖe me dzidziɖedzi la nava eme nɛ to dukɔa ƒe agbagbadzedze kple dukɔwo dome nuwɔwɔ aduadowo dzi, le dukɔ ɖesiaɖe ƒe ɖoɖowo kple nunɔamesiwo nu.

Kpodɔkpo 23

Amesiame kpɔ gome be yeawɔ dɔ, atia dɔ si dɔwɔla di la faa, dɔwɔnyawo nanɔ nyuie eye woasɔ, kple be woakpɔ ye ta tso dɔmakpɔwɔ ƒe nɔnɔme me.

Amesiame kpɔ gome be yeaxɔ fetu sɔsɔe ɖe dɔ sɔsɔe ta, le vovototodedeameme aɖeke manɔmee.

Ame sia ame si wɔa dɔ la gblɔ fetu si sɔ eye dze eƒe dɔwɔwɔ teƒe, si ana eya kple eƒe ƒomea nànɔ agbe si dze amegbetɔ ƒe bubu, eye woatsɔ hadomegbenɔnɔ ƒe takpɔkpɔ bubuwo akpe ɖe eŋu ne ehiã.

Amesiame kpɔ mɔɖeɖe be wòaɖo dɔwɔlawo ƒe habɔbɔ alo age ɖe eka me hena eƒe didiwo ta kpɔkpɔ.

Kpukpui 24

Amesiame kpɔ gbeɖiɖi kple gbɔɖeme ƒe gbe, si me dɔwɔƒoƒowo ƒe seɖoƒe si sɔ kple mɔkeke siwo woxɔna le ɣeyiɣi aɖewo me kple fetuxɔxɔ hã le.

Kpukpui 25

Amesiame kpɔ gome le agbenɔnɔ ɖoɖo si sɔ hena eya kple eƒe ƒometɔwo ƒe lãmesẽ kple dzidzemegbenɔnɔ me, si me nuɖuɖu, awudodo, xɔtutu kple atikeyɔyɔ kpakple hadomekpekpeɖeŋu hiãhiãwo le, kple gomekpɔkpɔ le dedienɔnɔ me ne dɔwɔwɔ mele anyi o, dɔléle, lankpekpe, ahositsitsi, tsigbɔdzɔe alo agbenɔnɔ ƒe nu hiãhiãwo bubu bu le go siwo dzi maɖu o me.

Vidada kple ɖevi me nɔnɔ dze na beléle kple kpekpeɖeŋu tɔxɛ. Ðevi sia ɖevi, eɖanye srɔ̃ɖeɖe me wòdze le alo le godo o, akpɔ hadome takpɔkpɔ ma ke.

Kplɔ̃ɖoɖo 26

Amewo katã kpɔ gome le sukudede me. Sukudede anye femaxee, vevietɔ le gɔmedzesuku kple suku gɔmedzetɔwo me. Gɔmedzesukudede anye dɔ si dzi wòle be amesiame nawɔ godoo. Asitsanyawo kple aɖaŋudɔwo fiafia anye nu si bɔ na amesiame eye suku kɔkɔ dede anye nu si asu amesiame si sɔsɔe le amesiame ƒe ŋutete nu.

Hehenana adze be wòahe amegbetɔ ƒe amenyenye blibo la va do goe eye wòado ŋusẽ bubudede amegbetɔ ƒe gomenɔamesiwo kple gɔmedzesesẽblamasiwo ŋu. Ele be wòado gɔmesese, dzigbɔɖi kple xɔlɔ̃wɔwɔ ɖe ŋgɔ le dukɔwo katã, towo alo subɔsubɔha vovovowo dome, eye wòado Dukɔ Ƒoƒuawo ƒe dɔwɔnawo ɖe ŋgɔ hena ŋutifafa me nɔnɔ.

Dzidzilawoe wòdze be woatso nya me gbã le hehenana ƒomevi si woana woviwo ŋu.

Kpukpui 27

Amesiame kpɔ mɔ fãa be wòakpɔ gome le eƒe duametɔwo ƒe dekɔnuwo me, asea vivi na aɖaŋuwo kple nutatawo, eye wòakpɔ gome le dzɔdzɔmeŋutinunya ƒe ŋgɔyiyi kple eƒe viɖewo me.

Amesiame kpɔ mɔɖeɖe be woakpɔ yeƒe susumenugblɔɖi kple ŋutilãmenu siwo tso dzɔdzɔmeŋutinunya, agbalẽŋɔŋlɔ alo aɖaŋunuwɔwɔ ƒe dɔwɔwɔ ɖesiaɖe si ƒe agbalẽŋlɔla wònye la ta nɛ.

Kpekpeɖeŋununya 28

Amesiame kpɔ gome le hadomegbenɔnɔ kple dukɔwo dome ɖoɖo aɖe me, afisi woate ŋu aɖe gomekpɔkpɔ kple ablɔɖe siwo ŋu wofo nu tsoe le Gbeƒãɖeɖe sia me la afia bliboe le.

Kpukpui 29

Dɔdeasiwo le ame sia ame dzi na kɔƒe alo dukɔ si me ɖeɖeko eƒe ameŋutɔnyenye ate ŋu atsi blibo le ablɔɖe me le la.

Le eƒe gomenɔamesiwo kple ablɔɖewo zazã me la, ame sia ame anɔ se ƒe seɖoƒewo te ko, siwo dzi woɖo ko kple susu be woade bubu kple sidzedze si dze ame bubuwo ƒe gomenɔamesiwo kple ablɔɖewo ŋu, eye woaɖo agbenyuinɔnɔ, dutoƒoŋutifafa kple amesiame ƒe nyonyo ƒe biahiã siwo sɔ gbɔ le demokrasidukɔ aɖe me.

Gomenɔamesi kple ablɔɖe siawo zazã magblɔ̃ le mɔ aɖeke nu le Xexeame Ƒe Dukɔ Ƒoƒuawo Ƒe tameɖoɖowo kple gɔmeɖosewo ŋu o.

Kpukpui 30

Nane aɖeke mele Kpeɖodzi Nuŋɔŋlɔ sia me si woate ŋu agɔme ɖe mɔ aɖe nu be ena mɔɖeɖe dukɔ, habɔbɔ alo ame aɖe be wòawɔ nu aɖe alo aɖe afɔ aɖe si ate ŋu agblẽ gomekpɔkpɔ kple ablɔɖe siwo katã ŋu nya wogblɔ le afi sia la dometɔ aɖeke me o.
