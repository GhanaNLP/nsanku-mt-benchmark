SƐNEA ƆKƆTƆ NYAA N’AKYI BƆTƆ, NA ABEREWA NYAA TIKƆKƆƆ (TIHWI)

Ɔbaa bi woo ne ba; sɛ ɔsoma no a, ɔse, "Merenkɔ." Ɔna no see no sɛ, "Ɛnde tena hɔ, wo ne wo nyansa." Dabiara sɛ ɔsoma no a, ɔse, "Merenkɔ." Ɛnna da koro bi ɔsee no sɛ, "Kɔsaw nsuo brɛ me." Ɔse, "Merenkɔ." Ɛnna aberewa no boroo no see no sɛ, "Tena hɔ na wobɛhunu." Ɛnna abofra no kaa sɛ, "Mɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ 'mɛhunu' ahu nea ɛteɛ." Ɛnna ɔfaa kwan no so, na ɔde mmirika kɛseɛ kɔe, yiridi, yiridi, yiridi. Akyire no ɔduruu akuraa bi ase, na ɔhunuu aberewa bi sɛ ɔte hɔ. Bere a ɔhunuu no no, na ɔrepɛ sɛ ɔsane n'akyi. Aberewa no see no sɛ, "Me nena, nsane w'akyi efisɛ mahunu wo." Na abofra no kɔɔ n'anim; ɛnna aberewa no bisaa no sɛ, "Adɛn nti na nsuo afiri w'ani agu wo so saa?" Ɔsee no sɛ, "Me ne me maame na ɛtete hɔ, da biara sɛ ɔsɔre a, ɔse, 'Tena hɔ na wobɛhunu,' na ɔboro me, enti mekae sɛ, mɛba abɛhwehwɛ 'mɛhunu' na mahunu nea ɛteɛ." Ɛnna aberewa no see no sɛ, "Mene no." Afei ɔsee no sɛ, "Kɔ efie no akyi hɔ kɔtu bayere brɛ me; sɛ wokɔduru hɔ a, deɛ ɛbɛka sɛ, 'Tu me, tu me, tu me' no, ntu no; na deɛ ɛbɛka sɛ, 'Ntu me, ntu me' no, ɛno na tu." Ɔbaa no gyee so sɛ, "Hoo!" Nokorɛm no ɔkɔɔ hɔ; bayerɛ no bi see no sɛ, "Tu me, tu me, tu me;" ebi nso see no sɛ, "Ntu me, ntu me, ntu me." Deɛ ɛkaa sɛ, "Ntu me" no, ɛno na ɔtuee. Na ɔde bae; ɛnna aberewa no see no sɛ, "Worɔ ho na to gu gya so." Na ɔworɔɔ ho to guu gya so, na bere a ɛben no, ɔguu so koraam, na ɔde kɔsii aberewa no anim. Ɛnna ɔsee no sɛ, "Kɔ kuro no ano, na hwɛ pata a nam gu so, hwɛ mu na fa deɛ wopɛ." Na abofra no faa bi bae, na ɔde guu gya so, yɛɛ nkwan. Bere a ɔwieeɛ no, ɔde sii aberewa no anim. Aberewa no see sɛ, "Woawie anaa?" Ɔbuae sɛ, "Aane, Nana." Ɔsee sɛ, "Bra bɛwɔ fufuo no." Na aberewa no kɔbutuu ne yafunu so butubutu; ɔsee sɛ, "Boaboa bayere a yanoa no ano, gu me hwenem, na wɔ mu." Abofra no see sɛ, "Adeɛ nwanwasoɔ nie, sɛ yɛbɛwɔ fufuo wɔ nipa hwenem; deɛ maame kaeɛ no ara nie." Yeinom nyinaa ɔkae wɔ n'adwene mu. Ɔkɔe kɔwɔe; nanso berɛ a ɔwɔe no, ɔwɔɔ no bɔkɔɔbɔkɔɔ pɛɛ. Aberewa no see sɛ, "Wɔ no denneennen, sɛ woanwɔ no denneennen a, wo bo bɛpaepae." Na ɔwɔɔ no, pi! pi! pi! na berɛ a ɔwieeɛ no, ɔguu nkwan no guu so, na ɔde sii aberewa no anim hɔ. Aberewa no see sɛ, "Me banana, bɔ me din na fa bi di." Na ɔno nso see sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ wo Aberewa-nana." Ɔsee sɛ, "Ee! ɔnhunu adeɛ a ɔbɛtumi adi." Na aberewa no wiee ne nyinaa di. Nna nwɔtwe mu no, ɔyɛɛ no saa ara berɛ biara. Na da koro bi abofra no kɔɔ asubɔnten mu kɔguaree nsuo. Kɔtɔ hunuu no, na ɔsee sɛ, "Adɛn nti na obi ba afɔn saa yi?" Ɔsee sɛ, "Aberewa-nana bi wɔ hɔ a me ne no te, na sɛ meno aduane ma no a, ɔse, 'Bɔ me din na fa bi di'; mese, 'Wɔfrɛ wo Aberewa-nana,' na ɔse, 'Ee! woanhunu kwan a wobɛfa so adi,' na afei wadi; ɛno nti na mafɔn no." Kɔtɔ no see sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ no Aberewa-Mosono." Abofra no see sɛ, "Ho." Na ɔde n'ahina sii ne tiri so. Kɔtɔ no see sɛ, "Berɛ a worekɔ no, mma wo werɛ mmfiri." Abofra no tuu kwan; berɛ a ɔrenam reba no nyinaa, ɔkɔɔ so kaa sɛ, "Aberewa-Mosono, Mosono, Mosono." Berɛ a ɔduruu kuro no ano no, ne werɛ firiiɛ, na ɔsuntiiɛ ma n'ahina bɔeɛ, na ɔkɔɔ fie. Aberewa no see sɛ, "Ahina no wɔ he?" Ɔsee sɛ, "Berɛ a mereba no, mesuntiiɛ, ma ɛbɔeɛ." Ɔsee sɛ, "Fa foforɔ na kɔ." Na ɔfaa bi kɔe; na Kɔtɔ no see sɛ, "Woasane aba dada?" Na ɔsee sɛ, "Din a wokyerɛɛ me no, makɔma me werɛ afiri, na masane aba sɛ merebɛbisa bio." Kɔtɔ no see sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ no Aberewa-Mosono." Na ɔsee sɛ, "Ho." Ɔkɔɔ so kaa kɔduruu fie, na berɛ a ɔduruu fie no ɔsii (ahina no) fam. Abrewa no kaa sɛ, "Kɔkɔtu bayerɛ na fa bra." Ɔyɛɛ biribiara sɛnea na wayɛ kane no, na ɔde aduane no bae, de sii abrewa no anim, na abrewa no kaa sɛ, "Bɔ me din na fa di." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wofre wo Abrewa-nana." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me din ankasa na merebisa." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo din ankasa?" Abrewa no kaa sɛ, "Aane." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wofre wo Abrewa-Mosono." Abrewa no kaa sɛ, "Fa di." Abofra no fa diee. Abrewa no de ne nsa too kwan so, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Merekɔhwehwɛ deɛ ɔkaa kyerɛɛ wo sɛ me din de Mosono no koraa." Na ɔkɔɔ asubɔnten no ho, na ɔkɔhyiaa Obonto Ya, mpatuawa no, (na ɔyeyaa no sɛ), "Wo 'Bonto, wo maame! wo 'Bonto, wo papa! wo na wokaa kyerɛɛ me nana ba kumaa no sɛ wɔfrɛ me Mosono?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ me o." Abrewa no hunuu sɛ Adwene gyina bɛn hɔ; ɔdomee no sɛ, "Wo Adwene, wo maame! maame! maame! wo Adwene, wo papa! papa! papa! wo na wokaa kyerɛɛ me nana ba kumaa no sɛ wɔfrɛ me Mosono?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ me o." Ɔhunuu sɛ Kɔtɔ gyina hɔ. Ɔdomee no sɛ, "Wo Kɔtɔ, wo maame! maame! maame! wo Kɔtɔ, wo papa! papa! papa! wo na wokaa kyerɛɛ me nana ba kumaa no sɛ mete Nkran, meyɛ dehyeɛ, na me din de Mosono?" Kɔtɔ kaa sɛ, "Aane, me na mekaa kyerɛɛ no, na adɛn nti na womaa obi ba fonoe saa?" Na abrewa no bo fuiɛ, na ne kora a ɔkura mu no, ɔma so na ɔtow de bɔɔ Kɔtɔ no. Ɛno nti na Kɔtɔ wɔ n'akyi apɔnkye no. Kane no na nsensaneeɛ biara nni Kɔtɔ akyi. Na Kɔtɔ nso tasee hyire, na ɔtow guu abrewa no ti so. Ɛno nti na mmerewa ti so wɔ dwen no.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, ebi nkɔ na ebi mmra.
