THIS IS THE FIRST CENTURY

“IT WILL not be long before God will make his name known to the people. Some people will be his witnesses on earth.” These words were stated in the January 1, 1926, Watchtower.

In 1926, Jehovah, through Jesus Christ, who is the head of the congregation, helped Christians understand that the preaching work should be the most important work in their lives. This caused the “faithful and discreet slave” to take up this work vigorously. For example, they printed publications, made adjustments to Christian meetings, and made all Christians aware of the specific work they were to do. (Matt. 24:45) With Christ’s help, buildings were constructed and they also acquired machines to print literature; this helped them to be able to carry out the preaching work.

A NEW PUBLICATION AND ADJUSTMENTS MADE TO THE MEETINGS

In April, the magazine called The Golden Age (now known as Awake!) announced the release of a new book entitled Deliverance. This book would replace the primary publication used in the preaching work at that time, which was Studies in the Scriptures (or Études des Écritures). Because the brothers wanted to print the new book in the required quantity, the Bethel workers purchased a printing press capable of printing 22 books per minute. The Bible Students made efforts to offer the new book to everyone they preached to.

The Bible Students studied the book Studies in the Scriptures at their meetings for many years. But beginning in May, they began studying articles published in The Watchtower. What benefits resulted from this?

One congregation wrote to Brother Rutherford, saying, “The Little Bunker Hill congregation sends greetings to you. On Sundays, we study the topics chosen by the organization for the congregations. As a result, when new explanations come out, we recognize them immediately. We are grateful for this.”

These “new clarifications” helped the Bible Students to be zealous in the preaching work. For example, an article made it clear that some people focused entirely on trying to cultivate good qualities such as kindness and humility, and they forgot the important work that true Christians should do. The article made it clear that Jehovah told Christians to be his witnesses. Therefore, because Christians love Jehovah, each of them should be ready to obey his command to preach.

Another article emphasized that true Christians serve Jehovah loyally to show that they love him. The article encouraged the Bible Students to exert themselves to preach zealously to as many people as possible.—Isa. 43:10, 12; Matt. 24:14.

CONVENTIONS ENCOURAGED GOD’S PEOPLE TO PREACH

At the end of May, the Bible Students attended a convention in London, England. At that time, all the printing trade unions in Britain were on strike. Therefore, the brothers at the Brooklyn branch worked many hours and printed 117,000 copies of a new booklet. This booklet was about God's guidance for people, and it was distributed to those who attended the convention.

On Saturday, May 29, the conventioners took to the streets to distribute the tract. The convention program that day encouraged the brothers: “Every Bible Student here is invited to preach Jesus and the Kingdom. So tomorrow morning, go to Alexandra Palace, and the brothers there will let you know what to do.”

More than 100,000 booklets were distributed. The July 15, 1926 Watchtower stated: “The brothers were very happy. They felt that they had carried out the work Jehovah gave them, which was to preach.”

The next day, Brother Rutherford gave a talk on the fate of world governments in the Royal Albert Hall. Shortly after the doors opened, 13,000 people filled the hall to capacity. Loudspeakers were placed outside so that those who could not get inside the hall could hear the words.

Brother Rutherford explained in his speech that the reason world governments failed was that they rejected Jehovah and His Kingdom. Brother Rutherford went on to say that the League of Nations would no longer exist.

NEW HOUSE BUILT ON ADAMS STREET

The number of people who needed our publications continued to increase, so the brothers in Brooklyn saw the need to build a larger printing plant. For many years, a building had been rented on Concord Street, where our publications were printed. But they could not print the large number of publications that people needed at this location because it was not big enough. When Jehovah’s Witnesses studied the Bible with you, what convinced you that you had found the “one faith” or the only way God wants us to worship him? (Eph. 4:4-6) Was it the love among Jehovah’s people that made you choose to associate with them? Or were you impressed by their fine conduct? Many people want to associate with God’s people because they feel loved and secure among them. This is not surprising, because love and fine conduct are the qualities that identify true Christians.—John 13:35; 1 Pet. 2:12.

What if a brother offends you or does not treat you well? Or what if a brother commits a shocking sin like the one committed by the man in the Corinthian congregation? (1 Cor. 5:1) If you were convinced that this is the truth solely because of the actions and words of people, then your faith would not stand firm. On what must you build your faith so that it stands firm?

WHAT YOU SHOULD BASE YOUR FAITH ON

Your faith will be strengthened if you are convinced of the truths you have learned in the Bible. How will this help you? Consider this example.

One day, many of Jesus' disciples turned away from him. What Jesus said about eating his flesh and drinking his blood shocked them so much that they decided not to follow him anymore. They said: “What hard speech this is.”—John 6:56-60, 66.

After this, Jesus asked his apostles: “Do you also want to go away?” Peter answered him: “Lord, whom shall we go away to? You have sayings of everlasting life. We have believed and have come to know that you are the Holy One of God.”—John 6:67-69.

Why were Peter and the other apostles not stumbled by Jesus’ words? It appears that their faith was built on something solid, not merely on what someone had said. Peter stated: “We have believed and have come to know that you are the Holy One of God.” These faithful ones knew what they believed and the reason they believed it. Comparing Jesus’ actions and words with what they had learned in the Scriptures, they were fully convinced that Jesus was the Messiah. Because they believed this vital truth, their faith was firm, and this prevented them from being stumbled by Jesus’ words as others were.

What can we learn from this event? If we know Bible truth well, it will help us not to stop serving Jehovah even if a fellow believer offends us. Why? Because the truths in the Bible, for example, God’s name, his purpose, and the ransom, will never change. People’s words or actions may hurt us, but those circumstances cannot change Bible truth in any way. Therefore, if we believe Bible teachings and are convinced of

Keep on the Path to Life

Imagine that you have boarded a bus and are traveling on a long journey to a beautiful place. Various things will make this trip enjoyable for you, especially as you keep in mind that you are going to a lovely destination.

However, while you are in the vehicle, some people are talking loudly, or even the driver is not treating people well. This can make you very angry. But this is the only vehicle that will take you to where you are going. Therefore, you just have to be patient and stay in it.

In the same way, we are all journeying toward God’s new world. And we are happy to be in his organization. Sometimes, however, the actions of our brothers can hurt us, or someone we trust may do something wrong that discourages us. Even so, no matter what someone does, this is the only organization that will lead us into God’s new world. Therefore, if we believe the Bible’s truth and trust the organization that taught us these truths, our faith will be firm and we will remain on this path of life.

EXPERIENCE

Jehovah Rewards Us When We Exercise Faith

A sister named Emma worked at a hotel nine hours a day for three days a week. She asked her supervisor for one day off so that she could attend one of our conventions. The supervisor did not permit her, but rather let her know that from that day on, she would work five days a week, and told her that if she did not agree to it, then she would have to quit the job. Emma was confident that Jehovah would take care of her, so she chose to quit the job so that she could attend the convention.

Emma wrote a letter resigning from the job. When she took the letter to the supervisor, she met the company director. The director found out what was going on and promised Emma that he would do something about the situation. However, two weeks later, the supervisor fired Emma from the job. Surprisingly, Emma met the company director again. The director was angry about the way the supervisor had fired Emma. The director handled the matter and arranged for Emma to work four hours every day, five days a week. This new arrangement suited Emma even better than before, because it gave her more time for the ministry! Jehovah cared for her needs even more than she had expected.
