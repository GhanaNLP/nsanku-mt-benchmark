SƐNEA ƆSEBƆ HONAM YƐE NSO-NSO

Wansana ne Ɔsebo na ɛtenaa hɔ (bere bi), ɛnna Ɔsebo ka kyerɛɛ Wansana sɛ, "Ma yɛnkɔkɔpɛ mmaa nware." Wansana kaa sɛ, "Yoo." Ɛnna Ɔsebo guaree, sraa ngo, de ne sika agudeɛ hyehyɛɛ ne ho, na ɔkyekyeree ne kɛtɛ dadaa no, de maa Wansana sɛ ɔnsoa wɔ ne ti so. Na wɔtuu kwan; wɔkɔduruu akuraa bi ase. Ɛhɔ ara pɛ na Wansana kaa sɛ, "Ɛnanom nyinaa ne Agyanom nyinaa, mekyia mo anwummerɛ." Wɔgyee so sɛ, "Yɛn kunu pa." Ɔsebo nso duruu hɔ; ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnanom nyinaa ne Agyanom nyinaa, mekyia mo anwummerɛ." Wɔgyee so sɛ, "Wo a w'ano bɔn yi wotumi kyia yɛn anwummerɛ?" Wɔtuu kwan, na wɔduruu akuraa a edi so no so. 'Wansana kaa sɛ, "Ɛnanom nyinaa ne Agyanom nyinaa, mekyia mo anwummerɛ." Wɔgyee so saa ara. Ɔsebo nso duruu hɔ, na ɔkyiaa wɔn, na wɔgyee so,

"Wo a w'anom bɔn, wo na worekyea yɛn anwummere anaa?" Wɔsii mu; wɔduruu kwan no so pɛ, Ɔsebɔ kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ ha! fa kɛtɛ dada no brɛ me na wo nso fa sika agudeɛ yi hyɛ, sɛ kɛtɛ dada no nti na mmabaawa no dɔ wo a, mɛhunu." Na (Nwansena) gyee (agudeɛ no), de hyɛɛ ne ho, na wɔsii mu duruu akuraa a edi so no so. Na Ɔsebɔ di anim, na kɛtɛ dada no gu n'atifi; ɔkyeaa (akuraasefoɔ no). Wɔkaa sɛ, "Mma nkyea yɛn, wo a w'anom bɔn; ɛfata wo sɛ wokura kɛtɛ dada yi." Ɛnna Nwansena duruu hɔ; ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnanom nyinaa ne Agyanom nyinaa, mekyea mo anwummere." Na wɔgyee so sɛ, "O, wo dɔfo; hwɛ aberanteɛ a ɔfata hwɛ koraa nie; sika agudeɛ; sɛdeɛ ɛfata wo fɛfɛɛfɛ; sɛ ɛnyɛ borɔ a yɛbɛnya nti a, anka yɛbɛka wo ho akɔ w'akwantu no mu." Na wɔkɔɔ so tenaa kwan so. Wɔduruu kwan no so pɛ na Ɔsebɔ kaa sɛ, "Hei! m'adamfo, gyina hɔ dinn, worɔ me sika agudeɛ no ma me ntɛm, ntɛm, ntɛm." Berɛ a Nwansena worɔɔ agudeɛ no de maa no wieeɛ no, Ɔsebɔ hwehwɛɛ kwan no so na ɔtwee ahahan bi a ɔde bɛkyekyere Nwansena. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mɛkyekyere wo, na ɛdeɛn na wowɔ ka?" Na Nwansena kaa sɛ, "Menni hwee bɛka." Ɔsebɔ toaa so sɛ, "Me ne wo nso yɛnnya nnuruu baabi a yɛrekɔ mpo . . . (na mmaa no redɔ wo saa)." Ɛnna Ɔsebɔ sɔɔ ne mu kyekyeree no bɔɔ dua ho. Na ɔno nso kɔhuntaa baabi foforɔ. Ankyɛ koraa na Bɔrɔdeɛ retwam rekɔ dwa so, na berɛ a ɔhunuu no (Nwansena) no, ɔkaa sɛ:

“Hwan na ɔsɛn hɔ a ne ho ayɛ tuntum saa no?

Hwan na ɔsɛn hɔ a ne ho hyerɛn saa no?”

(Nwansena no) kaa sɛ:

"Ɛyɛ me, Nwansena no, Kwao Boakye.

Adenen!

Gyahene Kofi Amo, Adenen!

Ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meba, na yɛnkɔhwehwɛ mmaa.

Adenen!

Mmaayewa no se wɔnnnɔ no.

Adenen!

Ɔakyekyere me.

Adenen!

Wakyekyere me koraa.

Adenen!

Agyenkwa, gye me.

Adenen!

Sɛ wogye me nkwa wie a, fa kwan no so na kɔ.

Adenen!” Nanso Bɔrɔdeɛ kaa sɛ, “Ɔsebo de wo akyekyere, enti mentumi nsan wo”; na ɔkɔeɛ. Amankani nso bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Aburo nso bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Atwibo nso bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Adua nso bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Akam nso bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Akapinkye nso bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Bankye nso bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Brɔfre nso bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Ɛmo nso bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Ɛfrɛ nso bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Ahabayere nso bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Kwadu nso bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Kontomire nso bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Krobona nso bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Mako nso bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Nkatie nso bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Nyaadewa nso bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Nkuruma nso bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; afei Dokon'fa baa ne ho bɛtwa mu kaa sɛ:

Hwan na ɔsɛn hɔ a ne ho ayɛ tuntum tumm saa no?

Hwan na ɔsɛn hɔ a ne ho hyerɛn saa no?

(Nwansena no) kaa sɛ:

Ɛyɛ me, Nwantwene no, Kwao Boakye, Adenen!

Gyahene Kofi Amo, Adenen!

Ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meba, na yɛnkɔhwehwɛ mmaa.

Adenen!

Mmaayewa no se wɔnnnɔ no.

Adenen!

Ɔakyekyere me.

Adenen!

Wakyekyere me koraa.

Adenen!

Agyenkwa, gye me.

Adenen!

Sɛ wogye me nkwa wie a, fa kwan no so na kɔ.

"Adenen!" translates to Asante Twi. However, "Adenen!" appears to be an exclamation (possibly from German, Scandinavian, or an invented name/word, or a typo of "Aden!"). In Twi, names or unique foreign exclamations are generally retained as they are. If "Adenen" is a proper name or specific exclamation, it remains: Adenen!

Dɔkono-fã see sɛ, "Mate, mɛsane wo." Ɔkɔɔ hɔ, kɔsanee no. Ɔwiee saa yɛ no, mpofirim ara na Ɔsebo puei. Ɔsee sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woasane me nipa no?" Ɔno (Dɔkono-fã) see sɛ, "Masane no, na deɛ wobɛyɛ biara, yɛ." Ɔno (Ɔsebo) see sɛ, "Me fam deɛ, me ne wo renyɛ hwee koraa, gye sɛ me ne wo bɛko abɔ ntɔkwaw." Dɔkono-fã see sɛ, "Sɛ yɛrebɛko a, ma yɛnkɔ wuram, na yɛnkɔbubu nnyina na yɛnsɔ gya." Enti wɔkɔe kɔbubuu nnyina, na wɔsane bae. Na ɔno (Dɔkono-fã) see sɛ, "Ma yɛnsɔ gya no." Na Ɔsebo sɔɔ gya no. Prɛko pɛ na wɔkɔɔ ntɔkwa no mu, (na ne nnyigyei te sɛ) Yiri! Yiri! Yiri! Na Ɔsebo twee Dɔkono-fã (firi ne ho), na ɔtoo no twene, bam! Dɔkono-fã see sɛ, "(Adeɛ a ɛte saa nyɛ hwee), wɔfa adeɛ a ɛte saa de sesa baabi a obi yere dware." Na wɔhyiaa bio. Na Dɔkono-fã nso too Ɔsebo twene firi ne ho, bam! Na Ɔsebo see sɛ, "Me nso mefa adeɛ a ɛte saa de sesa me yere adwareeɛ, (nanso) yɛn nhyiamu a ɛdi so yi mu deɛ, sɛ obi to baako twene a, ɛnde na ɛno ne akyire asotwene no." Na wɔkɔɔ (ntɔkwa no mu, ne nnyigyei te sɛ) Kikiri! Kikiri! Kikiri! Na Dɔkono-fã maamaa Ɔsebo so too no guu ogya no mu. Ɔsebo see sɛ, "_Me firim, me firim, me firim_" (Mafi mu, mafi mu, mafi mu. Ɛkwan a Ɔsebo faa so nyaa ne su "_Me firim_" no nen). (Afei) wɔhunuu sɛ ne ho ayɛ sei, kurukyire, kurukyire, kurukyire nyinaa (kyerɛ sɛ, nsunsunsoɔ nyinaa); baabi a fitaa no wɔ no, ɛhɔ na nso no kaa no; na baabi a tuntum no wɔ no, ɛhɔ nso na bidie tuntum no kaa no. Na sɛ worenante, na wohunu ahaban a Dɔkono-fã da mu (a wɔde abɔ no) gu hɔ a, wobɛhunu nwansena pii sɛ wɔredidi so; na ɛnyɛ biribi foforɔ biara nti (gye yei nko ara), wɔreda no ase.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, ebi nkɔ na ebi mmra.
