SƐDEƐ ƐYƐEƐ A ƆSEBƆ HONAM HO YƐƐ NKRƆNKRƆN

Ɛkɔbaa sɛ Ɔnwansena ne Ɔsebɔ na ɛwɔ hɔ, ɛnna Ɔsebɔ ka kyerɛɛ Ɔnwansena sɛ, "Ma yɛnkɔkɔpɛ mmaa nware." Ɔnwansena see sɛ, "Yoo." Ɛnna Ɔsebɔ guareeɛ, na ɔsraa ngo, na ɔde ne sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ hyehyɛɛ ne ho, na ɔkyekyeree ne kɛtɛ dada no, na ɔde maa Ɔnwansena sɛ ɔnsoa wɔ ne tiri so. Na wɔtuu kwan; wɔduruu akuraa bi ase. Ɛnna Ɔnwansena no kaa sɛ, "Ɛnanom nyinaa ne Agyanom nyinaa, mereka mo anwummere." Wɔgyee so sɛ, "Yɛn kunu pa." Ɔsebo nso d噓 hɔ; ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnanom nyinaa ne Agyanom nyinaa, mereka mo anwummere." Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Wo a w'ano bɔne no, wowɔ akokoduru sɛ woreka yɛn anwummere?" Wɔtuu kwan, na wɔduruu akuraa a edi so no so. Wansana, Nwansena no, kaa sɛ, "Ɛnanom nyinaa ne Agyanom nyinaa, mereka mo anwummere." Wɔgyee so saa ara. Ɔsebo nso duruu hɔ, na ɔkyiaa wɔn, na wɔgyee so,

"Wo a w'ano bɔne yi, worekyea yɛn anwummere anaa?" Wɔsii mu; wɔduruu kwan no so no, Ɔsebɔ kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ ha! fa kɛtɛ dada no brɛ me na wo nso fa sika nneɛma a wɔde siesie wɔn ho yi na fa siesie wo ho, sɛ ɛyɛ kɛtɛ dada no nti na mmabaawa no dɔ wo a, mɛhunu." Na (Nwansena) gyee (nneɛma no), de siesiee ne ho, na wɔsii mu kɔduruu akuraa a ɛdi hɔ no so. Ɔsebɔ no na ɔdi anim, na kɛtɛ dada no gu ne ti so; ɔkyiaa (akuramfɔ no). Wɔkaa sɛ, "Nkyia yɛn, wo a w'anom bɔn; ɛfata sɛ woso kɛtɛ dada yi." Afei Nwansena nso duruu hɔ; ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnanom nyinaa ne Agyanom nyinaa, mekyea mo anwummere." Na wɔgyee so, sɛ, "Oo wo dɔfo; hwɛ aberanteɛ a ɔsɛ nhwɛ pa ara nie; sikakɔkɔɔ nneɛma a wɔde hyehyɛ wɔn ho; sɛdeɛ ɛfata wo fa; sɛ ɛnyɛ abaa a yɛbɛnya nti a, anka yɛbɛka wo ho akɔ wo akwantuo." Enti wɔkɔɔ so tenaa mu. Wɔannya nnuru kwan no so pɛ na Ɔsebo kaa sɛ, "Hee! m'adamfo, gyina hɔ dinn, worɔ me sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ no na fa ma me ntɛm, ntɛm, ntɛm." Berɛ a Ɔnwansena no wiee agudeɛ no worɔ na ɔde maa no no, Ɔsebo hwɛɛ kwan no so nyinaa na ɔtetee hama bi a ɔde bɛkyekyere Ɔnwansena no. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Mɛkyekyere wo, na ɛdeɛn na wowɔ ka?” Na Ɔnwansena nso kaa sɛ, “Menni biribiara ka.” Ɔsebo toaa so sɛ, “Me ne wo mpo nnya nnuduu baabi a yɛrekɔ no . . . (nanso mmaa no deɛ wɔaphyɛ aseɛ redɔ wo dedaw).” Afei Ɔsebo soom no mu kyekyeree no bɔɔ dua ho. Na ɔno nso kɔe kɔhomee baabi foforɔ. Ɛnkyɛe koraa na Bɔrɔdeɛ retwamu rekɔ dwa so, na berɛ a ɔhunuu no (Nwansena no), ɔkaa sɛ:

“Hwan na ɔsɛn hɔ a ne ho ayɛ tuntum saa no?

Hwan na ɔsɛn hɔ a ne ho hyerɛn saa no?”

(Nwansena no) kaa sɛ:

“Ɛyɛ me, Nwantwene no, Kwao Boakye.

Adenen!

Gyahene Kofi Amo, Adenen!

Ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meba, na yɛnkɔhwehwɛ mmaa.

Adenen!

Mmmaa no se wɔnnɔ no.

Adenen!

Wakyekyere me.

Adenen!

Wakyekyere me koraa.

Adenen!

Ogyefoɔ, gye me nkwa.

Adenen!

Sɛ wogye me nkwa a, fa kwan no so na kɔ.

Adenen!” Nanso Bɔrɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Ɔsebo na wakye wo, enti mentumi nsan wo"; na ɔkɔe. Amankani, Mankani no, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Aburo, Aburoo no, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Atwibo, Bayaere no, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Adua, Asee no, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Akam, Bɔbore no, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Akapinkye, Bɔrɔdeɛ a wɔato no, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Bankye, Bankye no, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Brofere, Bɔfrɛ no, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Ɛmo, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Ɛfrɛre, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Habayere, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Kwadu, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Nkontommire, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Krobona, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Mako, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara er; Nkatee, the Ground-nut, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Nwiadowa, the Garden-egg, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; Nkuruma, the Okro, bae (na ɔkɔe) saa ara; na afei Dokon'fa, Half-a-ball-of-Kenki (Boiled-pounded-corn), bɛtwaam na ɔkaa sɛ:

Hwan na ɔsɛn hɔ a ne ho ayɛ tuntum saa?

Hwan na ɔsɛn hɔ a ne ho hyerɛn saa no?

(Nwansena no) kaa sɛ:

Ɛyɛ me, Nwansena no, Kwao Boakye, Adenen!

Gyahene Kofi Amo, Adenen!

Ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meba, na yɛnkɔhwehwɛ mmaa.

Adenen!

Mmmaa no se wɔnnɔ no.

Adenen!

Wakyekyere me.

Adenen!

Wakyekyere me koraa.

Adenen!

Ogyefoɔ, gye me nkwa.

Adenen!

Sɛ wogye me nkwa a, fa kwan no so na kɔ.

"Adenen!"

Dɔkono-fa kaa sɛ, "Mate, mɛsane wo." Ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔsane no. Berɛ a ɔwiee saa yɔ pɛ na prɛko pɛ Ɔsebɔ puei. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woasane me nipa no?" Ɔno (Dɔkono fa no) kaa sɛ, "Masane no, na nea wobɛyɛ biara, yɛ." Ɔno (Ɔsebo no) kaa sɛ, "Me fam deɛ, me ne wo renyɛ hwee koraa, gye sɛ me ne wo de yɛn nsa bɛko." Dɔkono fa kaa sɛ, "Sɛ yɛrebɛko a, ma yɛnkɔ wuram, na yɛmmubu nnyina na yɛnsɔ gya." Enti wɔkɔe kɔbubuu nnyina, na ɔsane bae. Na ɔno (Dɔkono-Fã) kaa sɛ, "Momma yɛnto mu gya." Na Ɔsebɔ no tɔɔ mu gya. Ɛhɔ ara na wɔhyɛɛ ko no ase, (na ne nnyigyei te sɛ) Yiri! Yiri! Yiri! Na Ɔsebɔ no asunsuan Dɔkono-Fã (afi ne ho), na ɔtoo no twene, bam! Dɔkono-fa kaa sɛ, "(Afei biribi a ɛte saa nyɛ hwee), obi fa ade a ɛte saa de sesa baabi a ne yere gware." Na wɔhyiae bio. Na Dɔkono-fa nso too Ɔsebo no guu fam firi ne ho, bamm! Na Ɔsebo kaa sɛ, "Me nso mede ade a ɛte saa na ɛhwire me yere adwaree mu, (nanso) yɛn nkitahodie a ɛdi so no mu, sɛ obi tow obi gu a, ɛnneɛ ɛno ne atowkorɔ no." Na wɔhyiaa mu (koo no, ɛgyee sɛ) Kikiri! Kikiri! Kikiri! Na Dɔkono Fa maa Ɔsebo so na ɔtow no kɔguu ogya no mu. Ɔseboɔ no kaa sɛ, "_Me firim, me firim, me firim_" (M'apue, m'apue, m'apue. Ɛno na ɛmaa Ɔseboɔ nyaa ne su a ɛne "_Me firim_"). (Afei) wɔhunuu sɛ ne ho ayɛ sɛɛ, kurukyire, kurukyire, kurukyire nyinaa (kyerɛ sɛ, nsonsonoa kɛkɛ); baabi a fitaa no wɔ no, ɛhɔ na nsowee no kaa no; na baabi a tuntum no wɔ no, ɛno nso na gyabidie tuntum no kaa no. Na sɛ worenante, na wohunu ahahan a Dɔkono-Fā faahoo mu gu hɔ a, wobɛhunu nwonwene pii sɛ wɔretutu wɔ so; na ɛnyɛ ade foforɔ biara nti (gye sɛ yei nko ara), wɔreda no ase.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, ebi nkɔ na ebi mmra.
