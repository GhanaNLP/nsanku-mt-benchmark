Yaa Nɔ Nɛ O Tsɔse O “Mumi Mi Hiami Níhi”

WA JEHA NGAMI KƐ HA 2026:

Gblamatsɛmɛ ji nihi nɛ le kaa a hia Mawu mumi ɔ.

—MAT. 5:3.

YEOHWA bɔ adesahi nɛ e ha mɛ hiami ní kometi. Kɛ̃kɛ nɛ a plɛ kɛ bɔ wɔ ɔ kɛ ba ɔ, ja wɔ ná níyeetí, tade, kɛ he nɛ wɔ maa hi, loko wɔ ma nyɛ maa hi wami mi. Ke wɔ be ní nɛ ɔmɛ a kpɛti kake tete po, be kukuoo ko mi ɔ, wami ba plɛɔ kahi. Pi helo nya ní nɛ ɔmɛ a he nɛ wɔ hia pɛ kɛkɛ nɛ Yeohwa bɔ wɔ kɛ ha, se Yeohwa bɔ wɔ kɛ mumi mi hiami níhi hu. (Kane Mateo 5:3.) Ke wɔ ngla kaa wɔ maa ná bua jɔmi nitsɛ ɔ, e he hia nɛ wɔ le hiami ní nɛ ɔ, nɛ lɔ ɔ se ɔ, wɔ ya nɔ nɛ wɔ tsu he ní.

2 Kɛ́ a ke nɔ ko “le” kaa e “hia Mawu mumi ɔ,” mɛni lɔɔ tsɔɔ? Munyu nɛ a kɛ tsu ní ngɛ kekleekle gbi ɔ mi ɔ kɔɔ nɔ ko nɛ e ngɛ mumi ɔ he baabani kpae ɔ he. Jamɛ a munyu ɔ haa nɛ nɔ ko susuɔ nɔ́ ko nɛ he wa he. Mo susu nɔ ko nɛ e he gbɔjɔ nɛ e wo tade hɛdɔhi nɛ e kotoo ngɛ ma a mi blɔ nya ko he. Niye ní nɛ e nɛ́ ha nɛ e gbɔjɔ, nihi kpoɔ lɛ ngɛ bɔ nɛ e he ngɛ ha a he je, nɛ pu dɔɔ lɛ ngɛ pia nɛ fɛĩ gbaa lɛ ngɛ nyɔɔ mi. Baabanikpalo ɔ le saminya kaa kɛ́ e ma nya sihimɛ kpakpa ngɛ si himi mi ɔ, e maa hia yemi kɛ buami. Jã kɛ̃ nɛ nɔ ko nɛ le kaa e hia mumi mi níhi—mumi ɔ he baabanikpalo—maa hia yemi kɛ buami kɛ kpɔ̃ e sihimɛ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e kɛ bua jɔmi kɛ he fɔɔ mumi mi ní babauu nɛ Yehowa kɛ haa nihi nɛ suɔ Lɛ ɔ nɔ.

3. Mɛni he wa ma susu ngɛ womi nɛ ɔ mi?

4 Be ko ɔ, Foinishi yo ko ba Yesu ngɔ. E biyo ɔ “ngɛ nimli gbegbeehi a dɛ mi nɛ e ngɛ nɔ́ nae wawɛɛ.” (Mat. 15:21-28) Yo ɔ kpla si nɛ e kpa Yesu pɛɛ konɛ e ye bua lɛ. Foinishi yo nɛ ɔ je su kpakpahi kpo benɛ e ba Yesu ngɔ ɔ. Mo susu a kpɛti komɛ a he nɛ o hyɛ.

5 Foenikia yo ɔ je he si bami nitsɛ kpo. Mɛni he je wa ma nyɛ ma de jã? Ejakaa benɛ Yesu kɛ nɔhyɛnɔ ko tsu ní nɛ ngɛ kaa e ngɛ e kɛ gbee wayoo ko kɛ ngɛ toe, ekoɔ lohwe nɛ a lalaa ngɛ Ma Je li a we mi ɔ, e mi fu we. Kɛ o ko pee o ní ha kɛɛ ngɛ Yesu munyuhi kɛ e nípeehi a he? Anɛ o ko nu he kaa a dɔɔ mo nɛ o ko kpa e yemi kɛ buami hlaami lo? Pi jã nɛ Foenikia yo ɔ pee e ní. E je pi he si bami kɛkɛ kpo, se kɛ tsui si kami hu. Enɛ ɔ je kpo heii kɛ gu bɔ nɛ e ya nɔ nɛ e kpa Yesu pɛɛ konɛ e ye bua lɛ ɔ nɔ. Mɛni tsirɛ lɛ nɛ e pee jã? E hemi kɛ yemi nɛ e ngɛ ngɛ Yesu mi ɔ nɛ. Ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ, yo nɛ ɔ hemi kɛ yemi ɔ ta Yesu tsui he wawɛɛ nɛ e pee nɔ́ ko nɛ ngɛ nyakpɛ. E ngɛ mi kaa e de kɛkɛ nɛ ɔ nɛ nɛ ke a tsɔ lɛ konɛ e ba ye bua “Israel we ɔ tohi nɛ a laa a” kɛkɛ mohu lɛɛ, se Yesu fie mumi yayami ɔ kɛ je Ma Je yo nɛ ɔ biyo ɔ mi.

E sa nɛ waa ná hemi kɛ yemi nɛ mi wa kɛ fɔ Kristo Yesu nɔ hulɔ, nɛ waa fɔ ni nɛmɛ nɛ e ngɛ kɛ tsuɔ ní kɛ tsɔɔ e kaseli ɔmɛ blɔ ɔ nɔ. Amlɔ nɛ ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ waa hyɛ blɔ nɛ Yehowa guɔ nɔ kɛ haa wɔ mumi mi niye ní, tade, kɛ he hihe. Wa maa na nɔ́ nɛ e sa nɛ waa pee konɛ waa ná mumi mi hiami ní nɛ ɔmɛ kɛ gu bɔ nɛ bɔfo Petro kɛ Paulo kɛ Matsɛ David pee a ní ha a kɛ fɔ si ɔ hyɛmi mi.

KĀA BƆ NƐ PETRO PEE Ɔ—HA NƐ O HEMIKƐYEMI MI NIYENYIHI Ɔ NƐ HI BE TSUAA BE

7 Mo susu bɔfo Petro nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ he nɛ o hyɛ. E ji Yudafo nyumuhi nɛ a kɛ fɔ a hɛ mi nɛ a yɔse kaa Yesu ji Mesia a—nɔ nɛ Yehowa ngɛ e nɔ guee kɛ ngɛ E webi ɔmɛ “mwɔnɛ munyahi nɛ hamiɔ nɛ a náami he nɔ nanemi” ɔ kɛ haa a ti nɔ kake. (Yoh. 6:66-68) Loko Yesu nɛ a tle lɛ si ɔ ma ku e se kɛ ho hiɔwe ya a, e ha Petro dɔmi nɛ ɔ: “Lɛɛ hyɛ ye jigbɔwi ɔmɛ a nɔ.” (Yoh. 21:17) Petro kɛ anɔkwayeli tsu dɔmi nɛ ɔ he ní, nɛ Yehowa kɛ lɛ tsu ní kɛ ngma womi enyɔ nɛ ba fã kake ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi. Se Petro hulɔɔ he hia nɛ e lɛ́ lɛ nitsɛ e he kɛ mumi mi níye ní. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, e kase womihi nɛ a kɛ mumi klɔuklɔu ɔ ha bɔfo Paulo ngma kɛ sɛ hlami ɔ. Petro yɔse kaa ní komɛ nɛ Paulo ngma a he munyu ɔ “se numi wa.” (2 Pet. 3:15, 16) Se e he we nɛ kɛ̃ Petro ya nɔ nɛ e kase ní nɛ ɔmɛ, nɛ e ngɛ hemi kɛ yemi kaa Yehowa maa ye bua lɛ konɛ e nu “níye ní kpakpa” nɛ a ha Paulo ngma ngɛ e womi ɔmɛ a mi ɔ sisi nɛ e kɛ tsu ní.—Kane Hebrubi 5:14–6:1.

8 Petro je kpo kaa e ngɛ hemi kɛ yemi ngɛ Yehowa mi kɛ gu blɔ tsɔɔmi nɛ e nine su nɔ ɔ nɔ nɛ e bo tue ɔ nɔ. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, benɛ Petro ngɛ kpo he ma nɛ ji Yopa a mi ɔ, e na nina ko nɛ Mawu teblenyɔ ko de lɛ ngɛ mi kaa e gbe kɛ ye lohwehi nɛ ngɛ Mosa Mlaa a nya a, a he tsɔɛ. Kɛ ha Yuda bi ɔ, blɔ tsɔɔmi nɛ ɔ maa ye dɔ. Kekleekle ɔ, Petro ha heto ke: “Dabi kɔkɔɔkɔ, Nyɔmtsɛ, ejakaa i yeko nɔ́ ko nɛ he tsɔɛ loo nɔ́ nɛ he sɔ hyɛ.” Kɛkɛ nɛ a de lɛ ke: “Koo tsɛ níhi nɛ Mawu ha nɛ a he tsɔ ɔ kaa a he sɔ.” (Bɔfo 10:9-15) Petro nu munyu ɔ sisi. Kɛ wa plɛ kɛ le kɛɛ? Benɛ e na nina a se e kɛ we nɛ nyumuhi etɛ nɛ Ma Zunyɛ No Kornelio tso mɛ ɔ ba su kɛ fɔ Petro nine kaa e ba nɛ e ba tu munyu kɛ ha a nyɔmtsɛ ɔ. Behi nɛ be ɔ, jinɛ kɔkɔɔkɔ Petro be kplɛɛ nɛ e maa sɛ Ma Zunyɛ No we mi. Yuda bi ɔmɛ buɔ Ma Zunyɛ Bimɛ kaa nihi nɛ a he tsɔɛ. (Bɔfo 10:28, 29) Se amlɔ nɔuu nɛ Petro kplɛɛ la ehe nɛ Yehowa je kpo kɛ ha lɛ ɔ nɔ. (Abɛ 4:18) E fiɛɛ kɛ ha Kornelio kɛ e weku ɔ nɛ e na bua jɔmi kaa e na nɛ a kplɛɛ anɔkuale ɔ nɔ, a nine su mumi klɔuklɔu ɔ nɔ, nɛ a baptisi mɛ.—Bɔfo 10:44-48.

Wa náa he se ngɛ blɔ enyɔ nɔ pe kulaa: Kɛkɛ nɛji, wa kaseɔ níhi kɛ kɔɔ Yehowa he nɛ haa nɛ wa suɔmi kɛ bumi kɛ ha lɛ mi wa; nɛ enyɔne se nɛji, e haa nɛ wa he beɔ kɛ ya hiɛ nɛ wa haa ni kpahi leɔ wa hiɔwe Tsɛ nɛ he ngɛ gbeye ɔ he munyu. (Rom. 11:33; Kpoj. 4:11) Petro nɔhyɛwe nɔ ɔ tsɔɔ wɔ ní kpa ko: Ke wa ná tsakeehi ngɛ wa Mawu Munyi ɔ sisi numi mi ɔ, e sa nɛ waa kɛ hehiami nɛ tsake wa susumi kɛ wa nípeehi. Jamɛ a be ɔ pɛ nɛ wa maa hi si nɛ wa he beɔ mumi mi níye ní he nɛ wa maa pee nihi nɛ Yehowa kɛ mɛ ma tsu ní kɛ hiɔ.

KĀĀ BƆ NƐ PAUL PEƐ Ɔ—YĀ NƆ NƐ O WO MUMƆ MI TLƆMI KPAKKPA

10 Kɛ̃ nɛ wa maa sa Mawu hɛ mi ɔ, ja wa ngɔ nɔ́ kpa nɛ Yehowa to kɛ ha wɔ ɔ kɛ tsu ní: sunsum mi tade. Mɛni lɔɔ tsɔɔ? Bɔfo Paulo ngma kaa e sa nɛ waa “je he su gblɔgblɔ ɔ kɛ pue he” nɛ waa wo “he su he ɔ.” (Kane Kolosebi 3:8-10.) Tade nɛ waa wo ngɛ lɔɔ nya a ma nyɛ maa pee nɔ́ ko nɛ he wa nɛ yaa nɔ daa. Mo susu Paulo nitsɛ e nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ he nɛ o hyɛ. Kɛje e jokuɛwiisi a, e bɔ mɔde wawɛɛ nɛ e ná Mawu hɛ mi nyami. (Gal. 1:14; Fil. 3:4, 5) Se e be Mawu yi mi tomi ɔ he nilee nɛ da, kɛkɛ nɛ e ba hi sunsum mi si fɔfɔɛ nɛ nya wa mi. E nilee nɛ e be ngɛ Kristo e tsɔɔmi ɔmɛ a he ɔ, kɛ he nɔ womi ha nɛ e ba pee “nyumu nɛ he ngɛ gbeye,” nɛ subai yayamihi ba wo e he tade.—1 Tim. 1:13.

10. Mɛni e biɔ kaa nɔ ko nɛ wo mumi mi he dlami nɛ da? (Kolosebi 3:8-10)

11 Loko Paulo maa ba pee Kristofono ɔ, e mi mi fuɔ mlamla. Bɔ ni a tsɔɔ he munyu ngɛ Bɔfo ɔmɛ a Ní Tsumi ɔ mi ɔ tsɔɔ kaa, Paulo mi mi fu Yesu kaseli ɔmɛ nɛ e ngɛ ‘mɛ kɔkɔ bɔe nɛ e ngɛ hlae nɛ e gbe mɛ.’ (Bɔf. 9:1) Benɛ Paulo ba pee Kristofono se ɔ, hetsɔ ko be he kaa e bɔ mɔde wawɛː nɛ e kuɛ subai yaya nɛ ɔ ngo fɔ he. (Efe. 4:22, 31) Kɛ̃ ɔ, benɛ Paulo kɛ Barnaba pɛ ɔ, “a mi mi fu wawɛː kɛ te si kɛ wo a he.” (Bɔf. 15:37-39) Enɛ ɔ ji nane tɔtɔmi kɛ ha Paulo, se e kpa we. E ya nɔ ‘fĩa e nɔmlɔtso ɔ nɔ’—kɛ kpe e gbɔjɔmi ɔmɛ a nya—konɛ Yehowa bua nɛ jɔ e he.—1 Kor. 9:27.

12 Paulo nyɛ nɛ e po e jemi ba su yayami ɔmɛ ngo fɔ he nɛ lɔ ɔ se ɔ, e wo su he ɔ akɛnɛ e nɛɛ we e nitsɛ e hewalo nɔ heje. (Filip. 4:13) Kaa bɔ nɛ Petro pee ɔ, Paulo nɛɛ “hewalo nɛ Mawu haa” a nɔ. (1 Pet. 4:11) Se kɛ̃ ɔ, be komɛ ɔ, Paulo nuɔ he kaa e yi manye. Se be nɛ e kɔɔni jɔ ɔ, e susu nɔ́ kpakpa nɛ e hiɔwe Tsɛ ɔ pee kɛ ha lɛ ɔ he, nɛ lɔ ɔ wo lɛ hewalo konɛ e ya nɔ nɛ e bɔ mɔde.—Rom. 7:21-25.

14-15. Mɛni blɔ nɔ Yehowa yeɔ bua e webi nɛ a ná he jɔɔmi he ngɛ mumi mi? (La 27:5) (Hyɛ foni nɛ ngɛ he wamihiɛmi womi ɔ he ɔ kɛ pi si.)

O Ma Nyɛ Ma Hwu Kɛ Si Susu Yayahi Kɛ Nɔ Yemi

“Kɔɔliatsɛ ji mi!”—ROM. 7:24.

MẸNI baa o juɛmi mi ke o susu bɔfo Paulo he? Anɛ o susuo ma se sɔmɔlɔ nɛ ngɛ kã, tsɔɔlɔ nɛ le ní saminya, aloo Baiblo ngmalɔ nɛ ngma níhi babauu he lo? Tsɔɔmi nɛ ɔmɛ da kpa. Se Paulo ji nɔ ko nɛ ngɛ henumihi nɛ mi kuɔ hulɔ. Kaa bɔ nɛ e ji ngɛ wa kpɛti nihi babauu a blɔ faa a, be komɛ ɔ e kɛ henumi yaya nɛ e náa he nɔ́ ko he ɔ hwuɔ kɔni.

² Kane Romabi 7:21-24. Ngɛ Paulo womi nɛ e ngma kɛ ya ha Romabi ɔ mi ɔ, e tsɔɔ henumihi nɛ wa ti nihi fuu nɔɔ hu náa kpa. E ngɛ mi kaa e ji Kristofono nɛ yeɔ anɔkuale mohu lɛɛ, se e ná nɔmlɔ mlimi hwuɔmi ko—hwuɔmi ngɛ e suɔmi nɛ e ngɛ kɛ ha Mawu suɔmi nya ní peemi kɛ e he ko nɛ gbaa lɛ kɛ yaa bɔmi yayamihi peemi a kpɛti. Kɛ piɛɛ henumi nɛ ɔ he ɔ, be komɛ ɔ Paulo gbaa ngɛ henumi yaya nɛ e ngɛ ngɛ e blema si himi he kɛ haomɔ ko nɛ e ngɛ kpee kɛ ngɛ hwɛɛ he.

³ Ke kɛ̃ si kɛ̃ a, Paulo ha we nɛ e he susuumi yayamihi nɛ ye e he nɔ. Ngɛ munyu nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, wa ma susu sane bimi nɛ ɔmɛ a he: Mɛni ha Paulo nu he be komɛ kaa e ye “kɔmɔ”? Kɛ e pee kɛ da susuumi yayami nɛ ɔmɛ a nya ha kɛɛ? Nɛ kɛ wa ma pee kɛ da wɔ nitsɛmɛ wa he susuumi yayamihi a nya saminya ha kɛɛ?

* MUNYUKPOMI NƐ A TSƆƆ NƆ: Ngɛ munyhiɛ nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, munyu nɛ ji “susumi yayaahi” kɔɔ aywilɛhoyeli kɛ su nɛ tsakeɔ be fɛɛ be nɛ e kɔɔ wa he nɛ wa ma nyɛ ma ná a he. E kɔɛ aywilɛhoyeli nɛ hiɔ be gagaaga, nɛ ji tsaba yami hiɔ nɛ nya wa a he.

4 Níhi nɛ e pee ngɛ be ko nɛ be o mi. Loko Paulo, nɛ kɛ gu nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Saulo ɔ maa ba pee Kristofo no ɔ, e pee níhi nɛ pee se ɔ e pia e he ngɛ a he. Kɛ̃ɛnɛ ko ji kaa, e hyɛ nɛ e kplɛɛ nɔ benɛ a fia weku no anɔkputsɛ Stefano tɛhi kɛ ya si nɛ e gbo ɔ. (Bɔfohi 7:58; 8:1) Jehanɛ se hu ɔ, Saulo nyɛɛ hɛ mi kɛ wa Kristofohi yi mi wawɛɛ nitsɛ.—Bɔfohi 8:3; 26:9-11.

5 Benɛ bɔfo Paulo plɛ Kristofono se ɔ, be komɛ ngɛ nɛ e blema nípeehi haoɔ lɛ. Eko ɔ, e he si fɔfɔe nɛ e fɔ ngɛ Kristofohi a nɔ womi mi kasikasi nɔ ɔ ya nɔ hao lɛ nɛ e su se po kɛ ya ngɛ jehahi a nya. Kɛ mɔ nɛ, benɛ e ngma e kekle wolo kɛ ya ha Korintobi ɔmɛ ngɛ maa e se jeha 55 C.E. mi ɔ, e de ke: “I sɔ he nɛ a tsɛ mi ke bɔfo, ejakaa i wa Mawu asafo ɔ yi.” (1 Kor. 15:9) E he maa je jeha kake a se ngɛ e wolo kɛ ya ha Efesobi ɔ mi ɔ, e tsɔɔ e he si kɛ de ke e ji “nɔ nɛ nɔ kuɔ pe nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ klanɔli ɔmɛ a ti nyaalɔ pe kulaa.” (Efe. 3:8) Benɛ e ngma wolo kɛ ha Timote ɔ, Paulo tsɔɔ e he blɔ nɛ e ngɛ kɛ sa si nɛ e je blɔ po ke e ji “musubɔlɔ kɛ yiwatɔ kɛ nɔ nɛ gbaa nɔ nya.” (1 Tim. 1:13) Anɛ o ma nyɛ maa susu bɔ nɛ Paulo he nu ha benɛ e ya slaa asafo ko nɛ e ya kpe nihi nɛ e wa mɛ yi aloo weku li kɛ ha ni nɛmɛ nɛ e wa mɛ yi ɔ he lo?

6 Mio ngɔ he lo ɔ mi. Paulo kɛ nɔ́ ko nɛ ha nɛ e kɔ̃ɔ wawɛɛ kɛ to “mio ko ngɔ he lo ɔ mi” he. (2 Kor. 12:7) Bɔfo ɔ tui nɔ́ tutuutu nɛ ha nɛ e ba jã a he munyɔ, se munyu nɛ e de ɔ tsɔɔ kaa nɔ́ ko—e he lo mi haomi, e bua jɔmi he haomi, aloo nɔ́ kpa ko—waa nɛ gbaa e nya.*

7 E nyagbahi. Paulo kɛ e nyagbahi hwo no. (Kane Romabi 7:18, 19.) E ngɛ mi kaa e suɔ nɛ e pee nɔ́ nɛ da mohu lɛɛ, se e helo nɛ yi mluku ɔ tsi e nya ngɛ nɔ́ nɛ e fia e yi mi kaa e maa pee ɔ he. E kplɛɛ nɔ kaa hwuɔ si fɔfɔɛ ngɛ e helo ɔ he kɔkamihi kɛ suɔmi nɛ e ngɛ kaa e maa pee nɔ́ nɛ da a kpueti be fɛɛ be. Se kɛ̃ ɔ, Paulo bɔ mɔde wawɛɛ nɛ e pee tsakemihi. (1 Kor. 9:27) Hyɛ bɔ nɛ e he maa jɔ̃ e he ha be fɛɛ be nɛ e subai yaya amɛ kpale je kpo ekɔ!

BƆ NƐ PAULO PE NƐ E DA SUSUWI YAYAMIHI A NYƆ MI

8 Kaa bɔ nɛ a tsɔɔ he si ngɛ e womihi a mi ɔ, Paulo susu bɔ nɛ lɛ kɛ e nyɛmimɛ hemi kɛ yemi bimɛ ɔmɛ ma nyɛ maa gu Mawu mumi ɔ nɔ kɛ hwu kɛ je yayami subai a nɔ ha a he. (Rom. 8:13; Gal. 5:16, 17) Behi fuu ɔ, Paulo tuɔ subai kɛ akɔnɔhi nɛ sɛ nɛ e sa kaa Kristofohi nɛ a hwu kɛ si ɔ he munyu. (Gal. 5:19-21, 26) E he we yemi ko kaa Paulo susu lɛ nitsɛ e gbgblɛɛhi a he, e hla ga womi ngɛ Ngmami ɔ mi, nɛ e hla blɔhi pɔtɛɛ nɛ e maa gu nɔ kɛ tsu e hegbɔhi a he ní. Wa ma nyɛ ma ná nɔ mami kaa e nitsɛ e kɛ ga womi nɛ e kɛ ha ni kpahi ɔ tsu ní.

2 Wɔ ti nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ si fɔfɔɛ nɛ ngɛ kaa jã a nɔuu mi. Kɛ e ji ha kɛɛ? Benɛ Adam tso e Bɔlɔ ɔ nɔ atuã a, e ba pee yayami peelɔ. Pee se ɔ, e ya ha e bimɛ ɔmɛ yayami si fɔfɔɛ nɛ e ngɛ mi ɔ. Bɔfo Paulo tsɔɔ wa yayami si fɔfɔɛ ɔ mi saminya ngɛ e sɛvɔ nɛ e kɛ mane Roma bi ɔmɛ ɔ mi. (Kane Roma Bi 7:22-24.) E kpa pɛɛ kaa a he e yi wami kɛ “je nɔmlɔtso nɛ ngɛ gbee kɛ yaa gbenɔ nɛ ɔ mi.” Gbɔjɔɔmi yayami nɛ nyɛɔ nɔ kɛ yaa gbenɔ mi kɛ gu blɔ nɛ a be nyɛe maa tsi nya mi ɔ ha nɛ Paulo piã. Baiblo ɔ hɛɛ blema sanehi fuu nɛ kɔɔ adesahi a he nɛ tsɔɔ wɔ ní nitsɛmitsɛmɛhi. Kɛ gu nɔ́ nɛ wa kanee ɔ he foni nɛ wa maa pee ngɛ wa juɛmi mi ɔ nɔ ɔ, wa ma nyɛ maa nu nɔ́ nɛ wa kanee ɔ sisi kpamisɛ nɛ waa ná he se kpaako. Mo susu ga womi komɛ nɛ ma nyɛ maa ye bua mo kɔnɛ o pee Baiblo mi sanehi a he foni ngɛ o juɛmi mi ɔ a he nɛ o hyɛ.

• Kane kɛ gbee nɔ kuɔ. Baiblo ɔ kanemi kɛ gbee nɔ kuɔ ma nyɛ ma ye bua mo nɛ o ná níhi nɛ yaa nɔ ɔ he fɔfli nɛ mi tsɔ kɛ su se. Ke nyɛ ngɛ Baiblo ɔ kane kɛ yaa nɔ kaa weku ɔ, nyɛ ma nyɛ ma ha nɛ munyugbami ɔ nɛ ba je mi kɛ gu nɔ fɛɛ nɔ nɛ a ma ha lɛ nítsumɔ kɛ nɔ nɛ e kɛ e gbɛi ma kane ɔ nɔ.

• Mo susu nɔ́ nɛ ya nɔ ɔ he. Bɔɔ mɔde nɛ o nu nɔmlɔ nɛ a he munyu a ngɛ tue ɔ susuomi kɛ e he numihi a sisi. Kɛ kɛnɛ, bi o he ke: ‘Mɛni heje nɛ nɔ ɔ tu munyu loo e pee nɔ́ nɛ e pee ɔ? Kɛ ma susu loo ma nu he ha kɛɛ ke i ngɛ jamɛ a si fɔfɔɛ ɔ mi?’
