1926

THE PAST CENTURY

THE WATCHTOWER of January 1, 1926 said, “it will not be long before God will make everyone see that he is the Almighty.” It also said, “God will choose a people to bear witness about him.”

In the year 1926, Jehovah, through Jesus Christ, the head of the congregation, made it clear to Christians that the preaching work should come first in their lives. Therefore, the “faithful and discreet slave” produced new publications, and changes were also made to the way congregation meetings were conducted. Furthermore, the brothers were helped to see what they must do to truly show that they are Christians. (Matt. 24:45) Christ also helped them acquire buildings in which to work and printing presses to produce various publications so that they could carry out the preaching work.

A NEW BOOK WAS RELEASED, AND THE WAY CONGREGATION MEETINGS ARE CONDUCTED WAS ALSO CHANGED

In April, The Golden Age, now called Awake!, announced that a new book called Deliverance had been released. It was the one that would come to replace the book Studies in the Scriptures in the preaching work. In order for the brothers to get the number of books they needed for preaching, the brothers at Bethel bought a new printing press, and it could print 22 books in one minute. The Bible Students gave a copy of the book to anyone they preached to, if possible.

Many years ago, the Bible Students studied the book Studies in the Scriptures during meetings, but starting in May 1926, they began studying The Watchtower. How did it help them?

The Little Bunker Hill congregation in Illinois wrote a letter to Brother Rutherford. They said: “The Little Bunker Hill congregation greets you. When Sunday comes, we study the material that Jehovah's organization has directed us to study. We are very glad that whenever any new light comes, we all quickly see it in The Watchtower.”

"New light," or the new changes that came, helped the Bible Students focus their attention on the preaching work. For example, one Watchtower study explained that although it is appropriate to display good conduct, treat people well, and show them respect, we should not ignore the most important work. That study article said: 'If anyone loves the Lord, he must obey his commandments. Jehovah says: You are my witnesses, and I am your God. If anyone is a Christian, he should be eager to bear witness about God.'

Another Watchtower study also emphasized that what true Christians do to show that they truly love Jehovah is to serve him with all their heart. That study article encouraged the Bible Students to exert themselves to preach the good news to everyone, come rain or shine.—Isa. 43:10, 12; Matt. 24:14.

The changes introduced by those taking the lead soon began to bear fruit. A few weeks later, a letter written by a congregation appeared in the July 15, 1926 issue of The Watchtower. It said: “The two recent Watchtower studies

MEETINGS WERE HELD, AND THE BROTHERS WERE ENCOURAGED TO EXERT THEMSELVES IN THE PREACHING WORK

At the end of May, many Bible Students attended a convention held in London, England. At that time, workers throughout Britain had gone on strike, so it was difficult to find a printing company to print literature for them. As a result, the brothers at Brooklyn Bethel worked long hours to print an additional 117,000 copies of the booklet entitled The Standard for the People. These booklets were distributed to the brothers who attended the London convention.

On Saturday, May 29, they began using the booklet in preaching. That day, the statement printed on the convention program encouraged the brothers: “Everyone who is baptized and attending this convention should preach to people about Jesus and his Kingdom. Tomorrow morning, everyone should try to arrive early at Alexandra Palace, and the brothers there will show you what to do.”

The brothers distributed over 100,000 booklets. The Watch Tower of July 15, 1926, reported: “The brothers rejoiced greatly. They felt that they had obeyed Jehovah by preaching the good news to the people to show that they are his Witnesses.”

The next day, Brother Rutherford gave a talk at the Royal Albert Hall. His topic was “Why World Powers Are Staggering—The Remedy.” In no time, as soon as the doors were opened, 13,000 people filled the place. For those who could not get inside the hall, large speakers were set up outside so they could also listen.

In Brother Rutherford's speech, he explained that the reason why the world rulers are struggling is that they have rejected Jehovah and his Kingdom. Then he spoke against the League of Nations, stating that it would soon disappear.

TRAVELING OVERSEERS TOOK THE LEAD IN THE PREACHING WORK

From about 1894, Jehovah’s organization sent out representatives to visit the Bible Students, just as circuit overseers do today. In July 1926, the way they did their work changed. Previously, they did it in one day, but now they were to spend two days visiting each group. Also, they had to spend 100 hours preaching each month.

Brother George Draper, who was a traveling overseer, wrote a letter to the congregation. He said: “Jehovah’s servants are like watchdogs, so we must warn people so they see the coming danger. [Isa. 56:10] Studying The Watchtower is what will help us to be able to do that, and that is what I am focused on.” Brother Draper finished his earthly course in 1938.

A MEETING WE HELD IN NEW YORK CITY

The last convention that we held in that year was held on October 14-18, 1926, in New York City. Brother Rutherford was to give a talk titled “A Great Battle Approaching the Entire World.” Newspaper syndicates agreed to advertise Brother Rutherford’s talk. However, shortly before the time arrived, many of the prominent ones among them withdrew.

What the newspaper syndicate did, the brothers decided to make known to the public. On Friday, October 15, Brother R. J. Martin released a new tract entitled Against the People. Its aim was to expose what the syndicate had done and to advertise Brother Rutherford’s talk. Brother Martin said: “We have started printing our literature using our own presses. Our brothers at Bethel will work hard day and night until they have printed 1,250,000 copies of the tract, and we will have the brothers at this convention distribute them to the people.” When the convention ended on Friday, the brothers began distributing the tracts. By evening, they had distributed 300,000 of the tracts.

On Saturday and Sunday too, many more were distributed, and the number distributed reached 1,000,000. Apart from that, the brothers worked hard to distribute 3,783 Deliverance books, and also distributed 35,140 booklets.

On Sunday, October 17, at three o'clock in the afternoon, about 12,000 people came to listen to Brother Rutherford's discourse. After he finished the talk, those who were interested asked many questions. At that meeting which was held, they distributed boo-

A BUILDING WAS CONSTRUCTED AT 117 ADAMS STREET

Because many people wanted our publications, the responsible brothers in Brooklyn saw the need to expand our printing facilities. For many years, a building had been rented at 18 Concord Street; that was where the printing was done. However, because the work was expanding, the place had become too small. So the brothers bought a piece of land on Adams Street, near their existing location, and began constructing a large eight-story building on it. Now, they would have a large enough place where they could print publications.

The building that was being constructed at 117 Adams Street was to be put into operation in 1927. It would help the brothers prepare for the expansion that was to come. In 1927, many people came into the congregation, and Jehovah continued to prepare and purify them, so that in 1931, they could be called Jehovah’s Witnesses.

Make Sure Your Faith Rests on a Strong Foundation

WHEN you studied the Bible with Jehovah’s Witnesses, what made you believe that you had found the “one faith,” that is, the only way God wants us to worship him? (Eph. 4:4-6) We might ask, was it the way Jehovah’s Witnesses love one another that drew you to associate with them? Or was it their honesty and good conduct? The reason many people have come to associate with God’s servants is that God’s congregation shows genuine love to them, making them feel secure. And if they feel that way, it is rightly so, because love and good conduct are what identify true Christians.—John 13:35; 1 Pet. 2:12.

However, suppose a fellow Christian has done something that has troubled you, or has failed to show you love, or has committed a serious sin that everyone is talking about. Perhaps his sin is like what happened in the Corinthian congregation in the apostle Paul's day. If something like that happens, what will you do? (1 Cor. 5:1) If you allow the actions or words of others to ruin your faith, it shows that the foundation on which your faith rests is not strong. What will you do to prevent something like that from happening?

HOW WILL YOU MAKE YOUR FAITH STRONG?

If you continue to search for evidence that what you learned from the Bible is true, it will strengthen your faith. Consider this.

At one time, Jesus told his followers that they must eat his flesh and drink his blood. When he said that, many of his disciples said: “This saying is hard.” What

What Jesus said stumbled many of them, and they stopped following him.—John 6:56-60, 66.

After that, he asked his apostles: “Do you also want to go?” Peter answered: “Lord, to whom shall we go? You have the sayings of everlasting life. We have believed and come to know that you are the Holy One of God.”—John 6:67-69.

Why did what Jesus said not cause Peter and the remaining apostles to stumble? Their faith was based on a solid foundation, and not merely on emotions. Peter said: “We have believed and have come to know that you are the Holy One of God.” These faithful apostles knew what they believed and why they believed it. They compared the words Jesus spoke and the things he did with what they knew from the Scriptures, and they came to believe that Jesus was the Messiah. Therefore, when some stopped following Jesus, their own faith was not shaken.

What can we learn from this matter? It is that what we know is far more important than how we feel. Why? Because as for the feelings we have, if someone does not treat us well, they can change. But the truth we have learned from the Bible, such as God’s name, his purpose, and what pertains to the ransom, whatever someone says or does can never change it. Therefore, if we truly believe what the Bible teaches, and also believe that it is Jehovah who directs his organization, then we will not let what someone does stumble us into quitting Jehovah’s worship.

Jehovah Blesses Those Who Have Faith

Every week, Emma worked three days at a hotel. Whenever she went, she worked for nine hours. When the time came for the regional convention she was supposed to attend, she went to ask her manager for one day off so that she could go. Not only did the manager refuse, but he also told her that from that day on, instead of working three days, she would have to work five days. Then he added that if she did not agree to the new arrangement, she should consider herself dismissed. Emma decided to quit the job so that she could attend the convention, and she relied on Jehovah to care for her.

After the meeting, when Emma went to submit her resignation letter, she met the main manager of the hotel. When the manager heard what had happened, he promised Emma that he would personally speak with her supervisor so that she could continue working. However, two weeks later, Emma's supervisor told her that she had been dismissed. After a few days, Emma met the hotel's main manager again, and he was upset that the supervisor had not listened to him and had dismissed Emma. Right there, he gave Emma her job back, and said she would work five days every week. Interestingly, instead of working nine hours, she was to work only four hours each day. It turned out that this new arrangement was far better than the previous one, because it allowed her to do her pioneer service and other activities in Jehovah's service. She was even amazed at how Jehovah provided what she needed.
