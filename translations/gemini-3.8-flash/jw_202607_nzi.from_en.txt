ADEHILƐ Nea ɛzukoalɛdeɛ mɔɔ yɛbahola yɛazukoa yɛavi Gebeɔnma ne mɔ nee kɛzi Gyihova nee bɛ lile la anu.

Sukoa Debie Fi Gibeyɔnma Ne Mɔ Ɛkɛ

“Gibeonma nee Yizilayɛma vi anzodwole nu dɛnlanle na bɛ nee bɛ dɔle bɛ nwo.”—DZO. 10:1.

ƐVOLƐ ne a le 1473 K.M. Yizilayɛ maanle ne abɔ ɔ bo kɛ ɔlie Ɛwɔkɛ Azɛlɛ ne kɛkala. Yizilayɛma ɛli nkonim kpole kpalɛ wɔ Gyɛleko mini kpole ne nee Ayi anwo zo. Noko nzinlii, debie mɔɔ bɛ nye ɛnla la sisile. Akwantufoɔ eku bie rale bɛ nwo lɔ, mɔɔ bɛhanle kɛ bɛvi aseeke azɛlɛ bie azo a na bɛkpondɛ kɛ bɛ nee Nyamenle menli ne yɛ anzodwolɛ ngyehyɛleɛ a.

2 Ndenlehyelɛma zɔhane mɔ a le Gibeonma. Ɛke a le fɛlɛko mɔɔ baha Gibeonma anwo edwɛkɛ wɔ Baebolo ne anu a, noko nwolɛ edwɛkɛ ɛtɛwiele. Baebolo nu edwɛkɛ dɔɔnwo kile kɛ Gibeonma nee Yizilayɛma bɔle nu tɛnlanle wɔ ɛvolɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ li zolɛ la anu. Edwɛkɛ ɛhye mɔ maa yɛsukoa ninyɛne kɛnlɛma, yɛɛ bɛkile Gyihova Nyamenle anwo ninyɛne dɔɔnwo.

YƐ GYIMA WƆ DIDILIE NE MGBƆDƆMGBƆDOƆ NU

3 Mɔɔ Yizilayɛlema bɔle ɔ bo kɛ bɛkɔ Kēnan azɛlɛ ne azo la, ɛnee Gibeɔnma de suakpole Gibeɔn mɔɔ bɛdo bane bɛyia ye kpalɛ la azo. Ɛnee bɛboka Hɛvima mbusuafoɔ ne anwo, Kēnan mbusuafoɔ nsuu ne mɔɔ bɛle “dɔɔnwo yɛɛ bɛlɛ tumi kpalɛ” tɛla Yizilayɛlema la ko. (Dɛt. 7:1) Ɔti ɛnee Gibeɔnma le menli mɔɔ ɛlɛ sɔgea tumi kpalɛ. amua Yizilayɛma anwo konle la ɛnrɛyɛ boɛ. Ɛnee bɛnwu kɛ Gyihova ɛlɛko amaa ye menli ne, yɛɛ ɛnee bɛze kɛ yebɔ ɛwɔkɛ kɛ ɔbavundu Keneanma yeavi azɛlɛ ne azo. ⁴ Kenga Dwɔhyua 9:8-13. Gibeonma ne mɔ yɛle kɛ asoloze a bɛvi a. Bɛliele bɛdole nu kɛ Gyihova a boale Yizilayɛma manle bɛlile Yigyibiti nee Amɔlema Ahemhene Sihon nee Ɔge anwo zo konim a. Noko wɔ nrɛlɛbɛ nu, bɛambɔ mɔɔ dole Gyɛleko nee Ayi la anwo edwɛkɛ,

kɛmɔ ɛnee edwɛkɛ fofolɛ zɛhae ɛnriale bɛ azɛlɛ mɔɔ bɛhanle kɛ ɔde “moamoa kpalɛ” la azo la ati. Menli mɔɔ gyi Yizilayɛma agyakɛ nu la ambiza Gyihova, bɛliele daselɛ mɔɔ Gibeonma lale ye ali la bɛdole nu na bɛ nee bɛ yɛle anzodwolɛ ngyegyelɛ. (Gyo. 9:14, 15) Yɛkɛ ekyi la, bɛnwunle kɛ Gibeonma bɛlɛbɛlale bɛ, noko Yizilayɛma lile bɛ anzodwolɛ ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ bɛ nee Gibeonma yɛle la azo ɔluakɛ bɛhanle “ndane bɛmaanle bɛ wɔ Gyihova duma nu.” (Gyo. 9:16-19) Bɛvale ɛzonlenlɛ gyima mɔɔ ɔwɔ aze la bɛmaanle Gibeonma kɛ bɛzonle dahuu kɛ “nyile ɛgboloma nee nzule ɛsalema maa eku ne yɛɛ Gyihova afɔlemokyea ne.”—Gyo. 9:27.

⁵ Mɔɔ Amɔleebema arelemgbunli nnu ne dele kɛ Gibeonbema nee Yizilayɛbema ɛyɛ anzodwolɛ la, bɛbɔle nu bɛhole kɛ bɛkɔho bɛatia Gibeon.

5. Ndenle boni mɔ azo a Gibeonma lale diedi ali wɔ Gyihova anu a?

Gibiyɔnma ne mɔ srenle Dwɔhsuwa kɛ ɔboa bɛ. (Dwo. 10:3-7) Dwɔhsuwa lile Yizilayɛma anyunlu hɔliele bɛ, na Nyamenle yilale bɛ mɔdenlebɔlɛ ne azo. Gyihova bɔbɔ doale esukpunli mgbole guale bɛ kpɔvolɛma Amɔlema ne anwo zo na ɔmaanle ewia tɛnlenle nuhua dɔɔnwo amaa bɛaho. (Dwo. 10:9-14) Kɛ mɔɔ bɛkpɔle anzodwolɛ ngyehyɛleɛ bɛbokale Yizilayɛma anwo na bɛsrenle Dwɔhsuwa kɛ ɔboa bɛ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛwɔ esiane nu la, Gibiyɔnma ne lale diedi ali wɔ Gyihova anu—diedi mɔɔ bɛlɛ wɔ tumi mɔɔ ɔlɛ kɛ ɔbaali ye ɛwɔkɛ nzonle na yealie bɛ la anu.

6 Duzu a edwɛkɛ ɛhye kilehile yɛ wɔ Gyihova anwo a? Gyihova bɛlɛ ɔ nwo aze yɛɛ ɔle anwunvɔnezelɛnli. Ɛnee yerɛlɛ yeamaa Yizilayɛma kɛ bɛ “voazu azɛlɛ ne azo amra kɔsɔɔti,” mɔɔ Gibeonma boka nwo la. (Pɛl. 33:51, 52) Noko, kɛmɔ ɛnee Gyihova kpondɛ kɛ Yizilayɛma di anzodwolɛ ngyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ bɛ nee bɛ yɛle la azo la ati, ɔzale ye anwunvɔne ɔmaanle Gibeonma dɛnlanle aze. Gyihova yɛle zɔ ɔnva nwo kɛ Yizilayɛma ambiza ye adwenle kolaa na bɛayɛ ngyehyɛleɛ zɔhane la. Akee ɔboale Yizilayɛma ɔmaanle bɛlile bɛ ngyehyɛleɛ ne azo yɛɛ ɔluale nwanwane adenle zo ɔliele Gibeonma.—Gyɔh. 9:26; 11:19.

7 Kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na yɛazukoa Gebeɔnma ne mɔ ɛ? Ɔlua diedi kpole mɔɔ yɛbanyia ye wɔ Gyihova anu la azo. Kɛmɔ yɛze Gyihova anwo ninyɛne dɔɔnwo tɛla kɛmɔ Gebeɔnma ne mɔ ze ye wɔ mekɛ zɔhane la ati, yɛlɛ ngilenu dɔɔnwo mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛdua zo yɛfa yɛ nwo yɛto Gyihova azo bɔkɔɔ a. (Edw. 40:4, 5) Eza yɛbahola yɛazukoa Gebeɔnma ne mɔ ɔlua mɛlɛbɛnwoaze gyima mɔɔ yɛbazi yɛ nwo kɛ yɛbayɛ ye wɔ Gyihova ɛzonlenlɛ nu la azo. (Dwo. 9:23, 27) Adiema raalɛ ko mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Luke la arɛle ɛnrɛvi mekɛ mɔɔ ɔnwunle kɛ adiema nrenyia kpanyinli bie mɔɔ wɔ Bɛtɛle abusua ne anu yɛle zɔhane la bɔkɔɔ. Ɔwɔ nuhua kɛ ɛnee Bɛtɛlenli zɔhane lɛ gyima kpole mɔɔ da ali bɔbɔ ɛdeɛ, noko ɔvale ɔ nwo mɛlɛbɛnwoaze zo ɔyɛle gyima nɔɔzo kɛ ɔbanlea Belemgbunlililɛ Asalo bie mɔɔ ɛnee bɛlɛsi la azo. Luke ka kɛ: “Menzukoale kɛ mɛlɛbɛnwoaze ɛnle nganeɛdelɛ ala. Ɔda ali wɔ ninyɛne mɔɔ yɛkpa kɛ yɛbayɛ anzɛɛ yɛnrɛyɛ la anu.” Ɔrɛla ye kɛ yɛbada yɛ diedi mɔɔ yɛlɛ ye wɔ Gyihova anu la ali dahuu ɔlua ɔ nwo mɔɔ yɛbava yɛado zolɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛbayia tɛnlabelɛ mɔɔ anu yɛ se la azo, na yɛali teokelase gyima biala anwo gyima wɔ mɛlɛbɛnwoaze nu.

KENDƐ GYIHOVA MAA YEZIEZIE NYELƐBƐBA TƐNLE

8 Bɛhanle Gyibeonma anwo edwɛkɛ bieko wɔ Baebolo ne anu wɔ ɛvolɛ dɔɔnwo anzi. Belemgbunli Sɔɔle hwenle ɔ nwo vile Gyibeonma mɔɔ de Yizilayɛ la anwo. “Ɔluakɛ ɛnee ɔkulo Yizilayɛ nee Dwuda menli ne kpalɛ la ati,” ɔbɔle mɔdenle kɛ ɔbazi Gyibeonma anwo eza.* Ɔti, bɛhunle bɛ nuhua dɔɔnwo. (2 Sam. 21:2, 5, 6) Nzinzanu kpole boni a le ɛhye! Sɔɔle bubule anzodwolɛ ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ ɛnee gyi Yizilayɛ nee Gyibeonma avinli mekɛ tendenle la.

9 Nzisi ɛtane kpole ɛhye mɔɔ bɛyɛle Gibeonma la, bɛanlie nwolɛ ndɛba. Nɔhalɛ nu, kɔkpula kɛ Seɔle agyidilɛnoma, Belemgbunli Devidi bara la, a Gyihova luale ɛhɔne nwo zo maanle maanle ne nwunle edwɛkɛ ne a.

* Baebolo ne engile fɔɔnwo deɛmoti Sɔɔle yɛle nvonleɛ ɛhye la. Abɔnsam nwo nrɛlɛbɛvolɛma bie mɔ kile kɛ mɔoti Sɔɔle yɛle zɔ la a le kɛ ɛnee ɔlɛ maanle nwo ɛhulolɛ kpole.

6. Kɛzi Gyihova nee Gibiɔnma lile wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ Yizilayɛma hɔlile Ɛwɔkɛ Azɛlɛ ne azo konle la maa yɛsukoa duzu wɔ ɔ nwo?

4 ƐZINZANLƐ ARANEA NE

Sukoa Gibeonma ne mɔ na nyia ɛhulolɛ kɛ ɛbayɛ gyima mɔɔ bɛbu ye aze la (Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 7)

ɛlɔne mɔɔ lile ɛvolɛ nsa. Mɔɔ Devidi bizale deɛmɔti ɛlɔne ne rale la, Gyihova hanle hilele ye kɛ Sɔɔlo nee ye sua nu amra ɛnli bembo wɔ kɛzi bɛyɛle Gibiɔnma amra ɛvolɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ ɛze ɛlɛ la ati.—2 Sam. 21:1.

10 Kenga 2 Samuel 21:3-6. David dendale Gibeyɔnma ne mɔ anwo kɛ ɔbanwu mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛyɛ na bɛava bɛali Sɔɔle ɛtane mɔɔ ɔyɛle bɛ la anwo gyima a. Asoo anrɛɛ bɛbabɔ mɔdenle kɛ bɛbanyia ezukoa wɔ kɛzi bɛbamaa David alie ezukoa kɛ ɔnleele bɛ aze ndɛba la ati? Gibeyɔnma ne mɔ buale kɛ nɔhalɛ nu ɔnle “dwetɛ fufule anzɛɛ dwetɛ kɔkɔle edwɛkɛ,” ɔluakɛ kɛ mɔɔ Mɛla ne kile la, bɛnlie kpɔtedɛne bɛmmaa sonlabuvolɛ. (Num. 35:30, 31) Eza bɛnwunle kɛ bɛngola bɛngu sonla mɔɔ mɛla mmaale bɛ adenle a. Awieleɛ koraa ne, David manle adenle kɛ bɛhu Sɔɔle amunli nrenyia nsuu mɔɔ bie a bɛvale bɛ nwo bɛwulale kɛzi Sɔɔle bɔle mɔdenle kɛ ɔkunkumi menli ne kɔsɔɔti la anu. Nzinlii, ezule dɔle, ɔyele ɛhɔne ne na ɔhilele kɛ bɛlie nenzilɛ ne anwo kakɛ mɔɔ sɔ Gyihova anye la.—2 Sam. 21:9, 10, 14.

11 Duzu a edwɛkɛ ɛhye kilehile yɛ wɔ Gyihova anwo a? Yɛnwu ye fofolɛ kɛ Gyihova le Nyamenle mɔɔ pɛ pɛlepɛle. (Edw. 37:28) Ɔkulo kɛ bɛli menli kɔsɔɔti anwo tenlene. Ɛhye kile bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛyɛ bɛ nyebolo bɔbɔ la, kɛ mɔɔ fane dɔɔnwo ne ala ɔtoa maanle gyɛne mɔ nee aneɛ gyɛne mɔ la. Wɔ ye mekɛ kpalɛ nu, Gyihova bayɛ nzenzaleɛ wɔ pɛlepɛlelilɛ biala mɔɔ ye azonvolɛ ɛnwu ye la anwo. Eza yɛsukoa kɛ Gyihova kpondɛ kɛ yɛdi yɛ ɛwɔkɛ nzonle, kɛ mɔɔ ɔkpɔlɛle kɛ Yizilayɛma kɛli bɛ anzodwolɛ ɛwɔkɛ mɔɔ bɛbɔle Gibiyɔnma la azo la.—Fa toto Emɔso 1:9 anwo.

12 Kɛzi yɛbahola yɛazukoa Gebeɔnma ne a? Saa yɛ mediema mrenyia nee mraalɛ ne mɔ ɛnyɛ yɛ kpalɛ bɔbɔ a, yɛbahola yɛava abotane yɛanlea Gyihova. Saa yɛyɛ zɔ a, yɛkile kɛ yɛlɛ diedi kɛ Gyihova batene ninyɛne wɔ ye mekɛ kpalɛ nu. Nea Adiema Raalɛ Laura French neazo ne. Ɔbɔle ɔ bo

sonle wɔ Canada Bɛtɛle wɔ 1926. Kɛyɛ ɛvolɛ bulu nzi, ɔyiale pɛlepɛlelilɛ nvonle mɔɔ yɛ alɔbɔlɛ kpalɛ la. Bɛdoole ɛzonle bɛdole ɔ nwo zo kɛ ɔ nee mɛtanevolɛma ekpunli bie la bɔ nu, na bɛdule ye bɛvile Bɛtɛle. Duzu a ɔbayɛ a? Ɔwɔ nuhua kɛ ɔdele nyane ɛdeɛ, noko yeangane nwiinwii kɛlɛ. DI NƆHALƐ SƆNƆLZOMƆLE NƆHALƐ NU ZO

13 Kɛ mɔɔ kɛyɛ ɛvolɛ 500 pɛle nu wɔ Belemgbunli David mekɛ ne anzi la, Gibeonma ne anu bie mɔ finle ɛkɛ ne bieko wɔ Baebolo kɛlɛtokɛ ne anu. Babilɔn ngaapezo ɛvolɛ 70 ne rale awieleɛ, na wɔ 537 K.Y.M. nu, Dwuuma ngaapezoma ekpunli mɔɔ lumua la liale Amrado Zerubabɛle anzi ziale rale Gyɛlusalɛm. (Ɛzɛla 2:1, 2, 58) Wɔ 468 K.Y.M. nu, ekpunli fofolɛ ziale rale noko bɛliale kɛlɛvonle Ɛzɛla anzi. (Ɛzɛla 7:1-7) Ngaapezoma ekpunli nwiɔ ne amuala mɔɔ bɛ nuhua menli ɛnzonle la anu bie mɔ a le “Nɛtenemma” ne mɔ abusua ne mɔ. (Nea ɔbodane ninyɛne mɔɔ wɔ Ɛzɛla 2:58 nee 7:7 la.) Nwane mɔ a le Nɛtenemma ne mɔ? Ɛnee bɛle “nyɛnzolema mɔɔ bɛnle Yizilayɛma mɔɔ bɛsonle wɔ ɛzonlenlɛ sua ne anu la,” na ɔda ali kɛ bɛ nuhua dɔɔnwo le Gibeonma ne mɔ abozoamra.—Nea Ngilenu, “Nɛtenemma.”

14 Kenga 1 Edwekɛnehe 9:2 nee ɔ bo ɛdendɛlɛ ne. “Ɛzonlenlɛsua ne azo azonvolɛ” ne mɔ boka menli mɔɔ lumua rale Yizilayɛ la anwo. Ɛhye anwo hyia ɔluakɛ tɛ Djuu mɔɔ wɔ akɛlɛzonlenlɛ nu la amuala a hɔle a. Ɔda ali kɛ Djuu dɔɔnwo nyianle ninyɛne dɔɔnwo wɔ Babelɔn, na ninyɛne mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛyɛ na bɛahɔboa bɛamaa bɛaziezie bɛ maanle mɔɔ ɛzɛkye la amba bɛ nye zo. Noko nɔhalɛ nzonlenlɛma ne mɔ, mɔɔ bie mɔ bie a le Gibeɔnma amozii la, kpondɛle kɛ bɛnwu kɛ bɛziezie nɔhalɛ ɛzonlenlɛ ne wɔ ɛzonlenlɛsua ne anu wɔ Gyɛlusalɛm, na bɛbɔle mɔdenle biala kɛ bɛbakɔ. Gibeɔnma amozii ne mɔ annyia azɛlɛ agya biala wɔ Yizilayɛ kɛ mɔɔ Djuu ne mɔ nyianle la. Noko, bɛziale bɛrale na bɛali bɛ ɛzonlenlɛsua ne azo gyima ne mɔ na bɛaboa bɛaziezie Gyɛlusalɛm bane ne amɔleboka.—Nɛh. 3:26.

15 Duzu a edwɛkɛ ɛhye kilehile yɛ wɔ Gyihova anwo a? Yɛnwu kɛ yɛ Nyamenle ne kpondɛ ye azonvolɛ nɔhalɛfoɔ ne mɔ edwɛkɛ kpole na dahuu ɔnea bɛ boɛ. Mɔɔ Babilɔn ngaapezo ne rale awieleɛ la, ɛnee kɛyɛ ɛvolɛ 1,000 ɛpɛ nu kɛvi mekɛ mɔɔ Gyihova liele Gebeɔnma wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ Yizilayɛma vale Ɛwɔkɛ Azɛlɛ ne la. Noko akee, ɔhɔle zolɛ ɔyilale bɛ. Gebeɔnma abo zo amra dele ngoane wɔ Yuda nee Gyɛlusalɛm ɛzɛkyelɛ ne mɔɔ rale wɔ 607 B.C.E. la anu. Ngaapezo ne anzi, bɛhɔle zolɛ bɛvale bɛ nwo bɛwulale nɔhalɛ ɛzonlenlɛ ne anu kpalɛ, na bɛ nee Livaima sonlele wɔ ɛzonlenlɛ sua ne anu. Ɔda ali kɛ bɛ nuhua bie mɔ dɛnlanle ɛzonlenlɛ sua ne anwo kɛkɛ. (Ɛzɛla 2:70; Nih. 11:21) Na bɛamma bɛandua adɔkɔ, duuti, nee ɛnwomenle ɛtane wɔ bɛ ɛzonlenlɛ sua ne anu gyima ne ati.—Ɛzɛla 7:24.

16 Kɛzi yɛbahola yɛazukoa Gibeonma ne ɛ? Yɛdayɛ noko yɛkulo kɛ yɛyɛ mɔɔ yɛbahola biala la yɛboa nɔhalɛ ɛzonlenlɛ ne. Yɛfi yɛ kulo nu yɛbɔ afɔle yɛsonle Gyihova ɔluakɛ yɛkulo ye na yɛkulo kɛ yɛsɔ ye anye. Zɔhane a Alwin mɔɔ le adiema nrenyia mɔɔ wɔ Philippines la yɛle a. Ɛnee ɔlɛ gyima mɔɔ bɛtua ye kakɛ kpole wɔ kɛnlaneɛ kɔmpɛne bie anu. Noko ɛnee ɔkulo kɛ ɔyɛ dɔɔnwo wɔ ye ɛzonlenlɛ nu ɔmaa Gyihova. Mekɛ biala mɔɔ maangyebakyi zo neavunli ne baha ye asafo ne anwo la, Alwin gyae gyima na yeaboa edwɛkɛhanlɛ gyima ne na ɔdie dahuu dɔɔnwo ɛbɔ nu wɔ daselɛlilɛ gyima ne anu. Mekɛ ekyi yɛɛ asɔneyɛlɛ dɔɔnwo anzi, Alwin bɔle kpɔkɛ kɛ ɔbaye ye gyima ne ɔbazi ɛkɛ na yeakpondɛ ko mɔɔ ɔbamaa yeayɛ dahuu dahuu adekpakyelɛnli la. Ɛhye mɔɔ ɔyɛle la manle ɔbɔle ninyɛne dɔɔnwo afɔle wɔ ezukwa afoa nu. Noko akee, ɔdwule ye bodane ne anwo. Ye aye noko bokale nwole manle bɛyɛle adekpakyelɛ gyima ne, na kɛ agyalɛma la, bɛholale bɛboale menli 21 bɛzukoale nɔhalɛ ne. Ɔda ali wienyi kɛ, Gyihova kpondɛ agyalɛma ɛhye mɔ mɔdenlebɔlɛ ne azo. Yɛdayɛ noko yɛbahola yɛanyia anwodozo kɛ Gyihova anye sɔ afɔlebɔlɛ biala mɔɔ yɛbɔ wɔ ye ɛzonlenlɛ nu la, na yemɔ ati, dahuu ɔbanlea yɛ ngyianlɛ nwo zo.—Mat. 6:33.

17 Baebolo nu ngɛlɛlera mɔɔ fane Gebeonma anwo la maa yɛnwu Gyihova subane kɛnlɛma ne mɔ kpalɛ. Ɔbɛlɛ ɔ nwo aze, ɔse anwunvɔne, ɔtenrɛ, yɛɛ ɔdi nɔhalɛ. Ɔyɛ ye azonvolɛ mɔɔ di nɔhalɛ la ahatualɛ dahuu. Eza Baebolo nu ngɛlɛlera ɛhye mɔ maa yenwu ninyɛne mɔɔ bamaa yɛanyia nvasoɛ la dɔɔnwo. Kɛ mɔɔ Gebeonma yɛle la, yɛkulo kɛ yɛnyia Gyihova anu diedi kpole, na yɛfa yɛ nwo yɛto ɔ nwo zo saa yɛkɔ ninyɛne mɔɔ bɔ ɛzulolɛ la anu a. Diedi ɛhye noko bamaa yɛabɛlɛ yɛ nwo aze yɛanlea teokrasi gyima biala mɔɔ bɛbava bɛawula yɛ sa la anwo debie. Yɛkulo kɛ yɛkɔ zo yɛto yɛ rɛle yɛgua Gyihova anwo zo, mɔɔ yenyia anwodozo kɛ awieleɛ bɔbɔ la ɔbaye nzonlazonlenyia biala yeavi ɛkɛ a. Na yɛfa mɔdenlebɔlɛ nee nɔhalɛlilɛ yɛboa nɔhalɛ ɛzonlenlɛ. Saa yɛyɛ zɔ a, yɛbahile kɛ yɛzukoa ninyɛne kpalɛ dɔɔnwo yɛvi Gebeonma ɛkɛ.

Nyia Arɛlekyekyelɛ Wɔ Ayezaya Buluku Ne Anu

“Mɔɔ Gyihova ka la ɛne: . . . ‘Kɛ mɔɔ mɛlɛbela kyekye ɔ ra nrenyia rɛle la, zɔhane ala a mebadɔ bɛ rɛle a.’”—AYIS. 66:12, 13.

PƐ KAKULA bie mɔɔ ye werɛ ahɔ ye aze na ɔlɛsu kpole la anwo nvoninli. Bie a ye abusua ne ɛlɛtu ahɔ azuazonle fofolɛ nu na ɛhye kile kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ ɔgyakyi ye gɔnwo mɔ ɛkɛ. Anzɛɛ bie a nvonle bie mɔɔ ɔyɛle la ɛmaa ye werɛ ɛbɔ kpole kpalɛ. Mekɛ ɛhye mɔ anu, duzu a maame mɔɔ lɛ ɛlɔlɛ la bayɛ a? Ɔbava ye kakula ne ɔbado ye kɛlɛkɛlɛ nu bɛtɛɛ, ɔbadie ye adwenlenzonlɛ mɔɔ ɔlɛsu ɔlɛka la, na yeamaa yeanyia anwodozo kɛ ɔkulo ye.

2 Wɔ Baebolo ne anu, Gyihova fa ɔ nwo toto nrenyia nu maame mɔɔ lɛ ɛlɔlɛ na ɔkyekye ɔ ra rɛle la anwo. Ɔka kɛ: “Kɛ mɔɔ maame kyekye ɔ ra nrenyia arɛle la, zɔhane ala a mebadɔ zo meakyekye bɛ arɛle a.” (Aye. 66:13) Gyihova ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ ɔlɛ maa yɛ la tɛla awovolɛ biala ɛdeɛ. (Aye. 49:15) Ɔlua Ye Edwɛkɛ Baebolo ne azo, Gyihova maa yɛ arɛlekyekyelɛ nee anwosesebɛ mɔɔ yɛhyia nwo ye mekɛ mɔɔ yɛ rɛle ɛbɔ anzɛɛ yɛwɔ ngyegyelɛ nu la.—Wlo. 15:4.

3 Yzaya buluku ne yɛ kɛkɛ wɔ arɛlekyekyelɛ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ vi Gyihova ɛkɛ la anu. (Ayz. 40:1) Yɛ Selɛ mɔɔ lala ɛlɔlɛ ali la maanle bɛhɛlɛle buluku ne amaa yeamaa ye menli ne anwosesebɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɛnee edwɛkɛ ɛlɛsi wɔ Dyuuda abakɛ anu la. Yɛdaye noko yɛwɔ “mekɛ mɔɔ yɛ se kɛ bɛgyinla ɔ nloa” la anu, yemɔti yɛhyia arɛlekyekyelɛ nwo. (2 Tim. 3:1) Bɛmaa yɛzuzu ngyɛnu bie mɔ mɔɔ wɔ Yzaya buluku ne anu mɔɔ bahola akyekye yɛ rɛle na yeamaa yɛ anwosesebɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ (1) yɛyia nzenzaleɛ mɔɔ yɛ nye ɛnla la anwo,

1-2. Kɛzi Gyihova yɛ ye nyɛleɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛkɔ ninyɛne mɔɔ maa yɛ rɛle bɔ nu la anu ɛ?

MEKƐ MƆƆ NGYEHYƐLEƐ KAKYI ARƐLEGBƆLƐ NU LA

4 Ninyɛne bahola azakyi wɔ yɛ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nu mɔɔ yɛnze kɛ ɔbazi a. (Nol. 9:11; 1 Kɔl. 7:31) Arɛlevilɛ nu kpondɛyenle bie bahola ado yɛ, ezukwo ngyegyelɛ, maanyɛlɛ nu basabasayɛlɛ, anzɛɛ yɛ gyima nu kakyelɛ. Nzenzaleɛ zɛhae mɔ bahola amaa yɛazi adwenle anzɛɛ yɛadendɛ bɛlɛbɛla. Ɔnle nwanwane kɛ yɛbabiza kɛ: ‘Kɛzi mebɔ me bo fofolɛ ɛ?’ Adiema nrenyia bie mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Joseph mɔɔ bɛdole ye bɛmanle ɔhɔle ɛzonlenlɛ gyima ne azo mɔɔ ɔzonlenle wɔ Bɛtɛle kɛyɛ ɛvolɛ 17 la ka kɛ: “Mendele nganeɛ kɛ mewo akpɔsa. Ɛnee ɔyɛ se kpalɛ kɛ mebɔ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ fofolɛ bo wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ medɛnla Bɛtɛle mekɛ tendenle la. Ɛnee menze ɛleka mɔɔ mebɔ bo a.” Anwodozo boni a Gyihova fa dua Ayezaya azo maa yɛ saa yɛyia nzenzaleɛ mɔɔ yɛnze kɛ ɔbazi a?

5 Kenga Ayezaya 42:16. Gyihova bɔ ɛwɔkɛ kɛ ɔbahile menli mɔɔ le anyezinliramgbɔkɛ wɔ sunsum nu la adenle wɔ ɛleka mɔɔ bɛnze ɛkɛ la na yeahakyi awozinli yeayɛ kɛnlema. Edwɛkɛmunli “anyezinliramgbɔkɛ,” “ɛleka mɔɔ bɛnze ɛkɛ,” “awozinli,” yɛɛ “awoka nu adenle” kola kile kɛzi yɛbu yɛ kenlebie mekɛ mɔɔ ninyɛne kakyile arɛlevilenu la. Ɔbahola yeayɛ yɛ kɛ asɛɛ yɛlɛtia ehyi bie mɔɔ ɛtɔ kpole nu, na yɛnze adenle mɔɔ yɛbalua zo la. Noko, Gyihova maa yɛ anwodoɔso kɛ tɛnlabelɛ biala mɔɔ yɛbayia la, ɔbamaa yɛ adehilelɛ mɔɔ yɛhyia nwo la na ɔnrɛgyakyi yɛ ɛlɛ. Ɔbasɔ yɛ sa na yealie yɛ yeavi awozinli ne anu, yeawula kɛnlema wɔ yɛ adenle zo na yeamaa awoka nu adenle ne ayɛ fɛtɛfɛtɛ. Kɛzi Gyihova yɛ ye

4. Kɛzi nzenzaleɛ arɛlevilɛ mɔɔ bazi la baha yɛ ɛ?

5. Kɛ mɔɔ yɛnwu ye wɔ Ayezaya 42:16 la, anwodozo boni a Gyihova maa yɛnyia a? (Nea nvoninli ne noko.)

Saa wɔ tɛnlabelɛ kakyi arɛlevilɛ nu bɔbɔ a, Gyihova ɛnkpɔkɛ wɔ ɛlɛ

(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 5)

“Wɔ adenle mɔɔ ɛnze la azo . . . , mengɛkpo [wɔ]”

ɛhye? Ɔdua ye Edwɛkɛ ne nee ye ahyehyɛdeɛ ne azo ɔmaa yɛ folɛducɛlɛ kpalɛ. (Edw. 119:105; Aye. 30:21) Ɔdua yɛ mediema diedima nee awie mɔ noko azo ɔboa yɛ wɔ ninyɛne dɔɔnwo anu. Saa adenle mɔɔ yɛwɔ zo la anu ɛnla kɛlata yɛɛ ɔyɛ se bɔbɔ a, yɛbahola yɛalie yɛali kɛ Gyihova balua ɛlɔlɛ nee nteaseɛ zo ahile yɛ adenle.—Aye. 41:10; 1 Pita 5:6, 7.

6 Joseph mɔɔ yɛlimoa yɛhanle ɔ nwo edwɛkɛ la hilele kɛzi ɔ kɛrɛ mɔɔ ɔvi Bɛtɛle la, ɔ nee ɔ ye nwunle Gyihova ɛlɔlɛ moalɛ la. Ɔka kɛ: “Yɛnwunle Gyihova ɛlɔlɛ saa yɛwɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛkakyi yɛ gyima la anu. Ɔluale mediema mrenyia nee mraalɛ kɛnlɛma zo ɔboale yɛ. Mekɛ kpalɛ pɛkɛpɛkɛ anu, ɔnwunle nwonane afoa nu nee nganeɛdelɛ afoa nu ninyɛne mɔɔ yɛhyia nwo la, na ɔkɔ zo ɔyɛ zɔ.” Saa debie mɔɔ ɛnze kakyi wɔ asetɛnla nu a, ɛdawɔ noko ɛbahola wɔanyia anwodozo kɛ, Gyihova bamaa wɔ anwosesebɛ nee adehilelɛ mɔɔ ɛhyia nwo la, ɔnva nwo kɛzi kakyelɛ ne bayɛ se amaa wɔ la. (Aye. 54:10; 58:11) Joseph dozo kɛ: “Edwɛkɛ mɔɔ wɔ yɛ edwɛne fofolɛ ne mɔ ko anu la ka ye kɛnlɛma kɛ, ‘Mɔɔ ɛhyema bava ara la, menze, noko menze kɛ menrɛli nwolɛ gyima ngome.’* Ɛhɛe, ɔwɔ nuhua kɛ bie a ninyɛne ɛnrɛhɔ kɛ mɔɔ yɛkpondɛ la ɛdeɛ, noko debie ko le nɔhalɛ: Gyihova nee yɛ badu agyakɛ biala.”

SAANE NGOANE DI WƆ FO DƆƆNWO LA

7 Saa yɛka ɛtane kpole mɔɔ yɛyɛ la anwo edwɛkɛ yɛkile a, yɛnyia ɛlɔl-

* Edwɛkɛ ne mɔ vi edwɛne kɛlɛboka ne mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye “Meze Kɛ Gyihova Boka Me Nwo” la anu.

6. Saa yɛ tɛnlabelɛ kakyi arɛlevilenu a, duzu a yɛbahola yɛanyia nwolɛ anwodozo a?

7. Duzu ngyegyelɛ a bie mɔ bahola anyia ye wɔ bɛ nvonle dɛba ne mɔ ati a?

kɛ yɛkpondɛ moalɛ yɛfi mgbanyima ne mɔ ɛkɛ na akee yɛbɔ mɔdenle kɛ yɛnrɛyɛ ɛtane ne bieko la. (Mrɛ. 28:13; Jms. 5:14-16; 1 Jɔn 1:9) Noko akee, saa bɛse bɛze yɛ anwunvɔne bɔbɔ a, bie a yɛbahɔ zo yɛadwenle kɛ yɛle fɔfonle. Bie a yɛbadwenle kɛ, ‘Kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na Gyihova ade me ɛtane mɔɔ meyɛ la ɛlɛ?’ (2 Kɔr. 2:7) Ɔnyɛ nwanwane kɛ yɛbade nganeɛ zɛhane, ɔluakɛ nvonleɛ mɔɔ yɛyɛle la anwo adwenle mɔɔ yɛbanyia la bahola ayɛ kpole na yɛnrɛlafi nwolɛ. Belemgbunli Devidi hɛlɛle kɛ: “Me nwonane mɔ anu ɛnde me kpalɛ ɔlua me ɛtane ne ati. Mebɔ me kɛkɛ na mebubu me bɔbɔ.” (Edw. 38:3, 8) Saa ɔdwu mekɛ na ɛte nganeɛ zɛhane ɔlua nvonleɛ mɔɔ ɛyɛle la ati a, Ayezaya buluku ne kyelekyele yɛ rɛle.

8 Kenga Ayezaya 1:18. Gyihova bɔle edwɛkɛ mɔɔ da ali wienyi la mɔɔ kile kɛzi ɔfa yɛ ɛtane ɔkyɛ yɛ kpalɛ la anwo mfonin. Ɔka kɛ, saa yɛ ɛtane ne mɔ “yɛ kɔkɔlɛ kɛ mogya bɔbɔ” a, ɔbamaa bɛayɛ “fufule kɛ suulo la.” Kɔkɔlɛ mɔɔ le kɛ mogya la le kɔkɔlɛ mɔɔ te kpalɛ na ɔmmaa ɔndu mɔnwo ndɛ. Noko, Gyihova maa yɛ nzonnilɛ kɛ saa ɔfa yɛ ɛtane ɔkyɛ yɛ a, zɔhane ɛtane ne mɔ ɛnzonlɛ ɛnrɛhɛ yɛ kenle amuala.

9 Suzu Belemgbunli Devidi neazo ne anwo. Ɔwɔ nuhua kɛ ɔyɛle agbɔfe yɛɛ ɔhunle sonla ɛdeɛ, noko Gyihova dɔle ye ahyɛ yɛɛ nzinlii ɔhanle kɛ ɔle sonla mɔɔ di nɔhalɛ. (2 Sam. 11:3, 4, 14, 15; 12:13; 1 Ar. 9:4, 5) Gyihova amva Devidi nvonleɛ ne mɔ amvua ye ndɛne, emomu ɔnleanle nɔhalɛ mɔɔ ɔlile la. Zɔhane ala a Gyihova fa ɛlɔlɛ kyɛ yɛ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛnu yɛ nwo la, yɛɛ ɔboa yɛ ma yɛkɔ yɛ nyunlu wɔ adwenle mɔɔ anu te la anu a. Anwodozo fofolɛ boni a yɛbahola yɛanyia ye wɔ Ayezaya buluku ne anu saa yɛte nganeɛ kɛ yɛ nvonleɛ ne mɔ ɛnzo kpole sombaa a?

8-9. Kɛ mɔɔ bɛha nwolɛ edwɛkɛ wɔ Yzaya 1:18 la, kɛzi Gyihova ɛtanefakyɛ ne di munli ɛ?

10 ƐZINLÎ NE

“Wovonle me nvonle kɔsɔɔti woguale wɔ azi”

Gyihova engyinla yɛ nvonle mɔɔ yɛyɛle wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɛze ɛlɛ la azo ɛmbua yɛ (Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 11)

Mmbu adenle kɛ wɔ nvonle mɔɔ ɛyɛle wɔ tete ne anzɛɛ mɔɔ ɛze ɛlɛ la ɛzɛkye wɔ anyelielɛ mɔɔ ɛlɛ ye kɛkala la dɔɔnwo.

10 Kenga Ayezaya 38:17 nee ɔ bo ɛdendɛlɛ ne. Ɛdendɛkpunli ne mɔɔ se “wɔdo me nvonle kɔsauti wɔdo ɛ nzi” la kɔhola kɛyɛ “wɔye me nvonle kɔsauti wɔvi ɛ nye zo.” Ɛke bɛkile kɛ Gyihova fa ahenle mɔɔ ɛnu ɔ nwo la nvonle to ɔ nzi, ɛleka mɔɔ ɔnnwu bɛ bieko anzɛɛ ɔndwenle nwolɛ la. Kɛ mɔɔ neɛnleanu buluku bie kile la, bɛhola bɛka edwɛkɛ ne kɛ: “Wɔyɛ [me nvonle] kɛ asɛɛ ɔnzile bɔbɔ.” Ɔti saa Gyihova fa yɛ ɛtane kyɛ yɛ a, ɔte kɛ asɛɛ yɛtɛyɛle ɛtane ɛlɛ. Saa Gyihova fa kyɛ yɛ a, ɔnrɛha yɛ alagye ɛtane ne mɔ edwɛkɛ bieko kɛ ɔfa yeabɔ yɛ somolɛ anzɛɛ yeawea yɛ nzo. Ɔkpea nvonle zɔhane mɔ ngɛlata bɔkɔɔ. Ɔbɔ ɛwɔkɛ kɛ: “Me rɛle bɛ nvonle ne mɔ avi bɔkɔɔ.” (Aye. 43:25) Nea kɛzi ɛhye kyekye yɛ rɛle! EDWENE 12 Nyamenle Kpole, Gyihova

AGYA Mɔɔ Gyihova mɔɔ yɛbanwu ye kpalɛ kile, deɛmɔti ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkpondɛ kɛ yɛnwu ye kpalɛ, yɛɛ kɛzi yɛbahola yɛayɛ zɔ la.

Ɛlɛnwu Gyihova Kpalɛ Ɔ?

“Nwu wɔ selɛ Nyamenle ne na fa ahonle mɔɔ di munli sonle ye.”—1 EDW. 28:9.

WƆDWEŊLE Gyihova ndelebɛbo ne anwuma, ye nrɛlɛbɛ kpole ne, anzɛɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ yeyɛ la anwo ɛlɛ a? Saa yɛdwenledwenle yɛ Bɔvolɛ ne anwo a, yɛbɛlɛ yɛ nwo aze. Bie a ɛte nganeɛ kɛ mɔɔ ɛzoanvolɛ Pɔɔlo hanle la, mɔɔ ɔhɛlɛle kɛ: “O Nyamenle anwonyia nee ye nrɛlɛbɛ nee ye ndelebɛbo ne dɔɔnwo amgba! Kɛzi yɛnrɛhola yɛnrɛpɛ ye ndɛnebualɛ nwo kpole na yɛnrɛnwu ye ndenle ne mɔ kpalɛ la!”—Wulo. 11:33, 34.

2 Ɔwɔ nuhua kɛ yɛnrɛhola yɛnrɛze ninyɛne mɔɔ fane Gyihova anwo la amuala ɛlɛ ɛdeɛ, noko ɔyɛ boɛ kɛ yɛbaze subane mɔɔ ɔlɛ la wɔ debie bie mɔ anu. Belemgbunli Devidi hanle hilele ɔ ra Sɔlɔmɔn kɛ: “Nwu wɔ papa Nyamenle ne na fa ahonle mɔɔ tɛla munli sonle ye . . . Saa ɛkpondɛ ye a, ɔbamaa wɔanwu ye.” (1 Ekun. 28:9) Kɛ mɔɔ ɛvoya dɔɔnwo anzi ɛzoanvolɛ Dwɔn hakyele ye Kilisienenma gɔnwo mɔ la, yɛlɛ “nwunlɛ kɛ yɛbanyia nɔhalɛ nwozelɛ mɔɔ kɔ nɔhalɛ Sonla ne anwo la.” (1 Dwɔn 5:20) Yɛɛ Gyisɛse mumua ne hanle kɛ nɔhalɛ nwozelɛ mɔɔ fane Gyihova anwo la wɔ ɛkɛ maa “awie biala mɔɔ Ɔ Ra ne kpondɛ kɛ ɔda ye ali ɔkile ye la.”—Mat. 11:27.

3 Menli mɔɔ bɛyila bɛ nwo zo bɛmaa Gyihova la amuala ɛzukoa ye nwo ninyɛne dɔɔnwo dɛba. Noko, yɛngyakyi kɛ yɛbamaa yɛ ‘anwosesebɛ ayɛ kpole wɔ Gyihova anwo nrɛlɛbɛ mɔɔ yɛnyia la anu.’ (Dwɔn 17:3; nea ɛzukoalɛ edwɛkɛ.)

4 Yɛsukoa debie mɔɔ boka Gyihova mɔɔ yɛbanwu ye la anwo wɔ mɔɔ ɔhanle ɔhlanle Dyuuma mɔɔ bavi akɛlɛzonlenlɛ nu azia arale la anwo la anu. Gyihova bɔle ɛwɔkɛ kɛ: “Mebanrɛ bɛ ahonle mɔɔ banwu me kɛ medame a le Gyihova a.” (Gyɛ. 24:7) Nɔhalɛ nu, menli mɔɔ vi Babelɔn ziale rale la ɛnee ze Gyihova anwo ninyɛne bie mɔ dɛba. Noko ɔwɔ kɛ bɛ ahonle, mɔɔ boka bɛ nganeɛdelɛ nee bɛ ngyinlazo nwo la kɔ bɛ mɔdenlebɔlɛ mɔɔ bɛbaye kɛ bɛbanwu Gyihova la anu. Mbuluku bie ka ye kɛ: “Ngakyile kpole wɔ Nyamenle anwo debie mɔɔ bɛze nee Nyamenle mɔɔ bɛbanwu ye la anu. Ɔle kɛ ngakyile mɔɔ wɔ ɛlɔlɛ nwo debie mɔɔ bɛkenga nee ɛlɔlɛ nu mɔɔ bɛbadɔ la anu la.” Ɔhyia kɛ yɛnwu nɔhalɛ mɔɔ le kɛ Gyihova lɛ duma na ɔle Nyamenle la azo tɛla zɔ. Ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkpondɛ kɛ yɛnwu Sonla mɔɔ wɔ duma ne anzi la.

5 Mekɛ mɔɔ yɛbanwu Gyihova la, yɛsukoa ninyɛne mɔɔ maa ye anye gye nee mɔɔ ɔngulo la. Kɛ neazo la, suzu Belemgbunli Dwosaea neazo ne anwo. Baebolo ne ka kɛ: “Wɔ ye ebiaye ne anu ɛvolɛ mɔɔ tɔ zo 8 la, mekɛ mɔɔ ɛnee ɔtɛle kakula la, ɔbɔle ɔ bo kɛ

kpondɛ ye nlenya Devidi Nyamenle ne.” (2 Kroy. 34:3) Noko Dwosaea anyɛ ye kɛ ɔbazukoa debie ala. Ɔvale mɔɔ ɔsukoale la ɔbɔle ye ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nu. Gyɛlemaya ngapezonli ne dua zo, Gyihova hanle kɛ Dwosaea “lile pɛlepɛlelilɛ mini” na ɔliele amgbɔnbɔnli nee ehyianli. Akee Gyihova doale zo kɛ: “Asoo kɛ ɛbanwu me la a le zɔ?”—Gyɛ. 22:15, 16.

6 Kenga Edwɛne 9:10. Mekɛ mɔɔ yɛbanwu Gyihova kpalɛ la, yɛbanyia ye nu anwodozo kpalɛ. Zɔhane ngyinlazo ne rale nu wɔ ɛzoanvo Pɔɔlo afoa nu. Mekɛ mɔɔ ɛnee bado ye efiade ne mɔɔ li amozi la na ɔnrɛhyɛ na yeawu la, ɔhɛlɛle kɛ: “Menyɛ nyiane. Ɔluakɛ menze Ahenle mɔɔ medie ye medi la.” (2 Tim. 1:12) Ninyɛne mɔɔ Pɔɔlo zukoale na ɔyiale wɔ ye ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nu la hanle ye manle ɔnyianle ye Nyamenle ne anu anwodozo kpalɛ. Kɛ mɔɔ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ baha nwolɛ edwɛkɛ kile wɔ zɔhane ngyɛnu ne anwo la kilehile nu la: “Pɔɔlo . . . nyianle anwodozo kɛ Gyihova bakakye ye nɔhalɛlilɛ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ ne kɔkpula ‘kenle zɔhane’ mɔɔ Ɔbadwazo ye la.”

8 Mekɛ mɔɔ yɛbanwu Gyihova kpalɛ la, yɛ nye mɔɔ yɛ nee ye lɛ la bayɛ kpole. Kɛ neazo la, kɛzi ɛte nganeɛ wɔ Gyihova anwo wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ wɔgenga Baebolo ne dahuu, wɔnea siane biala gyima ne bie, anzɛɛ wɔhɔ maangyebakyi anzɛɛ nyianu la ɛ? Fane dɔɔnwo ne ala, yɛte nganeɛ kɛ yɛbikye yɛ anwuma Selɛ ne kpalɛ. Duzu ati ɔ? Ɔluakɛ yɛsukoa ye anwo ninyɛne dɔɔnwo. Ɔlua ɛhye ati, yɛkulo kɛ yɛye ye ayɛlɛ na yɛda ye ase. Yɛte nganeɛ kɛ edwɛndolɛnli ne mɔɔ dwenledwenle kɛzi Gyihova neɛnlea ɔ nwo la anwo na ɔhanle kɛ: “Nyamenle a le me ahonle bolɛ nee me kɛnyɛ dahuu amgba a.” (Kenga Edwɛndolɛ 73:24-28.) Mɔdenle biala mɔɔ yɛbɔ kɛ yɛbanwu Gyihova la maa yɛ nye die agɔnwolɛvalɛ sonlebolɛ mɔɔ yɛ nee ye lɛ la anwo kpalɛ.

9 Saa yɛ “nea” Gyihova—yɛkakye mɔɔ yɛzukoa yɛvile ɔ nwo la na yɛdwenledwenle kɛzi yɛ nyɛleɛ ka ye la anwo a—yɛbazi kpɔkɛ kpalɛ. (Mrɛ. 3:5, 6) Kɛ neazo la: Mediemanynli nee mediemambaa dɔɔnwo fa bɛ ye anzɛɛ bɛ hu fotu sie bɛ ɛkponle zo, bɛ fukponle nu, anzɛɛ bɛ fonu zo. Ɛhye boa bɛ ma bɛdwenledwenle bɛ ye anzɛɛ bɛ hu anwo dahuu kɛlɛkɛlɛ, bɛsi kpɔkɛ mɔɔ bɛze kɛ ɔbazɔ ye anye, na bɛkoati debie mɔɔ bɛyɛ mɔɔ bamaa ahenle ali nyane la. Zɔhane ala a kɛzi yɛdwenledwenle Gyihova anwo dahuu la, zɔhane ala a ɔbamaa yɛazi kpɔkɛ mɔɔ bazo ye anye a.—Edw. 19:13, 14.

10 Bɔbɔle yɛ wɔ Gyihova subane zo, yemɔ a maa yɛkola yɛda ye subane ne mɔ ali a. (Gyn. 1:26) Eza kɛ mɔɔ yɛsukoa Gyihova subane ne mɔ anwo ninyɛne dɔɔnwo la, zɔhane ala a yɛbade kɛzi yɛbazukoa ye la abo kpalɛ a. Gyisɛse hwenle mɛla kɛlɛvoma mini Falasifooma ne mɔ anzo kɛ bɛanda Nyamenle subane ne mɔ bɛanli la ati. Boa Awie Mɔ Maa Bɛnwu Gyihova

“Ɛhye a le dahuu ngoane, kɛ bɛbanwu wɔ, wɔ mɔɔ ɛ ngome ɛle Nyamenle nɔhalɛ ne la.”—DWƆN 17:3.

KƐZI ɛte nganeɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ Baebolo sukunli bie kɔ ye asafo ayia mɔɔ limoa, ɔbɔ ɔ bo kɛ ɔbua ngɛlɛlera ne mualɛ, ɔka ye diedi ne anwo edwɛkɛ ɔkile awie mɔ, yɛɛ nzinlii ɔsi kpɔkɛ kɛ ɔbatu ɔ nwo yeamaa Gyihova Nyamenle a? Ɔkɛyɛ zɔhane a, ɔbale ɛ sa nu kpalɛ! Nɔhalɛ nu, Gyihova a fɛta kɛ yɛye ye ayɛlɛ wɔ sunsum nu anyuhɔlɛ mɔɔ yɛ Baebolo sukunli ne nyianle la anwo a. Saa yɛnwu kɛzi Gyihova boa awie maamaa ɔnyinli sunsum nu a, yɛye Gyihova ayɛlɛ.—1 Kɔl. 3:5-9; 3 Dwɔn 3, 4.

2 Nyamenle ɛhulolɛdeɛ a le kɛ “menli ngakyile kɔsɔɔti ɛnyia ngoane na bɛanwu nɔhalɛ ne kpalɛ.” (Kenga 1 Timote 2:3, 4.) Gyihova kpondɛ kɛ menli banwu ɔdaye nee ye bodane ne mɔ kpalɛ kɛmɔ bɛbahola la. (Gye. 9:24) Ɛhye kile kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛnyia nɔhalɛ nwonlɛ kpalɛ. Bɛhilele edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ mɔɔ bɛlile mɔlebɛbo aneɛ ne anu kɛ “nɔhalɛ nwonlɛ kpalɛ” la abo kɛ, tɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ awie suzu nwo ala a ɔsukoa a, na emomu nwonlɛ mɔɔ “ka ahenle kpalɛ,” kɛmɔ Baebolo nwomamrenyi bie hanle la. Saa Baebolo sukoavo bie banwu Gyihova a, zɔhane nwonlɛ ne ka adenle mɔɔ ɔdua zo ɔbɔ ye ɛbɛla la. Eza ɔbuke adenle ɔmaa ye kɛ ɔbanyia “dahuu ngoane.”—Dwɔn 17:3.

3 Gyihova ɛva nwolɛ adenle ɛmaa yɛ kɛ yɛboa awie mɔ maa bɛnwu ye. Wɔ edwɛkɛ ɛhye anu, yɛbazuzu kpuya ɛhye mɔ anwo: Kɛmɔti a ɔhyia kɛ Baebolo sukoavoma nwu Gyihova kpalɛ a? Kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na yɛahilehile bɛ subane ngakyile mɔɔ Gyihova lɛ la? Na kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na yɛaboa bɛ na bɛava bɛ

4 Gyisɛse hanle kɛ mɛla kpole kpatu a le kɛ yɛkulo Gyihova kɛvi yɛ ahonle, yɛ ɛkɛla, yɛ adwenle, nee yɛ anwosesebɛ muala anu. (Kenga Maake 12:30.) Ɔwɔ kɛ Baebolo sukoavoo bie nwu Gyihova na yeahola yeakulo ye. Yɛkpondɛ kɛ yɛ Baebolo sukoavoo ne yila ɔ nwo zo—tɛ yɛdayɛ a, tɛ ngilehilelɛ bie a, tɛ ahyehyɛdeɛ bie a—emomu Gyihova. Yemɔti, bodane kpole mɔɔ yɛlɛ ye wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛyɛ Baebolo ɛzukoalɛ la a le kɛ yɛbaboa yɛ sukoavoo ne yeanwu Gyihova na yeakulo ye.

5 Ɛlɔlɛ nee nɛhalɛlilɛ le ko. Saa wɔ Baebolo suani ne banwu Gyihova na ɔkulo ye a, ɔbanyia ɛhulolɛ kɛ ɔbedie ye. Gyisɛse hanle kɛ: “Amaamuo ewiade ne anwu kɛ mekulo Selɛ ne la ati, meleyɛ kɛmɔ Selɛ ne ɛha ɛhile me kɛ menyɛ la pɛpɛɛpɛ.” (Dwɔn 14:31) Zɔhane ala a ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ Baebolo suani banyia amaa Gyihova la—mɔɔ tɛla debie biala la—bamaa yealie ye na yeava ɔ nwo yeabeta ɔ nwo a.—1 Dwɔn 5:3.

6 Baebolo sukoavoma mɔɔ banwu Gyihova kpalɛ la bazi kpɔkɛ kpalɛ. Kɛ neazo la: Manle manle avinli anwoma-lɔ-hyɛn kafo ɛnsua kɛ ɔkanyi hyɛn la ɔlua adehilelɛ mɔɔ ɔbazukoa anzɛɛ sɔtlhɛ mɔɔ ɔbagye la ala azo. Ɔwɔ kɛ ɔkanyi hyɛn ne dɔnehwele dɔɔnwo noko. Mɔɔ mekɛ ɛlɛpɛ nu la, ɔnwu kɛzi anwoma-lɔ-hyɛn ne yɛ ye adeɛ wɔ tɛnlabelɛ ngakyile nu la yɛɛ kɛzi kpɔkɛ mɔɔ ɔsi la ka anwoma-lɔ-hyɛn ne ɛka la. Zɔhane ala a Baebolo sukoavo banwu Gyihova ɔnle kɛ ɔbazukoa debie ala a, emomu ɔbanwu kɛzi ninyɛne si kɔ boɛ maa ye wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɔsi kpɔkɛ mɔɔ gyi Ngɛlɛlera ngyinlazo zo la. Kɛ mɔɔ ɔlɛnyia Gyihova anwo nɔhalɛ nrɛlɛbɛ dɔɔnwo yɛɛ ɔnwu kɛzi ye anwuma Selɛ ne boa ye la, ɔbayɛ krɛkrɛ kɛ ɔbazi kpɔkɛ kpalɛ wɔ tɛnlabelɛ biala anu.

7 Ninyɛne mɔɔ Dwosɛfi ze ye wɔ Gyihova anwo la boale ye mekɛ mɔɔ Pɔtefa aye bɔle mɔdenle kɛ ɔbɛlɛbɛla ye la. Ɔda ali wienyi kɛ, ɔvi Dwosɛfi ngakula nu la, ɔdele Adamo nee Yivi yɛɛ Ebileham nee Sɛla anwo kɛlɛtokɛ ne mɔ. (Gyn. 2:24; 20:3, 6) Dwosɛfi nwunle kɛzi Gyihova bu agyaleɛ nee nna kyɛfa ne la vile kɛlɛtokɛ zɔhane mɔ anu. Dwosɛfi ranwunle Gyihova kpalɛ kɛ ɔkpɔle Pɔtefa aye abodane ne na ɔzele ye kɛ: “Kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na menyɛ ɛtane kpole ɛhye na menzɔ Gyihova nyane ɛ?” (Gyn. 39:7-9) Dwosɛfi ze mɔɔ le kpalɛ nee mɔɔ le ɛtane wɔ Gyihova anyunlu la. Ɔdele mɔɔ babɔ ye Nyamenle ne nyane la abo. Ɔlua ɛhye ati, Dwosɛfi ziele kpɔkɛ kpalɛ. Saa yɛboa yɛ Baebolo sukoavoma ma bɛnwu Gyihova kpalɛ na bɛnwu kɛzi ɔde la a, bɛdaye noko bɛazie kpɔkɛ mɔɔ sɔ ye anye la.

MENRƐLƐVI kenle mɔɔ limoa mɔɔ menganle me nwo wɔ Buluku Ɛyɛlɛ Ɛleka ne wɔ Bɛtɛle mɔɔ wɔ Brooklyn, New York, U.S.A. la ɛlɛ. Buluku ɛwɔlɔ ne mɔ ɛlɛkɔ anwoma ne anu, na anwoma ne nu amuala gyinlanle ngo ɛnwonlɛ.

Ninyɛne dɔɔnwo ne mɔɔ hanle me ahonle la anu, menli ne a hanle me ahonle kpole kpalɛ a. Mgbavolɛ nee mbɛra ɛhye mɔ yɛ gyima mɔɔ zɔho kɛ ɛnee ɛnle anyelielɛ la. Ɛnee bɛ nye ɛnlie duma kpole nwo. Noko, ɛnee bɛ nye lie yɛɛ bɛkpondɛ dɔɔnwo. Bɛ neazo ne hanle me ahonle mɔɔ kile mɔɔ ɔkile kɛ bɛbazi bɛ nwo aze bɛazonle Nyamenle la.

Wɔ ɛvolɛ ne mɔ anu, Gyihova ɛhɔ zo ɛdɛlɛ me amaa meayɛ ye sonvolɛ kpalɛ. Noko mɔɔ lumua la, maa menga kɛzi menwunle nɔhalɛ ne mɔɔ ɛnee mele Buda ɛzonlenlɛnli la anwo edwɛkɛ mehile wɔ.

NVILE NNYEERA NE MENKPUKPUNLE NYANMELE

Bɛwole me yɛɛ bɛtetɛle me wɔ Chicago, Illinois, U.S.A., me a mele ngakula nna ne anu kpanyinli a. Me awovolɛ hɔle Chicago vile Japan kɛ bɛkɔpɛ mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye American dream ne la. Ɛnee bɛkulo kɛ bɛ mra nyia ngɛlata kpalɛ yɛɛ bɛsi bɛ bo zo wɔ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nu.

Me awovolɛ noko mɔɔ vi bɛ ahonle nu la yɛle Buddha ɛzonlenlɛma. Bɛtetele me mame wɔ sua mɔɔ Buddha ɛzonlenlɛ sua wɔ nu la anu. Ye papa nee ye amra mrenyia nwiɔ sonle kɛ Buddha ɛzonlenlɛ asɔfoma. Yemɔti, Buddha ɛzonlenlɛ lile dwuma kpole kpalɛ wɔ me ngakula nu. Debie mɔɔ limoa mɔɔ mekayɛ ye wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ mevi sukulu merazia sua nu la a le kɛ mebawula ehyire na meava ɛlesi nee nzule meamaa yɛ Buddha awozonle ne. Wɔ Molɛkɛ, yɛkɔ Buddha ɛzonlenlɛ sua nu ɛleka mɔɔ yɛto edwɛne yɛmaa awozonle bie mɔɔ le kɛ yemɔ ala la—mɔɔ ngakyile ne ala a le kɛ noko yemɔ le kpole na bɛfa sika kɔkɔla bɛbula nwole.

Mekɛ mɔɔ ɛnee meli kɛyɛ ɛvolɛ nsuu la, mekakye kɛ menea awozonle mɔɔ ɛnee wɔ yɛ sua nu la na medwenle nwo kɛ, ‘Saa mewu a, kɛ me ngoane bayɛ ɛ?’ Ɛnee Buddha ɛzonlenlɛ ɛhilehile me kɛ saa mewu a mebadɛnla sunsum arole ne azo ɛleka bie. Mɔɔ lumua, bie a ɛkpondɛ kɛ ɛbu ɛ nye ɛgua tɛnlabelɛ ne azo na wɔanlie wɔanli, bie a ɔluakɛ ɛngpondɛ kɛ ɛka ngyegyelɛ nwo edwɛkɛ.# Dwenle Yuwodiya nee Sentekɛ nwo, bɛle Kilisiene mraalɛ mɔɔ ɔzɔho kɛ bɛangola bɛanlie bɛ adwenlenyianu ne bɛanli a. Ɔda ali kɛ bɛ ngyegyelɛ ne hɔle zo, ɔluakɛ ɛzoanvolɛ Pɔɔlo mɔɔ ɛnee wɔ moa wɔ Wulomu la bɔbɔ dele nwolɛ edwɛkɛ. Ɔdule bɛ folɛ kɛ bɛyɛ biala mɔɔ bɛbahola la na amaa bɛ adwenle ayɛ ko bieko. (Fil. 4:2) Wɔ bɛtɛyɛlɛ, adwelielilɛ kpalɛ, nee nɔhalɛ ɛtanikyekye nu la, ɛnee bɛ agonwolɛvalɛ ne bahola ayɛ kpalɛ bieko.

Mɔɔ tɔ zo nwiɔ, bie a ɛbaye ɛ nzonle kɛ akee ahenle kola gye ye fɔlɛto nu. Noko ɛkulo kɛ ɛnyia ɛ gɔnwo ne bieko, tɛ kɛ ɛbamaa yeanwu fɔlɛ mɔɔ ɔbɔle la ɔ. Dɔketa mɔɔ ɛlɛyɛ ahenle mɔɔ ye bowule ɛbu la ayile la fa ye adwenle sie kɛzi ɔbaye la azo, tɛ debie biala mɔɔ maanle bowule ne bule la a ɔkpondɛ ɔbanwu ye a. Saa ɛ nee ɛ gɔnwo ne ɛlɛtendɛ a, mmadwenle nuhua debie biala mɔɔ ɔbɔle fɔlɛ wɔ edwɛkɛ ne anwo la azo. Emomu, kakye kɛzi ɛ gɔnwolilɛ ne sonle bolɛ maa wɔ la na fa wɔ adwenle sie mɔɔ ɛbahola wɔayɛ na bɛaziezie la azo.

Duzu ati a ɛnleka ɛ nee ɛ mediema nrenyia anzɛɛ raalɛ nwo adwelie ɛnɛ, amaa wɔadziezie ngyegyelɛ ne na bɛazonle bɛ agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne abo bieko a? “Bɛmmamaa sɛnzɛne tɔ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛ ɛya ɛtɛtɔle la.” (Ɛfɛ. 4:26) Ɔlua ndelebɛbo mɔɔ ɛbaye amaa wɔadziezie ngyegyelɛ ne ndɛndɛ la azo, ɛbaboa wɔamaa bɛabɔ yɛ “anzodwolɛ kabozo mɔɔ kɔ bɔnu la” anwo bane.—Ɛfɛ. 4:1-3.

* Ɔwɔ kɛ bɛdi ngyegyelɛ nsisilɛ nwo gyima wɔ bɛ nwo kpalɛ yɛɛ bɛfa bɛkyɛ bɛ nwo fee. (Mat. 5:23, 24) Bie a ɛtane anyebolo bie mɔ a sonla yɛ la kɛhyehyɛ mgbanyima ne mɔ moalɛ. (Mat. 18:17) Noko akee, Kilisienenli kɛkpondɛ moalɛ zɛhae noko kɛ ɔbali debie mɔɔ bɛha wɔ Mateyu 18:15, 16 la anzi kpalɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ ala.

# Saa yɛ mediema diedi nli kɛyɛ nvonleɛ kɛtia yɛ, noko yeambuba yɛ agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne azo a, yɛbahola yɛakpa nwolɛ adenle kɛ yɛnrɛbu yɛ sa yɛnrɛdo zo.—Mrɛ. 26:20.

NEZUKOƐ FOA Siezie Ngilenu Mɔɔ Tu Menle Eza Maa Anwosesebɛ

Yɛ asafo debiezukoalɛ ne mɔ azo, yɛlɛ adenle kɛ yɛdua yɛ mualɛ zo yɛamaye yɛ mediema mrenyia nee mraalɛ arɛle. (Hib. 10:24, 25) Kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na yɛaziezie mualɛ mɔɔ amgba kyekye awie mɔ arɛle la ɛ?

• Sɛlɛ Gyihova. Sɛlɛ ye kɛ ɔboa wɔ ɔmaa ɛbua mualɛ mɔɔ kɛwula ye anyunlunyia na kɛmaa awie mɔ anwosesebɛ la.—Edw. 35:18.

• Bɔ mɔdenle kɛ ɛnrɛgenga mbuluku ne mɔ anu ngomekye. Saa ɛfa ɛ ngakyile ɛbua a, menli gyɛne asa baha nvasoɛ ɔluakɛ bɛbade ɛ diedi ne mɔɔ ɛkile la.

• Maa wɔ mualɛ ɛyɛ sikalɛ. Mualɛ sikalɛ mɔɔ anu la ɛkɛ la fɛta kpalɛ na ɔmaa menli dɔɔnwo nyia adenle bua bie wɔ debiezielɛ ne anu.—Mrɛ. 10:19.

• Maa wɔ edwɛkɛ ne ɛyɛ mɔɔ maa anwosesebɛ. Ɛmmafa wɔ edwɛkɛ ne ɛntu asafo ne anzɛɛ mediema Kilisienenle bie folɛ yɛɛ mmatendɛ ɛtia bɛ.

Saa bɛangome wɔ kɛ bua la amee, ɛbahola wɔazia wɔamaa awie mɔ anwosesebɛ. Tie kpalɛ, na kile kɛ wɔ nye sɔ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ bɛhanle la.—Mrɛ. 10:21.
