Yɛnni aseɛ koraa, yɛnni aseɛ koraa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokorɛ)

DEƐ ƐYƆƆ A ANƆPA AFEI ANƆKƆSI SƐ OBI KƆ DAN MU ANADWO A, ƐMU YƐ SUM

Wɔse Kuntukurunku, Odds, ne Kuntunamoa, Ends, firi soro hɔ tɔɔ fam. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Asaase Yaa, woatrɛw dodo." Asaase Yaa kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na mose meatrɛw dodo?" Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Efiri sɛ wontutuu amena mmaa yɛn sɛ yɛnda mu." Asaase Yaa danee ne ho kyerɛɛ Praeɛ, the Broom, ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "W’ano ayɛ akokɔnkɔnɔn." Ɔno nso buaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na wredidim atɛm?" Asaase Yaa kaa sɛ, "Sɛ madidim atɛm a, madidi wo atɛm; sɛ woante aseɛ a, gyae asɛmbisa." Praeɛ danee ne ho kyerɛɛ Abena, The-strip-of-bark (a wɔpra nwura gu mu no), ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Wogye nnoɔma kɔtow gu." Strip-of-bark kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na wredidim atɛm?" Praeɛ kaa sɛ, "Moyɛɛ saa ara." Strip-of-bark nso ka kyerɛɛ Sumina, Midden-heap, sɛ, "Deɛ wogyeɛ biara, wode sie." Sumina kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na wredidim atɛm?" Ɔno (Strip-of-bark) buaa sɛ, "Madidi wo atɛm, na sɛ wonnim aseɛ a, gyae asɛmbisa." Sumina nso ka kyerɛɛ Abe, Palm-tree, sɛ, "Wo kɔn akyi nyinaa ayɛ nwura kɛkɛ." Abe kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na wredidim atɛm?" Sumina buaa sɛ, "Madidi wo atɛm, na sɛ wonnim aseɛ a, gyae asɛmbisa." Abe nso danee ne ho kyerɛɛ Tetea, Little-black-ant, ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "W’aso ayɛ tuntum." Little-black-ant kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na wredidim atɛm?" Abe buaa sɛ, "Madidi wo atɛm, na sɛ wonnim aseɛ a, gyae asɛmbisa." Little-black-ant nso kɔɔ Esono, the Elephant hɔ, kɔtenaa ne nan so; na ɔbɔɔ Esono koto. Berɛ a ɔno (Esono) rema ne nan so no, ɔtiatiaa Tamiriwa, the Black-snail, ani ase pɛɛ, na Black-snail ani sueeɛ, na ɔde nsuo no hohoroo efan a ɛwɔ nkyɛn no ahaban ho. (Afei), aduane a Nana-aberewa die ne efan no, na ɛno na Tamiriwa, the Black-snail, de nsuo a ɛfiri n'ani mu hohoroo ho no. Ɔkraman, the Dog, retena mu resene, na ɔno (Nana-aberewa) frɛɛ Ɔkraman na ɔfaa nnyina (a na ɔsoa no) de soaa Ɔkraman. Berɛ a Ɔkraman nam kwan so rekɔ no, ɔnyaa bise, na ɔpaae mmienu, na ɔtow baako guu Okagya, the Squirrel, amena ano. Okagya nso faa bise no de bɔɔ Onyina, the Silk-cotton-tree. Silk-cotton-tree nso, abufuo hyɛɛ ne koko ma. Ɔhunuu sɛ abɔfra ketewa bi nam rekɔ, na ɔtew ne baa de bɔɔ no kum no. Afei, saa abofra ketewa no yɛɛ Nyankopɔn ba. Ɛhɔ ara na wɔkyeree Onyina, de no kɔɔ Nyankopɔn anim. Wɔbisaa no (Onyina) sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn asɛm koraa na asi?" Onyina kaa sɛ, "Megyinaa me baabi komm, na Akagya—abofra a ɔte saa yi—(sii ne bo) maa bise so de bɔɔ me, enti me nso, berɛ a saa abofra yi retwam no, mesusuii sɛ ɔyɛ Akagya, Opuro no, enti mepaa me baha baako de bɔɔ no, nso hwɛ, na ɛyɛ wo ba." Nyankopɔn kaa sɛ, "Monto no aduam, na monfrɛ Akagya, Opuro no mma." Akagya bae; ɔkaa sɛ, "Nana, wonim sɛ mekyi ne bise, nanso Ɔkraman kɔfaa bi bae, de bɛtoo me tu anom, na berɛ a merewosow ato agu no, ɛkɔbɔɔ Nana Onyina." Nyankopɔn kaa sɛ, "Monto no aduam, na monfrɛ Ɔkraman mma." Wɔkɔfrɛɛ Ɔkraman bae. Ɔkraman kaa sɛ, "Nana, na menante me baabi komm—afei wohwɛ sɛdeɛ mete—na Abrewatan faa ne nyinaa soa dwoodwoo de bɛsii me ti so; afei Akagya, Opuro no, ɔno na ɔda ne tu mu wɔ afuom a Abrewatan ayɛ sɛ ɔbenya biribi adi no mu, nanso me na Abrewatan maa mefaa ne nyinaa; me nso, mefaa bise de too ne tu anom." Nyankopɔn kaa sɛ, "Monto no aduam, na monfrɛ Abrewatan mma." Abrewatan bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Aane, me mmanana, monhwɛ m'anom, me a mentumi nwe nam, ɛfan na medi; afei nso, meguare Bosompra; (enti) mekyi ne Ɛnwa, Ɛnwa tuntum no; Ɔkraman nko ara na ɔnim yei, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ Ɛnwa, maa no de nsu firii n'anom de guu ɛfan a medi so no so. Ɛno nti na memaa no soaa me nyinaa no." Nyankopɔn kaa sɛ, "O, monto no aduam, na monkɔfa Ɛnwa, Ɛnwa tuntum no mra." Ɛnwa tuntum no bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Nana, na mewɔ me baabi komm, hwɛ, Ɛsono kɛseɛ kɛseɛ bi tiatiaa m'ani ase pɛɛ, na mekɔfaa ɛfan (ahaban) de pee so. Ɛno nko ara na menim fa ho." Nyankopɔn kaa sɛ, "Monto no aduam, na momfa Ɛsono mra." Wɔkɔfaa Ɛsono bae. Ɛsono see, "Nnansa bi a atwam no na mete hɔ, na me honam hyɛɛ aseɛ yɛɛ me henehene, na berɛ a mehwɛɛ faako no—mepa mo kyɛw sɛ mede asɛmfua yi di dwuma—saa kwasea Tetea no akɔhyɛ me honam mu sɛ ɔrekɔhwehwɛ nam awe, ama wanyini abɛyɛ kɛse; na berɛ a merepagya me nan no, ɛkɔtiae Tamiriwa so." Nyankopɔn see, "Momfa no nkɔkyekyere no bɔtɔ so, na momfa Tetea mmra." Berɛ a Tetea bae no, ɔsee, "Nana, Abe bi wɔ hɔ; sɛ ɛsow a, mfiase no na ne ba yɛ tuntum; sɛ me Tetea no, menante so a, ɛma ne ho bere. Ɛnora na mete hɔ, na Abe ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ m'aso yɛ tuntum, na esiane sɛ meyɛ ketewa dodo nti, medwenee sɛ mɛkɔ Ɛsono hɔ, akɔhwehwɛ nam awe ama manyini abɛyɛ kɛse." Nyankopɔn see, "Momfa no nkɔkyekyere no bɔtɔ so, na monkɔfrɛ Abe mmra." Abe bae; ɔsee, "Nana, deɛ mereka nie: Me ne Sumina na ɛtete faako, na sɛ wogu nwura gu ne so ma ɛbu so, na wantumi ansoa bio a, megye bi hyɛ me kɔn mu; afei deɛ, ɔyeyaa me, see me sɛ mewɔ nwura wɔ me kɔn mu. Ɛno nti, me deɛ, m'ani beree, na me nso meyeyaa Tetea." Nyankopɔn see, "Momfa no nkɔkyekyere no bɔtɔ so, na monkɔfrɛ Sumina mmra." Sumina bae; ɔsee, "Nana, Abena, Baa a, sɛ wɔpra dan mu nwura a, wɔde ma no ma ɔno nso de brɛ me. Ɛnora no, afei a menyɛɛ no hwee no, ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, 'Wodeɛ wo kɔn dɔ agyapadeɛ' (kyerɛ sɛ wɔyɛ adifudepɛ); me nso mekaa sɛ, ‘a’, na sɛdeɛ Abe bɛn hɔ no nti, me nso meyeyaw no.” Nyankopɔn kaa sɛ, “Momfa no nkɔkyekyere no gu dua mu, na monfrɛ Abena, Nwoma-sin, mma no mmra.” Nwoma-sin bae; ɔkaa sɛ, “Nana, sɛ Praeɛ pra dan mu a, sɛ wɔamfa (ne wura) amma me a, sɛ mframa bɔ a, nwura no nyinaa san ba bio; afei nnaano, ɛmfa ho sɛ menyɛɛ no hwee no, ɔkaa sɛ, ‘Wogye nneɛma de to gu kwa.’ Ɛno nti, me nso m’ani bɛberee, na mekɔyeyaw Sumina.” Nyankopɔn kaa sɛ, “Momfa no, na momfa no nkɔkyekyere no gu dua mu, na monfrɛ Praeɛ mma no mmra ha.” Praeɛ bae; ɔkaa sɛ, “Nana, Asease da hɔ trɔtrɔɔ, ne so yɛ fĩ, enti berɛ biara a mɛsɔre no, mepra ne so fĩ nyinaa na meyɛ no fɛ; nanso nnaano no, Asease kasa kyerɛɛ me sɛ, ‘W’ano yɛ akokɔbĩ,’ na me bo nso fuiɛ, na mekɔyeyaw Abena, Nwoma-sin no.” Nyankopɔn kaa sɛ, “Momfa no nkɔkyekyere no gu dua mu, na monfrɛ Asease mmra.” Berɛ a Asease bae no, ɔkaa sɛ, “Nana, medeɛ meda hɔ; wɔtiatia me so; menka hwee da; wɔyɛ nneɛma ahodoɔ nyinaa wɔ me so, nanso menka hwee da. Afei nnaano berɛ a mewɔ hɔ no, Kuntukurunku ne Kuntunamoa, Nneɛma nketenkete, bɛhwee me so, na mekaa sɛ, ‘O! ɛdeɛn na ɛrekɔ so?’ na wɔkaa sɛ, ‘Woantu amena biara a yɛbɛtumi ada mu, na wo ne wo trɔtrɔɔ nyinaa nso mfasoɔ biara nni so (mma obiara).’ Ɛno nti, me nso meyeyaw Praeɛ.” Nyankopɔn kaa sɛ, “Momfa no nkɔkyekyere no gu dua mu, na monkɔfa Nneɛma nketenkete mmra.” Nneɛma nketenkete bae. Wɔkaa sɛ, “Nana, na yɛwɔ dan atifi, na yɛredi agorɔ, na yɛhwee fam na yɛpirae; ɛno nti na yɛyeyaw Asease, sɛ ɔyɛ trɔtrɔɔ no.” Nyankopɔn kaa sɛ, “Ahemfo, yɛate asɛm yi nyinaa, enti momma yɛnsɔre nkɔ dan mu nkɔhwehwɛ adanseɛ no mu na yɛmmra mmɛbu atɛn.” Enti wɔsɔree kɔɔ dan bi mu, na wɔtoo ɔpono no mu. Ɛno nti na, sɛ wokɔ dan mu anadwo a,—kusuubii—Nyankopɔn ne n’Ahemfo na wɔredwene asɛm no ho wɔ hɔ no.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
