Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka yi yɛ nokware)

NEA ƐYƐE A SƐ OBI KƆ PREDAN MU ANADWO A, ƐHƆ YƐ SUM

Wɔse Kuntukurunku, Odds, ne Kuntunamoa, Ends, firi siriŋ no so bɔɔ fam. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Asaase Yaa, woatrɛw dodo." Asaase Yaa kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na mowhehwɛ sɛ matrɛw dodo?" Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Efisɛ wontuu amena mmaa yɛn sɛ yɛnda mu." Asaase Yaa dan kɔɔ Prae hɔ na ɔkaa sɛ, "Akoko bin agugu w'anom." Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woyeyau me?" Asaase Yaa kaa sɛ, "Sɛ mayeyau wo a, mayeyau wo; sɛ woan- te aseɛ no ase, gyae asɛmbisa." Prayeɛ no danee ne ho kyerɛɛ Abena, Dua-honono (deɛ wɔpra wura gu mu no), na ɔkaa sɛ, "Wofa (nneɛma) na wokɔto gu." Dua-honono kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woka m'anim?" Prayeɛ kaa sɛ, "(Sɛdeɛ ɛteɛ biara) mayɛ saa." Dua-honono nso kasa kyerɛɛ Sumina, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Wokora deɛ wonya no so." Sumina kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woka m'anim?" Ɔno (Dua-honono) b buae sɛ, "Mayeyaw wo, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a, ɛnde gyae abisabisa." Sumina nso kɔka kyerɛɛ Abe sɛ, "W'apɔw mu nyinaa afifiri sumina." Abe kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woreyaw me?" Sumina buae sɛ, "Mayeyaw wo, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a, ɛnde gyae hwehwɛ mu." Abe nso danee ne ho kyerɛɛ Tetea, na ɔkaa sɛ, "W'aso yɛ tu ck." Ntɛtea kakraba no kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woyeyaw me?" Abɛ no buaa sɛ, "Mayeyaw wo, na sɛ wonnim deɛ enti a, gyae akyinnyegyeɛ." Ntɛtea kakraba no nso kɔɔ Esono hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ne nan so; na ɔkɔkeka Esono ho ma ɔsereeɛ. Bere a ɔno (Ɛsono no) ma ne nan so no, ɛnna ɔtiatiaa Tamiriwa, Ɔnwa tuntum no ani ase pɛɛ, na Ɔnwa tuntum no ani firii aseɛ sɛ ɛresu nsuo, na ɔde nsuo no hohoroo efan a (ɛrefifi) bɛn hɔ no ahaban ho. (Afei), aduane a Aberewa-nanabea die ne efan no, na ɛho na Tamiriwa, Ɔnwa tuntum no de nsuo a ɛfiri n'ani mu no hohoroeɛ. Ɔkraman retwam no, aberewa nana no frɛɛ Ɔkraman no faa nnyina a ɔsoa no de soaa Ɔkraman no. Bere a Ɔkraman rekɔ no, ɔnyaa bise, na ɔpaee baako mu mmienu, na ɔtow baako guu Okagya, Opuro no, tokuro ano. Okagya nso faa bise no de bɔɔ Onyina no. Onyaa nso, abufuo hyɛɛ ne kɔn ma. Ɔhunuu sɛ abɔfra ketewa bi retwa mu rekɔ, ɛnna ɔtetee ne baa bɔɔ no kum no. Na saa abɔfra ketewa no yɛ Nyankopɔn, Ɔsoro-nyame no ba. Ɛhɔ ara na wɔkyeree Onyaa no, de no kɔɔ Ɔsoro-nyame no anim. Wɔbisaa no (Onyaa no) sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn asɛm koraa na asi?" Onyaa no see sɛ, "Me deɛ megyina hɔ me baabi, na saa Opuro yi—abofra a ɔte sɛ ɔno ara—(tumi) maa bese so de bɔɔ me, enti me nso, berɛ a saa abofra yi retwam no, mesusuii sɛ ɔyɛ Okagya, Opuro no, enti mepaa me baha baako de bɔɔ no, na hwɛ, na ɛyɛ wo ba." Nyame, Nyankopɔn se, "Monkye no na mommɔ no dua mu, na monfrɛ Okagya, Opuro no, mma ɔmmra." Okagya bae; ɔkaa sɛ, "Nana, wonim sɛ me kyiwadeɛ ne bise, nanso Okraman, Kraman no, kɔfa baako bae, bɛto guu m'abɔn ano, na berɛ a merebɛto atwene no, ɛkɔbɔɔ Nana Onyina." Nyankopɔn se, "Monkye no, na mommɔ no dua mu, na monfrɛ Ɔkraman no mma ɔmmra." T Wɔkɔfrɛɛ Kraman no sɛ ɔmmra. Ɔkraman no see sɛ, "Me wura, na menante me baabi—afei wohunu sɛdeɛ meteɛ (meretɔ)—berɛ a Apenkwaa-nananom faa ne nyinaa kɛtɛbɔ de too me tirim; afei Okagya, Amoako no, ɔno na ɔda n'amena mu wɔ afuom a Apenkwaa-nananom ayɛ sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a obenya biribi adi, nanso me na Apenkwaa-nananom maa meso ne nnyina; me nso, mefaa bɛse baako na ɔtoo no guu n'amena no ano." Nyankopɔn kaa sɛ, "Monfa no, mommɔ no dua mu, na monfrɛ Nana-aberewa mma ɔmmra." Nana-aberewa bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ao, me mmanana, monhwɛ m'anom, me a mentumi nwe nam yi, ɛfan na medi; afei nso, medware Bosompra; me kyiri (enti) ne Tamiriwa; Ɔkraman nko ara na na onim yei, na ɔkɔbɔɔ Tamiriwa amanneɛ, ma ɔyɛe ma ɔtwee nsu firi n'anom, de guu ɛfan a metena ase wɔ so no mu. Ɛno nti na mede me nnyina maa no sɛ ɔnsoa." Nyankopɔn see sɛ, "O, monfa no nkyekyere dua bi ho, na monkɔfa Tamiriwa, Ɔnwa kuntunnii no mmra." Ɔnwa kuntunnii no bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me wura, na mete me baabi, hwɛ, Ɛsono kɛse kɛse bi bɛtiatiaa bɛbi a ɛbɛn m'ani ase pɛɛ, na mekɔfaa efan de popaa ho. Nea menim fa ho ara nen." Nyankopɔn see sɛ, "Monkɔfa no nkɔkyekyere no gu dua ho, na mommfa Esono mmra." Wɔkɔfaa Esono bae. Ɛsono kaa sɛ, "Bɛyɛ nna mmiɛnsa a atwam ni na mete hɔ, na me wedeɛ hyɛɛ aseɛ yɛɛ me henehene, na merehwɛ beaeɛ hɔ no—mepa mo kyɛw wɔ asɛm a mereka yi ho—saa kwasea Teatea kɔkɔkɔ yi akɔwura (me wedeɛ mu) sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ nam awe, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛyɛ kɛse; na merema me nan so no, ɛkɔtiae Tamiriwa, Nyankonsoroma Tuntum no so." Onyankopɔn kaa sɛ, "Monfa no nkɔkyekyere no dunsitete ho, na momfa Tetea, Teatea-kokoɔketebreba." Berɛ a Tetea baaeɛ no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Nana, abɛ bi wɔ hɔ; sɛ ɛsow a, (n'aba) no di kan yɛ tuntum; sɛ me, Teatea-kokoɔketebreba no, nante so a, ɛma ɛbere. Nnansa yi na mewɔ hɔ a, Abɛ ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ m’aso yɛ tuntum, na ɛsiane sɛ mesua dodo nti, medwenee sɛ mɛkɔ Ɛsono nkyɛn wɔ hɔ akɔpɛ nam awe na manyin abɛyɛ kɛse.” Nyankopɔn kaa sɛ, “Monkofa no nkɔbɔ no dua mu, na monkɔfrɛ Abɛ mmra.” Abe, Abɛ no, bae; ɔkaa sɛ, “Nana, deɛ mereka nie: Me ne Sumina, Sumina no, te h bom, na sɛ sumina a wɔtow gu no so no dɔɔso dodo, na ontumi nso bio a, migye ebi na mede to me kɔn mu; nanso afei, ɔyeyaw me, kaa sɛ me kɔn ho afi. Ɛno nti, me deɛ, m’ani bɛgyee kɔɔ, na me nso, metee Ntetɛa-tuntum no so.” Nyankopɔn see sɛ, “Monkɔfa no nkɔkyekyere no gu dua mu, na monfrɛ Sumina, Sumina-kuo no, ma ɔmmra.” Sumina bae; ɔsee sɛ, “Nana, Abena, Dua-hon-sin, deɛ sɛ wɔpra dan mu fi a, wɔde ma no ma ɔno nso de brɛ me no. Nnansa yi ara, akyi a menyaa asɛm biara anka no, ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, 'Wode deɛ wonya kora' (kyerɛ sɛ wɔyɛ adidifɔ); mekae sɛ, ‘a’, na ɛnam sɛ Abɛ no bɛn hɔ nti, me nso meyeyaw no.” Nyankopɔn kae sɛ, “Mompfa no nkɔkyekyere no wɔ dua kɛseɛ ho, na momfrɛ Abena, Dua-honono, mma no mmra.” Dua-honono bae; ɔkae sɛ, “Nana, sɛ Praeɛ pra dan mu a, sɛ wɔamfa (ne nwura) amma me a, sɛ mframa bɔ a, nwura no nyinaa san ba bio; afei, ɛnnora akyi no, ɛmfa ho sɛ manyɛ no hwee no, ɔno kaa sɛ, ‘Wogye nneɛma de tow gu kɛkɛ.’ Ɛno nti, me nso m’ani beree, na mekɔkaa Sumina, Sumina-kuw no animtiaa.” Nyankopɔn kaa sɛ, “Monkye no, na mommɔ no dua mu, na monfrɛ Prae, Praeɛ no, mma no mmra ha.” Praeɛ bae; ɔkaa sɛ, “Nana, Asaase Yaa da hɔ trɔtrɔɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ, ne ho yɛ fi, enti berɛ biara a mɛsɔre no, mepra efi a ɛwɔ ne so nyinaa na mema ne ho yɛ ahoɔfɛ; nanso nansa yi ara, Asase Yaa kã kyerɛɛ me sɛ, ‘W’ano ahyɛ akokɔbiree ma,’ na me kɔn nso hyehyee me, na mekɔka guu Abena Okyenam so.” Nyankopɔn see sɛ, “Monkɔfa no nkɔkyekyere no gu dua mu, na monfrɛ Asase Yaa mma.” Asase Yaa bae no, ɔsee sɛ, “Me wura, mete hɔ a, wɔtiatia me so; menkã hwee da; wɔyɛ biribiara bi ba me so, nanso memmɔka hwee da. Afei ɛnora a na mewɔ hɔ no, Kuntukurunku ne Kuntunamoa, nneɛma nketenkete nyinaa, guu me so, na mekaa sɛ, ‘O! asɛm bɛn na asi?’ na wɔkaa sɛ, ‘Woantu amoa a yɛbɛtumi ada mu, na wo ne wo trɛtrɛ nyinaa nso mfasoɔ biara nni so (mma obiara).’ Ɛno nti, me nso metutuu Prae, Ɔprayeɛ no anim.” Ɔnyankopɔn kaa sɛ, “Monfa no na mommɔ no dua, na monkɔfa Nkorabata ne Nsinsan mmra.” Nkorabata ne Nsinsan baeɛ. Wɔkaa sɛ, “Nana, na yɛwɔ dan atifi, na na yɛredi agorɔ, na yɛhwee fam na yɛpirapirae; ɛno nti na yɛyeyaa Asase Yaa sɛ ne ho tɔtɔe.” Nyankopɔn kaa sɛ, “Mpaninfoɔ, yɛate asɛm yi nyinaa, enti momma yɛnsɔre nkɔ dan no mu nkɔhwehwɛ adanseɛ no mu na yɛmmɛbu atɛn.” Enti wɔsɔree kɔɔ dan bi mu, na wɔtoo ɛpono no mu. Ɛno nti na sɛ wokɔ dan mu anadwo a,—kusuubii—Nyankopɔn ne ne Mpanimfoɔ retu agina wɔ hɔ.

M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi nso brɛ me.
