HOW HYPOCRISY CAME INTO THE NATION

It is said that a certain hunter went into the forest; he looked into a pit, and a Human, a Leopard, a Snake, and a Rat were standing inside. When these creatures saw the hunter, this one said, "Father hunter, come and rescue me." That one said, "Father, come and rescue me." The hunter said, "My children, tomorrow, if I rescue you and you get hold of me, what you will do to me, I do not want it." This one said, "I could never do that." That one said, "I could never do that." The hunter said, "If that is so, then I will rescue you." Truly, he rescued all these creatures, and he went back to town. One day, while the Hunter was there, the Leopard brought plenty of meat. He said, "Father Hunter, this is my thanks for the good you did for me; I thank you, Apiafo." He brought twelve loads of meat to the Hunter. The Hunter thanked him, and the Leopard departed. Again, while the Hunter was there, the Rat came. He said, "Father Hunter, do you want gold?" The Hunter said, "Oh! Why not? For that is what humans labor to seek." The Rat set off to where God kept his gold. He dug a hole beneath the house, entered the room, took all of God’s gold dust, and brought it to the Hunter. The Hunter said, "Thank you, Aku." And the Rat went away. One day while he was there, he heard, "Ago!" The Hunter said, "Ame!" The Human came; he said, "Father Hunter, good morning." He replied, "Good morning, brother!" He said, "Here is a small calabash of wine with which I come to thank you." Father Hunter said, "I do not want it; human, as I said, I should not have rescued you; look at what the Leopard brought me, look at what the Rat brought me." The Human took his wine and left, and he went to God and told him, "Your gold that was lost, it was the Rat who took it and gave it to the Hunter." God said, "Is that true?" He said, "Nana, absolutely true." God sent executioners, who arrested the Hunter and bound his waist with rope. They beat him severely! Now, when they reached the road with the Hunter, he said, "Elders, I beg you, I need to relieve myself." The executioners said, "No, a thief will run away." He said, "Oh! I will not run away, but if you wish, tie a rope to my waist and tie it to a tree so that I may go." The executioners agreed, and allowed him to go. While he was there, he relieved himself, and the Little Snake came. He said, "Look, my friend, take this medicine ball and go with it; as you go, when they bring you to stand before the gathering and it is about time they kill you, I will go to the King's harem and bite his favorite wife; when I bite her, you will see all the people rise up, then you too say, 'I have medicine,' and when you treat the child, she will become well." The Hunter said, "Thank you, Aku." The executioners took the Hunter away, and made him stand before God and the Kontire, the Akwam, the Adonten, the Gyase, and the Kyidom. The Hunter stated his case; as the Spokesman began to deliver the verdict, they heard from the harem, sudden shouting and commotion! They said, "What is it?" They said, "A snake has bitten God's favorite wife." All the people left there. Now, as the Hunter stood there, he said, "Ah! If I were not a prisoner, I would have had medicine." A certain servant heard it, and went and said, "Go bring the one in the stocks; let him come." So the Hunter came. Nyame said, "If you can look after this child of mine so that he does not die, I will spare your life." The hunter said, "I have heard, benefactor." The hunter said, "Nana, the thing I will show you, if you can get some of it, then I can look after him." They said, "What is it?" He said, "The blood of a hypocrite, that is what is used to apply." They said, "As for the blood of a hypocrite, where are we going to get it?" One wise linguist said, "Now, where is the fellow who came to tell Nyame that the hunter had come to steal Nyame's gold?" They said, "You are right, you are right." They went and brought that fellow. The hunter said, "Cut off his hand and give me his blood." Right away, they cut it off. The hunter used the blood to apply the medicine, going to treat the King's child. Right away, he got up. They said, "Praise the Hunter." They said, "Eee!" Nyame said, "I spare your life, and as for the Hypocrite, kill him, and cut up his flesh, and scatter it around." They killed him, and they cut up his flesh into small pieces, and scattered it around. That is why hypocrisy has come into the world in such great measure.

This folktale that I have told, whether it is sweet or whether it is not sweet, take some of it away, and bring some of it back.
