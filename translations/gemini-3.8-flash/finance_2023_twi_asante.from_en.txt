ANIMUONYAM NSƐM

Afe yi bɛkɔ so ayɛ afe a ɛyɛ den paa wɔ yɛn man sikasɛm abakɔsɛm mu a akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho. Te sɛ wiase afanan nyinaa no, COVID-19 yarebɔne no bɔɔ yɛn wɔ afe 2020 afa a ɛdi kan no mu, a ɛmaa sikasɛm dwumadie brɛoo, na ɛmaa yɛn sika a yɛnya no so tee ne sika foforɔ a yɛanhyɛ da anyɛ ho nhyehyɛeɛ bɔɔ yɛn ho de ko tiaa yarebɔne no.

Berɛ a yɛdwen sɛ yɛrenya nkɔsoɔ kɛseɛ wɔ yɛn ho a yɛresan anya firi ɔyaredɔm no nsunsuansoɔ bɔne no mu denam yɛn nsesaeɛ nhyehyɛeɛ a yɛhyɛɛ aseɛ de yɛɛ adwuma no so pɛpɛɛpɛ no, nneɛma ahodoɔ bi ba bɔɔ yɛn a ɛno na ama berɛ kakra a atwam yi mu no, sikasɛm mu nkunimdie akɛseɛ a yɛdii firi afe 2017 kɔsi 2019 no asɛe. Nea ɛdi kan no, ɛyɛɛ Russia ne Ukraine ko no, afei akyire yi, twentwɛnsoɔ a ɛbaa sika a wɔgyegye ho nhyehyɛeɛ atitire a ɛwɔ afe 2022 sika nhyehyɛeɛ mu no gye a wɔamfiri mu ammoa no maa yɛn sikasɛm mu gyinabea to twenee. Mpofirim ara, nea ɛdi kan koraa wɔ mfeɛ du mu no, International Capital Market no too wɔn apono mu ma yɛn, ɔman no mu sika a wɔde bɔ bosea bɛyɛɛ den na boseabɔ ho ka nso yɛɛ kɛseɛ, a ɛno kɔfaa ka a yɛtua kɛseɛ ne cedi no a ɛkɔɔ fam ntɛmntɛm baeɛ.

Enti, berɛ mpo a amannɔne sika mmoa a ɛfiri sikasɛm dwa no so ne yɛn amannɔne nkonya so teeɛ no, nnoɔma a yɛgye firi amannɔne ba yɛn man mu ho ka a ɛkɔɔ soro esiane sɛdeɛ ɛteɛ sɛ yɛn kɔn dɔ nnoɔma a ɛfiri amannɔne dodo sɛ ɔman no, a yɛn ka a yɛtua ho ka a ɛrekɔ soro nso ka ho no, maa yɛn sika no boɔ so tee kɛseɛ pa ara. Woi de asetena mu ka a ɛkɔ soro brɛɛ yɛn nyinaa, Aban no ne ankorankorɛ mpo. Ngo boɔ a ɛrekɔ soro no ama nnoɔma ne ɔsom pii boɔ akɔ soro, berɛ a nnoɔma boɔ kɔɔ soro koduu 40.4% wɔ Ɔbɛnem 2022 mu a ɛyɛ deɛ ɛkɔɔ soro sen biara.

Yɛasɔ yɛn ahwɛ ampa sɛ ɔmanfoɔ, na Aban no te mo ase kɛseɛ wɔ nhyɛsoɔ a ɛnsɛ a yei de aba no ho. Sɛ Aban no, yɛpɛ sɛ yɛda yɛn aseda adi kyerɛ yɛn ahokafoɔ ahodoɔ nyinaa, a Mmarahyɛbadwa, Adwumawurafoɔ Nkabomkuo, Adwumayɛfoɔ Nkabomkuo, Ɔmanfoɔ Ahyehyɛdeɛ, Nyamesom Ahyehyɛdeɛ, Association of Ghana Industries, Ghana Union of ka ho.

v | Ghana Aban - 2023 Ɔman mma Sikakoraa ho Nhyehyɛe

Sikasɛm Mu Gyinapɛn Kɛse ne Tumi a Ɛtumi Gyina Pintinn a Yɛbɛsan De Aba na Yɛakora So Ama Ɔmanfo Nyinaa Anyankyerɛ ne Nnoɔma Ho Mfaso a Yɛbɛka Ho

Nguadifoɔ Fekuo, Nhomasuafoɔ, Nhwehwɛmufoɔ Fekuo, ne Ghanafoɔ kɛkɛ a wɔkɔ so nya yɛn ho abotare mpo berɛ a yɛrefa saa mmere a emu yɛ den yi mu.

Wɔ Ɔmanpanyin Nana Addo Danquah Akuffo-Addo din mu no, mepɛ sɛ mehyɛ Ghanafoɔ nyinaa bɔ sɛ Aban de nhyehyɛeɛ gu hɔ a ɛbɛsan asiesie sikasɛm no na ama macroeconomic gyinapɛn asan aba, anya sika pii aba, adi nhyehyɛeɛ ne amansan nsakraeɛ so, atrɛ dijital ne nipadua mu nneɛma a ɛwɔ hɔ no mu, ate nneɛma a ɛho nhia a yɛde ba no so, ahyɛ nneɛma a yɛde kɔ amannɔne no ho nkuran, atrɛ nneɛma a yɛyɛ wɔ mpɔtam hɔ de si nneɛma a yɛde firi amannɔne ba ananmu no mu, na abɔ wɔn a wɔn ho nni asɛm ne wɔn a wɔatwe wɔn ho afiri asetena mu no ho ban.

Nnwomasua a yɛasua sɛ ɔman wɔ saa berɛ yi mu no ama ayɛ mmerɛkyerɛ sɛ yɛbɛsakra yɛn sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ koraa afebɔɔ na yɛahyɛ mu den nso. Yɛbɛyɛ no yie pa ara. Yɛbɛfiri yei mu aba sɛ nnipa a yɛn ho yɛ den kɛse. Awurade bɛkura yɛn mu na wama yɛn ahoɔden ama yɛatumi ayɛ yei.

Onyame nhyira wo!

NKYERƐKYERƐMU AHYƐASEƐ

1. Afe 2023 Citizen’s Budget no yɛ nea Aban no nyae wɔ afe 2022 mu ne sika dodoɔ a ɔpɛ sɛ ɔboaboa ano na ɔde yɛ adwuma de di n'ahyɛdeɛ ne nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ so no nyinaa emu tɔfa. Afe 2023 budget no asɛmti ne "Restoring and Sustaining Macroeconomic Stability and Resilience for Inclusive Growth & Value Addition”.

2. Mfeɛ pii a atwam no mu no, sika a wɔhyehyɛ foforɔ biara kura dwumadie foforɔ pii a wɔbɛhyɛ aseɛ na agya dwumadie a ɛrekɔ so no akyi. Ɛnyɛ sɛ yei de nhyɛsoɔ gu yɛn sikasɛm so nko, na mmom abakɔsɛm kyerɛ sɛ ɛde aka a ɛsɛ sɛ yɛtua ba, na yei ma dwumadie no nyinaa boɔ yɛ den efisɛ aka a wɔtwentwɛn so no ma nsonsuansoɔ anaa mfasoɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ yɛtua no kɔ soro.

3. Wɔ 2023 mu no, Aban bɛdi nhyehyɛeɛ foforɔ ne dwumadie foforɔ so, nanso ɔbɛhyɛ suban a ɛne sɛ wɔbɛwie dwumadie a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw nyinaa no nso nkuran.

Saa nneɛma a esisii yi ada adi pefee denam sika foforo sesa a ɛkɔ fam ntɛmntɛm, nneɛma bo a ɛkɔ soro kɛse, ka a yɛtua a emu yɛ duru dodo, sikasɛm mu ahokyere ne amannɔne sikasɛm mu nhyɛso ahodoɔ so, ɛmfa ho sɛ Aban de sika ne amammuo ho nhyehyeɛ a ɛbɛsiesie tebea no baeɛ. Sikasɛm no kwan a ɛfaa so a ɛnyɛ nea etwa toɔ firii Jan-Sept 2022 no, wɔde ato dwa sɛnea ɛdidi soɔ yi:

i. Real GDP nyin yɛɛ mfinimfini bɛyɛ 4.0 ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu afe-ne-afe ma 2022 fã a ɛdi kan no de toto 3.9 ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu wɔ saa berɛ korɔ no ara mu wɔ 2021;

ii. Headline nneɛma boɔ kɔ soro kɔduruu ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 40.4 wɔ Ahinime 2022 mu firii ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 37.2 wɔ Ɛbɔ mu ne ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 33.9 wɔ Ɔsannaa mu, a na ɛfiri nnuane ne nneɛma a ɛnyɛ nnuane nyinaa boɔ a ɛkɔɔ soro;

6. 2022 Mid-Year Fiscal Policy Review no yɛɛ nsakrae wɔ afe 2022 sikasɛm nhyehyɛe no mu esiane wiase nyinaa ne ɔman no mu nnoɔma a ɛnkɔ yie a ɛresisi nti.

7. Aban sikasɛm dwumadie ho amanneɛbɔ a ɛnni baabiara a ɛfa Ɔpɛpɔn – Ɛbɔ 2022 ho kyerɛ sɛ nkontabuo a ɛfa sika a ɛbaaeɛ ho no anyɛ yie, nanso sika a wɔsɛeeɛ no deɛ, ne kɛseɛ no ara kɔɔ so yie pɛpɛɛpɛ sɛdeɛ wɔhyɛɛ da yɛɛ ho nhyehyeɛ no.

Sika a Wɔnyaeɛ Ho Dwumadie

8. Sika a Wɔnyae Nyinaa ne Mmoa sika yɛɛ GH¢65,399 million (GDP ɔha mu nkyekyem 11.0), sɛ yɛde toto botae a na ɛyɛ GH¢67,307 (GDP ɔha mu nkyekyem 11.4) ne GH¢49,108 million (GDP ɔha mu nkyekyem 10.7) a wɔtwerɛɛ no saa berɛ korɔ no ara mu wɔ afe 2021 mu no ho a.

9. Sɛ yɛde toto botaeɛ a wɔde sii hɔ maa berɛ no ho a, Sika Nyinaa a Ɛbaee ne Mmoa a Wɔnyae no hyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ fam GH¢1,909 million anaa ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 2.8, na afe-kɔ-afe nkɔsoɔ no nso yɛ ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 33.2. Sika a ɛbaee a ɛkɔɔ fam no firi adwumaden a anyɛ kɛseɛ a wɔhunuu no wɔ sika a ɛba no ahodoɔ nyinaa mu.

10. Sika a wɔgyeeɛ wɔ nketenkete ahodoɔ a ɛkɔto sika a ɛba mu no ɔha nkyekyɛmu ma Jan-Sept 2022 no, wɔakyerɛ wɔ mfonini 1 mu.

Trunk Road: Ɛyɛ kwan kɛse a mpɛn pii no ɛbɔ nkuropɔn mmienu anaa nea ɛboro saa, hyɛn gyinabea ahodoɔ, wimhyɛn gyinabea ahodoɔ ne mmeaeɛ foforɔ ntam, a ɛyɛ kwan a wɔkamfo kyerɛ ma akwantuo tenteenten ne nneɛma a wɔde akwantuo akɛseɛ fa so.
