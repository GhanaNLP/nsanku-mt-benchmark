NNIANIM ADWENE

Akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho sɛ, saa afe yi bɛkae sɛ mfeɛ a ɛyɛ den paa no mu baako wɔ yɛn man sikasɛm abakɔsɛm mu. Sɛdeɛ ɛtoo wiase afoforɔ no, COVID-19 yareɛ kɛseɛ no bɔɔ yɛn wɔ afe 2020 abosome mmiɛnsa a ɛdi kan no mu, a ɛmaa sikasɛm dwumadie brɛoo, na ɛde yɛn sika a yɛnya no so teeɛ baeɛ ne nneɛma a yɛnhyehyɛɛ ho sika a yɛde ko tiaa yareɛ kɛseɛ no ho ka kaa ho.

Bere pɔtee a yɛdwenee sɛ yɛrenya nkɔso kɛse wɔ yɛn ho a yɛresan agye afiri yareɛ mmoawa wudifoɔ no sunsuansoɔ bɔne mu denam yɛn nsesamu nhyehyɛeɛ a yɛhyɛɛ aseɛ de yɛɛ adwuma no, nnoɔma bebree bi ba bɛtoo yɛn a wɔ berɛ kakra a atwam yi mu no, asɛe sika ne sikasɛm mu nkonimdie a yɛnyaeɛ firi afe 2017 kɔsi afe 2019 no. Nea ɛdi kan no, ɛyɛ Russia ne Ukraine ko no, afei akyire no, ahotosoɔ a ɛwɔ sika a ɛbɛba ho nhyehyɛeɛ titire a ɛwɔ afe 2022 sika nhyehyɛeɛ mu no gye berɛ ansa na wɔagye atom, a ɛno maa yɛn aban sikasɛm gyinabea hwee ase. Prɛko pɛ, nea ɛdi kan wɔ mfeɛ du mu no, International Capital Market no too wɔn apono mu ma yɛn, ɔman no mu sika a wɔde bɔ bosea bɛyɛɛ den na ɛho ka nso yɛɛ kɛse, a ɛde ɛka a yɛtua kɛseɛ ne cedi a ɛhwe fam ntɛmntɛm baeɛ.

Enti, berɛ a amannɔne sika mmoa a ɛfiri sika guam no ne yɛn amannɔne sikanwene so tee no, nnoɔma a yɛde ba ɔman no mu a ɛkɔɔ soro esiane sɛnea ɛte sɛ nea yɛn kɔn dɔ nnoɔma a ɛfiri amannɔne no nti, a yɛn ɛka a yɛtua a ɛkɔɔ soro kaa ho no, guu yɛn sika no boɔ ase kɛse. Yei ama asetena mu ayɛ den ama yɛn nyinaa, sɛ Aban ne ankorankoro nyinaa. Fangoo boɔ a ɛkɔɔ soro no ama nnoɔma ne ɔsom pii boɔ akɔ soro, berɛ a nnoɔma boɔ kɔɔ soro kɛse kɔduruu 40.4% wɔ Ɔbɛnem 2022 mu.

Sɛ ɔmanfoɔ no, yɛasɔ yɛn ahwɛ ampa, na Aban no te nka kɛseɛ ma mo wɔ nhyɛsoɔ a ɛmfata a yei de aba no ho. Yɛpɛ sɛ yɛda yɛn ase si hɔ sɛ Aban kɔma yɛn nneɛma mu kyɛfafoɔ ahodoɔ no, a Mmarahyɛbadwa, Adwumawurafoɔ Nkabomkuo (Employers’ Associations), Adwumayɛfoɔ Nkabomkuo (Labour Unions), Ɔmanfoɔ Ahyehyɛdeɛ (Civil Society), Ɔsom mu Ahyehyɛdeɛ (Faith Based Organizations), Association of Ghana Industries, Ghana Union of ka ho.

Adwadicfoɔ Nkabomkuo, Nhomasuafoɔ, Nhwehwɛmufoɔ, ne Ghanafoɔ kɛkɛ a wɔkɔ so nya yɛn ho aboterɛ berɛ mpo a yɛrefa saa mmere a emu yɛ den yi mu.

Wɔ H. E. Nana Addo Danquah Akuffo-Addo din mu no, mepɛ sɛ mehyɛ Ghanafoɔ nyinaa bɔ sɛ Aban de nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛbɛsan asiesie sikasɛm no ne botaeɛ a ɛbɛma ɔman no sikasɛm agyina ne nan so reto hɔ, abɔ mmɔden anya sika pii aba ɔman no mu, adi nsakraeɛ a ɛfa adwumayɛbea ne amansan nhyehyeɛ ho dwuma, atrɛ dijitaal ne dan ne akwan ho nkɔsoɔ mu, ate nneɛma a ɛho nhia pii a yɛde firi aburokyi ba no so, ahyɛ nneɛma a yɛtɔn kɔ amannɔne no ho nkuran, atrɛ mfididwuma a ɛwɔ ha a ɛyɛ nneɛma a yɛtɔ firi amannɔne no mu, na wabɔ mmerɛwyɛfoɔ ne wɔn a wɔatwe wɔn ho afiri amansan mu no ho ban.

Nnwomasua a yɛasua sɛ ɔman wɔ saa berɛ yi mu no ama ayɛ dɛn sɛ yɛbɛsakra na yɛasiesie yɛn sikasɛm afebɔɔ na yɛahyɛ mu den. Yɛbɛyɛ no yie pa ara. Yɛbɛfiri yei mu aba a yɛwɔ ahoɔden sene kan no. Awurade bɛkura yɛn mu na wama yɛn ahoɔden de ayɛ yei.

NNHIANIM

1. 2023 Ɔmanmma Baajɛte no yɛ deɛ Aban no tumi yɛeɛ wɔ afe 2022 mu ne sika dodoɔ a ɔpɛ sɛ ɔboaboa ano de yɛ n'adwuma de di nhyehyɛeɛ ne dwumadie ahodoɔ so no ho mmuaeɛ tiawa. 2023 baajɛte no asɛmti ne "Restoring and Sustaining Macroeconomic Stability and Resilience for Inclusive Growth & Value Addition”.

2. Mfeɛ no mu nyinaa, sikasɛm nhyehyɛe foforɔ biara kura adwuma foforɔ pii a wɔrebɛhyɛ aseɛ na agya adwuma a ɛrekɔ so no akyi. Ɛnyɛ sɛ yei de nhyɛsoɔ ba yɛn sikasɛm so nko, na mmom abakɔsɛm kyerɛ sɛ ɛno nso na ɛde aka a yɛtua ba a ɛma adwuma no nyinaa boɔ yɛ kɛseɛ esiane sɛ sika a wɔatwe akyi no nya mfasoɔ boɔ nti.

3. Wɔ afe 2023 mu no, Aban bɛdi nhyehyɛe ne dwumadie foforɔ so, nanso ɔbɛhyɛ nkuran nso ama wɔawie dwumadie a ɛrekɔ so nyinaa.

5. Nsɛm a ɛwɔ hɔ fa sikasɛm no kɔ anim ho firi Jan-Sept 2022 kyerɛ sɛnea wiase afanan nyinaa ne ɔman no mu tebea a ɛyɛ den no kɔ so nya nkɛntɛnsoɔ bɔne wɔ so. Saa nneɛma a asisi yi ada adi wɔ sɛnea sika sesa dodoɔ so ate ntɛmntɛm, nneɛma boɔ a akɔ soro dodo, ɛka a wotua a ɛboro so, sikasɛm mu ahokyere ne abɔnten dwumadie mu ahokyerɛ nyinaa mu, ɛmfa ho sɛ Aban de sika ne sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɛbɛdi ho dwuma no. Sikasɛm kɔ anim a ɛntaa nsi pi firi Jan-Sept 2022 no, wɔde ato dwa sɛdeɛ ɛdidi soɔ yi:

i. Real GDP mpuntuo kɔɔ soro bɛyɛ ɔha nkyekyɛmu 4.0 afi-de-kɔ-afi mu wɔ afe 2022 fa a ɛdi kan no mu sɛ yɛde toto ɔha nkyekyɛmu 3.9 a ɛbae wɔ saa berɛ korɔ no ara mu wɔ afe 2021 ho a;

ii. Headline inflation kɔɔ soro kɔduruu ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 40.4 wɔ Ahinime 2022 mu firii ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 37.2 wɔ Ɛbɔ mu ne ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 33.9 wɔ Ɔsannaa mu, a na ɛnam aduane ne nnoɔma a ɛnyɛ aduane nyinaa boɔ a ɛkɔɔ soro no so;

iii. Sika a ɛtɔ sin wɔ nhyehyɛeɛ nyinaa mu wɔ sika pa ara a wɔde yɛɛ adwuma mu no yɛɛ GDP ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 7.4 sɛ wɔde toto botaeɛ a ɛyɛ GDP ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 6.2 ho a, na

Squadron: Ɛyɛ wimhyɛn asraafoɔ kuo foforɔ bi a ɛwɔ wimhyɛn akuo mmienu anaa nea ɛboro saa ne nnipa a wɔbɛtumi aka wimhyɛn no.

Subsidy: Yei yɛ sika mmoa a aban de ma ɔmanfoɔ de te wɔn nnesoa so.

Ahwɛyie: Ɔkwan a wɔfa so de nhumu anaa anifre hwehwɛ asɛm bi mu yie.

Po asorɔkye kɛse (Tidal wave): Ɛyɛ ɛpo asorɔkye kɛse a ɛboro so, ne titiriw no deɛ asase wosow a ɛsi po ase anaa ogya bepɔ a ɛpae na ɛde ba.

Pefee: Yei ne berɛ a Aban ma ne manfoɔ nkyerɛkyerɛmu a ɛfata na ɛba berɛ ano fa deɛ ɔreyɛ ho.

Kwantenten (Trunk Road): Ɛyɛ kwan kɛse a mpɛn pii no ɛbɔ nkuropɔn mmienu anaa nea ɛboro saa, hyɛn gyinabea ahorow, wimhyɛn gyinabea ahorow ne mmeae afoforo ntam, a ɛyɛ kwan a wɔkamfo kyerɛ ma akwantenten ne adesoa akwantuo.

34 | Ghana Aban - 2023 Ɔman mma Sikakyerɛ

Sikasɛm mu gyinapɛn ne ahoɔden a ɛbɛkɔ so atim a yɛbɛsan de aba ama obiara anya mu kyɛfa wɔ nkɔsoɔ ne mfasoɔ a wɔde bɛka ho mu

Nnoɔma ne Ɔsom a Wɔde Di Dwuma: Sika dodow a Aban tua de di n'adwuma ne sika a ɔde ma ɔmanfoɔ som.

Value Added Tax (VAT): Toɔ a wɔgye berɛ a mfasoɔ kɔ nnoɔma ne ɔsom a wɔde ma so wɔ nnoɔma yɛbea baabiara.

V20 (Vulnerable Twenty): Wɔayɛ sikasɛm mu ɔsɛe kɛse a ewiem nsakrae de aba aman a wobu wɔn sɛ wɔn ho bɛtɔ kyɛn biara wɔ ewiem nsakrae ho no sikasɛm so no ho nkyerɛkyerɛmu kɔ akyiri.

Subsector - sikasɛm dwumadie fã bi a ɛka mfa-mfa akɛseɛ a wɔakyɛ ɔman bi sikasɛm dwumadie mu no mu baako ho.

YouStart: Ɛyɛ nhyehyɛe a Aban nam so pɛ sɛ ɔde sika ne mfididwuma ho mmoa ma mmabun (18-40) ne nnwuma a mmabun a wɔwɔ saa mfeɛ yi mu guaso di anim, de boa wɔn ma wɔhyɛ aseɛ, si, na wɔtrɛ wɔn ankasa nnwuma mu

Youth Employment Agency (YEA): Wɔde Youth Employment Act 2015 (Act 887) na ɛtew YEA, sɛ ɛnhwɛ mmeranteɛ ne mmabaawa adwumayɛ mpuntuo, nkabom, nhwɛsoɔ ne mmoa a wɔde bɛma wɔn, ne nsɛm a ɛfa ho wɔ Ghana mu
