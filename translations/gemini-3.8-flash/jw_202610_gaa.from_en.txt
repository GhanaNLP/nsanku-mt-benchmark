1926

Afi ohai ni eho

“BE kpaa shi nɛ Nyɔŋmɔ baafee gbɛ́i eha ehe. Nyɔŋmɔ baaná mɛi komɛi ni baatsɔmɔ e-odasefoi yɛ shikpɔŋ nɛɛ nɔ,” bɔ ni The Watch Tower ni ji January 1, 1926 lɛ kɛɛ.

Beni afi 1926 lɛ yaa nɔ lɛ, Yehowa—kɛtsɔ asafo lɛ yitso, Kristo nɔ—hã efee faŋŋ hewalɛ kɛ yaa nɔ akɛ shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ esa akɛ efee Kristofonyo shihilɛ mli nɔ titri. Kɛhã nakai yiŋtoo lɛ, “tsulɔ anɔkwafo kɛ nileeji” lɛ kɛ woji hee hã Nyɔŋmɔ tsuji lɛ, efee tsakemɔi yɛ kpeei lɛ amli, ni ehã amɛnu Kristofonyo gbɛnaanii lɛ ashishi faŋŋ. (Mat. 24:45) Kristo kwɛ ni amɛná tsũi kɛ nitsumɔ nɔbii ni he hiaa amɛ kɛhã shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ tsuɔmɔ.

WOLO HEE KƐ KPEEI AHE TSƆƆMƆI

Yɛ April mli lɛ, The Golden Age (nɔ ni atsɛɔ lɛ bianɛ Awake!) bɔ wolo hee ko ni yitso ji Deliverance lɛ kpojiemɔ he amaniɛ. Wolo nɛɛ baaye Studies in the Scriptures najiaŋ akɛ wolo titri ni akɛtsuɔ nii yɛ shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ mli. Bɔ ni afee ni anyɛ afee woloji lɛ babaoo kɛha gbɔmɛi lɛ, nyɛmimɛi hii ni yɔɔ Bethel lɛ hé nitsumɔ tsɔne hee ko ni nyɛɔ ekalaɔ woloji 22 yɛ miniti kome mli. Biblia Kaselɔi lɛ kɛ wolo hee nɛɛ ha yɛ hegbɛ fɛɛ hegbɛ ni amɛnáa mli.

Afii pii lɛ, Biblia Kaselɔi lɛ kase Studies in the Scriptures woji lɛ yɛ amɛkpeei lɛ ashishi. Shi kɛjɛ May mli lɛ, amɛbɔi The Watch Tower mli saji lɛ kasemɔ. Mɛni jɛ mli kɛba?

“Bunker Hill, Illinois asafo bibioo lɛ miina nyɛ,” asafo ko ŋma yɛ wolo mli kɛyaha Nyɛminuu Rutherford. “Yɛ Hɔgbaai anɔ lɛ, wɔkaseɔ [nikasemɔi] ni Gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ eto kɛha nakai gbijurɔi lɛ. Wɔda shi aahu akɛ wɔ fɛɛ wɔbaanyɛ wɔna la hee lɛ kɛji eshɛ be ni esa akɛ eba.”

“Laa hee lɛ,” loo shishinumɔ ni he tse kɛteŋŋ lɛ, ye gbɛi Biblia Kaselɔi lɛ koni amɛhiɛ akakpa shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ nɔ. Ákɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, saji tɛtrɛɛ nɔ ni ji “Su loo Kpaŋmɔ—Te nɔ ni Ji?” lɛ tsɔɔ mli akɛ “suban he nitsumɔ,” ni kɛ ehiɛ ma mɔbɔnalɔ kɛ heshibalɔ suban hepeumɔ pɛ nɔ lɛ, ha mɛi komɛi buuu anɔkwa Kristofoi anitsumɔ krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ nɔ ko. Saji tɛtrɛɛ nɔ lɛ bɔ kɔkɔ akɛ: “Mɔ ni sumɔɔ Nuŋtsɔ lɛ esa akɛ amrɔ nɛɛ . . . ebo ekitiwai lɛ atoi. . . . Yehowa kɛɛ: ‘Nyɛji midasefoi akɛ miji Nyɔŋmɔ.’ . . . Kristofo lɛ baafee he kãa amrɔ nɛɛ koni eye odase ákɛ Nyɔŋmɔ dasefo krɔŋkrɔŋ.”

Nikasemɔ kroko ni yitso ji, “Afɔleshaa Kɛ Sɔɔmɔ,” ma nɔ mi akɛ Kristofoi anɔkwafoi jieɔ suɔmɔ ni amɛyɔɔ kɛha Yehowa kpo kɛtsɔ anɔkwayeli sɔɔmɔ ni amɛkɛhaa lɛ nɔ. Nikasemɔ lɛ wo Biblia Kaselɔi lɛ fɛɛ hewalɛ ni amɛkɛ kãa, hiɛdɔɔ, kɛ hewalɛ fɛɛ atsu Nuŋtsɔ lɛ sɔɔmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ, ni amɛfee “odasefoi kɛha Nuŋtsɔ lɛ ni amɛjaje eshɛɛ sane lɛ amɛtsɔɔ jeŋmaji kɛ maŋbii ni yɔɔ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ lɛ.”—Yes. 43:10, 12; Mat. 24:14.

2 BUU MƆƆ LƐ

Tsakemɔi nɛɛ ahe sɛɛnamɔ je kpo oya nɔŋŋ yɛ asafoi lɛ amli. Otsii fioo pɛ sɛɛ lɛ, July 15, 1926 Watch Tower lɛ kɛ wolo nɛɛ pue: “Nikasemɔi ni ba nyɛsɛɛ nɛɛ, ‘Character or Covenant—Which?’ kɛ ‘Sacrifice and Service,’ ewo wɔ hewalɛ ni eba wɔ tsui shi naakpa. Ebihiaaa ni wɔkane wɔ-Towers lɛ kɛkɛ amrɔ nɛɛ, esa akɛ wɔkase amɛ.” Wolo lɛ mu sane naa kɛkɛ akɛ “biane po lɛ, etamɔ nɔ ni mɔ fɛɛ mɔ miisumɔ tsɔ nɔŋŋ ni ejie esusuŋ kpo faŋŋ yɛ nikasemɔi lɛ amli, ni kɛfata he lɛ, eyeɔ ebuaa wɔ-ecclesia lɛ naakpa.”

KPEI MAAJI ANƆ SHIƐMƆ NITSUMƆ LƐ NƆ MIISHII

May naagbee mli lɛ, Biblia Kaselɔi babaoo kpe yɛ kpokpaa nɔ kpe ko shishi yɛ London, England. Nitsulɔi anɔkpɛlɛmɔ ko ni yaa nɔ yɛ Britain lɛ ha ewa akɛ abaana mɛi ni kalaa woji ni akɛ amɛ atsu nii, no hewɔ lɛ nyɛmimɛi hii ni yɔɔ Brooklyn Bethel lɛ tsu nii be kuku kɛteke nɔ koni amɛnyɛ amɛfee woji tɛtrɛbii ni yitso ji The Standard for the People lɛ ekrokomɛi 117,000 kɛha kpokpaa nɔ kpe lɛ.

Yɛ Hɔɔ, May 29 nɔ lɛ, akɛ woji bibii nɛɛ tsu nii yɛ gbɛ kpakpa nɔ. Kpee lɛ nifeemɔ gbɛjianɔtoo kɛha nakai gbi lɛ wo hewalɛ akɛ: “Aafɔ̃ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni ejɔɔ ehe nɔ ni baakpee lɛ nine koni etsa mfoniri kɛtsɔ Maŋtsɛ lɛ kɛ Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ gbɛɛmɔ nɔ. Nyɛyaa Alexandra Palace mra fe bɔ ni nyɛbaanyɛ, ni nyɛbɔa nyɛhe amaniɛ yɛ Sɔɔmɔ Gbɛjianɔtoo He lɛ kɛha sɔɔmɔ.”

Woji fe 100,000 amli agbá. July 15, 1926 Watch Tower lɛ bɔ amaniɛ akɛ: “Minaŋtsɛmɛi lɛ anii yɛ miishɛɛ naakpa. Amɛnu he akɛ amɛbɔ mɔdɛŋ fɛɛ ni amɛbaanyɛ kɛbo Yehowa famɔ nɛɛ toi: ‘Nyɛji midasefoi akɛ miji Nyɔŋmɔ.’ ”

Enɔ jetsɛremɔ lɛ, yɛ Royal Albert Hall lɛ, Nyɛminuu Rutherford wie yisɛɛsane ni ji “Nɔ Hewɔ Ni Jeŋ Henɔyelihei Lɛ Miishɛriero—Tsabaa Lɛ” lɛ he. Be kukuoo pɛ kɛkɛ ni agbóei lɛ gbele sɛɛ lɛ, gbɔmɛi 13,000 eyi he ni akpeɔ yɛ lɛ teŋŋ. Mɛi babaoo ni nyɛɛɛ abote mli lɛ kɛ jwɛŋmɔ ni ehi shi bo nifeemɔ lɛ toi kɛtsɔ gbagbalatii ni awo yɛ kpo lɛ nɔ.

Yɛ Nyɛminuu Rutherford wiemɔ lɛ mli lɛ, etsɔɔ mli akɛ jeŋ hewalɛi lɛ afee shi kɔkɔɔkɔ lɛ jɛ bɔ ni amɛkpo Yehowa kɛ E-Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ. Nyɛminuu Rutherford tee nɔ ewie eshi Jeŋmaji Akpe Bɔɔ lɛ, ni egba akɛ ebaaba naagbee.

TSU NI YƆƆ 117 ADAMS STREET

Akɛnɛ woji ahe hiamɔ lɛ tee nɔ efã hewɔ lɛ, nyɛmimɛi nukpai ni yɔɔ Brooklyn lɛ na akɛ ehe miihia ni ahã he ni akalaa woji yɛ lɛ afee agbo. Afii babaoo nɛ ni amɛhayi shikpɔŋ kɛ tsũ ni yɔɔ 18 Concord Street lɛ, shi tsũ nɛɛ bɛ he ni akɛtswa kpo ni he hiaa lɛ. Nyɛmimɛi lɛ hé shikpɔŋ ko yɛ Adams Street, he ko ni kɛ tsũ ni amɛyɔɔ mli lɛ teŋ jekɛɛɛ kɔkɔɔkɔ. Amɛje shishi amɛma tsũ ko ni nɔ tsɔ̃i kpaanyɔ ni akɛ simɛnti fee, ni tsũ nɛɛ feemɔ hã he ni amɛyɔɔ lɛ fã fe bɔ ni eji momo lɛ toi enyɔ heŋmɛi.

Tsũ nɛɛ, ni yɔɔ 117 Adams Street lɛ, abaabote mli yɛ afi 1927, ni ebaafee shishitoo nɔ kɛha shwere nɔŋŋ babaoo. Afi ni baanyiɛ sɛɛ lɛ baana gbɛkɛkɛmɔ ni mli wa kɛ gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ mli hetsemɔ ni yaa nɔ, ni ebaafee Nyɔŋmɔ webii lɛ klalo koni amɛsa kɛha gbɛi ni ji Yehowa Odasefoi lɛ mɔɔ.

4 Subaŋ ni anyeɔ hiɛ shi ehiii. Mɛi ni nyee hiɛ shi lɛ fɔɔ nibii anaa gbee kɛtsɔɔ. Biblia lɛ kɛɛ: “Amanehutsɛ lɛ gbii fɛɛ ehiii.” (Abɛi 15:15) Bei komɛi lɛ, wɔ fɛɛ wɔkpeɔ kaa komɛi ahe nɔ ni haa wɔtsui fãa. Shihilɛ he susumɔ kɛ henunɔ nɛkɛ haa wɔkpee miishɛɛ kɛjɛɔ wɔhe, ejaakɛ ehaa wɔnaaa nibii kpakpai ni yɔɔ wɔshihilɛ mli lɛ.

Lala 86:5 nɔŋŋ nɛ, ni kɛɛ: “O Yehowa, ohi ni oyɛ hesaamɔ akɛ ookɛ afa; omli eyi kɛ anɔkwayeli suɔmɔ kɛha mɛi fɛɛ ni boɔ ohe.” Ekɛɛ: “Amrɔ nɔŋŋ lɛ minu he akɛ miyɛ toiŋjɔlɛ mli, ni mida Yehowa shi waa akɛ ekɛ miishɛjemɔ ni he hiaa mi lɛ ha mi. Nakai niiashikpamɔ lɛ woɔ mi hewalɛ ni misusuɔ ŋmalo lɛ he gbi fɛɛ gbi.”

9 Be pii lɛ, gbi lɛ ŋmɛnɛŋmɛnɛ ŋmalɛ lɛ hãa wɔkainaamɔi ni yeɔ bua wɔ koni wɔkɛ “wɔhe afɔ̃ Yehowa nɔ” kɛ́ wɔkɛ shihilɛ mli naagbai kpe. (Abɛi 3:5, 6) Ákɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, gbi nɔ ni tawuu lɛ je shishi yɛ Russia kɛ Ukraine teŋ lɛ, aŋɔ ŋmalɛ lɛ kɛjɛ Yesaia 30:15, he ni kɛɛ: “Kɛ́ nyɛba nyɛhe shi ni nyɛŋmɛ nyɛtsui shi lɛ, ebaafee nyɛhewalɛ.” Nakai kuku lɛ baatsɔ miishɛjemɔ kɛhã wɔnyɛmimɛi hii kɛ yei ni yɔɔ Ukraine lɛ. Ákɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, nyɛminuu ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Eduard lɛ kɛɛ: “Nakai ŋmalɛ lɛ fee tamɔ mumɔ kpakpa ko kɛhã wɔ. Mi kɛ miŋa kane yɛ leebi maŋkpa nakai gbi lɛ nɔ. Kɛ́kɛ ni wɔsɔle, ni ehã wɔ hewalɛ ni wɔhe hiaa koni wɔnyɛ wɔdamɔ bɔ ni wɔnuɔ he wɔhã lɛ naa.” Nyɛmiyei ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Svitlana gba akɛ: “Yesaia 30:15 batsɔ nɔ ko krɛdɛɛ diɛŋtsɛ kɛhã wɔ. Wɔtee nɔ wɔkɛbaa wɔjwɛŋmɔ mli ni wɔjwɛŋ nɔ ni etsɔɔ lɛ nɔ be ni tawuu kpele lɛ yaa nɔ yɛ wɔhe kpaŋŋ lɛ.”

10 Wɔsusuɔ gbi ko gbi ko ŋmalɛ lɛ he ejaakɛ ewajeɔ wɔtsui koni wɔhiɛ akpiɛ ni wɔya nɔ wɔye anɔkwa. Ehe miihia ni wɔná ekãa koni wɔye srɔto kɛjɛ wɔskulbii kɛ wɔnitsulɔmɛi ahe, ni wɔkɛ mɛi krokomɛi agbála Biblia mli anɔkwalei amli, ni wɔhiɛ akpiɛ kɛ́ wɔkɛ yiwalɛ kpe. Be ni ahala Yosua akɛ Israel maŋ lɛ nɔkwɛlɔ lɛ, Yehowa kɛɛ lɛ toi etɛ akɛ ená ekãa ni ewo egbɔmɔtsoŋ hewalɛ. Bɔ ni afee ni Yehowa awaje Yosua ekãa lɛ, Ewo lɛ hewalɛ hu ni ekane Mla wolo lɛ daa gbi. (Kane Yosua 1:6-9.) Kɛ́ wɔkane gbi ko gbi ko ŋmalɛ lɛ, kɛfata wɔ-daa gbi Biblia kanemɔ lɛ he lɛ, ebaaye ebua wɔ koni wɔwo wɔgbɔmɔtsoŋ hewalɛ ni wɔya nɔ wɔye anɔkwa.

11 Wɔsusuɔ gbi lɛ ŋmɛnɛŋmɛnɛ ŋmalɛ lɛ he ejaakɛ ewaa wɔ ni wɔyaa wɔhiɛ yɛ mumɔŋ. Paulo kɛɛ oblanyo Timoteo akɛ eya nɔ eya ehiɛ yɛ nibii tamɔ wiemɔ, jeŋba, suɔmɔ, hemɔkɛyeli, kɛ hetsɛmɔ mli. (1 Tim. 4:12, 15) Kɛ́ wɔsusu

9. Mɛni gbɛ nɔ gbi ko ŋmɛnɛŋmɛnɛ ŋmalɛ lɛ ye ebua wɔ-nyɛmimɛi hii kɛ yei ni yɔɔ Ukraine lɛ?

daa gbi ŋmalɛ lε, ehaa wɔ hegbɛ ni wɔsusu bɔ ni wɔbaafee wɔtsɔ mumɔŋ gbɔmɔ he. Mumɔŋ gbɔmɔ ji mɔ ko ni kaseɔ bɔ ni Yehowa susuɔ nii ahe ehãa kɛ mɔ ni bɔɔ mɔdɛŋ kɛsaa E-hiɛ kɛ hiɛŋ ewala shi yɛ E-tɛi ahe.—Rom. 8:5, 6.

12 Wɔkaseɔ gbi lɛ nɔ ŋmalɛ lɛ ejaakɛ ebaaye ebua wɔ ni wɔsaa wɔhe kɛha teokrase nitsumɔi yɛ wɔsɛɛ. Yɛ Yehowa gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ mli lɛ, ehe miihia ni wɔna nyɛmimɛi hii kɛ yei ni eda yɛ mumɔŋ ni baanyɛ aye abua yɛ nitsumɔ ni miiya nɔ ebuaa he lolo lɛ mli. Ehe miihia ni wɔna nyɛmimɛi hii kɛ yei ni baanyɛ asɔmɔ akɛ gbɛgbalɔi kɛ maŋsɛɛ sanekpakpa jatsɛlɔi, kɛ agbɛnɛ kɛha Betel, tsumaa nitsumɔi, kɛ shishitsɔɔmɔ hejimai komɛi anɔ. Ehe miihia ni wɔna gbekɛbii hii ni yɔɔ hewalɛ ni baa amɛhe shi koni amɛsɔmɔ akɛ sɔɔlɔi, asafoŋ onukpai, kɛ kpokpaa nɔkwɛlɔi. Yehowa miitao nyɛmimɛi hii ni anyɛɔ akɛ he fɔɔ amɛnɔ, hii ni le bɔ ni akɛ Biblia shishitoo mlai tsuɔ nii koni amɛnyiɛ hiɛ yɛ egbɛjianɔtoo lɛ mli. (5 Mo. 1:13-15) Kɛ́ okase gbi lɛ nɔ ŋmalɛ lɛ akɛ ogbɛjianɔtoo kɛha mumɔŋ nibii lɛ he fã lɛ, ebaatsɔɔ bo nakai shishitoo mlai lɛ ni ebaaye ebua bo ni osaa ohe kɛha nitsumɔi kpokpoi krokomɛi.

Mɛni gbɛ nɔ obaatsɔ osusu wolo nɛɛ he?

13 Mɛni obaanyɛ ofee kɛkase daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ? Betel wekui ni yɔɔ je lɛŋ fɛɛ kɛ sɔlemɔ kuku ni jɛ tsuiŋ haa Yehowa jeɔ amɛ nitsumɔ gbi lɛ shishi, no sɛɛ lɛ amɛsusuɔ gbi lɛ ŋmalɛ lɛ he. Bo hu obaanyɛ ohala akɛ obáje shishi kɛ sɔlemɔ kuku, ni obi Yehowa ni eye ebua bo koni oná nɔ ni obaakane lɛ he sɛɛ. (Yak. 1:5) No sɛɛ lɛ kane ŋmalɛ lɛ kɛ wiemɔi ni atsɔɔ mli lɛ. No sɛɛ lɛ, bi ohe saji tamɔ: ‘Mɛni gbɛ nɔ ŋmalɛ nɛɛ baanyɛ awo mi hemɔkɛyeli lɛ hewalɛ? Mɛni gbɛ nɔ ejwɛŋmɔ he baanyɛ eha matsɔ bɛŋkɛ Yehowa kpaakpa? Mɛni gbɛ nɔ ebaanyɛ eye ebua mi ni makpe ŋmɛnɛ gbi nɛɛ naagbai lɛ anaa?’ Kɛ́ okɛ oweku loo nanemɛi miihe asusu ŋmalɛ lɛ he lɛ, mɛni hewɔ osusuuu he akɛ nɔ ko ni baawo

14 Mɛni gbɛi amɔ̃ nɔ obaanyɛ oná gbi lɛ ŋmɛnɛŋmɛnɛ ŋmalɛ lɛ he sɛɛ kpatashii? Ka ŋaawoo nɛɛ ateŋ ekome loo babaoo okwɛ: (1) Halamɔ oti kpokpoi ni yɔɔ Buu-Mɔɔ lɛ mli wiemɔi lɛ amli lɛ koni ojwejuwe nɔ. (2) Kanemɔ ŋmalɛi ni afee he mfoniri yɛ wiemɔi lɛ amli lɛ. (3) Kanemɔ Biblia lɛ mli yitso muu lɛ fɛɛ he ni ŋmalɛ lɛ yɔɔ lɛ. (4) Kwɛmɔ Nii Amli Halamɔ Wolo Kɛhã Yehowa Odasefoi lɛ loo nikaselɔi akadumɔi ni yɔɔ Biblia lɛ mli lɛ koni okase babaoo. (5) Kanemɔ nikasemɔ saji ni akɔ wiemɔi lɛ kɛjɛ mli lɛ fãi komɛi. Kɛ́ oomiikpɛlɛ dijital wolo lɛ nɔ lɛ, obaanyɛ oya nikasemɔ sane lɛ nɔ kɛtsɔ he ni aŋma yɛ wiemɔi lɛ agbɛgbee nɔ lɛ nɔ mli tsiimɔ nɔ.*

15 Yɛ gbi lɛ ŋmalɛ lɛ he nɔjwɛŋmɔ hewɔ lɛ, nyɛminyɛoo ko ni eye afii 19 ni atsɛɔ lɛ Mackenzie kɛɛ: “Kɛ́ mikɛ be fioo eko he kɛfata he, ekolɛ miniti enumɔ kɛkɛ, ni mikpalaŋ misɛɛ kɛkane ni mijwɛŋ ŋmalɛ lɛ kɛ hewiemɔi lɛ ahe lɛ, minyɛɔ mikaiɔ saji lɛ jogbaŋŋ fe tsɔ.”

Kɛkɛ ni bɔ ni afee ni amɛhiɛ akakpa nɔ lɛ, amɛtoɔ *Ŋmalɛ Kpɛŋŋi lɛ Amlihomɔ Daa Gbi* wolo lɛ eko nɔ yɛ okpɔlɔ lɛ nɔ. Eyɛ faŋŋ akɛ, kɛ́ nibii komɛi ba ni ohiɛ kpaaa nɔ lɛ, ebaabi ni otsake ohe kɛyaba he kɛ o-be-to-he lɛ.

LALA 94 Wɔda Nyɔŋmɔ Wiemɔ Lɛ Shi

Maa Ohemɔkɛyeli Lɛ Shi Shiishiŋ Ni Kpɛŋŋ

BE ni okase Biblia lɛ kɛ Yehowa Odasefoi lɛ, mɛni ha oná nɔmimaa akɛ oná “hemɔkɛyeli kome” lɛ—jamɔ gbɛ kome pɛ ni sa ni Nyɔŋmɔ kpɛlɛɔ nɔ lɛ? (Efe. 4:4-6) Ani suɔmɔ ni ona yɛ Yehowa webii lɛ ateŋ lɛ ha otsɔ amɛbɛŋkɛ? Ani amɛjeŋba kpakpa lɛ ba onɔ gbɛ̀i kpakpa nɔ? Mɛi pii baa anɔkwale lɛ mli ejaakɛ Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ haa amɛnuɔ he akɛ amɛyɛ shweshweeshwe ni asumɔɔ amɛ diɛŋtsɛ, ni enɛ ji nɔ ni anuɔ shishi. Suɔmɔ kɛ jeŋba kpakpa ji anɔkwa jamɔ kadimɔnɔi.—Yoh. 13:35; 1 Pet. 2:12.

Shi kɛji nyɛmimɛi heyelilɔ ko tɔ̃ o nɔ aloo kɛtsɔ gbɛ kroko nɔ ejeee suɔmɔ kpo etsɔɔɔ bo nɛ? Aloo kɛji nyɛmimɛi heyelilɔ ko yabɔ esha ko ni yɔɔ hiɛdɔɔ mli—po jeŋba fɔŋ ko ni he yɔɔ nyaŋegbɔmɔ, tamɔ bɔ ni eba lɛ yɛ mɔ ko gbɛfaŋ yɛ klɛŋklɛŋ afii oha lɛ mli asafo lɛ mli yɛ Korinto lɛ nɛ? (1 Kor. 5:1) Kɛji akɛ ohemɔkɛyeli lɛ damɔ bɔ pɛ ni mɛi krokomɛi anifeemɔi haa onuɔ he ohaa lɛ nɔ lɛ, belɛ edamɔ nɔ ko ni mli ewaaa nɔ. Ani fãishikpɔŋ ko ni mli wa fe nakai yɛ kɛhã ohemɔkɛyeli lɛ?

HEMI KƐ YELI MAŊ SHI KPATIŊ NI MA SHI KPƐŊŊ

Oobaanyɛ oto heshima nɔŋŋ fãi shi kɛha ohemɔkɛyeli kɛtsɔ anɔkwale nina titrii ni okase kɛjɛ Biblia lɛ mli lɛ nɔ ni oooma ohiɛ kpo nɔ jogbaŋŋ. Mɛni he enɛ baaye abua? Susumɔ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ ko he okwɛ.

Be ko mli lɛ, Yesu kaselɔi lɛ ateŋ mɛi pii shi lɛ. Amɛtɔtɔ yɛ e-wiemɔ ni kɔɔ eheloo ni abaaye kɛ ela ni abaana he lɛ hew. Amɛkɛɛ: “Wiemɔ nɛɛ yɛ gbeyei.” Nɔ ni Yesu kɛɛ lɛ dɔ amɛ aahu akɛ amɛkpa eyi sɛɛ faimɔ.—Yohane 6:56-60, 66.

No sɛɛ lɛ, Yesu bi ebɔfoi lɛ akɛ: “Nyɛ hu nyɛsumɔɔɔ ni nyɛyaa, aloo?” Petro ha hetoo akɛ: “Nuŋtsɔ, namɔ ŋɔɔ wɔbaaya? Bo ojio naanɔ wala wiemɔi lɛ. Wɔhe wɔye ni wɔle hu akɛ bo ji Nyɔŋmɔ Krɔŋkrɔŋno lɛ.”—Yohane 6:67-69.

Mɛni ha Petro kɛ bɔfoi krokomɛi lɛ yɔɔ srɔto kɛje mɛi ni nane tɔ̃tɔ̃ lɛ ahe? Amɛhemɔkɛyeli lɛ damɔ nɔ ko ni mli wa fe amɛhenumɔi anɔ. Petro kɛɛ: “Wɔhe wɔye ni wɔle akɛ bo ji Nyɔŋmɔ Krɔŋkrɔŋ Nɔ lɛ.” Anɔkwafoi nɛɛ le nɔ ni amɛheɔ amɛyeɔ kɛ nɔ hewɔ ni amɛheɔ amɛyeɔ lɛ. Akɛni amɛto Yesu wiemɔi kɛ enifeemɔi lɛ ahe kɛteke nɔ ni amɛle yɛ Ŋmalɛi lɛ amli lɛ hewɔ lɛ, amɛná nɔmimaa akɛ Yesu ji Mesia lɛ. Amɛhemɔkɛyeli ni mli wa yɛ nakai shishijee anɔkwale nɛɛ mli lɛ ha amɛhemɔkɛyeli lɛ mli wa be ni mɛi krokomɛi anane tɔ̃tɔ̃ yɛ nɔ ni Yesu kɛɛ lɛ hewɔ lɛ.

Mɛni sane nɛɛ tsɔɔ wɔ? Nɔ ni wɔleɔ lɛ baanyɛ afee nɔ ni mli wa fe bɔ ni wɔnuɔ he wɔhãa. Mɛni hew? Ejaakɛ Biblia mli tsɔɔmɔi lɛ—ni anɔkwalei ni wɔkase yɛ Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛ́i, eyiŋtoo, kɛ kpɔmɔnɔ lɛ he lɛ fata he lɛ—tsakeee. Ekãaa wɔhenumɔi lɛ, nɔ ni gbɛmɛi feɔ aloo amɛkɛɔ lɛ tsakeee Biblia mli anɔkwalei lɛ.

30 BUU MƆƆ LE

No hewɔ lɛ, kɛ́ wɔhe Biblia mli anɔkwalei lɛ wɔye ni wɔtsɔɔ gbɛ kuku ni Yehowa kɛtsuɔ nii lɛ nɔ wɔkɛ anɔkwalei nɛɛ bɔɔ gbee lɛ, mɛi krokomɛi ajeŋba tɔtɔŋ wɔnane.

E yɛ lɛɛlɛŋ akɛ, nyɛmimɛi lɛ asuɔmɔ lɛ woɔ wɔ hewalɛ. Ni wɔnyɛmimɛi heyelilɔi lɛ ajeŋba kpakpa lɛ haa wɔmii shɛɔ. Shi kɛ́ eba mli akɛ nakai henumɔi lɛ bɔi tɔɔlɔmɔ yɛ mɛi krokomɛi anifeemɔi ahewɔ lɛ, belɛ wɔhemɔkɛyeli ni mli wa yɛ Biblia mli anɔkwalei lɛ amli lɛ baanyɛ aha wɔhemɔkɛyeli lɛ afee nɔ ni ma shi shiŋŋ. Ebaaye ebua wɔ ni wɔna akɛ wɔyɛ gbɛjianɔtoo ni ja lɛ mli—ekɔɔɔ he eko nɔ ni mɛi komɛi baafee.

Kɛ́ wɔyɛ be kpakpa kɛha wɔ diɛŋtsɛ wɔ-Biblu mli nikasemɔ lɛ, wɔhemɔkɛyeli ni yɔɔ anɔkwalei ni wɔkase kɛjɛ Nyɔŋmɔ Wiemɔ lɛ mli lɛ mli lɛ mli waa. Enɛ hu baaye abua wɔ ni wɔya nɔ wɔná nɔmimaa akɛ wɔna jamɔ gbɛ kome pɛ ni Nyɔŋmɔ kplɛɔ nɔ lɛ. Kɛkɛ lɛ wɔhemɔkɛyeli lɛ baadamɔ shishifai ni ma shi shiŋŋ nɔ, ni mɛi krokomɛi awiemɔi loo anifeemɔi nyɛŋ ahoso wɔ.—Lala 16:8; kwɛmɔ daka ni yitso ji “Hiɛ Gbɛ Kpakpa Lɛ Nɔ.”

Hiɛ gbɛ kpakpa lɛ nɔ

Feemɔ he mfoniri akɛ oteŋ bɔs ko ni baanyiɛ mayli ohai abɔ kɛya he ko ni yɔɔ fɛo tamɔ nɔ ko. Yɛ gbɛfãa kakadaŋŋ nɛɛ mli lɛ, ewaŋ akɛ obaanu henaabuamɔi srɔtoi he—titri lɛ miishɛɛ akɛ oyɛ gbɛfãa nɛɛ nɔ.

Kɛlɛ, beni oyɔɔ gbɛfãa nɛɛ nɔ lɛ, nibii komɛi baanyɛ afee nɔ ni baagba onáa—ekolɛ mɛi enyɔ komɛi ni yɔɔ gbɛfãlɔi lɛ ateŋ ni wieɔ kɛ gbee ni wa loo drajva lɛ hewalɛ kpo ni etsɔɔ po. Shi enɛɛmɛi ateŋ eko eko etsakeee anɔkwale ni eji akɛ oyɔɔ bɔs ni ja lɛ mli, kɛ agbɛnɛ hu akɛ ehe miihia ni ohi mli bɔni afee ni oshɛ he ni oyaa lɛ.

Yɛ wɔfãatsee kɛmiiya Nyɔŋmɔ jeŋ hee lɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaanu hewalɛi srɔtoi ahe—titri lɛ, miishɛɛ akɛ wɔyɛ hegbɛ akɛ wɔji Nyɔŋmɔ asafo lɛ fã. Kɛ̃lɛ be komɛi lɛ, nanemɛi heyelilɔ ko baanyɛ atɔ̃ wɔnɔ aloo mɔ ko ni wɔbuaa lɛ he lɛ nifeemɔ fɔŋ baanyɛ afee wɔ naakpɛɛ po. Shi enɛɛmɛi ateŋ nɔ ko nɔ ko etsakeee anɔkwale ni eji akɛ wɔyɛ bɔs ni ja lɛ mli, kɛ́ wɔbaakɛɛ nakai. Wɔyɛ asafo ni ja lɛ mli. Kɛ́ wɔyɛ Biblia mli shishijee anɔkwalei lɛ ahe nilee kpele—kɛ agbɛnɛ hiɛshikamɔ kɛha he nɔ ni wɔkase nakai anɔkwalei lɛ yɛ lɛ—wɔbaahi gbɛ ni ja lɛ nɔ. Wɔhemɔkɛyeli lɛ baahi shi kpɛŋŋ.

Kɛji wɔhemɔkɛyeli damɔ bɔ ni mɛi krokomɛi anifeemɔi haa wɔnuɔ he wɔhaa nɔ kɛkɛ nɔ lɛ, belɛ edamɔ shishifai ni gbaŋgbamɔɔ nɔ

Yehowa Gbaa Hemɔkɛyeli Naa Nyɔmɔ

Emma tsu nii yɛ gbɔmɛiatsuu ko mli gbii etɛ yɛ otsi mli, ŋmɛlɛtswai nɛɛhu gbi ko gbi ko. Ebi enitsumɔ nɔkwɛlɔ lɛ hegbɛ ni ehã lɛ gbi ko kɛtsɔ shi koni enyɛ eya ekpokpaa nɔ kpeemɔ lɛ. Yɛ nɔ ni enitsumɔ nɔkwɛlɔ lɛ aaahã lɛ nɔ ni ebi lɛ nɔŋŋ lɛ, etsake ebeblamɔ lɛ kɛtee gbii enumɔ yɛ otsi mli ni ehã lɛ nɔmimaa fɔŋ: Kpɛlɛ beblamɔ hee lɛ nɔ aloo okpã nitsumɔ lɛ. Emma kɛ ehiɛ fɔ̃ Yehowa nɔ, ni ehala akɛ eeekpã nitsumɔ lɛ ni eya kpeemɔ lɛ.

Be ni Emma tee koni eyafɔ̃ nitsumɔ lɛ mli lɛ, hotel lɛ nɔkwɛlɔ kpatakpa lɛ yɛ jɛmɛ nakai beiaŋ. Be ni enu sane lɛ he nɔ̃ ko lɛ, ewo shi akɛ ebaakwɛ sane lɛ nɔ. Kɛbashi nakai beiaŋ lɛ, be ni otsii enyɔ eho lɛ, akɛɛ Emma akɛ ashwie lɛ kɛjɛ nitsumɔ lɛ mli. Ekɛ hotel lɛ nɔkwɛlɔ kpatakpa lɛ kpe ekoŋŋ, mɔ ni mli fu akɛ agbo etsɔsmɔi lɛ anɔ toi. Etsa sane lɛ nɔ, ni akɛ be heele ha Emma: gbii enumɔ yɛ otsi mli, ŋmɛlɛtswai ejwɛ yɛ gbi mli. Ebafee nɔ ni hi kwraa fe be ni etsuɔ nii yɛ mli tsutsu lɛ; eha enyɛ etsu e-teokrase nitsumɔi lɛ amli gbɛfaŋ nitsumɔi lɛ ahe nii! Yehowa kwɛ eniashikpamɔi lɛ anɔ yɛ gbɛ ko nɔ ni fe nɔ ni ekpaa gbɛ kwraa.
