TITIRI MMƆ Mma Satan nnnaadaa wo na ɔnsɛe Jehovah Nyame ne Jesus Christ duma.

“Bonsu Abonsam, na obɔgwannɛ wɔ.”—JAS. 4:7.

EFI kpaan kɛ yɛyeyɛ yɛ adwen kɛ yɛkɔsom Jehovah pɛ, Satan n’ahe sunsum mɔne mɔ yɛ yɛatamfo denden. Nso Satan nim kɛ ɔntumi yɛ nneɛma a bɔkɔsom Jehovah yɛ. Ɛhye ti, Bonsam bɔ mmɔden kɛ ɔkɔsɛɛ sunsum mɔne mɔ n’adwuma kɛ ɔkɔfa yɛ afi Jehovah nkyɛn. Ɔkwan foforo a ɔfa so yɛ ɛhye ne kɛ ɔbɔ mmɔden kɛ ɔbɛma yɛanya akyinnye kɛ Jehovah ne ne Ba no wɔ yɛn afa ampa.—2 Cor. 11:14; Jas. 1:6.

2 Sɛ asoasoafo kɔ akono a, ɔsɛ kɛ bɛnyan anwukelo wɔ bɛ mumuayifo nu. Sɛ bɛ bɔ bɛ anwukelo zɔ a, bie a bɛbaye bɛ nwo ayɛ ndwenndwen nu ndinyɛ. Ɛhe bahola ama bɛawu. Wɔ zɔhane ara nu, yɛle asoasoafo wɔ sunsum nu akono nu tia “awule sunsum mɔ.” (Efe. 6:12) Satan Bonsam ze kɛ, sɛ yɛ bɔ yɛ anwukelo wɔ Yehowa Nyamenle nee Yesu Kristo nu a, ɛkɛne bie a yɛkpo bɛ akwankyerɛ nee bɛ mmoa. Zɔhane ti, ɔyɛ ninyɛne muala mɔɔ ɔbahola kɛ ɔbama yɛ anwukelo wɔ bɛ nu adɔ kɔma. Wɔ edwɛkɛhe nu, yɛbanwu kɛzi ɔvalɛ zɔhane kɛlɛkɛlɛ kwan zɔ lile gyuma mɔɔ pɛ nu nee kɛzi ɔda so ɔfa ɔdi gyuma ndɛ. Akee yɛbasuzyan kɛzi yɛbahola yɛazi ye ngyedua mɔ anyi. Wɔ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ di adua nu, yɛbanwu kɛlɛkɛlɛ akwan nwiɔ gyɛne mɔɔ Satan fa di gyuma tia yɛ wɔ yɛ sunsum nu akono nu.*

* Satan nwoe nyɛ ninyeni mɔɔ ɔfa sɔhwɛ ngoko biala mɔɔ yɛto nu la ba. Na nwonso wɔ “ewie aze sunsum” mɔɔ ka atiafo ma bɛyɛ Nyame manmanfo bɔne la aze. Ɛhye zɔti, nwonso dwo nu anzeɛ ɔnwoe ndwo nu biala, Satan dwo kɔnsi bɔnbɔn biala mɔɔ yɛto nu wɔ yɛ sunsum nu kɔne la aze.—1 Cor. 2:12; 1 John 5:19.

3 Mmerɛ a adesamma asetena hyɛɛ aseɛ foforɔ no, Satan bɔɔ ne tirim sɛ ɔbɛtwe Adam ne Eve afiri Jehovah ho. Satan lakaa Eve ma ɔgyee dii sɛ ne Bɔfoɔ no nni n’apɛdeɛ pa wɔ ne koma mu. Satan bisoo no sɛ: “Ampa na Onyankopɔ kaa sɛ ɛnsɛ sɛ motwe mo nsa firi turo no mu dua biara so?” (Gen. 3:1-5) Satan maa Eve gye toom sɛ Jehovah de ahyɛdeɛ a ɛnteɛ rehyɛ ɔno ne ne kunu so. Awerɛhosɛm ne sɛ, Eve na akyire yi Adam nso kɔdɔɔ Satan a ɔyɛ ɔtamfo no akyi. Wɔdanee wɔn akyi kyerɛɛ Jehovah—Deɛ ɔde nneɛma nyinaa maa wɔn no.—Gen. 3:6.

4 Ofi Adam na Hawa berɛ zo no, Satan kɔ zo ka amumuze fa Yehowa nwo. Kɛ nhwɛso no, Satan hɛnene Hiob anyenam bɔ bɛte abosomfo no anwo kɛ bɛma Hiob anye nlo bɔbɔ kɛ Yehowa dɔ ye na ɔbu ye nɔhalɛlilɛ no kɛ bɔbɔ. (Hiob 22:1-3) Obonsam dwenene kɛ amaneɛnwunɛ bɔ ɔbato Hiob no bamaa yeapo Yehowa. Ɔmuka kɛ Hiob yiale nhyɛso kpole wɔ Satan nnahɔlɛ no ngati zo no, saa nɔhalɛnli no lili nɔhalɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ, na yeamgayinle ye anye yeamfi abusuabɔlɛ bɔ ɔne Nyamenle lɛ no zo kyɛkye.—Hiob 27:1, 5.

5 Mfe pii akyi berɛ a Yesu baa asase yi so no, Satan bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛsi nnipa kwan na wɔannidi Yesu akyi. Kwan koro a ɔfaa so yɛɛ yei ne sɛ ɔkanyan asɔre akannifo a wɔwɔ ɔtan ne afoforo ma wɔkeka atoro faa Yesu ho. Na awieeɛ koraa no, wɔkũũ no te sɛ nkontompofo bi.—Kenkan

Mateo 11:19; 27:27-31; Maakɩ 14:55, 56; Dwɔɔn 8:41-45.

6 Yesu wuo akyi no, Satan dan n’adwen kɔɔ Yesu akyidifo no adwuma so. Nea edi kan no, Ɔbonsam bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛma nnipa anya adwenkyerɛ a enni mu wɔ Yesu wusɔre no ho. Wɔ Satan nkɛntɛnso ase no, asɛm a ɛnyɛ nokware bi trɛwee sɛ Yesu asuafo no awia ne funu no. (Matt. 28:11-15) Afei wɔ nna a edi hɔ no mu no, Satan bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛfa ɔtaa so ahunahuna asafo no. Na Satan pɛ sɛ ɔma adwuma a wɔn Wura no de ahyɛ wɔn nsa no yɛ kɛse dodo ma wɔn sɛ wobedi ho dwuma.—Acts 7:57–8:1; 12:1-3; 28:22.

7 Ɛyɛ nani mɔɔ tete Kristofoɔ bie mɔ hyɛle kɛ bɛbaagyina asɛnkɛnga ne azo bie no, dɔɔnwo gyinli kpundii na bɛampene kɛ bɛti bɛ nuanze. Bɛnwunle ye kɛ Satan ne abɔnsam mɔɔ aka ne yɛ bɛ kpɔfoɔ amapa ne a. (Asomafo. 4:18-20; 1 Pet. 5:8) Na nnɛ nso ɛ? Adwenle bɔne bɛni yɛ Satan fefa ye dwuma kɛ ɔfa bɔ mmɔden kɛ ɔbaagu yɛ abamu na yɛannyi Gyihova ɛzonlɛ nu a?

Ye zɔhane ti, nnyuama mɔɔ bɛlɛ ahonle kpalɛ la bɛ nwo kɔhɔ kyengye kɛ bɛbanwu Yehowa kɛ Adamfo a bɛbahola bɛabikye ye. (Yak. 4:8) Na so, Satan kpondɛ kɛ ɔsɛkye Yehowa nnyuama gyidie.

“Yɛnne mogya ne honam na ɛreko, na mmom . . . honhommɔne dɔm a wɔwɔ soro hɔ no.” —EF. 6:12.

KƐ YELE Jehovah nzonlinimma ti, yɛnnyɛ konko bia yɛnnkɔ nsa. (Isa. 2:4; John 18:36) Nanso, Baebolo ne ka konko bie mɔɔ yɛ mu bieara ɛnnkɔhɔla ɛnnyia nwoma—konko mɔɔ yɛne sunsum ɛtane mɔ lile. Bɛbɔ mmɔden kɛ bɛbelɛ yɛ, kɛ bɛkɔhɔla atwe yɛ afi nokware asɔredze nu. Bɛyɛ bɛ adwen koso kɛ bɛkasa bɛto nkorɔfo ne ahyehyɛde mɔ—bɔ bɛ nu bie mɔ le “bɛ bɔ bɛkyi yɛ” la—zo ama bɛatan yɛ asɔredze. Ɔwɔ kɛ bɛgye yɛ bɛfi nfɔtefo kɛhye mɔ nsa nu.

Yehowa aboa me ma manya adwene papa wɔ atumfoɔ ho na mebrɛ me ho ase. Megyaee amannsan nnamfonom bɔ ama matumi abɔ me bra sɛdeɛ Yehowa mmara kyerɛ no, nanso Yehowa de nnamfonom ampaampa dɔɔn asiesie eyi koraa ama me. Manya ɔyere pa kɛseɛ. Afei nso mewɔ anidasoɔ kɛseɛ sɛ mɛnya Yehowa ayamyɛ no daa daa nyinaa ara.—Mark 10:29, 30.

ASƆ ƐNƖ?

Nzu dwuma ahõnle yɛ bɛyɛle wɔ suanba anyunlu wɔ Baebolo mmerɛ nu?

SUA tiiti nnyianle biala te kɛ ahenle biala mɔɔ bɛ kulo kɛ bɛ kɔ nu la ala. Kɛmɔ menli dɔɔnwo fa ɛkɛ kɔ la ati, ɛkɛ ne bɛyɛle ɛleka mɔɔ menli dɔɔnwo yɛ bɛ daa gyima la. Baebolo ne hanle sua tiiti nnyianle nwo edwɛkɛ dɛla fane 50, na ɔ kilele gyima mɔɔ bɛyɛle ye wɔ ɛkɛ ne la.

Menma booboo wɔ suokuro kpunsua nu. Hebri asɛm mɔ bɛfɛre ye “kpunsua” no kile dɔɔn koraa tɛ suokuro no anuma anuanua no ngunmin. Ɔkile baabi mɔ bɛdua asua wɔ suokuro anzɛɛ taon bi anuanua no. Wɔ mbuasu bi nu no, adabasa kpole wɔ hɔ wɔ kpunsua no nu mɔ menma dɔɔn koraa kɔhɔla boɔboɔ wɔ hɔ.—Neh. 8:1.

Badwifoɔ do gua kuro pon no nkyɛn. Kuro pon bie mɔ wɔ Jerusalem, te sɛ Kenyɛ Pon ne Nguan Pon no, gyama nyaa bɛ duma firii nneɛma mɔ bɛ tɔn wɔ bɛ nkyɛn no mu.—Zeph. 1:10; John 5:2.

Menli yɛɛ ngua ne ahenli ngyikyi ngyikyi nso dwumadi wɔ kurow ne aponno anuo. Abraham nee Boaz yɛɛ amambuo dwumadi wɔ adansefo aniso wɔ kurow ne aponno anuo. (Gen. 23:10-15, 17, 18; Ruth 4:1, 10) Akwantufo bie mɔ da gua kɛse mɔɔ bɔ kurow ne aponno anuo n’aso sɛ obi angye bɛ hɔ fie a. (Judg. 19:15-21) Menli kɔ kurow ne aponno anuo nso sɛ bɛpɛ kɛ bɛte amaneɛbɔ foforo a.—2 Sam. 18:24, 25.

Atɛmudafoɔ di asɛm wɔ kuropɔn pon n’ano. Wɔdi asɛm wɔ nnipa a wɔrehwɛ na wɔbɛtumi ate adanseɛ ne asɛm no awieeɛ anim. (Deut. 21:18-20) Ɛyɛ abɔnten asɛmdii yi maa atɛmudafoɔ no dii asɛntreneɛ na wɔanyi animhwɛ wɔ wɔn atɛmmudeɛ mu. Sɛ atɛmudafoɔ no bu atɛntreneɛ wie a, mpanimfoɔ no de asotwe no to wɔn so ntɛm ara. (Deut. 25:2) Wɔ kuropɔn no aponnua ano,

Kuro m’aboboano na ɛyɛ beae a nnipa pii hyia di da biara da dwumadi ahorow

Nde ɛnee ɔsɛ kɛ ndɛnbufoɔ boa ma tɛnene di ne suban kpa ba.—Deut. 16:18-20; Job 29:7, 11-17.

Mmarima anokwafo hyɛɛ Israel mman a wɔwɔ kurow n’ano npono no ano no nkuran sɛ wɔmfa wɔn ho nto Yehowa so. Nkɔmhyɛfo ne afoforo kãa asɛm wɔ anpono no ano efisɛ na wɔnim sɛ nnipa hyia hɔ. (1 Ki. 22:10; Jer. 17:19, 20) Sɛ nhwɛso no, Yehu kã kyerɛɛ nnipa no nyinaa sɛ Yehowa ayɛ n’asɛm a ɔkã tiaa Ahab fie no. (2 Ki. 10:8-10) Ɔhene Hezekiah hyiaa ne sahene mmarima wɔ kurow n’ano pon koro ano de hyɛɛ wɔn nkuran sɛ wɔmfa wɔn ho nto Yehowa mmoa so.—2 Chron. 32:6-8.

Baebolo kɛ “nyilile kpale” su tea kpu kɛɛ ɔ teɛne ye nne do wɔ “kuro kpole anuanlo atilate nun.” (Mfu. 1:20, 21) Mɔɔ bɛwo tetenyi Israel manle nun lɔ no, kuro kpole anuanlo yɛ nrenyia abodokɛngalɛma manle anyumbuo afotu kɛɛ bɛbule buoliɛ tenlene a. Ɛnɛ, yɛhyehyɛ Yehowa nyilile wɔ Baebolo ne nun, na ye asunsumvoma wɔ baabiara su boa menma kɛɛ bɛhɔ nyilile kpale yi aseɛ lɔ.

BAABOLO NU ANOHỌLE Baka Mɔɔ Sɔre Ndɛndɛ

Almond baka le boka mbaka bɛyɛ 30 mɔ bakaa nwo asɛm wo Baebolo ne anu le ko. Ɔboka mbaka mɔ bɛfefɛ ndɛndɛ wo awole berɛ n'akyi le nwo. Ɔtɔ bɛsese fɛfɛɛfɛ anzɛɛ fufule ne, bɛtumi behu ye wo January awieleɛ pɛɛ. Ɛhye ti, Hebri duma mɔ bɛfɛ almond baka ne kyerɛ “deɛ ɔte nwo.” Asɛdie bie wo baka ne ne Ye Bɔfo ne ntam a?

Yeremiya 7:25 aaseɛ asɛm kile kɛɛ Yehowa kilehile ɔ ti kɛ ɔ sɔre ndwankye. March 15, 2011, The Watchtower nhoma ne ka kɛ: “Kɛ mɔ almond waka ne ‘nyane’ ndwankye no, sɔ koro no ala Yehowa nso ninyɛne kile kɛ ɔ ‘sɔre ndwankye’ . . . kɛ ɔrebɔ ye manfo kɔkɔ wɔ asoɔden a bɛbaye no sunsum mu. . . . Na ɔnloma ɔ lale—ɔbaye ‘nyane’—kɔpem kɛ ye nkɔmhyɛ asɛm ne ba mu.”—Yer. 1:11, 12.

Almond dua ne fofolɛ fufuo ne so bɛfa kyerɛ kokoloo ti fufuo wɔ Baabolo ne mu. (Eccl. 12:5) Na kɛ mɔ dua ne fofolɛ ne wo fe kpalɛ ti no, bɛfale ye bɛyɛle nsan-kuru mɔ ɛwɔ asua tɛntɛn ne mu kande dua ne nsalɛ ne so no.—Ex. 37:17-20.

Nwoma ye, bɛntɔn. Ɔboka Baebolo nkyrekyre dwuma mɔɔ bɛyɛ wɔ ewiase nyinaa na bɛfa akyɛdeɛ mɔɔ bɛfi bɛ pɛ mu bɛyɛ bɛsua akyi la ho. Sɛ ɛpɛ kɛ ɛyɛ akyɛdeɛ a, yɛsrɛ wɔ kɔ donate.jw.org.
