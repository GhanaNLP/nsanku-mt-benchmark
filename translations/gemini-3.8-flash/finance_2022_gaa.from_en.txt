HIƐBIMƆ

COVID-19 hela kpatamɔ lɛ he jaramɔ ninaa lɛ ba shikpɔji fɛɛ nɔ hejɔmɔ hewalɛi anɔ, ni Ghana hu fata he. Haomɔ ni Ghanabii kɛkpe yɛ gbolɛ, shihilɛ kɛ nitsumɔi ni laaje lɛ nɔ lɛ, Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ le enɛ jogbaŋŋ. Miishe mihe akɛ manɛɛ akɛ nɔ ni wɔfeɛ kɛtee hela kpatamɔ lɛ he jaramɔ ninaa nɛɛ he lɛ ji nɔ ko ni ka shi faŋŋ kɛ ekpakpa ni jeŋ fɛɛ jie eyi. Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ je mla kɛ gbɛjianɔtooi ashishi hu kɛtsɔɔ koni akɛye abua nitsumɔi koni amɛhiekoŋŋ, ni wɔ shihilɛ aba enɔ daa gbi gbɛjianɔ lɛ mli ekonn.

Kɛfata he lɛ, kɛha gbeyeishemɔi kɛ hegbɛi ni je kpo kɛjɛ helagbɛgbɛlɛ nɛɛ mli lɛ ahetoo lɛ, Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ, yɛ H.E. Maŋtsɛ Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo hiɛnyiɛmɔ kpakpa lɛ shishi lɛ, fee GH¢100 billion COVID-19 Alleviation and Revitalisation of Enterprises gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ oyayaaka, (GhanaCARES “Obaatan Pa” gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ). Enɛ eha wɔ maŋbii lɛ ena hejɔlɛ amrɔ nɔŋŋ ni yɔɔ maŋtsɛyeli nɔ kɛteke nɔ, ni eemiha wɔ maŋ lɛ mihe ewalɛ ekoŋŋ.

Wɔhiɛ sɔɔ bɔ ni nyɛyaa nɔ nyɛkɛ nyɛhe fɔɔ Nɔyeli lɛ nɔ beni wɔtsuɔ nii koni wɔje hela kpatamɔ nɛɛ mli kɛ hewalɛ lɛ. Yɛ be nɛɛ mli lɛ, wɔtsui kɛ hewalɛ ni wɔkɛbaahe wɔwala akɛ maŋ lɛ ena kaamɔ. Biblia lɛ haa wɔ gbɛtsɔɔmɔ ni sa be fɛɛ be yɛ be ni tamɔ nɛkɛ mli. Titri lɛ, tamɔ bɔ ni aŋma yɛ Yesaia 9:10 lɛ, “Abloku lɛ efite, shi wɔkɛ tɛi ni atsaa baama; ayikpitso tsei lɛ atutso, shi wɔkɛ sida tsei baato najiaŋ”. Nakai nɔŋŋ Nɔyeli lɛ hu etswa emli shi akɛ ebaaye shiwoo ni ekɛha lɛ nɔ. Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, wɔbaaya nɔ wɔtsu mlai kɛ gbɛjianɔtoi ni kɛ wɔ baaya nɔ ni wɔwo nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni eŋmɛɛ wɔ lɛ he najiaŋ kɛha maŋbii lɛ ahewalɛ kɛ shihilɛ nɔyaa he nii.

2022 Sikayeli he Gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ, kɛ amrɔ nɛɛ kɛya afii etɛ (3) ni baa lɛ ateŋ oti titri kome ji, ni ama Nitsumɔi Atsɛmɔ Maŋ ni baahi shi daa ni nyɛɔ ebaa shikaŋ hewalɛyaa ni wa, ni hiɔ shi daa, kɛ nɔ ni he fɛɛ gbɔmɔ kpaa mli kɛbaa kɛto nitsumɔ kpakpa amɛhã mɔ fɛɛ mɔ, titri lɛ Oblahii kɛ Oblayei lɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, nɔyeli lɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔi lɛ baatsɔ gbɛ kɛye amɛbua oblahii kɛ oblayei lɛ koni amɛkɛ amɛnitsumɔ he nilee atsu nii ni amɛkpoo hewalɛ ni yɔɔ amɛmli lɛ kɛba gbɔmɛi fɛɛ anɔkwale shidaamɔ ni tsɔɔ hegbɛ ni ja ni akɛhã mɔ fɛɛ mɔ nɔ.

Kɛkɛ ni akɛwo oblahii kɛ oblayei anɔ hewalɛ ni ama amɛ nitsumɔjeŋ mumɔ nɔ lɛ, Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ baaje YouStart gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ shishi yɛ afi 2022 mli koni akɛha oblahii kɛ oblayei ni feɔ nitsumɔ lɛ aná nyɔmɔwoo koni amɛkɛto loo amɛkɛha amɛnitsumɔ lɛ amli agbɔ.

Yɛ afi 2022 Shikaŋtoo gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ mli lɛ, wɔtsɔɔ gbɛjianɔtoi ni akɛbaaya nɔ atsu hegbɛ nɔŋŋ naagboi ni shikpalɛ lɛ kɛkpeɔ lɛ he nii, kɛ bɔ ni wɔbaafee wɔwa fe tsɔ yɛ hela kpatrooi be lɛ sɛɛ. Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ ewo ehe nɔ hewalɛ kɛha gbɛjianɔtoi ni kɔɔ nii pii ahe ni abua naa kɛtsu nii koni akɛha hewalɛ kɛ hesaa kɛteke nɔ awo nitsumɔ he jwɛŋmɔ mli yɛ oblahii kɛ oblayei ateŋ. 3. Yisɔ̃ɔ nibii ni kɔɔ bɔ ni heatalɛ lɛ nyie eha yɛ afi 2021 mli lɛ ji nɛkɛ:

• Real GDP fɛɛ yɛ mli kɛfata he lɛ fee 3.5 percent yɛ 2021 gbii nyɔŋmai etɛ kɛ enumɔ klɛŋklɛŋ lɛ mli, yɛ be ni efee 0.7 percent yɛ nakai beiaŋ nɔŋŋ yɛ 2020 mli;

• Mu-heko Real GDP lɛ da kɛyashi afii 2021 klɛŋklɛŋ fã lɛ mli kɛyashi 4.8 percent kɛkɛwɔ aŋkro 1.1 percent yɛ nakai beiaŋ nɔŋŋ yɛ 2020 mli;

• Níi anɔkwa jara nɔtee yɛ September naagbee lɛ ji 10.6 percent, kɛ́ akɛto 10.4 percent yɛ nakai beiaŋ nɔŋŋ yɛ 2020 lɛ he;

• Sikayeli gbɛjianɔtoo nɔ gbee fɛɛ (shika krɔŋŋ) nɛji GDP mli 7.7 yɛ yitsoŋtoo nɛji GDP mli 7.4 lɛ hewɔ;

• Primary balance lɛ ŋmɛɛ shikpɔŋ nɔ kɛteŋ mli kɛba shi 2.0 percent yɛ GDP mli, kɛ akɛto oti ni akɛma hiɛ akɛ ebaaje kpo ni ji 1.7 percent lɛ he;

• Maŋ nyɔmɔ (kɛji akwɛ yɛ GDP mli) yashi 77.7 percent yɛ September 2021 naagbee, ni efa kɛjɛ 76.1 percent yɛ December 2020 naagbee;

• Jeŋmaji ateŋ Shika Tɔ̃ɔ̃mɔ nɔ ko kwraa yɛ August 2021 naagbee lɛ ji US$11.44 billion, ni fa kɛha nitsumɔ nyɔji 5.2 nibii kɛbaŋ lɛ he, kɛ́ akɛto US$8.62 billion ni tamɔ nitsumɔ nyɔji 4.1 nibii kɛbaŋ lɛ he ni aŋma yɛ December 2020 naagbee lɛ he; kɛ

• Shika tsakemɔ he jara lɛ mɔ shi kpoŋŋ yɛ jeŋmaji anɔkwa shika bibii wuji lɛ fɛɛ ateŋ yɛ afi 2021 gbɛfaŋ gbii etɛ lɛ mli. Nɔ ni ja titri lɛ, Ghana cedi lɛ he walo lɛ ba shi kɛyashɛ 1.7 percent yɛ US Dollar lɛ he kɛyashi September 2021 naagbee, kɛ́ akɛto 2.9 percent shiba he yɛ nɛkɛ be nɔŋŋ mli yɛ afi 2020 mli.

FREE SHS: Free SHS (Free Senior High School) mlaa nɛɛ ji Ghana Nɔyeli lɛ gbɛjianɔtoo ni Maŋtsɛ Nana Akuffo-Addo nɔyeli lɛ kɛba yɛ September 2017 mli ni ehaa JHS nikaselo fɛɛ nikaselo ni esa kɛha SHS nikasemɔ lɛ náa hegbɛ kɛyaa efolo.

Grants: Enɛmɛi ji shika yelikɛbuamɔ henɔi ni hefatalɔi kɛha hewalɛnamɔ kɛhãa Amralo lɛ, ni eshɛɛɛ akɛ Amralo lɛ woɔ he nyɔmɔ ekoŋŋ.

Yelikɛbuamɔ nitsumɔhei krokomɛi ni yɔɔ nɔyeli lɛ shishi: Enɛɛmɛi ji nyɔmɔwoo ni mla fã akɛ afee tamɔ District Assemblies’ Common Fund (DACF), Road Fund, Ghana Education Trust Fund (GETFUND), Petroleum Related Fund kɛ National Health Fund.

Gross Domestic Product (GDP): Enɛ tsɔɔ nibii kɛ sɔɔmɔ nibii fɛɛ ni afee yɛ maŋ lɛ mli yɛ be pɔtɛɛ ko mli lɛ ajara fɛɛ nɔbɔ.

Gross International Reserves: Enɛ kɛɔ hewalɛ ni maŋ ko yɔɔ yɛ maŋsɛɛ shikpɔji anɔ shika mli kɛheɔ nibii kɛ sɔɔmɔ nitsumɔi kɛjɛɔ maŋsɛɛ shikpɔji anɔ yɛ be pɔtɛɛ ko mli; ewoɔ maŋ lɛ diɛŋtsɛ shika lɛ hewalɛ hu yɛ heshikpamɔ mli kɛmaŋsɛɛ shikpɔji krokomɛi ashika lɛ.

Inflation: Nibii kɛ nitsumɔi anaa jaramɔ mli hewalɛnamɔ yɛ be pɔtɛɛ ko mli.

IPEP (Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme): Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme (IPEP) lẹ ji NPP Aban lẹ gbɛjianɔtooi wuji lẹ ateŋ ekome ni kɛ etsui ewo ohia shwiemɔ kɛ emli nibii amli gbalamɔ fioo tsɔɔmɔ mli, titri lɛ yɛ kɔnjiakpoji kɛ hei ni heloonaa nibii bɛ lẹ kɛtsɔ heloonaa nibii titrii ni aatsɔ nɔ afee nibii ahanmɔ nɔ.

Macroeconomic targets: Enɛmɛi ji susumɔ nibii ni akɛkwɛɔ bɔ ni hegbɛ kɛ shika he nitsumɔi yaa nɔ eha yɛ maŋ lɛ mli. Nɔkwɛmɔnɔi ekomɛi ji shika kɛ hegbɛ he nitsumɔi ashwere, nibii ayitso ni teɔ shi, tsakemɔi ni baa nitsumɔi anaa hegbɛ mli, maŋtsɛ yeli lɛ kɛ maji krokomɛi anitsumɔ yɛ jara nɔ, nɔ ni maŋ nɔyelɔi lɛ kɛba ni eye omanye aloo efe gbagba yɛ shika he mla kɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔi amli kɛ Bank of Ghana yiŋkpɔi ni kɔɔ shika ni aheɔ kɛ nɔ ni ajeɔ kpo yɛ maŋ lɛ hegbɛ he nitsumɔi amli.

37 | Ghana Maŋ Nɔyeli - 2022 Maŋbii A-Budget

Nitsumɔi Atsɛmɛi Amaŋ Ni Hiɛ Kpɛ̃ɛ Kɛyaa Nɔ Maamɔ: Shika Nyiemɔ Nɔ Mumɔ Kɛ Nitsumɔi Ahegbɛi Ajelemɔ

MSME (Micro And Small Buisness Developmet Programme): Be pii lɛ aheɔ akɛ micro-enterprise (loo microenterprise) ji nitsumɔ bibioo ko ni mɛi fioo ko pɛ tsuɔ nii yɛ mli, ni shika babaoo loo hewalɛ ni yɔɔ mli lɛ shɛɛɛ he ko ni awo tɔɔ.

NABCo (Nation Builders Corps): ni aleɔ lɛ hu akɛ NABCO ji nitsumɔ ko ni Ghana Amralo lɛ kɛba koni ekɛha yunivɛsiti skulbii ni egbe skul naa ni bɛ nitsumɔ lɛ nitsumɔ kɛtsɔ hefatalɔi kɛ maŋ nitsumɔheibii lɛ anɔ kɛtsu hefatalɔi kɛ maŋ naagbai srɔtoi ahe nii ni ekɛwo amɛ nyɛmɔ mli hewalɛ kɛwo maŋ lɛ shika he shwelɔi ahewalɛ mli.

NBSSI (National Board For Small And Medium Scale Industry): National Board for Small Scale Industries (NBSSI) ji maŋgbinaa nitsumɔhe ni heee yitso kɛha sɛɛnamɔ ni yɔɔ Ministry of Trade and Industry shishi, ni awo amɛ gbɛfaŋnɔ kɛha hewalɛwo kɛ shwelɛ kɛha Nitsumɔ Bibii kɛ Nitsumɔ Bibii Tsɔji (MSEs).

Non-Oil Real GDP: Ji nibii fɛɛ ni afee yɛ shikaŋsɛɛ nitsumɔ lɛ mli ni nu he nitsumɔi lɛ bɛ mli, ni abuɔ nɔ ni haa nibii awaji yaa nɔ lɛ hu awoɔ mli.

Toojikpɔŋ Ni Jeee Toi: Maŋ nɔyeli shika ni jeee tooi anɔ kwɛmɔ nɔ ajɛɔ anaa, nɔkwɛmɔnii ji hewalɛwoo woji loo laasisi woji ahe nyɔmɔwoo, nitsumɔ he nyɔmɔwoo kɛ hefatalɔi krokomɛi ahe nyɔmɔwoo.

Nyɛmɔ Ni Kã Nɔ: Nyɛmɔ ni kã nɔ lɛ biɔ MDA kitai ni akɛ Ministry of Finance kã shi kɛ agbɛnɛ nyɛmɔ ni kã shi yɛ afi 2016 mli ni awo Statutory Funds lɛ.

Primary balance : Enɛ ji srɔto ni yɔɔ Maŋ nɔyeli lɛ shika ni ebuaa naa kɛ e-gbɛjianɔtoo ni jeee seshitemɔ shika he nyɔmɔwoo (kɛ nyɔmɔwoo he gbɛjianɔtoi lɛ ehaaa mli) lɛ teŋ. Abaanyɛ akɛ enɛ ato Gross Domestic Product (GDP) lɛ shika mli faa ji nɔŋŋ lɛ he.

Nɔ ni akpaa gbɛ akɛ ebaawa: Bɔ ni Ghana shika he nitsumɔ lɛ baawa eha yɛ afi kome mli.

Shikashika: Enɛ ji shika fɛɛ nɔ́ ni Aban náa kɛ́jeŋ maŋ lɛ mli kɛ maŋsɛɛ fɛɛ kɛhã enitsumɔi lɛ.

RFJ (Rearing For Food and Jobs): baawo koolooilɛ ahe nitsumɔ ni he wa ni nitsumɔ mli kpakpa yɔɔ, nɔ ni baaha maŋ mli koolooilɛ nitsumɔ anɔ afã, etsee koolooilɛ anibii anɔ ni ajieɔ kɛbaa lɛ nɔ, efee nɔ ko kɛha nitsumɔi ahegbɛi afee, ni ewo koolooilɛ nitsumɔ mli gbɛtsɔɔmɔ nitsulɔi lɛ ashihilɛ nɔ”.

38 | Ghana Nɔyeli - 2022 Maŋbii Ashikashitsɔɔmɔ

Nitsumɔi Atsɛmɛi Amaŋ Ni Hiɛ Kpɛ̃ɛ Kɛyaa Nɔ Maamɔ: Shika Nyiemɔ Nɔ Mumɔ Kɛ Nitsumɔi Ahegbɛi Ajelemɔ

Gbɛjianɔtoo Kɛha Maŋbii: Aban yeɔ bua kɛhaa la kɛ nu he nitsumɔhei lɛ kɛ fɔfɔi nibii ahe nitsumɔhei lɛ yɛ maŋbii lɛ agbɛfaŋ.

Subsidy: Nɛkɛ ji shika he yelikɛbuamɔ ni amralo lɛ kɛhaa maŋbii lɛ koni ekɛba nitsulɔi lɛ anɔjatsuu lɛ shi.

Kwɛmɔ nɔ jogbaŋŋ: Nɔ ko mli ni akwɛɔ kɛ jwɛŋmɔ kɛ hiŋmɛi ni ekã shi pɛpɛɛpɛ.

Hetoo-kɛ-fee nɔ̃: Enɛ ji be ni Amralo lɛ kɛ amaniɛbɔɔ ni fa ni sa be fãa kɛhaa e-manbii lɛ yɛ nɔ ni efeɔ lɛ hewɔ.

Nibii kɛ Sɔɔmɔi anitsumɔ: Shika gbɛdzee nɔ ni Aban tsuɔ kɛtsuɔ enitsumɔi ahe nii kɛ kɛhãa maŋbii lɛ sɔɔmɔi.

Value Added Tax (VAT): Tooi ni awoɔ yɛ be mli ni anaa nɔkɔ yɛ nibii kɛ sɔɔmɔ nibii ahe yɛ nitsumɔ lɛ he fɛɛ he.
