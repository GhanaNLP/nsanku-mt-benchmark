5 | Ghana Aban - 2022 Maŋbii Ashikashishitoo Akɔntaabuu

Maŋ ni hiɛ kãa shi ni woɔ bisinɛsi nitsumɔi ahe hewalɛ mamɔɔ: Shikashishitoo mli hewalɛwoo kɛ Nitsumɔi ahegbɛi afee

HIƐWƆƆ HE WIEMƆ

COVID-19 hela gbekɛ kpatolɔ lɛ hewalɛ kpii lɛ eba hewalɛnamɔ hei fɛɛ anɔ, ni Ghana hu fata he. Haomɔ ni Ghanabii kɛkpe yɛ susumaŋ, shihilɛ kɛ dɔkɔdɔkɔ-nii ahe ŋmɛɛmɔ mli lɛ, nɔyeli lɛ le he nɔ ko pɔtɛɛ. Miihe miishɛɛ akɛ mafee gbɛi kɛtsɔ nɔ ni wɔfee kɛkpe hela gbeyeigbeyei nɛɛ hiɛ lɛ nɔ, eji ekãa kɛ mwɛɛmɔ nɔ ni eje kpo, ni jeŋ fɛɛ ekpɛlɛ nɔ. Nɔyeli lɛ hu to gbɛjianɔi kɛtsɔ mlaa kɛ shishitooi anɔ koni ekɛwaje bisinɛsi nitsumɔi ekoŋŋ ni ekɛ wɔ shihilɛ aba he ni ehi ekoŋŋ tamɔ bɔ ni eji tsutsu lɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ.

Kɛfata he lɛ, kɛha gbeyeishemɔi kɛ hegbɛi ni jɛ hela kpekpe lɛ mli ba lɛ ahetoo lɛ, Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ, yɛ H.E. Maŋtsɛ Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo nilee kɛ hewalɛ nɔyeli lɛ shishi lɛ, fee GH¢100 billion COVID-19 Alleviation and Revitalisation of Enterprises gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ oyayaai, (GhanaCARES “Obaatan Pa” gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ). Enɛ eha wɔwebii lɛ aná hejɔlɛ kpele amrɔ nɔŋŋ, ni eetsɔɔ wɔmaŋ lɛ mli hewalɛ ekoŋŋ.

Wɔhiɛ sɔɔ bɔ ni nyɛyaa nɔ nyɛkɛ nyɛhe fɔɔ Aban nɔ he, yɛ be mli ni wɔtsuɔ nii koni wɔte shi kɛjɛ hela kpatrooi nɛɛ mli hewalɛnamɔ mli. Yɛ be nɛɛ mli lɛ, wɔtsui-shi-kamɔ akɛ maŋ koni wɔhi shi lɛ ena kaa. Biblia lɛ haa gbɛtsɔɔmɔ ni sa be fɛɛ be yɛ bei tamɔ nɛkɛ mli. Titri lɛ, bɔ ni aŋma yɛ Yesaya 9:10 mli lɛ, “Amlalo lɛ egboi, shi wɔkɛ tɛi ni asaa amɛ he lɛ baama; kpakpa tsei lɛ akpɔ amɛ afɔ, shi wɔkɛ sida tsei baato najiaŋ”. Nakai nɔŋŋ lɛ, Aban hu efwa shi kɛmɔ shi akɛ ebaafee nɔ ni ewo shi yɛ he lɛ. Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, wɔbaaya nɔ wɔtsu gbɛjianɔtoi kɛ nifeemɔi ni kɛ shidaamɔ baato najiaŋ kɛha he fɛɛ he ni laaje lɛ, ni wɔha maŋbii lɛ ashihilɛ afee nɔ ni hi jogbaŋŋ.

Nɔ ni he hiaa fe fɛɛ yɛ 2022 Sikatsɛ̃ɛ gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ mli, kɛ anɔkwa yɛ afii etɛ (3) ni baa lɛ mli ji, ni ama Nitsumɔi Ashihilɛ ni baahi shi daa ni baanyɛ eha shika nitsumɔ lɛ ashwere kɛ hewalɛ, ni ahi shi daa, ni ekɔ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ he koni eha nitsumɔ kpakpa aba kɛha mɔ fɛɛ mɔ, titri lɛ Oblahii kɛ Gbekɛbii yei. No hewɔ lɛ, nɔyeli lɛ mlaai kɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔi lɛ baakwɛ gbɛ ni akɛbaaye abua oblahii kɛ gbekɛbii yei lɛ koni amɛkɛ amɛhenɔnikaselɔi atsu nii ni amɛjie amɛhewalɛ kpo koni anaa shwere kɛtsɔ hegbɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ ni aabaaha mɔ fɛɛ mɔ nɔ.

Kɛkoba oblahii kɛ oblayei anɔkwahemɔ kɛ bɔni afee ni awo amɛ jwetseyeli musuŋ tsuiŋ hewalɛ lɛ, Aban baaje YouStart gbalamɔ nitsumɔ nɔ yɛ 2022 mli kɛha jwetsebiiaŋ oblahii kɛ oblayei nyɛmɔ kɛtsɔ nyɔmɔwoo he koni amɛto amɛjwetseyeli nitsumɔ shishi aloo amɛha egbɛ eshwie he.

Yɛ afi 2022 Shika Tootɔ̃ɔ lɛ mli lɛ, wɔtsɔɔ gbɛjianɔtoi ni wɔto koni wɔya nɔ wɔtsu shika he naagbee nitsumɔi ni yɔɔ amrɔ nɛɛ ahe nii kɛ nyɛmɔ, koni wɔte shi wɔwa babaoo yɛ hela babaoo lɛ sɛɛ gbii lɛ amli. Nɔyeli lɛ kɛ ehiɛ eba kpaakpa yɛ gbɛjianɔtoi babaoo ni afee yɛ kpeemɔ mli lɛ ahe koni akɛha nyɛmɔi kɛ wo gbalashihilɛ he gbɛjianɔtoo he hewalɛ yɛ oblahii kɛ oblayei lɛ ateŋ. Be ni wɔtiuɔ Maŋtsɛ lɛ

• Jeŋmaji ateŋ Shika Toi Fɛɛ yɛ August 2021 naagbee lɛ ji US$11.44 billion, ni efa kɛha nibii ni akɛbaajeŋ nyɔji 5.2, kɛ akɛto US$8.62 billion he ni tamɔ nibii ni akɛbaajeŋ nyɔji 4.1 ni aŋma yɛ December 2020 naagbee lɛ; kɛ

Gross International Reserves: Enɛ kɛɔ hewalɛ ni maŋ ko maŋsɛɛ shika yɔɔ kɛheɔ nibii kɛ sɔɔmɔ nibii kɛjɛɔ maji krokomɛi amli yɛ be ko mli; efiɔ hewalɛ ni maŋ lɛ diɛŋtsɛ shika yɔɔ kɛkpeɔ maji krokomɛi ashika lɛ sɛɛ hu.

Inflation: Níbii kɛ nitsumɔi ajaramɔ nɔkwɔlɔmɔ yɛ be pɔtɛɛ ko mli.

IPEP (Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme): Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme (IPEP) lɛ ji NPP Amralo lɛ gbɛjianɔtooi kpekpeei lɛ ateŋ ekome ni kɛ etsui eto ohia shishitswere kɛ hegbɛ ni kãaa gbɛjianɔ he mligbee he, titri lɛ yɛ akrowai kɛ maŋbii ni ahiɛ ebɔ kɔŋ lɛ amli kɛtsɔ nibii ahe hiaani kɛha shihilɛ nɔ hewalɛwoo nɔ.

Macroeconomic targets: Enɛmɛi ji susumɔi ni akɛtsuɔ nii kɛkwɛɔ bɔ ni heloonaa nibii ahe sikayeli lɛ yaa ehiɛ ehaa. Nɔkwɛmɔnɔi lɛ ekomɛi ji sikayeli lɛ mli shwereyeli, nibii anai ayiteŋkɛlɛ, tsakemɔi ni baa yɛ nitsumɔi anaamɔ he gbɛfaŋ, heloonaa nibii ajaramɔ kɛ maji krokomɛi ateŋ nitsumɔ, nɔ ni baanyɛ afee kunimyeli aloo yitiripɔɔ kɛha nɔyeli lɛ heloonaa nibii ahe sikayeli mlilaai, kɛ yiŋkpɔi ni Bank of Ghana feɔ ni sa shika taoomɔ kɛ egbɛɛmɔ he yɛ heloonaa nibii asikayeli lɛ mli.

37 | Ghana Aban - 2022 Maŋbii Lɛ Abɔdgyɛti

Maŋ ni hiɛ kãa shi ni woɔ bisinɛsi nitsumɔi ahe hewalɛ mamɔɔ: Shikashishitoo mli hewalɛwoo kɛ Nitsumɔi ahegbɛi afee

MSME (Micro And Small Buisness Developmet Programme): Micro-enterprise (aloo microenterprise) ji nitsumɔ bibioo ko ni kɛ gbɛi kpanŋ nɔ lɛ, etsuɔ mɛi fioo pɛ kɛji abua naa, ni enitsumɔ he akɔntaabuu aloo shika ninaa feee yitso kɛyashi shika fɛɛ shika yii nɔ ko nɔ

NABCo (Nation Builders Corps): ni atsɛɔɔ lɛ hu NABCO lɛ ji nitsumɔ ko ni Ghana Aban lɛ kɛba koni akɛha yunivɛsiti skulbii ni egbe naa ni bɛ nitsumɔ lɛ nitsumɔ kɛtsɔ hefatalɔi kɛ maŋ nitsumɔheji lɛ anɔ kɛtsa maŋ lɛ he naagboi babaoo ahe nibii, kɛ agbɛnɛ hu koni akɛma amɛ hewalɛ kɛ nibii amli kɛha maŋ lɛ he shika he gbɛfaŋ shwere-mɔ.

NBSSI (National Board For Small And Medium Scale Industry): National Board for Small Scale Industries (NBSSI) ji maŋ gbɛjianɔtoo ni eto-oo sɛɛnamɔ ko kɛhã he pɔtɛɛ ko ní yɔɔ Ministry of Trade and Industry shishi, ni awo amɛ dɛŋ hewalɛ ni amɛwo Micro kɛ Small Enterprises (MSEs) ashwere kɛ nɔya he hewalɛ.

Non-Oil Real GDP: Ji nibii fɛɛ nɛɛ kɛtsɔ hewalɛnamɔ mli kɛba ni kɛ fɔ nu he nitsumɔi lɛ ewooo mli, ni abuɔ shika hewalɛ kpatamɔ lɛ hu he akɔnta.

Non Tax Revenue: Nɔ̃ ni abuaa naa kɛhãa Aban ni jeee too ni agbaa kɛjeɔ he, nɔkwɛmɔnɔ ji hewalɛwoo wolo aloo laisɛns hetsoo nyɔmɔwoo, nɔ ni mɔ ko kɛ nɔ ko tsuɔ nii ni ewoɔ he nyɔmɔ, kɛ nyɔmɔwoo krokomɛi.

Nyɔmɔi Ni Eshwɛ: Nyɔmɔi ni eshwɛ nɛɛ kɔɔ MDA shiwoo kɛha Ministry of Finance lɛ kɛ agbɛnɛ nyɔmɔi ni eshwɛ yɛ afi 2016 ni akɛbaaha Statutory Funds lɛ ahe.

Klɛŋklɛŋ hefatalɛ : Enɛ ji srɔtofeemɔ ni yɔɔ Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ shika ni baa kɛ efĩe ni jeee dɔ̃ɔ shika (kɛjeee nyɔmɔwoo Gbɛjianɔtoo nɔ). Abaanyɛ afo enɛ kɛtsɔ Gross Domestic Product (GDP) lɛ faŋtsɔ nɔ.

Shidaamɔ Ni Ahia Kɛha Nɔyaa: Bɔ ni Ghana shika he nibii lɛ baawa eha yɛ afi kome mli.

Shika-nitsumɔ: Enɛ ji shika fɛɛ nɔ́ ni Aban náa kɛ́hã e-nitsumɔi lɛ kɛjɛɔ maŋ lɛ mli kɛ maŋsɛɛ fɛɛ.

RFJ (Rearing For Food and Jobs): baatsu koolooilɛ ajiemɔ nitsumɔ ni he wa ni nilee yɔɔ mli, ni baaha maŋ mli nibii ahe nitsumɔ ahiɛ aya, ebaagbe koolooilɛ ahe nibii ni akɛjɛɔ maŋse kɛbaa lɛ anɔ, ebaaye ebua kɛto nitsumɔi ahe gbɛjianɔ, ni ebaaha mɛi ni yɔɔ koolooilɛ anitsumɔ lɛ mli lɛ ashwilɛ ahi”.
