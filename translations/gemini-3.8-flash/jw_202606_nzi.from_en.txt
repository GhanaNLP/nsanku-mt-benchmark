MA W’ADWENLE YƐ KƐNKƐN Yɛbahola yɛali nɔhalɛ yɛamaa Gyihova ɔnva nɔhalɛlilɛ sɔtlɔngɔ biala mɔɔ yɛbayia la anwo.

"Awulae, nwane ɛkɛ a yɛbahɔ a? Ɛlɛ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ maa awozonle ngoane."-DWƆN 6:68.

"Edwɛkɛ ɛhye yɛ ɛzulolɛ; nwane a bahola adie a?" (Dwɔn 6:60) Zɔhane a Gyisɛse ɛdoavolɛma dɔɔnwo dwenlenle ninyɛne mɔɔ Gyisɛse hanle ye mekɛ mɔɔ ɔlɛkilehile wɔ Kapɛɛneyum la anwo a. Gyisɛse hanle kɛ: "Saa amumunamule amunli sonla Ralɛ ne nwonane na amunno ye mogya a, amunle ngoane wɔ amun nu." Eza ɔdoale zo kɛ: "Awie biala mɔɔ di me nwonane na ɔno me mogya la lɛ dahuu ngoane, na mebadwazo ye wɔ kenlenza ne anu."-Dwɔn 6:53, 54.

2 Menli mɔɔ Gyisɛse edwɛkɛ ne hanle bɛ kpole la anli ɔ nzi bieko. Asoo ɛnee bɛnlɛ ye nu diedi fee ɔ? Nɔhalɛ nu, kolaa na ɛhye ara la, bie mɔ—mɔɔ Gyisɛse ɛdoavolɛma boka nwo la—anyelielie mɔɔ Gyisɛse yɛle la anwo kpole kpalɛ yemɔti bɛhanle kɛ: “Nɔhalɛ nu, ɛhye a le Ngapezonyi ne mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ ɔba ewiade la.” (Dwɔn 6:14) Ɔti ɔda ali kɛ ɛnee diedi ekyi wɔ ɛdoavolɛma zɔhane mɔ asa nu wɔ Gyisɛse anu. Noko mɔɔ bɛdiedi ne yiale sɔnea la, bɛanli nɔhalɛ bɛammaa ye.

3 Ɛzoanvolɛ ne mɔ liele Gyisɛse edwɛkɛ ne dole nu wɔ adenle ngakyile zo. Mɔɔ Gyisɛse bizale bɛ saa bɛkulo kɛ bɛgyakyi ye bɛkɔ noko a, Pita buale kɛ: “Awurade, nwane ɛkɛ a yɛkɔ a?

1-2. Mɔɔ Gyisɛse kpale ngilehilelɛ wɔ Kapɛenaom la, duzu a sisile a?

Ɛlɛ duma mɔɔ maa awoherelielɛ mɔɔ enli awieleɛ la. Yɛlie yɛli yɛɛ yɛnwu ye kɛ ɛle Nyamenle Sonla Nwuanzanwuanza ne.” (Dwɔn 6:68, 69) Yɛ nzonlɛ kɛ Pita amba kɛ ɔdaye nee ɛzoanvolɛma mɔɔ ɛha la dele mɔɔ Gyisɛse hanle kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛdi ye nwonane na bɛno ye mogya la abo kpalɛ. Ɔnva nwo mɔɔ bɛdele ɔ bo wɔ mekɛ zɔhane la, bɛkpɔle kɛ bɛbali nɔhalɛ bɛamaa Gyisɛse. Kɛmɔti ɔ? Ɔluakɛ na bɛ diedi ne gyi nɔhalɛ mɔɔ bɛnrɛhola bɛnrɛkpo zo—Gyisɛse a le “Nyamenle Kelaese ne.”​—Luku 9:20, 35.

⁴ Yɛ nɔhalɛlilɛ kɛhɔ Gyihova ɛkɛ ne la bahola ayɛ sɔnea wɔ adenle ngakyile zo. Kɛ neazo la, kɛzi yɛyɛ yɛ nyɛleɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛte kɛ bɛhakyi Baebolo ngɛlɛlera bie ase mɔɔ bɛkile la anzɛɛ kɛzi bɛhyehyɛ ɛdoavolɛ-yɛlɛ gyima ne la ɛ? Anzɛɛ kɛzi yɛyɛ yɛ nyɛleɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɔyɛ se kɛ yɛbade deɛmɔti Gyihova maa yɛkɔ amaneɛnwunlɛ bie anu la abo ɛ? Yɛ diedi ne noko bahola ayɛ sɔnea saa Kilisienenle agɔnwolɛ kpalɛ bie gyegye yɛ anzɛɛ saa awie mɔɔ yɛbu ye la yɛ yɛ nyɛleɛ mɔɔ zɔho kɛ ɛnzɔ la.

⁵ Amaa yɛanwu kɛzi ɔhyia kɛ yɛnyia gyima fɔlɔ kpalɛ wɔ yɛ diedi nwo la, dwenle sua mɔɔ bɛzile ye wɔ ɛleka mɔɔ aze kpusu la anwo. Amaa sua zɔhane agyinla kpundii la, ɔhyia fɔlɔ kpalɛ. Zɔhane ala a, amaa yɛagyinla yɛ diedi sɔnea mɔɔ bɛha nwolɛ edwɛkɛ wɔ ɛdendɛkpunli mɔɔ li ɛke anwo debie la anloa la, yɛ diedi hyia fɔlɔ mɔɔ anu mia la. Saa yɛkɔ zo yɛmaa yɛ diedi fɔlɔ ne anu mia a, yɛbahɔ zo yɛagyinla kpundii, yɛayɛ Gyihova azonvolɛ nɔhavo mɔɔ sɔnea ba a yɛnli aze.​—1 Kɔl. 15:58; kenga Kɔlɔsaebinli 1:23.

di nɔhalɛ maa Gyisɛse wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ menli dɔɔnwo gyakyile ye la? Ɛzoanvolɛ ne mɔ nyianle anwodozo kɛ Gyihova fɛta ɛzonlenlɛ kɛmɔ ɔle ninyɛne kɔsɔɔti Bɔvolɛ la ati; kɛ Gyisɛse a le Nyamenle Ara ne mɔɔ lɛ “dahuwa ngoane edwɛkɛ” la; na Gyihova di Gyisɛse anzi bɔkɔɔ. Kɛ anrɛɛ yɛbazuzu adenle dɔɔnwo fɔɔnwo mɔɔ bɛbahola bɛalua zo bɛasɔ yɛ diedi bɛanlea la anwo kɛkala la, bɛmaa yɛva yɛ adwenle yɛzie nɔhalɛ edwɛkɛ nsa mɔɔ bahola aboa yɛ amaa yɛagyina kpundii bɔbɔ wɔ sɔtlɛ biala mɔɔ yɛbayia la anu la azo. Ɛhye mɔ a le: (1) Gyihova a le ninyɛne kɔsɔɔti Bɔvolɛ a. (2) Gyihova a maanle bɛhɛlɛle Baebolo ne a. (3) Gyihova ɛlɛdi ye menli ne anzi ɛnɛ. Saa yɛdie nɔhalɛ edwɛkɛ nsa ɛhye mɔ yɛto nu bɔkɔɔ a, yɛbadoa zo yɛali nɔhalɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛyia diedi nwo sɔtlɛ la.

7 Kenga Yekile 4:11. Ngɛlɛlera ɛhye kakye yɛ kɛ Gyihova a bɔle ninyɛne muala a. Ɛhye ala maa ɔfɛta kɛ yɛsonle ye. Yɛɛ saa yɛdwenledwenle ye tumi kpole ne anwo a, yɛnyia anwodozo kɛ ɔbahola yealie yɛ yeavi yɛ sɔnea muala anu. Noko, Seetan ɛva adalɛ ngilehilelɛ ɛyiyi ewiade ɛhye anu kɛ ɔbamaa sonla anyia Gyihova anwo adwenledwenle. Abɔdeɛ nwo nwomanynli bɔbɔ bahola aha kɛ ngoane rale ɔ nwo kɛkɛ. Saa yɛnlie yɛnli kpalɛ kɛ Gyihova a le Bɔvolɛ ne na ɔkulo yɛ a, bie a yɛbanyia adwenledwenle kɛ ɔwɔ ɛkɛ na ɔbaboa yɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛkayia diedi nwo sɔnea la.—Hib. 11:6.

8 Saa ɛbɔ ɔ bo kɛ ɛnyia adwenlenle kɛ Gyihova a bɔle ninyɛne a, yɛ nwolɛ edwɛkɛ ndɛndɛ. Kpɔkɛ yɛ mbuluku ne mɔ anu kpalɛ kɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ di adanse kpole kɛ Gyihova a bɔle ninyɛne kɔsɔɔti la anwo edwɛkɛ. Ɛbahola wɔanyia ninyɛne dɔɔnwo mɔɔ maa diedi yɛ kse wɔ edwɛkɛti ɛhye anwo la wɔ brohyua mɔɔ le Was Life Created? nee The Origin of Life—Five Questions la anu

Bie a ɛgengalɛ brohyua Was Life Created? la kɛkala. Duzu ati a ɛngenga ye bieko a? (Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 8)

Ɔfɛta Kɛ Ɛbiza. Bɛsuzu daselɛ fofolɛ mɔɔ di adanrɛ la anwo wɔ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ bɛto duma kɛ “Asoo Bɛbɔle Ye Ɔ?” mɔɔ wɔ jw.org la anu. Jessica, adiema raalɛ kakula bie mɔɔ wɔ United States la ka kɛ: “Mɔɔ megengale brohyua mɔɔ se Origin of Life la, mennyale anwodozo kɛ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ bɛha la kile kɛ Bɔvolɛ bie mɔɔ ze nrɛlɛbɛ la a wɔ ɛkɛ ne a. Ɛnee meze ɛhye dɛba, noko mɔɔ mesuzule adwenle mɔɔ wɔ brohyua ne anu la anwo la, menwunle kɛ nɔhalɛ a kpalɛ mɔɔ adwenleadwenle biala ɛnle nuhua a.” Eza menli dɔɔnwo anyia buluku mɔɔ se Asoo Bɔvolɛ Bie Wɔ Ɛkɛ Mɔɔ Dwenle Ɛ nwo Ɔ? la mɔɔ bɛgenga la azo nvasoɛ. Ɔbayɛ kɛ wɔgenga edwɛkɛ ɛhye dɛba. Noko duzu ati a ɛngenga ye bieko a?

⁹ Wɔ wɔ edwɛkɛ bieko mɔɔ ɛkpondɛ nwolɛ edwɛkɛ la anu, ɛbahola wɔanwunwonle ninyɛne mɔɔ kile kɛ Nyamenle wɔ ɛkɛ la anwo. Wɔ neɛnleanu ne ɛnrɛboa wɔ ngomekye kɛ ɛbamaa wɔ diedi mɔɔ ɛlɛ kɛ Gyihova wɔ ɛkɛ la anu amia, emomu ɔbamaa wɔanwu ye subane kpalɛ. (Wlo. 1:20) Bieko, ɛbahola wɔava mɔɔ ɛsukoa la wɔaboa awie mɔ mɔɔ bie a bɛlɛ adwenlenle kɛ Nyamenle ɛnle ɛkɛ la. Edwɛkɛ titili ne: Gyihova ɛva daselɛ dɔɔnwo ɛmaa yɛ mɔɔ kile kɛ ɔdaye a ɔle yɛ Bɔvolɛ a.

(Mrɛ. 13:20; 15:21; Mat. 7:12) Yɛɛ Baebolo ngapezidwe dɔɔnwo ɛra nu, mɔɔ le dasehɔlɛ fofolɛ mɔɔ kile kɛ yɛbahola yɛalie Baebolo ne yɛali a. (Gye. 23:14) Ɛhɛe, amgba Nyamenle sunsum ne a manle bɛhɛlɛle Baebolo ne a!

Nɔhalɛ nu, mebadwu mekɛ bie mɔ a mebaanyia adwenledwenle bie mɔ. 13 Saa ɔba na yɛbɔ ɔ bo kɛ yɛnyia adwenle kɛ Gyihova Alasevolɛ ɛnle menli mɔɔ Nyamenle ɛlɛboa bɛ ɛnɛ a, ɔnle kɛ yɛmaa adwenle zɔhane tɛnla yɛ nu. Emomu, yɛbahola yɛapɛ adwenledwenle zɔhane azo saa yɛbiza yɛ nwo kpuya le kɛ: ‘Nwane mɔ a ɛlɛbɔ mɔdenle kpalɛ kɛ bɛbamaa bɛ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ ayia Baebolo ne ngyinlazo a? Nwane mɔ a bu Baebolo ne kɛ ninyɛne muala nzonlɛ a?’ (2 Tim. 4:3, 4) ‘Nwane mɔ a ɛlɛyɛ Gyihova ɛhulolɛdeɛ ɛnɛ a?’—Mat. 7:20, 21.

14 Ɔboka Nyamenle ɛhulolɛdeɛ mɔɔ yɛkɛyɛ ɛnɛ nwo la, yɛbahola yɛabiza yɛ nwo kɛ, ‘Nwane a ɛlɛyɛ edwɛkɛhanlɛ gyima mɔɔ Gyisɛse hanle kɛ bɛbayɛ ye wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ li awieleɛ la a?’ (Mat. 24:14) Mualɛ ne da ali wienyi kpalɛ. Nyamenle ɛlɛfa ye menli—Gyihova Alasevolɛ—ayɛ edwɛkɛhanlɛ gyima ne. Yɛ ngomekye a yɛlɛka Nyamenle Belemgbunlililɛ ne anwo edwɛkpa ne wɔ azɛlɛ kɛyɛ 240 azo a. Yɛlɛyɛ gyima ɛhye yɛadwu ɛvolɛ mɔɔ bo 100 wɔ Belemgbunlililɛ ne anyunlu. Ɛhɛe, yɛva yɛ adwenle yɛzie yɛ edwɛkɛhanlɛ nee ngilehilelɛ gyima mɔɔ hyia kpalɛ la azo.—Mat. 28:19, 20.

2 Agɔnwolɛvalɛ mɔɔ yɛkpondɛ kɛ yɛnyia yɛboka yɛ mediema mrenyia nee mraalɛ nwo la ɛnle debie mɔɔ bɛyɛ ye ngomekye a. Emomu, ɔlɛ nvasoɛ yɛɛ ɔnwu aze kpalɛ. Yɛboka azonlenlɛ abusua mɔɔ kulo Gyihova nee Gyisɛse la anwo, na ɛlɔlɛ ɛhye kɔka yɛ bɔ nu. (Dwɔn 13:35) Noko Keleseɛne agɔnwolɛvalɛ ɛhye mɔ ɛnzi zɔhane ala. Ɔhyia mɔdenlebɔlɛ. Na kɛ mɔɔ ɔde wɔ agɔnwolɛvalɛ biala mɔɔ wɔ menli mɔɔ ɛnli munli avinli la anu la, agɔnwolɛvalɛ ɛhye mɔ noko kɔhola kɔnyia ngyegyelɛ.

4 Amaa yɛamaa Kilisienenye agɔnwolɛvalɛ ayɛ kpole la, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛnea kpalɛ mɔɔ wɔ awie mɔ anu la. Mekɛ mɔɔ yɛ nee yɛ mediema mrenyia nee mraalɛ kɛli la bahola amaa yɛahakye bɛ subue kpalɛ ne mɔ. Ɛnee ɛzoanvo Pɔɔlo kulo kɛ ɔnee ye mediema mrenyia nee mraalɛ tɛnla. Ɔhɛlɛle Tɛsalonaekama kɛ: “Yɛbɔle mɔdenle kpole kɛ yɛbanwu bɛ mbɔnleba.” (1 Tɛs. 2:17) Menli dɔɔnwo nwu ye kɛ debie biala ɛnle ɛkɛ mɔɔ le kɛ bɛbadendɛ bɛali ngakyile amaa bɛ agɔnwolɛvalɛ ayɛ kpole a. Yɛnyia adenle dɔɔnwo kɛ yɛkɛyɛ zɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛbɔ nu yɛkɔ daselɛlilɛ na yɛkɔ debiezukoalɛ, maanyɛlɛ nee nyianu ngakyile la. Noko bie a yɛbanwu ye kɛ ngɛba mɔɔ yɛbɔ la ngomekye ɛnsisi ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ bahola azɛkye yɛ agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne la adenle.

5 Nyamenle subane mɔɔ yɛbanya la bahola aboa yɛ amaa yɛali agɔnwolɛvalɛ mɔɔ ɛkɛnlamgba sɔ la anwo bane. (Kenga Kɔlɔsaebimane 3:12.) Saa yɛpelɛ bɛtɛyɛlɛ, abotane, atiakunlukɛnlɛma, nee anwunvɔnezelɛ a, yɛbahola yɛali ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ bagya agɔnwolɛvalɛ nu la azo konim. Kɛ neazo la, kɛmɔ ngo maa kali engyenle bɔ kpalɛ la, zɔhane ala a Nyamenle subane mɔɔ yɛbada ye ali la bamaa agbegbanwuanɛ biala mɔɔ bazu yɛ agɔnwolɛvalɛ nu la azo adwe a. Yɛzuzu ninyɛne nsa mɔɔ kola gya yɛ nee yɛ mediema diedima agɔnwolɛvalɛ nu la anwo. Mekɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛyɛ zɔ la, yɛbazuzu kɛzi Gyisɛse bɛtɛyɛlɛ, abotane, atiakunlukɛnlɛma, nee anwunvɔnezelɛ mɔɔ yɛbazukoa la bamaa yɛahɔ zo yɛayɛ nɔhalɛ gɔnwo mɔ yɛamaa awie mɔ la anwo.

⁸ Kɛzi ɛbahola wɔazi wɔamaa wɔ agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne adɛnla ɛkɛ ne dahuu ɛ? Mɛlɛbɛnwoaze bahola aboa wɔ amaa wɔabu ɛ nye wɔagua ninyɛne nketewa mɔɔ ɛ nee awie anli adwenle kɔnwiɛ wɔ nwole la azo na wɔava wɔahyɛ awie mɔ bɔkɔɔ. (Kenga Kɔlɔsaema 3:13.) (Edw. 4:4) Saa bɛka edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ɛnle kpalɛ bɔbɔ a, kakye kɛ yɛ muala yɛka ninyɛne mɔɔ nzinlii yɛnwu kɛ nvonleɛ a la. (Nol. 7:21, 22) Biza wɔ nwo kɛ, ‘Kɛ mebazi me ti anwoma kɛ me a melie nɔhalɛ la sonle bolɛ tɛla yɛ agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne ɔ?’ Ɛbahola wɔakpa nwolɛ adenle kɛ ɛbalie ahenle edwɛkɛ ne wɔado nu ala. Maa ɔvi wɔ ahonle nu na mmadwenledwenle edwɛkɛ biala mɔɔ bɛanli nwolɛ adwenle kɔnwiɛ la anwo anzɛɛ mmatwe edwɛkɛ ne kɔ ebia bieko.

(Kenga Mrɛlɛbulɛ 17:9; 1 Kɔl. 6:7) Ɛbayɛ kpalɛ kɛ ɛbava ye wɔ bodane kɛ ɛbakɔ zo wɔali wɔ gɔnwo anwo nɔhalɛ. Menli dɔɔnwo ɛbɔ mɔdenle kɛ bɛbanyia bɛ gɔnwo anwo kɛnlɛma na bɛaziezie edwɛkɛ ne. MEKƐ MƆƆ SUBANE LE NGAKYILE LA

10 Duzu ati a ɔyɛ se a? Bie a ɔbayɛ se kɛ yɛbanye mediema mrenyia nee mraalɛ mɔɔ bɛ subue le ngakyile fi yɛ ɛdeɛ ne anwo la agɔnwolɛ kpalɛ. Bie a ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛyɛ la bie mɔ gyegye yɛ. Bie a bɔbɔ awie mɔ ɛnze kɛzi bɛ nee menli di la kpalɛ ɔluakɛ awie mɔ yɛle bɛ basabasa wɔ tete ne anzɛɛ kɛmɔ bɛvi amaamuo mɔɔ menli mɔɔ wɔ nu la ɛngola ɛngasa dɔɔnwo anzɛɛ bɛkulo kɛ bɛtwe adwelie dɔɔnwo la ati. Anzɛɛ bie a yɛ subue nee bɛ ɛdeɛ ne ɛnli—bie a yɛkulo menli nu ɛhɔlɛ noko bɛmɛ bɛsubue yɛ nyunludwɛkɛ anzɛɛ zɔhane ala a ɔte wɔ yɛ afoa nu.

11 Sukoa Gyisɛse abotane ne. Ɔnee menli mɔɔ bɛ subue le ngakyile wɔ ye ɛdeɛ ne anwo la le ye agɔnwonma. Kɛ neazo la, ɛzoanvolɛma Gyemise nee Dwɔn hilele kɛ bɛkpondɛ kɛ bɛdi ninyɛne nzonle bie mɔ ati wɔ Belemgbunlililɛ ne anu. (Maake 10:35-37) Noko akee, Gyisɛse bɛlɛle ɔ nwo aze hwenle ɔ nwo vile dibilɛ kpole mɔɔ ɔlɛ wɔ anwuma nee nwolɛ adenle dɔɔnwo anwo rale azɛlɛ ye azo. (Fel. 2:5-8) Dwenle kɛzi bɛ subue ne mɔ le ngakyile kpalɛ la anwo. Noko akee, Gyisɛse nyianle abotane manle Gyemise nee Dwɔn yɛɛ awie mɔ gyɛne.

12 Gyisɛse ampe mɔɔ bo zolɛ la anye anzɔ ye agɔnwolɛma ne mɔ asa. Ɛnee ɔze kɛ Gyemise nee Gyɔn adwenle mɔɔ le kɛ bɛnyia dibilɛ kpole la kile dahuu agoya sunsum mɔɔ ɛdoalɛma ne mɔ dɔɔnwo lɛ la. (Maake 9:34) Gyisɛse nyianle lehyenle ɛzonlenlɛ nu tɛnlabelɛ kokye ne ala mɔɔ bɛdaye noko bɛwɔ nu la anu. Ɛnee bɛbu dibilɛ kpole nee gyinlabelɛ kpole kpalɛ, yemɔti ɛnee ɔte mɔɔ bɛdwenle la abo. Noko akee, ɔnyianle abotane wɔ bɛ nwo, ɔmanle bɛ mekɛ na bɛhɔ zo bɛye mgbonyianye nee atisesebɛyɛlɛ mɔɔ bɛnyianle ye wɔ ɛvolɛ dɔɔnwo anu la bɛvi bɛ nu.—Maake 10:42-45.

13 Kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na wɔadoa zo wɔazɔ wɔ agɔnwolɛvalɛ nu ɛ? Doa zo nyianɛ abotane kɛ ɔlua mɔɔ ɛbaye ɛ nwo awole kɛ ɛbadɛnla subiane mɔɔ kɛzi mɛla mɔɔ wɔ awie mɔ anwo mɔɔ maa ɛ sa nu to la anwo. (Mrɛ. 14:29) Yɛ muala yɛlɛ yɛ subiane ngakyile, mɔɔ dɔɔnwo ɛnle nvonleɛ na emomu ninyɛne mɔɔ kile kɛzi yɛde la ala a. Ɔti ɔda ali kɛ yɛ nye die nwo kɛ awie mɔ bado bɛ rɛle aze adɛnla yɛ nwo la. (Kenga Ɛfɛsɛsembunli 4:2.) Ɔtɛla nwolɛ kɛ yɛbabu ninyɛne kɛmɔ bɛde amgba la kɛ ɔlua mɔɔ yɛbalie awie mɔ yɛado nu la azo. Ɔwɔ kɛ yɛdwenle mɔdenle mɔɔ awie mɔɔ le koonwu na ɔle nyiane bɔbɔ bɔ na yeahola yeabɔ awie mɔ anwo na yeadoa zo yeali bɛ nwo adwelie la anwo. Eza noko, yɛ mɔdenle kɛ ɛnrɛhɔ ɛ nye zo kɛ ɛbamaa ɛ rɛle awole awie mɔɔ le amba na ɔka mɔɔ wɔ ye ahonle nu la anwo. Daselɛlilɛ ekpunli ne mɔ boa yɛ kpole maa yɛdi yɛ ɛzonlelilɛ mɔɔ le kɛ yɛbɔ Belemgbunlililɛ edwɛkpa ne nolo “wɔ aleɛbo amuala” la azo.—Mat. 24:14.

2 Kɛzi ɛte nganeɛ wɔ daselɛlilɛ eku ngyehyɛleɛ ne anwo ɛ? Adiema raalɛ bie mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Wendy* la ka kɛ: “Ngyehyɛleɛ ɛhye ɛboa me daselɛlilɛ gyima ne kpole kpalɛ. Mɔɔ nolobɔlɛma bie mɔ banyia nwolɛ adenle aboa sonla bie amaa yeanyia anyuhɔlɛ yeado ɛzulekɛ la, eku zo neavolɛ lɛ adenle titili mɔɔ ɔfa ɔboa zɔhane ahenle amaa yeanyia sunsum nu anyuhɔlɛ wɔ ɛvolɛ mɔɔ bara la anu.” Adiema raalɛ bie mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Dinah la ka kɛ: “Daselɛlilɛ eku ngyehyɛleɛ ne le kpalɛ kpalɛ! “Bɛnlea kɛzi bɛtie la boɛ.”—LUKE 8:18.

ƐNƐ, Gyihova ɛlɛmaa yɛ sunsum nu aleɛ dɔɔnwo kpalɛ. Ɔda ali kɛ yɛbahola yɛalie Devidi mɔɔ dole edwɛne kɛ: “Gyihova a le me neavolɛ. Debie biala ɛnrɛhyia me.”—Kenga Edwɛndolɛ 23:1, 2, 5.

2 Adenle ko mɔɔ Gyihova dua zo maa yɛ sunsum nu aleɛ ɛnɛ la a le yɛ asafo ayia ne mɔ. Wɔ ayia ɛhye mɔ azo la, dɔɔnwo ne ala yɛsuzu edwɛkɛ mɔɔ yɛze ye dedee la anwo. Saa bɔbɔ yɛ gyinlabelɛ “ɛgyinla kpundii wɔ nɔhalɛ ne anu” a, yɛhyia ngakyeleɛ ɛhye mɔ anwo. (2 Pita 1:12) Noko akee, saa sunsum nu gyimalilɛ mɔɔ bɛyɛ la kilehile edwɛkɛ mɔɔ yede ye fane dɔɔnwo la anu a, ɔyɛ se kɛ ɛbadie ɔ? Edwɛkɛtile ɛhye bahola aboa wɔ. Yɛbazuzu deɛmɔti ɔwɔ kɛ yɛtie debie kpalɛ wɔ ayia bo la anwo. Eza yɛbanwu kɛzi menli mɔɔ kilehile wɔ asafo ne anu la bahola aboa awie mɔ amaa bɛadie la. Na yɛbazuzu nyɛleɛ ekyi mɔɔ baboa yɛ amaa yɛayɛ menli mɔɔ tie debie kpalɛ la anwo. Noko mɔlebɛbo ne, bɛmaa yɛnlea ahenle mɔɔ ɛlɛtendɛ ahile yɛ la.

8 Mediema mrenyia mɔɔ kilehile wɔ bɛnso ne azo la, titili mɔɔ bɛmaa bagua nu ɛdendɛlɛ la, ɔwɔ kɛ bɛkɔ zo bɛyɛ anyhɔlɛ wɔ bɛ ɛdendɛlɛ nu. ngɛnngrɛlatwɛnɛ ɛhye mɔ anu dɔɔnwo bahola aboa wɔ amaa wɔadie debie kpalɛ ɔ? (1) Saa ɛdi aleɛ kolaa na debiezukoalɛ abɔ ɔ bo a, nea kɛ ɛbali aleɛ ekyi amaa wɔ nye ala ɛ gulozo. KƐ MƆƆ GUSTAVO JOSEPH HANLE LA

Bɛwole me wɔ Cuba supɔ kɛnlɛma ne azo wɔ Caribbean wɔ 1947. Ɔwɔ ɛleka mɔɔ Caribbean Nyevile ne mɔɔ wɔ ɛnwomenle nu la anzole mɔɔ le wuliraba la nee Atlantic Nyevile ne yia la. Mebale me awieleɛ la, me awovolɛ wole me diema mrenya nwiɔ. Yɛdaye yɛ mrenyia nnu yɛdɛnlanle Esmeralda namule ne azo.

Mekakye kɛ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ mɔɔ wɔ yɛ namule ekyi ne azo la dɛlɛ anzodwɔlɛ. Yɛ nee yɛ mbusuanli bie mɔ dɛnlanle bikyenenle, mɔɔ bie a le yɛ selemgbanyima mraalɛ, yɛ selemgbanyima mrenya, nee yɛ nanamgbanyima. Yɛnyianle aleɛ kpole mɔɔ yɛdi la na yɛbɔle anyelielɛ ɛbɛla.

Mekɛ mɔɔ ɛnee meli kɛyɛ ɛvolɛ nnu la, me awovolɛ bɔle ɔ bo kɛ bɛ nee Walton Jones ɛsukoa Baebolo ne. Ɛnee ɔle edwɛkpa hanlɛnli mɔɔ bɔ mɔdenle mɔɔ kɔ dɔnwo kɛyɛ bulu amaa yeara yɛ suakpole ne azo ala. Mekɛ biala mɔɔ ɔbara la, me abusua ne anu amra dɔnwo yia wɔ me nenyia nee me nwole sua nu dɔnehwele dɔnwo bɛ nee ye suzu Baebolo ne anwo. Me awovolɛ, me papa diema Pedro nee me nwole Ela anye liele mɔɔ ɛnee bɛlɛsukoa la anwo yɛɛ yeangyɛ kpolera bɛzɔnenle bɛ kɛ Gyihova Alasevolɛ. Ɛnɛ Ela ɛli kɛyɛ ɛvolɛ 100 yɛɛ ɔtɛkɔ zo ɔsomonle kɛ dahuu adekpakyelɛnli wɔ Cuba.

Zɔhane mekɛ ne, ɛnee Gyihova Alasevolɛ bahola azonle wɔ Cuba kɛ mɔɔ bɛkulo la. Ɛnee bɛze yɛ kpole kɛ yɛbɔ suamee suamee edwɛkpa ne, yɛsɔ kotoku mɔɔ Baebolo mbuluku mɔɔ sonle bolɛ la yɛ ye tɛkɛtɛkɛ. Yɛɛ ɛnee yɛtia kpole kpalɛ! Me ngyegyelɛ mekɛ mɔɔ lumua la anu mɔɔ mevale mezonle Gyihova wɔ zɔhane “mekɛ kpalɛ ne” anu la yɛ me fɛ kpalɛ. Noko ɛnee “mekɛ ɛsesebɛ” la yɛ nyunlu.—2 Tim. 4:2.

Titili ne, yɛ nye die kɛ yɛnwunle kɛ yɛ mra nee yɛ mra mra kɛ bɛlɛsonle Gyihova. Yɛte nganeɛ kɛ mɔɔ ɛnee ɛzonlenlɛma kpole Dwɔn te la, ɔhanle kɛ: “Anyelielɛ biala ɛnle kpole ɛntɛla ɛhye, kɛ mebade kɛ me mra ɛlɛkɔ zo adu bɛ nwo kɛtebɛbɔ nu.”​—3 Dwɔn 4.

Kɛkala, mɔɔ yɛzonle wɔ Bɛtɛle kɛyɛ ɛvolɛ 30 la, me ne Emilia yɛbɔ mɔdenle yɛdi yɛ gyima ne mɔ mɔɔ kansele nee mgbanyinli mɔɔ yɛwula la maa yɛkɔkpɔla nwolɛ dahuu la azo. Yɛ ɛvolɛ dɔɔnwo ne mɔ mɔɔ yɛva yɛzonle Gyihova la anu, yɛyia ngyegyelɛ bie mɔ, noko yɛ nye die kɛ yɛhola yɛzonle “anyiɛlɛ Nyamenle ne” kɛyɛ ɛvolɛ 70 wɔ Cuba bɔnsue ne azo ɛke!—1 Tim. 1:11; Edw. 97:1.

KPUKPUDILƐ MAVI KENGAVOMA ƐKƐ

Kɛ mɔɔ bɛha ye wɔ Galeehyeabikɔ 4:24 la, ngyekyeleɛ nwiɔ boni mɔ a ɛzoanvolɛ Pɔɔlo hanle nwolɛ edwɛkɛ a?

◼ Mɔɔ ɛzoanvolɛ Pɔɔlo hanle Abraham nee Seela yɛɛ Hega agɔnwolɛvalɛ nwo edwɛkɛ la, ɔhanle kɛ: “Edwɛkɛ ɛhye mɔ le sɛkɛlɛneɛ edwɛkɛ; ɔluakɛ mraalɛ nwiɔ ɛhye mɔ gyi ɛkɛ maa ngyekyeleɛ nwiɔ.” (Gal. 4:22-24) Mɔlebɛbo ne, yɛdele ɔ bo kɛ ngyekyeleɛ nwiɔ ne a le Mɛla ngyekyeleɛ ne nee ngyekyeleɛ fofolɛ ne. Noko akee, mɔɔ yɛdwenledwenle nwo kpalɛ la, yɛdie yɛto nu kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkakyi yɛ adwenle. Ɔzɔho kɛ tɛ ngyekyeleɛ fofolɛ ne a ɛnee Pɔɔlo ɛlɛka nwolɛ edwɛkɛ a, emomu, Abraham ngyekyeleɛ ne a. Suzu edwɛkɛ ekyi bie mɔ anwo.*

Pɔɔlo hwenle sonvolɛ raalɛ Hega anwo edwɛkɛ doale Sayenae Boka ne anwo. (Gal. 4:25) Hega gyinlanle ɛkɛ ne maanle Mɛla ngyekyeleɛ ne mɔɔ bɛvale bɛmaanle wɔ Sayenae Boka ne azo wɔ 1513 K.Y.M. la. (Ɛzk. 19:5, 6) Sonla mɔɔ tɔ sinli biala amvula anli Mɛla zɔhane azo pɛpɛɛpɛ, yemɔti ɔkakyele Dyuuma ne kɛ bɛle kɛsonle bɛmaa ɛtane. Ɛngyekyeleɛ ɛhye bahola aboa Yizilayɛ manle ne amaa bɛanwu Mɛzaya ne, akee sonla kokye mɔɔ bɔholɛ ali Mɛla ne azo pɛpɛɛpɛ la. Mɛzaya ne vale ye ngoane mɔɔ di munli la bɔle afɔle la, ɔmaanle ɔyɛle bɔkɔbɔ kɛ alesama mɔɔ tɔ sinli la banyia fanwodi bɛavi ɛtane mini ewule nu. (Gal. 3:19, 24, 25) Mekɛ mɔɔ Gyisɛse lile Nyamenle bodane nwo gyima guale la, ɛnee bɛngyia Mɛla ne anwo bieko.—Wlo. 10:4.

Pɔɔlo hanle Hega nee Seela nwo edwɛkɛ totole nuhua, Seela a le “bɛlɛra ne mɔɔ le amonipɔkɛ” mɔɔ wole “ɔlua ɛwɔkɛ nwo zo.” (Gal. 4:23) Ɛwɔkɛ zɔhane a le Ebileham ngyekyeleɛ ne. (Gal. 3:29; 4:28, 30) Bɛha nwolɛ edwɛkɛ ezinra nu wɔ Mɔlebɛbo 22:18 kɛ: “Eza azɛlɛ ye azo maanle maanle kɔsɔɔti balua wɔ [Ebileham] amozii ne azo anyia nyilalɛ amaa bɛ nwo kɛmɔ wɔdie me ɛne la ati.”

* Ɛhye kɔkye mɔɔ bɛyɛle ye wɔ The Watchtower, March 15, 2006, pp. 10-12 anu la azo.

Ngyegyelɛ zɔhane mɔɔ bɛ nee Ebileham yɛle la doale ɛwɔkɛ mɔɔ Gyihova bɔle wɔ Yidɛn la azo. (Gɛn. 3:15) Ɔhilele kɛ “awolɛsoa” mɔɔ bɛhanle nwolɛ edwɛkɛ la bavi Ebileham awozonle nu. Pɔɔlo hilehilele nu kɛ zɔhane “awolɛsoa” ne titili a le Gyisɛse Kelaese. Noko mɔɔ “bɛle Kelaese ɛdeɛ,” ahyɛdadeɛma 144,000 mɔɔ bɛ nee Gyisɛse bali tumi wɔ ye Belemgbunlililɛ ne anu la, boka sɛkɛlɛneɛ adenle zo awolɛsoa ɛhye anwo. (Gal. 3:16, 29; Yek. 14:1-3) Nyamenle ɛwɔkɛ mɔɔ ɔbɔle Ebileham la mɔɔ bara nu la bava nyilalɛ dɔɔnwo arɛlɛ menli mɔɔ bɛkpa Gyisɛse bɛto nu na bɛtie ye la.

Duzu ati a Pɔɔlo lile “mgbanyidwɛkɛ ɛhye” nzonlɛ a? Ɔhilele kɛ saa Dwuuma mɔɔ wɔ ye mekɛ zo la kɔ zo fa Mɛla ne di gyima a, bɛbadɛnla ngɛkɛlɛyɛlɛ nu kɛmɔ Heega dɛnlanle la. Noko, saa bɛdie Ebeleham “abozo” titilii ne, Gyisɛse Kelaese, bɛto nu a, bɛbanyia bɛ ti kɛmɔ Sɛla nyianle la. Bɛbanyia fanwodi kpalɛ bɛavi ɛtane nee ewule nu. (Dwɔn 8:32-34) Saa bɛanglie Gyisɛse bɛando nu a, bɛnrɛnwu bodane titilii mɔɔ Mɛla ngyekyeleɛ ne wɔ nwo la, mɔɔ a le kɛ ɔbamaa bɛahɔ Kelaese ɛkɛ ne la.

Galeehyele mɔɔ Pɔɔlo hɛlɛle bɛ la dɔɔnwo ɛnle Djuufoɔ na bɛtɛwɔle Mɛla ne abo ɛlɛ. Djuufoɔ bie mɔ hanle kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ Maandane Kilisiene mɔɔ ɛnle Djuufoɔ la di Mɛla ne azo, mɔɔ ɛhotonlɛ mɛla ne boka nwo la. Pɔɔlo hilehilele nu kɛ ɔbayɛ ngelebela kɛ ɛhye mɔ balie Mɛla ne “akɛlɛyɛlɛ” ne ado nu ɔluakɛ bɛnyia “fanwodi” mɔɔ Kilisito de la dɛba.—Gal. 5:1, 10-14.

BAEBOLO NU NƆHALƐ

Ebanu Mɔɔ Bɛvale Bɛyɛle Egyalilɛ Wɔ Baebolo Mekɛ Ne Anu

Baanlɛma mɔɔ kenga Baebolo ne la bahakye kɛ Gyisɛse ɛdoavolɛma ne bie mɔ yɛle foya mgbavolɛ. (Maake 1:16-20) Ɛnee bɛfa asau bɛyɛ foya wɔ Galeli Nyevile ne azo.

Bɛvale nyɛle anzɛɛ abodoɛ bɛhyelehyelale na bɛvale bɛyɛle asawu. Asawu ne anwo ɛloma ne mgbole kɛzi ɔde la gyi kenle ko bieala mɔɔ bɛkulo kɛ bɛto la azo. Taata nsesebɛ dɔɔnwo lɛ nvile anzɛɛ bolɛ bɔbɔ mɔɔ maa ɔkɔ aze a, na asawu mgbole mɔɔ bɛtwe la noko lɛ bolɛ wɔ ɔ bo lɔ na baka anzɛɛ kɔɔke mɔɔ pɛ la wɔ ɔ nzi lɔ.

Esua ɛnee sonle bolɛ. Ɛnee nyɛlɛ mɔɔ wɔ nyevile ne abo la kola sɛkye bɛ anzɛɛ fɛlɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ bɛbahwe la ɛhɛlɛ bɛ. (Luku 5:6) Baebolo ne ka kɛ Gyemise nee Dwɔn “lɛto bɛ sua ne mɔ”—ɔda ali kɛ bɛvale panɛ nee linɛn nyɛma a bɛyɛle a. (Mat. 4:21) Bie a ebie fofɔlɛ biala mɔɔ menli mɔɔ kye fɛlɛ ne mɔ baho la bɛto bɛ sua ne mɔ. Luku eza ka kɛ menli mɔɔ kye fɛlɛ ne mɔ “wulale bɛ sua ne mɔ” amaa bɛaye ɛsɛkɛ anzɛɛ nwɔho bɛavi nu. (Luku 5:2) Ɔvi ɛkɛ ne la, menli mɔɔ kye fɛlɛ ne mɔ bahaha bɛ sua ne mɔ amaa yaze—bie a amaa yeambɔlo.—Yiz. 47:10.

Gyisɛse dɔle mrenyia mɔɔ ɛnee ɛyɛ bɛ nyianuu kɛ bɛdua ɛsa la kɛ bɛrayɛ “menli ekuvoma.”

Bɛndɔne buluku ɛhye. Bɛyɛ bɛmaa ewiade amuala Baebolo ɛhwehyɛle gyima ne mɔɔ bɛfa ndonwo ahyɛlɛdeɛ bɛyɛ la. Saa ɛkulo kɛ ɛye ahyɛlɛdeɛ a, yɛsɛlɛ wɔ kɔ donate.jw.org.
