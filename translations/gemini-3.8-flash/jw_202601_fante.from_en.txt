HƐN AFE 2026 ASƐMTSIR:

Enyigye nye hɔn a wonyim dɛ sunsum mu nkyerɛwee ho hia hɔn.

—MATT. 5:3.

JEHOVAH abɔ nyimpa a ɔdze ahyɛ hɛn mu dɛ yehia ndzɛmba etsitsir bi. Dɛ nhwɛdo no, ama yaatsena ase no, yehia edziban, ntar, na beebi a yɛbɛtsena. Sɛ dɛm ndzɛmba etsitsir yi mu biako mpo bɔ hɛn, sɛ ɔyɛ aber tsiabaa mpo a, asetsena bɛyɛ dzen. Nyankopɔn ammɔ hɛn dɛ yehia dɛm honam afamu ndzɛmba no nko, mbom ɔbɔɔ hɛn dɛ yehia sunsum afamu ndzɛmba so. (Ken Matthew 5:3.) Sɛ yɛpɛ dɛ yenya enyigye a ɔwɔ hɔ tsimbɔee a, ɔwɔ dɛ yehu dɛ yehia dɛm ahiawdze no na yaakɔ do ama ho kwan.

2 Ebɛn na ɔkyerɛ dɛ obi benya “nyansapo” wɔ n’abrabɔ mu dɛ “ɔbɔhwehwɛ sunsum mu nkyerɛkyerɛ”? Kan kasa a wɔdze kyerɛwee no kyerɛ adwen a ɔnye dɛ obi yɛ sunsum mu hwehwɛfo anaa ehiafo. Dɛm asɛm no ma adwenmu mfonyin a emu yɛ dzen ba. Fa no dɛ ihu obi a n’ahoɔfɛ asɛe a ntar a asunsun gu no do, a ɔhyehyɛ kurowpɔn bi mu kwan nkyɛn. Ɔayɛ mmerɛw osian edziban a onnyi ntsi, wɔtwe hɔnho fi no ho osian sɛnea ɔtse ntsi, na awia hyehyew a ano yɛ dzen nye anafua awɔw a emu yɛ dzen nyinara bɔ no. Dɛm ehiafo yi nyim yie paa dɛ ɔbɔhwehwɛ mboa sɛ ɔpɛ dɛ n’asetena mu yɛ yie a. Dɛmara so na obi a ɔnyim dɛ ɔbɔhwehwɛ sunsum mu nkyerɛkyerɛ—sunsum mu ehiafo—bɔhwehwɛ mboa na n’asetena ayɛ yie. Dɛm ntsi, n’enyi gye ho dɛ ɔdze sunsum mu ndzɛmba pii a Jehovah dze ma hɔn a wɔdɔ No no bɛyɛ edwuma.

4 Ber bi no, Fonisia basia bi bɛtwee bɛn Jesus. Ná sunsum bɔn “rehyɛ no babaa dɛnnenndzenndzen.” (Matt. 15:21-28) Basia no hwee famu kotowee na ɔserɛɛ Jesus dɛ ɔmbɔboa no. Ber a dɛm Fonisia basia yi bɛtwee bɛn Jesus no, ɔdaa su horow a ɔfata dɛ yɛhyɛ no nsew edzi. Susu dɛm su horow yi bi ho hwɛ.

5 Fenisia basia no daa ahobrɛadze pa edzi. Ebɛnadze ntsi na yebotum aka dɛm? Osiandɛ ɔammfa annhyɛ ne mu ber a Jesus dze mfatoho bi dzii dwuma a nna ɔtse dɛ dza ɔdze no rototo kraman ketsewa ho, bi a aboa a wɔtsetse no wɔ fie wɔ Amanamanmufo fie bi mu. Nkyɛ ebɛn kwan do na ibesi etse Jesus n’asɛm na ne ndzeyɛe ase? Nkyɛ ibenya nkanyim na egyaa dɛ erohwehwɛ ne mboa anaa? Nnyɛ dɛm kwan no do na Fenisia basia no fae. Ɔannkyerɛ ahobrɛadze nko na mbom ɔkɔr do so pɛree. Iyi da edzi wɔ kwan a ɔkɔr do suer Jesus dɛ ɔmboa no no mu. Ebɛnadze na ɔkaa no ma ɔyɛɛ dɛm? Gyedzi a nna ɔwɔ wɔ Jesus mu no. Nokwasɛm no, basia yi ne gyedzi kaa Jesus kɛse araa ma ɔyɛɛ adze kɛse bi. Ɔwɔ mu dɛ nna oenya aaka dɛ wɔsomaa no dɛ ɔmmbɔboa “Israel fi no mu nguan a ayew” nko dze, nanso Jesus tuu sunsum fi no fii dɛm Amanamanmu basia yi ne babaa no mu.

DƐ PETER NO—KƆ DO NYA SUNSUM MU EDZIBAN A ƆDƆƆSO

7 Susuw asomafo Peter n'nhwɛdo no ho. Nna ɔka Djudanyi a wodzii kan hun dɛ Jesus nye Messia no—nyia nna Jehovah rofa no do ama n'ekyirzifo “daa nkwa nsɛm” no ho. (John 6:66-68) Ansaana Jesus a wɔaanyan no no rokɔ sor no, ɔmaa Peter dɛm ahyɛdze yi: “Yɛn mo nguan nkumaa no.” (John 21:17) Peter dze nokwardzi dzii dɛm ahyɛdze no do, na Jehovah mpo faa no do kyerɛɛw nkrataa ebien a ɔbɛkaa Bible no ho. Naaso nna ɔsɛ dɛ Peter so dze sunsum mu edziban yɛn no ho. Dɛ nhwɛdo no, osuaa nkrataa a Paul kyerɛwee ansaana ɔrowo a nna sunsum ahyɛ no do no. Peter gyeeto mu dɛ Paul no nsɛm a ɔkyerɛwee no bi “yɛ dzen dɛ wɔbɛtse ase.” (2 Pet. 3:15, 16) Naaso ɔda edzi kɛse dɛ Peter kɔr do yɛe, a nna ɔwɔ gyedzi dɛ Jehovah bɔboa no ma oeatumi atse edziban a “ɛyɛ dzen” a Paul nam sunsum a ɔhyɛɛ no do dze baa no nkrataa no mu no ase, na dze abɔ ne bra.—Kenkan Hebrews 5:14–6:1.

8 Peter daa no edzi dɛ ɔwɔ gyedzi wɔ Jehovah mu osiandɛ ɔyɛɛ dzetseewaa wɔ akwankyerɛ a onyae no ho. Dɛ nhwɛdo no, ber a nna ɔwɔ Joppa a ɔyɛ hyɛn gyinabea kurow no mu no, Peter nyaa ananmusifo a ɔfiri Nyankopɔn hɔ a ɔsee no dɛ onkum mboa a Moses Mbra no kyerɛ dɛ hɔn ho nnyi ahotew no na ondzi hɔn ho enyido-adhu. Dɛm akwankyerɛ yi bɛyɛɛ biribi a nna ɔyɛ nwanwa ma Jewfo no kɛse. Mfiase no, Peter yii ano dɛ: “Oho, Ewuradze, osiandɛ minndzii biribiara a efi wom na no ho nnyi ahotew da.” Afei wɔsee no dɛ: “Gyaa ndzɛmba a Nyankopɔn ahotew hɔn no dɛ efi wom a efrɛ hɔn dɛm.” (Asomafo 10:9-15) Peter tsee asɛm no ase. Yɛyɛ dɛn hu dɛm? Ber a onyaa enyido-adhu no ekyir ara pɛ no, mbarimba baasa a Amananba Cornelius somaa hɔn no bae na wɔtoo nsa frɛɛ Peter dɛ ɔmbra mbɛkasa nkyerɛ hɔn wura no. Dɛ mbrɛ ɔtaa kɔ do no, nna Peter remmpene da dɛ ɔbɛkɔ Amananba bi fie. Jewfo no buu Amananba no dɛ hɔn ho nnyi ahotew. (Asomafo 10:28, 29) Naaso Peter gyee kan fofor a Jehovah aada no edzi akyerɛ no no ntɛmara. (Mbɛb. 4:18) Ɔkãa asɛm no kyerɛɛ Cornelius na n'efiefi na onyaa enyigye dɛ ohun dɛ wɔgyee nokwar no, wonyaa sunsum krɔnkrɔn, na wonyaa asubɔ.—Asomafo 10:44-48.

TESƆ PAUL NO—KƆ DO NYA SUNSUM MU NSIHO PA

10 Sɛ yɛbɛsɔ Nyankopɔn enyi a, ɔwɔ dɛ yɛdze biribi fofor a Jehovah dze ama no dzi dwuma: sunsum afamu ntarhyɛ. Ebɛnadze na yɛrekyerɛ wɔ dɛm asɛm no mu? Ɔsomafo Paul kyerɛwee dɛ ɔwɔ dɛ “yɛpa suban akyen” na yɛhyɛ “suban fofor.” (Kenkan Colosaefo 3:8-10.) Dɛ yɛbɛhyɛ ntar dɛm kwan yi do no botum ayɛ nsɔnwensɔm a ɔkɔ do daa. Susu Paul n’ankasa n’nhwɛdo no ho hwɛ. Osua koraa na ɔyɛɛ edwuma dzen dɛ obenya Nyankopɔn n’enyimnyam. (Gal. 1:14; Fil. 3:4, 5) Naaso nna onnyi Nyankopɔn n’atirimpɔw ho nyimdzee pɛpɛɛpɛ, dɛm ntsi nna ɔwɔ sunsum afamu tsebea bɔn mu. Christ n’akyerɛkyerɛ ho a nna onnyim hwee, a ahantan kã ho no, maa ɔbɛyɛɛ “banyin a ommbu ade” a nna ɔhyɛ suban bɔn ntar.—1 Tim. 1:13.

10. Ebɛn na sunsum fam a woasoa ntar pa kyerɛ? (Kolosefo 3:8-10)

11 Ansaana Paul rebɛyɛ Christian no, nna n’ebufuw yɛ kɛse papaapa. Asomafo no Asɛm kyerɛwtohɔ no ma yehu dɛ nna Paul ne bo efuw Jesus no esuafo no kɛse araa ma nna ɔrehwehwɛ kwan biara a ɔbɛfa do ‘ehunahuna hɔn na eku hɔn.’ (Acts 9:1) Akyir yi a Paul bɛyɛɛ Christian no, akyinnye biara nnyi ho dɛ ɔbɔɔ mbɔdzen kɛse paara dɛ obeyi nyimnyamhun suban a ɔtse dɛm no efi ne ho. (Eph. 4:22, 31) Dɛm mpo no, ber a Paul nye Barnabas nyaa akyinnyegye no, ebufuw a nna ɔda hɔn ntsam no mu yɛɛ dzen paara ma “hɔn ntsam tetsee koraa.” (Acts 15:37-39) Nna iyi yɛ asɛndzen ma Paul, naaso wammba n’abaw mu emmbu. Ɔkɔr do ‘bɔɔ ne nyimpadua no kɔnmu’—ɔnye ne sinto horow dzii ako—ama ne ho aatɔ Jehovah.—1 Cor. 9:27.

12 Paul tumii yii no ho suban bɔn guu na ɔhyɛɛ nyimpa fofor no suban osiandɛ wammfa no ho annto noara n’ahoɔdzen do. (Phil. 4:13) Dɛm nso na Peter yɛe, Paul dze no ho too “ahoɔdzen a Nyankopɔn ma no” do. (1 Pet. 4:11) Naaso, mber bi wɔ hɔ a Paul tsee nka dɛ n’abaw ebu. Na mbom ber a n’abaw bue no, ɔdwenee ndzɛmba pa a n’sor Egya ayɛ ama no no ho, na iyi hyɛɛ no dzen dɛ ɔnkɔ do mbɔ mbɔdzen.—Rom. 7:21-25.

13 Yebotum asuasua Paul ber a yehu dɛ mbrɛ ber a yɛasom Jehovah akyɛ biara no, ɔwɔ dɛ yɛkɔ do bɔ mbɔdzen gu suban dada no gu na yɛhyɛ suban fofor no—sunsum afamu ntar a Nyankopɔn dze ma no. Sɛ yɛhyia sintɔ bi—tse dɛ

Ebɛn kwan do na Jehovah bɔ n’asomfo ho ban fi ekyir amandze ho? Ɔhyɛ bɔ dɛ akodze biara a wɔbɔbɔ etia hɔn no runndzi mu. (Dw. 34:7; Isa. 54:17) Ɔwɔ mu dɛ Satan nye n’asomafo wɔ tum dze, naaso worunntum nnyɛ hɛn hwee a obosunsum hɛn daa. Sɛ wogye hɛn nkwa mpo a, Jehovah bɛma hɛn bio wɔ wusɔr no mu. (1 Cor. 15:55-57; Nyi. 21:3, 4) Jehovah san so boa hɛn ma yetum gyina mu wɔ hɛn mu adwendwen a obotum asɛɛ hɛn daa no ho. (Mbɛb. 12:25; Matt. 6:27-29) Na hɛn Agya dɔfo no ma hɛn sunsum mu ebusua a wɔyɛ enuanom mbarimba nye mbea a wɔboa hɛn, na mpanyimfo a wɔhwɛ hɛn do. KƆ DO MA WO SUNSUM MU AHIADZE HO NHIA WO

18 Hɛn 2026 afe asɛm no se: “Enyigye nye hɔn a wonyim dɛ wohia sunsum mu ndzɛmba.” Dɛm asɛm yi yɛ nokwar ndɛ kyɛn ber biara. Ebanyɛn ntsi? Osiandɛ nyimpa a wonnyi enyigye a wokyir dɛ wohia dɛm adze no, anaa wɔbɔ mbɔdzen dɛ wobodzidzi dɛm ehiadze no do wɔ hɔn ankasa kwan do afa ator som anaa nyimpa nyansapɛ do no atwa hɛn ho ehyia. Ɔwɔ dɛ yɛkwetsir dɛ yɛbɛma hɔn suban no ahyɛ hɛn do. Ebɛn kwan do? “Woana bɛgye me efi nyimpadua a ɔrokɔ owu yi mu?” —ROM. 7:24.

FA NO dɛ iyi resi. Dan bi abubu, ma ɔatsiw banyin bi wɔ dɔmpɔw no mu. Ɔtse ase dze, naaso ontum mfi amamfõ no mu. Dza obotum ayɛ ara nye dɛ obosu afrɛ mboa na w’aanya enyidado dɛ wobegye no nkwa.

2 Hɛn nyinara wɔ tsebea a ɔtse dɛm mu. Ɔkwan bɛn do? Ber a Adam tsew n’Etsir do tsiaa ne Bɔfo no, ɔbɛyɛɛ bɔnfonyi. Ekyir no, ɔdze ne bɔn tsebea no yɛɛ egyapadze maa ne mba. Dɛm ntsi, nyimpa afa nyinara kɔhyɛɛ bɔn a wɔdze yɛɛ egyapadze no no ndamfo mu, na yentumi mfa hɛnho mfi emu nsunsando no ho. Ɔsomafo Paul kãa hɛn bɔn tsebea no ho asɛm yie wɔ ne krataa a ɔkɛkyerɛɛ Romafo no mu. (Kenkan Romafo 7:22-24.) Ɔsrɛɛ dɛ wonnye no “mfi nyimpadua a orunwu yi mu.” Bɔn a Paul nyae dɛ egyapadze no hyɛɛ no do, a no mu nsunsuando a yenntum nkwati nye owu. (Rom. 6:23) Hɛn nso yɛwɔ dɛm tsebea koro no ara mu. Hia a yehia nye dɛ wonnye hɛn nkwa!

3 Paul anntwe n’ano wɔ bɔn ho asɛm a ɔkãe no mu wɔ kwan bɔn do. Dɛ nhwɛdo no, ndwomkyerɛwnyi David kãa dɛ: “Mobɔwɛn m’anamon ama mammfa me tɛkyerɛma anyɛ bɔn.” (Ndw. 39:1) Nyew, ɔtɔ mber bi a hɛn kãnsɛm mpo betumi ayɛ bɔn.—Jas. 3:2.

5 Dwen wo ndzeyɛe na nsɛm a ahendze a aka no ho hwɛ. Ahyɛ da aka asɛm bi akyerɛ afofor a akyir no eyii wo werɛ dɛ nkyɛ anka? Aso edzi mfomdo a ebedzii akyir dɛ nkyɛ ennyɛ? Ɔmfa ho dɛ wɔayɛ nkɔdo kakra biara no, dɛm mbɔdenbɔ ahorow no nyina ayɛ kwa. Ebɛn kwan do na agyede no betum agye hɛn efi hɛn bɔn gyinabea no mu?

Bioaso, mbɔdzen a yɛbɔ dɛ yɛbɛhwɛ asaase no do na yɛatsena ase asomdwee mu no ronyɛ kwa bio, na mbom obesi yiye wɔ nyia ɔgyee hɛn no, “Asomdwee Hene,” Jesus Christ n’amammbusɛm no ase.—Isa. 9:6, 7.

AGYEDE NO MA MPATAA BA

14 Efitsir aber a Adam na Eve yɛɛ bɔn no, nyimpa etwe hɔnho, anaa wɔatsetsew hɔnho efi Nyankopɔn ho. Bible mpo kã dɛ nyimpadɔm nyinara atwe hɔnho efi Ɔbɔadzefo no ho. (Rom. 8:7, 8; Col. 1:21) Ebɛnadze ntsi a? Osiandɛ Jehovah n'ahyɛdze a ɔyɛ pɛr no mma kwan dɛ ɔbɔto n’enyi egu bɔn do. Bible kã fa Jehovah ho dɛ: “W’enyiwa tew dodo dɛ ɔbɔhwɛ bɔn, na iruntum nntwe wo mu nnhwɛ emumuyɛdze.” (Hab. 1:13) Dɛm ntsi bɔn ahyɛ ntetsewmu wɔ Nyankopɔn na nyimpa ntamu. Hɛn mu biara nnyi hɔ a obotum anya ebusuabɔ kɛse a ɔnye Jehovah ntamu gye dɛ wɔayɛ dɛm ntetsewmu no ho edwuma na yɛnye no asan abam. Agyapadze no ma dɛm mpatam yi tum yɛ yie.

15 Bible no kã dɛ Jesus yɛ “mpata afɔrbɔ ma hɛn bɔn.” (1 John 2:2) Hela asɛmfua a wɔakyerɛ ase dɛ “mpata afɔrbɔ” no botum akyerɛ “biribi a wɔdze pata.” Ebɛn kwan do na agyede no pataa Jehovah? Afei dze onyaa fapem a ɔbɛfa do asan asiesie abusuabɔ a ɔda ɔno na nyimpa ntamu no. (Rom. 3:23-26) Ɔbɔtoo mpo ‘akã’ tsenenee akyerɛ hɔn a wɔsɔɔ no anokwardzi mu ansaana Christ rewu no. (Gen. 15:1, 6, ftn.) Ɔyɛɛ dɛn? Ɔnam daakye agyede no do. Nna Jehovah wɔ ahotodo koraa dɛ ne Ba, Jesus, bɛma dɛm agyede no. (Isa. 46:10) Agyede no buee kwan maa nyimpa nyaa kwan bɛbambaa Nyankopɔ ho.

17 Agyapadze no ma yehu pefee dɛ Jehovah yɛ “ahumbɔmbɔr mu durfo.” Ɔnam ma “yenyaa nkwa . . . ber a nna yɛewu wɔ bɔn mu no.” (Ken Efesofo 2:4, 5.) Na wɔ n'adom kɛse mu no, Jehovah ma hɛn kwan—wɔ hɛn bɔn tsebea no mu mpo—dɛ yɛmfa hɛn ho nhyɛ Satan asɛnsɔhwɛ no mubua mu. (Prov. 27:11) Ebɛn kwan do na ibotum akyerɛ w'enisɔ ama agyedze no? Fa Wo Mfonyin Bu Bible Mu Nsɛm No
