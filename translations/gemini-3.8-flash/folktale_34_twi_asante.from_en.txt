Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka yi yɛ nokware)

NEA ENTI A ONIPA BAAKO NTI NKAE ONIPA BAAKO ASE A OFI MU BAE

Ɔbɔfoɔ bi na ɔwɔ hɔ mmerɛ bi. Ɔsɔre a na ɔtaa kɔ wuram kɔhwehwɛ aboa bi a ɔbɛkum no, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛnya bi adi, na watɔn bi nso. Enti da bi ɔkɔɔ kwaeɛ mu; ɔtee sɛ Kokotee, kwaeɛ mu prako no, refrɛ ne nua sɛ, "Kokotee Asamoa e!" Ɔno nso gyee so sɛ, "Aane, me nua, aane." Ɔno (prako a ɔdi kan no) kaa sɛ, "Afum adwuma berɛ no aduru, enti ma yɛnkɔ atomfoɔ no dan mu, na ɔnwene dadeɛ no na ɔnsɛe ano (mma yɛn ntwitwa adeɛ), na sɛ ɛba sɛ yɛretwa nnua bi a, yɛatumi atwa." ." Bere a Kokote frɛɛ ne nuanom no, ɔbɔfoɔ no butuu hɔ de ne ho huntee, na ɔtee nkɔmmɔ no nyinaa. Afei (Kookoote a odi kan no) nua no bisaa ne nua Kookoote no sɛ, "Na akuraa bɛn so na yɛnkɔ, na wɔmmɔ dade no mma yɛn?" Ɔbuae sɛ, "Yɛbɛkɔ akuraa a wɔfrɛ no Asubɔnten-Akyi no so." Na ɔkaa sɛ, "Da bɛn?" Ɔbuae sɛ, "Ɛdwoada." Ɔbɔfo no tee nhyehyɛe yi nyinaa, na ɔtuu kwan kɔɔ fie. Afei, bere a ɔbaa fie no, ɔbɔɔ Asutifi kurotia panyin no amanneɛ, a ɛne nea ɔtee bere a ɔkɔɔ kwae mu no. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ akuraa no so Panin sɛ, "Ma mmofra no nkɔtwitwa nnyina mfa mmra, na Dwoada, sɛ Nkukuo no sakra wɔn ho (bɛyɛ nnipa) na wɔba a, yɛbɛfa wɔn na yɛde wɔn akyekyere nnyina no ho." Mmofra no kɔtwitwaa nnyina no, na wɔde baeɛ. Akuraa no so Panin no kɔka kyerɛɛ akuraa no so dadefwɛfoɔ no sɛ ɔmmɔ dade kɔntɔn mma no. Na atomfoɔ no bisaa Panin no sɛ, “Na dadeɛ nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn dodoɔ bebrebe yi nyinaa a woreka sɛ memmɔ no, wobɛde ayɛ deɛn?” Na ɔka kyerɛɛ no asɛm no, sɛdeɛ ɔbɔfoɔ bi kɔɔ kwaeɛ mu, na ɔba bɛbɔɔ amanneɛ sɛ Ɛdwoada bi mmoa bi bɛdane wɔn ho nnipa, ama wɔaba ne tumpan ano abɛma wabɔ nnoɔma ama wɔn. Otumpofoɔ no tuu mmirika kɔbobɔɔ dadeɛ nkabomdeɛ no ntɛmntɛm. Ntɛm ara a ɔhene no de ne nsa kaa nnyina no ne nkawa no wieiɛ pɛ, ɔmaa ɔdawurubɔfoɔ bɔɔ dawuro sɛ, “Ɛdwoada, sɛ ɛyɛ ɔbaa oo, anaa sɛ ɛyɛ ɔbarima oo, obiara nnsɛ sɛ ɔkɔ baabiara.” Ɛdwoada duruiɛ, na anɔpa no abrewa bi ka kyerɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no sɛ, “Kɔ na kɔyam mako, ne nkyene, ne gyeene, na faako a mmoa no bɛworɔ wɔn nwoma agu hɔ no; wo nso enti, sɛ wokɔ hɔ a, fa mako no na fa sra ho, na sɛ mmoa no ba ha, na sɛ yɛkyere wɔn, na sɛ ebi guane kɔfa wɔn wedeɛ, na wɔfira a, sɛ mako no ya wɔn, na wɔto gu a, wɔbɛdane nipa (bio).” Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔeɛ na ɔkɔhuntaa hɔ. Na ɔtee sɛ wɔrefrɛ sɛ, “Kokotee Asamoa, Ɛdwoada aduru, ma yɛnkɔ.” Enti wɔn nyinaa baeɛ. Wɔworɔɔ wɔn nwoma no, na wɔde guu hɔ. Afei, (Mprako no baako,) a na ɔyɛ adunsini no ka wɔn ho, na ɔfaa ne nwoma no kɔtoo baabi foforo. Ɔbɔfɔ no hwɛɛ wɔn, na ɔmaa wɔkɔe. Ɔkɔfaa nwoma no nyinaa de mako a na wɔayam no twitwii ho, na ɔfaa kɔtoo asuten no mu, maa nsuo no fa kɔe. Nanso dunsini no deɛ a na ɔde asi nkyɛn afiri nkaeɛ no ho no, ɔbɔfɔ no anhunu. Ɔbɔfɔ no baa fie, na Ɔhene no nso frɛɛ nnipa a na wɔaboaboa wɔn ho ano wɔ atomfoɔ no dadebɔbea hɔ no na ɔmaa wɔbaa ne fie. Berɛ a saa nkorɔfoɔ yi baa Ɔhene no fie no, wɔbisaa Ɔhene no sɛ, “Adɛn nti na woafrɛ yɛn?” Ɔhene no kaa sɛ, “Ná moyɛ m’amansofoɔ, na motwee mo ho kɔtenaa faako baabi; ɛnnɛ moasan aba, ɛno nti na mese ɛsɛ sɛ moba, efisɛ meremma mo kwan bio sɛ monkɔ.” Mmoa no ka kyerɛɛ Ɔhene no sɛ, “Deɛ woaka no, yɛate; nanso yɛnim sɛ Ɔbɔadeɛ no hu nter bae bɛkāa yɛn ho asɛm nyinaa kyerɛɛ mo, nanso ɛno mfa ho, yɛne mo bɛtena faako; nanso yɛnim sɛ deɛ moreka no yɛ atorɔ; nanso, ɛno mfa ho; yɛda ɔbɔfoɔ no ase. Enti yɛne mo bɛtena; nanso biribi baako na yɛkyiri, ɛne sɛ mobɛda baabi a yɛfiri adi; anaa sɛ w’asomfoɔ no mu biara bɛda baabi a yɛfiri adi; sɛ ɛba saa a, yɛbɛsɛe w’abusuakuo yi, na yɛafiri hɔ akɔ.” Afei, berɛ a Ɔhene no kɔfrɛɛ wɔn no, dunsini no ne ebinom guanee. Na ɔdunsini no kɔfaa ne honam na ɔdwanee; nanso nkaeɛ no sane kɔɔ akyi. Ɛno ne sɛ wɔantumi anhunu wɔn honam no. Ɔhene no penee nsɛm a mmoa no de too hɔ no so, na wɔne mmoa no tenaa faako anigyeɛ so. Afei akuraa no so mmarima no waree mmoa no mmaa no bi; wɔwoow mma. Afei, da koro bi aboa no mu baako ne akurasefoɔ no mu baako de wɔn nsa kɔntɔnkrɔn gyinaa hɔ reko. Ɛnna akuraasebɔfoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ aboa no sɛ, “Firi hɔ kɔ, aboa te sɛ wo a wobɔ Kokote asafo.” Ɔkaa saa ara pɛ na mmoa no nyinaa ani beree kɔɔɔ, na wɔkɔɔ Ɔhene fie kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Nea yɛka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ ebetumi asi no mprempren asi ampa, enti dɛn na worebɛyɛ?” Ɔhene no maa wɔkɔfrɛɛ nkorɔfoɔ no hwan na ɔde manso no baeɛ. Ɔhene no hwehwɛɛ asɛm no mu yie na ɔbuu atɛn sɛ mmoa no nnyae asɛm no, ɛfiri sɛ (ɔkaa sɛ) ɛyɛ berɛ tenten a wɔbaeɛ, na nea ɛka ho bio no, yei ne berɛ a edi kan koraa a obi akasa akyerɛ wɔn (fa baabi a wɔfiri ho). Nanso mmoa no kaa sɛ, “Yɛrempene so.” Na Ɔhene no kaa sɛ, “Morentie, na mosusu sɛ deɛ ɔbarima yi kaeɛ no yɛ atorɔ, monyɛ Kokote anaa?” Na mmoa no kaa sɛ, “O, yɛate.” Ɛnna mmoa no ne Asutwarefoɔ no kɔkoe. Mmoa no sɛee akuraa no, kɔsii sɛ nnipa bɛyɛ edu pefee na aka. Na wɔsrɛɛ ahummɔbɔ na wɔka kyerɛɛ mmoa no sɛ wɔn (mmoa no) na asɛm no yɛ wɔn deɛ. Mmoa no tieeɛ, na wɔbɔɔ nnipa no amanneɛ sɛ, “Asɛm a wɔaka ato hɔ dedaw no nnyɛ den sɛ wɔbɛte aseɛ, na sɛ me ne mo bɛtena baabi a, yɛkyi sɛ obi bɛbɔ yɛn asehyɛeɛ din biara. Sɛ mobɛdwene ho anaasɛ mobɛbɔ ho aso bio a, ɛnde yɛbɛka akyerɛ mo sɛ monkyerɛ yɛn kwae tenten koro no ara a yɛnam so baa ha no, na ama yɛasan akɔ hɔ bio.” Na nkorɔfo no kae sɛ, “Yɛrentumi nyɛ biribi a ɛte saa da.” Deɛ yɛayɛ yi ama yɛn abusuakuo asɛe; yɛrenyɛ saa bio da.” Enti wɔmaa wɔbɔɔ dawuro guu bagua mu sɛ obiara nka obi foforɔ ase bio da, na anyɛ saa a deɛ wɔbɛda no adi no bɛma kuro no asɛe. M'anansesɛm a metooeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, ebi nkɔ na ebi mmra.
