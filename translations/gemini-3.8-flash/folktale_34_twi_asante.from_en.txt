Yɛnni aseɛ koraa, yɛnni aseɛ koraa, (sɛ deɛ yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokorɛ)

NEA ƐYƐE A ONIPA BAAKO NKA BEAE A ONIPA FOFORO FIRI BAE

Ɔbɔfoɔ bi na ɔwɔ hɔ kane. Sɛ ɔsɔre a, na ɔkɔ wuram kɔhwehwɛ aboa bi akum no, na wanya bi adi, na watɔn bi nso. Da koro bi ɔkɔɔ wuram; ɔtee sɛ Kokotee, kɔkɔte no, frɛɛ ne nua sɛ, "Kokotee Asamoa e!" Ɔno nso gyee so sɛ, "Aane, me nua, aane." Ɔno (kɔkɔte a ɔdi kan no) kaa sɛ, "Yɛn adwumayɛ berɛ (wɔ yɛn mfuw mu) aduru, enti ma yɛnkɔ atomfoɔ no tonso, na ɔntoto dadeɛ no na ɔnsew (yɛn nnwinnadeɛ a yɛde twitwa adeɛ ano), sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a, sɛ yɛbɛtwitwa nnua bi a, yɛbɛtumi atwitwa." Berɛ a Kɔkɔte no frɛɛ ne nuanom no, ɔbɔfoɔ no butuu fam hyehyɛɛ ne ho, na ɔtee nkɔmmɔ no nyinaa. Afei (kɔkɔte a ɔdi kan no) nua no bisaa ne nua Kɔkɔte no sɛ, "Na akuraa bɛn so na yɛbɛkɔ na wɔabɔ dadeɛ no ama yɛn?" Ɔgyee so sɛ, "Yɛbɛkɔ akuraa a wɔfrɛ no Asubɔnten-Akyi no so." Na ɔkaa sɛ, "Da bɛn?" Ɔgyee so sɛ, "Ɛdwoada." Ɔbɔfoɔ no tee nnhyehyɛeɛ yi nyinaa, na ɔtuu kwan kɔɔ fie. Afei, berɛ a ɔduruu fie no, ɔbɔɔ Asubɔnten-Akyi panin no amanneɛ, a ɛne nea ɔteeɛ berɛ a ɔkɔɔ wuram no. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ akuraa no so Panin no sɛ, "Ma mmofra no nkɔtwitwa nnua akɛseɛ mmra, na sɛ Ɛdwoada, Kɔkɔtefoɔ no dane wɔn ho (sɛ nnipa) ba a, yɛbɛfa wɔn na yɛde wɔn akyekyere nnua no ho." Mmofra no kɔtwitwaa nnua akɛseɛ no, na wɔde baeɛ. Akuraa no so Panin no kɔka kyerɛɛ akuraa no so tomfoɔ no sɛ ɔmmɔ dadeɛ kurukuruwa mma no. Na tomfoɔ no bisaa Panin no sɛ, "Na dadeɛ kurukuruwa bebree a wose memmɔ yi nyinaa, ɛdeɛn na wode rekɔyɛ?" Na ɔbɔɔ no amanneɛ, sɛdeɛ ɔbɔfoɔ bi kɔɔ wuram, na ɔba bɛbɔɔ amanneɛ sɛ Ɛdwoada mmoa bi bɛdane wɔn ho nnipa, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a wɔbɛba ne tonso abɔ nnwinnadeɛ. Tomfoɔ no tuu mmirika kɔbɔɔ dadeɛ kurukuruwa no ntɛm so. Ntɛm ara a ɔhene no wiee nsa a ɔde guu nnua ne dadeɛ no so no, ɔmaa dawubɔfoɔ bɔɔ dawuro sɛ, “Ɛdwoada, sɛ ɛyɛ ɔbaa o, sɛ ɛyɛ ɔbarima o, obiara nnkɔ baabiara.” Ɛdwoada duruiɛ, na anɔpa no aberewa bi ka kyerɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no sɛ, “Kɔyam mako, ne nkyene, ne gyeene, na faako a mmoa no bɛworɔ wɔn wedeɛ agu no; wo nso, sɛ wokɔ hɔ a, fa mako no sra ho, na sɛ mmoa no ba ha, na sɛ yɛkyere wɔn, na ebi guan kɔfa wɔn wedeɛ, na wɔfira a, sɛ mako no ya wɔn, na wɔto gu a, wɔbɛyɛ nnipa (bio).” Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔe na ɔkɔhuntaa hɔ. Na ɔtee sɛ wɔrefrɛ sɛ, “Kokotee Asamoa, Ɛdwoada aduru, momma yɛnkɔ.” Enti wɔn nyinaa bae. Wɔworɔɔ wɔn wedeɛ, na wɔde guu hɔ. Afei, (Kokootee no mu baako,) a ɔyɛ odunsini no ka wɔn ho, na ɔfaa ne wedeɛ de guu baabi foforo. Ɔbɔfoɔ no hwɛɛ wɔn, na ɔmaa wɔkɔe. Ɔkɔfaa wedeɛ no nyinaa na ɔde mako a wɔayam no srasraa ho, na ɔfaa ne nyinaa kɔto guu asubɔnten no mu, maa nsuo no fa kɔe. Nanso Odunsini no deɛ a ɔde too nkyɛn no, ɔbɔfoɔ no anhunu. Ɔbɔfoɔ no baa fie, na Ɔhene no nso frɛɛ nnipa a wɔahyia atwa atomfoɔ dadebɔbea no ho ahyia no maa wɔbaa ne fie. Berɛ a saa nnipa yi baa Ɔhene no fie no, wɔbisaa Ɔhene no sɛ, “Adɛn nti na woafrɛ yɛn?” Ɔhene no kaa sɛ, “Mona moyɛ me mmarima, na mogwanee (berɛ bi) kɔtenaa baabi foforo; ɛnnɛ moasan aba, ɛno nti na mese mommra, ɛfiri sɛ meremma mo kwan mma monnkɔ bio.” Mmoa no ka kyerɛɛ Ɔhene no sɛ, “Deɛ woaka no, yɛate; nanso yɛnim sɛ Ɔbɔadeɛ bɔfoɔ no na ɔbɛkaa yɛn ho asɛm nyinaa kyerɛɛ wo, nanso ɛnyɛ hwee, yɛne wo bɛtena; nanso yɛnim sɛ deɛ woreka no yɛ atorɔ; nanso ɛnyɛ hwee; yɛda saa bɔfoɔ no ase. Enti yɛne wo bɛtena; nanso ade baako pɛ na yɛkyiri, ɛne sɛ wobɛda yɛn ahyɛaseɛ adi; anaasɛ wo manfoɔ mu bi bɛda yɛn ahyɛaseɛ adi; sɛ ɛba saa a, yɛbɛsɛe wo man yi, na yɛafiri ha akɔ.” Afei, berɛ a Ɔhene no kɔfrɛɛ wɔn no, odunsini no ne afoforo bi dwanee. Na odunsini no kɔfaa ne wedeɛ na ɔdwanee; nanso nkaeɛ no sanee wɔn akyi. Ɛnam sɛ wɔanhu wɔn wedeɛ no nti. Ɔhene no penee nhyehyeɛ a mmoa no de too hɔ no so, na wɔne mmoa no tenaa faako anigyeɛ mu. Afei kuro no mu mmarima waree mmoa no mmaa no bi; wɔwoow mma. Ɛnna da bi, mmoa no mu baako ne akurasefoɔ no mu baako de wɔn nsa bɔɔ apaw. Ɛhɔ ara pɛ na akurase nipa no ka kyerɛɛ aboa no sɛ, “Firi hɔ kɔ, aboa a wote sɛ wo a wokɔfa Kokote kuo.” Ɔkaa saa pɛ na mmoa no nyinaa ani beree, na wɔkɔɔ Ɔhene no fi kɔbɔɔ no amanneɛ sɛ, “Adeɛ a yɛkaa ho asɛm kyerɛɛ wo no aba mu seesei, enti dɛn na worebɛyɛ?” Ɔhene no maa wɔkɔfrɛɛ nkorɔfoɔ a wɔkɔfaa manso no baeɛ no. Ɔhene no hwehwɛɛ asɛm no mu yie na ɔbuu atɛn sɛ mmoa no nnyaa asɛm no, efisɛ (ɔkaa sɛ) afei deɛ ɛkyɛe a wɔbaeɛ, na afei nso, ɛyɛ nea edi kan a obi aka asɛm bi akyerɛ wɔn (fa baabi a wɔfiri ho). Nanso mmoa no kaa sɛ, “Yɛrempene so.” Na Ɔhene no kaa sɛ, “Morentie, na mosusu sɛ deɛ ɔbarima yi kaeɛ no yɛ atorɔ, monyɛ Kokotefoɔ anaa?” Na mmoa no kaa sɛ, “O, yɛate.” Ɛhɔ ara pɛ na mmoa no ne Asubonten-agyafoɔ no koee. Mmoa no sɛee kuro no, kɔsii sɛ nipa bɛyɛ du pɛ na aka. Na wɔsrɛɛ mmɔborɔhunu na wɔka kyerɛɛ mmoa no sɛ wɔn (mmoa no) atɛn tia. Mmoa no tieeɛ, na wɔbɔɔ nkorɔfoɔ no amanneɛ sɛ, “Asɛm a wɔaka ato hɔ dedaw nnye den sɛ yɛbɛte aseɛ, na sɛ me ne mo bɛtena faako a, yɛnkyi biribiara a ɛfa baabi a yɛfiri ho asɛmka. Sɛ moadwene kɔ so anaasɛ mobɔ din (bio) a, ɛnde (yɛbɛka sɛ) monkyerɛ yɛn saa wirakwammɔne koro no ara a yɛfirii mu baa ha no, na yɛasan akɔ hɔ.” Na nkorɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Yɛrentumi nyɛ biribi a ɛte saa bio da. Deɛ yɛayɛ yi ama yɛn abusuakuo asɛe; yɛrenyɛ saa bio da.” Enti wɔhyɛɛ dawuro, bɔɔ odawuro no sɛ obiara nka baabi a obi firi ho asɛm da, na amanneɛbɔ no ansae kuro no. M’anansesɛm a metoeɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ o, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ o, momfa bi nyɛ nokorɛ, na nea aka no mommɔ me aboso (sɛ meka kyerɛɛ mo nti).
