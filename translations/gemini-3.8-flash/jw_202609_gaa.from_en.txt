Kɛ́ wɔkɛ e-shishitoo mlai kɛ e-nɔyeli he gbɛtsɔɔmɔi tsu nii lɛ, ebuɔ wɔhe kɛjɛɔ nifeemɔi kɛ shihilɛi babaoo ni baanyɛ eye wɔ hewalɛnamɔ lɛ awui lɛ ahe. (Abɛi 14:29, 30; 22:3; 2 Kor. 7:1) Kɛ̃lɛ, wɔkpɛ̃ɛɛ gbɛ akɛ ebaabu wɔhe kɛjɛ hewalɛnamɔ nɔ sane fɔŋ fɛɛ mɔ he amrɔ nɛɛ. Kɛ́ efee nakai lɛ, ekã shi faŋŋ akɛ Satan baaha amale lɛ aje kpo akɛ hebuu ni Yehowa kɛhaa wɔ lɛ pɛ hewɔ wɔtsuɔ e-gbɛianɔ nii lɛ. (Hiob 1:10, 11) Wɔle akɛ kɛtsɔ sane kpele ni kɔɔ e-gbɛ́i krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ hetsemɔ he lɛ hewɔ lɛ, Yehowa “ekɛ tsuishitoo babaoo etsi etoi anɔ” yɛ mɛi fɔji ni teɔ shi woɔ e-tsɔɔlɔi ni amɛyeɔ amɛ awui lɛ ahe. (Rom. 9:22) Kɛ̃lɛ, e-tsuishitoo yɛ sane ni jaaa nɛɛ he lɛ miiya naagbee. Yɛ be nɛɛ mli lɛ, ebaaye ebua wɔ ni wɔkpee kaai fɛɛ ni wɔkɛbaakpe lɛ anaa koni wɔbaanyɛ wɔbu wekukpitiŋkamɔ ni jara wa ni kã wɔ kɛ lɛ teŋ lɛ he.—1 Kor. 10:13; 1 Pet. 5:10.

MƐNI YEHOWA BAAFEE KƐBAA WƆHE YƐ WƆSƐƐ BE MLI?

18 Yɛ amanehulu kpeteŋkpele lɛ mli lɛ, Yehowa yaa nɔ ebu etsɔji lɛ ahe yɛ mumɔŋ. Ni helooŋ buɔ he nɔŋŋ hu? Kɛ́ jeŋmaji lɛ tutua etsɔji lɛ kwraa lɛ, Yehowa ŋmɛŋ gbɛ ni akpata ejálɔi lɛ ahiɛ akɛ kuo. (Ezek. 38:1, 2, 10-16) Enɛ baatsɔ Armagedon ta lɛ he, he ni Yehowa baaha etserɛbii—Yesu Kristo kɛ eŋwɛi asraafoi lɛ—atutua Nyɔŋmɔ henyɛlɔi lɛ. (Kpo. 17:12-14; 19:11, 14, 15) Mɛni Yehowa baafei kɛbu etsɔji lɛ ahe yɛ Armagedon mli?

16. Mɛni kaa Yehowa eŋmɛ gbɛ ni ejálɔi lɛ ekomɛi ekɛkpe, ni mɛni he wɔbaanyɛ wɔná nɔmimaa yɛ?

17. Mɛni hewɔ esaŋ akɛ wɔkpa gbɛ akɛ Yehowa baabu wɔhe kɛje hegbɔmɔtɔ gbeyeishemɔ fɛɛ nɔ mli yɛ be nɛɛ mli?

18. Mɛni Yehowa baafe kɛbu wɔhe yɛ wɔsɛɛ?

6 BUU MƆƆ

Kɛ́ Yesu kɛ e-ŋwɛi asraafoi lɛ tsu Nyɔŋmɔ kojomɔ lɛ he nii yɛ Harmagedon lɛ, Nyɔŋmɔ henyɛlɔi lɛ ateŋ mɔ ko mɔ ko efeŋ mɔ ni damɔ shi. . . . Shi yɛ nakai ta lɛ mli lɛ, Nyɔŋmɔ toikpoŋi ni jara wa lɛ ateŋ mɔ ko mɔ ko egboŋ yɛ E-henyɛlɔi lɛ adɛŋ

19 Ehe ehiaaa ni wɔshe jeŋ fɔŋ nɛɛ hiɛkpatɔɔ ni baa yɛ Harmagedon ta lɛ mli lɛ gbeyei. Mɛni hewɔ? Wɔkɛ wɔhiɛ fɔ̃ɔ Yehowa nɔ akɛ wɔ-webolaahe kɛ wɔ-mɔɔ ni wa. No hewɔ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔná nɔmimaa kwraa yɛ enɛ he: Kɛ́ Yesu kɛ eŋwɛi asraafoi lɛ tsu Nyɔŋmɔ kojomɔ lɛ he nii yɛ Harmagedon lɛ, Nyɔŋmɔ henyɛlɔi lɛ ateŋ mɔ ko mɔ ko ebaŋ shi. (Yer. 25:33; Zak. 14:12) Shi yɛ ta kpele nɛɛ mli lɛ, Nyɔŋmɔ toi ni jara wa lɛ ateŋ mɔ ko mɔ ko egboŋ yɛ ehe-nyɛlɔi—gbɔmɛi loo daimonioi—adɛŋ.—Luka 21:28; 2 Pet. 2:9.

20 Yɛ Harmagedon ta lɛ mli lɛ, lalafo lɛ wiemɔi ni ajɛ mumɔŋ awo eŋɔɔ lɛ baanyɛ aba mli kɛmɔ shi pɛpɛɛpɛ: “Akpe baagboi yɛ ogbɛjegba kɛ akpei nyɔŋma yɛ oninenaa; shi ebaagbɛlɛ bo. Ejaakɛ okɛ ohiŋmɛii baakwɛ kɛkɛ, kɛ mɛi fɔji anɔ nyɔmɔwoo baana. Ejaakɛ bo lɛ: Yehowa ji mishitɛrumɔhe! Okɛ Ŋwɛiŋwɛinyɔ lɛ efee ohiɛmɔhe.” (Lala 91:7-9) Kɛ́ Harmagedon je shishi pɛ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔkpa gbɛ akɛ Yehowa baabu etsui kɛ emumɔŋ gbɔmɔtso kɛ mumɔŋ buu kɛteke nɔ aha ewebii lɛ, jeee akɛ kuu kɛkɛ, shi moŋ akɛ aŋkroaŋkroi hu. Akɛni okɛɛ: “Yehowa ji mishitɛrumɔhe” hewɔ lɛ, Yehowa ewo shi akɛ: “Ebaagbɛlɛ bo.” No hewɔ lɛ, nyɛhaa wɔkɛ hekɛnɔfɔɔ akwɛa wɔsɛɛ gbɛ, yɛ bei mli ni wɔle akɛ wɔbaanyɛ wɔkɛ wɔhiɛ afɔ̃ Yehowa nɔ kɛmɔ shi kwraa kɛha hebuu.

19-20. Mɛni hewɔ eshɛɛɛ akɛ wɔshe Nyɔŋmɔ kojomɔ ni baaba yɛ Harmagedon lɛ he gbeyei? (Kwɛmɔ dade so mfoniri lɛ hu.)

BƆ NI A MAƆ NYƐ A BO YEOVA TOI KƐJƐ TSUI MU

Bɔ Ni Afeɔ Ni Anaa “Toigbɔlɔ Tsui”

“Ha otsɔɔlɔ lɛ tsui ni boɔ toi.”—1 MAŊ. 3:9.

YIBII srɔtoi ahewɔ mɛi boɔ mɛi ni yɔɔ hegbɛ lɛ atoi. Ekolɛ mɛi pii feɔ nakai ja akwɛ amɛ nɔŋŋ loo ja amɛmiisumɔ ni amɛtsi amɛhe kɛje toigbalamɔ he. Kɛ́ eba Nyɔŋmɔ toiboo he lɛ, mɛi komɛi feɔ amɛhe tamɔ mɛi ni boɔ toi koni amɛná gbɛ́i, tamɔ bɔ ni jamɔŋ hiɛnyielɔi ni yɔɔ Yesu beiaŋ lɛ fee lɛ. (Mat. 23:5) Shi kɛ̃lɛ, wɔboɔ Yehowa toi ejaakɛ wɔnaa sɛɛnamɔi ni yɔɔ e-gbɛtsɔɔmɔ lɛ mli. (Yes. 48:17, 18) Enɛ ji yiŋtoo kpakpa hewɔ ni esa akɛ wɔfee toiboo, shi wɔyɛ yiŋtoo ko ni he hiaa fe fɛɛ ni hewɔ ni esa akɛ wɔbo lɛ toi.

2 Yiŋtitsoo hewɔ ni wɔboɔ Yehowa toi ji akɛ wɔsumɔɔ lɛ kɛ wɔtsui fɛɛ. (Mat. 22:37, 38) Suɔmɔ nɛɛ kanyaa wɔ ni wɔfeɔ “toibolɔi kɛjɛɔ tsui mli.” (Rom. 6:17) Yehowa nɔ suɔmɔ kɛ “toiboo tsui” kanyaa wɔ ni wɔbɔ mɔdɛŋ fɛɛ ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔye ekitai lɛ anɔ, jeee beni mɛi komɛi kwɛɔ wɔ kɛkɛ loo beni wɔnaa sɛɛnamɔ ni yɔɔ nɔ ni wɔfeɔ lɛ mli amrɔ nɔŋŋ lɛ kɛkɛ, shi moŋ be fɛɛ be. Eji anɔkwale akɛ, wɔtsui ji lakatsui, no hewɔ lɛ jwɛŋmɔ kpakpa hiɛmɔ baanyɛ afee nɔ ko ni wa yɛ bei komɛi amli. Yɛ nikasemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaasusu Biblia mli sanebimɔi enyɔ komɛi ahe ni baanyɛ aye abua wɔ ni wɔkɛ toiboo tsui atsu nii. Klɛŋklɛŋ lɛ, wɔbaasusu be ni Yehowa kpɔ̃ Israelbii lɛ kɛjɛ Ejipt lɛ he. No sɛɛ lɛ, wɔbaasusu Yuda Maŋtsɛ Yehoshafat nɔkwɛmɔnɔ kpakpa lɛ he.

KLƐNGKLƐŊ TOIBƆƆ KƐ KƐKƐ NI ATSƆ ATUATSE

3 Dani Yehowa baajie Israelbii lɛ kɛjɛ Ejipt lɛ, ewo amɛ kitã ko ni kɛ amɛtohe baaka amɛtoiŋgbee lɛ.

1. Mɛni hewɔ mɛi baanyɛ abo mɛi ni yɔɔ hegbɛ lɛ toi?

2. Mɛni ji nɔ titri hewɔ ni wɔboɔ Yehowa toi lɛ?

3. Mɛni hewɔ ekolɛ famɔ ni Yehowa kɛha akɛ akɛ lá awo shinaa tsei lɛ ahe lɛ ka Israelbii lɛ atoibolɔmɔ? (Kwɛmɔ mfoniri lɛ hu.)

8

Israelbii lɛ bo Yehowa toi ekɔɔɔ he eko nɔ ni Miziribii lɛ baanyɛ afee

(Kwɛmɔ kuku 3)

Kɛkɔɔ nɔ ni baatsɔ nɛɛ, bɔ ni afee ni amɛyi aná wala kɛjɛ haomɔ ni ji nyɔŋma kɛ naagbee nɔ lɛ he lɛ, esa akɛ amɛgbe toogbantu loo abotia nuu ni amɛkɛ lá lɛ afee amɛshĩai lɛ agbónaatai lɛ ahe fĩi. (2 Mo. 12:5, 7) Yehowa baatsɔ shĩai ni akɛ lá lɛ efee nɔkadɔŋmɔ yɛ he lɛ ahe kɛtsɔ, ni ebaahere weku lɛ mli kromɔbi kɛ kooloo fɛɛ kromɔbi yiwala. Shi kɛbɛ nakai lɛ, ekolɛ ebaafee nɔ ni wa kɛha Israelbii lɛ akɛ amɛbaabo toi. Mɛni hewɔ? Ekolɛ amɛbaashe gbeyei akɛ Egeptbii lɛ baato amɛ najiaŋ, ejaakɛ Egeptbii lɛ buɔ toogbantuuji akɛ amɛji krɔŋkrɔŋ. (2 Mo. 8:25-27) Kɛbɛ nakai fɛɛ sɛɛ lɛ, Israelbii lɛ na he hiaa ni yɔɔ toiboo he lɛ, ni amɛfee “tamɔ bɔ pɛ ni Yehowa fã lɛ.” (2 Mo. 12:28) Yehowa jɔɔ amɛ kɛtsɔ amɛkromɔbii lɛ ayiwala ni ehere lɛ nɔ.

4 Nakai gbɛkɛ lɛ nɔŋŋ lɛ, Israelbii lɛ shi Ejipt. Klɛŋklɛŋ lɛ, Yehowa kanyiɛ amɛhiɛ kɛtee Ŋshɔ Tsuru lɛ kooyigbɛ naagbee lɛ, shi kɛkɛ ni eha amɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔi komɛi ni kpa adesai anilee mli kwraa. Ekɛɛ amɛ akɛ amɛtsɔmɔ kɛya wuoyigbɛ ni amɛbɔ asafoŋtsɛi yɛ ŋshɔ lɛ kɛ gɔji lɛ ateŋ. Yɛ nsaahe nɛɛ, efee tamɔ nɔ ni amɛkpee yɛ Farao asraafoi ni tiiu amɛ lɛ kɛ Ŋshɔ Tsuru lɛ teŋ. Shi kɛbɔ fɛɛ kɛba lɛ, Israelbii lɛ kpale amɛfee nɔ ni Yehowa fã amɛ kɛtsɔ etsulɔ Mose nɔ lɛ ekoŋŋ. (2 Mo. 14:1-4) Shi beni amɛna akɛ Ejiptbii lɛ miihe amɛ lɛ, “gbeyei mɔ amɛ naakpa” ni amɛfɔ̃ nɔ kɛshweshwe Mose po akɛ ekanyiɛ amɛhiɛ kɛmiiya amɛgbele mli! (2 Mo. 14:5-11) Mɛni hew amɛbɔi amɛkpɔ̃ŋmɔ akɛ amɛbo toi lɛ he hiamɔ? Ni mɛni Yehowa nu he yɛ subaŋ nɛɛ he?

5 Biblia lɛ kɛɛ akɛ, yɛ Ŋshɔ Tsuru lɛ naa lɛ, Israelbii lɛ “hiɛ esɔɔɔ [Yehowa] naakpɛɛ nitsumɔi” yɛ Ejipt loo “akai” ehe-kɛ-afɔleshĩi suɔmɔ lɛ. (Kanemɔ Lala 106:7 kɛ shishigbɛlɛtsɔɔmɔ.) Amɛna bɔ ni Yehowa bu amɛkromɔbii lɛ ahe yɛ haomɔ ni ji nyɔŋma lɛ mli. Shi amɛkɔɔɔ Yehowa suɔmɔ ni eyɔɔ kɛha amɛ lɛ mli tsɔ loo amɛhe amɛye akɛ ebaahere amɛwala ekoŋŋ. Akɛni Yehowa yɔse nɔ ni yɔɔ amɛtsui mli hewɔ lɛ, ekɛɛ akɛ gbɔmɛi lɛ “tse atua . . . yɛ Ŋshɔ Tsuru lɛ naa.” Eyɛ mli akɛ amɛnyiɛ Mose sɛɛ kɛtsɔ Ŋshɔ Tsuru lɛ mli moŋ, shi amɛtsakeee amɛsuban kɔkɔɔkɔ. Naagbee lɛ, Yehowa kpoo nakai gbɔmɛi fɛɛ

4. Mɛni gbɛtsɔɔmɔi ni yɔɔ srɔto Yehowa kɛha Israelbii lɛ be ni amɛjeɔ Mizraim lɛ?

5. Mɛni Israelbii lɛ nyɛɛɛ afee yɛ Ŋshɔ Tsuru lɛ naa? (Lala 106:7 kɛ shishigbɛlɛtsɔɔmɔ)

GƆBLƆBI 2026

9

Israelbii lɛ ayinɔ lɛ nɔ bii lɛ ni eŋmɛɛɛ amɛ gbɛ ni amɛbote Shiwoo Shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ. (Lala 106:21, 24-26) Mɛni wɔbaanyɛ wɔkase kɛjɛ nɔ ni ba amɛnɔ lɛ mli?

KASE NƆ́ KƆƆ ISRAELBI LƐI AKƆKƆBƆƆ NƆKWƐMƆNƆ LƐ HE

6 Klapatemɔ sanegbaa lɛ tsɔɔ akɛ ehe miihia ni wɔpɛi nibii ahewɔ ni wɔboɔ Yehowa kɛ mɛi ni ehala amɛ koni amɛnyiɛ ewebii lɛ ahiɛ lɛ toi lɛ mli. Ani wɔboɔ toi kɛ́ wɔna akɛ wɔbaaná nɔ ko amrɔ nɔŋŋ pɛ? Ekolɛ Israelbii lɛ ekomɛi kɛ toogwantenŋ lɛ lá lɛ fɔfɔ agbóji lɛ anɔ kɛkɛ koni amɛnáa amɛkrombihi lɛ ahewalɛ. Kɛfata he lɛ, ani wɔtoiboo lɛ damɔ nɔ ni mɛi krokomɛi feɔ lɛ nɔ? Hɔɔmɔ Gbi lɛ gbɛkɛ lɛ, Yehowa ha Israelbii lɛ “ná hefatalɔi yɛ Eŋiptbii lɛ ahiɛ.” (2 Mo. 12:36) Shi be ni Eŋiptbii lɛ tse amɛhiɛ amɛfɔ̃ amɛnɔ sɛɛ mli lɛ, Israelbii lɛ kpa Yehowa nɔ hemɔkɛyeli ni amɛtse atua. (2 Mo. 14:10-12) Kɛ́ wɔkɛ amɛsubaŋ lɛ to he lɛ, kɛ́ wɔyɛ toiboo tsui lɛ, ebaatsirɛ wɔ ni wɔkɛ wɔhiɛ afɔ̃ Yehowa kɛ mɛi ni ete shi kɛtsɔɔ amɛ nɔ koni amɛnyiɛ hiɛ lɛ nɔ. (Kanemɔ Yeremia 23:4.) Wɔbaanyiɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔ ni wɔnine shɛɔ nɔ lɛ sɛɛ kɛ́ wɔnuuu yiŋtoo hewɔ ni awo lolo mɔŋ loo kɛ́ nakai feemɔ ha mɛi ni nyɛɔ Yehowa lɛ amifɔ̃ wɔnɔ po. Mɛni wɔbaanyɛ wɔfee kɛtsi wɔhe kɛje tɔmɔi komei nɔŋŋ ni Israelbii lɛ fee lɛ ahe ni wɔná toiboo tsui?

7 Hã suɔmɔ ni oyɔɔ kɛha Yehowa lɛ mli awa. Kɛ́ wɔnaaa gbɛtsɔɔmɔ ni teokrasi lɛ kɛhãa lɛ he sɛɛ amrɔ nɔŋŋ loo wɔkɛ shihilɛi ni jara baakpe yɛ wɔtoigbalamɔ lɛ hewɔ lɛ, ebaanyɛ eka wɔtoigbalamɔ lɛ. Kɛ́ eba lɛ nakai

baa lɛ, esa akɛ wɔkai akɛ nɔ titri hewɔ ni wɔboɔ Yehowa toi ji akɛ wɔsumɔɔ lɛ ni wɔheɔ lɛ wɔyeɔ. (1 Yohane 5:3) Suɔmɔ ni wɔyɔɔ kɛha lɛ lɛ yeɔ buaɔ wɔ ni wɔtoɔ wɔtsui shi wɔmɛɔ lɛ, ni wɔkpɛlɛɔ nɔ akɛ ekolɛ ebaabi be dani wɔna wɔtoiŋwoo lɛ he sɛɛnamɔ kpakpa.—Jalɔ. 7:8; Mika 7:7.

8 Yɛ wɔ beiaŋ nɛɛ, Nyɔŋmɔ webii efɛ̃ɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ kpakpa yɛ toiboo mli kɛ́ amɛnaaa he sɛɛ nɔŋŋ po. Ákɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, be ni COVID-19 hela lɛ gbɛ eshwie shi lɛ, awo asafoi lɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔ akɛ amɛkɛ vidio nɔ kpee akpe. Asafoŋ onukpa ko yɛ Peru kai akɛ: “Kɛtsɔ shishijee mli lɛ, misusu akɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔ nɛɛ nyɛŋ atsu nii yɛ wɔ he lɛ.” Eyake enyɛɛ enɔ po lɛ, efĩa shi akɛ ebaabo Nyɔŋmɔ toi ni ekɛ ehe afɔ̃ enɔ. (Abɛi 3:5, 6) Mɛni jɛ mli ba? Ekɛɛ akɛ, “Mina bɔ ni Yehowa gowaa toiboo nɔ. Wɔ asafo lɛ mli waje ni kpee kpa ba amɛteŋ lolo.” Susumɔ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ kroko he okwɛ. Yɛ sɛɛ mli yɛ afi 2020 mli lɛ, gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ tsake gbɛtsɔɔmɔi ni kɔɔ maŋsɛɛ wiemɔ asafoi kɛ kuui ahe lɛ. Be ni nyɛminuu ko yɛ Japan le akɛ akuu ni esɔmɔɔ yɛ mli lɛ baaba naagbee lɛ, ekɛɛ akɛ: “Miisumɔ ni mabo tsakemɔ nɛɛ toi ni mikashwie he wiemɔ gbohii. Miikwɛ he sɛɛ ni jɛŋ mli kɛbaaba lɛ gbɛ.”

9 Saa ohe kɛha te shi ni abaate shi awo bo. Enɛ he miihia titri kɛkɔ wɔ Maŋtsɛyeli shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ he. Yɛ je lɛ fãhi babaoo lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔshiɛ yɛ hejɔlɛ mli. Kɛbɔ ni eji lɛ po lɛ, wɔ shiɛ-

6. Mɛni toiboo tsui baaye abua wɔ ni wɔfee? (Yeremia 23:4)

7. Mɛni hewɔ kɛ́ wɔhã suɔmɔ ni wɔyɔɔ kɛhã Yehowa lɛ mli wa lɛ, ebaaye ebua wɔ ni wɔbo lɛ toi?

8. Mɛni Odasefoi enyɔ komɛi fee kɛtsɔɔ akɛ amɛtswa amɛfai shi akɛ amɛbaabo toi?

9. Mɛni wɔbaanyɛ wɔfee koni wɔfee toigbolɔi kɛ́ ate shi awo wɔ po? (Kwɛmɔ mfoniri lɛ hu.)

10 BUU-MƆƆ LƐ

Nyɔŋmɔ Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ he sane kpakpa lɛ shiɛmɔ biɔ ni wɔgba mɛi krokomɛi akɛ ebe kukuoo nɛɛ nibii agbɛjianɔtoo fɔŋ nɛɛ baaba naagbee. Wɔyaanɔ wɔshiɛ sane nɛɛ aahu kɛyashi be kukuoo dani nibii agbɛjianɔtoo nɛɛ baaba naagbee yɛ Harmagedon. (Mat. 24:14) Kɛfata he lɛ, yɛ amanehulu kpele lɛ mli lɛ, sane ni wɔshiɛɔ lɛ baanyɛ afee nɔ ni mli wa ni tsɔɔ gbee kpaako, ni eha mɛi amɔ yiŋ kɛji amɛbaakpɛlɛ Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ nɔ loo amɛkpɛlɛŋ nɔ. “Hii nyɔŋma ni jɛ jeŋmaji lɛ ateŋ wiemɔi fɛɛ mli lɛ baamɔ . . . Yudafo ko atade he.”—ZAK. 8:23.

JEHOVAH futu Babel Mɔɔ lɛ matselɔi atuatsemɛi lɛ anajiashi kɛtsɔ amɛwiemɔ lɛ ni ewo mli basabasa lɛ nɔ. Enɛ ha adesai agbɛ amɛhe ashwã kɛyashɛ wiemɔ kui amli, ni amɛgbɛ amɛshwã shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ fɛɛ. (1 Mose 11:6-9) Shi eji Jehovah yiŋtoo ni ewo he shi akɛ ebaafee “asafo babaoo . . . ni jɛ jeŋmaji kɛ wekui kɛ maŋbii kɛ wiemɔi fɛɛ amli” ekome, ni ekanyiɛ amɛhiɛ kɛtsɔ amanehulu kpeteŋkpele lɛ mli kɛya jeŋ hee lɛ mli. (Kpo. 7:9, shishigbɛl. w., 10, 14) Asafo babaoo nɛɛ baanyɛ atsɛ Jehovah gbɛi lɛ yɛ amanehulu kpeteŋkpele lɛ mli ejaakɛ Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ he sane kpakpa lɛ eshɛ amɛŋɔɔ yɛ wiemɔ ni amɛnuɔ shishi mli. (Kanemɔ Romabii 10:13, 14.) Mɛɛ hegbɛ kpeteŋkpele po wɔyɔɔ akɛ wɔbaaye wɔbua yɛ nitsumɔ ni herɔ yiwala nɛɛ mli!

Yudafo ko atade he shiŋŋ, ni amɛbaakɛɛ: “Wɔmiisumɔ ni wɔkɛ nyɛ aya, ejaakɛ wɔnu akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ yɛ nyɛ gbɛfaŋ.”’”

Ekɛɛ: "Daa gbi ni mate shi lɛ, miyɔseɔ akɛ miyɛ shihilɛ kpakpa ko—ni ehi fe shihilɛ ni miyɛ tsutsu lɛ kwraa. Enɛ ji jɔɔmɔ ko ni haa minuɔ he akɛ miyɛ nibii babaoo diɛŋtsɛ."

17 Mɛni he wɔsusuko yɛ nikasemɔ nɛɛ mli? Eka shi faŋŋ akɛ, esa akɛ wɔkwa je nɛɛ mumɔ lɛ. Enɛ biɔ ni wɔtsi wɔhe kɛje wiemɔ gbonyo he, wɔkwa bɔlɛnamɔ he jeŋba sha, ni wɔkwa oshara ni yɔɔ intanɛt nɔ lɛ. BIBLIA MLI WIEMƆ

Hã Kristo Lɛ Hejɔlɛ Lɛ Aye Nyɛtsuii Lɛ Anɔ Nyɔŋ

Kolosebii 3:15 tsĩĩ “Kristo hejõlemɔ lɛ” tã. Mɛni ji “Kristo hejõlemɔ lɛ”? Enɛ kɔɔ mlĩ hejɔlɛ nĩ mɔ ko baanyɛ aná akɛ Kristo kaselɔ lɛ he. Nakai hejõlemɔ nĩ tamɔ nɛkɛ lɛ jɛɔ he nĩ wɔleɔ akɛ Yehowa kɛ e-Bi lɛ sumɔɔ wɔ lɛ mlĩ.—Yohane 14:23, 27.

Bɔfo Paulo wo Kristofoi ni yɔɔ Kolose asafo lɛ mli lɛ hewalɛ ni amɛhã hejɔlɛ nɛɛ aye amɛtsuii, aloo amɛmli gbɔmɔ lɛ nɔ. Mɛni hewɔ? Hela wiemɔ ni Paulo kɛtsu nii yɛ biɛ kɛhã “ye nɔ” lɛ kɛ wiemɔ ni damɔ shi kɛhã kojolɔ yɛ feemɔ he ko lɛ yɛ tsakpãa. Ani enɛ etsɔɔɔ akɛ esa akɛ “Kristo hejɔlɛ lɛ” aye amɛtsuii anɔ naagbee sane?

Ákɛ nɔkwɛmɔ nɔ lɛ, kɛ́ sane ko te shi yɛ wɔ kɛ nyɛminuu ko teŋ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔha “Kristo lɛ hejɔlɛ lɛ” afee “kɔjɔlɔ.” Enɛ baaye abua wɔ ni wɔtsɔɔ nɔ ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔfee kɛkɛŋ hejɔlɛ kɛ ekomefeemɔ yi. Kɛ́ wɔnyiɛ nakai gbɛ lɛ nɔ, kɛ́ ebiɔ po ni wɔŋmɛɛ nɔ ni wɔ diɛŋtsɛ wɔsumɔɔ loo nɔ ni efee wɔ ni edɔ wɔ lɛ he lɛ, wɔtsɔɔ akɛ wɔhaŋ “Kristo lɛ hejɔlɛ lɛ” aye nɔ yɛ wɔtsuiiaŋ. —Kol. 3:12-14.

Akɛ wolo nɛɛ haaa koni ahɔɔ. Eji jeŋ muu fɛɛ Biblia kasemɔ nitsumɔ ni afeɔ kɛtsɔɔ suɔmɔ mli onai anɔ lɛ fã kome. Kɛ́ oomiisumɔ ni ofee onai lɛ, ofainɛ yaa donate.jw.org.
