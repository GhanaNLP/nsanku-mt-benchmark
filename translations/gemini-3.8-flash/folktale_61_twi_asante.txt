THEY SAY that Kuntukurunku and Kuntunamoa fell down from a scaffold to the ground. They said, "Earth, you are wide." Earth said, "Why do you say I am wide?" They said, "Because you do not dig a trench for us to sleep in." Earth said to Broom, "Your mouth is filthy." He said, "Why do you insult me?" Earth said, "If I have insulted you, I have insulted you, and if you do not understand, let it be." Broom said to Pot, "You, take-and-pour-away." Pot said, "Why do you insult me?" Broom said, "I have insulted you." Pot also said to Dunghill, "You, receiver of filth." Dunghill said, "Why do you insult me?" He said, "I have insulted you, and if you do not understand, let it be." Dunghill also said to Palm Tree, "You, bearer of a hairy neck." Palm Tree said, "Why do you insult me?" Dunghill said, "I have insulted you, and if you do not understand, let it be." Palm Tree also said to Ant, "Your black ear." Ant said, "Why do you insult me?" Palm Tree said, "I have insulted you, and if you do not understand, let it be." Ant also went to Elephant, entered inside his foot, and it caused Elephant pain. And as he lifted his foot, he stepped beneath Snail's eye, and from beneath Snail's eye water flowed out, and he went and put it on the spinach. The food that the Old Woman eats is spinach, and Snail took his eye-water and put it on it. Dog was passing by, and she called Dog and loaded fire onto him. As Dog went, he found two split cola nuts and went and put them at the entrance of Crab's hole. And Crab also took the cola nuts and threw them at the Silk Cotton Tree. The Silk Cotton Tree also became angry. He looked and saw a child passing there, and he shed his branch and struck him, killing him. It turned out the child was the child of God. Then right there they caught the Silk Cotton Tree and took him before God. They asked him, "What is the matter?" The Silk Cotton Tree said, "I was standing at my place, and Crab, a little child like that, picked up cola nuts and threw them at me, and I also, seeing this child passing by, thought it was Crab, and I shed my branch and struck him, and it turned out to be your child." God said, "Take him and put him in the stocks, and call Crab to come." Crab arrived; he said, "Grandfather, you know that my taboo is cola nuts, and Dog went and placed them at the mouth of my hole, and as I tried to throw them away, they went and struck Elder Silk Cotton Tree." God said, "Take him and put him in the stocks, and call Dog to come." They went and brought Dog. Dog said, "Grandfather, I was walking at my place, look how slender I am, and Old Woman loaded fire onto me; yet Crab, when Old Woman cleared her farm where she eats, it is he who goes to sleep in his hole in the farm, but it was I whom Old Woman loaded fire onto; that is why I also placed cola nuts at the mouth of his hole." God said, "Take him and put him in the stocks, and go call Old Woman to come." Old Woman arrived. She said, "Oh! As for me, my grandchildren, look in my mouth, I cannot chew meat, it is spinach that I eat; I also wash in the river Pra; I taboo Snail; only Dog knew this, and he spoke to Snail so that he went and put his spit on the spinach that I eat. That is why I loaded fire onto him." God said, "Oh!" put him in the stocks, and go bring Tamiriwa to come." Tamiriwa came. He said, "Nana, I was lying in my place, look, this huge Esono came and stepped beneath my eye, and I went to take leaves to wipe it off. And that is all I know about it." Nyame said, "Put him in the stocks, and go bring Esono." They went and brought Esono. Esono said, "Recently while I was sitting there, my skin was itching me; when I looked, with all due respect, it was this foolish Tetea who had entered inside me saying he wanted meat to eat so that he would become big; and as I meant to lift my foot, it went and stepped on Tamiriwa." Nyame said, "Put him in the stocks and go bring Tetea." When Tetea came, he said, "Nana, as Abe is there, when it bears fruit, it becomes black; but when I, Tetea, pass over it, it ripens. The other day, while I was there, Abe told me that my ears were black, and I was small, so I also said I would go to Esono to look for meat to eat so that I would become big." Nyame said, "Put him in the stocks, and go call Abe to come." Abe came; he said, "Nana, my matter is this: I and Sumina were seated, when the rubbish was brought to him, and if he could not contain it, I received some around my neck; now he insulted me that there was rubbish on my neck. That is why I also became angry, and I insulted Tetea." Nyame said, "Put him in the stocks, and go call Sumina." Sumina came; he said, "Nana, when the rubbish in the house is swept, it is given to Abena, and he also brings it to me. The other day I had not done anything to him, and he said, 'Sumina, you receiver-of-waste'; I said 'ah', and Abe was also there, so I insulted him." Nyame said, "Put him in the stocks and go call Abena." Abena came; he said, "Nana, 'Prae sweeps the house and if he does not bring it to me, and if the wind blows, the rubbish comes back again; the other day I did not do anything to him, and he said, 'you receiver-of-refuse'. That is why I also became angry and went to insult Sumina." Nyame said, "Put him in the stocks, and go call 'Prae to come." 'Prae came; he said, "Nana, Asase lies there wide and flat; when it becomes dirty and I rise up, I sweep away all the filth from it, and it becomes clean; the other day Asase told me, 'your mouth is filthy', so I also got angry, and I went to insult Abena." Nyame said, "Put him in the stocks, and go call Asase to come." When Asase came, he said, "Nana, I lie there; they walk on me; I say nothing; they do all things on me, I say nothing. Now the other day while I was there, Kuntukurunku and Kuntunamoa came and fell upon me, and I said, 'oh! why?' they said, 'you do not dig pits for us to sleep in', and 'you have just become wide and flat for nothing'. That is why I also insulted 'Prae." Nyame said, "Put him in the stocks, and go bring Kuntukurunku and Kuntunamoa." Kuntukurunku and Kuntunamoa came. They said, "Nana, we were on the shed playing, and we fell from it to the ground and hurt ourselves, that is why we insulted the Earth, calling it flat." God said, "Elders, we have heard all these matters, so let us rise and go into the room to deliberate on it, and then come back to answer." And they rose, went into the hall, and shut the doors. That is to say, as when you go into a room at night, it is dark. And God and his elders were there consulting together.
