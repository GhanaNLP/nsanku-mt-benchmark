HOW THE MONKEY'S TAIL BECAME WHITE

It is said that Elephant, Monkey, Black-and-white Colobus, and Diana Monkey were there; and they decided to go to a certain town to seek women for marriage. So they set off. As they went, they came upon some mud. Black-and-white Colobus said, “You all stop here so I can ask something.” He asked, “On this journey that we are making, who is the most handsome?” Elephant said, “It is Diana Monkey.” Monkey said, “If that is how it is, let us beat him up and throw him into the mud.” Truly, they beat him, threw him into the mud, and went on their way. Then Diana Monkey quietly followed behind them slowly, slowly, and went to sit on a dunghill. When those people arrived in the town, they saw the young women gathered under the shade tree. The young women saw them. This one said, “I want this one”; that one said, “I want that one.” And those people married all the women. There was one young maiden who had gone to the farm, and she told her mother, “When the young men arrive, choose one for me.” When she returned from the farm, she asked her mother, “Where is mine?” She said, “When I went, they were already taken.” The maiden said, “It doesn’t matter.” Then she peeled plantains and said she was going to throw the peelings onto the dunghill. Meanwhile, Diana Monkey had arrived and was sitting on the dunghill. As the maiden was about to throw the rubbish, Diana Monkey said, “Do not throw it on me.” The maiden asked, “Who are you?” He said, “It is me.” The maiden asked, “What is the matter?” He said, “I came with my companions to seek wives, but they beat me and made me very, very dirty, and that is why I have come to sit here.” She said, “As for me, I like you, so come, let us go home.” She went with him, fetched water, and bathed him. As soon as she applied the sponge to him a little, she looked at him and saw that he was shining brilliantly! The maiden said, “Mother, come and see; his beauty is no joke.” And her mother said, “Finish bathing him.” So she finished bathing him; she rubbed oil on him, gave him sandals, and he took his harp, went to sit under the shade tree, and played it. As soon as the women who were already married saw him, this one said, “I do not want my husband anymore,” and that one said, “I do not want my husband anymore; it is Diana Monkey that I want.” Diana Monkey said, “It is the one I have got that I want.” Then the animals took their leave, saying they were going to get ready and return. But when the animals left, they did not go home; they went to wait on the road for Diana Monkey so that when he came, they would beat him and kill him. Diana Monkey also took his leave, saying, “I am also going to prepare myself and return.” So he set off. When he reached the road, the animals saw him; they said, “Here is Diana Monkey! Here is Diana Monkey!” Then Diana Monkey climbed up a tree quickly! quickly! quickly! Then the animals took white clay and threw it at him, and some of it hit his tail. That is why his tail turned white; some hit his chin. That is why his chin is white. In the past, Diana Monkey’s tail and his chin were completely black.

This story of mine that I have told, whether it is sweet or not sweet, take some away, and bring some back.
