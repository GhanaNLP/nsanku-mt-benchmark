MFIE ƆHA A ATWAM NO

“BERE no rebɛn a Onyankopɔn bɛyɛ din ama ne ho. Onyankopɔn bɛnya obi a ɔbɛyɛ n’adansefo wɔ asase so,” sɛnea The Watch Tower a ɛbaa January 1, 1926 kae no.

Bere a afe 1926 reba no, Yehowa—nam asafo no ti, Kristo so—maa emuu daa hɔ kɛse sɛ asɛnka adwuma no na ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ Kristoni asetena mu ade titiriw. Enti, “akoa nokwafo ne ɔbadwemma no” maa Onyankopɔn nkoa nhoma foforo, yɛɛ nsakrae wɔ asafo nhyiam ahorow no mu, na ɔmaa wɔtee asodi a Kristoni wɔ no ase yiye. (Mat. 24:45) Kristo hwɛe ma wonyaa adan ne nneɛma a wɔhia na de ayɛ asɛnka adwuma no.

NHOMA FOFORO BI NE NSESAE A WƆAYƐ WƆ ASAFO NHYIAMU MU

Afrhyimu no mu no, The Golden Age (a mprempren wɔfrɛ no Awake!) bɔɔ amanneɛ sɛ wɔayi nhoma foforo bi a wɔato din Deliverance adi. Na saa nhoma yi bɛhyɛ Studies in the Scriptures ananmu sɛ nhoma titiriw a wɔde di dwuma wɔ asɛnka adwuma no mu. Nea ɛbɛyɛ na wɔatumi ama nnipa dodow a wɔhwɛ kwan sɛ wɔbɛhwehwɛ bi no anya bi no, anuanom a wɔwɔ Bethel tɔɔ afiri foforo a wotumi de tintim nhoma 22 miniti biara mu. Bible Asuafo no de nhoma foforo no maa bere biara a wobenya hokwan.

Mfe pii no, na Bible Asuafo no de Studies in the Scriptures nhoma ahorow no na esua ade wɔ wɔn asafo nhyiam ase. Nanso efi May no, wofii ase susuw The Watch Tower mu nsɛm ho. Dɛn na efii mu bae?

“Bunker Hill, Illinois asafo ketewa no kyea mo,” saa na asafo bi kyerɛwee wɔ krataa bi a wɔde kɔmaa Onua Rutherford mu. “Kwasida biara yɛsua [nsɛm] a Asafo no ahyehyɛ ama saa nna no. Yɛda ase pii sɛ yɛn nyinaa tumi hu hann foforo no bere a ɛba no.”

“Hann foforo no,” anaa ntease a emu da hɔ a wonyae no, boaa Bible Asuafo no ma wɔde wɔn adwene sii asɛnka adwuma no so. Sɛ nhwɛso no, asɛm a n’asɛmti ne “Suban anaa Apam—Emu Nea Ɛwɔ He?” kyerɛkyerɛɛ mu sɛ “suban pa a obi benya,” a na ɛfa nneyɛe a obu ne ayamye wom a obi benya nkutoo ho no, maa ebinom buu nokware Kristofo adwuma titiriw no adewa. Asɛm no tuu fo sɛ: “Nea ɔdɔ Awurade no ɛsɛ sɛ . . . odi ne mmara so mprempren. . . . Yehowa se: ‘Mone m’adansefo sɛ mene Onyankopɔ.’ . . . Kristoni no bɛpɛ denneennen seesei sɛ obedi adanse sɛ Onyankopɔ odansefo kronkron.”

Asɛm foforo bi a wɔato din, “Afɔrebɔ ne Ɔsom,” sii so dua sɛ nokware Kristofo da ɔdɔ a wɔwɔ ma Yehowa adi denam nokware som a wɔde bɛma no so. Asɛm no hyɛɛ Bible Asuafo no nyinaa nkuran sɛ wɔmfa nsi, anibere, ne nsi mfa wɔn ho nhyɛ Awurade som no mu denneennen na “wɔnyɛ adansefo mma Awurade na wɔnka ne nkrasɛm nkyerɛ asase so aman ne nkurɔfo.”—Yes. 43:10, 12; Mat. 24:14.

NHYIAMPO MA ASƐMPA KAEKAE ADWUMA NO DA HO ADI KYERƐ PA ARA

May awiei no, Bible Students pii hyiae wɔ nhyiam kɛse bi ase wɔ London, England. Adwumayɛfo tew atua wɔ Britain maa ɛyɛɛ den sɛ wɔbɛbɔ aguadifo nhoma tintimfo paa, enti anuanom a wɔwɔ Brooklyn Bethel no yɛɛ adwuma traa bere a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔyɛ na ama wɔatumi de nhomawa a wɔato din The Standard for the People no bi 117,000 aka ho akɔ nhyiam kɛse no ase.

Memeneda, May 29 no, wɔde nhomawa no dii dwuma yiye. Nhyehyɛe a wɔyɛe maa nhyiam no da no hyɛɛ nkuran sɛ: “Wɔreto nsa afrɛ obiara a wahyira ne ho so a ɔwɔ nhyiam no ase sɛ ɔmfa ne ho nhyɛ Ɔhene no ne Ahenni no nkaebɔ mu. Kɔ Alexandra Palace ntɛm ara pɛ sɛnea wubetumi, na kɔbɔ wo ho amanneɛ wɔ Ɔsom Dwumadibea hɔ sɛ woasiesie wo ho ama asɛnka dwuma no.”

Wɔkyekyɛɛ nhoma ahorow bɔbor 100,000. July 15, 1926, The Watch Tower bɔɔ amanneɛ sɛ: “Na anuanom no ani agye paa. Wɔtee nka sɛ wɔayɛ nea wobetumi biara de adi Yehowa ahyɛde yi so: ‘Mone m’adansefo sɛ mene Onyankopɔn.’ ”

Adehyee kɛse no akyi, wɔ Royal Albert Hall no, Onua Rutherford maa ɔkasa a n’asɛmti ne “Nea Enti a Wiase Ahemman Retutu Akɔ Ase—Ano Aduru No.” Simma kakraa bi pɛ a wɔbuee apon no akyi no, nnipa 13,000 hyɛɛ asa no ma. Nnipadɔm a wɔannya kwan no tiee dwumadi no yiye denam akasafidie a wɔde sisii abɔnten no so.

Onua Rutherford kyerɛkyerɛɛ mu wɔ ne kasa no mu sɛ, wiase tumidi ahorow no a ɛhwee ase no fi sɛ wɔpoo Yehowa ne N’ahendni no. Onua Rutherford kɔɔ so kasa tiaa Aman Nkabom Kuw no na ɔhyɛɛ nkɔm sɛ ɛrenkɔ so ntena hɔ bio.

4 Aseda a obi nna no yɛ bɔne. Nnipa a wɔnni aseda taa bu nneɛma nyinaa sɛ ɛnyɛ. Bible ka sɛ: “Nea ɔrehu amane no nna nyinaa yɛ bɔne.” (Mmeb. 15:15) Ɛtɔ da a, yɛn nyinaa hyia sɔhwɛ ahorow a ebetumi ama yɛn abam abu. Si Wo Gyidi Wɔ Fapem A Ɛtim Hɔ So

BERE a wo ne Yehowa Adansefo suaa Bible no, dɛn na ɛma wogye dii sɛ woahu “gyidi baako” no—ɔkwan koro a Nyankopɔn penee so a wɔfa so som no? (Efe. 4:4-6) So ɔdɔ a wuhui wɔ Yehowa nkurɔfo ntam no na ɛtwetwe wo baa wɔn nkyɛn? So wɔn abrabɔ pa no kaa wo koma? Nnipa pii ba nokware no mu efisɛ Nyankopɔn ahyehyɛde no ma wɔte nka sɛ wɔwɔ ahobammɔ na wɔdɔ wɔn ankasa, na yɛte aseɛ sɛ ɛbɛtumi ayɛ saa. Ɔdɔ ne abrabɔ pa yɛ agyiraehyɛde a ɛda nokware som adi.—Yoh. 13:35; 1 Pet. 2:12.

Nanso sɛ wo nuanom gyidini bi fow wo anaa ɔkwan foforo bi so wantumi ankyerɛ wo dɔ nso ɛ? Anaa sɛ wo nuanom gyidini bi kɔbɔ bɔne a emu yɛ duru mu—mpo aniwude nneyɛe, sɛnea ɛbaa onipa bi so wɔ Korinto asafo a ɛwɔ afeha a edi kan no mu no nso ɛ? (1 Kor. 5:1) Sɛ wo gyidi gyina sɛnea afoforo nneyɛe ma wote nka nkutoo so a, ɛnde na ɛgyina fapem a entim so. Fapem bi a ɛyɛ den kɛse wɔ hɔ ma wo gyidi anaa?

GYIDIE HO FAPEM A ETIM PIM

Wubetumi ama wo gyidi atim yiye denam nokware atitiriw a woasua afi Bible mu no a wobɛma akyerɛkyerɛ wo mu kɔ akyiri no so. Ɛbɛyɛ dɛn na ɛno aboa? Susuw nhwɛso bi ho.

Bere koro bi, Yesu asuafo pii gyaee n’akyi di. Nsɛm a ɔka faa ne honam a wɔbɛwe ne ne mogya a wɔbɛnom ho no hyɛɛ wɔn kɔn. Wɔkaa sɛ: “Asɛm yi yɛ ahodwiriw.” Nea Yesu kae no haw wɔn ara ma enti wɔgyaee n’akyi di.—Yohane 6:56-60, 66.

Akyire yi, Yesu bisaa n’asomafo no sɛ: “Mo nso mopɛ sɛ mokɔ anaa?” Petro buaa sɛ: “Awurade, hwan nkyɛn na yɛnkɔ? Wowɔ nkwa a ɛnnim awiei nsɛm. Na yɛagye adi na yɛahu nso sɛ wone Onyankopɔn Kronkronni no.”—Yohane 6:67-69.

Dɛn na ɛmaa Petro ne asomafo nkae no yɛɛ soronko koraa wɔ wɔn a wɔsuntontonee no ho? Na wɔn gyidi gyina biribi a emu yɛ den koraa sen wɔn nkate so. Petro kae sɛ: “Yɛagye adi na yɛabehu sɛ wone Onyankopɔn Kronkronni no.” Ná saa anokwafo yi nim nea wogye di ne nea enti a wogye di. Bere a wɔde Yesu nsɛm ne ne nneyɛe totoodɛɛ nea na wonim wɔ Kyerɛwnsɛm no mu ho no, na wogye di koraa sɛ Yesu ne Mesia no. Ahotoso a na wɔwɔ wɔ saa nokware titiriw yi mu no maa wɔn gyidi timii bere a afoforo suntontonee wɔ asɛm a Yesu kae no ho no.

Dɛn na saa asɛm no kyerɛ yɛn? Nea yenim no betumi anya tumi kɛse asen sɛnea yɛte nka. Adɛn ntia? Efisɛ Bible nkyerɛkyerɛ—a nokware a yɛasua afa Onyankopɔn din, ne tirimpɔw, ne agyede no ho ka ho no—nsakra da. Ɛnte sɛ yɛn nkate, nea nkurɔfo yɛ anaa nea wɔka nka Bible mu nokware no.

Enti, sɛ yegye Bible mu nokware no di paa na yɛde nokware yi bata nneɛma a Yehowa nam so reyɛ adwuma no ho a, yebetumi akwati sɛ afoforo nneyɛe bɛto yɛn hintidua.

Ɛwom, anuanom dɔ no hyɛ yɛn den. Afei nso, yɛn mfɛfo gyidifo suban pa ma yɛn werɛ kyekye. Nanso sɛ nkurɔfo nneyɛe nti, yɛn abam bu mpo a, ahotoso a yɛwɔ wɔ Bible mu nokware no mu no betumi ahyɛ yɛn gyidi den. Ɛbɛma yɛahu sɛ yɛwɔ ahyehyɛde a ɛteɛ no mu—mfa ho nea nnipa kakraa bi bɛyɛ.

Afei nso, gye a yɛagye adi no bɛma yɛakɔ so anya awerɛhyem sɛ yɛahu ɔsom koro pɛ a Onyankopɔn pene so no. Ɛba saa a, yɛn gyidi begyina fapem a ɛyɛ den so, na afoforo nsɛm anaa wɔn nneyɛe renhinhim yɛn.—Dw. 16:8; hwɛ adaka a yɛato din “Kɔ So Nante Kwan Pa No So.”

Tena Kwanpa No So

Fa no sɛ woate bɔs bi a ɛretu kwan akwansin ɔhaha pii akɔ baabi a ɛhɔ yɛ fɛ pa ara. Wɔ saa akwantu tenten no mu no, ɛbɛyɛ sɛ wobɛte nka wɔ akwan ahodoɔ pii so—ɛfa kɛseɛ no ara bɛyɛ anigyeɛ a wobɛnya ɛsiane sɛ wowɔ akwantu no so nti.

Bere a wowɔ akwantu yi so no, nanso, nneɛma bi betumi aba a ɛbɛma wo bo afuw—ebia akwantufo mmienu ntam nkɔmmɔbɔ a emu yɛ den anaa kyerɛkyerɛkwanfo no nyeyɛ bɔne mpo. Nanso eyinom mu biara nsesa nokware a ɛwɔ hɔ sɛ wowɔ bɔs pa no so na ɛsɛ sɛ wotena so kɔpem sɛ wobɛduru baabi a worekɔ no.

Bere a yɛretu yɛn kwan akɔ Onyankopɔn wiase foforo no mu no, yɛbɛte nka wɔ akwan horow pii so—ne titiriw no, anigye a ɛbɛka yɛn ho sɛ yɛwɔ hokwan sɛ yɛka Onyankopɔn ahyehyɛde no ho. Nanso ɛtɔ da a, yɛn nuanom gyidifo betumi ahaw yɛn anaa obi a yɛhwɛ no kwan mpo bɔne a ɔbɛyɛ betumi abɔ yɛn hu. Nanso eyinom mu biara nsesa nokwasɛm a ɛyɛ sɛ yɛte bɔs a ɛfata no mu, sɛnea yebetumi aka no. Yɛwɔ ahyehyɛde a ɛteɛ no mu. Sɛ yɛwɔ Bible mu nokware atitiriw no ho nimdeɛ a edi mu—na yɛkyerɛ baabi a yenyaa saa nokware no ho anisɔ a—yɛbɛkɔ so atra kwan pa no so. Yɛn gyidi bɛkɔ so atim.

Sɛ yɛn gyidi gyina nea afoforo nneyɛe ma yɛte nka pɛ so a, ɛnde ɛgyina fapem a ɛnhyɛ dzen so

Yehowa Tua Gyidi So Akatua

Emma yɛɛ adwuma wɔ ahɔhodan bi mu nna mmiɛnsa wɔ dapɛn biara mu, nnɔnhwerew nkron da koro. Ɔbisaa ne panyin sɛ ɔmma no gye n’ahome da koro sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛtumi akɔ n’amansin nhyiam no bi. Sɛ anka ne panyin no bɛpene n’abisade no so no, ɔsesaa ne bere kɔɔ nna nnum wɔ dapɛn biara mu na ɔmaa no nsiho: Gye nhyehyɛe foforo no tom anaa gyae adwuma no. Esiane sɛ Emma de ne ho too Yehowa so nti, ɔpaw sɛ ɔbɛgyae adwuma no na wakɔ nhyiam no.

Bere a Emma kɔe sɛ ɔrekɔde ne gyinaesi a ɛfa adwuma a ɔregyae ho akɔto hɔ no, na hotɛl no panyin kɛse no wɔ hɔ saa bere no pɛpɛɛpɛ. Bere a ɔtee nsɛm no, ɔhyɛɛ bɔ sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ asɛm no so. Nanso, nnawɔtwe mmienu akyi no, wɔka kyerɛɛ Emma sɛ wɔatu no adwuma mu. Ɔhyiaa panyin kɛse no bio, na ne bo fui sɛ wɔbuu n'akwankyerɛ no animtiaa. Ɔsiesiee asɛm no, na wɔmaa Emma bere foforo a ɔde bɛyɛ adwuma: nna nnum nnawɔtwe biara, dɔnhwerew nnan da koro biara. Ɛbɛyɛɛ papa mpo sen ne bere a na ɔde yɛ adwuma kane no; na ɛma kwan ma ne teokrase dwumadie ahorow! Yehowa hwɛɛ ne nsaano mhiaadeɛ wɔ ɔkwan a ɛboro nea na ɔhwɛ kwan so.
