Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA KAKRA NO ENTIRI BƐYƐƐ KOKOO

Wɔka sɛna na obi wɔ hɔ a ɔyɛ Ɔhemaa, na ɔwoo abaayewa fɛfɛ bi. Na ɔwɔ hɔ; nanso sɛ wɔde no kɔma obi bi sɛ ɔnyɛ ayaw a, ɔka sɛ ɔmpɛ. Da biara sɛ ne nnipa kɔkasa ne no (fa ayaw ho) a, ɔka sɛ, “Ɛnyɛ saa koraa.” Na Onini no, ɔtena kuro bi mu wɔ kwae mu, na ɔtena ponkronom bi mu. Da koro bi a ɔwɔ hɔ (ne ntan hɔ) no, ɔtee abɔfo bi a wɔfa hɔ, na wɔredi nkɔmmɔ fa ɔbaa fɛfɛ no ho, sɛ sɛ wɔma obiara no wɔ ayawuo a, ɔka sɛ ɔnni no anigye. Ɔpɔn no da hɔ, na ɔka kyerɛ ne ho sɛ, “Mɛyɛ akokoa fɛfɛ bi, na makɔgye Ɔhemaa ba no ayɛ me yere.” Ampa, Ɔpɔn no yɛɛ akokoa; obiara ntumi nnya no anowase mmerɛ tenten. Ɔkɔɔ kuro no. Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, na ɔreka kɔkɔɔ. Berɛ a ɔduu kurom no ponkuo no, Nanaa baa no, a ɔkɔyɛɛ ntadeɛ no, na ɔaba na ɔrepa ntadeɛ no ma ɛmome. Python no tɔɔ nsuo, na ɛtɔɔ Nanaa baa no nsaatwa no so. Ɔhemaa baa no kaa sɛ, “Adɛn nti na wopue me mpaabo so?” Python no kaa sɛ, “Woyɛ pɛ (sɛ woka saa), Amu.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Me nton nkyia nnyɛ Amu.” Ɔyɛɛ no mmuae sɛ, “Woyɛ pɛ (sɛ woka saa), Eson.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Me nton nkyia nnyɛ Eson.” Ɔyɛɛ no mmuae sɛ, “Woyɛ pɛ (sɛ woka saa), me yere.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Enti m’awɔ w’asɔre.” Ɔhemaa baa no ne Python no hyɛɛ aseɛ ff ma ne fie. Ɛnɛ ara na Ɔhemaa ba kɔɔ ne nana hɔ kasee sɛ, “Eh! eh! eh! (na ɔreka kasa mmerɛ mmerɛ efisɛ anigye), meahu nea mepɛ.” Ɔhemaa see, “Akyirei!” “Me baakofoɔ wɔ he?” Ɔka sew, “Mewɔ ha, nana.” Nanaa ne Python na wɔnam nsa, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sew, “Mo ne wo yere nna anigyeɛ mu.” Berɛ a anwummere tɔɔ mu yɛɛ nwunu, ɔkɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn kɔdwene. Ɛyɛɛ saa nnawɔtwe nwɔtwe. Akyirei no, ɔseɛ, “Me yere, merekɔ me akurafoɔ hɔ nanso mɛsan aba.” Ne yere seɛ, “Ɛnna me ne wo bɛkɔ (bɔkɔɔ).” Python no seɛ, “Dabi.” Python no kasaeɛ, kasaeɛ (na ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkyerɛ no kwan sɛ ɔnkɔ), nanso Nanahemaa baa no amtee (ase). Berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ no, ɔne ne kunu no hyɛɛ aseɛ rekɔ. Berɛ a wɔnam kɔrɛ no, Python no bɔɔ kwan (firi kwan no so) kɔɔ kwae mu. Nanahemaa baa no kaa sɛ, “Dɛn na woreyɛ, eyi ne kwan no?” Python no kaa sɛ, “Wo nso, tura wo nan mu na susu me.” Wɔnommaa kɔe, na wɔkɔe, na (na akyirei) Python no nyekyere kɔɔ nsamanmu. Nanahemaa baa no nso kɔɔ mu. Mpofirim, Python no sesaa ne ho bɛyɛɛ mmoa kɛse; ɛnna ɔkaa asɛm kyerɛɛ Nanahemaa ba no sɛ, “Wei ne nea mede yɛ paa, efisɛ mesesaae me ho na makɔgye wo ayɛ me yere, efisɛ wo tenaa wo na ma wo papa nsam na woka sɛ, ‘Hwee nni hwee (a mɛsuro)'; seesei mɛkum wo; skra me ti na fa kraman no ma me; sɛ woyɛ ampa na wannyini bi a, wo awu.” ne aduane.” Nana Obaa no ba no tenaa ne nkyɛn, na ɔrekyere na ɔrehwɛ kɔkɔɔrɔ. Ɛna wɔnna, Nana Obaa no ba no huu sɛ Papaye bi renyina hɔ. Nana Obaa no ba no repɛ sɛ ɔbue ne nwam sɛ, "Papa Papaye," nanso Papaye no too ne nsa so. Papaye nofaa ne gun no, siesieee no, na ɔtoo (ne nsam) no ne dada so. Ɔpyton no see sɛ, "Krakra me ti." Nana Ba no see sɛ, "Mabrɛ sɛ mɛkra; sɛ wopɛ sɛ wum me a, wum me." Ɔpyton no repɛ sɛ ɔgyina ne ti ase na ɔkye Nana Ba no, na ɛnna Ɔpapayee no maa gun no yiee, pomm! Kpapara kɔɔ ntan koro mu kɔfaa Obaahemaa baa ne Awɔn kɔmaa hɔ kɔhyɛɛ hɔn nka sɛnea ɔhunuu Obaahemaa baa ne Awɔn no, na ɔkaaa kwan a ɔfaa so a ɔwuii Awɔn no. Obaahemaa baa no nso kaa ne nsɛm nyinaa fa asɛm no ho. Ɔhemaa kasae sɛ, "Kpapara, dɛn na mɛma wo sɛ mekyia wo?" Kpapara no kasae sɛ, "Nana, mimpɛ hwee, me nkoaa mpɛ palm-oil kakra." Ɔhemaa kɔtoo palm-oil bae. Ɔse sɛ, "Nana, mɛfa me ntade no atow palm-oil yi mu, na ama ɛyɛ adanse (deɛ ɛsi saa asɛm yi ho)." Saa na Kpapara ntade no bae sɛ ɛyɛ kɔkɔɔ. Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
