Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA KAKRA NO ENTIRI BƐYƐƐ KOKOO

Wɔka sɛ tete bi, na obi wɔ hɔ a ɔyɛ Nanahemaa, na ɔwoo abaayewa fɛfɛ bi. Na ɔwɔ hɔ; nanso sɛ wɔde no hyɛ obi mmaa mu a, ɔka sɛ ɔmpɛ sɛ ɔware. Da biara sɛ (ne) nnipa kɔkasa (ne ho, fa aware ho a), ɔka sɛ, “Ɛmpɛ saa koraa.” Na Onini no, na ɔwɔ kwae bi mu, na ɔtena ponkronom bi mu. Da bi a ɔwɔ hɔ (ne ponkronom mu no), ɔtee abɔfo bi a wɔfa hɔ kwan, na wɔreka abaayewa fɛfɛ no ho asɛm, sɛ sɛ wɔde no hyɛ obi mmaa mu a, ɔka sɛ ɔmpɛ no. Onini no da hɔ, na ɔka (ne ho) sɛ, “Mɛyɛ abarima akuma fɛfɛ bi, na makɔware Nanahemaa baa no.” Nokwarene, Onini no yɛɛ akuma; obi a ɔbɛhwɛ no mmerɛ tenten, ɛmpɛ saa. Ɔfaa kwan kɔɔ kuro no. Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, na ɔretu kola. Berɛ a ɔduu kuro no pon no, Nanahemaa baa no, a ɔkɔtwee ntadeɛ, na ɔaba na ɔretwa ntadeɛ no mmaa sɛ ɛmɔ. Onini no tui, na ɛtɔɔ Nanahemaa baa no mmua so. Nanahemaa baa no ka sɛ, “Adɛn nti na wutui mmua me so?” Onini no ka sɛ, “Wo na woyɛ nokware (sɛ woka saa), Amu.” Ɔka sɛ, “Me nton nkyerɛwee nyɛ Amu.” Ɔgyae sɛ, “Wo na woyɛ nokware (sɛ woka saa), Eson.” Ɔka sɛ, “Me nton nkyerɛwee nyɛ Eson.” Ɔgyae sɛ, “Wo na woyɛ nokware (sɛ woka saa), me yere.” Ɔka sɛ, “Afei maware wo.” Nanahemaa baa no ne Onini no faa kwan kɔɔ (ne) fie. Ɛno ara a Nanahemaa baa no duu hɔ, ɔkɔɔ ne maame nkyɛn na ɔka sɛ, “Eh! eh! eh! (na ɔreka asɛm no mmerɛ mmerɛ efisɛ anigyeɛ), mihu onipa a mepɛ.” Nanahemaa ka sɛ, “Afei na ɛba! me baakofo wɔ he?” Ɔka sɛ, “Me wɔ ha, nana.” Nanahemaa ne Onini no nnamnee, na ɔka kyerɛ no sɛ, “Mo ne mo yere nnom anigyeɛ wɔ mo nkwa mu.” Berɛ a anadwo tɔɔ mu yɛɛ nwunu, ɔkɔdae ne yere nkyɛn. Ɛyɛɛ saa nna nwɔtwe. Akyiri no ɔka sɛ, “Me yere, mrekɔ me kuro nanso mɛba.” Ne yere ka sɛ, “Afei me ne wo bɛkɔ (bom).” Onini no ka sɛ, “Dabi.” Onini no kasae, kasae (na ɔpɛ sɛ ɔka kyerɛ no sɛ ɔnkɔ), nanso Nanahemaa baa no ntie (no). Berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ no, ɔne ne kunu faa kwan. Berɛ a wɔrekɔ no, Onini no firi kwan no kɔɔ kwae mu. Ɔhemaa baa no kaa sɛ, “Dɛn na woreyɛ, woyɛ kwan yi?” Aberewa no kaa sɛ, “Wo nso, tura wo nan mu na susu me.” Wɔnommoa na wɔkɔe, na (aafoa) Aberewa no nyyɛe kwan kɔfaa nsɔre bi mu. Ɔhemaa baa no nso kɔyɛe. Mpofota ara, Aberewa no sesaee bɔɔne bi; na ɔkaa kyerɛ Ɔhemaa baa no sɛ, “Eyi ne dɛn a meyɛ ankasa, efisɛ mesesaee me ho na makyɛ wo, efisɛ wode wo ho tohye wo na wo kaa sɛ, ‘Hwee nni hɔ a mɛsu’; mɛkum wo seesei; kura me ti na fa kuku mma me; sɛ woyɛ amfa bi mma me a, wo aduane awie.” Ɔhemaa baa no tenaa ne nkyɛn, na ɔreka ne ti na ɔrehwɛ kuku. Ɛna wɔretena hɔ no, Ɔhemaa baa no huu sɛ Papaye bi renyina hɔ. Ɔhemaa baa no pɛɛ sɛ ɔbue ne nwam sɛ, "Papa Papaye," nanso Papaye no trutruee ne nwam. Papaye no faa ne gun, siesiee no, na ɔde (ne nkyɛn) tohye ne dada so. Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Kura me ti." Ɔhemaa baa no kaa sɛ, "Mabrɛ sɛ mɛkura; sɛ wopɛ sɛ wokum me a, kum me." Aberewa no pɛɛ sɛ ɔgyae ne ti na ɔkye Ɔhemaa baa no, na Papaye no maae gun no yiee, pomm! Papaye no kɔfaa nsɔre no mu kɔfaa Ɔhemaa baa no ne Aberewa no na ɔde wɔn kɔfaa fie kɔka amanneɛ sɛ sεnea ɔhuu Ɔhemaa baa no ne Aberewa no, na ɔkaaa kyerɛ wɔn sɛnea ɔkumee Aberewa no. Ɔhemaa baa no nso kaa ne nsɛm nyinaa. Ɔhemaa kaa sɛ, "Papaye, dɛn na mɛde mma wo sɛ meda wo ase?" Papaye no kaa sɛ, "Nana, mimpɛ hwee, me nkoaa mimpɛ sɛnkyɛ." Ɔhemaa kɔfaa sɛnkyɛ bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Nana, mɛde me ntade a ɛwɔ me nkyɛn no hyɛ sɛnkyɛ yi mu, na ama ɛyɛ adanse (deɛ ɛsi saa asɛm yi ho)." Ɛno ne sɛnea Papaye ntade no bɛyɛɛ kɔkɔɔ. Eyi, me nsɛm a mika yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi kɔ mfinimfini, na ma bi mma me.
