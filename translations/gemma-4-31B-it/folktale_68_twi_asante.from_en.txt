Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo ampa, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo ampa (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNE ANANSE, KƆKƆRƆ NO, BƐYƐƐ OHIƐ

KWAKU ANANSE, Kɔkɔre no, kɔɔ Nyankonpon, Wɔso-Nyame hɔ, na ɔsee no sɛ, “Ma me aburodua baako na me nso mefa nnipa kuro biara ama wo (ma yɛatumi ayɛ nsika).” Wɔso-Nyame no see no sɛ, “Wontumi.” Kɔkɔre no hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, “Sɛ me ntumi a, me tiri mfa wo nawo, Nsia, nti mɛde me ti bɛsɔ me ntamfo a me ayɛ no.” Wɔso-Nyame no see no sɛ, “Fa (aburodua no) na woa... “off.” Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ kuro bi mu. Ɔkaee ɔmanhene no asɛm yi. “Nyame atumfo me; ɔse, ɛsɛ sɛ mefa mmoa mmoa kɔkɔɔ baako yi kɔ beaeɛ bi, na sɛ mefi (beaeɛ biara) a, ɛsɛ sɛ mema mmoa mmoa kɔkɔɔ yi kɔda akoko hɔ.” Ɔmanhene no see, “E! “Sɛ akoko ne aburo da bam koro mu a, ɛbɛyɛ yie?” Ananse ayɛ adwene sɛ, “Nea ɛsɛ sɛ woyɛ ne sɛ woma wɔda bam koro mu.” Odikro no see no sɛ, “Kɔtoo hɔ.” Anɔpa a ɛba no, Ananse see no sɛ ɔrekɔ hwee no, nanso aburo (baako) no nni hɔ bio. Kwebla se, “Ɔmanhene, akoko no adidi Nyame aburo.” Ɔmanhene se, “Menntumi nni nsɛnnennen biara ho wɔ eyi ho, enti fa akoko no na kɔ.” Ananse kɔfaa akoko kɛse bi, na ɔfa no kɔɔ kuro foforo bi. Sɛ ɔduu hɔ no, ɔkaasɛm kyerɛɛ ɔmpanyimfo no sɛ, “Nyame na ɔde me baa ha, na mɛda ha, na saa akoko a mede kura yi nso mma no nna ne nguan no ho.” Ɔmpanyimfo no see sɛ, “Fa bra na fa hyɛ hɔ.” Anadwo no, Ananse kɔɔ nguan kura hɔ na ɔwui akoko no. Ɔfaa akoko no mpaebɔ no hyɛɛ ɔkraman kɛse bi nkaka ho. Anɔpa a ɛdi so no ntɛm, ɔkɔɔ ɔhene hɔ na ɔsee no sɛ, "Ma me akoko no." Berɛ a wɔrepɛ sɛ wɔkɔfa ama no no, ohwee, akoko no awu. Ananse see sɛ, "Ah! ɛyɛ Nyame akoko, a onuaba no akum no." Ɔhene no see sɛ, "Menntumi nni nsɛnnennen ho asɛm bi, enti fa onuaba baako na kɔ." Ananse faa onuaba no na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ. Ɔduu kuro foforo bi. Ɔkɔɔ ɔmpanyimfo no fie. Ɔmpanyimfo no bisaa no sɛ, "Kaeɛn?" Kwaku Ananse hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, "Nyame na ɔde me abrɛ, na mekura nnuaba no; wɔn ne nananim no bɛda abɔm." Ɔmpanyimfo no see sɛ, "Ho! "ma ɛnka mmoa no." Kwebla see, "Dabi, efisɛ ɛyɛ Onyame mmoa, ɛnka nananom no." Odikanning no see, "Kɔ hyɛ hɔ mpo a." Kwebla kɔhyɛɛ hɔ. Anadwo, Ananse kɔyɛɛ mmoa no mogyaa, na ɔhyɛɛ ne mogya wɔ nananom mu baako ti so. Anɔpa a ɛdi so no ntɛm, Ananse see, "Ɔmanhene, ma me me nguan no." Wɔn kɔɔ nguan-kuro no mu; ɛhɔ na nguan no hyɛe, wowi. Ananse see, "Ɔmanhene, Onyame nguan ne saa a nananom nnuane no awowi." Ɔmanhene see, "Menntumi nni wo ne me nkontum bi bi saa asɛm yi ho, enti fa nananom nnuane a awowi no no kɔ." Ananse faa nananom nnuane no, na ɔkɔɔ, na ɔduu kuro foforo bi. Ɔfaa ɔnam no kɔkyekyereɛ no hɔ, na ɔkɔdaaa. Berɛ a ɔda hɔ no, wɔbaa wei kyerɛɛ ɔmpanyimfo no sɛ ne nkoa bi baako awu. Ɔmanmueni no see sɛ, "Mo mpo mpo no ato, efisɛ me nni aduane koro." Kwebla no see sɛ, "Me nso, mepɛ no, efisɛ Onyame ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, sɛ menya okuaa a wɔrepɛ sɛ wɔto no a, ɛsɛ sɛ mekyerɛ no na mekyere nnaman." Akurafo no see sɛ, "Daabi." (Nanso) Ananse (twe ne ho), na ɔse, "Mo mma me no, na sɛ moyɛ na mokɔtɔ nnaman no a, mobɛnya sika kɔkɔɔ asuanu baako (yeɛ." £4), na wubetumi de (sika yi) akɔtɔ ɔkunu foforo." Nnipa no see, "Saa asɛm yi yɛ nokware." Enti wɔyɛɛ nsakra no. Anansefaa okuafo no na ɔkɔɔ, na ɔduu kuro foforo bi. Sɛ ɔduu hɔ no, ɔkasaa ɔmpanyin no kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ma me ba yi nnye nka wo mma no ne wɔn nna wɔ saa pato yi mu; seesei (merekyerɛ wo) saa ba yi nyɛ eyie, ɔwɔ amanneɛ sɛ ɔyɛ fufuo anadwo, enti sɛ ɔyɛ fufuo a, ma mma (efofor) no bɔ no." Ɛnna ɔbarima no awu mfeɛ mmiɛnsa. Mma no ne ɔno (okuaa no) nnaa. Ɛnnɛ wɔn asaen biribi a ɛyɛ pon. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔba no ayɛ mmofra-mmofra, ma yɛnbɔ no." Wɔtɔɔ ne so, titim! titim! titim! (na ɛne wɔn bɔne nnyigyei). Anɔpa a ɛba no, Ananse kɔree ne ba. Berɛ a ɔkɔe no, ɔhɔ na ɔda, owuo. Ananse see, "Ɔmanhene, wo mma no ada Nyame abodin a ɔwɔ me nkyɛn no." Ɔmanhene no see, "Menntumi nnyɛ nsɛnnennen biara wɔ saa asɛm yi ho, enti wubetumi fa mma no akɔ." Kwebla no hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, "(Menntumi ngye) gye sɛ wo, ɔmanhene, ne nnipa a wɔwɔ kurom yi nyinaa bɛkɔ Nyame anim, na mobɛka mo asɛm." Ɔmanhene no bɔɔ 'dawuro no, na ɔka sɛ, "Mo nyinaa..." "Ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ Nyankopɔn anim hɔ, kɔka yɛn asɛm." Kwebla na ɔda kan; nnipa nyinaa suu no akyi. Berɛ a Ananse duruu kurom hɔ, ɔtwe obi kɔka kyerɛɛ Nyankopɔn sɛ ɔreba ne kurom nnipa ne no. Nyankopɔn frɛɛ ne mpanyimfo, na ɔtwɛɛ (ne atirimpɔfo nkyɛn). Berɛ a Ananse kɔree mu no, ɔtoo (ne nnwom): "Ananse, awu, awu, m’ani kɔtwee aburo baako a mɛkɔ ne no anya akoko."

Ananse, awu, awu, mefa akoko no kɔ sɛ mepɛ sɛ megye agutukron.

Faagya no faa mmoa no kɔfaa nabo, faa nabo no kɔfaa owuo, faa owuo no faa kuromfoɔ.

Obiara kaa sɛ, "Monyɛ anigyeɛ, mobɛtie nnwom a Ananse reto yi?" Ananse too ne nnwom no bio:

Ananse, awu, awu, m'anya aburo baako pɛ sɛ mekɔ neyɛ akoko.

Ananse, awu, awu, mefa akoko no kɔ sɛ mepɛ sɛ megye agutukron.

Faagya no faa mmoa no kɔfaa nabo, faa nabo no kɔfaa owuo, faa owuo no faa kuromfoɔ.

Ananse baa Nyame anim, na Nyame bisaa Ananse amanneɛ. Ananse see, "Aburo baako na mifuaeɛ, na mekaa sɛ mɛba ne kuromfoɔ; hɔ na kuromfoɔ no gyina." Nyame see, "Kamfo no." (Wɔn bɔɔ mframa,) "E! e! "Nyame kasae sɛ, "Dɛn na mɛma wo sɛ aseda?" Kwebla kasae sɛ, "Hwee nni hɔ a mepɛ; (beae) a mɛtena wɔ aban mu no nea mepɛ nyinaa." Nyame kasae sɛ, "O, o! me nua yi, wo bɛwu wɔ ohia mu, wo a wode nnoɔma pii bae, nanso woka sɛ hwee nni hɔ a wopɛ." Eyi ara ne nea nti ohia mpue Ananse hɔ da."

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
