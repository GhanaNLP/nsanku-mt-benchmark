Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo ampa, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo ampa (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNE ANANSE, KƆKƆRƆ NO, BƐYƐƐ OHIƐ

KWAKU ANANSE, Kɔkɔre no, kɔɔ Nyankonpon, Nimskyaman no hɔ, na ɔsee no sɛ, “Ma me aburodua baako na me nso mɛma wo nnipa kuro biara (ma m'atware).” Nimskyaman no see no sɛ, “Wontumi.” Kɔkɔre no hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, “Sɛ me ntumi a, me ntona wo nawo, Nsia, na m'atware no nti, mɛde me ti asua.” Nimskyaman no see no sɛ, “Fa (aburodua no) na kɔ.” Kɔkɔre no hyɛɛ aseɛ na ɔkɔɔ kuro bi mu. Ɔkaee kurofoɔ mpanyimfo no asɛm yi. “Nimskyaman na ɔde me abrɛ; ɔse, mɛfa aburodua baako yi akɔ baabi, na sɛ me du (baabiara) a, mɛma aburodua yi neda akoko hɔ.” Mpanyimfo no see no sɛ, “E! sɛ akoko ne aburodua da bam a, ɛbɛyɛ yie?” Ananse hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, “Nea ɛsɛ sɛ woyɛ ne sɛ woma wɔda bam.” Mpanyimfo no see no sɛ, “Kɔ na to ho.” Anokwa anɔpa, Ananse see no sɛ ɔrekɔ rehwehwɛ, nanso (aburodua baako no) nni hɔ bio. Kɔkɔre no see no sɛ, “Mpanyimfo, akoko no adidi Nimskyaman aburodua no.” Mpanyimfo no see no sɛ, “Mentumi nka asɛm bi ho, enti fa akoko no na kɔ.” Ananse kɔfaa akoko kɛse bi, na ɔde no kɔɔ kuro foforo bi. Sɛ ɔdu hɔ a, ɔkaee mpanyimfo no asɛm bi, ɔsee no sɛ, “Nimskyaman na ɔde me abrɛ, na mɛda ha, na saa akoko a mekura yi nso neda nguan hɔ.” Mpanyimfo no see no sɛ, “Fa mma na to ho.” Anadwo, Ananse kɔɔ nguan kuro no mu na ɔkumee akoko no. Ɔfaa akoko no mmoa na ɔtoo no ɔkraman kɛse bi nwu ho. Anɔpa anɔpa, ɔkɔɔ mpanyimfo no hɔ na ɔsee no, "Ma me akoko no." Sɛ wɔrekɔ rekɔfa ama no a, hwɛ, akoko no awu. Ananse see no, "Ah! Nimskyaman akoko no ne eyi, a nguan no akum." Mpanyimfo no see no, "Mentumi nka asɛm bi ho, enti fa ngua baako na kɔ." Ananse faa ngua no na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ. Ɔduu kuro foforo bi. Ɔkɔɔ mpanyimfo no fie. Mpanyimfo no see no, "Kae nka?" Kɔkɔre no hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, "Nimskyaman na ɔde me abrɛ, na me kura ngua no; ɛne nananom nna mma wɔnda bam." Mpanyimfo no see no, "Ho! ma ɛnna nguan hɔ." Kɔkɔre no see no, "Dabi, efisɛ eyi ne Nimskyaman ngua pɔtee, ɛnna nananom hɔ." Mpanyimfo no see no, "Kɔ na to ho, ɛnna." Kɔkɔre no kɔtoo ho. Anadwo, Ananse kɔkumee ngua no, na ɔtoo ne mogya nananom mu baako ti ho. Anɔpa a ɛdi so, Ananse see, "Ɔmanhene, ma me me nguan no." Wɔn kɔɔ nguan-kuro no mu; ɛhɔ na nguan no hyɛe, awu. Ananse see, "Ɔmanhene, Nyame nguan ne eyi a nananom nnuane no akura." Ɔmanhene see, "Menntumi nnyɛ wo asɛm bi wɔ eyi ho, enti fa nananom nnuane a wakura no no fi ha." Ananse faa nananom nnuane no, na ɔkɔɔ, na ɔduu kuro foforo bi. Ɔfaa nananom nnuane no na ɔkyekyereɛ hɔ, na ɔkɔdaaa. Berɛ a ɔda hɔ no, wɔbaa na wɔka kyerɛɛ ɔmanhene no sɛ ne nkoa bi awu. Ɔmanhene see, "Mo mpo no afutu, efisɛ m'ani nni aduane-koro biara ho." Kwebla see, "Me nso, mepɛ no, efisɛ Nyame ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ sɛ mehu okwu a wɔrepɔ no a, ɛsɛ sɛ mekyerɛ no nananom nnuane." Kuroba no see, "Dabi." (Nanso) Ananse (tweneɛ), na ɔse, "Mo mma no mma me, na sɛ mokɔtɔn nananom nnuane no a, mobɛnya sika kɔkɔɔ asuanu-baako (i.e. £4), na mobɛtumi fa (ei sika yi) akɔtɔn nkoa foforo." Nnipa no see, "Nokware wɔ hɔ." Enti wɔyɛɛ nsakyere no. Ananse faa okwu no na ɔkɔɔ, na ɔduu kuro foforo bi. Berɛ a ɔduu hɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔmanhene no sɛ, "Ma me ba yi nni mma yi nkyɛn, wɔ saa pato (open veranda room) yi mu; seesei (mɛka kyerɛ wo) ba yi nyɛ fɛ, ɔwɔ amane sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔpue fufuo anadwo, enti sɛ ɔpue fufuo a, mma no mma wɔnhwene no." Seesei ɔyɛ okwu mfeɛ mmiɛnsa. Mma no ne ɔno (okwu no) daa. Afei wɔnkaa sɛ biribi repon. Wɔsee, "Ba yi apue fufuo, ma yɛnhwene no." Wɔtɔɔ ne so, titim! titim! titim! (ne nkabom no bɔɔ saa). Anɔpa a ɛdi so, Ananse kɔfrɛɛ ne ba. Berɛ a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, ɔhɔ na ɔwɔ, awu. Ananse see, "Ɔmanhene, wo mma akura Nyame abɔfo a ɔwɔ me nkyɛn no." Ɔmanhene see, "Menntumi nnyɛ asɛm bi wɔ eyi ho, enti wubetumi fa mma no fi ha." Kwebla hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, "(Menntumi ngye) gye sɛ wo, ɔmanhene, ne nnipa a wɔwɔ kuro yi mu nyinaa bɛkɔ Nyame anim, na mokae mo asɛm." Ɔmanhene bɔɔ 'dawuro gong no, na ɔka sɛ, "Mo nyinaa mma mo nkɔ Nyame anim hɔ, na yɛnkɔka yɛn asɛm." Kwebla kɔɔ anim; nnipa no nyinaa suu ne akyi. Berɛ a Ananse duu kuro no mfiase no, ɔtweɛ abɔfo kɔka kyerɛɛ Nyame sɛ ɔreba ne kuro bi nnipa. Nyame frɛɛ ne mpanyimfo, na ɔtwɛɛ (ne asɛmpanyin ho). Berɛ a Ananse kɔree mu no, ɔtoo (ne nnwom): "Ananse, awu, awu, m’ani kɔtwee aburo baako a mɛkɔ ne no anya akoko."

Ananse, awu, awu, mefa akoko no kɔ sɛ mepɛ sɛ megye agutukron.

Faagya no faa mmoa no kɔfaa nabo, faa nabo no kɔfaa owuo, faa owuo no faa kuromfoɔ.

Obiara kaa sɛ, "Monyɛ anigyeɛ, mobɛtie nnwom a Ananse reto yi?" Ananse too ne nnwom no bio:

Ananse, awu, awu, m'anya aburo baako pɛ sɛ mekɔ neyɛ akoko.

Ananse, awu, awu, mefa akoko no kɔ sɛ mepɛ sɛ megye agutukron.

Faagya no faa mmoa no kɔfaa nabo, faa nabo no kɔfaa owuo, faa owuo no faa kuromfoɔ.

Ananse baa Nyame anim, na Nyame bisaa Ananse amanneɛ. Ananse see, "Aburo mmoa baako pɛ na mifuaeɛ, na meka sɛ mɛba ne kuromfoɔ; hɔ na kuromfoɔ no gyina." Nyame see, "Kamfo no." (Wɔn bɔɔ mframa,) "E! e! e!" Nyame see, "Dɛn na mɛma wo sɛ aseda?" Kweblaa see, "Hwee nni hɔ a mɛpɛ; (beaeɛ) a ɛwɔ abodinto no ara ne nea mɛpɛ." Nyame see, "O, o! me mma yi, wo bɛwu wɔ ohia mu, wo a wokofaa pii ba, nanso woka sɛ hwee nni hɔ a wopɛ." Ɛno nti na ohia rennya Ananse akyi koraa.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
