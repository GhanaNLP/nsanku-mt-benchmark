Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

TWA SO WOHA NA WO BƐ HU

Obia bi ne ne ba bi teyɛɛ wɔ hɔ tete no. Berɛ a ɔde no kɔe (baabiara), ɔse, "Me mpɛ sɛ mekɔ." Ne maame no bɔɔ no kɔkɔ sɛ, "Twɛn hɔ na wubɛhu." Da biara, berɛ a ɔde no kɔe (baabiara), ɔse, "Me mpɛ sɛ mekɔ." Ɛna ne maame no ka kyerɛ no sɛ, "Twɛn hɔ na wubɛhu." Da koro bi, abofra no se, "Da biara, maame ka kyerɛ me sɛ, 'Twɛn hɔ na wubɛhu'; nnɛ yi ara, mekɔtɔ kɔhwehwɛ 'Mebɛhu' no." "na matumi hwɛ no." Enti ɔkɔɔ. Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, (na) abaayɛ-dua bi wɔ hɔ a ɛwo oro. Abaayɛ-dua no bisaa no sɛ, "Adɛn nti na berɛ a obiara nni hɔ mma saa, seesei wo aba ha?" Abofra no see sɛ, "Mete hɔ daa ne me maame; ɔsee me sɛ, 'Twɛn hɔ na wobɛhu,' na mekaa sɛ mɛba mɛhwehwɛ 'Mɛhu' no, na matumi mhwɛ no." Pea-tree no see sɛ, "Meyɛ wɔn mu baako, nanso yɛn mu tini no wɔ mprenu." Abofra no hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔrekɔ bio. Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu Long-piece-of-firewood reba ne nkyɛn. Nnua-tenten bisaa no sɛ, "Adɛn nti, ɛnna obiara nni hɔ a wafa saa kwan yi so yi, na seesei woafiri so?" Abofra no hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, "Da biara, me maame ka sɛ, 'Mɛhu', enti mekaa sɛ mereba 'Mɛhu' hwehwɛ mu, na matumi ahwɛ no.” Nnua-tenten no gyee no ase sɛ, “Me yɛ wɔn mu bi, nanso kɔ anim.” Na abofra no hyɛɛ ase kɔ bio. Sɛ ɔrekɔ no, (na) Glowing-Sparks wɔ hɔ, na ɔda kwan mu kwan-poma bi so, na ɔreba (ne nkyɛn). Ɔhyiaa no. Glowing-Sparks kaa sɛ, “Adɛn nti na woaba ha, ɛnna obiara nni hɔ a ɔbɛba ha?” Abofra no hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, “Bere a me ne me maame tebea no, ɔkaa sɛ, ‘Mɛhu,’ enti mekaa sɛ mɛkɔ ‘Mɛhu’ hwee, na matumi ahwɛ no.” Glowing-Sparks kaa sɛ, “Me yɛ wɔn mu baako, nanso kɔ anim, efisɛ yɛn mu tini no wɔ hɔ.” Abofra no hyɛɛ ase kɔ bio. Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, (na) Papayɛfoɔ no rehyɛ dan bi ho nsuo. Papayie no kaa sɛ, “Seesei yɛbɛnya obi mogya a yɛbɛdɔrɔ saa dan yi so.” Enti berɛ a abofra no duu hɔ no, wɔkaa sɛ, “Adɛn nti na woaba ha seesei, berɛ a obiara nni hɔ a ɔaba ha koraa?” Ɔgyee ase kaa sɛ, “Mete me maame nkyɛn berɛtɛm; ɔkaa sɛ, ‘Twɛn hɔ na wobɛhu,’ enti mekaa sɛ mɛkɔ ‘Mebɛhu’ rehwehwɛ, na matumi ahwɛ no.” Ɛno akyi no, Papayie no kyere abofra no, na wɔtwaa ne ti na wɔfaa ne mogya na wɔtuu no into kɔtenu kɛseɛ bi mu; wɔfaa bi na wɔtoo no wɔ dan no so. Ɛna Papayew no nyinaa tuu kɔ na wɔkɔtɔɔ dua tenten bi so, na wɔba baako baako na wɔtoo wɔn ntadeɛ mu mogya no mu, na wɔn ntadeɛ no bɛyɛɛ kɔkɔɔ. Papayew no nyinaa faa wɔn ntadeɛ too mogya no mu, sɛ eyɛ nkaeɛ ma saa anadwo yi. Na da bi, Papayew bi se ɔrekɔ adidi. Ɔkɔkɔɔ na ɔhyiaa abofra no maame. Na maame no bisaa Papire no sɛ, “Me ba a me ne no tenaa no yɛ ɔkwaseɛ koraa. Sɛ me sɔre a, meka sɛ, ɔntena hɔ na ɔnhu; ɔno, sɛnea ɔyɛ kwaseɛ no—fa me nkyerɛaseɛ mma me—ɔkaa sɛ ɔbɛkɔ ‘Mihu’ ankasa hwee, na ama ɔatumi ahwɛ no.” Ɛyɛ nnawɔtwe a ɛakɔ fi nnɛ na meanhunu no, na mebɔ wo mpae, sɛ woanhunu no a, fa ɔba me nkyɛn.” Papire no see, “O, ɔbaa yɛn akuraa mu, na yɛn nso, yɛwuree no. Ne mogya bi, a yɛfaa sɛ yɛbɛkae saa asɛm yi, wɔyɛ me ntade yi so.” Ɛno nti, sɛ wokɔ (wo kwan no), kɔ kyerɛ wo nkurɔfoɔ nyinaa sɛ, sɛ obi ka kyerɛ obi koraa sɛ, ‘Tena hɔ na wubɛhu,’ a, ɔde musuu agye no.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
