Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea wɔde "KURONTO" ne "BURU" (Nkyia) hyɛɛ aseɛ

Na abɔdeɛ bi wɔ hɔ a yɛfrɛ no Kwaku, ne yere nso yɛfrɛ no Buruwa, na efisɛ wɔyɛ ahyiafoɔ, enti wɔkɔɔ sɛ wɔbɛyɛ akumadan foforo. Kwaku, abɔadeɛ no, berɛ a ɔkɔɔ kwaa mu no, ɔbɔɔ eku kɔkɔɔ bi, nanso berɛ a ɔde baa fie no, ɔsee, "Wei nnyɛ eyie (ma aduane)." Enti ɔkɔtɔnoo. Berɛ biara a ɔbɔɔ biribi, ɛyɛ saa. Nanso ne yere anhunu sika no daa, enti ɔbisaa no sɛ, "Wonnfa sika mba, na wonnfa nam mba, enti nea eyi nyinaa ara ase ne dɛn?" Ɔgyee no sɛ, "Sɛ mekɔ a, nnipa mpɔ (nam no), na ɛpue, enti metow no gu." Da koro bi, ɔbɔɔ biribi, na ɔtoo no kwan ho, na ne yere kɔɔ ne akyi kɔhwɛɛ no. Ɛnna ɔhunuu sɛ ɔtɔnoo kɔkɔɔ no, na ɔfaa sika no kɔtɔɔ dɔnto bi na ɔnom. Ɛnna ne yere san kɔɔ fie. Ankyɛ koraa na Kwaku, abɔadeɛ no, nso baa. Berɛ a ɔduu fie no, ɔsee, "Maadwo, Buruwa." Ɔgyee no sɛ, "Ya Ekudonto." Ɔbisaa no sɛ, "Adɛn nti na wugyee me saa?" Ɔgyee no sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuaeɛ a ɛfata nea ɛwɔ wo ti mu." Abɔadeɛ no anka hwee. Anɔpa a ɛbaeɛ no, berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ hwee, ɔkɔɔ afuo na ɔsan baa; ɔsee, "Maadwo." Ɔgyee no sɛ, "Ya Buru." Buruwa bisaa no sɛ, "Adɛn nti na wugyee (me nkyia) saa?" Abɔadeɛ no gyee no sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuaeɛ a ɛfata nea ɛwɔ wo ti mu." Saa na ɛyɛɛ amammere wɔ abusua no mu sɛ wɔde "Ya Kuronto" ne "Ya Buru" yɛ nkyia. Wɔn mfitiaseɛ firi abɔadeɛ Kwaku ne ne yere Buruwa hɔ. Me nsɛm a mika yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaasɛ sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi, na ma bi mma me.
