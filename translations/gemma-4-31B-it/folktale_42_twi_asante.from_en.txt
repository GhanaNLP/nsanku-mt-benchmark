Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea Osebo, Atikora no, ne Adowa, Atikora no, bɛyɛɛ nnamfo

OKUROTWIAMANSA (He-who-holds-the-nation-in-fear) nna ɔte hɔ, na ɔse ɔbɛkɔ akɔsiesie obi a ɔbɛsu ɔnkyɛn (na ɔmmpɔ ntia bi sɛ ɔbɛka no wɔ ne anim). Mmoa nyinaa mu no, obiara ampuu sɛ ɔbɛtumi ayɛ saa. Ɛnnɛ, Adowa Antelope ketewa no na ɔtena hɔ; ɔsee, "Agya Leopard, dɛn na woka fa me ne wo ho sɛ yɛbɛyɛ (ahyia ne nkyerɛmu) amanehunu?" Osebo, Leopard no, see, "Ɛno ara ne nea mepɛ." Enti wɔyɛɛ (ahyia ne nkyerɛmu) amanehunu. Leopard no boaa palm-wine. Antelope no kɔɔ, nomme palm-wine no, na ɔbɔɔ boom; ɔantumi ansɔre, na ɔdae palm-tree no ase. Anwummerɛ mmerɛ a ɛyɛ nwunu no, Opuan no see sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwɛ ne nnoɔma. Berɛ a ɔkɔe no, Adowa no nom ne nsa no nyinaa na ɔda hɔ a ɔyɛ ayɛ. Ɔpyene no see sɛ, "Ei, wo na woka sɛ wo ne me bɛyɛ atamfo a yɛnnyɛ yɛn ho nkwa; ɛnna, Esono, Ɛna, mpo nnentumi nyɛ me atamfo a ɔnyɛ ne ho nkwa, mpɛn nyinaa wo bɛhyia nsɛm." Ɔpyene no kɔɔ, na ɔtwaa abrobɔdua nhwiren, na ɔtanne abrobɔdua kete bi, na ɔtoo Antelope no mu; na ɔfaa no, na ɔtoo no ne tiwuo. Berɛ a ɔreba na ɔduru kɔnkyene no, Adowa no nyansa baa ne mu. Ɔhui sɛ Leopard no akura no. Adowa no hwɛɛ soro, na nsuo-dua bi hyɛ hɔ. Adowa no kyere no. Seesei, kasa ne ne mmuaeɛ (yehwɛ, meyi biribi fii mu), Adowa no mmoa bi hyɛɛ palm-leaf basket no mu. Ɔbrɔnno no nnim nea na ɛrekɔ onsa; enti ɔfa no (sɛnea ɔdwen kɛse) kɔ fie. Ɔkaa kyerɛ ne maame sɛ, "Kwaku Dua no, a ɔyɛ kwasea nyinaa mu panyin, ɔse me ne no nyɛ ayɔnkofa, enti mede no aba; fa abrobɛ hyɛ gyata mu." Gyata maame no kaa kyerɛ ne ba sɛ, "Abrobɛ nkwan no ayɛ, enti fa no mma me na matumi awu no." Berɛ a Gyata hwɛɛ kɛtɛ no, ɛyɛyɛɛ kuku. Ɔakɔ, na ne nnuane nkoaa na ɛtena hɔ. Ɔpyɛm no see, "Maame, wadi kwan, na ne mmoa nna hɔ; ma yɛn nfa wo na yɛde atɔ soup no mu." Enti wɔde hyɛɛ soup no mu, na wɔnoaeɛ aduane no nyinaa no a, wɔtenaa ase kɔdee dii. Akoraa nketewa no hyɛɛ ne asaase soup no mu na ɔnnumaa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, Kwadwo (efisɛ Leopard nnuaba da no yɛ Dwowda), na eyi yɛ ne mmoa nkoa, enti ne nam no bɛyɛ dɛ dɛn; ɛbɛyɛ dɛ sen atadwe, enti baabiara a ɔda hɔ, bɔ mmɔden fa no ba." Na Leopard no kɔɔ ne abrobɔdua no ho. Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, Adowa no da hɔ, ɔno nso ayɛ nsa bio. Ɔpyene no see sɛ, "Oh, sɛ meye mabrɛ nea ɛdi kan no a, seesei mɛkɔ fie akɔfa kɔtokuo baa na mɛsan mba." Ɔpyene no kɔɔ fie, faa ne ntade nyinaa (firi ne kɔtokuo mu), too hɔ, na ɔfa kɔtokuo no ne ne kɔtokuo kɔkɔɔ no kɔɔ abrobɔdua no hɔ. Ɔfaa Ɔnwene no, hyɛɛ no kɔtokuo no mu, gyinaa mu, na ɔfaa no too ne ti so na ɔkɔɔ. Sɛ ɔduu fie no, ɔsee, "Maame, mede no aba; yɛrebu dampon no pon no na matumi ahyɛ no mu." Akoraa no buee no, na ɔhyɛɛ no mu. Ɛna Aberewa no kaa kyerɛ ne maame sɛ, "Merekɔ Tenten-kɔkɔɔ-manno no mu rekɔsiesie ebunu, ne nkyene, ne jinjɛ a wɔatwa, na matumi aba mu, na yɛnni Adowa Antelope no ayɛ nsuomduane a yɛbɛtumi adi." Aberewa no hyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ ne akwantuo no so. Anɔpa a ɛbae no, berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ anim no, ɔbaa kɛseɛ no na ɔrepɔ palm-nuts, na berɛ a Antelope hwee n'ani kɔɔ abɔdeɛ bi mu (pono no mu) no, ɔhunuu ɔbaa kɛseɛ no. Duyker no see sɛ, "Nana kɔkɔɔ, yɛnni wo yaw; sɛ wobue me kwan a, mɛyɛ nkokɔɔ no ma wo." Kɔkɔɔ no see sɛ, "Wo, Duyker, wobɛtu mmirika." Duyker no hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, "Aserampon, ne Ta' Kese, ne Dwomo, ne Apeya, ne Konkroma, ne Ta' Kora, ne Krakye Dente mma me awu, sɛ mebɛtu mmirika wɔ berɛ a wobue me kwan no." "Sɛ wopɛ a, mu pondu nyinaa." Akraa no see, "Manya." Ɔmua pondu no, na ɔbue (adanmu a) Duyker no wom. Duyker no bae, na ɔfaa poma no, na ɔpɔɔ abrobɛ no. Akraa no repɛ sɛ ɔyɛ n'anim kwan sɛ ɔbɛfa biribi afi asaase so, ɛnna Duyker no faa poma no na ɔbɔɔ ne ti akyi, pe! na ɔwui. Duyker no tuu ne skin firi ne ho, na ɔde hyɛɛ ne ho, na ɔtwaa ne nam no gu gyata mu. Ankyɛ koraa no, Kwadwo baa. Duyker no see no, "Adɛn nti na woatwa mmerɛ pii saa?" "Mepue (pono no) ama Duyker na mabu no, ne nam no nso wɔ abrobɔ no so." Leopard no see, "Woayɛ yie, Apeafo." Odii ne ho so took nneɛma a ɔde bae no hyɛɛ soup-stew no mu. Wokɔtɔɛ no; berɛ a wɔkɔtee nneɛma no ase kɔtwee sɛ wɔbɛdi no, Leopard no faa bone bi. Berɛ a ɔtwee no, ɛyɛɛ pu! pu! Duyker no bɔɔ ne nsa mu kaa sɛ, "Wo naayɛn nnamfo na worepɛpɛ." Leopard no bisaa sɛ, "Dɛn na wokae?" Duyker no hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, "Mekaa sɛ saa Duyker yi ayɛ mpanin; ne nnamfo yɛ den." Leopard no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ saa, me nso mampɛpɛ wɔn." Afei Leopard no hwee nnuane no nyinaa hyɛɛ ne anomu, na ɔyɛɛ dɔm dɔm! dɔm dɔm! Duyker no see sɛ, "Ah, ɛyɛ soup-stew a wɔyɛe fii wo naana ti mu na wode rehyɛ wo anom." Leopard no bisaa sɛ, "Dɛn na wose?" Duyker no hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, "Mereka ho asɛm sɛ, ɛnna ne mmoa nso (yɛdee yɛkaa sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ), na ne nam no, woahu ne dɛ?" Leopard no see sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ hia koraa, koraa, koraa sɛ yɛbɛka saa." Na wɔawie adidi. Duyker no boaa dan no hɔ; ɔkaa kyerɛ Leopard no sɛ, "Merekɔ gu borɔne no so." Leopard no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ, ɛnna, dɛn na mede wɔ ho, me yɛ obaa?" Duyker no duruu borɔne no ho. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Hwɛ hɔ, Leopard! Hwɛ hɔ, Leopard!" "Sɛ woyɛ kwasea, sɛ woyɛ aboa, na mɛkum wo na mɛyɛ soup-stew ma wo adi anaa?" Leopard no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ meba wo nkyɛn a, mɛhu wo (wo gyina hɔ ara)?" Duyker no kaa sɛ, "Wobɛtumi mpo anya me." Leopard no tuu mmirika, yiridi! yiridi! (ne nan nnyigyei no yɛ saa), na Duyker no nso hyɛɛ aseɛ tuu mmirika, prada! prada! prada! Duyker no dɔɔ ne fie, na ɔkɔtoo ne poku mu. Berɛ a ɔyɛɛ (dan no), ɔmpɛ kwan biara a obi bɛfa akɔ mu, na kwan baako a ɔyɛɛ no pɛ na ɛwɔ. Berɛ a Duyker no kɔɔ mu no, ɔguu ne pon no mu, bam! Se Leopard no hwɛ mpo a, ɔanhunu kwan a Duyker no fa kɔɔ mu no koraa. Ɔpyene no kɔɔ na ɔfrɛɛ mmoa nyinaa, ɔse, "Braa, na yɛnkɔnkyɛ Duyker no ama me, efisɛ ɔayɛ me kɔkɔɔduru a, mentumi mmoa mo asɛm nyinaa masi sɛ moakyɛ no ama me." Mmoa nyinaa baa hɔ kɔɔ Duyker no fie. Berɛ a Duyker tee sɛ nnuonyam no aba ne fie ha sɛ wɔbɛkyere no no, ɔtookoo mako, nkyene, ne efie, na ɔtɔɔ mu, na ɔtoo hɔ. Seesei nnuonyam no nyinaa gyina hɔ. Eyi hwɛ eyi anim. Ɛna Kusie, Okraman no, see sɛ, “Kwakuo, Aberewa no, wɔhɔ, na ɔwɔ ntadeɛ tenten, enti ma yɛnma ɔkɔ soro kan, na yɛbɛtumi de ne ntadeɛ no ayɛ kwan na yɛakɔ fie hɔ.” Kwakuo, Aberewa no, hyɛɛ aseɛ, kra! kra! kra! na ɔkɔree soro. Ɔtwɛnne firɛ dan no ase, nanso ansa na ɔbɛhwɛ Duyker no, Duyker no faa mako a ɔno ayɛ no, hyɛɛ ne nsa mu, na ɔtwaa no kɔɔ Monkey no ani mu. Ɛno a ɛyɛe no, Monkey no gyee ne nan na ɔtɔɔ ase. Mmoa no baa ha na wɔhunuu sɛ biribi wɔ ne ani mu. Baako faa ne nsa kaa no, na ɔnam ne nsa so. Ɔse, “O, m'adamfo, bra (na hwɛ) nea Kwakuo akɔfa aba.” Hafo nyinaa bae. Wɔnnuu, wɔnnuu, na wɔnnuu. Ɛno nti na Kwakuo, Aberewa no, ani kɔ mu saa. Afei wɔse, “Kokotee (Kɔkɔɔ), wo nso, wo tɔn kwan berɛtɛm, nuntun! nuntun!” entisɛ wobɛtumi akɔ fie hɔ.” Berɛ a Duyker tee saa, ɔkɔtwee ne cutu no, ma ɛyɛɛ nnam pa ara. Ɔbusuapig no tow hwee, tow hwee, na ne nsa kɔtoo fie hɔ mu. Ɛnna Duyker no faa ne cutu no, too ɔbusuapig no anom, na ɔhyɛɛ no twee. Busapig no see sɛ, “O, Busapig Asamoa, ɔretwa me ano.” Mmoa a na wɔgyina ne akyi no (na wɔnenteɛn nte nea ɔseɛ no yie) see sɛ, “Yɛyɛ adwuma pa, Apeafo; pira wo ani, na fa no mma yɛn.” Kokotee, Busapig no, tuu ne ti fii hɔ; ɔne ne ano nyinaa yɛɛ mogya. Berɛ a mmoa no huu sɛ Kokotee Asamoa, Kwaku Duaa no, anom atwe no, na wɔnyini fii hɔ, na yii! (na ɛne wɔn mmuaeɛ nnyigyeɛ). Efisɛ saa na Kwaku Duaa nyaa anom a ɛyɛ tɛntɛn saa. Afei, berɛ a mmoa no nyinaa dwatwaeɛ no, Osebo, Gyata no, anhunu nea ɔbɛyɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Sɛnea ɛteɛ, mɛnkɔ baabi ara; mɛtena ha daa, kosi sɛ mɛwu, gye sɛ mehu Adowa, Duyker no.” Berɛ a Adowa tee saa, na ɔfaa (Ɔbrɔnno no) maame skin no na ɔhyiraa ne ho mu, na ɔkɔɔ dan no ase na ɔkɔɔ emu efie no akyi. Ɛnnɛ na ɔkɔɔ Leopard no hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “O, Kwadwo, dɛn na ama wo ayɛ tɛn saa; adɛn nti na woyɛ wo ho yaw ho Duyker bi?” “Ma yɛnkɔ fie na yɛasiesie yɛn baabi, na yɛasane aba.” Wɔnnam kɔɔ fie. Duyker no kaa kyerɛ Leopard no sɛ, “Fa wo nnaman nnoɔma nyinaa firi hɔ na ma me, na mɛhwɛ so ama wo.” Leopard no kaa sɛ, “Ɛnyɛ adwene bɔne.” Ɛnna ɔfaa ne nnoɔma nyinaa firi hɔ na ɔmaa Duyker no. Duyker no nso nna ɔnka wɔn kɔtoo hɔ, na ɔde wɔn hyɛɛ hɔ (yie), a ɔkɔgyinaa baabi. Ɔsee no, “Ei, Leopard!” Ɔgyee no ase sɛ, “Aane, nua, aane!” Ɔsee no, “Sɛ woyɛ kwasea anaa woyɛ aboa a, mɛtumi ayɛ wo naama na mɛma no nyinaa ayɛ soup-stew ma wo na woenom?” “Seesei bio na m’atwa wo ayere na m’afa wo adwinne nyinaa.” Leopard no kaa sɛ, “Mɛhyia wo hɔ (sɛ meba a)?” Duyker no kaa sɛ, “Wobɛtwa me kwan mpo.” Ɛnna Leopard no tutuu (no), kiri! kiri! kiri! nanso Duyker no nso tutuu kɔɔ. Ɔkɔɔ ne fie mu na ɔguaa ɛpono no, bam! Ɛnnɛ Leopard no gyina hɔ, ne bo kɔtɔe; na ne ntade no kɔne kɔne firi nkyɛn kɔ nifa (ɛyɛ dɔm) fre! fre! fre! Annye asɛm biara a ɔbɛka. Akyirei no, ɔka kyerɛɛ Duyker no sɛ, "Meka Thursday din a wɔaka sɛ ɛyɛ mmerɛw no, sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wo ba, na yɛabɛyɛ nnamfo." Duyker no kaa sɛ, "Mennba, ɛnna sɛ wopɛ paa ara, gye sɛ wosiesie nea ɛbɛma yɛnom nyamefo no." Leopard no kaa sɛ, "Megyina ho, ɛnna mɛtumi ayɛ biribiara a ɛbɛyɛ wo yaw." Duyker no baa hɔ sɛ ɔbɛnom nyamefo no ne Leopard no. Duyker no kaa kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Mma wo nkyere me koraa; sɛ wuhu me, na woakye me wɔ mpaempa mu a, gya me ma menkɔ." Leopard no kaa sɛ, "Mase." Na Leopard no nso kasaa, ɔkaa sɛ, "Kwan a wobɛfa no so mpo, mɛde m'anim nka so, na woahu sɛ me ne wo nni asomdwoe bi a ɛkyerɛ sɛ yɛn nni nsɛnee bi wɔ yɛn ntam." Eyi ara ne nea nti, sɛ Leopard bi wɔ hɔ a, onkyere Duyker no. Bio, sɛ mfiri bi afi Duyker no a, ɔ (Gyata no) kɔtwe no angwa.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
