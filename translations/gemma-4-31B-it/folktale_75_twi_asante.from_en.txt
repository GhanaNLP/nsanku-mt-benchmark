Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea (Ntorɔ a wɔfrɛ no) "AKYEM ABOADEE" baa amansan mu no

Na obiara a ɔyɛ fɛ, abofra baa bi te ase. Onipa biara a ɔka kyerɛ no sɛ ɔdɔ no, ɔka kyerɛ no sɛ, "Mendɔ wo." Ɛna Leopard no sesaa ne ho bɛyɛɛ ɔbarima fɛfɛ bi, na ɔbaa ɔwaree no, na ɔfaa no kɔɔ ne kuro mu. (Sɛ ɔduu hɔ no) ɔsee no, "Me yɛ Aberewa, na mebae baa na me ne wo bɔɔ mmɔden." Ɔno nso gyee no ase sɛ, "Agya, me dɔ wo, sɛnea wote sɛn ara a." Ne ne no wɔbaa ba bi; ɔyɛ onipa aba. Ɔsaa so maa ba bi, na ɔyɛ abaayewa. Ɛnnɛ da bi, ne maame a ɔyɛ mpaninfoɔ no see no sɛ, "Merekɔ sɛ mɛhwehwɛ me ba baakɔ." Enti ne maame no kɔe, na (abaayewa no) faa no kɔhyee no hwee. Na ne kunu baa; ɔkaa sɛ, "Obi aba ha?" Na ɔkaa sɛ, "Obi mmaaa ha." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛrehyɛ kukuru, kukuru wɔ ayaresɛm (atabea) no mu no; na bio, nsuo retɔ me so." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Akukrudu." Anɔpa a ɛbaa no, anɔpa paa, ne kunu kɔɔ kwae, na ɔbaa no kaa kyerɛ ne maame sɛ, "Kɔ, na sɛ wo du nkran no, mma wo mmoa, sɛ wo du asu no, mpa, efisɛ sɛ woka a, ɔbɛba na wakye wo." Ɛnnɛ ɔduu hɔ; wannye ne ho aso (deɛ wɔka kyerɛɛ no) na ɔfrɛɛ no sɛ, "Me yaba, me yaba!" Leopard no bae na okyee no na ɔfaa no kɔe, na berɛ a ne yere huu ne (honam) a, ɔtɔɔ ne ho gu fam na ɔsuu. Ne kunu bisaa no sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛrekɔ onua?" Ɔyɛɛ no mmuae sɛ, "Me yamu yɛ me." Da a ɛba no, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ no, ɔbaa no see sɛ, "Merekɔ me akurukyi." Ɔpɔtwee eto ne nnuaba, na ɔtoo hɔ, na ɔfaa ne mma mmienu no; baako nantee ne anim, na ɔfoforo nantee ne akyi. Ɛnnɛ, berɛ a ɔtwaa eto bi maa ne ba kɔdee no, ɔsee no sɛ, "Fa na didi, ɔno a ɔyɛ ɔbarima pa no ba." Na ɔtwaa bi maa ɔtoa no nso, ɔsee no sɛ, "Fa na didi, ɔno a ɔyɛ ɔbarima pa no ba." Saa berɛ yi nyinaa, ne kunu nam (a wahu no) ne nkyɛn wɔ kwan no so. Ɛna berɛ a wɔduruu kuro no ho no, Aberewa no see sɛ, "Aso, me hyia wo anigyeɛ efisɛ wo na wo maame baa ha na wo ammu me kwan, na wo nso na wo maame kɔtoo me nsam, enti gyina hɔ na ma me ma me mma yi din: ɔbarima no din ne Ofori; ɔbaa no nso din ne Oforiwa." Aberewa no puee sika nneɛma na ɔmaa ne mma no, ɔse, "Mo m" mpɛn biara, mma mo nni anyinam biara a ɔbrɔm ayi; bio, sɛ mo hyɛ ɔbrɔm no anim a, ɛsɛ sɛ mo fa sɔpɔn (na mo hohoro no), ne hyire mfutuma na mo hye no; seesei me ma mo me mpaebɔ, kose! kose! efisɛ mo yɛ Akyem Aboadee Ntoro mma.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
