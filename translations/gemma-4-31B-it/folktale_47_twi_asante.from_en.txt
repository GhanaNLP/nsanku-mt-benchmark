Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNE NSETENE PAIN A NE ANOMAA BAI WƆ AKYIRƐ MU

Wɔka sɛ tete no, na obi kɛseɛ bi wɔ hɔ, na nnoma bi nso na ɔsoro mmerɛ biara na ɔreba retu nnipa anim mpaabo. Ananse, Kɔkɔreko no, tee saa asɛm yi ho, enti ɔfaa kwan kɔɔ saa kurom no. Berɛ a ɔduu kurom no, ɔbisaa kuromfo no sɛ, “Adɛn nti na kuro kɛseɛ a ɛte sɛ eyi mu, nnipa nyinaa mpaabo awu?” Kuro no Odikro no kaa sɛ, “Agya, nnoma ketewa bi, berɛ a owia no gyina hɔ saa (ɔkyerɛɛ soro), na ɔba bɛka yɛn, na ɔtu yɛn mpaabo.” Kwaku Ananse, Kɔkɔreko no, kaa sɛ, “Saa asɛm yi, me nnyɛ adwene ho, na me ara na mɛkum no.” Akuraa no Odikro no kaa sɛ, “O, saa kuro yi yɛnni tumi bi, na wo nso, a wogyina hɔ nkoko, dɛn na wobɛtumi ayɛ?” Ananse kaa sɛ, “Hwɛ me bɛtɛm yi; sɛ mɛsetre no a, mɛtwa ne ti saa, bam!” Berɛ a owia no gyina hɔ (yehwie, anadwo mma), nnoma no baa na ɔtwɛɛ dua bi so, na ɔpawee ne nne:

“E, e, jaws, jaws, e, E!

Jaws, mereko, E!

Jaws, me aba, o!”

Na Kwebla (Kwebla) yɛɛ ne nne kɛse:

“Jaws, ne papa, e, E!

Jaws, ne papa, E!

Ne papa, ne papa, E!

Ne papa, me aba, o!

Ei! ne papa.”

Ɛna Animfoo no bae na ɔgyina kwan no awie. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Hwan nneɛma ne saa nneɛma a ɛgye me mmuaeɛ, na ɛdi me nananom ho dwum na ɛyɛ wɔn nkontompo? Mepueu kuro no nnipa nyinaa mpaabo, obiara nni hɔ a ɔtumi kasa.” Na ɔyɛɛ ne nne den bio:

“E, e, jaws, jaws, e, E!

Jaws, mereko, E!

Jaws, me aba, o!”

Na Ananse ayɛ mmuae sɛ:

“Jaws, ne papa, e, E!

Jaws, ne papa, E!

Ne papa, ne papa, E!

Ne papa, me aba, o!

Ɛɛ, ne papa.

Ɛna Kwaku Ananse baeɛ na ɔtwɛnɛ ne fie ponkuo no. Afei, asɛm ne sɛ, kuro no nnipa nyinaa dwatwaeɛ na wɔgyawii Ananse. Na Annum no tuu kwan baeɛ na ɔduu Kwaku Ananse fie ponkuo no; na Kwaku Ananse dwatwaeɛ kɔɔ ne pokuwo mu, na ɔtwaa nkyɛnkyɛn no (ponkuo no so), na Annum no gyinaeɛ na ɔtee ne nne:

“ E, e, jaws, jaws, e, E!

Jaws, mereko, E!”

Ɛnnɛ Kwaku Ananse tutu kɔɔ, na ɔtoo n'anim kɔtoo aduane kuku mu, na ɔkaa (ne nne) bombom:

“ Jaws, ne papa, e, E!

Jaws, ne papa, E!

Ne papa, me aba, o!

Ei! ne papa.”

Saa mprempren yi, Anomaa no repɛ sɛ ɔkɔ mu kɔpue aduane kuku no, na wakukoo Ananse, nanso Kwaku Ananse faa ne cutu twaree ne ti, bam! na Anomaa no ati hwee kɔree Mmpo-atifi-man. Kwaku Kwebia no faa mpaabo a ɛda Anomaa no so no too hɔ; na ɔtoo Anomaa no ti no ase; na ɔkɔfrɛɛ kuromfo no, ɔse, “Monyɛ mmo, efisɛ madi Anomaa no.” Wɔse, “O, Kwaku Ananse no, ɔyɛ atirigye a yɛnim no yie, ebia ɔnka nokware.” Wɔse, “Yɛnkye baakye a ɔwɔ nyansa na wakɔnhwɛ.” Baabi a ɔwɔ nyansa no kɔhwee baabi, na ɔhwɛe, na ɔhunuu sɛ aboa no ti da hɔ, na ɔkɔse, “Monyɛ mmo, mmofra, efisɛ Kwaku Ananse anka atirigye.” Nnipa nyinaa bae. Enti saa baa yi, berɛ a ɔfaa ne mpaabo no, ɔfaa no hyɛɛ mu. Nanso wo, a woannya wo dea, wo kɔfaa wo adamfo dea; ɛno nti na woya nsetee ya. Enti, fa Anomaa no ho a, wɔkɔfaa no fii Mmpo-atifi-man, na wɔtwee no kaa kaa ketewa, na wɔhyɛɛ no mmoa. Saa Asaaeɛ ne sɛdeɛ anomaa pii bae wɔ amansan mu.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, obiara betumi agye bewee sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na naayɛn nso betumi ayɛ me ayɛyie (ase sɛ mekae).
