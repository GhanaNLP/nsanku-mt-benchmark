Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNE NSETENE PAIN A NE ANOMAA BAI WƆ AKYIRƐ MU

Wɔka sɛ obiara nim kurom bi a ɛyɛ kɛse paa, na nnoma bi wɔ hɔ a ɔsoro mprempren da biara na wakɔtwe nkurɔfo no mpaabo. Ananse, Kɔkɔreko no, tee saa asɛm yi ho, enti ɔfaa kwan kɔɔ saa kurom no. Sɛ ɔduu kuropɔn no mu no, ɔkaa kyerɛɛ kuromfo no sɛ, “Adɛn nti na wɔ kuropɔn kɛseɛ a ɛte sɛ eyi mu, nnipa nyinaa a wɔwɔ ha a wɔn akramafuo nyinaa apue?” Kuromfo no mpanyin no kaa sɛ, “Agya, anomaa ketewa bi, berɛ a owia no gyina hɔ te sɛ eyi (ɔkyerɛɛ soro), ɔbaa ha na ɔkaaye yɛn, na ɔpue yɛn akramafuo.” Kwaku Ananse, Kɔkɔro no, kaa sɛ, “Sɛ ɛyɛ eyi a, meyɛ mmuaeɛ kyerɛ eyi, na mɛm “me ho ara na mɛyɛ no.” Kuro no mpanyimfo no see, “O, kuro yi ayɛ mmerɛ, na wo, obi baako a wo gyina hɔ, dɛn na wobɛtumi ayɛ?” Ananse see, “Hwɛ me bɛtɛm yi; sɛ mɛsetre no a, mɛtwa ne ti firi hɔ saa, bam!” Berɛ a owia no gyinaa hɔ (yehwie, awia mfidie) na Akufo no bae na ɔtwɛnɛa dua bi so, na ɔyɛɛ ne nne den:

“E, e, jaws, jaws, e, E!

Jaws, mereko, E!

Jaws, me aba, o!”

Na Kwebla (Kwebla) yɛɛ ne nne kɛse:

“Jaws, ne papa, e, E!

Jaws, ne papa, E!

Ne papa, ne papa, E!

Ne papa, me aba, o!

Ei! ne papa.”

Ɛna Animfoo no bae na ɔgyina kwan no awie. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Hwan nneɛma ne saa nneɛma a ɛgye me mmuaeɛ, na ɛdi me nananom ho dwum na ɛyɛ wɔn nkontompo? Mepueu kuro no nnipa nyinaa mpaabo, obiara nni hɔ a ɔtumi kasa.” Na ɔyɛɛ ne nne den bio:

“E, e, jaws, jaws, e, E!

Jaws, mereko, E!

Jaws, me aba, o!”

Na Ananse ayɛ mmuae sɛ:

“Jaws, ne papa, e, E!

Jaws, ne papa, E!

Ne papa, ne papa, E!

Ne papa, me aba, o!

Ɛɛ, ne papa.

Ɛna Kwaku Ananse baeɛ na ɔtwɛnɛ ne fie ponkuo no. Afei, asɛm ne sɛ, kuro no nnipa nyinaa dwatwaeɛ na wɔgyawii Ananse. Na Annum no tuu kwan baeɛ na ɔduu Kwaku Ananse fie ponkuo no; na Kwaku Ananse dwatwaeɛ kɔɔ ne pokuwo mu, na ɔtwaa ntentene no (wɔ ponkuo no ano), na Annum no gyinaeɛ na ɔtee ne nne:

“ E, e, jaws, jaws, e, E!

Jaws, mereko, E!”

Ɛnnɛ Kwaku Ananse tutu kɔɔ, na ɔtoo n'anim kɔtoo aduane kuku mu, na ɔkaa (ne nne) bombom:

“ Jaws, ne papa, e, E!

Jaws, ne papa, E!

Ne papa, me aba, o!

Ei! ne papa.”

Ɛnnɛ, Akufo no rehyɛ adanse no mu kɔ sɛ ɔbɛpue aduane kuku no, na ɔatumi akukua Ananse, nanso Kwaku Ananse faa ne bɛtɛɛ na ɔtwaa ne ti, bam! na Akufo no mma no baako tuu kɔ Bosompon-man. Se saa koro yi faa ne mpaaboae no, ɔde hyɛɛ ho. Na mo a moanni mo deɛ no, mokɔfaa mo nnamfo deɛ; ɛno nti na moya nsa na mo nyɔku yɛ mo yaw. Sei, efie no ho, wɔkɔfaa no fii Po-nkuran mu, na wɔtwaa no ayɛ nketenkete, na wɔhyɛɛ no mmaa. Saa saa na nnoma pii baa amansan no mu.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, obiara betumi agye baabi fofor sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na naayɛn nso betumi akye me ayɛyie (ase sɛ mekae).
