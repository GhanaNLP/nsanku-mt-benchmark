Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐ OBIAA PRAE WO ADE BI (KƆNKONKO), MMA SƐ WOPA SƐ WOGYE SIKA MA NO

Wɔka sɛ tete bi, twamfoɔ mmienu a wɔn maame awu na wagyaw wɔn (ankasa) tenaa bam. Dwadie bi na wɔreye, na twamfoɔ panin no, Ata Panin, tooku nkyekyem pa ne sika nketewa bi na ɔde hyɛɛ ne nua ketwa no, Ata Kuma, kɔn mu. Twamfoɔ ketwa no ne ne nnamfoɔ kaa sɛ wɔrekɔ asubɔn akɔ ho. Berɛ a wɔreho no, nsoroma a sika nketewa no hyɛe ho wɔ Ata Kuma kɔn mu no nyinaa puee, na sika no too nsu mu. Sɛnea ɔhwehwɛe no, ɔanhunu biara. Ɔsan baa fie bekasa ne nua no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Sika nketewa no ayera." Ata Panin (twamfoɔ panin no) kaa sɛ, "Menim sɛ ayera, na sɛ ayera mpo a, ade bi wɔ a mɛpɛ firi wo hɔ." Ata Kuma (twamfoɔ ketwa no) bisaa sɛ, "Dɛn na wopɛ firi me hɔ?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mepɛ ɔpuna a ɛyɛ gyata dea, Aserewa nnoma ketewa no nkuo, Nsu a ɛyɛ ponponponponsa, aba a ɛyɛ baako pɛ (wɔ dua a wofa no firi hɔ no so), ne Ena a ɔyɛ Elephants mmaa nyinaa panin no ntadeɛ." Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, "Mete." Ata Kuma hyɛɛ aseɛ (kɔhwehwɛ aaedeɛ yi), na ɔbaa baabi a dua baako pɛ na ɛgyina hɔ. Berɛ a dua no hunuu no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Ata Kuma aba ha, (a nnipa ka sɛ) ɔba kurom mu te sɛ osuo a ɛyɛ dɛ." (Ata Kuma) kaa sɛ, "Nea ama me aba ha ne sɛ, agya baa bi yɛ Ata Panin, na maame baa bi nso yɛ Ata Panin, na ɔde nkyekyem fɛfɛɛfɛ hyɛɛ me kɔn mu, na ɛpuee na ɛyera." (Wɔ ha na Ata Kuma hyɛɛ aseɛ dwene):

Ɔka sɛ ɔpɛ biribi fi me hɔ.

Ɔrehyɛ me ase sɛ memma no aba a ɛfiri dua bi a, aba baako pɛ na ɛwɔ so.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrepɛ biribi fi me hɔ.

Ɔrehyɛ me nkonimdua.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrepɛ biribi fi me hɔ.

Ɔreka sɛ Aserewa nnoma no muo no neyɛ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrepɛ biribi fi me hɔ.

Ɔrehyɛ nsuo, ɔreponponponponsa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔpɛ ne ade bi fii me hɔ. Ɔpɛ ɔkyena a ɛyɛ ɔpapa Leopard deɛ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrepɛ biribi fi me hɔ.

Ɔreka sɛ ɔpɛ Elephants Queen Mother no ase kɔsi ne ntɛm.

Na dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ, O agya, na anya bi?

Ɔse abaayewa ketewa bi wɔ ha, ɔyɛ Akwasi Toboro Kojo baa.

Kojo Nsa.

Papa Elephant, eyɛ yie sɛ wokɔ, eyɛ yie sɛ wofiri ha.

Kojo Nsa a a.

Na Dua a aba koro pɛ wɔ ne so no see no sɛ, “Ɛno nti na woresu, bra na ma wo biribi.” Ata Kuma kɔfaa (aba a ɛwɔ dua a aba koro pɛ wɔ ne so no), na ɔdaa no (dua no) ase. Na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔhyiaa Aserewa anomaa. Na Aserewa anomaa no see no sɛ, “Hwan ne Ata Kuma, a nea ɔyɛ ne sɛ, sɛ ɔba kurom mpo a, obiara se ɔte sɛ nsuo a ɛyɛ dɛ a ɛtɔ.” Ata Kuma hyyɛɛ no ase sɛ, “Nea ama me aba ha ne sɛ, me papa baa bi wɔ hɔ a yɛfrɛ no Ata ’Panin, na me maame baa bi wɔ hɔ a yɛfrɛ no Ata ’Panin, na ɔtan nketia a ɛyɛ nsamanu na ɔde hyɛɛ me, na ɛbɔeɛ na ɛyi gyee.” (Na wɔ ha no, Ata Kuma hyɛɛ nnwom):

Ɔka sɛ ɔpɛ biribi fi me hɔ.

Ɔrehyɛ me ase sɛ memma no aba a ɛfiri dua bi a, aba baako pɛ na ɛwɔ so.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrepɛ biribi fi me hɔ.

Ɔrehyɛ me nkonimdua aba.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrepɛ biribi fi me hɔ.

Ɔreka sɛ Aserewa nnoma no muo no neyɛ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrepɛ biribi fi me hɔ.

Ɔrehyɛ nsuo, ɔreponponponponsa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrepɛ biribi fi me hɔ.

Ɔreka sɛ ɔyɛ Agya Leopard mramase.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrepɛ biribi fi me hɔ.

Ɔreka sɛ ɔpɛ Elephants Queen Mother no ase kɔsi ne ntɛm.

Na dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ, O agya, na anya bi?

Ɔse abaayewa ketewa bi wɔ ha, ɔyɛ Akwasi Toboro Kojo baa.

Kojo Nsa.

Papa Elephant, eyɛ yie sɛ wokɔ, eyɛ yie sɛ wofiri ha.

Kojo Nsa a a.

Aserewa nnoma no see, “Ɛno nti na wosure, bra na mma wo biribi.” Ata Kuma nso kɔtoo (Aserewa nnoma no mpa), na ɔdaa no ase. Ɔkɔe, na ɔhyia Nsuo, a ɔreponponponponsa. Ɔsee, “Ata Kuma ara ne wei, nea sɛ ɔyɛ mfiase kwan no mu a, nnipa nso nte ase te sɛ osuo a ɛyɛ dɛ.” Ata Kuma ka amanneɛ a ɔaka kyerɛ afofor no no kyerɛ no. Nsuo a ɔreponponponponsa no maa no (ne nsuo bi), na ɔfaa no kɔe. Akyi no, ɔhyiaa Gyata. Berɛ a Gyata huu no, ɔsee, “Ata Kuma ara ne wei; sɛ ɔyɛ mfiase kwan no mu a, ɔte sɛ osuo a ɛyɛ dɛ.” Ata Kuma ka ne dwumadie no kyerɛ no. Ɔsee, “Bra na mma wo biribi,” (na ɔmaa no ne nsa mmienu mu baako). Ata Kuma nso see, “Gyata Agya, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ na matumi anya Elephants Queen Mother no ase?” Ɔsee, "Ah, sɛ wokɔ a, mmoa nyinaa bɛda ase, na Elephants Queen Mother no bɛda mfinimfini, na sɛ wokɔ a, tra wɔn so dennen, na sɛ wo du Elephants Queen Mother hɔ a, twa ne ase, nanso sɛ wotra wɔn so ntanntan a, wɔbɛkum wo." Ata Kuma see, "Meda wo ase." Na berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu mmoa yi; ɔtraa wɔn so dennen paa, na ɔduu Queen Mother hɔ. Ɔtwaa ne ase, na ɔsan kɔe. W’akwan no so no, ɔtɔɔ mmofra-kyɛn (looking-glass) baako, na ɔfaa no (kɔ fie). Ɔduu fie. Ɔsee, "Ata 'Panin, eyi ne nnoa a wose sɛ wobɛfa afiri me hɔ." Ata 'Panin see, "Me nsa aka wɔn." Da koro bi, wɔdae mma, na Ata Kuma faa kyɛn no na ɔhwɛɛ mu. Ɔsee, "Kyɛn yi, sɛ obi hwɛ mu a, ɔhunu ne maame a ɔwɔ Asaman." Ata 'Panin see, "Ɛyɛ saa anaa, fa mma me na mehwɛ." Ɔmaa no. Berɛ a ɔrepɛ sɛ ɔhwɛ mu no, ɛtɔɔ ne nsa mu, na ɛbɔe. Ata Kuma see, "Sɛn na eyi, nea wugyee afiri me hɔ no, mɛpɛ sɛ wode ma me." Ata 'Panin nso kɔfaa nnoa a Ata Kuma de baa ma no no. Ata Kuma see, "Mempɛ (yei), nea mepɛ ne sɛ wokɔtɔ foforo baa ma me." Ata 'Panin kɔe, na ɔhyiaa dua a nnaba baako pɛ wɔ no so. Ɔsee, "Ata 'Panin ara ne wei, nea sɛ ɔyɛ mfiase kwan no mu a, nnipa nte ase te sɛ osuo." Ata 'Panin hyyɛɛ no mmuae, "Wo maame na ɔyɛ 'mfiase kwan no mu'!" ne wo papa nso!, sɛ me ho asɛm a, ma me wo aba koro no na menkɔ." Dua a aba koro nni so no see no sɛ, "Kɔ anim, obi a ɔnim (sɛnea wɔgye wo) wɔ hɔ." Ɔkɔe, na ɔhyiaa Aserewa anomaa. Aserewa anomaa no see no sɛ, "Hwan ne Ata 'Panin; sɛ ɔba kuro no kwan awiee no, ɔyɛ tɛnteɛ te sɛ osuo a ɛtɔ." Ata 'Panin see no sɛ, "Sɛ me 'ba' wo maame ne wo papa, me ho asɛm a, nea mepɛ ne wo mpanimpon mu baako, na menkɔ." Aserewa anomaa no see no sɛ, "Kɔ anim, obi a ɔnim (sɛnea wɔgye wo) wɔ hɔ." Ɛnnɛ ɔhyiaa Nsuo-a-re-pue. Nsuo-a-re-pue see no sɛ, "Hwan ne Ata 'Panin; sɛ ɔba kurom awiee no, ɔte sɛ osuo a ɛtɔ na ɛyɛ tɛnteɛ." Ata 'Panin see no sɛ, "Sɛ me 'ba' wo maame, sɛ me 'ba' wo papa, me ho asɛm a, ma me wo nsuo kakra na menkɔ anim." Nsuo-a-re-pue see no sɛ, "Kɔ anim, obi a ɔnim (sɛnea wɔgye wo) wɔ hɔ." Akyi no ɔhyiaa Aberewa. Aberewa no see no sɛ, "Hwan ne Ata 'Panin, a sɛ ɔba kwan awiee no, ɔyɛ tɛnteɛ te sɛ osuo a ɛtɔ." Ata 'Panin hyyɛɛ no mmuae sɛ, "Me 'ba' wo maame! ne wo papa! me ho asɛm a, ma me wo nsa kakra na menkɔ anim." Aberewa no see no sɛ, "Obi a ɔnim (sɛnea wɔgye wo) wɔ hɔ." Ɔkɔe, na ɔhyiaa nnua mmoa, na nnua mmoa no Hemaa da mmuamea. Ɛnna ɔkɔtwee ne ntadeɛ. Nnua mmoa no nyinaa kyee no, na wɔtramaa no, na ɔwui. Ata Kuma hwehwɛɛ ne nua wɔ kwan no so. Ɔanba. Ɛno nti na yɛse, "Sɛ wo ne wo abusua bi te hɔ, na baako wɔn mu siesie wo nneɛma bi, mma wo mfa sika anaa nneɛma mfa mfa mfa no, efisɛ Ata 'Panin yɛɛ saa, na ɔkɔwui."

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
