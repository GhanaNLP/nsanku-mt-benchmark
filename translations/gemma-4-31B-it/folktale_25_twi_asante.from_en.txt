Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐ OBIAA PRAE WO ADE BI (KƆNKONKO), MMA SƐ WOPA SƐ WOGYE SIKA MA NO

Wɔka sɛ tete bi, twamfoɔ a wɔn maame awu na ɔgyaa wɔn (nko) bi tenaa bam. Dwadie bi na wɔreye, enti twamfoɔ panin no (Ata Panin) took precious beads ne sika nketewa bi na ɔde hyɛɛ twamfoɔ ketewa no, Ata Kuma, kɔn mu. Twamfoɔ ketewa no ne ne nnamfoɔ kae sɛ wɔrekɔ asubɔn kɔyɛ wɔn ho nwiase. Berɛ a wɔredi aduane no, nsoroma a ɛyɛ sika a ɛyɛ Ata Kuma kɔn no nso, ne nsoroma no nso kɔtɔeɛ, na eyɛyɛyɛ no nyinaa tɔɔ nsuo no mu. Sɛdeɛ ɔhwehwɛe no, ɔanhunu biara. Ɔba fie kɔka kyerɛ ne nua sɛ, "Sika nketese no ayera." Ata Panin se, "Mennim sɛ ayera, na sɛ ayera mpo a, ade bi wɔ a mɛpɛ fi wo hɔ." Ata Kuma bisaa sɛ, "Dɛn na wopɛ fi me hɔ?" Ɔse, "Mepɛ leopard mmoa no nsa, Aserewa nnoma nketese no mpa, Nsu a gur gles ponponponponsa, aba a ɛyɛ baako pɛ (wɔ dua a wofua no no so), ne Ena Adaa a ɔyɛ Atufoɔ mmaa no ntadeɛ. Ata Kuma see, "Mase." Ata Kuma hyɛɛ aseɛ (deɛ ɔbɛhwehwɛ saa nnoɔsaa yi), na ɔduu beaeɛ bi a dua baako pɛ na ɛgyina hɔ. Berɛ a dua no huu no, ɔse, "Ata Kuma wei ne no, (a nnipa se) ɔmi gyina kurom mfiase te sɛ osuo a ɛyɛ dɛ." (Ata Kuma) se, "Nea ama meaba ha ne sɛ, agya baa ne Ata 'Panin, na ɛnaa baa nso ne Ata 'Panin, na ɔde nketia a ɛyɛ nsamanhyia hyɛɛ me kɔn, na ɛbɔɔ na ɛyi gyina hɔ." (Wha na Ata Kuma fii n'asuo mu g):

Ɔka sɛ ɔpɛ biribi fi me hɔ.

Ɔrehyɛ me ase sɛ memma no aba a ɛfiri dua bi a, aba baako pɛ na ɛwɔ so.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrepɛ biribi fi me hɔ.

Ɔrehyɛ me nkonimdua.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrepɛ biribi fi me hɔ.

Ɔreka sɛ Aserewa nnoma no muo no neyɛ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrepɛ biribi fi me hɔ.

Ɔrehyɛ nsuo, ɔreponponponponsa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔpɛ ne ade bi fii me hɔ. Ɔpɛ ɔkyena a ɛyɛ ɔpapa Leopard deɛ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrepɛ biribi fi me hɔ.

Ɔreka sɛ ɔpɛ Elephants Queen Mother no ase kɔsi ne ntɛm.

Na dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ, O agya, na anya bi?

Ɔse abaayewa ketewa bi wɔ ha, ɔyɛ Akwasi Toboro Kojo baa.

Kojo Nsa.

Papa Elephant, eyɛ yie sɛ wokɔ, eyɛ yie sɛ wofiri ha.

Kojo Nsa a a.

Na Dua a aba koro pɛ wɔ ne so no see no sɛ, “Ɛno nti na woresu, bra na nya biribi.” Na Ata Kuma kɔtoo na ɔnyaa (aba a ɛwɔ dua a aba koro pɛ wɔ ne so no), na ɔda no ase (dua no). Na ɔkɔɔ anim, na ɔhyiaa Aserewa anomaa bi. Na Aserewa nnoma no see sɛ, “Ata Kuma wɔ ha, nea obiara se sɛ ɔba kurom mfiase a, ɔte sɛ nsuo a ɛyɛ dɛ.” Ata Kuma anyaa mmuae sɛ, “Nea ama meaba ha ne sɛ, agya baa bi yɛ Ata ’Panin, na ɛna baa bi nso yɛ Ata ’Panin, na ɔtan nketia a ɛyɛ nneɛma pii na ɔde hyɛɛ me, na ɛbɔɔ na ɛyi gyinaa.” (Na ha na Ata Kuma twa yɛyɛ anuonyam anaa nnwom:

Ɔka sɛ ɔpɛ biribi fi me hɔ.

Ɔrehyɛ me ase sɛ memma no aba a ɛfiri dua bi a, aba baako pɛ na ɛwɔ so.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrepɛ biribi fi me hɔ.

Ɔrehyɛ me nkonimdua aba.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrepɛ biribi fi me hɔ.

Ɔreka sɛ Aserewa nnoma no muo no neyɛ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrepɛ biribi fi me hɔ.

Ɔrehyɛ nsuo, ɔreponponponponsa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrepɛ biribi fi me hɔ.

Ɔreka sɛ ɔyɛ Agya Leopard mramase.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrepɛ biribi fi me hɔ.

Ɔreka sɛ ɔpɛ Elephants Queen Mother no ase kɔsi ne ntɛm.

Na dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ, O agya, na anya bi?

Ɔse abaayewa ketewa bi wɔ ha, ɔyɛ Akwasi Toboro Kojo baa.

Kojo Nsa.

Papa Elephant, eyɛ yie sɛ wokɔ, eyɛ yie sɛ wofiri ha.

Kojo Nsa a a.

Aserewa nnoma no see sɛ, “Ɛno nti na wosusu, bra na mma wo biribi.” Na Ata Kuma kɔtoo (Aserewa nnoma no mpa), na ɔdaa no ase. Na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔe, na ɔhyiaa Nsuo, a ɔreponponponponsa. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ata Kuma ne wei, nea sɛ ɔbaa kuro no kwan akyiri a, nnipa nso nteɛ no sɛ nsuo a ɛyɛ dɛ a ɛtɔ.” Ata Kuma kae amanneɛ no a ɔaka kyerɛɛ afofor no dennen. Na Gurgling-water maa no bi (efi ne gurgling water no mu), na ɔfaa no na ɔkɔɔ anim. Akyi no, ɔhyiaa Leopard. Berɛ a Opuanu huu no, ɔsee no sɛ, “Ata Kuma wɔ ha; sɛ ɔba kuro no mu kwan no awie a, ɔte sɛ nsuo a ɛyɛ dɛ a ɛtɔ.” Ata Kuma kae ne dwumadie no kyerɛɛ no. Ɔsee no sɛ, “Bra na mia nneɛma bi, (na ɔmaa no ne nsa mmienu mu baako).” Ɛna Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, “Papa Gyata, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ na matumi anya Enyama-Kuku-Kuku a ɛyɛ mmaa no ntade?” Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ah, sɛ worekɔ a, mmoa nyinaa bɛda ase, na Enyama-Kuku-Kuku a ɛyɛ mmaa no bɛda mfidie no mu, na sɛ worekɔ a, tra wɔn so dennen, na sɛ odu Enyama-Kuku-Kuku a ɛyɛ mmaa no hɔ a, twa ne ntade no, nanso sɛ wotra wɔn so bo bo a, wɔn "Mɛyɛ wo mmerɛ." Ata Kuma see, "Meda wo ase." Na berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu mmoa yi; ɔtramaa wɔn dennen, na ɔduu Ɔhemaa hɔ. Ɔtwaa ne ntadeɛ, na ɔsan kɔɔ efie. Wɔ kwan no so no, ɔtɔɔ mmofra-kyɛn (looking-glass), na ɔfaa no (fie). Na ɔduu fie. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panin, eyi ne nnoɔma a wokae sɛ wobɛfa fii me hɔ." Ata 'Panin kaa sɛ, "Me nsa aka wɔn." Ɛnnɛ, da koro bi wɔ a wɔda kɔn nso, na Ata Kuma faa mmaa no na ɔhwɛɛ mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mmaa yi, sɛ obi hwɛ mu a, ɔhu ne maame a ɔwɔ Brumo Asase." Ata 'Panin kaa sɛ, "Ɛno ne sɛn, fa mma me na me nhwɛ." Ɔmaa no. Berɛ a ɔrepɛ sɛ ɔhwɛ mu no, ɛyɛɛ ne nsa so na ɛbɔɔ. Ata Kuma see, "Sɛnne yi, nea wugyee fii me hɔ no, mɛbɔ wo ho asɛm." Ɛnna Ata 'Panin kɔfaa nnoemaa a Ata Kuma de aba ma no no. Ata Kuma see, "Mmpɛ (yei), nea mepɛ ne sɛ wokɔfa foforɔ aba ama me." Ata 'Panin tɔne ne nan kɔe, na ɔhyiaa dua bi a aduane baako pɛ na ɛwɔ ho. Ɔse, "Hwan ne Ata 'Panin, nea sɛ ɔba kuro no kwan akyiri a, ɔno nso yɛ anigye sɛ osuo tɔ." Ata 'Panin anyaa no ase sɛ, "Madi 'mfi' wo nawo! ne wo papa!, sɛ m'asɛm ne me a, ma me wo abaayewa baako no na mɛkɔ." (Duahyeɛ a) abaayewa baako no wɔ no se, "Kɔ anim, nea ɔnim (sɛnea wɔgye wo) wɔ hɔ." Ɔkɔe, na ɔhyiaa Aserewa anomaa bi. Aserewa nnoma no see sɛ, "Hwan ne Ata 'Panin; sɛ ɔba kuro no kwan akyiri a, ɔyɛ dɛ te sɛ osuo a ɛtɔ." Ata 'Panin see sɛ, "Sɛ me 'ba' wo maame ne wo papa a, me nso, nea mepɛ ne wo mmoa mu baako, na ma me nkɔ." Aserewa nnoma no see sɛ, "Kɔ anim, obi a ɔnim (sɛnea wɔgye wo) wɔ hɔ." Ɛna ɔhyiaa Gurgling-water. Gurgling-water see sɛ, "Ata 'Panin wɔ ha; sɛ ɔba kurom mpanin hɔ a, ɔte sɛ nsuo a ɛyɛ dɛ a ɛtɔ." Ata 'Panin see sɛ, "Sɛ me 'ba' wo naama, sɛ me 'ba' wo papa, m'asɛm ne sɛ, ma me wo nsuo kakra na matwa kwan." Gurgling-water see sɛ, "Twene kwan no, obi a ɔnim (sɛnea ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ wo) wɔ hɔ." Afei ɔhyiaa Leopard. Ɔpyɛ ne sɛ, "Hwan ne Ata 'Panin, nea ɔyɛ dɛ te sɛ osuo a ɛtɔ wɔ kwan no awie no." Ata 'Panin anyaa no sɛ, "Me 'mi' wo nawo! ne wo papa!" "Sɛ me ho asɛm a, ma me wo nsa mmienu na ma me nkɔ." Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Obi a ɔnim (sɛnea ɛyɛ a ɔbɛtumi agyina wo) wɔ hɔ." Ɔkɔeɛ, na ɔhyia Elephants mmofra bi, na Elephants no Queen Mother no da mma mu. Ɛnna ɔkɔtwee ne ntade no. Elephants no nyinaa kyere no, na wɔtram no, na ɔwui. Ata Kuma hwehwɛɛ ne nuabaa no wɔ kwan no so. Ɔanba. Ɛno nti na yɛse, "Sɛ wo ne wo abusua bi te wɔ hɔ, na baako wɔn mu siesie wo nneɛma bi, a mma wɔmmpɛ sika, efisɛ Ata 'Panin yɛɛ saa, na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔwui."

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
