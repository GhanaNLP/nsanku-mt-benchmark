SƐNE EFUO, KƆBƆ-KƆBƆ NO, NE NTADUA BƐYƐƐ FUFU

Wɔka sɛ (tete bi) na Esono, Kurentene; Kwakuo, Aberewa; Anyinhima, Aberewa a ne hwene yɛ nketewa; ne Efuo, Aberewa kɔkɔɔ teyɛe; na wɔka sɛ wɔbɛkɔ kuro bi mu akɔhwehwɛ mmaa a wɔbɛware. Enti wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ. Berɛ a wɔrekɔ no, wɔhyiaa tɛntɛn mudu bi. Aberewa a ne hwene yɛ nketewa no see, "Gyina hɔ na mebisie mo biribi." Ɔsee, "Wɔ akwantuo a yɛreyɛ yi mu, hwan (wɔ yɛn mu) na ne ho yɛ fɛ?" Esono see, "Ɛyɛ Efuo, Aberewa kɔkɔɔ no." Kwakuo (aberewa foforo no) see, "Sɛ saa na ɛte no, ma yɛnbɔ no, na yɛntu no atɔ mudu no mu." Nokwarene, wɔbɔɔ no, na wɔfaa no tutu no mudu no mu, na wɔkɔɔ anim. Afei Efuo, Aberewa kɔkɔɔ no soree (firi mudu no mu), na ɔsuae wɔn akyi kɔkɔɔ, (ɔnantee) brɛoo, na ɔkɔ tweɛ n'anim tenaa kuro no mmoa-dwomo no so. Na nnipa a wɔda so no dodo kuro no, wɔhunuu mmaa ketewa a wɔahyia Gyedua-dua bi ase. Mmaa ketewa no hunuu wɔn. Baako see, "Me pɛ eyi"; ɔfoforo see, "Me pɛ eyi." Na wɔwaree mmaa no nyinaa. Na ɔbaa ketewa baako wɔ hɔ, a ɔkɔɔ afuo, (nanso ansa na wakɔ no) ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Sɛ mmeranteɛ no ba a, paapaa baako ma me." Na berɛ a ɔfiri afuo no bae no, ɔbisie ne maame sɛ, "Me deɛ ɛwɔ he?" Ɔgyee me answers sɛ, "Berɛ a mekɔe (kɔhunuu ho) no, na wɔakyekyere (mmeranteɛ no) kyɛ wɔn nkyɛn." Baa ketewa no see, "Ɛnyɛ hwee." Na ɔpuee agutukuru bi, na ɔreka ne ho tɔ mmoa-dwomo no so.

Ɛnnɛ, Colobus Monkey no bae na ɔtwɛɛ n'anim tenaa nnwuma-dwomo no so. Abofra baa no repɛ sɛ ɔto mmoa no so, na Colobus Monkey no see no sɛ, “Mma mfa nto me so.” Abofra baa no bisaa sɛ, “Hwan ne saa?” Ɔsee no sɛ, “Me ne me.” Abofra baa no bisaa sɛ, “Dɛn na ɛrekɔ so?” Ɔsee no sɛ, “Me ne me adamfo a yɛdan mmoa nyinaa reba sɛ yɛbɛware mo mmaa, nanso wɔbɔɔ me, na wɔmaa me nipadua nyinaa dwurui, ɛno nti na maba ha aba.” Ɔsee no sɛ, “Me nso, me dɔ wo, enti bra na yɛnkɔ fie.” Wɔnyinaa kɔe, na ɔfaa nsuo na ɔhoroo no. Berɛ a ɔfaa fofie na ɔde hohoroɔ ne nipadua kakra, ɔhunuu sɛ ne nipadua no hyerɛn yie. Abofra baa no see sɛ, “Maame, bra na hwɛ, efisɛ ahofra a ɛte saa yi nyɛ ade a yɛbɛyɛ no fɛw.” Na na maame no see no sɛ, “Horo no ma woawie.” Ɔno nso horoo no ma ɔwiee, na ɔde nnuane guu ne ho, na ɔfaa ne sandals, na ɔfaa ne sankuo, na ɔkɔtee Gyedua-tree no ase, na ɔdane. Ɛnnɛ, mmaa a wɔawɔware no, berɛ a wɔhunuu no, eyi see sɛ, “Mendɔ me kunu bio,” foforo see sɛ, “Mendɔ me kunu bio, Black-Colobus Monkey no na medɔ.” Black-Colobus Monkey no see sɛ, “Obaa a m'anya no, me dɔ no.” Afei mmoa nyinaa kɔree sɛ wɔrekyia wɔn ho, na wɔse wɔrekɔbo wɔn nneɛma na wɔasan aba. Na berɛ a mmoa no kɔe no, anka wɔankɔ (so), na wɔkɔtenaa kwan no so, na wɔhwɛɛ Black-Colobus Monkey no sɛ ɔbɛba na wɔabɔ no na wɔawu no. Black-Colobus Monkey no nso kɔree sɛ ɔrekyia wɔn, ɔse, “Me nso, mɛkɔbo me nneɛma na mɛsan aba.” Na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ. Berɛ a ɔduu kwan no so, mmoa no hunuu no; wɔsee, “Black-Colobus Monkey no aba, Black-Colobus Monkey no aba.” Ɛno nti Black-Colobus Monkey no kuraa dua (dua baako) (na ɔtwee ne ho), kra! kra! kra! Afei mmoa nofaa honam fitaa na wɔtoo no so, na kakra faa ne nsɔre so. Ɛno nenea ne nsɔre no bae fitaa; na kakra nso bɔɔ ne hwen. Ɛno nti na ne hwen no bae fitaa. Tete no, Black-Colobus Monkey no nsɔre ne hwen no yɛɛ tun, tun, tun, tuntum yie.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
