SƐNE EFUO, KƆBƆ-KƆBƆ NO, NE NTADUA BƐYƐƐ FUFU

Wɔka sɛ (tete bi) na Esono, Kete; Kwakuo, Aberewa; Anyinhima, Aberewa a ne hwene yɛ nketewa; ne Efuo, Aberewa kɔkɔɔ tenaa hɔ; na wɔse wɔbɛkɔ kuro bi mu akɔ hwehwe mmaa a wɔbɛware. Enti wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ. Berɛ a wɔrekɔ no, wɔhyiaa tɛntɛn bi a muduu mu. Putty-nosed Monkey no see sɛ, "Gyina hɔ pɛ sɛ mebisra wo biribi." Ɔsee sɛ, "Akwantuo a yɛreka yi mu no, hwan (yɛn mu) na ɔyɛ fɛ?" Elephant no see sɛ, "Efuo, Black-Colobus Monkey no na ɔyɛ fɛ." Kwakuo (monkey foforo no) see sɛ, "Sɛ saa na ɛte sɛn a, ma yɛnbɔ no, na yɛntow no akɔ mudu pool no mu." Nokware sɛ, wɔbɔɔ no, na wɔfaa no tow kyekyere mudu pool no mu, na wɔkɔe. Ɛnna Colobus Monkey soe (efi asuom no mu), na ɔsuae wɔn kɔm kɔm, (ɔnantee) brɛoo, na ɔkɔtee n'ananu wɔ akuraa no mmoa-dwomo no so. Na nnipa baako a wɔduruu akuraa no, wɔhunuu mmaa ketewa a wɔahyia Gyedua-dua bi ase. Mmaa ketewa no huu hɔn. Eyi kaa sɛ, "Me pɛ eyi"; eyi kaa sɛ, "Me pɛ eyi." Na wɔwarem mmaa no nyinaa. Ɛna abofra baa bi wɔ hɔ, a ɔkɔɔ afuo, (nanso ansa na wakɔ) ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Sɛ mmeranteɛ no ba a, paa baako ma me." Na ɔno nso ansa na wakɔ afuo no, ɔbisii ne maame sɛ, "Me deɛ ɛwɔ he?" Ɔgyee no ase sɛ, "Berɛ a mekɔeɛ (kɔtwee ho asɛm no), na wɔakyekyere (mmeranteɛ no) kyɛ wɔn nso." Abofra baa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ hwee Na ɔpuee borɔdeɛ bi ho po, na ɔrepɛ sɛ ɔto n’atadeɛ no mmoa-atadeɛ mpɔtɛ no so.

Seesei, Colobus Monkey no aba na wode n'anim atena nnwuma-atwa no so. Abofra baa no repɛ sɛ otow nwasaeɛ no too so, na Colobus Monkey no see no sɛ, “Matow no too me so.” Abofra baa no bisaa sɛ, “Hwan ne no?” Ɔsee no sɛ, “Me ne no.” Abofra baa no bisaa sɛ, “Dɛn na asi?” Ɔsee no sɛ, “Me ne me adamfo a yɛboaboaboa ano no reba sɛ yɛbɛware (mo) mmaa, na wɔbɔɔ me, na wɔmaa me nipadua nyinaa dɔɔ, ɛno nti na maba ha aba sɛ mɛda.” Ɔsee no sɛ, “Me nso, me dɔ wo, enti bra na yɛnkɔ fie.” Wɔn adua kɔe, na ɔkɔfaa nsuo na ɔhohoroo no. Berɛ a ɔfaa nsa-mmoa kwan hyɛɛ no ho kakra no, ɔhunuu sɛ ne honam no yɛ fɛ na ɛhyerɛne paa. Abofra no see, “Maame, bra na hwɛ, efisɛ ahohorow a ɛte saa yi nyɛ ade a obi bɛyɛ ho ayɛ.” Na na maame no see, “Wɔho ho ho ho.” Na ɔho ho ho ho, na ɔde nnuane guu ne ho, na ɔfaa ne santamaa, na ɔfaa ne sankuo, na ɔkɔɔ Gyedua-dua no ase kɔtee hɔ, na ɔdane. Ɛna mmaa a wɔawɔ asoma no, mpo sɛ wɔahu no, eyi kaa sɛ, “Mendɔ me kunu bio,” ɛno nso kaa sɛ, “Mendɔ me kunu bio, ɔno a medɔ ne Black-Colobus Monkey.” Black-Colobus Monkey no kaa sɛ, “Nea meanya no, medɔ no.” Ɛna mmoa nyinaa kɔɔ hɔ kɔtoo nnamfo, na wɔka sɛ wɔrekɔfa wɔn nnoɔma na wɔasane aba. Na mmoa no kɔeɛ no, dɛn na ɛyɛɛ? Wɔn ankɔ (so), na wɔkɔtena kwan no so, na wɔhwɛ Black-Colobus Monkey no sɛnea ɛbɛba na wɔabebɔ no na wɔawu no. Black-Colobus Monkey no nso kɔgyaei wɔn, na ɔse, “Me nso, mrekɔbo me nnoɔma na maba.” Na ɔkɔeɛ. Sɛ ɔduu kwan no so no, mmoa no huu no; wɔse, “Black-Colobus Monkey aba, Black-Colobus Monkey aba.” Ɛnna Black-Colobus Monkey kuraa dua baako (na ɔtwene kɔ soro), kra! kra! kra! Ɛnna mmoa nofaa hyire-ntadeɛ na wɔtowee no so, na ebɛyɛɛ ne nsow so. Asaaeɛ no ne sɛnea ne nsow no yɛɛ fitaa; na ebɛyɛɛ ne bam so nso. Asaaeɛ no ne sɛnea ne bam no yɛɛ fitaa. Tete no, Black-Colobus Monkey no ase ne ne bam na ɛyɛ tun, tun, tun, tuntum paa.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
