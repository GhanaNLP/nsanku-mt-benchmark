YƐNMPAƐ KƆKƆƆRE BI FI HƆ, MA YƐKƆTƆW ABƆREƐ MA ASEREWA, NTONSƐ NO

ANANSE, Kwebia no, na ɔte hɔ, na Adae Kwasieda bi, ɔkɔɔ Nyame hɔ kɔmaa no akye. Nyame maa no nnuaba mpaebɔ bi. Ananse faa no na ɔkɔɔ. Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, na obiara na ɔwɔ asubɔn hɔ rewa ntoma. Ananse kaa kyerɛ wɔn sɛ ma yɛn nnuaba mpaebɔ yi na monma mo ba no. Wɔn nyinii no na wɔmaa abofra no. Ananse see, “Ma me biribi na matumi akɔ.” Ɛnna wɔfaa nsuo guu kuku mu maa no, na ɔfaa no kɔɔ. Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa nnipa a wɔreye nkyene na wɔde ayɛ yam, na Ananse maa wɔn nsuo nom. Nnipa no maa no yam baako. Ɔkɔɔ so na ɔhyiaa mpanyimfo a ɔreche charcoal. Ɔmaa no yam no, na ɔkaa sɛ ɔnnyɛ charcoal no na onom, efisɛ charcoal nnom nyɛ yie. Na Ananse see, “Ma me biribi na matumi akɔ.” Ɔmaa no soso, na ɔfaa no kɔɔ. Ɔkɔe na ɔhunuu ɔbarima bi, a ɔde ne yefie retwene abrobɛ. Ananse maa no soso no, na ɔno nso maa Ananse abrobɛ nkrona, nsuo kuku baako a ɛyɛ dodo. Na ɔkɔɔ so. Ɔkɔɔ so na ɔhyiaa obaa bi a ɔwoaa mmofra du, na ɔde ne nsuo rehyɛ wɔn nipai. Kwaku Ananse faa nkrona no maa obaa no, na ama ɔatumi ahyɛ ne mmofra no. Obaa no maa no abofra baako, na ɔfaa no kɔɔ, na ɔkɔɔ so hyiaa obaa bi. Obaa no nni abofra, na ɔde nnuaba mpaebɔ a ɛyɛ dua na ɔde nketia hyɛɛ ho. Ɛnna Papa Ananse maa abofra no obaa no, na obaa no nso maa no dua nnuaba mpaebɔ no, na ɔfaa no kɔɔ. Ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔhyiaa obi a ɔde ne nsa nkyɛn reto mmoa. Ananse maa no nnuaba mpaebɔ no, na ɔno nso maa Ananse mmoa. Ɔfaa no kɔɔ, na ɔhyiaa Elephants mmoa bi. Elephants no na wɔrenom nsuo wɔ mud hole bi mu. Ananse see, “Fa mmoa yi na monnom, efisɛ saa mud pool yi nyɛ yie.” Ɛnna Elephants no faa no nom.
