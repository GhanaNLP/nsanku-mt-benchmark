Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

YƐNMPAƐ ENNAMA BI AKYƐN ENNAMA KRONKRON, NA YƐKƆTƆW ABƆTƆ ASEREWA, NNWA NO SO

ANANSE, Kwebia no, na ɔte hɔ, na Adae Kwasieda bi, ɔkɔɔ Nyame hɔ kɔmaa no akye. Nyame maa no nnuaba kɛkɛ bi. Ananse faa no na ɔkɔɔ anim. Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, na mmaa wɔ asubɔn hɔ renam ntadeɛ. Ananse kaa kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ ma yɛn nnuaba kɛkɛ yi na moma mo ba no. Wɔnyinii na wɔmaa ba no. Ananse seeว่า, “Ma me biribi na mɛnkɔ.” Ɛnna wɔfa nsuo guu kuku mu, maa no, na ɔfaeɛ kɔeɛ. Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, nnipa bi resiesie asaase mmoa a wɔbɛdua yam mu, na Ananse maa hɔn nsuo nom. Nnipa no maa no yam baako. Ɔkɔeɛ na ɔhyiaa dadefo baako, na ɔno nso repɛpɛ charcoal. Ɔmaa no pampam no, na ɔkaa sɛ ɔmpsɛ ɔnyɛ no nka na ɔnni, efisɛ sɛ obi bɛdi charcoal a ɛnyɛ yie. Na Ananse kaa sɛ, “Ma me biribi na matu kwan.” Ɔmaa no soso, na ɔfaa no na ɔkɔɔ. Ɔkɔɔ hɔ na ɔhunuu ɔbarima bi, a ɔde ne kɔn kura palm-nuts. Na Ananse maa no soso no, na ɔno nso maa Ananse palm-oil, nsuo-kuru bi, a ɛyɛ dodo. Na ɔtuu kwan. Na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔhyiaa obaa bi a ɔwoaa mma du, na ɔrehyɛ wɔn honam nsuo a ɛfiri ne n'ano mu. Kwaku Ananse took the oil na ɔmaa obaa no, na ama ɔatumi ahyɛ ne mma no nsuo no. Obaa no maa no ba baako, na ɔfa no kɔɔ, na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔhyiaa obaa bi. Obaa no nni ba biara, na ɔde nnuaba dua bi a ɔde nketia afa ho. Ɛna Papa Ananse hyɛɛ abofra no wɔ obaa no nsam, na obaa no nso maa no aduane kuredua no, na ɔfaa no kɔɔ. Ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔhyiaa obi a ɔrepare borɔdeɛ mfutuma a ɔde ne nsa nnom. Ananse maa no kuredua no, na obaa no nso maa Ananse borɔdeɛ mfutuma. Ɔfaa no kɔɔ, na ɔhyiaa Ekuikui bi. Ekuikui no na wɔrenom nsuo wɔ tɛntɛm bi mu. Ananse see sɛ, “Nya saa aburo-mfutuma yi didi, efisɛ saa mudu-pono yi nyɛ eyie.” Ɛnna Elephants a wɔn ho ayɛ koro no nyaa no na wɔnomee. Berɛ a wɔwɔwie nom no, Kweku Ananse see, “Ma me biribi na mɛnkɔ.” Wɔsee no, “Yɛnni hwee, na asubɔnten a ɛda hɔ yi, ma yɛnnyina mu na yɛntumi nntu mu, na obiara a ɔtɔ mu no, wubetumi afa no.” Ɛnnɛ, Elephants bebree mu no baako retwa mmofra. Mmoa no nyinaa bɔɔ mmofra, nanso nea na ɔwoa no tɔɔ nsuo mu, na Ananse kɔfaa no, na ɔde bomu nsuo kɔkyekyereɛ ɔkɔkɔɔ no nan mu, na ɔkyere no. Ɛnna ɔhunuu sɛ aserewa prutuo bi refa hɔ. Na Ananse see sɛ, "Ei, eyi yɛ anomaa fɛfɛ, mɛkɔgye no ama me ba, Ntikuma." Na Ananse suu aserewa anomaa no akyi; ɔtoo aboɔ sɛ ɔbɛpira no; nanso ɔanyɛ no. Berɛ a ɔbaaa no, Enyama no akɔ. Eyi ara ne nea ɛma yɛwɔ abebusem a ɛse, "Yɛmpɛ n’asɛm sɛ yɛgya Enyama a yɛakoraa no akɔtoo aboɔ mma aserewa anomaa."

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
