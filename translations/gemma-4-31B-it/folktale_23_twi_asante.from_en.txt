Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea yadeɛ pii baa amansan no mu no kwan

Ɛnnɛ, Kwaku Ananse teyɛɛ, na ɔkɔɔ Nyankonpon, Nimskyaman hɔ, na ɔsee no sɛ, "Nana, fa wo mmoa a wofra no a wofrae no sɛ Kra Kwame (yeɛ, eyi a wode yɛ sacrifice ma wo kra Memeneda) na ma me mmpaw na mme di no, na matumi akɔ fa fɛfɛyɛyɛyɛ abofra bi aba ma wo (de ɛyɛ ne nkyɛ)." Nimskyaman maa no mmoa no, na Ananse kɔɔ na ɔsan baa ne kuro mu, na ɔpaw mmoa no na ɔdii no. Ɛna Ɔkwaku Ananse kɔɔ kuro bi mu—saa kuro no mu, obiara nnyɛ barima; mmaa nyinaa na wɔwɔ hɔ. Ananse gyawɔɔ wɔn nyinaa na ɔne wɔn tenaa hɔ. Da bi, abɔdeɛfoɔ bi baa hɔ. Berɛ a abɔdeɛfoɔ no kɔɔ no, ɔkɔkaee Nimskyaman sɛ, "Sɛ Ananse ho asɛm ne sɛ, saa mmoa a wamaa no no, ɔapaw no na ɔama mmaa bi adii na (ɛnnɛ) ɔawɔn." Nimskyaman see no sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nokware?" Abɔdeɛfoɔ no see no sɛ, "Nana, ɛyɛ nokware." Nimskyaman somaa abotirefoɔ, ɔkaee wɔn sɛ wɔnkɔ saa kuro no na wɔnka mmaa a wɔwɔ hɔ nyinaa aba. Abotirefoɔ no kɔɔ, hyiaa mmaa no, na wɔfaa wɔn nyinaa, na obi baako a ɔyɛ yadeɛ na wɔmgya no, na wɔfaa wɔn kɔmaa Nimskyaman. Ananse see no sɛ, "Wo a wura hɔ, dɛn na metumi ayɛ wo, wonntumi ayɛ hwee ma me?" Yadeɛfoɔ no see no sɛ, "Kɔfa kuku bi mma me." Ananse kɔfaa kuku bi baa. Ɔsee no sɛ, "Hɔra me na fa (nsuo a wode hɔraaa me no) na fa to saa kuku yi mu." Ananse hɔraa ne nipadua na ɔtoo (nsuo a ɔde hɔraaa no) no kuku no mu. Saa obiayewa yi fɛyɛɛ paa, obiara nnyɛ ne ntease wɔ amammere no mu. Ananse gyawɔɔ nea ɔwɔ ansa na ɛyɛ. Ɛnnɛ, abɔdeɛfoɔ no baa bio, na ɔhunuu saa abofra yi. Ɔkɔkaee Nimskyaman sɛ, "Ananse ayɛ wo yie, ɔde mmaa a wɔyɛ huhu baa wo hɔ na ɔagye fɛfɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛ

Ntikuma (bɔɔ atumpon na ɔtoo dwom):

Fɛfɛɛfɛ, Fɛfɛɛfɛ!

Na Afudotwedotwe ne Nyiwankonfwea dancee. Anene, kurokuro no, tuu mmirika kɔka kyerɛɛ Nyame sɛ, “Ananse wɔ dance bi a ɛfata wo nanso ɛmfata Kwebla.” Ɛno ara a, NyameTwe kɔɔ Ananse hɔ kɔfaa ne dance no. Ananse see, “Me dance yi, yɛyɛ no wɔ mmaa hɔ ara, na sɛ Nyame pɛ a, mɛfa aba.” Wɔkɔkaa kyerɛɛ Nyame. Ɔsee, “Ɛnyɛ hwee, ma ɔnfa mmra.” Ananse faa (atumfoɔ no) kɔfaa mmaa hɔ, na Nyame bae na ɔdancee, na ne mmaa nyinaa dancee. Afei, na obi baako a ɔyɛ yadeɛ wɔ hɔ. Berɛ a ɔhunuu sɛ Ananse aspan skin bi wɔ kɔkɔɔ a ne yadeɛ nyinaa hyɛ mu no, ɛno nti, ɔsee ɔrennyɛ dance. Na seesei Nyame tiri no, na ɔbaa; na berɛ a ɔrehyɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔbɛdance no, Anansefaa kɔkɔɔ no na ɔbɔɔ ɔbaa no, na yadeɛ no nyinaa hwee (ne nne te sɛ), tese! Asaaeɛ no ne sɛ syphilis, stomach-ache, headache, leprosy, guinea-worm, small-pox, yaws, fits, diabetes, madness baa amansan mu. Daakye, yadeɛ biara nnim nnipa mu, nanso Nyame na ɔyɛɛ kwan a Ananse faa yadeɛ baa amansan mu.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
