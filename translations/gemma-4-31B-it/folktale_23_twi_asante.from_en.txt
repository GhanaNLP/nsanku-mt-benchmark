Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea yadeɛ pii baa amansan no mu no kwan

Ɛnnɛ, Kwaku Ananse teyɛɛ, na ɔkɔɔ Nyankonpon, Wɔso-Nyame hɔ, na ɔsee no sɛ, "Nana, fa wo mmoa a wofrae no a wofrae no sɛ Kra Kwame (yeɛ, eyi a wode yɛ ayɛ mmoa a wode hyɛ wo honhom anim Memeneda) na ma me mmpaw na mme, na matumi akɔ fa hyirema fɛfɛre ba ama wo (sɛ mmoa no ho mmoa)." Wɔso-Nyame maa no mmoa no, na Ananse kɔɔ na ɔsan baa ne akuraa mu, na ɔpaw mmoa no na ɔdee dee. Kwasia Ananse kɔɔ kuro bi mu—ɛhɔ mma biara nni hɔ a ɔyɛ barima; mmaa nyinaa na wɔwɔ hɔ. Ananse yɛɛ ayɔnkofa ne wɔn nyinaa na wɔtena hɔ. Da koro bi, abɔdeɛfoɔ bi baa hɔ. Berɛ a abɔdeɛfoɔ no kɔeɛ no, ɔkɔkaee Nyame, ɔsee no sɛ, "Ananse ho asɛm ne sɛ, saa onompo a woama no no, awu no na wama mmaa bi adidi na (ɛnna) awɔw mpokuo ne hɔ." Nyame bisaa no sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nokware?" Abɔdeɛfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Nana, ɛyɛ nokware." NyameTwe abotirenfoɔ kɔɔ, ɔkaee hɔn sɛ wɔnkɔ saa kuro no na wɔnfa mmaa a wɔwɔ hɔ nyinaa aba. Abirikyire no kɔeɛ, hyiaa mmaa no, na wɔfaa wɔn nyinaa, na obi baako a ɔyɛ ya na wɔmpae no, na wɔfaa wɔn kɔɔ Nyame hɔ. Ananse see, "Wo a wura ha, dɛn na metumi ayɛ wo, wonni hwee a wobetumi ayɛ ama me?" Ɔbaa a ɔyɛ ya no see, "Kɔfa kuku kɔkɔɔ baako bra me." Ananse kɔfaa kuku kɔkɔɔ baako braa no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wɔho mma me na fa (nsuo a wode ayɛ no) na fa tohɔ yi mu." Ananse wɔhö mmaa no na ɔtoo (nsuo a ɔde ayɛ no) no tohɔ no mu. Saa obiayewa yi yɛɛ fɛ paa, obiayewa biara nni ne nkabom mu a ɔte ne sɛ. Ananse gyawnee nea ɛyɛ ne dea deda. Afei ɔbɔdeɛ no baa bio, na ɔhunuu saa abaayewa yi. Ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔkaee Nyame, ɔsee no sɛ, "Ananse ayɛ wo mofuo, ɔde mmaa hyehyɛe no a wɔnyɛ fɛ baa wo nkyɛn na ɔde fɛfɛɛyɛ no akura." NyameTweɛ kɔɔ hɔ kɔhwɛɛ Kwaku Ananse, na ɔmaa wɔkɔfaa saa ɔbaa no baa ne nkyɛn. Wɔde saa asɛm yi kɔkaee Ananse. Ɔse, "Annye me nso anigye?" Abirikyire no se, "Nyame se yɛnsie ɔbaa no aba ne nkyɛn." Ananse se, "Ɔno ne ɔbaa no, ɔte hɔ, fa no kɔ." Ɛnnɛ, Ananse kɔfaa kuku a yɛgu mu adeɛ a ɔfaa afuo no nyinaa a ɔfaa fii ɔbaa no hɔ, na ɔtrɛ kɔtɛ ne ano, na ɔtrɛ kɔtɛ kuku foforo bi ano na ɔmaa ne ba Ntikuma, na Ananse bɔɔ (atumpon a ɔyɛɛ no) na ɔtoo dwom sɛ:

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

Aso Ya e!

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

W'ani ayɛ kɔkɔɔ nnwom!

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

Wo nsa nni hɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ!

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

Ɛno ne Aso Ya?

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

Wo nan nnuae!

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

Wo hwene yɛ kɔtɔ wɔ wo anim!

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

Wo nan yɛ kɛse te sɛ ɔkunu nnan!

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

Wo ti te sɛ nabo!

Y'odende dende den, Y'odende den.

Ntikuma (bɔɔ atumpon na ɔtoo dwom):

Fɛfɛɛfɛ, Fɛfɛɛfɛ!

Na Afudotwedotwe ne Nyiwankonfwea dancee. Anene, kurokuro no, tuu mmirika kɔka kyerɛɛ Onyame sɛ, “Ananse wɔ dance bi a ɛfata wo nanso ɛmfata Kwebla.” Ɛno ara a, OnyameTwe kɔɔ Ananse hɔ kɔfaa ne dance no. Ananse see sɛ, “Me dance yi, yɛyɛ no wɔ harem mu pɛ, na sɛ Onyame pɛ a, mɛde ba.” Wɔnkɔka kyerɛɛ Onyame. Ɔsee sɛ, “Ɛnyɛ hwee, ma ɔnde nnyɛ.” Ananse faa (drums no) kɔɔ harem no mu, na Onyame baeɛ na ɔdancee, na ne yere nyinaa dancee. Afei, na ɛkyerɛ sɛ obi baako a ɔyɛɛ yadeɛ no na ɔtena hɔ. Berɛ a ɔhunuu sɛ Ananse asesa kɔtoku mmoa a ɛwɔ ne yadeɛ kɔkɔɔ no mu no, ɛno nti, ɔse ɔrennyina. Na seesei Nyame hyɛɛ no mpa, na ɔbaa; na berɛ a ɔrehyɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔrekyere no, Anansefaa kɔtoku no na ɔpiraa ɔbaa no ano, na yadeɛ no nyinaa bɔɔ (ne nne te sɛ), tese! Saa na syphilis, yamu-yare, ti-yare, kɔm-yare, nsuo-mmu, nsa-yare, yaws, fits, diabetes, ne kwaseasɛm baa amansan mu. Tete no, yare biara nnim nnipa mu, nanso Nyame na ɔmaa Ananse faa yare baa amansan mu.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
