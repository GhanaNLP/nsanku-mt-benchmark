Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNE ABOSOM, NYAME-NTONYI NO, BAA AMANMU NO

Abɔntenfoɔ bi ne ne yere teyɛɛ abɔntenfoɔ camp bi mu. Wɔn yɛyɛ ahyia, na wɔte hɔ kɔtɔ kɔtɔ ma wɔatumi anya obi a wɔbɛtena. Abɔntenfoɔ no kɔɔ kwaa, na ɔkɔɔ nwedomase—nanso ɔanya hwee. Sɛ ɔba fie na ɔde nnam-atwe gu asuo mu, a ɔgye nnam ne nnam-ketewa no na ɔde ba ma ne yere. Ɛnna wɔte hɔ, na ne yere bisa no sɛ, “Me tie sɛ sɛ wopaw nam a, wode ma afofor, anaasɛ wogyi na wo-di (sika no), enti nnɛ sɛ wokɔ kwaa na wo-mfa nam bi ba a, mɛsan akɔ me akuraa.” Abɔntenfoɔ no kɔɔ nwedomase. Ɔnantee, ɔnantee, nanso ɔanhunu mmoa biara. Ɔkaa (ne ho) sɛ, “Oh, amanehunu na mahu.” Ɛnna berɛ a ɔrehwɛ hwɛ no, ɔnhunuu Duyker bi a ɔrenante hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Wo, Duyker, mɛpawa wo.” Abɔntenfoɔ no suree no, kyiri! kyiri! kyiri! (ne nan nnyigyei no yɛ saa). Duyker no dwenee na ɔkɔɔ dua bi nnam mu. Ɔno (Duyker no) kaa sɛ, “Nana Tano, me dwene ba wo nkyɛn sɛ wobɛbo me, na abɔntenfoɔ no anya me.” Abɔntenfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Me, mennim nea wɔfrɛ ‘Tano’ no, enti kɔ hɔ na matumi agye wo na makɔ ma me yere.” Tano gyinaa hɔ, na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kaa sɛ, "Abɔntenfoɔ, gya Duyker no na fa *me* kɔ fie, efisɛ m'anim mfa wo aba mfa mfa wo nyepa." Abɔntenfoɔ no gyinaa hɔ, na ɔhorowee, biribiri. Ɔfaa no (Tano) na ɔde guu ne ti. Berɛ a ɔreba no, ɔhyiaa Owuo. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abɔntenfoɔ, wo-de m' *okyeame* (spokesman) kɔ hene?" Abɔntenfoɔ no gyee me answers sɛ, "Agya, me ani hye; merehwehwɛ nam kɔma me yere, ɛnna mihuu Duyker no, na berɛ a merekɔ pawa no, ɔkaa sɛ ɔdwene ba eyi nkyɛn sɛ wobɛbo no; mekaa sɛ mennim hwee fa ho. Eyi (ɛnna) kaa sɛ mfa no nkɔ fie, efisɛ (ɔkaa sɛ) ɔwɔ fa mu pii." Owuo bisaa Tano sɛ, "Worekɔ fie, na wunim sɛ sɛ obi yare, na mefa me kɔkɔɔ ba, na megye ayɛ no nkyɛn a, obiara nnka ho, enti sɛ wokɔ fie a, nea mɛka kyerɛ wo ne eyi: sɛ obi yare, na mennyɛ ne kɔkɔɔ a, mɛka kyerɛ wo, na wobɛnya biribi ma me, na fa saa nkyɛnea no ho a, mogya ne nea mɛpɛ, na wubetumi agye nam no." Abosom no kaa kyerɛ Owuo sɛ, "Nana, meate, panin." Abɔntenfoɔ no faa Tano, na ɔne no baa fie. Berɛ a Tano gyinaa hɔ no, ɔtoo edwene, na wɔne no too chorus no. Da a ɛdi so no, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ, na obi yadeɛ, a wɔfaa no kɔɔ Tano hɔ. Ɔseɛ no, "Wobɛwu." Na ɛyɛ nokware, ɔwui. (Kɔbɔ obi foforo) ɔseɛ no, "Wonnwu, enti fa eyi ne eyi mma me." Berɛ a obi faa nneɛma no baeɛ no, ɔseɛ, "Mekɔ gye wo nkwa." Na ɛfirisɛ wamaa (nsamansaman yi) Tano, nokwarem, wo yɛɛ yaw. Asaase no ne sɛ yɛhu sɛ abosom yɛ ayɛyɛ; enti nnipa kɔ Tano hɔ na wɔfa ne mma bi firi hɔ.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
