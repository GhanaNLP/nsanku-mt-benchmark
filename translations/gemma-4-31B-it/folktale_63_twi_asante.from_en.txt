Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNE ABOSOM, NYAME Nketia no, BAWII ABUSUA NO MU

ƆBƆFOA bi ne ne yere teyɛɛ abɔfoa camp bi mu. Wɔn yɛyɛ ahyia, na wɔte hɔ kɔtɔ kɔtɔ ma wɔbɛtumi anya obi a wɔbɛtena ho. Ɔbɔfoa no yɛyɛ sɔre kɔ nwedomase—nanso ne ho nni hwee. Sɛ ɔba fie na ɔde nnamyɛ kɔ asuo mu a, ɔgye nnamyɛ ne nnamyɛ ketewa no na ɔde ba ma ne yere. Ɛnnɛ wɔdu hɔ, na ne yere bisaa no sɛ, “Me tie sɛ sɛ wopue kɔyɛ nnam a, woma nnipa anaa wogyina mu na woadi (ne sika), enti nnɛ sɛ wukɔ kwaa mu na woankɔfa nnam bi aba a, mɛsan akɔ me akuraa.” Ɔyadeɛ no kɔɔ kwaa mu. Ɔnantee, ɔnantee, nanso ɔanhunu mmoa biara. Ɔkaa (ne ho), “O, meahu amane.” Ɛnnɛ, berɛ a ɔrehwɛ hwɛ no, ɔhunuu Duyker bi a ɔrenante kɔ hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Wo nso, Duyker, mɛyɛ wo yaw.” Ɔyadeɛ no suu no akyi, kyiri! kyiri! kyiri! (ɛne ne nan nnyigyei no). Duyker no dwenei na ɔkɔɔ dua bi nnuane mu. Ɔse (Duyker no), “Nana Tano, meba wo nkyɛn sɛ wobɛbo me ano, na ɔbɔdeɛ no anya me.” Ɔbɔdeɛ no se, “Me nso, mennim nea wɔfrɛ ‘Tano’ no, enti kɔ anim na matumi afa wo akɔ ma me yere.” Tano gyina hɔ, na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔrekasa, ɔse, "Ɔbɔdeɛ, gya Duyker no na fa *me* nkɔ fie, efisɛ wobɛnya mfasoɔ pii wɔ me mu." Ɔbɔdeɛ no gyina hɔ, na ɔho ho hwee, biribiri. Ɔfaa no (Tano) maa ɔtoo no ne tiwuo. Berɛ a ɔde no reba no, ɔhyiaa Owuo. Ɔse, "Ɔyerefo, wo kura me *okyeame* kɔ he?" Ɔyerefo no hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, "Agya, me ani ayɛ kɔkɔɔ; merehwehwɛ nam kɔma me yere, na mɛkɔhuu Duyker no, na berɛ a merekɔyere no, ɔse ɔduruu ha kɔhwɛ mu; me se mennim hwee fa ho." Wei kyerɛɛ me sɛ mɛfa no akɔ fie, efisɛ ɛwɔ mu profit pii." Owuo bisaa Tano sɛ, "Worekɔ fie, na wuhu sɛ obiara a ɔyareɛ, na mefa me mpaa meba, na metoo no ne nkyɛn a, obiara nnka n'anim, enti sɛ worekɔ fie a, nea mɛka kyerɛ wo ne eyi: sɛ obi yareɛ na menni ho pɛ sɛ mefa no a, mɛbɔ wo amanneɛ u, na wobɛnya biribi a ɛyɛ m'atua, na fa saa atua no ho a, mogya ne nea mɛpɛ, na wubɛtumi gye nam no." Abosom no kaa kyerɛ Owuo sɛ, "Nana, meate ase, mpaninfo." Abɔdeɛfoɔ no faa Tano, na ɔde no baa fie. Berɛ a Tano gyina hɔ no, ɔtoo edwene, na wɔnyɛɛ ne nkyerɛwee ho chorus. Da a ɛdi so no, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ, na obi yadeɛyɛe no, wɔfaa no kɔɔ Tano hɔ. Ɔseɛ no, "Wobɛwu." Na ɛyɛ nokware, ɔwui. (Kɔbɔ obi foforo) ɔseɛ no, "Wonnwu, enti fa eyi ne eyi mma me." Berɛ a obi faa nneɛma no bae no, ɔseɛ, "Mrekɔ gye nkwa ama wo." Na ɛfirisɛ wamaa (nsamansaman yi) Tano, nokwarene wo yɛɛ yaw. Ɛno nenea yɛhu sɛ abosom no yɛ ayeyi; enti nnipa kɔ Tano hɔ na wɔgye ne mma bi fii ase.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
