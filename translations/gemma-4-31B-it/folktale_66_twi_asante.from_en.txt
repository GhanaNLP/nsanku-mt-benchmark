Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

KOKOKYINAKA, BLUE-CRESTED TURACOU NO, ANKƆKƆ ANANSE, KƆKƆRƆ NO, SE OREBƆ NE NSORE.

Tete bi, Kokokyinaka nnoma kɔɔ Ananse Kwaku Ananse hɔ kɔtweɛ sika kɔkɔɔ a ɛmu yɛ asuanu weight (£4). Efisɛ Ananse onni bi, ɔkɔɔ ne adamfo Ntontom mmoa hɔ kɔtweɛ sika kɔkɔɔ no de kɔmaa ne adamfo Kokokyinaka nnoma no. Da a Ntontom hyɛɛ sɛ ɔbɛgye ne sika no baaye, Ntontom kaa kyerɛ Ananse sɛ, “Kɔfa me sika kɔkɔɔ no mma me.” Ananse hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, “Kokokyinaka nnoma no atena Odua-Kese no atifi, enti kɔfa wo sika kɔkɔɔ no.” Ntontom bisaa sɛ, “Mɛyɛ dɛn na mafa; ɔyɛ nnoma a ɔtumi tutu.” Kwaku Ananse hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, “Kɔtwa Odua-Kese no, na sɛ wuhu sɛ dua no repu a, ɛnna w’akwan kɔfa Kokokyinaka nnoma no na woafa wo sika kɔkɔɔ no.” Ntontom kɔtwaa dua no. Berɛ a dua no repu no, Kokokyinaka nnoma no tutuu fii hɔ, pa bu bu bu bu! Ɛnna Ntontom baa, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Ananse, me nnyia wo.” Eyi ara ne nea nti yɛka sɛ, “Kokokyinaka nnoma ntweɛ sika kɔkɔɔ wɔ Ananse hɔ na Ntontom mmoa nka kɔtwa Odua-Kese.” Me nsɛm a mika yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaasɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ, na fa bi mma me.
