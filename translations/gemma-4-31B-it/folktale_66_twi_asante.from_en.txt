Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

KOKOKYINAKA, BLUE-CRESTED TURACOU NO, ANKƆKƆ ANANSE, KƆKƆRƆ NO, SE OREBƆ NE NSORE.

Tete bi, Kokokyinaka nnoma kɔɔ Ananse Kwaku Ananse hɔ kɔtweɛ sika kɔkɔɔ asuanu baako (£4) wutwa. Efisɛ Ananse onni bi, ɔkɔɔ ne adamfo Ntontom mmoa hɔ kɔtweɛ bi na watumi akɔma ne adamfo Kokokyinaka nnoma no. Da a Mmaniniyɛe kuraa (deɛ ɔde bɛgye ne sika a ɔbɔtoo no) no bae, na Mmaniniyɛe kaa kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, “Kɔfa me sika hu no bra ma me.” Ananse hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, “Kokokyinaka nnoma no atena Silk-cotton-tree no atifi, enti kɔgye wo sika hu no.” Mmaniniyɛe bisaa sɛ, “Mɛyɛ dɛn na magya wo; ɛnam no tumi tu.” Kwebia no hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, “Kɔtutu dua no, na sɛ wuhu , dua no bɛpɔ, ɛnna wobɛkyere Kokokyinaka nnoma no, na woatumi anya wo sika hu no bio.” Mmanini no kɔtwee dua no so. Berɛ a dua no rebɛpɔ no, Kokokyinaka nnoma no tutu fii hɔ, pa bu bu bu! Ɛna Mmanini bae, na ɔse, “Ananse, meye wo anigye.” Ɛno nti na yɛwɔ asɛm a ɛse, “Kokokyinaka annye Ananse nsa sɛ Mmanini bɛkɔ akasa Onyina.” Me nsɛm a mika yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi, na fa bi mma me.
