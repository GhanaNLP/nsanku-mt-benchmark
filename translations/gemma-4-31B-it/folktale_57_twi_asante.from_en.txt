ABOFRA-KWASI-GYINAMOA

Obiara nim abofra bi a ne din de Kwasi Gyinamoa, na ɔwɔ nua baa nwɔtwe, na obiara among wɔn nni ɔkunaa, enti wɔkɔɔ, na wɔka sɛ wɔrekɔbea bi kɔhwehwɛ akunaa. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa seeว่า, “Me ne wo bɛkɔ.” Wɔn hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, “Yɛn ne wo renkɔ.” Nea ɔyɛ panyin wɔn mu seeว่า, “Ma yɛn nnyɛ no kwan, na sɛ yɛkɔ kɔnkyɛn kwan no so na nkyɛn hyɛ yɛn mu a, ɔbɛtumi atu ama yɛn.” Wɔn hyɛɛ aseɛ rekɔ, na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kɔe (ne wɔn). Wɔduu asuo bi ho, na nea ɛfa bi tɛte sɛ tɛkrɛma fitaa; nea ɛfa bi tɛte sɛ tɛkrɛma kɔkɔɔ; na nea ɛfa bi tɛte sɛ ewusi tuntum. Wɔka sɛ, “Eh, dɛn na yɛbɛyɛ na yɛatumi akɔ asuo yi ase?” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa ne nnaman-mmoa-mmoa no, bɔɔ (nsuo no), na ɔmaa nsuo no kaa mmienu, na wɔfaa mu kɔe, na wɔkɔɔ anim. Wɔbaa kurom bi mu; na ɛyɛ kuro kɛse paa. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa see, “Kuro no dinn (yeaa, ɛyɛ komm na cicada nnom mpo), obiara nni mu?” Ɛna Anomaa bi kasaa, see, “Obi wɔ efie no mu, kɔyɛ.” Wɔkɔɔ anim, na ɔbaa mpaninfoa bi wɔ hɔ, ɔde ne ti atoa ne nan so, na ɔrehwɛ kɔkɔɔ wɔ mu. Berɛ a ɔhunuu Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, na ɔmaa ne tie kɔ sorɔ na ɔde baa hɔ; ɔsee, “Me nananom, adɛn nti, berɛ a obiara nnim sɛ ɔbɛba ha no, na mobaa ha?” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, “Me nnuaba nnyɛ nwɔtwe, na wɔnni akokɔfo a wɔbɛyɛ wɔn kɔkɔfo, enti wɔsee sɛ wɔbɛba ha abyɛ akokɔfo a wɔbɛware.” Okunini-nanaba see, “O, eyi yɛ” t, efisɛ me nso, m'aba nyenam nwɔtwe. Akrabea na ayɛ ne nananom mma a ɔde ne nnawoma kɔfrɛ ne mma. Berɛ a wɔbaa hɔ no, eyi se, “Me kunu wɔ ha,” eyi nso se, “Me kunu wɔ ha.” Berɛ a wɔayɛ wɔn nyinaa mpaadae no, na akrabea no kɔfaa adeɛ a wɔfrɛ no _dokum_, na ɔkyekyere wɔn kɔn mu. Ɛnnɛ, obiara kɔdaa ne yere ho. Na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔkɔɔ kɔdwee ne yere kɔkɔɔ no nkyɛn. Anadwo berɛ a ɛduruu no, yere kɔkɔɔ no hyerɛɛ ne nnum, a ɛyɛ kɔkɔ paa, na wakɔne ne nnum no mma ɔnkɔyere mmaa no. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa see, “E! e! “e!” Akraa no see, “Ɛdeɛn ni?” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, “Sɛ me wɔ me kuro mu, na me kɔ dumi nanso menntumi nkɔda a, ɛnna me maame twa yam mpa na ɔhyɛ no gyata mu, na ɔfa kuku a ɛwo nka na ɔkɔ hwee nsuo aba sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mɛnom.” Akraa-nanaba no faa kwan kɔɔ asubɔn, na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kɔtoo suman a wɔfrɛ no _dok um_ fii ɔbaa kɔkɔɔ no mma kɔn hɔ, na ɔfaa hɔn kɔhyɛɛ ne nua baa awofo mma kɔn mu. Ɔbaa kɔkɔɔ no san baa nsuo no fi; ɔse, “Mepere sɛ mɛpue nsuo (wo saa nsuo-pue no mu), nanso mempue anka.” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa anyaa no se, “Kɔfa mud kakra, na fa nhwedua hyehyɛ (nsuo-pue no) mu, na fa kɔpue.” Ɔbaa kɔkɔɔ no kɔyɛɛ saa, na ɔpuee nsuo wɔ mu, na eyi yɛɛ ade yie. Ɔde baa, na ɔtuu pampampaa no fii abrobɔ mu, na ɔmaa Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa dɛ ɔnni, na ɔnom nsuo no nso. Ɛnna ɔtɔɔ ne ho aba na ɔdae. Ɔbaa panin-nanan no soɔree; ɔsee, “Me na me ne.” Ɔhyɛɛ ne nnum no nkanyan, (kosi sɛ ɛhyerɛɛ) kɔkɔɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ne nnom nso na ɔhyɛɛ adan no mu; ɔfa ne nsa kɔkaa mmofra no kɔn ho, na obi a _dokum_ ayaresɛm no nni ne kɔn ho no, ɔde ne nnum hyɛɛ no gyigyi. Sɛnea ɛte saa na ɔwui mmofra otɔn no. Onnim sɛ ne mmofra ankasa na ɔwui hɔ. Berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ, anɔpa paa, paa, paa, Nana-Kɔkɔɔ kɔɔ afuo mu. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kɔkaee ne nnuaba na ɔmaa wɔn dweneɛ. Nana-Kɔkɔɔ firi afuo mu bae. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa see, “Nana-Kɔkɔɔ e! Nana-Kɔkɔɔ e! sɛ woyɛ kwasea mpo a, wobɛyɛ wo baawon mpo mfi?” “Me nso, me ne me nnuaba rekɔ.” Nana-okunmuu se, “Ɛnyɛ hwee, obi a ɔnim (sɛnea ɛsɛ sɛ ɔyɛ wo) wɔ hɔ.” Ɔkɔe, na Nana-okunmuu nso sesaa ne ho bɛyɛɛ hyɛnea fɛfɛ, na ɔkuraa seka-nkran, na ɔkɔe Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kuro mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Onipa a obetumi de nkyene apu saa ahwentoa yi mu no, mebɛware no.” Nnipa nyinaa a wɔwɔ kuro no mu (prabii sɛ) wɔbɛpu mu, nanso wɔantumi. Abofra Kwasi Gyinamoa puu mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Maware wo." Abofra Kwasi Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Me nso, mampɛ sɛ meware wo." Enti ne wofa waree no. Ɔkɔɔ ne nkyɛn kɔdaaa, nanso ɔtuu ne ani mmienu no fii, na ɔfa wɔn kɔɔ. Ɛnnɛ na abofra no see, "Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa wɔ hɔ! "Mepueu wo wuo anim anigye baako mmienu no na merekɔ anim." Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa anyaa mmuae sɛ, "Fa wɔn nkɔ, efisɛ obi a ɔnim (sɛnea wɔgye wo) wɔ hɔ." Anɔpa a ɛba no, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ, wɔka sɛ, "Abofra, kɔfa wo wuo anigye no mma yɛn." Ɔka sɛ, "Mrenkan." Wɔpɛɛ sɛ wɔka kyerɛ no mprenu, na (awie) ɔkɔɔ. Ɛnnɛ, Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa huu sɛ Old-woman-grandmother baabaa no yɛ _enceinte_, enti ɔno nso gyee kuku koro na ɔde guu ne yafunu so, na ɔde ne ntoma kyekyereɛ mu, na ɔkɔɔ Old-woman-grandmother kuro mu. Berɛ a ɔduu kurom no awie, ɔsuu. Ɔbaa-panin-nanama no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛrekɔ so?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me ne me kunu reba, na berɛ a ɔduuBepo no ananmu no, ɔtɔɔ fam na ɛmaa ne ani mmienu no nyinaa puee na atɔ fam." Ɔbaa-panin-nanama no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ" , "Wei ne saa ɔkwasea a ɔasore no, Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no wura panin no benkum ani, na wei nso ne ne nifa ani." Sɛ wo kɔ (baabi a wo kunu a ɔnyina no gyina) a, ɛsɛ sɛ wo ka sɛ, 'Me ani tumpu, me ani ayɛ clear.' Ansa na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nsa bɛka wɔn no, ɔka sɛ, "Wo, Old-woman-grandmother, wei ne me wura-panin ani a wo tuu no, na me repa wɔn kɔ." Old-woman-grandmother ka sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ asɛm, obi a ɔnim (sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛtumi agyina wo) no wɔ hɔ." Child-Kwasi -Gyinamoa baa fie, na ɔfaa anigye no kɔtoo ne wukuu no anigye baabi. Ɔsew, "Me anigye repopopopo, me anigye ayɛ clear," na ne wukuu no anigye tee paa. Anɔpa a ɛbaaa no, berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ hɔ no, mmofra nyinaa (kuro no mu) kɔɔ asubɔn kɔtwee. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa see, "Me nni dwo." Wɔn see, "Ebia wo wɔ nsa-ntoma?" Berɛ a wɔn see saa no, ne ho yɛɛ no den, na ɔdwoe. Ansa na ɔbɛtumi atu mpoano, na Okunini-Nana kyere no, twom! Ɔsee, "Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, mekyere wo," na ɔfa no kɔɔ ne kuro. Ɛna Nana-Kyerɛma kɔfaa kɔtukuu na ɔtrataa no, na ɔfaa abrobɛ na ɔtoo no anim. Ɔsee, "Merekɔ nnɛm-nnuane kurom, merekɔ nnuane-nnuane kurom, na mɛba abɛyere Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa." Ɔkɔe, na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa boaa nsafoa na ɔhyɛɛ asei tweekuu mu, pupru! pupru! Ɔbaa panin nananom nananom ba no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na worenam?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Atadwe." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma me kakra na matam." Ɔgyae kaa sɛ, "Yɛyɛ me nkyɛ." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wobɛtu." Ɔgyae kaa sɛ, "Dabi, mɛntu." Ɔyɛɛ no nkyɛ, na ɔbaa puei; ɔbaa panin no ba no na ɔrepow abrobɛ. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa took palm-nut pestle no, bɔɔ Akokɔ-Kakra-Nana nnanom ba no, na ɔwui. Afei ɔpuee Akokɔ-Kakra-Nana nnanom ba no skin no, na ɔhyɛɛ ho. Ɔbaa-panin-nanaba baa fie; ɔkaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na mo nyinaa ayɛ dɔteɛ saa?" Ɔyɛɛ no mmuae sɛ, "Madi Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa ho; madi no gya so." Saa amanneɛ yi yɛɛ Ɔbaa-panin-nanaba anigye, enti ɔdansaa na ɔyɛɛ ne nne tenten (na ɔtoo dwom): "Abofra, woadi Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa ho, Da bi na wobɛyɛ obi."

Ɔnoaeɛ aduan no no cooking, wɔtenaa ne nkyɛn, na wɔdidi. Nana kɔtɔkɔo no sɔɔ nkrakra no (kɔtɔkɔo no sɔɔ nkrakra no), fweo! fweo! Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa see, "Worepue soup (wo anom mu) a wo nananom mma tiri na yɛayɛ no." Okunini-nanaba see, "Dɛn na wokae?" Ɔyɛɛ no mmuae sɛ, "Meka sɛ soup-stew yi mu nni nkyɛm." Okunini-nanaba see, "Kɔfa bi mma me." Enti ɔkɔfaa bi baa. Wɔwieei adidi, na Nana Akraa se, "Sɛnse m’awu Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, bra ha, me nananom." Sɛ ɔbaa hɔ no, ɔfaa sika nneɛma na ɔhyɛɛ no fɛfɛɛfɛ. Afei, Nana Akraa hwɛɛ ne anim kakra. Ɔse, "Ah! "abaayewa yi anim ma me kae Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa n'anim." Abaayewa no see, "Nana, adɛn nti, sɛ wuhu sɛ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa baa ha na ɔyɛɛ me abusua nnanan nyɔnnum nyinaa (na woka saa), efisɛ woma me werɛ how." Nana-okunini no see, "Yɛ wo nan." Afei abaayewa no yɛɛ ne ho mmerɛ wɔ efie hɔ. Nana no boaa no fii hɔ. Ɔse, "Ma me nkɔ ntow no afutuo no so." Enti Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa no kɔɔ. Berɛ a ɔduu afutuo no so no, ɔtuu sika kɔkɔɔ nneɛma nyinaa a ɔbaa panin no de ayɛ no fɛ no na ɔkyekyere wɔn akum. Ɛna ɔse, "Kyerɛ me, Nana-Kɔkɔɔ, kyerɛ me, Nana-Kɔkɔɔ, me ne me, Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, seesei mprempren, maba mpo mmienu, na maba abum wo nananom, na mafa wo sika nkuku nso." Nana-Kɔkɔɔ anyaa mmuae se, "Mise wo anim te sɛ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa; seesei baabiara a wobɛkɔ, mekɔ wo yɛn." Ɛna wɔnompo nyinaa tutuu kɔe, kiri! kiri! kiri! Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa duu ne akuraa mu, na ɔmaa ne nfamfoa sika kɔkɔɔ nneɛma no, na ɔkɔɔ kwan no so, na ɔkɔnnawii kwan no nkyɛn, na Nana-Kɔkɔɔ baa hɔ nso. Ɛno nti, sɛ woyɛ kwan so ntenten a, wobɛte nneɛma a ɛyɛ "pe" dwen dwen. Ɛno ne Nana-Kɔkɔɔ a ɔretu Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa anim.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
