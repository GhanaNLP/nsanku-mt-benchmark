ABOFRA-KWASI-GYINAMOA

Na abofra bi wɔ hɔ, Kwasi Gyinamoa, na ɔwɔ nua baayadeɛ otɔ nkron, na obiara nni hɔ a ɔwɔ ɔkunaa, enti wɔnam kɔɔ, na wɔse wɔrekɔ biakɔ mmaa kɔhwehwɛ akunaa. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa se, “Me ne mo bɛkɔ.” Wɔgyaei se, “Yɛn ne wo renkɔ.” Ne nua panin no se, “Ma yɛn nna ɔnkɔ yɛn nti, na sɛ yɛnam kwan no so na nkyene hwee yɛn a, ɔbɛtumi atu ama yɛn.” Wɔnam kɔɔ, na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔe (ne nti). Wɔduu asu bi, na asu no fa bi tɛ teaseɛ fitaa; fa bi tɛ teaseɛ kɔkɔɔ; na fa bi nso tɛ bebree tuntum. Wɔkasɛe, “Eh, dɛn na yɛbɛyɛ na yɛatumi akɔ asu yi ase?” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa ne (nsɛm) nnam-atwa no, bɔe (nsu no), na ɔmaa nsu no piraa mmienu, na wɔfaa mu kɔe. Wɔduu kurom bi; na ɛyɛ kurom kɛse bi. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa se, “Kurom yi dinn (ye), obiara nnim mu?” Na anomaa bi kasae, se, “Obi wɔ efie hɔ, nam kɔ.” Wɔnam kɔe, na ɔbaa sankofa bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔde ne ti atoa ne nan so, na ɔrehwehwɛ atokwa wɔ mu. Berɛ a ɔhunuu Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, ɔmaa ne ti kɔɔ sorɔ na ɔtoo no ba; ɔse, “Me nananom, adɛn nti na mo ba ha, berɛ a obiara nnim sɛ ɔbɛba ha?” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa gyee kyɛn se, “Me nua baayadeɛ otɔ nkron, na wɔnni akunaa a wɔbɛyɛ ne wɔn, enti wɔse wɔbɛba ha behwehwɛ akunaa a wɔbɛware.” Nana-ɔbaa no se, “Oh, ɛyɛ adeɛ pa, efisɛ me nso m'aba otɔ nkron.” Nana-ɔbaa no somaa ne nantin kɔfrɛ ne mma. Berɛ a wɔduu hɔ no, eyi se, “Me kunaa wɔ ha,” ɛno nso se, “Me kunaa wɔ ha.” Berɛ a wɔayɛ ayɛyɛ nyinaa no, na ɔbaa sankofa no kɔfaa suman a wɔfrɛ no _dokum_, na ɔkyekyereɛ wɔ ne mma nkonsonu neck so. Afei obiara kɔdae ne yere nti. Na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔdae ne nana-ɔbaa no nti. Berɛ a anadwo bae no, ɔbaa sankofa no hyerɛɛ ne nsetee, kɔkɔɔ paa, na ama wakɔgye mmaa no nyinaa. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa se, “E! e! “e!” Akraa no see, “Dɛn na ɛrekɔ so?” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, “Sɛ me wɔ me kuro mu, na me kɔ dumi nanso me ntumi nnum, ɛnna me maame twa yam kwan koro na ɔto no aba aboboyɛ mu, na ɔfa kuku a ɛwɔ nkyɛn na ɔkɔ nsuo hɔ kɔtwa nsuo aba me nsa ma me nom.” Akraa-nanaba kɔɔ asuom, na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kɔtweɛ sika-kɔkɔɔ a wɔfrɛ no _dokum_ fii akraa no mma kɔn mu, na ɔfaa wɔn kɔhyɛɛ ne nua baafoa kɔn mu. Akraa no firi nsuo hɔ baa; ɔsee, “Mepruei sɛ mɛtwa nsuo (wɔ saa nkyɛn no mu), nanso me ntumi antwa.” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, “Kɔfa mudu, na fa nhwedaa hyɛ (saa nkyɛn no) mu, na fa kɔtwa nsuo.” Akraa no kɔyɛɛ saa, na ɔtwaa nsuo wɔ mu, na ɛyɛɛ yie. Ɔfaa nsuo no baa, na ɔfaa yam no firi aboboyɛ mu, na ɔmaa Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nni, na ɔnom nsuo no nso. Ɛnna ɔtoo ne ho mpa na ɔdaaa. Akraa-nanaba sɔree; ɔsee, “Me na me ne.” Ɔhyɛɛ ne nnum kɔkɔɔ (kosi sɛ ɛhyerɛɛ), na ɔfaa wɔn kɔhyɛɛ dan mu; ɔfaa ne nsa kɔhwehwɛɛ mma no kɔn ho, na obi a _dokum_ sika-kɔkɔɔ no nni ne kɔn ho no, ɔde ne nnum hyɛɛ no gya. Sɛnea ɛte saa na ɔwui mma otɔmpɔn no. Onnim sɛ ne mma pɔtee na ɔwui. Berɛ a anigye baa, anɔpa anɔpa anɔpa paa, Akraa-nanaba kɔɔ afuo. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔkaee ne nua baafoa na ɔmaa wɔn dwenee. Akraa-nanaba firi afuo hɔ baa. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa see, “Akraa-nanaba e! Akraa-nanaba e! sɛ woyɛ kwasee anaa, na wode wo mma pɔtee ayɛ wui? Me nso, me ne me nua baafoa rekɔ.” Akraa-nanaba see, “Ɛnyɛ hwee, obi a ɔnim (sɛnea ɛyɛ wo) wɔ hɔ.” Ɔkɔɔ, na Akraa-nanaba nso sesaee ne ho bɛyɛɛ abofra fɛfɛfɛ, na ɔfaa kuku-seka, na ɔkɔɔ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kuro. Ɔsee, “Obi a ɔtumi de nkyɛn kɔpɛ saa kuku-seka yi, me bɛware no.” Nnipa nyinaa a wɔwɔ kuro no mu (pruei sɛ) wɔbɛpɛ no, nanso wɔantumi. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa pɛɛ no. Ɔsee, "Maware wo." Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa see, "Me nso, me renware wo." Enti ne wofa waree no. Ɔkɔdaaa ne nkyɛn, nanso ɔtwe ne ani mmienu no firi ne mu, na ɔfaa wɔn kɔɔ. Ɛna abofra baa no see, "Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa! Meatwa wo wofa ani mmienu no afiri ne mu na merekɔ anim." Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, "Fa wɔn nkɔ, efisɛ obi a ɔnim (sɛnea wɔgye wo) wɔ hɔ." Anɔpa a ɛbaeɛ no, berɛ a nneɛma yɛɛ hwee, wɔsee, "Abofra, kɔfa wo wofa ani no mma yɛn." Ɔsee, "Mrenkan kɔ." Wɔhyɛɛ no mpaebɔ pii, na (awie) ɔkɔeɛ. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nim sɛ Old-woman-grandmother baa no yɛ ɔyere a ɔwo na, ɔno nso gyee kuku koro na ɔde guu ne yafunu so, na ɔde ne ntoma kyekyereɛ mu, na ɔkɔeɛ Old-woman-grandmother kuro mu. Berɛ a ɔduu kuro no awie, ɔsuu. Old-woman-grandmother bisaa no sɛ, "Dɛn na asie?" Ɔsee, "Me ne me kunu reba, na berɛ a ɔduuBepɔ no atifi no, ɔtɔɔ ase na ɛma ne ani mmienu no tɔeɛ." Old-woman-grandmother see, "Ɛyɛ, eyi ne saa ɔkwasea a ɔasore no, Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa wofa ani benkum no, na eyi nso ne ne ani nifa. Berɛ a wobɛkɔ (baabi a wo kunu a ɔnyina no gyina) no, wobɛka sɛ, 'Me ani tumpu, me ani ayɛ hwee.'" Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nsa anka wɔn mpo ansa na ɔse, "Wo, Old-woman-grandmother, eyi ne me wofa ani a woatwaeɛ no, na merekɔ anim." Old-woman-grandmother see, "Ɛnyɛ hwee, obi a ɔnim (sɛnea wɔgye wo) wɔ hɔ." Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa baa fie, na ɔfaa ani no hyɛɛ ne wofa ani-kuo no mu. Ɔsee, "Me ani tumpu, me ani ayɛ hwee," na ne wofa ani no buee hwee. Anɔpa a ɛbaeɛ no, berɛ a nneɛma yɛɛ hwee, mmofra nyinaa (kuro no mu) kɔree asuom kɔhoroo. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa see, "Mennhoroo." Wɔsee, "Ebia wo wɔ ntoma-ntoma wɔ wo nan ase?" Berɛ a wɔkaeɛ no, ne bo fuu, na ɔkɔhoroo. Ansa na wakɔ nsuo no mu, Old-woman-grandmother kyinii no, twom! Ɔsee, "Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, m'anya wo," na ɔfaa no kɔeɛ ne kuro mu. Ɛna Old-woman-grandmother kɔfaa ketewa koro na ɔde hyɛɛ no mu, na ɔfaa abrobɛ na ɔde guu gya so. Ɔsee, "Merekɔ onion tribe hɔ, merekɔ spice tribe hɔ, na mɛba abɛkum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa." Ɔkɔeɛ, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa boaa nsaebɔ na ɔhyɛɛ ase tweekwee, pupru! pupru! Ɔnoaeɛ aduan no no cooking, wɔtenaa ne nkyɛn na wɔdidi. Nana-kɔkɔɔ no sɔɔ nkrakra no (kɔɔ ne can mu a nka bɔɔ), fweo! fweo! Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa see, "Woresɔ nkrakra a wo nananom mma ti na ɛyɛ." Nana-kɔkɔɔ no bisaa no sɛ, "Dɛn na wokae?" Ɔgyee no sɛ, "Mise sɛ nnɛma a ɛwɔ nkrakra-nkwan yi mu nni mu." Nana-kɔkɔɔ no see no, "Kɔfa bi mma me." Enti ɔkɔfaa bi baa. Wɔdidi wiei, na Nana-kɔkɔɔ no see sɛ, "Sɛn na m'awu Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, bra ha, me nananom ba." Berɛ a ɔbaa no, ɔfaa sika nketenkete na ɔhyɛɛ no fɛ. Afei, Nana-kɔkɔɔ no hwɛɛ ne anim kakra. Ɔsee sɛ, "Ah! saa abofra yi anim yɛ te sɛ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa n'anim." Abofra no see sɛ, "Nana, adɛn nti, berɛ a wuhu sɛ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa baa na ɔwui me nnamfo nyinnsa nyenyie (na woka saa), efisɛ woma me werɛ how." Nana-kɔkɔɔ no see no, "Yɛ mu." Afei abofra no yɛɛ ne ho mmerɛ wɔ efie hɔ. Nana no boaee. Ɔsee sɛ, "Ma me nkɔto mpoano." Enti Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa no kɔɔ. Berɛ a ɔduu mpoano no, ɔtuu sika nketenkete a nana no hyɛɛ no nyinaa na ɔkyekyereɛ. Afei ɔsee sɛ, "Ei, Nana-kɔkɔɔ, ei, Nana-kɔkɔɔ, me ne me, Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, seesei mprempren, maba na m'awu wo nananom ba, na mafa wo sika nketenkete nso." Nana-kɔkɔɔ no gyee no sɛ, "Mise wo anim te sɛ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa; seesei baabiara a wokɔ, mekɔ wo nkyɛn." Afei wɔnyinaa tuu mmirika, kiri! kiri! kiri! Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa duu ne kuro, na ɔmaa sika nketenkete no ne nnamfo, na ɔkɔɔ kwan no ho, na ɔkɔnnawii, na Nana-kɔkɔɔ no nso baa hɔ. Ɛno nti, berɛ a wokɔ kwan so a, wobɛte nneɛma a ɛreka "pe." Ɛno ne Nana-kɔkɔɔ a ɔretu Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa mmirika.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
