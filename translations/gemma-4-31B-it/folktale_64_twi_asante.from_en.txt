Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea Ananse, kɔkɔɔro no, kɔɔ aban mu no kwan

ANANSE O! ɔwaree Nsia baa no, a ɔyɛ Sorɔ-onyame maame, na ɔkɔɔ Nyame (Sorɔ-onyame) nkyɛn kasee no sɛ, "Da a wo maame, Nsia, bɛwu no, bra na kyerɛ me da a yɛapaw ma ayie no."

Ɛnnɛn mmiɛnsa akyi no, wɔbaa wei kyerɛɛ no sɛ Nsia, Onyame Maame no, awu. Ananse abusuafoa ne 'Berekuo, Kraman Jay, Kwakuo, Aberewa, ne Odwan, Odwan no. Ɛnna Ananse kasaa wɔn kyerɛɛ sɛ, "Wɔaba aba wei abɔ me amanneɛ fa ayie amamre ho, enti mo mpɛn m'akyi." Ɛnna Ananse ma obi kɔbɔɔ Onyame amanneɛ sɛ ɔreba asɔre no mu. Ɛna Nyame ma ne nkurɔfoɔ nyinaa bɔɔ nkɔmmɔ, sɛnea Omanhene hyiam ne nkurɔfoɔ saa. Na Ananse see, "Sɛ anwummerɛ dwo a, mɛba." Kwebra no yɛɛ atekese, na ɔtoo 'Berekuo, the King Jay, Kwakuo, the Monkey, ne Odwan, the Sheep' wɔ mu, na ɔtoo atekese no ne mmanmu, na ɔhyiraa ne ntoma no wɔ ho. Ɛna ɔkaa asɛm kyerɛɛ mmoa no sɛ, "Sɛ mefi ha kɔ Nyankopɔn hɔ a, mo bɛsu." Sɛ ɔduruu Nyankopɔn anim no, 'Berekuo yɛɛ ne nne tenten:

Kurukusu, ku! ku! ku!

Kurukusu, ku! ku! ku! ni.

Na Obere no gyaei (ne nne):

"Kakum!"

Na Onuama no gyaeɛ (ne nne):

"Me!"

Wei ne Kwaku Ananse dusu-sua no. Nnipa nyinaa kaa sɛ, "Woayɛ yie, ha na yɛnni ɔbarima a obetumi asu aa na wasu-sua wɔ akwan ahorow mmiɛnsa so." Sɛ ɔwiee (ne dusu-sua no) no, Nyame hwehwɛɛ fie fɛfɛɛfɛ ma no na watena mu. Anɔpa a ɛba no, berɛ a nneɛma nyinaa baa hɔ no, Nyame no noaa aduane fɛfɛɛfɛ maa Ananse. Berɛ a Kwaku Ananse huu aduane yi no, ɔkaa (ne ho), "Mrenkan biara ama Berekuo, mrenkan biara ama Kwakuo, mrenkan biara ama Odwan, efisɛ madi ayamyam bebree." Enti Ananse dinn, na wannka ne abusua biara. Ɛnnɛ, ɔne wɔn tenaa hɔ kɔsi sɛ Onyame see, "Yɛbɛyɛ nna nwɔtwe no ayie adwene." Nanso Nguano see (Ananse), "Kɔkrom kura me, menkɔ"; Kwakuo ne Berekuo nso see saa ara. Ɛnnɛ Onyame kɔɔ nkurɔfoɔ a wɔabɔ mu no nka, na ɔmaa wɔbɔɔ Ananse amanneɛ sɛ, eyi a ɔyɛɛ no da a ɛtekɛn no, ɔbɛtumi ayɛ no bio, efisɛ ɔne ne mpanyimfoɔ abɔ mu (deɛ wɔbɛtie). Ananse ayɛ mmuae sɛ, "Me nni ayɛ." Da a ɛdi so no, Onyame kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, "Wo ama me nsu adɔɔso efisɛ wo ayɛ me anigye." Na 'Berekuo, ne Kwakuo, ne Odwan kaa sɛ, "Yɛn nso, wo ama yɛekɔ kɔm, enti yɛ ne wo bɛyɛ ayɛyɛ firi abusua mu." Enti Kwakuo ne 'Berekuo kɔree kwae mu, nanso Odwan, anomadu no, tenaa fie; na Kwebia no bɔɔ mmɔden na ɔkɔtɔɔ nnuaba no so; ɛno na na ɛyɛ efisɛ ɔani gyee.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
