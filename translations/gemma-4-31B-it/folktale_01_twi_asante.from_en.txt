Sɛnea Obonto ya, nsuo-nam no, baa mu sɛ ɔnam nsuo mu no, ɔnam kwan a ɔnni hɔ saa koraa.

Wɔka sɛ Akokɔ-Nana wo hɔ, na ne nnoɔma yiayi. Ɔkaaa sɛ "nsuo mu mmoa" na wɔafa (nnoɔma no). Kraman no tena hɔ, na ɔka sɛ, "Ah, woama yɛn ani adwo (asɛm a ɛyɛ anigyei), na nea yɛpɛ sɛ yɛyɛ de kyerɛ wo nea ɛwɔ yɛn ti mu ne (yei): kɔfa kuku kɔkɔɔ ba, na yɛn nyinaa mma yɛn sure nsuo nnyini mu, na obi a onntumi nsu kosi sɛ ɛhyɛ mu no, ne no na wafa (nnoɔma no)."

Ɔde kɔkɔɔ no baa. Okoto, kraman no, suu na ɔhyɛɛ mu; wɔtɔɔ no firi mu; Mmobonse baa na ɔsuu; Okawa baa na ɔsuu; Oyoyo, nsuo-ɔwɔ no, baa na ɔsuu; Otidi baa na ɔsuu; Obonko, nsuo-kraman ketewa no, baa na ɔsuu; Akorese baa na ɔsuu; Opitiri baa na ɔsuu; Okobo baa na ɔsuu; nsuo mu mmoa nyinaa baa na wɔsuu. Akyiri yi, Obonto Ya, nsuo-nam ketewa no, baa sɛ ɔbɛsu; nsuo anfi ne ani mu; enti wɔhuu sɛ Obonto Ya na ɔde (nee nno).

Na Nana Panin no see sɛ, "Mrenni hwee mma wo." Ɛno nti, mmoa no nyinaa see sɛ, "Yɛn nti, yɛbɛbɔ no, efisɛ ɔayɛ yɛn anigye." Enti wɔbɔɔ no.

Berɛ a wɔwɔwie no bɔɔ no, Nana Akraa se, "Me gyae wo (daa) ma wo nante kwan a ɛnni hɔ; obiara renka wo nka bio."

Eyi ne nea nti a, sɛ mmaa "kɔtwa" (nam nsuo mu kɔfa namfo) a, wɔhu OBONTO YA a ɔrekwantuo ha ne hɔ seesei.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
