Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNE ADAMFO ADWENE NE NE NYINA MU ABƐBA AMANMU NO

Abɔnten bi ne ne yere kɔɔ abɔnten camp bi mu. Na Osebo, ɔbrɔn no, kyere abɔnten no na ɔdee ɔdii no, nanso ɔgyaa ne yere a na ɔyɛ yere-yɛyɛ. Da bi, ɔbaa no kɔɔ aburo-nan mmoa mu, na ɛhɔ na ɔhyiaa ɔbrɔn no ba, na ɔbisaa no sɛ, “Maame, dɛn na worehwehwɛ?” Ɔbaa no gyee me answers sɛ, “Me ne me kunu baa (ha), nanso ɔbrɔn bi akum no.” Ɔbrɔn no ba kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Me pɛ wo, enti mma woankɔ ha bio, efisɛ sɛ woba ha, na papa hyia wo a, ɔbɛkum wo.” Ɔbaa no amfa amma ananse, na ɔsan kɔɔ aburo-nan mmoa mu, na ɔbrɔn no kyere no, na ɔfaa no kɔɔ fie. Berɛ a ɔbrɔn no ba huu ɔbaa no, ɔkaa kyerɛɛ ne papa sɛ, “Meyare.” Na ɛyɛ ɔbaa no nti, a ɔahu no ansa. Ɔbrɔn no kaa kyerɛɛ ne ba sɛ, “Wubɛtumi ayare, nanso bra, kɔ na twa nam no.” Ɔtwaa (no), na ɔhunuu sɛ abofra bi da ne yamu, na ɔfaa no puei, na ɔtoo no baabi. Afei papa no bisaa no sɛ, “Dɛn na woakɔ too?” Abofra no gyee me answers sɛ, “Deɛ mɛkɔ too no, sɛ wahu no a, woankɔ di saa nam yi.” Ɔbrɔn no noaa aduane no. Ɔkaa kyerɛɛ ne ba sɛ ɔmmeba mme, nanso ɔgyee me answers sɛ, “Nam a, berɛ a mehwee m'ani so pɛ, ɛmaa meyaree, m'adwene ne sɛ, sɛ mede bi di a, mɛwu.” Papa no gyee me answers sɛ, “Wonyɛ mmarima mmarima.” Da a ɛba no, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ, berɛ a papa no kɔɔ kwae, na ne ba kɔfaa abofra no, faa no baa fie, na ɔhoroo no. Afei ɔkɔfaa aberewa; ɔnoaa no, ɔmiii ne nsuo no, na ɔmaa abofra no nom. Ɔhwɛɛ berɛ a ne papa san baa, na ɔfaa abofra no, na ɔde hyee no. Abofra no yɛ woren mfeɛ baako berɛ a ɔnyinii—abofra a ɔwɔ Ananse-asɛm mu nyini ntɛm. Afei Onipa no ba bisaa ɔbrɔn no ba sɛ, “Wo din dɛn?” Ɔgyee me answers sɛ, “Yɛfrɛ me Kofi-abɔnten-ba.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Me nso, yɛfrɛ me Kofi-onipa-ba.” Afei Kofi-abɔnten-ba kaa kyerɛɛ Kofi-onipa-ba sɛ, “Mepɛ sɛ me ne wo bɔ game, nanso mebo papa, na wo nso wobo no, enti, nnawɔtwe mmiɛnsa a ɛreba yi, mɛka kyerɛ no sɛ ɔnyansie me sɛnea mɛtumi atutu, na berɛ a ɔnyansie me, na mehu sɛnea wɔyɛ no, mɛtutu na mɛkyere no, na berɛ a wote sɛ m'anya no, oh!” “wobɛfa cutu, na wobɛtwa ne ti.” Agya no bae, na Kofi-the-beast’s-child kaa kyerɛ no sɛ, “Kyerɛ me sɛnea mɛtumi atu.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Adekyire anɔpa.” Adekyire anɔpa no, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ, ɔne ne father kɔe baabi, wore baabi pɛpɛɛpɛ, na father no tuu, na ne ba no nso tuu, na father no daa ne yafunu so. Father no kaa sɛ, “Tu na woatɔ me so.” Kofi-the-beast’s-child tuu, na ɔtɔɔ ne so, na Kofi-the-beast’s-child kyere ne kɔn. Ɔteɛm frɛɛ Kofi-the-human-being’s-child, ɔse, “M’anya no, enti bra.” Ɔtuu mmirika kɔfaa ne cutu, na ɔtwaa ne ti. Kofi-the-beast’s-child kaa kyerɛ Kofi-the-human-being’s-child sɛ, “Saa ara, wo ne me ayɛ pɛ seesei, enti ma yɛnkɔ nka no.” Wɔnkɔtwaa no, na wɔsan kɔɔ fie. Kofi-the-beast’s-child kaa kyerɛ Kofi-the-human-being’s-child sɛ, “M’ani gye sɛ wobɛpɛ sɛ wokɔ wo kuro.” Kofi-the-human-being’s-child kaa sɛ, “Aane, nanso mɛtumi ammu ne baabi.” Kofi-the-beast’s-child kaa sɛ, “Me ara mɛkɔ hɔ akɔhwehwɛ, na masan aba.” Ɔfaa kwan, na berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu nnipa bi wɔ aduane-pɛpɛɛpɛ baabi, na wɔreka kyerɛ wɔn ara sɛ, “Aane, ɛyɛ nokware sɛ ɔyadeɛfoɔ ne ne yere kɔe ɔyadeɛfoɔ camp no, na obiara ammu wɔn nka bio.” Ɛhɔ ara na Kofi-the-beast’s-child tuu mmirika kɔkaa kyerɛ Kofi-the-human-being’s-child sɛ, “M’ahunu wo kuro, na nnipa a m’ahunu no nso wɔ ntoma, enti mɛhwehwɛ bi ama wo na maka aba.” Kofi-the-beast’s-child faa kwan kɔe, na nnɔbae rekɔ. Ɔtuu mmirika kɔɔ wɔn so, na nnɔbae no towu wɔn nneɛma gu fam, na wɔtuu mmirika fii hɔ. Kofi-the-beast’s-child faa ntoma na ɔde baa Kofi-the-human-being’s-child hɔ. Afei Kofi-the-human-being’s-child kaa sɛ, “Gun ne gun-medicine (powder) na ɛda hɔ.” Kofi-the-beast’s-child hwehwɛɛ bi na ɔde baa ne nkyɛn. Kofi-the-beast’s-child bisaa Kofi-the-human-being’s-child sɛ, “Wobɛgye me ase?” Kofi-the-human-being’s-child kaa kyerɛ no sɛ, “Metumi ankasa anka hwee seesei, berɛ a mede m'anim gyina wo anim saa.” Kofi-the-beast’s-child faa Kofi-the-human-being’s-child na wɔfaa kwan, na wakyea no kɔ ne kuro. Berɛ a wɔduu kurom mfiase no, Kofi-aboa-ba kyerɛɛ Kofi-nnipa-ba sɛ, “Yɛadu wo kuro, na baabi a worekɔ yi, da biara mɛma wo nam kɛse; bio nnɔre a ɛbɛba no, mɛba wo nkyɛn, enti sɛ baabi a worekɔ yi woaware a, ma wo yere neda dan no baako mu, na wo nso neda baako mu.” Kofi-nnipa-ba duu fie, na ɔkae asɛm no sɛnea ɔne ne maame ne ne papa kɔɔ kwae, na sɛnea wɔwui nyinaa, ne sɛnea ɔfiri hɔ baa. Nnipa no suu ne ho. Ɔyɛɛ gun firing, ɔyɛɛ ayie, na ɔwiee adeɛ biara a ɛsɛ. Afei, Kofi-aboa-ba, berɛ a ɔsɔree no, ɔde nam baa. Berɛ a anadwo tɔɔ no, ɔbaa ne nkyɛn. Ankyɛ koraa na ɔwaree. Berɛ a ɔyere no kɔdae no, ɔmaa ɔdae baabi foforo (a ɛnyɛ ne nkyɛn). Anɔpa, ɔde nam kɛse ba ma ne yere. Afei, ne yere huu sɛ ɔnkɔ kwae, nanso ɛnam saa so, ɔde nam ba da biara, nanso ɔanka hwee. Da bi anadwo, berɛ a wɔda hɔ (ne dan mu), ɔyere no pɛɛ sɛ ɔde ne nsa aka ne kunu, na ɔkɔkaa adeɛ bi a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, na ne kunu nso da hɔ. Anɔpa a ɛbae no, berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ hwee, ɔkɔkae asɛm no ma nnipa no, ɔse, “Ampa, me kunu adamfo yi yɛ Gyata, na ampa, ɔno na ɔde nam ba ma no.” Ɛnna nnipa no faa wɔn gun kɔnnɔɔ aduane-kanea (baabi a wɔde nam no to) ho, na berɛ a anadwo tɔɔ, na Gyata no reba rede nam no ba reto aduane-kanea no so no, wɔyɛɛ no gun. Kofi-aboa-ba se, “Kofi-nnipa-ba, nnipa nni aseda.” Kofi-nnipa-ba tee asɛm no, ɔtuu mmirika kɔfaa no, ɔde guu hɔ, na ɔkɔkae asɛm no nyinaa ma kuro hɛne, sɛnea ɔne ne maame ne ne papa kɔree, ne amanehunu nyinaa a wɔhunuu. Ɔse, “Sɛsei a aboa yi awu no, me ne no bɛkɔ (bom), efisɛ me asɛm na ɛma ɔwui.” Na ɔmaa wɔyɛɛ aduom mmienu; wɔde Kofi-aboa-ba hyɛɛ baako mu; na ɔno (Kofi-nnipa-ba) nso yɛɛ ne ho gun, na wɔde no hyɛɛ aduom foforo no mu. Ɛnna wɔgyaei wɔn. Na wɔse, “Kɔyɛ ayɛ na monyɛ anigye wɔ mo adamfo mmoa mu.” Saa na adamfo fafo baa amansan yi mu.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
