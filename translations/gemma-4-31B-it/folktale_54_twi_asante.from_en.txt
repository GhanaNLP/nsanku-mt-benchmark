Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNE ADAMFO ADWENE NE NE NYINA MU ABƐBA AMANMU NO

AHUNTER ne ne yere kɔɔ abɔnten kamp mmienu bi mu. Na Osebo, Leopard no, kyere ahunter no na ɔdee dee no, nanso ne yere, a na ɔyɛ yefuo, no gyina hɔ. Ɔbaa no kɔɔ abrobɛbea mu (da koro bi), na (hɔ no) ɔhyiaa Gyata ba no, na ɔbisaa no sɛ, “Maame, dɛn na worehwehwɛ?” Ɔbaa no gyee kyerɛ no sɛ, “Me ne me kunu bae (ha), nanso Gyata bi akum no.” Gyata ba no kaa kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Me pɛ wo, enti mma woankɔ ha bio, efisɛ sɛ woba ha, na gye sɛ papa hyia wo a, ɔbɛkum wo.” Ɔbaa no amfa anya... na ɔsan kɔɔ abrobɛ no mu, na Gyata no kyere no, na ɔfaa no kɔɔ fie. Berɛ a Gyata no baa huu ɔbaa no, ɔsee ne papa sɛ, “Meyare.” Ɛyɛ ɔbaa no nti, a wahu no ansa. Ɔpyim no kaa kyerɛ ne ba sɛ, “Wobɛtumi ayare, nanso bra, kɔ anim, na woatwa nam no.” Ɔtwaa (no), na ɔhunuu sɛ abofra bi da ne yafunu mu, enti ɔfaa no fii hɔ, na ɔde guu baabi. Afei agya no bisaa no sɛ, “Dɛn ne dɛn na woakɔ tɔw agu?” Abofra no gyee kyerɛ no sɛ, “De a mekɔtɔw agu no, sɛ woahu no a, woankɔdi saa nam yi.” Ɔpyim no nohuu aduane no. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ba sɛ ɔmmpue mma ɔnni, nanso ɔyɛɛ no mmuae sɛ, “Nam, a m’ani a ɛtoo so no, ɛmaa me yadeɛ, m’adwene ne sɛ, sɛ mede bi nni a, mɛwu.” Agya no hyɛɛ no mmuae sɛ, “Wonte nkyene mpofirim.” Da a ɛdi so no, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ, berɛ a agya no kɔree kɔɔ kwaeɛ no, na ne ba no kɔfaa abofra no, faa no baa fie, na ɔhoroo no. Ɛna ɔkɔkɔɔ borɔdeɛ; ɔnoaa no, ɔmiii nsuo no firi mu, na ɔmaa abofra no nom. Ɔhwɛɛ berɛ a ne papa baeɛ no, na ɔfaa abofra no kɔhwee no. Abofra no yɛ worennyɛ afe baako pɛ a ɔnyinii—Ananse-asɛm mu abofra nyini mpɛnnyɛ. Ɛnnɛ Onipa ba no bisaa Gyata ba no sɛ, “Wo din de hwee?” Ɔyɛɛ no mmuae sɛ, “Yɛfrɛ me Kofi-gyata-ba.” Ɔseɛ, “Me nso, yɛfrɛ me Kofi-onipa-ba.” Ɛna Kofi-gyata-ba no ka kyerɛɛ Kofi-onipa-ba no sɛ, “Mepɛ sɛ me ne wo bɔu, nanso mebo me papa, na wo nso, wobo no, enti, nnawɔtwe mmiɛnsa a ɛreba yi mu, mɛka kyerɛ no sɛ ɔnyansie kyerɛ me sɛnea yɛtumi tumpa, na sɛ ɔkyerɛyɛe na mehu sɛnea yɛyɛ no a, mɛtumpa na matumi afa no, na sɛ moyɛ nka sɛ m’anya no a, oh! wobɛfa naba, na wobɛba abɛtwa ne ti.” Agya no baa, na Kofi-aboa-ba no kaa kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Kyerɛ me sɛnea mɛtumi atu.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Adekyire anɔpa.” Adekyire anɔpa no, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ, ɔne ne father kɔɔ baabi, wɔpɔpɔɔ baabi pɛpɛɛpɛ, na father no tuu, na ne ba no nso tuu, na father no dɔɔ ne yafunu. Agya no see sɛ, “Twa mmofra na tɔ me so.” Kofi-aboa-ba no bɔɔ mmɔden twaa so, na ɔtɔɔ ne so, na Kofi-aboa-ba no kyere ne kɔn. Ɔteew Kofi-nnipa-ba no kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “M’awura no, enti bra.” Ɔtuu mmirika kɔfaa ne cutu, na ɔtwaa ne ti. Kofi-the-beast’s-child ka kyerɛɛ Kofi-the-human-being’s-child sɛ, “Saa mprennea yi, wo ne me ayɛ pɛ, enti ma yɛnkɔ nka no.” Wɔnkɔtoo na wɔgyaei no, na wɔnsan kɔɔ fie. Kofi-the-beast’s-child kaa kyerɛ Kofi-the-human-being’s-child sɛ, “Mepɛ sɛ wopɛ sɛ kokɔ wo kuro.” Kofi-the-human-being’s-child kaa sɛ, “Aane, nanso mɛtumi mma mimmumu baabi a ɛwɔ.” Kofi-the-beast’s-child kaa sɛ, “Me ara na mɛkɔ hɔ akɔhwehwɛ ama wo, na mɛaba.” Ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ, na berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu nnipa bi wɔ aduane-pɛpɛ-beae, na wɔreka kyerɛ wɔn ho sɛ, “Aane, ɛyɛ nokware sɛ ɔbɔfoɔ ne ne yere kɔɔ ɔbɔfoɔ kampu no mu, na obiara anhu hɔn nka bio.” Ɛhɔ ara na Kofi-aboa-ba tuu mmirika kɔka kyerɛɛ Kofi-nnipa-ba sɛ, “Mahu wo kuro, na nnipa a mihuu no nso hyɛɛ ntoma, enti mɛkɔsiesie bi ama wo na maka aba.” Kofi-aboa-ba no hyɛɛ aseɛ na ɔfa kwan no so kɔe, na nkwafoɔ a wɔretɔ nneɛma na wɔreakɔ. Ɔtuu mmirika kɔɔ wɔn so, na nkwafoɔ no towii wɔn nneɛma gu fam na wɔtuu mmirika fii hɔ. Kofi-aboa-ba no faa ntoma no kɔmaa Kofi-nnipa-ba no. Ɛnnɛ Kofi-nnipa-ba no see sɛ, “Sika kanea ne kanea-aduro (powder) na ɛnni hɔ” (powder). Kofi-aboa-ba no hwehwɛɛ bi na ɔde baa no nkyɛn. Kofi-the-beast’s-child bisaa Kofi-the-human-being’s-child sɛ, “Wobɛda me ase?” Kofi-the-human-being’s-child kaa kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Mentumi mika biara seesei, sɛ megyina wo anim saa saa.” Kofi-the-beast’s-child faa Kofi-the-human-being’s-child na wɔnnam kɔɔ, na wakyea no akɔ ne akuraa mu. Berɛ a wɔduu kurom no nkyɛn no, Kofi-aboa-ba kyerɛɛ Kofi-nnipa-ba sɛ, “W’akuraa no ha ne ha, na baabi a worekɔ yi, da biara mɛma wo nam kɛseɛ; bio nnɔma a ɛbɛba anadwo no, mɛba wo nkyɛn, enti sɛ baabi a worekɔ no woaware a, ma wo yere neda dan no baako mu, na wo nso neda baako mu.” Ko fi-the-human-being’s-child duaa fie, na ɔkae asɛm no sɛ ɔne ne maame ne ne papa kɔɔ kwae mu, ne sɛ wɔn nyinaa wui, ne sɛ ɔde ne ho gyinaa hɔ, na ɔaba bio. Nnipa no suu ne ho. Ɔyɛɛ gun firing, ɔyɛɛ ayie no, na ɔwiee adeɛ biara (a ɛhia). Seesei, Kofi-the-beast’s-child, berɛ a ɔsɔree no, ɔde nam bae. Berɛ a anadwo tɔɔ nwunu no, ɔbaa ne nkyɛn. Ankyɛ koraa na ɔwaree. Berɛ a ɔbaa no kɔda no, ɔmaa ɔdaa baabi foforo (a ɛnyɛ ne nkyɛn). Anɔpa anɔpa, ɔde nam kɛseɛ bi ba ma ne yere. Ɛnna, ne yere huu sɛ ɔmman kɔ kwaeɛ, nanso ɛnam saa so, ɔde nam ba daa, nanso ɔmpɛ sɛ ɔka bi. Da bi bio anadwo, berɛ a wɔda hɔ (ne poku mu), ɔbaa no pɛɛ sɛ ɔfa ne nsa akasa ne kunu no, na ɔkɔkaa biribi a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, na ne kunu no nso da hɔ. Anɔpa a ɛba no, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ, ɔkɔkae amansan no asɛm no sɛ, "Ampa, me kunu adamfo no yɛ Aberewa, na ampa, ɔno na ɔde nam ba ma no." Ɛnna nnipa no faa wɔn gunn no kɔnnawii nnam-atwa no ho, na berɛ a ewia no tɔɔ, na Aberewa no reba sɛ ɔde nam no bɛto atwa no so no, wɔyɛɛ no gunn. Kofi-the-beast's-child see sɛ, "Kofi-the-human-being's-child, nnipa nni aseda." Kofi-the-human-being's-child tee, na ɔtuu mmirika kɔ, na ɔfaa no, na ɔmee no hɔ, na ɔkɔkae kuropon no nyinaa, sɛnea ɛyɛɛ a ɔne ne maame ne ne papa kɔree, ne amanehunu nyinaa a wɔahu. Ɔse, "Ɛfirisɛ mmoa yi awu, me ne no bɛkɔ (bom), efisɛ me nkoaa na m'asɛe ne sɛ ɔawu." Na ɔma wɔyɛ aduom mmienu; na wɔde Kofi-mmoa-ba no hyɛɛ baako mu; na ɔno nso (Kofi-nnipa-ba no) yɛɛ ne ho mpoa, na wɔde no hyɛɛ aduom foforo no mu. Afei wɔgyaei wɔn. Na wɔse, "Kɔ na wo nso nnoa na wo nso nnye anigye wɔ mo adamfo nkabom mu." Asaae koro no na adamfo fafoo baa amansan no mu.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
