Sɛnea nyansa baa amansan no mu

Wɔka sɛ Kwaku Ananse wɔ hɔ, na ɔboaa nimdeɛ nyinaa ano, hyee no bom baakɔ, na ɔtoo no kɔ kookoo mu. Afei ɔka sɛ ɔbɛkɔtwa dua kɔhyɛ ho, na asase so nyansa nyinaa ayɛ awie.

Enti ɔfaa no kɔɔ ne nkyɛn, na berɛ a ɔduu dua no ase baabi a ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkɔkyekyere no, ɔfaa ntoma bi, na ɔkyekyere no wɔ kɔkɔɔ no mu, na ɔkyekyere no ne anim, na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔbɔ dua no. Ɔborɔɔ, ɔborɔɔ, ɔborɔɔ; nanso hwee nnim. Ɔyɛɛ no bio, ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ bio sɛ ɔbɔ, ɔbɔ, ɔbɔ; nanso hwee nnim.

Seesei, ne ba Ntikuma, a ɔgyina hɔ no, see sɛ, "O, w'ani adu (ammɔne), ɛnyɛ yie koraa sɛ woatwa kuku no atron mu na woatwa no wo akyi, ɛnna wobetumi akɔ soro?" Ɔno (Kweku Ananse) see sɛ, "Kɔ, wo ne wo asɛm a ɛyɛ atitire no." Ɛnna ɔsan hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ soro bio sɛnea ɔyɛɛ no kan no, nanso ɔmpɛe bio. Ɛna ɔsuman mu yiye, na (awieeɛ) ɔfaa kɔkɔɔ no na ɔde guu neyɛn. Ɛna ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔbɔtena, na ɔkɔɔ soro ntɛm, Kra! kra! kra! (ne bɔtena nneɛma no yɛ saa); ɔkɔree. Ɔduu baabi a nnua no hyɛ aseɛ sɛ wɔrepɛpɛ firi dua no mu, na ɔkaa (ne ho), "Me, Kwaku Ananse, nyame ketewa no, Afio! Mebetumi aka sɛ mede awu, me ba a ɔyɛ ketewa saa, ketewa saa, ketewa saa—me nkoaa, meboaa nyansa nyinaa (sɛnea mede anwum) bom beae koro, nanso obiara dwoe a me nkoaa mpɛn nso anhu no, na hwɛ! me ba, saa abofra a ɔda nufu saa, na wanyi ama me. Ɛnna ɔfaa kuku no, na nne a ɛte sɛ nnyigyei tee, tintini! na ɔtoo no afiri, na nne a ɛte sɛ nnyigyei tee, tesee! Ɛno ne sɛ obiara nyaa nyansa; na obiara a onkɔhɔɔ hɔ wɔ berɛ mu (deɛ ɔbɛfa bi) no—fa me kasa no mma—yɛ kwasea.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
