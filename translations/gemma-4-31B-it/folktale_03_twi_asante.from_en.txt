Sɛnea nyansa baa amansan no mu

Wɔka sɛ Kwaku Ananse wɔ hɔ, na ɔboaa nimdeɛ nyinaa ano, hyee no bom baakɔ, na ɔtoo no kɔ kookoo mu. Afei ɔka sɛ ɔbɛkɔtwa dua kɔhyɛ ho, na asase so nyansa nyinaa ayɛ awie.

Enti ɔfaa no kɔɔ ne nkyɛn, na berɛ a ɔduu dua no ase baabi a ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkɔkyekyere no, ɔfaa ntoma kɛkɛɛ, na ɔkyekyere no hyɛɛ kɔkɔɔ no mu, na ɔkyekyere no ne anim, na ɔhyɛɛ asei sɛ ɔbɔ dua no. Ɔborɔɔ, ɔborɔɔ, ɔborɔɔ; nanso hwee nnim. Ɔyɛɛ no bio, ɔhyɛɛ asei bio sɛ ɔbɔ, ɔbɔ, ɔbɔ; nanso hwee nnim.

Seesei, ne ba Ntikuma, a ɔgyina hɔ no, see sɛ, "O, w'ani adu (ammɔne), ɛnyɛ yie koraa sɛ woatwa kuku no atron mu na woatwa no wɔ w'akyi, na ɛnna wobetumi abɔne?" Ɔno (Kwebia) see sɛ, "Yɛn kɔ, wo ne wo asɛm a ɛyɛ atitire no." Ɛna ɔsan hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔbɔne bio te sɛ mberɛ a ɛdi kan no, nanso ɛnyɛe hwee bio. Ɛna ɔsuman mpo, na (awie foo) ɔfaa kuku no na ɔde guu ne akyi. Ɛna ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔbɔne, na ɔbɔnee ntɛm, Kra! kra! kra! (ne bɔne no nne yɛ saa); ɔkɔe. Ɔduu baabi a nnua no hyɛ aseɛ sɛ wɔrepre firi dua no mu, na ɔsee (ne ho) sɛ, "Me, Kwaku Ananse, nyame ketewa Afio nka me! Mebetumi asie mpo, me ba a ɔyɛ ketewa, ketewa, ketewa yi—me, meboaa nyansa nyinaa (sɛnea meadwen) gu baabi baako, nanso bi tena hɔ a me mpo anhu, na hwɛ! me ba, eyi a ɔda nufu mpo, na wakyɛra me." Ɛna ɔfaa kuku no, na nne bi tee, tintini! na ɔtowee no afire, na nne bi tee sɛ biribi repere, tesee! Saa na obiara nyaa nyansa; na obiara a ɔankɔ hɔ wɔ berɛ mu (de nea ɛwɔ hɔ nyyɛ) yɛ—fa me asɛm yi mma—kwasea.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
