Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea nnipa ne mmoa hyɛɛ asei sɛ wɔgyae sleep na wɔsɔree

Wɔka sɛ tete no, Owuo ne Adae yɛdeɛ koro pɛ. Ɛnna, da koro bi, Owuo bisaa sɛ, “Hwan na obetumi nyi hwee afi m'aban mu?” Akokonini, Akoko no, gyina hɔ; ɔse, “Kokuro, me betumi.” Oberekuo se, “Du du, du du du, me betumi!” Apatupere se, “Twetwero, twetwero, me betumi.” Owuo se, “Momma m'anim, mobɛnim sɛ onipa nkwa ne ne kra, na sɛ onipa da, na mefa ne kra afi n'anim a, ɛnna ɔyɛ dɛn; mobɛnim sɛ ɔyɛ okuaa?” Atirigye nnanom no se, “Ɛnna yɛbɛsɔre wɔn.” Owuo se, “Sɛ mobetumi a, mɛma mo ayɛ mpanyimfo.” Biribiara hyɛɛ asei sɛ ɛrekyere, na nnipa nyinaa daa. Ɛdaadeɛ a ɛtɔ so mmienu no kɔɔ, ɛnna Oberekuo gyinaa ne nne, “Du du, du du du.” Ɛnna Otwe, Duyker no, tɔɔ ne ho na ɔsɔree fi adaa mu; ɔse, “Ei, ansa na nneɛma bɛyɛ hɔ a, mɛkɔ adi kkakra; seesei ɔyɛkofo no nnim sɛ ɔsɔre.” Duyker no kɔɔ dua bi ase; ɔredi. Akyi no, Akokonini, Akoko no, gyinaa (ne nne): “Anɔpa ayɛ, wahu? Anɔpa ayɛ, wahu?” Na ɔyɛkofo no tɔɔ ne ho na ɔsɔree fi adaa mu na ɔfaa ne gun; ɔkɔɔ kwae; ɔhunuu Duyker no gyina hɔ; ɔmaa ne gun no kɔɔ soro, pamm! ɔwui no. Afei Apatupere nnoma no nso gyinaa ne nne, “Momba mo nsɔre, momba mo nsɔre.” Na nnipa nyinaa tɔɔ wɔn ho na wɔsɔree. Ɛnna, atirigye nnanom no kɔɔ Owuo hɔ, sɛ wɔbɛka amanneɛ no ama no, wɔse, “Wahu, yɛasɔre nnipa nyinaa.” Owuo se, “Oh, ɛyɛ nwanwa ma me; efie nnɛ kɔronu, moayɛ Mpanyimfo (deɛ nyinaa a ɛwɔ) wim ase.”

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
