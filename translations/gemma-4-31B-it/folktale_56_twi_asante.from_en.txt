Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea nnipa ne mmoa hyɛɛ asei sɛ wɔgyae sleep na wɔsɔree

Wɔka sɛ tete no, Owuo ne Adae yɛ ade baako pɛ. Da koro bi, Owuo bisaa asɛm sɛ, “Hwan na obetumi agye biribi afiri m'aban mu?” Akokonini, Kokrokoo no, gyina hɔ; ɔseɛ, “Kokuro, metumi.” Oberekuo seɛ, “Du du, du du du, metumi!” Apatupere seɛ, “Twetwero, twetwero, metumi.” Owuo seɛ, “Momma m'anim, mo nim sɛ onipa nkwa ne ne kra, na sɛ onipa da, na mafa ne kra afiri ne nkyɛn a, ɛnna hwan ne no; mo nim sɛ ɔ...” “yɛyɛ okuaa?” Atufoɔ nnanom no see, “Ɛnna yɛbɛsɔre wɔn.” Owuo see, “Sɛ mobetumi a, mɛma mobɛyɛ mpanyimfo.” Nsɛm no hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɛreka, na nnipa nyinaa daa. Nsuo a ɛtɔ mprempren no apue, ɛnna Oberekuo gyinaa hɔ kasae sɛ, “Du du, du du du.” Ɛnna Otwe, Duyker no, sɔree fi n’oburoni mu; ɔse sɛ, “Ei, ansa na nneɛma bɛyɛ hwee, mɛkɔ adi kkakra; seesei ɔbɔdeɛ no nnyɛ n’asɔre.” Duyker no kɔree dua bi ase; na ɔredi. Akyerɛ, Akokonini, Akoko no, tee ne nneɛma kɔ soro: “Anɔpa ayɛ, wahu? Anɔpa ayɛ, wahu?” Na ɔyadeɛ no tɔɔ ne ho na ɔsɔree fi nna mu na ɔfaa ne gun; ɔkɔɔ kwaeɛ mu; ɔhunuu Duyker no sɛ ɛgyina hɔ; ɔtee ne gun no, pamm! wadi no. Ɛnnɛ Apatupere nnoma no nso gyaeɛ ne nne, “Mo mpɛn mpo, mo mpɛn mpo.” Na nnipa nyinaa hwee wɔn ho na wɔsɔree. Ɛnnɛ, saa abɔdeɛ yi kɔɔ Owuo hɔ, kɔɔ hɔ kɔka amanneɛ no kyerɛɛ no, sɛ, “Woahu, yɛagye nnipa nyinaa afi nna mu.” Owuo see, “O, ɛyɛ nwanwa kɛse ma me; efie nnɛ kɔsi da a ɛbɛba, mo ne Mpanyimfo (deɛ nyinaa a ɛwɔ) wim ase.”

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
