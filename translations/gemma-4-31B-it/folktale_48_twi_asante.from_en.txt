Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA EKUANA AKYENE BƐYƐƐ KETE

Ɔkɔm kɛse bi baa, na Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ Esono hɔ, na ɔsee no sɛ, “Agya Esono, ma yɛnkɔ (na yɛnhwehwɛ) aburodu.” Esono see no sɛ, “Bra, ma yɛnkɔ.” Ɔne Kwaku, Kwebia no, hyɛɛ aseɛ. Enti, deda saa berɛ no, Esono wɔnini kɛse; na sɛ ɔne Kwaku Ananse repa hɔ a, Esono nsam no hyɛa ntenten mu, enti wɔnni kwan koraa sɛ wɔbɛkɔ. Ɛnna Kwaku Ananse hwɛɛ (no) yiye, na ɔka kyerɛɛ Esono sɛ, “Ma me ntwa wo nsam no mu kakra, na yɛabɛtumi akɔ mpra mpra.” Esono see no sɛ, “Twa no, na yɛnhwɛ (sɛ ɛbɛboa anaa).” Ɛnna Kwebia no twaa nam, nam mpaabu mmiɛnsa kɛse, fii ne nsam no. Ananse faa no hyɛɛ ne ti. Ɔsee Esono sɛ, “Mɛfa eyi akɔto me fie na mɛaba.” Ananse duu fie; ɔsee sɛ, “Aso (ne yere), m’apira Esono a ɔyɛ kwasea a ɔyɛ panyin nyinaa, na matwa nam nyinaa fii ne nsam.” Aso gyee no ase sɛ, “Anigye, Apeafo.” Ɛhɔ ara na Ananse ne ne mma ne ne yere gyinaa ponu no, na Ananse maa Ntikuma (ne ba) dono kyerɛw baako, Tikonokono baako, na Afudotwedotwe nso baako. Ansa berɛ kakra, obi bɔɔ Ananse ponu no, pan! pan! pan! Ɛnna Ananse ne ne mma gyinaa (nee wɔn nne) na wɔtoo dwom:

Hwan na ɔrebɔ me pon? Hwan na ɔrebɔ me pon?

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den !

Ɛna Enyamaa ba no (a ne papa na ɔde bae) hyɛɛ anansesɛm no ase:

Me nnyɛ na me yɛ Ena’s ba no?

Me nnyɛ na me yɛ Ɛno (Elephant) baa?

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den !

Na Ananse yɛɛ ne nne kɛse :

Dɛn na wopɛ?

Dɛn na wopɛ?

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den !

Na Ena’s ba no gyae (ne nne):

Meresi aba father akyi nam no!

Meresi nnam a ɛwɔ agya akyi no!

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den !

Na Ananse yɛɛ ne nne kɛse :

“ Wo papa akyi nam yɛ dɛ sen onoa nam !

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den !

Metwa bi, na matwa no sɛ mɛnam so!

Metwa bi, na makyɛ!

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den !

Gon ! gon ! gon ! gon ! gon !

Na Enyama no ba no dancee, dancee, dancee, na wakɔ. Berɛ a ɔbaa (fie) no, ne papa bisaa no sɛ, “Nam no wɔ he?” Ɔgyee ase sɛ, “Ananse nni hɔ, berɛ a mekɔɔ no.” Enyama no mma duaa no nyinaa kɔɔ Kwebla (Spider) no fie. Berɛ a wɔkɔe na wɔaba no, wɔn papa bisaa wɔn (ho); wɔse, “Berɛ a mekɔɔ no, ɔnni hɔ.” Nanso nea ɛsi saa koraa ne sɛ, mprempren ara a Ananse hyɛɛ dance music no ase, Enyama no mma no dancee, dancee, dancee kɔɔ mfimre mu, na wɔwerɛɛ adwuma a wɔn papa de kɔɔ no nyinaa. Afei ɔkɛse no se, “Me ara na mɛkɔ gyɛ me dea.” Ɔduu Ananse pon no anim, na ɔfaa ne ti bɔɔ no, pan ! pan ! pan ! na Kwebla (Spider) no hyɛɛ (refrain) no ase :

Hwan na ɔrebɔ me pon?

Hwan na ɔrebɔ me pon?

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den !

Ɛna Ena no gyaeɛ (ne nne):

Me nnyɛ me, Agya Elephant ?

Me nnyɛ me, Agya Elephant ?

Na Ananse yɛɛ ne nne kɛse :

Dɛn na wopɛ?

Dɛn na wopɛ?

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den !

Na Ena kɔtɔe ne nne:

Meresi me nanam mmofra no!

Mereba sɛ mɛfa me nanam mmofra no!

“Denkye, denkye, denkye, den!” Na Ananse yi ne nne kɔ soro:

Wo mpaabo nam yɛ dɛ sen onom nam!

Metwa kakra kɔnam!

Metwa bi maa mebɛma afoforo!

Kon! kon! kon! kon! kon!

Agya Elephant dancee, na ɔdancee, na ɔdancee. Afei ɔsee, "Spider, bra ha." Spider no see, "Ɛsɛ sɛ meba na woatram me na woahu me?" Elephant no see, "Sɛ woba a, mɛnyɛ biribiara a ɛbɛyɛ wo yaw, na sɛ eyi betumi asɛe a, yɛbɛka sɛ mɛtɔɔ saa dance yi ne m'atumi." Spider no puee na ɔdaadee, ɔdaadee, ɔdaadee, na ɔdaadee, ɛmaa Elephant no dancee, na ɔdancee, na ɔdancee; afei Elephant no kɔɔ. Ɛno nti na Elephant no atumi yɛ ketewa saa; ɛyɛ dance a ɔtɔɔ no nti; tete no, ɛnyɛ saa.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
