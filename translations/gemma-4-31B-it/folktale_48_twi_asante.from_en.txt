Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA EKUANA AKYENE BƐYƐƐ KETE

Kɔkɔɔrɔ bi baa, enti Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ Esono hɔ na ɔsee no sɛ, “Agya Esono, ma yɛnkɔ (na yɛnhwehwɛ) amampire.” Esono no see no sɛ, “Bra, ma yɛnkɔ.” Ɔne Kwaku, Kwebia no, hyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ. Ɛno ara, kosi saa berɛ no, Ena na ɔwɔ akɔn kɛse; na sɛ ɔne Kwaku Ananse repa hɔ a, Ena akɔn no bɛkɔ mmoa mu, enti wɔrentumi nkɔ anim koraa. Ɛna Kwaku Ananse hwɛɛ no yiye, na ɔkaa kyerɛɛ Ena, sɛ, “Ma me ntwa wo akyi kotoku kakra, na yɛabɛtumi anya kwan ntɛm.” Ena no kaa sɛ, “Twa no na yɛnhwɛ (sɛ ɛbɛboa yɛn anaa).” Enti Kwebia no twae nam, nam kɛseɛ a ɛhyɛ kɛtɛ mmiɛnsa mu, fii ne akyi. Ananse too no ne tiw. Ɔkaa kyerɛɛ Esono sɛ, “Mɛfa eyi akɔ gyina me fie na mɛaba.” Ananse duu fie; ɔkaa sɛ, “Aso (ne yere), m’apira Esono a ɔyɛ kwaseafoɔ mu panyin no, na matwa n’atadeɛ mu nam nyinaa.” Aso gyee kyɛn sɛ, “Anigye ne wo, Apeafo.” Ɛhɔ ara na Ananse ne ne mma ne ne yere gyinaa pon no, na Ananse maa Ntikuma (ne ba) dono koto, Tikonokono koto, na Afudotwedotwe baako nso. Ansa berɛ kɔa, obi bɔɔ Ananse ponu no, pan! pan! pan! Ɛno nti Ananse ne ne mma kɔɔ sorɔ (wɔn nne) na wɔtoo dwom:

Hwan na ɔrebɔ me pon? Hwan na ɔrebɔ me pon?

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den !

Ɛna Enyamaa ba no (a ne papa na ɔde bae) hyɛɛ anansesɛm no ase:

Me nnyɛ na me yɛ Ena’s ba no?

Me nnyɛ na me yɛ Ɛno (Elephant) baa?

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den !

Na Ananse yɛɛ ne nne kɛse :

Dɛn na wopɛ?

Dɛn na wopɛ?

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den !

Na Ena’s ba no gyae (ne nne):

Meresi aba father akyi nam no!

Meresi nnam a ɛwɔ agya akyi no!

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den !

Na Ananse yɛɛ ne nne kɛse :

“ Wo papa akyi nam yɛ dɛ sen onoa nam !

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den !

Metwa bi, na matwa no sɛ mɛnam so!

Metwa bi, na makyɛ!

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den !

Gon ! gon ! gon ! gon ! gon !

Na Enyama no ba no dwenei, na odwenei, na odwenei, na okɔɔ. Berɛ a ɔkɔɔ (fie) no, ne papa bisaa no sɛ, “Ɛhene na nnam no wɔ?” Ɔgyee ase sɛ, “Ananse nni hɔ, berɛ a mekɔɔ no.” Enyama no mma duaa no nyinaa kɔɔ Kwebla (Spider) fie. Berɛ a wɔakɔ na wɔaba a, wɔn agya bisaa hɔn (ho) ; wɔse, “Berɛ a mekɔe no, ɔnni hɔ.” Nanso nea ɛsiɛɛ ankasa ne sɛ, Ananse bɔɔ nnwom no mpo a, Elephant mma no dancee, dancee, dancee kɔɔ mfimre mu, na wɔwerɛɛ adwuma a wɔn agya kɔɔ hɔ ma wɔn no nyinaa. Ɛnnɛ ɔyɛn kɛse no see sɛ, “Me ara na mɛkɔ fa me dea.” Ɔduu Ananse ponu anim, na ɔfaa ne ti na ɔbɔɔ no, pan ! pan ! pan ! na Kwaku Ananse hyɛɛ aseɛ (asɛm a ɛsan aba no) :

Hwan na ɔrebɔ me pon?

Hwan na ɔrebɔ me pon?

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den !

Ɛna Ena no gyaeɛ (ne nne):

Me nnyɛ me, Agya Elephant ?

Me nnyɛ me, Agya Elephant ?

Na Ananse yɛɛ ne nne kɛse :

Dɛn na wopɛ?

Dɛn na wopɛ?

Denkye, denkye, denkye, den !

Na Ena kɔtɔe ne nne:

Meresi me nanam mmofra no!

Mereba sɛ mɛfa me nanam mmofra no!

“Denkye, denkye, denkye, den!” Na Ananse yi ne nne kɔ soro:

Wo mpaabo nam yɛ dɛ sen onom nam!

Metwa kakra kɔnam!

Metwa bi maa mebɛma afoforo!

Kon! kon! kon! kon! kon!"

Agya Elephant dancee, na ɔdancee, na ɔdancee. Afei ɔsee, "Kwebla, bra ha." Kwebla no see, "Ɛsɛ sɛ meba na woatram me na woahu me kɔm?" Ɛlefant no see sɛ, "Sɛ woba a, mɛyɛ wo hwee, na sɛ eyi betumi asene a, yɛbɛka sɛ m'akɔn mu na m'atɔ saa asansa yi." Kwebla baa hɔ na ɔdidi, ɔdidi, ɔdidi, ɔdidi, na ɛma Ɛlefant no dance, dance, dance; ɛnna Ɛlefant no kɔɔ. Ɛno nti na Ena’s akɔn yɛ ketewa saa; ɛyɛ asɔre a ɔtɔe no nti; tete no, ɛnyɛ saa.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
