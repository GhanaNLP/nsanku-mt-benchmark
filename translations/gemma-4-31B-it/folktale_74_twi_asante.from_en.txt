Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

KWAMENA, DWOMANTON A ƐWƆ NNUA NNUA NO MU

Wɔka sɛ tete bi, ɔhemaa bi na ɔwɔ abaaba, na ɛnka sɛ wɔde no hyɛ hwan nsa sɛ ɔbɛware no, ɔbɛka sɛ ɔnni no anigyeɛ. Nanso (awieeɛ), Brumo Omanhene, Adu Gyanfi, baa na ɔware ɔhemaa no ba no. Ɔhemaa baa no kaa kyerɛ ne kunu sɛ, "Me ne wo bɛkɔ wo kurom." Ɔbarima no hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, "Ma me nkɔ (kan), na Adae a ɛbɛtɔ Kwasieda so no, Monday a ɛdi so no, mɛba aba wo." Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "Dabi, me ne wo bɛkɔ bɔm." Enti wɔnannee kɔɔ. Berɛ a wɔnam kɔrono no, wɔhunuu kuro kɛse bi a ɛwɔ ɛponu fɛfe wɔ ne ponu no ho, na fɛfe hyi kuro no nyinaa. Se wo nni obi a ɔne wo yɛ mogya a wowi wɔ kurom yi mu, na wogyina dwunumu na woteɛ, obiara anka wo nkyerɛ. Na saa Ɔhemaa baa yi ho, ne nkabom mu obiara nni hɔ a wowi. Kasamu ne ne nkaeɛ (yehwɛ, me werɛ fii sɛ mɛka sɛ) berɛ a Adu Gyanfi duruu dade dwunumu no, ɔkɔɔ ne fie; ɔankasa ne kasa bio kɔɔ Ɔhemaa baa no ho. Ɔhemaa baa ne ne nkurufu aberewa, Kra, tenaa hɔ, na wɔgyina panyin dade kuro no akyi, na wɔannya kwan bi a wɔbɛkɔ fie. Na Ɔhemaa baa no gyae ne nne (na ɔtoo dwom):

Adodowa, bue me, me na maba, Adodowa Kwasi Kampire, bue me, Adodowa, bue me o.

Na Adodowa angye no. Onua no nso, anhu kwan a ɔbɛsan kɔ ne kurom. Ɛna ɔne Kra kɔɔ ntoma mmienu mu. Na Ɔhemaa baa no faa kuku koro kɔtweɛ so, na ɔfaa ebi foforo twaree ne ti; na Kra nso twɛɛ ne nkyɛn. Ɛno nti, sɛ wokɔ baabi a nnuaba (gyina) no wɔ, a Ɛhemaa ba ne ne nsafoa Kra na wɔtenn wɔ hɔ; wɔayi wɔn ayɛ mmoa-dua a wɔfrɛ no "Kwamena" a ɛso kɔ soro kɔkɔɔkɔɔ.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
