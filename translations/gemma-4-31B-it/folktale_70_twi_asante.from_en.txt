Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ANANSESƐM

Na abayadeɛ mmiɛnsa bi wɔ hɔ tete; wɔn nyinaa dɔ baa baako. Saa abayadeɛ mmiɛnsa yi firii Bontuku kɔɔ Cape Coast, na wɔse wɔrekɔtɔ nneɛma. Berɛ a wɔduu Cape Coast no, baako wɔn mu wɔ kyɛnkyɛn. Ɔfaa kyɛnkyɛn no, na ɔtwaa (nsuo-efu) bi guu kyɛnkyɛn no so, na ɔhwɛɛ mu. Ɔhunuu sɛ wɔn dɔfo no awu, na ɔda veranda-room a ɛyɛ mpanimpon mu. Ɔsee, "Me nnamfo, yɛn dɔfo no awu." Baako wɔn mu see, "Mɛma yɛdu Bontuku mpofirim na yɛakɔ gyae no." Ɔfoforo no nso see, "Sɛ wubetumi ama yɛadu hɔ a, mɛma ɔnsɔre mpofirim." Enti baako kɔtwaa aduro, guu wɔn nnam so, na wɔfirii ase kɔɔ. Mpofirim ara na wɔduu hɔ. Ɔfoforo nso kɔfaa aduro guu kuku kɔkɔɔ mu; na ɔfaa ne nnam-atwa no tɔɔ aduro no mu, na ɔsprinkled owuo no. Ɔsee, "Owuo, nsɔre; owuo, nsɔre!" na ɔsɔree. Enti saa nnipa mmiɛnsa yi mu no, hwan ne onipa a ɔyɛɛ adeɛ a ɛyɛ eyie?
