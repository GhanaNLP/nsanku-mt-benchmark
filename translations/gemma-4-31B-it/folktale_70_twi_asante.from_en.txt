Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ANANSESƐM

Na abofra mma mmiɛnsa bi wɔ hɔ; wɔn nyinaa dɔa abofra baa baako. Saa mma mmiɛnsa yi fii Bontuku kɔɔ Cape Coast, na wɔse wɔrekɔ tɔ nneɛma. Berɛ a wɔduu Cape Coast no, baako wɔn mu wɔ kyɛnkɛn. Ɔfaa kyɛnkɛn no, na ɔtwaa (nnuabaa) bi guu kyɛnkɛn no so, na ɔhwɛɛ mu. Ɔhunuu sɛ wɔn dɔfo no awu, na ɔda veranda-room a ɛyɛ mpanimpon mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Adamfo, yɛn dɔfo awu." Baako kyerɛɛ sɛ, "Mɛma yɛaduru Bontuku ansa na yɛakɔ hyɛ no fam." Ɔfoforo nso kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wotumi ma yɛduru hɔ a, mɛma ɔsɔre ntɛm." Ɛnnɛ, baako kɔtoo aduro, na ɔde guu wɔn nwu so, na wɔfa kwan. Ɛno ara na wɔduruu hɔ. Bi foforo nso kɔkɔɔ aduro baaa na ɔtoo no kɔtɛ kuku mu; na ɔfaa ne nnaman-atwa no too no aduro no mu, na ɔpɛpɛɛ no wɔ owuo no so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Owuo, sɔre; owuo, sɔre!" na ɛsɔree. Enti wɔ saa nnipa mmiɛnsa yi mu no, hwan na ɔyɛɛ adeɛ a ɛyɛ eyie koraa?
