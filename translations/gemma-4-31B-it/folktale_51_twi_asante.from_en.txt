Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea Aturukuku, anomaa no, kɔɔ kwaeɛ mu, na ɔmaa Akoko, akoko baa no, baa fie no.

AKOKO, Akoko-aba, ne Aturukuku, Aturukuku-anomaa, firi agya baako ne na baako. Ɛnnɛ, Akoko ka kyerɛ Aturukuku sɛ, “Kɔ Dwumfuo-kuro no mu, na me nso mɛkɔ Kwae-kuro no mu, na sɛ w’anya aduane a, ɛnna wobɛba aba m’akyerɛ.” Ɛnnɛ wɔgyae onu Onyansa. Mfe mmiɛnsa Aturukuku anhunu ne nua. Ɔka kyerɛ ne kunu sɛ, “Kɔ me akyi, efisɛ mɛkɔ rehwehwɛ me nua.” Ɛnnɛ ne kunu ka sɛ, “Sɛ woaka a, me asi, kɔ, na ma me nkɔ wo akyi.” Na wɔnomaa na wɔdu kuro bi mu (baabi a ne nua te). Wɔhunuu dɔteɛ a wɔatwa no sɛ kɔm no wɔ hɔ, na ɛhɔ na ɔne ne nua hyiaa. Na Akoko bisa Aturukuku sɛ, “Sɛ wokɔɔ Dwumfuo-kuro no mu no, w’anya aduane tɛn?” Ɔgyee mmuae sɛ, “Baabi a mekɔɔ no, guinea-grain nko ara na ɛwɔ hɔ; na berɛ a mepuee me mma, mmienu anaa mmiensro, na wɔfa me akyi no, na wɔadi kɛseɛ sɛ saa (ɔkyerɛɛ wɔn kɛseɛ), mehwɛɛ wɔn yie, na ɔbɔdeɛ bi yɛɛ ne gun, pom! na ɔkumee me mma, na sɛ mennim nyansa a, wode m'akumyia nso.” Sɛ ɔwie ne kasa no a, Akoko ka sɛ, “Baabi a mekɔe no, meyɛɛ obi a ɔwɔ animuonyam kɛse.” Aturukuku bisaa no sɛ, “Kyerɛ me ne ‘obi a ɔwɔ animuonyam kɛse’ no ase.” Ɛnnɛ Akoko ka sɛ, “Ne ‘obi a ɔwɔ animuonyam kɛse’ no ase ne sɛ, mfatoho, sɛ me mpanyin trã ntoma fɛfɛɛfɛ bi a, meka kɔ da so, na me fɛfɛɛfɛ tɔ so, na berɛ a me mpanyin fa nam gu nkwan mu no, mefa nea mepɛ; aduane biara a me mpanyin bɛdi, meka kɔ fa bi, na berɛ a medidi saa no, me mpanyin ntumi mfa ne gun anaa apɔm mma me. Ɛno ne nea nti na meka sɛ meyɛ obi a ɔwɔ animuonyam kɛse.” Akoko ka sɛ, “Baabi a wote no, ɛnyɛ beae pa, enti Memeneda a ɛdi Kwasieda Adae anim no, fa wo mma ne wo kunu bra, na momma wɔn nyinaa ntena yɛn fie.” Aturukuku ka sɛ, “Me asi, mɛkɔ, na Memeneda a ɛdi Kwasieda Adae anim no, mɛba.” Ɛnnɛ ɔsuman bi baa Akoko kuro no mu, ɔrehyɛ ne nkabom nketekete no. Wɔbisaa no sɛ, “Nana, dɛn na ɛrekɔ so?” Ɔkae sɛ, “Dente se woama no akɔm mfeɛ mmienu, enti ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ ɔhene hɔ na woka kyerɛ no sɛ ɔmfa akoko (ne) nneɛma duoduo mmienu (eggs 12) aba.” Ɛnna ɔhene kɔfaa akoko nan, ɔfaa nneɛma duoduo mmienu (eggs 12), ɔnoaa eto, na ɔkɔmaa Dente abosom no wɔ ne nkyɛn mud altar no ase, ɔtwaa akoko nan no tii na ɔtoo hɔ, na ɔfaa wɔn nwi ne wɔn ti, ne wɔn nan, na ɔfaa hɔ hyɛɛ mud altar no nkyɛn; na ɔfaa eto no hyɛɛ so. Afei, Adae ansa ne da no, Aturukuku rekɔ hyia ne nua baa. Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu ne nua agya a wɔwɔ Dente abosom altar no hɔ, ne ne ti, ne ne nan, ne ne ntaban, ne ne liver, ne ne mma; Aturukuku kae sɛ, “Afei, mentumi mpim hɔ bio.” Na Turtle-dove tuu kɔtena silk-cotton tree bi so, na ɔkaa (ɔtoo): " Me su ma me nua baa o, Awuawu o."

Wo, Hen, a woyɛ obi a wuwɔ animdeɛ, eyi ne wo ti?

Wo, Akoko, a woyɛ obi a wuwɔ animuonyam, eyi ne wo ntaban?

Wo, Hen, a woyɛ obi a wuwɔ animdeɛ, eyi ne wo nan?

Wo, Hen, a woyɛ obi a wuwɔ animdeɛ, eyi ne wo ya?

Wo, Akoko, a woyɛ obi a wuwɔ animdeɛ, eyi ne wo yafunu?

Na Aturukuku see sɛ, "Me renkɔ kwaafoɔ kuro mu bio, efisɛ kwaafoɔ no nyɛ eyie." Asaase a ɛwɔ nwini tenten no mu na Aturukuku tena hɔ, na Akoko no nso tena fie.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, obiara betumi agye bewee sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na naayɛn nso betumi ayɛ me ayɛyie (ase sɛ mekae).
