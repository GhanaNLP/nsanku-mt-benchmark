Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea Aturukuku, anomaa no, kɔɔ kwaeɛ mu, na ɔmaa Akoko, akoko baa no, baa fie no.

AKOKO, Akoko-aba, ne Aturukuku, Anomaa, firii agya baako ne na baako. Ɛnnɛ, Akoko-aba kaa kyerɛ Aturukuku sɛ, “Kɔ Dwumfuo-a-ɛyɛ-tenten no mu, na me nso mɛkɔ Kwadie-kɛse no mu, na sɛ wuhu aduane a, ɛnna wobɛba aba m'asɛm.” Ɛnna wɔyɛɛ wɔn ho mmuae. Mfeɛ mmiɛnsa Aturukuku anhunu ne nuaba. Ɔkaa kyerɛ ne kunu sɛ, “Nante me nkyɛn, efisɛ merekɔ rehwehwɛ me nuaba.” Ɛnna ne kunu no kaa sɛ, “Sɛ woaka a, meate, kɔ, na me nante wo nkyɛn.” Ɛnna wɔnyinaa hyɛɛ aseɛ na wɔduu kurom bi mu (baabi a ne nuaba no tena). Wɔhunuu (mud) mmoa a ɛyɛ conical bi wɔ hɔ, na ɛhɔ na ɔne ne nuaba no hyiaa. Na Akoko no bisaa Ɔkɔkɔkɔ-anoma no sɛ, “Berɛ a wukɔɔ Atete-kɔkɔ-man no, wanya aduane tɛn?” Ɔgyee ase sɛ, “Baabi a mekɔeɛ no, guinea-grain na ɛwɔ hɔ; na berɛ a mepuee me mma, bebree anaa ensia, na wɔfa me ho, na berɛ a wɔyɛɛ kɛse te sɛ wei (ɔkyerɛɛ wɔn kɛse), mehyɛɛ wɔn mu den, na ɔyabiadeyɛfoɔ bi yɛɛ ne gun no, pom! na wɔwoo me mma, na sɛ me nni nyansa a, na wɔawowo me nso.” Sɛ ɔwie ne kasa no, Akoko no see, “Baabi a mekɔe no, meyɛɛ obi a ɔwɔ nidi kɛse ntɛm.” Aturukuku bisaa no, see, “Kyerɛ me nea wode ‘obi a ɔwɔ nidi kɛse’ kyerɛ.” Ɛnna Akoko no see, “Nea ‘obi a ɔwɔ nidi kɛse’ kyerɛ ne sɛ, mfatoho, sɛ me mpanyin trã ntadua fɛfɛɛfɛ bi, t Sɛ me kɔ da so no, na me mmoa tɔ so no, na sɛ me mpanyin fa nam gu soup-stew no mu a, ɛnna me fa nea me pɛ; aduane biara a me mpanyin bɛdi no, me kɔ fa bi, na sɛ me di saa saa no, me mpanyin ntumi nfa ne gun anaa apɔm na wakura me. “Ɛno ne nea nti na meka sɛ me yɛ obi a me ho hia paa.” Akoko no see, “Baabi a mote saa hɔ no, ɛnyɛ beaeɛ pa, enti Memeneda Adae a ɛreba no ne ne Memeneda a ɛdi kan no, fa wo mma ne wo kunu ba, na wɔnnyina hɔ ntena yɛn fie.” Aturukuku see, “Manya aso, mɛkɔ, na Memeneda a ɛdi Sunday Adae kan no, mɛba.” Ɛnnɛ, ɔkomfo bi baa Akoko no akuraa mu, ɔshak rehyia ne nkabom ketewa no. Wɔbisaa no sɛ, “Nana, dɛn na ɛrekɔ so?” Ɔkae sɛ, “Dente se woama no akɔm mfeɛ mmienu, enti ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ ɔhene hɔ na woka kyerɛ no sɛ ɔmfa akoko (ne) nneɛma duoduo mmienu (eggs) aba.” Ɛnna ɔhene kɔtoo akoko nan, faa nneɛma duoduo mmienu (eggs) bae, na ɔnoaa eto, na ɔkɔmaa Dente nyame no wɔ ne nkyɛn mud altar no ase, na ɔtwaa akoko nan no ti na ɔp... na ɔde wɔn hyɛ hɔ, na ɔfa wɔn nnuane ne wɔn ti, ne wɔn nan, na ɔde hyɛ mud altar no nkyɛn; na ɔde eto a ayɛ mmoa no hyɛ so nso. Afei, Adae no anadwo, Aturukuku kɔree ne nua no nkyɛn. Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu ne nua no ti, ne nan, ne ntaban, ne dɔteɛ, ne mmoa, wɔ Dente altar a ɛyɛ kronkron no hɔ, a ɔyɛ ne nua no agyafoɔ. Aturukuku see sɛ, “Nea nyinaa a ɛasii yi a, mentumi mma mma hɔ bio.” Na Turtle-dove tuu ne nsa kɔtoo silk-cotton tree bi so, na ɔsee (too dwom) sɛ: "Mesu ma me nuaba o, Awuayɛ o."

Wo, Akoko, a woyɛ obi a wuwɔ animdeɛ, eyi ne wo yafunu?

Na Aturukuku see sɛ, "Me renkɔ kwaafoɔ kuro mu bio, efisɛ kwaafoɔ no nyɛ eyie." Asaase a ɛwɔ nwini tenten no mu na Aturukuku tena hɔ, na Akoko no nso tena fie.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, obiara betumi agye baabi fofor sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na naayɛn nso betumi akye me ayɛyie (ase sɛ mekae).
