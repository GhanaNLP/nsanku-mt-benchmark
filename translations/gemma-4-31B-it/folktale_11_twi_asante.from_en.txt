Sɛnea Kwatima, ɔhwene no, nyaa Odwanini din no

Na obiara nim sɛ, na ɔyɛ ɔkraman bi a yɛfrɛ no Kwatima, na ɔse, "Merekɔ rekɔhwehwɛ asaase a mɛsi fie so." Na ɔgyata no nso, wɔ saa berɛ no ara, na ne nsusuw nsusuw no yɛ pɛ. Ɛnna Kwatima, ɔkraman no, hyɛɛ aseɛ rekɔhwehwɛ asaase a ɔpɛ no; na ɔpɛpɛɛ no, na ama wakɔsi fie so. Na Osebo, ɔgyata no, nso kɔe (na when ɔduu baabi a ɔkraman no apɛpɛ no), ɔse, "Me Yehowa! Kuraa a mɛsi no, nanso menni adwuma biara wɔ hɔ, me nananom nnipa a wɔwui no ayɛ aseɛ wɔ hɔ." Na ɔno nso, ɔpɛpɛɛ bi na ɔkɔe. Da a ɛdi so, berɛ a anɔpa kɔree, ɔkraman no kɔe bio. Berɛ a ɔduu hɔ no, ɔse, "Me Yehowa! Nokwarene, me nananom nnipa a wɔwui no bɛboa me, hwɛ asaase a wɔapɛpɛ no." Na ɔno nso, ɔpɔpɔɔ (nwura no) na ɔkɔe. Na ɔgyata no nso bae. Ɔse, "Oh! asɛm yi yɛ kɛse; m'abofrɛ me." Na ɔno nso kɔɔ kwae; ɔtwaa nnuane na ɔde guu hɔ (baabi a wɔresi fie no). Na ɔkraman no nso bae, na ɔhwee no mmae mmae wɔ nwonwa mu. Ɔno nso kɔɔ kwae, kokɔɔ nnuane a ɛyɛ nkoda, na ɔde guu hɔ na ɔkɔe. Na ɔgyata no bae bio. Ɔno nso hwee no mmae mmae (deɛ wɔayɛ no), na ɔtwee fie no mfonini na ɔkɔe. Bio Kwatima bae, na ɔtwee awu na ɔde nnuane no guu mu, na ɔkɔe. Na ɔgyata no bae bio, na ɔtrɔɔ tɛntɛn na ɔyɛɛ nkyekyɛmu; na ɔkɔe. Na bio Kwatima bae na ɔyɛɛ abofra, na ɔkɔe. Na bio ɔgyata no bae, na ɔbɔɔ fam no, na ɔkɔe. Ɛnna Kwatima nso bae, na ɔhwehwɛɛ tɛntɛn fitaa na ɔde yɛɛ (ban no), na ɔkɔɔ fie. Berɛ a ɔkɔe no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ba sɛ, "Ɛnnɛ madi fie no awie, na sɛ Kwasieda Adae ba a, Dwoada a ɛdi so no yɛbɛyɛ fie foforo no mpaebɔ, enti hwehwɛ nnipa a wɔwɔ gunyee na wɔnkɔ sɔre mu." Na ɔgyata no nso bae (na ɔhunuu fie a wɔawie no). Ɔse, "Eh! "Hwee nni hɔ a mɛka, na mɛkɔfa me nnoɔma na maba." Ɛno nti ɔkɔe, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ ne ba sɛ, "Tu mmirika pɛpɛɛpɛ kɔka kyerɛ Sunsum-hwehwɛfoɔ no sɛ, sɛ Adae tɔ Kwasieda a, Dwoada a ɛdi no akyi no, mɛyɛ me fie foforo no nnwuma, enti ma wakɔyɛ mpaebɔ mma me." Na ne ba no kɔkae kyerɛɛ no. Ɔno (Sunsum-hwehwɛfoɔ no) see, "Manya." Ɛna Adae ansa nnawɔtwe no, Kwatima boaa ne nnoɔma ano, na ɔfaa wɔn kɔtoo hɔ fie hɔ, na ɔbaaa. Na Gyata no nso boaa ne nnoɔma ano, na ɔfaa wɔn (kɔhɔ). Berɛ a anigyeɛ buee kɛse, Kwasieda Adae no, Kwatima kɔhyiaa nnipa a ɔtena ne nkyɛn nyinaa kyerɛɛ wɔn nka; na Gyata no nso yɛɛ ne nka. Dwoada, berɛ a anigyeɛ buee (anɔpa), Kwatima ne ne ba kɔe (fie foforo no); na wɔkɔyɛɛ gun no mu wɔ dan no mu. Na Gyata no nso bae; ɔtee gun no; ɔsee, "Ɛnnɛ nyinaa nyɛ da pa, ɛnnɛ nnipa a wɔwui no na wɔyɛ gun." Na ɔno nso bae; na Sunsum-hwehwɛfoɔ no nso kɔe fie hɔ, na ɔmaae gun no tee, pomm! Gyata no bae na ɔhunuu Kwatima ne ne ba a wɔtenn hɔ. Ɔsee, "Dɛn na ɛde wo baa ha?" Kwatima nso see, "Wo nso, dɛn na ɛde wo baa ha?" Gyata no see, "Sɛ wo nni asɛm bi (a ɛde wo baa ha) a, me nso mmani." Na Kwatima see, "Sɛ saa na ɛte, a yɛnyɛn nyinaa bɛtena ha." Enti wɔtenae hɔ. Da biara, berɛ a Gyata no sɔree, ɔkɔɔ abɔdeɛ mu kɔhwehwɛɛ nam, na ɔde bae. Ɛte saa da biara. Na Kwatima nso, ɔde hwee mbae. Enti da bi, ɔno a ɔyɛ ɔkwatɛ no kɔe kwaa; ɔyɛɛ ne bo nyinaa sɛ ɔbɛkyere hwee, nanso ɔanyɛ bi. Ɔbaae fie, na ɔsee, "Kwatima, sɛ ɛnnɛ woankɔ kwaa na woande nam bi mba a, nea mɛyɛ wo no, wobɛpɛ." Kwatima kɔe, na ɔkɔe kwaa, na ɔde kɔkɔɔ bi bae na ɔtoo no hɔ. Berɛ a Gyata no hunuu bɔɔdeɛ no, ɛyɛɛ no nwonwa kɛse. Berɛ a anigyeɛ buee (anɔpa), Kwatima kɔe, na ɔde ebi foforo (antelope) bae bio. Ɔpunaa no see, "E!" Ɔsee ne ba no sɛ, "Hwɛ ha, merekɔ kwae, na mefa Kwatima nso rekɔ, ne mmoa ketewa a mefa baa wo nkyɛn ɛno nso. Sɛ yɛakɔ a, frɛ Kwatima ba no na bisa no sɛ, 'Sɛ wo papa kɔ kwae kɔkyere nam a, kyerɛ yɛn wɔ saa mmoa ketewa yi so sɛ ɔyɛ saa adeɛ no sɛn.'" Na nnipa no (Osebo ne Kwatima) nan kɔɔ. Na Ɔpunaa ba no see, "Kwatima ba, bra na yɛnnom." Ɔbaa hɔ; ɔsee, "Sɛ me papa kɔ kwae a, saa na ɔkyere mmoa." Na ɔtwene, ɔkyere mmoa ketewa no na ɔkyere ne kɔn. Kwatima ba no see, "Kɔfa aboɔ kɛse bi bra." Ɔpunaa ba no kɔfaa baa. Ɔtoo no hɔ. Kwatima ba no bɔɔ ne ho afa ho na ɔbaa hɔ na ɔbɔɔ aboɔ no ne ti ano, pim! na ɛpɛpɛɛ. Ɔpunaa ba no see, "Ah." Na wɔn agya no firi kwae baa, na Ɔpunaa ba no ka kyerɛɛ ne papa nea asie, na Kwatima ba no nso ka kyerɛɛ ne papa nea asie. Na wɔwɔ hɔ, berɛ a da koro bi mmoa bi hyɛɛ Kwatima nan mu, na ɔrepɛ sɛ ɔtwerɛ no firi hɔ, nanso Ɔpunaa no asusuw sɛ ɔrebɔ ne ho afa ho na wakyllere no. Mpofota ara na Ɔpunaa no tuu mmirika kɔ Kwatima hɔ na wakyeere no. Na Kwatima tuu mmirika kiri, kiri, kiri. Ɔkɔɔ asuom bi a ɛwɔ hɔ, a ɔwɔn no mu yɛ ɔwɔn kɛse. Kwatima see, "Papa ɔwɔn, metu mmirika ba wo nkyɛn sɛ wobetumi aboa me na Ɔpunaa no ankyeere me." Ɛhɔ ara na ɔwɔn no see, "Wo, ɔyɛyɛ, woayɛ adeɛ a ɛyɛ (sɛ woaba me nkyɛn), enti fa me nsa." Kwatima yɛɛ no den na ɔne no nsa hyɛɛ. Saa na Kwatima nyaa edin Odwa-nini (ne ara ase: Hyɛ nsa ne ɔwɔn). Ɛfiri ne nsa hyɛ ne ɔwɔn no mu.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, obiara betumi agye bewee sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na naayɛn nso betumi ayɛ me ayɛyie (ase sɛ mekae).
