Sɛnea Kwatima, ɔhwene no, nyaa Odwanini din no

Na obiara nim Ram bi a ne din de Kwatima, na ɔse, "Merekɔ rehwehwɛ asaase a mɛsi fie so." Na Leopard nso, wɔ saa berɛ no ara, na ne nsusuw nsusuw ne nea Kwatima asusuw no ara. Ɛnna Kwatima, Ram no, hyɛɛ aseɛ kɔhwee asaase a ɔpɛ no; na ɔpɛɛ no, na ama wakɔsi fie. Na Osebo, Leopard no, kɔɔ n’akwan so nso (na when ɔduu baabi a Ram no apue), ɔse, "Me nyame! Kuraa a mepɛ sɛ meyi ma meto kuro, nanso menni adwuma biara wɔ hɔ, me nananom nnipa a wɔawu no adi kan ayi no." Na ɔno nso, ɔyi baabi ketewa na ɔkɔɔ. Da a ɛdi so, berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ asei sɛ wɔremi (aei, anɔpa), Ram no kɔɔ bio. Berɛ a ɔduu hɔ no, ɔse, "Me nyame! Nokwarene, me nananom adwofo bɛboa me, (hwɛ) fam a wɔatwa no." Na ɔno nso, ɔboaa (atadeɛ no) na ɔkɔɔ. Na Gyata nso, ɔbaa. Ɔsee, "O! wei yɛ asɛm bi; m'ani agyina ho koraa." Na ɔno nso, ɔkɔɔ kwae; ɔtwaa nnuaba na ɔde guu hɔ (baabi a wɔreka ho asɛm no). Na Gyaam nso baa, na ɔno nso hwɛɛ no yiye wɔ nwonwaso mu. Ɔkɔɔ kwaa mu bio, kɔtoo nnam, na ɔtoo hɔ na ɔkɔɔ. Na Leopard no baa bio. Ɔno nso hwɛɛ (deɛ wɔayɛ no) yiye, na ɔtwee (dan no) mmaa na ɔkɔɔ. Kwatima baa bio, na ɔtwee awu na ɔtoo nnuane mu, na ɔkɔɔ. Na Leopard no baa bio, na ɔkyekyere nnuane no, na ɔkɔɔ. Kwatima baa bio, na ɔkɔfaa nkyene no guu hɔ na ɔkɔɔ. Na Leopard baa bio, na ɔtrɔa tɛkrɛma no na ɔyɛɛ n’atere; na ɔkɔɔ. Kwatima baa bio na ɔyɛɛ abofra no, na ɔkɔɔ. Leopard baa bio, na ɔbɔɔ fam no, na ɔkɔɔ. Ɛna Kwatima baa nso, na ɔfaa tɛkrɛma fitaa na ɔtɛree (ban no), na ɔsan kɔɔ fie. Bere a ɔkɔe no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ba sɛ, "Ɛnnɛ meawie dan no, na sɛ Kwasieda Adae ba a, Dwoada a ɛdi so no yɛbɛyɛ dan foforo no mpaebɔ, enti hwehwɛ nnipa a wɔwɔ gunyere na wɔnkɔ sɔre mu." Ɛnnɛ Leopard no nso bae (na ɔhunuu dan a wɔawie no). Ɔse, "Eh! "Hwee nni hɔ a mɛka, na mɛkɔfa me nnoɔma na mɛaba." Ɛno nti ɔkɔeɛ, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ ne ba sɛ, "Tu mmirika pɛpɛɛpɛ kɔka kyerɛ Ghost-hunter sɛ, sɛ Adae tɔ Kwasieda a, Monday a ɛdi so no, mɛyɛ me fie foforo no nnwuma, enti ma wakɔ na wakɔ sradeɛ ma me." Na ne ba no kɔkaeɛ kyerɛɛ no. Ɔse (Ɔsuman-hwehwɛfo no), "Manya asɛm no." Ɛna Adae ansa nnawɔtwe no, Kwatima boaa ne nnoɔma ano, na ɔfaa hɔ kɔtoo hɔ wɔ efie no mu, na ɔsan baa. Na Gyata no nso, ɔboaa ne nnoɔma ano, na ɔfaa hɔ (kɔtoo hɔ). Berɛ a ɛyɛɛ kanea sɛ obi bɛhu nneɛma, Adae Kwasieda no, Kwatima kɔɔ hɔa hɔ kɔgyee nkurɔfo a ɔtena ne nkyɛn no akyire; na Gyata no nso yɛɛ ne nkyɛmu. Dwoada, berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ aseɛ (yeɛ, anɔpa anɔpa), Kwatima ne ne ba kɔɔ (dan foforo no mu); na ɔkɔyɛɛ gun sɔre wɔ dan no mu. Na Aberewa no nso bae; ɔtee gun no; ɔsee, "Ɛnnɛ nyɛ da pa, ɛnnɛ nnipa a wɔawu no na wɔde gun rekyere." Na ɔno nso bae; na Ghost-hunter no nso kɔɔ dan no mu hɔ, na ɔmaa gun no bɔɔ, pomm! Aberewa no bae na ɔhunuu Kwatima ne ne ba a wɔdennua hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn ne wo dwuma?" Kwatima nso kaa sɛ, "Wo nso, dɛn ne wo dwuma?" Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wo nni dwuma bi (a ɛde aba ha a), me nso menni bi." Na Kwatima kaa sɛ, "Sɛ saa na ɛte sɛ no a, ɛnna yɛnyɛn nyiram a yɛbɛtena ha." Enti wɔtrae wɔ hɔ. Da biara, sɛ Aberewa no sɔre a, ɔkɔ abɔdeɛ, na ɔde nam ba. Ɛte saa da biara. Nanso Kwatima nti, ɔannde hwee ba. Ɛnna da koro bi, obi a ɔyɛ ɔkwapa no kɔɔ kwae; ɔyɛɛ ne bofo nyinaa sɛ ɔbɛkyere biribi, nanso ɔanyɛ bi. Ɔsan baa fie, na ɔsee, "Kwatima, sɛ nnɛ woankɔ kwae na woankura nam baa a, nea mɛyɛ wo no, wobɛpɛ." Kwatima tɔɔ ne nan, na ɔkɔɔ kwae, na ɔkuraa kwae-gyata bae na ɔtoo no hɔ. Berɛ a Opuano huu mmoa no, ɛyɛɛ no nwonwa paa. Berɛ a nneɛma baa (yehwie, ɛne anɔpa), Kwatima kɔɔ, na ɔde ebi foforɔ (antelope) baa bio. Ɔpyece no see, "E!" Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ba sɛ, "Hwɛ ha, merekɔ kwae, na mefa Kwatima nso rekɔ, ne mmoa ketewa a mefa baa wo nkyɛn ɛno nso, sɛ yɛakɔ a, frɛ Kwatima ba no na bisa no sɛ, 'Sɛ wo papa kɔ kwae kɔkyere nam a, kyerɛ yɛn wɔ saa mmoa ketewa yi so sɛ ɔyɛ saa adeɛ no sɛn.'" Na nnipa no (Osebo ne Kwatima) nan ase kɔɔ. Na Aberewa no baa no see, "Kwatima ba, bra na yɛnnom." Ɔbaa hɔ; ɔsee, "Sɛ me papa kɔ kwaa mu a, saa Asaaeɛmɔ ne sɛnea ɔkyere animfo." Na ɔtweneɛ, na ɔkyeree duyker ketewa no na ɔkyere ne kɔn. Kwatima ba no see, "Kɔfa aboɔ kɛse bi bra." Aberewa no ba no kɔfaa baako aba. Ɔtoo no hɔ. Kwatima ba no bɔɔ ne ho afiri hɔ na ɔbaaa na ɔfa ne ti kan bɔɔ aboɔ no, pim! na ɛbɔɔ mframa. Aberewa ba no see, "Ah." Na wɔn awofoɔ no firi kwaa mu baa, na Aberewa ba no ka kyerɛɛ ne papa nea ɛsi, na Kwatima ba no nso ka kyerɛɛ ne papa nea ɛsi. Ɛnna wɔn wɔ hɔ, berɛ a da koro bi na biribi hyɛɛ Kwatima nan mu, na ɔrepɛ sɛ ɔtwe no firi hɔ, nanso Aberewa no asusuw sɛ ɔreka ne nan akɔ akyi na wakyi no. Mpofirim ara, Aberewa no tutuu Kwatima so na wakyi no. Na Kwatima tuu mmirika kiri, kiri, kiri. Ɔkɔɔ asuom a ɛda hɔ, a Awɔdennwa bi wɔ mu. Kwatima seeว่า, "Agya Python, meba wo nkyɛn sɛ wobɛgye me to gyae na Leopard anka me." Ɛhɔ ara na Python seeว่า, "Wo, ɔyere, woayɛ adeɛ a ɛyɛ (sɛ woaba me nkyɛn), enti fa me nsa." Kwatima yɛɛ no den na ɔne no kyea nsa. Ɛno nenea Kwatima nyaa edin Odwa-nini (lit. Shake hands with the Python). Ɛfi ne nsa kyeae ne Python no mu.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, obiara betumi agye baabi fofor sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na naayɛn nso betumi akye me ayɛyie (ase sɛ mekae).
