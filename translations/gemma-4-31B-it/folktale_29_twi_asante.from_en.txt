Ɔhwɛɛ ne akyi, na ɔhunuu abofra baako a ɔgyina hɔ; ɔfaa aboɔ koro maa abofra no sɛ ɔnfa nko ma ne yere. Berɛ a ne yere nyaa aboɔ no, ɔkaa kyerɛ ne maame sɛ, "Me kunu pɛ sɛ ɔsan kɔ ne kurom, efisɛ ɔse sɛ sɛ anomaa tena asaase so tedw a, aboɔ bɛtɔ no." Na Obaahemaa no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ ɔkɔ a, wo nso mfa wo ho nko ne no." Nokwarene, ɔne no kɔɔ. Na wɔhyiaa Kwaku-who-takes-trouble-to-think nnuanom. Berɛ bi akyi, wɔkaa kyerɛ no sɛ, "Kwaku-who-takes-trouble-to-think, fa nsa-nkwa bi na yɛnni kwae kɔsii kwan na yɛnkyere aberewa a yɛbɛma wo yere, a ɔyɛ ɔhɔra, aduane." Enti wɔkɔɔ, nanso mpanyimfoɔ yi agu nkɔsom sɛ wɔbɛkum no. Wɔne Kwaku-who-takes-trouble-to-think kɔɔ; wɔduu kwan bi a obiara nni hɔ; wɔkyekyere no na wɔkaa kyerɛ no sɛ, "Yɛbɛkum wo." Ɛnna ɔkaa kyerɛ wɔn sɛ, "Berɛ a mekɔɔ me yere kuro no, mehunuu sika pii, na sɛ monnyɛ me kwan ma me nkyerɛ mo baabi a ɛwɔ na mewui a, monnwonu." Wɔbisaa no sɛ, "Sika no wɔ hene?" Ɔgyee mmuae sɛ, "Mo mmo kɔ ma me yere na monka kyerɛ no sɛ m'atɔ nam, na (adidifoɔ no) se ne sika yɛ peredwan (£80) duaa, na ɔmmu mu wɔ kɔtoku a wɔyɛe fii ɔnuaba skin mu; sɛ ɔhwɛ hɔ na ɔnwonu a, ɔmmu mu wɔ Krampan kɔtoku no mu, na sɛ ɔnwonu hɔ nso a, ɔmmu mu wɔ mmanmu a ntokwa wɔ mu no." Enti wɔbaa na wɔmaa ne yere amanneɛ no. Na ɔno nso kaa sɛ, "Nneɛma no wɔ ɔkuro mpanyin no fie, enti ma yɛnkɔ fa." Ɔne wɔn kɔɔ ɔkuro mpanyin no fie; na ɔbaa no kaa amanneɛ no kyerɛ ɔkuro mpanyin no, ɔkyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɔnuaba skin kɔtoku no kyerɛ sɛ wɔakyekyere no; Krampan no kyerɛ sɛ akɔkɔduo repɛ sɛ wɔdi ne nam; mmanmu a ntokwa wɔ mu no kyerɛ sɛ wɔrepɛ sɛ wɔtwa ne kɔn." Ɛhweaseɛ no, ɔkuro mpanyin no bɔɔ gong-gong, na wɔnkɔ hɔ akɔgye Kwaku-who-takes-trouble-to-think. Na wɔsan baa ne ho. Ɔmaa wɔkum mpanyimfoɔ nnan no. Ɛno nti na yɛse, “Sika-nkyerɛaeɛ mu nkyerɛaseɛ mu no, obi gyaeɛ yere na ɔgyee ne nkwa nso.” Me nsɛm a meka a, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaasɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi, na ma bi nso mma me.
