Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea obi hyɛɛ yaba (na ɔgyee n'nkwa nso) wɔ nsɛm a ɛwɔ nsɛm-kyerɛwee mu nkyerɛase mu

Na ɛna ɛyɛ mmarima baasa a wɔpɛ abaaden. Wɔkɔɔ Atwuo-kɛse-man no mu kɔtɔɔ nkyekye ntoma ne nneɛma afoforo. (Berɛ a wɔsan baa Ashanti) wɔmaa wɔn nnuaba bi kyere nneɛma no, na wɔkɔɔ anim kɔtɔnoo wɔn. Wɔduu kurom kɛse bi mu. Ɛhɔ na Ɔhemaa baa bi teyɛɛ, na ɔka kyerɛɛ obiara a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔhyira no kɔ ayawuo sɛ ɔmpɛ no. Saa ɔhɔfoɔ (adwenefoɔ) no kɔɔ hɔ (ne fie), nanso Nanaa ba no akɔ "bɔ" ntadeɛ. Nanaa (nanso) tɔɔ ntoma mmienu na ɔde hyɛɛ aside ma ne baa. Berɛ a ne ba no san baa no, ɔhunuu mmarima nnanom no. Ɔkaa kyerɛ ne ɔbaa panin no sɛ, "Ɛnnɛ meahu obi a mepɛ no, enti ma yɛma onuasoma bi nkwa na matumi ayɛ ayɛyɛ ama wɔn." Sɛ wɔwui no a, abofra no kɔfaa ɛponu, kɔtɔn, kola nut, ne nketia, na ɔtoo so wɔ aduane no so kɔhyɛ mmarima no anim. Na ɔse, "Obi a obetumi ayɛ wɔn akyerɛyɛ no paa, ɔno na mɛware." Na wɔkɔɔ ne nnuane no ne wɔn. Mmarima no buee mu, na wɔse, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ Obaahemaa baa a ɔde saa aduane yi aba a, yɛbɛka nsɛm bi ne no." Mmarima no nua ba no se, "Sɛ meyɛnne mo nso a, mɛbetumi aka saa nnoɔma yi akyerɛ mo." Wɔse, "Kyere yɛn." Abofra no hyɛɛ aseɛ se, "Abofra baa no se wadi ne nsusuw nsusuw no ayɛ fitaa te sɛ watuo, na sɛ ɔbetumi anya baako wɔn a yɛaba mu" ha, ɛnyɛ mmarima bi na ɔsɛ sɛ ɔware? Ɛgua no akyerɛ ne sɛ, da bi bɛba a ɔbɛyera; nso kola no kyerɛ sɛ, da bi bɛba a ɔbɛma no (ne kunu) nnu kola (yehwɛ, ɔbɛma no ayɛ yaw). Nnampene no kyerɛ sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ wotanim nnampene ma no, na afe biara a ɔbɛba aba mu mmoa a, ɔbɛnya bi a ɔbɛtu. Enti Ɔhemaa baa no gyaei mmarima no nua panin. Berɛ a ɔwaree no, Ɔhemaa baa no bisaa no sɛ, "Wo din de hwan?" Ɔkae sɛ, "Me din de Kwaku Susufuo," kyerɛ sɛ, Kwaku-a-ɔyɛ-den-ma-ne-adwene-yɛ-adwuma. Abofra no hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, "Me nso, yɛfrɛ me Akua-a-yɛ-den-ma-ɔsumansum." Ɔwaree no wɔ adwene fɛfɛ a ɛfata. Anɔpa a ɛbaa no, ɛna akyirefoom nnananom mmiɛnsa no kɔɔ wɔn kurom. Kwaku-a-yɛ-den-ma-ɔsumansum tenaa ne yere kurom. Ɔtenaa hɔ afe baako, na sɛ ɔsɔre (anɔpa) a, ɔne ne nnamfo tenaa hɔTweɛ nte. Da koro bi a ɔresepere saa ara saa, ɔpuee nkyɛn-nkyɛn mmienu pɛ pɛ wɔ mpoano baako mu, nanso nnipa a ɔresepere ne wɔn no see sɛ wɔrenkyɛ wɔn ama no, na wɔpɛ sɛ wɔbɔ no mpo. Ɔhwɛɛ ne akyi, na ɔhunuu abofra baako a ɔgyina hɔ; ɔfaa aboɔ baako maa abofra no sɛ ɔnyɛ no fa akɔ ma ne yere. Ɛnnɛ, berɛ a ne yere nyaa aboɔ no, ɔkaa kyerɛ ne maame sɛ, "Me kunu pɛ sɛ ɔsan kɔ ne kurom, efisɛ ɔse sɛ sɛ anomaa bi tena asaase so tenten dodo a, aboɔ bɛtɔ no." Na Awuameaa no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ ɔkɔ a, wo nso mma wo nso nka ne nsa akɔ ne yɛn." Nokwarene, wɔn adua kɔɔ. Na wɔhyiaa Kwaku-who-takes-trouble-to-think mpanyimfo. Afei, berɛ bi a ɛkyerɛ a, wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Kwaku-who-takes-trouble-to-think, kɔfa ntoma anaa ntade a yɛbɛtumi akɔ kwaa mu akɔsii sɛ yɛde bɛkyere akokoɔ a yɛbɛtumi ayɛ wo yere, a ɔyɛ ɔhɔho, adidi." Enti wɔkɔɔ, nanso saa mpanyimfoɔ yi na wɔasopɔ sɛ wɔbɛkum no. Wɔn ne Kwaku-who-takes-trouble-to-think kɔɔ; wɔduu (beaeɛ a ɛyɛ kɔkɔɔ) bi wɔ kwan no so; wɔkyekyere no tie na wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Yɛbɛkum wo.” Ɛnna ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, “Berɛ a mekɔɔ me yere kuro no, mehunuu sika pii, na sɛ monnyɛ me kwan ma me nyyɛ mo a ɛwɔ hɔ a, na mewu, mobɛhunu biara.” Wɔbisaa no sɛ, “Sika no hyɛ hene?” Ɔgyee nsɛkyerɛmu sɛ, “Mo mpo mpo” kɔ me yere hɔ kɔka kyerɛ no sɛ m’atɔ nam, na (adidifo no) se ne bo ne peredwan eduu’n (£80), na ɔnsusuw bag a yɛayɛ fi ononsom aduane mu; sɛ ɔhwɛ hɔ na ɔnhu no a, ɔnhwɛ Krampan bag no mu, sɛ ɔnhu no hɔ nso a, ɔnhwɛ belt a nsetee wɔ mu no mu.” Enti wɔbaa na wɔde asɛm no kɔkaa kyerɛɛ ne yere. Na ɔno nso kaa sɛ, “Nsɛm no wɔ ɔmanhene fie, enti ma yɛnkɔ fa hɔ.” Ɔne wɔn kɔɔ ɔmanhene fie; na ɔbaa no ka amanneɛ no kyerɛɛ ɔmanhene no, ɔkyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Sika kɔtoku a yɛayɛ mframa fitaa mu no kyerɛ sɛ wɔatwa no; Krampan no kyerɛ sɛ akɔkɔsro repere ne nam; na katan a nknam wɔ mu no kyerɛ sɛ wɔrepɛ sɛ wɔtwa ne kɔn.” Ɛhwee koraa na ɔmanhene no bɔɔ gong-gong, sɛ wɔnnyɛ kwan kɔ hɔ akɔgye Kwaku-who-takes-trouble-to-think. Na wɔsan baa ne nso. Ɔmaa wɔnyɛ mpanyimfo mmiɛnsa no mogyaa. Ɛno nti na yɛse, “Sika-nkyerɛaeɛ mu nkyerɛaseɛ mu no, obi gyaeɛ yere na ɔgyee ne nkwa nso.” Me nsɛm a meka a, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaasɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi, na ma bi nso mma me.
