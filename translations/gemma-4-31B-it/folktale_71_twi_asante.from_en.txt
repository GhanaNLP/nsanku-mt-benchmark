Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

WO YE NWONWA TE SƐ ANANSE, KƆKƆRƆ NO

ANANSE, Kwebla, tebea hɔ beao a, Nsia, Onyame Maame, yadeɛ hyɛɛ no. Wɔkɔkaa aduane-foɔ (oduruyɛfoɔ) ma ɔbaa ne nkyɛn. Berɛ a ɔbaa no, ɔhwehwɛɛ sika mfutuma pereduan (e. i. £8) sɛ deposit ma ne yadeɛ no ho dwumadie. Ɛnnɛ, Ananse kɔkaee Onyame, na ɔse no, "Wei yefo yi refa wo sikafoɔ no fɛɛfɛɛfɛ; onntumi nhwɛ no yie, enti ma me nhwɛ no ama wo, na sɛ me nhwɛ no yie a, me ti nni hɔ." Ananse faa sikafoɔ asuanu baako (ye obi). £4), na ɔkaa kyerɛ Onyame, "Sɛ me ntumi nsa saa yadeɛ yi a, mɛma wo gyata a ɛte ase." Anɔpa a ɛba no, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ, Nyankonpon, Onyame no maame, Nsia, akɔ. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Ananse, dɛn na ɛsi?" Ananse gyaw hɔ kɔe; ɔadidi. Seesei, kasa ne ne nkaeɛ (yehwɛ, me werɛ mfiri sɛ mɛka sɛ) Ananse adamfo kɛseɛ no ne Okusie, Okraman no. Ɔkae nea asie no kyerɛ Okura no, na ɔse, "Eyi ne nea mehunuu, enti seesei wo mpo mmoa me." Okura no abusaa se, "Sɛn na mbetumi mmoa wo?" (Ananse) abusaa se, "Mepɛ sɛ kokɔ mpo kɔtwa kwan kɔsi sɛ ɛduru Nyame a ɔte wɔ mmoa a ɛso no ase." Okura no se, "Mebɛyɛ ama wo." Enti ɔyɛɛ no. Okraman baa hɔ kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, "Mewie." Ananse kɔfrɛɛ ne ba, Ntikuma; na ɔtwaa mpaebɔ kakuo ma no; na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔnkɔ nkyɛn no mu na ɔnten nkyɛn a wɔama ɛsi no ase, na sɛ ɔhunu sɛ Onyame atena nkyɛn a wɔama ɛsi no so no, ɛnna ɔnka ne mpaebɔ no na ɔnka sɛ:

Sɛ wowi Ananse a, abusua no bɛsɛe!

Sɛ wo kyɛ Ananse kwan a, abusua no bɛyɛ dede wɔ nne mu!

Ntikuma kɔɔtena nkabombeae no ase, na Ananse tɔne ne kwan, ɔse ɔrekɔ Nyankonpon, Wɔsoratumi no hɔ wɔ ne pɛ. Berɛ a ɔkɔe no, na Wɔsoratumi no atirigyefoɔ kuraa no; wɔde no kɔɔ Nyankonpon anim. Ɛna ɔkasamfo no kaa kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Wo, Kwaku Ananse, wo kaa kyerɛɛ Nyankonpon, Wɔman Nyame no sɛ, sɛ ne maame, Nsia, wui a, ɔbɛtumi ayɛ wo yum; enti ɛfirisɛ Nsia akɔ, ɛno nti yɛbɛyɛ wo yum." Ananse kaa sɛ, "Mo mma me mka me asɛm." Nanso amanehunufo no kuraa no sɛ wɔbɛkɔ akɔyɛ no yum. Ntikuma yɛɛ ne nne den: "Sɛ wogyina Ananse a, amansan bɛsɛe!"

Sɛ wo kyɛ Ananse kwan a, abusua no bɛyɛ dede wɔ nne mu!

Wɔkaa sɛ, "Tie, tie!" Na mpaebɔ no bɔɔ bio:

Sɛ wowi Ananse a, abusua no bɛsɛe!

Sɛ wo kyɛ Ananse kwan a, abusua no bɛyɛ dede wɔ nne mu!

Ɛna Kontirehene soduree na ɔkaa kyerɛ Onyame sɛ, "Wɔn kuw yi yɛ wo de, Onyame, na ɛyɛ Ya, Asaase Yaa de nso. Ɛnnɛ woanya Ananse na wopɛ sɛ wogyaw no. Ya, Asaase Yaa, se 'ma no nnyɛ', enti sɛ woma no kɔ a, ɛbɛyɛ yie." Ɛna Onyame maa wɔn a wɔde no baa no gyee no. Ɛno nti na wɔka sɛ, "Wo yɛ nwanwa te sɛ Ananse."

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
