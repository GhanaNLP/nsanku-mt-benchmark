SƐNEA ASƆRE BAEEI ABUSUA NO MU

Na obi bi wɔ hɔ a yɛfrɛ no Hate-to-be-contradicted, na ɛno nti, ɔkɔtwee kuro ketewa bi (ne ho ankasa) na ɔkɔtena mu. Na bɔɔtwe a yɛfrɛ no Duyker kɔbɔɔ no nkɔmmɔ, na ɔne no kɔtee kɔtenaa abrobɔdua bi ase. Na abrobɔdua nnuaba bi tɔe. Duyker no see, "Agya Hate-to-be-contradicted, wo abrobɔdua nnuaba no ayɛ fɛ." Hate-to-be-contradicted see, "Ɛno ne abrobɔdua nnuaba no suade; sɛ wɔyɛ fɛ a, nnuaba mmiɛnsa yɛ fɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ; sɛ wɔyɛ fɛ a, me twa wɔn, na sɛ mehu wɔn ma mmoa nnuaba no nneɛma a, ɛma nsuo-kuku mmiɛnsa a ɛyɛ nnuaba nnuaba; na mefa nnuaba no kɔ Akase kɔtɔ Akase ɔbaa panyin bi; Akase ɔbaa panyin no ba na ɔwo me nananom, a ɔno nso wo me maame, a ɔno nso wo me; sɛ me maame wo me a, m'ani agyigyei na m'ani agyigyei." Duyker no see, "Sɛn na wose saa, woretwa nkontompo." Na Hate-to-be-contradicted fa dua kɔbɔɔ Duyker no ti, na ɔhyɛɛ no anim. Akyi no, Abedee kraman ketewa no baa. Na ɔne no kɔtee kɔtenaa abrobɔdua no ase, na eyie ara kɔɔ so. Na saa ara na ɛyɛ anim anim mmoa nyinaa. Afei, Kwaku Ananse, Kɔkɔɔro no, kɔfaa ne ntoma, na ɔfaa ne kotokuo, tɔɔ no ne mmanmu, na ɔkɔree Hate-to-be-contradicted kuro mu. Ɔbɔɔ no akye, "Agya, maakye." Hate-to-be-contradicted hyɛɛ no ase, "Y'Aku, na wopae kɔ he?" Ɔhyɛɛ no ase, "Meresi wo nkyɛn." Na ɔfaa ne asa tee kɔtenaa abrobɔdua no ase. Hate-to-be-contradicted see, "Noa aduane ma Kɔkɔɔro no na ɔnni." Na berɛ a wɔreno aduane no, Ananse ne Hate-to-be-contradicted tenaa abrobɔdua no ase. Na abrobɔdua nnuaba bi tɔe. Na Ananse faa wɔn hyɛɛ ne kotokuo mu. Na ɔkɔɔ so yɛɛ saa kosi sɛ ne kotokuo no yɛɛ dodo. Wɔfaa aduane no baa, na Ananse nii. Berɛ a ɔwie nii no, abrobɔdua nnuaba bi tɔe, na Ananse see, "Agya Hate-to-be-contradicted, wo abrobɔdua nnuaba no ayɛ fɛ." Hate-to-be-contradicted see, "Ɛno ne wɔn suade sɛ wɔyɛ fɛ saa; sɛ wɔyɛ fɛ a, nnuaba mmiɛnsa yɛ fɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ; sɛ wɔyɛ fɛ a, me twa wɔn, na sɛ mehu wɔn ma mmoa nnuaba no nneɛma a, ɛma nsuo-kuku mmiɛnsa a ɛyɛ nnuaba nnuaba, na mefa nnuaba no kɔ Akase, kɔtɔ Akase ɔbaa panyin bi; Akase ɔbaa panyin no ba na ɔwo me nananom, a ɔno wo me maame, na ɔno nso wo me. "Sɛ me maame wone me a, madi gyina hɔ." woyɛ onipa a wohunu sɛ obi bɔ wo asɛm, nanso woara woabɔ obi asɛm, ɛno nti na meka sɛ wɔmma wɔmbɔ wo ma wowo.” Enti wɔbɔɔ Hate-to-be-contradicted no ma ɔwoo.
