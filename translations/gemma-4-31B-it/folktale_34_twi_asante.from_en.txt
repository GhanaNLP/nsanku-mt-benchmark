Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea ɛyɛi sɛ onipa baako mpue baabi a onipa foforo firi

Na abɔdeɛ bi wɔ hɔ. Sɛ ɔsoro a, ɔkɔ kwaa mu kɔhwehwɛ animfo a ɔbɛyere, na ama ɔanya bi na wakɔdi, na ɔanya bi na wakɔtɔn. Da bi, ɔkɔɔ kwaa mu; ɔtee Kokotee, Kwaa-krokye no, a ɔrefrɛ ne nnamfo, ɔse, "Kokotee Asamoa e!" Ɔno nso gyee me, ɔse, "Aane, nua, aane." Ɔno (krokye a edi kan no) see, "Adwuma (yɛn afuo so) berɛ adu, enti ma yɛnkɔ mpanyimfo no hɔ, ama wakɔyɛ dadeɛ na ɔtwa (yɛn nneɛma a yɛfa twene), na sɛ yɛbɛtwa dua bi a, yɛbɛtumi atwa wɔn." Berɛ a Kwaa-krokye no frɛɛ ne nnuanom no, abɔdeɛ no hwee hwee hɔ hwee, na ɔtee asɛm no nyinaa. Afei, (kan) Kwaa-krokye no nua no bisaa ne nua Kwaa-krokye no sɛ, "Na kuro bɛn na yɛbɛkɔ, na yɛatwa dadeɛ no?" Ɔgyee me sɛ, "Yɛbɛkɔ kuro a wɔfrɛ no Across-the-stream." Na ɔbisaa no sɛ, "Da bɛn?" Ɔgyee me sɛ, "Dwoada." Abɔdeɛ no tee nneɛma a wɔasiesie yi nyinaa, na ɔsan kɔɔ fie. Sɛ ɔduu fie no, ɔkae Across-the-stream Odikro no asɛm no, kyerɛ sɛ, nea ɔtee berɛ a ɔkɔɔ kwaa mu no. Na abɔdeɛ no see kuro no Odikro no sɛ, "Ma mmofra no nkɔntwa dua mmaa, na berɛ a, Dwoada no, Kwaa-krokye no bɛsesa wɔn ho (ayɛ nnipa) na wɔaba a, yɛbɛfa wɔn na yɛde bɛkyekyere wɔn wɔ dua no ho." Mmofra no kɔtwaa dua no na wɔde baa. Kuro-panyin no kɔkae kuro no mpanyimfo no sɛ ɔnyɛ dadeɛ kyetew mmaa no. Na mpanyimfo no bisaa Odikro no sɛ, "Na dadeɛ kyetew pii a wose me mma ayɛ yi, dɛn na wobɛyɛ wɔn?" Na ɔkae asɛm no ama no, sɛ abɔdeɛ bi kɔɔ kwaa mu, na ɔsan baa kyerɛɛ sɛ Dwoada no, mmoa bi bɛsesa wɔn ho ayɛ nnipa, na ama wɔankɔ ne hɔ akɔtwa nneɛma. Mpanyimfo no tuu mmirika kɔyɛɛ dadeɛ kyetew no ntɛm. Ɔhene no tɔn n’ansa mma n’ansa ne n’atadeɛ no wie no, ɔma ɔkasafo no bɔɔ kyerɛma no, ɔse, “Dwoada, ɛnna ɔyɛ ɔbaa anaa ɔyɛ ɔbarima, obiara nnkɔ kwan biara.” Dwoada bae, na anɔpa no ɔbaa panyin bi ka kyerɛ ɔyadeɛfoɔ no se, “Kɔ na kɔtɔ mako ne nnuane ne nnuane a ɛyɛ mako, ne baabi a mmoa no bɛpue wɔn nnipa-skin no na wɔatwa mu; wo nso, sɛ wo kɔ hɔ a, fa mako no na kɔtwa mu, na sɛ mmoa no ba ha, na yɛka wɔn, na baako bɛtumi anya kwan na wakɔfa ne skin na watow so, na sɛ mako no yɛ no yaw a, na wɔtowe no, ɛnna wɔbɛyɛ nnipa (bio).” Ɔyadeɛfoɔ no kɔe na ɔkɔnnɔ hɔ. Na ɔtee sɛ wɔfrɛ no, “Kokotee Asamoa, Dwoada aba, ma yɛnkɔ.” Enti wɔbaa nyinaa. Wɔpuee wɔn skin no, na wɔde guu hɔ. Efie, (Krokuro baako,) a ɔyɛ aduane-yawofoɔ, na ɔwɔ wɔn mu, na ɔfaa ne skin no na ɔde guu baabi foforo. Ɔyadeɛfoɔ no hwɛe wɔn, na ɔmaa wɔnkɔ. Ɔkɔfaa skin no nyinaa na ɔtwaa mako a wɔatɔ no so, na ɔfaa wɔn kɔtɔɔ asuom mu, ma nsuo no kuraa wɔn kɔe. Nanso, aduane-yawofoɔ no deɛ, a ɔde agyigyei firi afoforo hɔ, ɔyadeɛfoɔ no anhunu. Ɔyadeɛfoɔ no baa fie, na Ɔhene no nso frɛɛ nnipa a wɔahyia wɔ ɔkɔtɔfoɔ no kwan ho na ɔmaa wɔbaa ne fie. Sɛ saa nnipa yi baa Ɔhene no fie a, wɔbisae Ɔhene no se, “Adɛn nti na woafrɛ yɛn?” Ɔhene no se, “Mo yɛ me nnipa, na mo tutu kɔfaa baabi foforo; nnɛ mo aba bio, ɛno ne nea nti na meka se mo mpɛ sɛ mo ba, efisɛ mɛma mo nkɔ bio.” Mmoa no ka kyerɛ Ɔhene no se, “Nea woaka no, yɛate; nanso yɛnim sɛ Bɔfo no ɔyadeɛfoɔ bae na ɔkae wo nyinaa fa yɛn ho, nanso ɛnyɛ hwee, yɛ ne wo bɛtena bɔ; nanso yɛnim sɛ nea woka no yɛ n'asɛm a ɛnyɛ nokware; nanso, ɛnyɛ hwee; yɛda ɔyadeɛfoɔ no ase. Enti yɛ ne wo bɛtena bɔ; nanso ade baako wɔ a yɛatwa mu, ne sɛ, mo mmpue yɛn fianor; anaa wo nnipa biara mmpue yɛn fianor; sɛ saa ade no ba a, yɛbɛsɛe mo abusua yi, na yɛbɛkɔ.” Efie, wɔ berɛ a Ɔhene no kɔfrɛe wɔn no, aduane-yawofoɔ no ne ebi tutu kɔe. Na aduane-yawofoɔ no kɔfaa ne skin no na ɔtutu kɔe; nanso na akyerɛyɛ no san bae. Ɛyɛ efisɛ wɔanhunu wɔn skin no. Ohene no gyee mmoa no nkyerɛkyerɛmu no, na wɔn ne mmoa no tenaa bam. Ɛnnɛ, kuro no mmarima no gyawaeɛ mmoa no mmaa bi; na wɔwoo mma. Da koro bi, mmoa no mu baako ne kuro no nnipa mu baako redi nkonu. Ɛno nti, kuro no obi no kaa kyerɛ mmoa no sɛ, “Fi hɔ, aboa a woyɛ te sɛ wo, a woyɛ Bush-pig kuo ba.” Ansa na ɔbɛwie no, mmoa no nyinaa ani bɛyɛɛ kɔkɔɔ, na wɔkɔɔ Ohene no fie kɔka kyerɛ no sɛ, “Nea yɛka kyerɛɛ wo no aso, enti dɛn na wobɛyɛ?” Ohene no maaeɛ kɔfrɛ nnipa a wɔde bɔɔ nkonu no. Ohene no hwɛɛ asɛm no yie na ɔhyɛɛ nsɛm sɛ mmoa no mma wɔmfa asɛm no mma, efisɛ (ɔkaa sɛ) ɛyɛ bere pii na wɔbaeɛ, na bio, ɛyɛ mprempren ne mprempren a obi aka biribi kyerɛ wɔn (fa wɔn fianan ho). Nanso mmoa no kaa sɛ, “Yɛrenkyɛ mu.” Na Ohene no kaa sɛ, “Monnte, na monsu sɛ nea obi yi kaa no yɛ nkontompo, monnyɛ Bush-pigs?” Na mmoa no kaa sɛ, “O, yɛate.” Ɛno nti, mmoa no ne Across-the-water nnipa no bɔɔ nkonu. Mmoa no prasɛɛ kuro no, kosi sɛ nnipa biara nni hɔ a ɛyɛ nnipa du. Na wɔbɔɔ mmoa no mpaebɔ na wɔka kyerɛ wɔn sɛ (mmoa no) wɔn na wɔwɔ nokware. Mmoa no tiee, na wɔka kyerɛ nnipa no sɛ, “Asɛm a yɛaka no nyɛ den sɛ wobɛte ase, na sɛ wo ne yɛ bɛtena bam a, yɛasiesie sɛ obiara mma ne ho mfa yɛn fianan mmu. Sɛ wobɛsusuw anaa wobɛka ho asɛm (bio) a, ɛnna (yɛbɛsrɛ wo) sɛ kyerɛ yɛn ɛhɔ kwaadu kɛseɛ a yɛfiri baa ha, na yɛatumi afi hɔ akɔ hɔ.” Na nnipa no kaa sɛ, “Yɛrentumi anyɛ saa bio. Nea yɛayɛ no ama yɛn abusua apra; yɛrenyɛ saa bio.” Enti wɔmaaeɛ bɔɔ odawuro kyerɛ obiara sɛ obiara mma ne ho mfa obi foforo fianan mmu, na ama asɛm a ɛbɛba no amma kuro no anpra. Me nsɛm a mika yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, mo mu bi betumi agye ase sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na na akyerɛ no, mobɛtumi ayɛ me ayɛyɛ (sɛ mika asɛm yi).
