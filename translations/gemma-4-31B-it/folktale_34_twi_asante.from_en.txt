Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea ɛyɛi sɛ onipa baako mpue baabi a onipa foforo firi

Ɔkunu bi wɔ hɔ tete no. Sɛ ɔsɔre a, ɔkɔ kwae kɔhwehwɛ animfo na ɔde anya bi a ɔbɛdi, na ɔde bi nso akwata. Da koro bi, ɔkɔɔ kwae; ɔtee Kokotee, Kwae-krokofe no, a ɔrefrɛ ne nnamfo, ɔse, "Kokotee Asamoa e!" Ɔyɛɛ no mmuae se, "Aane, nua, aane." Ɔse (krokofe a edi kan no), "Adwuma (yɛn afuo so) berɛ adu, enti ma yɛnkɔ mpanyimfo no hɔ, na ɔde ayɛ yɛn nneɛma a yɛde twa adeɛ, na sɛ yɛbɛtwa dua bi a, yɛbɛtumi atwa wɔn" Berɛ a Ɔbusu-kraman frɛɛ ne nuanom no, ɔbɔdeɛfoɔ no bɔɔ ne ho mpaa hɔ hwee, na ɔtee asɛm a wɔkasaa no nyinaa. Ɛnnɛ, (ne pɛpɛpɛ) Bush-pig nua no bisaa ne nua Bush-pig no sɛ, "Na kuro bɛn na yɛbɛkɔ, na yɛatwa dadeɛ no?" Ɔgyee sika sɛ, "Yɛbɛkɔ kuro a wɔfrɛ no Across-the-stream." Na ɔbisaa no sɛ, "Da bɛn?" Ɔgyee sika sɛ, "Monday." Abɔdeɛfoɔ no tee saa nhyehyɛe nyinaa no, na ɔfaa kwan kɔɔ fie. Ɛnnɛ, berɛ a ɔbaae fie no, ɔka amanneɛ no kyerɛɛ Across-the-stream mpanyimfo no, kyerɛ sɛ, nea ɔteee ase berɛ a ɔkɔɔ kwaa no. Na abɔdeɛfoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ kuro no Odikro no sɛ, "Ma mmofra no nko na wɔtwa nnompe na wɔde aba, na Sande, sɛ mmoa-busuam no sesa wɔn ho (bɛyɛ nnipa) na wɔba a, yɛbɛfa wɔn na yɛde bɛkyekyere wɔn wɔ nnompe no mu." Mmofra no kɔtwaa nnompe no na wɔde baa. Kuro no Odikro no kɔka kyerɛɛ kuro no mpanyimfoɔ-dwafo no sɛ ɔntu dade-asika mma no. Na dwasofo no bisaa Odikro no sɛ, “Na saa nkyene pii a woka sɛ mɛnam m'anim yi, dɛn na wobɛyɛ ho?” Ɔnkae amanneɛ no kyerɛɛ no, sɛ abɔnten bi kɔree kwae, na ɔbaa fii hɔ bɛkaa sɛ Dwoada bi, mmoa bi bɛyɛ nnipa, na wɔnba ne dwaso hɔ sɛ wɔbɛma no ayɛ nneɛma a wɔde bɛyɛ adwuma. Dwafo no tuu mmirika kɔmaa ɔbɔɔ dade nkyene no ntɛm. Ɔhene no noe a owie ne nsa ttenn nnompe ne nkyene no so, ɔmaa ɔkasafo no bɔɔ kyerɛma no, ɔse, “Dwoada, ɛnna ɔbaa, anaa ɔbarima, obiara nnkɔ kwan biara.” Dwoada bae, na anɔpa no ɔbaa mpaninfoa bi ka kyerɛ ɔyadeɛfo no se, “Kɔ na kɔtɔ mako, ne nnuane, ne nnuane, ne baabi a mmoa no bɛpue wɔn nnipa; wo nso... entii, sɛ wokɔ hɔ a, fa mako no na fa tiri mu, na sɛ mmoa no ba ha, na yɛkyere hɔn, na sɛ ebii baako tutu kɔfa hɔn nnusuwa na wode hyɛ hɔn ho a, sɛ mako no yɛ hɔn yaw na wɔtu hɔn firi hɔ a, wɔbɛyɛ nnipa (bio).” Odwumfo no kɔɔ na ɔkɔnnaw hɔ. Na ɔtee hɔn a wɔfrɛ no sɛ, “Kokotee Asamoa, Monday aba, ma yɛnkɔ.” Enti wɔbaa hɔ nyinaa. Wɔpuee wɔn nnipa-skin no, na wɔde hyee hɔ. Seesei, (Abaayewa bi,) a ɔyɛ aduane-aduane dɔkota, na ɔwɔ wɔn mu, na ɔtookoo ne skin no na ɔde hyee baabi foforo. Abɔdeɛ-hwefi no hwɛɛ wɔn, na ɔmaa wɔn kɔɔ. Ɔkɔtookoo skin no nyinaa na ɔde mako a wɔatwa no rubee so, na ɔtookoo na ɔde hyee asuom mu, maa nsuo no faa wɔn kɔɔ. Nanso, adurufoɔ no deɛ, a ɔde ayɛ ketene firi afoforo no mu no, ɔyadeɛfoɔ no anhunu. Ɔyadeɛfoɔ no baa fie, na Ɔhene nso frɛɛ nnipa a wɔahyia wɔ aduane-yɛfoɔ no afie hɔ no na ɔmaa wɔbaa ne fie. Berɛ a nnipa yi baa Ɔhene fie no, wansie Ɔhene no bɛbisaa no sɛ, “Adɛn nti na wofrɛ yɛn?” Ɔhene no see sɛ, “Mo yɛɛ me nnipa, na mo tutu kɔɔ baabi efie; nnɛ mo aba bio, ɛno ne nea nti na meka sɛ mo mpɛ sɛ mo ba, efisɛ mɛma mo nkɔ bio.” Mmoa no see Ɔhene no sɛ, “Nea woaka no, yɛate; nanso yɛnim sɛ Bɔfo no hu Nter baa ha kyerɛɛ mo obiara ho asɛm nyinaa, nanso ɛnyɛ ade, yɛ ne mo bɛtena bɔ; nanso yɛnim sɛ nea mo ka no yɛ n'asɛm a ɛnyɛ nokware; akyerɛ sɛ, ɛnyɛ ade; yɛda ɔyadeɛfoɔ no ase. Ɛnna yɛ ne mo bɛtena bɔ; nanso ade baako wɔ hɔ a yɛayɛ kyɛw, ɛne sɛ mo bɛka yɛn fianan; anaasɛ mo mma bi bɛka yɛn fianan; sɛ saa eyi a, yɛbɛsɛe mo kuruwa yi na yɛakɔ. Ɛnnɛ, berɛ a Ɔhene kɔree sɛ ɔbɛfrɛ hɔn no, oduruyɛfoɔ ne ebi pii dwapae kɔe. Na oduruyefo no kɔfaa ne skin no na ɔtoo ne ho; nanso na akyerɛyɛ no san kɔɔ efie. Na ɛyɛ ɛno efisɛ wɔanhwehwɛ wɔn skin no anka. Ɔhene no gyee mmoa no a wɔde asɛm a wɔde hyɛɛ hɔ no, na wɔn ne mmoa no tenaa animuonyam mu wɔn nkaso. Afei, kuro no mmarima no gyee mmoa no mmaa bi; na wɔwoo mma. Da koro bi, mmoa no mu baako ne kuroba no mu baako na wɔreko mmarima. Ɛno akyi no, kuroba no kaa kyerɛ aboa no sɛ, “Fi hɔ, aboa a wo te sɛ wo, a woyɛ Bush-pig company ba.” Ansa na ɔbɛwie no, abofa nyinaa ani bɛyɛɛ kɔkɔɔ, na wɔkɔɔ Ɔhene fie kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Deɛ yɛkaaa kyerɛɛ wo no aso ara aba, enti dɛn na wobɛyɛ?” Ɔhene maae wɔkɔfrɛɛ nnipa no. nea na ayɛ a ɛde baabi a ɛkyerɛ nkonkonsonsom no. Ɔhene hwɛɛ asɛm no yie na ɔde nsɛm kyerɛɛ sɛ mmoa no mma wɔn nnyɛ asɛm, efisɛ (ɔse) ɛyɛ bere pii na wɔbae, na eyi nso yɛ nea edi kan a obi aka biribi kyerɛ wɔn (fa wɔn baabi a wɔfiri ho). Na mmoa no see, “Yɛnni pɛ.” Na Ɔhene no see, “Mo nte, na mo dwene sɛ nea ɔbarima yi kae no yɛ nkontompo, mo nyɛ Aberewa-kraman?” Na mmoa no see, “O, yɛate.” Ɛno nti, mmoa no ne Nsuo-ase nnipa no kɔɔ kō. Mmoa no hyɛɛ kuro no hian, kosi sɛ nnipa biara nni hɔ a ɛyɛ nnipa duu mpem ten. Na wɔbɔɔ mmoa no mpaebɔ sɛ wɔmfa aduane mma wɔn, na wɔka kyerɛɛ mmoa no sɛ mmoa no na wɔwɔ nidi. Mmoa no tiee, na wɔka kyerɛɛ nnipa no sɛ, “Asɛm a yɛaka no nyɛ den sɛ yɛbɛte ase, na sɛ wo ne me bɛtena bɔ a, yɛde yɛn amanehunu a ɛfiri yɛn mfiase no yɛ akatampon.” Sɛ wosusum anaa woka ho asɛm (bio) a, ɛnna (yɛbɛsrɛ wo) sɛ kyerɛ yɛn saa nwunu kɛseɛ a yɛfiri hɔ baa ha no, na yɛatumi afi hɔ akɔ hɔ bio.” Na nkurɔfo no kaa sɛ, “Yɛntumi mma saa asɛm yi mmu bio.” Deɛ yɛayɛ no ama yɛn abusua ayɛ yaw; yɛrenyɛ saa bio.” Enti wɔmaa wɔhyɛhyɛɛ nsɛm kyerɛɛ nnipa nyinaa, na wɔbɔɔ odawuro kyerɛɛ sɛ obiara nnka obi foforo a ɔfiri baabi, na saa asɛm no mma kuro no anhyɛ ase. Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, obiara betumi agye baabi fofor sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na naayɛn nso betumi akye me ayɛyie (sɛ mekae).
