Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea ɛyɛɛ kwan a wɔde hyɛɛ mmofra mpaapaa (mmerɛ a ɛdi kan)

Wɔka sɛ (tete bi) kɔm kɛse bɛtɔɔ hɔ, na Papa Ananse, Kɔkɔrefo no, ne ne yere Aso, ne ne mma, Ntikuma, Nyiwankonfwea, Afudotwedotwe, ne Tikonokono, na wɔyɛɛ kuro ketewa bi na wɔtena mu. Da biara Kɔkɔrefo no kɔfa aduane, pampam, baa na wɔpiraa na wɔdii. Na da bi, Papa Ananse kɔɔ kwae; ɔhunuu sɛ kuku fɛfɛ bi gyina hɔ. Ɔse, “Kuku yi fɛ.” Kuku no se, “Me din nyɛ ‘fɛ’.” Kɔkɔrefo no bisaa sɛ, “Wodin dɛn?” Ɛhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, “Me din ne ‘Fa-bi-na-di’.” Kɔkɔrefo no se, “Fa bi ma me nhunu.” Kuku no hyɛɛ nsuo-nkrakra (abrobɛ) mu, na Ananse dii no nyinaa. Sɛ ɔwiei a, ɔbisaa kuku no sɛ, “Wo akyeam dɛn?” Kuku no hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, “M’ani nnye gun-wad ne kuku ketewa.” Kɔkɔrefo no faa kuku no kɔɔ fie, na ɔkɔtoo no wɔ ɔdan no atabea. Afei Ananse kɔɔ kwae na ɔfaa aduane baa, na Aso, sɛ ɔwiee no nnuokwa a, ɔfrɛɛ Ananse, na ɔse, “O, wo na wo hia paa, m’asɛm mma, me yɛ mehou, aduane dɛn na mɛyɛ ho hwee, wo ne saa mma yi na mo hia paa; sɛ mo adi mma mo yɛ a, me sow no ayɛ komm (efisɛ mo nsuo no agyina).” Sɛ wɔwiei adidi no, a Kɔkɔrefo no faa kwan kɔɔ dan no akyi, na ɔkɔtee (tenaa) atabea a kuku no hyɛ hɔ. Ɔse, “Kuku yi fɛ.” Ɛhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, “Me din nyɛ ‘fɛ’.” Ɔse, “Wodin dɛn?” Ɛse, “Me din ne ‘Fa-bi-na-di’.” Ananse se, “Fa bi ma me nhunu.” Na ɛhyɛɛ nkontomire-nkrakra (nketia) mu, na Ananse dii. Da biara sɛ ɔsɔree a, saa na ɛkɔ. Afei, Ntikuma huu sɛ ne papa nnɛn, ɛwom sɛ wɔn ne no nnidi bom, na Ntikuma hwɛɛ ne papa na ɔhunuu nea ne papa anya. Sɛ ne papa kɔɔ kwae no, Ntikuma kɔtee atabea no so na ɔhunuu kuku no. Ɔfrɛɛ ne maame ne ne nua mma, na wɔkɔɔ atabea no so. Ntikuma se, “Kuku yi fɛ.” Ɛse, “Wɔn nfrɛ me ‘fɛ’.” Ɔse, “Enti wodin dɛn?” Ɛse, “Me din ne ‘Fa-bi-na-di’.” Ɔse, “Fa bi ketewa ma me nhunu.” Na kuku no hyɛɛ nsuo-nkrakra (abrobɛ) mu kɔsi anan. Ntikuma ne ne maame ne ne nua mma dii kɔsi sɛ ɛnyinii. Na seesei Ntikuma bisaa kukuo no sɛ, “Dɛn ne wo kyɛw?” Kukuo no kaa sɛ, “M’ani ngye gun-wad ne kukuo nketewa ho.” Ntikuma kaa kyerɛɛ Afudotwedotwe sɛ, “Kɔfa bi mma me.” Ɔno nso kɔfaa baa, na ɔtooku gun-wad no kaee (kukuo no), na ɔtooku kukuo nkete no kaee (kukuo no). Ɛnnɛm a, wɔnnyinaa kɔree ase. Na Papa Kɔkɔɔre (ɛberɛ no) afiri kwae (a ɔde amampire aba). Aso no noae. Wɔfrɛɛ Ananse; ɔgyee mmuae sɛ, “Ebia m’asɛm no nnyee wo; mekaa sɛ, efie baabi a mɛba ne aduane, mobɛdi, efisɛ mo ne nnipa a mo hia.” Aso ne ne mma no didii. Papa Kɔkɔɔre horoo; ɔkɔtɔɔ ɔdan no ase; ɔkaa sɛ, “Kukuo yi fɛ.” Komm! “Kukuo yi fɛ.” Komm! Papa Kɔkɔɔre kaa sɛ, “Ah! ɛyɛ efisɛ ntoma yi mfɛ; mɛkɔfa Oyoko clan tartan no aba na matow.” Na ɔkɔree ase kɔfaa Oyoko clan tartan ntoma no aba na watow. Ɔhyɛɛ ne santama; ɔtɔɔ soro (bio). Ɔkaa sɛ, “Kukuo yi fɛ.” Komm! “Kukuo yi fɛ.” Komm! Ɔhwɛɛ ɔdan no mu na ɔhunuu sɛ gun-wad ne kukuo nkete bi wɔ mu. Ananse kaa sɛ, “Ɛnyɛ ade baako, ɛnyɛ ade mmienu (kyerɛ sɛ, ɛnyɛ anigye), Ntikuma ne ɔno.” Ananse bɔɔ kukuo no pɛpɛɛ, na ɔkɔree ase; ɔtooku Oyoko tartan ntoma no guu fam, na ɔkɔree kwae. Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu biribi fɛ fɛ a wɔfrɛ no Mpere, mmarima-mmoa, a ɛsi hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Oh, nwanwa! eyi fɛ sen ne a ɛyɛɛ no; mmoa yi fɛ.” Mmoa no kaa sɛ, “Wɔmfrɛ me ‘fɛ’.” Ɔno (Kɔkɔɔre no) bisaa sɛ, “Ɛnna dɛn na wɔfrɛ wo?” Ɛkaa sɛ, “Wɔfrɛ me ‘Abiridiabrada’.” Na ɔkaa sɛ, “Bɔ me kakra na mahunu.” Na mmoa no tɔɔ ne so, “Swish, swish, swish.” Papa Kɔkɔɔre suu sɛ, “Pui! pui!” Anomaa bi (a ɔte hɔ) kaa kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, “Ka Adwobere.” Na Ananse kaa sɛ, “Adwobere.” Na mmoa no gyee ne bɔtɔ. Na Ananse faa mmoa yi baa fie; na ɔkɔtoo no ɔdan no ase. Aso no noae aduane no; ɔkaa sɛ, “Ananse, bra na midi.” Ɔgyee mmuae sɛ, “Sɛ mo da ase (asase yi so) a, ebia mo anka mampire wɔ mo asɔre mu (na mo nte ne a mekaa), mɛnni aduane.” Ananse tɔɔ soro na ɔkɔtee hɔ komm. Na ɔba ase bio; na ɔkɔ bio na ɔkɔ hwee baabi. Ɛna Ntikuma kɔɔ soro. Ɔse, "O, me papa ayɛ dɛn ama ɔde biribi aba bio." Ntikuma se, "Maame, Nyiwankonfwea, Afudotwedotwe, bra ha, efisɛ nea papa aba adeɛ yi mu no, ɛyɛ kɛse paa sene ne a ɛdi kan no." Ɛna wɔn nyinaa kɔɔ soro (aban no so). Ntikuma se, "Adeɛ yi fɛ." Ɛyɛɛ no mmuae se, "Yɛnfrɛ me 'fɛ'." Ɔse, "Wo din de hwee?" Ɛse, "Yɛfrɛ me 'Swish-and-raise-weals'." Ɔse, "Swish kakra ma me nhu." Na kɔtɔkɔ no tɔɔ ne so na ɛbɔɔ hɔn den den. Ananse gyinaa fa, na ɔteɛm se, "Bɔ no, bɔ no, titiriw Ntikuma, bɔ no." Na Ananse hwɛe na ɔhuu sɛ wɔabɔ hɔn yie no, ɔse, "Adwobere". Ananse bae na ɔfaa kɔtɔkɔ no na ɔtwaa no mmoa ketewa ketewa na ɔhyɛɛ no fam. Ɛno na ɛmaa kɔtɔkɔ baa abusua no mu. Enti ɛno na sɛ woka biribi kyerɛ wo ba, na wannte wo ase a, yɛbɔ no kɔtɔkɔ.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
