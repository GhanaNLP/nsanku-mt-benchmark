NSƐM A ƐDI KAN

COVID-19 yareefo no ho nsɛm a ɛyɛ yaw no akanyan nkɔbea nyinaa, efisɛ Ghana nso wɔ mu. Amanehunu a Ghanafoɔ anya wɔ nkwa, adwuma ne dwumadie a wɔyi gyinaa mu no, Aban no nim ho asɛm. Mepere sɛ mɛka sɛ yɛn kwan a yɛfaeɛ sɛ yɛbɛyɛ biribi ma yareefo no nsem no gyinaa hɔ yɛyɛ den na ɛyɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ, na wiase nyinaa ayɛ no nidi. Aban no fee nnwuma ne akwan foforo a ɛbɛboa adwumadie nyinaa ma ɛasane aba na yɛn asetena nso asane aba ne baabi a ɛfata.

Bio, sɛ eyɛ mmuae ma nsɛm a ɛyɛ ahofama ne akwantu a ɛfiri pandemic yi mu no, Aban no, wɔ H.E. President Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo animuonyam mu, yɛɛ GH¢100 billion COVID-19 Alleviation and Revitalisation of Enterprises programme (GhanaCARES “Obaatan Pa” programme) ntɛm. Wei akɔma mmoa a ɛyɛ kɛse ne a ɛyɛ ntɛm ma yɛn manfoɔ na ɛrehyɛ yɛn man no den bio.

Yɛda mo ase sɛ mo wɔ gyidi wɔ Aban no mu mpo berɛ a yɛreyɛ adwuma sɛ yɛbɛgyina pintinn wɔ saa kɔrantire yi akyi. Wɔ saa berɛ yi mu no, yɛn ahodenom sɛ yɛbɛtena yɛn ho sɛ amanfoɔ bi a yɛayɛ ayɛyɛ. Bible no ma yɛn akwan pa berɛ a eyie te saa yi mu. Titiriw, sɛnea ɛhyɛ ase wɔ Isaya 9:10 mu no, “duahyeɛ a wɔde ayɛ adan no atɔ, nanso yɛbɛyɛ adan foforo a yɛde aboɔ a ayɛ fɛ; sycamores no atwa, nanso yɛbɛde cedars gyina hɔ”. Sɛnea ɛte saa no, Aban no nso ayɛ ahodenom sɛ ɛbɛyɛ ne nniyiremu nyinaa. Enti, yɛbɛkɔ so ayɛ nnwuma ne nhyehyɛe a ɛbɛboa ma yɛasiesie nea yɛayi kwan no nyinaa na yɛasiesie nnipa nyinaa asetena.

2022 Budget no ne mfeɛ mmiɛnsa (3) a ɛreba noyɛ mmoa kɛseɛ bi a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔyɛ ne sɛ, wɔbɛsi Entrepreneurial State a ɛbɛyɛ den a ɛbɛtumi ama ɔsika ne nnwuma anyɛ mmerɛ, na ama obiara anya adwuma pa, titiriw Mmerante ne Mmerantea. Enti, Aban no mmara bɛyɛ adeɛ a ɛbɛboa mmerante ne mmerantea ama wɔanya kwan atumi afa wɔn nnabɔ ne wɔn tumi nyinaa ayɛ adwuma na ama obiara anya anigye ne ahofuo wɔ kwan a ɛyɛ pɛ ma obiara.

Sɛ yɛbɛma mmeranteɛ ne mmabaa anigyeɛ ayɛ kɛse na yɛayɛ wɔn adwumayɛ nkuran a, Aban bɛyɛ YouStart programme no mfitiaseɛ wɔ 2022 mu na ama mmeranteɛ ne mmabaa a wɔyɛ adwumayɛfoɔ anya sika a wɔbɛtwa a wɔbɛtumi afi adwuma foforo ase anaa wɔatumi ayɛ wɔn adwuma no akɛse.

Wɔ 2022 Budget no mu no, yɛa yɛatwerɛ nsɛm a ɛkyerɛ sɛ yɛbɛkɔ so akasa ɔkotoku mmoa a ɛfa ɔsika ne nneɛma ho nsɛnnennen a ɛwɔ hɔ seesei yi mu na yɛanya ahoɔden kɛseɛ wɔ pandemic a ɛakɔ yi akyi. Aban no ayɛ adwuma den sɛ ɛbɛyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛyɛ kɛseɛ na ɛyɛ pɛ de ma mmoa ne nimdeɛ a ɛfa dwumadie ho nkɔmɔnkyɛ ma mmeranteɛ ne mmeranteɛyɛfoɔ. Sɛ yɛrekɔ President no akyi...

Macroeconomic Performance

3. Adwene a ɛyɛ mmofra ho nsɛm a ɛfa ɔman bi economy mu dwumadie wɔ 2021 mu ne saa:

• GDP a ɛyɛ nokware no nyinaa average yɛɛ 3.5 percent wɔ 2021 mfeɛ no mfitiaseɛ mpɔn mmienu no mu, sɛ woyɛ ne ne nkyerɛwee 0.7 percent growth a ɛyɛɛ wɔ 2020 afei saa berɛ koro no mu;

• Non-Oil real GDP nyini yɛyɛɛ mmerɛ a ɛyɛ 4.8 percent wɔ 2021 mfeɛ no mfeɛ mmienu a ɛdi kan no mu, sɛ yɛtwitwa ho kyerɛ 1.1 percent a ɛyɛɛ wɔ 2020 mfeɛ no mmerɛ koro no mu;

• Inflation wɔ September awieeɛ yɛ 10.6 percent, sɛ yɛtwitwa ho kyerɛ 10.4 percent a ɛyɛɛ wɔ saa berɛ koro no ara wɔ 2020 mu;

• Sika a ɛhia (cash) a ɛyɛ GDP 7.7 percent, wɔ target 7.4 percent a yɛde hwee no ho;

• Primary balance no kyerɛɛ deficit a ɛyɛ GDP 2.0 percent, sɛ wopɛ sɛ ɛyɛ 1.7 percent;

• Amanfoɔ mu mfutuma (sɛ GDP percentage) kɔree 77.7 percent wɔ September 2021 awieeɛ, efiri 76.1 percent a ɛwɔ December 2020 awieeɛ;

• Gross International Reserves wɔ August 2021 awieeɛ yɛ US$11.44 billion, a ɛyɛ aduane ne nneɛma a yɛtɔ firi amana mu (imports) nnonsa mmoa 5.2, sɛ yɛtwitwa ho kyerɛ US$8.62 billion a ɛyɛ imports nnonsa mmoa 4.1 a yɛkaa ho asɛm wɔ December 2020 awieeɛ; na

• Sika nsonsonoeɛ no gyinaa bam wɔ mfutuma kɛseɛ a ɛfiri aman ahorow no nyinaa mu wɔ 2021 mfeɛ mmiɛnsa no mu. Nea ɛyɛ pɛ ne sɛ, Ghana cedi no taeɛ 1.7 percent wɔ US Dollar ho kɔsi September 2021 awieeɛ, sɛ yɛtwintwɛn no 2.9 percent a ɛtaeɛ wɔ saa berɛ koro no ara mu wɔ 2020.

Asɛmfua Nkyekyɛmu (Budget Nkyekyɛmu)

1D1F (One District, One Factory): Adwumayɛbea ne nnwuma kɛseɛ a wɔresiesie, a ɛbɛma ɔman no akɔ anim wɔ nnwuma kɛseɛ ho. Wɔyɛ saa sɔaa sɛ wɔbɛyɛ adwuma pii ma Ghanafoɔ wɔ ɔman no mma mma.

Accountability: Eyi ne berɛ a Aban no hyɛ ne ho kwan sɛ ɛbɛma ne manfoɔ mmu ne nneyɛeɛ na ɛaka ne nneyɛeɛ no abaayɛ a ɛba mu. Eyi betumi abi nsika anaa nneɛma foforo a Aban no agye to gyina ho asɛm.

Allocation: Adeɛ a wɔfa so kyɛ resources wɔ aban nnwuma gyinaa ne nnawuo mmoa mu.

Alleviation: Yeinom ne adeɛ a yɛyɛ de ma asiane bi nnyɛ den dodo.

Budget: Budget yɛ nhyehyɛe a ɛkyerɛ baabi a yɛbɛfi sika firi ne nea yɛbɛde asom. Sɛ yɛde nhwɛso a, abusua bi betumi ayɛ budget a ɛbɛkyerɛ sika a wɔwɔ ne sɛnea wɔbɛde ayɛ nneɛma a ɛho hia kɛse (te sɛ, fie kɔye, ebire ne aduane). Sɛ sika bebree hia a, abusua no bɛhia sɛ wɔsusu sɛ wɔbɛhwehwɛ sika foforo anaa wɔbɛgye awie.

Budget deficit: Wei si berɛ a Aban no nsa a ɛkɔ so yɛ pii sen ne sika a ɛba.

Sika a wɔde si nneɛma kɛse: Yeiyi ne sika a wɔde sɔre nneɛma kɛse a ɛyɛ mmaa te sɛ nkram, sukuu, ayaresabea, ponkram, nkram-akwan, nsuo-asuo, nnidiso ne mmanini nea ɛfa ho nyinaa.

Adwumayɛfoɔ mmoa: Yehu yi yɛ sika a wɔde ma adwumayɛfoɔ ne sika a ɛka ho a ɛfa sika a wɔde gyina hɔ ma wɔn mma mma, sika a wɔde ma wɔn berɛ a wɔgye wɔn nsa firi adwuma mu, ne sika a wɔde ma wɔn berɛ a wɔgye adwuma.

Ɔman mmoafoɔ (DPs): Yehu wei sɛ aman ne/anaa nnidie a wɔne nkɔsoɔ mman nyinaa bɔ mu yɛ adwuma na wɔanya hɔn nkɔsoɔ nsɛm a wɔpɛ. DPs yi tumi yɛ multilateral, te sɛ International Monetary Fund (IMF), World Bank, African Development Bank (AfDB) anaa bilateral a ɛyɛ aman bebree te sɛ Germany, United Kingdom ne United States of America (USA).

Feefe’e a Ɛyɛ Inflation wɔ Afe No Awie: Inflation a ɛba DDecember wɔ afe a yɛrehwɛ so no mu.

Sika a wɔde bɔne kyen nea wɔgye gyae: Wei si saa berɛ a Aban sika a wɔde bɔ no kyen Budget a Parliament agye gyae no.

FREE SHS: Free SHS (Free Senior High School) bolifom yɛ Ghana aban mu dwumadie a President Nana Akuffo-Addo aban hyɛɛ aseɛ wɔ September 2017 a ɛma JHS sukuufoɔ a wɔanya animdeɛ nyinaa kwan a ɛyɛ efie sɛ wɔbɛnya SHS adesua a ɛyɛ ɔhorɔ.

Adomfo mmoa (Grants): Yi yɛ sika mmoa bi a nnwontofo a wɔboa nkwasamanea ma Aban, na Aban mpɛ sɛ ɛsan chi sika no.

Akyede a wɔde ma Aban mfitiaseɛ afoforo: Yeinom yɛ sika a mmara hyɛ hɔ sɛ wɔbɛde ma, te sɛ District Assemblies’ Common Fund (DACF), Road Fund, Ghana Education Trust Fund (GETFUND), Petroleum Related Fund ne National Health Fund.

Gross Domestic Product (GDP): Yei kyerɛ nnoɔma ne adwuma nyinaa a wɔayɛ wɔman mu wɔ mmerɛ bi mu a wɔde ayɛ adwene.

Gross International Reserves: Yehu mu ne ɔman bi foreign currency a ɔwɔ de kɔtɔ nneɛma ne nnwuma firi mfoni mman mu wɔ berɛ bi mu; ɛboaa ma ɔman no sika no ayɛ den wɔ mfoni mman sika ho.

Inflation: Sika ne nneɛma a yɛtɔn nneɛma ne nsɛm a yɛtɔn no ahoɔden a ɛkyɛre sɛ nneɛma ne nsɛm a yɛtɔn no ahoɔden kɔ soro wɔ mmerɛ bi mu.

IPEP (Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme): Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme (IPEP) yɛ NPP Aban dwumadie kɛseɛ bi a neyɛyɛ ne sɛ wɔbɛyi ohia afi hɔ na wɔatumi abɔ amanehunu a ɛda hɔ mu mmu, titiriw wɔ akuraa ne mpɔtam a wɔnni nneɛma pii mu, wɔ mmoa a ɛfa mfapaso ne nnidisoɔ a ɛhia kɛseɛ ho.

Macroeconomic targets: Yi yɛ nsɛm a yɛde hwe nea ɛrekɔ so wɔ ɔman bi sika ne nneɛma mu. Mpempetem bi ne ɔman sika ne nneɛma mu nyinnye, inflation, nkyerɛwee a ɛba adwumayɛ mu, nnwuma a ɔman no yɛ ne aman afoforo, abɔnten sika ne nneɛma ho mmara a aban no ayɛ ne nea ɛayɛ yie anaa nea ɛanyɛ yie, ne Bank of Ghana gyinaeɛ a ɛfa sika a nnipa pɛ ne sika a ɛwɔ ɔman no mu ho.

Sika a ɛnyɛ Atufoɔ Sika: Akyɛdeɛ a aban nya a ɛnyɛ efisɛ wɔatua atuo, nhwɛso ne sika a wɔ tua ma permit anaa license, sika a wɔtua ma nneɛma a wɔde di dwuma ne afɛfɛɛfɛ a wɔtua.

Primary balance : Yei ne mmuae a ɛda Aban ne ne sika a ɛba ne ne sika a ɔde yɛ nneɛma a ɛnyɛ sukuu anaa mfa (yɛmfa mmoa a wɔde siesie awoɔ). Yɛbetumi ahwehwɛ eyi sɛ GDP no mmofra mmoa.
