Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Kwan biara a wuhu sɛ Osebo, gyata no, akra ho a, ɛhɔ Adanko, ɔbrani no, akɔ afiri.

Wɔka sɛ ɔkraman Leopard ne ɔkroma Leopard, ne ɔkraman Hare ne ɔkroma Hare, tenaa kuro ketewa bi mu beao a kɔm bae. Ɛno a ɔkroma Leopard see sɛ ɔrekɔ asubɔn. Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no (ɔhyiaa) ɔkroma Hare a ɔrepopopɔ aburo. Ɔkraman Leopard no see sɛ, "Ah, maame, worefa saa eyieɛ yi fii he?" Ɔkraman Hare no hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, "Kwan akɛseɛ bi wɔ ha a ɛkɔ akyiri, na me kunu kɔɔ so kɔree wild yams, na ɔkɔfaa saa aburo yi, enti fa eyi (na) kɔno na woatwa)." Ɔhyɛɛ no ase sɛ, “Meda wo ase, Aku.” Ɛnna Ɔkraman Leopard no faa no kɔɔ fie, kɔtoo no anim. Na ne kunu baa, na ɔbisaa no (sɛ ɔfaa aba no wɔ he), na ɔsee no sɛ, “Ɔkraman kɔkɔɔ no na ɔmaa me, na ɔsee me sɛ kwan akɛse bi wɔ hɔ akyi, na ne kunu kɔɔ hɔ, na ɔfaa (aba bi) baa.” Ɔgyata kɔkɔɔ no see sɛ, “Ɛnna ɔkyena anɔpa paa, mɛkɔ hɔ.” Enti ɔkɔɔ. Berɛ a ɔkɔɔ no, aba pii wɔ hɔ. Ɛna ɔfaa ne bɛna ɔtwaa bi, na ɔyɛɛ borɔdwo kɔtokuo, na ɔboaa (aburo no) na ɔtoo (no mu). Adanko, ɔkraman ɔkraman no, wɔnna ne fie, na ɔhunuu sɛ ɔkraman ɔpuna no akɔ afuo. Ɛno ara na ɔno nso kɔɔ. Ɔduu kurom no awie a, ɔyɛɛ duiker. Ne mmoa koro a ɛyɛ bebree no, ne mmoa koro a ɛnyɛ bebree. Na ɔkɔɔ anim bio, na ɔyɛɛ Oyuo. Ne honam koro a ɛyɛ bebree popɔyɛɛ; foforo no nso anpopɔ. Na ɔkɔɔ anim bio, na ɔyɛɛ Bongo. Ne honam koro a ɛyɛ bebree popɔyɛɛ; foforo no nso anpopɔ. Na ɔkɔɔ anim bio, na ɔyɛɛ Buffalo. Ne honam nyinaa foforo. Afei Leopard no reba a ɔde kɛtɛ ne aburotoo aba ne tiwuo. Ɔbaa hɔ na ɔhyiaa Buffalo no. Ɔpyim no kaa (ne ho), “Ɔkɔm bɛba, nanso ɛnnyɛ (den) da, nanso mmoa yi rewu.” Na ɔfaa ho kɔeɛ, na ɔhyiaa Bongo no; na ɔfaa ho kɔeɛ, na ɔhyiaa Oyuo no; na ɔfaa ho kɔeɛ, na ɔhyiaa Duyker no. Ɛna ɔse, “O, mmoa yi bebree paa, enti mɛkɔgye (bi).” Ɔtoo kɛtɛ ne aburo no gu fam, na ɔkɔɔ sɛ ɔbɛgye mmoa no, ɔhyɛɛ asei fii nea ɛdi kan no. Berɛ a ɔkɔe no, wɔn nni hɔ bio. Ɛna ɔsan baa, nanso kɛtɛ ne aburo no nso (apae). Ɔkɔɔ fie, na ɔtwɛnɛɛ mu yɛɛ komm. Ɔkraman Aberewa no bisaa no sɛ, “Ɛhene na aburo no wɔ?” Ɔkaaa sɛ, “Mekɔe (hɔ), nanso mihyiaa biara a metumi atu.” Ɛnnɛ, Ɔkraman Aberewa no kɔɔ asupono no ho, na (hɔ) na ohiaa Ɔkraman Kraman Aberewa no. Ɔpyimma no see sɛ, “Deɛ mepɛ sɛ meka ne sɛ, nnɛma a ɛsi anadwo no, me kunu kaa sɛ berɛ a ɔkɔe no, ɔanhwehwɛ aburo biara a ɔbɛtɔ.” Ɔapemmaa no hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, “Ɔredi wo nkontompo, efisɛ nnɛma a ɛsi anadwo no, berɛ a ɔkɔe na ɔnyae (bi) no, me kunu bɔɔ no na ɔfaa no firi ne nsam.” Ɔpyimma no kɔɔ fie. Ɔkaa kyerɛɛ ne kunu sɛ, “Yaa agya! yaa agya! yaa agya!” wo kaa sɛ wo kɔeɛ na wonya aba ayɛn biara, nanso ɛyɛ ɔHare no na ɔbɔɔ wo na ɔfaa no fii wo nsam.” ɔ Leopard no kaa sɛ, “Hwan ne ɔHare? Ɔkyena mɛkɔ na mɛne no atuo a mɛde nnuaba bɔ no.” ɔHare barima no tenaa fie na ɔtee (wei). Ɔkɔɔ hɔ kasee ne yere sɛ, “Ɔkyena, Leopard barima no bɛba ha aba me ne no nkoom, enti mɛkɔ hyia no wɔ kwan no so, efisɛ ebia wubɛsuro.” Anɔpa a ɛdi akyi no, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ paa no, Hare no fii ase tɔɔ ne nan. Ɔduu kurom no ano, na ɔyɛɛ Adowa antelope, na ɔrepuu fitaa, charcoal, ne mogya. Ɔkɔɔ anim, na ɔyɛɛ ɔbusubuku, na ɔrepopɔ saa ara. Ɔkɔɔ anim, na ɔyɛɛ ɔkɔkɔɔ. Ɔgyata no nso reba. Adowa mmoa no see, “Agya Leopard, boa me, efisɛ merewu.” Leopard no see, “Dɛn na asi?” Adowa mmoa no see, “Me ne Hare kɔɔ sɛ yɛbɛsiesie nsɛnnennen a ɛfa aburo ho, na ɔbɔɔ me, enti merewu.” Leopard no see, “Ma me nkɔ.” Ɔkɔɔ anim, na ɔhyiaa Bush-buck. Bush-buck no see sɛ, "Leopard, boa me, efisɛ Hare no nyɛ obi a obi bɛne no ho asomdwoe wɔ aburode ho." Leopard no bisaa sɛ, "Dɛn na asi?" Bush-buck no see sɛ, "Mekɔɔ hɔ na ɔbɔɔ me, ɛno nti na merewu." Leopard no see sɛ, "Ma me nkɔ." Ɔkɔe, na ɔhyiaa Buffalo. Buffalo no see sɛ, "Agya Osebo, boa me, efisɛ merewu." Osebo no see sɛ, "Dɛn na asi?" Buffalo no see sɛ, "Mekɔɔ hɔ na me ne HareTwetwe hyiaa nsɛnnennen fa aburo ho, na ɔbɔɔ me, na merewu." Ɛno akyi no, Osebo no suman anansesɛm no yiye (na akyiri no ɔsee ne ho sɛ), "E! Mɛsan akɔ, efisɛ corn case a ɛte saa ara na mɛsiesie ne ho, na sɛ ɔbɔɔ me a... Na Leopard no hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔdwen, kiri! kiri! Ɛno ara a, Hare no nso soɔree. Ɔtuu no, na Leopard no gyigyei kɛse, "Female Leopard, dwene o!" Leopard no duu ne fie, na ɔguan kɔɔ ne poku mu, na ɔfa ne ti bɔɔ mpɔmu mu kɔɔ anim, nanso Hare no kyereɛ no ne nan mmienu a ɛwɔ ne nkyɛn no. Na Aberewaa no see, "Papa Ware, ma me nko." Ware no see, "Mrenkan, mampɛ sɛ mema wo nkɔ, mɛbɔ wo." Aberewaa no see, "Me srɛ wo, mma wo mmpɔ me, na ma me nsusuwa mnom na me hoam sɛ kwan biara a wobɛfa, mɛba mɛpue." Eyi ara ne nea nti, sɛ wofa kwan biara na wuhu sɛ Aberewaa no akra hɔ, (wohu sɛ) Ware afa hɔ.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
