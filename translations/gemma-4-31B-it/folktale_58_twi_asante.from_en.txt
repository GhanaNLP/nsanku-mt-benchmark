Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Kwan biara a wuhu sɛ Osebo, gyata no, akra ho a, ɛhɔ Adanko, ɔbrani no, akɔ afiri.

Wɔka sɛ ɔkraman Aberewa ne ɔkrawaa Aberewa, ne ɔkraman Kwaku Dua ne ɔkrawaa Kwaku Dua, tenaa kuro ketewa bi mu, na kɔm bae. Ɛno a ɛyɛ no, ɔkrawaa Aberewa no see sɛ ɔrekɔ asubɔn. Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no (ɔhyiaa) ɔkrawaa Kwaku Dua a ɔrepopopɔ aburo. Ɔkrawaa Aberewa no bisaa no sɛ, "Ah, ɛna wo na woanya eyi?" Ɔkrawaa Kwaku Dua no hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, "Kwan akɛse bi wɔ ha akyi, na me kunu kɔɔ hɔ kɔree pampam, na ɔkɔkɔɔ aburo yi, enti fa eyi (na) kɔno na woatwa." Ɔhyɛɛ no ase sɛ, "Meda wo ase, Aku." Ɛnna ɔkrawaa Aberewa no faa no kɔɔ fie, kɔtoo no anim. Na ne kunu bae, na ɔbisaa no (sɛ ɔfaa aburo no wɔ he), na ɔsee no sɛ, "Ɔkrawaa Kwaku Dua no na ɔmaa me, na ɔsee me sɛ kwan akɛse bi wɔ hɔ akyi, na ne kunu kɔfaa no, na ɔde (aburo bi) bae." Ɔkraman Aberewa no see sɛ, "Ɛnna ɔkyena anɔpa anɔpa, mɛkɔ hɔ." Ɛnna ɔkɔe. Berɛ a ɔkɔe no, aburo pii wɔ hɔ. Ɛnna ɔfaa ne bɛtɛɛ na ɔtwaa bi, na ɔyɛɛ nkyene mmoa, boaa (aburo no) ano, na ɔtoo (no mu). Ɛnna Adanko, ɔkraman Kwaku Dua no, wɔ ne fie, na ɔhunuu sɛ ɔkraman Aberewa no akɔ afuo. Ɛnna ɔno nso kɔe. Ɔkɔɔ kuro no awie na ɔyɛɛ Duyker. Ne honam koro a ɛyɛ bebree no puduee; fɛkoro nso anpudue. Na ɔkɔɔ anim bio, na ɔyɛɛ Oyuo. Ne honam koro a ɛyɛ bebree no puduee; fɛkoro nso anpudue. Na ɔkɔɔ anim bio, na ɔyɛɛ Bongo. Ne honam koro a ɛyɛ bebree no puduee; fɛkoro nso anpudue. Na ɔkɔɔ anim bio, na ɔyɛɛ Buffalo. Ne honam nyinaa fɛfɛɛfɛ. Ɛnna Aberewa no reba a ɔde nkyene ne aburo no atoo ne ti. Ɔbaa na ɔhyiaa Buffalo no. Aberewa no see (ne ho) sɛ, "Kɔm bɛba, nanso ɛnnyɛ den koraa, nanso mmoa yi repu." Na ɔfaa ne ho kɔe, na ɔhyiaa Bongo no; na ɔfaa ne ho kɔe, na ɔhyiaa Oyuo no; na ɔfaa ne ho kɔe, na ɔhyiaa Duyker no. Ɛnna ɔsee sɛ, "Oh, mmoa yi pii paa, enti mɛkɔboaa (hɔ)." Ɔtoo nkyene ne aburo no gu fam, na ɔkɔree kɔfa mmoa no, efifi nea ɛdi kan no. Berɛ a ɔkɔe no, wɔn nni hɔ bio. Ɛnna ɔbaa, nanso nkyene ne aburo no nso akɔ. Ɔkɔɔ fie, na ɔtenaa hɔ wɔ komm mu. Ɔsebo baa no bisaa no sɛ, “Ɛhene na aburodeɛ no wɔ?” Ɔkae sɛ, “Mekɔeɛ, nanso mihyiaa biara a metumi agla.” Ɛnnɛ, Ɔsebo baa no kɔɔ asuom, na (hɔ) na ohiaa ɔkraman baa no. Ɔsebo baa no kaa sɛ, “Deɛ mɛka ne sɛ, nnawku m'akofi kaa sɛ berɛ a ɔkɔeɛ no, ohiaa aburodeɛ biara a ɔbɛtumi agla.” Ɔkraman baa no hyɛɛ aseɛ kaa sɛ, “Ɔredi wo nkontompo, efisɛ nnawku, berɛ a ɔkɔhiaeɛ no, m'akofi bɔɔ no na ɔtookoo no firi ne nsam.” Ɔsebo baa no kɔɔ fie. Ɔbɔɔ ne akofi amanneɛ kaa sɛ, “Yie! yie! yie! woka sɛ wokɔeɛ na wohiaa aburodeɛ biara, nanso ɛyɛ ɔkraman no na ɔbɔɔ wo na ɔtookoo no firi wo nsam.” Ɔsebo no kaa sɛ, “Hwan ne Ɔkraman? Ɔkyena mɛkɔ ne nkyɛn akɔtɔ ne nnompe.” Ɔkraman panin no tenaa fie na tee (deɛ ɛsii yi). Ɔkɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn kaa sɛ, “Ɔkyena, Ɔsebo panin bɛba ha reba akɔtɔ me, enti mɛkɔnhia no wɔ kwan no so, efisɛ ebia wobɛsu.” Anɔpa a ɛbaeɛ no, berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ aseɛ kyerɛɛ, Ɔkraman no hyɛɛ aseɛ kɔeɛ. Ɔduu kurom mfinimfini, na ɔyɛyɛɛ Adowa, na ɔrepopɔ hyire, nkuku, ne mogya. Ɔkɔɔ anim, na ɔyɛyɛɛ Ɔkraman-dwum, na ɔrepopɔ saa ara. Ɔkɔɔ anim, na ɔyɛyɛɛ Gyata. Ɔsebo no reba. Adowa no kaa sɛ, “Agya Ɔsebo, boa me, efisɛ merewu.” Ɔsebo no bisaa sɛ, “Dɛn na asii?” Adowa no kaa sɛ, “Me ne Ɔkraman kɔeɛ sɛ yɛbɛsiesie aburodeɛ asɛm bi, na ɔbɔɔ me, enti merewu.” Ɔsebo no kaa sɛ, “Ma me nkɔ.” Ɔkɔeɛ, na ohiaa Ɔkraman-dwum no. Ɔkraman-dwum no kaa sɛ, “Ɔsebo, boa me, efisɛ Ɔkraman no nyɛ obi a wobɛtumi ayɛ aburodeɛ asɛm ne no.” Ɔsebo no bisaa sɛ, “Dɛn na asii?” Ɔkraman-dwum no kaa sɛ, “Mekɔeɛ na ɔbɔɔ me, enti ɛno ne nea nti a merewu.” Ɔsebo no kaa sɛ, “Ma me nkɔ.” Ɔkɔeɛ, na ohiaa Gyata no. Gyata no kaa sɛ, “Agya Osebo, boa me, efisɛ merewu.” Ɔsebo no bisaa sɛ, “Dɛn na asii?” Gyata no kaa sɛ, “Mekɔeɛ na meyɛɛ aburodeɛ asɛm ne Ɔkraman, na ɔbɔɔ me, enti merewu.” Ɛno akyi no, Ɔsebo no sumsumuu yiye (na ɔkaa kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ), “E! Mɛsan akɔ, efisɛ aburodeɛ asɛm a ɛte saa ara na mɛkɔsiesie ne no, na sɛ ɔbɔɔ me a...” Na Ɔsebo no hyɛɛ aseɛ dwene, kiri! kiri! Mpofirim ara, Ɔkraman no nso gyinaa aseɛ. Ɔtuu no, na Kwaku Dua tee nne kɛseɛ sɛ, "Ɔdua baa, tutu yɛ o!" Kwaku Dua duu ne fie, na ɔyɛɛ n’akwan kɔɔ ne poku mu, na ɔfa ne ti bɔɔ ban bi mu na ɔfaa mu, nanso Kwaku Dua kuraa ne nan mmienu a ɛwɔ akyi no. Na Kwaku Dua see sɛ, "Kwaku Dua agya, ma me nko." Kwaku Dua see sɛ, "Mrenkan mma wo nko, mɛbɔ wo." Kwaku Dua see sɛ, "Me srɛ wo, mma wo mmɔ me, na ma me nsusuwa nnom na me ntoni sɛ kwan biara a wobɛfa, mɛba mɛpue." Ɛno nti, sɛ wofa kwan biara na wohu sɛ Kwaku Dua ahwee hɔ, (wohu sɛ) Kwaku Dua afa saa kwan no.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
