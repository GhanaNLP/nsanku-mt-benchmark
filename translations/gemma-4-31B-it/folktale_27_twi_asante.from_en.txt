Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Abusua bi mu no, obiara nni hɔ a onni nyansa koraa

Obaa bi wɔn na ɔwɔ mma animan. Ne ba panin no yɛ kɔkɔɔ koraa. Onnim ade biara a ɔbɛyɛ, enti ne nua mma no kaa sɛ, "Yɛntumi nante ne yɛn nkyɛn, enti ma n'ani ngye na ɔnfiri fie." Enti—fa me nkyɛm (wɔ mo mpanyimfoɔ yi anim)—saaa kɔkɔɔ no kɔyɛɛ ne nkɔsomdeɛ na ɔtena mu. Ɛhɔmpon no mu no, hwee nni hɔ a ɛyɛ sand. Na ɔyɛɛ mpaaboaeɛ na ɔdi n’ani kɛse ne no. Okrokrokron no (yehwɛ, ne maame) ne ne mma no tenaa hɔ. Da koro bi, ɔsɔree kɔɔ nnɔbaeɛ no, na ɔhunuu sɛ abofra bi te wɔ dua bi mu kɔkɔɔ bi mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Hwan ne ɔno?" Abofra no ankyerɛ no hwee. Ɔbaa no kɔɔ hɔ, na ɔhwɛɛ abofra no yie, na ɔkyɛw ne nsa, na ɔsee no sɛ, "Ma yɛnkɔ fie." Na abofra no sɔree, na ɔkɔɔ ne akyi, na wɔduu (wɔn) fie. Ɔbaa no kasaa ne mma no, ɔsee wɔn sɛ, "Hwɛ abofra fɛfɛ a meanya yi, ma me me nnaawa na matwa ne nwini a ɛyɛ tenten yi." Ɛnna ɔbaa no ammu sɛ abofra no yɛ nnipa ketewa no mu ba, na ɛno nti na ne nwini no yɛ nka saa. Enti ɔfaa nnaawa no na ɔtwaa ne ti nwini no, na ɔtoa gui. Berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ (ye. anɔpa a ɛba no), berɛ a wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔnni aduane no, ɔkaa sɛ ɔrenni; na wɔbisaa no sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛrekɔ so?" Berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ wɔda adi, (ɛyɛ,) "Fa me ti mu nwini bra me." Berɛ a nneɛma nni hɔ sɛ wɔbɛhu hɔ bio, (ɛyɛ,) "Fa me ti mu nwini bra me." Na seesei wɔyɛɛ mmerɛ na wɔyɛɛ ketewa paa (efisɛ wɔhu animuonyam). Da koro bi, abofra a ɔyɛ kwasea no see sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhu ne na. Bere a ɔbaa no, (hwɛ) ne maame ne ne nua mmarima ne nua mmaa no ayɛ mmerɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn nti na moayɛ mmerɛ saa?" Maame no repɛ sɛ ɔkyerɛ no nea edi mu paa a ama wɔayɛ mmerɛ no, na mma no kaa sɛ, "Mma mma no nnim, ɔyɛ kwasea, ɛnneɛn na ɔhu nyansa?" Maame no kaa sɛ, "Mɛka." Berɛ a ɔkae no, ɔkaa sɛ, "O, fa sunsumfoɔ no mma me na mefa no akɔ me akuraa mu." Maame ne nnuaba ne nnuaba no faa sunsumfoɔ no an na ɔtwe ne ti nwini na wɔkɔɔ kwasea no akuraa mu. Berɛ a wɔduu hɔ, ne akuraa mu no, ɔhyɛɛ ɔsuman no bɔ gyae, ɔsee no sɛ, "Kɔtena me mpanimpan no so na woanya anigye kakra." Ɔkɔtee so, na ɔtwee no, na ɔtoo saa nnwom yi:

Me maame wowi me, abofra kwasea bi, Hurotom.

Me papa wɔnne me, abofra kwasea bi, Hurotom.

Me maame wowi me, kwasea koro, Hurotom. Me maame wowi me, kwasea koro, Hurotom.

Me maame wowi me, aboa bi a me yɛ, Hurotom.

Na naayɛ kwasea koro pɛ, Hurotom.

Ɔsuman no noaba kosi sɛ ɔyɛɛ nkum, ɛnna abofra kwasea no kaa kyerɛ no sɛ, "Ma yɛnkɔ anya wo ti mu nwini." Ɔne Ɔsuman no dɔɔm fie; ɔno (Ɔsuman no) kaa sɛ, "Fa me ti mu nwini no bra na fa ho na ma menkɔ." Abofra kwasea no nso kaa sɛ, "Wo nso, nkotoku a woatram no, ɛsɛ sɛ wopae nso nkyekyem no nyinaa ansa na mefa wo ti mu nwini no atu ho." Ɔsuman no kɔɔ ab dwumayɛ no; anka mpo sɛ ɔapue n'ananan nkyerɛwee baako bi a, ɔnam n'ananan so yɛɛ foforo. Seesei, ɛnna ɔyɛ biara a ɔbɛyɛ no, ɔntumi mpue ne nan nkyerɛwee nyinaa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Papa, menkum 'palaver' biara, enti mema wo naama, na m'asɛm nso, ma me nso nsan me nsa, na matumi akɔ." Abofra a ɔyɛ kwasea no kaa sɛ, "Ɛnna kɔ, na me nso, mafa me naama (afiri wo nsam)." Ɛnnɛ na naama no bae na ɔtwere knee, ɔse, "Meda wo ase, na seesei (meahu sɛ) obiara nnyɛ kwasea wɔ abusua mu." Ɛno nti na yɛse, sɛ wowɔ mmofra, tware mo ani, na hwɛ yɛn yie, efisɛ mo nnim sɛ nnepa bi, wɔ amanehunu da no mu, (baako wɔn mu) bɛba na wahwɛ mo.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
