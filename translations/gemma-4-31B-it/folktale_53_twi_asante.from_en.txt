Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNE EKUONA ABUSUA BAI WƆ AKYIDWEE MU NO

Obaa bi wɔ hɔ a yɛfrɛ no Abena; berɛ a ne maame ne ne papa faa no kɔhyɛɛ ayaresɛm mu (ma obiara), ɔse, “Ɛnyɛ saa koraa.” Na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne papa se, “Da a mɛhu ɔbarima a medɔ no, saa da no, sɛ meba na woreyɛ nsuo a, mɛfa wo nsuo-kuku no na mɛbɔ wo (ene no) wɔ wo ti atifi.” Enti, aboa no, Buffalo, tee saa asɛm yi, ɔtrans ɔyɛɛ ɔbarima, na ɔkɔɔ, na ɔduu ɔbaa no kuro mu. Ɛnnɛ, berɛ a Abena hunuu no, ɔtuu mmirika kɔɔ baabi a ne papa retu nufu. Ɔfaa nufu-kutu no fii ne nsam, na ɔpiraa no wɔ ne ti so. Na saa da no yɛ anigye da wɔ akurafo no mu, a wɔse, “Yɛn panin no ba ahunu obi a ɔpɛ no.” Ɛnna wɔkɔfaa ɔbarima no baa fie. Na abaayewa no see, “Wei ne obi a mepɛ.” Ɔbarima no nso pɛɛ saa, na ɔwaree no. Ɔtena hɔ nnansa, na afei ɔsee sɛ ɔbɛkɔ ne akurafoɔ hɔ, na obiayewa no see sɛ ɔbɛkɔ ne nkyɛn. Enti Nana Konadu sa ne nneɛma nyinaa (ma akwantuo no) na ɔmaa no nnipa twenty-four na ama wakɔ ne wɔn. Ɛna wɔnnam kɔe, na wɔduu kuro bi a ɛwɔ hɔ mfitiaseɛ, na akoko bi bɔɔ. Nnipa no kaa sɛ, “Yɛadu fie anaa?” Ɔbaa no kunu no kaa sɛ, “Mewaa yere foforo, na ɛsɛ sɛ mefa no kɔ me fie kan, na ɔatwa nneɛma nyinaa apɛpɛɛ, ansa na wakɔ akɔnka nnipa.” Ɛno nti wɔnam kɔe. Woduu kwaeɛ mu kwan a ɛyɛ anopɛ. Berɛ a wɔduu kwaeɛ no kuro no mu no, ɔbarima no gyinaa baako; ɔsee, “Abena, berɛ no adu se mɛka kyerɛ wo nea medeɛ.” Abena bisaa no, “Dɛn na wo yɛ?” Ɔbarima no see, “‘Meyɛ ‘pa!’ na matumi ayɛ ‘gyiren!’;’” na ɔyɛɛ gyata. Nnipa nyinaa see, “E! “ɛnne, dɛn na ɔse, ɔse ɔbɛyɛ ‘pa!’ na ɔbɛyɛ ‘gyiren!’” (Ansa na wɔabɛyɛ saa) na wɔnyinaa bɛyɛɛ gyiren. Onipa baako na ɔtan hɔ na ɔsan kɔɔ fie. Na ɔkɔkae kuro hɔ Odikro no ne nea ɔahu no. Ɔhene ne Ɔhemaa frɛɛ adwene-yiɛ; ɔno (nea ɔkɔɔ ne ho) kaa amanneɛ a ɛsi hɔ kyerɛɛ hɔn berɛ a wɔnam kɔron kɔron no, na berɛ a ɔduu baabi a ɔse, “Ɔyɛɛ ‘pa!’ na ama ɔatumi ayɛ ‘gyiren!’,” ɔno nso yɛyɛɛ kɔkɔɔ. Berɛ biara a obiara bɛpɛ sɛ ɔka saa nsɛm yi bio, ɔyɛyɛɛ kɔkɔɔ. Kuro no mu nnipa nyinaa bɛyɛɛ nkwakuo, na ɔbaa panyin baako pɛ na ɔnnyɛ. Berɛ bi a saa nneɛma yi sii, aduane-foɔ bi baa kuro no mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ah! kurom kɛseɛ, na nnipa nni mu?” Dinn! (Hwee nni hɔ a ɛyɛ mmuaeɛ gye cicada no nnyigyeɛ.) Ɔkaa bio. Akyirei na ɔbaa a ɔyɛ mpaninfoɔ no see, “Mewɔ ha.” Oduruyefo no see, “Ɛdeɛn ne amanneɛ no; na adɛn nti na wote wo nkoa wɔ kuro yi mu?” Ɔbaa no hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, “Wubetumi aba ha, (nanso menni hwee a mɛyɛ wɔ wo ho), kɔ na ma me ntena me baabi.” Oduruyefo no see, “Woyɛ bayefo; wo na woatwe kuro yi mu nnipa nyinaa, enti, gye sɛ wopɛ... “Sɛ woka a, mɛyɛ wo mmerɛw mmerɛw.” Okrokrokron no see sɛ, “Sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meka a, (kanto) kɔfa nnompe na fa kyekyere me, na fa fa gyedua no mu kyekyere me.” Oduruyefo no bae na ɔkyekyere no, na ɔkyekyere no hyɛɛ gyedua no mu. Ɛnnɛ na ɔbaa kɔkɔɔ no kae ne nneɛma nyinaa a asiw, nanso berɛ a ɔduu baabi a ɛsɛ sɛ ɔka sɛ, “Ɔse ɔbɛyɛ ‘pa!’ na ɔatumi ayɛ gyiren!” no, ahwene no puee; ɔbaa kɔkɔɔ no bɛyɛɛ kɔkɔɔ kɛseɛ na ɔtuu kwan. Ɛna oduruyefo no see sɛ, “Seesei, mehu sɛ ɛnyɛ ɔbaa kɔkɔɔ no na ɔwui akurafoɔ a wɔte kuro yi nyinaa.” Ɛna ɔkɔtweeduru (dua) na ɔmiii wɔn hyɛɛ wɔn kɔbeaeɛ a ɛyɛ kɔpa mu, na ɔsuaa nsuo gu mu; na ɔfaa ne (sumsum) nnaman-nsesa no tɔɔ mu; ɛna ɔmaa ne sukuufoɔ kuraa (kɔpa no) maa no. Ɔfaa kwan kɔɔ kwaa mu. Ɔhunuu nkwakuo wɔ hɔ a wɔgyina hɔ, na ɔtɔɔ ne nsa nsuo no mu na ɔpuee wɔn so, na wɔsan yɛɛ nnipa bio. Na ade a ɛtena hɔ ne wɔn a na wɔyɛ nkwakuo ankasa. Na nnipa no san kɔɔ fie. Saa na Ekuona abusua baa amansan no mu; ɛfiri ayawadeɛ a Abena yɛɛ no mu.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
