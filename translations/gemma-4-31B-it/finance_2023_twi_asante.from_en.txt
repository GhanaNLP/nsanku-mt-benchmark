NSƐM A ƐDI KAN

FFEɛ yi bɛtena sɛ eyi a ɛyɛ den paa wɔ yɛn man sika ho nsɛm abakɔsɛm mu. Sɛnea wiase nyinaa te, COVID-19 yareefu no hyɛɛ yɛn so wɔ 2020 mfeɛ no mmerɛ a ɛdi kan, na eyi maa sika ho dwumadie no tɔɔ mu, a ɛmaa yɛn sika a ɛba no kaa, na ɛde sika a yɛanhyɛ no budget mu a ɛhyɛ yɛn nsa pii tEFI yareefu no ho ako mu.

Berɛ a yɛdwene sɛ yɛregye nnamu kɛse wɔ yɛn nneyɛe mu fi pandemic no mmofra a ɛyɛ hu mu, na yɛrehyɛ yɛn transformation agenda no ase, na nneɛma pii baa yɛn so a, wɔ saa berɛ no, ɛpraa macroeconomic nkunim a yɛnyaa fi 2017 kɔsi 2019. Ne kan ne Russia-Ukraine kɔgya no, na akyi no, 2022 budget mu revenue measures titiriw bi anhyɛ ase ntɛm, na ɛmaa credit rating no tɔree. Mpofirim, mfeɛ dusan mu ne kan, International Capital Market no gyinaa yɛn anim, domestic financing conditions yɛyɛ den na sika a yɛboaboa so gyinaa mu, na ɛboa ama debt service ayɛ kɛse ne cedi no agyina ntɛm.

Saa ara, berɛ a sika a ɛfiri mframa mu ne sika a yɛnya firi mframa mu gyaei, nneɛma a yɛtɔ firi mframa mu ho sika a yɛboa so tua kɔɔ sorɔ efisɛ yɛpɛ nneɛma pii firi mframa mu, ne sika a yɛde tua yɛn akyɛdeɛ a yɛboaboa so tua no nso kɔɔ sorɔ, eyi maa yɛn sika ne bo tee. Eyi ama asetena mu nneɛma ho sika akɔ sorɔ ma yɛn nyinaa, sɛ aban ne sɛ nnipa. Petrolem sika a ɛkɔ sorɔ no ama nneɛma ne dwumadie pii ho sika akɔ sorɔ, na inflation no nso kɔɔ ne nea ɛyɛ kɛse paa a ɛyɛ 40.4% wɔ October 2022 mu.

Memorandum of Understanding (MoU): Ɛyɛ nkɔsomdeɛ a ɛyɛ den a ɛkyerɛ nsɛm a nnipa anaa kurom mmienu anaa mmienu kɔm a wɔapaw mu kyerɛ sɛnea wɔbɛyɛ adeɛ bi bɔ mu.

Module: Ɛyɛ nneɛma a wɔatwa atwa a yɛbetumi abɔ mu ama ɛayɛ kɛse

Sika ho amanneɛ: Ɛyɛ amanneɛ a ɔman bi sika ho mpanyimfo adoptive ma wɔbɛtumi akontrol interest rate a wɔbɛsoa mmoa a ɛyɛ tiawa anaa sika a ɛwɔ nnwuma mu, interest rate, na ama ahiahoɔ mu ahia ho nni nsakyerae.

MSME (Micro And Small Buisness Developmet Programme): Micro-enterprise (ana microenterprise) yɛ dwumadie ketewa a ɛde nnipa kakra na ɛyɛ adwuma, na ne sika a ɛba anaasɛ ne sika a ɛwɔ ne nsa koraa nni mmoa kɛse bi ho.

NABCo (Nation Builders Corps): a wɔfrɛ no NABCO nso, yɛɛ adwene bi a Ghana Aban yɛɛ no de hyɛɛ aseɛ na ama nnipa a wɔawu university a wɔnni adwuma anya adwuma, na wɔatumi ayɛ mmuaeɛ ama samansaman nsɛnnennen a ɛwɔ private ne public sectors mu, na wɔatumi ayɛ wɔn adeɛ yie na ama ɔman no sika ne nkwasam anyɛ kɛse.

National Afforestation Programme (NAP): Ɛyɛ nsaman-nwunu a ɛkyerɛ sɛ yɛbɛsiesie nwunu a awu anaa a ayɛ mmerɛ, na yɛayɛ nwunu ho adeɛ nyinaa yie wɔ nnipa mmoa so, na yɛde anansew kyerɛ sɛ yɛbɛyɛ nnwunu a wɔte ho mmofra ne mpanyimfo a wɔn asetena ayɛ den, titiriw aduane ne sika a ɛnnyɛ pii.

Non-Oil Real GDP: Yɛne sika a ɛba ɔman mu nyinaa a yɛmpue nneɛma a ɛfa petroleum ho, na ɛhwɛ inflation level no so.

Non-Oil Real GDP Growth: Ɛyɛ ade a ɛkyerɛ nnwuma nyinaa a ɛma ɔman bi nkɔga fee afe biara, na ɛmfata nneɛma a ɛfa petroleum ho.

Sika a ɛnyɛ Atufoɔ Sika: Akyɛdeɛ a aban nya a ɛnyɛ efisɛ wɔatua atuo, nhwɛso ne sika a wɔ tua ma permit anaa license, sika a wɔtua ma nneɛma a wɔde di dwuma ne afɛfɛɛfɛ a wɔtua.

Nneɛma a ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ a ɛnka bi: Nneɛma a ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ a ɛnka no bi ne MDA nkabom a wɔyɛeɛ ne Ministry of Finance, ne 2016 sika a ɛda Statutory Funds ho.

Outturn: Ɛyɛ adeɛ a ɛfi dwumadie bi anaa nnoɔma a ɛkɔ so wɔ asɔre bi mu.

Primary balance : Yei ne mmuae a ɛda Aban ne ne sika a ɛba ne ne sika a ɔde yɛ nneɛma a ɛnyɛ sukuu anaa mfa (yɛmfa mmoa a wɔde siesie awoɔ). Yɛbetumi ahwehwɛ eyi sɛ GDP no mmofra mmoa.

Primary surplus (deficit): Ɛkyerɛ sika a ɛka kɔtɔn (deficit) no mu a ɛfa aban no nnwuma a ɛyɛ nnɛ ne sika a ɛfiri atuo mu, na ɛmpue sika a wɔatua wɔ aban no akyɛde ho.

RFJ (Rearing For Food and Jobs): bɛyɛ livestock industry a ɛyɛ den na ɛyɛ efisien, a ɛbɛma mfuo mu nnwuma nyinaa ayɛ pii, ama livestock products a yɛtumi tɔ firi aman a ɛsoro mu adidi, ama adwuma apii aba, na ama livestock value chain actors nyinaa asetena ayɛ yie.

Squadron: Ɛyɛ air force mu operational unit a ɛda adi wɔn a wɔde aircraft flights mmienu anaa eyi pii ne nnipa a ɛhia ma wɔbɛtumi atu wɔn.

Subsidy: Yei yɛ sika a aban ma manfoɔ sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɛbɛte nsunsua a ɛwɔ wɔn so.
