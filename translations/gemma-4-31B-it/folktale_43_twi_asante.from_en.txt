Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea ɛyɛɛ koraa sɛ sɛ Nwansana, mmoa no, tɔ Kraman, kraman no so a, ɔpɛɛ no, kam! kam!

Abɔnten bi wɔ kraman anan; baako din de Sniff-sniff; baako din de Lick-lick; baako din de Tie-in-knots; na baako din de Gulp-down. Ɔyɛɛ abɔnten ne ne kraman no. Da biara, sɛ ɔkɔ kwaa mu a, ɔne wɔn kɔ bɔmu. Da koro bi, ɔkɔɔ kwaa mu na ɔhunuu kola-dua bi; nnuaba a ɛatwa n’anim a ɛatwa n’anim kɔ ase sɛnea ɛte sɛ eyi teyɛɛ ne ho. Ɛnna ɔsan kɔɔ fie, na ɔkae ne yere amanneɛ no sɛ, "Kola-dua bi a mehunuu no, nnuaba a ɛatwa n’anim a ɛatwa n’anim kɔ ase sɛnea ɛte sɛ eyi teyɛɛ ne ho." Ne yere see no sɛ, "Kɔfa wo nnuaba no, efisɛ wuhu sɛ yɛnni kola-nut baako mpo a yɛbɛnam so ayɛ nnuaba na yɛnni bi a yɛbɛtɔn." Abɔnten no faa kwan no, nanso ɔgyae kraman no wɔ fie, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a kola-dua no wɔ. Ɛnna ɔtɔɔ so, na ɔfaa kola-nut bi. Afei ɔtee nne firi hɔ sɛ, "Twa! be! kukuru! kukuru! hu! pi!" Sasabonsam bi (gyinaa) ne anim. Ɔbisee no sɛ, "Hwan na wafu me kola-nuts?" Abɔnten no see no sɛ, "Me." Sasabonsam no see no sɛ, "Mɛkum wo seesei efisɛ nea woayɛ yi nti." Abɔnten no see no sɛ, "Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wokum me a, ma me nso mfrɛ me kraman." Ɔsee no sɛ, "Frɛ, nanso sɛ woafrɛ wɔn mprenu mmiɛnsa na wɔanni mma a, ɛnna mɛkum wo." Ɛnna abɔnten no gyinaa (ne nne) so :

Hwɛ-hwɛ, bra na yɛnhyia, Nam-nam, bra na yɛnhyia, Kyekyere-kyekyere, bra na yɛnhyia, Wen-wen, bra na yɛnhyia.

Na kraman a wɔwɔ fie no tee no kakra paa, na wɔtuu kwan kɔ town no anyinamu. Ɛnnɛ na ɔyadeɛfoɔ no tiee (ne nne) bio:

“Sniff-sniff, bra na yɛnhyia, Lick-lick, bra na yɛnhyia, Tie-in-knots, bra na yɛnhyia, Gulp-down, bra na yɛnhyia.

Saa na kraman no ahu; wɔn tutuu, prada! prada! Sasabonsam no see, “Frɛ bio, na ɛno na ɛyɛ teesaa.” Odwumfoɔ no gyaeɛ (ne nne) den nnɛn:

“Sniff-sniff, bra na yɛnhyia, Lick-lick, bra na yɛnhyia, Tie-in-knots, bra na yɛnhyia, Gulp-down, bra na yɛnhyia.

Wai, wai, wai, wɔn retwa mmerɛ pii ansa na wɔabɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, wɔretwa mmerɛ pii ansa na wɔabɛ! Ne nsa anka n’ano, na kurekwase no abɛdur no anim. Abɔfo no see, "Sniff-sniff, Sasabonsam bi wɔ ha, hwen ho." Na Sniff-sniff hwen ne ho. Ɔsee, "Lick-lick, nnum no." Ɔsee, "Tie-in-knots, kyekyere no." Tie-in-knots kyekyere ne kɔn; na Sasabonsam no tɔe hɔ, kum! Ɛnna Gulp-down faa no kɔtoo no. Abɔfo no ne kurekwase no boaa kola nutu no ano de kɔɔ fie. Berɛ a abɔfo no san baa fie no, ɔnoaa nketia soup kɛse bi, na ɔde too hɔ ma kurekwase no; ɔhyɛɛ nsuo na ɔde too hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ne yere hɔ kɔdii aduane. Berɛ a owie didi no, ɔkɔɔ kwan ho kɔtwee gyedua bi ase. Ɛnna abɔfo no yawu, Fly, kɔhunuu aduane no (a wɔde asiesie ma kurekwase no), na ɔfaa no kɔtoo ceiling no so. Kurekwase no firi kwan ho baa fie na ɔkɔm kura wɔn. Lick-lick see efiefo no, "Mo ahu nea yɛayɛ yɛn panin no, nanso deda yi, ɔmmpɛ sɛ ɔma yɛn aduane bi na yɛdi, enti ma yɛnkɔye wo." Wɔgyee no mma, "Wonnɔ, wonnɔ." Lick-lick kɔɔ kwan ho kɔnnum abɔfo no nipadua, na ɔne abɔfo no bɔɔ ayɛ; na Sniff-sniff baa hɔ nso; na ɔhwen abɔfo no nipadua; na ɔnso kɔɔ. Ɛnna Tie-in-knots baa hɔ kɔkyekyere ne kɔn, na Gulp-down nso baa hɔ kɔtoo no. Ɛnna kurekwase no san kɔɔ fie; wɔrepɛ sɛ wɔhwɛ pato no mu, na wɔhunuu soup nsuo a ɛatɔ hɔ. Wɔsee, "Fly, ebia abɔfo no gyaa aduane ma yɛn na woadi?" Fly see, "Mennhunu bi baabiara." Sniff-sniff, berɛ a ɔhwee ne ani no, ɔhunuu aduane no a wɔde asiee hɔ (ceiling no so). Ɔsee, "O, agya! yɛakyll abɔfo no efiri hwee, efisɛ nokwarene aduane a ɔde asiee ma yɛn wɔ ha, na ɛnyɛ baako, na ɛnyɛ mmienu, Fly na ɔfaa no kɔtoo hɔ." Fly nyantee hɔ kɔɔ. Ɛno nti, berɛ a kurekwase bi te hɔ, na fly bi tɔ ne so no, ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkyere no.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
