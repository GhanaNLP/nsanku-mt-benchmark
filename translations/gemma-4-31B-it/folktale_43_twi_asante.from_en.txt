Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea ɛyɛɛ koraa sɛ sɛ Nwansana, mmoa no, tɔ Kraman, kraman no so a, ɔpɛɛ no, kam! kam!

ƆHUNIKANNIKAN bi wɔ kraman anan; baako din de Sniff-sniff; baako din de Lick-lick; baako din de Tie-in-knots; na baako din de Gulp-down. Ɔyɛɛ ne adwuma ne ne kraman no. Da biara, sɛ ɔkɔ kwaa mu a, ɔne wɔn kɔ bɔkɔɔ. Da koro bi, ɔkɔɔ kwaa mu na ɔhunuu kola-dua bi; nnuaba pii a ɛatwa n’atwa a ɛsi mmoa mmoa te sɛ eyi hyɛɛ no. Ɛna ɔsan kɔɔ fie, na ɔka amanneɛ no kyerɛɛ ne yere, ɔsee no sɛ, "Kola-dua bi a meahu no, aba a ɛyɛ pii atwa mu a ɛreka ase sɛnea ɛte sɛ eyi." Ne yere see no sɛ, "Kɔfa wo ho, efisɛ wuhu sɛ yɛnni kola-aba baako koraa a yɛbɛnam so anaa bi a yɛbɛtɔn." Ɔyadeɛfoɔ no faa kwan no hyɛɛ ase, nanso ɔgyaa akurefo no wɔ fie, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a kola-dua no wɔ. Ɛna ɔtɔɔ so, na ɔpue kola nnuane bi. Ɛna ɔtee nnema firi hɔ, "Twa! be! kukuru! kukuru! hu! pi!" Sasabonsam bi gyinaa ne anim. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Hwan na wahwee me kola nut?" Abɔdeɛfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Me." Sasabonsam no kaa sɛ, "Mɛyɛ wo yum mprempren ara ɛnna nea woayɛ yi nti." Abɔdeɛfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wopɛ sɛ woyɛ me yum a, ma me nso nkasa me kraman." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Frɛ hɔ, nanso sɛ woafrɛ wɔn mprenu mmiɛnsa na wannim a, ɛnna mɛyɛ wo yum." Ɛnna abɔdeɛfoɔ no gyaeɛ (ne nne) :

Hwɛ-hwɛ, bra na yɛnhyia, Nam-nam, bra na yɛnhyia, Kyekyere-kyekyere, bra na yɛnhyia, Wen-wen, bra na yɛnhyia.

Na kraman a wɔwɔ fie no tee no kakra paa, na wɔtuu kwan kɔ town no anyinamu. Ɛnnɛ na ɔyadeɛfoɔ no tiee (ne nne) bio:

“Sniff-sniff, bra na yɛnhyia, Lick-lick, bra na yɛnhyia, Tie-in-knots, bra na yɛnhyia, Gulp-down, bra na yɛnhyia.

Saa na kraman no ahu; wɔn tutuu, prada! prada! Sasabonsam no see, “Frɛ bio, na ɛno na ɛyɛ teesaa.” Odwumfoɔ no gyaeɛ (ne nne) den nnɛn:

“Sniff-sniff, bra na yɛnhyia, Lick-lick, bra na yɛnhyia, Tie-in-knots, bra na yɛnhyia, Gulp-down, bra na yɛnhyia.

Wai, wai, wai, wɔretwa mmerɛ pii ansa na wɔabɛ! Ne nsa anka nka, na kurekwase no agyina ne anim. Abɔdeɛfoɔ no see, "Sniff-sniff, Sasabonsam bi wɔ ha, hwen ho." Na Sniff-sniff hwen ho. Ɔsee, "Lick-lick, nnum no nyinaa." Ɔsee, "Tie-in-knots, kyekyere no." Tie-in-knots kyekyere ne kɔn; na Sasabonsam no tɔree hɔ, kum! Ɛna Gulp-down faa no kɔɔ na ɔhwee no. Abɔdeɛfoɔ ne kureyɛfoɔ no boaa kola nutu no ano na wɔde kɔɔ fie. Berɛ a abɔdeɛfoɔ no san baa no, ɔbaa hɔ na ɔnoaa nketia soup kɛse, na ɔde gyinaa hɔ ma kureyɛfoɔ no; ɔhyɛɛ nsuo na ɔde guu hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ne yere hɔ kɔdee aduane. Sɛ ɔwie edi no, ɔkɔɔ kwan ho kɔtee gyedua bi ase. Ɛnnɛ, ɔyadeɛ-kyini no aboa, Fly, kɔhunuu aduane no (a wɔasiesie ama akurekwase no), na ɔyɛɛ no kɔtoo ɔdan no atabea. Akurekwase no firi kwan ho baa mu na kɔm kura hɔ. Lick-lick kaa kyerɛɛ efofor no sɛ, "Yɛahu nea yɛayɛ ama yɛn panin no, nanso deda seesei, ɔnma yɛn aduane bi koraa na yɛnni, enti ma yɛnkɔ nkwa no." Wɔn hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, "Wonnɔ, wonnɔ." Lick-lick kɔɔ kwan ho na ɔnam abɔfo no nipadua no so; na ɔne abɔfo no nobɔɔ; na Sniff-sniff baa nso; na ɔhwenhwɛɛ abɔfo no nipadua no; na ɔnso kɔɔ. Ɛnna Tie-in-knots baeɛ ansa na wakyea ne kɔn mu koraa, na Gulp-down nso baeɛ na wenuam no koraa. Afei kɛseɛ no, akurefo no san kɔɔ fie; wɔrepɛ sɛ wɔhwɛ pato no mu, na wɔhuu soup nsuu a ɛatɔ hɔ. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Kɔhwehwɛ, ebia abɔfo no gyatɔ aduane akum na woadi?" Mpire kaa sɛ, "Mihunu bi baabiara." Sniff-sniff, berɛ a ɔwoo n'ani kɔ soro no, ɔhunuu aduane a wɔde asie hɔ (soro). Ɔkaa sɛ, "O, agya! yɛawu yɛn panin no mfomsoɔ, efisɛ nokwarene aduane a ɔde asiesie yɛn no wɔ hɔ, na ɛnyɛ baako, na ɛnyɛ mmienu, Fly ne onnimyɛ a ɔfaa no kɔtoo hɔ." Fly no tutu fii hɔ na ɔkɔɔ. Ɛno nti, sɛ kraman bi tena hɔ, na fly bi tɔ ne so a, ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkyere no.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
