Sɛnea Oman a wɔn ayɛ ananmufoɔ no abue wɔn ho sɛ, wɔbɛyɛ adwuma ne Mmomrebefoɔ (United Nations) mma obiara nnyini anidie na wadi nkyem wɔ nnipa ho ho kwan ne mfitiaseɛ gyefuoɔ ho,

Sɛnea eyi ne nkyerɛase a ɛyɛ pɛ wɔ saa dwumadie yi mu, ɛno na ɛho hia kɛse paa ma saa bɔhyɛ yi nyinaa ayɛ adwuma,

Hyɛ Onyame nkyɛn sɛ Onyame Nkyɛnea a ɛfa Nnipa Ho a ɛyɛ kɛse yi yɛ kwan a nnipa nyinaa ne aman nyinaa bɛfa so adi nkoso, na obiara ne nnwuma nyinaa a ɛwɔ amansan mu, a wɔde saa Nkyɛnea yi hyɛ wɔn adwene mu berɛtɛm, bɛyɛ biakoyɛ wɔ adesua ne nkyerɛkyerɛ mu de akanyan saa ho obu ne nkwa a ɛwɔ mu, na wɔde akwan a ɛrekɔ anim, wɔ man mu ne aman nyinaa mu, akwakyere sɛ wɔbɛgye saa ho nyinaa na wɔadi wɔn ho nyansa, wɔ Ananmu Aman a wɔyɛ adi a wɔn mu nnipa ne nnipa a wɔwɔ mman a saa aman no wɔ wɔn ho.

Kasa 1

Nnipa nyinaa wuo a wɔwoa hɔ yɛ nidi ne ho hia mu nyinaa yɛ pɛ na wɔwoa hɔ a wɔyɛ nkwa. Wɔama wɔn nyansa ne animuonyam, na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔyɛ nneɛma a ɛyɛ pa ma wɔn ho wɔ anuanom akokoɔduru mu.

Kasa 2

Obiara wɔ ho kwan kɔn nkyɛn dwumadie ne ahofama nyinaa a wɔatwerɛ wɔ saa Declaration yi mu, efisɛ obiara nyɛ ne ho nyansa, sɛ ɛyɛ nnipa mmofra, animu nnuane, mmeae, kasa, gyidi, mpanyimfo anaa nsusuwee foforo, aban anaa amansan a obi firi mu, ahodeɛ, awufo anaa anya mmofra.

Bio, obiara nni hwee a ɛkyerɛ sɛ wɔbɛyɛ n'anim nka wɔ mfitiaseɛ a ɛfa man anaa Oman hantan a obi wɔ hɔ, sɛ ɛyɛ man a ɛgyina ne ho ara, anaa ɛyɛ man a obi hwɛ no, anaa man a ɛnni ne ho gyinaeɛ, anaa obiara a ɔwɔ man a wɔatwa ne ho mfitiaseɛ biara.

Kasa 3

Obiara wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔbɛtena ase, ɔbɛnya anigye ne ne ho ahodenom.

Kasa 4

Obiara nnimma nnyɛ ɔkoma anaa ɔsrɛfo; ɔkoma ne ɔsrɛfo nnwomayɛ no nyinaa bɛyɛ ade a ɛyɛ mfomso wɔ ne nyinaa mu.

Kasa 5

Obiara nnimma sɛ wɔbɛyɛ no ya anaa wɔbɛma no nneyɛe a ɛyɛ huhu, anaa ɛnyɛ onipa asɛm, anaa ɛyɛ aniwuo.

Kasa 6

Obiara wɔ ho kwan sɛ wɔbɛgye no ase wɔ baabiara sɛ onipa bi wɔ mmara anim.

Kasa 7

obiara yɛ pɛ wɔ mmara anim na obiara wɔ ho kwan, a obiara nni n'anim, sɛ ɔbɛnya mmara mu bohyɛ a ɛyɛ pɛ. Obiara wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔbɛnya bohyɛ a ɛyɛ pɛ atwa nkyɛn obiara a ɔyɛ n'anim a ɛsi saa Declaration yi ho na ɔbɛnya bohyɛ atwa nkyɛn obiara a ɔka asɛm a ɛkyerɛ sɛ wɔnnyɛ obiara pɛ.

Kasa 8

Obiara wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔbɛnya mmuae a ɛyɛ adwuma firi anansesem mpanyimfo a wɔwɔ man no mu ma nneɛma a ɛkyiri anaa ɛboaboa ne ho a ɛkyiri ne nkyerɛwee a constitution anaa mmara no ma no.

Kasa 9

Obiara nnim sɛ wɔbɛkyere no, bɛhyɛ no fie anaa wɔbɛpue no fi man mu wɔ kwan a ɛnyɛ pɛ so.

Kasa 10

Obiara wɔ ho kwan pɛpɛpɛ sɛ ɔbɛnya nsiesie a ɛyɛ pɛ na ɛyɛ mmofra anaa nnipa nyinaa anim wɔ adansefo a wɔnyɛ obi ayaresɛ na wɔnni obi anim wɔ ne ho nyinaa, berɛ a wɔregyina ne ho ama wɔahwehwɛ ne ho kwan ne ne woyɛ ne ne ho asɛm bi a wɔasɛn no.

Kasa 11

Obiara a wɔasɛksɛkse no wɔ mfomso bi a ɛfa mmara ho wɔ ho ho kwan sɛ wɔagye no sɛ ɔyɛ ɔdenkumi kosi sɛ wɔapaw ne ho sɛ ɔyɛ onipa a ɔyɛ bɔne wɔ mmara mu wɔ amansan anim nsiesie bi a wanya nneɛma nyinaa a ɛhia ma ne ho bofo.

Obiara nnim mfaso anaa anigye biara wɔ nneɛma a ɔyɛe anaa nneɛma a ɔammɛyɛ a ɛnnim mfaso anaa anigye biara, wɔ man no mmara anaa kuromma mmara mu, wɔ berɛ a ɔyɛe no. Afei, ɛnnkyɛ nkabom a ɛyɛ den sen ne a ɛwɔ hɔ wɔ berɛ a mfaso anaa anigye no bae no.

Kasa 12

Obiara nnim sɛ wɔbɛyɛ ne ho anaa ne abusua, ne fie anaa ne krataa nkyerɛwee ho nnyinaso a ɛnyɛ kwan. Afei, obiara nnim sɛ wɔbɛyɛ ne din ne ne animuonyam ho nnyinaso. Obiara wɔ ho kwan sɛ mmara bɛbo no anim firi saa nnyinaso anaa nnyinaso a ɛyɛ saa nyinaa mu.

Kasa 13

Obiara wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔbɛtane ne ho anaa ɔbɛtena baabiara wɔ Ɔman biara mpasua mu.

Obiara wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔbɛfi man biara mu afi, efisɛ ne man ankasa ho mpo, na ɔbɛsan aba ne man mu.

Kasa 14

Obiara wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔbɛhwehwɛ na ɔanya nsiesiebea wɔ afoforo man mu efisɛ ɔyɛ amanehunu.

Yɛntumi mmpɛ mmarikyire yi wɔ nneɛma a ɛmfiri mpɔtam political asɛm anaa nneɛma a ɛkyerɛ United Nations nsonsom ne ne nhyira.

Kasa 15

Obiara wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔbɛnya manyɛ.

Obiara nnim sɛ wɔbɛyi ne man-manmu firi ne nsam amanehunu anaa wɔadeɛ ne ho kwan a ɔwɔ sɛ ɔsesa ne man-manmu.

Kasa 16

Mmarima ne mmaa a wɔawu mmofra, a obiara nni nkyekyem wɔ amammere, man anaa agyapadeɛ mu, wɔwɔ ho kwan sɛ wɔbɛayɔyɔɛ na wɔanya abusua. Wɔwɔ ho kwan kwan koro pɛ wɔ ayɔyɔɛ mu, berɛ a wɔwɔ ayɔyɔɛ mu, ne berɛ a ayɔyɔɛ no pue.

Awɔbra mu ayɔko bɛyɛ a, ɛsɛ sɛ nnipa a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔyɔ ayɔko no nyinaa pɛ adeɛ no pɛpɛɛpɛ.

Abusua ne jamu a ɛyɛ mfitiaseɛ ne nea ɛho hia paa wɔ amansan mu, na ɛsɛ sɛ amansan ne Ɔman bɔ ho ban.

Kasa 17

Obiara wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔbɛgye nsamanyɛ anaa nnoɔma ne no nko ara anaa ne afoforɛ.

Obiara nnim sɛ wɔbɛyi ne nneɛma fii ne nsam amanehunu.

Kasa 18

Obiara wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔbɛyɛ ne sumsum, ne dwumadie, ne nsigye; saa kwan yi bi ne sɛ ɔbɛtumi asesa ne nsigye anaa ne gyidi, na ɔbɛtumi, sɛ ɔno nko anaa ne afofor ne ne ho, wɔ amansan mu anaa wɔ asɛm a ɛyɛ koro mu, ama ne nsigye anaa ne gyidi anyɛ adwuma wɔ ɔkyerɛkyerɛ, dwumadie, som, ne amanneɛ mu.

Kasa 19

Obiara wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔbɛka ne nsusuw nsusuw ne ne nteasem; saa kwan yi bi ne sɛ obi nni hɔ a ɔbɛka ne nsusuw nsusuw mu, na ɔbɛhwehwɛ, ɔbɛnya, na ɔbɛkyerɛ nsɛm ne nsusuw nsusuw mmoa wɔ kwan biara so, ɛnna ɛmpɛ nkyerɛwee biara.

Kasa 20

Obiara wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔbɛhyia ne nnamfo anaa ɔbɛyɛ kuw wɔ asomdwoe mu.

Obiara nnimyɛ ne ho sɛ ɔbɛyɛ kuw bi member.

Kasa 21

Obiara wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔbɛgye tin wɔ ne man mpaninfoɔ adwuma mu, anaa ɔbɛyɛ saa mpo wɔ nnipa a ɔno ara apaw wɔ adwene a ɛyɛ kɔkɔɔ mu.

Obiara wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔbɛnya akwan pɛpɛpɛ a ɛkyerɛ sɛ ɔbɛtumi anya nnwuma a ɔman no yɛ ma obiara wɔ ne man mu.

Nnipa nyinaa pɛpɛm no na ɛbɛyɛ aban no tumi kwan; saa pɛpɛm yi nso bɛba mmofra ne mpaninfoɔ nyinaa a wɔatwa mfifire a ɛyɛ nokware ne a ɛba mmerɛ a wɔapae ho, na ɛbɛyɛ kwan a obiara bɛtumi atwa mfifire a ɛyɛ pɛ, na wɔbɛyɛ no wɔ ahokyerɛ mu anaa kwan foforo a ɛyɛ efie.

Kasa 23

Obiara wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔbɛyɛ adwuma, sɛ ɔbɛpa adwuma a ɔpɛ, sɛ ɔbɛnya adwumayɛbea a ɛyɛ pɛ a ɛyɛ fɛ, ne sɛ ɔbɛnya gyomfa a ɛbɛkyerɛ no fi adwumadenyi mu.

Obiara, a obiara nni n'anim, wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔno nso nnya sika a ɛte sɛ obi a ɔyɛ adwuma a ɛte sɛ neyɛ no.

Obiara a ɔyɛ adwuma wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔnyɛ nea ɛfata na ɛyɛ fɛ ma no, na ɛboa no ne ne abusua ma wɔanya asetena a ɛyɛ anigye ne anidie, na sɛ ɛhia a, obi mmoa no wɔ ɔman mu.

Obiara wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔbɛhyɛ adwumayɛfoɔ nkrom mu anaa ɔbɛyɛ member wɔ mu ama ne ho nni awu.

Kasa 24

Obiara wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔbɛgye ahyɛaseɛ ne ahomebo, efisɛ ɛka ho sɛ wɔbɛyɛ adwuma nnwuma a ɛyɛ mmerɛ a ɛfata ne sɛ ɔbɛgye nkyɛn nwiase a ɔbɛnya sika ho.

Kasa 25

Obiara wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔbɛnya asetena a ɛyɛ fɛ a ɛbɛma ne ho ne ne abusua ahofuo anya ahoɔden ne ahomgye, efisɛ eyi bi ne aduane, ntade, fie, aduru ne mmoa a ɔhia firi nnigyea mu, ne kwan a ɔwɔ sɛ ɔbɛnya ahobammɔ sɛ ɔyɛ adwumadenyɛ a ɔnni adwuma, anaa ɔyɛ yadeɛ, anaa ɔyɛ okuramfo, anaa ɔyɛ ɔwiduo, anaa ɔyɛ akorafo, anaa biribiara a ɛma ɔnni anigyeɛ wɔ nneɛma a ɔnnim sɛnea ɔbɛyɛ ho biribi.

Mmaa a wɔwɔ mma ne mmofra hia aduane ne mmoa pii. Mmofra nyinaa, ɛnna wɔwoa wɔ ayawuo mu anaa wɔ ayawuo mfitiaseɛ mu, nyia obiara nnyina hɔ a ɔno nso bɛnya saa ahobammɔ yie no.

Suaade no bɛyɛ adwuma sɛ ɛbɛma onipa animdeɛ nyinaa anyini yiye na ɛbɛhyɛ nnipa ho ho ho kwan ne mfitiaseɛ tumi ho obu den. Ɛbɛma nteaseɛ, abotare ne adamfo adwene anyini wɔ aman nyinaa, amammere anaa agyapadeɛ nkukuu mu, na ɛbɛboa United Nations adwuma a wɔyɛ ma asomdwoeɛ gyina hɔ.

Kasa 27

Obiara wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔbɛgye gyi kurom no amammere mu nyinaa, ɔbɛdi adwene ne nsɛm a ɛyɛ fɛ ho anigye, na ɔbɛkyɛ nyansahu mu animdeɛ ne ne mfa ho nneɛma.

Obiara wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔbɛnya awodaaden ma ne nneɛma a ɛfa ne honam ne ne nneɛma ho a ɛfiri adwuma biara a ɔyɛ wɔ nyansahu, nwoma anaa adwene-yɛ mu a ɔno ne ne dwumayɛfoɔ.

Kasa 28

Obiara wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔbɛnya mmarima ne mpanyimfo a wɔn mmoa ne wɔn nneyɛe bɛma saa Declaration yi mu ho ho dwumadie ne kwan a wɔde hyɛ nkwa mu nyinaa ayɛ adeɛ a ɛyɛ den.

Kasa 29

Obiara wɔ adwuma a ɔsɛ sɛ ɔyɛ ama ne kurom, na ɛhɔnko ara na obi betumi anya anigyeɛ kɛseɛ na ne ho anyɛ den yie.

Wɔ ne ho nyinaa ho kwan ne ne tumi mu, obiara nni hwee a ɛsɔre ne kwan a mmara mpɛ a, na ɛyɛ sɛ wɔbɛtumi ahwɛ so sɛ obiara nya ne kwan ne ne tumi a ɛfata, na wɔbɛtumi ahwɛ so sɛ obiara bu obi, na yɛbɛtumi asɔ mfaso a ɛfata mmaratumi, nnipa nyinaa asomdwoe ne mfaso a ɛyɛ pa wɔ demokratik somdwee mu.

Ɛnyɛ mmerɛ biara na wɔbɛtumi ayɛ saa ho ho hia ne ahofama yi wɔ akwan a ɛyɛ nneɛma a United Nations hwehwɛ ne ne nhyira ho nsɛm.

Kasa 30

Sɛnea wɔatwerɛ saa Declaration yi mu no, obiara nnim sɛ ɛkyerɛ sɛ Ɛkuo, group anaa onipa biara wɔ ho kwan sɛ ɔyɛ adeɛ bi anaa ɔyɛ dwumadi bi a ɛbɛtumi asɛe dwumadie ne kwan bi a wɔatwerɛ wɔ ha.
