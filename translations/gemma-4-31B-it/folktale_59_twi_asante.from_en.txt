Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea ɛyɛɛ koraa sɛ 'KRAMAN, kraman no, ntumi nnya awie wɔ ne ɔkɔkɔn mu'

Na obiara nim Kraman bi, na ama n'asorew mpaboa nti, ɔammepii obiara (ne kuro mu) a ɔbɛyɛ ne yere. Enti ɔkɔɔ kurow bi mu kɔhwehwɛɛ ɔbaa bi a ɔbɛyɛ ne yere. (Ɔwaree, na ɔde no baa ne kuro.) Wɔnomu tenaa hɔ (beaou), a ne yere no kɔɔ ne kuro. Akyi no, ne kunu noTwe kɔtwee no sɛ ɔreba. Ne yere papa no faa ne gun kɔɔ kwae kɔhwee kɔkɔɔ kɔkɔɔ bi ama Kraman no mma ɔreba. Nnansa akyi no, Kraman no kɔɔ hɔ, na berɛ a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, ɔhunuu sɛ wɔde kɔkɔɔ nam guu gya so wɔ akyire. Anadwo berɛ a ɛbaa no, ɔne ne yere kɔdaaa. Kraman no pɛɛ sɛ ɔkɔ akyire kɔyɛ ne nsuo, na ɔhunuu nam no a ne nnamyɛ repon kɔ gya no mu. Kraman no san kɔdaaa, na ɔde ne ti too hua so (na ɔdae) na ɔhwɛɛ nam no mmae. Afei nam no nka baa ne nsa; ɔyɛɛ biara sɛ ɔbɛtena mu, nanso ɔantumi. Oh! ɔsɔree kɔɔ baabi a nam no hyɛe; ɔnamyɛɛ no nyinaa, na ɔsan kɔdaaa. Anɔpa a ɛkyee no, berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ hwee, berɛ a ne yere no repɛ sɛ ɔpue hɔ, ɔhwehwɛɛ nam no, nanso nam biara nnim hɔ. Ne yere no see, "Oh, maame! oh, maame! obi anyɛ nam no nyinaa." Berɛ a Kraman no tee eyi no, ne ani tɔɔ (kyerɛ sɛ ɔyɛɛ anigye), na ɔfaa ne ntoma hwee ne ti, na ɔdae hɔ yɛɛ komm. Afei mpofirim no, ɔsɔree na ɔfaa kwan no; ɔdwene kɔɔ ne kuro, na ɔkae amanneɛ no, sɛnea ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔnamyɛɛ ne yere papa nam no, na sɛnea ne ani tɔɔ anigye nti, na ɛno nti, ɔrekɔ hwehwɛ obi a ɔwɔ asorew aduro, na ama wɔnma no bi. Nnipa no see, "Adanko, ɔKraman no, wɔnna aduro a ɛyɛ den paa, den paa, den paa." Kraman no faa kwan no, na ɔyɛɛ ntɛm kɔduu ɔKraman no kuro. Ɔsee, "Papa Kraman, meba wo nkyɛn sɛ mɛhwehwɛ mmoa; meyɛ asorewfoɔ, asorewfoɔ, asorewfoɔ, na wɔse wowɔ aduro (a ɛyɛ nnawu) ma eyi, enti mesrɛ wo, ma me bi, na sika a ɛbɛyɛ ne bo, mɛsoa." ɔKraman no gyee no mmuae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nokware sɛ mewɔ bi, enti kɔfa nnansa nam baa me."

Kraman no kɔɔ kuro bi mu, na ɔtɔɔ nan koro a ɛyɛ akyi nan wɔ domafa sika mfutuma mu (ɛne 3s. 6d.) na ɔde baa. ɔKraman-kɔkɔɔ no noaa no, na ɔtwaa no, na ɔde hyɛɛ nnuaba nketewa so, na ɔtɔɔ mako, ne nnuane, ne nkraman, na ɔde tɔɔ nam no mu, na ɔde guu gya so. Berɛ a gya no kae no, nnipa kakra akyi no, ɔteee nka sɛ ɛrepopopoo, hyee, e e e! Kraman no hwɛɛ no mmerɛ pii. Ɛnnɛ ɔKraman-kɔkɔɔ no see no sɛ, "Kraman, kɔda adan mu koro, na me nso mɛkɔ ada ebi foforo mu, na ɔkyena anɔpa, mɛfa saa nam yi afi (gya no so) na mɛyɛ aduro ama wo." Kraman no kɔdae. ɔKraman-kɔkɔɔ no nso kɔdae. Enti Kraman no dae, ɔdae (nanso ɔantumi anka), na nam no nka baa ne nose mu, nanso ɔankasa. Afei ɔtee nka bio. Ɔsee no sɛ, "Papa ɔKraman-kɔkɔɔ." ɔKraman-kɔkɔɔ no gyee me answers sɛ, "Aane!" Ɔsee no sɛ, "Mmoa nketewa (mice) wɔ wo kuro mu, enti mɛkɔfa nam no na mɛde aba me nkyɛn." ɔKraman-kɔkɔɔ no see no sɛ, "Kɔfa; m'ani arayɛ mmu sɛ mɛka ne so ɔkyena, na matumi ayɛ aduro ama wo." Enti Kraman no kɔfaa no. Afei Kraman no da hɔ. Ɔsee no sɛ, "Papa ɔKraman-kɔkɔɔ." Ɔgyee me answers sɛ, "Aane!" Ɔsee no sɛ, "Mmoa nketewa no bɛnam so, enti mɛde hyɛ me mpaaso akyi, na mɛkɔ ada." ɔKraman-kɔkɔɔ no see no sɛ, "Deɛ m'ani arayɛ mmu ne sɛ ɔkyena, berɛ a nneɛma bɛyɛ kɛse, mɛyɛ aduro ama wo." Kraman no dae bio; ɛmfa sɛ ɔyɛɛ dɛn, ɔantumi anka ne ho. Ɔsee no sɛ, "Papa ɔKraman-kɔkɔɔ, saa mmoa nketewa yi aba me mpaaso akyi, enti mɛfa nam no mɛde tohye me ti so, na mɛgyina hɔ kosi sɛ nneɛma bɛyɛ kɛse." ɔKraman-kɔkɔɔ no gyee me answers sɛ, "O, meka kɔkɔɔ sɛ, deɛ m'ani arayɛ mmu ne sɛ mɛnya (nam no) ɔkyena na mɛyɛ aduro ama wo." Kraman no faa nam no tohye ne ti so, na ɔgyinaa hɔ. Ɛna nsuo kakra firi nam no mu tɔɔ ne yadeɛ so, na ɔnomee. Ɔsee no sɛ, "O, Papa ɔKraman-kɔkɔɔ, saa nam yi ho, saa mmoa nketewa no akɔ me so, na wɔaba me ti so, enti mɛde (nam no) hyɛ me anom." ɔKraman-kɔkɔɔ no gyee me answers sɛ, "Oho o! Mekaa sɛ ɔkyena, berɛ a nneɛma bɛyɛ kɛse, mɛyɛ aduro ama wo." Afei ɛhyɛɛ ne anom mu den, na ɔnam adan no mu mpɔtɔmpɔtɔ; ɔhwee ne ntadeɛ (ne nsa) (ne nne bɔɔ), fre! fre! fre! Ɔse, "Agya Bosomfo, ma me nnyɛ wo ayaresɛm wɔ ei nam yi ho." Bosomfo no aseɛ sɛ, "Adɛn nti na wopɛ sɛ woyɛ me ayaresɛm ho?" Kraman no hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, "Me pa wo kyɛw, sɛn na mma saa mmoa nketese yi nni no, ma me ntɔ fi wo." Bosomfo no se, "Aane, ɛnyɛ saa! Ma ɔkyena anɔpa nnyɛ." O! nanso seesei Kraman no ma (nam no) kɔɔ (ne kɔn mu). Ɛnnɛ na ɔtɔɔ fam na ɔdae. Anɔpa a ɛbae no, anɔpa paa, Bosomfo no se, "Kraman, fa saa nam no mma me." Kraman no hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, "Ho, Agya Bosomfo, adɛn nti, woante nea mekae kyerɛɛ wo nnawku no sɛ, mɛyɛ wo ayaresɛm (wɔ nam no ho)?" Bosomfo no hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, "Enti woadi no?" Kraman no se, "Aane! Madi no, efisɛ matɔ no." Bosomfo no se, "Wo, Kraman, 'owu' a wɔfrɛ no 'owu' no rennya wo kwan koraa."

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
