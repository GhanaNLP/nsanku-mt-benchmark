SƐ OBIAA YƐ WO ADƆ A, KYERƐ NE NKAWSOƆ DEN BYE NO ADƆ NSO.

Wɔka sɛ (tete bi) na Okurukru bi wɔ hɔ, na wɔ ne nante-ntee mu no, ɔkɔhyia ɔbaa paapae bi a ne nan so yɛ yare. Na Okurukru no ka sɛ, "Me Yehowa! Saa yare yi yɛ eyie; nea ɛte saa yi, ɛmfa sɛ woyɛ dɛn, wotumi nante?" Ɔbaa paapae no ka sɛ, "O, mimi kakra." Okurukru no ka sɛ, "Mo nnipa, nnɛ yi, sɛ meyɛ ade pa bi ma mo a, ɔkyena mobɛma me ade bɔne sɛ mo da m'ase." Ɔbaa paapae no ka sɛ, "O, mentumi mɛyɛ (saa)." Okurukru no ka sɛ, "Sɛ wotumi mma wo ho nni saa, a meate ase." Ɔka sɛ, "Tumi wo ani na fa." Na ɔbaa paapae no tumi ne ani na ɔfa. Okurukru no ka sɛ, "Hwɛ wo yare no." Na berɛ a ɔbaa paapae no kɔhwɛ no—ade biara nni hɔ. Afei ɔtumi ne ani bio; ɔfa hɔ, na ɔhunu sɛ wɔatwa asase no nyinaa. Ɔka sɛ, "Tumi bio." Ɔtumi hɔ, na ɔfa hɔ, na afie gyina hɔ, dennen. Na ɔtumi hɔ bio. Ɔfa hɔ na ɔhunu kuropɔn bi; ɛhɔ na ɛwɔ—ekɛse paa. Okurukru no ka sɛ, "Ɔbaa paapae, ɛyɛ wo de (lit. ɛwɔ wo nsam)." Ɔbaa paapae no ka sɛ, "Me da wo ase." Afei ɔbaa paapae no bisaa sɛ, "Dɛn na mɛma wo sɛ mɛda wo ase?" Okurukru no ka sɛ, "Mempɛ ade ketewa biara, m'ani agyigye ne saa Odua-Kese a ɛgyina hɔ yi." Ɔbaa paapae no ka sɛ, "Saa ade yi (a wopɛ) no, fa." Afei Okurukru no tuu kɔ, na ɔtɔɔ so, na ɔyɛɛ n'atutuo, na ɔtɔɔ nnuaba mmienu, na ɔde hyɛɛ mu. Na ɔtwaa nnuaba mmienu no, na ɔtuu kɔhwehwɛ ade a ne mma bɛdi. Ɔbaa paapae no nananom, a ɔtena ne nkyɛn no, hyɛɛ asei sɛ, "Ehe! Ehe!" Ɔbaa paapae no bisaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛrekɔ so?" Ɔka sɛ, "Ma me nnunumi Okurukru mma." Ɔbaa paapae no bisaa sɛ, "Ɛhe na mɛnya Okurukru mma?" Nananom ketewa no hyɛɛ asei bio, ehe! ehe! Ɔbaa paapae no bisaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛrekɔ so?" Abofra no ka sɛ, "Ma me nnunumi Okurukru mma, efisɛ sɛ mianya bi a mɛnnunumi a, mɛwu." Ɔbaa paapae no ka sɛ, "Ah! Me nananom yi mɛwu efisɛ Okurukru mma nni hɔ a ɔnnunumi? Sɔre, fa apɔm na kɔpira Odua-Kese no na fa Okurukru mma no ba ma me." Kurofo no kɔɔ hɔ, (apɔm no bɔɔ), Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! Ɛte sɛ sɛ dua no repɔ, na ɛhɔ na Okurukru mma no mu a ɔyɛ kɛse no bɔɔ ne ho, na ɔba gyinaa atutuo no ano, na ɔteew n'asɛm; ɔfrɛɛ ne maame, ɔka sɛ:

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, anomaa no, Okuruku ba!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔdee adidi a, ɔnnyia mma me!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, o! o!

Na naayewa no tee sɛ ne ba resu; ɔsɔree, (na ne ntaban nnom no yɛ,) fa! Ɔbaa hɔ; ɔse, "Sanguri," na ɛdua no (a na wɔapae no koraa) yɛɛ baako bio, na nnipa nyinaa a na wɔrebɔ no no, na wɔwen hɔ. Ɛnna ɔfaa aduane a ɔde bae no maa ne mma. Afei ɔgyee wɔn ase, ɔse, "Merekɔ, sɛ ɔbaa panin no ba sɛ ɔbɛfa mo a, ma ɔnfa mo nkɔ." Na ɔbaa panin no se, "Kɔbɔ ɛdua no na monde nnoɔma no mma me nananom ba no nnam." Enti wɔkɔɔ hɔ mprempren, Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! Ɛyɛ sɛ ɛdua no bɛtɔ asase so, ɛnna (Okuafo no) ba no puee na ɔbaa ɔgyina mpaabo no nkyɛn, na ɔfrɛ ne na, ɔse:

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, anomaa no, Okuruku ba!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔdee adidi a, ɔnnyia mma me!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, o! o!

Ɔfrɛɛ ne maame, ɔfrɛe, ɔfrɛe, ɔfrɛe—biara onyim—na seesei dua no kasae berɛ a ɔtɔɔ fam no, "Brim!" (ɔse). Wɔfaa Okuafo no mma no; wɔmaa baako ma ɔbaa sankofa no, nanso baako a ɔtena hɔ no tuu kwan kɔ na ɔtɔɔ Wawa-dua bi so. Baako, wɔpuee no, na wɔmaa ɔbaa sankofa no nananom, na ɔde hyɛɛ plantain a ɔpuee no mu (a ɔrewe). Ankyɛ koraa no, Okuafo no bae. Berɛ a ɔduu dua a wɔtwae no hɔ no, ɔhunuu ne mma baako a ɔtena hɔ; ɔbisae ne ho sɛ dea ɛsi, na ɔkae amanneɛ nyinaa kyerɛɛ no. Okuafo no faa kwan kɔɔ ɔbaa sankofa no akuraa mu. Berɛ a ɔduu hɔ no, ɔbaa sankofa no nananom rewe ne mma baako. Ɔse, "Ɔbaa sankofa, me hyɛ wo ase." Ɔfii ɔbaa sankofa no fie mu na ɔhyɛɛ (ne yadeɛ) ase wɔ kurom mfimfa hɔ. Ɔse, "Sanguri," na obiara nyinaa pirae, na ɔse bio, "Sanguri," na fie biara nyinaa bɔe mpofirim na obiara angyina hɔ bio. "Sanguri," akuraa no yɛɛ nwedomfo bio. "Sanguri," na ɔbaa sankofa no mpaebɔ no bae. Na ɔse, "Ɔbaa sankofa, woahu, ɛno nti na mpanyimfo se, sɛ obi yɛ wo yie a, da no ase wɔ ne yie mu na mma m'anyɛ bɔne ma no." Me nsɛm a mika yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, moatumi agye baako sɛ nokware, na na akyerɛ no moatumi ayɛ me ayɛyɛ (ma me nsɛm yi).
