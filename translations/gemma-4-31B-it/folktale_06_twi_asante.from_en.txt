SƐ OBIAA YƐ WO ADƆ A, KYERƐ NE NKAWSOƆ DEN BYE NO ADƆ NSO.

Wɔka sɛ (teaeɛ bi) na Okuruku bi wɔn, na wɔ ne akwantu mu no, ɔkɔhyia ɔbaa sankofa bi a ne nan so yɛ ya. Na Okuruku no se, "Me Nyame! "Ei, eyi yɛ akopa a ɛyɛ soronko; sɛ eyi te saa a, ɛnka sɛ woyɛ biara, wotumi nanten?" Okrokrokwa no kaa sɛ, "O, mpo mpo." Akɔkɔsro no kaa sɛ, "Mo nnipa, nnɛ yi, sɛ meyɛ ade pa bi ama mo a, ɔkyena mobɛma me ade bɔne sɛ mobɛda me ase." Okrokrokwa no kaa sɛ, "O, mentumi mɛyɛ (saa)." Akɔkɔsro no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wotumi nnyina saa ara a, meate ase." Ɔseɛ, "Mu wo ani na fa wo ani." Na ɔbaa a ɔyɛu no muu ne ani na ɔfaa no. Eagle no seɛ, "Hwɛ wo akokoɔ no." Na berɛ a ɔbaa a ɔyɛu no baa sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ no—ɛnni hwee a ɛkyerɛ sɛ ɛwoaa hɔ. Ɛnna ɔmuu ne ani bio; ɔfaa no, na ɔhunuu sɛ wɔagye asase no nyinaa. Ɔseɛ, "Mu no bio." Ɔmuu no, na ɔfaa no, na afie gyinaa hɔ, dennen. Na ɔmaa wɔmu gyine bio. Ɔbuee mu kɛseɛ sɛ ɔbɛhwehwɛ kurom a ɛte saa; ɛhɔ na ɛwɔ—ɛyɛ kɛseɛ paa. Akɔkorɔfo no see no sɛ, "Krokoro, ɛyɛ wo de (lit. w’abrabɔ mu)." Okrokrokoro no see, "Me da wo ase." Afei okrokrokoro no see, "Dɛn na mɛma wo sɛ meda wo ase?" Okurukuru no see, "Me mpɛ hwee, m'ani agyina saa Onyina-dua a ɛgyina hɔ no." Okrokrokoro no see, "Saa adei (a wo bɔrɔfo) yi, fa." Afei Okurukuru no tuu kwan, na ɔtɔɔ so, na ɔyɛɛ mpaabo, na ɔtɔɔ nnuaba mmienu, deposit na ɔde wɔn hyɛɛ mu. Na ɔpuee nnuaba no mmienu no, na ɔkɔyɛɛ adeɛ a ne mma bɛdi. Onaa no nananom mma, a ɔtena ne nkyɛn no, hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔrehwene, "Ehe! Ehe!" Onaa no bisaa no sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛrekɔ on?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma me nnnam Akɔkɔdua mma." Onaa no bisaa sɛ, "Ɛhe na mɛnya Akɔkɔdua mma?" Nananom mma ketewa no hyɛɛ aseɛ bio, hwene! hwene! Ɔbaa kɔkɔɔ no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛrekɔ on?" Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ma me nnane Okuafo mma, efisɛ sɛ me nnya bi a mɛnam so, mɛwu." Ɔbaa kɔkɔɔ no kaa sɛ, "Ah! me nananom mma yi mɛwu efisɛ ɔnni Okuafo mma a ɔbɛnam so?" Sɔre, fa ɔpɛpɛpɛ na kɔ bɔ ɔdua kɛse no na fa Okuafo mma no bra me." Kurofo no kɔɔ hɔ, (ɔpɛpɛpɛ no bɔɔ), Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! Ɛte sɛ dua no repue, ɛnna Akɔkɔsradee mma no mpanyimfo no bɔɔ mmirika kɔgyinaa nkuo no ano, na ɔteɛ n’abɔne; ɔfrɛɛ ne maame, ɔse:

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, anomaa no, Okuruku ba!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔdee adidi a, ɔnnyia mma me!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, o! o!

Na naayewa no tee sɛ ne ba no resu; ɔsɔree, (na ne ntaban no nnyigyeɛ ne,) fa! Ɔbaa hɔ; ɔse, "Sanguri," na ɛdua no (a na wɔapae no koraa) hyee bom bio, na nnipa nyinaa a na wɔrebɔ no no, woyɛɛ wɔn nnuane. Ɛnna ɔfaa aduane a ɔde bae no maa ne mma. Ɛna ɔkaa wɔn nkyia, ɔsee wɔn sɛ, "Merekɔ, sɛ ɔbaa no ba aba mo nso a, ma ɔnfa mo nkɔ." Na ɔbaa no see wɔn sɛ, "Kɔ bɔ dua no na fa nnwuma no bra ma me nananom ba no nnu." Na wɔkɔɔ hɔ mprempren a ɛtɔ so mmienu, Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! Ɛte sɛnea dua no bɛkɔ asase so, ɛnna (Eagle's) ba no puee na ɔbaa na ɔgyina mfuo no nkyɛn, na ɔfrɛɛ ne maame, ɔse:

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, anomaa no, Okuruku ba!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔdee adidi a, ɔnnyia mma me!

Sango, anomaa no e!

Sango, o! o!

Ɔfrɛɛ ne maame, ɔfrɛe, ɔfrɛe, ɔfrɛe—obi annyɛ nka—na seesei dua no kasae berɛ a ɔtɔɔ fam no, "Brim!" (ɛse). Wɔfaa Okuafo no mma no kɔɔ; wɔmaa baako ma ɔbaa sankofa no, nanso baako a ɔtena hɔ no tuu kwan kɔtoo Wawa-dua bi so. Baako, wɔboaa no, na wɔmaa ɔbaa sankofa no nananom ba no, na ɔde hyɛɛ roasted plantain (a ɔrewe) mu. Ankyɛ koraa na Okuruku baa. Berɛ a ɔduu dua a wɔtwaa no, ɔhunuu ne mma baako a ɔte hɔ; ɔbisaa no nea aseni, na ɔka amanneɛ no nyinaa kyerɛɛ no. Okuruku no kɔɔ ɔbaa sankofa no akuraa mu. Berɛ a ɔduu hɔ no, ɔbaa sankofa no nananom ba no na ɔredi ne mma baako. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Krokopaan, me hyira wo." Ɔfiri krokopaan no fie puei na ɔhyɛɛ (ne yadeɛ/magic) ase wɔ kurom mfimba no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sanguri," na obiara nyinaa pirae, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Sanguri" bio, na fie biara nyinaa bɔɔ mpaew pɛpɛɛpɛ na obiara angyina hɔ bio. "Sanguri," na kuro no yɛyɛ kwae bio. "Sanguri," na krokopaan no yadeɛ no baa bio. Na ɔse, "Krokopanim, woahu, ɛno nti na mpanyimfo se, sɛ obi yɛ wo adwuma pa a, yɛ no ayɛm by yɛ no adwuma pa, na mma wo mmu no." Me nsɛm a mika yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, mo bɛtumi agye nea ɛyɛ nokware mu, na na akyerɛ no a, mo bɛtumi akom m'ayɛ (sɛ mikae).
