Sɛnea OKOTO, kraman no, nyaa ne kɔtɔ, na ABEREWA, ɔbaa mpaninfoɔ no, nyaa mfutuma fitaa

OBRAA bi wɔ baa; sɛ ɔde no kɔmee (baabiara), na ɔse, "Me nni hɔ sɛ mɛkɔ." Ne maame no se, "Afei tena hɔ, wo ne wo nyansa no." Da biara sɛ ɔde no kɔmee (adeɛ biara), na ɔse, "Me nni hɔ sɛ mɛkɔ." Ɛnna da bi na ɔse, "Kɔ hwan nsuo na fa aba." Ɔse, "Me nni hɔ sɛ mɛkɔ." Na ɔbaa panin no bɔɔ no na ɔse, "Tena hɔ hwɛ, na wubɛhu." Na abofra no kaa sɛ, "Mɛkɔ rehwehu 'mɛhu' no na matumi ahu (sɛnea ɛte)." Na ɔfaa kwan no so, na ɔtuu mmirika den, (na ne nan nnyigyei no te sɛ) yiridi, yiridi, yiridi. Akyirei na ɔduu kuro bi, na ɔhunuu ɔbaa panin bi a ɔtenaa hɔ. Sɛ ɔhunuu no, na ɔrepɛ sɛ ɔsan nkɔ. Ɔbaa panin no se, "Me nananom, mma wo mfaso na ahwɛ wo." Na abofra no kɔɔ anim; na ɔbaa panin no se, "Adɛn nti na nsuo afi wo ani akɔkɔ wo saa?" Ɔse, "Me na me maame tenaa fam hɔ, da biara sɛ ɔsɔre, na ɔse, 'Tena hɔ hwɛ na wubɛhu,' na ɔbɔ me, enti me se, mɛba rehwehu 'mɛhu' no na matumi ahu (sɛnea ɛte)." Na ɔbaa panin no se, "Me ne no." Afei ɔse, "Kɔ dan no akyi hɔ na twene pampana na fa aba; sɛ wodu hɔ, nea ɔbɛse, 'Twene me, twene me, twene me,' mma wo mtwene no; na nea ɔse, 'Mma wo mtwene me, mma wo mtwene me,' ɛno na twene." Ɔgyee no ase se, "Ho!" Nokwarely ɔkɔɔ hɔ; pampana bi se no, "Twene me, twene me, twene me;" afoforo nso se, "Mma wo mtwene me, mma wo mtwene me, mma wo mtwene me." Nea wɔse, "Mma wo mtwene me," ɛno na ɔtwenee. Na ɔde wɔn baa; na ɔbaa panin no se no, "Pue wɔn na fa wɔn hyɛ gyata." Na ɔpuee wɔn na ɔhyɛɛ wɔn (gyata no so), na sɛ wɔnoaa a, ɔtwee wɔn gu kuku mu, na ɔde hyɛɛ ɔbaa panin no anim. Na ɔse, "Kɔ kuro no mpɔso, na hwɛ aduane kwan a nnono da so, hwɛ (nnono) no mu na fa nea wopɛ." Na abofra no faa bi, na ɔbaa, na ɔhyɛɛ no gyata, na ɔnoaa sopɔ. Sɛ ɔwiee a, ɔde hyɛɛ ɔbaa panin no anim. Ɔbaa panin no see, "Woawie?" Ɔyɛɛ no mmuae sɛ, "Aane, Nana." Ɔsee, "Bra na yɛnpɔ fufu." Na ɔbaa panin no kɔtoo ne yafunu so; ɔsee, "Bo nkrane a wɔnoaa no no, na fa to me hwene mu, na pɔ." Abofra no see, "Asɛm a ɛyɛ anigye ne sɛ, yɛpɔ fufu wɔ nnipa hwene mu; eyi ne nea me maame kae." Eyi nyinaa na ɔkae wɔ ne tie mu. Ɔkɔpɔɔ; nanso berɛ a ɔpɔɔ no, ɔpɔɔ no mmerɛ. Ɔbaa panin no see, "Pɔ no dennen, sɛ wompɔ no dennen a, wo kɔn bɛpue." Na ɔpɔɔ no, pi! pi! pi! na berɛ a wawee no, ɔfaa nsupu gu mu, na ɔde bɛtoo ɔbaa panin no anim. Ɔbaa panin no see, "Me nananom, frɛ me din na fa na didi." Na ɔsee, "Wɔfrɛ wo Nana-Obaa-Panin." Ɔsee, "Ee! wannyini, na ama wadi." Na ɔbaa panin no didi no nyinaa. Nnawɔtwe nwɔtwe nyinaa, ɔyɛɛ no saa saa. Na da koro bi, abofra no kɔɔ asubɔn kɔtɔɔ nsuo. Kraman no huu no, na ɔsee, "Adɛn nti na obi ba a ɔayɛ mmerɛ saa?" Ɔsee, "Nana-Obaa-Panin bi wɔ hɔ a me te ne nkyɛn, na berɛ a me noaa aduane na memaa no a, ɔse, 'Frɛ me din na fa na didi'; meka sɛ, 'Wɔfrɛ wo Nana-Obaa-Panin,' na ɔse, 'Ee! wunim kwan a wubɛdi ho,' na ɛnnɛ ɔdidi; ɛno ne nea nti na meyɛ mmerɛ." Kraman no see, "Wɔfrɛ no Obaa-Panin-Mosono." Abofra no see, "Ho." Na ɔde ne nsuo-pate hyɛɛ ne ti. Kraman no see, "Mma wo werɛ mmpue berɛ a worefi hɔ." Abofra no fii hɔ; berɛ a ɔreba no nyinaa, ɔreka sɛ, "Obaa-Panin-Mosono, Mosono, Mosono." Berɛ a ɔduu kurom no nkyɛn, ne werɛ puee, na ɔtwaa n'anan na ne nsuo-pate no bɔɔ, na ɔsan kɔɔ fie. Ɔbaa panin no see, "Nsuo-pate no wɔ he?" Ɔsee, "Berɛ a mereba no, metwaa m'anan, na ɛbɔɔ." Ɔsee, "Fa foforo kɔ." Na ɔfaa no kɔɔ; na Kraman no see, "Wasan aba akum?" Na ɔsee, "Din a wokaee me no, me werɛ apue, na maba sɛ mɛbisabi bio." Kraman no see, "Wɔfrɛ no Obaa-Panin-Mosono." Na ɔsee, "Ho." Ɔkɔree no kɔɔ fie, na berɛ a ɔduu fie no, ɔde (pate no) too asa. Ɔbaa panin no see, "Kɔ digu pampam na fa bra." Ɔyɛɛ biribiara te sɛ mberɛ a ɛtwa mu no, na ɔfa aduane no baa, na ɔtoo no ɔbaa panin no anim, na ɔbaa panin no see, "Frɛ me din na fa na edi." Ɔsee, "Wɔfrɛ wo ɔbaa-panin-nananom." Ɔsee, "Me din pa a me wɔ no na me kyerɛ." Ɔsee, "Wo din pa?" Ɔbaa panin no see, "Aane." Ɔsee, "Wɔfrɛ wo ɔbaa-panin-Mosono." Ɔbaa panin no see, "Fa na edi." Abofra no faa na ɔdii. Ɔbaa panin no hyɛɛ kwan no so, na ɔsee, "Merekɔ rehwehu onipa a ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ me din ne Mosono no." Na ɔkɔɔ asubɔn, na ɔhyiaa Obonto Ya, nsuo-nam no, (na ɔka n'anim, ɔsee no), "Wo 'Bonto, wo maame! wo 'Bonto, wo papa! wo na wo ka kyerɛɛ me nananom abofra no sɛ wɔfrɛ me Mosono?" Ɔsee, "Ɛnyɛ me." Ɔbaa panin no huu sɛ Adwen' (nsuo-nam no) gyina hɔ; ɔbɔɔ no mpaebɔ, ɔsee, "Wo Adwen', wo maame! maame! maame! wo Adwen', wo papa! papa! papa! wo na wo ka kyerɛɛ me nananom abofra no sɛ wɔfrɛ me Mosono?" Ɔsee, "Ɛnyɛ me." Ɔhuu sɛ Kraman-nsuo (Crab) gyina hɔ. Ɔbɔɔ no mpaebɔ, ɔsee, "Wo Crab, wo maame! maame! maame! wo Crab, wo papa! papa! papa! wo na wo ka kyerɛɛ me nananom abofra no sɛ mete Accra, sɛ me yɛ ɔhenema, na wɔfrɛ me Mosono?" Crab no see, "Aane, me na me ka kyerɛɛ no, na adɛn nti na wo maa obi ba bɔɔ ne ho te saa?" Na ɔbaa panin no bo fui, na ne kuku a ɔkuraa no, ɔmaa ɛyɛɛ sorɔ na ɔtorɔɔ no na ɛbɔɔ Crab no. Ɛno nti na Crab no wɔ kuku wɔ ne bekyere so. Tete no, Crab no bekyere so anaa kuku biara nni hɔ. Na Crab no faa hyeene fitaa, na ɔtorɔɔ no ɔbaa panin no ti ataso. Ɛno nti na ɔbaa panin nyinaa ti ataso wɔ nhwi fitaa.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, obiara betumi agye bewee sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na naayɛn nso betumi ayɛ me ayɛyie (ase sɛ mekae).
