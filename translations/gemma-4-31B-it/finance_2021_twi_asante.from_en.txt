ASƐM FI SIKA MPƐNINI MANNIFOƆ MPANYIN NO FI SE

Ghana nnipa pa no too mfoɔ mmienu wɔ December 2020 mu sɛ H.E. Ɔmanpanyin, Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo nnyina hɔ mma mfeɛ afoforo sɛnea ɔbɛkɔ so ayɛ adwuma pa no na watu kwan ayɛ foforo pii. Gyidi a yɛn Ghana nuanom gu saa Aban yi so yi, yɛnmpɛ sɛ yɛfa no sɛ ade a ɛyɛ kɔkɔɔ.

2021 Budget yɛ budget titiriw a ɛsesa yɛn sika ho dwumadie koraa na ama yɛatumi agye nkwa, ama adwumayɛ a ɛhyehyɛn mu ayɛ den anya ahoɔden, na yɛde yɛn borɔne nyinaa ahyehyɛ Ghana foforo a ɛyɛ eyie, COVID-19 akyi. Budget yi gyina yɛn anigye ho sɛ yɛbɛsesa saa man yi na yɛasie sika ho dwumadie a ɛtumi gyina ne nan so.

Mfitiaseɛ term a ɛdi kan no kɛseɛ no, yɛasiesie aduane ne sika ho dwumadie no sɛ ɛnyɛ nka, wɔ dwumadie a yɛapɛpɛɛ mu a ɛfa sika ne aduane ho, yɛatumi ayɛ adeɛ a ɛde sika ba, yɛayɛ nneɛma a ɛma ɔman no nyigye, yɛaboaboa nnipa a wɔyɛ adwuma wɔ wɔn ankasa nsam, na yɛaayɛ mmoa dwumadie a ɛfa nnipa nyinaa ho. Ɛberɛ a 2019 ba awieeɛ no, yɛwɔ kwan pa so a ɛrekyerɛ sɛ ɔman no sika ne aduane ho dwumadie no renyigye, efisɛ sika ne aduane ho nkyerɛwee no yɛ dɛ na aman a ɛwɔ wiase nyinaa nso aka sɛ Ghana yɛ ɔman a ɔrenyigye, ɛnna COVID-19 yareɛ no baa yɛn so. Saa yareɛ yi nsem a ɛyɛ yaw no akasa aduane ne sika ho dwumadie nyinaa wɔ wiase nyinaa, efisɛ Ghana nso nyɛ nka.

Covid-19 Pandemic no a puei no akɔ so ama nsɛnnɛɛ pii aba a ɛasɛe yɛn dwumadie ne yɛn ahoɔden a yɛfa so tera. Nanso, Aban no ahu nea pandemic yi ama yɛn nyinaa asuadee mu ansa na wɔahyɛ nsɛm ne mmara foforɔ ase a ɛbɛboa ama dwumadie a ɛasɛe no asɔre bio na yɛn asetena asɔre. Anigye bi wɔ hɔ efisɛ yɛahu akwan foforɔ a yɛbɛfa so ayɛ nneɛma a ɛkyerɛ sɛ eyɛ mmerɛ. Aban no apaw ne ho sɛ wɔbɛhwɛ ama yɛn nyinaa anya ahoɔden, enti wɔasiesie adeɛ ama Ghanafoɔ anya dadeɛ a ɛbɛbo abɔne (vaccination) na yɛn nyinaa abɛyɛ den ama yɛatumi ayɛ Ghana a yɛpɛ sɛ yɛhu.

Yɛayɛ mo ayɔnkofa mpo wɔ amanehunu mu te sɛ yɛn nkɔsoɔ dwumadie no fa koro, na yɛatumi ayɛ nsɛm a ɛyɛ den na yɛasan aka yɛn ɔman biakoye no mu ma ɛnkɔ nkɔsoɔ mu. Ghanafoɔ bebree a wɔsom bo 1.2 million tumi nya sukuu a ɛyɛ pa na ɛyɛ den ma wɔn nkwa nnɛ, yɛasesa banku dwumadie no na abɔketene pii anya wɔn sika a wɔde aba banku a wɔatwa mu no mu. Afei, yɛasi kar yɛyɛ-bea kɛseɛ bi, na yɛahu kar a wɔyɛe wɔ ha a ɛdi kan wɔ yɛn dwumadie ne yɛn kwan so.

Yɛka nokware sɛ yɛhyiaa nsɛnnennen bi wɔ berɛ a yɛpɛ sɛ yɛyɛ yɛn nkɔsoo nhyehyɛe no. Aban reyi mfasoɔ kɛseɛ ma nneɛma a ɛbɛyɛ aduane ama saa nsɛm yi asiesie na ama adwumayɛfoɔ anya ahomegye. Menara, yɛhyɛɛ GH¢750 million CAP-BuSS Programme no ase wɔ May 2020 mu, na eyi ama Micro, Small and Medium-Sized Enterprises (MSMEs) ne afoforɔ pii anya mmoa.

Sɛnea ɛnnyɛyɛ ansa yi, yɛa yɛfrɛ yɛn sɛ yɛnka nsa ano na yɛapue yɛn man anim wɔ dwumadie kɛse a yɛrehyɛ aseɛ no so, na ɔnam so ma obiara nya nkɔsoɔ. Efisɛ, yɛahyira yɛn ho sɛ yɛbɛyɛ Ghana adwuma wɔ yɛn ahoɔden nyinaa ne yɛn akoma nyinaa mu.

Nyame nhyira yɛn nyinaa.

Ghana nnyɛ mmerɛ!

Ken Ofori-Atta Sika Minister

NHYIADEƐ

1. 2021 Citizen’s Budget yɛ mfupifoɔ a ɛkyerɛ nea Aban yɛɛ wɔ 2020 mu ne sika a ɛpɛ sɛ ɛboaboa ano de yɛ nnwuma ne nhyehyɛeɛ wɔ 2021 mu. 2021 Budget no nsɛm a ɛhyɛ aseɛ ne “Completion, Consolidation, and Continuation”. Nea ɛhyɛ saa nsɛm yi anim ne sɛ, mfeɛ a atwam no, Budget foforo biara mu wɔ nnwuma foforo pii a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔhyɛ aseɛ, na ɛma nnwuma a wɔreye no tɔ mfiase. Eyie nnyɛ yie ma yɛn sika kwan no pɛ, na ɛso ma sika a ɛfere nnwuma yɛfoɔ nkoa nni hɔ, enti nnwuma yɛfoɔ pii miani nnya wɔn sika wɔ berɛ a ɛfata. Eyie ma nnwuma no sika a ɛfa ho kɔ soro efisɛ sika a wɔatwa mu no hyɛ nkyɛmu ho.

2. Wɔ 2021 mu no, Aban no pɛ sɛ ɛyɛ akwan foforo. Ne mpaebɔ ne sɛ ɛbɛgye amanehunu a ɛfa adwuma a wɔantumi awie ho, na ama wɔatumi awie adwuma a wɔafii ase no nyinaa.

Macroeconomic Performance

3. Adwene a ɛyɛ mmofra ho nsɛm a ɛfa ɔman bi economy mu dwumadie wɔ 2020 mu ne saa:

• Fie mfeɛ no nnonsa mmofra a ɛdi kan no kyerɛɛ GDP nyinaa mmofra a ɛtrɛn 0.2 percent. Yɛhwɛ kwan sɛ GDP mmofra a ɛtrɛn 0.9 percent bɛba afe no awieeɛ;

• Non-Oil real GDP nyinaa nyɛe ne growth 0.4 percent wɔ afe no bosome nine a edi kan no mu, na yɛhwɛ kwan sɛ ɛbɛwie afe no wɔ 1.6 percent;

• DDecember awieeɛ inflation yɛ 10.4 percent, sɛ yɛtwitwa ho kyerɛ target a yɛasesa no a ɛyɛ 11.0 percent;

• Sika a ɛhia mma budget no nyinaa wɔ cash basis so yɛ GDP 11.7 percent, na target a wɔasesa no yɛ GDP 11.4 percent;

• Primary balance no kyerɛɛ deficit a ɛyɛ GDP 5.3 percent, nanso target deficit no yɛ GDP 4.6 percent; na

• Gross International Reserves yɛ US$8.6 billion, a ɛyɛ 4.1 months a yɛbɛtumi de akasa nnoɔma a yɛtɔ firi mframa.

7. Akyɛdeɛ a wɔde bɔɔ mmoa no kɔɔ GH¢291.6 billion (US$50.8 billion) wɔ DDecember 2020 awieeɛ, a ɛkyerɛ GDP 76.1 percent. Sɛ yɛmpɛ sika a wɔde gyee sika nnwuma kurom no a, 2020 debt stock no yɛ GDP 71.5 percent. Debt stock no nkyekyɛmu ne saa:

• Akyiwadeɛ a ɛfiri nnipa anaa aman a ɛwɔ mpaninfie: GH¢141.7 billion (US$24.7 billion) a ɛkyerɛ 48.6 percent a ɛwɔ akyiwadeɛ nyinaa mu; na

• Ahome mfutuma: GH¢149.8 billion (US$26.1 billion) a ɛkyerɛ mfutuma nyinaa mu 51.4 percent.

• GDP a ɛyɛ nokware nyinaa nyinasi a ɛyɛ 5.0 percent;

• Non-Oil Real GDP nyigyei a ɛyɛ 6.7 percent;

• DDecember awie inflation 8.0 percent;

• Sika a ɛhia (Fiscal deficit) a ɛyɛ GDP 9.5 percent;

• GDP 1.3 percent primary deficit; na

• Gross International Reserves a ɛbɛyɛ dodoɔ sɛ ɛbɛtumi akasa nnoɔma ne asɛm a yɛtumi tɔ firi mfoni mu mmerɛ a ɛnkɔ ne 4.0 months.

Asɛmfua Nkyekyɛmu (Budget Nkyekyɛmu)

1D1F (One District One Factory): Adwumayɛbea ne nnwuma kɛseɛ a wɔresiesie, a ɛbɛma ɔman no akɔ anim wɔ nnwuma kɛseɛ ho. Ne mpaebɔ ne sɛ ɛbɛyɛ adwuma pii ma Ghanafoɔ wɔ ɔman no mma mma.

Accountability: Eyi ne berɛ a Aban no hyɛ ne ho kwan sɛ ɛbɛma ne manfoɔ mmu ne nneyɛeɛ na ɛaka ne nneyɛeɛ no abaayɛ a ɛba mu. Eyi betumi abi nsika anaa nneɛma foforo a Aban no agye to gyina ho asɛm.
