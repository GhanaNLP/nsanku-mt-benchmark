Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐ WOREKƆ BAABI, NA WO ABUSua KA SE ƆBƐKƆ WO NE NSO A, ƐNNA KƆ NE NE NSO

Na obiara nim ɔbaa bi a ɔwɔ mma baasa. Ne mma mu a ɔyɛ ketena no, yaws yadeɛ bi hyɛɛ no. Ne nua a ɔyɛ panyin no kaa kyerɛ wɔn maame sɛ ɔmma wɔn sika kɔkɔɔ na wɔakɔ adwuma. Ne nua ketena no nso kaa sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkɔ. Nnipa no kaa sɛ ɔnnkyɛ nnyɛ wɔn kɔ. Na maame no kaa sɛ wɔn nyinaa mma wɔn nkɔ. Ɛnnɛ maame no maa mma panyin no sika kɔkɔɔ a ne bo yɛ £5, na ɔmaa ketena no sika kɔkɔɔ a ne bo yɛ £2. Berɛ a anɔpa kɔɔ so no, wɔnam kɔɔ. Panyin no kɔɔ mprenu na wɔgyaei abofra no a ɔnam wɔn akyi. Abofra no nam kɛkɛ. Panyin no hyiaa ɔbarima bi a ɔde nsuomkyɛ reba. Ɔkaa kyerɛ panyin no sɛ, "Tɔ!" Wɔn nso gyee me answers sɛ, "Kɔ, na wobɛhyia abofra bi; ma ɔntɔ, na sɛ ɔse ɔrentɔ a, bɔ no, na ma ɔntɔ; na sɛ ɔka sɛ ɔrentɔ ara a, fa no nkɔ ma ne maame." Ɔbarima no kɔe, na ampa, ɔhyiaa abofra no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Panyin bi a mehyiaa hɔ se sɛ wopɛ sɛ wotɔ saa nsuomkyɛ yi." Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Mɛtɔ, berɛ a me nua panyin no rentɔ?" Ɔbarima no kaa sɛ, "Wɔse sɛ worentɔ a, mɛbɔ wo, na mɛfa wo akɔ ma wo maame." Abofra no bisaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ sika sɛn?" Ɔbarima no kaa sɛ, "Sika kɔkɔɔ osua baako (ɛne £2)." Abofra no tuae na ɔfaa no. Ɔnam kɔe, na ɔhyiaa panyin no wɔ kuro bi mu. Wɔnoaa nnuaba, nanso nnansae biara nnim hɔ a wɔde bɛka ho. Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Hwan, nsuomkyɛ a matɔ yi." Wɔtoo no nkwan mu. Berɛ a wɔwiee no, panyin no maa no nsuomkyɛ no ti. Berɛ a abofra no repɛ sɛ ɔpɛtɛ no, ɔhunuu sika kɔkɔɔ kɔkɔkɔ wɔ mu, na ɔkyekyere no hyɛɛ ne ntoma mu. Da a ɛba no, berɛ a anɔpa kɔɔ so, panyin no nam kɔe, na berɛ a wɔnam no, wɔhyiaa ɔbarima bi, na akoko bi hyɛn nneɛma a ɔde reba no ase. Berɛ a ɔbaa panyin no nkyɛn no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Tɔ saa akoko yi." Wɔgyee me answers sɛ, "Kɔ, na wobɛhyia abofra bi; ma ɔntɔ, na sɛ ɔrentɔ a, bɔ no." Ampa, ɔhyiaa abofra no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo nua panyin no se sɛ wopɛ sɛ wotɔ saa akoko yi, na sɛ worentɔ a, mɛbɔ wo, na mɛfa wo akɔ ma wo maame." Ɔgyee me answers sɛ, "Fa, fa nea mewɔ yi." Ɔbarima no nso maa no akoko no. Wɔduu kuro bi mu. Kuro no nyinaa mu, akoko biara nnim hɔ a ɛbɛte. Anɔpa a ɛdi so, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ, aberewa no akoko a ɛyɛ abofra no de kɔɔ. Kuro no Odikro no see, "Abofra, fa akoko no mma me na matɔ." Abofra no see, "Sika no ne osua ne suru mmuae (i. e. £3)." Odikro no tuaa sika no. Da a ɛdi so, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ, wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ wɔrekɔ bio. Berɛ a wɔrekɔ no, mpanyimfo no hyiaa ɔbarima bi a ɔkura akuma. Ɔsee, "Tɔ akuma yi." Mpanyimfo no see, "Fa akuma yi kɔ, na wobɛhyia abofra bi; fa kɔma no na ɔntɔ. Sɛ ɔse ɔrenntɔ a, bɔ no, na fa no kɔma ne na." Ɔbarima no kɔe, na ɔhyiaa abofra no. Ɔsee, "Wo mpanyimfo se sɛ wo ntɔ akuma yi a, mɛbɔ wo na mafa wo akɔma wo na." Abofra no bisaa, "Ɛyɛ sika sɛn?" Ɔbarima no see, "Osua ne suru mmuae sika" (i. e. £3). Abofra no tuaa sika no; na ɔkɔe, na ɔbaa kuro bi mu hyiaa ne mpanyimfo. Ɛna mmoa mmoa (mice) no yɛ Odikro a ɔwɔ hɔ no nan nnam berɛ biara a ɔpɛ sɛ ɔda. Berɛ a abofra no faa akuma no kɔe no, akuma no kɔɔ ɔhene no fie na ɛkyinii mmoa mmoa no a wɔwɔ hɔ. Odikro no see, "Mɛtɔ akuma yi fi obi a ɛyɛ ne nne." Abofra no see, "Ɛyɛ me dea." Odikro no bisaa, "Ɛyɛ sika sɛn?" Abofra no see, "Osua ne suru mmuae sika" (i. e. £3). Odikro no faa sika no guu ne nsam. Da a ɛdi so, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ, wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ wɔrekɔ. Mpanyimfo no rekɔ; wɔhyiaa awufo-foforo (thieves) a wɔafi ɔhene bi a wuwui no dɔteɛ. Awufo-foforo no see, "Mo mma mo ntɔ dɔteɛ yi." Mpanyimfo no see, "Fa, na kɔ, na wobɛhyia abofra bi; fa kɔma no na ɔntɔ. Sɛ ɔse ɔrenntɔ a, bɔ no, na fa no kɔma ne na." Awufo-foforo no kɔe, na wɔhyiaa abofra no; wɔsee, "Mpanyimfo no se wo mma wo ntɔ dɔteɛ yi." Abofra no see, "Eh! Adɛn nti na mɛtɔ dɔteɛ, mentumi mfa amma; nea mede bɛyɛ no nyinaa, ɛrennya fa bi." Awufo-foforo no see, "Wo mpanyimfo see sɛ sɛ wo renntɔ a, yɛbɔ wo, na yɛfa wo akɔma wo na." Abofra no bisaa, "Ɛyɛ sika sɛn?" Wɔsee, "Osua ne suru mmuae sika." Abofra no tuaa sika no, faa dɔteɛ no, na ɔtoo no gu kwae mu. Ɛna ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔe, na ɔbaa kuro bi, na ɔkɔɔ fie bi kɔsrɛɛ aduane. Danfo no se, “Hwee nni hɔ.” Abofra no se, “Nana, mebɔ wo mpae.” Danfo no se, “Hwee nni hɔ.” Abofra no se, “Nana, mebɔ wo mpae.” Danfo no se, “Adɛn nti na abofra yi reha me saa; yɛn Ɔhene awu, yɛreyɛ ayamyam; ɛyɛ da nwɔtwe; nanso sɛ yɛhwehwɛ mpo a, yɛnni ne ho.” Obaa kɛse bi a ɔte hɔ nkyɛn se, “Ma no bi.” Obaa no maa no aduane ne nam. Berɛ a abofra no dee no, ɔse, “Danfo, meahu Ɔhene ho; efisɛ nnɛnne a mereba no, nkorɔfo bi maa metɔn osua ne suru mpaewu sika.” Obaa no tuu mmirika, yiridi, yiridi, yiridi! (ɛne ne mmirika nne) na ɔkɔka amanneɛ no kyerɛ mpanyimfo no. Wɔfaa abofra no, na ɔkɔkyerɛɛ hɔn ne ho. Wɔkɔfaa no na wɔgyaei no yie. Wɔse, “Saa na wubɛyɛ Ɔhene.” Enti abofra no bɛyɛɛ Ɔhene. Berɛ a ne nua a wɔyɛ ne nkyɛn huu no, wɔbaa ha, na wɔka se wɔyɛ ne nkabom. Abofra no se, “Mobɛyɛ mpaebɔ; mennim mo, enti monkɔ.” Na ɔmaa ne nkoa tuu hɔn fii hɔ. Ɛno nti na yɛse, “Sɛ worekɔ baabiara, na wo nua ketewa se ɔreka wo nkyɛn a, kɔ ne ne.”

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
