Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

DEƐN NTI NA APUPUO, A ƐMA NE Nsuo Soɔ, Ɛda Nsuo Mpɔmu No So

Wɔka sɛ Okawa, Opitire, 'Bonko, Shrimp, ne nsuo mu mmoa nyinaa (tenaa baako) na prɛkɔ kɛse bi baa na nsuo nyinaa puee, enti eyi maa mmoa yi huu amane pii. Wɔdae boom (nsuo no ase). Akyirei na wɔse, "Sɛnne amanehunu a yɛahu yi ho, ma yɛnkɔ Nyankonpon, Wɔnkyɛm-Onyame hɔ, na yɛndidi kyerɛ no na wakɔfa osu a ɛbɛtɔ yɛn so." Atoro nyinaa hyɛɛ aseɛ. Na Okawa gyinaa hɔ na ɔpawee ne nne (na ɔtoo dwom):

Me, Oka' Abena, menni hɔ anaa, a mɛkɔ ha, na mɛkɔ hɔ?

Mepira na megyina me nan nso mpo so na makaa Wo.

Me pue aburobia nketia a ɛwɔ me nananom no.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔyɛ me yere, Abuya.

Nanso osuo no antɔ. Na Opitire nso bae na ɔtoo dwom:

Me, Opitire Abena, menni mmofra anaa, sɛ me kɔ ha, me kɔ hɔ?

Mepira na megyina me nan nso mpo so na makaa Wo.

Me pue aburobia nketia a ɛwɔ me nananom no.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔyɛ me yere, Abuya.

Nanso osuo no antɔ. Na Bonko nso bae na ɔtoo dwom:

Me, 'Bonko Abena, menni hɔ sɛ mekɔ ha, na mekɔ hɔ?

Mepira na megyina me nan nso mpo so na makaa Wo.

Me pue aburobia nketia a ɛwɔ me nananom no.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔyɛ me yere, Abuya.

Nanso osuo no nnim. Atoro nyinaa bae na wɔsuu, na Apupuo nkoaa na ɔtena hɔ. Ɔse, "Ma me msu kakra." Wɔse, "Kɔ fie, yɛn suu no annyɛ hwee, enti dɛn na wuhu sɛ wo suu no bɛyɛ?" Ɔse, "Ma me msu." Nanso wɔbɔɔ no, na wɔse wɔaka kyerɛ no sɛ ɔnnkyɛn. Nanso Nsu-mussel no see sɛ, "Mɛsu." Enti wɔseɛ, "Su." Na ɔhyɛɛ (anomaa no) aseɛ:

Me, Apupuo Abena, menni mmofra anaa, sɛ me kɔ ha, me kɔ hɔ?

Mepira na megyina me nan nso mpo so na makaa Wo.

Me pue aburobia nketia a ɛwɔ me nananom no.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔyɛ me yere, Abuya.

Na Onyame bɔɔ ne nsunsum, kukuru! Atoro nyinaa see, "Kɔ so, kɔ so." Na ɔsuu bio, na Onyame bɔɔ ne anim hwee. Na seesei atoro no bɔɔ no wɔ wɔn abepɔn so, na wɔse, "Kɔ so pempɛ." Na ɔsaa saa asɛm no bio. Na seesei Onyame gyee (nsuo no), na ɛtɔɔ, ta! ta! ta! ta! na asubɔnten nyinaa pum. Saa baa yi nso (se saa na) ɔsɔree, na twom! nsuo no bɔɔ (berɛ a wɔbɔɔ mu no). Atoro nyinaa bɔɔ mu. Apupuo, berɛ a ɔpɛ sɛ ɔbɔ nsuo no mu no, wɔtwaa no akyi, na ɔdaasɛm asuo no nkyɛn. Ɛno nti na yɛse, "Apupuo, nea ɔmaa ne nsusuom tɔɔ, da nsuo nkyɛn." Me nsɛm a mɛaka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ foforɔ, na ma bi nso mma me.
