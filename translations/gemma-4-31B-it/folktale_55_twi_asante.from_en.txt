SƐNE SPEAR-GRASS BAA ABUSUA MU NO

Kɔkrom kɛse bi baa na Ntikuma kɔɔ, ɔse ɔrekɔ siesie aduane na wakyɛre ama ne papa, Papa Ananse, Kɔkɔɔro no. Ɔkɔɔ na ɔduu kuro bi mu; (ɛhɔ) na ɔhyiaa Akɔkɔdua-Nana. Ɔbaa kɔkɔɔno no kaa kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Me nananom, adɛn nti na woaba ha, wɔnni hɔ a obi bɛba ha?” Ntikuma hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, “Ɛyɛ kɔkɔɔm nti, na maba sɛ mɛhwehwɛ biribi a mɛtumi akɔ didi.” Ɔbaa-nananom no kaa sɛ, “Kɔ me afuo no a ɛwɔ hɔ no, nanso yam a ɔbɛka sɛ, ‘Tu me, tu me,’ ntu no, nanso yam a ɔbɛka sɛ, ‘Ntu me, ntu me,’ tu no na fa bra .” Ntikuma kɔɔ. Yam bi ka sɛ, “Tu me, tu me”; efie bi nso ka sɛ, “Mmtu me, mmtu me.” Ɛnna Ntikuma tuu nea wɔka sɛ, “Mmtu me, mmtu me” no. Ɔde wɔn baa fie; ɔpuee wɔn. Ɛna Nana Panin no see sɛ, “Twa aduane no fapem, na fa nnusuwa no hyɛ nkuku mu na fa hyɛ gyata so.” Ntikuma pɔɔ no na ɔfaa nnusuwa no hyɛɛ gyata so. Berɛ a ɛyɛɛ fɛ no, ɔfaa no firi gyata so, ɔtɔɔ nkuku no mu, na biribiara (mu) ayɛ aduane pa. Ntikuma see, “Mewiei, nanso ma me nam na makɔ hyɛ (nkwan no) mu.” Nana kɔkɔɔ see, “Kɔka kyerɛ Nkwakuo, Aberewa a wɔn wɔ dua no so no, sɛ ‘Nana kɔkɔɔ se, mo nyinaa, mo maame’——!’” Aberewa no see, “Sɛ ɛnyɛ Nana kɔkɔɔ nti a, yɛbɛbɔ wo.” Nana kɔkɔɔ see, “Sɛ me ho firi mu a, ɛnyɛ me asɛm, na mo m “Ɔbaa bɛbɔ no.” Hafoo no nyinaa baa hɔ sɛ wɔbɛbɔ no. Akrabea se, “Me Sɛm-koro, twa saa hafoo yi anim ma me.” Ɛnna Sɛm-koro no twaa hafoo no anim. Ntikuma boaa hafoo no anim nyinaa ano, na ɔtuu pampam kakra nso, na ɔfa wɔn nyinaa kɔɔ ne kuro. Papa Kwebla huu (ne ba) Ntikuma a ɔgyina ne anim, na ɔsee no sɛ, “Akwaaba, mmoa-dwumakuo” (yehwɛ, ne ba panyin). Ɔkɔtɔe sɛ, “Ma me nso wo me kyɛn.” Ntikuma kɔtoo hɔ. Ɛnnɛ Ananse kɔtoo yam baako; ɔsee, “Sɛ me mmofra tɔ me a, mɛdi eyi.” Ne yere Aso see no, “Ɛfɔrɔ bɔne a Bɔfoɔ bɔɔ adeɛ no, woahu ɔbarima bi a ne mmofra tɔ no?” Ananse ne ne yere, ne mmofra nso, dii yam no nyinaa, na ɛkyerɛyɛɛ baako pɛ a ɛyɛ Papa Kwebla Ananse dea. Anɔpa a ɛba no, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ no, Ananse see sɛ, “Me nsa ayɛ me.” Na ɔkɔɔ fie no akyi (kɔtweɛ hɔ), noaa yam no, na ɔdidi no, kosi sɛ ɛnyinii. Ntikuma seeว่า, “Mɛkɔ bio.” Ananse seeว่า, “Me ne wo bɛkɔ (bom).” Ntikuma seeว่า, “Me ne wo renkɔ bom.” Berɛ a Ntikuma kɔdae no, Ananse kɔfaa abɔnten ntoma kwan kwan, na ɔyɛɛ mframa ketewa pii wɔ mu, na ɔhyɛɛ mfutuma mu, na ɔkyekyereɛ no hyɛɛ Ntikuma ntoma nkyɛn. Berɛ a anɔpa kɔsi, anɔpa paa, anɔpa paa, anɔpa paa no, Ntikuma hyɛɛ kwan so kɔe. Berɛ a (sum a ɛhwee) (nneɛma) anim no bɔɔ no, Ananse, sɛnea ɔhwehwɛɛ Ntikuma no, ɔanhunu no, ɛnna ɔkɔhwehwɛɛ (baabi a) mfutuma no (tɔeɛ). Berɛ a ɔhunuu hɔ no, ɔsuwee n’ananu no kɔ, kɔ, kɔ. Ɔnam kɔ, a, ɛhɔ na Ntikuma ne Akɔkɔdua-Nana tenaeɛ. Ɔbaa panin no see sɛ, “Papa, dɛn na wo pɛ?” Ananse hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, “Kɔkrom akyere me, enti ma me biribi na madi.” Ɔbaa panin no see sɛ, “Ɛhe na mɛfiri ‘biribi’ no?” Ananse hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, “Wosusu sɛ meyɛ kwasea? Me ba Ntikuma mpo baa ha, na woama no biribi.” Ɔbaa panin no see sɛ, “Kɔ afuo no hɔ; yamfoo a wɔse, ‘Twa me, twa me’ ,’ mma wo mmu wo nnua no, na dea ɔse, ‘Mma mo mmu me, mma mo mmu me,’ no, mu no.’ Kwebla (hyɛɛ no ase se), “Ah! asɛm bɔne bɛn ne yi a woka kyerɛ me, sɛ obi se ɔdɔ obi a, woka kyerɛ me sɛ mɛka se mɛkyere no tan, m’ani nni hwee a ɛte saa?” Nanso Kwebla kɔɔ afuo no mu. Berɛ a ɔduu hɔ no, gútutu bi ka sɛ, “Twa me, twa me,” ebi foforo nso ka sɛ, “Twa me mma, twa me mma.” Na ɔtwaa nea kae sɛ, “Twa me, twa me” no, na ɔde wɔn baa fie. Ɔbaa hɔ na ɔyɛɛ wɔn nnusua no nyinaa; Nana kɔtɔkɔrɔ no see no sɛ, “Kɔ tow nnaman no a ɛyɛ dɛ no afutu, na fa nnusua no hyɛ dan mu, na fa hyɛ gyata so.” Ananse hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, “Ah, asɛm bɔne bɛn ne eyi a woka kyerɛ me; efisɛ efɔre bɔne bɛn na yɛtow nnaman a ɛyɛ dɛ no afutu, na yɛgyina nnusua no?” Kɔbra no faa nnaman a ɛyɛ dɛ no hyɛɛ gyata so, nanso ɔ... faa nnusua baako guu so. Berɛ a aduane no huu no, ɔfaa no fii hɔ, na nea nyinaa ayɛ aboɔ, nanso nnusua baako a ɔde guu so no ayɛ aduane pa. Papa Kwebla see, "Eh! saa ɔkwaseɛ a ɔasore yi annyɛ nkontompo (bio)." Na Kwebla kɔfaa nnusua no nyinaa boaa mu na ɔde baa hɔ guu gya no so. Berɛ a aduane no huu no, ɔtoo no afire, na ɛyɛɛ nkyene a ɛnoaa yie te sɛnea wobɛpɛ sɛ wohu. Ɔsee, "Ɔbaa-panyin-nananom, eyi nyinaa yɛ, nanso seesei ɛsɛ sɛ wama me nam a mɛde aka ho." Ɔbaa-panyin-nananom see, "Kɔhwehwɛ wɔ dua no so, Aberewa wɔ hɔ; sɛ wokɔ a, wobɛse, 'Mo yɛ mmoa.'" Kweblaa see, "Ɔbaa yi!" Me ne nnansa no nni asomdwoe mmuae biara, enti adɛn nti na mɛkɔ akasa wɔn kasa kasa saa? "Ɔbaa panin no see, "Kɔ, na sɛ asiane ba a, ɛyɛ me asɛm." Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ. Ɔse, "Mo Monkey, mo a mo wɔ saa nnua yi so, Nana Akokɔfo se moyɛ mmoa." Mmoa no se, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ Nana Akokɔfo a, yɛbɛbɔ wo." Nana Akokɔfo se, "Ɛnna bra na mobɔ no."

Ananse seeว่า, "Kɔkɔɔkɔ, eyi nyɛ pɛ." Ɛnna mmoa no too ne so na wɔba bɔɔ Ananse. Kɔkɔɔkɔ no seeว่า, "Me nnaa mmerɛ-mu-mmerɛ, twa wɔn ti." Tintin! (ɛne nnaa no dwennee berɛ a ɛyɛɛ ne dwuma no). Kɔkɔɔkɔ-nananom no seeว่า, "Yɛ no brɛoo na yɛ no mmerɛoo seesei." Nanso Kwebla ante (ne kasa no). Ɛnnɛ, Kwebla boaa nam no ano na ɔsan kɔɔ fie, na Okrokrokuma no kɔɔ baabi kakra. Ɛno akyi no, Ananse dwii ne maba na ɔtoo no ne kotokuo mu, na ɔtuu mmirika kɔɔ ne kuro. Berɛ a ɔsan kɔɔ ne akurafoɔ hɔ no, ɔkaa kyerɛɛ (ne ba no) sɛ, "Ntikuma, ɛnna mma yɛnkɔ kwaafoɔ mu, ɛfiri sɛ m'ani agye saa cutu a m'ani aba yi ho." Enti wɔnam kɔɔ, na wɔkɔhunuu Boa-kwaa bi. Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ntikuma, kɔyɛ wɔn yaw." Ntikuma kɔɔ hɔ kasee sɛ, "Mmɔbor, mo yɛ dɔteɛ." Mmɔbor no hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, "Sɛ wo nnyɛ Kwaku Ananse, Kwebia ba no a, yɛbɛba abebɔ wo." Kwaku Ananse see, "Moma na mobɔ no, ɔyɛ onipa bɔne paa, ne ahantan no nyɛ asɛm a yɛbɛyɛ ho mmofra." Berɛ a Mmɔbor no repɛ sɛ wobɔ no no, Kwaku Ananse see, "Akraaba Mmoa, Ɔkrona, twa wɔn ti ma me." O na Knife no tɔɔ wɔn so, na ɛyɛɛ mmoa no anim nyinaa pɛpɛpɛ. Afei ɛhyɛɛ aseɛ twaa nnua no. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Hai! dɛn na woyɛ yi?" "Metwee sɛ twene mmoa no ti, na woaewie saa, enti dɛn na eyi ara ne neyɛ?" Ɛnnɛ Ntikuma hwɛɛ no kakra, a ɔsee, "Kasa brɛoo na yɛnkɔ." Kwaku Ananse ante ase, na nknam no tɔɔ fam, na Ananse kɔfaa no. Wɔsan kɔɔ fie. Da a ɛdi so, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ, Kwaku Ananse see sɛ wɔbɛkɔ Sky-god hɔ. Ntikuma seeว่า, "Papa, mma woankɔ sɛ ɛyɛ eyi ho Knife nti." Ananse seeว่า, "Kɔ hɔ." Ntikuma seeว่า, "Kɔ wo kara." Ananse hyɛɛ aseɛ kɔeɛ, na ɔkɔɔ Sky-god hɔ, na ɔsee no sɛ, "Ɔhene, nnɛ, sɛ obiara ayɛ wo bɔne a, me nko, me, Kwaku Ananse, mɛtwa wɔn animdeɛ nyinaa wɔ da baako pɛ na makɔma wo." Sky-god seeว่า, "Wɔn a wɔ..." tumi yɛ saa, efisɛ ɔkɔmfoɔ asraafoɔ kɛse bi gyina kurom no anim, enti kɔ.) Ananse see, “Ma wɔn nna nsa ne onuasoma ba.” Ɛno ara na wɔde baa. Ananse kɔɔ kwan no so; Ananse bisaa asraafoɔ no sɛ, “Mo ne nnipa a mo se mo bɛkɔ akɔpɔn ne Nyame?” Wɔn gyee me answers sɛ, “Aane, ɛno ne nea yɛse.” Ɛnnɛ, Nyame asraafoɔ gyinaa Ananse akyi. Ɛna Kwaku Ananse kasaa kyerɛ ɔbaa paapo no Sebere, Ɔkanyan-no, sɛ, “Twa saa asafo a wɔyɛ akokoɔ yi anim mma me.” Na Sebere no twaa wɔn anim, tan! tan! na ɔwiee. Ananse see, “Ɛyɛ, gyae.” Nanso Sebere no tɔɔ Nyame asafo no so na ɔretwa (wɔn anim). Ananse see, “O, ɛno ne Nyame asafo, enti gyae.” Nanso ɔkɔɔ so twaa. Ananse seeว่า, “Sɛ wo bo ayɛ den a, ma ɛnkɔ dwo.” Oh! Nyame nkurɔfoɔ nyinaa hwee wɔn. Afei Knife tuu kwan wɔ wim; ɔbaa baabi a Kwaku Ananse bepue. Ɔsee no, “Hwɛ yie, wunnim me? Wunnim me? Me ne Kwaku Ananse, me na mede wo fii ɔbaa a ɔyɛ mpanin hɔ.” Oh! ɛnna Kwaku Ananse ti tɔeɛ, tan! Na Knife no kɔe kɔe; na baabi a ɛgyinae no bɛyɛɛ dua bi a yɛfrɛ no “Etene”; ne nkyɛn no yɛ mmerɛ paa, paa, paa.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
