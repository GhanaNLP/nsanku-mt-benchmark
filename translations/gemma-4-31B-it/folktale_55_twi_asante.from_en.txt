Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNE SPEAR-GRASS BAA ABUSUA MU NO

Kɔkrom kɛse bi bae, enti Ntikuma kɔe, ɔse ɔrekɔ sika-aduan hwehwɛ ama ne papa, Father Ananse, Kwebla no. Ɔkɔe na ɔduu kuro bi; (hɔ no) ɔhyiaa Nana Akɔkɔdua. Nana no kaa kyerɛ no se, “Me nananom, adɛn nti na woaba ha, woara hɔ a obiara nnim sɛ ɔbɛba?” Ntikuma hyyɛɛ no ase se, “Kɔkrom nti na maba, na maba sɛ mɛhwehwɛ biribi a mɛtumi adi.” Nana Akɔkɔdua se, “Kɔ me afuo no hɔ, nanso yam a ɔbɛka se, ‘Tumi me, tumi me,’ nna tumi no, nanso yam a ɔbɛka se, ‘Tumi me mma, tumi me mma,’ no na tumi na woama me.” Ntikuma kɔe. Yam bi kaa se, “Tumi me, tumi me”; ebi nso kaa se, “Tumi me mma, tumi me mma.” Enti Ntikuma tumi aa no a wokaa se, “Tumi me mma, tumi me mma.” Ɔde aa wo fie; ɔpuee wɔn. Afei Nana Akɔkɔdua se, “Tɔ aduan no firi hɔ, na fa nnuane no hyɛlɛ mu na fa hyɛ gyata so.” Ntikuma puee wɔn na ɔfaa nnuane no hyɛɛ gyata so. Berɛ a ɛyɛɛ fɛ no, ɔfaa no firi gyata so, ɔtɔɔ ne mu firi hɔ, na ne mu nyinaa ayɛ aduan pa. Ntikuma se, “Mewiei, nanso ma me nam na matu (nkrakra mu).” Nana Akɔkɔdua se, “Kɔ kyerɛ Nkwakuo, Abɔnten no, a wɔwɔ dua no so, se ‘Nana Akɔkɔdua se, mo nyinaa, mo maame——!’” Abɔnten no kaa se, “Sɛ ɛnyɛ Nana Akɔkɔdua nti a, yɛbɛbɔ wo.” Nana Akɔkɔdua se, “Sɛ me ho asɛm a, ɛnyɛ me asɛm, na mobɛtumi aba aba no.” Mmoa no siire sɛ wɔrebɔ no. Nana Akɔkɔdua se, “Me Se-mmoa, twa saa mmoa yi ti ma me.” Afei Se-mmoa no twae mmoa no ti. Ntikuma boaa mmoa no ti nyinaa ano, na ɔtumiyɛɛ yam bi nso, na ɔde nyinaa kɔe ne kuro. Father Spider huu (ne ba) Ntikuma a ɔgyina ne anim, na ɔse, “Akwaaba, mmoa-dwumakuo” (se ne paapae). Ɔkɔtɔe se, “Ma me nso mfa wo hyɛ me kyɛn.” Ntikuma kɔhyɛɛ hɔ. Afei Ananse kɔfaa yam baako; ɔse, “Sɛ me nnuane-nkwa ba a, mɛdi eyi.” (Ne yere) Aso kaa se, “Sɛ Bɔfoɔ bɔɔ adeɛ no firi hɔ no, woahu barima bi a nnuane-nkwa ba no?” Ananse ne ne yere, ne ne mma, dii yam no nyinaa, na Father Spider deɛ nko ara na ne baako no tena hɔ. Anɔpa a ɛdi so no, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ no, Ananse see, “Me nsa ayɛ me.” Na ɔkɔɔ fie no akyi (kɔtweɛ) hɔ, noo pampaa no, na ɔdidi no kɔsi sɛ ɛnyinii. Ntikuma see, “Mɛkɔ bio.” Ananse see, “Me ne wo bɛkɔ (bɔmu).” Ntikuma see, “Me ne wo renkɔ bɔmu.” Berɛ a Ntikuma dae no, Ananse kɔfaa abɔfoɔ ntoma kɛkɛnkɛn bi, bɔɔ mframa ketekete wɔ mu, na ɔhyɛɛ nsusuo mu, na ɔkyekyereɛ no wɔ Ntikuma ntoma no nkyɛn. Berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ, anɔpa anɔpa anɔpa no, Ntikuma hyɛɛ kwan no so. Berɛ a sum a ɛahyira nneɛma anim no bɔɔ no, Ananse hwehwɛɛ Ntikuma sɛnea ɔbɛtumi, nanso ɔanhunu no, enti ɔkɔhwehwɛɛ (baabi a) nsusuo no (atɔ). Berɛ a ɔhunuu no, ɔsuwee n’akwan no so, kɔɔ anim kɔɔ anim. Ɔrekɔ no, ɛnna Ntikuma ne Akrabea tenaa hɔ. Akrabea see, “Agya, dɛn na wo nni hɔ?” Ananse hyɛɛ aseɛ, “Kɔm akɔm me, enti ma me biribi na madi.” Akrabea see, “Mefi he na mɛnya ‘biribi’?” Ananse hyɛɛ aseɛ, “Wopɛ sɛ woka sɛ meyɛ kwasea, m’aba Ntikuma mpo baa ha, na woama no biribi.” Akrabea see, “Kɔ afuo no hɔ; pampae a ɛse, ‘Tu me, tu me,’ ntuu no, na dɛn a ɛse, ‘Ntu me, ntu me,’ tu no.” Kɔkɔɔ no (hyɛɛ aseɛ), “Ah! asɛm bɛn a ɛyɛ kwasea na woka kyerɛ me yi, sɛ obi se ɔdɔ obi, woka kyerɛ me sɛ mɛka sɛ mɛkyere no tan, m’ani nni hwee a ɛte saa?” Nanso Kɔkɔɔ no kɔɔ afuo no. Berɛ a ɔduu hɔ no, pampae bi see, “Tu me, tu me,” afoforo bio see, “Ntu me, ntu me.” Na ɔtuu dɛn a ɛse, “Tu me, tu me,” na ɔde wɔn baa fie. Ɔbaa ha na ɔpuee wɔn; Akrabea see, “Kɔto aduane pa no firi hɔ, na fa ntoma no, hyɛlɛ dan mu, na fa hyɛ gyaman so.” Ananse hyɛɛ aseɛ, “Ah, asɛm bɔne bɛn na woka kyerɛ me yi; efisɛ Onyame bɔɔ nneɛma no, yɛto aduane pa no firi hɔ, na yɛgye ntoma no?” Kɔkɔɔ no faa aduane pa no, na ɔhyɛɛ no gyaman so, nanso ɔfaa ntoma ketewa bi hyɛɛ no ananmu. Berɛ a aduane no huu no, ɔfaa no, na ne nyinaa ayɛ aboɔ, nanso ntoma ketewa a ɔde hyɛɛ no ananmu no ayɛ aduane pa. Agya Kɔkɔɔ see, "Eh! "Saa ɔkwaseɛ a ɔasore yi anka n'asɛm nnyɛ atirigyeɛ (mpae)." Na Kwaku Ananse kɔtoo nnipa no nwene no na ɔde baa hɔ na ɔtoo no afuo mu. Berɛ a aduane no huu no, ɔfaa no efie, na ɛayɛ yampɔ a ɛyɛ fɛ te sɛnea wobɛpɛ sɛ wohu. Ɔsee, "Nana kɔkɔɔ, eyi yɛ fɛ paa, nanso seesei ɛsɛ sɛ wama me nam a mɛde aka ho." Nana kɔkɔɔ see, "Kɔhwehwɛ wɔ dua no so, ɛhɔ wɔ Aberewa; sɛ wukɔ a, wobɛse, 'Mo yɛ mmoa.'" Ananse see, "Aa, saa obiayewu yi! Me ne Aberewa nni nsɛnee biara, enti adɛn nti na mɛkɔ akasa wɔn saa?" Nana kɔkɔɔ see, "Kɔ, na sɛ asiane ba a, ɛyɛ me asɛm." Ananse kɔɔ. Ɔsee, "Mo Aberewa, mo a mo wɔ saa ndua yi so, Nana kɔkɔɔ se mo yɛ mmoa." Mmoa no see, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ Nana kɔkɔɔ nti a, yɛbɛbɔ wo." Nana kɔkɔɔ see, "Ɛnna bra na mobɔ no."

Ananse seeว่า, "Krokoro, eyi nyɛ nokware." Ɛno a ɛyɛ no, mmoa no bɔɔ no na wɔbaa hɔ bɔɔ Ananse. Krokoro no seeว่า, "Me nkyɛn mmerɛ-mu-mmerɛ, twa wɔn ti." Tintin! (ɛne nkyɛn no nne a ɛyɛɛ berɛ a ɔyɛɛ ne dwuma no). Krokoro-nananom no seeว่า, "Yɛyɛ no mmerɛ-mmerɛ seesei." Nanso Kwebia no antegye (ne asɛm no). Ɛnnɛ, Kwebia no boaa nam no ano na ɔsan kɔɔ fie, na Krokoro no kɔɔ baabi kakra. Ɛno a ɛyɛ no, Ananse sɔree ne nkyɛn no hyɛɛ no ne kotokuo mu, na ɔtuu mmirika kɔɔ ne kuro. Berɛ a ɔsan baa ne kuro no, ɔsee (ne ba no)ว่า, "Ntikuma, ɛnkyɛn yi nti a m'ani agye ho yi, ma yɛnkɔ kwaa." Enti wɔtuu mmirika, na wɔkɔhwee mmoa-kuro-mmoa (Wild Pigs) bi. Ananse seeว่า, "Ntikuma, kɔyɛ wɔn ho yaw." Ntikuma kɔseeว่า, "Kwaa-mmoa, mo yɛ fɛfɛ." Kwaa-mmoa no hyɛɛ aseɛ seeว่า, "Sɛ wo nnyɛ Kwaku Ananse, Kwebia ba, a yɛbɛba abɔ wo." Kwaku Ananse seeว่า, "Moma bɔ no, ɔyɛ onipa bɔne paa, ne ahantan nyɛ asɔre." Kwaa-mmoa no rehyɛ aseɛ rebɔ no, na Kwaku Ananse seeว่า, "Krokoro nkyɛn, Mmerɛ-mu-mmerɛ, twa wɔn ti ma me." Oh! na nkyɛn no bɔɔ wɔn, na ɔtwaa mmoa no ti nyinaa. Ɛno a ɛyɛ no, ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ retwa nnua no. Kwaku Ananse seeว่า, "Hai! dɛn na woreyɛ yi? Mekyereɛ sɛ twa mmoa no ti, na woayɛ no, na seesei dɛn na eyi yɛ?" Ntikuma hwɛɛ no kakra, na ɔseeว่า, "Yɛyɛ no mmerɛ-mmerɛ seesei." Kwaku Ananse antegye no, na nkyɛn no tɔɔ fam, na Ananse kɔfaa no. Wɔsan kɔɔ fie. Da a ɛba no, berɛ a nneɛma baa anim, Kwaku Ananse seeว่า wɔbɛkɔ Nyame hɔ. Ntikuma seeว่า, "Papa, mma woankɔ sɛ ɛyɛ nkyɛn yi nti." Ananse seeว่า, "Fitaa hɔ." Ntikuma seeว่า, "Kɔ wo kwan so." Ananse tuu mmirika, na ɔkɔɔ Nyame hɔ, na ɔsee noว่า, "Me Panin, nnɛ, sɛ obi bi a ɔayɛ wo yaw wɔ hɔ a, me nko, me, Kwaku Ananse, mɛtwa wɔn ti nyinaa da baako pɛ na makɔma wo." Nyame seeว่า, "Aseda-ayɛ wɔ hɔ ma obiara a obetumi ayɛ saa, efisɛ asafo kɛse bi gyina ku kuro no ho, enti kɔ." Ananse seeว่า, "Ma wɔn nna nsa ne onuasoma baako." Ɛno ara na wɔde baa. Ananse kɔɔ kwan no so; Ananse bisaa asraafoɔ a wɔyɛ ataki no sɛ, “Mo na mobɛkɔ ako ɔhaw ne Nyame?” Wɔgyee me ayɛ sɛ, “Aane, ɛno na yɛse.” Efiri hɔ, Nyame asraafoɔ no gyinaa Ananse akyi. Ɛnna Kwaku Ananse kasaa kyerɛ Akraa a ɛyɛ mmerɛne no, Ɔkwan-mu-yɛ-mmerɛne no, sɛ, “Twa saa asraafoɔ ataki yi anim ma me.” Na Akraa no twaa wɔn anim, tan! tan! na ɛwiee. Ananse see, “Ɛyɛ, gyae.” Nanso Akraa no tɔɔ Nyame asraafoɔ no so na ɛretwa (wɔn anim). Ananse see, “O, ɛno ne Nyame asraafoɔ, enti gyae.” Nanso ɛkɔɔ so twaa. Ananse see, “Sɛ wo bo afu a, ma ɛnyɛ dwo.” O! Nyame asraafoɔ nyinaa hwee. Ɛnna Akraa no tuu kwan wɔ wim; ɛbaa baabi a Kwaku Ananse bepue. Ɔsee, “Hwɛ yie, wonnim me? Wonnim me? Me ne Kwaku Ananse, me na mede wo fii mmerɛne no hɔ.” O! Ɛnna Kwaku Ananse ti tɔɔ fam, tan! Na Akraa no kɔɔ so kɔɔ; na baabi a ɛgyinaa no yɛyɛɛ dwumrudum bi a yɛfrɛ no “Etene”; ne nkyɛn yɛ mmerɛne paa, paa, paa.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
