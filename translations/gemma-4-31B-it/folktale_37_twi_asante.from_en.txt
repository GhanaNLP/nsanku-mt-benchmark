Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

DEƐN NTI NA ANANSE, KƆKƆRƆ NO, TUMM DEN BERƐ A ƆWƆ Nsuo Ano

KWAKU ANANSE, Kɔkɔre no, kɔka kyerɛɛ Okraman, Kraman no sɛ, “Mepɛ sɛ me ne wo kɔyɛ akuraa bebree a yɛbɛtena mu.” Kraman no kaa sɛ, “Me nso, mepɛ saa.” Ananse kaa sɛ, “Sɛ Adae-Kwasieda ba a, Dwumadie a ɛdi no akyi no, kɔfa nkuran; me nso, mɛfa nkuran na yɛnni akwan.” Bio, ɔkaa sɛ, “Mɛfa kuku na mɛfa nsuo afa mu, na wo nso, fa biribi, efisɛ beae a yɛrekɔ no, ebia nsuo renni hɔ.” Ananse faa nsuo; Kraman no nso, faa honey hyɛɛ ne kuku mu. Wɔnnam kɔree, na berɛ a wɔduu akwan no mma no, wɔbrɛɛ. Ananse kaa sɛ, “Ma yɛnom yɛn nsuo no.” Kraman no kaa sɛ, “Nom me dea bi.” Ananse nomee bi, na ɛka ne nsa; ɔkaa sɛ, “O, wei firi he?” Kraman no kaa sɛ, “Ɛwɔ me yamu.” Kɔkɔre no kaa sɛ, “Aane!” Berɛ a wɔtenaa hɔ sɛ wɔgye wɔn nkwa mu no, Ananse kaa sɛ, “Kraman, ma yɛnoa ayɛ.” Kraman no bisaa sɛ, “Ayɛ bɛn?” Kɔkɔre no gyee me answers sɛ, “Kyan-kyan ayɛ; wo na wobue me kan, na me nso, mɛbue wo, nanso sɛ wote me se, ‘Wui!’ a, gya.” Kraman no kaa sɛ, “Dabi, bue me kan.” Kɔkɔre no kaa sɛ, “Yɛn kɔ, sɛ wo yefuo reka biribi kyerɛ wo a, woka ho asɛm?” Kraman no kaa sɛ, “Ɛyɛ, ma ɛnkɔ sɛnea wopɛ.” Kɔkɔre no kaa sɛ, “Bra na bue me.” Kraman no faa ne nkuran no na ɔkyannee Kɔkɔre no. Na ɔfaa no hyɛɛ ne ti, ɔse ɔrekɔtɔ no na wakɔhwehwɛ aduane. Berɛ a Ananse tee saa no, ɔhyɛɛ ahoho no ase na ɔkaa sɛ:

“Me, Ananse Akuamoa, m’enni mmoa pa bi a mɛtɔn;

Kraman kwasea yi nkoaa, ɔrebɛtɔ me.

Hwan nea metumi anya a ɔbɛgyɛ me?

“O, Path, gyɛ me!”

Ananse ntumi ara na ɔsu, ɔsu. Kraman no faa no kɔsie nea ɔduu asubɔn bi. Ɛha na Odenkyem, Okrokor no, tee Spider no resu. Berɛ a ɔbaa hɔ no, ɔbisaa Kraman no sɛ dea ɛrekɔdwo ne den. Kraman no tɔɔ Ananse ha, na ɔtuu guan, na Okrokor no gyee Ananse. Ananse nso kaa sɛ, “Agya, adeɛ bɛn na metumi ama wo ama wuhu sɛ meye wo aseda?” Okrokor no kaa sɛ, “Yɛmpɛ adeɛ biara.” Ananse kaa sɛ, “Sɛ wowɔ mma a, ɛnna ɔkyena mɛba na mɛsere wɔn ti fɛfɛ.” Okrokor no kaa sɛ, "Mete." Ananse baa fie; ɔkaa sɛ, "Aso, kɔhwehwɛ abrobɛ na afuwo, efisɛ ɔkyena mɛkɔ bɛyere okrokor na yɛanya nkwan na yɛadi." Aso kɔfaa bi, na Spider no bɔɔ ne cutu no anim, na ɔnoae eto, na ɔfaa no kɔɔ asubɔn hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya Okrokor, Nsuo-aboa no, maba sɛ mɛma wo aseda." Na ɔfaa yam pɔtɛɛ no guu nsuo no mu. Okrokor no baa hɔ, na ɔrepa yam pɔtɛɛ no sɛ ɔbɛdi, ɛnna Spider no faa ne cutu no twaree no, gya! nanso ɔanni no (yie), na ɔtuu guan. Ananse san baa fie. Aso bisaa no sɛ, "Aboa no wɔ he?" Ananse kaa sɛ, "Gya me; obi ba akwantuo fi, na ɔnni ne nsam mpo, na wo na wofisa no nsɛm pii." Aso kaa sɛ, "Mahu sɛ woanni no." Ananse anka biribiara. Anɔpa a ɛdi so no, anɔpa paa, Aso kaa sɛ ɔrekɔ asubɔn hɔ. Berɛ a ɔduu hɔ no, Okrokor no daa asu no nkyɛn, na mmoa-mmoa (blue-bottle-flies) na ɛreka ho. Aso san baa fie na ɔkaa kyerɛɛ ne kunu. Ananse kaa sɛ, "Saa na aduro bi a mewɔ yɛ; sɛ meyere nam (da koro bi) a, mma adeɛ nyinaa nnyɛ hɔ, ɔkyena, na mɛkɔ fa, nanso mekyia wo sɛ wuhu." Ananse twaa dua bi na ɔfaa no kɔɔ asubɔn hɔ, na ɛha na ɔhyiaa Okrokor no. Ɔfaa dua no hwee no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Okrokor, woawu? Okrokor, woawu?" na ɔtrante no hwan. Okrokor no daa hɔ, nanso wuannee. Berɛ a Ananse repa ne nsam sɛ ɔbɛka no no, Okrokor no twaree no mpɛse, kua! Ananse bɔɔ mmɔden, petere! petere! na ɔhwehwɛɛ kwan na ɔtuu guan. Ɛno nti, sɛ Spider no fa nsuo so a, ɔtuu mmirika; ɔboa sɛ Okrokor no bɛba abyere no bio.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
