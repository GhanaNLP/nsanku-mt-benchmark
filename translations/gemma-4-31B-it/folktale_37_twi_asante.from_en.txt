Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

DEƐN NTI NA ANANSE, KƆKƆRƆ NO, TUMM DEN BERƐ A ƆWƆ Nsuo Ano

KWAKU ANANSE, Kwebia no, kɔkasaa Okraman, Kraman no sɛ, “Mepɛ sɛ me ne wo kɔyɛ akuraa ketewa bi a yɛbɛtena mu.” Kraman no kaa sɛ, “Me nso, mepɛ saa.” Ananse kaa sɛ, “Sɛ Kwasiedae ba a, ne mmofra Dwoada no, kɔfa nsoroma-kanea; me nso, mɛfa nsoroma-kanea na yɛnni akwan.” Bio, mɛfa kuku koro na mɛfa nsuo afura mu, na wo nso, fa nsuo kɔfa, efisɛ beae a yɛrekɔ no, ebia nsuo renni hɔ. Ananse faa nsuo kɔfa; Kraman nso, nyaa honey na wakɔhyɛ ne kuku mu. Wɔnam kɔe, na wɔadu kwan no mma, na wɔbrɛ. Ananse seeว่า, “Ma yɛnom yɛn nsuo bi.” Kraman seeว่า, “Nom me dea bi.” Ananse faa bi, na ɔfaa ne ano; ɔseeว่า, “O, ɛfi he ne ɛ?” Kraman seeว่า, “Ɛwɔ me yamu.” Kwebla seeว่า, “Aane!” Sɛ wɔtenaa hɔ kɔsaa wɔn nkwa no, Ananse seeว่า, “Kraman, ma yɛn nnom ayɛ ayɛ.” Kraman seeว่า, “Ayɛ dɛn?” Kwebla gyee kyɛn seeว่า, “Kyerɛ-kyerɛ ayɛ; y “Wo kyekyere me kan, na me nso mɛkyekyere wo, nanso sɛ wote me se, ‘Wui!’ a, ɛnna wo gyae.” Kraman no see, “Dabi, kyekyere me kan.” Kwebla no see, “Yɛn kɔ, sɛ wo yonu ka biribi kyerɛ wo a, wone no kasa?” Kraman no see, “Ɛyɛ, mogyae no (sɛnea wopɛ).” Kwebla no see, “Bra na kyekyere me.” Kraman no faa ne nkuran no na ɔkyekyere Kwebla no. Na ɔtoo no ne ti so, kyerɛɛ sɛ ɔrebɛtɔ no akata na ama ɔanya biribi adi. Berɛ a Ananse tee saa, ɔyɛɛ amanehunu na ɔsee:

“Me, Ananse Akuamoa, m’enni mmoa pa bi a mɛtɔn;

Kraman kwasea yi nkoaa, ɔrebɛtɔ me.

Hwan nea metumi anya a ɔbɛgyɛ me?

“O, Path, gyɛ me!”

Ananse betumi suuuuu kɛkɛ. Kraman faa no kɔɔ baabi a asuom bi wɔ. Ɛnnɛ, Odenkyem, Okrokrokuma no, tee Spider no resu. Berɛ a ɔbaa hɔ no, ɔbisaa Kraman no sɛ dɛn na ɛrekɔ on. Kraman no towii Ananse ase, na ɔtuu mmirika kɔɔ, na Okrokrokuma no gyee Ananse. Na Ananse see, “Agya, ade bɛn na metumi ama wo de akyerɛ sɛ me nnyɛ aseda?” Okrokro no see, “Yɛnmpɛ ade biara.” Ananse see, “Sɛ wowɔ mma a, ɛnna ɔkyena mɛba aba mu na mɛsere wɔn ti fɛfɛ.” Okrokro no see, "Mete." Ananse baa fie; ɔsee, "Aso, kɔ hwehwɛ abrobɛ ne efu, efisɛ ɔkyena mɛkɔ yɛ mogyabɔ na mɛde ba" "Kɔfa okrokɔbea aba na yɛanya sopɔm a yɛbɛdi." Aso kɔfaa bi, na Kwebla sɔree ne cutu no na ɔpɔɔ eto, na ɔfaa no kɔɔ asuo no ho. Ɔsee, "Agya Okrokɔbea, Nsuo-aboa no, mereba sɛ mɛde ayɛ wo nkyɛm." Na ɔfaa pɔnkɔtɛ no guu nsuo no mu. Ɔkrokɔre no bae, na ɔrepɛ sɛ ɔfa pampam no edi, nanso Ananse fa ne nsemma no twaa no, gya! nanso ɔanni no (yie), enti ɔkɔe. Ananse san kɔɔ fie. Aso bisaa no sɛ, "Ɛhene na ɛhɔ mmoa no wɔ?" Ananse kaa sɛ, "Kɔ hɔ; obi ba akwantuo mu, na ɔnni ne nnuane mu koraa, na wo hyɛ asei bɔ no nsɛm." Aso kaa sɛ, "Mahu sɛ woannya no." Ananse anka hwee. Anokwa anɔpa, anɔpa paa, Aso kaa sɛ ɔrekɔ asubɔn. Sɛ ɔduu hɔ no, Okrodaa bi da river bank no so, na mmoa a yɛfrɛ blue-bottle-flies no retu kwan wɔ ne ho. Aso san kɔɔ fie kɔkaee ne kunu. Ananse seeว่า, "Saa na wuhu sɛ aduro bi a me wɔ no yɛ adwuma; sɛ mehyɛ nam (da bi) a, mgyina kosi sɛ nneɛma nyinaa bɛyɛ hɔ, ɔkyena, na mekɔfa no, nanso mekyia wo sɛ woahu no." Ananse twaa dua bi na ɔfaa no kɔɔ asuom no ho, na ɛhɔ na ɔhyiaa Okrokyɛ. Ɔnoaa no ne dua no, na ɔka sɛ, "Okrokyɛ, woawu?" "Okrokro, woawu?" na ɔtrutruu no. Okrokro no tena hɔ anka, nanso w'anwu. Berɛ a Ananse repɛ sɛ ɔfa ne nsa akasa no no, Okrokro no bɔɔ no pɛpɛɛpɛ, kua! Ananse bɔɔ mmɔden, petere! petere! na ɔhwehwɛɛ kwan bɛtɔɔ ne ho. Eyi ara ne nea nti, sɛ Kwaku Ananse fa nsuo ho kɔ a, ɔtu mmirika; ɔboa sɛ Okrokro bɛba abisa no bio.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
