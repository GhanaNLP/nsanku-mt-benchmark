SƐNE OPƆNKO NO HONAM YƐƐ NTONTON

Na ɛyɛ mmerɛ a Atu ne Gyata bi tena hɔ, na Gyata no kaa kyerɛ Atu no sɛ, "Ma yɛnkɔ nhwehwɛ mmaa a yɛbɛware." Atu no kaa sɛ, "Ho." Ɛnna Gyata no hohorɔɔ, na ɔhyɛɛ n’ahoɔden, na ɔhyɛɛ ne sika nnuane, na ɔkyekyere ne mpaabo a ɛyɛ mmerɛ no, na ɔmaa Atu no sɛ ɔde akura ne ti. Na wɔtɔɔ nea ɛkyerɛ; wɔduu kuro bi. Ɛnna Atu no kaa sɛ, "Mmaa nyinaa ne Atadeɛ nyinaa, mekyia mo anwummerɛ." Wɔn nso gyee me answers sɛ, "Yɛn kɔkɔɔ." Gyata no nso duu hɔ; ɔkaa sɛ, "Mmaa nyinaa ne Atadeɛ nyinaa, mekyia mo anwummerɛ." Wɔn gyee no answers sɛ, "Wo a wo anom yɛ mmerɛ, woyɛ den sɛ wobɛkyia yɛn anwummerɛ?" Wɔtɔɔ nea ɛkyerɛ, na wɔduu kuro a ɛtɔ ne no akyi. Wansana, Atu no, kaa sɛ, "Mmaa nyinaa ne Atadeɛ nyinaa, mekyia mo anwummerɛ." Wɔn gyee no answers saa ara. Gyata no nso duu hɔ, na ɔkyiaa hɔn, na wɔn gyee no answers,

"Wo a wo anom yɛ mmerɛw no, wopɛ sɛ woma yɛn anwummerɛ nkyia?" Wɔn nan hyɛɛ ase; wɔn hyɛɛ kwan no so, na Leopard se, "Hwɛ ha! fa saa papaa a ɛyɛ mmerɛw no ma me, na wo nso nfa saa sika nketenkete yi na wo ho nhyɛ, sɛ ɛyɛ papaa a ɛyɛ mmerɛw no nti a mmaa no dɔ wo a, mɛhu." Na (Fly) nyaa (sika no), ɔhyɛɛ ne ho, na wɔn nan hyɛɛ ase kɔsi sɛ wɔduu kuro a ɛtɔ ne so. Leopard na ɔda anim, na papaa a ɛyɛ mmerɛw no wɔ ne ti; ɔhyɛɛ (kurofo no) nkyia. Wɔn se, "Mma wo mma yɛn nkyia, wo a wo anom yɛ mmerɛw no; ɛyɛ fɛ sɛ wode saa papaa a ɛyɛ mmerɛw yi kura." Afei Fly duu hɔ; ɔse, "Mmaa nyinaa ne Agya nyinaa, mema mo anwummerɛ nkyia." Na wɔn hyɛɛ no ae’n, na wɔse, "O wo dɔfo; wuo ne abofra a ɛyɛ fɛ sɛ yɛhwɛ no; sika nketenkete; ɛyɛ wo fɛ paa; sɛ ɛnyɛ bɔne a yɛbɛnya a, yɛbɛkɔ wo akyi wɔ wo akwantuo yi so." Na wɔkɔɔ anim. Ansa na wɔadu kwan no so, Leopard se, "Hei! m'adamfo, gyina hɔ, pue me sika nketenkete yi na ma me ntɛm, ntɛm, ntɛm." Berɛ a Fly puee sika no na ɔmaa no no, Leopard hwɛɛ kwan no ho na ɔtuu abebie a ɔbɛfa ama ɔbɛkyekyere Fly. Ɔse, “Mebɛkyekyere wo, na dɛn na wopɛ sɛ woka?” Na Fly se, “Hwee nni hɔ a mɛka.” Leopard kɔɔ so se, “Me ne wo nni hɔ a yɛadu yɛn kwan no... (na mmaa no nso adɔ wo akum).” Afei Leopard kyere no na ɔkyekyere no hyɛɛ beaeɛ bi wɔ dua bi ho. Na ɔkɔɔ hɔ na ɔkɔnnɔ hɔ. Ansa na berɛ pii bɛyɛ, Plantain faa kwan no so kɔɔ dwadwa, na berɛ a ɔhunuu no (Fly) no, ɔse:

Hwan ne ɔdeɛ ɔsi hɔ a ɔyɛ tuntum saa?

Hwan ne ɔde ho hyɛ hɔ a ɔho hyerɛ saa?

(Mmoa a ɛtu) see:

Me ne me, Mmoa, Kwao Boakye.

Adenen!

Gyahene Kofi Amo, Adenen!

Ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meba, na yɛabɛhwehwɛ mmaa.

Adenen!

Mmaa no se wɔnndɔ no.

Adenen!

Ɔapue me.

Adenen!

Adenen!

Ɔkwan-kyerɛfo, gyɛ me.

Adenen!

Sɛ woatumi akura me ayɛ a, fa kwan no kɔ.

“Adenen!” Nanso Borɔdeɛ no see no sɛ, “Gyata na wakyere wo, enti mentumi mma wo nkwa”; na ɔkɔɔ. Amankani, Kokoo, bae (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Aburo, Pepere, bae (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Atwibo, Pampana, bae (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Adua, Abobodeɛ, bae (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Akam, Mpotampotam, bae (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Akapinkye, Borɔdeɛ tɔro, bae (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Bankye, Bankye, bae (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Brofere, Brofere, bae (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Emo, Omɔ, bae (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Efrere, Pere, bae (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Habayere, Pampana kɔkɔɔ, bae (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Kwadu, Borɔdeɛ, bae (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Kontomere, Kokoo nnwura, bae (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Krobona, Abobodeɛ, bae (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Mako, Mako, bae (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Nkatee, Nkate, bae (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Nwiadowa, Nwiadowa, bae (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Nkuruma, Okro, bae (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; na seesei Dokon'fa, Kenki koraa, faa hɔ kɔe na ɔsee:

Hwan ne ɔdeɛ ɔsi hɔ a ɔyɛ tuntum saa?

Hwan ne ɔde ho hyɛ hɔ a ɔho hyerɛ saa?

Adenen!

Kenki-Koraa se, "Mete ase, mɛyɛ wo nka." Ɔkɔhɔn hɔ, na ɔyɛɛ no nka. Berɛ a ɔyɛeɛ no, mpofirim na Gyata baa hɔ. Ɔse, "Adɛn nti na woayɛ me onipa nka?" Ɔno (Kenki-Koraa) se, "Meyɛ no nka, na nea wopɛ sɛ woyɛ no, yɛ." Ɔno (Gyata) se, "Me nkyɛn, me ne wo rennyɛ hwee koraa, ɛnna me ne wo bɛkɔ nsa-nkɔm." Kenki-Koraa se, "Sɛ yɛrepɛ sɛ yɛkɔ nkɔm a, ma yɛnkɔ kwae, na yɛntwa ntan na yɛntan." Enti wɔkɔtwaa ntan, na wɔsan baa. Na ɔno (Kenki-Koraa) se, "Ma yɛntan." Na Gyata tanaa. Wɔn aasan kɔhyiaa, (na ne nne te sɛ) Yiri! Yiri! Yiri! Na Gyata tuu Kenki-Koraa (firi ne nsam), na ɔtoo no, bam! Kenki-Koraa se, "(Nea te sɛ saa nnyɛ hwee), obi fa saa efie kɔhura baa ne wiaa beae." Na wɔhyiaa bio. Na Kenki-Koraa nso tuu Gyata firi ne nsam, bam! Na Gyata se, "Me nso fa nea te sɛ saa kɔhura me baa wiaa beae, (nanso) yɛn hyia a ɛtɔ so mmienu yi, obi a ɔtu obi firi ne nsam no, ɛno ne ne tow a ɛkɔsie." Na wɔkɔhyiaa (nkɔm, ne nne te sɛ) Kikiri! Kikiri! Kikiri! Na Kenki-Koraa gyee Gyata ansa na ɔtoo no gya mu. Gyata se, "_Me firim, me firim, me firim_" (Mefi hɔ, mefi hɔ, mefi hɔ. Asaae na Gyata nyaa ne nne "_Me firim_" no). (Ɛnna) wɔhunuu sɛ ne nipadua te sɛ saa, kurukyire, kurukyire, kurukyire (yehwɛ, ne ho nyinaa yɛ nsoroma); baabi a ɛyɛ funuf no, ɛno ne baabi a nkyekyɛmu bɔɔ no; na baabi a ɛyɛ tuntum no, ɛno nso ne baabi a nkuku tuntum bɔɔ no. Na sɛ worenantia, na wohunu dwumfuo a Kenki-Koraa (wrapped) no wɔ hɔ a, wobɛhunu mmoa mmoa pii a wɔatumi ano; na ɛnyɛ ade foforo biara (na mmom), wɔredi no ase.

Me asɛm yi a meaka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, obiara betumi agye nea ɛyɛ nokware, na na akyerɛ no nso, mobɛtumi ayɛ me ayɛyie (sɛ meaka no).
