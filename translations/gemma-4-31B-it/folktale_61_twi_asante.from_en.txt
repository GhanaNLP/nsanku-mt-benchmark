Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛ obi kɔ adan mu anadwo a, ɛyɛ sum siane nea ɛsi.

Wɔka sɛ Kuntukurunku, Odds, ne Kuntunamoa, Ends, tɔɔ fii ceiling no ase baa asase so. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Asase-yefuɛ, wo yɛ (too) papapaye." Asase-yefuɛ kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na mokaa sɛ me yɛ (too) papapaye?" Wɔgyee komm sɛ, "Ɛyɛ efisɛ woantwa awu yie a yɛbɛda mu." Asase-yefuɛ hwehwɛɛ Prae, Prakyɛ, na wakaa sɛ, "Wo nan (awura) mu yɛ akɔkɔɔ mmoa mmoa." Ɔgyee komm sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woyɛ me nkontompo?" Asase-yefuɛ kaa sɛ, "Sɛ meyɛ wo nkontompo a, meyɛ wo nkontompo; sɛ wo nni" "te asew nea enti, gyae nsibu." Broom hwehwɛɛ Abena, The-strip-of-bark (bea a wɔbo dwomo gu mu no), na ɔsee no, "Wo fa (nneɛma) kɔ tow agu akyirikyiri." Strip-of-bark see no, "Adɛn nti na woyɛ me yaw?" Broom see no, "(Sɛnea ɛte) na mɛyɛe." Strip-of-bark kasaa Sumina, Midden-heap, nso, ɔsee no, "Wo gyina nea w’anya no." Midden-heap see no, "Adɛn nti na woyɛ me yaw?" Ɔno (Strip-of-bark) r gyee sɛ, "Madi wo ho ye, na sɛ worenim nea enti a, gyae nansie." Midden-heap nso kasaa kyerɛɛ Abe, Palm-tree, sɛ, "Wo kɔn no nyinaa ayɛ mmoa." Palm-tree kasaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na wodi me ho ye?" Midden-heap gyee nkwa sɛ, "Madi wo ho ye, na sɛ worennim nea enti a, gyae nansie." Palm-tree nso dan kɔɔ Tetea, Little-black-ant, nkyɛn na ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo soore yi yɛ bla" "ck." Kwaku Duatwe se, "Adɛn nti na woyɛ me yaw?" Abufuo dua no hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, "Meyɛ wo yaw, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a, gyae nsɛmyɛ." Kwaku Duatwe no nso kɔɔ Esono, na ɔkɔɔ ne nan ho; na ɔkɔtwee Esono no. Berɛ a ɔregyae ne nan no, ɔtraa Tamiriwa, Kɔkɔɔ-Snail no ani ase pɛpɛɛpɛ, na Kɔkɔɔ-Snail no ani hyɛɛ nsuo, na ɔpuee nsuo no wɔ efan aba leaf a ɛwɔ hɔ no so. (Saa ara), aduane a Nana-Kɔkɔɔ di ne efan plant no, na ɛno na Tamiriwa, Kɔkɔɔ-Snail no de puee nsuo a ɛhyɛɛ ne ani no. Kraman, Kraman Kraman no, na ɔreakɔ, na ɔna (Ɔbaa kɛseɛ no) frɛɛ Kraman na ɔtoo n’atadeɛ (a ɔde akura) no hyɛɛ Kraman tirim. Berɛ a Kraman rekɔ no, ɔnyaa kola bi, na ɔtwee baako kaa no mmienu, na ɔtoo baako hyɛɛ Okagya, Sika-kraman no, n’apɔn mu. Okagya no nso, ɔfaa kola no na ɔde bɔɔ Onyina, Ɔdan-dua no. Ɔdan-dua no nso, bo fuu ne bo. Ɔhunuu sɛ abofra ketewa bi renyina hɔ, enti ɔtuu ne nnua baako na ɔbɔɔ no kɔsi sɛ ɔwui. Saa abofra ketewa no ne Nyankonpon, Onyame a ɔwɔ soro no ba. Ɛhɔ ara na wɔkyinii Ɔdan-dua no, na wɔde no kɔɔ Onyame anim. Wɔbisaa no (Onyina), sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsii?" Onyina no kaa sɛ, "Megyei m'akwan so wɔ me baabi, na saa Okraman yi—ɛfɛ sɛ ɔyɛ akokoa—(yɛɛ dɛ) ɔyɛɛ nkrane koraa na ɔtoo me so, enti me nso, berɛ a saa abofra yi fa kwan no, me dwene sɛ ɔyɛ Okagya, Okraman no, na mepueu me nnua baako na mebɔɔ no, na hwɛ, ɛyɛ wo ba." Nyame, Nyame se, "Fa no kɔ na fa kɔtɔ no wɔ dua bi mu, na frɛ Okagya, Aberewa no, ma ɔnyɛ." Okagya bae; ɔse, "Me Panin, wuhu sɛ me kyɛn ne kola, nanso Okraman, Kraman no, kɔfaa baako, na ɔtoo no me pɔn mu, na berɛ a merepɔ no no, ɛkɔbɔɔ Ogya-Odua-Kenten." Nyame se, "Fa no, na fa kɔtɔ no wɔ dua bi mu, na frɛ Kraman no ma ɔnyɛ." T Wɔkɔfrɛɛ Kraman no sɛ ɔmme. Kraman no see, "Me panin, merenantia me ankasa baabi—seesei wuhu sɛnea (mɛyɛ mmerɛ) yi—na Nana Akokɔfo took her bundle of firewood na ɔde guu me ti so; seesei Okagya, Sika-dwa no, ɔno na ɔda ne abu mu wɔ afuo hɔ a Nana Akokɔfo ayɛ na ama ɔanya biribi adi, nanso me na Nana Akokɔfo maame ma me kyere ne ntan; me nso, mefaa kola-nut a na ɔtoo no ne pɔnkyɛn no mu." Onyame see, "Fa no, kyekyere no hyɛn nnompe bi mu, na frɛ Nana-Kyerɛma ma ɔnyɛ." Nana-Kyerɛma bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ah, me nananom, hwɛ me anom, me a menntumi nntu nam, efan nnua ne nea mede di; bio, me wo Bosompra; enti me kyia ne Tamiriwa, kɔkɔɔ-suna; Kraman nkoaa na ɔnim eyi, na ɔkɔkaa Tamiriwa, na ɔmaa ɔfaa nsuo fii ne anom, na ɔtoo no efan nnua a mede tera ho no so." "Ɛno nti na mede me ntan nnuane hyɛɛ ne nsam na wakura." Nyame see, "O, fa no na kɔkyere no hyɛ nnuaba mu, na kɔfa Tamiriwa, Snail-Kɔkɔɔ no mma." Snail-Kɔkɔɔ no bae. Ɔsee, "Me Panin, na me wɔ hɔ biara, hwɛ, Elephant kɛse bi trodee me ani ase, enti me kɔfaa efan (leaf) bi na makasa so." "Ɛno ne nea mehu fa ho nyinaa." Onyame see, "Fa no kɔ na fa n'anim kyea mpaabo a ɛyɛ kɛse, na monka Esono, Ɛna." Wɔn kɔfaa Esono baa. Ɛlefant no see sɛ, "Ɛnnɛn mmiɛnsa a ɛkyɛ no, na mete hɔ, na me skin no hyɛɛ me nsa, na berɛ a mehwee hɔ no—fa me nkyerɛaseɛ—saa kwasea Little-black-ant yi hyɛree (me skin no) sɛnea ɛbɛhwehwɛ nnam na akɔm mma no, na ama ɛatumi ayɛ kɛse; na berɛ a merepue me nan no, ɛkɔtwaa Tamiriwa, Black-snail no." Onyame see sɛ, "Fa no na kɔkyekyere no wɔ dua kɛse bi mu, na fa bra "Tetea, mmoa-tuntum ketewa no." Berɛ a Tetea bae no, ɔse, "Ɔhene, wɔn nni palm-tree bi; berɛ a ɛwo no, (ne nnuaba no) yɛ tuntum kan; berɛ a me, Tetea mmoa-tuntum ketewa no, nantew wɔn so no, ɛma wɔn yɛ fɛ." Da bi na mebaa ha, na Palm-tree ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ me nso mmofra nti, me dwene sɛ mɛkɔ Elephant hɔ, kɔhwehwɛ nam na matu kwan ama me akɔre kɛse.” Sky-god see, “Fa no na fa kyekyere no hyɛ nnompe mu, na kɔfrɛ Palm-tree ma ɔmra.” Abe, Palm-tree no bae; Seesei ɛnnɛ mprempren a mede kɔɔ hɔ no, Kuntukurunku ne Kuntunamoa, Odds and Ends, tooi me so, na meka sɛ, ‘O! ‘Dɛn na ɛrekɔ?’ na wɔse, ‘Woantwa awu a yɛbɛda mu, na wo ne wo nkyene nyinaa nni faeda (biara).’ Enti, me nso, meboaa Prae, Ɔkɔm. Onyame se, “Fa no kɔkyekyere no wɔ dua bi mu, na kɔfa Odds and Ends.” Odds and Ends baa. Wɔkaa sɛ, “Ɔhene, yɛwɔ soro, na yɛrebrɛ, na yɛtɔɔ fam na yɛyɛɛ yɛn ho yaw; ɛno nti na yɛdidi Asase-yɛfuo no, na yɛse ɔyɛ papaye.” Nyame kaa sɛ, “Kwadwomfo, yɛate asɛm yi nyinaa, enti ma yɛnsɔre na yɛnkɔ adom hɔ na yɛhwɛ adanse no na yɛaba na yɛmfa nsɛm no nnyɛ nsɛm.” Enti wɔsɔree na wɔnkɔɔ adom hɔ, na wɔgyae door no. Ɛno nti, sɛ wokɔ adan mu anadwo—sum—a, Nyame ne ne Mpanyimfoɔ rehyia wɔ hɔ.
