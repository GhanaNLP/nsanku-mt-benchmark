Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛ obi kɔ adan mu anadwo a, ɛyɛ sum siane nea ɛsi.

Wɔka sɛ Kuntukurunku, Odds, ne Kuntunamoa, Ends, tɔɔ fii soro ba asase so. Wɔka sɛ, "Asase-akorafo, wo yɛ (too) papapaye." Asase-akorafo ka sɛ, "Adɛn nti na mokase sɛ me yɛ (too) papapaye?" Wɔgyee mmuae sɛ, "Efisɛ woantwa awu yie ma yɛn na yɛn akɔda mu." Asase-akorafo hwehwɛɛ Prae, Pra, na ɔka sɛ, "Wo ano (atwa) akoko mmoa." Ɔgyee mmuae sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woyɛ me nkontompo?" Asase-akorafo ka sɛ, "Sɛ meyɛ wo nkontompo a, meyɛ wo nkontompo; sɛ wonte nea enti a, gyae nansie." Pra hwehwɛɛ Abena, Abena-kuku (a wɔka nsuo ne nnuane mu), na ɔka sɛ, "Wogye (nneɛma) na wokɔ tow wɔn afire." Abena-kuku ka sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woyɛ me nkontompo?" Pra ka sɛ, "(Sɛdeɛ ɛte) meyɛ saa." Abena-kuku nso kasaa Sumina, Sumina-dwum, na ɔka sɛ, "Wokura nea wugye." Sumina-dwum ka sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woyɛ me nkontompo?" Ɔ (Abena-kuku) gyee mmuae sɛ, "Meyɛ wo nkontompo, na sɛ wonte nea enti a, gyae nansie." Sumina-dwum nso kasaa Abe, Abe, na ɔka sɛ, "Wo kɔn nyinaa ayɛ mmoa." Abe ka sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woyɛ me nkontompo?" Sumina-dwum gyee mmuae sɛ, "Meyɛ wo nkontompo, na sɛ wonte nea enti a, gyae nansie." Abe nso hwehwɛɛ Tetea, Tetea-mmoa, na ɔka sɛ, "Wo aso yɛ tuntum." Tetea-mmoa ka sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woyɛ me nkontompo?" Abe gyee mmuae sɛ, "Meyɛ wo nkontompo, na sɛ wonte nea enti a, gyae nansie." Tetea-mmoa nso kɔɔ Esono, Esono hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ne nan ho; na ɔtwaa Esono no. Berɛ a ɔ (Esono) regyae ne nan no, ɔtraa Tamiriwa, Tamiriwa-tuntum, ne ani ase, na Tamiriwa-tuntum ani mframa hyɛɛ, na ɔpuee mframa no wɔ efan abaawa a ɛwɔ hɔ. (Saa ara), aduane a Akorafo-kuna di ne efan abaawa no, na ɛhɔ na Tamiriwa, Tamiriwa-tuntum, puee ne ani mframa no. Kraman, Kraman, refa hɔ, na ɔ (Akorafo-kuna) frɛɛ Kraman na ɔfaa n’aduan (a ɔde reba) na ɔmaa Kraman faa no ne ti so. Berɛ a Kraman refa hɔ no, ɔnyaa kola-nut, na ɔpiraa baako mmienu, na ɔtoo baako kɔ Okagya, Okagya, ne awu mu. Okagya nso gyee kola-nut no na ɔpiraa Onyina, Onyina, ne ho. Onyina nso, ne bo fuu yie. Ɔhunuu sɛ abofra ketewa bi refa hɔ, na ɔtwaa ne nnua baako na ɔbɔɔ no na ɔwui. Ɛnnɛ, abofra keten no ne Nyankonpon, Onyame, ba. Ɛhɔ ara na wɔkyee Odum no, na wɔde kɔɔ Onyame anim. Wɔbisaa no (Odum no) sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsi?" Odum no kaa sɛ, "Megye me ho gyinaa me baabi, na saa Okagya-Sika-kraman yi—ɛnam sɛ ɔyɛ abofra—(yɛɛ ahantan) na ɔfaa kola-nut kɔbɔɔ me, enti me nso, berɛ a saa abofra yi faakyerɛ me, me dwene sɛ ɔyɛ Okagya, Sika-kraman no, na mepuee me nnua baako kɔbɔɔ no, na hwɛ, ɔyɛ wo ba." Nyame, Onyame, kaa sɛ, "Fa no nkɔkyekyere no wɔ dua bi mu, na frɛ Okagya, Sika-kraman no ma ɔnyɛ." Okagya bae; ɔkaa sɛ, "Me Panin, wuhu sɛ me kyia ne kola-nut, nanso Okraman, Kraman no, kɔfaa baako, na ɔtoo no me pɔn mu anamu, na berɛ a meretoo no firi hɔ no, ɛkɔbɔɔ Odum Panin no." Onyame kaa sɛ, "Fa no, kyekyere no wɔ dua bi mu, na frɛ Kraman no ma ɔnyɛ." Wɔkɔfrɛɛ Kraman no ma ɔbae. Kraman no kaa sɛ, "Me Panin, merenantee me baabi—hwɛ sɛnea (mɛyɛ mmerɛ)—na Nana Panin baa, ɔfaa ne ntan duaa kɔtoo me tiw; nanso Okagya, Sika-kraman no, ɔno na ɔda ne pɔn mu wɔ afuo a Nana Panin ayɛ na ɔanya aduane, nanso me na Nana Panin maame kuraa ne ntan duaa; me nso, mefaa kola-nut kɔtoo no ne pɔn mu anamu." Onyame kaa sɛ, "Fa no, kyekyere no wɔ dua bi mu, na frɛ Nana Panin ma ɔnyɛ." Nana Panin bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ah, me mma, hwɛ me anom, me a menntumi nnam nam, efan nnua ne dɛn a mede di; bio, me wo Bosompra; enti me kyia ne Tamiriwa, Sika-kraman-kɔkɔɔ no; Kraman no nko ara na ɔnim eyi, na ɔkɔkaaa Tamiriwa, na ɔmaa ɔfaa nsuo firi ne anom, na ɔtoo no efan nnua a mede te ho no so. Ɛno nti na mede me ntan duaa maa no kuraa." Onyame kaa sɛ, "Oh, fa no kyekyere no wɔ dua bi mu, na kɔfa Tamiriwa, Sika-kraman-kɔkɔɔ no mma." Sika-kraman-kɔkɔɔ no bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me Panin, megyee me ho gyinaa me baabi, hwɛ, Esono kɛse bi trɛɛ me anigye ase, na mefaa efan (nnua) kɔhwee so. Ɛno ne nea mehu." Onyame kaa sɛ, "Fa no kyekyere no wɔ dua bi mu, na fa Esono mma." Wɔkɔfaa Esono. Ɛlefu no see sɛ, "Da mmiensa a ɛkyɛ no, na mete hɔ, na me skin no hyɛɛ me nka, na berɛ a mehwee hɔ—fa me nkyerɛkyerɛn ne asɛm yi—saa kwasea Little-black-ant yi hyɛɛ (me skin) mu sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛhwehwɛ nam akasa, na ama ɔatumi ayɛ kɛse; na berɛ a merepue me nan no, ɛkɔtwaa Tamiriwa, Black-snail no." Nyame see, "Fa no kɔkyekyere no wɔ dua kɛse bi mu, na bra Tetea, Little-black-ant no." Berɛ a Tetea bae no, ɔsee, "Ɔhene, Palm-tree bi wɔ hɔ; berɛ a ɛwo no, (ne nnuaba no) yɛ tuntum mpanin; berɛ a me, Little-black-ant, nantew wɔ ne so no, ɛma ɛyɛ fɛ. Da bi na mete hɔ, na Palm-tree no kaa kyerɛ me sɛ me nso mmienu yɛ tuntum, na ɛfirisɛ meyɛ ketewa, enti mepem sɛ mɛkɔ Ɛlefu no hɔ, na makɔhwehwɛ nam akasa na ama mɛatumi ayɛ kɛse.” Nyame see, “Fa no kɔkyekyere no wɔ dua kɛse bi mu, na kɔfrɛ Palm-tree no na ɔmra.” Abe, Palm-tree no bae; ɔsee, “Ɔhene, eyi ne nea mɛka: Me ne Sumina, Midden-heap, yɛte bɔm, na berɛ a nnuaba a wɔto no so yɛ pii, na onni nka bio no, megye bi na mehyɛ me kɔn no; nanso seesei, ɔyɛɛ me nnyigyei, ɔkaa sɛ m'anim wɔ me kɔn no. Enti, ɛno nti, me nso, me ani bɛyɛɛ kɔkɔ, na me nso, meyɛɛ Little-black-ant no nnyigyei.” Nyame see, “Fa no kɔkyekyere no wɔ dua kɛse bi mu, na frɛ Sumina, Midden-heap, na ɔmra.” Midden-heap bae; ɔsee, “Ɔhene, Abena, Strip-of-bark, nea berɛ a wɔpue dwum no wɔ dan mu no, wɔma no na ɔde bra me hɔ. Da bi, ɛnna obi ayɛ no hwee, ɔkaa kyerɛ me sɛ, ‘Wode nea w’anya no hyɛ mu’ (ɛyɛ)." yɛyɛ anifere); mekaa ‘a’, na efisɛ Palm-tree wɔ hɔ nso, me nso meboaa no. Nyame kaa sɛ, “Fa no kɔ na fa no kyekyere mu wɔ duku bi mu, na frɛ Abena, Strip-of-bark, ma ɔnyɛ.” Strip-of-bark bae; ɔkaa sɛ, “Me Pan, sɛ Prae, the Broom, sɔre mpaebɔ mu a, sɛ wɔmpɛ m’akwan a, mframa bɔ a, ntokuro nyinaa san ba; nnɛ, da bi a, ɛmfa asa me hwee, nanso ɔkaa sɛ, ‘Wogye nneɛma na woatow no fɔ.’ Efisɛ saa, me nso m’ani bɔɔ me, na mekɔboaa Sumina, the Midden-heap.” Nyame kaa sɛ, “Fa no kɔ, na fa no kyekyere mu wɔ duku bi mu, na frɛ Prae, the Broom, ma ɔnyɛ ha.” The Broom bae; ɔkaa sɛ, “Me Pan, Old-mother-earth da hɔ tɛntɛn, ne wiase yɛ dɔtɛ, enti sɛnea me sɔre a, meboaa ntokuro nyinaa a ɛwɔ ne so na mema ɔyɛ fɛ; nanso da bi a, Mother-earth kasaa me, ɔkaa sɛ, ‘Wo ano yɛ (awo ne) akoko mmoa,’ na me nso, me bo fuu, na mekɔboaa Abena, the Strip-of-bark.” Nyame kaa sɛ, “Fa no kɔ na fa no kyekyere mu wɔ duku bi mu, na frɛ Mother-earth ma ɔnyɛ.” Berɛ a Mother-earth bae no, ɔkaa sɛ, “Me Pan, me da hɔ; wɔtram me; me nka hwee; wɔyɛ nneɛma ahodoɔ nyinaa wɔ me so, nanso me nka hwee. Nnɛ da bi a, berɛ a me da hɔ no, Kuntukurunku ne Kuntunamoa, Odds and Ends, tɔɔ me so, na mekaa sɛ, ‘O! dɛn na ɛrekɔ so?’ na wɔkaa sɛ, ‘Wontwa awu a yɛbɛda mu, na wo ne wo tɛntɛn nyinaa nnyɛ asomdwoe (ma obiara).’ Efisɛ saa, me nso meboaa Prae, the Broom.” Nyame kaa sɛ, “Fa no kɔ na fa no kyekyere mu wɔ duku bi mu, na kɔfa Odds and Ends.” Odds and Ends bae. Wɔkaa sɛ, “Me Pan, yɛwɔ ceiling no so, na yɛresekye, na yɛtɔɔ ase na yɛyɛɛ yɛn ho yaw; efisɛ saa na yɛboaa Mother-earth, yɛkaa sɛ ɔyɛ tɛntɛn.” Nyame kaa sɛ, “Kwadwofo, yɛase asɛm yi nyinaa, enti ma yɛnsɔre nkɔ mu na yɛhwɛ adanse no na yɛasan mba ma yɛde nsɛm no bɔ nsɛm.” Enti wɔsɔree na wɔkɔɔ mu, na wɔgyaeɛ pon no. Efisɛ saa, sɛ wukɔ mu anadwo—sum—a, Nyame ne ne Kwadwofo rehyia hɔ.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
