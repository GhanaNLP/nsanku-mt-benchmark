Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea ɛyɛɛ koraa sɛ abɔfoɔ yɛ mmofra

Aberewa bi (tease) kɔtoo gun-medicine (powder) ne lead mmienu ma bullets wɔ borɔne so, na ɔne ne yere ne ne ba ketewa kɔɔ kwae kɔtoo nam. Na ɔyɛɛ aberewa dan na ɔne ne yere ne ne ba tenaa mu. Berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ, ɔkɔɔ kwae. Berɛ a ɔhunuu aboa bi na ɔyɛɛ ne gun no, ɛnyinii. Da biara na ɛkɔɔ saa, nnansa mmienu mmienu nna mmienu mpem. Na ne yere kaa sɛ, “Sɛdeɛ menyaa nam bi a mɛdi a, mɛsan akɔ fie.” Da a ɛdi so no, berɛ a ɔbɔdeɛ kɔɔ kwae no, ne yere ne ne ba nso kɔɔ wɔn akuraa mu. Seesei, nsuo baako pɛ na ɛtena hɔ. Afei meyi foo sɛ ɔyadeɛ no din ne Gyekye, na ne yere no din nso ne Boniaye-kae-dabi (a nenkyerɛase ne sɛ, Da bi na onnyaso-ase-fo no bɛkae). Berɛ a ɔyadeɛ no rekɔwɔn no, ɔduu odum dua bi ase, na hɔ yɛ beaeɛ a ɛyɛ fɛ paa. Ɛhɔ na ɔbɔfo no sumsumu muTwe kyerɛɛ ne ho, sɛ, “Sɛ mekɔ fie a, wɔbɛhwehwɛ sika hu a mɛtɔ nsuo no, na menni bi, enti mɛfa asemu yi na mɛyɛ me ho mpaebɔ wɔ me kɔn mu na mawu.” Mpofirim na Biribi frɛɛ no, sɛ, “Bɔfo no Bɔfo.” Na ɔbɔfo no kaa sɛ, “Agya.” Na Biribi no kaa sɛ, “Adɛn nti na w'ani tiri kɔkɔɔ saa?” Sɛ obi a yɛfrɛ no ‘Kɔ-sɛe-fie’ nni wo fie a, na mɛmaa wo biribi.’ Ɛnna ɔbɔfo no see, “Hwee nni fie.” Biribi see, “Sɛ hwee nni fie a, kɔ odum dua no mframa mu, adawuru nnawɔtwe mmienu wɔ hɔ, enti faa wɔn bra.” Ɛnna ɔbɔfo no kɔfaa wɔn baa. Na biribi kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Kɔ twa odwen nnuaba mmienu, na fa wo ani tiri, na tutu kɔ fa baako to hɔ, na fa ebi foforo no nso nkyerɛ wo ho na to hɔ.” Berɛ a ɔbaa hɔ no, ɔseɛ, “Mewie.” Biribi kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Fa ’dawuru kɛse no na kwa.” Ɔno nso kwaa no, na kwae no yɛɛ kuro a ɛtrɛ nnuaba mmienu no ntam. Na odum dua no gyina ne mmuame. Na saa Biribi no hyɛ ne ho anim kyerɛɛ sɛ ɔyɛ Akɔkorɔ. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔyadeɛfoɔ no sɛ, sɛ asɛm bi a ɛho hia ba sɛ wɔbɛsiesie no a, ɛsɛ sɛ wɔtie no odum dua no ase. Ɔyadeɛfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Ho, meate.” Berɛ a Akɔkorɔ no kaa eyi kyerɛɛ no no, ɔyadeɛfoɔ no nyaa yɛnkyɛfoɔ, akyeame, ne asuafoɔ. Ɛno berɛ no, ne yere a wɔfrɛ no “Da bi na onnyaso-ase-no bɛkae” huu sɛ ɔayɛ odikani, enti ɔtɔɔ nsa duaa, na ɔfaa no kɔɔ odikani no kuro mu, na ne ba no wɔ ne kyɛn. Berɛ a ɔduu hɔ no, ɔkaa ne baeɛ nsɛm no sɛ, “Wo baafoɔ no se ɔde nsa ba sɛ neyɛ a ɔbɛhu wo anim.” Ɛna ɔnyinii nsa no, na ɔmaa obiara a ɔwɔ hɔ nom. Na ɔfaa ɔbaa no maa Gyasehene sɛ ɔnfa no akɔ ne fie. Berɛ a anadwo tɔɔ nwunu no, ɔbaa no woyɛɛ ne ho mmerɛ na ɔkɔɔ ɔhene hɔ ne poku mu. Ɔbaa no kaa kyerɛ ɔhene no sɛ, “Wo ne me anya nsuo ne owia, nnɛ wo anya baabi a wobɛtena, enti ɛnyɛ nokware sɛ woapɛ sɛ wofrɛ me?” Berɛ a ɔreka saa asɛm yi—sɛnne mmarima ne wɔn kwaseaa—ɔhene no anya biribiara a ɔbɛka. Ɛhɔ ara na ɔkyere ɔbaa no, na wɔn adua mmoa. Anɔpa a ɛdi so no, ɔfaa no fii Gyase Chief hɔ kɔmaa ɔhene no harem mu. Ɔtena hɔ kosi sɛ nsɛnee kɛse bi bae. Ɔhene ne ne mpanyimfo nyinaa kɔtee ase odum dua no. Ɔbaa no ba no gyina ne agya anim, na ɔhunu Okuruo no ba no wɔ odum dua no ataso, na ɔse, “Agya, mepɛ no.” Na ne agya bɔɔ ne mpaa, na ɔtumi ne ti akyi, na ɔma ɔsan kɔɔ fie. Na mmaa foforo no ba bi nso bae, na ɔgyinaa ne anim. Ɔhwee Okuruku no ba no nso, na ɔse, “Papa, mepɛ no.” Na ɔbɔɔ ne nsam mpo saa ara a, ɔtumiu ne ti a ɛma ɔtɔɔ ase, na berɛ a ɔkɔe no, ɔsuu. Na ne maame bisa no se, “Dɛn nti na wosu?” Na ɔse, “Papa bɔɔ me.” Na ne maame se, “O! Adɛn nti na obiara nnim sɛ ɔrenbɔ wo, sɛ Some-day-the-ungrateful-one-will-remember baa na ɛyɛ a, ɔrenbɔ no.” Na Some-day-the-ungrateful-one-will-remember see, “Sɛdeɛ ɛte no, hwee na m'ayɛ wo, na woyɛ me saa.” Ansa na ɔbɛka no awie, na wɔnompoa na wɔakɔ bɔ mu akɔtɔ. Na ankafoɔ no see, “Sɛdeɛ eyi a ɛwɔ dua yi ase yi nti, yɛn agya nkuasefoɔ no reko; yɛbɛtwa dua yi.” Ɛno ara a wɔfaa wɔn pawa no, pimpen! pimpen! (ɛno ne nne a wɔyɛɛ no.) Saa berɛ no, Okɔkɔɔ no kɔree nkwagye a ɔfa ne mma no kɔtoo hɔ, na ɔgyee wɔn hyɛ Ɔkraman ne Okɔkɔɔ Kɛseɛ nsam. Berɛ a nnipa no twaa dua no na ɛreware no, Ɔkraman no teɛɛ nsɛm a ɛyɛ animuonyam no sɛ, "Pimpeao!" Na dua no bɔɔ mu. Na wɔsan kɔɔ ne nkyɛn bio. Na Ɔkraman no kaa kyerɛ Okuruku no sɛ, "Kɔ hwehwɛ Akɔkɔdua na ama wakɔhwɛ ne mma, efisɛ me nso, owuo aba me so." Mpofirim ara, nnipa no kɔfaa wɔn gun no na wɔnam so kɔtoo no na wɔwoo no. Na Old-Hawk tuu ne ho kɔfrɛ Eagle. Ɔkɔdwee no nyinaa no, ɔteɛm kyerɛɛ sɛ:

Nana e! wo mma o!

Nana e! wo mma o!

Berɛ a ɔfrɛɛ saa na Okurɛkroni anyɛ nka no, ɔyɛɛ ne nne den na ɔsuu:

Sankur' e, Anomaa e, sankur' e, Anomaa, Akɔkorɔ.

Sankur' e, Anoma e, sankur' e, sɛ woakɔ adidi a, san bra.

Na Okɔkɔɔno no tee. Na ɔbaa, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛrekɔ so wɔ ha?" Na Old-Hawk kaa sɛ, "Wɔatwa dua no akum na atu wo mma, na Lizard nso, wɔayɛ gunu so akum." Ɛno ara a, Eagle tɔne kɔ soro, wa (ne ntaban nka no yɛ saa), na ɔtɔɔ dua bi a ɛwɔ kurom no ponu, na ɔhwee n'ani, na ɛyɛ nokware sɛ wɔretwa dua no. Ɔanka hwee. Na se saa nkyɛn, berɛ a dua no tɔɔ fam no, Okuafoa no tuu mmirika kɔɔ hɔ, ho (ne ntaban nnyigyei), na ɔfaa ne mma no. Ɔfaa hɔn kɔtoo dua no a ɔduu so no. Na ɛnnɛ ɔtɔɔ fam na ɔka kyerɛ ne ho sɛ, "Ah, nnipa yɛ saa, anaa?" Na eyie no yɛɛ no yaw. Na ɔsuu, na ɔkaa sɛ:

Na gun no nkyerɛ, na ɔwɔn no nkyerɛ.

Na ɔtuu ne nanan mu, enti ɔfaa mmaa atumfoɔ mmienu, na ɔbɔɔ hɔn na ɔkyerɛɛ saa asɛm yi:

Ma nnoma nyinaa mra, (kosi sɛ wim bɛyɛ) tuntum, tuntum, tuntum.

Na nnoma nyinaa bae. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ hɔn nyinaa nea aseni no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ hɔn sɛ wɔn nyinaa mma mma ne nkyɛn akɔ da Gyekye ase, na obiara a ɔnim sεnea wɔsu no mmasu. Apitie a ɔyɛ nnoma no na ɔhyɛɛ ase kɔsuu kan:

Mɛwu owuo a ɛyɛ mmerɛ!

Mɛwu owuo a ɛyɛ mpofirim!

Apatupere nnoma no suu:

Mennidi nsawu!

Mennidi nsawu!

Mennne Kosante ba ntade no ho.

Akokofwedee, anomaa a ne nan yɛ kɔkɔɔ no, suu:

Owuraye’s adaa, adaa, O!

Owuraye’s afuom, afuom, O!

"Owuraye's hoe, hoe, hoe, O!" Atewa nnoma ketena no suu:

Doye!

Doye!

Afua Anwea ne ne (husband) Ano Kofi, Sɛ abofra nam mprempren ara a ɔwo no, ɛnyɛ ade pa, Kwaku Amoaten (a name).

Doye!

Doye!

Odiawuo ne ne Ano Mmirikisie a ɔtu Ena nnuaba no, wagi wa, wagi wa.

Agya abɔdeɛfoɔ, san sɔre kɔ hwe kɔsua kɔkɔɔ no na woatumi ayɛ sopu na wonome, (sɛ abɔdeɛfoɔ no yere ka saa,)

Annamɔn biara (a te ne ka no) nnimma kɔ du dua no ase (baabi a wɔwɔ no).

Opodee nnoma no suu:

Kyakyakya! nkawurubi!

Hwɛ abofra no tenten tuntum no no, ɔrekyerɛ me kwan, na ama wakun mu ne borɔde a wato no.

Opatuo, Owuo no, suu:

E! Mm!

E! Mm!"

Onwam, Hornbill no, suu:

(Hunter) Akuaferi-Saka kofukron no!

Hornbill no yabae se:

Onni hɔ.

Hornbill no se:

"Aso (ne yere), didi na yɛnkɔ."

Na Saka yɛ ne gun no na ɔpue no, pa! Na ne (Hornbill no) yere tuu kwan kɔ na ɔse:

Ɔwo hɔ!

Ɔwo hɔ!

Na Aserewa nnoma ketena no suu:

Obeko Asamoa, Sɛ kwasea koraa tɔ ne nsusua dua no, anɔpa koro mu nkurɔfo kɔ hɔ na wɔfiri hɔ!

Aburuburo, Dove no, suu:

Berɛ a ɛna me maame regya me no, ɔgyaa me sɛ me nso mma nnuaba a ɛyɛ nnuaba-mmienu bi ho;

Berɛ a nnuaba a ɛyɛ nnuaba-mmienu no wui no, ɔgyaa me sɛ mɛhwɛ borɔdwo aba a ɛyɛ nnuaba-mmienu no.

Berɛ a borɔdwo nnuaba no wui no, ɔgyaa me sɛ mɛhwɛ nnua a ɛnehyia bi ho;

Berɛ a nnua a ɛneɛm no kyere me no, ɛyɛɛ me hu paa, paa, paa!

Akyenkyena nnoma no suu:

"Me ara, me ara, me ara." Na seesei Okuaa no ara bae, na ɔteew:

Nkensen nkensen, mmoa a wɔwɔ nnuane ya ya!

Me da Gyekye ase!

Nkensen nkensen, nnoma ya ya!

(Na berɛ a ɔsuu saa) kuromfoɔ nyinaa tutu fii mu; na Gyekye anhunu baabi a mmarima, nguan, mmaa, yɛnua, anaa akoko anaa biribiara kɔe. Ɔno ne ne yere ne ne ba pɛ na wɔtan hɔ, odum dua no kɔtɔ no ho. Na Okuronti no kaa kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Efie yi firi nnɛ, abɔdeɛfoɔ biara rennya ahodwo bio." Saa m'asɛm a mekae yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, mo bi betumi agye ase sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na na akyerɛ no nso, mobɛtumi akom m'ayɛ (sɛ mekae yi).
