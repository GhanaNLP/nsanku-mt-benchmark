Sɛnea ɛyɛɛ koraa sɛ abɔfoɔ yɛ mmofra

Abɔnten bi (teaeɛ) kɔtoo gun-medicine (powuda) ne lead mmienu ma anomaa, wɔnkyɛn, na ɔne ne yere ne ne ba ketewa kɔɔ kwaeɛ sɛ wɔbɛyɛ nam. Na ɔyɛɛ abɔnten dan na ɔne ne yere ne ne ba tenaa mu. Berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ no, ɔkɔɔ kwaeɛ. Berɛ a ɔhunuu mmoa na ɔyɛɛ ne gun no, anka no. Da biara na ɛte saa, mmerɛ a ɛyɛ nnawɔtwe mmienu. Na ne yere se, “Sɛdeɛ me nni nam bi a mɛdi no, mɛsan akɔ fie.” Da a ɛbaa no, berɛ a abɔnten no kɔɔ kwaeɛ no, ne yere ne ne ba nso san kɔɔ wɔn akuraa mu. Seesei, anomaa baako pɛ na ɛtena hɔ. Me werɛ afi sɛ mɛka sɛ abɔnten no din ne Gyekye, na ne yere din nso ne Boniaye-kae-dabi. Berɛ a abɔnten no nam kwaeɛ mu no, ɔduu odum dua bi ase, na hɔ yɛ beaeɛ fɛfɛ. Ɛhɔ na abɔnten no sumsumu mu kaa sɛ, “Sɛ mekɔ fie a, wɔbɛhwehwɛ sika mfutuma a mɛtɔ powuda no, na menni bi, enti mɛfa saa gun yi mɛyɛ me throat na matow.” Mpofirim na Biribi frɛɛ no, se, “Bɔsom abɔnten.” Na abɔnten no kaa sɛ, “Agya.” Na Biribi no kaa sɛ, “Adɛn nti na w'ani yɛ kɔkɔɔ saa? Sɛ ɔno a wɔfrɛ no ‘Kɔ-sɛe-fie’ nni wo fie a, na mɛmaa wo biribi.” Ɛnna abɔnten no kaa sɛ, “Hwee nni fie.” Biribi no kaa sɛ, “Sɛ hwee nni fie a, kɔ odum dua no atadeɛ mu, na adawuru mmienu da hɔ, enti faa wɔn bra.” Na abɔnten no kɔfaa wɔn baa. Na Biribi no kaa sɛ, “Kɔtwa odwen mmienu, na petew w'ani, na tu kɔfa baako na to hɔ, na fa ebi foforo na nkyerɛ mu na to hɔ.” Berɛ a ɔbaa no, ɔse, “Mewie.” Biribi no kaa sɛ, “Fa adawuru kɛse no na bɔ.” Na ɔbɔɔ no, na kwaeɛ no yɛɛ akuraa a ɛtrɛ mmienu no ntam. Na odum dua no gyina ne mmu. Na Biribi no hyiaa ne ho sɛ ɔyɛ Okɔkɔ. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ abɔnten no sɛ sɛ asɛm bi a ɛho hia baa hɔ a, wɔbɛtie asɛm no odum dua no ase. Abɔnten no kaa sɛ, “Ho, meate.” Berɛ a Okɔkɔ no kaa eyi kyerɛ no no, ɔ(abɔnten no) nyaa yere, akyeame, ne asuafo. Ɛno berɛ no, ne yere a wɔfrɛ no “Da-bi-na-asɛm-nnyɛ-asɛm-no-bɛka-ho” huu sɛ ɔayɛ ɔhene, enti ɔtɔɔ nsa duaa, na ɔfaa no kɔɔ ɔhene no kuro mu, na ne ba no wɔ ne kyɛn. Berɛ a ɔduu hɔ no, ɔkae ne baabi a ɔfiri, ɔse, “Wo ba se ɔde nsa duaa aba sɛ nea ɛyɛ ne sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ wo yafunu.” Na ɔgyeeyɛ nsa no, na ɔma obiara a ɔwɔ hɔ nom. Na ɔfaa ɔbaa no maa Gyasehene na ama ɔatumi akɔfa no akɔ ne fie. Berɛ a anadwo tɔɔ nwunu no, ɔbaa no woyɛɛ ne ho, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a ɔhene no wɔ ne poku mu. Ɔbaa no kaa kyerɛɛ ɔhene no sɛ, “Wo ne me ayɛ nsuo ne owia, nnɛ woanya baabi a wobɛtena, enti ɛnyɛ nokware sɛ wode obi abɛfrɛ me?” Berɛ a ɔreka saa asɛm yi—sɛnea mmarima ne wɔn kwaseaee te—ɔhene no anhunu asɛm biara a ɔbɛka. Ɛhɔ ara na ɔfaa ɔbaa no, na wɔnompɔn. Anɔpa a ɛdi so no, ɔfaa no fii Gyasehene fie kɔgyee no hyɛɛ ɔhene no mpanyimfo mmaa mu. Ɔtenaa hɔ kosi sɛ asɛm kɛse bi bae. Ɔhene ne ne mpanyimfo nyinaa kɔtwaa odum dua no ase. Ɔbaa no ba no gyinaa ne papa anim, na ɔhunuu Okuafo ba no wɔ odum dua no atifi, na ɔse, “Papa, mepɛ no.” Na ne papa bɔɔ ne mpaa, na ɔtumiu ne ti akyi, na ɔmaa ɔkɔɔ fie. Na mmaa foforo no baako no ba kɔgyinaa ne anim. Ɔno nso hunuu Okuafo ba no, na ɔse, “Papa, mepɛ no.” Na ɔbɔɔ ne mpaa saa ara, na ɔtumiu ne ti akyi, enti ɔtɔɔ ase, na berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔsuu. Na ne maame bisaa no sɛ, “Dɛn nti na woresu?” Na ɔse, “Papa bɔɔ me.” Na ne maame se, “O! Adɛn nti na ɔrennyɛ wo saa, sɛ ɛyɛ Some-day-the-ungrateful-one-will-remember ba a, ɔrennyɛ no saa.” Na Some-day-the-ungrateful-one-will-remember se, “Sɛnea ɛte yi, meannya hwee a mɛyɛ wo, na woayɛ me saa.” Ansa na ɔbɛka no, wɔnyinaa tutuu mma wɔn na wɔkɔɔ asaase. Na asuadefo no se, “Sɛnea eyi a ɛwɔ dua yi atifi te, yɛn papa mmaa no reko; yɛbɛtwa dua yi.” Ɛhɔ ara na wɔfaa wɔn pawa, pimpen! pimpen! (na saa na ɛyɛɛ). Ɛno berɛ no, Okuafo no akɔakɔ, na ɔde ne mma no ahyɛɛ Ɔkraman ne Okuafo-Koro nsa. Berɛ a nnipa no twaa dua no na ɛreware no, Ɔkraman no tee n'aboa kyerɛɛ ne nsɛm a ɛyɛ animuonyam no sɛ, "Pimpeao!" Na dua no bɔɔ mu baako. Na wɔsan kɔɔ ne nkyɛn bio. Na Ɔkraman no kaa kyerɛ Ɔkɔkɔ-Koro no sɛ, "Kɔ hwehwɛ Ɔkɔkɔ-Kese na ɔba aba ne mma ho, efisɛ me nso, owuo aba me so." Mpofifoɔ no kɔfaa wɔn gun no na wɔnam no yɛɛ no owuo. Na Ɔkɔkɔ-Koro no tuu ne ho kɔɔ sɛ ɔbɛfrɛ Ɔkɔkɔ-Kese no. Ɔkwan a ɔfa kɔe no nyinaa, ɔsuu na ɔkaa sɛ:

Nana e! wo mma o!

Nana e! wo mma o!

Berɛ a ɔfrɛɛ saa na Okurɛkroni anyɛ nka no, ɔyɛɛ ne nne den na ɔsuu:

Sankur' e, Anoma e, sankur' e, sɛ woakɔ adidi a, san bra.

Na Okuruku tee ase. Na ɔbaa, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛrekɔ so wɔ ha?" Na Okuruku-Koro kɛseɛ no kaa sɛ, "Wɔatwa dua no afa damituo sɛ wɔde wo mma bɛfa, na wo Lizard ho nso, wɔasow dua so mmaa no na wɔakyllam no." Mpofifo ara na Okuruku no tɔɔ soro, wa (ne ntaban nka mmofra), na ɔtɔɔ dua bi so wɔ kurom anofie, na ɔhwee n'ani, na ɛyɛ nokware sɛ wɔretwa dua no. Wanka hwee. Na seesei berɛ a dua no tɔɔ fam no, Okuruku no tuu mmirika kɔ hɔ, ho (ne ntaban nka mmofra), na ɔfaa ne mma no. Ɔfaa hɔn kɔtɔɔ dua no so a ɔtɔɔ so ansa no. Na ɛnnɛ ɔtɔɔ fam na ɔkaa ne ho (ne ti mu) sɛ, "Ah, nnipa yɛ saa, anaa?" Na ɛmaa ɔyɛɛ yaw. Na ɔsuu, na ɔkaa sɛ:

Na gun no nkyerɛ, na ɔwɔn no nkyerɛ.

Na ɔtuu ne nanan mu, enti ɔfaa mmaa atumfoɔ mmienu, na ɔbɔɔ hɔn na ɔkyerɛɛ saa asɛm yi:

Ma nnoma nyinaa mra, (kosi sɛ wim bɛyɛ) tuntum, tuntum, tuntum.

Na nnoma nyinaa bae. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ hɔn nyinaa nea aseni no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ hɔn sɛ wɔn nyinaa mma mma ne nkyɛn akɔ da Gyekye ase, na obiara a ɔnim sεnea wɔsu no mmasu. Apitie a ɔyɛ nnoma no na ɔhyɛɛ ase kɔsuu kan:
