Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNE ASƐM-KASƐM BAA AMANMU

Wɔka sɛ abɔfoɔ bi kɔɔ kwaeɛ; ɔbaa ha na ɔhwɛɛ awatwe bi mu, na woomu na ’Nipa, Osebo, Owo, ne ’Kusie gyina hɔ. Sɛ saa nnipa yi hunuu Abɔfoɔ no, a eyi kaa sɛ, “Agya Abɔfoɔ, bra na fa me pue.” Eyi nso kaa sɛ, “Agya, bra na fa me pue.” Abɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Mrenkan, efisɛ ɔkyena ara, sɛ mefa mo pue na mo ba me nkyɛn a, nea mobɛyɛ me no (me nim) mmpɛ sɛ mehwɛ ho.” Eyi kaa sɛ, “Mampɛ (ayɛ saa).” Eyi nso kaa sɛ, “Mampɛ (ayɛ saa).” Abɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Sɛ saa na ɛte saa ankasa a, mefa mo pue.” Na nokware ne sɛ, ɔfaa nnipa no nyinaa puee na ɔkɔɔ ne akuraa mu. Da bi, berɛ a Abɔfoɔ no wɔ hɔ no, Osebo, ɔkraman no, faa nam pii baa. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Agya Abɔfoɔ, eyi yɛ aseda ama adɔeɛ a woayɛ me, me da wo ase, Apiafo.” Ɔfaa nam a ɛwɔ nkyene duá kɔtɛn dwumkubea duá duá (12) baa Abɔfoɔ no nkyɛn. Abɔfoɔ no daa no ase na Osebo kɔɔ. Ɛnna foforo bi, Abɔfoɔ no wɔ hɔ, na Okusie, ɔkraman no, baa. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Agya Abɔfoɔ, wopɛ sika kɔkɔɔ?” Abɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Ho! adɛn nti, eyi ne nea nnipa yɛ amane kɔhwehwɛ.” Okusie no kɔɔ baabi a Nyankonpon, ɔsoro-onyame, gyina ne sika kɔkɔɔ no. Ɔkɔtoo awatwe bi efie no ase, na ɔkɔɔ efie no mu, na ɔkɔfaa Nyankonpon sika kɔkɔɔ no nyinaa, na ɔfaa baa Abɔfoɔ no nkyɛn. Abɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Me da wo ase, Aku.” Na Okusie no kɔɔ. Da foforo bi, ɔwɔ hɔ, na ɔtee sɛ obi rekyia no, “Ago.” Abɔfoɔ no hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, “Ame.” Nipa no baa; ɔkaa sɛ, “Agya Abɔfoɔ, maakye.” Ɔyɛɛ no mmuae sɛ, “Aane, me nua.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Eyi yɛ nsa dua kɔtɛn ketewa bi a mede aba sɛ meda wo ase.” Agya Abɔfoɔ kaa sɛ, “Mmpɛ; wo Nipa, ɛte sɛnea mekaa sɛ ɛbɛyɛ, berɛ a mekaaa sɛ mampɛ sɛ mefa wo pue; hwɛ nea Osebo de baa me nkyɛn, hwɛ nea Okusie de baa me nkyɛn.” Nipa no faa ne nsa dua no kɔɔ hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ Nyankonpon sɛ, “Wo sika kɔkɔɔ a ɛyera no, Okusie no na ɔbaa faa kɔmaa Abɔfoɔ no.” Nyankonpon kaa sɛ, “Ɛte saa?” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Nana, ɛyɛ nokware pɛ.” Nyankonpon sɔree abotire kɔfaa Abɔfoɔ no, na wɔkyekyerea nsawa wɔ ne mma mu. Wɔbɔɔ no mpaebɔ paa. Ɛnnɛ, berɛ a wɔaduru kwan no ho ne Abɔfoɔ no, ɔseɛ, “Mpanyimfo, mɛkɔ mɛyɛ me ho mmuae.” Abotire no seɛ, “Daabi, ɔkorɔfo, wobɛdwene.” Ɔseɛ, “Daabi, mɛnndwene, na sɛ mobɛpɛ a, monyi nkonta no, monkyekyere me yan mu, monkyekyere no hyɛ nnompe mu, na monma me nkɔ.” Abotire no tiee no, na wɔmaa no nkɔ. Ɔkɔhɔn hɔ, ɔyɛɛ ne ho mmuae, ɛnna ɛhɔn-tenten-ketewa no bae. Ɔseɛ, “Hwɛ ha, me damfo, fa aduro ketewa yi, na fa kɔ, na sɛ wokɔ, na wɔgyina wo anim wɔ mpanyimfo anim, na ɛba sɛ wobɛkum wo, mɛkɔ Ɔhene no mmaa hɔ, na makane ne yere a ɔdɔ no paa no ba; berɛ a makane no, wobɛhu sɛ obiara akɔsoro, na wo nso wobɛka, na wakye, ‘Aduro wɔ me nsam,’ na sɛ wode adi no ba no anim a, ɔbɛyɛ yie.” Abɔfoɔ no seɛ, “Meda wo ase, Aku.” Abotire no faa Abɔfoɔ no kɔgyinaa Onyame Anim, (ne ne mpanyimfo anim) Kontire, Akwum’, Adonten, Gyase, ne Kyidom mpanyimfo. Abɔfoɔ no kaa ne asɛm, na berɛ a Ɔkasafo no gyinaa hɔ de gyinaa nsɛm no, wɔtiee amanehunu nkuduo, pui! pui! a ɛfiri akyirikyiri wɔ mmaa hɔ. Wɔseɛ, “Dɛn ne eyi?” Wɔseɛ, “Awɔn akane Onyame yere a ɔdɔ no paa no ba.” Obiara kɔe. Enti Abɔfoɔ no gyinaa hɔ; ɔseɛ, “Sɛ mɛnyɛ nnamfo a, mɛtumi anya aduro (aduro a ɛyɛ nsumansum).” Onipa bi tiee (no) na ɔkɔkaaa wɔn, ɔseɛ, “Monkɔ fa nnamfo no mma ha.” Ɛnna Abɔfoɔ no bae. Onyame seɛ, “Sɛ wubetumi hwɛ me ba yi a, na wanwu a, mɛma wo wo ho.” Abɔfoɔ no seɛ, “Mete, Adɔfo.” Na Abɔfoɔ no seɛ (bio), “Nana, nea mɛkyerɛ mo yi, sɛ mobetumi anya bi a, mɛtumi ahwɛ mo ba no.” Wɔseɛ, “Ɛdɛn ne eyi?” Ɔseɛ, “Asɛm-kafo no mogya na yɛde bɛka aduro a wɔapɔ no mu.” Wɔseɛ, “Yɛbɛnya Asɛm-kafo mogya wɔ he?” Ɔkasafo bi, a ɔyɛ onyansafo, seɛ, “Na onipa a ɔbae kaa kyerɛɛ Onyame sɛ Abɔfoɔ no aba na wakɔdwene Onyame sika no, ɔwɔ he?” Wɔseɛ, “Wampae, wampae.” Wɔkɔfaa onipa no bae. Abɔfoɔ no seɛ, “Twa ne nsa firi ne joint no, na monfa ne mogya mma me.” Ɛnna ɛno ara na wɔtwaa no. Ɔyadeɛfoɔ no faa mogya no kɔtwee ho ne aduro no, na ɔde guu Ɔhene ba no nan no so. Ɛnneɛ ara na ɔsɔree. Wɔkaa sɛ, “To Ɔyadeɛfoɔ no ayɛ,” na wɔteɛ “Eee!” Onyame kaa sɛ, “Mefa wo ti na mekyɛ wo, na wo a woyɛ nkasaeɛfoɔ no, ma wɔmmehu no, na wɔntwa ne nam nsusuansusuo, na wɔde afura mframa hɔ nyinaa.” Wɔhui no, na wɔtwaa ne nam no yɛɛ nketenkete, na wɔde guu hɔ nyinaa. Asaaeɛfoɔ pii baa amansan no mu saa ara.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
