Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNE ASƐM-KASƐM BAA AMANMU

Wɔka sɛ abɔnten bi kɔɔ kwae; ɔbaa hɔ na ɔhwɛɛ awuaputuo bi mu, na woyɛe mu no ’Nipa, Osebo, Owo, ne ’Kusie gyina hɔ. Ɛnnɛ, berɛ a nnipa yi huu Abɔfoɔ no, eyi kaa sɛ, “Agya Abɔfoɔ, bra na fa me firi ha.” Eyi nso kaa sɛ, “Agya, bra na fa me firi ha.” Abɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Mrenkan, efisɛ ɔkyena mpo, sɛ meba bɛgya mo a, na mo ba me nkyɛn a, nea mobɛyɛ me no (me nim) ɛrenya m'adwene.” Eyi kaa sɛ, “Mentumi (reyɛ saa).” Eyi nso kaa sɛ, “Mentumi (reyɛ saa).” Abɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ , “Sɛ saa na ɛte saa ankasa a, mɛfa mo afi ha.” Na nokwarene, ɔfaa nnipa no nyinaa kɔɔ ne akuraa mu. Da koro bi, berɛ a Abɔdeɛfoɔ no wɔ hɔ no, Osebo, ɔkraman no, baeɛ ne nnam pii. Ɔsee no sɛ, “Agya Abɔdeɛfoɔ, eyi yɛ aseda ama adɔeɛ a woayɛ me, me da wo ase, Apiafo.” Ɔde nnam a wɔde asodie nketen duaa (12) atu mu baa Abɔdeɛfoɔ no hɔ. Ɔyadeɛfoɔ no daasei no na Gyata no kɔɔ. Ɛnna foforo bi, Ɔyadeɛfoɔ no wɔ hɔ, na Okusie, Okraman no, bae. Ɔsee no sɛ, “Agya Ɔyadeɛfoɔ, wopɛ sika kɔkɔɔ huhu bi?” Ɔyadeɛfoɔ no see no sɛ, “Ho! adɛn nti mampɛ, ɛno ne nea nnipa yɛ amaneɛ pii wɔ ne hwehwɛ mu.” Okraman no kɔe, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a Nyankonpon, Onyame a ɔwɔ soro no, gyina ne sika kɔkɔɔ huhu no. Ɔkɔtoo awu kwan bi efie no ase, na ɔhyɛɛ efie no mu, na ɔkɔtookuu Nyame sika kɔkɔɔ no nyinaa, na ɔde baa ɔyadeɛfoɔ no nkyɛn. Ɔyadeɛfoɔ no see no sɛ, “Aku, me da wo ase.” Na ɔkraman no kɔɔ. Da foforo bi, ɔwɔ hɔ, na ɔtee obi a ɔrekyia no sɛ, “Ago.” Ɔyadeɛfoɔ no hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, “Ame.” Ɔbarima no bae; ɔsee, “Agya Abɔntenfo, maakye.” Ɔno (Abɔntenfo no) ayɛ no mmuae sɛ, “Aane, me nua.” Ɔsee, “Palm-wine kɔkɔɔ ketewa bi ni a mede aba sɛ medi wo nkyɛn aseda.” Agya Abɔntenfo see, “Menka ho; wo nso, Ɔbarima, ɛyɛ pɛ sɛnea mekaa sɛ ɛbɛyɛ, berɛ a mehyɛɛ kyerɛ sɛ mɛnfa wo mma; hwɛ nea Leopard no de baa me nkyɛn, hwɛ nea Rat no de baa me nkyɛn.” Ɔbarima no faa ne palm-wine no kɔe. na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ Onyame sɛ, “Wo sika hu a ɛyera no, ɛno na Opɔnkɔ no baa hɔ gyee kɔmaa Abɔntenfoɔ no.” Onyame bisaa no sɛ, “Ɛno ne nea ɛsi?” Ɔkae sɛ, “Nana, ɛno ne nokware paa.” OnyameTweɛ abosomfoɔ kɔkɔree Abɔntenfoɔ no, na wɔkyekyereɛ nsawa wɔ ne mma mu. Wɔbɔɔ no mpaebɔ paa. Ɛnnɛ, berɛ a wɔaduru kwan no ho ne Abɔdeɛfoɔ no, ɔseɛ, “Mpanyimfo, mɛpɛ sɛ mekɔ kɔyɛ me ho mmuae.” Abotire no seɛ, “Dabi, ɔkorɔfo, wobɛdwene.” Ɔseɛ, “Dabi, minnyɛ saa, na sɛ mobɛpɛ a, fa nkodo no, kyekyere me mma mu, na kyea no wɔ dua kɛse bi mu, na momma me nkɔ.” Abotire no tiee no, na wɔmaa ɔkɔe. Ɔwo hɔ, ɔyɛɛ ne ho dɛ, ɛnna ɛno a ɛyɛ tenten na ɛyɛ mmerɛ no bae. Ɔse, “Hwɛ ha, me damfo, nya aduro ketewa yi, na fa kɔ, na sɛ wo kɔ, na wɔgyina wo anim wɔ mpanyimfo anim, na ɛba sɛ wɔpɛ sɛ wɔhyɛ wo ya, mɛkɔ Ɔhene no ayefuo hɔ, na makane ne yere a ɔdɔ no paa no ba; sɛ makane no a, wobɛhu sɛ obiara akɔpue, na wo nso wobɛka, na wokyea se, ‘Mewɔ aduro,’ na sɛ wobɛde ayɛ abofra no na ɔbɛyɛ yie.” Abɔdeɛfoɔ no see sɛ, “Aku, me da wo ase.” Abirikyerefoɔ no faa Abɔdeɛfoɔ no kɔtoo Onyame anim, (ne ne mpanyimfoɔ anim) Kontire, Akwum’, Adonten, Gyase, ne Kyidom mpanyimfoɔ. Abɔdeɛfoɔ no kaa ne asɛm, na berɛ a Ɔkasa no gyinaa hɔ de gyinaa nsɛm no anim, wɔteee amanehunu nusuo, pui! pui! akyirikyiri wɔ ɔhenema no harem mu. Wɔkaa sɛ, “Dɛn ne eyi?” Wɔkaa sɛ, “Awɔ anka Onyame no yɔfo a ɔdɔ no paa no ba.” Obiara kɔɔ ne kwan so. Ɛnnɛ Ɔbɔfo no gyina hɔ; ɔse, “Sɛ me nnyɛ nnamfo a, ɛyɛ a mɛtumi anya aduro (adeɛ a ɛbɛyɛ nsumansum maom).” Onipa bi tee (no) na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ hɔn sɛ, “Kɔna momma mo nna nnamfo no aba ha.” Ɛnna Ɔbɔfo no bae. Nyame see, “Sɛ wotumi hwɛ me ba yi, na wanwu a, mɛma wo wo ti.” Abɔnten no see, “Mete ase, Adɔfo.” Na Abɔnten no see (bio), “Nana, nea mede reba adiwɔ kyerɛ wo no, sɛ wotumi nya bi a, ɛnna mɛtumi ahwɛ wo ba no.” Wɔn see, “Ɛdeɛ bɛn ni?” Ɔsee, “Asɛm-kafo no mogya a yɛbɛka ho aduru no.” “ine.” Wɔkaa sɛ, “Yɛbɛfi he Tell-tale no mogya?” Anansesemfoɔ mu no baako, a ɔyɛ onyansafoɔ, kaa sɛ, “Ɔno a ɔbaa ha kyerɛɛ Onyame sɛ Abɔntenfoɔ no aba na waowɔ Onyame sika no, ɔwɔ he?” Wɔkaa sɛ, “Womampɔ, womampɔ.” Wɔkɔfaa ɔno bae. Ɔbɔdeɛ no see sɛ, “Twa ne nsa no fi ne joint no mu, na fa ne mogya no ama me.” Ɛno ara a, wɔtwaa no. Ɔbɔdeɛ no faa mogya no kɔtɔɔ adi ne aduro no mu, na ɔde guu Ɔhene ba no nan no so. Ɛno ara a, ɔsɔree. Wɔkaa sɛ, “To Aberewafoɔ/Ɔyerefoɔ no so.” Wɔteɛ sɛ, “Eee!” Onyame kaa sɛ, “Megye wo ti na mema wo, na Tell-tale no nso, ma wɔmmpere no, na wɔntwa ne nam nsiesie, na wɔde atwa afa baabiara.” Wɔpereee no, na wɔtwaa ne nam kaa kaa, na wɔde atwaa afa baabiara. Saa na Tell-tales pii baa abusua no mu.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
