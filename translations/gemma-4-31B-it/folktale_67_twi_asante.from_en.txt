Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ANANSE, KƆKƆRƆ NO, KAA SE ƆREKƆ KWÁ KWÁN SO REhwehwɛ kwasea, nanso ɔno ara na ɔyɛ kwasea no.

ANANSE, Kwebla, tee ase wɔ hɔ beao a, ɔse ɔpɛ kwasea bi a ɔbɛkɔ ne nkyɛn akɔ siesie nnam-atwe. Ɔkɔɔ hɔ ne hɔ nyinaa, nanso ɔanhunu bi. Ɛno a ɛyɛɛ no, ɔhyiaa Osansa, Akɔkɔduae no. Ɔse, “Ma yɛnkɔ nsawa.” Ɔkɔkɔrefo no hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, “Nam pii wɔ me nsam a mɛdi.” Akyire yi, ɔhyiaa Anene, Ɔkɔkɔrefo no, na ɔse no, “Ma yɛnkɔ nsawa.” Ɔkɔkɔrefo no se, “Mase.” Enti, Ɔkɔkɔrefo no se kyerɛɛ Ɔkɔkɔrefo no sɛ, “Saa nsawa akwantuo a wo ne Ananse repa ada so yi, mkɔ, efisɛ Ananse se ɔrehwehwɛ kwasea bi, sɛ ɔne saa kwasea no bɛsawa a s, ɔno na ɔbɛnya sika no nyinaa.” Akɔkɔsra no ayɛ adwene sɛ, “Mpɛse sɛ woka hwee bio, efisɛ mɛhwɛ so sɛ me na mɛnya sika no nyinaa.” Akɔkɔsra ne Kwebla kɔɔ kwae kɔtwaa abrobɔdua nnua. Akɔkɔsradee no kaa kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, “Ma me ntomme no na mɛkɔ twa (no), na wo adwuma ne sɛ woagya brɛ (a ɛfiri me adwuma mu).” Kwebia no hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, “Wode me yɛ kwasea; ma me ntwa no, na wo nkoaa na wobɛfa brɛ a ɛfiri mu.” Ananse na ɔretwa (abrobɔ no) berɛ a Anene, Akɔkɔsradee no, tena hɔ resu. Sɛ ɔtwitwaa wɔn fee no, a wɔkyekyereɛ wɔn (de bɔɔ katan). Akɔkɔsradee no see, “Kwebla, ma me nfa nna mesoa, na wo nso wo nni nka.” Kwebla no hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, “Kɔ hɔ, wode me yɛ kwasea?” Na ɔfaa wɔn na ɔtoo wɔn ne ti atasɛm. Akɔkɔsradee no suree ne akyi na ɔrewe. Berɛ a wɔduu fie no, Akɔkorɔ no see sɛ, "Ma me nyɛ nsuo-kɔtɔ-nketene no na wo nso nya m'adwuma no nkyɛm." Ananse hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ saa; ma me nyɛ wɔn na wo nso nya nkyɛm no." Ɛnna Akɔkorɔ no kɔtoo ne yafunu so, na ɔrebi. Ananse see sɛ, "Kwasea, woka sɛ wo wɔ nyansa, nanso worewu." Kwebla no namyɛɛ nsuo-kɔtɔ-nketene no. Akɔkɔsro no see, "Ma me nsoa nsuo-kɔtɔfo no nna mefa akɔ nsuo no ho, na wo nso nfa n'abrabo yaw no." Kweku Ananse see, "Dabi, mɛkɔ ne nkyɛn, na wo nfa n'abrabo yaw no." Wɔduu nsuo no ho; Akɔkɔsro no see, "Agya Ananse, aboa bi te nsuo no mu, enti ma me ntaso nsuo no mu na mehyɛ nsuo-kɔtɔfo no, na sɛ aboa no ka me a, wo nfa owuo no." Ananse seeว่า, "Ah, wode me yɛ kwasea kɛse; me ara na mɛsi nketene no, na sɛ ɔka me a, wo na wobɛfa owuo no." Ananse faa nnam-nketene no kɔsii nsuom, na wɔsan kɔɔ fie. Anokwa anɔpa, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ hwan, wɔkɔhwehwɛɛ nketene no, na wɔafa nnam mmienu. Kwakwaree no see sɛ, "Kweku Ananse, fa eyi mmienu yi, na ɔkyena yɛn baa ha a, (afiri no) afa nan, ɛnna me nso mɛfa hɔ." Ananse anyaa mmuae sɛ, "Onipa yi! Wode me yɛ kwasea; fa eyi mmienu yi, na ɔkyena, me nso mɛfa nan no." Kwakwaree no faa hɔ. Anɔpa a ɛbaa no, wɔkɔɔ (afiri no nkyɛn bio), na wɔfaa nan (namfo). Kwakwaree no see sɛ, "Fa eyi anan yi, na ɔkyena yɛn ba ha na yɛakukua nwɔtwe, ɛnna me nso mɛfa hɔn." Kweku Spider no hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, "Wode me yɛ kwasea; fa eyi anan yi, na ɔkyena me nso mɛfa nwɔtwe no." Anɔpa a ɛba no, wɔkɔe (kɔhwehwɛɛ nketia no) na wɔfaa nwɔtwe nwɔtwe. Kwakwaree no see, "Fa (won), na ɔkyena me nso, mɛfa duaa ne nnum no." Kwebla no see, "Wode me yɛ kwasea; fa nwɔtwe no, na ɔkyena mɛnya duaa ne nnum no." Afei nna nnam-kyere no ayɛ mmerɛ, na Kwakwaree no kasa kyerɛɛ Kwebla no, see, “Kɔfa saa nnam-kyere a ayɛ mmerɛ yi, na obi a ɔbɔn wo no, ɔno bɛnya ntanu (se." (£16) ma (trap) biara, enti, Papa Kwebla, fa nsuomfuu yi, na fa nsuomfuu a awu no ma me.” Kwebla no see, “Dabi, fa nsuomfuu no kɔtɔ, na me nso mɛfa nsuomfuu a awu no na mɛfa (sika no).” Wɔnfae (hɔn nnoɔma) kɔɔ dwadwa. Anofire Crow no tɔɔ ne nnoɔma nyinaa; ɔnyaa sika kɔkɔɔ pii. Nanso Kwebla (nneɛma) no tena hɔ. Ɛnna Akɔkɔsradeɛ kaa kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Fa saa nsuo-kɔtɔkɔ mmoa a awu yi kɔtɔn nkyɛn na wɔbɛtɔ.” Kweblafaa wɔn; ɔkaa sɛ, “Merewere nsuo-kɔtɔkɔ mmoa a awu!” Ɛnna kuro no mpanyimfoɔ tɛn (no), na ɔkaa sɛ, “Wei firi hene; kɔfrɛ no ma wakɔ.” Kwebla kɔe. Ɔmanhene no see sɛ, “Wosusu sɛ yɛn a yɛte kuro yi mu nyinaa yɛ kwaseaa; seesei wo adamfo (ha) baa ha na ɔtɔɔ nsuomnam (ne nnyigyei), woanhu no, nanso wopɛ sɛ wotɔ nsuomnam-kwan a ayɛ mmerɛ ma yɛn?” Ɔmanhene no see sɛ, “Bɔ no mpaabo.” Enti wɔbɔɔ no. Ɛna Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ so kaa sɛ, “Kweku Ananse, woahu, wo anim ayɛ wo aniwuo; wokaase sɛ worekɔpɛ obi a ɔyɛ kwasea a ɔbɛboa wo ayɛ adwuma, na seesei woahu sɛ wo ara ne kwasea no.”

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
