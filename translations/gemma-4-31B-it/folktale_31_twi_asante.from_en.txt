Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo ampa, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo ampa (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea ɛyɛɛ sɛ yɛbɛhunu Okra, kraman no, a ɔda velvet mmoa so daa, nanso 'Kraman, kraman no, bɛda efie mmoa mmoa mu.

Wɔka sɛ obiara nni hɔ a ɔyɛ ɔbaa bi a (ne nneɛma annye koraa efisɛ) berɛ biara a ɔwo ba, na ɛwu. Enti ɔkɔɔ abosom bi nkyɛn kɔtwe no ho asɛm, na ɔka kyerɛ no sɛ ɔpɛ ba. Abosom no see sɛ, "Mɛma wo baako, nanso fa abofra no ho a, adwuma biara a ɔbɛyɛ no yɛ sɛ ɔbɛma wo ayɛ adeɛ a wode bɛkyerɛ obi, nanso ɛnna a ɛbɛba bi, ɔbɛsana wo." Ɛnnyɛ nnawɔtwe mmienu, ɛnnyɛ nnawɔtwe mmiɛnsa (bere a ɔne abosom no bɔɔ nkɔmmɔ no) a ɔbaa no yɛa. Ɔwoo ba—na ɛyɛ ananse-asɛm ba—efisɛ ɔnyɛyɛɛ kɛse mpofirim. Abofra no nyini baa abasɛm a ɛyɛ fɛ. Da koro bi na ɔne ne maame wɔ hɔ, na ɔsee no sɛ, "Maame, ma me sika kɔkɔɔ, na matumi akɔ Po-nkufa-man, kɔtɔ ntomo." Ne maame bisaa no sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dodo sɛn?" Ɔsee no sɛ, "Asuanu" (ɛne £4). Na ne maame faa no maa no, na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ akwantuo no so. Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa ɔbarima bi ne ne kraman a ɛyɛ ntokwa. Ɔse, "Fa bra na matɔ." Kraman no wura no se, "Wontumi ntɔ." Abofra no bisaa se, "Ɛyɛ sika sɛn?" Kraman no wura no se, "Sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma a Asuanu baako tumi tutu" (ɛne £4). Ɔse, "Ɛyɛ dɛn (ma me), fa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma a ɛyɛ £4 yi." Ɔfaa kraman no, na ɔde kɔɔ fie. Bere a ɔsan bae no, ne maame bisaa no sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woannya wo kwan?" Ɔyɛɛ no mmuae sɛ, "Mefuraa sika no tɔɔ kraman." Ne maame see, "Ho!" Seesei wɔtena hɔ, ɛbɛyɛ bebree mmofra mmoa baako. Ɔseɛ, "Maame, ma me sika kɔkɔɔ na matumi akɔ dwadwa." Ɔno nso seɛ, "Wo nso, sɛnea wo ara yɛ no, wobɛfa sika kɔkɔɔ no akɔ tow kyɛn, nanso sika sɛn na wopɛ?" Ɔyɛɛ no mmuae sɛ, "Sika kɔkɔɔ Asuanu ne suru" (i. e. £5). Ɔseɛ, "Fa ɛnna." Enti ɔfaa kwan a wɔdwadwa so no kɔɔ. Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa ɔbarima bi a ɔkura agyina bi. Ɔse, "Ɔbarima, fa saa animfo a ɔtwe n'anan mu dodo no ba, na matɔ." Ɔse, "Sɛ meda me poku mu a, akokɔɔ bɔ me nan, enti na matɔ." Ɔse, "Mebɔ wo mpae sɛ ma me nni." Ɔse, "Wontumi ntɔ." Abofra no ase, "Sɛn na wopɛ ma ɛ?" Ɔgyae se, "Asuanu ne suru sika kɔkɔɔ." Ɔse, "Enti ɛno ne nea nti na wose, mentumi ntɔ;" "Fa, fa." Abofra no faa okraman no, na ɔkɔɔ fie ne ne nso. Berɛ a ɔduu fie no, ɔsee, "Maame, hwɛ nea mede abaa ha." Ɔno nso gyee me answerseɛ sɛ, "Ah, eyi ne nea wɔseɛ (sɛ ɛbɛsi saa)." Abofra no tenaa hɔ (fie). Ɛbɛyɛ nnawɔtwe aduonu mmienu a ɛkyerɛ, na ɔkaa ne maame kyerɛ no sɛ, "Ma me sika kɔkɔɔ na makɔ dwadwa." Na na maame no se, "Sika a me nsa mu wɔ nyinaa awie, na sika kɔkɔɔ Asuasa baako na ɛda hɔ (i. e. £6); sɛ mede eyi ma wo, na wokɔ, na wonnyɛ nneɛma ntonom anim, a saa na eyi aseni." Abofra no see, "Mase." Anɔpa a ɛba no, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ, ɔfaa ne kotokuo no, na ɔkɔe, pa! Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa Asante bi, na ɔno nso kura anomaa bi. Ɔse, "Adamfo, fa wo aboa no ba, na matɔ." Ɔyɛɛ no mmuae se, "Mennu ampa, efisɛ mede m'ani gye ho." Ɔse, "Mɛtɔ." Anomaa no wura no se, "Mennu ampa, efisɛ mehu nea ebɛyɛ ama me." Abofra no se, "O, ma me." Ɔse, "Wubetumi atɔ?" Abofra no se, "Sɛn nko?" Ɔyɛɛ no mmuae se, "Sika kɔkɔɔ Asuasa baako" (i.e. £6). Ɔse, "Wosusu sɛ ɛnti na mɛntɔ no, hwɛ sika no wɔ ha." Ɔde anomaa no baa fie. Ne maame see, "Wei nnyɛ yie sen nea ɛyɛɛ ansa, enti wei ne nea woade bae?" Ɔyɛɛ no mmuae sɛ, "Ɛnna, wei ne nea mende bae." Da koro bi, abofra no tenure hɔ (efie), na ɔpono no frɛɛ no, see sɛ, "Bra." Berɛ a ɔkɔɔ ne nkyɛn no, ɔpono no hyɛɛ no ase, see sɛ, "Me kuro mu no, meyɛ ɔhene, na mere prepare sɛ mɛkɔ akwantuo bi, na obi bi c na wɔkyee me, ɛnna wo nso, wɔ wo adɔe mu no, wo tɔɔ me, na seesei me srɛ wo, sɛ wubetumi fa me akɔ me kurom a, ɛnna wɔbɛda wo ase paa." Abofra no see, "Woreka nkontomire ama me, wobɛtu mmirika." Ɔhopere no see, "Sɛ woyɛ m'asɛm no mu a, ɛnna fa ntoma kɛkɛnkɛn, na fa kyekyere me nan mu, na fa me nan ase." Abofra no faa ntoma kɛkɛnkɛn no na ɔkyekyere no kɔɔ ne nan ho, na ɔsuwee ne akyi brɛoo, kosi sɛ wɔduu Pigeon town. Sɛ wɔduu town no anim no, mmofra no na wɔrebaabi nte marbles. Sɛ wɔhunuu no no, wɔse, "Ɔhene aba!" "Ɔhene aba!" Baako kyerɛɛ Korenti ɔhene, nanso wɔkyinii no, na wɔtwaa ne kɔn, na wɔse, "Worema yɛka yɛn amanehunu a ɛyɛ ayɛ a yɛhui no." Nanso obi foforo kɔɔ bio kɔka saa asɛm no. Na seesei Akwamu ɔhene se, "Wo, Gyasehene, kɔ hɔ kɔhwɛ nea eyi nyinaa fa ho." Ɔkɔhwɛɛ, na ɔbaa. Ɔse, "O, ɛyɛ nokware." Afei wɔfaa kɔm-mmoa ne atadeɛ a ɛyɛ kɛse no na wɔkɔfaa Ɔhene kɔ ne fie. Wɔka amanneɛ no kyerɛɛ abusua no nyinaa, sɛnea ɔrehyɛ kwan, ne sɛnea obi bi kuraa no, ne sɛnea abofra yi, efisɛ ɔyɛ ayamye, na ɔtɔɔ no, ne sɛnea ɔde no baa fie nnɛ. Mpanyimfo ne mmeranteɛ nyinaa sɔree na wɔda abofra no ase. Ɔhemaa baaye sika kɔkɔɔ a ɛyɛ nsuo-kɔtokuo bi; mpanyimfoɔ nyinaa nso, obiara baaye sika kɔkɔɔ a ɛyɛ nsuo-kɔtokuo bi. Ɔhene ankasa hwɛ ne nsa, na ɔtuu kɔkɔɔ-kyere bi na ɔmaa no. Na ɔse, "Fa saa ketane yi, na nea biara a wopɛ, saa ketane yi bɛma wo." Ɔse, "Mase." Na ɔfa ketane no kɔɔ ne akuraa mu; na ɔkyerɛɛ ne maame sikafoo ne ketane no. Ɛna naayewa no see no sɛ, "Akwaaba Aku, akwaaba Aku." Tete no, berɛ a ɔyɛɛ ne sikafoo no "dede", na berɛ a ɔfiri ne akwantuo mu ba no, berɛ a ɔgye ne naayewa no nkyia no, na onnyɛ no mmuae. Na ɔmaa ne naayewa no amanneɛ sɛ, "Woahu sikafoo ne kɔkɔɔ yi; mɛkɔ akɔsi sɛ mɛsi kuro kɛse bi a yɛbɛtena mu." Naayewa no see no sɛ, "Pa wo ani yie (yeaa" yɛ wo tɔn nyinaa ma ɛyɛ)." Abofra no kɔɔ kwaa mu; ɔtookoo kɔkɔɔ no gu fam; ɔsee, "Kɔkɔɔ, paa saa (asase) yi nyinaa, yi kwaa ne nwunu nyinaa fi ha ma me." Na baabi a ɛyɛ no nyinaa yɛɛ pɛ. Ɔsee, "Bo moa adeɛ a woapaa no nyinaa akumu na yɛmmi." Na ɛyɛɛ saa. Ɔsee, "Si efie." Na ɛsii efie pii. Ɔsew, "Ring, ma nnipa mba na wɔatena saa fie yi mu." Na nnipa bae. Ɔmaa ne na bɛyɛ Obaahemaa; ɔno nso bɛyɛɛ ɔhene. Seesei (kyerɛ sɛ, wardrobe bi wɔ a mede werɛ fii), Ananse, Kɔkɔɔro no, na ɔyɛ ne adamfo pa. Da koro bi na ɔrete hɔ (ne akuraa foforo no mu) a Kwaku Ananse hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔbɛba saa akwadaa yi akuraa mu. (Sɛ ɔduu hɔ no,) ɔsee no sɛ, "O, maame aba, papa aba, woanya nkyɛnea ne nkunim, na woantumi amma me ho mmɔyɛ bio sɛ wobɛhwɛ me, nanso dɛn na asi a ɛde saa nyinaa aba?" Ɛnna abofra no kae amanneɛ no nyinaa kyerɛɛ no. Kwebia no see sɛ, "Mɛkɔ me akuraa mu (kɔgye biribi) na masan aba." Ananse kɔɔ ne akuraa mu; ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nua ba no sɛ, "Mɛkyerɛ wo akwan akɔ me adamfo a ɔwɔ hɔ no nkyɛn, na sɛ woko a, fa saa nsusuo fitaa yi ma no, na tie asɛm yie na yɛ nea ɔbɛka kyerɛ wo; na ɛsɛ sɛ woyɛ no yie na woyɛ kwan a obi nnim na wogye saa kasa no." Abofra no faa kwan kɔɔ ɔbarima no akuraa mu. Ɔbarima no kaa kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Sɛnne yi ho, mɛhwɛ so sɛ wo nsannkɔ, efisɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wote me nkyɛn nnawɔtwe mmiɛnsa ansa na woasannkɔ." Abofra baa no kaa sɛ, "Mete." Enti ɔne abofra baa no hyiaa hɔ, na ɔbarima no kɔɔ ne ho dwene; na ɔtuu kɔkɔro no fii ne nsa na ɔtoo no table bi so; na abofra baa no faa no, na ɔde kɔɔ ne wofa, Kwaku Ananse hɔ. Ananse tebea mu no, ɔde (ketewa no) hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔsi kuro kɛse bi. Ɛnnɛ, onipa no, berɛ a ɔbaa ne ketewa no so sɛ ɔbɛhwehwɛ no a, ɔanhunu. Ɔtee aseɛ sɛ Kwaku Ananse asii kuro kɛse bi a ɛyɛ kɛse sen nea ɔno ara ayɛ. Ɛno nti, ɔkɔɔ hɔ sɛ ɔbɛkasa ne abosom bi a ɔwɔ hɔ. Abosom no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ananse nua ba a ɔbaa hɔn nkyɛn no, na wayi ayɛ, na wakɔ hyɛ ne wofa hɔ." Kwebla kɔɔ nea ɔbɛbisa (abosom no na ɔkyerɛɛ no) sɛ Okra, Kraman, ne Okraman, Kraman no na wɔbɛka wɔn (kɔgye kɔkɔɔ no), enti ɔkɔfaa aduro a ɔbɛde asiesie nam, na watow gu kwan no so, na sɛ nnuom no fa kwan no a, sɛ wɔfa bi edii a, wɔrenni tumi bio sɛ wɔbɛkɔ baabi bio. Ɔbarima no (nea na ne kɔkɔɔrɔ no gyina hɔ) baa fie, na ɔkaa kyerɛɛ Kraman ne Kraman no sɛ, "Berɛ a mɛka mo kyerɛ mo nea nti a matɔ mo no adu seesei, (na ɛne eyi); biribi a ɛyɛ me de ayera, na wɔse ɛwɔ Kwaku Ananse nsam, nanso ɛda kɔtokuo bi mu a ɛwɔ afie nyinaa mmuamea." Na bio, wɔse wadi aduro na wafoɔ no mu mmoa nam na waatwa no to kwan so, enti sɛ wo du hɔ a, mfa amma wo nsa na fa ho kɔ. Kraman no see, "Kraman, woate ase, wo na wobɛnam so." Kraman no see, "Kɔ hɔ, wo a wode wo hwene kɔsɔre kɔsɔre berɛ biara." Ɛnna wɔhyɛɛ asei sɛ wɔka asɛm pii, na wɔn panin no see, "Ɛyɛ, mo yɛ" "kɔ." Mmoa no mmienu no hyɛɛ aseɛ, na wɔnam kɔron. Seesei, bere kakra na ɛka kosi sɛ wɔbɛdu baabi a nam no hyɛ, ɛnna Kraman no "tee" nam no nka. Ɔsee, "Kraman, me yamu yɛ me, na menntumi nkɔ yie." Kraman no see, "Ma yɛnkɔ, adeɛ a yɛrekɔ yɛ adeɛ titiriw." (Kraman no) see, "Kraman, menntumi." Kraman no kɔe. Kraman no kɔɔ baabi a nam no hyɛe; ɔtwee nam no nyinaa; ɔdae hɔ; ɔnnidi kwan bio. Na Kraman-kraman no duruu Ananse kuro mu, na ɔdae Ananse daakye-dan mu, wɔ ɔdan no atifi. Berɛ a ɔda hɔ no, ɔhunuu nkokoa bi a ɔrefa. Squeak! saa na ɔtɔɔ ne ti so. (Kraman no) seee, "Mmpia me, dɛn na asi?" Kraman no hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, "Me Panin kɔkɔɔ no ato mfiase, na wɔse ɛwɔ Ananse kɔtokuo mu, a ɛgyina mmofra no mma (ne kɔtokuo afoforo nyinaa mu), na sɛ wotumi kɔfa aba me nkyɛn a, mɛma wo akɔ." Kraman no seee, "Metumi." Kraman no seee, "Sɛ mema wo kɔ, na wokɔ na woampfa aba, na wotu mfirii a?" Kraman no se, "Sɛ wopɛ a, kyekyere ntoma bi wɔ me mma mu." Kraman no tookoo ntoma bi na ɔkyekyereɛ no wɔ ne mma mu. Ɛnnɛ, Nkuku no kɔɔ Kwebia no dan mu, na ɔtwee ebɔdeɛ bi wɔ adaka a ɛgyina nea afoforo nyinaa mmuaeɛ no mu. Bekyere bekyere no yɛɛ mfoni bi, na ɛyɛɛ kɛse. Ɔfaa mu kɔkɔtwee kɔfaa kɔkɔɔ no de baa Kraman no hɔ. Ɛnna Cat no too ne nsa so no, ɔtuu kwan kɔ, na ɔhyiaa Dog no, na ɔda baabi a ɔgyaa no da. Ɔse, "Wo da ha ara, na ɛhene na ɛnam nnam no?" Kraman no se, "Oh, mehunu (deɛ ɛbaa ho), ebia nnipa a ɛyɛ wɔn deɛ no baa na wɔfaa; nanso ɛhene na kɔkɔre no hyɛ?" Katam no se, "Ɛha na ɛwɔ." Kraman no se, "Wɔse asuo a ɛda kwan no asɔ, na sɛ woka nsuo mu a, wone nsuo no bottom nan, enti ebia (kɔkɔre no) bɛtɔ fam, enti" "ma me no, efisɛ, me ho asɛm a, wuhu sɛ, me nante nsuo ano." Kraman no see, "Ɛyɛ saa, fa (no)." Wɔduu asuo no ho, na Kraman no bɔɔ mu. Kraman no nso yɛɛ saa. Mpofifoɔ no, Kraman no tɔɔ asuo no ase. Kraman no nso duu asuo no mfinimfini na ɔyɛɛ mmerɛ, na berɛ a ɔrepɛ sɛ ɔsu nsuo mu mmofra, kɔkɔɔ no tɔɔ (ne anomu fii) nsuo no mu. Ɔtwene kɔɔ baabi a Kraman no hyiaa no. Kraman no kaa sɛ, "Ɛhene na kɔkɔɔ no wɔ?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛhweasee (me ano mu) kɔɔ nsuo no mu." Kraman no tuu mmirika kɔɔ nsuo no mu; ɔhunuu nsuo namfo kɛse bi a ɔrefa. Na Kraman no kyere no. Nsuo namfo no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛrekɔ so?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me kɔkɔɔ no atɔ asuo yi mu seesei, enti sɛ wopɛ asiane a, ma me nea ɛyɛ me dea mpofirim." "Sɛ woyɛn ampa a, mɛyɛ wo mmerɛ mmerɛ." Nsuo-namfo no see, "Ma yɛnkɔ nsuo no ho na matu nea ɛyɛ wo de ma wo." Berɛ a wɔnompoa nyinaa duu nsuo no ho no, nsuo-namfo no tɔɔ ne nneɛma afiri ne mu, na kɔkɔɔ no puee. Kraman no faa no na ɔbaa ɔkyerɛɛ Kraman no. Kraman no see, "Agya, mesrɛ wo, sɛ wokɔ a, moka biara about nea asie yi." Kraman no kaa ne nsa mu. Wɔduu fie, na Kraman no kae ne mpanyin biara a ɛsi, ɔsee no sɛ, "Sɛn nnam a Kraman no tannee wɔn kwan mu no nti, ɔantumi ankɔ yie. Bio, mekɔfaa kɔkɔre no aba, Kraman no ka kyerɛɛ me, berɛ a ɔtwaa asuo no, ɔnam ne wiase so, sɛ mede kɔkɔre no ama no. Medee amaa no, na ɔtoo no gu asuo no mu." Ankyɛ koraa na metoo me nsa so bio." Woɔmanfoɔ a wɔnwo hɔ nyinaa see, "Yɛnkamfo Cat." Na wɔteew, "E!" Ɛna ɔhene see, "Wo, Cat, aduane biara a meredi, mɛhwɛ sɛ mepue bi na mehyɛ wo kuku ketewa no mu. Mpapata biara a meda so, (mɛda so nko) sɛ woda ne bi so a. Wo nso, Kraman, wo bɛda gyaman a ɛyɛ hye a ɛatwa n’atwa mpo berɛ a anadwo nwunu no ba. Mmofra bɛbɔ wo mpaebɔ pɛ. Ɛno nti na awia biara a wuhu Katu no, hwee na ɔda so a ɛnyɛ mpa a ɛyɛ eyie. Bio, sɛ wotow aduane bi ase ma no a, ɔrenni bi (gye sɛ ɛwɔ mpaatease). Nanso wo Kraman ho a, yɛbɛhu no daa ɔda kuro-atwɛnmu no mu wɔ anɔpa mmerɛ a gyata no nnuane no ho. Afei wobɛhu no wɔ hɔ a wɔrebɔ no, ɔbɛsu sɛ, "Kao!" Eyi nyinaa yɛ mmerɛ a wɔkyɛɛ wɔn (de kɔyɛ saa kɔkɔɔ no asɛm no ho) nti.

Me asɛm yi a meaka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, obiara betumi agye nea ɛyɛ nokware, na na akyerɛ no nso, mobɛtumi ayɛ me ayɛyie (sɛ meaka no).
