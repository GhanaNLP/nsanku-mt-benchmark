Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo ampa, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo ampa (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea ɛyɛɛ sɛ yɛbɛhunu Okra, kraman no, a ɔda velvet mmoa so daa, nanso 'Kraman, kraman no, bɛda efie mmoa mmoa mu.

Wɔka sɛ obiara na ɔyɛyɛ ɔbaa bi a (ne nneɛma annye no koraa), sɛ obiara a ɔwo no, na ɛwu. Enti ɔkɔkɔɔ abosom bi a ɔbɛbisa no ho asɛm, na ɔbɛka kyerɛ no sɛ ɔpɛ abofra. Abosom no kaa sɛ, "Mɛma wo baako, nanso fa abofra no ho a, adwuma a ɔbɛyɛ nyinaa ne sɛ ɔbɛma wo ayɛ adeɛ, nanso da bi ɔbɛsana wo." Ɛnnamma mmienu anaa mmiɛnsa nni hɔ (a ɔbɛtwa fi abosom no nkyɛn) a ɔbaa no yɛa. Ɔwoo abofra—na ɛyɛ anansesɛm abofra—efisɛ ansa na ɔnyini kɛse. Abofra no nyinii baakofoɔ fɛfɛ bi. Da bi ɔne (ne maame) wɔ hɔ, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Maame, ma me sika kɔkɔɔ, na makɔ 'Edge-of-the-sea-country' akɔtɔ nkyene." Maame no bisaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dodo sɛn?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Asuanu" (i. e. £4). Na maame no faa sika no maa no, na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ akwan no so. Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa ɔbarima bi ne ne kraman a n'ahome yɛ nnam. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa ma me na makɔtɔ." Kraman no wura no kaa sɛ, "Wontumi ntɔ." Abofra no bisaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ sika dodo sɛn?" Kraman no wura no kaa sɛ, "Sika kɔkɔɔ a ɛyɛ Asuanu mmuaeɛ" (i. e. £4). Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dodo sɛn (ma me), fa sika kɔkɔɔ £4 yi." Ɔfaa kraman no, na ɔde baa fie. Berɛ a ɔbaaa no, ne maame kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woankɔ baabi a wopɛ kɔ no?" Ɔgyee no sɛ, "Mefaa sika kɔkɔɔ no tɔɔ kraman." Ne maame kaa sɛ, "Ho!" Wɔtena hɔ mpo mmoonsa bi. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Maame, ma me sika kɔkɔɔ na makɔ adwuma." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo nso, sɛnea woyɛ no, wofa sika kɔkɔɔ no akɔtow no bio, nanso sika dodo sɛn na wopɛ?" Ɔgyee no sɛ, "Sika kɔkɔɔ a ɛyɛ Asuanu ne suru" (i. e. £5). Ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa, ɛnna." Enti ɔkɔɔ adwumakwan no so. Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa ɔbarima bi a ɔde okraman bi akura. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔbarima, fa saa mmoa a ɛtɔn n'anan ne ne nan no ma me na makɔtɔ." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sɛ meda me poku mu a, mmoa nketewa (mice) kani me nan, enti na matɔ." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mebɔ wo mpaebɔ ma wo mma me." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wontumi ntɔ." Abofra no bisaa sɛ, "Sika dodo sɛn na wopɛ?" Ɔgyee no sɛ, "Sika kɔkɔɔ a ɛyɛ Asuanu ne suru." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛno nti na woka sɛ, wontumi ntɔ; fa, fa sika yi." Abofra no faa okraman no, na ɔde kɔɔ fie. Berɛ a ɔduu fie no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Maame, hwɛ nea mede aba yi." Ɔgyee no sɛ, "Ah, ɛne nea wɔkaa sɛ (ɛbɛyɛ) no." Abofra no tenaa hɔ (fie). Ɛyɛyɛ nnawɔtwe aduonan a ɛkyerɛ, na ɔkasaa ne na no, ɔsee no sɛ, "Ma me sika kɔkɔɔ na matumi akɔ dwadwa." Na naayɛ no see no sɛ, "Sika a me nsa ka nyinaa awie, na Asuasa baako nko ara na ɛda hɔ (£6); sɛ me ma wo eyi, na wokɔ, na wo ntɔ nneɛma a, saa na asɛm yi bɛwie." Abofra no see no sɛ, "Mete." Anɔpa a ɛba no, berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ hwee, ɔfaa ne kotokuo no na ɔkɔe, pa! Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa Ashanti bi, na ɔno nso na ɔresoa ɔpono. Ɔsee no sɛ, "Adamfo, fa wo mmoa yi mma me na matumi atɔ." Ɔno nso gyee me answers sɛ, "Mennum no, efisɛ meyɛ ho anigye." Ɔsee no sɛ, "Mɛtɔ." Anomaa no wura no see no sɛ, "Mennum no, efisɛ mehu nea ɔbetumi ayɛ ama me." Abofra no see no sɛ, "O, ma me." Ɔno see no sɛ, "Wubetumi atɔ?" Abofra no bisaa no sɛ, "Sɛn?" Ɔno gyee me answers sɛ, "Sika kɔkɔɔ Asuasa baako" (£6). Ɔsee no sɛ, "Wosusu sɛ ɛnam eyi nti na mɛnntɔ, fa sika no." Ɔfaa anomaa no kɔɔ fie. Ne na no see no sɛ, "Eyi nnyɛ yie sen nea ɛyɛɛ ansa, enti eyi ne nea woade ba?" Ɔgyee me answers sɛ, "Baabiara, eyi ne nea mende ba." Da bi, berɛ a abofra no wɔ fie no, ɔpono no frɛɛ no, ɔsee no sɛ, "Bra." Berɛ a ɔkɔɔ ne nkyɛn no, ɔpono no hyɛɛ no ase, ɔsee no sɛ, "Me kuro mu no, me yɛ ɔhene, na merekyɛ akwantuo, na obi bae na ɔkyere me, na wo nso, efisɛ woya m'asɛm, wo tɔɔ me, na seesei mebɔ wo mpaebɔ, sɛ wubetumi fa me akɔ me kuro mu a, wɔbɛda wo ase paa." Abofra no see no sɛ, "Woreka nkontompo, wobɛtu." Ɔpono no see no sɛ, "Sɛ woyɛ nea mebisa wo no nnim a, fa ntoma bi na fa kyekyere me nan mu, na fa me nan ase." Abofra no faa ntoma bi na ɔkyekyereɛ ne nan mu, na ɔkɔɔ ne akyi brɛoo, kosi sɛ wɔduu ɔpono no kuro mu. Berɛ a wɔduu kuro no anim no, mmofra na wɔrebaabi nte marbles. Berɛ a wɔhunuu no, wɔsee sɛ, "Ɔhene aba! Ɔhene aba!" Baako frɛɛ Korenti' ɔhene, na wɔkyereɛ no, na wɔtwaa ne kɔn, wɔsee sɛ, "Worema yɛka yɛn awerɛhow a ɛyɛyɛyɛ no." Na obi foforo kɔe na ɔde amanneɛ no kɔɔ. Na seesei Akwamu ɔhene see sɛ, "Wo, Gyase ɔhene, kɔ hɔ na hwɛ nea asɛm yi fa ho." Ɔkɔhwee, ɔhunuu, na ɔsan baa. Ɔse, "O, ɛyɛ nokware." Afei wɔfaa kɔm-mmoa ne atadeɛ a ɛyɛ kɛse no na wɔkɔfaa Ɔhene kɔɔ ne fie. Wɔbɔɔ abusua no nyinaa amanneɛ sɛ, sɛnea ɔdeɛ ɔrehyɛ akwantuo ase, ne sɛnea obi bi kuraa no, ne sɛnea abaayewa/abawofoɔ yi, efisɛ ɔyɛ obi a ɔwɔ ayamye, na ɔtɔɔ no, ne sɛnea ɔde no baa fie nnɛ. Mpanyimfo ne mmeranteɛ nyinaa sɔree na wɔda abaayewa/abawofoɔ no ase. Nanahemaa baa ne nsa mu a ɔde nsuo-kɔtoku a sika kɔkɔɔ dwuma trɛ n’mu; mpanyimfo no nyinaa, obiara faa nsuo-kɔtoku a sika kɔkɔɔ dwuma trɛ n’mu. Ɔhene ankasa hwɛɛ ne nsa, na ɔtuu kɔkɔɔ-beaeɛ (ring) bi fii hɔ na ɔmaa no. Na ɔse, "Fa saa kɔkɔɔ-beaeɛ yi, na nea biara a wopɛ, saa kɔkɔɔ-beaeɛ yi bɛma wo." Ɔse, "Mete ase." Na ɔde kɔkɔɔ-beaeɛ no kɔɔ ne kuro; na ɔkyerɛɛ ne maame sika kɔkɔɔ dwuma ne kɔkɔɔ-beaeɛ no. Afei na ne maame se, "Akwaaba Aku, akwaaba Aku." Tete no, berɛ a ɔ "hyɛɛ" ne sika kɔkɔɔ dwuma no, na berɛ a ɔfiri ne akwantuo mu baa, berɛ a ɔhyiraa ne maame no, na ɔmmu no. Na ɔbɔɔ ne maame amanneɛ se, "Wahu saa sika kɔkɔɔ dwuma ne saa kɔkɔɔ-beaeɛ yi; mɛkɔ na mebɛsi kuro kɛse bi a yɛbɛtena mu." Ne maame se, "Yɛ wo ho den (kyerɛ sɛ, yɛ wo ho den ma ɛnyɛ)." Abaayewa/abawofoɔ no kɔɔ na ɔgyinaa kwaeɛ mu; ɔtuu kɔkɔɔ-beaeɛ no fii hɔ na ɔtoo no dɔteɛ so; ɔse, "Kɔkɔɔ-beaeɛ, pae saa (asase) kwaeɛ ne nwunu nyinaa ama me." Na baabi a ɛyɛ no nyinaa yɛɛ pɛ. Ɔse, "Bo nea woapae no nyinaa mmoa mmoa na ma yɛmmi." Na ɛyɛɛ saa. Ɔse, "Si fie." Na ɛsii fie pii. Ɔse, "Kɔkɔɔ-beaeɛ, ma nnipa mma wɔmmeba na wɔmmentena saa fie yi mu." Na nnipa bae. Ɔmaa ne maame bɛyɛɛ Nanahemaa; ɔno nso bɛyɛɛ ɔhene. Seesei (kyerɛ sɛ, obiara wɔ nea mewerɛ fii sɛ mɛka), Ananse, Kɔkɔɔro no, na ɔyɛ ne adamfo pa. Da koro bi ɔrete hɔ (ne kuro foforo no mu) berɛ a Kwaku Ananse hyɛɛ ase kɔɔ saa mmeranteɛ yi kuro mu. (Berɛ a ɔduu hɔ,) ɔse, "O, maame ba ketewa, agya ba ketewa, woanya nkyɛnea ne nkunim, na wammɔ me ho bio sɛ wobɛhwɛ me, nanso dɛn na asi a ɛde saa nyinaa ba?" Afei na abaayewa/abawofoɔ no ka amanneɛ no nyinaa kyerɛɛ no. Kwebia no see sɛ, "Mɛkɔ me akurafoɔ hɔ (kɔgye biribi) na masan aba." Ananse kɔɔ ne akurafoɔ hɔ; ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nua baa no sɛ, "Mɛkyerɛ wo akwan kɔ me adamfo a ɔwɔ hɔ no hɔ, na sɛ woko a, fa saa nsusuo fitaa yi ma no, na tie asɛm yie na yɛ nea ɔbɛka kyerɛ wo; na wopɛ sɛ wofa saa ketene no ayɛ yie." Baa no kɔɔ, na ɔkɔɔ saa ɔbarima no akurafoɔ hɔ. Ɔbarima no ka kyerɛ no sɛ, "Sɛn na eyi, mɛhwɛ so sɛ wonsan nkɔ bio, efisɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wote me nkyɛn nnansa ansa na woasan nkɔ." Baa no see sɛ, "Mete." Afei ɔne baa no tenaa hɔ bɔm, na ɔbarima no kɔɔ ne ho nwiase; ɔpuee ketene no na ɔtoo no table so; na baa no faa no, na ɔfaa no kɔmaa ne wura, Kwaku Ananse. Ananse tee ne nsa so no, ɔde (ketene no) hyɛɛ kuro kɛse bi. Afei, ɔbarima no, berɛ a ɔbaa ne ketene no hwehwɛ, ɔanhunu. Ɔtee ase sɛ Kwebia no ahyɛ kuro kɛse a ɛyɛ kɛse sen nea ɔwɔ. Ɛno nti ɔkɔɔ abosom ketewa bi hɔ kɔbisaa no. Abosom ketewa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ananse nua baa a ɔbaa wo hɔ no, na ɔfaa no kɔmaa ne wura." Kwebia no nso kɔbisaa (abosom ketewa no na ɔkyerɛɛ no) sɛ Okra, Kraman, ne Okraman, Kraman no, bɛba (de kɔgye ketene no), enti ɔkɔgyee aduro a wɔde siesie nam, na ɔtoo no kwan so, na sɛ nnuom no fa kwan no a, sɛ wɔfa bi edi a, wɔrenkyene kwan bio. Ɔbarima no (a ne ketene no gyawu) baa fie, na ɔka kyerɛɛ Kraman ne Kraman no sɛ, "Berɛ a mɛka mo kyerɛ sɛ adɛn nti na matɔ mo no, ayɛ; biribi a ɛyɛ me de ayɛ gyawu, na wɔse ɛwɔ Kwebia nsam, na ɛda adaka bi mu a ɛwɔ mmuaeɛ nyinaa mmuamea. Bio, wɔse ɔde aduro guu onuasɛm nam mu na ɔtoo no kwan so, enti sɛ mokuu hɔ a, mfa mmo a, twene mu." Kraman no see sɛ, "Kraman, wote, wo na wobɛnam so." Kraman no see sɛ, "Kɔ hɔ, wo a wode wo hwene hwehwɛ asɛm berɛ biara." Afei wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kyerɛɛ anansesɛm pii, na wɔn panyin no see sɛ, "Ɛyɛ, monkɔ." Nnuom mmienu no kɔɔ, na wɔnam kwan no so. Berɛ a ɛkyɛ nkyɛ nkyɛ na wɔbɛdu baabi a nam no teyɛ, na Kraman no "tee" nam no nka. Ɔse, "Kraman, me yamu yɛ me, na menntumi nkɔ so." Krakman no se, "Ma yɛnkɔ, asɛm a ɛwɔ hɔ yi ho hia." (Kraman no) se, "Kraman, menntumi." Krakman no kɔe. Kraman no kɔɔ baabi a nam no hyɛe; ɔtwee nam no nyinaa; ɔdae hɔ; ɔntumi ankɔ so bio. Na Krakman no dɔɔ Ananse kuro mu, na ɔdae Ananse daakye-dan mu, wɔ ɔdan no ase (ɔdan no wiase). Berɛ a ɔda hɔ no, ɔhunuu mmoa ketewa bi a ɔreka. Squeak! sɛ ɔtɔɔ ne ti so no. (Mmoa ketewa no) se, "Mmpam me, dɛn na ɛrekɔ on?" Krakman no hyɛɛ no ase se, "Me Panin ketewa bi a ɔwɔ finger no ato, na wɔse ɛwɔ Ananse kɔtoku mu, a ɛgyina mmua no mma (ne kɔtoku afoforo nyinaa mu), na sɛ wotumi kɔfa ama me a, mɛma wo nkɔ." Mmoa ketewa no se, "Metumi." Krakman no se, "Sɛ mema wo nkɔ, na wokɔ na woanfa amma me, na wotu mfi?" Mmoa ketewa no se, "Sɛ wopɛ a, kyekyere ntoma bi wɔ me mma mu." Krakman no faa ntoma bi na ɔkyekyereɛ no wɔ ne mma mu. Ɛnna mmoa ketewa no kɔɔ Kwebra no dan mu, na ɔtwee kɔtoku a ɛgyina mmua no mma no mu. Bekae bekae no, ɔyɛɛ mfoni ketewa bi, na ɛyɛɛ kɛse. Ɔfaa mu kɔfaa ketewa no kɔmaa Krakman no. Krakman no nna ne nsa nka no, ɔtuu mfi, na ɔhyiaa Kraman no, na ɔda baabi a ɔgyaae no. Ɔse, "Woda ha ara, na nam no wɔ he?" Kraman no se, "Oh, menghunuu (deɛ ɛbaa ne ho), ebia nnipa a ɛyɛ wɔn deɛ no baa na wɔfaa; na ketewa no wɔ he?" Krakman no se, "Ɛwɔ ha." Kraman no se, "Wɔse asu a ɛwɔ akwan no so no ayɛ kɛse, na sɛ woka nsuo a, woyɛ nnam wɔ ase, enti ebia (ketewa no) bɛtɔ ase, enti ma me, efisɛ, me nso, wuhu, meka nsuo no wiase." Krakman no se, "Ɛyɛ saa, fa (no)." Wɔdɔɔ asu no ho, na Kraman no bɔɔ mu. Krakman no nso bɔɔ mu. Ɛhwease no, Krakman no tɔɔ asu no ase. Kraman no nso dɔɔ asu no mma mu na ɔyɛɛ nkum, na berɛ a ɔrepɛ sɛ ɔhwee nsuo mu no, ketewa no tɔɔ (ne anom fii) nsuo no mu. Ɔtɔɔ asu no ase na ɔbaa baabi a Krakman no wɔ. Krakman no se, "Ketewa no wɔ he?" Ɔse, "Ɛtɔɔ (me anom fii) nsuo no mu." Krakman no tuu mmirika kɔɔ nsuo no mu; ɔhunuu nsuo-nam kɛse bi a ɔreka. Na Krakman no kyinii no. Nsuo-nam no se, "Dɛn na ɛrekɔ?" Ɔse, "Me ketewa no tɔɔ asu yi mu seesei, enti sɛ wopɛ asɛm a, ma me me deɛ mpra mpra." "Sɛ wumpa me a, mɛyɛ wo mmerɛw mpra so." Nsuo-nam no see, "Ma yɛnkɔ nsuo no nkyɛn na makyɛ wo nea ɛyɛ wo de." Berɛ a wɔnommoa no duu nkyɛn no, nsuo-nam no tɔɔ ne nneɛma afiri ne mu, na kɔkɔɔ no puee. Kraman no faa no baa na ɔkyerɛɛ Kraman no. Kraman no see, "Agya, mesrɛ wo, sɛ wokɔ a, mma wo mka nea asiw yi ho." Kraman no kaa ne nsa. Wɔduu fie, na Kraman no kaaa ne panin no nea asiw nyinaa, ɔsee, "Sɛn nnam a Kraman no nam guaeɛ no nti, ɔntumi ankɔ yie. Bio, me kɔfaa kɔkɔɔ no baa, Kraman no see me, berɛ a ɔtwaa nsuo no, ɔnam ne wiase so, sɛ mmpa kɔkɔɔ no ama no. Me paa no ma no, na ɔtoo no gu nsuo no mu. Berɛ kakra a ɛtwɛɛ no, na mefaa me nsa too so bio." Nnipa a wɔwɔ hɔ nyinaa see, "Ayeyi nka Kraman no." Na wɔteew mu see, "E!" Ɛnna ɔhene no see, "Wo, Kraman, aduane biara a merenu, mɛhwɛ sɛ mepue kakra na mehyɛ wo kuku ketewa no mu. Mpapata biara a meda so, (mɛda so pɛ) sɛ woda ne mfa bi so. Wo nso, Kraman, wobehu wo wɔ gyata a ɛatwa no mu berɛ a anadwo nwunu no ba. Mmerɛw mpɛn pii na nnipa bɛbɔ wo." Ɛno nti na awia biara a wuhu Kraman no, hwee nso nna na ɛnyɛ mpapata a ɛyɛ eyie. Bio, sɛ wotow aduane bi gu asaase so ma no a, ɔrenni (gye sɛ ɛwɔ mpa so). Na Kraman no nso, dabiara yɛhu no ɔda afie hɔ wɔ anadwo gyata a ɛatwa no mu. Bio wuhu no wɔ hɔ a wɔrebɔ no, ɔbɛteew, "Kao!" Ɛyɛ nneɛma a ɛsi berɛ a wɔkyɛɛ wɔn (de kɔfaa kɔkɔɔ no) no nti.

Me asɛm yi a meaka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, obiara betumi agye nea ɛyɛ nokware, na na akyerɛ no nso, mobɛtumi ayɛ me ayɛyie (sɛ meaka no).
