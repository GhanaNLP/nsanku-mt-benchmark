Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea Owea, Abosom-akotere, ne Opatuo nyaa wɔn nnyigyei a ɛyɛ soronko no

OWEA, Dua-kraman no, (ne adiwaa ne) Kwaduampon Kyerefo, Opatuo, Akyekyere no, ne Abosom-Akotere, Akokrobɛa no, (ne adiwaa ne), “Bɔfoɔ owuo”, “Yɛ-nnwoma-afe-baako-na-yɛ-yɛ-biribi-yɛn-no”, “Ɔnantee-tee-tee-nanso-ɔbɛ-du-aburokyire-a-ɛwɔ-akyirikyiri”, (saman yi nyinaa) tenaa baako ara. Saa berɛ no, obiara nni yɛfɛ. Na Adobe-dua no tɔɔ ne ho maa wɔn. Da biara, sɛ Adobe-dua no sɔree a, ɔkɔtwa ne ho ma obi; ɔkyena ɔkɔtwa ne ho ma obi foforɔ; na ɔkyena bio ɔkɔtwa ne ho ma obi foforɔ. Da koro bi, berɛ a ɛyɛɛ Bɔfoɔ-owuo no kyɛfa ( sɛ ɔbɛka Adobe no sɛ ɔnyɛ adwuma) na ɔhwehwɛɛ Adobe-dua no, ɛhe na ɔhunuu no? Enti ɔkɔkaeɛ OWEA ne Opatuo. Na wɔse, “Kɔfrɛ no.” Enti Bɔfoɔ-owuo no gyinaa ne nne: “Adobe o! Adobe o!”

Ɛdua-kraman no suu:

“Berɛ a ɔkɔe no, ɔannsan baa; ɔkɔe, ɔannsan baa, ɔannsan baa, ɔannsan baa!”

Na Aberewa no frɛɛ:

“Yɛatwe (sika no) na yɛbɛtumi ayɛ sɛ yɛatow no afutu, m! Yɛatwe (sika no) na yɛbɛtumi ayɛ sɛ yɛatow no afutu, m! m!”

Na ɛno nenea saa mmoa mmiɛnsa yi suu.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
