Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea Owea, Abosom-akotere, ne Opatuo nyaa wɔn nnyigyei a ɛyɛ soronko no

OWEA, Dua-kraman no, (nea ɔwɔ din foforo sɛ) Kwaduampon Kyerefo, Opatuo, Nyampɔn-kɔkɔɔ no, ne Abosom-Akotere, Akokɔsradee no, (ne din a ɛyɛ), “Bɔfo no owuo”, “Yɛ-mfe-baako-na-ɔyɛ-biribi-yɛyɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no-yɛ-no-no- Da biara, berɛ a Adobe-tree sorɔɔ, ɔkɔɔ ne nkyɛn kɔtweei no; ɔkyena ɔkɔtweei afoforo bi; na ɔkyena nso ɔkɔtweei afoforo bi. Da bi, berɛ a ɛyɛɛ Creator’s-death nkyɛn ( sɛ ɔbɛhyɛ Adobe no so sɛ ɔnyɛ adwuma ama no) na ɔhwehwɛɛ Adobe-tree no, ɛhe na ɔhunuu no? Enti ɔkɔɔ kyerɛɛ Tree-bear ne Owl amanneɛ no. Na wɔse, “Kɔfrɛ no.” Na Bɔfoɔ-owuo no gyina hɔ kasaa kɛse: “Adobe o! Adobe o!”

Ɛdua-kraman no suu:

“Berɛ a ɔkɔe no, ɔannsan baa; ɔkɔe, ɔannsan baa, ɔannsan baa, ɔannsan baa!”

Na Aberewa no frɛɛ:

“Yɛatwe (sika no) na yɛbɛtumi ayɛ sɛ yɛatow no afutu, m! Yɛatwe (sika no) na yɛbɛtumi ayɛ sɛ yɛatow no afutu, m! m!”

Na ɛno nenea saa mmoa mmiɛnsa yi suu.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
