Sɛnea ɛyɛɛ koraa sɛ mmaa a yɛnne obi baako ayɛ ayere no nnante mmoa baako so

Wɔka sɛ tete bi, na ɔyere mmienu a yɛnka wɔn bɔne wɔn wɔ hɔ; baako wɔn mu na ɔwɔ ba, na ɔyɛnka ne nnamfo no nni ba. Wɔn mmienu no tenaa fie baako mu; wɔyɛɛ aduane wɔ dua baako so. Da koro bi, obi a ɔwɔ ba no kɔɔ, ɔse ɔrekɔ ayɛ (abusua) ntade nwede, na berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔfaa ne ba no maa ne kɔtɔfo no, ɔse, "Mennnyɛ mmerɛ pii wɔ saa (nwede) yi ho, enti fa saa akokoa yi; sɛ wuhu anɔpa owia no rehyerɛn mframa mu a, fa ɔno mu, nanso sɛ owia no yɛ den a, fa ɔno akyirikyiri kɔ hyɛ nnuane mu." Ɔkaa sɛ, “Me ba wɔ hene?” Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, “Mefa no akɔ hyɛ ne papa hɔ, enti kɔfa wo aduane a ɛwɔ ɔdan no ase hɔ na edi.” Ɔbaa no kɔfaa aduane no; ɔtuu aduane no ho mfutuma na ɔkaa sɛ, “Wofua saa nsuom foforo yi wɔ hene?” Ɔgyee me ase sɛ, “Berɛ a wukɔree ntade hohoro hɔ no, na meguam no.” Ɔbaa no redi aduane no; berɛ a ɔreedi no na ɔkaa sɛ, “Me ba wɔ hene” “ba?” Ne kɔtɔ-yere no ayɛ no mmuae sɛ, “Maka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ ɔwɔ ne papa hɔ.” Ɔbaa no dwee ne aduane, na ɔkɔɔ ne kunu hɔ, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma me me ba no, efisɛ m’anhu no koraa nnɛ.” Papa no kaa sɛ, "M’anhu no."
