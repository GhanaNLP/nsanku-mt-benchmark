Abofra biara ɛsɛ sɛ ɔware obi a wɔde no hyɛ ne nsam wɔ awareɛ mu.

Na obiara a wɔde no kɔmaa no sɛ ɔnyagye no no, ɔka sɛ, "Menya no anigye." Wɔde no kɔmaa ɔyadeɛfoɔ bi, na ɔka sɛ, "Ei! saa ɔbarima yi wuo wɔ ne ho, menya no anigye."

Da koro bi, ɔkɔɔ nnɔbae no mu, ɔse ɔrekɔ tutu plantains. Berɛ a ɔkɔe no, Kwaboaso worehɔ, na asamanfoɔ no repia no. Ɛnna ɔbɔdeɛ no yɛɛ asuadeɛ kɔ asamanfoɔ no so, na baako tee gyaeɛ. Abofra mmoa panyin no kaa sɛ, "Ɔkrantifo yi nomaa nsa dua na nsa no akɔ n'ase, fa no kɔto hɔ." Ɔbɔdefo no wuree abofra mmoa no nyinaa, na panyin no nkoaa na ɔgyaei.
