Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Abofra biara ɛsɛ sɛ ɔware obi a wɔde no hyɛ ne nsam wɔ awareɛ mu.

Na obiara a wɔde no kɔmaa no sɛ ɔnyagye no no, ɔka sɛ, "Menya no anigye." Wɔde no kɔmaa ɔyadeɛfoɔ bi, na ɔka sɛ, "Ei! saa ɔbarima yi wuo wɔ ne ho, menya no anigye."

Da koro bi, ɔkɔɔ nnɔbae no mu, ɔse ɔrekɔ tutu plantains. Ɔfaa cuter na ɔpiraa plantain no, to! ɛnna ɔhunu sɛ nnipa ketewa no atena plantains no so; wɔtɔɔ fam, na wɔbaa, na wɔkyinii Kwaboaso; wɔse, "Wo ne obi a, sɛ wɔfa wo kɔma obi a, wofura wo ti, pusu! pusu!" Na asamanfo no kyinii no na wɔse: "Yɛnna, ma yɛmpira no."

Yɛmiimi no, O!

Yɛmiimi Kwaboaso.

Bra, ma yɛmpia no.

Yɛmiimi no, O!

Ɛnnɛ, berɛ a ɔbɔfoɔ a wɔde Kwaboaso hyɛɛ ne nsam no tee Kwaboaso nneɛma no, ɔsee, "Merekɔ sɛ mɛhwehwɛ nea ɛrekɔ so, efisɛ yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛde bɔne siesie bɔne." Berɛ a ɔkɔe no, Kwaboaso worehɔ, na asamanfoɔ no repia no. Ɛnna ɔbɔfoɔ no yɛɛ asuade kɔ asamanfoɔ no so, na baako tee gyaei. Asamanfoɔ no mu kɛse no see (ne nnamfoɔ no), "Wano adinom, na awaren; ma yɛmfa no akɔto hɔ mprempren, (na yɛkɔpya no bio)." Ɔbɔfoɔ no yɛɛ asuade bio, na baako foforo gyaei. Asamanfoɔ no mu kɛse no see, "Ɔkrantifoɔ yi adinom na awaren, faa no akɔto hɔ." Ɔbɔfoɔ no bum asamanfoɔ no nyinaa, na ɔgyaei kɛse no pɛ. Asamanfoɔ no mu kɛse no frɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no, ɔsee no, "Bra." Ɔbɔfoɔ no see, "Menba." Asamanfoɔ no mu kɛse no see, "O, bra, mɛnyɛ wo hwee." Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔe. Asamanfoɔ no mu kɛse no see, "Hwɛ me dan mu hɔ, na wubutu asuade no nnua ( gunpowder) ne nnamfoɔ nyinaa a woyɛɛ asuade no, fa nea ɛyɛ wo deɛ, na fa Kwaboaso nso, nanso ansa na wakɔ, kɔtwa aberewa, na berɛ a worekɔ no, tow wɔn afire, na sɛ asamanfoɔ foforo no sɔre na wɔreba wo nkyɛn a, berɛ a wɔbɛgyina hɔ repa wɔn nkuma, baako baako, na wo akɔ akyirikyiri, akyirikyiri, akyirikyiri." Enti ɔkɔtwaa aberewa, na ɔfaa Kwaboaso nso, na berɛ a ɔduu kwan no, ɔtoo aberewa baako afire. Ɔkɔɔ so yɛɛ saa kɔsi sɛ ɔduu fie. Na berɛ a ɛnkyɛ na wɔadu fie no, hwɛ, asamanfoɔ no retwa wɔn nanan. Ɔtoo aberewa a ɛkyɛɛ no afire, na asamanfoɔ no kɔɔ ne akyi na (wosan) san kɔe. Ɛnna ɔbɔfoɔ no san Kwaboaso maa ne abusua. Ɛnna ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔɔ ne fie. Ɛnnɛ, ɔbɔfoɔ no te hɔ, na ɔhunuu sɛ abotiren aduru ne fie, na ɔsee, "Dɛn ne amanneɛ no?" Abotiren no see, "Kwaboaso se wabi asɔfoɔ a wɔbɛbɔ ne ho ban, ɔse se seesei ɔpɛ sɛ ɔware wo."
