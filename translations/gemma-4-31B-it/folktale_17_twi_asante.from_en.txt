Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛnea Onyame anansesem bɛyɛɛ "Ananse-nsɛm"

KWAKU ANANSE, Kɔkɔɔro no, kɔɔ Nyankonpon, Nimdeɛ-Nyame hɔ, damitɛ sɛ ɔtɔ Nyame anansesɛm. Nyame bisaa no sɛ, "Wobetumi atɔ wɔn?" Kɔkɔɔro no kaa sɛ, "Aane, metumi." Nyame kaa sɛ, "Kuro kɛseɛ a wɔwɔ tumi te sɛ Kokofu, Bekwai, Asumengya ayɛ, nanso wɔantumi antɔ wɔn, na wo a woyɛ ɔbarima a onni hwee, (wokaa) sɛ wobetumi?" Kɔkɔɔro no bisaa sɛ, "(Anansesɛm no) sika a wɔde tɔ no yɛ dɛn?" Nyame kaa sɛ, "Wontumi ntɔ wɔn a biribiara nni hɔ a wobɛde tɔ wɔn, na mmom Onini; Osebo, Gyata no; Mmoatia; (ne) Mmoboro." Kɔkɔɔro no kaa sɛ, "Mɛfa eyi nyinaa bi mma wo, na (deɛ ɛka ho), mɛka me yere kɔkɔɔro, Nsia (Nea ɛtɔ aduonu), mfa mma wo." Nyame kaa sɛ, "Kɔfa mma me ɛnna." Kɔkɔɔro no baa, na ɔkae ne naama nyinaa fa ho, ɔse, "Mepɛ sɛ matɔ Nyame anansesɛm, na Nyame se mɛfa Onini; Osebo, Gyata no; Mmoatia; ne Mmoboro; na mika sɛ mɛka wo mfa mma Nyame." Ɛnnɛ Kɔkɔɔro no ne ne yere Aso hwehwɛɛ kwan, ɔse, "Yɛbɛyɛ dɛn na yɛanya Onini?" Aso kaa kyerɛ no sɛ, "Kɔtwa abebie dua dua baako, na twa nsuo-nkwa bi nso, na fa bra." Kɔkɔɔro no nso baa ne nneɛma no mu. Aso kaa sɛ, "Fa kɔ asuom." Ananse faa wɔn kɔɔ; berɛ a ɔnam kɔ no, ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ tenten sen no, ɛnyɛ tenten sen no; woka nkontompo, ɛyɛ tenten sen no." Kɔkɔɔro no kaa sɛ, "Ɔwo hɔ, ɔda hɔ." Onini (a ɔteee saa nkɔmɔ a ɔyɛ ne ho no) bisaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛrekɔ so?" Kɔkɔɔro no kaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ (me yere) Aso, a ɔne me renoa sɛ saa abebie dua yi tenten sen wo, na meka sɛ ɔreka nkontompo?" Onini nso kaa sɛ, "Fa mma me, na bra na yɛtutu me." Ɔfaa abebie dua no na ɔtoo no Onini nipadua no so. Ɔse, "Yɛyɛ tenten." Onini nso yɛɛ tenten, na Ananse faa nsuo-nkwa no na ɔkyekyere no (na nwenene! nwenene! nwenene! ne nne a ɔde kyekyere no no kɔɔ ne ti hɔ). Ananse, Kɔkɔɔro no, kaa sɛ, "Kwasea, mɛfa wo akɔ Nyame hɔ na mafa wo atɔ Nyame anansesɛm." Ananse faa no kɔɔ Nyame hɔ. Nyame kaa sɛ, "Me nsa aka (se." (Meanya no), nneɛma a ɛda hɔ too da hɔ." Kwebla san baa, na ɔbaa kyerɛɛ ne yere nea aseni no, ɔse, "Nnamene na ɛda hɔ." Ne yere se, "Kɔhwehwɛ kuku, na fa nsuo hyɛ mu, na fa kɔ." Kwebla kɔɔ kwae mu, na ɔhunuu Nnamene (akwanhyia) a wɔasiesie hɔ, na ɔtɔɔ nsuo kakra gu hɔ na ɔsprinkled wɔ Nnamene no so. Ɛna Kwebla tɔɔ nsuo a ɛda hɔ no nyinaa gu ne ho, na ɔtwaa aburo aba na ɔde hyɛɛ ne ti. Afei ɔkasaa Nnamene no se, "Sɛdeɛ osuo aa, ɛnyɛ yie sɛ mobɛhyɛ me kuku yi mu, na osuo anka mo; monnhunu sɛ meafa aburo aba asie me ho?" Ɛna Nnamene no se, "Yɛda wo ase Aku, yɛda wo ase Aku." Nnamene no nyinaa tuu, na wɔkɔhyɛɛ kuku no mu, fom! Kwebla kasaa anom no, na ɔse, "Kwaseaa, meanya mo, na merefa mo kɔgye Onyame anansesɛm (de asesa)." Na ɔfaa Nnamene no kɔhyɛɛ Onyame hɔ. Onyame se, "Me nsa aka no, nea ɛda hɔ too da hɔ." Kwebla san baa bio, na ɔkaa kyerɛɛ ne yere, ɔse, "Osebo, Gyata no na ɛda hɔ." Aso se, "Kɔtoo awu." Ananse se, "Ɛyɛ aduane, mehu." Ɛna Kwebla kɔhwehwɛɛ Gyata no nan nkyekyem, na (sɛ ɔhunuu hɔ no) ɔtoo awu kɛse bi, na ɔtrasie no, na ɔsan baa fie. Da a ɛdi so anɔpa paa, berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ ase kɔgye hɔ no, Kwebla se ɔbɛkɔ, na berɛ a ɔkɔe no, (hwan) Gyata bi da awu no mu. Ananse se: "Papa ketewa ba, maame ketewa ba, meka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ mma wo nnom, na seesei, sɛdeɛ obi bɛhwɛ wo a, woanom, ɛno nti na woatɔ awu no mu; sɛ meka se mɛyi wo afiri hɔ a, ɔkyena, sɛ wohunu me, anaa me mma bi, wobɛkɔka wɔn." Gyata no se, "O! Me ntumi nyɛ saa." Ananse kɔtwaa nnuane mmienu, ɔde baako guu ha, na baako guu hɔ. Ɔse, "Fa wo nan baako gu ha, na fa wo nan baako gu hɔ nso." Na Gyata no gyinaa (baabi a ɔkae no). Berɛ a ɔrepɛ sɛ ɔkɔ soro no, Ananse tee ne bɛtɛɛ, na ansa obi anya n'ani, ɛtɔɔ ne ti so, gao! (ne nne no). Awu no huu Gyata no (na) fom! (ne nne a ɔtɔɔ mu no). Anansefaa nnidiso kɔtɔɔ awu no mu kɔgye Gyata no firi hɔ. Ɔfaa Leopard no baa; ɔsee, "Kwasea, merefa wo kɔsɛn Nyame anansesɛm no." Ɔmaa Leopard no kɔhyɛɛ Nyame, Onyame a ɔwɔ soro no hɔ. Onyame see, "Me nsa aka, deɛ a ɛka no, ɛka." Kwasia Ananse bae, na ɔtɔɔ Akuaa (adeɛ a yɛyɛ efie fɛfɛɛfɛ a ɛyɛ dua), na ɔfaa nsuo a ɛyɛ mmerɛ (fitaa) tɔɔ ne ho, na ɔpɔɔ eto, na ɔde kakra hyɛɛ Akuaa no nsa mu, na ɔpɔɔ bio na ɔde guu kuku mu; ɔde ntoma kyekyereɛ Akuaa no mma, na ɔde kɔtoo odum dua bi ase, baabi a Asamanfoɔ no ba kɔda. Na Asamanfoɔ baakoe bae. Ɔsee, “Akua, mɛtumi adi saa eto yi kakra?” Ananse tɔɔ ntoma no, na Akuaa no tee ne ti. Asamanfoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ ne nua baako, ɔsee, “Ɔse mɛtumi adi kakra.” Ne nua no see, “Di mpo, ɛnna.” Na ɔdii no nyinaa, na ɔdaasee no (Akuaa no). Nanso berɛ a ɔdaasee no, anka anka. Na Asamanfoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ ne nua, “Sɛ mɛdaase no a, ɔnka bi.” Asamanfoɔ no nua no see, “Pa ne nusua be.” Na ɔpaa no, pa! Na ne nsa hyɛɛ mu. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nua, “Me nsa ahyɛ mu.” Ne nua no see, “Fa deɛ a ɛka no mpo pa ne nusua bio.” Na ɔfaa no na ɔpaa no, pa! na eyi nso hyɛɛ mu. Na Asamanfoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ ne nua, “Me nsa mmienu nyinaa ahyɛ mu.” Ne nua no see, “Fa wo yafunu mmpɔ no.” Ɔmpɔɔ no, na ne yafunu nso hyɛɛ mu. Na Ananse bae na ɔkyekyereɛ no, na ɔsee, “Kwasea, m’anya wo, mɛfa wo kɔma Onyame (sɛ mɛsɛn) ne anansesɛm no.” Na ɔde no kɔɔ fie. Na Ananse ka kyerɛɛ ne na, Ya Nsia, ɔsee, “Sɔre, ma yɛnkɔ, efisɛ merefa wo ne Asamanfoɔ yi kɔma Onyame (sɛ mɛsɛn) ne anansesɛm no.” Ɔfaa wɔn na ɔkɔɔ baabi a Onyame wɔ. Ɔsee, “Onyame, Asamanfoɔ baako ne me kɔkɔɔ a mekae no, wɔwɔ ha.” Afei Onyame frɛɛ (ne mpanyimfo), Kontire ne Akwam mpanyimfo, Adonten, Gyase; Oyoko, Ankobea, ne Kyidom. Na ɔde asɛm no gyee wɔn anim, ɔse, “Ɔhene kɛse bebree na wɔreba, nanso wɔnnyɛe biara sɛ wɔbɛtɔ Nyame anansesɛm no, nanso Kwaku Ananse, Kɔkɔrofo no, atumi aboa sika a ɛsɛ; Mmoboro, Namfo no, na ɔde baa me nsam; Mmoatia, Aberewa no, na ɔde baa me nsam; Osebo, Gyata no, na ɔde baa me nsam; Onini, Awɔden no, na ɔde baa me nsam; na Ananse ankasa na ɔde ne maame trɔ aawofo no mu; eyi nyinaa hyɛ ha.” Ɔse, “To ne ho nkate.” “Eee!” (na wɔteɛ). Nyame se, “Kwaku Ananse, efie nnɛ ne mmaeɛ kɔsi dabi, megye me Nyame anansesɛm no na mede ma wo, kose! kose! kose! me nhyira, nhyira, nhyira. Yɛrenfrɛ wɔn Nyame anansesɛm bio, na mmom yɛbɛfrɛ wɔn Anansesɛm.”

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
