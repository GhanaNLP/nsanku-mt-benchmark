Sɛnea Onyame anansesem bɛyɛɛ "Ananse-nsɛm"

KWAKU ANANSE, Kɔkɔɔro no, kɔɔ Nyankonpon, Onyame a ɔwɔ soro no hɔ, na wakɔtɔ ne nsɛm. Nyame see, "Wobetumi aglo wɔn?" Kweku Ananse see, "Aane, metumi." Nyame see, "Kuro kɛseɛ a wɔwɔ tumi te sɛ Kokofu, Bekwai, Asumengya ayɛ, nanso wɔannu tumi atɔ wɔn, na wo a woyɛ ɔbarima a onni hwee, (wo se) wobetumi?" Kweku Ananse see, "(Anansesɛm no) sika a wɔde tɔ no yɛ dɛn?" Nyame see, "Hwee ntumi ntɔ wɔn." na Onini; Osebo, Gyata no; Mmoatia; (ne) Mmoboro." Kwebla see, "Mɛba mɛfa wei nyinaa mu kakra, na (deɛ ɛyɛ kɛse), mɛka me yere kɔkɔɔ no, Nsia, ho mma." Nyame see, "Kɔ faa wɔn mma." Kwebla baa, na ɔkae ne na a ne ho nyinaa, ɔsee no, "Mepɛ sɛ metwɛ Nyame anansesɛm, na Sk Nyame se mede Onini, Awɔ; Osebo, Gyata; Mmoatia, Aberewa; ne Mmoboro, Nnaman ba; na me se mɛde wo mma wɔn nkaso a mɛkɔ ma Nyame." Ɛnna Kwaku Ananse ne ne yere Aso hwehwɛɛ kwan, ɔka kyerɛ no sɛ, "Yɛbɛyɛ dɛn na yɛanya Onini, Awɔ no?" Aso nso ka kyerɛ no sɛ, "Kɔ twa abɔnten dua dua baako, na twa nsuo-dua nso, na fa bra." Na th Spider ba ne wɔn. Na Aso see sɛ, "Fa wɔn kɔ asuo no ho." Na Ananse faa wɔn kɔe; berɛ a ɔnam kɔ no, ɔse, "Ɛyɛ tenten sen no, ɛnyɛ tenten sen no; wure, ɛyɛ tenten sen no." Kwebla no see sɛ, "Ɔwo hɔ, ɔda hɔ." Awɔden no (a ɔteee saa nkɔmmɔ a ɛyɛ n'adwene mu no) bisaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛrekɔ so?" Kwebla no see sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ (me yere) Aso, a ɔreka me ho asɛm sɛ saa abɔnten yi yɛ l "yɛn nyinaa sen wo, na meka sɛ ɔyɛ atirigye." Na Onini, Atuokoro no, see sɛ, "Fa bra, na bra na woatutu me." Ɔfaa abɔnten no na ɔtoo no Onini nipadua no so. Ɔsee no, "Yɛyɛ wo ho tenten." Na Atuokoro no yɛɛ ne ho tenten, na Ananse faa nkuran no na ɔkyekyere no (na nkuran no kyekyere nnyigyei no yɛɛ nwenene! nwenene! nwenene! kosi sɛ ɔduu ne ti ho. Ananse, Kwebla, see sɛ, "Kwasea, mɛfa wo akɔ Nyame hɔ na mɛnya Nyame anansesɛm (ma wo)." Ananse faa no kɔɔ Nyame hɔ. Nyame see sɛ, "Me nsa aka (ye. (Meanya no), nea ɛda so wɔ hɔ no, ɛda so wɔ hɔ." Kwebla san baa, na ɔbaa ɔkaa ne yere bi a ɛsii no, ɔse, "Nnamene na ɛda so wɔ hɔ." Ne yere see no, "Hwehwɛ kuku koro, na fa nsu hyɛ mu, na fa kɔ." Kwebla kɔɔ kwae mu, na ɔhunuu Nnamene (akwanhyia) a wɔasiesie wɔ hɔ, na ɔtɔɔ nsu bi gu hɔ na ɔpɛpɛɛ no wɔ Nnamene no so. Kwasia no sɔɔ nea ɛka no gu ne ho, na ɔtwaa kyɛkye akyɛdeɛ baako na ɔde hyɛɛ ne ti. Na seesei ɔkasaa kyerɛe Nanaman, ɔsee wɔn sɛ, "Sɛnea osuo bae no, anyɛ yie sɛ mobɛhyɛ me kuku yi mu, na osuo anka mo; monnuhu sɛ meafa aburodua leaf bi a meafura me ho?" Ɛnna Nanaman no see sɛ, "Yɛda wo ase Aku, yɛda wo ase Aku." Nanaman no nyinaa tuu, na wɔnyɛe kuku no mu, fom! Ananse Papa trahaa anom, na ɔseɛ, "Kwaseaa, m’ayɛ mo mmofra, na merefa mo kɔ Onyame hɔ kɔgye anansesɛm (ma mo)." Na ɔfa Namfuo no kɔ Onyame hɔ. Nyame see, "Me nsa aka, nea a ɛka no aka." Kwebla baa bio, na ɔkaa ne yere no ase, na ɔsee no, "Osebo, Aberewa no, na ɔka." Aso see no, "Kɔ hwee awa." Ananse see no, "Ɛyɛ aduane, mete ase." Ɛnna Kwebla kɔhwehwɛɛ Aberewa no nan nkyekyɛmu, na (berɛ a ɔhwehwɛɛ no ahuu) ɔhwee awa kɛse bi, na ɔtrataa so, na ɔsan baa fie. Da a ɛdi so anɔpa kɛse, berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ asei sɛ wɔda adi, Kwebla kaa sɛ ɔbɛkɔ, na berɛ a ɔkɔe no, (hwɛ) Aberewa bi da aba abura no mu. Ananse see: "Agya ketewa ba, ɛna ɛna ketewa ba, meka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ woankɔ nsa mu, na seesei, sɛnea obi bɛhwɛ wo a, woakɔ nsa mu, ɛno nti na woatɔ abura mu; sɛ meka sɛ mɛyi wo afiri mu a, ɔkyena, sɛ wohu me, anaa me mma biara, wobɛkɔ akukoo hɔ." Leopard no see, "O! "Mentumi nni saa adeɛ yi ase." Ananse kɔtweee nnuaba mmienu, ɔde baako guu ha, na ɔde baako guu hɔ. Ɔsee no, "Fa wo nan baako too ha, na fa wo nan baako foforo too ha." Na Leopard fee wɔn (baabi a ɔka kyerɛɛ no). Berɛ a ɔrepue no, Ananse gyee ne cuter no, na ansa na obi ahu, ɛtɔɔ ne ti so, gao! (ɛno ne nne a ɛyɛɛ). Pit no huu Leopard no (na) fom! (ne nneɛma a ɛtɔɔ mu no nne). Anansefaa nnidua kɔtɔɔ abura no mu kɔgyee Leopard no. Ɔgyee Leopard no na ɔde baa; ɔsee no sɛ, "Kwasea, merefa wo kɔkyerɛ anansesɛm a ɛwɔ Nyame hɔ." Ɔfaa Leopard no kɔhyɛɛ Nyame, Onyame, no anim. Nyame see, "Me nsa aka, nea a ɛtinkum no, ɛtinkum." Kweku Ananse bae, na ɔtwaa Akuafoɔ ba (adeɛ a yɛtwa fi dua mu a ɛyɛ tuntum na ɛyɛ papap), na ɔgyaeɛ nsuo a ɛyɛ kɔkɔɔ (firi dua mu) na ɔde hyɛɛ adeɛ no nipadua so, na ɔpɔnee eto, na ɔde kakra hyɛɛ adeɛ no nsa mu, na ɔpɔnee bio na ɔde guu kuku kɔkɔɔ mu; ɔde ntoma kyekyeree adeɛ no mma. na ɔfaa no kɔtoo odum dua bi ase, baabi a Aberewa-asamanfo no ba kɔda. Na Aberewa-asamanfo baakoe baa hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Akua, mɛtumi adi saa mmoa yi kotokuo?” Ananse tɔɔ ntade no, na mmoa no tee ne ti. Aberewa-asamanfo no kaa kyerɛ ne nua baako sɛ, “Ɔse mɛtumi adi bi.” Ɔno nso kaa sɛ, “Ɛnna di bi.” Na ɔdii no nyinaa, na ɔdae no (mmoa no) ase. Nanso berɛ a ɔdaasee no, wanka biara. Na Aberewa-Sika-Kɔkɔɔ no kaa kyerɛ ne nua sɛ, “Sɛ medaase no a, ɔnka biara.” (Nea edi kan no) Aberewa-Sika-Kɔkɔɔ no nua no kaa sɛ, “Pa ne dwinim.” Ɛnna ɔpaa no, pa! Na ne nsa kɔfaa hɔ. Ɔkaa kyerɛ ne nua sɛ, “Me nsa akɔfa hɔ.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Fa nea akɔsie no na mpɛ ne dwinim bio.” Ɛnna ɔfaa no na ɔpaa no, pa! na eyi nso, ɛkyere mu. Na Onyame-nkwa no ka kyerɛɛ ne nua sɛ, “Me nsa mmienu nyinaa akyeere mu.” Ɔkae sɛ, “Fa wo yafunu tɔ no so.” Ɔtɔɔ no so, na ne yafunu nso kyerɛɛ mu. Na Ananse bae na ɔkyekyere no, na ɔkae sɛ, “Kwasea, meanya wo, mɛfa wo akɔma Nyame (de kyerɛ no) na ɔmfa ne nsɛm nkyerɛ me.” Na ɔfa no kɔɔ fie. Na Ananse ka kyerɛɛ ne na, Ya Nsia (abofra a ɛtɔtɔ ne nsia), sɛ, “Sɔre, ma yɛnkɔ, efisɛ mɛfa wo ne Onyame-nkwafo no nkɔ ma Nyame (de kɔkyerɛ no) na ɔde ne nsɛm ama yɛn.” Ɔfaa hɔn kɔɔ baabi a Nyame wɔ hɔ. Ɔse, “Nyame, ɔha na Fairy wɔ, na me yere a mede kyerɛɛ wo no nso, ɔha na ɔwɔ.” Ɛnnɛ Nyame frɛɛ (ne mpanyimfo), Kontire ne Akwam mpanyimfo, Adonten, Gyase; Oyoko, Ankobea, ne Kyidom. Na ɔde asɛm no hyɛɛ wɔn anim, ɔse, “Ɔhene kɛse bebree aba, na wɔnnyɛ tumi sɛ wɔbɛtɔ Onyame anansesɛm no, nanso Kwaku Ananse, Kɔkɔro no, abetumi asua ne bo; Mmoboro, Namfo no, na m’anya fii ne hɔ; Mmoatia, Aberewa no, na m’anya fii ne hɔ; Osebo, Gyata no, na m’anya fii ne hɔ; Onini, Awɔden no, na m’anya fii ne hɔ; na Ananse ankasa ayɛ adwuma de ahyɛ so” de ne na kɔtwee hɔ; nsɛm nyinaa hyɛ ha.” Ɔse, “To ne ho ayɛ.” “Eee!” (wɔteɛ). Onyame se, “Kwaku Ananse, efie ne nnɛ ne mmaeɛ nyinaa, mefa me Onyame anansesɛm yi ma wo, kose! kose! kose! me nhyira, nhyira, nhyira. Yɛrenfrɛ wɔn Onyame anansesɛm bio, na mmom yɛbɛfrɛ wɔn Ananse-anansesɛm.”

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
