Sɛnea ɛyɛɛ koraa sɛ yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛgya abofra bi wɔ fie a obiara nni mu no

Wɔka sɛ abɔnten ne ne ba kɔdwee kuro ketewa bi, na wɔtena mu. Na abɔnten no bɔɔ mmoa, ɔbɔɔ, ɔbɔɔ, ɔbɔɔ. Mmoa biara na ɛwɔ hɔ. Ɛnnɛ, berɛ a ɔbɔɔ saa mmoa yi no, na ɔdi nam no, na ɔtwa wɔn ti, na ɔde gu hɔ; odaa baako pɛ na ɛyɛ mmoa ti a ɛyɛ dodo, ɛyɛ dodo te sɛ eyi. Mpɛn bi na asɛm bi firi yɛn nsam (yehwɛ sɛ asɛm bi wɔ a m'apae); saa abɔnten no, berɛ a ɔkɔɔ kwaa mu no, na ɔgya ne ba fiem hɔ daa. Da koro bi, berɛ a abofra no tena hɔ no, ɔhunuu mmoa fitaa (a ɛwɔ nsoroma); na ɛyɛ mpru. Ɔse, “Me nananom ba, maadwo.” Ɔse, “Wo papa wɔ he.” Ɔno (abofra no) se, “Papa akɔ kwaa mu.” Atikpam, berɛ a ɔrehwɛ hwɛ no, ɔhunuu mmoa no ti, na ɛpii na ɛwɔ hɔ. Ɔse, “Ah! asiane abɔ wo.” Ɔse, “Fa mmoa no ti nyinaa mma me nhunu.” Na ɔde ahobɔne no gyina hɔ na ɔrehyia ne ntade (a ɛyɛ dɔm dɔm te sɛ) f’re! f’re! f’re! Na abofra no faa mmoa no ti na ɔde hyiaa hɔ wɔ kwan tenten bi a ɛfi ha kɔ hɔ. Na Atikpam no se, “Frɛ wɔn din ma me nte.” Na ɔgyinaa abofra no kɔn ho, na ɔse, “Frɛ (wɔn din); eyi, mmoa bɛn na ɛyɛ?”

Ɔse (abaayewa no):

“Wei ne adowa ketewa no, Adowa, Adowa, Adowa, Kwadwo Berefi Adowa, Papa wumee Adowa yi, sɛ gun no yɛ pii, mehyia Papa, damirifa!” (Alas).

“Sɛnea mmoa tɛn yi te?”

“Wei ne Otwe, Duyker no, Duyker no, Duyker no (ne din ne), ɔyɛ-yɛ-yɛ-sɛ-ɔnyɛ-anigye-a-asɛm-no-ne-ɔbɔfo-no-asɛm. Agya gui saa Duyker yi, sɛ gun no yɛ pii, mehyɛ Agya anim, damirifa!”

“Sɛnea mmoa tɛn yi te?”

“Wei ne oyuo kɔkɔɔ no, Oyuo, Oyuo, Oyuo, Oyuo Kwabi, Agya bɔɔ saa Oyuo yi, sɛ gun no bɔ a, mehyia Agya, damirifa!”

“Sɛnea mmoa tɛn yi te?”

“Wei ne abedie kɔkɔɔ no, Abedie, Abedie, Abedie, Agya na ɔwoo saa Abedie yi, sɛ gun no yɛ pii, mehyia Agya, damirifa!”

“Sɛnea mmoa tɛn yi te?”

“Wei ne Otromo, Bongo no, Bongo no, Bongo no, Sakwa Bongo no, Agya gui Bongo wei, sɛ gun no yɛ dwo a, mehyɛ Agya no anim, damirifa!”

“Sɛnea mmoa tɛn yi te?”

“ Yeiyi Wansane, Ɔkraman, Ɔkraman, Ɔkraman, Mmomire Ɔkraman, Agya bɔɔ Ɔkraman yi, sɛ gun no bɔ a, mehyɛ Agya anim, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn ne eyi? ”

“ Eyi ne Kotoko, Ɔkraman, Ɔkraman, Ɔkraman, Gyanbibi, Ɔkraman, Agya bɔɔ Ɔkraman yi, sɛ gun no yɛ pii a, mehyia Agya, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn ne eyi? ”

“ Eyi ne Ekuo, Kwaku Dua, Kwaku Dua, Asempi, Kwaku Dua, Agya bɔɔ Kwaku Dua yi, sɛ gun no bɔ a, mehyɛ Agya anim, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn ne eyi? ”

“ Yeiyi ne Oforotie, Cob no, Cob no, Cob no, Pantan, Cob no, Agya gui Cob yi, sɛ gun no yɛ dwo a, mehyia Agya, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn ne eyi? ”

“ Yeiyi Osonson, Kwoa, Kwoa, Kwoa, Ofuntum Kwoa, Kwoa, Agya bɔɔ Kwoa yi, sɛ gun no bɔ a, mehyia Agya, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn ne eyi? ”

“ Yeinom ne Oko, Roan no, Roan no, Roan no, Agya gui Roan yi, sɛ gun no yɛ dwo a, mehyɛ Agya anim, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn ne eyi? ”

“ Eyi ne Aboa Kesie, Aboa Kesie, Aboa Kesie, Aboa Kesie, ’Sai, mfutuma-manno no ɔhene, Agya hwee Aboa Kesie yi, sɛ gun no yɛ pii, mehyɛ Agya anim, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn ne eyi? ”

“ Eyi ne Esono, Esono, Esono, Esono, Okronpa kɛseɛ a ɔnantew ankasa, nea ɔpira apɔm, Agya na ɔwui Esono yi, sɛ gun no yɛ pii a, mehyɛ Agya anim, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn ne eyi? ”

“ Eyi ne Susono, Hippo no, Hippo no, Hippo no, Agya bɔɔ Hippo yi, sɛ gun no yɛ dwo a, mehyia Agya, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn ne eyi? ”

“Eyi ne Ebie, Kutu-kutu-kutu, Kutu-kutu-kutu, Kutu-kutu-kutu, Papa wuiee Kutu-kutu-kutu yi, sɛ gun no yɛ pii, mehyia Papa, damirifa!”

“ Aboa bɛn ne eyi? ”

“Ɛyɛ Kokotee, Aberewa, Aberewa, Aberewa, Asamoa, Aberewa, Agya bɔɔ Aberewa yi, sɛ gun no yɛ pii a, mehyɛ Agya anim, damirifa!” “Sɛnea mmoa tɛn yi ase?”

Wei ne Ofosuo, nsuo-mmoa no, nsuo-mmoa no, nsuo-mmoa no, Agya bɔɔ nsuo-mmoa wei, sɛ gun no yɛ dɔm, mehyɛ Agya anim, _damirifa!_

Aboa bɛn ne eyi?

Wei ne Kwaduo, Yellow-backed-duyker, Yellow-backed-duyker, Yellow-backed duyker, Boapampa, Agya buee saa Yellow-backed-duyker yi, sɛ gun no yɛ pii, mehyia Agya, _damirifa!_

Aboa bɛn ne eyi?

Wei ne Apese, nkyene-nkyene-kɔkɔɔ, nkyene-nkyene-kɔkɔɔ, nkyene-nkyene-kɔkɔɔ, Agya bɔɔ wei, sɛ gun no yɛ pii, mehyia Agya, _damirifa!_

Aboa bɛn ne eyi?

Wei ne Ebuo, Colobus Monkey no, Colobus Monkey no, Colobus Monkey no, Ya, Colobus Monkey no, Agya bɔɔ saa Colobus Monkey yi, sɛ gun no yɛ dɔm, me hyɛ Agya anim, _damirifa!_

Na seesei ɔduru baabi a Nea-a-yɛ-fitaa-mframa-so no ti da hɔ no, na ɔse, "Na wei nso, anaa?" Ɔno (abofra no) se:

Ma Agya mba ansa kPa (me frɛ no), efisɛ eyi yɛ ayɛ defraud.

Ɔpuna no see sɛ, "Mete ase; merekɔ; ɔkyena mɛba bio, na sɛ wo Papa ba fie a, bisa no ne din." Papa no baa kwae fi, na ɔno (abofra no) see sɛ: "Amanehunu na mahu." Papa no bisaa sɛ: "Dɛn na asi?" Ɔno (abofra no) see sɛ: "Wogyaame me ha kɔɔ kwae, na Ɔpuna no baa ha, na ɔma me hyɛa mmoa nyinaa ti wɔ asɛm baako mu na mefrɛ wɔn din baako baako, kosi sɛ (yɛ) duu ne nnamfo ti no, na ɔse me mma meka ne din, nanso mekaa sɛ ɔmma wo aba mansa, efisɛ eyi yɛ ade a ɛha." Papa no see sɛ: "O, eyi nnyɛ hwee, yɛbɛhu dɛn na ɔkyena bɛsi." Na nokwaren, berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ asei sɛ wɔda adi (yeɛ, anɔpa anɔpa) no, Papa no kɔɔ kwae bio. Ɔpuna no baa ha bio na ɔmaa no (abofra no) hyɛa ti no wɔ asɛm baako mu bio, na ɔmaa ɔfrɛ mmoa no ti baako baako. Berɛ a, mprempren a ɛtɔ so mmienu no, ɔduu Ɔpuna no ti no, ɔsee sɛ:

Ma Agya mba ansa kPa (me frɛ no), efisɛ eyi yɛ ayɛ defraud.

Ɔpyɛm no see: "Ɛyɛ, nanso ɔkyena sɛ meba na woantɛm me no, mɛtwa wo ti nso." Abofra no see: "Mete!" Anwummerɛ bɛyɛɛ nwunu, na Agya no san ba fie a (abofra no) see: "Sɛ nnɛ yi—ɔkyena woantumi anka nea wopɛ sɛ woyɛ ho biribi a, mɛfi ha mɛkɔ na magya wo wɔ kuro yi mu." Agya no see: "Dɛn na ɛaseɛ bio?" Abofra no see: "Ɔpyɛm no ba baako a ɛtɔ so mmienu, na ɔse sɛ ɔkyena meanka a, nea ɔbɛyɛ me no bɛyɛ dodo sene nea mɛtumi adi (lit. adi)." Agya no see: "Ɛyɛ, mete." Ɛnna ɔkɔhwehwɛɛ nhwiren ne nnawa aduru a ɔbɛtumi afa akɔhuro ne gun no. Ɔhyɛɛ mu, na ɔtoo no hɔ. Sɛ nneɛma hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ wɔda adi a, ɔkɔhwee ne ho wɔ baabi. Ankyɛ koraa no, ɔhunuu Ɔpyɛm no sɛ ɔreba mpru mpru.

Bere a Aberewa no duu fie no, ɔne ɔyadeɛfoɔ no ba no kasaeɛ, ɔsee no sɛ, “Ɛnnɛ, nea ɛbɛsi no, ɛbɛsi.” Abofra no see no sɛ: “Ah, ɛyɛ efisɛ wunnim (biribiara).” Aberewa no bisaa no sɛ: “Dɛn na wokaeɛ, minteɛ?” Abofra no see no sɛ: “Waka kyerɛɛ me sɛ me nhyia (atifi hɔ), enti dɛn na metumi aka (ho)?” Aberewa no see no sɛ: “Kɔyɛ (adwuma no).” Abofra no faa mmoa no atifi a ɔhyiaa hɔ, na ɔka kyerɛɛ (ne papa) sɛ, “Ɛhe na mede (yei) bɛto?” Aberewa no bisaa no sɛ: “Dɛn na wokaeɛ?” Abofra no see no sɛ: “Mereka mmoa yi din kyerɛ me ho kɛkɛ.” Aberewa no kɔgyinaa abofra no kɔn ho na ɔsee no sɛ, “Kɔ so, na yei nso, mmoa bɛn na ɛyɛ?”

“Wei ne Otwe, Bush-buck, Bush-buck, Bush-buck, Agya bɔɔ saa Bush-buck yi, sɛ gun no yɛ dɔm, mehyia Agya, damirifa!”

Na ɔtoo mmoa nyinaa din saa ara, na ɔduu Aberewa no hɔ. Ɔno (Aberewa no) kaa sɛ: “Na wei nso, eh?” Ɔno (abofra no) kaa sɛ: “Wei ne Etwie, Aberewa, Aberewa, Aberewa, Agya Aberewa, Agya huu Aberewa yi, sɛ gun no yɛ pii, mehyia Agya, damirifa!”

Ɔpyim no see: “O, nanso amanehunu aba wo so; me nso, me nso nso baako ayɛ, na ɛka baako.” Na ɔsan kɔɔ hɔ kɔbisaa no bio, na abofra no san kaa no, na ɔpyim no see: “Me nso mmienu nyinaa ayɛ seesei, na me kuku ketewa a me sumsum wɔ mu a mede agyigyei me anim no nko ara na ɛka.” Ɛna Agya no bɔɔ foforo, na ɔfrɛɛ ne ba no na ɔsee no, “Yɛ kɔ mma mmu,” na abofra no tutu kɔgyina mmu. Na Ɔpyim no bisaa: “Wokɔ he?” Na abofra no see: “Mepue kɔmmu kakra.” Ɔpyim no see: “Me nananom ba, wo wɔ nyansa.” Na ɔpyim no see: “Na eyi nso, ne din de dɛn?” Ɔno (abofra no) see:

“Wei ne Etwie, Aberewa, Aberewa, Aberewa, Agya Aberewa, Agya hwee Aberewa yi, sɛ gun no yɛ pii, mehyia Agya, damirifa!”

Ne ano gyina hɔ, na Leopard no rehyɛ nkuran sɛ ɔbɛtumi atumi no, ɛnna abofra no Agya maa gun no yii dɔm, Tomm! Na Leopard no tɔe hɔ, na ne tɔe nneɛma no yɛɛ, Tim! Na Agya bae na ɔtwaa Leopard no nam no na ɔtowaa no hɔ nyinaa.

Ɛno nti na mpanyimfo ka sɛ yɛnnka abofra nkoko wɔ fie mu, efisɛ abɔdeɛfoɔ no yɛɛ saa, na ɔdodo koraa sɛ ɔbɛma gyata no kyere ne ba.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
