Sɛnea ɛyɛɛ koraa sɛ yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛgya abofra bi wɔ fie a obiara nni mu no

Wɔka sɛ abɔnten ne ne ba kɔdwee na wɔyɛɛ kuro ketewa bi, na wɔtena mu. Na abɔnten no bɔɔ mmoa, ɔbɔɔ, ɔbɔɔ, ɔbɔɔ. Mmoa biara na ɛwɔ hɔ. Enti, berɛ a ɔbɔɔ saa mmoa yi no, na ɔdi nam no, na ɔtwa wɔn ti, na ɔde gu hɔ; odaa baako pɛ na ɛyɛ mmoa ti a ɛyɛ dodo, sɛdeɛ eyi te. Ɛnnɛ, mpɛn bi asɛm kɔnka (yehwɛ sɛ, wáwo bi a meka mma); ɔyadeɛ no, berɛ a ɔkɔɔ kwaa mu no, ɔgyaa ne ba wɔ fie daa. Da koro bi, berɛ a ɔba no tena hɔ no, ɔhunuu ɔyereefo (nkyekyɛmu) no; na ɛyɛɛ mprempren. Ɔse, “Me nananom ba, maadwo.” Ɔse, “Wo papa wɔ he.” Ɔno (abofra no) se, “Papa akɔ kwaa.” Aberewa no, berɛ a ɔrehwɛ hwɛ no, ɔhunuu mmoa no ti, na ɛpii wɔ hɔ. Ɔse, “Ah! asiane aba wo so.” Ɔse, “Fa mmoa no ti nyinaa bra ma me nhu.” Na ɔdeɛ ɔyɛ ɔyerefo no gyina hɔ na ɔrehyia ne ntade (deɛ ɛyɛ dwenene sɛ) f’re! f’re! f’re! Na aba no tooku mmoa no ti na ɔde hyɛɛ hɔ wɔ kwan tenten bi a ɛfi ha kɔ hɔ. Na Aberewa no see, “Frɛ wɔn din ma me nte.” Na ɔgyina aba no kɔn ho, na ɔsee, “Frɛ (wɔn din); eyi, mmoa bɛn ne eyi?”

Ɔse (abaayewa no):

“Wei ne adowa ketewa no, Adowa, Adowa, Adowa, Kwadwo Berefi Adowa, Papa wumee Adowa yi, sɛ gun no yɛ pii, mehyia Papa, damirifa!” (Alas).

“Sɛnea mmoa tɛn yi te?”

“Wei ne Otwe, Duyker no, Duyker no, Duyker no (ne din ne), ɔyɛ-yɛ-yɛ-sɛ-ɔnyɛ-anigye-a-asɛm-no-ne-ɔbɔfo-no-asɛm. Agya gui saa Duyker yi, sɛ gun no yɛ pii, mehyɛ Agya anim, damirifa!”

“Sɛnea mmoa tɛn yi te?”

“Wei ne oyuo kɔkɔɔ no, Oyuo, Oyuo, Oyuo, Oyuo Kwabi, Agya bɔɔ saa Oyuo yi, sɛ gun no bɔ a, mehyia Agya, damirifa!”

“Sɛnea mmoa tɛn yi te?”

“Wei ne abedie kɔkɔɔ no, Abedie, Abedie, Abedie, Agya na ɔwoo saa Abedie yi, sɛ gun no yɛ pii, mehyia Agya, damirifa!”

“Sɛnea mmoa tɛn yi te?”

“Wei ne Otromo, Bongo no, Bongo no, Bongo no, Sakwa Bongo no, Agya gui Bongo wei, sɛ gun no yɛ dwo a, mehyɛ Agya no anim, damirifa!”

“Sɛnea mmoa tɛn yi te?”

“ Yeiyi Wansane, Ɔkraman, Ɔkraman, Ɔkraman, Mmomire Ɔkraman, Agya bɔɔ Ɔkraman yi, sɛ gun no bɔ a, mehyɛ Agya anim, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn ne eyi? ”

“ Eyi ne Kotoko, Ɔkraman, Ɔkraman, Ɔkraman, Gyanbibi, Ɔkraman, Agya bɔɔ Ɔkraman yi, sɛ gun no yɛ pii a, mehyia Agya, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn ne eyi? ”

“ Eyi ne Ekuo, Kwaku Dua, Kwaku Dua, Asempi, Kwaku Dua, Agya bɔɔ Kwaku Dua yi, sɛ gun no bɔ a, mehyɛ Agya anim, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn ne eyi? ”

“ Yeiyi ne Oforotie, Cob no, Cob no, Cob no, Pantan, Cob no, Agya gui Cob yi, sɛ gun no yɛ dwo a, mehyia Agya, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn ne eyi? ”

“ Yeiyi Osonson, Kwoa, Kwoa, Kwoa, Ofuntum Kwoa, Kwoa, Agya bɔɔ Kwoa yi, sɛ gun no bɔ a, mehyia Agya, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn ne eyi? ”

“ Yeinom ne Oko, Roan no, Roan no, Roan no, Agya gui Roan yi, sɛ gun no yɛ dwo a, mehyɛ Agya anim, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn ne eyi? ”

“ Eyi ne Aboa Kesie, Aboa Kesie, Aboa Kesie, Aboa Kesie, ’Sai, mfutuma-manno no ɔhene, Agya hwee Aboa Kesie yi, sɛ gun no yɛ pii, mehyɛ Agya anim, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn ne eyi? ”

“ Eyi ne Esono, Esono, Esono, Esono, Okronpa kɛseɛ a ɔnantew ankasa, nea ɔpira apɔm, Agya na ɔwui Esono yi, sɛ gun no yɛ pii a, mehyɛ Agya anim, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn ne eyi? ”

“ Eyi ne Susono, Hippo no, Hippo no, Hippo no, Agya bɔɔ Hippo yi, sɛ gun no yɛ dɔm a, mehyia Agya, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn ne eyi? ”

“Eyi ne Ebie, Kutu-kutu-kutu, Kutu-kutu-kutu, Kutu-kutu-kutu, Papa woyɛɛ saa Kutu-kutu-kutu yi, sɛ gun no yɛ pii, mehyɛ Papa anim, damirifa!”

“ Aboa bɛn ne eyi? ”

“Ɛyɛ Kokotee, Aberewa, Aberewa, Aberewa, Asamoa, Aberewa, Agya bɔɔ Aberewa yi, sɛ gun no yɛ pii a, mehyɛ Agya anim, damirifa!” “Sɛnea mmoa tɛn yi ase?”

Wei ne Ofosuo, nsuo-mmoa no, nsuo-mmoa no, nsuo-mmoa no, Agya hwee nsuo-mmoa wei, sɛ gun no yɛ dwo, mehyɛ Agya anim, _damirifa!_

Aboa bɛn ne eyi?

Wei ne Kwaduo, Yellow-backed-duyker, Yellow-backed-duyker, Yellow-backed duyker, Boapampa, Agya buee saa Yellow-backed-duyker yi, sɛ gun no yɛ pii, mehyia Agya, _damirifa!_

Aboa bɛn ne eyi?

Wei ne Apese, nkyene-nkyene-kɔkɔɔ, nkyene-nkyene-kɔkɔɔ, nkyene-nkyene-kɔkɔɔ, Agya bɔɔ wei, sɛ gun no yɛ pii, mehyia Agya, _damirifa!_

Aboa bɛn ne eyi?

Wei ne Ebuo, Colobus Monkey no, Colobus Monkey no, Colobus Monkey no, Ya, Colobus Monkey no, Agya bɔɔ saa Colobus Monkey yi, sɛ gun no yɛ dɔm, me hyɛ Agya anim, _damirifa!_

Na seesei ɔduru baabi a Nea-a-yɛ-fitaa-mframa-so no ti da hɔ no, na ɔse, "Na wei nso, anaa?" Ɔno (abofra no) se:

Ma Agya mba ansa kPa (me frɛ no), efisɛ eyi yɛ ayɛ defraud.

Ɔpyim no see sɛ, "Mete ase; merekɔ; ɔkyena mɛba bio, na sɛ wo Papa ba fie a, bisa no ne din." Papa no baa kwae fi, na ɔno (abofra no) see sɛ: "Amanehunu na mahu." Papa no bisaa sɛ: "Dɛn na ɛrekɔ so?" Ɔno (abofra no) see sɛ: "Wogyaame ha kɔɔ kwae, na Ɔpyim no baa ha, na ɔma me hyɛa mmoa nyinaa ti wɔ asɛm baako mu na n frɛ wɔn baako baako, kosi sɛ (yɛn) dodo ne ne nnamfo deɛ, na ɔse sɛ mɛka ne din, nanso mekaa sɛ ɔmma wo nnyina hɔ ansa, efisɛ eyi yɛ ade a ɛha hwee." Agya no see: "O, eyi nnyɛ hwee, yɛbɛhwɛ nea ɛbɛsi kɔnkyerɛga ɔkyena." Na nokwaren, berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ asei sɛ wɔda adi (yeɛ, anɔpa anɔpa) Agya no kɔɔ kwaa mu. Ɔpyene no baa bio na ɔmaa ɔba no hyɛɛ atiw no akyekyere wɔ hɔ bio, na ɔmaa ɔfrɛa mmoa no atiw no baako baako. Berɛ a ɔduu ɔpyene no tiw no a twene, ɔsee:

Ma Agya mba ansa kPa (me frɛ no), efisɛ eyi yɛ ayɛ defraud.

Ɔbrɔnɔ no seei: "Ɛyɛ, nanso ɔkyena sɛ meba na woantɛm me no, mɛtwa wo ti nso." Abofra no seei: "Mete!" Anwummerɛ bɛyɛɛ nwunu, na Agya no san baa fie na (abofra no) seei: "Sɛ nnɛ mpo—ɔkyena sɛ woyɛ biribi na wuhunu nea wopɛ sɛ woyɛ ho no, a mɛkɔ na magya wo fiha wɔ kuro yi mu." Agya no seei: "Dɛn ne" "asɛm no bio?" Abofra no see: "Ɔbrɔfo no baa mprempren, na ɔse sɛ ɔkyena me anka mɛka bi, a nea ɔbɛyɛ me no bɛyɛ denten paa (lit. bɛyɛ kɛse sene nea metumi adi)." Agya no see: "Ɛyɛ, meate." Ɛnna ɔkɔyɛɛ ne ho sɛ ɔrekɔsere nhwiren ne nnam dwumadie, na ɔde asesa ne gun no. Ɔhyɛɛ mu, na ɔtoo no hɔ. Berɛ a anopa kɔsi sɛ eyɛ kyerɛ, ɔkɔhwee baabi. Ankyɛ koraa no, ɔhunuu sɛ Aberewa no reba ntɛm.

Bere a Aberewa no duu fie no, ɔne ɔbɔadeɛfoɔ no ba no kasae, ɔsee no sɛ, “Ɛnnɛ, nea ɛbɛsi no, ɛbɛsi.” Abofra no see no sɛ: “Ah, ɛyɛ efisɛ wunnim (biribiara pɛpɛɛpɛ).” Aberewa no bisaa no sɛ: “Dɛn na wokae no, minte?” Abofra no see no sɛ: “Waka kyerɛɛ me sɛ mehyɛ (atifi no) hɔ, enti dɛn na metumi aka (ho)?” Aberewa no see no sɛ: “Kɔyɛ (adwuma no).” Abofra no nso fa mmoa no ti no na ɔpɛpɛɛ hɔ wɔ kwan baako mu, na ɔka kyerɛ ne papa (wɔ ne ho), “Ɛhe na mɛde (eyiha) bɛto?” Gyata no kaa sɛ: “Dɛn na woka?” Abofra no kaa sɛ: “Mereka kyerɛ me ho mmoa yi din.” Gyata no kɔgyinaa abofra no kɔn ho na ɔkaa sɛ, “Kɔ so, na eyi nso, mmoa bɛn na ɛyɛ?”

“Wei ne Otwe, Bush-buck, Bush-buck, Bush-buck, Agya bɔɔ saa Bush-buck yi, sɛ gun no yɛ dɔm, mehyia Agya, damirifa!”

Na ɔtoo mmoa nyinaa din saa ara, na ɔduu Aberewa no hɔ. Ɔno (Aberewa no) kaa sɛ: “Na wei nso, eh?” Ɔno (abofra no) kaa sɛ: “Wei ne Etwie, Aberewa, Aberewa, Aberewa, Agya Aberewa, Agya huu Aberewa yi, sɛ gun no yɛ pii, mehyia Agya, damirifa!”

Ɔpyim no see: “O, nanso woahu amane; me nso, me nso nso baako na ako te, na ɛka kɔkɔɔ baako nso wɔ hɔ.” Na ɔsan kɔɔ hɔ kɔbisaa no bio, na abofra no san kaa no, na ɔ (Ɔpyim no) see: “Me nso mmienu nyinaa ate seesei, na me kuku nketewa a me sumsum wɔ mu a mede atwa me anim no nko ara na ɛka.” Ɛnna Agya no bɔɔ foforo, na ɔfrɛɛ ne ba na ɔsee no, “Fa wo ho” “yi kɔ mfim,” na abaayewa no bɔɔ mmɔden kɔɔ mfim. Na Aberewa no kaa sɛ: “Wokɔ he?” Na abaayewa no kaa sɛ: “Mepɛ sɛ meyi me ho kɔ mfim kakra.” Aberewa no kaa sɛ: “Me nananom, wo w’adwene.” Na ɔno (Aberewa no) kaa sɛ: “Na eyi nso, ne din de sɛn?” Ɔno (abaayewa no) kaa sɛ:

“Wei ne Etwie, Aberewa, Aberewa, Aberewa, Agya Aberewa, Agya hwee Aberewa yi, sɛ gun no yɛ pii, mehyia Agya, damirifa!”

Ne ano gyina hɔ, na Leopard no rehyɛ nkuran sɛ ɔbɛtumi atumi no, ɛnna abofra no Agya maa gun no yii dɔm, Tomm! Na Leopard no tɔe hɔ, na ne tɔe nneɛma no yɛɛ, Tim! Na Agya bae na ɔtwaa Leopard no nam no na ɔtowaa no hɔ nyinaa.

Ɛno nti na mpanyimfo ka sɛ yɛnnka abofra nkoko wɔ fie mu, efisɛ abɔdeɛfoɔ no yɛɛ saa, na ɔdodo koraa sɛ ɔbɛma gyata no kyere ne ba.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
