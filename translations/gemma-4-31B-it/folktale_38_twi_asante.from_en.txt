Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

MPANYIMFO KA SE, "SƐ ƐYƐ WO ABUSua, ANAA WO KUNKUNI, ANAA OBIARA A ƆWƆ ADWUMA A ƆREYE, SƐ ƆBƆ WO MPAE A, BOA NO"

ANANSE, Kwebla, kaa kyerɛɛ Aso (ne yere) sɛ, "Ma yɛnkɔ na yɛayɛ abɔdeɛ." Aso see, "Me renkɔ." Ananse kɔɔ hɔ kɔpɛɛ fam no. Bere a ɔbaaa no, ɔkaa kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nokware paa sɛ wo renkɔ (reboa me)?" Aso hyɛɛ no ase sɛ, "Obaa ayɛ ayie sɛ ɔbɛpɛ asase." Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɔkyena ma yɛnkɔ nna yɛmmi garden weeds no." Aso kaa sɛ, "Obaa ayɛ ayie sɛ ɔbɛmmi garden weeds, na me nso, mɛnkɔ na gya mmi me?" Enti Ananse kɔeɛ na ɔbaaa. Da a ɛdi so no, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ no, ɔseɛ, "Aso, mesrɛ wo, ma yɛnkɔ bɔm na yɛatua nneɛma." Ɔseɛ, "Miseɛ, mɛnkɔ." Ananse seɛ, "Meahu amane." Ɛnna ɔkɔtoo nneɛma no nyinaa yie. Afei wɔwɔ hɔ, na nnɔbae no bae. Da koro bi Aso kɔɔ afuo no hɔ. Ɔbaa baa na ɔkaa kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, "Kwaku, nnɔbae ayɛ mmerɛ wɔ abɔnten no mu, na berɛ bɛyɛ den sɛ yɛbɛkɔ hɔ (akɔbo won)?" Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ho! "deɛ ɔ Mohammedan no kae no, mentumi nte ase koraa." Aso kaa sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn ni; ka no paapaa na matumi ate wo ase." Ananse kaa sɛ, "Merekɔ na mɛsan mba." Ananse kɔɔ abɔdeɛ no mu, na ɔgyina hɔtwe kɛse, na ɔkaa (ne ho) sɛ, "Me, Kwaku Kwebra, me wɔ ha, na mɛtena ase na matumi ama obi a ɔyɛ ɔbaa pɛ, ama me ayɛ kwasea?" Ananse took knife, axe, ne hoe, na ɔtoo awu yie bi. Ɔtookaa afuo mu nnuaba nyinaa hyɛɛ mu, na ɔsan kɔɔ fie. Ɔsee, "Aso, bra ha, na matumi aka amanneɛ no akyerɛ wo." Aso bae. Ɔse, "Mekɔɔ sɛ mɛbisa Mohammedan bi fa me nkyirɛmu ho; ɔse nnawɔtwe nyɔnnu pɛ a ɛkyerɛ me ansa na mɛwu, enti menkɔ afuo bio, na mo nso monnkɔ hɔ bio, na sɛ mewu a, hɔ na mobɛkɔ akwase me. Mepere awu kɛse bi wɔ hɔ, na ɛhɔ na mobɛkwase me." Nnawɔtwe nyɔnnu no baa; Ananse yɛɛ sɛ ɔwui. Nnipa no bɔɔ Kwebla so na wɔkɔtoo no hɔ ne afuo mu. Wɔsan kɔɔ fie na wɔyɛɛ ayie kɛse. Aso nso yɛɛ n'ani-atwa dwumadie. Berɛ a wɔredi n'ayie da otɔnkron no, Aso see, "Ah, ma me nkɔnhwɛ me kunu afuo no." Aso kɔe; ɛhɔ na afuo no hyɛe, dadwa! Aso see sɛ, "O, kuraa a me kunu yɛɛ a m'ani gyinaeɛ ho (ama me aduane), nnipa bi aba na wɔadi ne mu biara." Ɛnna ɔsuu na ɔde nkasɛɛ (too wɔn so), ɔse, " (Mifrɛ) Kwaku Ananse honhom, adeɛ nyinaa a wogyaeɛ ama me wɔ wo afuo mu no, nnipa aba na wɔadi."

Kwaku Ananse yɛɛ ne nne den (na ɔtoo dwom):

Gyae (nsuo)! Deɛ ɛyɛ me de pɛ nea madi!

Gyae (nsuhu), Aso taboos kura mmoa.

Gyae (nsuhu), nea ɛyɛ me de pɛ nea madi!

Gyae (nsuhu), Aso taboos mma (afuo mmofra no nnɔ). Gyae (nsuhu), nea ɛyɛ me de pɛ nea madi!

Gyae (nsuhu), Aso taboos planting.

Gyae (nsuhu), nea ɛyɛ me de pɛ nea madi!

Ɛno akyi no, Aso gyinaa hɔ, na ɔhui. Ɔkɔɔ fie kɔfrɛɛ Adanko, ɔKraman no, sɛ ɔmmpue na wakɔte nneɛma bi a ɛwɔ ne kunu afuo hɔ. ɔKraman ne no hyɛɛ aseɛ. Aso duruu afuo hɔ, na ɔyɛɛ ne nne bio na ɔsuu:

Me kunu yɛ Kwaku, Kweku Ananse, na ɔwui na ɔgyaa me ne afuo, na nnipa bae na wɔdidiu mu aduane nyinaa.

Kwaku Ananse sunsum, me twaa wo anim, me srɛ wo, gyira wɔn ma me.

Kwaku Ananse yɛɛ ne nne den:

Gyae (nsu), nea ɛyɛ me de pɛ nea madi!

Gyae (nsuhu), Aso taboos kura mmoa.

Gyae (nsuhu), nea ɛyɛ me de pɛ nea madi!

Gyae (nsuhu), Aso taboos mma (afuo mmoa).

Gyae (nsuhu), nea ɛyɛ me de nea madi!

Gyae (nsuhu), Aso taboos planting.

Gyae (nsuhu), me adidi ne me de pɛ; gyae (nsuhu)!

Ɛfiri sɛ Bɔfoe bɔɔ (nsɛm no), woahu obaa bi a onnyɛ ne kunu mmoa?

Gyae (nsuhu), me adidi ne me de pɛ; gyae (nsuhu)!

Ɔkra no see sɛ, "Mate biakoye, ma yɛnkɔ fie na matumi akyerɛ mo nea ɛfa ho." Ɔne Aso kɔɔ fie. Hare see, "Ananse see, ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ, 'Ma yɛnkɔ nsɔre afuo'; wo kaa sɛ wonkɔ; 'Ma yɛnkɔ sɔre (nwunu a ɛyɛ hye no)'; na wo kaa sɛ wonkɔ; 'Ma yɛnkɔ dua'; na wo kaa sɛ wonkɔ; enti wubetumi agyegye su, efisɛ ɔredi ne dea pɛ, efisɛ, firi berɛ a Bɔfo bɔɔ wiase no, wammahu obaa bi a onnyɛne ne kunu mmoa; enti gyina su no." (ɔse), efisɛ nea ɛyɛ ne dea pɛn nea ɔdidi.) Ɛno nti na yɛse, "Sɛ obiara yɛ adwuma bi a na ɔbisa wo mmoa, boa no."

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
