Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

MPANYIMFO KA SE, "SƐ ƐYƐ WO ABUSua, ANAA WO KUNKUNI, ANAA OBIARA A ƆWƆ ADWUMA A ƆREYE, SƐ ƆBISA WO MMOA A, BOA NO"

ANANSE, Kwebia no, kaa kyerɛɛ Aso (ne yere) sɛ, "Ma yɛnkɔ na yɛayɛ abɔdeɛ." Aso kaa sɛ, "Me renkɔ." Ananse kɔɔ hɔ kɔtwee asase no. Berɛ a ɔbaaa no, ɔkaa kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nokware sɛ wo renkɔ (reboa me)?" Aso hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, "Ɔbaa nni kwan sɛ ɔtwee asase." Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɔkyena ma yɛnkɔ na yɛmmpɔ abɔdeɛ no mmoa." Aso kaa sɛ, "Ɔbaa nni kwan sɛ ɔpɔ abɔdeɛ mmoa, na me nso, mɛnkɔ na gya mpɔ me?" Enti Ananse kɔe na ɔbaaa. Da a ɛdi so no, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Aso, me srɛ wo, ma yɛnkɔ bɔm na yɛatua nneɛma." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mise sɛ, me renkɔ." Ananse kaa sɛ, "Amampa a meahu." Afei ɔkɔe na ɔtuu biribiara fɛfɛɛfɛ. Afei wɔwɔ hɔ, na nnuabaeɛ no bae. Da koro bi Aso kɔe afarm no hɔ. Ɔbaaa na ɔkaa kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, "Kwaku, nnuabaeɛ aba abɔdeɛ no mu, na berɛ bɛba hene na yɛnkɔ hɔ (akɔbo bi)? " Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ho! nea Mohammedan ɔno se no, me ntumi nte ase koraa." Aso kaa sɛ, "Hwan ne no; ka no paapaa na matumi ate wo ase." Ananse kaa sɛ, "Merekɔ na mɛba." Ananse kɔe abɔdeɛ no hɔ, na ɔgyina hɔtwe, na ɔkaa (ne ho) sɛ, "Me, Kwaku Kwebia, me wɔ ha, na mɛtena ase na mampɛ sɛ ɔbaa koro pɛ, nka me yɛ kwasea?" Ananse faa cuter, apɔm, ne twayie, na ɔtwee awu kɛse bi. Ɔfaa afarm no mu nnuabaeɛ nyinaa hyɛɛ mu, na ɔsan kɔɔ fie. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Aso, bra ha, na makyerɛ wo amanneɛ." Aso bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mekɔkɔtɔe ne Mohammedan bi ho fa me gyidi ho; ɔse nnawɔtwe nyɔnso na ɛwɔ me nkyɛn ansa na mewe, enti me renkɔ afarm no mu bio, na wo nso wo renkɔ hɔ bio, na berɛ a mewe no, hɔ na wobɛkɔ ayi me. Matwee awu kɛse bi hɔ, na ɛhɔ na wobɛyi me." Nnawɔtwe nyɔnso no nyinii; Ananse yɛɛ sɛ ɔwui. Nnipa no bɔɔ Kwebia no so kɔe ayii no hɔ ne abɔdeɛ no mu. Wɔsan kɔɔ fie na wɔyɛɛ ayie fɛfɛɛfɛ. Aso nso, yɛɛ nnuabaeɛ a ɔyere a ne kunu awu yɛ. Berɛ a wɔreyɛ nnawɔtwe da ayie no, Aso kaa sɛ, "Ah, ma me nkɔ nhwɛ me kunu abɔdeɛ no." Aso kɔe; abɔdeɛ no da hɔ, dadwa! Aso see sɛ, "O, kuraa a me kunu yɛɛ a m'ani gyinaeɛ ho (ama me aduane), nnipa bi aba na wɔadi ne mu biara." Ɛnna ɔsuu na ɔde nkasɛɛ (too wɔn so), ɔse, " (Mifrɛ) Kwaku Ananse honhom, adeɛ nyinaa a wogyaeɛ ama me wɔ wo afuo mu no, nnipa aba na wɔadi."

Kwaku Ananse yɛɛ ne nne den (na ɔtoo dwom):

Gyae (nsuo)! Deɛ ɛyɛ me de pɛ nea madi!

Gyae (nsuhu), Aso taboos kura mmoa.

Gyae (nsuhu), nea ɛyɛ me de pɛ nea madi!

Gyae (nsuhu), Aso taboos mma (afuo mmofra no nnɔ). Gyae (nsuhu), nea ɛyɛ me de pɛ nea madi!

Gyae (nsuhu), Aso taboos planting.

Gyae (nsuhu), me adidi ne me de pɛ!

Ɛno akyi no, Aso gyinaa hɔ, na ɔhui. Ɔkɔɔ fie kɔfrɛɛ Adanko, ɔKraman no, sɛ ɔmmpue na wakɔte nneɛma bi a ɛwɔ ne kunu afuo hɔ. ɔKraman ne no hyɛɛ aseɛ. Aso duruu afuo hɔ, na ɔyɛɛ ne nne bio na ɔsuu:

Me kunu yɛ Kwaku, Kweku Ananse, na ɔwui na ɔgyaa me ne afuo, na nnipa bae na wɔdidiu mu aduane nyinaa.

Kwaku Ananse sunsum, me twaa wo anim, me srɛ wo, gyira wɔn ma me.

Kwaku Ananse yɛɛ ne nne den:

Gyae (nsu), nea ɛyɛ me de pɛ nea madi!

Gyae (nsuhu), Aso taboos kura mmoa.

Gyae (nsuhu), nea ɛyɛ me de pɛ nea madi!

Gyae (nsuhu), Aso taboos mma (afuo mmofra).

Gyae (nsuhu), nea ɛyɛ me de nea madi!

Gyae (nsuhu), Aso taboos planting.

Gyae (nsuhu), me adidi ne me de pɛ; gyae (nsuhu)!

Ɛfiri sɛ Bɔfoe bɔɔ (nsɛm no), woahu obaa bi a onnyɛ ne kunu mmoa?

Gyae (nsuhu), me adidi ne me de pɛ; gyae (nsuhu)!

Ɔkra no seei sɛ, "Mate biara, ma yɛnkɔ fie na matumi akyerɛ wo nea ɛkɔn so." Ɔne Aso kɔree fie. Ɔkra no seei sɛ, "Ananse seei sɛ, ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ, 'Ma yɛnkɔ nsɔre afuo'; wo seei sɛ wo renkɔ; 'Ma yɛnkɔ hyɛ (mmoa hɔ) gyigye'; na wo seei sɛ wo renkɔ; 'Ma yɛnkɔ dua'; na wo seei sɛ wo renkɔ; enti wo betumi agyegye su, efisɛ ɔrewe (nea) ne nneɛma pɛ, efisɛ, efisɛ Bɔfo no bɔɔ wiase no, wammahu obaa bi a onnyɛne ne kunu mmoa; enti gyae awuwa (ɔse), efisɛ ɔrewe nea na ɛyɛ ne dea nyinaa." Ɛno nti na yɛse, "Sɛ obiara yɛ adwuma bi na ɔsrɛ wo, boa no."

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ bam, na ma bi mma me.
