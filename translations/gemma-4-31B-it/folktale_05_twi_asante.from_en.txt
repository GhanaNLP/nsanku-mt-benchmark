Sɛnea ɛyɛɛ koraa sɛ ɔkɔkɔɔ no tena mmoa nnuane ne nnuane mu daa

Na obiara bɛyɛɛ Ɔkɔkɔkɔ ne Akɔkɔ, na Akɔkɔ no see, “Ma yɛnkɔ ntwa atumfoɔ kyerɛ na yɛnno nso yɛno.” Wɔn bɔɔ mmɔden kɔɔ kwae, na wɔn hyiaa ɔdan tree bi. Teew nea Ɔkɔkɔ no huu ɔdan tree no, ɔsee, “Wei yɛ me dea, efisɛ, me nso, meyɛ panyin, enti mɛtwa saa kɛseɛ yi.” Akɔkɔ no see, “Mete.” Akɔkɔ no nso, berɛ a ɔrehwɛ hwɛ no, ɔhuu “Cedar” tree bi a ɛgyina hɔ, ɔkɔtwaa no nso. Wɔn nyiram mmienu no twayɛɛ atumfoɔ. Berɛ a Ɔkɔkɔ no bɔɔ ne atum no, ɛyɛɛ saa ara:

Futuru! Futuru! Meanya me dea ntɛm!

Kwakye Gyaene! Meanya me dea ntɛm!

Berɛ a Akɔkɔdua no bɔɔ ne nneɛma no, ɛyɛɛ sɛ saa:

Akɔkɔdua, Kwa! Kwa!

Akɔkɔdua, Kwa! Kwa!

Wɔkɔɔ so twaa dwom, na Nyame tee, na ɔsommaa Ɔkɔkɔ (ma ɔnkɔ frɛ wɔn); na Ɔkɔkɔ bae, (na ɔfrɛɛ wɔn) "Ago!" Na wɔgyee no ase sɛ, "Ame!" Ɔsee, "Nyame se wate afahyɛ dwom bi ha, enti ɛsɛ sɛ mobring no aba na motwa ama ɔnte." Atirika no kɔɔ Nyame anim; na wɔtwaa dwom, wɔtwaa, wɔtwaa, na Nyame see, "Mate sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ; dwom aenu no yɛ dɛ, enti mema mo (biara) ade bi." Ɛnna Nyame kɔfaa kootuo mmienu na ɔde bɛtoo hɔ, baako ne ne nkyɛn yɛ fɛ paa; nanso mmoa ne nkontomire hyɛɛ mu kɔ mmu; ɔfoforo no nso, ne nkyɛn yɛ mmerɛ paa; nanso sika kɔkɔɔ hyɛɛ mu kɔ mmu. Nyame kasae, (see), "Mo mmienu biara mma mo nnyɛ mmoa na mfa mo de." Ɔkɔkɔ-kɔkɔ (Vulture) see, "Me ne me nua panin, enti ma me nkɔ nfa baako akyerɛ." Berɛ a ɔkɔe no, ɔfaa eyi a ne nkyɛn yɛ fɛ paa no, na Ɔkɔkɔ-tuntum (Crow) nso faa eyi a ne nkyɛn yɛ mmerɛ no. Wɔfaa wɔn na wɔkɔɔ wɔn akuraa mu. Berɛ a wɔkɔpuee wɔn no, Ɔkɔkɔ-kɔkɔ, berɛ a ɔhwɛɛ ne nkyɛn mu no, ɔhunuu nkontomire. Ɔkɔkɔ-tuntum deɛ, berɛ a ɔhwɛɛ ne nkyɛn mu no, ɔhunuu sika kɔkɔɔ a ɛhyerɛn kɔkɔɔ paa. Ankyɛ koraa na abirikyere bi bae na ɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔkɔkɔ-kɔkɔ (see), "Wansom me ba sɛ wo mpanin-maa awu." Ɔno (Ɔkɔkɔ-kɔkɔ) see, "Ɔkɔkɔ-tuntum, ma me sika kɔkɔɔ baako na makye me nsa mfa nkɔ ayie no." Ɔfaa sika no na ɔkyee no, na ɔkɔɔ ayie no. Berɛ a ɔduu hɔ no, ɔsee ɛsɛ sɛ ɔkɔ yɛ ne ho mmuae; ɔtwɛɛ, nanso berɛ a ɔrepue no, sika no tɔe, na ɛyi gyee. Ɔsee, "O, Ɔkɔkɔ-tuntum ahodee ayera, na mɛsan nkɔ fie sɛn?" Enti ɔtwɛɛ into nkontomire no mu na ɔhwehwɛɛ (sika no). Ɛno nti na wubutu Ɔkɔkɔ-kɔkɔ, dabiara ɔrekyekye, ɔrekyekye, wɔ asɔre hɔ, efisɛ ɔrehwɛ Ɔkɔkɔ-tuntum sika no na ɔde ama no.

Ɛno nti na Mpanyimfo no se, "Anifere nyɛ eyie."

Me asɛm yi a meaka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, obiara betumi agye nea ɛyɛ nokware, na na akyerɛ no nso, mobɛtumi ayɛ me ayɛyie (sɛ meaka no).
