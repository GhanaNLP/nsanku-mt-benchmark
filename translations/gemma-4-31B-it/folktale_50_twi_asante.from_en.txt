Yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, yɛmpɛ sɛ yɛka kyerɛ wo, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNE ABOSOM, NYAME NTONA NO, BAa WIASƐM MU

Obaa bi wɔ hɔ; ɔwoo mma dusaa; na da biara sɛ ɔsɔre kɔnoa aduane a, mma no nnwum no nyinaa. Ne maame annya bi a ɔbɛdi. Enti ɔsuman mmuaei yiye, na ɔkɔɔ afuo kɔka kyerɛe Odum kɛse no sɛ, "Mebɛkyerɛ me mma dusaa ma wɔn nyinaa mba ha wo ase aba kɔpɛ kɔpɛ; na sɛ wɔba a, fa wo nnipaa dusaa twa ma wokum me mma no." Odum kɛse no kaa sɛ, "Mete ase, na mɛyɛ saa ma wo." Ne maame kɔhome; ɔkaa kyerɛ ne mma sɛ, "Mo mmoa kɔ afuo, kɔ Odum kɛse no ase; kɔpɛ wɔ hɔ, mmoa kɔpɛ mma mo na monya (aba)." Mma no kɔɔ. Wɔkɔduruu Odum kɛse no hɔ. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Number One, gyina hɔ; Number Two, gyina hɔ; Number Three, gyina hɔ; Number Four, gyina hɔ; Number Five, gyina hɔ; Number Six, gyina hɔ; Number Seven, gyina hɔ; Number Eight, gyina hɔ; Number Nine, gyina hɔ; Number Ten, gyina hɔ; na me ara, Number Eleven, me nso mogyina hɔ." Number Eleven kɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Monnim asɛm a ɛma maame se yɛnkɔpɛ kɔpɛ no?" Ne nuanom kaa sɛ, "Yɛnnim." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Waka kyerɛɛ Odum kɛse yi sɛ, sɛ yɛba ha a, ɛnsɛn nnipaa atwa mma ɛnbɔ yɛn; enti mo nyinaa twa nnuane, na monyi wo Odum kɛse yi so." Wɔtwaa nnuane no, na wɔnyii wo Odum kɛse no so, pim! pen! pim! pen! (ɛno ne nne a ɛyɛɛ). Odum kɛse no dwene sɛ mma no aba. Ɔtwaa ne nnipaa dusaa, na ɔmaa ɛtɔɔ asase so. Number Eleven ketewa no kaa sɛ, "Wahu, sɛ yɛkɔɔ hɔ a, Odum kɛse no bɛkum yɛn." Wɔpɛɛ kɔpɛ no na wɔfaa kɔmaa wɔn maame. Ɔnoaa no; oh! mpofota mma no nnwum (no nyinaa). Ne maame kaa sɛ, "Ah! sɛnne asɛm yi ho, mentumi nso mma no, enti mɛfa mmofra yi akɔ ma Nyame." Anɔpa a ɛbae no, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ, ɔkɔkae Nyame asɛm no nyinaa sɛ, "Mmofra bi a madi wɔn mma, berɛ a mɛpɛ sɛ midi no, mentumi nnya hwee mmidi (efisɛ wɔdi ntɛm paa na wɔdi pii); kɔm rekum me; enti mebɔ wo mpae, ma wɔn nna mmofra no mma wɔn nka, na matumi nya hwee mmidi." Nyame bisae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ saa ankasa?" Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "Merekasa a me ti mu yɛ fitaa." Nyame paa abotire, na wɔkɔtwaa awu kɛse bi, na wɔtoo botia (a awu) wom; ɔkɔfaa awɔ na ɔfaa ɔwɔn na ɔtoo hɔ, na ɔtrataa so. Afei abotire no kɔfrɛɛ mmofra no. Ansa na wɔabɛdu baabi a awu no hyɛ, Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Number One, gyina hɔ; Number Two, gyina hɔ; Number Three, gyina hɔ; Number Four, gyina hɔ; Number Five, gyina hɔ; Number Six, gyina hɔ; Number Seven, gyina hɔ; Number Eight, gyina hɔ; Number Nine, gyina hɔ; Number Ten, gyina hɔ; na me ara, Number Eleven ketewa yi, me gyina hɔ; mo mfa kwan yi; mo mfa kwan no." Ne nuanom bisae sɛ, "Adɛn nti, berɛ a kwan akɛse hyɛ hɔ no, ɛyɛ a yɛfa kwae mu?" Berɛ a wɔnam kwan no so no, wɔn nyinaa akura apɔm. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Tɔyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛyɛ Nyame see, "Yɛasiesie dua mmaa hɔ, mobɛtena so." Ɛnna Number Eleven see, "Hwan ne yɛn, na yɛbɛtumi atena saa dua fɛfɛfɛ a ɛyɛ saa so yi; enti, Me Panin, yɛbɛtena ha wɔn nkyɛn." Ɛno akyi no, Nyame hwɛ abafoɔ no; ɔsee, "Mɛde saa abafoɔ yi akɔ Owuo kuro mu." Anɔpa a ɛba no, berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ hwee, ɔfrɛɛ abafoɔ no; ɔsee, "Mobɛkɔ hɔ a Owuo te, na mokɔfa sika kuku, sika nnam, sika nsa-kuku, sika nsa-nkwa, ne sika mmoa-prapa firi ne nsam." Number Eleven see, "Wo ne yɛn panin, baabiara a wobɛkyerɛ yɛn akɔ, yɛbɛkɔ." Nyame see, "Monkɔ." Abafoɔ no hyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ Owuo kuro mu. Wɔduu hɔ; Owuo see, "Adɛn nti, berɛ a obiara nnim sɛ ɔbɛba ha, na moaba ha?" Wɔgyee no ase see, "Yɛnam kwan so na yɛduu ha (asɛm a ɛyɛ n'ani)." Owuo see, "O, ɛyɛ." Owuo na ɔwɔ mma dun; ne ho nso yɛ mmienu, enti wɔyɛ du a baako. Berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɛregye (anaa anadwo), Owuo kyekyere abafoɔ no baako baako na ɔmaa ne mma baako baako, na ɔne Number Eleven kɔdae. Berɛ a ɛyɛɛ sum no, Owuo ma ne ninyam hyerɛn (kosi sɛ ɛhyerɛn) kɔkɔɔ, na apatu Number Eleven. Number Eleven see, "Owuo, me nnyɛ da da." Owuo see, "Ɛnnɛn na wobɛda?" Number Eleven see, "Sɛ wode sika kuku bɛma me na makuku mputuo kakra a, mɛtumi ada." Owuo kɔfaa ama no. Berɛ kakra akyi no, Owuo ma ne ninyam hyerɛn bio, na wakɔpɛ Number Eleven. Number Eleven see, "Owuo, me nnyɛ da da." Owuo see, "Ɛnnɛn na wobɛda?" Number Eleven see, "Sɛ wode sika nsa-kuku bɛma me a, mɛtumi ada." Owuo faa ama no. Bio, ankyɛ koraa, Owuo pɛɛ sɛ ɔpatu Number Eleven. Number Eleven see, "Me nnyɛ da da." Owuo see, "Ɛnnɛn na wobɛda?" Number Eleven see, "Sɛ wokɔfa sika nnam bɛma me na matu nnam mputuo kakra a, mɛtumi ada." Owuo kɔfaa ama no. Berɛ kakra kɔɔ, na Owuo pɛ sɛ ɔpatu no. Number Eleven see, "Nana, me nnyɛ da da." Owuo see, "Enti, ɛnnɛn na wobɛda?" Number Eleven see, "Nana, sɛ wokɔfa sika nsa-nkwa bɛma me a, mɛtumi ada." Owuo kɔfaa ama no. Bio, ankyɛ koraa, Owuo sɔree bio. Number Eleven se, "O, Nana, me se me nnim da." Owuo se, "Na da bɛyɛ dɛn a wubɛda?" Number Eleven se, "Sɛ wokɔfa kuku a ɛyɛ mampam na wokɔtwa nsuo wom, na wohu aduane ma me na mɛdi a, mɛtumi ada." Owuo faa kuku kɔɔ asuom. Sɛ ɔtwaa nsuo (wom) no, na kuku no mampam no maa nsuo no kɔɔ fam. Ɛnnɛ Number Eleven se kyerɛ ne nuanom se, "Sɔre na monan." Enti wɔsɔree na wɔnan; na Number Eleven kɔtwee aburo dua, na ɔde guu baabi a ne nuanom da hɔ no, na ɔfaa ntoma hwee wɔn so. Owuo no nso na ɔwɔ asuom; ɔretwa nsuo. Ɛnnɛ Owuo Barima frɛɛ Owuo Baa se, "Ei, Owuo." Ɔgyee no ase se, "Adwo." Ɔse, "Dɛn na woreyɛ?" Ɔse, "Aane, ɛnyɛ abofra ketewa bi na m'anya; sɛ mɛka no a, ɔse, 'me nnim da'; ɔde me nnoema nyinaa akɔ, na seesei ɔse mede kuku ntwa nsuo." Owuo Barima se, "Ah, woyɛ abofra ketewa? Sɛ wotwa nhwiren na wode hyɛ kuku no mu, na wotwa nsuo a, ɛnyɛ yie?" Owuo Baa se, "O, ɛyɛ nokware." Ɔtwaa nhwiren, hyɛɛ no mu, na ɔtwaa nsuo no na ɔkɔɔ. Number Eleven se, "Owuo, woaba akum? Hu aduane no." Owuo huu aduane no; ɔhyɛɛ ne nnum so sɛ ɔbɛkum Number Eleven nuanom na ɔnhu hɔn aduane. Sɛ ɔkɔe no, ɔnhwehwɛɛ hɔn (yie), na ɔno ara kumu ne mma duaa nyinaa. Da a ɛba no, anɔpa anɔpa, berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ hwee, Owuo sɔree, na ɔtwɛɛ hɔn gya no ho. Number Eleven se, "Nana, tsetse mmoa bi atena wo bɔkɔ so." Owuo se, "Kɔfa mmoa-kɔtɔ a ɛda hɔ no, na tumi kumu no ma me." Number Eleven se, "Me ye owu; obi a woyɛ kɛse saa, sɛ tsetse mmoa tena wo so, na mmoa-kɔtɔ sika da hɔ (wobɛfa saa nneɛma a ɛyɛ mmerɛ yi), ma me nkɔfa ama mɛba mmu no." Owuo se, "Kɔfa no firi dan mu." Number Eleven kɔfaa aawe. Ɔyɛɛ no mmoa no kɔɔ fam; ɔnkumuu no. Number Eleven se, "O, nnɛ, baabiara a saa tsetse mmoa yi bɛtena, hɔ na mɛtena ne nkyɛn." Ɛnnɛ Number Eleven kɔɔ dan mu na ɔfaa ne kɔtoku a sika nnipa-kɔkɔkɔ na nneɛma nyinaa da mu no. Ɔse, "Owuo Nana, hwee rennyɛ adeyɛ mma me, gye sɛ megye tsetse mmoa no, na matow no hyɛ saa bag no mu, na makyɛn ama wo." Number Eleven hyɛɛ aseɛ, yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! Ɔduu kurom no awie; ɔse, "Ho, hɔ, Owuo Nana, fa me nka m'asɛm yi, nanso sɛ woyɛ kwasea kɛse a, mɛtumi ayi wo nnoɔma nyinaa; m'anuonyam a me ne hɔ bae no bɛtumi anya kwan a wɔbɛtumi anya nkwa; na mɛtumi ama wo nso anya wo mma duaa no nyinaa? Me nso, merekɔ." Owuo se, "Wo, abofra a woyɛ saa; baabiara a wobɛgye wo ho, hɔ na mɛgye me ho." Number Eleven bɔɔ mmirika, yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! na Owuo nso tuu no. Berɛ a Number Eleven rekɔ no, ɔhyiaa ne nuanom, na wɔda kwan no ho. Woreyɛ anomaa-atrap. Number Eleven se, "Mo nnkɔ ara? Owuo reba, enti ma yɛnhwehwɛ kwan a yɛbɛtumi anya nkwa." Afei Owuo baa wɔn nkyɛn. Number Eleven gyee aduro na ɔtɔɔ no wɔ ne nuanom so, na wɔkɔɔ silk-cotton tree bi atifi. Na Owuo gyinaa Silk-cotton tree no nan ase. Ɔse, "Hane, mehunuu saa mmofra yi, na wɔkɔe he?" Number Eleven daa atifi; ɔseɛ ne nuanom se, "Merekɔ sɛ mɛtɔ nsuo wɔ ne so." Ne nuanom se, "E, ɔrehwehwɛ yɛn sɛ ɔde yɛn ako, na yɛafu ba ha asi, nanso wose, 'Merekɔ sɛ mɛtɔ nsuo wɔ ne so.' " Number Eleven annye wɔn asɛm, na ɔtɔɔ nsuo wɔ Owuo so. Owuo se, "Ah, wodeɛ, nnɛ woahu amane." Owuo se, "Wo, abofra a woda hɔ, 'Kyere-he-ne, Kyere-he-ne'." Ɛno nti mmofra no mu baako tɔɔ ase. "Kyere-he-ne", ɔtɔfo a ɛtɔ so mmienu nso tɔɔ ase. Afei Number Eleven nko ara na ɔgyina hɔ. Owuo se, "Abofra, 'kyere-he-ne '," na Number Eleven bɔɔ ne ho na ɔtɔɔ asase so, kirim! Na Owuo nso kɔɔ Silk-cotton tree no so. Number Eleven se, "Wo, ɔbaa kɛse, wo nso, 'kyere-he-ne'." Na Owuo nso tɔɔ ase, tum! ɔwui. Number Eleven kɔtoo aduro, na ɔtrutruu no wɔ ne nsa mu, na ɔtɔɔ no wɔ ne nuanom so, na wɔsɔree. Number Eleven repɛ sɛ ɔto aduro no afutu, na aduro no bi tɔɔ Owuo so, na Owuo sɔree. Ɔse, "Woakum me, na woasɔre me nso; nnɛ wo ne me bɛyɛ asɛm." Afei wɔn nyinaa hyɛɛ aseɛ dɔɔm, kiri! kiri! kiri! Afei Owuo retu wɔn. Berɛ a wɔrekɔ no, asuopim kɛse bi a ɛatutu gu hɔ hyɛ hɔ. Berɛ a Number Eleven ne ne nuafoɔ dɔɔ no, na wɔnim sukuu, enti wɔsukuu kɔɔ afaeɛ. Number Eleven nkoaa na onnim sukuu. Mmofra no gyinaa afaeɛ no; wɔsuu, wɔsuu, na wɔsuu; enti wɔn anomu bɔɔ. Number Eleven nso, ɔyɛɛ aboɔ. Owuo dɔɔ asu no. Ɔsee, "O, mmofra yi, mongyina hɔ, ma me nkɔnhwe aboɔ bi na matow mfa mpa wɔn anomu a ɛabɔ no." Berɛ a Owuo hwɛɛ ase no, ɔhunuu aboɔ bi a ɛda hɔ; ɔfaa no na ɔtowee; berɛ a aboɔ no rekɔ no, ɛsee, "Mmoa, fa me nkɔto hɔ afaeɛ." Ɛtɔɔ afaeɛ no. Number Eleven see, "Me wɔ ha." Owuo see, "Ah, abaayewa yi! Asɛm biara nnim mfa mmoa wo, na nea mɛka kyerɛ wo ne eyi: kɔ na kɔtena fie, na yɛ aboɔ (lesser-gods) mu baako, na sɛ obi a mepɛ sɛ mefa no ba baabi a wowɔ no, wobɛka kyerɛ me; sɛ mepɛ a, mɛgya no na makyɛ wo no; nanso nea mepɛ (wɔ ne nkyɛnea) no, wye mfa na me." (Kyerɛ sɛ, nsawa.) Asaaeɛ no ne sɛ Abosom (lesser-gods) baa wiase. Wɔn nyinaa firi abaayewa ketewa Number Eleven hɔ.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, obiara betumi agye bewee sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na naayɛn nso betumi ayɛ me ayɛyie (ase sɛ mekae).
