SƐNE ABOSOM, NYAME Nketenfo no, BAa wiase mu

Obaa bi wɔ hɔ; ɔwoo mma dusaa; na da biara sɛ ɔsɔre kɔnoo aduane a, mma no nnum kɔɔ no nyinaa. Wɔn maame annya bi a ɔbɛdi. Ɛnnɛ ɔsumannee yie, na ɔkɔɔ afuo no mu kɔka kyerɛɛ Odua-Kkronkron no sɛ, "Mɛkyerɛ me mma dusaa-baako ma wɔn aba wo ase ha aba sɛ wɔrebo pampuna; na sɛ wɔba a, twa wo nnua dusaa-baako na wokum me mma no." Odua-Kkronkron no kaa sɛ, "Mete ase, na mɛyɛ saa ma wo." Na na ɛna ɔkɔhome; ɔkaa kyerɛɛ ne mma no sɛ, "Mo be..." "Kɔ afuo no mu, ɔdan tree no ase; ayaresɛe wɔ hɔ, kɔfa na bra." Mmofra no hyɛɛ aseɛ. Wɔkɔɔ na wɔduu ɔdan tree no hɔ. Number Eleven se, "Number One, gyina hɔ; Number Two, gyina hɔ; Number Three, gyina hɔ; Number Four, gyina hɔ; Number Five, gyina hɔ; Number Six, gyina hɔ; Number Seven, gyina hɔ; Number Eight, gyina hɔ; Number Nine, gyina hɔ; Number Ten, gyina hɔ; na me ara, Number Eleven, me gyina hɔ." Number Eleven kasa kyerɛɛ hɔn, se, "Moni mmoa r "Adepa a ɛma maame se yɛnnyɛ na yɛnkɔ nkyɛn kɔdwum?" Ne nuanom se, "Dabi." Ɔse, "Waka kyerɛ saa Onyina dua yi sɛ, sɛ yɛkɔ hɔ a, ɛnsɛn ne nnuaba na ɛnbɔ yɛn; enti mo nyinaa twene nnuaba, na monyi wo saa Onyina dua yi so." Wɔtwene nnuaba no, na wɔnyii wo Onyina dua no so, pim! pen! pim! pen! (na saa na nne no te). Silk-cotton tree no dwene sɛ mmofra no aba. Ɔtwee ne nnua du a baako, na ɔmaa wɔn tɔɔ fam. Number Eleven ketewa no see, "Mo ahu, sɛ yɛkɔɔ hɔ a, Silk-cotton tree no bɛgye yɛn nkwa." Wɔfaa pumpkin no kɔmaa wɔn maame. Ɔno nso noo no; oh! mmofra no dinn na wɔdidi (nyinaa). Wɔn maame see, "Ah! sɛ asɛm yi ho a, mentumi nso mma no, enti mɛfa mmofra yi akɔ ma Nyame." Anɔpa a ɛbae no, berɛ a nneɛma baa hɔ, ɔkɔkae Nyame asɛm no nyinaa sɛ, "Mmofra bi a madi wo mma, sɛ mepɛ sɛ midi a, mentumi nnya hwee mmidi (efisɛ wɔdi ntɛm paa na wɔdi pii); kɔkrom rehyɛ me; enti mebɔ wo mpae, ma mmofra no mma na woawu, na matumi anya biribi adi." Onyame kasaa sɛ, "Ɛno ankasa ne nea ɛrekɔ?" Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "Merekasa wɔ ti bi a, ne mu yɛ fitaa." Onyame paa abirikyirefoɔ, na wɔkɔtoo awutuo kɛse bi, a wɔtoo (nsuo) kɔtɔkɔtɔ mu; ɔkɔfaa awɔ na ɔfaa gyata na ɔtoo hɔ, na ɔtrataa so. Na seesei abibirefo no kɔfrɛ abafo no. Anka wɔn nso nnyɛ mmerɛ a wɔdu baabi a pɔnkɔ no hyɛ, na Number Eleven se, "Number One, gyina hɔ; Number Two, gyina hɔ; Number Three, gyina hɔ; Number Four, gyina hɔ; Number Five, gyina hɔ; Number Six, gyina hɔ; Number Seven, gyina hɔ; Number Eight, gyina hɔ; Number Nine, gyina hɔ; Number Ten, gyina hɔ; na me ara, Number Eleven ketewa yi, me gyina hɔ; wo na ɛsɛ sɛ wo fa" ha; mpɛn nni hɔ a mobɛtwa mu." Ne nuanom kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti, sɛ kwan akɛseɛ bi wɔ hɔ a, ɛyɛ a yɛbɛtwa kwaa mu?" Ɛnnɛ, berɛ a na wɔnam kwan no so no, wɔn nyinaa akura apɔm. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Tɔ borɔne bi a ɛwɔ ha yi tow kwan yi so." Wɔtowee no hɔ, na ɛtɔɔ mu (into the pit), yiridi (na ɛyɛ ne nne a ɛyɛe berɛ a ɛtɔɔ mu no). Number Eleven see sɛ, "Hwan hɔ hwɛ, sɛ yɛakɔ fa hɔ a, yɛn nyinaa bɛwu." Enti wɔfa kwan fitaa bi na wɔkɔ Nyame hɔ. Nyame ama wɔatwa awu, (na wɔatwa ho,) na wɔade akokoo atoa so, na sɛ mmofra no ba sɛ wɔpɛ sɛ wɔtena so a, wɔbɛtɔ awu no mu. Mmofra no duru Nyame anim. Nyame see, "Wɔasiesie akokoa wɔ hɔ, mobɛkɔ na monte ase." Ɛnna Number Eleven see, "Hwan ne yɛn, na yɛbɛtumi akɔ ate ase wɔ saa akokoa a ɛyɛ fɛ saa yi so; enti, Me Panin, yɛbɛkɔ akɔte ase wɔ ha." Ɛno akyi no, Nyame hwɛ abafoɔ no; ɔsee, "Mɛkyerɛ saa abafoɔ yi kɔ Owuo kuro." Anɔpa a ɛba no, berɛ a nneɛma nyinaa baa hɔ, ɔfrɛɛ abafoɔ no; ɔsee, " "Ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔtɔ Owuo (a ɔte) hɔ, na wakɔfa sika kɔkɔɔ nnawa, sika kɔkɔɔ nnam, sika kɔkɔɔ nsa-kɔkɔɔ-kuku, sika kɔkɔɔ nsa-mmoa, ne sika kɔkɔɔ mmoa-prapa." Number Eleven see sɛ, "Wo ne yɛn panin, baabiara a wobɛkyerɛ yɛn kwan, yɛbɛkɔ." Onyame see sɛ, "Kɔ." Mmofra no hyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ Owuo kuro mu. Wɔduu hɔ; Owuo see, "Adɛn nti, ɛnna obiara nnante ha, na moaba ha?" Wɔgyee me answers see, "Yɛnam kwan so na yɛduu ha (mfomsoɔ)." Owuo see, "O, ɛyɛ)." Owuo wɔ mma duaa; ne ho nso yɛnka ho a, wɔyɛ du a baako. Berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ wɔyera (ye obi aa) anadwo), Owuo kyekyere mmofra no baako baako na ɔmaa ne mma baako baako, na ɔne Number Eleven kɔdwee. Berɛ a anadwo bae no, Owuo ma ne ninyam hye kɔkɔɔ (kosi sɛ ɛhyerɛn), na ama wakwan Number Eleven. Number Eleven see sɛ, "Owuo, menneda da." Owuo nso bisaa no sɛ, "Ɛnnɛm ɛnne na wobɛda?" Number Eleven see sɛ, "Sɛ wode sika kɔkɔɔ nnaba ba ma me nsua kakra a, ɛyɛ a mɛtumi ada." Owuo nso kɔfaa aa own baa ma no. Ankyɛ koraa no, Owuo hyɛɛ ne nnumu nnuane bio, na ama wakɔka Number Eleven. Number Eleven see sɛ, "Owuo, menneda da." Owuo see sɛ, "Ɛnnɛm ɛnne na wobɛda?" Number Eleven see sɛ, "Sɛ wode sika kɔkɔɔ nsa-kuku ba ma me a, mɛtumi ada." Na Owuo fa ba maa no. Bio, ankyɛ koraa, Owuo kɔree sɛ ɔbɛkyere Number Eleven. Number Eleven see sɛ, "Mennante da da." Owuo see, "Sɛn na wobɛda?" Number Eleven see, "Sɛ wokɔfa sika nnam-duaeɛ ba ma me na matwa ho mmerɛ kakra a, ɛno a mɛtumi ada." Owuo kɔfaa ama no. Mmerɛ kakra kɔɔ, na Owuo rekɔfa no. Number Eleven see sɛ, "Nana, menneda da." Owuo see, "Ɛnna, berɛ bɛyɛ dɛn a wobɛda?" Number Eleven see, "Nana, sɛ wokɔfa sika nsuman-atutuo aba ama me a, mɛtumi ada." Owuo nso kɔfaa aba. Bio, ankyɛ koraa no, Owuo soɔɔ ne ho bio. Number Eleven see sɛ, "O, Nana, me see sɛ me nnyɛ da da." Owuo see, "Na da bɛn na wobɛda?" Number Eleven see, "Sɛ wokɔfa kuku a mfuna pii wɔ mu na wokɔtwa nsuo wom, na wohu aduane ma me na mɛdi a, ɛno a mɛtumi ada." Owuo faa kuku kɔɔ asuom. Berɛ a ɔpuee nsuo no, asɛm-atere no mframa no maa nsuo no kɔɔ mu. Ɛnnɛ Number Eleven kaa kyerɛ ne nua mma no sɛ, "Sɔre na monnwene." Enti wɔsɔree na wɔnwenee; na Number Eleven kɔtoo plantain nnuaba, na ɔde guu baabi a ne nua mma no dae, na ɔfaa ntoma na ɔde tabaab wɔn. Ɛnnɛ Owuo wɔ asuom no ho; ɔrepue nsuo. Seesei Owuo Barima frɛɛ Owuo Baa, na ɔse, "Ei, Owuo." Ɔno nso ayɛ no mmuae sɛ, "Adwo." Ɔseɛ no, "Dɛn na woreyɛ?" Ɔno nso se, "Aane, abofra ketewa bi na m'anya; sɛ mɛka no a, ɔse, 'Mennante mma me'; wafa me nnoɔma nyinaa, na seesei ɔse mɛfa asɛnkɛ na mɛpue nsuo." Owuo Barima se, "Ah, woyɛ abofra ketewa; seesei, sɛ wotwa nwini na woyɛ..." "Sɛ mehu asɛm a ɛwɔ asɛm-atwa no mu, na mehyɛ nsuo mu a, ɛnyɛ yie?" Owuo-ba kaa sɛ, "O, ɛyɛ nokware." Ɔtwe efie, ɔtoo no mu, ɔhyɛɛ nsuo mu na ɔkɔɔ. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Owuo, woaba akum? Pra aduane no." Owuo no noaa aduane no; ɔhyɛɛ ne nnum so na ama wakil Number Eleven nnuan no na watuo wɔn yɛ aduane. Berɛ a ɔkɔe no, ɔnhwehwɛɛ hɔ yie, na ɔno ara na ɔyɛɛ ne mma duaa no nyinaa mogyapae. Da a ɛdi so no, anɔpa anɔpa paa, berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ wɔda adi no, Owuo sɔree, na ɔtwɛɛ hɔ bebree wɔ gya no ho. Number Eleven se, "Nana, tsetse mmoa bi atena wo bo so." Owuo se, "Kɔfa mmoa-papo a ɛda hɔ no, na fa kyea ma me." Number Eleven se, "Me Yehowa; obi a woyɛ kɛse te sɛ wo, sɛ tsetse mmoa atena wo so, na sika mmoa-papo da hɔ (wopɛ sɛ woka saa nneɛma a ɛyɛ mmerɛ yi), ma me nkɔfa no na maba mma me kyea." Owuo se, "Kɔfa no firi r "oom." Number Eleven kɔtoo baa. Ɔyɛɛ no yie sɛ ɔpiraa nnaman no; ɔampue. Number Eleven see sɛ, "Oh, nnɛ, baabiara a nnaman yi bɛdu hɔ, me nso mɛdu hɔ ne nkyɛn." Ɛnna Number Eleven kɔɔ dan no mu na ɔfaa ne bag no a sika kɔkɔɔ nnompe ne nneɛma nyinaa hyɛ mu. Ɔse, "Nana Owuo, hwee renkyɛ m'adwene sen sɛ megye tsetse mmoa no, hyɛ eyi mu, na mede aba wo nkyɛn." Number Eleven fii ase kɔɔ, yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! Ɔduu kurom no awie; ɔsee, "Ho, hɔ, Nana Owuo, fa me nsɛm yi nka, nanso sɛ woyɛ kwasea kɛse a, mɛtumi ayi wo nnoɔma nyinaa afiri wo hɔ; m'anuonyam a me ne hɔm baa no bɛtumi ayɛ akwan a wɔbɛfi hɔ ntia; na mɛtumi ama wo nso anya wo mma du a wo wɔ no nyinaa?" Me nso, mɛkɔ." Owuo see, "Wo, abofra a wo te saa; baabiara a wobetena, hɔ mɛtena." Number Eleven bɔɔ mmirika kɔe, yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! na Owuo nso tuu no mmirika. Berɛ a Number Eleven rekɔ no, ɔhyiaa ne nuanom, na wɔda kwan ho. Woreyɛ anomaa-atere. Number Eleven bisaa sɛ, "Mo nnkɔ nnɛ?" "Owuo reba, enti ma yɛnhwehwɛ kwan bi a yɛbɛtumi afi ase." Afei Owuo baa wɔn nkyɛn. Number Eleven faa aduro tɔɔ ne nuanom so, na wɔkɔɔ odum dua bi a ɛyɛ kɛse ataso. Na Owuo gyinaa odum dua no nan ase. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ha ara, mehunuu mmofra yi, na wɔnkɔ he?" Number Eleven a ɔda akyirikyiri no kaa kyerɛ ne nuanom sɛ, "Merekɔ sɛ mɛtɔ nsuo wɔ ne so." Ne nuanom kaa sɛ, "E, ɔrehwehwɛ yɛn sɛ ɔde yɛn ako, na yɛafu na yɛaba abɛten ha, nanso woka sɛ, 'Merekɔ sɛ mɛtɔ nsuo wɔ ne so.' " Number Eleven ntumi ntie, na ɔtɔɔ nsuo wɔ Owuo so. Owuo see, "Ah, woaba, nnɛ woahu amane." Owuo see, "Wo, aba a wote hɔ no, 'Kyere-he-ne, Kyere-he-ne'." Ɛno akyi no, mmofra no mu baako tɔree ase. "Kyere-he-ne", ɔtani tɔree ase. Seesei, Number Eleven nko ara na ɔtena hɔ. Owuo see, "Ba, 'kyere-he-ne'," na Number Eleven bɔɔ ntiaa tɔree asase so, kirim! Na Owuo nso kɔɔ Odua kɛseɛ no ase. No Eleven see, "Wo, obiadeɛ kɛseɛ, wo nso, 'kyere-he-ne'." Na Owuo nso tɔɔ ase, tum! ɔwui. No Eleven kɔtoo aduro, tweneɛ no ne nsa mmienu mu, na ɔhyɛɛ no ne nuanom so, na wɔsɔree. No Eleven repae aduro no atow, na aduro bi tɔɔ Owuo so, na Owuo nyansuee. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Woatumi awu me, na woatumi asɔre me nso; nnɛ me ne wo bɛyɛ asɛm." Ɛnna wɔn nyinaa hyɛɛ aseɛ dwoa kɔ, kiri! kiri! kiri! Seesei Owuo na ɔretu wɔn fo. Berɛ a wɔrekɔ no, nsuo kɛseɛ bi a ayɛ mpoa gu hɔ. Berɛ a Number Eleven ne ne nuanom duu hɔ no, wɔnim senea yɛtane, enti wɔtanee kɔɔ afaeɛ. Number Eleven nko ara na onnim senea yɛtane. Mmofra no gyinaa baabi foforo; wɔsuu, wɔsuu, na wɔsuu; wɔn anomu bɔɔ pum. Number Eleven nso, ɔyɛɛ aboɔ. Owuo duruu asuo no ho. Ɔkaa sɛ, "O, mmofra yi, mongyina hɔ, ma menkɔfa aboɔ kɔtow na matow wɔn anomu a ayɛ kuku no." Owuo, berɛ a ɔhwɛɛ ase no, ɔhunuu aboɔ bi a ɛda hɔ; ɔfaa no na ɔtowee; berɛ a aboɔ no rekɔ no, ɛkaa sɛ, "Mmofra, fa me kɔto fapem." Ɛtɔɔ fapem hɔ. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Me wɔ ha." Owuo kaa sɛ, "Ah, abaayewa yi!" Me nni biribi foforo a mɛka kyerɛ wo, na nea mɛka kyerɛ wo ne eyi: kɔ na kɔtena fie, na yɛ m'abosom mu baako, na sɛ obi a mepɛ sɛ mefa ba wo nkyɛn a, wobɛbɔ me amanneɛ; sɛ mepɛ a, mɛgya no na makyɛ no ama wo; nanso nea mepɛ (deɛ ɛbɛba ne nkyɛn) no, wobɛgye no ama me." (Kyerɛ sɛ, nsawa.) Asaase Abosom (ketewa -nyame) baa wiase. Wɔn nyinaa firi abofra ketewa Number Eleven hɔ.

Me nsɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, obiara betumi agye baabi fofor sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na naayɛn nso betumi akye me ayɛyie (ase sɛ mekae).
