Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ANANSESƐM BI

Na bere bi mmeranteɛ baasa wɔ hɔ, na wɔn nyinaa dɔɔ ɔbea bi. Afei mmeranteɛ baasa yi firi Bontuku kɔɔ Cape Coast, na wɔka sɛ wɔrekɔtɔ nneɛma. Bere a wɔduruu Cape Coast no, na wɔn mu biako wɔ mirror. Ɔfaa mirror no, na ɔtwee nhaban bi (magic leaves) na ɔde guu mirror no so, na ɔhwɛɛ mu. Ɔhunuu sɛ wɔn dɔfo no wuwui, na ɔda apono a wɔabue no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Me nnamfoɔ, yɛn dɔfo no wuwui. " Wɔn mu biako kae sɛ, "Mɛma yɛaduru Bontuku ntɛm ara sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a yɛbɛkɔsie no. " Nea ɔdi akyi no nso kae sɛ, "Sɛ wotumi ma yɛduru hɔ a, mɛma no asɔre ntɛm ara. " Afei biako kɔtwee aduru, na ɔde guu wɔn nkotodwe so, na wɔfiri hɔ. Ɛno ara na wɔduruu hɔ. Foforo nso kɔtwee aduru na ɔde guu brass bowl mu; na ɔde ne nnuan-twitwa guu aduru no mu, na ɔde guu amane no so. Ɔkae sɛ, "Amane, sɔre! Amane, sɔre! " na ɛsɔree. Afei nnipa baasa yi mu, hena na ɔyɛɛ nea ɛyɛ papa?
