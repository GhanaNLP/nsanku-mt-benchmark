Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ YƐBƐHU OKRA KRAMAN NO DAA REDA VELVET PON, BERE A KRAMAN NO BƐDA WƆ ADAN MU OGYA NO NSÕ MU

They nna aduanan Aane Nkwammoa no kae sɛ, "Mɛkɔ m’akuraa (sɛ mɛkɔfa biribi) na mɛsan. " Ananse kɔɔ n’akuraa; ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nua baa sɛ, "Mɛbɛsoma wo akɔ me nnamfoɔ a ɔwɔ akyiri no, na sɛ wukɔ a, wode nsuo fitaa yi kɔma no na woa fa wo tirim na woayɛ nea ɔhyɛ wo; na ɛsɛ sɛ woyɛ mmɔden wɔ kokoam sɛ wobɛfa wo nsa aka saa kɔkɔɔ no. " Ababaa no firi hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ɔbarima no akuraa. Ɔbarima no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Eyi deɛ, mɛhwɛ sɛ worenkɔ bio, efisɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wotena 27. "Sɛ womfa mma me a, mebɛkum wo ntɛm ara. " Mpataa no kae sɛ, "Yɛnkɔ asubɔnten so sɛ mebɛma wo nea ɛyɛ wo dea. " Bere a wɔn baanu duruu asubɔnten no, mpataa no yii ne ho, na kɔla no bae. Koko no faa no bae kyerɛɛ no kraman no. Kraman no kae sɛ, "Agya, me srɛ wo, sɛ wode kɔ a, nka nea asisi no ho asɛm. " Koko no de ne ano too mu. Wɔduruu fie, na Koko no ka kyerɛɛ ne panin no nea asisi no nyinaa sɛ, "Ɛnam nam a kraman no bɔɔ no bere a yɛrekɔ no nti, ɔyɛɛ nea ɔntumi nkɔ akyiri bio. Na bio nso

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom betumi agye no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no nso wobetumi akamfo me.
