Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

HOW OWEA (OWIA-DUA NO), ABOSOM-AKOTERE (ABOSOM-AKOTERE NO) NE OPATUO (OWU-KƆN NO) BAA HƆ BƐNYAA WƆN NNYIGYEƐ SORONKO Bere a owia-dua no baa hɔ bɛnyaa wɔn nnyigyeɛ soronko no. Bere a abosom-akoterɛ no baa hɔ bɛnyaa wɔn nnyigyeɛ soronko no. Bere a owu-kɔn no baa hɔ bɛnyaa wɔn nnyigyeɛ soronko no.

OWEA, dua-mmoa, a ɔwɔ abodin nso sɛ Kwaduampon Kyerefo, Opatuo, ne Abosom-Akotere, a wɔn din ne “Ɔbɔfoɔ no wu”, “ɔfeɛ biako a ɔyɛ adeɛ biara”, “Ɔnam brɛoo nanso ɔbɛduru borɔfoɔ asase a ɛwɔ akyirikyiri no”, saa mmoa yi nyinaa na wɔte aseɛ bom. Afei na wɔn mu biara nni ɔyere. Na Adobe-dua no tɔn ne ho maa wɔn. Da biara, bere a Adobe-dua no sɔree no, ɔkɔɔ ɔhwɛɛ saa baako yi; da a edi hɔ no, ɔhwɛɛ wɔn mu biako foforo; na da a edi hɔ no, ɔhwɛɛ foforo. Da biako, bere a ɛyɛɛ Ɔbɔfoɔ-wu no ho na ɔhwehwɛɛ Adobe-dua no, ɛhe na ɔbɛhunu no? Na ɔkɔɔ kɔka dua-mmoa no ne Opatuo amanneɛ. Na wɔkae sɛ, “kɔfrɛ no. ” Na Ɔbɔfoɔ-wu no maa ne nne kɔɔ soro: “Adobe o! Adobe o! ”

Dua-mmoa no suu:

“Bere a ɔkɔɔ no, ɔansan na ɔansan, ɔkɔɔ, ɔansan, ɔansan, ɔansan baeɛ!”

Na owufoɔ no frɛɛ sɛ:

“Yɛasusu sika kusuu no na yɛbɛtɔ agu m! Yɛasusu sika kusuu no na yɛbɛtɔ agu m! m!”

Na ɛno ne mfiaseɛ a ɛfiri abɔdeɛ mmiɛnsa yi teɛteɛ mu.

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
