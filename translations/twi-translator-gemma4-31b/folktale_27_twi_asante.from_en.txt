Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

WƆ ABUSUAKUO BI MU NO, ONIPA A ƆNNI NYANSA KORAA NNI HƆ.

bi Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dɛn na woyɛ mmerɛw saa? " Ɛna no na ɔreka nokware asɛm a enti a wɔayɛ mmerɛw no, bere a ne mma kae sɛ, "nka no, eyi ne nkwasea, ɛbɛn na ɔbɛhu nteaseɛ? " Ɛna no kaa sɛ, "meka. " Bere a ɔka kyerɛɛ no no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Aane, fa nsuo-nsuo no mfa no akɔ me nkuraase. " Ɛna no ne ne nuanom de nsuo-nsuo no faa ne ti nwi kɔɔ nkwasea no nkuraase. Bere a wɔduruu hɔ no, ne nkuraase no, ɔhyɛɛ nsuo-nsuo no sɛ, "kɔtena me dɔm-dɔm so na woafrɛ wo ho kakra. " Ɔkɔɔ na ɔtenaa so, na ɔdɔm no, na ɔmaa saa dwom yi:

Ɛna woo me a ɔyɛ nkwasea no, Hurotom.

Agya woo me abofra nkwasea bi a ɔne Hurotom.

Ɛna woo me a me yɛ nkwasea kɛkɛ na me yɛ Hurotom. Ɛna woo me a me yɛ nkwasea kɛkɛ na me yɛ Hurotom.

Ɛna woo me a me yɛ nkwasea no, Hurotom na ɔwoo me.

Ɛna

Fairy no dii agorɔ kɔsii sɛ ɔbrɛɛ no, na afei abofra nkwaseɛ no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "yɛnkɔfa w’atifi ti nhwi. " Ɔne Fairy no kɔduruu fie; ɔkae sɛ, "fa me ti nhwi na fa si hɔ ma me kɔ. " Abofra nkwaseɛ no nso kae sɛ, "wo nso, me ano a wotwaa no, ɛsɛ sɛ wota nsɛnkyerɛnne no nyinaa ansa na mebɛfa w’atifi ti nhwi no ahyɛ. " Fairy no kɔɔ adwuma no ho; na ɔkataa nsɛnkyerɛnne biako a ne nan yɛe no a, na ɔnam no yɛɛ foforo. Afei, ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛtumi, nanso antumi nnyae ne nnanom nyinaa. Ɔkae sɛ, "Papa, mepɛ 'palaver' biara, enti meyi wo maame, na me deɛ, ma me san me ho, na ma me kɔ. " Abofra a ɔyɛ nkwaseɛ no kae sɛ, "ɛnne kɔ, na me nso mefii me maame. " Afei maame no bae bɛbɔɔ n’akyi, na ɔkae sɛ, "meda wo ase, na afei (mehu sɛ) nkwaseɛ biara nni abusuakuo no mu. " Ɛno nti yɛka sɛ, sɛ wunya mma a, bue wo ani kɛse, na hwɛ wɔn yie, efisɛ wunim sɛ daakye da bi a ɛyɛ ɔhaw da no, ɔbɛba abɛhwɛ wo.

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
