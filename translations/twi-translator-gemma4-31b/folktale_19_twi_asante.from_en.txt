Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ WƆBƆƆ MMOFRA DII KAN

They say that Na afei Afei ɔsan baa fam; na ɔsan kɔɔ na ɔde ne ho siee baabi. Afei Ntikuma kɔɔ soro. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, saa agya no de biribi aba bio. " Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Ɛna Nyiwankonfwea, Afudotwedotwe, moma ɛfiri sɛ nea agya de aba yi, ɛboro nea etwa to so koraa. " Afei wɔn nyinaa kɔɔ soro wɔ abɔnten no so. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Saa adeɛ yi yɛ fɛ. " Ɛbuae sɛ, "Mfrɛ me 'fɛfɛ'. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wodin dɛn? " Ɛkaa sɛ, "Mfrɛ me 'Swish-and-raise-weals'. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Swish kakra ma mehu. " Na saa asau no kɔɔ ne so na ɛbɔɔ wɔn denneennen. Ananse gyinaa nkyɛn, na ɔteɛm sɛ, "Fa no to so, fa no to so, titiriw fa no to Ntikuma so, fa no to no so. " Afei bere a Ananse hwɛɛ na ɔhunuu sɛ wɔabɔ wɔn yie no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Adwobere. " Ananse bae na ɔde asau no twaa no nketenkete na ɔbɔɔ no. Ɛno na ɛmaa asau no baa mmusuakuo no mu. Ɛno nti, ɛba sɛ sɛ woka wo ba biribi a, na ɔntie wo, yɛbɔ no.

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
