Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

Twɛn hɔ na wobɛhu

da Afei Parrots no tuu kwan kɔtoo dua tenten bi so, na wɔn akyi bɛdii mogya no mu, na wɔn akyi bɛyɛɛ kɔkɔɔ. Parrots no nyinaa de wɔn akyi bɛdii mu, sɛ ade a wɔde bɛkae adeyɛ no. Afei, ɛda bi, Parrots no mu biako kae sɛ ɔbɛdidi. Ɔkɔɔ na ɔhyiaa abofra no maame. Na maame no bisaa Parrot no sɛ, “Me ba a me ne no teɛ no, na ɔyɛ mmerɛw sɛnea biribiara. Bere a me sɔre no, na me ka sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔtwɛn hɔ na ɔhu; ɔte sɛ nkwasea, kyerɛ me asɛm no, ɔka sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ ‘I-will-see’ sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ no. Ɛyɛ nna awotwe akyi ɛnnɛ na me nhu no, na me srɛ wo, sɛ woahu no a, fa no ba me nkyɛn. ” Parrot no kae sɛ, “Oh, ɔbaa yɛn akuraa no mu, na yɛn nso, yɛkum no. Ne mogya no bi a yɛde yɛɛ nkaeɛ wɔ adeyɛ no ho no, wɔ me akyi yi so. Ɛno nti, sɛ wode kɔ (wo kwan) a, kɔka kyerɛ wo nkurɔfoɔ nyinaa sɛ, sɛ obi ka kyerɛ obi sɛ, ‘Twɛn hɔ na wubehu,’ ɛnneɛ ɔaka no nna. ”

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
