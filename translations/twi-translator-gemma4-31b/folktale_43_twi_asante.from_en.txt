Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ NWANSANA, NNAM A ƆYƐ NNAM NO, BERE A ƆTO KRAMAN, KRAMAN NO SO NO, ƆBƆ NO, KAM! KAM! Na nnam no to kraman no so, ɔbɔ no, kam! kam! Na nnam no to kraman no so, ɔbɔ no, kam! kam!

Abɔmmɔfoɔ bi na ɔwɔ akraman baanan; na wɔfrɛɛ bi Sniff-sniff; na wɔfrɛɛ bi Lick-lick; na wɔfrɛɛ bi Tie-in-knots; na wɔfrɛɛ bi Gulp-down. Na ɔtaa ne ne akraman kyinkyin. Bere nyinaa, bere a ɔkɔɔ kwaeɛ no, ɔne wɔn kɔɔ baako. Ɛda bi ɔkɔɔ kwaeɛ na ɔhunuu kola-dua bi; na ɛhyɛ nnuaba a ɛsɛeɛ nkuo nkuo te sɛɛ. Afei ɔsan kɔɔ fie, na ɔbɔɔ ne yere amanneɛ sɛ, "Kola-dua bi a mehu no, ɛhyɛ nnuaba a ɛsɛeɛ nkuo nkuo te sɛɛ. " Ne yere kae sɛ, "Mfa nni, efisɛ wunim sɛ yɛnni kola-nnuaba biara a yɛbɛnam so adidi na yɛnni biara a yɛbɛtɔn. " Abɔmmɔfoɔ no kɔɔ kwan so, nanso ɔgyaee akraman no wɔ fie, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a kola-dua no wɔ. Afei ɔforoɔ so, na ɔtwaa kola-nnuaba no bi. Afei ɔtee nneɛma bi firi akyirikyiri, "Twa! be! kukuru! kukuru! hu! pi! " Sasabonsam bi gyinaa ne anim. Ɔkae sɛ, "Hene na watwa me kola-nnuaba no? " Abɔmmɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, "Me ara. " Sasabonsam no kae sɛ, "Mɛkum wo ntɛm ara wɔ saa adeɛ a woyɛeɛ yi ho. " Abɔmmɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, "Sɛ wobɛkum me a, mfrɛ me akraman no di kan. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Frɛ, nanso sɛ wode bɛbɔ mmɔden mmiɛnsa sɛ wobɛfrɛ wɔn a wɔrenba a,

Hwɛ-hwɛ, bɛhyia me, Nsu-nsu, bɛhyia me, Kyerɛ-kyerɛ, bɛhyia me, Nsu-nsu, bɛhyia me.

“Hwɛ-hwɛ, bɛhyia me, Nsu-nsu, bɛhyia me, Kyerɛ-kyerɛ, bɛhyia me, Nsu-nsu, bɛhyia me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔkyɛ a wɔreba no!

Afei nkraman no ate, wɔatu mmirika, prada! prada! Sasabonsam no kae sɛ, “Frɛ bio, na ɛno na ɛyɛ nea ɛtɔ so mmiɛnsa. ” Abɔmmɔfoɔ no bɔɔ ne nne kɛseɛ paa:
