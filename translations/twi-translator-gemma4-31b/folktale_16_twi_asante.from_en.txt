Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

Ɛsɛ sɛ abeawa biara ware nea wɔde no ma no wɔ aware mu no.

Na bere bi abanwa bi wɔ hɔ a wɔfrɛ no Kwaboaso. Obiara a wɔde no kɔma no sɛ ɔmmɛware no, ɔkae sɛ, "Me mpɛ no. " Wɔde no kɔmaa ɔbɔfoɔ bi, na ɔkae sɛ, "Ugh! Ɔbarima yi wɔ no so, me mpɛ no. "

Ɛda bi ɔkɔɔ afuo no mu kɔɔ, ɔkae sɛ ɔretwa akutu. Ɔde sekan kɔtwaa, ɔbɔɔ akutu no, to! na ɛno ara na nkurase nketewa no na wɔte akutu no so; wɔkɔɔ fam, na wɔbaa hɔ, na wɔkyeree Kwaboaso; wɔkae sɛ, "Wo na wɔde wo kɔma obiara a, wo werɛw wo ti, pusu! pusu! " Na mmofra no kyeree no na wɔkae sɛ: "Bra, yɛnsɛe no.

Afei, ɔbɔfoɔ a wɔde Kwaboaso ama no no tee Kwaboaso nne no, ɔkae sɛ, "Mekɔhwɛ dɛn na ɛwɔ hɔ, efisɛ yɛn nni bɔne biara de tua bɔne. " Bere a ɔkɔeɛ no, na Kwaboaso wɔ hɔ, na mmoa no rehyɛ no. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no bɔɔ tuo wɔ mmoa no so, na baako hwee ase. Mmoa no mu panyin no kae sɛ, "Ɔnom nsa, na ɔyɛɛ n’anim; fa no to hɔ, na ɛno nso. " Bio nso ɔbɔfoɔ no bɔɔ tuo, na foforo hwee ase.
