SƐNEA ANENE AKOKƆƆ NO KƆN BƐYƐƐ FITAA NA NE AKYI BƐYƐƐ TUNTUM

Ɔbaa bi a ne din ne Ata (the Twin) ne ne ba kɔɔ kɔpɔpɔɔ (for fish). Bere a wɔduruu nsuo no no, n'abofra kae sɛ, "Yɛnkɔ nsuo no so. " Nanso ɔbaa no kae sɛ, "Yɛnkɔ nsuo no ase, efisɛ nkawa nketewa na ɛwɔ hɔ. " Ne ba no kae sɛ, "Me deɛ, mekɔ nsuo no so, efisɛ nkawa nketewa no na ɛwɔ hɔ. " Enti ne maame kɔɔ nsuo no ase. Wɔkɔɔ akyiri bere tenten, na ne ba no nyaa bi, na ɔbaa bɛhwehwɛɛ ne maame. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na ne maame nkukuo no wɔ nsuo no anim. Abofra no kae sɛ, "Ah! ɛnyɛ baako, ɛnyɛ mmienu, na ɛyɛ ne maame nkukuo. " Na ɔmaa ne nne so, suu na ɔfrɛɛ ne maame sɛ:

Me kae saa, na wontie, me kae wo saa, na wontie.

Mekaeɛ, ‘Ɛna, momma yɛnkɔ nsuo no atifi, efisɛ ɛhɔ na nkawa nketewa te hɔ’.

Na ɔkae sɛ ɔrekɔ asubɔnten no ase efisɛ asubɔnten no ase na nkawa nketewa te.

Na dɛn na mebɛyɛ na? Aane me maame, Ata, mhm!

Na ɔkraman no bae, ɔkae sɛ, "abofra, wo dwom yɛ dɛ, to dwom no na mebɛyi wo maame ama wo. " Na abofra no maa ne nne so.

Me kae saa, na wontie, me kae wo saa, na wontie.

Mekaeɛ, ‘Ɛna, momma yɛnkɔ nsuo no atifi, efisɛ ɛhɔ na nkawa nketewa te hɔ’.

Na ɔkae sɛ ɔrekɔ asubɔnten no ase efisɛ asubɔnten no ase na nkawa nketewa te.

Na dɛn na mebɛyɛ na? Aane me maame, Ata, mhm!

Na ɔkraman no faa ne mmoa ntini-ntini no de bɔɔ nsuo no anim, nanso nsuo no gyinaa nea ɛgyinaa hɔ. Na ɔkɔɔ so. Na Kokosaki, ɔkraman no, baa hɔ, na ɔno nso kaa sɛ, "Sing that song for me to hear, that I may get your mother out for you. " Na abofra no too dwom no:

Me kae saa, na wontie, me kae wo saa, na wontie.

Mekaeɛ, ‘Ɛna, momma yɛnkɔ nsuo no atifi, efisɛ ɛhɔ na nkawa nketewa te hɔ’.

Na ɔkae sɛ ɔrekɔ asubɔnten no ase efisɛ asubɔnten no ase na nkawa nketewa te.

Na dɛn na mebɛyɛ na? Aane me maame, Ata, mhm!

Na ɔnso faa ne nsow bɔɔ asubɔnten no anim, nanso biribiara ansɛe. Na ɔnso kɔɔ. Na seesei Anene kwaa nso baa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abofra, dɛn nti na wosuro saa? Su na me te sɛ me bɛyi wo ma wo. Na abofra no too dwom no:

Me kae saa, na wontie, me kae wo saa, na wontie.

Mekaeɛ, ‘Ɛna, momma yɛnkɔ nsuo no atifi, efisɛ ɛhɔ na nkawa nketewa te hɔ’.

Na ɔkae sɛ ɔrekɔ asubɔnten no ase efisɛ asubɔnten no ase na nkawa nketewa te.

Na dɛn na mebɛyɛ na? Aane me maame, Ata, mhm!

Kwakwaa no faa aduru bi de guu nsuo no mu, na ɔde ne ntwitwaa a ɛyɛ nwanwa no bɔɔ nsuo no anim, na nsuo no paapae mmienu; na ɔkaa sɛ, "To dwom no"; na abofra no too dwom no. Na ɔhunuu ne maame ti nhwi; na n’ani sɔree no kakra. Ɔkaa sɛ, "to dwom bio," na ɔtoo dwom no. Na ne maame puee kɔsii ne wa no so. Kwakwaa no kaa sɛ, "kɔ to dwom no. " Na abofra no too dwom no, na ne maame puee kɔsii ne ntwitwaa so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "to dwom bio"; na ɔtoo dwom no. Ne maame tuu ne ho kɔɔ na ɔgyinaa mpoano. Ata, ntwitwaa no, kaa sɛ, "Kwakwaa, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mefa kyerɛ wo aseda? " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Me deɛ, mepɛ biribiara, kɔtɔ ntama a ɛyɛ bruu ne fitaa. " Ata tuu mmirika kɔtɔɔ bi, na ɔde baa Kwakwaa no nkyɛn, na Kwakwaa no de bruu no kataa ne ho, na ɔde fitaa no kyekyeree ne kɔn mu. Ɛno nti na wubehu Kwakwaa no, ne akyi yɛ bruu, nanso ne kɔn mu yɛ fitaa. Ɛyɛ akyɛde a ɔnyaa fi Ata, ntwitwaa no hɔ.

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom betumi agye sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no nso wobetumi akamfo me.
