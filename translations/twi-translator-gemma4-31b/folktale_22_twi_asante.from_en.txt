Wim-onyame Bere a ɔduruu Sun akuraa ase no, ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Wo papa se ɛsɛ sɛ wobɛba me nkyɛn. " Na Sun kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ yie, yɛbɛkɔ, nanso mma mfa biribi ma wo adidi. " Na Ananse kae sɛ, "Ho. " Na Sun kɔɔ na ɔkyeree ɔwan bi. Bere a ɔsan bae no, ɔka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, "Sɛ me papa ba ha a, anka ɔbɛhu nea mereyɛ, sɛ ɛyɛ yie, anaasɛ ɛyɛ bɔne, ɛnyɛ saa nyinaa no, ɔbɛhu; nanso ɛfiri sɛ wamma, na woaba, ɛte sɛ nea papa aba, enti, eyi na ɛwɔ ha, me ɔwan, mebɛkum ama wo adidi. " Na ɔkum ɔwan no na ɔsiesiee no fɛfɛɛfɛ maa nwansena no ɔnni. Na Ananse kae sɛ, "Yɛnkɔ dua bi a ɛhwe ase so. " Na bere a wɔakɔ hɔ no, na Ananse ka kyerɛɛ Sun sɛ, "Wo papa asɛnka bi ayɛ tuntum a ɛwɔ hɔ, na ɔpɛ sɛ wodi saa asɛnka no, na ɔatwe yam bi na ɛda hɔ, na sɛ wobehu sɛnea woka ne din a, ɔbɛfa asɛnka no ama wo, na wɔfrɛ no 'Kintinkyi'. Na sɛ wopɛ sɛ wwerɛ mfi ne din a, mebɛtwa simma tiaa bi ama wo, na mebɛyɛ mpintini simma bi a ɛne no bɛkɔ, na bere a wɔbɔ simma tiaa no ne mpintini sim

Na wɔfiri kɔɔ Ɔsoro-onyame kuro no mu. Na seesei wɔduruu Ɔsram nkuraa no mu de no kɔɔ, na wɔduruu Anadwo nkuraa no mu de no kɔɔ. Wɔ kwan no nyinaa bɔɔ mpintini dwom. Na bere a wɔduruu kuro no akyi na Ananse hunuu ɔbarima bi no, ɔde no kɔɔ sɛ ɔnkɔka nkyerɛ Ɔsoro-onyame sɛ wɔreba. Na Ɔsoro-onyame frɛɛ nhyiamu bi, na Ananse ne wɔn bae bɔɔ nsa biara. Na Ananse de asɛm no kɔmaa Ɔkasafoɔ no sɛ, "Ɔhene no asɛm a ɔde m’asɛm no kɔmaa no, m’abɔ, mede aba. " Na Ɔsoro-onyame kaa sɛ, "Me mma, nea enti a meyɛɛ mma no ne sɛ: mebuu asɛnka a ɛda hɔ no, na mewii yam a ɛda hɔ no nso; mebɛfa asɛnka yi na ama ɔno a ɔhunu yam no na ɔfrɛ no din. M’abofra panyin nti, Anadwo, ma ɔfrɛ no din. " Na Anadwo kaa sɛ, "Na wɔfrɛ no 'Pona'. " Na nnipa no nyinaa kae sɛ, "Ye e e e. " Na Ɔsoro-onyame kaa sɛ, "Me mma a ɔto so abien ne Ɔsram, nti ma ɔka ne din. " Na Ɔsram kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ yam a wɔfrɛ no 'Asante'. " Wɔkaa sɛ, "Ye e e e. " Ɔsoro-onyame kaa sɛ, "Me mma a ɔto so abiɛsa ne Owia, nti ma ɔfrɛ no din. " Afei, na me werɛ fi sɛ meka sɛ na asaw dwom no rekɔ so:

Kintinkyi bomo!

Kintinkyi bomo!

na Ananse retwaa ne ho kɔɔ n’anim, na Owia sɔree gyinaa hɔ na ɔde ne nsa kuraa yam no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, ɛno deɛ, ɛfiri berɛ a me ne me papa nam nkyɛn na me yɛ ketewa no, na ɔka me din kyerɛ me, na mwerɛ; wɔfrɛ no 'Kintinkyi'. " Na mmusuakuw no bɔɔ nsa mprɛnsa, "E! E! E! " Na ne papa sɔree gyinaa hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ, "Wo, Anadwo, woyɛ ɔpanyin, nanso nsɛm a me ka kyerɛɛ wo no, woama wo ho werɛ afi, efisɛ wonsii wo adwene wɔ me nsɛm so; ɛno nti, (it is decreed) nneɛma bɔne nkutoo bɛyɛ wɔ w’abere mu. Na wo nso, Ɔsram, nsɛm a wo ne me nam kyerɛɛ wo no, wanni so, (it is decreed therefore) mmofra nkutoo bɛdi agorɔ wɔ w’abere mu. Na wo nso, Owia, bere a me kae nsɛm kyerɛɛ wo no, wowerɛɛ, wotiee me afotuo, enti wɔayɛ wo panyin; sɛ obiara wɔ asɛm bi a ɔbɛsiesie a, ma wɔnte no wɔ w’abere mu, afie mu nsɛm nkutoo, anwiase asɛm biara nni hɔ. Na fa kwan a measi wo no, na sɛ Ɔsram pɛ sɛ ɔbɛtwa so a, ma kontonkurowie to to ne ho wɔ wo ho, na ama ɔntumi mfa nka wo; na bio nso bere a osutɔre
