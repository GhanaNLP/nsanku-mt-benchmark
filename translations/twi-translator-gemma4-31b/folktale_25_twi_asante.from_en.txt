SƐ OBI SƐE ADE BI A WODE (WƆ AKWANHYIA MU) A, MGYE SIKA MA NO

Wɔka sɛ na ntafoɔ bi a wɔn maame wui na ɔgyaa wɔn. Na afahyɛ bi rekɔ so, na Ata Panin de nkateɛ a ɛsom bo ne sika nketenkete kɔtoo Ata Kuma kɔn mu. Ntafoɔ ketewa no ne ne nnamfoɔ kae sɛ wɔrekɔ asubɔnten no ho akɔhohoro. Bere a wɔrehohoro no, ahom a sika nketenkete a ɛwɔ Ata Kuma kɔn mu no bubu, na nneɛma no hwee nsuo no mu. Ɔhwehwɛɛ sɛnea ɔbɛtumi, nanso na ontumi nnya wɔn. Ɔsan baa fie bɛbɔɔ ne nuabea amanneɛ sɛ, "Sika nketenkete no ayera. " Ata Panin (Ntafoɔ panin no) kae sɛ, "Me nni ho akyinnye, na sɛ ɛyera mpo a, me wɔ biribi a mebɛgye afi wo nsam. " Ata Kuma (Ntafoɔ ketewa no) kae sɛ, "Dɛn na wobɛgye afi me nsam? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Mebɛgye afi wo nsam ɔpono bi, Aserewa nkokɔ nketewa no nkatɛ, nsuo a ɛyɛ ponponponponsa, abɔn biako pɛ, ne ɛlemu a ɛwɔ ɛlemu a wotwitwa no. " Ata Kuma kae sɛ, "Mate.
