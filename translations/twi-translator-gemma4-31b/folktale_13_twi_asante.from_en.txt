SƐNEA AKOKONINI NO NYAA ANO A ƐYƐ NWONWA

AGYA ANANSE, the spider, na ɔte hɔ ne ne mma Afudotwedwe (Yafunu-a-ɛbɛpae), Nyiwankonfwea (Nyam-a-ɛyɛ-kusuu), ne Tikonokono (Ti-kɛse-kɛse). Na Spider no ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ mo kɔ hwehwɛ fig ma me di. " Enti saa mmofra yi firi hɔ; wɔnantee na wɔnantee, na wɔnantee, na wɔkɔɔ fig-tree bi so, na na fig no yɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ, na mmofra no kɔɔ ase. Afudotwedwe (Yafunu-a-ɛbɛpae) ka kyerɛɛ Nyiwankonfwea (Nyam-a-ɛyɛ-kusuu) sɛ, "Woforo ntwitwi no bi. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Aane, sɛ me foro a, me nan bɛpae. " Tikonokono (Ti-kɛse-kɛse) kae sɛ, "Wo, Afudotwedwe deɛ, woforo so. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Aane, sɛ me foro so a, me yafunu bɛpae; woforo wo ara. " Enti Tikonokono (Ti-kɛse-kɛse) foroɔ. Ɔtwee Fig-tree no mma bi na ɔkɔɔ so twitwii ne twitwii, afei ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛbɛyɛ saa? " Wɔn a aka no kae sɛ, "Aah, (yɛbɛyɛ dɛn hu?). " Enti na ɔkɔɔ so twaa wɔn; afei ɔsan bisaa wɔn sɛ, "Ɛbɛyɛ saa? Ɛno nti na mpanimfoɔ ka sɛ, "Sɛ obiara hwe ase, kɔhwɛ no, na wonsere. " Afei kokoɔ no n’adeɛ yɛɛ saa, na ɔsereɛ, nti ne ano bɛyɛɛ nokwaa; kan no na ɔwɔ ano fɛfɛɛfɛ.

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
