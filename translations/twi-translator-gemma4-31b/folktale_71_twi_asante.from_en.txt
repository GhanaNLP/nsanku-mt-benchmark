Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

Wo yɛ te sɛ Ananse mpɔkorɔ.

Na Ananse, nwansena no, retena hɔ bere a Nsia, soro-onyankopɔn no maame, yaree no. Wɔkɔɔ kɔfaa aduro-ɔsafoɔ bi sɛ ɔmmɛhwɛ no. Bere a ɔbaa no, ɔkae sɛ ɔbɛgye sika kusuu pereduan bi (i. e. £8) sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ ne yareɛ no. Afei Ananse kɔka kyerɛɛ soro-onyankopɔn no sɛ, "Saa ɔbarima yi regye wo sika kusuu no kwa, ɔntumi nhwɛ no, enti mnhwɛ no mma wo, na sɛ me nhwɛ no a, me ti nni hɔ. " Ananse faa sika kusuu asuanu bi (i. e. £4), na ɔka kyerɛɛ soro-onyankopɔn no sɛ, "Sɛ mentumi nansa saa yareɛ yi a, mɛma wo ɔgya-kraman a ɛte ase. " Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma daa adi no, Nsia, Nyankopɔn, soro-onyankopɔn no maame, akɔ. Wɔkae sɛ, "Ananse, ɛyɛ dɛn? " Ananse fii saa beaeɛ no, ɔaguan. Afei, nkɔmmɔ ne ne nkaeɛ (i. e. me werɛ fi sɛ) Ananse adamfo kɛseɛ ne Okusi, mpɔtɔ no. Ɔka kyerɛɛ mpɔtɔ no nea ɛkɔɔ so no sɛ, "Ɛno na mehu, enti afei ɛsɛ sɛ wo boa me. " Mpɔtɔ no buae sɛ, "Wɔ ɔkwan bɛn so na mɛboa wo? " (Ananse) buae sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ wode abura tu kɔsi sɛ ɛduru ɔhyɛn a soro-onyankopɔn no te so no ase. " Mpɔtɔ no kae sɛ, "Mɛyɛ ama wo. " Ɛno nti ɔyɛɛ no. Mpɔtɔ no baa bɛka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, "Mewie. " Ananse kɔɔ hɔ na ɔfrɛɛ ne ba, Ntikuma; na ɔtwaa nea ɔde bɛyɛ horn no; na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔnkɔ abura no mu ntena ɔhyɛn no ase, na ɔhu sɛ soro-onyankopɔ

Sɛ wobɛkum Ananse a, mmusuakuo no bɛba asɛe mu!

Sɛ wofa Ananse ho kyɛ a, mmusuakuw no bɛhunu nneɛma a ɛyɛ nnyigyei!

Ntikuma kɔɔ na ɔtenaa agyanmu nkongua no ase, na Ananse firi hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ ɔrekɔ Nyankonpon, ɔsoro ɔnyankopɔn no, hɔ ne pɛ mu. Bere a ɔkɔeɛ no, na Nyankonpon a ɔwɔ soro no asotwefoɔ kyeree no; wɔde no kɔɔ Nyankonpon anim. Afei ɔkasafoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Wo, Kwaku Ananse, wo ka kyerɛɛ Nyankonpon, ɔsoro ɔnyankopɔn no, sɛ sɛ ne maame Nsia wu a, ɔbɛkum wo; enti esiane sɛ Nsia akɔ nti, enti yɛbɛkum wo. " Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ woma me kwan sɛ meka me asɛm. " Nanso asotwefoɔ no kyeree no sɛ wɔbɛkɔ akum no. Ntikuma maa ne nne so: "Bere a wobekum Ananse no, abusuakuo no bɛba asɛe!

Sɛ wofa Ananse ho kyɛ a, mmusuakuw no bɛhunu nneɛma a ɛyɛ nnyigyei!

Wɔkae sɛ, "tie, tie!" Na bio nso abɛn no bɔɔ:

Sɛ wobɛkum Ananse a, mmusuakuo no bɛba asɛe mu!

Sɛ wofa Ananse ho kyɛ a, mmusuakuw no bɛhunu nneɛma a ɛyɛ nnyigyei!

Afei Kontirehene panyin no sɔree kyerɛɛ ɔsoro nyame sɛ, "Saa mmusuakuo yi yɛ wo de, ɔsoro nyame, na Ya, asase maame dedaw. Ɛnnɛ woanya Ananse na worekum no. Ya, asase maame dedaw ka sɛ, ‘Ma no kɔ,’ nti sɛ wogyae no a, ɛbɛyɛ yie. " Afei ɔsoro nyame no maa atuatefoɔ no gyaee no. Ɛno nti na wɔ asɛm bi a, "Wo yɛ nwanwa sɛ Ananse. "

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
