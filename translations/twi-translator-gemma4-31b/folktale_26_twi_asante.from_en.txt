Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐYƐ DƐN NA APUPUO (FRESH-WATER MUSSEL) A ƐMAA N’ASUBƆNTEN NO SEN NO, ƐNO ANKASA DA ASUBƆNTEN NO ANO SO

Wɔka sɛ Okawa, Opitire, ‘Bonko, Shrimp, ne nsuo mu mmoa nyinaa tenaa bom na ɔpɛ bere bi bae na nsubɔnten no nyinaa yowee, maa saa mmoa yi nyaa amaneɛ pii. Wɔda wɔ anhwea so (wɔ asubɔnten no mu). Awiei no wɔkae sɛ, "Saa amaneɛ yi a yɛahu no, yɛnkɔ hɔ nkɔ Nyankopɔn, Ahama so Onyankopɔn, yɛnsu nka no sɛ ɔmfa osuo mma yɛn. " Mmoa no nyinaa kɔe. Na Okawa maa ne nne so (na ɔtoo dwom):

Ɛnyɛ me, Oka' Abena, seesei rekɔ ha, seesei rekɔ hɔ?

Me tu na me gyina me tiptoes so de frɛ wo.

Me yi aburobia beads wɔ me nan so.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔyɛ me yere, Abuya.

Nanso osuo no antɔ. Na Opitire nso bae na ɔtoo dwom:

Ɛnyɛ me, Opitire Abena, seesei rekɔ ha, seesei rekɔ hɔ?

Me tu na me gyina me tiptoes so de frɛ wo.

Me yi aburobia beads wɔ me nan so.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔyɛ me yere, Abuya.

Nanso osuo antɔ no. Na ‘Bonko nso baa na ɔtoo no:

Ɛnyɛ me, ‘Bonko Abena, seesei rekɔ ha, seesei rekɔ hɔ?

Me tu na me gyina me tiptoes so de frɛ wo.

Me yi aburobia beads wɔ me nan so.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔyɛ me yere, Abuya.

Nanso osuo antɔ. Abɔdeɛ no nyinaa baa hɔ suu, na Apupuo (nsuo mu nsuo) nko ara na ɛda hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma me su kakraa bi. " Wɔkaa sɛ, "Firi hɔ, yɛn su no nni mfasoɔ biara, na wode dɛn na wobehu? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma me su. " Nanso wɔbɔɔ no sɛ wɔaka akyerɛ no sɛ ɔnsusu. Nanso nsuo mu nsuo no kae sɛ, "Me bɛsu. " Enti wɔkaa sɛ, "Su kɔ. " Na ɔmaa dwom no so:

Ɛnyɛ me, Apupuo Abena, seesei kɔ ha, na seesei kɔ hɔ anaa?

Me tu na me gyina me tiptoes so de frɛ wo.

Me yi aburobia beads wɔ me nan so.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔyɛ me yere, Abuya.

Na soro-onyankopɔn bɔɔ ne nnyigyaa, kukuru! Abɔdeɛ nyinaa kae, "kɔ, kɔ. " Na bio nso ɔsuu, na soro-onyankopɔn no yɛɛ n’anim kusuu. Na seesei abɔdeɛ no de ne mmati maa no, na wɔkae sɛ, "kɔ bio pɛnkoro. " Na bio nso ɔmaa no so saa refrain no ara. Na seesei soro-onyankopɔn no gyaee, na ɛtɔe, ta! ta! ta! ta! na nsubɔnten no nyinaa yɛɛ nsuo. Seesei eyi (sɛ ɛsɔre no), na twom! nsuo no kɔe. Abɔdeɛ no nyinaa kɔɔ mu. Apupuo (Fresh-water mussel), bere a ɔbɛkɔ nsuo no mu no, wɔbɔɔ no akɔ akyi, na ɔda nsubɔnten no ano. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Apupuo a ɔmaa nsubɔnten no kɔe no, ɔda nsubɔnten no ano. " Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, anaasɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi nso ba me nkyɛn.
