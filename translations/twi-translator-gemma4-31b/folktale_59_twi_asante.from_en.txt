Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ ‘KRAMAN, KRAMAN NO, RENTUMI NSA NO HO WƆ NE KORƆNBƆ HO

There once was

Ɔkraman no Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya Hare, mme yɛ wo ka wɔ saa nam yi ho. " Hare no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na wopɛ sɛ woyɛ ho ka? " Kraman no buae sɛ, "Mekae sɛ, sen sɛ saa mprako yi bɛdi no, mme tua wo ho ka. " Hare no kaa sɛ, "Aane, ɛnyɛ saa! mmeba anɔpa daakye. " Awe! nanso seesei kraman no maa no kɔɔ ne kɔn mu. Afei ɔhwee na ɔdae. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, anɔpa anɔpa, Hare no kaa sɛ, "Kraman, fa saa nam no. " Kraman no buae sɛ, "Ho, Agya Hare, adɛn nti wante nea me ka kyerɛɛ wo nna yi sɛ meyɛ wo ka? " Hare no buae sɛ, "Enti woadi no anaa? " Kraman no kaa sɛ, "Yiw! medii no, efisɛ metɔɔ no. " Hare no kaa sɛ, "Wo, kraman, adwumawɔ a wɔfrɛ no adwumawɔ no rentwa wo nkyɛn da. "

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
