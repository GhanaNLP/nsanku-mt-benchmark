Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ABOSOM, NYAMESUA NKETEWA NO BAE WIASE MU Abosom na ɛbaa wiase no mu. Sɛnea Abosom a wɔfrɛ wɔn nyamesua nketewa no bae wiase no mu.

bi "Asɛm yi deɛ, m-n-tumi nnyina ano, enti mɛfa mmofra yi de ama Ɔsoro-Nyame. "Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma daa adi no, ɔkɔɔ na ɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔsoro-Nyame ho asɛm nyinaa sɛ, "M’abofra bi a m’awo wɔn no, bere a mɛpɛ sɛ mɛdi no, m-n-tumi nnya adeɛ bi nni (efisɛ wɔdi ntɛm na wɔdi pii); ɔkɔm rekum me; enti mepɛ sɛ wɔnfa mmofra no mma na wɔnku, na m-anya adeɛ bi nni. "Ɔsoro-Nyame kaa sɛ, "Ɛno na ɛteɛ? "Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "Mɛreka de ti a emu yɛ fitaa. "Ɔsoro-Nyame paw ntamfoɔ, na wɔkɔɔ na wɔtuu atɛkyɛ kɛseɛ, a wɔde (broken) bottles guu mu; ɔkɔɔ kɔfaa awɔw ne ɔpɔnkɔ na ɔde guu atɛkyɛ no mu, na ɔkataa so. Na afei ntamfoɔ no kɔfrɛɛ mmofra no. Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom betumi agye sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no nso wobetumi akamfo me.
