Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

Mpanimfoɔ no ka sɛ, "Sɛ w’abusua ba, anaa kunu, anaa obiara a ɔwɔ adwuma bi a ɔyɛ, sɛ ɔbisa wo a, wo boa no. "

mpɔtɔ Aso kaa sɛ, "Awe, afuo a me kunu yɛe na me ani da so de aduan bɛma me no, nkurɔfoɔ bi aba adi nea ɛwɔ mu no nyinaa. " Afei osuu na ɔbɔɔ wɔn nsa, ɔkae sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse asamanfoɔ no, nnɔbaeɛ no nyinaa a ɛwɔ w’afuo mu a wogyaeɛ ma me no, nkurɔfoɔ aba adi. "

Kwaku Ananse de ne nne soɔ na ɔtoo dwom:

Gyae (nko)! Meadi nea ɛyɛ me dea nko!

Gyae (nwiinwii), Aso taboos reyi nkekae no.

Gyae nkokodua, madi nko nea ɛyɛ me dea!

Gyae (nkokoduru), Aso taboos rehyew afuw mu nwura. Gyae (nkokoduru), me adidi nea ɛyɛ me dea nko!

Gyae sɛ wonwiinwii, Aso taboos planting na ɛyɛ.

Gyae nkokodua, me adi nea ɛyɛ me dea nko!

Ɛno akyi no, Aso gyinaa hɔ na ne ho dwiriw no. Ɔkɔɔ fie na ɔfrɛɛ Adanko, Hare, sɛ ɔmmra sɛ ɔmmɛtie nsɛm bi a ɛwɔ ne kunu afuo hɔ no. Adanko ne ɔno fii ase. Aso duu afuo no hɔ, na ɔsan kyerɛɛ ne nne na ɔsuu.

Me kunu na na ɔyɛ Kwaku Ananse, na ɔwui na ɔgyaa me ne afuo no, Na nkurɔfoɔ baeɛ na wɔdii aduane a ɛwɔ mu no.

Kwaku Honhom no, Mpɔnkɔn, mebɔ me nkotodwe, me srɛ wo, mukum wɔn ma me.

Kwaku Ananse de ne nne kɔɔ soro:

Gyae nkokoduru, midi nea ɛyɛ me dea pɛ!

Gyae (nwiinwii), Aso taboos reyi nkekae no.

Gyae nkokodua, madi nko nea ɛyɛ me dea!

Gyae (nwiinwii), Aso taboos rehyew (afuw mu nwura).

Gyae nko, meadi nea ɛyɛ me dea nko na.

Gyae sɛ wonwiinwii, Aso taboos planting na ɛyɛ.

Gyae nkokoduru, medidi nea ɛyɛ me dea pɛ; gyae nkokoduru!

Efi bere a Ɔbɔfoɔ bɔɔ no, woahu ɔbaa a ɔmmoa ne kuna?

Gyae (nkokoduru), medidi nea ɛyɛ me dea pɛ; gyae (nkokoduru)!

Abɔmmɔno no kae sɛ, "Me atie awie, yɛnkɔ fie na meaka akyerɛ wo nea ɛfa ho. " Ɔne Aso kɔɔ fie. Abɔmmɔno no kae sɛ, "Ananse kae sɛ, ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ, 'Yɛnkɔ na yɛtwa afuo'; wo kae sɛ worenkɔ; 'Yɛnkɔ na yɛhyew'; na wo kae sɛ worenkɔ; 'Yɛnkɔ na yɛdua'; na wo kae sɛ worenkɔ; enti wogyae su, efisɛ ɔredi nea ɛyɛ ne de, efisɛ, ɛfiri berɛ a ɔbɔfoɔ bɔɔ no, ɔnhuu ɔbea biara a ɔmmoaa ne kunu; enti wogyae su (ɔka), efisɛ wadi n'adeɛ nyinaa. " Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Sɛ obiara reyɛ adwuma biara na ɔbisa wo, boa no. "

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
