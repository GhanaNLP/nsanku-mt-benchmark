Ɛna ɛteɛ tee sɛ ne ba no rebu, ɔsɔree, na ne ntaban nnyigyeɛ no yɛɛ fa! Ɔbaa, ɔkaa sɛ, "Sanguri," na saa dua no a na ɛkame ayɛ sɛ ɛbɛtwa no bɛsan bɛka bom, na nnipa a wɔrebɔ no nyinaa wɔn nsuo. Ɔfrɛɛ ne maame, na ɔfrɛɛ, na ɔfrɛɛ, na ɔfrɛɛ—na mmuaeɛ biara nni hɔ—na afei dua no kasaeɛ bere a ɛbɔɔ fam no, "Brim!" (ɛkaeɛ). Wɔyii ɔkrɔmpon no mma kɔeɛ; wɔde baako maa ɔbaa no, nanso baako a ɛkaeɛ no tuu kɔeɛ na ɛbɔɔ Wawa-dua so. Baako no, wɔpaa no, na wɔde maa ɔbaa no nana no, a ɔde kaa plantain a wɔpaa no (a na ɔredi) ho. Ɛnkyɛreeɛ, na ɔkrɔmpon no baeɛ. Bere a ɔduruu dua a wɔtwaeɛ no, ɔhunuu ne mma no mu baako a ɔte hɔ; ɔbisaa no sɛ dɛn na ɛkɔɔ so, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no nsɛm no nyinaa. Ɔkrɔmpon no kɔɔ ɔbaa no akuraa no mu. Bere a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, na ɔbaa no nana no redi ne mma no mu baako. Ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "ɔbaa no, me kamfo wo. " Ɔfiri ɔbaa no fie mu baeɛ na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ wɔ kuro no akyi.
