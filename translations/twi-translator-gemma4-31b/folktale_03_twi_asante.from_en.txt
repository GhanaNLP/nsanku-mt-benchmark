SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ NYANSA BAA WƆ MMUSUAKUO NO MU

Wɔka sɛ na Kwaku, ananse no, wɔ hɔ, na ɔboaa nimdeɛ nyinaa ano, ɔboaboaa ano wɔ baabi koro, na ɔde guu nkuku mu. Afei ɔkae sɛ ɔbɛforo dua bi so akɔsi hɔ na ama asase so nyansa nyinaa aba awiei.

Enti ɔde kɔɔ ne nkyɛn, na bere a ɔduruu dua no ase baabi a ɔrekɔ de asɛe no, ɔfaa ahama bi, na ɔde kyekyeree no kɔɔ ahama no mu, na ɔde sii n’anim, na ɔde ne ho kɔɔ dua no so. Ɔforo na ɔforoɔ a na ɛnyɛ hwee. Ɔbɔɔ mmɔden bio, ɔsan bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛforo, ɔbɛforo, ɔbɛforo, a na ɛnyɛ hwee.

Afei, ne ba, Ntikuma, a na ɔregyina hɔ no, kae sɛ, "Oh, w’ani awu, ɛnyɛ yiye sɛ wotumi dan kuku no na wode to wo akyi, na wotumi kɔ soro? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Yɛn nni hɔ, wo ne wo nsɛm a atwam. " Afei ɔdanee sɛ ɔbɛkɔ soro bio sɛnea na ɛte kan no, nanso ɔsan nso, ɔnyɛɛ hwee. Afei ɔsusuu ho kɛse, na awiei koraa no, ɔfaa kuku no na ɔde too n’akyi. Ɔde ne ho sii hɔ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ soro, na ɔkɔɔ soro ntɛm, Kra! kra! kra! ɛhɔ na ɔkɔ. Ɔduruu baabi a nkorabata no firi dɔte no mu hyɛɛ aseɛ trɛw, na ɔkae sɛ, "Me, Kwaku Ananse, nyankopɔn ketewa, Afio! Mea na mebɛwu, me ba a ɔyɛ ketewa, ɔyɛ ketewa, ɔyɛ ketewa—na meboaboa nyansa nyinaa ano wɔ baabiako, nanso ebinom nka na mehu, na hwɛ! me ba yi, ɔyɛ nsuo, na ɔkyerɛɛ me. " Afei ɔfaa saa kuku no, na ɛyɛɛ nnyigyei, tintini! na ɔtow guu hɔ, na ɛyɛɛ nnyigyei, tesee! Saa na obiara nyaa nyansa; na obiara a ɔankɔ hɔ wɔ bere mu sɛ ɔbɛfa bi a, ɔyɛ nkwaseasɛm—kyɛw me nka.

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
