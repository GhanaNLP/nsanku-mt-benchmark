Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

WƆ KWAN BIARA SO, BAABI A WUHU SƐ OSEBO ATWITWA, ƆBRƐƐ, ƐHƆ ADANKO ATWAM.

They Ɔpɔnkɔ aboa no Ɔdii ne akyi, na Ɔpɛpɔn no bɔɔ n’amene kɛse sɛ, "Ɔpɛpɔn ababaa, mmua kɔ o! " Ɔpɛpɔn no duruu ne fie, na ɔkɔɔ ne dan mu yeraa, na ɔde ne ti bɔɔ ɔdan no mu biako na ɔfaa mu, nanso Hare no kyeree ne nsa wɔ ne nan mmienu akyi. Na Ɔpɛpɔn no kae sɛ, "Agya Hare, mma me kɔ. " Hare no kae sɛ, "mrenma wo kɔ, mebɛbɔ wo. " Ɔpɛpɔn no kae sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ nbɔ me, na ma me nyamesem nnom, na mehyɛ ntam sɛ kwan biara a wobɛfa so no, mebɛba atutu. " Ɛno nti, sɛ wofa kwan biara a, na wuhu sɛ Ɔpɛpɔn no abɔ hɔ, Hare no afa saa kwan no so.

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
