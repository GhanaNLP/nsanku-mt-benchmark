Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

Ananse

mpɔnkɔnini Ɔpanyin Bere a Ananse kɔɔ mu no, ɔmaa ne dwom no kɔɔ soro: "Ananse, awerɛho, awerɛho, menya atoko biako de kɔgye akokɔ.

awerɛho, awerɛho, Ananse, me faraa akokɔ no de kɔgye ɔnimma bi.

Me faa ngewa no kɔfaa nanom, na me faa nanom no kɔfaa afunu, me faa afunu no na me faa nkurɔfoɔ kurow bi mu.

Obiara kae sɛ, "Wote dwom a Ananse retoto no? " Ananse maa ne dwom no so bio pɛnkoro:

awerɛho, awerɛho

awerɛho, awerɛho, Ananse, me faraa akokɔ no de kɔgye ɔnimma bi.

Me faa ngewa no kɔfaa nanom, na me faa nanom no kɔfaa afunu, me faa afunu no na me faa nkurɔfoɔ kurow bi mu.

Anansem duu wɔ Sorononon anim, na Sorononon bisaa Anansem nsɛm. Ananse kaa sɛ, "Atoko biako na manya, meka sɛ mebɛba nkurow bi mufoɔ; nkurow bi mufoɔ wɔgyina hɔ. " Sorononon no kaa sɛ, "Kamfo no. " (Wɔteɛm,) "E! e! e! " Sorononon no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mema wo sɛ aseda? " Mpɔkorɔ no kaa sɛ, "Mepɛ hwee; nkyeneɛ so nko ara na mepɛ. " Sorononon no kaa sɛ, "Oh, oh! Wei me asɛm, wobɛwu wɔ ohia mu, wo a wokɔkɔɔ pii, nanso wose wopɛ hwee. " Ɛno nti na ohia rennya Anansem.

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
