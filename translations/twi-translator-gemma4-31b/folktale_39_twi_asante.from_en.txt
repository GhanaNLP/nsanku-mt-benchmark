Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ANANTWI BAE KƆƆ WƆN ASASE A ƐWƆ SARE TENTEN SO NO

ɔsono bi kaa sɛ Ɔsono no buae sɛ, "Me ti mebɛgye no. " Ananse faa wedge no, ɔde too hɔ, na ɔde dade hammer no bɔɔ no pɔn! pɔn! pɔn! Ananse san bae ne fie. Afei na ɔwɔ hɔ, bere a ntamgyinafoɔ bi bae na ɔkae sɛ, "Ananse, Elephants no nyinaa papa ka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meba kyerɛ wo sɛ wo adamfo, Ɔsono no, bere a ɔdae no, ɔansɔre bio; enti bra na fa abotan no mu na fa no na fa no to "Ɛno nti na ɔafrɛ me, na ɔafrɛ me sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ megyina nkyɛn. " Bere a na agya-mmoa no bɛhwehwɛ wɔ kwae no mu no, ampa wɔhuu sɛ obi na ɔgyina hɔ, na wɔdwane. Ananse kaa sɛ, " Agya abɔfoɔ, kunkum wɔn ma me. " Agya-mmoa no dwanee, ɛkɔɔ twa-kwae-kuro no mu, na wɔgyaa agya-mmoa a wawu no hɔ, na Ananse faa no kɔɔ hɔ kɔdii no. Saa na agya-mmoa no bɛkɔɔ twa-kwae-kuro no mu, efisɛ bere bi a na agya-mmoa nko ara na wɔte kwae no mu.

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom betumi agye sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no nso wobetumi akamfo me.
