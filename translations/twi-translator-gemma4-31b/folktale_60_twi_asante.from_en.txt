Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

akyiwadeɛ

Na abɔfoɔ bi wɔ hɔ a wɔfrɛ no Kwaku, ne yere Buruwa, na wɔn ho yɛ wɔn dɛm, wɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔyɛɛ asetena foforɔ. Kwaku, abɔfoɔ no, bere a ɔkɔɔ kwaeɛ no, ɔkummɔ eku mprako bi, nanso bere a ɔde baa afie hɔ no, ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Eyi nni hɔ. " Enti ɔkɔɔ na ɔtɔn. Daa, bere biara a ɔkum biribi, na ɛte saa. Nanso ne yere no ankasa anhuu sika no, na ɔbisaa no sɛ, "Wɔmfa sika mma, na wɔmfa nam mma, na ɛyɛ dɛn? " Ɔbuaeɛ sɛ, "Mekɔ, na nnipa ntɔ, na ɛyɛ mmerɛw, mehyɛ mu. " Ɛda bi ɔkummɔ biribi, na ɔde too kwan so, na ɔne ne yere kɔɔ n'akyi sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ no. Afei ɔhunuu sɛ ɔtɔn saa mprako no, ɔde sika no tɔɔ nkwadaa kusu (a wɔfrɛ no dɔnto) na ɔnom. Afei ne yere dan kɔɔ fie. Ɛnkyɛeɛ, Kwaku, abɔfoɔ no, nso baa. Bere a ɔduruu fie no, ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Anwummere, Buruwa. " Ɔbuaeɛ sɛ, "Yiw, Ekudonto. " Ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wobua me saa kwan yi so? " Ɔbuaeɛ sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuaeɛ a ɛfata nea ɛwɔ wo ti mu. " Abɔfoɔ no anka hwee. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma daa adi
