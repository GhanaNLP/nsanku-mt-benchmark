Efuo, black colobus monkey no

Wɔka sɛ na Esono, the Elephant; Kwakuo, the Monkey; Anyinhima, the Putty-nosed Monkey; ne Efuo, the Black-Colobus Monkey te hɔ, na wɔka sɛ wɔbɛkɔ akuraa bi akɔhwehwɛ mmea a wɔbɛware wɔn. Afei wɔtuu kwan. Wɔrekɔ no, wɔhyiaa dɔteɛ bi. Putty-nosed Monkey no kaa sɛ, "Gyina hɔ, me rebisa wo asɛm bi. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Saa akwantu yi a yɛrekɔ so no, hena na ɔyɛ fɛ? " Ɔpono no kaa sɛ, "Efuo, the Black-Colobus Monkey, na ɔyɛ. " Kwakuo (the other monkey) kaa sɛ, "Sɛ ɛte saa a, momma yɛbɔ no, na yɛde no hyɛ dɔteɛ no mu. " Nokwarem no, wɔbɔɔ no, na wɔfaa no na wɔhyɛɛ no dɔteɛ no mu, na wɔkɔɔ so. Afei Colobus Monkey no sɔree, na ɔdii wɔn akyi kwaa, nkakrankakra, na ɔkɔtenaa akuraa no midden-heap no so. Na nnipa no nkae no duruu akuraa no, wɔhunuu mmabaa a wɔte Gyedua dua ase. Mmaabaa no hunuu wɔn. Eyi kaa sɛ, "Me pɛ eyi"; eyi kaa sɛ, "Me pɛ eyi. " Na wɔwaree mmabaa no nyinaa. Afei, na ababaa bi wɔ hɔ, a na ɔkɔɔ afuo no, na ɔaka ne maame sɛ, "Mmeranteɛ no ba a, yi bi de to nkyɛn ma me. " Na bere a ɔsan baa afuo no, ɔbisaa ne maame sɛ, "Me deɛ ɛwɔ he? " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Bere a me kɔe no, na wɔakyekyɛ wɔn dedaw wɔn mu. " Ababaa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ hwee. " Na ɔbɔɔ plantain bi, na ɔrebɛto nsuo no wɔ midden-heap no so.

nwura-ntini

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
