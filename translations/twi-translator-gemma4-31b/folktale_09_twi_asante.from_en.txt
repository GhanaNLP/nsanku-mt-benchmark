Ɔbaa bi woo ne ba; bere a ɔde no kɔɔ (baabiara), ɔkae sɛ, "mɛnkɔ. " Ɛnneɛ ɔkae sɛ, "tena hɔ, wo ne wo nyansa kwan. " Daa bere a ɔde no kɔɔ (baabiara), ɔkae sɛ, "mɛnkɔ. " Ɛnneɛ da bi ɔkae sɛ, "kɔ fa nsuo bɔ ba mma me. " Ɔkae sɛ, "mɛnkɔ. " Na ɔbaa no bɔɔ no na ɔkae sɛ, "Wo nna hɔ, na wobɛhu. " Na abofra no kasae kyerɛe sɛ, "mɛkɔ hwehwɛ 'mehu' ama meahu. " Na ɔkɔɔ kwan no so, na ɔkɔɔ no kɛseɛ, (na ne nan nne a ɛretu no te sɛ) yiridi, yiridi, yiridi. Awiei koraa no, ɔduruu akuraa bi, na ɔhunuu ɔbaa bi a ɔrete hɔ. Bere a ɔhunuu no no, ɔresan. Ɔbaa no kae sɛ, "Me nua, nsan wo, na meahu wo. " Na abofra no kɔɔ anim; na ɔbaa no kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wo ani nsuo ayɛ wo saa? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me maame ne me, na yɛretena hɔ, da biara bere a ɔsɔre no, ɔkae sɛ, 'Wo nna hɔ na wobɛhu,' na ɔbɔɔ me, enti mekae sɛ, mɛba abɛhwehwɛ 'mehu' ama meahu. " Na ɔbaa no kae sɛ, "Me na me yɛ ɔno. " Ɛnneɛ ɔkae sɛ, "Kɔ fie no akyi hɔ tu yams na fa ba; bere a wubɛkɔ aduru hɔ no, nea ɔbɛka sɛ, 'Tu me, tu me, tu a wadi mfeɛ no Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "kɔ tu yɛn na de ba. " Ɔyɛɛ nneɛma nyinaa sɛnea na ɛteɛ kan no, na ɔde aduane no baeɛ, na ɔde too ɔbaa no anim, na ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "ka me din na fa di. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ wo Ɔbaa-nanan. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me kyerɛ me din ankasa. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "W'ankasa din anaa? " Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "Yiw. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ wo Ɔbaa-Mosono. " Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "Fa di. " Abofra no faa dii. Ɔbaa no faa kwan no so, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Mekɔ hwehwɛ obi a ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ me din yɛ Mosono. " Ɔkɔɔ asubɔnten no nkyɛn, na ɔhyiaa Obonto Ya, minnow no, (na ɔbɔɔ no asɛm sɛ), "Wo 'Bonto, wo ma wo! wo 'Bonto, wo ma wo! wo ka kyerɛɛ me nkwadaa abofra sɛ me din yɛ Mosono? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ me. " Ɔbaa no huu sɛ Adwen' (mpataa no) gyina hɔ; ɔbɔɔ no nseɛm sɛ, "Wo Adwen', wo ma wo! ma wo! ma wo! wo Adwen', wo ma wo! ma wo! ma wo! wo ka kyerɛɛ me nkwadaa abofra sɛ me din yɛ Mosono? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ me. " Ɔhunuu sɛ Crab no gyina hɔ.
