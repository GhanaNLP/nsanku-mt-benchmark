Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ ONIPA BI NKYERƐ BAABI A ONIPA FOFORO FI MU BAE NO

Na bere bi na ɔbɛbɔfoɔ bi wɔ hɔ. Bere a ɔsɔre no, na ɔkɔ kwae kɔhwehwɛ abɔmmɔfoɔ sɛ ɔbɛkum, na ama ɔanya bi adi, na ɔanya bi nso atɔn. Afei, da bi no, ɔkɔɔ kwae; ɔtee abɔmmɔfoɔ Kokotee frɛɛ ne nua sɛ, "Kokotee Asamoa e! " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Yiw, me nua, yiw. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Adwumayɛ bere no adu, nti yɛnkɔ dadefoɔ no hɔ, sɛ ɔnyɛ dade no na ɔmfa nsi nnyinasoɔ, na sɛ yɛbɛtwa nnua bi a, yɛbɛtumi atwa. " Bere a abɔmmɔfoɔ no frɛɛ ne nuanom no, ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔhyɛɛ aseɛ na Bere a ɔpanyin no wiee sɛ ɔde ne nsa kaa nnuadua ne nnuadua no so no, ɔmaa ɔkasafoɔ no bɔɔ dade no, na ɔkae sɛ, “Memeneda, ɔbaa anaa ɔbarima, obiara mfa kɔ baabiara. ” Memeneda baeɛ, na anɔpa no ɔbaa bi a ɔyɛɛ nea ɔyɛɛ ka kyerɛɛ ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no sɛ, “Mfa mmoa mmoa, ne nkyene, ne nnuadua, ne baabi a mmoa no bɛtu wɔn honam na wɔde agu hɔ; wo nso, sɛ wode kɔ hɔ a, fa mmoa no na wo de gu so, na mmoa no ba ha, na yɛkyere wɔn, na sɛ ebi gu na wɔkɔfa wɔn honam na wɔde gu so no, bere a mmoa no yɛ wɔn ya na wɔtow wɔn gu no, wɔbɛyɛ nkurɔfoɔ (bio). ” Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kɔɔ hɔ na ɔde ne ho siee. Na ɔtee sɛ wɔrefrɛ sɛ, “Kokotee Asamoa, Memeneda aba, yɛnkɔ. ” Enti wɔn nyinaa baa hɔ. Wɔtuu wɔn honam, na wɔde guu hɔ. Afei, (one of the Pigs,) na ɔyɛ nhabannuru oduruyɛfoɔ no na ɔwɔ wɔn mu, na ɔde ne honam guu baabi foforɔ. Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no hwɛɛ wɔn, na ɔmaa wɔkɔɔ akyiri. Ɔkɔɔ hɔ na ɔde honam no nyinaa guu so, na ɔde mmoa no a wɔabɔ no guu so, na ɔde guu asubɔnten no mu, maa Ɔhene no
