SƐNEA AKƆKONINI, AKOKƆNINI NO, NYAA ANO A ANO YƐ NNAM

AGYA ANANSE, Ananse, ne ne mmofra Afudotwedotwe (Ayafunu-te sɛ-burst), Ciwankonfwea (Thin-shanks) ne Takononkono (Big-head) na na wɔte hɔ. Ananse no ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ moakɔ hwehwɛ borɔdɔma a mebɛdi. " Enti mmofra yi nyinaa kɔeɛ; wɔnam, nantew, na wɔnam, na wɔhunuu nnuaba bi a ɛyɛ dua, na (borɔdɔma no) abere, na mmofra no kɔɔ dua no ase. Afudotwedotwe (Yafunu-like-to-paeeɛ) kyerɛɛ Nyiwankɔnfwea (Thin-shanks) sɛ, "Sɔre na twa wɔn mu bi. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "O, sɛ meforo a, manan bɛbubu. " Takononkono (Big-head) kaa sɛ, "Wo, Afudotwedotwe, wforo. " Ɔkae sɛ, "O, sɛ meforo a, myafunu paeeɛ; wforo wo ho. " Enti Takononkono foro. Ɔtwee Atoko dua no mu mmofra bi kɔɔ so twitwaa na ɔtweeɛ, afei ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛbɛyɛ saa anaa? " Wɔn ahomegyeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Ah, (ɛyɛ dɛn na yɛnim)?" Enti ɔkɔɔ so twitwaa wɔn, afei ɔsan bisaa wɔn bio sɛ, "Ɛbɛyɛ saa? " Na wɔbuaa sɛ, "Mommra mo etire ase na monhwɛ. " Na bere a ɔrebɛto netire ase ahwɛ no, afei netire no bɔɔ fam kae (asɛmfua bi te sɛ) "Kum! " Bere a awotwaa no te sɛ E-to-paeeɛ no, anka ɔbɛsereɛ, ne yafunu paeeɛ wɔ ɔtopae, ta! Na anka ɔbɛdwane akɔka akyerɛ nagya sɛ, nanan abubu na aka ɛban no akyi. Akokɔnini no renoa ne ɔkra, na ɔrekɔtu afɔdeɛ no, na ɔhunuu sɛ ne nan ase ahwe ase na ne anan abubu na aka ɛban no afa bi. Bere a ɔregye naseresɛm no, nano bɛdanee sɛ:

"Bere a ɔreserew no, nano bɛyɛɛ nnam.

Bere a ɔregye fɛw no, nano yɛɛ no den. "

Ɛno nti na Mpanimfoɔ no ka sɛ, "Sɛ obi bɔ fam a, kɔhwɛ no, na ɔnsere. " Akokɔnini no deɛ, ɔyɛɛ nadeɛ sɛɛ, na ɔsereeɛ, ɛno nti na nano bɛyɛɛ nea ɛyɛ fɛ. Kan no na ɔwɔ ano a ɛyɛ fɛ a ɛyɛ fɛ.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
