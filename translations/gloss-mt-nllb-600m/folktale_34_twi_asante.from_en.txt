Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ ONIPA BIAKO NKYERƐ MFITIASEƐ A ONIPA FOFORƆ BI FIRI MU

NA ƐWƆ HƆ BERE BI A ɔbɔfoɔ bi te hɔ. Sɛ ɔsɔre a, na ɔkɔ nwura no mu kɔhwehwɛ agorɔ a ɔde bekum no, na ebia ɔbɛnya bi adi na ebi nso atɔn. Ɛnneɛ da bi a ɔkɔɔ nwura hɔ no, ɔtee sɛ Kokotee, Nwura-ɔPONKYERƐNI no, refrɛ ne busuani sɛ, "Kokotee Asamoa e! " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Aane, me nua, yiw. " Ɔno (ɔPONKYERƐNI a odi kan no) kae sɛ, "Adwuma berɛ adu, enti yɛnkɔ aduruyɛfoɔ nkongua so, sɛ ɔmfa dadeɛ no nsiesie na ɔnsi ano (yɛn twedua nnwinneɛ), na sɛ yɛwɔ sɛ yɛtwa nnua biara a, yɛbɛtumi atwa. " . " Berɛ a Bush-pig no frɛɛ n'anuanom no, ɔbɔfoɔ no kotoo ehuntahunu, na ɔtee nkɔmmɔdie no nyinaa. Afei Bush-Pig no onuabarima bisaa ne nuabarima Bush-Pig no sɛ, "Na akuraa bɛn na yɛbɛkɔ, ama dadeɛ abɔ? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Yɛbɛkɔ akuraa a wɔfrɛ no Across-the-asuwa no. " Na ɔse, "Da bɛn? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Monday. " Na ɔbɔfoɔ no ante saa nhyehyɛeɛ yi nyinaa, na ɔfirii kwan kɔɔ fie. Mprempren, bere a ɔbaa fie no, ɔkaa nsɛm no kyerɛɛ Across-the-asuwa odikro, ɛne nea ɔtee bere a ɔkɔɔ nwura no mu no. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ akuraa no panin no sɛ, "Momma mmofra no nkɔtwa nnyina mma wɔn, na Ɛdwoada no, bosom-mprako no bɛsakra wɔn ho (bɛyɛ nnipa) na wɔabɛba no, yɛbɛfa wɔn na yɛde wɔn akyekyere nnyina no. " Mmɔfra no kɔtwa nnyina no, na wɔde baeɛ. Ɛdwoada no, ɔpanyin a ɔwɔ akuraa hɔ no kɔka kyerɛɛ akuraa tomfoɔ no sɛ wɔmmɔ no dade stapo. Na tomfoɔ no bisaa Ɔpanyin-panyin no sɛ, Na saa dade stapo dodoɔ yi nyinaa, a wose ɛsɛ sɛ mebɔ no, dɛn na wode bɛyɛ? Na ɔka kyerɛɛ no amaneɛbɔ no, sɛnea ɔbɔfoɔ bi akɔ nwura mu, na asan aba abɛbɔ amanneɛ sɛ Ɛdwoada mmoadoma bi bɛdan wɔn ho ayɛ nnipa, wɔ ahyɛdeɛ sɛ wɔmmra ne dɔnka sɛ wɔnni nnwinnadeɛ ayɛ. Ɛnkyɛree na tomfoɔ no dwanee kɔboroo dade stapo no. Bere a ɔhene no de ne nsa too nnyina ne staplosa dade mma no so wiei ara pɛ, ɔbɔɔ kurow no mu nteɛmfo no dawuru sɛ, Ɛdwoada, sɛ ɛyɛ ɔbaa anaa ɔbarima, obiara nkɔ baabiara. Ɛdwoada dui, na anɔpa no, aberewa bi ka kyerɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no sɛ, Kɔ na monyam ɛmo, ne nkyene, ne gyeene, ne baabi a mmoadoma bɛtutu wɔn nwoma ahoroɔ ato hɔ no, wugye to hɔ anaa? enti, bere a wokɔ hɔ no, fa ɛmo no dwira wɔn so, na bere a mmoadoma no ba ha, na yɛkyere wɔn, na ɛsɛ sɛ ebinom dwane kɔfa wɔn nwoma ahoroɔ, na wɔhyɛ, bere a ɛmo no apira wɔn, na wɔtow gu no, wɔbɛyɛ nnipa (aga). Ɛneɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔeɛ na ɔde ne ho kɔsiee hɔ. Na ɔtee sɛ wɔrefrɛ sɛ, Kokotee Asamoa, Ɛdwoada adu, yɛnkɔ. Wɔbaa no nyinaa. Wɔtwee wɔn nwoma ahoroɔ no, na wɔde sii hɔ. Afei, (Mprako no mu baako, ) a ɔyɛ ɔyaresafoɔ a ɔhwɛ habannuro so, na ɔka wɔn ho, na ɔde ne wedeɛ no kɔtoo baabi foforɔ. ɔbɔfoɔ no hwɛɛ wɔn, na wɔgyaa wɔn ma wɔkɔɔ. Ɔkɔɔ fa nwoma ahoroɔ no nyinaa, na ɔde ɛmo a na wɔde afra mu no guu so, na ɔde guu asuwa no mu, na nsuo no de wɔn kɔe. Nhabannuru ho ɔbenfo deɛ, nanso ɔde too nkyɛn wɔ ahomegyeɛ no ho no, ɔbɔfoɔ no anhu hwee. ɔbɔfoɔ no baa fie, na Ɔhene nso frɛɛ nnipa a wɔahyia hɔ atwa dwetɛfoɔ no abantenten no ho ahyia na ɔmaa wɔn baa ne fie. Bere a saa nnipa yi baa Ɔhene fie no, wɔbisaa Ɔhene no sɛ, Dɛn nti na wofrɛɛ yɛn? Ɔhene no kaa sɛ, Na woyɛ me mmarima, na wuguan kɔtenaa baabi foforɔ; kɔsi da a wasan aba no, ɛno ne sianteɛ a meka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ woba, ɛfiri sɛ merennye wo ho kwan sɛ wobɛkɔ bio. Saa mmoadoma no ka kyerɛɛ Ɔhene no sɛ, Nea wokaeɛ no, yɛate, nanso yɛnim sɛ Ɔbɔadeɛ no hu. nter baeɛ na ɔkaa yɛn nyinaa ho asɛm kyerɛɛ wo, nanso ɛno mfa ho, yɛn ne wo bɛtena abɔmu; nanso yɛnim sɛ nea woka no yɛ atoro; nevertheless, that doesnt matter; yɛda saa ɔbɔfoɔ no ase. Enti, yɛn ne wo bɛtena ase; nanso adeɛ baako na ɛwɔ hɔ a yɛhyɛ ho kwan, ɛne sɛ, wobɛda yɛn mfitiaseɛ adi; anaasɛ sɛ wadesuadeɛ biara bɛda yɛn mfitiaseɛ adi; sɛ ɛba saa a, yɛbɛpaapae yi mu, wo abusuakuo no, na yɛakɔ. Mprempren, wɔ berɛ a Ɔhene kɔfrɛɛ wɔn no, dunsini no ne afoforo bi dwanee. Na dunsini no kɔgyee ne wedeɛ dwanee, nanso ahomegyeɛ no sanee. Na eyi yɛ sɛ wɔanhu wɔn nwoma ahoroɔ. Ɔhene no penee tebea a mmoadoma no de sii hɔ no so, na wɔne mmoadoma no tenaa ase yie. Mprempren na akuraa no mu mmarima no aware mmoadoma no mu mmea bi, wowoo mmofra. Ɛbaa no, da bi a na mmoadoma no mu baako ne nkuraasefoɔ no mu baako de wɔn nkuturu reko. Ɛhɔ ara na akuraaseni no ka kyerɛɛ aboa kɛseɛ no sɛ, Yi wo ho fi hɔ, aboa a ɔte sɛ wo a woyɛ Bush-pig adwumakuo no mu. Ansa na ɔreka saa no, na mmoadoma no nyinaa aniwa abere, na wɔkɔɔ Ɔhene fie kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Saayɛ kwan a ɛbɛtumi ayɛ yiye a yɛaka akyerɛ wo no aba ampa, enti ɛdeɛn na wobɛyɛ? Ɔhene no maa wɔn kɔfrɛɛ nnipa no a na ɔde mansotwe no aba no. Ɔhene no hwɛɛ asɛm no mu yie na ɔmaa atɛmmuo sɛ mmoadoma no ntu sɔboɔ no, ɛfiri sɛ (ɔkaa sɛ) na ɛyɛ berɛ tenten a wɔmmra na wei nso, na ɛyɛ dwumadie a ɛdi kan a obiara anka asɛm biara ankyerɛ wɔn (wɔ wɔn mfitiaseɛ ho). Nanso mmoadoma no kae sɛ, Yɛrennye ntom. Na Ɔhene no kae sɛ, Worentie, na wosusuw sɛ nea ɔbarima yi kae no yɛ atoro, ɛnyɛ Bush-pigs? Ɛnna mmoadoma no kae sɛ, O, yɛate. Ɛneɛ na mmoadoma no ne nsuo no so nnipa no koo. Mmoadoma no sɛee akuraa no, ma nnipa bɛyɛ du pɛ na wɔkaa wɔn. Na wɔsrɛɛ mmɔborɔhunu, na wɔka kyerɛɛ mmoadoma no sɛ wɔn ho nni asɛm. Mmoadoma no tiee, na wɔbɔɔ nnipa no amanneɛ sɛ, asɛm bi a wɔaka dada no, ɛnyɛ den sɛ yɛbɛte aseɛ, na sɛ wo ne yɛn bɛbom atra a, yɛato kasamfonin a ɛtwe adwene kyerɛ yɛn mfitiaseɛ no nyinaa ano hye. Sɛ wo dwene anaa woka bi bio (again), ɛnde (yɛbɛbisa wo) sɛ fa wani kyerɛ yɛn saa baabi a ɛyɛ den pa ara a yɛfiri baeɛ yi, na ama yɛasan ɛhɔ. Na nnipa no kaa sɛ, Yɛntumi nyɛ adeɛ a ɛte saa bio da. Nea yɛayɛ yi ama yɛn abusuakuo no asɛe, yɛrenyɛ saa bio. Nti, wɔbɔɔ amansan dawubɔ sɛ, wɔbɔɔ dawuro bɔɔ nsunsuansoɔ sɛ, ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara ka nea ɔfiri, na ɛnyɛ saa a, nsɛm a wɔada no adi no bɛma kuro no asɛe. Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi ayɛ wo nokware, ne ahomegyeɛ a wubetumi akamfo me (wɔ nasɛmka ho).
