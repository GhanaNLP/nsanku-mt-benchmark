Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ OKA NAM NO DUA YƐ KƐKƐ, NA 'KƆBƆ NAM' NO MENEWA YƐ NSENSANEE

NA ƐWƆ HƆ BERE BI A ɔbaa bi a ɔyɛ Ɔhemmaa, na ɔnni abofra, na ɔkɔɔ ɔsorofoɔ nketewa no mu baako nkyɛn wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ onyankopɔn bɛtumi ama no awo abofra. Na ɔmaa no bi, nanso ɔbɔɔ no amanneɛ sɛ, Ɛnsɛ sɛ abofra no di nam biara gye sɛ oguanom mmerɛbo, efisɛ ne honam mpo yɛ den. Afei, da bi (bere a ɔbaa no woo abofra no akyi) maame no kɔɔ afuw mu, na ɔde abofra mmerɛbo maa nafenaa, Ɔkra, sɛ ɔnnoa na ɔmfa mma ne abofra. Mprempren Ɔkra faa no, ɔnoaa aduane no, na mmom ɔkɔgyee odwan no nam ankasa bi, na ɔde bi guu mu (berɛboɔ no ka ho). Ɔhemmaa no babarima no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Maame kae sɛ me akyiwadeɛ ne nam ankasa, na woahyɛ bi ahyɛ me mu, na sɛ me di a, me bɛwuwu. " Ɔkra buae sɛ, "Aane, mehyɛ bi ahyɛ mu, na sɛ wobɛwu a, wu. " Maame babarima no buae sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ me di na ama meku? " Na ɔkɔɔ dan no mu kɔgyee ne maame silk ntama kɔkɔɔ no. Na abofra no de kwan no sii hɔ, na Ɔkra dii nakyi. Ɔkra suro sɛ sɛ ne mpena baa bɛhyia no a, ɔbɛkɔ ɔhaw mu. Na wɔrekɔ no, Ɔhene Yawa abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ɔkra! " Ɔkra kaa sɛ, "Aane! " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sɛ mema wo nkra a, wobɛka anaa? " (i.e. wode bɛma). Ɔkra kaa sɛ, "Mebɛka, mebɛka, ansa na mewu, mebɛka, ansa na mewu.

Bere a me kɔɔ hɔ dada no, bayere bi bɛgyinaa hɔ.

Mprempren meba na kokote no da hɔ.

Kwae mu prɛko a wɔfrɛ no Asamoa, Kuruti Amoa, na ɔda hɔ.

Me nni Gyanoa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma biara, Boafo Denkye, Ɔpanin, kosi da a me nkwa asa.

Ɔka sɛ, momma wɔnkum odwan mma no nni, momma wɔnkum anomaa mma no nni.

Ɔse, Owura, anɔpa pa.

Na ɔse, 'Ya'ku.'

Ɔse, Owudifo no se ɔma wo anɔpa.

Ɔse, 'Yiw, Abrawo.'

Ɔka sɛ: 'Me nuabea, worekɔ he na mayɛ wo nkra?'

Ɔka sɛ, 'Mekɔ nananom akuraa, Sɛkyerɛ Gyamase.'

Ɔse, Dɛn na aba nana akuraa ase, Sɛkyerɛ Gyamase, a akyene nna da? Oboama agya, Agyei Ampɔn; agya akɔ ɔko anaa agya akɔ dwadie sɛ ɔrehu sɛ demmire tenten no ahwe ase afra wuram nhama mu?

Mmienu nsan mma bio, Ntim Sekyere ne Kwakye nsan mma bio'.

Ɔse, O, Ɔmoawa, Boama, woadi nkɔmmɔ na woadi nkɔmmɔ, gyae na ma me nni nkɔmmɔ kakra, wo aniwa tu wɔ nsuo mu.

Wo nana, Mpampinam, kɔɔ mpataayie, na ɔkyeree ɔtwee kɛse pa ara.

Kwao Adu Dakowa, Bantama Mpedu Okyere, Adwoa Kɛseɛ, Odum Abena ne ne fetish (suman) a wɔfrɛ no "you walk in vain".

Adwoa e, Adwoa e, asubɔnten Kobi banana, Tontoroni ya, a ewiase yɛ ne dea. "

Na afei abofra no hyiaa ne maame. Na maame no kae sɛ, "Wei, me abofra a mehyia wo yi, ɛwom sɛ megyaa no wɔ fie nanso, bɔne bɛn na aka no sɛ ɔreguan, yiridiyiridiyiridi? " Ɔhemmaa no hyɛɛ ne nkoa a wɔtaa nakyi no sɛ wɔnkyere no mma no. Nanso wɔantumi annye no; na ɔtuu mmirika kɔɔ so hwee asubonten no mu, na Ɔkra (afenaa) no nso hwee mu. Bere a woduu hɔ no, na ɔhemmaa no ba no adan Oka mpataa a ne dua kɔkɔɔ wom; Na Ɔkra nso adan Ɔkɔbɔ nam a nnsenene wɔ nafono so. Oka no dua kɔkɔɔ ne ntoma kɔkɔɔ a Ɔhemmaa Ɛna ba no hyɛe a ɔhwee nsuo no mu, na nnsenene a ɛwɔ Ɔkɔbɔ no nsawa so no yɛ afenaa Ɔkra no nsonsoo.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
