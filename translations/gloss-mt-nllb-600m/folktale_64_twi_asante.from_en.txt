Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐBƐYƐƐ DƐN NA KWAKU ANANSE, ANANSE NO, KƆƆ APRANNAA SO

ANANSE O! ɔwaree Nsia babaa, Ewiem-onyankopɔn maame, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ Niamey (Ewiem-onyankopɔn) sɛ, " Ɛda a wo maame, Nsia, bɛwu no, bra bɛka ɛda a wɔayiyi ama ayi amanneɛ no kyerɛ me. "

Ɛbɛyɛ nnansa akyi no, wɔbɛka kyerɛɛ no sɛ Nsia, Ewiem-onyankopɔn maame no awu. Seesei, Kwaku Ananse mogya-abusuabɔ ne Berekuo, King Jay, Ɔkwakuo, Kwaadu, ne Odwan, Odwan. Afei Kwaku Ananse ne wɔn kasae sɛ, Wɔaba abɛka ayi amanneɛ bi akyerɛ me, enti ɛsɛ sɛ mo ne me fa. Enti Kwaku Ananse maa wɔde nkra kɔmaa Ewiem-onyankopɔn sɛ ɔreba dwumadie no ase. Ɛnna ɔsoro-hene no boaboaa nadesuadeɛ nyinaa ano, sɛdeɛ Omanhene asɛmbli teɛ. Na Kwaku Ananse se, "Sɛ anwummere dwo a, me bɛba. " Ananse no yɛɛ kotokuo bi, na emu na ɔde Berekuo, King Jay, Ɔkwakuo, Kwaadu, ne Odwan, Odwan, kotokuo no guu nabatiri so, de ne ntoma kyekyeree so. Afei ɔkasa kyerɛɛ abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no sɛ, "Sɛ me duru ɔsoro-anyame hɔ a, na ɛsɛ sɛ mosu. " Bere a ɔduruu ɔsoro-anyame no anim no, "Berekuo" no maa ne nne so sɛ:

"Kurukusu, kuw! kuw! kuw!

Kurukusu, kuw! kuw! kuw! nI. "

Na Kwaadu maa ne nne so sɛ:

"Kakum! "

Na Odwan maa ne nne so sɛ:

"Me! "

Ɛno ne Kwaku Ananse awerɛhow. Nnipa no nyinaa kaa sɛ, "Woayɛ yie, ha yɛ ɔbarima bi a ɔtumi su na ɔdi awerɛhoɔ wɔ akwan ahodoɔ mmiɛnsa so. " Bere a ɔwiee (nawerɛhow no), ɔsoro-anyame no hwehwɛɛ ofie fɛfɛ a ɔbɛtena. Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔsoro-anyame no yɛɛ aduan pa maa Kwaku Ananse. Bere a Kwaku Ananse huu saa aduane yi, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ, "Merennya bi mma Berekuo, merennya bi mma Ɔkwakuo, merennya bi mma Odwan, ɛfiri sɛ midii mmuada berɛ tenten. " Enti Kwaku Ananse dii, wamma ne blood-relatives biara. Ɛnneɛ, ɔne wɔn tenaa so kɔɔ so, na ɔsoro-anyame no kaa sɛ, "Yɛbɛkura da no ayiyɛ no. " Nanso Odwan kaa (maa Kwaku Ananse) sɛ, "Ɛkɔm rekum me, me nkɔ;" Ɔkwakuo ne "Berekuo" kaa adeɛ koro no ara. Afei ɔsoro-anyame no kɔɔ so tenaa nnipa a wɔahyia no mfinimfini, na ɔmaa wɔn bɔɔ Kwaku Ananse amanneɛ sɛ, ebia adepa a ɔyɛeɛ da a ɛtwaa mu no, ɔbɛsan ayɛ bio, efiri sɛ ɔne ne mpanimfoɔ no nyinaa hyiaeɛ (bɛtiee no). Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "Mente yie. " Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, "Woama maniwa atɔ me aniwu. " Na "Berekuo, ne Ɔkwakuo, ne Odwan se, "Yɛn nso, woama yɛn kɔm de yɛn nti, yɛn ne wo bɛtetew yɛn ho afiri abusuakuw no ho. " Enti Ɔkwakuo ne "Berekuo kɔɔ nwura mu, nanso Odwan, Odwan, na ɔtenaa fie. Ananse no huri kɔtoo aprannaa no so, na ɛno na ɛmaa ne ho yɛɛ petee. efisɛ na ne ho fɛre no.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
