SƐNEA BABADUA NO NYAA NSƆMU (WƆ NE DUA SO)

Ɔbaa bi (bere bi) nyinsɛnee, na ɔkɔɔ abosom nkumaa no mu biako nkyɛn kɔbisabisaa no (abofra no ho asɛm). Na onyankopɔn kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Ɛbɛyɛ sɛ bere a wobɛsoa abofra no a, ɛbɛwu, nanso mebɛyɛ nea metumi biara de ama wo, nanso adeɛ a ɛsɛ sɛ meka kyerɛ wo ne sɛ, abofra no yɛ Ɔhemmaa bi abofra wɔ soro ne Niamey, Ewiem-onyankopɔn, na sɛ woreso a, mma ɛnnyɛ hwee, nanso sɛ wosan sɔre a, ɛsɛ sɛ wohohoro, ngo, na wofa, na woto akonnwa so. th ɔwoo no. (Like all) mmofra in Anansesɛm mu no, ɛnkyɛ na wanyin. Na wɔfrɛ no abofra akosua dentaa. Sɛnea obosom kumaa no kyerɛɛ no, ɛna no sɔre a, ɔhohoroo no, de ngo guu no, na ɔnoaa biribi maa no sɛ ɔmmɛdi, na afei ɔkɔɔ nafuo. Ɛbaa da bi a ɔnni berɛ a ɔbɛnoa biribi, ɔmaa nafenaa, Ɔkra, ahyɛdeɛ sɛ, Merekɔ afuo, enti noa bi na fa ma Akosua Dentaa nni. Na maame no kɔeɛ. Mprempren, bere a Ɔkra wiee borɔdeɛ no, ɔkae sɛ, "Akosua Dentaa, bra ha na fa toto saa fufu_ yi, wo ne wo Queen-Mother asɛm no. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Merenkɔ. " Ɔkra kae sɛ, "Bra, wo ne wo ahantan. " Na Akosua Dentaa sɔre kɔeɛ; ɔkɔgyee pestle no; ɔfa totoe saafufu no. Bere a ɔwieeɛ pɛ no, ɔkɔdaa dan no mu, faa ne ntoma, na ɔkataa netire so. Ɔkra kɔɔ (ne akyi), ɔkaa sɛ, "Denta. " Obuaa kwan. Ɔyii ntoma no firii netire mu, Akosua Dentaa wuiɛ; ɔde ntoma no sii nanan so. Ɛnkyɛreeɛ, maame no firii afuo baeɛ. Bere a ɔbaa no, ɔanhwehwɛ nabofra no, na mmom ɔkɔɔ sukyea ho kɔwiee aduannoa. Ɛnna ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔkra, Denta wɔ he? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Nana, ne aduane koraa a meyii ato hɔ no, ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ. " Ɔmaame no kaa sɛ, "Mate. " Ɛnna maame no de ahyɛdeɛ kɔɔ dan no mu sɛ ɔrekɔfa ntoma akɔpɛ ne abofra no wɔ abɔnten. Bere a ɔkɔɔ dan no mu no, na adeɛ bi a ɛyɛ kusuu da hɔ wɔ ebuo mu. Ɔmaa ne ntoma so, na ɔhunuu sɛ Akosua Dentaa awu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔkra, woka sɛ Denta afiri abɔnten, na seesei ha deɛ, wawuo? " Ɔkra kaa sɛ, "Nokuru mehunuu no da. " Ɛneɛ ɔbaa no kyekyeree ntoma abɔso kyekyeree nasene, de ne dead) abofra no kɔɔ nakyi, na ɔne no kɔɔ abɔnten so, na ɔsu; na akuraa Etire baa hɔ bɛkaa sɛ, "Fa wo abofra no (firi nakyi) na yɛnkɔsie no. " Ɔbaa no buae sɛ,

"Na me ne me abofra akosua dentaa, na me nyinsɛnee no, me ne no kɔbisa nsuo-gazer no.

Wɔka sɛ, 'Ɔhemmaa Ɛna ba'.

Mepɛ sɛ obi kɔka sɛ, Queen Mother no abofra, Siankwa Adamowa ba, wei, me abofra no, merensee no wɔ ha. "

Na ɔfiri hɔ, kɔɔ akuraase bi, na ɛduruu hɔ no, Etire akuraa no so panyin no kaa sɛ, "yi abofra no (wo akyi) na bra me nsie, ɛfiri sɛ yɛhyɛ (adeɛ a ɛte saa) mmara. " Nanso ɔbaa no kaa sɛ:

"Na me ne me abofra akosua dentaa, na me nyinsɛnee no, me ne no kɔbisa nsuo-gazer no.

Wɔka sɛ, 'Ɔhemmaa Ɛna ba'.

Mepɛ sɛ obi kɔka sɛ, Queen Mother no abofra, Siankwa Adamowa ba, wei, me abofra no, merensee no wɔ ha. "

Na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ bio. Ɔnam, nantew, nantew, nam kwaebirentuo bi mu, na mprempren yi, abofra no ho ayɛ no fĩ, na ɔyii no guu akyi, na ɔsan kɔɔ fie. Etire akuraa no mu panin no bisaa no sɛ, "Ɛhe na abofra no wɔ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mede no kɔtoo hɔ. " Etire akuraa no mu panin no kaa sɛ, "Wo ho nni fɛre, sɛ woama kwan wɔ ha a, anka yɛbɛsie no wɔ nsaman-saman no kwaeburuwutu mu, ɛno na ɛfata. " Saa na mpanimfoɔ kyerɛ, "Sɛ wo abofra wu a, fa "moderation" di awerɛhoɔ. Ɛfiri sɛ, saa ɔbaa no yɛɛ saa, na ɔtow nabofra no guu hɔ bere a na ɛnsɛ sɛ ɔyɛ saa. " Na afei ɔbaa no reka sɛ, "Ɔkra yi deɛ, fa no kɔto no mɔta mu na bɔ no ma me. " Ɛneɛ nkuraasefoɔ no kɔɔ hɔ (baabi a Ɔkra wɔ no, na ɛyɛ nnyigyei a ɛbɔ Pu! Pu! Pu! na wɔbɔɔ no ma no bɛyɛɛ doro koraa. Na afei na "the ayamye so" baako" no wɔ hɔ; bere a wɔbɔɔ no no, na ɛyɛ den, na bere a wɔbɔɔ no no, ɛtuu mmirika bɛbataa Babaadua no ho. Ɛno nti na Babaadua no wɔ apɔ (wɔ ne dua so), kan no na ɛyɛ trɔm, trɔm, kyerɛ sɛ, trɔm.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi ayɛ wo nokware, ne ahomegyeɛ a wubetumi akamfo me (wɔ nasɛmka ho).
