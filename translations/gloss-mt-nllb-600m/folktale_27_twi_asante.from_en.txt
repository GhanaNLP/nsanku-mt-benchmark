Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

WƆ ABUSUAKUO MU NO, ONIPA NEA BIRIBIARA NNI MU KORAA NNI ADWENE MU

NA ƐWƆ HƆ BERE BI A ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔwoo mmofra baanan. Wɔn mu panyin no, na ɔyɛ kwasea kɛkɛ. Na ɔnnim sɛnea ɔbɛyɛ biribi baako pɛ, enti ne anuanom ne ne nuammaa kae sɛ, "Yɛntumi ne no ntena, enti momma ɔnfiri fie. " Enti, mepa kyɛw sɛ mede asɛmfua (wɔ mo mpanimfoɔ biara mbaeɛ) aka sɛ, saa kwasea yi kɔsii atenaeɛ bi maa ne ho na ɔtenaa mu. Saa atenaeɛ no, na biribiara nni hɔ gye sɛ anwea. Na ɔyɛɛ asensene bi de dii nani. Saa aberewa no (ie. ɛna) ne ne mmofra no na wɔaka akyi. Ɔsɔree da bi kɔɔ afuw hɔ, na ɔhunuu abofra bi a ɔte bon mu wɔ dua bi mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Hwan na ɔwɔ hɔ? " Nanso abofra no ammua no. Ɛnna ɔbaa no kɔɔ hɔ, na ɔnyaa abofra no ho hwɛbea pa, faa ne nsa, na ɔkaa sɛ, Yɛnkɔ fie. Ɛnna abofra no sɔre dii nakyi baa ne fie. Ɔbaa no srɛɛ ne mmofra no sɛ, Hwɛ abofra fɛfɛ bi a menyae no, ma me sekan no na metwa ne ti nhwi tenten. Mprempren ɔbaa no nnim sɛ abofra no yɛ nnipa nketewa no mu biako, na ɛno nti na ne ti nwi no ayɛ mmerɛw saa no. Enti ɔgyee Sekkan no na ɔtwaa ne etire so ti nwi no tow gui. Bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi (i.e. ) anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, wɔkae sɛ ɔnni, ɔkae sɛ ɔrennidi; na wɔbisaa no sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ asɛm? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Sɛ wompɛ sɛ wode wo ho bɛhyɛ ɔhaw mu a, san fa me etire no ti nwi no san bra na fa kura mu bio. " Ɛneɛ, ɔbaa no ne ne mmofra no kɔɔ hɔ kɔfaa amamfoɔ a ɛkamfam rubber-tree no bi de hyɛɛ fairy no etire so, na wɔde ne ti nwi no bɔɔ so. Mmoatia no faa ne nsateaa ahodoɔ no faa ne ti nwi mu, tuu, na wɔtow gui. Bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, na ɛyɛ: "Momma me etire ti nwi. " Bere a nneɛma no anhu bio no, na ɛyɛ: "Momma me etire ti nwi. " Na afei wɔanyinyin bɛyɛɛ nketenkete paa (wɔ adwendwen ne dadwen mu). Da bi, abofra a ɔyɛ kwasea no kaa sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhunu ne maame. Ɔbaa no, na nmaame ne ne anuanom ne ne nuammaa so atew. Ɔse, "Ɛbɛyɛ dɛn na woayɛ nketenkete saa? " Ɛkame ayɛ sɛ maame no bɛkaa neenti ankasa nea enti a na wɔn ho ayɛ nketenkete no kyerɛɛ no, bere a ne mmofra no kae sɛ, "Nka nkyerɛ no, eyi ne kwasea, bere bɛn na ɔnyaa adwene? " maame no kae sɛ, "Mɛbɛka. " Bere a ɔka kyerɛɛ no no, ɔkae sɛ, "O, fa mmoatia no mmra na mema no mefa no nkɔ me akuraa. " Maame ne anuanom ne nuammaa no faa mmoatia no kɔe. d ne etire ti nwi na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ kwasea no akuraa hɔ. Bere a wɔduruu hɔ no, nakuraa ho no, ɔhyɛɛ mmoatia no sɛ, "Kɔ tena masensene so na gye wo ani kakra. " Ɔkɔɔ tenaa so, na ɔdɔe no, na ɔmaa ne dwom yi so:

"Maame woo me, abofra kwasea, Hurotom.

Agya woo me, abofra kwasea, Hurotom.

Maame woo me, kwasea kɛkɛ, Hurotom. Maame woo me, kwasea kɛkɛ, Hurotom.

Maame woo me, abofra kwasea, Hurotom.

Maame woo kwasea kɛkɛ, Hurotom. "

Mmoatia no bɔɔ agoru kɔsii sɛ ɔmrɛeɛ, ɛnna abofra kwasea no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔ gye wo etire ti nwi no. " Ɔne Mmoatia no kɔduruu fie, ɔse, "Fa me etire ti nwi no mmra na fa si ananmu na fa ma me kɔ. " Mmoatia no nso kae sɛ, "Wo nso, me anwea a woatia so yi, ɛsɛ sɛ wode nsɛnkyerɛnne no nyinaa kata so ansa na me aka wo etire ti nwi no. " Mmoatia no bɔɔ ne ho. fi dwumadi no mu; Ansa na ɔakata nsɛnkyerɛnne no mu baako so (a nanan yɛe) no, tiatia so, ɔyɛɛ foforo. Seesei, ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛnea ɔbɛtumi biara no, wantumi ansɛe nanammɔntena no nyinaa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya, me kɔn dɔ manso biara nni hɔ, enti mema wo maame kwan, na me nso, fa me ho ma me, na ma me kɔ. " Saa abofra no a na ɔyɛ kwasea no kaa sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ gyae, na me nso mefa me maame afiri wo tumi mu. " Seesei maame no baeɛ na ɔkotow kaa sɛ, "Meda wo ase, na seisei (mehu sɛ) adeɛ a ɛte sɛ kwasea nni abusuakuo mu. " Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, sɛ wowɔ mmofra, monyɛ den, na monhwɛ wɔn yie, ɛfiri sɛ wonnim nanso daakye berɛ bi wɔ ɔhaw da mu no, wɔn mu baako bɛba abɛhwɛ wo.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
