Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ ANAMMƆ NO ANKAE NO BƐYƐƐ KƐKƐ

WƆKYERƐ SƐ, NA Ɔhemmaa bi wɔ hɔ pɛn, na ɔwoo abaayewa fɛfɛ. Ɛhɔ na ɔwɔ; nanso bere a wɔde no awareɛ maa obiara no, ɔpow sɛ ɔbɛware. Berɛ biara a nnipa bɛkɔ akɔkasa akyerɛ no afa awareɛ ho no, na ɔka sɛ, "Akyinnye biara nni ho. " Mprempren abɔdeɛ no, Ɔnini (Ɔnanka), wɔ nwura no mu baabi, na ɔte ɔbɔn kɛse bi mu. Ɛda bi a ɔwɔ hɔ (ne ɔbodan mu) na ɔtee sɛ abɔfoɔ bi retwam, na wɔredi ababaawa fɛfɛ no ho asɛmti, sɛdeɛ, berɛ a wɔde no maa obiara wɔ awareɛ mu no, ɔkaa sɛ ɔmpɛ no. Ɔnanka no daeɛ wɔ hɔ, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ, Merekyerɛ me ho sɛ aberante pa, na meakɔware Ɔhemmaa Ɛna ba no. Ɛyɛ nokware sɛ, Ɔnanka no danee ne ho sɛ mmabunu, aniwa a ɔbɛhwɛ akyɛ no, na ɛnyɛ yie. Ɔde ne ho kɔduruu akuraa hɔ. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na ɔrekeka kola-nuts. Berɛ a ɔduruu kuro no kwanano no, na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna ba no, a na wakɔbɔ ahohoro no, asan aba na na ɔretrɛw ntaadeɛ no mu ama ayow. Ɔnanka ntasuo no, na ɛhwee aseɛ wɔ Ɔhemmaa Ɛna ba no gyegyeegye so. Ɔhemmaa no ba no kae sɛ, Dɛn nti na worehwie me gyegyeegye no agu? Ɔnanka no kae sɛ, Wo ne hokwan (sɛ wobɛka saa a), Amu. Ɔkaa sɛ, Me ntɔn abodin nnyɛ Amu. Ɔbuaa sɛ, Wo wɔ hokwan (sɛ wobɛka saa a), Esono. Ɔkaa sɛ, Me ntɔn abodin nnyɛ Esono. Ɔbuaa sɛ, Wo ne hokwan (sɛ wobɛka saa a), me yere. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ɛneɛ mɛware wo. Ɔhemmaa no ba ne Ɔnanka no sii o FFF for (her) fie. Ntɛm ara na Ɔhemmaa ba no kɔɔ hɔ kɔkaa asɛm bi kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Ɛ! eh! eh! (ɔde anigyeɛ a emu yɛ den rebɔ ne tirim), mehunu deɛ mepɛ no! " Ɔhemmaa no kaa sɛ, "Finally! Me ɔbabarima a ɔwɔ mmara mu no, he is my father-in-law? " Na ɔse, "Me ni, nanabea. " Ɔhemmaa no de nsa bɔɔ Ɔnanka no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Wo ne wo yere ntena ase yie. " Bere a anwummere no dwoe no, ɔne ne yere kɔtoo nsa. Na ɛyɛ saa nna nwɔtwe. Ɛduruu tenten no, ɔkae sɛ, Me yere, merekɔ me akuraa nanso mɛsan aba. Ne yere kae sɛ, Ɛneɛ me ne wo bɛkɔ (together). Ɔnanka no kae sɛ, Dabi. Ɔnanka no kasae na ɔkasae (na ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛma wakɔ), nanso Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no antie. Bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔne ne kunu no fii hɔ. Wɔkɔɔ so no, Ɔnanka no trɛw kɔɔ nwura no mu. Ɔhemmaa no ba no kae sɛ, Dɛn na woreyɛ, eyi ne kwan? Ɔnanka no kae sɛ, Wo deɛ, to wano na di makyi. Ɔno ne no fii hɔ, na wɔkɔɔ so, na (nneɛ ewiei koraa) Ɔnanka no yerae kɔɔ ɔbodan bi mu. Ɔhemmaa no ba no nso baa mu. Ntɛm ara na Ɔnanka no danee ne ho yɛɛ no aboa kɛseɛ, afei ɔkasa kyerɛɛ Ɔhemmaa Maame ba no sɛ, "Eyi ne nea meyɛ ankasa, efisɛ mesesaa me ho ahyɛdeɛ sɛ mɛware wo, ɛfiri sɛ na wote wo maame ne wo papa kɔn mu na woka sɛ, Baabiara nni hɔ (suro) ; me (seesei) mɛkum wo; kra me etire na fa ntontom no mma me; sɛ woannya bi a, woyɛ finishable. d ne aduane. Queen Mother no ba no tenaa nafã, na ɔrepere na ɔrehwehwɛ ntontom. Seesei, ɛhɔ na wɔwɔ, bere a Queen Mother ba no hunuu Akoo bi a ɔretwam. Queen Mother ba no rebɛbue nano aka sɛ, "Agya Ako," bere a Akoo no de ne nsa bɛtoo nano no. Akoo no faa ne tuo, ɔsiesiee ne nyinaa, na ɔde (kokoam fã) too ne kokoɔ no so. Ɔnanka no kaa sɛ, "Scratch my etire. " Ɔhemmaa no ba no kaa sɛ, "Mani abrɛ me sɛ me bɛbɔ no; sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wokum me a, kum me. " Ɔnanka no pɔ so sɛ ɔrekyerɛ ne etire sɛ ɔrekyere Ɔhemmaa no ba no, bere a Akoo no tuo no suanee sɛ, "POMm! " Akoo no kɔɔ ɔbodan mu kɔgyee Ɔhemmaa Ɛna ba no ne Ɔnanka no sɛ ɔmfa wɔn nkɔ fie nkɔbɔ amaneɛbɔ sɛnea ɛbae sɛ wahu Ɔhemmaa Ɛna ba ne Ɔnanka no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛnea wakum Ɔnanka no. Ɔhemmaa Ɛna ba no nso de nabasɛm no ho nsakyerɛeɛ nyinaa maeɛ. Ɔhemmaa no kae sɛ, "Akoo, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ megye de meda wo ase? " Akoo no kae sɛ, "Nnanom, me kɔn dɔ hwee, me kɔn dɔ ara pɛ na mepɛ. " Ɔhemmaa no kɔɔ so de mmɛw ngo bi bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Nnanom, mefa me dua no na mehyɛ saa mmɛw ngo yi mu, na ɛnam so ayɛ no sɛnkyerɛnneɛ (of this akwantu a akokoɔduro wom). " Saa na Akoo dua no bɛyɛɛ kɔkɔɔ. Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
