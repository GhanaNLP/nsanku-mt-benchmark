HOW ƆKƆTƆ, KƆTƆ NO, NYAA HONO, NA ABEREWA, ABEREWA NO, NYAA DWEN

Ɔbaa bi woo nabofra; bere a ɔde no kɔe no, ɔkae sɛ, "Merenkɔ. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛneɛ tra hɔ, wo ne wo nyansa kwan. " Daa bere a ɔde no kɔe (wɔ eRRAND biara), ɔkae sɛ, "Merenkɔ. " Ɛneɛ da bi ɔkae sɛ, "Kɔ petepese nsuo na fa mmra me. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Merenkɔ. " Na aberewa no bɔɔ no sɛ, "Twɛn hɔ nko, na woahwɛ. " abofra no kasae sɛ, Mekɔhwehwɛ Mɛbɛhwɛ sɛ ebia mebɛhu (sɛnea ɛteɛ). Na ɔhyɛɛ kwan no so, na ɔde mmirikatuo kɛseɛ kɔɔ, (na ne anan a ɛretu mmirika no nnyigyei te sɛ) yirdidi, yirdidi, yirdidi. Ɛduruu tenten no, ɔduruu akuraa bi so, na ɔhunuu aberewa bi a ɔte hɔ. Bere a ɔhunuu no, na ɔrebɛtwetwe ne ho. Ɔaberewa no kae sɛ, "Me ba, ntwe wo ho ntwe wo ho, mahu wo. " Ɛnna abofra no kɔɔ anim, na aberewa no kae sɛ, "Adɛn nti na nsuo a ɛfiri wo aniwa mu no hohoro wo saa? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Na me ne me maame te hɔ bɔ mu, da biara a ɔbɛsɔre no, na ɔka sɛ, "Twɛn hɔ nko na woahunu," na ɔbɔɔ me nti, mekae sɛ, mɛba abɛhwehwɛ sɛ 'mebɛhwɛ' sɛ ebia mebɛhu (nea ɛbɛba) te sɛ). " Ɛnna aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ me no. " Ɛnna ɔkaa sɛ, "Kɔ fie no akyi kɔtu bayerɛ na fa ba; sɛ wokɔduru hɔ a, nea ɛbɛka sɛ, Tutu me, tutu me, tutu me, no, nntutu no nkɔ; nanso nea ɛbɛka sɛ, Ntu me, ntu me, ɛno deɛ, tutu. Ɔbuaa sɛ, Ho! Ɛyɛ nokware a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, bayerɛ no bi ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Tutu me. p, dig me up, dig me up;" afoforɔ nso kae sɛ, "Don't dig me up, don't dig me up, don't dig me up. " Saa ebinom a wɔkae sɛ, "Don't dig me up no, na ɛyɛ ebinom a ɔtutu no. Ɛnna ɔne wɔn bae; na aberewa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Skin them and put them on the ogya. " Na ɔkutu wɔn de hyɛɛ wɔn so (ogya no), na sɛ ɛno a, ɔyii wɔn guu aduane so, na ɔgyee de sii aberewa no anim. Na ɔse, Kɔ kuro akyi kuro no mu, na hwɛ asaworowa bi a nam da so, hwɛ ntam (nam) no na fa wo apawde no. Ɛnna abofra no faa bi, na ɔba bɛtoo ogya no so, na ɔyɛɛ nkwan. Bere a odii no, ɔde too aberewa no anim. Ɔaberewa no kae sɛ, "Woawie anaa? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Aane, Nana. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Bra na bɔ fufuo no. " Ɛnna aberewa no kɔtoo nakyi sɛ, "Boaboa bayerɛ a wɔanoa no ano, fa ɛhwene me mu, na bɔ no. " Na abofra no kae sɛ, "Wei ne adeɛ a ɛyɛ nwanwa, sɛ yɛbɔ fufuo wɔ nipa tirim; wei ne nea maame kaa ho asɛm. " Wei nyinaa ɔkaa wɔ netire mu. Ɔkɔɔ kɔbɔɔ no, nanso berɛ a ɔbɔɔ no, ɔbɔɔ no brɛoo paa. Ɔbaa aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Bɔ no den, sɛ woabɔ no den a, wo kokoɔ bɛpae. " Na ɔbɔɔ no, pi! pi! pi! Na sɛ ɔwie a, ɔhwiee guu nkwan no so, na ɔde sii hɔ wɔ aberewa no anim. Ɔaberewa no kae sɛ, "Me nana, fa me din ka na fa didi. " Na ɔse, "Wɔmɔ na wɔfrɛ wo Old-woman-grandma. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "E! ɔnhunu, na watumi adidi. " Na aberewa no adi ne nyinaa. Ɛdii nna nwɔtwe, ɔyɛɛ no saa daa. Na da bi na abofra no kɔɔ asuwa hɔ kɔprɛ nsuo. Kɔtɔ no huu no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Adɛn nti na obi ba no so atew saa? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔbaa panyin bi wɔ hɔ a me ne no te, na sɛ mebɔ aduane na mema no a, ɔka sɛ, 'Ka me din na fa didi'. Mese, 'Wɔfrɛ wo Old Woman-Grandma', na ɔse, 'Ey!' woanhu kwan adidi,' na afei odidi; ɛno nti na me ho yɛ me hare. " Kɔtɔ no kae sɛ, "Ɔno wɔfrɛ no Old-woman-Mosono. " abofra no kae sɛ, "Ho. " Na ɔde ne nsuo fononoo no sii netire so. Kɔtɔ no kaa sɛ, "Mommwerɛ mma sɛ morekɔ akyiri. " Saa abofra no de ne ho kɔeɛ; bere a na ɔreba no nyinaa, na ɔreka sɛ, "Old-woman-Mosono, Mosono, Mosono. " Bere a ɔduruu kuro no kuro akyi no, ɔwerɛ firii, na ɔhweeɛ ne nsuo-nsuo-nsuo no mu, na ɔkɔɔ fie. aberewa no kae sɛ, "Ɛhe na nsuo-bɔ no wɔ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Bere a na mereba no, mehinhim, na ɛbɔɔ mu. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa foforo kɔ. " Na ɔgyee no kɔeɛ; na Kɔtɔ no kae sɛ, "Woasan aba dedaw? " Na ɔse, "Saa din a woka kyerɛɛ me no, me kɔeɛ na me werɛ afi, na me aba sɛ mɛbisa bio. " Kɔtɔ no kae sɛ, "Ne din ne Old-woman-Mosono. " Na ɔse, "Ho. " Ɔkɔɔ so kaeɛ no nyinaa. kwan fie, na ɔduruu fie no, ɔtenaa (kuku no). Ɔaberewa no kae sɛ, "Kɔ tu bayerɛ na fa mmra. " Ɔyɛɛ ne nyinaa sɛnea na ɛteɛ dedaw, na ɔde aduane no bae, na ɔde sii aberewa no anim, na aberewa no kae sɛ, "Ka me din na fa didi. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wose wo din Old-woman-Grandma. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ meka me ankasa din. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wo din ankasa? " Ohui sɛ aberewa no kae sɛ, "Aane saa. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wo din Old-woman-Mosono. " na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Fa na didi. " Ɛnna abofra no gyee na ɔdii. Ɔbaa aberewa no de ne ho sii kwan so, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Merekɔ hwehwɛ saa onipa a ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ me din ne Mosono no. " Na ɔkɔɔ asuwa hɔ, na ɔkɔhyiaa Ɔbontoya, minnow no, (na ɔtoo no mmonnaa), sɛ, "Wo 'Bonto, wo maame! Wo 'Bonto, wo agya! woaka akyerɛ me banana akokoaa sɛ me din de Mosono? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ me na ɛyɛeɛ. " Adwene aberewa no hunuu sɛ apataa no regyina nkyɛn hɔ, ɔbɛɛ no ntamhyɛ, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo Adwene, wo maame! Wo maame! Wo maame! Wo Adwene, wo agya! Wo agya! Wo agya! Wo agya! Wo ka kyerɛɛ me banana akokoaa sɛ me din de Mosono? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ me na ɛyɛeɛ. " Ɔhunuu sɛ Kɔtɔ no gyina hɔ. Ɔdomee no sɛ, Wo Kɔtɔ, wo maame! maame! wo maame! wo Kɔtɔ, wo agya! wo agya! wo agya! wo na woka kyerɛɛ me banana akokoaa sɛ me te Nkran, meyɛ Ɔdehye mogya, na me din ne Mosono? " Kɔtɔ no kae sɛ, "Aane, ɛyɛ me na meka kyerɛɛ no, na adɛn nti na woama obi ba no so atew saa? " Na aberewa no bo fuwii, na ne calabash a na okura mu no, ɔgyee so tow bɔɔ Kɔtɔ no. Ɛno nti na Kɔtɔ no wɔ hono wɔ nakyi. Bere tenten bi na Kɔtɔ no akyi no, na hono biara nni hɔ. Na Kɔtɔ no nso faa dɔte fitaa bi, na ɔtow guu aberewa no ti so. Ɛno nti na atifi ntaadeɛ a ɛwɔ mmaa aberewa ti so no wɔ ti nwi fitaa.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi ayɛ wo nokware, ne ahomegyeɛ a wubetumi akamfo me (wɔ nasɛmka ho).
