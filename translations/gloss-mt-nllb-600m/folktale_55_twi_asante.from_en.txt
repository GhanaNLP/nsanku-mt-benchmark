Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA SPEAR-GRASS BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU

Ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bi baeɛ na Ntikuma hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔrekɔ hwehwɛ aduane de akɔfa akɔma nagya, Agya Ananse, Ananse. Ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ akuraase bi; (hɔ) na ɔkɔhyiaa Old-woman-nanom. Ɔpanyin no aberewa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Me ba, adɛn nti, bere a obiara nntumi mmra ha no, woba ha? Ntikuma buae sɛ, Ɛyɛ ɛkɔm nti, na mebae hwehwɛ biribi a mebɛkɔ adidi. Ɔpanyin-ɔbea-nanom kaa sɛ, Kɔ me afuw hɔ, nanso bayere a ɛbɛka sɛ, Pluke me, pluke me, no, ɛntwiri, nanso bayere no deɛ, ɛbɛka sɛ, Dont rip me, dont rip me, pluke it and bring Ɛhɔ. Ntikuma bɔɔ. Bayerɛ no mu bi kaa sɛ, Pluke me, pillame; afoforɔ nso kaa sɛ, Dont pillame, dont pillame. Ɛnna Ntikuma bɔɔ nea ɛka sɛ, Dont pillame, dont pillame no. Ɔde baa fie hɔ, ɔpillae. Ɛnna Ɔbaa Panyin no kae sɛ, Tow ankasa fa no kɔ aduane mu, na fa nwoma ahoroɔ no gu kuku no mu na fa to ogya no so. Ntikuma yii no, na ɔfaa nwoma ahoroɔ no de guu ogya no so. Bere a wɔasiesiee wɔn ho no, oyii ogya no mu, yii kuku no mu emu nsɛm no, na ne nyinaa (emu) adane aduane pa. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Mawie, nanso ma me nam mfa nhyɛ mu. Ɔpanyin-ayaa-nanom kaa sɛ, Kɔka kyerɛ Nkwakuo, Asono no, a wɔte dua no so, sɛ Ɔpanyin-ayaa-ananom ka sɛ, mo nyinaa deɛ, mo ɛnanom! Asono no kaa sɛ, Sɛ ɛnyɛ Ɔpanyin-ayaa-ananom nti, anka yɛbɛbɔ mo. Ɔpanyin-ayaa-ananom kaa sɛ, Me deɛ, ɛnyɛ me eguadwa, na mo m ay ba bɛbɔɔ no. Ɛmmoa no de ahyɛdeɛ sian bɛbɔɔ no. Ɔpanyin-panyin-nanom kae sɛ, Me Sharp-knife, twa mmoa yi atitirefoɔ mma me. Ɛnna sharp-one no twaa mmoa no atitirefoɔ. Ntikuma boaboaa mmoa no atitirefoɔ no nyinaa ano, yii bayerɛ bi wɔ nkaaho, na ɔne wɔn nyinaa kɔɔ akuraa. Agya Ananse hunuu (ne ɔbabarima) Ntikuma a ɔgyina nanim, na ɔkaa sɛ, Nkyea wo ba, nkraman-dwamma (i.e. firstborn). Ɔtoaa so sɛ, Mema wo so wɔ makyi. Ntikuma kɔɔ hɔ kɔtoo hɔ. Mprempren Kwaku Ananse kɔyii bayere bi adi; ɔkae sɛ, Mewɔ mabosonsoɔ a, mebɛdi yi. (Ne yere) "Aso" kae sɛ, Efi bere a Ɔbɔadeɛ no yɛɛ nneɛma no, woahunu ɔbarima a ɔwɔ abosonsoɔ bi? Kwaku Ananse ne ne yere, ne ne mmofra, dii bayerɛ no wiee, na ɛno nko ara na ɛkaa Agya Ananse. Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka me abosonsoɔ. " Na ɔkɔtraa (wɔ akwankyɛn tena) fie akyi, na ɔyii bayere no, na ɔedidii kosii sɛ na (all) awie. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Mepue bio. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Me ne wo bɛkɔ (together). Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Me ne wo rennkɔ. Berɛ a Ntikuma kɔtoo nsa no, ɛnneɛ Kwaku Ananse kɔgyee Huntersskin bag, de amena nketewa guu mu, de nso hyɛɛ mu ma, na ɔde hyɛɛ Ntikuma ntama no ano. Berɛ a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ deɛ ɛda adi no, ɛhɔn, ɛhɔn, ɛhɔn, Ntikuma hyɛɛ kwan. Bere a (sum a wɔde ahintaw) anim no apaapae akɔ nkyɛn no, Ananse, hwehwɛɛ Ntikuma sɛnea ɔpɛ, anhu no, afei ɔkɔhwehwɛɛ baabi a ne nso no ahwe ase. Bere a ohuu wɔn no, odii aboa anammɔn no akyi, kɔɔ so kɔɔ so. Na ɔrekɔ so no, bere a, na Ntikuma ne Old-woman-nanom te hɔ. Ɔpanyin-awofoɔ no kaa sɛ, Dad man, dɛn ne wo eguadwa? Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, Ɛkɔm aka me, enti ma me biribi nni. Ɔpanyin-awofoɔ no kaa sɛ, Ɛhe na mebɛnya biako afiri? Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, Wofa no sɛ meyɛ kwasea, me abofra koraa, Ntikuma, baeɛ a, wode bi maa no. Ɔpanyin-awofoɔ no kaa sɛ, Kɔ afuw a ɛwɔ hɔ no, yam a ɛne sɛ T pull me up, pull me up , ntwe wɔn nkɔ soro, nanso ebinom a ɛne sɛ, Ntwe me nkɔ soro, ntwe me nkɔ soro, ntwe saa ebinom no. Ananse no (replied), Ah! nkwaseafo adwuma bɛn na eyi ka kyerɛ me, bere a obi ka sɛ ɔdɔ obi, woka kyerɛ me sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ me kɔn dɔ no, me nte sɛɛ da? Nanso Ananse no kɔɔ afuw mu. Bere a ɔduruu hɔ no, bayerɛ no mu bi kaa sɛ, Traw me up, pull me up, foforɔ nso kaa sɛ, Dont pull me up, dont pull me up. Na ɔtwee ebinom a na ɛka sɛ, T pull me up, pull me up, na ɔde wɔn baa fie. Ɔbaa na ɔwiee wɔn hohoroo; Ɔbaa Panyin-nanom kae sɛ, Kɔ fa aduane pa no fa kɔ, na fa nwoma ahoroɔ no kɔhyɛ kuku no mu, na fa to ogya so. Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, Aah, ɛyɛ nkasa a ɛporɔw bɛn na woka yi kyerɛ me; ɛfiri sɛ Ɔbɔadeɛ na ɔyɛɛ nneɛma no, yɛtow aduane pa no gu na yɛkora nsensanee no so anaa? Ananse no faa aduane no fa a ɛyɛ papa no, de sii ogya so, nanso ɔ na ɔde wedeɛ no kakra baako too so. Bere a aduane no ayɛ no, oyii no, na ne nyinaa adane aboɔ, nanso wedeɛ sin baako a na ɔde ato soro no adane aduane pa. Agya Ananse kae sɛ, "Ei! kwasampani awosankɔ yi nni nkontompo (after all). " Na Ananse no kɔtwee nwoma ahoroɔ no de baeɛ na wɔde guu ogya no so. Bere a aduane no noae no, oyii ogya no mu, na ɛdanee bayerɛ a wɔanoa sɛdeɛ wani bɛtumi ahu no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Old-ɔbaa-nanom, eyi nyinaa yɛ yie paa, nanso seesei ɛsɛ sɛ woma me nam nka ho. " Nana-ananom kaa sɛ, "Kɔhwɛ dua no, Asono wɔ so. Wokɔ a, wobɛka sɛ, Woyɛ mmoadoma. Ananse no kaa sɛ, ɔbaa yi! Me ne Asono nni nea ɛde ba manso, na dɛn nti na menkɔyɛ wɔn ayayade saa ɔkwan yi so? " Ɔpanyin-ɔbaa no kae sɛ, "Kɔ, na sɛ ɔhaw ba a, ɛno ne me abusuabɔ. " Ananse no hyɛɛ aseɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo Asono, wo a wote nnua yi so no, Old-woman-nanom se woyɛ mmoadoma. " Wɔde mmoa no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ Old-woman-nanom nti na yɛbɛbɔ wo. " Old-woman-nanom se, "Ɛneɛ bra bɛboro no. "

Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Aberewa, ɛno nteɛ. " Ɛneɛ na mmoa no bɛtoo ne so na ɛbaa bɛbɔɔ Kwaku Ananse. Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Sekkan the-sharp-one, twa wɔn atitirefoɔ no. " Tenten! (na ɛyɛ nnyigyei a Sekkan no yɛe bere a ɔrekyerɛ ne adwuma no. Ɔpanyin-ɔbaa-nanom kaa sɛ, "Softly and easy now. " Nanso Ananse no antie. Ɛnneɛ, Ananse no boaboaa nam no ano baa fie, na Aberewa no kɔɔ nkyɛn kakra. Ɛneɛ, Kwaku Ananse faa ne Sekkan no de hyɛɛ ne kotokuo mu, na ɔdwanee baa nakuraa. Bere a ɔsan baa nakuraa so no, ɔka kyerɛɛ (nɔbabarima) sɛ, "Ntikuma, saa Sekkan yi nti, nea mede me nsa ato so yi, momma yɛnkɔ nwura no mu. " Enti wɔtuu kwan, na wɔkɔhunuu Wuram mprako bi. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ntikuma, kɔyɛ wɔn ayayadeɛ. Ntikuma kɔɔ hɔ kɔkaa sɛ, "Bush-pigs, wo ho agu fĩ. " Bush-pigs no buae sɛ, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ sɛ woyɛ Kwaku Ananse, the Spider's son, anka yɛbɛba abɛbɔ wo. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Mommra mmɛbɔ no akwan nyinaa so, ɔyɛ koraa bɔne, n'animtiaa nni aseresɛm. " Bush-pigs no rebɛbɔ no, bere a Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Old woman's knife, The-sharp-one, twa wɔn atitirefoɔ mma me. " h! na Sekkan no hwee wɔn so, na ɔwiee sɛ ɔtwaa mmoa no nyinaa ti. Ɛnna ɛhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɛretwa nnua no. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ayi! Eyi yɛ dɛn na wo ho? Mekaeɛ sɛ twa mmoa no tiri, na wowiee saa yɛ no, na afei ɛdeɛn na eyi nyinaa yɛ? " Mprempren Ntikuma de nani bɛhwɛɛ kakra, afei ɔkae sɛ, "Softly and easy now. " Kwaku Ananse antie no, na Sekkan no baa fam, na Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ akɔgye. Wɔsan kɔɔ fie. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, na Kwaku Ananse akyerɛ sɛ wɔbɛkɔ ɔsoro-anyame hɔ. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Abba, nkɔ sɛ ɛyɛ Sekkan yi akawnto. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Firi hɔ. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Kɔ wankasa kwan so. Kwaku Ananse firii hɔ, kɔɔ ɔsoro-anyame no nkyɛn wɔ hɔ, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Agya, kɔsi da yi, sɛ obi bi ato wo bo a, me nko ara, me, Kwaku Ananse, mebɛtwa wɔn atitirefoɔ nyinaa wɔ da koro pɛ mu na mede ama wo. Ɔsoro-anyame no kaa sɛ, Aseda afɔdeɛ wɔ hɔ ma obiara a ɔbɛbɔɔ bɛtumi ayɛ saa, ɛfiri sɛ atamfo asraafodɔm kɛseɛ bi resi kuro no kuro akyi, enti kɔ. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Momma wɔmfa nsa ne odwan baako mmra. Ntɛm ara na wɔde wɔn bae. Kwaku Ananse firii adi kɔɔ kwan so, Kwaku Ananse bisaa atamfo asraafo no sɛ, So ɛyɛ mo na wose mobɛko atia Ewiem-onyankopɔn? Wɔbuaa sɛ, Aane saa na yɛka. Afei Ewiem-onyankopɔn asraafoɔ no bɛgyinaa Kwaku Ananse akyi. Afei Kwaku Ananse de nano kyerɛɛ Ɔbaa Panyin Sekkan, The-Sharp-One, sɛ, Twitwa atamfo asraafo yi atitirefoɔ mma me. Na Sekkan no twaa wɔn atitirefoɔ, tan! tan! Na ɛwieeɛ. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Ɛno yie, gyae. Nanso Sekkan no hwee Ewiem-onyankopɔn asraafoɔ no so na ɔretwa wɔn atitirefoɔ. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, O, ɛno ne Ewiem-onyankopɔn asraafoɔ, nti gyae. Nanso ɛkɔɔ so. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Sɛ wo nufuɔ akyea a, ma ɛnnyɛ nwini. Oh! Sky-gods no asraafoɔ no nyinaa popoe. Seesei Sekkan no tuu kwan wɔ mframa mu; ɛbaa nea Kwaku Ananse wɔ no. Ɔkaeɛ sɛ, Hwɛ, wunnim me? Wonnim me? Meyɛ Kwaku Ananse, medii wo firi Old Woman hɔ. Oh! seisei Kwaku Ananse tiri bɔɔ tan! Na ɛkɔɔ so kɔeɛ wɔ Sekkan no so, na baabi a ɛgyinaeɛ no adane ɛserɛ bi, a yɛfrɛ no Etene (spear-grass); nano yɛ nnam pa ara.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
