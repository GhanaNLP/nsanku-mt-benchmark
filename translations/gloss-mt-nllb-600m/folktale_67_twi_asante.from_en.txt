Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

KWAKU ANANSE, ANANSE NO, KAE SƐ ƆREKƆ KWASEA BI HWEHWƐ MU, BERE A NE BERE NYINAA MU NA ƆYƐ KWASEA

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse no, na ɔte hɔ pɛn, na ɔkae sɛ ɔpɛ kwasea bi sɛ ɔne no fa mpataa-nnadaa si. Ɔkyinkyin-kyinkyin-hyia, nanso wannye bi. Ɛneɛ na ɔkɔhyiaa Osansa, Akrɔma no. Ɔkaa sɛ, Yɛnkɔ na yɛmfa mpataa-nkyere nsi hɔ. Akrɔma no buae sɛ, Mewɔ nam (plenty) a mebɛdi. Akyire yi na ohyiaa Anene, Kwaakwaadabi no, na ɔkae sɛ, Momma yɛnkɔ na yɛmfa mpataa-nkyere nsi hɔ. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, Metee ase. Afei, Akrɔma no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaakwaadabi no sɛ, Saa mpataa-nkyere-setting expedition a wo ne Kwaku Ananse rekɔ yi, nkɔ, efisɛ Kwaku Ananse kyerɛ sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ kwasea bi, saa bere a ɔne kwasea no de mpataa-nkyere sii hɔ no, s, ɔbɛnya sika no nyinaa. Kwaakwaadabi no buae sɛ, Ɛho nhia sɛ woka asɛm biara bio, efisɛ mebɛhwɛ ama me nsa aka sika no nyinaa. Kwaakwaadabi no ne Ananse no sii hɔ sɛ wɔbɛtwa nwura no mmɛreba. Kwaakwaadabi no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, Mma me Sekkan no na menkɔtwa, na wo adwuma ne sɛ wubegye brɛ no afiri me adwumayɛ mu. Ananse no buae sɛ, Wofa me sɛ meyɛ kwasea, fa ma me nyɛ atwaeɛ no, bere a wo na wubegye ne brɛ no. Kwaku Ananse yɛɛ atwaeɛ (pALMS) bere a Anene, Kwaakwaadabi no, tenaa hɔ a ɔresu. Bere a otwaee no wiei no, wɔkyekyeree no (wɔ babaso bi mu). Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Ananse, ma me nkyere wɔn na memfa wɔn, bere a mo na mobɛfa wɔn brɛ no. " Ananse no buae sɛ, "Firi adi, woyɛ me kwasea? " Na ɔgyee wɔn de wɔn too nakyi etire so. Kwaakwaadabi no dii nakyi a ɔresu. Bere a wɔduruu fie no, Kwaakwaadabi no kae sɛ, "Momma mɛyɛ mpataa-nsuo no na woagye me nnwuma no brɛ. " Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "Dabi; ma mɛyɛ na woagye brɛ no. " Ɛneɛ Kwaakwaadabi no kɔtoo nakyi, na ɔresu. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɔkwasea, wose woyɛ adwene, na woda so ara rewu. " Ananse no wiee mpataa-nsuo no. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Ma me soa mpataa-nsuo no nkɔ nsuo no mu, na wo na wofa ne brɛ no. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Dabi, me ne wɔn bɛkɔ, na wo na wofa ne brɛ no. " Wɔduruu nsuo no mu no, Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Ananse, agya Ananse, aboa kɛseɛ bi te nsuo no mu, enti ma me nnyina nsuo no mu na memfa mpataa-nsuo no nsi hɔ sɛ, sɛ aboa kɛseɛ no bɛka me a, wo na wobɛfa ne owuo. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ah, wofa me sɛ ɔkwasea ankasa, me ara me na mɛhyehyɛ afidie no, na sɛ ɔbɔ me a, wo bɛgye ne owuo. " Kwaku Ananse faa apataa-afidie no de guu nsuo no mu, na wɔsan baa fie. Ade kyee anɔpa, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, na wɔkɔhwɛɛ afidie no, na wɔanya apataa mmienu. Kwaakwaadabi no kae sɛ, "Ananse, wofa eyinom mmienu, na bere a to-ɔkyena bɛba, na afidie no akyere nnan no, me ne dane bɛgye wɔn. " Ananse no buae sɛ, "Wofa me sɛ ɔkwasea ankasa! Wofa eyinom mmienu, na ɔkyena me ne nanse yi bɛkɔ dane. " Kwaakwaadabi no faa wɔn. Ade kyee anɔpa na wɔkɔsraa afidie no, na wofirii anan (apataa) no mu bae. Kwaakwaadabi no kaeɛ sɛ, "Wo deɛ, fa eyinom anan, na ɔkyena a yɛba na yɛakyere awotwe no, me na mebɛfa wɔn wɔ dane mu. " Ananse no buae sɛ, "Wo deɛ, fa me sɛ meyɛ kwasea; wo deɛ, ɛwɔ anan yi, na ɔkyena nso, mebɛfa nea ɛto so awotwe no. " Ade kyee anɔpa no, wɔkɔsraa afidie no, na wofirii awotwe no mu. Kwaakwaadabi no kae sɛ, "Fa, na me nso mebɛfa dunsia no akɔ ɔkyena. " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Wofa me sɛ kwasea, wofa awotwe no, na ɔkyena mebɛfa dunsia no. " Mprempren mpataa afiri no asɛe, na Kwaakwaadabi no de nano kyerɛɛ Ananse no sɛ, "Kɔfa mpataa afiri a ɛporɔw yi, na sɛ obi tɔn a, ɔbɛnya ntanu (i.e. (£16) wɔ afiri biara ho, saa ara na wo, Agya Ananse, fa apataa yi, fa apataa a asɛe no mma me. Ananse no kaa sɛ, Dabi, wofa apataa no kɔtɔn, na mefa apataa a asɛe no na mefa mfasoɔ no. Wɔde nneɛma kɔɔ dwaso. Ntɛm ara na Kwaakwaadabi yii ne nneɛma nyinaa gui, ɔnyaa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma pii. Nanso, na Ananse deɛ no da so ara wɔ hɔ. Ɛnna Kwaakwaadabi no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Fa saa mpataa-nsuo a ɛporɔw yi nkyinkyin na wɔntɔ. Ananse no maa wɔn so, ɔkaa sɛ, Metɔn mpataa-nsuo a ɛporɔw! Afei akuraa no so panyin no tee (him), na ɔkae sɛ, Ɛhe na saa yɔnkoɔ yi firi? Kɔfrɛ no sɛ ɔmmra. Ananse no kɔɔ. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, Wofa no sɛ yɛn nkurɔfoɔ a yɛwɔ akuraa ha no yɛ nkwaseafoɔ; afei wadamfo (here) abɛtɔn apataa (with mfasoɔ), woannhu no anaa, nanso wobɛtow apataa-nnadaa a ɛporɔw ato yɛn? ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, Bɔ no akuturuku. Enti wɔbɔɔ no akuturuku. Ɛnna Kwaakwaadabi no kae sɛ, Spider, woahunu sɛ waniwa awu ama aniwu; woahunu sɛ na worekɔ hwehwɛ kwasea bi sɛ ɔne wo bɛyɛ adwuma, na woahunu sɛ na wo nso woyɛ kwasea.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
