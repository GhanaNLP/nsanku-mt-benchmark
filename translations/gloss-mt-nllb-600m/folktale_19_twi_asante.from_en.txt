Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ WƆDII MMOFRA (DI KAN)

WƆKYERƐ SƐ (bere bi) ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bi baeɛ, na Agya Ananse, Ananse, ne ne yere "Aso", ne ne mmofra Ntikuma, Nyiwankɔnfwea (Thin-shanks), Afudotwedotwe (Belly-like-to-burst), ne Tonkono (Big-Big-head), sii atenaeɛ ketewa bi tenaa mu. Da biara na Ananse kɔfa aduane, wuram bayere, na wɔnoa na wodi. Ɛnneɛ da bi, Agya Ananse kɔɔ nwura no mu, ɔhunuu sɛ aduan dɛdɛ bi regyina hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. Aduane no kaa sɛ, Me din nyɛ beautiful. Ananse no kaa sɛ, Wo din de dɛn? Ɔbuaa sɛ, Me din de me, Fill-up-some-and-eat. Ananse no kaa sɛ, Fill up some that I may see. Aduane no hyɛɛ mu ne nnuadewa ngo soup, na Kwaku Ananse dii no nyinaa. Bere a ɔwiee no, ɔbisaa aduane no sɛ, Wo akyiwadeɛ ne dɛn? Na aduane no bua sɛ, Mekyerɛ otuo tɛtea, ne gourd cup kakra. Ananse no faa nkyɛnsee fie no, na wɔkɔde sii silin no so. Ɛnna Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ nwura so de aduane baeɛ, na "Aso", bere a ɔdii aduannoa wieeɛ no, ɔfrɛɛ Kwaku Ananse, na ɔkaa sɛ, O, wo deɛ ɛyɛ ahiade ankasa, me deɛ, me akoraa no, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ me ne aduane yɛ, wo deɛ ne mmofra yi deɛ ɛyɛ ade a ɛho hia ankasa; sɛ woahyɛ mu ma a, na maso ayɛ dwo (wɔ wo kwadwom no nnyeeɛ mu). Bere a wɔdii aduane wieeɛ no, na anka anka ahwene no pa ara. Sɛɛ wɔ aseseɛ no akyi, na ɔkɔtenaa silin a aduane no wɔ no so. Ɔkaa sɛ, Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. Ɔbuaa sɛ, Me din nnyɛ beautiful. Ɔkaa sɛ, Wo din de dɛn? Ɔkaa sɛ, M wɔfrɛ me, Fill-up-some-and-eat. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Fill up some for me to see. Na ɛhyɛɛ prɛte a ground-nut soup ahyɛ mu ma, na Kwaku Ananse dii. Ɛda biara a ɔsɔre no, na ɛyɛ saa. Mprempren, Ntikuma bɛhyɛɛ no nsow sɛ nagya no nyinii, wɔ nitan a ɔwɔ wɔ nokwasɛm a ɛyɛ sɛ ɔne no nni nkabom no ho, na Ntikuma kuraa nagya no so dɔn na ɔhunuu nea agya no anya ho nsɔmu. Berɛ a agya no kɔɔ nwura no so no, Ntikuma foro kɔɔ soro (silin no) na ɔhunuu aduane no. Ɔfrɛɛ ne maame ne ne anuanom, na wɔkɔɔ soro. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Saa aduane yi fɛfɛɛfɛ. Ɛkaa sɛ, Me din nyɛ me fɛfɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ɛnneɛ dɛn na wo din? Ɔkaa sɛ, Me din ne Fill-up-some-and-eat. Ɔkaa sɛ, Fill up a little so that I may see. Na aduane no de mmɛw ngo soup a ma sɛe no ahyɛ mu ma. Ntikuma ne ne maame ne ne anuanom didii kosii sɛ ne nyinaa wiei. Na afei Ntikuma bisaa aduane no sɛ, Dɛn na morehyɛ ho kwan? Aduane no kaa sɛ, Mekyerɛ otuo tɛtea ne kora ketewa. Ntikuma ka kyerɛɛ Afudotwedotwe no sɛ, Kɔ na fa bi mma me. Na ɔde baeɛ, na ɔfaa otuo tɛtea no de kaa aduane no, ne kora ketewa no de kaa aduane no ho. Ɛnna wɔn nyinaa sian. Na Agya Ananse (mean-time) afiri nwura a wuram bayere no mu aba. "Aso" na ɔde aduane no wieeɛ. Wɔtoo nsa frɛɛ Kwaku Ananse, na ɔbuaa sɛ, "Ebia woannye nea mekae no; me kae sɛ, efi da yi, sɛ me ne aduane ba fie a, wobɛtumi adi bi, ɛfiri sɛ mo na ebinom wɔ ahiadeɛ mu. " "Aso" ne ne mmofra no didii. Agya Ananse hohoroo no, foro kɔɔ silin no so; ɔkae sɛ, Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ. Kommm koraa! Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ. Kommm koraa! Agya Ananse kae sɛ, Ah! ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ ntoma yi a ɛnyɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ yi akawnto so; mebɛkɔfa Ɔyokofoɔ kente no bi abɛhyɛ. Na ɔsian kɔfa Ɔyoko abusuakuo kente ntoma no. Ɔhyɛɛ ne mpaboa no, foro kɔɔ soro hɔ (mprenu so). Ɔkaa sɛ, Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. Kommm koraa! Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. Kommm koraa! Ɔtwaa nani hwɛɛ dan no mu na ɔhunuu otuo tɛtea bi a kora ketewa bi wom. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Ɛnyɛ adeɛ baako, ɛnyɛ nneɛma mmienu (i.e. a akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho), ɛyɛ Ntikuma. Kwaku Ananse bɔɔ aduane no mu, na ɔbaa fam; oyii Ɔyoko kente ntoma no, na ɔdaa so, na ɔkɔɔ nwura no mu. Ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu adeɛ bi a ɛyɛ fɛ paa (called) Mpere, abapra no, asɛn hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, O, ɛyɛ nwanwa! saa adeɛ yi yɛ fɛ sen nea ɛtwaa toɔ no; saa abapra yi yɛ fɛ. Ananse no kaa sɛ, Mefrɛ me fɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ɛnneɛ dɛn na wɔfrɛ wo? Ɛkaa sɛ, Mefrɛ me Abiridibrada (ɛne sɛ, Swish-and-raise-weals). Na ɔse, Swed kakra ma mehu. Na abapra no hwee ne so sɛ, Swed, swish, swish. Agya Ananse teɛɛ mu sɛ, Pui! pui! " Na anomaa bi (a ɔtenatenaa nkyɛn) ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, "Ka Adwombie" (nea ɛkyerɛ sɛ, "cool-and-easy-now). Na Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Adwombie. " Ɛnna abapra no gyaee bɔ no. Na Kwaku Ananse de abapra yi baa fie, na ɔkɔde sii silin no so. "Aso" na ɔwiee aduane no, ɔkae sɛ, Kwaku Ananse, bra bɛdidi. Ɔbuaa sɛ, Esiane sɛ woda so ara wɔ ha (asase so), ebia wunni ɛtokuro biara wɔ waso (na woante nea mekae no), nti merenni. Kwaku Ananse foro kɔɔ soro kɔtenaa ase dwoodwoo. Ɛnna ɔsan sian bae; na afei ɔsan kɔɔ baabi kɔhyɛɛ ne ho nsie. Ɛnna Ntikuma foro kɔɔ soro. Ɔkaa sɛ, "O, saa agya no de adeɛ foforɔ aba. " Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Maame, nyiwankonfwea, Afudotwedotwe, bra ha, ma deɛ agya de aba (berɛ yi) sen nea ɛtwa toɔ no koraa. " Ɛneɛ na wɔn nyinaa foro kɔɔ soro (wɔ silin no so). Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Saa adeɛ yi yɛ fɛ. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Mefrɛ me 'beautiful'. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo din de sɛn? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me din de 'Swich-and-raise-weals'. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mefrɛ me 'Swich-and-raise-weals'. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Swich kakra ma mehu. " Na saa abapra no sian bɛbɔɔ no akuturuku kɛseɛ. Kwaku Ananse gyinaa nkyɛnkyɛn, na ɔtoo nne sɛ, "Lay it on, lay it on, especially on Ntikuma, lay it on him. " Seesei bere a Kwaku Ananse ahwɛe na wahu sɛ wɔabɔ wɔn akuturuku a ɛfata no, ɔkae sɛ, "Adwombie. " Kwaku Ananse baeɛ na ɔgyee abapra no, twaa no nketenkete, na ɔhwetee wɔn ho. Ɛno na ɛmaa abapra no baa abusuakuo no mu. Enti ɛba sɛ woka asɛm bi kyerɛ wo abofra na ɔrentie wo a, yɛbɔ no akuturuku.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
