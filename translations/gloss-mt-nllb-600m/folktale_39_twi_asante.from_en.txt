Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ASONO NO BAA LONG-GRASS-LAND NO MU

NA Ɛsono bi wɔ hɔ pɛn, na ɔkae sɛ ɔpɛ obi a ɔne no bɛdi agorɔ a wɔfrɛ no "nti a wɔde bobɔ wɔn ho". Wɔn mmoa no nyinaa kae sɛ wɔntumi nni Ɛsono yi agorɔ. Na Kwaku Ananse (Ananse) wɔ hɔ, na ɔse, "Me deɛ, me bɛtumi, nanso me ne wo bɛhyɛ tebea bi sɛ, sɛ wobɛtɔ me akum me a, na ɛsɛ sɛ wosie me wɔ baabiara gye sɛ ɔbotan mu. " Ɛsono no kaa sɛ, "Mete no, na me bɛba abɛbɔ wo ndanson, na wo nso wo bɛba abɛbɔ me ndanson. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ kɔtu bayerɛ, na ɔtu Wɔde guu ogya mu, na wɔayam, na na ɔkɔyɛ tiafi bi wɔ kwan bi ho. Mprempren, bere biara a Kwaku Ananse bɛsɔre no, na ɔde bayere bi hyɛ ne nsa, na ɔkɔtra tiafi no so. Na ɔhu sɛ adowa ketewa no retwam. Mprempren nsɛmfua a wɔwɔ wɔn benkum akyi (i. e. Me werɛ firii sɛ mɛka bi) no, na ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bi aba (wɔ berɛ no mu). Ɔ (Adowa) twaamuu no, Ananse tow bayere no guu kwan so. Adowa no de ntɛm ara na ɛgyeeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya Ananse, ɛkɔm kɛseɛ a ɛte sɛɛ na aba, na eyi na wode di dwuma bere a wokɔhome? " Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "Me fie aduane ho ɔhaw biara nni hɔ. " Adowa no kaa sɛ, "Me ne wo bɛkɔ akɔgye bi. " Kwaku Ananse na ɔduruu fie hɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ Adowa no sɛ, "obarima bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔno na ɔde aduane brɛ me anadwo, enti kɔda hɔ wɔ ɔpɔn no ho. y, na sɛ ɔba a ɔbɛka sɛ, Asikoto! (Na wobɛkotow wo etire) na woabua sɛ, Mefa me etire gye, na ntɛm ara a woaka no, ɔbɛfa aduane no bi ato hɔ ama wo. Adowa no kaa sɛ, Meda wo ase, Aku; wo deɛ, Agya Ananse, mehunuu ɔbarima a ɔte sɛ wo da. Adowa no kɔɔ kwanano kɔtoo adiwo no. Ɛnkyɛree koraa na Ɛsono no baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Asikoto. " Adowa no buaeɛ sɛ, "Mede me etire kɔgye no," na Ɛsono no bɔɔ no, pan! na Adowa no wuiɛ. Ɛsono no bɔɔ. Ade kyee anɔpa koraa, bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, Ananse no faa Adowa maa "Aso" (ne yere) sɛ ɔnyɛ no soup-stew. Kwaku Ananse afei na ɔkɔɔ Ɛsono kɔmaa no anɔpa nkyea. Ɛsono no kaa sɛ, "Wo ho ye anaa? " Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "Me ho ye me. " Kwaku Ananse de kwan kɔɔ nfie. Ɔfaa bayerɛ foforɔ bi, na ɔsan kɔɔ tiafi hɔ. Ɔhunuu Ɔtwe, Ɔtwee no, sɛ ɔretwam hɔ, na ɔtow bayerɛ no gui. Ɔtwee no kae sɛ, "Oh, Father Kwaku Ananse, ɛkɔm kɛseɛ a ɛte sɛɛ aba, na eyi na wode kɔ tiafi anaa? " Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "Sɛ wopɛ bi a, bra, yɛnkɔ me fie, na ebia me bɛkɔ akɔma wo bi. " Ɔtwee no ne Kwaku Ananse bɔɔ mu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Tena ha wɔ kwanano kɔ kar dan mu, na anwummere yi me akoa bɛba ne aduane, na sɛ ɔduru ha a, ɔbɛka sɛ, 'Asikoto'; wose nso, 'Mefa me etire gye'. " Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, "Meda wo ase, Aku, wo na wotumi siesie nneɛma a ɛte sɛɛ. " Ɔtwee no kɔɔ hɔ kɔdaa hɔ. Ɛwɔ anwummere no, Ɛsono no baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Asikoto. " Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, "Me de me etire na megye no. " Na ɔbɔɔ Ɔtwee no, na ɔhwee fam, na na ne hweaseɛ no nnyigyei yɛ) kum! Ɛneɛ Ɛsono no bɔɔ. Anɔpa kɛseɛ a ɛdi hɔ, berɛ a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ deɛ ɛda adi no, na Kwaku Ananse de Ɔtwee no kɔmaa "Aso", na ɔfirii aseɛ kɔɔ Ɛsono fie sɛ ɔbɛma no anɔpa. Ɛsono kae sɛ, "Okuamoa wɔ ha, wote yie anaa? " Okuamoa buae sɛ, "Wo ho ye. " Ɛnna wɔkasaa kakra, na Kwaku Ananse srɛɛ no sɛ ɔnkɔ, na ɔsan kɔɔ ne fie. Na afei Kwaku Ananse nso yɛɛ saa adeɛ korɔ no ara ne Ɔyuo a ɔyɛ kwaduo no; Wansane a ɔyɛ bush-buck no; Kokotee-Asamoa a ɔyɛ bush-pig no; Ɔtromo a ɔyɛ Bongo no; ne Oko a ɔyɛ Ɔpɔnkɔ kɔkɔɔ-fitaa no. Afei, na ɛyɛ Kwaku Ananse nso sɛ ɔnkɔto Ɛsono no. Afei ɔkɔɔ dɔteɛ adwini bi nkyɛn kɔyɛɛ dadeɛ, na ɔyɛɛ dade hama bi a ɛka ho. Ɛbɛduruu anwummere no, ɔkɔɔ Ɛsono fie. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Asikoto. " Ɛsono no kaa sɛ, "Mede me etire kɔgye. " Ananse no faa dade hama no, de sii Ɛsono no etire no ahenkyɛ so, na ɔgyee dade hama no bɔɔ mu sɛ, pan! pan! pan! na ɛ (teaa no) ayera (wɔ asono no ti mu). Kwaku Ananse afei ɔbaa fie. Anɔpa kɛseɛ a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, Ɛsono ka kyerɛɛ ne yere sɛ, "Me wɔ tipae a emu yɛ den," na ne yere se, "Nka asɛm no saa, anka ɛsɛ sɛ wani gu so; Kwaku Ananse a wobɔɔ no ndanson no mpo, wannya ne tipaeɛ, na wanyera nso, na woka asɛm a ɛte sɛɛ; afei yi, gyae wo bo na ma Kwaku Ananse anɔpa pa sɛnea na ɔyɛ. " Ɛsono no bɔɔ naniwa (i.e. ) yɛɛ mmɔdenbɔ kɛseɛ, na ɔkɔeɛ; ɔduruu baabi a Kwaku Ananse te. Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Ɔhoɔdenfo kɛse, wote yie anaa? " Ɛsono no buae sɛ, "O, me ho nni asɛm. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Dabi. " Kwaku Ananse kɔbɔɔ teaa foforo. Bere a anadwo dwoeɛ no, ɔhyɛɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ Ɛsono hɔ. "Asikoto," ɔkaa saa. Ɛsono no buae sɛ, "Me de me etire na megye. " Kwaku Ananse faa teaa no, de sii hɔ, na ɔde dade hama no bɔɔ no, pan! pan! pan! Kwaku Ananse san baa ne fie. Nnɛ na ɔwɔ hɔ, bere a messenger bi baa hɔ bɛkaa sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse, Asono nyinaa nana kyerɛ sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ me ba bɛbɔ wo amanneɛ sɛ wadamfo, Ɛsono, berɛ a ɔdaeɛ no, wansɔre bio; nti bra na wobue ɔbotan bi, fa no na fa no hyɛ mu. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Dabi, ɛno na ɛnyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ a yɛyɛɛ no. Yɛkaa sɛ, sɛ obi wu a, ɔfoforɔ no nwene no mma ɔnwe. " Na messenger no rekɔ sɛ ɔbɛma nkra no ama (dead) no mogya-abusuabɔ. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Kɔfa no na fa no mmra ntɛm ara. " Messenger no baa hɔ bɛtoo Ananse no nkyɛn kaa sɛ, "Wɔse woreba ntɛm ara. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ wotwɛn me. " Ananse no kɔɔ nwura no mu, twaa dua, na ɔtwee kɔɔ dua-man mu, na ɔtwee tuo bi, de hyɛɛ ne nsa mu, na ɔde maa "Aso" sɛ, "Fa no, kɔ anim, fa to baabi a wɔka sɛ Me na ɛsɛ sɛ mepae ɔbotan no mu, na mo nso munnyina hɔ, na meba a, mobɛfrɛ me. " "Aso" faa (dua honi) kɔeɛ. Ananse no nso, wɔne asɛnkafoɔ no hyɛɛ aseɛ. Kwaku Ananse duruu Asono anim, na wɔfaa asɔ, ne crow-bar, ne agyan, ne Sekkan, hyɛɛ ne nsa. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Kɔ so. " Ɛnna wɔde Ananse no kɔɔ baabi a na botan kɛseɛ bi wɔ. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Plet it open, dig a ɛtokuro, and place the (dead) elephant inside. " Ananse kɔɔ hɔ sɛ, pE! pE! pE! (ɔde ne nnwinnadeɛ bɔɔ so). Ɛhɔ na na Kwaku Ananse wɔ, bere a ɔfrɛɛ prɛko pɛ mu sɛ, "Aane. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Elephants, wote sɛ obi refrɛ me anaa? " " Asono no kaa sɛ, " Mfi adi, mobɛpɛ kwan a mobɛfa so aguan. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, " Mate. " Na ɔkɔɔ so yɛɛ nadwuma, pE! pE! Afei bio ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, "Aane. " Ɔkaa sɛ, " Obi refrɛ me. " Nnipa no kaa sɛ, " Yɛremma wo kwan mma nkɔ. " (Ɛyɛɛ) saa, saa, mpɛn nson. Wɔ tenten mu no wɔkae sɛ, "Kɔ tie (who it is) na san ba ntɛm ara, na sɛ wuguan a, yɛbɛgye wo. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ baabi a Akua ba no da. Seesei Kwaku Ananse adwane (akyi), kiri! kiri! kiri! (na ɛyɛ ne anan nnyigyei). Asono no kae sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse, ɛyɛ dɛn? " Ɔkaa sɛ, " Oh, ɛsɛ sɛ woyi wo ho, efiri sɛ ɔbɔfoɔ bi wɔ hɔ a wɔregyina hɔ, na ɔse ɛsɛ sɛ mema me ho kakra kɔ nkyɛn ma ɔgye ne tuo abɔ wo. Ɛno nti na ɔfrɛɛ me, na ɔfrɛɛ me sɛ memfa me ho nhyɛ nkyɛn. " Asono berɛ a wɔrebɛdi hwehwɛ wɔ nwura no mu hɔ no, nokware bi a wɔhunuu sɛ obi regyina hɔ no, dwanee. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, " Pa hunter, kunkum wɔn mma me. " Asono no dwanee, na ɛwɔ Tall-grass-country no mu, na wɔgyaa ɔsono a wawu no wɔ hɔ, na Kwaku Ananse faa no kɔdweeɛ. Saa na Asono baa Tall-grass-country no kɔeɛ, berɛ bi a na Asono (only) te kwaeɛ mu.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi ayɛ wo nokware, ne ahomegyeɛ a wubetumi akamfo me (wɔ nasɛmka ho).
