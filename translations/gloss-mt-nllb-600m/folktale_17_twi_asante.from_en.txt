Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ SKY-GOD NSƐM NO BƐYƐƐ "SPIDER-STORIES"

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse, kɔɔ Nyankopɔn, Ɔsoro-onyankopɔn, wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ ɔrekɔtɔ Ɔsoro-onyankopɔn nsɛm. Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, "Wobɛtumi atɔ? " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Nokwarem no, mɛtumi. " Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, "Nkurow akɛseɛ ne akɛseɛ te sɛ Kokofu, Bekwai, Asumegya aba, nanso wɔantumi atɔ, na wo a woyɛ ɔbarima kɛkɛ a ɔnni wura no, wose wobɛtumi? " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na ɛyɛ nsɛm no boɔ? " Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, "Wontumi ntɔ wɔn kwa. gye Abɔdeɛ ɔnini Ɔnanka; Osebo, Daboɔ; Mmoatia, Mmoatia; (na) Mmɔborɔ, Nwonwonwade. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Mede saa nneɛma yi nyinaa bi bɛba, na (ɛno nso ne sɛ), mede me aberewa, Nsia (The Sixth-child), bɛka bebree no ho. " Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na fa wɔn ba afei. " Ananse no baeɛ, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame ne ne nyinaa, sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ me tɔ Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no ne Nyan-bɔfoɔ no nsɛm. " y-onyankopɔn se ɛsɛ sɛ mede Ɔnini, Ɔnanka; Osebo, Daboɔ; Mmoatia, Mmoatia; ne Mmɔborɔ, Nwonwonbone; na mekae sɛ mebɛka wo ho bebree na woakɔ ama ɔsoro-onipa. " Ananse no dii ne yere "Aso" nkɔmmɔ, na ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ na yɛanya Ɔnini, Ɔnanka no? " Na "Aso" ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Kɔtwa hama a ɛfow baa, na wotwa hama a ɛfow nso. " Na wo de ba. E-Spider san baa wɔn nkyɛn. Na "Aso" kae sɛ, "Momma wɔn nkɔ asuwa mu. " Na Kwaku Ananse de wɔn kɔɔ nanim, ɔrekɔ no ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛdware sen no, ɛnyɛ ne tenten; woda hɔ a, ɛdware sen no. " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Ɛhɔ na ɔda, baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ. " Ɔnanka no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ nkɔmmɔbɔ a wɔsusuwii yi nyinaa? " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ (me yere) "Aso" na ɔne me rekasa yi sɛ ne nsam-nsa yi na ɛyɛ l onger than you, and I say she is a ɔtorofoɔ. " Ɛnna Ɔnini, Ɔnanka no, kae sɛ, "Fa no, na bra susuw me. " Ɔfaa nsateaa no de too Ɔnanka no ho. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Straigh out. " Ɔnanka no nso trɛw ne ho, na Kwaku Ananse faa rope-creeper no bɔɔ no (na akyekyereɛ no nnyigyei no yɛɛ nsew! nsew! nsew! kosii sɛ ɔbɛduruu etire no. Kwaku Ananse, Ananse no, kae sɛ, "Ogyimfo, mede wo bɛkɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn nkyɛn na magye Ewiem-onyankopɔn anansesɛm no atom (wɔ nsasaeɛ mu)." Kwaku Ananse de no kɔɔ Niamey (Ewiem-onyankopɔn). Ewiem-onyankopɔn no kaa sɛ, "Me nsa aka (i.e. Me nsa aka no), ɛwɔ hɔ nea aka. " Ananse no san bae, na ɔbɛkaa nea asi no kyerɛɛ ne yere sɛ, "Nwonwonwade no da so wɔ hɔ. " Ne yere no kae sɛ, "Hwehwɛ akutu bi, na fa nsuo hyɛ mu, na wo ne no nkɔ. " Ananse no nam nwura no mu, bere a ohuu Nwonwonwade dɔm bi a ɛrek sɛn hɔ no, ɔhwiee nsuo no bi guu Nwonwonwade no so. Ananse no hwiee deɛ ɛkae no guu ne ho so, twaa borɔdeɛ no ahaban, na ɔde kataa ne etire so. Na afei ɔde nano kyerɛɛ Nwonwonwon no sɛ, Sɛnea osuo no aba no, anka ɛsɛ sɛ woba ha, me gourd, na ama osuo no anbɔ wo; wuhunu sɛ mefa borɔdeɛ ahahan akata me ho? Ɛnna Nwonwonwon no kaa sɛ, Yɛda wo ase, yɛda wo ase, Aku! Nwonwonwonwon no nyinaa tuu kwan, yeraa kɔɔ gourd no mu, fom! Agya Ananse kataa nano, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Mfonini, me nsa aka mo, na mefa mo gye Ewiem-onyankopɔn anansesɛm no (wɔ nsasaeɛ mu)." Na ɔde Nwonwade no kɔɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn nkyɛn. Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Me nsa aka deɛ aka no, nea aka no na ɛda so. " Ananse no san baa bio, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Osebo, Daboɔ no, ɔda so ara wɔ hɔ. " "Aso" kaa sɛ, "Kɔ tu ɛtokuro. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Wei dɔɔso, mete aseɛ. " Ɛneɛ Ananse no kɔɔ hɔ kɔhwehwɛɛ Daboɔ no akwan, na (bere a wahu no) ɔtu amoa a emu dɔ paa, na ɔkata so, na ɔsan baa fie. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ kɛseɛ, berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ aseɛ rebɛda adi no, na Ananse no kyerɛ sɛ ɔrebɛbɔ, na berɛ a ɔkɔeɛ no, (lO) Daboɔ bi da amena no mu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ: "Aba ketewa, ɛna ketewa, me kyerɛɛ wo sɛ mma woanom nsa, na afei deɛ, sɛnea wɔbɛhwɛ kwan no, wanom nsa, na ɛno nti na woahwe amena no mu; sɛ anka mɛka sɛ mɛyi wo afiri mu a, ɔkyena, sɛ wuhu me anaa me mmofra no mu biara a, wobɛkɔ akyere wɔn saa ara. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Aane! Meantumi anyɛ saa adeɛ. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ hɔ kɔtwaa nnua mmienu, de baako sii ha, ne baako nso sii hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Momma mo mmoa nanpɔw baako wɔ ha, na mo mmoa nanpɔw baako nso wɔ ha. " Daboɔ no de no sii hɔ (baabi a wɔka kyerɛɛ no). Bere a ɔrekorɔ no, Kwaku Ananse maa ne Sekkan so, na hann fɛrɛfɛrɛ mu no, ɛbɛdaa ne etire so, gao! (ɛyɛ nnyigyei a ɛyɛɛ no saa). amena no nyaa Daboɔ (and) fOM no! (ɛno ne nnyigyei a ɔhwee ase no). Kwaku Ananse nyaa atwerɛ sɛ ɔnkɔdɔm amena no mu nkɔgye Daboɔ no mfiri. Ɔyii Daboɔ no firii mu na ɔde baeɛ; ɔkaa sɛ, "Damfo, mefa wo resesa ɔsoro-anyame no nsɛm. " Ɔmaa Daboɔ no so sɛ ɔnkɔma Niamey, ɔsoro-anyame no. Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Me nsa aka, nea aka no na ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ. " Ananse no baeɛ, na ɔtwee Akua ba (dua tuntum a ɛyɛ dua a ɛwɔ anim a ɛyɛ fitaa) na ɔde ne nsa bɔɔ amamfoɔ a ɛkamfam (a ɛfiri dua mu) bi na ɔde hyɛɛ abɔfra no ho, na ɔtwee ɛto (bayerɛ ɛtɔ) na ɔde bi guu abɔfra no nsa mu, na ɔtwee foforɔ de hyɛɛ ayowa mu. Ɔde hama kyekyeree abɔfra no asen, Na ɔde kɔɔ hɔ kɔhyɛɛ odum dua bi nan ase, baabi a Mmoatia ba bɛdi agorɔ. Na Mmoatia bi nso baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Akua, mɛntumi nni pɔtɔ yi bi? Kwaku Ananse twee ne hama a etwa to no, na akuaaba no yii ne etire. Mmoatia no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuammaa no mu baako sɛ, Ɔse mɛtumi adi bi. Ɔkaa sɛ, Didi bi, ɛnneɛ. Na ɔdidi wieeɛ no, ɔdaa nase maa no (akuaaba no). Nanso bere a ɔdaa no ase no, ɔmmuu. Na Mmoatia no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no sɛ, Meda no ase a, ɔmmua. Mmoatia nuabea a odi kan no kae sɛ, Bɔ no asu. Na ɔbɔɔ no, pa! Na ne nsa kuraa hɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no sɛ, Me nsa aka hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, Fa nea aka no na bɔ no asu bio. Na ɔfaa no bɔɔ no asu, pa! na wei nso kuraa mu. Na Mmoatia no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no sɛ, Me nsa abien no nyinaa aka ntam. Ɔkaa sɛ, Push it with your yafunu. Ɔhyɛɛ no, na ne yafunu kuraa mu. Na Kwaku Ananse ba kyekyeree no, na ɔkae sɛ, Fool, me nsa aka wo a, mede wo bɛkɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn (wɔ nsasaeɛ) ama ne nsɛm. Na ɔne no kɔɔ fie. Na Kwaku Ananse ka kyerɛɛ ne maame, Ya nsia (ba a ɔtɔ so nsia), sɛ, Sɔre, ma yɛnkɔ, ɛfiri sɛ me ne Mmoatia rekɔ de Ewiem-onyankopɔn (wɔ nsasaeɛ) ama ne nsɛm. Ɔmaa wɔn so, na ɔfiri hɔ kɔɔ baabi a Ewiem-onyankopɔn no wɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ewiem-onyankopɔn, ha Mmoatia bi wɔ hɔ, na me nananom a mekaa wɔn ho asɛm no, ha nso wɔ hɔ. Afei Ewiem-onyankopɔn a wɔfrɛɛ no (ne mpanimfoɔ), ne Contire ne Akwam atitire, Adonten (asradoom kuw titiriw no ɔkannifoɔ), Gyaase (major-domo); Oyoko, Ankɔbea, ne Kalidɔm (akyi so awɛmfoɔ ɔkannifoɔ). Na ɔde asɛm no too wɔn anim sɛ, Ahemfo akɛseɛ pii aba, na wɔantumi anntɔ ɔsoro-anyame no nsɛm, nanso Kwaku Ananse, Ananse no, atumi atua ne boɔ; Me nsa aka Mmɔborɔ, Nwonwonwono, afiri ne hɔ; Me nsa aka Mmoatia, Mmoatia, Me nsa aka Osebo, Daboɔ afiri ne hɔ; Me nsa aka Ɔnini, Ɔnanka, afiri ne hɔ; Na nankasa apam nso, Kwaku Ananse aka ho. ed ne maame kɔ bebree; saa nneɛma yi nyinaa da ha. Ɔkaa sɛ, Mono nayeyi. Eee! (wɔteɛɛm). Sky-god no kaa sɛ, Kwaku Ananse, ɛfiri da kɔsi nnɛ ne kɔsi daa, mefa me Sky-god no nsɛm na mede ma wo, kosɛ! kosɛ! kosɛ! kosɛ! me nhyira, nhyira, nhyira. Yɛrenfrɛ wɔn Sky-god no nsɛm bio, na mmom yɛbɛfrɛ wɔn Spider-god.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
