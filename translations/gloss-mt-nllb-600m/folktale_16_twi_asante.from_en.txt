Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐSƐ SƐ ABAAYEWA BIARA WARE OBIARA A WƆDE NO AMA NO WƆ AWAREƐ MU.

NA abaayewa bi wɔ hɔ a wɔfrɛ no Kwaboaso. Nea wɔde no kɔware no no, ɔkae sɛ, "Mempɛ no. " Wɔde no kɔmaa ɔbɔfoɔ bi, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Ugh! saa ɔbarima yi wɔ ntontom wɔ ne ho, mempɛ no. "

Ɛdii da bi a ɔkɔɔ afuw no mu, kyerɛɛ sɛ ɔrekɔtwa bɔrɔde. Ɔfaa Sekkan bɔɔ borɔdeɛ no, kɔ! berɛ a ɔhunuu nnipa nketewa no te bɔrɔde no so; wɔkɔɔ fam, na wɔbae, na wɔkyeree Kwaboaso; wɔkaa sɛ, "Wo na wogye sɛ wɔmfa mma obiara a, wugye wo etire, tuu! pusu! " Na mmoatia no kyeree no nsɔmu kae sɛ: "Mommra, momma yɛnpiapia no.

Yɛpiapia no, O!

Yɛpiapia Kwaboaso.

Bra, momma yɛnhyɛ no.

Yɛpiapia no, O! "

Ɛnneɛ, bere a saa ɔbɔfoɔ a na wɔde Kwaboaso ama no no tee Kwaboaso nne no, ɔkaa sɛ, Merekɔhwɛ sɛ asɛm no ne deɛn, efiri sɛ yɛnni bɔne mfa ntua bɔne. Bere a ɔkɔɔ no, na Kwaboaso wɔ hɔ, na mmoatia no reborɔ no. Ɛneɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no tuu tuo wɔ mmoatia no so, na baako hwee aseɛ. MMOATI no mu panin no ka kyerɛɛ mmoatia no sɛ, Ɔwɔ nsafufuo a wɔanom, na wanom nsa; fa no to hɔ wɔ saa berɛ yi mu, na afei kɔ so piapia no. Bio nso, ɔbɔfoɔ no too tuo, na ɔfoforɔ bi nso hwee ase. MMOATI no mu panin no kaa sɛ, Kodurufoɔ no wɔ nsafufuo a wɔanom na wɔadi no so nkonim, momfa no nkɔto hɔ. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no kum mmoatia no nyinaa gye nea ɔpanin no nko ara. Ɔpanyin no frɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no sɛ, "Bra. " Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Merenkɔ. " Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Owura, menyɛ wo hwee. " Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Bra, menyɛ wo hwee. " Ɔpanyin no kɔɔ ɔbɔfoɔ no nkyɛn. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ me dan a ɛwɔ hɔ no mu, na woahunu tuo (i.e. atuduro) ne otuo ano aba a woatow no nyinaa, fa nea ɛyɛ wo de, na fa Kwaboaso nso, nanso ansa na woakɔ no, kɔtwa banana, na woakɔ no, tow gu, na ama mmoatia afoforo no akyea, na wɔabɛkyere wo, afei berɛ a wɔagyae abɛfa no, mmiako mmiako, woakɔ akyiri, akyɛ. " Na ɛne no kɔtwa banana no, na ɔfae Kwaboaso. waboaso nso, na ɔduruu kwan no so no, ɔtow kwadu baako gui. Ɔkɔɔ so yɛɛ saa kwan no nyinaa kɔɔ fie. Na berɛ tiawa pɛ na aka a wɔbɛduru fie no, na mmoatia no reto wɔn akyi. Na ɔtow kwadu baako pɛ a aka no, na mmoatia no dii akyi kɔdanee. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no san de Kwaboaso baa ne mogya ntam. Ɛneɛ na ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔɔ nankasa fie. Ɛnneɛ, na ɔbɔfoɔ no te hɔ, bere a ɔhunuu sɛ asɛnkafoɔ bi adu ne baabi, na ɔse, "Ɛdeɛn na ɛyɛ amaneɛbɔ no? " Asɛnkafoɔ no kaeɛ sɛ, "Kwaboaso kyerɛ sɛ wasrɛ akuraa Etire sɛ ɔmmɛgye no, seesei ɔse ɔwɔ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔbɛware wo. " Ɔɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Meda wo ase wɔ nsɛmfua a ɛfiri akuraa Odikro ano no, nanso merentumi nnyare abaayewa no, ɛfiri sɛ meda so ara nya t icks on my nipadua. " Ɛno nti na Mpanimfoɔ kyerɛ sɛ, "Sɛ wɔde wo kɔma obi awareɛ a, ɔware no, efisɛ wonnim nanso da bi a wowɔ ahiadeɛ mu no, obegye wo. "

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
