Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ NNIPA BI YƐ NEA ƐYƐ FƐ, NA AFOFORO YƐ NEA ƐNYƐ FƐ

WƆKYERƐ SƐ NA ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ (bere bi), na ɔkɔɔ mmɛw dua bi a ɛgyina hɔ wɔ nsuo mu sɛ ɔmfa nsɛmbisa no mma awoɔ ho. Na bere a ɔkɔɔ no, mmua no kaa sɛ, Mema wo nea wopɛ, nanso abofra a me ne no bɛhyia no no, sɛ ɔsɔre a, ɔrenyɛ adwuma biara da. Ɔkaa sɛ, Mepene so. Ɛnyɛ nna mmienu, ɛnyɛ nna mmiɛnsa, berɛ a ɔnyinsɛɛ na ɔwoo awoɔ. Te sɛ mmofra a wɔwɔ spider-stories mu no, ankyɛ na wanyin, wanyin prɛko pɛ. Na ne maame ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Abusuakuo no nyinaa mu mbra no, ɛnni biara a ɛsɛ sɛ wohwɛ gye mmɛw dua no, ɛnsɛ sɛ wopue bi da. Ɔkaa sɛ, Maame, metee. Afei, da bi maame no gyaw no hɔ kɔɔ afuo. Ɔkɔɔ frɛɛ m'adɔfo no, na ɔne no bɛdii agoru, na ɔkyeree ne asen nsɔmu na ɛbuu fam. Na abaayewa no kaa sɛ, Saa waist-beads hama a etwa to no a woabubu no yi, kɔ ma me palm-trees fiber bi a metumi akyekyere no. abarimaa no kɔɔ hɔ kɔtɔɔ bi na ɔde bae. Abaayewa no kaa sɛ, Mempɛ saa, gye nea woapɛ no bi wɔ palm-trees a ɛgyina nsuo mu no. Enti abofra no hyɛɛ aseɛ hwehwɛ bi. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na nsuo no mu dua bi regyina hɔ. Ɔforoɔ no, ɔkra! Ɔkra! Ɔkra! Ɔkra! Ɔkɔsii sɛ ɔduruu dua no kɔn mu. Ɔyii ne sikkan no, na bere a ɔde ne nsa kaa no, ne dua no mu paapaee, na abofra no yafunu kɔɔ mu, na ne dua no nso toe. Ɛhɔ na ɔwɔ, na ɛrehyɛ no. Ɛnkyɛree na ne maame no baeɛ; ɔduruu nsuo no ho no, ɔhunuu shadow bi wɔ ne so, na ɔkaa sɛ:

Hwan shadow na ɛda nsuo mu te sɛ me abofra Akwasi Kwasaman?

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Maame, ɛyɛ me. "

Ɔkaa sɛ: Adɛn nti na mmɛw dua no rehyɛ wo?

Ɔkaa sɛ, Makɔgyee Palm fiber kɔmaa me dɔfo na Palm-dua no apiapiaa me.

Ɛmaame no kaa sɛ, Wo, saa mmɛw-dua yi, mia no, mia no, mia no, me ba Kwasaman.

Palm-tree squeeze no, squeeze no, squeeze no, palm-tree squeeze no.

Ɛnna maame no kɔeɛ. Ɛnna agya no nso baeɛ, na ɔkaeɛ sɛ:

Hwan shadow na ɛwɔ saa nsuo yi so te sɛ me abofra kwasaman no?

Ɔkaa sɛ, Aba, ɛyɛ me.

Ɔagya no bisaa no sɛ, "Adɛn nti na mmɛw dua no rehyɛ wo?"

Ɔkaa sɛ: "Mekɔɔ sɛ merekɔtɔ Palm fiber de kɔmaa me dɔfo na Palm-dua no piapiaa me. "

Ɔ (agya) kae sɛ, "Wo, me mmɛw dua yi, mia no, mia no, me ba Kwasaman.

Palm-tree squeeze no, squeeze no, palm-tree squeeze no. "

Na agya no firi fie kɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ Etire a ɔwɔ akuraa hɔ, na Etire a ɔwɔ akuraa hɔ nso frɛɛ nnipa no nyinaa, na wɔbɛduruu mmɛw dua no nan ho, na Etire a ɔwɔ akuraa hɔ no maa ne nne so sɛ:

"Hwan shadow na ɛwɔ nsuo no so sɛ me nana Akwasi Kwasaman? "

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Nokuru, ɛyɛ me. "

Etire a ɔwɔ akuraa no mu kae sɛ: Dɛn nti na mmɛw dua no rehyɛ mo?

Ɔkaa sɛ: "Mekɔɔ sɛ merekɔtɔ Palm fiber de kɔmaa me dɔfo na Palm-dua no piapiaa me. "

Etire a ɔwɔ akuraa hɔ no kae sɛ, "Wo, saa mmɛw-dua yi, squeeze no, squeeze no, my grandson Kwasaman.

Palm-tree squeeze no, squeeze no, palm-tree squeeze no. "

Etire akuraa no mu bɔɔ, na nkuraasefoɔ nyinaa too dwom koro kyerɛɛ mmɛw-dua no sɛ wɔnpiapim no. Na afei ne dɔfo no baeɛ, na ɔkaa sɛ: "Hwan shadow na ɛwɔ nsuo no mu, te sɛ me dɔfo Akwasi Kwasaman deɛ no? "

Ɔkaa sɛ: "Ɛyɛ me, wo ɔdɔfo ankasa. "

Ɔkaa sɛ: Adɛn nti na mmɛw dua no rehyɛ wo?

Ɔkaa sɛ: "Mekɔɔ sɛ merebɛtwa Palm fiber ama wo, na Palm-dua no apiapiaa me. "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Palm-tree no gyae no, gyae no, gyae no.

Palm-tree gyae me dɔfo, Akwasi Kwasaman.

Palm-tree gyae no, gyae no, gyae no, Palm-tree gyae no. "

Bere a ɔkaa saa no, mmɛw dua no bueeɛ kakra, na nadɔfo no yafunu puei kakra. Ɔsan maa ne nne so bio, na ɔkae sɛ:

"Hwan shadow na ɛwɔ nsuo no so, sɛ me dɔfo Akwasi Kwasaman deɛ no? "

Ɔkaa sɛ: "Ɛyɛ me, wo ɔdɔfo ankasa. "

Ɔkaa sɛ: Adɛn nti na mmɛw dua no rehyɛ wo?

Ɔkaa sɛ: "Mekɔɔ sɛ merebɛtwa Palm fiber ama wo, na Palm-dua no apiapiaa me. "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Palm-tree no gyae no, gyae no, gyae no.

Palm-tree gyae me dɔfo, Akwasi Kwasaman.

Palm-tree gyae no, gyae no, gyae no, Palm-tree gyae no. "

Na afei ne dɔfo no puei prɛko pɛ, na ɔbaa bɛkyeree m'adɔfo bosom no, na wɔwosow kɔe; na wɔdanee ngo. Na nnipa a wɔkɔɔ hɔ ntɛm ara kɔbɔɔ bi wɔ wɔn anim no, na wɔn ho yɛ fɛ paa; nanso wɔn a wɔnnyaa bi no, na wɔn ho nni fɛ. Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
