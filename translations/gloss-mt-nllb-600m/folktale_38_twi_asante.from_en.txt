Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

MPANIMFOƆ KYERƐ SƐ, "ƐYƐ WO BUSUANI, ANAA KUNU, ANAA OBIARA A ƆWƆ ADWUMA A ƆBƐYƐ, SƐ ƆBISA WO A, BOA NO"

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse no, ka kyerɛɛ "Aso" (ne yere), "Momma yɛnkɔyɛ turo. " "Aso" kaa sɛ, "Ɛrenkɔ. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ fam kɔpraa hɔ. Bere a ɔsan baeɛ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nokware ampa sɛ worenkɔ? " "Aso" buaeɛ sɛ, "ɔbea akyiwade bi a ɔrepra fam. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Sɛ ɔkyena a, yɛnkɔ hyew turo mu vyera no. " "Aso" kae sɛ, "Ɔbea akyiwade bi a ɔrehyew turo mu vyera, na me deɛ, mɛnkɔhwehwɛ sɛ ogya no hyew me anaa? " Enti Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ na ɔsan baeɛ. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Aso", me srɛ mo, momma yɛnka bom na yɛn dua nneɛma. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mekaa sɛ, merenni hɔ. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, " mehunu mmusu. " Ɛneɛ ɔkɔdonoe ne nyinaa fɛfɛɛfɛ. Seesei na ɛwɔ hɔ no, na nnɔbaeɛ no aba. Ɛda bi na "Aso" kɔɔ afuo a ɛwɔ hɔ no. Ɔbaa bɛka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, Kwaku, nnɔbaeɛ no aba turo no mu, na da bɛn na yɛbɛkɔ hɔ akɔboaboa wɔn ano? Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Ho! nea Nkramoni kae no, mentumi nte aseɛ koraa. " "Aso" kae sɛ, " Ɛdeɛn na ɛyɛ; kasa sɛ ebia mɛte wo ase. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Merekɔ na meresane aba. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ hɔ kɔɔ turo no mu, na ɛhɔ na ɔgyinaa hɔ kyɛe, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ, " Me, Kwaku Ananse no, ha na me wɔ, na mɛtra ase ama me, ɔbaa kɛkɛ yi, ayɛ me kwasea? "Kwaku Ananse faa segkan, agyan, ne asɔ, na ɔtwee amena kɛse pa ara. Ɔfaa afuo no mu nnɔbaeɛ no nyinaa de guu mu, na ɔsan kɔɔ fie. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Aso" no, bra ha, na menka amaneɛbɔ no nkyerɛ wo. "Aso" baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ: "Mekɔɔ kɔbisabisaa kramoni bi me ahonyadeɛ ho asɛm, ɔka sɛ aka nna nwɔtwe ama me sɛ mewu, enti merenni afuo bio, nso worenni hɔ bio, na sɛ mewu a, ɛhɔ na wobɛkɔ asie me. Meku amena kɛseɛ wɔ hɔ, na ɛhɔ na wode me bɛsie. " Nna nwɔtwe na ɛkɔɔ so; Kwaku Ananse yɛɛ ne ho sɛ wawu. Ananse no maa wɔn ho so, na nnipa no kɔsiee no wɔ ne turo no mu hɔ. Wɔsan baa fie na wɔyɛɛ ayiyɛ amanne pa. "Aso" nso yɛɛ kununa ho amanneɛ. Bere a wɔreyɛ awotwe-da sie no, "Aso" kae sɛ, "Awoo, ma menkɔhwɛ me kunu turo! " "Aso" kɔɔ hɔ, na ɛhɔ na turo no da, dadwa! "Aso" kae sɛ, "Ao, turo a me kunu yɛɛ no na maniwa da so no, nnipa bi abɛdidi mu aduane nyinaa. " Ɛneɛ ɔsuo na ɔtoo nnome (wɔ wɔn so), kae sɛ, " (Me frɛ) Kwaku Ananse obi a ɔwɔ honhom, wo afuo mu nnɔbaeɛ a wode gyaw me no nyinaa, nnipa abɛdidi. "

Kwaku Ananse maa ne nne so na ɔtoo dwom sɛ:

" Gyae (complaining)! Me kɔn mu aduane nko na meadi!

Stop (complaining), "Aso" taboos clearing nwira.

gyae (complaining), me kɔn mu aduane nko na meadi!

Gyae (complaining), Aso akyiwadeɛ a ɛrehyew (turo mu wira). Gyae (complaining), me kɔn dɔteɛ nko na meadi!

Gyae (complaining), "Aso" taboos planting.

Gyae (nwiinwii), me kɔn mu aduan nkutoo na meedi!"

Ɛhɔ ara na "Aso" gyinaa hɔ dwoodwoo, na ɛbɔɔ hu. Ɔkɔɔ fie kɔfrɛɛ Adanko, Hare no, sɛ ɔmmra mmɛte nsɛmfua bi a ɛwɔ ne kunu afuo hɔ. Hare no ne no kyinkyin. "Aso" bɛduruu afuo a ɛwɔ hɔ no, ɔsan maa ne nne so bio na ɔsu:

"Me kunu ne Kwaku, Ananse no, na wawu na ɔgyaa me ne afuo, Na nnipa baeɛ bɛdii mu aduane no.

Spirit of Kwaku, Ananse, me kotow ma wo, me srɛ wo, kunkum wɔn mma me. "

Kwaku Ananse maa ne nne so sɛ:

"Cease (complaining), I have only eaten what was mine!

Stop (complaining), "Aso" taboos clearing nwira.

gyae (complaining), me kɔn mu aduane nko na meadi!

Gyae (nwiinwii), Aso akyiwadeɛ a ɛrehyew (turo mu wira).

Gyae (complaining), nea anka me deɛ no, medidi!

Gyae (complaining), "Aso" taboos planting.

gyae (complaining), me kɔn mu aduane nko na me didi; gyae (complaining)!

Ɔbɔadeɛ yɛɛ (nneɛma), woahunu ɔbaa a ɔmmoa ne kunu?

Gyae (complaining), me kɔn mu aduan nkutoo na me di; gyae (complaining)! "

Hare no kaa sɛ, "Mawie atie, ma yɛnkɔ fie na metumi aka nea ɛyɛ nyinaa akyerɛ wo. " Ɔne "Aso" kɔɔ fie. Hare kae sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ, Yɛnkɔ na yɛntwa turo bi, wokae sɛ worenkɔ, ɛnkɔ na yɛnkɔhyew (the dry weeds) , na wokae sɛ worenkɔ, ɛnkɔ na yɛnkɔdwa, na wokae sɛ worenkɔ, enti gyae su, ɛfiri sɛ ɔdi nea ɛyɛ ne dea, ɛfiri sɛ, ɛfiri berɛ a Ɔbɔadeɛ bɔɔ no, onhuu ɔbaa bi a wammoa ne kunu, nti gyae nteɛteɛ. ng (ɔse), na wadi ne deɛ no nyinaa. " Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Sɛ obiara reyɛ adwuma bi na ɔrebisa wo a, boa no. "

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
