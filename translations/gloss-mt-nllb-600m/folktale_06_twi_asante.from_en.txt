WƆKYERƐ SƐ NA Ɔkɔdeɛ bi wɔ hɔ (Once) a, na ne akyinkyinakyinkyin no ɔkɔhyiaa aberewa bi a ne nan so kuru. Na Ɔkɔdeɛ no se, "Gracious me! Eyi yɛ kuru a ɛwɔ claase koro no ara mu; baako a ɛte sɛɛ no, ɛmfa ho sɛ wobɔ mmɔden a, wubetumi nantew anaa? " Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Aane, kakra koraa. " Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Mo nkurɔfoɔ, ɛnnɛ yi, sɛ mebɛma mo ayɛ biribi pa (da), anka ɔkyena mobɛfa bɔne bi abɛda me ase. " Ɔkɔkɔɔ no kaa sɛ, "Aane, anka menyɛ saa. " Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ moantumi nni saa a, mate. "Ɔkaa sɛ, "To waniwa mu na bue. " Ɛnna aberewa no too naniwa mu na ɔbuee. Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ wo kuru no. " Ɛnna aberewa no baa bɛhwɛɛ no nkaedum mpo. Ɛnna ɔtoo naniwa mu bio, ɔbuee no, na ɔhunuu sɛ asase no nyinaa afiri hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, "Lock them again. " Ɔtoo wɔn mu, na obuee wɔn, na afie gyinaa hɔ pintinn. Na ɔmaa no bɛn wɔn bio. Ɔbuaa wɔn kwan ma wɔhunuu kuro bi a ne kɛseɛ yi; ɛhɔ na ɛyɛhuge. Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Old woman, it's yours (lit. wo nsa. " Ɔaberewa no kae sɛ, "Thanks I give to you. " Ɛneɛ na aberewa no kae sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mefa so da wo ase? " Ɔkɔdeɛ no kae sɛ, "Mempɛ mpo ade ketewa a ɛho nhia, nanso me deɛ, mepɛ ne saa silk-cotton-tree a ɛgyina hɔ yi. " Ɔkɔdeɛ no kae sɛ, "Saa ade yi (wodi) nea ɛwɔ hɔ no, fa. " Ɛneɛ na aberewa no de wimhyɛn tu, na ɛsii so, na ɔhyehyɛɛ berebuo, na ɔtoo nkosua mmienu, de siee hɔ. ing wɔn mu. Na ɔtwee nkosua mmienu no, na ɔkɔhwehwɛɛ biribi a ɔbɛdi ama ne mmofra. Aberewa no banana a na ɔne no te no, fii aseɛ kwiikwii sɛ, "Ehe! Ware! " Aberewa no kae sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na ɛyɛ asɛm? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Momma me nkwe akraman mma. " Aberewa no kae sɛ, "Ɛhe na mebɛgye akraman mmofra? " Aberewa no nana ketewa no fii aseɛ bio sɛ, " kwiikwii! " kwiikwii! " Na aberewa no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ asɛm? " Na abofra no kae sɛ, "Momma me nkyinkyim akraman mma, efisɛ sɛ minnya bi nkyinkyim a, me wu. " Na aberewa no kae sɛ, "Ah! ɛsɛ sɛ eyi, me nana wu wɔ akraman mma ahiasɛm ho? Sɔre, fa nkuma kɔbɔ silk-cotton-dua no na fa akraman mma no brɛ me. " Na akuraasefoɔ no kɔɔ hɔ, (nkuma no bɔɔ), Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! Ɛkame ayɛ sɛ dua no rekɔ fam, bere a akɔre mma no ɔpanin no huri kɔɔ soro, na ɔbaa bɛgyinaa berebuo no ano, na ɔbɔɔ su bi; ɔfrɛɛ maame no, na ɔkae sɛ:

Afei ɔgyee aduane a na ɔde ama ne mmofra no. Ɛnna ɔgyaee wɔn kwan sɛ, "Mekɔ, me kɔ, sɛ aberewa no ba bɛgye wo akɔ a, ma no nnye wo. " Ɛnna aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Kɔbɔ dua no na fa abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no brɛ me nana sɛ ɔnwe. " Na wɔkɔɔ hɔ ne mprenu so, Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! Ɛyɛɛ sɛ dua no bɛkɔ fam, berɛ a Eagle ba no firi adi baeɛ na ɔgyinaa berebuo no ano, na ɔfrɛɛ ne maame sɛ:

Wɔde akɔre no mma no kɔe, wɔde baako maa aberewa no, nanso baako a aka no de wimhyɛn kɔɔ hɔ wɔ Wawa-dua so. Baako no, wɔroasted no, na wɔde maa aberewa no nua ba no, na ɔde kaa bɔdeɛ a wɔato no ho. Ɛnkyɛree koraa na Ɔkɔdeɛ no baeɛ. Ɔduruu dua a na wɔatwa no no, ɔhunuu ne mmofra no mu baako a ɔte hɔ, ɔbisaa no nea asi, na ɔkaa amaneɛbɔ no nyinaa kyerɛɛ no. Ɔkɔdeɛ no firii hɔ kɔɔ akuraa ase baa aberewa no. Bere a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, na aberewa no babarima no redi ne mmofra no mu baako. Ɔkae sɛ, "Old woman, I congratulate you. " Ɔpuee firi ɔbaa panyin no fie na ɔhyɛɛ ne nkonyaayi ase wɔ kuro no kuro akyi. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sanguri," na onipa biara ayera, na ɔsan kaa sɛ, "Sanguri," na fie biara mu paapaee prɛko pɛ na tenabea biara anka. "Sanguri," akuraa no san bɛyɛɛ kwaeɛ bio. "Sanguri," na ɔbaa panyin no amane san bae. Na ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Ɔbaa dada, woahu, ɛno nti na mpanimfoɔ kyerɛ sɛ, sɛ obi yɛ wo papa a, gya no ase denam papa a woyɛ no so na nnye no bɔne mfa nkamfo no. " Wei deɛ, mabasɛm a meaka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi ayɛ nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a wode bɛkamfo me (wɔ nasɛmka ho).
