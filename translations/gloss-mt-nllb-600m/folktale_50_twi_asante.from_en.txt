SƐNEA ABOSOM, NYAMONYAN KAKRAA NO, BAA EWIASE

NA ƐWƆ HƆ BERE BI A ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ; ɔwoo mmofra du-baako; na da biara a ɔbɛsɔre na wasiesie aduane no, na mmofra no adi ne nyinaa. Wɔn maame annya bi nni. Mprempren ɔdwenee ho bere tenten, na ɔkɔɔ afuw no mu kɔbɔɔ silk-cotton dua no amanneɛ sɛ, Merebɛsoma me mmofra dumien aba wo ase ha asafuntum tetew; na sɛ wɔba a, twa wo nkorabata du-baako na kum me mmofra no. Silk-cotton dua no kaa sɛ, Mete no, na mɛyɛ ama wo. Ɛnna maame no fii hɔ kɔɔ fie, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne mmofra sɛ, Morekum mo. t kɔ afuw mu, silk-cotton dua no ase; sakora ahorow na ɛwɔ hɔ, kɔgyee na kɔ (akyi). " Ɛnna mmofra no fii hɔ. Wɔkɔɔ kɔduruu silk-cotton dua no ho. Nɔmba Dubiako kaa sɛ, "Nɔmba Biako, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Abien, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Abiɛsa, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Anan, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Anum, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Nsia, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Ason, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Awotwe, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Nkron, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Du, gyina hɔ; Na me nso, Nɔmba Dubiako, megyina hɔ. " Nɔmba Dubiako, afei ɔkasa kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Munnim r baako pɛ? eason nti na maame kae sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ yɛkɔ na yɛpaw sakora ahorow? " Ne anuanom kae sɛ, "Dabi. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔaka akyerɛ saa silk-cotton tree yi sɛ, sɛ yɛkɔ hɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ ɛtwa nkorabata abɔ yɛn; enti mo nyinaa ntwa nnua, na montow ngu saa silk-cotton tree yi so. " Wɔtwaa nnua no, na wɔtow guu silk-cotton tree no so, pim! twerɛdeɛ! pim! twerɛdeɛ! (saa na ɛyɛ nnyigyei a wɔyɛeɛ no). Silk-cotton dua no susuwii sɛ mmofra no aba. Ɔyii ne nkorabata dumien, na ɔmaa ɛhwehwee fam. Dodoɔ ketewa Dubiako kaa sɛ, "Woahunu sɛ, anka sɛ yɛkɔ hɔ a, anka silk-cotton dua no bɛkunkum yɛn. " Wɔfaa sakora ahorow no de kɔɔ wɔn maame nkyɛn. Ɔnoa wɔn, na ah! Amonom hɔ ara na mmofra no adi. Wɔn maame kaa sɛ, "A! Saa asɛm yi deɛ, mentumi nnyina ano, enti mebɛfa saa mmofra yi de wɔn ama Ewiem-onyankopɔn. " Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔkɔɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn nkyɛn kɔkaa ne nyinaa, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Mmofra bi, a mede wɔn ama wɔn awoɔ no, bere a mepɛ sɛ mewe no, mentumi nnya biribi nni (efiri sɛ wodi no ntɛm ara na ɛdɔɔso dodo), ɛkɔm rekum me; ɛno nti me srɛ mo, momma mmofra no ho mbɔ wɔn. Tɛɛ na meku, na ama mayɛ biribi adi. " Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Ɛno ne asɛm no ankasa anaa? " Na ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "Mene etire bi a ne emu yɛ fitaa na ɛrekasa. " Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no yii asɛnkafoɔ, na wɔkɔtwee amoa kɛseɛ bi, a wɔde nkotokuo a abubu guu mu, kɔgyee ɔwɔ ne daboɔ bi de guu amena no mu, na wɔkataa so. Na afei messenger no kɔɔfrɛ mmofra no. Wɔduruu baabi a amena no da no, ntɛm ara na Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Nɔmba Biako, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Abien, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Abiɛsa, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Anan, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Anum, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Nsia, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Ason, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Nwɔtwe, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Nkron, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Du, gyina hɔ; Na me nso, dodoɔ ketewa Dubiako, megyina hɔ; Ɛsɛ sɛ wotwa mu. s ha, ɛnsɛ sɛ wotwam wɔ hɔ. " Nanuanom kae sɛ, "Adɛn nti, sɛ kwan a ɛtrɛw da hɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛtwa nwura no mu? " Mprempren, ɛrekɔ no, na wɔn nyinaa akura nnua. Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Tow saa nnua yi mu baako gu kwan yi so. " Wɔtow guu hɔ, na ɛtwee mu kɔtoo amena no mu, na ɛno ne ahweaseɛ no nnyigyei. Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Wɔre wɔ hɔ a, wuhu, anka sɛ yɛnam hɔ a, anka yɛn nyinaa awu. " Enti wɔfaa kwan bi kɔɔ ɔsoro-anyame no nkyɛn. Na ɔsoro-anyame no ayɛ sɛ wɔatu tokuro, (covered over, and nkonnwa asisi wɔn so, na ama sɛ mmofra no bɛba atena wɔn so a, wɔbɛkɔ akɔhwe tokuro no mu. Na mmofra no adu ɔsoro-anyame no anim. Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Nkonnwa no ato hɔ, ebia wobɛkɔ akɔtena so. " Ɛneɛ Nɔmba Dubiako kaa sɛ, "Yɛn ni, sɛ yɛbɛtumi atena nkongua fɛfɛ sɛɛ so a, ɛnneɛ, Sire, yɛbɛtena nkyɛn ha. " Ɛhɔ ara na Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no hwɛɛ mmofra no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Mɛsoma saa mmofra yi akɔ Owu akuraa ase. " Ɛduruu anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔfrɛɛ mmofra no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Monhwɛ! Ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ Owuo baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ, na wokɔgye sika abua, sika kɔkɔɔ a wɔde kum ade, sika kɔkɔɔ a wɔde kum ade, sika kɔkɔɔ whet-stone, ne sika kɔkɔɔ fly-switch. " Nɔmba Du-baako kaa sɛ, "Wo ne yɛn wura, baabiara a wobɛsoma yɛn no, yɛbɛkɔ. " Ɔsoro-anyame no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ. " Wɔmaa mmofra no kwan kɔɔ Owuo akuraa ase. Wɔduruu hɔ no, Owuo kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti, bere a ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara ba ha da no, moaba ha? " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Na yɛrekyinkyin na yɛaba ha (hokwan so koraa). " Owuo kae sɛ, "O, all right then. " Mprempren na Owuo wɔ mmofra du; ne ho ka ho no, na ɛyɛ du-baako. Bere a nneɛma fii aseɛ yera (i. e. anwummerɛ no), Owuo kyekyɛɛ mmofra no mu mmiako mmiako maa ne mmofra mmiako mmiako, berɛ a ɔne Nɔmba Dubiako kɔhyiaa. Berɛ a ɛyɛ sum no, ɛnna Owuo hyɛɛ ne se (kosi sɛ ɛhyerɛn) kɔkɔɔ, na ama ɔne wɔn akye Nɔmba Dubiako. Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Owuo, mennya nna. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda? " Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Sɛ wode sika abua bi bɛma me nom sigaret bere tiaa bi a, anka mɛda. " Owuo gyee maa no. Bere tiaa bi akyi no, Owuo san sɔɔ ne se no bio, wɔ ahyɛdeɛ sɛ ɔnkɔkyere Nɔmba Dubiako. Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Owuo, mennya nna. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Berɛ bɛn na wobɛda? " Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Sɛ wobɛfa sika kɔkɔɔ nsa kotokuo bi aba me nkyɛn a, anka mɛkɔ ada. " Owuo de baa ne nkyɛn. Bio nso, ankyɛ na Owuo bɛkyeree Nɔmba Dubiako. Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Minnya nna. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Berɛ bɛn na wobɛda? " Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa sika kɔkɔɔ a mede bɛwe kakra, afei ebia mɛda. " Owuo gyee maa no. berɛ tiawa bi twaam, na na Owuo rebɛkyere no. Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Nananom, mennya nna. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ, bere bɛn na wobɛda? " Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Nananom, sɛ wobɛkɔ na woabɛn me sika kɔkɔɔ whet-stone bi a, anka mɛtumi ada. " Owuo kɔɔ na ɔde bae. Bio, ankyɛ na Owuo sɔreee bio. Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Oh, Nana, mekae sɛ mennya nnae. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Na ɛbɛyɛ dɛn da a wobɛda? " Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Sɛ wobɛfa koraa bi a nokuro ahyɛ mu ma, na woakɔ petepete nsuo mu, na woayɛ aduane bi ama me adi a, anka metumi ada. " Owuo maa ne sɔneɛ so, na ɔkɔɔ asuwa no ho. Bere a ɔde nsuo no petepetee mu no, tokuro a ɛwɔ sɔneɛ no mu no maa ɛtwam. Afei Nɔmba Dubiako ka kyerɛɛ ne anuanom sɛ, "Sɔre dwane. " Wɔsɔree dwanee. Ɛnna Nɔmba Dubiako kɔtwaa borɔdeɛ nsateaa, de hyɛɛ baabi a nanuanom da no, na wɔde ntoma kataa so. Afei na Owuo wɔ asuwa no ho, ɔretete nsuo. Mprempren Ɔbarima wuo frɛɛ Ɔbaa wuo no sɛ, "Ho wɔ hɔ, Owuo. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Adwo. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn na woreyɛ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Alas, ɛnyɛ abofra ketewa bi na me nsa aka no? Bere a merekɔkyere no no, ɔka sɛ, 'Minnya nna da'; ɔde me nneɛma nyinaa akɔ, na afei ɔka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mefa sɔneɛ na meprɛte nsuo mu. " Ɔbarima wuo no kaa sɛ, "Ah, woyɛ abofra ketewa anaa? Seesei, sɛ wotutu nhaban na wotwiri a, ne sɔneɛ emu, na afei wosow nsuo no, anka ɛnyɛ yiye? " Ɔbaa wuo no kae sɛ, "O, ɛyɛ nokware dɛn. " Ɔtwee nhaban no, de emu guu so, na ɔsɔɔ nsuo no guu mu, na ɛkɔɔ so. Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Owuo, wobaa ha dedaw? Woi aduane no. " Owuo no yɛɛ aduane no, ɔsɔɔ ne se wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ ɔnkum Nɔmba Dubiako nuanom na ɔnnoa wɔn aduane. Berɛ a ɔkɔɔ no, ɔanhwehwɛ wɔn mu yie, na ɔno ankasa kum ne mmofra du no nyinaa. Ade kyee, anɔpa pa ara, bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, Owuo sɔreee, na ɔtenaa hɔ wɔ ogya no ho. Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Nanabea, huruiɛ bi te wo nufuɔ so. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Fetch the fly-switch which lies there, and kill it for me. " Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Ao me Nyame! me; wo nsunsuansoɔ no mu nipa, bere a huruiɛ bi bɛsi wo so, na sika kɔkɔɔ fly-switch bi nso ada hɔ (wo de biribi dada yi bɛdi dwuma), ma mennye no mma wo mmɛkum no. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Kɔ fa firi r no so. Om. " Nɔmba Dubiako na ɛkɔɔ so de bae. Ɔhyɛɛ da pam nwansena no, ɔankum no. Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "O, saa da no, baabi a tsetse nwansena yi bɛhome no, ɛhɔ na me ne no bɛhome. " Ɛneɛ Nɔmba Dubiako kɔɔ dan no mu, na ɔgyee ne kotokuo a ɔde sika abua no ne nneɛma no nyinaa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Nnanom owuo, biribiara rennɔɔso gye sɛ menya huruiɛ no, mehyɛ kotokuo yi mu, na mede me ba wo nkyɛn. " Nɔmba Dubiako hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ, yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! Ɔduruu kuro no awieeɛ no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Ho, hɔ, Nanabaa wuo, fa me bɔne kyɛ me abebuo yi, nanso sɛ woannye kwaseampa a, anka mɛtumi ayi wo nneɛma nyinaa afiri wo ho; anka me anuanom a me ne wɔn baeɛ no bɛtumi anya dwane kwan; na anka mɛtumi ama wo nso woawu wo mmofra du no nyinaa? Me deɛ, me rekɔ. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Wo, abofra a ɔte sɛɛ, baabiara a wobɛhome no, ɛhɔ na mebɛhome. " Nɔmba Dubiako huri kɔɔ nanim, yiridi! yiridi! Yiridi! Na Owuo nso dii nakyi. Nɔmba Dubiako rekɔ no, ɔboroo nanuanom so, na na wɔte kwan no so. Na wɔreyɛ nnomaa afiri. Nɔmba Dubiako kae sɛ, "Wonnya ankɔ? Owuo reba, enti momma yɛnhwehwɛ kwan bi a yɛbɛfa so aguan. " Mprempren Owuo bɛtoo wɔn so. Nɔmba Dubiako faa aduro bi bɛhwie guu ne anuanom so, na wɔkɔɔ silk-cotton dua bi atifi. Owuo gyinaa Silk-cotton dua no nan ase. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Here nko ara, mihuu saa mmofra yi, na wɔakɔ he? " Nɔmba Dubiako na na ɔte soro hɔ, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne anuanom sɛ, "Merebɛyɛ no nsuo. " Ne anuanom kaa sɛ, "E, ɔrehwehwɛ sɛ yɛbɛkyere no, na yɛadwane atena ha, na woda so ara se, 'Merebɛyɛ no nsuo.' " Nɔmba Dubiako ampene so, na ɔyɛɛ nsuo wɔ Owuo ho. Owuo kae sɛ, "Ah, ha na wayɛ, kosi da a woahunu ɔhaw. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Wo, abofra, a wote hɔ, 'Kyere-he-ne, Kyere-he-ne'. " Ɛhɔ na mmofra no mu biako hwee ase. "Kyere-he-ne," sɛkɛnd biako hwee ase. Seesei Nɔmba Du-baako pɛ na aka. Owuo kae sɛ, "Abofra, 'kyere-he-ne,' na Nɔmba Du-baako huri kɔtoo fam, nkyere! " Na Owuo nso kɔɔ Silk-cotton dua no so. Nɔmba Du-baako kae sɛ, "Wo, ɔbaa kɛse pa ara, wo nso, 'kyere-he-ne'." Na Owuo nso ba fam, tum! na wawu. Nɔmba Du-baako no kɔtwee aduro bi, bobɔɔ no wɔ ne pALMS ntam, na wɔde petepete ne anuanom so, na wɔsɔree. Nɔmba Du-baako no na ɔrekɔto aduro no agu, na emu bi guu Owuo so, na Owuo nyanee. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Woakum me, na woasane akanyan me; kosi da a me ne wo bɛnya asɛm. " Ɛneɛ na wɔn nyinaa fii aseɛ dwanee prɛko pɛ, kiri! kiri! Owuo na na ɔredi wɔn akyi. Wɔrekɔ no, na asuo kɛseɛ bi da nsuyiri mu. Nɔmba Du-baako ne nanuanom kɔduruu hɔ no, na wɔnim asuguare, na wɔwim. Nɔmba Du-baako pɛ na na ɔnnim asuguare. Ɛnna mmofra no gyinaa nkyɛn; na wɔresu, na wɔresu, na wɔn anom abere. Nɔmba Dubiako no deɛ, ɔdanee ɔboɔ. Owuo bɛduruu asubonten. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, mmofra yi, mugyina hɔ a, momma me nnya ɔboɔ a me bɛto de abɔ wɔn anom a ahonhon. " Owuo, bere a ɔhwehwɛɛ fam no, ɔhunuu ɔboɔ bi a ɛda hɔ; ɔgyeeɛ, na ɔtow guu hɔ. Bere a ɔboɔ no retwa kwan no, ɛkaa sɛ, "Mframa, momfa me nkɔto nkyɛn. " Ɛdanee kɔɔ nkyɛn. Nɔmba Du-baako kaa sɛ, "Mewɔ ha. " Owuo kaa sɛ, "Aha! saa abofra yi! Me nni asɛm biara bio a mebɛka akyerɛ mo, nanso nea mewɔ kyerɛ mo ne sɛ: kɔtena fie, na sesa wo ho bɛyɛ nkumaa no mu baako, na sɛ obiara a mepɛ sɛ mefa no ba wo nkyɛn a, wobɛbɔ me amanneɛ; sɛ mepɛ a, mebɛfiri hɔ na mayɛ no akyɛdeɛ ama wo; nanso nea mepɛ (wɔ nsasaeɛ mu) no, ɛsɛ sɛ wogye ma me. " (Ɛno ne afɔdeɛ) Saa na Abosom yɛ. -gods) baa ewiase. Wɔfiri mmɔfra nketewa dodoɔ du-baako mu.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi ayɛ wo nokware, ne ahomegyeɛ a wubetumi akamfo me (wɔ nasɛmka ho).
