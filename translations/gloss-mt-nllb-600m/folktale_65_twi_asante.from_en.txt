Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA AKETEKYIRE, SAKRAWI NO, NYAA ƐSE A AHYE

AKETEKYIRE, Sakrawi no, ne Kwaku Ananse, Ananse no, hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ wɔrebɛkɔ hwehwɛ aburoo a ɛwɔ ahyɛdeɛ sɛ wɔbɛdono. Ɛnna wɔnyaa aburoo aba mmienu pɛ. Ɛnna Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Mekɔdono me deɛ. " Ɛnna Sakrawi no kaa sɛ, "Merebɛroast mine no wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ me bɛwe. " Kwaku Ananse kɔdono ne deɛ. Sakrawi no nso, ɔgyee ne deɛ, de too ogya no so. Egyee no pii sɛ ɛnoa, na ɔtenaa hɔ (ne nkyɛn). Ananse no nso, ɔkɔɔ afuw mu, na ɔregye nani wɔ nkrantɛbɔtɔ a ɛkata aburoo no ase. Aburoo no kaa sɛ, "Don't peep into my aniwa, otherwise your nsateaa goes into my aniwa. " Kwaku Ananse na ɔredane n'aniwa ahwɛ ne Kraman; ne Kraman no kaa sɛ, "Don't need to look at me, it wasn't me who did it. " Kwaku Ananse rebɛbu poma bi de abɔ Kraman no, bere a poma no kaa sɛ, "Don't break me, for (if you do) your Kraman will bite me. " Kwaku Ananse baa fie kɔkaa asɛm no nyinaa kyerɛɛ Sakrawi. Bere a ɔresere no, ɔhwee ogya no mu. Saa na Sakrawi no nyaa ne se hyeeɛ. Wei, me abasɛm a me aka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
