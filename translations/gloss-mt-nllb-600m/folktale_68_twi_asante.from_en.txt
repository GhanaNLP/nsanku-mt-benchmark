Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA KWAKU ANANSE, ANANSE NO, BƐYƐƐ POW

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse no, kɔɔ hɔ kɔtoo Nyankopɔn, Ewiem-onyankopɔn, na ɔkaa sɛ, Ma me aba baako aburoo na mema wo nnipa akuraa bi nyinaa (wɔ nsasaeɛ mu). Ewiem-onyankopɔn no kaa sɛ, Wuntumi. Ananse no buae sɛ, Sɛ mentumi a, mehyɛ wo maame, Nsia, sɛ me ne me etire ntam no bɛtua mmara toɔ. Ewiem-onyankopɔn kaa sɛ, gye no (aburoo no nnɔbaeɛ aba) na yɛ. off. Ananse no hyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ akuraase bi. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no asɛm a ɛdi hɔ. Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no asoma me; ɔse, ɛsɛ sɛ mede aburoo aba baako yi kɔ baabi, na meba (nkɔ baabiara) a, ɛsɛ sɛ mede aburoo nna nnɔbaeɛ aba yi to nkokɔ so. ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, E! sɛ nkokɔ ne aburoo da baako a, ɛbɛyɛ yie anaa? Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, All you have to do is let them sleep together. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, Kɔ na fa to hɔ. Ade kyee anɔpa kɛseɛ no, Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ ɔrebɛhwehwɛ no, nanso aburoo no aba baako no nni hɔ bio. Ananse no kae sɛ, "Head-man, the nkokɔ have eaten the Sky-gods maize. " The head-man said, I am not able to have any mansotwe about this, so take the anomaa and go. Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ faako akɔkɔ kɛseɛ bi, na ɔde kɔɔ akuraa foforɔ so. Ɛduruu hɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no abasɛm bi sɛ, Ewiem-onyankopɔn na ɔsomaa me, na mebɛda ha, na saa anomaa yi a me kura no, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔne odwan no da. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, Momma no mmra na monhyɛ no hɔ. Anadwo no, Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ nguandaban no mu kunkum anomaa no. Ɔfaa anomaa no dwensɔtwaa na ɔde hyɛɛ odwennini kɛse bi mmɛn so. Ade kyee anɔpa kɛseɛ, ɔkɔɔ ɔpanyin no nkyɛn wɔ hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Fa anomaa no mma me. " Bere a wɔbɛkɔ akɔgye no no, na wɔahunu sɛ anomaa no awu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ah! ɛno ne ɔsoro-anyame no anomaa a odwan akum no no. " ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Mentumi nni ho mansotwe biara, enti fa odwan kɔ. " Kwaku Ananse faa odwan no kɔeɛ. Ɔduruu akuraa foforɔ so. Ɔkɔɔ ɔpanyin no fie. ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, "Aban bɛn? " Ananse no buae sɛ, "Ɔsoro-anyame no asoma me, na me na me kura odwan no, ɛne anantwie no bɛda abom. " ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, "O! ma ɛnne odwan no. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Dabi, ɛfiri sɛ eyi ne ɔsoro-anyame no nguanten soronko no, ɛne anantwie no na ɛnne. " Etire-onipa no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na fa to hɔ, ɛnneɛ. " Ananse no kɔɔ na ɔde sii hɔ. Ɛduruu anadwo no, Kwaku Ananse kɔkum odwan no, na ɔde ne mogya kɔtoo anantwie no mu baako etire so. Ade kyee anɔpa kɛseɛ no, Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Head-man, ma me me me me odwan. " Wɔkɔɔ anantwifie hɔ, odwan no na ɛwɔ hɔ, awu. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Head-man, ɛyɛ Sky-god no odwan a anantwie no akum no. " Head-man kae sɛ, "Me ntumi nni mo ho mansotwe biara wɔ saa asɛm yi ho, enti monyi nantwie a ɛkunkum no no. " Kwaku Ananse faa nantwie no, na ɔfirii hɔ, na ɔbaa akuraa foforɔ so. Ɔde nantwie no kɔkyekyeree hɔ, na ɔkɔdaa hɔ. Bere a ɔda hɔ no, wɔbɛka kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no sɛ ne nkoa no mu biako awu. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ wotow no gu, ɛfiri sɛ me nni adaka no. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, mepɛ, ɛfiri sɛ ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no kyerɛɛ me sɛ, sɛ menya funu bi a wɔbɛto ato hɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ mede sesa no nantwie. " Nkuraasefoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Dabi. " (Nanso) Kwaku Ananse (insisted), "Ɛsɛ sɛ wode ba me nkyɛn, na sɛ wokɔtɔn nantwie no a, wobɛnya asuanu sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i.e. £4), na wobɛtumi de (saa sika yi) atɔ odonkɔ foforɔ. " Nnipa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nokware wɔ mu. " Enti wɔyɛɛ nsasaeɛ no. Kwaku Ananse faa funu no kɔeɛ, na ɔbaa akuraa foforɔ so. Bere a ɔduruu hɔ no, ɔkasa kyerɛɛ ɔbarima no ti so panyin no sɛ, Ma me abofra yi kwan ma ɔne wo mmofra nna wɔ saa pato yi mu, abrannaa dan a ɛbue yi mu. Afei (ɛsɛ sɛ meka kyerɛ wo) abofra yi nyɛ fɛ, ɔwɔ suban a ahyɛ mu sɛ ɔrebubu mframa anadwo, enti sɛ mframa pae a, ma mmofra foforo no mmɔ no. Afei na ɔbarima no awu nna abiɛsa ni. Ná mmofra no ne ne funu da hɔ. Mprempren wɔtee nka sɛ ade bi rehyew. Wɔkae sɛ, "Saabofra no abubu mframa, momma yɛn bɔ no. " Wɔhwee no so, titim! titim! titim! (na ɛyɛ wɔn akontontu ahodoɔ no nnyigyei). Ade kyee anɔpa koraa, Kwaku Ananse kɔfrɛɛ nabofra no. Bere a ɔkɔɔ no, na ɔwɔ hɔ a wawu. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Head-man, wo mmofra akum Ewiem-Onyankopɔn n'anom asɛmpakafo a na ɔka me ho no. " Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Me ntumi nni asɛm yi ho mansotwe biara, enti wobɛtumi de mmofra no akɔ. " Ananse no buae sɛ, "(Mempene so) gye sɛ wo, ɔpanyin, ne nnipa a wɔwɔ kuro yi mu nyinaa kɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn anim, na woka w'asɛm. " Ewiem-Man no bɔɔ ne dawuro sɛ, "I can't all proclaim, " ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn anim, kɔka yɛn asɛm. " Ananse no dii kwan, nnipa no nyinaa dii nakyi. Bere a Kwaku Ananse duu kuro no kuro akyi no, ɔsomaa messenger bi sɛ ɔnkɔka nkyerɛ Ewiem-onyankopɔn sɛ ɔne kuro bi mu nnipa reba. Ewiem-onyankopɔn no frɛɛ nasɛmbli, na ɔtenaa aseɛ (wɔ nahemfiefo ntam). Bere a Kwaku Ananse hyɛn mu no, ɔmaa ne dwom so sɛ: Kwaku Ananse, ɛwoo, ɛwoo, menyaa aburoo aba baako sɛ menkɔgye anomaa.

Kwaku Ananse, ɔwoo, ɔwoo, mede anomaa no kɔgyee odwan.

Mefaa odwan no kɔɔ kɔgyee nantwie, mefaa nantwie no kɔɔ kɔgyee funu, mefaa funu no kɔgyee nnipa a wɔwɔ kuro bi mu.

Wɔn mu biara kae sɛ, "Wo te dwom a Kwaku Ananse reto no anaa? " Kwaku Ananse maa ne dwom so bio sɛ:

"Kwaku Ananse, oh, oh, me nyaa aburoo aba baako sɛ me nkɔfa anomaa.

Kwaku Ananse, ɔwoo, ɔwoo, mede anomaa no kɔgyee odwan.

Mefaa odwan no kɔɔ kɔgyee nantwie, mefaa nantwie no kɔɔ kɔgyee funu, mefaa funu no kɔgyee nnipa a wɔwɔ kuro bi mu.

Kwaku Ananse bɛduruu ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no anim, na ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no bisaa Kwaku Ananse amaneɛbɔ. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Aburoo aba baako a me nsa kaa sɛ me ne kuro bi tefoɔ bɛba no, kuro bi nnipa na ɛgyina hɔ. " Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Mmɔ no ase. " (Wɔteɛɛm, "E! e! e! "Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no kaeɛ sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mede ma wo sɛ aseda? " Ananse no kaeɛ sɛ, "Mempɛ hwee; (baabi) a ɛwɔ aprannaa so na mepɛ. " Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no kaeɛ sɛ, "O, oh! me deɛ yi, wobɛwu wɔ ohia mu, wo a woakɔfa pii aba nanso wose wo mpɛ hwee no. " Ɛno nti na ohia renfa Kwaku Ananse nkyɛn da.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
