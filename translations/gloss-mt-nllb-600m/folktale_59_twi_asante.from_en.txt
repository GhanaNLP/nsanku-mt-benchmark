Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ 'ƆKRAMAN, KRAMAN NO, NTUMI NYƐ NE AKORƆMMƆ (AKWAN) NO AYARESA A ANYA DA'

NA ƐWƆ HƆ BERE BI A Kraman BI TE, na akorɔmmɔ suban nti, wantumi annya ɔbaa amfa no. Enti ɔtuu kwan kɔɔ kuro bi mu sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔfa ɔbaa ne no atra. (Ɔwaree, na ɔne no san kɔɔ nakuraa hɔ.) Ɔne ne yere tenaa hɔ (berɛ bi), bere a ɔtuu kwan no, ɔsan kɔɔ nakuraa hɔ. Ɛbɛduruu berɛ bi akyi no, ne kunu de nkra kɔmaa sɛ ɔreba hɔ. Ne agya-in-law no faa ne tuo no kɔɔ nwura mu sɛ ɔrekɔkum adowa bi de ahoboa ama Kraman no ba. Ɛbɛyɛ nnansa akyi no, Kraman no kɔɔ hɔ, na ɔkɔeɛ no, ɔhunuu sɛ adowa bi honam da ogya so, adiwo mu. Ɛbɛduruu anadwo a, ɔne ne yere no kɔtoo nsa. Kraman no reyɛ sɛ ɔbɛnantew abɔnten ayɛ nsuo, berɛ a ohuu nam a ne srade no retow akɔ ogya no mu no. Kraman no san bae na ɔkɔtoo hɔ, na ɔde ne etire too sumiiɛ no so (and lay down) na ɔhwɛɛ nam no kwan tenten. Seesei nam no hwam no bɛduruu ne ho, ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛsoa, nanso wantumi. O! ɔsɔre kɔɔ baabi a nam no wɔ no, ɔweam ne nyinaa, na ɔsan bae sɛ ɔrekɔto hɔ. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, ɛduruu berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, bere a ne yere rebɛhohoro no, ɔhwehwɛɛ nam no, na sin baako mpo nni hɔ. Ne yere no kaa sɛ, "O, maame! oh, maame! obi adwe ne nam no nyinaa. " Kraman tee eyi a, naniwa wui (ie. ɔfɛre), na ɔde ne ntoma kataa netire, na ɔdaa hɔ komm. Ɛnna prɛko pɛ ara na ɔsɔreeɛ sii kwan so, ɔdwanee kɔɔ nankasa akuraa so, na ɔkaa amaneɛbɔ no, sɛnea ɔkɔdwe ne kunu-aseɛ nam, ne sɛnea naniwa awu wɔ aniwu mu, ne sɛnea ɛnam saa nti na ɔrekɔ hwehwɛ obi a ɔwɔ awuduro ano aduro bi sɛ ɔmfa akorɔmmɔ no, na ama no bi. Ɛmaa nnipa no kae sɛ, "Adanko, Hare no, ɔwɔ "medicine" bi a ɛyɛ den pa ara. " Kraman no de hareyɛ sii kwan, na ɔde kɔduruu Hare akuraa ase. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Owura Hare, medwane me ba wo nkyɛn sɛ wɔmma me mmoa, meyɛ ɔfɔmfoɔ, ɔfɔmfoɔ, ɔfɔmfoɔ, na wɔka sɛ wo wɔ aduro (a ɛyɛ ayaresa) ma saa, enti me srɛ wo, fa bi ma me, na nea ɛbɛyɛ biara ne boɔ no, me betua. " Hare no buae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nokware sɛ mewɔ bi, fa mprako nam brɛ me. "

Kraman no hyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ akuraase bi na ɔnyaa akyi nan baako maa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i.e. 3s. 6d) a ne duru yɛ duru. Hare no noa, twaa mu, na ɔde petee nnua nketewa so, na ɔyam ɛmo, ne nkyene, ne gyeene, na ɔde guu nam mu, de guu ogya so. Bere a ogya no kaa no, berɛ bi akyi no, ɔno (Kraman) tee sɛ ɛrebɔ, hyee, eeee! Kraman no hwɛɛ no berɛ tenten. Mprempren Hare no kae sɛ, "Kraman, kɔda dan ahodoɔ no mu baako mu, na me nso mɛkɔda foforɔ mu, na kɔ-ɔkyena-anwummerɛ, mɛyi saa nam (ogya) yi na mede ayɛ wo aduro. " Kraman no kɔɔ da. Hare no nso kɔɔ da. Mprempren Kraman no daeɛ daeɛ daeɛ (nanso ɔrentumi nna), na nam hwam no baa ne so ɛhwene no mu, nanso ɔkasae. Mprempren ɔtee nka bio. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Father Hare. " Hare no buae sɛ, "Aane! " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo akuraa no mu nkura bi wɔ hɔ, enti mebɛgye nam no, na mede ato me nkyɛn. " Hare no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ gye, me deɛ, me werɛ how ara ne sɛ mebɛto me nsa agu so ama ɔkyena, wɔ ahyɛdeɛ sɛ metumi ayɛ wo aduro. " Enti Kraman no kɔgyee no. Seesei Kraman no da hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Owura Hare. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Aane! " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me nkura no bɛwe, enti merebɛhyɛ me sumiiɛ ase, na me akɔda. " Hare no kaa sɛ, "Me dwene nyinaa ne sɛ, ɔkyena, bere a nneɛma bɛda adi no, mede bɛyɛ wo aduro. " Kraman no de ne ho too hɔ bio; ɔbɔ mmɔden sɛnea ɔbɛtumi biara no, ɔrentumi nkura ne ho. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Awofoɔ Hare, saa nkura yi aba me sumiiɛ ase, enti mebɛfa nam no de ato me etire so, na ɛhɔ na megyina akosi sɛ nneɛma bɛsɔre. " Hare buae sɛ, "O, me kɔ so ka sɛ, nea ɛfa me ho ne sɛ me nsa ka ɔkyena nam no de ayɛ aduro ama wo. " Kraman no faa nam no de too ne etire so, na ɛhɔ na ɔgyinaa. Ɛnna forowe no mu kakra bi bɛtɔe wɔ nanim, na ɔyam no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "O, Father Hare, saa nam yi de, saa nkura no aforo me, na ɛmmra me etire, enti mebɛkura (nam no) wɔ mano. " Hare buae sɛ, "Oho o! Me kɔ so ka sɛ, ɔkyena, bere a nneɛma bɛda adi no, me ne no bɛyɛ aduro ama wo. " Afei na ɛyɛ ntɛm wɔ nano, na ɔrekyinkyin dan no mu wɔ hɔ, ɔrekyinkyin ne dua (wɔ nsakraeɛ nnyigyei mu), fre! fre! Ɔkaa sɛ, "Father Hare, ma me nni wo ho ɛka wɔ saa nam yi ho. " Hare no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wopɛ sɛ wodi ho ɛka? " Kraman no buae sɛ, "Me kyerɛ sɛ, sen sɛ nkura yi bɛdidi no, ma me ntua wo ka. " Hare no kaa sɛ, "Oei dabi! ma ɛnkyena anɔpa mmra. " Owee! Nanso seisei Kraman no gyaee nam no. Ɛnna ɔhwee ase daeɛ. Ade kyee, anɔpa kɛseɛ pa ara, Hare no kae sɛ, "Kraman, fa saa nam no ba. " Hare no buae sɛ, "Owura Hare, adɛn nti na woante nea meka kyerɛɛ wo ɛnora sɛ, me ne wo ɛka (nam no) nni? " Hare no buae sɛ, "Enti woadi? " Kraman no kae sɛ, "Aane! Me na me dii, efiri sɛ me na me tɔɔ no. " Hare no kae sɛ, "Wo, Kraman, korɔno a wɔfrɛ no korɔno no, ɛremfa wo nkyɛn da. "

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
