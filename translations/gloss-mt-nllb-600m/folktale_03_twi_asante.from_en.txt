ƐBƐYƐƐ DƐN NA NYANSA BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU

WƆKYERƐ SƐ Kwaku, Ananse no, na ɔwɔ hɔ, na ɔtwee ne nimdeɛ nyinaa ano, boaboaa ano wɔ beae koro, de too koraa toa bi mu. Afei ɔbɔɔ dawuro sɛ ɔbɛforo dua bi akɔsɛn no so, na ama asase so nyansa nyinaa awie.

Enti ɔfaa no de kɔɔ so, na bere a ɔduruu dua no ase baabi a ɔrebɛsɛn no no, ɔfaa hama a etwa to no, na ɔde towa no kyekyeree no, na ɔde sɛn nanim, na ɔhyɛɛ ne ho sɛ ɔbɛforo dua no. Ɔforooo, na ɔforooo, na ɔforooo, na ɔforooo, kwa. Ɔbɔɔ mmɔden bio, na ɔforooo, na ɔforooo, na ɔforooo, na ɔforooo, kwa.

Afei, nɔbabarima, Ntikuma, a na ɔgyina hɔ no, kae sɛ, "Aane, waniwa awu (a ɛyɛ aniwu), anka ɛbɛyɛ yiye sɛ wobɛdanee towa no na woato wo akyi, na akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ wobɛtumi aforo? " Ɔkaa sɛ (Ananse no) "Ma emu nna hɔ, wo ne wo kasa a ɛyɛ dedaw no. " Afei ɔdanee sɛ ɔbɛforo bio sɛnea na ɛte kan no, nanso bio, ɛsow aba. Afei ɔdwenee akyiri, na (awieeɛ koraa no) ɔgyee towa no de too nakyi. Afei ɔhyɛɛ ne ho sɛ ɔbɛforo, na ɔde ahoɔhareɔ foro kɔɔ soro sɛ, Ɔkra! ɔkra! ɔkra! (Na ɛyɛ ne foro no nnyigyei); ɛhɔ na ɔkɔ. Ɔduruu baabi a nkorabata no hyɛɛ aseɛ trɛw firii dua dua no so no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ, "Me, Kwaku Ananse, obosom kumaa, Afio! Anka me nso mɛwu, me abofra a woyɛ nketewa, wo nketewa, wo nketewa - ɛhɔ na me wɔ, me boaboaa nyansa nyinaa ano (m'adwene yɛɛ me saa) wɔ baabi, nanso ebi da so a me mpo menhunuu, na lO! me abofra yi, akokoaa a ɔnom nufu yi, akyerɛ me. " Afei ɔfaa saa towa no, na na ɛyɛ nnyigyei a ɛyɛ nteteso, tintim! na ɔtow gui, na ɛyɛ nnyigyei a ɛyɛ apete tebea, tesee! Saa na obiara nyaa nyansa; na obiara a wankɔ hɔ wɔ berɛ ano no, mepa me abebuo sɛ ɔyɛ ɔkwasea.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
