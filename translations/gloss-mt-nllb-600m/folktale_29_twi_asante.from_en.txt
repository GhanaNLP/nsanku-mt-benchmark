Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA EBIARA NYAA YERE (ANSO NEA WƆAGYE NO NKWA) NNKWA WƆ AKWAN A WƆFA SO KYERƐKYERƐ NSƐNKYERƐNNE AHODOƆ MU

NA ƐWƆ HƆ BERE TITIRIW NO, NA mmarima BAASA BI TE HƆ A NA WƆPƐ adwuma. Wɔkɔɔ Tall-grass-country kɔtɔɔ nkyekye ntoma ne nneɛma foforo bi. (Wɔ Asante san ba no, wɔyɛɛ sɛ wɔn wofase ba no bɛsoa nneɛma no bi, na wɔakɔtɔn. Wɔbaa kuro kɛseɛ bi mu. Wɔ kuro no mu na ɔhemmaa bi babea bi wɔ hɔ a ɔpow sɛ ɔbɛware obi wɔ awareɛ mu. Mprempren ahɔhoɔ yi (adwadifoɔ) kɔɔ hɔ (kɔɔ ne fie), nanso na ɔhemmaa no ba no akɔ "to beat" ahohoro no. Ɔhemmaa no (however) tɔɔ ntoma mmienu na ɔde sii hɔ maa ne babaa no. Bere a ne abofra no san bae no, ɔhunuu mmarima baasa no. Ɔka kyerɛɛ naberewa sɛ, Ɛde besi nnɛ mehunu nea me kɔn dɔ no, enti kum odwan bi na mema wɔn. Bere a wɔwiee aduane no, abaayewa no kɔɔ fa esɔ, asaawa, bese, ne nkateɛ dua bi kɔtoo aduane no so kɔmaa mmarima no. Na ɔse, "Nea ɔbɛtumi akyerɛkyerɛ wɔn aseɛ mu no, ɔno na mɛware no. " Na wɔde aduane no kɔɔ hɔ. Ɛnna mmarima no yii no adi, na wɔkaa sɛ, " Sɛ ɛnyɛ Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba na ɔde aduane yi aba a, anka ɛsɛ sɛ yɛnya nsɛmfua bi bisa no. " Mmarima no wofase ba no kaa sɛ, " Sɛ na meansuro wo a, anka mɛtumi aka saa nneɛma no aseɛ akyerɛ wo. " Wɔkaa sɛ, " Kyerɛkyerɛ mu. " abofra no buae sɛ, " abaayewa no ka sɛ wadwene ayɛ no fitaa sɛ asaawa, na sɛ ɔbɛtumi anya yɛn mu biako a waba yi ha no, ɛnsɛ sɛ ɔware? Esɔ no aseɛ ne sɛ, da bi bɛba a ɔbɛyera; bese no nso kyerɛ sɛ, da bi bɛba a ɔbɛma no (ne kunu) anom kola (i. e. bɛyɛ awerɛhow). Nkateɛ no kyerɛ sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ wodua nkateɛ ma no, na afe biara, sɛ ɔkɔhwɛ nhini no a ɔbɛnya bi atete. " Enti, Ɔhemmaa Yawa ba no waree mmarima no nua ba. Bere a ɔwaree no, Ɔhemmaa Yawa ba no bisaa no sɛ, "Wo din de dɛn? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me din ne Kwaku Susufoɔ," kyerɛ sɛ, Kwaku-who-takes-ɔhaw-to-think. Ɛnna abaayewa no buae sɛ, "Me nso, me din de me Akua-who-takes-think. " Ɔde afahyɛ a ɛfata na ɛwaree no. Ɛduruu anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, wɔfafoɔ mmiɛnsa no de kwan kɔɔ wɔn ankasa kuro mu. Kwaku-who-takes-ɔhaw-to-think no tenaa ne yere akuraa ase. Ɔtenaa hɔ afe baako, na sɛ ɔsɔre (anɔpa) a, ɔne ne nnamfofoɔ tena hɔ di agorɔ wɔ nte mu. Ɛnneɛ, da bi a na ɔredi agorɔ no saa no, ɔbɔɔ marbuls mmienu wɔ tuo toɔ baako mu, nanso nnipa a ɔne no redi agorɔ no kaa sɛ wɔremfa mma no, na wɔpɛɛ mpo sɛ wɔbɔ no. Ɔtoo sɛnea wɔ nakyi, na ɔhunuu abofra bi a ɔgyina hɔ, ɔfaa ɔboɔ bi de maa abofra no sɛ ɔmfa nkɔma ne yere. Mprempren bere a ne yere nsa kaa ɔboɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Me kunu pɛ sɛ ɔsan kɔ ne kuro mu, efiri sɛ ɔka sɛ anomaa tra fam kyɛ a, ɔnya ɔboɔ. " Ɔhemmaa no nso kaa sɛ, "Sɛ ɔkɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ wo ne no fa. " Ɛyɛ nokware sɛ ɔno ne Kwaku-who-takes-ɔhaw-to-think. Mprempren, bere bi a eyi akyi no, wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Kwaku-who-takes-ɔhaw-to-think, gye hama a etwa to no bi sɛ yɛnkɔ nwura mu nkɔhyehyɛ fidie wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ yɛbɛkyere mborɔnoma na yɛde ahɔhoɔgyeɛ ama wo yere, a ɔyɛ ɔhɔhoɔ. Wɔyɛɛ saa, nanso na mpanimfoɔ yi (really) apene so sɛ wɔbɛkum no. Wɔn ne Kwaku-who-takes-to-think no kɔɔ so, wɔduruu (beaeɛ a ɛyɛ ankonam) wɔ ɔkwan so, wɔbɛkyekyeree no na wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Yɛbɛkum wo. Ɛnna ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, Bere a me kɔɔ me yere akuraa ase no, mihuu sika bebree bi, na sɛ moamma me kwan sɛ menkyerɛ mo baabi a ɛwɔ a, na mewu a, morenhu. Wɔbisaa no sɛ, Ɛhe na sika no wɔ? Ɔbuaa sɛ, Ɛsɛ sɛ Kɔ me yere nkyɛn kɔbɔ no amanneɛ sɛ meatɔ nam, na ɛno (adwetɔfoɔ) kyerɛ sɛ ne boɔ yɛ peredwan du (£80), na ɛsɛ sɛ ɔhwehwɛ kotokuo a wɔde ntoma nketewa ayɛ mu; sɛ ɔhwehwɛ hɔ na wannya a, ɔnhwehwɛ Ɔkrampan kotoku no mu, sɛ wannya a, ɔnhwehwɛ abɔsoɔ a nsekan wom no mu. Wɔbɛyɛɛ saa maa wɔn yere no nyaa nkrasɛm no. Na ɔno nso kaa sɛ, The nneɛma are in the village head-man's fie, so let's go get them. Ɔno ne wɔn kɔɔ headman no fie hɔ, na ɔbaa no kaa amaneɛbɔ no kyerɛɛ head-man no, kyerɛkyerɛɛ mu sɛ, hama kotokuo a wɔayɛ no sheep-skin no kyerɛ sɛ wɔakyekyere no; Ɔkrampan no kyerɛ sɛ apɛtɛ no rebɔ mmɔden sɛ wɔbɛdidi ne honam; abɔsoɔ a ɛkura nsekan no kyerɛ sɛ wɔrebɛtwa ne menem no. Ntɛm ara na ɔpanyin no bɔɔ gong-gong sɛ wɔnkɔ so nkɔ hɔ nkɔgye Kwaku-who-takes-ɔhaw-to-think. Na wɔne no san baeɛ. Ɔmaa wɔkumm mpanimfoɔ mmiɛnsa no. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, By akwan of interpreting nsɛnkyerɛnne ahodoɔ someone got a yere and (also) saved his nkwa. Eyi, me abasɛm a me aka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
