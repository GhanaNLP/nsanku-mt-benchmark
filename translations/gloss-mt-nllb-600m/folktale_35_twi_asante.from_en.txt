Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

NTIANSO MPANIMFOƆ KYERƐ SƐ ƐNSƐ SƐ YƐSAN NNI NNA A WƆDE WƆN HO TO WƆN SO NO BIO.

WƆKYERƐ SƐ (wɔ berɛ bi) na Nyankopɔn Kwame, ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no, yii afuo kɛseɛ bi, na ɔduaa nkruma, gyeene, adua, turo-nsuo, ɛmo, ne ɛmo. Nwura a ɛwɔ turo no mu no yɛɛ den, na honhon nyiniiɛ. Ewiem-onyankopɔn bɔɔ dawurubɔ de gong-gong kyerɛɛ nsunsuansoɔ sɛ ne afuw no ayɛ nwira so, na obiara a ɔbɛtumi atwa no a wammɔ ne ho no, ɔbɛba abɛfa ne babaa Abena Nkroma (kyerɛ sɛ Abena ba a ɔtɔ so nkron) wɔ awareɛ mu. Eyinom mu biako, bere a ɔkɔe (ɔbɔɔ mmɔden), bɔɔ ne ho (baabi a honhonhon no tickled), na wɔtoo no hoot; ɔno deɛ, ɔkɔɔ na wɔtoo no hoot; mmarima nyinaa kɔɔ (wɔbɔɔ mmɔden) nanso wɔantumi. Mprempren Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Me deɛ, metumi. Mprempren ɔsoro-onyankopɔn afifideɛ no da kwan no nkyɛn, na saa kwan no na nnipa fa so kɔ dwaso no Fida biara. Seesei Ananse no, ɛfiri sɛ na ɔnim saa nokwasɛm yi, na ɔde kɔpra nwira no nkutoo Fida biara. Na ɔreborɔ no, na nnipa a wɔretwam fa mu no de akye no sɛ, Hail to you at your adwuma, Agya Ananse! , na ɔtaa bua sɛ, Thank you, Aku. Wɔkɔɔ so sɛ, A afuw a obiara ntumi mpopae no, (wose sɛ) woresɛe? Ananse no bɛbua sɛ, Ah, ɛyɛ abaayewa biako nti na mehyɛ me ho sɛɛ. Na ne basa baako yɛ sɛɛ. " Na ɔbɔ ne basa no petepete baabi a na ɛrekye no, na sɛ ɔyɛ saa a, ɔbɛnya ahotɔ afiri abufuhyew no mu. Obi foforo nso bɛtwa mu bio akamfo no wɔ nadwuma no ho; na ɔgye ne nsa abɔ baabi a na ɛyaw no. Sɛ nhwɛsoɔ no, sɛ ne sere no, anka ɔbɛka sɛ, "Saayɛ abaayewa sigyafoɔ no, wɔka sɛ ne sere te sɛɛ" (slepping and rubbing his own). Saa ɔkwan yi so na ɔde bɛwiee afuw no hohoroo. Ɛnna ɔkɔgyee kɔka kyerɛɛ Ewiem-onyankopɔn sɛnea na wawie ne afuo no dɔw. Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no bisaa messenger no sɛ, "So wawie ampa? " Messenger no kaa sɛ, "Aane. " Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no bisaa no sɛ, "So ɔde ne ho bɔɔ anaa? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dabi, ɔammrɛ ne ho. " Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no faa Abena Nkroma kɔmaa Kwaku Ananse wɔ awareɛ mu. Anadwo koro bi a Kwaku Ananse ne nayeforɔ no kɔɔ wɔn home, na ayeforɔ no bisaa no sɛ, "Nanso ɛyɛɛ dɛn na wo ne nnipa nyinaa tumi yii agya afuw a ɛyɛ nwira, afuw a ɛne sɛ, obiara a ɔkɔɔ so no, san nakyi, nanso wotumi yii no? " Ɛneɛ Ananse no kaa sɛ, "So wosusu sɛ meyɛ kwasea? Na me hohoroo me, na sɛ obi fa me nkyɛn kɔbisa me sɛ, 'Ananse, woreyi afuo yi a obiara ntumi nni so da a,' na mede me nsa bɛbɔ me wedeɛ baabi a ɛyɛ me dɛw no, na meyam no, na mepae mu ka kyerɛ onipa no sɛ, nhwɛsoɔ no, na wo sere no te sɛ ɛkoɔ sere, na ɛyɛ fɛ na ɛyɛ fɛ. Ɛbaa no saa na metumi weed no. " Ɛnna Abena, ba a ɔtɔ so nkron no, kae sɛ, "Ɛneɛ kɔ-ɔkyena na mebɛka akyerɛ agya sɛ woatwa wo ho (ne nyinaa akyi). " Ananse no bɛkasa kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛnsɛ sɛ woka ho asɛm, eyi yɛ sleep-mat confidence. " Abena, ba a ɔtɔ so nkron no, kae sɛ, "Me nni hwee nim fa sleep-mat confidences ho, na mebɛka. " Abena Nkroma de ne slee-mat kɔeɛ. eping-mat away (from next to Kwaku Ananse) na ɔkɔdaa dan no awieeɛ foforɔ. Afei Kwaku Ananse ani beree (i.e. awerɛhow), na ɔkɔfaa ne bentaa, na ɔbɔɔ (ahoma ahorow) na ɔtoo dwom sɛ:

"Abena, ba a ɔtɔ so nkron, eyi nyɛ asɛm a ɛsɛ sɛ yɛgye akyinnye.

Momma yɛmfa no sɛ awerɛhyem a wɔde da mpa so.

Ɔse: Dabi.

Ɔwɔ asɛm (against me), (but) ɔfoforo nso wɔ asɛm a ɛrekɔ kwan so dedaw. "

Ɛnna Ananse no sɔreee, na ɔkɔdaa. Kwaku Ananse dii hɔ berɛ kakra akyi no, ɛnna ɔsɔree. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abena Nkroma" ɛnyɛ nnyigyei (save the dede of the babadua su) dinn! Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Me nsa aka wo. " Ɔfaa kora ketewa bi, de nsuo hyɛɛ mu, na ɔhwiee guu Abena Nkroma mpa so. Ɛnna Kwaku Ananse kɔɔdaa hɔ. Bere a ɔdaa fam kakra akyi no, ɔkae sɛ, "Ko! Abena Nkroma, ɛdeɛn na eyi? Woayɛ mpa so nsuo, wo fɛreɛ abɔdeɛ, akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho sɛ wo ho nyɛ fɛ koraa; bere a nneɛma rebɛda adi no, mebɛka akyerɛ obiara. Na ɛyɛ nokwareɛ, ɛnneɛ (nea wɔn nyinaa kaeɛ), sɛ obiara kɔ saa afuw no mu a, na ɔse, 'abaayewa a wets...!' Mempɛ sɛ meyi honhonhon afuo (ma onipa a ɔte saa). " Ɛnna Abena ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Me srɛ wo, gyae, na ma asɛm no nkɔ. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Mempene so, me asɛm na ɛdii kan; wokae sɛ wobɛka akyerɛ wo agya; mekaa sɛ 'Desist'; wokae sɛ 'Dabi'. Ɛno nti, merennyae asɛm no nkɔ. " Na Abena, ba a ɔtɔ so nkron no, kaa sɛ, "Gyae me asɛm no ne wo asɛm no nso, nea me ho asɛm no, fa kɔ. okay, me bɛto hɔ; efisɛ sɛ woamfi me deɛ a, me aniwa bɛwu ama aniwu. Ɛneɛ Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Mete sɛ, ɛfiri sɛ wopɛ saa, ma ɛnnyɛ sleep-mat confidence, nti asɛm no kɔsi hɔ. Saa na mpanimfoɔ no baa abɛka sɛ Sleep-mat confidences are not repeated.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi ayɛ wo nokware, ne ahomegyeɛ a wubetumi akamfo me (wɔ nasɛmka ho).
