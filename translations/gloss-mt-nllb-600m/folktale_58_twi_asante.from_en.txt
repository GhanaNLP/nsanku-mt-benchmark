Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

WƆ KWAN BIARA SO NO, ƐHƆ NA WOHU SƐ OSEBO, DABOƆ NO, ATETEW, ƐHƆ NA ADANKO, HARE NO, ATWAM (ALREADY)

WƆKYERƐ SƐ ɔsebo kandi ne ɔsebɔ Bedeɛ, ne adanko ne adanko bendɛ, na da bi wɔte akuraa ketewa bi so, na ɛkɔm bi baeɛ. Ɛneɛ na ɔsebɔ Bedeɛ kyerɛ sɛ ɔrekɔ asuwa no so. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa adanko bendɛ no grinding aburoo no. ɔsebɔ Bedeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Ah, maame, ɛhe na woanya adeɛ a ɛte sɛɛ? " adanko Bedeɛ no buae sɛ, "Ɛwɔ kwan a ɛtrɛw wɔ ha, na na me kunu no di akyi rekɔ hwehwɛ wuram bayere, na ɔkɔgyee saa aburoo yi, enti fa yi (and) kɔnoa na fa. " Ɔsan kaa sɛ, "aseda wo, Aku. " Ɛneɛ na ɔsebɔ Bedeɛ no yii no fii fie, de kɔtoo ogya no so. Na ne kunu no baeɛ, na ɔbisaa no (baabi a na wanya aburoo no), na ɔkaa sɛ, adankoɛ a ɔde maa me no, na ɔkaeɛ sɛ kwan a ɛtrɛw na ɛda akyiri hɔ, na ɛne sɛ ne kunu no faa no, de aburoo bi baeɛ. Na ɔsebooe no reka sɛ, Afei kɔ-ɔkyena koraa, mebɛkɔ hɔ anɔpa paa. Enti ɔfirii aseɛ. Bere a ɔkɔɔ no, na aburoo wɔ bebrebee. Afei ɔgyee ne Sekkan no twaa bi, na ɔyɛɛ nkorabata kɛntɛn, boaboaa ano (aburoo) no, na ɔde hyɛɛ mu. Afei Adanko, adanko Nini no, na ɔwɔ ne fie, na ɔhunuu sɛ ɔsebonini no atwam akɔ afuo no mu. Amonom hɔ ara na ɔno nso hyɛɛ aseɛ. Ɔbaa kuro no awieeɛ na ɔdanee Ɔtwee. Fa no mu ayɛ hwa, na fa no nnya nnue. Na ɔkɔɔ akyiri kɔɔ so, na ɔdanee Oyuo (antelope). Fa no ayɛ fĩ, ne fa no nnyae fĩ. Na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔdanee Bongo. Fa no ayɛ fĩ, ne fa no nnyae fĩ. Na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔdanee Ɛkoɔ. Na ne nyinaa yɛ foforo. Seesei na Daboɔ no reba a ɔso kɛntɛn ne aburoo wɔ ne etire. Ɔbɛhyiaa Ɛkoɔ no. Daboɔ no ka kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ, ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bi bɛba, nanso ɛnyɛ nea ɛbɛdɔɔ so (severe), nanso mmoadoma yi rewu. Na ɔtwa mu, na ɔba Bongo no so; na ɔtwa mu, na ɔba Ɔyuo no so; na ɔtwa mu, na ɔba Ɔtwee no so. Ɛnna ɔkaa sɛ, Aane, saa mmoadoma yi dɔɔso paa, enti mebɛkɔ akɔgye. Ɔde kɛntɛn no ne aburoo no guu hɔ, na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔrekɔ akɔgye mmoa no, ɛfiri nea ɛdi kan no. Ɔkɔeɛ a, na wɔnni hɔ bio. Ɛnna ɔsan baeɛ nanso na kɛntɛn no ne aburoo no nso ayera. Ofiri fie hɔ kɔtenaa kommyɛ mu. ɔsebɔ Bedeɛ no bisaa no sɛ, Ɛhe na aburoo no wɔ? Ɔkaa sɛ, Mekɔɔ hɔ (hɔ), nanso mannya bi ntɔ. Afei ɔsebɔ Bedeɛ no kɔɔ asuwa no ho, na (hɔ) na ɔhyiaa adanko Bedeɛ no. ɔsebɔ Bedeɛ no kaa sɛ, All I have to say is that ɛnora my kunu said that when he went, he didnt get any aburoo to buy. The adanko Bedeɛ no buae sɛ, Ɔregyigya wo, ɛnora no, bere a ɔkɔeɛ na ɔnyaa bi no, na me kunu no bɔɔ no na ne ho tɔɔ no. The ɔsebɔ Bedeɛ set off fie. Ɔbɔɔ ne kunu amanneɛ sɛ, Well done! well done! well done! wokae sɛ woakɔ na woanya aburoo biara, bere a na ɛyɛ saa Hare no na ɔbɔɔ wo na oyii wo nsa. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, Hwan ne Hare no? To-ɔkyena me ne no bɛkɔ ko ne nnua. Hare no adankoyini no bɛtenaa fie na ɔtee (eyi). Ɔkɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn sɛ, Sɛ ɔkyena a, ɔsebo no bɛba ha abɛyɛ ko ne me, ɛno nti na mebɛkɔ akɔhyia no wɔ kwan mu, ebia na wo bɛsuro. Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, Hare no hyɛɛ aseɛ. Ɔduruu kuro no kuro akyi, na ɔdanee Adowa, na ɔfe dɔte fitaa, bidiɛ, ne mogya. Ɔkɔɔ anim, na ɔdanee Bush-buck, na ɔrebufuo wɔ saa kwan korɔ no ara so. Ɔkɔɔ anim, na ɔdanee Ɛkoɔ. Daboɔ no na ɛreba so. Adowa no kaa sɛ, Agya Ɔsebɔ, boa me, ɛfiri sɛ merewu. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, Dɛn na ɛyɛ asɛm? Adowa no kaa sɛ, Hare no ne me kɔsiesiee aburoo ho mansotwe, na ɔboroo me, na mewu. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, Momma me nkɔ so. Ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔhyiaa Bush-buck no. Bush-buck no kae sɛ, Daboɔ, monyɛ me so, ɛfiri sɛ saa Hare no nyɛ nea ɔne no bɛwɔ aburoo ho manso. Daboɔ no kae sɛ, Dɛn na ɛyɛ asɛm? Bush-buck no kae sɛ, Mekɔɔ na ɔboroo me, ɛno nti na mewu yi. Daboɔ no kae sɛ, Momma me nkɔ so. Ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔbaa Ɛkoɔ no so. Ɛkoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Father Osebo, support me, for I am dying. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "What is the asɛm? " Ɛkoɔ no kaa sɛ, "I went and had a mansotwe about aburoo with the Hare, and he beat me, and I am dying. " Ɛneɛ na Daboɔ no dwenee ho kɛseɛ (na ɛwɔ tenten) kaa no kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ, "E! Mbɛdane akɔ bio, ɛfiri sɛ ɛyɛ aburoo asɛm a ɛte saa ara na me ne no bɛsiesie, na ma yɛnka sɛ ɔboro me... " Daboɔ no de ahopere guanee, kiri! kiri! Amonom hɔ ara, Hare no nso sɔreee. Ɔdii nakyi, na Daboɔ de nne kɛseɛ kae sɛ, "Ɔsebɔ Bedeɛ, guan o! " Daboɔ duu ne fie, na ɔdwanee kɔɔ ne dan mu, na ɔde ne etire bɔɔ afasuo no mu baako na ɔtwam mu, nanso Hare kyeree nakyi nan no nsɔmu. Na Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Father Hare, ma me kɔ. " Hare no kaa sɛ, "Me remma wo kwan, me bɛbɔ wo. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Me srɛ wo, mma me bɔ, na mmom ma me anyame no mennom na mehyɛ nseɛ sɛ kwan biara a wobɛfa so no, me bɛba abɛtew. " Ɛno nti, bere a wofa kwan biara so, na woahu sɛ Daboɔ no atwetwe wɔ hɔ no, (wunim) Hare no atwa mu kwan no.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
