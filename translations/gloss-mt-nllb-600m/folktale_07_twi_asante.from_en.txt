SƐNEA LEOPARD FUNU YƐƐ NEA ƐDAA ADI

ƐHƆ (Once) NA Nwansena bi ne Daboɔ bi te, wɔ hɔ, na Daboɔ no ka kyerɛɛ Nwansena sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔhwehwɛ mmaa mma yɛnware. " Nwansena no kaa sɛ, "Ho. " Ɛna Daboɔ no guare, na ɔde ngo guu mu, na ɔde ne sika kɔkɔɔ agude asiesiee ne ho, na ɔkyekyeree ne mpa dada no, na ɔde maa Nwansena sɛ wɔnni netire so. Na wɔfirii mu baeɛ; wɔbaa akuraase bi. Ɛhɔ ara na Nwansena no kaeɛ sɛ, "Mmothers all and Agyanom all, I give you anwummere nkyea. " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Yɛn kunu pa. " Daboɔ no nso bɛduruu hɔ, ɔkaa sɛ, "Mothers all and Agyanom all, I give you anwummere nkyea. " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Wo ne ano fĩ a wode bɛma yɛn anwummere nkyea no? " Wɔtuu kwan, na wɔduruu akuraa a ɛdi hɔ no. 'Wansana, Nwansena no, kae sɛ, "Ɛnanom nyinaa ne Agyanom nyinaa, mede anwummere nkyea ma mo. " Wɔn nso buae saa kwan koro no ara. Daboɔ no nso duruu hɔ, na ɔmaa wɔn akyea, na wɔbuaa sɛ,

"Wo a wode ano fĩ no, wopɛ sɛ yɛbɛnya anwummere nkyea? " Wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ, wɔkɔɔ kwan so no, Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ ha! Fa kɛtɛ dada no brɛ me na fa sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ yi siesie wo ho, sɛ ɛyɛ kɛtɛ dada a mmabaawa no dɔ wo nti a, mebɛhunu. " Na (Nwansena) gyee (ahosiesiedeɛ no), siesiee ne ho, na wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ akuraa a ɛdi hɔ no. Daboɔ no na ɛwɔ anim, na abɔsoɔ dedaw no na ɛda ne etire so; ɔkyiiaa (nkuraasefoɔ) no. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Nhyira mma yɛn, wo a wode ano fĩ no, ɛfata sɛ wode wo nsa kura saa mpa dedaw yi. " Ɛneɛ na Nwansena no bɛduruu hɔ, ɔkaa sɛ, "Mmothers all and Agyanom all, I give you anwummere nkyea. " Na wɔbuaa sɛ, "Oh you dɔfo; ha deɛ, ɛyɛ mmabunu a ɛfata sɛ yɛhwɛ; sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ; sɛnea wɔn ho bɛyɛ wo; sɛ ɛnyɛ borɔ a anka yɛbɛnya no nti, anka yɛbɛka wo ho wɔ wo kwan so. akwantu. " Na wɔkɔɔ so. Ɛnkyɛree na wɔduruu kwan no so no, Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Ayi! adamfo, gyina hɔ, yi me sika agudeɛ no na fa ntɛm, fa ntɛm, fa ntɛm. " Nwansena no wiee sɛ wɔfiri ahosiesiedeɛ no mu na wɔde ama no, Daboɔ no hwɛɛ kwan no mu yii nhabannwono a wɔde bɛkyekyere Nwansena no. Ɔkaa sɛ, Me bɛkyekyere wo, na dɛn na woanya aka? Na Nwansena no kaa sɛ, Me nni hwee ka. Daboɔ no kɔɔ so kaa sɛ, Me ne wo nnya nduruu yɛn beae a obi rekɔ mpo... (na ɛneɛ mmaa no redɔ wo ho ɔdɔ dedaw). Ɛnna Daboɔ no faa ne ho nsɔmu kyekyeree no wɔ dua bi so. Na ɔno nso kɔhyɛɛ baabi foforɔ. Ɛnkyɛree koraa na Borɔdeɛ no twaamuu wɔ dwaso kwan so, na ɔhunuu no (Nwansena no) ɔkae sɛ:

Hwan na ɔrekyekyere hɔ a ne ho yɛ tuntum saa?

Hena na ɛrehyerɛn wɔ hɔ a ɛhyerɛn saa?

(Nwansena) kaa sɛ:

Ɛyɛ me, Nwansena, Kwao Boakye.

Adenen!

Gyahene Kofi Amo, Adenen!

Ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ me ba, na yɛakɔ hwehwɛ mmabaawa.

Adenen!

Wɔn mmabaawa no ka sɛ wɔmpɛ no.

Adenen!

Wayɛ me nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn.

Adenen!

Waka me koraa.

Adenen!

Gye me, gye me.

Adenen!

Sɛ woagye me nkwa a, fa kwan no so kɔ.

Adenen! " Nanso Borɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Daboɔ no akyekyere wo nti, mentumi nyi wo;" na ɔkɔgyee mu. Mankani, Coco-bayere no, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) sɛdeɛ ɔkwan no teɛ; Aburoo, Aburoo no, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) sɛdeɛ ɔkwan no teɛ; Atwibo, Bayere no, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) sɛdeɛ ɔkwan no teɛ; Adua, Adua no, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) sɛdeɛ ɔkwan no teɛ; Akam, Netherlandsmo no, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) sɛdeɛ ɔkwan no teɛ; Akapinkye, Red-roosted plantain no, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) sɛdeɛ ɔkwan no teɛ; Bankye, Bankye no baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) sɛdeɛ ɔkwan no teɛ; Borɔferɛ, Paw-paw, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) sɛdeɛ ɔkwan no teɛ. (na ɛkɔɔ) wɔ ɔkwan koro no ara so; Emo, Ɛmo, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) wɔ ɔkwan koro no ara so; Efrere, Fɛre, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) wɔ ɔkwan koro no ara so; Habayere, Bush-yam, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) wɔ ɔkwan koro no ara so; Kwadu, Kwadu, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) wɔ ɔkwan koro no ara so; Nkontommire, Coco-yam leaves, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) wɔ ɔkwan koro no ara so; Krobona, Adua, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) wɔ ɔkwan koro no ara so; Mako, the red-pepper, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) wɔ mann koro no ara so er; Nkateɛ, Ground-nut no, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) wɔ ɔkwan a ɛteɛ so; Nwiadowa, Garden-egg no, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) wɔ ɔkwan a ɛteɛ so; Nkruma, Nkruma no, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) wɔ ɔkwan a ɛteɛ so; na afei Dokon'fa, Fa-a-ball-of-Dɔkono, baeɛ (Boiled-pounded-corn) kaeɛ sɛ:

"Hwan na ɛsɛn hɔ a ne ho yɛ tuntum saa?

Hena na ɛrehyerɛn wɔ hɔ a ɛhyerɛn saa? "

(Nwansena) kaa sɛ:

"Ɛyɛ me, Nwansena, Kwao Boakye, Adenen!

Gyahene Kofi Amo, Adenen!

Ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ me ba, na yɛakɔ hwehwɛ mmabaawa.

Adenen!

Wɔn mmabaawa no ka sɛ wɔmpɛ no.

Adenen!

Wayɛ me nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn.

Adenen!

Waka me koraa.

Adenen!

Gye me, gye me.

Adenen!

Sɛ woagye me nkwa a, fa kwan no so kɔ.

Adenen!"

Half-a-ball of Dɔkono kaa sɛ, "Meteate, mebɛma wo so ahome. " Ɔkɔɔ hɔ, na ɔtoo no so. Bere a ɔyɛɛ saa wieeɛ no, mpofirim ara na Daboɔ no pueeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woama me ɔbarima no so? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Meama no so, na nea wobɛyɛ no, yɛ. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, me ne wo renyɛ hwee koraa, me ne wo nko na yɛde yɛn nkuturu bɛko. " Dɔkono bɔɔlo no fã bi kaa sɛ, "Sɛ yɛbɛko a, yɛnkɔ nwura no mu, na yɛmmɛbubu nnyina no na yɛmfa no sisian. " Enti wɔkɔeɛ na wɔbubu nnyina no, na ɔsan bae. Na ɔno (Half-a-ball-of-Dɔkono) kae sɛ, "Momma yɛn hyehye no. " Na Daboɔ no de sisian sii hɔ. Amonom hɔ ara na wɔde wɔn ho bɔɔ ɔko, (na ne nnyigyei te sɛ) Yiri! Yiri! Yiri! Na Daboɔ no atete Half-a-ball-of-Dɔkono, na wɔatow no agu, bam! Half-a-ball of Dɔkono kaa sɛ, "(adeɛ a ɛte saa nnyɛ hwee), obi fa adeɛ a ɛte saa de hohoro baabi a ne yere guare. " Na wɔsan hyiaa bio. Na Half-a-ball of Dɔkono nso abɔ ne ho Daboɔ, bam! Na Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Me nso mefa adeɛ a ɛte saa ara mehoro me yere tiafi, (nanso) yɛn ahyiadie a ɛdi hɔ no, sɛ obi bɛto ɔfoforɔ agu ne ho so a, na ɛno ne tow a etwa toɔ. " Na wɔaka abom (ɔko no, na nnyigyei no te sɛ) Kikiri! Kikiri! Kiseki! Na Half-a-bɔɔlo-of-Dɔkono faa Daboɔ no so tow no guu ogya no mu. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "_Me firim, me firim, me firim_" (me wɔ abɔnten, me wɔ abɔnten, me wɔ abɔnten. Saa na Daboɔ no nyaa ne su "_Me firim_" no). (Afei) wɔhunuu sɛ ne nipadua te sɛɛ, kurukyire, kurukyire, kurukyire nyinaa (ɛne sɛ, nyinaa spotted); baabi a na fitaa no wɔ no, ɛhɔ na na nso no aka no; na baabi a na tuntum no wɔ no, ɛhɔ na na bidie tuntum no aka no. Na sɛ worenantew mu a, na wuhu ahaban a ɛda hɔ a wɔahyɛ Dɔkono bɔɔlo fã (has been wrapped) no, wubehu sɛ nwansena bebree reborɔ so; na ɛno nni sianteɛ foforo biara (except this one) nti, wɔrekamfo no.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi ayɛ wo nokware, ne ahomegyeɛ a wubetumi akamfo me (wɔ ne asɛmka ho).
