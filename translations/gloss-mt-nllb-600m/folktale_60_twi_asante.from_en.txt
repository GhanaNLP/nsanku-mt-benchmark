Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA (THE SALUTATIONS) "KURONTO" NE "BURU" BƐDII DWUMA NO

NA ƐWƆ HƆ BERE BI A ɔbɔfoɔ bi (a wɔfrɛ no) Kwaku, ne ne yere (a wɔfrɛ no Buruwa), na wɔn hiani nti, wɔrekɔyɛ atenae foforɔ. Kwaku, ɔbɔfoɔ no, bere a ɔkɔɔ nwura so no, ɔkunkum eku bi, nanso bere a ɔde baa fie ne asase so no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Eyi nyɛ papa (for aduane). " Enti ɔkɔtɔn no. Daa na sɛ ɔkunkum adeɛ a, na ɛyɛ saa. Ne yere deɛ, ɔhunuu sika no da, na ɔbisaa no sɛ, Woannye sika mma, na woannye nam mma, na ɛdeɛn na ɛyɛ ne nyinaa aseɛ? Ɔbuaa sɛ, Sɛ merekɔ a, na nnipa ntɔ (nam no), na ɛreporɔ a, me tow gu. Da bi ɔkunkum biribi, de too kwan mu, na ne yere dii nakyi bɛhwɛɛ no. Ɛnna ɔhunuu sɛ ɔtɔn saa kwaadu no, de sika no, na ɔtɔɔ nsafufuo kuku (a wɔfrɛ no dɔnto) na ɔnom. Ɛnna ne yere san baa fie. Ɛnkyɛree koraa na Kwaku, ɔbɔfoɔ no nso baeɛ. Bere biara a ɔbɛduruu fie hɔ no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Anwummere pa, Buruwa. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Ya Eudonto (Yiw, Monkey for kuku of nsafufuo). " Ɔse, "Dɛn nti na wobua me saa kwan yi so? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuaeɛ a ɛfata wɔ nea ɛwɔ wo etire. " Nanso, ɔbɔfoɔ no anka hwee. Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔkɔɔ afuo na ɔsan bae; ɔkae sɛ, "Anwummere pa. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Ya bur. " Buruwa kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wobua (me nkyea) saa ɔkwan yi so? " ɔbɔfoɔ no buae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuae a ɛfata ma nea ɛwɔ wo etire. " Saa na ɛbɛyɛɛ amammrɛ wɔ abusuakuo no mu sɛ wɔmfa nkyea ahorow "Ya kuronto" ne "Ya bur" nni dwuma. Wɔde wɔn mfitiaseɛ ma ɔbɔfoɔ, Kwaku, ne ne yere, Buruwa. Wei deɛ, me abasɛm a meaka akyerɛ mo no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ (anaasɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, momfa bi nkɔ baabi foforɔ, na momma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
