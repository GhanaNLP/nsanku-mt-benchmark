SƐNEA ƆWEA (THRE-BEAR), ABOSOM-AKOTERE (ABOSOMAKOTERƐ), NE ƆPATUO (PATU) BAEƐ BƐGYEE WƆN OSU SORONKO

ƆWEA, ODUA A WƆDE KƆ, (a ɔwɔ abodin nso sɛ) Kwaduampɔn Kyerɛfo, Ɔpatuo, Patu, ne Abosom-Akotere, Abosomakoterɛ (a nabodin ne), Ɔbɔfoɔ Owu, Afe-Afe-Ade-YƐ-Biribiara Bɔfo, Ɔnam-akutu-akutu-akutu-akɔ-asase-a-mmarima-a-akɔ akyiri, (saa mmoa yi nyinaa) traa ase pɛn. Seesei, wɔn mu biara nni hɔ a ɔwɔ yere. Na Adobe-dua no tɔn ne ho maa wɔn. Da biara, bere a Adobe-tree no sɔre no, na ɔkɔtwɛn yi; da a ɛdi hɔ no, na ɔtwɛn foforo bi wɔ wɔn mu; na da a ɛdi hɔ no, na ɛyɛ foforo. Da bi, bere a na ɛyɛ Ɔbɔfoɔ-owuo dane (sɛ ɔbɛhyɛ Adobe-services) na wahwehwɛ Adobe-tree no, ɛhe na ɔbɛtumi ahu no? Na ɔsian kɔka amaneɛbɔ no kyerɛɛ Dua-bearer no ne Patu no. Na wɔkaa sɛ, Kɔfrɛ no. Na Ɔbɔfoɔ no-wu no maa ne nne so sɛ: Adobe o! Adobe o!

Dua no soafoɔ no su frɛɛ sɛ:

Ɔkɔɔ no, ɔmmɛsan mmae; ɔkɔɔ, ɔmmɛsan mmae, ɔmmɛsan mmae!

Na Patu teɛɛ mu sɛ:

Yɛakari (sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma) na anka yɛbɛto ato hɔ, m! Yɛakari (sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma) na anka yɛbɛto ato hɔ, m!

Na ɛno ne abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ mmiɛnsa yi osuo mfitiaseɛ.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
