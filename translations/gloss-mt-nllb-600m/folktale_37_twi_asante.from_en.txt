Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

WHY ANANSE, THE ANANSE, MMIRIKA AHOROƆ BERE A ƐWƆ NSUO NO ANI NO

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse no, kɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔkraman, Kraman no, Mepɛ sɛ me ne wo kɔkyekye akuraa ketewa bi a wɔbɛtena mu. Kraman no kaa sɛ, Me nso me pɛ saa. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Sɛ Sunday-Adae tɔ a, ɛdwoada a ɛdi hɔ yi, kɔ gye rope-creeper; me nso me nsa bɛka rope-creeper na yɛahyɛ aseɛ. Bio nso, mebɛfa towa bi ahyɛ mu ma nsuo, na wo nso woapaapae mu bi, efisɛ ebia, baabi a yɛrekɔ no, ebia nsuo biara nni hɔ. Kwaku Ananse epaapae nsuo bi; Kraman no nso, ɛwoɔ bɛhyɛɛ n'towa mu. Na wɔrekɔ so no, na wɔadu kwan ntam, na wɔn abrɛ. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Momma yɛnnom yɛn nsuo no bi. Kraman no kaa sɛ, Momma yɛnnom me bi. Kwaku Ananse faa bi, na ɔkekaa nano. Ɔkaa sɛ, O, ɛhe na eyi firi baeɛ? Kraman no kaa sɛ, Me yafunu mu ni. Ananse no kaa sɛ, Aane! Bere a wɔte hɔ sɛ wɔrebɛsan anya wɔn ahome no, Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Kraman, momma yɛnka yɛn ho nni agorɔ. Kraman no kaa sɛ, Agorɔ bɛn na ɛwɔ hɔ? Ananse no buae sɛ, Agorɔ a ɛkyekyere nkitahodi; y Ou bɛkyekyere me kan, afei me nso mɛkyekyere wo, nanso bere a wote sɛ meka sɛ, "Wui! " na woagyae. Kraman no kaa sɛ, "Dabi, wo bɛkyekyere me kan. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Yi wo ho, bere a wo ɔpanin reka asɛm bi akyerɛ wo no, woka ho asɛm anaa? " Kraman no kaa sɛ, "Yiw, ma ɛntena hɔ (sɛnea wapɛ). " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Bra bɛkyekyere me. " Kraman no faa ne hama no kyekyeree Ananse no. Na ɔde no kɔɔ ne etire so, sɛ ɔretɔn no sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na wanya biribi adi. Bere a Kwaku Ananse tee saa no, ɔmaa ne awerɛhosu so kaa sɛ:

Me, Ananse Akuamoa, minnya aboa kɛseɛ mpo nea ɛfata sɛ me tɔn me;

Kraman nkwaseafo yi nko ara, ɔbɛtɔn me.

Hwan na mɛhwehwɛ sɛ megye me?

O, Ɔkwan, gye me!

Kwaku Ananse nko ara na ɔtumi su na ɔsu. Kraman no faa no kɔsii sɛ ɔduruu asuwa bi so. Afei, Ɔdɛnkyɛm, Akokɔsrade no, tee sɛ ɔkraman no su. Ɔbaa no, ɔbisaa Kraman no sɛ asɛm bɛn na ɛwɔ hɔ. Kraman no tow Kwaku Ananse gui, dwanee, na Akokɔsrade no yii Kwaku Ananse. Na Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Abba, ayamye bɛn na metumi de ama wo de akyerɛ sɛ meda ase paa? Akokɔsrade no kae sɛ, Yɛn ani nnye hwee koraa. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Sɛ wowo mmofra a, ɛnneɛ ɔkyena me bɛba abɛhyɛ wɔn ti nwi ama wɔn fɛfɛɛfɛ paa. Akokɔsrade no kae sɛ, Metee. Kwaku Ananse baa fie; ɔkae sɛ, Aso, kɔhwehwɛ mmɛw-nuts ne gyeene, ɛfiri sɛ ɔkyena mebɛkum na meabɔ bɔɔl. g san de akraman bi bae sɛ ebia yɛbɛnya nkwan ne foroe adi. " "Aso" kɔgyee bi, na Ananse no de ne Sekkan ne ɛtɔ kɔɔ asuwa no mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Father Crocodile, the Nsuo-animal, I am coming to reward you. " Na ɔgyee fufuo no de guu nsuo no mu. Akokɔsrade no baeɛ, na ɔrebɛdi fufuo adidi, berɛ a Ananse no de ne sekan no bɔɔ no, gyaee! nanso wannya no (properly), na ɔkɔgyee. Kwaku Ananse san baa fie. "Aso" bisaa no sɛ, "Ɛhe na saa aboa no wɔ? " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Yi wo ho; onipa bi firi akwantu mu aba, na wannya ne ahome mpo, na wofiri aseɛ rebisa no nsɛmmisa. " "Aso" kae sɛ, "Mehunu sɛ woannye no. " Kwaku Ananse anka hwee. Ade kyee anɔpa koraa, "Aso" kae sɛ ɔrekɔ asuwa hɔ. Ɛduruu hɔ no, na Akokɔsrade no da asubonten ano, na ɛmo-blue-bottle-flies no rebu kurukuru wɔ ne ho. "Aso" san baa fie kɔka kyerɛɛ ne kunu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Sesei wuhu sɛnea aduru bi a mewɔ no, ɛyɛ adwuma; sɛ meku nam (da bi) a, ɛnyɛ kɔsi sɛ nneɛma bɛsɔre, da a ɛdi hɔ no, na me kɔgye, nanso ɛmfa ho no, mema wo akwaaba sɛ woahu. " Kwaku Ananse twaa poma bi de kɔɔ asuwa a ɛwɔ hɔ no mu, na ɛhɔ na ɔhunuu Akokɔsrade no. Ɔde poma no guu mu kaa sɛ, "Akokɔsrade, awu wo anaa? Akokɔsrade no, awu anaa? " na ɔdanee kɔɔ nakyi ne nakyi. Na Akokɔsrade no da hɔ ara, nanso na onwu. Bere a Kwaku Ananse rebɛgye ne nsa aka no, Akokɔsrade no bɔɔ no mpofirim, kuadwuma! Kwaku Ananse bɔɔ mmoden, petere! petere! Na ɔnyaa kwan dwanee. Ɛno nti, bere a Ananse no twa nsuo no mu no, ɔtu mmirika kɔ nanim; ɔsuro sɛ ebia Akokɔsrade no bɛba abɛkyere no bio.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
