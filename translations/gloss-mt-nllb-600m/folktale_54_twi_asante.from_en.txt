SƐNEA ADAMFOFA NKITAHODI BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU

ƆBƆMMƆFOƆ NE NE yere tuu kwan kɔɔ ɔbɔfoɔ bi nsraban mu. Na Osebo a ɔyɛ Daboɔ no kyeree ɔbɔfoɔ no na ɔyee no, nanso na ne yere a wanyinsɛn no da so ara wɔ hɔ. Ɔbaa no kɔɔ aborɔbɛ no mu, na (ɛhɔ) na ɔkɔhyiaa Daboɔ ba no, na ɔbisaa no sɛ, Maame, dɛn na wopɛ? Ɔbaa no buae sɛ, Me ne me kunu na yɛbaa ha, nanso Daboɔ bi akum no. Daboɔ ba no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Mepɛ wo, enti mma ha bio, efisɛ sɛ woba ha a, sɛ agya bɛhyia wo a, ɔbɛkum wo. Ɔbaa no antua aTT biara. ention, na ɔsan kɔɔ ananse-ananse no ntam, na Daboɔ kyeree no, na ɛde no kɔɔ fie. Daboɔ ba no hunuu ɔbaa no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ nagya sɛ, "Me yareɛ. " Ɛyɛɛ ɔbaa no nti, a na wahu no dada. Daboɔ no ka kyerɛɛ nabofra no sɛ, Wobɛtumi ayare, nanso bra, fa kɔ, twa ne nam. Ɔtwaa ne ti, na ɔhunuu abofra bi a ɔda ne yafunu mu, na ɔyii no, de kɔtoo baabi. Afei agya no bisaa no sɛ, Dɛn na woakɔto ato hɔ? Na abofra no bua sɛ, Deɛn na mede kɔtoo hɔ no, sɛ woahunu a, anka worenni nam yi. Daboɔ no yɛɛ aduane no. Ɔka kyerɛɛ nabofra no sɛ wɔmmɛdidi, nanso ɔbuaa sɛ, Nam, a mehwɛeɛ ara na ɛyɛɛ me yareɛ no, anka ɛsɛ sɛ me dwene sɛ sɛ me di bi a, anka mɛwu. Ɔagya no buae sɛ, Woanni fɔ koraa. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, bere a agya no fii kwan so sɛ ɔbɛkɔ nwura hɔ no, ɛnneɛ nabofra no kɔgyee abofra no, de baa fie, na ɔhohoroo no. Afei ɔkɔfaa banana, na ɔnoaa, na ɔpiapiaa aba nsuo no, na ɔmaa ne ba no anom. Ɔfaa nkyerɛwee a ɛfa berɛ a nagya san bae no, na ɔfaa abofra no de no siee. Na ne ba no adi bɛyɛ mfeɛ baako berɛ a ɔduruu mmabunbere - abofra a ɔwɔ "Spider-story" mu no renkyɛ na wanyin. Afei Onipa ba no bisaa akraman ba no sɛ, Wo din deɛn? Ɔbuaa sɛ, Me din de Kofi-the-beastba. Ɔkaa sɛ, Me nso, me din de Kofi-the-beastba. Afei Kofi-the-beastba no ka kyerɛɛ Kofi-the-beastba no sɛ, Mepɛ sɛ me ne wo di agorɔ, nanso mesuro agya, na mo nso musuro no, enti nna mmiɛnsa a ɛreba yi nyinaa mebɛka akyerɛ no sɛ ɔnkyerɛkyerɛ. me sɛnea meprɛ, na bere a wakyerɛkyerɛ me, na me nim sɛnea ɛyɛ, meprɛ na meakyere no, na bere a wobɛte sɛ me nsa aka no, oh! wobɛfa Sekkan, na woabɛtwa netire. Na agya no baeɛ, na Kofi-the-beasts-abofra no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Kyerɛkyerɛ me how to spring. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ɛkɔ ɔkyena anɔpa. Ade kyeɛ, bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔne agya no kɔɔ baabi, dwiraa baabi a ɛho tew, na agya no huruiɛ, na nabofra no nso huri, na agya no da nakyi. agya no kaa sɛ, Tutuw na bra me so. Kofi-the-beasts-child sprang, na ɔbɔɔ no so, na Kofi-the-beasts-child kyeree ne menem. Ɔteɛɛm guu Kofi-the-beasts-child no so sɛ, Me nsa aka no, fa ba. Ɔtuu mmirika faa ne Sekkan no, kɔɔ hɔ ntɛm ara, na ɔtwaa ne etire. Kofi-The-Beast ba no ka kyerɛɛ Kofi-the-human-beings-child sɛ, Seesei, ampa ara, wo ne me ayɛ sɛ nnipa a wɔyɛ pɛ, enti ma yɛnkɔsie no. Wɔkɔsiee no, na wɔsan baa fie. Kofi-the-beasts-child kaa sɛ Kofi-the-beasts-child no, I expect you would like to go to your akuraa. Kofi-the-beasts-child kaa sɛ, Yiw, nanso merentumi nhu baabi a ɛwɔ. Kofi-the-beasts-child kaa sɛ, Me ara mebɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ hɔ na asan aba. Ɔde ne ho sii hɔ, na ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu nnipa bi a wɔwɔ sumina bi mu, na wɔrekasa (na wɔreka), Yiw, ɛyɛ nokware paa sɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no ne ne yere no rekɔ ɔbɔfoɔ no nsraban mu, na obiara nnya wɔn ho hua bio. Ɛhɔ na ɛhɔ na Kofi-the-beasts-child no dwane kɔbɔɔ Kofi-the-human-beings-child amaneɛ sɛ, Mahu wo akuraa, na nnipa a mihuu wɔn no wɔ ntoma mu, enti mebɛhwehwɛ bi ama wo na mede aba wo nkyɛn. Kofi-the-beasts-child no sii hɔ kɔɔ kwan so, na adwadifoɔ retwa mu. Ɔbɔɔ mmirika kɔɔ wɔn so, na adwadifoɔ no too wɔn nnesoa gui dwanee. Kofi-The-Beast ba no faa ntoma de baa Kofi-the-Beast ba no nkyɛn. Afei Kofi-the-Beast ba no kaa sɛ, Pistola ne pistola-medicine (powder) da so wɔ hɔ. Kofi-The-Beast ba no hwehwɛɛ bi de baa. Kofi-the-beasts-child bisaa Kofi-the-beast, Wobɛda ase ama me anaa? Kofi-the-beasts-child buaa no sɛ, Mentumi nkasa mprempren ara, migyina wanim saa. Kofi-the-beasts-child faa Kofi-the-beasts-child na ɔkɔgyee no sɛ ɔmfa no nkɔ nakuraa. Bere a wɔduruu kuro no kuro akyi no, Kofi-beasts-child no ka kyerɛɛ Kofi-the-human-beings-child no sɛ, Ɛno ne wo akuraa, na saa baabi a worekɔ yi, da biara mebɛma wo nam pɔ kɛseɛ bi; na afei nso anwummere no, mebɛba wo nkyɛn, enti, sɛ wote nea worekɔ seesei no, woware, afei ma wo yere nna dan no fã baako mu, na wo nso wo wɔ foforo mu. Kofi-the-beasts-child no fi-the-human-beings-child no bɛduruu fie, na ɔkaa abasɛm sɛnea ɔne ne maame ne nagya kɔɔ nwura mu, ne sɛnea na wɔn nyinaa awu, ne sɛnea na ɔfiri hɔ aba bio. Na nnipa no werɛ ahow ne no. Ɔtoo atuo, yɛɛ ayie, na ɔdii nea ɛhia nyinaa wiee. Afei, Kofi-the-beasts-child no, ɔsɔre baeɛ no, ɔde nam baeɛ. Bere a anadwo dwoeɛ no, ɔbaa ne nkyɛn. Afei ɛnkyɛ na ɔwaree. Bere a ɔbaa no kɔ nna no, na ɔyɛ ne nna wɔ baabi foforo (sen ne nkyɛn). Wɔ anɔpatutuutuutu, na ɔde nam pɔ bi nyinaa brɛ ne yere. Mprempren, na ne yere hu sɛ ɔnkɔ nwura no, nanso wɔ nitan a ɛwɔ hɔ no, daa na ɔnya nam, nanso wamfa nsɛm tiawa biara ankɔ. Ɛdii da bi nso wɔ anadwo, bere a wɔda hɔ (ne dan mu) no, na ɔbaa no rebɛfa ne nsa aka ne kunu no, na ɔkɔkaa adeɛ a ɛyɛ dɛɛdɛɛ bi, na ne kunu no nso da hɔ. Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔkɔkaa amaneɛbɔ no kyerɛɛ folk no sɛ, "Akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho sɛ me kunu adamfo yɛ Daboɔ, na akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho sɛ ɔno na ɔde nam no ba. " Ɛneɛ na nnipa no de wɔn atuo kɔhyɛɛ asaworowa (baabi a wɔde nam no sii) nkyɛn, na berɛ a ɛdwoɔ no, na Daboɔ no rebɛgye nam no abɛto asaworowa no so no, wɔde wɔn atuo bɔɔ no. Kofi-the-beast no ba no kaa sɛ, "Kofi-the-beast no ba, nnipa dasani nni aseda. " Kofi-the-beast no ba no tee asɛm no, ɔtuu mmirika firii adi, faa no kɔtoo no hɔ, na ɔkɔkaa asɛm no nyinaa kyerɛɛ akuraa no etire, sɛnea ɔne ne maame ne agya kɔeɛ, ne amanehunu ahorow a wɔahunu no nyinaa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Saa aboa kɛseɛ yi awu nti, me ne no bɛkɔ (bomu), efiri sɛ ɛyɛ me mfomsoɔ nyinaa sɛ wawuo. " Na ɔhyɛɛ sɛ wɔnyɛ funnaka mmienu; na wɔde Kofi-the-aboa kɛseɛ no ba bɛhyɛɛ baako mu; na ɔno (Kofi-the-onipa-being's-child) nso de tuo kɔtoo ne ho, na wɔde no kɔtoo adaka no mu baako mu. Ɛnna wɔsiee wɔn. Na wɔse, "Kɔdi agoru na wani nnye wɔ wadamfofa nkitahodi mu. " Saa na adamfo bi fadie baa abusuakuo no mu.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
