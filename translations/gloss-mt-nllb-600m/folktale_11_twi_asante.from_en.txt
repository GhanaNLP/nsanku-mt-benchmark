SƐNEA KWATIMA, ODWENNINI NO, BƐNYAA ODWANINI DIN

ƐHƆ (bere bi) na Odwennini bi, Kwatima, wɔ hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ, "Merebɛhwehwɛ asase bi a mebɛsi fie wɔ so. " Na Daboɔ no nso, wɔ berɛ koro no ara mu, na ɔwɔ adwene koro no ara pɛpɛɛpɛ. Ɛneɛ Kwatima, Odwennini no, hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwɛ asase a ɔpɛ no, na ɔsiesiee no, wɔ ahyɛdeɛ sɛ ɔbɛtumi asi fie. Na Osebo, Daboɔ no, nso bɔɔ ne ho (na ɛduruu baabi a na Odwennini no ayi no), ɔkae sɛ, "Ayamye me! Me akuraa a na mɛsi no, nanso wonnya nyɛɛ ho adwuma biara no, me nananom nsamanfo afi ase dedaw. " Na ɔno nso bɔɔ petee ketewa na ɔbɔɔ ne ho. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma fii aseɛ yii ne ho adi (ie. anɔpatutuutuutu), Odwennini no san kɔɔ so bio. Bere a ɔduruu baabi no, ɔkae sɛ, "Ayamye me! " Of a nokware my nananom nsamanfo rebɛboa me, (hwɛ) fam a wɔapopa no. " Na ɔno nso, ɔhyɛɛ (nwura no) mu na ɔkɔnyinii. Na Daboɔ no nso, ɔsan baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh! eyi yɛ asɛm bi; me ho rebɔ me hu. " Na ɔno nso, ɔkɔɔ nwura no mu, ɔtwaa adunsina kɔtoo hɔ (wɔ beaeɛ) hɔ. Na Odwennini no nso baeɛ, na ɔno nso hwɛɛ akyiri wɔ ahodwiriw mu. Ɔsan kɔɔ nwura no mu, kɔtwaa nhahamama, na ɔtoo hɔ kɔeɛ. Na bio Daboɔ no baeɛ. Ɔno nso, ɔkyɛɛ nani (bere a nkɔsoɔ no baeɛ), na ɔtwaa mu (fie) kɔeɛ. Kwatima nso baeɛ, na ɔtwaa atosuro no de adunsina no hyɛɛ mu, na ɔtwee mu. Na bio Daboɔ no baeɛ, na ɔkyekyeree nnua a ɛtwam no, na ɔkɔgyee mu. Na bio Kwatima baeɛ, na ɔkɔgyee sare no de sii hɔ kɔeɛ. Na Daboɔ no baeɛ bio, na ɔde atɛkyɛ no fraa mu yɛɛ plaster no, na ɛkɔɔ so. Na bio Kwatima baeɛ na ɛdan atifi kata no, na ɛkɔɔ so. Na bio Daboɔ no baeɛ, na ɛbɔɔ fam ahodoɔ no, na ɛkɔɔ so. Ɛnna Kwatima nso baeɛ, na ɔgyee dɔte fitaa ne plaster (afasuo) no, na ɔfiri fie kɔeɛ. Bere a ɔkɔɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ nabofra no sɛ, "To-da a mewiee fie no, na Akwasidae no bɛtɔ no, ɛda (following) Monday no na yɛbɛyɛ ofie foforɔ hyɛ ase afahyɛ no, enti monhwehwɛ nnipa a wɔwɔ atuo nkɔto gya wɔ dan no mu. " Daboɔ no nso baeɛ (na wɔhunuu fie a awie no). Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛno! Me nni hwee ka, gye sɛ mebɛkɔ akɔgye me agyapadeɛ ne me san ba. " Ɛneɛ na ɔkɔɔ, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ nabofra no sɛ, "Dwane ntɛm ara sɛdeɛ wutumi na ka kyerɛ ghost-hunter no sɛ, Adae bɛtɔ wɔ Kwasiada bi, na ɛda (following) Monday no, mebɛdi ofie foforɔ hyɛ ase afahyɛ, enti ma ɔmmɛto tuo mma me. " Na nabofra no kɔɔka kyerɛɛ no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Metee. " Ɛneɛ da a ɛdi Adae no, Kwatima boaboaa nagyapadeɛ ano, na ɔde kɔtoo fie hɔ, na ɔsan baeɛ. Daboɔ no nso boaboaa ne deɛ ano, na ɔde kɔeɛ. Bere a na ɛda adi sɛ wɔbɛhunu nneɛma no, Akwasidae so no, Kwatima kyinkyinkyin kyekye nnipa a na ɔne wɔn te no; na Daboɔ nso bɔɔ ne nkrabew. Ɛdwoada, bere a nneɛma puei (ie. anɔpatutuutu), Kwatima ne nabofra no kɔɔ (ofie foforɔ hɔ), ɛnna ɔkɔtoo tuo bi guu dan no mu. Daboɔ no nso baeɛ; ɔtee tuo no; ɔkae sɛ, "To-da no nyɛ da pa, to-da a ahonhom no rebɔ atuo. " Na ɔno nso baeɛ; na Phantom-hunter no nso kɔɔ fie hɔ, na ɔma tuo no suanee sɛ, pomm! Daboɔ no baeɛ na ɔhunuu Kwatima ne nabofra te hɔ. Ɔse, "Wo eguadwa yɛ dɛn? " Kwatima nso kae sɛ, "Wo nso, wo eguadwa yɛ dɛn? " Daboɔ no kae sɛ, "Wo nni sianteɛ (sɛ woba ha a), me nso me nni bi. " Kwatima kae sɛ, "Sɛ saa a, ɛnneɛ yɛn mmienu nyinaa bɛtena ha. " Enti wɔtenaa hɔ. Ɛda biara, berɛ a Daboɔ sɔre no, na ɔkɔ ahayɔ, na ɔde nam ba. Daa na ɛte saa. Nanso Kwatima deɛ, ɔmfa hwee mmaeɛ. Ɛnna da bi osurofoɔ no kɔɔ nwura no mu, ɔbɔɔ mmɔden paa sɛ ɔbɛkyere adeɛ bi nanso wantumi. Ɔsan baa fie, na ɔkaa sɛ, Kwatima, sɛ da a wobɛkɔ nwura no mu na woamfa nam bi amma a, nea merebɛyɛ wo no, worennye ntom. Kwatima hyɛɛ aseɛ, na ɔkɔɔ nwura no mu, na ɔsan de nwura-buck bi bae na ɔtoo hɔ. Daboɔ no hunuu aboa no, ɛyɛɛ no nwanwa sɛdeɛ biribiara te. Bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi (ie. anɔpatutuutu) no, Kwatima bɔɔ, na ɔsan de foforɔ (antelope) bae. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "E! " Ɔka kyerɛɛ nabofra no sɛ, "Hwɛ ha, merekɔ nwura no mu, na me ne Kwatima nso rekɔ, na ɔtwa ba a mede brɛɛ wo no, sɛ yɛakɔ a, frɛ Kwatima ba no na wobisa no sɛ, 'Sɛ wagya kɔ nwura no mu kɔyi nam a, kyerɛ yɛn wɔ ɔtwee yi so sɛnea ɔyɛ. '" Na nnipa no (Osebo ne Kwatima) hyɛɛ aseɛ. Na akraman ba no kae sɛ, "Kwatima ba, bra yɛnni agorɔ. " Ɔbaeɛ, ɔkaa sɛ, "Sɛ agya kɔ nwura mu a, saa na ɔkyere agorɔ. " Na ɔtuu mmirika, na ɔkyeree ɔtwa ba no kyeree ne menem. Kwatima ba no kae sɛ, "Kɔ fa ɔbotan bi. " Akraman ba no kɔfaa baako baeɛ. Ɔde sii hɔ. Kwatima ba no twee ne ho firii ho baeɛ na ɔde ne etire pim bɔɔ ɔboɔ no! Ɛhɔ na ɛbɔɔ no nsinnsin. Akraman ba no kaa sɛ, "Aah. " Na wɔn agyanom firi nwura no mu baeɛ, na akraman ba no ka kyerɛɛ nagya nea asi no, na Kwatima ba no nso ka kyerɛɛ nagya nea asi no. Ɛnneɛ na wɔwɔ hɔ, da bi a adeɛ bi bɛkaa Kwatima nan ho, na ɔregye ne ho, nanso Daboɔ no dwene sɛ ɔreboa wɔ ahyɛdeɛ bi mu sɛ wɔbɛkum no. Ntɛm ara na Daboɔ no de ahyɛdeɛ bi tuu Kwatima so kɔkyeree no. Na Kwatima dwane firii kiri, kiri, kiri mu. Ɔkɔɔ asuwea bi a na ɛda hɔ a Ɔnanka wɔ mu. Kwatima kaa sɛ, "Paapa Ɔnanka, medwane me ba wo nkyɛn sɛ bammɔ a ɛnyɛ sɛ Daboɔ bɛkyere me. " Ɛhɔ na Ɔnanka no kaa sɛ, "Wo, wo yɔnkoɔ, woayɛ hokwan sɛ woba me nkyɛn, fa me nsa. " Kwatima bɔɔ mmɔden na ɔde ne nsa bɔɔ no. Saa na Kwatima bɛgyee din Odwa-nini (lit. Shake His nsa with the Ɔnanka). Ɛfiri ne nsukyea a ɔne Ɔnanka no dii.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi ayɛ wo nokware, ne ahomegyeɛ a wubetumi akamfo me (wɔ nasɛmka ho).
