Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ABAKƆSƐM BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU

WƆKYERƐ SƐ ɔbɔfoɔ bi (bere bi) kɔɔ nwura mu; ɔbaeɛ na ɔhwɛɛ amena bi mu, na emu na ɛgyina Onipa, Ɔbarima, Osebo, Daboɔ, Ɔwɔ, Ɔwɔ, ne Okusie, Okisi. Mprempren bere a saa nnipa yi hunuu Ɔbɔfoɔ no, baako yi kae sɛ, Father, come and take me out. Obiako no kae sɛ, Father, come and take me out. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, I will not come, for by-ɔkyena koraa, sɛ mebɛgye wo, na woaka me a, dɛn na wobɛyɛ me? Me nim sɛ ɛnsɛ sɛ me werɛ how. Obiako no kae sɛ, I couldnt (act so). Obiako no kae sɛ, I couldnt (act so). Ɔbɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, I could not (act so) Sɛ saa na ɛyɛ asɛm no ampa a, mebɛyi wo afiri hɔ. Na ɛyɛ nokware a ɔde nnipa no nyinaa afiri hɔ kɔɔ nakuraa hɔ. Ɛda bi, bere a Ɔbɔfoɔ no wɔ hɔ no, Osebo, Daboɔ no, de nam pii bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ɔbɔfoɔ, agya, eyi yɛ aseda afɔreɛ ma wo ayamye a woyɛeɛ no, meda wo ase, Apiafo. Ɔde mmɛw ahaban basket dumienu maa Ɔbɔfoɔ no. Ɔbɔfoɔ no daa no ase ma Daboɔ no kɔɔ so. Wɔ dwumadie foforɔ bi nso ase, na Ɔbɔfoɔ no wɔ hɔ, na Okusie, Okisi no, baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ɔbɔfoɔ, wopɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma bi? Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Ho! dɛn nti na ɛnyɛ saa, ɛno ne adeɛ a ɛwɔ mu hwehwɛ a mmarima ho yeraw wɔn. Okisi no hyɛɛ aseɛ, na ɛkɔɔ baabi a Nyankopɔn, ɔsoro-nyame no, de ne sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no sieeɛ. Ɔkɔɔ kɔtwaa ɛtokuro bi wɔ dan no ase, na ɔkɔɔ dan no mu, na ɔkɔgyee ɔsoro-anyame sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no nyinaa, na ɔde baa Ɔbɔfoɔ no nkyɛn. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Me da wo ase, Aku. " Na Okisi no kɔeɛ. Ɛda foforɔ nso, na ɔwɔ hɔ, na ɔtee sɛ obi rebɔ no akyea, "Berɛ a atwam. " Ɔbɔfoɔ no buaeɛ sɛ, "Ame (come in). Ɔbarima no baeɛ, ɔkaa sɛ, Agya Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ, anɔpa pa. Ɔbuaa sɛ, Aane, kinsman. Ɔkaa sɛ, Eyi yɛ palm-wine kukuo ketewa bi a mede abɛma wo. Agya Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Mempɛ; wo deɛ, Ɔbarima, ɛyɛ sɛnea mekaeɛ no, bere a mekaa sɛ merennye wo nni no, hwɛ nea Daboɔ no de brɛɛ me, hwɛ nea Okisi de brɛɛ me. Ɔbarima no yii ne palm-wine no. na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔkaa Ewiem-onyankopɔn no, sɛ, Wo sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma a ɛyeraeɛ no, Okisi no bɛgyeeɛ kɔeɛ de maa Ɔbɔfoɔ no. Ewiem-onyankopɔn no kaa sɛ, Ɛyɛ saa anaa? Ɔkaa sɛ, Grandsire, ɛyɛ nokorɛ pefee. Ewiem-onyankopɔn no somaa asɛnkafoɔ sɛ wɔnkɔkyere Ɔbɔfoɔ no, na wɔde hama bɔɔ nasene. Wɔbɔɔ no akuturuku kɛseɛ. Afei a wɔne Ɔbɔfoɔ no bɛduruu kwan so no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Ananmusini, mepɛ sɛ mekɔdwo me ho. " Asɛnkafoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Dabi, ɔfɔmfoɔ, wobɛdwane. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dabi, merenswane, na sɛ wopɛ a, fa hama no, fa me asene kyekyere no dunsin na fa me kwan. " Asɛnkafoɔ no tiee no, na wɔgyaa no. Ɛhɔ na na ɔwɔ, ɔmaa ne ho tɔɔ no, berɛ a aboa tenten na ne ho yɛ mmerɛw no baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Hwɛ ha, me adamfo, gye aduro kɔɔn ketewa yi, na fa no fa wo ho, na sɛ wokɔ, na wɔgyina wo asɛmbli anim, na ɛba sɛ wɔbɛkum wo a, mebɛkɔ Ɔhene harem, na matumi aka ne yere a ɔdɔ no paa no ba no; sɛ me nka no wie a, wubehu sɛ obiara asɔre, na wo nso wobɛka, na woaka sɛ, Mewɔ aduro, na sɛ wode bedi dwuma wɔ abofra a ɔbɛnya no so. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Meda wo ase, Aku. Asɛnkafoɔ no faa Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔgyinaa ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no anim, (n'anim ne ne Mpɔtam badwafoɔ) Kronti, Akwum, Adonten, Gyaase ne Kɔm ahemfo. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa n'asɛm, na berɛ a Kasafoɔ no sɔre maa atɛmmuo no, wɔtee awerɛhow dede, pui! pui! akyirikyiri wɔ ahemfo mpenafo fie. Wɔkae sɛ, Dɛn na ɛyɛ saa? Wɔkae sɛ, Ɔwɔ akeka Ɔsorosoro Nyankopɔn no yere a ɔdɔ no no abofra. Obiara kɔeɛ. Na Ɔbɔfoɔ no gyina hɔ, na ɔse, Sɛ mennyɛ deduani a, anka mɛtumi anya aduro (awuduro ano aduro). onipa bi a ɔtee ne no kɔɔ akɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, Monkɔfa deduani no mmra ha. Ɛneɛ na Ɔbɔfoɔ no baeɛ. Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Sɛ wotumi hwɛ yi a, me abofra, na wanwu a, mede wo etire bɛkyerɛ wo. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Mete no, Kind-one. Na Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa bio sɛ, Nanabarima, nea mekyerɛ wo yi, sɛ wotumi nya bi a, ɛnneɛ mɛtumi ahwɛ wo abofra. Wɔkaa sɛ, Ɛyɛ adeɛ bɛn? Ɔkaa sɛ, Tell-tale mogya bɛfra pounded medic ine. Wɔkaa sɛ, Ɛhe na yɛbɛnya anansesɛm mogya? Kasamafoɔ no mu baako, a na ɔyɛ ɔnyansani, kaa sɛ, Na yɔnkoɔ a ɔbaa abɛka kyerɛɛ Ewiem-onyankopɔn sɛ Ɔbɔfoɔ abɛfa Ewiem-onyankopɔn sika no, ɔwɔ he? Wɔkaa sɛ, Worenni mfomsoɔ, worenni mfomsoɔ. Wɔkɔɔ kɔfaa yɔnkoɔ no bae. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Bu ne nsa wɔ joint no so, na fa ne mogya no ma me. Ɛhɔ ara na wɔbubuu no. Ɔbɔfoɔ no faa mogya no de sɔɔ aduro no, na ɔde guu ɔhene ba no nan so. Ɛhɔ ara na ɔsɔreee. Wɔkaa sɛ, Monto Hunter no nnwom. Wɔteɛɛm sɛ, Eee! Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Mefa wo etire na mede rema wo, na Tell-tale no deɛ, ma wɔnkum no, na wɔntwa ne honam mu, na wɔntow gu afã ahodoɔ no nyinaa so. Wokum no, na wotwaa ne honam mu nketenkete, na wɔtow guu ne nyinaa ho. Saa Tell-Tales pii baa abusuakuo no mu.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
