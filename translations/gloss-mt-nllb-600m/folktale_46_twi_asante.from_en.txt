Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐNSƐ SƐ OBIARA KYERƐ SƐ, "YERE YI NA MANI GYE HO DEƐ ƐYE PA ARA"

ƆBEREKUO, Wild-pigeon, ne Ɔnwam, Anwam, ne Okusie, Okisi, nyinaa waree ɔbarima koro. Seesei saa ɔbarima no yɛɛ Okusie, Okisi, ne yere a ɔdɔ no no; na Ɔberekuo, Wild-pigeon, ne Anwam deɛ, na ɔnhwɛ wɔn yie koraa. Sɛ ɔbɛsɔre akɔ abɔnten na wahu biribi a ɛyɛ fɛ a, na ɔtɔ na ɔde aba Adueku (a ɛyɛ din foforɔ ma Okusie). Ɛdii nna mmienu ni, na ɛnnii nna mmiɛnsa, yareɛ bi bɛkaa wɔn kunu ma ɔwuiɛ. Berɛ a ɔwuiɛ no, nabusuafoɔ frɛɛ akunafoɔ no sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ mo ba bɛsu. " Wild-pigeon no baeɛ na ɔmaa ne nne so sɛ: "Du du, du du du!

Du Du!

Du du, du du du du!

Kunu awu, Du du!

Me nnidi bio da, Du Du!

Kosi sɛ mebɛhyia me kunu.

Du du!"

Ɔwarefoɔ (late) no nuanom kaa sɛ, "Woayɛ adeɛ, woayɛ adeɛ (worthy of ayeyi); wo nso, Ɔnwam, bra (here). " Ɔnwam baeɛ, na ɔde (her awerɛhoɔ dwom) sii hɔ sɛ:

"Kya, kya, me kunu awu!

Kyaa, ky?

Me kunu awu deɛ, kyia, kyia.

Kunu awu.

Kyaa, ky?

Minni aduan bio!

Kyaa, ky?

Kosi sɛ mebɛhyia (my) kunu.

Kya, kya".

Ɔkunu (late) no nuanom kaa sɛ, "Onwam, woayɛ adeɛ, woayɛ adeɛ (worthy of nkamfoɔ); Okusie, bra (ha), ma wo ne wo kunu adidi adeɛ a ɛdɛ paa. " Okusie kaa sɛ, "O, mma me nte saa. " Na ɔbaa so maa ne nne so:

"Sankuroro Dampofo!

Nsankuroro.

Me deɛ, Sankuroro Dampofo, ɛnyɛ den sɛ wɔbɛhunu okununom.

Sɛ wawu a, wawu!

Sankuroro. "

Seesei na ɔwiee, na obiara faa no sɛ nankasa adesua, sɛ, "Ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara ka wɔ abusuakuo yi mu sɛ, 'Yɛn yere yi ne me yere a ɔdɔ no no. " (abebuo) Okusie awerɛhoɔ no na ɛde ne mfitiaseɛ ba.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
