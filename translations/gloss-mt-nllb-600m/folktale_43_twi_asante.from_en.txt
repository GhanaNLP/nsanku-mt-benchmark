Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐKƆƆ SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ NWANSANA, NWANSENA NO, BƐTRA KRAMAN, KRAMAN NO, ƆPƐPƐ NO, KAM! KAM!

Ɔbɔfoɔ bi wɔ nkraman nnan; baako ne Sniff-sniff; baako nso ne Lick-lick; baako nso ne Tie-in-apɔ; na baako nso ne Mene kɛse-down. Ná ɔde ne nkraman bɔ abɔmmɔ. Daa, sɛ ɔkɔ nwura mu a, na ɔne wɔn kɔ bɔmu. Da bi ɔkɔɔ nwura mu na ɔhunuu kola-dua bi a ɛte sɛɛ, a wɔde aduaba akata so a ɛrekyekyere wɔ ancesã ahorow mu. Ɛnna ɔsan baa fie, na ɔkaa amaneɛbɔ no kyerɛɛ ne yere sɛ, " kola-dua bi, a mahunu no, wɔde aduaba akata so wɔ yaleã ahorow a ɛte sɛɛ mu. " Ne yere kaa sɛ, "Kɔ fa, na wunim sɛ yɛnni kola-dua baako mpo a yɛbɛwe na yɛatɔn. " Ɔbɔfoɔ no de ɔkwan no sii hɔ, nanso ɔgyaee nkraman no wɔ fie, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a kola-dua no wɔ. Afei ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔfaa kola-nuts no bi. Seesei ɔtee nne a ɛfiri baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ sɛ: "Twa! Be! kukuru! kukuru! hu! pi! " Sasabonsam bi (gyinaa) nanim. Ɔse, "Hwan na watu me kola-nuts? " ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "I. " Sasabonsam kaa sɛ, "I will kill you immediately on akawnto of this adeɛ a wayɛ yi. " Ɔɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wobɛkum me a, ma me kan frɛ me nkraman. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Call, but when you have three mmɔdenbɔ to call them and they dont come, then I will kill you. " Ɛneɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no maa ne ho so (ne nne) sɛ:

"Sniff-sniff, (come and) meet me, Lick-lick, (come and) meet me, Tie-in-apɔ, (come and) meet me, Mene kɛse-down, (come and) meet me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn akyɛ!

Na nkraman a wɔwɔ fie hɔ no tee no dwoodwoo pa ara, na wɔtuu mmirika kɔɔ kuro no kuro akyi. Ɛneɛ na ɔbɔfoɔ no maa ne nne so bio:

Sniff-sniff, (come and) meet me, Lick-lick, (come and) meet me, Tie-in-apɔ, (come and) meet me, GULP-DOWN, (come and) meet me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn akyɛ!

Mprempren nkraman no ate; wɔatu mmirika, prada! prada! Sasabonsam no kaa sɛ, R frɛ bio, na ɛno wie berɛ a ɛtɔ so mmiɛnsa. Ɛnna ɔbɔfoɔ no maa ne nne so kɛseɛ ara:

Sniff-sniff, (come and) meet me, Lick-lick, (come and) meet me, Tie-in-apɔ, (come and) meet me, GULP-DOWN, (come and) meet me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔn akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, ɛkyɛ na ɛreba! Na nano nnyina hɔ ara, bere a na nkraman no nyinaa wɔ nanim no. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no reka sɛ, "Sniff-sniff, hwɛ Sasabonsam bi, sniff no. " Na sniff-sniff no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Lick-lick, lick him over. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Tie-in-apɔ, tie no pɔ mu. " Tie-in-apɔ kyekyeree ne menem wɔ apɔ mu, na Sasabonsam no hwee fam hɔ ara, kum! Ɛneɛ Mene kɛse no de no kɔeɛ na ɔdweeɛ. Ɛnna ɔbɔfoɔ no ne nkraman no boaboaa kola-nuts no ano de baa fie. Bere a ɔbɔfoɔ no san bae no, ɔboaboaa aduane a ground-nut soup ahyɛ mu ma, na ɔde sii hɔ maa nkraman no, ɔpete nsuo guu hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn kɔdii. Bere a ɔdii aduane wieeɛ no, ɔkɔɔ abɔnten kɔtenaa gyedua (dua) ase. Afei abɔmmɔfoɔ no akoa, Nwansena, kɔɔ hɔ kɔhunuu aduane (a wɔde asie ama nkraman no), na ɔgyeeɛ de too silin no so. Nkraman no firii abɔnten baa mu na na wɔn kɔm de wɔn. Lick-lick ka kyerɛɛ afoforɔ no sɛ, "Moahunu nea yɛayɛ yɛn wura no, nanso ɛde besi seisei, wamma yɛn aduane biara a yɛbɛdi, enti momma yɛnkɔkum no. " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Worenni atoro, worenni atoro. " Lick-lick kɔɔ abɔnten hɔ kɔdwe ɔbɔfoɔ no amu, na ɔne ɔbɔfoɔ no bɔɔ agoro. Na Sniff-sniff nso baeɛ, na ɔdwe ɔbɔfoɔ no amu, na ɔno nso kɔeɛ. Ɛneɛ ntɛm ara na Tie-in-apɔ no bae sɛ ɔbɛkyekyere ne menem wɔ apɔ mu, sen sɛ Mene kɛse no nso bae sɛ ɔbɛdwe no. Seesei nkraman no san baa fie; wɔrebɛhwɛ pato no mu, berɛ a wɔhunuu nkwan no mu nsunsuansoɔ. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Fly, ebia ɔbɔfoɔ no gyaw yɛn aduane na woadi? " Fly kaa sɛ, "Minhuu no baabiara. " Sniff-sniff, bere a ɔmaa naniwa so no, ɔhunuu aduane a ɛda hɔ (wɔ silin no so). Ɔkaa sɛ, "O, papa! yɛakum yɛn wura kwa, ampa ara, aduane a ɔde asie ama yɛn no wɔ hɔ, na ɛnyɛ baako anaa mmienu, ɛyɛ Nwansena na ɔde kɔtoo hɔ. " Nwansena no dwane firii hɔ kɔeɛ. Ɛno nti, sɛ kraman te hɔ a, nwansena bi tena ne so a, ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkyere no.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
