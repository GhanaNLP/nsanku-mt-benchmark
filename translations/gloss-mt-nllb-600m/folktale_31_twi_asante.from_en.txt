Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ YƐBƐHUNU NKRUMA, AGYINAMOA NO, BERE NYINAA SƐ WƆDA VELVƐT SƆRESƆRE SO, BERE A 'ƆKRAMAN, KRAMAN NO, RENA GYAADE GYA NSO MU

WƆKYERƐ SƐ, NA ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ a na ne awoɔ yɛ awerɛhoɔ araa ma sɛ ɔbɛwo abofra a, na ɔwuwu. Enti ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔbɛbisa anyame nketewa no mu baako asɛm, na ɔbɛka akyerɛ no sɛ ɔpɛ abofra. Nyame nketewa no kae sɛ, Mede biako bɛma wo, nanso abofra no deɛ, nadwuma a ɔbɛyɛ nyinaa ne sɛ ɔbɛma wo ɛka, nanso da bi ɔbɛtua wo ka. Ɛnyɛ nna abien, na ɛnyɛ nna abiɛsa (bere a wasusuw onyame nketewa no ho akyi) na ɔbaa no nyinsɛnee. Ɔwoo abofra bi a wɔfrɛ no spider-story, efisɛ na ɛnkyɛ na wanyin. Ne ba no nyin bɛyɛɛ aberante fɛfɛ. Ɛdii da bi a na ɔne ne maame wɔ hɔ, na ɔse, "Maame, ma me sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma, na menkɔ Ano-of-the-po-ɔman, nkɔtɔ nkyene. " Ɔmaame no kae sɛ, "Dodo? " Ɔse, "Asuanu. " (i. e. £4). Na maame no faa no maa no, na ɔfirii akwantu no ase. Seesei, ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa obarima bi ne ɔkraman a ne ho asonsono. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mommra mma metɔ. " Kraman wura no kaa sɛ, "Wontumi ntɔ. " abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ɛdɛn na ɛyɛ? " Kraman wura no kaa sɛ, "An Asuanu mu duru a ɛyɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. e. £4). "Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ (me deɛ), fa £4 yi sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma boɔ. " Ɔgyee kraman no, na ɔsan de baa fie. Bere a ɔsan bae no, ne maame kae sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woantumi ankɔ wo beae a obi rekɔ? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Mede sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no tɔɔ kraman bi. " Ne maame kae sɛ, "Aane! " Seesei a wɔte hɔ no, ɛbɛyɛ sɛ ɔsrane biako. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Maame, ma me sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma a metumi akɔ dwadie. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo deɛ, sɛnea wo nneyɛeɛ teɛ no, sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no nko ara na wobɛtow ato hɔ, nanso ɛdɛn na wopɛ? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "A Asuanu ne Surudu sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma" (i.e. £5). Ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa no, ɛnneɛ. " Enti ɔhyɛɛ dwadie kwan no ho kwan. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa obarima bi a okura agyinamoa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Adamfo, fa saa aboa a ɛtɔ daa wɔ nanan so no ba, na ma metɔ. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mepue me dan mu a, nkura no ka me anan, esiane saa sianteɛ a metɔ no nti. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me srɛ wo ma menya. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wuntumi ntɔ. " Saa abofra no kaa sɛ, "Wo bɛgye no ahe? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "An Asuanu ne kuru a ne boɔ yɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Enti ɛno nti na wose, mentumi ntɔ; ha, fa no. " Abofra no gyee agyinamoa no, na ɔde firii fie. Bere a ɔduruu fie hɔ no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Maame, hwɛ ha nea mede aba yi. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Aah, ɛno ara ne nea wɔaka (bɛba) no. " Abofra no kɔɔ so tenaa hɔ (wɔ fie). Ɛbɛyɛɛ bɛyɛ nnafua aduanan akyi, bere a ɔkasa kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, Momma me sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma mma me nkɔ dwadie no. Na maame no se, Me sika a mewɔ nyinaa asa, a Asuasa mu sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i.e. £6); bere a mema wo eyi, na woakɔ, na woamfa ntɔ nneɛma no, ɛno na ɛyɛ eguadwa yi awieeɛ. " Ɔabofra no kaa sɛ, "Metee. " Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔfaa ne kotokuo, na ɔfirii, pa! Ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa asanteni bi, na na ɔkura aborɔnoma. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Adamfo, fa wo abɔdeɛ no mmra na metɔ. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Menni tɔn, na mmom mani gye ho. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Medɔn. " Anomaa wura no kaa sɛ, "Menni tɔn, na menim deɛ ɛbɛtumi ayɛ ama me. " Owura no kaa sɛ, "O, fa ma me. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wobɛtumi atɔ anaa? " Owura no kaa sɛ, "Ɛdɛn? " Owura no buae sɛ, "Asuasa mu duru yɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (£6). " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ususu sɛ ɛno nti na merentɔ no, ha na nkaebɔ dodoɔ no wɔ? " Ɔde anomaa berebuw no bae. Ne maame kae sɛ, "Eyi ankɔ yiye sen kan no, enti eyi ne nea wode aba yi? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Nanso, eyi ne nea mede aba yi. " Seesei (da bi) na abofra no te hɔ (fie), bere a Aborɔnoma no frɛɛ no sɛ, "Bra. " Bere a ɔkɔɔ so no, Aborɔnoma no bɔɔ no amanneɛ sɛ, "Me akuraa so na meyɛ ɔhene, na anka mɛkɔ akwantu bi ase no, ɔpanyin bi c ame na ɛgyee me nsɔmu, bere a wo, wo ayamye mu, wo nso woatɔ me, na afei me srɛ wo sɛ, sɛ wobɛsan de me akɔ me kuro no mu nko ara a, na wɔbɛda wo ase kɛse. " Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Wo reka atorosɛm akyerɛ me a, wobɛdwane. " Aborɔnoma no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ woantumi anhu wo kwan sɛ wobɛyɛ nea me bisa no a, fa hama a etwa to no, fa kyekyere me nan na fa me nkɔ. " Abofra no faa hama a etwa to no, na ɔkyekyeree me. Ɛde kɔɔ ne nan mu, na odii nakyi nkakrankakra kosii sɛ woduu akokɔ no kuro no mu. Bere a woduu kuro no skɛɛte ahorow akyi no, na mmofra no redi nte. Bere a wohuu no no, wɔkae sɛ, "Ɔhene no ni! Ɛha na Ɔhene no wɔ! " Wɔn mu baako tuu mmirika kɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ Kɔrenti amrado no, nanso wɔkyeree no, wotwaa ne menem, na wɔkaa sɛ, "Worema yɛn nkae yɛn awerɛhoɔ a ɛba akyire. " Nanso, ɔfoforo nso de amanneɛbɔ koro no ara kɔɔ bio. Na afei Akwamuhene no kaa sɛ, "Wo, Gyaasehene, wankasa kɔhwɛ sɛ asɛm yi yɛ dɛn? " Ɔkɔɔ kɔhwɛɛ, na ɔsan bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, ɛyɛ nokware. " Ɛneɛ wɔgyee mpa a wɔasɛn ne ahennneɛma no na wɔde Ɔhene no baa ne fie. Na abusuakuo no nyinaa ka amaneɛbɔ no, sɛnea ɔreyɛ akwantu, ne sɛnea onipa bi akyere no, ne sɛnea abofra yi, nayamye nti, atɔ no, ne sɛnea ɔsan de no baa fie no. Mpanimfoɔ ne mmabun nyinaa sɔre daa abofra no ase. Ɔhemmaa no de nsuo kuruwa a sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma ahyɛ mu ma baeɛ, mpanimfoɔ no nyinaa, nsuo kuruwa a sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma ahyɛ mu ma, obiara. Ɔhene no ankasa hwɛɛ ne nsa, yii kawa bi, na ɔmaa no. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Fa kawa yi, na nea wopɛ nyinaa, kawa yi bɛma wo. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mate. " Na ɔde kawa no kɔɔ nakuraa, na ɔde sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma ne kawa no kyerɛɛ ne maame. Ɛnna maame no kae sɛ, "Akwaaba Aku, akwaaba Aku. " Kan no, bere a na "ɔhyew" ne sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no, na ɔsan firi akwantu ahodoɔ mu, bere a na ɔregye ne maame akwaaba no, na ɔnnye no mmua. Na ɔde amaneɛbɔ no kɔmaa ne maame sɛ, "Woahunu saa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma yi ne saa kawa yi, mebɛsi akuraa kɛseɛ bi ama yɛn atena mu. " Maame no kae sɛ, "Sisi waniwa den. bɔ mmɔden biara sɛ wobɛyɛ saa). " Ɛnna abofra no de ne ho kɔgyinaa nwura no mu, oyii ne kawa no fii mu na ɔde sii fam. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Kawa, yi kwaeɛ ne nwura asase yi nyinaa ma me. " Na saa baabi no nyinaa bɛyɛɛ nea ɛho tew. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Monboaboa nea moayi no nyinaa ano nkɔ eboɔ a wɔde bɛhyew mu. " Na ɛyɛeɛ saa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Monhyehyɛ afie. " Na ɛyɛɛ saa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Kawa, momma nnipa mmra ntena saa afie yi mu. " Na nnipa baeɛ. Ɔmaa ne maame yɛɛ Ɔhemmaa, ɔno nso bɛyɛɛ ɔpanyin. Seesei (i.e. na asɛm bi wɔ hɔ a me werɛ firi sɛ mɛka), Kwaku Ananse, Ananse no, na ɔyɛ wɔn adamfo paa. Da bi a na ɔte hɔ (ne akuraa foforɔ) na Kwaku Ananse bɔɔ ne ho kwan sɛ ɔbɛba saa ababunu yi akuraa ase. (Bere a ɔduruu ho no, na ɔse, O, ɛna kumaa abofra, agya kumaa abofra, woanya anigyeɛ na woadi nkonim, na woanwene me ho bio sɛ wobɛhwɛ me, nanso ɛdeɛn na asi sɛ ɛde eyi nyinaa aba? Ɛnna abofra no ka kyerɛɛ no amaneɛbɔ no nyinaa. Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Merekɔ makuraa na masane aba. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ nakuraa hɔ, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nua ba baa sɛ, "Merebɛsoma wo akɔ me adamfo baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ, na wakɔ no, fa saa bobesa fitaa yi ma no, na fa adwene si so na yɛ nea ɔbɛhyɛ wo nyinaa, na fa koko bɔ mmɔden sɛ wode wo nsa bɛto saa kawa no so. " Saa ababaawa no hyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ ɔbarima no akuraa hɔ. Ɔɔbarima no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Wei deɛ, mɛhwɛ sɛ worensan nkɔ bio, ɛfiri sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wotena me nkyɛn nnansa, na woasan aba. " Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "Mete aseɛ. " Afei ɔne ababaawa no nyinaa hyiaa hɔ, na ɔbarima no kɔhohoroo ne ho, na ɔtwee ne kawa no gui, na ɔde too ɛpono bi so. Ɛnna ababaawa no faa no, na ɔde kɔɔ ne wofa, Kwaku Ananse nkyɛn. Bere a Kwaku Ananse de ne nsa too so pɛ, ɔyɛɛ kawa no mu dwumadie bi sɛ ɔrekyekye kuropɔn. Sesei, ɔbarima no, bere a ɔbɛbɛhwehwɛ ne kawa no, wannya bi. Ɔbɛtee sɛ Ananse no akyekye kuropɔn a ɛsõ sen ne deɛ no. Ɛnna ɔkɔnsɛnee nkumaa-anyame no mu baako. Low-god no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Ananse nua ba a ɔbaa hɔ no, wafa no akɔma ne wofa. Ananse no nso kɔɔ kɔbisabisaa (the lesser-god and it was revealed to him) sɛ wɔbɛsoma Nkruma, Agyinamoa, ne Ɔkraman, Kraman (to recover the kawa) nti, ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔgyee aduro a ɔde bɛsa nam no, de sii kwan so, na ama mmoa no rekɔ so no, sɛ wɔwe bi adi a, wɔrentumi nkɔ baabiara bio. Ɔbarima no (who had lost his kawa) baa fie, na ɔka kyerɛɛ Agyinamoa ne Kraman sɛ, The berɛ has now come for me to tell you the sianteɛ I bought you, (and it is this); biribi a ɛyɛ me deɛ no ayera, na wɔka sɛ ɛwɔ Ananse agyapadeɛ mu, nanso ɛwɔ adaka bi a ɛwɔ ahomegyeɛ nyinaa mfinimfini. Na bio, wɔka sɛ ɔde aduro bi afrafra odwan no honam mu na ɔde ahyɛ kwan no so, enti sɛ wote hɔ a, fa no nko ara na fa so tu. " Kraman no kaa sɛ, "Agyinamoa, woate, wo na wobɛwe. " Agyinamoa no kaa sɛ, "Firi adi, wo a wotwa ɛhwene kɔ hwamhwam ne hwamhwam bere biara. " Ɛneɛ wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kasa bebree, na wɔn wura kaa sɛ, "Ɛno yie, monyɛ kɔeɛ. " Ɛmaa mmoa mmienu no sii hɔ, na wɔrekɔ so. Seesei na aka berɛ tiawa pɛ na wɔaduru baabi a nam no wɔ, bere a Kraman "heard" nam no hua no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agyinamoa, me yafunu ayɛ me yaw, na mentumi nkɔ so. " Agyinamoa no kaa sɛ, "Yɛnni, nsa so eguadwa no ho hia. " (Kraman no) kaa sɛ, "Agyinamoa, mentumi. " Agyinamoa no kɔɔ so. Kraman no kɔɔ baabi a nam no wɔ no, ɔweam nam no nyinaa; ɛhɔ na ɔdaeɛ a, ɔrentumi nkɔ so bio. Na Agyinamoa no kɔduruu Ananse akuraa ase, kɔdaa Ananse dan mu, wɔ silin no so. Bere a ɔdaeɛ no, ɔhunuu ɔdan bi a ɛretwam. Kyeeka! Bere a ɔduruu ne etire so no. (akura no) kae sɛ, "Nkyere me, asɛm bɛn na ɛwɔ hɔ? " Agyinamoa no buae sɛ, "Me wura ring no ayera, na wɔkyerɛ sɛ ɛda Ananse kotoku no mu, a ɛgyina nnaka no mfinimfini (ahomegyeɛ nyinaa), na sɛ wotumi kɔfa me ba a, me bɛma wo kwan. " Akura no kae sɛ, "Metumi. " Agyinamoa no kae sɛ, "Mese mɛma wo kwan, na woakɔ na woamfa amma, na mmom guan? " Akura no dwanee. na ɔkaa sɛ, "Sɛ wopɛ a, fa hama a etwa to no kyekyere me asene ho. " Agyinamoa no faa hama a etwa to no kyekyeree nasene ho. Ɛnna Akura no firii mu kɔɔ Nhwiren no dan mu, na ɔkekaa ɛtokuro bi a ɛwɔ adaka no mu a na ɛgyina ahomegyeɛ no nyinaa mfinimfini. Ɛnkakrankakra ɔyɛɛ mmueano, na ɛbɛyɛɛ kɛseɛ. Ɔnam mu no, kɔgyee kawa a ɔde bɛkɔ Agyinamoa. Agyinamoa no amfa ne nsa anka ho ntɛm ara, ansa na ɔtutuu mmirika, na ɔbaa Kraman so, na ɔdaeɛ wɔ baabi a na wafiri. Ɔse, "Wo da so ara wɔ ha, na ɛhe na saa nam no wɔ? " Kraman no kae sɛ, "Aane, manhuui (ɛdeɛn na ɛbɛyɛɛ no), ebia nnipa a wɔwɔ no na wɔayiyi no; nanso ɛhe na kawa no wɔ? " Agyinamoa no kae sɛ, "Here na ɛwɔ. " Kraman no kae sɛ, "Wɔse asubonten a ɛda kwan no so no wɔ nsuyiri mu, na sɛnea wo, sɛ wotwa nsuo no, nantew ase no, ɛbɛyɛ sɛ (kawa no) bɛhwe ase nti, Fa ma, na me deɛ, wunim sɛ, mefa nsuo no ani so. " Agyinamoa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛno saa, gye. " Wɔduruu asubonten no mu no, Kraman no huruw kɔɔ mu. Saa ara na Agyinamoa no nso yɛɛ. Amonom hɔ ara na Agyinamoa no twaa mu. Kraman no nso, ɔduruu asuwa no mfinimfini no, ɔyareeɛ, na berɛ a ɔregye ahomegye kɛse a emu dɔ no, kawa no hwee nsuo no mu. Ɔtwaa asu no baa baabi a Agyinamoa no wɔ. Agyinamoa no bisaa sɛ, "Ɛhe na saa kawa no wɔ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛhwee aseɛ firii me ano kɔɔ nsuo no mu. " Agyinamoa no tuu mmirika kɔɔ nsuo no mu, ɔhunuu apataa kɛseɛ bi a ɛretwam. Agyinamoa no bɛkyeree no. Apataa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na ɛwɔ hɔ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me kawa no ahwe aseɛ wɔ asubonten yi mu, enti gye sɛ wopɛ ɔhaw a, fa me deɛ mprempren ara. Sɛ womfa mma me a, mebɛkum wo ntɛm ara. " Apataa no kaa sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔ asubonten ano sɛ metumi de nea ɛyɛ wo no ama wo. " Bere a wɔn mmienu nyinaa duruu sikakorabea hɔ no, Apataa no ho tɔɔ no, na kawa no firii adi. Agyinamoa no faa no baa bɛkyerɛɛ Kraman no. Kraman no kaa sɛ, "Agya, me srɛ wo, sɛ wokɔ a, nka nea asi yi ho asɛm. " Agyinamoa no kuraa nano to mu. Wɔduruu fie no, Agyinamoa no ka kyerɛɛ ne wura no nsɛm a asi no nyinaa, sɛ, "Kraman no nam a ɔwe bere a yɛrekɔ no nti, ɔrentumi nkɔ akyiri bio. Na bio, na me akɔfa kawa no asan de aba no, Kraman no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, bere a ɔatwa asubonten bi a ɔde nani afa mu no, ɛsɛ sɛ mede kawa no hyɛ ne nsa. Me de maa no, na ɔtow guu asubonten no mu. Ɛbɛyɛɛ kakra akyi na mede me nsa bɛtoo so bio. " Nnipa a na wɔwɔ hɔ no nyinaa kae sɛ, "Ayeyi nka Agyinamoa no. " Na wɔteɛm sɛ, "E! " Ɛnna ɔhene no kae sɛ, "Wo, Agyinamoa, aduane biara a meredi yi, mɛhwɛ sɛ mede ayamye bi bɛbubu wo asanka ketewa no mu. Me kɛtɛ biara a mɛda so no, (me bɛda so ara) gye sɛ woda so. Wo nso, Ɔkraman, bere a anadwo nwunu bɛba no, wobɛda ogya a ɛredum nnyansramma a ɛredɛ so nkutoo. Wɔde abaa bɔ nko ara na folk no bɛbɔ wo akuturuku. Ɛno nti na nna nyinaa wobɛhunu Agyinamoa, onni baabiara gye sɛ ɔda kɛtɛ a ɛye pa ara so. Afei nso, sɛ wotow aduane bi gu ne fam a, ɔrennidi (sɛ ɛnyɛ prɛte so). Nanso Kraman deɛ, daa na yɛbɛhunu no sɛ ɔda adibea hɔ wɔ awia hyew no mu nsõ mu. Afei nso wobɛhunu no sɛ wɔrebu no wɔ hɔ, ɔbɛteɛm sɛ "Kao! " Ne nyinaa wɔ berɛ a wɔsomaa wɔn (wɔ kawa no eguadwa yi) no akawnto so.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi ayɛ wo nokware, ne ahomegyeɛ a wubetumi akamfo me (wɔ ne asɛmka ho).
