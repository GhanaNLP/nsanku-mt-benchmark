Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ SI KWAN A ƐKA KWAKU ANANSE, ANANSE NO, BƐYƐƐ KƐSE, WƆ NE ETIRE A ƐBƐYƐƐ KƐSE NO KA SO

WƆKYERƐ SƐ, bere bi na ɛkɔm kɛseɛ baeɛ, na Kwaku Ananse kyerɛɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔpɛ nam ne nhabannuane na ɔde abɛma ɔne ne yere "Aso" adidi. Ɔkɔɔ asuo bi mu na ɔkɔhyiaa nnipa; afei nnipa yi a ɔne wɔn hyiaa no, mepa me abebuo nti, na wɔyɛ ahonhom. Bere a Kwaku Ananse hyiaa Ahonhom no, na wɔregyina nsuo no mu na wɔreprɛte nsuo-dan no mu ama apataa akyere. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Me nuanom, metumi aba abɛprɛ kakra nso anaa? " Ahonhom no kaa sɛ, "Mommra. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ hɔ, na ɔhunuu sɛ wɔde wɔn ti nnompe reprɛ asu no. Ahonhom no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, "Woahunu sɛ nea yɛfa so petepete nsuo no mu no, wobɛma kwan ma yɛayi wo tifi dompe no wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ ebia wobɛpete nso anaa? " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Mema kwan ma woayiyi ama me. " Sɛ ɛyɛ nokware a, Ahonhom no yii no maa no. Kwaku Ananse ne Ahonhom no bɔɔ mu petepete nsuo no mu mpa no mu. Bere a wɔpetepetepete no, Ahonhom no bɔɔ dwom sɛ:

"Yɛn, Ahonhom, sɛ yɛfere nsuo a ɛyɛ kusuu mu kɔyi apataa a, yɛde yɛn atitirefoɔ na yɛfere nsuo no,

Oh, ahonhom no, yɛrehyɛ nsuo no. "

Ananse no kae sɛ, "Saa dwom yi yɛ dɛ, metumi ako bi anaa? " Ahonhom no kae sɛ, "To bi. " Na ɔmaa ne nne so sɛ:

"Ahonhom no, yɛretwitwa nsuo no, yɛde yɛn atitirefoɔ rekɔtwa nsuo no.

Oh, ahonhom no, yɛrehyɛ nsuo no.

Ɔbɔadeɛ yɛɛ nneɛma no, yɛnkɔfa yɛn atitirefoɔ kɔprɛ nsuo no anaa?

Oh, Ahonhom no, yɛrehyɛ nsuo no. Mefa me etire mehyɛ nsuo a ɛyɛ dry no da o, oh, Ahonhom no, yɛrehyɛ nsuo no. "

Kwaku Ananse wiee dwom no, na Ahonhom no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Yɛapete, yɛanya apataa, wo kyɛfa yɛ kɛntɛn a ɛmu yɛ duru, fa na kɔ didi, fa wo tifi dompe, fa nka ho (wɔ wo nipadua so), na kɔ; nanso nea yɛwɔ sɛ yɛka titire ne yi, da koro a wobɛto saa dwom no biara no, wo tifi dompe bɛbue na afiri fam. Ananse no kaa sɛ, Apataa a ɛwɔ mborosoɔ a wode ama me yi nyinaa yɛ Me kɔn dɔ, na dwom deɛ, ɛyɛ sianteɛ bɛn na mede bɛto? " Ahonhom no kaa sɛ, "Ɛno yie, kɔ. " Ananse no hyɛɛ aseɛ. Ahonhom no nso boaboaa wɔn ho ano, na wɔn nso, wɔkɔeɛ. Bere a Ahonhom no duu baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ no, ɛneɛ na ɛteɛ no, ɛnneɛ na wɔmaa wɔn dwom so:

"Yɛn, Ahonhom, sɛ yɛfere nsuo a ɛyɛ kusuu mu kɔyi apataa a, yɛn ara yɛde yɛn atitirefoɔ na yɛfira nsuo no.

Oh, ahonhom no, yɛrehyɛ nsuo no. "

Ananse no tee dwom no, na ɔno nso faa so sɛ:

"Ɔbɔadeɛ no yɛɛ nneɛma firi bere a yɛde yɛn atitirefoɔ kɔtwaa nsuo no? "

Oh, ahonhom no, yɛrehyɛ nsuo no. "

Ɛnkyɛree na ɔwieeɛ no, ne tifi dompe no bueeɛ hwee aseɛ. Kwaku Ananse maa ne so kuraa ne kokoɔ mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ahonhom, Ahonhom, metire atɔ me ase. " Ahonhom no tee no, wɔkaa sɛ, "Ananse no deɛ, wantie nea yɛaka no, na ɔrebisa yɛn sɛ, yɛn nyinaa nsan nkɔ bio na yɛnte nea ɔbɛka. " Ɛkame ayɛ sɛ ntɛm ara na Ananse no peree ne ho bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Puo! Me agya mmofra, metire abue na atɔ, enti me srɛ mo, sɛ mayɛ mo opira bi a, momfa kyɛ no; mowɔ hokwan, nanso fa me etire no san kɔsi ne baabi ma me. " Ahonhom no faa no, na ɛbɛsii ananmu. Wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Seesei sɛ woto dwom yi bio a, na ɛsan bɔ, sɛ wobɔ yɛn frɛ a, yɛremmua, enti fa wo ho nni yie. " Ahonhom no hyɛɛ aseɛ bio. Wɔrekɔ no, wɔtoo wɔn dwom. Na afei na Kwaku Ananse rehyɛ aseɛ reto dwom bio, na netire pae gui, na ɔhwee aseɛ, kotoo! Na ɔgyee no so, excuse me kasafĩ ne ahohwisɛm, bɔɔ no akyi berɛmo, na ɔhuruw kɔɔ kwan nkyɛn, sora! (na ɛyɛ wira mu paepae no nnyigyei bere a ɔhyɛn mu no). Ɔkaa sɛ, Kwan, gye me, na da a ɛbɛba sɛ mɛyɛ ɔdefoɔ no, mede bi bɛma wo. Ɛno nti na wobɛhunu Kwaku Ananse a ne ti ketewaa bi ne ne ase yɛ kɛse pa ara; ne nyinaa fi ne aso denyeɛ mu.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
