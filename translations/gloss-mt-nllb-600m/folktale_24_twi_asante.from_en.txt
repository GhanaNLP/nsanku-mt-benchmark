Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐBƐYƐƐ DƐN NA ABƆFOƆ YƐ ADEƐ

ƆBƆFOƆ (Once) kɔɔ na ɔgyee tuo-medicine (powder) ne nsa adwumayɛbea ahoroɔ mmienu maa kannifoɔ sɛ otuo ano aba, wɔ ayeyi, na ɔne ne yere ne abofra ketewa kɔɔ nwura no mu wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ wɔrekum nam. Na ɔsii ɔbɔfoɔ dan bi a ɔne ne yere ne ne abofra te mu. Bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔkɔɔ nwura no ho. Berɛ a ɔhunuu aboa kɛseɛ bi na ɔde ne tuo guu mu no, ɔhwee ase. Ɛyɛɛ saa da biara, bɛyɛ nna mmienu ne awotwe. Na ne yere se, Esiane sɛ minnya nam nni nti, mefiri fie bɛkɔ. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ a ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔɔ nwura no, ne yere ne ne abofra no nso kɔɔ wɔn akuraa. Seesei na tuo baako pɛ na aka. Mprempren me werɛ firi sɛɛ sɛ me kae sɛ saa ɔbɔfoɔ no din de Gyekye, na saa ara nso na ne yere no din de Boniaye-kae-dabi. Bere a ɔbɔfoɔ no rekyinkyin no, na ɔduruu beae bi wɔ odum dua bi ase, na ɛhɔ na ɛyɛ beae fɛfɛ. Ɛhɔ na ɔbɔfoɔ no dwenee ne etire mu sɛ, Sɛ mekɔ fie a wɔbɛgye sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no ama powder no, na minnya bi, enti mɛfa saa tuo yi ato me menem mu sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na meku. Ɛhɔ ara na biribi frɛɛ no sɛ, Creators ɔbɔfoɔ. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Agya. Na biribi no kaa sɛ, Adɛn nti na waniwa abere sɛɛ? mprempren sɛ ɔno deɛ, wɔfrɛ no Go-and-spoil-the-fie, nni wo fie a, anka mema wo biribi. Ɛneɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Nneɛma nni fie. Biribi kaa sɛ, Ɛfiri sɛ fie nni hɔ deɛ, kɔ bon a ɛwɔ odum-dua no dua sini mu, na fa nnawuro mmienu da hɔ, nti fa wɔn mmra. Ɛnna ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔɔ hɔ kɔfaa wɔn bae. Na Ebi kaa sɛ, Kɔ twa dua mma mmienu ɔdwen, na to waniwa mu, na dwane fa baako si baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ, na fa baako no dan kɔsi baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ. Bere a ɔbaa no, ɔkae sɛ, Mawie. Biribi kaa sɛ, Fa dawuru kɛseɛ no na bɔ. Na ɔbɔɔ no, na nwura no bɛyɛɛ akuraa a ɛtrɛw wɔ dua mma mmienu no ntam. Na odum dua no gyinaa wɔ ne mfinimfini. Na ɛno Something yii ne ho adi sɛ ɛyɛ Ɔkɔdeɛ. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no sɛ, asɛmpɔw titiriw biara a ɛbɛba atenaeɛ no, ɛsɛ sɛ wɔtie no odum-dua no ase. ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Ho, metee. Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa saa asɛm yi kyerɛɛ no no, ɔno (ɔbɔfoɔ) nyaa yernom, mpɔtam badwafoɔ, ne asomfoɔ. Ɛduruu saa berɛ no, ne yere (a wɔfrɛ no) Some-day-the-ungrateful-one-will-remember no nyaa ne hwam sɛ wabɛyɛ ɔhene, enti ɔtɔɔ nnuadewa bobesa, de kɔɔ ɔhene no akuraa ase, na na nabofra no da nakyi. Ɛduruu hɔ no, ɔbɔɔ sianteɛ ma ɔreba no, na ɔkaa sɛ, Wo abofra no se ɔde nsa bɛba sɛ ɔmfa nhwɛ wo anim. Na ɔgyee nsa no, na ɔyɛɛ nsa a wɔde ba biara. Na ɔde ɔbaa no kɔmaa Gyaasehene sɛ ɔmfa no nkɔ ne fie. Berɛ a anwummere dwoeɛ no, ɔbaa no guareɛ kɔɔ baabi a na ɔhene no wɔ ne dan mu. Ɔbaa no ka kyerɛɛ ɔhene no sɛ, Me ne wo anya osuo ne awia, kosi da a woanya baabi atena, na anka anka ɛnyɛ yiye sɛ wobɛsrɛ me? Seesei bere a ɔrekasa yi ara sɛ mmarima ne wɔn nkwasiasɛmntumi nnya hwee nka. Ɛneɛ na ɛhɔ ara na ɔkyeree ɔbaa no nsɔmu na ɔne no daeɛ. Anɔpa kɛseɛ kyeeɛ no, oyii no firii natenaeɛ wɔ Gyaasehene nkyɛn de no kɔɔ Ɔhene atraeɛ hɔ. Ɛhɔ na ɔtenaa kɔsii sɛ akyinnyegyeɛ kɛseɛ bi baeɛ. Ɔhene no ne ne mpɔtam badwafoɔ nyinaa kɔtenaa odum-dua no ase. Ɔbaa no ba no bɛgyinaa nagya anim, na ɔhunuu akɔre ba no wɔ odum dua no atifi, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya, mepɛ. " Na nagya no bɔɔ no ase, na ɔhyɛɛ netire no akyi, na ɔmaa no kɔɔ fie. Ɛnna mmaa no ba foforɔ no mu baako baeɛ, na ɔno nso bɛgyinaa nanim. Ɔsan nso hunuu akɔre no ba no, na ɔkaa sɛ, Agya, mepɛ. Na saa kwan korɔ no ara na ɔbɔɔ no ase, na ɔhyɛɛ netire no akyi ma ɛhwee fam, na ɔrekɔ no, ɔsuuu. Na ne maame no kaa sɛ, Dɛn nti na woresu? Na ɔkae sɛ, Agya bɔɔ me. Na ne maame se, O! Sɛ ɛyɛ One-day-the-ungrateful-one-will-remember no abofra a, anka ɔremma no so. Na One-day-the-ungrateful-one-will-remember kae sɛ, Wei deɛ, menyɛɛ wo hwee sɛ woyɛ me saa. Ansa na ɔreka saa no, wɔn baanu nyinaa tuu mmirika bɔɔ mu ko. Na asomfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Yɛn agya yerenom reko wɔ saa ade pɔtee a ɛwɔ dua yi atifi nti, yɛbɛhwe ase. Ntɛm ara na wɔgyee wɔn nkuma, pinthe! pinthe! (ɛno ne nnyigyei a wɔyɛeɛ no.) Saa berɛ no na Ɔkɔdeɛ no akyinkyin akɔfa ne mmofra no de wɔn akɔhyɛ Koterɛ, ne Old-Hawk sɔboɔ mu. Ɛduruu berɛ a nnipa no twaa dua no na ɛhwe aseɛ no, Koterɛ no bɔɔ nkonyaayisɛm sɛ, "Pimpeao! " Na dua no bɔɔ mu. Ɛnna wɔsan baa mu bio. Koterɛ no ka kyerɛɛ akraman dedaw no sɛ, "Kɔ hwehwɛ Ɔkɔdeɛ sɛ ɔmmɛhwɛ ne mmofra, me deɛ, owuo aba me so. " Amonom hɔ ara na nnipa no kɔgyee wɔn atuo bɔɔ no tuo kunkum no. Na Old-Hawk de wimhyɛn kɔɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ ɔmmɛfrɛ Ɔkɔdeɛ no. Ɛkwan nyinaa a ɔrekɔ no ɔsuui sɛ:

"Namoa e! wo mma o!

Nanabaa e! wo mmofra o!"

Bere a ɔfrɛɛ sɛɛ na Ɔkɔdeɛ no antie no, ɔmaa ne nne so na ɔsuui sɛ:

"Sankur e, Anomaa E, sankur e, Anomaa, Ɔkɔdeɛ.

Sankur e, Anomaa E, sankur e, sɛ woakɔ adidi a, san ba. "

Na Ɔkɔdeɛ no tee. Na ɔbaeɛ sɛ, "Dɛn na eyi nyinaa fa ho? " Ɛnna Old-Hawk kaeɛ sɛ, "Wɔatutu dua no wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ wɔmfa wo mmofra nkɔ, na Koterɛ deɛ, wɔato ne tuo akum no. " Ntɛm ara na Ɔkɔdeɛ no sɔreee, wa (ɛno ne nnyigyei a ne ntaban no yɛe), na ɔde ne ho sii dua bi so wɔ kuro no kwanano, na ɔmaa naniwa so, na ɛyɛ nokware sɛ wɔretwitwa dua no. Ɔkaa hwee. Na afei berɛ a dua no kɔhwee aseɛ kɔeɛ no, Ɔkɔdeɛ no tuu mmirika kɔɔ hɔ, ho (ɛno ne ntaban no nnyigyei), faa ne mmofra no. Ɔfaa wɔn kɔhyɛɛ dua no so baabi a na ɔhwee aseɛ no. Na afei ɔbaa fam hɔ bɛka kyerɛɛ ne ho (lit. wɔ netire) sɛ, "Aah, mmarima te sɛɛ na wɔyɛ? " Na ɛmaa no werɛ howee. Na ɔsuuiɛ, na ɔkaa sɛ:

"Except the tuo, except the ɔwɔ".

Na ɔtwee ne nan ase, na ɛneɛ ɔnyaa atwene Bedeɛ mmienu, na ɔboroo wɔn de nkra yi kɔmaa:

"Ma abɔdeɛ a wɔwɔ ntakra nyinaa mmra, (kosi sɛ ewiem bɛyɛ tuntum, tuntum, tuntum. "

Na abɔdeɛ a wɔwɔ ntakra no nyinaa baeɛ. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn nyinaa nea asi no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ wɔne no fa kɔda Gyekye ase, na obiara a ɔnim su su no, ɔnsu. Apitie anomaa no na ɔdii kan suuiɛ sɛ:

"Ɛbɛyɛ me wu mpofirim!

Mewu wu mpofirim! "

Apatupere anomaa nteɛm sɛ:

"Mempɛ akasakasa!

Mempɛ akasakasa!

Mani nnye Kosante ba no ntama ho. "

Akɔkɔhwedɛɛ, kraman a ne nan kɔkɔɔ no, su frɛɛ sɛ:

"Owuraye hoe, hoe, oh!

Owuraye asɔ, asɔ, O!

Owuraye asɔ, asɔ, asɔ, O! " atewa ketewa no suanee sɛ:

"Doye!

Doye!

Afua Anwea ne ne kunu Ano Kofi, Sɛ abofra nante ara pɛ a, ɛno nyɛ ade pa, Kwaku Amoaten (a name).

Doye!

Doye!

Odiawuo ne nAno Mmirikisiie a ɔgyaa Ɔsono se no, Wagya no, Wagya no.

Agya Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ, sɔre na kɔ hwehwɛ kraman a ne hwene ayɛ no na woatumi ayɛ no nkwan ne anonne, (when the hunter's wife speaks thus, though)

abɔdeɛ biara (who hears her say so) nni a ɛreba dua no ase. "

Ɔpɔdee no sui sɛ:

"Kyakyakya! nkawurubi!

(Hwɛ) saa abofra kumaa no tuo tuntum tenten no, ɔde ahyɛdeɛ rehyɛ me sɛ ɔmfa ne plantain a wɔayam no nka me ho.

Ɔpatuo, Patu, su frɛɛ sɛ:

"E! Mm!

E! Mm! "

Ɔnwam, Anwam, su frɛɛ sɛ:

"(Hunter) Akuaferi-Saka a ɔyɛ kɔkɔɔ ketewa no! "

Hornbill yere ka sɛ:

"Ɔnni hɔ. "

Anwam ka sɛ:

""Aso" (ne yere), didi na yɛnni kwan. "

Na Saka bɔ ne tuo na ɔbɔ no, pa! Na ne (Hornbill) yere dwane kɔ:

"Ɔwɔ hɔ!

Ɛwɔ hɔ! "

Na aserewa ketewa no su kae sɛ:

"Obeko Asamoa, Bere a kwasea kɛkɛ no bɔ ne nsateaa bobesa no, anɔpa anɔpa nnipa kɔ akyiri kɔ akyiri! "

Aburuburo, Aburuburo no, su frɛɛ sɛ:

"Bere a na maame rewu no, ɔgyaa me wɔ nkorabata sɔboɔ mu;

Bere a Forked-branch no wui no, ɛgyaa me wɔ mfeɛ sɔboɔ a ɛyɛ abe nhaban mu;

Bere a abe nhaban mfeɛ no wuiɛ no, ɛgyaa me wɔ baa a akɔntɔn sɔboɔ mu;

Bere a baa a akɔntɔn no nyaa me no, ɛyɛɛ me basabasa, basabasa, basabasa! "

Akyenkyena anomaa nteɛm sɛ:

Ɛyɛ me, ɛyɛ me, ɛyɛ me. " Na afei na Ɔkɔdeɛ no ankasa baeɛ, na ɔsuui sɛ:

"Nkensen nkensen, abɔdeɛ a wɔwɔ ntakra bebree!

Meda Gyekye ase!

Nkensen nkensen, feathered abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ ya ya! "

(Na bere a ɔsu sɛɛ no), kuro no mu nnipa nyinaa dwane firii mu; na Gyekye anhu baabi a mmarima, odwan, mmaa, yernom, ne nkokɔ anaa biribiara akɔ. Ɔno nko ara na ɔkaa ne ho ne ne yere ne ne abofra, wɔ odum dua no dua sin ho. Na Ɔkɔdeɛ no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛfiri saa da yi kɔ so no, ɔbɔfoɔ biara rennya ahonyadeɛ da. " Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi ayɛ nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a wode bɛkamfo me (for its asɛmka).
