FOREWORD

Akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ afe yi bɛkɔ so ayɛ nea ɛyɛ den sen biara wɔ yɛn ɔman no sikasɛm abakɔsɛm mu. Te sɛ ewiase ahomegyeɛ no, Kovid-19 yarebɔne a ɛtrɛɛ wiase nyinaa bɛkaa yɛn wɔ afe 2020 nan mu baako mu, na ɛde sikasɛm dwumadie a brɛooyɛ aba, na ɛde sika a wɔnya ne sika a ɛbɛka ho a ɛbata yaredom a wɔko tia no soteɛ.

Ɛduruu berɛ a na yɛdwene sɛ, yɛn ayaresa mu kɔ anim paa firi nsunsuanso bɔne a ɔyareɛ no de baeɛ wɔ yɛn nsakraeɛ nhyehyɛeɛ no dwumadie mfitiaseɛ no, yɛde dwumadi ahodoɔ bi abɔ yɛn a berɛ atwam no, asɛe sikasɛm kɛseɛ nkonimdiɛ a yɛdii firi 2017 kɔsi 2019. Nea edi kan ne ɔko a ɛkɔɔ so wɔ Russia ne Ukraine ntam, afei sika a wɔboaboa ano akwan atitire a wɔakyɛ wɔ 2022 sika nhyehyɛeɛ no mu, na ɛde femgyinaeɛ nsian aba. Amanaman dwadibea kɛseɛ no, mpofirim ara wɔtoo mu maa yɛn berɛ a ɛdi kan wɔ mfeɛ du mu, ɔman mu sika a wɔde boa tebea no mu yɛɛ den, na boseagye boɔ kɔɔ soro, na ɛboaa ma ɛka tua a emu yɛ duru ne sika boɔ a ɛresiane ntɛmntɛm wɔ sidi no ho.

Enti, bere a amannɔne sika mmoa a wonya fi dwetiri gua so ne amannɔne sika so tew no, nnoɔma a wɔde ba man mu ho ka a kɔ soro no ama yɛn sika mfasoɔ asɛe kɛseɛ ɛnam nnoɔma a wɔde firi amannɔne ba a yɛbɛnya sɛ ɔman no akɔnnɔ a ɛnso da a ɛda adi ne ɛka a wotua ho ka a ɛrekɔ soro nti. Eyi ama yɛn nyinaa asetra boɔ a ɛyɛ den aba sɛ Aban ne ankorankoro. Fango bo a ɛrokɔ soro no ama nneɛma ne ɔsom dodow no ara boɔ anya nkɔanim, na nea ɛkorɔn koraa no, boɔ kɔ soro 40. 4% wɔ Ahinime 2022.

Wɔasɔ yɛn ahwɛ sɛ nnipa ampa, na Aban tema mo wɔ nhyɛsoɔ a ɛboro so a eyi de aba yi ho. Yɛpɛ sɛ yɛda yɛn aseda adi sɛ Aban ma yɛn kyɛfafoɔ a Mmarahyɛbadwa, Employers Associations, Adwumayɛfoɔ nkabomkuo, Ɔmanfoɔ fekuo, Nyamesom ahyehyɛdeɛ, Ghana nnwumakuo Fekuo, Ghana Aguadifoɔ

NNIANIM

1. Afe 2023 Ɔmanfoɔ Budget no yɛ mmuano a ɛkyerɛ nea Aban no tumi nyaa wɔ afe 2022 mu ne sika a wɔnya ne sika dodoɔ a ɛwɔ nhyehyɛeɛ sɛ wɔbɛboaboa ano na wɔde adi dwuma de adi nhyehyɛeɛ ne dwumadi ahorow ho dwuma. 2023 sika nhyehyɛeɛ no asɛmti ne "Sikasɛm mu timsɛm a wɔbɛsan asiesie na wɔakura mu ne Pintinnyɛ ama Nyini a ɛka obiara bom ne Mfasoɔ a wɔde ka ho.

2. Wɔ mfeɛ mu no, sikasɛm nhyehyɛe foforo biara kura nnwuma foforɔ dwumadiwura a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔhyɛ aseɛ wɔ nnwuma a ɛrekɔ so no ho ka mu. Ɛnyɛ sɛ eyi de nhyɛsoɔ ba yɛn sika nsɛm so nko, na mmom, abakɔsɛm mu no, ɛde ka a aka ntua a ɛma dwumadi ahodoɔ no ne nyinaa boɔ kɔ soro berɛ a sikatua a wɔatwentwɛn so no twetwe mfasoɔ boɔ.

3. Wɔ afe 2023 mu no, Aban de nhyehyɛeɛ foforɔ ne dwumadi ahorow bedi dwuma, nanso ɛbɛhyɛ amammerɛ a ɛne sɛ wɔbɛwie dwumadi ahorow a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw nyinaa nso nkuran.

i. Real GDP growth yɛ 4. 0 percent afe-on-afe wɔ afe 2022 fa a edi kan no mu sɛ wɔde toto 3. 9% ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu ho wɔ berɛ koro no ara mu wɔ afe 2021 mu a;

mmienu. Nneɛma bo a ɛkɔ soro titiriw no kɔɔ soro koduu ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 40. 4 wɔ Ahinime 2022 mu fi ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 37. 2 wɔ Ɛbɔ mu ne 33. 9 ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu wɔ Ɔsannaa mu, a aduan ne nea ɛnyɛ aduan bo kɔɔ soro.

mmiɛnsa. Kabimɛn kɛseɛ a ɛtɔ sin wɔ sika pɔtee gyinabea no mu ne GDP ɔha mu nkyekyem 7.4 sɛ wɔde toto GDP botaeɛ a ɛne sɛ ɔha mu nkyekyem 6. 2 no ho a.
