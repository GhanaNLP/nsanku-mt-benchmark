Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

CHILD-KWASI-GYINAMOA

NA ƐWƆ HƆ BERE BI A abofra bi a wɔfrɛ no Kwasi Gyinamoa, na ɔwɔ nuammaa baawɔtwe, na wɔn mu biako mpo nni ɔhokafoɔ, enti wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ wɔrebɛkɔ baabi akɔhwehwɛ okununom. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Me ne wo bɛkɔ. " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Yɛn ne wo nkɔ. " Wɔn mu panin no kaa sɛ, "Ma ɔnka yɛn ho, na sɛ yɛduru kwan no so yie a, na dua atwa yɛn mu a, ɔbɛtumi atwe ama yɛn. " Wɔtuu kwan, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa ne wɔn kɔɔ. Wɔbaa asubɔnten bi ho, na emu fã bi te sɛ dɔte fitaa, fã bi te sɛ dɔte kɔkɔɔ, na fã bi nso te sɛ fango tuntum. Wɔteɛɛm sɛ, "Ɛyɛ, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ na yɛatumi atwa asuwa yi? " Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa ne (magic) cow-tail switch no, bɔɔ nsuo no, na ɔmaa nsuo no mu paapaeɛ, na wɔfaa mu kɔɔ so. Wɔbaa kuro bi mu; na ɛyɛ kuro kɛseɛ pa ara. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, The kuro is dinn (kyerɛ sɛ silent save for the nnomaa su of babadua), isn no onipa nni mu? Afei Anomaa bi kasae sɛ, There is someone there in the fie, go on. Wɔkɔɔ so no, na aberewa bi wɔ hɔ a, netire da nanan so, na ɔrehwehwɛ mu ntontom. Berɛ a ɔhunuu Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, afei na ɔmaa netire so sii nananmu; ɔkaa sɛ, Me mmanana, adɛn nti na obiara mma ha, moaba ha? Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, Me nuammaa yi adi mfeɛ nwɔtwe, na wɔnni okununom a wɔne wɔn bɛware, enti wɔaka sɛ wɔbɛba abɛhwehwɛ okununom bi a wɔbɛware. Ɔpanyin-awerɛhow no kaa sɛ, Aane, ɛno nyinaa teɛ. t, me nso me wɔ mmofra baawɔtwe. Ɔbaa panyin-nanom soma ne nana sɛ ɔnkɔfrɛ ne mmofra. Wɔbaeɛ no, ɔkannifoɔ yi kaa sɛ, Eyi ne me kunu, ɛno nso kaa sɛ, Eyi ne me kunu. Bere a wɔn nyinaa wiee sɛ wɔrepae no, ɛnneɛ aberewa no kɔgyee suman bi a wɔfrɛ no dokum_, na wɔde kyekyeree ne mmofra akɔn mu. Ɛneɛ obiara ne ne yere kɔtoo nsa. Na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔeɛ sɛ ɔne aberewa no bɛhome. Ɛbɛduruu anadwo a, aberewa no bɔɔ ne se, kɔkɔɔ fann, wɔ ahyɛdeɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔkum ne mmabaawa no ka ho. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, E! e! e! Ɔaberewa no kae sɛ, Dɛn na ɛyɛ? Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, Bere a mewɔ mankasa akuraa mu, na me kɔ ahomegyeɛ nanso mentumi nna, ɛnneɛ maame twa bayere slice na ɔde gu ogya mu, na ɔde kora a ɛtokurotokuro wom kɔ petepete nsuo ma me nom. Ɔpanyin-ɔbaa nana no hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ asuwa hɔ, na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kɔɔ suman a wɔfrɛ no Doc um_ firi akwakoraa no mma no akɔn mu, na ɔde wɔn kɔtoo ne nuammaa so. Aberewa no san firii nsuo no mu bae, ɔkaa sɛ, "Mebɔɔ mmɔden sɛ mebɛpete nsuo (wɔ saa sɔneɛ no mu), nanso mempɛe. " Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, "Kɔ fa atɛkyɛ bi, na fa nhaban fa si (sɔneɛ no mu, na fa na petepete. " Aberewa no kɔɔ hɔ yɛɛ saa, na ɔde nsuo petepete mu, na na ɛyɛ yie. Ɔde bae, na oyii bayere no fii ogya no so, na ɔde maa Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa sɛ ɔmmɛdi, na ɔnom nsuo no nso. Afei ɔhwee ne ho gui kɔtoo nna. Ɔbaa-panyin-nanom no sɔreee, ɔkae sɛ, Ɛyɛ me. Ɔsɔɔ ne se, (kosi sɛ ɛhyerɛn) kɔkɔɔ, na ɔne wɔn kɔɔ dan no mu; ɔde ne nsa kyinkyin mmofra no akɔn, na nea ne kɔn so na dokum_suman no nna no, ɔde ne se no hyeeɛ. Saa kwan yi so na ɔkunkum mmofra baawɔtwe no. Na ɔnnim sɛ ɛyɛ ne mmofra a ɔkunkum wɔn no. Bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, Ɔbaa Panyin-Nanaaseɛ hyɛɛ aseɛ yie sɛ ɔbɛkɔ afuw no mu. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔkaa no kyerɛɛ ne nuammaa ma wɔdwanee. Ɔbaa Panyin-Nanaaseɛ san firii afuw mu baeɛ. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, Ɔbaa Panyin-Nanaaseɛ e! Ɔbaa Panyin-Nanaaseɛ e! Sɛ woyɛ kwasea a, anka wobɛkum wo mmofra? Me deɛ, me ne me nuammaa rekɔ. Ɔpanyin-aseɛnom kae sɛ, Ɛnyɛ nsunsuansoɔ, nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo di dwuma) no ne baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ. Ɔkɔɔ so, na Ɔpanyin-aseɛnom nso danee ne ho yɛɛ ababaawa fɛfɛ, na ɔkuraa akutu-ntama, na ɔkɔɔ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa akuraa ase. Ɔkaa sɛ, Nea ɔbɛtumi de agyan atwa gourd-rattle yi mu no, me ne no bɛware. Nnipa a na wɔwɔ akuraa hɔ no nyinaa bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ wɔbɛtwa mu nanso wɔantumi. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa twaa mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, Maware wo. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, Me deɛ, merenni wo awareɛ. Enti ne wofa no waree no. Ɔkɔɔ ne ho, nanso oyii ne aniwa mmienu no, na ɔne wɔn kɔɔ. Ɛneɛ abaayewa no kaa sɛ, "Awofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa wɔ hɔ! Metu wo aniwa abien no na me ne wɔn rekɔ. " Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, "Fa no kɔ, ɛfiri sɛ nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi dwuma) no na ɔwɔ baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ. " Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "abofra, kɔ na ma wo ho ntɔ wo na fa wo wofa aniwa san ba. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Merenkɔ. " Wɔhyɛɛ no den, na wɔhyɛɛ no den, na (awieeɛ koraa) ɔfirii hɔ. Ɛnneɛ, Na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nim sɛ Ɔbaa Dedaa no banana no yɛ enceinte_, enti ɔno nso gyee koraa bɔɔ ne yafunu mu, de ne ntoma kyekyeree no, na ɔfirii aseɛ kɔɔ Ɔbaa Dedaa no akuraa ase. Bere a ɔduruu kuro no awieeɛ no, ɔsuuu. Ɔpanyin-asɛmmea no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ asɛm? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me ne me kunu na yɛreba, na bere a ɔduruu bepɔwa no atifi no, ɔhwee aseɛ na ɛno ma naniwa mmienu firii adi hwee aseɛ. " Ɔpanyin-asɛmmea no kae sɛ, "Ɛno yie, ha na ani benkum wɔ saa ɔsɛmmɔnedifo a wasan aba wiase bio no, Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nuabarima no, ne ne nifa ani no nso wɔ. Sɛ wokɔ (baabi a wo kunu onifirani gyina) a, ɛsɛ sɛ woka sɛ, 'Maniwa huri, maniwa mu da hɔ. '" Ansa na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nsa bɛka no, ansa na ɔbɛka sɛ, "Wo wɔ hɔ, Old-woman-nanom, eyinom ne me uncle aniwa a woyii no, na me ne wɔn rekɔ. " Old-woman-nanom kaa sɛ, "Ɛno mfa ho, nea ɔnim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi dwuma) no, ɔne baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ. " Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa -Gyinamoa baa fie, na ɔde aniwa no kɔsii wɔn ananmu wɔ ne wofa no kanyane afidie hyɛbea hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Maniwa tu, maniwa mu yɛ fann," na ne wofa no aniwa abue pefee. Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, mmofra a wɔwɔ akuraa hɔ no nyinaa kɔɔ asuwa no mu bɛtoo nsuo. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Menni hohoro. " Wɔkaa sɛ, "Ebia wowɔ awotwaa dua? " Bere a wɔkaa saa no, ne nufuɔ memee, na ɔhohoroo ne ho. Ɛnkyɛree na ɔhuruw kɔɔ nsuo no mu no, na Ɔpanyin-Ahyɛnee-Nana akyere no, twom! Ɔkaa sɛ, "Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, me nsa aka wo," na ɔne no kɔɔ nakuraa. Ɛnna Ɔbaa Panyin-nanom kɔɔ bɛgyee kɛntɛn bi kataa so, na wɔgyee mmɛw-nuts, na wɔde guu ogya no so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mekɔ gyeene abusua no nkyɛn, merekɔ nnuahwamfo abusuakuo no nkyɛn, na afei mɛba abɛku Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa. " Ɔde ne ho kɔeɛ, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa boaboaa abotan ano hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔbɛbubu no, pupru! pupru! Nana a ɔyɛ ɔbaa panyin no nana bisaa no sɛ, "Dɛn na woredidi? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Tiger-nuts. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Momma me bi mma me nni. " Obuaa sɛ, "Hwɛ me. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wobɛkɔ. " Obuaa sɛ, "Dabi, merenni. " Ɔdaa no adi. " Ohui sɛ aberewa no nana rebɔ ne nsa nnua. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa nnuadewa pestle no kum Ɔpanyin-Ahyɛne no banana. Afei oyii Ɔpanyin-Ahyɛne no banana wedeɛ na ɔhyɛɛ ne ho. Ɔpanyin-asɛm no san bae; ɔkae sɛ, "Adɛn nti na mo nyinaa ho agu fĩ saa? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Makum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa; mede no ato ogya so. " Saa amaneɛbɔ yi yɛɛ dɛ ma Ɔpanyin-asɛm no, na ɔsaw de ne nne so (na ɔtoo dwom): "Aba, woa kum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, da bi wobɛyɛ obi. "

Ɔwiee aduane no, wɔtenaa nafã, na wɔdidiɛ. Ɔpanyin-asɛmma no de dede kɛseɛ petee nkwan no so (wɔ nano), fweo! fweo! Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, "Woretew nkwan (wɔ wano) a wo nua ba ti ayɛ no. " Ɔpanyin-ayaa nana no kae sɛ, "Wo kae dɛn? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Na me reka sɛ nkyene biara nni nkwan-stew yi mu. " Ɔpanyin-ayaa nana no kae sɛ, "Kɔ fa bi. " Enti ɔkɔgyee bi. Wɔdidi wieeɛ no, Ɔbaa Panyin-Nana kaa sɛ, "Mprempren a meku Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, bra ha, me nana. " Bere a ɔbaa no, ɔgyee sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ na ɔde siesiee no fɛfɛɛfɛ. Mprempren, Ɔbaa Panyin-Nana hwɛɛ nanim berɛ bi. Ɔkaa sɛ, "A! saa abofra yi anim ma mekae me sɛ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa. " Saa abofra no kaa sɛ, "Nanabea, adɛn nti, bere a wunim sɛ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa na ɔkunkum me abusuafoɔ baawɔtwe (wɔkasa saa), woyɛ me awerɛhoɔ. " Saa aberewa-nanabea no kaa sɛ, "Gyae wano. " Seesei abofra no dwoeɛ wɔ fie hɔ ara. Saa nanabea no yii mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Momma menkɔto nto mfimfini-pii no so. " Enti Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa de kɔɔ nanim. Ɔduruu mfimfini-pii no, oyii sika kɔkɔɔ agude a na aberewa no de asiesie no no nyinaa kyekyeree no. Ɛnna ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Ho hɔ, Old-woman-nanom, ho hɔ, Old-woman-nanom, me ne no, Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, mprempren mprɛnu so ni, me abɛkum wo nana, na ɛsen saa no, meafa wo sika kɔkɔɔ agude. " Old-woman-nanom buaeɛ sɛ, "Mekaeɛ sɛ wo anim te sɛ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa deɛ; afei deɛ woakɔ baabiara no, me ne wo bɛkɔ. " Ɛnna wɔn mmienu nyinaa dwanee, kiri! kiri! kiri! Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa duu nakuraa, na ɔde sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ no maa ne mogya-abusuafoɔ, na ɔkɔɔ kwan no ho, na ɔde ne ho kɔhyɛɛ ne nkyɛn, ɛna-awofoɔ nso baa hɔ. Ɛno nti, sɛ woanantew kwan so a, wobɛte kakasakakasa nnyigyei, "pe. " Ɛno ne Old-woman-grandmother a ɔretwe Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
