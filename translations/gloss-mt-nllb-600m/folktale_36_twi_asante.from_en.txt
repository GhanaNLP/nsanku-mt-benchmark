Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA KWAKU ANANSE NYAA "ASO" WƆ AWAREƐ MU

NA obarima bi te hɔ a wɔfrɛ no Akwasi-The-Jealous-One na ne yere ne "Aso", na ɔmpɛ sɛ obiara hu "Aso" anaasɛ ɔne no bɛkasa. Enti ɔkɔsii akuraa ketewa maa "Aso" bɛtenaa mu.

Na obiara nkɔ akuraa no mu da. Seesei ɔno, Akwasi-The-jealous-one no, antumi anwo mmofra. Ɛno nti, sɛ ɔne ne yere tenaa kuro mu a, na obi bɛfa no akɔ. Mprempren, ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ mmeranteɛ sɛ, Akwasi-The-jealous-one aware "Aso" berɛ tenten paa; ɔnnyinsɛnee ne ho na ɔwoo abofra, enti nea ɔbɛtumi no, ɔmfa "Aso" nkɔfa no, na sɛ ɔbɛtumi anyinsɛn ne ho a, ɔmfa no nkɔfa no (sɛ ne yere). Mmeranteɛ no nyinaa bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ wɔbɛto ne nsa agu ne so, nanso obiara antumi. Mprempren na Kwaku Ananse wɔ hɔ rehwɛ saa dwumadi yi, (and) he said, I can go to Akwasi-the-jealous-ones village. Sky-god said, Can you really do that? Kwaku Ananse said, If you will give me what I need. Sky-god said, What kind of adeɛ? Ɔkaa sɛ, Aduro ma tuo ne otuo ano aba. Na Sky-god no maa no. Na Kwaku Ananse de powder ne otuo ano aba no kɔɔ nkuraase nkumaa various, na ɔkaa sɛ, Ɔsoro-nyame no ahyɛ me sɛ memfa powder ne otuo ano aba mma mo, na monkɔ kunkum nam, na da a mebɛsan aba ha no, mebɛfa na meakɔ. Ɔkyekyɛɛ powder no ne otuo ano aba no maa nkuraase nkumaa bebree, kosii sɛ ne nyinaa baa awieeɛ. Nkuraasefoɔ no nyinaa maa no nam bi. Wɔ da bi a atwam no, Kwaku Ananse yɛɛ mmɛw ahaban akutu bi. Na ne tenten fi ha kɔboro baabi a ɛwɔ hɔ no. Kwaku Ananse de kɔɔ nkuraase nkumaa a ɔkyekyɛɛ powder ne otuo ano aba no mu sɛ ɔrekɔgye nam a wɔakum wɔn no nyinaa. Agya Ananse faa nam ne mmɛw ahaban akutu no de sii netire so na ɔde sii kwan a ɛkɔ Akwasi-The-Jealous-One mpɔtam hɔ. Bere a ɔduruu asuwa a Akwasi ne ne yere nom no mu no, ɔfa nam bi de hyɛɛ mu. Kwaku Ananse bɔɔ mmɔden de kɛntɛn a ne nam ayɛ no bae, na ɔfaa ponkɛse ano a ɛde Akwasi-The-Jealous-One abansoro dan no mu. "Aso" hunuu no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Akwasi E! Bra bɛhwɛ adeɛ a ɛreba fie ha no, ɛyɛ dɛn na ɛbɛtumi ayɛ? " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ Sky-god no na ɔrekyinkyim me, na mabrɛ na meba ha mɛda. " Akwasi-ninkunu-baako kaa sɛ, "Mete me wura akoa nka. " "Aso" ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, "Agya, wo nam bi atɔ aseɛ wɔ ponkɛse ano wɔ fie mu. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Aane, sɛ wobɛwo kraman a, ma ɔnkɔfa no na ɔnwe. " Enti "Aso" kɔgyeeɛ de maa ne kunu. Ɛnna Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Maame, si aduane bi (wɔ ogya so) ama me. " "Aso" hyɛɛ bi, na Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Maame, ɛyɛ fufuo sɛ woresi anaa eto? " "Aso" buae sɛ, "Fufuo. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɛneɛ ɛyɛ kakra dodo, kɔ gye kokukɛse bi. " "Aso" kɔgyee kɛse bi, na Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Mommra mmɛnya nam. " Na mmoa akɛseɛ aduanan na ɛwɔ akyi. Ɔkaa sɛ: "These only take and put them in, and if you had a kuku big enough, I would give you enough nam to chew and make your se fall out. " "Aso" na ɔwiee aduane no, yii (firi kuku no mu) na ɔde sii ɛpono, nsu a apete so, na ɔde sii nkyɛn (aduane no ahomegyeɛ). "Aso" faa ne kyɛfa no kɔsɔɔ no wɔ ogya no nkyɛn, na mmarima no kɔɔ tenaa ɛpono no nkyɛn. Wɔkaa wɔn ho wɔn ho nsa akyi (ie. didii aduane koro no ara). Bere a wɔredidi no nyinaa, Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "nkyene biara nni saa fufuo yi mu. " Akwasi ka kyerɛɛ "Aso" sɛ, "Momma bi mma. " Nanso Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ koraa, bere a ɔbaa no redi aduane no, woka kyerɛ no sɛ ɔnsɔre mfa nkyene mmra, wo ara kɔfa mmra. " Akwasi sɔreee, na Kwaku Ananse hwɛɛ ne kotokuo mu, yii ayamtuo duro nsiho bi guu fufuo no mu. Afei ɔfrɛɛ Akwasi sɛ, "Bra, na me ne me bi aba. " Bere a Akwasi bae no, Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "O, merenni bio, me nsa aka. " Akwasi a ɔnsusu hwee no, ɔkɔɔ so dii. Bere a wɔdidi wiei no, Akwasi kae sɛ, "Adamfo, yɛn ne wo te ha nanso yɛnnim wo din. " Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "Me din ne Kɔ soro-na-yɛ-ɔdɔ-to-"Aso". " Akwasi kae sɛ, "Metee, na wo, "Aso", woate ɔbarima yi din anaa? " Aso" buae sɛ, "Aane metee. " Akwasi sɔre sɛ ɔrekɔsiesie nnaadan a yɛmfa nyɛ hwee no mu baako, na ama obiara ho atɔ no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sɔre-up-and-make-love-to-"Aso", eyi ne wo dan, kɔ da hɔ. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Mene-Soul-washer ma Ewiem-onyankopɔn, na meda abrannaa dan a ɛbue mu; ɛfiri berɛ a maame woo me na agya woo me no, menna da wɔ pia a wɔato mu da. " Akwasi kaa sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ, ɛhe na wo bɛda? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ meda abrannaa dan a ɛbue yi mu ha, sɛ mɛyɛ saa a, ɛbɛyɛ sɛ mɛma mo ayɛ pɛ. ɔsoro-nyame, efisɛ na ɛkyerɛ sɛ meda ɔsoro-nyame abansoro dan a ɛbue mu, efisɛ menna obiara dan a ɛbue mu da (Gyame-Ɔsoro de no da), na afei mɛda mpa a wɔato mu yi anim a wugye wo ahome no. " Na ɔbarima no ayi mpa na ɔde ato hɔ ama no. Akwasi ne ne yere kɔtoo hɔ, na Kwaku Ananse nso da hɔ. Kwaku Ananse daeɛ wɔ hɔ na ɔdwirii ne ho kɔɔ nsanombea no mu. Kwaku Ananse daeɛ wɔ hɔ, na ɔfaa ne bentaa (na ɔtoo dwom):

"Akuamoa Ananse, da biara yɛbɛnya biribi da.

Kwaku Ananse, Nsia abofra, Niamey maame, Ewiem-onyankopɔn, Ɛ-da bɛn na yɛadu, da bɛn.

Ananse, Soul-washer kɔ Niamey, Ewiem-onyankopɔn, To-da I will see something.

Afei, ɔgyaee ne sɛpɛrɛwa no bɔ, na ɔde too nkyɛn, na ɔdaa ase. Ɔdaa berɛ bi, berɛ a ɔtee Akwasi-The-Jealous-One frɛ sɛ, Agya mannot a nnyigyei in mmuaeɛ (except for the nnomaa su of the babadua), dinn! Agya mannot a nnyigyei in mmuaeɛ (except for the nnomaa su of the babadua), dinn! Akwasi-ninkunu-baako rewu; aduro no agye ne so nsunsuansoɔ, nanso ɔfrɛ sɛ, Agya manno nnyigyei wɔ mmuaeɛ mu (babadua no nnomaa su nko ara), dinn! Awiei koraa no ɔkae sɛ, Rise-up-and-make-love-to-"Aso". Ananse no kae sɛ, M! M! M! Ɔne Akwasi kae sɛ, Open (the ɔpono) for me. Kwaku Ananse buee ano (ɔpono no), na Akwasi kɔɔ baabi. Ananse no sɔre kɔɔ dan no mu wɔ hɔ. Ɔse, "Aso", woante nea wo kunu kae no? Ɔbuaa sɛ, Dɛn na ɔkae? Ɔbuaa sɛ, Ɔkaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ migyina hɔ na mebɔ wo ɔdɔ. "Aso" kae sɛ, Worenni atoro. Na ɔyɛɛ no saa, na ɔkɔdaa. Anadwo no ara, Akwasi sɔreee mpɛn akron; Ananse no nso kɔɔ hɔ mpɛn akron koduu baabi a "Aso" wɔ. Bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, Kwaku Ananse fii mu. Ɛbɛyɛɛ bɛyɛ asram abien akyi bere a Aso yafunu yɛɛ kɛse no. Akwasi bisaa no sɛ, Adɛn nti na wo yafunu ayɛ sɛɛ, ebia na wo yareɛ, efiri sɛ (wunim) sɛ me a me ne wo te ha yi, metumi nwo mmofra? Aso buae sɛ, Wo werɛ fi saa ɔbarima a ɔbaa ha a woka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔnsɔre na ɔnni ɔdɔ mma "Aso" no, yie, ɔgyee me na me nyinsɛnee no. Akwasi-ninkunu-baako kae sɛ, Sɔre na ma memfa wo nkɔma no. The y kɔɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn kurow no mu. Wɔ kwan mu no, "Aso" bɔɔ awoɔ. Wɔduruu Ewiem-onyankopɔn kurow no mu, na wɔkɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ Ewiem-Onyankopɔn, na wɔkae sɛ, Wo asɛmti bi, a wosomaa no, kɔdaa me fie, na ɔfaa "Aso", na ɔnyinsɛnee ne ho. Ewiem-Onyankopɔn kae sɛ, Me adesuadeɛ nyinaa da asase so, kɔ kyerɛ nea worepɛ akyerɛ no. Wɔkɔɔ so, na Ananse no te ɔbodua bi so. "Aso" kaa sɛ, "Ɔwɔ hɔ. " Na Kwaku Ananse tuu mmirika bɛtenaa mfinimfini hɔ. Na bio "Aso" kaa sɛ, "Ɔwɔ hɔ. " Ɛneɛ Kwaku Ananse firii soro hɔ hwee aseɛ (baabi a na ɔte). Seesei saa da no na ɛyɛ Efiada. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Me, me a mehohoro Skies-god no kra, woagye wo nsa akyerɛ me, ma me ahwe ase ama me anya dɔteɛ kɔkɔɔ. Ɛhɔ ara na asomfoɔ bɛkyeree Akwasi-the-jealous-one no nsɔmu na wɔbɔɔ no odwan. Bere a Akwasi-the-jealous-one no bɔɔ odwan no wieeɛ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no sɛ, "Ɔbaa no ni, ma Kwaku Ananse nnye no. " Enti, Kwaku Ananse faa "Aso" no, nanso ne ba no deɛ, wɔkumm no, twitwaa no mu yɛɛ no nsinnsin, na wɔhwetee wɔn ho. Saa na ahoɔyaw baa abusuakuo no mu.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi ayɛ wo nokware, ne ahomegyeɛ a wubetumi akamfo me (wɔ nasɛmka ho).
