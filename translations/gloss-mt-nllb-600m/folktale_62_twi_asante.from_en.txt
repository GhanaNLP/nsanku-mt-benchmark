Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA AWAREGYAEƐ BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU

Ɔbɔfoɔ bi, a wɔfrɛ no Kwasi Gyaba, ne ne yere kɔɔ nsra kɔtenaa ahyɛdeɛ mu kɔbɔɔ abɔmmɔ. Seesei, bere a Kwasi Gyaba kɔɔ nwura mu no, ɔmfa nam biara mmaeɛ. Ɔmfa nam biara mmaeɛ da.

Ɛnneɛ, da bi a ne yere bisaa no sɛ, "Me gye di sɛ, sɛ wokum nam a, wotɔn na wodi (mfasoɔ) anaasɛ wode ma akyɛde ahodoɔ, enti ɛnnɛ da yi, sɛ wokɔ kwaeɛ na woamfa nam ammra a, merekɔ me maame baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ, efiri sɛ me werɛ ahow sɛ mɛtena nkura akɛse so, ne mpataa, ne akutu, ne nneɛma a ɛtete saa. " Saa ɔbɔfoɔ no, Kwasi Gyaba, de ne ho kɔhyɛɛ nwura no mu. Ɔhunuu ɔtwee bi a ɛretwa mu. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ, Ɔtwee, baabi a woda no, ɛhɔ na me ne wo bɛhome. Ɔtwee no kɔɔ dua bi ase kɔgyinaa hɔ. Ɔ (ɔbɔfoɔ no) maa ne tuo so, pAM! Ɔnanka bi a ɛfiri baabi a ɛfiri papayɛ mu no, ɔno nko ara na ɔnim, bɛsii Ɔtwee no so, na Daboɔ nso huri kɔɔ so. Ɔtwee no de ne ho siei, na ɔyɛɛ dinn. Ɔkaa (towards himself), A! nea mmusu a meahunu no. " Ɔnanka no kaa sɛ, "Agya Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ, bra, nea etire bi redwene no, etire foforɔ ate bi, enti, ɔbɔfoɔ, bra ha, na yɛnkɔ yɛn akuraa, na yɛnkyɛ ɔtwee yi, ɛfiri sɛ yɛrenyɛ wo opira biara. " Saa ɔbɔfoɔ no de ne tuo bɛdii wɔn akyi. Wɔkyɛɛ nam no. Ɔnanka no kaa sɛ, "Abba, me kɔn dɔ wo, enti gyae ha kɔsi da, na mema wo aduro; sesei aduro yi, bere a wode bɛhyɛ w'aso no, wobɛte nhaban, nnua, ne asubɔnten nne, ne mmoa nyinaa kasa. Enti anadwo yi kɔ da, nanso nnyae home. " Ɔnanka no kɔɔ da, ɔgyaa ahomegye no. Ɔnanka no gyee aduro hyɛɛ n'aso. Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔbɔfoɔ no tee nneɛma no nyinaa kasa ase. Ɔfirii hɔ, na ɔbaa nakuraa hɔ. Seesei, bere a ɔbaa no, ɔtee sɛ ne maame-in-law aba ne babea fie. Seesei, ne maame-in-law no ani afura wɔ ne nifa ani no mu. Ɛneɛ, ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔtenaa dan no mu. Seesei, ɔkasa ne ne benkum akyi (i. e. Me werɛ firii sɛ mekae), kae sɛ bere a ohyiaa ne maame-in-law wɔ mmara mu no, na ɔrebɔ aburoo. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no te hɔ kommyɛ mu, na nkokɔ mmienu bi a wɔwɔ hɔ no (wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ wɔrekasa) rebisa ɔfoforɔ sɛ, "Woyɛ dɛn na wutumi nya aburoo no, na sɛ me kɔgye a, ɔno deɛ, ɔpam me? " Ɔfoforɔ no buae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woannwene sɛ ɔyɛ onifura wɔ ne nifa ani no, enti sɛ wopɛ sɛ wogye aburoo a, sɛ wotwam asɛe baako a, ɔrentumi nhu wo anaa? " Kwasi Gyaba tee (eyi); Ɔde ne ho too nakyi na ɔserew, ɔtwe! Ɔtwe! Ɔtwe! Ɔtwe! Ne maame-in-law no kae sɛ, "Aah, me mmara ba, adɛn na woresere me sɛɛ? " Nanso Kwasi Gyaba de ne ho too serew hwe fam, na ɔkɔɔ so serewee. Ɛhɔ na hɔ, ne maame-in-law no de ɔsoro-hene no asɛm a wɔabara no too ne so sɛ, Kwasi Gyaba nkyerɛ ne sereɛ nea ɛde ba wɔ ɔsoro-hene no anim. Sky-god no somaa asɛnkafoɔ sɛ wɔmmɛfrɛ Kwasi Gyaba. Bere a ɔduruu kuro no awieeɛ no, ɔmaa ne dwom so sɛ:

"Mprempren mebɛka na awu, Kwasi Gyaba e!

Mebɛkasa na meku, berɛ a eyi bɛba a, ɛnsɛ sɛ wugye me werɛ. Kwasi Mpuroko Gyaba, ɔrekɔ, berɛ a eyi bɛba a, ɛnsɛ sɛ wugye me werɛ.

Ewiem-onyankopɔn mpanimfoɔ kaa sɛ, "Mo ntie ɔdeduani no dwom. " Wɔfaa Kwasi Gyaba kɔɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn anim. Ɔkyeame (kasafoɔ) no sɔre kyerɛɛ sɛ, Kwasi Gyaba, woasere wo maame-in-law ankasa anaa ɔresere? Kwasi Gyaba buae sɛ, Agya, esiane aduru bi nti, nkɛntɛnsoɔ a ɛwɔ me so yi, sɛ mepa me sereɛ sianteɛ adi a, mɛwu. Ɔsoro-anyame no kaa sɛ, Esiane sɛ woyɛ me ɔbɔfoɔ nti, meremma wo kwan sɛ wu. Ne maame-in-law kaa sɛ, Agya, ɔresere, ɔresere kɛkɛ. ing at me, so I will be contented with nothing but a nkyerɛkyerɛmu. Na kasafoɔ no kaa sɛ, Kwasi Gyaba, woate anaa? Kwasi Gyaba buae sɛ, Afei me srɛ ɛda bi, na me wu a, fa me to me mu. Ɛnkyɛree na nano gyinaa hɔ ara, na Akokɔnini ne Hɛn baeɛ; na Akokɔnini no ka kyerɛɛ Hɛn sɛ, Stand still and let me ride you. Hɛn no kaa sɛ, Dɛn, nnipa yi anim? Akokɔnini no kaa sɛ, Sɛ wonnyina hɔ a, mebɛka asɛm a wɔabara no nsunsuansoɔ a wobɛtumi atwa wo ho. Mprempren na Kwasi Gyaba ate saa. Ntɛm ara na ɔkaa sɛ, Sir, Ewiem-onyankopɔn, ma wɔn nnyae nteɛ ɛda bi, efiri sɛ mewɔ asɛm bi ka. Ewiem-onyankopɔn no kaa sɛ, Speak. Kwasi Gyaba kaa sɛ, Meka asɛm a wɔabara, sɛ me maame-in-law mmara no betumi ayi ne abofra no afiri hɔ. Mpanimfoɔ no nyinaa kaa sɛ, Ɛyɛ nokware, ɛyɛ nokware, asɛm no asa. Ɛhɔ na Ewiem-onyankopɔn no hyɛɛ mmara bi wɔ ne nsunsuansoɔ mu sɛ, obiara, wadamfo mpo, sɛ ɔhaw wo a, s peak the asɛm a wɔabara no wɔ ne so, anaasɛ sɛ wo yere na ɛyɛ a, ka asɛm a wɔabara no na awaregyaeɛ no mma no. Ɛnneɛ na ɛbaa sɛ awaregyaeɛ no baa abusuakuo no mu. Ɛyɛ Akokɔnini a yɛ copied no.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
