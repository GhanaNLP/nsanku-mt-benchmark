Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ABOSOM, NYAMONYAN NKETEWA NO, BAA ABUSUAKUO MU

Ɔbɔfoɔ bi ne ne yere tenaa kuro bi mu. Ná wɔyɛ ahiafoɔ, na wɔte hɔ ko tia sɛ wɔhɔyɛ. Ná ɔbɔfoɔ no sɔre kɔ kwaeɛ hɔwɔ ahuhudeɛ mu. Bere a ɔbaa fie kɔtoo apataa-nkonnwa wɔ asubonten no mu, na ɔde apataa ne nswim nketewa no kɔmaa ne yere. Afei na wɔwɔ hɔ, na ne yere bisaa no sɛ, Me gye di sɛ sɛ wokum nam a, wode ma, anaasɛ wotɔn na wodi (mfasoɔ) a, ɛnneɛ-da a wobɛkɔ nwura na woamfa nam biara amma no, mɛsan akɔ me akuraa. Saa ɔbɔfoɔ no hyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ kwaeɛ hɔ. Ɔanteɛanteɛanteɛanteɛanteɛanteɛ, wanhu ayamye biara aboa. Ɔkaa ne ho sɛ, Aane, mahunu mmusu. Afei, bere a ɔhwɛɛ nakyi no, ɔhuu Ɔtwee bi a ɔnam baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Wo deɛ, Ɔtwee, mebɛkum wo. Ɔɔbɔfoɔ no dii nakyi, kyiri! kyiri! kyiri! kyiri! (Ɛyɛ anan no nnyigyei). Ɔtwee no dwanee kɔɔ dua nhini a agyina pintinn ntam. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ɔtwee Tano, medwane me ba wo nkyɛn sɛ mepɛ bammɔ, na ama ɔbɔfoɔ no agyae me. Ɔɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Na me deɛ, menyim adeɛ a wɔfrɛ no Tano, fa kɔ sɛ mebɛfa wo akɔma me yere. Tano gyinaa hɔ, na ɔhyɛɛ ɔkasa no mu sɛ, Ɔbɔfoɔ, firi Ɔtwee hɔ na fa me kɔ fie, na ɛbɛyɛ wo mfasoɔ pii wɔ me mu. Ɔtwee no gyinaa hɔ, na ɔhinhim, biribiri. Ɔgyee no (Tano) de no too ne etire so. Bere a ɔde no reba no, ɔhyiaa Owuo. Ɔbisaa sɛ, "Ɔbɔfoɔ, me ɔkyeame* (spokesman) no, wode me rekɔ he? " ɔbɔfoɔ no buae sɛ, "Abba, maniwa abere, na mepɛ sɛ mede nam kɔma me yere, bere a me kɔhunuu Ɔtwee no, na bere a me bɛkum no no, ɔkae sɛ waguan ama adeɛ yi ho bammɔ, mekae sɛ minnim hwee. Saa adeɛ yi (then) said I must take it to fie, as there was (it said) much mfasoɔ in it. " Owuo bisaa Tano sɛ, "Worekɔ fie no, na wunim sɛ sɛ obi yareɛ a, mefa me mpa a wɔasɛn, na meba bɛto ne nkyɛn a, ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara de ne nsa ka, enti sɛ worekɔ fie a, nea mewɔ sɛ meka kyerɛ wo ne sɛ: sɛ obi yareɛ a, na meremmɔ wo a, mebɛbɔ yo amanneɛ u, na wobɛnya biribi akatua ama me, na saa akatua no deɛ, mogya nko ara na mepɛ, na ebia na woanya honam no. " Osoro-nyame no ka kyerɛɛ Owuo sɛ, "Grandsire, I have heard, master. " Saa ɔbɔfoɔ no de Tano kɔɔ fie, na ɔne no bae. Bere a Tano gyina hɔ no, ɔtoo dwom bi, na wɔde wɔn ho bɔɔ kuw no mu. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛdaa adi, na berɛ a obiako yareɛ no, na wɔde no akɔ hɔ akɔ Tano. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wobɛwu. " Ɛyɛ nokware, ɔwui. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wobɛwu, enti fa eyi ne eyi ba. " Berɛ a obiako faa nneɛma no, ɔno (lesser-god) kaa sɛ, "Mekɔsrɛ nkwa ama wo. " Na ɛnam sɛ wode afɔdeɛ yi kɔmaa Tano nti, wo ho yɛɛ wo nokware. Saa na yɛhunu sɛ abɔfoɔ nkumaa no wɔ adom, enti nnipa kɔ hɔ kɔgye Tano ne ne mmofra no bi.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
