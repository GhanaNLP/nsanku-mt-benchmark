ƐBƐYƐƐ DƐN NA ƆPƐTƐ TENA FI MU BERE NYINAA

NA ƐWƆ Ɔpɛtɛ BI NE Kwaakwaadabi BI, NA Kwaakwaadabi KAE SƐ, Yɛnkɔtwa twene bi nni agorɔ. Wɔtuu kwan kɔɔ nwura no ho, na wɔbaa silk-cotton dua bi so. Ɔpɛtɛ no hunuu silk-cotton dua no, ntɛm ara na ɔkaa sɛ, Eyi deɛ, ɛyɛ me deɛ, ɛfiri sɛ me deɛ, me ne ɔpanin no, ɛno nti mɛtwa kɛseɛ yi. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, Metee. Kwaakwaadabi no nso, ɔhwɛɛ ne ho, huu Cedar dua bi a ɛgyina hɔ, ɔno nso kɔtwaa no. Wɔn baanu nyinaa twaa akyene. Bere a Ɔpɛtɛ no bɔɔ ne twene no, ɛtee sɛ:

Futuro! Futuro! Me nsa aka (mine) ntɛm!

Ɔpɛtɛ Kwakye! Me nsa aka (mine) ntɛm!

Bere a Kwaakwaadabi bɔɔ ne deɛ no, na ɛte sɛ nea ɛdidi soɔ yi:

Kwaakwaadabi, Kwa! Kwa!

Kwaakwaadabi, Kwa! Kwa!

Wɔkɔɔ so bɔɔ, na ɔsoro-nyame no tee, na ɔsomaa Akrɔma no baeɛ; na Akrɔma no baeɛ, na wɔkasaa kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Berɛ a atwam! " Wɔbuaeɛ sɛ, "Ame! " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔsoro-nyame no kyerɛ sɛ ɔtee asaw-nnwom bi wɔ ha, enti ɛsɛ sɛ wode ba bɛka ho na wobɔ ama wate. " Ɔsoro-nyame no anim na abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no sii hɔ kɔɔ ɔsoro-nyame no anim, na wɔbɔɔ, bɔɔ, bɔɔ, bɔɔ, bɔɔ, bɔɔ, na wɔbɔɔ, na ɔsoro-nyame no nso tee. aid, "I have heard that it is sweet; the two nnwom nnyigyei are sweet, therefore I present you (each) with something. " Ɛneɛ Sky-god no kɔgyee nnaka mmienu na ɔde sii hɔ, ebi ɛho akyi yɛɛ fɛ paa na wohuu; filth to ma sɛe sɛɛ wɔ mu; foforo no nso, a very ugly one; sika kɔkɔɔ to ma sɛe sɛɛ wɔ mu. Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no kasaeɛ sɛ, "Mo mmienu biara mmra na monnye. " Ɔpɛtɛ no kaa sɛ, "Mene ɔpanin no, enti ma me nni kan kɔfa baako. " Berɛ a ɔkɔɔ no, ɔyii nea ɛyɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ paa no, berɛ a Kwaakwaadabi nso faa nea ɛyɛ mfɛfɛ paa no. Wɔmaa wɔn so kɔɔ wɔn akuraa so. Berɛ a wɔkɔeɛ sɛ wɔrebebue wɔn ano no, Ɔpɛtɛ no, ɔhwɛɛ ne mu sɛ atɛkyɛ fĩ. Wɔ Kwaakwaadabi no, bere a ɔhwɛɛ mu no, na sika kɔkɔɔ rehyerɛn sɛ ade biara. Ɛnkyɛree koraa na messenger bi baa hɔ bɛkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ Ɔpɛtɛ sɛ, "Merebɛka akyerɛ wo sɛ wo maame-in-law (a ɔhyɛ mmara ase) awu. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Kwaakwaadabi, ma me sikakɔkɔɔ pow baako nkyekyere me basa no na ma menkɔ ayie. " Ɔgyee sikakɔkɔɔ sin no kyekyeree no, na ɔkɔɔ ayie hɔ. Ɛduruu hɔ no, ɔkaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ ɔkɔ home, ɔtenaa ase, nanso berɛ a ɔregye ne so no, sikakɔkɔɔ sin no bubuu, na ɛyeraeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Aah, Kwaakwaadabi agyapadeɛ no ayera, na ɛbɛyɛ dɛn na mɛsan akɔ fie? " Enti, ɔhuruw kɔɔ fi no mu kɔhwehwɛɛ sikakɔkɔɔ sin no. Ɛno nti na wobɛhunu Ɔpɛtɛ no, daa na ɔreka, reka, wɔ tiafi, ɛfiri sɛ ɔhwehwɛ akɔre no akutu de aba ne nkyɛn.

Ɛno nti na Mpanimfoɔ kyerɛ sɛ, "Anibereɛ nyɛ yie. "

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi ayɛ wo nokware, ne ahomegyeɛ a wubetumi akamfo me (wɔ ne asɛmka ho).
