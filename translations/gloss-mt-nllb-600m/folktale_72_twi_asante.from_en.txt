Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐNYƐ BAABIARA (NEA YƐBƆ HU)

WƆKYERƐ SƐ, Daboɔ bi ne ne yere tenaa hɔ pɛn, na wɔwoo mmofra baanu. Sesei, saa mmofra yi maame ne wɔn agya kɔ nwura hɔ daa kɔkyere nam de ba wɔn nkyɛn. Da bi wɔn maame ne wɔn agya kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Sɛ mutete a, ɛsɛ sɛ mode nne bɔ mu sɛ, Baabi nni hɔ (a yɛsuro), biara nni hɔ (a yɛsuro). Afei wɔkɔɔ nwura no mu, na wɔgyaa wɔn mmofra hɔ. Ɛneɛ Dankwanta, Night-jar no, bɛdii hɔ, na mmofra no teɛɛ mu sɛ:

"Ɛnyɛ hwee wɔ baabiara (a yɛsuro), ɛnyɛ hwee wɔ baabiara (a yɛsuro)! "

Anadwo-jar kae sɛ, "Hwan na ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ wobɛteɛm adeɛ a ɛte sɛɛ? " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Wo agya ne wo maame na wɔkaa no. " Night-jar kae sɛ, "Wɔdi mfomsoɔ; ɛsɛ sɛ woteɛm sɛ, Mmere sesa ntɛm, mmere sesa ntɛm. " Mprempren, saa nkokoaa no de, wɔn maame ne wɔn agya san bae, na wɔtee sɛ wɔn mmofra su:

"Mmere sesa, Mmere sesa! "

Wɔbisaa no sɛ, "Hwan na ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ wotow adeɛ a ɛte sɛɛ? " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "obarima bi na ɔka kyerɛɛ yɛn. " Ɛneɛ wɔn maame ne wɔn agya kaa sɛ, "Ɛnsɛ sɛ wotow frɛ no saa, ɛsɛ sɛ wotow frɛ sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ hwee wɔ baabiara (sɛ yɛsuro), ɛnnyɛ hwee wɔ baabiara (sɛ yɛsuro). " Wɔmaa nkokoaa no nam na wɔkɔeɛ. Night-jar no san baeɛ a, na wayɛ peaw mmienu. Seesei wɔ baabi a nkokoaa no daeɛ no, nnua mmienu bi sii. Night-jar no baeɛ no, ɔbubu nnua mmienu no, na ɔde peaw no ahyɛ ne stumps mu. Ɔka kyerɛɛ nkokoaa no sɛ, Ɛsɛ sɛ wugyae su, ka sɛ, Mmere sesa ntɛm, mmere sesa ntɛm, na sɛ wo maame ne wo agya san ba a, ɛsɛ sɛ wose ɛyɛ me na meka akyerɛ wo. Night-jar no tuu kɔtenaa peaw no mu baako pɔ so. Ɛnkyɛree koraa na nkokoaa no maame ne wɔn agya san bae. Wɔkae sɛ: "Hwan na ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ to nteɛm yi? " Night-jar no buae sɛ: "Yiw. " Bere a na ɔsebo no bedu akɔfa no no, Night-jar no de wimhyɛn tu fii hɔ na Daboɔ no de ne ho bɔɔ peaw no so. Night-jar no tuu kɔɔ peaw a ɛto so mmienu no so. Bere a anka ɔsebɔ Bedeɛ bɛtu akɔkyere no no, ɔno nso de sekan bɔɔ peaw no. Night-jar no ka kyerɛɛ mmofra no sɛ, Seesei, wuhu sɛ worennya nam nni bio. Ɛno na me kyerɛeɛ bere a meka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ, mmere bɛsesa nnansa yi ara.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
