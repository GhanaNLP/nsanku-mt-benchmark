Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

KWAMENA, TIERED ANT-HILL, A ƐGYINA NHINI A AGYINA PINTINN MU NO

WƆKYERƐ SƐ NA Ɔhemmaa BI babaa wɔ hɔ pɛn, na obiara a wɔde no awareɛ no, na ɔbɛka sɛ ɔmpɛ no. Nanso (awieeɛ koraa) Ɔhene a ɔwɔ Nsaman-saman no, obiako Adu Gyamfi, baeɛ na ɔwaree saa Ɔhemmaa abofra no. Ɔhemmaa no ba no ka kyerɛɛ ne kunu no sɛ, "Me ne wo yɛbɛkɔ wo kuro no mu. " Ɔɔbarima no buae sɛ, "Ma me kɔ (first), na Adae bɛtɔ wɔ Kwasiada so no, ɛdwoada a ɛdi hɔ yi na me bɛba abɛgye wo. " Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "Awoo dabi, me ne wo yɛbɛkɔ abɔmu. " Enti wɔkɔeɛ. Wɔrekɔ no, wɔhunuu kuropɔn bi a ɛwɔ dadeɛ pono wɔ ne kwanano, na dadeɛ akyinkyin kuro no mu. Afei, wɔ kuro yi mu, sɛ wo busuani biara anwu a, na wogyina ɛpono akyi afrɛ a, anka obiara mmua wo. Afei Ɔhemmaa Ɛna ba yi deɛ, na ne busuani biara anwu da. Ɔkasa ne ne benkum akyi (i. e. Me werɛ firii sɛ mɛka sɛ) bere a Adu Gyamfi duu dadeɛ pono no, afei ɔkɔɔ ne fie; ɔne Ɔhemmaa Ɛna ba no anka asɛm biom. Ɔhemmaa no ba ne nabofra, Ɔkra, bɛkaa hɔ, na wɔgyinaa dadeɛpono no akyi, na wɔnnyaa kwan nkɔ fie. Na Ɔhemmaa no ba no maa ne nne so (na ɔtoo dwom):

"Adodowa, bue me, ɛyɛ me a mayɛ, Adodowa Kwasi Kampire, bue me, Adodowa, bue me o. "

Nanso Adodowa anka ankyerɛ no. Ɔno nso antumi anhu kwan a ɔfa so kɔ ne kuro mu. Ɛnna ɔne Ɔkra kɔɔ nhini a agyina pintinn mmienu ntam. Ɛnna Ɔhemmaa no ba no faa kɔbere datɛn bi tenaa so, na ɔfaa foforɔ kataa netire so, na Ɔkra bɛtenaa nafã. Ɛno nti, sɛ wokɔ baabi a nhini a agyina pintinn no si no, ɛhɔ na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna ba no ne nafenaa somaa Kra te; wɔadane nwansena a wɔfrɛ no "Kwamena" a wɔsɔre gyinabea wɔ gyinabea so.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
