Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

TWƐ HƆ NA WOBƐHU

NA ɔbaa bi ne nabofra te hɔ pɛn. Berɛ a ɔde no kɔeɛ no, na ɔka sɛ, "Me nkɔ. " Ne maame na na ɔse, "Twɛn hɔ na woahunu. " Daa, berɛ a ɔde no kɔeɛ no, na ɔse, "Me nkɔ. " Ɛneɛ na ne maame ka kyerɛ no sɛ, "Twɛn hɔ na woahunu. " Da bi na abofra no kae sɛ, "Da biara, maame reka me sɛ, "Twɛn hɔ na woahunu. " Ɛnnɛ yi ara na mebɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ 'me-twɛn-hwɛ' Sɛ anka mebɛhwɛ. " Enti ɔfirii aseɛ. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na akutu dua bi a ɛkura sika kɔkɔɔ a wɔabɔ no asinasin. Akutu dua no bisaa no sɛ, "Adɛn na enti a obiara mma ha da, afei woaba ha? " Ɛnna abofra no kaa sɛ, "Me ne me maame te hɔ daa," ɔkae, "twɛn hɔ na wobehu," na mekae sɛ mebɛba abɛhwehwɛ "I-will-see," na ebia mebɛhwɛ. " Pea-tree no kaa sɛ, "Me ne wɔn mu baako, nanso yɛn mu kɛse no wɔ akyiri. " Ɛnna abofra no san fii aseɛ bio. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu Long-piece-of-nnyina bi a ɔreba abɛhyia no. Long-piece-of-nnyina bisaa no sɛ, "Dɛn nti, bere a obiara nnii saa kwan yi so da no, mprempren woakɔ so? " Na abofra no bua sɛ, "Da biara, na maame reka sɛ, "Mbɛhwɛ," enti mekae sɛ meba hwehwɛ 'I-will-see' mu, sɛ ebia mɛhwɛ. " Long-piece-of-nnyina buae sɛ, "Me nso meka wɔn ho, nanso kɔ wanim. " Na abofra no fii aseɛ bio. Ɔkɔɔ so no, na ahum-hyerɛn wɔ hɔ, na ɔda kuku a abubu sin bi so, na ɔreba ne nkyɛn. Ohyiaa no. Glowing-Sparks kae sɛ, Adɛn nti, bere a obiara nnya mma ha bio no, woba ha? Na abofra no bua sɛ, Bere a na me ne maame te hɔ no, ɔka sɛ, Mɛbɛhu, enti mekae sɛ mebɛkɔ hwehwɛ I-will-see, wɔ ahyɛdeɛ sɛ metumi ahwɛ so. Glowing-Sparks kae sɛ, Me ne wɔn no mu biako, nanso kɔ wanim, efiri sɛ nea ɔsen biara wɔ hɔ. Na abofra no hyɛɛ aseɛ bio. Ɔrekɔ no, na Nkoo no de hwiirim nnyegyeeɛ reto fie bi so. Nkoo no kae sɛ, Seesei yɛbɛnya obi mogya ato yɛn fie yi so. Seesei bere a abofra no duu hɔ no, wɔkae sɛ, Adɛn nti na obiara mma ha da a, afei woaba ha? Ɔbuaa sɛ, Me ne maame te daa; ɔkaa sɛ, Twɛn hɔ na woahwɛ, enti mekae sɛ mebɛkɔ hwehwɛ me-upon-will-see, na ama meahwɛ. Ɛneɛ Nkoo no bɛkyeree abofra no, na wotwaa no ad, na wɔgyee ne mogya guu kɔbere kuku mu, na wɔgyee bi bɛtoo fie. Ɛneɛ Nkoo no tuu kwan kɔtenaa dua tenten bi so, na wɔbaa mmiako mmiako de wɔn adua ahoroɔ guu mogya no mu, na wɔn adua ahoroɔ no yɛɛ kɔkɔɔ. Nkoo no nyinaa de wɔn adua ahoroɔ guu mu, yɛɛ dwumadie no ho nkaedi ade. Ɛnneɛ, da bi, Nkoo no mu biako kae sɛ ɔrekɔdidi. Ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔhyiaa abofra no maame. Na maame no bisaa Akoo no sɛ, - Na me abofra a na me ne no te no yɛ anihafo te sɛ ade biara. Na me sɔre a, na mɛka sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔtwɛn hɔ na ɔhwɛ; ɔno, te sɛ kwasea, mepa kyɛw sɛ mede asɛmfua no di dwuma, ɔkae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ hwehwɛ I-will-see no, na ɔahwɛ no. Ɛbɛyɛ da a ɛtɔ so awotwe ni a menhunuu no, na me srɛ mo sɛ, sɛ moahu no a, momfa no mmra me nkyɛn. Akoo no kaa sɛ, O, ɔbaa yɛn akuraa hɔ, na yɛn nso yɛkumm no. Ne mogya a yɛfaeɛ de yɛɛ dwumadie no ho nkaeɛ no bi wɔ me dua yi so. Enti, sɛ wokɔ a, kɔka kyerɛ wo nnipa nyinaa sɛ, sɛ obi ka kyerɛ ɔfoforo sɛ, "Twɛn hɔ na wobɛhu," ɛnde wapa ne ho nnome.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
