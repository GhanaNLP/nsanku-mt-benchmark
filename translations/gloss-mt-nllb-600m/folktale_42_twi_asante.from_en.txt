Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐBƐYƐƐ DƐN NA OSEBO, DABOƆ, NE ADOWA, ADOWA NO BƐYƐƐ NNAMFOƆ

Na ƆKUROTWIAMANSA (He-who-holds-the-man-in-ehu) te hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ hwehwɛ obi a ɔbɛsɔre ama no (na ɔnsuro sɛ ɔbɛka akyerɛ nanim). Wɔ mmoa no nyinaa mu no, baako mpo antumi anyɛ saa. Mprempren, na adowa ketewa no te hɔ, ɔkae sɛ, "Awofoɔ Daboɔ, ɛdɛn na woka fa me ho, na woreyɛ me atamfoɔ a wɔabue ano? " Osebo, Daboɔ no, kae sɛ, "Ɛno ara na mepɛ. " Enti, wɔbɛyɛɛ atamfoɔ a wɔabue ano. Daboɔ no de mmɛw bobesa kɔtoo mu. Adowa no kɔeɛ, ɔnom mmɛw bobesa no, na ɔnom nsa, wantumi annyane, na ɔdaa mmɛw dua no ase. Wɔ anwummere nwunu no mu, Daboɔ no kaa sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwɛ nagyapadeɛ. Bere a ɔkɔɔ no, na Adowa no anom ne nsa ada hɔ asanom. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Aane, wo na woka sɛ me ne wo bɛyɛ atamfo a wɔda adi pefee; adɛn nti, sɛ Esono, Ɛsono no mpo, antumi anyɛ me ɔtamfo a ɔda adi pefee a, akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ wowɔ ɔhaw mu. " Daboɔ no kɔɔ hɔ kɔtwee mmɛw ahaban, na ɔyɛɛ kɛntɛn a ɛwɔ mmɛw ahaban, de Adowa no guu mu, na ɔfaa no de no too ne etire so. Bere a ɔreba no, na ɛbɛyɛ sɛ ɔduruu nafã no, Adowa no mu yɛɛ no den. Ɔhunuu sɛ Daboɔ no reto no. Adowa no maa nani so, na ɔhunuu homa bi a ɛrekyekyere wɔ hɔ. Adowa no faa ne mu nsɔmu. Seesei na ɔkasa no ne ne nneɛma a yɛagyae mu (i.e. Me werɛ firii sɛ mɛka asɛm bi) no, na Adowa no mmoa bi wɔ nnua kɛntɛn no mu. Daboɔ no nnim hwee a ɛrekɔ so, na ɔde no akɔkura (sɛnea na ɔdwene) fie. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, Saa nkwaseafoɔ panyin no, Adowa no, ɔkae sɛ ɔne me bɛyɛ atamfo a wɔagye atom, enti mede no aba; fa mmɛw-mu-teɛ gu ogya so. Daboɔ maame no ka kyerɛɛ ne abofra sɛ, Palm-nut soup no ayɛ ready, so fa no ba me kunkum no. Berɛ a Daboɔ no hwɛɛ kɛntɛn no mu no, na emu da mpan. Na wafi hɔ, na nea aka ne ne mmoa bin. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Maame, wakɔ, na ne mmoa bin no aka; yɛnni no mfa wɔn nkɔto nkwan mu. " Enti wɔde wɔn too nkwan no mu, na wɔwiee aduannoa no nyinaa; na wɔtenaa ase. Abofra no de ne nsateaa no too nkwan no mu, na ɔyam. Ɔkaa sɛ, "O, Kwadwo (ɛfiri sɛ na Daboɔ awoda yɛ Ɛdwoada), na eyi ne ne mmoa pɛ, sɛ anka ne honam anka dɛn, anka ɛbɛyɛ dɛ sen akraman-nuts, enti baabiara a ɔbɛtumi ada, bɔ mmɔden fa no ba. " Daboɔ no de ne ho kɔhyɛɛ ne mmɛw dua no so. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na Adowa no da hɔ a wabow nsa bio. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Aane, nea edi kan a meyɛɛ me ho kwasea no, sesei mebɛkɔ fie akɔfa adaka ne san ba. " Daboɔ no firii fie, faa ne ntaadeɛ nyinaa (a ɛfiri nadaka mu) kɔtoo hɔ, na ɔde adaka ne safeɛ kɔɔ mmɛw dua no so. Ɔfaa Adowa no de kɔtoo adaka no mu, na ɔde too mu, na ɔgyee faa so, na ɔsoaa ne etire so. Bere a ɔduruu fie no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Maame, me de no aba yi; bue dampon no kwan na metumi de no akɔ mu. " Ababaawa no buee no, na ɔde no kɔɔ mu. Ɛnna Daboɔ ka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, Mekɔ Tall-grass-land kɔhwehwɛ gyeene, ne nkyene, ne akakaduro yamye, na yɛmfa mmra, na yɛmfa Adowa no ayɛ no stew a yɛbɛdi. Daboɔ no hyɛɛ nakwantu no ase. Ade kyee anɔpa, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, na ɔbaa kumaa no rebɔ ne nsam nnua, na berɛ a Adowa de naniwa too ɛtokuro bi so (wɔ ɔpono mu) no, ɔhunuu aberewa no. Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, "Old-woman-nanom, you are to be pitied; if you would open for me, I would do the nteɛm for you. " Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, "Wo, Ɔtwee, wobɛdwane. " Ɔtwee no buae sɛ, "May (the lesser-gods) Aserampon, ne Ta' Kese, ne Dwomo, ne Apeya, ne Konkroma, ne Ta' Kora, ne Krakye Dente, kill me, if I should run when you open for me. " Sɛ wopɛ mpo a, to apono no nyinaa mu. " Ɔbaa aberewa no kae sɛ, "Meteame. " Ɔtoo apono no mu, na ɔbuee (ɔpono a ɛwɔ dan a Ɔtwee no wɔ mu no). Ɔtwee no baeɛ, ɔkuraa pestle no, na ɔbɔɔ palm-nuts no. Ɔbaa kumaa no reyɛ akyerɛ ne ho ase sɛ ɔrekɔfa biribi afiri fam, berɛ a Ɔtwee no faa pestle no bɔɔ netire no akyi, pe! na ɔwuiɛ. Ɔtwee no yii ne wedeɛ no, na ɔde akata ne ho so, na ɔtwaa ne honam so de hyɛɛ ogya no so. Ɛnkyɛree koraa na Kwadwo no baeɛ. Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woatwe akyɛ sɛɛ? Mebuee ɔpono no maa Ɔtwee no kunkum no, ha na ne honam wɔ ogya no so. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Good done, Apeafo. " Ɔno nso faa ne nneɛma a ɔde aba no, de hyɛɛ soup-stew no mu. Ná wɔawie aduannoa no, na wɔkɔtenaa hɔ adidi no, na Daboɔ no faa dompe bi. Bere a ɔbɔɔ no, na ɛyɛ pu! pu! Ɔtwee no bɔɔ nano wɔ ɛhwene mu sɛ, "Ɛno ne wo maame nnompe a worehwete yi. " Daboɔ no kae sɛ, "Wo ka dɛn? " Ɔtwee no buae sɛ, "Mepenee so sɛ Ɔtwee yi abɔ akwakoraa dodo; ne nnompe no ayɛ den. " Daboɔ no kae sɛ, "Ɛno teɛ, me mpo me ntumi mmu no. " Seesei Daboɔ no de stew no bɔɔ nano, na ɔbɔɔ dede sɛ fEAO! fEAO! Ɔtwee no kae sɛ, "Ah, ɛno ne soup-stew a wo maame ti ayɛ a worehwie agu (wɔ wano). " Daboɔ no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na woka? " Ɔtwee no buae sɛ, "Na mekae sɛ, ne mmoa bin koraa (sɛnea na yɛdwene sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ) no, woahunu ne honam no dɛ anaa? " Daboɔ no kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dodo, dodo, ɛho nhia sɛ yɛbɛka mpo. " Seesei, na wɔadi awie. . Ɔtwee no dwirii fie hɔ, ɔka kyerɛɛ Daboɔ no sɛ, "Merebɛto mpopaho no agu mfinimfini-paeɛ no so. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ, ɛnneɛ, me ne no yɛ dɛn, meyɛ ɔbaa? " Ɔtwee no bɛduruu mfinimfini-paeɛ no so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ho hɔ, Daboɔ! Ho hɔ, Daboɔ! Sɛ woannye kwasea, sɛ woannye aboa kɛseɛ a, anka mɛkum wo maame na mayɛ no soup-stew ama woadidi? " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Mɛbɛhu wo (a meda so gyina hɔ, sɛ meba wo nkyɛn) anaa? " Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, "Wo bɛtwa me ho mpo. " Daboɔ no tuu mmirika, yiridi! yiridi! (Na ɛyɛ nanan nnyigyei), na Ɔtwee no nso, ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ tuu mmirika, prada! prada! prada! Ɔtwee no kɔduruu ne fie, na ɛyera kɔɔ ne dan mu. Bere a na ɔsii fie no, na ɔnnyaa kwan biara mu, ne ade a ɛda nkyɛn ne kwan baako a na ɔasi no nyinaa. Ɔtwee no hyɛn mu no, ɔtoo napon mu, bam! Seesei, hwɛ sɛnea Daboɔ no bɛyɛ no, na ɔnhunu kwan a na Ɔtwee no atwa mu akɔ no koraa. Daboɔ no kɔɔ so frɛɛ mmoa no nyinaa kae sɛ, "Mommra, na yɛnkɔ nkyere Ɔtwee no mma me, efisɛ ɔyɛ me kwasea araa ma merentumi nka amaneɛbɔ no nyinaa nkyerɛ mo kosi sɛ mobɛkyere no ama me. " Ɔtwee no mmoa no nyinaa baa hɔ kɔsii Ɔtwee fie. Ɔtwee no tee sɛ mmoa no aba ne fie hɔ abɛkyere no, ɔfa mako kɔkɔɔ, nkyene, ne gyeene, bɔɔ wɔn kɔn mu, na ɔde guu hɔ. Seesei na mmoa no nyinaa nnyina ho. Eyi hwɛɛ eyi mu wɔ eyi anim. Ɛnna Okusie, Okisi no, kae sɛ, Ɔkwakuo bi wɔ hɔ, Kwaadu no, na ɔwɔ dua tenten, enti momma no nni kan nkɔ soro, na yɛmfa ne dua no nkɔwura fie hɔ. Ɔkwakuo, Kwaadu no, sii hɔ, ɔkra! ɔkra! ɔkra! na ɔkɔɔ soro. Ɔtenaa fie no atifi, nanso ankyɛ na ɔbɔɔ ne aniwa hwehwɛɛ Ɔtwee no, ansa na Ɔtwee no faa ɛmo a na ɔde akisi no de hyɛɛ nano, na ɔtwee guu Kwaadu aniwa no mu. Ɛneɛ Kwaadu no gyaee hɔ hwee aseɛ. Mmoa no baeɛ na ɔhunuu sɛ biribi wɔ naniwa mu. Obi kaa bi, na ɔyam ne nsateaa ahodoɔ no. Ɔkaa sɛ, Oh, friends, come (and see) what Ɔkwakuo no akɔfa abɛba. Saa mmoa no nyinaa bae. Wɔdɔɔ, na wɔdɔɔ, na wɔdɔɔ. Ɛno nti na Ɔkwakuo, Kwaadu no, ani abere paa. Seesei wɔkaa sɛ, Kokotee (Bush-pig), wo deɛ, daa na woretu, nuntun! nuntun! na ama woatumi akɔ fie hɔ. Ɔtwee no tee saa asɛm no, ɔkɔtwee ne Sekkan no, de ano a ɛyɛ nnam paa sii hɔ. Bush-pig no tuu na ɔtwee, na ne aboa hwene puee wɔ fie hɔ. Ɛnna Ɔtwee no faa ne Sekkan no, de too Bush-pig no ano, na ɔretwitwa. Bush-pig no kae sɛ, O, Bush-pig Asamoa, ɔretwa mano. Saa mmoa a na wɔgyina nakyi (na wɔante nea ɔreka no pefee) no kae sɛ, Yɛyɛ yie, Apeafo; mia waniwa so, na fa no ba abɔnten. Kokotee, Bush-pig no, twee netire, na ne ano ayɛ no mogya. Bere a mmoa no hunuu sɛ Kokotee Asamoa, Bush-pig no, ano atwa no, wɔfirii baabi no, na yii! (na ɛyɛ wɔn nnipadɔm guanguan no nnyigyei). Saa na ɛmaa Kokotee Asamoa no nyaa hwene kumpɔ. Mprempren, bere a na mmoadoma no adwane no, Osebo, Daboɔ no, anhu nea ɔbɛtumi ayɛ. Ɔpaee mu kae sɛ, Ahow, I will go nowhere else; here I remain forever, until I die, unless I kill Adowa the Ɔtwee. Bere a Adowa tee saa no, na ɔde ne maame wedeɛ no akyekyere ne nipadua mu, na ɔde ne ho foro fie no kɔɔ nakyi. Afei ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔtoo Daboɔ no. Ɔkaa sɛ, O, Kwadwo, dɛn na ama wo ho ayɛ wo hare sɛɛ? Dɛn nti na wode wo ho bɔ Ɔtwee ho ahokyere? Momma yɛnkɔ fie na yɛmfa yɛn baabi nsi ahyɛdeɛ, na yɛmmra. " Wɔhyɛɛ kwan sɛ wɔbɛkɔ fie. Ɔtwee no ka kyerɛɛ Daboɔ no sɛ, "Firi wo agyapadeɛ no nyinaa mma me, na mɛkora so ama wo. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Ɛno nyɛ adwene bɔne. " Ɛneɛ ɔyii ne nneɛma no nyinaa maa Ɔtwee. Ɔtwee no amfa ne nsa anka wɔn so, na ɔde wɔn kɔe, gye sɛ ɔkɔgyinaa baabi. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ho wɔ hɔ, Daboɔ! " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Aane, me nua, yiw! " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sɛ na wo nyɛ kwasea, na wo nyɛ aboa kɛseɛ a, anka mɛtumi akum wo maame na mayɛ no soup stew ama woadi? Mprempren bio, mahunahuna wo de wo agyapadeɛ nyinaa akɔ. Daboɔ no kae sɛ, Mɛbɛhyia wo hɔ anaa (sɛ meba a)? Ɔtwee no kae sɛ, Wobɛfa me mpo. Ɛneɛ Daboɔ no tuu (n'akyi), kiri! kiri! kiri! Ɔtwee no nso twaa mu. Ɔkɔɔ kɔɔ ne fie mu kɔtoo ɔpono no, bam! Daboɔ no gyinaa hɔ, ne kokoɔ no amene; na ne dua no dwirii ne ho fii nkyɛn kɔɔ nkyɛn (deɛ ɛyɛɛ sɛ) frɛ! frɛ! frɛ! Na ɔnnya asɛmfua nka. Wɔ tenten mu no ɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔtwee no sɛ, "Meka Yawoada akyiwadeɛ no, sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ woba, na yɛbɛyɛ nnamfofoɔ. " Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, "Merenkɔ, dodoɔ a wopɛ, gye sɛ wosiesie ma yɛnom anyame no. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Mepene so, ɛnneɛ merentumi nyɛ wo hwee da. " Ɔtwee no baeɛ sɛ ɔbɛnom anyame no de Daboɔ no. Ɔtwee no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Nkyere me da; sɛ wuhyia me a, na sɛ wɔkyere me wɔ fidie mu a, gyae me. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Mete aseɛ. " Na Daboɔ no nso kaa sɛ, "Ɛwɔ kwan a wobɛfa so no, mefa me nsa mpo, na mepae so, na ama woahu sɛ me ne wo nni nea ɛde ba manso biara ntam. " Ɛno nti, Daboɔ biara wɔ hɔ a, ɔnkyere. Ɔtwee no. Afei nso, sɛ fidie bi akura Ɔtwee no a, ɔno (Daboɔ) kɔgye no.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
