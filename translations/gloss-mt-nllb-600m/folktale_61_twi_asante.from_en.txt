Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ SƐ OBI KƆ DWADIE BI MU ANADWO NO, ƐYƐ DUNKURU

WƆKYERƐ SƐ Kuntunkurunku, Akwanya, NE Kuntunamoa, Awieeɛ, firii silin no mu hwee fam. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Old-mother-earth, you are (too) flat. " Asase Yaa kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wose me ho yɛ (too) flat? " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Esiane sɛ woantutu ɛtokuro bi amma yɛn nna mu nti. " Old-mother-earth danee nani kyerɛɛ Prae, Mpopaho, kae sɛ, "Wo ano akata nkokɔ mmoa so. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wobɔ me ayayadeɛ? " Old-mother-earth kaa sɛ, "Sɛ mayɛ wo ayayadeɛ a, mayɛ wo ayayadeɛ; sɛ woanhyɛ me ayayadeɛ a, Te sianteɛ no ase, gyae bisa. " Mpopaho no danee nani kyerɛɛ Abena, dua hono, fa a ɛteateaa, na ɔkae sɛ, "Wo deɛ, fa nneɛma kɔto hɔ. " Fa a ɛteateaa dua hono kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wohyɛ me ayayade? " Mpopaho kae sɛ, "(Anyhow) me ayɛ saa. " Fa a ɛteateaa dua hono nso de ne nsa kyerɛɛ Sumina, Midden-heap, sɛ, "Kɔ so kura nea wunya no. " Midden-heap kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wohyɛ me ayayade? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wohyɛ me ayayade? " (Fa a ɛteateaa dua hono) r eplied, "I have abused you, and if you do not know the sianteɛ, then stop asking. " Midden-heap, too, addressed the Abe, palm-tree, saying, "Your kɔn is all overgrown with nwura. " Palm-tree said, "Why are you abusing me? " Midden-heap answered, "I have abused you, and if you do not know the sianteɛ why, then stop asking. " Palm-tree nso, ɔdanee nani kyerɛɛ Tetea, Little-black-ant, na ɔkae sɛ, "Wo aso abere. " ck. "Dɛn nti na woayɛ me ayayade? " Na palm-tree no buae sɛ, "Makyɛ wo ayayade, na sɛ wonnim sianteɛ a, gyae bisa. " Little-black-ant no nso, ɔne Esono, Ɛsono a ɛwɔ hɔ no, kɔɔ ne nan so, na ɔtoo Ɛsono no. Bere a Ɛsono no rema ne nan so no, afei ɔhyɛɛ Tamiriwa black-snaker no aniwa ase pɛɛ, na Snail tuntum no aniwa fii ase nsuo, na ɔpopaa nsuo no wɔ fan nhahan bi a ɛbɛn hɔ no so. (Mprempren), aduane a Ɔpanyin-Ahyɛna didi ne fan dua, na Tamiriwa, Tamiriwa, the Black-Snaker, popaa nsuo no fii naniwa mu. 'Ɔkraman, Kraman no, na ɔretwam, na ɔfrɛɛ Kraman no faa nnyina no maa Kraman no de too ne etire so. Kraman rekɔ no, ɔnyaa kola-nut, na ɔkyɛɛ baako mu mmienu, na ɔtoo baako guu Okagya kɔnɔnɔ no ano mu. Okagya nso faa kola-nut no de bɔɔ Onina, silk-cotton-treet. Silk-cotton dua no nso de abufuhyeɛ memee ne nufuɔ. Ɔhunuu sɛ abofra ketewa bi retwa mu, na ɔtwaa ne baa no kɔtoo no kum no. Seesei, saa abofra ketewa no ne Nyankopɔn, Ewiem-onyankopɔn, abofra. Ɛhɔ na afei wɔfaa silk-cotton dua no de kɔɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn anim. Wɔbisaa no (Silk-cotton-tree no), na wɔse, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ ne nyinaa? " Silk-cotton-tree no kae sɛ, "Na me gyina hɔ wɔ mankasa baabi, bere a Opuro yi (ne ba a ɔyɛ (odare) sɛ ɔbɛma kola-nut so na ɔde abɔ me no, saa ara na me nso, bere a abofra yi retwam no, medwene sɛ ɔyɛ Okagya, Opuro no, na meyii me nkorabata baako bɔɔ no, na hwɛ, na ɛyɛ wo abofra. " Niamey, Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no, ɔkae sɛ, "Monkyekyere no wɔ dunsin mu, na monfrɛ Okagya, Opuro, sɛ ɔmmra. " Okagya baeɛ; ɔkae sɛ, "Sir, wunim sɛ makyiwadeɛ ne kola-nut no, nanso Ɔkraman, Kraman, kɔgyee baako, na ɔtoo no wɔ kwanano kɔsii ɛtokuro no ho, na me a me tow no, ɛkɔɔ kɔtoo Oldman-silk-cotton-tree no. " Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Monkyekyere no wɔ dunsin mu, na monfrɛ Kraman sɛ wɔmmra. " T. oye kɔfrɛɛ Kraman no sɛ wɔmmra. Kraman no kae sɛ, "Sir, na menam mankasa me baabiseisei woahunu sɛnea me ho yɛ me dɛwbere a Ɔpanyin-Ayi nana faa ne nnyina babaso no de too me etire so no; sesei Okagya, Opuro no, na ɛyɛ ɔno na ɔda ne ɛtokuro mu wɔ afuo a Ɔpanyin-Ayi nana ayɛ no ahyɛdeɛ sɛ ɔbɛnya biribi adi, nanso me na Ɔpanyin-Ayi nana-Ayi na ɔma no soa ne nnyina; me nso, mefaa kola-nut a nd guu kwanano kɔɔ ɛtokuro no mu. "Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Mfa no, monkyekyere no wɔ dunsin mu, na monfrɛ Ɔpanyin-Ahyɛnee sɛ ɔmmra. " Ɔpanyin-Ahyɛnee baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ: "Ah, me mmanana, monhwɛ me ano, me a mentumi nni nam no, fan nhaban na me di; ɛsan nso, mehoro Bosompra; me akyiwadeɛ ne (nneɛ) Tamiriwa, Black-Snakes no; Kraman nko ara na na onim yi, na ɔkɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ Tamiriwa, na ɔmaa no sɛ ɔmmɛgye nsuo a ɛfiri nano, na ɔmfa nhyɛ fan nhaban a me te so yi mu. Ɛno nti na mede me nnyina maa no sɛ ɔmmɛsoa. " Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "O, fa no nkyere no dunsin mu, na kɔfa Tamiriwa, Black-Snakel no mmra. " Black-Snakel no baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sir, na me nko ara me baabi no, hwɛ, asono kɛseɛ bi bɛbɔɔ baabi a ɛwɔ maniwa ase pɛɛ, na me kɔfa efan (leaf) bɛpopaeɛ. Ɛno nko ara na menim fa ho. " Sky-god no kaa sɛ, "Monkyekyere no wɔ dunsin mu, na monyi Esono, Ɛsono no mmra. " Wɔkɔɔ kɔgyee Esono. Ɛsono no kaa sɛ, "Nna mmiɛnsa bi ni na me te hɔ, na me wedeɛ no hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɛrehye me, na berɛ a mehwɛɛ baabi a "excuse me for using the asɛmfua" (me wedeɛ) a Little-black-ant no de ahyɛ ne ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ ɔbɛhwehwɛ nam a ɔbɛwe, na ama no anyin ayɛ kɛseɛ no, ɛkɔhyɛɛ Tamiriwa, Black-Snel no so. " Ɔsoro-anyame no kaa sɛ, "Fa no nkɔkyekyere no dunsin mu, na fa Tetea, the Little-black-ant. " Tetea baeɛ no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Sir, there is a palm tree; when it bears, its nuts are first black; when I, the Little-black-ant, walk over them, it makes them ripen. " Ɛda a ɛtwaa toɔ no, berɛ a mmɛw-dua no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ maso yɛ tuntum, na me nkumaa nti, medwene sɛ mebɛkɔ Ɛsono hɔ, akɔhwehwɛ nam a mɛdi na ama mayɛ kɛseɛ. Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Fa no nkɔkyekyere no dunsin mu, na kɔfrɛ mmɛw-dua no mma mmra. Abe, mmɛw-dua no, baeɛ; ɔkaa sɛ, Sir, eyi ne nea mewɔ sɛ meka: Me ne Sumina, mfimfini-twetwe no, yɛte aseɛ t ogether, na bere a nwura a wɔtow gu no so no dɔɔso dodo, na ontumi nnyina ano bio no, migye bi kyekyere me kɔn mu; nanso afei deɛ, ɔhyɛɛ me ahohora sɛ mehyɛɛ nwura wɔ me kɔn mu. Ɛno nti, me deɛ, me aniwa bɛyɛɛ me kɔkɔɔ, na me nso meyɛɛ Little-black-ant no ayayadeɛ. Sky-god no kae sɛ, Fa no nkyere no dunsin mu, na fa Sumina, Middle-heap no, ma ɔmmra. Middle-heap no bae; ɔkae sɛ, Sir, Abena, Fa a ɛteateaa-of-dua hono, a sɛ wɔyiyi nwura wɔ dan mu a, wɔma no sɛ ɔmmra dane mu mma me. Ɛda a ɛtwaa toɔ no, a na ɔnni nea ɛde ba biara wɔ mfomsoɔ ho no, ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, Wo de nea wogye no sie (i. e. yɛ greedy); mekae sɛ, a, na sɛnea Palm-dua no bɛn no, me nso meyɛɛ no bɔne. Sky-god no kae sɛ, Fa no nkyere no dunsin mu, na fa Abena, Fa a ɛteateaa-of-dua hono, bra. Fa a ɛteateaa-of-dua hono bae; ɔkae sɛ, Sir, bere a Prae, Mpopaho no, swee dan bi mu, sɛ wɔmfa mma me (ne mpopaho), bere a mframa bɔ no, nwura no nyinaa san ba bio; sesei, ɛnora, ɛwom sɛ mannyɛ no hwee, nanso ɔhyɛɛ no anuonyam. kae sɛ, Wofa nneɛma kɔtow gu nko ara. Ɛno nti, me nso me aniwa abere, na me kɔhyɛɛ Sumina, Midden-heap no ayayade. Sky-god no kae sɛ, Fa no, na fa no sie dunsin mu, na fa Prae, Mpopaho no, mmra ha. Mpopaho no bae; ɔkae sɛ, Agya, Old-mother-earth da hɔ a ɛyɛ flat so, nani ho yɛ no dɔte, enti bere biara a me sɔre a, me dwiri me fi a ɛwɔ ne so nyinaa ma ɔyɛ autiful; yet the other day, Mother-earth addressed me, saying, Thou ano is (full) of anomaas mmoa, na me nufuɔ nso, ɛyɛɛ me dɛw, na me kɔbɔɔ Abena, Fa a ɛteateaa-of-dua honor no ayayadeɛ. Sky-god no kaa sɛ, Fa no nkɔkyekyere no dunsin mu, na frɛ Mother-earth sɛ ɔmmra. Bere a Mother-earth baeɛ no, ɔkaa sɛ, Sir, ɛhɔ na me da; wɔnam me so; me nka adeɛ da; wɔyɛ ahodoɔ nyinaa. s me so, nanso me nka hwee da. Sesei da a ɛtwaa toɔ a na me wɔ hɔ no, Kuntunkurunku ne Kuntunamoa, Akwanya ne Awieeɛ, bɛhwee me so, na mekae sɛ, O! Ɛdɛn na ɛyɛ asɛm? na wɔkaa sɛ, Woankɔtu ɛtokuro bi a yɛbɛda mu, na wo ne wo tratrayɛ nyinaa nyɛ dwumadie biara. Ɛno nti, me nso mehyɛɛ Prae, Mpopaho, ayayadeɛ. Ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Fa no nkɔkyekyere no dunsin mu, na kɔgye Akwanya ne Awieeɛ. Akwanya ne Awieeɛ no baeɛ. Wɔkaa sɛ, Sir, na yɛwɔ silin no so, na yɛredi agoru, na yɛhwe aseɛ na yɛapira yɛn ho; ɛno nti na yɛdii Mother-earth ho fɛw sɛ ɔyɛ flat. Sky-god no kaa sɛ, Councillors, yɛate saa asɛm yi nyinaa, nti momma yɛnsɔre nkɔ dan no mu na yɛnsusuw adanseɛ no ho na yɛmmɛdi atɛmmuo. Enti wɔsɔre kɔɔ dan bi mu, na wɔtoo ɔpono no mu. Ɛno nti, sɛ wokɔ dan mu anadwo a,sum kabiiEwiem-onyankopɔn ne ne Mpɔtam badwafoɔ rebɔ nkɔmmɔ wɔ hɔ.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
