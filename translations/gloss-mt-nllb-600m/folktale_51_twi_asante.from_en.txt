ƐBƐYƐƐ DƐN SƐ ATURUKUKU, TURTLE-DOVE NO, KƆƆ NWURA NO MU, NA ƆMAA AKƆKƆ, HƐN NO, BAA FIE NO

AKƆKƆ, Hɛn, ne Aturukuku, akraman-dove no, firii agya baako mu na efiri maame baako mu. Mprempren Hɛn ka kyerɛɛ akraman-dove no sɛ, Kɔ Tall-grass-country, na me nso mebɛkɔ Thick-forest-country, na woanya aduane a, ɛnneɛ wobɛba abɛka akyerɛ me. Ɛneɛ wɔtetew mu. Aturukuku mfeɛ mmiɛnsa a ɔnhunuu ne nuabea. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne kunu no sɛ, Fa me, ɛfiri sɛ merekɔ hwehwɛ me nuabea. Ɛnna ne kunu no kaa sɛ, Sɛ woaka a, mate, kɔ, na ma me nka wo ho. Ɛnna ɔne no kɔduruu kuro bi kuro akyi. Wɔhunuu atɛkyɛ pɔsɔ bepɔw ketewa bi wɔ hɔ, na ɛhɔ na ɔne ne nuabea no hyiaa. Hɛn bisaa akokɔ no sɛ, Woakɔ Tall-grass-country no, woanya aduane adi anaa? Ɔbuaa sɛ, Ɛhɔ na na guinea-grain nko ara na ɛwɔ hɔ; na bere a medii me mmofra no, bɛyɛ nsia, na wɔde wɔn ho bɔɔ me, na bere a na wɔn kɛse te sɛɛ (a ɛkyerɛ sɛ wɔn kɛseɛ no), na mema wɔn bɛn me, na ɔbɔfoɔ bi yii ne tuo, pom! na wɔkumm me mmofra, na, sɛ menhunuu me adwene a, anka wɔkunkum me nso. Bere a ɔkasae wiei no, Hɛn kae sɛ, Ɛhe na me kɔɔ no, ɛhɔ ara na mebɛyɛɛ onipa a me ho hia paa. Aturukuku bisaa no sɛ, Kyerɛ me nea wonipa a hia a ɛho hia ankasa kyerɛ. Afei Hɛn kae sɛ, onipa a ɔwɔ hia a ɛho hia ankasa aseɛ ne sɛ, s nhwɛsoɔ no, sɛ me wura trɛw kɛtɛ pa a, t Me kɔda so, na me mmoa bɔ so, na me wura fa nam kɔhyɛ soup-stew no mu a, ɛnneɛ meyi nea mepɛ; aduane biara a me wura bɛdidi, me kɔfa bi, na me di saa a, me wura ntumi mfa ne tuo anaa ne poma mmɔbɔ me. Kyerɛ sɛ nea enti a meka yi ne sɛ, meyɛ onipa a ɔwɔ hia a ɛho hia ankasa. Hɛn no kaa sɛ, Wɔrete hɔ a, ɛnyɛ baabi pa, enti da Memeneda bi, ansa na Akwasidae bi reba no, fa wo mmofra, ne wo kunu, na wɔn nyinaa ntena yɛn fie. Aturukuku kaa sɛ, Metee sɛ, mebɛkɔ, na Memeneda a ɛdi Akwasidae anim no, me bɛba. Afei ɔsɔfoɔ bi baa Hɛn akuraa ase, ɔbɔɔ no asu. ing ne dɔn nkumaa. Wɔbisaa no sɛ, Nokuru, ɛyɛ dɛn? Ɔkaa sɛ, Dente ka sɛ woama no kɔm mfeɛ mmienu ni, enti ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ ɔhene no nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛ no sɛ ɔmfa nkokɔ (ne) nkosua dumienu mmra. Afei Ɔhene no kɔkyeree nkokɔ nnan, de nkosua dumienu baeɛ, faa ɛtɔ a wɔawɔ, na ɔde maa dente bosom no wɔ ne dɔte afɔrebukyia a ano yɛ sɔnn, twaa nkokɔ anan no atitirefoɔ twaa wɔn ho. Ɛhɔ na wɔkyekyeree wɔn, na wɔde wɔn ntakra ne wɔn atitirefoɔ, ne wɔn anan guu dɔte afɔrebukyea no nkyɛn, na ɔno nso de ɛtɔ no too so. Afei, wɔ da a ɛdi Adae no anim no, na Aturukuku rekɔhyiaa ne nuabea. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu sɛ wɔ afɔrebukyia a ɛyɛ kronkron ma Dente no, ne nuabea no agyanom ne netire, nanan, ne ntaban, ne berɛboɔ, ne ne nsono. Na Aturukuku kaa sɛ, " Merenni Kwaeɛ man no mu bio da, kwaemfoɔ no nyɛ yie. " Saa na Aturukuku baeɛ sɛ ɔbɛtena Tall-grass-country no, berɛ a Hɛn bɛtenaa fie hɔ.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi ayɛ wo nokware, ne ahomegyeɛ a wubetumi akamfo me (wɔ nasɛmka ho).
