ƐBAA SƐNEA ƆBONTOYA, MPATWA KETEWA NO, DAA "WALK" FA NSUO MU BASABASA HO

WƆKYERƐ SƐ Old-woman-nanom no na ɔwɔ hɔ, na nagyapadeɛ ayera. Ɔkaa sɛ "nsuo mu mmoa" no na na wɔafa (nneɛma no). Na kɔtɔ no te hɔ, na ɔse, "Aane, wama yɛn aniwa atɔ yɛn yam (wɔ aniwu mu), na nea yɛhyɛ ho nyansa sɛ yɛbɛyɛ na yɛakyerɛ wo nea ɛwɔ yɛn etire ne (eyi): kɔfa kɔbere nedesuɛn bi mmra na yɛn nyinaa mfa nhyɛ (yɛn) nnisuo mu, na nea ontumi nteɛ nhyɛ no, ɔno na wanya (nneɛma no). "

Ɔde kɔbereezeɛn no baeɛ. Okoto, kɔtɔ no, sui na wɔhyɛɛ no ma, woyii no adi; Mmɔborɔhunu baeɛ na wɔsu; Okawa baeɛ na wɔsu; Oyoyo, ɔwɔ-nsuom, baeɛ na ɔsuom; Otidi baeɛ na ɔsuom; Ɔbonko, mmɔtɔ baeɛ na ɔsuom; Akɔreseɛ baeɛ na ɔsuom; Opitiri baeɛ na ɔsuom; Ɔkɔbɔ baeɛ na ɔsuom; nsuo mu mmoa nyinaa baeɛ na wɔsuom. Nea etwa to koraa no, Ɔbontoya, Mpatwa ketewa no, baeɛ sɛ ɔbɛsu; ɔnnyaa nsuo mfi naniwa mu, ɛno nti na wɔhunuu sɛ Ɔbontoya agye nneɛma no.

Nanso Ɔbaa Kunini no kaa sɛ, "Merennyɛ wo hwee". Ɛhɔ ara na abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no nyinaa kaa sɛ, "Yɛn deɛ, yɛbɛbɔ no, ɛfiri sɛ ɔde yɛn ahyɛ aniwu mu. " Enti wɔbɔɔ no.

Bere a wowiee ɔboro no no, Ɔpanyin-Ahyɛse no kae sɛ: "Megyaw wo hɔ (daa) sɛ wobɛkyinkyin a wunni hwee; obiara rempɛ sɛ ɔne wo nya abusuabɔ da. "

Eyi ne sianteɛ a ɛwɔ hɔ sɛ, bere a mmaa "go splashing" (apataa ho no), wɔhu Ɔbontoya kyinkyin (now) ɛha ne hɔ.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
