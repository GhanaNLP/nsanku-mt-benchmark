Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐ WƆREKƆ BAABI, BERƐ A WBUSUANI KYERƐ SƐ ƆNE WO BƐKA YƐN HO A, ƐNO NA WƆNE NO KƆ SO.

NA ƐWƆ HƆ BERE BI A ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔwoo mmofra baasa. Ná wɔn mu kumaa no rehu amane wɔ yaws mu. Na anuanom no mu panyin no ka kyerɛɛ wɔn maame sɛ wɔmfa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma mma wɔn na wɔnkɔ dwadie. Wɔn mu kumaa no nso kaa sɛ ɔbɛpɛ sɛ ɔne wɔn kɔ. Wɔbɔɔ dawuro sɛ ɔne wɔn nkɔ. Na maame no se wɔn ne no ɛsɛ sɛ wɔkɔ. Afei wɔn maame de sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma maa ɔpanyin (mmabarima) no de mfasoɔ yɛ £5, afei nso de sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma maa kumaa no de mfasoɔ yɛ £2. Bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, wɔfirii aseɛ. Mpɔtam hɔ mpanimfoɔ no kɔeɛ (anim) na wɔgyaa abofra no sɛ ɔnni nakyi. Abofra no de nkakrankakra na ɛreba. Mpɔtam hɔ mpanimfo no hyiaa obarima bi a ɔde apataa reba. Ɔka kyerɛɛ mpanimfoɔ sɛ, "Tɔ! " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Kɔ, na wobɛhunu abofra bi; ma ɔntɔ, na ɔnka sɛ ɔrentɔ, bɔ no, na wɔmma ɔntɔ; na sɛ ɔda so ara mpɛ sɛ ɔtɔ a, fa no san kɔ ne maame nkyɛn. " Saa yɔnkoɔ no kɔɔ so, na ɔhunuu abofra no deɛ. Ɔse, "Mpanimfoɔ bi, a mehyiaa wɔn no, kae sɛ wobɛtɔ apataa yi. " Na abofra no se, "Ɛsɛ sɛ me tɔ, bere a wɔn a wɔyɛ me mpanimfoɔ no ntɔ? " Na yɔnkoɔ no se, "Wɔse sɛ worentɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ me bɔ wo, na ɛsɛ sɛ medane wo kɔma wo maame. " Na abofra no se, "Ɛdɛn na ɛyɛ? " Na yɔnkoɔ no se, "An osua no mu duru yɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. e. £2). " Na abofra no tua ne ho ka na ɔgyee no. Ɔkɔɔ so kɔduruu akuraase bi kɔtoo mpanimfoɔ no. Wɔayɛ atosodeɛ, nanso wɔnni nam biara a wɔde bɛkɔ. Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Here is a soup that I bought. " Wɔde guu soup-stew no mu. Bere a wɔwiee (cooking) no, mpanimfoɔ no maa no apataa no etire. Bere a abofra no rebɛbu no, ɔhunuu sika kɔkɔɔ trɔm a ɛwɔ mu, na ɔde akyekyere ne ntoma ano. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, mpanimfoɔ no firii hɔ, na ɛrekɔ so no, wɔhyiaa ɔbarima bi, na akokɔnini bi tenaa nneɛma no atifi, a na ɔso. Ɛhɔ ara na ɔne mpanimfoɔ no baeɛ no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Monnye saa akokɔnini yi. " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Kɔ, na wobɛhunu abofra bi; ma ɔnnye, na sɛ ɔnnye a, bɔ no. " Ɛyɛ nokware sɛ ɔhyiaa abofra no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo abusuafo mpanimfo kae sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ wotɔ saa akokɔnini yi, na sɛ woantɔ a, mɛbɔ wo, na mede wo akɔ wo maame nkyɛn. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Here, take what I have. " Ne yɔnkoɔ no nso de ne nsa hyɛɛ akokɔnini no nsa. Wɔduruu akuraase bi. Wɔ akuraa hɔ nyinaa, na kwaakwaadabi akokɔnini biara nni hɔ. Ɛduruu anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, akokɔnini a ɛyɛ abofra no de no kɔeɛ. Ɔpanyin a ɔwɔ akuraa hɔ no kae sɛ, "Abofra, monnye akokɔnini no mma me ntɔ. " Abofra no kae sɛ, "Ne boɔ yɛ osua ne sare mu sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. e. £3). " Ɔpanyin no tuaa ne boɔ. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa hɔ no, wɔsan hyɛɛ aseɛ bio. Bere a wɔrekɔ no, mpanimfoɔ no hyiaa ɔbarima bi a okura agyinamoa. Ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Tɔ saa agyinamoa yi. " Ɛnna mpanimfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Fa kɔ, na wobɛhunu abofra bi; fa kɔma no sɛ ɔntɔ. Sɛ ɔbɛka sɛ ɔrentɔ a, bɔ no, na fa no kɔ ne maame nkyɛn. " Saa yɔnkoɔ no twaa mu kɔhyiaa abofra no. Ɔkaa sɛ, Mo mpanimfoɔ se mobɛtɔ agyinamoa yi, na sɛ moantɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ mebɔ mo na mede mo san kɔma mo maame. Na abofra no se, Ɛbɛyɛ ahe? Na yɔnkoɔ se, osua ne suru sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma mu duru (i.e. £3). Na abofra no tua boɔ no, na ɔkɔeɛ, na ɔne ne mpanimfoɔ no baeɛ wɔ akuraase bi. Mprempren nkura no na ɛrewe Head-man no anan wɔ hɔ bere biara a ɔbɛsɔ nani ada. Bere a abofra no de agyinamoa no kɔɔ hɔ no, ɛkɔɔ ɔhene no fie na ɛgyee nkura a ɛwɔ hɔ no. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, Mɛbɛtɔ saa agyinamoa yi afiri onipa a ɛyɛ ne dea no nsam. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, Ɛyɛ me dea. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, Ɛbɛyɛ ahe? Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, An osua and suruas weight of sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i.e. £3). Ɔpanyin no faa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no de hyɛɛ ne nsa. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, wɔkɔgyee kwan. Na mpanimfoɔ no rekɔ so, wɔkɔhyiaa awifoɔ a wɔwiaa ɔhene bi funu. awifoɔ no kaa sɛ, Ɛsɛ sɛ wotɔ saa funu yi. Ɛnna mpanimfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Fa no, kɔ so, na wobɛhunu abofra bi; fa no na fa no ma no ntɔ. Sɛ ɔse ɔrentɔ a, bɔ no, na fa no kɔma ne maame. awifoɔ no kɔɔ so, na wɔhyiaa abofra no; wɔkae sɛ, The mpanimfoɔ say you are to buy this funu. The abofra said, Eh! Deɛn nti na mɛtɔ funu no, merentumi nsoa; biribiara a mede bɛyɛ no, ɛbɛyɛ me hwee. Na awifoɔ no ka sɛ, Wo mpanimfoɔ no kaa sɛ, sɛ worentɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛto wo so, na yɛgye wo, na yɛde wo ma wo maame. Na abofra no ka sɛ, Ɛdɛn? Wɔkaeɛ sɛ, An osua and a weight of sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma. Na abofra no tuaa boɔ no, gyee funu no, na ɔde guu nwura no mu. Afei ɔfirii hɔ, na ɔbaa akuraase bi, na ɔkɔɔ fie baako bi kɔsrɛ aduane. Na fie wura no kae sɛ, Anyɛ biara nni hɔ. Na abofra no kae sɛ, Nokuru, me srɛ wo. Na fie wura no kae sɛ, Ebi nni hɔ. Na abofra no kae sɛ, Nokuru, me srɛ wo. Na fie wura no kae sɛ, Adɛn nti na abofra yi haw me saa? Yɛn Ɔhene awu, yɛredi mmuada; eyi ne da a ɛtɔ so nwɔtwe; nanso senea yɛbɛhwehwɛ no, yɛrentumi nnya ne funu. Na aberewa bi rebu no Ɔbɛn hɔ ara, ɔse, Momma no bi. Ɔbaa no de aduane ne nam maa no. Bere a abofra no adidi wiei no, ɔkae sɛ, Master of the fie, I have seen the Chiefs body; for ɛnora a na me reba yi, awifoɔ bi hyɛɛ me sɛ metɔ osua ne suru sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma mu duru. Ɔbaa no dwanee, yii, yii! na ɔka kyerɛɛ akuraa mpanimfoɔ no amaneɛbɔ no. Wɔfaa abofra no, na ɔkɔkyerɛɛ wɔn nipadua no. Wɔkɔɔ so de no bae, na wɔsiee no yiye. Wɔkae sɛ, Seesei wobedi nkonim wɔ Ɔhene no nsam. Enti abofra no bɛyɛɛ Ɔhene. Seesei, bere a ne nuabarimanom mpanimfo tee ne ho asɛm no, wɔbɛbae, na afei wɔregye ne ho akyinnye sɛ ɔne no wɔ mogya mu abusuabɔ. Abofra no kaa sɛ, Metumi afiri hɔ; menyim mo, enti monyɛ nnam. Na ɔmaa ne nkoa no de wɔn kɔɔ wɔn nkyɛn. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, Sɛ worekɔ baabiara a, na wo nuabarima kumaa aka sɛ ɔbɛka wo ho a, wo ne no nkɔ.

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
