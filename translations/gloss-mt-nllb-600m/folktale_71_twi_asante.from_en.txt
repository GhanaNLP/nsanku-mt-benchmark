Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

WOYƐ NWONWASO SƐ KWAKU ANANSE, KOKOAMMOA NO

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse, na ɔte hɔ pɛn, bere a Nsia, ɔsoro-anyame no maame, yaree no. Wɔkɔɔ kɔtoo aduru-man bi sɛ wɔmmɛhwɛ no. Ɔbaa no, ɔgyee sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma peredwan (i.e. £8) sikasiee sɛ ɔhwɛɛ nyareɛ no. Afei, Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn nkyɛn kɔkaa sɛ, "Wo yɔnkoɔ yi de wo sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma rekɔ kwa, ɔrentumi nhwɛ no, enti ma me nhwehwɛ no mma wo, na sɛ menhwɛ no a, me etire bɛtumi ayɛ me atoro. " Kwaku Ananse faa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma asuanu bi (i. e. £4), na ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no sɛ, "Sɛ mantumi annsa yareɛ yi a, me bɛma wo gyata a ɔte ase. " Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, na Nsia, Nyankopɔn, ɔsoro-bɔfoɔ no maame, akɔ. Wɔkae sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse, ɛyɛ dɛn koraa? " Kwaku Ananse fii saa baabi no, wakɔ. Seesei, ɔkasa ne ne benkum akyi (i.e. Me werɛ firii sɛ mɛka sɛ) Kwaku Ananse adamfo paa ne Okusie, Okisi. Ɔka kyerɛɛ Okisi no nea asi no, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛno na mehunu, enti seesei ɛsɛ sɛ moboa me. " Okisi no buae sɛ, "Mepɛ kwan bɛn na metumi aboa wo? " (Kwaku Ananse) buae sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ wokɔ tu ɔkwan no kɔsii sɛ ɛbɛduru pempe a ɔsoro-anyame no te so no ase. " Okisi no kaa sɛ, "Mɛyɛ ama wo. " Enti ɔyɛɛ no saa. Okisi baeɛ na ɔbɔɔ Kwaku Ananse amanneɛ sɛ, "Mawie. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ frɛɛ nɔbabarima, Ntikuma, na ɔtwee aben maa no; na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔnkɔ ɔkwan no mu na ɔntena pempe no ase, na sɛ ɔhu sɛ ɔsoro-anyame no atena pempe no so a, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔbɔ naben no ka sɛ:

"Sɛ wokum Kwaku Ananse a, abusuakuo no bɛba asɛeɛ!

Sɛ wode Kwaku Ananse bɔne kyɛ a, abusuakuo no de nne bɛhinhim! "

Ntikuma kɔɔ tenaa ahɔhogyeɛ akongua no ase, na Kwaku Ananse hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɔrekɔ Nyankopɔn, Ewiem-onyankopɔn, nankasa apam hɔ. Berɛ a ɔkɔɔ no, ɛnneɛ Ewiem-onyankopɔn nsɔmu bɛkyeree no; wɔde no kɔɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn anim. Ɛnna kasafoɔ no kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Wo, Kwaku Ananse, woka kyerɛɛ Nyankopɔn, Ewiem-onyankopɔn, sɛ ne maame, Nsia, awu a, ɔbɛtumi akum wo; enti esiane sɛ Nsia akɔ nti, ɛno nti na yɛbɛkum wo. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ wɔma me kwan ma meka me asɛm. " Nanso, akyerɛfoɔ no kyeree no nsɔmu wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ ɔnkɔkum no. Ntikuma maa ne nne so sɛ: "Wokum Kwaku Ananse a, abusuakuo no bɛba asɛeɛ!

Sɛ wode Kwaku Ananse bɔne kyɛ a, abusuakuo no de nne bɛhinhim! "

Wɔkaa sɛ, "M Muntie, muntie! " Na bio aben no bɔɔ ose:

"Sɛ wokum Kwaku Ananse a, abusuakuo no bɛba asɛeɛ!

Sɛ wode Kwaku Ananse bɔne kyɛ a, abusuakuo no de nne bɛhinhim! "

Ɛnna Krontihene no sɔre bɔɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn, abebuo no amanneɛ sɛ, "Wei ne wo abusuakuo, Ewiem-onyankopɔn, ne Ya, Old-Mother-Earth. Ɛde besi da a woanya Kwaku Ananse na wɔrebɛkum no. Ya, Old-Mother-Earth, ka sɛ, 'Momma ɔnkɔ', enti sɛ wogyae no a, ɛbɛyɛ yie. " Ɛneɛ Ewiem-onyankopɔn maa akyerɛfoɔ no gyaee no. Ɛno nti na abebuo bi wɔ hɔ sɛ, "Woyɛ nwanwa te sɛ Kwaku Ananse. "

Wei, me abasɛm a meka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
