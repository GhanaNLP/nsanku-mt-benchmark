SƐNEA ANENE KƆN, KWAAKWAADABI NO, BƐYƐƐ FƐ NA NE DAN BƐYƐƐ TUNTUM

Ɔbaa bi a wɔfrɛ no Ata (Nta) de ne abofra kɔɔ hɔ sɛ ɔrekɔpopa (apataa). Bere a wɔduruu asuwa no so no, ne abofra no kaa sɛ, Yɛnkɔ nsuo no mu. Nanso aberewa no kaa sɛ, Yɛnkɔ nsuo no ase, ɛfiri sɛ nsuo no ase na nkawa nketewa no wɔ. Ne abofra kaa sɛ, Me deɛ, merekɔ nsuo no so, ɛfiri sɛ ɛwɔ nsuo no atifi na nkawa nketewa no wɔ. Enti ne maame kɔɔ nsuo no ase. Wɔkyɛɛ kwan berɛ tenten, na ne abofra nyaa bi, na ɔbaa na ɔretwam akɔpɛ ne maame. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na ne maame calabash no retu wɔ nsuo no anim. Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Aah! ɛnyɛ baako (adeɛ), ɛnyɛ mmienu (nneɛma), ɛyɛ ne maame calabash. " Na ɔde ne nne kɔɔ soro, ɔsu frɛɛ ne maame, na ɔkae sɛ:

"Mekae, na mo ntiee, mekae, na mo ntiee.

Mekae sɛ, 'Maame, momma yɛnkɔ nsuo no soro, ɛfiri sɛ nsuo no atifi na nkawa nketewa te'.

Na ɔkaeɛ sɛ ɔrekɔ nsuo ase, efiri sɛ asuwa ase na nkawa nketewa te.

Na me bɛyɛ dɛn? Me maame, Ata, mhm! "

Akrɔma no bɛkaa ho; ɔkaa sɛ, "Abofra, wo dwom no yɛ dɛ, to no na mɛyi wo maame no afiri wo nsam. " Na abofra no maa ne nne so sɛ:

"Mekae, na mo ntiee, mekae, na mo ntiee.

Mekae sɛ, 'Maame, momma yɛnkɔ nsuo no soro, ɛfiri sɛ nsuo no atifi na nkawa nketewa te'.

Na ɔkaeɛ sɛ ɔrekɔ nsuo ase, efiri sɛ asuwa ase na nkawa nketewa te.

Na me bɛyɛ dɛn? Me maame, Ata, mhm! "

Na Akrɔma no faa ne (magic) aboa tail-switch no bɔɔ nsuo no anim, nanso nsuo no gyinaa nea ɛgyina. Na ɔno (Akrɔma no) kɔɔ so. Na Kɔkɔsakyi, Ɔpɛtɛ no, bɛkaa ho, na ɔno nso, ɔkae sɛ, "To saa dwom no ma me nte, na may I get your maame out for you. " Na abofra no too dwom no:

"Mekae, na mo ntiee, mekae, na mo ntiee.

Mekae sɛ, 'Maame, momma yɛnkɔ nsuo no soro, ɛfiri sɛ nsuo no atifi na nkawa nketewa te'.

Na ɔkaeɛ sɛ ɔrekɔ nsuo ase, efiri sɛ asuwa ase na nkawa nketewa te.

Na me bɛyɛ dɛn? Me maame, Ata, mhm! "

Na ɔno nso de ne (magic) tail bɛbɔɔ asubonten no anim, nanso hwee nsii. Na ɔno nso kɔɔ so. Na (now) Anene, Kwaakwaadabi no, nso ka ho. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abofra, dɛn nti na woresu sɛɛ? Su na ma mete sɛ ebia mɛgye wo maame afiri wo nsam. " Na abofra no too dwom no sɛ:

"Mekae, na mo ntiee, mekae, na mo ntiee.

Mekae sɛ, 'Maame, momma yɛnkɔ nsuo no soro, ɛfiri sɛ nsuo no atifi na nkawa nketewa te'.

Na ɔkaeɛ sɛ ɔrekɔ nsuo ase, efiri sɛ asuwa ase na nkawa nketewa te.

Na me bɛyɛ dɛn? Me maame, Ata, mhm! "

Kwaakwaadabi no faa aduro cone bi tow guu nsuo no mu, na ɔfaa ne bodua bɔɔ nsuo no anim, na nsuo no mu paapaee mu mmienu, na ɔkae sɛ, "to dwom no". Ɛnna abofra no too dwom. Na ohu ne maame ti no ti nwi no, na ɔserew kakra. Ɔkae sɛ, "to dwom bio," na ɔto dwom. Ɛnna ne maame no pue kɔɔ asene no so. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ so dwomtoɔ. " Na abofra no too dwom, na ne maame puei koduu ne nanpɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "to dwom bio! " na ɔtoo dwom. Ne maame no nyinaa huri firii adi prɛko pɛ na ɔgyinaa sikakorabea hɔ. Ata, Nta no, kae sɛ, "Kwaakwaadabi, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ megye de da wo ase? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, me mpɛ hwee koraa, kɔtɔ kɔla a ɛyɛ bruu tuntum ne fitaa. " Ata tuu mmirika tɔɔ bi, na ɔde kɔɔ Kwaakwaadabi, na Kwaakwaadabi de kɔla a ɛyɛ bruu tuntum kataa ne ho, na ne fitaa a ɔde kyekyeree ne kɔn ho no nyinaa. Ɛno nti na wobɛhunu Kwaakwaadabi, nakyi yɛ tuntum, nanso ne kɔn ho yɛ fitaa. Ɛyɛ akyɛdeɛ a ɔnya firii Ata, Nta.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi betumi ayɛ wo nokware, ne ahomegyeɛ a wubetumi akamfo me (wɔ nasɛmka ho).
