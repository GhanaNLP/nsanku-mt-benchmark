Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ NWANSANA, NKOEKOEMMOA A ƆYƐ NKOEKOEMMOA NO, BERE A ƆTO KRAMAN, KRAMAN NO, ƆBƆ NO, KAM! KAM! Na nkoekoemmoa no to kraman no, ɔbɔ no, KAM! KAM!

Abɔmmɔfoɔ bi na ɔwɔ akraman baanan; wɔfrɛ bi Sniff-sniff; wɔfrɛ bi Lick-lick; wɔfrɛ bi Tie-in-knots; na wɔfrɛ bi Gulp-down. Na ɔtaa kyere ɔne ne akraman no. Bere nyinaa, bere a ɔkɔɔ kwaeɛ no, ɔne wɔn kɔɔ baako. Ɛda bi ɔkɔɔ kwaeɛ na ɔhunuu kola dua bi; ɛhyɛ nnuaba a ɛsɛeɛ sɛnea ɛteɛ. Afei ɔsan kɔɔ fie, na ɔbɔɔ ne yere amanneɛ sɛ, "Kola dua bi a meahu no, ɛhyɛ nnuaba a ɛsɛeɛ sɛnea ɛteɛ. " Ne yere kae sɛ, "Mfa nnuaba no, efisɛ wunim sɛ yɛnni kola nnuaba biara a yɛbɛnam so adidi na yɛnni biara a yɛbɛtɔn. " Abɔmmɔfoɔ no kɔɔ kwan so, nanso ɔgyaa akraman no wɔ fie, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a kola dua no wɔ. Afei ɔforoo so, na ɔtwaa kola nnuaba bi. Afei ɔtee nneɛma bi firi akyirikyiri, "Twa! be! kukuru! kukuru! hu! pi! " Sasabonsam bi gyinaa n’anim. Ɔkae sɛ, "Hwan na watwa me kola nnuaba no? " Abɔmmɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, "Me ara. " Sasabonsam no kae sɛ, "Mɛkum wo ntɛm ara wɔ saa adeɛ a woyɛ yi ho. " Abɔmmɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, "Sɛ wobɛkum me a, ma mfrɛ me akraman no di kan. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Frɛ, nanso bere a wode bɛbɔ mmɔden abiɛsa sɛ wobɛfrɛ wɔn no, na wɔamma a, ɛnde mɛkum wo. " Afei abɔmmɔfoɔ no maa ne nne kɔɔ soro:

Hwɛ-hwɛ, bɛhyia me, Nsu-nsu, bɛhyia me, Kyerɛ-kyerɛ, bɛhyia me, Nsu-nsu, bɛhyia me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔrekyɛ a!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔrekyɛ a!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔrekyɛ a!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔrekyɛ a!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔkyɛ a wɔreba no!

Na akraman a wɔwɔ fie no tee no kakraa bi koraa, na wɔtuu kɔɔ kurow no akyi. Afei odumgyafoɔ no maa ne nne so bio:

“Hwɛ-hwɛ, bɛhyia me, Nsu-nsu, bɛhyia me, Kyerɛ-kyerɛ, bɛhyia me, Nsu-nsu, bɛhyia me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔrekyɛ a!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔrekyɛ a!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔrekyɛ a!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔrekyɛ a!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔkyɛ a wɔreba no!

Afei nkraman no ate, wɔatu mmirika, prada! prada! Sasabonsam no kae sɛ, “Frɛ bio, na ɛno na ɛyɛ nea ɛtɔ so mmiɛnsa. ” Abɔmmɔfoɔ no bɔɔ ne nne kɛseɛ paa:

“Hwɛ-hwɛ, bɛhyia me, Nsu-nsu, bɛhyia me, Kyerɛ-kyerɛ, bɛhyia me, Nsu-nsu, bɛhyia me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔrekyɛ a!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔrekyɛ a!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔrekyɛ a!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔrekyɛ a!

They are long in coming!

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
