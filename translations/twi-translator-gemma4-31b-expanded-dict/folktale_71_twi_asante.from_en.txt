Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

Wo yɛ nwanwa te sɛ Ananse mpɔkorɔ.

Na Ananse, nwansena no, retena hɔ bere a Nsia, soro-onyankopɔn no maame, yareɛ. Wɔkɔɔ kɔfaa aduro-ɔsafoɔ bi sɛ ɔmmɛhwɛ no. Bere a ɔbaa no, ɔkae sɛ ɔbɛgye sika kusuu pereduan bi (i. e. £8) wɔ ne yareɛ no ho. Afei Ananse kɔka kyerɛɛ soro-onyankopɔn no sɛ, "Saa ɔbarima yi regye wo sika kusuu kwa; ɔntumi nhwɛ no, enti mnhwɛ no mma wo, na sɛ me nhwɛ no a, me ti nni hɔ. " Ananse faa sika kusuu asuanu bi (i. e. £4), na ɔka kyerɛɛ soro-onyankopɔn no sɛ, "Sɛ mentumi nsa saa yareɛ yi a, mɛma wo ɔgyafoɔ a ɔte aseɛ. " Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma daa adi no, Nsia, Nyankopɔn, soro-onyankopɔn no maame, akɔ. Wɔkae sɛ, "Ananse, ɛyɛ dɛn? " Ananse fii saa beaeɛ no; ɔagyan. Afei, nkɔmmɔ ne ne nkaeɛ (i. e. me werɛ fi sɛ) Ananse adamfo kɛseɛ ne ɔpɔnkɔ, ɔpɔnkɔ-kɔkɔɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔpɔnkɔ no nea ɛkɔɔ so sɛ, "Ɛno na mehu, enti afei ɛsɛ sɛ wo boa me. " Ɔpɔnkɔ no buaa sɛ, "Wɔ ɔkwan bɛn so na mɛboa wo? " (Ananse) buaa sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ wɔnkɔ tu tunnel kɔsi sɛ ɛbɛduru ɔhyɛn a wɔama no so a soro-onyankopɔn no te so no ase. " Ɔpɔnkɔ no kae sɛ, "Mɛyɛ ama wo. " Ɛno nti ɔyɛɛ no. Ɔpɔnkɔ no baa bɛka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, "Mewie. " Ananse kɔɔ hɔ kɔfrɛɛ ne ba, Ntikuma; na ɔtwaa nea ɔde bɛyɛ horn no; na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔnkɔ tunnel no mu ntena ɔhyɛn a wɔama no so no ase, na bere a ɔhu sɛ soro-ony

Sɛ wobɛkum Ananse a, mmusuakuw no bɛba sɛnea ɛbɛsɛe!

Sɛ wofa Ananse ho kyɛ a, mmusuakuw no bɛhunu nneɛma a ɛyɛ nnyigyei!

Ntikuma kɔɔ na ɔtenaa nkaeɛ nkongua no ase, na Ananse firi hɔ sɛ ɔreka sɛ ɔkɔ Nyankonpon, ɔsoro-onum, hɔ ne pɛ mu. Sɛ ɔkɔeɛ a, ɛnneɛ ɔsoro-onum no asotwefoɔ kyeree no; wɔde no kɔɔ ɔsoro-onum no anim. Ɛnneɛ ɔkasafoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Wo, Kwaku Ananse, wo kaeɛ Nyankonpon, ɔsoro-onum, sɛ sɛ ne maame Nsia wuwui a, ɔbɛkum wo; enti esiane sɛ Nsia akɔ nti, yɛbɛkum wo. " Ananse kaeɛ sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ woma me kwan sɛ meka me asɛm. " Nanso asotwefoɔ no kyeree no sɛ wɔbɛkɔ akum no. Ntikuma maa ne nne so sɛ: "Sɛ wokum Ananse a, mmusuakuo no bɛsɛe!

Sɛ wofa Ananse ho kyɛ a, mmusuakuw no bɛhunu nneɛma a ɛyɛ nnyigyei!

Wɔkae sɛ, "tie, tie!" Na bio nso abɛn no bɔɔ:

Sɛ wobɛkum Ananse a, mmusuakuw no bɛba sɛnea ɛbɛsɛe!

Sɛ wofa Ananse ho kyɛ a, mmusuakuw no bɛhunu nneɛma a ɛyɛ nnyigyei!

Afei Kontirehene panyin no sɔree bɛka kyerɛɛ Sky-god sɛ, "Saa mmusuakuo yi yɛ wo de, Sky-god, na Ya, Asase-maame dedaw. Ɛnnɛ woanya Ananse na wɔrebu no kum. Ya, Asase-maame dedaw, ka sɛ, ‘Ma no kɔ,’ enti sɛ wogyae no a, ɛbɛyɛ yie. " Afei Sky-god maa asotwefoɔ no sɛ wɔmfa no nkɔ. Ɛno nti na asɛm bi wɔ hɔ a ɛne sɛ, "Woyɛ asɛm a ɛyɛ nwanwa sɛ Ananse. "

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
