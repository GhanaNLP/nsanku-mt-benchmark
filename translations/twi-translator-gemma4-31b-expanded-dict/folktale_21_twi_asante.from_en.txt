Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

HOW OWEA (DUA SO MMOA), ABOSOM-AKOTERE (ƆKRAMAN A ƆSAKRA NE HO), NE OPATUO (ƆPATUO A ƆYƐ NNYIGYEI) BAA HƆ BƐNYAA WƆN NNYIGYEI SORONKO

OWEA, dua-mmoa, a ɔwɔ abodin Kwaduampon Kyerefo, Opatuo, patu, ne Abosom-Akotere, abosom-akoterɛ, a wɔn din ne, “Ɔbɔfoɔ”, “Afe-mmoa-a-ɔyɛ-biribi-biara”, “Ɔnam-brɛoo-koraa-nanso-ɔbɛduru-borɔfoɔ-asase-a-ɛwɔ-akyirikyiri”, mmoa yi nyinaa tenaa bom. Mprempren, na obiara nni wɔn mu. Na Adobe-dua no tɔnɛɛ ne ho ma wɔn. Da biara, bere a Adobe-dua no sɔree no, ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔhwɛɛ saa baako yi; da a edi hɔ no, ɔhwɛɛ wɔn mu biako; na da a edi hɔ no biako. Da biako, bere a ɛduruu Ɔbɔfoɔ-wu nsa na ɔhwehwɛɛ Adobe-dua no, ɛhe na ɔbɛhu no? Ɔde ne ho kɔɔ dua-mmoa ne patu no nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn asɛm no. Na wɔkae sɛ, “mfa no frɛ no. ” Na Ɔbɔfoɔ-wu no maa ne nne kɔɔ soro: “Adobe o! Adobe o! ”

Dua-mmoa no suu:

“Bere a ɔkɔɔ no, ɔansan mma; ɔkɔɔ, ɔansan ba, ɔansan ba!”

Na owufoɔ no frɛɛ sɛ:

“Yɛasusuw sika kusuu no na yɛtumi tow gu, m! Yɛasusuw sika kusuu no na yɛtumi tow gu no, m! m! a”

Na ɛno ne mfitiaseɛ a ɛfiri teɛteɛ a ɛwɔ abɔdeɛ mmiɛnsa yi.

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
