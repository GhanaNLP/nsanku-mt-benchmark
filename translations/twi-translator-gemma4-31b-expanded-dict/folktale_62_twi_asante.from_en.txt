Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

How

Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ bi a wɔfrɛ no Kwasi Gyaba ne ne yere kɔɔ asraafoɔ atraeɛ bi kɔbɔɔ abɔmmɔ. Afei, bere a Kwasi Gyaba kɔɔ kwaeɛ no, amfa nam biara mma. Ɔamfa nam mfi hɔ da.

Afei Ne yokommua kae sɛ, "Ah, me yommua, dɛn nti na wore sere me saa? " Nanso Kwasi Gyaba dae wɔ fam sɛ ɔregye sere, na ɔkɔɔ so sere. Ɛno ara na ne yokommua bɔɔ asɛm a wɔabara wɔ soro onyamfo no so sɛ ɔmfa ne sere no nkyerɛ ansa na wɔ soro onyamfo no anim. Soro onyamfo no somaa nkrɔfoɔ a wɔbɛfa Kwasi Gyaba. Bere a ɔduruu kuro no awiei no, ɔmaa ne dwom no so:

Now

Me bɛka na awu, bere a eyi si a, ɛnsɛ sɛ wonsu mma me. Kwasi Mpuroko Gyaba, ɔrekɔ, bere a eyi si a, ɛnsɛ sɛ wonsu mma me.

Wɔn a wɔwɔ soro no mpanimfoɔ no kae sɛ, “Tie afiri no dwom no. ” Wɔde Kwasi Gyaba kɔɔ soro onipa no anim. Ɔkasafoɔ no sɔree kɔɔ so kae sɛ, “Kwasi Gyaba, wofiri wo maame ankasa, anaa ɔreka atoro? ” Kwasi Gyaba buae sɛ, “Me panin, ɛnam aduru bi nti a me ne no wɔ nkitaho mu no, sɛ me kyerɛ nea enti a me sere no a, me bɛwu. ” Soro onipa no kae sɛ, “Ɛfiri sɛ woyɛ me abɔfoɔ, me remma woawu. ” Ne maame no kae sɛ, “Me panin, ɔreka atoro, na ɔreka me sere, enti me nni hwee gye nkyerɛkyerɛmu. ” Ɔkasafoɔ no kae sɛ, “Kwasi Gyaba, woate? ” Kwasi Gyaba buae sɛ, “Ɛnneɛ, me srɛ sɛ mehu mpaeɛ, bere a mewu a, mfa me nsi mu. ” Anka ne ano nnyina hɔ, na akokɔɔ ne akokɔɔ bae; na akokɔɔ no ka kyerɛɛ akokɔɔ no sɛ, “Gyina hɔ ma me nni wo. ” Akokɔɔ no kae sɛ, “Dɛn, wɔ nnipa yi anim? ” Akokɔɔ no kae sɛ, “Sɛ wontena hɔ a, me bɛka asɛm a wɔabara no sɛ wotumi fa

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
