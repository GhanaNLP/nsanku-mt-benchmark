iv | Ghana Aban - Ɔmanfoɔ Sikasɛm ne Ɔmanfoɔ Sikasɛm no wɔ Mfeɛ 2023

“Sikasɛm mu ahotɔ ne ahoɔden a wɔbɛsan de ama nkɔso a ɛka obiara ho ne mfaso a wɔde bɛka ho no bɛkura mu”

Nsɛm a Ɛdi Kan

Afe yi akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ ɛbɛyɛ afe a ɛyɛ den sen biara wɔ yɛn man no sikasɛm abakɔsɛm mu. Sɛnea wiase afoforo te no, wɔde COVID-19 ɔyaredɔm no too yɛn so wɔ afe no fã a edi kan no mu, na ɛmaa sikasɛm mu dwumadi brɛɛ ase, na ɛmaa yɛn sika a yɛnyae no so tew na ɛka foforo a wɔmfa nhyehyɛe mma a ɛfa ɔyaredɔm no ho.

Bere a yɛresusuw sɛ na yɛreyɛ nkɔso kɛse wɔ yɛn nsakrae nhyehyɛe a yɛde bedi dwuma no mu wɔ ɔyaredɔm no nkɛntɛnso bɔne a yɛafi mu no mu no, nsɛm ahorow bi baa yɛn so a ɛsɛe sikasɛm mu nkonimdi a yɛnyae fi afe 2017 kosi 2019. Nea edi kan ne Russia ne Ukraine ɔko no, afei akyɛde a ɛbaa sika a wɔbɔ ho nhyehyɛe titiriw a wɔde bɛma wɔ 2022 sikasɛm nhyehyɛe no mu no maa boseabɔ ho akontaabu so tew. Mpofirim, mfe du a edi kan no, Amanaman Ntam Kapital Gua no tooyɛɛ yɛn, ɔman no mu sikasɛm tebea yɛɛ den na bosea ho ka kɔɔ soro, na ɛmaa ɛka a wɔbɔe no yɛɛ kɛse na CD boɔ kɔɔ fam ntɛmntɛm.

Enti, bere a amannɔne sika mmoa a efi sika kɛseɛ gua ne yɛn amannɔne sika a yɛnya so tew no, nneɛma a yɛde fi amannɔne ba no ho ka a ɛkɔɔ soro esiane yɛn ahwehwɛde a ɛntumi nni n’ase sɛ ɔman ne ɛka a yɛbɔ wɔ ɛka a yɛde tua ho no maa yɛn sika no bo so tew. Eyi ama asetra ho ka akɔ soro ama yɛn nyinaa sɛ aban ne sɛ ankorankoro. Pɛtro bo a ɛkɔ soro no ama nneɛma ne nnwuma dodow no ara bo akɔ soro, na nneɛma bo akɔ soro 40. 4% wɔ October 2022 mu.

Wɔsɔɔ yɛn sɔ sɛ nnipa a wɔayɛ yɛn sɔ, na aban no te nka kɛse wɔ nhyɛso a ɛfata a ɛde bae no ho. Yɛpɛ sɛ yɛda yɛn anisɔ adi sɛ aban no kyerɛ wɔn a wɔde wɔn ho hyɛ mu, a Mmarahyɛ Bagua, Adwumawuranfoɔ Fekuo, Adwumayɛfoɔ Fekuo, Ɔmanfoɔ Fekuo, Ahyehyɛdeɛ a Ɛgyina Gyidi So, Ghana Nnwuma Fekuo ne

v | Ghana Aban - 2023 Ɔmanfoɔ Sikasɛm

Bɛsan asiesie na wɔakura sikasɛm mu ahotɔ ne ahoɔden mu de ama nkɔso a ɛka obiara ho ne mfaso a wɔde bɛka ho.

Aguadifoɔ Fekuo, Nhomanimfoɔ, Adwene Kuw ne Ghanafoɔ a wɔyɛ daa a wɔrekɔ so agyina yɛn ano bere a yɛretwam saa mmere a emu yɛ den yi mu.

Wɔ H.E. Nana Addo Danquah Akuffo-Addo ananmu no, mepɛ sɛ mehwɛ sɛ Ghanafoɔ nyinaa hu sɛ aban no reyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛbɛsan de sikasɛm aba na ama sikasɛm mu ahotɔ, bɛma sika a ɛho hia aba, de nhyehyɛe ne ɔmanfo nsakraeɛ adi dwuma, atrɛw dijitaal ne honam fam nneɛma mu, atew nneɛma a ɛho nhia a wɔde fi amannɔne ba so, ahyɛ nneɛma a wɔde kɔ amannɔne ho nkuran, atrɛw nneɛma a wɔde si nneɛma a wɔde fi amannɔne ba ananmu a wɔyɛ wɔ mpɔtam hɔ no mu na wɔabɔ wɔn a wɔn ho yɛ den ne wɔn a wɔayi wɔn afi asetra mu no ho ban.

Adesua a yɛasua sɛ ɔman no afiri saa bere yi mu no ama ɛsɛ sɛ yɛsan yɛ yɛn sikasɛm foforo na yɛsesa no yɛ ahoɔden daa. Yɛbɛyɛ yie. Yɛbɛfiri eyi mu yɛden. Awurade bɛkura yɛn mu na ɔma yɛn ahoɔden ma yɛyɛ eyi.

Ghana Aban - Ɔmanfoɔ Sikasɛm a ɛfa afe 2023 ho vi

“Sikasɛm mu ahotɔ ne ahoɔden a wɔbɛsan de ama nkɔso a ɛka obiara ho ne mfaso a wɔde bɛka ho no bɛkura mu”

Nnwom Mfiase

1. 2023 Ɔmanfoɔ Sikakorabea no yɛ nsɛm a wɔaka abom a ɛfa nea aban no ayɛ wɔ afe 2022 mu ne sɛnea ɛbɛboaboa ano na ɛbɛgye de ne sika a ɛbɛnya no adi dwuma de adi nhyehyɛeɛ ne nhyehyɛeɛ ho dwuma. Asɛmti a ɛwɔ 2023 sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ no mu ne "Restoring and Sustaining Macroeconomic Stability and Resilience for Inclusive Growth & Value Addition".

2. Mfeɛ no mu no, sikasɛm nhyehyɛɛ foforɔ biara wɔ nnwuma foforɔ pii a wɔbɛhyɛ aseɛ a wɔde bɛto nnwuma a ɛrekɔ so no so. Eyi nko ara nni yɛn sikasɛm so nanso abakɔsɛm mu no ɛma sika a wɔatua akyiri yi, a ɛma nnwuma no nyinaa ho ka kɔ soro efisɛ sika a wɔatwe adwene asi so no ma wɔbɔ mfɛntom.

3. Wɔ afe 2023 mu no, aban no bɛyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ne nhyehyɛeɛ foforɔ nanso ɛbɛhyɛ amammerɛ a ɛne sɛ wɔbɛwie nnwuma a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw no nyinaa nkuran.

4. Wɔ 2022 afe mfinimfini sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ mu nhwehwɛmu no mu no, aban no de sikasɛm kɛseɛ botaeɛ a ɛdi akyi yi sii hɔ maa afe 2022:

iii. Nneɛma boɔ kɔ soro a ɛkɔɔ soro ɔha biara mu aduonu awotwe nsɛntitiriw anum wɔ bere a ɛtwa toɔ no mu.

iv. Sikasɛm mu ka a ɛkɔ soro nyinaa a ɛyɛ GDP ɔha biara mu 6. 6.

v. Mfitiaseɛ sika a ɛboro so ɔha biara mu nkyem anan wɔ GDP mu na

5. Nsɛm a ɛwɔ hɔ a ɛfa sikasɛm mu nkɔsoɔ ho fi Ɔpɛpɔn kɔsi September 2022 no si nkɛntɛnsoɔ bɔne a ɛkɔ so ba fi wiase ne ɔman no mu tebea a ɛyɛ den no so dua. Saa nkɔsoɔ yi ada adi denam sika a wɔde sesa nneɛma a ɛkɔ fam ntɛmntɛm, nneɛma boɔ a ɛkɔ soro, ɛka a ɛnyɛ nea ɛbɛkɔ so atra hɔ daa, sikasɛm mu nhyɛsoɔ ne amannɔne adwumayɛkuo mu ahodwiriw so, ɛmfa ho sɛ sikasɛm ne sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ a aban no de aba no. Wɔakyerɛ sikasɛm mu nkɔsoɔ a ɛyɛ bere tiaa mu fi Ɔpɛpɔn kɔsi September 2022 sɛnea edidi so yi:

ɔha biara mu nkyem 4

ii. Nneɛma bo a ɛkɔ soro titiriw kɔɔ 40. 4% wɔ October 2022 mu firi 37. 2% wɔ September ne 33. 9% wɔ August mu ɔha biara mu esiane aduane ne nneɛma a ɛnyɛ aduane bo a ɛkɔɔ soro nti.

iii. Ná sikasɛm mu ka a wɔbɔ nyinaa wɔ sika a wɔde di dwuma mu yɛ GDP ɔha biara mu 7.4 sen botae a ɛne sɛ GDP ɔha biara mu 6.2.

2 | Ghana Aban - Mfeɛ 2023 ne Ɔmanfoɔ Sikasɛm a ɛfa Ɔmanfoɔ Sikasɛm ho na Ɔmanfoɔ Sikasɛm

“Sikasɛm mu ahotɔ ne ahoɔden a wɔbɛsan de ama nkɔso a ɛka obiara ho ne mfaso a wɔde bɛka ho no bɛkura mu”

Ná sikasɛm mu ahokyere titiriw no yɛ GDP ɔha biara mu nkyem abien a wɔde bɔ botae a ɛne sɛ wɔbɛma GDP ɔha biara mu biako no ho. Ade titiriw a ɛde sikasɛm mu ahokyere a egyina sika so ba ne sika a wɔatua a ɛkɔ akyiri fi afe 2021 ne mfe a atwam no mu a wɔde ka mprempren afe yi ka ho.

iv. Na Ɔman bosea a ɛne GDP nsusuwii yɛ ɔha biara mu aduɔson anum nkron wɔ September 2022 awiei, na ɛso tew fi ɔha biara mu aduɔson nsia esiane sika bo a ɛkɔɔ fam no nkɛntɛnso titiriw nti.

v. Amanaman ntam sika a wɔde asie nyinaa yɛ US$6,591. 8 ɔpepem, a ɛne nneɛma a wɔde fi amannɔne ba asram 2. 9 yɛ pɛ wɔ September 2022 awiei, so tew fii sika a wɔde asie a ɛyɛ US$9,695. 2 ɔpepem (a ɛne nneɛma a wɔde fi amannɔne ba asram 4. 3 yɛ pɛ) wɔ December 2021 awiei.

vi. Ghana sika so tew bere nyinaa mu ɔha biara mu 54. 2 wɔ U.S. dɔla ho sɛ wɔde toto bo a ɛso tew ɔha biara mu 4. 1 wɔ December 2021 awiei no ho a. Saa ara nso na Ghana sika so tew ɔha biara mu 48. 5 wɔ Britain sika ho bere a ɛso tew ɔha biara mu 3. 1 wɔ December 2021 awiei no.

Sika a wɔnya dwumadi

8. Wɔ afe 2021 mu no, wɔkyerɛw bere koro no ara mu. Sika a wonya ne mmoa nyinaa yɛɛ GH¢65,399 ɔpepem (GDP ɔha biara mu 11. 0), bere a wɔde toto botae a ɛyɛ GH¢67,307 (GDP ɔha biara mu 11. 4) ne GH¢49,108 ɔpepem (GDP ɔha biara mu 10. 7) a wɔkyerɛw too hɔ wɔ bere koro no ara mu wɔ afe 2021 mu no ho no.

yɛ ɔman adwumayɛbea a ɛnyɛ mfasoɔ a ɛwɔ Aguadi ne Nnwuma Dwumadibea ase na wɔahyɛ no sɛ ɛnhyɛ nnwuma nketewa ne nketewa ho nkuran.

Ɔman Kwaeɛ Nhyehyɛeɛ (NAP): Ɛyɛ abɔdeɛ mu nsakraeɛ a ɛbɛsiesie kwaeɛ a asɛe no na ama kwaeɛ mu nneɛma anya nkɔsoɔ denam nnipa a wɔde wɔn ho bɛhyɛ mu, a wɔde wɔn adwene asi mpɔtam a ɛwɔ kwaeɛ ano, titiriw ahiafoɔ, asetra a ɛbɛtu mpɔn so.

Ɔman Akwanhyia Ho Dwumadibea (NADMO): Ɛyɛ aban adwumakuo a ɛhwɛ akwanhyia ne nteɛso afoforo so wɔ Ghana.

GDP a ɛnyɛ ngo ankasa no yɛ nneɛma a wɔyɛ nyinaa wɔ sikasɛm mu a wɔayi afi mu wɔ ngo adwuma mu a ɛbu nneɛma bo a ɛkɔ soro ho akontaa.

Nngoɔ a ɛnyɛ ngoɔ GDP nkɔsoɔ ankasa: Ɛyɛ sɛnkyerɛnne a ɛsusuw afe afe sikasɛm mu nkɔso ankasa a ɛwɔ nnwuma nyinaa mu gye ngo nkutoo.

Sika a ɛnyɛ towtua: Aban sika a ɛnyɛ towtua, nhwɛsoɔ ne sika a wɔbɔ wɔ tumi krataa anaa tumi krataa a wɔde ma, sika a wɔde di dwuma ne sika foforɔ a wɔbɔ.

Asɛyɛdeɛ a ɛda so wɔ hɔ: Asɛyɛdeɛ a ɛda so wɔ hɔ ne MDA bɔhyɛ a ɛne Sikasɛm Dwumadibea no ne 2016 sikatua a ɛda so a wɔde ma Mmara Sikakorabea no.

Nea ɛfiri mu ba: ɛyɛ nea efi mu ba wɔ adeyɛ anaa nsɛm a ɛtoatoa so mu.

Titiri sika a ɛkari pɛ: Eyi yɛ nsonsonoe a ɛda aban sika a wonya ne sika a wɔsɛe a ɛnyɛ mfɛntom (a wɔayi afi mu sɛ ɛka a wɔtua ho ka) ntam. Wobetumi asusuw eyi sɛ ɔha biara mu nkyem a ɛwɔ ɔman no mu nneɛma a wɔyɛ nyinaa (GDP) ho.

Mfitiaseɛ sika a ɛboro so (sika a ɛho nhia): Ɛkyerɛ sikasɛm mu sika a ɛboro so (sika a ɛho nhia) no fã a ɛka ho mprempren aban sika a wɔsɛe no a wɔayi afi mu mprempren sika a wonya fi towtua mu na ɛnyɛ mfɛntom a wotua wɔ aban bosea ho.

33 | Ghana Aban - Mfeɛ 2023 Ɔmanfoɔ Sikasɛm

“Sikasɛm mu ahotɔ ne ahoɔden a wɔbɛsan de ama nkɔso a ɛka obiara ho ne mfaso a wɔde bɛka ho no bɛkura mu”

Nkɔsoɔ a wɔahyɛ ho nkɔm: Sɛnea Ghana sikasɛm bɛnyin wɔ afe bi mu.

GDP ankasa yɛ susuw a ɛkyerɛ ɔman bi nneɛma a wɔyɛ wɔ ɔman no mu nyinaa a wɔayɛ nsakrae wɔ nneɛma bo a ɛkɔ soro ho.

Sika a wonya: Eyi yɛ sika a wonya nyinaa a aban no nya wɔ n’adwumayɛ mu fi ɔman no mu ne amannɔne nyinaa.

RFJ (Rearing For Food and Jobs): bɛma mmoa a wɔyɛ ho adwuma a akansi na ɛyɛ adwuma yiye, a ɛbɛma ɔman no mu nneɛma a wɔyɛ akɔ soro, na ateɛm mmoa nneɛma a wɔde fi amannɔne ba, na ama wɔanya adwuma, na ama mmoa a wɔyɛ ho adwuma no mufo asetena atu mpɔn.

Nteaseɛ yɛ su anaa tebea a ɛma obi yɛ nteaseɛ wɔ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ nteaseɛ so wɔ mmuaeɛ a ɔyɛ ma nneɛma bi a ɛkanyan obi no ho.

Nnwumayɛbea nketewa ne akɛse (SMEs): Wɔyɛ nnwumakuw a ɛnyɛ nnwumakuw nketewa, nnwumakuw a wɔde wɔn ho a wɔfa adwumayɛfo a ɛba fam sen adwumayɛfo dodow bi.

Asetena mu mfasoɔ: Aban no tua nnwumakuo a wɔyɛ nneɛma a wɔde di dwuma ne pɛtro nneɛma ho ka wɔ ɔmanfoɔ ananmu.

Squadron: Ɛyɛ adwumayɛkuo a ɛka ho wɔ wimhyɛn asraafo mu a wimhyɛn a ɛyɛ abien anaa nea ɛboro saa ne adwumayɛfo a wɔhwehwɛ sɛ wɔtu wimhyɛn.

Mmoa: Eyi yɛ sikasɛm mu ahotɔ a aban de ma ɔmanfo de tew adesoa a ɛwɔ wɔn so no so.

Ahwɛyiye: Adeyɛ a ɛfa asɛm bi a wɔhwɛ so wɔ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ katee anaa adwene mu no.

Po asorɔkye: Ɛyɛ po mu asorɔkye kɛseɛ titire, titire nea ɛde ba asase ase nsõ anaa ɔhyew a ɛtɔ no.

Nokwaredi: Eyi kyerɛ sɛ aban no de nsɛm a ɛfata na ɛyɛ bere ano ma n’amanfoɔ wɔ nea ɛreyɛ ho.

Ɔkwan kɛseɛ: Ɛyɛ ɔkwan kɛseɛ a mpɛn pii no ɛka nkurow abien anaa nea ɛboro saa, hyɛn gyinabea ahorow, wimhyɛn gyinabea ahorow ne mmeae afoforo bom, a wɔkamfo kyerɛ sɛ ɛyɛ kwan a wɔfa so fa akwansin tenten ne nneɛma a wɔde fa so.

“Sikasɛm mu ahotɔ ne ahoɔden a wɔbɛsan de ama nkɔso a ɛka obiara ho ne mfaso a wɔde bɛka ho no bɛkura mu”

Nneɛma ne nnwuma a wɔde di dwuma: Sika dodow a aban de tua de yɛ n’adwuma na wɔde nnwuma ma ɔmanfoɔ.

Bo a Wɔde Ka Ho (VAT): Tow a wɔbɔ bere a wɔde bo ka ho nneɛma ne nnwuma ho wɔ nneɛma a wɔyɛ no fã biara mu.

Aduonu a Wɔahaw (V20): Wɔyɛ sikasɛm mu ɔhaw kɛse a wim nsakrae de ba wɔ aman sikasɛm so a wogye di sɛ aman a wim nsakrae betumi asɛe wɔn kɛse no gyina hɔ ma.

Ɔfã ketewa yɛ sikasɛm mu dwumadi beae a ɛyɛ mmeae akɛse no mu biako a wɔkyekyɛ ɔman bi sikasɛm mu dwumadi mu.

YouStart: Ɛyɛ kar a wɔnam so pɛ sɛ aban no de sika ne mfiridwuma mu mmoa bɛma mmerante ne mmabaa (a wɔadi mfe 18 kosi 40) ne nnwuma a mmerante di anim a wɔka kuw yi ho na ama wɔatumi afi ase akyekye na ama anyin wɔn ankasa nnwuma.

Mmeranteɛ Adwumayɛ Dwumadibea (YEA): Wɔhyehyɛɛ YEA wɔ Mmeranteɛ Adwumayɛ Mmara 2015 (Mmara 887) ase sɛ ɛbɛhwɛ nkɔsoɔ, nhyehyɛeɛ, sohwɛ ne adwumayɛ a wɔbɛma ayɛ mmerɛw wɔ adwumayɛ a wɔde bɛma mmabun ne nsɛm a ɛfa ho wɔ Ghana.

Kyerɛwtohɔ Mfeɛ aduonu mmienu. Wɔakora hokwan nyinaa so. Wontumi mfa saa nhoma yi fã biara nsie nhyehyɛe a wɔde gye nneɛma mu anaasɛ wɔmfa nkɔ kɔmputa so, mfiri so, mfonini a wɔatwe, nneɛma a wɔakyerɛw anaasɛ ɔkwan foforo so a wonni Sikasɛm Dwumadibea no kwan a wɔakyerɛw adi kan ma.
