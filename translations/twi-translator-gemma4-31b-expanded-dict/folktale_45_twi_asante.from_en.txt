Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

BERE A KOKOSAKI, ƆHWEHWƐMU, KASA WƆ ANANSESƐM MU KƆMA BONEKYEREFO, ƆHYƐN NO, ƆTE NO NO

hyɛn Afei n’adamfo no fii ase na wɔsan kɔɔ fie. Ɛno nti na yɛwɔ asɛm bi a ɛne sɛ, "Sɛ ɔkɔkɔɔ ka asɛm kyerɛ ɔkraman wɔ anansesɛm mu a, ɔte n’asɛm a ɛda hɔ no ase. "

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
