SƐNEA AKYIWADEƐ "KURONTO" NE "BURU" BAEƐ SƐ WƆBƐDI DWUMA Na wɔde akyiwadeɛ a wɔfrɛ no "Kuronto" no de di dwuma wɔ ɔkwan a ɛfata so. Na wɔde akyiwadeɛ a ɛyɛ fɛ de di dwuma so. Na wɔde akyiwadeɛ a ɛyɛ fɛ de di dwuma so.

Na abɔfoɔ bi wɔ hɔ a wɔfrɛ no Kwaku, ne yere Buruwa, na wɔn ho yɛ wɔn dɛm, wɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔyɛɛ asetena foforɔ. Kwaku, abɔfoɔ no, bere a ɔkɔɔ kwaeɛ no, ɔkunkum eku mprako bi, nanso bere a ɔde baa afie no, ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Eyi nni hɔ. " Enti ɔkɔɔ hɔ na ɔtɔn. Daa, bere biara a ɔkunkum biribi, na ɛte saa. Nanso ne yere no ankasa anhuu sika no, na ɔbisaa no sɛ, "Wode sika mma, na wode nam mma, na ɛyɛ dɛn na ɛte saa? " Ɔbuaeɛ sɛ, "Me kɔ, na nnipa ntɔ, na ɛyɛ mmerɛw, me tow gu. " Ɛda bi ɔkunkum biribi, na ɔde too kwan so, na ɔne ne yere kɔɔ n'akyi sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ no. Afei ɔhunuu sɛ ɔtɔn saa mprako no, ɔde sika no tɔɔ nsa kookoo a wɔfrɛ no dɔnto na ɔnom. Afei ne yere no dan kɔɔ fie. Ɛnkyɛeɛ, abɔfoɔ Kwaku nso baa hɔ. Bere a ɔduruu fie no, ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Anwummere, Buruwa
