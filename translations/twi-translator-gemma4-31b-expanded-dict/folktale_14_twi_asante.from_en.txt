Efuo, black colobus monkey no

Wɔka sɛ na Esono a ɔyɛ Elephant, Kwakuo a ɔyɛ Monkey, Anyinhima a ɔyɛ Putty-nosed Monkey, ne Efuo a ɔyɛ Black-Colobus Monkey te hɔ, na wɔka sɛ wɔbɛkɔ akuraa bi mu akɔhwehwɛ mmaa ama wɔaware. Afei wɔfii ase. Bere a wɔrekɔ no, wɔhyiaa dɔteɛ bi a ɛyɛ dɔteɛ. Putty-nosed Monkey no kae, "Gyina hɔ bere a merɛbɛbisa wo asɛm bi. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wɔ saa akwantu yi so a yɛrekɔ no, hena a ɔwɔ yɛn mu na ɔyɛ fɛ? " Ɔsɔno no kae sɛ, "Efuo na ɔyɛ Black-Colobus Monkey. " Kwakuo (monkey foforo no) kae sɛ, "Sɛ ɛte saa a, yɛnbɔ no, na yɛnfa no ntom dɔteɛ no mu. " Wɔ amanneɛ mu no, wɔbɔɔ no, na wɔde no kɔtoo dɔteɛ no mu, na wɔkɔɔ so. Afei Colobus Monkey no sɔree kɔɔ n’akyi nkakrankakra, na ɔkɔtenaa akuraa no midden-heap no so. Na nnipa no aaka no duruu akuraa no, wɔhunuu mmabaa a wɔahyia wɔ Gyedua dua no ase. Mmabaa no hunuu wɔn. Eyi kae sɛ, "Me pɛ eyi"; eyi kae sɛ, "Me pɛ eyi. " Na wɔwaree mmabaa no nyinaa. Na saa bere no, na ababaa bi wɔ hɔ a na ɔakɔ afuo, na ansa na ɔrekɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Mmeranteɛ no ba a, yi bi de to me nkyɛn. " Na bere a ɔsan baa afuo no, ɔbisaa ne maame sɛ, "Me deɛ ɛwɔ he? " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Bere a me kɔe no, na wɔakyekyɛ wɔn. " Ababaa no kae sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ hwee. " Na ɔyii plantain bi, na ɔrekɔto husks no wɔ midden-heap no so.

nwura-ntini

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
