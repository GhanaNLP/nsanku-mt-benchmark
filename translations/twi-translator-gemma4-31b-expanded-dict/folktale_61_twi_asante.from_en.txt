Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ BERE A OBI KƆ DAN BI MU ANADWO NO, NA ƐYƐ SUM

ka Now Ɔsono no kae sɛ, “Nna mmiɛnsa bi a atwam no, na mete hɔ, na me ho hyɛɛ aseɛ, na berɛ a mehwɛɛ baabi no—me mfa asɛm no nni dwuma—saa nkwaseɛ yi, nkwammoa nketewa no, akɔhyɛ (me ho) sɛ ɔbɛhwehwɛ nam a ɔbɛnam so adidi, na wanyin ayɛ kɛseɛ; na berɛ a metwe me nan so no, ɛkɔtoo Tamiriwa, Black-snail no. ” Ɔsoro Onyankopɔn kae sɛ, “Fa no kɔkyekyere no nnuadua so, na fa Tetea, nkwammoa nketewa no. ” Bere a Tetea bae no, ɔkae sɛ, “Agya, Palm-tree bi wɔ hɔ; bere a ɛwo no, ɛdi kan yɛ tuntum; bere a me, nkwammoa nketewa no, nam so no, ɛma ɛyɛ fɛ. Ɛda bi a atwam no, na yɛ aniberefoɔ

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
