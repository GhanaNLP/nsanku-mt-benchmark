Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐ WOREKƆ BAABIARA A, BERE A W’ABUSUA KA SƐ ƆNE WO BƐKƆ, ƐNDE KƆ NE NKYƐN

bere bi Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no anɔpa Ɔdan no panin kae sɛ, “Ɛnni hɔ. ” Abofra no kae sɛ, “Nana, me srɛ wo. ” Ɔdan no panin kae sɛ, “Ɛnni hɔ. ” Abofra no kae sɛ, “Nana, me srɛ wo. ” Ɔdan no panin kae sɛ, “Adɛn nti na abofra yi hyɛ me haw sɛnea ɛteɛ yi; yɛn panyin no awu, yɛn nsa nni hɔ; ɛyɛ da a ɛtɔ so awotwe; nanso sɛnea yɛbɛtumi ahwehwɛ no, yɛnni ne nufu. ” Ɔbaa bi a wadi mfeɛ pii teɛ wɔ nkyɛn; ɔkae sɛ, “Ma no bi. ” Ɔbaa no de aduan ne nam maa no. Bere a abofra no dii n’adidi wiee no, ɔkae sɛ, “Ɔdan no panin, meahu panyin no nufu; ɛfiri sɛ ɛnora mewi no, awudifoɔ bi maa me tɔɔ no osua ne suru mu sika kusuu. ” Ɔbaa no dwanee yiridi, yiridi, yiridi! na ɔkae mpanimfoɔ asɛm no. Wɔde abofra no kɔe, na ɔkɔkyerɛɛ wɔn nufu no. Wɔkɔfae, na wɔsie no yie. Wɔkae sɛ, “Afei wobɛdi panyin no so. ” Nti abofra no bɛyɛɛ panyin. Afei, bere a ne nuanom mpanin teɛ no, wɔbaa hɔ, na wɔkae sɛ wɔne no wɔ mogya mu abusuabɔ. Abofra no kae sɛ, “Wobɛtumi afi hɔ; me nnim mo, nti monkɔ. ” Na ɔmaa ne

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
