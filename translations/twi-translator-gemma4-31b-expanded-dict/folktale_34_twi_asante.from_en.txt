Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ ONIPA BIAKO NKYERƐ BAABI A OBI FOFORO FI MU BAE NO

Na bere bi na ɔbɛbɔfoɔ bi wɔ hɔ. Bere a ɔsɔre no, na ɔkɔ kwae kɔhwehwɛ abɔmmɔfoɔ sɛ ɔbɛkum, na ama ɔanya bi adi, na ɔanya bi nso atɔn. Afei, da bi no, ɔkɔɔ kwae; ɔtee abɔmmɔfoɔ Kokotee frɛɛ ne nua sɛ, "Kokotee Asamoa e! " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Yiw, me nua, yiw. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Adwumayɛ bere no adu, nti yɛnkɔ dadefoɔ no hɔ, sɛ ɔnyɛ dade no na ɔmfa nsi nnyinasoɔ, na sɛ yɛbɛtwa nnua bi a, yɛbɛtumi atwa. " Bere a abɔmmɔfoɔ no frɛɛ ne nuanom no, ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔhyɛɛ aseɛ na Bere a ɔpanyin no awie sɛ ɔde ne nsa ahyɛ nnuadua ne nnuadua no so no, ɔmaa ɔkasafoɔ no bɔɔ dade no, na ɔkaeɛ sɛ, “Memeneda, ɔbaa anaa ɔbarima, obiara nkɔ hɔ. ” Memeneda baeɛ, na anɔpa no ɔbaa bi a ɔyɛɛ nea ka kyerɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no sɛ, “Kɔyɛ mako, ne nkyene, ne nnuadua, ne baabi a mmoa no bɛpaa wɔn honam na wɔde guu hɔ; wo nso, sɛ wode kɔ hɔ a, fa mako no gu so, na sɛ mmoa no ba ha, na yɛkyere wɔn, na sɛ ebi dwane kɔfa wɔn honam, na wɔde gu so, bere a mako no yɛ wɔn ya, na wɔtow gu no, wɔbɛyɛ nkurɔfoɔ (bio). ” Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔɔ akyiri na ɔde ne ho siee hɔ. Na ɔtee sɛ wɔrefrɛ sɛ, “Kokotee Asamoa, Memeneda aba, yɛnkɔ. ” Enti wɔn nyinaa baeɛ. Wɔpaa wɔn honam, na wɔde guu hɔ. Afei, (one of the Pigs,) na ɔyɛ nhabannuru ho oduruyɛfoɔ no, na ɔwɔ wɔn mu, na ɔde ne honam guu baabi foforɔ. Ɔbɔfoɔ no hwɛɛ wɔn, na ɔmaa wɔkɔɔ akyiri. Ɔkɔɔ na ɔde honam no nyinaa guu mako a wɔayam no so, na ɔde guu asubɔnten no mu, ma nsuo no faa wɔn kɔeɛ. Nhabannurufoɔ no deɛ, a ɔde guu nkaeɛ no ho no, ɔbɔfoɔ no anhunu. Ɔbɔfoɔ no baa fie, na ɔpanyin no nso frɛɛ nkurɔfoɔ a wɔahyia wɔ dɔtefoɔ no hɔ no ma wɔbaeɛ Ɔhene no
