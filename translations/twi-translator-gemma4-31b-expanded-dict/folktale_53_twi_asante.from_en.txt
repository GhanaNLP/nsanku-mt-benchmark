Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

How

There once was Bere bi a saa nsɛm yi sii akyi no, adurufo bi bae akuraa no mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ah! a big town, and no people in it? ” Dinn! (Nnyigye biara nni hɔ gye cicada nnyigye.) Ɔkaa bio. Awiei koraa no, ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, “Me wɔ ha. ” Adurufo no kaa sɛ, “Dɛn na ɛyɛ asɛm; na dɛn nti na wote nko ara wɔ akuraa yi mu? ” Ɔbaa no bɔɔ no mmuae sɛ, “Wobɛbaa ha, (mepɛ sɛ me ne wo nni hwee), kɔ na me ntra me baabi. ” Adurufo no kaa sɛ, “Woyɛ abɔfo; wo na woadi nkurɔfo a wɔwɔ akuraa yi mu nyinaa, enti, sɛ woka a, mebɛkum wo ntɛm ara. ” Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, “Sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meka a, di kan kɔfa nkɔnsɔn kyekye me, na fa me kyekye me wɔ gyedua dua no ho. ” Adurufo no bae na ɔkyekyeree no, na ɔde no kyekyeree no wɔ gyedua dua no ho. Afei ɔbaa no ka kyerɛɛ no nea

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
