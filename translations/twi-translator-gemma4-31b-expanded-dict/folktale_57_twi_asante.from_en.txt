Abofra ne Kwasi ne Gynamoa

Na abofra bi wɔ hɔ, Kwasi Gyinamoa, na na ɔwɔ nua baawɔtwe, na wɔn Ɔbaa no Then Ɔbaa-nananom nana no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na wodidi? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Tiger-nuts. " Ɔkae sɛ, "fa bi ma me mɛdidi. " Ɔbuae sɛ, "yi me ho. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wobɛkɔ a. " Ɔbuae sɛ, "Dabi, merenkɔ. " Ɔyii no ho na ɔpuee, na ɔbaa no nana no rebu palm-nuts. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa palm-nut pestle no bɔɔ ɔbaa-nananom nana no kunkum no. Afei ɔyii ɔbaa-nananom nana no honam na ɔde hyɛɛ ne ho. Ɔbaa-nananom no san bae, ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na moyɛ fĩ saa? " Ɔbuae sɛ, "maku Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, mde ogya ahyɛ. " Saa asɛm yi yɛɛ dɛ ma ɔbaa-nananom no, na ɔsaa ne ho na ɔmaa ne nne so (na ɔtoo dwom): "Abofra, waku Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, da bi wobɛyɛ obi. "
