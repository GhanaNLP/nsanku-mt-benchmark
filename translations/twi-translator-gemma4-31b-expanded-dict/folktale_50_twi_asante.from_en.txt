Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

Abosom

Na ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ a ɔwoɔ mma du-baako; na da biara bere a ɔsɔre noa aduane no, mma no dii nyinaa. Wɔn maame annya biara nni. Afei ɔdwen ho kɛse, na ɔkɔɔ afuo no mu kɔbɔɔ Silk-cotton dua no amanneɛ sɛ, "Mɛsoma me mma du-baako mma wɔn ase ha mma wotwitwa pumpkins; na bere a wɔba no, twitwa wo nkorabata du-baako na wokum me mma no. " Silk-cotton dua no kaa sɛ, "Mete, na mɛyɛ ama wo. " Maame no firi hɔ kɔ "Asɛm yi deɛ, m’antumi nni mu, enti mɛfa mmofra yi de akɔma Ɔsoro-Nyame. "Ɛda akyi no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔkɔɔ kyerɛɛ Ɔsoro-Nyame ho asɛm nyinaa sɛ, "Mmofra bi a m’awo wɔn no, berɛ a mɛpɛ sɛ m’adi dumi no, mentumi nnya adeɛ bi nni (ɛfiri sɛ wɔdi ntɛm na wɔdi pii); ɛkɔm rekum me; enti mepɛ sɛ wɔnfa mmofra no mma na wɔnku wɔn, na m’anya adeɛ bi nni. "Ɔsoro-Nyame kaa sɛ, "Ɛno na ɛte saa? "Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "Mekasa ti bi a ne mu yɛ fitaa. "Ɔsoro-Nyame paw nkrɔfoɔ bi, na wɔkɔɔ tuu atɛ Ɔsoro-onipa no Dua du-baako kae, "Oh, Nana, mekae sɛ mankɔ da. " Owuo kae, "Na dɛn na ɛbɛyɛ da a wobɛda? " Dua du-baako kae, "Sɛ wokɔfa nkuku a ɛwɔ ntokuru kɔfa nsuo gu mu, na wɔ aduane bi ma me adidi a, ɛnde mebɛda. " Owuo maa Ɔkaa sɛ Dua Du-Baako

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom betumi agye sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no nso wobetumi akamfo me.
