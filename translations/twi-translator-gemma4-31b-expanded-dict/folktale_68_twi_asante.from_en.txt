Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

Ananse

mpɔnkɔnini Ɔpanyin Bere a Ananse kɔɔ mu no, ɔmaa ne dwom no kɔɔ soro: "Ananse, awerɛho, awerɛho, menya atoko biako de kɔgye akokɔ.

Ananse, awerɛho, awerɛho, me faraa akokɔ no de kɔgye ɔnimma.

Me faa ngewa kɔfaa nanom, me faa nanom kɔfaa funu, me faa funu no na me faa nkurɔfoɔ kurow bi mu.

Obiara kae sɛ, "Wote dwom a Ananse retoto no? " Ananse maa ne dwom no so bio pɛnkoro:

Ananse, awerɛho, awerɛho, manyaa atoko biako sɛ mebɛkɔ akɔnya akokɔ.

Ananse, awerɛho, awerɛho, me faraa akokɔ no de kɔgye ɔnimma.

Me faa ngewa kɔfaa nanom, me faa nanom kɔfaa funu, me faa funu no na me faa nkurɔfoɔ kurow bi mu.

Anansem duu wɔ Sorononon anim, na Sorononon bisaa Anansem nsɛm. Ananse kaa sɛ, "Atoko biako me nyae, sɛ mebɛka nkurow bi mufo; nkurow bi mufo wɔgyina hɔ. " Sorononon no kaa sɛ, "Kamfo no. " (Wɔteɛm,) "E! e! e! " Sorononon no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ me de ma wo sɛ aseda? " Mpɔkorɔ no kaa sɛ, "Me mpɛ hwee; nkyene so nko ara na me pɛ. " Sorononon no kaa sɛ, "Oh, oh! Me asɛm yi, wobɛwu wɔ ohia mu, wo a wokɔfae pii, nanso wose wopɛ hwee. " Ɛno nti na ohia rennya Anansem.

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
