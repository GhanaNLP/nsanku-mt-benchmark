Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

nothing is not there

Wɔka sɛ ɔpɔnkɔ bi ne ne yere tenaa hɔ, na na wɔwɔ mmofra baanu. Afei ɛna ne agya taa kɔ kwae mu da biara kɔkyere nam de ba ma wɔn saa mmofra yi. Da bi, wɔn maame ne agya ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "sɛ wosɔre a, ɛsɛ sɛ wosusuw na woka sɛ, ‘hwee nni hɔ, hwee nni hɔ. ’" Afei wɔkɔɔ kwae mu gyaa wɔn mma wɔ hɔ. Afei anadwo nkokɔ Dankwanta baa hɔ, na mmofra no suu.

Hwee nni hɔ a yɛsuro, Hwee nni hɔ a yɛsuro!

Anadwo-kɔkɔɔ no kae sɛ, "Hena ka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ wo bɛteɛm saa adeɛ? " Wɔn buae sɛ, "agya ne ɛna na wɔkae. " Anadwo-kɔkɔɔ no kae sɛ, "Wɔn nni ho asɛm, ɛsɛ sɛ woteɛm sɛ, ‘Mmerɛ no bɛsakra, mmerɛ no bɛsakra. ’ " Afei, saa mmofra no, wɔn maame ne wɔn papa san bae, na wɔtee wɔn mma nteɛm:

Mmere ɛbɛba no sesa, mmere ɛbɛba no sesa!

Wɔkae sɛ, "Hena na ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ wofrɛ nneɛma a ɛte saa? " Wɔbuae sɛ, "Ɔbarima bi kae yɛn. " Afei wɔn maame ne wɔn papa kae sɛ, "Ɛnsɛ sɛ wofra saa, ɛsɛ sɛ wofra sɛ, 'Hia biara nni baabiara, hia biara nni baabiara. ' " Wɔde nam maa nkokoraa no na wɔkɔe. Anadwo-kɔkɔɔ no san bae, na ɔayɛ akuturukwa mmienu. Mprempren baabi a nkokoraa no da no, na nnua mmienu gyina hɔ. Bere a anadwo-kɔkɔɔ no bae no, ɔbɔɔ nnua mmienu no, na ɔde akuturukwa no guu nnua no ntiatiaa no mu. Ɔka kyerɛɛ nkokoraa no sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ wosusuw sɛ, 'Mmerɛ no bɛsakra ntɛm, mmerɛ no bɛsakra ntɛm, ' na bere a wo maame ne wo papa bɛsan aba no, ɛsɛ sɛ woka sɛ ɛyɛ me na meka kyerɛ mo. " Anadwo-kɔkɔɔ no tuu na ɔtoo ne ho wɔ akuturukwa no biako no ano. Ɛnkyɛreeɛ, nkokoraa no maame ne papa san bae. Wɔkae sɛ, "Hena na ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ wofrɛ nneɛma a ɛte saa? " Anadwo-kɔkɔɔ no buae sɛ, "Me na. " Ɔbarima ɔpono no na ɔrebu ne ho sɛ ɔbɛkyere no, na anadwo-kɔkɔɔ no tuu ne ho na ɔpono no bɔɔ ne ho wɔ

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
