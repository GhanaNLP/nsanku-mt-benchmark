Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ WƆFRƐƐ SORO ONYAM NSƐM NO "NSƐM-NSƐM-NSƐM"

KWAKU ANANSE, Nkwakɔa, kɔɔ Soronum, Soronum, kɔtɔɔ Soronum nsɛm. Soronum kaa sɛ, "Wobɛtumi atɔ wɔn? " Nkwakɔa no kaa sɛ, "Mmom, mebɛtumi atɔ wɔn. " Soronum kaa sɛ, "Nkurow akɛseɛ ne tumi te sɛ Kokofu, Bekwai, Asumengya aba, nanso wɔantumi antɔ wɔn, na wo a woyɛ ɔbarima a onni panyin no, wose wobɛtumi? " Nkwakɔa no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ boɔ? " Soronum kaa sɛ, "Wɔntumi ntɔ wɔn gye sɛ Onini aboa Python, Osebo, Leopard, Mmoatia, Fairy, ne Mmoboro, Hornets. " Nkwakɔa no kaa sɛ, "Mebɛfa saa nneɛma yi nyinaa bi, na me nananom Nsia (Sixth-child) nso mɛka ho aka dɔm no mu. " Soronum kaa sɛ, "Kɔ fa wɔn na. " Nkwakɔa no baa hɔ na ɔbɔɔ ne maame amanneɛ sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ metɔ Soronum nsɛm, na Soronum se ɛsɛ sɛ meba Onini, Python, Osebo, Leopard, Mmoatia, Fairy, ne Mmoboro, Hornets, na meka sɛ mɛka manya Ɔyii Leopard no bae na ɔbaa; ɔkae sɛ, "Nkwasea, merɛfa wo akɔsesa Ahama-Onyankopɔn nsɛm. " Ɔmaa Leopard no kɔmaa Nyame, Ahama-Onyankopɔn. Ahama-Onyankopɔn no kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka, nea ɛka no da so ara ɛka. " Nkwammoa no bae, na ɔyɛɛ Akua ba (nnua a ɛyɛ tuntum a ɛyɛ tratraa), na ɔbɔɔ latex bi (a ɛfiri dua mu) na ɔde guu aboa no nipadua so, na ɔbɔɔ eto (yam a wɔayam), na ɔ Na ɔde asɛm no too wɔn anim, na ɔkae sɛ, “Ahemman akɛseɛ bi aba, na wɔantumi antɔ Ɔsoro Nyame nsɛm, nanso Kwaku Ananse, Spider, atumi atua ka; Mefiri ne nkyɛn gye Mmoboro, Hornets; Mefiri ne nkyɛn gye Mmoatia, Fairy; Mefiri ne nkyɛn gye Osebo, Leopard; Mefiri ne nkyɛn gye Onini, Python; na ne pɛ ne pɛ, Ananse de ne maame aka ne ho; saa nneɛma yi nyinaa da ha. ” Ɔkae sɛ, “To no nkamfo. ” “Eee! ” (na wɔteɛm). Ɔsoro Nyame kae sɛ, “Kwaku Ananse, ɛfiri nnɛ kɔsi da a ɛbɛba daa, me de me Ɔsoro Nyame nsɛm ma wo, kose! kose! kose! me nhyira, nhyira, nhyira. Ɛnyɛ bio yɛbɛfrɛ wɔn Ɔsoro Nyame nsɛm, na mmom yɛbɛfrɛ wɔn Ananse nsɛm. ”

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
