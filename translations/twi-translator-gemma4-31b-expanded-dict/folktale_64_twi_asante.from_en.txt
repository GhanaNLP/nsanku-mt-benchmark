Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ɛbaa

ANANSE O! ɔware Nsia, ɔsoro Nyame maame no babea, na ɔkɔɔ Nyame nkyɛn kɔkae sɛ, "W’awofo Nsia da a owu no, ba bɛka kyerɛ me da a wɔapaw ama amammerɛ a wɔde sie no. "

Ɛbɛyɛɛ nna mmiɛnsa akyi no, wɔbae ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ Nsia, a ɔyɛ ɔsoro-onum no, awu. Afei Ananse mogya-abusuafoɔ ne ‘Berekuo, King Jay, Kwakuo, mprako, ne Odwan, ɔnanom. Afei Ananse bɔɔ wɔn nkra sɛ, "Wɔaba aka me ayie ho amanneɛ, enti ɛsɛ sɛ mo ka me ho. " Enti Ananse maa wɔde nkra kɔmaa ɔsoro-onum no sɛ ɔreba guasodeɛ no. Afei ɔsoro-onum no maa nkurɔfoɔ nyinaa hyiaa, sɛnea ɔmanpanyin nhyiamu teɛ. Na Ananse kae sɛ, "Anwummerɛ a ɛbɛyɛ nwini no, me bɛba. " Mprako no yɛɛ kotoku, na ɔde ‘Berekuo, King Jay, Kwakuo, mprako, ne Odwan, ɔnanom guu mu, na ɔde kotoku no guu ne mmerɛ so, na ɔhyɛɛ ne ntama so. Afei ɔbɔɔ abɔdeɛ no nkra sɛ, "Sɛ medu ɔsoro-onum no hɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ mosu. " Bere a ɔduruu ɔsoro-onum no anim no, ‘Berekuo maa ne nne so:

ku

Kurukusu, ku! ku! ku! ni.

Na mprako no maa ne nne kɔɔ soro:

Kakum!

Na nguan no maa n’asɛm no so.

Me!

Eyi ne Kwaku Ananse amaneɛ no. Nnipa no nyinaa kae sɛ, "Wo ayɛ yie, ɔbarima bi wɔ hɔ a ɔtumi su su amaneɛ wɔ akwan horow mmiɛnsa so. " Bere a ɔwiee no, Ɔsoro-onum hwehwɛɛ ofie pa bi sɛ ɔbɛtena mu. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma daa adi no, Ɔsoro-onum noaa aduan pa na ɔde maa Ananse. Bere a Kwaku Ananse huu aduan yi no, ɔkae sɛ, "Mremma 'Berekuo biara, mremma Kwakuo biara, mremma Odwan biara, efisɛ madi mpoa bere tenten. " Enti Ananse dii, amfa ne mogya-nkurɔfoɔ biara. Afei, ɔne wɔn kɔɔ so kɔɔ so, na Ɔsoro-onum kae sɛ, "Yɛbɛkura nna nwɔtwe no ayi. " Nanso Ɔwan no kae sɛ, "Ɛkɔm rekyere me, mrenkɔ. " Kwakuo ne 'Berekuo kaa saa ara. Afei Ɔsoro-onum kɔɔ nkurɔfoɔ a wɔahyia no mu, na ɔmaa wɔbɔɔ Ananse amanneɛ sɛ, nneɛma pa a ɔyɛeɛ no da no, ɔbɛtumi ayɛ no bio, efisɛ ɔne ne mpanimfoɔ no hyiaa. Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
