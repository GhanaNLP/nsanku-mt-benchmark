Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐYƐ DƐN NA ANANSE MPƆNKƆNINI DWANE BERE A ƆWƆ NSUO NO ANI

KWAKU ANANSE, Nkwammoaa no, kɔkae Okraman, Ɔkraman no, sɛ, “Me pɛ sɛ yɛbɛkɔ akye nkuraa ketewa a yɛbɛtena mu. ” Ɔkraman no kae, “Me nso me pɛ saa. ” Ananse kae, “Sɛ Sunday-Adae bi tɔ a, Monday a ɛdi hɔ no, nkɔfa nkɔn-nkɔn; me nso bɛfa nkɔn-nkɔn na yɛbɛkɔ. Afei nso, me de nkukuo bɛhyɛ mu nsuo, na wo nso fa bi nso, efisɛ ebia baabi a yɛrekɔ no, nsuo nni hɔ biara. ” Ananse faa nsuo bi;

Me, Ananse Akuamoa, manya aboa bi a ɛfata mpo mɛtɔn no.

Eyi kraman a ɔyɛ nkwasea no nko na ɔbɛtɔn me.

Hena na mebɛnya a ɔbɛgye me anaa?

O, kwan, mgye me m’nkwa!

su

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
