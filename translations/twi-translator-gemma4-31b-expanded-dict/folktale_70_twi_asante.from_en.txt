Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

ANANSESƐM BI

Na bere bi mmeranteɛ baasa wɔ hɔ, na wɔn nyinaa dɔɔ ɔbea bi. Afei mmeranteɛ baasa yi fii Bontuku kɔɔ Cape Coast sɛ wɔrekɔtɔ nneɛma. Bere a wɔduruu Cape Coast no, na wɔn mu biako wɔ kyerɛw-kyerɛw. Ɔfaa kyerɛw-kyerɛw no, na ɔtwee nhaban bi (magic leaves) na ɔde guu kyerɛw-kyerɛw no so, na ɔhwɛɛ mu. Ɔhunuu sɛ wɔn dɔfo no wuwui, na ɔda apon a wɔabue no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Nnamfoɔ, yɛn dɔfo no wuwui. " Wɔn mu biako kae sɛ, "Mɛma yɛaduru Bontuku ntɛm ara sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a yɛbɛkɔsie no. " Nea ɔdi akyi no nso kae sɛ, "Sɛ wotumi ma yɛduru hɔ a, mɛma no asɔre ntɛm ara. " Afei biako kɔtwee aduru, na ɔde guu wɔn nkotodwe so, na wɔfiri hɔ. Mpɛn biako pɛ na wɔduruu hɔ. Foforo nso kɔtwee aduru na ɔde guu kɔbere mu; na ɔde ne nan-ntini guu aduru no mu, na ɔde guu amane no so. Ɔkae sɛ, "Amane, sɔre; amane, sɔre! " na ɛsɔree. Afei nnipa baasa yi mu, hena na ɔyɛɛ nea ɛyɛ papa?
