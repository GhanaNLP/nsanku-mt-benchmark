Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ ‘KRAMAN NO RENTUMI NSA NO HO WƆ NE KORƆNBƆ HO

There once was

Ɔkraman no Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya Hare, mmeeka wo wɔ saa nam yi ho. " Hare no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na wopɛ sɛ woka ho ka? " Kraman no buaeɛ sɛ, "Mekae sɛ, sen sɛ saa mprako yi bɛdi no, mmetua wo. " Hare no kaa sɛ, "Aane, ɛnyɛ saa! mmra anɔpa daakye. " Awe! nanso seesei kraman no maa no kɔɔ ne kɔn mu. Afei ɔhwee ase na ɔdaeɛ. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, anɔpa anɔpa paa, Hare no kaa sɛ, "Kraman, fa saa nam no. " Kraman no buaeɛ sɛ, "Ho, Agya Hare, adɛn nti wonte nea me ka kyerɛɛ wo nna yi sɛ meka wo ka? " Hare no buaeɛ sɛ, "Enti wadi no anaa? " Kraman no kaa sɛ, "Yiw! medii no, efisɛ metɔɔ no. " Hare no kaa sɛ, "Wo, kraman, korɔn a wɔfrɛ no korɔn no rentwam wo nkyɛn. "

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
