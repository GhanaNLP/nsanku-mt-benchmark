Wim-onyame Bere a ɔduruu Sun akuraa ase no, ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Wo papa se ɛsɛ sɛ wobɛba me nkyɛn. " Na Sun kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ yie, yɛbɛkɔ, nanso mma mfa biribi ma wo adidi. " Na Ananse kae sɛ, "Ho. " Na Sun kɔɔ na ɔkyeree ɔwan bi. Bere a ɔsan bae no, ɔka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, "Sɛ me papa ba ha a, anka ɔbɛhu nea mereyɛ, sɛ ɛyɛ yie, anaasɛ ɛyɛ bɔne, ɛnyɛ saa nyinaa no, ɔbɛhu; nanso ɛfiri sɛ wamma, na woaba, ɛte sɛ nea papa aba, enti, eyi na ɛwɔ ha, me ɔwan, mebɛkum ama wo adidi. " Na ɔkum ɔwan no na ɔsiesiee no fɛfɛɛfɛ maa nwansena no ɔnni. Na Ananse kae sɛ, "Yɛnkɔ dua bi a ɛhwe ase so. " Na bere a wɔakɔ hɔ no, na Ananse ka kyerɛɛ Sun sɛ, "Wo papa asɛnka bi ayɛ tuntum a ɛwɔ hɔ, na ɔpɛ sɛ wodi saa asɛnka no, na ɔatwe yam bi na ɛda hɔ, na sɛ wobehu sɛnea woka ne din a, ɔbɛfa asɛnka no ama wo, na wɔfrɛ no 'Kintinkyi'. Na sɛ wopɛ sɛ wwerɛ mfi ne din a, mebɛtwa simma tiaa bi ama wo, na mebɛyɛ mpintini simma bi a ɛne no bɛkɔ, na bere a wɔbɔ simma tiaa no ne mpintini sim

Na wɔfiri kɔɔ Ɔsoro-onyame kuro. Na seesei wɔduruu Ɔsram kuro na wɔde no kɔɔ, na wɔduruu Anadwo kuro na wɔde no kɔɔ. Wɔ kwan no nyinaa, wɔbɔɔ mpintini dwom. Na bere a wɔduruu kuro no akyi na Ananse hunuu ɔbarima bi no, ɔde no kɔɔ sɛ ɔnkɔka nkyerɛ Ɔsoro-onyame sɛ wɔreba. Na Ɔsoro-onyame no frɛɛ nhyiamu, na Ananse ne wɔn baa hɔ na wɔbɔɔ wɔn nsa. Na Ananse de asɛm no kyerɛɛ Ɔkasafoɔ no sɛ, "Ɔpanyin no asɛm a wɔde me kɔeɛ no, me de aba. " Na Ɔsoro-onyame no kae sɛ, "Me mma, nea enti a meyɛɛ no sɛ wɔnkɔfa mo ne: Mebuu asɛnka a ɛda hɔ no, na mebetwe yam a ɛda hɔ no nso; mebegye asɛnka yi na meama ɔno a ɔhunu na ɔfrɛ yam no. Ɛfiri sɛ me ba panyin ne Anadwo, ma ɔfrɛ no. " Na Anadwo kae sɛ, "Ɛfrɛ no 'Pona'. " Na nnipa no nyinaa kae sɛ, "Ye e e e. " Na Ɔsoro-onyame no kae sɛ, "Me ba a ɔto so abien ne Ɔsram, nti ma ɔka ne din. " Na Ɔsram kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ yam a wɔfrɛ no 'Asante'. " Wɔkae sɛ, "Ye e e e. " Ɔsoro-onyame no kae sɛ, "Me ba a ɔto so abiɛsa ne Owia, nti ma ɔfrɛ no. " Afei, me werɛ fi sɛ na asaw mu nnwom kɔ so:

na Ananse retwaa ne ho kɔɔ n’anim, na Owia sɔree gyinaa hɔ na ɔde ne nsa kuraa yam no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, ɛno deɛ, ɛfiri berɛ a me ne me papa nam nkyɛn na me yɛ ketewa no, na ɔka me din kyerɛ me, na mwerɛ; wɔfrɛ no 'Kintinkyi'. " Na mmusuakuw no bɔɔ nsa mprɛnsa, "E! E! E! " Na ne papa sɔree gyinaa hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ, "Wo, Anadwo, woyɛ ɔpanyin, nanso nsɛm a me ka kyerɛɛ wo no, woama wo ho werɛ afi, efisɛ wonsii wo adwene wɔ me nsɛm so; ɛno nti, (it is decreed) nneɛma bɔne nkutoo bɛyɛ wɔ w’abere mu. Na wo nso, Ɔsram, nsɛm a wo ne me nam kyerɛɛ wo no, wanni so, (it is decreed therefore) mmofra nkutoo bɛdi agorɔ wɔ w’abere mu. Na wo nso, Owia, bere a me kae nsɛm kyerɛɛ wo no, wowerɛɛ, wotiee me afotuo, enti wɔayɛ wo panyin; sɛ obiara wɔ asɛm bi a ɔbɛsiesie a, ma wɔnte no wɔ w’abere mu, afie mu nsɛm nkutoo, anwiase asɛm biara nni hɔ.
