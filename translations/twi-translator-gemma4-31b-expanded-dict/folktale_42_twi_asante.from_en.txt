Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ OSEBO, ƆPƐNKƆN NE ADOWA, ƆPƐNKƆN NO BƐYƐƐ NNAMFOƆ

OKUROTWIAMANSA (ɔman no mu hufo) na ɔte hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ ahwehwɛ obi a ɔne no nni adwene Bere a na ɔrekɔ no, na Adowa no da hɔ, ɔno nso nsa nni no. Ɔpono no kaa sɛ, "Oh, bere a edi kan no, sɛ meyɛ me ho nse a, mprempren mɛkɔ fie mɛkɔ akɔfa adaka bi na mɛsan aba. " Ɔpono no kɔɔ fie, ɔfaa ne ntade nyinaa, ɔde too hɔ, na ɔde adaka ne mfata kɔɔ nnuadua no ase. Ɔfaa antelope no, ɔde too adaka no mu, ɔtoo mu, ɔfaa no, na ɔde ne ti kɔe. Bere a ɔduruu fie no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Maame, me de no aba; bue dampon no ano ma me mfa no nto mu. " Opanin ketewa no buee no, na ɔde no too mu. Afei ɔpono no kaa kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Mekɔ Tall-grass-country kɔhwehwɛ onions, ne nkyene, ne ground ginger, na mefa, na yɛbɛfa Ad Mumbled in his nose so "Afei nso meayɛ wo nwanwa na meafa w’agyapade nyinaa. ” Ɔpɔnkɔ no kaa sɛ, “Mebɛhyia wo wɔ hɔ anaa? ” Duyker no kaa sɛ, “Wobɛfa me so mpo. ” Ɛnneɛ ɔpɔnkɔ no tuu ne ntaban, kiri! kiri! kiri! nanso duyker no nso tuu ne ho kɔɔ akyiri. Ɔkɔɔ ne fie mu na ɔtoo, bam! Afei ɔpɔnkɔ no gyinaa hɔ, ne boɔ bɛyɛɛ no dɛm; na ne twitwa kɔɔ nkyɛn nkyɛn fre! fre! fre! Na ontumi nnya asɛm biara nka. Awiei koraa no ɔka kyerɛɛ duyker no sɛ, "Meka dwoda din a wɔabara no, sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ woba, na yɛbɛyɛ nnamfoɔ. " Duyker no kaa sɛ, "Me nni hɔ sɛnea wopɛ pɛ, gye sɛ wobɛsiesie sɛ yɛbɛnom nyamefoɔ. " Ɔpɔnkɔ no kaa sɛ, "Me gye tom, na me nni hwee akyerɛ wo. " Duyker no baa hɔ sɛ ɔne ɔpɔnkɔ no bɛnom nyamefoɔ. Duyker no kaa kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Wɔnkyere me da; sɛ w’ahyia me a, na mehyɛɛ atuo mu a, fa me gyae. " Ɔpɔnkɔ no kaa sɛ, "Meate. " Na ɔpɔnkɔ no nso kae sɛ, "Wɔ kwan a wobɛfa so no mpo, me bɛfa me nsa, na meatwitwa so, na woahu sɛ me ne wo nni ntam ntam. " Ɛno nti, bere a ɔpɔnkɔ biara nni hɔ a, ɔnkyere duyker no. Bio nso, bere a atuo bi akyere duyker no, ɔkɔ hɔ na ɔma no gyae.

Eyi, m’asɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, anaa sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nsan ba me nkyɛn.
