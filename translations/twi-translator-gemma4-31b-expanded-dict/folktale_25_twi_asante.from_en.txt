Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (nea yɛbɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐ OBIARA SƐE ADE BI A WODE (WƆ AKWANHYIA MU) A, MGYE TUA WƆ NO HO

Wɔka sɛ bere bi na mmofra baanu a na wɔn maame awu na wɔn ho nni hɔ te ase. Na wɔreyɛ afahyɛ bi, na mmofra baanu no mu panin (Ata Panin) de ahwehwɛ a ɛsom bo ne sika nketenkete bi sii mmofra baanu no mu baa no, Ata Kuma, kɔɔ ne kɔn mu. Mmofra baanu no mu baa no ne ne nnamfo kae sɛ wɔrekɔ asubɔnten no ho sɛ wɔbɛhohoro. Bere a na wɔrehohoro no, ntoma a na sika nketenkete no hyɛ Ata Kuma kɔn mu no bubu, na ahwehwɛ no kɔ nsuo no mu. Sɛnea ɔhwehwɛɛ no, onhuu. Ɔsan baa fie bɛbɔɔ ne nuabea amanneɛ, na ɔkae sɛ, "Sika nketenkete no ayera. " Ata Panin (mmofra baanu no mu panin) kae sɛ, "Me nni ho adwenem sɛ ɛyera, na sɛ ɛte saa mpo a, biribi wɔ hɔ a mebɛgye na wobehu. " Ata Kuma (mmofra baanu no mu baa no) kae sɛ, "Dɛn na wobɛgye mfi me nkyɛn? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Mebɛgye wo ɔpɛpɔn nsa, Aserewa nkokɔ nketewa no ɔdan, nsuo a ɛyɛ ponponponponsa, nhaban a ɛyɛ baako pɛ, ne ɛlemu a ɔyɛ ɔhemmaa no dwensɔn. " Ata Kuma kae sɛ, "Mate. " Ata Kuma fii ase, na ɔbaa baabi bi a na dua biako wɔ hɔ. Bere a dua no huu no no, ɔkae sɛ, "Ata Kuma wɔ hɔ, ɔda kuro no akyi sɛ osu a ɛyɛ fɛ. " (Ata Kuma) kae sɛ, "Mea a meba ha ne sɛ, agya no ba yɛ Ata Panin, na ɛna no ba yɛ Ata Panin, na ɔde ahwehwɛ a ɛyɛ kɔkɔɔ hyɛɛ, na ɔde sii me kɔn mu, na ɛbubu na ɛyerae. " (Wɔ ha Ata Kuma bɔɔ dwom):

Ɔka sɛ ɔreka biribi firi me nkyɛn.

Ɔreka mefi me nkyɛn nhaban a ɛwɔ dua bi so a ɛso nhaban biako pɛ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔreka biribi afiri me nkyɛn.

Ɔreka nhaban bi fi me nkyɛn.

Ɔka sɛ ɔreka biribi afiri me nkyɛn.

Ɔreka ɔdan a Aserewa no.

Ɔka sɛ ɔreka biribi afiri me nkyɛn.

Ɔreka nsuo, redɔ ponponponponsa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔreka ade bi firi me nkyɛn. Ɔreka ɔpɛpɛn no agya.

Ɔka sɛ ɔreka biribi afiri me nkyɛn.

Ɔreka dua a ɛwɔ Ɔhemmaa ason kosi ntini.

Na ɛsɛ sɛ me yɛ dɛn, Agya, na menya bi?

Ɔka sɛ ɛwɔ hɔ abofra nketenkete, a ɔyɛ Akwasi Toboro Kojo ba.

Kojo Nsa.

Agya Elefant, ɛsɛ sɛ woyi wo ho fi hɔ, ɛsɛ sɛ wofiri hɔ.

Kojo Nsa a a.

Na nhaban biako a ɛwɔ dua no so no kae sɛ, “Na ɛno nti na woresu, ba bɛgye biribi. ” Na Ata Kuma kɔɔ ɔgyee (nhaban biako a ɛwɔ dua no so no) na ɔdaa no ase. Na ɔkɔɔ so na ɔhyiaa Aserewa anomaa bi. Na Aserewa anomaa no kae sɛ, “Ata Kuma yi a ɔda kuro no akyi no, obiara ka sɛ ɔte sɛ osu a ɛyɛ fɛ. ” Ata Kuma bɔɔ no mmuaeɛ sɛ, “Nea enti a meba ha ne sɛ, agya ba no yɛ Ata Panin biako, na ɛna ba no yɛ Ata Panin biako, na ɔde ahwehwɛ a ɛyɛ kɔkɔɔ hyɛɛ me ho, na ɛbɔɔ na ɛyerae. ” (Na Ata Kuma bɔɔ dwom wɔ ha):
