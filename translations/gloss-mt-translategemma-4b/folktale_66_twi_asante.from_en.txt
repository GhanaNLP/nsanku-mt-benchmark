WƆ berɛ bi, Kɔkɔkyinaka anomaa no kɔɔ Kwaku Ananse, Ananse, hwehwɛ bɔseɛ a ɛyɛ asuanu sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (£4). Ɛnneɛ, Kwaku Ananse ankasa nni bi, ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔɔ n’adamfo, Ntontom, Ntontom, sɛ ɔbɛgye bi na ɔde akɔma n’adamfo, Kɔkɔkyinaka anomaa no. Ɛda a atwam a Ntontom no de sii hɔ sɛ wɔbɛyɛ bɔseɛ no ntua no, na Ntontom no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, “Kɔfa me sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no mma me. ” Kwaku Ananse no buae sɛ, “Kɔkɔkyinaka anomaa no te Silk-cotton dua no so, nti kɔfa wo sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no. ” Ntontom no bisaa sɛ, “Ɛbɛyɛ dɛn na mebɛkɔ akɔfa no, abɔdeɛ no bɛtumi atutu. ” Ananse no buae sɛ, “Kɔkɔkɔ dua no so, na sɛ wuhu a , dua no bɛpae, ɛneɛ wode wo nsa aka Kɔkɔkyinaka anomaa no, na woanya wo sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no bio. ” Ntontom no kɔɔ so bɔɔ dua no. Ɛbɛduruu sɛ ɛbɛpae no, Kɔkɔkyinaka anomaa no firi hɔ kɔɔ so, pa bu bu bu! Ɛneɛ Ntontom no baeɛ, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Congratulations, Kwaku Ananse. ” Ɛno nti na yɛwɔ abebuo bi a ɛkɔ so sɛ, “Abɔdeɛ a wɔfrɛ no Kɔkɔkyinaka anomaa no, ɛnyɛ Kwaku Ananse na ɛbɛbɔɔ Ntontom sɛ wɔnkɔdwiri Silk-cotton-tree no. ” Wei deɛ, me abasɛm yi, mekaeɛ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ foforɔ, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
