SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ ƆPƐTƐ NO TENA FI SO BERE BIARA

NA Ɔpɛtɛ ne Kwaakwaadabi bi te hɔ da bi, na Kwaakwaadabi no kae sɛ, “Yɛn kɔ twitwa twene bi na yɛdi agorɔ wɔ so. ” Wɔn kɔɔ nwura no kwan so, na wɔhunuu silk-cotton dua bi. Ɔpɛtɛ no ara na ɔhunuu silk-cotton dua no, ɔkaa sɛ, “Eyi yɛ me dea, ɛyɛ me deɛ, me ne ɔpanin nti mebɛtwa nea ɛso yi. ” Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, “M’ate asɛm no. ” Kwaakwaadabi no nso, berɛ a ɔrehwɛ nneɛma no, ɔhunuu “Cedar” dua bi a ɛgyina hɔ no, ɔno nso kɔtwaa no. Wɔn baanu nyinaa kyerɛɛ akyene. Ɔpɛtɛ no bɔɔ ne twene no, ɛyɛɛ sɛɛ:

Futuro! Futuro! Me nsa aka (mine) ntɛm!

Ɔpɛtɛ Kwakye! Me nsa aka (mine) ntɛm!

Kwaakwaadabi no bɔɔ no, na ɛyɛɛ seesei:

Kwaakwaadabi, Kwa! Kwa!

Kwaakwaadabi, Kwa! Kwa!

Wɔkɔɔ so bɔɔ agorɔ no, na Sky-god no tee, na ɔde Akrɔma (to summon them) no baeɛ; na Akrɔma no baeɛ, (na wɔbɔɔ wɔn) "Berɛ a atwam! " Na wɔn nso buaeɛ sɛ, "Ame! " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sky-god no se ɔate dance-music bi wɔ ha nti, ɛsɛ sɛ mo de ɛno ba na mo bɔ no ma ɔte. " Akrɔma no de wɔn ho kɔɔ soro kɔɔ Sky-god no anim; na wɔbɔɔ, na wɔbɔɔ, na wɔbɔɔ, na Sky-god no s aid, "I have heard that it is sweet; the two nnwom nnyigyei are sweet, therefore I present you (each) with something. " Ɛneɛ na ɔpanyin no kɔgyee nnaka mmienu kɔtoo hɔ, ɛho akyi no yɛɛ fɛ paa sɛ obi bɛhwɛ; filth to ma sɛe saa mu no, ɛno na ɛyɛ fɛ. Ɔsoro-ɔhene no kaa asɛm sɛ, (kae sɛ), "Mo mmienu no, mo ba biara mma mo mma. " Ɔpɛtɛ no kaa sɛ, "Me ne ɔpanin nti, me kɔfa baako mma me. " Ɔkɔeɛ no, ɔyii nea ɛyɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ paa, berɛ a Kwaakwaadabi no nso gyee nea ɛyɛ dɔɔso paa. Wɔsɔreɛ no kɔɔ wɔn akuraa. Wɔkɔɔ hɔ bueeɛ no, Ɔpɛtɛ no, berɛ a ɔhwɛɛ ne mu no, atɛkyɛ fĩ. Kwaakwaadabi no, bere a ɔhwɛɛ mu no, sika kɔkɔɔ bɔɔ no sɛ ade biara. Ɛnkyɛeɛ koraa na messenger bi baeɛ na ɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔpɛtɛ no sɛ, "Mene sɛ wo maame wɔ mmara mu awu. " Ɔno (ɔpɛtɛ no) kae sɛ, "Kwaakwaadabi, ma me sikakɔkɔɔ pow baako mma mekyekyere me basa so sɛ mekɔ ayie. " Ɔnyaa sikakɔkɔɔ sin no na ɔkyekyeree so, na ɔkɔɔ ayie hɔ. Duruu hɔ no, ɔkaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ ɔkɔ fa ne ho; ɔtenaa aseɛ, nanso berɛ a ɔrebɛsɔre no, sikakɔkɔɔ sin no bɔɔ, na ɛyeraa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, Kwaakwaadabi no agyapadeɛ no ayera, na ɛbɛyɛ dɛn na mebɛtumi atena fie? " Ɛnneɛ, ɔhuruu fi no mu kɔhwehwɛɛ (sikakɔkɔɔ sin no). Ɛno nti na wobɛhu Ɔpɛtɛ, daa na ɔrekyinkyin, rekyinkyin, wɔ ne tiafi ho, efisɛ ɔrehwehwɛ Crow’s nugget no sɛ ɔde bɛba ne nkyɛn.

Ɛno nti, Mpanimfoɔ no ka sɛ, "Anibereɛ nni yie. "

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom na mo betumi afa no nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a mo bɛbɔ me (wɔ asɛmka no ho) no.
