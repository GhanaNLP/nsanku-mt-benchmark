Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SAA WƆN OSEBO, WƆN DABOƆ, NE WƆN ADOWA, ADOWA NO BƐYƐƐ NNAMFOFOƆ.

ƆKUROTWIAMANSA (He-who-holds-the-ɔman-in-ehu) na na ɔte hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ hwehwɛ obi a ɔremmɔ no (na ɔrennyae n’anim sɛ ɔbɛka akyerɛ no). Wɔ mmoa no nyinaa mu no, na ɛnyɛ biako pɛ na ɛtumi yɛe saa. Saa, na adowa ketewa no te hɔ; ɔkae sɛ, "Papa Daboɔ, wose me ne wo bɛyɛ (open and confessed) enemies? " Osebo, Daboɔ no, kae sɛ, "Ɛno ne nea mepɛ ara. " Ɛnneɛ wɔbɛyɛɛ (open and confessed) enemies. Daboɔ no bɔɔ nsa-wine. Adowa no kɔɔ so, anom nsa-wine no, na ɔbɔɔ adwene; wantumi annyina so, na ɔda nsa-tree no ase. Wɔ anwummere nwunu no, Daboɔ kyerɛɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwɛ n’agyapadeɛ no. Ɔkɔeɛ no, na Adowa anom ne nsa na ɛda hɔ a ɔdi nsa. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Well, you are the one who says that you and I shall be atamfo a wɔda adi pefee; adɛn nti, Esono mpo, Ɛsono, antumi annyɛ me ɔtamfo a ɔda adi pefee, akyinnyeɛ sɛ wode ɔhaw mu. " Ɛkɔɔ so sɛ Daboɔ no twitwaa nnuadewa, na ɔde nnuadewa bɛyɛɛ bɛnkyi, na ɔde Adowa no guu mu; na ɔde no kɔeɛ, na ɔde no kɔɔ n’etire so. Bere a na ɔreba na wadu bɛyɛ kwan-mu no, Adowa no ho adwo. Ɔhunuu sɛ Daboɔ no reka no. Adowa no de ne ani kyerɛɛ soro, na homa bi na ɛda hɔ. Adowa no gyee ne ho nsɔmu. Seesei ɔkasa ne nneɛma a yɛagyae mu no, na Adowa no mmoa bini wɔ palm-leaf basket no mu. Daboɔ anhu hwee a ɛrekɔ so; na ɔde no (sɛnea ɔteeɛ) kɔɔ fie. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Saa senior nkwaseafoɔ no, Adowa no, ɔkae sɛ ɔne me bɛyɛ atamfo a wɔagye atom, ɛnneɛ me de no aba; monyɛ no nnuadewa wɔ ogya so. " Daboɔ no maame no ka kyerɛɛ n’abofra no sɛ, "Soup (palm-nut) no ayɛ, ɛnneɛ monni no mma me. " Bere a Daboɔ no hwɛɛ kɛntɛn no (na ɛyɛ) kwa. Ɔkɔɔ hɔ dedaw, na nea ɛkaeɛ ne ne mmoa bini. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Maame, w’akɔ, na nea ɛka ne ne mmoa bini; yɛmfa wɔn nkɔto nkwan no mu. " Ɛnneɛ wɔde wɔn kɔɔ nkwan no mu, na wɔwiee aduannoa no nyinaa; na wɔtenaa aseɛ dii. Aberewaa no de ne nsateaa kɔɔ nkwan no mu na ɔperee no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, Kwadwo (ma Daboɔ awoda no, na ɛyɛ Ɛdwoada), na eyi ne ne mmoa bini pɛ, anka ne honam bɛyɛ dɛn, anka ɛbɛyɛ dɛɛdɛɛ sen tiger-nuts, ɛno nti sɛ ɔbɛtumi atena baabiara a, bɔ mmɔden sɛ wobɛfa no aba. " Na Daboɔ no de ne ho kɔɔ ne nnuadewa no so. Ɛberɛ a ɔrekɔ no, na Adowa no wɔ hɔ bio, ɔdɔm bio. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Oh, mfeɛ a edi kan no me yɛɛ me ho kwasea a, seesei mebɛkɔ fie akɔfa adaka ne san ba. " Daboɔ no kɔɔ fie, de ne ntaadeɛ no nyinaa guu hɔ, na ɔde adaka ne safeɛ no kɔɔ palm-tree no ase. Adowa no, ɔfaa no, de guu adaka no mu, kɔto mu, na ɔde etire kɔe. Bere a ɔduruu fie no, ɔkae sɛ, "Maame, me de no aba; monbue dampon (closed sleeping-room) no ma memfa no nka mu. " Awaabea no buee no, na ɔde no guu mu. Ɛneɛ Daboɔ no kaa ne maame sɛ, "Me rekɔ akwantu kɔ Tall-grass-country kɔpɛ gyeene, ne nkyene, ne akakaduro yamye, na yɛde akɔyɛ Adowa no ayɛ no stew a yɛbɛdi. " Daboɔ no de ne ho kɔɔ akwantu no mu. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, na ɔbaa panyin no rebɔ nnuaba-pɛpeɛm wɔ ɔpono bi mu, na berɛ a Adowa de aniwa bɛkaa ɛtokuro bi ho no, ɔhunuu aberewa. Ɔtwee no kae sɛ, "Abaawa-babaawa, me werɛ hye me, sɛ mebɛbue ama me a, mebɛyɛ nteɛm ama wo. " Ɔtwee no kae sɛ, "Wo, Ɔtwee, wobɛdwane. " Ɔtwee no buae sɛ, "May (the lesser-gods) Aserampon, ne Ta' Kese, ne Dwomo, ne Apeya, ne Konkroma, ne Ta' Kora, ne Krakye Dente, momma me, sɛ mebɛdwane bere a mobue me. Sɛ wopɛ a, to apono no nyinaa. " Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "M’ate asɛm. " Ɔto apono no, na ɔbuee dan no ɔpono no mu. Ɔtwee no bae, kuraa pestle no, na ɔbɔɔ palm-nuts no. Na aberewa no rebɛn sɛ ɔbɛbɔ fam no bi, bere a Ɔtwee no de pestle no bɛkaa n’etire no so, pe! na ɔwui. Ɔtwee no flayed off ne wedeɛ no, na ɔde ne ho akata so, na watwa ne honam no guu ogya so. Ɛnkyɛreeɛ bi na Kwadwo baeɛ. Ɔtwee no kaeɛ sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wotwee akyiri saa? Mebuee (the soup) no maa Ɔtwee no na mekum no, ha mpo ne honam wɔ ogya no so. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Yie, Apeafo. " Ɔno nso, ɔde ne nneɛma a ɔde baeɛ no, ɔde guu soup-stew no mu. Wɔawie aduannoa no, berɛ a wɔkɔɔ tena aseɛ sɛ wɔbɛdi no, Daboɔ no de dompe bi faa mu. Bere a ɔcrunch no, na ɛkɔɔ pu! pu! Ɔtwee no bɔɔ ne ho ɛhwene mu sɛ, "Ɛno ne wo maame nnompe a woredi no. " Daboɔ no kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dɛn na woka? " Ɔtwee no buae sɛ, "Me (me nko ara) mekae sɛ Ɔtwee yi mfeɛ dodo; ne nnompe no yɛ den. " Daboɔ no kae sɛ, "Ɛno teɛ, me mpo me ntumi nni no. " Seesei Daboɔ no de ne ano bɛduruu stew no mu, na ɛyɛɛ dede sɛ fEAO! fEAO! Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, "Ah, ɛno ne soup-stew a wɔde wo maame ti ayɛ no a wopue (wopue ano). " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dɛn na woka? " Ɔtwee no buae sɛ, "Na mekae sɛ, na me mmoa bini mpo (me dwen sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ), nanso ne honam no, wuhu ne dɛ? " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dɛn dɛn dɛn na ɛho nhia sɛ yɛka. " Seesei, na wɔadi aduane no. . Ɔtwee no bɛdanee fie hɔ; ɔka kyerɛɛ Daboɔ no sɛ, "Me kɔto mpopaho no gu mmeaeɛ a ɛyɛ mmeaeɛ. " Daboɔ no kae sɛ, "Kɔ, ɛnneɛ, dɛn na ɛyɛ me, ɛyɛ ɔbaa? " Ɔtwee no bɛduruu mmeaeɛ a ɛyɛ mmeaeɛ no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ho hɔ, Daboɔ! Ho hɔ, Daboɔ! Sɛ woyɛɛ kwasea a, sɛ woyɛɛ aboa kɛseɛ a, anka mebɛkum wo maame na meayɛ no soup-stew ama wo adi? " Daboɔ no kae sɛ, "So mehu wo (sɛ meba wo nkyɛn a)?" Ɔtwee no kae sɛ, "Wobɛtumi atwa me mpo. " Daboɔ no tuu mmirika, yiridi! yiridi! (na ɛyɛ ne anan ho nnyigyei), na Ɔtwee no nso hyɛɛ aseɛ tuu mmirika, prada! prada! prada! Ɔtwee no bɛduruu ne fie, na ɔyeraa ne dan mu. Bere a ɔsii fie no, wanyɛ kwan biara wɔ mu, a ɛne kwan bi a ɔde sii hɔ no ade a ɛda nkyɛn. Ɔtwee no baa mu a, ɔto ne dan no mu, bam! Mprempren, hwɛ sɛnea Daboɔ no bɛhwɛ no, wanhunuu kwan biara a Ɔtwee no faa so bɛduruu mu. Daboɔ no kɔɔ so frɛɛ mmoa no nyinaa sɛ, "Mommra, na yɛmmɛkyere Ɔtwee no mma me, kwasea saa na ɔde ayɛ me, me ntumi nka amaneɛbɔ no mma mo kosi sɛ mo bɛkyere no ama me. " Mmoa no nyinaa baa hɔ wɔ Ɔtwee no fie. Ɔtwee no tee sɛ mmoa no aba ne fie hɔ sɛ wɔrebɛkyere no, ɔde mako kɔkɔɔ, nkyene, ne gyeene guu mu, na ɔbɔɔ no guu hɔ. Mmoa no nyinaa gyinaa hɔ. Ɛneɛ Kwaadu no gyaee ne ho hwee aseɛ. Ɛno na mmoa no baeɛ na ɛhunuu sɛ biribi wɔ n’aniwa mu. Ɛno bi na ɛkaeɛ, na ɔbɔɔ ne nsateaa ahodoɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, “Oh, nnamfoɔ, monhwɛ nea Ɔkwakuo akɔfa aba. ” Wɔn mmoa no nyinaa baeɛ. Wɔn kɔn kɔn kɔn kɔn. Ɛno nti na Ɔkwakuo no, Kwaadu no, n’ani dɔɔ so saa. Seesei wɔkae sɛ, “Kokotee, wo deɛ, woyɛ nuntun daa, nuntun! nuntun! nso wobɛtumi akɔ fie hɔ. ” Ɔtwee no tee saa asɛm no, ɔkɔɔ ne sekkan no yɛɛ nnam, de ano a ɛyɛ nnam koraa. Bush-pig no tutuu ne ho kɔɔ fie hɔ. Ɛneɛ Ɔtwee no de ne sekkan no, ɔde too Bush-pig no ano, na ɔretwa. Bush-pig no kae sɛ, “Oh, Bush-pig Asamoa, ɔretwa me ano. ” Mmoa a wɔregyina n’akyi (na wɔnte nea ɔka no pefee) no kae sɛ, “Yie, Apeafo; fa wo aniwa so, na fa no ba. ” Kokotee, Bush-pig no, yii etire no; ɔne ne ano no rewo. Bere a mmoa no huu sɛ Bush-pig no ano atwa no, wɔfiri saa baabi no kɔeɛ, na yii! (na ɛyɛ wɔn nnipadɔm guanguan no nnyigyei.) Saa na Bush-pig no nyaa ne hwene kumpɔ. Seesei, bere a mmoadoma no adwane no, Osebo, Daboɔ no, anhu nea ɔbɛtumi ayɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Anyhow, I shall go nowhere else; here I remain for ever, until I die, unless I kill Adowa, the Ɔtwee. ” Adowa teɛ no, na ɔde Daboɔ maame wedeɛ no bɛkyekyeree ne nipadua mu, na ɔforo kɔɔ fie hɔ na ɔforo akyiri. Ɛneɛ na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔduruu Daboɔ no nkyɛn. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Oh, Kwadwo, what has made you thin like this; why distress yourself about a Ɔtwee? Yɛn kɔ fie na yɛn nsi yɛn baabi wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu, na yɛn san ba. ” Wɔn kɔɔ fie. Ɔtwee no kaa Daboɔ no sɛ, “Moyi mo agyapadeɛ no nyinaa mma me, na me kora so mma mo. ” Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, “Ɛno nyɛ adwene bɔne. ” Ɛneɛ na ɔyii ne nneɛma no nyinaa maa Ɔtwee no. Ɔtwee no nka wɔn nsa nni hɔ, ansa na ɔde wɔn (safely) akɔto hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ho there, Daboɔ! ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Yiw, ɔbroda, yiw! ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Sɛ woyɛɛ kwasea, sɛ woyɛɛ aboa kɛseɛ a, anka metumi akum wo maame na meyɛ no soup-stew ama wo adi? Sei bio me nso mekyere wo na megye wo agyapadeɛ nyinaa. ” Daboɔ no kae sɛ, “Sɛ meba hɔ a, mebɛhyia wo anaa? ” Ɔtwee no kae sɛ, “Wo bɛtumi atwa me mpo. ” Ɛneɛ Daboɔ no tuu mmirika (at him), kiri! kiri! kiri! nanso Ɔtwee no nso tuu mmirika. Ɔkɔɔ ne fie na ɔtoo ɔpono no mu, bam! Mprempren Daboɔ no gyinaa hɔ, ne kokoɔ no mu yɛɛ no kɔkɔɔ; na ne dua no nso bɔɔ nkyinkyin nkyinkyin (making a sound like) frɛ! frɛ! frɛ! Ɔanhu asɛmfua biara anka. Ɛduruu tenten no, ɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔtwee no sɛ, "Meka Yawoada din akyiwadeɛ no, ɛsɛ sɛ mo ba, na yɛbɛyɛ nnamfofoɔ. " Ɔtwee no kae sɛ, "Me mma, ɛmfa ho sɛ mo pɛ dodo, gye sɛ mo hyehyɛ sɛ yɛbɛnom anyame no. " Daboɔ no kae sɛ, "Me gye toom, ɛnneɛ me mma mo hwee. " Ɔtwee no ne Daboɔ no bɛnom anyame no. Ɔtwee no kaa no sɛ, "Mfa me nni da, sɛ wuhyia me a, sɛ mekyere me wɔ fidie mu a, gye me. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Meate. " Na Daboɔ no nso kaa sɛ, "Mema kwan a wobɛfa so no mpo, mebɛfa me nsa akye so, na ama woahunu sɛ me ne wo nni manso nea ɛde ba. " Ɛno nti, Daboɔ biara bɛba a, ɔnni akyi. Ɔtwee no. Afei nso, sɛ fidie bi akyere Ɔtwee a, ɔno (ɔtwee no) kɔgye no.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
