Yɛnnyɛ nea ɛyɛ nokware, yɛnnyɛ nea ɛyɛ nokware (sɛ yɛbɛka asɛm bi a, ɛyɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ YƐBƐHU NKRUMA, AGYINAMOA NO, DA VELVƐT SƆRESƆRE SO, BERE A ‘ƆKRAMAN, KRAMAN NO, BƐDA GYAADE GYA NO NSO NO NNAFUA SO.

WƆKYERƐ SƐ, ɛduruu berɛ bi a, na ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ a (was so unfortunate that) bere biara a ɔbɛma abofra awoɔ no, ɛyɛeɛ no wuwu. Ɛno nti, ɔde ne ho kɔɔ hɔ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔbisa abosomfoɔ no bi, na waka akyerɛ no sɛ ɔpɛ abofra. Lesser-god no kae sɛ, "Me bɛma wo baako, nanso abofra no deɛ, adwuma biara a ɔbɛyɛ no, ɛbɛyɛ sɛ ɛka bɛto wo so, nanso da bi, ɔbɛtua wo ka. " Ɛnyɛ nna mmienu, ɛnyɛ nna mmiensa (bere a wɔkɔɔ lesser-god no nkyɛn) na ɔbaa no dwoeɛ. Ɔde awoɔ bi maa no—spider-story abofra bi na ɛyɛeɛ—ɛfiri sɛ na ɛnkyɛeɛ. Ɛnkyɛeɛ no, ne ba no nyinii bɛyɛɛ aberante fɛfɛ. Ɛdii da bi a na ɔne ne maame wɔ hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ, "Maame, ma me sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma, na mekɔ po no ano kɔtɔ nkyene. " Saa maame no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Asuanu" (i. e. £4). Na maame no de no maa no, na ɔde kɔɔ akwantu no mu. Mprempren, berɛ a na ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa obarima bi ne ɔkraman a ne ho asonsono. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mfa no mmra sɛ mebɛtumi atɔ. " Kraman no panyin kaa sɛ, "Wuntumi ntɔ. " Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dɛn? " Kraman no panyin kaa sɛ, "Asuanu bɛyɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma " (i. e. £4). Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dɛn (me) deɛ, fa saa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma yi. " Ɔgyee kraman no, na ɔde no baa fie. Bere a ɔsan bae no, ne maame kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woantumi ankɔ beae a obi rekɔ? " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Me de sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no kɔtɔɔ kraman. " Ne maame kae sɛ, "Ho! " Ɛnneɛ na wɔte hɔ no, na ɛbɛyɛ ɔsrane baako. Ɔkae sɛ, "Mama, ma me sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma mma me sɛ mekɔ dwadie. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wo deɛ, sɛnea ɛteɛ no, wode sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no nko ara na wobɛtutu akɔ, nanso dɛn na wopɛ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Asuanu ne suru sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma a ɛyɛ pɛ" (i.e. £5). Ɔkae sɛ, "Gye no. " Ɛneɛ na ɔde ne ho kɔɔ dwadie-kwan no so. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhyiaa obarima bi a ɔde agyinamoa bi kura. Ɔkae sɛ, "Obarima, fa saa aboa a ɛtaa hwe fam wɔ ne anan so no, ma me ntɔ no. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me kɔto me dan mu a, me anan so nkura me, saa sianteɛ no me tɔeɛ. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me srɛ mo ma me ma. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Muntumi ntɔ. " Saa abofra no kae sɛ, "Ɛbɛyɛ dɛn na wobɛfa? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Asuanu ne suru sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛno nti na wose me ntumi ntɔ; Ɛha, fa kɔ. " Saa abofra no gyee agyinamoa no, na ɔde kɔɔ fie. Bere a ɔduruu fie no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Mama, hwɛ nea me de aba yi. " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ, ɛno ara na wɔkae sɛ ɛbɛba. " Saa abofra no da so wɔ fie. Ɛbɛyɛɛ bɛyɛ aduanan akyi, berɛ a ɔkasa kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Ma me sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma mma me kɔ dwadie. " Ɔmaame no kaa sɛ, "sika a mewɔ no nyinaa asɛe, ade a ɛda nkyɛn ne Asuasa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. e. £6); me de eyi ma wo, na wo kɔ, na woamfa nni nneɛma a, ɛno ne awieeɛ a eguadwa yi wɔ. " Saa abofra no kaa sɛ, "Meate. " Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔde ne kotokuo no kɔeɛ, na ɔkɔɔ so pa! Sɛnea ɔrekɔ no, ɔne asanteni bi hyiaa, na ɔde aborɔnoma bi rekɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Adamfo, fa wo abɔdeɛ no ba, sɛ mebɛtumi atɔ. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me ntɔn, efisɛ me werɛ hyɛ me. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me bɛtɔ. " Abɔdeɛ no panyin no kaa sɛ, "Me rentɔn, efisɛ me nim nea ɛbɛtumi ayɛ me. " Abɔdeɛ no abofra no kaa sɛ, "Oh, ma me. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "So wubetumi atɔ anaa? " Abɔdeɛ no abofra no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Asuasa mu sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma bi. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Do you suppose because of that I would not buy it, here is the nkaebɔ dodoɔ. " Ɔde anomaa berebuw no bae. Ne maame kae sɛ, "Eyi adane yie sen kan no, ɛno na wode aba? " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Nanso, eyi na meba. " Seesei (da bi) na abofra no te hɔ (wɔ fie), bere a Aborɔnoma no frɛɛ no sɛ, "Bra. " Bere a ɔkɔɔ no so no, Aborɔnoma no bɔɔ no sɛ, "Me ho akuraa so me yɛ ɔhene, na mebɛkɔ akwantu bi, ɛberɛ a ɔbarima bi bɛbɔɔ me. " ame and seized nsɔmu of me, when you, out of your ayamye, you, too, bought me, and now I beseech you, if you will only take me back to my kuro, then they will thank you greatly. " The abofra said, "You are telling me atorosɛm, you will run away. " Aborɔnoma no said, "If you can't see your kwan to do as I ask, then take a hama a etwa to no, and tie it to my nan, and take me along. " The abofra took a hama a etwa to no and fastened no ne ne nan, na ɔdii n’akyi dwoodwoo, kosi sɛ wɔduruu Pigeon no kuro no ho. Bere a wɔduruu kuro no ho skɛɛte ahorow no, na mmofra no redi nte. Bere a wɔhunuu no, wɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛneɛ Ɔhene! Ɛnneɛ Ɔhene no! " Wɔn mu baako tuu mmirika kɔka Korenti ɔhene no, nanso wɔkyeree no, na wɔtwaa ne menem sɛ, "Moyɛ adeɛ a ɛma yɛn nkae yɛn awerɛhoɔ a ɛba akyire. " Nanso foforɔ bi nso de amanneɛbɔ koro no ara kɔe. Na seesei Akwamuhene no kae sɛ, "Mo, Gyaasehene, mo ankasa mo kɔhwɛ nea eyi kɔ so. " Ɔkɔhwɛeɛ, na ɔsan baeɛ. Ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, ɛyɛ nokware. " Ɛneɛ wɔnyaa mpa a wɔasɛn no ne ahennneɛma no kɔɔ Ɔhene no nkyɛn kɔɔ ne fie. Wɔka kyerɛɛ abusuakuo no nyinaa amaneɛbɔ no sɛnea ɔrehyɛ akwantu no, ne sɛnea onipa bi kyeree no, ne sɛnea saa abofra yi de ayamye no atɔ no, ne sɛnea da bi a ɔde no baa fie. Mpanimfoɔ ne mmabun nyinaa sɔree na wɔdaa abofra no ase. Ɔhemmaa no de nsuo-pot a ɛhyɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no baeɛ; mpanimfoɔ no nyinaa, nsuo-pot a ɛhyɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no, biara. Ɔhene no ankasa de ne nsa kɔhwɛɛ ne ho, na ɔdane kawa bi maa no. Na ɔkae sɛ, "Mfa kawa yi, na nea wopɛ biara, kawa yi bɛma wo. " Ɔkae sɛ, "M'ate asɛm. " Na ɔde kawa no kɔɔ n'akuraa hɔ; na ɔde sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma ne kawa no kyerɛɛ ne maame. Ɛneɛ na maame no kaa sɛ, "Akwaaba Aku, akwaaba Aku. " Kan no, berɛ a na w'adi ne sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no "burn" no, na ɔsan firi n'akwantu mu no, na ɔbɛkyia ne maame no, na ɔnni no mmuaeɛ. Na ɔde amaneɛbɔ no maa ne maame no sɛ, "Moahunu sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma ne kawa yi, mebɛkɔ akɔsi akuraa kɛseɛ bi ama yɛn atena mu. " Saa maame no kaa sɛ, "Hyɛ wo aniwa den (i.e. Sɔ wo ho mmɔden sɛ wobɛyɛ saa. " Saa abofra no yii ne ho kɔɔ nwura no mu, ɔtwee ne ho firii kawa no so de sii fam, ɔkaa sɛ, "Kawa, fa kwaeɛ ne nwura yi nyinaa nkyerɛ me. " Na baabi no nyinaa yɛɛ kwan. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Boaboa nea wode kyerɛɛ no nyinaa ano bɛyɛ eboɔ. " Na ɛyɛɛ saa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Yɛ afie. " Na ɛyɛɛ afie bebree. Ɔkae sɛ, "Kawa, ma nnipa mmra bɛtena saa afie yi. " Na nnipa baeɛ. Ɔyɛɛ ne maame Ɔhemmaa; ɔno nso bɛyɛɛ ɔhemmaa. Seesei (i.e. na asɛm bi wɔ hɔ a me werɛ fi sɛ mekae), Kwaku Ananse, Ananse no, na ɔyɛ wɔn adamfo paa. Ɛda bi a na ɔte hɔ (wɔ n’akuraa foforɔ so) no, Kwaku Ananse tu kwan bɛduruu saa mmeranteɛ yi nkurase. (Bere a ɔduruu hɔ no,) ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, little mother's abofra, little father's abofra, woanya nneɛma a ɛduruu wo so, na wo mmu wo ho bio sɛ wobɛhwɛ me, nanso dɛn na ɛbaee sɛ ɛde eyi bae? " Ɛneɛ abofra no kyerɛɛ no amaneɛbɔ no nyinaa. Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Me kɔ me akuraa na meba. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ n’akuraa hɔ, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nua ba baa sɛ, "Me de wo bɛma me adamfo baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ, na wode bobesa fitaa yi bɛkɔ ama no na woatua adwene na woayɛ nea ɔbɛka kyerɛ wo biara; na ɛsɛ sɛ wode kokoam bɔ mmɔden sɛ wode wo nsa bɛka saa kawa no so. " Saa ababaawa no kɔɔ akuraa hɔ, na wɔkɔɔ ɔbarima no akuraa hɔ. Saa ɔbarima no kaa no sɛ, "As for this, I shall see to it, that you do not go back again, for you must stay with me three days before you return. " Saa ababaawa no kaa sɛ, "I have heard. " Mprempren na ɔne ababaawa no wɔ hɔ bom, na ɔbarima no kɔhwie ne ho; na ɔhwe kawa no kɔtoo ɛpono so; na ababaawa no faa no, na wɔde no kɔɔ wofa Kwaku Ananse nkyɛn. Kwaku Ananse nka ne nsa so no, ɔyɛɛ dwumadie (de kawa) sɛ ɔbɛyɛ kuropɔn. Seesei, ɔbarima no, bere a ɔbɛhwehwɛ ne kawa no, na ɔnnyaa no. Ɔbɛtee sɛ Ananse no ayɛ kuropɔn a ɛkyɛn ne deɛ. Ɛneɛ na ɔkɔɔ so kɔɔ asase so kɔhwehwɛɛ ɛsoro deɛ bi. Ananse abarimaa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ananse abusuani a ɔbaa wo nkyɛn no, wafa no akɔma wofa. Ananse nso kɔɔ nkyerɛaseɛ (the lesser-god and it was revealed to him) sɛ Nkruma, Agyinamoa no, ne Kraman, Ɔkraman no, wɔbɛsoma wɔn (to recover the kawa) no, ɛno nti na ɔkɔɔ kɔgyee aduro a wɔde bɛsa nam no, na ɔde bɛsi kwan no so, na mmoa no rekɔ no, sɛ wɔfa bi di a, wɔrentumi nni hɔ bio. Agyinamoa ne Kraman no, ɔbarima no baa fie, na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Mprempren berɛ no aba sɛ meka mekyerɛ mo sianteɛ a mekyerɛɛ mo, (na ɛneɛ ɛyɛ); adeɛ bi a ɛyɛ me deɛ no ayera, na wɔse ɛwɔ Ananse agyapadeɛ mu, nanso ɛwɔ adaka bi a ɛwɔ ahomegyeɛ no nyinaa mfinimfini. " Na bio, wɔka sɛ ɔde aduro a ɛne odwan honam afra no guu kwan so, ɛnneɛ sɛ wodu hɔ a, nnye no nni, huruw ne so. " Kraman no kaa sɛ, "Agyinamoa, wote aseɛ, woyɛ nea ɛbɛdi no. " Agyinamoa no kaa sɛ, "Wugyae, woyɛ a wode ɛhwene kɔ hwamhwam ne hwamhwam no. " Ɛneɛ wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kasaa pii, na wɔn wura no kaa sɛ, "Ɛno 's all right, bɛyɛ... " off. " Mmoa mmienu no de wɔn ho kɔɔ so, na wɔrekɔ. Afei na ɛka berɛ tiawa bi a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔduru baabi a nam no wɔ no, berɛ a Kraman no "tee" nam no hua no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Agyinamoa, me yafunu mu yɛ me yaw, na me ntumi nkɔ so. " Agyinamoa no kae sɛ, "Yɛnkɔ so, eguadwa no nsa ho hia. " (Kraman) no kae sɛ, "Agyinamoa, me ntumi. " Agyinamoa no kɔɔ so. Kraman no kɔɔ nea ɛhɔ nam no; ɔkyeɛ nam no nyinaa; ɛhɔ na ɔda; ɔantumi ankɔ so bio. Agyinamoa no bɛduruu Ananse akuraa no, na ɛda dan Ananse nna mu, wɔ silin (ɛwɔ dan no so). Ɛhɔ na ɔda, ɔhunuu nam bi a ɛretwam. Kyeeka! ɛduruu etire no so. (Akura no) kaa sɛ, "Mɛnkyere me, dɛn ne asɛm? " Agyinamoa no buae sɛ, "Me wura no nsaano aduru no ayera, na wɔka sɛ ɛda Ananse adaka a ɛgyina ahomegyeɛ (nnaka) no mfinimfini no mu, na sɛ wotumi kɔfa mma me a, ɛnneɛ mebɛma wo kwan. " Akura no kaa sɛ, "Metumi. " Agyinamoa no kaa sɛ, "Susu sɛ mema wo kwan, na wofiri kɔ na woamfa amma, nanso wuguan? " Akura no kae sɛ, "Sɛ wopɛ a, fa hama a etwa to no kyekyere me asene ho. " Agyinamoa no faa hama a etwa to no na ɔkyekyeree ne asene ho. Ɛneɛ Akura no kɔɔ Spider no dan mu, na ɔkɔkɔɔ ɛtokuro wɔ adaka a ɛgyina ahomegyeɛ no nyinaa mfinimfini no mu. Ɛtwee mmueano bi, na ɛyɛɛ kɛseɛ. Ɔtwaa mu kɔɔ kawa a ɔde bɛkɔ Agyinamoa no nkyɛn. Agyinamoa no anka ne nsa so, ankyɛ na ɔtutuu kɔɔ Kraman so, na ɔhɔ na na ɔda. Ɔkae sɛ, "Wɔda so ara nna hɔ, na ɛhe na saa nam no wɔ? " Kraman no kae sɛ, "Oh, mehuu (nea ɛbɛyɛɛ no), ebia nnipa a wɔwɔ ne ho no baeɛ na wɔgyee no; nanso ɛhe na kawa no wɔ? " Agyinamoa no kae sɛ, "Ɛha na ɛwɔ. " Kraman no kae sɛ, "Wɔka sɛ asubonten a ɛda kwan no so no wɔ nsuyiri mu, na sɛnea woyɛ no, sɛ wotwa nsuo no a, ɛbɛyɛ sɛ (wɔn kawa) no bɛhwe fam, ɛnneɛ... ma me, ɛno deɛ, me deɛ, wunim, me de nsuo no ani kwan. " Agyinamoa no kae sɛ, "Ɛno 's so, fa (no). " Wɔduruu asubonten no, na Kraman no huri kɔɔ asuwa no mu. Saa ara na Agyinamoa no nso yɛe. Ɛno ara na Agyinamoa no twaa nsuo no so. Kraman no nso, ɔduruu asuawa no mfinimfini na ɔbɛyɛɛ dɔ, na ɔbɛduruu sɛ ɔbɛfa ahomegye kɛse a emu dɔ no, na kawa no fi ne ano hwee nsuo no mu. Ɔtwaa mu baa nea Agyinamoa no wɔ no. Agyinamoa no kae sɛ, "Ɛhe na saa kawa no wɔ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛhwe fam (out of my ano) kɔɔ nsuo no mu. " Agyinamoa no tuu mmirika kɔɔ nsuo no mu, ɔhunuu apataa kɛseɛ bi a ɛretwam. Na Agyinamoa no kyeree no. Apataa no kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dɛn? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me kawa no hwe fam kɔɔ asubonten yi mu, nti sɛ mɛpɛ ɔhaw a, ma me deɛ ɛyɛ me deɛ yi me sesei. Sɛ me mma me a, mebɛkum wo ntɛm ara. " Apataa no kae sɛ, "Momma yɛn kɔ asubonten ano ma me mma wo deɛ ɛyɛ wo dea. " Bere a wɔn baanu nyinaa duu sikakorabea hɔ no, Apataa no yii ne ho, na ne kawa no baa adi. Agyinamoa no gyee no baeɛ kyerɛɛ Kraman. Kraman no kae sɛ, "Papa, me srɛ wo, sɛ wukɔ a, nka nea ɛkɔɔ so no ho asɛm. " Agyinamoa no kuraa n’ano to mu. Wɔduruu fie no, na Agyinamoa no ka ne wura no ne nyinaa sɛnea ɛkɔɔ so no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Esiane sɛ Kraman no nam a ɔde bɛdidi no bere a yɛrekɔ no nti, ɔbɛyɛɛ nea ɔntumi nkɔ akyiri bio. Na bio, na mekɔfa kawa no na meba no, Kraman no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, bere a ɔtwaa asubonten bi so a ɔtwaa ne ani so no, ɛsɛ sɛ mema kawa no ma no. Mehyɛɛ no ma no, na ɔde guu asubonten no mu. Ɛyɛɛ kakra akyi na meka me nsa so bio. " Ayeyi ma Agyinamoa. " Na nnipa a wɔwɔ hɔ no nyinaa kae sɛ, "Ayeyi ma Agyinamoa. " Na wɔteɛm sɛ, "E! " Ɛneɛ ɔhene no kae sɛ, "Mo, Agyinamoa, aduane ayamye biara a meredi no, mebɛhwɛ sɛ mebɛbubu bi ato mo asanka ketewa so. Sɛ kɛtɛ biara a mebɛda so no, (mebɛda so kɛkɛ) sɛ mo da so bi. Sɛ wo deɛ, Dog, wobɛda nnyansramma a ɛredɛ so anadwo nwunu bɛba a. Sɛ abaa bɔ nko ara, folk bɛbɔ wo. Ɛno nti, nna nyinaa wobɛhu Agyinamoa no, ɔnna hɔ nni a, ɔda kɛtɛ a ɛye pa ara so. Afei nso, sɛ wuhyɛ aduane bi gu fam ma no a, ɔrenni (gye sɛ ɛfiri kɛtɛ so). Nanso Kraman deɛ, daa yɛbɛhu no sɛ ɔda kawa no so wɔ court-yard no nsõ a ɛyɛ adeɛ no. Ɛno nko ara na wobehu no sɛ wɔrebɔ no, ɔbɛteɛm sɛ "Kao! " Ɛyɛ akawnto a ɛwɔ berɛ a wɔde wɔn kɔeɛ (on this eguadwa of the kawa) no.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom na mo betumi afa no nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a mo bɛbɔ me (wɔ asɛmka no ho) no.
