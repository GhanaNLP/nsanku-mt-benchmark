Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ ABƆFOƆ NO DI HIA NO

ƆBƆFOƆ (bere bi) kɔgyee tuo aduru ne kannifoɔ nsa adwumayɛbea ahoroɔ mmienu sɛ wɔmmɛdi otuo ano aba, wɔ ayeyi, na ɔne ne yere ne abofra ketewa kɔɔ nwura no mu wɔ ahyɛdeɛ sɛ wɔnkum nam. Na ɔsii ɔbɔfoɔ adan bi na ɛmu na ɔne ne yere ne abofra no tenaa. Berɛ a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔkɔɔ nwura no mu. Berɛ a ɔhunuu aboa kɛseɛ bi na ɔtow ne tuo no kɔɔ no so no, ɔhwereɛ. Ɛda biara na ɛte saa, bɛyɛ nnafoɔ mmienu a ɛtɔ so awotwe. Na ne yere ka sɛ, “Me nni nam biara nni, mebɛkɔ fie. ” Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔɔ nwura no ho akyi no, ne yere ne ne abofra no nso kɔɔ wɔn akuraa. Ɛno na ɛkae sɛ tuo baako pɛ na ɛwɔ hɔ. Mprempren me werɛ fi sɛ mekae sɛ saa ɔbɔfoɔ no din ne Gyekye, na nso ne ne yere no din ne Boniaye-kae-dabi (a ɛkyerɛ sɛ, Ebi-da-a-dɔfoɔ-no-bɛkae). Bere a ɔbɔfoɔ no rekyinkyin no, na wadu (kɔ) beae bi a ɛwɔ odum-dua bi ase, na ɛhɔ na ɛyɛ beae fɛfɛ. Ɛhɔ na ɔbɔfoɔ no dwen ne ho etire no, na ɔkaeɛ sɛ (kyerɛɛ ne ho), “Sɛ mekɔ fie a, wɔbɛkyerɛ sɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no yɛ powder no, na me nni bi, ɛnneɛ mebɛfa saa tuo yi na mebɔ me ho menem sɛ mebɛwu. ” Ɛno ara na asɛm bi a wɔfrɛ no no bɛkae no sɛ, “Creator’s ɔbɔfoɔ. ” Na ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Papa. ” Na asɛm no kaa sɛ, “Dɛn nti na w’aniwa ayɛ wo kɔkɔɔ saa? sesei na ɔwɔ, a wɔfrɛ no ‘Go-and-spoil-the-house’, anka ɛwɔ wo fie hɔ a, anka mebɛma wo biribi. ” Ɛneɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Hwee nni fie. ” Biribi kaa sɛ, “Ɛsiane sɛ hwee nni fie hɔ nti, kɔ bon a ɛwɔ dua sini a ɛwɔ odum-tree no mu, na nnawuro mmienu bi da hɔ, nti fa brɛ. ” Na ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔde brɛɛ. Na asɛm bi kae sɛ, “Kɔ twitwa ɔdwen dua mma mmienu, na fa wo aniwa to hɔ, na dwane kɔfa baako kɔto baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ, na fa baako nso nkyinkyin kɔto baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ. ” Bere a ɔbaa no, ɔkae sɛ, “Meawie. ” Asɛm bi kae sɛ, “Fa dawuru kɛseɛ no nnyigye. ” Na ɔnnyigyeɛ no, na nwura no bɛyɛɛ akuraa a ɛtrɛw wɔ dua mma mmienu no ntam. Na odum-tree no gyinaa saa akurua no mfinimfini. Na saa adeɛ no bɛdaa ne ho adi sɛ ɛyɛ Ɔkɔdeɛ. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no sɛ, sɛ asɛmpɔw titiriw biara ba atenaeɛ ho a, ɛsɛ sɛ wɔte no wɔ odum-tree no ase. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Ho, meate. ” Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa eyi kyerɛɛ no a, na ɔbɔfoɔ no anya yernom, mpɔtam badwafoɔ, ne asomfoɔ. Ɛbɛyɛɛ saa berɛ no, na ne yere (a wɔfrɛ no) “Some-day-the-ungrateful-one-will-remember” no anya ne ho hwam sɛ ɔbɛyɛɛ ɔhene bi, enti ɔtɔɔ palm-wine, na ɔde kɔɔ ɔhene no nkurase, na n’abofra no wɔ ne akyi. Bere a ɔduruu hɔ no, ɔbɔɔ ne sianteɛ sɛ ɔreba sɛ, “Mo abofra no ka sɛ ɔde nsa bɛba sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ mo anim. ” Na ɔgyee nsa no, na ɔyɛɛ nsa a wɔde ba biara. Na ɔde ɔbaa no kɔmaa Gyaasehene sɛ ɔmfa no nkɔ ne fie. Bere a anwummere tɔɔ no, ɔbaa no hohoroo ne ho kɔɔ nea ɔhene wɔ wɔ ne dan mu. Saa ɔbaa no kaa no kyerɛɛ ɔhene no sɛ, “Woyɛ me ne wo, yɛadi osuo ne awia, nanso da a ɛwɔ hɔ no, wunya baabi a wotena, na anka ɛnsɛ sɛ woyi me ba? ” Ɛnneɛ a ɔreka asɛm yi, ɛne mmarima ne wɔn nkwasiasɛm no, na ɔntumi nka hwee. Ɛhɔ ara na ɔkyeree ɔbaa no nsɔmu na ɔne no bɔɔ bi. Anɔpa koro no, ɔgyee no firii n’atenaeɛ wɔ Gyaasehene nkyɛn na ɔde no kɔɔ Ɔhene harem. Ɛhɔ na ɔtenaa kɔsii sɛ akyinnyegyeɛ kɛseɛ bi baeɛ. Ɔhene ne ne mpɔtam badwafoɔ nyinaa kɔtenaa odum-dua no ase. Ababaa no abɔfoɔ no gyinaa n’agya anim, na ɔhunuu Aserefoɔ no abɔfoɔ no wɔ odum-dua no so, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Agya, mepɛ. ” Na n’agya no bɔɔ ne aseɛ, na ɔtui ne etire no akyi, na ɔmaa no kɔɔ fie. Na mmea no mu baako bi baeɛ, na ɔno nso gyinaa n’anim. Ɔsan nso huu Eagle no ba, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Agya, mepɛ. ” Na ɔbɔɔ ne kwan no so na ɔtwee n’etire no akyi, na ɛmaa ɛhwee aseɛ, na ɔkɔeɛ no, na ɔsu. Na ne maame kaa sɛ, “Dɛn nti na woresu? ” Na ɔkaa sɛ, “Agya bɔɔ me. ” Na ne maame kaa sɛ, “O! dɛn nti na ɔmmɛbɔ wo, anka ɔyɛɛ da-bi a ɔbɛkae sɛ ɔda-bi a ɔnni aseda no, anka ɔremmɛbɔ no. ” Na Some-day-the-ungrateful-one-will-remember no kae sɛ, “As for this, I have not done anything to you, that you should act like this to me. ” Ansa na ɔreka saa asɛm no, na wɔn baanu nyinaa aduru kɔɔ wɔn ho so na wɔko. Na asomfoɔ no kae sɛ, “Ɛnam saa ade pɔtee a ɛwɔ dua yi so nti, yɛn papa nua no reko; yɛbɛtwa no. ” Ɛno ara na wɔnyaa wɔn nkuma, pimpen! pimpen! (Ɛno ne nnyigyei a wɔyɛeɛ.) Saa berɛ no na Ɔkɔdeɛ no adwane akɔfa ne mmofra no akɔgyaa wɔn wɔ Koterɛ ne Old-Hawk sɔboɔ no mu. Ɛwɔ berɛ a nnipa no tew dua no na ɛhwe fam no, Koterɛ no bɔɔ nkonyaayisɛm sɛ, "Pimpeao! " Na dua no bɔɔ mu. Na wɔsan kɔɔ so. Na Koterɛ no kaa kyerɛɛ Old-Hawk no sɛ, "Kɔ hwehwɛ Ɔkɔdeɛ sɛ ɔbɛba abɛhwɛ ne mmofra, efisɛ me deɛ, owuo aba me so. " Ɛno ara na nnipa no kɔɔ so nyaa wɔn atuo no na wɔbɔɔ no kum no. Na Old-Hawk de ahyɛdeɛ bi kɔe sɛ ɔbɛfrɛ Ɔkɔdeɛ. Ɛkwan no nyinaa na ɔkɔeɛ no, na ɔteɛm sɛ:

"Abusuani! wo mma!

Nanabaa e! wo mmofra o!

Bere a ɔtoo so saa na Ɔkɔdeɛ no antie no, ɔsɔɔ ne nne so su:

"Sankur' e, Anomaa E, sankur' e, Anomaa, Ɔkɔdeɛ.

Sankur' e, Anomaa E, sankur' e, woakɔ adidie a, san ba.

Na Ɔkɔdeɛ no teɛ. Na ɔbaa, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn na eyi yɛ? " Na Old-Hawk kaa sɛ, "Wɔabɔ dua no wɔ ahyɛdeɛ sɛ wɔbɛfa wo mmofra, na Koterɛ deɛ, wɔatutu no tuo na wɔakunkum no. " Ɛno ara na Ɔkɔdeɛ no sɔree, wa (na ɛyɛ nnyigyei a ne ntaban no yɛeɛ), na ɔduruu dua bi so wɔ kuro no kwanano, na ɔsɔɔ n’aniwa no, na ɛyɛ nokware sɛ wɔretwa dua no. Ɔnka asɛm biara. Na seesei berɛ a dua no bɔɔ fam kɔɔ aseɛ no, Ɔkɔdeɛ no tuu mmirika kɔɔ hɔ, ho (na ɛyɛ ne ntaban no nnyigyei), na ɔkyeree ne mmofra no. Ɔde wɔn kɔɔ dua no so baabi a ɔbɛduruu no. Na afei ɔbɛduruu fam na ɔkaa asɛm bi (lit. in her etire) sɛ, "Ah, mmarima te sɛɛ anaa? " Na ɛmaa ne ho yɛɛ no ya. Na ɔsuu, na ɔkaa sɛ:

"Except the tuo, Except the ɔwɔ. "

Na ɔtwee ne ho aseɛ, na ɛno so nyaa atwene bedeɛ mmienu, na ɔbɔɔ wɔn de nkra yi kɔeɛ sɛ:

"Ma abɔdeɛ a wɔwɔ ntakra no nyinaa ba, (kosi sɛ) ewiem no yɛ tuntum, tuntum, tuntum. "

Na abɔdeɛ a wɔwɔ ntakra no nyinaa baeɛ. Na ɔka ne nyinaa kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ wɔka ne ho kɔma no kɔda Gyekye aseɛ, na nea ɔnim su a ɔsu no nyinaa ɛsɛ sɛ ɔsu. Saa anomaa a wɔfrɛ no Apitie no dii kan hyɛɛ aseɛ suu:

"Mebɛwu wu mpofirim!

Mebɛwu wu mpofirim!

Apatupere anomaa no hyɛɛ su:

"Me kɔnnye nsekurow!

Me kɔn dɔ akasakasa!

Me kɔnnye Kosante abarimaa no ntama. "

Akɔkɔhwedɛɛ, ɔkɔkɔɔ ntaban nsuo no, hyɛɛ su:

"Owuraye hoe, hoe, O!

Owuraye asɔ, asɔ, O!

Owuraye asɔ, asɔ, asɔ, O! " atewa ketewa no su:

"Doye!

Doye!

Afua Anwea ne ne (kunu) Ano Kofi, sɛ abofra bi kɔ so pɛ a ɔrewo no, ɛno nyɛ ade pa, Kwaku Amoaten (a name).

Doye!

Doye!

Odiawuo ne ne Ano Mmirikisie a ɔsɛe Ɔsono se no, wafi ne ho, wafi ne ho.

Agya Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ, sɔre kɔ hwehwɛ putty-nosed monkey no a wubetumi ayɛ no nkwan ne anonne (when the hunter's wife speaks thus,);

Abɔdeɛ biara nni a ɛbɛba dua (baabi a wɔwɔ no) ase. "

Ɔpɔdee no hyɛɛ su:

"Kyakyakya! nkawurubi!

(Hwɛ) saa abarimaa ketewa no atuo atoko no, ɔrekyerɛ me sɛ, ahyɛdeɛ sɛ ɔbɛbɛdi me ne ne plantain a wɔayam no. "

Ɔpatuo, Patu, hyɛɛ su:

"E! Mm!

E! Ɔsɛe!

Ɔnwam, Anwam, hyeeɛ sɛ:

"(Hunter) Akuaferi-Saka the little red one! "

Hornbill yere no ka sɛ:

"Ɔnni hɔ. "

Anwam no ka sɛ:

"Aso" (ne yere) di na yɛkɔ.

Na Saka de ne tuo no bɔ no, pa! Na ne yere (the Hornbill) no de ne ho bɛdwiri kɔeɛ na ɔka sɛ:

"Ɔwɔ hɔ!

Ɔwɔ hɔ! "

Na aserewa ketewa no su:

"Obeko Asamoa, bere a kwasea kɛkɛ no bɔ ne nnuan-wine no, anɔpa kwan so nnipa rekɔ ne bae! "

Aburuburo, Aburuburo, suu:

"Bere a na maame rewu no, ɔgyaa me sɔboɔ bi a ɛwɔ nkorabata a ɛyɛ nkokoraa;

Bere a forked-branch no wui no, ɛgyaa me mfeɛ a ɛwɔ abe nhaban mu sɔboɔ.

Bere a abe nhaban no mfeɛ wui no, ɛgyaa me sɔboɔ baa a akɔntɔn.

Bere a baa a akɔntɔn no bɛkaa me no, ɛde me dii dwuma kɛse, kɛse, kɛse!

Akyenkyena anomaa no hyɛɛ su:

Ɛyɛ me, ɛyɛ me, ɛyɛ me. " Na seesei Ɔkɔdeɛ no ankasa baeɛ, na ɛteɛm sɛ:

"Nkensen nkensen, abɔdeɛ a wɔwɔ ntakra bebree!

Gyekye me ase!

Nkensen nkensen, abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ a ya ya!

(Na bere a ɔsuiɛ saa) nnipa a wɔwɔ kuro no mu no nyinaa dwane firii ho; na Gyekye anhu nea mmarima, odwan, mmaa, yernom, ne nkokɔ anaa ade biara kɔeɛ. Ɔno ne ne yere ne ne abofra pɛ na na ɛwɔ hɔ, wɔ odum-dua no dua sin no nkyɛn. Na Ɔkɔdeɛ no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Efi da yi ɔbɔfoɔ biara remmɔne. " Eyi, me abasɛm yi, mekae sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom mpo wotumi fa no nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a wode bɛyɛ me (wɔ asɛmka ho).
