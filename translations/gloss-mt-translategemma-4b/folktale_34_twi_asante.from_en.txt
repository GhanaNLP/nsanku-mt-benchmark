Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ ONIPA NO NKYERƐ MFITIASEƐ A ONIPA FOFORO BI FIRI MU NO NNI N'AKYIRI.

NA ɛyɛɛ berɛ bi a, na ɔbɔfoɔ bi wɔ hɔ. Bere a ɔsɔre no na ɔkɔ nwura hɔ kɔpɛ agorɔ a ɔbɛdi, sɛ ɔbɛnya bi adi, na bi nso atɔn. Ɛnneɛ da bi a ɔkɔɔ nwura no nkyɛn no, ɔtee Kokotee, Nwura no ɔPONKYERƐNI, refrɛ ne busuani sɛ, "Kokotee Asamoa e! " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Yiw, ɔbroda, yiw. " Ɔno (ɔPONKYERƐNI a odi kan no) kaa sɛ, "adwuma no berɛ adu, nti yɛnkɔ ɔPONKYERƐNI no nkyɛn, sɛ ɔnni dadeɛ no na ɔmfa ano (yɛn twatwa nnwinneɛ), na sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ yɛtwa nnua biara a, yɛbɛtumi atwa. ." Bere a Bush-pig no frɛɛ n’anuanom no, ɔbɔfoɔ no dɔm ehuntahunu, na ɔtee nkɔmmɔdie no nyinaa. Mprempren Bush-pig (a ɔdi kan) no onuabarima no bisaa ne Bush-pig onuabarima no sɛ, "Na akuraa bɛn na yɛbɛkɔ, na yɛanya dadeɛ no abɔ? " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Yɛbɛkɔ akuraa a wɔfrɛ no Across-the-River. " Na ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛda bɛn? " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Monday. " Saa nhyehyɛeɛ yi nyinaa na ɔbɔfoɔ no tee aseɛ, na wɔde kɔɔ fie. Mprempren, bere a ɔbaa fie no, ɔka kyerɛɛ Across-the-asuwa no odikro, ɛne sɛ, nea ɔtee bere a ɔkɔɔ nwura no. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ akuraa no panyin sɛ, "Mommma mmofra no nkɔ twitwa nnyina no na mfa mmra, na Ɛdwoada no, Bush-pigs no bɛsesa wɔn ho (abɛyɛ nnipa) na wɔabɛba a, yɛbɛfa wɔn akɔkyekyere wɔn wɔ nnyina no ho. " Mmofra no kɔtwaa nnyina no, na wɔde baeɛ. Akyire-akuraa no panyin no kɔka kyerɛɛ akuraa no tomfoɔ sɛ wɔn bɔ wɔn dade stapo mma no. Na tomfoɔ no bisaa Head-man no sɛ, “Na dade stapo dodoɔ yi nyinaa, a wose ɛsɛ sɛ mebu no, dɛn na wode bɛyɛ? ” Na ɔka kyerɛɛ no amaneɛbɔ no sɛ, sɛnea ɔbɔfoɔ bi akɔ nwura no ho, na aba abɛka sɛ Ɛdwoada mmoadoma bi rebɛdane wɔn ho ayɛ nnipa, wɔ ahyɛdeɛ a ɛne sɛ wɔmmra ne dɔnka no mu mma wɔn nni nnwinnadeɛ. Wɔn tomfoɔ no tu mmirika kɔɔ so bɔɔ dade stapo no ntɛm. Bere a ɔhene no de ne nsa bɛtoo nnyina ne staplosa dade mma no so no, ɔhyɛɛ nkurofoɔ no sɛ wɔn bɔ dawuru sɛ, “Ɛdwoada no, ɛyɛ ɔbaa, anaa ɛyɛ ɔbarima, obiara nkɔ baabi biara. ” Ɛdwoada no adu, na anɔpa no, aberewa bi ka kyerɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no sɛ, “Kɔkɔkɔ ɛmo, ne nkyene, ne gyeene, na baabi a mmoadoma no bɛpepe wɔn nwoma ahoroɔ no so na wɔato hɔ; mo al. Ɛnneɛ, sɛ wukɔ hɔ a, fa ɛmo no bɔ wɔn ho, na mmoadoma no ba ha a, na yɛkyere wɔn, na ɛsɛ sɛ ebi gu kɔfa wɔn nwoma ahoroɔ no, na yɛde wɔn bɔ ho a, ɛmo no bɛyɛ wɔn ya, na wɔbɛtow wɔn afiri wɔn ho a, wɔbɛsan ayɛ nnipa. ” Saa ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔɔ ne ho kɔhyeɛ hɔ. Na ɔte sɛ wɔrefrɛ no, “Kokotee Asamoa, Ɛdwoada adu, yɛmmɛkɔ. ” Ɛnneɛ wɔn nyinaa baeɛ. Wɔn nwoma ahoroɔ no, wɔtwee no, na wɔde sii hɔ. Ɛnneɛ, (mprɛko no mu baako), a na ɔyɛ ɔyaresafoɔ wɔ habannuro ho no, na ɔwɔ wɔn mu, na ɔde ne wedeɛ no sii hɔ. Ɛno na ɔbɔfoɔ no hwɛɛ wɔn, na wɔgyaa wɔn kɔeɛ. Ɔkɔɔ so faa nwoma ahoroɔ no nyinaa, na ɔbɔɔ ɛmo a wɔatutu no wɔ wɔn so no, na ɔde kɔɔ asuwa no mu, ma nsuo no faa wɔn kɔeɛ. Nhabannuru ho ɔbenfo no deɛ, nanso, a na wadi ahomegyeɛ no ho no, ɔbɔfoɔ no anhu. Ɛduruu fie hɔ no, Ɔhene nso hyɛɛ nnipa a wɔahyiam wɔ dadeɛ bɔfoɔ no nkyɛn no baa ne fie. Saa nnipa yi baa Ɔhene fie no, wɔbisaa Ɔhene sɛ, “Adɛn nti na woafrɛ yɛn? ” Ɔhene no kaa sɛ, “Na mo yɛ me mmarima, na mo dwane (pɛnkoro) kɔtenaa baabi foforɔ; ɛda a moasan aba yi, ɛno ne sianteɛ a meka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ moba, efisɛ me remma mo kwan sɛ mo bɛkɔ akɔtra hɔ bio. ” Saa mmoadoma no kaa no sɛ, “Nea moaka no, yɛate; nanso yɛnim sɛ Onyankopɔn hu no nter baeɛ na ɔka kyerɛɛ wo nyinaa yɛn ho asɛm, nanso ɛno mfa ho, yɛne wo bɛtena aseɛ; nanso yɛnim sɛ nea woka no yɛ atoro; nanso, ɛno mfa ho; yɛda saa ɔbɔfoɔ no ase. Ɛnneɛ yɛn ne mo bɛtena aseɛ; nanso adeɛ baako wɔ hɔ a yɛtaboo, ɛne sɛ, wode w’ano bɛda yɛn mfitiaseɛ adi; anaasɛ w’adesuadeɛ biara mfa yɛn mfitiaseɛ nkyerɛ. Sɛ ɛba saa a, yɛbɛgyae yɛn ho wɔ wo abusuakuo yi mu, na yɛakɔ. ” Mprempren, berɛ a Ɔhene no kɔɔ wɔn nkyɛn no, dunsini ne afoforɔ bi guanee. Na dunsini no kɔɔ ne ho so wedeɛ na ɔdwanee, nanso ahomegyeɛ no danee. Ɛno ne sɛ, wɔantumi annya wɔn nwoma ahoroɔ. Ɔhene no penee tebea a mmoadoma no de sii hɔ no so, na wɔne mmoadoma no tenaa aseɛ anigyeɛ. Seesei, akuraa no mmarima no ne mmoadoma no mmea bi ware mmofra. Sesei, da bi a ɛyɛ mmoadoma no mu baako ne nkuraasefoɔ baako de wɔn nkuturu reko. Ɛno na ɛbaa so, akuraaseni no kaa aboa kɛseɛ no sɛ, “Fa wo ho fi hɔ, aboa bi te sɛ wo, a ɛyɛ Bush-pig company no dea. ” Ankyɛ na waka saa no, mmoadoma no nyinaa aniwa bɛyɛɛ kɔkɔɔ, na wɔkɔɔ Ɔhene fie kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “kwan a ɛbɛtumi ayɛ yiye a yɛka kyerɛɛ mo no, seesei ɛba mu baa mu, ɛnneɛ woyɛ dɛn? ” Ɔhene no maa wɔn kɔɔ so frɛɛ nnipa no. a ɔno na ɔhyɛɛ mansotwe no. Ɔhene no de n’ani kɔɔ asɛm no mu yie, na ɔde atɛmmuo mae sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mmoadoma no gyae sɔboɔ no, ɛfiri sɛ (ɔkaeɛ) ɛyɛ berɛ tenten a wɔaba hɔ yi, na ɛno nso yɛ dwumadie a ɛdi kan a obiara abɛka asɛm biara wɔ wɔn ho (mfitiaseɛ). Nanso mmoadoma no kae sɛ, “Yɛrenpene so. ” Na Ɔhene no kae sɛ, “Mummu wo ntie, na wosusu sɛ nea ɔbarima yi kae no yɛ atoro a, woyɛ Bush-pigs anaa? ” Na mmoadoma no kae sɛ, “Oh, yɛate. ” Ɛno na ɛbaa sɛ mmoadoma no ne Across-the-nsuo nnipa no dii ko. Mmoadoma no sɛee akuraa no, kosi sɛ nnipa bɛyɛ du pɛ na ɛkaa hɔ. Na wɔsrɛɛ mmɔborɔhunu na wɔka kyerɛɛ mmoadoma no sɛ wɔn (wɔn a wɔwɔ hɔ no) wɔ nni mu. Mmoadoma no tiee, na wɔbɔɔ nnipa no amaneɛ sɛ, “asɛm a ɛwɔ hɔ no, ɛnyɛ asɛm a ɛyɛ den sɛ yɛbɛte aseɛ, na sɛ yɛne mmoadoma no bɛtena aseɛ a, yɛbɛtaboo kasamfonin a ɛtwe adwene wɔ yɛn mfitiaseɛ. Sɛ wosusuw anaa woka no (bio) a, ɛnneɛ (yɛbɛbisa wo) sɛ kyerɛ yɛn thicket a ɛyɛ den pa ara a yɛfiri baabi yi baeɛ no, na yɛatumi asan akɔ ɛhɔ.” Na nnipa no kaa sɛ, “Yɛrentumi nni adeɛ a ɛte saa bio. Nea yɛayɛ yi ama yɛn abusuakuo asɛe; yɛrenyɛ saa bio. ” Ɛnneɛ wɔyɛɛ amansan dawubɔ, ɛnam sɛ wɔbɔɔ dawuro no kɔɔ nsunsuansoɔ sɛ ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara ka obi ho asɛm biara, na nsɛm a wɔada no adi no mma kuro no asɛe. Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom na mo betumi afa no nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a mo bɛbɔ me (wɔ asɛmka ho) no.
