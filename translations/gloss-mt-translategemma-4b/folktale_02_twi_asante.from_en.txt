MPITRIPI NE MPATRAPA

MPITRIPI ne Mpatrapa ne Anyankama-Kyerebi ne Kyerebi-Koso-Adawurampon kɔtuu nnuadewa aduasa (palm-wine trees) a, wɔkae sɛ, "Yɛnni ahurututu (the palm-wine) ne fɛfɛeɛ (nsateaa ahodoɔ) no nka. "

Sesei Agya Ananse kɔɔ so bɛtoo nsa (ne nsateaa ahodoɔ) nsa bɛduruu palm-wine no ahurututu no, na nnipa (lit. (Wɔn) kae sɛ, "Mfa ɛka no mma. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Dɛn ɛka na mebɔ? " Wɔn bɛkaa sɛ, "Woabɔ ɛka bi sɛ, Mpitripi ne Mpatrapa ne Anyankama-Kyerebi ne Kyerebi-Koso-Adawurampon akɔtu palm-wine nnua aduasa, na wɔaka sɛ mma obiara nka palm-wine no ahurututu ne fɛfɛeɛ (ne nsateaa ahodoɔ), na woaka nka palm-wine no ahurututu na woanom (wo nsateaa ahodoɔ), ɛno nti, na ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, 'fa ɛka no' (i.e. ɛsɛ sɛ wotua asotwe no); na Ananse no dunnii wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ ɔbɛgye (wɔn akatua no), nti ɔhuruu ne ho bɛbɔɔ silin no, (ne dede a ɛte sɛ) Kado!

Ɛno nti na wɔka sɛ daa, obiara bɛhu Kwaku Ananse a wɔapam no wɔ silin so, ɛwom sɛ ɛtɔ da a na ɔnam fam sɛ Ɔkɔtɔ, Kɔtɔ de, nanso.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
