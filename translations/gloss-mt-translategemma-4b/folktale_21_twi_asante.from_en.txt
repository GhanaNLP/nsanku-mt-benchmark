Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƆWEA (THE TREE-BEAR), ABOSOM-AKOTERE (THE TREE-BEAR), NE ƆPATUO (THE PATU) BAE SƐ WƆRENYA WƆN OSU SORONKO NO.

ƆWEA, the Tree-bear, (a ɔno nso wɔ abodin sɛ) Kwaduampɔn Kyerɛfo, Ɔpatuo, Patu, ne Abosom-Akotere, Abosomakoterɛ, (a wɔn abodin ne), “The Creator’s death”, “The take-a-year-to-do-anything-creature”, “He-walks-very-slowly-but-he-will-reach-the-white-snow-far-away”, (saa mmoa yi nyinaa) na na wɔte aseɛ biako. Seesei, na wɔn mu biara nni yere. Na Adobe-tree no de ne ho kɔmaa wɔn. Ɛda biara, da a Adobe-tree no sɔre no, na ɔkɔhwɛɛ saa baako so; da a ɛdi hɔ no ɔhwɛɛ baako foforɔ so; na ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no nso ɔhwɛɛ baako foforɔ so. Ɛda bi, da a na ɛyɛ Creator’s-owuo dane (to command the Adobe’s services) na ɔhwehwɛɛ Adobe-tree no, hena na ɔbɛtumi ahu no? Na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kyerɛɛ Tree-bear ne Patu no amaneɛbɔ. Na wɔkae sɛ, “Kɔkɔɔ no. ” Na Creator-death no de ne nne so: “Adobe o! Adobe o! ”

Nnuadewa-oso no hyɛɛ su:

“Bere a ɔkɔeɛ no, ɔnsan amma; ɔkɔeɛ, ɔnsan amma, ɔnsan amma, ɔnsan amma! ”

Na Patu no kae sɛ:

“Yɛakari (yɛakari) sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no na anka ɛsɛ sɛ yɛtow gu, m! Yɛakari (yɛakari) sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no na anka ɛsɛ sɛ yɛtow gu, m! m!”

Na ɛno ne osuo a ɛfa abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ mmiɛnsa yi ho no mfitiaseɛ.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
