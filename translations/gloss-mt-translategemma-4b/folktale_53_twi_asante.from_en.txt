SƐNEA EKUONA ABUSUAKUO NO BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU

NAA ɛdii da bi a, na ɔbaa bi a wɔfrɛ no Abena wɔ hɔ; berɛ a ne maame ne n’agya de no kɔmaa awareɛ (to any one) no, ɔkaa sɛ, “Certainly not. ” Na ɔka kyerɛɛ n’agya sɛ, “Ɛwɔ da a mehu ɔbarima a mepɛ no, saa da no, meba a, na wode asraba rekɔ a, mebɛfa wo asraba-box no abɔ wo etire so. ” Seesei, aboa no, Ɛkoɔ, tee saa abusuabɔ yi ho asɛm a, ɔtrans yɛɛ ne ho sɛ ɔbarima, na ɔde ne ho kɔɔ hɔ baa ɔbaa no nkurase. Ɛnneɛ, berɛ a Abena huu no, ɔtutuu kɔɔ nea na ne agya reyi asraba no. Ɔgyee asraba-box no fii ne nsam, na ɔde bɔɔ no etire so. Na saa da no yɛ anigyeɛ wɔ nkuraasefoɔ mu, a wɔkae sɛ, “Yɛn master no abarimaa no ahu nea ɔpɛ no. ” Ɛnneɛ wɔkɔɔ de ɔbarima no baa fie. Na abaayewa no kae sɛ, “Eyi ne nea mepɛ no. ” Na ɔbarima no nso pɛɛ no, na ɔwaree no. Ɔdii nna mmiɛnsa wɔ hɔ, na afei ɔkae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ ne akuraa, na ɔbaa no kae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ ne no. Ɔhemmaa no siesiee ne nneɛma nyinaa (ma akwantu) na ɔde nnipa aduonu-nan (24) maa no sɛ ɔne wɔn nkɔ. Ɛneɛ na wɔfiri hɔ kɔɔ akuraase bi kuro akyi, na akokɔnini bi bɔɔ n’ano. Na nnipa no ka sɛ, “Yɛadu fie anaa? ” Na ɔbaa no kunu no kae sɛ, “Mɛwaree yere foforo, na ɛsɛ sɛ mede no di kan kɔ me fie, na ɔanya ne ho nyinaa wɔ ahoboa mu, ansa na w’akɔkyinkyin nnipa nyinaa. ” Ɛneɛ na wɔfiri hɔ kɔe. Wɔduruu kwaeɛ mu petee bi. Berɛ a wɔduruu saa kwaeɛ mu petee no, ɔbarima no gyinaa nkyɛn; ɔkaa sɛ, “Abena, sesei berɛ no aba a ɛsɛ sɛ meka nea meyɛ no kyerɛ wo. ” Abena kaa sɛ, “Dɛn na woyɛ? ” Ɔbarima no kaa sɛ, “Meyɛ ‘pA! saa mebɛyɛ ‘ahabanmono! ” na ɔdane ɛkoɔ. Ɛno ne berɛ a nnipa no nyinaa kaa sɛ, “E! e, nea ɔka ne sɛ, ɔka sɛ ɔyɛ ‘pA!’, sɛ ɔbɛtumi ayɛ ‘ahabanmono! ” (No sooner had they said these nsɛmfua than) wɔn nyinaa danee nkoɔ. Ɛda soɔ ne ɔbarima baako a ɔdwane kɔeɛ na ɔbaa fie. Na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ akuraa no Head-ɔbarima nea ɔhunuu. Ɔhene ne Ɔhemmaa no frɛɛ badwa; ɔno (deɛ ɔfiri hɔ no) maa wɔn amaneɛbɔ sɛ nea ɛkɔɔ so no sɛdeɛ wɔkɔɔ so, na berɛ a ɔduruu pɔ a ɔkae sɛ, “Ɔyɛɛ ‘pA!’ sɛ ɔbɛtumi ayɛ ‘ahabanmono! ” no, ɔno nso danee ɛkoɔ. Berɛ biara a wɔn mu biako rebɛsan aka nsɛmfua yi, na ɔdane ɛkoɔ. Wɔ nnipa a wɔwɔ akuraa hɔ no nyinaa danee nkoɔ, na ɛno ne ade a ɛda nkyɛn wɔ aberewa bi mu. Ɛbɛyɛ bere a dwumadi yi kɔɔ so no, odunsini bi baa akuraa hɔ. Ɔteɛm sɛ, “Ɛnneɛ! kuropɔn bi, na nnipa biara nni mu? ” Dinn! (Ɛnyɛ nnyigyei wɔ mmuaeɛ mu gye sɛ nnomaa su a ɛwɔ babadua mu.) Ɔsan kae.
