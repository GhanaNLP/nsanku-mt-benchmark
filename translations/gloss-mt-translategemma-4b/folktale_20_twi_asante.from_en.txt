Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ KWAKU ANANSE NO SI KWAN NO, ANANSE NO YƐƐ KƐSE NO, WƆ NE ETIRE NO KA NO, NA ƐYƐƐ NKETEEM.

WƆka sɛ, ɛkɔm bi a ɛkɔm kɛseɛ baeɛ no, na Kwaku Ananse aka sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ nam ne nhabannuane na ɔde abɛyɛ sɛ ɔne ne yere "Aso" bɛdi. Ɔkɔɔ asuo bi mu na ɔhyiaa nnipa, seesei nnipa yi a ɔhyiaa wɔn no, me kwan so abebuo nti, na wɔyɛ ahonhom. Kwaku Ananse hyiaa Ahonhom no, na wɔregyina nsuo no mu na wɔregyigye nsuo no dry de akye apataa no. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Anuanom, meka me ba me nso bɛtumi? " Ahonhom no kae sɛ, "Mma me ba. " Kwaku Ananse kɔe, na ɔhunuu sɛ wɔde wɔn ti nnompe no rebu nsuo no dry. Ahonhom no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, "Woahu nea yɛde kɔn dɔn mpa no dry no, wobɛma yɛn kwan ma yɛyi wo tifi dompe no wɔ ahyɛdeɛ bi a wobɛma yɛn nso yɛn dɔn? " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Me kwan ma mo, yɛyi no mma me. " Ɛyɛ nokware bi, Ahonhom no yii no maa no. Kwaku Ananse ne Ahonhom no bɔɔ mu bɔɔ mpa no dry. Sɛdeɛ wɔrebɔ no, Ahonhom no yii ne dwom bi adi sɛ:

"Ahonhom no, yɛn nsa bɛka nsuo no ho a, yɛn atitirefoɔ na yɛde wɔn nsa bɛka nsuo no ho.

Oh, abɔfoɔ no, yɛregyigye nsuo no. "

Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Saa dwom yi yɛ dɛ, ma me kɔto bi? " Ahonhom no kaa sɛ, "Kɔto bi. " Na ɔde ne nne so anaa so:

"Abɔfoɔ no, yɛrepa nsuo no, yɛde yɛn atitirefoɔ kɔpa nsuo no.

Oh, abɔfoɔ no, yɛregyigye nsuo no.

Ɔbɔadeɛ no yɛɛ nneɛma akyi no, yɛde yɛn atitirefoɔ kɔdwiri nsuo no anaa?

Oh, Ahonhom, yɛrepae nsuo no. Me de etire bɛpae nsuo no dry da o, Oh, Ahonhom, yɛrepae nsuo no.

Kwaku Ananse bɛdii dwom no, na Ahonhom no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Yɛapere, yɛanya apataa, wo kyɛfa no yɛ kɛntɛn, fa no kɔdi, fa wo tifi dompe no, fa no bɔ (wɔ wo nipadua so), na kɔ; nanso nea ɛsɛ sɛ yɛka no titiriw ne sɛ, ɛda a wobe to saa dwom no biara, wo tifi dompe no bɛbue na ɛahwe. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Apataa a ɛwɔ mborosoɔ a moama me no, ɛyɛ nyinaa Me kɔn dɔ, na dwom bi deɛ, ɛyɛ sianteɛ bɛn na mebɛto? " Ahonhom no kae sɛ, "Ɛno yɛ yie, kɔ kɔ. " Ananse no de ne ho kɔeɛ. Ahonhom no nso, wɔn nsa bɛka nneɛma no nyinaa, na wɔn nso kɔeɛ. Berɛ a Ahonhom no aduru baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ no, ɛneɛ na wɔn nso yii wɔn dwom no so:

"Ahonhom no, yɛn nsa bɛka nsuo no berɛ a yɛrepae nsuo no dry de akye apataa no, yɛde yɛn atitirefoɔ na ɛrepae nsuo no.

Oh, abɔfoɔ no, yɛregyigye nsuo no. "

Na Ananse tee dwom no, na ɔno nso faa so.

"Ɔbɔadeɛ no, nneɛma a yɛde akɔto atitirefoɔ no, yɛde bɛdɔɔ nsuo no anaa? "

Oh, abɔfoɔ no, yɛregyigye nsuo no. "

Ansa na ɔbɛwie no, ne tifi dompe bɛtɔɔ ne ho. Kwaku Ananse maa no so, na ɔde ne ho bɛbɔɔ ne kokoɔ no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ahonhom, Ahonhom, etire no atɔ me ho. " Ahonhom no tee, na wɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛno ne Ananse no, ɔntie nea yɛka kyerɛɛ no, na ɔrefrɛ yɛn sɛ yɛn nyinaa mmɛnka na yɛnte nea ɔbɛka. " Ɛkame ayɛ sɛ ɛno ara na Ananse no baa hɔ ntɛm. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Puo!
