SƐNEA ABOSOM, ABANFOƆ KAKRAA NO, BAA EWIASE NO

Ɛdii da bi a, na ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ; ɔyɛɛ mmofra du-baako; na da biara a ɔsɔre no, ɔno nni bi. Ne maame nni bi a ɔbɛdi. Afei ɔdwen ho kɛseɛ, na ɔkɔɔ afuw kɔbɔɔ Silk-cotton dua no sɛ, "Me de me mmofra du-baako bɛba wo ase asafuntum tetew; na wɔba a, twi wo nkorabata du-baako na wo kum me mmofra no. " Silk-cotton dua no kaa sɛ, "M’ate asɛm no, na mebɛyɛ ama wo. " Ɔpanyin no kɔɔ fie, na ɔkaa ne mmofra no sɛ, "Mo nni kan. " Wɔn kɔ afuw no ase, wɔ Silk-cotton dua no ase; sakora ahorow wɔ hɔ, kɔfa bi mma (ba). "Yɛn mmofra no set off. Wɔn kɔduruu Silk-cotton dua no ase. Nɔmba Du-baako kae sɛ, "Nɔmba Baako, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Mmienu, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Mmiɛnsa, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Anan, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Anum, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Nsia, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Nson, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Awotwe, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Akron, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Du, gyina hɔ; na me ankasa, Nɔmba Du-baako, me gyina hɔ. " Nɔmba Du-baako akyi no, ɔkasa kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Moannim nkoaa na ɛwɔ hɔ. Eason nti na maame kae sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ yɛkɔ kɔfa sakora ahorow? " No anuanom no kae sɛ, "Dabi. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔka kyerɛɛ Silk-cotton dua yi sɛ, bere a yɛbɛkɔ hɔ no, ɛsɛ sɛ ɛtu nkorabata na ɛbɔ yɛn; ɛno nti, mo nyinaa twitwa nnua, na mo bɔ no wɔ Silk-cotton dua yi so. " Wɔtwaa nnua no, na wɔbɔɔ no wɔ Silk-cotton dua no so, pim! twerɛdeɛ! pim! twerɛdeɛ! (ɛno ne nnyigyei a wɔyɛeɛ). Silk-cotton dua no asusu sɛ mmofra no aba. Ɔgyee ne nkorabata du-baako, na ɔmaa ɛhwe fam. Dodoɔ ketewa no du-baako kaa sɛ, "Moahunu, anka yɛkɔɔ hɔ a, Silk-cotton dua no bɛkum yɛn. " Wɔgyee sakora ahorow no, na wɔde kɔɔ wɔn maame nkyɛn. Ɔno na ɔnoaa wɔn; ɛyɛ anwanwa! Ɛno ara na mmofra no dii (nyinaa). Wɔn maame kaa sɛ, "Ah! asɛm yi deɛ, me ho ntumi nni so, ɛnneɛ me de mmofra yi bɛma Ewiem onyankopɔn. " Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔkɔka Ewiem onyankopɔn no nyinaa asɛm no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Me mmofra bi a me awoɔ wɔn no, sɛ mepɛ sɛ me di adeɛ a, me nsa nka hwee (efisɛ wɔdi adidi dodo na wɔdidi dodo); ɛkɔm rebu me, ɛno nti me srɛ mo sɛ, momma mmofra no nni hɔ. t na ɛkunkum no, na ama meanya biribi adi. " Sky-god no kaa sɛ, "Is that really the asɛm? " The ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "I am speaking with a etire, the emu of which is white. " Sky-god no yii asɛnkafoɔ bi, na wɔkɔdii amoa kɛseɛ, a wɔde nkotokuo (broken) guu mu; ɔkɔgyee ɔwɔ ne daboɔ bi guu amena mu, na ɔkata so. Na ɛnneɛ messenger no kɔɔ so bɛtoo nsa frɛɛ mmofra no. Ansa na wɔbɛduru baabi a amena no sii no, Nɔmba du-baako no kae sɛ, "Nɔmba Baako, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Mmienu, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Mmiɛnsa, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Anan, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Anum, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Nsia, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Ason, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Awenwene, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Akron, gyina hɔ; Nɔmba Du, gyina hɔ; na me ankasa, dodoɔ ketewa du-baako, me nso me gyina hɔ; ɛsɛ sɛ mo pas Ɛha neɛ, ɛnsɛ sɛ wotwam hɔ. "Wɔn anuanom no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti, sɛ kwan a ɛtrɛw da hɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛnam nwura mu kwan? " Ɛnneɛ, berɛ a wɔrekɔ no, na wɔn nyinaa kura nnua. Nɔmba du-baako no kaa sɛ, "Mfa nnua yi mu baako ntu kwan yi so. " Wɔtoo no guu hɔ, na ɛhwe (kɔɔ amena mu), nnyigyei (na ɛyɛɛ ne ahweaseɛ). Nɔmba du-baako no kaa sɛ, "Ɛhɔ na ɛwɔ hɔ, na yɛatwam hɔ a, anka ɛsɛ sɛ yɛn nyinaa wuwu. " Ɛnneɛ wɔfaa kwan bi so kɔɔ hɔ kɔɔ Sky-god no nkyɛn. Sky-god no de atokuro bi atuo, (covered over,) na nkonnwa bi ato so, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na mmofra no bɛba abɛtena so a, wɔbɛkɔ akɔtɔ atokuro no mu. Mmofra no bɛduruu Sky-god no anim. Osutɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Nkonnwa na wɔde sii hɔ, wotumi kɔ tena so. " Ɛnneɛ Nɔmba Du-baako no kaa sɛ, "Hwan na yɛyɛ, sɛ yɛbɛtumi atena nkongua fɛfɛ a ɛte saa so, ɛnneɛ, Sire, yɛrekɔ tena nkyɛn ha. " Ɛno na osutɔfoɔ no de n’ani kyerɛɛ mmofra no; ɔkaa sɛ, "Me de mmofra yi bɛkɔ Awu-nkurase. " Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔtoo nsa frɛɛ mmofra no; ɔkaa sɛ, " Ɛsɛ sɛ wode kɔ Owuo (a ɔte) baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ, na wode kɔgye ne sika abua, sika abua a wɔde di aduan, sika abua a wɔde gu nsa, sika abua a wɔde pete, ne sika abua a wɔde sika abua." Nɔmba du-baako kae sɛ, "Mo ne yɛn wura, baabiara a wobɛfa akɔma yɛn no, yɛbɛkɔ. " Osuo-nyamfo no kae sɛ, "Mma mo kwan. " Mmofra no kɔɔ Owuo kuro no mu. Wɔduruu hɔ no, Owuo kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti, bere a ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara ba ha da no, woaba ha? " Wɔn nso kaa sɛ, "Yɛrekyinkyin na yɛaba ha (quite by hokwan). " Owuo kaa sɛ, "Oh, ɛno na ɛyɛ. " Saa berɛ no, na Owuo wɔ mmofra du; ne ho bɛka ho a, wɔduru du-baako. Bere a nneɛma hyɛɛ aseɛ yeraa no, Owuo kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti, bere a ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara ba ha da no, woaba ha? " Wɔn nso kaa sɛ, "Yɛrekyinkyin na yɛaba ha (quite by hokwan). " Owuo kaa sɛ, "Oh, ɛno na ɛyɛ. " (Wɔ kɔkɔɔ berɛ mu), Owuo kyekyɛɛ mmofra no mu baako baako, na ɔde baako maa ne mmofra biara, na ɔno ne Nɔmba Dubiako no ankasa kɔɔ nna aseɛ. Ɛduruu Owuo berɛ no, ɛneɛ Nɔmba du-baako no, Owuo bɔɔ ne se (till they shone) sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na ɔbɛtumi atwa Nɔmba Dubiako no. Nɔmba du-baako no kae sɛ, "Owuo, me nnya nna. " Owuo no kae sɛ, "Ɛduru dɛn na woada? " Nɔmba du-baako no kae sɛ, "Sɛ wode sika abua bɛbɔ a, anka mebɛtumi adaa. " Na Owuo no de no maa no. Ɔbɛyɛɛ bere kakra akyi no, Owuo san sɔɔ ne se no bio, ahyɛdeɛ sɛ ɔne wɔn bɛkɔ akɔfa Nɔmba du-baako no. Nɔmba du-baako no kae sɛ, "Owuo, me nnyaa nna. " Owuo kaa sɛ, "Ɛbɛyɛ dɛn na wobɛda? " Nɔmba du-baako no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wode sika kɔkɔɔ akyi-dan (snuff-box) bɛma me a, ebia mebɛda. " Na Owuo de no baa ne nkyɛn. Bio, ɛnkyɛeɛ, na Owuo rebɛfa Nɔmba du-baako no. Nɔmba du-baako no kae sɛ, "Me nnya nnyaa. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Wobɛda berɛ bɛn? " Nɔmba du-baako no kae sɛ, "Sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa sika-kɔm (golden chewing-stick) bi ama me a, ebia mebɛtumi adaa. " Owuo kɔfaa no maa no. Ɛduruu berɛ tiawa bi, na Owuo rebɛkyere no. Nɔmba du-baako no kae sɛ, "Aberewaa, me nnyaa nna. " Owuo no kae sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ, ɛhe na wobɛda? " Nɔmba du-baako no kae sɛ, "Aberewaa, sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa me sika-boɔ a, anka mebɛda. " Na Owuo kɔɔ so de no bae. Bio, ɛnkyɛeɛ, Owuo sɔree bio. Nɔmba du-baako no kae sɛ, "Oh, Grandmother, I said I was not yet asleep. " Owuo no kae sɛ, "And what will be the da when you will be asleep? " Nɔmba du-baako no kae sɛ, "If you were to go and take a koraa full of atokuro and go and splash nsuo in it, and boil some aduane for me to eat, then I might sleep. " Owuo no yii ne sɔneɛ no so kɔɔ asuwa. Bere a ɔbɔɔ nsuo no, sɔneɛ no mu atokuro no maa ɛno kɔɔ mu. Nɔmba du-baako no seesei ka kyerɛɛ n’anuanom sɛ, "Mommɔ so na mɔdwiri. " Na wɔsɔre kɔɔ dwiri, na Nɔmba du-baako no kɔtwa borɔdeɛ no, na ɔde sii hɔ a na n’anuanom no atɔ so, na ɔde ntoma akata so. Owuo no na ɛwɔ asuwa hɔ, na ɔrebɔ nsuo. Mprempren Ɔbarima wuo frɛɛ Ɔbaa wuo, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Ho there, Death. " Ɔbaa no nso kaa sɛ, "Adwo. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn na woyɛ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Alas, ɛnyɛ abofra ketewa bi a meanya no, ɛberɛ a mebɛkyere no, ɔka sɛ, 'Me nna ase'; wafa me nneɛma no nyinaa, na seesei ɔka sɛ mefa sɔneɛ na mepete nsuo. " Ɔbarima wuo kaa sɛ, "Ah, woyɛ abofra ketewa; sesei, sɛ woyiyi nhaban na woyɛ... " ne sɔneɛ emu, na afei ɔbɛhwie nsuo no, ɛbɛyɛ yie anaa? " Ɔbaa wuo no kaa sɛ, "Oh, ɛyɛ nokware paa. " Ɔtwee nhaban no, de guu emu, na ɔbɛhwie nsuo no, na ɔkɔeɛ. Nɔmba du-baako no kaa sɛ, "Owuo, woabɛduru ha dedaw? Yɛnno aduane no. " Owuo no yɛɛ aduane no, ɔde ne se bɔɔ ahyɛdeɛ sɛ ɔbɛkum Nɔmba du-baako no nuanom na wadi wɔn aduane. Bere a ɔkɔeɛ no, wanhwehwɛɛ wɔn mu yie, na ɔno ankasa kunkum ne mmofra du no nyinaa. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, anɔpa pa ara, berɛ a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, Owuo sɔree, na ɛbɛtenaa hɔ wɔ ogya nkyɛn. Nɔmba Du-baako no kae sɛ, "Nanabea, huruiɛ bi atena wo nufuɔ so. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Fa fly-switch a ɛda hɔ no, na kunkum no mma me. " Nɔmba Du-baako no kae sɛ, "Ao me Nyame! me; obi a wo nsunsuansoɔ no, huruiɛ bi bɛtena wo so a, na golden fly-switch bi da hɔ (wode biribi dada na wode bedi dwuma), ma me nnye no mma me. " Owuo kae sɛ, "Kɔfa mfi r oom. " Nɔmba Eleven kɔɔ so de baeɛ. Ɔhyɛɛ da yii nwansena no kwan, ɔammɔ no. Nɔmba Eleven kaa sɛ, "Oh, da, baabi a tsetse nwansena yi bɛda, ɛhɔ na me ne no bɛda. " Ɛneɛ Nɔmba Eleven kɔɔ dan no mu kɔgyee ne kotokuo a sika abua no wɔ mu, ne nneɛma no nyinaa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abusuaniwura Owuo, hwee rennɔɔso gye sɛ mebɛnya huruiɛ no, fa no hyɛ kotokuo yi mu, na fa brɛ wo nkyɛn. " Nɔmba du-baako no kɔɔ so, yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! Ɔduruu kuro no awieeɛ no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Ho, ɛhɔ, Nanabaa wuo, bɛkyɛ me abebuo yi, nanso sɛ woyɛɛ kwaseampa a, anka metumi agye wo nneɛma nyinaa ama wo; anka me anuanom a meka wɔn baeɛ no, anka wɔbɛtumi anya dwane; na anka metumi ma wo nso akum wo mmofra du no. Ɛyɛ me deɛ, me kɔ me ho so. " Owuo kaa sɛ, "Wo, abofra a ɔte sɛɛ; baabiara a wobɛbɔ wo ho, ɛhɔ na mebɛbɔ me ho. " Nɔmba du-baako no tuu ne ho kɔeɛ, yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! na Owuo nso, ɔdii n'akyi. Nɔmba du-baako no kɔeɛ no, ɔduruu n'ananom nkyɛn, na wɔte kwan so. Wɔreyɛ nsuo-kwan (bird-trap). Nɔmba du-baako no kaa sɛ, "Woankɔ da? Owuo reba, nti yɛnhwɛ kwan bi a yɛbɛfa so akwan. " Sesei Owuo bɛduruu wɔn so. Nɔmba du-baako no de aduro guu so guu n’anuanom so, na wɔkɔɔ silk-cotton dua bi so. Na Owuo gyinaa silk-cotton dua no nan so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛha ara, mehuu mmofra yi, na ɛhe na wɔkɔɔ? " Nɔmba du-baako no na ɔte aseɛ wɔ ne fam; ɔkaa no kyerɛɛ n’anuanom sɛ, "Me rekɔyɛ no nsuo. " N’anuanom no kaa sɛ, "E, ɔrehwehwɛ sɛ yɛkyere no, na yɛadwane abɛtena ha, na ɛnneɛ wose 'me rekɔyɛ no nsuo. " Nɔmba du-baako no anntie, na ɔyɛɛ Owuo so nsuo. Owuo kaa sɛ, "Ah, ha na wo wɔ, da a woahu ɔhaw. " Owuo kaa sɛ, "Wo, abofra, a wote hɔ no, kyere-he-ne, kyere-he-ne. " Ɛno na mmofra no mu biako bɛhwe fam. "Kyere-he-ne", sɛkɛnd bi bɛhwe fam. Nɔmba du-baako pɛ na ɛka hɔ. Owuo kaa sɛ, "Abofra, kyere-he-ne ", na Nɔmba du-baako no bɔɔ ne ho kɔɔ fam, kirim! Na Owuo nso, kɔɔ Silk-cotton dua no so. Nɔmba du-baako no kae sɛ, "Wo, ɔbaa kɛse pa ara, wo nso, 'kyere-he-ne'. " Na Owuo nso tɔɔ fam, tum! na ɔwui. Nɔmba du-baako no kɔtwee aduro no, kɔbɔɔ ne pALMS ntam, na ɔde guu ne anuanom so, na wɔsɔre. Nɔmba du-baako no na ɛbɛyɛɛ sɛ ɔbɛtow aduro no agu, berɛ a ɛbi bɛtɔɔ Owuo so, na Owuo nyan. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mokum me, na mo nso mo nyane me; ɛda na mo ne me bɛdi asɛm. " Ɛneɛ na wɔn nyinaa hyɛɛ aseɛ tuu mmirikaa prɛko, kiri! kiri! kiri! Ɛnneɛ na Owuo rekyere wɔn. Sɛdeɛ wɔrekɔ no, na asuo kɛseɛ bi wɔ hɔ a ɛyɛ nsuyiri. Nɔmba Du-baako ne n’anuanom no aduru hɔ a, na wɔnim sɛnea wɔbɛtumi atutu, na wɔtutu kɔeɛ. Nɔmba Du-baako no nko ara na na onnim sɛnea wɔbɛtutu. Ɛwɔ ɔfa foforɔ so no, mmofra no gyinaa hɔ sɛe, na wɔtee, na wɔtee; wɔn anom yɛɛ wɔn nso. Nɔmba du-baako deɛ, ɔdane ɔboɔ. Owuo no bɛduruu asubonten no so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, mmofra yi, monnyina hɔ mma me nnya ɔboɔ nka wɔn anom a ahonhon. " Owuo no, berɛ a ɔhwɛɛ aseɛ no, ɔhunuu sɛ ɔboɔ bi atɔ hɔ; ɔgyee no na ɔtow; berɛ a ɔboɔ no retu no, ɛkaa sɛ, "Winds, take me and set me on the other side. " Ɛduruu afa foforɔ no so. Nɔmba du-baako no kaa sɛ, "Here I am. " Owuo kaa sɛ, "Ah, this child! M’nni asɛm foforɔ biara a meka kyerɛ wo, na meka kyerɛ wo ne yi pɛ: kɔtra fie na sesa kɔ abosomfoɔ no mu baako, na sɛ obiara a mepɛ sɛ mefa no ba baabi a wote no, wobɛbɔ me amanneɛ; sɛ mepɛ saa a, mebɛgyaa no na mebɛma wo de no ama; nanso nea mepɛ (in nsasaeɛ) ɛsɛ sɛ wogye no ma me. " (Ɛne sɛ, afɔdeɛ.) Saa na Abosom (lesser) kyerɛɛ me. - (Gods) no baa ewiase. Wɔfiri mmɔfra nketewa dodoɔ du-baako mu.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom na mo betumi afa no nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a mo bɛbɔ me (wɔ asɛmka ho) no.
