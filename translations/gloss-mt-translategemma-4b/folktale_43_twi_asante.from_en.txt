Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ NWANSANA, NWANSENA NO, TUMI KƆ SO SIESIE WƆ KRAMAN NO, ƆBƆƆ NO KƆNKOON, KAM! KAM!

Ɔbɔfoɔ bi na na ɔwɔ nkraman nnan; baako na wɔfrɛ no Sniff-sniff; baako nso na wɔfrɛ no Lick-lick; baako nso na wɔfrɛ no Tie-in-a-pɔ; na baako nso na wɔfrɛ no Mene kɛse-down. Na ɔne ne nkraman no kɔ hɔ kɔhwehwɛ. Da biara, sɛ ɔkɔ nwura hɔ a, ɔne wɔn kɔ hɔ. Ɛda bi a ɔkɔɔ nwura hɔ na ɔhunuu kola-dua bi; ɛyɛ aduaba a ɛhyeɛ aseɛ wɔ asãã ahorow so. Ɛneɛ na ɔsan kɔɔ fie, na ɔka amaneɛbɔ no kyerɛɛ ne yere, sɛ, "Kola-dua bi a mehuu no, wɔde aduaba a ɛsɛn asãã ahorow so akata so. " Ne yere no kae sɛ, "Kɔfa no, na woahu sɛ yɛnni kola-nut biara mpo a yɛbɛbɛn na yɛnni biara a yɛbɛtɔn. " Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔɔ ɔkwan so, nanso ɔgyaa nkraman no wɔ fie, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a kola-dua no wɔ. Ɛneɛ na ɔforoɔ no, na ɔgyee kola-nuts no bi. Seesei na ɔtee ne nneɛma bi a ɛfiri baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ sɛ, "Twa! be! kukuru! kukuru! hu! pi! " Sasabonsam bi (stood) wɔ ne anim. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Hwan na ɔtwee me kola-nuts no? " Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Me. " Sasabonsam no kaa sɛ, "Me bɛkum wo ntɛm ara wɔ akawnto a woyɛɛ saa adeɛ yi. " Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wobɛkum me a, ma me di kan frɛ me nkraman. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Frɛ me, nanso sɛ wubɛdi mmɔdenbɔ mmiɛnsa na woanfrɛ wɔn na wɔammra a, ɛnneɛ mebɛkum wo. " Ɛneɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no maa ne ho so (ne nne) :

"Sniff-sniff, (ba ne) hyia me, lick-lick, (ba ne) hyia me, tie-in-tie, (ba ne) hyia me, mene kɛse-down, (ba ne) hyia me.

Wai, wai, wai, ɛrekyɛreɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, ɛrekyɛreɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, ɛrekyɛreɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, ɛrekyɛreɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, ɛrekyɛreɛ!

Na nkraman a wɔwɔ fie no tee no dwo-dwo, na wɔtutuu kɔɔ kuro akyi. Ɛneɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no maa (ne) nne so bio:

“Sniff-sniff, (ba ne nkyɛn), lick-lick, (ba ne nkyɛn), tie-in-two, (ba ne nkyɛn), gulp-down, (ba ne nkyɛn).

Wai, wai, wai, ɛrekyɛreɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, ɛrekyɛreɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, ɛrekyɛreɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, ɛrekyɛreɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, ɛrekyɛreɛ!

Sesei nkraman no ate; wɔtutuu, prada! prada! Sasabonsam no kaa sɛ, “San frɛ bio, na ɛno na ɛbɛyɛ berɛ a ɛtɔ so mmiɛnsa no. ” Ɔbɔfoɔ no de ne ho so (ne nne) dɔɔ so paa:

“Sniff-sniff, (ba ne nkyɛn), lick-lick, (ba ne nkyɛn), tie-in-two, (ba ne nkyɛn), gulp-down, (ba ne nkyɛn).

Wai, wai, wai, ɛrekyɛreɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, ɛrekyɛreɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, ɛrekyɛreɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, ɛrekyɛreɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, ɛkyɛreɛ wɔ hɔ! " N’ano nnyinaa hɔ, berɛ a nkraman no wɔ n’anim. " Ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Sniff-sniff, Sasabonsam bi wɔ ha, sniff no. " Na Sniff-sniff sniff no. Ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Lick-lick, lick him over. " Ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Tie-in-apɔ, tie him in a pɔ. " Tie-in-apɔ no de apɔ no kyekyeree ne menem no mu; na Sasabonsam no bɛhwe fam wɔ hɔ, kum! Ɛneɛ Mene kɛse no kɔɔ ne so kɔduruu no. Ɛneɛ ɔbɔfoɔ ne nkraman no hyiaa kola-nuts no na wɔde baa fie. Bere a ɔbɔfoɔ no san baeɛ no, ɔbɛduruu aduane a ɛyɛ ground-nut soup a ɛyɛ ma, na ɔde sii hɔ maa nkraman no; ɔbɔɔ nsuo guu hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ne yere hɔ kɔdii. Bere a ɔdii aduane no, ɔfiri adi kɔɔ abɔnten so na ɔtenaa gyedua bi ase. Seesei ɔpɔwfoɔ no nkoa, Nwansena, kɔhwɛɛ aduane a wɔde ahoboa (placed in a safe) ama nkraman no, na ɔmaa no so kɔɔ silin so. Nkrantaa no firi abɔnten so baeɛ na wɔn kɔm de wɔn. Lick-lick kaa no kyerɛɛ afoforo no sɛ, "Moahunu nea yɛayɛ ama yɛn wura, nanso ɛde besi seesei, ɔmfa aduane biara mma yɛn mpo, ɛnneɛ yɛnkɔkuma no. " Wɔn nso kaa sɛ, "Mo nni atoro, mo nni atoro. " Lick-lick firi hɔ kɔɔ abɔnten mu kɔkɔɔ ɔbɔfoɔ no ho, na ɔne ɔbɔfoɔ no dii agorɔ; na Sniff-sniff nso baeɛ; na ɔhwie ɔbɔfoɔ no ho; na ɔno nso kɔeɛ. Ɛnneɛ na ɛnkyɛ na Tie-in-apɔ no baeɛ bɛkyeree ne menem apɔ mu no, ansa na Mene kɛse nso abɛduru no so. Seesei nkraman no san kɔɔ fie, ɛnneɛ na wɔrebɛyɛ sɛ wɔbɛhwɛ pato no mu, berɛ a wɔhunuu nkwan mu nsunsuansoɔ wɔ hɔ. Wɔkae sɛ, "Wuntu mfiaseɛ, ebia ɔbɔfoɔ no gyaw yɛn aduane na wudi? " Fly no kae sɛ, "Mehuu no baabiara. " Sniff-sniff, berɛ a ɔsɔɔ n'aniwa no, ɔhunuu sɛ aduane no asisi wɔ hɔ (wɔ silin no so). Ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, papa! yɛakunkum yɛn wura kwa, ɛfiri sɛ ampa ara na aduane a na wadi ho dwuma no wɔ hɔ, na ɛnyɛ baako, ɛnyɛ mmienu, ɛyɛ nwansena na ɛfaa no kɔɔ hɔ. Nwansena no guanee firii hɔ kɔeɛ. Ɛno nti, sɛ kraman bi te hɔ a, na nwansena bi bɛtena no so a, ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkyere no.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
