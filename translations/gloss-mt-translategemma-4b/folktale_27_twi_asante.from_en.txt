Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

WƆ ABUSUAKUO BI MU NO, ADWENE A ƐNE ONIPA NEA BIRIBIARA NNI MU KORAA.

NA ɛdii da bi a, na ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔwo mmofra baanan. Wɔn mu dodoɔ no, nea ɔdɔɔso sen biara no, ne kwasea yɛɛ no kɛseɛ sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na ɔbɛtumi ayɛ biribi baako pɛ. Ɛno nti, n’anuanom ne ne nuammaa kae sɛ, "Yɛrentumi nni ne ho dwuma, nti yɛmma ɔmfi fie. " Ɛnneɛ, asɛmfua a mekaeɛ yi, saa kwasea no kɔyɛɛ ne ho atenaeɛ na ɔtenaa mu. Wɔ saa atenaeɛ hɔ no, na anwea nko ara na ɛwɔ hɔ. Na ɔyɛɛ asensene bi de gyee n’ani. Na aberewa no ne ne mmofra no da soɔ. Ɛdii da bi a, ɔsɔre kɔɔ afuw hɔ, na ɔhunuu sɛ abofra bi te bon bi so wɔ dua bi so. Ɔkae sɛ, "Hwan na ɛwɔ ha? " Abofra no annye no. Saa ɔbaa no kɔɔ hɔ, na ɔwɔ hwɛbea pa wɔ abofra no ho, faa ne nsa kɔɔ ne so, na ɔkae sɛ, "Yɛn kɔ fie. " Na abofra no sɔre sɔre, na ɔdii n'akyi kɔe, na ɛduruu (wɔn) fie. Ɔbaa no kɔkaa ne mmofra no sɛ, "Monhwɛ abofra fɛfɛ bi a meanya no, momma me sekkan mma me ntwa ne ti nhwi tenten. " Ɛnneɛ ɔbaa no nni nteaseɛ sɛ abofra no yɛ nnipa nketewa no mu baako, na ɛno nti na ne ti nwi no nni hɔ saa. Ɛnneɛ ɔgyee sekkan no na ɔtwaa ne ti nwi no wɔ n’etire so, na ɔde guu hɔ. Bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, na ɔbaa no nni nteaseɛ sɛ abofra no yɛ nnipa nketewa no mu baako, na ɛno nti na ne ti nwi no nni hɔ saa. Ɛnneɛ ɔgyee sekkan no na ɔtwaa ne ti nwi no wɔ n’etire so, na ɔde guu hɔ. Bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, na ɔbaa no nni nteaseɛ sɛ abofra no yɛ nnipa nketewa no mu baako, na ɛno nti na ne ti nwi no nni hɔ saa. Ɛnneɛ ɔgyee sekkan no na ɔtwaa ne ti nwi no wɔ n’etire so, na ɔde guu hɔ. (Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no), bere a wɔkae sɛ ɔmmɛdi no, ɔkae sɛ ɔremmɛdi; na wɔbisaa no sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ asɛm? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sɛ moammɛdi ɔhaw mu a, monka me etire ti nwi no mma na monka bio. " Saa ɔbaa no ne ne mmofra no kɔɔ amamfoɔ a ɛkamfam wɔ etire no ho, na wɔde ti nwi no bɔɔ etire no so, na wɔde ne ti nwi no bɔɔ ne ho so. Mmoatia no de ne nsateaa ahodoɔ no kɔɔ ne ti nwi mu, yii no, na ɔde guu hɔ. Bere a nneɛma no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, na ɛyɛ sɛ, "Mommra me etire ti nwi no. " Bere a nneɛma no annyɛ nea ɛda adi bio no, na ɛyɛ sɛ, "Mommra me etire ti nwi no. " Na seesei wɔn ho yɛɛ wɔn na wɔn ho yɛɛ nketenkete (with lean and small). Ɛda bi, abofra a na ɔyɛ kwasea no kae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwɛ ne maame. Bere a ɔbaa no, (behold) na ne maame ne n’anuanom ne ne nuammaa ayɛ wɔn ho kusuu. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dɛn na ɛbaa sɛ woyɛɛ dɛm saa? " Na maame no rebɛka neenti ankasa a ɛmaa wɔn ho yɛɛ dɛm, berɛ a ne mmofra no kae sɛ, "Nka nkyerɛ no, eyi ne kwasea, ɛdɛn na ɔtee adwene? " Na maame no kae sɛ, "Mebɛka. " Berɛ a ɔka kyerɛɛ no, ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, monnye mmoatia no mma me, na mefa no mma me akuraa. " Na maame no ne anuanom ne nuammaa no de mmoatia no kɔe. d ne etire ti nwi na ɔde kɔɔ kwasea akuraa hɔ. Berɛ a wɔduruu hɔ, wɔ n’akuraa hɔ, ɔhyɛɛ mmoatia no sɛ, "Mokɔ tena me asensene so na mo nnye wo ani kakra. " Ɔkɔtenaa so, na ɔbɔɔ no, na ɔmaa saa dwom yi so:

"Maame na ɔwoo me, abofra kwasea, Hurotom.

Agya begat me, abofra kwasea, Hurotom.

Maame woo me, kwasea kɛkɛ, Hurotom. Maame woo me, kwasea kɛkɛ, Hurotom.

Maame woo me, abofra kwasea, Hurotom.

Maame woo kwasea kɛkɛ, Hurotom. "

Mmoatia no bɔɔ bɔɔl no kɔsii sɛ ne ho bɔɔ no, ɛneɛ abofra kwasea no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Momma yɛn kɔgye mo etire ti nwi no. " Ɔne Mmoatia no duu fie, ɔno (mmoatia) no kae sɛ, "Momma mo etire ti nwi no mma na fa sese, na momma me kɔ. " Saa ara na abofra kwasea no nso kae sɛ, "Wo nso, anwea a wotutu no, ɛsɛ sɛ wode nsɛnkyerɛnne no nyinaa kata so ansa na mebɛbɔ mo etire ti nwi no. " Mmoatia no kɔɔ hɔ kɔɔ Wɔ dwumadi no ho; ansa na ɔbɛka nsɛnkyerɛnne (a ɔne n’anan ayɛ) no biara, na ɔbɛka foforo. Seesei, sɛnea ɔbɛyɛ biara, ɔrentumi nka n’anammɔntena no nyinaa. Ɔkae sɛ, "Papa, me kɔnni manso biara, enti meyi me maame, na me de me ho me, momma me, na me kɔ. " Saa abofra a na ɔyɛ kwasea no kae sɛ, "Na me kɔ, na me nso meyi me maame (firi wo tumi mu). " Seesei maame no baa ase kɔtoo, na ɔkae sɛ, "Me da mo ase, na seesei (mehu sɛ) adeɛ biara nni hɔ a ɛte sɛ kwasea wɔ abusuakuo mu. " Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, sɛ wode wo ho bɛto hɔ a... mmofra, monnye aniwa nkyerɛ, na monhwɛ wɔn yiye, efisɛ moannim sɛ daakye berɛ bi, ɔhaw da mu no, (mo mu bi) bɛba abɛhwɛ mo.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
