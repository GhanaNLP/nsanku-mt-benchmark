Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ NNIPA BINOM YƐ FƐ, NA BINOM NSO NYƐ FƐ

WƆKA SƐ (bere bi) na ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔno kɔɔ Palm-tree bi a ɛgyina hɔ wɔ nsuo mu sɛ ɔrebɛtumi atwitwiri no wɔ abofra-sɛm ho. Na ɛberɛ a ɔkɔeɛ no, Palm-tree no kaa sɛ, “Me bɛma mo nea mo pɛ, nanso abofra a me ne no bɛbɔ no, sɛ ɔsɔre a, ɔnyɛ adwuma biara da. ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Me gye to mu (to that). ” Ɛnyɛ nna mmienu, ɛnyɛ nna mmiensa, berɛ a ɔdwoeɛ no, ɔdii awoɔ. Sɛdeɛ mmofra a wɔwɔ Spider-stories mu no, na wanyini pii, na wanyini pɛpɛɛpɛ. Na ne maame ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Wɔ tribe no taboos nyinaa mu no, ɛnni hɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ wuhwɛ gye Palm-tree no, ɛnsɛ sɛ wufiri biara so da. ” Ɔkae sɛ, “Maame, metee. ” Mprempren, da bi a maame no gyaw no hɔ kɔɔ afuo. Ɔkɔɔ so frɛɛ m'adɔfo, na ɔne no hyɛɛ aseɛ dii agorɔ, na ɔkyeree ne wasɛm-beads no nsɔmu na ɛbɔɔ no hwee. Na abaayewa no kae sɛ, “Wa hama a etwa to no, kɔfa Palm-tree fibre bi mma me sɛ mebɛsan asiesie no. ” Saa abarimaa no kɔɔ so tɔɔ bi na ɔde baeɛ. Abayewa no kae sɛ, “Me kɔnni saa, me kɔfa bi a meahwehwɛ wɔ Palm-tree a ɛwɔ nsuo no mu. ” Ɛneɛ abofra no kɔɔ hwehwɛɛ bi. Ɔkɔɔ so no, na Palm-tree bi wɔ nsuo no mu. Ɔforo so, ɔkra! ɔkra! ɔkra! kosi sɛ ɔduruu dua no kɔn mu. Ɔyii ne sekkan no adi, na ɛhɔ ara na ɔduruu no, dua no bɔɔ mu bɔɔ ɔkra no yafunu no mu, na dua no bɔɔ mu bio. Ɛhɔ ara na na ɔwɔ, na ɔrekyere no. Ɛnkyɛ na ne maame baeɛ; ɔduruu nsuo no ho a, ɔhunuu shadow bi wɔ so, na ɔkaa sɛ:

“Hwan shadow na ɛrebu nsuo so sɛ me abofra Akwasi Kwasaman no? ”

Ɔkae sɛ, “Mama, ɛyɛ me. ”

Ɔkaa sɛ, “Dɛn nti na Palm-tree no renhyɛ wo? ”

Ɔkae sɛ, “Me kɔɔ kɔtɔɔ Palm fiber no kɔma me dɔfo na palm-tree no squeezed me. ”

Ɛneɛ maame no kae sɛ, “Wo, saa Palm-tree no sra no, sra no, sra no, me ba Kwasaman.

Palm-tree squeeze him, squeeze him, squeeze him, Palm-tree squeeze him.

Na maame no kɔɔ so. Na agya no nso baeɛ, na ɔkaa sɛ:

“Hwan shadow na ɛda nsuo yi so sɛ me abofra kwasaman? ”

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Papa, ɛyɛ me. "

Ɔno (agya) no kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dɛn na Palm-tree no renhyɛ wo? "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Me kɔɔ kɔtɔɔ Palm fiber no kɔma me dɔfo ne me Palm-tree no squeezed me. "

Ɔno (ɔagya) kaa sɛ, "Wo, saa Palm-tree no sra no, sra no, me ba Kwasaman.

Palm-tree squeeze him, squeeze him, Palm-tree squeeze him.

Na agya no kɔɔ fie, na ɔka kyerɛɛ Etire a ɛwɔ akuraa no so, na Etire a ɛwɔ akuraa no so nso, frɛɛ nnipa no nyinaa; na wɔbaa Palm-tree no nan so, na Etire a ɛwɔ akuraa no so no de ne ho sɔreɛ (ne nne) kyerɛɛ sɛ:

"Hwan shadow na ɛda nsuo so sɛ me nana Akwasi Kwasaman?

Ɔkae sɛ, "Panyin, me ne me. "

Ɔno (akuraa no Etire) kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dɛn na Palm-tree no renhyɛ wo? "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Me kɔɔ kɔtɔɔ Palm fiber no kɔma me dɔfo ne me Palm-tree no squeezed me. "

Etire akuraa no kae sɛ, "Wo, saa akutu-dua yi nhyɛ no, nhyɛ no, nhyɛ no, me nansafo Kwasaman.

Palm-tree squeeze him, squeeze him, Palm-tree squeeze him.

Na Etire akuraa no kɔɔ so, na nkuraasefoɔ no nyinaa too dwom koro sɛ wɔrekae Palm-tree no. Na seesei ne dɔfo no baa ne nkyɛn, na ɔkae sɛ: "Hwan shadow na ɛda nsuo no so, ɛne me dɔfo Akwasi Kwasaman no? "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ me, mo ɔdɔfo ankasa. "

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na Palm-tree no renhyɛ wo? "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Me kɔɔ so twitwaa Palm fiber maa wo, na palm-tree no hyɛɛ me. "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Palm tree release him, release him, release him.

Palm-tree release my dɔfo, Akwasi Kwasaman.

Palm-tree release him, release him, release him, Palm-tree release him.

Bere a ɔkaa saa no, palm-tree no buee ne ho kakra, na ne dɔfo no yafunu buee ne ho kakra. Na ɔsɔɔ ne nne bio, na ɔkaa sɛ:

"Hwan shadow na ɛda nsuo so, ɛte sɛ me dɔfo Akwasi Kwasaman?

Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ me, mo ɔdɔfo ankasa. "

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na Palm-tree no renhyɛ wo? "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Me kɔɔ so twitwaa Palm fiber maa wo, na palm-tree no hyɛɛ me. "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Palm tree release him, release him, release him.

Palm-tree release my dɔfo, Akwasi Kwasaman.

Palm-tree release him, release him, release him, Palm-tree release him.

Na ɛnneɛ, ne dɔfo no baa anim nyinaa, na ɔbɛkaa m'adɔfo bosom so, na wɔn ho bɔɔ wɔn ho bɔɔ wɔn ho, na wɔsesaa ngo. Na nnipa a wɔkɔɔ hɔ ntɛm ara na wɔde bi guu wɔn anim no, wɔn na wɔn ho yɛ fɛ paa; nanso wɔn a wɔannya bi no, wɔn na wɔn ho nyɛ fɛ. Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
