Yɛnnyɛ nea ɛyɛ nokware, yɛnnyɛ nea ɛyɛ nokware (sɛ yɛbɛka asɛm bi a, ɛyɛ nokware)

SƐNEA KWAKU ANANSE NO BƐYƐƐ POO NO.

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse, kɔɔ hɔ kɔɔ Nyankopɔn, Ewiem-onyankopɔn no, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Ma me aburoo aba baako na mefa nnipa akuraa (in nsasaeɛ) mma wo. ” Ewiem-onyankopɔn no kaa sɛ, “Wo nni ho kwan. ” Ananse no buaa sɛ, “Sɛ me nni ho kwan a, meka me ntam etire ho mmara toɔ me maame, Nsia, na meka sɛ mebɛtua me ntam etire no ka. ” Ewiem-onyankopɔn no kaa sɛ, “Gye (wogye aburoo nnɔbaeɛ aba) na wo. off. ” Ananse no yii ne ho kɔɔ akuraase bi. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔpanin no asɛm a ɛdi hɔ. “Sky-god no de me kɔ, ɔse, ɛsɛ sɛ mefa aburoo aba baako yi kɔ baabi, na sɛ meba (baabiara) a, ɛsɛ sɛ mefa aburoo nna nnɔbaeɛ aba yi ne nkokɔ no kɔ. ” Ɔpanin no kaa sɛ, “E! Sɛ nkokɔ ne aburoo bɛda abom a, ɛbɛyɛ yie anaa? ” Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, “Wɔn kɛkɛ na ɛsɛ sɛ wode wɔn bɔ mu. ” Head-man no kaa sɛ, “Kɔfa kɔsi hɔ. ” Anɔpa kɛseɛ no, Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ ɔrekɔhwehwɛ no, nanso aburoo no aba baako nni hɔ bio. Ananse no kaa sɛ, “Head-man, nkokɔ no adidi Sky-god no corn no. ” Head-man no kaa sɛ, “Me nni ho kwan sɛ mebɛdi mansotwe biara wɔ eyi ho, nti monnye anomaa no na mekɔ. ” Kwaku Ananse kɔfa akɔkɔ kɛseɛ bi, na ɔde akɔ akuraa foforɔ bi so. Bere a ɔduruu hɔ no, ɔka abasɛm bi kyerɛɛ ɔpanin no sɛ, Ewiem no de me kɔeɛ, na mebɛda ha, na ɛsɛ sɛ anomaa yi ne odwan no da. ” Ɔpanin no kaa sɛ, “Brɛ no bɛto hɔ. ” Ɛwɔ anadwo no, Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ nguandaban no mu kɔtwaa anomaa no. Ɔde anomaa no nsam kɔtɔɔ odwennini kɛse bi mmɛn so. Anɔpa koraa no, ɔkɔɔ ɔpanyin a ɔwɔ hɔ no nkyɛn kɔkaa sɛ, "Mma me anomaa no. " Bere a wɔrebɛkɔ akɔfa no ama no, ɛhɔ na na anomaa no awu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ah! ɛno ne Soronko Anomaa, a odwan no akum no. " Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Me nni ho mansotwe biara, nti monnye odwan bi na monkɔ. " Kwaku Ananse faa odwan no kɔɔ so. Ɔduruu akuraa foforo bi so. Ɔkɔɔ ɔpanin fie. Ɔpanin no kae sɛ, "Dɛn asɛm? " Ananse no buae sɛ, "Oshenefo no de me kɔ, na me kura odwan no; ɛne anantwie no yɛ sɛ wɔbɛda abom. " Ɔpanin no kae sɛ, "Ho! ma ɛnna odwan no. " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Dabi, eyi ne nguanten soronko a ɛwɔ Soronko-nyini no, ɛnna anantwie no. " Ɛtirenfo no kae sɛ, "Kɔfa hɔ, ɛnneɛ. " Ananse no kɔɔ hɔ kɔtoo hɔ. Ɛduruu anadwo no, Kwaku Ananse kɔkunkum odwan no, na ɔde ne mogya no guu anantwie bi etire so. Anɔpa koro no ara, Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Head-man, ma me odwan no. " Wɔkɔɔ anantwifie hɔ, na odwan no wɔ hɔ, wui. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Head-man, ɛyɛ Sky-god odwan a anantwie no akum no. " Head-man no kae sɛ, "Me ne wo nni asɛm yi ho mansotwe biara, nti fa nantwie a ɛkum no no. " Kwaku Ananse faa nantwie no, na ɔkɔɔ akuraa foforo so. Ɔde nantwie no kɔtoo hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ nna. Berɛ a ɔda hɔ no, wɔbɛkaa ɔpanyin no sɛ ne nkoa bi awu. Ɔhene no kaa sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ woyi no kɔ, efisɛ me nni adaka no. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, mepɛ no, efisɛ Sky-god no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ sɛ mebɛnya funu a wɔrebɛto no kɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ mebɛsesa no nantwie. " Na nkuraasefoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Dabi. " (Nanso) Kwaku Ananse (insisted), sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ woyi no ma me, na sɛ wode nantwie no kɔtɔn a, wubenya sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i.e. £4), na wotumi fa (saa dodoɔ) no kɔtɔ odonkɔ foforɔ. " Ɛno na nnipa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nokware. " Ɛnneɛ wɔyɛɛ nsasaeɛ no. Kwaku Ananse gyee funu no kɔɔ kwan so, na ɛduruu akuraa foforɔ bi so. Bere a ɔduruu hɔ no, ɔkasa kyerɛɛ ɔbarima no sɛ, "Ma me abofra yi kɔ ne mmofra no nna, wɔ pato (abrannaa dan a ɛbue) yi mu; sesei (ɛsɛ sɛ meka no kyerɛ wo) abofra yi nni fɛ, ɔwɔ suban a ahyɛ mu sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mframa wɔ anadwo, nti sɛ ɔbɛbɔ mframa a, ma (ɔfoforo) mmofra no bɔ no. " Mprempren na ɔbarima no awu nna mmiɛnsa. Mmofra no ne no (ne funu) no daa hɔ. Afei wɔtee nka sɛ biribi a ɛyɛ dɔteɛ. Wɔkae sɛ, "Saa abofra yi abubu mframa, yɛmmɛbɔ no. " Wɔbɛdɔɔ no so, titim! titim! titim! (na ɛyɛ wɔn akontontu ahodoɔ no nnyigyei). Anɔpa kɛseɛ no, Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ so frɛɛ n’abofra no. Ɔkɔeɛ no, na ɔwɔ hɔ, wawu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Tiawahene, wo mmofra no akum Ewiem onyankopɔn no nkabɔfoɔ a na ɔka me ho no. " Tiawahene no kaa sɛ, "Me nni asɛm yi ho mansotwe biara, enti mo mmofra no nni. " Ananse no buae sɛ, "Sɛ me ne wo, tiawahene, ne nnipa a wɔwɔ kuro yi mu nyinaa nkɔ Ewiem onyankopɔn anim kyerɛ wo asɛm. " Tiawahene no bɔɔ ne dawuro sɛ, "All of Ɛsɛ sɛ wode Ewiem onyankopɔn a ɔwɔ hɔ no kɔ, kɔka yɛn asɛm. " Ananse no dii kwan no anim, nnipa no nyinaa dii n’akyi. Kwaku Ananse bɛduruu kuro akyi no, ɔde messenger kɔka kyerɛɛ Ewiem onyankopɔn sɛ ɔreba kuro bi nnipa so. Ewiem onyankopɔn no frɛɛ n’asɛmbli no, na ɔtenaa aseɛ (wɔ n’ahemfiefo nkyɛn). Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ so no, ɔgyee ne dwom sɛ: "Kwaku Ananse, alas, alas, me nsa aka aburoo aba baako de akɔ akɔgye anomaa.

Kwaku Ananse, alas, alas, mefa anomaa no kɔgyee odwan.

Me kɔɔ odwan kɔgyee nantwie, me kɔɔ nantwie kɔgyee funu, me kɔɔ funu kɔgyee nnipa kuro."

Nnipa biara kae sɛ, "Mo te dwom a Kwaku Ananse reyi no anaa? " Kwaku Ananse yii ne dwom no so bio:

"Kwaku Ananse, alas, alas, me nsa aka aburoo aba baako de akɔ akɔgye anomaa.

Kwaku Ananse, alas, alas, mefa anomaa no kɔgyee odwan.

Me kɔɔ odwan kɔgyee nantwie, me kɔɔ nantwie kɔgyee funu, me kɔɔ funu kɔgyee nnipa kuro."

Kwaku Ananse bɛduruu animuonyam ansa na ɔreba Sky-god no anim, na Sky-god no bisaa Kwaku Ananse amaneɛbɔ. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "aburoo aba baako na megyeɛ sɛ me ne kuro bi tefoɔ bɛba; ɛhɔ na nnipa a wɔwɔ kuro bi mu no gyina hɔ. " Sky-god no kaa sɛ, "Yɛyi no ayɛ. " (Wɔteɛɛ so sɛ,) "E! e! e! " Sky-god no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ megye ma wo de aseda? " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Me kɔn dɔ hwee, (wɔ) aprannaa so ara na mepɛ. " Sky-god no kae sɛ, "Oh, oh! me asɛm yi, wobɛwu ohia mu, wo a wode pii kɔeɛ no, na wose wuhwe me kwan. " Ɛno nti na ohia remmɛn Kwaku Ananse da.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
