Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ASONO NO BAE SƐ ƐBƐKƆ KƆƆ MANPƆW A WƆN AMANNƆNE TƆ NO.

Ɛsono bi na na ɛwɔ hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔne obi bɔ agorɔ a wɔfrɛ no "nti a wɔde bobɔ wɔn ho". Wɔn a wɔwɔ mmoa no nyinaa kae sɛ wɔntumi nni agorɔ a wɔne Ɛsono bɔ no. Mprempren na na Kwaku Ananse wɔ hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ, "As for me, I shall be able, but the tebea I stipulate upon with you is that, if you should happen to kill me, then you must bury me anywhere else but in a ɔbotan. " Ɛsono no kae sɛ, "I have heard, and I shall come and strike you for ndanson, and you, too, will come and strike me for ndanson. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ so tuu bayerɛ, na ɔ wɔde no guu ogya mu, sɛ wɔbɛbɔ no, na ɔkɔɔ kɔyɛɛ tiafi bi wɔ kwan bi akyi. Mprempren, berɛ biara a Kwaku Ananse sɔre, na ɔde bayere bi kura ne nsa mu, na ɔkɔtenaa tiafi no so. Ɔhunuu adowa ketewa a ɛretwam no. Mprempren nsɛmfua a ɛne wɔn nkyɛnkyɛn (i. e. I forgot to mention something), na ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bi aba. Adowa no kwan no, Ananse no de bayere no guu kwan no so. Adowa no nso gyee no ntɛm ara. Ɔkae sɛ, "Agya Ananse, ɛkɔm kɛseɛ a ɛte sɛɛ no, na ɛno na wode kɔ fa wo ho? " Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "Me fie aduane ho ɔhaw biara nni hɔ. " Adowa no kae sɛ, "Me ne wo bɛkɔ akɔgye bi. " Kwaku Ananse ne ɔduruu fie; ɔka kyerɛɛ Adowa no sɛ, "Me ne wo bɛkɔ akɔgye bi. " y, na sɛ ɔba a, ɔbɛka sɛ, 'Asikoto!' (na woabɛyɛ etire) na woabua sɛ, 'Me fa me etire no bɛgye no,' na ansa na woaka saa no, ɔbɛfa aduane no bi ato hɔ, ma woafa. " Adowa no kaa sɛ, "Me da wo ase, Aku; wo deɛ, Agya Ananse, mehuu ɔbarima bi te sɛ wo da. " Adowa no kɔtoo kwanano wɔ adiwo no nkyɛn. Ɛnkyɛeɛ biara, Ɛsono no baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Asikoto. " Adowa no buaeɛ sɛ, "Me kɔfa etire me nsa," na Ɛsono no bɔɔ no, pan! na Adowa no awu. Ɛsono no kɔɔ so. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, Ananse no de Adowa no kɔmaa "Aso" (ne yere) sɛ ɔnyɛ soup-stew mma no. Kwaku Ananse afei kɔɔ Ɛsono hɔ kɔmaa no anɔpa nkyea. Ɛsono no kae sɛ, "Are you quite well? " Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "I am well. " Kwaku Ananse tuu kwan baa ne fie. Ɔfaa bayerɛ foforo bi, na ɔsan kɔɔ tiafi. Ɔtwe, Ɔtwee no, na ɔrehwɛ kwan, na ɔde bayere no guu hɔ. Ɔtwee no kae sɛ, "Oh, Father Kwaku Ananse, ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bi te sɛɛ aba, na eyi ne nea wode kɔ tiafi? " Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "Sɛ wopɛ bi a, monka, yɛmmɛkɔ me fie, na mekɔma mo bi. " Ɔtwee no ne Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ wɔn ho. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Da kwanano wɔ kar dan mu ha, na anwummere yi me akoa no de aduane bɛba, na sɛ ɔduru ha a, ɔka sɛ, 'Asikoto'; wo nso ka sɛ, 'Me fa etire bɛgye'. " Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, "Me da wo ase, Aku, wo (nko) na wotumi hyehyɛ nneɛma a ɛte sɛɛ. " Ɔtwee no kɔɔ hɔ kɔtoo. Ɛsono no anwummere no na ɛbae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Asikoto. " Ɔtwee no kaa sɛ, "Me kɔfa etire me nsa. " Na ɔbɔɔ Ɔtwee no, na ɔhwee fam, na nnyigyei a ɛhwee fam no, na ɛyɛ kum! Ɛneɛ Ɛsono no kɔɔ so. Anɔpa koro no ara, berɛ a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, na Kwaku Ananse de Ɔtwee no kɔmaa "Aso", na ɔde kɔɔ Ɛsono fie kɔkyee no anɔpa. Ɛsono no kae sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ Okuamoa, wodi yie anaa? " Okuamoa no buae sɛ, "Me ho yɛ me dɛ. " Ɛneɛ wɔkasaa kakra, na Kwaku Ananse srɛɛ sɛ ɔkɔ, na ɔsan kɔɔ ne fie. Na seesei Kwaku Ananse yɛɛ adeɛ koro no ara ne Ɔyuo, kwaduo no; Wansane, Bush-buck no; Kokotee-Asamoa, Bush-pig no; Ɔtromo, Bongo no; ne Oko, Ɔpɔnkɔ kɔkɔɔ-fitaa no. Seesei, na ɛyɛ Kwaku Ananse adwuma sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔbɔ Ɛsono no. Ɛneɛ na ɔkɔɔ dade-panyin bi nkyɛn kɔyɛɛ dade payeɛ; na ɔyɛɛ dade hama bi nso kɔka ho. Ɛduruu anwummere no, ɔkɔɔ Ɛsono fie. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Asikoto. " Ɛsono kaa sɛ, "Me fa etire no bɛgye no. " Ananse no de dade payeɛ no guu Ɛsono etire no ahenkyɛ so, na ɔde dade hama no bɔɔ no, pan! pan! pan! na ɛhɔ (the teaa) no yeraa (into the Elephant's head). Kwaku Ananse ansa na ɛreba fie. Anɔpa koro no ara, berɛ a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, Ɛsono kaa ne yere sɛ, "Me kura tipae a emu yɛ den," na ne yere no kaa sɛ, "Nka asɛm saa, ɛsɛ sɛ woyɛ ho aniwuo; Kwaku Ananse mpo a wopirii no ndanson no, anka ɔnnyaa tipaeɛ, anka ɔnwu mpo, na woka asɛm yi; sesei fa wo ho kɔ na fa Kwaku Ananse anɔpa pa sɛnea na ɔyɛ no." Ɛsono no de ne aniwa bɔɔ ne ho (i. e. yɛɛ mmɔdenbɔ kɛseɛ), na ɛkɔɔ so; ɔduruu baabi a Kwaku Ananse teɛ. Ananse no kae sɛ, "Ɔhoɔdenfo kɛse, wode yie anaa? " Ɛsono no buae sɛ, "Oh, me ho yɛ me yie. " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Aane. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ so yɛɛ teaa foforɔ bi. Ɛduruu anadwo a ɛyɛ dwoɔ no, ɔpueeɛ kɔɔ Ɛsono hɔ. "Asikoto," na ɔkae. Ɛsono no buae sɛ, "Me kɔfa etire me nsa. " Kwaku Ananse gyee teaa no, de sii hɔ, na ɔde dade hama bɔɔ no pan! pan! pan! Kwaku Ananse san baa ne fie. Ɛnneɛ na ɔwɔ hɔ, berɛ a messenger bi baeɛ na ɔkaa sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse, Asono nana no nyinaa ka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meba bɛbɔ mo amaneɛ sɛ mo adamfo, Ɛsono, berɛ a ɔbɛda hɔ no, wansɔre bio; nti monba bɛbue ɔbotan bi na monfa no bɛhyɛ mu. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ koraa, ɛno na ɛnyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ a yɛyɛeɛ no. Yɛkae sɛ sɛ obi wuo a, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔfoforo no noa no ma ɔdidi. " Ɛno na messenger no kɔɔ kɔmaa blood-relations of the (dead) Elephant no. Wɔkae sɛ, "Kɔfa no kɔfa no (ha) ntɛm ara. " messenger no baa ɛhɔ Ananse no nkyɛn na ɔkae sɛ, "Wɔka sɛ woba ha ntɛm ara. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ wotwɛn me. " Ananse no kɔɔ nwura no nkyɛn na ɔtwaa dua bi, na ɔtwaa no bɛyɛɛ dua-ni, na ɔtwaa tuo bi, na ɔde guu ne nsa mu, na ɔde maa "Aso" sɛ, "Fa no kɔ anim, fa si baabi a wɔkae no. " Ɛsɛ sɛ mepae ɔbotan no mu, na wo nso bɛgyina hɔ, na meba a, wobɛto me nkaeɛ. " Aso no gyee (de dua honi no) kɔeɛ. Ananse no nso, ɔne asɛnkafoɔ no kɔeɛ. Kwaku Ananse bɛduruu Asono anim, na wɔde asɔ, ne crow-bar, ne agyan ne sekkan, ne nsa hyɛɛ ne nsam. Wɔkae sɛ, "Get on. " Ɛneɛ na wɔde Ananse no kɔɔ baabi a botan kɛseɛ bi wɔ. Wɔkae sɛ, "Split it open, dig a hole, and place the (dead) Elephant inside. " Ananse no kɔɔ hɔ, pE! pE! pE! (na ɔbɔɔ ne nnwinnadeɛ no). Ɛhɔ na na Kwaku Ananse wɔ, bere a (suddenly called out) "Yiw. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Elephants, do you hear, (it is) as if some one calls me? " Asono no kae sɛ, " Munfi ha, mo hwehwɛ kwan a mo bɛfa so adwane. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, " Meate aso. " Na ɔne n’adwuma no kɔɔ so, pE! pE! pE! Afei bio ɔteɛm sɛ, " Yiw. " Ɔkae sɛ, " Obi bi refrɛ me. " Nnipa no kae sɛ, " Yɛremma mo kwan. " (Na ɛyɛ) saa, saa, mpɛn nson. Ɛduruu tenten no, wɔkae sɛ, " Kɔtie (hwan na ɛyɛ) na san mma me ntɛm, na sɛ wuguan a, yɛbɛkyere wo. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ ne ho kɔɔ baabi a Akua ba no wɔ. Seesei Kwaku Ananse tuu mmirika (kɔɔ akyiri), kiri! kiri! kiri! (na ɛyɛ ne anan no nnyigyei). Asono no kae sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse, dɛn na ɛyɛ? " Ɔkae sɛ, " Oh, ɛsɛ sɛ wofiri hɔ, ɛfiri sɛ ɔbɔfoɔ bi wɔ hɔ a ɔregyina hɔ, na ɔreka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ me twetwe me ho kɔ nkyɛn bi ma ɔtumi tow ne tuo no bɔ wo. Ɛno nti na ɔafrɛ me, na ɔafrɛ me sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ me gyina nkyɛn. " Asono no bɛyɛɛ sɛ wɔrebɛyɛ hwehwɛ wɔ nwura no mu, nokware bi na wɔhunuu sɛ obi bi wɔ hɔ a ɔregyina hɔ, na wɔdwiri kɔeɛ. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Papa hunter, kunkum wɔn mma me. " Asono no dwane kɔeɛ, ɛyɛɛ tall-grass-country no mu, na wɔgyaa ɔsono a wawu no wɔ hɔ, na Kwaku Ananse faa no kɔdii. Ɛno na Asono baa kwan sɛ ɔbɛkɔ tall-grass-country no, ɛfiri sɛ berɛ bi Asono (only) na ɔtenaa kwaeɛ no mu.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom na mo betumi afa no nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a mo bɛbɔ me (wɔ asɛmka ho) no.
