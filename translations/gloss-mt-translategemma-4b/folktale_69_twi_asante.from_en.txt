SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ WƆDUA NWIADOWA, GARDEN-EGGS NO, WONYIN FAM, BERE A NKRUMA, NKRUMA NO, GYINA HƆ KƐSE.

GARDEN-EGGS ne Nkruma pɛn na wɔtenaa dan baako mu; fa dan no sɛ wɔn nwura a wɔperee no, fa sɛ wɔn mpaeɛ-dan. Nea ɛsɛ sɛ wɔyɛ biara, wɔyɛ no wɔ saa dan baako no mu. Mprempren Garden-eggs ka kyerɛɛ Nkruma sɛ, "Me werɛ fi (ɔdan no mu) nti, yɛnkɔtu ɛtokuro bi, na yɛmfa nwura no nsie mu. " Nkruma kae sɛ, "Me nkɔ (kɔtu). " Garden-eggs no de ne ho kɔɔ hɔ na ɔtuu amena bi. Bere a ɔtuu no wiei no, Nkruma bɔɔ no so, na Garden-eggs nso bɔɔ no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Monyi ɛtokuro no so mma mehu. " Garden-eggs no nso buae sɛ, "Ah! Ɔbɔadeɛ no bɛyɛɛ akyire no, wuhunuu da bi sɛ onipa bi bɛtumi atutu ɛtokuro bi? " Nkruma kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wunka w'ano a, mebɛba abɛbɔ wo. " Garden-eggs no kaa sɛ, "Na wunya sɔneɛ bi, fa nsuo bɔ no ma, na me nnom no, na mebɛtutu (ɛtokuro) no ama wo. " Nkruma kaa sɛ, "Wode me kɔ kwasea, ɛfiri sɛ yɛn (ahenni) a yɛdii kan tenaeɛ yi, yɛde sɔneɛ bi na ɛdii dwuma. " lash water? " Nkruma firi nea ɔteɛ (sitting) no, na ɔkɔɔ ɛtokuro no mu bɛbɔɔ Garden-eggs. Seesei, berɛ a ɔrebɔ no, Sky-god no tee, na ɔsomaa Kɔkɔsakyi, Ɔpɛtɛ no, sɛ ɔmfa wɔn mmienu no mmra. Wɔn mmienu no kɔeɛ. Garden-eggs kaa nsɛmfua a na ɛwɔ n’ano no; Nkruma kaa nea ɛwɔ ne deɛ no. Mprempren, Ewiem-onyankopɔn no hyɛɛ ne kasafoɔ sɛ wɔmfa atɛmmuo mma Nkruma, na wɔ adom Garden-eggs. Ɛno nti, sɛ yɛdua Garden-eggs a, ɛda fam so pɛpɛɛpɛ; ɔrekyerɛ Ewiem-onyankopɔn ne ne mpɔtam badwafoɔ no ase. Na Nkruma deɛ, ɔgyina hɔ kɛseɛ; ɔreka sɛ ɔne Ewiem-onyankopɔn no bɛko efisɛ ɔde atɛnkyea bi ama no.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
