DƐN NTI NA APUPUO (FRESH-WATER MUSSEL) A ƆMAA N’ASUBON NO, ANKASA NO DA ASUBONTEN ANO

WƆka sɛ Okawa, Opitire, ‘Bonko, Mmɔtɔ, ne nsuo mu mmoa’ (were living together) ne ɔpɛ berɛ bi baeɛ a, asubɔnten no nyinaa yii, na ɛmaa abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ yi hyiaa amaneɛ kɛseɛ. Wɔtenaa anwea so wɔ asubonteɛ aseɛ. Ɛduruu tenten no, wɔkaa sɛ, "As for this mmusu a yɛahu no, yɛn kɔ hɔ mma Nyankopɔn, Sky-God, na yɛnsu frɛ no sɛ ɔmfa osuo mma yɛn. " Saa abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no nyinaa hyɛɛ aseɛ. Na Okawa de ne nne so (to dwom) sɛ:

" Ɛnnyɛ me, Oka' Abena, sesei me kɔ ha, sesei me kɔ hɔ?

Mehu ne me ho wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ mebɛfrɛ Wo.

Me nsa ka aburobia ahweneɛ wɔ me nanpɔ so.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔne me yere, Abuya. "

Nanso osuo no anntɔ. Na Opitire nso, ɔbɛkaa ne nne:

" Ɛnnyɛ me, Opitire Abena, sesei me kɔ ha, sesei me kɔ hɔ?

Mehu ne me ho wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ mebɛfrɛ Wo.

Me nsa ka aburobia ahweneɛ wɔ me nanpɔ so.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔne me yere, Abuya. "

Nanso osuo no anntɔ. Na ‘Bonko nso, ɔbɛkaa sɛ:

" Ɛnnyɛ me, Bonko Abena, seesei rekɔ ha, seesei rekɔ hɔ?

Mehu ne me ho wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ mebɛfrɛ Wo.

Me nsa ka aburobia ahweneɛ wɔ me nanpɔ so.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔne me yere, Abuya. "

Nanso osuo no anbu. abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no nyinaa baeɛ bɔɔ su, na Apupuo (Fresh-water mussel) nko ara na ɛda hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, " Yɛn ma me kɔ su kakra. " Wɔkaa sɛ, " Firi ha, yɛn osu no nni papa biara, na wode dɛn na wode wo deɛ bɛyɛ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, " Yɛn ma me nyan. " Nanso wɔbɔɔ no, na wɔka sɛ wɔadi kan aka akyerɛ no sɛ ɔnni su. Nanso, Fresh-water mussel no kaa sɛ, " Me bɛsu. " Ɛnneɛ, wɔkaa sɛ, " Su. " Na ɔde ne ho kɔɔ soro (the song).

" Ɛnnyɛ me, Apupuo Abena, sesei me kɔ ha, sesei me kɔ hɔ?

Mehu ne me ho wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ mebɛfrɛ Wo.

Me nsa ka aburobia ahweneɛ wɔ me nanpɔ so.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔne me yere, Abuya. "

Na Ewiem-onyankopɔn no bɔɔ n’aprannaa mu, kukuru! abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no nyinaa kaa sɛ, " Kɔ so, kɔ so. " Na bio ɔsree, na Ewiem-onyankopɔn no bɔɔ no ho kɔɔ kɔɔ. Na seesei abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no de no too wɔn mmatiri so, sɛ, " Kɔ so pɛnkoro. " Na bio ɔsan de saa (nnyesoɔ) no so. Na seesei Ewiem-onyankopɔn no yii (osuo) no adi, na ɛbɔɔ fam, ta! ta! ta! ta! na nsuka no nyinaa boro so. Mprempren yi (now that) sɔree, na, twom! (sɛnea wɔhuruu nsuo no mu) abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no nyinaa kɔɔ mu. Apupuo (Fresh-water mussel), bere a ɔbɛduruu nsuo no mu no, ahomegyeɛ bi maa no kɔɔ akyiri, na ɔda soɔ wɔ asuwa no sikakorabea. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Apupuo a ɔyɛɛ n’asubɔnten no, ɔda sikakorabea. " Eyi, me abasɛm yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na fa bi nso bɛba me nkyɛn.
