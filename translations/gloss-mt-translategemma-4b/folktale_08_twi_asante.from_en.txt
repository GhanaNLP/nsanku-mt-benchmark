SƐNEA BABADUA NO NYAA NSƆMU (NE DUA) NO.

Ɔbaa bi (once) yɛɛ adwene sɛ ɔbɛyɛ abofra, na ɔkɔɔ abosom nkumaa nkyɛn kɔhwehwɛɛ mu. Na onyankopɔn no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛbɛyɛ sɛ bere a wobɛwo abofra no, ɛbɛwu, nanso mebɛyɛ nea metumi biara sɛ mebɛma wo, nanso adeɛ a ɛsɛ sɛ meka kyerɛ wo ne sɛ, abofra no yɛ Ɔhemmaa a ɔwɔ soro ne Niamey, Ewiem-onyankopɔn no abofra ankasa, na bere a wobɛwo no, mma no nyɛ hwee, nanso bere a wobɛsɔre no, ɛsɛ sɛ wuhohoro no, ngo no, na wode no kɔ, na wode no si akonnwa so. " Of a tru Ɔno na ɔduruu. (Sɛnea ɛte wɔ Anansesɛm mu no, ɛnkyɛ na ɛbɛnyini. ) Wɔfrɛɛ no abofra akosua dentaa. Sɛnea obosom kumaa no kyerɛɛ no, maame no, bere a ɔsɔreɛ no, ɔhohoro no, ɔde sradeɛ no guu no so, na ɔnoa biribi de maa no, na afei ɔkɔɔ n’anim kɔɔ n’afuo. Afei, da bi a na ɔnni berɛ a ɔbɛtumi ayɛ adeɛ biara amma no, ɔde ahyɛdeɛ kɔmaa n’afenaa, Ɔkra, sɛ, "Me kɔ afuo hɔ, monyɛ adeɛ bi na monma Akosua Dentaa mma no. " Na maame no kɔɔ kwan. Mprempren, berɛ a Ɔkra aduane borɔdeɛ no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Akosua Dentaa, ba ha bɛbɔ saa _fufu_ yi, wo ne wo Queen-Mother asɛm no. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me mma memmra. " Ɔkra kaa sɛ, "Mommra, wobɛba, wo ne wo ahantan. " Na Akosua Dentaa sɔre kɔeɛ; ɔkɔgyee pestle no; ɔbɔɔ fufu no. Bere a ɔwieeɛ no, ɔkɔtoo dan mu, faa ne ntoma so, na ɔkataa ne etire so. Ɔkra kɔɔ (wɔn akyi no), ɔkaa sɛ, "Denta"; mmuaeɛ biara nni hɔ. Ɔyi ntoma no firii ne etire so; Akosua Dentaa awu; ɔde ntoma foforɔ guu ne so. Ɛnkyɛeɛ biara, maame no firi afuo no baeɛ. Bere a ɔbaa no, ɔnhwehwɛɛ ne abofra no (at once), na mmom ɔkɔɔ mukyea ho kɔwiee aduannoa. Ɛnneɛ na ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔkra, Denta wɔ he? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Aberewa, m’aduanmudeɛ mpo a me-noo no, ɛhɔ na ɛda so. " Ɔbaa no maame no kae sɛ, "Meate asɛm no. " Ɛnneɛ na maame no kɔɔ dan mu wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ ɔbɛgye ntoma akɔ abɔnten so akɔhwehwɛ ne abofra. Bere a ɔkɔɔ dan no mu no, na adeɛ bi a ɛyɛ kusuu da hɔ wɔ ebuo bi mu. Ɔsɔɔ ntoma no, na ɔhunuu sɛ Akosua Dentaa awu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔkra, wose Denta afiri abɔnten so, na seesei ha na ɔwɔ, ɔwuo? " Ɔkra kaa sɛ, "Aberewa me nni hɔ. " Ɛneɛ ɔbaa no de ntoma abɔso kyekyeree ne asene so, na ɔde ne abofra (wuo) no kɔɔ n’akyi, na ɔne no baa abɔnten so, na ɔsuu; na akuraa no Etire no baeɛ, na wɔkaa sɛ, "Mommfa mo abofra no mma yɛn na yɛmmɛsie no. " Ɔbaa no buaeɛ sɛ:

"Na me ne me abofra akosua dentaa, na me kɔnee no, me ne no kɔɔ nsuo-mu-kenkan.

Wɔse, 'Ɛyɛ ɔhene maame ba.'

Me kɔn dɔ sɛ obi bɛkɔ akɔka sɛ, The Queen Mother abofra, Siankwa Adamowa abofra yi, eyi, me abofra, me mmɛsie no ha. "

Na ɔde ne ho kɔeɛ, kɔɔ akuraase bi, na berɛ a ɔduruu hɔ no, akuraa no Etire no kae sɛ, "Fa abofra no firi (wɔn) so na wɔmmra mma me nsie no, ɛfiri sɛ yɛtaboo (sɛn adeɛ). " Nanso ɔbaa no kae sɛ:

"Na me ne me abofra akosua dentaa, na me kɔnee no, me ne no kɔɔ nsuo-mu-kenkan.

Wɔse, 'Ɛyɛ ɔhene maame ba.'

Me kɔn dɔ sɛ obi bɛkɔ akɔka sɛ, The Queen Mother abofra, Siankwa Adamowa abofra yi, eyi, me abofra, me mmɛsie no ha. "

Na ɔsan kɔɔ so. Ɔnanteɛ, na ɔnanteɛ, na ɔnanteɛ, faa kwaebirentuo bi mu, na ɛnneɛ yi, abofra no rottɔɔ, na ɔgyee no (ɔn akyi) de kɔeɛ, na ɔsan baa fie. Na akuraa no Etire no bisaa no sɛ, "Ɛhe na abofra no wɔ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me de no kɔeɛ. " Etire no kaa sɛ, "Woyɛ animguaseɛ, anka sɛ wode kwan ma wɔ ha a, anka ɛsɛ sɛ yɛsie no wɔ nsaman-saman no kwaebirentutu no mu, ɛno na ɛbɛyɛɛ nea ɛfata. " Ɛneɛ na mpanimfoɔ no ka sɛ, "Sɛ w’abofra no wu a, ɛnka ɛdi awerɛhoɔ wɔ "moderation" mu; mma ɛnka wo. Saa ɔbaa no yɛɛ saa, na ɔde ne abofra no guu hɔ bere a na ɛnhia sɛ ɔyɛ saa. " Na seesei ɔbaa no kae sɛ, " Ɔkra yi deɛ, fa no kɔ mɔta mu na yɛn no bɔ no ma me. " Ɛneɛ nkuraasefoɔ no kɔɔ hɔ (baabi a Ɔkra wɔ no, na nnyigyei bi wɔ hɔ a wɔbɔ no) Pu! Pu! Pu! na wɔbɔɔ no ma ne ho yɛɛ dwo. Na ɛnneɛ, na ɛda soɔ ne "the ayamye so one"; bere a wɔbɔɔ no, na ɛyɛ den, na bere a wɔbɔɔ no, na ɛhuru ne Babadua no. Ɛno nti na Babadua no wɔ apɔ (on its dua), kan no na ɛyɛ trom, trom, ɛne sɛ ɛyɛ smooth paa.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom na mo betumi afa no nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a mo bɛbɔ me (wɔ asɛmka ho) no.
