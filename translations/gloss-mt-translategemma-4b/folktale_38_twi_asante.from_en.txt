MPANIMFOƆ NO KYERƐ SƐ, "SƐ ƐYƐ W’AWA BUSUANI, ANAA KUNU, ANAA OBIARA A ƆWƆ DWUMA BI A ƆREKA NO, SƐ ƆSRƐ WO A, BOABOA NO"

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse no, ka kyerɛɛ "Aso" (ne yere) sɛ, "Momma yɛn kɔyɛ turo bi. " "Aso" kae sɛ, "Me nkɔ. " Kwaku Ananse kɔperee fam. Bere a ɔsan baeɛ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "So ɛyɛ nokware sɛ worenkɔ (bɛboa me)? " "Aso" buaeɛ sɛ, "ɔbea akyiwade bi a ɛregyae fam. " Kwaku Ananse kaeɛ sɛ, "Ma ɔkyena ma yɛkɔ hyew turo mu wira. " "Aso" kaeɛ sɛ, "ɔbea akyiwade bi a ɛrehyew turo mu wira, na me deɛ, mekɔma ogya bɛhyew me? " Ɛneɛ Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ na ɔsan baeɛ. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, " Ho! Nea saa Nkramoni no kae no, me nte aseɛ koraa. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, " Dɛn na ɛyɛ no; ka no adi sɛ me nte aseɛ. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, " Me rekɔ na me reba. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ hɔ turo no so, na ɛhɔ na ɔgyinaa hɔ kyɛeɛ, na ɔkaa (ne ho asɛm) sɛ, " Me, Kwaku Ananse, ɛha me wɔ hene, na ɛyɛ me sɛ mebɛtena aseɛ na saa ɔbaa kɛkɛ yi abɛyɛ me kwasea? " Kwaku Ananse faa sekkan, agyan, ne asɔ, na ɔtuu amena kɛse pa ara. Ɔde nnɔbaeɛ a ɛwɔ afuo no mu nyinaa guu mu, na ɔdane kɔɔ fie. Ɔkae sɛ, "Aso" ba ha, na meka me amaneɛbɔ no nkyerɛ wo. " Aso no bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me kɔɔ kramoni bi nkyɛn kɔkaa me ahonyadeɛ ho asɛm, ɔka sɛ nna nwɔtwe bi na ɛda me so ansa na me wu, ɛnneɛ me nkɔ afuo bio, na mo nso mo nkɔ hɔ bio, na me wu a, mo bɛkɔ akɔsie me. Me atutu amena kɛseɛ bi wɔ hɔ, na mo mu na mo bɛsie me. " Nna nwɔtwe na ɛduruu, Kwaku Ananse yɛɛ ne ho sɛ ɔrewu. Ananse no, nnipa no maa no so kɔsiee no wɔ ne turo no mu. Wɔsan baa fie yɛɛ ayiyɛ amanne pa ara. "Aso" nso, ɔyɛɛ amanneɛ a ɛfa kununa no ho. Bere a wɔrekyerɛ da a ɛtɔ so nwɔtwe no, "Aso" kae sɛ, "Eh, ma me kɔhwɛ me kunu no turo no. " "Aso" kɔeɛ, ɛhɔ na ɛwɔ turo no, dadwa! "Aso" kaa sɛ, "Oh, garden a me kunu no yɛeɛ na me aniwa bɛduru so (ma me aduane no), nnipa bi aba a wɔadidi mu nyinaa. " Ɛnneɛ na ɔsui na ɔbɔɔ nnome (a curse) sɛ, " (Mefrɛ) Kwaku Ananse obi a ɔwɔ honhom, nnɔbaeɛ a ɛwɔ w’afuo mu a wode gyaw maa me no, nnipa aba a wɔadidi. "

Kwaku Ananse de ne nne kɔɔ soro (too nni) na ɔtoo dwom sɛ:

" Gyae (complaining)! Me kɛkɛ na me didi!

Gyae (nwiinwii), "Aso" atetesɛm a wɔde yi nwira no.

Gyae (complaining), me nsa aka nea ɛyɛ me deɛ!

Gyina (complaining), Aso akyiwadeɛ rehyew (the turo mu wira). Gyina (complaining), me nni aduan a ɛnyɛ me dea!

Gyae (ka nwiinwii), "Aso" ntotoe.

Gyina (complaining), me nni nea me ho yɛ me deɛ!

Ɛno na ɛbaa so, "Aso" gyinaa hɔ, na ne ho yɛɛ no ​​suro. Ɔkɔɔ fie na ɔtoo Adanko, Hare no, sɛ ɔmmra sɛ ɔmmɛte nsɛmfua bi a ɛwɔ ne kunu afuo hɔ. Hare no ne ɔno kɔɔ kwan. "Aso" duu afuo hɔ, na ɔsɔree (ne nne) bio sɛ ɔteɛ:

"Me kunu ne Kwaku, Ananse, na ɔwuiɛ na ɔgyaa n’afuo no, Na nnipa bi baeɛ na wɔdidi n’afuo no mu aduane.

Kwaku, Ananse no, me kɔnkyerɛ me, me srɛ mo, momma mo kumm me.

Kwaku Ananse maa ne nne so:

"Me nteɛm (complaining), me dii nea ɛyɛ me dea!

Gyae (nwiinwii), "Aso" atetesɛm a wɔde yi nwira no.

Gyae (complaining), me nsa aka nea ɛyɛ me deɛ!

Gyina (complaining), Aso akyiwadeɛ rehyew (the turo mu wira).

Mugyae (complaining), me nni nea ɛyɛ me nko!

Gyae (ka nwiinwii), "Aso" ntotoe.

Mugyae (complain), me nni nea me ho kura; Mugyae (complain)!

Ɔbɔadeɛ a wɔyɛeɛ no, woahu ɔbaa bi a ɔmmoa ne kunu?

Gyae (complain), me nni biribi a ɛyɛ me deɛ; Gyae (complain)!"

Hare no kaa sɛ, "Me werɛ awie, yɛn kɔ fie na meka nea ɛkɔ so no nyinaa kyerɛ mo. " Ɔne "Aso" no kɔɔ fie. Hare kaa sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ, 'Momma yɛnkɔsi turo'; wokae sɛ worenkɔ; 'Momma yɛnkɔhyew (the dry weeds)'; na wokae sɛ worenkɔ; 'Momma yɛnkɔdɔ'; na wokae sɛ worenkɔ; ɛno nti wotumi gyae su, efisɛ ɔredi ne deɛ ɛyɛ no, efisɛ Ɔbɔadeɛ no bɛyɛɛ a, wanhunu ɔbaa biara a ɔmmoa no kunu; ɛnneɛ gyae lamenti ng (he says), for he has only eaten all that belonged to him. " Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Sɛ obiara a ɔwɔ adwuma biara mu na ɔbisa wo a, boa no. "

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
