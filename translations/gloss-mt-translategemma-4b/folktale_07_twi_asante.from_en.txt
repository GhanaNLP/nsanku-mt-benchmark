SƐNEA LEOPARD NO ANI BƐYƐƐ NO KƐSEƐ

NA (bere bi) na Nwansena ne Daboɔ bi, wɔwɔ hɔ, na Daboɔ no ka kyerɛɛ Nwansena sɛ, "Momma yɛn kɔhwɛ mmaa a yɛbɛware wɔn. " Nwansena no kae sɛ, "Ho. " Ɛneɛ Daboɔ no hohoroo ne ho, na ɔde ne sika kɔkɔɔ agude no siesiee ne ho, na ɔkyekyeree ne mpa a na ɔda so no, na ɔde maa Nwansena sɛ ɔmfa nna etire so. Na wɔkɔɔ kwan; wɔbɛduruu akuraase bi. Ɛno na Nwansena kaa sɛ, "Mma ne Agyanom nyinaa, mema mo anwummere nkyea. " Wɔn nso kaa sɛ, "Yɛn kunu pa. " Daboɔ nso bɛduruu hɔ, ɔkaa sɛ, "Mma ne Agyanom nyinaa, mema mo anwummere nkyea. " Wɔn nso kaa sɛ, "Wo ne ano fĩ a wopɛ sɛ wode anwummere nkyea ma yɛn? " Wɔn kɔɔ so kɔɔ akuraa a ɛdi hɔ no, na wɔduruu hɔ. ‘Wansana, Nwansena, kae sɛ, “Agyanom nyinaa ne mma nyinaa, mema mo anwummere nkyea. ” Wɔn nso yɛɛ wɔn adeɛ wɔ saa kwan no so. Daboɔ no nso bɛduruu hɔ, na ɔhyiaa wɔn, na wɔn nso yɛɛ wɔn adeɛ sɛ,

"Wo ne ano fĩ no, wopɛ sɛ yɛma wo anwummere nkyea? " Wɔpueeɛ, wɔkɔɔ kwan so, Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ ha! monni kɛtɛ dada no mma me na moafa sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ yi siesie wo ho, sɛ ɛyɛ kɛtɛ dada nti na mmabaawa no dɔ wo a, mebɛhu. " Na (ɔne Nwansena) gyee ahosiesiedeɛ no, ɔsiesiee ne ho, na wɔpueeɛ kɔduruu akuraa a ɛdi hɔ no. Daboɔ no na ɛwɔ anim, na ɔpanyin no mat a ɛwɔ etire no; ɔtoo nsa (maa nkuraasefoɔ). Wɔkae sɛ, "Mma mo nsa nka yɛn a yɛwɔ ano fĩ yi, ɛfata sɛ wode saa dɔn-dɔn a ɛyɛ dedaw yi so. " Ɛneɛ Nwansena no bɛduruu hɔ a, ɔkaa sɛ, "Agyanom nyinaa ne Agyanom nyinaa, mema mo anwummere nkyea. " Na wɔbɛyɛɛ wɔn nsɛm sɛ, "Oh woyɛ dɔfo; ha na ɛwɔ mmabunu a ɛfata sɛ yɛhwɛ no, sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ; sɛnea woyɛ wo no, sɛ ɛnyɛ borɔ a yɛbɛnya no, anka yɛbɛka wo ho wɔ wo. "Na wɔkɔɔ so. Ɛnkyɛree na wɔduruu kwan no so, na Daboɔ no kae sɛ, "Hey! friend, gyina hɔ, gye me sika agudeɛ no mma me ntɛm, ntɛm, ntɛm. " Nwansena no wiee ahosiesiedeɛ no ne nhabannwono no akyi a, Daboɔ no hwɛɛ kwan no so na ɔde nhabannwono no kyekyeree Nwansena no. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Me bɛkyekyere wo, na wodeɛn na wodeɛbɛka? ” Na Nwansena no kaa sɛ, “Me nni hwee ka. ” Daboɔ no kɔɔ so kaa sɛ, “Me ne wo mpo nni beae a obi rekɔ . . . (nanso mmaa no deɛ, wɔrebu wo ɔdɔ no dedaw. )” Ɛneɛ Daboɔ no bɛkyeree no nsɔmu no, na ɔde no bɔɔ dua bi so. Na ɔno nso kɔɔ baabi foforɔ kɔhuntaa ne ho. Ɛnkyɛeɛ bi na Borɔdeɛ no twaa kwan so kɔɔ dwaso, na berɛ a ɔhunuu no (Nwansena) no, ɔkaa sɛ:

“Hwan na ɔregyina hɔ a ɔyɛ tuntum dodo?

Hwan na ɛregyina hɔ a ɛyɛ glossy saa? ”

(Wɔn a wɔfrɛ wɔn Nwansena) no kaa sɛ:

“Ɛyɛ me, Nwansena, Kwao Boakye.

Adenen!

Gyahene Kofi Amo, Adenen!

Ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meba, na yɛakɔ hwehwɛ mmabaawa.

Adenen!

Wɔn mmabaawa no kyerɛ sɛ wɔn nni no dɔ.

Adenen!

Waka me ho.

Adenen!

Waka me ho koraa.

Adenen!

Ogye me, ogye me.

Adenen!

Wode me bɛgye a, fa kwan kɔ.

Adenen! ” Nanso Borɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Daboɔ no akyekyere wo, enti me ntumi mma wo kwan"; na ɔkɔɔ ne ho. Mankani, Coco-plantain no, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) ɔkwan so; Aburoo, Aburoo, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) ɔkwan so; Atwibo, Bayere, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) ɔkwan so; Adua, Adua, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) ɔkwan so; Akam, Ntɔmmo, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) ɔkwan so; Akapinkye, Red-roasted plantain no, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) ɔkwan so; Bankye, Bankye, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) ɔkwan so; Borɔferɛ, Paw-paw no, baeɛ (na ɛkɔɔ) no saa; Emo, Ɛmo, no baa (na ɛkɔɔ) no saa; Efrere, Fɛre, no baa (na ɛkɔɔ) no saa; Habayere, Bush-yam, no baa (na ɛkɔɔ) no saa; Kwadu, Kwadu, no baa (na ɛkɔɔ) no saa; Nkontommire, Coco-yam leaves, no baa (na ɛkɔɔ) no saa; Krobona, Adua, no baa (na ɛkɔɔ) no saa; Mako, red-pepper, no baa (na ɛkɔɔ) no saa er; Nkateɛ, Ground-nut, no baeɛ (kɔɔ) ɔkwan so; Nwiadowa, Garden-egg, no baeɛ (kɔɔ) ɔkwan so; Nkruma, Nkruma, no baeɛ (kɔɔ) ɔkwan so; na seesei Dokon'fa, Fa-a-ball-of-Corn (Boiled-pounded-corn), no baeɛ kwan so kae sɛ:

"Hwan na ɔregyina hɔ a ɔyɛ tuntum dodo?

"Hwan na ɛregyina hɔ a ɛyɛ glossy saa? "

(Wɔn a wɔfrɛ wɔn Nwansena) no kaa sɛ:

"Ɛyɛ me, Nwansena, Kwao Boakye, Adenen!

Gyahene Kofi Amo, Adenen!

Ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meba, na yɛakɔ hwehwɛ mmabaawa.

Adenen!

Wɔn mmabaawa no kyerɛ sɛ wɔn nni no dɔ.

Adenen!

Waka me ho.

Adenen!

Waka me ho koraa.

Adenen!

Ogye me, ogye me.

Adenen!

Adenen!

Dɔkono fã-fã bi kae sɛ, "Meate, mebɛgyigye wo. " Ɔkɔɔ hɔ, na ɔgyee no. Bere a ɔyɛɛ saa wieeɛ no, mpofirim na Daboɔ no puei. Ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woyii me ɔbarima no? " (Half-a-bɔɔlo-of-Dɔkono) no kaa sɛ, "Me yii no, na wode nea wobɛyɛ no yɛ. " (The Daboɔ) no kaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, me ne wo renyɛ hwee koraa, me ne wo nko ara na yɛbɛdi yɛn nkuturu no. " Half-a-bɔɔlo-of-Dɔkono kaa sɛ, "Sɛ yɛbɛdi a, yɛn kɔ nwura no nkyɛn, na yɛn nni nnyina no mu nnyina no sisian. " Ɛnneɛ wɔkɔɔ so bɔɔ nnyina no, na ɛsan baeɛ. Na ɔno (Half-a-ball-of-Dɔkono) no kae sɛ, "Yɛnhyɛ no ogya. " Na Daboɔ no de sisian sii hɔ. Simultaneous no na wɔbɔɔ ɔko no, na nnyigyei no yɛɛ Yiri! Yiri! Yiri! Na Daboɔ no atutu Half-a-ball-of-Dɔkono no, na wɔatutu no, bam! Half-a-ball-of-Dɔkono kaa sɛ, "(adeɛ bi a ɛte saa no nni hwee), obi fa adeɛ a ɛte saa na ɔde hohoro baabi a ne yere hohoro no. " Na wɔn hyiaa bio. Na Half-a-ball-of-Dɔkono nso abɔ Daboɔ no afiri ne ho, bam! Na Daboɔ no kae sɛ, "Me nso mefa adeɛ a ɛte saa mma me yere bɔɔdrom, (nanso) yɛn ahyiadie a ɛdi hɔ no, sɛ obi bɔ obi so a, ɛneɛ ɛno ne tow a etwa toɔ. " Na wɔne wɔn ho bɔɔ mu (ɔko no, na nnyigyei no te sɛ) Kikiri! Kikiri! Kikiri! Na Half-a-bɔɔlo-of-Dɔkono no yii Daboɔ no maa ɔhwiee no ogya mu. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "_Me firim, me firim, me firim_" (Me firim, me firim, me firim. Saa na Daboɔ no nyaa ne su "_Me firim_"). (Ɛneɛ) wɔhunuu sɛ ne nipadua no te sɛɛ, kurukyire, kurukyire, kurukyire (ɛne sɛ, ne nyinaa yɛ kurukyire); baabi a fitaa no wɔ no, ɛhɔ na nso no aka no; na baabi a tuntum no wɔ no, ɛhɔ nso na bidie tuntum no aka no. Na bere a worenanteɛ, na wuhu ahaban a ɛda hɔ a Half-a-bɔɔlo-of-Dɔkono (has been wrapped) no, wubehu sɛ nwansena pii rebɛn so; na ɛnyɛ sianteɛ biara nti, wɔrekyerɛ no.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom na mo betumi afa no nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a mo bɛbɔ me (wɔ asɛmka no ho) no.
