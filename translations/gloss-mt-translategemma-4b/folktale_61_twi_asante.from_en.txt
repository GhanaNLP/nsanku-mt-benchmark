Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ BERE A OBI KƆ DANFIE BI MU ANADWO NO, ƐYƐ SUM.

WƆka sɛ Kuntunkurunku, Akwanya, ne Kuntunamoa, Awieeɛ, hwe fam fi silin so kɔɔ fam. Wɔkae sɛ, "Old-mother-earth, woyɛ (too) flat. " Asase Yaa no kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wose me (too) flat? " Wɔn nso kaa sɛ, "Ɛfiri sɛ woantwitwa ɛtokuro bi amma yɛn nna. " Old-mother-earth no de ne ho kyerɛɛ Prae, Mpopaho no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Wo ano (is covered with) nkokɔ mmoa bini. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wodi me bɔne? " Old-mother-earth kaa sɛ, "Sɛ mede me bɔne a, mede me bɔne; sɛ woanni me bɔne a, woanni me bɔne. Te sianteɛ ase, gyae bisa. " Mpopaho no danee ne ho kyerɛɛ Abena, fa a ɛteateaa (a refuse no gu mu), na ɔkae sɛ, "Wode (nneɛma) kɔ na woyi gu. " Fa a ɛteateaa no kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woyɛ me bɔne? " Mpopaho no kae sɛ, "Sɛnea ɛte biara, me ayɛ saa. " Fa a ɛteateaa no nso ne Sumina ne Midden-heap no dii nkɔmmɔ sɛ, "Mo kura nea mo nsa ka. " Midden-heap no kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woyɛ me bɔne? " Fa a ɛteateaa no kae sɛ, "Me yɛɛ saa. " Mmuaeɛ no sɛe sɛ, "Me dii wo bɔne, na sɛ woannim sianteɛ no a, gyae bisa. " Midden-heap nso, ɔne Abe, Palm-tree no, kɔɔ so kae sɛ, "Wo kɔn no nyinaa adɔɔso nwura. " Palm-tree no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wodi me bɔne? " Midden-heap no nso kaa sɛ, "Me dii wo bɔne, na sɛ woannim sianteɛ no nti, gyae bisa. " Palm-tree nso, ɔne Tetea, Little-black-ant no, kɔɔ so kae sɛ, "Wo aso no yɛ bla ck. "Little-black-ant kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na woyɛ me basabasa? " Palm-tree no buae sɛ, "Me yɛɛ wo basabasa, na sɛ woannim sianteɛ no a, gyae me bisa. " Little-black-ant no, ɔno nso, ɔkɔɔ Esono, Ɛsono a ɛwɔ hɔ no, na ɔkɔɔ ne nan so; na ɔbɔɔ Ɛsono no. Ɛsono no reyi ne nan, ɛneɛ na ɔtwaa Tamiriwa, Black-snail no, aniwa ase, na Black-snail no aniwa no hyɛɛ aseɛ yɛɛ nsuo, na ɔde nsuo no guu fan nhahan bi a ɛbɛn hɔ so. (Mprempren), aduane a Old-woman-grandmother no di no ne fan dua no, na ɛwɔ so sɛ Tamiriwa, Black-snail no, atwa nsuo no afiri n’aniwa so. ‘Ɔkraman, Kraman, na ɔrepaeɛ, na ɔbaa (Old-woman-grandmother) no frɛɛ Kraman no na ɔfaa ne nnyina (she was carrying) no kɔmaa Kraman no sɛ ɔmfa n’etire so. Sɛnea Kraman rekɔ no, ɔnyaa kola-nut bi, na ɔkyekyee bi mu mmienu, na ɔde bi guu Okagya (Squirrel) no hole no ano. Okagya no, ɔno nso, ɔfaa kola-nut no bɔɔ Onina, Silk-cotton-tree no, so. Silk-cotton-tree no nso, abufuhyeɛ bɛyɛɛ ne nufuɔ no kɔkɔɔ. Ɔhunuu sɛ abofra ketewa bi refiri hɔ, na ɔtwee ne baa bɔɔ no ma ɔwui. Seesei, saa abofra ketewa no na ɔyɛ Nyankopɔn, Ewiem-onyankopɔn no abofra. Ɛhɔ ara na Silk-cotton-tree no bɛyɛɛ nea wɔkyeree no, na wɔde no kɔɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn no anim. Wɔbisaa no (Silk-cotton-tree) sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ asɛm? " Silk-cotton-tree no kaa sɛ, "Na me gyina hɔ wɔ me ho bi, berɛ a saa Opuro yi—ne ba a ɔte aseɛ—(dared) sɛ ɔbɛtwe kola-nut bi abɔ me, ɛnneɛ me deɛ, berɛ a saa abofra yi retwam no, na me susuw sɛ ɔyɛ Okagya, Opuro no, na me yii me nkorabata bi bɔɔ no, na ɛhɔ na ɛhɔ na ɛhɔ, ɛyɛ wo abofra. " Niamey, Osutɔ-hene no, ɔkae sɛ, "Fa no kɔ na fa no bɔ dunsin, na frɛ Okagya, Opuro no, sɛ ɔmmra. " Okagya baeɛ; ɔkae sɛ, "Sire, wunim sɛ me akyiwadeɛ ne kola-nut, nanso Ɔkraman, Kraman no, kɔgyee baako, na ɔtow guu ɛtokuro no kwanano, na mebɔ no, ɛkɔɔ Old-man-silk-cotton-tree no so. " Osutɔ-hene no kae sɛ, "Fa no kɔ, na fa no bɔ dunsin, na frɛ Kraman no sɛ ɔmmra. " T hey kɔɔ so frɛɛ Kraman sɛ ɔmmra. Kraman no kaa sɛ, "Sire, na me kɔnkyini me ho bi—seesei wuhunu sɛnea (delicate) me—bere a Old-woman-grandmother de ne babaso nnyina no sii me etire so no, seesei Okagya, Opuro no, ɛyɛ ɔno na ɔda ɛtokuro no mu wɔ afuo a Old-woman-grandmother ayɛ no mu sɛ ɔbɛnya biribi adi, nanso ɛyɛ me na Old-woman-grandmother yɛɛ me sɛ mefa ne nnyina no so; me nso, me gyee kola-nut bi a nd threw it at the kwanano to his ɛtokuro. " Sky-god no kaa sɛ, "Fa no, fa no bɛkyekyere dunsin bi, na frɛ Old-woman-grandmother sɛ ɔmmra. " Old-woman-grandmother no baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ah, me mmananom, monhwɛ me ano, me a me ntumi nni nam no, fan nhaban na me di; na me koraa, me hohoro Bosompra; me akyiwadeɛ (ɛno nti) Tamiriwa, Black-snail no; Kraman nko ara na na onim eyi, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ Tamiriwa, na ɔmaa no faa nsuo firii ne ano mu, na ɔde guu fan nhaban a me te so no mu. Ɛno nti na mehyɛɛ no nnyina sɛ ɔnsoa no. " Sky-god no kaa sɛ, "Oh, fa no kɔkyekyere no dunsin so, na kɔfa Tamiriwa, Black-snail no. " Black-snail no baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sire, me ho na me wɔ baabi, hwɛ, asono kɛseɛ bi atia me wɔ baabi a ɛwɔ me aniwa ase, na mekɔfa efan (leaf) kɔpira no. Ɛno ne nea me nim fa ho. " Sky-god no kae sɛ, "Fa no kɔkyekyere no wɔ dunsin bi so, na fa Esono, Ɛsono no. " Wɔkɔgyee Esono. Ɛsono no kaa sɛ, "Ɛbɛyɛ nnansa a atwam ni na me te hɔ, na me wedeɛ no hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ ɛyɛ me kɔɔkɔɔ, na mehwɛɛ baabi no a, mekyerɛkyerɛ me asɛmfua mu sɛ, kwasea a Little-black-ant yi de ne ho hyɛɛ me wedeɛ mu sɛ mepɛ nam mma me nyini no, ɛno na ɔde ne ho hyɛɛ Tamiriwa, Black-snail no so. " Sky-god no kaa sɛ, "Fa no kɔ na fa no bɔ dunsin bi ho, na fa no brɛ. Tetea, the Little-black-ant. " Ɛberɛ a Tetea baeɛ no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Sire, there is a Palm-tree; when it bears, (its nuts) are first black; when I, the Little-black-ant, walk over them, it causes them to ripen. Ɛda a ɛtwa toɔ no, na me wɔ hɔ, berɛ a Palm-tree no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ me aso ayɛ me tuntum, na ɛfiri sɛ me ho yɛ me ketewa dodo, na me dwen sɛ mebɛkɔ akɔ Ɛsono hɔ, akɔhwehwɛ nam bi adi a mebɛnyini akɛseɛ. ” Sky-god no kaa sɛ, “Fa no kɔkyekyere no wɔ dunsin bi so, na kɔfrɛ Palm-tree no ba. ” Abe, Palm-tree no, baeɛ; ɔkaa sɛ, “Sire, eyi ne nea ɛsɛ sɛ meka: me ne Sumina, Midden-heap no, yɛte t Me ne no bom, na nwura a wɔtow gu no so no bɛdɔɔso dodo, na ɔantumi annyina bio, megye no bi ato me kɔn ho; nanso seesei, ɔbɔɔ me soboɔ sɛ me nni kɔn ho. Ɛno nti, me deɛ, me aniwa bɛyɛɛ me kɔkɔɔ, na me nso mebɛdii Little-black-ant no ho fɛw. ” Sky-god no kaa sɛ, “Fa no kɔkyekyere no kɔ dunsin so, na frɛ Sumina, Midden-heap, sɛ ɔmmra. ” Midden-heap no baeɛ; ɔkaa sɛ, “Sire, Abena, Fa a ɛteateaa-of-dua hono, a wɔn a wɔbɛbɔ nwura wɔ dan mu no, wɔmma wɔn sɛ wɔmfa dane mma me. Ɛda a ɛtwaa toɔ no, mfomsoɔ biara nni ho, ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, ‘You store up what you receive’ (i. e. yɛɛ greedy); Me kae sɛ, ‘a’, na Palm-tree no nso bɛn hɔ a, me nso mekyerɛɛ no bɔne. ” Sky-god no kae sɛ, “Fa no na fa no bɔ dunsin so, na frɛ Abena, Fa a ɛteateaa-of-dua hono, sɛ ɔmmra. ” Fa a ɛteateaa-of-dua hono no bae; ɔkae sɛ, “Sire, bere a Prae, Mpopaho no, reprepra dan bi mu a, sɛ wɔmfa mpopaho mma me a, mframa no bɔ a, nwura no nyinaa san ba bio; sesei, ɛda a ɛtwa to no, ɛwom sɛ me nyɛɛ no hwee de, nanso ɔno deɛ. kae sɛ, ‘Wode nneɛma kɔ nko ara sɛ wobɛto afiri hɔ. ’ Ɛno nti, me nso me aniwa bɛyɛɛ me kɔkɔɔ, na me kɔdii Sumina, Midden-heap no, ho firi. ’ Sky-god no kae sɛ, ‘Fa no, na fa no bɔ dunsin, na frɛ Prae, Mpopaho no, sɛ ɔmmra ha. ” Mpopaho no baeɛ; ɔkae sɛ, ‘Agya, Old-mother-earth no da hɔ ara pɛpɛɛpɛ, n’ani bɔne nti, bere biara a mebɛsɔre no, mebɛbɔ ne fi nyinaa ato ne so ama no ayɛ. Beautiful; yet the other day, Mother-earth addressed me, saying, ‘Your ano is (full of) Mother-earth’s mmoa bini,’ and my nufuɔ too, became choked, and I went and abused Abena, the Sky-god of dua hono. ” The Sky-god said, “Take her and fasten her to a dunsin, and call on Mother-earth to come. ” When Mother-earth came, she said, “Sire, there I lie; they tread upon me; I never say anything; they do all adeɛ Me so, nanso meka asɛm biara. Ɛnneɛ ɛda a ɛtwa toɔ no, Kuntunkurunku ne Kuntunamoa, Akwanya ne Awieeɛ, bɛhwee me so, na mekae sɛ, ‘O! Ɛdɛn na ɛyɛ asɛm? ’ na wɔkae sɛ, ‘Woantutu ɛtokuro bi a yɛbɛtumi atoro so, na wo ne wo tratrayɛ nyinaa nni dwumadie biara (mfa biara). ’ Ɛno nti, me nso, mekyerɛɛ Prae, Mpopaho no. ’ Sky-god no kae sɛ, ‘Fa no kɔkyekyere no wɔ dunsin bi so, na kɔgye Akwanya ne Awieeɛ’. Akwanya ne Awieeɛ baeɛ. Wɔkaa sɛ, “Sire, na yɛwɔ silin so, na yɛredi agorɔ, na yɛhwe fam yɛpiraa yɛn ho; ɛno nti na yɛbɔɔ Mother-earth no soboɔ sɛ ɔyɛ flat. ” Sky-god no kaa sɛ, “Councillors, yɛate asɛm yi nyinaa, nti yɛnsɔre nkɔ dan no mu na yɛnsusu adanseɛ no na yɛmmra mmɛdi atɛmmuo. ” Ɛnneɛ wɔsɔre kɔɔ dan bi mu, na wɔtoo ɔpono no. Ɛno nti, sɛ woko dan bi mu anadwo,—sum kabii—Ewiem ne Mpɔtam badwafoɔ no redi nkɔmmɔ wɔ hɔ.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
