SƐNEA AKOKƆNINI NO AKƆKONINI NO NYAA ANO A ANO YƐ NNAM NO.

AGYA ANANSE, Ananse no, na ɔne ne mmofra Afudotwedotwe, Nyiwankonfwea (Thin-shanks), (ne) Tikononkono (Big-big-head) no te hɔ. Na Ananse ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ mo kɔ hwehwɛ borɔdɔma ma me di. " Ɛnneɛ, mmofra yi kɔɔ kwan so, na wɔnantew na wɔnantew, na wɔnantew, na wɔn nsa bɛka fig-tree bi, na (borɔdɔma) no abere, na mmofra no kɔɔ dua no ase. Afudotwedotwe (Yafunu-like-to-paeeɛ) kaa sɛ Nyiwankɔnfwea (Thin-shanks), "Climb and pluck some of them. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, if I climb my anan will break. " Tikononkono (Big-big-head) kaa sɛ, "You, Afudotwedotwe, do you climb up. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, if I climb up, my yafunu will paeeɛ; you climb yourself. " Ɛnneɛ Tikononkono (Big-big-head) no foroo so. Ɔtwee fig-tree no mmofra no bi kɔɔ so tuu kɔɔ so, ɛneɛ na ɔkae sɛ, "Anka ɛno bɛyɛ? " Wɔn ahomegyeɛ no kae sɛ, "Ah, (how do we know? )." Ɛneɛ na ɔkɔɔ so tuu wɔn; ɛneɛ na ɔsan bisaa wɔn sɛ, "Anka ɛno bɛyɛ? " Na wɔn nso buae sɛ, "Lower your etire and look. " Na berɛ a ɔrebɛtew etire no, sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ a, na etire no bɛbɛka fam, kae sɛ (a asɛmfua bi te sɛ) "Kum! " Berɛ a Belly-lik E-to-paeeɛ no, anka ɛbɛsere, ne yafunu paeeɛ no de ɔtopae bi, ta! Thin-shanks no, bere a ɔbɛdwiri akɔka n’agya no, na n’anan no abubu na aka ne ho wɔ ɛban no akyi. Akokɔnini no, na ɔrehoro ne ɔkra no, na ɔrekɔ akɔto afɔdeɛ no (egg-shells) no, bere a ɔhunuu sɛ Thin-shanks no abɔ fam na n’anan no abubu, na aka no wɔ ɛban no foforɔ so. Bere a ɔrebɛsere no, n’ano bɛyɛɛ nea ɛyɛ nnam:

"Bere a ɔrebɛsere no, na n’ano ayɛ nna.

Bere a ɔrebɛsere no, na n’ano ayɛ n’asɛn. "

Ɛno nti, Mpanimfoɔ no ka sɛ, "Sɛ obiara bɛhwe fam a, kɔhwɛ no, na mmu no fɛre. " Ɛnneɛ Akokɔnini no deɛ, ɔyɛɛ saa, na ɔsereɛ nti, n’ano bɛyɛɛ nea ɛyɛ nsem; kan no na ɔwɔ ano a ɛyɛ fɛ, fɛfɛɛfɛ.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
