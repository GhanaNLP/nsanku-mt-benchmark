SƐNEA ƐBAA NO, ODWENNINI NO KWATIMA BAE SƐ WƆREBƐGYE ODWENNINI NO DIN.

Ɛneɛ Kwatima, Odwennini no, de ne ho kɔɔ hɔ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwɛ asase a ɔpɛ sɛ ɔgye so; na ɔde ahyɛdeɛ a ɛwɔ mu sɛ ɔbɛtumi atete fie. Ayamye me! Ayamye me! Ayamye me! Ayamye me Ɛyɛ nokware sɛ me nananom nsamanfo rebɛboa me, (hwɛ) fam a wɔatutu no. " Na ɔno nso, ɔde ne ho bɛbɔɔ nwura no mu kɔe. Daboɔ no nso, ɛno nso na ɛbaeɛ. Ɔkae sɛ, "Oh! ɛyɛ asɛm bi; ɛkame ayɛ sɛ mebɛyɛ me ho hu wɔ ho. " Na ɔno nso kɔɔ nwura no nkyɛn, ɔtwaa adunsina no guu hɔ (wɔ beaeɛ hɔ). Odwennini no nso baeɛ, na ɔno nso de ahodwiriw bɛhwɛɛ kwan. Ɔno nso kɔɔ nwura no mu, kɔtwaa nhahamama, na ɔde guu hɔ kɔɔ. Na bio Daboɔ no baeɛ. Ɔno nso hwɛɛ akyiri (wɔ nkɔsoɔ a ɛwɔ hɔ no ho), na ɔde ne ho bɔɔ fie kɔɔ. Bio Kwatima baeɛ, na ɔtutu atokuro no mu de adunsina guu mu, na ɔkɔɔ. Na bio Daboɔ no baeɛ, na ɔkyekyeree nnua a ɛtwam no so, na ɔkɔɔ. Na bio Kwatima baeɛ, na ɔkɔgyee sare no kɔtoo hɔ kɔeɛ. Daboɔ no bio baeɛ, na ɛkeka atɛkyɛ no mu yɛɛ plaster no; na ɛkɔeɛ. Na bio Kwatima baeɛ, na ɔyɛɛ ɛdan atifi kata, na ɔkɔeɛ. Na bio Daboɔ no baeɛ, na ɛbɔɔ fam ahodoɔ no, na ɛkɔeɛ. Ɛneɛ Kwatima baeɛ nso, na ɔyi dɔte fitaa yɛɛ afasuo no, na ɔkɔɔ fie. Bere a ɔkɔeɛ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ n’abofra sɛ, "Me da akyi meawie fie no, na Akwasidae no bɛtɔ a, Memeneda a ɛdi hɔ no, yɛbɛyɛ ofie foforɔ hyɛ ase afahyɛ no, nti monhwɛ nnipa a wɔwɔ atuo sɛ wɔbɛkɔ akɔtutu dan no mu. " Seesei Daboɔ no nso baeɛ (na wɔhunuu fie a awie no). Ɔkae sɛ, "Eh! Me nni hwee nka, na mmom mebɛkɔ akɔfa me agyapadeɛ ne me san ba. " Ɛneɛ na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ n’abofra no sɛ, "Muntumi sɛnea wubetumi na ka kyerɛ Ghost-hunter no sɛ, Adae no bɛtɔ wɔ Kwasiada bi so a, Memeneda a ɛdi hɔ no mebɛdi ofie foforɔ hyɛ ase afahyɛ no, ɛnneɛ ma ɔmmra na ɔmmɔ tuo mma me. " Na n’abofra no kɔɔ so ka kyerɛɛ no. Ɔno (Ghost-hunter) no kaa sɛ, "Meate". Ɛneɛ wɔ da a ɛdi Adae no, Kwatima gyee n’agyapadeɛ no, na ɔde kɔtoo fie hɔ, na ɔsan baeɛ. Na Daboɔ no nso gyee ne deɛ, na ɔde kɔtoo hɔ. Bere a ɛda adi sɛ nneɛma bɛhu no, wɔ Akwasidae no, Kwatima kɔɔ n’anim kɔtoo nnipa a ɔne wɔn tena no nyinaa; na Daboɔ nso yɛɛ ne nkrabew. Ɛdwoada, berɛ a nneɛma bɛpuee (i.e. anɔpatutuutu no), Kwatima ne n’abofra no kɔɔ (kɔɔ ofie foforɔ no mu); ɛhɔ kɔtoo tuo guu dan no mu. Na Daboɔ no nso baeɛ; ɔtee tuo no; ɔkaa sɛ, "To-da no nyɛ da pa, to-da ahonhom no reyi atuo. " Na ɔno nso baeɛ; na Ghost-hunter no nso kɔɔ fie hɔ, na ɔmaa tuo no teɛm, pomm! Daboɔ no baa hɔ na ɔhunuu Kwatima ne n’abofra no te hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn ne wo eguadwa? " Kwatima nso kaa sɛ, "Wo nso, dɛn ne wo eguadwa? " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ mo nni sianteɛ (for coming here) a, me nso me nni. " Na Kwatima kaa sɛ, "Sɛ ɛte saa a, ɛnneɛ yɛn mmienu nyinaa bɛtena hɔ. " Ɛnneɛ wɔtenaa hɔ. Da biara, Daboɔ no sɔre a, na ɔkɔ ahayɔ, na ɔsan de nam ba. Ɛyɛɛ daa. Nanso Kwatima deɛ, na ɔmfa biribiara mmra. Ɛneɛ da bi, osurofoɔ no mu baako kɔɔ nwura no so, ɔbɔɔ ne ho mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛkyere adeɛ bi, nanso wannya. Ɔsan baa fie, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Kwatima, sɛ da bi woankɔ nwura no so na woamfa nam bi amma a, nea mebɛyɛ wo no, worennye. " Kwatima yii ne ho kɔɔ nwura no so, na ɔsan de nwura bi baa hɔ. Daboɔ no hunuu aboa no, ɛyɛɛ no nwanwa sɛ adeɛ biara. Berɛ a nneɛma no puei (i.e. anɔpatutuutu no), Kwatima kɔɔ ne so, na bio ɔsan de aboa foforɔ bi (antelope) baeɛ. Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "E! " Ɔkaa no kyerɛɛ n’abofra no sɛ, "Hwɛ ha, me kɔ nwura no mu, na me de Kwatima nso rekɔ, na ɔtwa ba a me de baa wo nkyɛn da a ɛtwaa mu no, bere a yɛakɔ no, frɛ Kwatima abofra no na bisa no sɛ, 'W’agya no kɔ nwura no mu kɔkyere nam no, kyerɛkyerɛ ne ho wɔ ɔtwee yi so sɛnea ɔyɛ. " Na nnipa no (Osebo ne Kwatima) no kɔɔ so. Na Leopard ba no kae sɛ, "Kwatima ba, ba bɛdi agorɔ. " Ɔbaa; ɔkae sɛ, "Sɛ agya kɔ nwura a, ɛno na ɔfa so kyere agorɔ. " Na ɔhuruu, na ɔkyeree ɔtwa ba no na ɔkuraa ne menem. Kwatima ba no kae sɛ, "Wokɔfa ɔbotan bi bɛba. " Leopard ba no kɔɔ hɔ de bi bae. Ɔde sii hɔ. Kwatima ba no de n’akyi kɔeɛ na ɛbaa hɔ na ɛne ɔboɔ no bɔɔ etire, pim! na ɛbɔɔ no nsinnsin. Leopard ba no kaa sɛ, "Ah. " Na wɔn agyanom no firi nwura no mu baeɛ, na Leopard ba no kaa n’agya nea ɛkɔɔ so, na Kwatima ba no nso kaa n’agya nea ɛkɔɔ so. Ɛnneɛ na wɔwɔ hɔ, berɛ a da bi Kwatima nan bɛyɛɛ no, na ɔrebɛbɔ no afiri so, nanso Daboɔ no dwen sɛ ɔrehyɛ no ahyɛdeɛ sɛ ɔnkum no. Ɛnneɛ Daboɔ no tuu mmirika kɔɔ Kwatima so wɔ ahyɛdeɛ sɛ wɔbɛkyere no. Na Kwatima tuu mmirika kɔɔ kiri, kiri, kiri. Ɔkɔɔ asuwea bi a ɛwɔ hɔ, a Ɔnanka bi wɔ mu. Kwatima kaa sɛ, "Papa Ɔnanka, me dwane me ba wo nkyɛn sɛ me nni bammɔ sɛ Daboɔ bɛkyere me. " Ɛhɔ ara na Ɔnanka kaa sɛ, "Wo, yɔnkoɔ, wo adi hokwan (aba me nkyɛn), nti fa me nsa. " Kwatima bɔɔ ne ho mmɔden yie na ɔne no bɔɔ nsa. Ɛno na ɛbaa sɛ Kwatima bɛnyaa Odwa-nini din (lit. Shake nsa with the Ɔnanka). Ɛfiri ne nsamukyea a ɔne Ɔnanka yɛeɛ no.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom na mo betumi afa no nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a mo bɛbɔ me (wɔ asɛmka ho) no.
