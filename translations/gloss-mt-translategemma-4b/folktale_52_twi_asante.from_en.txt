Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA NTORO A WƆFRƐ NO "ANINIEDIE" NO BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU.

Ɛdii da bi a, na obarima bi wɔ hɔ, ɔbɔfoɔ, na ɔne ne yere te akuraa ketewa bi so, na ɔyɛɛ n’adwene sɛ ɔbɛwo, nanso na ɔbarima no ntumi nhwɛ no yie, na ɔbaa no kɔɔ hɔ kɔɔ Nyankopɔn nkyɛn sɛ, " Me baeɛ yi, meba wo nkyɛn sɛ mehyɛ wo nkitahodie sɛ 'in-mmara' bi, efisɛ meba no, ɛsɛ sɛ wogye me abofra no awareɛ. " Ɛnneɛ Nyankopɔn no gyee saa asɛm yi to mu. Ɛneɛ na ɔkɔɔ Osebo, Daboɔ no, ne nso kɔhyɛɛ mmara mu nkitahodie bi mu sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na wanya nam abɔ. Daboɔ no nso penee so. Ɛneɛ na ɔkɔka ne kunu amaneɛbɔ no. Bere a Daboɔ no nyaa nam no, na ɔtaa de bi kɔma ne maame a ɔwɔ mmara mu. Ewiem onyankopɔn no nso, bere a wɔnyaa nam no, na wɔtaa de kɔma ne maame a ɔwɔ mmara mu. Wɔkɔɔ so saa ara, na wɔhwɛɛ ɔbaa no kwan kɔsii sɛ ɔwoo. Bere a ɔawoɔ no, na ɛyɛ ababaawa bi a ɔwoɔ no, na ɔma wɔn kɔ Sky-god no nkyɛn kɔkaa sɛ, " W’asɛm maame-in-mmara no de awoɔ ama ɔbabaa. " Ɛnneɛ na ɔsan nso kaa Daboɔ no sɛ, " W’asɛm maame-in-mmara no de awoɔ ama ɔbabaa. " Mprempren Daboɔ no boaboaa akyɛde ahodoɔ ebuo bi ano, na ɔmaa wɔn kɔe sɛ wɔmfa ne ba no mfi wɔn akyide ne ɔman a ɛwɔ efi. Na Ewiem-onyankopɔn no de akyɛdeɛ bi baa hɔ a, na ɛne botaeɛ koro no ara. Wɔtenaa no, na wɔmaa no nyini yie na ɛyɛ fɛ. Seesei (bere a abofra no aduru mmabunbere no), wɔne Ewiem-onyankopɔn no kɔkaa amaneɛbɔ no. Wɔne Daboɔ no nso kɔkaa amaneɛbɔ no. Ewiem-onyankopɔn no maa akyɛdeɛ bi baa nea wɔagye no awareɛ (a wɔfrɛ no akyɛdeɛ), " nneɛma a wɔde bɛbɔ bragro ɛtoɔ ". Na Daboɔ no nso, ɔde " akyɛdeɛ a wɔde bɔ bragro ɛtoɔ " baeɛ; na ɔde akyɛdeɛ a ɛyɛ dwetɛ baha nso kaa ho. Wɔwiee saa nyinaa. Daboɔ no annyim sɛ ɔno ne ɔsoro-nyam-nyame yere; na ɔsoro-nyam-nyame no nso annyim sɛ ɔno ne Daboɔ no yere. Mprempren, akyiri yi, Daboɔ no baa bɛtee ho asɛm, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame-in-mmara sɛ, " Woyɛɛ me kwasea, nanso ɛno nitan mu me nyɛ wo hwee; W’abofra no deɛ, sika kɔkɔɔ a mekyɛɛ no no, mebɛgye nea ɛyɛ me deɛ. " Ɔkyeree ne nsa, na ɔtwaa no wɔ nkasaho no so, na ɔtwee kaa no, na ɔkɔɔ n’akuraa hɔ. Mprempren Ewiem onyankopɔn no maa maame in mmara no bɔɔ amanneɛ sɛ ne yere no nyini no, ɛsɛ sɛ wɔde no ba ne nkyɛn. Ɛnneɛ a na ɔrekɔ no, wɔde batakari hyɛɛ no, na obiara anhu ne nsa. Ɛnneɛ na wɔkɔɔ kwan. Bere a na ɔreborɔ no, na ɔreborɔ anadwo. Mprempren abaayewa no na ɔwɔ nsa-maid bi a wɔfrɛ no " Ɔkra ", a ɔne no tena, na Ɔkra de bere nyinaa na ɔbɔɔ no hwɛ, na ɔhunuu sɛ ɔwɔ dua sin ma nsa. Ɛneɛ na ɔka kyerɛɛ abaayewa no co-yernom sɛ, "ɔbaa a waba ha no, ɛyɛ nsa dua sin bi, ɛnneɛ, Adae no bɛtɔ Kwasiada a, Ɛdwoada no, ma hiti ɛmo ma ɔsoro-nyam (Sky-god) no fa baabi wɔ afɔdeɛ (a ɔde ma ɔkra no). " Wɔka amaneɛbɔ no kyerɛɛ abaayewa no. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔbɔɔ ɛto, ne nkesua a wɔde aka ho (to the pɔtɔ). Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me kɔ maame wɔ hɔ, mebɛgye me ho ahoboa. " Berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔbaa tadeɛ bi a nsuo wɔ mu, na ɛmu na Ɔnini, Ɔnanka no, wɔteɛ. Ɔbisaa no sɛ, "Dɛn nti, berɛ a ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara ba ha no, woba ha? " Na ɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔnanka no amaneɛbɔ no nyinaa. Ɔnanka no kae sɛ, "Eyi nni asɛm, na ɛyɛ dɛn na wode no bae? " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Me de ɛto ne nkosua bae. " Na Ɔnanka no gyee wɔn na ɔdidi. Afei Ɔnanka no yii n’ano kyerɛɛ abaayewa no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Mommfa mo nsa dua sin no, na mo de gu me ano. " Na ɔde guu ne ano. Ɛduruu kakra bi, ɔkae sɛ, "Yiyi no. " Ɔyii no, na dua sin no afiri hɔ, na ɛbɛyɛɛ nsa ankasa. Ɛneɛ abaayewa no bisaa no sɛ, "Dɛn na wobɛfa afiri me (wɔ san ba mu)? " Ɔno na ɔkaa sɛ, "Me nni hwee mma wo, nanso abofra a wobɛsoa no Sky-god no, de no mma me. " Ɛneɛ Ɔnanka no hwɛɛ n'akyi, yii sika waduru ne sika woma, na ɔde maa abaayewa no, na ɔne no kɔɔ soro kɔɔ Sky-god no nkurase. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, na Sky-god no atena ne ho aseɛ sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mmaa no ba bɛyi ɛmo ama no. Ɛneɛ abaayewa no yii n’atadeɛ tenten no, na ɔde ne nsa (from the mmɔho) no yii ɛmo no. Ɛneɛ nnipa no nyinaa teɛm sɛ, "Yie e! " Ɛneɛ na ɔde sika waduru ne pestle no yii adi, na wɔwieeɛ ɛmo no. Ɔsoro-nyamfo no ne ɔbɛduruu hɔ, na ɔduruu, na ɔduruu ɔbabaa bi. Bere a nna aduanan no atwam no, ɔbɛgyee ne nnipa no kɔkɔɔ sɛ ɔrekɔ ne maame nkurase. Ɛneɛ na ɔbɔɔ bebrebee a ɛto no, na ɔfaa nkosua ɔha, na ɔfiri hɔ kɔduruu baabi a Ɔnanka no wɔ. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Me baa da a meba me nkyɛn me, ha me ne ba. " Na ɔdaa nkosua ne ɛto no adi wɔ nkaaho mu. Ɔnanka no kae sɛ, "Me deɛ, me remmɛkum ne ba yi, na mmom mebɛbɔ no din sɛ 'Ɔnanka ketewa', na m'ani nni hɔ da. " Ɛneɛ na ɔdaa Ɔnanka no ase; na ɔtutu kwan kɔɔ fie. Aniniedie (ntoro) no na ɛbaa abusuakuo no mu.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom na mo betumi afa no nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a mo bɛbɔ me (wɔ asɛmka ho) no.
