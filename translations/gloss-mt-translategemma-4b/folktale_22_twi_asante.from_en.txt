Ewiem-onyankopɔn no begat mmofra baasa, a wɔne Esum, Sum; Ɔsrane, Ɔsrane; ne Owia, Awia. Bere a ne mmofra baasa no nyinii no, Ewiem-onyankopɔn no hyɛɛ wɔn sɛ wɔnkɔ nkuraaseɛ. Eyi kɔɔ ne akuraa; eyi nso kɔɔ ne akuraa, na eyi nso kɔɔ ne akuraa. Na ɛhɔ na wɔwɔ, na wɔn agya no dɔ Awia. Na berɛ a Ewiem-onyankopɔn no (reigning) wɔ hɔ no, ɔbɔɔ Akonnwa bi, na ɔka kyerɛɛ n’asomfoɔ no sɛ, "Hwan na onim sɛ me adwene (lit. me nsɛmfua a ɛwɔ me etire) yɛ dɛn? " Na Kwaku Ananse, Ananse no, kae sɛ, "Me deɛ, me nim. " Ɛwɔ berɛ a ɔkae sɛ, "Me deɛ, me nim," ɛno na Ewiem-onyankopɔn no maa asomfoɔ no nyinaa sɔree. Ɛhɔ na ɛbaa sɛ Ananse nso sɔreɛ, sɛ ɔrekɔ Sky-god no mma nkuraaseɛ. Kwaku Ananse bɛduruu kwan no so no, ɔkaa ne ho sɛ, "Me nni ne ho adwene, na mekae sɛ me nim wɔn. " Na ɔtwee ntakra bi firii anomaa biara mu, na ɔde bɔɔ ne ho, na ɔtutu kɔɔ soro, kɔduruu gyedua-dua bi so wɔ Sky-god no akuraa aseɛ. Na berɛ a nnipa no huu anomaa no, wɔn nyinaa yɛɛ gyegyeegye kɛseɛ (which sounded like) "Ye e e e! " Na Sky-god no firi fie bɛba gyedua-tree no ase na ɔkaa sɛ, "Na Kwaku Ananse wɔ ha a, anka ɔbɛhu anomaa yi din, efisɛ na meadi adwene sɛ Owia, Awia, na mepɛ sɛ meyɛ no ɔhene, na mebisaa hwan na onim nea ɛwɔ me etire, na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ ɔnim, na mekɔɔ an" d yii Bayere a wɔfrɛ no ‘Kintinkyi’ no, na ɔno a ɔbɛtumi ahu ne din na waka no, me deɛ mebɛma no akonnwa. Ɛno nti na ɔde me mmofra no akɔ, na sɛ ɔwɔ ha a, anka ɔbɛhu anomaa yi din. " Na seesei anomaa no tuu kɔeɛ, na ɔtwee ntakra no guu hɔ, na ɔde ne ho kɔduruu Anadwo akuraa hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ, "Wo agya no aka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wode me ka. " Na Anadwo kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ yie, me ne wo bɛkɔ. " Na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Me kɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu kɔ fe tch Ɔsrane ne Awia. " Na Anadwo ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ma me hwehwɛ biribi mma me. " Na Ananse no nso buae sɛ, "Ho. " Anadwo kɔɔ aborɔ a wɔato bi kɔmaa Kwaku Ananse. Bere a ɔdii no wiei no, ɔde ne ho kɔɔ Ɔsrane nkurase. Bere a ɔduruu Ɔsrane akuraa no, ɔkae sɛ, "Wo agya no ka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ woba me ho. " Na Ɔsrane kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ yie, mebɛkɔ. " Na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Me bɛkɔ Ɔsrane akuraa no wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ meba no. " Na Ɔsrane kaa sɛ, "Ma me nnya wo biribi di. " Na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ho. " Na Ɔsrane yɛɛ yam a wɔayam maa no dii. Na Ananse no de ne ho kɔɔ Ɔsrane akuraa no. Bere a ɔduruu Awia nkurase no, ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Wo agya se ɛsɛ sɛ woba me ho. " Na Awia kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ yiye, me ne wo bɛkɔ, nanso momma me nnya wo biribi di. " Na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ho. " Na Awia kɔkyeree odwan bi. Bere a ɔsan baeɛ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, "Anka me kɔn dɔ sɛ me agya ba ha a, anka ɔbɛhu nea me yɛ yi, sɛ ɛyɛ papa a, anaa ɛyɛ bɔne a, asɛm biara ɔbɛhu; nanso ɛfiri sɛ ɔmmra nti, na woaba yi, ɛte sɛ nea agya aba, ɛno nti me odwan yi, mekum sɛ mo mma mo. " Na ɔkum odwan no na ɔyɛɛ no fɛfɛɛfɛ maa Ananse bɛdidi. Na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Yɛn kɔ (fallen) tree so. " Na berɛ a wɔakɔ (there) no, na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ Awia, "Wo agya no abɔ akonnwa bi a ɛwɔ hɔ no ho kɔkɔɔ, na ɔpɛ sɛ wotumi di saa akonnwa no so, na wafa bayere bi nso akɔ hɔ na ɛwɔ hɔ, na sɛ wunim sɛnea woka ne din a, ɔbɛfa akonnwa no ama wo, na wɔfrɛ no 'Kintinkyi'. Na wɔ ahyɛdeɛ bi a worenkae ne din, mebɛtwa twene tiatia bi ama wo, na mebɛyɛ mpintin twene bi a ɛne no bɛkɔ, na berɛ a wɔbɛbɔ (short) drum no ne mpintin twene no a, ɛnneɛ worenkae da, efisɛ twene tiatia no bɛkasa aka sɛ:

‘Firi bomo!

Firi bomo!'

Ɛneɛ, mpintin twene no bɛka sɛ:

‘Kintinkyi bomo!

Kintinkyi bomo!

Na wɔsii kwan kɔɔ Ewiem Onyankopɔn kuro no mu. Na seesei wɔduruu Ewiem kuro no mu na wɔde no kɔeɛ, na wɔduruu Anadwo kuro no mu na wɔde no kɔeɛ. Wɔn kwan a wɔfa so dii mpintin twene no nyinaa. Na berɛ a wɔduruu kuro akyi a Kwaku Ananse huu ɔbarima bi, ɔde no kɔeɛ sɛ ɔnka nkyerɛ Ewiem onyankopɔn sɛ wɔreba. Na Ewiem onyankopɔn no hyiaa asɛmbli bi, na Kwaku Ananse ne wɔn baeɛ bɛtoo nsa (obiara). Na Kwaku Ananse de amaneɛbɔ no maa Kasafoɔ no sɛ, "Kasafoɔ no nkrataa a wɔde kɔmaa me no, meadi ho dwuma. " Na Sky-god no kaa sɛ, "Me mmofra, sianteɛ a wɔde kɔmaa mo no, meadi ho dwuma sɛ: mebɔɔ akonnwa a ɛda hɔ no ho kɔkɔɔ, na mebɛtwee bayere a ɛda hɔ no nso; mebɛfa saa akonnwa yi de ama nea ɔhu ne bayere no. Esiane sɛ me abakan ne Anadwo nti, ma ɔmmɔ no din. " Na Anadwo kaa sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ no ‘Pona’. " Na nnipa no nyinaa kaa sɛ, Ye e e e. " Na ɔsoro-nyamfo no kaa sɛ, "Me ne ba a ɔto so abien ne Ɔsrane, ɛnneɛ ma ɔmmɔ ne din. " Na Ɔsrane kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ bayere a wɔfrɛ no Asante. " Wɔkaa sɛ, Ye e e e. " Ɔsoro-nyamfo no kaa sɛ, "Me abofra a ɔto so abiɛsa ne Awia, ɛnneɛ ma ɔmmɔ no din. " Mprempren, me werɛ fi sɛ mekae sɛ saa danc e music no kɔɔ so:

"Kintinkyi bomo!

Kintinkyi bomo!

na Kwaku Ananse reyi cart-wheels, na Awia sɔre sɔre gyinaa hɔ na ɔgyee bayere no nsɔmu, na ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, asɛm yi deɛ, ɛfiri bere a mehyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ me ne me agya nkyɛn na me ho yɛ me nketenkete paa no, na ɔka ne din kyerɛɛ me, na me werɛ mfi; wɔfrɛ no 'Kintinkyi'. " Abusuakuo no teɛm nsambɔ mprɛnsa sɛ, "E! E! E! " Na n’agya sɔre sɔre gyinaa hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ, "Wo, anadwo no, woyɛ nea odi kan, nanso nsɛmfua a meka kyerɛɛ wo no, woama wo ho werɛ fi, ɛfiri sɛ woamfa adwene anhyɛ me nsɛmfua no mu; ɛno nti, (ɛyɛ nea ɛyɛ nteaseɛ) sɛ nnebɔne no nko ara na ɛbɛyɛ wɔ wo berɛ yi mu. Na wo nso, Ɔsrane, nsɛmfua a me ne wo nam so na meka kyerɛɛ wo no, woanni so, (ɛyɛ nea ɛhyɛ da nti) mmofra nko ara na wɔbɛdi agoru wɔ w’ohendie no ase. Na ɛfa wo ho, Awia, bere a meka nsɛmfua kyerɛɛ wo no, wo werɛ mfi, wotiee me afutu nti, wɔayɛ wo Ɔhene nti, ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara nya asɛm biara a ɛsɛ sɛ ɛdi, ma ɛnka wɔ wo berɛ mu, ɛyɛ afie mu nsɛm nti, asɔretia biara nni wɔn a wɔteɛ no ho. Na fa kwan a mefa so no, na Ɔsrane pɛ sɛ ɔtwa so a, ma nyankontɔn kurukuruwa (rain clouds) no ntutu ne ho hyɛ wo ho so, na ama ɔno (Ɔsrane) antumi ammɛka wo; na bio bere a nsuo kurukuruwa no bɛhyia no, Ewiem no bɔ no bɛbɔ wo mmofra a wɔwɔ wo ase no ma wɔahunu bere a mebɔɔ no (sɛ nsuo ahoroɔ no) no. t overflow and carry them away. Na bio, nsɛmfua yi a kan no na na wɔfrɛ no ‘Ewiem-onyankopɔn Mmɛ’ no, ɛfiri sɛ Kwaku Ananse, Ananse no, tumi kenkan (ahunu) nsɛmfua no wɔ etire me so no, ma wɔnkyerɛ no sɛ ‘Ananse Mmɛ’ yi. Eyi, me abasɛm yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebi wotumi fa no nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a wotumi kamfo me (wɔ asɛmka ho).
