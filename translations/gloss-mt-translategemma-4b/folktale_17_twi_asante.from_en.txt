SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ, SƐNEA OGYA-NYINAA NNAFUA NO BƐYƐƐ NEA WƆNIM NO SƐ "SPIDER-STORIES"

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse, pɛnkoro kɔɔ Nyankopɔn, Sky-onyankopɔn, wɔ ahyɛdeɛ sɛ ɔntɔ Sky-onyankopɔn abakɔsɛm. Osutɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, "So wubetumi atɔ wɔn anaa? " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Mmom, mebɛtumi. " Osutɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, "Nkurow akɛseɛ ne tumi te sɛ Kokofu, Bekwai, Asumegya aba, nanso wɔantumi antɔ, na wo a woyɛ ɔbarima kɛkɛ a ɔnni wura no, wubetumi anaa? " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Ɛhe na ɛyɛ nsɛm yi boɔ? " Osutɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, "Wɔrentumi nni hwee. Abɔdeɛ ɔnini ne Ɔnanka; Osebo, Daboɔ; Mmoatia, Mmoatia; (na) Mmɔborɔ, Nwonwone. " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Me de nneɛma yi bi bɛba, na (nea ɛsen saa no), me de me aberewa Nsia bɛka bebree no ho. " Osoro-ɔhene no kae sɛ, "Kɔfa mma me. " Ananse no baeɛ, na ɔka ne maame nyinaa ho asɛm sɛ, "Me kɔtɔ nni Osoro-ɔhene no nsɛm, na me kɔtɔ Sk y-onyankopɔn se ɛsɛ sɛ mefa Ɔnini, Ɔnanka; Osebo, Daboɔ; Mmoatia, Mmoatia; ne Mmɔborɔ, Nwonwone; na mekae sɛ mefa mo bɛka bebree ho na mekɔma Sky-onyankopɔn no. " Mprempren Ananse ne ne yere "Aso" susuw ho sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ na yɛanya Ɔnini, Ɔnanka? " Na "Aso" ka kyerɛ no sɛ, "Wokɔ fa baa hama a ɛfow na wotwa bi nso, na fa mma. " Na na ɛhɔ na ɛbaa no. e Spider san baa wɔn nkyɛn. Na "Aso" kae sɛ, "Mfa wɔn kɔ asuwa hɔ. " Na Kwaku Ananse de wɔn kɔeɛ, berɛ a na ɔrekɔ no, ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛkyɛn no, ɛnyɛ no saa; wodi atoro a, ɛkyɛn no. " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Ɛhɔ na ɛwɔ no, ɔda baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ. " Ɔnanka (a na wate nkɔmmɔbɔ a wɔsusuwii yi) kae sɛ, "Ɛhe na ɛkɔ? " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ (me yere) "Aso", a ɔne me reka asɛm sɛ saa nsam-ba yi yɛ me deɛ. onger than you, and I say she is a ɔtorofoɔ. " Na Ɔnini, Ɔnanka, kae sɛ, "Brɛ no, na brɛ me. " Ɔfaa Ɔnanka no nsa no bɛtoo Ɔnanka no ho. Ɔkae sɛ, "Stretch yourself out. " Na Ɔnanka no de ne ho bɛtrɛɛ, na Kwaku Ananse de ɔnanka-creeper no twitwi ne ho (na akyekyereɛ no nnyigyei no yɛɛ nwenene! nwenene! nwenene! kosi sɛ ɔbɛduruu etire no so. Kwaku Ananse, Ananse no, kaa sɛ, "Okwasee, mefa wo bɛkɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn nkyɛn na megye Ewiem-onyankopɔn nsɛm (in nsasaeɛ). " Kwaku Ananse de no kɔɔ Niamey (wɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn nkyɛn). Ewiem-onyankopɔn no kaa sɛ, "Me nsa aka (i. e. Me nsa kaa no), ɛda so ara nea ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ. " Ananse no san baeɛ, na ɛbaa bɛka ne yere no nea ɛkɔɔ so no, na ɔkae sɛ, " Nwonwone no da so ara wɔ hɔ. " Ne yere no kae sɛ, "Hwehwɛ nsuo dɔm bi, na fa nsuo no hyɛ mu ma, na fa kɔ. " Ananse no kɔɔ nwura no mu, berɛ a ɔhunuu Nwonwone a ɛda hɔ no, na ɔhwie nsuo no bi guu hɔ guu Nwonwone no so. Ananse no de deɛ ɛkae no guu ne ho so, na ɔtwaa ahaban a ɛyɛ borɔdeɛ no na ɔde akata ne etire so. Na ɛnneɛ, ɔkasa kyerɛɛ Nwonwone no sɛ, "Sɛnea osuo no aba no, anka ɛsɛ sɛ mo ba ha yie, me gourd, na ama osuo no anbɔ mo; mo hu sɛ me de borɔdeɛ ahahan akata me ho? " Ɛnneɛ Nwonwone no kae sɛ, "Yɛda mo ase Aku, yɛda mo ase Aku. " Nwonwone no nyinaa tuu kwan kɔɔ gourd no mu, fom! Agya Ananse de ne ho bɛkaa ano, na ɔkae sɛ, "Mɔfoɔ, meanya mo, na mefa mo rekɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn nkyɛn (in nsasaeɛ). " Na ɔde Nwonwone no kɔɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn nkyɛn. Sky-god no kaa sɛ, "Me nsa aka no, nea ɛka no na ɛda so. " Ananse no san baeɛ bio, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere, na ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛda so Osebo, Daboɔ no. " "Aso" kaa sɛ, "Kɔkɔtu ɛtokuro. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛno dɔɔso, me te aseɛ. " Ɛneɛ Ananse no kɔɔ kɔhwehwɛɛ Daboɔ no akwan, na (wɔhunuu no) ɔtuu amoa a emu dɔ, na ɔkata so, na ɔsan baa fie. Ɛda a ɛdi kan pa ara, berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ aseɛ bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, Ananse no kae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ ne ho, na berɛ a ɔkɔeɛ no, (lO) Daboɔ bi a ɛwɔ amena so. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ: "Little father's child, little mother's child, I have told you not to get drunk, and now, just as one would expect of you, you have become intoxicated, and that's why you have fallen into the amena; if I were to say I would get you out, next day, if you saw me, or likewise any of my mmofra, you would go and catch them. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "O! Me nsa nka adeɛ a ɛte saa. " Kwaku Ananse kɔtwaa nnua mmienu, de baako si ha, na baako nso si hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Monya wo mmoa nanpɔw baako nsi ha, na wo mmoa nanpɔw baako nso nsi ha. " Na Daboɔ no de sii (baabi a wɔka kyerɛɛ no). Berɛ a ɔrebɛforo no, Kwaku Ananse maa ne sekkan so, na ɛduruu ne etire so hann fɛrɛfɛrɛ bi. (Ɛno ne nnyigyei a ɛyɛeɛ). Daboɔ no gyee amena no fOM! (na ɛyɛ ne nnyigyei a ɛbaa so). Kwaku Ananse nyaa atwerɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ amena mu akɔfa Daboɔ no. Ɔnyaa Daboɔ no na ɔde baeɛ, ɔkaa sɛ, "Okwasefoɔ, mefa me kɔ Sky-god no nsɛm mu. " Ɔde Daboɔ no so kɔmaa Niamey, Sky-god no. Sky-god no kaa sɛ, "Me nsa aka no, nea ɛka no da so ara ka. " Ananse no baeɛ, na ɔbɔɔ Akua ba (wooden doll a ne ho yɛ tuntum), na ɔde amamfoɔ a ɛkamfam (from a dua) no bɔɔ doll no ho, na ɔde ɛto (bayerɛ ɛtɔ) no bɔɔ doll no ho, na ɔde ɛto no bi guu doll no nsa, na ɔbɔɔ ɛto no bio na ɔde guu ayowa mu; ɔde ɛto kyekyeree doll no ho, na ɔne no kɔeɛ kɔtoo nan a ɛwɔ odum-tree bi so, baabi a Mmoatia no ba bɛdi agorɔ. Na Mmoatia bi baa ne nkyɛn. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Akua, ma me kɔdi saa pɔtɔ yi kakra? ” Kwaku Ananse yii ne hama a etwa to no, na akuaaba no de ne etire kyerɛɛ hɔ. Mmoatia no kaa ne nuammaa bi sɛ, “Ɔka sɛ me kɔtumi di kakra. ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Di kakra, na ɛnneɛ. ” Na ɔdii kakra no, na ɔdaa ne ho aseɛ (ɔne akuaaba no). Nanso bere a ɔdaa no ase no, wammua no. Na Mmoatia no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea sɛ, “Sɛ mede me ase a, me mmua. ” Mmoatia no nuabea no kae sɛ, “Mma no su ne ne su. ” Na ɔbɔɔ no, pa! Na ne nsa bɛyɛɛ hɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea sɛ, “Me nsa bɛyɛɛ hɔ. ” Ɔkae sɛ, “Fa nea aka no na san bɔ no su. ” Na ɔfaa no bɔɔ no, pa! na eyi nso, ɛbɛyɛɛ den. Mmoatia no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea sɛ, “Me nsa mmienu abɛyɛ den. ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Fa wo yafunu no bɔ no. ” Ɔbɔɔ no, na ne yafunu no bɛyɛɛ nea ɛbɛyɛɛ den. Na Kwaku Ananse bɛkyeree no, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Okwasea, meanya wo, mebɛfa wo akɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn (in nsasaeɛ) ama ne nsɛm. ” Na ɔne no kɔɔ fie. Na Kwaku Ananse ka ne maame, Ya nsia, sɛ, “Monnyina so, yɛnkɔ, mefa mo bɛka Mmoatia ho kɔma me de mo bɛma Ewiem-onyankopɔn no (in nsasaeɛ) wɔ ne nsɛm no ho. ” Ɔmaa wɔn so, na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔɔ baabi a Ewiem-onyankopɔn no wɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ewiem onyankopɔn, ha ne Mmoatia, na me yere a meka ho asɛm no, ha ɔno nso. ” Seesei Ewiem-onyankopɔn no frɛɛ (ne mpanimfoɔ), Kontire ne Akwam mpanimfoɔ, Adonten (ɔkannifoɔ a ɔwɔ asraadom kuw titiriw no), Gyaase (major-domo); Oyoko, Ankɔbea ne Kyidɔm (ɔkannifoɔ a wɔwɔ rear-guard). Na ɔde asɛm no too wɔn anim sɛ, ahemfo akɛseɛ bi aba, na wɔantumi antɔ Sky-god no stories no, nanso Kwaku Ananse, Ananse no, watumi atua boɔ no; Mmɔborɔ bi, Nwonwone no, mefiri ne hɔ; Mmoatia bi, Mmoatia no, mefiri ne hɔ; Osebo bi, Daboɔ no, mefiri ne hɔ; Ɔnini bi, Ɔnanka no, mefiri ne hɔ; na ne ho apam no, Kwaku Ananse atumi de bɛka ho. ɔne ne maame bebree, saa nneɛma yi nyinaa da ha. ” Ɔkae sɛ, “Eee ayeyi no. ” “Eee! ” (wɔteɛm). Sky-god no kae sɛ, “Kwaku Ananse, ɛfiri da a ɛkɔ so kɔ daa, me Sky-god ayeyi no meka me Sky-god nsɛm no mema mo, kosɛ! kosɛ! kosɛ! me nsɛm, kosɛ, me nsɛm. Ɛnkyɛn na yɛbɛfrɛ wɔn Sky-god nsɛm no bio, na mmom yɛbɛfrɛ wɔn Spider-nsɛm. ”

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
