SƐ OBI YƐ WO PAPA A, KYERƐ W’ASEDA SƐ WOYƐ NO PAPA WƆ SAN BA

WƆKA SƐ (bere bi) na Ɔkɔdeɛ bi wɔ hɔ, na ne ho akyinkyinakyin no, ɔkɔhyiaa aberewa bi a ɔwɔ kuru wɔ ne nan so. Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Gracious me! Eyi yɛ kuru bi a ɛwɔ klaase bi mu; ɛneɛ bi a ɛte sɛɛ no, ɛmfa ho nea wobɔ mmɔden kɛseɛ no, wotumi nantew? " Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Oh, ɛyɛ kakra paa. " Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Mo nkurɔfoɔ, ɛnnɛ yi, sɛ mebɛyɛ biribi pa ama mo (to-da) a, ɔkyena mo bɛfa biribi bɔne abɛkyerɛ me. " Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Oh, meantumi nyɛ (ɛno). " Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ moantumi nni nneyɛeɛ a ɛte saa a, meate. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Mmu aniwa no na monue. " Na aberewa no bɛtoo aniwa no mu na wɔabue. Ɔkɔdeɛ no kae sɛ, "Mhwɛ mo kuru no. " Na aberewa no baeɛ sɛ wɔbɛhwɛ a, ɛnyɛ nkaedum. Ɛneɛ na ɔbɛyɛɛ no sɛ ɔbɛto aniwa no mu bio; ɔbuee no, na ɔhunuu sɛ asase no nyinaa ayi. Ɔkae sɛ, "Mmu no bio. " Ɔtoo mu, na ɔbuee no, na afie no gyinaa hɔ yie. Na ɔsan bɔɔ wɔn ntam. Ɔbuee wɔn ani hunuu kuro a ɛyɛ kɛseɛ saa. Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Aberewa, ɛyɛ wo deɛ (lit. wɔ wo nsa). "Aseda me ma wo. " Ɛneɛ aberewa no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mefa so ma wo? " Ɔkɔdeɛ no kae sɛ, "Me kɔn dɔ ade ketewa a ɛho nhia, me deɛ, me kɔn dɔ Silk-cotton-tree yi pɛ. " Aberewa no kae sɛ, "Ade yi (wose no) dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ wofa? " Ɔkɔdeɛ no tuu kɔɔ n’anim, na ɛbɛduruu so, na ɛbɛyɛɛ berebuo, na ɛbɛtoo nkosua mmienu, de guu hɔ. Wɔn ho hyɛɛ mu. Na ɔtoo nkosua mmienu no, na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔpɛɛ adeɛ bi maa ne mmofra. Aberewa no nansafoɔ, a na ɔne no teɛ no, hyɛɛ aseɛ kwiikwii sɛ, "Ehe! Ehe! " aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn ne asɛm no? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma me nni nsuo bi a ɛyɛ Eagle mmofra. " aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛhe na mebɛnya mmofra a ɛyɛ Eagle? " nana ketewa no hyɛɛ aseɛ bio, kwiikwii! kwiikwii! Aberewa no kae sɛ, "Dɛn ne asɛm no? " Abofra no kae sɛ, "Ma me nni nsuo wɔ Eagle mma no ho, efisɛ sɛ me nnya bi nni a, mebɛwu. " Aberewa no kae sɛ, "Ah! Ɛsɛ sɛ me nana yi wu ma Eagle mma no nni? Munya nkuma no, fa nkuma no kɔ hwe silk-cotton-tree no na fa Eagle mma no no mma me. " Wɔn akuraasefoɔ no kɔɔ hɔ, (nkuma no bɔɔ), Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! Ɛkame ayɛ sɛ dua no rekɔ fam, bere a Eagle's children no ɔpanin no tuu mpɔn, na ɔbaa bɛgyinaa berebuo no ano, na ɔsɔre su; ɔtoo maame no, na ɔkaa sɛ:

Sango, anomaa e!

Sango, anomaa, Kɔntɔre no ba!

Sango, anomaa e!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔɔ adidie a, san ba!

Sango, anomaa e!

Sango, o! o!

Ɔbaa no tee sɛ ne abofra no resu; ɔsɔre, (na ne ntaban no nnyigyei no,) fa! Ɔbaa no; ɔkaa sɛ, "Sanguri," na saa dua (a na ɛkame ayɛ sɛ wɔatwitwa no) no san bɔɔ mu bio, na nnipa a na wɔrebɔ no nyinaa, wɔduruu mu. Ɛneɛ na ɔde aduane a na ɔde aba no, ɔmaa ne mmofra. Ɛneɛ na ɔgyaa wɔn kwan sɛ, "Me rekɔ, sɛ aberewa no ba bɛfa wo a, ma ɔfa wo. " Na aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Kɔkɔkɔ dua so na fa abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no bɛma me nana no adidi. " Na wɔkɔɔ hɔ nea ɛtɔ so mmienu, Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! Ɛbɛyɛɛ sɛ dua no kɔɔ fam, bere a (Eagle's) ba no bɔɔ ne ho puei baeɛ na ɛbɛgyinaa berebuo no ano, na ɛtoo n'ano frɛɛ ne maame, sɛ:

Sango, anomaa e!

Sango, anomaa, Kɔntɔre no ba!

Sango, anomaa e!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔɔ adidie a, san ba!

Sango, anomaa e!

Sango, o! o!

Ɔtoo ne maame din, na ɔtoo, na ɔtoo, na ɔtoo—mmuaeɛ biara nni hɔ, na seesei dua no bɛkaa sɛ ɛbɛduruu fam no, "Ma sɛe! " (ɛkaa). Wɔde Eagle no mma no kɔeɛ, wɔde baako maa aberewa, nanso baako a ɛkaeɛ no, ɛtutu kɔɔ Wawa-dua bi so. Baako no, wɔnnyee no, na wɔde maa aberewa no abarimaa, a ɔde kaa bɔdeɛ a wɔato no ho. Ɛnkyɛeɛ bi na Ɔkɔdeɛ no baeɛ. Berɛ a ɔduruu dua a wɔatutu no, ɔhunuu ne mmofra baako a ɔte hɔ; ɔbisaa no nea ɛsii, na ɔka amaneɛbɔ no nyinaa kyerɛɛ no. Ɔkɔdeɛ no kɔɔ aberewa no nkurase. Berɛ a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, na aberewa no abarimaa bi redidi ne mmofra baako. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Aberewa, mekae wo. " Ɔfiri aberewa no fie hɔ na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ (ne nkonyaayi) wɔ kuro akyi. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sanguri," na onipa biara yeraa, na bio ɔkaa sɛ, "Sanguri," na fie biara bɔɔ baako pɛ na tenabea biara nni hɔ. "Sanguri," akuraa no bɛyɛɛ kwaeɛ no bio. "Sanguri," na aberewa no yareɛ no san baeɛ. Na ɔkae sɛ, "Aberewa, woahu sɛ, ɛno nti na mpanimfoɔ no reka sɛ, sɛ obi yɛ wo papa a, da no aseɛ sɛ ɔyɛ wo papa na wunni bɔne nni no aseɛ. " Eyi, me abasɛm yi, a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom wotumi fa no nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a wode bɛyi me (wɔ asɛmka no ho) no.
