Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

KWAKU ANANSE, ANANSE NO, NA ƆKA SƐ NA ƆREKƆ HWEHWƐ KWASEA, BERE A NA NE HO NO ARA NA ƆYƐ KWASEA.

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse no, na ɔte hɔ pɛn, na ɔkae sɛ ɔpɛ kwasea bi a ɔne no bɛkɔ akɔyɛ mpata-trap. Ɔkyinkyin faa afanan nyinaa, nanso wantumi annya bi. Ɛneɛ na ɔne Osansa, Akrɔma no, hyiaa. Ɔkae sɛ, “Yɛnkɔyɛ nsuo-trap. ” Akrɔma no buae sɛ, “Me nni (plenty) of meat a mebɛdi. ” Akyiri yi ɔne Anene a ɔyɛ Kwaakwaadabi no hyiaa, na ɔkae sɛ, “Yɛnkɔyɛ nsuo-trap. ” Kwaakwaadabi no kae sɛ, “Me ate. ” Mprempren, Akrɔma no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaakwaadabi no sɛ, “Wɔn a wɔne Kwaku Ananse rekɔ nsuo-trap yi a wɔrekyerɛ no, mma wɔn kɔ, efisɛ Kwaku Ananse ka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ kwasea, sɛ ɔne kwasea no bɛyɛ nsuo-trap a. ” s, ɔbɛnya sika no nyinaa. ” Kwaakwaadabi no buae sɛ, “Wo nkae bio, mebɛhwɛ ama me nsa aka sika no nyinaa. ” Kwaakwaadabi ne Ananse no kɔɔ nwura no mu sɛ wɔrebɛtwa mmɛbaha. Kwaakwaadabi no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, “Ma me sekkan no na mebɛkɔ akɔtwa (wɔn), na w’adwuma ne sɛ wobɛgye brɛ (wɔn adwumayɛ no). ” Ananse no buae sɛ, “Wode me kwan sɛ kwasea; ma me yɛ atwaeɛ no, na mebɛgye brɛ no. ” Kwaku Ananse atwaeɛ (wɔn pALMS) no bere a Anene, Kwaakwaadabi no, te hɔ a ɔregyigye. Bere a ɔtwaa (wɔn) no, wɔkyekyeree wɔn (in a babaso). Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, “Ananse, ma mefa mma me, ɛberɛ a wodeɛ brɛ no bɛkɔ. ” Ananse no buae sɛ, “Wopue, wode me kɔ kwasea? ” Na ɔde wɔn kɔtoo n’etire so. Kwaakwaadabi no dii n’akyi kɔɔ so nteɛm. Bere a wɔduruu fie no, Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Ma me yɛ mpata-nsuo no na mobrɛ me nnwuma no. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Daabi; ma me yɛ no na mobrɛ me. " Ɛneɛ Kwaakwaadabi no kɔɔ ne ho so, na na ɔregyigye. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Okwaseafoɔ, wose wode adwene, na woyera. " Ananse no yɛɛ mpata-nsuo no wiee. Kwaakwaadabi no kae sɛ, "Ma mefa mpata-traps no kɔ nsuo no mu, na wo fa brɛ no kɔ. " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Dabi, me ne wɔn bɛkɔ, na wo fa brɛ no kɔ. " Wɔduruu nsuo no ho, Kwaakwaadabi no kae sɛ, "Papa Ananse, aboa kɛseɛ bi te nsuo no mu, nti ma me nnyina nsuo no mu nsi mpata-traps no, sɛ aboa kɛseɛ no bɛka me a, wode owuo no bɛkɔ. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ah, woyi me sɛ ɔkwasea ankasa; me ankasa na mebɛsi afidie no, na sɛ ɔbɛka me a, wode owuo no bɛka wo. " Kwaku Ananse de apataa-afidie no sii nsuo no mu, na ɛsan baa fie. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, wɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔhwɛɛ afidie no, na wɔakye apataa mmienu. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Ananse, wode eyinom mmienu no, na berɛ a ɔkyena yɛn baeɛ no, na (afidie) no akyere apataa nnan, dane me bɛfa wɔn. " Ananse no buaeɛ sɛ, "Saa yɔnkoɔ yi! Wode me kɔ ɔkwasea ankasa; wode eyinom mmienu no, ɔkyena yɛn baeɛ no, dane me bɛfa nnan no. " Kwaakwaadabi no de wɔn kɔeɛ. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no wɔn kɔɔ (bio kɔsra afidie no), na wɔnyaa apataa nnan. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Wo faan anan yi, na berɛ a ɔkyena yɛn bɛduru a yɛakye nnum no, me dane mebɛfa wɔn. " Ananse no buaa sɛ, "Wo fa me kwasea; wo wɔ anan yi, na ɔkyena me nso, me nso bɛfa nnum no. " Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no wɔn kɔɔ (kɔsraa afidie) no na wɔnyaa nnum. Kwaakwaadabi no kae sɛ, "Mfa (wɔn) kɔ, na me nso meɔkyena me, me nso mefa dunsia no. " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Wofa me kwasea; wofa dunsia no, na meɔkyena me dunsia no. " Ɛnneɛ yi na mpatafoni no (fish-traps) no adɔre, na Kwaakwaadabi no bɛkaa Ananse no sɛ, "Mokɔ na mofa mpatafoni a ɛsɛe yi, na sɛ baako tɔn a, wobenya ntanu. " (£16) ma single (trap) biara, nti wo, Agya Ananse, fa apataa yi, na fa rotten apataa-traps no nso ma me. ” Ananse no kaa sɛ, “Dabi, wode apataa no kɔtɔn, na mefa rotten apataa-traps no nso bɛfa (the cost). ” Wɔde (wɔn nneɛma) no kɔɔ dwaso bi so. Ɛno ara na Kwaakwaadabi no de ne nneɛma nyinaa kɔeɛ, ɔnyaa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma. Nanso, na ɛda soɔ ne Ananse no nneɛma. Ɛneɛ Kwaakwaadabi no kaa no sɛ, “Fa saa nnuan-traps a ɛsɛe yi kɔtɔn no kɔnkyini kwan so na wɔbɛtɔ no. ” Ananse no maa no so, ɔkaa sɛ, “Me retɔn nnuan-traps a ɛsɛe yi! ” Mprempren akuraa no panin no tee (no), na ɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛhe na saa yɔnkoɔ yi firi? Kɔfrɛ no mma ɔmmra. ” Ananse no kɔeɛ. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, “So wosusu sɛ yɛn akuraa yi mu nipa yɛ nkwaseafoɔ; seesei wo adamfo (wɔ ha) baeɛ sɛ ɔtɔn apataa (wɔ mfasoɔ mu) a, woanhu no, nanso wobɛgyae apataa a ɛsɛe no ama yɛn? ” Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, “Mɔ no. ” Ɛnneɛ wɔbɔɔ no. Ɛneɛ Kwaakwaadabi no kae sɛ, “Spider, woahu sɛ, wo aniwa abere aniwu; wokae sɛ wode kwasea bi bɛkɔ akɔne wo, na wode (seesei) woahu sɛ wo ankasa na wo yɛ kwasea. ”

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
