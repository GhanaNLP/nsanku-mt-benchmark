Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

DƐN NTI NA ANANSE, ANANSE NO, MMIRIKA AHOROƆ BERE A ƆWƆ NSUO ANI NO

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse, kɔkaa kyerɛɛ Kraman, Kraman, sɛ, “Me kɔpɛ sɛ me ne mo kɔsi akuraa ketewa bi a yɛbɛtena mu. ” Kraman no kaa sɛ, “Me nso me kɔpɛ saa. ” Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, “Sɛ Sunday-Adae bi bɛtɔ a, ɛdwoada a ɛdi hɔ yi, kɔfa rope-creeper bi; me nso mebɛfa rope-creeper na yɛakɔ. Bio, mebɛfa towa bi na mehyɛ no nsuo, na wo nso, wode wo ho bɔ bi, ebia nea yɛrekɔ no, nsuo biara nni hɔ. Kwaku Ananse bɔɔ nsuo bi; Kraman nso, ɛwoɔ bi a wɔde bɛhyɛɛ (ne) towa no mu. Wɔrekɔ so, na wɔadu fa-fa-mfinimfini, na wɔn nsa aka. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, “Momma yɛnnom yɛn nsuo no bi. ” Kraman no kaa sɛ, “Momma me nnom bi. ” Kwaku Ananse nom bi, na ɔde ne nsa bɛkaa ne ano, ɔkaa sɛ, “Oh, ɛhe na eyi firi? ” Kraman no kaa sɛ, “Me yafunu mu na ɛwɔ. ” Ananse no kaa sɛ, “Yiw! ” Sɛdeɛ wɔtenaa hɔ sɛ wɔbɛnya wɔn ahome no, Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, “Kraman, momma yɛnka agorɔ. ” Kraman no kaa sɛ, “agorɔ bɛn na ɛwɔ hɔ? ” Ananse no buaeɛ sɛ, “Binding-binding game no; y ou bind me first, then I, too, shall bind you, but when you hear me say, ‘Wui!’ then you leave off. ” Kraman no kaa sɛ, “Dabi, mo nkyekyere me kan. ” Ananse no kaa sɛ, “Gye wo ho, bere a ɔpanin bi reka asɛm bi akyerɛ wo no, woyɛ ho asɛm anaa? ” Kraman no kaa sɛ, “Ɛnneɛ, ma ɛtena hɔ (asɛm biara nka). ” Ananse no kaa sɛ, “Bra me kyekyere me. ” Kraman no de ne rope-creeper no kyerɛɛ Ananse no. Na ɔde no kɔɔ n’etire so sɛ ɔrekɔtɔn no sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na wanya adeɛ bi adi. Kwaku Ananse teɛ no, ɔyɛɛ awerɛhosu na ɔkaa sɛ:

“Me, Ananse Akuamoa, me nnyaa aboa kɛseɛ mpo a ɛfata sɛ mebɛtɔn me;

Kraman foo a ɔte sɛɛ yi, ɔreye me.

Hwan na mebɛtumi anya obi a ɔbɛgye me?

O, kwan, gye me nkra!

Kwaku Ananse bɛdii su ne su. Kraman no de no kɔɔ asuwa bi so. Ɔdɛnkyɛm no, Akokɔsrade no tee nsuo sɛ Spider no resu. Ɔbaa hɔ no, ɔbisaa Kraman no sɛ dɛn na ɛyɛ asɛm. Kraman no de Kwaku Ananse guu fam, na ɔdwanee kɔeɛ, na Akokɔsrade no yii Kwaku Ananse adi. Na Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, “Papa, adeɛ bɛn na mebɛtumi de ama wo de akyerɛ wo sɛnea mekyɛ wo? ” Akokɔsrade no kae sɛ, “Yɛmpɛ adeɛ biara. ” Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, “Sɛ mo wɔ mmofra a, ɛnde ɔkyena me bɛba abɛhyɛ wɔn ti nwi ama wɔn yie. ” Akokɔsrade no kae sɛ, “M’ate asɛm no. ” Kwaku Ananse baa fie; ɔkae sɛ, “Aso, kɔhwehwɛ nnuaba ne gyeene, ɛfiri sɛ ɔkyena me rekɔkum na mekɔfa. g back a crocodile that we may have a nkwan ne foroe to eat. " "Aso" kɔfaa bi, na Ananse de ne sekkan ne ne ho ɛtɔ no, na ɔde kɔɔ asuwa hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, "Father Crocodile, Nsuo mu mmoa no, meba bɛgye wo. " Na ɔde fufuo no guu nsuo no mu. Akokɔsrade no bae, na na ɔrebɛfa fufuo no adi, berɛ a Ananse no gyee ne sekkan no twitwaa no, gya! nanso wannya no (properly), na ɔkɔɔ so. Kwaku Ananse san baa fie. "Aso" bisaa no sɛ, "Ɛhe na saa aboa no wɔ? " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Fa wo ho kɔ; onipa bi fi akwantu mu, annya n’ahome mpo nni, na wofiri aseɛ reha no nsɛmmisa. " "Aso" kaa sɛ, "Mehu sɛ woannya no. " Kwaku Ananse anka hwee. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, anɔpa koraa, "Aso" kaa sɛ ɔrekɔ asuwa. Bere a ɔduruu hɔ no, na Akokɔsrade no da asubonten ano, na blue-bottle-flies no rebɔ kurukuru no. "Aso" san baa fie kɔka ne kunu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Seesei wuhu sɛnea aduru bi a mewɔ no, ɛyɛ adwuma; sɛ mekum nam (baako) a, ɛnka kosi sɛ nneɛma bɛda adi, da a ɛdi hɔ no, na mekɔfa no, nanso saa biara mekae mo sɛ mohu no. " Kwaku Ananse twitwaa poma bi na ɔde kɔɔ asuwa hɔ, na ɛhɔ na ɔhunuu Akokɔsrade no. Ɔde poma no bɔɔ Akokɔsrade no sɛ, "Akokɔsrade, woawu anaa? Akokɔsrade, wuwui anaa? " na ɔdane no dɔre dɔre. Akokɔsrade no da so ara yɛɛ dwoɔ, nanso na ɔnwu. Bere a Kwaku Ananse rebɛka ne nsa aka no, Akokɔsrade no bɛyɛɛ no kuadwuma! Kwaku Ananse bɔɔ mmɔden, petere! petere! na ɔnyaa kwan kɔeɛ. Ɛno nti, Ananse no fa nsuo no so a, ɔyɛ ntɛm; ɔsuro sɛ Akokɔsrade no bɛba abɛkyere no bio.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
