Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

WƆN NE WƆN A WƆFRƐ NO NWONWASO SƐ KWAKU ANANSE.

KWAKU ANANSE, Ananse no, na ɔte hɔ da bi, bere a Nsia, ɔsoro-nyamfoɔ no maame, yareɛ. Wɔkɔkyeree aduro-panyin bi sɛ ɔmmɛhwɛ no. Ɔbɛduruu hɔ no, ɔkyerɛɛ sɛ sikasiee a ɛyɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma peredwan (i. e. £8) sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ ne yareɛ. Mprempren, Kwaku Ananse kɔka kyerɛɛ Ewiem onyankopɔn sɛ, "Saa yɔnkoɔ yi refiri wo sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no kɔ kwa, ɔrentumi nhwɛ no, nti momma momma mehwɛ no mma wo, na sɛ mehwɛ no a, me etire no bɛtumi ayɛ. " Kwaku Ananse de sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma a ɛyɛ asuanu biako (i.e. £4), na ɔka kyerɛɛ Sky-god no sɛ, "Sɛ mebɛtumi antutu yareɛ yi a, mebɛma wo gyata a ɔte ase. " Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, na Nyankopɔn maame, Nsia, afiri hɔ. Wɔkae sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse, ɛyɛ dɛn? " Kwaku Ananse firi saa baabi no, wama ne ho adwane. Seesei, ɔkasa ne nea ɛwɔ akyi (i. e. me werɛ fi sɛ mekae sɛ) Kwaku Ananse nnamfoɔ kɛseɛ ne Okusie, Okisi no. Ɔka ne ho asɛm kyerɛɛ Okisi sɛ, "Ɛno na mehuu no, ɛnneɛ ɛsɛ sɛ moaa me. " Okisi no buae sɛ, "Ɛwɔ heneɛ bɛn na mebɛtumi aboa wo? " (Kwaku Ananse) buae sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ wode kɔtu ɔkwan no kɔpem sɛ ɛbɛduru pempe a ɔsoro-nyamfoɔ no te so no ase. " Okisi no kae sɛ, "Mebɛyɛ no ama wo. " Ɛnneɛ ɔyɛɛ no. Okisi bɛkaa Kwaku Ananse sɛ, "Mewie. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ ne nkyɛn frɛɛ ɔbabarima, Ntikuma; na ɔtwaa aben maa no; na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔnkɔ ɔkwan no so nkɔtena pempe ase, na, bere a ɔhunuu sɛ Sky-god no atena ne ho ato pempe so no, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔbɔ n’aben no na ɔka sɛ:

"Sɛ wukum Kwaku Ananse a, abusuakuo no bɛba abɛsɛe!

Sɛ wogye Kwaku Ananse kyɛn a, abusuakuo bɛhinhim wɔn nne! "

Ntikuma kɔtenaa aseɛ wɔ ahɔhogyeɛ akongua no ase, na Kwaku Ananse nso kɔɔ so kɔeɛ sɛ ɔrekɔ Nyankopɔn, Ewiem onyankopɔn, ne n’ankasa apam nti. Bere a ɔkɔeɛ no, na Ewiem onyankopɔn no abɔfoɔ no akyere ne nsɔmu; wɔde no kɔɔ Ewiem onyankopɔn no anim. Ɛneɛ na kasafoɔ no kaa no sɛ, "Wo, Kwaku Ananse, wode Nyankopɔn, Ewiem onyankopɔn, kyerɛɛ sɛ sɛ wo maame, Nsia, wuwu a, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔkunkum wo; ɛnneɛ Nsia afiri hɔ nti, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛkunkum wo. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ woyi me ma meka me asɛm. " Nanso akyerɛfoɔ no gyee no nsɔmu wɔ ahyɛdeɛ sɛ ɔnkɔkunkum no. Ntikuma sɔre ne nne: "Sɛ wukum Kwaku Ananse a, abusuakuo bɛba abɛsɛe!

Sɛ wogye Kwaku Ananse kyɛn a, abusuakuo bɛhinhim wɔn nne! "

Wɔkae sɛ, "Monie, monie! " Na bio aben no nso bɔɔ:

"Sɛ wukum Kwaku Ananse a, abusuakuo no bɛba abɛsɛe!

Sɛ wogye Kwaku Ananse kyɛn a, abusuakuo bɛhinhim wɔn nne! "

Ɛneɛ Krontihene sɔre kɔɔ Ewiem-onyankopɔn nkyɛn, abebuo, sɛ, "Saa abusuakuo yi yɛ wo dea, Ewiem-onyankopɔn, ne Ya, Old-mother-earth. Wɔ da a woanya Kwaku Ananse yi, na worebɛkum no. Ya, Old-mother-earth, se, 'Ma no kɔ,' nti sɛ wogyae no kɔ a, ɛbɛyɛ yie. " Ɛneɛ Ewiem-onyankopɔn no yɛɛ akyerɛfoɔ no sɛ wɔnyi no. Ɛno nti na abebuo bi wɔ hɔ sɛ, "Woyɛ nwanwa sɛ Kwaku Ananse. "

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
