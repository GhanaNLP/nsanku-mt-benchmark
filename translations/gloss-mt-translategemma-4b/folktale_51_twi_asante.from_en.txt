Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SAA WƆ ATURUKUKU NO HO NO, TURTLE-DOVE NO KƆƆ NWURA NO HO, NA MAA AKƆKƆ, HƐN NO, BAE FIE HƆ

AKƆKƆ, Hɛn, ne Aturukuku, Turtle-dove no, firi agya baako mu na ɛfiri maame baako mu. Seesei Hɛn no ka kyerɛɛ Turtle-dove no sɛ, “Kɔ fa Tall-grass-country no, na me nso mekɔ Thick-forest-country no, na sɛ wunya aduane a, na woba bɛka me. ” Ɛneɛ wɔkyerɛɛ wɔn ho. Aturukuku no mfeɛ mmiɛnsa na wanhunu ne nuabea. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne kunu sɛ, “Kɔka me ho, mekɔhwehwɛ me nuabea. ” Ɛneɛ ne kunu no kae sɛ, “Woka asɛm no, meate, wode kɔ, na meka kɔka wo ho. ” Na ɔne no kɔɔ kwan so kɔduruu kuro akyi a ɛwɔ kuro bi mu (baabi a ne nuabea te). Wɔhunuu pɔkɔsɔ bepɔw ketewa bi a ɛwɔ hɔ, na ɛhɔ na ɔne ne nuabea no hyiaa. Na Hɛn no bisaa Turtle-dove no sɛ, “Wɔn a wode wɔn kɔɔ Tall-grass-country no, wunyaa aduane bi dii anaa? ” Ɔno na ɔkaa sɛ, “Wɔ baabi a mekɔeɛ no, na guinea-grain nko ara na ɛwɔ hɔ; na mefiri me mmofra no bɛyɛ nsia, na wɔbɛbɔɔ wɔn ho bɛkyekyeree me ho, na wɔbɛyɛɛ wɔn kɛseɛ sɛɛ (kyerɛɛ wɔn kɛseɛ no), na mebɛkuraa wɔn bɛn me, na ɔbɔfoɔ bi de ne tuo bɔɔ no, pom! na ɔkunkum me mmofra, na, anka me nnim adwene no, anka ɔbɛkunkum me nso. ” Bere a ɔkaa asɛm no, Hɛn no kaa sɛ, “Me kɔɔ baabi a me kɔeɛ no, me bɛyɛɛ onipa a ɔwɔ hia a ɛho hia ankasa. ” Aturukuku bisaa no sɛ, “Kyerɛ me nea wose woyɛ ‘onipa a ɔwɔ hia a ɛho hia ankasa’ no. ” Ɛneɛ Hɛn no kaa sɛ, “Saa ‘onipa a ɔwɔ hia a ɛho hia ankasa’ no aseɛ ne sɛ, nhwɛsoɔ no, sɛ me wura no bɛdii kɛtɛ pa a, t Me kɔda so, na me mmoa bini bɛhwe ase, na me wura de nam ahyɛ soup-stew no mu a, ɛnneɛ meyi nea mepɛ; aduan biara a me wura bɛdi no, me kɔfa bi, na me di saa a, me wura rentumi nni me tuo anaa poma nni me. Kyerɛ sɛ meka me ho asɛm sɛ me yɛ onipa a ɔwɔ hia a ɛho hia ankasa. ” Hɛn no kaa sɛ, “Wɔ baabi a wote hɔ no, ɛnyɛ baabi pa, nti Memeneda bi, ansa na Akwasidae adu no, fa wo mmofra ne wo kunu no ba, na wɔn nyinaa bɛtena yɛn fie. ” Aturukuku kaa sɛ, “M’ate asɛm sɛ, mebɛkɔ, na Memeneda a ɛdi Akwasidae anim no, mebɛduru hɔ. ” Seesei ɔsɔfoɔ bi baa Hɛn no nkurase, na ɔkeka ne ho. wɔ ne dɔn nkumaa. Wɔbisaa no sɛ, “Dente, dɛn na ɛyɛ? ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Dente ka sɛ w’ama no kɔm mfeɛ mmienu, ɛsɛ sɛ wode nkokɔ (and) nkosua dumien kɔ ɔhene nkyɛn kɔka no sɛ ɔmfa nkokɔ no mma no. Ɔhene no kɔkyeree nkokɔ nnan, de nkosua dumien baeɛ, faa ɛtɔ a wɔawɔ, na ɔde kɔmaa dente bosom no wɔ ne dɔte afɔrebukyia a ano yɛ sɔnn no mu, twaa nkokɔ nnan no atitirefoɔ no na ɔde kɔmaa no. Lacing wɔn hɔ, na ɔde wɔn ntakra ne wɔn atitirefoɔ, ne wɔn anan, ne plastering guu dɔte afɔrebukyea hɔ; na ɔsan nso de ɛtɔ no guu so. Mprempren, da a ɛdi Adae no, na Aturukuku rebɛhyia ne nuabea. Ɛkɔɔ so no ɔhunuu, wɔ afɔrebukyia a ɛyɛ Dente nuabea no papa ne etire no, ne anan, ne ntaban, ne berɛboɔ, ne ne nsono. Aturukuku kaa sɛ, "Eyi nyinaa akyi no, me ntumi nkɔ hɔ bio. " Na Turtle-dove tuu kɔɔ silk-cotton dua bi so, na ɔkaa (too dwom): " Me werɛ hwee me nuabea o, Awerɛhow o

Wo, Hɛn, a na woyɛ onipa bi a ɔwɔ mfasoɔ bi, ɛno ne wo etire?

Wo, Hɛn, a na woyɛ onipa bi a ɔwɔ mfasoɔ bi, ɛneɛ ɛyɛ wo ntaban?

Wo, Hɛn, a na woyɛ onipa bi a ɔwɔ mfasoɔ bi, ɛneɛ ɛyɛ wo anan yi?

Wo, Hɛn, a na woyɛ onipa bi a ɔwɔ mfasoɔ bi, ɛno ne wo berɛboɔ?

Wo, Hɛn, a na woyɛ onipa bi a ɔwɔ mfasoɔ bi, ɛneɛ ɛyɛ wo nsono? "

Na Aturukuku kaa sɛ, " Me kɔ Kwaeɛ man no bio, ɛfiri sɛ kwaemfoɔ no nni yie. " Saa na Aturukuku baa nea ɛbɛyɛ sɛ ɔbɛtena Tall-grass-country no, berɛ a Hɛn no da so wɔ fie.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom na mo betumi afa no nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a mo bɛbɔ me (wɔ asɛmka ho) no.
