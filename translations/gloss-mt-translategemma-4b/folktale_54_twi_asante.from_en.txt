SƐNEA ADAMFOFA NKITAHODI NO BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU.

ƆBƆMMƆFOƆ ne ne yere pɛnsoɔ kɔɔ ɔbɔfoɔ bi ntam. Na Osebo, Daboɔ no, akyere ɔbɔfoɔ no na ɔdidi no, nanso ne yere a na ɔwura no, wɔgyaa no hɔ. (Ɛda bi) ɔbaa no kɔɔ aborɔbɛ hɔ, na (hɔ) na ɔhyiaa Daboɔ ba no, na ɔbisaa no sɛ, “ɔbaa, dɛn na wuhwehwɛ? ” Ɔbaa no buae sɛ, “Me ne me kunu na yɛba (hɔ), nanso Daboɔ bi akum no. ” Daboɔ ba no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Me kɔn dɔ wo, nti mma mmra ha bio, efisɛ sɛ woba ha a, na agya bɛhyia wo a, ɔbɛkum wo. ” Ɔbaa no antua aTT biara. ention, na ɔsan kɔɔ pine-apples no ntam, na Daboɔ no kyeree no, na ɔde no kɔɔ fie. Daboɔ no ba no huu ɔbaa no a, ɔka kyerɛɛ n’agya sɛ, “Me yare. ” Ɛyɛ ɔbaa no nti, a na wahu no dedaw. Daboɔ no kaa n’abofra no sɛ, “Wo ho bɛyɛ wo, nanso fa so, twitwa nam no. ” Ɔtwaa no, na ɔhunuu sɛ abofra bi da ne yafunu mu, na ɔgyee no, na ɔde guu baabi. Afei agya no bisaa no sɛ, “Ɛyɛ dɛn na woakɔto afiri hɔ? ” Abofra no buaa no sɛ, “Nea mekɔtoɔ no, sɛ wuhunuu a, anka worenni saa nam yi. ” Daboɔ no noaa aduane no. Ɔka kyerɛɛ n’abofra sɛ ɔmmra mmidi, nanso ɔno nso buae sɛ, “Nam a mehwɛɛ no hɔ ara me yareɛ no, sɛ mebɛdi bi a, anka mebɛwu. ” Ɔagya no buae sɛ, “Wɔn nni nkyɛn koraa. ” Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi, agya no tu kwan kɔɔ nwura no, ɛneɛ n’abofra no kɔgyee abofra no, de baa fie, na ɔhohoro no. Ɛneɛ na ɔkɔgyee banana, noaa no, yii aba nsuo no, na ɔmaa ne ba no nnom. Ɔkyerɛw berɛ a ne agya san baeɛ no ho nkyerɛwee, na ɔde abofra no kɔhuntaa hɔ. Na ne ba no adi bɛyɛ afe berɛ a ɔduruu mmabunbere mu, abofra a ɔwɔ Spider-story no mu no nni mfeɛ pii na wanyini. Onipa no abofra bisaa Leopard no abofra sɛ, “Dɛn ne wo din? ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Me din ne Kofi-the-beast-child. ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Me nso me din ne Kofi-the-beast-being-child. ” Ɛneɛ Kofi-the-beast-child no kaa Kofi-the-beast-being-child no sɛ, “Me pɛ sɛ me ne mo di agorɔ, nanso me suro agya, na mo nso mo suro no, enti nna mmiɛnsa a ɛreba yi, mebɛka akyerɛ no sɛ ɔnkyerɛ me. ” me sɛnea mebɛbɔ no, na bere a waka me, na mehu sɛnea ɛyɛ a, mebɛbɔ no na meakyere no, na wote sɛ meanya no a, ɛnneɛ! wobɛfa sekkan, na woabɛtwa ne etire no. ” Ɔpanyin no baeɛ, na Kofi-the-beast’s abofra no kaa no sɛ, “Kyerɛ me sɛnea mebɛspring. ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Mma ɔkyena anɔpa. ” Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔne agya no kɔɔ baabi, bɛdwee baabi, na agya no huruiɛ no, na n’abofra no nso, ɔbɛhuruu, na agya no daa ne akyi. Kofi-the-beast-child no agya kaa sɛ, “Jump and land upon me. ” Kofi-the-beast-child no tuu ne ho kɔɔ agya no so, na Kofi-the-beast-child no kuraa ne menem. Ɔteɛm Kofi-the-beast-child no sɛ, “I have got him, so come. ” Ɔtutuu mmirika kɔɔ ne sekkan no nkyɛn, na ɔtwaa ne etire no. Kofi-the-beast’s-child no kaa Kofi-the-human-being no-mma-abofra no sɛ, “Seesei, ɛyɛ nokware sɛ me ne mo abɛyɛ nnipa a wɔyɛ pɛ, ɛnneɛ momma yɛn kɔsie no. ” Wɔkɔsie no, na wɔsan baa fie. Kofi-the-beast’s-child no ka kyerɛɛ Kofi-the-being’s-child no sɛ, “Mehwɛ kwan sɛ wobɛpɛ sɛ wotumi kɔ w’akuraa. ” Kofi-the-being’s-child no kae sɛ, “Yiw, nanso mebɛtumi ammu nea ɛwɔ. ” Kofi-the-beast’s-child no kae sɛ, “Me ara mebɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ hɔ, na mebɛsan aba. ” Ɔno na ɔde ne ho kɔeɛ, na ɔrekɔ no na ɔhunuu nnipa bi wɔ sumina bi so, na wɔne wɔn redi nkɔmmɔ (na wɔreka sɛ), “Yiw, Ɛyɛ nokware paa sɛ ɔbɔfoɔ ne ne yere no kɔɔ ɔbɔfoɔ no ntamgyina hɔ, na obiara annya wɔn ho hua da. ” Ɛhɔ ara na Kofi-the-beast-child no tuu mmirika kɔɔ Kofi-the-human-being-child no nkyɛn sɛ, “M’ahu mo akuraa, na nnipa a mehuu wɔn no wɔ ntoma mu, ɛno nti mebɛhwehwɛ mo bi ama mo. ” Kofi-the-beast-child no tuu kwan kɔɔ kwan so. na adwadifoɔ retwam. Ɔperee wɔn so, na adwadifoɔ no de wɔn nnesoa guu hɔ, na wɔdwanee. Kofi-the-beast-child no de ntoma kɔmaa Kofi-the-beast-being no-child. Seesei Kofi-the-beast-being no-child no kae sɛ, “There remains a gun and gun-medicine” (powder). Kofi-the-beast-child no hwehwɛɛ bi na ɔde bae. Kofi-the-beast’s-child bisaa Kofi-the-being’s-child sɛ, “So w’ani bɛgye wo? ” Kofi-the-being’s-child kaa no sɛ, “Muntumi nka asɛm sesei, me gyina wo anim yi. ” Kofi-the-beast’s-child faa Kofi-the-being’s-child no kɔeɛ sɛ, ɔbɛfa no akɔ ne akuraa. Bere a wɔduruu kuro akyi no, Kofi-the-beast’s-child kaa Kofi-the-human-being’s-child sɛ, “Ɛnneɛ ɛyɛ wo akuraa, na ɛnneɛ ɛyɛ wo baabi a worekɔ no, da biara me de nam pɔ kɛseɛ bɛba wo nkyɛn; na ɛnneɛ, sɛ worekɔ baabi a worekɔ no, wofiri aseɛ sɛ wofiri aseɛ, ɛnneɛ wofiri aseɛ sɛ wo yere da dan no fã baako mu, na wo nso da fã baako mu. ” Ko fi-the-human-being’s-child no bɛduruu fie, na ɔkaa abasɛm no sɛnea ɔne ne maame ne agya no kɔɔ nwura no ho, na sɛnea wɔn nyinaa awu, na ɔno nso afiri hɔ aba. Nnipa no dii ne ho awerɛhoɔ. Ɔbɔɔ atuo, yɛɛ ayie, na ɔwiee sɛ ɔyɛɛ ne nyinaa (required). Seesei, Kofi-the-beast’s-child no, bere a ɔsɔre no, na ɔde nam ba. Bere a anadwo bɛduruu cool no, ɔbɛn no. Ɛnneɛ ɛnkyɛeɛ na ɔwaree. Bere a ɔbaa no kɔɔ nna no, ɔyɛɛ no nna wɔ baabi foforɔ. Ɛduruu anɔpatutuutu no, ɔde nam pɔ biara bɛma ne yere. Seesei, ne yere no huu sɛ ɔnni nwura no kɔ, nanso ɛno deɛ, daa biara ɔnya nam, nanso ɔnni nsɛm tiawa biara. Ɛda bi nso anadwo, berɛ a wɔrebu no wɔ ne dan mu no, na ɔbaa no rebɛfa ne nsa aka ne kunu no, na ɔkɔkaa adeɛ bi a ɛyɛ dwoodwoo, na ne kunu no nso da hɔ. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔkɔka amaneɛbɔ no kyerɛɛ folk no sɛ, "Akyinnyeɛ sɛ me kunu nnamfoɔ no yɛ Daboɔ, na akyinnyeɛ sɛ ɛyɛ ɔno na ɔde nam no ba no. " Ɛneɛ nnipa no de wɔn atuo no kɔhuntaa wɔ asaworowa (baabi a wɔde nam no gu) no nkyɛn, na berɛ a ɛhwe aseɛ, na Daboɔ no rebɛfa nam no abɛduru abɛto asaworowa no so no, wɔde wɔn atuo no bɔɔ no. Kofi-the-beast's-child no kae sɛ, "Kofi-the-being's-child, nnipa dasani nni aseda. " Kofi-the-being's-child no tee, na ɔpueeɛ, na ɔde no kɔtoo hɔ, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ akuraa no etire nyinaa ne ho asɛm, sɛnea ɛbaa sɛ ɔne ne maame ne agya no kɔeɛ, ne amanehunu ahorow a wɔahunu no nyinaa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Esiane sɛ aboa kɛseɛ yi awu nti, me ne no bɛkɔ (abom), ɛyɛ me mfomsoɔ koraa sɛ ɔwuo. " Na ɔyɛɛ funnaka mmienu; na wɔde Kofi-the-aboa kɛseɛ no bafoɔ no guu baako mu; na ɔno (Kofi-the-onipa-being's-child) nso bɔɔ ne ho, na wɔde no guu adaka no foforɔ mu. Ɛnneɛ wɔsiee wɔn. Na wɔkae sɛ, "Mɔne bɔ na fa w’adamfofa nkitahodi nni ani. " Saa na adamfo bi fadie baa abusuakuo no mu.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
