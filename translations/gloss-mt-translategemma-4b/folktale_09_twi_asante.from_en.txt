Na aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Me ne no. " Ɛneɛ na ɔkaa sɛ, "Kɔ fie akyi hɔ na fa bayerɛ no ba; sɛ wode kɔduru hɔ a, nea ɛbɛka sɛ, 'Yɛ me, yɛ me, yɛ me,' ntu no; na nea ɛbɛka sɛ, 'Mmu me, mmu me,' ɛno, tu no. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Ho! " Ɛyɛ nokware sɛ ɔkɔɔ hɔ; bayerɛ no bi a ɛwɔ hɔ no kaa no sɛ, "Tu me u p, dig me up, dig me up; " afoforo nso kae sɛ, "Mmu me, mmu me, mmu me. " Wɔn ebinom a wɔkae sɛ "Mmu me" no, na wɔn ebinom na ɔtuu no. Na ɔne wɔn baeɛ; na aberewa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Mommra wɔn na monka wɔn wɔ ogya no so. " Na ɔmomma wɔn na ɔde wɔn guu (the ogya) so, na bere a ɛyɛɛ no, ɔdan no yii no adi wɔ aduane bi so, na ɔde kɔtoo aberewa no anim. Na ɔkae sɛ, "Kɔ kuro akyi, na hwɛ asaworowa bi a nam bi ato so, hwɛ nam no mu na fa wo apawde bi. " Na abofra no faa bi, bae, na ɔde guu ogya no so, na ɛyɛɛ nkwan. Bere a ɔwiee no, ɔde guu aberewa no anim. Bere a aberewa no kae sɛ, "So woawie anaa? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Yiw, Nana. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Bra bɛbɔ fufuo no. " Na aberewa no kɔtoo ne akyi, ɔkaa sɛ, "Boaboa bayerɛ a wɔanoa no ano, hwie gu ɛhwene no mu, na bɔ no. " Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛha ne adeɛ a ɛyɛ nwanwa, sɛ yɛbɔ fufuo wɔ nipa ɛhwene mu a, eyi ne nea maame kaa ho asɛm no. " Eyi nyinaa na ɔkaa no wɔ etire no mu. Ɔno na ɔkɔɔ so bɔɔ no; nanso bere a ɔbɔɔ no, ɔbɔɔ no dwoodwoo. Berewa no kaa sɛ, "Bɔ no den, sɛ wunbɔ no den a, wo kokoɔ no bɛpae. " Na ɔbɔɔ no pi! pi! pi! na bere a ɔwieeɛ no, ɔhwiei no guu nkwan so, na ɔde sii hɔ maa aberewa no. Aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Me nana, meka me din na mefa nni. " Na ɔkaa sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ wo Old-woman-grandmother. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ee! ɔnni sɛ ɔbɛdi. " Na aberewa no dii ne nyinaa. Ɛduruu nna awotwe no, na ɔne no dii saa daa. Na ɛda bi, abofra no kɔɔ asuwa kɔbɔɔ nsuo. Kɔtɔ huu no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na obi ba no ho ayɛ no dwo dɛm yi? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Old-grandmother-woman bi wɔ hɔ a me ne no te, na sɛ me no aduane no ma no a, ɔka sɛ, 'Ka me din na fa di'; meka sɛ, 'Wɔfrɛ wo Old-woman-grandmother,' na ɔka sɛ, 'Ee! wɔn ani nnaa kwan a wɔfa so di,' na ɛnneɛ ɔdidi; ɛno nti na me ho yɛ me kwan. " Kɔtɔ no kaa sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ no Old-woman-Mosono. " Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ho. " Na ɔde ne nsuo-pot no sii n’etire so. Kɔtɔ no kae sɛ, "Mommɛn werɛ mma mo kɔ. " Saa abofra no de ne ho kɔeɛ; bere a ɔreba no nyinaa, na ɔkeka sɛ, "Old-woman-Mosono, Mosono, Mosono. " Ɛduruu kuro akyi no, ɔwerɛ fi, na ɔhwe aseɛ na ne nsuo-pot no bɔeɛ, na ɔkɔɔ fie. Wɔn aberewa no kae sɛ, "Ɛhe na nsuo-pot no wɔ? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me reba no, me ho bɔɔ me, na ɛbɔɔ. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Fa foforo na kɔ. " Na ɔfaa no kɔe; na Kɔtɔ no kae sɛ, "Wɔsan aba dedaw? " Na ɔkae sɛ, "Saa din a wode maa me no, me werɛ afiri me, na meba mebisa no bio. " Kɔtɔ no kae sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ no Old-woman-Mosono. " Na ɔkae sɛ, "Ho. " Ɔkɔɔ so kae no nyinaa, Ɔbaa fie, na berɛ a ɔduruu fie no, ɔde ne ho too hɔ (kuku). Ɔbaa no kae sɛ, "Kɔkɔtu bayerɛ no na fa brɛ. " Ɔyɛɛ ne nyinaa sɛnea na ɛteɛ, na ɔde aduane no baa aberewa no anim, na aberewa no kae sɛ, "Mɔ me din na fa bɛdi. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ wo Old-woman-grandmother. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me kyerɛ me ankasa din. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wo ankasa din? " Ɔbaa no kae sɛ, "Yiw. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ wo Old-woman-Mosono. " Ɔbaa no kae sɛ, "Mfa nni. " Abofra no gyee dii. Berewa no de ne ho kɔɔ kwan so, na ɔkae sɛ, "Me kɔ hwehwɛ onipa a ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ me din ne Mosono no. " Na ɔkɔɔ asuwa hɔ, na ɔhyiaa Ɔbontoya, aberewa no, (na ɔbɔɔ no soboɔ sɛ), "Wo 'Bonto, wo maame! wo 'Bonto, wo agya! woka me banana akokoaa no sɛ wɔfrɛɛ me Mosono? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Na ɛnyɛ me. " Adwene no aberewa no huu sɛ apataa no gyina hɔ bɛn hɔ; ɔbɔɔ no sobo sɛ, "Wo Adwene, wo maame! maame! maame! Adwene wo, wo agya! agya! agya! woka me banana akokoaa no sɛ wɔfrɛɛ me Mosono? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Na ɛnyɛ me. " Kɔtɔ no huu sɛ apataa no gyina hɔ. Adwene no aberewa no huu sɛ apataa no gyina hɔ. Ɔbɔɔ no ​​sobo sɛ, “Kɔtɔ, wo maame! maame! maame! Kɔtɔ, wo agya! agya! agya! Na ɛyɛ wo na woka me banana akokoaa sɛ me te Nkran, me yɛ Ɔdehye mogya, na wɔfrɛ me Mosono? " Kɔtɔ no kaa sɛ, "Yiw, ɛyɛ me na meka kyerɛɛ no, na ɛyɛ dɛn na wode too mu sɛ wubɛma obi abofra aduru saa? " Na aberewa no de ne ho bɔɔ no, na ne calabash a ɔkura no, ɔmaa no so kyerɛɛ Kɔtɔ no. Ɛno nti na Kɔtɔ no hono wɔ n'akyi. Kɔtɔ no akyi no, na hono biara nni hɔ. Na Kɔtɔ no nso faa dɔte fitaa, na ɔde guu aberewa no ti so. Ɛno nti na aberewa ti atifi ntaadeɛ no, wɔn ti nwi fitaa.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom na mo betumi afa no nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a mo bɛbɔ me (wɔ asɛmka ho) no.
