Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐ WOREKƆ BAABIARA A, SƐ W’AKƆNNAA W’AKO BUSUANI NO SƐ ƆBƐKA W’AKO A, ƐNNEƐ FA NEKA NO.

NAA ɛKƆƆ SO BAAKO NO, NA ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔwo mmofra baasa. Nea ɔtwaa so no, na ɔyare yaws. Nea ɔtwaa so no, na ɔreka sɛ ɔne anuanom no nni sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma a wɔbɛtumi de akɔ dwadie. Nea ɔtwaa so no, na ɔreka sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkɔ nso. Wɔkae sɛ ɔnkɔ. Ne maame no kae sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔne no kɔ. Ɛneɛ na wɔn maame de sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (small change) a ɛyɛ £5, ne sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma a ɛyɛ £2, kɔmaa mmabarima no mfasoɔ. Bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, wɔtutu kwan. Mpanimfoɔ no kɔɔ (anim) na wɔgyaa abofra no (ma ɔdii akyiri). Abofra no de nkakrankakra na ɛbaeɛ. Mpanimfoɔ no hyiaa obarima bi a na ɔde apataa reba. Ɔka kyerɛɛ mpanimfoɔ no sɛ, "Mma! " Wɔn nso buae sɛ, "Kɔ so, na wobɛhyia abofra bi; ma no bɛtɔ, na sɛ ɔbɛka sɛ ɔrentɔ a, bɔ no, na ma no bɛtɔ; na sɛ ɔda so ara pow sɛ ɔbɛtɔ a, fa no kɔ maame nkyɛn. " Saa yɔnkoɔ no kɔɔ so, na wɔn nsa bɛka abofra no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mpem mpanimfoɔ bi a mehyiaa wɔn no, wɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wotɔ saa apataa yi. " Saa abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ me tɔ, ɛberɛ a me mpanimfoɔ a wɔyɛ me deɛ no antɔ? " Saa yɔnkoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Wɔka sɛ sɛ woantɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ me bɔ wo, na mefa wo bɛsan de ma wo maame. " Saa abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dɛn? " Saa yɔnkoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Osua biako sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. e. £2). " Saa abofra no tɔɔ no na ɔgyee no. Ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔduruu mpanimfoɔ no so wɔ akuraase bi. Wɔn nnuane atosodeɛ a wɔanoa, nanso wɔnni nam biara a wɔde bɛkɔ. Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ɛha ne apataa bi a me tɔeɛ. " Wɔde no guu soup-stew no mu. Bere a wɔn nnuane wieeɛ no, mpanimfoɔ no maa no apataa no etire. Bere a abofra no rebɛpae no, ɔhunuu sika kɔkɔɔ trɔm wɔ mu, na ɔde ne ntoma no ano kyekyeree no. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, mpanimfoɔ no yii wɔn ho adi, na berɛ a wɔrekɔ no, wɔhyiaa ɔbarima bi, na akokɔnini bi nso da nneɛma no so, a na ɔde no rekɔ. Ɛduruu berɛ a ɔne mpanimfoɔ no hyiaa no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Mommma saa akokɔnini yi. " Wɔn a wɔkaa no sɛ, "Kɔ so, na wobɛhyia abofra bi; ma no tɔ, na sɛ ɔremmɔ a, bɔ no. " Ɛyɛ nokware a ɔne abofra no hyiaa no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Mo abusuafo mpanimfo kae sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ wotɔ saa akokɔnini yi, na sɛ wotɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ meka wo, na mefa wo kɔ maame nkyɛn. " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Hwɛ, fa nea mewɔ yi. " Ne yɔnkoɔ no, ne nso, ɔde akokɔnini no hyɛɛ no. Wɔduruu akuraase bi. Wɔ akuraa no nyinaa, akokɔnini biara nni hɔ a ɛyɛ kwaakwaadabi. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, anɔpa, nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi, akokɔnini no, ɛyɛ abofra no de. Abofra no panyin, ɔbarima no, kaa sɛ, "Abofra, fa akokɔnini no mma me, mebɛtɔ. " Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Abofra no, boɔ no yɛ osua ne suru sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. e. £3). " Ɔpanyin no de sika no kɔmaa no. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi pefee, na wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ bio. Wɔrekɔ no, mpanimfoɔ no hyiaa ɔbarima bi a ɔde agyinamoa bi kɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mɔ agyinamoa yi. " Saa mpanimfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Fa no kɔ, na wobɛhyia abofra bi; fa no ma ɔntɔ. Sɛ ɔbɛka sɛ ɔrentɔ a, bɔ no, na fa no kɔ ne maame nkyɛn. " Saa yɔnkoɔ no kɔɔ so, na wɔhyiaa abofra no. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Mo mpanimfoɔ se mo bɛtɔ saa agyinamoa yi, na sɛ moantɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ mebɔ mo na mefa mo bɛkɔ wo maame nkyɛn. ” Saa abofra no kaa sɛ, “Ɛdɔɔso dɛn? ” Saa yɔnkoɔ no kaa sɛ, “osua ne suru mu duru a ɛyɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma” (i. e. £3). Saa abofra no tuaa boɔ; na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔne ne mpanimfoɔ no baa akuraase bi. Seesei nkura a wɔde bɛbɔɔ ɔpanin no anan no, bere biara a ɔbɛbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛda no, ɔbɛbɔɔ no. Bere a abofra no de agyinamoa no kɔɔ hɔ no, ɛkɔɔ ɔpanin fie na ɛkyeree nkura a ɛwɔ hɔ no. Ɔhene no kae sɛ, “Me bɛtɔ saa agyinamoa yi afiri onipa a ɔwɔ hɔ no nkyɛn. ” Abofra no kae sɛ, “Ɛyɛ me dea. ” Ɔhene no kae sɛ, “Ɛdɔɔso dɛn? ” Abofra no kae sɛ, “Osua ne suru sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma biako. ” Ɔhene no gyee sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no na ɔde guu ne nsa mu. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi a, wɔfiri hɔ kɔe. Na mpanimfoɔ no rekɔ so; wɔhyiaa awifoɔ a, na wɔadi ɔhene bi funu. Saa awifoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Ɛsɛ sɛ moɔ saa funu yi. ” Saa mpanimfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Moɔ fa so, na moɔ kɔ so, na moɔ hyia abofra bi; moɔ fa no ma no. Sɛ ɔka sɛ ɔrentɔ, bɔ no, na fa no bɛma ne maame. ” Saa awifoɔ no kɔɔ so, na wɔhyiaa abofra no; wɔkae sɛ, “mpanimfoɔ no kyerɛ sɛ wotɔ funu yi. ” Abofra no kae sɛ, “Eh! dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mebɛtɔ funu no, me ntumi nfa; dɛn na mebɛyɛ no, ɛremma me yie. ” awifoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Wo mpanimfoɔ no kaa sɛ sɛ woantɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛbɔ wo, na yɛfa wo, na yɛde wo bɛma wo maame. ” Abofra no kaa sɛ, “Ɛdɔɔso dɛn? ” Wɔkaa sɛ, “Osua ne suru sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma bi. ” abofra no tuaa boɔ no, faa funu no, na ɔde guu nwura no mu. Ɛneɛ na ɔpueeɛ, na ɔduruu akuraase bi, na ɔkɔɔ fie baako bi kɔsrɛɛ aduane. fie no wura no kae sɛ, “Ɛnni hɔ biara. ” Abofra no kae sɛ, “Nana, me srɛ wo. ” fie no wura no kae sɛ, “Ɛnni hɔ biara. ” Abofra no kae sɛ, “Nana, me srɛ wo. ” fie no wura no kae sɛ, “Dɛn nti na abofra yi haw me saa; yɛn Ɔhene awu, yɛredi mmuada; ɛno ne da a ɛtɔ so nwɔtwe; nanso yɛhwehwɛ sɛnea yɛbɛtumi, nanso yɛnni ne funu. ” Na aberewa bi te hɔ a, na ɔreka asɛm bi. ng near by; she said, “Give him some. ” The ɔbaa no de aduane ne nam maa no. Bere a abofra no dii aduane no, ɔkae sɛ, “Master of the fie, mehu Chief no ho; ɛnora a meba no, awifoɔ bi maa me sɛ mebɛtɔ no osua ne suru sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma mu duru. ” Ɔbaa no dwane kɔe, yiridi, yiridi, yiridi! (na ɛyɛ ne nnyigyei a na ɔde retu mmirika) na ɔka kyerɛɛ (akuraa) mpanimfoɔ no amaneɛbɔ. Wɔde abofra no kɔe, na ɔkɔkyerɛɛ wɔn nipadua no. Wɔkɔde no bae, na wɔsiee no yiye. Wɔkae sɛ, “Mprempren mo bɛdi Ɔhene no nkonim. ” Ɛno nti, abofra no bɛyɛɛ Ɔhene. Mprempren, bere a ne nuabarimanom mpanimfo no tee ne ho asɛm no, wɔbaae, na seesei wɔrekyerɛ sɛ wɔne no wɔ mmonnaa. Abofra no kae sɛ, “Mo mpo mo ho, me ne mo nni asɛm, mo kɔ. ” Na ɔma ne nkoa no kɔgyee wɔn. Ɛno nti, yɛka sɛ, “Sɛ wobɛkɔ baabiara a, na wo nuabarima kumaa bɛka sɛ ɔne wo bɛkɔ a, fa ne ho. ”

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
