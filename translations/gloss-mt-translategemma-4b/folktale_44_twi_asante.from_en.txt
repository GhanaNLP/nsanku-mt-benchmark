Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ PARROT NO TAIL NO BƐYƐƐ KƆLA NO

WƆka sɛ na Ɔhemmaa bi wɔ hɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ, na ɔyɛɛ abaayewa fɛfɛ. Ɛhɔ na na ɔwɔ; nanso bere a wɔde no awareɛ maa obiara no, ɔpoo sɛ ɔbɛware. Da biara a (ne) nnipa kɔɔ ne nkyɛn kɔkaa (ne ho asɛm wɔ awareɛ ho no), ɔka sɛ, “Dabi. ” Mprempren abɔdeɛ no, Ɔnini (Ɔne Ɔnanka) no, na ɛwɔ baabi a ɛwɔ nwura mu, na ɔtenaa ɔbɔn kɛse bi mu. Ɛyɛɛ da bi a na ɔwɔ ɔbodan mu no, ɔtee abɔfoɔ bi a wɔretwam, na wɔredi ababaawa fɛfɛ no ho asɛmti sɛ, sɛnea wɔde no maa obiara wɔ awareɛ mu no, ɔkae sɛ ɔmpɛ no. Ɔnanka no daa hɔ, na ɔkaa (kasa kyerɛɛ ne ho) sɛ, “Mebɛsesa me ho ayɛ me aberante pa, na mebɛkɔ akɔware Ɔhene Maame ba no. ” Ɛyɛ nokware bi, Ɔnanka no danee ne ho yɛɛ no mmabunu; nea ɔbɛhwɛ no tenten, na ɛyɛ yie. Ɔpuee kɔɔ akuraa hɔ. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na ɔrewe kola-nuts. Bere a ɔduruu kuro no kwanano no, na ɔhene yere no abarimaa a ɔkɔɔ ahohoro no, wasan aba na ɔrekyekyɛ ntaadeɛ no ma ɛreborɔ. Ɔnanka ntasuo no, na ɛbɛduruu ɔhene yere no abarimaa no gyegyeegye so. Ɔhene Maame ba no kae sɛ, “Dɛn nti na wopirapira me gyegyeegye so? ” Ɔnanka no kae sɛ, “Wo ne hokwan (sɛ woka saa), Amu. ” Ɔkae sɛ, “Me ntɔn abodin no nni Amu. ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Me ntɔn abodin no nni Esono. ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Me hokwan (sɛ woka saa), Esono. ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Me hokwan (sɛ woka saa), me gyegyeegye. ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛneɛ meware wo. ” Ɔhene Maame ba no ne Ɔnanka no sii hɔ. ff for (her) fie. Ɛno ara na ɔhemmaa no ba no kɔɔ hɔ kɔɔ ne maame nkyɛn na ɔkaa sɛ, “Eh! eh! eh! (she was stammering with anigyeɛ a emu yɛ den), mehuu nea mepɛ. ” Ɔhemmaa no kaa sɛ, “Ɛnkyɛ! Ɛhe na me ɔbabarima mmara no wɔ? ” Ɔkae sɛ, “Ɛha me, nanabea. ” Ɔhemmaa no de nsa bɔɔ Ɔnanka no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Momma mo ne mo yere ntra ase yie. ” Ɛduruu anwummere a ɛyɛ dwo no, ɔne ne yere kɔdaa nna ase. Ɛno ne nna a ɛtɔ so nwɔtwe. Ɔbɛyɛɛ tenten no, ɔkae sɛ, “Me yere, me rekɔ me akuraa nanso mebɛsan aba. ” Ne yere no kae sɛ, “Ɛnneɛ me ne wo bɛkɔ (akɔ). ” Ɔnanka no kae sɛ, “Dabi. ” Ɔnanka no kasaa ne kasaa (na ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛda no n’adwene nti sɛ ɔnkɔ), nanso ɔhene no ba no antie. Bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔne ne kunu no yii wɔn ho adi. Wɔkɔɔ so no, Ɔnanka no twitwiw (twitwiw) ne ho (firi kwan so) kɔɔ nwura no so. Ɔhenanamma no ba no kae sɛ, “Dɛn na woyɛ yi, ɛno ne kwan? ” Ɔnanka no kae sɛ, “Wode wo ho bɛto w’ano so na wodi me akyi. ” Ɔne no kɔɔ so, na ɛkɔɔ so, na (afei deɛ) Ɔnanka no yeraa ɔbodan bi mu. Ɔhenanamma no ba no nso kɔɔ mu. Ɔnanka no, ɔdane ne ho bɛyɛɛ no aboa kɛseɛ, na ɔkɔɔ Queen Maame ba no nkyɛn sɛ, "Eyi ne me ankasa, efisɛ me danee me ho wɔ ahyɛdeɛ sɛ meware wo, efisɛ na wote w’ase wɔ wo maame ne wo papa nsam sɛ 'hwee nni hɔ (me suro)', mebɛkum wo; monkeka me etire no na monnye ntontom no mma me; sɛ mo nnya bi a, wowie. d ne aduane. "The Queen Mother’s child sat beside him, scratching and searching for ntontom. Seesei, na wɔwɔ hɔ, bere a Queen Mother no ba no huu sɛ Akoo bi retwam. Queen Mother no ba no rebɛbue n’ano aka sɛ, "Agya Ako," bere a Akoo no de ne nsa bɛtoo n’ano. Akoo no gyee ne tuo no, yɛɛ no nyinaa krado, na ɛde (the kokoam fã) no bɛtoo ne kokoɔ so. Ɔnanka no kae sɛ, "Scratch my etire. " Ɔhene Maame ba no kae sɛ, "I am weary of scratching it; if you must kill me, then kill me. " Ɔnanka no pɔ sɛ ɔbɛma n'etire so akɔfa Ɔhene Maame ba no, berɛ a Akoo no yɛɛ tuo sɛ ɔbɛteɛm, pomm! Akoo no kɔɔ ɔbodan no mu bɛgyee ɔhene no ba ne Ɔnanka no sɛ wɔmmɛfa wɔn mmra fie mma wɔn amaneɛbɔ sɛnea ɛbaa sɛ wɔhunuu ɔhene no ba ne Ɔnanka no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛnea ɔkumm Ɔnanka no. Ɔhene no ba no nso, ɔde n’abasɛm no ho nsakyerɛeɛ nyinaa maeɛ. Ɔhemmaa no kaa sɛ, "Akoo, ɛdɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mefa so ma wo? " Akoo no kaa sɛ, "Nana, me kɔnni hwee, me ho me kɔnni palm-oil kakra pɛ. " Ɔhemmaa no kɔɔ so de palm-oil no baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Nana, me de me dua no reba me nsa wɔ saa palm-oil yi mu, na ɛnam so reyi no ayɛ akwantu a akokoɔduro wom. " Ɛno na ɛbaa sɛ Akoo bi dua no bɛyɛɛ kɔkɔɔ. Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
