Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SAA SAA, KRAMAN NO, ƆKRAMAN, AKORƆMMƆ (AKWAN) A ƆFA SO NO, WƆNNUM NO SƐ AYARESA A ANYA DA.

Ɛdii da bi a, na Kraman bi wɔ hɔ, na akorɔmmɔ suban nti, na ɔntumi nnya ɔbaa bi (n’ankasa akuraa) sɛ ɔbɛware no. Ɛno nti, ɔde ne ho kɔɔ kuro bi mu sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔgye ɔbaa bi a ɔne no bɛdɔ. (Ɔwareeɛ, na ɔsan de no baa n’akuraa hɔ.) Ɔne ne yere no tenaa hɔ (wɔ berɛ bi mu), berɛ a ɔde ne ho kɔeɛ, na ɔsan kɔɔ n’akuraa hɔ. Ɛduruu berɛ bi akyi no, ne kunu no de nkra kɔeɛ sɛ ɔreba hɔ. Ne agya ne mmara no de ne tuo kɔɔ nwura mu kɔtwaa Kraman adowa bi wɔ ahoboa mu sɛ Kraman bae. Ɛbɛyɛ nnansa akyi no, Kraman no kɔɔ hɔ, na ɔkɔeɛ no, ɔhunuu sɛ adowa bi honam wɔ ogya mu, adiwo mu. Ɛduruu anadwo no, ɔne ne yere kɔɔ mpaeɛ. Kraman no reba abɔnten kɔyɛ nsuo, berɛ a ɔhunuu nam a ɛwɔ srade a ɛretu gu ogya no mu. Kraman no san kɔɔ n’akyi kɔtoo hɔ, na ɔde etire no sii sumiiɛ no so, na ɔde n’ani kyerɛɛ nam no kɛseɛ. Seesei nam no hwam no bɛduruu ne nkyɛn, ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛdi no, nanso ɔantumi. Ɔ! ɔsɔre kɔɔ baabi a nam no wɔ no, ɔkamee no nyinaa, na ɔsan kɔtoo hɔ. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi, bere a ne yere rebɛprapra no, ɔhwehwɛɛ nam no, sin biara nni hɔ. Ne yere no kae sɛ, "Oh, maame! oh, maame! obi abɛdidi nam no nyinaa. " Kraman no tee eyi a, n’aniwa wui (i.e. ɔyɛɛ no aniwuo), na ɔde ne ntoma bɛkataa n’etire so, na ɔda hɔ yɛɛ komm. Ɛneɛ, ɔpɛpɛɛpɛ na ɔsɔre kɔɔ kwan so, ɔdwiri kɔɔ n’ankasa akuraa hɔ, na ɔkaa amaneɛbɔ no sɛnea ɛteɛ sɛ ɔne ne papa-in-law no nam no, na ɔne n’aniwa nni aniwu nti, na ɛno nti na ɔrekɔ hwehwɛ obi a ɔwɔ awuduro ano aduro bi ama akorɔmmɔ no, sɛ wɔmma no bi a. Adanko, Hare no, na ɔwɔ aduru bi a ɛyɛ den pa ara. Kraman no de ne hareyɛ kɔɔ Hare no nkurase. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Papa Hare, me tu mmirika kɔ wo nkyɛn sɛ mepɛ mmoa, meyɛ ɔfɔmfoɔ, ɔfɔmfoɔ, ɔfɔmfoɔ, na wɔreka sɛ wo wɔ aduro bi a ɛfa saa ho, enti me srɛ wo, ma me bi mma me, na sɛnea ɛyɛ boɔ biara, mebɛtua. " Hare no buae sɛ, "Ɛwom sɛ mewɔ bi, nti me kɔfa mpaigye bi mma me. "

Kraman no yii ne ho kɔɔ akuraase bi na ɔnyaa akyi nan bi maa domafa bi a ne mu duru yɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i.e. 3s. 6d.) na ɔde no bae. Hare no noa no, na ɔtwa no, na ɔde ne nufu guu nnua nketewa so, na ɔtwaa ɛmo, ne nkyene, ne gyeene, na ɔde ne nsa bɔɔ ne nam mu, na ɔde guu ogya so. Bere a ogya no bɛkaa no, berɛ bi akyi no, ɔne Kraman no tee sɛ ɛhyee, hyee, e e e! Kraman no hwɛɛ no berɛ tenten. Afei Hare no kae sɛ, "Kraman, wode wo ho kɔda dan ahodoɔ no mu baako mu, na me nso me kɔda foforɔ mu, ansa na ɔkyena no, mebɛfa nam yi (nam) ogya no so na meayɛ no aduro ama wo. " Kraman no kɔɔ nna mu. Hare no nso kɔɔ nna mu. Sesei Kraman no da so da so (nanso ɔntumi nna), na ne nam hwam no baa no so wɔ ɛhwene mu, nanso wankaa. Seesei ɔsan hu no bio. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Papa Hare. " Hare no buae sɛ, "Yiw! " Ɔkaa sɛ, "M’akuraa no mu nkura wɔ hɔ, ɛnneɛ me kɔfa nam no, na me de bɛto me nkyɛn. " Hare no kaa sɛ, "Kɔfa no; me deɛ, me kwan biara nni ho sɛ mebɛto me nsa so ama ɔkyena, ahyɛdeɛ sɛ mebɛtumi de no ayɛ aduro ama mo. " Ɛnneɛ Kraman no kɔfaa no. Sesei Kraman no da hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Papa Hare. " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Yiw! " Ɔkaa sɛ, "nkura no bɛkeka no, ɛnneɛ me kɔfa me sumiiɛ ase, na mekɔda. " Hare no kaa sɛ, "Me kwan biara nni ho sɛ ɔkyena, sɛ nneɛma bɛda hɔ a, me ne no bɛyɛ aduro ama mo. " Kraman no daa ne ho so bio; ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛtumi, nanso ɔantumi annye ne ho. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Papa Hare, saa nkura yi aba me sumiiɛ ase, enti mebɛfa nam no akɔto me etire so, na ɛhɔ mebɛgyina kosi sɛ nneɛma bɛda adi. " Hare no buae sɛ, "Oh, meka kyerɛ, nea ɛfa me ho ne sɛ mebɛnya nam no ama ɔkyena ayɛ aduro ama mo. " Kraman no gyee nam no na ɔde sii n’etire so, na ɛhɔ na ɔgyinaa. Na forowe no kakra bi refiri n’anim, na ɔreka. Ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, Father Hare, me nam yi deɛ, saa nkura no asɔre me so, na ɛba me etire so, enti me kura (me nam) no me ano. " Hare no buae sɛ, "Oho o! Me kɔ so ka saa asɛm no sɛ, ɔkyena, sɛ nneɛma bɛyɛ nea ɛda adi a, mebɛyɛ aduro ama wo. " Mprempren na ɛyɛ no ntɛm wɔ n’ano, na ɔkyinkyin dan no mu kɔɔ kɔɔ; ɔde ne dua (with a nsakraeɛ nnyigyei) no, fre! fre! fre! Ɔkae sɛ, "Papa Hare, ma meka ɛka wɔ me ho wɔ saa nam yi ho. " Hare no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na wopɛ sɛ woka ho ɛka? " Kraman no buae sɛ, "Me kyerɛ sɛ, sen sɛ saa nkura yi bɛdi no, ma meka no ho nam. " Hare no kae sɛ, "Oh dabi! ma ɔkyena anɔpa no ba. " Oh! nanso seesei Kraman no ma (nam) no kɔɔ ne menem so. Ɛneɛ na ɔhwee fam daa. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, anɔpa pa ara, Hare no kae sɛ, "Kraman, fa saa nam no ba. " Kraman no buae sɛ, "Ho, Papa Hare, adɛn nti na woante nea meka kyerɛɛ wo nnora, sɛ meka sɛ mehyɛ wo ɛka (for the nam) no? " Hare no buae sɛ, "Enti wodi no anaa? " Kraman no kae sɛ, "Yiw! Medii no, efisɛ mekyeree no. " Hare no kae sɛ, "Wo, Kraman, wo korɔno a wɔfrɛ no 'korɔno' no remmɛn wo da. "

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
