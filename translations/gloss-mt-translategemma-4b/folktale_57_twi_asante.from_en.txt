Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

CHILD-KWASI-GYINAMOA

Ɛdii da bi a, na abofra bi wɔ hɔ, Kwasi Gyinamoa, na ɔwɔ nuammaa baawɔtwe, na wɔn mu biara nni ɔhokafoɔ, ɛno nti, wɔn tu kwan kɔeɛ sɛ wɔrekɔ baabi kɔ okununom hwehwɛ. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, “Me ne mo bɛkɔ. ” Wɔn nso kaa sɛ, “Yɛn ne mo bɛkɔ. ” Wɔn mu dodoɔ no, nea ɔdi kan no kaa sɛ, “Ma ɔne yɛn kɔ, na sɛ yɛduru kwan no so a, na ɔbɛtumi atwe no ama yɛn. ” Wɔkɔɔ kwan so, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kɔɔ (ne wɔn) so. Wɔbɛduruu asubɔnten bi so, na ne fã bi (flow) no yɛ dɔte fitaa; fã bi (flow) no yɛ dɔte kɔkɔɔ; na fã bi (flow) no yɛ dɔte tuntum. Wɔteɛm sɛ, “Eh, dɛn na ɛbɛyɛ na yɛatumi atwa asuwa yi so? ” Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa de ne (magic) cow-tail-switch no bɔɔ nsuo no, na ɔmaa nsuo no bɔɔ mu yɛɛ no mmienu, na wɔtwaa mu kɔɔ so. Wɔbaa kuro bi so; na ɛyɛ kuro kɛseɛ paa. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, “Kuro no mu dinn (kyerɛ sɛ silent save ma nnomaa su a ɛwɔ babadua mu) no, onipa biara nni mu anaa? ” Anomaa bi kaa sɛ, “Ɛwɔ onipa bi wɔ fie hɔ, kɔ so. ” Wɔkɔɔ so, na na aberewa bi wɔ hɔ, a etire bɛdaa ne anan so, na ɔrehwehwɛ ne mu ntontom. Bere a ɔhunuu Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, na ɔde etire no sɔee no na ɔsesaa no; ɔkaa sɛ, “Me nuammaa, adɛn nti na wɔabɛba ha a obiara nni hɔ a ɔrebɛba? ” Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no buae sɛ, “Me nuammaa no yɛ baawɔtwe, na wɔn nni okununom a wɔne wɔn bɛbɔ, na ɛno nti na wɔkaa sɛ wɔrebɛba abɛhwehwɛ okununom a wɔbɛware wɔn. ” Old-woman-grandmother no kaa sɛ, “Oh, ɛno nyinaa teɛ t, ɛfiri sɛ me nso mewɔ mmofra baawɔtwe. Aberewa-panyin no de ne nana kɔɔ ne mmofra nkyɛn. Wɔn baeɛ no, eyi ka sɛ, “Me kunu yi wɔ ha,” ɛno nso ka sɛ, “Me kunu yi wɔ ha. ” Bere a wɔn nyinaa wieeɛ sɛ wɔayɛ wɔn mmofra no, ɛneɛ aberewa no kɔgyee suman bi a wɔfrɛ no _dokum_, na wɔkyekyeree no guu ne mmofra no akɔn so. Ɛneɛ biara kɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn. Na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔɔ aberewa no nkyɛn. Ɛduruu anadwo no, aberewa no de ne se kɔkɔɔ fann kɔɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akum mmabaawa no ne wɔn. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, “E! e! e! ” Aberewa no kae sɛ, “Ɛyɛ dɛn? ” Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, “Bere a me wɔ me ho akuraa mu, na me kɔ ahomegyeɛ nanso me ntumi nna aseɛ no, ɛneɛ maame twitwa bayere slice bi na ɔde gu ogya mu, na ɔfa kora a ɛtokurotokuro wom kɔ gugu nsuo kɔma me nnom. ” Aberewa-amaame no de ne ho kɔɔ asuwa, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kɔɔ ne suman a wɔfrɛ no _dok um_ ɛfiri aberewa no mma akɔn mu, na ɔde wɔn kɔtoo ne nuammaa so. Aberewa no san firii nsuo no mu, ɔkaa sɛ, “Me bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ mebɛbɔ nsuo (wɔ saa sɔneɛ no mu), nanso mebɛtumi anbɔ no. ” Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, “Kɔfa atɛkyɛ bi, na fa nhaban no gu (wɔ sɔneɛ no mu), na fa bɔ. ” Aberewa no kɔyɛɛ saa, na ɔbɔɔ nsuo no wɔ mu, na ɛyɛɛ yie. Ɔde no baeɛ, na ɔde bayere no yii ogya no ho, na ɔde maa Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa sɛ ɔmmɛdi, na ɔnom nsuo no nso. Ɛneɛ na ɔtutu ne ho guu aseɛ daa. Aberewa-babaa no sɔreɛ; ɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛyɛ me. ” Ɔde ne se bɔɔ no kɔkɔɔ, na ɔne wɔn kɔɔ dan no mu; ɔde ne nsa kyerɛɛ mmofra no akɔn no ho, na nea ne kɔn so _dokum_ suman no nni no, ɔde ne se bɔɔ no. Saa kwan yi so na ɔkumm mmofra baawɔtwe no. Na onnim sɛ ɛyɛ ne mmofra ankasa na wakum wɔn. Bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, aberewa-anaanafoɔ bi sii hɔ kɛseɛ, kɛseɛ, kɛseɛ. Aberewa-anaanafoɔ no nso kɔɔ ne nuammaa nkyɛn na ɔmaa wɔn dwiriwii. Aberewa-anaanafoɔ no san baa afuw no mu. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, “Aberewa-anaanafoɔ e! Aberewa-anaanafoɔ e! Sɛ woyɛɛ kwasea a, anka wobekum wo mmofra? Ɛyɛ me deɛ, me ne me nuammaa rekɔ. ” Old-woman-grandmother kaa sɛ, “Ɛnni nsunsuansoɔ biara, nea onim (how to deal with you) na ɛwɔ baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ. ” Ɔkɔɔ so, na Old-woman-grandmother nso danee ne ho bɛyɛɛ ababaawa fɛfɛ, na ɔde gourd-rattle kuraa mu, na ɔkɔɔ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa akuraa hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, “Obi a ɔbɛtumi atwa agyan yi, mebɛware no. ” Nnipa a wɔwɔ akuraa hɔ no nyinaa bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ wɔbɛtwa no, nanso wɔantumi. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa na ɔtwaa no. Ɔkae sɛ, “Mɛware wo. ” Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, “Me deɛ, meware wo. ” Ɛnneɛ na ne wofa no ware no. Ɔne no kɔɔ nna, nanso ɔyii n’aniwa mmienu, na ɔde kɔeɛ. Ɛneɛ abaayewa no kae sɛ, "Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa ɛwɔ ha! Me yii wofa aniwa mmienu no na mekɔ. " Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, "Mfa kɔ na mma kɔ, efisɛ nea onim (sɛnea wobedi wo ho dwuma) no, ɛwɔ baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ. " Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi, na wɔkae sɛ, "Abarimaa, kɔ na kɔ, na kɔsan fa wo wofa aniwa no. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me nkɔ. " Wɔhyɛɛ no hyɛɛ no, na (ɛno ne) na ɔpueeɛ. Mprempren, Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nim sɛ Old-woman-grandmother ababaa no, na ɔyɛ enceinte, nti ɔno nso nyaa ne koraa bɔɔ ne yafunu, na ɔkyekyeree ne ntoma bɛduruu so, na ɔkɔɔ Old-woman-grandmother akuraa ase. Ɛduruu kuro no awieeɛ no, ɔsui. Aberewa-babaanom kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ dɛn asɛm? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me ne me kunu no reba, na bere a ɔduruu bepɔwa no atifi no, ɔhwee fam na ɛno na ɛmaa ne aniwa mmienu no puei fam. " Aberewa-babaanom kae sɛ, "Ɛno nyinaa yɛ pɛ, ɛha ne ɔsɛmmɔnedifo a wasan aba wiase bio no, Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, ne nua no ani benkum, na ɛha nso ne ne nifa ani no. Sɛ wukɔ (kɔ baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ wose, 'Me aniwa huru me ho, me aniwa no mu da hɔ. " Ɛnkyɛree na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nsa bɛkae no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Wo wɔ hɔ, Old-woman-grandmother, ɛyɛ me kunu onifirani yi aniwa a woyi no, na me ne no rekɔ. " Old-woman-grandmother no kae sɛ, "Ɛno mfa ho, nea onim (sɛnea wodi ho dwuma) na ɛwɔ hɔ. " Child-Kwasi -Gyinamoa baa fie, na ɔde aniwa no bɛsesaa wɔn wɔ wofa no kanyane afidie hyɛbea. Ɔkae sɛ, "Me aniwa bɔn me ho, me aniwa no mu da hɔ," na wofa no aniwa no buee hɔ fann. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, mmofra no nyinaa kɔɔ asuwa no mu bɛhohoroɔ. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Me nni hɔ mma. " Wɔkaa sɛ, "Ebia wode awotwaa dua bi wɔ hɔ anaa? " Bere a wɔkaa saa no, ne nufuɔ no yɛɛ no kɔkɔɔ, na ɔhwiee hɔ. Ankyɛ na ɔhuruu nsuo no mu, Old-woman-grandmother bɛkyeree no, twom! Ɔkaa sɛ, "Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, meanya wo," na ɔne no kɔɔ n’akuraa hɔ. Ɛneɛ, Old-woman-grandmother kɔgyee kɛntɛn bi bɛkataa ne ho, na ɔnyaa palm-nuts, na ɔde sii ogya so. Ɔkae sɛ, "Me rekɔ gyeene abusua, me rekɔ nnuahwamfo abusuakuo, na ɛneɛ mebɛba abɛkum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa. " Ɔpueeɛ, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa boaboaa abotan ano, na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ crunch no, pupru! pupru! Aberewa-panyin nana no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na woredidi? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Tiger-nuts. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Momma me bi ma medidi. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo bɛkɔ. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me kɔ. " Ɔdaa no adi, na ɔpueeɛ, aberewa no nana no rebɔ palm-nuts. Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa palm-nut pestle no, bɔɔ Old-woman-grandmother ababaa no, na ɔkum no. Ɛnneɛ ɔtutu Old-woman-grandmother ababaa no wedeɛ, na ɔde ne ho hyɛɛ mu. Old-woman-grandmother no san baeɛ; ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na mo nyinaa ho fĩ saa? " Ɔno nso buaeɛ sɛ, "Me kunkum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa; me de no ato ogya so. " Saa amaneɛbɔ yi yɛɛ dɛ ma Old-woman-grandmother no, na ɔsawa so maa ne nne so (na ɔtoo dwom): "Child, woakunkum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, ɛda bi wo bɛyɛ obi. "

Ɔdii aduane no, wɔtenaa nkyɛn, na wɔdii. Aberewa-babaa no de nkwan (into her ano) dede kɛseɛ, fweo! fweo! Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, "You are splashing nkwan (into your ano). " Old-woman-grandmother no kae sɛ, "What's that you say? " Ɔno na ɔbɛkaa sɛ, "I was saying there is no nkyene in this nkwan-stew. " Old-woman-grandmother no kae sɛ, "Go and fetch some. " Ɛnneɛ na ɔkɔɔ kɔgyee bi. Wɔdii aduane no, na Old-woman-grandmother kaa sɛ, "Sei mekum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, monka me nkyɛn me nana. " Ɔbaa no, ɔde sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ no siesiee no yie. Seesei, Old-woman-grandmother de ne nsa kyerɛɛ n’anim berɛ bi. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ah! Saa abofra yi anim yɛ me ne Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no anim. " Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Nanabea, ɛdɛn nti, bere a wunim sɛ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa baeɛ bɛkunkum me abusuafoɔ baawɔtwe no, woyɛ me awerɛhoɔ. " Old-woman-nanabea no kaa sɛ, "Wo ano gu. " Seesei abofra no de ne ho tɔɔ hɔ wɔ fie. Wɔn nanabea no yii no kɔɔ hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ma me kɔ ne mfa nnto mfurow no so. " Ɛneɛ, Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa de aberewa no kɔe. Ɛduruu mfurow no so no, ɔgyee sika kɔkɔɔ agude a aberewa no de siesiee no no nyinaa na ɔkyekyeree wɔn. Ɛneɛ na ɔkae sɛ, "Ho there, Old-woman-grandmother, ho there, Old-woman-grandmother, I am he, Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, now for a mprɛnu so, I have come and killed your nana, and besides I have taken your sika kɔkɔɔ agude. " Old-woman-grandmother no buae sɛ, "I said your anim resembled that of Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa; now wherever you go, I go with you. " Ɛneɛ na wɔn baanu no nyinaa dwane kɔeɛ, kiri! kiri! kiri! Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa bɛduruu n’akuraa, na ɔde sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ no maa ne mmanom, na ɔkɔɔ kwan so, na ɔde ne ho siee ɛkwan no nkyɛn, na Old-woman-grandmother nso baa hɔ. Ɛno nti, sɛ wone kwan so a, wobɛte kakasakakasa nnyigyei, "pe. " Ɛno ne Old-woman-grand-mother a ɔrekyere Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
