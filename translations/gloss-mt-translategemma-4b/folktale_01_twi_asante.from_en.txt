SƐNEA ƐBAA SAA ƆBONTOYA NO, MPATWA KETEWA NO, NA ƐYƐ "WALKING" BERE NYINAA FA BASABASA A ƐWƆ NSUO NO MU.

WƆka sɛ Old-woman-grandmother no na na ɔwɔ hɔ, na n’agyapadeɛ no ayera. Ɔkaa sɛ "the nsuo mu mmoa" na ɛde (akɔfa) nneɛma no. Na kɔtɔ no te hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ, "Ah, moama yɛn aniwa bɔɔ yɛn aniwu, na nea yɛrehyɛ sɛ yɛbɛkyerɛ mo nea ɛwɔ yɛn etire no ne (eyi): kɔfa kɔbere dadesɛn bi na momma yɛn nyinaa de (yɛn) nnisuo ahyɛ mu ma, na nea ɔntumi nsuo mma nni mu no, ɛno na wafa nneɛma no. "

Ɔno na ɔde kɔbere dadesɛn no baeɛ. Okoto, kɔtɔ no, hyɛɛ suu ma ɛhyeɛ no ma; wɔgyaa ɛhyeɛ no mu; Mmɔborɔhunu baeɛ na ɔsuu; Okawa baeɛ na ɔsuu; Oyoyo, ɔwɔ-nsuom no, baeɛ na ɔsuu; Otidi baeɛ na ɔsuu; Ɔbonko, mmɔtɔ no, baeɛ na ɔsuu; Akɔreseɛ baeɛ na ɔsuu; Opitiri baeɛ na ɔsuu; Ɔkɔbɔ baeɛ na ɔsuu; nsuo mu mmoa no nyinaa baeɛ na wɔsuu. Ɛno ne (deɛ ɛtwa toɔ) Ɔbontoya, Mpatwa ketewa no, baa bɛsuu; wannya nsuo mfi n’aniwa mu nti, wɔhunuu sɛ Ɔbontoya adii (akɔfa nneɛma no).

Nanso Old-woman-grandmother no kaa sɛ, "Me nnyɛ mo hwee. " Ɛno so, abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no nyinaa kaa sɛ, "Yɛn deɛ, yɛbɛbɔ no, ɛfiri sɛ ɔde yɛn aniwu. " Ɛno so na wɔbɔɔ no.

Bere a wɔbɔɔ no kɔwiee no, Old-woman-grandmother kaa sɛ, "I leave you (for ever) to flit about aimlessly; no one will ever wish to have anything to do with you. "

Eyi ne sianteɛ a ɛne sɛ, mmaa "go splashing" (ma apataa no), wɔhu Ɔbontoya kyinkyin (sesei) wɔ heneɛ ne heneɛ.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
