DƐN NTI NA ƐYƐ SƐ MPANIMFOƆ NO KYERƐ SƐ YƐNNI NSI NSI AYEGYAE-DA (SLEEPING-MAT) SO YIYE?

WƆka sɛ (bere bi a atwam no), Nyankopɔn Kwame, ɔsoro-nyamfonom, ayi afuo kɛseɛ bi a ɛwɔ hɔ no, na wadi nkruma, gyeene, adua, turo-nkesua, ɛmo, ne turo a ɛwɔ hɔ no. Turo no mu nwira no yɛɛ den na honhon nyinii. Ewiem-onyankopɔn no de dawurubɔ bi bɔɔ nsunsuansoɔ sɛ n’afuw no aduru nwira so, na obiara a ɔbɛtumi atwitwiri no, sɛ ɔmmɛbɔ ne ho a, ɔmmɛtumi abɛn na wafa ne babaa, Abena Nkroma (kyerɛ sɛ Abena the ba a ɔtɔ so nkron) no akɔ awareɛ mu. Eyi deɛ, berɛ a ɔkɔɔ (kɔsɔɔ) no, ɔbɛbɔɔ ne ho (wɔ nea honhon no bɛdidi no), na wɔbɔɔ no nseɛ; ɛno deɛ, ɔno kɔɔ so (na wɔbɔɔ no nseɛ) nso; mmarima no nyinaa kɔɔ (kɔsɔɔ) no; wɔdii nkogu. Sesei Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, “Me deɛ, me ho yɛ me. ” Sesei na Sky-onyankopɔn no plantation no wɔ kwan bi so, na saa kwan no na nnipa no kɔ dwaso no Fida biara. Mprempren Ananse no, esiane sɛ na ɔnim nokwasɛm yi nti, na ɔde ne ho kɔ nwira no mu pɛpɛɛpɛ wɔ Fida biara. Ɛfiri sɛ na ɔreho, na nnipa a wɔretwam no de asan aka no sɛ, “Hail to you at your adwuma, Agya Ananse! ” Na ɔbɛbua sɛ, “Thank you, Aku. ” Wɔkɔɔ so kaa sɛ, “afuw bi a obiara ntumi nni no, (do you mean to say) woyɛ no weeding? ” Ananse no bɛbua sɛ, “Ah, ɛyɛ abaayewa bi a mehyɛ me ho sɛɛ nti. Ne basa baako te sɛɛ. " Na ɔbɛbɔ ne basa na wabɔ no baabi a ɛregyigye no, na sɛ ɔyɛ saa a, na ɛyɛ no abufuhyew no ma no nya ahotɔ. Obi foforo bi bɛfa ne ho bio wɔ n’adwuma mu; na ɔde ne nsa bɛbɔ ne ho baabi a ɛhɔ yɛ no. Sɛ nhwɛsoɔ no, sɛ ne sere bɔne a, na ɔbɛka sɛ, "Saa abaayewa sigyafoɔ no, wɔka sɛ ne sere no te sɛɛ" (na ɔbɔ ne ho). Ɛwɔ saa ɔkwan yi so na ɔtwaa afuw no nyinaa. Ɛneɛ Ananse no sɔree, na ɔkɔtoo hɔ. Kwaku Ananse bɛdii hɔ berɛ bi, ɛneɛ ɔsɔree. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abena Nkroma"—ɛnyɛ nnyigyei (save the babadua su) dinn! Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Me kura wo. " Ɔde kora ketewa bi bɛyɛɛ nsuo ma no ma ɛyɛɛ ma, na ɔde guu Abena Nkroma mpa so. Ɛneɛ Kwaku Ananse kɔtoo hɔ. Bere a ɔbɛduruu hɔ kakra no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Ko! Abena Nkroma, ɛhe na ɛyɛ? Wodeɛ nna ahyɛ mpa so, w’abɔdeɛ a w’ani nni ho no, ɛyɛ nokware sɛ w’ani nni ho koraa; Sɛ nneɛma bɛyɛ nea ɛda adi a, mebɛka no biara. Ɛno na ɛyɛɛ nokware, ɛneɛ (nea wɔn nyinaa kaa no), sɛ obiara kɔɔ saa afuw no mu a, na ɔka sɛ, 'abaayewa a ɔbɔ nna no...! Me renyɛ honhon afuo mma onipa a ɔte sɛɛ. " Ɛnneɛ Abena kaa no sɛ, "I implore you, desist, and let the matter drop. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "I will not leave it, my matter came first; you said you would tell your agya; I said, 'Desist'; you said, 'No. " Ɛno nti me nsa nka asɛm no.
