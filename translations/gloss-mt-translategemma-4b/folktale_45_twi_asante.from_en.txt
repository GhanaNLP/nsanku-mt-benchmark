Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

BERƐ A KƆKƆSAKYI, ƆPƐTƐ, REKASA WƆ ABƐBUO AHODOƆ MU KƆMA BƆNEKYERƐFOƆ NO, PATAKU NO, ƆTE ASEƐ.

BƆNEKYERƐFOƆ, Pataku, na na ɔte hɔ, na wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ne ɛna panin awu. Pataku hyɛɛ su, na hyɛɛ su, na hyɛɛ su, na ɔde ne ho kɔsrɛɛ Kɔkɔsakyi, Ɔpɛtɛ; Akɔkɔ, Hɛn; Odwan, Odwan; Nkruma Agyinamoa; ne Ɔkraman, Kraman, sɛ wɔmmɛka ne ho mmra ɛna no nkyerɛaseɛ no. Saa mmoa no nyinaa hyɛɛ aseɛ; na wɔne no kɔeɛ. Bere a wɔduruu hɔ no, wɔyɛɛ ayi amanneɛ no, na ɛkaeɛ sɛ wɔn ho tew wɔn ho fa nsuo. Wɔkɔɔ so ka kyerɛɛ Pataku sɛ, "Bɛbɔ wo, na yɛn nso yɛnni yɛn ho. " Pataku no buae sɛ, "Me deɛ, ɛna panin no da fam, na me nni m'ani so kosi sɛ da no ayiyɛ bɛkɔ so. " Wɔn nnamfofoɔ no kae sɛ, "Eyi yɛ asɛm a ɛmu yɛ duru a yɛahu" (efisɛ na ɛwɔ sɛ wɔn nso bɛdi nna aduanan). Ɛda biara a wɔsɔreɛ no, na wɔn afurunom abɔ (ɛkɔm), nanso Pataku deɛ, na ɔkuraa ahoɔden a edi mu. Mprempren, da bi a Ɔpɛtɛ no kae sɛ, "Ah, saa Pataku no, me nim no; saa berɛ yi ara na wama ne ho so akɔdi ne maame funu no. " Ɛduruu anwummere a ɛyɛ dwoɔ no, Kɔkɔsakyi, Ɔpɛtɛ no, de ne ho tuu kwan (na ne ntaban no nnyigyei ne) fA, na ɔbɛtenaa dua tenten bi so a ɛwɔ nsaman-saman no kwaebirentutu no, na ɛhɔ na ɔhunuu Pataku no a ɔrekyinkyin. Na ɔbaa, na ɔkɔɔ grave-mound no so, scratching, scratching, na ɔde ne maame funu no, kɔɔ kɔdii no. Ɔpɛtɛ no bɛtenaa hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, "Pataku, mo ne yɔ, yɛma mo aseda, nanso ɛyɛ aseresɛm aseda. " Pataku no kae sɛ, "Me srɛ mo, eyi deɛ, nka ho asɛm nkyerɛ obiara; nea mo bɛka kyerɛ me biara, mebɛte mo aseɛ ase na meayɛ ho adeɛ ntɛm ara. " Ɔpɛtɛ no tuu kɔeɛ, fa (na ɛyɛ ne ntaban no nnyigyei), na ɛbaa fie. Ɛnkyɛreeɛ, Pataku no duruu hɔ. Ɔpɛtɛ kaa no kyerɛɛ ne nnamfofoɔ no sɛ, "Ɛfiri da biara, sɛ ɛbɛyɛ sɛ ɛbɛyɛ den sɛ yɛbɛdi mu a, yɛbɛdi; ɛno akyi no, yɛbɛkɔ yɛn akuraa. " Wɔn mmoa no kaa sɛ, "So ɛyɛ nokware? " Ɔpɛtɛ no buaeɛ sɛ, "Mo bɛtumi ahwɛ me sɛ mebɛhyehyɛ. " Ɔpɛtɛ kaa sɛ, "Momma yɛn kɔ. " Wɔn kɔɔ Hyena fie. Ɔpɛtɛ no kaa sɛ, "Pataku, tie na meka kyerɛ wo, ɛnneɛ a yɛpɛ sɛ yɛdi, na wo nso, worebɛdi; ɛnkyɛn sɛ yɛbɛkɔ yɛn akuraa nso, na wodeɛ ɛwɔ hɔ sɛ wubɛka? " Pataku no kaa sɛ, "Me gye toom, mebɛdi, na wo nso woadi. " Ɛnneɛ na Pataku no kɔkyeree odwan mmiɛnsa, a wɔde bɛkyekyeree wɔn ɛkɔm no, na ɔde maa ne nnamfofoɔ no sɛ wɔnni, na ɔno nso dii. ome. Ɛneɛ na ne nnamfofoɔ no kɔɔ so kɔɔ fie. Ɛno nti na yɛwɔ abebuo sɛ, "Ɔpɛtɛ no kasa wɔ abɛbuo ahodoɔ mu kyerɛɛ Pataku no, ɔtee ne ntease a ahintaw. "

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
