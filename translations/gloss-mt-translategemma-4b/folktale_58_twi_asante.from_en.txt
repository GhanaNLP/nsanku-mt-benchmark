Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

WƆ KWAN BIARA SO, WOBƐHU SAA OSEBO NO, DABOƆ NO, WAKƆTWA NO, ADANKO NO, HARE NO, ABƐDURU HƆ DEDAW.

WƆka sɛ ɔsebo nini bi ne ɔsebɔ bedeɛ bi, ne adanko nini ne adanko bedeɛ bi, na ɛdii da bi wɔ akuraa ketewa bi so, na ɛkɔm bi baeɛ. Ɛneɛ ɔsebɔ bedeɛ no kae sɛ ɔrekɔ asuwa. Sɛnea ɔrekɔ no (ɔne) adanko bedeɛ no hyiaa sɛ ɛrebɔ aburoo. ɔsebɔ bedeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Ah, mother, where did you get such a adeɛ? " adanko bedeɛ no buae sɛ, "There is a kwan a ɛtrɛw a ɛtrɛw wɔ akyiri ha, and my kunu no dii akyiri kɔɔ wuram bayere hwehwɛ no, na ɔkɔgyee aburoo yi, so take this (and) go and cook and chew it. " She replied, “Thank you, Aku. ” Then the ɔsebɔ bedeɛ no yii no fii fie, de kɔɔ ogya no so. Na ne kunu no baeɛ, na ɔbisaa no (wɔhe na ɔnyaa aburoo no), na ɔkae sɛ, “adanko bedeɛ no na ɛmaa me, na ɔkae sɛ kwan a ɛtrɛw wɔ hɔ, na ne kunu no de (ɛde aburoo bi) na ɛbaa hɔ. ” Ɛne ɔsebo nini no kae sɛ, “Ɛnneɛ ɔkyena me bɛkɔ hɔ anɔpa pa ara. ” Ɛnneɛ na ɔfiri hɔ kɔeɛ. Bere a ɔkɔeɛ no, na aburoo wɔ bebrebee. Ɛneɛ na ɔde ne sekkan no twitwaa bi, na ɔyɛɛ palm-leaf basket, boaboa aburoo no ano, na ɔde guu mu. Seesei Adanko, adanko nini no, na ɔwɔ ne fie, na ɔhunuu sɛ ɔsebo nini no atwam kɔ afuo hɔ. Ɛno ara na ɔno nso, ɔtutu kwan. Ɔbaa kuro no awieeɛ hɔ na ɔdane Ɔtwee. Fa a ɛwɔ hɔ no, na ɛyɛ rotten; fa no nnya nnyɛ rotten. Na ɔkɔɔ akyiri koraa, na ɔdane Oyuo (antelope). Ne fa no na ɛro; ne fa no nnya ro. Na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔdane Bongo. Ne fa no na ɛro; ne fa no nnya ro. Na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔdane Ɛkoɔ. Ne nyinaa yɛɛ foforo. Daboɔ no na ɛreba sɛ ɛreka kɛntɛn ne aburoo wɔ etire so. Ɔbɛhyiaa Ɛkoɔ no. Daboɔ no kaa (ne ho) sɛ, “ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bi bɛba, nanso ɛnnya nnyɛɛ (severe) dodo, nanso saa mmoadoma yi rewu. ” Na ɔtwaa mu, na ɔbaa Bongo no so; na ɔtwaa mu, na ɔbaa Ɔyuo no so; na ɔtwaa mu, na ɔbaa Ɔtwee no so. Ɛneɛ na ɔkae sɛ, “Oh, mmoadoma yi dɔɔso paa, ɛnneɛ mebɛkɔ akɔfa (akɔboaboa). ” Ɔbɔɔ kɛntɛn ne aburoo no guu hɔ, na ɔde ne ho kɔe sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔfa mmoa no, ɛfiri nea ɛdi kan no so. Bere a ɔkɔe no, na wɔn nni hɔ bio. Ɛneɛ na ɔsan baeɛ, nanso na kɛntɛn ne aburoo no nso nni hɔ bio. Ɔkɔɔ fie, na ɔtenaa aseɛ wɔ kommyɛ mu. ɔsebɔ bedeɛ no bisaa no sɛ, “Ɛhe na aburoo no wɔ? ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Me kɔɔ (hɔ) nanso meantumi annya bi antɔ. ” Seesei ɔsebɔ bedeɛ no kɔɔ asuwa hɔ, na (hɔ) na ɔne adanko bedeɛ no hyiaa. ɔsebɔ bedeɛ no kae sɛ, “All I have to say is that ɛnora my kunu said that when he went, he did not get any aburoo to buy. ” Ɛnora adanko bedeɛ no buae sɛ, “He is deceiving you, for ɛnora, when he went and got (some), my kunu beat him and relieved him of it. ” Ɛnora ɔsebɔ bedeɛ no de ne ho bɔɔ fie. Ɔbɔɔ ne kunu no amaneɛ sɛ, “Well done! well done! well done! Wose wode kɔeɛ annya aburoo biara, ɛberɛ a Hare no na ɔbɔɔ wo na ɔgyee firii wo nsa mu no. ” Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, “Hwan ne Hare? Me ne no bɛkɔ akɔdi nnua ko. ” Wɔ fie hɔ na adanko nini no teɛ (sɛeɛ yi). Ɔkɔɔ so kaa ne yere sɛ, “To-ɔkyena, ɔsebo nini bɛba ha abɛdi me ko, ɛno nti mebɛkɔ akɔhyia no wɔ kwan so, ebia wosuro. ” Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, Hare no hyɛɛ aseɛ. Ɔduruu kuro akyi, na ɔdane Adowa, na ɔreborɔ dɔte fitaa, bidiɛ, ne mogya. Ɔkɔɔ so wɔ anim, na ɔdane bɛyɛɛ Bush-buck, na na ɔreborɔ kwan koro no ara so. Ɔkɔɔ so wɔ anim, na ɔdane bɛyɛɛ Ɛkoɔ. Daboɔ no na ɛreba. Adowa no kaa sɛ, “Agya Ɔsebɔ, boa me, efisɛ me rewu. ” Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, “Dɛn ne asɛm? ” Adowa no kaa sɛ, “Hare no ne me kɔdii aburoo ho mansotwe bi, na ɔbɔɔ me, na me rewu. ” Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Ma me mma mma. " Ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔne Bush-buck no hyiaa. Bush-buck no kaa sɛ, "Daboɔ, mmoa me, saa Hare no ne me nni aburoo ho manso. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn ne asɛm? " Bush-buck no kaa sɛ, "Me kɔɔ ne ɔbɔɔ me, ɛno nti na me rewu. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Ma me mma me mma. " Ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔduruu Ɛkoɔ no so. Ɛkoɔ no kae sɛ, "Papa Osebo, mmoa me, me rewu. " Daboɔ no kae sɛ, "Dɛn ne asɛm? " Ɛkoɔ no kae sɛ, "Me kɔɔ aburoo mansotwe wɔ Hare ho, na ɔbɔɔ me, na me rewu. " Ɛno na ɛbaa sɛ Daboɔ no dwen ho tenten (na ɔdwen ho sɛ), "E! Mebɛdane akɔ akyiri, efisɛ ɛyɛ aburoo asɛm a ɛte saa ara na ɛsɛ sɛ me ne no siesie, na mebu no sɛ ɔbɔɔ me... " Na Daboɔ no hyɛɛ aseɛ dwiriw, kiri! kiri! Ɛno ara na Hare no nso sɔree. Ɔdii no akyiri, na Daboɔ no teɛm kɛseɛ sɛ, "Ɔsebɔ bedeɛ, guane kɔ! " Daboɔ no bɛduruu ne fie, na ɔyeraa ne ho kɔɔ ne dan mu, na ɔde etire no bɔɔ afasuo baako so na ɔde kɔɔ mu, nanso Hare no kuraa n’akyi nan no mu nsɔmu. Na Daboɔ no kae sɛ, "Papa Hare, ma me kɔ. " Hare no kae sɛ, "Me remma wo kɔ, me bɛbɔ wo. " Daboɔ no kae sɛ, "Me srɛ mo, mma me nni, nanso momma me anyame nnom na meka ntam sɛ kwan biara a wobɛfa so no, mebɛba abɛsi no. " Ɛno nti, sɛ wofa kwan biara so, na wuhu sɛ Daboɔ no abɛkeka hɔ a, (wunim sɛ) Hare no atwam saa kwan no so.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
