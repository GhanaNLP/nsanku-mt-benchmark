Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA TALE-BEARING BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU.

WƆka sɛ ɔbɔfoɔ bi (bere bi) kɔɔ nwura no mu; ɔbaa mu hwehwɛɛ amena bi mu, na emu gyina hɔ sɛ ’Onipa, Ɔbarima, Osebo, Daboɔ, Ɔwɔ, Ɔwɔ ne ’Okusie, Okisi. Sesei berɛ a saa nnipa yi huu Ɔbɔfoɔ no, eyi kae sɛ, “Papa Ɔbɔfoɔ, bra me mfi. ” Ɛno kae sɛ, “Papa, bra me mfi. ” Ɔbɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, “Me remma, me ɔkyena mpo, sɛ megye wo a, na woakye me a, nea wode bɛyɛ me (menim) ɛnsɛ sɛ mehwɛ. ” Eyi kae sɛ, “Meantumi (anhyɛ). ” Ɛno kae sɛ, “Meantumi (anhyɛ). ” Ɔbɔfoɔ no kae , “Sɛ ɛno ne asɛm a, mefa wo bɛkɔ. ” Na ɛyɛ nokware a ɔde nnipa no nyinaa kɔe kɔɔ (n’akuraa) hɔ. Ɛdii da bi, berɛ a Ɔbɔfoɔ no wɔ hɔ no, Osebo, Daboɔ no, de nam pii baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ɔbɔfoɔ dada, eyi yɛ aseda a mekae wo wɔ ayamye a woyɛɛ me no ho, meka wo, Apiafo. ” Ɔde nam a ɛyɛ palm-leaf basket dumienu baa Ɔbɔfoɔ no nkyɛn. Ɔbɔfoɔ no daa no ase na Daboɔ no kɔeɛ. Wɔ dwumadie foforɔ nso mu, na Ɔbɔfoɔ no wɔ hɔ, na Okusie, Okisi no, baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Papa Ɔbɔfoɔ, wopɛ sɛ wunya sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma kakra anaa? ” Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Ho! adɛn nti na ɛnyɛ, ɛno ne adeɛ a mmarima hwehwɛ. ” Okisi no de ne ho kɔeɛ, na ɔkɔɔ nea Nyankopɔn, Sky-god, de ne sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no sieeɛ. Ɔkɔɔ kɔtuu ɛtokuro bi wɔ dan no ase, na ɔkɔɔ dan no mu, na ɔkɔgyee Sky-god no gold dust no nyinaa, na ɔde baa Ɔbɔfoɔ no nkyɛn. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Me da mo ase, Aku. ” Na Okisi no kɔɔ so. Ɛda foforɔ bi, na ɔwɔ hɔ, na ɔtee sɛ obi bi akye no sɛ, “Berɛ a atwam. ” Ɔbɔfoɔ no buae sɛ, “Ame” (come in). Ɔbarima no baeɛ; ɔkaa sɛ, “Agya Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ, anɔpa pa. ” Ɔno (ɔbɔfoɔ no) nso buaeɛ sɛ, “Yiw, kinsman. ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Eyi yɛ palm-wine kukuo ketewa bi a mefa abɛyɛ wo aseda. ” Agya Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Me kɔnni ho; wo deɛ, Ɔbarima, ɛyɛ me sɛdeɛ mekaeɛ no, berɛ a mekaeɛ sɛ megye wo mfi hɔ no, hwɛ nea Daboɔ no de baa me nkyɛn no, hwɛ nea Okisi no de baa me nkyɛn no. ” Ɔbarima no de ne palm-wine no kɔeɛ. na ɛkɔɔ so wɔ hɔ kyerɛɛ Ewiem-onyankopɔn sɛ, “Mo sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma a ɛyeraeɛ no, Okisi na ɛbaa hɔ bɛgyee no kɔeɛ na ɔde kɔmaa Ɔbɔfoɔ no. ” Ewiem-onyankopɔn no kaa sɛ, “Ɛno yɛ nokorɛ anaa? ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Grandsire, ɛyɛ nokorɛ pefee. ” Ewiem-onyankopɔn no de asɛnkafoɔ kɔeɛ sɛ wɔnkɔkyere Ɔbɔfoɔ no, na wɔde hama bɔɔ n’asene. Wɔbɔɔ no kɛseɛ. Sesei berɛ a wɔne Ɔbɔfoɔ no aduru kwan no so no, ɔkae sɛ, “Mpanimfoɔ, mehia sɛ mekɔ akɔgye me ho. ” Saa asɛnkafoɔ no kae sɛ, “Dabi, ɔfɔmfoɔ, wobɛdwane. ” Ɔkae sɛ, “Dabi, me nni dwane, na sɛ wopɛ a, fa hama no, fa kyekyere me asene so, fa kyekyere me dunsin so, na ma me kɔ. ” Saa asɛnkafoɔ no tiee no, na wɔgyaa no. Ɛhɔ na ɔwɔ, ɔyɛɛ ne ho dwo, berɛ a abɔfoɔ a wɔyɛ tenten-tenten no baeɛ. Ɔkae sɛ, “Hwɛ ha, me adamfo, gye aduro kɔɔn ketewa yi to mu, na fa kɔ, na wode kɔ, na wɔde wo bɛgyina asɛmbli anim, na ɛba sɛ wɔbɛkum wo, mebɛkɔ ɔhene harem no, na mebɛkeka ne yere a ɔdɔ no ba; mebɛkeka no a, wobɛhu sɛ obiara asɔre, na wo nso wobɛka, na woaka sɛ, ‘Me wɔ aduro,’ na sɛ wode no bɛdi dwuma wɔ abofra a ɔbɛsan anya no so. ” Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Me da mo ase, Aku. ” Ɔbɔfoɔ no de asɛnkafoɔ no kɔeɛ kɔgyinaa Sky-god (ne n’anim ne Mpɔtam badwafoɔ) Kronti, Akwum, Adonten, Gyaase ne Kyidɔm ahemfo no anim. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa n’asɛm, na berɛ a Kasafoɔ no sɔre bɛyɛɛ atɛmmuo no, wɔtee awerɛhow dede, pui! pui! akyirikyiri wɔ ahemfo mpenafo fie. Wɔkae sɛ, “Ɛyɛ dɛn? ” Wɔkae sɛ, “ɔwɔ bi akeka ɔsoro-nyam yere a ɔdɔ no no abofra. ” Ebiara kɔɔ so. Mprempren Ɔbɔfoɔ no gyina hɔ; ɔkae sɛ, “Sɛ me nyɛ deduani a, anka mebɛtumi de aduro (an awuduro ano aduro) ama awuduru no. ” Ɛne onipa bi a ɔtee (him) ne no kɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, “Mommɛfa deduani no mma yɛn. ” Ɛneɛ Ɔbɔfoɔ no baeɛ. Sky-god no kaa sɛ, “Sɛ wotumi hwɛ eyi a, me abofra, na ɔnwu a, mebɛma wo etire no ama wo. ” Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “M’ate aseɛ, Kind-one. ” Na Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa (bio) sɛ, “Nanabarima, nea mebɛkyerɛ wo yi, sɛ wotumi nya bi a, ɛnde mebɛtumi atete wo abofra no. ” Wɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛyɛ adeɛ bɛn? ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Tell-tale no mogyam no, ɛne aduru a wɔde abɔ no, ɛbɛyɛ aduru a wɔde bɛbɔ wo abofra no. ” ine.” Wɔkae sɛ, “Ɛhe na yɛbɛnya Tell-tale mogya? ” Kasamafoɔ no mu baako, a na ɔyɛ ɔnyansani, kae sɛ, “Ɛhe na yɔnkoɔ a ɔbaa hɔ bɛka kyerɛɛ Ewiem-onyankopɔn sɛ Ɔbɔfoɔ no abɛwia Ewiem-onyankopɔn sika no, ɛhe? ” Wɔkae sɛ, “Wɔnni nsekyerɛ, wɔnni nsekyerɛ. ” Wɔkɔɔ na wɔde yɔnkoɔ no baeɛ. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Break his nsa off at the joint, and take his mogya for me. ” Ɛno ara na wɔtwaa no. Ɔbɔfoɔ no de mogya no kɔɔ aduru no mu kɔduruu ɔhene no babarima no nan so. Ɛno ara na ɔsɔreɛ. Wɔkae sɛ, “Sing the Hunter’s praises. ” Wɔteɛm sɛ, “Eee! ” Sky-god no kaa sɛ, “I take your etire and present you with it, and as for the Tell-tale, let him be killed, and cut up his honam, and cast it about on all afã ahodoɔ. ” Wɔkum no, na wɔtwaa ne honam no mu nketenkete, na wɔde ne nyinaa guu hɔ. Saa na Tell-tales pii baa abusuakuo no mu.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
