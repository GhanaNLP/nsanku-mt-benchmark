Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

KƆ SO HƆ NA WO BƐHU

Ɛdii da bi a, na ɔbaa bi ne n’abofra te aseɛ. Bere a ɔde no kɔeɛ (kɔ baabiara) no, ɔkae sɛ, " Me nkɔ. " Na ne maame ka sɛ, " Twɛn hɔ na wobɛhu. " Da biara, bere a ɔde no kɔeɛ (kɔ baabiara) no, ɔka sɛ, " Me nkɔ. " Ɛneɛ na ne maame ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, " Twɛn hɔ na wobɛhu. " Ɛda bi, abofra no kae sɛ, " Da biara, maame reka me sɛ, ' Twɛn hɔ na wobɛhu '; ɛda yi ara me rekɔ akɔhwehwɛ ' mebɛhu ' no, sɛ mebɛhwɛ no. Ɛno na ɔde kɔeɛ. Ɔrekɔ no, na Pea-tree bi a ɛso sika kɔkɔɔ a wɔabɔ no asinasin wɔ hɔ. Pea-tree no bisaa no sɛ, " Dɛn nti na obiara mmae ha a, seesei woaba ha? " Saa abofra no kae sɛ, " Me ne maame te hɔ daa, ɔkae sɛ, ' Twɛn hɔ na wobɛhu', na mekae sɛ mebɛba abɛhwehwɛ ' I-will-see ', na mebɛhwɛ no. " Pea-tree no kae sɛ, " Me ne Wɔn no mu baako, nanso Yɛn mu kɛseɛ no akyiri. " Saa abofra no san kɔɔ so. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu Long-piece-of-nnyina bi a ɛreba abɛhyia no. Long-piece-of-nnyina kyeree no sɛe sɛ, " Dɛn nti, bere a obiara annyae kwan yi so da, sesei wode akɔma no? " Abofra no buae sɛ, " Daa, na maame reka sɛ ' Mehuu me', enti mekae sɛ meba hwehwɛ ' I-will-see ' no, na mebɛhwɛ. " Long-piece-of-nnyina buae sɛ, " Me ne wɔn no bi, nanso kɔ so. " Na abofra no san kɔɔ so. Ɛkɔɔ so no, (na) Glowing-Sparks wɔ hɔ, na ɔda kuku a abubu bi so, na ɔreba (ne nkyɛn). Ɔne no hyiaa. Glowing-Sparks kae sɛ, “Dɛn nti, bere a obiara mma mmra ha no, woaba ha? ” Abofra no buae sɛ, “Bere a me ne maame te hɔ no, ɔka sɛ ‘Mehuu me’, enti mekae sɛ mebɛkɔ hwehwɛ ‘I-will-see’, wɔ ahyɛdeɛ bi mu sɛ mebɛtumi ahwɛ so. ” Glowing-Sparks kae sɛ, “Me ne Wɔn no mu baako, nanso kɔ anim, nea ɛso sen biara no wɔ hɔ. ” Abofra no san kɔɔ hɔ bio. Ɔkɔɔ so no, (na) Nkoo no hwiirim nnyegyeeɛ wɔ fie bi so. Nkoo no kae sɛ, “Sei yɛbɛnya obi mogya ato fie yi so. ” Seesei bere a abofra no duu hɔ no, wɔkae sɛ, “Dɛn nti, bere a obiara mmae ha da, wubae ha seesei? ” Ɔno na ɔbuaa sɛ, “Me ne maame na ɛte daa, ɔkae sɛ ‘twɛn hɔ na wobɛhu’, enti mekae sɛ mebɛkɔ hwehwɛ ‘I-will-see’, na mebɛtumi ahwɛ no. ” Ɛno na ɛbaa so sɛ, Nkoo no gyee abofra no, na ɔtwaa no he ad, na wɔde ne mogya no guu kɔbere kuku bi mu; wɔde bi nso guu fie. Nkoo no tuu kɔɔ dua tenten bi so, na ɛbaa baako baako na wɔde wɔn adua ahoroɔ no guu mogya no mu, na wɔn adua ahoroɔ no bɛyɛɛ kɔkɔɔ. Nkoo no nyinaa de wɔn adua ahoroɔ no kɔɔ mu, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na wɔayɛ dwumadie no nkaedi ade. Mprempren, da bi, Nkoo no mu baako kae sɛ ɔbɛdi. Ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔhyiaa abofra no maame. Na maame no bisaa Akoo sɛ, “Me abofra a me ne no tenaeɛ no, na ɔyɛ dɔteɛ sɛ adeɛ biara. Me sɔre a me sɔre no, na meka sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔtwɛn hɔ na ɔbɛhu; ɔno, te sɛ kwasea, mepae sɛ meka me asɛmfua no, ɔkae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ hwehwɛ ‘I-will-see’ no mu, sɛ ɔbɛtumi atwa no. Ɛyɛ nnansa yi akyi da na mehuu no, na me srɛ mo sɛ, sɛ moahu no a, monfa no mmra me nkyɛn. ” Akoo no kaa sɛ, “Oh, ɔbaa yɛn akuraa hɔ, na yɛn nso, yɛkum no. Ne mogya no bi, a yɛde kɔyɛɛ dwumadie no nkaeɛ no, ɛwɔ me dua yi so. Ɛno nti, sɛ wode (wɔn kwan so) a, kɔka kyerɛ wo nnipa nyinaa sɛ sɛ obi ka kyerɛ obi sɛ ‘Dwen hɔ na wobɛhu’ a, ɛneɛ ɔbɔɔ no nnome. ”

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
