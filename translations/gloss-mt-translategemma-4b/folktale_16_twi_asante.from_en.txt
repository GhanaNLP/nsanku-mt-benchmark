Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐSƐ SƐ ABAAYEWA BIARA YERE NEA WƆDE NO MAA NO AWAREƐ.

NA ɛyɛɛ abaayewa bi a wɔfrɛ no Kwaboaso. Nea wɔde no bɛyɛɛ obiara a ɔkae sɛ, "Me kɔnni no. " Wɔde no bɛyɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ bi, na ɔkae sɛ, "Ugh! ɔbarima yi wɔ ntontom bi, me kɔnni no. "

Ɛdii da bi a, ɔkɔɔ afuw kɔtwaa bɔrɔde. Ɔde sekkan bɔɔ borɔdeɛ no, to! Bere a ɛhɔ na nnipa nketewa no te bɔrɔdeɛ no so no, wɔhwee aseɛ, baeɛ, na wɔkyeree Kwaboaso; wɔkae sɛ, "Wo ne nea, bere a wɔde no bɛma obiara, wosere wo etire no, pusu! " Na mmoatia no kyeree ne nsɔmu no na wɔkae sɛ: "Momma, momma yɛn nka no.

Yɛsɛe no, O!

Yɛsqueezze Kwaboaso.

Bra, yɛnka ne ho.

Yɛpɛpɛɛpɛ no, O!

Mprempren, berɛ a saa ɔbɔfoɔ no a wɔde Kwaboaso ama no no tee Kwaboaso nne, ɔkaa sɛ, "Me kɔhwɛ nea ɛyɛ asɛm, ɛfiri sɛ yɛnni bɔne biara mma bɔne. " Berɛ a ɔkɔeɛ no, na Kwaboaso wɔ hɔ, na mmoatia no rebu no. Ɛneɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no de tuo bɔɔ mmoatia no, na baako bɛhwee fam. Ɔpanyin mmoatia no kaeɛ sɛ (wɔn a wɔka ho no), "Ɔwɔ nsafufuo a wɔanom, na ɔduruu ne ho, fa no si hɔ saa berɛ yi, (na afei kɔkɔkɔ no so). " Bio nso, ɔbɔfoɔ no bɔɔ no bio, na mmoatia foforɔ bi nso hwee aseɛ. Ɔpanyin mmoatia no kaeɛ sɛ, "koodurufoɔ no wɔ nsafufuo a wɔanom na ɔduruu no, fa no kɔto hɔ. " Ɔbɔfoɔ no kunkum mmoatia no nyinaa gye nea ɔpanyin no. Nea ɔdii mfeɛ no, ɔtoo nne, "Mommra. " Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Me mmra. " Ɔpanyin a ɔwɔ mmoatia no mu no kaa sɛ, "Oh, momma mmra, me nyɛ wo hwee. " Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔeɛ. Ɔpanyin a ɔwɔ mmoatia no mu no kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ me dan mu, na wobehu aduro a wɔde bɛyɛ tuo no. " (Wɔ atuduro) ne otuo ano aba a wopere no nyinaa, fa nea ɛyɛ wo deɛ, na fa Kwaboaso nso, nanso ansa na woakɔ no, kɔ twitwa banana, na wode kɔ no, fa bɔ, na mmoatia foforɔ no nnyane a, na wɔabɛkyere wo, ɛnneɛ berɛ a wɔagyae sɛ wɔbɛfa no, baako baako no, woadi kan kɔ akyiri, akyiri, akyiri. " Na saa ara na ɔkɔɔ so twitwaa banana, na ɔde K waboaso as well, na berɛ a ɔduruu kwan no so no, ɔtwee kwadu bi guu hɔ. Ɔkɔɔ so yɛɛ saa kwan no nyinaa. Na berɛ tiawa bi a aka ansa na wɔadu fie no, ɛhɔ na mmoatia no rekyere wɔn. Na ɔtwee kwadu a aka no guu hɔ, na mmoatia no kɔɔ akyiri kɔdii no. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no san de Kwaboaso baa ne mmanom fam. Ɛneɛ na ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔɔ n’akyi kɔɔ ne fie. Mprempren, na ɔbɔfoɔ no te hɔ, berɛ a ɔhunuu sɛ asɛnkafoɔ bi aba ne baabi, na ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛdɛn ne amaneɛbɔ? " Asɛnkafoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Kwaboaso aka akyerɛ Etire a ɔwɔ akuraa no mu sɛ ɔmfa n’aseɛ mma no sɛ seesei ɔwɔ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔware wo. " Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Me kwan ma mo nsɛmfua a ɛfiri Odikro akuraa no ano, nanso me ntumi nware abaayewa no, efisɛ me da so ara wɔ t icks on my nipadua." Ɛno nti, Mpanimfoɔ no ka sɛ, "Wɔde wɔn bɛkɔ akɔma wo awareɛ biara, ware no, ɛfiri sɛ wunnim nanso da bi a wubɛyɛ ahiadeɛ mu no, ɔbɛgye wo. "

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
