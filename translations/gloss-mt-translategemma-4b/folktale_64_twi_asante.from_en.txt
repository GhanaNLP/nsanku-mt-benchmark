Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SAA WƆ KWAKU ANANSE NO, ANANSE NO, KƆƆ SORO WƆ APRANNAA NO.

ANANSE O! ɔwaree Nsia babaa, Ewiem onyankopɔn maame, na ɔkɔkaa Niamey (Ewiem onyankopɔn) sɛ, "Ɛda a wo maame, Nsia, bɛwu no, ba mmɛka ɛda a wɔayi ama ayi amanneɛ no. "

Ɛbɛyɛ nnansa akyi na wɔbɛka kyerɛɛ no sɛ Nsia, Ewiem onyankopɔn no maame, awu. Mprempren Kwaku Ananse ntam-abusuabɔ ne ‘Berekuo, King Jay, Ɔkwakuo, Kwaadu ne Odwan, Odwan. Ɛneɛ Kwaku Ananse bɛkaa wɔn sɛ, "Mommra meka ayi amanneɛ bi ho asɛm nti, ɛsɛ sɛ mo ne me kɔ. " Ɛneɛ Kwaku Ananse hyɛɛ sɛ wɔmfa nkra kɔma Ewiem onyankopɔn no sɛ ɔrebɛba dwumadie no ase. Ɛneɛ, Sky-god no de n’adesuadeɛ nyinaa hyiaa bom, sɛdeɛ Omanhene bi asɛmbli teɛ. Na Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Sɛ anwummere tɔ a, mebɛba. " Ananse no yɛɛ kotokuo, na ɛmu no na ɔde 'Berekuo, King Jay, Ɔkwakuo, Kwaadu, ne Odwan, Odwan, hyɛɛ kotokuo no so wɔ n’abatiri so, na ɔde ne ntoma kyekyeree so. Ɛneɛ na ɔkasa kyerɛɛ abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no sɛ, "Me kɔ Sky-god nkyɛn a, ɛsɛ sɛ mo su. " Berekuo no bɛduruu Sky-god nkyɛn no, na 'nne' no 'lifted up'.

"Kurukusu, kuw! kuw! kuw!

Kurukusu, kuw! kuw! kuw! nI."

Na Kwaadu no sɔre (ne nne) so.

"Kakum! "

Na Odwan no so (ne nne) no:

"Me! "

Eyi ne Kwaku Ananse awerɛhow. Nnipa no nyinaa kaa sɛ, "Woayɛ yie, ɛnneɛ ɔbarima bi a ɔbɛtumi asu ne ho wɔ akwan ahodoɔ mmiɛnsa so. " Bere a wawie (n’awerɛhow no), ɔsoro-nyamfoɔ no yii n’ani hwɛɛ ofie fɛfɛ bi a ɔbɛtena. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔsoro-nyamfoɔ no noaa aduan pa na ɔde maa Kwaku Ananse. Kwaku Ananse huu aduane yi a, ɔkaa (kyerɛɛ ne ho) sɛ, "Me mma Berekuo biara, me mma Ɔkwakuo biara, me mma Odwan biara, efisɛ me mfa mmuada berɛ tenten. " Ɛnneɛ Kwaku Ananse didii, ɔmfa ne mmonnaa nkyɛn biara amma. Mprempren, ɔne wɔn kɔɔ so yɛɛ nna ho nna, na Sky-god no kaa sɛ, "Yɛbɛkura da no ayiyɛ. " Nanso Odwan no kaa (Kwaku Ananse) sɛ, "Ɛkɔm reka me, me nkɔ"; Ɔkwakuo ne 'Berekuo' kaa adeɛ korɔ no ara. Mprempren Sky-god no kɔɔ so bɛtenaa nnipa a wɔahyia no ntam, na ɔmaa wɔn bɔɔ Kwaku Ananse amaneɛ sɛ nea ɔyɛɛ no foforɔ da no, ɔbɛtumi ayɛ no bio, efiri sɛ na ɔne ne mpanimfoɔ no abom (sɛe no). Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "Me ho nni yie. " Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no Sky-god no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, "Woama me aniwa abɔ me aniwu. " Na "Berekuo, ne Ɔkwakuo, ne Odwan kae sɛ, "Yɛn nso, woama yɛn kɔm de yɛn kɔm, ɛnneɛ yɛn ne wo bɛkyerɛkyerɛ abusuakuw. " Ɛnneɛ Ɔkwakuo ne "Berekuo" kɔɔ nwura no ho, nanso Odwan, Odwan no, ɔda so wɔ fie; na Ananse no huri ne ho guu aprannaa no so, ɛno na ɛbaa. na ɛyɛɛ no aniwuo.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
