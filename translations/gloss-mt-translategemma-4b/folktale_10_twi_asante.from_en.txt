SƐNEA ANENE NO KƆN, NE KWAAKWAADABI NO, BƐYƐƐ FITA NA NE AKYIRI BƐYƐƐ TUNTUM

Ɔbaa bi, ne din ne Ata (the Nta), kɔɔ n’anim kɔtoo n’abofra no. Bere a wɔduruu asuwa hɔ no, n’abofra no kae sɛ, "Yɛnkɔ nsuo no so. " Nanso aberewa no kae sɛ, "Yɛnkɔ downstream, ɛhɔ na nkawa nketewa no wɔ. " N’abofra no kae sɛ, "Me deɛ, mekɔ upstream, ɛhɔ na nkawa nketewa no wɔ. " Ɛneɛ, ne maame no kɔɔ downstream. Wɔda soɔ kɔɔ berɛ tenten, na n’abofra no nyaa bi, na ɔbaa bɛkyerɛɛ ne maame. Sɛnea na ɔrekɔ no, na ne maame calabash bi refiri nsuo no anim. N’abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ah! ɛnyɛ baako (adeɛ), ɛnyɛ mmienu (nneɛma), ɛyɛ maame calabash. " Na ɔsɔre (ne nne) teɛ teɛ frɛɛ ne maame, sɛ:

"Me kaeɛ saa, na mo ntie, meka kyerɛɛ mo, na mo nte.

Me kae sɛ, ‘Ahenewa, yɛnkɔ n’atifi, ɛhɔ na nkawa nketewa no te’.

Na ɔkae sɛ ɔrekɔ downstream, ɛfiri sɛ asuwa no mu na nkawa nketewa no te.

Na ɛnneɛ ɛyɛ me sɛn? Ɛyɛ me ma me maame, Ata, mhm!

Na Akrɔma no baa hɔ; ɔkaa sɛ, "Abofra, wo dwom no yɛ dɛ, to no na mebɛgye wo maame ama wo. " Na abofra no de ne ho so (ne nne):

"Me kaeɛ saa, na mo ntie, meka kyerɛɛ mo, na mo nte.

Me kae sɛ, ‘Ahenewa, yɛnkɔ n’atifi, ɛhɔ na nkawa nketewa no te’.

Na ɔkae sɛ ɔrekɔ downstream, ɛfiri sɛ asuwa no mu na nkawa nketewa no te.

Na ɛnneɛ ɛyɛ me sɛn? Ɛyɛ me ma me maame, Ata, mhm!

Na Akrɔma no de ne (magic) animal tail-switch no bɔɔ nsuo no anim, nanso nsuo no gyinaa nea ɛgyina no. Na ɔno (Akrɔma) no kɔɔ so. Na Kɔkɔsakyi, Ɔpɛtɛ no, de ne ho bɛkaa ho, na ɔno nso kae sɛ, "Sing that dwom no mma me sɛ mebɛte, na mebɛnya wo maame no ama wo. " Na abofra no too dwom no:

"Me kaeɛ saa, na mo ntie, meka kyerɛɛ mo, na mo nte.

Me kae sɛ, ‘Ahenewa, yɛnkɔ n’atifi, ɛhɔ na nkawa nketewa no te’.

Na ɔkae sɛ ɔrekɔ downstream, ɛfiri sɛ asuwa no mu na nkawa nketewa no te.

Na ɛnneɛ ɛyɛ me sɛn? Ɛyɛ me ma me maame, Ata, mhm!

Na ɔsan nso de ne (magic) asu no bɔɔ asubonten no anim, nanso hwee annyɛ. Na ɔno nso kɔɔ so. Na (now) Anene, Kwaakwaadabi no nso bɛkaa ho. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abofra, dɛn nti na wosui saa? Su na momma me nte sɛ mebɛtumi ayi wo maame ama wo. Na abofra no too dwom no:

"Me kaeɛ saa, na mo ntie, meka kyerɛɛ mo, na mo nte.

Me kae sɛ, ‘Ahenewa, yɛnkɔ n’atifi, ɛhɔ na nkawa nketewa no te’.

Na ɔkae sɛ ɔrekɔ downstream, ɛfiri sɛ asuwa no mu na nkawa nketewa no te.

Na ɛnneɛ ɛyɛ me sɛn? Ɛyɛ me ma me maame, Ata, mhm!

Kwaakwaadabi no faa aduro bi a ɛyɛ cone na ɔtow guu nsuo no mu, na ɔde ne bodua suman bɔɔ nsuo no anim, na nsuo no mu paapaa mu; na ɔkae sɛ, "Mɔ dwom"; na abofra no too dwom. Na ɔhunuu ne maame ti a ɛyɛ ti nwi no; na ɔserewee kakra. Ɔkae sɛ, "Mɔ bio," na ɔtoo dwom. Na ne maame no puei kɔɔ asene. Kwaakwaadabi no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ so dwomtoɔ. " Na abofra no too dwom, na ne maame puei kɔɔ ne nanpɔ so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Moma bio"; na ɔtoo dwom. Ne maame puei hɔ nyinaa na wɔgyinaa sikakorabea hɔ. Ata, Nta, kae sɛ, "Kwaakwaadabi, ɛdɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mefa so ma wo ase? " Ɔno na ɔbuaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, mempɛ hwee koraa, kɔtɔ dark-blue ne fitaa ntama pɛ. " Ata tutuu kɔtɔɔ bi, na ɔde no baa Kwaakwaadabi hɔ, na Kwaakwaadabi no de ne ho kɔkɔɔ no dark-blue, na fitaa a ɔkyekyeree no bɛyɛɛ ne kɔn ho. Ɛno nti na wobɛhu Kwaakwaadabi no, ne kɔn no yɛ dark coloured, nanso ne ho kɔn no yɛ fitaa. Ɛyɛ akyɛdeɛ a ɔnya fii Ata, Nta hɔ.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom na mo betumi afa no nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a mo bɛbɔ me (wɔ asɛmka ho) no.
