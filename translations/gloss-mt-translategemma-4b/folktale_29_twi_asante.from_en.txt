Ɛdii da bi a, na mmarima baasa bi te hɔ a wɔpɛ adwuma. Wɔkɔɔ Tall-grass-country kɔtɔɔ nkyekye ntoma ne nneɛma afoforo. (Wɔn san ba Asante no) wɔde wofase ba bi soaa nneɛma no bi, na wɔkɔɔ so tɔn. " Ɔne no, wɔde afahyɛ a ɛfata na ɛwaree no. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, anɔpa no, wɔfafoɔ mmiɛnsa no kɔɔ wɔn ankasa kuro mu. Kwaku-who-takes-to-think no, ɔda so ara wɔ ne yere kuro mu. Ɔtenaa hɔ afe baako, na berɛ a ɔsɔre (wɔ anɔpa) no, na ɔne ne nnamfofoɔ no te hɔ di nte. Ɛnneɛ, da bi a na ɔredi agorɔ saa no, ɔbɔɔ marbuls mmienu wɔ tuo toɔ mu, nanso nnipa a wɔne no redi agorɔ no, wɔkae sɛ wɔremma no, na mpo wɔpɛɛ sɛ wɔbɔ no. Ɔbɔɔ ‘sɛnea’ wɔ n’akyi, na ɔhunuu abofra bi a ɔregyina hɔ; ɔbɔɔ ɔboɔ bi maa no, na ɔde maa abofra no sɛ ɔmfa nkɔma ne yere. Sesei berɛ a ne yere gyee ɔboɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, " Me kunu pɛ sɛ ɔsan kɔ ne kuro mu, efisɛ ɔka sɛ anomaa a ɔtra fam dodoɔ no, ɛgye ɔboɔ. " Ɔhemmaa no kaa sɛ, " Sɛ ɔkɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ wode no kɔ. " Ɛyɛ nokware bi, ɔne no kɔɔ hɔ. Na wɔhyiaa Kwaku-a-fa-ɔhaw-to-dwen no nuanom. Ɛnneɛ, ɛberɛ bi akyi no, wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, " Kwaku-a-fa-nsusuw-ɔhaw-to-dwen no, fa hama a etwa to no kɔ nwura nkyɛn kɔyɛ fidie wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ yɛbɛkyere mborɔnoma a yɛbɛma ahɔhoɔgyeɛ ama wo yere a ɔyɛ ɔhɔhoɔ no. " Ɛnneɛ wɔfiri hɔ kɔeɛ, nanso na mpanimfoɔ yi apene so sɛ wɔbɛkum no. Wɔne Kwaku-a-fa-ɔhaw-susuu-no-kɔɔ so; wɔduruu (beaeɛ a ɛyɛ ankonam) wɔ ɔkwan no so; wɔkyeree no na wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Yɛbɛkum wo. ” Ɛnneɛ na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, “Me kɔɔ me yere nkurase no, mehuu sika bebree, na sɛ moamma me kyerɛ mo, na mewu a, morenhu. ” Wɔbisaa no sɛ, “Ɛhe na sika no wɔ? ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛsɛ sɛ mo kɔ me yere nkyɛn kɔka no sɛ meatɔ nam, na (the adwetɔfoɔ) se boɔ yɛ peredwan du (£80), na ɛsɛ sɛ ɔhwehwɛ kotokuo a wɔde ntoma nketewa (sheep hide) ayɛ no mu; sɛ ɔhwehwɛ hɔ annya a, ma ɔhwehwɛ Ɔkrampan kotoku no mu, sɛ ɔnhunu wɔ hɔ a, ma ɔhwehwɛ abɔsoɔ a nsekan wɔ mu no mu. ” Ɛnneɛ wɔbɛkaa no maa ne yere no nkrasɛm no.
