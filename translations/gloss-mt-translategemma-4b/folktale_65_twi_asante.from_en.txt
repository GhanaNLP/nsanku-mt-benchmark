Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA AKETEKYIRE, SAKRAWI NO, NYAA ƐSE A AHYE NO.

AKETEKYIRE, Sakrawi, ne Kwaku Ananse, Ananse, hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ wɔrekɔ hwehwɛ aburoo wɔ ahyɛdeɛ a wɔde bɛma wɔadua no. Na wɔanya aburoo aba mmienu pɛ. Na Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Me kɔkɔɔ me deɛ. " Na Sakrawi nso kaa sɛ, "Me kɔkɔɔ me deɛ. " Kwaku Ananse kɔdwaa ne deɛ. Sakrawi no deɛ, ɔno nso de ne deɛ no, ɔde too ogya so. Ɛyɛɛ akyɛre a wɔnoa no, na ɔtenaa hɔ (ne nkyɛn). Ananse no nso, ɔkɔɔ afuw hɔ; na ɔrebɛpepe wɔ nkrantɛbɔtɔ a ɛkata aburoo no so. Aburoo no kaa sɛ, "Mmupe me aniwa mu, na wo nsateaa no ankɔ me aniwa mu. " Kwaku Ananse de n’aniwa rekyerɛ ne Kraman, Kraman no kaa sɛ, "Wo nhwɛ me, ɛnyɛ me na me yɛe. " Kwaku Ananse rebɛtwa poma bi abɛbɔ Kraman no, berɛ a poma no kaa sɛ, "Mmu me, ɛfiri sɛ (sɛ woyɛ a) wo Kraman no bɛka me. " Kwaku Ananse baa fie kɔkaa ne ho asɛm no nyinaa kyerɛɛ Sakrawi. Bere a na ɔrewe no, ɔhwee ogya mu. Saa na ɛbaa sɛ Sakrawi no se no hyeɛ no. Eyi, me abasɛm yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforɔ, na fa bi nso bɛba me nkyɛn.
