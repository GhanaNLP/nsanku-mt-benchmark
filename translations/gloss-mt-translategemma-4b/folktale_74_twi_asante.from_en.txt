Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

KWAMENA, THE TIERED ANT-HILL, A ƐGYINA WƆ NHINI A AGYINA PINTINN NO.

WƆKA SƐ, bere bi na Ɔhemmaa babaa bi wɔ hɔ, na ɛmfa ho obiara a wɔde no awareɛ no, ɔbɛka sɛ, ɔmpɛ no. Nanso (at last) Nsaman-saman no Ɔhene, Adu Gyamfi, baeɛ na ɔwaree saa Ɔhemmaa abofra no. Queen Mother no ba no ka ne kunu sɛ, "Me ne wo bɛkɔ wo kuro no mu. " Ɔne ɔbarima no bɛkaa sɛ, "Ma me kɔ (kan), na ɛdwoada a ɛdi hɔ yi, mebɛba abɛgye wo. " Ɔne ɔbaa no bɛkaa sɛ, "Dabi, me ne wo bɛkɔ abom. " Ɛneɛ wɔkɔɔ kɔɔ. Wɔrekɔ no, wɔhunuu kuropɔn bi a dadeɛ pono wɔ ne kwanano, na dadeɛ ne dadeɛ na ɛkame kuro no nyinaa. Sesei, wɔ kuro yi mu, sɛ wo busuani biara wui a, anka wubegyina ɛpono no akyi na woabɔ no, obiara annye wo. Sesei Queen Mother yi busuani no, na ne busuani biara wui da. Ɔkasa ne nea ɛwɔ hɔ (i. e. me werɛ fi sɛ mekae sɛ) Adu Gyamfi bɛduruu dadeɛ pono no, na ɔkɔɔ ne fie; ɔne Queen Mother no busuani no anka asɛm bio. Ɔhene Maame bɔne ne ne nkoa-abea, Ɔkra, da soɔ, na wɔgyinaa dadeɛ pono no akyi, na wɔannya kwan ankɔ fie. Na Ɔhene Maame bɔne no de ne nne so (na ɔtoo dwom) sɛ:

"Adodowa, open to me, it is I who have come, Adodowa Kwasi Kampire, open to me, Adodowa, open to me o. "

Nanso Adodowa annye no. Ɔno nso annya ne ho anhu kwan a ɛbɛma ɔbɛsan akɔ ne kuro no mu. Ɛneɛ na ɔne Ɔkra kɔɔ nhini a agyina pintinn mmienu ntam. Na ɔhene ba no de kɔbere dadesɛn bi sii so, na ɔfaa foforo bi nso, na ɔde ne etire no so; na Ɔkra nso tena ne nkyɛn. Ɛno nti, bere a wukɔ nea ɛwɔ nhini a agyina pintinn no, ɛhɔ na ɔhene ba no abarimaa ne ne kɔkɔɔfoɔ Kra ato hɔ, wɔadane ant-hills a wɔfrɛ no "Kwamena" a ɛsɔre gyinabea so gyinabea.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
