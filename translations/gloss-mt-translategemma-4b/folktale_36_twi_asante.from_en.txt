Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA KWAKU ANANSE (THE ANANSE) NYAA "ASO" WƆ AWAREƐ MU NO.

Ɛdii da bi a, na obarima bi a wɔfrɛ no Akwasi-the-jealous-one ne ne yere "Aso" te hɔ, na ɔmpɛ sɛ obiara hu "Aso" anaa obiara ne no nkasa. Ɛnneɛ, ɔkɔyɛɛ akuraa ketewa bi maa "Aso" sɛ ɔntena mu.

Obiara ankɔ akuraa hɔ da. Seesei, Akwasi-a-wɔ-ahoɔden-no, wantumi anwo mmofra. Ɛno nti, sɛ ɔne ne yere te kuro mu a, na obi bɛfa no. Mprempren, Sky-god no ka kyerɛɛ mmeranteɛ no sɛ, "Akwasi-the-jealous-one has been married to "Aso" for a very very berɛ tenten; she has not conceived by him and borne a abofra, therefore he who is able, let him go and take "Aso", and should she conceive by him, let him take her (as his yere). " Saa mmeranteɛ no nyinaa bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ wɔbɛka ne nsa, nanso obiara antumi anka. Saa berɛ no, na Kwaku Ananse wɔ hɔ rehwɛ saa dwumadi no, (na) ɔkae sɛ, “Metumi kɔ Akwasi-the-jealous-one nkurase. ” Sky-god no kae sɛ, “Wubetumi ayɛ saa ampa? ” Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, “Sɛ wobɛma me nea mehia. ” Sky-god no kae sɛ, “Ɛyɛ adeɛ bɛn? ” Ɔkae sɛ, “Aduro ma tuo ne otuo ano aba. ” Na Sky-god no de maa no. Na Kwaku Ananse de powder ne otuo ano aba no kɔmaa nkuraase nkumaa (dɔm ahodoɔ), na ɔkae sɛ, “Sky-god no ahyɛ me sɛ mefa powder ne otuo ano aba bɛma mo, na mo kɔkum nam, na da a mebɛsan aba ha no, mebɛfa akɔ. ” Ɔkyekyɛɛ powder ne otuo ano aba no maa nkuraase nkumaa bebree, kosi sɛ wɔn nyinaa wiee. nkuraasefoɔ no nyinaa nyaa nam bi. Ɛwɔ da bi, Kwaku Ananse wove palm-leaf basket bi. Ne tenten no, ɛneɛ na ɛwɔ hɔ kɔsi baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ. Kwaku Ananse de kɔɔ nkuraase nkumaa, baabi a ɔde powder ne otuo ano aba akyekyɛ no, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na ɔgyee nam a wɔakum no nyinaa. Agya Ananse de nam ne palm-leaf basket no kɔɔ etire so, na ɔde kɔɔ kwan a ɛkɔ Akwasi-the-jealous-one no fie. Bere a ɔduruu asuwa a Akwasi ne ne yere no nom no, ɔgyee nam bi ahyɛ mu. Kwaku Ananse bɔɔ ne ho mmɔden yiye na ɔde palm-leaf basket (a nam ahyɛ mu ma) no faa ponkɛse ano kɔɔ Akwasi-the-jealous-one no fie. Na "Aso" huu no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Akwasi E, wode ne ho bɛyɛ dɛn? ba bɛhwɛ adeɛ bi a ɛreba fie ha, ɛyɛ dɛn na ɛbɛtumi ayɛ? " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Sky-god no na ɔreka me, na me werɛ afiri me, na me reba sɛ mebɛda fie ha. " Akwasi-the-ninkunu no kaa sɛ, "M’adwuma no, meate me ninkunu no. " "Aso" kaa sɛ, Kwaku Ananse, "Papa, wo nam no bi atɔ ponkɛse ano wɔ fie ha. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Oh, sɛ ɛba sɛ wunya kraman a... " ma no kɔfa no na ɔmmɛn. "So "Aso" kɔɔ ne ho kɔgyee no. Ɛneɛ na Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Maame, fa aduane bi (on the fire) mma me. " "Aso" no de bi guu hɔ, na Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Aso" no, ɛyɛ fufuo a woreso anaasɛ woreso? "Aso" no buae sɛ, "Fufuo. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Ɛneɛ ɛyɛ kakra dodo; kɔfa kukukɛse bi. " "Aso" no kɔfaa bi a ɛyɛ kɛse, na Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Mommra nam me. " Na ɛyɛ mmoa akɛseɛ aduanan. Ɔkae sɛ, "Mfa eyinom nko ara na fa mu, na sɛ wode kuku a ɛdɔɔso a, mebɛma wo nam a ɛdɔɔso a wobɛbɔ ama wo se afiri mu. " "Aso" no wiee (siesiee) aduane no, danee no (firi kuku no mu), na ɔde guu ɛpono, nsu a apete so, na ɔde guu aduane no nkyɛn (ahomegyeɛ no). Ɛneɛ "Aso" gyee ne kyɛfa kɔɔ hɔ bɛn ogya no, na mmarima no kɔɔ hɔ tenaa ɛpono no nkyɛn. Wɔn nsa bɛkaa wɔn ho wɔn ho akyi. Bere a wɔredidi no nyinaa, Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Yɛn fufuo yi nni nkyene biara. " Akwasi kaa sɛ, "Aso" mma no bi. Nanso Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ koraa, bere a ɔbaa no redidi no, woka no sɛ ɔmfa nkyene mmra, woankɔfa mmra. " Akwasi sɔre, na Kwaku Ananse hwɛɛ ne kotokuo no mu, na ɔgyee ayamtuo duro bi a ɛyɛ nsiho no, na ɔde guu fufuo no mu. Ɛneɛ na ɔfrɛɛ Akwasi, sɛ "Mommra, mekaeɛ bi meka me. " Akwasi baeɛ no, Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Oh, me nni bio, mebɛyɛ ma. " Akwasi a na ɔnni asɛm biara no, ɔkɔɔ so dii. Bere a wɔdii aduane no, Akwasi kae sɛ, "Friend, yɛne wo te ha na yɛnnim wo din. " Kwaku Ananse nso buae sɛ, "Me, wɔfrɛ me Kɔ soro-up-and-make-love-to-"Aso". " Akwasi kae sɛ, "M’ate asɛm yi, na wo nso "Aso", woate ɔbarima yi din anaa? " "Aso" buae sɛ, "Yiw, m’ate asɛm no. " Akwasi sɔre kɔɔ nnaadan a yɛmfa nyɛ hwee no mu biako, na ɔreyɛ ama wɔn nyinaa ayɛ yie. Ɔkae sɛ, "Sɔre-sɔre-ma-ɔdɔ-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman-abere-ne-ɔman ma Sky-god no, efisɛ na ɛbɛkyerɛ sɛ mebɛda Sky-god veranda dan a ɛbue no mu, efisɛ me nna da wɔ obiara dan a ɛbue mu (gye Sky-god de), na ɛno nti, mebɛda me ho ase wɔ saa dan a ɛbue yi anim. " Saa ɔbarima no yii nna-dan bi na ɔde sii hɔ maa no. Akwasi ne ne yere no kɔɔ hɔ kɔdaa ase, na Kwaku Ananse nso daa ne ho ase hɔ. Kwaku Ananse da hɔ na ɔdɔreɛ wɔ nsanombea (cross-road) no mu. Kwaku Ananse da hɔ, na ɔde ne bentaa (and he sang):

"Akuamoa Ananse, to-da we shall achieve something to-da.

Kwaku Ananse, abofra a ɔwɔ Nsia, Niamey maame, Ewiem-onyankopɔn, ɛnneɛ yɛbɛdi da bi a yɛbɛdi nkonim, ɛnneɛ da.

Ananse, ɔbra a ɔbra Niamey, Ewiem-onyankopɔn, da bi mebɛhu adeɛ.

Mprempren, ɔgyaee ne sɛpɛrɛwa no, na ɔde too nkyɛn, na ɔda hɔ. Ɔda berɛ bi, berɛ a ɔtee Akwasi-the-jealous-one no sɛ, “Agya man”—ɛnyɛ mmuaeɛ mu nnyigyei (gye sɛ nnomaa su a ɛwɔ babadua mu), dinn! “Agya man”—ɛnyɛ mmuaeɛ mu nnyigyei (gye sɛ nnomaa su a ɛwɔ babadua mu), dinn! Akwasi-the-ninkunu no rewu; aduro no akɔ ne so nsunsuansoɔ, nanso ɔteɛm sɛ, “Agya man”—ɛnyɛ mmuaeɛ mu nnyigyei (gye sɛ nnomaa su a ɛwɔ babadua mu), dɛn! Ɛduruu sɛ ɔkae sɛ, “Rise-up-and-make-love-to-"Aso". ” Ananse no kae sɛ, “M! M! M! ” Ɔno, Akwasi, kae sɛ, “Open (the ɔpono) for me. ” Kwaku Ananse no buee (the ɔpono) no, na Akwasi kɔɔ baabi. Na Ananse no sɔree kɔɔ dan no mu hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, "Aso" anka wo kunu no asɛm? Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɔkae? " Ɔne Kwaku Ananse buaa sɛ, "Ɔkae sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ me sɔre na mehyɛ ɔdɔ ma mo. " "Aso kae sɛ, "Wo nni atoro. " Na ɔyɛɛ no maa no, na ɔkɔtoo hɔ. Saa anadwo no, Akwasi sɔre mprenu; Ananse no nso kɔɔ hɔ mprenu kɔɔ nea "Aso" wɔ. Bere a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, adeɛ kuro no, Kwaku Ananse sɔre. Ɛbɛyɛ bɛyɛ abosome akyi na Aso yafunu yɛɛ no kɛseɛ. Akwasi bɛbisaa no sɛ, “Dɛn nti na wo yafunu ayɛ saa, ebia woyare, (wunim) sɛ me a me ne wo te ha yi, me ntumi nwo mmofra? ” "Aso" buae sɛ, “Wɔn werɛ fi sɛ ɔbarima a ɔbaa ha a woka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔmmɔ ɔdɔ mma "Aso" no, ɔde me kɔe na me nwoɔ no. ” Akwasi-the-ninkunu no kae sɛ, “Sɔre na ma mefa wo kɔma no. ” me kɔɔ Ewiem onyankopɔn kuro no mu. Wɔ kwan so no, "Aso" bɔɔ awoɔ. Wɔduruu Ewiem onyankopɔn kuro no mu, na wɔkɔka kyerɛɛ Ewiem onyankopɔn sɛ, "W’asɛm bi, wo de no kɔeɛ no, ɔda me fie so, na ɔde "Aso" kɔeɛ no, na ɔdɔɔ no. " Ewiem onyankopɔn no kaa sɛ, "Me adesuadeɛ nyinaa yɛ asese, kɔkyerɛ nea wopɛ. " Wɔkɔɔ hɔ, na Ananse teɛ wɔ ridge-pole bi so. "Aso" kae sɛ, "Ɛhɔ na ɔwɔ. " Na Kwaku Ananse tutuu mmirika kɔtenaa mfinimfini hɔ. Na bio "Aso" kae sɛ, "Ɛhɔ na ɔwɔ. " Ɛneɛ Kwaku Ananse hwee fam fii hɔ (ɛhɔ a na ɔteɛ no). Ɛnneɛ da no yɛ Efiada. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, “Me, a mehohoro Sky-god no, wo de wo nsa akyerɛ me so, ma mebɔɔ fam bɛnyaa dɔteɛ kɔkɔɔ wɔ me so. ” Ɛno ara na asomfoɔ no gyee Akwasi-the-jealous-one no nsɔmu (remains) na (wɔyɛɛ no) odwan. Bere a Akwasi-the-jealous-one no de odwan no bɔe no, ɔka kyerɛɛ Sky-god no sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ ɔbaa no, ma Kwaku Ananse nni no. " Ɛnneɛ Kwaku Ananse faa "Aso" no, nanso ne ba no deɛ, wɔkum no, twitwa no nsinnsin, na wɔpete no ho. Saa na ahoɔyaw no baa abusuakuo no mu.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom na mo betumi afa no nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a mo bɛbɔ me (wɔ asɛmka ho) no.
