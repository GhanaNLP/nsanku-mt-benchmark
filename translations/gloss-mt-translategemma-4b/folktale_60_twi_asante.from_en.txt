Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA (THE SALUTATIONS) “KURONTO” NE “BURU” NO BAE SƐNEA WƆDE DI DWUMA NO

Ɛdii da bi a, na ɔbɔfoɔ (wɔfrɛ no) Kwaku, ne yere (wɔfrɛ no Buruwa, na ɛnam sɛ na wɔn ho nni hɔ nti, wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ wɔbɛyɛ atenae foforɔ. Kwaku, ɔbɔfoɔ no, bere a ɔkɔɔ nwura no, ɔkunkum eku bi, nanso bere a ɔde no baa fie ne asase so no, ɔkae sɛ, "Eyi nni yie (ma aduane). " Ɛnneɛ na ɔkɔtɔn no. Da biara a ɔkunkum adeɛ bi, ɛte saa. Nanso, ne yere no, ɔnhunuu sika no da, na ɔbisaa no sɛ, "Woamfa sika no amma, na woamfa nam nni, na ɛhe na ɛyɛ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me kɔ a, na nnipa nntɔ nam no, na ɛyɛɛ bɔɔdɔɔ no, mebɔ no. " Ɛdii da bi a, ɔkumm adeɛ bi, na ɔde too kwan so, na ne yere no dii n'akyi kɔhwɛɛ no. Ɛneɛ na ɔhunuu sɛ ɔtɔn saa kwaadu no, faa sika no, na ɔtɔɔ nsafufuo kuku bi (a wɔfrɛ no dɔnto) na ɔnom. Ɛneɛ na ne yere no danee kɔɔ fie. Ɛnkyɛreeɛ, Kwaku a ɔyɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no nso baeɛ. Bere biara a ɔduruu fie no, ɔkae sɛ, "Anwummere pa, Buruwa. " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Ya Ekudonto (Yiw, kuku nsafufuo). " Ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wubua me wɔ kwan yi so? " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuaeɛ a ɛfata nea ɛwɔ etire no. " Ɔno ɔbɔfoɔ no anka asɛm biara. Anɔpa a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛyɛɛ nea ɛda adi no, ɔkɔɔ afuo hɔ kɔɔ so baeɛ; ɔkae sɛ, "Anwummere pa. " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Ya buru. " Buruwa no kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wubua (me nkyea) wɔ ɔkwan yi so? " Ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuae a ɛfata nea ɛwɔ etire no. " Ɛno na ɛbɛyɛɛ abusuakuo no amammrɛ sɛ wɔde nkyea ahorow "Ya kuronto" ne "Ya buru" di dwuma. Wɔn mfitiaseɛ no, ɛyɛ ɔbɔfoɔ, Kwaku, ne ne yere, Buruwa. Eyi, me abasɛm yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa kɔ baabi foforɔ, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
