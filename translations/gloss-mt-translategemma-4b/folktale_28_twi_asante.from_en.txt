Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ OKA NAM NO DUA NO YI BERE A, NA KƆBƆ NAM NO MENEWA NO YI NSENSANEE

ƐDII NNANANEM, NA ɔbaa bi te hɔ a na ɔyɛ Ɔhemmaa, na ɔnni abofra, na ɔkɔɔ ɛhe-deɛ bi a ɛwɔ hɔ no mu wɔ ahyɛdeɛ sɛ onyankopɔn mma no kwan ma ɔbɛwo abofra. Na ɔmaa no baako, nanso ɔbɔɔ no sɛ, "Maabea, ɛnsɛ sɛ abofra no di nam biara gye sɛ ɔbɛdi ɔbɔre mmerɛ, efisɛ ne honam mpo yɛ nea wɔabara no. " Mprempren, da bi, (bere a ɔbaa no bɛyɛɛ abofra no) maame no kɔɔ afuw hɔ, na ɔde ɔbɔre mmerɛ bi maa ne abofra, Ɔkra, sɛ ɔnyɛ no aduane mma ne maabea no. Sesei Ɔkra faa no, ɔnoaa aduane no, nanso ɔkɔɔ kwan so bɛfaa odwan no nam ankasa bi, na ɔde no bi ne berɛboɔ no (along with the sauce) no kɔɔ mu. Ɔhene Maame ba no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Maame kae sɛ me akyiwadeɛ no yɛ nam ankasa, na wo de bi (akyiwadeɛ) bi ahyɛ me aduane mu, na sɛ me di a, mebɛwu. " Ɔkra no buae sɛ, "Yiw, me de bi ahyɛ mu, na sɛ wobɛwu a, wuwu. " Na ɔhene Maame ba no nso buae sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ me di na me wu? " Na ɔkɔɔ dan no mu kɔfa ne maame red silk no bɛkyekyere ne ho. Na abofra no de ne ho kɔɔ kwan so, na Ɔkra dii n’akyi. Ɔkra suro sɛ sɛ ne mpena baa no hyia no a, ɛbɛma wanya ɔhaw mu. Na wɔrekɔ so no, ɔhene no ba abofra no kae sɛ, "Ɔkra! " Ɔkra kae sɛ, "Yiw! " Ɔkae sɛ, "Sɛ meka nkra bi ama mo a, mo bɛka anaa? " (i.e. deliver it). Ɔkra kae: "Me bɛka, me bɛka, ansa na me werɛ adu; me bɛka, me bɛka, ansa na me werɛ adu.

Bere a me kɔɔ hɔ kan no, na bayere bi gyina hɔ.

Sei me aba na kokote no da hɔ.

Kuruti Amoa, Asamoa, ɛno na ɔda hɔ.

Me nni Gyanoa sika fĩ, Boafo Denkye, Ɔpanin, me kɔda me nkwa nni.

Ɔse, ma wɔnkum odwan mma no, ma wɔnkum anomaa mma no.

Ɔse, ‘Okurofo, anɔpa pa.’

Ɔka sɛ, 'Ya'aku'.

Ɔse, ‘Owudifo no se ɔbɛma wo anɔpa.’

Ɔse, 'Yiw, Abrawo.

Ɔse, ‘Me nuabea, hene na worekɔ, sɛ mebɛtumi de nkra ama wo? ’

Ɔse, ‘Me kɔpanyin fie, Sɛkyerɛ Gyamase.’

Ɔse, ‘Nea ɛbaa Opapaa akuraa, Sɛkyerɛ Gyamase, akyene nna da hɔ da? Oboama agya, Agyei 'Ampɔn'; agya no kɔ ɔko, anaasɛ agya no kɔ dwadie sɛ demmire tenten no abɔ ne wuram nhama no?

Wɔn mmienu no mmra hɔ da. Ntim Sekyere ne Kwakye mmra hɔ da.'

Ɔse, ‘Oh, Parrot, Boama, woakasa akasa, gyae na ma meka kakra, w’aniwa nna nsuo so.

Wo nana, Mpampinam, kɔɔ mpataayi, na ɔtwee kɛse pa ara.

Kwao Adu Dakowa, Bantama Mpedu Okyere, Adwoa Kɛseɛ, Odum Abena ne ne fetish (suman) a wɔfrɛ no "you walk in vain".

Adwoa e, Adwoa e, Kobi asubɔnten no abarimaa, Tontoroni ya, a ɔne ewiase wɔ.' "

Na ɛnneɛ abofra no hyiaa ne maame. Na maame no kae sɛ, "Eyi, me abofra, mehyia no ha a, ɛwom sɛ megyaa no wɔ fie de, nanso dɛn (bɔne) na ɛaka no sɛ ɔretu mmirika, yiridi yiridi? " Ɔhemmaa no hyɛɛ ne nkoa a wɔdii n’akyi no sɛ wɔnkyere no mma no. Nanso wɔantumi annya no; na ɛkɔɔ so kɔɔ so, na ɔhwee asubonten no mu, na Ɔkra (the afenaa) nso hwee mu. Bere a wɔduruu wɔn nkyɛn no, na Ɔhene Maame ba no adan Oka nam a ne dua kɔkɔɔ; na Ɔkra nso, na ɔdane Ɔkɔbɔ nam a ne nnsenene gu ne afono so. Oka no dua kɔkɔɔ no, ɛne ntoma kɔkɔɔ a Ɔhene Maame ba no hyɛɛ no, ɛno na ɔhwee nsuo no mu. Ɔkɔbɔ no nsawa no nnsenene no, ɛne afenaa Ɔkra no nsonsoo.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
