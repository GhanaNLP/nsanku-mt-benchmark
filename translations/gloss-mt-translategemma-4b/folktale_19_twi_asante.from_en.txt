Yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, yɛnnyɛ nea yɛrekyerɛ, (sɛ́ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ WƆBƆƆ MMOFRA (FIRST) NO KAA NO.

WƆka sɛ (bere bi a atwam no), ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bi baeɛ, na Agya Ananse, Ananse no, ne ne yere "Aso", ne ne mmofra, Ntikuma, Nyiwankɔnfwea, Afudotwedotwe, ne Tikononkono, sii atenaeɛ ketewa bi na wɔtenaa mu. Da biara Ananse no de kɔfa aduane wuram bayere kɔ, na wɔnoa na wɔdi. Ɛnneɛ da bi, Agya Ananse kɔɔ nwura no nkyɛn; ɔhunuu sɛ aduan dɛdɛ bi na ɛgyina hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. ” Aduane no kaa sɛ, “Me din nni ‘beautiful’. ” Ananse no kaa sɛ, “Dɛn na wɔfrɛ wo? ” Ɛno na ɛbɛkaa sɛ, “Wɔfrɛ me sɛ, ‘Fill-up-some-and-eat’. ” Ananse no kaa sɛ, “Fill up some na mehu. ” Aduane no de palm-oil soup hyɛɛ mu ma, na Kwaku Ananse dii no nyinaa. Bere a ɔwiee no, ɔbisaa aduane no sɛ, “Dɛn ne mo akyiwadeɛ? ” Saa aduane no buae sɛ, “Me kɔn dɔ otuo tɛtea, na ɛtɛtea nketenkete bi. ” Ananse no gyee nkyɛnsee fie, na ɔkɔɔ kɔtoo silin no so. Ɛneɛ Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ nwura no mu kɔgyee aduane, na "Aso" nso, bere a wawie aduannoa no, ɔtoo nsa frɛɛ Kwaku Ananse, na ɔkae sɛ, “Oh, wo deɛ ɛyɛ ahiade ankasa, me deɛ, me akoraa no, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ me ne aduane yi di, wo deɛ ɛne mmofra yi deɛ ɛyɛ ade a ɛho hia ankasa; sɛ wo ho bɔ wo a, ɛneɛ me aso adwo me (nnyegyeeɛ a ɛwɔ wo kwadwom mu). ” Bere a wɔawie aduane no, ɛneɛ nwura no pa sɛe aseseɛ no akyi, na ɔkɔɔ (na ɔtenaa) silin a aduane no wɔ so. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. ” Ɛno nso buae sɛ, “Me din nni ‘beautiful’. ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Dɛn ne wo din? ” Ɛno kaa sɛ, “Me din ne sɛ, ‘Fill-up-some-and-eat’. ” Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, “Fill up some for me to see. ” Na ɛhyɛɛ prɛte bi a ɛyɛ ground-nut soup ahyɛ mu ma, na Kwaku Ananse dii. Da biara a ɔsɔre no na ɛte saa. Mprempren, Ntikuma hyɛɛ no nsow sɛ n’agya no nnyinii, nitan a ɛne sɛ wɔne no nni aduan, na Ntikuma bɔɔ n’agya no dɔn na ɔhunuu nea agya no anya nsɔmu. Bere a agya no kɔɔ nwura no so no, Ntikuma foroɔ ne ho kɔɔ soro (wɔ silin no so) na ɔhunuu aduane no. Ɔtoo n’ano frɛɛ ne maame ne n’anuanom, na wɔkɔɔ soro. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, “Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. ” Ɛkaa sɛ, “Me ne wɔn a wɔfrɛ me sɛ ‘beautiful’ no. ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛnneɛ wode bɛn na wɔfrɛ wo? ” Ɛkaa sɛ, “Me din ne ‘Fill-up-some-and-eat’. ” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Fill up a little that I may see. ” Na aduane no hyɛɛ ma sɛe a wɔde palm-oil soup no hyɛɛ mu ma. Ntikuma ne ne maame ne anuanom dii aduane no kosi sɛ ne nyinaa wiee. Na seesei Ntikuma bisaa aduane no sɛ, “Dɛn na wotuo? ” Saa aduane no kaa sɛ, “Me kɔn dɔ otuo tɛtea ne kora ketewa. ” Ntikuma kaa no kyerɛɛ Afudotwedotwe sɛ, “Monkɔfa bi mma me. ” Na ɔde kɔmaa no, na ɔde otuo tɛtea no bɛkaa no, na kora ketewa no bɛkaa (no de koraa no). Ɛneɛ wɔn nyinaa kɔɔ fam. Na Agya Ananse (mean-time) aba bio (firi nwura a ɔne wuram bayere no kɔeɛ no). "Aso" aduane no wiee wɔn. Wɔtoo nsa frɛɛ Kwaku Ananse, ɔno nso buae sɛ, "Ebia moante nea mekaeɛ no, mekaeɛ sɛ ɛfiri da a ɛdi kan no, sɛ meba fie a mewɔ aduane a, mo mma mo kwan, mo yɛ ebinom wɔ ahiadeɛ mu. " "Aso" ne ne mmofra no dii aduane no. Agya Ananse hohoroo ne ho; ɔforoɔ kɔɔ soro wɔ silin no so; ɔkaa sɛ, “Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ. ” Kommm koraa! “Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ. ” Kommm koraa! Agya Ananse kaa sɛ, “Ah! ɛsɛ sɛ ntoma yi akawnto a ɛnyɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ yi; mebɛkɔ akɔfa Ɔyokofoɔ kente no bi akɔhyɛ so. ” Na ɔforoɔ kɔɔ so kɔgyee Ɔyoko abusuakuo kente ntoma no. Ɔde ne mpaboa no hyɛɛ ne ho; ɔforoɔ kɔɔ soro (bio). Ɔkaa sɛ, “Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. ” Kommm koraa! “Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. ” Kommm koraa! Ɔkyinkyin dan no mu na ɔhunuu sɛ otuo tɛtea ne kora ketewa bi wɔ mu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, “Ɛnyɛ adeɛ baako, ɛnyɛ nneɛma mmienu (i.e. (Ɛnni akyinnyeɛ), ɛyɛ Ntikuma. ” Kwaku Ananse bɔɔ aduane no, na ɔbɛduruu aseɛ; ɔgyee Ɔyoko kente ntoma no bɛtoo hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ nwura no ho. Ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu (adeɛ bi a ɛyɛ fɛ) Mpere, abapra no, na ɛregyina hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Oh, ɛyɛ nwanwa! Saa adeɛ yi yɛ fɛ sen deɛ ɛtwa toɔ no; abapra yi yɛ fɛ. ” Saa abapra no kaa sɛ, “Me nni a wɔfrɛ me sɛ ‘beautiful’. ” Ɔno (Ananse) kaa sɛ, “Ɛnneɛ wodeɛn na wɔfrɛ wo? ” Ɛkaa sɛ, “Me nni a wɔfrɛ me sɛ ‘Abiridibrada’ (ɛne sɛ, Swish-and-raise-weals). ” Na ɔkaa sɛ, “Swish kakra mma mehu. ” Na abapra no bɛtoo no so sɛ, “Swish, swish, swish. ” Agya Ananse suu sɛ, “Pui! “pui! ” anomaa bi (sitting near by) ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, “Say Adwobere” (which means, cool-and-easy-now). Na Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, “Adwobere”. Na abapra no gyaee sɛ wɔrebɔ no. Na Kwaku Ananse de saa abapra yi baa fie; na ɔne no kɔɔ silin so. "Aso" no wiee (cooking) aduane no, ɔkae sɛ, “Kwaku Ananse, ba bɛdi. ” Ɔno nso buae sɛ, “Sɛ wo da so wɔ asase so a, ebia ɛtokuro biara nni w’aso (na woante nea mekae no), me nni. ” Kwaku Ananse foro " Ntikuma kae sɛ, "Ena, Nyiwankonfwea, Afudotwedotwe, momma yɛn ba ha, nea agya yi de aba (saa berɛ yi) sen nea etwa to no koraa. " Ɛneɛ wɔn nyinaa foroɔ soro (wɔ silin no so). Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Saa adeɛ yi yɛ fɛ. " Ɛno nso buae sɛ, "Me ne wɔn a wɔfrɛ me 'beautiful'. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn ne wo din? " Ɛno nso kaa sɛ, "Me din ne 'Swish-and-raise-weals'. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Swish me kakra ma mehu. " Na saa abapra no bɛduruu ne so kɔɔ no so kɛseɛ. Kwaku Ananse gyinaa nkyɛn, na ɔteɛm sɛ, "Lay it on, lay it on, especially on Ntikuma, lay it on him. " Sesei berɛ a Kwaku Ananse ahwɛ na wahunu sɛ wɔayɛ no flogging yiye no, ɔkae sɛ, "Adwobere" (Cool-and-easy-now). Kwaku Ananse bɛgyee abapra no na ɔtwaa no nketenkete akyekyɛɛ ho. Ɛno na ɛmaa abapra no baa abusuakuo no mu. Ɛnneɛ na ɛba sɛ, bere a woka wo abofra asɛm bi, na ɔremmɛtie wo, na yɛbɔ no.

Eyi, me abasɛm a mekae no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi nso reba me nkyɛn.
