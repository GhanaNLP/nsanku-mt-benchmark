Yɛmfa no aniberesɛm, yɛnka no aniberesɛm, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

KOKOKYINAKA, TURACOU a ne ti yɛ bruu no, WANKƆ ANANSE, ƆKYERƐKYERƐ no hɔ sɛ ɔmmoa no

BERE bi a atwam no, Kokokyinaka anomaa no kɔɔ Ananse, a ɔyɛ Spider no nkyɛn kɔpɛɛ sika mfutuma a emu duru yɛ asuanu (£4) sɛ ɔde bɛkyɛ no. Afei, esiane sɛ na Ananse ankasa nni bi nti, ɔkɔɔ ne yɔnko Ntontom, Mosquito, hɔ sɛ ɔrekɔfa bi akɔma n'adamfo Kokokyinaka anomaa no. Da a Mosquito ahyɛ sɛ ɔbɛtua bosea no twaam, na Mosquito ka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔfa me sika mfuturo no ma me". Ananse buae sɛ, "Kokokyinaka nnomaa no te Silk-cotton dua no atifi, enti kɔfa wo sika mfuturo no". Mosquito bisae sɛ, "Mɛyɛ dɛn anya; aboa no betumi atu?" Spider buae sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔfa dua no, na sɛ wuhu sɛ dua no bɛpae a, wo nsa bɛka Kokokyinaka nnomaa no, na wo nsa aka wo sika mfuturo no". Mosquito kɔfa dua no. Bere a na ɛrebɛbɔ no, Kokokyinaka anomaa no fii hɔ, pa bu bu bu! Afei Mosquito bae, na ɔkae sɛ, "Ananse, mepa wo kyɛw". Ɛno nti na yɛwɔ asɛm bi a ɛka sɛ, "Abɔ a wɔfrɛ no Kokokyinaka anomaa no amfa Ananse asɛm no amma Mosquito sɛ ɔnkɔ nkogyina Silk-cotton-tree no so". Eyi, m'asɛm a maka no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
