Yɛmfa no aniberesɛm, yɛnka no aniberesɛm, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

KOKOKYINAKA, TURACOU a ne ti yɛ bruu no, Wankɔ Ananse a ɔyɛ nwansena no hɔ sɛ ɔrekɔbɔ no bosea

BERE bi a atwam no, Kokokyinaka anomaa no kɔɔ Ananse, a ɔyɛ Spider no nkyɛn kɔpɛɛ sika mfutuma a emu duru yɛ asuanu (£4) sɛ ɔde bɛkyɛ no. Afei, esiane sɛ na Ananse ankasa nni bi nti, ɔkɔɔ ne yɔnko Ntontom, Mosquito, hɔ sɛ ɔrekɔfa bi akɔma n'adamfo Kokokyinaka anomaa no. Da a Mosquito ahyɛ sɛ ɔbɛtua bosea no twaam, na Mosquito ka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔfa me sika mfuturo no ma me". Ananse buae sɛ, "Kokokyinaka anomaa no te saa Silk-cotton dua no atifi, enti kɔ na kɔfa wo sika mfuturo no". Mosquito bisae sɛ, "Mɛyɛ dɛn anya bi; aboa no betumi atu?" Na Spider buae sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔforo dua no, na sɛ wuhu , dua no bɛpae, na afei woakyere Kokokyinaka anomaa no, na wo nsa aka wo sika mfutuma no. Mosquito no kɔe na ɔhwee dua no so. Bere a ɛrebɛpae no, Kokokyinaka anomaa no fii hɔ, pa bu bu bu! Afei Mosquito bae, na ɔkae sɛ, "Ananse, ma w'ani nnye". Ɛno nti na yɛwɔ asɛm bi a ɛka sɛ, "Abɔde a wɔfrɛ no Kokokyinaka anomaa no amfa Ananse asɛm no amfiri Mosquito hɔ sɛ ɔnkɔ nkogyina Silk-cotton-tree no so". Eyi ne m'asɛm a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
