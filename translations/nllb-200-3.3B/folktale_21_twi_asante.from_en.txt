SƐN NA OWEA (TREE-BEAR), ABOSOM-AKOTERE (CHAMELEON), NE OPATUO (OWL) BƐYƐƐ N'ANIGYE

OWEA, Dua-osisi, (a ɔwɔ din nso sɛ) Kwaduampon Kyerefo, Opatuo, Owl, ne Abosom-Akotere, Kameleon, (ne din ahorow yɛ), Ɔbɔadeɛ no wu, Abɔde a-ɔfa-afe-yɛ-ade-biara, Ɔ-nantew-ɔ-nantew-ɔ-nantew-ɔ-nantew-nantew-nanso-ɔ-bɛ-duru-ahotefo-asase-a-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ-wɔ- Da koro, bere a na ɛyɛ Ɔbɔadeɛ-Owuo (sɛ ɔbɛhyɛ Adobe's nnwuma so) na ɔhwehwɛɛ Adobe-dua no, ɛhe na ɔhuu no? Na ɔfirii hɔ kɔkaee Tree-bear ne Owl no. Na wɔkae sɛ, Kɔ na kɔfrɛ no. Na Ɔbɔadeɛ-Owuo maa ne nne so sɛ: Adobe o! Adobe o!

Dua-ɔsoro no teɛɛm sɛ:

Bere a ɔkɔe no, ɔsan bae bio; ɔkɔe no, ɔsan bae bio, ɔsan bae bio, ɔsan bae bio!

Na Owusu no frɛɛ sɛ:

Yɛaboro ( sika mfutuma no) na anka yɛatow akyene, m!

Na ɛno ne abɔde abiɛsa yi su no fibea.

M'asɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
