Yɛmfa no aniberesɛm, yɛnka no aniberesɛm, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐN NA OWEA (TREE-BEAR), ABOSOM-AKOTERE (CHAMELEON), NE OPATUO (OWL) BƐYƐƐ N'ANIGYE

OWEA, Dua-ɔso, (a ɔwɔ din nso sɛ) Kwaduampon Kyerefo, Opatuo, Owl, ne Abosom-Akotere, Kameleon, (ne din ne), Ɔbɔadeɛ no wu, Abɔde a-ɔfa-afe-a-bɛyɛ-biribiara, Ɔ-nantew-ɔ-brɛ-so-nanso-ɔ-bɛ-duru-aborɔfo-asase-a-wɔ-nkorɔn-kyɛn-nkorɔn, (mmoa yi nyinaa) traa ase bere bi. Afei, wɔn mu biara nni ɔyere. Na Adobe-dua no tɔn ne ho maa wɔn. Da biara, sɛ Adobe-dua no sɔre a, na ɔkɔhwɛ saa dua yi; n'adekyee so no na ɔhwɛ wɔn mu biako so; na n'adekyee so no nso ɔhwɛ wɔn mu biako so. Da koro bi, bere a na Ɔbɔadeɛ no wu (sɛ ɔhyɛ Adobe's nnwuma so) na ɔhwehwɛɛ Adobe-dua no so no, ɛhe na na obetumi ahu no? Na ɔfirii hɔ kɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ Tree-bear ne Owl. Na wɔkae sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔfrɛ no". Na Ɔbɔadeɛ-o-wu no maa ne nne so sɛ: "Adobe o! Adobe o!"

Dua-ɔsoro no teɛɛm sɛ:

Bere a ɔkɔe no, ɔsan bae bio; ɔkɔe no, ɔsan bae bio, ɔsan bae bio, ɔsan bae bio!

Na Owusu no frɛɛ sɛ:

Yɛaboro ( sika mfutuma no) na anka yɛatow akyene, m!

Na ɛno ne abɔde abiɛsa yi su no fibea.

M'asɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
