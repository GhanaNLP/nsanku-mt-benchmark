Yɛmfa no aniberesɛm, yɛnka no aniberesɛm, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Ananse, Nkwansan no, kae sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ ɔkwasea, bere a bere nyinaa na ɔno ankasa yɛ ɔkwasea

ANANSE, a ɔyɛ Spider no, traa hɔ bere bi, na ɔkae sɛ ɔpɛ ɔkwasea bi a ɔne no bɛkɔ akɔto mpataa afiri. Otuu mporoporo, nanso wanhu bi. Afei ohyiaa Osansa, a ɔyɛ Hawk no. Ɔkaa sɛ, Momma yɛnkɔ na yɛnkɔto mpataa afiri. Ɔtweaseɛ no buaa sɛ, Mewɔ (honam) pii a mɛdi. Ɛno akyi no, ɔhyiaa Anene, a ɔyɛ Ɔtweaseɛ no, na ɔkaa sɛ, Momma yɛnkɔto mpataa afiri. Ɔtweaseɛ no kaa sɛ, Mate. Afei, Ɔtweaseɛ no ka kyerɛɛ Ɔtweaseɛ no sɛ, Ɛfa mpataa afiri a wo ne Ananse rekɔto yi ho no, nkɔ, ɛfiri sɛ Ananse se ɔrehwehwɛ ɔkwasea bi, sɛ ɔne ɔkwasea no de mpataa afiri si hɔ a, obenya sika no nyinaa. Ɔtweaseɛ no buaa sɛ, Wonnka hwee bio, ɛfiri sɛ mɛhwɛ sɛ menya sika no nyinaa. Ɔtweaseɛ no ne Spider no fii hɔ kɔɔ kwae no mu sɛ wɔrekɔtwa mmɛdua mman. Kraman no ka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, 'Fa sekan no ma me na menkɔ na menkɔtwa (wɔn), na w'adwuma ne sɛ wobɛfa ɔbrɛ (a efi m'adwuma mu ba) no. Nkorͻfo no buae sɛ, 'Wofa me sɛ ɔkwasea; ma mentwa, bere a wobɛfa emu ɔbrɛ no.' Ananse na ɔretwa nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn no bere a Anene, Kraman no, na ɔte hɔ a ɔresu. Bere a otwaa no wiei no, wɔkyekyeree no. Kraman no kae sɛ, 'Kekaboa, ma me so na mensoa, bere a wugye emu ɔbrɛ no.' Nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn no buae sɛ, 'Sɔre, wofa me sɛ ɔkwasea?' Na ɔfaa no de guu ne ti so. Akoekoe no dii n'akyi, na na ɔresu. Bere a wɔduruu fie no, Akoekoe no kae sɛ, "Momma menyɛ mpataa afiri no na monkɔfa me brɛ no". Ananse buae sɛ, "Dabi, momma menyɛ no na monkɔfa brɛ no". Ɛno akyi no Akoekoe no kɔtenaa n'akyi, na na ɔresu. Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɔkwasea, woka sɛ wowɔ adwene, nanso worewu". Akoekoe no wiee mpataa afiri no. Akoekoe no kae sɛ: Ma mensoa mpataa afiri no mfa nkɔ nsu no mu, na wo de, w'abrɛ no. Akoekoe no kae sɛ: Dabi, me ne wɔn bɛkɔ, na wo de, w'abrɛ no. Wɔduruu nsu no mu no, Akoekoe no kae sɛ: Agya Akoekoe, aboa bi wɔ nsu no mu, enti ma minnyina nsu no mu na memfa mpataa afiri no, na sɛ aboa no ka me a, wo de, wuwu no. Akoekoe no kae sɛ: O, wofa me sɛ meyɛ ɔkwasea; me ara mede afiri no bɛto hɔ, na sɛ ɔka me a, wo de, wu wu no. Akoekoe no faa mpataa afiri no de guu nsu no mu, na wɔbae. Wɔsan kɔɔ fie. Ade kyee no, bere a nneɛma mu daa hɔ no, wɔkɔɔ sɛ wɔrekɔhwɛ afiri no, na wɔkyeree mpataa abien. Akoa no kae sɛ: "Akorɔmfo, fa eyinom abien yi, na sɛ yɛba ɔkyena na wɔakyere anan a, me nso mɛfa wɔn". Akoa no kae sɛ: "Eyi ne nea wofa me sɛ meyɛ ɔkwasea; fa eyinom abien yi, na ɔkyena me nso mɛfa anan no". Akoa no de wɔn kɔe. Ade kyee no, wɔsan kɔhwɛɛ afiri no, na wohuu mpataa anan. Akoekoe no kae sɛ: "Momfa anan yi, na sɛ yɛba ɔkyena na yɛkyere awotwe a, me nso mɛfa wɔn". Akoekoe no kae sɛ: "Wobɛdaadaa me; mowɔ anan yi, na ɔkyena me nso mɛfa awotwe no". Akoekoe se, "Fa, na ɔkyena me nso mɛfa mpem dunsia no". Akoekoe se, "Wofa me nkwaseafo; wofa awotwe no, na ɔkyena mɛfa mpem dunsia no. Seesei mpem dunsia no ayera, na Akoekoe ka kyerɛɛ Akoekoe se, Kɔ kɔfa mpem dunsia a aporɔw yi, na sɛ obi tɔn wɔn a, obenya ntanu (i.e. (£16) ma (trap) biara, saa ara na wo, Agya Spider, fa mpataa yi, na fa mpataa a aporɔw no na fa ma me. Spider kae sɛ, Dabi, wo de mpataa no kɔtɔn, na mɛfa mpataa a aporɔw no na magye sika no. Wɔde wɔn nneɛma no kɔɔ gua so. Ɛhɔ ara na akokɔ bɔ ne nneɛma nyinaa gui; ne nsa kaa sika mfutuma pii. Nanso na mpataa no na aka. Afei Kraman no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Fa mpataa afiri a aporɔ yi na kɔbɔ wɔn ho mmaa na wɔatɔ. Akorɔmfo no maa wɔn so; ɔkae sɛ, Merekɔtɔn mpataa afiri a aporɔ! Afei akuraa no mu panyin no tee no, na ɔkae sɛ, Ɛhe na saa nipa yi fi bae; kɔfrɛ no ma ɔmmra. Akorɔmfo no kɔe. Akuraa no mu panyin no kae sɛ, Wususuw sɛ yɛn a yɛwɔ akuraa no mu ha no yɛ nkwasea; afei w'adamfo (a ɔwɔ ha yi) bɛtɔn mpataa (wɔ mfaso so), woanhu no, nanso wobɛtɔn mpataa afiri a aporɔ yi ama yɛn? Akuraa no mu panyin no kae sɛ, Hwe no. Enti wɔboroo no. Afei Ɔtweaseɛ no kaa sɛ, "Ɔtweaseɛ, woahunu, w'ani awu wɔ aniwu ho; wokae sɛ worekɔhwehwɛ ɔkwasea a ɔne wo bɛyɛ adwuma, na afei woahunu sɛ wo ara na woyɛ ɔkwasea no".

M'asɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
