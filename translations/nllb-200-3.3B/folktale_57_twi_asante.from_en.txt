Yɛmfa no aniberesɛm, yɛnka no aniberesɛm, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

CHILD-KWASI-GYINAMOA

BERE bi na abofra bi a wɔfrɛ no Kwasi Gyinamoa wɔ hɔ, na na ɔwɔ nuabeanom baawɔtwe, na na wɔn mu biara nni ɔhokafo, enti wotuu kwan kae sɛ wɔrekɔ baabi akɔhwehwɛ okununom. Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, Me ne wo bɛkɔ. Wobuaa sɛ, Yɛn ne wo renkɔ. Wɔn mu panin no kaa sɛ, Ma ɔne yɛn nkɔ, na sɛ yɛduru kwan no so na nsɔe bi hyɛ yɛn mu a, ɔbɛtwe ama yɛn. Wɔfirii hɔ, na Kwasi-Gyinamoa ne wɔn kɔe. Bere a woduu asubɔnten bi ho no, na emu bi te sɛ dɔte fitaa, na emu bi nso te sɛ dɔte kɔkɔɔ, na emu bi nso te sɛ gyabiriw tuntum. Wɔkae sɛ: "Dɛn na yɛbɛyɛ na yɛatumi atwa asubɔnten yi?" Wɔduruu kuropɔn bi mu; na ɛyɛ kuropɔn a ɛso paa. Mmofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, Kurow no yɛ dinn (a ne nkyerɛase ne sɛ, ɛhɔ yɛ dinn, gye sɛ akekantwɛre wɔ hɔ), na onipa biara nni mu anaa? Afei, Anomaa bi kasae sɛ, Obi wɔ fie hɔ, kɔ so. Wɔkɔɔ so, na ɔbea akwakoraa bi wɔ hɔ, na ne ti da ne nan so, na na ɔrehwehwɛ ntontom wɔ mu. Bere a ɔhunuu Akokoaa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, ɔpagyaa ne ti na ɔsan no; ɔkae sɛ, Me mmanana, adɛn nti na, sɛ obiara mma ha a, moaba ha? Akokoaa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, Me nuabeanom yɛ awotwe, na wonni okununom a wɔne wɔn bɛware, enti wɔkaa sɛ wɔbɛba abɛhwehwɛ okununom a wɔne wɔn bɛware. Ɔpanyin-aberewa nanabea kae sɛ, Oh, saa na ɛteɛ Na me nso mewɔ mma baawɔtwe. Ɔpanyin-nananom soma ne banana sɛ ɔnkɔfrɛ ne mma. Bere a wɔbae no, obiako kae sɛ, Me kunu ni, ɔfoforo nso kae sɛ, Me kunu ni. Bere a wɔn nyinaa wiee wɔn ho a wɔakeka no, afei ɔpanin no kɔfaa ahyɛnsode bi a wɔfrɛ no _dokum_, na ɔde kyekyeree ne mma no kɔn ho. Afei wɔn mu biara kɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn kɔdae. Na Ɔba-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔdaa ɔbaa panyin no nkyɛn. Na berɛ a anadwo duruu no, ɔbaa panyin no hyerɛn ne se, kɔkɔɔ kɔkɔɔ, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔne wɔn bɛkɔ akɔkum mmabaa no. Ɔba-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, E! e! e! Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ, Ɛyɛ dɛn? Mmofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, Sɛ mewɔ m'ankasa akuraa mu, na sɛ mekɔhome na mintumi nna a, afei maame twitwa yam bi na ɔde gu gya no mu, na ɔfa kuruwa bi a wɔabobɔ mu kɔsɔ nsu de brɛ me ma mennom. Ɔbea panyin-nananom sii kwan so sɛ wɔrekɔ asubɔnten no ho, na Mmofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kɔe na ɔde nkonyaayi bi a wɔfrɛ no _dok um_ fii akwakoraa no mma kɔn mu, na ɔfaa wɔn de kyekyeree ne nuabeanom so. Ɔbea panyin no fi nsu no mu bae; ɔkae sɛ, Mɔɔ mmɔden sɛ mɛteɛ nsu (wɔ saa ahina no mu), nanso manteɛ. Mmofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, Kɔ na kɔfa dɔte bi, na yi nhaban na fa hyɛ (ahina no mu), na fa na teɛ. Ɔbea panyin no kɔe na ɔyɛɛ saa, na ɔteeɛ nsu wɔ mu, na na ne ho ye. Ɔde brɛɛ no, na oyii yam no fii gya no so, na ɔde maa Ɔba-Kwasi-Gyinamoa dii, na ɔno nso nom nsu no bi. Afei ɔtow ne ho hwee fam na ɔdae. Ɔbea-kwakoraa-nananom sɔre; ɔkae sɛ, Ɛyɛ me. Ɔhyerɛn ne se, (kosii sɛ ɛhyerɛn) kɔkɔɔ, na ɔne wɔn kɔe kɔhyɛn dan no mu; ɔde ne nsa hwɛɛ mmofra no kɔn ho, na nea _dokum_ nkonyaayi no ntra ne kɔn so no, ɔde ne se hyew no. Saa kwan yi so na okunkum mmofra baawɔtwe no. Ná onnim sɛ ɔno ankasa mma na wakunkum wɔn. Bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ankyɛ koraa, ankyɛ koraa na Ɔpanyin-aberewa-nananom sii mu sɛ wɔrekɔ afuw no mu. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔe kɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuabeanom ma woguanee. Ɔpanyin-aberewa-aberewa no fi afuw no mu bae. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, "Opanyin-aberewa e! Ɔpanyin-aberewa e! sɛ ɛnyɛ nkwaseafo a anka wobekum wo ankasa wo mma? Me de, me ne me nuabeanom rekɔ. Ɔpanyin-baa-nananom kaa sɛ, Ɛho nhia, nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi no) wɔ hɔ. Ɔkɔe, na Ɔpanyin-baa-nananom nso sesaa ne ho yɛɛ ababaa fɛfɛ, na ɔsoaa akyekyere, na ɔkɔe kɔɔ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoas akuraa ase. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Obiara a ɔbɛtumi afa agyan de ato yi so no, mɛware no". Nnipa a na wɔwɔ kuro no mu nyinaa bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ wɔbɛto no, nanso wɔantumi. Abofra Kwasi-Gyinamoa too no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Maware wo". Abofra Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, merennye wo". Enti ne wɔfa waree no. Ɔne no kɔdaeɛ, nanso ɔyii n'ani mmienu no, na ɔne wɔn kɔeɛ. Afei abaayewa no kaa sɛ, "Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa wɔ hɔ! Meyii w'adamfo no aniwa abien no, na mede wɔn rekɔ". Ɔbabarima no kae sɛ: "Fa wɔn kɔ, na nea onim sɛnea ɔne wo bedi no wɔ hɔ". Na ade kyee no, bere a nneɛma mu daa hɔ maa no no, wɔkae sɛ: "Me ba, kɔ na kɔsa w'adamfo no aniwa". Afei, Ɔba-Kwasi-Gyinamoa huu sɛ Ɔbaa-kwakoraa-nananom nanabea no yɛ _enceinte_, enti ɔno nso faa atoko de bɔɔ ne yafunu so, na ɔkyekyeree ne ntama guu so, na ofii adi kɔɔ Ɔbaa-kwakoraa-nananom akuraa no ase. Bere a oduu kurow no ano no, osui. Ɔbea-kwakoraa nanabea kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛrekɔ so?" Ɔkae sɛ, "Na me ne me kunu reba, na bere a oduu bepɔw no atifi no, ɔhwee ase na ɛno maa n'ani abien no puee na ɛhwee ase". Ɔbea-kwakoraa nanabea kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ no yiye, eyi ne abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa wɔfa a wɔsan woo no no ani benkum, na eyi nso ne ani nifa. Sɛ wokɔ (baabi a wo kunu anifuraefo no gyina hɔ) a, ɛsɛ sɛ woka sɛ, 'M'ani bɔ gu baabiara, m'ani da hɔ.' " Ansa na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nsa aka wɔn no, ɔkae sɛ, "Wo a wowɔ hɔ, Otumfoɔ-aberewa, eyinom yɛ me wɔfa aniwa a woyii no, na me ne wɔn rekɔ". Aberewa-aberewa no kae sɛ, "Ɛmfa ho, nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi no) no wɔ hɔ". Abofra-Kwasi. -Gyinamoa baa fie, na ɔfaa aniwa no na ɔde hyɛɛ ne wɔfa so no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me aniwa no bɔ ne ho so, me aniwa no mu da hɔ", na ne wɔfa no aniwa no mu daa hɔ. Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa hɔ no, mmofra no nyinaa (wɔ akuraa no mu) kɔɔ asuten no mu kɔhohoroo wɔn ho. Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ: "Merenhohoro me ho". Wɔkae sɛ: "Ebetumi aba sɛ wowɔ ɔyafunu dua bi?" Bere a wɔkae saa no, ne koko yɛɛ den, na ɔhohoroo ne ho. Na ɛnkyɛe koraa na otuu mmirika kɔɔ nsu no mu no, na nanabaa akwakoraa no kyeree no, na na wɔn baanu yɛ baanu. Ɔkae sɛ: "Kwasi-Gyinamoa, manya wo", na ɔne no kɔɔ ne kurom. Afei Ɔbaa-Nkwakoraa no kɔfaa kɛntɛn bi kyekyeree no so, na ɔfaa borɔdɔma, na ɔde too gya mu. Ɔkae sɛ, "Merekɔ onion abusuakuw no mu, merekɔ spice abusuakuw no mu, na afei mɛba abekum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa". Ɔfirii hɔ, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa boaboaa aboɔ ano na ɔhyɛɛ ase sɛee no, pupru! pupru! Ɔbea panyin no nena bisaa no sɛ: "Dɛn na woredi?" Obuae sɛ: "Ntiaa". Obuae sɛ: "Ma me bi nni". Obuae sɛ: "Bue m'atade". Obuae sɛ: "Wobɛkɔ". Obuae sɛ: "Dabi, merennkɔ". Obuee n'atade, na ofii adi; na ɔbaa panyin no nena rebɔ ntɛtɛwa. Ɔba-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa aburokyire-nsuo no, bɔɔ Ɔbaa-kwakoraa no nanabaa, na ɔkumm no. Afei ɔworɔɔ Ɔbaa-kwakoraa no nanabaa no honam, na ɔhyɛɛ no. Ɔbea panyin no san bae; ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na mo nyinaa ho ntew saa?" Obuae sɛ, "Makunkum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa; mede no ahyɛ ogya mu". Saa asɛm yi yɛɛ anigye maa Ɔbea panyin no, na ɔtwee asaw, na ɔmaa ne nne so (na ɔtoo dwom sɛ): "Ɔba, woakum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, Da bi wobɛyɛ obi".

Owiee aduan no ano a, wɔtraa ase, na wodii. Ɔpanyin-baa-nananom no hwiee sopa no guu n'anom (ɔteɛɛm denneennen), fweo! fweo! Kakula-Kwasi-Gyinamoa hanle kɛ, "Ɛfa ɛ ra nrenya ne ati a ɛyɛ wɔ sopa ne a". Nrenya-nrenya-nrenya-nrenya ne hanle kɛ, "Duzu a ɛsuzu nwo a?" ɔbuale kɛ, "Menganle kɛ nzule ɛnle sopa ɛhye anu". Nrenya-nrenya-nrenya ne hanle kɛ, "Kɔkpondɛ bie". Yemɔti ɔhɔkpondɛle bie. Bere a wɔdidi wiei no, Nanabea-kwakoraa no kae sɛ, "Seesei a makum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa yi, bra ha, m'abakan". Bere a ɔbae no, ɔfaa sikakɔkɔɔ nnwinnade na ɔhyehyɛɛ no fɛfɛɛfɛ. Afei, Nanabea-kwakoraa no hwɛɛ n'anim kakra. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ah! abɔfra yi anim ma mekae abɔfra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no". Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Nanabaa, adɛn, bere a wonim sɛ abɔfra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa bae na ɔkumm m'abusuafoɔ baawɔtwe no nyinaa (woka saa), na woama me werɛ aho". Aberewa-nanana no kaa sɛ, "Kata w'ano". Afei abɔfra no maa ne ho tɔɔ no wɔ fie hɔ. Nanabaa no yii ne nyinaa guu hɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Ma menkɔ na menkɔtow ngu ɔbo no mfinimfini". Enti abofra no soaa no kɔe, na bere a oduu ɔbo no mfinimfini no, oyii sika kɔkɔɔ ahosiesie a na ɔbea no de ahyehyɛ ne ho no fii ne ho na ɔkyekyeree. Afei ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Dabi, Ɔpanyin-Nanabea, dabi, Ɔpanyin-Nanabea, mene no, Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, ɛnnɛ ne mprenu so, maba sɛ merebɛkum wo babarima ba no, na afei nso mafa wo sika kɔkɔɔ no". Aberewa-Nanabea no buae sɛ, "Mekae sɛ w'anim te sɛ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa de; afei baabiara a wobɛkɔ no, me ne wo bɛkɔ". Na wɔn baanu nyinaa guanee, kiri! kiri! kiri! Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa duu ne akuraa no ase, na ɔde sika kɔkɔɔ no maa ne mogya-abusuafo, na ɔkɔɔ ɔkwan no so, na ɔde ne ho siei wɔ nkyɛn mu, na Ɔpanyin-baa-nananom nso baa hɔ. Ɛno nti, sɛ wonam ɔkwan no so a, wobɛte sɛ ɛnne bi rebɔ, "pe". Ɛno ne Ɔpanyin-baa-nananom a wɔtaa Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa.

M'asɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
