Yɛmfa no aniberesɛm, yɛnka no aniberesɛm, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

CHILD-KWASI-GYINAMOA

BERE bi, na abofra bi wɔ hɔ a wɔfrɛ no Kwasi Gyinamoa, na na ɔwɔ nuabeanom baawɔtwe, na wɔn mu biara nni ɔhokafo, enti wotuu kwan kae sɛ wɔrekɔ baabi akɔhwehwɛ okununom. Ɔba no kae sɛ, "Me ne wo bɛkɔ". Wɔkae sɛ, "Yɛn ne wo renkɔ". Wɔn mu panyin no kae sɛ, "Ma ɔne yɛn nkɔ, na sɛ yɛkɔ ɔkwan no so na nsɔe bi ka yɛn a, obetumi atutu ama yɛn". Bere a woduu asubɔnten bi ho no, na emu bi te sɛ dɔte fitaa, na emu bi te sɛ dɔte kɔkɔɔ, na emu bi nso te sɛ gyabiriw. Wɔkae sɛ: "Dɛn na yɛbɛyɛ na yɛatumi atwa asubɔnten yi?" Ɔkasae sɛ, "Obi wɔ fie hɔ, monkɔ so". Wɔkɔ so, na ɔbea panyin bi wɔ hɔ, na ne ti da ne nan so, na na ɔrehwehwɛ ntontom wɔ mu. Bere a ɔhunuu Akokoaa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, ɔpagyaa ne ti na ɔsan no; ɔsee sɛ, Me mmanana, adɛn nti na, sɛ obiara mma ha a, moaba ha? Akokoaa-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, Me nuabeanom yɛ awotwe, na wɔnni okununom a wɔne wɔn bɛware, enti wɔkaa sɛ wɔbɛba abɛhwehwɛ okununom a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔware wɔn. Akwakoraa-nananom kaa sɛ, Oh, ɛno yɛ papa, ɛfiri sɛ, me nso, mewɔ mma awotwe. Akwakoraa-nananom somaa ne banana sɛ ɔnkɔfrɛ ne mma. Bere a wɔbae no, baako kae sɛ, "Me kunu ni", na ɔfoforo nso kae sɛ, "Me kunu ni". Na wɔn nyinaa wiee wɔn ho a wɔkekae no, afei ɔbaa panyin no kɔfaa nkonyaayi bi a wɔfrɛ no _dokum_, na ɔde kyekyeree ne mma no kɔn ho. Na wɔn mu biara kɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn kɔdae. Na Ɔba Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔɔ ɔbaa panyin no nkyɛn kɔdaa hɔ. Bere a ade sae no, ɔbaa panyin no hyerɛn ne se, kɔkɔɔ kɔkɔɔ, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔne wɔn bɛkɔ akokum mmabaa no. Ɔba Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, "E! e! Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ, Ɛyɛ dɛn? Mmofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, Sɛ mewɔ m'ankasa akuraa mu, na sɛ mekɔhome na mintumi nna a, afei maame twitwa yam bi na ɔde gu gya no mu, na ɔfa kuruwa bi a wɔabobɔ mu kɔsɔ nsu de brɛ me ma mennom. Ɔbea panyin nanabea sii kwan so sɛ ɔrekɔ asuten no ho, na Mmofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kɔtwee nkonyaayi a wɔfrɛ no _dokum_ no fii ɔbea panyin no mma kɔn mu, na ɔfaa no de kyekyeree ne nuabeanom no so. Ɔbea panyin no fi nsu no mu bae; ɔkae sɛ, Mebɔɔ mmɔden sɛ mɛbɔ nsu (wɔ saa ahina no mu), nanso manhu. Mmofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, Kɔ na kɔfa dɔte bi, na yi nhaban na fa hyɛ (ahina no mu), na fa na bɔ nsu. Ɔbea panyin no kɔe na ɔyɛɛ saa, na ohwiee nsu guu mu, na na ne ho ye. Ɔde bae, na oyii yam no fii gya no mu, na ɔde maa Mmofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa dii, na ɔno nso nom nsu no bi. Afei ɔtow ne ho hwee fam na ɔdae. Ɔbea-kwakoraa no sɔre; ɔkae sɛ, Ɛyɛ me. Ɔmaa ne se hyerɛn, (kosii sɛ ɛhyerɛn) kɔkɔɔ, na ɔne wɔn kɔe kɔhyɛn dan no mu; ɔde ne nsa hwɛɛ mmofra no kɔn ho, na nea _dokum_ nkonyaayi no nni ne kɔn so no, ɔde ne se hyew no. Saa kwan yi so na okunkum mmofra baawɔtwe no. Ná onnim sɛ ɔno ankasa mma na wakunkum wɔn. Bere a nneɛma daa adi no, ankyɛ koraa na Ɔbea-kwakoraa no fii ase kɔɔ afuw no mu. Ɔba-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔe kɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuabeanom no ma woguanee. Ɔpanyin-baa-nananom fi afuw no mu bae. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, Opanyin-baa-nanom e! Ɔpanyin-baa-nanom e! sɛ wonyɛ ɔkwasea a, anka worekum wo ankasa mma? Me deɛ, me ne me nuabeanom rekɔ. Ɔpanyin-baa-nanom kae sɛ, Ɛho nhia, nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi no) wɔ hɔ. Ɔkɔe, na Ɔpanyin-baa-nanom nso sesaa ne ho yɛɛ ababaa fɛfɛ, na ɔsoo akyekyere, na ɔkɔɔ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoas akuraa no ase. Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "Obiara a ɔbɛtumi de agyan ato yi so no, mɛware no". Nnipa a wɔwɔ kuro no mu no nyinaa bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ wɔbɛtu no, nanso wɔantumi. Abofra Kwasi-Gyinamoa tu no. Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "Maware wo". Abofra Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, merenware wo". Enti ne papa nua no waree no. Ɔne no kɔdaeɛ, nanso ɔyii n'ani mmienu no, na ɔne wɔn kɔeɛ. Afei ababaa no kaa sɛ, "Abofra Kwasi-Gyinamoa wɔ hɔ. Meyii w'adamfo no aniwa abien no, na mede wɔn rekɔ". Ɔbaabun no kae sɛ: "Fa wɔn kɔ, na nea onim sɛnea ɔne wo bedi no wɔ hɔ". Na ade kyee no, bere a nneɛma no daa hɔ no, wɔkae sɛ: "Ɔbaabun, kɔ na kɔsa w'adamfo no aniwa". Afei, Ɔba-Kwasi-Gyinamoa huu sɛ Ɔbaa-kwakoraa-nananom nanabea no yɛ _enceinte_, enti ɔno nso faa atoko de bɔɔ ne yafunu so, na ɔkyekyeree n'atade guu so, na otuu kwan kɔɔ Ɔbaa-kwakoraa-nananom akuraa no ase. Bere a oduu kurow no ano no, osui. Ɔbea-kwakoraa nanabea kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛrekɔ so?" Ɔkae sɛ, "Na me ne me kunu reba, na bere a oduu bepɔw no atifi no, ɔhwee ase na ɛno maa n'ani abien no puee na ɛhwee ase". Ɔbea-kwakoraa nanabea kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ no yiye, eyi ne abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, onua a wabɔ abira no ani benkum, na eyi nso ne ani nifa. Sɛ wokɔ (baabi a wo kunu anifuraefo no gyina hɔ) a, ɛsɛ sɛ woka sɛ, 'M'ani guan, m'ani tew.' " Ansa na Ɔba-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nsa aka no no, ɔkae sɛ, "Wo a wowɔ hɔ, Ɔbaa-Nkwakoraa, eyinom yɛ me wɔfa ani a woyii no, na me ne wɔn rekɔ". Ɔbaa-Nkwakoraa no kae sɛ, "Ɛho nhia, nea onim (sɛnea wɔne wo bedi no) wɔ hɔ". Ɔba-Kwasi-Gyinamoa baa fie, na ɔfaa aniwa no de hyɛɛ ne wɔfa aniwa no mu. Ɔkae sɛ, "M'ani guan, m'ani tew", na obuee ne wɔfa aniwa no mu yiye. Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma mu daa hɔ no, mmofra no nyinaa kɔɔ asuten no mu kɔhohoroo wɔn ho. Abofra Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ: "Merenhohoro me ho". Wɔkae sɛ: "Ebetumi aba sɛ wowɔ ɔyafunu dua bi?" Bere a wɔkae saa no, ne kokom yɛɛ den, na ɔhohoroo ne ho. Ɛhɔ ara na otuu ne ho too nsu no mu no, Ɔpanyin-baa no kyeree no, twom! Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Abofra Kwasi-Gyinamoa, manya wo", na ɔne no kɔɔ ne kurom. Afei Ɔpanyin-baa no kɔfaa adaka kataa no so, na ɔfaa borɔdɔma de too gya mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Merekɔ onion abusuakuo no mu, merekɔ spice abusuakuo no mu, na afei mɛba na makum Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa". Ɔfirii hɔ, na Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa boaboaa aboɔ ano na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ bubuu no, pupru! pupru. Ɔpanyin no nena bisaa no sɛ, "Dɛn na woredi?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Tiger-nuts". Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma me bi menom". Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Bue me". Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Wobɛkɔ". Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Dabi, merennkɔ". Ɔbuee ne ho, na ɔfirii adi; na ɔbaa no nena rebɔ ntɛtɛ. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa borɔdɔma nhwiren no, bɔɔ Ɔbaa-kwakoraa no nanabaa, na ɔkum no. Afei ɔworɔɔ Ɔbaa-kwakoraa no nanabaa no honam, na ɔhyɛɛ no. Ɔbaa-kwakoraa no san bae; ɔkae sɛ, "Adɛn nti na mo nyinaa ho agu fĩ saa?" Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Makunkum Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa; mede no ahyɛ ogya mu". Saa asɛm yi yɛɛ Ɔbaa-kwakoraa no nanabaa no dɛ, na otuu asa na ɔmaa ne nne so (na ɔtoo dwom sɛ): "Abofra, woakum Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, Da bi, wobɛyɛ obi".

Owiee aduan no yɛ no, wɔtenaa ne nkyɛn, na wodii. Ɔpanyin-nananom no hwiee sopa no (wɔ n'anom de nne kɛse teɛɛm), fweo! fweo! Mmofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kae sɛ, "Worehwie sopa (wɔ w'anom a wɔde wo mmanana ti ayɛ) no". Ɔpanyin-nananom no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na woka saa?" Obuae sɛ, "Nea mekae ne sɛ, nkyene nni sopa yi mu". Ɔpanyin-nananom no kae sɛ, "Kɔ kɔfa bi". Enti ɔkɔfaa bi. Wɔdidi wiei no, Ɔbea-Nkwakoraa no kae sɛ, "Seesei a makum Ɔba-Kwasi-Gyinamoa yi, bra ha, me banana". Bere a ɔbae no, ɔfaa sikakɔkɔɔ ntade na ɔhyehyɛɛ no fɛfɛɛfɛ. Afei, Ɔbea-Nkwakoraa no hwɛɛ n'anim kakra. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ah! abofra yi anim ma mekae Ɔba-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no". Ɔba no kae sɛ, "Agya, adɛn, bere a wunim sɛ Ɔba-Kwasi-Gyinamoa bae na okunkum m'abusuafo baawɔtwe no nyinaa (woka saa), efisɛ w'abrɛ me werɛ. " Ɔbea-Nkwakoraa no kae sɛ, "Kata w'ano". Afei abofra no brɛɛ ne ho ase wɔ fie hɔ. Nanabaa no yii no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Momma menkɔ na menkɔtow no ngu ɔfasu no mfinimfini". Enti Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa no kɔe. Bere a oduu ɔfasu no mfinimfini no, oyii sika kɔkɔɔ agude a na ɔbaa no de ahyehyɛ no no nyinaa guu hɔ na ɔkyekyeree guu hɔ. Afei ɔka sɛ, "Obi wɔ hɔ, Aberewa-Nanabaa, obi wɔ hɔ, Aberewa-Nanabaa, mene no, Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, afei ne mprenu so, maba sɛ merebɛkum wo banana, na afei nso mafa wo sika kɔkɔɔ ahosiesie". Aberewa-Nanabaa buae sɛ, "Mekae sɛ w'anim te sɛ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa; afei baabiara a wobɛkɔ no, me ne wo bɛkɔ". Afei wɔn baanu nyinaa guanee, kiri! kiri! Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa duu ne akuraa, na ɔde sika kɔkɔɔ ahosiesie no maa n'abusuafo, na ɔde ne ho kosiee ɔkwan no ho, na Aberewa-Nanabaa no nso baa hɔ. Ɛno nti, sɛ wonam ɔkwan bi so a, wobɛte sɛ ɛnne bi rebɔ, "pe". Ɛno ne Ɔbea-Nkwakoraa-Nanabea a ɔtaa Ɔba-Kwasi-Gyinamoa.

M'asɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
