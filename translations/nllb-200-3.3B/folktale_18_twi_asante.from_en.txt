Yɛmfa no aniberesɛm, yɛnka no aniberesɛm, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ASƐM A ƐDA SO Sɛnea Atesɛmdi Baa Abusuakuw no Mu

WƆKA sɛ (bere bi) owiyamfo bi kɔɔ kwae mu; ɔbae na ɔhwɛɛ amoa bi mu, na na Nipa, Onipa no, Osebo, ɔsebɔ no, Owo, ɔwɔ no, ne Kusie, ɔsebɔ no gyina mu. Afei, bere a saa nnipa yi huu Oguanhwɛfo no, nea ɔka yi kae sɛ, "Agya Oguanhwɛfo, bra na yi me fi ha". Nea ɔka no kae sɛ, "Agya Oguanhwɛfo, bra na yi me fi ha". Oguanhwɛfo no kae sɛ, "Meremmɛba, efisɛ ɔkyena mpo, sɛ mibegye wo, na wo nso woaba me so a, nea wobɛyɛ me (mehu) no ho nhia me. Nea ɔka yi kae sɛ, "Merentumi (nyɛ saa). " Nea ɔka no kae sɛ, "Merentumi (nyɛ saa). " Oguanhwɛfo no kae sɛ , Sɛ saa na ɛte ankasa a, mɛyi wo adi. Na ampa ara ɔyii nnipa no nyinaa kɔɔ (ne) akuraa no ase. Da koro bi, bere a Ɔkyekyerefo no wɔ hɔ no, Osebo, ɔsebɔ no, de nam bebree brɛɛ no. Ɔkae sɛ, Agya Okyekyerefo, eyi yɛ aseda afɔrebɔ wɔ ayamye a woayɛ me no ho, meda wo ase, Apiafo. Ɔde nam nkɛntɛn dumien a wɔde mmɛ da no brɛɛ Okyekyerefo no. Oguanhwɛfo no daa no ase na Akata no fii hɔ. Da foforo, Oguanhwɛfo no wɔ hɔ, na Okusie, Oguanhwɛfo no, bae. Ɔkae sɛ, "Agya Oguanhwɛfo, wopɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma?" Oguanhwɛfo no kae sɛ, "Dɛn ntia, ɛno ne ade a nnipa pere hwehwɛ". Oguanhwɛfo no fii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a Nyankonpon, Ɔsoro nyame no, de ne sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no siee. Ɔkɔe na ɔtutuu abura wɔ dan no ase, na ɔhyɛn dan no mu, na ɔkɔe na ɔfaa ɔsoro nyame sika mfuturo no nyinaa, de brɛɛ Oguanhwɛfo no. Oguanhwɛfo no kae sɛ, "Meda wo ase, Aku". Na Oguanhwɛfo no kɔe. Da foforo, na ɔwɔ hɔ, na ɔtee sɛ obi rekyia no, "Ago". Oguanhwɛfo no buae sɛ, "Ame" (ba mu). Ɔbarima no bae; ɔkae sɛ, Agya Hunter, anɔpa pa. Ɔ (Agya Hunter) buae sɛ, Yiw, busuani. Ɔkae sɛ, Eyi ne borɔdɔma nsã kuruwa ketewaa bi a mede bae sɛ mede rebɛda wo ase. Agya Hunter kae sɛ, Mempɛ; wo de, Ɔbarima, ɛte sɛ nea mekae sɛ ɛbɛyɛ, bere a mekae sɛ meremfa wo nkɔ no; hwɛ nea Akekantwɛre no de brɛɛ me, hwɛ nea Oguamma no de brɛɛ me. Ɔbarima no gyee ne borɔdɔma nsã no. Ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔsoro-Nyame no sɛ, "Wo sika mfuturo a ayera no, na Rat no aba abɛfa, na ɔde akɔma Oguanhwɛfo no". Ɔsoro-Nyame no bisae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ saa anaa?" Osee sɛ, "Owura, ɛyɛ nokware pefee". Ɔsoro-Nyame no somaa abɔfo sɛ wɔnkɔkyere Oguanhwɛfo no, na wɔde hama kyekyeree no abɔso mu. Wɔbɔɔ no mmaa pii. Afei bere a wɔduruu kwan no so ne Ɔkyekyerefo no, ɔkae sɛ, 'Apaninfoɔ, mepɛ sɛ mekɔ na me ho tɔ me.' Asomafoɔ no kae sɛ, 'Dabi, ɔkorɔmfo, wobɛdwane.' Ɔkae sɛ, 'Dabi, merennwane, na sɛ wopɛ a, fa hama no kyekyere me abɔso mu, na fa kyekyere dua bi ho, na ma me kwan ma menkɔ.' Asomafoɔ no tiee no, na wogyaa no. Ɛhɔ na na ɔwɔ, na ɔretoto ne ho yiye, bere a aboa tenten a ne ho yɛ fɛ no bae no. Ɔkae sɛ, Hwɛ ha, m'adamfo, gye aduro yi, na fa ka wo ho, na sɛ wokɔ, na wɔgyina w'anim wɔ bagua no anim, na ɛba sɛ wobekum wo a, mɛkɔ ɔhene harem mu, na matwe ne yere a ɔdɔ no no ba no bi; sɛ metwe no a, wubehu sɛ obiara asɔre, na wo nso wobɛkasa aka sɛ, Mewɔ aduro, na sɛ wo de bedi dwuma wɔ abofra a obenya no so. Ɔkyekyerefo no kaa sɛ, "Meda wo ase, Aku". Asomafoɔ no faa Ɔkyekyerefo no kɔgyinaa Ɔsoro-nyame anim, (ne ne mpanimfoɔ anim) Kontire, Akwum, Adonten, Gyase, ne Kyidom asahene. Ɔkyekyerefo no kaa n'asɛm, na bere a Ɔkasafo no sɔre buu atɛn no, wɔtee awerɛhoɔ bi sɛ, pui! pui! Wɔbisaa sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn?" Na wɔbuaa sɛ, "Ɔwɔ bi akyekyere Ɔsorosoro nyame yere no ba no". Na wɔn nyinaa kɔeɛ. Na Ɔkyekyerefoɔ no gyina hɔ; ɔse, "Sɛ menyɛ ɔdeduani a, anka menya aduro". Ɔbarima bi tee no, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Monkɔfa ɔdeduani no mmra ha". Na Ɔkyekyerefoɔ no bae. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Sɛ wubetumi ahwɛ eyi, me ba, na wanwu a, mede wo ti no bɛma wo. Ɔkyekyerefo no kae sɛ, Mate, Kind-one. Na Ɔkyekyerefo no kae (san) sɛ, Agyapa, nea merebɛkyerɛ wo, sɛ wubetumi anya bi a, afei metumi ahwɛ wo ba no. Wɔkae sɛ, Ɛyɛ ade bɛn? Ɔkae sɛ, Kasafo mogya a wɔde bɛfra aduru a wɔde anoa no mu Wɔbisaa sɛ, "Ɛhe na yɛbɛnya Tell-tale mogya no?" Ɔkasafo no mu baako, a na ɔyɛ onyansafo no, kaa sɛ, "Na ɔbarima a ɔbaa bɛka kyerɛɛ Ɔsoro-nyame no sɛ Ɔkyekyerefoɔ no aba abɛwia Ɔsoro-nyame no sika no nso ɛhe?" Wɔbisaa sɛ, "Wonnim, wonnim". Wɔkɔfaa ɔbarima no bae. Oguanhwɛfo no kae sɛ, "Momfa ne nsa no mu, na momfa ne mogya no mma me". Ɛhɔ ara na wɔtwaa ne nsa no. Oguanhwɛfo no faa mogya no, na ɔde dɔkɔdɔkɔ yɛɛ no, na ɔde yɛɛ ɔhene no ba no nan ho adwuma. Amonom hɔ ara ɔsɔree. Wɔkae sɛ, "Montow Hunter no ayeyi dwom". Wɔteɛɛm sɛ, "Eee!" Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, "Megye wo ti na mede ama wo, na Tell-tale no de, kum no, na twa ne nam mu, na tow kyene baabiara. Wɔkunkum no, na wɔtwitwa ne nam mu nketenkete, na wɔtow kyene baabiara. Saa na Tell-tale pii baa abusuakuw no mu.

M'asɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
