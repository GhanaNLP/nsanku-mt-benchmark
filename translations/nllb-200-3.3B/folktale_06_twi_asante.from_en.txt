SƐ OBI yɛ wo adepa a, DA W'ASƐNKA adi denam adepa a wobɛyɛ ama no no so

Wɔka sɛ: "Na ɔkɔre bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔrekɔ n'akwantu mu no, ohyiaa akwakoraa bi a na ɔyare bi wɔ ne nan ho, na ɔkae sɛ: O! Eyi yɛ ɔyare bi a ɛwɔ ne su mu; sɛ wowɔ bi te sɛ eyi a, ɛmfa ho mmɔden biara a wobɛbɔ no, wobɛtumi anante anaa? " Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, "Oh, kakraa bi pɛ. " Osuani no kae sɛ, "Mo nnipa, ɛnnɛ, sɛ meyɛ biribi pa ma mo (ɛda yi), ɔkyena mobɛfa biribi bɔne de ada me ase". Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, "Oh, mintumi nyɛ (saa). " Osuani no kae sɛ, "Sɛ wuntumi nyɛ saa a, mate. " Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, "Twa w'ani na bue". Na ɔpanyin no too n'ani na obuee n'ani. Osuani no kae sɛ, "Hwɛ wo ɔyare no". Na bere a ɔpanyin no bae no, wanhu ne ho sɛnkyerɛnne biara. Afei ɔtotow n'ani bio, na ɔbuebuee, na ohuu sɛ asase no nyinaa ada hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ: "San kɔ wɔn nkyɛn bio". Ɔtotow wɔn anim, na ɔbuebuee wɔn anim, na afie no gyinaa hɔ pintinn. Na ɔtotow wɔn anim bio. Na ɔbuebuee wɔn anim no, ohuu kurow bi a ne kɛse te sɛ ɛno, na na ɛso yɛ kɛse. Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Meda wo ase". Afei ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Dɛn na memfa nkɔda wo ase?" Na ɔkɔre no kae sɛ: "Mempɛ ade ketewa bi mpo, me de, nea mepɛ ara ne dua a esi hɔ yi". Na ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Eyi (nea wubebisa) no, fa no". Afei ɔkɔre no tu fii hɔ, na osii ne so, na ɔforoo asa, na ɔtow nkesua abien de guu mu, na ɔde guu mu. Na ɔforoo nkesua abien no, na ofii hɔ kɔhwehwɛɛ biribi a ne mma bedi. Na ɔbea panyin no banana a na ɔne no te no fii ase nwiinwii sɛ: "Hwɛ! Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Dɛn na ɛrekɔ so?" Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Ma menkɔnom ɔkɔre mma". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Ɛhe na mɛkɔ akonya ɔkɔre mma?" Abɔfra ketewa no fii ase kaa bio sɛ: "Kɔnom! Kɔnom!" Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Dɛn na ɛrekɔ so?" Ɔba no kae sɛ: "Ma menkɔnom ɔkɔre mma, efisɛ sɛ minnya bi a menkɔnom a, mewu". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Ɛsɛ sɛ eyi, me mmanana wu esiane sɛ ɔmpɛ sɛ ɔkɔnom ɔkɔre mma nti? Pinpin! Pinpin! Na ɛte sɛ nea dua no rekɔ fam, bere a Ɔpɔnkɔ no mma mu panyin no huruw sɔre, na ɔbaa na ɔgyinaa ofie no ano, na ɔteɛɛm; ɔfrɛɛ ne maame, kaa sɛ:

Sango, anomaa no!

Sango, anomaa no, ɔkɔre no ba!

Sango, anomaa no!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔ adidi a, san bra!

Sango, anomaa no!

Sango, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh

Ɔbea no tee sɛ ne ba no resu no, ɔsɔree, (na ne ntaban a ɛrepaepae no tewee,) fa! ɔbae, na ɔkae sɛ, "Sanguri!" na dua no (a na aka kakraa bi ma wɔatwa no) san bae bio, na nnipa a na wɔrebobɔ no nyinaa, wɔmenee. Afei ɔfaa aduan a ɔde bae no de maa ne mma no. Afei ɔpaa wɔn kyɛw kae sɛ, "Merekɔ, sɛ akwakoraa no ba sɛ ɔrebɛfa wo a, ma ɔmfa wo". Na akwakoraa no kae sɛ, "Kɔ na bɔ dua no na kɔfa mmoa no bi ma me banana no". Na wɔkɔɔ hɔ ne mprenu so, Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! Na ɛte sɛ nea dua no rebɛkɔ fam, bere a (ɔtweaseɛ no) ba no fii adi na ɔbaa na ɔgyinaa ofie no ano, na ɔfrɛɛ ne maame sɛ:

Sango, anomaa no!

Sango, anomaa no, ɔkɔre no ba!

Sango, anomaa no!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔ adidi a, san bra!

Sango, anomaa no!

Sango, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh, oh

Ɔfrɛɛ ne maame, na ɔfrɛɛ, na ɔfrɛɛ, na ɔfrɛɛ mmuae biara amma na afei dua no kasae bere a ɛkaa fam no, "Brim!" (ese). Wɔfaa Eagle no mma; wɔmaa ɔbaa panyin no biako, nanso nea ɛkaa no tu fii hɔ na osii Wawa dua so. Wɔnwenee baako, na wɔmaa ɔbaa panyin no babarima no bi, na ɔde kaa plantain a wɔhyew no ho (na na ɔredidi). Ankyɛ na Eagle no bae. Bere a oduu dua a wɔatwa no, ohuu ne mma no mu biako sɛ ɔte hɔ; obisaa no nea asi no, na ɔkaa ne nsɛm no nyinaa kyerɛɛ no. Na ɔkɔre no siim kɔɔ ɔbaa no akuraa no ase. Bere a ɔkɔe no, na ɔbaa no banana no redidi ne mma no mu baako. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔbaa panyin, meda wo ase". Ɔfirii ɔbaa no fie na ɔfirii (n'asumansɛm) ase wɔ kuro no ano. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sanguri", na nnipa nyinaa yeraeɛ, na ɔsan kaa bio sɛ, "Sanguri", na afie nyinaa bubuu hɔ prɛko pɛ na baabiara annyae. "Sanguri", akuraa no dan kwae bio. "Sanguri", na ɔbaa no yareɛ san bae. Ɔbea no kae sɛ: "Otumfoɔ, woahu, ɛno nti na mpanyimfo no ka sɛ, sɛ obi yɛ wo yiye a, gye sɛ wo nso woyɛ no yiye na w'ani nnye bɔne ho". Eyi ne m'asɛm a meka kyerɛ wo, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ anaa ɛnyɛ dɛ a, wubetumi agye bi atom, na wubetumi akamfo me wɔ nea aka no ho.
