Yɛmfa no aniberesɛm, yɛnka no aniberesɛm, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ƐYƐƐƐ DƐN NA WƆBƐKA Ɔsoro-Nyame no Nsɛm Ho Asɛm sɛ "Ateaseɛnam-Asɛm"

KWAKU ANANSE, Spider, kɔɔ Nyankonpon, Ɔsoro-nyame no nkyɛn bere bi kɔtɔɔ Ɔsoro-nyame no nsɛm. Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ: Wubetumi atɔ wɔn? Abɔnkoro no kae sɛ: Yiw. Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ: Kokofu, Bekwai, ne Asumengya aba, nanso wontumi ntɔ wɔn, na wo a woyɛ obi a onni otumfoɔ no, wobɛtumi atɔ wɔn? Abɔnkoro no kae sɛ: Ɛdɛn ne wɔn akatua? Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ: Wontumi ntɔ wɔn hwee. Onini abɔde a wɔfrɛ no Python; Osebo, Leopard; Mmoatia, Fairy; (ne) Mmoboro, the Hornets". Spider no kae sɛ, "Mede eyinom nyinaa bi bɛba, na (nea ɛsen saa no), mɛde me yere panyin Nsia (Ɔba a ɔto so asia no) aka ho". Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔfa wɔn bra". Spider no bae, na ɔkaa ne maame ho asɛm nyinaa, na ɔkae sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ metɔ Ɔsoro nyame no ho nsɛm, ne Sk y-nyame se me de Onini, Python; Osebo, Leopard; Mmoatia, a Fairy; ne Mmoboro, Hornets; na mekae sɛ mede wo bɛka pii no ho na makɔma Sky-nyame". Afei Spider no bisaa ne yere Aso sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ na ama yɛatumi anya Onini, Python no?" Na Aso ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Kɔ na twa borɔdɔma dua bi, na twa ahama bi nso, na fa wɔn bra". Spider san baa wɔn nkyɛn. Na Aso kaa sɛ, "Fa wɔn kɔ asuten no ho". Na Ananse faa wɔn; bere a na ɔrekɔ no ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛware kyɛn ɔno, ɛnware kyɛn ɔno; woredi atoro, ɛware kyɛn ɔno". Akoekoe no kae sɛ, "Ɛhɔ na ɔda hɔ". Akoekoe no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛrekɔ so yi?" Akoekoe no kae sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ (me yere) Aso na ɔne me resusuw ho sɛ saa borɔdɔma dua yi yɛ me dea? Na Onini, Python no, kaa sɛ, "Fa no bra, na bra bɛsɔ me hwɛ". Ɔfaa borɔdɔma dua no de too Python no honam so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Teɛ wo ho". Na Python no teɛɛ ne ho, na Ananse faa ahoma no na ɔtwee no (na ɛnne a ɔde kyekyeree no no yɛ nwenene! nwenene! nwenene! kosii sɛ oduu ne ti ho. Ananse, Spider no kaa sɛ, "Ɔkwasea, mɛfa wo akɔ ɔsoro nyame nkyɛn na magye ɔsoro nyame no nsɛm". Ananse de no kɔ Nyame nkyɛn. Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka Ɔbea no kae sɛ: "Hwehwɛ kurobow bi, na fa nsu hyɛ no ma, na fa kɔ". Na ɔtweaseɛ no kɔtraa kwae no mu, na ohui sɛ kurobow bi sɛn hɔ, na ohwiee nsu no bi guu kurobow no so. Afei Nkorɔmma no hwiee ne nkae no guu ne ho so, na ɔtwitwaa nhwiren ahaban na ɔde kataa ne ti so. Na afei ɔdan n'ani kyerɛɛ Hornets no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Esiane sɛ osu no aba nti, monyɛ no yiye sɛ mobɛba abɛhyɛn eyi mu, me borɔdɔma, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a osu no renka mo; monhu sɛ makɔfa plantain ahaban de akata me ho anaa?" Afei Hornets no kae sɛ, "Yɛda wo ase Aku, yɛda wo ase Aku". Hornets no nyinaa tu fii hɔ, na wɔn nyinaa yerae wɔ borɔdɔma no mu, fom! Agya Spider kataa n'ano, na ɔkae sɛ, "Akwasea, manya mo, na mede mo rekɔ ma mo nsa aka Ɔsoro-nyame no nsɛm". Na ɔde Hornets no kɔmaa Ɔsoro-nyame no. Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Me nsa aka, nea aka no da so ara wɔ hɔ". Akoekoeboa no san bae bio, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere, na ɔkae sɛ, "Osebo, ɔsebɔ no da so wɔ hɔ". Aso kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔyɛ abura bi". Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛdɔɔso, mate ase". Afei Akoekoeboa no kɔe kɔhwehwɛɛ ɔsebɔ no anan, na (ɔhunuu wɔn no) ɔtutuu abura a emu dɔ paa, na ɔkataa so, na ɔsan baa fie. Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu no, bere a nneɛma no fii ase daa adi no, Spider kae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ, na bere a ɔkɔe no, na ɔsebɔ bi da amoa no mu. Ananse kae sɛ: "Agya ba kumaa, ɛna ba kumaa, maka akyerɛ wo sɛ nnom nsa, na afei, sɛnea obi bɛhwɛ kwan wɔ wo ho no, woaboro nsa, na ɛno nti na woahwe amoa no mu yi; sɛ meka sɛ meyi wo a, da a edi hɔ no, sɛ wuhu me, anaa me mma no mu biara a, wobɛkɔ akɔkyere wɔn". Ananse kɔɔ na ɔtwaa nnua mmienu, ɔde baako baa ha, na ɔde baako baa hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa wo nsa baako gu ha, na fa wo nsa baako nso gu ha". Na kwaakwaadabi no de wɔn guu hɔ (baa a wɔka kyerɛɛ no no). Bere a ɔrebɛforo no, Ananse maa ne sekan no so, na prɛko pɛ na ɛbaa ne ti so, gao! (saa na ɛte). Na kwaakwaadabi no gyee kwaakwaadabi no (na) fom! Ananse nyaa nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn bi a ɔde bɛkɔ abura no mu akɔyi ɔkraman no. Ɔyii ɔkraman no firii abura no mu baeɛ; ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔkwasea, mede wo rebɛsesa ama Ɔsoro-nyame no ho nsɛm". Ɔmaa ɔkraman no so sɛ ɔnkɔma Nyame, Ɔsoro-nyame no. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka, nea aka no da so ara wɔ hɔ. " Akɔnkɔnkɔn no bae, na ɔtasee Akua ba (bonniba tuntum bi a n'anim te sɛ dua) bi, na ɔtwee latex bi a ɛyɛ fɛw (firi dua bi so) na ɔde sraa bonniba no nipadua so, na ɔhwee eto (mashed yams), na ɔde bi hyɛɛ bonniba no nsa mu, na ɔhwee bi kaa ho de guu kɔbere kotoku mu; ɔkyekyeree ahama kyekyeree bonniba no abɔso ho, ɔfaa no de no kɔtoo odum dua bi ase, baabi a Fairies no ba di agoru. Na Fairy bi bae. Ɔkae sɛ, "Akua, metumi adi saa mash yi bi?" Ananse twee ahama no, na doll no bɔɔ ne ti. Fairy no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabeanom no mu biako sɛ, "Ɔse metumi adi bi. " Ɔkae sɛ", Di bi, afei. " Na owiee adidi no, na ɔdaa no ase (na doll no). Nanso bere a ɔdaa no ase no, wammua no. Na Fairy no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no sɛ, Bere a meda no ase no, wammua no. Fairy (kan) no nuabea no kae sɛ, Hyɛ ne su no so. Na ɔhwee no, pa! Na ne nsa no kyekyeree hɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no sɛ, Me nsa no kyekyeree hɔ. Ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Fa nea aka no na yɛ ne su no so bio. Na ɔfaa no na ɔhwee no, pa! Na Ananse bae bɛkyekyeree no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɔkwasea, manya wo, mɛfa wo akɔ ɔsoro-nyame no nkyɛn (a ɛne ne nsɛm no asesa) " Na ɔne no kɔɔ fie. Na Ananse ka kyerɛɛ ne maame, Ya Nsia (ɔba a ɔtɔ so nsia no), sɛ, Sɔre, ma yɛnkɔ, ɛfiri sɛ, me ne Fairy no de wo rekɔ akɔma Sky-god (asesa) ne nsɛm no. Ɔmaa wɔn so, na ɔfirii hɔ kɔɔ baabi a Sky-god wɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, Ɔsoro-nyame, Fairy bi wɔ ha, na me yere panyin a mekaa ne ho asɛm no nso wɔ ha. Afei Ɔsoro-nyame frɛɛ (ne mpanyimfo), Kontire ne Akwam asahene, Adonten (asafodɔm no mu panyin), Gyase (asafohene-domo); Oyoko, Ankobea, ne Kyidom (nkoa a wɔhwɛ wɔn akyi no so panyin). Na ɔde asɛm no too wɔn anim sɛ, Ahemfo akɛseɛ aba, na wɔantumi ntɔ Ɔsoro-nyame nsɛm no, nanso Kwaku Ananse, Nkorͻfo no, atumi atua ne bo no; me nsa aka Mmoboro, Nkorͻfo no afi ne hɔ; me nsa aka Mmoatia, Akokɔbea no afi ne hɔ; me nsa aka Osebo, Nkraman no afi ne hɔ; me nsa aka Onini, Akokɔbea no afi ne hɔ; na n'ankasa ne pɛ mu no, Ananse aka sɛ Ɔkaa sɛ, To n'ayeyi dwom. Eee! (wɔteɛɛm). Ɔsoro nyame kaa sɛ, Kwaku Ananse, efi nnɛ na ɛbɛkɔ so daa, mefa me Ɔsoro nyame no nsɛm no na mede ma mo, kose! kose! kose! me nhyira, nhyira, nhyira. Ɛnsɛ sɛ yɛfrɛ no Ɔsoro nyame no nsɛm bio, na mmom yɛbɛfrɛ no Spider-kekae.

M'asɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
