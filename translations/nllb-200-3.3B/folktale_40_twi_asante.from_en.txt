Yɛmfa no aniberesɛm, yɛnka no aniberesɛm, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Twɛn Ɛhɔ na Wubehu

NÁ ƆBAA bi ne ne ba te. Bere a ɔsomaa no (baabi) no, ɔkae sɛ: "Merenkɔ". Ne maame ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Twɛn na wubehu". Bere biara a ɔsomaa no (baabi) no, ɔkae sɛ: "Merenkɔ". Afei ne maame ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Twɛn na wubehu". Aberante no bisaa no sɛ: "Dɛn nti na bere a obiara mmaa ha da no, afei na woaba ha?" Aberante no kae sɛ: "Me ne me maame na ɛte hɔ bere nyinaa; ɔkae sɛ: 'Twɛn na wubehu,' na mekae sɛ mɛba abɛhwehwɛ 'me-behu,' na mahwɛ no". Aberante no kae sɛ: "Meyɛ Wɔn mu biako, nanso Yɛn mu ɔkɛseɛ no wɔ akyirikyiri". Na abofra no buae sɛ, "Me maame ka daa sɛ, 'Mɛhwɛ,' enti mekae sɛ merebɛhwehwɛ 'me-bɛ-hwɛ' no, na mahwɛ no. Na nnua tenten no buae sɛ, 'Meyɛ Wɔn mu biako, nanso kɔ w'anim.' Na abofra no fii ase bio. Bere a na ɔrekɔ no, (na nsoromma a ɛhyerɛn wɔ hɔ), na ɔdaa anwenne bi a abubu so, na na ɔreba ne nkyɛn. Ohyiaa no. Glowing-Sparks kaa sɛ, Adɛn nti, sɛ obiara mma ha da a, woaba ha? Abofra no buae sɛ, Bere a me ne me maame te hɔ no, ɔka sɛ, mehu, enti mekae sɛ mɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ mehu, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a metumi ahwɛ no. Glowing-Sparks kaa sɛ, meyɛ Wɔn mu biako, nanso kɔ w'anim, efisɛ Ɔkɛseɛ no wɔ hɔ. Abofra no fii hɔ bio. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na Apapaapafo no de wɔn ano bɔ fie bi. Papaw no kae sɛ, Afei yɛbɛfa obi mogya de agu fie ha. Afei bere a abofra no duu hɔ no, wɔkae sɛ, Dɛn nti na, bere a obiara mmaa ha da no, afei na woaba ha? Ɔbuaa sɛ, Me ne me maame na ɛte daa; ɔkae sɛ, Twɛn hɔ na wubehu, enti mekae sɛ mɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ Me-bɛ-hu, na mahwɛ no. Ɛno akyi no, Papaw no kyeree abofra no, twaa ne ti, na wɔfaa ne mogya no hwie guu kɔbere anwenne bi mu; wɔfaa bi de guu fie hɔ. Akyiri no, Papaw no fii hɔ kɔtenaa dua tenten bi so, na wɔbaa baako akyi baako, na wɔde wɔn mmirika no guu mogya no mu, na wɔn mmirika no yɛɛ kɔkɔɔ. Papaw no nyinaa faa wɔn mmirika no de guu mogya no mu, sɛ nkae ma afahyɛ no. Afei, da koro, Papaw no mu biako kae sɛ ɔrekodidi. Ɔkɔhyiaa abofra no maame. Na ɛna no bisaa Papaw no sɛ, "Me ba a me ne no te no yɛ ɔkwadwo te sɛ biribiara. Sɛ me sɔre a, na meka sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔtwɛn wɔ hɔ na ɔbɛhwɛ; sɛ ɔkwasea, mepa kyɛw sɛ mede asɛmfua no di dwuma no, ɔkae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ Me-bɛ-hu, na watumi ahwɛ so. Nnawɔtwe atwam nnɛ na minhuu no, na mesrɛ mo sɛ, sɛ moahu no a, momfa no mmrɛ me. Papaw no kae sɛ, Oh, ɔbaa yɛn akuraa no ase, na yɛn nso, yɛkum no. Ne mogya no bi, a yɛfaa no sɛ nkae wɔ asɛm no ho no, wɔ me dua yi so. Enti sɛ wokɔ a, kɔ wo nkurɔfo nyinaa nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛ wɔn sɛ, sɛ obi ka kyerɛ ne yɔnko sɛ, "Twɛn na wubehu" a, ɔde nnome to no so.

M'asɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
