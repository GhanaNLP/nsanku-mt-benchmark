Yɛmfa no aniberesɛm, yɛnka no aniberesɛm, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ASƐM A ƐDA SO Sɛ Obi Kɔ Ɔdan Bi Mu Anadwo a, Ɛyɛ Sum

WƆKA sɛ Kuntukurunku, a ɛkyerɛ Nsɛnnennen, ne Kuntunamoa, a ɛkyerɛ Awiei, fi soro bɛhwee fam. Wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Agya asase, wo ho yɛ den dodo". Maame asase kae sɛ: "Dɛn nti na woka sɛ me ho yɛ den dodo?" Wɔkae sɛ: "Esiane sɛ wonkotuu amoa amma yɛn na yɛatumi ada wɔ mu nti". Agya asase no kɔɔ Prae, ɔfasu no, nkyɛn, na ɔkae sɛ: "Wo anom de nnomaa ntontom na ayɛ ma". Broom kaa sɛ, "Anyway) meyɛɛ saa. " Strip-of-bark nso kaa Sumina, Midden-heap, anim sɛ, "You keep what you receive". Midden-heap kaa sɛ, "Why do you abuse me?" He (Strip-of-bark) replied, "I have abused you, and if you don't know the reason, then stop asking". Midden-heap, too, addressed the Abe, Palm-tree, saying, "Your neck is all overgrown with rubbish". Palm-tree kaa sɛ, "Why do you abuse me?" Midden-heap replied, "I have abused you, and if you don't know the reason, then stop inquiring". Palm-tree, too, turned to Tetea, Little-black-ant, and said, "Your ears are black. Akoekoe tuntum no kae sɛ: "Adɛn nti na woboa me?" Abedua no buae sɛ: "Moboa wo, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a, gyae bisabisa". Akoekoe tuntum no nso kɔɔ Esono, ɔsono a ɔwɔ hɔ no nkyɛn, na ɔkɔɔ ne nan ho; na ɔtetee ɔsono no. Bere a na ɔretutu ne nan so no, na ɔtiatia Tamiriwa, ɔbosom tuntum no aniwa ase pɛɛ, na ɔbosom tuntum no aniwa fii ase tɔnee nsu, na ɔpopaa nsu no guu efan ahaban (a enyin wɔ hɔ) so. (Afei), aduan a Ɔbea-kwakoraa-nananom di no yɛ efan dua, na na ɛyɛ ɛno so na Tamiriwa, Black-snail, apopa n'ani so nsu. 'Kraman, Ɔkraman, retwam, na ɔ (Ɔbea-kwakoraa-nananom) frɛɛ Ɔkraman na ɔfaa nnyina (na ɔso) na ɔmaa Ɔkraman soaa ne ti so. Bere a Ɔkraman rekɔ no, ɔfaa kola-nut, na ɔpaee emu abien, na ɔtow biako guu Okagya, Squirrel no, amoa no ano. Okagya, ɔno nso, faa kola-nut ne Onyina, Silk-cotton-tree no, de too mu. Silk-cotton-tree no nso, ne bo fuwii. Ɔhunuu sɛ abɔfra ketewa bi retwam, na ɔtwee n'abaa, na ɔboroo no kum no. Afei, na abɔfra ketewa no yɛ Nyankonpon, Ɔsoro-nyame no ba. Ɛhɔ ara na wɔkyeree Silk-cotton-tree no, na wɔde no kɔmaa Ɔsoro-nyame no. Wɔbisaa no (Silk-cotton-tree) sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na ɛrekɔ so yi?" Silk-cotton-tree no kaa sɛ, "Na megyina hɔ wɔ m'ankasa baabi, bere a Squirrel yi - abofra a ɔyɛ no - sii ne bo sɛ ɔbɛma me kola-nut, enti me nso, bere a abofra yi retwam no, misusuwii sɛ ɛyɛ Okagya, Squirrel no, na meyii m'abaa no mu biako na mebɔɔ no, na hwɛ, na ɛyɛ wo ba no". Nyame, Ɔsoro-nyame no, kae sɛ, "Fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na frɛ Okagya, Squirrel no, na ɔmmra". Okagya bae; ɔkae sɛ, "Owura, wunim sɛ me taboo ne kola-nut no, Okraman, Ɔkraman, na ɔkɔnkɔ no, na ɔkɔe o Na me de too m'abura no ano, na me de too hɔ no, ɛkɔhwee Otumfoɔ-ni-su-bo-dua no so. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Fa no, na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na frɛ Ɔkraman no ma ɔmmra". Ɔkraman no kae sɛ, "Owura, na menam m'ankasa me kwan so wɔ baabi - afei wuhu sɛnea (m'ani atew) no - bere a Ɔpanyin-baa-nananom faa ne nnua a ɔde gyae no de sii m'atifi so; afei Okagya, Akɔnkɔnkɔn no, ɔno na ɔda ne dan mu wɔ afuw a Ɔpanyin-baa-nananom ayɛ no mu sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obenya biribi adi, nanso me na Ɔpanyin-baa-nananom maa no kɔsoaa nnua no; me nso, mefaa kola-nut bi tow guu ne dan no ano". Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, "Fa no, fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na frɛ Ɔpanyin-baa-nanom no ma ɔmmra". Ɔpanyin-baa-nanom no bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ah, me mmanana, monhwɛ m'ano, me a mintumi nnwudwo nam, efan nnua na midi; afei nso, mehohoro Bosompra; me taboo ne (saa nti) Tamiriwa, Black-snail; Ɔkraman nko ara na na onim eyi, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ Tamiriwa, na ɔmaa no faa nsu fii n'ano, na ɔde guu efan nnua a mete so no mu. Ɛno nti na memaa no me nnyina sɛ ɔmfa". Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Oh, fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na kɔfa Tamiriwa, Black-snail no bra". Black-snail no bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Owura, na me nko ara mewɔ baabi, na hwɛ, ɔsono kɛseɛ bi tiatiaa baabi a ɛbɛn m'ani no so, na mekɔɔ kɔfaa ahaban sɛ merepopa no. Ɛno nko ara na minim wɔ ho". Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, "Momfa no, na momfa no nka dua bi ho, na momfa Esono a ɔyɛ ɔsono no mmrɛ me". Ɔsono no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nnansa a atwam no na mete hɔ, na me honam fii ase hyew, na bere a mehwɛɛ baabi a - mesrɛ me fa asɛm no kyɛw - ɔkwasea yi a ne ho yɛ hu no kɔ (me honam mu) sɛ ɔrebɛhwehwɛ nam a ɔde bɛtwetwe, na ama wanyin; na bere a na merepue me nan so no, ɔkɔhwanyan Tamiriwa, ɔmammɔfo tuntum no. " Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, "Fa no na fa no to dua so, na fa Tetea, ɔmammɔfo tuntum no brɛ me". Bere a Tetea bae no, ɔkae sɛ, "Owura, borɔdɔma dua bi wɔ hɔ; sɛ ɛso aba a, (nuhwam) yɛ tuntum kan; sɛ me, Ɔmammɔfo tuntum no, nantew so a, ɛma enyin. Nnansa yi na mewɔ hɔ, bere a Palm dua no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ m'aso yɛ tuntum, na esiane sɛ meyɛ ketewa nti, misusuwii sɛ mɛkɔ Elephant hɔ, akɔhwehwɛ nam a mɛdi na matumi ayɛ kɛse. Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, Fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na kɔfrɛ Palm dua no sɛ ɔmmra. Abe, Palm dua no, bae; ɔkae sɛ, Owura, eyi ne nea ɛsɛ sɛ meka: me ne Sumina, Midden-heap no, yɛtra faako, na bere a atɛkyɛ a wɔtow gu no so no yɛ kɛse dodo, na ontumi nnyina ano bio no, migye bi na mede kyekyere me kɔn ho; nanso afei, ɔtwee me ho, kae sɛ mempɛ sɛ me kyekyere me kɔn ho. Ɛno nti, m'ani danee kɔkɔɔ, na me nso, me bo fuw Black-ant no. Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, Fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na frɛ Sumina, Midden-heap no, na ɔmmra. Midden-heap bae; ɔkae sɛ, Sire, Abena, Strip-of-bark, a, sɛ wɔpopa atɛkyɛ wɔ dan bi mu a, wɔde ma no na ɔno nso de aba me nkyɛn. Da bi a atwam no, bere a na asɛm biara nni ho no, ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, Fa nea wunya no sie (i.e. me se, a, na bere a Palm dua no bɛn hɔ no, me nso, me ne no dii atirimɔdensɛm. Ɔsoro nyame se, Fa no kyekyere no dua bi ho, na frɛ Abena, Strip-of-bark, ma ɔmmra. Strip-of-bark bae; ɔkae sɛ, Sire, bere a Prae, the Broom, sweeps out a room, if they don't give me (his sweepings), when the wind blows, all the trash comes back again; afei, da foforo, ɛwom sɛ manyɛ hwee amma no de, ɔkae sɛ, You take things only to throw them away. Because of that, I became red of eye, too, and I went and abused Sumina, the Midden-appe-tree. Ɔsoro nyame se, Fa no kyekyere no dua bi ho, na ɔmfa no mmrɛ me. I Prae, the Broom, to come here. The Broom came; she said, Sire, Old-mother-earth lies there so flat, her surface gets dirty, so whenever I rise up, I sweep up all the filth upon her and make her beautiful; yet the other day, Mother-earth addressed me, saying, Your mouth is (full of) bird's droppings, na me nufu nso, yɛɛ me yaw, na mekɔɔ Abena, the Strip-of-bark. Ɔsoro nyame kae sɛ, Fa no kyekyere no dua so, na frɛ Maame-asase ma ɔmmra. When Mother-earth came, she said, Sire, there I am; they tread upon me; I never say anything; they do all kinds of thing me so, nanso menka hwee. Afei da foforo a na mewɔ hɔ no, Kuntukurunku ne Kuntunamoa, Odds and Ends, baa me so, na mekae sɛ, O! asɛm bɛn na asi? na wɔkae sɛ, Wonnuee amoa a yebetumi ada mu, na wo ne w'asisi no nyinaa ho nni mfaso (ma obiara). Ɛno nti, me nso, meyɛɛ Prae, Broom no basabasa. Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, Fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na kɔfa Odds and Ends. Odds and Ends bae. Wɔkae sɛ, 'Owura, na yɛwɔ ɔdan no atifi, na yɛredi agorɔ, na yɛhwee ase na yɛpiraa yɛn ho; ɛno nti na yɛkaa Maame Asase animtiaa, na yɛkae sɛ ɔyɛ tratraa. Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, 'Agyinatufo, yɛate asɛm yi nyinaa, enti momma yɛnsɔre nkɔ dan no mu na yensusuw adanse no ho na yɛmmra mmɛbu atɛn.' Enti wɔsɔre kɔɔ dan bi mu, na wɔtoo ɔpon no mu. Ɛno nti na sɛ wokɔ dan bi mu anadwo a, esum ba no. Ɔsoro nyame ne n'Agyinatufo no rebisabisa wɔn ho nsɛm wɔ hɔ.

M'asɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
