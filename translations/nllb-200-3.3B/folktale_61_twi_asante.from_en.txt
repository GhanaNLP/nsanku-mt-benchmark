Yɛmfa no aniberesɛm, yɛnka no aniberesɛm, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ASƐM BƐN NA ƐBƐYƐ EYI: Sɛ obi kɔ dan bi mu anadwo a, ɛhɔ yɛ sum

WƆKA sɛ Kuntukurunku, a ɛkyerɛ Nsɛnnennen, ne Kuntunamoa, a ɛkyerɛ Awiei, fi soro bɛhwee fam. Wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "O asase, w'ani afura!" Ɔkaa sɛ: "Ɛdɛn nti na woka sɛ me ho afura?" Wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Esiane sɛ wonkotuu amoa maa yɛn na yɛatumi ada wɔ mu ntia". Brom no danee ne ho kyerɛɛ Abena, The-strip-of-bark (a wɔsesa mu no), na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Wofa (nneɛma) na kɔ na kɔtow gu". Strip-of-bark se, "Adɛn nti na wohaw me?" Brom no se, "Anyway) mayɛ saa". Strip-of-bark nso kasa kyerɛɛ Sumina, Midden-heap, sɛ, "Wofa nea wugye". Midden-heap se, "Adɛn nti na wohaw me?" Ɔkaa sɛ: "Madi wo ho fɛw, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a, ɛnneɛ gyae sɛ wubisa". Na Middle-heap nso ka kyerɛɛ Abe, Palm-tree, sɛ: "Wo kɔn nyinaa ayɛ fĩ". Na Palm-tree kae sɛ: "Adɛn nti na woadi me ho fɛw?" Na Middle-heap buae sɛ: "Madi wo ho fɛw, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a, ɛnneɛ gyae sɛ wubisa". Na Palm-tree nso danee ne ho kyerɛɛ Tetea, Little-black-antwi, na ɔkae sɛ: "Wo aso yɛ bruu. Akoekoe ketewa no kae sɛ: "Adɛn nti na wohaw me?" Akoekoe ketewa no buae sɛ: "Mayɛ wo bɔne, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a, gyae bisabisa". Akoekoe ketewa no, ɔno nso kɔɔ Esono, ɔsono a ɔwɔ hɔ no nkyɛn, na ɔkɔɔ ne nan ho; na ɔtetee ɔsono no. Bere a na (nsono no) retutu ne nan so no, afei ɔtwee n'anammɔn Tamiriwa, Black-snail, aniwa ase pɛɛ, na Black-snail aniwa no fii ase tɔnee nsu, na ɔpopaa nsu no guu efan ahaban (a enyin) a ɛbɛn hɔ so. (Afei), aduan a Ɔpanyin-bea-nananom di ne efan dua no, na ɛno so na Tamiriwa, Black-snail, popaa n'aniwa mu nsu no. 'Kraman, Ɔkraman no, retwam, na ɔfrɛɛ Ɔkraman no, na ɔfaa nnyina no, na ɔmaa Ɔkraman no de sii ne ti so. Na Ɔkraman retwam no, ɔfaa kola-nut, na ɔpaee emu abien, na ɔtow biako guu Okagya, Akɔnkɔnkɔn no, amoa no ano. Okagya, ɔno nso, faa kola-nut no, na ɔde bɔɔ Onyina, Silk-cotton-tree no. Ɔhunuu sɛ abɔfra ketewa bi retwam, na ɔtwee n'abaa, na ɔboroo no kum no. Afei, na abɔfra ketewa no yɛ Nyankonpon, Ɔsoro nyame no ba. Ɛhɔ ara na wɔkyeree abɔfra ketewa no, na wɔde no kɔmaa Ɔsoro nyame no. Wɔbisaa no sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na ɛrekɔ so yi?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Na megyina hɔ wɔ m'ankasa baabi, bere a ɔkwatani yi - abofra a ɔwoo no no - sii ne bo sɛ ɔbɛma me kola-nut, enti me de, bere a na abofra yi retwam no, misusuwii sɛ ɛyɛ Okagya, ɔkwatani no, na meyii m'abaa no mu biako na mebɔɔ no, na hwɛ, na ɛyɛ wo ba no". Nyame, Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Fa no na kyekyere no to dua bi so, na frɛ Okagya, ɔkwatani no, na ɔmmra". Okagya bae; ɔkae sɛ, "Owura, wunim sɛ me taboo ne kola-nut, nanso Okraman, Ɔkraman no, kɔfaa bi, na ɔde too m'abun no ano, na bere a na meretu no agu no, ɛkɔbɔɔ Old-man-silk-cotton-tree". Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Fa no, na kyekyere no to dua bi so, na frɛ Ɔkraman no na ɔmmra". Wɔkɔfrɛɛ Ɔkraman no sɛ ɔmmra. Ɔkraman no kae sɛ, "Owura, na menam m'ankasa kwan so wɔ baabi, afei wuhu sɛnea me ho yɛ anika no? bere a Ɔpanyin-bea-nananom faa ne nnyina de too me ti so no; afei Okagya, Akɔnkɔnkɔn no, ɔno na ɔda ne dan mu wɔ afuw a Ɔpanyin-bea-nananom ayɛ no mu sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obenya biribi adi, nanso me na Ɔpanyin-bea-nananom maa no so nnyina no; me nso, mefaa kola-nut bi Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Momfa no, momfa no nka dua bi ho, na momfrɛ Ɔbea-Nkwakoraa no mmra". Ɔbea-Nkwakoraa no bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ah, me mmanana, monhwɛ m'ano mu, me a mintumi nnwudwo nam no, mewe efan ahaban; afei nso, mehohoro Bosompra; me taboo ne (saa nti) Tamiriwa, Black-snail; Ɔkraman nko ara na na onim eyi, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ Tamiriwa, na ɔmaa no nsu fii n'ano, na ɔde guu efan ahaban a mete so no mu. Ɛno nti na mede me nnua maa no sɛ ɔmfa no". Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "O, fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na kɔfa Tamiriwa, ɔkwatani tuntum no bra". Ɔkwatani tuntum no bae. Ɔkae sɛ, "Owura, na mewɔ me ho baabiara, na hwɛ, na ɔsono kɛse bi tiatiaa baabi a ɛwɔ m'ani ase pɛɛ, na mefaa efan (ahaban) sɛ mede bɛpopa. Eyi nkutoo ne nea minim". Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, "Momfa no nkɔkyekyere no dua bi ho, na momfa Asono, ɔsono no mmrɛ me". Ɔsono no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nnansa a atwam no na mete hɔ, na me honam fii ase hyew, na bere a mehwɛɛ baabi a - mesrɛ me fa asɛmfua no di dwuma no - ɔkwasea ketewa bi a ɔyɛ tuntum no kɔhyɛɛ (me honam mu) sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛhwehwɛ nam a ɔde bɛtwetwe, na ama wanyin; na bere a na merepue m'anammɔn so no, ɛkɔhwee Tamiriwa, ɔbosom tuntum no so". Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, "Fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na fa no brɛ me". Tetea, mmoadabi ketewa no. Bere a Tetea bae no, ɔkae sɛ: "Owura, dua bi wɔ hɔ a sɛ ɛsow aba a, ne nnuaba no yɛ tuntum, na sɛ me, mmoadabi ketewa no, nantew so a, ɛma enyin. Da bi a na mewɔ hɔ no, bere a Palm dua no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ m'aso yɛ tuntum, na esiane sɛ meyɛ ketewa nti, misusuwii sɛ mɛkɔ Elephant no hɔ, akɔhwehwɛ nam a mɛdi na ama manya ahoɔden. Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, Fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na kɔfrɛ Palm dua no sɛ ɔmmra. Abe, Palm dua no bae; ɔkae sɛ, Owura, eyi ne nea ɛsɛ sɛ meka: me ne Sumina, Midden-heap no, te ase t ne nyinaa, na bere a atɛkyɛ a wɔtow gu ne so no dɔɔso dodo, na ontumi nnyina ano bio no, migye bi na mede bɔ me kɔn ho; afei de, ɔtwee m'aso sɛ mewɔ atɛkyɛ wɔ me kɔn ho. Ɛno nti, me nso, m'ani yɛɛ kɔkɔɔ, na me nso, meyeyɛɛ Black-ant no. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na frɛ Sumina, a ɔyɛ Middle-heap no, ma ɔmmra. Middle-heap bae; ɔkae sɛ, Owura, Abena, Strip-of-bark, a, sɛ wɔpopa sumina wɔ dan bi mu a, wɔde ma no na ɔno nso de aba me nkyɛn. Nnansa yi ara, a asɛm biara nni ho no, ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, "Fa nea wunya no sie". yɛ adifudepɛfo); mekae sɛ, a, na bere a na Palm dua no bɛn hɔ no, me nso, me ne no dii atirimɔdensɛm. Ɔsoro nyame kae sɛ, Fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na frɛ Abena, Strip-of-bark, ma ɔmmra. Strip-of-bark bae; ɔkae sɛ, Owura, bere a Prae, the Broom, popa dan bi mu, sɛ wɔamma me (ne mpopae mu), bere a mframa bɔ no, atɛkyɛ no nyinaa san ba bio; afei, da foforo no, ɛwom sɛ manyɛ hwee amma no de, Wofa nneɛma de tow. Ɛno nti, me nso m'ani yɛɛ kɔkɔɔ, na mekɔɔ Sumina, Middle-heap no so. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, Fa no, na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na frɛ Prae, Broom no, ma ɔmmra ha. Broom no bae; ɔkae sɛ, Owura, ɛna-asase dedaw no da hɔ tẽẽ, n'anim yɛ fĩ, enti bere biara a mɛsɔre no, mepopa fĩ no nyinaa gu no so na me ma no yɛ na afei da foforo, Maame-asase ne me kasae sɛ, W'ano (de) nnomaa ntontom ayɛ ma, na me nufu nso, yɛɛ ma, na mekɔɔ Abena, Strip-of-bark. Ɔsoro-nyame kae sɛ, Fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na frɛ Maame-asase ma ɔmmra. Bere a Maame-asase bae no, ɔkae sɛ, Owura, me da hɔ; wɔtiatia me so; menka hwee da; wɔyɛ nneɛma ahorow nyinaa Da bi a na mewɔ hɔ no, Kuntukurunku ne Kuntunamoa, Odds and Ends, baa me so, na mekae sɛ, O! Ɛdeɛn na ɛrekɔ so? na wɔkae sɛ, Wonnuee ɔbodan a yɛbɛda mu, na wo ne w'asase nyinaa nyɛ hwee (ma obiara). Ɛno nti, me nso, metoo Prae, the Broom. Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, Fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na kɔ kɔfa Odds and Ends. Odds and Ends bae. Wɔkae sɛ, 'Owura, na yɛwɔ ɔdan no atifi, na yɛredi agoru, na yɛhwee ase na yɛpiraa yɛn ho; ɛno nti na yɛkaa Maame-asase animtiaa, kae sɛ ɔyɛ tratraa. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, 'Agyinatufo, yɛate asɛm yi nyinaa, enti momma yɛnsɔre nkɔ dan no mu na yɛnhwɛ adanse no mu na yɛmmra mmɛbu atɛn.' Enti wɔsɔre kɔɔ dan no mu, na wɔtoo pon no mu. Ɛno nti, sɛ wokɔ ɔdan bi mu anadwo a, "sum" no, Ɔsoro-nyame ne n'Agyinatufo no na wɔresusuw ho wɔ hɔ.

M'asɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
