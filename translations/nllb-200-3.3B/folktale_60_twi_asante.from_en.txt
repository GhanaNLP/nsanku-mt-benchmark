Yɛmfa no aniberesɛm, yɛnka no aniberesɛm, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐN NA WƆDE "KURONTO" NE "BURU" BƐYƐ DƐN

BERE bi na oguadifo bi (a wɔfrɛ no) Kwaku ne ne yere (a wɔfrɛ no Buruwa) te hɔ, na esiane sɛ na wɔyɛ ahiafo nti, wotuu kwan sɛ wɔbɛkyekyere kurow foforo. Kwaku, oguadifo no, bere a otuu kwan kɔɔ kwae mu no, okum eku abere bi, nanso bere a ɔde no baa fie no, ɔkae sɛ, "Eyi nyɛ papa (ma aduan). " Enti ɔkɔtɔn. Bere biara a okum biribi no, na ɛte saa. Nanso ne yere anhu sika no da, na obisaa no sɛ, "Woamfa sika no amma, na woamfa nam no amma, na eyi nyinaa kyerɛ dɛn?" Obuae sɛ, "Sɛ mekɔ, na nkurɔfo ntɔ (honam no), na ɛporɔw a, metutu gu". Da koro ɔkum biribi, na ɔde too ɔkwan no so, na ne yere dii n'akyi hwɛɛ no. Afei ohuu sɛ watɔn saa bonsu no, na ɔfaa sika no, na ɔtɔɔ nsã a wɔfrɛ no dɔnto, na ɔnom. Afei ne yere san kɔɔ fie. Ɔduruu fie hɔ ara pɛ na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Adeɛ kyee, Buruwa". Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Ya Ekudonto (Yiw, Monkey for pot of palm wine)." Ɔbisaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woma me saa mmuaeɛ yi?" Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuaeɛ a ɛfata ma nea ɛwɔ w'adwenem no". Ɔkyerefoɔ no anka hwee. Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma daa adi no, ɔkɔɔ afuw no mu na ɔsan bae; ɔkae sɛ, "Anwummere pa". Obuae sɛ, "Ya Buru". Buruwa kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wubua (me nkyia) wɔ saa kwan yi so?" Oguanhwɛfo no buae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuae a ɛfata nea ɛwɔ w'adwenem no". Saa na ɛyɛe na ɛbɛyɛɛ amammerɛ wɔ abusuakuw no mu sɛ wɔde nkyia "Ya Kuronto" ne "Ya Buru" di dwuma. Wɔde wɔn mfitiase no ma oguanhwɛfo no, Kwaku, ne ne yere, Buruwa. Eyi ne m'asɛm a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
