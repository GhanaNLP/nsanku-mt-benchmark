Yɛmfa no aniberesɛm, yɛnka no aniberesɛm, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐN NA WƆDE "KURONTO" NE "BURU" BƐYƐ DƐN

BERE bi na oguadifo bi (a wɔfrɛ no) Kwaku ne ne yere (a wɔfrɛ no Buruwa) te hɔ, na esiane sɛ na wɔyɛ ahiafo nti, wotuu kwan sɛ wɔbɛkyekyere kurow foforo. Kwaku, oguadifo no, bere a ɔkɔɔ kwae mu no, okum eku aberewa bi, nanso bere a ɔde no brɛɛ fie no, ɔkae sɛ, "Eyi nyɛ (aduan) papa". Enti ɔkɔtɔn. Bere biara a okum biribi no, na ɛte saa. Nanso ne yere no anhu sika no da, na obisaa no sɛ, "Woamfa sika no amma, na woamfa nam no amma, na eyi nyinaa kyerɛ dɛn?" Obuae sɛ, "Sɛ mekɔ, na nkurɔfo ntɔ (honam no), na ɛporɔw a, metow gu". Afei ohui sɛ ɔtɔn saa bonsu no, na ɔde sika no kɔtɔɔ borɔdɔma nsã (a wɔfrɛ no dɔnto) kuruwa na ɔnom. Afei ne yere san kɔɔ fie. Ankyɛ na Kwaku, ɔdeduani no nso bae. Ɔduruu fie hɔ ara pɛ na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Adeɛ kyee, Buruwa". Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Ya Ekudonto (Yiw, Monkey for pot of palm wine)." Ɔbisaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woma me saa mmuaeɛ yi?" Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuaeɛ a ɛfata ma nea ɛwɔ w'adwenem no". Ɔkyerefoɔ no anka hwee. Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma no daa adi no, ɔkɔɔ afuw no mu na ɔsan bae; ɔkae sɛ, "Anwummere pa". Obuae sɛ, "Ya Buru". Buruwa kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wubua (me nkyia) wɔ saa kwan yi so?" Oguanhwɛfo no buae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuae a ɛfata nea ɛwɔ w'adwenem no". Saa na ɛyɛe na ɛbɛyɛɛ amammerɛ wɔ abusuakuw no mu sɛ wɔde nkyia "Ya Kuronto" ne "Ya Buru" no di dwuma. Wɔde wɔn fibea no kɔma ɔkyekyefo, Kwaku, ne ne yere, Buruwa. Eyi, m'asɛm a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
