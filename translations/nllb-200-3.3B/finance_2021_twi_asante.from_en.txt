ASƐM A ƐFI ƆSƐMFO A WƆTƆN A WƆTƆN A WƆTƆN A WƆTƆN A WƆTƆN A WƆTƆN A WƆTƆN A WƆTƆN A WƆTƆN A WƆTƆN A WƆTƆN

Ghanafo pa wɔ December 2020 mu no, wɔtoo aba maa Ɔmanpanyin Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo sɛ ɔnkɔ so nyɛ adwuma pa no na ɔnkɔ so nyɛ pii.

2021 Sikasɛm Ho Nhyehyɛe no yɛ sikasɛm ho nhyehyɛe titiriw a ɛboa ma yɛhyehyɛ yɛn manfo sikasɛm mu sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a yɛbɛbɔ nnipa nkwa ho ban, ama nnwuma a ɛrekɔ fam no anya ahoɔden, na yɛde yɛn ahoɔden nyinaa asi yɛn ani so sɛ yɛbɛsan akyekye Ghana ama ayɛ yiye wɔ COVID-19 akyi. Sikasɛm ho nhyehyɛe yi gyina yɛn anisoadehu a ɛne sɛ yɛbɛsakra ɔman yi na yɛanya sikasɛm a etumi gyina ne ho so no so.

Ɔfã kɛse a edi kan no mu no, yɛama sikasɛm no asan asi ne dedaw mu, denam nhyehyɛe ahorow a wɔahyehyɛ no yiye a ɛfa sikasɛm mu nsakrae ho no so, de nsunsuanso a ɛde sika ba no bae, de nhyehyɛe ahorow a ɛde nkɔso ba no bae, de mmoa maa ankorankoro, na wɔde nhyehyɛe ahorow a ɛfa nnipa asetena ho bae. Eduu afe 2019 awiei no, na yɛwɔ sikasɛm mu nsakrae kwan no so yiye wɔ sikasɛm mu nsɛnkyerɛne a ɛyɛ nwonwa no akyi, na wiase nyinaa gye toom sɛ ɛyɛ ɔman a ɛrenya nkɔso, bere a COVID-19 yare no kaa yɛn no. Ɔhaw a ɔyaredɔm yi de aba no aka wiase nyinaa sikasɛm, a Ghana ka ho.

Ɔhaw a COVID-19 yare no de bae no de ɔhaw pii baa yɛn nnwuma ne yɛn asetena so. Nanso, aban no de n'adwene sii ɔhaw a yare no de aba yɛn so no so, na wɔhyɛɛ mmara ahorow a ɛbɛboa ama nnwuma no asan afi ase na yɛn asetena asan ayɛ sɛ kan no. Anidaso biara nni hɔ, efisɛ yɛahu akwan foforo a yɛbɛfa so ayɛ nneɛma a ɛnyɛ yiye. Aban no asi ne bo sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ ama yɛanya nnipa a wɔwɔ apɔwmuden, na ɛno nti wayɛ nhyehyɛe ama Ghanafo abɔ wɔn ho ban sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a yɛbɛkɔ so anya ahoɔden de asi Ghana a yɛpɛ sɛ yehu no.

Yɛne mo ayɛ ayɔnkofa wɔ ahokyere nyinaa mu sɛ yɛn nkɔsoɔ kwan no fã, na yɛafa akokoɔduro asisi akwan de yɛn sikasɛm asan akɔ nkɔsoɔ kwan no so. Ghanafoɔ bɛyɛ ɔpepem 1.2 seesei tumi nya ntoasoɔ nwomasua a ɛyɛ papa na ɛyɛ papa wɔ wɔn asetena nyinaa mu, yɛasiesiee banking adwuma no, na wɔn a wɔtɔ sika no mu dodow no ara anya wɔn sika a wɔakora wɔ sikakorabea a asɛe no mu. Bio nso, yɛasi kar a wɔboaboa ano, na yɛahu kar a wɔboaboa ano a ɛdi kan wɔ yɛn aguadi mu ne akwan so.

Yɛde nokwaredi gye tom sɛ yehyiaa nsɛnnennen bere a na yɛrebɔ mmɔden sɛ yɛbɛma yɛn nkɔso ho botae ahorow no aba mu no. Aban no de anibere rebɔ mmɔden sɛ ebegyina saa nsɛnnennen yi ano na ɛde ahotɔ abrɛ nnwuma no. Sɛ nhwɛso no, yɛde GH¢750 million CAP-BuSS Nhyehyɛe no too gua wɔ May 2020 mu, na eyi ama mmoa abrɛ Micro, Small and Medium-Sized Enterprises (MSMEs) ne afoforo pii.

Sɛnea na ebi mmae da no, wɔfrɛɛ yɛn sɛ yɛnka yɛn ho na yɛmma yɛn man no nkɔso denam akokoduru akwan a yɛbɛfa so de adi dwuma ama obiara anya nkɔso no so.

Onyankopɔn nhyira yɛn nyinaa.

Ghana nkwa so!

Adwumayɛfo akatua: Eyi yɛ akatua ne akatua ne akatua ho mmoa te sɛ asetra mu ahobammɔ, akatua ne pɛnhyen a wotua ma aban adwumayɛfo.

Development Partners (DPs): Eyi gyina hɔ ma aman ne/anaa ahyehyɛde ahorow a wɔne aman a wɔrenya nkɔso bɔ mu yɛ adwuma de di wɔn botae ahorow a ɛfa nkɔso ho no ho dwuma. DPs betumi ayɛ nnipa bebree, te sɛ International Monetary Fund (IMF), World Bank, ne African Development Bank (AfDB), anaa nnipa baanu a aman bi te sɛ Germany, United Kingdom, ne United States of America (USA) ka ho.

Afe Awiei Nkɔanim: Nkɔanim a ɛbae wɔ afe no mu wɔ December mu.

ESLA: Energy Sector Levies Act.

Nkurɔfo a wotu wɔn fi baabi: Ɔkwan a wɔfa so tu nkurɔfo fi baabi.

FREE SHS: Free SHS (Free Senior High School) nhyehyɛe no yɛ Ghana aban nhyehyɛe a Ɔmampanyin Nana Akuffo-Addo aban no de too dwa wɔ Ɔpɛnimma afe 2017 mu a ɛbɔ SHS nhomasua ho ban ma JHS sukuuni biara a ɔwɔ nhomasua mu nimdeɛ.

Fiscal Responsibility Act: Eyi yɛ mmara a ɛkyerɛ sɛnea aban betumi de sika ne tow atoto nneɛma ano.

Ntoboa: Eyi yɛ sika a aban no nya fi wɔn a wɔboa wɔn ma wonya nkɔso no hɔ a ɛho nhia sɛ aban no tua.

Ntoboa a wɔde ma aban nnwuma afoforo: Eyi yɛ akatua a mmara hwehwɛ sɛ wɔde kɔ sika a wɔahyehyɛ ato hɔ te sɛ Ɔmantam Nhyiamu Fekuw (DACF), Akwan Hyehyɛde, Ghana Nhomasua Nkorabata (GETFUND), Petroleum Nkorabata ne Ɔman Apɔwmuden Fekuw no mu.

Ɔman mu Gross Domestic Product (GDP): Ɛkyerɛ nneɛma a ɔman no bɔ ho ka ne nneɛma a ɔman no bɔ ho ka nyinaa wɔ bere pɔtee bi mu.

Gross International Reserves: Ɛkyerɛ sɛnea ɔman bi betumi atɔ nneɛma ne nnwuma afi amannɔne aman so wɔ bere tiaa bi mu.

Sikasɛm mu botae: Eyi yɛ nneɛma a wɔde susuw sikasɛm mu dwumadi ho. Sikasɛm mu nkɔso, sikasɛm mu nkɔanim, nnwuma mu nsakrae, aman foforo a wɔne wɔn di nsie, sikasɛm mu nkonimdi anaa nkogu a aban de aba, ne gyinae a Ghana Sikakorabea no si a ɛka sikasɛm mu sika a wɔhwehwɛ ne nea wɔde ma ho.

Nhyehyɛe foforo: Eyi yɛ nhyehyɛe no fã bi a ɛwɔ afã horow a wɔde nsɛm a wɔnhwɛ kwan no nyinaa ka ho.

Akwan a wɔfa so yɛ ho adwuma: Eyi yɛ bere a wɔfa so yɛ ho adwuma de sɛe ɔhaw ne nsunsuanso a efi nsɛm a emu yɛ den mu ba no.
