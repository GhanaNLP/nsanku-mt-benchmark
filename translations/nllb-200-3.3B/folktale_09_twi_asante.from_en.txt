SƐN NA OKOTO, KRAB no, WƆAKYERƐ N'AKYERƐ, NA ABEREWA, ƆBAA KAKYERƐ no, WƆAKYERƐ N'AKYERƐ

Na ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ a ɔnyinsɛn, na sɛ ɔkɔ ne ba no nkyɛn a, ose: M'ani nnye ho. Ɔka sɛ: Wo ne w'abusua! Bere biara a ɔbɛsoma no akɔ no nkyɛn no, ose: M'ani nnye ho. Afei ɔka sɛ: Kɔ na kɔtow nsu brɛ me. Ɔka sɛ: M'ani nnye ho. Na ɔboroo no, na ɔkae sɛ: Twɛn na hwɛ! Na ɔka sɛ: Mɛkɔ makɔhwehwɛ, na mahwɛ. Na ɔfaa kwan no, na na ɔrekɔ ntɛmntɛm. Iridi, yiridi, yiridi. Ɔduruu akuraa bi ase no, ɔhunuu akwakoraa bi sɛ ɔte hɔ. Ɔhunuu no no, na ɔrebɛsan n'akyi. Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Me ba, nsan w'akyi efisɛ mahu wo". Na abofra no kɔɔ anim, na ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Dɛn nti na nsu afi w'ani so aguare wo saa?" Ɔkae sɛ: "Me ne me maame traa hɔ, da biara sɛ ɔsɔre sɔre a, ɔka sɛ: 'Twɛn ha ara na wubehu,' na ɔhwe me, enti mekae sɛ, mɛba abɛhwehwɛ 'mehu' na mahu sɛnea ɛte". Na ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Mene no". Ɛnna ɔkae sɛ: "Kɔ fie no akyi hɔ kɔhwe yam na fa brɛ me; na sɛ wokɔ hɔ a, nea ɔbɛka sɛ: 'Hwe me, hwe me.' Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Ho!" Nokorɛm no ɔkɔe hɔ; yams no bi ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Hwehwɛ me mu, hwehwɛ me mu, hwehwɛ me mu"; afoforo nso kaa sɛ, "Nhu me mu, nhwehwɛ me mu, nhwehwɛ me mu". Wɔn a wɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Nhu me mu" no ne wɔn a ɔhwehwɛɛ wɔn no. Na ɔne wɔn bae; na akwakoraa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Twitwa wɔn ho nhwi, na fa wɔn gu gya no so". Na ɔtwitwaa wɔn ho nhwi, na ɔde wɔn guu (ogya no so), na bere a wɔnoaa wɔn no, ɔdan wɔn guu mporoporowa so, na ɔde wɔn too akwakoraa no anim. Ɔkae sɛ: "Monkɔ kurow no ano, na monkɔhwɛ ɔpon a nam wɔ so, na monhwɛ mu, na momfa nea mopɛ". Na abofra no faa bi bae, na ɔde koguu ogya so, na ɔyɛɛ brodo, na ɔde too ɔbea no anim. Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Woawie anaa?" Obuae sɛ: "Yiw, Nana. " Ɔkae sɛ: "Bra na bɔ fufu no". Na ɔbea panyin no kɔdaa n'akyi; ɔkae sɛ: "Boaboa yam a wɔatoto no ano, hwie gu me hwene mu, na bɔ wɔn". Na abofra no kae sɛ: "Hwɛ ade a ɛyɛ nwonwa, sɛ yɛbɔ fufu wɔ obi hwene mu; eyi ne nea ne maame kaa ho asɛm no". Eyi nyinaa na ɔka wɔ ne tirim. Ɔkɔɔ na ɔbɔɔ; nanso bere a ɔbɔɔ no, ɔbɔɔ no brɛoo. Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Bɔ no dennen, sɛ woanyɛ no dennen a, wo koko bɛpae. " Na ɔbɔɔ no dennen, pi! pi! pi! na ɔwiei no, ɔhwiee sopa no guu so, na ɔde too hɔ maa ɔbaa panyin no. Ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ, "Me banana, ka me din na fa di". Na ɔsee sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ wo Ɔbaa-kwakoraa". Na ɔsee sɛ, "Yiw! onhuu ade, na obetumi adi". Na ɔbaa panyin no dii ne nyinaa. Na nnaawɔtwe mu no, ɔyɛɛ no saa daa. Na da koro no, abofra no kɔɔ asuten no mu kɔsesaw nsu. Kraman no huu no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na obi ba abrɛ sɛɛ yi?" Ɔkae sɛ, "Nanaaberewa bi wɔ hɔ a me ne no te, na sɛ mebɔ aduan na mede ma no a, ɔka sɛ, 'Ka me din na di'; meka sɛ, 'Wɔfrɛ wo Nanaaberewa,' na ɔka sɛ, 'Yoo! wonhuu ɔkwan a wɔfa so di,' na afei odi; ɛno nti na me so atew". Kraman no kae sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ no Nanaaberewa-Mosono". Na abofra no kae sɛ, "Ho". Na ɔde ne nsu kuruwa no sii ne ti so. Crab no kae sɛ, "Mma wo werɛ mmfi sɛ worekɔ no". Abofra no fii hɔ; bere biara a ɔbɛba no, na ɔtoa so ka sɛ, "Nkwakoraa-Mosono, Mosono, Mosono". Bere a oduu kurow no ano no, ne werɛ fii, na ɔhwee ase na ne nsu kuruwa no bubuu, na ɔsan kɔɔ fie. Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Ɛhe na nsu kuruwa no wɔ?" Ɔkae sɛ: "Bere a mereba no, mehintiw, na ebubui". Ɔkae sɛ: "Fa foforo kɔ". Na ɔfaa no kɔe; na Krab no kae sɛ: "Woasan aba dedaw?" Na ɔkae sɛ: "Saa din no a woka kyerɛɛ me no, makɔ na me werɛ afi, na maba sɛ merebebisa bio". Krab no kae sɛ: "Wɔfrɛ no Ɔbea panyin Mosono". Na ɔkae sɛ: "Ho". Ɔkɔɔ so kaa saa asɛm no wɔ kwan no nyinaa mu, na bere a oduu fie no, ɔde kuruwa no too fam. Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: Kɔ na kɔhwehwɛ yam na fa brɛ me. Ɔyɛɛ biribiara sɛ kan no, na ɔde aduan no brɛɛ ɔbaa panyin no, na ɔbaa panyin no kae sɛ: "Ka me din na di". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Wɔfrɛ wo Ɔbea panyin nanabea". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Meka me din ankasa". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Wo din ankasa ne sɛn?" Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Yiw". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Wɔfrɛ wo Ɔbea panyin Mosono". Ɔbea panyin no kae sɛ: "Fa na di". Na abofra no dii. Ɔbea panyin no sii kwan so, na ɔkae sɛ, "Merekɔhwehwɛ onipa a ɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ me din de Mosono no". Na ɔkɔɔ asuten no ho, na ohyiaa Obonto Ya, minnow no, (na ɔsɛee no, kae sɛ), "Wo 'Bonto, wo maame! Wo 'Bonto, w'agya! woaka akyerɛ m'abakan ketewa no sɛ wɔfrɛ me Mosono?" Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ me". Ɔbea panyin no huu sɛ Adwen (apataa no) gyina ne nkyɛn; ɔdomee no sɛ, "Wo Adwen, wo maame! Wo maame! Wo Adwen, w'agya! W'agya! woaka akyerɛ me mmanana ketewa sɛ wɔfrɛ me Mosono?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ me". Ɔhunuu sɛ Crab no gyina hɔ. Ɔdomee no sɛ, "Wo Crab, wo maame! maame! wo Crab, w'agya! agya! agya! wo na woka kyerɛɛ me mmanana ketewa no sɛ mete Accra, meyɛ ɔdehye, na wɔfrɛ me Mosono?" Crab no kaa sɛ, "Yiw, ɛyɛ me na meka kyerɛɛ no, na adɛn nti na woma obi ba so tew saa?" Na ɔbaa panin no bo fuwii, na ɔtwee ne calabash a na ɔkura no sii hɔ na ɔtow too Crab no. Ɛno nti na Crab no wɔ n'akyi no. Bere bi a atwam no na Crab no wɔ n'akyi no. Na Crab no nso faa dɔte fitaa, na ɔtow guu ɔbaa panyin no ti so. Ɛno nti na ɔbaa panyin no ti so nhwi yɛ fitaa no.

Eyi ne m'asɛm a meka kyerɛ mo. Sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, munnye emu bi ntom, na sɛ ɛnte saa a, munyi me ayɛ.
