ASƐM BƐN NA ƐBƐYƐ ƐBƐƐ SƐ NWANSANA, N'AKYERƐKYERƐ, BƐYƐ KRAMAN, N'AKYERƐKYERƐ, N'AKYERƐKYERƐ, KAM! KAM!

NÁ ƆKUKUFO bi wɔ nkraman anan; wɔfrɛ biako Sniff-sniff; wɔfrɛ biako nso Lick-lick; wɔfrɛ biako nso Tie-in-knots; na wɔfrɛ biako nso Gulp-down. Ná ɔtaa ne ne nkraman kɔhwehwɛ mmoa. Bere biara a ɔbɛkɔ kwae mu no, na ɔne wɔn nyinaa kɔ. Da bi ɔkɔɔ kwae mu na ohuu kola dua bi; na nnua no so nnua a ɛso aba te sɛ eyi. Afei ɔsan kɔɔ fie, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere no, sɛ, "Mehu kola dua bi, na ɛso aba gu so a ɛgu so wɔ mpuran saa". Ne yere no kae sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔboaboa ano, efisɛ wunim sɛ yenni kola dua biako mpo a yɛbɛka na yɛntɔn". Ɔkyekyerefo no sii kwan so, nanso ogyaw akraman no wɔ fie, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a kola dua no wɔ. Afei ɔforoo so, na ɔboaboaa kola nnua no bi ano. Afei ɔtee wɔ akyirikyiri hɔ sɛ, "Twa! be! kukuru! hu! pi!" Sasabonsam (gyinaa n'anim) bi. Ɔbisaa sɛ, "Hwan na ɔboaboaa me kola ntɛtɛ?" Odekumfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Me". Sasabonsam kaa sɛ, "Mekum wo seesei ara esiane nea woayɛ yi nti". Odekumfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wokum me a, ma mefrɛ me nkraman no ansa". Ɔkae sɛ, "Frɛ, na sɛ woabɔ mmɔden mprɛnsa sɛ wobɛfrɛ wɔn na wɔmmɛba a, mɛkum wo". Ɛnna odekumfoɔ no teɛɛ mu sɛ:

Wai, wai, wai, They are long in coming! N'ano nnya nnyinae, bere a nkraman no wɔ n'anim no. Ɔkyerefo no kae sɛ, "Sniff-sniff, ha yɛ Sasabonsam, hu no". Na Sniff-sniff hu no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Lick-lick, lick him over". Ɔkae sɛ, "Tie-in-knots, tie him in a knot". Tie-in-knots kyekyeree ne menewa mu; na Sasabonsam hwee ase wɔ hɔ, kum! Afei Gulp-down faa no kɔe na ɔmenee no. Ɔkyerefo no ne nkraman no boaboaa kola-nuts no ano de wɔn baa fie. Bere a ɔbɔmmɔfo no san bae no, ɔboaboaa nnuan no ano na ɔde maa akraman no. Ɔde nsu guu so na ɔde too hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn kɔdidii. Bere a odidii wiei no, ɔfirii adi kɔtenaa gyedua (dua) bi ase. Afei ɔbɔmmɔfo no akoa, Fly, kɔhwɛɛ aduan no (a na wɔasiesie ama akraman no), na ɔsoaa no de too ɔdan no atifi. Akraman no fi kwan so bae na ɔkɔm dee wɔn. Lick-lick ka kyerɛɛ wɔn a aka no sɛ, "Moahunu deɛ yɛayɛ ama yɛn wura, nanso seesei de, wamma yɛn aduane mpo a yɛbɛdi, enti momma yɛnkɔ na yɛnkum no". Wɔbuaa no sɛ, "Wonnidi atorɔ, wondi atorɔ". Lick-lick kɔɔ mmɔnten so kɔfee ɔdɔmmarima no amu, na ɔne ɔdɔmmarima no dii agorɔ; na Sniff-sniff nso bae; na ɔsɔɔ ɔdɔmmarima no amu mu; na ɔno nso kɔɔ akyirikyiri. Afei ankyɛ koraa na Tie-in-knots bae na ɔkyekyeree ne mene mu, na Gulp-down nso bae, na ɔtɔɔ no. Na akraman no san baa fie, na na wɔrebɛhwɛ pato no mu no, wohuu aduhuam wɔ hɔ. Wɔkae sɛ: Nsu no, ebia ɔdeduani no gyaw aduan maa yɛn na woadi bi. Nsu no kae sɛ: Minhu bi baabiara. Na Sniff-sniff no maa n'ani so huu aduan no sɛ ɛda hɔ (wɔ ɔdan no atifi). Ɔkae sɛ: Agya, yɛakum yɛn wura no kwa, na nokwarem no aduan bi wɔ hɔ a ɔde asie ama yɛn, na ɛnyɛ biako, na ɛnyɛ abien nso, Nsu no na ɔfaa no na ɔde too hɔ. Na Nsu no guan fii hɔ na ɔkɔe. Ɛno nti, sɛ ɔkraman te hɔ, na ntɛtɛ si ne so a, ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkyere no.

M'asɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
