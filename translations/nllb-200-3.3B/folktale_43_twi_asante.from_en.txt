Yɛmfa no aniberesɛm, yɛnka no aniberesɛm, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ASƐM BƐN NA ƐBƐYƐ ƐBƐƐ SƐ NWANSANA, N'AKYERƐKYERƐ, BƐYƐ KRAMAN, N'AKYERƐKYERƐ, N'AKYERƐKYERƐ, KAM! KAM!

NÁ ƆKUKUFO bi wɔ nkraman anan; wɔfrɛ biako Sniff-sniff; wɔfrɛ biako nso Lick-lick; wɔfrɛ biako nso Tie-in-knots; na wɔfrɛ biako nso Gulp-down. Ná ɔtaa ne ne nkraman kɔhwehwɛ mmoa. Bere biara a ɔbɛkɔ kwae mu no, na ɔne wɔn nyinaa kɔ. Da bi ɔkɔɔ kwae mu na ohuu kola dua bi; na nnua no so nnua a ɛso aba te sɛ eyi. Afei ɔsan kɔɔ fie, na ɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ ne yere, na ɔkae sɛ: "Mehunuu kola dua bi a ɛso aba asisi nnua mu te sɛ eyi". Ne yere kae sɛ: "Kɔ na kɔboaboa ano, efisɛ wunim sɛ yenni kola dua biako mpo a yebedidi na yɛrentɔn". Ɔkyekyerefo no sii kwan so, nanso ogyaw akraman no wɔ fie, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a kola dua no wɔ. Afei ɔforoo no, na ɔboaboaa kola-nuts no bi ano. Afei ɔtee wɔ hɔ sɛ, "Twa! be! kukuru! kukuru! hu! pi!" Sasabonsam (gyinaa n'anim). Ɔbisaa sɛ, "Hwan na ɔboaboaa me kola ntɛtɛ?" Odekumfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Me". Sasabonsam kaa sɛ, "Mekum wo seesei ara esiane nea woayɛ yi nti". Odekumfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wokum me a, ma mefrɛ me nkraman no ansa". Ɔkae sɛ, "Frɛ, na sɛ woabɔ mmɔden mprɛnsa sɛ wobɛfrɛ wɔn na wɔmmɛba a, mɛkum wo". Ɛnna odekumfoɔ no teɛɛ mu sɛ:

"Sniff-sniff, (ba na) hu me, Lick-lick, (ba na) hu me, Tie-in-knots, (ba na) hu me, Gulp-down, (ba na) hu me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔrebɛba akyɛ! N'ano nnya nnyinae, bere a na nkraman no wɔ n'anim no. Ɔkyerefo no kae sɛ, "Sniff-sniff, hwɛ Sasabonsam bi wɔ ha, hu no". Na Sniff-sniff hu no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Lick-lick, lick him over". Ɔkae sɛ, "Tie-in-knots, tie him in a knot". Tie-in-knots kyekyeree ne menewa mu; na Sasabonsam no hwee fam wɔ hɔ, kum! Afei Gulp-down faa no kɔe na ɔwee no. Oguanfo no ne akraman no boaboaa kola-nuts no ano de wɔn baa fie. Bere a oguanfo no san bae no, ɔboaboaa asaawa-nuts aduannuru a ayɛ ma ma ano, na ɔde too nkyɛn maa akraman no; ɔhwiee nsu guu so na ɔde too hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn kɔdidii. Ɔdidi wiei no, ɔfirii adi kɔɔ borɔn no so kɔtenaa gyedua (dua) bi ase. Afei ɔkyerefoɔ no akoa, Fly, kɔɔ hɔ na ɔhunuu aduane no (a wɔasiesie ama nkraman no), na ɔsoaa no de too ɔdan no atifi. Na nkraman no fi borɔn no so baa hɔ na ɔkɔm dee wɔn. Lick-lick ka kyerɛɛ wɔn a aka no sɛ, "Moahunu deɛ yɛayɛ ama yɛn wura no, nanso seesei de, wamma yɛn aduane mpo a yɛbɛdi, enti momma yɛnkɔ na yɛnkum no". Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Wonnidi atorɔ, wondi atorɔ". Lick-lick firii adi kɔɔ mmɔnten so kɔfee ɔdɔmmarima no amu, na ɔne ɔdɔmmarima no dii agorɔ; na Sniff-sniff nso bae; na ɔsɔɔ ɔdɔmmarima no amu mu; na ɔno nso kɔe. Afei ankyɛ na Tie-in-knots bae bɛkyekyeree ne kɔn mu, sen sɛ Gulp-down nso bae bɛkyekyeree no. Afei nkraman no san baa fie; na wɔrebɛhwɛ pato no mu, bere a wohuu sopa a ɛresosɔ wɔ hɔ no. Na bere a ɔhwɛɛ soro no, ohui sɛ aduan bi wɔ hɔ ma wɔn, na ɔkae sɛ: O me papa! Yɛakum yɛn wura kwa, na ampa ara aduan bi wɔ hɔ a ɔde maa yɛn, na ɛnyɛ biako, na ɛnyɛ abien, na Nkasama na ɔfaa na ɔde too hɔ". Nkasama no guan fii hɔ kɔe. Ɛno nti, sɛ ɔkraman bi te hɔ a, sɛ Nkasama bi si ne so a, ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkyere no.

M'asɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
