Yɛmfa no aniberesɛm, yɛnka no aniberesɛm, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ASƐM A ƐYƐ KYERƐKYERƐ

BERE bi na mmerante baasa bi wɔ hɔ; na wɔn nyinaa dɔ abeawa biako. Afei mmerante baasa yi fii Bontuku sɛ wɔrekɔ Cape Coast, na wɔka sɛ wɔrekɔ akɔtɔ nneɛma bi. Bere a woduu Cape Coast no, na wɔn mu biako wɔ ahwehwɛ bi. Ɔfaa ahwehwɛ no, na oyii (nsoromma) bi na ɔde guu ahwehwɛ no so, na ɔhwɛɛ mu. Ohui sɛ wɔn dɔfo no awu, na ɔda dan bi a wɔabue so mu. Ɔkae sɛ: "Anuanom, yɛn dɔfo awu". Wɔn mu biako kae sɛ: "Mɛma yɛadu Bontuku hɔ ntɛm ara a yebetumi akɔ akɔsie no". Nea ɔto so abien nso kae sɛ: "Sɛ wubetumi ama yɛadu hɔ a, mɛma wanyane no ntɛm ara". Enti obiako kɔfaa nnuru, na ɔde guu wɔn nkotodwe so, na wofii akwantu no ase. Bere a wɔduruu hɔ ara pɛ na obiako nso kɔfaa nnuru de guu kɔbere kotoku mu; na ɔfaa ne nantwi akyi de guu nnuru no mu, na ɔde guu owufo no so. Ɔkae sɛ: "Owufo, nyan; owufo, nyan!" na onyanee. Afei nnipa baasa yi mu hena na ɔyɛɛ yiye?
