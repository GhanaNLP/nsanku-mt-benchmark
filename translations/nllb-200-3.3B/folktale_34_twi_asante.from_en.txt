BERE bi na ɔbɔmmɔfo bi wɔ hɔ a sɛ ɔsɔre a, na ɔkɔ kwae mu kɔhwehwɛ mmoa a obekunkum wɔn, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obenya bi adi, na watɔn bi. Afei da bi ɔkɔɔ kwae no mu; ɔtee sɛ Kokotee, kwae-mprako no, refrɛ ne busuani, reka sɛ, "Kokotee Asamoa e!" Obuae sɛ, "Yiw, onua, yiw". Na (mprako a odi kan no) kae sɛ, "Ɛbere no adu sɛ yɛyɛ adwuma (wɔ yɛn mfuw mu), enti momma yɛnkɔ ɔdamfo no adwumadan mu, na ɔnwene dade no na ɔmfa nsɔ ano (yɛn nnwinnade a yɛde twitwa no), na sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ yɛtwa nnua bi a, yebetumi atwa no . "Bere a kwaakwaadabi no frɛɛ ne nuanom no, ɔbɔmmɔfo no de ne ho siee hɔ, na ɔtee wɔn nkɔmmɔ no nyinaa. Afei Bush-tweaseɛ a odi kan no nua bisaa ne nua Bush-tweaseɛ no sɛ, "Na nkuraa bɛn mu na yɛbɛkɔ akɔbɔ dadeɛ no?" Obuae sɛ, "Yɛbɛkɔ akuraa a wɔfrɛ no Asubɔnten no agya no". Na obisae sɛ, "Da bɛn?" Obuae sɛ, "Montae". Ɔkyekyerefo no tee nhyehyɛe yi nyinaa, na osiim sɛ ɔrekɔ fie. Afei, bere a ɔbaa fie no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔpanin a ɔwɔ Asubɔnten no Atɔe fam no nsɛm no, ɛne sɛ, nea na wate bere a na wakɔ kwae mu no. Na ɔbɔmmɔfo no ka kyerɛɛ akuraa no mu panyin no sɛ, "Ma mmofra no nkɔtwa nnua nkɔfa mmra, na sɛ, Dwoda no, kwaakwaadabi no sesa wɔn ho (bɛyɛ nnipa) na wɔba a, yɛbɛfa wɔn de wɔn akɔhyɛ nnua no mu. Na dadeyɛfoɔ no bisaa Ɔpanyin no sɛ, Na dadeɛ nkyea akɛseɛ yi nyinaa a woka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meyi no, ɛdeɛn na wode bɛyɛ wɔn? Na ɔkaa no nsɛm no, sɛdeɛ ɔdɔmfoɔ bi kɔɔ kwae mu, na ɔsan bae bɛbɔɔ amanneɛ sɛ Dwoda no mmoa bi bɛdan wɔn ho nnipa, na wɔabɛba ne dwetɛ dan mu abɛkye nnwinnade. Ɔdwumfo no tuu mmirika kɔfaa dade no ntɛm ara. Ɛberɛ a ɔpanin no de ne nsa guu nnua no so wiei ara pɛ, ɔhyɛɛ kuro no mu bɔfo no sɛ ɔmmɔ dade gong no, na ɔse, Ɔdwoda, sɛ ɛyɛ ɔbaa, anaa ɔbarima, ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara kɔ baabiara. Ɔdwoda bɛduruu, na anɔpa no, ɔbaa akwakoraa bi ka kyerɛɛ ɔbɔmmɔfo no sɛ, Kɔ na kɔpɛ tɔfe, nkyene, ne borɔdɔma, na fa baabi a mmoa no bɛyi wɔn ho nhwi na wɔde wɔn akɔto hɔ; woyɛ saa anaa? Enti, sɛ wokɔ hɔ a, fa aduaba no na fa sɔ ne mu, na sɛ mmoa no ba ha a, sɛ yɛkyere wɔn, na sɛ ebinom guan kɔfa wɔn honam kɔhyɛ no, sɛ aduaba no pira wɔn na wɔtow gu a, wɔbɛdan nnipa (san). Ɔkyerefo no kɔe na ɔde ne ho siee hɔ. Na ɔtee sɛ wɔrefrɛ, Kokotee Asamoa, Dwoda aba, ma yɛnkɔ. Enti wɔn nyinaa bae. Na wɔn mu biako a ɔyɛ afifide ho ɔyaresafo wɔ wɔn mu, na ɔfaa ne honam de kɔtoo baabi foforo. Na ɔdɔmfo no hwɛɛ wɔn, na ɔmaa wɔn kɔe. Ɔkɔfaa honam no nyinaa, na ɔde aduaba a wɔaboro no guu so, na ɔde guu asuten no mu, na nsu no faa wɔn kɔe. Na ɔkyekyerefoɔ no deɛ, ɔkyekyerefoɔ no anhu. Ɔkyekyerefoɔ no baa fie, na ɔpanin no nso frɛɛ nnipa a wɔboaboaa wɔn ho ano wɔ ɔkyekyerefoɔ no dan ho no, na ɔmaa wɔbaa ne fie. Bere a saa nnipa yi baa Ɔpanyin no fie no, wobisaa Ɔpanyin no se, "Adɛn nti na wofrɛɛ yɛn?" Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, "Moyɛ me nkurɔfo, na bere bi a atwam no muguan kɔtenaa baabi foforo; ɛnnɛ moasan aba, ɛno nti na meka sɛ mommra, efisɛ meremma mo nkɔ bio". Mmoa no ka kyerɛɛ Ɔpanyin no sɛ, "Yɛate nea woaka no, nanso yenim sɛ Ɔbɔadeɛ no hu" nter bae na ɔkaa yɛn ho nsɛm nyinaa kyerɛɛ mo, nanso ɛno ho nhia, yɛn ne mo bɛbom atena; nanso yenim sɛ nea moka no yɛ atoro; nanso ɛno ho nhia; yɛda saa ɔbɔmmɔfo no ase. Enti wo ne yɛn bɛbom atena; nanso ade biako bi wɔ hɔ a yɛmpene so, ɛne sɛ, wobɛda yɛn amammerɛ adi; anaa sɛ w'asomfo no mu biara bɛda yɛn amammerɛ adi; sɛ ɛba saa a, yɛbɛtetew mo abusuakuw yi mu, na yɛafi hɔ. Afei, bere a Ɔpanyin no kɔfrɛɛ wɔn no, ɔyaresafo no ne afoforo bi guanee. Na oduruyɛfo no kɔɔ sɛ ɔrekogye ne honam, na oguan; nanso wɔn a aka no san n'akyi, efisɛ wɔantumi anhu wɔn honam. Ɔpanyin no penee nsɛm a mmoa no de too hɔ no so, na wɔne mmoa no boom traa ase anigye mu. Afei na akuraa no mu mmarima waree mmoa no mu mmea no bi; wɔwoo mma. Da koro bi, na mmoa no mu biako ne wɔn a wɔte akuraa no ase no mu biako de wɔn nsa redi ako. Ɛno akyi no, akuraa no mu nipa no ka kyerɛɛ aboa no sɛ, "Fa wo ho fi hɔ, aboa a ɔte sɛ wo, a ɔka Bush-pork kuw no ho. " Ɔkaa saa no, na aboa no nyinaa ani ayɛ kɔkɔɔ, na wɔkɔɔ ɔpanin no fie kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Nea yɛaka ho asɛm akyerɛ wo no aba mu ankasa, enti dɛn na wobɛyɛ? " Ɔpanin no ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ wɔnkɔfrɛ nnipa no. Ɔpanyin no hwɛɛ asɛm no mu yiye, na ɔyɛɛ n'adwene sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mmoa no gyae asɛm no ka, efisɛ (ɔkae sɛ) bere tenten ni na wɔamma, na eyi ne bere a edi kan koraa a obi aka wɔn ho asɛm (wɔ wɔn fibea ho). Nanso mmoa no kae sɛ: "Yɛmmpene so". Na Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ: "Munntie, na mususuw sɛ nea onipa yi kae no yɛ atoro, monyɛ kwaakwaadabi anaa?" Na mmoa no kae sɛ: "Aane, yɛate". Ɛno akyi no, mmoa no ne Atɔe famfo no koe. Mmoa no sɛee akuraa no, kosii sɛ nnipa bɛyɛ du pɛ na wɔkaa hɔ. Na wɔsrɛɛ ahummɔborɔ, na wɔka kyerɛɛ mmoa no sɛ wɔn na wɔfata. Mmoa no tiee, na wɔkaa kyerɛɛ nnipa no sɛ, 'Asɛm a wɔaka dedaw no nyɛ den sɛ wɔbɛte ase, na sɛ wo ne yɛn bɛbom atena a, yɛremma obiara nnka yɛn fi a yefi bae no ho asɛm. Sɛ wokae anaa wokae bio a, ɛnde kyerɛ yɛn ɛkwan a yɛfaa so baa ha no. Ebia yɛbɛsan akɔ hɔ". Nea yɛayɛ no ama yɛn abusua no asɛe; yɛrenyɛ saa bio da. Enti wɔde odawuro gong no bɔɔ dawuru wɔ baguam sɛ ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara ka baabi a obi fi ho asɛm kyerɛ obi, anyɛ saa a, ɛbɛsɛe kurow no. Eyi ne m'asɛm a meka kyerɛ mo. Sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, munnye emu bi ntom, na sɛ ɛnte saa a, munyi me ayɛ.
