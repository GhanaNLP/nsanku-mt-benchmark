Yɛmfa no aniberesɛm, yɛnka no aniberesɛm, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

Sɛ obi sɛe biribi a ɛyɛ wo dea a, nnye ho akatua

WƆKA sɛ bere bi na mmammarima baanu bi a wɔn maame awu agyaw wɔn (koro) traa ase. Na wɔreyɛ afahyɛ bi, na mmammarima baanu no mu panyin (Ata 'Panin) faa nhene a ne bo yɛ den ne sika kɔkɔɔ nkongua de kyekyeree mmammarima baanu no mu akumaa, Ata Kuma, kɔn ho. Bere a na wɔrehohoro wɔn ho no, hama a wɔde sika kɔkɔɔ ahosiesie no sɛn Ata Kuma kɔn mu no mu twae, na ahosiesie no totɔe wɔ nsu no mu. Ɔsan baa fie bɛbɔɔ ne nuabea amanneɛ sɛ, "Nneɛma a wɔde sika kɔkɔɔ ayɛ no ayera". Ata 'Panin (abakan) kae sɛ, "Minnye nni sɛ ayera, na sɛ mpo na ayera a, biribi wɔ hɔ a mɛhwehwɛ afi wo hɔ". Ata Kuma (abakan kumaa) kae sɛ, "Dɛn na wobɛhwehwɛ afi me hɔ?" Ata Kuma kae sɛ: "Mate". Ata Kuma fii adi kɔhwehwɛɛ eyinom, na okoduu baabi bi a na dua biako wɔ. Ɛberɛ a dua no hunuu no no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Eyi ne Ata Kuma, (a nnipa ka sɛ) ɔfiri kuro no abɔnten so te sɛ osu a ɛrebɛtɔ". (Ata Kuma) kaa sɛ, "Nea enti a mebaa ha ara ne sɛ agya ba yɛ Ata 'Panin, na ɛna ba yɛ Ata 'Panin, na ɔhyehyɛɛ nhwiren ahorow de kyekyeree me kɔn mu, na ɛpaee na ɛyeraeɛ". (Ɛha Ata Kuma sɛee ne ba no mu) g):

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ afi dua bi a n'aba yɛ kratafa biako pɛ ho ade afi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ Aserewa anomaa no atɛwee.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ Nsu, na ɔrebɔ ponponponponponponsoa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ Asono Ɔhemmaa Maame no dua no, kosi ne ntini so.

Na dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ, agya, na manya bi?

Ɔka sɛ (ɛha yi) abofra ketewa bi, Akwasi Toboro Kojo babea.

Kojo Nsa.

Agya Elephant, ɛbɛyɛ papa sɛ wufi hɔ, ɛbɛyɛ papa sɛ wufi hɔ.

Kojo Nsa a a.

Na Single-leaf-on-a-tree se, Na ɛno nti na woresu, bra na gye biribi. Na Ata Kuma kɔe na ogyee (ahaban a na ahaban biako pɛ na ɛwɔ so), na ɔdaa no ase. Na ɔkɔe, na ohyiaa Aserewa anomaa bi. Na Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, Eyi ne Ata Kuma, a, sɛ ɔda adi wɔ kurow no ano a, obiara ka sɛ ɔte sɛ osu a ɛrebɔ ahome. Ata Kuma buae sɛ, Nea enti a mebaa ha ara ne sɛ agya no ba yɛ Ata Panin, na ɛna no ba yɛ Ata Panin, na ɔhyehyɛɛ nhwiren ahorow de kyekyeree me ho, na ebubui na ɛyerae. (Na Ata Kuma bubui wɔ ha. wɔ dwom mu):

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ afi dua bi a n'aba yɛ kratafa biako pɛ ho ade afi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ m'ade bi afi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ Aserewa anomaa no atɛwee.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ Nsu, na ɔrebɔ ponponponponponponsoa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ Agya Leopard nsateaa no.

Ɔka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrehwehwɛ Asono Ɔhemmaa Maame no dua no, kosi ne ntini so.

Na dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ, agya, na manya bi?

Ɔka sɛ (ɛha yi) abofra ketewa bi, Akwasi Toboro Kojo babea.

Kojo Nsa.

Agya Elephant, ɛbɛyɛ papa sɛ wufi hɔ, ɛbɛyɛ papa sɛ wufi hɔ.

Kojo Nsa a a.

Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, Ɛno nti na wusu, bra bɛgye biribi. Na Ata Kuma kɔ gyee (anomaa no atɛ) no, na ɔdaa no ase. Na ɔfirii hɔ, na ohyiaa Nsu, a na ɛrebɔ ponponponponponponpon. Ɔkaa sɛ, Eyi ne Ata Kuma, a, sɛ ɔda adi wɔ akuraa kwan no ano a, nnipa gye no sɛ osu a ɛrebɔ no. Ata Kuma kaa asɛm a na waka akyerɛ afoforo no kyerɛɛ no. Na Gurgling-water maa no bi (ne gurgling nsu no bi), na ɔfaa no toaa so. Akyiri yi, ohyiaa Akraman no. Bere a Leopard no huu no no, ɔkae sɛ, Eyi ne Ata Kuma; bere a oyii ne ho adi wɔ akuraa kwan no ano no, na ɔte sɛ osu a ɛrebɔ no. Ata Kuma ka kyerɛɛ no n'asɛm. Ɔkae sɛ, Bra gye biribi, (na ɔde ne nsateaa no biako maa no). Afei Ata Kuma kae sɛ, "Agya Leopard, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ na ama manya ɔsono Wurababea no dua no?" Ɔkae sɛ, "Ah, sɛ worekɔ a, mmoa no nyinaa bɛda hɔ, na ɔsono Wurababea no bɛda wɔn mfimfini, na sɛ worekɔ a, fa wo nan si wɔn so pintinn, na sɛ wodu ɔsono Wurababea no nkyɛn a, twa ne dua no, nanso sɛ wugyina wɔn so yiye a, wɔbɛtɔre wɔn ase. Ata Kuma kae sɛ, "Meda wo ase". Na bere a ɔrekɔ no, ohuu mmoa yi; ɔtiatiaa wɔn so denneennen, na oduu Ɔhemmaa Maame no nkyɛn. Otwaa ne dua, na ɔsan n'akyi. Wɔ kwan so no ɔtɔɔ ahwehwɛ, na ɔfaa (fie). Na oduu fie. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ata 'Panin, eyi ne nneɛma a wokae sɛ wubegye afi me nkyɛn no". Ata 'Panin kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka". Afei, da koro bi a na wɔte faako no, Ata Kuma faa ahwehwɛ no hwɛɛ mu. Ɔkae sɛ, "Saa ahwehwɛ yi, sɛ obi hwɛ mu a, ohu ɛna a ɔwɔ Ahonhom asase so no". Ata 'Panin kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ saa, fa no bra, na ma menhwɛ". Ɔde maa no. Bere a na ɔrebɛhwɛ mu no, eguan fii ne nsam, na ɛpaee. Ata Kuma kae sɛ: "Nea wode maa me yi, me nso mɛhwehwɛ afi wo hɔ". Ɛno akyi no Ata 'Panin kɔfaa nneɛma a Ata Kuma de brɛɛ no no. Ata Kuma kae sɛ: "Mempɛ eyi, nea mepɛ ne sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa foforo ama me". Ata 'Panin fii hɔ, na ɔkɔtoo dua bi a na ɛkura ahaban biako pɛ. Ɔkae sɛ: "Eyi ne Ata 'Panin, a sɛ ɔba akuraa no kwan ano a, wogye no fɛw so te sɛ osu a ɛrebɔ". Ata 'Panin kae sɛ: "Mede wo maame ne w'agya 'agye me fɛw so, ma me wo kratafa biako na ma menkɔ". (Nea) kratafa biako no kae sɛ: "Bra, nea onim (nea ɛsɛ sɛ ɔyɛ wo) wɔ hɔ". Ɔkɔɔ so kɔe, na ohyiaa Aserewa anomaa bi. Aserewa anomaa no kae sɛ, "Eyi ne Ata 'Panin; bere a oyii ne ho adi wɔ akuraa no abɔnten ano no, ne ho yɛ anika te sɛ osu". Ata 'Panin kae sɛ, "Bere a 'meyi me ho adi' kyerɛ wo maame ne w'agya no, me de, nea mepɛ ara ne mo moma no mu biako, na ma menkɔ". Aserewa anomaa no kae sɛ, "Kɔ so, nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi no) wɔ hɔ". Gurgling-water kae sɛ, "Eyi ne Ata 'Panin; sɛ oyi ne ho adi wɔ kurow no ano a, ɔte sɛ osu a ɛrebɔ ahome". Ata 'Panin kae sɛ, "Sɛ 'miyi me ho adi' kyerɛ wo maame a, sɛ 'miyi me ho adi' kyerɛ wo papa a, ma me wo nsu no bi na ma menkɔ". Gurgling-water kae sɛ, "Kɔ w'anim, nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi no) wɔ hɔ". Ɛno akyi no, ohyiaa Akraman no. Leopard no kae sɛ, "Eyi ne Ata 'Panin, a sɛ oyi ne ho adi wɔ abɔnten no ano a, ɛma ne ho tɔ no sɛ osu". Ata 'Panin buae sɛ, "Me 'yi me ho adi' kyerɛ wo maame! ne wo papa! Na me de, fa wo nsateaa no bi ma me, na ma menkɔ". Ɔse, "Obi a onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi no) wɔ hɔ". Ɔkɔe, na ohyiaa asono kuw bi, na Asono no Ɔhemmaa no da wɔn mfinimfini. Afei ɔkɔ twaa ne dua. Asono kuw no nyinaa kyeree no, na wɔtiatiaa no so, na owui. Ata Kuma hwehwɛɛ ne nuabea no wɔ ɔkwan no so. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Sɛ wo ne wo busuani bi te hɔ, na sɛ wɔn mu bi sɛe biribi a ɛyɛ wo dea a, nhwehwɛ ka, efisɛ Ata 'Panin yɛɛ saa, na owui".

M'asɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
