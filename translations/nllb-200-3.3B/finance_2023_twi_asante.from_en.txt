ASƐM A ƐDA SO

Akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ, afe yi bɛyɛ afe a emu yɛ den sen biara wɔ yɛn man no sikasɛm mu abakɔsɛm mu. Te sɛ wiase nyinaa no, COVID-19 yare no kaa yɛn wɔ afe 2020 adesuakuw a edi kan no mu, na ɛde sikasɛm mu dwumadi brɛɛ ase, na ɛmaa yɛn sika a yenya fii mu so tewee, na ɛsan de ɛka foforo a wɔansɛe wɔ yare no ho baa yɛn so.

Bere a na yɛn adwene yɛ yɛn sɛ yɛreyɛ yɛn ho yiye wɔ ɔhaw a yare no de aba yɛn so no mu bere a yɛrehyɛ ase de yɛn nsakrae nhyehyɛe no adi dwuma no, nsɛm a ɛsisii pii baa yɛn so a ɛmaa yɛn sikasɛm mu nkonimdi a yenyaa no fi afe 2017 kosi 2019 no sɛee. Nea edi kan no, na ɛyɛ Russia ne Ukraine akodi no, afei ɔkyere a wɔde kaa nnwinnade ho nhyehyɛe titiriw a ɛwɔ 2022 sikasɛm mu no, na ɛno na ɛmaa wɔsesaa yɛn sikasɛm ho nhyehyɛe no. Mpofirim ara, bere a edi kan wɔ mfe du mu no, wɔtoo Amanaman Ntam Sika Ho Dwumadi Ahorow no mu maa yɛn, ɔman no mu sikasɛm tebea yɛɛ den, na ɛka a wɔbɔe no nso yɛɛ kɛse, na ɛmaa ɛka a wɔtua no so yɛɛ kɛse, na cedi no so tew ntɛmntɛm.

Enti, bere a ahonyade ho mmoa a yenya fi sikasɛm mu ne yɛn nsrahwɛ a yenya fi amannɔne no so tewee no, yɛn ahonyade ho ka a ɛkɔɔ soro no fi yɛn ahonyade ho hia a yɛn nsa ntumi nka no sɛ ɔman, ne yɛn bosea ho ka a ɛrekɔ soro no, sɛee yɛn sika no. Eyi ama yɛn nyinaa, sɛ aban ne ankorankoro no, asetena bo ayɛ den. Nframa bo a ɛrekɔ soro no ama nneɛma ne nnwuma dodow no ara bo akɔ soro, na atiridii bo akɔ soro 40.4% wɔ October 2022 mu.

Wɔasɔ yɛn ahwɛ sɛ ɔmanfo, na aban no te nka kɛse wɔ mo ho wɔ nhyɛso a ɛmfata a eyi de aba no ho. Yɛpɛ sɛ yɛda yɛn aseda adi sɛ aban wɔ wɔn a wɔn ani gye ho, a wɔn mu bi ne mmarahyɛ baguam, adwumawuranom, adwumakuw, ɔmanfo, nyamesom ahyehyɛde ahorow, Ghana Adwumakuw,
