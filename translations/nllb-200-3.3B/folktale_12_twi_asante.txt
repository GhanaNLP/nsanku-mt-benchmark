How to Be Thankful for What You Have

KWAKU ANANSE and his children, Tikonokono, Afudotwedotwe, and Nyiwankonfwea, lived with Kotoko. Kotoko worked in the fields, and Kwaku Ananse laughed at him saying that he had eaten and drunk. He gave him some. When Kwaku Ananse and his children had eaten, Kotoko went to the house to eat. So Kotoko came, and Oko lifted his head and fell to the ground and sang to him: "Gyensaworowa, Kotoko saw saw saw saw, Gyensaworowa, Kotoko saw saw saw, Gyensaworowa". His ears stopped. Enrakaie heard. He opened a table and Kotoko hid his ears. Kwaku Ananse heard. He said, "This is what I've seen, tomorrow morning, I'll come and take my job. " Truly, the morning came, it came, it came, and Ananse ko faye; ode koo arim. Ananse 'doesn't know whether it's good for him to stop, and he kept on saying: "Gyenasaworowa, Kotoko saworowa, Gyenasaworowa, Kotoko saworowa, Gyenasaworowa". The ear stopped and went on. And Edo added to the fight. Then he said, 'O Goddess Pom'. The vineyard was called Aburokyire, and the tree fell, and we fell on it, some. This ear would produce Asantm's many ears. The ear was small, but Kotoko grew and ate some. I believe in this saying, that if it's not good, it's good, take some time, and take me with you.
