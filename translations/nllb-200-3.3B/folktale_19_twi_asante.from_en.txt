Yɛmfa no aniberesɛm, yɛnka no aniberesɛm, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

ASƐM BƐN NA ƐDE ƆHAW A WƆDE MAA MMOFRA NO

WƆKA sɛ (bere bi a atwam no) ɔkɔm kɛse bi bae, na Agya Ananse, a ɔyɛ Nkorɔmma no, ne ne yere Aso, ne ne mma, Ntikuma, Nyiwankonfwea (Abantiaa), Afudotwedotwe (Abantiaa), ne Tikonokono (Ɔpanyin-panyin), yɛɛ akuraa ketewa bi traa mu. Da biara na Nkorɔmma no kɔfa aduan, wuram yams, ba, na wɔnoa di. Afei da koro, Agya Ananse kɔɔ kwae no mu; ohui sɛ biribi a ɛyɛ fɛ gyina hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Aduan yi yɛ fɛ. Aduan no kaa sɛ, Me din nyɛ fɛ. Awɔ no kaa sɛ, Wɔfrɛ wo sɛn? Ɛbuaa sɛ, Wɔfrɛ me, Fill-up-some-and-eat. Awɔ no kaa sɛ, Fill up some so that I may see. Aduan no yɛɛ ma wɔ safiram-mfaso tɔfe, na Ananse dii ne nyinaa. Bere a owiei no, ɔbisaa aduan no sɛ, Dɛn ne wo taboo? Aduan no buaa sɛ, Mekyi tuo, ne kuruwa ketewa bi. Awɔ no faa aduan no kɔɔ fie, na ɔde sii ɔdan no atifi. Afei Ananse kɔɔ kwae mu kɔfaa aduan, na Aso, bere a owiee aduan no, ɔfrɛɛ Ananse, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Oh, wo na wuhia ankasa, me deɛ, meyɛ akwakoraa, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ wɔ aduan ho, wo ne mmofra yi de; sɛ w'ahome a, m'aso ayɛ dinn (fi wo sufrɛ no ho). Bere a wɔdidi wiei no, na Spider no atwa wɔ ntomadan no akyi, na ɔkɔtraa ɔdan no atifi. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Aduan yi yɛ fɛ". Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Me din nyɛ "fɛ". Ɔbisaa sɛ, "Wo din de sɛn?" Ɔbisaa sɛ, "Me din de, "Fill-up-some-and-eat". Ananse kaa sɛ, "Fill up some for me to see". Ɔfrɛɛ ne maame ne ne nuanom, na wɔkɔɔ ɔdan no atifi. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Aduan yi yɛ fɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Wɔmfrɛ me beautiful. Ɔkaa sɛ, Afei wɔfrɛ wo sɛn? Ɔkaa sɛ, Me din de Fill-up-some-and-eat. Ɔkaa sɛ, Fill-up up a little so that I may see. Na nnuane no yɛɛ ma kɔsii ne mfinimfini. Ntikuma ne ne maame ne ne nuanom didii kɔsii sɛ wɔn nyinaa bɛdidi wieeɛ. Na afei Ntikuma bisaa abodoo no sɛ, Dɛn na w'ani nnye ho? Abodoo no kaa sɛ, M'ani nnye atuo ne kuruwa ketewa bi ho. Ntikuma ka kyerɛɛ Afudotwedotwe sɛ, Kɔ na kɔfa bi brɛ me. Na ɔde brɛɛ wɔn, na ɔfaa atuo no de kaa (abodoo no), ne kuruwa ketewa no nso de kaa (abodoo no). Afei wɔn nyinaa sianee. Na Agya Spider (a ne nkyerɛase ne bere) asan aba (fi kwae no mu a ɔwɔ wuram yams no). Aso wiee wɔn ano. Wɔfrɛɛ Ananse; ɔbuaa sɛ, Ebia wontee nea mekae no; mekae sɛ, efi nnɛ, sɛ meba fie de aduane ba a, mobɛtumi adi, ɛfiri sɛ mo na mohia. Aso ne ne mma didii. Agya Spider hohoroo ne ho; ɔforoo ɔdan no atifi; ɔbisaa sɛ, Aduan yi yɛ fɛ. Kɔkɔyɛ koraa! Aduan yi yɛ fɛ. Kɔkɔyɛ koraa! Agya Spider kae sɛ, Ah! ɛbɛyɛ sɛ saa ntama yi nyɛ fɛ; mɛkɔ akɔfa Oyoko abusuakuo no tartan no bi aka ho de ahyɛ. Na ɔsianee sɛ ɔrekɔfa Oyoko abusuakuo no tartan ntama akɔhyɛ. Ɔhyɛɛ ne mpaboa; ɔforoo hɔ (san) bio. Ɔkaa sɛ, Aduan yi yɛ fɛ. Koma mu dinn! Aduan yi yɛ fɛ. Koma mu dinn! Ɔhwɛɛ dan no ho hyiae na ɔhunuu tuo bi ne kuruwa ketewa bi wɔ mu. Ananse kaa sɛ, Ɛnyɛ ade baako, ɛnyɛ nneɛma mmienu (ɛno ne sɛ, akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho), ɛyɛ Ntikuma. Ananse bubuu aduane no, na ɔbaa fam; ɔyii Oyoko tartan ntama no de too hɔ, na ɔfirii hɔ kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu. Bere a na ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu biribi a ɛyɛ fɛ paa (a wɔfrɛ no) Mpere, ɔtwea no, na ɛgu hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Oh, ɛyɛ nwanwa! eyi yɛ fɛ sen nea edi kan no; mpire yi yɛ fɛ. Mpire no kae sɛ, Wɔmfrɛ me fɛ. Ɔ (Ɔtweaseɛ no) kae sɛ, Afei wɔfrɛ wo dɛn? Ɛkae sɛ, Wɔfrɛ me Abiridiabrada (a ne nkyerɛase ne sɛ, Swish-and-raise-weals). Na ɔkae sɛ, Swish kakra ma minhu. Na mpire no bɛhwee no so, Swish, swish, swish. Agya Spider teɛteɛɛm sɛ, Pui! pui! Anomaa bi (a ɔte bɛn no) ka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, Ka sɛ Adwobere (a ne nkyerɛase ne sɛ, ɛyɛ fɛ na ɛyɛ mmerɛw-seesei). Na Ananse kae sɛ, Adwobere. Na mpire no gyaee sɛ ɛreboro no. Na Ananse de saa mpire yi baa fie; na ɔkɔ de too ɔdan no atifi. Aso wiee aduane no, ɔsee, "Ananse, bra bɛdidi". Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Esiane sɛ wowɔ ha (asase so) nti, ebia w'aso no nni ntokuru (na wonte nea meka no) nti, merenni". Ananse foro kɔɔ soro kɔtenaa ase dinn. Afei ɔsan baa fam bio; na afei ɔsan kɔhyɛɛ baabi siee ne ho. Afei Ntikuma foro kɔɔ soro. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, saa agya (me papa) no de biribi aba bio". Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Ɔba, Nyiwankonfwea, Afudotwedotwe, bra ha, ɛfiri sɛ deɛ agya de aba (ɛberɛ yi) no kyɛn deɛ ɛtwa toɔ no koraa". Afei wɔn nyinaa foro kɔɔ soro. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Saa ade yi yɛ fɛ". Ɛbuaa sɛ, "Wɔmfrɛ me 'fɛ.' " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo din de sɛn?" Ɛbuaa sɛ, "Wɔfrɛ me 'Swish-and-raise-weals.' " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Swish kakra ma minhu". Na saa mpire no sii no so na ɔhwee wɔn pasaa. Ananse gyinaa nkyɛn, na ɔteɛɛm sɛ, "Momfa no, momfa no, ne titiriw no Ntikuma, momfa no". Afei, bere a Ananse hwɛɛ na ohui sɛ wɔabɔ wɔn mmaa yiye no, ɔkae sɛ, "Adwobere" (Cool-and-easy-now). Ananse bae na ɔfaa mpire no na ɔtwaa no mu nketenkete na ɔhwetee. Ɛno na ɛmaa mpire no baa abusuakuw no mu. Enti ɛba sɛ woka biribi kyerɛ wo ba, na wantie wo a, yɛhwe no.

M'asɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
