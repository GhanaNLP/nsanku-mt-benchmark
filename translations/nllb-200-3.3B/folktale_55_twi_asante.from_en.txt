Yɛmfa no aniberesɛm, yɛnka no aniberesɛm, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐN NA SPEAR-GRASS BƐYƐ AMAN no Mu

Ɔkɔm kɛse bi baa Ntikuma so, na ɔkae sɛ ɔrebɛhwehwɛ aduan de akɔma n'agya, Agya Ananse, a ɔyɛ Nkorɔnoma no. Ofii hɔ kɔɔ akuraa bi ase; (ɛhɔ) ohyiaa Ɔpanyin-baa-nananom. Ɔbea panyin no see no sɛ, Me banana, adɛn nti na, sɛ obiara ntumi mma ha a, woaba ha? Ntikuma buae sɛ, Ɛyɛ ɔkɔm nti, na mebae sɛ merebɛhwehwɛ biribi a mɛkɔ adi. Ɔbea panyin nana no kae sɛ, Kɔ m'afuw a ɛwɔ hɔ no mu, nanso yam a ɛbɛka sɛ, Twe me, twe me, nntwe no, nanso yam a ɛbɛka sɛ, Nntwe me, ntwe me, twe na fa no brɛ me. Ntikuma fii hɔ kɔe. Na Ntikuma twitwaa wɔn a wɔkae sɛ: "Monntwe me, montwe me" no. Ɔde wɔn baa fie; ɔworɔw wɔn. Afei Ntikuma nanabaa no kae sɛ: "Montow nea ɛyɛ nokware no nkyene, na momfa nhwi no ngu gya mu". Ntikuma twitwaa wɔn na ɔde nhwi no guu gya mu. Bere a na wɔasiesie wɔn ho no, oyii wɔn fii gya no mu, na nea ɛwɔ gya no mu no nyinaa danee nnuan pa. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Mawie, nanso ma me nam a mede bɛgu mu. Ɔpanyin-nananom kaa sɛ, Kɔ na kɔka kyerɛ Nkwakuo, Nkwan a ɛwɔ dua no so, sɛ Ɔpanyin-nananom se, mo nyinaa yɛ mo nanom! Nkwan no kaa sɛ, Sɛ ɛnyɛ Ɔpanyin-nanom a, anka yɛbɛbɔ mo. Ɔpanyin-nanom kaa sɛ, Sɛ me deɛ a, ɛnnyɛ m'adwuma, na mobɛtumi aba abɛbɔ no. Mmoa no sianee sɛ wɔrebɛbɔ no. Ɔbea-kwakoraa-nananom kaa sɛ, Me Sharp-kyi, twa mmoa yi ti ma me. Afei The-sharp-one twaa mmoa no ti. Ntikuma boaboaa mmoa no nyinaa ti ano, na ɔboaboaa yams bi nso ano, na ɔde wɔn nyinaa kɔɔ ne akuraa no ase. Agya Spider huu (ne ba) Ntikuma gyina n'anim, na ɔkae sɛ, Mekyea wo wɔ w'abae no ho, ɔsɛee ant-bepɔw no (i.e. abakan). Ɔtoaa so sɛ, Ma mensoa wo wɔ m'akyi. Ntikuma kɔɔ hɔ kɔsɛn hɔ. Afei Ananse kɔɔ na ɔyii yam baako; ɔkaa sɛ, Bere a m'awo aba me bosome mu no, mɛdi eyi. (Ne yere) Aso kaa sɛ, Efi bere a Ɔbɔadeɛ bɔɔ nneɛma no, woahu ɔbarima bi a wanya awo? Ananse ne ne yere, ne ne mma wiee yam no di, na nea aka ara ne nea ɛyɛ Agya Spider dea. Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma no daa adi no, Ananse kae sɛ, Manya me awo. Na ɔkɔtraa fie no akyi, na ɔnoaa yam no, na odii, kosii sɛ ne nyinaa wiee. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Mɛsan akɔ. Ananse kaa sɛ, Me ne wo bɛkɔ (bom). Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Me ne wo renkɔ. Bere a Ntikuma kɔdae no, Ananse kɔfaa odwanhwɛfoɔ nwoma kotoku bi, na ɔde ntokuru nketewa hyɛɛ no mu, na ɔde nsõ hyɛɛ no mu, na ɔde kyekyeree Ntikuma atadeɛ no ano. Bere a nneɛma no bɛdaa adi no, Ntikuma sii kwan no so. Bere a (sunsum a na ɛkata nneɛma no) anim tetewee no, Ananse hwehwɛɛ Ntikuma sɛnea ɔpɛ, antumi anhu no, na afei ɔkɔhwehwɛɛ baabi a nsõ no atɔ. Bere a ɔhunuu wɔn no, odii wɔn akyi, na ɔrekɔ no, na Ntikuma ne Ɔpanyin-baa-nananom te hɔ. Ɔpanyin-baa-nananom kaa sɛ, Agya barima, ɛdeɛn ne w'adwuma? Ananse buae sɛ, Ɔkɔm ato me, enti ma me biribi nni. Ɔpanyin-baa-nananom kaa sɛ, Ɛhe na ɛsɛ sɛ minya bi? Ananse buae sɛ, Wogye di sɛ meyɛ ɔkwasea, mpo me ba, Ntikuma, bae, na woama no bi. Ɔpanyin-baa-nananom kaa sɛ, Kɔ afuw a ɛwɔ hɔ no mu; yams a ɛka sɛ, Twe me, twe me, ntwe wɔn, nanso wɔn a wɔka sɛ, ntwe me, ntwe me, twe wɔn. Ɔtweaseɛ (buae sɛ), Ah! Ɔkwasea adwuma bɛn na wo ka kyerɛ me, sɛ obi ka sɛ ɔdɔ obi a, wo hyɛ me sɛ menka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mekyi no, mentee biribi a ɛte saa da? Nanso Spider no kɔɔ afuw no mu. Bere a oduu hɔ no, yams no bi kae sɛ, Twe me, twe me, ebinom nso kae sɛ, Ntwe me, ntwe me. Na ɔtwee wɔn a wɔkae sɛ, Twe me, twe me, no de wɔn baa fie. Ɔbae na ɔwiee wɔn hohoro; Ɔpanyin-aberewa-nananom kae sɛ, Kɔ na tow aduan a eye no gu, na fa nhwi no, na fa gu adaka no mu, na to gya mu. Ananse buae sɛ, Ah, nsɛm a ɛyɛ dɛ bɛn na woka kyerɛ me yi; esiane sɛ Ɔbɔadeɛ no na ɔbɔɔ nneɛma nti, so yɛtow aduan a eye no gu, na yɛde nkekae no to hɔ? Ɔtweaseɛ no faa aduan a eye no, na ɔde too gya mu, nanso ɔfaa ne honam no fã bi de guu so. Bere a aduan no tɔe no, oyii fii hɔ, na na ne nyinaa adan abo, nanso na aboa no a ɔde too so no adan aduan pa. Agya Spider kae sɛ, "Ah! Ɔkwadwofo yi a wɔsan de no baa nkwa mu no nni atoro". Na Nkwadwofo no kɔe kɔsesaw nhwi no de bae na ɔde guu ogya no mu. Bere a aduan no tɔe no, oyii fii ogya no mu, na na adan yams a wɔatete no yiye sɛnea w'ani begye ho sɛ wubehu no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Agyabea, eyi nyinaa ye, nanso afei ɛsɛ sɛ woma me nam de ka ho". Agyabea panyin no kae sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔhwɛ dua no, Mmoa wɔ so; sɛ wokɔ a, wobɛka sɛ, 'Moyɛ mmoa.' " Akoekoe no kae sɛ, "Ɔbea yi! Me ne Mmoa no nni asɛm, na adɛn nti na ɛsɛ sɛ mekɔ na me ne wɔn di atirimɔdensɛm saa kwan yi so?" Agyabea panyin no kae sɛ, "Kɔ, na sɛ ɔhaw bi ba a, ɛyɛ me asɛm". Akoekoe no kɔe. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abɔfoɔ, mo a mowɔ nnua yi so, Nana Nana se moyɛ mmoa". Mmoa no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ Nana Nana a anka yɛbɛbɔ mo". Nana Nana Nana no kaa sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ mommra mmɛbɔ no".

Ananse kaa sɛ, "Agyabaa, ɛno nyɛ papa". Afei mmoa no hwehwɛɛ no na wɔbaa sɛ wɔrebɛbɔ Ananse. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Me sekan no, The-sharp-one, twa wɔn ti". Tintin! (na ɛno ne nne a sekan no de yɛɛ adwuma). Ɔpanyin no nanabaa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmerɛ na ɛyɛ mmerɛ seesei". Nanso Akɔnkɔnkɔn no antie no. Afei, Akɔnkɔn no boaboaa nam no ano na ɔsan baa fie, na ɔbaa no twee ne ho kakra. Afei Ananse wiaa ne sekan no de hyɛɛ ne kotoku mu, na otuu mmirika san baa ne akuraa no mu. Bere a ɔsan kɔɔ ne kurom no, ɔka kyerɛɛ (ne babarima no) sɛ, "Ntikuma, esiane sekan yi a mede me nsa ato so yi nti, ma yɛnkɔ kwae no mu". Enti wɔkɔɔ, na wɔkɔhunuu Wild Pigs bi. Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ntikuma, kɔ na kɔhaw wɔn. Ntikuma kɔɔ ne nkyɛn kɔkaa sɛ, "Bush-pigs, mo ho ntew". Bush-pigs no buae sɛ, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ sɛ woyɛ Kwaku Ananse, Spider ba no a, anka yɛbɛba abɛhwe wo". Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Bra abɛhwe no ɔkwan biara so, ɔyɛ nipa bɔne koraa, n'ahoɔfɛ nyɛ anigyede". Na Bush-pigs no rebɛhwe no, bere a Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Otumfoɔ bi sekan, The-sharp-one, twa wɔn ti ma me". Oh! na sekan no bɛhwee wɔn so, na otwaa mmoa no ti nyinaa. Ɛno akyi no, Kwaku Ananse fii ase twitwaa nnua no. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ei! ɛdeɛn na woreyɛ yi? Mekae sɛ twa mmoa no ti, na wowiei no, na afei eyi nyinaa yɛ dɛn?" Afei Ntikuma hwɛɛ kakra, na afei ɔkae sɛ, "Afei yɛ no brɛoo". Kwaku Ananse antie no, na Knife no baa fam, na Ananse kɔfaa. Wɔsan kɔɔ fie. Da a edi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma no bɛdaa adi no, Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ wɔbɛkɔ ɔsoro-nyame no nkyɛn wɔ hɔ. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Agya, sɛ ɛyɛ Knife yi nti a, nkɔ. Ananse kaa sɛ, fi hɔ kɔ. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, kɔ w'ankasa kwan so. Ananse fii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ Ɔsoro-nyame hɔ, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Owura, ɛnnɛ, sɛ obi ayɛ wo bɔne a, me nko ara, me Kwaku Ananse, mɛtwa wɔn nyinaa ti da koro na mede ama wo. Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, Nhyira wɔ hɔ ma obiara a obetumi ayɛ saa, efisɛ atamfo dɔm kɛse bi gyina kurow no akyi, enti kɔ. Ananse kaa sɛ, Ma wɔnom bobesa ne oguan biako. Ɛhɔ ara na wɔde bae. Ananse fii kwan no so; Ananse bisaa ɔtamfo asraafo no sɛ, Mo na moka sɛ mobɛko atia Ɔsoro-nyame no? Wobuae sɛ, Yiw, ɛno na yɛreka. Afei na Ɔsoro-nyame no asraafo gyina Ananse akyi. Afei Kwaku Ananse ka kyerɛɛ Ɔpanyin no Knife, The-sharp-one sɛ, Twa ɔtamfo asraafo yi ti ma me. Na Knife no twitwaa wɔn ti, tan! tan! na ɛwiee. Ananse kae sɛ, Eye, gyae. Nanso Knife no bɔɔ Ɔsoro-nyame no asraafo no na na ɔretwa (wɔn ti). Ananse kae sɛ, Oh, ɛno ne Ɔsoro-nyame no asraafo, enti gyae. Nanso twa. Ananse kaa sɛ, Sɛ w'adwenem ayɛ wusiwusi a, ma ɛnnyɛ wusiwusi. Oh! Ɔsoro-nyame no asraafodɔm no nyinaa ayera. Seesei Knife no twaa mframa mu; ɛbaa baabi a Kwaku Ananse wɔ no. Ɔkae sɛ, Hwɛ yiye, wunnim me? Wonnim me? Mene Kwaku Ananse, me na mede wo fii Ɔpanyin no hɔ. Oh! Seesei Kwaku Ananse ti tɔree, tan! Na Knife no kɔɔ so ara; na baabi a egyaee no dan wura bi, a yɛfrɛ no Etene (tankɔ-nwura); ne ano yɛ nnam paa.

M'asɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
