Yɛmfa no aniberesɛm, yɛnka no aniberesɛm, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐN NA SPEAR-GRASS BƐYƐ AMAN no Mu

Ɔkɔm kɛse bi baa Ntikuma so, na ɔkae sɛ ɔrekɔhwehwɛ aduan de akɔma n'agya, Agya Ananse, a ɔyɛ Nkorɔmfo no. Ɔkɔtraa akuraa bi ase; (ɛhɔ) ohyiaa Ɔpanyin-baa-nananom. Ɔpanyin no see no sɛ, Me banana, adɛn nti na, sɛ obiara ntumi mma ha a, woaba ha? Ntikuma buae sɛ, Esiane ɔkɔm nti, na mebae sɛ merebɛhwehwɛ biribi a mɛkɔ adi. Ɔpanyin no nanabea kae sɛ, Kɔ m'afuw a ɛwɔ hɔ no so, nanso yam a ɛbɛka sɛ, Twe me, twe me, nntwe no, nanso yam a ɛbɛka sɛ, Nntwe me, ntwe me, twe no na fa brɛ me Ntikuma fii ase. Yams no bi kae sɛ, Twe me, twe me; ebinom nso kae sɛ, Ntwe me, ntwe me. Afei Ntikuma twitwaa wɔn a wɔkae sɛ, Ntwe me, ntwe me no. Ɔde wɔn baa fie, na oyii wɔn. Afei Ɔbaa-Nkwakoraa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Tow aduan no fã a ɛyɛ nokware no gu, na fa nhwi no gu adaka no mu na fa gu gya no so". Ntikuma twitwaa wɔn ho na ɔfaa nhwi no de guu gya no so. Bere a wɔasiesie wɔn ho no, oyii wɔn fii gya no mu, na ɔdan adaka no mu nneɛma, na biribiara (a ɛwɔ mu no) adan aduan pa. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Mawie, nanso ma me nam a mede bɛgu mu". Nkwakoraa nanabaa kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔka kyerɛ Nkwakuo, Nkwan a ɛwɔ dua no so no sɛ, "Nkwakoraa nanabaa se, mo nyinaa yɛ mo nanom!" Nkwan no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ Nkwakoraa nanabaa a, anka yɛbɛbɔ mo". Na Ntikuma boaboaa mmoa no nyinaa ano, na ɔtwitwaa yams bi kaa ho, na ɔde wɔn nyinaa kɔɔ ne akuraa no ase. Agya Spider huu (ne ba) Ntikuma sɛ ogyina n'anim, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Mekyea wo wɔ w'aba a woreba no ho, wo a woabrɛ ant-hill (i.e. abakan). Ɔtoaa so sɛ, Ma mensoa wo wɔ m'akyi. Ntikuma kɔɔ hɔ kɔsɛn hɔ. Afei Ananse kɔfaa yams biako; ɔkae sɛ, Bere a menya m'asram mu nna no, mɛwe eyi. (Ne yere) Aso kae sɛ, Efi bere a Ɔbɔadeɛ no bɔɔ nneɛma no, woahu ɔbarima bi a wanya ɔsram mu nna pɛn? Ananse ne ne yere, ne ne mma wiee sɛ wɔwe yams no, na nea aka ara ne nea na ɛyɛ Agya Spider de no. Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma no daa adi no, Ananse kae sɛ, Manya me ɔsram. Na ɔkɔtraa fie no akyi, na ɔnoaa yam no, na odii, kosii sɛ (ne nyinaa) wiee. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Mɛsan akɔ. Ananse kaa sɛ, Me ne wo nyinaa bɛkɔ (bom). Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Me ne wo nyinaa renkɔ. Bere a Ntikuma kɔdae no, Ananse kɔfaa odwumayɛni nhoma kotoku, na ɔde ntokuru nketewa hyɛɛ mu, na ɔde nsõ hyɛɛ mu, na ɔde kyekyeree Ntikuma atade ano. Bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, Ntikuma fii akwantu no ase ntɛm pa ara. Bere a esum a ɛkata nneɛma no anim tetewee no, Ananse hwehwɛɛ Ntikuma sɛnea ɔpɛ, nanso wanhu no, afei ɔkɔhwehwɛɛ nsõ no. Bere a ohuu wɔn no, odii n'anammɔn akyi, na ɔkɔe, bere a na Ntikuma ne ne nanabea no te hɔ no. Ɔbea-kwakoraa no kae sɛ, Agya barima, ɛdeɛn ne w'adwuma? Ananse buae sɛ, Ɔkɔm akyekyere me, enti ma me biribi nni. Ɔbea-kwakoraa no kae sɛ, Ɛhe na ɛsɛ sɛ minya bi? Ananse buae sɛ, Wususuw sɛ meyɛ ɔkwasea, mpo me ba, Ntikuma, bae, na wode bi maa no. Ɔbea-kwakoraa no kae sɛ, Kɔ afuw a ɛwɔ hɔ no mu; yams a ɛka sɛ, Twe me, twe me mmɛtwe wɔn, nanso wɔn a wɔka sɛ, mmfa me nto hɔ, mmfa me nto hɔ, twe wɔn. Spider (buaa sɛ), Ah! nkwasea adwuma bɛn na eyi yɛ wo ka kyerɛ me, sɛ obi ka sɛ ɔdɔ obi a, woka kyerɛ me sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mekyi no, mentee biribi a ɛte saa da? Nanso Spider kɔɔ afuw no mu. Ɔduruu hɔ no, yams no bi kaa sɛ, Twe me, twe me, bi nso kaa sɛ, Ntwe me, ntwe me. Na ɔtwee wɔn a wɔkaa sɛ, Twe me, twe me, no, na ɔne wɔn kɔɔ fie. Ɔbae na ɔwiee wɔn hohoroo; Ɔpanyin-baa-nananom kaa sɛ, Kɔ na to aduan pa no fã no gu, na fa nhwi no, na fa gu adaka no mu, na to gya no so. Ananse buae sɛ, Ah, kasa bɔne bɛn ni a woka kyerɛ me yi; esiane sɛ Ɔbɔadeɛ no na ɔbɔɔ nneɛma nti, so yɛtow aduan pa no gu, na yɛkora nkata no so? Nkwansena no faa aduan pa no fã, na ɔde too gya no so, nanso ɔfaa ne honam no bi na ɔde guu so. Bere a aduan no tɔe no, ɔyii no, na na ne nyinaa adan abo, nanso honam no a ɔde guu so no adan aduan pa. Agya Spider kae sɛ, "Eh! ɔkwadwofo yi a ɔsan baa nkwa mu no nni atoro (ɛno akyi nyinaa). " Na Nkwadwofo no kɔe kɔsesaw ne honam no de brɛɛ no na ɔde guu gya no so. Bere a aduan no tɔe no, oyii fii ogya no mu, na ɛdan aduaba a ɛyɛ dɛ sɛnea w'ani begye ho sɛ wubehu no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Agyabea panyin, eyi nyinaa ye, nanso afei de ɛsɛ sɛ wode nam ma me na mede ka ho". Agyabea panyin no kae sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔhwɛ dua no, Mmoa wɔ so; sɛ wokɔ a, wobɛka sɛ, 'Moyɛ mmoa.' " Akoekoe no kae sɛ, "Ɔbea yi! Me ne Ntontom no nni asɛm, na adɛn nti na ɛsɛ sɛ mekɔ na me ne wɔn di atirimɔdensɛm saa kwan yi so?" Aberewa nanabea no kae sɛ, "Kɔ, na sɛ ɔhaw bi ba a, ɛyɛ m'asɛm". Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abɔfoɔ, mo a mowɔ nnua yi so, Nana Nana se moyɛ mmoa". Mmoa no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ Nana Nana a anka yɛbɛbɔ mo". Nana Nana Nana no kaa sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ mommra mmɛbɔ no".

Ananse kaa sɛ, "Agyabaa, ɛno nyɛ papa". Afei mmoa no bɛhwee no so na wɔbaa sɛ wɔrebɛbɔ Ananse. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Me sekan no, nea ne ho yɛ nnam no, twa wɔn ti". Tintin! (na ɛno ne nne a sekan no de yɛɛ adwuma). Ɔpanyin no nanabaa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmerɛ na ɛyɛ mmerɛ seesei". Nanso Nkwanyimfo no antie no. Afei, Akɔnkɔnkɔn no boaboaa nam no ano na ɔsan baa fie, na Ɔpanyin no twee ne ho kakra. Afei Ananse wiaa ne sekan no de guu ne kotoku mu, na otuu mmirika san baa ne akuraa no ase. Bere a ɔsan kɔɔ ne kurom no, ɔka kyerɛɛ (ne babarima no) sɛ, "Ntikuma, esiane sekan yi a mede me nsa ato so yi nti, ma yɛnkɔ kwae no mu". Enti wɔkɔe, na wɔkɔhunuu Wild Pigs bi. Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ntikuma, kɔ na kɔhaw wɔn. Ntikuma kɔɔ ne nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Bush-pigs, wo ho ntew". Bush-pigs no buae sɛ, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ sɛ woyɛ Kwaku Ananse, Spider babarima a, anka ɛsɛ sɛ yɛba na yɛboro wo". Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Bra na fa ɔkwan biara so boro no, ɔyɛ onipa bɔne koraa, n'ahoɔfɛ nyɛ anigyede". Na Bush-pigs no rebɛboro no, bere a Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Otumfoɔ bi sekan, The-sharp-one, twa wɔn ti ma me". h! na Knife no too wɔn so, na otwaa mmoa no nyinaa ti. Afei ofii ase twitwaa nnua no. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Ao! ɛdeɛn na woreyɛ yi? Mekae sɛ twa mmoa no ti, na wowiee saa, na afei eyi nyinaa yɛ dɛn?" Afei Ntikuma hwɛɛ no kakra, na afei ɔkae sɛ, "Afei yɛ no brɛoo". Kwaku Ananse antie no, na Knife no baa fam, na Ananse kɔfaa. Wɔsan kɔɔ fie. Ade kyee no, bere a nneɛma no bɛdaa adi no, Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ wɔbɛkɔ ɔsoro-nyame no nkyɛn wɔ hɔ. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Agya, sɛ ɛyɛ Knife yi nti a, nkɔ. Ananse kaa sɛ, fi hɔ kɔ. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, kɔ w'ankasa kwan so. Ananse fii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ Ɔsoro-nyame hɔ, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Owura, ɛnnɛ, sɛ obi ayɛ wo bɔne a, me nko ara, me, Kwaku Ananse, mɛtwa wɔn nyinaa ti da koro na mede ama wo. Ɔsoro-nyame no kaa sɛ, Ɛwɔ aseda afɔrebɔ ma obiara a Ananse kaa sɛ, "Momma wɔmfa nsã ne oguan baako mmra". Ɛhɔ ara na wɔde brɛɛ no. Ananse firii adi kɔɔ kwan no so; Ananse bisaa n'asraafo no sɛ, "Mo na moka sɛ mobɛko atia Ɔsoro nyame no anaa?" Afei Kwaku Ananse kasa kyerɛɛ Ɔbaa-kwakoraa no Knife, The-sharp-one, kaa sɛ, Twa saa asraafodɔm a wɔsɔre tia me yi ti. Na Knife no twitwaa wɔn ti, tan! tan! na ɛwiee. Ananse kaa sɛ, Eye, gyae. Nanso Knife no bɛhwee Ɔsoro-nyame asraafodɔm no so na na ɔretwa (wɔn ti). Ananse kaa sɛ, Oh, ɛno ne Ɔsoro-nyame asraafodɔm no, enti gyae. Nanso ɛkɔɔ so. Ananse kaa sɛ, Sɛ w'akoma atɔ kyima a, ma no nyɛ nwunu. Oh! Ɔsoro-nyame no asraafodɔm no nyinaa awuwu. Seesei, Knife no twaa mframa mu; ɛbaa baabi a Kwaku Ananse wɔ no. Ɔkae sɛ, Hwɛ yiye, wunnim me? Wonnim me? Mene Kwaku Ananse, me na meyii wo fii Ɔbaa-kwakoraa no hɔ. Oh! Seesei Kwaku Ananse ti hwehwee, tan! Na Knife no kɔɔ so kɔɔ so; na baabi a egyaee no dan wura bi, a yɛfrɛ no Etene (spear-grass); ne ano yɛ nnam yiye.

M'asɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
