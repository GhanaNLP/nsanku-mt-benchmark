AKETEKYIRE and Ananse decided to fight over a grain of wheat. And we got two grains of wheat. And Ananse said, Me one tree my faith. And Aketekyire said, Me one tree my faith. Ananse one tree his faith. And Aketekyire put his faith on the fire. But no, he did not build it. Ananse on the vineyard; he said, Ofe eye afwe. Aburo said, Mfe my eye, and your hand will be my eye. Ananse said, Cut your eye afwe and Krwe; and Kraman said, Nine me, and me my mother. Ananse said, Fruit like fwe the Kraman, and Aba said, Me one tree my faith, I will kill you. Ananse said, Me one tree my faith. Ananse said, Aketekyire ought to suffer and die. He said that Aketekire's house was burned.
