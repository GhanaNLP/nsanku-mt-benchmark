BERE bi na ɔbea bi wɔ hɔ a ɔwoo mma duako, na da biara sɛ ɔsɔre to aduan a, na mmofra no nyinaa di. Na wɔn maame nnya aduan biara nni. Afei odwennwenee ho kyɛree, na ɔkɔe kɔtenaa dua no so, na ɔka kyerɛɛ Silk-cotton dua no sɛ, "Mɛsoma me mma dubaako no aba wo ase ha abɛbo aburow; na sɛ wɔba a, momfa mo mman dubaako no bi na munkum me mma no". Silk-cotton dua no kae sɛ, "Mate, na mɛyɛ ama mo". Na ɛna no fii hɔ kɔɔ fie, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne mma no sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ motwe aburow no. Kɔ mfuw no mu, Silk-cotton dua no ase; aburow wɔ hɔ, kɔ boaboa ano na bra (san bra)". Mmofra no fii hɔ. Wɔkɔɔ Silk-cotton dua no ho. Dodow a ɛto so dubaako kae sɛ, "Dodow a edi kan, gyina hɔ; Dodow a ɛto so abien, gyina hɔ; Dodow a ɛto so abiɛsa, gyina hɔ; Dodow a ɛto so anan, gyina hɔ; Dodow a ɛto so anum, gyina hɔ; Dodow a ɛto so asia, gyina hɔ; Dodow a ɛto so ason, gyina hɔ; Dodow a ɛto so awotwe, gyina hɔ; Dodow a ɛto so akron, gyina hɔ; Dodow a ɛto so du, gyina hɔ; na me ankasa, Dodow a ɛto so dubiako, masi gyina hɔ". Eason deɛn nti na maame se ɛsɛ sɛ yɛkɔ kɔboaboa aburokyiri ano?" Ne nuanom no kae sɛ, "Dabi". Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔka kyerɛɛ Silk-cotton dua yi sɛ, sɛ yɛkɔ hɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔtwe mman na ɔboro yɛn; enti mo nyinaa twa nnua, na momfa nto saa Silk-cotton dua yi so". Wɔtwee nnua no, na wɔtow too Silk-cotton dua no so, pim! pen! pim! pen! (saa na wɔte. Silk-cotton dua no susuwii sɛ mmofra no aba. Oyii ne mman du no mu biako, na ɔma wɔhwehwee fam. Nkyekyɛm Mmienu ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Woahu, sɛ yɛkɔɔ hɔ a, Silk-cotton dua no kunkum yɛn". Wɔboaboaa aburow no ano de kɔmaa wɔn maame. Ɔnoaa no; oh! ntɛm ara na mmofra no dii (ne nyinaa). Wɔn maame kae sɛ, "Ah! na asɛm yi deɛ, merentumi nnyina ano, enti mɛfa saa mma yi na mede wɔn ama Ɔsoro nyame no". Na ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa hɔ no, ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔsoro nyame no ho asɛm nyinaa sɛ, "M'awo mma bi, sɛ mepɛ sɛ midi a, minnya biribi nni (efisɛ wodi ntɛmntɛm na wodi pii); ɔkɔm rekum me; enti mesrɛ wo, ma mma no mmra Ɔbea no kae sɛ: "Meka ti bi a ne mu yɛ fitaa no ho asɛm". Ɔmaa abɔfo bi kɔe, na wɔkɔtutuu amoa kɛse bi, na wɔde nkotoku guu mu. Ɔkɔfaa ɔwɔ ne ɔsebɔ de guu amoa no mu, na ɔkataa so. Na afei somafo no kɔfrɛɛ mmofra no bae. Bere a wɔduruu baabi a abura no da no ara pɛ na Ɔpɛnson kae sɛ, "Ɔpɛnson, gyina hɔ; Ɔpɛnson, gyina hɔ; Ɔpɛnson, gyina hɔ; Ɔpɛnson, gyina hɔ; Ɔpɛnson, gyina hɔ; Ɔpɛnson, gyina hɔ; Ɔpɛnson, gyina hɔ; Ɔpɛnson, gyina hɔ; Ɔpɛnson, gyina hɔ; Ɔpɛnson, gyina hɔ; Ɔpɛnson, gyina hɔ; Ɔpɛnson, gyina hɔ; Ne nuanom kae sɛ: "Dɛn nti na, bere a kwan kɛse bi wɔ hɔ no, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛfa kwae no mu?" Afei, bere a na wɔrekɔ no, na wɔn nyinaa kura nkontimmaa. Na Da a ɛto so dubaako no kae sɛ: "Montow nkontimmaa yi mu biako ngu kwan yi so". 11 kae sɛ: Hwɛ, sɛ yɛfaa hɔ a, anka yɛn nyinaa awuwu. Enti wɔfaa ɔkwan bi so na wɔkɔɔ ɔsoro nyame no nkyɛn. Ɔsoro nyame no ma wosisii ntokuru, na wɔkataa so, na wɔde nkongua sisii wɔn so, na sɛ mmofra no ba wɔn so a, na wɔkɔtɔ ntokuru no mu. Mmofra no duu ɔsoro nyame anim. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Atease nkongua wɔ hɔ, mubetumi akɔ na moatena so". Ɛno akyi no, Ɔbaako no kae sɛ, "Hena ne yɛn, sɛ yɛbɛtumi atena nkongua a ɛyɛ fɛ saa so; enti, Owura, yɛbɛtena ha". Ɛno akyi no, Ɔsoro-nyame no hwɛɛ mmofra no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Mɛsoma mmofra yi akɔ Owuo akuraa no ase". Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma no daa adi no, ɔfrɛɛ mmofra no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Momfa mo ho mma no". Ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ Owu (a ɔte hɔ) hɔ, na kɔfa sikakɔkɔɔ sumina, sikakɔkɔɔ aporibaa, sikakɔkɔɔ sumina adaka, sikakɔkɔɔ sumina, ne sikakɔkɔɔ sumina. Wɔduruu hɔ no, Owu bisae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na, bere a ɛnsɛ sɛ obi ba ha da no, moaba ha?" wɔbuaa sɛ, "Na yɛretwa yɛn ho, na yɛbaa ha (ɛnyɛ asɛm biara)." Owu kae sɛ, "Oh, eye saa. " Afei Owu woo mma du, na ɔde ne ho kaa ho no, na wɔn dodow yɛ dubaako. Bere a owia duruu sum mu no, Owu kyekyɛɛ mma no mu mmaako mmaako, na ɔde biako maa ne mma no mu biara, bere a ɔno ne Da a Ɛto so Mmienu no kɔdae. Bere a owia duruu sum mu no, Owu maa ne se hyerɛn kɔkɔɔ, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obetumi afa Da a Ɛto so Mmienu no. Owuo kaa sɛ, "Owuo, menna da". Owuo kaa sɛ, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Owuo kaa sɛ, "Sɛ woma me sika pipe sɛ menom sigaret kakra a, ebia mɛda". Na Owuo kɔfaa bi maa no. Ankyɛ na Owuo san de ne nsateaa hyerɛn bio, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛkɔ akɔfa Owuo ne ne nsateaa. Owuo kaa sɛ, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Owuo kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wode sika kɔkɔɔ bi brɛ me a, anka mɛda". Owuo de brɛɛ no. Bio nso, ankyɛ na Owuo bɛkyeree Owuo. Ɔkaa sɛ: "Sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa sika kɔkɔɔ dua bi a mede bɛtwetwe biribi kakra, na afei mɛda". Ɔkaa sɛ: "Owuo de kɔmaa no. Bere tiaa bi twaam, na na Owuo rebɛkyere no". Ɔbaako kaa sɛ, "Nanabaa, menna da". Owuo kaa sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ, da bɛn na wobɛda?" Ɔbaako kaa sɛ, "Nanabaa, sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa sika kɔkɔɔ aboɔ a wɔde twa dadeɛ brɛ me a, ebia mɛda". Na Owuo kɔfaa no brɛɛ no. Bio nso, ankyɛ na Owuo sɔre firii owuo mu bio. Ɔbaako kaa sɛ, "O, Nana, meka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ menna da". Owuo kaa sɛ, "Na da bɛn na wobɛda?" Ɔbaako kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa borɔdɔma a ayɛ ntokuru, na woakɔbɔ nsu agu mu, na woahyɛ aduane bi ama me a, ebia mɛda". Owuo maa ahina so kɔɔ asuten no ho. Bere a ɔhwiee nsu guu mu no, mmera no mu ntokuru no maa nsu no faa mu. Afei Ɔbaako ka kyerɛɛ ne nuanom no sɛ: "Monsɔre na monnwane". Na wɔsɔree na woguanee; na Ɔbaako kɔtwitwaa nnuaba dua, na ɔde guu baabi a ne nuanom da no, na ɔfaa ntama kataa so. Afei Owuo wɔ asuten no ho; na ɔhwiee nsu gu mu. Afei Ɔbarima Owu frɛɛ Ɔbea Owu, kae sɛ, "Owu, Owu". Obuae sɛ, "Adwo". Obisae sɛ, "Dɛn na woreyɛ yi?" Obisae sɛ, "Dabi, ɛnyɛ abofra ketewa bi a mewɔ; sɛ merebɛkyere no a, ɔka sɛ, 'Menna; ɔfa me nneɛma nyinaa, na afei ɔka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mefa sumina na mehwie nsu gu mu". Ɔbarima Owu kae sɛ, "Ah, woyɛ abofra ketewa; afei, sɛ wusiw nhaban na woda hɔ Ɔbea a ɔyɛ Owu no kae sɛ, "Oh, ɛyɛ nokware. " Ɔtwee nhaban, na ɔde guu mu, na ohwiee nsu no guu so, na ofii hɔ kɔe. Baako kae sɛ, "Owu, woaba dedaw? Boaboa aduan no ano". Owu no noa aduan no; ɔhyehyɛɛ ne se de kunkum Baako nuanom no na ɔnom wɔn sɛ aduan. Bere a ɔkɔe no, wansusuw wɔn ho yiye, na ɔno ankasa kunkum n'ankasa mma du no nyinaa. Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu, bere a nneɛma no daa adi no, Owu sɔre tenaa hɔ wɔ ogya no ho. Na me de, merekɔ". Owuo kaa sɛ, "Wo, abofra a ɛte sɛɛ yi; baabiara a wobɛda no, ɛhɔ ara na mɛda". Ɔpɛn du-na-baako no huruwii, yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! na Owuo nso dii n'akyi. Afei Owu baa wɔn so. Ɔpɛnson no faa aduru na ohwie guu ne nuanom so, na wɔforo kɔɔ dua a wɔde nwera ayɛ no atifi. Na Owu gyinaa dua a wɔde nwera ayɛ no nan ase. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛha yi ara, mihuu mmofra yi, na ɛhe na wɔakɔ?" Na Ɔpɛnson te soro hɔ; ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuanom sɛ, "Merebɛma no nsu".
