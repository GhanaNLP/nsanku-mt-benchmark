Yɛmfa no aniberesɛm, yɛnka no aniberesɛm, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware)

SƐN NA ABOSOM, NYANKOPƆN A Wonnye Nni, Ba Wiase

BERE bi na ɔbea bi wɔ hɔ a na ɔwo mma dubaako, na da biara sɛ ɔsɔre to aduan a, na mmofra no nyinaa di. Na wɔn maame nnya aduan biara. Afei odwennwenee ho kyɛree, na ɔkɔe sɛ ɔrekɔ dua no so na wakyerɛkyerɛ Silk-cotton dua no sɛ, "Mɛsoma me mma dubaako no aba wo ase ha abɛbo aburow; na sɛ wɔba a, twa wo mman no mu dubaako na kum me mma no". Silk-cotton dua no kae sɛ, "Mate, na mɛyɛ ama wo". Na ɛna no fii hɔ kɔɔ fie; ɔka kyerɛɛ ne mma no sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ mokɔ dua no so, Silk-cotton dua no ase; aburow wɔ hɔ, monkɔboaboa ano na mommra (san mmra). " Mmofra no fii hɔ. Wɔkɔɔ Silk-cotton dua no ho. Na Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Number One, gyina hɔ; Number Two, gyina hɔ; Number Three, gyina hɔ; Number Four, gyina hɔ; Number Five, gyina hɔ; Number Six, gyina hɔ; Number Seven, gyina hɔ; Number Eight, gyina hɔ; Number Nine, gyina hɔ; Number Ten, gyina hɔ; na me ankasa, Number Eleven, masi gyina hɔ". Na Number Eleven kasa kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Munnim ade biako nti a Maame kae sɛ yɛnkɔtɔ aburosiam no?" Ne nuanom kae sɛ, "Dabi. " Ɔkae sɛ, "Waka akyerɛ Silk-cotton dua yi sɛ, sɛ yɛkɔ hɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ epiapia mman na ɔboro yɛn; enti yɛn nyinaa woatwa nnua, na wode ato Silk-cotton dua yi so. "Wotwaa nnua no, na wɔde too Silk-cotton dua no so, pim! pen! pim! pen! (ne nne a wɔyɛɛ no). Silk-cotton dua no susuwii sɛ mmofra no aba. Oyii ne mman du du, na ɔmaa wɔhwehwee fam. Akronkron ketewa no kae sɛ, "Woahu, sɛ yɛkɔɔ hɔ a, anka Silk-cotton dua no bekum yɛn". Wosii aburow no de kɔmaa wɔn maame no. Ɔnoaa no; oh! ntɛm ara na mmofra no adi (ne nyinaa). Wɔn maame kae sɛ, "Ah! Ɛdefa saa asɛm yi ho no, mintumi nnyina ano, enti mɛfa saa mma yi na mede wɔn ama ɔsoro nyame no. "Ade kyee no, bere a nneɛma no daa adi no, ɔkɔkaa ne nyinaa kyerɛɛ ɔsoro nyame no, na ɔkae sɛ, "M'awo mma no bi, sɛ mepɛ sɛ midi a, minnya biribi nni (efisɛ wodi ntɛmntɛm na wodi pii); ɔkɔm rekum me, enti mesrɛ wo, ma wɔmfa mmofra no mmra na wonkum wɔn, na minnya biribi nni". Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ saa ampa?" Ɔbea no kae sɛ, "Mekasa kyerɛ ti bi a ne mu yɛ fitaa". Ɔsoro nyame no yii mesa na wɔkɔe kɔtutuu abura kɛseɛ, na wɔde nkotoku guu mu. Ɔkɔeɛ kɔfaa ɔwɔ ne ɔsebɔ de guu abura no mu, na ɔkataa so. Na afei na ɔsomafoɔ no rekɔfrɛ mmɔfra no. Bere a wɔduruu amoa no mu ara pɛ na Ɔkannifo a ɔtɔ so dubiako kae sɛ, "Ɔkannifo, gyina hɔ; Ɔkannifo a ɔtɔ so abien, gyina hɔ; Ɔkannifo a ɔtɔ so abiɛsa, gyina hɔ; Ɔkannifo a ɔtɔ so anan, gyina hɔ; Ɔkannifo a ɔtɔ so anum, gyina hɔ; Ɔkannifo a ɔtɔ so asia, gyina hɔ; Ɔkannifo a ɔtɔ so ason, gyina hɔ; Ɔkannifo a ɔtɔ so awotwe, gyina hɔ; Ɔkannifo a ɔtɔ so du, gyina hɔ; na me, Ɔkannifo a ɔtɔ so dubaako, me nso, metena hɔ; ɛsɛ sɛ wofa ha; ɛnsɛ sɛ wofa hɔ". Ɔkaa sɛ: "Momfa nnua yi mu baako nto kwan yi so". Wɔtow too hɔ, na ɛhwee abura no mu, yiridi. Ɔkaa sɛ: "Ɛha na wowɔ, wuhu? Sɛ yɛfaa hɔ a, anka yɛn nyinaa awuwu". Enti wɔfaa ɔkwan bi so na wɔkɔɔ ɔsoro nyame no nkyɛn. Ɔsoro nyame no ma wotuu mmorɔn, na ɔkataa so, na ɔde nkongua sisii so, na sɛ mmofra no ba bɛtena so a, na wɔkɔtɔ mmorɔn no mu. Mmofra no duu ɔsoro nyame anim. Ɔsoro-nyame no kae sɛ, "Wɔde nkongua asi hɔ, wubetumi akɔtra so". Ɛno akyi no, Ɔpɛnsɛmbere kae sɛ, "Hwan ne yɛn, sɛ yɛbɛtumi atena nkongua a ɛyɛ fɛ saa so; enti, Owura, yɛbɛtra nkyɛn wɔ ha". Ɛno akyi no, Ɔsoro-nyame no hwɛɛ mmofra no; ɔkae sɛ, "Mɛsoma mmofra yi akɔ Owuo akuraa no ase". Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔfrɛɛ mmofra no; ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ mokɔ Owuo (a ɔte ase) hɔ no nkyɛn, na monkɔfa sikakɔkɔɔ pɔre, sikakɔkɔɔ apampaa, sikakɔkɔɔ gyaade, sikakɔkɔɔ ɔbo, ne sikakɔkɔɔ afiri a wɔde twe nnomaa". Ɔpɛnsɛmbere kae sɛ, "Mo, Wone yɛn wura, baabiara a wobɛsoma yɛn akɔ no, yɛbɛkɔ". Ɔsoro nyame no kae sɛ, "Kɔ". Mmofra no de wɔn ani kyerɛɛ Owuo akuraa no ase. Wɔduruu hɔ no, Owuo bisaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na, bere a ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara ba ha da no, woaba ha?" Wobuae sɛ, "Yɛnenam baabiara na yɛbaa ha (sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ biara)." Owuo kae sɛ, "Oh, eye saa. " Afei Owuo woo mma du; ɔde ne ho kaa ho, na wɔn dodow yɛ dubaako. Bere a nneɛma fii ase sɛee (te sɛ esum), Owuo kyekyɛɛ mma no mu mmiako mmiako, na ɔde baako maa ne mma no mu biara, bere a ɔno ankasa ne Owuo ne Baako no kɔdaa hɔ. Bere a esum duruu so no, Owu maa ne se hyerɛn kɔkɔɔ sɛ ɔmfa ne nsa nka Numero Du-ne-baako no. Numero Du-ne-baako no kae sɛ, "Owu, menna ada". Owu kae sɛ, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Numero Du-ne-baako no kae sɛ, "Sɛ wode sika pipe bi ma me sɛ menom a, mɛda". Na Owu de maa no. Bere bi akyi no, Owu san maa ne se hyerɛn bio sɛ ɔmfa ne nsa nka Numero Du-ne-baako no. Owuo se, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Owuo se, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Owuo se, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Owuo se, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Owuo se, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Owuo se, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Owuo se, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Owuo se, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Owuo se, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Owuo se, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Owuo se, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Owuo se, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Owuo se, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Owuo se, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Owuo se, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Owuo se, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Owuo se, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Owuo se, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Owuo se, "Bere bɛn na wobɛda?" Ɔbaako kaa sɛ, "Nanabaa, menna da". Owuo kaa sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ, da bɛn na wobɛda?" Ɔbaako kaa sɛ, "Nanabaa, sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa sika kɔkɔɔ aboɔ a wɔde twa dadeɛ brɛ me a, ebia mɛda". Na Owuo kɔfaa no brɛɛ no. Bio nso, ankyɛ na Owuo sɔre firii owuo mu bio. Owuo se, "Na da a wobɛda no bɛyɛ dɛn?" Owuo se, "Sɛ wokɔ na wokɔfa atoko a abobo ayɛ no ma, na wokɔ de nsu gu mu, na woboa aduan bi ma me di a, ebia mɛda". Owuo maa ahina bi so kɔɔ asu no mu. Bere a ohwiee nsu no guu mu no, ahina no mu mmobɔwee no maa nsu no faa mu. Afei Ɔbaako ka kyerɛɛ ne nuanom sɛ: "Monsɔre na monnwane!" Na wɔsɔree na wɔdwanee; na Ɔbaako kɔtwitwaa nhwiren nnua, na ɔde guu baabi a ne nuanom da no, na ɔfaa ntama kataa wɔn so. Afei na Owu wɔ asuten no ho; na ɔresaw nsu. Afei Ɔbarima Owu frɛɛ Ɔbea Owu, kae sɛ, "Hwɛ, Owu". Obuae sɛ, "Adwo". Obisae sɛ, "Dɛn na woreyɛ yi?" Ɔbea no kae sɛ, "Dabi, ɛnyɛ abofra ketewa bi a mewɔ; sɛ merebɛkyere no a, ɔka sɛ, 'Menna; ɔfa me nneɛma nyinaa, na afei ɔka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mefa sumina na mehwie nsu gu mu". Ɔbarima Owu kae sɛ, "Ah, woyɛ abofra ketewa; afei, sɛ wuyi nhaban na wode to sumina no mu, na afei wode nsu gu mu a, so ɛrenyɛ yiye?" Ɔbea Owu kae sɛ, "O, ɛyɛ nokware paa. " Ɔtew nhaban, de guu mu, na ɔde nsu no guu mu, na ofii hɔ kɔe. Owuo kaa sɛ, "Owuo, woaba dada? Boaboa aduane no ano". Owuo yɛɛ aduane no; ɔhyehyɛɛ ne se de kunkum Owuo nuabarimanom no na ɔnnoa wɔn sɛ aduane. Bere a ɔkɔeɛ no, wanhwehwɛ wɔn mu, na ɔno ankasa kunkum n'ankasa mma du no nyinaa. Ade kyee anɔpa paa no, bere a nneɛma no daa adi no, Owuo sɔre tenaa hɔ wɔ ogya no ho. Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Nanabaa, tsetse fly te wo koko so". Owuo kaa sɛ, "Fa fly-switch a ɛda hɔ no, na kum no ma me". Number Eleven kaa sɛ, "Me wura; obi a ɔte sɛ wo, sɛ tsetse fly te wo so, na sika fly-switch da hɔ a (wobɛfa saa ade dedaw yi), ma me mmra mmɛkum no". Owuo kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔfa no mfiri dan no mu". Number Eleven kɔfaa no bae. Ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛpam fly no; wankum no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, ɛnnɛ, baabiara a ɔsebɔ yi bɛhome no, ɛhɔ ara na me ne no bɛhome". Ɛno akyi no, Ɔsebɔ kɔɔ dan no mu kɔfaa ne kotoku a na sika kɔkɔɔ no ne nneɛma no nyinaa wɔ mu no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Nanabaa Owuo, biribiara rentumi mma me gye sɛ mefa ɔsebɔ no, na mede hyɛ kotoku yi mu, na mede brɛ wo". Ɔsebɔ siim kɔe, yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! Oduu kurow no ano; ɔkae sɛ, "O, Owu Nana, fa kyɛ me sɛ meka saa, nanso sɛ wonyɛ ɔkwasea koraa a, anka metumi agye w'ade nyinaa afi wo nsam; so anka me nuanom a me ne wɔn bae no betumi anya ɔkwan aguan; na so anka metumi ama woakum wo mma du no nyinaa nso? Me de, merefi hɔ". Owu kae sɛ, "Wo, abofra a ɔte sɛɛ yi; baabiara a wugye w'ahome no, ɛhɔ ara na me nso megye me ho ahome". Nkyekyem dubaako no huruwii, yiridi! yiridi! yiridi! na Owu nso dii n'akyi. Bere a Nkyekyem dubaako no rekɔ no, ohyiaa ne nuanom, na na wɔte ɔkwan no so. Na wɔreyɛ nnomaa afiri. Numero Du-ne-baako kae sɛ, "Wonkɔe ɛ? Owu reba, enti momma yɛnhwehwɛ ɔkwan bi a yɛbɛfa so aguan". Afei Owu baa wɔn so. Numero Du-ne-baako faa aduro na ohwie guu ne nuanom so, na wɔforo kɔɔ dua bi a wɔfrɛ no silk-cotton atifi. Na Owu gyinaa Silk-cotton dua no nan ase. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛha yi ara na mehunuu mma yi, na ɛhe na wɔakɔ?" Ɛha na Ɛto so dubaako no te wɔ soro hɔ; ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuanom sɛ, "Mede nsuo bɛgu no so". Ne nuanom no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ yɛn akyere yɛn, na yɛaguan na yɛabɛtena ha, nanso woka sɛ, 'Mede nsuo bɛgu no so.' " Ɛto so dubaako no antie, na ɔde nsuo bɛgu Owuo so. Owuo kaa sɛ, "Ah, woaba ha, ɛnnɛ woahu ɔhaw". Owuo kaa sɛ, "Wo, abofra, a wote hɔ no, 'Kyere-he-ne, Kyere-he-ne". Ɛhɔ na mmofra no mu baako hwee ase. "Kyere-he-ne", nea ɔto so mmienu hwee ase. Afei na Ɔbaako pɛ na aka. Owuo kaa sɛ, "Abofra, 'kyere-he-ne', " na Ɔbaako huri sii fam, kirim! Na Owuo nso kɔɔ Silk-cotton dua no ho. Ɔbaako kaa sɛ, "Wo nso, ɔbaa kɛse, wo nso, 'kyere-he-ne". Na Owuo nso baa fam, tum! na wawu. Ɔbaako kɔfaa aduro, na ɔbobɔɔ no ne nsateaa ntam, na ɔhwie guu ne nuanom so, na wɔsɔree. Ɔbaako pɛe sɛ ɔtow aduro no gu, bere a emu bi guu Owuo so no, Owuo nyanee. Ɔkaa sɛ: "Woakum me, na woasan nso anyane me; ɛnnɛ wo ne me bɛyɛ asɛm bi". Na wɔn nyinaa fii ase guanee prɛko pɛ, kiri! kiri! kiri! Afei na Owu redi wɔn akyi. Na wɔrekɔ no, na asubɔnten kɛse bi abu so. Bere a Numero dubaako ne ne nuanom duu hɔ no, na wonim sɛnea woguare, na woguare twaa mu. Na Numero dubaako nkutoo na onnim sɛnea woguare. Mmofra no gyinaa ɔfã foforo no so; wɔsu, na wɔsu, na wosu; wɔn ano yɛɛ kɛse. Na nea ɛfa Numero dubaako ho no, ɔdan ɔbo. Owu duu asubɔnten no ho. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, mmofra yi, wogyina hɔ, ma memfa ɔbo nto wɔn ano a abu so no". Owuo, bere a ɔhwɛɛ fam no, ɔhunuu sɛ ɔbo bi da hɔ; ɔboaa no, na ɔtow too hɔ; bere a ɔbo no nam no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Mframa, fa me na fa me si ɔfã no so". Ɛduruu ɔfã no so no. Dumienu kaa sɛ, "Mewɔ ha". Owuo kaa sɛ, "Ah, abofra yi! Me ne wo nni asɛm foforo biara ka, gye sɛ meka kyerɛ wo sɛ kɔ na tena fie na dan wo ho anyame nketewa no mu biako, na sɛ obi a mepɛ sɛ mefa no ba baabi a wowɔ a, wobɛka akyerɛ me, na sɛ mepɛ a, mɛfa no ama wo, na nea mepɛ sɛ wode ma me no, gye sɛ wugye ma me". Saa na Abosom (anyame nketewa) baa wiase no. Wɔyɛ mmɔfra nketewa Eleven no asefo.

Eyi ne m'asɛm a meka kyerɛ mo. Sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, munnye emu bi ntom, na sɛ ɛnte saa a, munyi me ayɛ.
