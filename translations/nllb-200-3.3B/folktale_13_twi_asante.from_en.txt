SƐN NA AKOKONINI, ƆKO no, N'ano AKA

PAPA ANANSE, Spider, ne ne mma Afudotwedotwe, Nyiwankonfwea ne Tikonokono te hɔ. Na Spider ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ mokɔ kɔhwehwɛ borɔdɔma ma midi". Enti mmofra yi fii hɔ; wɔkɔɔ so nantewee, na wɔkɔɔ so nantewee, na wohuu borɔdɔma dua bi, na (borɔdɔma no abere, na mmofra no kɔhyɛɛ dua no ase. Afudotwedotwe ka kyerɛɛ Nyiwankonfwea sɛ, "Sɔre na yi bi". Ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, sɛ meforo a me nan bɛtete". Tikonokono kaa sɛ, "Wo, Afudotwedotwe, wo nso worekɔ soro". Ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, sɛ meforo a, me yafunu bɛtetew; wo nso kɔ soro". Enti Tikonokono kɔ soro. Na ɔtwee mma no bi fii borɔdɔma dua no mu, na ɔtwee, na ɔtwee, na ɔtwee, na ɔkae sɛ: So eyi bɛyɛ? Ebinom kae sɛ: Dabi! Enti ɔtwee wɔn, na ɔsan bisaa wɔn bio sɛ: So eyi bɛyɛ? Na wɔkae sɛ: Bue w'atifi hwɛ. Na bere a ɔrebɛbɔ ne ti ase ahwɛ no, ne ti kaa fam, na ɔkae sɛ: Kum! Na bere a Belly-like-to-burst bɛserewee no, ne yafunu paee, ta-ta-ta-ta! Bere a ɔrebɛkɔ akɔka akyerɛ ne papa no, ne nan abien no bubuu, na ogyinaa ɔfasu no akyi. Na Akoa no rehohoro ne kra, na ɔrekɔ akɔtow nkesua no, bere a ohui sɛ Thin-shanks ahwe ase na ne nan abobɔ, na wɔagyaw no ɔfasu no akyi no. Bere a ɔrebɛserew no, n'ano yɛɛ den:

"Bere a ɔrebɛserew no, N'ano yɛɛ nnam.

Na ɔrebɛserew no, N'ano yɛɛ nnam".

Ɛno nti na Nkwakoraa no ka sɛ, "Sɛ obi hwe ase a, kɔ na hwɛ no, na nserew no". Afei ɔprammiri no, ɔyɛɛ n'ade saa, na ɔserewee, enti n'ano yɛɛ nsõ; kan no na n'ano yɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ.

M'asɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma bi mmra me nkyɛn.
