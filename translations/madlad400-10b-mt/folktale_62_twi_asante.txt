SE YOYE A AWAREGYAYEE BAA MANM’

OBOFUO bi na ye’fre no Kwasi Gyaba, ne no yere na ye’ko tenaa nnan so, na ye’yee ’ha. Kwasi Gyaba koo wuram’ a, ommfa nam mma. Da ommfa nam mma. Na dakoro bi, ne yere bisaa no se, “Me gye di se wo kum nam a, wo ton di, ana wo de kyekye, nti nne se wo ko wuram’, na se w’ammfa nam amma a, meko me ’ni ho, na mabere nama-nama wee.” Obofuo Kwasi Gyaba siim’ koo wuram’. Obefwe se adowa e’twam’. Osee, “Adowa yi, die wo beda, me ne wo beda.” Adowa ko gyinaa dua bi ase. Omaa etuo so pam! Onini firii he, firii he o, na wabu agu adowa so; na osebo so ahuri asi no so. Obofuo bataa ho komm. Osee, “E! Obofuo faa ne ’tuo, dii y’akyi koye. Ye’ko kyee nam no. Onini see, “Agya ’Bofuo me ne wo ka, nti da ha nne, na mema wo aduro; na sa aduro yi me de so w’asom’ a, wo be te nnono ne nhaha ne mmoammoa nnyina kasa. Nti anadwo yi wo ko da a, nhome.” Obofuo ko daye; otuaa home. Onini de aduro besoo n’asom’. Adeekyeye ’Bofuo tee nwaa nnyina kasa yie. Osiim’ baa ne kurom’. Na obaye no, obetee n’ase aba ne ’ba nkyen. Na n’ase no ani nifa abo. Na obofuo ko tenaa dan so. Kasa ne nyam’ oko too n’ase no, na o’si aburo. Obofuo tee ho komm! na nkoko mmienu wo ho; obako bisaa obako se, “Wo die wo nya aburo yi he, na me die me kor’a, na waka me?” Obako no see, “Aden, wo nfwe se n’ani nifa no abo, na wo fa aburo no na wo de fa toro no so a, onhuno?” Kwasi Gyaba teye; odanee n’akyi, twe! twe! twe! N’ase no see, “A, m’ase, aden na wo’ sere me seyie?” Na Kwasi Gyaba da fam’, seree’a na o’seree. Eho ara na n’ase kaa Nyankonpon ntam guu no so se Kwasi Gyaba nko kyere no seree no ase Nyankonpon anim’. Nyankonpon yii abofuo maa ye’befaa Kwasi Gyaba. Oduruu krotia na omaa dwom so:

“Na me’beka m’awu o, Kwasi Gyaba e!

Na me’beka m’awu o, ay’a, nsu me.

Kwasi Mpuroko Gyaba, na okoro no, Ay’a, nsu me.” Nyankonpon mpan’infuo see, “Mo ntie daduani no dwom a o’too.” Ye’de Kwasi Gyaba baa Nyankonpon anim’. , “Me ’bofuo ne wo, nti me’mma wo nnwu.” N’ase see, “Nana oboa; sere’a na oseree me, nti gye se okyere me asee.” N’ase see, “Nana oboa; sere’a na oseree me, nti gye se okyere me asee.” N’ase see, “Nana oboa; sere’a na oseree me, nti gye se okyere me asee.” N’ase see, “Nana oboa; sere’a na oseree me, nti gye se okyere me asee.” N’ase see, “Nana oboa; sere’a na oseree me, nti gye se okyere me asee.” N’ase see, “Nana oboa; sere’a na oseree me, nti gye se okyere me asee.” N’ase see, “Nana oboa; sere’a na oseree me, nti gye se okyere me asee.” N’ase see, “Nana oboa; sere’a na oseree me, nti gye se okyere me asee.” N’ase see, “Nana oboa; sere’a I Gyaba teye. Ono’so eho ara osee, “Nana Nyame, ma ye’nnyae amena no bo, na me wo asem meka.” Nyame see, “Ka e!” Kwasi Gyaba see, “Me ka ntam se m’ase yi omfa ne ’ba.” Mpan’infuo nnyina see, “Nokore, nokore, asem asa.” Eho ara na Nyame hyee doka se, “Obiara se wo yonko dwan wo ho a, ka ntam gu no so, ana se wo ye’ ye sa a, ka ntam gya no awaree.” Nti ne se wala awaregyayee aba manm’. Na Akokonini na ye’suaa no.

We all know that, whether it is one or another, the mother is the mother, and the father is the father.
