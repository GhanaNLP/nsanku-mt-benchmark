Ye' nse se, nse se o

SE EYOYE A ESONONOM KOO ESEREM'

Eson' see, "M'ate, na meba abebo wo nnanson, na wo so wabo me nnanson." Kwaku Ananse ko tuu bayere, na ode guu ogyam' totoye, na oko boo yanee 'kwan ho. Na Ananse sode a, na wafa bayere no afua, na wako sen yanee so. Obefwe se Adowa na o'twam'. Nanso kasa ne ne nyam', na okom kesie bi aba. O'twam' na Ananse ato bayere no atwene okwanm'. Seisei na Adowa afa. Eson' see, "M'ate, na meba abebo wo nnanson, na wo so wabo me nnanson." Kwaku Ananse ko tuu bayere, na ode guu ogyam' totoye, na oko boo yanee 'kwan ho. Na Ananse sode a, na wafa bayere no afua, na wako sen yanee so. Obefwe se Adowa na o'twam'. Nanso kasa ne ne nyam', na okom kesie bi aba. O'twam' na Ananse ato bayere no atwene okwanm'. Seisei na Adowa afa. Osee, "Agya Ananse, okom a ete se aba, na die wo de e'ba yanee soo ni?" Ananse see, "Aduane nnye na me 'fie kora." Adowa see, "Me ne wo beko akogye bi." Ananse ne no duruu 'fie; oka kyeree Adowa se, "Obarima bi wo ho, na ode aduane bere me anadwo; nti ko da ntwonom' ho, na se oba a obeka se, 'Asikoto,' na wo so w'agye so se, 'Me de me ti me gye.' Na wo ka sa a, ode aduane no bi begu ho, na w'afa." Adowa see, "Me da w'ase, Aku; Agya Ananse pa'a die me nhunoo wo so 'nipa da." Adowa ko daa ntwonom'. Ankye na Esono aba. Osee, "Asikoto." Adowa see, "Me de me ti me gye," na Esono de aka no, pan! Otwe see, "E! Agya Ananse, okom a ete se' aba, na die wo de e'ba yanee soo ni?" Ananse see, "Wo pe bi a, bera ma yenko me 'fie me nko ma wo bi." Otwe ne Ananse koye. Esono see, "Wo ho ye?" Ananse see, "Me ho ye." Ananse siim' baa ne 'fie. Obefaa bayere no bi, koo yanee so bio. Obefwe se Otwe e'twam' na wato bayere no atwene. Otwe see, "E! Agya Ananse, okom a ete se' aba, na die wo de e'ba yanee soo ni?" Ananse see, "Wo pe bi a, bera ma yenko me 'fie me nko ma wo bi." Otwe ne Ananse koye. Esono see, "Wo ho ye?" Ananse see, "Me ho ye." Ananse siim' baa ne 'fie. Obefaa bayere no bi, koo yanee so bio. Obefwe se Otwe e'twam' na wato bayere no atwene. Otwe see, "E! Agya Ananse, okom a ete se' aba, na die wo de e'ba yanee soo ni?" Ananse see, "Wo pe bi a, bera ma yenko me 'fie me nko ma wo bi." Otwe ne Ananse koye. Ananse see, "Da ntwonom' ha, na anadwo yi m'akoa de aduane beba, na oduru ha a, ose, 'Asikoto,' wo so ka se, 'Me de me ti me gye'." Otwe see, "Me da'se Aku, wo na wo beye sa." Otwe ko daa ho. Anandwo na Esono baye. Osee, "Asikoto." Otwe see, "Me de me ti me gye." Na ode aka Otwe na oda, kum! Na Esono koye. Adeekyeye anopa, na Ananse faa Otwe maa Aso na, osiim' koo Esono 'fie ko maa no akye. Esono see, "Okuamoa ni, wo ho ye?" Okuamoa see, "Pesee!" Na ye'dii nkommo kankan, na Ananse seree 'kwan baa no 'fie. Efei na Ananse de Oyuo ne 'Wansane, Kokotee Asamoa, ne Otromo, ne Oko aye sa’ra. Esono see, “Asikoto!” Esono see, “Me de me ti me gye.” Ananse de saye no sii n’apampam’, na ode dade-bena boo so pan! pan! na amem. Na Ananse baa ne ’fie. Adeekyeye anopa, Esono ka kyeree ne ye’ se, “Me tiri pae me,” na ne ye’see, “Nka, yei die eye aniwu, na Ananse mpo w’abo no nnanson, ne tiri ammpae no, na wannwu, na wo na wo’beka yei; na ko ma Ananse akye, sedie na ono so ye no.” Esono miaa n’ani koreye; oduruu Ananse ho. Ananse see, “Kantinka, wo ho ye?” Esono see, “O! me ho ye.” Ananse see, “Osempa.” Ananse ko boo saye no bio. Onwuno dwoye osiim’ koo Esono ho. Osee, “Asikoto.” Esono see, “Me de me ti me gye.” Ananse de saye no de asi ho, na ode dadebena no abo so, pan! pan! Ananse aba ne ’fie. Efei owo ho’a, na obofuo aba se, “Ananse, nana Esononom se me mmera mmeka nykere wo se, w’adamfo Esono daye a, wansore; nti bera ne bepae ’boom’ fa no hyem’.” Ananse see, “Dabi, y’anhyehye no sa. Ye’ see, die obewuo, na obako e’noa no awe.” Obofuo no ko boo Esono abusuafuo amanee. Ye’see, “Mo nko fa no mmera seisei.” Abofuo no baa Ananse ho, see, “Ye’se bera seisei.” Ananse see, “Mo ntwen me.” Ananse koo wuram’ ko twaa dua, na osenee no dua-nipa; na osenee ’tuo de too ne nsa so, na ode maa Aso se, “Fa di kan ko to babi a ye’se me nko pae boo ho, na wo so gyina ho, na se me ba a, na w’afre me.” Aso maa so koye. Ananse so one abofuo no siim’ koreye. Ananse duruu Esononom anim’, na ye’de aso, ne ’soso, ne akuma, ne ’sekan hyee ne nsam’. Ye’see, “Duom’.” Na ye’de Ananse koo oboo kakraka bi ho. Ye’see, “Paem’ na bo amena na fa Esono tom’.” Ananse koo ho, pe! pe! pe! Ananse wo ho’a, osee, “Yo e!” Osee, “Esononom, mo ate se obi e’fre me.” Esononom see, “So’ho, wo ’pe ’kwan adwane.” Ananse see, “M’ate,” na okoo n’adwuma ho, pe! pe! pe! Na wasan gye so, “Yo e!” Osee, “Obi e’fre me.” Nkrofuo no see, “Ye’mma wo nnko.” Sa, sa, mpen nson. Na efei die ye’see, “Ko tie na bera seisei, na wo dwane a, ye’benya wo.” Ananse koo babi a akua’ba no wo. Efei na Ananse di mmirika, kiri! kiri! kiri! Esononom see, “Ananse aden?” Osee, “E! mo nko na se obofuo bi na ogyina ho; na ose me ntwe me ho, mma no nto ’tuo mmo mo. Sa nti na wafre me afre me se me ntwe me ho.” Na Esononom se ye’fwe wuram’ ho, ye’befwe se obi na ogyina ho no ampa, na ye’de atu. Ananse see, "Agya 'bofuo ku yen ma me." Esononom dwaneye; ene eserem'. Na ye'gyaa Esono a wawu no ho, na Ananse faa no ko weye. Ene se Esononom e'beko 'Serem', na nkra kan no Esononom wo kwayem'.

I know that we all know, whether it is one or another, that one does not know, and that the other does not know.
