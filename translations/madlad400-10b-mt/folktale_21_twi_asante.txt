SEDIE OWEA, ABOSOM-SOME THINGS HAVE NOT HAPPENED

OWEA, Kwaduampon Kyerefo, Opatuo, ne Abosom-Akotere, Odomankoma-wuo, Aferehyia'boa, Onam-bere-bere-be-ko Aburokyire na ye wo ho. Na ye mu fuara enni ye'. Na Adobe be ton no ho maa yen. Adobe sore a, na wako som woi; adiyekye a na wako som woi so; adiyekye a na wako som woi so. Dakoro bi ekoduruu Odoankoma-wuo so, ope Adobe he a na ohu no? Na oko boo Owea ne Opatuo amanee. Na ye see, "Ko fre no." Odomankoma-wuo maa so:

"Adobe o, Adobe o!"

Owea see:

"Okora! oma! oko, oma, oma, oma!"

Na Opatuo see:

"Ye kari gu yi a, m! Ye kari gu yi a, m! m!"

Na se sa mmoa yi mmiensa ye su na asee no no.

I'm sure we all know that, whether it's one or another, the mother is the mother and the father is the father.
