SE YOYE A ABUSUA EKUONA BAA 'MANM'

OBA bi ye'fre no Abena na owo ho; oni ne ose de no ma awadee a, osee, "Dabi." Na oka kyeree ose se, "Da a mehu obarima a mepe no, da no, se me ba, na se wo'nom asra a, megye asratoa de m'abo w'apampam'." Yei na aboa Ekuo tee sa asem yi; odanee ne ho 'nipa siim' baa oba no kurom' ho. Na Abena hunoo no no, ode mmirika koreye, na ose e'nom asra. Ogyee asratoa no, na ode abo n'apampam'. Na da no akura no sofuo boo ose se, "Ye'wura 'ba ahu die ope no." Na ye'ko faa 'barima no koo 'fie. Na oba no kaa se, "Oyi na me pe no." Obarima no nso peneeso, na owaree no. Na mutane no nnyina adan ekuo. Ekaa 'nipa fua pe, na odwaneye baa 'fie. Na oko boo odekuro amanee asem a wahuno. Odekuro ne Ohema boo dwa; oboo yen amanee se eyoye a ye'koreye, na ode sii so se, "Osee oye pa! na waye gyiren!" na ono so aye Ekuo. Oyi see o'ka bi a, na wadan Ekuo. Kurom hofuo nnyina adane Ekuo, ama no aka aberewa fua. Akyire yi na odunsini bi baa kurom' ho. Osee, "A! kuro kakraka, mutane nnim?" Dinn! Na waka bio. Odunsini no see, “Amanee nti na wo nko’a na wo te kuro yim?” Aberewa see, “W’aba a, ko ma menso me ntena me babi.” ’Dunsini see, “Wo ye bayifuo; wo na w’awe kurom’ hafuo nnyina, nti meku wo seisei gye se wo ka.” Aberewa see, “Me nka de a, ko fa homa bekyekyere me, na fa me mantam gyeduam’.” Odunsini bekyekyeree no, na ode no mantam gyeduam’. Na efei die, aberewa boo no amanee nnyina na ode sii so se, “Osee oye pa! na waye gyiren!” Na homa no atete; aberewatia na wadan Ekuo na o’koro no. Yei na odunsini no see, “Se nye aberewa na wakum nkrofuo yi.” Na oko tetee nnuro posaye de guu yawam’, de nsuo guum’; na ode ne bodua toom’; na ode soaa ne nsumankoani. Ode siim’ koo wuram’ ho. Obefwe se Ekuonom gyinagyina ho, na ode bodua boo aduro nom’ de petepetee ye’so, na ye’danedanee mutane bio. Na ekaa Ekuo pa no. Na mutane na ebaa ’fie. Ene se Ekuona dangi ebeba ’manm’; na efiri Abena awadee a okoreye.

We all know that, whether it is one or another, the mother is the mother, and the father is the father.
