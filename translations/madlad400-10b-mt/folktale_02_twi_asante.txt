MPITRIPI NE MPATRAPA

“Therefore, I say to you, I will not go into the kingdom of heaven, but I will go into the kingdom of my Father, which is in heaven, and I will serve him with all my heart, and with all my soul, and with all my strength.”

Ananse se, “Me fa ka sen?” Na ye se, “Wo fa ka se, Mpitiripi ne Mpatrapa ne Anyankama Kyerebi ne Kyerebi-Koso-Adawurampon na yakoto mme ’duasa, na ye se, obi nnka ’sahuro ntafere, na waka ’sahuro atafere, nti na ye se, ‘fa ka’.” Na ye gu Ananse so, na ye gye, na ohuruye, na padie mu kado!

"Nti ne se, da wo behuno Ananse na ota’ padie mu, nankra eka’ no, nankra onenam fam te se Koto.

We all know, whether one or the other, that the father is the father and the mother is the mother.
