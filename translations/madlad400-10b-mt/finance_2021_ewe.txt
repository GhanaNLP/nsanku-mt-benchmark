THE WOMAN WHO WAS KILLED BY HER BROTHER'S DAUGHTER

In the February 2020 election, Ghanaians asked President Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo to continue the good work he and others have been doing for the next four years.

The 2021 Strategy is an important strategy that reflects our commitment to protect the nation, address the challenges posed by the COVID-19 pandemic, and make our contribution to the recovery of Ghana from the COVID-19 pandemic. The Strategy reflects our commitment to drive new transformations in the nation’s development to become economically self-sufficient.

For most of the past four years, we have focused our efforts on addressing a number of key challenges: advancing gender equality, initiating the implementation of commitments to gender equality, implementing a variety of initiatives, supporting self-employment, and advancing gender equality. At the end of February 2019, we introduced major changes to our strategies, reflecting the advancing gender equality standards and international appreciation of Ghana’s progress through the COVID-19 pandemic. The pandemic has had a devastating impact on tourism worldwide, and Ghana has not been unaffected.

The COVID-19 pandemic has brought many challenges to our work and daily lives. Nevertheless, the Government recognizes how the pandemic has affected us as a nation and has put in place various measures to address the issues and keep things running smoothly. We are not completely hopeless, as we rely on many new ways of doing things. The Government is committed to ensuring that we are healthy. Therefore, the Government is committed to empowering the Ghanaian people to be resilient to the pandemic so that we can continue to work hard to get Ghana where we want it to be.

Despite the difficult times we are going through, you have joined us in our progress, and we are striving to make our contribution to our progress. About 1.2 million students are receiving education in secondary schools for their future. We have taken over the problems of closed gas stations and we have repaid the majority of those who deposited money in these stations. In addition to this, we have installed and started to clean the stations and some of them are working on our roads.

In fact, we have faced a number of challenges in achieving our development goals. The Government is working hard at various levels to address these challenges in order to improve our services. For example, we have invested $750 million in Ghana’s Small Offices and Rural Homes through the Small Offices and Rural Homes Action Plan under Budget 2020 and others.

Now, they are asking us to help each other, and to do more together than we have done in the past to move the country forward. We will do this by focusing on the important tasks that we are working on so that we can all progress. We must persevere in our endeavors for the progress of the country, because we have committed ourselves from the bottom of our hearts to do our utmost for the country of Ghana.

The word precedes us all.

This is Ghana!

Interpreting specific concepts of the problem

The Ministry of Transport and Communications (MTC): The Ministry of Transport and Communications (MTC) is responsible for the development of the country’s transport and communications infrastructure. The objective of this ministry is to bring more services to the country so that the people of the country can be employed.

Accounting: It is the function of the government to account to the people for the performance of its operations, the state's finances and its obligations.

Finance and Resources: Manages the Government’s finances and resources for all government departments.

Relief: The effort to alleviate a problem or suffering.

Astronomy, Engineering, Mechanical Sciences, Engineering Studies and Accounting: These studies are essential in the production of literature and research to advance science and technology.

Budget: A budget is a set of rules that a government makes about where to get money from and how to spend it. For example, a family may make a budget for the expenses of a home and how to spend it. (e.g. housing, electricity, water, and food). If the family is in need of money, it must make a budget or reduce its spending.

Insolvency: This occurs when a government is unable to meet its debts or when the debts of the government are greater than its income.

Government Development Budget: This is a budget in which the Government invests in major development projects such as the construction of roads, schools, workplaces, radio and television stations, transportation, health care, roads, etc.

Government Employees’ Salaries: Salaries to government employees: This includes salaries and allowances paid by the government to government employees, Social Security contributions, bonuses and other allowances.
