KPLAYGAME

The COVID-19 pandemic has affected the economy of all countries and Ghana is not an exception. It is the responsibility of the Government that the pandemic has caused suffering, death and economic disruption to the country. It is encouraging to say that we are committed to addressing these issues urgently and have responded with determination. In fact, all of the countries have appreciated our actions. The Government is reviewing the measures it has taken to address the economic issues and ensure that our lives return to normal.

In addition, in response to the challenges and opportunities posed by the pandemic, the Government launched the COVID-19 Pandemic Impact Response, Communication and Facility Management Initiative under the leadership of President Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo and Ghana’s $100 billion budget.

I thank you for your confidence in the Government as we work diligently to bring our promises to fruition. At the same time, we are facing many challenges in terms of our livelihoods. Often, the Bible tells us the steps we need to take in these times. Just as Isaiah 9:10 says, "Our iniquities are broken, and we will put away the gravestones; we have taken away the graves, and we will set up the graves in their place", so the Government is determined to fix things and fulfill its promises. For this reason, the government will continue to implement various measures to alleviate the effects of climate change and improve the quality of life of the nation.

One of the key objectives of the February 2022 Decade is that from now until the next three years, we will advance the issue of self-reliance in the country with a view to enhancing our economy and ensuring stability. In addition, everyone must participate in the Decade of Action in order to provide employment to people, especially young people. Therefore, Government policies will encourage young people to showcase their talents and skills through the great opportunities offered to everyone, in order to enhance the issue of employment across the country.

In order to encourage self-employment and empowerment of young people, the Government will be providing funding opportunities for young people to start or expand their businesses through the “YouStart” Initiative in February 2022.

In the February 2022 Budget, we intend to continue to address the immediate challenges facing the economy and to ensure that the economy recovers from the COVID-19 pandemic. The Government is committed to implementing a range of initiatives that promote youth empowerment and promote self-reliance among young people. As we strive to meet the Government’s vision of ‘Ghana Needs Freedom’, I hope to encourage all stakeholders to participate in these initiatives and to initiate new ones. In addition, they must take part in self-determination and advancement issues for their integration into the nation.
