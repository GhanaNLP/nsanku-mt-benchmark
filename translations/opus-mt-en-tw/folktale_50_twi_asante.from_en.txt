Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa sɛ, nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware (kyerɛ sɛ nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ABSOM KA KYERƐE

BERE bi, na ɔbea bi wɔ hɔ; ɔwoo mma baanu; na da biara da, sɛ ɔsɔre na ɔnoa aduan a, na mmofra no di bi; ná wɔn maame nni bi. Afei ofii ase susuw ho bere tenten, na ɔkɔɔ afuw no mu kɔka kyerɛɛ dua no sɛ, "Mɛma me mma no aba ha akɔbɔ w'asom; na sɛ wɔba a, wotu wo nnubaa mu na wokunkum me mma no. " Kɔ afuw no mu, baabi a ɛwɔ dua no ase; awɔ bi wɔ hɔ a ɛkɔfa bi ba (akyi). " Mmofra no fii hɔ kɔe koduu baabi a nnua no wɔ no. Wɔn mu dodow no ara kae sɛ, "N'ani so da so ara gyina hɔ; nnipa dodow a ɛto so Abien, wɔn dodow yɛ abiɛsa; wɔn dodow yɛ anan, wɔda so ara gyina hɔ; wɔn dodow yɛ baasia, wɔn dodow yɛ ason, wɔn dodow yɛ baawɔtwe, wɔda so ara gyina hɔ; wɔn dodow si aduɔson, wɔn dodow si so; wɔn dodow soso du du du da so ara gyina hɔ; na wɔn mu dodow no da so ara gyina hɔ; wɔn dodow yɛ ahena. " eason, dɛn nti na ɛna no kae sɛ yɛnkɔfa n'awofo?, "Ne nuanom kae sɛ: "Dabi, waka akyerɛ saa dua yi sɛ, sɛ yɛkɔ hɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ wotwa nnua na wotow gu dua yi so; enti mo nyinaa tow gu dua yi so sɛ: "Wotow nnua no kyenee dua no so, na wɔtow kyene! ' Oyii ne nnubaa no fii ne dua mu, na ɔmaa wɔn hwee fam, na ne mma no nyinaa kae sɛ: "Woahuu sɛ yɛkɔɔ hɔ no, na dua no awu ama wɔakum yɛn, enti wɔfaa awɔ no de wɔn kɔmaa wɔn maame. " Ɛdefa asɛm yi ho no, mintumi nwo, enti mede mmofra yi bɛkɔ akɔma wɔn a wɔsom bo no anɔpa, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔkaa ho asɛm sɛ: "Mmofra bi a mawo wɔn, bere a mepɛ sɛ wodidi no, mintumi nnya biribi nni (efisɛ wodi pii na ɔkɔm de wɔn; enti ma wonkum me; enti ma wonkum mmofra a ɔkɔm de wɔn no bi. Na wokum me, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a menya biribi adi; "So ɛno ne asɛm no ankasa? " Afei ɔsomafo no frɛɛ mmofra no. Bere tiaa bi akyi no, woduu baabi a amoa no da, a na nnipa dodow a wɔwom no dodow boro nea wɔn dodow si no ara kae sɛ, "N'anim da so ara gyina hɔ; nnipa dodow a wogyina hɔ; wɔn dodow, wɔn dodow a ɛto so Abiɛsa; wɔn dodow a wogyina hɔ, wɔn dodow a ɛto so Asia no da so ara gyina hɔ; wɔn dodow a ɔto so awotwe, wɔn dodow a ɛto so awotwe; wɔn dodow a wogyina hɔ; wɔn dodow so du a wɔda so du; ne wɔn dodow a wɔda so ara; m'ankasa, m'ani da so ara; m'ani da so ara gyina hɔ; m'ani gye dodow ho; nnipa kakraa bi. Mfe bi a atwam no, "Ne nuanom kae sɛ: " Dɛn nti na sɛ ɔkwan kɛse bi da hɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛfa wuram? ' Wɔn mu dodow no ara kae sɛ, "Sɛ yetwam kɔ hɔ a, ɛnde ɛsɛ sɛ yɛn nyinaa wu. Odwontofo no kae sɛ: "Stool na wɔde asi hɔ, na moatra wɔn so; afei nnipa dodow no ara kae sɛ: Yɛn de, yɛyɛ nnipa a yebetumi atra agua a ɛyɛ fɛ saa so; enti Sire, yɛbɛtra ha yi, "Sɛ obi hwɛ mmofra no a, mɛka akyerɛ wɔn sɛ:" Owu, da a edi hɔ no, wɔbɛfrɛɛ yɛn. " Ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ Death (a ɔte nkwa mu) na wokɔ ne nkyɛn kogye sika adaka a wɔde sika kɔkɔɔ afra mu, sika kɔkɔɔ a wɔwe, asra a wɔde sika kɔkɔɔ ayɛ, sika kɔkɔɔ a wɔde tu kwan, ne sika kɔkɔɔ a wɔde tu kwan no ka sɛ: "Wone yɛn wura, baabiara a wobɛkɔ no, baabiara a wobɛkɔ no, wobɛsan akɔ. " Woduu hɔ; na owu kae sɛ: "Dɛn nti na bere a etwa sɛ obi ba ha no, obi amma ha? Wɔ esum bere mu no, owu kyekyɛɛ mmofra no mmiako mmiako maa wɔn mu biara, na ɔde biako maa ne mma no mu biara, bere a ɔno ankasa ne nnipa dodow bi kɔɔ wɔn nkyɛn; afei owu maa ne sẽ so yɛɛ kɔkɔɔ, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obetumi ne wɔn adi nkra. Owu a ɛto so anum no kae sɛ: "Bere bɛn na wubewu? " Wɔn mu dodow no ara kae sɛ: "Owu da so ara wɔ hɔ, nanso owu akyi pɛɛ no, nnipa dodow no ara kae sɛ, "Sɛ wode sika asra me a, anka mɛkɔ so ada. " Wɔn mu dodow no ara kae sɛ, "Bere bɛn na wubewu? " Na wɔn mu dodow no ara kae sɛ: "Maame, meda so ara adeda, " afei" Owu akyi no, bere bɛn na wobɛka sɛ, "M'agya, sɛ wokɔ na woma me sika kɔkɔɔ a, ɛnde metumi ada bio, "" Owu bae, na ɛno akyi bere tiaa bi no, owu akyi no, owu san sii bio? " Na wɔn mu dodow no ara kae sɛ, "Oo, me papa, mekae sɛ minnya nna. " Na wɔn mu dodow no ara sɔre guanee, na wɔde wɔn kɔɔ nsu no mu, na wɔde wɔn guu baabi a ne nuanom wɔ no, na wɔde ntama ne ntama kataa so; afei de na nsu no ayiri afa wɔn. Afei ɔbarima bi a wɔfrɛ no Owu a ɔwɔ hɔ kae sɛ, "Ho, Owu; obuae sɛ: Dɛn na woreyɛ yi? Na nsu a ɛwɔ nsu a ano yɛ den no mu, ne afei nea wɔde twitwiw aduan mu no renyɛ papa anaa? " Mmea Wu no kae sɛ: "Otu nhaban, ɔde gu mu, na ɔde nsu no guguu mu, na ofii hɔ kɔe. " Bere a ɔkɔe no, wanhwehwɛ wɔn (nea ɛto so anum), na ɔno ankasa kum n'ankasa mma du no nyinaa da a edi hɔ pɛɛ, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, owu sɔree, na ɔde ogya no traa hɔ. Wɔn mu fã kɛse no ara kae sɛ: "Owu, akokɔ te wo nufu so, na ɔka sɛ: "Owu, aboa bi a ɔda hɔ, na okum me. " N'atirimpɔw ne sɛ, "Nnipa dodow no ara kɔe na wɔde bae, na otuu aboa no kɔe; na wankum no; nnipa dodow no ara kae sɛ, " O, ɛda a aboa yi bɛtra hɔ no, ɛhɔ na me ne no bɛtra; ɛno akyi no, " wɔn mu fã kɛse no ara kɔɔ dan no mu kɔfaa ne bag a wɔde sika kɔkɔɔ agu mu, ne nneɛma nyinaa. Ɔkae sɛ: "Ne maame wu, biribiara nni hɔ a ebetumi agye me nkwa sɛ me nsa bɛka bonsu no, na mede ahyɛ bag yi mu, na mede abrɛ wo. Oduu kurow no awiei; ɔkae sɛ, "Ho, Maame Owu, ɛhɔ na mede me nsɛm firii me, nanso sɛ wonyɛ ɔkwasea a ɔyɛ pɛ a, anka metumi ama wo ho atɔ wo w'ade nyinaa; anka me nuanom a me ne wɔn bae no nso betumi aguan afi mu; na anka metumi akum wo mma du no nyinaa nso? Na me de, owu na merekɔ; na wo, abofra a ɔte sɛ eyi, baabiara a wugye w'ahome no, ɛhɔ na mɛkɔ; w'ani so tetew wo, w'ani so tetew wo, w'ani so tetew wo, w'ani so tetew wo, ne wu mu; ne dodow boroo ne nuanom so, na wonam wim. " Owu reba, enti momma yɛnhwehwɛ ɔkwan bi a yɛbɛfa so aguan. Ɔkae sɛ: "Hwɛ, mihuu mmofra yi wɔ ha kɛkɛ, na ɛhe na wɔkae sɛ: Nsu ho yɛ den sen wɔn mu dodow no ara, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuanom sɛ: " Mɛma nsu agu wɔn so; ne nuanom kae sɛ: " Ɔne yɛn akokum yɛn, na yɛatra ha, na yɛaka sɛ: Ɛhe na worekɔ yi? ' " Owu kaa sɛ, "Ah, ha da a wowɔ hɔ yi, woahu amane. " Owu kaa sɛ: " Wo, abofra a wote hɔ, 'Kyeree-hene-hene, Kyere-hene, a ɔyɛ mmofra no mu biako hwee ase. Na owu nso kɔɔ dua bi a wɔfrɛ no Naleon so, na n'anom asɛm ne sɛ: "Wo, ɔbea kɛse, wo nso, wo, wo a wo ne no te; na owu nso wui, na n'ani so tetew no, na n'ani so tetew no, na ne nsam paee no, na ne nuanom sɔre gyinae, na wonyanee no ma owuu owu, na wokunkum no. Ɔkae sɛ: "Woakum me, na wo nso woasan anyan me; ɛde besi- da koro wo ne wo benya asɛm bi; afei wɔn nyinaa fii ase tuu mmirika prɛko pɛ, kriririi, na afei de, na wɔakodi wɔn akyi, bere a na wɔrekɔ no, na asubɔnten kɛse bi wɔ hɔ, na na na wonim sɛnea wɔn nkutoo tumi guare, na na na na wonnim sɛnea wɔn nkutoo tumi fa. Mmofra no gyinaa ɔfã nohoa; sui, sui, sui; wɔn anom wosowee; dodow kabea mu no, ɔdanee ɔbo. Ɔkae sɛ: "Oo, mmofra yi, munnyina hɔ, na momfa ɔbo mmɔ wɔn hwene mu; na sɛ ɔhwɛ fam a, ohu sɛ ɔbo bi da hɔ; ɔfa na ɔtow kyenee; na sɛnea wokurakura ɔbo yi no, ɔkae sɛ: "Ɛyɛɛ me, na mede me gyinaa ɔfã nohoa. " Minni asɛm biara a mɛka akyerɛ wo, nanso nea meka kyerɛ wo ara ne sɛ: Kɔ fie na kɔhyɛ wo fie, na dan kɔ anyame nketewa no biako mu, na sɛ obi pɛ sɛ ɔfa wo a, ka kyerɛ me; sɛ mepɛ a, megyaw no hɔ; nanso hwɛ sɛnea m'ani begye ho (nea mepɛ biara). Wɔn a wɔkyerɛ sɛ wɔyɛ anyame no baa wiase, na wofi mmofra nketewa a wɔn dodow si 11 no mu.

Eyi, m'asɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) Sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom begye atom sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na wɔn a aka no nso, wubetumi ayi me ayɛ (nea wɔreka no nti).
