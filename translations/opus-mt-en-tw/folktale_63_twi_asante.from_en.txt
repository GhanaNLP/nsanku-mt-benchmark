Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa sɛ, nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware (kyerɛ sɛ nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ABSOM KA KYERƐE

BERE bi a atwam no, na obi a ɔtɔn nam ne ne yere te akuraa bi ase, na na wodi hia, na na wɔte hɔ ko ma wɔn asetra. Afei de na wɔwɔ hɔ, na ne yere bisabisaa no nsɛm kae sɛ: Sɛ wukum nam a, wode ma, anaasɛ wotɔn no (aba no), ma enti sɛ wokɔ wuram na woamfa nam biara ankɔ a, mɛsan akɔ m'akuraa hɔ, na wanantew kwae no mu, na wanhu aboa biara. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ: "Ohuu asiane bi, nanso bere a na ɔresusuw ho no, ohui sɛ Duyker nam yoder so, na ɔkae sɛ wo, Duyker, bekum wo; na hwɛ, awɔ no di n'akyi, ky! Ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ: "Me de, minnim nea wɔfrɛ no Tano, enti memfa wo nkɔ me yere Tno nkyɛn, na ɔnka nkyerɛ wo sɛ: Me de, m'ani begye ho, na maka akyerɛ me sɛ, "Herro, enti metumi akɔfa wo akɔ, na mama me yere nso asɔre. ' " Ɔfaa no (Tano) de no too ne ti so, bere a na ɔne no nam no, ohyiaa Owu. Ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, "Hunter, Enher, so worema me babea a ɔto so anan a wɔfrɛ no de m'ani ato asiane mu?," Agya no, m'ani yɛ kɔkɔɔ; na mepɛ sɛ mede nam kɔma me yere, bere a mekɔɔ Duyker no, na bere a merekum no no no, ɔkae sɛ oguan sɛ ɔbɛbɔ me ho ban; na minnim hwee. Saa asɛm yi (saa bere no) kae sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mede kɔ fie, sɛnea na ɛte (a ɛkae) no nya so mfaso kɛse. u, na wubenya biribi de atua me ka, na saa asotwe no ne nea mogya hia ara, na ebia wowɔ honam; "Nea osua koraa no ka kyerɛɛ Owu sɛ: "Ro, owura no, mate sɛ:" Aoni no faa Tano, na ɔbaa ne nkyɛn no, ɔtoo dwom bi, na ɔde ne ho kɔbɔɔ nnwontofo kuw no mu. Da a edi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi, na bere a ebinom yaree no, wɔde no kɔɔ Tano; (Tano) kae sɛ: "Sɛ wuwu a, anka ɔrewu. Saa na yehu sɛ wɔn a wɔsom sɛ anyame nketewa no dɔɔso; enti nkurɔfo kɔ Tano kogye ne mma no bi.

Eyi, m'asɛm a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
