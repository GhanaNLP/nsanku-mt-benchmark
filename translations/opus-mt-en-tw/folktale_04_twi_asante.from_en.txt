SƐNEA ENTI A ƐSƐ SƐ YƐTE SƐ YƐWƆ NEA EDIDI SO YI HO

SƐNEA aboa biara te mu no, saa mmoa yi bi na okum wɔn; afei, saa mmoa yi bi na okum wɔn, na otuu nam no gui, na otuu wɔn nyinaa gui, na ɔde aboa biako hyɛɛ ɔdan biako mu ma. Ɛtɔ mmere bi a, me werɛ fi sɛ mɛka saa nsɛm yi pɛn; bere a karkafo no kɔɔ wuram no, ogyaw ne ba no hɔ bere nyinaa wɔ fie; da koro bi, bere a abofra no te hɔ no, ohuu White (ab) no; na ne nyinaa bae mpofirim. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Ho, anwummere, baabi a w'agya wɔ no, ɛhɔ na wo papa wɔ. " Na abofra no faa mmoa no ti de wɔn too santen tenten (sɛnea yɛbɛka no no) fi ha beduu yoder; na ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ: "M'ani gyina wɔn, na ogyinaa mmofra no kɔn ho, na ɔkaa wɔn ho asɛm sɛ: Ne nyinaa yɛ aboa bɛn?

Ɔno (abofra no) kae sɛ:

Agya yi na ɔyɛ aboa ketewa, Adowa, Adowa, Adowa, Adowa, Kwadwo Berefi Adowa, Paapa, kum Adowa yi, bere a tuo no fii hɔ no, Mikunkum Paapa, Asubɔnten bi a wɔfrɛ no Adoira!

EBIA aboa bɛn ni?

NÁ eyi yɛ Otwe, Duyker, Duyker, Duyker (a ɛyɛ din no) Oglele-un-un-Cor-isis-theter) a Paapa ne Duyker, bere a tuo no fii adi no!

EBIA aboa bɛn ni?

Paapa a wɔfrɛ no Oyuo, Oyuo, Oyuo, Oyuo, Oyu Kwabi, kum Oyuo yi, bere a tuo no fii hɔ no, mikunkum no!

EBIA aboa bɛn ni?

Nea ɛyɛ nwonwa ne sɛ, eyi ne kuw kɔkɔɔ, Abedie, Abere, Abedie, Paapa, kum Abedie yi, bere a tuo no fii hɔ no, Miyii Paapa, Seaira!

EBIA aboa bɛn ni?

Wiɛ, eyi ne Otromo, Batmo, Barc, Bara, Basan, Sa 1955's Baba, Paapa, kum saa BaBa yi, bere a tuo no fii hɔ kɔe no, Miyii Paapa, Asubɔnten bi a wɔfrɛ no Nileirifa!

EBIA aboa bɛn ni?

NÁ Eyi ne Wansane a wɔfrɛ no Wsane, anwack, anwak-buck, Mmomire, Mmomire a ɔyɛ ɔbarima no, Paapa kum eyi, bere a tuo no fii hɔ no, Imang, Seaiira!

EBIA aboa bɛn ni?

Wiɛ, eyi ne Kotoko, Nauterine, Cuupine, Gyanibi, a na ɔyɛ ɔsraani no, Paapa kum saa tuo yi bere a tuo no fii hɔ no, Iangmmko, Seaira!

EBIA aboa bɛn ni?

M'ani gyee eyi ho paa. Ná wɔfrɛ no Ekuo, na na wɔfrɛ no Aempi, na na wɔfrɛ no Aempi, na Paapa nso di awu. Bere a tuo no fii ase no, miyii Paapa, Nileiiinia no fii hɔ!

EBIA aboa bɛn ni?

NÁ Eyi yɛ Aornene, Cob, Cob, Cob, Canitan, Cb, Cob, Paapa, kum Cob, bere a tuo no fii hɔ no, Miyii Paapa, Nile mu yare!

EBIA aboa bɛn ni?

Wiɛ, eyi ne Oton, Hartbeest, Hartbeest, Aunum Kwoda, Papa kum Hartbeest, bere a tuo no fii hɔ no, Imanga Papa, Seania!

EBIA aboa bɛn ni?

NÁ Eyi ne Oko, Roan, Roan, Roan, Paapa, kum saa Roan yi, bere a tuo no fii hɔ no, Miyii Papa, Nileinia, fii hɔ!

EBIA aboa bɛn ni?

Wiɛ, eyi ne Aboa Kesie, Greatae, Greatere, Greatee, Greataee, EderSi, Ɔhene a na ɔwɔ koko so a na ɔyɛ Ɔhene no, Paapa kum saa Agya Kɛse yi, bere a tuo no fii hɔ no, Metow hyɛɛ Agya, Nilei so!

EBIA aboa bɛn ni?

Wiɛ, eyi ne Esono a wɔfrɛ no Esono, an, The Greater, a ɔno nkutoo nantew no, nea obubuu asau no so no, Paapa kum saa nam yi, bere a tuo no fii hɔ no, metow hyɛɛ Agya, Asubɔnten bi a wɔfrɛ no Nileiria so!

EBIA aboa bɛn ni?

Wiɛ, eyi ne Susono, a wɔfrɛ no Susono, susono, an, Paapa, kum eyinom, bere a tuo no fii hɔ no, Miyii Agya, Nile mu nsu ho bannia!

EBIA aboa bɛn ni?

NÁ eyi yɛ Emie, a ɛyɛ ifoa-Hobo, abro-Ho-Hoo, a ɔyɛ ɔfo a odi kan, Paapa kum saa da yi-Host-Ho, bere a tuo no fi hɔ kɔ no, Mekoi Agya, Nileinia!

EBIA aboa bɛn ni?

Wiɛ, eyi ne Kokotee a wɔfrɛ no Kokotee, an a wɔfrɛ no "Akuda, " a ɔyɛ aboa bɛn ni? '

" Eyi ne Ofosuo, Water-bck, Water-buck, Water-buck, Water-bck, nsu-bck, nsu-buck, nsu yi kum Paapa, bere a tuo no fi hɔ no, Miyii Paapa, Imdadamira!

" Aboa bɛn ni? "

" Eyi ne Kwaduo, Yededro-duyker, Nana-duyker, a wɔfrɛ no Nar-duyker, Nad duyker, Boappa, a wokum no no, bere a tuo no fii hɔ no, Paapa kum saa Aram yi.

" Aboa bɛn ni? "

" Eyi yɛ iiresefo, Bassh-pororerine, Badsh-porlerine, the Bad-pororine, the Lesh-porlerine, bere a tuo no fii hɔ no!, Paapa kum saa beae yi.

" Aboa bɛn ni? "

" Eyi ne Ebuo, Cololoo, Cololous, Cololous, Yalous, Kumaa, Paapa, kum Cololous, bere a tuo no fii hɔ no, me Papa Iangwwwwwwwbeda! "

Na afei oduu baabi a na White-afo no ti wɔ, na ɔkae sɛ, "Na ɔno nso kaa sɛ: Ɔno (abofra no).

" Agya mmra ansa (Me din), efisɛ eyi yɛ ehu. "

Ɔsɔfo panyin no kae sɛ: "Metee sɛ merekɔ; meresan aba, na sɛ w'agya ba fie a, mibisa no din sɛ: "Agya no san fi wuram bae, na ɔno (abofra no) kae sɛ:" Dɛn na mahu yi: " Dɛn asɛm ni? ' Da koro bi, emu biako koduu ne nan ase, na ɔkae sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meka ne din, nanso mekae sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ odi kan ba, sɛnea agya no kae no: "O Agya no kae sɛ: Me de, biribiara nni hɔ a yɛbɛsan ahu bio, na sɛ ɛba saa a, yɛn werɛ befi. Ɔyɛɛ no bio de ne ti hyehyɛɛ mu (abofra no) bio, na ɔma ɔtoo aboa no ti din mmiako mmiako; na bere a ɛto so abien no, oduu aboa no ti so, na ɔkae sɛ:

" Agya mmra ansa (Me din), efisɛ eyi yɛ ehu. "

Ɔsɔfo panyin no kae sɛ: "Nea eye ara ne sɛ, sɛ meba na woka kyerɛ me a, me nso meyi wo ti afi mu; " Abofra no kae sɛ:" Awurade adwo, na agya no san baa fie ne (abofra no) na ɔkae sɛ: "Sɛ woamfa wo ho anhyɛ oprehyɛn yi mu yiye a, anka dɛn na wobɛyɛ? " Asɛm no san bisae sɛ: "Nea ɛto so abien no, abofra no san bae, na ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, sɛ wanka a, nea ɔbɛyɛ ama me no bɛyɛ nea ɛboro me yafunu so (nea metumi adi). Ɛno akyi bere tiaa bi no, ohui sɛ po so hyɛn no reba ntɛm.

Bere a oduu fie hɔ ara pɛ na ɔne awɔ no kasae, na ɔkae sɛ, sɛ edu- da no a, dɛn na ebesi? Fa mmoa no ti bɔ mu, na ka kyerɛ wɔn sɛ (agyaw wɔn maa n'Agya) fi he na mede (nea ɛwɔ he) bɛto hɔ, na nea ɔka ni: Dɛn na wobɛka? Abofra no kae: "Ná mekae aboa yi din kɛkɛ, na na metaa gyina ne kɔn ho ko tia ne kɔn, na na na eyi nso te dɛn?

M'ani so no, Paapa ne Ottwe, a ɔyɛ Ottwefo, a wɔfrɛ no "Asuk-buck, " no, na okum wɔn a wɔtoo wɔn tuo no, na bere a atuo no fii hɔ no, Miyii Paapa, Nile mu yare!

Na ɔtoo mmoa no nyinaa din saa ara, na eduu baabi no, ɔkae sɛ: "Bobania yi de, ne bo yɛ den, eh He (abofra no) kae sɛ: "Eyi yɛ Etwie, agya bi a ne ti nhwi yɛ den, Paapa, kum no bere a atuo no fi hɔ kɔ no!

Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ: "O, nanso woahu asiane bi; m'aso biako ate pɛn, na ɔfoforo no da so te hɔ; na ɔsan bae bebisaa no bio, na abofra no (n'aso abien no) kae sɛ: M'aso abien no ate afei de, m'aso ketewa a mede ato m'anim no nkutoo na ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ; m'aso a seesei mede ato n'agya no so, na mede ama no, na waka n'agya no. Na abofra no kae sɛ: "Na ɛhe na worekɔ yi, ɛhe na wowɔ? Na abofra no kae sɛ: N'ani kyerɛ ara na mede me nan sii.

M'ani gye eyi ho paa. Ná wɔfrɛ no Etwie, al, al, alanini, Paapa, Paapa, kum saa owudifo yi, bere a tuo no fii hɔ no, metow hyɛɛ Paapa, Nileinia so!

Ná n'ano da so ara gyina hɔ, na sɛ edu fefɛw bere a abofra no Agya bɛma tuo no apue, Tomm; na tuo no nnyigyei no da so wɔ hɔ, na n'anim ayɛ dinn no, Paapa bae beyii nam no tow kyenee baabiara.

Ɛno nti na mpanyimfo no ka sɛ ɛnsɛ sɛ yɛn nkutoo gyaw abofra bi wɔ fie hɔ, efisɛ adowa no yɛɛ saa, na ɛkaa kakraa bi ma anka awɔ no kyeree ne ba no.

Eyi, m'asɛm a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
