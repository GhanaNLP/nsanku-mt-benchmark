Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa sɛ, nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware (kyerɛ sɛ nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ENTI A WƆWƆ NEA ENTI A WƆWƆ HƆ NO, N'ANI YƐ NA ƆWƆ NEA EDIDI SO YI HO

NÁ ƐYƐ dɛn ara a, ɔwaree Nasia, a ɔyɛ ifo no maame no babea, na ɔkɔɔ Naya (Obrofo) nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Da no, wo maame, Nasia wu bɛka kyerɛ me sɛ woapaw no ama ayi ase amanne. "

Bɛyɛ nnansa akyi no, wɔbaa hɔ bɛka kyerɛɛ no sɛ Nasia, a ɔyɛ ifo no maame no awu; afei de na wɔde mogya-Brekuo, Ɔhene Jay, Kwao, ne Odan, nguan no, kasa kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Wɔaba sɛ wɔrebɛkɔ ayi no ase, enti ɛsɛ sɛ wɔka kyerɛ me (a). Afei ɔsɔfo panyin no maa ne manfo nyinaa boaboaa wɔn ho ano, te sɛ Ofol asafo bi nhyiam ara pɛ. Ɔkae sɛ: "Sɛ edu anwummere a me ho dwo me a, mɛba; bag a ɔde yɛɛ bag no mu, na ɔde Berekuo, Ɔhene Jayo, Kwakuo, ne Odsan, ne n'atade kaa n'atade ho. Afei ɔkasa kyerɛɛ mmoa no kae sɛ, "Sɛ miduu nyame no nkyɛn a, ɛsɛ sɛ wusu.

"Kur Zusu, ku!

Kurkusu, ku! ku. "

Na abɛn no teɛɛm (ne nne):

Ɔkae sɛ: "Kkum! "

Na nguan no teɛɛm (ne nne):

" Me! "

Na eyi yɛ Kwaku no sũ, efisɛ ɔman no nyinaa kae sɛ: "Woayɛ no yiye, ha yi ara ne ɔbarima biako a otumi su na ɔbɔ abubuw wɔ akwan horow abiɛsa so. Bere a Kwakuko huu saa aduan yi no, ɔkae sɛ (meremfa bi mma Berekuo, na meremfa mma Kwakuo, efisɛ madi mmuada bere tenten. Afei, ɔne wɔn kɔɔ so traa hɔ, na anyame no kae sɛ, "Yɛbɛkɔ so adi afahyɛ awotwe yi wɔ yɛn ayi da koro da biara da. Afei de na anyame no te nnipa a wɔahyiam no mu, na ɔmaa wɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ ade pa a ɔyɛe da a edi hɔ no, obetumi asan ayɛ bio, efisɛ na ɔne n'asafo mu mpanyimfo ahyiam (sɛ wobetie a). " Da a edi hɔ no, m'ani so tetew me no, m'ani so tetew me; " Na ' Donrekuo, ne Kwakuo, ne Odan kae sɛ, " Yɛn nso, ɔkɔm de yɛn, enti mugyaw yɛn, na moatew mo ho afi mmoahwɛfo no ho, na moahuruw kɔɔ mmoahwɛfo no nkyɛn, na moa yɛn ho kɔbɔɔ wɔn ho, na moahuruw hyɛɛ wɔn so, na moanhuruw hyɛɛ wɔn so, na moankunkum mmoahwɛfo no. ' Nea enti a ɛte saa ne sɛ na n'ani awu.

Eyi, m'asɛm a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
