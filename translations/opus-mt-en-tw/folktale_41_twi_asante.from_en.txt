Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa sɛ, nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware (kyerɛ sɛ nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware)

SƐ WƆDE W'ANI TO WO HO A, SƐ W'ANI NSO A WƆDE MA WO HO N'ANI N'AMƆ A, N'ANI N'AMMM NO

BERE bi na ɔbea bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔwoo mma baasa, na na anuanom no mu panyin ka kyerɛɛ wɔn maame sɛ ɔmma wɔn sika fam dɔte na wɔmfa nkɔ; wɔn mu akumaa no nso kae sɛ ɔmpɛ sɛ ɔkɔ, na wɔka wɔn ho. Afei wɔn maame maa ɔpanyin no sika kɔkɔɔ (wɔwoo no) ne sika kɔkɔɔ kɔkɔɔ kɔkɔɔ a na ɛsom bo te sɛ bo a na ɛsom, ne sika fam dɔte a na ɛsom ma mmofra nkumaa no; bere a nneɛma mu bɛdaa hɔ no, mpanyimfo no kɔe, na wogyaw abofra no (sɛ́ obedi n'akyi) no hɔ brɛoo. Ɔka kyerɛɛ asafo mu mpanyimfo no sɛ: "Wɔkɔɔ so ara buaa no sɛ, " Kɔ, na wubehyia abofra bi; tɔ no, na ɔnka sɛ ɔbɛtɔ no, ɔbɛbɔ no, na ɔtɔɔ no; na sɛ ɔda so ara tɔ no a, ɔne no san kɔ ne maame nkyɛn. Ɔkae sɛ: "Sɛ ɛba sɛ mpanyimfo bi a mihyiaa wɔn no antɔ apataa yi a, na metɔ, bere a wɔn a wɔyɛ m'ankasa mpanyimfo no antɔ no?, "" Wɔka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ, sɛ woanhwɛ a, mehwee wo, na ɛsɛ sɛ wosan gye wo, na woma wo maame sika no bi ka sɛ: "Ɛyɛɛ dɛn na me yɔnko ba no nyaa sika kɔkɔɔ? " Ɔkɔɔ so faa mpanyimfo no wɔ akuraa bi ase, na wɔnoa nhabamma, nanso wonni nam biara a wɔde bɛka wɔn ho, abofra no kae sɛ: "Wɔde hyɛɛ nkwan no mu, na wɔde hyɛɛ nkwan no mu, na ɔde maa no sɛ ɔmfa ne ba no ti nnye sika kɔkɔɔ, na ohuu sika kɔkɔɔ no wɔ n'atade mu. Ɛda a edi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, mpanyimfo no fii adi, na bere a wɔkɔe no wohyiaa ɔbarima bi, na n'ani so tetew no wɔ nneɛma a na okura no atifi; bere a ɔne mpanyimfo no bae ara pɛ no, ɔkae sɛ, "Wɔmaa mmuae yi so, " na wokɔɔ so hyiaa abofra no, na sɛ ohyiaa no a, ɔbɛbɔ nokware no. Ɔkae sɛ: "Wo yɔnko panyin no kae sɛ tɔ akasanoma yi, na sɛ woantumi antɔ a, mehwee wo, na mekɔɔ wo maame nkyɛn. " Ɔpanyin a ɔwɔ akuraa no ase kae sɛ: "Mmofra, momfa awɔ mmra mmɛtotɔ bi mmrɛ me. " Abofra no kae sɛ: "Ne bo yɛ sika kɔkɔɔ a emu duru yɛ sika kɔkɔɔ (yi). Ɔkae sɛ: "Fa saa aboa yi ka wo ho, na hu abofra bi, fa no kɔ, na tɔ no; ka kyerɛ no sɛ: " Ne yɔnko no faa no kɔe, na ohyiaa abofra no. Ɔkae sɛ: "Mo mpanyimfo ka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mototɔ saa aboa yi, na sɛ mo antɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ mehwee mo de mo san kɔ mo maame nkyɛn. Afei, bere biara a abofra no bɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛda no, na otua ne nan ho ka. Ti a odi kan no kae sɛ: "Mɛtɔ aboa yi afi onipa a ɔwɔ ne nkyɛn no hɔ; na abofra no aka sɛ, ɛyɛ me dea. Ná mpanyimfo no rekɔ; wohyiaa akorɔmfo bi a wowiaa ɔhene bi funu, na akorɔmfo no kae sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ wotɔ funu yi. Sɛ ɔka sɛ ɔretotɔ amu yi a, abofra no ka kyerɛ no sɛ: "E! Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ metɔ funu ma wo, abofra no kae sɛ: "Biribiara a mede bɛyɛ me no renyɛ me papa, na mmom mo mpanyimfo no kae sɛ, sɛ motɔ a, yɛmmɔ mo mmaa, na yɛmfa mo mma no mmrɛ mo, na wɔmfa mo mmra, na mommfa mo nsam, dodow ahe na wɔnka, na mommfa mo nsa nnka, na mommfa mo nsa nka sika kɔkɔɔ no bi, na wontua mo ka. " Afei osiim kɔɔ akuraa bi ase, na ɔkɔɔ ofie bi mu kɔsrɛɛ aduan. Abofra kumaa no kae sɛ: "Obiara nni hɔ a ɔyɛ ofie ha; me nana, meka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ: Da koro yɛ aberewa, nanso ɔbea yi de, yentumi ntra ase nhwɛ no. Bere a abofra no redidi awie no, ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, sɛ meba fie hɔ a, mahu afuw mu panyin no; efisɛ afei de, akorɔmfo na wɔmaa me sika kɔkɔɔ a emu duru yɛ kɔkɔɔ ne nea emu yɛ duru. Wɔfaa abofra no kɔe, na wɔde no kyerɛɛ wɔn; wofii adi de no bae, na wosiee no yiye; na wɔkae sɛ afei de, wobedi nkonim ama ɔhene Panyin no; enti bere a ne nuanom mpanyimfo tee ne ho asɛm no, wɔbaa hɔ, na wɔkae sɛ, abofra no ne no benya ayɔnkofa a emu da hɔ; ná minim sɛ ne nkoa no befi hɔ. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, sɛ wokɔ baabiara, na sɛ wo nua kumaa no ka sɛ ɔne wo bɛkɔ a, wo ne no nkɔ no.

Eyi, m'asɛm a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
