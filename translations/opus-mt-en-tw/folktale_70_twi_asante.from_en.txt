NEA WƆDE MA YƐN HO NSƐM

BERE bi a atwam no, na mmerante baasa yi nyinaa yɛ baasa; na wɔn nyinaa dɔ abeawa biako; nanso saa mmerante baasa yi gyaw Bontfii hɔ kɔɔ Cape Coast, kae sɛ wɔrekɔtotɔ nneɛma; bere a woduu Cape Coast no, na wɔn mu biako kura ahwehwɛ; ɔfaa ahwehwɛ, na ɔde wɔn guu ahwehwɛ mu, na ɔde wɔn guu ahwehwɛ mu hwɛɛ ahwehwɛ mu, na ohuu wɔn wu no, na oyii ne ho adi wɔ ɔdan no mu. Ɔkae sɛ: "Wɔn a wɔdɔ yɛn no awuwu; wɔn mu biako kae sɛ: "Mɛma yɛadu Bontwe ntɛm ara na yɛakum no. Ɔfoforo nso kɔe na onyaa aduru de guu toa mu; na ɔfaa ne nantwi de no hyɛɛ aduru no mu, na ɔde amu no hyɛɛ mu. Ɔkae sɛ: "Cororpe, wɛn; ɔdae, nyan, " seesei nnipa baasa yi asɔre; nanso wɔn baasa yi de, wɔn mu nea eye sen biara nen?
