Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa sɛ, nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware (kyerɛ sɛ nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware)

NEA ENTI A NEA ENTI A ƆSUUO (FRHHHHHSMSSEL), MA WƆDE MA MA W'ANI NSOOW, NA ƐYƐ NEA WƆWƆ NEA ƐWƆ NO HO

NEA ENTI a ɛte saa ne sɛ, na ababaa bi a wɔfrɛ no Oswa, Opiire, 'Bonko, Sahrko, ne mmoa (a wɔte nsu mu) nyinaa wɔ hɔ, na ɔpɛ bere bae, na nsubɔnten no nyinaa bae, na ɛmaa mmoa yi nyinaa ho yeraw wɔn kɛse, na wɔdaa anhwea no so (wɔ asubɔnten no mu). Awiei koraa no, wɔkae sɛ: "Na asiane yi a yɛahu yi nti, momma yɛnkɔ Nyanaonpon, a ɛwɔ hɔ no, na yensu nka nkyerɛ no sɛ osu ntɔ, efisɛ mmoa no nyinaa maa ne nne so (na wɔtoo dwom no).

" So ɛnyɛ me, Oka's Abena, a seesei watu kɔ ha no, na mitu kɔ hɔ seesei?

Mehuruw gyina me nsa akyi na ama matumi afrɛ wo.

Miyii m'atade mu nhene a wɔfrɛ no buroa no sii me nan so.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔyɛ me yere, Abuya. "

Na osu antɔ, na Opiire nso bae, na ɔtoo dwom sɛ:

" So ɛnyɛ me, Opiire Abena, a seesei otu kɔ ha, na seesei watu kɔ hɔ?

Mehuruw gyina me nsa akyi na ama matumi afrɛ wo.

Miyii m'atade mu nhene a wɔfrɛ no buroa no sii me nan so.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔyɛ me yere, Abuya. "

Nanso osu antɔ, naBonko nso bae, na ɔtoo dwom sɛ:

Bonko Abena a seesei watu akɔtra ha no ka sɛ: " So ɛnyɛ me anaa? '

Mehuruw gyina me nsa akyi na ama matumi afrɛ wo.

Miyii m'atade mu nhene a wɔfrɛ no buroa no sii me nan so.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔyɛ me yere, Abuya. "

Nanso osu antɔ, na hwɛ kwan sɛ mmoa no nyinaa besu, na nea ɔkae ara ne sɛ: "So mepɛ sɛ misu kakra ka sɛ: Sɔre kakra! " Nanso, ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ," Wosu a, ɛnyɛ papa, na dɛn na wohwɛ kwan sɛ ebefi wo su no mu aba? " Nanso nsu a ɛtɔɔ so abiɛsa no kae sɛ: "Enti wɔteɛɛm sɛ: "Na ɔno nso maa so (too dwom no).

" So ɛnyɛ me, Auuso Abena, a seesei watu akɔtra ha, na seesei watu kɔ hɔ?

Mehuruw gyina me nsa akyi na ama matumi afrɛ wo.

Miyii m'atade mu nhene a wɔfrɛ no buroa no sii me nan so.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔyɛ me yere, Abuya. "

Na osu kɛse a ɛwɔ ne mmati so ne sɛ, "Na afei de ɔma ne ho so, na ɔma ne ho so, na osu tɔ gu ne so, na osu tɔ gu ne so! Afei saa nipa yi (a afei de) sɔre kɔɔ nsu no mu (bere a wohuruw kɔɔ mu no), abɔde no nyinaa kɔɔ mu. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Ausususo a ɔmaa ne nsubɔnten sen kɔɔ sikakorabea hɔ no: Eyi, m'asɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
