Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa sɛ, nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware (kyerɛ sɛ nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA NA WƆDE N'ANI TRIBE KA KYERƐE

TETEFO no ka sɛ abɔmmɔfo (e) bi kɔɔ wuram; ɔbae bɛhwɛɛ amoa bi mu, na ogyinaa hɔ wɔ hɔ. Afei, bere a saa nkurɔfo yi huu yare mmoawa no, obiako kae sɛ, Agya a ne ho yɛ den yi na ɔreba abɛfa me; na nea ɔkae ne sɛ: Agya a ɔwɔ ahoɔden, ba bɛfa me kɔ, na meremfa me nkɔ, efisɛ sɛ mepɛ sɛ miyi wo a, ɛnde gye sɛ wunya nea mepɛ, na ɛsɛ sɛ woyɛ (merentumi nhu). Wiɛ, sɛ saa na ɛte ankasa a, ɛnde na mede nokware bi a oyii no adi kyerɛɛ ɔman no nyinaa no bɛkɔ (n'akuraa) ase da koro, bere a na aboa no wɔ hɔ, Osebo no de nam pii brɛɛ me no. Bere foforo no, nea ɔhwɛ sika so no daa no ase, na bere bi nso, na nea ɔhwɛ sika so no wɔ hɔ, na Osusie a ɔwɔ hɔ no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, n'ani begye sika kɔkɔɔ bi ho? Otuu amoa bi wɔ dan no ase, na ɔkɔɔ dan no mu, na ɔkɔfaa sika kɔkɔɔ kɔkɔɔ kɔkɔɔ kɔkɔɔ a ɛwɔ ne nyinaa, na ɔde kɔmaa asau no kae sɛ: A.ku. Da foforo no, ɔtee sɛ ebinom rekyia no. Ɔbarima no bae; na ɔkae sɛ: "Wo de, agya Pa, wo ho yɛ fɛ, wo ho yɛ fɛ. " Na osiim kɔɔ hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn a wɔtɔn sika kɔkɔɔ a ayera no sɛ, mo sika fam dɔte a ayera no, na akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ, Romania bae bɛfaa no de kɔmaa wɔn a wodi ako no. Ɔkae sɛ, bere a wɔde nkrante no duu kwan no so no, ɛho behiae sɛ me ho tɔ me, na asomafo no kae sɛ: "M'ani nnye ho, owifo, munguan; na sɛ mopɛ a, meremfa ahama no nto so, na memma no kwan, na memma no kwan mma ɔnkɔ. Ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, ɔmaa ne ho tɔɔ no, bere a aboa a ne ho yɛ den paa a wɔfrɛ no-sleer no bae no. Ɔkae sɛ: "M'adamfo, hwɛ ha yi ara, gye aduru ketewa yi, na fa ka wo ho, na sɛ wokɔ a, wogyina asafo nhyiam no anim, na wɔba sɛ wobekum wo, na mekɔ Ɔhene Mfirihyia Apem no nkyɛn kɔka ne yere a ne bo afuw no, na sɛ wotow no tuo a, wo nso woahu no, na woaka bi, na woaka akyerɛ no sɛ woa no, na woaka aduru biara a ɛwom, wo nso na woaka bi. Nea ɔka ho no kae sɛ: "M'ani begye ho sɛ wode bedi dwuma wɔ abofra no ho. Wɔkae sɛ, wɔ ahemfie nohoa no, dɛn ne saa asɛm no? Afei, ɔkae sɛ, sɛ menyɛ deduani a, anka menya aduru (a ebetumi ama awuduru no ano aduru). Odwontofo no kae sɛ, sɛ wutumi susuw eyi ho yiye, me ba, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔrenwu a, mede wo ti bɛma no; na ɔka kyerɛ wo sɛ: "W'ani nnye ho, m'ani nnye ho, m'ani nnye ho, na m'ani nnye ho sɛ me ne wo ba no benya ayɔnkofa a emu yɛ den. Wɔkaa no pen sɛ, ɛhe na yebenya Tell-tale mogya? Ɔsɔfo panyin no kae sɛ, "Eduu baabi no, ɔde ne nsa too ne nsa so faa ne mogya maa me, na ntɛm ara ɔtoo no kyenee, na ofii ase de aduru no sraa abofra no nan ho. Wɔkae sɛ, bere a wɔretow ayeyi pon no agu no, wɔteɛɛm, na wɔteɛɛm sɛ, "Mɛtow wo ti, na mede wo mɛma no, na wɔakum no, na wɔatow no akyene; wokunkum no, na wokunkum no afã nyinaa, na wokunkum ne honam nyinaa, na wɔtow no kyenee abusuakuw ketewa bi mu.

Eyi, m'asɛm a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
