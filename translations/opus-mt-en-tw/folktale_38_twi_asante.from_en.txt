Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa sɛ, nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware (kyerɛ sɛ nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware)

NSƐM A NKURƆFO TAA BISA: "Sɛ W'ANI Sɔ W'ANI a, MA W'ANI TO WO HO SƐ W'ANI Begye Ho, NA W'ANI Nnye, NA W'ANI Gye "

NEA edi kan no, nea ɔhwɛ turo no so no ka kyerɛɛ Aso (ne yere) sɛ: "Momma yɛnkɔyɛ turo. Bere a ɔsan bae no, ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "So ɛyɛ ampa sɛ worensan nkɔ (ɔbɛboa me)?, "" Ɔbea bi a ɔyɛ basabasa baa asase no so. " Da a edi hɔ, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔkae sɛ: "Na mesrɛ mo, momma yɛnkɔ na yɛnsow aba; ɔkae sɛ: Mensan nkɔ, na ose: M'ani so atan me, na wahu amane; na ɔkɔɔ baabiara a eye; na da koro bi bae a ɔkɔyɛɛ afuw no. Ɔbaa hɔ bɛka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, " Kwaku, nnɔbae no aba turo no mu, na bere bɛn na yɛbɛkɔ hɔ (abeyi afi hɔ)? " Sɛ́ m'ani bɛba me ho so no, na mate ase koraa sɛ, "Soo kae sɛ, " Dɛn koraa '; m'ano na mede reka akyerɛ wo sɛ mepɛ sɛ mesan ba ha. " Ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ: "Fa sekan, sekan a wɔde yi nam, ne nea wɔde tu amoa kɛse bi, na ɔfaa nnɔbae a ɛwɔ afuw no mu nyinaa de kɔhyɛɛ mu, na ɔsan kɔɔ fie. Ɔkae sɛ: "Mekɔɔ nkyɛn kobisaa obi a ɔka me sika ho asɛm; ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ aka nnawɔtwe ma mawu no, menkɔ afuw no mu bio, na sɛ miwu nso a, mɛkɔ hɔ bio, na sɛ miwu a, mɛkora me so, na wode me asie amoa kɛse bi mu. Nkurɔfo no maa kwan ma wɔde no kɔsiee no wɔ ne turo mu hɔ, na wɔsan kɔɔ fie kɔyɛɛ ayi ho amanne pa. Na me kunu yɛɛ turo bi, na m'ani gyei (sɛ ɔbɛma me aduan) na ebinom aba abedi biribiara a ɛwom; "na osu, na wɔadome (a akyiri yi wɔhwee wɔn) kae sɛ, " (mefrɛ ha) wɔ Kwakuku (m'agya ba) a w'afuw mu nnɔbae nyinaa bɛgyaw me hɔ bedi" no. "

Kwakuwa maa (ne nne so too dwom):

" M'ani so tetew me no, madi m'ankasa me aduan!

Wuram nam (nea wɔde wura gu mu) a wɔde wura gu mu.

M'ani so tetew me no, m'ankasa na midii!

M'ani so tetew me no, madi me nam nkutoo!

Kuropɔn mu nnua (nea wɔde gu nsu mu), Nao nnuru a wɔde gu nnɔbae so gu nnɔbae so.

M'ani so tetew me no, madi m'ankasa me aduan nkutoo! "

Ɛno akyi no, Nao gyinaa hɔ, na osuroe, na ɔkɔɔ fie, na ɔfrɛɛ no Adako, Hare, sɛ ɔmmra bɛte nsɛm bi a ɛwɔ ne kunu afuw mu.

" Ná me kunu ne Kwaku, na na ɔsan nso awu, na ogyaw me n'afuw, na nkurɔfo bae bedii aduan a ɛwɔ mu no.

Kwaku, po so hyɛn panyin no, mebuu nkotodwe kyerɛɛ wo, mesrɛ wo, kum wɔn maa me. "

Kwakukoo maa ne nne so sɛ:

" SƐ́ anka medi aduan a m'ankasa mewɔ no, nea madi ara ni!

Wuram nam (nea wɔde wura gu mu) a wɔde wura gu mu.

M'ani so tetew me no, m'ankasa na midii!

Nuklea ahoɔden (nea wɔde sɔ gya) a wɔfrɛ no Aonos no hyew ( turo mu wura bɔne no).

M'ani nnye ho koraa (sɛ́ anka m'ani bɛbɔ me ho so no), madi me de nkutoo!

Kuropɔn mu nnua (nea wɔde gu nsu mu), Nao nnuru a wɔde gu nnɔbae so gu nnɔbae so.

M'ani so tetew me no, m'ankasa m'aduan na madi; gyae (nea metumi de abɔ me ho ban)!

Efi bere a Ɔbɔadeɛ no yɛɛ nneɛma (nneɛma) no, so woahu ɔbea bi a ɔmmoa ne kunu pɛn?

M'ani so tetew me no, madi m'ankasa me aduan nkutoo; migyae (nea mepɛ sɛ meyɛ)! "

Hare kae sɛ, "Meretie asɛm no wiei no, momma yɛnkɔ fie na menka nea ɛrekɔ so ankasa nkyerɛ wo. Hare kae sɛ: " Aanse kae sɛ, " Fa no sɛ wowɔ hɔ na kɔfa turo bi si hɔ; woka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ: Ɛnyɛ papa sɛ wokɔ; ma wohyew (wura a awo) no; na wokae sɛ: Wo de, kɔ na kɔ; enti kɔ na kɔka; enti, anyɛ saa a, gyae, efisɛ ɔbea no ankasa na ɔbɔɔ ne kunu, efisɛ ɔno nkutoo na ɔbɔɔ no, na onni hɔ. Nea enti a yɛka saa ne sɛ, "Sɛ obi yɛ adwuma biara na obisa wo a, boa no. "

Eyi, m'asɛm a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
