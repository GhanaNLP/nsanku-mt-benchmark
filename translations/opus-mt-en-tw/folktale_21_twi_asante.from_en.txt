Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa sɛ, nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware (kyerɛ sɛ nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA OBA (ABEPEYA), ABSOMOOOAOAOA (ATTMON), NE OPAUUO (WL), CAO WƆN A WƆDE MA WƆN A WƆDE MA WƆN A WƆDE MA KOBOWY

OMA, dua no, (a ne din nso wɔ hɔ) Kwaduon Kyerefo, Opatufo, Owasro, ne Aboom-Akoemon, Chameon, (a ɛkyerɛ abodin) ne Ɔbɔadeɛ no wu (a afei nso ɔwɔ din). Da biara da, bere a Adobe-tre sɔre twɛn obi yi no; da a edi hɔ no, ɔtwɛn wɔn mu biako; na da foforo nso, bere a Ɔbɔadeɛ no (maa no ahyɛde sɛ wonkum Owu) na ɔhwehwɛɛ Adobefo (a ɔyɛ adwuma) no, ɛhe na ohuu no? Na wɔkae sɛ: "Kɔfrɛ no, na Ɔbɔadeɛ no de ne nne too n'anim (owu) sɛ: Adobe o!

Dua a ɛwɔ dua no so no sui sɛ:

Bere a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, ɔsan bae; ofii hɔ kɔe, na ɔsan bae, na ɔsan bae, na ɔsan bae, na ɔsan baa fie ntɛm ara!

Na Owl nso frɛɛ no:

YƐN mu dadwen (sika fam mfutuma) mu abu, na ebia wɔatow agu, me, yɛn koma atu (sika kɔkɔɔ), na ebia wɔato agu, me!

Na saa na ɛyɛe na saa mmoa baasa yi su fii ase.

Eyi, m'asɛm a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
