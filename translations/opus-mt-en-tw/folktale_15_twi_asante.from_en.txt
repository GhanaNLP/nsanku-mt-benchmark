Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa sɛ, nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware (kyerɛ sɛ nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA EBINOM KA SƐ NNIPA A WƆWƆ NEA ENTI A WƆWƆ HƆ NO, ƐNYƐ NA ƐFA YƐN HO, NA ƐFA YƐN HO WƆ NEA EDIDI SO YI HO

NÁ NEA ɔka wɔ hɔ no yɛ ɔbea, na ɔkɔɔ Palm-tree baabi a ogyina wɔ nsu no mu a obetumi abisa ho asɛm. Na bere a ɔkɔe no, Palmtree no kae sɛ, "Nea wopɛ na mede bɛma wo, nanso abofra a mede no bɛma wo no, sɛ ɔsɔre a, ɔrenyɛ adwuma biara da. " Na ne maame ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Da koro bi a ɛna no fii hɔ kɔɔ afuw no mu no, ɔfrɛɛ n'aduan a ɛyɛ dɛ, na ofii ase de ne ti too ne ho so, obiara nni hɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ wohwɛ ne ho ban wɔ Palm-tree no ho, na waforoo biako mpo da. Na abeawa no kae sɛ: "Me de, m'ani nnye ho sɛ ebinom a woahwehwɛ wɔn wɔ nsu no mu no bɛkɔ akogye wɔn ahome. Bere a ɔforo kɔɔ soro, kra, kra, kosii sɛ oduu dua no kɔn mu no, oyii ne sekan no, na bere a ɔde ne nsa kaa no ara pɛ, Palm-tre no mu paee abien, na abarimaa no yafunu no mu yɛɛ den bio; wɔtoo no mu bio; ná ne maame wɔ nsu no akyi bere tiaa bi; bere a oduu hɔ no, ohui sɛ ne maame no wɔ nsu no ani so, na ɔkae sɛ: Oduu hɔ ara pɛ na ohuu no.

Henanom sunsuma na ɛda nsu no so te sɛ me ba A 1985si Kwasaman?

Ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, Maame, me nana, me nana.

Ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wɔde Palm-tre no hyɛɛ wo nsa? "

Ɔkae sɛ, mehyɛɛ da yii Palmed hyehyɛɛ krataa de kɔmaa m'adamfo no, na Palm-tre no maa me ho dwiriw me.

Ɛna no kae sɛ: "Wo, saa Palm-tree yi na ɛhyɛ no so, na ɛfa no, me ba Kwasaman, di ne so.

Palm-atre hyɛ no so, hyɛ no so, hyɛ no so, na di no so.

Na ɛna no kɔe, na agya no nso bae, na ɔkae sɛ:

Hena sunsuma na ɛda nsu yi so te sɛ me ba Kwasaman?

Ɔkae sɛ, me, m'agya, ne me.

Ɔno (agya no) kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na Palm-tree wo? "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Mekɔɔ Palmtrele kɔyii ntoboa kɔmaa m'adamfo no, na Palm-tre no hyɛɛ me abufuw. "

Ɔno (agya no) kae sɛ, "Wo de, Palm-tree yi na anya no so nkɛntɛnso, me ba Kwasaman.

Palm-atre hyɛ no so, hyɛ no so, Palm-tree hyɛ no so. "

Na agya no fii fie kɔka kyerɛɛ akuraa no ase Ti, na akuraa no ase Ti nso frɛɛ nnipa no nyinaa; na wɔbaa Palmtree no ase, na akuraa no asefo maa wɔn so (ne nne), na wɔkae sɛ:

" Hena sunsuma na ɛda nsu no so te sɛ nea me papa a wɔfrɛ no A desi Kwasaman yɛe no? "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Me papa, ɛyɛ me. "

Ɔno (a ɔyɛ akuraa no ase Ti no) kae sɛ: "Dɛn nti na Palm-trei no hyɛ wo abufuw? "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Mekɔɔ Palmtrele kɔyii ntoboa kɔmaa m'adamfo no, na Palm-tre no hyɛɛ me abufuw. "

Akuraa no asefo no kae sɛ: "Wo, Palm-tree yi, di no so, hyɛ no so, di no so, me papa kumaa Kwasaman so.

Palm-atre hyɛ no so, hyɛ no so, Palm-tree hyɛ no so. "

Na akuraa no Ti fii adi, na akuraa no asefo nyinaa too dwom koro no ara bi ka kyerɛɛ Palm-tre sɛ onni ne so, na afei ne dɔfo no baa hɔ bɛkae sɛ: "Hena sunsuma na ɛda nsu no so te sɛ nea m'adɔfo A 1985 Kwasaman yɛe no? "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ me, wo dɔfo paa. "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na Palm-tree redaadaa wo? "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Metoo mu maa wo, na Palm-tree no maa me ho dwiriw me. "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Palm-tree yii no mae, gyaee no, na oyii no.

Palm-tree yii me dɔ, Akwasi Kwasaman, adi.

Palm-atre yii no mae, gyaee no, na oyii no mae, Palm-tre yii no. "

Enti bere a ɔne no kasae no, Palmtree buee kakra, na ne dɔfo no yafunu puei kakra; na ɔsan frɛɛ no bio kae sɛ:

" Hena sunsuma na ɛda nsu no so te sɛ nea m'adɔfo Akwasi Kwasaman yɛe no? "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ me, wo dɔfo paa. "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na Palm-tree redaadaa wo? "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Metoo mu maa wo, na Palm-tree no maa me ho dwiriw me. "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Palm-tree yii no mae, gyaee no, na oyii no.

Palm-tree yii me dɔ, Akwasi Kwasaman, adi.

Palm-atre yii no mae, gyaee no, na oyii no mae, Palm-tre yii no. "

Na nea ɔdɔɔ no no bae prɛko pɛ, na ɔsɔre gyinaa ne koma a ɛyɛ dɛ no kokom, na ɛhɔ dan ngo; na nnipa a wɔkɔɔ hɔ ntɛm ara no, wɔn ho yɛ fɛ, nanso wɔn a wonni bi no, wɔhwɛ wɔn a wɔn ho yɛ fɛ. Eyi, m'asɛm a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
