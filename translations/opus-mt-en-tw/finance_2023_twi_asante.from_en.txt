NEA ENTI A WƆWƆ HƆ

Akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ saa afe yi bɛyɛ yɛn man yi sikasɛm mu asɛnnennen a ɛsen biara no mu biako.

Bere a yesusuw ho kakraa bi no, na yɛrenya nkɔanim kɛse afi yare no a efii ase denam sɛnea yɛyɛɛ yɛn nipadua mu nsakrae a efii ase no so no mu, na nsɛm ahorow pii sisii wɔ bere tenten a atwam no mu, na ɛmaa nkɔanim a aba fi 2017 kosi 2019 no yɛɛ kɛse. Nea edi kan no, Russia-Ukraine ko no, afei bere a yɛtwentwɛn yɛn nan ase wɔ sika ho nhyehyɛe titiriw bi a wɔyɛe wɔ 2022 mu a ɛmaa yɛn ho dwiriw yɛn mpofirim no, mpofirim ara na wɔtoo Amanaman Ntam Nhyehyɛe no mu mfe du a edi kan no mu, wɔtoo fie hɔ, na ɛmaa bosea no bo kɔɔ soro, na ɛmaa nkurɔfo bɔɔ ka kɛse wɔ adwuma no mu.

Enti, bere a sika a aman foforo nya fii ahenkurow no aguadi mu ne sika a yenya fii amannɔne nsam no so tewee mpo no, ɛka a na yɛbɔ no fii nneɛma a na ɛda adi sɛ ɛho hia yɛn kɛse sɛ ɔman no, ne ɛka a na yɛbɔ no kɛse, ne sika a na yetua no a ɛsɛee mfaso a ɛwɔ yɛn sika so no, eyi ama yɛn nyinaa ahwere sika kɛse, sɛnea Aban ne ankorankoro no. Fango bo a ɛkɔ soro no ama nneɛma ne nnwuma bo ayɛ den, na ɛho ka akɔ soro ɔha biara mu 40.4 wɔ October 2022 mu.

Wɔsɔɔ yɛn hwɛe ankasa sɛ nnipa, na Aban no ne wo di no yiye wɔ nhyɛso a ɛmfata a ɛde aba no ho: Yɛpɛ sɛ yɛda yɛn ase sɛ Aban a yɛde bɔ yɛn dua ahorow ho ban, a Mmarahyɛ Bagua ahorow, Laour Unions, Ɔmanfo Fekuw, Gyidi Ho Ahyehyɛde a Ɛhwɛ Nnipa Dodow So, Ghana Aman Nkabom Kuw, no ka ho
