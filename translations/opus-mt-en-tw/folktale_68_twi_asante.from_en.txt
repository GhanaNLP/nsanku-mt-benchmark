SƐNEA ENTI A WƆDE MA HO NHYEHYƐE, NEA ƐWƆ HƆ, BESAM

NÁ WƆDE aburow aba biako pɛ brɛɛ me, na ɔde me kɔɔ akuraa biako (a ɛne no bɔ abira) a wɔfrɛ no KWUBAYE, a wɔfrɛ no The Nayaare no ase, na ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, me maame ntumi ntua ho ka, gye sɛ me maame ka, na ɔka m'ano, na ɔka m'ano to. Odii kan fii hɔ kɔɔ akuraa bi ase, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔman no ti sɛ: "Hok-man na asoma me; ɔka sɛ, memfa aburow biako pɛ yi nkɔ baabi na me ne no nkɔ, na bere biara a mɛba (bere a mepɛ) no, memfa aburow aba yi bi nka ho nkɔ. Sɛ aburow ne aburow ka bom a, so ɛbɛyɛ nea ɛfata? Ɔmantam ti no kae sɛ, nana Head-man adi aburow a wɔfrɛ no us is is is is. The Newman kae sɛ, wontumi nnya eyi ho akyinnye biara, enti fa aduru no kɔ na fa biako kɔ, na ɔde kɔ akuraa foforo ase. Bere a oduu hɔ no, ɔkaa asɛm bi kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Hofo no na asoma me, na meda ha yi, na ɛsɛ sɛ me ne oguan no da. " Da a edi hɔ anɔpa no, ɔkɔɔ ne panyin hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: "Sɛ anka wɔrekɔ akogye no a, ka kyerɛ me sɛ nguan no awuwu. " Ɔmantam ti no kae sɛ: "Nsɛm bɛn na m'ani gye ho sɛ mɛka akyerɛ me wɔ me ti-man mu sɛ, " Dɛn na ɔsɔfo panyin no de abrɛ me, na mede nguan no gu mu; na anantwi no bom da. ' Odii kan kae sɛ: "Dabi, efisɛ eyi ne nantwi ba titiriw a ɔwɔ anantwi mu no. Ade kyee anɔpa no, "Had-man, maa me nguan, "" Wɔkɔɔ kraal hɔ; na nguan a wɔawuwu no wɔ hɔ. Ɔfaa nantwi no de no bɔɔ afɔre wɔ hɔ, na ɔdaa; bere a ɔda hɔ no, wɔbae bɛka kyerɛɛ ne nkoa no mu biako sɛ wawu. Owura no kae sɛ: "Ɛsɛ sɛ motow no kyene, efisɛ minni nkaebo biara. " • Na wubetumi afa (saa sika yi) atɔ akoa foforo. Bere a oduu hɔ no, ɔkasa kyerɛɛ ɔman no ti sɛ, "Ma me ba yi ne wo mma nna wɔ ha (nea ɛda hɔ); afei (mese meka kyerɛ wo sɛ) abofra yi nyɛ onipa pa, ne su ne sɛ obebu nkotodwe anadwo, enti sɛ mframa bɔ a, ma (mma mmofra a wɔawuwu no) nwu. Afei de, wɔkae sɛ: "Saa abofra no abubu, ahwe no ase, titim, titim, (na ne su te sɛ wɔn haw no nnyigyei) anɔpa, na sɛ ɔkɔ a, na ɔfrɛ ne ba no ba; owui. Ɔbenfo no kae sɛ, "Had-man, wo mma akunkum ɔsɔfo a ɔka me ho no. " Ɔmantam ti no kae sɛ: "Merentumi nnye asɛm yi ho akyinnye, enti wubetumi agye mmofra no afi wo nsam. " Bere a nkurɔfo no nyinaa duu kurow no kurotia no, ɔsomaa ɔbɔfo bi sɛ ɔnkɔka nkyerɛ ɔsɔfo panyin no sɛ ɔne kurow bi reba. Bere a Paapa kɔɔ hɔ no, ɔmaa (ne dwom no) so sɛ: "Anana, Asans, allas, nyaa aburow aba biako pɛ a na mede bɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ awɔ.

Mefaa oguan bi de no kɔmaa no.

Mefaa nguan no kɔe, na mefaa nantwi, na mefaa nantwi no kɔe, na mefaa amu no, na migyee kurow no mufo. "

Wɔn mu biara kae sɛ, "So wote dwom bi a wɔfrɛ no, na wotoo bio?

" M'ani so tetew me no, minyaa aburow aba biako pɛ a na metumi de akɔ akɔhwehwɛ bi.

Mefaa oguan bi de no kɔmaa no.

Mefaa nguan no kɔe, na mefaa nantwi, na mefaa nantwi no kɔe, na mefaa amu no, na migyee kurow no mufo. "

Atesɛm krataa bi kae sɛ: "M'ani gyee aburow aba biako pɛ a me nsa kae no ho, na me ne kurom hɔfo bae; na kurow bi mufo gyina hɔ. " Sɛ nhwɛso no, "Nea mepɛ ara ne sɛ meda mo ase daa a, ɛnde dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mode ma mo?, "" Beae a mepɛ; (a ɛwɔ hɔ) m'ani gye mpuran ho sen biara. "

Eyi, m'asɛm a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
