Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa sɛ, nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware (kyerɛ sɛ nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA NEA ENTI A WƆWƆ NEA EDIDI SO YI HO SO NO, "W'ANI NMMMMMMW NE NEA EDIDI SO YI HO "

BERE bi a atwam no, KWUAYE, a na ɔyɛ afifide ho nimdefo no kɔɔ Nyanaonpon, a ɛyɛ po so hyɛn gyinabea no, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ na wɔatumi atɔ mfonini a wɔde di dwuma wɔ Intanɛt so no. The Atesɛm krataa no kae sɛ: "So wubetumi atɔ wɔn?, "" The Direve kae sɛ, "The ifo no kae sɛ:" Nkurow akɛse a emu yɛ den te sɛ Kokofu, Beiki, Na wontumi ntɔ wɔn, nanso wuntumi ntɔ wɔn, na mo a mo ho akokwaw no, morentumi ntɔ wɔn anaa? " (Worentumi ntɔ wɔn," Dɛn bio? ") Sɛ ɛnyɛ Onithon; Osebo; Mmotia, Themotiy; (ne) Mmoboro, Hons. M'ani gye ho sɛ mede Onini, Python; Osebo; Mmotia, bɔwerɛw; ne Mmoboro; na meka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ mede sika no bi bɛka ho na mede bi aka ho ama ne yere no. Ɔne wɔn san bae. Na sɛneato kae no, "Fa wɔn kɔ asuten no mu; na bere a na ɔrekɔ no, na akyɛ sen sɛnea ɔte yi, bere a odi atoro no, na aka akyi sen sɛnea ɔkae sɛ: "Otumi di atoro no koraa. " Na meka sɛ ɔyɛ ɔtorofo sen wo, na mise no sɛ: Onython, a wɔfrɛ no Pathon no kae sɛ: "Oyii no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ: Ɔfaa wo nsam de no kogyinaa Pyon amu no ho, na oyii ne ho fii mu, na oyii ne ti fii mu, na oyii ne ti fii mu de abufuw no kɔe! " The Nan of the Holy Scriptures kae sɛ: "Me de, mede wo bɛkɔ baabi a wogye awo no na me nsa aka mfonini no (a ɛne no bɔ abira). Nea aka no da so ara wɔ hɔ; "Nea aka no san bae bɛkaa nea asi no kyerɛɛ ne yere, na ɔkae sɛ: "Ne yere ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: " Hwɛ yiye, na fa nsu gu mu, na fa kɔ, na fa kɔ, bere a ohui sɛ ahum bi a ano yɛ den wɔ hɔ, na oyii nsu no fii mu no. Afei hyɛn no hwiee nea aka no guu ne ho so, na wotwitwaa n'ahaban ahaban de kataa ne ti so. Na afei ɔkasa kyerɛɛ Hornetfo no sɛ: "Sɛ osu tɔ a, so moamma eyi mu, na osu antɔ mo; munhu sɛ maso ahaban a mede akata m'ankasa so, " Yɛda mo ase sɛ," Nneɛma a Ɛho Hia Nyinaa, Yɛda Mo Ase! " Paapa kataa n'anom, na ɔkae sɛ: "Me de, manya wo, na mede wo redi m'agya akyi makogye wo abodin krataa (a ɛne no bɔ abira). Odwontofo no kae sɛ: "Me nsa aka nea aka (nea aka) no bio, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere sɛ, " Osebo da so ara wɔ hɔ, ' sɛnea" Kɔtu amoa mu, " kyerɛ no, " ɛno ara na metee ase. Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu, bere a wofii ase huu nneɛma no, nea ɔhwɛ nhoma no so no kae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ, na bere a ɔkɔe no (anaa) ɔda amoa no mu. " Agya ba kumaa, me maame ba ketewa, maka akyerɛ wo sɛ ɛnsɛ sɛ wonom nsa, na afei sɛnea obi bɛhwɛ kwan no, wowe nsa, na ɛno nti na wohwe amoa mu; sɛ meka kyerɛ wo da a edi hɔ no, sɛ wuhu me anaa me mma no mu biara a, ɛnde ka kyerɛ wo sɛ: "O hwɛ! " Ɔkae sɛ: "Fa wo sika biako hyɛ ha, na fa biako to hɔ. (Na ne su ne ne ntease no.) Enti Paapa nyaa antweri bi fii amoa no mu de sii kwan so de fii adi, na ofii adi de kaa ho sɛ: "Oyii wo fii adi, na ɔkae sɛ, " Mede wo redi nsesa wɔ mfonini adaka no ho. Kεn no kae sɛ: "Me nsa aka nea aka, na nea aka no da so ara wɔ hɔ. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuanom mmea bi sɛ: "Sɛ me ne wo nam a, minni bi; na meda wo ase. " Nanso bere a ɔdaa no ase no, mmuae no anyɛ no dɛ; na bere a medaa no ase no, ababaa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, bere a medaa no ase no, wamma mmuae. Ɔno nso kɔɔ so kuraa mu denneennen, na nea ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no ne sɛ: Fa me nsa abien ka wo yafunu mu denneennen; na ɔde ne yafunu kataa so, na ɔde ne yafunu kataa so, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ: Wo de, mede me nsa abien no akɔ (n'akyi) ne fie. Bere a woduu hɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame Yasia (abofra a ɔto so asia), kae sɛ: "Ma yɛnkɔ, na me ne wo nkɔ, na me ne mo rekɔ kokogye mo ani (a ɛne no bɔ abira) na mama mo ahoni (a ɛne no bɔ abira) no, Ɔmaa wɔn so, na yɛkɔɔ baabi a na wogye awo no tom no. Ɔkaa sɛ: "Me yere dedaw a mekaa ne ho asɛm sɛ ɔno nso te ha yi ara ne m'asɔrefo a wɔfrɛ wɔn m'asɔre mu mpanyimfo (ne mpanyimfo) ne Adonten, Adonten (a ɔyɛ asraafo kuw no muni titiriw). Na ɔde asɛm no too wɔn anim kae sɛ, ahene akɛse aba, na wontumi ntɔ nsɛm a ɛyɛ dɛ no, nanso Kwakuku no, ago no atumi atua ho ka; manya ne ho akatua afi Mmobo, Howbo, Mmorofo hɔ; manya ne nkyɛn, me nsa aka no; manya ne nkyɛn anuonyam, ne n'ankasa ne nkyɛn; me nsa aka n'asɛm afi ne nkyɛn, na m'ankasa ne sɔfo no hɔ; me nsa aka ne ho nimdeɛ, na m'ani agye no bi aka no. Ɔhyɛɛ ne maame abufuw; ɔkaa eyinom nyinaa wɔ ha, na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ: "Wɔteɛm sɛ: " Momfa n'ayeyi mmra! ' (Wɔteɛm); Oifo no kae sɛ, efi saa bere no rekɔ no, m'ani gye ho paa sɛ me ne wɔn bɛbɔ nkɔmmɔ, na me nso mahyira wɔn, nanso me werɛ remfi nhyira a manya no da!

Eyi, m'asɛm a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
