Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa sɛ, nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware (kyerɛ sɛ nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ENTI A WƆWƆ NNIPA pii A WƆWƆ NEA ENTI A WƆWƆ HƆ

NÁ Yvonne te Kwaku no, na ɔkɔɔ Nyareonpon, a ɛwɔ Nanyaon no nkyɛn, na ɔkae sɛ, "Leire, fa wo nguan no kɔhyɛɛ hɔ, na ɔno na wode wo nguan bɔɔ afɔre maa wo Memeneda, na mekum wɔn, na mede wɔn kɔe (wɔmaa wɔn kwan sɛ wɔmfa wɔn nyɛ hwee) maa wɔn, na mede wɔn san bae, na mede wɔn kɔe. Afei po so hyɛn gyinabea no kɔɔ akuraa bi a wɔfrɛ no Nalat a na ɔbarima biara nni hɔ ase; na wɔn nyinaa yɛ mmea; wɔn nyinaa waree na wɔne no traa hɔ da koro. Bere a otuafo no kɔe no, ɔkɔɔ anyame no anim kɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ: "Na mo nguan a ogyee wɔn no, okum wɔn de maa mmea bi sɛ wonni na wɔnware. Abɔfo no kɔe, hyiaa mmea no, na wɔfaa wɔn nyinaa kɔe a ɔbea biako a ɔyare nko ara na ɔfaa wɔn nyinaa kɔe, na ɔde wɔn kɔmaa onyamewa no sɛ, "Wo a woda so te wo nkyɛn no, dɛn na metumi ayɛ ama me, "" Ɔbea a ɔyare no, " na wode kuruwa bi brɛɛ me. Ɔkae sɛ, "Bathei me na fa (nsu a wode adi dwuma) no bi, na hwie gu hɔ, na ɔde nsu gu ne honam mu (nsu a ɔde dii dwuma no) kɔbɔɔ n'asen; ɔbea yi ho yɛ fɛ yiye, ɔbea biara nni abusua no mu a ɔwaree no dedaw; na ɔbea yi san bae, na ohuu no. Ofii adi kɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn a wɔkyerɛ sɛ wɔyɛ anyame no sɛ: "Hanse adaadaa wo, ɔde mmea a wɔn ho yɛ hu yi abrɛ wo, na wama wo ho ayɛ fɛ; "Ofowa no somaa abɔfo kɔɔ hɔ, na ɔde wɔn kɔɔ hɔ kɔmaa ɔbea no, na wɔkaa asɛm yi kyerɛɛ no. Ɔkae sɛ: "So ɔrempɛ me nso, na mmom abɔfo no kae sɛ yɛmfa ɔbea no nkɔ ne nkyɛn. nhomawa no bi, na wɔtoo dwom sɛ:

"Y'Aoddededede den, Y'odnde den.

Ao Ya e!

Y'soddedede den, Y'sodnde den.

W'aniwa yɛ kɔkɔɔ koraa!

Y'soddedede den, Y'sodnde den.

Wo ho yɛ hu yiye!

Y'soddedede den, Y'sodnde den.

So ɛno ne Wade Y.B.?

Y'soddedede den, Y'sodnde den.

Wɔbɔɔ wo asu-kGO!

Y'soddedede den, Y'sodnde den.

Wo hwene yɛ wo hwene!

Y'soddedede den, Y'sodnde den.

Wo nan yɛ akɛse te sɛ akoa de!

Y'soddedede den, Y'sodnde den.

Wo ti te sɛ nantwi!

Y'soddedede den, Y'odnde den. "

Natiule (a wɔatwa no tiaa na ɔtoo dwom):

Ababaa a ne ho yɛ fɛ! "

Na Afudodoedotwe (Beng-burst) ne Nyiwawadewfwea (Sindlews) de mmirika tuu mmirika kɔka kyerɛɛ ishorfo no sɛ, "Sridle (Sashs) no asaw bi fata wo na ɛnyɛ dede a wɔyɛ ma wo ntɛm ara. Na ɔka kyerɛ wɔn sɛ: "Mma ɔmfa hwee mmrɛ me (awɔtoo no), na mmom fa w'ani kyerɛ baabi foforo, na fa w'ani kyerɛ baabi foforo, na sɛ m'awofo gye tom saa bere no a, ɛnde fa wo nsa ka. Bere a ohui sɛ wɔde nhoma bi a n'ani gye ho aka n'anom a na ne nyarewa nyinaa gu ne ho, esiane ɛno nti no, ɔkae sɛ, sɛ edu saa bere no a, ɔrenkyia, na afei de, ɔsɔfo panyin no hyɛɛ no, na ɔbae; na bere a ɔpɛ sɛ osu no, ɔmaa ɔbea no so bɔɔ no, na nyarewa (te nnyigyei bi a ɛte sɛ dede bi)! Saa kwan yi so na yafunu a emu yɛ den, tipae, kwata, srade a ɛma mogya tu obi, mogya mmoroso, asikreyare, ne asikreyare baa abusua no mu; bere bi na ɔyare biara nni adesamma mu, nanso sɛ obi nya yare bi fi awo mu a, na aduru a ɛma obi nya nyarewa fi awo mu no na ɛde ba.

Eyi, m'asɛm a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
