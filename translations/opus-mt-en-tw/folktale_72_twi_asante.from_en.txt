Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa sɛ, nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware (kyerɛ sɛ nea yɛreka no yɛ nokware)

NEA ENTI A ƐSƐ SƐ YƐWƆ HƆ (ATUASSSSSS)

TETE no, na saa mmofra yi maame ne wɔn papa taa kɔ wuram daa kɔfa nam ba wɔn nkyɛn. Da bi, wɔn maame ne wɔn papa ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, " Sɛ sɔre a, ɛsɛ sɛ musu ka kyerɛ wɔn sɛ, ' (sɛ yesuro a) biribiara nni hɔ a ɛkyerɛ sɛ (a yesuro).

" Biribiara nni hɔ (a yesuro no), biribiara nni hɔ (a yesuro)! "

Anadwo-jar kae sɛ: "Hena na ɔka kyerɛɛ mo sɛ munsu?

" Ɛnkyɛ koraa na mmere asesa, na ɛnkyɛ na tebea no asesa! "

Wɔkae sɛ, "Hena na ɔka kyerɛɛ mo sɛ munsu saa? " Afei bere a nkokoaa no begyinaa faako a na mmofra no sisi nnua abien no, obubuu nnua abien no, na ɔde peaw no soo mu, na ɔka kyerɛɛ nkokoaa no sɛ, " Ɛsɛ sɛ musu ntɛm ara, ' na bere tiaa bi akyi no, wo maame ne wo papa ka kyerɛɛ wo sɛ, " Nea waka akyerɛ wo no, woaka akyerɛ wo sɛ: " Ɛsɛ sɛ woyɛ nsakrae. ' Bere tiaa bi akyi no, mmofra no maame ne wɔn papa kae sɛ: "Hena na ɔka kyerɛɛ mo sɛ munsu? Anadwo-jar ka kyerɛɛ mmofra no sɛ, sɛ midi nam a, worennye bio.

Eyi, m'asɛm a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaa) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
