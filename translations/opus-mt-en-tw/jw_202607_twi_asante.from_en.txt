Suasua Gibeonfo No

NÁ nnipa a wɔte hɔ no ne Israelfo no asiesie wɔn ntam, na wɔtraa wɔn mu.

WƆ AFE 1473 A.Y.B. mu no, na afei ara na Israel man no afi ase redi Bɔhyɛ Asase no so nkonim.

2 Saa akwantufo no ne Gibeonfo no; eyi ne bere a edi kan a wɔbɔɔ Gibeonfo no din wɔ Bible kyerɛwtohɔ no mu, nanso Bible mu nsɛm pii da no adi sɛ na Gibeonfo no ne Israelfo no wɔ abusuabɔ a emu yɛ den wɔ mfehaha a edi hɔ no nyinaa mu.

Gibeonfo no daadaa Yosua ma ɔne wɔn siesiee wɔn ntam (Hwɛ nkyekyɛm 8)

Ná wotumi hu sɛ Yehowa reko ama ne nkurɔfo, na na wonim sɛ wahyɛ bɔ sɛ obetu Kanaanfo no afi asase no so. (W. 34:11; Yos 9:24) Enti bere a Israelfo no dii Yeriko so nkonim na wɔbɔɔ nsra wiei no, Gibeonfo no somaa wɔn sɛ wɔnkɔhwehwɛ asomdwoe.

4 kenkan Yosua 9:8-13; Gibeonfo no yɛɛ wɔn ho sɛ wofi akyirikyiri asase bi so, na wogye toom sɛ Yehowa wɔ Israel a ɔma wodii nkonim wɔ ɔko a wɔko tiaa Misraim no mu, ne Ɔhene Ahemfo Ahene Dale ne Og.

Esiane sɛ na nsɛm a ɛte saa ntumi nnuu wɔn man a ɛwɔ akyirikyiri paa no mu nti, Israel ananmusifo a wɔne Yehowa nni nkitaho no gyee adanse a ɛkyerɛ sɛ Gibeonfo no ne wɔn yɛɛ asomdwoe apam no toom. 9:14, 15) Ɛno akyi bere tiaa bi no, wohuu Gibeonfo nnaadaa no, nanso Israelfo no kɔɔ so dii asomdwoe apam a wɔne Gibeonfo yɛe no so efisɛ Yehowa kaa ntam kyerɛɛ wɔn.

5 Bere a Asiria ahemfo baanum tee sɛ Gibeonfo no ne Israel asiesie wɔn ntam no, wɔkɔkaa wɔn ti pam ho kɔtow hyɛɛ Israel so.

(a) Sɛnea wɔada no adi wɔ Yosua 9:8-13 no, ɔkwan bɛn so na Gibeonfo no daadaa Israel de yɛɛ asomdwoe apam? (Hwɛ mfonini no nso.) (b) Dɛn na esii bere a wohuu wɔn nnaadaa no?

Akwan bɛn so na Gibeonfo no daa Yehowa mu gyidi adi?

JULY 2026 3

Gibeonfo no srɛɛ Yosua sɛ ɔmmoa wɔn. (Y.B. 10:3) Yosua dii Israel anim ma wɔyɛɛ wɔn mmoa, na wohyiraa wɔn mmɔdenbɔ no so. Yehowa tow atamfo asraafo no guu wɔn so, na ɔnam anwonwakwan so maa ɔko no kɔɔ soro. 10:9 (14) Bere a Gibeonfo no hwehwɛɛ asomdwoe apam a wɔne Israel bɛyɛ, na wɔmaa Yosua boaa wɔn bere a wohunahunaa wɔn no, wɔdaa Yehowa mu gyidi a emu yɛ den a na wɔwɔ sɛ obetumi adi n'asɛm so agye wɔn no adi.

6 Asɛm yi ma yehu sɛ Yehowa brɛ ne ho ase na ɔwɔ mmɔborɔhunu. Ná wadi kan aka akyerɛ Israelfo no sɛ wonyi asase no sofo nyinaa a Gibeonfo ka ho no mfi hɔ. (Num. 33: 52) Nanso, asomdwoe apam a Yehowa ne wɔn yɛe no nti, ɔmaa Gibeonfo no kwan ma wɔtenaa ase. Ɛwom mpo sɛ Israelfo no ankɔ Yehowa anim ansa na wɔrekɔ saa apam no mu de, nanso ɔboaa Israelfo no ma wodii wɔn nhyehyɛe no so, na ɔnam anwonwakwan so gyee Gibeonfo no. - Yoh. 9:26; 11:19.

7 Ɔkwan bɛn so na yebetumi asuasua Gibeonfo no denam Yehowa mu gyidi a emu yɛ den a yebenya so, efisɛ yenim Yehowa ho nsɛm pii sen Gibeonfo a na wɔwɔ hɔ saa bere no, na yɛwɔ nneɛma pii a yebetumi agyina so anya Yehowa mu ahotoso koraa mpo. (P. 40:4, 5) Yebetumi asuasua Gibeonfo no denam ɔpɛ a yebenya sɛ yɛbɛyɛ nnwuma a ɛba fam wɔ Yehowa som mu no so. 9: 27) Onua kumaa bi a wɔfrɛ no Luka werɛ remfi bere a ohui sɛ Betel abusua no mu onua panyin bi reyɛ saa pɛpɛɛpɛ no da. Ɛwom sɛ na ɔhwɛ adwuma bi a emu yɛ duru na agye din kakra de, nanso otuu ne ho mae yɛɛ komm hwɛɛ Ahenni Asa a ɛwɔ baabi a na wɔresi no anadwo bi. Sɛ yehyia tebea horow a emu yɛ den, na yefi ahobrɛase mu di teokrase dwumadi biara ho dwuma a, ɛmmra sɛ yɛbɛda gyidi a yɛwɔ wɔ Yehowa mu da biara da no adi.

Akyiri yi, osu tɔe, na ɛmaa ɔkɔm bae, na ɛmaa awerɛhow a wobuu Yehowa no brɛɛ ase koraa.

Dɛn na saa asɛm yi kyerɛ yɛn fa Yehowa ho? Yehu adanse pefee a ɛkyerɛ sɛ Yehowa yɛ Onyankopɔn a obu atɛntrenee. Yehu nso sɛ Yehowa hwɛ kwan sɛ yebedi yɛn bɔhyɛ ahorow so sɛnea ɔhwɛɛ kwan sɛ Israelfo no bedi wɔn asomdwoe ho bɔhyɛ a ɛfa Gibeonfo ho no so no.
