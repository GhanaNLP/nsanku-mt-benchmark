SƐNEA NEA ENTI A WƆWƆ NEA ENTI A WƆWƆ HƆ NO KA KYERƐE

BERE bi a atwam no, na Vulature ne Crow, na Crow kae sɛ, momma yɛnkɔtow kyene na yenni agoru wɔ so. Ɔkae sɛ, bere a Vulturefo no hui sɛ wɔde saa aduaba yi asɛw dua no ho pɛ na ɔkae sɛ, me na meyɛ ɔpanyin, enti me na me na mebɔɔ saa dua kɛse yi gui.

M'ani so tetew me!

Vulature Kwa nokware no ho! Manya (se) ntɛm ara!

Bere a Carrow no boroo no no, na ɛte sɛ nea edidi so yi:

Crow, Kwa!

Crow, Kwa!

Wɔkɔɔ so dii agoru, na anyame no tee, na ɔsomaa Hawk (sɛ wɔpɛ sɛ wɔto nsa frɛ wɔn) bae; na Hawk bae (na wɔabɔ wɔn nsam) sɛ: "Ago, na wobuae sɛ: "Ofowa no aka sɛ wate asaw wɔ ha, enti fa di agoru, na fa di agoru, na fa di agoru," di agoru, na wo ne mmoa no bom di agoru. " Nea ɛbɛboa wo no, " mate sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ; nnwom abien no nyinaa yɛ dɛ, enti mede biribi ma wo (a obiara): Afei, m'ani gye adaka abien no ho, biako ho yɛ fɛ yiye, biako hon, biako mu; biako nso yɛ sika kɔkɔɔ; biako te sɛ nea ɛwɔ ha yi. The Vulture kae sɛ: "Me na meyɛ ɔpanyin, enti ma minni kan nkɔ; bere a ɔkɔe no, ɔfaa nea ne ho yɛ fɛ paa no, ɔno nso faa wɔn de no kɔe, na bere a wobuee wɔn kwan no, wofii kurow no mu, na wofii adi. Wɔ Crow's mu no, bere a ɔhwɛɛ sika kɔkɔɔ a ɛhyerɛn no, ankyɛ koraa na ɔsomafo bi baa hɔ bɛka kyerɛɛ Vulature (a ɛkyerɛ sɛ) no sɛ: "Wo maame awu, "" He (Bulure), " a ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ memfa sika kɔkɔɔ nto me basa so mma ayi akyi pɛɛ. Bere a oduu hɔ no, ɔkae sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ oguan kɔ hɔ na ne ho tɔ no; nanso bere a ɔsɔre fii adi no, ofii adi, na ne ho tɔɔ no. Ɔkae sɛ: "O, O, Crow agyapade ayera, na ɛbɛyɛ dɛn na matumi asan akɔ fie? Ɛno nti na wubehu Vulture, a bere nyinaa ɔbɔ n'ani kyerɛ tiafi so, efisɛ ɔrehwehwɛ Crow ahenni no de abrɛ no no.

Ɛno nti na mpanyimfo no ka sɛ, "Ɛyɛ papa sɛ wogye obi di. "

Eyi, m'asɛm a maka yi, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (anaa) Sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom begye atom sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na wɔn a aka no nso, wubetumi ayi me ayɛ (nea wɔreka no nti).
