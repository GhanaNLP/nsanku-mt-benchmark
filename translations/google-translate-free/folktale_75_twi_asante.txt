And Okyem turned himself into a handsome young man, and married her, and took her to his city' He said, "I am Osebo and I will marry you. He said, "Father, I want you sara. And one gave birth to a 'man who was a 'man. And then he went back to her and had a 'son. And his old woman said, "I'm going to fight for my 'son's example. And his wife was gone, and he hid her. And the husband came; he said, "Has anyone come here?" And he said, "No one comes here." He said, "'Dieben is the heavenly hole, hole! now is the dwonso e'gu me?" He said, "Wounds!" The next morning, the husband went into the woods', and the son said to his wife, "Go and reach the woods that don't speak, when you reach the river, don't speak, and you speak that he will come and tell you. Then he reached it, did not listen, and shouted, "My thank you! my thank you!" And Osebo caught him koye, and when he ye' saw him, he fell down, fell down, and cried. The husband said, "Aden?" He said, "My stomach. In the morning the 'son said, "I'm going to my town. She stirred up her cow to carry it, and took her two children; they will go before him, they will follow him. And when he heard some of the 'to, he said, "Believe, defuo 'ba. And he heard one for the bridegroom, "Believe in the defuo 'ba. If the husband returned it. Then he reached his hometown', Osebo said, "Aso me! if your 'mother came here and you did not tell me, and you gave me a kiss', and stand up and let me name these children of mine: the son is Ofori; the daughter is Oforiwa. Osebo gave the money to his children and said, "You hate the animal that Okyem has killed; now that we have killed Okyem, you must throw 'sapo and hyire on him; I bless you kose! kose!
