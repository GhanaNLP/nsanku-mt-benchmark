SƐ OBI YƐ WO YIYE A, KYERƐ WO Aseda denam YIYE A WƆBƐYƐ NO AKATUA NO SO

WƆKA sɛ na Ɔkɔre bi wɔ hɔ (bere bi), na wɔ n’akyinkyin mu no ɔkɔhyiaa ɔbaa panyin bi a na kuru bi wɔ ne nan so. Na Ɔkɔre no kaa sɛ, "Adom me! Eyi yɛ kuru wɔ adesuakuw bi mu ankasa; sɛ wowɔ bi te sɛ eyi a, ɛmfa ho mmɔden biara a wobɛbɔ no, so wutumi nantew?" Ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, "Oh, kakraa bi pɛ." Ɔkɔre no kaa sɛ, "Mo nkurɔfoɔ, nnɛ, sɛ meyɛ biribi pa ma mo (ɛnnɛ) a, ɔkyena mobɛfa biribi bɔne de ada me ase." Ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, "Oh, mantumi anyɛ (ɛno)." Ɔkɔre no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ worentumi nyɛ w'ade saa a, mate." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Muta w'ani na bue." Na akwakoraa no mumii n’ani buee mu. Ɔkɔre no kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ wo kuru no." Na bere a ɔbea panyin no bae sɛ ɔrebɛhwɛ no —ɛnyɛ ne nkae biara. Afei ɔmaa no too (n’ani) bio; obuee wɔn, na ɔhunuu sɛ wɔaprapra asase no nyinaa so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "To wɔn mu bio." Ɔtoo mu, na obuee mu, na afie gyina hɔ, pintinn. Na ɔmaa ɔsan too wɔn mu bio. Obuee wɔn mu sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obehu kurow bi a ne kɛse te sɛɛ; ɛhɔ na na ɛwɔ —kɛse. Ɔkɔre no kaa sɛ, "Ɔbaa panyin, ɛyɛ wo dea (lit. wɔ wo nsam)." Ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, "Aseda a mede ma wo." Afei akwakoraa no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mefa de da wo ase?" Ɔkɔre no kaa sɛ, "Mempɛ ade ketewaa bi mpo, me deɛ, (nea) mepɛ ara ne Silk-cotton-dua yi a egyina hɔ." Ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, "Adeɛ yi (wobisa) ɛdeɛn na ɛwɔ mu, fa." Afei Ɔkɔre no tu kɔe, na ɔsiane kɔɔ so, na ɔpam berebuw, na ɔtow nkesua abien, de guu mu. Na ɔtoo nkesua mmienu no, na ɔkɔɔ sɛ ɔrekɔhwehwɛ biribi ama ne mma adi. Akwakoraa no banana a ɔne no te no fii ase de abubuw sɛ, "Ehe! Ehe!" Akwakoraa no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn ne asɛm no?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma menwe Ɔkɔre mma." Akwakoraa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛhe na mɛkɔ akɔfa Ɔkɔre mma?" Abana ketewa no fii ase bio, na ɔde abubuw! abubuw! Akwakoraa no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn ne asɛm no?" Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Ma menwe Ɔkɔre mma, ɛfiri sɛ sɛ mannya bi anwe a, mɛwu." Ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, "Ah! ɛsɛ sɛ eyi, me banana wu esiane sɛ onni Ɔkɔre mma a ɔbɛwe nti? Up wo nya, fa agyan na kɔbɔ silk-cotton-dua no na fa Ɔkɔre mma brɛ me." Akuraa no asefo kɔɔ hɔ, (agyan no bɔɔ), Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! Na ɛte sɛ nea dua no rekɔ fam, bere a Ɔkɔre mma no mu panyin no huruw kɔɔ soro, na ɔbaa begyinaa berebuw no ano, na ɔmaa osu so teɛm; ɔfrɛɛ ɛna no kaa sɛ:

Sango, anomaa e!

Sango, anomaa, Ɔkɔre ba!

Sango, anomaa e!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔdidi a, san bra!

Sango, anomaa e!

Sango, o! o!

Ɛna no tee sɛ ne ba no resu; ɔsɔree, (na ne ntaban a ɛrebɔ no nnyigyei yɛ,) fa! Ɔbaa hɔ; ɔkaa sɛ, "Sanguri," na saa dua no (a ɛkaa kakraa bi na wɔatwa) no boom bio, na nnipa a na wɔrebɔ no nyinaa, wɔmenee wɔn. Afei ɔfaa aduane a ɔde baeɛ no de maa ne mma. Afei ɔkaa sɛ, "Merekɔ, sɛ ɔbaa panyin no ba sɛ ɔrebɛfa mo a, ma ɔnkɔfa mo." Na ɔbaa panyin no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na bɔ dua no na fa abɔdeɛ no bra ma me banana no nwe." Na wɔkɔɔ hɔ ne mprenu so, Pinpin! Pinpin! Pinpin! Na ɛte sɛ nea dua no bɛkɔ fam, bere a (Ɔkɔre) ba no fi adi bae bɛgyinaa berebuw no ano, na ɔfrɛɛ ne maame kaa sɛ:

Sango, anomaa e!

Sango, anomaa, Ɔkɔre ba!

Sango, anomaa e!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔdidi a, san bra!

Sango, anomaa e!

Sango, o! o!

Ɔfrɛɛ ne maame, na ɔfrɛɛ, na ɔfrɛɛ, na ɔfrɛɛ —mmua biara nni hɔ —na afei dua no kasae bere a ɛbɔɔ fam no, "Brim!" (ɛkaa sɛ). Wɔfaa Ɔkɔre mma no kɔe; wɔde biako maa akwakoraa no, nanso biako a ɛkaa hɔ no tu kɔe na esi fam wɔ Wawa-dua bi so. Biako, wɔhyew, na wɔde maa akwakoraa no banana, na ɔde kaa plantain a wɔayam (na ɔredi) no ho. Ɛno akyi bere tiaa bi no, Ɔkɔre no bae. Ɔduruu dua a wɔatwa no ho no, ɔhunuu ne mma no mu baako sɛ ɔte hɔ; obisaa no nea asi, na ɔkaa nsɛm no nyinaa kyerɛɛ no. Ɔkɔre no sii kwan so kɔɔ akwakoraa no akuraa ase. Ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, na akwakoraa no banana redi ne mma no mu baako. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔbea panyin, mema wo akwaaba." Ɔfirii akwakoraa no fie baeɛ na ɔfirii (ne nkonyaayie) ase wɔ kuro no akyi. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sanguri," na onipa biara yerae, na ɔsan kaa sɛ, "Sanguri," na ofie biara bubui prɛko pɛ na atrae biara anka. "Sanguri," akuraa no san bɛyɛɛ kwae. "Sanguri," na ɔbaa panyin no kuru no san bae. Na ɔkaa sɛ, “Akwakoraa, woahunu, ɛno nti na mpanimfoɔ no ka sɛ, sɛ ebinom yɛ wo papa a, da no ase denam papa a wobɛyɛ no so na nnye bɔne mfa nna no ase.” Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebia wobɛfa ebinom sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no betumi akamfo me (wɔ nea wɔaka no ho).
