SƐNEA ASAFO NO NIPA MU BƐYƐE NSƐM

NA (bere bi) Nwansena ne Asono te, wɔ hɔ, na Asono ka kyerɛɛ Nwansena no sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔhwehwɛ mmaa a yɛbɛware." Nwansena no kaa sɛ, "Ho." Afei Asono no guaree, na ɔde ngo guu ne ho, na ɔde ne sika kɔkɔɔ agude siesiee ne ho, na ɔkyekyeree ne nna dedaw no, na ɔde maa Nwansena no sɛ ɔnsoa ne ti. Na wɔsiim; wɔbaa akuraa bi ase. Ɛno na Nwansena no kaa sɛ, "Ɛnanom nyinaa ne Agyanom nyinaa, mema mo anwummerɛ nkyia." Wobuae se, "Yen kunu pa." Asono nso duu hɔ; ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnanom nyinaa ne Agyanom nyinaa, mema mo anwummerɛ nkyia." Wobuae se, “Wo a wode ano fĩ no wo akokoduru ma yɛn anwummere nkyia?” Wosiim, na woduu akuraa a edi hɔ no ase. 'Wansana, Nwansena no kaa se, "Enanom nyinaa ne Agyanom nyinaa, mema mo anwummere nkyia." Saa ara na wobuae. Asono nso duu hɔ, na ɔkyeaa wɔn, na wɔbuae sɛ:

"Wo a w'ano yɛ fĩ, wopɛ yɛn anwummere nkyia?" Wosiim kwan; wɔforoo kwan no so, Asono no kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ ha! fa mpa dedaw no brɛ me na wofa sika kɔkɔɔ nneɛma nketenkete yi na wosiesie wo ho, sɛ ɛyɛ mpa dedaw no nti na mmeawa no dɔ wo a, mɛhwɛ." Na (Nwansena no) gyee (agudeɛ no), ɔsiesiee ne ho, na wɔsiim kɔduruu akuraa a ɛdi hɔ no ase. Na Asono no wɔ n’anim, na na mpa dedaw no wɔ ne ti so; ɔkyeaa (nkuraa asefo). Wokaa se, "Nkyea yen, wo a wode ano fĩ; ɛfata sɛ wosoa mpa dedaw yi." Afei Nwansena no duu hɔ; ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnanom nyinaa ne Agyanom nyinaa, mema mo anwummerɛ nkyia." Na wobuae se, "O wo dɔfo; ampa ara aberanteɛ bi wɔ ha a ɛfata sɛ wohwɛ; sika kɔkɔɔ nneɛma nketenkete; sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ wo; sɛ ɛnyɛ ɔhweɛ a anka yɛbɛnya no a, anka yɛbɛka wo ho wɔ w'akwantuo no mu." Na wɔkɔɔ so. Wɔduruu kwan no so ankyɛ na Asono no kaa sɛ, "Hey! adamfo, gyina hɔ dinn, twetwe me sika kɔkɔɔ nneɛma nketenkete na ma me ntɛm, ntɛm, ntɛm." Bere a Ɔkraman no wiee ne ho fii agude no so na ɔde maa no no, Ɔsebɔ no hwɛɛ kwan no ho hyiae na ɔtetew nwansena bi a ɔde bɛkyekyere Nwansena no. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Mɛkyekyere wo, na dɛn na wowɔ ka?” Na Nwansena no kaa sɛ, “Menni hwee ka.” Asono no toaa so sɛ, “Me ne wo nnuu baabi a yɛrekɔ no mpo [...] (na nanso mmea no redɔ wo dedaw).” Afei Asono no kyeree no de no kyekyeree dua bi ho. Na ɔsan nso kɔhintaw baabi foforo. Ɛno akyi bere tiaa bi no, Plantain no twaam wɔ kwan so rekɔ gua so, na bere a ohuu no (Nwansena no) no ɔkae sɛ:

“Hena na ɔsensɛn hɔ tuntum kɛse saa?

Hena na ɔsensɛn hɔ a ɛhyerɛn kɛse saa?”

(The Fly) kae sɛ:

“Ɛyɛ me, Nwansena, Kwao Boakye.

Adenen!

Gyahene Kofi Amo, Adenen!

Ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meba, na yɛakɔhwehwɛ mmabaa.

Adenen!

Mmabaa no ka sɛ wɔnnɔ no.

Adenen!

Wakyekyere me.

Adenen!

Wakyekyere me koraa.

Adenen!

Ogyefo, gye me nkwa.

Adenen!

Sɛ wogye me nkwa a, fa kwan no na kɔ.

Adenen!” Nanso Plantain no kaa sɛ, "Asono akyekyere wo, enti merentumi nnyae wo"; na ɔkɔɔ. Amankani, Coco-yam no, baa (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Aburo, Atoko no, baa (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Atwibo, Yam no, baa (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Adua, Bean no, baa (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Akam, Ɛmo no, baa (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Akapinkye, plantain a wɔayam no Kɔkɔɔ no, baa (na ɛkɔɔ) saa ara; Bankye, Cassava no, baa (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Brofere, Paw-paw no, baa (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Emo, Aburow no, baa (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Efrere, Pumpkin no, baa (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Habayere, Bush-yam no, baa (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Kwadu, Banana no, baa (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Kontomere, Coco-yam nhaban no, baa (na ɛkɔɔ) saa ara; Krobona, Bean no, baa (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Mako, ɛmo kɔkɔɔ no, baa (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Nkatee, Ground-nut no, baa (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Nwiadowa, Turo-egg no, baa (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; Nkuruma, Okrofoɔ no, baa (na ɔkɔɔ) saa ara; na afei Dokon'fa, Half-a-ball-of-Kenki (Boiled-pounded-corn), baa hɔ bɛkaa sɛ:

"Hena na ɔsensɛn hɔ tuntum paa saa?

Hena na ɔsensɛn hɔ glossy paa saa?"

(The Fly) kae sɛ:

"Ɛyɛ me, Nwansena, Kwao Boakye, Adenen!

Gyahene Kofi Amo, Adenen!

Ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meba, na yɛakɔhwehwɛ mmabaa.

Adenen!

Mmabaa no ka sɛ wɔnnɔ no.

Adenen!

Wakyekyere me.

Adenen!

Wakyekyere me koraa.

Adenen!

Ogyefo, gye me nkwa.

Adenen!

Sɛ wogye me nkwa a, fa kwan no na kɔ.

Adenen!"

Kenki bɔɔl fã kaa sɛ, "Mate, mɛsan wo." Ɔkɔɔ hɔ, na ɔsansan no. Bere a ɔyɛɛ saa wiei no, mpofirim ara Asono no puei. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Adɛn nti na woasansan me nipa no?” Ɔno (Half-a-ball-of-Kenki) kaa sɛ, "Masansan no, na nea wobɛyɛ no, yɛ." Ɔno (Asono) kaa sɛ, "Me fam no, me ne wo renyɛ hwee koraa, me ne wo nko ara na yɛde yɛn nsa bɛko." Half-a-ball-of-Kenki kaa sɛ, "Sɛ yɛrekɔko a, momma yɛnkɔ kwaeɛ no mu, na yɛnbubu nnua na yɛnsɔ gya." Enti wɔkɔebubuu nnua, na wɔsan bae. Na ɔno (Half-a-ball-of-Kenki) kaa sɛ, "Momma yɛmfa ogya nkɔ mu." Na Asono no de gya too mu. Bere koro no ara mu no wɔde wɔn ho bɔɔ ɔko, (na ne nnyigyei te sɛ) Yiri! Yiri! Yiri! Na Asono no atetew Half-a-ball-of-Kenki (afi ne mu), na watow no, bam! Half-a-ball-of-Kenki kaa sɛ, "(Adeɛ a ɛte saa nyɛ hwee), obi fa adeɛ a ɛte saa na ɔde hohoro baabi a ne yere guare no." Na wohyiae bio. Na Half-a-ball-of-Kenki nso atow Asono no afi ne nsam, bam! Na Asono no kaa sɛ, "Me nso mefa adeɛ a ɛte saa de hohoro me yere aguareeɛ, (nanso) wɔ yɛn nhyiamu a ɛdi hɔ no mu no, sɛ obiako tow ɔfoforo no firi ne so a, ɛnde ɛno ne tow a ɛtwa toɔ." Na wɔde wɔn ho bɔɔ (ɔko, na nnyigyei no te sɛ) Kikiri! Kikiri! Kikiri! Na Half-a-ball-of-Kenki maa Asono no so na ɔtow no guu ogya no mu. Asono no kaa sɛ, "_Me firim, me firim, me firim_" (Mapue, mapue, mapue. Saa na Asono nyaa ne nteɛm "_Me firim_"). (Afei) wohuu sɛ ne nipadua te sɛ eyi, ne nyinaa kurukyire, kurukyire, kurukyire (kyerɛ sɛ, ne nyinaa wɔ nsensanee); baabi a na fitaa no wɔ no, ɛhɔ na nsõ no aka no; na baabi a tuntum no wɔ no, ɛno nso, na fango tuntum no aka no. Na sɛ wonam, na wuhu ahaban a ɛda hɔ a wɔde Half-a-ball-of-Kenki (wɔde abɔ mu) a, wubehu nwansena pii a wɔrehuruhuruw wɔ so; na ɛno nyɛ biribi foforo biara nti (gye eyi nkutoo), wɔreda no ase.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebia wobɛfa ebinom sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no betumi akamfo me (wɔ nea wɔaka no ho).
