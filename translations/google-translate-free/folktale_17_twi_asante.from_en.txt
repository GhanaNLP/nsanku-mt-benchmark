SƐNEA ƐBAE SƐ WIM-NYANKOPƆN NO NSƐM BƐBƐYƐE "SPIDER-STORIES".

KWAKU ANANSE, Akɔre no, bere bi kɔɔ Nyankonpon, Wim-nyame no nkyɛn sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛtɔ Wim-nyame no nsɛm. Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Wobɛtumi atɔ wɔn?" Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "Mmom, mɛtumi." Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Nkuro akɛseɛ ne tumi te sɛ Kokofu, Bekwai, Asumengya aba, nanso wɔantumi antɔ, na wo a woyɛ ɔbarima a onni owura ara kwa no, (woka sɛ) wobɛtumi?" Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn ne (nsɛm no) boɔ?" Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Wɔnntumi ntɔ wɔn mma biribiara gye Onini abɔde Python; Osebo, Asono; Mmoatia, Fairy; (ne) Mmoboro, Hornets." Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "Mede saa nneɛma yi nyinaa bi bɛba, na (nea ɛboro saa), mede me ɔbaa panyin, Nsia (Abofra a Ɔto so Nsia) bɛka ntonto no ho." Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na fa wɔn bra afei." Akɔre no bae, na ɔkaa ne nyinaa kyerɛɛ ne maame, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ metɔ Wim-nyame no ho nsɛm, na Wim-nyame no se ɛsɛ sɛ mede Onini, Python; Osebo, Asono; Mmoatia, Fairy; ne Mmoboro, Hornets ba; na mekae sɛ mede wo bɛka ntonto no ho na makɔ akɔma Wim-nyame no." Afei Akɔre no bisabisaa ne yere Aso asɛm, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔyɛ na yɛanya Onini, Python no?" Na Aso ka kyerɛɛ no ​​sɛ, "Wo kɔ na wotwa mmɛdua bi nkorabata, na wotwa ahama a ɛwea nso, na wode wɔn ba." Na Akɔre no ne wɔn san bae. Na Aso kaa sɛ, "Fa wɔn kɔ asubɔnten no mu." Na Ananse faa wɔn; bere a ɔrekɔ no ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛware sen ɔno, ɛnyɛ tenten te sɛ ɔno; wodi atoro, ɛware sen no." Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "Ɛhɔ na ɔwɔ, ɔda hɔ." Python (a na wate saa nkɔmmɔbɔ a wɔde wɔn adwene bu yi) kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛfa ho?" Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "So ɛnyɛ (me yere) Aso na ɔne me regye akyinnye sɛ saa mmɛw nkorabata yi ware sen wo, na meka sɛ ɔyɛ ɔtorofo." Na Onini, Python no, kaa sɛ, "Fa bra, na bra bɛsusu me." Ɔfaa mmɛw nkorabata no de too Python no amu no ho. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Trɛɛ wo ho mu." Na Python no trɛw ne ho mu, na Ananse faa hama-wene no na ɔde kuru no (na nnyigyei a ɛkyekyere no yɛ nwenene! nwenene! nwenene! kɔsii sɛ ɔbaa ne ti so. Ananse, Akɔre no, kaa sɛ, "Ɔkwasea, mede wo bɛkɔ Wim-nyame no nkyɛn na magye Wim-nyame no anansesɛm (de sesa)." Ananse de no kɔɔ Nyame (Wim-kɔ). Wim-kɔ kaa se, "Me nsa aka (i. e. Manya), nea ɛda so ara aka wɔ hɔ." Akɔre no san bae bɛkaa nea asi no kyerɛɛ ne yere, na ɔkae sɛ, "Akɔre no da so ara wɔ hɔ." Ne yere kaa sɛ, "Hwehwɛ akutu, na fa nsu hyɛ mu ma, na fa kɔ." Akɔre no faa kwae no mu, bere a ohui sɛ (akuwakuw bi) Hornets sɛn hɔ, na ɔhwiee nsu no bi gui na ɔpetepetee ɛno guu Hornets so. Afei Akɔre no hwiee nkaeɛ no guu ne ho so, na ɔtwitwaa plantain no ahaban bi na ɔde kataa ne ti so sɛ, "Sɛnea osuo no aba no, sɛ ɛnyɛ papa sɛ mommra bɛhyɛn yei mu, me gourd, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a osuo no rentɔ mo; wonhu sɛ mafa plantain ahaban bi sɛ mede bɛkata me ho?" Afei Hornets no kaa sɛ, "Yɛda wo ase Aku, yɛda wo ase Aku." Hornets no nyinaa tuu, wɔyerae kɔɔ gourd no mu, fom! Father Spider kataa ano, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Nkwasea, manya mo, na mede mo rekɔgye Wim-kɔ (de sesa) ho anansesɛm." Na ɔfaa Hornets no ka kyerɛɛ Wim-nyame no (a wahu wɔn) tutuu amoa bi a emu dɔ paa, na ɔkataa so, na ɔsan baa fie Ade kyee ntɛm ara, bere a nneɛma fii ase daa adi no, Akɔre no kae sɛ ɔbɛtow, na bere a ɔkɔɔ no, (lo) Asono bi da amoa no mu no, Ananse kae sɛ: "Agya ketewa bi ba, ɛna kumaa ba, maka akyerɛ wo sɛ mma nnom nsa, na afei, sɛnea obi bɛhwɛ kwan afi wo hɔ no wobow nsa, na ɛno nti na woatɔ amoa no mu no; sɛ meka sɛ mɛyi wo afiri mu a, ade kyeeɛ no, sɛ wohunu me, anaa saa ara nso na me mma no mu biara a, wobɛkɔ akɔkyere wɔn." Asono no kaa sɛ, "O! Mantumi anyɛ adeɛ a ɛte saa." Ananse kɔtwaa nnua mmienu, de baako too ha, na ɔde baako too hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa wo nan baako to ha, na fa wo nan baako nso to ha." Na Asono no de sisii (baabi a wɔka kyerɛɛ no). Bere a ɔrebɛforo akɔ soro no, Ananse maa ne sekan no so, na wɔ flash mu no, ɛsiane wɔ ne ti so, gao! (ɛyɛ nnyigyei a ɛyɛeɛ). Amoa no gyee Asono (ne) fom!(na ɛyɛ n’ahwease nnyigyei). Ɔyii Asono no firii mu na ɔde baeɛ; ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔkwasea, mede wo rekɔsesa Wim-nyame no nsɛm." Ɔmaa Asono so sɛ ɔnkɔma Nyame, Wim-nyame no. Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Me nsa aka, nea aka no da so ara wɔ hɔ." Akɔre no bae, na ɔdwim Akua bi ba (nnua abofra tuntum bi a n’anim yɛ tratraa), na ɔbɔɔ latex a ɛyɛ nnam bi (a efi dua bi mu) na ɔde plaster guu doll no nipadua so, na ɔbɔɔ eto (yams a wɔayam), na ɔde bi guu doll no nsa mu, na ɔbɔɔ bi bio de guu kɔbere ahina bi mu; ɔde hama kyekyeree doll’s asen, na ɔne no kɔe na ɔde sii odum-dua bi ase, beae a Fairies no ba bɛbɔ agoru no. Na Fairy bi bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Akua, metumi adi saa mash yi kakra?” Ananse twee hama no, na abofraa no de ne ti too fam. Anansesɛm no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabeanom no mu baako sɛ, “Ɔse ebia mɛdi bi.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛnde, di bi.” Na odii wiei, na ɔdaa no ase (doll no). Nanso bere a ɔdaa no ase no, wammua. Na Fairy no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea sɛ, “Sɛ meda no ase a, ɔmmua.” (nea odi kan) Fairy no nuabea kae sɛ, “Bɔ ne su-beae.” Na ɔbɔɔ no akuturuku, pa! Na ne nsa no bataa ho. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea se, “Me nsa abata ho.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Fa deɛ aka no na bɔ ne su-bea bio.” Na ɔfaa no na ɔbɔɔ no akuturuku, pa! na eyi nso, bataa ho denneennen. Na Fairy no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no sɛ, “Me nsa mmienu abata denneennen.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Fa wo yafunu pia.” Ɔpiaa no, na ne yafunu no bataa ho. Na Ananse ba bɛkyekyeree no, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Ɔkwasea, manya wo, mede wo bɛkɔ Wim-nyame no nkyɛn (de sesa) n’asɛm no.” Na ɔne no kɔɔ fie. Na Ananse ka kyerɛɛ ne maame, Ya Nsia (abofra a ɔtɔ so nsia), sɛ, “Sɔre, ma yɛnkɔ, ɛfiri sɛ merefa wo ne Anansesɛm no akɔma Wim-nyame (de sesa) n’asɛm no.” Ɔmaa wɔn so, na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔɔ baabi a Wim-nyame no wɔ no. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Wim-nyame, Fairy bi ni, na me akwakoraa a mekaa ne ho asɛm no, ɔno nso ni.” Afei Wim-nyame frɛɛ (ne mpanimfoɔ), Kontire ne Akwam atitire, Adonten (asraafoɔ kuo titire no kannifoɔ), Gyase (major-domo); Oyoko, Ankobea, ne Kyidom (akyi awɛmfo no kannifo). Na ɔde asɛm no too wɔn anim, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Ahene akɛseɛ paa aba, na wɔantumi antɔ Wim-nyame no nsɛm, nanso Kwaku Ananse, Akɔre no, atumi atua ne boɔ;magye afiri ne hɔ Mmoboro, Hornets; manya afiri ne hɔ Mmoatia, Fairy; manya afiri ne hɔ Osebo, Asono; Python; Ɔkaa sɛ, “Monto n’ayeyi dwom.” “Eee!” (wɔteɛɛm). Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, “Kwaku Ananse, firi ɛnnɛ na ɛkɔ so daa, mefa me Wim-nyame no nsɛm na mede ma wo, kose! kose! kose! me nhyira, nhyira, nhyira. Ɛnyɛ bio yɛremfrɛ no Wim-nyame no nsɛm, na mmom yɛbɛfrɛ no Akɔre-asɛm.”

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
