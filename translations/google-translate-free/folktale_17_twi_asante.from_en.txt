KWAKU ANANSE, Akɔre no, bere bi kɔɔ Nyankonpon, Wim-nyame no nkyɛn sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛtɔ Wim-nyame no nsɛm. Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Wobɛtumi atɔ wɔn?" Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "Mmom, mɛtumi." Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Nkuro akɛseɛ ne tumi te sɛ Kokofu, Bekwai, Asumengya aba, nanso wɔantumi antɔ, na wo a woyɛ ɔbarima a onni owura ara kwa no, (woka sɛ) wobɛtumi?" Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn ne (nsɛm no) boɔ?" Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, “Wɔnntumi ntɔ wɔn mma biribiara.” gye Onini abɔde a ɔyɛ Python no nkutoo; Osebo, Ɔsono no; Mmoatia, Anansesɛm no; (ne) Mmoboro, Hornets." Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "Mede saa nneɛma yi nyinaa bi bɛba, na (nea ɛboro saa), mede me babea panyin, Nsia (Abofra a Ɔto so Nsia) bɛka lot no ho." Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na fa wɔn bra afei." Akɔre no bae, na ɔkaa ho asɛm nyinaa kyerɛɛ ne maame, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ metɔ Wim-nyame, ne Sk. no ho nsɛm y-nyame se ɛsɛ sɛ mede Onini, Python no ba; Osebo, Ɔsono no; Mmoatia, ɔyɛ Anansesɛm bi; ne Mmoboro, Hornets, ne wɔn a wɔyɛ adwuma wɔ hɔ; na mekaa sɛ mede wo bɛka ntonto no ho na makɔ akɔma Wim-nyame no." Afei Akɔre no bisabisaa ne yere Aso asɛm, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ na yɛanya Onini, Python no?" Na Aso ka kyerɛɛ no ​​sɛ, "Wo kɔ na kɔtwa mmɛdua bi nkorabata bi, na twa ahama a ɛwea bi nso, na fa wɔn bra." Na abiɛsa e Spider ne wɔn san bae. Na Aso kaa sɛ, "Fa wɔn kɔ asubɔnten no mu." Na Ananse faa wɔn; bere a ɔrekɔ no ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛware sen ɔno, ɛnyɛ tenten te sɛ ɔno; wodi atoro, ɛware sen no." Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "Ɛhɔ na ɔwɔ, ɔda hɔ." Python (a na wate saa nkɔmmɔbɔ a wɔde wɔn adwene bu yi) kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛfa ho?" Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ (me yere) Aso, na ɔne me regye akyinnye sɛ saa mmɛ-nkora yi yɛ l." onger sen wo, na meka sɛ ɔyɛ ɔtorofo." Na Onini, Python no, kaa sɛ, "Fa bra, na bra bɛsusu me." Ɔfaa nsateaa nkorabata no de too Python nipadua no ho. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Trɛɛ wo ho mu." Na Python no trɛw ne ho mu, na Ananse faa hama-abɔso no na ɔde kuru no (na tying no nnyigyei yɛ nwenene! nwenene! nwenene kosii sɛ ɔbaa ti. Ananse, Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "Ɔkwasea, mede wo bɛkɔ Wim-nyame no nkyɛn na manya Wim-nyame no anansesɛm (de sesa)." Ananse de no kɔɔ Nyame (Wim-nyame). Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, “Me nsa aka (i. e. Manya), nea ɛda so ara aka wɔ hɔ." Akɔre no san bae bɛkaa nea asi no kyerɛɛ ne yere, na ɔkae sɛ, "Akɔre no da so ara wɔ hɔ." Ne yere kaa sɛ, "Hwehwɛ akutu, na fa nsu hyɛ mu ma, na fa kɔ." Akɔre no faa kwae no mu, bere a ohui sɛ (akuwakuw bi) Hornets sɛn hɔ, na ɔhwiee nsu no bi gui na ɔpetepetee ɛno wɔ Hornets no so. Afei Akɔre no hwiee nea aka no guu ne ho, na ɔtwitwaa plantain no ahaban bi de kataa ne ti so. And now he addressed the Hornets, saying, "As the rain has come, had you not better come and enter this, my gourd, so that the rain will not beat you; don't you see that I have taken a plantain leaf to cover myself?" Afei Hornets no kaa sɛ, "Yɛda wo ase Aku, yɛda wo ase Aku." Hornets no nyinaa tu, na wɔyerae wɔ gourd no mu, fom! Paapa Akɔre kataa ano, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Nkwasea, manya mo, na mede mo rekɔgye Wim-nyame no anansesɛm (de sesa)." Na ɔde Hornets no kɔɔ Wim-nyame no nkyɛn. Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Me nsa aka, nea aka (da so ara) da so ara wɔ hɔ." Akɔre no san baeɛ bio, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Osebo, Asono no da so ara wɔ hɔ." Aso kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔtutu tokuru." Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛno dɔɔso, mete aseɛ." Afei Akɔre no kɔɔ sɛ ɔrekɔhwehwɛ Asono no akwan, na (ɔhunuu no) ɔtuu amoa bi a emu dɔ paa, na ɔkataa so, na ɔsan baa fie. Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu paa, bere a nneɛma fii ase huu no, Akɔre no kae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ, na bere a ɔkɔɔ no, (lo) Ɔsebɔ bi da amoa no mu. Ananse kaa sɛ: "Agya kumaa ba, ɛna kumaa ba, maka akyerɛ wo sɛ mma nnom nsa, na afei, sɛdeɛ obi bɛhwɛ kwan afiri wo hɔ no, woabow nsa, na ɛno nti na woahwe ase wɔ amoa no mu; sɛ meka sɛ mɛyi wo afiri mu, ade kyeeɛ, sɛ wohunu me, anaa saa ara nso na me mma no mu biara a, anka wobɛkɔ akɔkyere wɔn. Asono no kaa sɛ, "O! Mantumi anyɛ adeɛ a ɛte saa." Ananse kɔtwaa nnua mmienu, de baako too ha, na ɔde baako too ha. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Fa wo nan baako to ha, na fa wo nan baako nso to ha." Na Asono no de sisii (baabi a wɔka kyerɛɛ no). Bere a ɔrebɛforo akɔ soro no, Ananse maa ne sekan so, na wɔ flash mu no, ɛsiane kɔɔ ne ti so, gao! (ɛyɛ nnyigyei a ɛyɛeɛ). Ɔyii Asono no firii mu na ɔde baeɛ; ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔkwasea, mede wo rekɔsesa Wim-nyame no nsɛm." Ɔmaa Asono so sɛ ɔnkɔma Nyame, Wim-nyame no. Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Me nsa aka, nea aka no da so ara wɔ hɔ." Na Fairy bi baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Akua, metumi adi saa mash yi kakra?” Ananse twee hama no, na abofraa no de ne ti too fam. Anansesɛm no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabeanom no mu baako sɛ, “Ɔse ebia mɛdi bi.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛnde, di bi.” Na odii wiei, na ɔdaa no ase (doll no). Nanso bere a ɔdaa no ase no, wammua. Na Fairy no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea sɛ, “Sɛ meda no ase a, ɔmmua.” (nea odi kan) Fairy no nuabea kae sɛ, “Bɔ ne su-beae akuturuku.” Na ɔbɔɔ no akuturuku, pa! Na ne nsa no bataa ho. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea se, “Me nsa abata ho.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Fa deɛ aka no na bɔ ne su-bea bio.” Na ɔfaa no na ɔbɔɔ no akuturuku, pa! na eyi nso, bataa ho denneennen. Na Fairy no ka kyerɛɛ ne nuabea no sɛ, “Me nsa mmienu abata denneennen.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Fa wo yafunu pia.” Ɔpiaa no, na ne yafunu no bataa ho. Na Ananse ba bɛkyekyeree no, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Ɔkwasea, manya wo, mede wo bɛkɔ Wim-nyame no nkyɛn (de sesa) n’asɛm no.” Na ɔne no kɔɔ fie. Na Ananse ka kyerɛɛ ne maame, Ya Nsia (abofra a ɔtɔ so nsia), sɛ, “Sɔre, ma yɛnkɔ, ɛfiri sɛ merefa wo ne Anansesɛm no akɔma Wim-nyame (de sesa) n’asɛm no.” Ɔmaa wɔn so, na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔɔ baabi a Wim-nyame no wɔ no. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Wim-nyame, Fairy bi ni, na me akwakoraa a mekaa ne ho asɛm no, ɔno nso ni.” Afei Wim-nyame frɛɛ (ne mpanimfoɔ), Kontire ne Akwam atitire, Adonten (asraafoɔ kuo titire no kannifoɔ), Gyase (major-domo); Oyoko, Ankobea, ne Kyidom (akyi awɛmfo no kannifo). Na ɔde asɛm no too wɔn anim, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Ahene akɛseɛ paa aba, na wɔantumi antɔ Wim-nyame no nsɛm, nanso Kwaku Ananse, Akɔre no, atumi atua ne boɔ;magye afiri ne hɔ Mmoboro, Hornets; manya afiri ne hɔ Mmoatia, Fairy; manya afiri ne hɔ Osebo, Asono; Python;na n’ankasa pɛ mu no, Ananse de aka ho ed ne maame kɔɔ lot no so; eyinom nyinaa da ha.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Monto n’ayeyi dwom.” “Eee!” (wɔteɛɛm).
