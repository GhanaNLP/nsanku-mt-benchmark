Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

SƐNEA ƐBAE SƐNEA ƐBAE SƐ OBIARA NNYƐ MFISE A ONIPA FOFORO FIRI MU NSƐM

BERE bi na ɔbɔmmɔfo bi wɔ hɔ. Sɛ ɔsɔre a na ɔtaa kɔ kwae mu kɔhwehwɛ mmoa a wɔbɛkum wɔn, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obenya bi adi, na ebi nso atɔn. Afei da koro bi ɔkɔɔ wuram; ɔtee sɛ Kokotee, Bush-mprako no refrɛ ne busuani, sɛ, "Kokotee Asamoa e!" Obuae se, "Yiw, onua, yiw." Ɔno (mprako a odi kan) kaa sɛ, "Bere a yɛde bɛyɛ adwuma (wɔ yɛn mfuw mu) adu, enti momma yɛnkɔ dade adwumfo no adwumayɛbea, mma ɔnyɛ dade no na ɔmfa ano nhyɛ (yɛn nnwinnade a yɛde twa nnua) so, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a, sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ yetwa nnua biara a, yebetumi atwa. Bere a Bush-mprako frɛɛ ne nuanom no, ɔbɔmmɔfo no kotow de ne ho hintaw, na ɔtee nkɔmmɔbɔ no nyinaa. Afei Bush-mprako (a odi kan) no nua bisaa ne nua Bush-mprako, sɛ, "Na akuraa bɛn na yɛbɛkɔ, akɔbɔ dade no?" Obuae sɛ, "Yɛbɛkɔ akuraa a wɔfrɛ no Across-the-stream no ase." Na ɔkaa sɛ, “Da bɛn?” Obuae se, "Memeneda." Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no tee saa nhyehyɛeɛ yi nyinaa, na ɔsiim kɔɔ fie. Afei, bere a ɔbaa fie no, ɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ Across-the-stream panyin no, a ɛne nea ɔtee bere a ɔkɔɔ kwae no mu no. Na ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ akuraa no Titenani no sɛ, “Ma mmofra no nkɔ ntwa nnua na wɔmfa mmra, na sɛ, Memeneda no, Kwae mprako no sesa wɔn ho (wɔn nnipa) na wɔba a, yɛbɛfa wɔn na yɛakyekyere wɔn wɔ nnua no ho.” Mmofra no kɔtwaa nnua no, na wɔde bae. Akuraa no Titenani no kɔka kyerɛɛ akuraa no ase adwumfo no sɛ ɔmmɔ dade a ɛyɛ den mma no. Na ɔdansifoɔ no bisaa Titenani no sɛ, “Na dadeɛ a ɛdɔɔso yi nyinaa, a wose sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mebɔ no, dɛn na wode bɛyɛ?” Na ɔkaa amanneɛbɔ no kyerɛɛ no, sɛdeɛ ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ bi akɔ kwaeɛ mu, na ɔsan ba bɛbɔ amanneɛ sɛ Memeneda no mmoa binom bɛdan wɔn ho nnipa, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a wɔbɛba ne forge no mu abɛma wɔayɛ nnwinnadeɛ. Ɔdansifo no tuu mmirika kɔe sɛ ɔrekɔbɔ dade a wɔde ayɛ nneɛma no ntɛmntɛm. Bere a ɔpanyin no de ne nsa guu nnua ne nnuadewa no so wiei no, ɔmaa kurow no mu nteɛmfo no bɔɔ dade gong no, na ɔkae sɛ, “Memeneda, sɛ́ ɛyɛ ɔbea anaa ɔbarima no, ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara kɔ baabiara.” Memeneda dui, na anɔpa no, ɔbaa panyin bi ka kyerɛɛ ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no sɛ, “Kɔ na kɔyam ɛmo, ne nkyene, ne ayɛyɛdeɛ, ne baabi a mmoa no bɛpae wɔn nwoma na wɔde ato hɔ, so wo nso, sɛ wokɔ hɔ a, fa ɛmo no na wode petepete so, na sɛ mmoa no ba ha, ne berɛ a yɛkyere wɔn, na sɛ ebinom guan kɔfa wɔn nwoma, na wɔhyɛ, berɛ a ɛmo pira wɔn, na wɔtow wɔn gu, wɔbɛyɛ nnipa (bio).” Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kɔeɛ na ɔde ne ho hintaw wɔ hɔ. Na ɔtee sɛ wɔrefrɛ sɛ, “Kokotee Asamoa, Memeneda adu, momma yɛnkɔ.” Enti wɔn nyinaa bae. Woyii wɔn nwoma, na wɔde guu fam hɔ. Afei, ( Mprako no mu baako,) a na ɔyɛ nhabannuru ho oduruyɛfo no, ka wɔn ho, na ɔfaa ne were de too baabi foforo. Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no hwɛɛ wɔn, na ɔmaa wɔn kɔeɛ. Ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔfaa nwoma no nyinaa de petepete ɛmo a wɔayam no so, na ɔfaa de guu asubɔnten no mu, na ɔmaa nsuo no kɔeɛ. Nanso, Herbalist no de a ɔde too nkyɛn fi nea aka no ho no, ɔbɔmmɔfo no anhu. Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no baa fie, na Ɔpanin no nso frɛɛ nnipa a wɔaboaboa wɔn ho ano atwa adwumfo no ho ahyia no na ɔmaa wɔbaa ne fie. Ɛberɛ a saa nkurɔfoɔ yi baa Ɔpanin no fie no, wɔbisaa Ɔpanin no se, “Adɛn nti na woafrɛ yɛn?” Ɔpanin no kaa sɛ, “Na moyɛ me mmarima, na moguanee (pɛnkoro) sɛ mobɛtena baabi foforɔ, ɛnnɛ moasan aba, ɛno nti na meka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ moba, ɛfiri sɛ meremma mo kwan sɛ monkɔ bio.” Mmoa no ka kyerɛɛ Ɔpanyin no sɛ, “Deɛ woaka no, yɛate; nanso yɛnim sɛ Ɔbɔadeɛ no ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no baeɛ bɛkaa yɛn ho nsɛm nyinaa kyerɛɛ wo, nanso ɛno mfa ho, yɛne wo bɛbom atena; nanso yɛnim sɛ deɛ woka no yɛ atoro; nanso, ɛnyɛ hwee; yɛda saa ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no ase. Enti yɛn ne wo bɛtena faako;nanso adeɛ baako wɔ hɔ a yɛbara, a ɛne sɛ, wobɛda yɛn mfitiaseɛ adi;anaasɛ ɛno sɛ mo manfoɔ no mu biara da yɛn mfitiaseɛ adi a, sɛ ɛba saa a, yɛbɛtetew yei, mo abusuakuo mu, na yɛakɔ.” Afei, bere a Ɔpanyin no kɔfrɛɛ wɔn no, oduruyɛfo no ne afoforo bi guanee. Na oduruyɛfoɔ no kɔfaa ne were na ɔdwane; nanso wɔn a wɔaka no san wɔn akyi. Na ɛno te saa efisɛ wɔantumi anhu wɔn nwoma. Ɔpanyin no penee tebea horow a mmoa no de sii hɔ no so, na wɔne mmoa no boom traa ase anigye so. Afei akuraa no ase mmarima waree mmoa no mmaa no bi; wɔwoo mma. Afei, da koro bi na mmoa no mu baako ne akuraa no asefoɔ no mu baako de wɔn nsateaa reko. Ɛno so no, akuraa no aseni no ka kyerɛɛ aboa no sɛ, “Yi wo ho fi hɔ, aboa bi a ɔte sɛ wo, a ɔyɛ Bush-mprako kuw no muni.” Ɔkaa saa no ara pɛ na mmoa no nyinaa aniwa yɛɛ kɔkɔɔ, na wɔkɔɔ Ɔpanyin no fie sɛ wɔrekɔka akyerɛ no, na wɔkae sɛ, “Nea ebetumi aba a yɛkaa ho asɛm kyerɛɛ wo no aba ankasa mprempren, enti dɛn na wobɛyɛ?” Ɔpanyin no maa wɔkɔfrɛɛ nnipa a wɔde akasakasa no bae no. Ɔpanin no hwɛɛ asɛm no mu yie na ɔbuu atɛn sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mmoa no gyae soboɔ no, ɛfiri sɛ (ɔkaa sɛ) ɛyɛ berɛ tenten a wɔaba na yei, bio nso, ne berɛ a ɛdi kan a obiara aka biribi akyerɛ wɔn pɛn (ɛfa wɔn mfitiaseɛ ho). Nanso mmoa no kaa sɛ, “Yɛrenhyia.” Na Ɔpanin no kaa sɛ, “Morentie, na mosusu sɛ deɛ ɔbarima yi kaeɛ no yɛ atosɛm, ɛnna monyɛ Bush-mprako?” Na mmoa no kaa sɛ, “O, yɛate.” Ɛno so na mmoa ne nnipa a Wɔtwa Nsuo no ko. Mmoa no sɛee akuraa no, kosii sɛ nnipa bɛyɛ du pɛ na wɔkaa hɔ. Na wɔsrɛɛ mmɔborɔhunu na wɔka kyerɛɛ mmoa no sɛ wɔn (mmoa no) wɔ wɔn afã pɛɛ. Mmoa no tiee, na wɔbɔɔ nkurɔfoɔ amanneɛ, na wɔkaa sɛ, “Asɛm bi a wɔaka dedaw no nyɛ den sɛ wɔbɛte ase, na sɛ yɛne wo ne yɛn bɛbom atra a, yɛbara yɛn mfitiaseɛ ho nsɛm a wɔka nyinaa. Sɛ wodwene da bi anaa woka ho asɛm (bio) a, ɛnde (yɛbɛsrɛ mo) monkyerɛ yɛn saa wura no ara a yɛfiri baa beaeɛ yi, na yɛasan akɔ hɔ.” Na nkurɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Yɛrentumi nyɛ adeɛ a ɛte saa bio da, deɛ yɛayɛ no ama yɛn abusuakuo asɛe, yɛrennyɛ saa bio.” Enti wɔmaa wɔbɔɔ baguam dawurubɔ bi, denam odawuro gong a wɔhwee no ma enti ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara ka onipa foforo fibea da, anyɛ saa a nea wɔada no adi no bɛma kurow no asɛe no so. Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebia wobɛfa ebinom sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no betumi akamfo me (sɛ maka ho asɛm).
