iv | Ghana maŋ nɔyeli - 2021 maŋbii ashika he gbɛjianɔtoo

Yitso: “Shika Hewalɛwoo kɛtsɔ Naagbee, Ekomefeemɔ kɛ Nɔyaa Nɔ”

SANE NI JƐ SHIKA HE NYƆŊMƆ NYƆŊMƆ LƐ JƐ

Ghana maŋbii kpakpai lɛ fɔ oshiki yɛ December 2020 koni amɛhiɛ H.E. maŋ onukpa lɛ, Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo kɛha be kroko koni eya nɔ etsu nitsumɔ kpakpa lɛ ni etsu babaoo. Hekɛnɔfɔɔ ni wɔnanemɛi Ghanabii lɛ yɔɔ yɛ Nɔyeli nɛɛ mli lɛ ji nɔ ni wɔbuuu akɛ nɔ ko bibioo.

2021 shika he gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ ji shika he gbɛjianɔtoo ko ni sa kadimɔ waa ni toɔ wɔ maŋ shika he gbɛjianɔ ekoŋŋ koni ekɛbu wala he, ekɛ hewalɛ awo jarayeli nitsumɔi ni yɔɔ haomɔ mli lɛ amli ekoŋŋ, ni ekɛ wɔhewalɛi ama Ghana ni hi jogbaŋŋ lɛ maa nɔ ekoŋŋ, yɛ COVID-19 sɛɛ. Budget lɛ damɔ wɔ yiŋtoo ni ji maŋ nɛɛ tsakemɔ ni wɔma shika helɛtemɔ ni baanyɛ akwɛ ehe nɔ lɛ nɔ.

Yɛ klɛŋklɛŋ be lɛ fã ko mli lɛ, wɔkɛ shika helɛtemɔ lɛ etee gbɛ nɔ ekoŋŋ, kɛtsɔ gbɛjianɔtoi ni ato he gbɛjianɔ jogbaŋŋ ni ji shika helɛtemɔ mli tsakemɔi wuji lɛ anɔ, wɔkɛ gbɛjianɔtoi ni haa shika ni anáa lɛ yaa hiɛ lɛ eba, wɔkɛ nibii ni haa shika helɛtemɔ yaa hiɛ lɛ etsu nii, wɔye wɔbua aŋkroaŋkroi nitsumɔhei lɛ, ni wɔkɛ gbɛjianɔtoi ni akɛwoɔ gbɔmɛi ateŋ lɛ etsu nii. Yɛ afi 2019 naagbee lɛ, wɔyɛ shika helɛtemɔ tsakemɔ gbɛ nɔ waa yɛ shika helɛtemɔ he okadii ni yɔɔ naakpɛɛ kɛ majimaji ateŋ sɛɛfimɔ akɛ maŋ ni miiya hiɛ lɛ sɛɛ, beni COVID-19 hela lɛ tswa wɔ lɛ. Shikayeli hei fɛɛ ni yɔɔ jeŋ fɛɛ, ni Ghana fata he lɛ enu bɔ ni nɛkɛ tsɛŋemɔ hela nɛɛ efite nii eha lɛ he.

Covid-19 tsɛŋemɔ hela lɛ kɛ naagbai babaoo ba ni ena wɔ nitsumɔi kɛ daa gbi shihilɛ nɔ hewalɛ. Shi kɛlɛ, nɔyeli lɛ kɛ haomɔ kpele ekadi bɔ ni hela ni gbɛɔ eshwaa mɛi lɛ egba wɔ akɛ maŋ lɛ naa eha lɛ ni ebɔi gbɛjianɔtoi ni baaye abua ni aku sɛɛ atswa jarayeli nitsumɔi ni ekɛ wɔshihilɛ aba bɔ ni eyɔɔ lɛ ekoŋŋ lɛ shishi. Hiɛnɔkamɔ fɛɛ elaajeee ejaakɛ wɔna gbɛi heei ni wɔɔtsɔ nɔ wɔfee nibii ni nibii ni baanyɛ aba lɛ bɛ naagbee. Nɔyeli lɛ etswa efai shi akɛ ebaakwɛ koni wɔná gbɔmɛi ni yɔɔ hewalɛ ni akɛni nakai eji hewɔ lɛ eto gbɛjianɔ koni Ghanabii lɛ awo amɛ tsofa koni wɔ fɛɛ wɔhi shi shiŋŋ koni wɔma Ghana ni wɔsumɔɔ ni wɔna lɛ.

Wɔkɛ nyɛ efee ekome yɛ naagbai amli po akɛ wɔ hiɛyaa gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ fã ni wɔkɛ ekãa etsu nii koni wɔkɛ wɔ shika helɛtemɔ lɛ aku sɛɛ aba hiɛyaa gbɛ nɔ. Amrɔ nɛɛ Ghanabii aaafee akpekpei 1.2 baanyɛ aná sɛkɛndre skul ni hi jogbaŋŋ ni hi jogbaŋŋ kɛha amɛwala be fɛɛ, wɔtsumɔ shikatoohe nitsumɔ lɛ he ni mɛi ni heɔ nii lɛ ateŋ mɛi babaoo enine eshɛ amɛshika ni amɛto lɛ nɔ kɛjɛ shikatoohei ni efite lɛ amli. Kɛfata he lɛ, ama tsɔnei ni abua naa lɛ ahe nitsumɔhe ko ni wɔna klɛŋklɛŋ tsɔnei ni abua naa lɛ yɛ wɔ jarayelihe kɛ gbɛjegbɛi anɔ.

Wɔkpɛlɛɔ nɔ yɛ anɔkwale mli akɛ wɔkɛ naagbai komɛi kpe beni wɔbɔɔ mɔdɛŋ ni wɔkɛ wɔhiɛyaa otii lɛ aya hiɛ lɛ. Nɔyeli lɛ kɛ hiɛdɔɔ miifee gbɛjianɔtoi ni baaha atsu naagbai nɛɛ ahe nii ni ekɛ hejɔɔmɔ aba jarayeli nitsumɔi anɔ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, wɔkɛ GH¢750 million CAP-BuSS Program lɛ je shishi yɛ May 2020 ni enɛ eye ebua Nitsumɔhei Bibii, Bibii kɛ Teŋgbɛ Nitsumɔhei (MSMEs) kɛ ekrokomɛi babaoo.

Taakɛ eji tsutsu ko lɛ, atsɛɔ wɔ koni wɔkɛ wɔhe awo mli ni wɔha wɔmaŋ lɛ aya hiɛ kɛtsɔ ekãa nifeemɔi ni akɛbaatsu nii koni anyɛ ana hiɛyaa aha mɛi fɛɛ lɛ nɔyaa nɔ. Fɛɛ sɛɛ lɛ, wɔkɛ wɔhewalɛ fɛɛ kɛ wɔtsui fɛɛ wo wɔhe shi akɛ wɔkɛ wɔhewalɛ fɛɛ kɛ wɔtsui fɛɛ baatsu Ghana sɔɔmɔ lɛ he nii.

Nyɔŋmɔ ajɔɔ wɔ fɛɛ.

Ghana ahi shi!

v | Ghana maŋ nɔyeli - 2021 maŋbii ashika he gbɛjianɔtoo

Yitso: “Shika Hewalɛwoo kɛtsɔ Naagbee, Ekomefeemɔ kɛ Nɔyaa Nɔ”

MLIBOTEMƆ

1. 2021 Citizen’s Budget lɛ ji nɔ ni Nɔyeli lɛ enyɛ etsu yɛ afi 2020 mli kɛ bɔ ni ekpɛ eyiŋ akɛ ebaabua shika naa koni ekɛtsu mlai kɛ gbɛjianɔtoi ahe nii yɛ afi 2021 mli lɛ he kukufoo. Anɔkwale ni eji akɛ yɛ afii ni eho lɛ amli lɛ, nitsumɔi heei babaoo ni esa akɛ aje shishi yɛ nitsumɔi ni yaa nɔ lɛ ahe nyɔmɔwoo mli lɛ fiɔ saneyitso nɛɛ sɛɛ. Jeee akɛ enɛ kɛ nɔnyɛɛ baa wɔshika nɔ kɛkɛ, shi moŋ yɛ yinɔsane mli lɛ, ekɛ nyɔmɔwoo ni sɛɛ efo eba, ni awooo nitsulɔi babaoo nyɔmɔ yɛ be naa yɛ nitsumɔ ni amɛtsu lɛ hewɔ. Enɛ haa nitsumɔi lɛ ahe nyɔmɔwoo fɛɛ yaa hiɛ ejaakɛ nyɔmɔwoo ni akɛtoɔ sɛɛ lɛ gbalaa nyɔmɔwoo he nyɔmɔwoo.

2. Yɛ afi 2021 lɛ, Nɔyeli lɛ eto gbɛjianɔ koni ekɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔ hee ko awo mli. Oti ni ma ehiɛ ji ni ekɛ nitsumɔi ni agbeko naa lɛ he kusum lɛ aba naagbee kɛtsɔ shiwooi ni akɛhaa akɛ abaagbe nitsumɔi ni yɔɔ momo lɛ anaa lɛ nɔ.

SHIKA HE NITSUMƆ KƐHA 2020

Shikayeli nitsumɔi wuji

3. Be kukuoo mli saji ni kɔɔ shika helɛtemɔ nitsumɔ he yɛ afi 2020 mli lɛ ji nɔ ni nyiɛ sɛɛ nɛɛ:

• Afi lɛ klɛŋklɛŋ nyɔji nɛɛhu lɛ mli lɛ, kɛ́ aja mli lɛ, GDP diɛŋtsɛ lɛ fɛɛ tee hiɛ oha mlijaa 0.2. Amɛmiikpa gbɛ akɛ GDP lɛ baaya hiɛ oha mlijaa 0.9 yɛ afi lɛ naagbee gbɛ;

• Non-Oil real GDP lɛ tee hiɛ oha mlijaa 0.4 yɛ afi lɛ klɛŋklɛŋ nyɔji nɛɛhu lɛ mli, ni akwɛɔ gbɛ akɛ afi lɛ baagbe naa kɛ oha mlijaa 1.6;

• December naagbee lɛ, nibii ahe jara ni yaa hiɛ lɛ ji oha mlijaa 10.4 kɛ akɛto otii ni atsake ni ji oha mlijaa 11.0 lɛ he;

• Shika he gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ mli nyɔmɔwoo fɛɛ ni akɛtoɔ shika he lɛ ji GDP lɛ mlijaa oha mlijaa 11.7 kɛto oti ni atsake ni ji GDP lɛ mlijaa oha mlijaa 11.4 he;

• Primary balance lɛ tsɔɔ akɛ shika ni sɛɛ efo ni ji GDP lɛ mlijaa oha mlijaa 5.3 ji shika ni sɛɛ efo ni ji GDP lɛ mlijaa oha mlijaa 4.6 ni ato he gbɛjianɔ lɛ; kɛ

• Majimaji Ateŋ Shika ni Akɛto lɛ fɛɛ ji U.S. dɔlai akpekpei toi akpei 8.6, ni ji nyɔji 4.1 ni akɛwoɔ nibii ni akɛbaajɛ maŋsɛɛ aba lɛ mli.

Maŋ nyɔmɔwoo hiɛyaa kɛha 2020

7. Shika ni awo yɛ December 2020 naagbee lɛ ji GH¢291.6 billion (US$50.8 billion), ni damɔ shi kɛha GDP lɛ mlijaa 76.1. Shika ni Nɔyeli lɛ bua naa koni ekɛwo shika helɛtemɔ nitsumɔhe lɛ yelikɛbuamɔ kɛ hewalɛ nitsumɔhe lɛ Independent Power Purchase (IPPs) nyɛmɔ he nyɔmɔ lɛ fata nɛkɛ mlijaa nɛɛ he. Kɛ shika helɛtemɔ nitsumɔhe lɛ yelikɛbuamɔ bɛ lɛ, 2020 nyɔmɔwoo lɛ ji GDP lɛ mlijaa oha mlijaa 71.5. Shika ni awoɔ lɛ mligbalamɔ ji bɔ ni nyiɛ sɛɛ nɛɛ:

• Shika ni awoɔ yɛ maŋsɛɛ: GH¢141.7 akpekpei toi akpei (US$24.7 akpekpei toi akpei) ni damɔ shi kɛha nyɔmɔwoo fɛɛ ni awoɔ lɛ mlijaa oha mlijaa 48.6; kɛ

• Shikpɔŋ nɔ nyɔmɔwoo: GH¢149.8 akpekpei toi akpei (US$26.1 akpekpei toi akpei) ni damɔ shi kɛha nyɔmɔwoo fɛɛ mlijaa oha mlijaa 51.4.

• Real GDP lɛ fɛɛ mliyaa ni ji oha mlijaa 5.0;

• GDP ni jeee Mu lɛ mliyaa ni ji oha mlijaa 6.7;

• December naagbee lɛ, nibii ahe jara ni yaa hiɛ ni ji oha mlijaa 8.0;

• Shishijee shika ni sɛɛ efo ni ji GDP lɛ mlijaa 1.3; kɛ

• Gross International Reserves ni akɛbaatsu nibii kɛ nitsumɔi ni akɛbaajɛ maŋsɛɛ aba lɛ he nii ni shɛɛɛ nyɔji 4.0.
