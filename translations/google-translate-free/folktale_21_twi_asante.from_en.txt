Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

SƐNEA OWEA (DUA-ASOƆ), ABOSOM-AKOTERE (CHAMELEON), NE OPATUO (ƆKƆSƐM) BAA BƐKEA WƆN NSUUM A ƐDA SO NO

OWEA, Dua-asono, (a ɔno nso wɔ abodin) Kwaduampon Kyerefo, Opatuo, Ɔkraman, ne Abosom-Akotere, Chameleon, (a n’abodin ne), “Ɔbɔadeɛ no wu”, “Abɔde a ogye afe-de-yɛ-biribiara”, “Ɔ-nantew-brɛoo-nanso-obedu-asase-a-aborɔfo-a-wɔ-akyiri-kyiri”, (mmoa yi nyinaa) bere bi na wɔbom tra. Afei, na wɔn mu biara nni ɔyere. Na Adobe-dua no tɔn ne ho maa wɔn. Da biara, sɛ Adobe-tree sɔre a, na ɔkɔ twɛn eyi; ade kyee no ɔtwɛn wɔn mu foforo; na ade kyee no wɔ foforo so. Da bi, bere a ɛyɛ Ɔbɔadeɛ-wu (sɛ ɔbɛhyɛ Adobe’s services) no bere na ɔhwehwɛɛ Adobe-dua no, ɛhe na obetumi ahu no? Na ofii hɔ kɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ Dua-asono ne Ɔkraman no. Na wɔkaa sɛ, “Kɔ frɛ no.” Na Ɔbɔadeɛ no-wu maa ne nne so sɛ: “Adobe o! Adobe o!”

Dua-asono no teɛɛm sɛ:

“Bere a ɔkɔe no, wansan aba, ɔkɔɔ, wansan, wansan, wansan bio!”

Na Ɔkraman no frɛɛ sɛ:

“Yɛakari (sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no) na ebia anka yɛbɛtow agu, m! Yɛakari (sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no) na ebia anka yɛbɛtow agu, m! m!”

Na ɛno ne abɔdeɛ mmiɛnsa yi nteɛm no mfitiaseɛ.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
