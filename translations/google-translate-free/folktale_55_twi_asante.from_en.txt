SƐNEA SPEAR-GRASS BAA ABUSUE NO MU

ƆKƆM kɛse bi bae na Ntikuma sii kwan so, na ɔkae sɛ ɔrekɔhwehwɛ aduan a ɔde bɛbrɛ ne papa, Paapa Ananse, Akɔre no. Osiim kɔɔ akuraa bi ase; (ɛhɔ) ohyiaa Akwakoraa-nanabea. Akwakoraa no ka kyerɛɛ no ​​se, “Me banana, adɛn nti na, bere a ebia obiara mma ha no, woaba ha?” Ntikuma buaa sɛ, “Ɛyɛ ɔkɔm nti, na maba sɛ merebɛhwehwɛ biribi a mɛkɔ akɔdi.” Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, “Kɔ m’afuo mu wɔ hɔ, nanso yam a ɛbɛka sɛ, ‘Twe me, tutu me,’ no ntutu, na mmom yam, a ɛbɛka sɛ, ‘Ntetew me, ntutu me,’ tutu na fa bra.” Ntikuma kɔe. Yam no bi kae sɛ, “Twe me, tetew me”; afoforo nso kaa sɛ, “Nntutu me, ntutu me.” Afei Ntikuma tetew wɔn a wɔkae sɛ, “Nntutu me, ntutu me.” Ɔde wɔn baa fie hɔ; ɔpaapaee wɔn ho. Afei Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, “Tow ɔfa ankasa no gu ma aduane, na fa nwoma no gu kukuo no mu na fa to gya no mu.” Ntikuma yii wɔn ho na ɔfaa nwoma no de guu ogya no so. Bere a wɔsiesiee wɔn ho no, oyii wɔn fii ogya no mu, dum kuku no mu nneɛma, na biribiara (emu) adan aduan pa. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, “Mawie, nanso ma me nam mfa nhyɛ (atokoɔ no) mu.” Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, “Kɔka kyerɛ Nkwakuo, Bonsu no, a wɔwɔ dua no so, sɛ ‘Akwakoraa-nana ka sɛ, mo nyinaa deɛ, mo ɛnanom’——!’” Bonsu no kaa sɛ, “Sɛ ɛnyɛ Akwakoraa-nana a, anka ɛsɛ sɛ yɛboro mo.” Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, “Me fam no, ɛnyɛ m’asɛm, na ebia wobɛba abɛboro no.” Mmoa no siane sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wɔbɛboro no. Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, “Me Sharp-knife, twa mmoa yi ti ma me.” Afei The-sharp-one twaa mmoa no ti. Ntikuma boaboaa mmoa no nyinaa ti ano, na ɔtetew yam bi kaa ho, na ɔne wɔn nyinaa sii kwan so kɔɔ n’akuraa ase. Paapa Akɔre hunuu (ne ba) Ntikuma sɛ ɔgyina n’anim, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Wɔkyea wo mmaeɛ, ntɛtea-kokoɔ a ɔbubu” (i. e. abakan). Ɔtoaa so sɛ, “Ma mensoa wo wɔ m’akyi.” Ntikuma kɔ sɛn hɔ. Afei Ananse kɔfaa yam baako; ɔkaa sɛ, “Sɛ meyɛ bosome biara a, mɛdi yei.” (Ne yere) Aso kaa sɛ, “Efi bere a Ɔbɔadeɛ no yɛɛ nneɛma no, so woahu ɔbarima bi a na nna ɔsram biara?” Ananse ne ne yere, ne ne mma wiee yam no, na ɛno nko ara na ɛkaa hɔ a ɛyɛ Paapa Akɔre dea. Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma daa adi no, Ananse kae sɛ, “Manya me nna a ɛba ɔsram biara.” Na ɔkɔɔ sɛ ɔrekɔtena (wɔ atew ne ho) fie no akyi, noaa yam no, na ɔdii, kɔsii sɛ (ne nyinaa) wiee. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, “Mɛkɔ bio.” Ananse kaa sɛ, “Me ne wo bɛkɔ (abom).” Ntikuma kaa sɛ, “Me ne wo renkɔ bom.” Bere a Ntikuma kɔdae no, afei Ananse kɔfaa ɔbɔmmɔfo were bag, de ntokuru nketewa guu mu, na ɔde nsõ hyɛɛ mu ma, na ɔde kyekyeree Ntikuma ntama no ano. Bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ntɛm pa ara, yiye, paa no, Ntikuma sii kwan no so. Bere a (esum a ahintaw) anim (nneɛma) no tetew mu no, Ananse, hwehwɛ Ntikuma sɛnea ɔpɛ no, antumi anhu no, afei ɔkɔhwehwɛɛ (beae) nsõ (ahwe ase). Bere a ohuu wɔn no odii spor no akyi, kɔɔ so na ɔkɔɔ so. Na ɔrekɔ, bere a, na Ntikuma ne Akwakoraa-nana te hɔ. Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, “Agya man, w’asɛm ne dɛn?” Ananse buae se, “Ɔkɔm agye me, enti ma me biribi nni.” Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, “Ɛhe na ɛsɛ sɛ minya ‘biribi’?” Ananse buaa sɛ, “Wosusu sɛ meyɛ ɔkwasea, mpo me ba, Ntikuma, baeɛ, na womaa no bi.” Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, “Kɔ afuo a ɛwɔ hɔ no mu;yam a ɛka sɛ, ‘Twe me kɔ soro, twe me kɔ soro’ no ntwe wɔn nkɔ soro, na mmom wɔn a wɔka sɛ, ‘Ntwe me nkɔ soro, ntwe me nkɔ soro no, twe saa wɔn no kɔ soro.” Akɔre no (buae), “Ah! nkwaseasɛm bɛn ni na woka kyerɛ me, sɛ obi ka sɛ ɔdɔ obi a, wohyɛ me sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ metan no, mentee nea ɛte saa da?” Nanso Akɔre no kɔɔ afuw no mu. Bere a oduu hɔ no, yam no bi kae sɛ, “Twe me kɔ soro, twe me kɔ soro,” ebinom san kae sɛ, “Ntwe me nkɔ soro, ntwe me nkɔ soro.” Na ɔtwee wɔn a wɔkaa sɛ, “Twe me, twe me kɔ soro,” na ɔne wɔn baa fie. Ɔbaa hɔ na ɔtwaa wɔn nwoma wieeɛ; Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, “Kɔ tow aduane pa fã no ngu, na fa nwoma no, fa gu kukuo no mu, na fa to ogya mu.” Ananse buae se, “Ah, asɛm a ɛporɔw bɛn na woka kyerɛ me yi, ɛfiri sɛ Ɔbɔadeɛ no na ɔyɛɛ nneɛma nti, yɛtow aduane pa no gu, na yɛkora nkorabata no?” Akɔre no faa aduane no fã pa no, na ɔde too ogya no so, nanso ɔfaa were no fã baako de guu so. Bere a aduan no noae no, oyii no, na ne nyinaa adan abo, nanso nwoma biako a ɔde guu soro no adan aduan pa. Agya Akɔre kaa sɛ, "Eh! this reincarnation of a rascal didn't lie (after all)." Na Akɔre no kɔprapraa nwoma no na ɔde baeɛ na ɔde too ogya no so. Bere a wɔnoaa aduane no, ɔyii no firii ogya no so, na na adan yam a wɔanoa yie sɛdeɛ wobɛpɛ sɛ wohunu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔbaa-nanabea, yei nyinaa ye paa, nanso afei ɛsɛ sɛ woma me meat sɛ wode bɛka ho." Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na hwɛ dua no, Bonsu wɔ so; sɛ wokɔ a, wobɛka sɛ, 'Moyɛ mmoa.'" Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "Ɔbea yi! Me ne Bonsu no nni akasakasa biara, na adɛn nti na mɛkɔ akɔyɛ wɔn ayayade wɔ saa kwan yi so?" Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, "Kɔ, na sɛ ɔhaw ba a, ɛno ne m'asɛm." Akɔre no sii kwan so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mo Bonsu, mo a mowɔ nnua yi so, Akwakoraa-nana se moyɛ mmoa." Mmoa no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ sɛ Akwakoraa-nana ɛsɛ sɛ yɛboro wo." Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, "Ɛnde bra bɛboro no."

Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɔbaa panyin, ɛno nyɛ nea ɛfata." Afei mmoa no hwehwee ne so na wɔbaa bɛboroo Ananse. Ɔbaa Panyin no kaa sɛ, "Me Sekan The-sharp-one, twa wɔn ti." Tintin! (na ɛyɛ nnyigyei a sekan no yɛe bere a ɛrefi n’adwuma ase no). Ɔbaa-nanabea no kaa sɛ, "Bɔbrɛa na ɛnyɛ den seesei." Nanso Akɔre no ante (ne ka saa). Afei, Akɔre no boaboaa nam no ano na ɔsan baa fie hɔ, na Akwakoraa no kɔɔ nkyɛn kakra. Afei Ananse wiaa ne sekan no de guu ne bag mu, na ɔde mmirika kɔe san baa n’akuraa ase. Bere a ɔsan duu n'akuraa ase no, ɔka kyerɛɛ (ne ba no sɛ), "Ntikuma, esiane sekan a mede me nsa ato so yi nti, momma yɛnkɔ kwae no mu." Enti wosiim, na wɔkɔhwɛɛ Wim Mprako bi. Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ntikuma, kɔ na yɛ wɔn ayayadeɛ. Ntikuma kɔkaa sɛ, "Bush-pigs, moyɛ fĩ. " Bush-pigs no buae sɛ, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ sɛ woyɛ Kwaku Ananse, Spider no ba a, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛba bɛboro wo. Kwaku Ananse ante no, na Sekan no baa fam, na Ananse kɔ bɛgyee. Wɔsan kɔɔ fie. Ade kyee, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ wɔbɛkɔ Wim-kɔ hɔ. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Agya, sɛ ɛnam Sekan yi nti a, nkɔ.” Ananse kaa sɛ, “Fi adi hɔ.” Ntikuma kaa sɛ, “Kɔ w’ankasa wo kwan so.” Ananse sii kwan so, na ɔkɔɔ Wim-nyame no nkyɛn wɔ hɔ, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no ​​sɛ, “Owura, ɛnnɛ, sɛ obi wɔ hɔ a wafom wo a, me nko, me, Kwaku Ananse, mɛtwa wɔn tiri nyinaa da koro na mede ama wo.” Wim nyame no kaa sɛ, “Aseda afɔrebɔ wɔ hɔ ma obiara a obetumi ayɛ saa, efisɛ asraafo dɔm kɛse bi a wɔyɛ atamfo gyina kurow no akyi, enti off you go.” Ananse kaa sɛ, “Momma wɔmfa nsã ne oguan baako mmra.” Ntɛm ara na wɔde wɔn bae. Ananse firii adi faa kwan no so; Ananse bisaa asraafoɔ a wɔyɛ atamfo no sɛ, “So wo na woka sɛ wo ne Wim-nyame no bɛko?” Wobuae se, “Yiw, saa na yɛka.” Afei na Wim-nyame no asraafo gyina Ananse akyi. Afei Kwaku Ananse kasa kyerɛɛ Akwakoraa Sekan, The-sharp-one, kaa sɛ, “Mutwa saa dɔm a wɔyɛ atamfo yi ti mma me.” Na Sekan no twaa wɔn ti, tan! tan! na ɔwiee. Ananse kaa sɛ, “Ɛno ye, gyae.” Nanso Sekan no hwee Wim-nyame no dɔm no so na na ɛretwitwa (wɔn ti). Ananse kaa sɛ, “Oo, ɛno ne Wim-nyame no asraafoɔ, enti gyae.” Nanso ɛtwaa so. Ananse kaa sɛ, “Sɛ wo nufu mu atɔ a, ma ɛnnwo.” Ow! wɔpopaa Wim-nyame asraafo no nyinaa fii hɔ. Afei Sekan no tu kwan wɔ wim; ɛbaa baabi a na Kwaku Ananse wɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Hwɛ yie, wunnim me? Wonnim me?
