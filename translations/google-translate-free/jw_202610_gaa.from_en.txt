1926

AFII OHA KO NI HO

JANUary 1, 1926 Buu-Mɔɔ lɛ kɛɛ akɛ: “Be ni Nyɔŋmɔ baafee gbɛi eha ehe lɛ ebɛŋkɛ, Nyɔŋmɔ baana mɔ ko ni baaye odase yɛ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ.”

Beni afi 1926 lɛ jeɔ shishi lɛ, Yehowa—kɛtsɔ asafo lɛ yitso, Kristo nɔ—ha efee faŋŋ babaoo akɛ esa akɛ shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ afee nɔ titri ni esa akɛ Kristofonyo kɛtsu nii yɛ eshihilɛ mli. Bɔni afee ni enyɛ etsu enɛ he nii lɛ, “tsulɔ anɔkwafo kɛ nilelɔ” lɛ kɛ woji heei ha Nyɔŋmɔ tsuji, efee tsakemɔi yɛ kpeei ahe, ni eha amɛnu gbɛnaa nii ni kã Kristofoi anɔ lɛ shishi jogbaŋŋ. ( Mat. 24:45 ) Kristo kwɛ koni amɛná tsũi kɛ nibii ni he hiaa koni amɛkɛtsu shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ.

WOJI HEE KƐ TSAKEMƆI NI AKƐBA KPEEI AHE

Yɛ April mli lɛ, The Golden Age (amrɔ nɛɛ atsɛɔ lɛ Awake! ) lɛ tswa adafi akɛ ajie wolo hee ko ni yitso ji Deliverance lɛ kpo. Wolo nɛɛ baaye Studies in the Scriptures lɛ najiaŋ akɛ wolo titri ni akɛtsuɔ nii yɛ shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ mli. Bɔni afee ni nyɛmimɛi ni yɔɔ Betel lɛ atsu nibii ni akpaa gbɛ lɛ ahe nii lɛ, amɛhe tsɔne hee ko ni nyɛɔ ekalaa woji 22 yɛ minitii kome mli. Biblia Kaselɔi lɛ kɛ wolo hee lɛ ha be fɛɛ be ni hegbɛ lɛ baaná.

Jehahi fuu ji nɛ ɔ nɛ Baiblo Kaseli ɔmɛ susu Studies in the Scriptures womi ɔmɛ a he ngɛ a kpehi a sisi. Se kɛ je May ɔ, a bɔni Hwɔɔmi Mɔ ɔ mi munyuhi a he susumi. Mɛni jɛ mli kɛba?

Asafo ko ngma ngɛ sɛ womi nɛ a ngma kɛ ya ha Nyɛminyumu Rutherford ɔ mi ke: “Bunker Hill, Illinois ecclesia [asafo] nyafii ɔ ngɛ nyɛ buae.” “Yɛ Hɔgbaa lɛ wɔkaseɔ [saji] ni Asafo lɛ eto kɛha nakai gbii lɛ. Wɔdaa shi waa akɛ wɔ fɛɛ wɔbaanyɛ wɔna la hee lɛ be mli ni eshɛɔ lɛ.”

“La hee” loo shishinumɔ ni mli tse lɛ ye ebua Biblia Kaselɔi lɛ ni amɛtee nɔ amɛkɛ amɛjwɛŋmɔ ma shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ nɔ. Ákɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, sane ni ji “Su loo Kpaŋmɔ—Mɛni?” lɛ tsɔɔ mli akɛ “sui kpakpai ni anáa,” ni kɛ ejwɛŋmɔ maa bulɛ kɛ mlihilɛ su pɛ nɔ lɛ ha mɛi komɛi ba nitsumɔ diɛŋtsɛ ni anɔkwa Kristofoi tsuɔ lɛ akɛ nɔ ko bibioo. Niŋmaa lɛ wo ŋaa akɛ: “Mɔ ni sumɔɔ Nuŋtsɔ lɛ, esa akɛ ebo ekitai lɛ atoi, Yehowa kɛɛ: ‘Nyɛji midasefoi akɛ miji Nyɔŋmɔ.’ Kristofonyo lɛ baasumɔ ni amrɔ nɛɛ eye odase akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ odasefonyo krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ.”

Sane kroko ni yitso ji, “Afɔleshaa kɛ Sɔɔmɔ” lɛ ma nɔ mi akɛ anɔkwa Kristofoi jieɔ suɔmɔ ni amɛyɔɔ kɛha Yehowa lɛ kpo kɛtsɔ anɔkwayeli ni amɛkɛsɔmɔɔ lɛ lɛ nɔ. Sane lɛ wo Biblia Kaselɔi fɛɛ hewalɛ ni amɛkɛ amɛhe awo Nuŋtsɔ lɛ sɔɔmɔ lɛ mli kɛ ekãa, kɛ hiɛdɔɔ, kɛ hiɛdɔɔ ni “amɛye Nuŋtsɔ lɛ odasefoi, ni amɛjaje eshɛɛ sane lɛ amɛtsɔɔ jeŋmaji kɛ maji ni yɔɔ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ lɛ.”— Yes. 43:​10, 12; Mat. 24:14 lɛ he.

KPEE MII MAA SHIƐMƆ NITSUMƆ LƐ NƆ MI

Ngɛ May nyagbe ɔ, Baiblo Kaseli fuu ya bua a he nya ngɛ kpokpa nɔ kpe ko nɛ a pee ngɛ London ngɛ England ɔ sisi. Nitsulɔi ni kpa nitsumɔ yɛ Britain lɛ ha ebafee nɔ ni wa akɛ abaahe nitsulɔi ni tsuɔ woji akalamɔ nitsumɔi ahe nii, no hewɔ lɛ nyɛmimɛi ni yɔɔ Brooklyn Betel lɛ tsu nii kɛteke nɔ koni amɛkɛ wolo bibioo ko ni yitso ji The Standard for the People lɛ ekomekomei 117,000 fata kpee lɛ he.

Yɛ Sohaa, May 29 lɛ, akɛ nakai woji bibii lɛ tsu nii jogbaŋŋ. Kpee lɛ he gbɛjianɔtoo kɛha nakai gbi lɛ wo hewalɛ akɛ: “Amiifɔ̃ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni ajɔɔ ehe krɔŋkrɔŋ ni eba kpee lɛ nine koni ekɛ ehe awo Maŋtsɛ lɛ kɛ Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ he adafi mli.

Aja woji ni fa fe 100,000. July 15, 1926 Buu-Mɔɔ lɛ bɔ amaniɛ akɛ: “Nanemɛi lɛ kɛ miishɛɛ tsu nii, ni amɛnu he akɛ amɛfee nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni amɛaanyɛ koni amɛbo Yehowa famɔ nɛɛ toi akɛ: ‘Nyɛji midasefoi akɛ miji Nyɔŋmɔ.’”

Enɔ jetsɛremɔ yɛ Royal Albert Hall lɛ, Nyɛminuu Rutherford wie saneyitso ni ji “Nɔ Hewɔ ni Jeŋ Hewalɛi lɛ Miihoso—Tsabaa Lɛ” lɛ he. Beni agbele shinaai lɛ sɛɛ minitii fioo pɛ mli lɛ, mɛi 13,000 yi asa lɛ nɔ obɔ. Asafoi babaoo ni eyi obɔ lɛ bo gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ toi jogbaŋŋ kɛtsɔ wiemɔ tsɔnei ni akɛwo kpo lɛ nɔ lɛ anɔ.

Ngɛ Nyɛminyumu Rutherford munyu ɔ mi ɔ, e tsɔɔ mi kaa je mi he wami ɔmɛ a hɛ mi kpatami ɔ je kpo nɛ a kua Yehowa kɛ E Matsɛ Yemi ɔ he. Nyɛminuu Rutherford tee nɔ ewie eshi Jeŋmaji Akpaŋmɔ lɛ, ni egba akɛ ebaakpa shihilɛ.

BENI okɛ Yehowa Odasefoi kaseɔ Biblia lɛ, mɛni ha ona nɔmimaa akɛ ona “hemɔkɛyeli kome”—ni ji jamɔ gbɛ kome ni Nyɔŋmɔ kpɛlɛɔ nɔ? ( Efe. 4:4-6 ) Anɛ suɔmi nɛ o na ngɛ Yehowa we bi a kpɛti ɔ ha nɛ o hɛ mo suɔ lo? Ani amɛjeŋba ni ja lɛ ná ojwɛŋmɔ kpakpa yɛ ohe? Mɛi babaoo nyaa anɔkwale lɛ he ejaakɛ Nyɔŋmɔ asafo lɛ haa amɛnuɔ he akɛ amɛyɛ shweshweeshwe ni asumɔɔ amɛ diɛŋtsɛ, ni shishinumɔ yɛ enɛ he. Suɔmɔ kɛ jeŋba kpakpa ji nibii ni kadiɔ anɔkwa jamɔ.— Yohane 13:35; 1 Pet. 2:12 lɛ he.

Shi kɛ́ onaanyo heyelilɔ ko tɔ onɔ loo yɛ gbɛ kroko nɔ lɛ ejieee suɔmɔ kpo etsɔɔ bo hu? Aloo kɛji akɛ naanyo heyelilɔ ko kɛ ehe wo esha ni yɔɔ hiɛdɔɔ mli—ni eji hiɛgbele nifeemɔ po mli, taakɛ eba aŋkro ko nɔ yɛ klɛŋklɛŋ afii oha lɛ mli Korinto asafo lɛ mli lɛ hu? ( 1 Kor. 5:1 ) Kɛ́ ohemɔkɛyeli lɛ damɔ bɔ ni mɛi krokomɛi anifeemɔi haa onuɔ he ohaa lɛ pɛ nɔ lɛ, belɛ ehemɔkɛyeli lɛ ema shishitoo ni hosoɔ nɔ. Ani shishitoo nɔ ni ma shi shiŋŋ fe enɛ yɛ kɛha ohemɔkɛyeli lɛ?

SHIŊTSƆƆMƆ NI HIƐ HIƐ KƐHA HEMƐKƐƐ

Obaanyɛ otswa shishitoo nɔ ni mli wa oma shi oha ohemɔkɛyeli lɛ kɛtsɔ nɔmimaa ni oyɔɔ yɛ shishijee anɔkwalei ni okase kɛjɛ Biblia lɛ mli lɛ ni oooha oná nɔmimaa ni mli kwɔ lɛ nɔ. Mɛɛ gbɛ nɔ no baanyɛ aye abua? Susumɔ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ ko he okwɛ.

Be ko ɔ, Yesu kaseli ɔmɛ fuu je e he. No mli lɛ, amɛtɔtɔ yɛ ewiemɔ ni kɔɔ eheloo lɛ yeli kɛ ela ni amɛnu lɛ he lɛ he. A de ke: “Wiemi nɛ ɔ ngɛ nyakpɛ.” Nɔ́ nɛ Yesu de mɛ ɔ hao mɛ wawɛɛ, nɛ a kpa e se nyɛɛmi.​— Yohane 6:56-60, 66 .

Lɔ ɔ se ɔ, Yesu bi e bɔfo ɔmɛ ke: “Anɛ nyɛ hu nyɛ suɔ kaa nyɛ maa ya lo?” Petro here nɔ akɛ: “Nuŋtsɔ, namɔ ŋɔɔ wɔɔya, bo oyɛ naanɔ wala wiemɔi lɛ, wɔhe wɔye, ni wɔle akɛ bo ji Nyɔŋmɔ Mɔ Krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ.”— Yohane 6:67-69 .

Mɛni ha nɛ e slo Petro kɛ bɔfo kpa amɛ ngɛ nihi nɛ a nane tɔ̃tɔ̃ ɔ a he ɔ? Amɛhemɔkɛyeli lɛ damɔ nɔ ko ni mli wa fe amɛhenumɔi anɔ. Petro kɛɛ akɛ: “Wɔhe wɔye, ni wɔle akɛ bo ji Nyɔŋmɔ Mɔ Krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ.” Anɔkwafoi nɛɛ le nɔ ni amɛheɔ amɛyeɔ kɛ nɔ hewɔ ni amɛheɔ amɛyeɔ lɛ. Akɛnɛ a ngɔ Yesu munyu ɔmɛ kɛ e ní peepee ɔmɛ kɛ to níhi nɛ a le ngɛ Ngmami ɔ mi ɔ he he je ɔ, a ná nɔ mi mami kaa Yesu ji Mesia a nɛ. Nɔmimaa ni mli wa ni amɛyɔɔ yɛ shishijee anɔkwale nɛɛ mli lɛ ha amɛhemɔkɛyeli lɛ ma shi shiŋŋ beni nɔ ni Yesu wie lɛ gba mɛi krokomɛi anane lɛ.

Mɛni nakai sane lɛ tsɔɔ wɔ? Nɔ ni wɔle lɛ baanyɛ aná hewalɛ fe bɔ ni wɔnuɔ he wɔhaa lɛ. Maaba? Ejaakɛ Biblia mli tsɔɔmɔi—ni anɔkwa saji ni wɔkase yɛ Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛi lɛ, eyiŋtoo, kɛ kpɔmɔnɔ lɛ he lɛ fata he lɛ—hiɔ shi be fɛɛ be. Nɔ ni tamɔɔɔ bɔ ni wɔnuɔ he wɔhaa lɛ, nɔ ni mɛi feɔ loo amɛwieɔ lɛ náaa Biblia mli anɔkwalei lɛ anɔ hewalɛ.

Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, ke wa ngɛ nɔ mi mami ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi anɔkuale ɔmɛ a he, nɛ waa ngɔ anɔkuale nɛ ɔmɛ kɛ tsa blɔ nɛ Yehowa ngɛ nɔ kɛ guɔ nɔ ɔ nɔ ɔ, wa ma nyɛ maa yu wa he ngɛ ni kpahi a ní peepeehi a he.

Ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ, wa nuɔ he kaa suɔmi nɛ ngɛ nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ a kpɛti ɔ woɔ wɔ he wami. Ni wɔnuɔ he akɛ wɔnanemɛi heyelilɔi lɛ ajeŋba kpakpa lɛ woɔ wɔ hewalɛ. Shi kɛji akɛ nakai henumɔi lɛ je shishi yɛ mɛi krokomɛi anifeemɔi ahewɔ lɛ, belɛ nɔmimaa ni wɔyɔɔ yɛ Biblia mli anɔkwalei lɛ amli lɛ baanyɛ aha wɔhemɔkɛyeli lɛ ama shi shiŋŋ. Ke wa peeɔ Baiblo ɔ kasemi daa a, lɔ ɔ haa nɛ nɔ mi mami nɛ wa ngɛ ngɛ anɔkualehi nɛ wa kase ngɛ Mawu Munyu ɔ mi ɔ mi waa. Nakai nɔmimaa lɛ hu baaye abua wɔ ni wɔya nɔ wɔná nɔmimaa akɛ wɔna jamɔ gbɛ kome pɛ ni Nyɔŋmɔ kpɛlɛɔ nɔ lɛ. Kɛkɛ ɔ, wa hemi kɛ yemi ɔ maa da sisi tomi nɛ mi wa nɔ, nɛ nihi a munyu tutui loo a ní peepee be hae nɛ wa hemi kɛ yemi ɔ nɛ wo wɔ.​— La 12:10 . 16:8; kwɛmɔ akrabatsa ni yitso ji “Hiɛ Gbɛ Kpakpa lɛ Nɔ.”

Hiɛ Gbɛ ni Ja lɛ Nɔ

Ŋɔɔ lɛ akɛ ota bɔs ko ni baafã gbɛ shitoi ohai abɔ kɛya he ko ni yɔɔ fɛo waa. Yɛ nakai gbɛfaa kakadaŋŋ lɛ mli lɛ, ekolɛ obaana henumɔi srɔtoi babaoo—miishɛɛ ni fe fɛɛ ji anɔkwale ni eji akɛ onyiɛ gbɛfaa lɛ mli lɛ.

Shi, beni oyɔɔ gbɛfaa nɛɛ mli lɛ, nibii komɛi baanyɛ aba ni baaha otsui anyɔ omli—ekolɛ sanegbaa ni naa wa ni gbɛfalɔi enyɔ komɛi miigba lɛ loo tsɔne kudɔlɔ lɛ nifeemɔ ni bɛ bulɛ po. Shi enɛɛmɛi ateŋ eko kwraa tsakeee anɔkwale ni eji akɛ oyɛ bɔs ni sa mli, ni ehe miihia ni ohi mli koni oyashɛ he ni oyaa lɛ.

Yɛ wɔ gbɛfãa kɛmiiya Nyɔŋmɔ jeŋ hee lɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaaná henumɔi srɔtoi—miishɛɛ ni wɔná yɛ hegbɛ ni wɔná akɛ wɔbafata Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ he lɛ he. Shi bei komɛi lɛ, wɔnaanyo heyelilɔ ko mli efu wɔ, loo wɔnaa kpɛ wɔhe po yɛ mɔ ko ni wɔbuɔ ehiɛ eshwieɔ enɔ lɛ efɔŋfeemɔ he. Shi enɛɛmɛi ateŋ eko kwraa tsakeee anɔkwale ni eji akɛ wɔyɛ bɔs ni sa lɛ mli lɛ, yɛ gbɛ ko nɔ lɛ. Wɔyɛ gbɛjianɔtoo ni ja mli. Kɛ wɔyɛ Biblia mli shishijee anɔkwalei lɛ ahe nilee ni mli kwɔ—ni wɔhiɛ sɔɔ he ni wɔkase nakai anɔkwalei lɛ kɛjɛ lɛ—wɔbaaya nɔ wɔnyiɛ gbɛ ni ja lɛ nɔ. Wɔhemɔkɛyeli lɛ baakã he ahi shi shiŋŋ.

Kɛ́ wɔhemɔkɛyeli lɛ damɔ bɔ ni mɛi krokomɛi anifeemɔi haa wɔnuɔ he wɔhaa lɛ pɛ nɔ lɛ, no lɛ edamɔɔ shishitoo ni hosoɔ nɔ .

NÁANII

Yehowa Woɔ Hemɔkɛyeli Nyɔmɔ

Emma tsuɔ nii yɛ hotɛl ko mli gbii etɛ yɛ otsi mli, ŋmɛlɛtswai nɛɛhu daa gbi. Ebi ni enitsumɔ nɔkwɛlɔ lɛ aha lɛ hejɔɔmɔ gbi kome koni enyɛ eya ekpokpaa wulu nɔ kpee lɛ. Yɛ nɔ najiaŋ ni nitsumɔ nɔkwɛlɔ lɛ baakpɛlɛ ebimɔ lɛ nɔ lɛ, etsake egbɛjianɔtoo lɛ ni efee gbii enumɔ yɛ otsi mli, ni ekɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔ ko ha lɛ akɛ: Kpɛlɛmɔ gbɛjianɔtoo hee lɛ nɔ loo shi nitsumɔ lɛ. Akɛni Emma kɛ ehe fɔ̃ɔ Yehowa nɔ hewɔ lɛ, ehala akɛ ebaakpa nitsumɔ lɛ koni eya kpee lɛ.

Beni Emma tee koni ekɛ enitsumɔ lɛ he wolo lɛ aha lɛ, ebalɛ akɛ hotɛl lɛ nɔkwɛlɔ nukpa lɛ yɛ jɛmɛ. Benɛ e nu si fɔfɔɛ ɔ he ɔ, e wo si kaa e ma tsu sane ɔ he ní. Shi, otsii enyɔ sɛɛ lɛ, akɛɛ Emma akɛ ashwie lɛ kɛjɛ nitsumɔ lɛ mli. Ekɛ nitsumɔ nɔkwɛlɔ nukpa lɛ kpe ekoŋŋ, ni emli fu akɛ aku hiɛ ashwie egbɛtsɔɔmɔi lɛ anɔ. Etsu sane lɛ he nii, ni aha Emma gbɛjianɔtoo hee: gbii enumɔ yɛ otsi mli, ŋmɛlɛtswai ejwɛ daa gbi. Ebafee nɔ ni hi fe eshishijee gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ po; ekɛ e-teokrase nitsumɔi lɛ tsu nii! Yehowa tsu ehiamɔ nii ahe nii yɛ gbɛ ni fe bɔ ni ekpaa gbɛ lɛ nɔ.
