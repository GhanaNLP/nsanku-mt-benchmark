WƆKA sɛ bere bi na mmanoma a wɔn maame awu na wagyaw wɔn (wɔn nkutoo) bom tra hɔ. Na wɔreyɛ afahyɛ bi, na mmanoma panyin (Ata ‘Panin) faa nhwiren a ɛsom bo ne sika kɔkɔɔ nneɛma nketenkete bi de sɛn mmanoma kumaa, Ata Kuma, kɔn mu. Nwansena kumaa no ne ne nnamfonom kaa sɛ wɔresian akɔ asubɔnten no mu akɔguare. Bere a wɔreguare no, hama a wɔde sika kɔkɔɔ nneɛma nketenkete no abɔ Ata Kuma kɔn ho no bubui, na agude no hwee nsu no mu. Bere biara a ɔbɛhwehwɛ mu no, wanhu wɔn. Ɔsan baa fie sɛ ɔrebɛbɔ ne nuabea amanneɛ, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Sika kɔkɔɔ nneɛma nketenkete no ayera." Ata 'Panin (twin panyin) kaa sɛ, "Me nnim saa sɛ wɔayera, na sɛ wɔyera mpo a, biribi wɔ hɔ a mɛka afi wo hɔ." Ata Kuma (twin kumaa no) kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na wobɛka afiri me hɔ?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mebɛgye asono nsateaa afiri wo hɔ, Aserewa anomaa ketewa no berebuw, Nsuo saa gur no." gles ponponsa, ahaban a ɛyɛ ne nkutoo (wɔ dua a wotetew fi so no so), ne Ɔhemmaa Asono Maame dua. Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, "Mate." Ata Kuma sii kwan so (sɛ ɔrekɔhwehwɛ saa nneɛma yi), na ɔbaa baabi pɔtee bi a na dua baako gyina hɔ. Bere a dua no huu no no, ɔkae sɛ, "Ata Kuma ni, (a nkurɔfo ka ne ho asɛm) ɔda ne ho adi wɔ kurow no akyi te sɛ osu a ɛtɔ a ɛma ahoɔden." Na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔhyiaa Aserewa anomaa bi. Na Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, “Ata Kuma nie, sɛ ɔpue wɔ kuro no akyi a, obiara ka sɛ ɔte sɛ osuo a ɛtɔ a ɛma ahoɔden.” Ata Kuma buae sɛ, “Adeɛ baako pɛ nti a maba ha ne sɛ agya ba yɛ Ata ’Panin baako, na ɛna ba yɛ Ata ’Panin baako, na ɔde asaawa kyekyeree nhwiren a ɛyɛ ahodoɔ na ɔde sɛn me ho, na ɛbubuu na ɛyeraeɛ.” (Na ha na Ata Kuma bubuu akɔ dwom mu):

Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, “Ɛno nti na wosu, bra bɛgye biribi.” Na Ata Kuma kɔgyee (Aserewa anomaa no berebuw), na ɔdaa no ase. Na ɔsiim, na ɔhyiaa Nsuo, na ɔrebɔ ponponponsa. Ɔkae sɛ, “Ata Kuma ni, a sɛ opue wɔ akuraa no ase abɔnten so a, nkurɔfo ma no akwaaba te sɛ osu a ɛtɔ a ɛma ahoɔden.” Ata Kuma kaa amanneɛbɔ a na waka akyerɛ afoforo no dedaw no kyerɛɛ no. Na Gurgling-water maa no (ne gurgling nsuo no bi), na ɔfaa no na ɔkɔɔ so. Afei ɔne Asono no hyiae. Bere a Asono huu no no, ɔkae sɛ, “Ata Kuma ni, sɛ opue wɔ akuraa no ase abɔnten so a, ɔte sɛ osu a ɛtɔ a ɛma ahoɔden.” Ata Kuma kaa n’adwuma kyerɛɛ no. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Mommra mmɛgye biribi, (na ɔde ne nsateaa baako maeɛ). Afei Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, “Agya Asono, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔyɛ na manya Ɔhemmaa Asono Ɛna no dua? Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ah, sɛ worekɔ no, mmoa no nyinaa bɛda, na Asono Maame Ɔhemmaa no bɛda mfimfini, na sɛ worekɔ a, wotiatia wɔn so pintinn, na sɛ woduru Asono Maame Ɔhemmaa no nkyɛn a, twa ne dua, nanso sɛ wotiatia wɔn so yie a, w ill kill you." Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, "Meda wo ase." Na bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu saa mmoa yi; ɔtiatia wɔn so denneennen, na ɔduruu Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no nkyɛn. Ɔtwaa ne dua, na ɔdanee n'akyi. Wɔ kwan so no ɔtɔɔ ahwehwɛ a wɔde hwɛ ade, na ɔde kɔɔ (fie). Na ɔduruu fie. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panin, nneɛma a wokaa sɛ wobɛgye afiri me nsam no nie." Ata 'Panin kaa se, "Me nsa aka won." Afei, da bi na wɔte faako, na Ata Kuma faa ahwehwɛ no hwɛɛ mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ahwehwɛ yi, sɛ obi hwɛ mu a, ohu ɛna a ɔwɔ Ahonhom asase so." Ata 'Panin kaa sɛ, "Ɛte saa, fa bra, na ma menhwɛ." Ɔde maa no. Bere a ɔrebɛhwɛ mu no, eguan fii ne nsam, na ɛbubui. Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, "Eyi deɛ, deɛ wo nsa kaa firii me hɔ no, mɛhwehwɛ afiri wo hɔ." Afei Ata 'Panin kɔfaa nneɛma a Ata Kuma de brɛɛ no ​​no. Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, "Mempɛ (eyinom), deɛ mepɛ ne sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa foforɔ abrɛ me." Ata 'Panin sii kwan so, na ɔkɔhyiaa dua bi a ahaban baako. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panin ni, a sɛ ɔda ne ho adi wɔ akuraa no ase abɔnten so a, wɔma no akwaaba te sɛ osu a ɛtɔ." Ata 'Panin buae se, "Ma 'mada adi' wo maame! ne w'agya!, sɛnea ɛte wɔ me fam no, ma me wo ahaban a ɛyɛ biako no na ma menkɔ." (Dua a ɛwɔ) ahaban a ɛyɛ ankonam no kae sɛ, "Twam, nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi) no wɔ hɔ." Ɔkɔɔ so, na ohyiaa Aserewa anomaa bi. Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panin ni; sɛ opue wɔ akuraa no ase abɔnten so a, ɔma ahoɔden te sɛ osu a ɛtɔ." Ata 'Panin kaa sɛ, "Sɛ me 'pue' wo maame ne w'agya, me deɛ, nea mepɛ ara ne wo berebuw no mu baako, na ma menkɔ." Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, "Twam, nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi) wɔ hɔ." Afei ohyiaa Gurgling-nsu. Gurgling-water kaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panin ni; sɛ ɔda ne ho adi wɔ kurow no awiei a, ɔte sɛ osu a ɛtɔ a ɛma ahoɔden." Ata 'Panin kaa sɛ, "Sɛ me 'pue' wo maame, sɛ me 'pue' wo papa a, me deɛ, ma me wo nsuo no bi na ma memfa nkɔ." Gurgling-water kaa sɛ, "Twam wɔ anim, nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi) wɔ hɔ." Afei ohyiaa Asono no. Asono no kaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panin ni, a sɛ ɔda ne ho adi wɔ abɔnten no awiei a, ɔma ahoɔden te sɛ osu a ɛtɔ." Ata 'Panin buae se, "Me 'pue' wo maame! ne wo papa! me deɛ, ma me wo nsateaa bi na ma mentwam." Asono no kaa sɛ, "Nea ɔnim (sɛdeɛ ɔne wo bedi) wɔ hɔ." Ɔsiim kwan, na ɔhyiaa Asono kuo bi, na Asono Maame Ɔhemmaa no da mfimfini. Afei ɔkɔɔ so twaa ne dua. Asono kuo no nyinaa kyeree no, na wɔtiatia no so, na ɔyɛɛ. Ata Kuma hwɛɛ ma ne nuabea a ɔwɔ kwan no so. Wamma. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Sɛ wo ne wo mogya mu bi te hɔ, na sɛ wɔn mu baako sɛe wo dea bi a, nnye akatua biara, ɛfiri sɛ Ata 'Panin na ɔyɛɛ saa, na ɔkɔɔ na ɔwuiɛ."
