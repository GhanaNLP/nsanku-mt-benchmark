Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

SƐ OBI SƐE BIBI A ƐYƐ WO DE (AKWANKYERƐ MU) A, MMFA AKATUA A WƆATUA HO

WƆKA sɛ bere bi na mmanoma a wɔn maame awu na wagyaw wɔn (wɔn nkutoo) bom tra hɔ. Na wɔreyɛ afahyɛ bi, na mmanoma panyin (Ata ‘Panin) faa nhwiren a ɛsom bo ne sika kɔkɔɔ nneɛma nketenkete bi de sɛn mmanoma kumaa Ata Kuma kɔn mu. Nwansena kumaa no ne ne nnamfonom kaa sɛ wɔresian akɔ asubɔnten no mu akɔguare. Bere a wɔreguare no, hama a wɔde sika kɔkɔɔ nneɛma nketenkete no abɔ Ata Kuma kɔn ho no bubui, na agude no hwee nsu no mu. Bere biara a ɔbɛhwehwɛ mu no, wanhu wɔn. Ɔsan baa fie sɛ ɔrebɛbɔ ne nuabea amanneɛ, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Sika kɔkɔɔ nneɛma nketenkete no ayera." Ata 'Panin (twin panyin) kaa sɛ, "Me nnim saa sɛ wɔayera, na sɛ wɔyera mpo a, biribi wɔ hɔ a mɛka afi wo hɔ." Ata Kuma (twin kumaa no) kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na wobɛka afiri me hɔ?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mebɛgye asono nsateaa, Aserewa anomaa ketewa no berebuw, Nsuo a ɛbɔ ponponponsa, ahaban a ɛyɛ ne nko ara (wɔ dua a wotetew no so), ne Ɔhemmaa Asono Maame dua. Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, "Mate. " Ata Kuma sii kwan so (sɛ ɔrekɔhwehwɛ saa nneɛma yi), na ɔbaa beaeɛ pɔtee bi a na dua baako gyina hɔ. Bere a dua no hunuu no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Ata Kuma nie, (a nkurɔfoɔ ka ne ho asɛm) ɔda ne ho adi wɔ kuro no akyi te sɛ osuo a ɛtɔ a ɛma ahoɔden." (Ata Kuma) kaa sɛ, "Adeɛ baako pɛ nti a maba ha ne sɛ agya ba ne Ata 'Panin baako, na ɛna ba yɛ Ata 'Panin baako, na ɔde nhama a ɛyɛ soronko sɛn me kɔn mu, na ɛbubuu na got lost." (Ɛha na Ata Kuma bubuu dwom mu):

"Ɔse ɔregye biribi afi me hɔ."

Ɔregye ahaban bi a efi dua bi mu, a ɛsow ahaban biako pɛ afi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔregye biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔregye ahaban bi afi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔregye biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrekyerɛ sɛ ɔyɛ Aserewa anomaa no berebuw.

Ɔka sɛ ɔregye biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔreka sɛ Nsu, gurgling ponponponsa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔregye biribi afi me hɔ. Ɔrekyerɛ sɛ ɔyɛ Paapa Asono nsateaa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔregye biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrekyerɛ sɛ ɔyɛ Ɔhemmaa Asono Ɛna dua, kosi ntini no so.

Na dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ, O agya, na manya bi?

Ɔse (ha na) abofra ketewa bi, Akwasi Toboro Kojo babaa.

Kojo Nsa.

Agya Asono, anka eye sɛ wubeyi wo ho, anka eye sɛ wobɛkɔ.

Kojo Nsa a a.”

Na Ahaban Baako-wɔ-dua so no kaa sɛ, “Na ɛno nti na woresu no, bra bɛgye biribi.” Na Ata Kuma kɔgyee (ahaban no firi dua bi a na ahaban baako pɛ wɔ so), na ɔdaa no ase (dua no). Na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔhyiaa Aserewa anomaa bi. Na Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, “Ata Kuma nie, sɛ ɔpue wɔ kuro no akyi a, obiara ka sɛ ɔte sɛ osuo a ɛtɔ a ɛma ahoɔden.” Ata Kuma buae sɛ, “Adeɛ baako pɛ nti a maba ha ne sɛ agya ba yɛ Ata ’Panin baako, na ɛna ba yɛ Ata ’Panin baako, na ɔde asaawa kyekyeree nhwiren a ɛyɛ ahodoɔ na ɔde sɛn me ho, na ɛbubuu na ɛyeraeɛ.” (Na ha na Ata Kuma bubuu dwom mu):

“Ɔka sɛ ɔregye biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔregye ahaban bi a efi dua bi mu, a ɛsow ahaban biako pɛ afi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔregye biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔregye ahaban bi afi me hɔ.

Ɔka sɛ ɔregye biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrekyerɛ sɛ ɔyɛ Aserewa anomaa no berebuw.

Ɔka sɛ ɔregye biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔreka sɛ Nsu, gurgling ponponponsa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔregye biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrekyerɛ sɛ ɔyɛ Paapa Asono nsateaa.

Ɔka sɛ ɔregye biribi afi me hɔ.

Ɔrekyerɛ sɛ ɔyɛ Ɔhemmaa Asono Ɛna dua, kosi ntini no so.

Na dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ, O agya, na manya bi?

Ɔse (ha na) abofra ketewa bi, Akwasi Toboro Kojo babaa.

Kojo Nsa.

Agya Asono, anka eye sɛ wubeyi wo ho, anka eye sɛ wobɛkɔ.

Kojo Nsa a a.”

Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, “Ɛno nti na wosu, bra bɛgye biribi.” Na Ata Kuma kɔgyee (Aserewa anomaa no berebuw), na ɔdaa no ase. Na ɔsiim, na ɔhyiaa Nsuo, na ɔrebɔ ponponponsa. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ata Kuma ni, a sɛ opue wɔ akuraa no ase abɔnten so a, nkurɔfo ma no akwaaba te sɛ osu a ɛtɔ a ɛma ahoɔden.” Ata Kuma kaa amanneɛbɔ a na waka akyerɛ afoforo no dedaw no kyerɛɛ no. Na Gurgling-water maa no (ne gurgling nsuo no bi), na ɔfaa no na ɔkɔɔ so. Afei ɔne Asono no hyiae. Bere a Asono huu no no, ɔkae sɛ, “Ata Kuma ni, sɛ opue wɔ akuraa no ase abɔnten so a, ɔte sɛ osu a ɛtɔ a ɛma ahoɔden.” Ata Kuma kaa n’adwuma kyerɛɛ no. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Bra bɛgye biribi, (na ɔde ne nsateaa baako maeɛ).Afei Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, “Agya Asono, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔyɛ na ama manya Asono Maame Ɔhemmaa no dua?’ Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ah, berɛ a worekɔ no, mmoa no nyinaa bɛda fam, na Asono Maame Ɔhemmaa no bɛda mfimfini, na sɛ worekɔ a, tread pintinn wɔ wɔn so, na sɛ woduru a Asono Maame Ɔhemmaa no, twa ne dua, nanso sɛ wotiatia wɔn so yie a, wɔbɛkum wo." Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, "Meda wo ase." Na berɛ a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu saa mmoa yi; ɔtiatia wɔn so denneennen, na ɔduruu Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no nkyɛn. Ɔtwaa ne dua, na ɔdanee n'akyi. Wɔ kwan so no ɔtɔɔ ahwehwɛ a wɔde hwɛ ade, na ɔfaa (fie). Na ɔduruu fie. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panin, nneɛma a wokae sɛ wobɛgye afi me nsam no nie." me hwɛ." Ɔde maa no. Bere a ɔrebɛhwɛ mu no, eguan fii ne nsam, na ɛbubui. Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, "Na eyi deɛ, nea wo nsa kaa firii me hɔ no, mɛhwehwɛ afiri wo hɔ." Afei Ata 'Panin kɔfaa nneɛma a Ata Kuma de brɛɛ no no. Ata Kuma kaa sɛ, "Mempɛ (eyinom), deɛ mepɛ ne sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa foforɔ aba me." Ata 'Panin siim, na kɔhyiaa dua bi a ahaban baako na ɛwɔ hɔ Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panin ni, sɛ ɔpue wɔ akuraa no ase abɔnten so a, wɔma no akwaaba te sɛ osuo a ɛtɔ gu fam no." na w'agya!, sɛnea ɛte wɔ me fam no, ma me wo ahaban biako na ma menkɔ." (Dua a ɛwɔ) ahaban a ɛyɛ ankonam no kae sɛ, "Twam, nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi) wɔ hɔ." Ɔkɔɔ so, na ohyiaa Aserewa anomaa bi. Aserewa anomaa no kae sɛ, "Ata 'Panin ni; sɛ ɔpue wɔ akuraa no abɔnten so awieeɛ a ɔma ahoɔden te sɛ osuo a ɛtɔ." Ata 'Panin kaa sɛ, "Sɛ me 'pue' wo maame ne w'agya, me deɛ, nea mepɛ ara ne wo berebuw no mu baako, na ma menkɔ." Aserewa anomaa no kaa sɛ, "Twam, nea ɔnim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi) wɔ hɔ." Afei ɔbaa Gurgling-nsuo. Gurgling-nsu kaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panin ni; sɛ opue wɔ kurow no awiei a, ɔte sɛ osu a ɛtɔ a ɛma ahoɔden." Ata 'Panin kaa sɛ, "Sɛ me 'pue' wo maame, sɛ me 'pue' wo papa a, me deɛ, ma me wo nsuo bi na ma mentwam." Gurgling-water kaa sɛ, "Twam wɔ anim, deɛ ɔnim (sɛnea ɔbɛdi wo ho dwuma) wɔ hɔ." Next she met the Leopard. The Leopard kaa sɛ, "Ata 'Panin ni, a sɛ ɔda ne ho adi wɔ abɔnten no awiei a, ɔma ahoɔden te sɛ osu a ɛtɔ gu fam no Ata 'Panin buae sɛ, "Me 'pue' wo maame! ne w’agya! me deɛ, ma me wo nsateaa bi na ma mentwam." Ɔsono no kaa sɛ, "Nea ɔnim (sɛdeɛ ɔne wo bedi) wɔ hɔ." Ɔsiim kwan, na ɔhyiaa Asono kuo bi, na Asono Maame Ɔhemmaa no da mfimfini. Afei ɔkɔɔ so twaa ne dua. Asono kuo no nyinaa kyeree no, na wɔtiatia no so, na ɔyɛɛ. Ata Kuma hwɛɛ ɛfiri sɛ ne nuabea a ɔwɔ kwan so no Wamma. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Sɛ wo ne wo mogya no mu bi te hɔ, na sɛ wɔn mu baako sɛe wo dea a, nnye akatua, ɛfiri sɛ Ata 'Panin na ɔyɛɛ saa, na ɔkɔɔ na ɔwuiɛ.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
