Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

KWAMENA, TIERED ANT-HILL, A ƐGYINA BUTRESS NHINI NO MU

WƆKA sɛ bere bi na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna bi babea wɔ hɔ, na ɛmfa ho nea wɔde no maa no wɔ aware mu no, ɔbɛka sɛ ɔmpɛ no. Nanso (awiei koraa no) Ahonhommɔne Hene, Adu Gyanfi biako, bae bɛwaree saa Ɔhemmaa Ɛna bi ba no. Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba ka kyerɛɛ ne kunu sɛ, "Me ne wo bɛkɔ wo kurom." Ɔbarima no buae sɛ, "Ma menkɔ (nea edi kan), na sɛ Adae no hwe Kwasida bi a, Memeneda a edi hɔ no mɛba abɛfa wo." Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ, "Oh dabi, me ne wo bɛbom akɔ." Enti wɔkɔe. Wɔrekɔ no, wɔhunuu kuro kɛseɛ bi a dadeɛ pon wɔ ne pon ano, na dadeɛ atwa kuro no ho ahyia. Afei, wɔ kuro yi mu no, sɛ wo mogya busuani biara anwu, (na) wogyinaa ɔpon no akyi na wofrɛɛ a, obiara annye wo so. Afei na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna yi ba no de, na ne busuani biara nwu da. Ɔkasa ne nea aka (i. e. me werɛ fii sɛ mɛka saa) bere a Adu Gyanfi duu dade pon no ho no, afei ɔkɔɔ ne fie; wankasa ankyerɛ Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no babea bio. Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no babaa ne n’akoa, Kra, kaa hɔ, na wɔgyinaa dadeɛ pon no akyi, na wɔannya kwan ankɔ fie hɔ. Na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba maa ne nne so (na ɔtoo dwom sɛ:

"Adodowa, bue ma me, ɛyɛ me na maba, Adodowa Kwasi Kampire, bue ma me, Adodowa, bue ma me o."

Nanso Adodowa anbue n’ano ankyerɛ no. Ɔno nso antumi anhu ɔkwan a ɔbɛfa so asan akɔ n’ankasa kurom. Afei ɔne Kra kɔɔ ntini abien a ɛyɛ den ntam. Na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna ba no faa kɔbere kyɛnsee bi tenaa so, na ɔfaa foforo kataa ne ti; na Kra tenaa ne nkyɛn. Ɛno nti na, sɛ wokɔ baabi a buttress ntini (gyina) a, ɛhɔ na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna ba ne n’afenaa Kra te; wɔadan akɔ ntɛtea nkoko a wɔfrɛ no "Kwamena" a ɛforo kɔ soro wɔ sorosoro.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
