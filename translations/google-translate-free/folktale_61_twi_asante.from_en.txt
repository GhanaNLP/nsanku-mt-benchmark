SƐNEA ƐBAE SƐ SƐ OBI KƆ DAN BI MU ANADWO A, ƐSUM

WƆKA sɛ Kuntukurunku, Odds, ne Kuntunamoa, Ends, fii ɔdan no atifi hwee fam. Wokaa se, "Asase-Maame-akora, wo (dodo) wo petee." Asase Maame kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woka sɛ meyɛ (dodoɔ) petee?" Wobuae se, "Efisɛ woantu tokuru amma yɛn nna mu." Asase ɛna dedaw danee ne ho kyerɛɛ Prae, Broom no, na ɔkae sɛ, "W'ano (wɔde) nnomaa nsu akata so." Obuae sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woyɛ me ayayade?" Asase ɛna dedaw kaa sɛ, "Sɛ mayɛ wo ayayade a, mayɛ wo ayayade; sɛ woante nea enti a ɛte saa ase a, gyae bisa." Broom no danee ne ho kyerɛɛ Abena, The-strip-of-bark (a wɔprapra nwura kɔ mu), na ɔkaa sɛ, "Wofa (nneɛma) na kɔ na wotow gu." Strip-of-bark kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woyɛ me ayayade?" Broom kaa sɛ, "(Anyhow) mayɛ saa." Strip-of-bark nso kasa kyerɛɛ Sumina, Midden-heap, sɛ, "Wo de nea wo nsa ka no sie." Midden-heap kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woreyɛ me ayayadeɛ?" Ɔno (Strip-of-bark) buae sɛ, "Mayɛ wo ayayade, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a, ɛnde gyae bisa." Midden-heap nso kasa kyerɛɛ Abe, Palm-dua no, sɛ, "Nwura ayɛ wo kɔn nyinaa ma." Palm-tree kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woyɛ me ayayade?" Midden-heap buae sɛ, "Mayɛ wo ayayade, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a, ɛnde gyae bisabisa." Palm-dua nso, danee ne ho kyerɛɛ Tetea, Little-black-ant, na ɔkaa sɛ, "W'aso yɛ tuntum." Little-black-ant kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na woyɛ me ayayade?" Palm-tree buae sɛ, "Mayɛ wo ayayade, na sɛ wunnim nea enti a, gyae bisa." Little-black-ant, ɔno nso, kɔɔ Esono, Asono a ɔwɔ hɔ no nkyɛn, na ɔkɔɔ ne nan so; na ɔde Tickled Asono no. Bere a ɔno (Asono) rema ne nan so no, afei ɔtiatia Tamiriwa aniwa ase pɛɛ, Black-snail no, na Black-snail no aniwa fii ase yɛɛ nsu, na ɔpopaa nsu no fii efan ahaban bi (a ɛrenyin) a ɛbɛn hɔ so. (Afei), aduane a Akwakoraa-nana di ne efan afifide, na ɛyɛ soro na Tamiriwa, Tuntum-nkraman no, apopa nsuo no afiri n’ani so. 'Kraman, Ɔkraman no, na ɔretwam, na ɔno (Akwakoraa-nana) frɛɛ Ɔkraman no na ɔfaa ogya nnua (na ɔsoa) no na ɔmaa Ɔkraman no soaa ne ti. Bere a Ɔkraman rekɔ no, onyaa kola-nut, na ɔpaapaee biako mu abien, na ɔtow biako guu Okagya, Ɔkraman, tokuru no ano. Okagya, ɔno nso, faa kola-nut no na ɔde bɔɔ Onyina, Silk-cotton-dua no. Silk-cotton-dua no nso, abufuw maa ne nufu memee. Ɔhunuu sɛ abofra ketewa bi retwam, na ɔtetew ne nkorabata na ɔbɔɔ no kum no. Afei, na saa abofra ketewa no yɛ Nyankonpon, Wim-nyame no ba. Ɛhɔ na afei wɔkyeree Silk-cotton-dua no, na wɔde no kɔɔ Wim-nyame no anim. Wobisaa no (Silk-cotton-dua no), se, "Ede ne nyinaa?" Silk-cotton-dua no kaa sɛ, "Na megyina hɔ wɔ m'ankasa me baabi, berɛ a saa Ɔkraman yi —akokoaa a ɔyɛ no —(onyaa akokoɔduro) maa kola-nut bi so na ɔde bɔɔ me, enti me deɛ, berɛ a abofra yi retwam no, mesusuu sɛ ɔyɛ Okagya, Ɔkraman no, na meyii me nkorabata no mu baako na mebɔɔ ​​no, na hwɛ, ɛyɛ wo ba. Nyame, Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na frɛ Okagya, Ɔkraman no sɛ ɔmmra." Okagya baa hɔ; ɔkaa sɛ, "Owura, wunim sɛ me taboo ne kola-nut, nanso Okraman, Ɔkraman no, kɔfaa bi, na ɔtow guu me tokuru no ano, na bere a na meretow agu no, ɛkɔbɔɔ Akwakoraa-silk-cotton-dua." Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Fa no, na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na frɛ Ɔkraman no sɛ ɔmmra." Wɔkɔfrɛɛ Ɔkraman no sɛ ɔmmra. Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Owura, na menam m'ankasa me mu wɔ baabi — afei wohyɛ sɛnea (delicate) meyɛ no nsow —bere a Akwakoraa-nana faa ne ogya nnua a wɔaboaboa ano no de too me tirim; afei Okagya, Ɔkraman no, ɛyɛ ɔno na ɔda ne tokuru mu wɔ afuw a Akwakoraa-nana ayɛ sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obenya biribi adi, nanso ɛyɛ me na meyɛ saa Akwakoraa-nanabea maa soaa ne nnua; Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Fa no, kyekyere no wɔ dua bi so, na frɛ Akwakoraa-nanabea sɛ ɔmmra." Akwakoraa-nanabea bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ah, me mmanana, monhwɛ m'anom, me a mintumi nwe nam, efan nhaban ne deɛ medi; bio nso, meguare Bosompra; me taboo ne (enti) Tamiriwa, Black-snail; Ɔkraman nko ara na na ɔnim yei, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ Tamiriwa, na ɔmaa no gyee nsuo firii n'anom, na ɔde guu efan nhaban a mete so no mu. Ɛne nea enti a mede me nnua a mede hyew no maa no sɛ ɔnsoa." Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Oh, fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na kɔ na fa Tamiriwa, Tuntum-nkraman no mmra." Black-snail no bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Owura, na mewɔ m'ankasa me ho wɔ baabi, hwɛ, Asono kɛseɛ kɛseɛ bi tiatiaa beaeɛ a ɛwɔ m'ani ase pɛɛ no ​​so, na mekɔɔ hɔ kɔfaa efan (ahaban) sɛ mɛpopa. Ɛno ara ne nea minim fa ho." Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na fa Esono, Asono no bra." Wɔkɔfaa Esono. Asono no kaa sɛ, "Bɛyɛ nnansa a atwam no na mete hɔ, na me honam ani fii ase yɛɛ me yaw, na bere a mehwɛɛ beae no —pa kyɛw wɔ asɛmfua no a mede dii dwuma no mu —na ɔkwasea-tunfo-ntɛtea yi akɔ (me honam ani) mu sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛhwehwɛ nam a ɔbɛwe, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obenyin kɛse; na bere a na meretwe me nan akɔ soro no, ɛkɔɔ tiatiaa Tamiriwa, Tuntum-ntɛtea no so. Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na fa Tetea, Little-black-ant no bra." Bere a Tetea bae no, ɔkae sɛ, "Owura, Palm-dua bi wɔ hɔ; sɛ ɛsow, (ne nnuaba) di kan yɛ tuntum; sɛ me, Little-black-ant, nantew wɔn so a, ɛma wɔbere. Da bi a na mewɔ hɔ, bere a Palm-dua no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ m'aso yɛ tuntum, na esiane sɛ meyɛ ketewaa nti, misusuwii sɛ mɛkɔ Asono no nkyɛn wɔ hɔ, akɔhwehwɛ me a medi a metumi." nyin kɛse.” Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, “Fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na kɔfrɛ Nnua no sɛ ɔmmra.” Abe, Palm-dua no, bae; ɔkaa sɛ, “Owura, nea ɛsɛ sɛ meka ni: Me ne Sumina, Midden-heap no, bom tena, na sɛ nwura a wɔtow gu ne so no dɔɔso dodo, na ontumi nnyina ano bio a, migye bi tom na mede to me kɔn mu; afei, nanso, ɔyɛɛ me ayayade, na ɔkae sɛ mapow wɔ me kɔn ho.Esiane ɛno nti, me deɛ, m’aniwa yɛɛ kɔkɔɔ, na me nso, meyɛɛ no ayayade Ketekete-tun-nwansena.” Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, “Fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na frɛ Sumina, Midden-heap no sɛ ɔmmra.” Mfinimfini-aboaboa ano bae; ɔkaa sɛ, “Owura, Abena, Strip-of-bark, a sɛ wɔprapra nwura a ɛwɔ dan bi mu a, wɔma no sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔno nso de bɛbrɛ me. Da bi, a na biribiara nni hɔ a ɛbɛma wafom no, ɔka kyerɛɛ me sɛ, ‘Wode nea wo nsa bɛka no sie’ (i. e. wɔyɛ adifudepɛfo); Mekaa sɛ, ‘a’, na bere a na Palm-dua no bɛn hɔ no, me nso, meyɛɛ no ​​ayayade.” Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, “Fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na frɛ Abena, Strip-of-bark, sɛ ɔmmra.” Strip-of-bark bae; ɔkaa sɛ, “Owura, sɛ Prae, Broom, popa dan bi mu a, sɛ wɔamfa (ne sweepings) amma me a, sɛ mframa bɔ a, nwura nyinaa san ba bio; afei, da bi no, ɛwom sɛ manyɛ no hwee de, nanso ɔkae sɛ, ‘Wofa nneɛma de tow gu nkutoo.’ Esiane ɛno nti, me nso m’ani yɛɛ kɔkɔɔ, na mekɔɔ Sumina, Midden-heap no ayayade.” Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, “Fa no, na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na frɛ Prae, Broom no, sɛ ɔmmra ha.” Broom no bae; ɔkaa sɛ, “Owura, Ɛna-Asaase-Akwakoraa da hɔ petee saa, n’anim yɛ fĩ, enti bere biara a mɛsɔre no, meprapra efĩ nyinaa gu ne so na mema ne ho yɛ fɛ; nanso da bi no, Asase Maame kasa kyerɛɛ me sɛ, ‘W’anom (ahyɛ) nnomaa nsu,’ na me nufu nso, memee, na mekɔɔ hɔ kɔyɛɛ Abena, Strip-of-bark no ayayade.” Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, “Fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na frɛ Ɛna-asase sɛ ɔmmra.” Bere a Ɛna-asase bae no, ɔkae sɛ, “Owura, ɛhɔ na meda; wɔtiatia me so; Menka hwee da; wɔyɛ nneɛma ahorow nyinaa wɔ me so, nanso menka hwee da. Afei da bi a na mewɔ hɔ no, Kuntukurunku ne Kuntunamoa, Odds ne Ends, hwee me so, na mekae sɛ, ‘O! asɛm no ne dɛn?’ na wɔkae sɛ, ‘Wantutu tokuru a yebetumi ada mu, na wo ne wo tratraa nyinaa nyɛ mfaso biara (mma obiara).’ Esiane ɛno nti, me nso, meyɛɛ Prae, Broom no ayayade.” Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, “Fa no na fa no kyekyere dua bi ho, na kɔ na kɔgye Odds and Ends.” Odds ne Ends bae. Wɔkaa sɛ, “Owura, na yɛwɔ ɔdan atifi, na yɛredi agorɔ, na yɛhwee fam na yɛpiraa yɛn ho; ɛno nti yɛyɛɛ Ɛna-asase ayayade, na yɛkae sɛ ɔyɛ tratraa.” Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, “Agyinatufoɔ, yɛate asɛm yi nyinaa, enti momma yɛnsɔre na yɛnkɔ dan no mu na yɛnsusu adanseɛ no ho na yɛmmra mmɛbu atɛn.” Enti wɔsɔre kɔɔ dan bi mu, na wɔtoo ɔpon no mu. Ɛno nti na, sɛ wokɔ dan bi mu anadwo a, —tuntum —Wim-nyame no ne n’Agyinatufo no resusuw ho wɔ hɔ.
