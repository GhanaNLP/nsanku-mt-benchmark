Jeŋ Muu Fɛɛ Adesai Ahegbɛi Ahe Jajemɔ

Hiɛkpamɔ

Yɛ be mli ni bulɛ ni jɛ fɔmɔ mli kɛ hegbɛi ni yeɔ egbɔ ni anyɛɛɛ ajie kɛjɛ adesai aweku lɛ mli lɛ ji heyeli, jalɛsaneyeli kɛ toiŋjɔlɛ shishitoo nɔ yɛ je lɛ mli lɛ,

Yɛ be mli ni bulɛ ni abuuu kɛ adesai ahegbɛi ni abuuu lɛ kɛ yiwalɛ nifeemɔi ni ewo adesai ahenilee mli la eba, ni jeŋ ni adesai baaná heyeli kɛ hemɔkɛyeli kɛ heyeli kɛjɛ gbeyeishemɔ kɛ hiamɔ nii amli lɛ baa lɛ ji maŋbii lɛ ashwelɛ ni nɔ kwɔ fe fɛɛ.

Akɛni ehe hiaa waa akɛ, kɛji akɛ anyɛŋ gbɔmɔ nɔ ni ekɛ ehe awo atuatsemɔ ni teɔ shi ewoɔ yiwalɛ kɛ nɔnyɛɛ mli lɛ, akɛ mla abu adesai ahegbɛi ahe,

Yɛ be mli ni ehe hiaa ni atswa naanyobɔɔ wekukpaa ni yɔɔ maji ateŋ lɛ ama shi lɛ, .

Akɛni maŋbii ni yɔɔ Jeŋmaji Ekomefeemɔ lɛ mli lɛ ema amɛhemɔkɛyeli yɛ adesai ahegbɛi ni he hiaa waa lɛ amli, yɛ adesa bulɛ kɛ sɛɛnamɔ ni yɔɔ hii kɛ yei fɛɛ ahe lɛ mli, ni amɛkpɛ amɛyiŋ akɛ amɛbaawo shihilɛ mli hiɛyaa kɛ shihilɛ shishitoo mlai kpakpai ahe hewalɛ yɛ heyeli ni da mli lɛ mli.

Yɛ be mli ni maji ni yɔɔ mli lɛ ewo amɛhe shi akɛ amɛkɛ Jeŋmaji Ekomefeemɔ lɛ baatsu nii, ni amɛha jeŋ muu fɛɛ bulɛ kɛ adesai ahegbɛi kɛ heyeli ni he hiaa waa lɛ nɔyeli aya hiɛ lɛ,

Yɛ be mli ni hegbɛi kɛ heyelii nɛɛ ahe shishinumɔ kome he hiaa fe fɛɛ kɛha shiwoo nɛɛ mlibaa lɛ,

No hewɔ lɛ, amrɔ nɛɛ, .

Kpee Kpeteŋkpele lɛ

Ejajeɔ Jeŋ Muu Fɛɛ Adesai Ahegbɛi Ahe Jajemɔ nɛɛ akɛ nɔ ni gbɔmɛi fɛɛ kɛ maji fɛɛ baanyɛ ashɛ he, koni mɔ fɛɛ mɔ kɛ maŋ lɛ mli nibii fɛɛ, kɛ Jajemɔ nɛɛ ahi ejwɛŋmɔ mli daa lɛ, baabɔ mɔdɛŋ kɛtsɔ tsɔɔmɔ kɛ tsɔsemɔ nɔ koni ekɛ bulɛ aha hegbɛi kɛ heyelii nɛɛ aya hiɛ, kɛ jeŋ muu fɛɛ gbɛjianɔtoo kɛ amɛ hiɛyaa. kpɛlɛmɔ kɛ eyeli, yɛ Maji ni fata he lɛ diɛŋtsɛ kɛ shikpɔŋkukuji ni yɔɔ amɛ nɔyeli shishi lɛ amli bii lɛ ateŋ.

Niŋmaa 1

Afɔɔ adesai fɛɛ ni amɛye amɛhe ni amɛyeɔ egbɔ yɛ bulɛ kɛ hegbɛi amli. Akɛ jwɛŋmɔ kɛ henilee eha amɛ ni esa akɛ amɛfee amɛnii amɛha amɛhe yɛ nyɛmifeemɔ mumɔ mli.

Niŋmaa 2

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛi kɛ heyeli fɛɛ ni yɔɔ Jajemɔ nɛɛ mli, ni srɔtofeemɔ ko bɛ he, tamɔ hewolonɔ su, hewolonɔ su, bɔlɛnamɔ, wiemɔ, jamɔ, maŋkwramɔ loo susumɔ kroko, maŋ nɔ ni ejɛ, nibii, fɔmɔ loo gbɛhe kroko.

Agbɛnɛ hu, srɔtofeemɔ ko bɛ maŋ loo shikpɔŋkuku ni mɔ ko yɔɔ mli lɛ mli, kɛji eji maŋ ni yɔɔ eheyeli, hekɛnɔfɔɔ, nɔyeli ni yeee ehe nɔ loo nɔyeli he husu kroko shishi.

Niŋmaa 3

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ehiɔ shi, eyeɔ ehe, ni ehiɔ shi shweshweeshwe.

Niŋmaa 4

Akɛ mɔ ko mɔ ko akaye nyɔŋ loo nyɔŋ; agu nyɔŋyeli kɛ nyɔŋyeli yɛ gbɛ fɛɛ gbɛ nɔ.

Niŋmaa 5

Esaaa akɛ awaa mɔ ko mɔ ko yiwalɛ, aloo agbeɔ lɛ yiwalɛ, ni esaaa adesa su, loo agbeɔ mɔ ko he guɔ.

Niŋmaa 6

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ akpɛlɛɔ enɔ yɛ he fɛɛ he akɛ gbɔmɔ yɛ mla hiɛ.

Niŋmaa 7

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yeɔ egbɔ yɛ mla hiɛ, ni mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ebuɔ ehe yɛ mla naa, ni hiɛaŋkwɛmɔ ko bɛ he. Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ abuɔ ehe kɛjɛɔ mɛi ahe jwɛŋmɔ fɔŋ ni ajieɔ lɛ kpo yɛ Jajemɔ nɛɛ mli, kɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni kanyaa mɛi ahe jwɛŋmɔ fɔŋ ni ajieɔ lɛ kpo lɛ he.

Niŋmaa 8

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ maŋ kojomɔhei lɛ baatsu he nii kɛji efee nɔ ko ni teɔ shi ewoɔ hegbɛi ni maŋ mla loo mla kɛha lɛ lɛ.

Niŋmaa 9

Esaaa akɛ amɔmɔɔ mɔ ko mɔ ko, awoɔ lɛ tsuŋ loo ajieɔ lɛ kɛjɛɔ maŋsɛɛ.

Niŋmaa 10

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ kojomɔhe ko ni yɔɔ eheyeli ni kwɛɛɛ hiɛaŋ lɛ aboɔ saneyeli ni ja yɛ maŋ hiɛ, yɛ hegbɛi kɛ gbɛnaa nii ni kɔɔ efɔŋfeemɔ he lɛ mli.

Niŋmaa 11

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni afolɔ enaa yɛ efɔŋ ko he lɛ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ abuɔ lɛ akɛ efeko efɔŋ kɛyashi be mli ni abaatsɔɔ akɛ eye fɔ yɛ maŋ kojomɔ mli ni ena nɔmimaa fɛɛ ni he hiaa kɛha ehefamɔ.

Mɔ ko mɔ ko akaye efɔŋ yɛ nifeemɔ loo nifeemɔ ko ni efee ni efeee efɔŋ yɛ maŋ loo majimaji ateŋ mla naa, yɛ be mli ni efee lɛ hewɔ. Ni asaŋ akɛ toigbalamɔ ni tsii fe nɔ ni akɛtsu nii yɛ be mli ni afee efɔŋ lɛ.

Niŋmaa 12

Mɔ ko mɔ ko teemɔŋ saji, eweku, shia loo woji ni aŋmalaa kɛŋmalaa woji amli, ni akawo ehiɛ nyam kɛ gbɛi kpakpa mli. Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ mla abuɔ ehe kɛjɛɔ saji ni akɛwoɔ mɔ mli loo tutuamɔi ni tamɔ nɛkɛ he.

Niŋmaa 13

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ efãa gbɛ ni esumɔɔ ni ehi shi yɛ maŋ fɛɛ maŋ husui amli.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ eshiɔ maŋ fɛɛ maŋ, kɛ lɛ diɛŋtsɛ emaŋ fata he, ni ekuɔ esɛɛ kɛyaa emaŋ.

Niŋmaa 14

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ etaoɔ abobaahe yɛ maŋ kroko nɔ kɛjɛ yiwaa mli.

Akɛ hegbɛ nɛɛ tsuuu nii kɛji akɛ kojomɔ ni jɛ efɔŋfeemɔi ni jeee maŋkwramɔŋ saji amli loo nifeemɔi ni teɔ shi woɔ Jeŋmaji Ekomefeemɔ lɛ yiŋtoi kɛ shishitoo mlai lɛ mli ba diɛŋtsɛ.

Niŋmaa 15

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ejɛɔ maŋ nɔ.

Mɔ ko mɔ ko bɛ hegbɛ akɛ aaaŋmɛɛ maŋ nɔ ni ejɛ lɛ he yɛ gbɛ ni ejaaa nɔ.

Niŋmaa 16

Hii kɛ yei ni amɛye afii ni amɛye lɛ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ amɛboteɔ gbalashihilɛ mli ni amɛtoɔ weku. Amɛyɛ hegbɛi ni tamɔ amɛhe yɛ gbalashihilɛ mli, yɛ gbalashihilɛ be mli kɛ be mli ni afoɔ mli.

Abaanyɛ abote gbalashihilɛ mli kɛji hefatalɔi ni miisumɔ ni amɛbote gbalashihilɛ mli lɛ kpɛlɛ nɔ jogbaŋŋ.

Weku lɛ ji adebɔɔ su kɛ kuu ni yɔɔ maŋ lɛ mli, ni maŋ kɛ maŋ lɛ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ amɛbuɔ amɛhe.

Niŋmaa 17

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ lɛ diɛŋtsɛ enáa nɔ ko loo ekɛ mɛi krokomɛi feɔ ekome.

Mɔ ko mɔ ko akashɔ̃ enii yɛ edɛŋ yɛ gbɛ ni ejaaa nɔ.

Niŋmaa 18

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ esusuɔ nii ahe, ehenilee kɛ ejamɔ he heyeli; nɔ ni fata hegbɛ nɛɛ he ji heyeli ni eyɔɔ ni ekɛtsakeɔ ejamɔ loo ehemɔkɛyeli, kɛ heyeli, ekome too loo yɛ kuu mli kɛ mɛi krokomɛi kɛ maŋ loo teemɔŋ, ni ejie ehemɔkɛyeli loo ehemɔkɛyeli kpo yɛ tsɔɔmɔ, nifeemɔ, jamɔ kɛ niyeli mli.

Niŋmaa 19

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ejieɔ esusumɔi akpo; hegbɛ nɛɛ fata he ni mɔ ko kɛ ehe wooo mli, ni etao, enine ashɛ ni ekɛ saji kɛ susumɔi aha kɛtsɔ adafitswaa woji fɛɛ nɔ, ni ekɔɔɔ husui ahe.

Niŋmaa 20

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ekɛ ehe woɔ henaabuamɔi kɛ kuu mli yɛ toiŋjɔlɛ mli.

Anyɛŋ mɔ ko nɔ ni ekɛ ehe awo kuu ko mli.

Niŋmaa 21

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ekɛ ehe woɔ maŋ nɔyeli mli, tɛ̃ɛ loo kɛtsɔ najiaŋdamɔlɔi ni ehala lɛ anɔ.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ etsuɔ ​​maŋ nitsumɔ yɛ maŋ ni eyɔɔ mli lɛ mli.

Maŋbii lɛ asuɔmɔnaa nii ji nɔ ni damɔ nɔyeli hegbɛ nɔ; abaajie suɔmɔnaa nii nɛɛ kpo yɛ oshikifɔɔ ni afeɔ yɛ be kɛ bei amli ni ji anɔkwale ni mɛi fɛɛ baanyɛ ahala yɛ teemɔŋ oshikifɔɔ mli loo yɛ oshikifɔɔ gbɛjianɔtoo ni tamɔ nakai ni yeɔ ehe nɔ lɛ mli.

Niŋmaa 22

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ, akɛ akutso lɛ mlinyo, yɛ hegbɛ akɛ enáa shweshweeshwe shihilɛ, kɛtsɔ maŋ mɔdɛŋbɔɔ kɛ majimaji ateŋ nitsumɔ kɛ maŋ fɛɛ maŋ gbɛjianɔtoo kɛ nibii anɔ, shika gbɛfaŋ, shihilɛ kɛ kusum hegbɛi ni he hiaa waa kɛha bulɛ kɛ egbɔmɔtso shweremɔ ni yɔɔ heyeli mli.

Niŋmaa 23

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ etsuɔ ​​nii, ehalaa nitsumɔ ni esumɔɔ, yɛ nitsumɔ shihilɛi ni ja ni hi, kɛ hebuu kɛjɛ nitsumɔ ni anaaa lɛ he.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ awoɔ lɛ nyɔmɔ yɛ nitsumɔ kome mli, ni hiɛaŋkwɛmɔ ko bɛ he.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni tsuɔ nii lɛ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ena nyɔmɔwoo ni sa ni baaha lɛ diɛŋtsɛ kɛ eweku lɛ aná shihilɛ ni sa adesa bulɛ, ni kɛ ehe miihia lɛ, akɛ gbɛi krokomɛi ni baabu lɛ afata he.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ etoɔ nitsulɔi akui ni ekɛ ehe woɔ mli koni ekɛbu ehe.

Niŋmaa 24

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ enaa hejɔɔmɔ kɛ hejɔɔmɔ, ni nitsumɔ be ni sa kɛ gbijurɔyelii ni awoɔ lɛ fata he.

Niŋmaa 25

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ehiɔ shi yɛ gbɛ ni sa nɔ kɛha lɛ diɛŋtsɛ kɛ eweku hewalɛnamɔ, ni niyenii, atade, shĩa kɛ tsofafeemɔ kɛ gbɔmɛi ashihilɛ mli nitsumɔi ni he hiaa fata he, kɛ hegbɛ akɛ ena shweshweeshwe shihilɛ kɛji ebɛ nitsumɔ, hela, gbɔlɛ, okulafoyeli, onukpayeli loo shihilɛ kroko ni enyɛɛɛ ekwɛ nɔ.

Nyɛmɛi kɛ gbekɛbii yɛ hegbɛ akɛ amɛnaa kwɛmɔ kɛ yelikɛbuamɔ krɛdɛɛ. Gbekɛbii fɛɛ, kɛji afɔ amɛ yɛ gbalashihilɛ mli jio, afɔ amɛ yɛ gbalashihilɛ sɛɛ jio, esa akɛ amɛná shihilɛ mli hebuu kome.

Niŋmaa 26

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ etsɔɔ nii. Tsɔsemɔ afee nɔ ni aheee nɔ ko, kɛ hoo lɛ yɛ shishijee kɛ shishijee fãi amli. Shishijee skul nikasemɔ baafee nɔ ni esa akɛ afee. Abaaha ŋaa kɛ nitsumɔ he nikasemɔ aya hiɛ ni mɛi fɛɛ baanyɛ aná nikasemɔ ni nɔ kwɔ yɛ gbɛ ni sa nɔ.

Esa akɛ tsɔsemɔ aha adesa gbɔmɔtso lɛ aya hiɛ jogbaŋŋ ni ewaje bulɛ ni ayɔɔ aha adesai ahegbɛi kɛ heyelii ni he hiaa waa lɛ. Ebaaha shishinumɔ, tsuishitoo kɛ naanyobɔɔ aya hiɛ yɛ jeŋmaji fɛɛ, hewolonɔ su loo jamɔ kui fɛɛ ateŋ, ni ebaaha Jeŋmaji Ekomefeemɔ lɛ nitsumɔi aya hiɛ koni toiŋjɔlɛ ahi shi.

Fɔlɔi yɛ hegbɛ akɛ amɛhalaa tsɔsemɔ ni esa akɛ akɛha amɛbii.

Niŋmaa 27

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ekɛ ehe woɔ akutso lɛ kusum shihilɛ mli, enaa nitɛŋmɔ nibii ahe miishɛɛ, ni enaa jeŋ nilee hiɛyaa kɛ sɛɛnamɔi ni jɛɔ mli baa lɛ mli gbɛfaŋnɔ.

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ebuɔ jeŋ nilee, niŋmaa loo nitɛŋmɔ nibii ni lɛ diɛŋtsɛ efee lɛ ahe.

Niŋmaa 28

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ hegbɛ akɛ ehiɔ shi yɛ maŋ kɛ majimaji ateŋ gbɛjianɔtoo ko ni baanyɛ aha atsu hegbɛi kɛ heyeli ni yɔɔ Jajemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ he nii jogbaŋŋ.

Niŋmaa 29

Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɛ gbɛnaa nii yɛ akutso mli, ni he pɛ ni enyɛɔ enáa esubaŋ shweremɔ ni yeɔ emuu.

Yɛ hegbɛi kɛ heyeli ni mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɔɔ lɛ mli lɛ, esa akɛ ekɛ ehe awo husui ni mla tsɔɔ lɛ pɛ mli, koni aná bulɛ ni sa aha mɛi krokomɛi ahegbɛi kɛ heyelii, kɛ jeŋba kpakpa, maŋ gbɛjianɔtoo kɛ shweremɔ ni yɔɔ demokrase maŋ mli.

Anyɛŋ akɛ hegbɛi kɛ heyelii nɛɛ atsu nii yɛ gbɛ ko nɔ ni teɔ shi ewoɔ Jeŋmaji Ekomefeemɔ lɛ yiŋtoi kɛ shishitoo mlai.

Niŋmaa 30

Anyɛŋ atsɔɔ nɔ ko nɔ ko shishi yɛ Jajemɔ nɛɛ mli akɛ etsɔɔ maŋ ko, kuu ko loo gbɔmɔ ko hegbɛ akɛ ekɛ ehe aaawo nifeemɔ ko mli loo efee nɔ ko ni yiŋtoo ji ni efite hegbɛi kɛ heyelii ni atsɔɔ yɛ biɛ lɛ eko.
