5 | Ghana nɔyeli - 2022 maŋbii ashika he gbɛjianɔtoo

“Nitsumɔhe Maŋ ni Hiɔ Shi Daa: Shika Helɛtemɔ kɛ Nitsumɔi Afeemɔ”

HIƐKƐKƐMƆ

COVID-19 tsɛŋemɔ hela lɛ he naagbai ni yeɔ mɔ awui lɛ enu he yɛ shika helɛtemɔ hei fɛɛ, ni Ghana fata he. Naagba ni Ghanabii kɛkpeɔ kɛtsɔ wala, shihilɛ mli nibii kɛ jarayeli nitsumɔi ni amɛfiteɔ lɛ nɔ lɛ elaajeee Nɔyeli lɛ nɔ. Mikɛ henɔwomɔ kɛɛ akɛ wɔ hetoo ni wɔha koni wɔtsu hela ni gbɛ eshwa lɛ he nii lɛ ji ekãa kɛ yiŋkpɛɛ ni ajie eyi yɛ je lɛŋ he fɛɛ he. Nɔyeli lɛ hu je mlai ni baaye abua ni atswa jarayeli nitsumɔi ama shi ekoŋŋ ni eha wɔshihilɛ aku sɛɛ aba bɔ ni eyɔɔ lɛ shishi.

Agbɛnɛ hu, yɛ gbeyeishemɔi kɛ hegbɛi ni jɛɔ hela ni gbɛ eshwa lɛ mli baa lɛ hetoohamɔ mli lɛ, Nɔyeli lɛ, yɛ H.E. Nɔyelɔ Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo, fee oya ni ekɛ GH¢100 akpekpei toi akpei 100 COVID-19 Hewalɛwoo kɛ Nitsumɔhei Amli Hewalɛwoo gbɛjianɔtoo, (GhanaCARES “Obaatan Pa” gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ). Enɛ kɛ hejɔɔmɔ ni sa kadimɔ eba wɔmaŋbii lɛ anɔ amrɔ nɔŋŋ ni ekɛ hewalɛ miiwo wɔmaŋ lɛ mli ekoŋŋ.

Wɔhiɛ sɔɔ hemɔkɛyeli ni okɛ Nɔyeli lɛ hiɛɔ mli lɛ po yɛ be mli ni wɔtsuɔ nii koni wɔkwɛ koni hela ni gbɛɔ eshwaa mɛi lɛ aya hiɛ waa lɛ. Yɛ be nɛɛ mli lɛ, aka wɔshifimɔ ni wɔkɛaahi shi akɛ maŋ lɛ akwɛ. Biblia lɛ kɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔ ni he hiaa lɛ haa yɛ bei ni tamɔ nɛkɛ mli be fɛɛ be. Titri lɛ, taakɛ awie yɛ Yesaia 9:10 lɛ, “Brikii lɛ egbee shi, shi wɔkɛ tɛi ni asaa ama, afo sikamore tsei lɛ, shi wɔkɛ tseneduru tsei baawo amɛ najiaŋ”. Yɛ nakai gbɛ nɔ nɔŋŋ nɔ lɛ, Nɔyeli lɛ hu etswa efai shi nakai nɔŋŋ akɛ ebaatsu eshiwoi lɛ ahe nii. Yɛ yiŋtoo nɛɛ hewɔ lɛ, wɔbaatsa nɔ wɔkɛ mlai kɛ gbɛjianɔtoi ni kɔɔ shikpɔji fɛɛ ni elaaje lɛ aku sɛɛ aba ni aha maŋbii lɛ ashihilɛ aya hiɛ lɛ atsu nii.

Otii titri kome ni yɔɔ 2022 Shika Helɛtemɔ He lɛ mli, kɛ anɔkwale mli lɛ, kɛha afii etɛ (3) ni baa lɛ, ji ni ama Nitsumɔhe Maŋ ni baanyɛ ahi shi daa ni baanyɛ aha shika helɛtemɔ mli dalɛ ni yɔɔ hewalɛ, ni yaa nɔ daa, ni mɛi fɛɛ fata he lɛ aba koni ekɛ nitsumɔ ni sa aba aha mɛi fɛɛ, titri lɛ Oblahii kɛ Oblayei. No hewɔ lɛ, nɔyeli mlai lɛ baafee nɔ ni akɛyeɔ abuaa oblahii kɛ oblayei koni amɛkɛ amɛhenumɔi atsu nii ni amɛjie amɛnyɛmɔi lɛ kpo koni amɛkwɛ koni shweremɔ aba kɛtsɔ hegbɛ ni tamɔ amɛhe ni mɛi fɛɛ yɔɔ lɛ nɔ.

Bɔni afee ni oblahii kɛ oblayei lɛ ahekɛnɔfɔɔ lɛ aya hiɛ ni amɛtswa amɛ nitsumɔ he mumɔ lɛ ema shi lɛ, Nɔyeli lɛ baatsu YouStart gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ he nii yɛ afi 2022 mli koni ekɛ shika aha oblahii kɛ oblayei ni tsuɔ nii yɛ jarayeli nitsumɔ mli lɛ koni amɛto amɛ nitsumɔ lɛ shishi loo amɛgbɛ amɛshwã.

Yɛ afi 2022 Shika Helɛtemɔ lɛ mli lɛ, wɔtsɔɔ gbɛjianɔi ni ato koni wɔya nɔ wɔtsu shika helɛtemɔ mli naagbai ni baa amrɔ nɔŋŋ lɛ ahe nii ni wɔje kpo kɛ hewalɛ yɛ hela ni gbɛ eshwa lɛ sɛɛ be lɛ mli. Nɔyeli lɛ kɛ ehe ewo nifeemɔi ni mli kwɔ ni ato he gbɛjianɔ koni ekɛ ŋaalee aha ni ekɛ nitsumɔ he nitsumɔ aya hiɛ yɛ oblahii kɛ oblayei ateŋ. Beni wɔtiuɔ Nɔyeli lɛ sɛɛ lɛ
