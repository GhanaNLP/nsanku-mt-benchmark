Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

SƐNEA TALE-BEARING BAA ABUSUE NO MU

WƆKA sɛ ɔbɔmmɔfo bi (bere bi) kɔɔ wuram; ɔbaa hɔ bɛhwɛɛ amoa bi mu, na na ’Nipa, Ɔbarima, Osebo, Ɔsebɔ, Owo, Ɔwɔ, ne ’Kusie, Ɔkraman gyina hɔ. Afei berɛ a saa nnipa yi hunuu Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no, oyi kaa sɛ, “Agya Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ, bra bɛyi me afiri adi.” Saa baako no kaa sɛ, “Agya, bra bɛyi me afiri adi.” Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Meremma, ɛfiri sɛ ɛbɛduru ɔkyena mpo, sɛ mɛgye wo, na wo nso wobɛduru me so a, deɛ wobɛyɛ me (minim) ɛnsɛ sɛ medwene ho.” Oyi kaa sɛ, “Mantumi (anyɛ saa).” Saa baako no kaa sɛ, “Mantumi (anyɛ saa).” Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Sɛ ɛte saa ampa a, mɛyi wo afiri adi.” Na nokwarem no, oyii nnipa no nyinaa fii adi na ɔkɔ (ne) akuraa ase. Da bi, bere a Ɔbɔmmɔfo no wɔ hɔ no, Osebo, Asono, de nam pii bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Agya Hunter, yei yɛ aseda afɔrebɔ wɔ adɔeɛ a woyɛɛ me no ho, meda wo ase, Apiafo.” Ɔde nam nketewa dumien a wɔde mmɛ ahaban ayɛ brɛɛ Ɔbɔmmɔfo no. Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no daa no ase na Asono no kɔeɛ. Bere foforo nso, na Ɔbɔmmɔfo no wɔ hɔ, na Okusie, Ɔkraman no bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Agya Hunter, wobɛpɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfuturo bi?” Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Ho!, adɛn nti na ɛnte saa, ɛno ne adeɛ a mmarima hwehwɛ a wɔbrɛ.” Ɔkraman no sii kwan so, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a Nyankonpon, Wim nyame no, de ne sika mfuturo sie. Ɔkɔtuu tokuru wɔ dan no ase, na ɔhyɛnee dan no mu, na ɔkɔfaa Wim-nyame no sika mfuturo nyinaa, na ɔde brɛɛ Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no. Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Meda wo ase, Aku.” Na Ɔkraman no kɔe. Da foforo nso, na ɔwɔ hɔ, na ɔtee sɛ obi kyia no sɛ, “Ago.” Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no buaa sɛ, “Ame” (bra mu). Ɔbarima no bae; ɔkaa sɛ, “Agya Hunter, adekyee pa.” Ɔno (Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ) buaa sɛ, “Yiw, obusuani.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Eyi yɛ kuruwa ketewa bi a wɔde mmɛw nsa wom a mede aba sɛ mede bɛda mo ase.” Paapa Hunter kaa sɛ, “Mempɛ, wo deɛ, Onipa, ɛte sɛdeɛ mekaa sɛ ɛbɛyɛ no ara pɛ, berɛ a mepaee mu kaa sɛ merenyi wo mfi adi;hwɛ deɛ Asono de brɛɛ me, hwɛ deɛ Ɔkraman de brɛɛ me.” Onipa faa ne nnua-nsã na ɔkɔɔ hɔ sɛ ɔrekɔka akyerɛ Wim nyame no, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Wo sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma a ayera no, ampa ara sɛ Ɔkraman baa bɛfa na ɔkɔɔ hɔ de maa Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no.” Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, “Ɛte saa?” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Nana, ɛyɛ nokware a ɛda adi pefee no.” Wim-nyame no somaa abɔfo sɛ wɔnkɔfa Ɔbɔmmɔfo no, na wɔde hama bi kyekyeree n’asen. Wɔbɔɔ no mpire denneennen. Afei berɛ a wɔne Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no duruu kwan no so no, ɔkaa sɛ, “Mpanyimfo, mehwehwɛ sɛ mekɔ na me ho dwo me.” Abɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Dabi, owifoɔ, wobɛdwane.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Dabi, merenguan, na sɛ wopɛ a, fa hama no, kyekyere m’asen mu, kyekyere no wɔ dua bi ho, na ma me kwan ma menkɔ.” Abɔfoɔ no tiee no, na wɔgyaa no. Ɛhɔ na na ɔwɔ no, ɔmaa ne ho tɔɔ no, bere a abɔde a ne tenten na ne ho yɛ hare no bae no. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Hwɛ ha, m’adamfo, gye saa aduru ketewa yi, na fa ka wo ho, na sɛ wokɔ, na wogyina asafo no anim, na ɛba sɛ wɔbɛkum wo a, mɛkɔ Ɔhene no harem, ama maka ne yere a n’ani gye ne ho no ba; sɛ meka no a, wobɛhunu sɛ obiara asɔre, na wo nso wobɛkasa, na woaka sɛ, ‘Mewɔ aduru,’ na sɛ wo nso de bedi dwuma wɔ abofra a ɔbɛsan anya no so.” Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Meda wo ase, Aku.” Abɔfoɔ no faa Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no na wɔkɔeɛ na wɔgyinaa Wim-nyame no anim, (n’anim ne n’Agyinatufoɔ anim) Kontire, Akwum’, Adonten, Gyase, ne Kyidom atitire. Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa n’asɛm, na berɛ a Ɔkasafoɔ no sɔre sɛ ɔrebɛbu atɛn no, wɔtee awerɛhoɔ dede bi, pui! pui! akyirikyiri wɔ ahemfo atrae hɔ. Wɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛno ne dɛn?” Wɔkaa sɛ, “Ɔwɔ aka Ɔsoro nyame yere a n’ani gye ne ho no ba.” Obiara kɔe. Afei deɛ na Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no gyina hɔ; ɔkaa sɛ, “Sɛ menyɛ ɔdeduani a, ɛnde ebia menya aduru (aduru a wɔde kum awuduru).” Ɔbarima bi tee (no) na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, “Monkɔfa ɔdeduani no mmra ha.” Afei Ɔbɔmmɔfo no bae. Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, “Sɛ wotumi hwɛ yei, me ba, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wanwu a, mede wo ti bɛkyerɛ wo.” Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Mate, Ɔyamyefoɔ.” Na Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa (san sɛ), “Nana, deɛ mɛkyerɛ wo, sɛ wobɛtumi anya bi a, ɛnde mɛtumi ahwɛ wo ba no.” Wɔkaa sɛ, “Adeɛ bɛn na ɛyɛ?” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Tell-tale mogya a wɔde bɛfra aduru a wɔabɔ no mu.” Wɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛhe na yɛbɛnya Tell-tale mogya?” Ɔkasafoɔ no mu baako a na ɔyɛ onyansafoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Na yɔnko a ɔbaa bɛka kyerɛɛ Wim-nyame no sɛ Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no aba bɛwia Wim-nyame no sika no nso ɛ, ɔte he?” Wɔkaa sɛ, “Monni mfomsoɔ, monni mfomsoɔ.” Wɔkɔ de ɔyɔnko no bae. Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Bu ne nsa wɔ nkwaa no ho, na fa ne mogya ma me.” Ntɛm ara na wobubuu mu. Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no faa mogya no sɛ ɔde bɛyam aduru no na ɔde guu Ɔhene ba no nan so. Ntɛm ara na ɔsɔree. Wɔkaa sɛ, “Monto Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no ayeyi dwom.” Wɔteɛɛm sɛ, “Eee!” Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, “Mefa wo tiri na mede ma wo, na Tell-tale no deɛ, ma wonkum no, na wɔntwitwa ne honam, na wɔntow ngu afanu nyinaa.” Wɔkumm no, na wɔtwitwaa ne nam mu asinasin, na wɔtow guu baabiara. Saa na Tell-tales pii baa abusuakuw no mu.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
