Yaa Nɔ Otsu ‘Omumɔŋ Hiamɔ Nii’ lɛ He nii

WƆ JEHA 2026 NGƐMI NƆ:

“Miishɛɛ ji mɛi ni le akɛ amɛmumɔŋ hiamɔ nii ji amɛ lɛ.”

— MAT. 5:3 lɛ nɔ.

YEHOWA kɛ hiamɔ nii komɛi ebɔ adesai. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, loko wa ma nyɛ maa hi wami mi ɔ, wa hia niye ní, tade, kɛ hwɔ he. Kɛ́ wɔbɛ nibii ni he hiaa nɛɛ eko, yɛ be kukuoo mli po lɛ, shihilɛ bafeɔ kaa. Kɛfata nakai heloonaa nibii lɛ ahe hiamɔ nii ni Yehowa bɔ wɔ lɛ he lɛ, ebɔ wɔ kɛ mumɔŋ hiamɔ nii hu. (Kane Mateo 5:3 .) Ke wa suɔ nɛ wa ná bua jɔmi nitsɛnitsɛ ɔ, e sa nɛ waa le jamɛ a hiami ní ɔ, nɛ waa ya nɔ nɛ wa tsu he ní.

2 Ke wa “le” wa ‘mumi mi hiami níhi’ ɔ, mɛni e tsɔɔ? Wiemɔ ni akɛtsu nii yɛ shishijee wiemɔ lɛ mli lɛ hiɛ susumɔ akɛ mɔ ko ji mumɔ lɛ falɔ. Nakai wiemɔ kuku lɛ haa jwɛŋmɔŋ mfoniri ko ni yɔɔ hewalɛ baa. Feemɔ gbɔmɔ ko ni egbɔjɔ ni ewo atadei ni etsilɛ, ni ekoto maŋ lɛ gbɛjegbɛ ko naa lɛ he mfoniri okwɛ. Egbɔjɔ kɛjɛ niyenii ni enáaa lɛ hewɔ, mɛi tsiɔ amɛhe kɛjɛɔ ehe yɛ bɔ ni eyɔɔ lɛ hewɔ, ni ekɛ ehe woɔ hulutsoo be mli latsaa ni naa wa kɛ gbɛkɛ fɛ̃i ni naa wa mli. Nikpalɔ lɛ le jogbaŋŋ akɛ yelikɛbuamɔ he baahia lɛ kɛji eesumɔ ni eshihilɛ aya hiɛ yɛ shihilɛ mli. Nakai nɔŋŋ yelikɛbuamɔ he baahia mɔ ko ni hiɛ kã emumɔŋ hiamɔ nii anɔ—mɔ ni biɔ mumɔ lɛ—lɛ koni eha eshihilɛ lɛ aya hiɛ. No hewɔ lɛ eshweɔ waa koni ekɛ mumɔŋ nibii babaoo ni Yehowa kɛhaa mɛi ni sumɔɔ Lɛ lɛ atsu nii.

3 Ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, kekleekle ɔ, wa ma susu níhi nɛ wa ma nyɛ maa kase ɔ he

3. Mɛni he wa ma susu ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi?

4 Be ko ɔ, Foinike yo ko ba Yesu ngɔ. Ebiyoo lɛ ji mɔ ko ni “daimonioi emɔ lɛ waa.” ( Mat. 15:21-28 ) Yo ɔ kpla si nɛ e kpa Yesu pɛɛ kaa e ye bua lɛ. Foinikeyoo nɛɛ jie sui ni sa kadimɔ waa kpo beni etee Yesu ŋɔɔ lɛ. Susumɔ enɛɛmɛi ekomɛi ahe okwɛ.

5 Foinike yoo lɛ jie heshibaa diɛŋtsɛ kpo. Mɛni he je nɛ wa ma nyɛ ma de jã a? Ejaakɛ emli fuuu beni Yesu kɛ abɛbua ko ni tamɔ nɔ ni ekɛ lɛ to gbee bibioo ko he lɛ tsu nii lɛ hewɔ lɛ, ekolɛ eji kooloo ni akwɛɔ lɛ yɛ Jeŋmajiaŋbii ashia lɛ. Jinɛ o maa pee o ní ngɛ Yesu munyu ɔmɛ kɛ e ní peepee ɔ he ha kɛɛ? Ani kulɛ onu he akɛ agbe bo ni okpa eyelikɛbuamɔ taomɔ? Jeee nakai ji bɔ ni Foenike yoo lɛ fee enii eha. Jeee heshibaa pɛ ejie lɛ kpo, shi ejie hiɛmiamɔ hu kpo. Enɛ ɔ jeɔ kpo ngɛ bɔ nɛ e ya nɔ nɛ e kpa Yesu pɛɛ kaa e ye bua lɛ ɔ he je. Mɛni tsirɛ lɛ ni efee nakai? Hemɔkɛyeli ni eyɔɔ yɛ Yesu mli lɛ. Ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ, yo nɛ ɔ hemi kɛ yemi ɔ wo Yesu he wami wawɛɛ nɛ e pee nɔ́ ko nɛ sa kadimi. Eyɛ mli akɛ Yesu kɛɛ kɛkɛ akɛ atsu lɛ koni eyaye ebua “Israel we tooi ni elaaje” lɛ pɛ moŋ, shi ejie mumɔ ni he tseee lɛ kɛjɛ Jeŋmajiaŋ yoo nɛɛ biyoo lɛ mli.

Jehanɛ se hu ɔ, e sa nɛ wa ná hemi kɛ yemi nɛ mi wa ngɛ Kristo Yesu mi, nɛ wa ná hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi ngɛ nihi nɛ e ngɛ a nɔ kɛ tsɔɔ e kaseli ɔmɛ blɔ ɔ a nɔ. Wa maa na nɔ́ nɛ e sa kaa waa pee konɛ wa nine nɛ su ní nɛ ɔmɛ a nɔ kɛ gu nɔ hyɛmi níhi nɛ bɔfo Petro kɛ Paulo kɛ Matsɛ David pee ɔ nɔ.

TAAKE PETRO—YAA SHI AKƐ MUMƆŊ NYƐŊMƆ JOGBƆ

7 Mo susu bɔfo Petro nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ he nɛ o hyɛ. Eji klɛŋklɛŋ Yudafoi ni yɔse akɛ Yesu ji Mesia lɛ—mɔ ni Yehowa kɛ lɛ tsuɔ nii koni ekɛlɛ E-webii lɛ “naanɔ wala wiemɔi” lɛ ateŋ mɔ kome. ( Yoh. 6:66-68 ) Loko Yesu nɛ a tle lɛ si ɔ maa ya hiɔwe ɔ, e kɛ fami nɛ ɔ wo Petro dɛ ke: “Lɛɛ ye jijɔ tsɔwi ɔmɛ.” ( Yohane 21:17 ) Petro kɛ anɔkwayeli tsu nakai nitsumɔ lɛ he nii, ni Yehowa tsɔ enɔ po eŋmala woji enyɔ ni batsɔ Biblia lɛ fã. Shi ehe bahia ni Petro kɛ mumɔŋ niyenii hu alɛ lɛ diɛŋtsɛ ehe. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, e kase sɛ womihi nɛ Mawu mumi ɔ ha nɛ bɔfo Paulo ngma be ko nɛ be ɔ. Petro kplɛɛ nɔ kaa níhi nɛ Paulo ngma ngɛ he ɔ ekomɛ “a sisi numi yee.” ( 2 Pet. 3:15, 16 ) Shi ekã shi faŋŋ akɛ Petro tee nɔ ekã shi, ejaakɛ eyɛ hemɔkɛyeli akɛ Yehowa baaye abua lɛ ni enu “niyenii ni wa” ni akɛ mumɔ tsirɛ Paulo ni esɔmɔ yɛ ewoji lɛ amli lɛ shishi ni ekɛtsu nii.— Hebribii 5:14–6:1 .

8 Petro bu blɔ tsɔɔmihi nɛ a kɛ ha lɛ ɔ tue kɛ tsɔɔ kaa e ngɛ hemi kɛ yemi ngɛ Yehowa mi. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, benɛ Petro ngɛ Yopa nɛ e ngɛ womi nya he ɔ, e na nina ko nɛ Mawu nane mi dalɔ ko de lɛ ke e gbe lohwehi nɛ ngɛ Mose Mlaa a nya a, a he tsɔ we nɛ e ye. Kɛha Yudafoi lɛ, kulɛ nakai gbɛtsɔɔmɔ lɛ baafee nɔ ni haa mɔ he jɔ̃ɔ ehe. Shishijee mli lɛ, Petro ha hetoo akɛ: “Dabi kwraa, Nuntsɔ, ejaakɛ miyeko nɔ ko ni he tseee loo nɔ ko ni he tseee dã.” Kɛkɛ ni akɛɛ lɛ akɛ: “Kaatsɛ nibii ni Nyɔŋmɔ etsuu he lɛ akɛ amɛhe tseee.” ( Bɔfoi lɛ Asaji 10:9-15 ) Petro nu oti lɛ shishi. Te wɔfeɔ tɛŋŋ wɔleɔ? Beni ena ninaa lɛ sɛɛ etsɛɛɛ lɛ, hii etɛ komɛi ni Jeŋmajiaŋnyo Kornelio tsu lɛ bashɛ shi ni amɛfɔ̃ Petro nine koni eba ni ekɛ amɛnuŋtsɔ lɛ abawie. Kɛ́ eji bɔ ni eji lɛ, kulɛ Petro ekpɛlɛŋ nɔ kɔkɔɔkɔ akɛ ebaabote Jeŋmajiaŋnyo ko shia. Yudafoi lɛ buɔ Jeŋmajiaŋbii lɛ akɛ mɛi ni he tseee. ( Bɔfoi lɛ Asaji 10:28, 29 ) Shi amrɔ nɔŋŋ lɛ, Petro kpɛlɛ la hee ni Yehowa jie lɛ kpo etsɔɔ lɛ lɛ nɔ. ( Abɛ 4:18 ) E fiɛɛ ha Kornelio kɛ e we mi bimɛ ɔmɛ, nɛ e bua jɔ kaa e na kaa a kplɛɛ anɔkuale ɔ nɔ, nɛ a ná mumi klɔuklɔu ɔ, nɛ a baptisi mɛ.​— Níts.

No dani wɔbaaya nɔ wɔhi mumɔŋ niyenii kpakpa mli ni wɔbaaná sɛɛnamɔ kɛha Yehowa.

TAAKE PAULO—YAA HE OWO MUMƆ ATADE KPAKPA

10 Bɔ nɛ pee nɛ waa sa Mawu hɛ mi ɔ, e sa nɛ waa ngɔ blɔ nya tomi kpa ko hu nɛ Yehowa kɛ ha wɔ ɔ kɛ tsu ní: mumi mi tade. Mɛni wɔtsɔɔ yɛ nakai wiemɔ lɛ he? Bɔfo Paulo ngma kaa e sa nɛ waa ‘je nɔmlɔ su momo ɔ kɛ je,’ nɛ waa wo “nɔmlɔ su ehe ɔ.” ( Nyɛkanea Kolosebii 3:8-10 . ) Tade ni tamɔ nɛkɛ ni wɔɔwo lɛ baanyɛ afee naagba ni yaa nɔ be fɛɛ be. Susumɔ Paulo diɛŋtsɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ he okwɛ. Kɛjɛ egbekɛbiiashi tɔɔ lɛ, ebɔ mɔdɛŋ waa koni ena Nyɔŋmɔ hiɛ duromɔ. ( Gal. 1:14; Filip. 3:4, 5 ) Se e be Mawu yi mi tomi ɔ he anɔkuale nile, enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e be mumi mi si fɔfɔɛ kpakpa. Kristo tsɔɔmɔi lɛ ahe nilee ni ebɛ, kɛ henɔwomɔ lɛ ha ebatsɔ “musuŋtsɔmɔ” ni ewo sui gbohii.— 1 Tim. 1:13 lɛ nɔ.

10. Mɛni mumɔŋ tade kpakpa ni wɔɔwo lɛ biɔ ni wɔfee? ( Kolosebii 3:​8-10 )

11 Loko Paulo maa pee Kristofo no ɔ, e mi mi fuɔ wawɛɛ. Amaniɛbɔɔ ko ni yɔɔ Bɔfoi lɛ Asaji lɛ mli lɛ tsɔɔ akɛ Paulo mli fu Yesu kaselɔi lɛ aahu akɛ ‘ewo amɛhe gbeyei kɛ gbɔmɔgbee mumɔ’ eshi amɛ. ( Bɔfoi lɛ Asaji 9:1 ) Beni Paulo batsɔ Kristofonyo sɛɛ lɛ, ŋwanejee ko bɛ he akɛ ebɔ mɔdɛŋ waa koni ejie esu momo lɛ fã nɛɛ. ( Efe. 4:22, 31 ) Ngɛ jã po ɔ, se benɛ Paulo kɛ Barnaba sã nya a, e kɛ “mi mi fu wawɛɛ.” ( Bɔfo Ɔmɛ A Ní Tsumi 15:37-39 ) Enɛ ɔ ji nyagbee kɛ ha Paulo, se e ngmɛɛ we he. Etee nɔ ‘eyi egbɔmɔtso lɛ yi’—ekɛ emuu ni eyeee lɛ wuu—koni Yehowa akpɛlɛ enɔ.— 1 Kor. 9:27 lɛ he.

12 Paulo nyɛ nɛ e je e su yayahi kɛ pue, nɛ lɔ ɔ se ɔ, e wo su ehe ɔ, ejakaa e ngɔɛ e hɛ kɛ fɔɛ lɛ nitsɛ e he wami nɔ. ( Filip. 4:13 ) Kaa bɔ nɛ Petro pee ɔ, Paulo hu ngɔ e hɛ kɛ fɔ ‘he wami nɛ Mawu haa nɔ ɔ’ nɔ. ( 1 Pet. 4:11 ) Se kɛ̃ ɔ, be komɛ ɔ, Paulo nu he kaa e nyɛ we nɔ́ ko. Se benɛ e kɔni mi jɔ ɔ, e susu ní kpakpahi nɛ e hiɔwe Tsɛ ɔ pee ha lɛ ɔ he, nɛ lɔ ɔ wo e pɛɛ si kaa e maa ya nɔ maa bɔ mɔde.​— Rom. 7:21-25 he.

13 Wa ma nyɛ maa kase Paulo konɛ wa yɔse kaa be babauu nɛ waa kɛ ngɛ Yehowa sɔmɔe ɔ tsuo se ɔ, e sa nɛ waa ya nɔ nɛ waa bɔ mɔde wawɛɛ nɛ wa je nɔmlɔ su momo ɔ nɛ wa wo nɔmlɔ su ehe ɔ, nɛ ji mumi mi tade nɛ Mawu haa wɔ ɔ. Kɛji wɔyɛ sɛɛkuu ko—tamɔ

Nilee yɛ mli akɛ wɔbaatsake mɔ ni wɔji koni wɔkɛ Nyɔŋmɔ taomɔ nii lɛ akpã gbee.

TAAKƐ DAVID TSƆƆ LƐ—YAA SHI AMƆŊMƆ JOGBƆ

14 Loko wa ma ná bua jɔmi nitsɛnitsɛ ɔ, e he hia babauu pe mumi mi niye ní kɛ mumi mi tade kɛkɛ. Mumɔŋ abobaahe hu he miihia wɔ. Mɛni ji no, ni te wɔɔfee tɛŋŋ wɔná nɔmimaa akɛ wɔbaahi ehebuu shishi?

14-15 lɛ nɔ. Mɛɛ shishinumɔ naa Yehowa kɛ mumɔŋ abobaahe haa ewebii lɛ? ( Lala 27:5 ) (Kwɛmɔ wolo lɛ hiɛ mfoniri lɛ hu.)

15 Matsɛ David tu mumi mi wesa he nɛ Yehowa kɛ haa wɔ ɔ he munyu. (Kane La 27:5 .) Yehowa poɔ e we bi a he piɛ kɛ jeɔ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ kɛ nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ nɛ ma nyɛ ma puɛ hemi kɛ yemi nɛ a ngɛ ngɛ e mi ɔ he daa. Mɛɛ gbɛ nɔ Yehowa tsɔɔ ebuɔ etsuji ahe kɛjɛɔ hegbeyeiwoo ni jɛɔ kponɔgbɛ lɛ he? Ewo shi akɛ tawuu nii fɛɛ tawuu nii ni abaafee ashi amɛ lɛ nyɛŋ aye omanye. ( La 34:7; Yes. 54:17 ) E ngɛ mi kaa Satan kɛ e we bi ɔmɛ ngɛ he wami mohu lɛɛ, se a be nyɛe maa ye wɔ awi kɛ ya neneene. Ke a ngɔ wa wami po ɔ, Yehowa maa ngɔ kɛ ha wɔ ngɛ gbogboehi a si tlemi ɔ mi. ( 1 Kor. 15:55-57; Kpoj. 21:3, 4 ) Jehanɛ se hu ɔ, Yehowa yeɔ bua wɔ nɛ wa nyɛɔ da wa tsui mi yeyeeye peemi nɛ ma nyɛ maa ye wɔ awi kɛ ya neneene ɔ nya. (Abɛ 12:25; Mat. 6:27-29 ) Nɛ wa Tsɛ nɛ ngɛ suɔmi ɔ ha nɛ wa ná mumi mi weku nɛ ji nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi nɛ a fĩɔ wa se, kɛ asafo mi nikɔtɔmahi nɛ a hyɛɛ wa nɔ. ( Yes. 32:1, 2 ) Behi fuu ɔ, ke wa kpe ɔ, a kaiɔ wɔ blɔ pɔtɛɛ komɛ nɛ wa ma nyɛ maa gu nɔ .

KE WA kɛ nyɛmi Yehowa jalɔ ko kpe kekleekle ɔ, sanehi nɛ wa pɔɔ wa sibi bimi ɔ eko ji, “Mɛni blɔ nɔ nɛ o ba le anɔkuale ɔ?” Mɛi komɛi haa sanebimɔ nɛɛ hetoo kɛtsɔ wiemɔ ni amɛkɛɔ akɛ “atsɔse amɛ yɛ anɔkwale lɛ mli” lɛ nɔ. Ni komɛ hu ma nyɛ ma de ke lingmi nɛ ɔ nɛ a “ba anɔkuale ɔ mi.” Wɔhaa hetoo yɛ gbɛi ni tamɔ nɛkɛ nɔ ejaakɛ Nyɔŋmɔ Wiemɔ lɛ mli anɔkwale lɛ náa wɔshihilɛ fɛɛ nɔ hewalɛ. Maaba? Ejakaa wa le kaa ja wa tsɔɔ kaa wa suɔ anɔkuale ɔ nɛ wa hiɔ si ngɛ anɔkuale ɔ nya loko wa ma nyɛ maa piɛɛ Yehowa weku ɔ he. Nɔ ni fata enɛ he ji anɔkwa ni wɔɔye yɛ wɔwiemɔi kɛ wɔnifeemɔi amli.​—La 12:10. 15:1-3 he.

4 Yehowa ji anɔkuale ɔ Jɛɛhe. Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni ewieɔ lɛ ji anɔkwale. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, munyuhi nɛ e tuɔ ngɛ nɔ́ nɛ da kɛ nɔ́ nɛ dɛ he ɔ ji anɔkuale. 5 Ngɛ nɔ́ nɛ ni komɛ deɔ ɔ tsuo se ɔ, e he be wae kaa wa maa na Yehowa nɛ ji “Mawu nɛ e yeɔ anɔkuale ɔ.” Odaseyeli ni tsɔɔ akɛ ehi shi lɛ ebɔle wɔhe fɛɛ. ( Rom. 1:20 ) Beni bɔfo Paulo kɛ Hela nilelɔi akuu ko ni yɔɔ Atene lɛ wieɔ lɛ, ekɛɛ akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ miisumɔ ni ‘wana lɛ,’ ni “ekɛ wɔteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ kɛ wɔteŋ jɛkɛɛɛ.” (Kane Bɔfo Ɔmɛ A Ní Tsumi 17:27 .) Ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ, Yehowa gblaa nihi nɛ a baa a he si nɛ a ngɛ anɔkuale ɔ hlae ɔ kɛ ba e ngɔ.— Yohane 6:44 .

6 Blɔ kake nɛ wa ma nyɛ maa gu nɔ kɛ na Yehowa ji kaa wa maa kase Baiblo ɔ. Nyɔŋmɔ mumɔ krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ tsirɛ hii ni ŋma nakai wolo lɛ. ( 2 Pet. 1:20, 21 ) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, nɔ́ tsuaa nɔ́ nɛ ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi ɔ ji anɔkuale, nɛ e sa kaa waa ngɔ wa hɛ kɛ fɔ nɔ. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, wa ma nyɛ maa ngɔ wa hɛ kɛ fɔ nɔ́ nɛ e de ngɛ bɔ nɛ wa je mluku ɔ kɛ níhi nɛ wami ngɛ zugba a nɔ ɔ je sisi ha a he ɔ nɔ. (1 Mose 1:1, 26) Wa ma nyɛ ma he nɔ́ nɛ e de ngɛ nɔ́ he je nɛ wɔ tsuo wa peeɔ yayami, kɛ nɔ́ he je nɛ nɔ́ nami kɛ gbenɔ babauu ngɛ ɔ he ɔ maa ye. ( Rom. 5:12; 6:23 ) Wa ma nyɛ maa ngɔ wa hɛ kɛ fɔ nɔ kulaa ke e de ke Yehowa maa gu e Bi ɔ nɔ kɛ dla níhi tsuo nɛ Satan nɛ ji “lakpa tsɛ” ɔ puɛ ɔ. ( Yoh. 8:44; Rom. 16:20 ) Jehanɛ hu ɔ, wa ma nyɛ maa ngɔ wa hɛ kɛ fɔ Baiblo ɔ mi si womi nɛ ji kaa Yesu ma kpata yayami peeli a hɛ mi, e ma tle gbogboehi si, e ma dla zugba a nɔ ekohu, nɛ e maa ye bua wɔ konɛ waa ye mluku. ( Yoh. 11:25, 26; 1 Yoh. 3:8 ) Enɛ ɔ ji he blɔ ngua nɛ Yehowa ha wɔ! E tsɔɔ wɔ anɔkuale ɔ, nɛ e ha wɔ he blɔ konɛ waa tsɔɔ nihi hu anɔkuale ɔ.​— Mat. 28:19, 20 lɛ he.

Kanemɔ Kolosebii 4:6 . Bɔfo Paulo kai Kristofohi nɛ a hi Ko-lose ɔ kaa e sa nɛ a munyu tutui nɛ “bua nɛ jɔ be tsuaa be.” Mɛni no tsɔɔ? Shishijee wiemɔ lɛ tsɔɔ susumɔ akɛ jeee akɛ esa akɛ wɔwiemɔi lɛ afee nɔ ni he baaba sɛɛnamɔ kɛha toibolɔ lɛ pɛ, shi moŋ esa akɛ efee mlihilɛ ni haa mɔ tsui nyɔɔ emli hu.

E sa nɛ waa ngɔ ga womi nɛ Paulo wo kaa wa je mi mi sami kpo kɛ tsɔɔ ni kpahi anɔkuale ɔ ɔ kɛ tsu ní. Biblia lɛ diɛŋtsɛ kɛ anɔkwalei ni yɔɔ mli lɛ toɔ klante ni naa ba ni baanyɛ agbala wɔsusuma kɛ wɔmumɔ mli lɛ he. Yɛ gbɛ kroko nɔ lɛ, ebaanyɛ ejie wɔmligbɛ henumɔi diɛŋtsɛ kɛ yiŋtoi akpo. ( Heb. 4:12 ) Se ke waa kɛ Baiblo ɔ tsu we ní saminya a, wa ma nyɛ maa pee jã

kɛ efɔŋfeemɔ ni he ehiaaa kɛ béi ni he ehiaaa aba. Mɛɛ gbɛ nɔ ebaatsɔ nakai eba lɛ?

Feemɔ shihilɛ nɛɛ he mfoniri okwɛ. Yɛ wɔshiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ mli lɛ, wɔkɛ nuu ko ni hiɛ tsui kpakpa ni sɔleɔ ehaa amagai daa lɛ kpeɔ, ni esumɔɔ ni ekɛ eweku lɛ aye Blonya kɛ Easter. Wɔbaanyɛ wɔkɛ Biblia lɛ atsu nii ni wɔkɛtsɔɔ nuu lɛ bɔ ni eji kwashiai asane akɛ aaasɔle aha amagai ni wala bɛ amɛmli lɛ, ni wɔma nɔ mi eha lɛ akɛ Blonya kɛ Easter ji wɔŋjalɔi agbijurɔyelii. ( Yes. 44:14-20; 2 Kor. 6:14-17 ) Ke wa pee jã ngɛ kekleekle be nɛ waa kɛ lɛ sɛɛ ní ɔ, lɔ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa wa ngɛ anɔkuale yee, se wa be Mawu Munyu ɔ tsu we ní saminya.

Paulo kɛɛ hu akɛ esa akɛ mɛi ni kaneɔ ewolo lɛ kɛ ŋoo awo nɔ ni amɛwieɔ lɛ mli. E ngɛ tsɔɔe kaa e sa nɛ waa bɔ mɔde nɛ waa ngɔ anɔkuale ɔ kɛ laa aloo waa kɛ laa. Mohu ɔ, e ji

Nyiɛmɔ Yesu nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ sɛɛ yɛ be mli ni esa akɛ owie anɔkwale kɛ nɔ ni esa akɛ owie lɛ he yiŋkpɛɛ mli (Kwɛmɔ kuku 15)

ni miiwo wɔ hewalɛ ni wɔkɛ wiemɔi ni “awo mli” atsu nii bɔni afee ni wɔkɛ anɔkwale lɛ aha yɛ gbɛ ni baaha toibolɔ lɛ “anya he” lɛ nɔ. ( Hiob 12:11 ) Enɛ feemɔ baanyɛ afee nɔ ni wa. Mɛi ni jɛ kusumii krokomɛi amli lɛ baanyɛ ana akɛ nakai sanegbaa lɛ ji nɔ ni akpɛlɛɛɛ nɔ, ni eji nɔ ni woɔ mɔ awui po. Feemɔ Biblia mli Saji lɛ ahe mfoniri yɛ ojwɛŋmɔ mli

Yinɔsane mli saji babaoo yɛ Biblia lɛ mli ni tsɔɔ nibii ni he hiaa waa. Kɛtsɔ saji nɛɛ ahe mfonirii ni wɔfeɔ yɛ wɔjwɛŋmɔ mli lɛ nɔ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔnu nɔ ni wɔkaneɔ lɛ shishi jogbaŋŋ ni wɔná he sɛɛ babaoo. Naa ŋaawoi komɛi ni baaye abua bo ni ofee Biblia mli saji ahe mfoniri yɛ ojwɛŋmɔ mli.

• Kanemɔ waa. Kɛ́ okane Biblia lɛ waa lɛ, ebaanyɛ eye ebua bo ni ofee nibii ni yaa nɔ lɛ ahe mfoniri yɛ ojwɛŋmɔ mli jogbaŋŋ. Kɛ́ nyɛmiikane Biblia lɛ akɛ weku lɛ, nyɛbaanyɛ nyɛha sane lɛ afee nɔ ni yɔɔ wala mli kɛtsɔ sui sɔrɔtoi ni nyɛaaha mɔ fɛɛ mɔ akane lɛ nɔ.

• Feemɔ nibii ni yaa nɔ lɛ ahe mfoniri okwɛ. Bɔɔ mɔdɛŋ ni omɔ mɛi ni awieɔ amɛhe lɛ ajwɛŋmɔi kɛ henumɔi amli. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, mo bi o he ke: ‘Mɛni he je nɛ nɔ ɔ tu munyu loo e pee e ní kaa bɔ nɛ e pee ɔ? Eji miyɛ nakai shihilɛ lɛ mli kulɛ, mɛni ji nɔ ni masusu loo minu he?’

• Tsɔɔmɔ loo sketch. Biblia mli nibii ni tee nɔ lɛ ahe mfoniri ni ooofee lɛ baanyɛ aye abua bo ni ofee saji lɛ ahe mfoniri yɛ ojwɛŋmɔ mli ni okai amɛ jogbaŋŋ, kɛji akɛ nɔ ni ofee ogbe naa lɛ jeee nitsumɔ ko ni akɛ niji fee po.
