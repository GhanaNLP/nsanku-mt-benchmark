YOU ARE GOING TO BE ANANSE BOSEA

ANANSE, and Cockroach came to him to borrow, river. The Duck himself did not have any, and went to his friend Ntontom and got some for his friend Kokokyinaka. The day the Frog was very angry, and the Frog said to the Duck, "Go and get my money when I am. Ananse said, "Go and stand on the ground, and go and get your money. The lizard said, "It will be hard for me to get it; the animal flies. Ananse said, “Go and hit the big tree, you will see that the tree will fall, and you will catch Kokokyinaka and take your money.” The ants fought against the tree. Under die e’buo, and Cockroach from it pa, bu, bu, bu! When Ntontom came, he said, “Ananse mo!” It’s like the proverb, “If the Cockroach doesn’t kill the Duck, don’t let the Frog bow to the big one.”

My story that I have told, whether it is good or bad, take some, and bring some.
