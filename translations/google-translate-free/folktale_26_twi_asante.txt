APUPUO A OBOO AND A RIVER, DA NSU’KAKYE

YE that Okawa, Opitire, Bonko and asuom’s animal cow and ope kesie some buried and water cow eaten for the animal cow suffered. We are lying on the sand. Then we said, “Let us go up to God and cry out to him for water.” Animals stand siim’ koye. And Okawa raised it:

“Isn’t it me Oka’ Abena, pimpim-a-ko-ha, pimpim-aba-ha?

I have jumped and stood on datahin, I have cried and called upon you.

I have heard my grandmother aburobia.

Aberefi Sempa my wife, Abuya.”

And the rain didn't. And Opitire so fruit, he said:

“Am I Mr. Abena, a pimpim-a-ko-ha, a pimpim-aba-ha?

I have jumped and stood on datahin, I have cried and called upon you.

I have heard my grandmother aburobia.

Aberefi Sempa my wife, Abuya.”

And the rain didn't. And Bonko so comes, he says:

“Are you my Bonko Abena, apimpim-a-ko-ha, apimpim-aba-ha?

I have jumped and stood on datahin, I have cried and called upon you. I have heard my grandmother aburobia.

Aberefi Sempa my wife, Abuya.”

And the rain didn't. The animals mother besuye, and it was Apupuo. “Give me some tears,” he said. And we said, “So’s our tears not good, and yours?” “Give me water,” he said. And we beat him and told him plainly not to cry. And Apupuo said, "I'm crying. And he said, “Su!” And he gave it:

“Are you my Apupuo Abena, apimpim-a-ko-ha, apimpim-aba-ha?

I have jumped and stood on datahin, I have cried and called upon you.

I have heard my grandmother aburobia.

Aberefi Sempa my wife, Abuya.”

And God boom’, kukuru! animals stand saying, “Go on, go on.” And he cried again and ’God munaa tumm! Then the animal's sword struck, saying, "Go on again. And he gave it to sa’ra. Efei is God’s gift! ta! ta! ta! water source yielded. Now when the animal ’s up, the water’s twom! Animals don’t stand still’. Apupuo said o’kom’ and we told him, and he slept on the couch. Ene se ye se, “The rabbit that gave birth to its river, sleeps in the river.”
