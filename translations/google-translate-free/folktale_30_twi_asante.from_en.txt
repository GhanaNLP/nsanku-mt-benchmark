Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

SƐNEA NKYERƐKYERƐMU BAA ABUSUE NO MU

BERE bi na ɔbarima bi wɔ hɔ a wɔfrɛ no Hate-to-be-contradicted, na esiane ɛno nti, ɔkɔsii atrae ketewaa bi (ne nyinaa nkutoo) na ɔkɔtraa mu. Na abɔdeɛ a wɔfrɛ no Duyker no kɔɔ (kɔsraa) no, na ɔne no kɔtenaa mmɛdua bi ase. Na nnuadewa no bi hwee fam. Duyker no kaa sɛ, "Agya tan-sɛ-wɔbɛbɔ abira, wo nsateaa abere." Hate-to-be-contradicted kaa sɛ, "Saa ne nnuadewa no su; sɛ ɛberee a, nkorabata mmiɛnsa ɛbere prɛko pɛ; sɛ ɛbere a, metwitwa, na sɛ menoa de yi ngo no a, wɔyɛ nsuo-nkukuo mmiɛnsa a ngo ahyɛ mu ma; na mede ngo no kɔ Akase sɛ ɔrekɔtɔ Akase akwakoraa bi; Akase akwakoraa no ba bɛwo ma me." nanabea, a ɔwo me maame, a ɔno nso wo me, sɛ ɛna wo me a, migyina hɔ dedaw." Duyker no kaa sɛ, "Ɛno nyinaa deɛ, wodi atoro." Na Hate-to-be-contradicted faa dua bi bɔɔ Duyker no ti, na okum no. Nea edi hɔ no, Abedee akraman ketewa no bae. Na ɔne no kɔtenaa mmɛdua no ase, na saa ara na ɛbaa. Na saa na na ɛte wɔ mmoa no nyinaa ho. Afei, Kwaku Ananse, Akɔre no kɔfaa ne ntama, na ɔfaa ne bag, de bɔɔ ne mmati so, na ɔkɔɔ Hate-to-be-contradicted akuraa ase. Ɔkyeaa no sɛ, "Agya, anɔpa pa." Hate-to-be-contradicted buae sɛ, "Y'Aku, na ɛhe na worekɔ?" Obuae se, "Mereba wo nkyɛn." Na ɔfaa ne ntoma no de guu mmɛdua no ase. Hate-to-be-contradicted kae sɛ, "Noa aduan ma Akɔre no nni." Na bere a ɛrenoa no, Ananse ne Hate-to-be-contradicted tenaa mmɛdua no ase. Na nnuadewa no bi hwee fam. Na Ananse faa wɔn de guu ne bag mu. Na ɔkɔɔ so yɛɛ saa kosii sɛ ne bag yɛɛ ma. Wɔde aduan no bae, na Ananse dii. Bere a odii wiei no, nnuadewa no bi hwee fam, na Ananse kae sɛ, "Agya tan-sɛ-wɔbɛbɔ abira, wo nnuadewa no abere." Hate-to-be-contradicted kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ wɔn su sɛ wɔbɛbere saa; sɛ ɛberee a, nkorabata mmiɛnsa ɛbere prɛko pɛ; sɛ ɛberee a, metwitwa, na sɛ menoa de yi ngo no a, wɔyɛ nsuo-nkukuo mmiɛnsa a ngo ahyɛ mu ma, na mede ngo no kɔ Akase, sɛ ɔrekɔtɔ Akase akwakoraa bi; Akase akwakoraa no ba bɛwo ma me." nanabea, a ɔwo me maame, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔno nso betumi awo me. Sɛ ɛna wo me a, megyina hɔ dedaw." Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "Wonni atoro, nea woka no yɛ nokware; me deɛ, mewɔ okros bi a ɛgyina m’afuo mu, na sɛ ɛberee a, mede nhama atenten aduɔson nson bom (sɛ mɛduru wɔn nkyɛn atwe akɔ fam), nanso ɛno mpo no, ɛnkɔ, enti meda m’akyi, na metumi de m’akyi berɛmo adi dwuma de atutu wɔn." Hate-to-be-contradicted said, "Oh, I understand; ɔkyena mɛba abɛhwɛ." Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "Ampa ara." Bere a Akɔre no rekɔ (fie) no, ɔwea nnuadewa a waboaboa ano no, na ɔteɛteɛɛm guu kwan no so. Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma fii ase daa adi no, Ɔtan-sɛ-wɔ-bɔ-abɔ abira siim sɛ ɔbɛkɔ Akɔre akuraa ase. Afei bere a Akɔre no adi kan aba (da a edii ɛno anim) no, na wakɔ akɔka ne mma, sɛ, “Onipa bi bɛba ha, na ɔtan sɛ wɔbɛkasa atia no, na sɛ ɔduru hɔ na ɔbisa m’akyi a, ɛsɛ sɛ woka kyerɛ no sɛ nnɛra na maka sɛ merekɔ baabi, berɛ a m’akyi no bubui wɔ mmeaeɛ nson, na na ɛsɛ sɛ mede kɔ ɔdansifoɔ nkyɛn kɔsiesie, na sɛdeɛ wantumi anwie saa berɛ no, ɔka sɛ wakɔ akɔyɛ adwuma no awie.” Ɛno akyi bere tiaa bi no, Hate-to-be-contradicted bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛhe na w’agya akɔ?” Wobuae se, “Ampa, papa kɔɔ baabi nnɛra, na ne barima no bubui wɔ mmeae ahorow nson, na ɔde kɔɔ ɔdansifo bi nkyɛn, nanso wantumi anwie adwuma no saa bere no, na agya akɔ sɛ ɔrekɔma wɔawie, na (wo,) Agya, woanhu mogya no wɔ kwan no so?” Hate-to-be-contradicted kae sɛ, “Mihuu.” Ɔtan-to-be-contradicted bisae sɛ, “Na wo maame wɔ he?” (Akɔre ba no buae sɛ,) “Maame nso, nnɛra ɔkɔɔ asubɔnten no ho, na anka ne nsu-kuruwa no ahwe ase na abubu sɛ wannye no amfi saa yɛ mu denam bere a ɔkyeree no ara kwa so, nanso wanwie koraa sɛ obegye no amfi asehwe mu, na wasan aba nnɛ sɛ ɔbɛyɛ saa.” Ɔtan a wɔde bɛbɔ abira no anka hwee. Afei Ananse duu hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Noa aduane bi a Hate-to-be-contradicted betumi adi.” Bere a mmofra no renoa aduan no, na wɔde perch ketewaa biako pɛ na edi dwuma nanso wɔde ɛmo pii na ɛyɛe; wɔmaa soup a wɔayam no yɛɛ hyew yiye. Bere a wowiee no, wɔde sii hɔ wɔ Hate-to-be-contradicted anim. Ɔtan-sɛ-wɔbɛbɔ abira dii. Afei ɛmo no yɛɛ no yaw; na ɔpɛ sɛ owu. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ntikuma (Akɔre ba), twe nsuo ma me ma mennom.” Ntikuma kɔɔ nsuo no mu; wamfa nsu no amma da. Hate-to-be-contradicted kaa sɛ, “Ntikuma, ɛhe na saa nsuo no wɔ?” Ntikuma kaa sɛ, “Ah, nsuo a yɛwɔ wɔ ha (wɔ yɛn nsuo-kuo mu) no yɛ ahodoɔ mmiɛnsa;sɛ agya dea na ɛdi kan, me maame yɔnko yere deɛ na ɛwɔ mfimfini, na sɛ m’ankasa me maame deɛ na ɛwɔ aseɛ (wɔ kukuo no mu);na ɛnam sɛ mepɛ sɛ metwe nsuo a ɛyɛ m’ankasa me maame dea nko ara ma wo nti, sɛ manhwɛ yie kɛseɛ berɛ a meretwe a, ɛde abusuakuo bi bɛba akasakasa.” Hate-to-be-contradicted kae sɛ, “Wo brat ketewa, wodi atoro.” Ntɛm ara, Ananse kaa sɛ, “Monhwe no ma owu.” Hate-to-be-contradicted kae sɛ, “Adɛn nti na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔboro me ma mawu?” Akɔre no kaa sɛ, “Wose wotan sɛ wɔbɛbɔ wo abira, na nanso woabɔ obi abira, ɛno nti na meka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wɔboro wo sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a wobɛwu.” Enti wɔboroo Hate-to-be-contradicted ma owui. Afei Ananse twitwaa ne nam mu nketenkete na ɔpetepetee so. Ɛno nti na ɛsɛ sɛ wohu nnipa pii a wɔtan sɛ wɔbɛbɔ wɔn abira wɔ abusuakuw no mu no.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
