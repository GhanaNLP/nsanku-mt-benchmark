THINGS ARE BAD

YE'SEE Osebo and his wife have two children. And the cows 'ni and we'se went to the woods' day to catch meat and feed us. One day die ye'ni and ye'se said to us, "You get up and cry, 'There's nothing here, there's nothing here. And we went into the woods and left our children with him. And Dankwata came, and the sword e'su said:

"There's nothing there, There's nothing there!"

Dankwata said, "Who told you to drink this water?" Ye'see, "He's going with this and he's right. Dankwata said, "We're helping; you don't say, 'Times don't turn, times don't turn' And the night we'ni and ye'se baye, and we heard ye'mma e'su say:

"Time doesn't turn, Time doesn't turn!"

Ye'see, "Who's washing your water now?" Ye'see, "A man is right. And we're saying, "Don't cry, and don't cry, 'Nothing here, nothing here. We've given meat to the cows; sane head. Dankwata came, and he struck two spears. Then die bed lays there, two trees standing by it. When Dankwata came, he cut down the trees and placed the spear in front of the trees. He said to the cows, "Don't say, 'Time doesn't change, time doesn't change,' and when your 'ni and your 'se come, say that I'm the one. Dankwata tuye fought in front of the spear' Soon the cows 'ni and ye'se baye. Ye'see, "Who said you weren't watering now?" Dankwata said, "I'm a!" Osebonini said he'd jumped on him, and Dankwanta had fled, and Osebo had fought on the spear. Dankwanta fought on a spear. Osebo bedee to'jump at Dankwata, and he too fights on the spear. Dankwanta said to the cows, "You see you don't have any more meat to eat. That's why I told you, 'Time doesn't change.

My story that I have told, whether it is good or bad, take some, and bring some.
