Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

SƐNEA ƐBAE SƐNEA PƆKƆBƆ NO DUA BƐYƐ KƆƆBƆ

WƆKA sɛ bere bi na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔwoo abeawa bi a ne ho yɛ fɛ. Ɛhɔ na na ɔwɔ; nanso bere a wɔde no maa obiara sɛ ɔwaree no, ɔpowee sɛ ɔbɛware. Bere nyinaa sɛ (ne) nkurɔfo kɔkasa (ne no, aware ho asɛm) a, ɔkae sɛ, “Akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ ɛnte saa.” Afei na abɔde a wɔfrɛ no Onini (Python) no wɔ kwae no mu baabi, na na ɔte ɔbodan kɛse bi mu. Da bi a ɔwɔ hɔ (wɔ ne ɔbodan mu) no ɔtee sɛ abɔmmɔfo binom retwam, na na wɔresusuw ababaa fɛfɛ no ho asɛm, sɛnea, bere a wɔde no maa obiara wɔ aware mu no, ɔkae sɛ ɔmpɛ no. Python no da hɔ, na ɔkaa (wɔ ne tirim sɛ), “Mɛsesa me ho ayɛ aberanteɛ pa, na makɔ akɔware Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba.” Nokwarem no, Python no danee ne ho bɛyɛɛ aberante; sɛ wobɛhwɛ onii bere tenten, na ɛnyɛ yiye. Osiim kɔɔ akuraa no ase. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na ɔrewe kola-nuts. Bere a oduu kurow no ano no, na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba a na wakɔbɔ aguare no asan aba na ɔretrɛw ntade no mu ma ayow. Python no teɛteɛɛm, na ɛhwee Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no gyegyeegye so. Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no kaa sɛ, “Adɛn nti na wotete me ho so?” Python no kaa sɛ, “Wo na woteɛ (sɛ woka saa), Amu.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Me nton nkyia nyɛ Amu.” Ɔbuaa sɛ, “Wowɔ nea ɛteɛ (wɔ saa ka mu), Eson.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Me nton nkyia nyɛ Eson.” Ɔbuaa sɛ, “Woayɛ no yie (sɛ woka saa), me yere.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛnde maware wo.” Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba ne Python no sii kwan so kɔɔ (ne) fie. Ntɛm ara na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no kɔɔ hɔ kɔɔ ne maame nkyɛn kɔkaa sɛ, “Eh! Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no kaa sɛ, “Awiei koraa no!, ɛhe na m’asew no wɔ?” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Me ni, nanabea.” Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no bobɔɔ Python no nsa, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no ​​sɛ, “Ma wo ne wo yere ntra ase anigyeɛ mu.” Bere a anwummere yɛɛ nwini no, ɔne ne yere kɔhome. Saa na ɛkɔɔ so nnawɔtwe. Awiei koraa no ɔkae sɛ, “Me yere, merekɔ m’akuraa ase nanso mɛsan aba.” Ne yere kaa sɛ, “Afei me ne wo bɛkɔ (abom).” Python no kaa sɛ, “Dabi.” Python no kasae na ɔkasae (na ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛdaadaa no sɛ ɔnkɔ), nanso Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no antie (no). Bere a nneɛma daa adi no, ɔne ne kunu sii kwan so. Bere a wɔrekɔ no, Python no yɛɛ nkorabata (fi ɔkwan no so) na ɛkɔɔ kwae no mu. Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no kaa sɛ, “Dɛn na woreyɛ, ɔkwan nie?” Python no kaa sɛ, “Wo deɛ, to w’ano na di m’akyi.” Ɔne ɔne no sii kwan so, na wɔkɔɔ so kɔɔ, na (afei awiei koraa no) Python no yerae wɔ ɔbodan bi mu. Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba nso hyɛnee mu. Ntɛm ara, Python no danee ne ho bɛyɛɛ aboa; afei ɔkasa kyerɛɛ Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no sɛ, “Eyi ne nea meyɛ ankasa, efisɛ mesesaa me ho sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mɛware wo, efisɛ na wotaa tena wo maame de so ne w’agya asen so na wopae mu ka sɛ, ‘Biribiara nni baabiara (misuro)’;Mɛkum wo (afei);abɔ me tirim na makyere nwansena ama me;sɛ woannya bi a, woawie aduan.” Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no tenaa ne nkyɛn, na ɔde ne nsa twitwiw na ɔhwehwɛɛ nwansena. Afei, na wɔwɔ hɔ, bere a Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba huu sɛ Ɔkraman bi retwam no. Na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no rebue n’ano sɛ, "Agya Ɔkɔre," na Ɔkraman no de ne nsa too n'ano. Ɔkraman no faa ne tuo no, siesiee ne nyinaa, na ɔde (asen no) too ne moma so. Python no kaa sɛ, "Twe me ti." Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no kaa sɛ, "Mabrɛ sɛ mɛtwetwe; sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wukum me a, ɛnde kum me." Na Python no reyɛ ama ne ti so akɔkyere Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no, bere a Ɔkraman no maa tuo no teɛɛm sɛ, pomm! Ɔkraman no kɔɔ ɔbodan no mu sɛ ɔrekɔfa Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba ne Python no de wɔn akɔ fie akɔma amanneɛbɔ no sɛnea ɛbaa sɛ wahu Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba ne Python no, na ɔkaa ɔkwan a ɔfaa so kum Python no kyerɛɛ wɔn. Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba nso de n’asɛm no nkyerɛase nyinaa mae. Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no kaa sɛ, "Ɔkraman, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mefa de da wo ase?" Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Nanabea, mempɛ biribiara, m'ankasa mepɛ sɛ menya nnuadewa ngo bi kɛkɛ." Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no kɔfaa mmɛw ngo bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Nana, merefa me dua na mede ahyɛ saa mmɛw-ngo yi mu, na menam saayɛ so ayɛ no sɛnkyerɛnne (a ɛfa saa akwantu yi ho)." Saa na Ɔkɔre dua yɛɛ kɔkɔɔ. Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
