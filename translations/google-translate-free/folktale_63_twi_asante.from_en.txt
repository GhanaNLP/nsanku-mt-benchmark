Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

SƐNEA ABOSOM, ANYAME NKWAA NO BAA ABUSUE NO MU

BERE bi na ɔbɔmmɔfo bi ne ne yere te abɔmmɔfo nsraban mu. Ná wɔyɛ ahiafo, na wɔtraa hɔ koe sɛ wɔbɛtra hɔ. Ná ɔbɔmmɔfo no sɔre kɔ kwae mu—nkwa. Bere a ɔbaa fie na ɔde mpataa afiri sisii asubɔnten no mu, na ɔfaa mpataa ne minnows de brɛɛ ne yere no. Afei na wɔwɔ hɔ, na ne yere bisaa no nsɛm kaa sɛ, “Migye di sɛ sɛ wokum nam a, wode ma, anyɛ saa a wotɔn na wodi (sika a wonya no), enti ɛnnɛ sɛ wokɔ wuram na woamfa nam biara amma a, mɛsan akɔ m’akuraa ase.” Ɔbɔmmɔfo no sii kwan so kɔɔ kwae no mu. Ɔnantew na ɔnantew, wanhu aboa biara. Ɔkaa (kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ), “Oo, mahu amanehunu.” Afei, bere a ɔrehwɛ ne ho no, ohui sɛ Duyker bi nam hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Wo deɛ, Duyker, mɛkum wo.” Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no dii n’akyi, kyiri! kyiri! kyiri! (na ne nan nnyigyei). Duyker no guan kɔɔ dua bi ntini a ɛhyɛ akyi no ntam. Ɔno (Duyker no) kaa sɛ, “Nana Tano, medwane kɔ wo nkyɛn kɔhwehwɛ ahobanbɔ, na ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no agyae me.” Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Me deɛ, mennim adeɛ a wɔfrɛ no ‘Tano’ no, enti fa kɔ sɛ mɛfa wo akɔma me yere.” Tano gyinaa hɔ, na ɔbubuu kasa mu, kaa sɛ, "Hunter, gyae Duyker no na fa *me* kɔ fie, ɛfiri sɛ mfasoɔ pii bɛba wo mu wɔ me mu." Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no gyinaa hɔ, na ɔwosow, biri. Ɔfaa no (Tano) de no too ne ti so. Bere a ɔde no reba no, ohyiaa Owu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Hunter, ɛhe na wode me *okyeame* (kasamafoɔ) rekɔ?" Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no buae sɛ, "Agya, m'ani ayɛ kɔkɔɔ; na merehwehwɛ nam sɛ mɛkɔ akɔma me yere, berɛ a mekɔɔ hɔ kɔhunuu Duyker no, na berɛ a merebɛkum no no, ɔkaa sɛ waguan akɔhwehwɛ ahobanbɔ akɔ saa adeɛ yi so; mekae sɛ mennim ho hwee. Saa adeɛ yi (afei) kaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mede kɔ fie, sɛdeɛ (ɛkaa sɛ) mfasoɔ pii wɔ mu. Owu bisaa Tano nsɛm, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Worekɔ fie hɔ, na wunim sɛ sɛ obi yare, na mefa me hammock, na meba, na mede si ne nkyɛn a, ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara de ne ho gye mu, enti sɛ wokɔ fie a, nea ɛsɛ sɛ meka kyerɛ wo ne sɛ: sɛ obi yare, na meremfa no a, mɛbɔ wo amanneɛ, na wobɛnya biribi de akatua ama me, na sɛnea saa akatua no, . mogya ne nea mepɛ nyinaa, na ebia wubenya honam no." Onyame ketewa no ka kyerɛɛ Owu sɛ, "Nana, mate, owura." Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no faa Tano, na ɔne no baa fie. Bere a Tano gyina hɔ no, ɔto dwom bi, na wɔde wɔn ho bɔɔ nnwontofo kuw no ho. Ade kyee no, bere a nneɛma daa adi, na obi yaree no, wɔde no kɔɔ hɔ kɔɔ Tano. Ɔno (Tano) kaa sɛ, "Wobɛwu." Ná ɛyɛ nokware, owui ampa. (Wɔ foforo nkyɛn) ɔkaa sɛ, "Worenwu, enti fa eyi ne eyi bra." Bere a obi faa nneɛma no, ɔno (onyame ketewa no) kae sɛ, "Mekɔsrɛ nkwa ma wo." Na esiane sɛ wode (ayɛyɛde yi) maa Tano nti, nokwarem no, wo ho tɔɔ wo. Saa na yehu sɛ anyame nketewa no wɔ mfaso; enti nkurɔfo kɔ hɔ kɔ Tano na wogye ne mma no bi fi ne hɔ.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
