SƐNEA ƐBAE SƐ ABƆMBƆFO YƐ AHIAFO

Ɔbɔmmɔfo bi (bere bi) kɔɔ kɔgyee atuo-aduru (powder) ne kɔbere abien a wɔde yɛ atuo, wɔ boseabɔ mu, na ɔne ne yere ne ne ba ketewa no kɔɔ kwae mu sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wobekum nam. Na ɔsii abɔmmɔfoɔ apata na ɛno mu na ɔne ne yere ne ne ba no tenaa. Bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔkɔɔ wuram. Bere a ohuu aboa bi na ɔtow ne tuo guu no so no, ɔpaa no. Da biara na ɛte saa, bɛyɛ nnawɔtwe mprenu. Na ne yere kaa sɛ, “Esiane sɛ minnyaa nam biara a mɛdi nti, mɛkɔ fie.” Ade kyee bere a ɔbɔmmɔfo no kɔɔ wuram no, ne yere ne ne ba nso kɔɔ wɔn akuraa ase. Afei na tuo biako pɛ na aka. Afei me werɛ firii sɛ mɛka sɛ saa ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no din de Gyekye, na nso sɛ ne yere deɛ ne Boniaye-kae-dabi (a ɛkyerɛ sɛ, Da bi-nea-nni-anisɔ-bɛkae). Bere a na ɔbɔmmɔfo no kyinkyin no, na wadu (beae bi) wɔ odum-dua bi ase, na ɛhɔ na na beae fɛfɛ bi wɔ. Ɛhɔ na ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no susuu ho wɔ ne tirim, kaa (kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ), “Sɛ mekɔ fie a wɔbɛgye sika kɔkɔɔ mfuturo ama powder no, na mennyaa bi, enti mɛfa saa tuo yi na mato me menewam na mawu.” Ntɛm ara Biribi frɛɛ no, kaa sɛ, “Ɔbɔadeɛ ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ.” Na ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Agya.” Na Biribi no kaa sɛ, “Adɛn nti na w’ani ayɛ kɔkɔɔ saa?, afei sɛ ɔno a wɔfrɛ no ‘Kɔ-na-sɛe-fie’ no nni wo fie a, anka mɛma wo biribi.” Afei ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Biribiara nni fie.” Biribi kaa sɛ, “Esiane sɛ biribiara nni fie nti, kɔ tokuru a ɛwɔ odum-dua no dua no mu, na adawuru gong mmienu da hɔ, enti bra.” Na ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kɔfaa wɔn baeɛ. Na Biribi kaa sɛ, “Kɔ na twitwa odwen peg mmienu, na to w’ani, na tu mmirika kɔfa baako na fa si hɔ, na fa baako no na dane na fa si hɔ.” Ɔbaa hɔ no, ɔkaa sɛ, “Mawie.” Biribi kaa sɛ, “Fa ’dawuru (gong) kɛseɛ no na bɔ no.” Na ɔbɔɔ no, na kwaeɛ no bɛyɛɛ akuraa a ɛtrɛ wɔ mpaboa mmienu no ntam. Na odum-dua no gyina ne mfimfini. Na sɛ Biribi daa ne ho adi sɛ ɛyɛ Ɔkɔre. Na ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no sɛ sɛ asɛm bi a ɛho hia ba sɛ wɔbɛsiesie a, ɛsɛ sɛ wɔte wɔ odum-dua no ase. Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Ho, mate.” Bere a Ɔkɔre no kaa eyi kyerɛɛ no ​​wiei no, ɔno (ɔbɔmmɔfo no) nyaa ɔyerenom, agyinatufo, ne asomfo. Eduu saa bere no, ne yere (a wɔfrɛ no) “Da bi-nea onni aseda-bɛkae” nyaa hua a ɛkyerɛ sɛ wabɛyɛ ɔpanyin, enti ɔtɔɔ mmɛw-nsã, na ɔde sii kwan so kɔɔ ɔpanyin no akuraa ase, na na ne ba no da n’akyi. Bere a oduu hɔ no ɔbɔɔ nea enti a ɔbae no too gua, na ɔkae sɛ, “Wo ba se ɔde bobesa reba sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛhwɛ w’anim.” Na ɔgyee bobesa no, na ɔmaa obiara a ɔwɔ hɔ no nomee. Na ɔfaa ɔbaa no de no hyɛɛ Gyasehene nsa sɛ ɔmfa no nkɔ ne fie. Bere a anwummere no yɛɛ nwini no, ɔbea no guare kɔɔ baabi a ɔpanyin no wɔ ne dan mu. Ɔbea no ka kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no se, “Me ne wo anya osu ne owia, ɛnnɛ woanya baabi a wobɛtena, na sɛ wosoma kɔfrɛɛ me a, anka ɛrenyɛ yie?” Afei bere a na ɔrekasa saa—te sɛ mmarima ne wɔn nkwaseasɛm ara pɛ—wannya hwee nka. Afei na ɛhɔ na ɔkyeree ɔbea no na ɔne no bom dae. Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu yii no fii ne dan mu wɔ Gyase Chief’s na ɔde no kɔɔ Chief’s harem. Ɛhɔ na ɔtraa hɔ kosii sɛ akasakasa kɛse bi bae. Ɔpanin no ne n’agyinatufoɔ nyinaa kɔtenaa odum-dua no ase. Ɔbaa no ba no gyinaa n’agya anim, na ɔhunuu Ɔkɔre ba no wɔ odum-dua no atifi, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Agya, mepɛ.” Na n’agya bɔɔ n’ase, na ɔpiaa ne ti akyi, na ɔmaa ɔkɔɔ fie. Na mmaa afoforɔ no mma no mu baako baeɛ, na ɔno nso begyinaa n’anim. Ɔsan huu Ɔkɔre ba no, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Agya, mepɛ.” Na ɔbɔɔ n’ase saa ara na ɔpiaa ne ti akyi, maa ɛhwee ase, na bere a ɔrekɔ no, osui. Na ne maame kaa sɛ, “Dɛn na woresu?” Na ɔkaa sɛ, “Agya boroo me.” Na ne maame kaa sɛ, “O!, adɛn nti na ɛnsɛ sɛ ɔhwe wo, sɛ ɛyɛ Da bi-nea-nni-anisɔ-bɛkae no ba a, anka ɔrenhwe no.” Na Da bi-nea-nni-anisɔ-bɛkae-kaa sɛ, “Na eyi deɛ, menyɛɛ wo hwee, sɛ wobɛyɛ me adeɛ saa.” Ɔkaa saa ara pɛ na wɔn baanu nyinaa de ahopere boom ko. Na asomfo no kaa sɛ, “Esiane ade pɔtee yi a ɛwɔ dua yi atifi nti, yɛn agya yerenom reko; Ntɛm ara na wonyaa wɔn agyan, pimpen! pimpen! (ne nnyigyei a wɔyɛe.) Saa bere no na Ɔkɔre no akɔ kyinkyin na wafa ne mma no na wagyaw wɔn wɔ Ɔkraman, ne Ɔkraman Dedaw no nsam. Bere a nkurɔfo no twitwaa dua no na ɛrehwe ase no, Ɔkraman no teɛɛm de nkonyaayi asɛmfua no sɛ, "Pimpeao!" Na dua no boaboaa ano. Na wɔsan kɔɔ so bio. Na Ɔkraman no ka kyerɛɛ Ɔkraman Dedaw no sɛ, "Kɔ hwehwɛ Ɔkɔre na waba abɛhwɛ ne mma, ɛfiri sɛ me deɛ, owuo aba me so." Ntɛm ara na nkurɔfo no kɔfaa wɔn atuo na wɔtoo no tuo kum no. Na Old-Hawk de wimhyɛn kɔe sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛfrɛ Ɔkɔre no. Ɔkwan no nyinaa so bere a ɔrekɔ no ɔteɛɛm sɛ:

"Nana e! wo mma o!

Nanabea e! wo mma o!"

Bere a ɔfrɛɛ saa na Ɔkɔre no ante no, ɔmaa ne nne so teɛɛm sɛ:

"Sankur' e, Anomaa e, sankur' e, Anomaa, Ɔkɔre."

Sankur' e, Anomaa no e, sankur' e, sɛ woakɔ adidi a, san kɔ."

Na Ɔkɔre no tee. Na ɔbaa hɔ, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Dɛn na eyi te saa?” Na Old-Hawk kaa sɛ, "Wɔabɔ dua no agu sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a wɔbɛfa wo mma, na Lizard deɛ, wɔato tuo agu no so akum no." Ntɛm ara na Ɔkɔre no sɔree, wa (ne nnyigyei a ne ntaban yɛ), na ɔsiane wɔ dua bi so wɔ kuro no ano, na ɔmaa n’ani so, na ɛyɛ nokware sɛ wɔretwa dua no. Wanka hwee. Na afei bere a dua no hwee fam na esian no, Ɔkɔre no de ahopere kɔɔ hɔ, ho (na ɛyɛ ne ntaban nnyigyei), na ɔfaa ne mma no. Ɔfaa wɔn de wɔn guu dua a osii fam no so. Na afei ɔsiane baa fam bɛka kyerɛɛ ne ho (lit. wɔ ne tirim sɛ), "Ah, mmarima te sɛ yei, ɛnte saa?" Na ɛmaa odii awerɛhow. Na ɔsui, na ɔkaa sɛ:

"Gye sɛ tuo no, Gye sɛ ɔwɔ no."

Na ɔtetew n’ase, na ɔnyaa mmaa sankuo mmienu, na ɔbɔɔ wɔn de nkra yi kɔmaa:

"Ma abɔde a wɔwɔ ntakra nyinaa mmra, (kɔsi sɛ wim bɛyɛ) tuntum, tuntum, tuntum."

Na abɔde a wɔwɔ ntakra nyinaa bae. Na ɔkaa nea asi no nyinaa kyerɛɛ wɔn, na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wɔne no kɔ kɔda Gyekye ase, na ɛsɛ sɛ obiara a ɔnim su. Anomaa a wɔfrɛ no Apitie no na odii kan fii ase sui sɛ:

"Mɛwu mpofirim!

Mewu mpofirim!"

Apatupere anomaa no teɛɛm sɛ:

"M'ani nnye akasakasa ho!

M’ani nnye akasakasa ho!

M'ani nnye Kosante ba ntama ho."

Akokofwedee, ɔkraman a ne nan yɛ kɔkɔɔ no teɛɛm sɛ:

"Owuraye ne hoe, hoe, O!

Wuuraye hoe, hoe, O!

Owuraye hoe, hoe, hoe, O!" Atewa anomaa ketewa no teɛɛm sɛ:

"Doye!"

Doye!

Afua Anwea ne ne (kunu) Ano Kofi, Sɛ abofra nantew sɛ wɔawo no ara pɛ a, ɛno nyɛ adepa, Kwaku Amoaten (din bi).

Doye!

Doye!

Odiawuo ne ne Ano Mmirikisie a ɔsansan Asono ntini no, Wagyae, Wagyae.

Agya ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ, sɔre na kɔhwehwɛ bonsu a ne hwene yɛ putty no sɛ wobɛtumi ayɛ soup na woanom, (bere a ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ yere kasa saa,)

Abɔde biara (a ɔte sɛ ɔka saa) renkɔ dua no ase (baabi a wɔwɔ)."

(Hwɛ) saa abofra ketewa no tuo tuntum tenten no, Ɔrekyerɛ me, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛwe me aka ne plantains a wɔayam no ho."

Opatuo, Ɔkraman no teɛɛm sɛ:

"E! Mm!

"Obeko Asamoa, Sɛ ɔkwasea a ɔyɛ mmerɛw no bɔ ne mmɛw-nsã dua a, anɔpatutuutu nkurɔfo rekɔ akyi ne wɔn anim akɔ so!"

Aburuburo, Aborɔnoma no teɛɛm sɛ:

"Bere a na maame rewu no, ogyaw me sɛ mehwɛɛ nkorabata bi a wɔde fork ayɛ so;

Bere a nkorabata a ɛwɔ nkorabata no wui no, egyaw me sɛ mehwɛɛ mmɛw ahaban bi mpampam so;

Bere a mmɛw ahaban no mpampam wui no, ɛmaa mehwɛɛ nkorabata bi a wɔabobɔw so;

Ɛyɛ me, Ɛyɛ me, Ɛyɛ me." Na afei Ɔkɔre no ankasa bae, na ɔteɛɛm sɛ:

"Nkensen nkensen, abɔde a wɔwɔ ntakra ya ya!
