Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

SƐNEA (NKYIA NO) "KURONTO" NE "BURU" BAA BƐDE DI DWUMA

BERE bi na ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ bi (a wɔfrɛ no) Kwaku wɔ hɔ, ne ne yere (a wɔfrɛ no Buruwa, na sɛdeɛ na wɔyɛ ahiafoɔ no, wɔsiim sɛ wɔrekɔyɛ atenaeɛ foforɔ. Kwaku, ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no, berɛ a ɔkɔɔ kwaeɛ mu no, okum eku bonsu bi, nanso berɛ a ɔde no san baa fie hɔ no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Eyi nyɛ papa (ma aduane)." Enti ɔkɔtɔn. Bere nyinaa sɛ okum biribi a, ɛyɛ saa na Ne yere deɛ, wanhunu sika no da, na ɔbisaa no sɛ, "Wo mfa sika nsan mmra, na womfa nam nsan mmra, na ne nyinaa nteaseɛ ne dɛn, na obuae sɛ, "Sɛ mekɔ, na nkurɔfoɔ rentɔ (nam no), na ɛbɛyɛ porɔwee a, meto guu kwan so, na ne yere nso dii n'akyi hwɛ no. Afei ɔhunuu sɛ ɔtɔn saa bonsuo no, na ɔtɔɔ kukuo bi a wɔde mmɛw nsa (a wɔfrɛ no dɔnto) na ɔnom Afei ankyɛ na Kwaku, ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no nso baeɛ palm wine)." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na wubua me saa kwan yi so?" Obuae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuae a ɛfata ma nea ɛwɔ wo tirim." Ɔbɔmmɔfo no anka hwee. Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma daa adi no, ɔkɔɔ afuw no mu na ɔsan bae; ɔkae sɛ, "Anwummere pa." Obuae sɛ, "Ya Buru." Buruwa kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wubua (me nkyia) wɔ saa kwan yi so?" The ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ buaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuaeɛ a ɛfata ma deɛ ɛwɔ wo tirim."Saa na ɛbɛyɛɛ amanne wɔ abusuakuo no mu sɛ wɔde wɔn mfitiaseɛ ka ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ, Kwaku, ne ne yere, Buruwa san bra me nkyɛn.
