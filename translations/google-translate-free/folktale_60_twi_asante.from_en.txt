Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

SƐNEA (NKYIA NO) "KURONTO" NE "BURU" BAA BƐDE DI DWUMA

BERE bi na ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ bi (a wɔfrɛ no) Kwaku wɔ hɔ, ne ne yere (a wɔfrɛ no Buruwa, na sɛdeɛ na wɔyɛ ahiafoɔ no, wɔsiim sɛ wɔrekɔyɛ atenaeɛ foforɔ. Kwaku, ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no, berɛ a ɔkɔɔ kwaeɛ mu no, okum eku bonsu bi, nanso berɛ a ɔde no san baa fie hɔ no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Eyi nyɛ papa (ma aduane)." Enti ɔkɔtɔn. Bere nyinaa sɛ okum biribi a, ɛyɛ enti. Nanso, ne yere anhunu sika no da, na obisaa no se, "Womfa sika nsan mmra, na womfa nam nsan mmra, na ne nyinaa asem ne dεn?" Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Sɛ mekɔ, na nkurɔfoɔ ntɔ (nam no), na ɛporɔw a, meto gu." Da bi ɔkumm biribi, na ɔde sii kwan no so, na ne yere dii n’akyi sɛ ɔrebɛhwɛ no. Afei ɔhunuu sɛ ɔtɔn saa bonsu no, faa sika no, na ɔtɔɔ kukuo a wɔde nnuadewa bobesa (a wɔfrɛ no dɔnto) nom. Afei ne yere san kɔɔ fie. Ɛno akyi bere tiaa bi no, Kwaku, ɔbɔmmɔfo no nso bae. Bere a oduu fie hɔ ara pɛ na ɔkae sɛ, "Anwummere pa, Buruwa." Obuae sɛ, "Ya Ekudonto (Yiw, Bonsu ma kuku a wɔde nnuadewa bobesa ayɛ)." Ɔkaa sɛ, “Adɛn nti na wobua me saa kwan yi so?” Obuae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuae a ɛfata ma nea ɛwɔ wo tirim." Ɔbɔmmɔfo no anka hwee. Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma daa adi no, ɔkɔɔ afuw no mu san bae; ɔkaa sɛ, "Anwummere pa." Obuae se, "Ya Buru." Buruwa kaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na wobua (me nkyia) wɔ saa kwan yi so?" Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no buaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuaeɛ a ɛfata ma deɛ ɛwɔ wo tirim." Saa na ɛbɛyɛɛ amanne wɔ abusuakuw no mu sɛ wɔde nkyia "Ya Kuronto" ne "Ya Buru" bedi dwuma. Wɔde wɔn mfiase ka ɔbɔmmɔfo, Kwaku, ne ne yere, Buruwa. Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
