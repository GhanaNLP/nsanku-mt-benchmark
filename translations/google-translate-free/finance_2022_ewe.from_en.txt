5 | Ghana Dziɖuɖu - Ƒe 2022 Dukɔmeviwo ƒe Gazazã

“Dukɔ si me Asitsalawo le Si Li Eteƒe Didi Tutu: Ganyawo ƒe Ðekawɔwɔ Kple Dɔwɔɖuiwo Dodo”

NYAGBLƆÐI

COVID-19 dɔvɔ̃a gblẽ nu le ganyawo katã ŋu, Ghana hã le eme. Kuxi si Ghanatɔwo to to amewo ƒe agbe, agbemenuhiahiãwo kple asitsanyawo ƒe bublu me la mebu ɖe Dziɖuɖua dzi o. Enye dada nam be magblɔ be míaƒe ŋuɖoɖo tsɔ kpɔ dɔvɔ̃a ƒe ŋusẽkpɔɖeamedzi gbɔ la nye dzideƒo kple nyametsotso eye wokpɔ dzidzɔ ɖe eŋu le xexeame katã. Dziɖuɖua hã dze ɖoɖowo gɔme be woakpe ɖe asitsalawo ŋu woagafɔ agbe eye míaƒe agbenɔnɔ nagaɖo nɔnɔme si sɔ me.

Gawu la, le ŋɔdzidoname kple mɔnukpɔkpɔ siwo do tso dɔvɔ̃a me ta la, Dziɖuɖua, le H.E. Dukplɔla Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo, wɔ COVID-19 ƒe Dɔwɔnawo Dzi Ðeɖe Akpɔtɔ Kple Wo Gbugbɔgaɖoanyi ƒe ɖoɖo si ƒe home nye GH¢100 biliɔn kabakaba, (GhanaCARES “Obaatan Pa” ɖoɖo). Esia he gbɔdzɔe si ɖe dzesi vɛ na míaƒe amewo enumake eye wòle ŋusẽ dom míaƒe dukɔa.

Míekpɔ ŋudzedze ɖe miaƒe xɔse me léle ɖe asi na Dziɖuɖua ŋu esi míele dɔ wɔm be míakpɔ egbɔ be wohaya sesĩe tso dɔvɔ̃a me gɔ̃ hã. To ɣeyiɣi sia me la, wodo míaƒe tenɔnɔ ɖe nɔnɔme sesẽwo nu abe dukɔ ene kpɔ. Biblia naa mɔfiame si hiã la ɣesiaɣi le ɣeyiɣi siawo tɔgbe me. Vevietɔ, abe alesi wogblɔe le Yesaya 9:10 ene be, “Anyikpewo mu, gake míatsɔ kpe siwo wofa la atu, woŋe aŋɔ, ke míatsɔ sedertiwo aɖo wo teƒe”. Le mɔ ma ke nu la, Dziɖuɖua hã ɖoe kplikpaa nenema ke be yeawɔ ɖe yeƒe ŋugbedodowo dzi. Le susu sia ta la, míayi edzi awɔ ɖoɖowo kple ɖoɖo siwo wotu ɖe anyigba siwo katã bu la gbugbɔgaɖoanyi dzi eye míana dukɔmeviwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ nanyo ɖe edzi.

Taɖodzinu vevi siwo le ƒe 2022 ƒe Gaŋutiɖoɖoa me dometɔ ɖeka, eye le nyateƒe me la, le ƒe etɔ̃ (3) siwo gbɔna me la, enye be woatu Dɔwɔha si anɔ anyi ɖaa si ate ŋu ana ganyawo ƒe ŋgɔyiyi si li ke, si anɔ anyi ɖaa, eye wòaxɔ amewo katã ɖe eme be wòahe dɔ nyui wɔwɔ vɛ na amesiame, vevietɔ Sɔhɛwo. Eyata dziɖuɖu ƒe ɖoɖowo atrɔ ɖe kpekpeɖeŋu nana sɔhɛwo be woawɔ woƒe ŋutetewo ŋudɔ eye woaɖe woƒe ŋutetewo afia be woakpɔ egbɔ be nudzedziname to mɔnukpɔkpɔ sɔsɔe nana amesiame me.

Be woado sɔhɛwo ƒe kakaɖedzi ɖe ŋgɔ eye woatu woƒe asitsatsa ƒe gbɔgbɔ ɖo la, Dziɖuɖua awɔ YouStart ɖoɖoa ŋudɔ le ƒe 2022 me be wòana mɔnukpɔkpɔ sɔhɛ asitsalawo be woaɖo woƒe dɔwɔƒea alo akeke wo ɖe enu.

Le ƒe 2022 ƒe Gaŋutiɖoɖoa me la, míegblɔ ɖoɖo siwo wowɔ be míayi edzi akpɔ ganyawo ƒe kuxi siwo do mo ɖa enumake gbɔ eye míado ŋusẽ wu le dɔvɔ̃a megbe ɣeyiɣia me. Dziɖuɖua tsɔ eɖokui na be yeawɔ ɖoɖo siwo me kɔ eye wowɔ ɖeka be woana aɖaŋuwo eye woado asitsatsa ɖe ŋgɔ le sɔhɛwo dome. Esi míele Dukplɔla la ƒe...

Gazazã ƒe gbɔdzɔgbɔdzɔ: Esia dzɔna ne Dziɖuɖua ƒe gazazãwo sɔ gbɔ wu gakpɔkpɔ.

Gazazã: Esia nye ga si wozãna ɖe xɔtuɖoɖo gãwo abe mɔdodowo, sukuwo, kɔdziwo, tɔdzisasrãwo, ʋuɖoɖo, tsi ƒe ɖoɖowo, dɔwɔƒewo kple mɔ̃wo kple bubuawo ŋu.

Dɔwɔlawo ƒe Fexexe: Esia nye fetu kple gakpekpeɖeŋu siwo ku ɖe fetu ŋu abe hadomekpekpeɖeŋunana, femaxee kple dzudzɔxɔxɔledɔme ƒe fetu siwo woxena na dukɔa ƒe dɔwɔlawo.

Ŋgɔyidɔwo (DPs): Esia fia dukɔwo kple/alo habɔbɔ siwo wɔa dɔ aduadu kple dukɔ madeŋgɔwo be woaɖo woƒe ŋgɔyiyi ƒe taɖodzinuwo gbɔ. DPwo ateŋu anye dukɔ geɖe, e.g. Dukɔwo Dome Ganyawo Gbɔkpɔha (IMF), Xexeame ƒe Gadzraɖoƒegã, Afrika Ŋgɔyidɔwo ƒe Gadzraɖoƒe (AfDB) alo dukɔ eve siwo me dukɔ ɖekaɖekawo abe Germany, United Kingdom kple United States of America (USA) le.

Ƒe Nuwuwu ƒe Ga ƒe Ga ƒe Dzidzedze: Ga ƒe asixɔxɔ ƒe agbɔsɔsɔ si wokpɔ le December me le ƒe ɖeka si ŋu wobu.

IPEP (Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme): Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme (IPEP) nye NPP Dziuu e oo veviwo dometɔ ɖeka si ƒe taɖodzinue nye be yeaɖe ahedada ɖa eye yeaɖe tasɔsɔmasɔ dzi akpɔtɔ, vevietɔ le kɔƒenutowo me kple nuto siwo me hiãtɔwo le me to xɔtuɖoɖo veviwo nana me.
