Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

DƐN NTI A APUPUO (FRESH-WATER MUSSEL), A ƐMAA N’ASUBƆN SEN NO NO NOKWARE DA ASUSUW NO PO SO

WƆKA sɛ Okawa, Opitire, ‘Bonko, Shrimp, ne nsu mu abɔde nyinaa (na wɔte faako) na ɔpɛ bere kɛse bi bae na nsubɔnten no nyinaa wee, na ɛde saa abɔde yi werɛ howee kɛse. Wɔda anhwea no so (wɔ asubɔnten no mu). Awiei koraa no wɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛdefa saa asɛmmɔne a yɛahu yi ho no, momma yɛnkɔ hɔ nkɔ Nyankonpon, Wim nyame no nkyɛn, na yɛnsu nkɔka nkyerɛ no sɛ ebia ɔbɛma osu atɔ yɛn." Abɔde no nyinaa sii kwan so. Na Okawa maa ne nne so (na ɔtoo dwom sɛ):

" Ɛnyɛ me, Oka' Abena, seesei metu kɔ ha, seesei metu kɔ hɔ?

Mihuruw na migyina me nansoaa so sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mɛfrɛ Wo.

Meyi aburobia nhwiren a ɛwɔ me nansoaa so no.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔyɛ me yere, Abuya."

Nanso osu no antɔ. Na Opitire nso, baa hɔ bɛtoo dwom sɛ:

" Ɛnyɛ me, Opitre Abena, seesei meretu akɔtena ha, seesei meretu akɔtena hɔ?

Mihuruw na migyina me nansoaa so sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mɛfrɛ Wo.

Meyi aburobia nhwiren a ɛwɔ me nansoaa so no.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔyɛ me yere, Abuya."

Nanso osu no antɔ. Na ‘Bonko nso, baa hɔ bɛtoo dwom sɛ:

" Ɛnyɛ me, 'Bonko Abena, seesei metu kɔ ha, seesei metu kɔ hɔ?

Mihuruw na migyina me nansoaa so sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mɛfrɛ Wo.

Meyi aburobia nhwiren a ɛwɔ me nansoaa so no.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔyɛ me yere, Abuya."

Nanso osu no antɔ. Abɔde no nyinaa bae bɛsui, na Apupuo (Fresh-water mussel) nkutoo na ɛkaa hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, " Ma me kwan ma mensu kakra." Wokaa se, " Fi adi, yen su no nyɛɛ papa biara, na dɛn na wohwɛ kwan sɛ wobɛnya wɔ wo de no so? " Ɔkaa sɛ, " Ma me kwan ma mensu." Nanso wɔboroo no kae sɛ wɔaka akyerɛ no dedaw sɛ ebia ɔrensu. Nanso Fresh-water mussel no kaa sɛ, " Mɛsu." Enti wɔkaa sɛ, " Munsu kɔ." Na ɔmaa (dwom no so sɛ):

" Ɛnyɛ me, Apupuo Abena, seesei metu kɔ ha, seesei metu kɔ hɔ?

Mihuruw na migyina me nansoaa so sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mɛfrɛ Wo.

Meyi aburobia nhwiren a ɛwɔ me nansoaa so no.

Aberefi Sempa, ɔyɛ me yere, Abuya."

Na Wim-nyame no bɔɔ ne aprannaa, kukuru! Abɔdeɛ no nyinaa kaa sɛ, " Kɔ so, kɔ so." Na ɔsan sui, na Wim nyame no yɛɛ n’anim basabasa. Na afei abɔdeɛ no maa no so wɔ wɔn mmati so kaa sɛ, " Kɔ so bio." Na bio ɔmaa saa ara so (refrain). Na afei Wim-nyame no gyaee (osuo no), na ɛbɔɔ fam, ta! ta! ta! ta! na nsubɔnten no nyinaa abura. Afei oyi (afei deɛ) sɔree, na, twom! kɔɔ nsu no mu (bere a wohuruw kɔɔ mu no). Abɔde no nyinaa kɔɔ mu Apupuo (Fresh-water mussel), bere a anka ɔbɛhyɛn nsu no mu no, wɔpam no san n’akyi (wɔn a wɔaka no), na ɔdaa asubɔnten no ano. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Apupuo a ɔmaa n'asubɔnten sen no da mpoano." Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
