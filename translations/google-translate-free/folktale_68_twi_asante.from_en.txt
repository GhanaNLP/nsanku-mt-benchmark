Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

SƐNEA ANANSE, AKWANKYERƐ, BAAYƐ OHIAFO

KWAKU ANANSE, Akɔre no, kɔɔ hɔ kɔɔ Nyankonpon, Wim nyame no nkyɛn, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Ma me atokoɔ baako na ma memfa nnipa akuraa mũ nyinaa mmrɛ wo (de sesa).” Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, “Montumi.” Akɔre no buae sɛ, “Sɛ mentumi a, meka wo maame Nsia ntam, ɛnde mede me ti bɛtua me ntam a wɔabu so no ho ka.” Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, “Gye no (atoko aba) na kɔ.” Akɔre no sii kwan so kɔɔ akuraa pɔtee bi ase. Ɔkaa asɛm a edi so yi kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no. “Wim-nyame no asoma me, ɔka sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ mefa atoko aba biako yi na me ne no kɔ baabi, na sɛ meba (baabiara) a, ɛsɛ sɛ mema atoko aba yi ne nnomaa no da.” Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, “E!, sɛ nnomaa ne atoko da bom a, ɛbɛyɛ yiye anaa?” Ananse buae sɛ, “Nea ɛsɛ sɛ woyɛ ara ne sɛ wobɛma wɔn kwan ma wɔabom ada.” Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, “Kɔ na fa gu hɔ.” Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu Ananse kae sɛ ɔrekɔhwehwɛ, nanso atoko (atoko biako) no nni hɔ bio. Akɔre no kaa sɛ, “Ti-nipa, nnomaa awe Wim-nyame no atoko.” Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, “Me ntumi nnya akasakasa biara wɔ yei ho, enti fa nnomaa no kɔ.” Ananse kɔfaa nnomaa kɛseɛ baako, na ɔne no sii kwan so kɔɔ akuraa foforɔ ase. Bere a oduu hɔ no, ɔkaa asɛm bi kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no, na ɔkae sɛ, “Wim nyame no somaa me, na mɛda ha, na nnomaa a mekura mu yi ne nguan no bɛda.” Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, “Fa bra na fa to hɔ.” Anadwo no, Ananse kɔɔ nguan kraal no nkyɛn kɔkumm nnomaa no. Ɔfaa nnomaa no dwensɔtwaa de guu odwennini kɛse bi mmɛn so. Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu no, ɔkɔɔ ɔpanyin a ɔwɔ hɔ no nkyɛn kɔkae sɛ, "Ma me nnomaa no." Bere a anka wɔpɛ sɛ wɔkɔfa kɔfa ma no no, hwɛ, nnomaa no awu. Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ah! ɛno ne Wim-nyame nnomaa, a nguan no akum no." Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Me ntumi nnya akasakasa biara wɔ ho, enti fa oguan na kɔ." Ananse faa nguan no na ɔsiim. Oduu akuraa foforo ase. Ɔkɔɔ ɔpanyin no fie. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Asɛm bɛn?" Akɔre no buae sɛ, "Wim nyame no asoma me, na mekura nguan no; ɛsɛ sɛ ɔne anantwi no bom da." Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Ho! ma ɛnhome nguan no so." Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "Dabi, ɛfiri sɛ yei ne Wim-nyame no nguan soronko no, ɛda anantwi no nkyɛn." Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na fa to hɔ, afei." Akɔre no kɔe na ɔde too hɔ. Anadwo no, Ananse kɔkumm nguan no, na ɔde ne mogya guu anantwi no mu baako ti. Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu no, Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɔpanyin, ma me me nguan." Wɔkɔɔ kraal no nkyɛn; na nguan no wɔ hɔ, a wɔawuwu. Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ti-nipa, ɛyɛ Wim nyame nguan a anantwi akunkum wɔn." Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Me ne wo ntumi nnya akasakasa biara wɔ asɛm yi ho, enti fa nantwi a okum no no kɔ." Ananse faa nantwi no, na ɔsiim, na ɔbaa akuraa foforɔ ase. Ɔfaa nantwi no kyekyeree no wɔ hɔ, na ɔdaa. Bere a ɔda hɔ no, wɔbaa bɛka kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no sɛ ne nkoa no mu biako awu. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ wotow no gu, efisɛ minni funnaka." Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, mepɛ, ɛfiri sɛ Wim-nyame no ka kyerɛɛ me sɛ sɛ menya funu bi a wɔrebɛtow agu a, ɛsɛ sɛ mesesa no ma nantwi." Akuraa no asefo kaa sɛ, "Dabi." (Nanso) Ananse (si so dua), kaa sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ wode brɛ me, na sɛ wokɔ tɔn nantwi no a wobɛnya sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma a ne boɔ yɛ asuanu (i.e. £4), na wobɛtumi afa (dodoɔ yi) na woatɔ akoa foforɔ." Nkurɔfo no kaa sɛ, "Nokware wɔ ɛno mu." Enti wɔyɛɛ nsakrae no so nkɛntɛnso. Ananse faa funu no na ɔsiim, na ɔbaa akuraa foforɔ ase. Bere a oduu hɔ no, ɔkasa kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no sɛ, "Ma me ba yi kwan ma ɔne wo mma nkɔda, wɔ pato (branda dan a wɔabue mu) yi mu; afei (ɛsɛ sɛ meka kyerɛ wo) abofra yi ho nyɛ fɛ, ɔwɔ su sɛ ɔbɛbubu mframa anadwo, enti sɛ ɔbubu mframa a, ma mmofra (afoforo) no nhwe no. Afei na ɔbarima no awu nnansa. Mmofra no ne ɔno ( funu no) da. Afei wɔtee nka sɛ biribi a ɛyɛ hu. Wɔkaa sɛ, “Saa abofra no abubu mframa, momma yɛnhwe no.” Wɔhwee ne so, titim! titim! titim! (na wɔn akuturuku no nnyigyei). Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu no, Ananse kɔfrɛɛ ne ba. Ɔkɔe no, ɛhɔ na na ɔwɔ, wawu. Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ti-nipa, wo mma akum Wim nyame somafoɔ a na ɔka me ho no." Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, "Me ntumi nnya akasakasa biara wɔ asɛm yi ho, enti wobɛtumi de mmofra no akɔ." Akɔre no buae sɛ, "(Me ntumi nnye ntom) gye sɛ wo, ti-nipa, ne nnipa a wɔwɔ kurow yi mu nyinaa kɔ Wim-nyame no anim, kɔka w'asɛm." Ɔpanyin no bɔɔ 'dawuro gong no, na ɔbɔɔ dawuru sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ mo nyinaa kɔ Wim nyame no anim wɔ ha, sɛ mokɔka yɛn asɛm no." Akɔre no dii anim; nkurɔfo no nyinaa dii n’akyi. Bere a Ananse duu kurow no akyi no, ɔsomaa ɔbɔfo bi sɛ ɔnkɔka nkyerɛ Wim-nyame no sɛ ɔne kurow bi mufo reba. Wim-nyame no frɛɛ ne baguafo, na ɔtraa ase (n’ahemfie mpanyimfo mu). Bere a Ananse retu kwan akɔ mu no, ɔmaa (ne dwom) so sɛ: "Ananse, awerɛhosɛm, awerɛhosɛm, minyaa atoko atoko biako sɛ mɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ nnomaa bi."

Ananse, awerɛhosɛm, awerɛhosɛm, mefaa nnomaa no sɛ merekɔgye oguan.

Mefaa nguan no na mekɔɔ kɔfaa nantwi, mefaa nantwi no kɔfaa funu, mefaa funu no kɔfaa kuro bi mu nnipa."

Obiara kaa sɛ, "Wote dwom a Ananse reto no?" Ananse maa ne dwom no so prɛko bio:

"Ananse, awerɛhosɛm, awerɛhosɛm, menyaa atoko aba baako sɛ mɛkɔ akɔgye nnomaa."

Ananse, awerɛhosɛm, awerɛhosɛm, mefaa nnomaa no sɛ merekɔgye oguan.

Mefaa nguan no na mekɔɔ kɔfaa nantwi, mefaa nantwi no kɔfaa funu, mefaa funu no kɔfaa kuro bi mu nnipa."

Ananse duu Wim-nyame no anim, na Wim-nyame no bisaa Ananse asɛm no. Ananse kaa sɛ, "Atokoɔ baako pɛ na me nsa kaa, sɛ me ne kuro bi mufoɔ bɛba; kuro bi mu nnipa gyina hɔ." Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Monyi no ayɛ." (Wɔteɛɛm sɛ,) "E! e! e!" Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mede ma wo sɛ aseda afɔre?" Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "Mempɛ biribiara; (beae) wɔ nnua no so ne nea mepɛ nyinaa." Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Oh, oh! saa asɛmti yi (me deɛ), wobɛwu wɔ ohia mu, wo, a wokɔɔ na wode pii baeɛ, nanso ka sɛ wompɛ biribiara." Ɛno nti na ohia rentwam Ananse da.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
