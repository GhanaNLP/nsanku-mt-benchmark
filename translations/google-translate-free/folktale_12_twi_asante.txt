KWAKU ANANSE one and his children, Tikonokono, Afudotwedotwe, and Nyiwankonfwea lived with Kotoko at home. And Kotoko 'went to the field, and Kwaku Ananse laughed at him to give him some to drink. And he went and gave her some. And when Kwaku Ananse and his children slept, Kotoko went 'home to eat. And now Kotoko came, and he went and took off his ear and put it down' and he sang: "Gyensaworowa, Kotoko saworowa, Gyensaworowa, Kotoko saworowa, Gyensaworowa. The ears doo slightly. Enie is a single pony and Kotoko hides the ear. And Kwaku Ananse saw it. He said, "This is the ear I have seen, tomorrow morning I will come and take it and do my work. Ampa'a in the morning go, go, go, and Ananse will go; ode koo and the farm' Ananse 'didn't know that we were making him stop, and he raised: "Gyensaworowa, Kotoko saworowa, Gyensaworowa, Kotoko saworowa, Gyensaworowa. The dog's do'a and edo. And edo fights the ho'a. Now it's 'Bosom Pom' From there ene Aburokyire, and Mmorafuo faye, and we fwee so, did some. Ene se ears will come to Asantemanm' many.
