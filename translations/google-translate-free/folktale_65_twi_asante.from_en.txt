Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

SƐNEA AKETEKYIRE, KRIKƐT NO, AHYƐ NE NSƐM

AKETEKYIRE, Kriket, ne Ananse, Akɔre, fii ase, na wɔkae sɛ wɔrekɔhwehwɛ atoko sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wobedua. Na wonyaa atoko aba abien pɛ. Na Ananse kaa sɛ, "Merekɔ akɔdua me deɛ." Na Kriket no kaa sɛ, "Merebɛto me deɛ no sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a mɛwe no." Ananse kɔduaa ne de. Kriket no, ɔno nso, faa ne de, na ɔde too ogya no so. Na ɛkyɛe wɔ aduannoa mu, na ɔtenaa hɔ (ne nkyɛn). Akɔre no nso, ɔkɔɔ afuo no mu; na ɔrebɛhwɛ kotoku a ɛkata atoko no so no ase. Atoko no kaa sɛ, "Nhwɛ m'ani so, na wo nsateaa ankɔ m'ani so." Na Ananse redan n’ani ahwɛ ne Ɔkraman; ne Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Ɛnsɛ sɛ wohwɛ me, ɛnyɛ me na meyɛɛ saa." Ananse rebɛbubu dua bi sɛ ɔrebɛboro Ɔkraman no, na dua no kaa sɛ, "Nbubu me, ɛfiri sɛ (sɛ woyɛ saa a) wo Ɔkraman bɛka me." Ananse baa fie bɛkaa ne nyinaa kyerɛɛ Kriket no. Bere a ɔreserew no, ɔhwee ogya no mu. Saa na Cricket no maa ne sẽ hyewee. Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
