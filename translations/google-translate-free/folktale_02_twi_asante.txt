SHORT AND SHORT

MPITIRIPI and Mpatrapa, and Anyankama Kyerebi, and Kyerebi-Koso-Adawurampon, and we bowed to you for thirty days. And we said, “No one tells ’sahuro tricks.” And Father ’Nanse went to the ’sahuro atafere, and we said, “Take it.”

Ananse said, “What is my father?” And we said, “You say, Mpitiripi and Mpatrapa and Anyankama Kyerebi and Kyerebi-Koso-Adawurampon and we bowed for thirty times, and we said, no one should lie, and he lied, so we said, ‘take it’.” And we fall on Ananse, and we take, and ohuruye, and padie in kado!

So he said, never you see Ananse and he’s in a padi, but eka’ is, but he’s walking on the ground like Koto.

My legend, whether it is good or bad, just take it and give it to me.
