ANNOUNCEMENT OF THE PRESIDENT

Akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho sɛ afe yi bɛyɛ afe a ɔman yi kɔɔ sikasɛm mu ahokyere pa ara wɔ yɛn abakɔsɛm mu. Sɛdeɛ ɛkɔɔ so wɔ ewiase aman ahodoɔ soɔ no, yadeɛ kontonkurowi COVID-19 to hyɛɛ yɛn so wɔ afe 2020 ɔfa a ɛdi kan no mu ma sikadwuma tɔɔ ape nam so ma sika a ɛba ɔman fotoɔ mu no so teeɛ na afei nso yɛbɔɔ ɛka a na yɛn ani nna de ko tiaa yadeɛ kontonkurowi yi.

Ɛberɛ a yɛhyɛɛ aseɛ tuu Nsakraeɛ Pa Nhyehyɛeɛ ho anammɔn na yɛsusuu sɛ afei deɛ, yɛrebɔ yɛn ho mmɔden ade yɛn ho afiri yadeɛ kontonkurowi yi ho nsunsuansoɔ mu ara pɛ na ɔhaw afoforɔ bɛbɔɔ mu a, ɛnam so ama nneɛma a ɛma sikasɛm gyina yie a yɛde totoo yie firi afe 2017 kɔsi 2019 mu no nyinaa asane agye agu. Deɛ ɛdi kan yɛ Russia ne Ukraine ɔko no, deɛ ɛdi hɔ nso yɛ ɛberɛ a yɛsɛee wɔ akwanhodoɔ a yɛbɛfa so anya sika wɔ 2022 sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ mu no nnyetom ho a, ɛnam so maa amansan boseabɔfoɔ de too edwa sɛ yɛmfata sɛ wɔbɔ yɛn bosea. Afei, bɛyɛ mfeɛ edu nie, prɛko pɛ Amansan Boseabɔ ho Edwadifoɔ mpɛ sɛ wɔbɛma yɛn bosea, ɔman yi ara mu boseabɔ ho nsɛm nso bɛyɛɛ basaa ma nsiho boɔ kɔɔ soro pa ara na ɛnam so ma boseabɔ ho ɛka kɔɔ soro a, ama yɛn sika mu duru no kɔ ara na ɛrekɔ fam.

Yei kyerɛ sɛ, ɛberɛ a amannɔne sika a yɛnya firi amansan boseabɔ edwa so ne sika a ɛfiri adwadeɛ a yɛde kɔ amannɔne mu no so reteɛ, na afei ɛsiane sɛdeɛ yɛpɛ amannɔne nneɛma pa ara enti, sika a yɛde kra nneɛma firi amannɔne no nso kɔ ara na ɛrekɔ soro, na amannɔne ɛka a yɛde no nso kɔ ara na ɛrekɔ soro no, na yɛn sika mu duru no so te ara na ɛreteɛ. Yei ama asetena mu ayɛ den ama Aban ne ankorankoro nyinaa. Fango boɔ a ɛgu so rekɔ soro enti, ama nneɛma boɔ ayɛ den a bosome Ahineme mu wɔ afe 2022 mu, nneɛma-boɔ-sorokɔ kɔduruu ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduanan ne akyire pɔ ɛnan (40.4% in October 2022).

Yɛakɔ nsɔhwɛ a ano yɛ den pa ara mu na Aban nim ɔhaw a saa tebea yi de abɛto mo so. Aban de nnaseɛ a emu dɔ ma akuhodoɔ

INTRODUCTION

1. Afe 2023 Ɔman Mma Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ yi yɛ dwuma a Aban dii wɔ 2022 mu no ho tɔfabɔ ne sika dodoɔ a Aban hwɛ anim sɛ ɔbɛnya de atu ne nhyehyɛeɛ ne ne dwumadie ahodoɔ ho anammɔn wɔ afe 2023 mu. Asɛntire a ɛda Ɔman Mma Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ yi so ne "Yɛreka abom adi dwuma de Mpontuo ne Adwadeɛyɛ asane ama ahoɔden aba Ɔman Sikasɛm mu na yɛama aseɛ atim”.

2. Mfeɛ a abɛsene korɔ no, ɛrekame ayɛ sɛ Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ foforɔ biara a yɛbɛyɛ no, yɛhyehyɛ mpontunnwuma foforɔ a yɛbɛyɛ wɔ mu a, yɛyi yɛn ani firi deɛ ɛrekɔ soɔ no so. Ɛnyɛ ahokyere nko ara na saa tebea no de ba yɛn sikasɛm so na mmom ama ɔman yi da so tua ɛka wɔ mpontunnwuma ho a ama mpontunnwuma ho ɛka akɔ soro pa ara ɛsiane sɛ ɛka a anka ɛsɛ sɛ yɛtua a yɛatu ahyɛ ɛda no wɔ nsiho wɔ ho a, ɛno nso kɔ soro.

3. Afe 2023 mu no, Aban bɛyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ne dwumadie afoforɔ, nanso yɛbɛhwɛ sɛ ɛbɛyɛ yɛn asɛnhia na yɛawie mpontunnwuma ahodoɔ a ɛrekɔ soɔ no nyinaa.

SƐDEƐ ƆMAN SIKASƐM YƐƐ WƆ AFE 2022 MU

As was the basis of State economic development

4. Afe 2022 mu Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ mu Nsakramu a yɛyɛɛ no afe no mfinimfini (Mid-Year Fiscal Policy Review) no, Aban de sii n’ani so sɛ ne nsa bɛka ɔman sikasɛm mpontuo nnyinasoɔ a ɛdidi soɔ yi wɔ afe 2022 mu:

i. Ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ nyinaa kankorɔ ɔha mu nkyɛmu mmiɛnsa ne akyire pɔ nson (Overall Real GDP growth of 3.7 percent);

ii. Ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ a Fango nka ho bie kankorɔ ɔha mu nkyɛmu ɛnan akyire pɔ mmiɛnsa (Non-Oil Real GDP Growth rate of to 4.3 percent);

iii. Afe awieeɛ nneɛma boɔ-sorokɔ ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduonu nwɔtwe ne akyire pɔ enum (End-period inflation of 28.5 percent);

iv. Sika a yɛbɛdi aboro deɛ yɛwɔ so- ɔha mu nkyɛmu nsia ne akyire pɔ nsia (Overall fiscal deficit of 6.6 percent of GDP);

v. Sika dodoɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ ɛka yɛn fotoɔ mu - ɔha mu nkyɛmu hwee ne akyire pɔ ɛnan (Primary surplus of 0.4 percent of GDP); ɛnna

vi. Amannɔne Sika Dodoɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ yɛnya de kra nneɛma bɛyɛ abosome mmiɛnsa ne ɛfa.

5. Amaneɛbɔ a ɛfa ɔman sikasɛm gyinapɛn ho a ɛfiri Ɔpɛpɔn kɔsi Ɛbɔ awieeɛ wɔ afe 2022 mu no kyerɛ sɛ ahokyere a ɛrekɔ so wɔ ewiase afanan ne ɔman yi mu no ho nsunsuansoɔ gu so redi ɔmanfoɔ nya. Tebea yi farebae ne ɔman yi sika mu duru a kɔ ara na ɛrekɔ fam, nneɛma-boɔ-sorokɔ, ɛka a ɛremene ɔman yi, sikasɛm ho ahohyeɛsoɔ a ɛnkɔ yie ne amannɔne sikasɛm a ɛno nso nkɔ yie. Yeinom nyinaa de ɔhaw abɛto yɛn so a ɛmfa ho ne nhyehyɛeɛ a Aban de agu akwan mu. Amanneɛbɔ a ɛfa ɔman yi sikasɛm gyinapɛn ho firi Ɔpɛpɔn kɔsi Ɛbɔ wɔ afe 2022 mu no na ɛdidi soɔ yi:

i. Ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ nyinaa kankorɔ kɔduruu ɔha mu nkyɛmu hwee ne akyire pɔ ɛnan wɔ afe 2022 mu wɔ abosome nsia a ɛdi kan no a wode toto afe 2021 wɔ ɛberɛ korɔ no ara ho a na ɛyɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu mmiɛnsa ne akyire pɔ nkron;

SIKA A ƐBAA ƆMAN FOTOƆ MU

8. 9. Sɛ yɛde Sika Dodoɔ ankasa a ɛbaa fotoɔ mu toto deɛ yɛde sii yɛn ani so sɛ yɛbɛnya no ho a, na ɛda adi sɛ sika no so tee ɔpepem baako, ahankron ne nkron anaa ɔha mu nkyɛmu mmienu ne akyire pɔ nwɔtwe (GH¢1,909 million or 2.8 percent) ne sikasɛm mu mpontuo ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduasa mmiɛnsa ne akyire pɔ mmienu (33.2 percent). Sika nkamu yi a yɛn nsa anka no firi sika ahodoɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ ɛba wɔ ɔman no nkorabata ahodoɔ no mu a antumi amma no enti.

10. Sika ahodoɔ a ɛbaa fotoɔ mu firi Ɔpɛpɔn kɔsi Ɛbɔ awieeɛ wɔ afe 2022 mu no na yɛakyerɛ mu wɔ ɔha mu nkyɛmu mu wɔ hyehyɛpono 1 so no.

Ghana Ɔhwɛpa Dwumadie (Ghana Cares): Dwumadie yi wɔ nhyehyɛeɛ mmienu a ɛyɛ anammɔn ahodoɔ a Aban adi kan atu wɔ Coronavirus Nsunsuansoɔ Anosie Dwumadie ho no ntoasoɔ.

Grants: These are types of financial assistance provided by Travel Assistants to the Government that are not payable by the Government.

Sika a wɔtua ma Aban Nnwuma ahodoɔ (Grants to other Government Units): Yei gyina hɔ ma sika a, mmara hyɛ sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wɔtua kɔgu Mansini Mmarahyɛbadwa Fotoɔ “DACF” no mu, Lɔrekwan Fotoɔ “Road Fund” mu, Nwomasua Fotoɔ “GETFUND” mu, Fango ho Dwumadie Fotoɔ “Petroleum Related Fund” ne Ɔman Apɔmuden ho Fotoɔ “National Health Fund” mu.

Gross Domestic Product (GDP): This refers to the value of all the goods produced in the country during a given period.

Gross International Reserves:This is a measure of the extent of the purchasing power of a country’s economy at a particular point in time. This helps to compare the budget of one country with that of another country by seeing the economic strength of the country.

Nsiho ho Eka (Interest cost): Yei yɛ bosea ho nsiho dodoɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ ɔkafoɔ tua wɔ bosea a wabɔ ho anaa biribi a ɔde ho ɛka wɔ ɛberɛ a ɔntuaa sika botene no ankasa nwieeɛ.
