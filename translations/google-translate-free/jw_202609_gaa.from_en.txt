NGƐ WUMI BƆ Nɔ nɛ wa ma plɛ kɛ je wa tsui mi kɛ bu Yehowa tue.

Bɔ ni Afeɔ Anáa "Toiboo Tsui"

"Ha otsulɔ toiboo tsui."-1 KI. 3:9 lɛ nɔ.

MƐI buɔ mɛi ni yɔɔ hegbɛ lɛ atoi yɛ yiŋtoi srɔtoi ahewɔ. Mɛi babaoo baanyɛ afee nakai pɛ kɛji akɛ mɛi miikwɛ amɛ loo amɛmiisumɔ ni amɛtsi amɛhe kɛjɛ toigbalamɔ he. Kɛ eba Nyɔŋmɔ toiboo he lɛ, mɛi komɛi feɔ toiboo ni amɛfeɔ lɛ kpo koni amɛná hiɛnyam, taakɛ jamɔŋ hiɛnyiɛlɔi ni hi shi yɛ Yesu beaŋ lɛ fee lɛ. ( Mat. 23:5 ) Se kɛ̃ ɔ, wa buɔ Yehowa tue ejakaa wa naa se nami nɛ ngɛ e blɔ tsɔɔmi ɔ he. ( Yes. 48:17, 18 ) Enɛ ji yiŋtoo kpakpa ni haa wɔfeɔ toiboo, shi wɔyɛ yiŋtoo ko ni he hiaa fe no hewɔ ni esa akɛ wɔbo lɛ toi.

2 Nɔ́ titli he je nɛ wa buɔ Yehowa tue ji kaa wa suɔ lɛ wawɛɛ. ( Mat. 22:37, 38 ) Suɔmi nɛ wa ngɛ ɔ haa nɛ wa “jeɔ wa tsui mi kɛ buɔ tue.” ( Rom. 6:17 ) Suɔmɔ kɛha Yehowa kɛ “toiboo tsui” tsirɛɔ wɔ koni wɔfee bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni wɔɔnyɛ koni wɔye ekitãi lɛ anɔ, jeee be mli ni mɛi krokomɛi miikwɛ loo be mli ni wɔnaa sɛɛnamɔi ni jɛɔ nakai feemɔ mli baa amrɔ nɔŋŋ kɛkɛ, shi moŋ be fɛɛ be. Ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ, wa tsui sisiɔ nɔ, enɛ ɔ he ɔ, be komɛ ɔ, e ma nyɛ maa wa kaa wa ma ná juɛmi nɛ da. Ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, wa ma susu Baiblo mi sane enyɔ komɛ nɛ ma nyɛ maa ye bua wɔ konɛ wa ná tue bumi tsui ɔ he. Kekleekle ɔ, wa ma susu be nɛ Yehowa kpɔ Israel bi ɔmɛ kɛ je Egipt ɔ he. Lɔ ɔ se ɔ, wa ma susu nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ kpakpa nɛ Yuda matsɛ Yehoshafat pee ɔ he.

4 Nakai gbɛkɛ lɛ nɔŋŋ lɛ, Israelbii lɛ shi Ejipt. Yehowa nyiɛ amɛhiɛ klɛŋklɛŋ kɛtee Ŋshɔ Tsuru lɛ kooyigbɛ naagbee gbɛ, shi no sɛɛ lɛ ekɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔi ni teɔ shi ewoɔ adesai ajwɛŋmɔi ha amɛ. Ekɛɛ amɛ akɛ amɛtsɔ wuoyigbɛ ni amɛyabɔ nsara yɛ ŋshɔ lɛ kɛ gɔji lɛ ateŋ. Yɛ nakai nsara lɛ mli lɛ, etamɔ nɔ ni amɛbaakã Farao asraafoi ni nyiɛ amɛsɛɛ lɛ kɛ Ŋshɔ Tsuru lɛ teŋ. Kɛ̃lɛ, Israelbii lɛ fee shikome ekoŋŋ taakɛ Yehowa tsɔ etsulɔ Mose nɔ efã amɛ lɛ. ( 2 Mose 14:1-4 ) Se benɛ a na kaa Egipt bi ɔmɛ ma a, ‘a he gbeye,’ nɛ a po Mose nya kaa e nyɛɛ a hɛ mi kɛ ya su gbenɔ mi! (2 Mose 14:5-11) Mɛni he je nɛ a bɔni a he piae kaa a maa pee tue bumi ɔ? A na bɔ nɛ Yehowa po a bitɛte bimɛ ɔmɛ a he piɛ ngɛ haomi nyɔngma nyɔngma a mi ɔ. Se a nui suɔmi nɛ Yehowa ngɛ kɛ ha mɛ ɔ sisi saminya, nɛ a he we mɛ hu kaa e ma he a yi wami ekohu. 10. Benɛ mahi nɛ a ba tua Matsɛ Yehoshafat ɔ wo e he gbeye ɔ, kɛ e nu he ha kɛɛ?

Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, Matsɛ Yehoshafat kɛ mahi etɛ nɛ a pee kake konɛ a kɛ Yuda a hwu ta a kpe. Shishinumɔ yɛ he akɛ Yehoshafat kɛ ewebii lɛ she gbeyei. ( 2 Kron. 20:1-3 ) Shi jeee gbeyeishemɔ ha maŋtsɛ lɛ gbo; etsu he nii. Ke wa kase nɔ́ nɛ Yehoshafat pee pee se ɔ, e maa ye bua wɔ konɛ wa ná tue bumi tsui.

11 Moo fia o pɛɛ si kaa o ma hla Yehowa se blɔ nɛ o maa bu lɛ tue. Benɛ Yehoshafat ye gbeye ɔ, “e pee e juɛmi kaa e maa hla Yehowa se blɔ.” ( 2 Kron. 20:3 ) No mli lɛ, Yehoshafat kɛ ehiɛ fɔ̃ Yehowa nɔ kɛha gbɛtsɔɔmɔ momo. (2 Kron. 19:3) Se ngɛ si fɔfɔɛ nɛ mi wa nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, ‘e mwɔ e yi mi kpɔ,’ aloo e waje e pɛɛ si kaa e maa hla Yehowa se blɔ, nɛ e bu blɔ tsɔɔmi fɛɛ blɔ tsɔɔmi nɛ e kɛ ma ha mɛ ɔ tue. Kɛkɛ ni Yehoshafat nyiɛ sɛɛ kɛ sɔlemɔ ni jɛ tsuiŋ. (Kane 2 Kronika 20:5, 6, 10-12 .) Ke waa kɛ nyagbahi ngɛ kpee ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ waa kase e nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ. Wa ma nyɛ maa fia wa pɛɛ si kaa wa maa bu Yehowa tue, nɛ wa hla e blɔ tsɔɔmi

6 Baiblo ɔ gba kɛ fɔ si kaa ngɛ nyagbe ligbi ɔmɛ a mi ɔ, nihi maa ‘wo a he nɔ, nɛ a maa wo a he nɔ,’ nɛ a “be mɛ kplɛɛe.” ( 2 Tim. 3:1-3 ) Mwɔnɛ ɔ, nihi pɔɔ jamɛ a su ɔmɛ kpo ngɛ a munyu tutui mi. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔ nɔ lɛ, adafitswaa woji wieɔ wiemɔi amli béi ni yaa hiɛ kɛyaa yiwalɛ nifeemɔi ahe. Wiemɔi ni shaa hu ji nɔ ni afɔɔ shi yɛ shiai pii amli. Nakai nɔŋŋ eji yɛ yakayaka wiemɔi, ni anyɛɔ anuɔ kɛjɛɔ onukpai kɛ gbekɛbii fɛɛ adaaŋ lɛ he. ( Efe. 5:3, 4 ) Ŋwanejee ko bɛ he akɛ wiemɔ ni tamɔ nɛkɛ jieɔ je lɛ mumɔ lɛ kpo.

7 Akɛnɛ wa ji Kristofohi he je ɔ, e sa nɛ waa pee nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ kpakpa ngɛ wa munyu tutui mi ke waa kɛ nɔ ko ngɛ ní sɛɛe ngɛ kɔmpiuta nɔ jio. ( Yak. 1:26, 27 ) Ke wa ngɛ wa munyu tutui hyɛe ɔ, wa ma nyɛ ma bi wa he ke: ‘Anɛ ke ni ko wo mi mi mi la, eko ɔ, ke i kɛ nihi nɛ a li kaa Yehowa Odasefo no ji mi ɔ ngɛ ní sɛɛe ɔ, i tuɔ munyu yaya lo? Ani sanegbaai ni mikɛ nanemɛi heyelilɔi lɛ gbaa lɛ feɔ nɔ ni ehiii, nɔ ni mikɛyeɔ mɔ he fɛo, kɛ hefɛoyeli po? Ani mikɛ miweku lɛ mli bii lɛ wieɔ basabasa? Mɛni amɛbaakɛɛ yɛ miwiemɔ lɛ he?’

6. Mɛni blɔ nɔ nɛ je ɔ mumi ɔ jeɔ kpo ngɛ nihi a munyu tutui mi?

7. Kɛ wa ma plɛ kɛ hyɛ wa munyu tutui ɔ mi ha kɛɛ?

8 Mɛni wa ma nyɛ maa pee ke wa yɔse kaa wa munyu tutui jeɔ je ɔ mumi ɔ kpo? Ehe miihia ni wɔtsake babaoo fe wɔwiemɔi pɛ. Mo kai nɔ́ nɛ Yesu de ɔ: “Nɔ́ tsuaa nɔ́ nɛ jeɔ nya mi ɔ, tsui mi nɛ e jeɔ, nɛ lɔ ɔ buleɔ nɔmlɔ.” ( Mat. 15:18 ) Kaa bɔ nɛ Yesu tsɔɔ ɔ, behi fuu ɔ, wa munyu tutui tsɔɔ bɔ nɛ wa nuɔ he ha ngɛ wa tsui mi. No hewɔ ni Biblia lɛ kɛ “wiemɔ ni shaa” kɛ henumɔi ni ejaaa—tamɔ mlifu, mlifu, kɛ mlifu ni mlifu yɔɔ mli lɛ tsakpãa lɛ. ( Efe. 4:31 ) Ke o yɔse kaa e sa nɛ o je su nɛ ɔmɛ ngɛ o he ɔ, bi Yehowa ngɛ sɔlemi mi konɛ e ye bua mo konɛ o ná “nɔmlɔ su ehe” ɔ saminya. (Kane Kolose Bi 3:8-10 .) Jehanɛ se hu ɔ, o ma nyɛ ma hla níhi a mi konɛ o na munyuhi nɛ ngɛ wa womi ɔmɛ a mi nɛ maa ye bua wɔ ngɛ munyu nɛ ɔ he. Moo fia o pɛɛ si kaa o be hae nɛ je ɔ mumi ɔ nɛ ná o je mi bami nɔ he wami, aloo o bule o munyu tutui.​— Efe. 4:29 lɛ he.

KPOO JEŊBA HE NYƆŊMƆI

9 Je lɛ mumɔ lɛ jeɔ kpo faŋŋ ŋmɛnɛ ji bɔ ni mɛi babaoo kuɔ amɛhiɛ amɛshwieɔ Yehowa jeŋba he mlai lɛ anɔ kwraa lɛ. Efɔɔ kaa akɛ gbɔmɛi ni yɔɔ je lɛŋ lɛ kpɛlɛɔ hɔkpɔmɔ, hii kɛ hii ni kɛ amɛhe feɔ yakayaka nii, kɛ bɔlɛnamɔ ni tsɔɔ gbalashihilɛ hiɛ lɛ nɔ loo amɛwoɔ he hewalɛ po. (Yes. 5:20) Ke wa hyɛ we nɛ hi ɔ, bɔɔbɔɔbɔɔ ɔ, je ɔ susumi ɔ ma nyɛ ma ha nɛ wa nyɛɛ ní yayahi. ( 2 Kor. 11:3 ) Eko ɔ, wa ma susu po kaa nihi nɛ a ngɛ je ɔ mi ɔ a kpɛti nihi a juɛmi, se Baiblo ɔ mi mlaa amɛ be be. Nɔ ko bɛ ni baanyɛ afee nɔ ni kɛ anɔkwale lɛ teŋ jɛkɛ fe enɛ! 11 Ni kɛ́ onuɔ he akɛ oosumɔ ni obɛŋkɛ nɔ ko ni Yehowa nyɛɔ lɛ hu? Koo susu kaa o be nyɛe maa hi si ngɛ Yehowa mlaa amɛ a nya. Kaimɔ akɛ adesai fɛɛ ni yeee emuu lɛ sumɔɔ ni amɛfee esha. ( La 51:5; 103:14 ) Se o ma nyɛ maa hla kaa o be akɔnɔ yayahi nɛ o ngɛ ɔ tsue, mohu ɔ, o be “[konɛ o pee] he lo ɔ suɔmi nya ní.” ( Efe. 2:3 ) Jehanɛ se hu ɔ, e sɛ nɛ o susu ní yayahi a he be fɛɛ be. Kɛ́ ebashɛ be ko ni oyɔse akɛ akɔnɔ fɔŋ ko eba otsui mli lɛ, jiemɔ ohiŋmɛii kɛ ojwɛŋmɔ kɛjɛ nibii ni Yehowa nyɛɔ lɛ anɔ amrɔ nɔŋŋ. (1 Mose 39:9, 12) Nɛ mo susu bɔ nɛ ní nɛ ɔmɛ ma nyɛ ma puɛ huɛ bɔmi nɛ ngɛ o kɛ Yehowa kɛ ni kpahi nyɛ kpɛti ɔ ha a he. Tswaa ofai shi akɛ ohãŋ nakai aba mli. ( Efe. 5:5 ) Ngɛ Yehowa yemi kɛ buami nya a, o ma nyɛ maa kase bɔ nɛ o nyɛɔ yayami ha.

TSI HE OSHARAI NI YƐ INTANƐT LƐ HE

12 Kɛha wɔteŋ mɛi pii lɛ, Intanɛt lɛ haa a

12. Mɛni blɔ nɔ a jeɔ je ɔ susumi ɔ kpo ngɛ Intanɛti ɔ nɔ?

gbɛfaŋnɔ yɛ nɔ ni miihe ashɛ wɔshihilɛ mli nɔ fɛɛ nɔ mli, ni wɔjamɔ hu fata he. Shi kɛlɛ, nibii babaoo ni anaa yɛ Intanɛt lɛ nɔ lɛ woɔ je lɛ mumɔ lɛ he hewalɛ. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, ajuama bɔmi he fonihi nɛ a hyɛɔ ngɛ Intanɛti ɔ nɔ ɔ pɔ he wawɛɛ, nɛ nikɔtɔmahi kɛ jokuɛwi hu ma nyɛ maa hyɛ lɔ ɔ gbɔjɔɔ. Sexting—ni ji mfonirii, vidioi, loo shɛɛ saji ni teɔ bɔlɛnamɔ he akɔnɔi amajemɔ—egbɛ eshwã waa, titri lɛ yɛ oblahii kɛ oblayei ateŋ. Agbɛnɛ hu, saji babaoo yɛ Intanɛt lɛ nɔ ni fiteɔ nibii ni yɔɔ diɛŋtsɛ ni elakaa mɛi babaoo. Bei pii lɛ, saji ni aŋmalaa yɛ social media nɔ lɛ jieɔ mɛi ashihilɛ mli saji otii akpo. Fonihi, munyuhi, kɛ video kpiti nɛ ɔmɛ ma nyɛ ma ha nɛ wa nu he kaa e he hia nɛ waa ngɔ be fuu kɛ je wa hɛja, kɛ blɔ hiami hulɔ, nɛ lɔ ɔ ma nyɛ ma puɛ wa jami ɔ po.​— Heb. 10:​24, 25 lɛ he.

13 E sa nɛ waa hyɛ saminya ngɛ níhi tsuo nɛ ma nyɛ ma puɛ wa be ngɛ Intanɛti ɔ nɔ aloo e sa wa susumi he ngɛ blɔ yaya nɔ ɔ he. Ngɛ enɛ ɔ he ɔ, wa ma nyɛ ma bi wa he ke: ‘Be enyɛmɛ nɛ i kɛ kwɛɔ níhi nɛ ngɛ Intanɛti ɔ nɔ nɛ yi mi tomi pɔtɛɛ ko be he je nɛ i peeɔ jã a?’ ( Efe. 5:15, 16 ) ‘Mɛni níhi nɛ i haa nɛ nihi naa? Kɛ i yeɔ mla kɛ jeɔ níhi nɛ sɛ ɔ a he mla ha kɛɛ?’ (La 119:37) ‘Anɛ i heɔ nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ nɛ i kaneɔ ngɛ Intanɛti ɔ nɔ ɔ yeɔ oya lo, aloo i hyɛɔ saminya nɛ i hyɛɔ anɔkualehi lo?’—Abɛ 119:37. 14:15 lɛ he.

14 Bɔ nɛ pee nɛ o hyɛ o he ngɛ je ɔ mumi ɔ he ɔ, moo to be abɔ nɛ o kɛ puɛɔ ngɛ Intanɛti ɔ nɔ ɔ he, nɛ o ko hyɛ nɔ́ ko nɛ sɛ. Agbɛnɛ hu, esa akɛ fɔlɔi akwɛ bɔ ni amɛbii lɛ kɛ social media tsuɔ nii amɛhaa lɛ jogbaŋŋ. ( Efe. 6:4 ) Níhi a mi hlami fuu yɔse kaa nihewi kɛ yihewi nɛ a puɛɔ sika fuu ɔ

BIBLIA MLI WIEMƆ

"Nyɛhaa Kristo Toiŋjɔlɛ lɛ Aye Nyɛtsuii Amli"

Kolosebii 3:15 tsiɔ “Kristo toiŋjɔlɛ” lɛ tã. Mɛni ji “Kristo toiŋjɔlɛ lɛ”? Ekɔɔ mligbɛ toiŋjɔlɛ ni mɔ ko baanyɛ aná akɛ Kristo kaselɔ lɛ he. Ke wa le kaa Yehowa kɛ e Bi ɔ suɔ wɔ ɔ, wa ma ná tue mi jɔmi nɛ ɔ.— Yohane 14:23, 27 .

Bɔfo Paulo wo Kristofoi ni yɔɔ Kolose asafo lɛ mli lɛ hewalɛ koni amɛha toiŋjɔlɛ ni tamɔ nɛkɛ aye amɛtsuii loo amɛmligbɛ gbɔmɔ lɛ nɔ. Mɛɛ gbɛ nɔ? Hela wiemɔ ni Paulo kɛtsu nii yɛ biɛ kɛha "nɔyeli" lɛ kɛ wiemɔ ni akɛtsɔɔ kojolɔ ni yɔɔ akaŋshii mli lɛ yɛ tsakpaa. Ani enɛ etsɔɔɔ akɛ esa akɛ "Kristo toiŋjɔlɛ" lɛ afee naagbee wiemɔ yɛ wɔtsuii amli?

Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔ nɔ lɛ, kɛji akɛ wɔkɛ naanyo heyelilɔ ko te shi lɛ, esa akɛ wɔha "Kristo toiŋjɔlɛ" lɛ afee "kojolɔ." E maa ye bua wɔ konɛ waa mwɔ wa yi mi kpɔ ngɛ nɔ́ nɛ wa ma nyɛ maa pee konɛ tue mi jɔmi kɛ kake peemi nɛ hi wa kpɛti. Kɛ́ wɔnyiɛ nakai gbɛ lɛ nɔ, kɛji akɛ no tsɔɔ akɛ wɔbaaŋmɛɛ wɔ diɛŋtsɛ wɔsuɔmɔnaa nii loo wɔmlifu lɛ he po lɛ, wɔtsɔɔ akɛ wɔmiiha "Kristo toiŋjɔlɛ" lɛ miiye nɔ yɛ wɔtsuii amli. -Kol. 3:12-14 he.
