Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

TWƐN HƆ NA WOBEHWEHWƐ

BERE bi na ɔbaa bi ne ne ba te hɔ. Bere a ɔsomaa no (baabiara) no, ɔkae sɛ, " Merenkɔ." Ná ne maame taa ka sɛ, " Twɛn hɔ na wubehu." Bere nyinaa, sɛ ɔsoma no (baabiara) a, ɔka sɛ, " Merenkɔ." Afei na ne maame taa ka kyerɛ no sɛ, " Twɛn hɔ na wobɛhunu." Da bi abofra no kaa sɛ, "Bere biara, ɛna reka akyerɛ me sɛ, ' Twɛn hɔ na wobɛhunu '; da yi ara merekɔ akɔhwehwɛ 'Mɛ-bɛhunu ', na mahwɛ no." Enti osiim kwan. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, (na) Pea-dua bi a ɛsow sika kɔkɔɔ. Pea-dua no bisaa no se, " Adɛn nti na bere a obiara mmaa ha da no, afei woaba ha? " Abofra no kaa sɛ, " Me ne maame te hɔ bere nyinaa; ɔkae sɛ, ' Twɛn hɔ na wubehu,' na mekae sɛ mɛba abɛhwehwɛ ' Me-bɛ-hwɛ ', na mahwɛ so." Pea-dua no kaa sɛ, " Meyɛ Wɔn mu baako, nanso Yɛn mu kɛseɛ no wɔ akyirikyiri." Abofra no san sii kwan so bio. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ohui sɛ Nnua Tenten reba abɛhyia no. Nnua tenten bi bisabisaa no nsɛm, na ɔkaa sɛ, " Adɛn nti na, bere a obiara mfaa saa kwan yi so da no, seesei woatwam wɔ so? " Abofra no buae sɛ, "Bere nyinaa, na ɛna reka sɛ, ' Mɛhwɛ,' enti mekae sɛ mereba abɛhwehwɛ ' Mɛ-bɛhunu ', na mahwɛ so.” Nnua atenten buae sɛ, “Meyɛ Wɔn mu bi, nanso kɔ w’anim.” Na abofra no san sii kwan so bio. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, (na) Glowing-Sparks wɔ hɔ, na ɔdaa kuku bi a abubu so, na na ɔreba (rekɔ ne nkyɛn). Ohyiaa no. Glowing-Sparks kaa sɛ, “Adɛn nti na, bere a ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara ba ha da no, woaba ha?” Abofra no buae sɛ, “Bere a na me ne maame te hɔ no, ɔka sɛ, ‘Mɛhwɛ,’ enti mekae sɛ mɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ ‘Mɛhwɛ’, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mɛhwɛ mu.” Glowing-Sparks kaa sɛ, “Meyɛ Wɔn mu baako, nanso kɔ w’anim, ɛfiri sɛ (Yɛn mu) kɛseɛ no wɔ hɔ.” Abofra no sii kwan so bio. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, (na) Parrots no de swish reto ofie bi so. Akɔre no kaa sɛ, “Afei yɛbɛgye obi mogya de agu fie yi so.” Afei bere a abofra no duu hɔ no, wɔkaa sɛ, “Adɛn nti na bere a obiara mma ha da no, afei moaba ha?” Obuae sɛ, “Me ne maame te bere nyinaa, ɔkae sɛ, ‘Twɛn hɔ na wubehu,’ enti mekae sɛ mɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ ‘Mɛhwɛ’, na mahwɛ.” Ɛno na Akɔre no kyeree abofra no, na wɔtwaa ne ti, na wɔfaa ne mogya hwie guu kɔbere anwenne mu; wɔfaa bi de hyɛɛ fie hɔ. Afei Akɔre no tu kɔtenaa dua tenten bi so, na wɔbaa wɔn ho wɔn ho akyi de wɔn dua guu mogya no mu, na wɔn dua yɛɛ kɔkɔɔ. Akɔre no nyinaa faa wɔn dua na wɔde guu nsu mu, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wɔbɛyɛ adeyɛ no ho nkae. Afei, da bi, Akɔre no mu baako kaa sɛ ɔrekɔdidi. Ɔkɔhyiaa abofra no maame. Na ɛna no bisaa Ɔkraman no sɛ, "Me ba a me ne no te no yɛ ɔkwasea te sɛ biribiara. Sɛ na metaa sɔre a, na meka sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔtwɛn wɔ hɔ na ɔhunu;ɔno, te sɛ ɔkwasea, fa kyɛw wɔ asɛmfua no a mede dii dwuma no mu, ɔkaa sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ ‘Mɛbɛhunu’, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛhwɛ so. Ɛyɛ nnawɔtwe a atwam nnɛ na menhunuu no, na mesrɛ wo, sɛ woahunu no a huu no, fa no bra me nkyɛn.” Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, “Oo, ɔbaa yɛn akuraa ase, na yɛn nso, yɛkumm no. Ne mogya no bi, a yɛfaa de kaee asɛm a esii no, wɔ me dua yi so. Enti, sɛ wokɔ (wo kwan) a, kɔ na kɔka kyerɛ wo nkurɔfoɔ nyinaa sɛ sɛ obi ka kyerɛ obi sɛ, ‘Twɛn hɔ na wobɛhunu a,’ ɛnde na waka nnome agu no so.”

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
