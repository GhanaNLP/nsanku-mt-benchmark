Tee Shi Owo Satan Ŋaatsɔɔi lɛ—Okɛ Ohiɛ Afɔ̃ Yehowa kɛ Yesu Nɔ

LALA 55 Nyɛkashea Amɛ Gbeyei!

FOCUS Kaaha mɔdɛŋ ni Satan bɔɔ koni ekɛ Yehowa Nyɔŋmɔ kɛ Yesu Kristo he saji ni ejaaa lɛ miilaka bo.

“Nyɛɛ te si kɛ wo Abosiami, nɛ e maa tu nyɛ nya fo.”​— YAK. 4:7 lɛ nɔ.

KƐ JE be nɛ wa mwɔ wa yi mi kpɔ kaa wa ma sɔmɔ Yehowa a, Satan kɛ e daimonio ɔmɛ ba peeɔ wa he nyɛli nɛ a kpa we mɛ. Se Satan le kaa ke Yehowa fĩ wa se ɔ, e be nyɛe maa ye wa nɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, Abosiami bɔɔ mɔde kaa e maa puɛ jamɛ a yemi kɛ buami ɔ kɛ gu e he nɛ e haa nɛ wa kuɔ wa hɛ kɛ jeɔ Yehowa a nɔ. Blɔ kake nɛ e guɔ nɔ kɛ peeɔ jã ji kaa e ma ha nɛ wa yi mi nɛ pee wɔ enyɔɔnyɔ kaa Yehowa kɛ e Bi ɔ fĩ wa se niinɛ.​— 2 Kor. 11:14; Yakobo. 1:6 lɛ nɔ.

2 Ke ta buli ngɛ ta hwue ɔ, e sa nɛ a ná hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi ngɛ a hɛ mi nyɛɛli ɔmɛ a mi. Kɛji amɛlaaje nakai hekɛnɔfɔɔ lɛ, amɛbaanyɛ amɛfee amɛ diɛŋtsɛ amɛnii. Enɛ baanyɛ aha amɛwala afite. Yɛ gbɛ ni tamɔ nakai nɔŋŋ nɔ lɛ, wɔji asraafoi ni kɛ “mumɔi fɔji asafoi” lɛ miiwu yɛ mumɔŋ ta mli. ( Efe. 6:12 ) Satan Abosiami le kaa ke wa be wa hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi ngɛ Yehowa Mawu kɛ Yesu Kristo mi ɔ, eko ɔ, wa kua a blɔ tsɔɔmi kɛ a se bumi. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e peeɔ nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ nɛ e ma nyɛ konɛ hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi nɛ wa ngɛ ngɛ a mi ɔ mi nɛ gbɔjɔ. Ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, wa ma susu bɔ nɛ e kɛ ga blɔ nɛ ɔ tsu ní ha be ko nɛ be ɔ, kɛ bɔ nɛ e kɛ ga nɛ ɔ ngɛ ní tsue ha mwɔnɛ ɔ he. Lɔ ɔ se ɔ, wa ma susu bɔ nɛ wa ma plɛ kɛ te si kɛ wo e ga tsɔmi ɔmɛ a he. Ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ nyɛɛ se ɔ mi ɔ, wa ma susu blɔ kpahi enyɔ nɛ Satan guu nɔ kɛ siɔ wɔ ngɛ wa mumi mi ta a mi ɔ he.

* Jeee Satan kɛ kaai fɛɛ ni wɔkɛkpeɔ lɛ baa wɔnɔ tɛ̃ɛ. Shi lɛ ji mɔ ni kɛ “je nɛŋ mumɔ lɛ” ni haa shitee-kɛ-wolɔi yeɔ Nyɔŋmɔ webii lɛ ayi lɛ baa. No hewɔ lɛ, kɛ́ wɔkɛkpe tɛ̃ɛ jio, yɛ gbɛ kroko nɔ jio lɛ, Satan ji mɔ ni hɔ naagbai babaoo ni wɔkɛkpeɔ yɛ wɔmumɔŋ ta ni wɔwuɔ lɛ mli lɛ asɛɛ.— 1 Kor. 2:12; 1 Yohane 5:19 he.

1. Mɛni ga Satan kɛ tsuɔ ní konɛ e kɛ ha nɛ Mawu sɔmɔli nɛ a yi anɔkuale?

2. Mɛni he je nɛ e he hia nɛ wa ná hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi ngɛ Yehowa Mawu kɛ Yesu Kristo mi ɔ?

2

SATAN TU YEHOWA KƐ YESU HE NƆ NI YƐ HE TSƆƆ

3 Ngɛ adesahi a yi nɔ sane sisije ɔ, Satan to kaa e maa gbla Adam kɛ Hawa kɛ je Yehowa he. Satan laka Hawa ni eha ehe eye akɛ e-Bɔlɔ lɛ susuuu ehilɛ-kɛhamɔ he. Satan bi lɛ akɛ: “Ani Nyɔŋmɔ kɛɛ nyɛkaye trom lɛ mli tsei lɛ eko fɛɛ eko nɔ yibii lɛɛlɛŋ?” (1 Mose 3:1-5) Satan ha nɛ Hawa ná nɔ mi mami kaa Yehowa ngɛ husuhi nɛ sisi numi ngɛ e kɛ e huno ɔ tsuo a nɔ. Aywilɛho sane ji kaa Hawa kɛ Adam hu fĩ he nyɛlɔ Satan se. Amɛku amɛsɛɛ amɛha Yehowa—Mɔ ni kɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ hã amɛ lɛ.— 1 Mo. 3:6 lɛ nɔ.

4 Kɛ je Adam kɛ Hawa be ɔ mi kɛ ma nɛ ɔ, Satan ya nɔ nɛ e ngɛ Yehowa he munyu yayae. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, Satan ha nɛ Hiob lakpa huɛmɛ ɔmɛ ha nɛ e yi mi pee we lɛ enyɔɔnyɔ kaa Yehowa suɔ lɛ, nɛ e buɔ anɔkuale nɛ e yeɔ ɔ kaa e he jua wa. ( Hiob 22:1-3 ) Abosiami susu kaa haomihi nɛ maa ba Hiob nɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ e kua Yehowa. Eyɛ mli akɛ Satan ŋaa lɛ nyɛ Hiob nɔ waa moŋ, shi nakai nuu anɔkwafo lɛ hiɛ emuuyeli mli, ni ehiɛ kpaaa ekɛ Nyɔŋmɔ teŋ naanyobɔɔ lɛ nɔ kɔkɔɔkɔ.— Hiob 27:1, 5 .

5 Jeha lafahi abɔ se benɛ Yesu ba zugba a nɔ ɔ, Satan bɔ mɔde kaa e maa tsi nihi a nya konɛ a ko nyɛɛ Yesu se. Blɔ kake nɛ e gu kɛ pee jã ji kaa e wo jami hɛ mi nyɛɛli kɛ ni kpahi a he wami konɛ a gbɛ Yesu he lakpa munyuhi kɛ fĩa. Ni yɛ naagbee lɛ, amɛha agbe lɛ tamɔ awuiyelɔ ko ni afɔɔ feemɔ.— Kanemɔ

Mateo 11:19; 27:​27-31; Marko 14:​55, 56; Yohane 8:41-45 he.

6 Ngɛ Yesu gbenɔ se ɔ, Satan ngɔ e juɛmi kɛ ma ní tsumi nɛ Yesu se nyɛɛli ɔmɛ ngɛ tsue ɔ nɔ. Kekleekle ɔ, Abosiami bɔ mɔde kaa e maa ngɔ Yesu si temi ɔ he yi mi pee kɛ ba wa mi. Ngɛ Satan he wami nya a, lakpa munyu ko gbɛ fĩa kaa Yesu kaseli ɔmɛ ju e gbogboe ɔ. ( Mat. 28:11-15 ) Lɔ ɔ se ɔ, ngɛ ligbihi nɛ nyɛɛ se ɔ mi ɔ, Satan bɔ mɔde kaa e maa wo asafo ɔ he gbeye kɛ gu yi mi wami nɔ. Satan suɔ nɛ e ha nɛ ní tsumi nɛ a Nyɔmtsɛ ɔ kɛ wo a dɛ ɔ nɛ pee kaa ní tsumi nɛ mi wa tsɔ ha mɛ.​— Bɔfo Ɔmɛ A Ní Tsumi 7:57–8:1; 12:​1-3; 28:22 lɛ he.

7 E ngɛ mi kaa blema Kristofohi ɔmɛ a kpɛti ni komɛ ngmɛɛ nɔnyɛɛ nɛ ɔ he kaa a kpa fiɛɛmi ní tsumi ɔ mohu lɛɛ, se a kpɛti nihi fuu fĩ si nɛ a sume kaa a maa pee dii. A yɔse kaa Satan kɛ daimonio ɔmɛ ji a he nyɛli nitsɛnitsɛ. ( Bɔf. 4:18-20; 1 Pet. 5:8 ) Ni ŋmɛnɛ hu? Mɛni ga tsɔmihi nɛ Satan kɛ ngɛ ní tsue konɛ e kɛ je wa kɔni mi nyu konɛ wa kpa Yehowa sɔmɔmi?

SATAN TEŊƆ EKƐ YEHOWA KƐ YESU HE HE NYƆŊMƆ NI EJI TSƆƆ HE

8 Mwɔnɛ ɔ, Satan ga tsɔmi ɔmɛ a kpɛti kake ji kaa e ma ha nɛ nihi nɛ a le anɔkuale nɛ kɔɔ Yehowa kɛ Yesu he ɔ. Akɛni Abonsam ji “malemɔ tsɛ” hewɔ lɛ, egbɛɔ amalei eshwaa amɛ kɛtsɔɔ apasa jamɔ nɔ. ( Yohane 8:44 ) Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, e ha nɛ nihi nɛ a le nɔ tutuutu nɛ Yehowa kɛ e Bi ɔ ji. ( 2 Kor. 4:3, 4 ) Bei pii lɛ Kristendom

3. Ngɛ mɛni blɔ nɔ nɛ Satan puɛ hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi nɛ Hawa ngɛ ngɛ Yehowa mi ɔ?

4. Ngɛ Hiob be ɔ mi ɔ, mɛni blɔ nɔ Satan tu Yehowa he munyu yaya ngɛ?

5. Mɛni Satan pee konɛ nihi nɛ a ko nyɛɛ Yesu se? ( Mateo 11:19 )

6-7 lɛ nɔ. Mɛni blɔ nɔ Satan gu kɛ tua Yesu kekleekle kaseli ɔmɛ?

8. Mɛni blɔ nɔ Satan guɔ lakpa jami nɔ kɛ gbɛɔ Yehowa kɛ Yesu he lakpa munyuhi kɛ fĩaa?

Satan je ɔ ngɔɔ níhi nɛ puɛɔ Mawu sisi tomi mlaa amɛ a he munyu wawɛɛ (Hyɛ kuku 11)

lɛ tsɔɔ akɛ Tsɛ lɛ kɛ Bi lɛ fɛɛ yeɔ egbɔ. Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, gbɔmɛi sumɔɔ ni amɛku amɛhiɛ amɛshwie Tsɛ lɛ, ni amɛleee egbɛi lɛ nɔ, ni amɛjaa Bi lɛ. Yehowa kɛ Yesu hu kplɛɛ we jami nɛ ɔ nɔ. ( Mat. 4:10; Yoh. 4:23 ) Ke nihi a hɛ ngmɛ yu ngɛ blɔ nɛ ɔmɛ a nɔ ɔ, e he ma nyɛ maa wa kaa waa kɛ mɛ maa susu níhi a he, aloo e maa te si kɛ wo wa fiɛɛmi ní tsumi ɔ po.

9 Ke waa kɛ nɔ ko bɔni huɛ bɔmi ɔ, kekleekle níhi nɛ wa peeɔ ji kaa wa maa le nɔ ɔ biɛ. Satan bɔɔ mɔdɛŋ ni etsĩ gbɔmɛi anaa koni amɛkɛ Yehowa akaná naanyobɔɔ ni bɛŋkɛ kpaakpa kɛtsɔ Yehowa gbɛi lɛ po ni ehãaa amɛle lɛ nɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, Satan gu lakpa jami nɔ kɛ ha nɛ nihi a hɛ je Mawu biɛ ɔ nɔ jee babauu. (Kane Yeremia 23:26, 27.) Jamɛ a he nyɛlɔ ɔ ha nɛ a ngɔ Mawu biɛ ɔ nɛ Baiblo ɔ kɛ to mɔɔ nɛ he wa nɛ buɔ nɔ he ɔ po kɛ je Baiblo sisi tsɔɔmihi fuu a mi. ( Abɛ 18:10 ) Jehanɛ se hu ɔ, Abosiami hɛɛ lakpa munyu nɛ ji kaa Yehowa lɛ nɛ haa nihi naa nɔ́ ɔ se. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, eko ɔ, e he maa wa ha nihi nɛ a hɛɛ anɔkuale ɔ kaa a maa na Yehowa kaa Huɛ nɛ a ma nyɛ maa hɛ mɛ kɛ su e he. ( Yak. 4:8 ) Se Satan hu bɔɔ mɔde kaa e ma puɛ Yehowa we bi a hemi kɛ yemi ɔ.

9. Akɛnɛ lakpa jami ngɛ Satan kudɔe he je ɔ, mɛni ha nɛ nihi nyɛ we nɛ a kase? ( Yeremia 23:26, 27 ) lɛ.

4 BUU-MƆ LƐ

10 Ke waa kɛ nyagbahi ngɛ kpee ɔ, Satan suɔ nɛ waa susu kaa Yehowa susuu we wa he. Sa-tan ngɛ hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi kaa ke Yehowa ngmɛ blɔ nɛ wa piɛɛmi ɔ ya nɔ ɔ, wa mi mi ma fu. ( Abɛ 19:3 ) Eko ɔ, nihi nɛ a nuɔ he jã a ma bi ke: ‘Mɛni se nami ngɛ he kaa waa ya nɔ nɛ waa kɛ Yehowa mlaahi kɛ e sisi tomi mlaa amɛ nɛ tsu ní? Etamɔ nɔ ni mináaa he sɛɛ!’ Susumɔ ni tamɔ nɛkɛ baanyɛ aha wɔyaje Satan hewalɛ shishi.

11 Satan guɔ blɔ nya tomi komɛ kaa Intanɛti nɔ kɛ haa nihi a yi mi pee ngɛ Yehowa mlaa amɛ a he. Yɛ kpokui nɛɛ anɔ lɛ, mɛi babaoo jieɔ amɛsusumɔ kpo akɛ jeŋba he mlai ni yɔɔ Biblia lɛ mli lɛ etsɛ, ni esa akɛ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ aná heyeli ni ekɛ lɛ diɛŋtsɛ eshishitoo mlai ama shi. Nɔ ni yɔɔ faŋŋ fe fɛɛ ji akɛ, “je nɛŋ lumɔ lɛ” etsɔ ŋaa waa yɛ srɔtofeemɔ ni yɔɔ nɔ ni hi kɛ nɔ ni ehiii teŋ lɛ mli.— Yohane 14:30; Yes. 5:20; 1 Tim. 4:1, 2 lɛ he.

12 Ke wa buɔ Satan lakpa munyuhi tue ɔ, e ma nyɛ ma ha nɛ wa je Yehowa he. Nyɛmi yoo fioo ko ni wɔbaatsɛ lɛ Shannon lɛ kɛɛ akɛ: “Ehe mi be fioo dani miyɔse akɛ jeŋba kpakpa ni atsɔɔ mi yɛ skul kɛ mɛi ni mikɛ amɛ bɔɔ lɛ miifite bɔ ni minuɔ nɔ ni ja kɛ nɔ ni ejaaa he lɛ. kɛ nyɛmimɛi yei.” Yɛ naagbee lɛ, Shannon na gbɛ kɛku esɛɛ kɛtee eŋwɛi Tsɛ lɛ ŋɔɔ ni ejo Abonsam tsɔne lɛ naa foi. Mɛni ye bua lɛ?

13 Shannon yɔse kaa e sa nɛ e bɔ mɔde wawɛɛ konɛ e ya nɔ nɛ e kɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu nɛ pee huɛmɛ. Ekɛɛ akɛ: “Misɔle ni mitsu nii yɛ misɔlemɔi lɛ anaa kɛtsɔ niiamlitaomɔ ni mifee yɛ wɔwoji lɛ amli lɛ nɔ. Vidio ni ji Young People Ask—How Can I Make Real Friends? kɛ nyɛmimɛi yei Kristofoi ni eda yɛ mumɔŋ ni yɔɔ asafo lɛ mli lɛ ye amɛbua mi ni mikpa naanyobɔɔ fɔŋ lɛ, ni mibayɔse akɛ jeee kpeei ni maya ni esa mifɔlɔi ahiɛ pɛ ji anɔkwale lɛ, shi moŋ anɔkwale lɛ ni mikɛaafee mi diɛŋtsɛ minɔ̃ kɛ wekukpaa ni bɛŋkɛ kpaakpa ni mahi mikɛ Yehowa teŋ lɛ mli.”

SHIDAA NI AKƐHAA BÍBLIKO

Wɔteɔ shi wɔwoɔ Satan hewalɛi lɛ kɛtsɔ bɔ ni wɔɔfee wɔha wekukpaa ni bɛŋkɛ kpaakpa ahi wɔ kɛ Yehowa teŋ lɛ he susumɔ nɔ (Kwɛmɔ kuku 14)

he saji ni anɔkwale fã loo amalei ni yɔɔ faŋŋ yɔɔ mli lɛ eye. Se ke wa kaseɔ Baiblo ɔ kɛ wa womi ɔmɛ, nɛ waa kɛ Kristofohi nɛ a nane pi si ngɛ Mawu jami mi ɔ bɔɔ daa a, e be hae nɛ wa kplɛɛ we nɔ mla. Ke wa le anɔkuale ɔ saminya a, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ wa ma nyɛ maa kua mɔde nɛ Satan ngɛ bɔe kaa e ma ha nɛ wa hemi kɛ yemi ɔ mi nɛ gbɔjɔ ɔ.​— Abɛ 15:10 . 13:20; Bɔfoi lɛ Asaji 17:11 .

15 Ke wa kase Baiblo ɔ saminya a, wa ma nyɛ ma ná nɔ mi mami kaa Yehowa kɛ Yesu fĩ wa se. (Abɛ 22:19)

Ke wa pue wa yi mi tɛ ngɛ munyuhi nɛ tsɔɔ bɔ nɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu susuɔ adesahi a he ha a he ɔ, lɔ ɔ maa wo wɔ he wami nɛ waa kɛ mɛ maa pee wa huɛmɛ nɛ wa ma nyɛ maa ngɔ wa hɛ kɛ fɔ a nɔ. Baiblo ɔ hyi tɔ kɛ nɔ mi mami nɛ tsɔɔ kaa Yehowa kɛ Yesu suɔ wɔ. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, Yehowa tsɔɔ kaa bɔ nɛ e nuɔ he ha e we bi ɔ mi wa pe bɔ nɛ binyɛ nɛ ngɛ fufɔ nuɔ he ha e bi ɔ. ( Yes. 49:15 ) E buɔ nɔ tsuaa nɔ nɛ bua jɔ e biɛ ɔ he ɔ kaa e ji “weto ní nɛ he jua wa.” ( Mal. 3:16, 17 , shishigbɛ. ) Ni Biblia lɛ hãa wɔnaa bɔ ni Yesu sumɔɔ wɔ waa ha kɛtsɔ bɔ ni enu he eha yɛ efɔleshaa lɛ he lɛ nɔ.— Yoh.

15. Mɛni wa ma nyɛ maa pee konɛ wa ná nɔ mi mami kaa Yehowa kɛ Yesu fĩ wa se?

16 Ke wa susu yemi kɛ buami nɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu kɛ haa wɔ mwɔnɛ ɔ he ɔ, lɔ ɔ hu maa ye bua wɔ nɛ wa ma ná nɔ mi mami kaa a suɔ wɔ. Wa ma nyɛ ma kai behi nɛ wa nu he kaa Yehowa ngɛ wa kasa mi ɔ, nɛ be nɛ wa kaiɔ ɔ ma nyɛ maa wo wɔ he wami. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, wa ma nyɛ ma bi wa nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi nɛ a ná yemi kɛ buami nɛ ɔ he níhi a si kpami ha. ( Abɛ 22:17 ) Ke wa pee jã a, wa ma ná nɔ mi mami kaa ke nyagba fɛɛ nyagba nɛ waa kɛ maa kpe ɔ, Yehowa kɛ Yesu fĩ wa se.​— Kane La 118:5, 6 .

17 Wɔ tsuo wa suɔ nɛ waa ya nɔ nɛ waa pee Yehowa Mawu kɛ Yesu Kristo huɛmɛ nɛ nyu bi ngɛ wa kpɛti. Se kɛ ya si be nɛ a ma kpata Satan kɛ e daimonio ɔmɛ a hɛ mi kɛ ya neneene ɔ, e he maa hia nɛ waa hwu kɛ si hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi nɛ wa ngɛ ngɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu mi ɔ. ( Kpoj. 20:1-3, 10 ) Loko wa ma nyɛ maa da wa he nyɛli ɔmɛ a ga tsɔmi ɔmɛ a nya a, e sa nɛ huɛ bɔmi gbagbanii nɛ hi waa kɛ Yehowa wa kpɛti, nɛ waa ya nɔ nɛ wa ná nɔ mi mami kaa e kɛ e Bi nɛ e suɔ lɛ ɔ suɔ wɔ, nɛ wa bua wɔ wawɛɛ. Kɛkɛ ɔ, kaa bɔ nɛ Yesu de ɔ, nɔ ko be nyɛe maa ‘shɔ [mo] kɛ je Tsɛ ɔ dɛ mi.’— Yohane 10:29; Yakobo. 4:7 lɛ nɔ.

18 Ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ nyɛɛ se ɔ mi ɔ, wa ma susu ga blɔ enyɔ komɛ nɛ Satan guɔ nɔ kɛ siɔ wɔ ngɛ wa mumi mi ta a mi ɔ he, nɛ ji mɔde nɛ e bɔɔ kaa e maa ngɔ mi gbami kɛ ba asafo ɔ mi, kɛ si temi kɛ womi nɛ jeɔ kpo kɛ baa a he. Kɛ wa ma plɛ kɛ hwu kɛ si ní nɛ ɔmɛ ha kɛɛ? Wɔbaana.

16. Ke wa kai wɔ nitsɛmɛ wa níhi a si kpamihi aloo wa bu ni kpahi a níhi a si kpami tue ɔ, kɛ wa ma ná he se ha kɛɛ? ( Lala 118:5, 6 )

17. Ngɛ Yohane 10:29 ɔ nya a, mɛni nɔ mi mami Yesu wo nihi tsuo nɛ a hiɔ e kɛ e Tsɛ ɔ kasa nya a?

18. Mɛni he wa ma susu ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ nyɛɛ se ɔ mi?

NƆ NI AKƐ WOƆ MLI Kaimɔ be fɛɛ be akɛ anɔkwa Kristofoi kɛ mumɔi fɔji asraafoi lɛ miiwu, shi jeee amɛhe.

Obaanyɛ Oye Kunim yɛ Ta lɛ Mli!

“Jeee lá kɛ heloo wɔkɛwuɔ, shi moŋ mumɔi fɔji asafoi ni yɔɔ ŋwɛi hei lɛ wɔkɛwuɔ.” — EFE. 6:12 lɛ he.

AKƐ Yehowa tsuji lɛ, wɔkɛ wɔhe wooo tawuu mli. ( Yes. 2:4; Yoh. 18:36 ) Se Baiblo ɔ tu ta ko nɛ wa ti nɔ ko be nyɛe maa tu e he ɔ he munyu, nɛ ji ta nɛ waa kɛ mumi yayami ɔmɛ maa hwu. (Kane Efeso Bi 6:11, 12 .) Kɛ wa ma plɛ kɛ ná hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi kaa wa ma nyɛ maa ye he nyɛli nɛ ɔmɛ nɛ a ngɛ he wami ɔ a nɔ ha kɛɛ? Nɔ ni fe fɛɛ lɛ, ehe miihia ni wɔkɛ wɔhe afɔ̃ Yehowa yelikɛbuamɔ nɔ. Shi esa akɛ wɔyɔse ŋaa gbɛ̀i ni Satan kɛ edaimonioi lɛ tsɔɔ nɔ amɛshiɔ wɔ kɛ wɔmumɔŋ weku lɛ hu.

2 Ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ sɛ hlami ɔ mi ɔ, wa susu bɔ nɛ Abosiami bɔɔ mɔde kaa e maa puɛ hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi nɛ wa ngɛ ngɛ Yehowa Mawu kɛ Yesu Kristo mi ɔ he. Ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, wa ma susu ga blɔ enyɔ komɛ nɛ Satan guu nɔ kɛ hwuɔ wɔ ɔ a he. E bɔɔ mɔde kaa e ma puɛ kake peemi nɛ ngɛ waa kɛ wa nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi wa kpɛti ɔ. Ni eteeɔ shitee-kɛ-woo shi koni ekɛgbala wɔjwɛŋmɔ loo ewo wɔhe gbeyei, kɛ hiɛnɔkamɔ akɛ ebaatsĩ wɔnaa koni wɔkatsu nitsumɔ ni Yehowa kɛwo wɔdɛŋ akɛ wɔtsu lɛ.

( 1 Sam. 18:5 ) Se pee se ɔ, Saul ye hɛ kɔ̃ benɛ e na kaa ni ɔmɛ suɔ David wawɛɛ pe lɛ ɔ. Nɔ ni jɛ mli ba ji akɛ, Saul kɛ ehe wo tafãa ko ni sɛɛ tsɛɔ koni egbe David lɛ mli. Feemɔ Satan he mfoniri ni etsui enyɔ emli beni Saul ni ji awuŋayelɔ lɛ kɛ ehe wo nuu ni Yehowa ehala lɛ akɛ maŋtsɛ ni baaye esɛɛ lɛ gbele mli lɛ he okwɛ.

Satan le kaa kake peemi nɛ ngɛ wa kpɛti ɔ ma ha nɛ Yehowa maa jɔɔ wɔ, nɛ e maa ye bua wɔ. (La 133:1) Akɛnɛ Satan ngɛ mɔde bɔe kaa e ma puɛ kake peemi nɛ ɔ he je ɔ, e suɔ nɛ waa ye nihi a hɛ kɔ̃ nɛ wa mi mi nɛ fu wɔ kaa bɔ nɛ Saul pee David ɔ. Satan le kaa ke wa hɛ kɔ̃ wa nyɛmi ko nɔ ɔ, e maa ye ha wɔ wawɛɛ kaa wa ma je suɔmi kpo kɛ tsɔɔ mɛ. ( Marko 12:30, 31 ) Ke e ba jã a, anɛ Yehowa maa kplɛɛ wa jami ɔ nɔ lo? 6 Ke wa pee kake kɛ da si kaa nyɛmimɛ nɛ a pee kake ɔ, wa ma nyɛ ma ha nɛ hɛkɔ kɛ hɛkɔ nɛ he si ngɛ wa tsui mi. Susumɔ nɔkwɛmɔ nɔ kpakpa ni Saul binuu Yonatan fee lɛ he okwɛ. Nɔ ni tamɔɔɔ etsɛ ni ye David he awuŋa lɛ, Yonatan kɛɛ enaanyo oblanyo David akɛ eyɛ hemɔkɛyeli ni mli wa akɛ Yehowa yiŋtoo lɛ baaba mli. Yonatan kɛ nɔmimaa kɛɛ lɛ akɛ: “Obaatsɔ Israel nɔ maŋtsɛ.” Yonatan kɛ heshibaa diɛŋtsɛ kpɛlɛ Yehowa suɔmɔnaa nii anɔ, ni efĩ David sɛɛ akɛ Israel maŋtsɛ.— 1 Sam. 23:16, 17 lɛ he.

7 Jehanɛ se hu ɔ, Satan maa suɔ nɛ e kɛ slɔɔtohi nɛ ngɛ wa kpɛti ɔ nɛ puɛ kake peemi nɛ ngɛ wa kpɛti ɔ. E suɔ nɛ wa he ye kaa akɛnɛ e slo wa juɛmi, wa su, wa ma su, aloo kusumihi nɛ wa ngɛ ɔ he je ɔ, waa kɛ wa sibi be nyɛe maa hi si saminya. Mɛni o maa pee konɛ o ko ha lɛ he blɔ nɛ e kɛ mi gbami nɛ ba wa kpɛti? Tsu gbeekpamɔi loo naagbai fɛɛ ni baa lɛ ahe nii oya. (Kane Efeso Bi 4:25-27 .) Mo ha nɛ suɔmi nɛ o ngɛ kɛ ha nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi ɔ mi nɛ wa. Ná jwɛŋmɔ ni ja yɛ amɛhe. Eyɛ mli akɛ esoro wɔ kwraa moŋ, shi wɔ fɛɛ wɔji gbɔmɔtso kome mli henii. ( 1 Kor. 12:12 ) Wa nyɛmimɛ Kristofohi daa si ngɛ wa kasa nya, nɛ a kɛ he nyɛli nɛ waa kɛ mɛ ngɛ ta hwue ɔ, nɛ ji Satan kɛ daimonio ɔmɛ ngɛ hwue.​— 1 Pet. 5:8, 9 lɛ.

8 Ke o nyɛmi Mawu jalɔ ko tu munyu ko aloo e pee nɔ́ ko nɛ dɔ mo ɔ, mo kai kaa mo hu o yi mluku kaa bɔ nɛ lɛ hu o yi mluku ɔ. Kɛ okɛ mɛi krokomɛi tsu nii jogbaŋŋ lɛ, eji anɔkwale akɛ oookpa gbɛ akɛ béi komɛi baaba. ( Jaj. 7:20 ) Kɛ́ eba lɛ nakai lɛ, nyiɛmɔ ŋaawoo ni jɛ mumɔŋ akɛ nyɛnaa nyɛhe tsui shi lɛ sɛɛ. Ni kɛ́ mɔ ko fee bo nɔ ko lɛ, feemɔ oya ni okɛke lɛ. ( Kol. 3:12-14 ) O kɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu nɛ pee kake kɛ tsu a yi mi tomi kaa wa Kristofohi a weku ɔ maa pee kake.— Yoh.

( Marko 3:21 ) Jamɔ mli hiɛnyiɛlɔi teɔ shi ewoɔ hegbɛ ni eyɔɔ akɛ eshiɛɔ lɛ be fɛɛ be. ( Luka 11:15, 16 ) E huɛmɛ nɛ e kɛ mɛ bɔ gbagbanii ɔ a kpɛti nɔ kake tsɔɔ lɛ se blɔ. ( Mat. 26:14-16 ) Osɔfo nikɔtɔma amɛ ya piɛɛ ma kudɔmi blɔ nya tomi ɔmɛ a he konɛ a bu lɛ gbenɔ fɔ. ( Maak. 15:1, 13-15 ) Se Yesu ha we nɛ si temi kɛ womi nɛ ha nɛ e kpa Yehowa sɔmɔmi. Ni ejieee oweletɔɔ su kpo kɔkɔɔkɔ. Benɛ a nu Yesu ɔ, bɔfo Petro bɔ mɔde kaa e maa fã e he. Petro fo osɔfonukpa lɛ tsulɔ ko toi. Shi Yesu kɛ yiwalɛ nifeemɔ ni Petro fee lɛ kpãaa gbee, ni etsɔ naakpɛɛ gbɛ nɔ etsa nuu lɛ pilamɔi lɛ. ( Luka 22:50, 51 ) Ngɛ si fɔfɔɛ nɛ ɔmɛ tsuo a mi ɔ, Yesu ha we nɛ nyagbahi nɛ e kɛ kpe ɔ nɛ je e juɛmi kɛ je e juɛmi mi, aloo e ha nɛ e tao owele.

12 Jã kɛ̃ nɛ mwɔnɛ ɔ hu ɔ, waa kɛ si fɔfɔɛ komɛ kpeɔ nɛ ma nyɛ ma ha nɛ wa kɔni mi nɛ jɔ̃. Satan bɔɔ mɔde kaa e ma ha nɛ wa ko tsu fiɛɛmi ní tsumi ɔ. Eko ɔ, wa weku mi bimɛ loo ni kpahi nɛ a he we yi ɔ maa te si kɛ wo wɔ aloo a maa wa wɔ yi mi po. Se kɛ̃ ɔ, Yesu tsɔɔ wɔ kaa waa suɔ nihi nɛ waa wɔ yi mi po. (Kane Mateo 5:43, 44 .) Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa ke a ngɛ wɔ yi mi wae ɔ, e sɛ nɛ waa kɛ nitsɛmɛ nɛ tsu ní. Mohu ɔ, ngɛ Yesu blɔ tsɔɔmi nɛ ngɛ heii ɔ se ɔ, wa bɔɔ mɔde wawɛɛ nɛ waa kɛ nimli tsuo maa hi si ngɛ mi mi jɔmi mi, wa toɔ wa tsui si, nɛ wa jeɔ bumi kpo kɛ haa nyumuhi tsuo po .

16 Baiblo ɔ ha nɛ wa ná nɔ mi mami kaa wa ma nyɛ maa ye mumi yayami asafo ɔmɛ a nɔ kunimi. ( Yak. 4:7; 1 Yoh. 5:4, 5 ) Ke waa kɛ mɛ ngɛ hwue ɔ, Mawu Munyu ɔ tsɔɔ kaa hemi kɛ yemi ji nɔ́ titli nɛ ma ha nɛ wa maa ye manye. ( Efe. 6:16 ) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, yaa nɔ nɛ o ma hemi kɛ yemi nɛ o ngɛ ngɛ Yehowa Mawu kɛ Yesu Kristo mi ɔ mi nɛ wa. Ha nɛ hemi kɛ yemi nɛ ɔ nɛ wo mo he wami nɛ o ya nɔ nɛ o suɔ o nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi, nɛ kake peemi nɛ hi o kɛ mɛ a kpɛti.

17 Nyɛ ha nɛ wa ko ngmɛɛ ga blɔ komɛ nɛ Abosiami maa gu nɔ ngɛ ligbi nɛ maa ba a mi konɛ e kɛ gba wa mi nɛ e je wɔ kɔni mi ɔ kɔkɔɔkɔ. Yehowa kɛ Yesu wo wɔ si kaa a maa hi wa blɔ fa mi be tsuaa be nɛ wa maa piɛɛ a he ɔ. Wa nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi peeɔ kake kɛ ngɛ ta nɛ waa kɛ ngɛ wa he nyɛlɔ titli nɛ ji Satan hwue ɔ mi. ( Luka 6:27, 28 ) No hewɔ lɛ, yaa nɔ ote shi owo Satan ŋaatsɔɔi lɛ, ni oná nɔmimaa akɛ obaaye kunim yɛ ta ni okɛ mumɔi fɔji lɛ wuɔ lɛ mli.

5 Paulo kɛ Filemon ye kaa e huɛ. ( File. 17 ) Paulo susuu we kaa e ma nyɛ maa fã Filemon kɛkɛ kaa e nyɔmtsɛ ji lɛ. ( 2 Kor. 1:24 ) Paulo bu Filemon kaa e kɛ e he piɛɛlɔ, nɛ e kai lɛ huɛ bɔmi nɛ a ngɛ ɔ. Mɛɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ kpakpa Paulo fee efɔ shi eha Filemon yɛ bɔ ni esa akɛ ekɛ Onesimo, ni ji enyɛmi nuu hee lɛ aye aha lɛ he nɛkɛ! Eji nɔkwɛmɔ nɔ kpakpa hu kɛha wɔ fɛɛ, titri lɛ kɛha mɛi ni sɔmɔɔ akɛ onukpai yɛ asafo lɛ mli lɛ. Onukpai ni kɛ mɛi krokomɛi yeɔ tamɔ amɛnanemɛi lɛ haa toiŋjɔlɛ baa. Ákɛ tookwɛlɔi ni yɔɔ suɔmɔ lɛ, ‘amɛyeee’ tooku lɛ nɔ. ( 1 Pet. 5:1-3 ) Mohu ɔ, a náa deka kɛ haa Mawu to ɔmɛ, a buɔ mɛ tue, a naa mɛ kaa a he jua wa, nɛ a yeɔ bua mɛ ke a hia mɛ. Taakɛ Paulo ji lɛ, onukpai ni kɛ mɛi krokomɛi yeɔ akɛ amɛnanemɛi lɛ feɔ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ kpakpa amɛhaa tooku lɛ, ni amɛyeɔ amɛbuaa koni shihilɛ ahi shi yɛ he ni toiŋjɔlɛ baanyɛ ashwere yɛ mli.

6 Paulo je Filemon yi. ( File. 4, 5, 7 ) Wɔ tsuo wa hia yi jemi. Maaba? Nɔ kome ji akɛ, wɔteŋ mɛi pii kɛ henumɔi ni haa mɔ tsui nyɔɔ emli lɛ wuɔ. Eko ɔ, wa ma hao ngɛ bɔ nɛ wa ngɛ ha a he, ke ni kpahi bua maa jɔ wɔ he, aloo sɔmɔmi he blɔ ko nɛ wa bua jɔ wawɛɛ ɔ maa je wa dɛ. Henumɔi nɛɛ ji nɔ ni yaa nɔ daa. Shi kɛlɛ, bei komɛi lɛ, amɛbaanyɛ amɛha wɔhao, wɔfee mɛi ni he yɔɔ oya fe nine, wɔfã wɔhe, loo wɔfee mɛi ni kɛ amɛhe woɔ akaŋshii mli po—ni ji sui ni baanyɛ atsĩ mɔdɛŋ ni wɔbɔɔ koni toiŋjɔlɛ aba lɛ naa. Mɔ ko baanyɛ adamɔ henumɔ ni eyɔɔ akɛ ebɛ shweshweeshwe lɛ naa jogbaŋŋ kɛ́ ajie eyi. (Abɛ 12:25) E haa nɛ e náa nɔ mi mami kaa a suɔ lɛ nɛ e bua jɔ e he. No hewɔ lɛ, efã gbɛ ni jɛkɛmɔ fe kilomitai 1,400 (kilomitai 900) ni efite shika babaoo ni etɔ lɛ koni eku esɛɛ kɛya Filemon ŋɔɔ. Ehe bahia ni efee klalo akɛ ebaaŋmɛɛ heyeli ni eyɔɔ lɛ he, ni ekolɛ ekpɛlɛ nɔ krokomɛi ni baajɛ enifeemɔi lɛ amli aba lɛ nɔ. Kulɛ nakai feemɔ nyɛŋ afee nɔ ni yɔɔ mlɛo. Je shishi kɛ kpatamɔ ni jɛ tsuiŋ. Feemɔ klalo akɛ obaabo mɔ kroko lɛ toi ni okpɛlɛ bɔ ni enuɔ he ehaa lɛ nɔ. Kpɛlɛmɔ sɔ̃ ni kã onɔ yɛ otɔmɔ lɛ he lɛ nɔ. Tsi ohe kɛjɛ onifeemɔi lɛ ahe ni ooobu loo fɔyeli lɛ ni oootsi kɛjɛ mɔ kroko nɔ lɛ he. Nakai feemɔ baanyɛ aha kpatamɔ lɛ agbɔjɔ, eha béi lɛ asɛɛ atsɛ, loo eha nibii aya hiɛ po. HE NI AKƐ WOƆ MLI Koni eye ebua wɔ ni wɔhiɛ asɔ nikeenii ni ji yiwalaheremɔ lɛ kɛ agbɛnɛ hu hegbɛ kɛ gbɛnaa nii ni fata he lɛ.

“MƐNI gbekɛ fioo nɛɛ baatsɔ?” ( Luka 1:57-66 ) Akutsoŋbii lɛ kɛ wekumɛi lɛ miisumɔ ni amɛle Yohane, ni ji binuu ni osɔfo Zakaria ni egbɔ lɛ kɛ eŋa onukpa Elizabet ni be ko ni eho lɛ eji kene lɛ fɔ lɛ yɛ naakpɛɛ gbɛ nɔ lɛ he sane. Yehowa gu Zakaria nɔ kɛ ha sane bimi nɛ ɔ heto. Etsɔɔ mli akɛ etsɛŋ ni Mesia lɛ ni akpa gbɛ aahu lɛ baaba, ni akɛ Yohane baasaa mɛi ni tsakeɔ amɛtsui lɛ koni amɛyoo nakai Mesia lɛ ni amɛnyiɛ esɛɛ. ( Amos 3:7; Luka 1:67-79 ) Se pi nɔ́ nɛ Yohane nɛ ji e binyumu ɔ maa pee ɔ pɛ nɛ Zakaria de lɛ. Ngɛ e sɛ gbi ɔ mi ɔ, wa maa kase níhi etɛ nɛ e sa kaa wa bua nɛ jɔ he: (1) yi wami hemi nike ní nɛ ji, (2) sɔmɔmi klɔuklɔu he blɔ nɛ wa ná, kɛ (3) sɔ̃ nɛ wa ngɛ kaa wa fiɛɛ ɔ.

gbɔmɛi nikeenii ko ni yɔɔ naakpɛɛ: yiwalaheremɔ. Shi yiwalaheremɔ kɛtsɔ namɔ nɔ? Zakaria de ke: “[Yehowa] je yi wami he walɔ ko si ha wɔ ngɛ David we ɔ mi.” ( Luka 1:69 ; hyɛ study note.) Nakai “yiwalaherelɔ ni he wa” lɛ ji Mesia lɛ. Yuda bi nɛ a hi si ngɛ Zakaria be ɔ mi ɔ ngɛ Mesia nɛ a wo e he si ɔ blɔ hyɛe momo, nɛ a le kaa e ma je Abraham nina kɛ je David matsɛ weku ɔ mi. (1 Mose 22:17, 18; Mat. 22:42 ) Lɛɛ mɛni he je nɛ Yehowa ha nɛ Zakaria fiɛɛ “yi wami he walɔ” nɛ ɔ nɛ e ba a he adafi ɔ? Ejaakɛ Mesia lɛ baa lɛ ebɛŋkɛ kpaakpa fe bei fɛɛ ni eho! Yɛ nyɔji enumɔ mli lɛ, oblayoo ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Maria lɛ baafɔ Yesu, ni taakɛ bɔfo Gabriel kɛɛ lɛ, abaatsɛ lɛ akɛ “Nyɔŋmɔ Bi” ni ebaaye nɔ akɛ Maŋtsɛ yɛ “etsɛ David maŋtsɛsɛi lɛ nɔ.” ( Luka 1:30-35 ) A tle “yi wami he walɔ” ɔ si benɛ a baptisi Yesu benɛ e ye jeha 30. AKƐ nikaselɔ ji mi lɛ, minyaaa skulyaa he miishɛɛ, ni mikasemɔ he nɔkwɛmɔnii hu ehiii kwraa. Misumɔɔ ni mikome too mafee nibii ni akadi mi. Eji anɔkwale akɛ mibɛ nɔ ko ni madamɔ nɔ makpa gbɛ akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ baakadi mi. Meni hewɔ?

Akɛni mida yɛ shihilɛ ko ni mɛi bɛ Nyɔŋmɔ ko mli yɛ Paris, France hewɔ lɛ, miheee Nyɔŋmɔ nɔ miyeee. Mohu ɔ, i kplɛɛ su tsakemi tsɔɔmi ɔ nɔ. Mikpɛlɛ nɔ ni sanebimɔ ko bɛ he taakɛ mɛi babaoo feɔ lɛ, ni misusu akɛ esa akɛ nɔ ni atsɔɔ yɛ skul lɛ afee anɔkwale. Nɛkɛ tsɔɔmɔ nɛɛ tamɔ nɔ ni jwɛŋmɔ yɔɔ mli, no hewɔ lɛ minaaa yiŋtoo ko ni madamɔ nɔ mapɛi odaseyeli kroko ni kɔɔ bɔ ni wala je shishi eha lɛ mli.

Mifɔlɔi hiɛ jwɛŋmɔ ni ejaaa yɛ jamɔ he. A susu kaa ke Mawu ko ngɛ ɔ, jinɛ nɔ́ nami kɛ dami sane yemi be je ɔ mi hyi tɔ. Ni akɛni mina osatofeemɔ babaoo yɛ jamɔ mli hewɔ lɛ, mi hu misusu akɛ eji kwashiai asane akɛ mahe Nyɔŋmɔ ni yɔɔ suɔmɔ lɛ maye. Miye mɛi ni heɔ Nyɔŋmɔ nɔ amɛyeɔ lɛ ahe fɛo po.

Shi kɛlɛ, amrɔ nɛɛ minaa nɔmimaa akɛ Bɔlɔ lɛ yɛ kɛkɛ, shi moŋ esusuɔ mihe, yɛ migbɔjɔmɔi lɛ fɛɛ sɛɛ. Ha magba bo nɔ ni tsake mijwɛŋmɔ kɛ bɔ ni nakai nɔmimaa lɛ tsake mishihilɛ.

Be ni miye afii nyɔŋma kɛ enyɔ lɛ, atsɛ mi kɛtee asraafoi anitsumɔ mli, ni akɛ mi tee French Guiana yɛ South America afi kome. Nɛkɛ he ni yɔɔ shɔŋŋ ni yɔɔ fɛo nɛɛ baaná minɔ hewalɛ ni hiɔ shi daa.

Be ni miye afii 20 lɛ, miku misɛɛ kɛtee Paris. Etsɛɛɛ ni mishihilɛ batsɔ otsi fɛɛ otsi ni mitsuɔ nii waa yɛ otsi lɛ mli, ni minuɔ dãa ni miyeɔ gbijurɔ waa yɛ otsi lɛ naagbee. Shi shihilɛ nɛɛ fee efolo. Miishɛɛ fɛɛ miishɛɛ hiɔ shi be kukuoo, ni yiŋtoo diɛŋtsɛ ko bɛ he. Minijiaŋjewui yɛ ju kɛ fɔ̃, hewolonɔ su mligbalamɔ, kɛ ohia ni yɔɔ maŋ lɛ mli lɛ hewɔ.

MITAO SHIHILƐ KPAKPA YƐ NUŊTSƆ KOO lɛ MLI

Miku misɛɛ kɛtee French Guiana akrowai ni yɔɔ shɔŋŋ lɛ ateŋ ekome mli, he ni misɔmɔ akɛ asraafoi anitsumɔ yɛ lɛ. Mihiɛ kã nɔ akɛ mishihilɛ baaná shishinumɔ babaoo kɛji akɛ adebɔɔ ebɔle mihe, ni mishɛreɔ tawa ni mikɛ mihe woɔ miishɛɛ mli. Shi yɛ be fioo ko sɛɛ lɛ, tsutsu nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ je kpo. Mitsu nii otsi muu lɛ fɛɛ bɔni afee ni manyɛ maya henaabuamɔ yɛ otsi lɛ naagbee.

Aaafee nakai be lɛ mli lɛ, mɔ ko ha mi Biblia. Mikane emli ekomɛi kɛ hiɛnɔkamɔ akɛ mana ŋaawoo kpakpa ni baaye abua mi ni mahi shi jogbaŋŋ. Shi minuuu nɔ ni mikaneɔ lɛ shishi.

Miyɛ nanemɛi ni amɛ hu amɛmiitao shishinumɔ ni yɔɔ shihilɛ mli, ni amɛfɔ̃ mi nine koni mikɛ amɛ ayahi amɛ akutso ni bɛ gbɛjianɔtoo mli lɛ mli yɛ nugbɔshiimɔ koo lɛ mli, he ko ni amɛtsɛɔ lɛ Ekomefeemɔ.

Yɛ koo lɛ mli lɛ, shihilɛ waaa, ni wɔnuɔ he akɛ wɔye wɔhe. Wɔfee nɔ fɛɛ nɔ yɛ wɔ diɛŋtsɛ wɔhe oyaiyeli naa ni wɔbuuu mɔ ko mɔ ko he akɔntaa. Wɔteɔ shi beni hulu lɛ teɔ shi lɛ, wɔwɔɔ beni hulu lɛ enyɔ shi lɛ, wɔduɔ wɔ niyenii, wɔtswaa lalai, ni wɔshɛreɔ tawa. Wɔkwɛɔ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ, ni wɔjaa nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni wɔyɔɔ lɛ wɔhaa wɔhe. Wɔyeɔ loo ejaakɛ wɔsumɔɔɔ ni wɔyeɔ kooloi lɛ awui. Wɔbɛ bulɛ tsɔ kɛha nɔyeli hegbɛi, ni wɔshashaooo shi akɛ wɔbaaku mla mli kɛji nakai feemɔ baahi aha wɔ, ni kɛ́ mɔ ko mɔ ko yeŋ awui.

Wɔmiisumɔ ni wɔfee je lɛ ekoŋŋ bɔ ni wɔsusuɔ akɛ esa akɛ efee lɛ. Mɔ ko mɔ ko bɛ wɔkutso lɛ mli ni yɔɔ hegbɛ yɛ mɛi krokomɛi anɔ, ni wɔbuɔ wɔhe ekɔɔɔ he eko he ni wɔjɛ. Beni mikpɛ nakai yiŋ lɛ sɛɛ nɔŋŋ lɛ, mikɛ tawa kɛ tsofai fɔji ni eshwɛ lɛ wo la mli ni mishɛreee tawa dɔŋŋ. Mibɔi kpeei komɛi yaa. Yɛ kpee ko shishi lɛ, mibi nyɛmi nuu ko koni eku miyitsɔi kakadaji lɛ, ni eku nakai yɛ Maŋtsɛyeli Asa lɛ nɔ tuutu.

Yɛ otsii fioo komɛi asɛɛ lɛ, mishi akutso lɛ mli yɛ French Guiana ni miku misɛɛ kɛtee France. Miná Maŋtsɛyeli Asa, ni mije mi-Biblia lɛ kasemɔ shishi ekoŋŋ. Eyɛ mli akɛ mishihilɛ etee hiɛ moŋ, shi miwekumɛi lɛ ayiŋ fee amɛ kɔshikɔshi yɛ bɔ ni mitsake kwraa lɛ he. Mishɛreee zigaret, minuuu dãa, ni miyaaa okpɔlɔŋmɛɛi ashishi dɔŋŋ. Shi kɛlɛ, amɛhiɛ esɔɔɔ mɔdɛŋ ni mibɔ yɛ gbɛ ni ejaaa nɔ akɛ mashiɛ maha amɛ lɛ. Sɛɛ mli lɛ, mibayɔse akɛ ehe miihia ni mikɛ hiɛshikamɔ atsu nii babaoo ni masusu amɛhenumɔi ahe.

I ha nɛ a baptisi mi ngɛ jeha 1996. I sɔle ha Yehowa kaa ma nyɛ ma pee ma se sane kpakpa fiɛɛlɔ kaa Michel. Etamɔ nɔ ni enɛ ji gbɛ ni hi fe fɛɛ ni matsɔ nɔ majie shidaa kpo matsɔɔ Yehowa.

Miyɛ gbalashihilɛ mlibotemɔ he miishɛɛ, shi klɛŋklɛŋ lɛ, miitao male kɛji Claudine yɛ maŋsɛɛ sanekpakpa shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ he miishɛɛ. Lɛ ji lɛ! Wɔbote gbalashihilɛ mli yɛ April afi 2000 lɛ mli.

Enɔ afi lɛ, wɔfee ekome kɛje gbɛgbamɔ nitsumɔ lɛ shishi. Be ko sɛɛ lɛ, wɔkpɛ wɔyiŋ akɛ wɔbaakase Mandarin koni wɔkɛye wɔbua Chinabii akuu ko ni yɔɔ asafo ko ni bɛŋkɛ wɔ lɛ mli lɛ. Ewa waa kɛha mi akɛ makase wiemɔ nɛɛ. Ehe bahia ni matsake mihe kɛya gbɛ hee ni mitsɔɔ nɔ misusuɔ nii ahe lɛ nɔ, ni mafee nɔ ko yɛ gbɛ ni yɔɔ mlɛo nɔ mikɛ anɔkwale lɛ haa. Anɔkwa, mikaseee Mandarin wiemɔ jogbaŋŋ kɔkɔɔkɔ. Shi mibɔ mɔdɛŋ fɛɛ ni manyɛ, ni wɔkɛ Chinese nyɛmimɛi hii kɛ yei lɛ ye afii babaoo ni wɔhiɛ kpaŋ nɔ ni yɔɔ naakpɛɛ. Jeee akɛ eji jɔɔmɔ kpele kɛkɛ, shi moŋ eji tsɔsemɔ ni hi jogbaŋŋ kɛha sɛɛ mli nitsumɔi.

Yɛ afii ni eho lɛ amli lɛ, ehe bahia ni maye naagbai anɔ kunim. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, i he ye kaa mɔde nɛ i ngɛ bɔe kaa ma ha Yehowa kɛ ye nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi ní kpakpahi ɔ pi kɔkɔɔkɔ. No hewɔ lɛ, mikɛ be babaoo fe bɔ ni ehe hiaa lɛ saa mitsɔɔmɔ nitsumɔi lɛ ahe.

Miisɔmɔ yɛ kpokpaa nɔkwɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ mli yɛ New Caledonia

Shi kɛlɛ, minijiaŋ je wui ejaakɛ be fɛɛ be lɛ, mishɛɛɛ mishishitoo mlai ni nɔ kwɔlɔ ni jwɛŋmɔ bɛ mli lɛ ahe. ( Jaj. 7:16 ) Ehe bahia ni makase akɛ Yehowa ji Tsɛ ni yɔɔ suɔmɔ ni ebiii ni wɔye emuu, shi moŋ ehiɛ sɔɔ nɔ ni hi fe fɛɛ ni wɔfeɔ lɛ.

Yɛ Paris lɛ, aha mi nitsumɔ akɛ kpokpaa nɔkwɛlɔ najiaŋdamɔlɔ. Minu he akɛ miji gbekɛ tsɔ ni mibɛ niiashikpamɔ kɛha nitsumɔ nɛɛ. Shi mikase akɛ Yehowa mumɔ krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ yeɔ ebuaa wɔ ni wɔtsuɔ babaoo fe bɔ ni wɔkome wɔbaanyɛ wɔtsu.

Mikɛ kaa kroko hu bɔɔ mɔdɛŋ. Kɛjɛ migbekɛbiiashi tɔ̃ɔ lɛ, hela ko ni haa mɔ he jɔ̃ɔ ehe fe nine lɛ miiye mi. Shihilɛ nɛɛ saa miniji ahe titri. Ewoɔ mli waa yɛ shihilɛi ni haa mɔ tsui nyɔɔ emli amli, tamɔ beni mikɛ toibolɔi miiwie yɛ kpokpaa nɔ kpee loo kpokpaa nɔ kpee shishi lɛ. Shi manyɛ mahã gbɔmɔtsoŋ nifeemɔ nɛɛ aba shi kɛtsɔ sɔlemɔ ni misɔleɔ mihaa Yehowa koni eha mi jwɛŋmɔŋ toiŋjɔlɛ lɛ nɔ. Naagba lɛ sɛɛ efooo, shi kɛ́ mikɛ mihe fɔ̃ Yehowa nɔ babaoo lɛ, mifeɔ kpoo ni mihe waaa.

Ngɛ jeha 2011 ɔ mi ɔ, Yehowa je mi mi himi mi nɛ e ha wɔ ní bimi ko nɛ wa tsui bi lɛ kɛ je blema a. Wɔje maŋsɛɛ sanekpakpa shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ shishi yɛ New Caledonia, ni ji ŋshɔkpɔ ko ni yɔɔ Pasifik wuoyi-anaigbɛ, ni kɛ Paris jɛkɛmɔ fe kilomitai 16,000 lɛ nɔ. Nitsumɔ nɛɛ hu kɛ naagbai ba. Ehe bahia ni wɔtsake wɔhe wɔwo niyenii heei tamɔ ŋmei, taro, kɛ ŋmei. Ni ehe bahia ni wɔkɛ ŋmeii ni tswaa kɛ gbeei ni tswaa wɔwɔ lɛ mli leebi maŋkpa lɛ akase wɔhe. Ehe bahia ni wɔtsake wɔhe wɔwo sanegbaa gbɛ hee ko hu mli. Bei pii lɛ, maŋbii lɛ haa hetoo, jeee kɛ wiemɔi, shi moŋ kɛtsɔ amɛhiŋmɛii ni amɛwoɔ nɔ kɛkɛ nɔ.

Jehanɛ se hu ɔ, Yehowa ye bua wɔ nɛ wa nyɛ nɛ wa tsake wa he ngɛ teokrase ní tsumi ehehi a mi. Wɔsɔmɔ akɛ maŋsɛɛ sanekpakpa shiɛlɔi, ni wɔkɛ afii fioo ko sɔmɔ akɛ Betelbii. Wa ya Sukuu Kɛ Ha Matsɛ Yemi Sane Kpakpa Fiɛɛli ɔ, nɛ amlɔ nɛ ɔ, wa ngɛ kpokpa nɔ hyɛmi ní tsumi ɔ mi. Wa na kaa Yehowa gu e mumi klɔuklɔu ɔ nɔ kɛ ye bua wɔ ngɛ ní tsumi fɛɛ ní tsumi nɛ wa ngɛ tsue ɔ mi. Etsɔɔ mi akɛ jeee akɛ wɔ diɛŋtsɛ wɔgbɔjɔmɔi haa wɔhe ehiaaa lɛ kwraa. No hewɔ lɛ, misɔleɔ shii abɔ kɛ hiɛdɔɔ koni aye abua mi koni manyɛ matsu nɔ ni he hiaa lɛ he nii. Eyɛ mli akɛ loloolo lɛ, minuuu he akɛ mihe ejɔɔɔ mi yɛ toibolɔi ahiɛ moŋ, shi kɛtsɔ Nyɔŋmɔ yelikɛbuamɔ nɔ lɛ, minyɛɔ mikɛ hewalɛ kɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔ haa asafoi ni wɔsaraa amɛ lɛ.

Yɛ tsakemɔi nɛɛ fɛɛ mli lɛ, Claudine eye ebua mi be fɛɛ be. Etoɔ etsui shi waa yɛ mihe ni miyeee lɛ he. Efiɔ wɔsɛɛ yɛ nitsumɔ ni wɔtsuɔ lɛ mli, ni efee klalo be fɛɛ be akɛ ekɛ ekãa baaye odase. Ewieee huhuuhu yɛ bɔ ni kɔɔyɔɔ lɛ mli dɔɔ waa kɛ naagbai ni yɔɔ kpokpaa nɔkwɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ mli lɛ he.

Yɛ miklɛŋklɛŋ kpee lɛ shishi lɛ, mikase akɛ Yehowa, ni ji Bɔlɔ Kpeteŋkpele lɛ susuɔ ejálɔi ahe be fɛɛ be. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, i ná jɔɔmihi fuu. Yehowa eye ebua mi ni miná jwɛŋmɔ ni ja yɛ hegbɛ he, ni miba mihe shi. ANI OLE?

Mɛɛ nifeemɔi yaa nɔ yɛ maŋ lɛ agbói lɛ anaa yɛ Biblia beaŋ?

MAŊ lɛ agbói lɛ fe he ni aboteɔ mli kɛkɛ. Akɛni mɛi babaoo tsɔɔ amɛmli hewɔ lɛ, amɛbatsɔmɔ hei ni mɛi babaoo kɛ amɛhe woɔ daa gbi shihilɛ mli. Biblia lɛ tsiɔ maŋ lɛ agbói lɛ atã fe shii 50, ni etsɔɔ nibii ni tee nɔ yɛ jɛmɛ lɛ amli.

Gbɔmɛi bua amɛhe naa yɛ maŋ lɛ agbói lɛ anaa. Hebri wiemɔ kɛha “agbó” lɛ kɔɔ babaoo he fe maŋ ko hiɛ shinaa kɛkɛ. Etsɔɔ he ko ni ama yɛ maŋ loo maŋ ko shinaa naa. Yɛ hei komɛi lɛ, ayɛ kpoji wuji yɛ agbói lɛ amli ni mɛi babaoo baanyɛ akpe yɛ jɛmɛ.— Neh. 8:1 lɛ nɔ.

Jarayelɔi toɔ jarayeli hei yɛ maŋ lɛ agbói lɛ ahe. Eko ɔ, agbo komɛ nɛ ngɛ Yerusalɛm kaa Loo Agbó ɔ kɛ To Agbó ɔ, a ná biɛ ɔmɛ kɛ je níhi nɛ a jua ngɛ lejɛ ɔ kasa nya a mi.​— Zef. 1:10; Yohane 5:2 he.

Mɛi tsuɔ jarayeli kɛ amɛ diɛŋtsɛ amɛsaji fɛɛ ahe nii yɛ maŋ lɛ agbói lɛ anaa. Abraham kɛ Boaz kɛ amɛhe woɔ nibii amli yɛ odasefoi ahiɛ yɛ maŋ lɛ agbói lɛ anaa. ( 1 Mose 23:10-15, 17, 18; Rut 4:1, 10 ) Mɛi komɛi ni fãa gbɛ lɛ baaye jenamɔ lɛ yɛ gbɛjegbɛ ni bɛŋkɛ maŋ lɛ agbói lɛ anɔ lɛ nɔ, kɛ́ mɔ ko efeee amɛ nibwɔ. ( Koj. 19:15-21 ) Nihi yaa ma a agbo ɔmɛ a nya hu konɛ a ya nu adafi fiamihi nɛ ngɛ nɔ yae.​—2 Sam. 18:24, 25 lɛ he.

Kojolɔi yeɔ kojomɔ he yɛ he ko ni bɛŋkɛ maŋ lɛ agbói lɛ. Amɛkpɛɔ amɛyiŋ yɛ saji ahe yɛ mɛi ni kwɛɔ ni baanyɛ anu odaseyeli lɛ kɛ nɔ ni baajɛ mli aba lɛ fɛɛ hiɛ. (5 Mose 21:18-20) Ma nɔ he nɛ ɔ woɔ kojoli ɔmɛ he wami konɛ a ye dami sane ngɛ a juɛmi nya si. Beni kojolɔi lɛ kpɛ amɛyiŋ sɛɛ lɛ, onukpai lɛ tsu fɔyeli lɛ he nii amrɔ nɔŋŋ. ( 5 Mo. 25:2 ) Yɛ maŋ lɛ agbói lɛ anaa lɛ, .

akpa gbɛ akɛ kojolɔi lɛ baaye jalɛ sane kɛ jeŋba kpakpa he hewalɛ.— 5 Mo. 16:​18-20; Hiob 29:7, 11-17 lɛ he.

Hii anɔkwafoi wo Israelbii ni yɔɔ maŋ lɛ agbói lɛ anaa lɛ hewalɛ koni amɛkɛ amɛhiɛ afɔ̃ Yehowa nɔ. Gbalɔi kɛ mɛi krokomɛi wieɔ yɛ agbói lɛ anaa ejaakɛ amɛle akɛ mɛi buaa amɛhe naa yɛ jɛmɛ. (1 Ma. 22:10; Yer. 17:19, 20) Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, Yehu jaje ngɛ ni ɔmɛ tsuo a hɛ mi kaa Yehowa ha nɛ munyu nɛ e tu kɛ si Ahab we ɔ ba mi. ( 2 Maŋ. 10:8-10 ) Maŋtsɛ Hezekia bua etatsɛmɛi lɛ anaa yɛ maŋ lɛ agbó lɛ naa koni ewo amɛ hewalɛ ni amɛkɛ amɛhiɛ afɔ Yehowa yelikɛbuamɔ nɔ.— 2 Kron. 32:6-8 he.

Biblia lɛ kɛɔ akɛ “anɔkwa nilee” bolɔɔ waa ni ewoɔ egbee nɔ “yɛ maŋ lɛ agbói lɛ anaa.” ( Abɛ 1:20, 21 ) Blema Israel ɔ, ma a agbo ɔmɛ a nya ji nɛ nyumu anɔkualetsɛmɛ woɔ nihi ga nɛ nile ngɛ mi, nɛ a mwɔɔ yi mi kpɔ nɛ da ngɛ. Ŋmɛnɛ, aŋma Yehowa nilee lɛ he sane yɛ Biblia lɛ mli ni etsuji ni yɔɔ he fɛɛ he lɛ miiye amɛmiibua gbɔmɛi ni amɛmiiba nilee diɛŋtsɛ jɛɛhe nɛɛ ŋɔɔ.

BIBLIA MLI ANƆKWALE Tso ni Teɔ Shi Mra

Almond tso lɛ ji tsei sɔrɔtoi aaafee 30 ni Biblia lɛ wieɔ he lɛ ateŋ ekome. Efata tsei ni fɔɔ fɔfɔi mra fe fɛɛ yɛ fɛ̃i be sɛɛ lɛ ahe. Abaanyɛ ana efɔfɔi ni yɔɔ fɛo ni ji pinki loo yɛŋ lɛ yɛ January naagbee gbɛ. Eja jogbaŋŋ akɛ Hebri gbɛi ni akɛtsɛɔ almond tso lɛ shishi diɛŋtsɛ ji “mɔ ni teɔ shi.” Ani nɔ ko yɛ ni jeɔ nakai tso lɛ kɛ e-Bɔlɔ lɛ teŋ?

Yeremia 7:25 lɛ shishigbɛ niŋmaa lɛ tsɔɔ akɛ Yehowa wieɔ ehe akɛ eteɔ shi mra. March 15, 2011 Buu-Mɔɔ lɛ kɛɛ akɛ: “Kaa bɔ ni almond tso lɛ ‘te shi’ mra lɛ, nakai nɔŋŋ Yehowa hu ‘teɔ shi mra’ yɛ okadi gbɛ nɔ koni ebɔ ewebii lɛ kɔkɔ yɛ nɔ ni baajɛ toigbele mli aba lɛ he. agbe naa.”— Yer. 1:11, 12 lɛ he.

Akɛ almond tso lɛ fɔfɔi yɛŋ lɛ hu tsu nii yɛ Biblia lɛ mli kɛmiitsɔɔ yitsoŋ yɛŋ ni gbɔlɛ baa lɛ mli. ( Jaj. 12:5 ) Ni eeenyɛ efee akɛ akɛni fɔfɔii lɛ yɛ fɛo waa hewɔ lɛ, amɛfee kpului ni yɔɔ kanetso lɛ niji lɛ anɔ lɛ ahe mfoniri.— 2 Mo. 37:17-20 he.
