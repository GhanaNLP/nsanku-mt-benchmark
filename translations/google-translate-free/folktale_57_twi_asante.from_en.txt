Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

ABƆFRA-KWASI-GYINAMOA

BERE bi na abofra bi wɔ hɔ, Kwasi Gyinamoa, na ɔwɔ nuabeanom baawɔtwe, na wɔn mu biako mpo nni hokafo, enti wosiim sɛ wɔrekɔ baabi akɔhwehwɛ okununom. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, “Me ne wo bɛkɔ.” Wobuae se, “Yɛn ne wo renkɔ.” Wɔn mu panin no kaa sɛ, “Ma ɔne yɛn nkɔ, na sɛ yɛduru (wim) kwan no so, na nsɔe to yɛn mu a, ɔbɛtumi atwe ama yɛn.” Wosiim, na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kɔ (ne wɔn). Woduu asubɔnten bi ho, na ne fã bi sen sɛ dɔte fitaa; ne fã bi sen (te sɛ) dɔte kɔkɔɔ; na ne fã bi sen (te sɛ) fango tuntum. Wɔteɛɛm sɛ, “Eh, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ na yɛatwa asubɔnten yi?” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa ne (nkrabea) nantwi-dua-switch, bɔɔ (nsu no), na ɔmaa nsuo no mu paapaee mmienu, na wɔfaa mu, na wɔtoaa so. Wɔbaa kuro bi mu; na ɛyɛ kurow kɛse paa. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, “Kurow no yɛ dinn (i.e. komm gye cicada a ɛrebɔ gyegyeegye), so onipa biara nni mu?” Afei Anomaa bi kasae se, “Obi wɔ fie hɔ, kɔ so.” Wɔkɔɔ so, na ɔbaa panyin bi wɔ hɔ, na ne ti da ne nan so, na ɔrehwehwɛ ntontom wɔ mu. Bere a ohuu Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, afei ɔmaa ne ti so de sii ananmu; ɔkaa sɛ, “Me mmanana, adɛn nti na, bere a obiara mma ha no, moaba ha?” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buaa sɛ, “Me nuabeanom adi mfe awotwe, na wonnyaa kununom a wɔne wɔn bɛware, na enti wɔkae sɛ wɔbɛba abɛhwehwɛ okununom a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔware wɔn.” Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, “Oo, ɛno nko ara, ɛfiri sɛ me nso, mewɔ mma baawɔtwe.” Ɔbea panyin-nana somaa ne banana sɛ ɔmmɛfrɛ ne mma. Wɔbaa hɔ no, oyi kaa sɛ, “Me kunu ni,” saa na ɔkaa sɛ, “Me kunu ni.” Bere a wɔn nyinaa wiee wɔn baanu baanu no, afei ɔbaa panyin no kɔfaa aduru bi a wɔfrɛ no _dokum_, na ɔkyekyeree ne mma kɔn mu. Afei obiara ne ne yere kɔdae. Na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔɔ akwakoraa no nkyɛn kɔgyee n’ahome. Anadwo dui no, akwakoraa no hyerɛn ne sẽ a ɛyɛ kɔkɔɔ a ɛyɛ hyew sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛkɔ akɔkum mmeawa no. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, “E! e! e!” Akwakoraa no kaa sɛ, “Ɛyɛ dɛn?” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, “Sɛ mewɔ m’ankasa m’akuraa ase, na sɛ mekɔ ahome nanso mintumi nna a, ɛnde na maame twa yam srade bi de gu ogya mu, na ɔfa katabi a ntokuru wom na ɔkɔ na ɔde nsu petepete mu de brɛ me ma mennom.” Akwakoraa-nana sii kwan so sɛ ɔrekɔ asuo no ho, na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kɔe na ɔde afɛfɛdeɛ a wɔfrɛ no _dokum_ no firii akwakoraa no mma kɔn mu, na ɔfaa wɔn de sɛn ne nuabeanom’ so. Akwakoraa no san firii nsuo no mu baeɛ; ɔkae sɛ, “Mebɔɔ mmɔden sɛ mɛpete nsu (wɔ saa afiri a wɔde yiyi mu no mu), nanso mantumi anpetepete so.” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae se, “Kɔ na kɔfa atɛkyɛ, na ntetew nhaban na fa gu (afiri a wɔde yiyi mu), na fa na fa petepete.” Akwakoraa no kɔyɛɛ saa, na ɔde nsuo petepetee mu, na ɛyɛɛ yie. Ɔde baeɛ, na ɔyii yam no firii ogya no so, na ɔde maa Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa sɛ ɔnni, na ɔnom nsuo no nso. Afei ɔtow ne ho guu fam na ɔdae. Ɔbea panyin-nana sɔree; ɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛyɛ me.” Ɔhyerɛn ne sẽ, (kosii sɛ ɛhyerɛn) kɔkɔɔ, na ɔne no kɔe kɔhyɛn dan no mu; ɔtee nka sɛ ɔde ne nsa abɔ mmofra no kɔn mu, na nea _dokum_ aduru no nni ne kɔn so no, ɔde ne sẽ hyewee. Saa kwan yi so na okum mmofra baawɔtwe no. Na onnim sɛ ɛyɛ n’ankasa mma ankasa na wakum wɔn. Bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ntɛm paa, paa, paa Akwakoraa-nanabea sii gyinae sɛ ɔrekɔ afuw no mu. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuabeanom na ɔmaa wɔn dwane. Ɔbea panyin-nanabea fi afuw no mu san bae. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, “Akwakoraa-nana e! Akwakoraa-nanabea e! sɛ wonyɛ ɔkwasea a, anka wobɛkum w’ankasa wo mma? Me deɛ, me ne me nuabeanom rekɔ.” Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, “Ɛnyɛ hwee, nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi) no wɔ ha.” Ɔkɔɔ, na Akwakoraa-nana nso danee ne ho yɛɛ ababaa fɛfɛ, na ɔkuraa gourd-rattle, na ɔkɔɔ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa akuraa ase. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Onipa a ɔbɛtumi afa agyan de atutu akuturuku yi no, mɛware no.” Nnipa a na wɔwɔ akuraa no ase nyinaa (bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ) wɔbɛbɔ mu, nanso wɔantumi. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa twitwaa mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Maware wo." Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, merenware wo." Enti ne papa nua barima waree no. Ɔne no kɔdaeɛ, nanso ɔtetew n’ani mmienu, na ɔne no kɔeɛ. Afei abeawa kumaa no kaa sɛ, "Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa wɔ hɔ! Matutu wo papa nua barima aniwa abien na me ne wɔn rekɔ." Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae se, "Momfa wɔn nkɔ, efisɛ nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi) wɔ hɔ." Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, wɔkae sɛ, "Abofra, kɔ na wo ho tɔ wo na fa wo papa nua barima ani san ba. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Merenkɔ. " Wɔhyɛɛ no nkuran na wɔhyɛɛ no nkuran, na (awiei koraa no) osiim. Afei, Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nim sɛ Akwakoraa-nanabea banana no yɛ _enceinte_, enti ɔno nso, onyaa calabash na ɔbɔɔ ne nsam no guu ne yafunu so, na ɔkyekyeree ne ntama guu so, na ɔsiim sɛ ɔrekɔ Akwakoraa-nanabea akuraa ase no, ɔsuiɛ sɛ, "Dɛn na asɛm no teɛ?" Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ no yie, ɛha na saa ɔkwasea a ɔsan woo no no benkum ani, Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, papa nua deɛ, na n'ani nifa nso wɔ ha." Sɛ wokɔ (baabi a wo kunu anifuraefo no gyina) a ɛsɛ sɛ woka sɛ, 'M'ani huruw di akɔneaba, m'ani mu da hɔ.'" Ankyɛ na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nsa kaa wɔn, na ɔkae sɛ, "Wo wɔ hɔ, Akwakoraa-nana, eyinom ne me papa nua ba aniwa a wotutu fii mu, na me ne wɔn rekɔ." Akwakoraa-Nanabea kae sɛ, "Ɛno mfa ho, nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi) no wɔ hɔ." Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa baa fie, na ɔfaa aniwa no de sii ananmu wɔ ne papa nua barima no sockets mu Ɔkae sɛ, "M'ani huruw, m'ani da hɔ," na ne papa nua barima ani buei pefee anɔpa a edi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, mmofra no nyinaa (wɔ akuraa no ase) kɔɔ mu sɛ wɔrekɔguare asubɔnten no.Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Ebia wowɔ nkwaboaa dua?" off with him to her village. Afei Akwakoraa-nanabea kɔfaa kɛntɛn na ɔkataa no so, na ɔde nnuadewa too ogya so, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Merekɔ akɔyɛ ayɛyɛdeɛ abusuakuo no, na afei mɛba abɛkum Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa." abotan nketenkete na ofii ase twitwiw wɔn, pupru! Akwakoraa-nanabea banana kaa sɛ, "Dɛn ne nea worewe?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Tiger-nuts." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma me bi na menwe." Obuae se, "Bue me ho." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Anka wobɛkɔ." Obuae se, "Dabi, merenkɔ." Ɔyii ne ho, na ɔpueeɛ; na akwakoraa no banana rebɔ nnuadewa. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa palm-nut pestle no, bɔɔ Akwakoraa-nanabea banana, na okum no. Afei ɔyii Akwakoraa-nanabea banana were, na ɔhyɛɛ ne ho. Ɔbea panyin-nana san bae; ɔkaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na mo nyinaa ho agu fĩ saa?" Obuae se, "Makum Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa; mede no ato ogya mu." Saa asɛm yi yɛɛ dɛ ma Akwakoraa-nanabea, na ɔsaw na ɔmaa ne nne so (na ɔtoo dwom): "Abofra, woakum Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, Da bi wobɛyɛ obi."

Ɔnoa aduan no wiei, wɔtenaa ho, na wodii. Ɔbea panyin-nanabea de dede kɛse petepetee soup no mu (de dede kɛse guu n’anom), fweo! fweo! Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Worepete soup (ahyɛ w'anom) a wɔde wo banana ti ayɛ." Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, "Dɛn ne ɛno na woka?" Obuae sɛ, "Ná mereka sɛ nkyene biara nni saa soup-stew yi mu." Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔfa bi." Enti ɔkɔfaa bi. Wodidi wiee, na Akwakoraa-nana kaa se, "Afei a makum Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, bra ha, me banana." Bere a ɔbaa hɔ no, ɔfaa sika kɔkɔɔ nneɛma nketenkete de siesiee no fɛfɛɛfɛ. Afei, Akwakoraa-nanabea hwɛɛ n’anim bere bi. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ah! abofra yi anim kae me Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa deɛ." Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Nanabea, adɛn nti na, bere a wunim sɛ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa bae bɛkumm m'abusuafo baawɔtwe no nyinaa (so wokasa saa), efisɛ woma me di awerɛhow." Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, "To w'ano." Afei abofra no brɛɛ ne ho ase wɔ fie hɔ. Nanabea no tew so fii hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma menkɔ na mentow ngu midden-heap no so." Enti Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa soaa no kɔe. Bere a oduu midden-heap no ho no, oyii sika kɔkɔɔ agude a akwakoraa no de asiesie no nyinaa kyekyeree. Afei ɔkaa sɛ, "Ho hɔ, Akwakoraa-nana, ho hɔ, Akwakoraa-nana, me ne no, Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, afei ne mprenu so, maba akum wo banana, na ɛno da nkyɛn a, mafa wo sika kɔkɔɔ agude." Akwakoraa-nana buae sɛ, "Mekaa sɛ w'anim te sɛ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa de; afei baabiara a wobɛkɔ no, me ne wo kɔ." Afei wɔn baanu nyinaa tuu mmirika kɔe, kiri! kiri! kiri! Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa duu n’akuraa ase, na ɔde sika kɔkɔɔ nkukuo no maa ne mogya-abusuafoɔ, na ɔkɔɔ kwan no so, na ɔde ne ho siee ne nkyɛn, na Akwakoraa-nana nso baa hɔ. Ɛno nti na, sɛ wonam ɔkwan bi so a, wobɛte nnyigyei bi a ɛyɛ gyegyeegye, "pe." Ɛno ne Akwakoraa-nana-nana a ɔtaa Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
