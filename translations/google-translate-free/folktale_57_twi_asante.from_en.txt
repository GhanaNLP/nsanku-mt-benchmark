ABƆFRA-KWASI-GYINAMOA

BERE bi na abofra bi wɔ hɔ, Kwasi Gyinamoa, na ɔwɔ nuabeanom baawɔtwe, na wɔn mu biako mpo nni hokafo, enti wosiim sɛ wɔrekɔ baabi akɔhwehwɛ okununom. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, “Me ne wo bɛkɔ.” Wobuae se, “Yɛn ne wo renkɔ.” Wɔn mu panin no kaa sɛ, “Ma ɔne yɛn nkɔ, na sɛ yɛduru (wim) kwan no so, na nsɔe to yɛn mu a, ɔbɛtumi atwe ama yɛn.” Wosiim, na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kɔ (ne wɔn). Woduu asubɔnten bi ho, na ne fã bi sen sɛ dɔte fitaa; ne fã bi sen (te sɛ) dɔte kɔkɔɔ; na ne fã bi sen (te sɛ) fango tuntum. Wɔteɛɛm sɛ, “Eh, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ na yɛatwa asubɔnten yi?” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa ne (nkrabea) nantwi-dua-switch, bɔɔ (nsu no), na ɔmaa nsuo no mu paapaee mmienu, na wɔfaa mu, na wɔtoaa so. Wɔbaa kuro bi mu; na ɛyɛ kurow kɛse paa. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, “Kurow no yɛ dinn (i.e. komm gye cicada a ɛrebɔ gyegyeegye), so onipa biara nni mu?” Afei Anomaa bi kasae se, “Obi wɔ fie hɔ, kɔ so.” Wɔkɔɔ so, na ɔbaa panyin bi wɔ hɔ, na ne ti da ne nan so, na ɔrehwehwɛ ntontom wɔ mu. Bere a ohuu Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, afei ɔmaa ne ti so de sii ananmu; ɔkaa sɛ, “Me mmanana, adɛn nti na, bere a obiara mma ha no, moaba ha?” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buaa sɛ, “Me nuabeanom adi mfe awotwe, na wonnyaa kununom a wɔne wɔn bɛware, na enti wɔkae sɛ wɔbɛba abɛhwehwɛ okununom a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔware wɔn.” Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, “Oo, ɛno nyinaa teɛ t, efisɛ me nso, mewɔ mma baawɔtwe.” Ɔbea panyin-nana somaa ne banana sɛ ɔmmɛfrɛ ne mma. Wɔbaa hɔ no, oyi kaa sɛ, “Me kunu ni,” saa na ɔkaa sɛ, “Me kunu ni.” Bere a wɔn nyinaa wiee wɔn baanu baanu no, afei ɔbaa panyin no kɔfaa aduru bi a wɔfrɛ no _dokum_, na ɔkyekyeree ne mma kɔn mu. Afei obiara ne ne yere kɔdae. Na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔɔ akwakoraa no nkyɛn kɔgyee n’ahome. Bere a anadwo dui no, ɔbea panyin no hyerɛn ne sẽ a ɛyɛ kɔkɔɔ a ɛyɛ hyew sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛkɔ akɔkum mmeawa no. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, “E! e! e!” Akwakoraa no kaa sɛ, “Ɛyɛ dɛn?” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, “Sɛ mewɔ m’ankasa m’akuraa ase, na sɛ mekɔ ahome nanso mintumi nna a, ɛnde na maame twa yam srade bi de gu ogya mu, na ɔfa katabi a ntokuru wom na ɔkɔ na ɔde nsu petepete mu de brɛ me ma mennom.” Akwakoraa-nana sii kwan so sɛ ɔrekɔ asubɔnten no mu, na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kɔe na ɔde afɛfɛdeɛ a wɔfrɛ no _dok no hwirew um_ firii akwakoraa no mma kɔn mu, na ɔfaa wɔn de sɛn ne nuabeanom’. Akwakoraa no san firii nsuo no mu baeɛ; ɔkae sɛ, “Mebɔɔ mmɔden sɛ mɛpete nsu (wɔ saa afiri a wɔde yiyi mu no mu), nanso mantumi anpetepete so.” Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae se, “Kɔ na kɔfa atɛkyɛ, na ntetew nhaban na fa gu (afiri a wɔde yiyi mu), na fa na fa petepete.” Akwakoraa no kɔyɛɛ saa, na ɔde nsuo petepetee mu, na ɛyɛɛ yie. Ɔde baeɛ, na ɔyii yam no firii ogya no so, na ɔde maa Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa sɛ ɔnni, na ɔnom nsuo no nso. Afei ɔtow ne ho guu fam na ɔdae. Ɔbea panyin-nana sɔree; ɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛyɛ me.” Ɔhyerɛn ne sẽ, (kosii sɛ ɛhyerɛn) kɔkɔɔ, na ɔne no kɔe kɔhyɛn dan no mu; ɔtee nka sɛ ɔde ne nsa abɔ mmofra no kɔn mu, na nea _dokum_ aduru no nni ne kɔn so no, ɔde ne sẽ hyewee. Saa kwan yi so na okum mmofra baawɔtwe no. Na onnim sɛ ɛyɛ n’ankasa mma ankasa na wakum wɔn. Bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ntɛm paa, paa, paa Akwakoraa-nanabea sii gyinae sɛ ɔrekɔ afuw no mu. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nso kɔka kyerɛɛ ne nuabeanom na ɔmaa wɔn dwane. Ɔbea panyin-nanabea fi afuw no mu san bae. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, “Akwakoraa-nana e! Akwakoraa-nana e! sɛ wonyɛ ɔkwasea a, anka wobɛkum w’ankasa wo mma? Me deɛ, me ne me nuabeanom rekɔ.” Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, “Ɛnyɛ hwee, deɛ ɔnim (sɛdeɛ ɔne wo bedi) no wɔ ha.” Ɔkɔɔ, na Akwakoraa-nana nso danee ne ho bɛyɛɛ ababaa fɛfɛ, na okura akutu-rattle, na ɔkɔɔ Child-Kwasi-Gyinamoa akuraa ase. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Onipa a ɔbɛtumi afa agyan de abɔ saa akuturuku yi no, mɛware no.” Nnipa a na wɔwɔ akuraa no ase nyinaa (bɔɔ mmɔden sɛ) wɔbɛbɔ mu, nanso wɔantumi. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa twitwaa mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Maware wo." Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, merenware wo." Enti ne papa nua barima waree no. Ɔne no kɔdaeɛ, nanso ɔtetew n’ani mmienu, na ɔne no kɔeɛ. Afei abeawa kumaa no kaa sɛ, "Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa wɔ hɔ! Matutu wo papa nua barima aniwa mmienu na me ne no rekɔ." Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa buae sɛ, "Fa wɔn na kɔ, ɛfiri sɛ deɛ ɔnim (sɛdeɛ ɔne wo bedi) wɔ ha." Ade kyeeɛ anɔpa, berɛ a nneɛma daa adi no, wɔkaa sɛ, "Abofra, kɔ na wo ho tɔ na fa wo papa nua barima ani san ba. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Merenkɔ." Wɔhyɛɛ no ​​na wɔhyɛɛ no ​​nkuran, na (awiei koraa no) osiim. Afei, na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nim sɛ Akwakoraa-nanabea nanabea yɛ _enceinte_, enti ɔno nso, nyaa calabash bi na ɔbɔɔ ne nsam wɔ ne yafunu mu, na ɔkyekyeree ne ntama guu so, na ɔde sii kwan so sɛ ɔrekɔ Akwakoraa-nanabea akuraa ase. Bere a oduu kurow no awiei no, osui. Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, "Dɛn ne asɛm no?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Na me ne me kunu reba, na ɔduruu kokoɔ no atifi no ɔhwee fam na ɛno maa n'ani mmienu puei na ɛhwee fam." Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ no yie, ɛha na saa ɔkwasea a ɔsan woo no no benkum ani, Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, papa nua deɛ, na n'ani nifa nso wɔ ha." Sɛ wokɔ (baabi a wo kunu anifuraefo no gyina) a ɛsɛ sɛ woka sɛ, 'M'ani huruw di akɔneaba, m'ani mu da hɔ.'" Ankyɛ na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa nsa kaa wɔn, na ɔkae sɛ, "Wo wɔ hɔ, Akwakoraa-nana, eyinom ne me papa nua ba aniwa a wotutu fii mu, na me ne wɔn rekɔ." Akwakoraa-Nanabea kae sɛ, "Ɛno nnyɛ hwee, nea onim (sɛnea ɔne wo bedi) no wɔ ha." -Gyinamoa baa fie, na ogyee aniwa no de sii ananmu wo ne papa nua no sockets mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "M'ani huruw, m'ani mu da hɔ," na ne papa nua barima ani buee pefee. Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, mmofra no nyinaa (wɔ akuraa no ase) kɔguaree wɔ asubɔnten no mu. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Menguare." Wɔkaa sɛ, "Ebia wowɔ nkwaboaa dua?" Wɔkaa saa no, ne nufu mu yɛɛ no ​​yaw, na oguare. Ankyɛ na ohuruw kɔɔ nsu no mu no, na Akwakoraa-nanabea kyeree no, twom! Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, manya wo," na ɔne no kɔɔ n'akuraa ase. Afei Akwakoraa-nana kɔfaa kɛntɛn na ɔkataa ne so, na ɔfaa nnuadewa, na ɔde too ogya mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Merekɔ onion abusuakuo no mu, merekɔ spice abusuakuo no mu, na afei mɛba abɛkum Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa." Ɔsiim kwan, na Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa boaboaa abotan ano na ɔfirii aseɛ twitwaa mu, pupru! pupru! Akwakoraa-nanabea banana kaa sɛ, "Dɛn ne nea worewe?" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Tiger-nuts." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma me bi na menwe." Obuae se, "Bue me ho." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Anka wobɛkɔ." Obuae se, "Dabi, merenkɔ." Ɔyii ne ho, na ɔpueeɛ; na akwakoraa no banana rebɔ nnuadewa. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa faa palm-nut pestle no, bɔɔ Akwakoraa-nanabea banana, na okum no. Afei ɔyii Akwakoraa-nanabea banana were, na ɔhyɛɛ ne ho. Ɔbea panyin-nana san bae; ɔkaa sɛ, "Adɛn nti na mo nyinaa ho agu fĩ saa?" Obuae se, "Makum Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa; mede no ato ogya mu." Saa asɛm yi yɛɛ dɛ ma Akwakoraa-nanabea, na ɔsaw na ɔmaa ne nne so (na ɔtoo dwom): "Abofra, woakum Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, Da bi wobɛyɛ obi."

Ɔnoa aduan no wiei, wɔtenaa ho, na wodii. Ɔbea panyin-nanabea de soup no petepetee (de dede kɛse bi guu n’anom), fweo! fweo! Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa kaa sɛ, "Worepete soup (ahyɛ w'anom) a wɔde wo banana ti ayɛ." Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, "Dɛn ne ɛno na woka?" Obuae sɛ, "Ná mereka sɛ nkyene biara nni saa soup-stew yi mu." Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔfa bi." Enti ɔkɔfaa bi. Wodidi wiee, na Akwakoraa-nana kaa se, "Afei a makum Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa no, bra ha, me banana." Bere a ɔbaa hɔ no, ɔfaa sika kɔkɔɔ nneɛma nketenkete de siesiee no fɛfɛɛfɛ. Afei, Akwakoraa-nanabea hwɛɛ n’anim bere bi. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ah! abofra yi anim kae me Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa deɛ." Abofra no kaa sɛ, "Nana, adɛn nti na, ɛberɛ a wonim sɛ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa ba bɛkumm m'abusuafoɔ baawɔtwe no nyinaa (so wokasa saa), ɛfiri sɛ woma me di awerɛhoɔ." Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, "To w'ano." Afei abofra no brɛɛ ne ho ase wɔ fie hɔ. Nanabea no yii mu akyirikyiri. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma menkɔ na mentow ngu midden-heap no so." Enti Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa soaa no kɔe. Bere a oduu midden-heap no ho no, oyii sika kɔkɔɔ agude a akwakoraa no de asiesie no nyinaa kyekyeree. Afei ɔkaa sɛ, "Ho hɔ, Akwakoraa-nana, ho hɔ, Akwakoraa-nana, me ne no, Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa, afei ne mprenu so, maba akum wo banana, na ɛno da nkyɛn a, mafa wo sika kɔkɔɔ agude." Akwakoraa-nana buae sɛ, "Mekaa sɛ w'anim te sɛ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa de; afei baabiara a wobɛkɔ no, me ne wo kɔ." Afei wɔn baanu nyinaa tuu mmirika kɔe, kiri! kiri! kiri! Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa duu n’akuraa ase, na ɔde sika kɔkɔɔ nkukuo no maa ne mogya-abusuafoɔ, na ɔkɔɔ kwan no so, na ɔde ne ho siee ne nkyɛn, na Akwakoraa-nana nso baa hɔ. Ɛno nti na, sɛ wonam ɔkwan bi so a, wobɛte nnyigyei bi a ɛyɛ gyegyeegye, "pe." Ɛno ne Akwakoraa-nana-nana a ɔtaa Abofra-Kwasi-Gyinamoa.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
