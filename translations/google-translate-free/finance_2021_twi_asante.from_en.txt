NKYERƐKYERƐMU A ƐFI ƆSOAFOƆ A ƆHWEHWƐ SIKASƐM HO

Ghana nnipa pa wɔ December 2020 mu no too aba sɛ wɔbɛkora H.E. Ɔmanpanyin, Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo ma ahennie bio sɛ ​​ɔbɛtoa adwuma pa no so na wayɛ pii. Ahotosoɔ a yɛn mfɛfoɔ Ghanafoɔ de ahyɛ Aban yi mu no yɛ deɛ yɛmfa nyɛ hwee.

Afe 2021 Budget no yɛ sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛyɛ nwonwa a ɛsan hyehyɛ yɛn ɔman sikasɛm de bɔ nkwa ho ban, san ma nnwuma a ɔhaw wɔ mu no nya ahoɔden, na yɛde adwene si yɛn ahoɔden so sɛ yɛbɛsan akyekye Ghana pa, wɔ COVID-19 akyi. Budget no gyina yɛn anisoadehunu a yɛde bɛsakra ɔman yi na yɛakyekye sikasɛm a ɛbɛtumi ahwɛ ne ho so.

Wɔ afe a edi kan no fã kɛse no ara mu no, yɛasan de sikasɛm no akɔ ne kwan so, denam nhyehyɛe ahorow a wɔayɛ ho nhyehyɛe yiye a ɛfa sikasɛm mu nsakrae akɛse ho so, de akwan horow a ɛbɛma sika a wonya no anya nkɔso aba, de nneɛma a ɛbɛma wɔanya nkɔso adi dwuma, yɛaboa ankorankoro adwumayɛkuw ahorow, na yɛde asetra mu nnwuma a wɔde wɔn ho bɛhyɛ mu ho nhyehyɛe ahorow adi dwuma. Ɛduruu afe 2019 awieeɛ no, na yɛwɔ sikasɛm mu nsakraeɛ kwan so pintinn wɔ sikasɛm mu nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ akɛseɛ a ɛyɛ nwonwa ne amanaman ntam nkaeɛ sɛ ɔman a ɛrekɔ soro, berɛ a COVID-19 ɔyaredɔm no bɔɔ yɛn no. Nkɛntɛnso a ɛyɛ hu a ɔyaredɔm yi anya no, sikasɛm nyinaa ate nka wɔ wiase nyinaa, a Ghana ka ho.

Covid-19 Yaredɔm a ɛpae gui no de nsɛnnennen pii bae a aka yɛn nnwuma ne da biara da asetena. Nanso, aban de adwennwene kɛse ahyɛ nkɛntɛnso a ɔyaredɔm no anya wɔ yɛn so sɛ nnipa no nsow na wɔahyɛ nhyehyɛe ahorow a ɛbɛboa ma wɔasan akanyan nnwuma na ama yɛn asetra asan ayɛ sɛnea na ɛte kan no ase. Anidaso nyinaa nsɛe efisɛ yɛanya akwan foforo a yɛfa so yɛ nneɛma a nneɛma a enni awiei betumi aba. Aban asi ne bo sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ sɛ yɛbɛnya nnipa dodoɔ a wɔwɔ apɔmuden na sɛdeɛ ɛteɛ no wayɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ama Ghanafoɔ de vaccination ahwɛ sɛ yɛn nyinaa bɛtena ahoɔden mu de akyekye Ghana a yɛpɛ sɛ yɛhunu no.

Yɛne mo ayɛ adwuma wɔ nsɛnnennen mu mpo sɛ yɛn nkɔsoɔ nhyehyɛeɛ no fã na yɛatu anammɔn akokoɔduro so de yɛn sikasɛm asan akɔ nkɔsoɔ kwan so. Mprempren Ghanafo bɛyɛ ɔpepem 1.2 betumi anya ntoaso sukuu a eye na ɛyɛ papa wɔ wɔn nkwa nna nyinaa mu, yɛatew sikakorabea adwuma no ho na adetɔfo dodow no ara anya wɔn sika a wɔde asie no afi sikakorabea ahorow a abubu no hɔ. Bio nso, wɔasi adwumayɛbea a wɔboaboa kar ano na yɛahu kar a wɔaboaboa ano a edi kan wɔ yɛn gua ne akwan so.

Yɛgye tom nokwarem sɛ yehyiaa nsɛnnennen bi bere a yɛbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ yɛbɛhyɛ yɛn nkɔso botae ahorow ho nkuran no. Aban de anibere reyɛ nneɛma bi de adi ɔhaw ahorow yi ho dwuma na ama nnwuma anya ahotɔ. Sɛ nhwɛsoɔ no, yɛde GH¢750 ɔpepem CAP-BuSS Dwumadie no sii hɔ wɔ May 2020 mu na yei ama mmoa ama Micro, Small and Medium-Sized Enterprises (MSMEs) ne afoforɔ bebree.

Te sɛ nea ɛnni da no, wɔafrɛ yɛn sɛ yɛmfa yɛn nsa nka yɛn ho na yɛnhyɛ yɛn man no nkɔso denam nnwuma a wɔde akokoduru ayɛ a yɛbɛtoa so de adi dwuma de anya nkɔso ama obiara no so. Ne nyinaa akyi no, yɛde yɛn ahoɔden nyinaa ne yɛn koma nyinaa hyɛɛ bɔ sɛ yɛbɛsom Ghana.

Nyame nhyira yɛn nyinaa.

Ghana nkwa nna!

Ken Ofori-Atta Ɔmanpanyin a ɔhwɛ Sikasɛm so

NNIANIMU

1. Afe 2021 Ɔmanfoɔ Budget no yɛ deɛ Aban no atumi ayɛ wɔ afe 2020 mu ne dodoɔ a wabɔ ne tirim sɛ ɔbɛboaboa ano wɔ sika a wɔbɛnya mu de adi dwuma de ayɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ne nhyehyɛeɛ wɔ afe 2021. Afe 2021 Budget no asɛmti ne “Wɔawie, Wɔbɛka abom, na wɔatoa so”. Saa asɛmti yi gyina nokwasɛm a ɛyɛ sɛ mfeɛ pii mu no, Budget foforɔ biara kura nnwuma foforɔ pii a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔhyɛ aseɛ a wɔbɛbɔ nnwuma a ɛkɔ so no ho ka. Ɛnyɛ sɛ eyi de nhyɛso ba yɛn sikasɛm so nko, na mmom abakɔsɛm mu no ɛde akatua a wɔde tua ho ka nso aba, a wontua adwumayɛfo pii ka wɔ bere ano wɔ adwuma a wɔayɛ ho. Eyi ma nnwuma ho ka nyinaa kɔ soro bere a sika a wotua a wɔatu ahyɛ da no twetwe mfɛntom ho ka no.

2. Wɔ afe 2021 mu no, Aban ayɛ nhyehyɛɛ sɛ wɔbɛhyehyɛ akwankyerɛ foforɔ. Botae no ne sɛ wɔde nnwuma a wonwiei ho amammerɛ bɛba awiei denam bɔhyɛ ahorow a wobedi so sɛ wobewie nnwuma a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw no so.

SIKASƐM NKYERƐKYERƐMU A ƐBƐYƐ AFE 2020

Sikasɛm mu Adwumayɛ Kɛse

3. Bere tiaa mu nsɛm a ɛfa sɛnea sikasɛm no bɛyɛ adwuma wɔ afe 2020 mu no te sɛ nea edidi so yi:

• Asram akron a edi kan wɔ afe no mu no, sɛ wɔkyekyem pɛpɛɛpɛ a, GDP ankasa nkɔso a ɛyɛ ɔha biara mu nkyem 0.2. Wɔhwɛ kwan sɛ GDP bɛkɔ soro ɔha biara mu nkyem 0.9 wɔ afe no awiei;

• GDP ankasa a ɛnyɛ Petrol no nyaa nkɔanim ɔha biara mu nkyem 0.4 wɔ asram akron a edi kan wɔ afe no mu, na wɔhwɛ kwan sɛ ebewie afe no ɔha biara mu nkyem 1.6;

• December awiei nneɛma bo a ɛkɔɔ soro no yɛ ɔha biara mu nkyem 10.4 bere a wɔde toto botae a wɔayɛ mu nsakrae a ɛyɛ ɔha biara mu nkyem 11.0 ho no;

• Sikasɛm mu sika a wɔsɛee no nyinaa yɛ GDP ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 11.7 wɔ botaeɛ a wɔayɛ mu nsakraeɛ a ɛyɛ GDP ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 11.4 ho;

• Titiriw a ɛkari pɛ no kyerɛe sɛ GDP mu ɔha biara mu nkyem 5.3 na ɛkaa sika wɔ GDP mu ɔha biara mu nkyem 4.6 a wɔde asi wɔn ani so no ho; ne

• Amanaman Ntam Sika a Wɔde Sie no nyinaa yɛ U.S. dɔla ɔpepepem 8.6, a ɛne asram 4.1 a wɔde kataa nneɛma a wɔde fi amannɔne ba so no yɛ pɛ.

Ɔmanfoɔ Ɛka Nkɔsoɔ a ɛbɛba wɔ afe 2020 mu

7. Ɛka dodoɔ a ɛwɔ December 2020 awieeɛ no gyinaa GH¢291.6 ɔpepepem (US$50.8 ɔpepepem), a ɛyɛ GDP ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 76.1. Saa nsusuiɛ yi ka sika a Aban boaboaa ano de tua sikasɛm mu mmoa a wɔde ma ne ahoɔden adwumayɛkuo no Independent Power Purchase (IPPs) tumi ho ka. Sɛ wɔamfa sikasɛm mu mmoa no amma a, na afe 2020 ɛka a wɔde too gua no yɛ GDP ɔha biara mu nkyem 71.5. Ɛka a wɔde asie no mu nkyekyɛmu te sɛ nea edidi so yi:

• Abɔnten so ɛka: GH¢141.7 ɔpepepem (U.S. dɔla ɔpepepem 24.7) a egyina hɔ ma ɛka a ɛwɔ hɔ nyinaa mu ɔha biara mu nkyem 48.6; ne

• Ɔman no mu ɛka: GH¢149.8 ɔpepepem (U.S. dɔla ɔpepepem 26.1) a egyina hɔ ma ɛka a ɛwɔ hɔ nyinaa mu ɔha biara mu nkyem 51.4.

Sikasɛm mu Botaeɛ Kɛseɛ a Wɔde Sisi Afe 2021 mu

8. Aban sikasɛm nhyehyeɛ ne nneyɛeɛ (i.e. toɔ ne gyinaesie a ɛfa sika a wɔsɛe no ho) ka sikasɛm mu nkɔsoɔ wɔ mfimfini berɛ mu. Sikasɛm mu botaeɛ anaa nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ akɛseɛ a ɛdidi soɔ yi na ɛhyɛ afe 2021 Budget no ase:

• GDP ankasa nkɔsoɔ a ɛyɛ ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 5.0;

• GDP ankasa a ɛnyɛ Petrole no nkɔanim ɔha biara mu nkyem 6.7;

• December awiei nneɛma bo a ɛkɔɔ soro ɔha biara mu nkyem 8.0;

• Sikasɛm mu sika a ɛho ayɛ na a ɛyɛ GDP ɔha biara mu nkyem 9.5;

• Sika a wɔsɛe no titiriw a ɛyɛ GDP ɔha biara mu nkyem 1.3; ne
