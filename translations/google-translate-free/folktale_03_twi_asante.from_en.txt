SƐNEA ƐBAE SƐ NYANSƐM BAA ABUSUE NO MU

WƆKA sɛ na Kwaku, Akɔre no, wɔ hɔ, na ɔprapraa nimdeɛ nyinaa, boaboaa ano wɔ baabi biako, na ɔde guu akutu kuku mu. Afei ɔpaee mu kae sɛ ɔbɛforo dua na wakɔ akɔsɛn so, na nyansa a ɛwɔ asase so nyinaa awie.

Enti ɔfaa no sɛ ɔne no nkɔ, na ɔduruu dua no ase baabi a ɔrebɛsɛn no no, ɔfaa hama, na ɔkyekyeree no wɔ akutuo no ho, na ɔde sɛn n’anim, na ɔde ne ho sii hɔ sɛ ɔbɛforo dua no. Ɔforoo, na ɔforoo, na ɔforoo; kwa. Ɔperee bio, bio ɔyɛɛ sɛ ɔbɛforo, na waforo, na waforo; kwa.

Afei, ne ba, Ntikuma a na ogyina hɔ no kae sɛ, "Oo, akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ w'ani awu (aniwu nti), so anka ɛrenyɛ papa sɛ wodan wo ho twaa akutu no ho hyia na wode to w'akyi, afei akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ anka wubetumi aforo?" Ɔno (Akɔre no) kaa sɛ, "Pefe, wo ne wo nsɛm dedaw no." Afei ɔdanee ne ho sɛ ɔreforo bio sɛnea na ɔyɛ kan no, nanso ɔsan nso, wansow aba. Afei osusuw tenten ho, na (awiei koraa no) ɔfaa akutu no de too n’akyi. Afei ɔde ne ho sii hɔ sɛ ɔbɛforo, na ɔforoo ntɛmntɛm, Kra! kra! kra! (na ne foro no nnyigyei); ɛhɔ na ɔkɔ. Oduu baabi a nkorabata no fii ase trɛw fii dua no mu, na ɔkae (kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ), "Me, Kwaku Ananse, nam onyame ketewa, Afio so! Ebia ɛbɛyɛ sɛ mawu, me ba a ɔyɛ ketewa paa, ketewa paa, ketewa paa—na mewɔ hɔ, meboaboaa nyansa nyinaa ano (enti mesusuwii) wɔ beae biako, nanso ebinom kaa hɔ a me mpo manhu, na hwɛ! me ba, eyi da so ara-su." akokoaa, akyerɛ me." Afei ɔkyeree saa akutu no, na ɛte sɛ nea ɛrepaapae, tintini! na ɔtow gui, na ɔpetepete nnyigyei bi bae, tesee! Saa na obiara nyaa nyansa; na obiara a wankɔ hɔ wɔ bere ano (sɛ ɔrekɔfa bi) yɛ —pa kyɛ me sɛ meka saa —ɔkwasea.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
