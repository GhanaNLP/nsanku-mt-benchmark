SƐNEA ƐBAE SƐNEA ƐBAE SƐ AKWANKYERƐ NO TE FFE MU BERƐ BIARA

BERE bi na Ɔkraman ne Ɔkraman bi te hɔ, na Ɔkraman no kae sɛ, “Momma yɛnkɔ ntwa sanku na yɛmmɔ so.” Wosiim kɔɔ kwae no mu, na wohyiaa dua bi a wɔde sirikyi ayɛ. Akɔre no huu sirikyi kotokuo dua no ara pɛ na ɔkaa sɛ, “Oyi yɛ me dea, ɛfiri sɛ, me deɛ, me ne ɔpanin no, ɛno nti mɛtwa ɔkɛseɛ yi.” Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, “Mate.” Ɔkraman no nso, berɛ a ɔrehwɛ ne ho no, ɔhunuu sɛ “Cedar” dua bi gyina hɔ, ɔno nso kɔtwaa. Wɔn baanu nyinaa dii sanku. Bere a Vulture no bɔɔ ne sanku no na ɛte sɛ eyi ara pɛ:

Futuru! Futuru! Manya (me) ntɛmntɛm!

Akɔre Kwakye! Manya (me) ntɛmntɛm!

Bere a Ɔkraman no bɔɔ ne de no, na ɛte sɛ eyi:

Ɔkraman, Kwa! Kwa!

Ɔkraman, Kwa! Kwa!

Wɔkɔɔ so dii agorɔ, na Wim-nyame no tee, na ɔsomaa Ɔkraman (sɛ ɔmfrɛ wɔn); na Ɔkraman no bae, (na ɔkamfoo wɔn) "Ago!" Na wobuaa se, “Ame!” Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wim-nyame no se wate asaw-nnwom bi wɔ ha enti ɛsɛ sɛ wode ba na wobɔ ma ɔte." Abɔde no sii kwan so na wɔkɔɔ Wim-nyame no anim; na wɔbɔɔ, na wɔbɔɔ, na wɔbɔɔ, na Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, "Mate sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ; nnwom abien no yɛ dɛ, enti mede biribi rema mo (biara)." Afei Wim-nyame no kɔfaa nnaka mmienu de sii hɔ, na baako akyi yɛ fɛ yie sɛ wobɛhunu; efĩ to brim te sɛ eyi mu; ɔfoforo nso, nea ɛyɛ abofono yiye; sika kɔkɔɔ kosi ano te sɛ eyi mu. Wim-nyame no kasae, (kaa sɛ), "Mo baanu mu biara mmra mmɛfa." Vulture no kaa sɛ, "Me ne ɔpanyin no, enti ma menkɔ na menkɔfa bi kan." Ɔkɔe no, ɔpaw nea ɛyɛ fɛ yiye no, bere a Ɔkraman no nso faa nea ɛyɛ abofono yiye no. Wɔmaa wɔn so kɔɔ wɔn akuraa ase. Bere a wɔkɔe sɛ wɔrekɔbue wɔn no, Ɔkraman no, bere a ɔhwɛɛ ne slime a ɛho ntew no mu no. Wɔ Crow no mu no, bere a ɔhwɛɛ mu no, sika kɔkɔɔ hyerɛn kɔkɔɔ sɛ biribiara. Ɛnkyɛe koraa na ɔbɔfoɔ bi baeɛ na ɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔkraman (ɔkaa sɛ), "Wɔsoma me sɛ menka sɛ w'asew awu." Ɔno (Vulture) kaa sɛ, "Crow, ma me sika kɔkɔɔ nugget baako a menkyekyere wɔ me basa so sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a mɛkɔ ayie." Ɔfaa nugget no na ɔkyekyeree no, na ɔkɔɔ ayi no ase. Bere a oduu hɔ no, ɔkae sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ ɔkɔ na ɔbrɛ ne ho ase; ɔkotow, nanso bere a ɔrebɛsɔre no, nugget no hwee ase, na ɛyerae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oo, Ɔkraman agyapadeɛ ayera, na mɛyɛ dɛn asan akɔ fie?" Enti ohuruw kɔɔ efĩ no mu na ɔhwehwɛɛ (nugget no). Ɛno nti na wobɛhunu Ɔkraman no, bere nyinaa ɔrebɔ, ɔrebɔ, wɔ tiafi, ɛfiri sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ Ɔkraman no nugget sɛ ɔde bɛbrɛ no.

Ɛno nti na Mpanyimfo no ka sɛ, "Adifudepɛ nyɛ papa."

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebia wobɛfa ebinom sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no betumi akamfo me (wɔ nea wɔaka no ho).
