NƆ NI AKƐ WOƆ MLI Wɔbaanyɛ wɔye Yehowa anɔkwa ekɔɔɔ he eko nɔ ni wɔkɛ hemɔkɛyeli he kaai baakpe.

"Nuŋtsɔ, namɔ ŋɔɔ wɔbaaya? Bo oyɛ naanɔ wala wiemɔi."-YOHANE 6:68.

"WIEMƆ nɛɛ haa mɔ naa kpɛɔ ehe; namɔ baanyɛ abo toi?" ( Yohane 6:60 ) Jã ji bɔ nɛ Yesu kaseli ɔmɛ fuu pee a ní ngɛ munyuhi nɛ Yesu tu benɛ e ngɛ ní tsɔɔe ngɛ Kapernaum ɔ he ɔ. Yesu de momo ke: “Ke nyɛ ye nɔmlɔ Bi ɔ he lo nɛ nyɛ nu e muɔ ɔ, nyɛ be wami ngɛ nyɛ nitsɛmɛ nyɛ mi.” Ni etsa nɔ akɛ: “Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni yeɔ miheloo lɛ ni enuɔ milá lɛ yɛ naanɔ wala, ni matee lɛ shi yɛ naagbee gbi lɛ nɔ.”— Yohane 6:53, 54 .

2 Nihi nɛ Yesu munyu ɔ ha nɛ a mi mi fu wawɛɛ ɔ kpa e se nyɛɛmi. Ani amɛbɛ emli hemɔkɛyeli ko kwraa? Yɛ anɔkwale mli lɛ, dani sane nɛɛ baaba lɛ, mɛi komɛi—ni Yesu kaselɔi lɛ fata he lɛ—na nɔ ni Yesu fee lɛ he miishɛɛ waa aahu akɛ amɛkɛɛ: “Gbalɔ ni ba je lɛŋ lɛ nɛ.” ( Yoh. 6:14 ) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e ngɛ heii kaa jamɛ a kaseli ɔmɛ ná hemi kɛ yemi ngɛ Yesu mi kɛ ya su. Se benɛ a ka a hemi kɛ yemi ɔ, a ya nɔ nɛ a ye lɛ anɔkuale.

Wɔhe wɔye ni wɔle akɛ bo ji Nyɔŋmɔ Mɔ Krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ.” ( Yohane 6:68, 69 ) Mo kadi kaa Petro de we ke e kɛ bɔfo kpa amɛ nu nɔ́ nɛ Yesu de mɛ ke e sa nɛ a ye e he lo nɛ a nu e muɔ ɔ sisi saminya. Ekɔɔɔ he eko nɔ ni amɛnu shishi yɛ nakai beaŋ lɛ, amɛhala akɛ amɛbaaya nɔ amɛye Yesu anɔkwa. Mɛni hewɔ? Ejakaa a hemi kɛ yemi ɔ da si ngɛ anɔkuale ko nɛ a be nyɛe maa je he nya—Yesu ji “Mawu Kristo ɔ nɛ.”​— Luka 9:20, 35 .

A ma nyɛ maa gu blɔ slɔɔtohi a nɔ kɛ ka anɔkuale nɛ wa yeɔ Yehowa a. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, ke wa nu kaa a pee tsakemi ko ngɛ Baiblo ngmami ko sisi numi sisi, aloo a tsake bɔ nɛ a toɔ kaselɔ peemi ní tsumi ɔ he blɔ nya ha a, kɛ wa peeɔ wa ní ha kɛɛ? Aloo ke e he wa ha wɔ kaa wa maa nu nɔ́ he je nɛ Yehowa ngmɛ blɔ nɛ waa kɛ haomi ko kpeɔ ɔ sisi ɔ, kɛ wa peeɔ wa ní ha kɛɛ? Kɛ́ wɔnaanyo ko ni bɛŋkɛ wɔ kpaakpa ni ji Kristofonyo lɛ fee wɔ dɔlɛ loo mɔ ko ni wɔbuɔ lɛ waa lɛ kɛ wɔ ye yɛ gbɛ ni tamɔ nɔ ni ejaaa nɔ lɛ hu baanyɛ aka wɔhemɔkɛyeli lɛ akwɛ.

Bɔni afee ni wɔtsɔɔ bɔ ni ehe hiaa ni wɔná shishitoo ni ma shi shiŋŋ kɛha wɔhemɔkɛyeli lɛ, susumɔ tsu ko ni ama yɛ he ko ni shikpɔŋhosomɔi fɔɔ baa lɛ he okwɛ. Shishitoo ni wa he miihia tsu ni tamɔ nɛkɛ bɔni afee ni enyɛ ehi shi. Jã kɛ̃ nɛ loko wa ma nyɛ maa je wa hemi kɛ yemi he kahi nɛ a tu he munyu ngɛ kuku nɛ sɛ hlami ɔ mi ɔ a mi ɔ, e sa nɛ wa hemi kɛ yemi ɔ nɛ ná sisi tomi nɛ mi wa. Ke wa ya nɔ nɛ wa ha nɛ wa hemi kɛ yemi sisi tomi ɔ mi wa a, wa maa ya nɔ maa pee Yehowa sɔmɔli nɛ a fĩ si, nɛ a yeɔ anɔkuale ngɛ kahi a mi.​— 1 Kor. 15:58; kane Kolose Bi 1:23 ɔ.

Bɔfo ɔmɛ ná nɔ mi mami kaa Yehowa sa jami ejakaa lɛ nɛ e bɔ níhi tsuo; akɛ Yesu ji Nyɔŋmɔ Bi, ni yɔɔ “naanɔ wala wiemɔi lɛ”; nɛ kaa Yehowa fĩ Yesu se kɛ pi si. Amrɔ nɛɛ, yɛ nɔ najiaŋ ni wɔɔsusu gbɛ̀i pɔtɛɛi babaoo ni abaanyɛ atsɔ nɔ aka wɔhemɔkɛyeli lɛ akwɛ lɛ he lɛ, nyɛhaa wɔkɛ wɔjwɛŋmɔ amaa anɔkwalei etɛ ni baanyɛ aye abua wɔ ni wɔhi shi shiŋŋ yɛ kaai ni wɔkɛbaakpe lɛ kɔɔɔ he eko nɔ. Nomɛi ji: (1) Yehowa ji mɔ ni bɔ nibii fɛɛ. (2) Yehowa ha nɛ a ngma Baiblo ɔ. (3) Yehowa fĩ e we bi a se mwɔnɛ ɔ. Ke wa ná nɔ mi mami wawɛɛ ngɛ anɔkuale etɛ nɛ ɔmɛ a he ɔ, wa maa ya nɔ maa ye anɔkuale ke waa kɛ wa hemi kɛ yemi ɔ ngɛ kahi kpee.

YEHOWA JI BƆLƆ

7 Kanemɔ Kpojiemɔ 4:11 . Ngmami nɛ ɔ kaiɔ wɔ kaa Yehowa lɛ bɔ níhi tsuo. Anɔkwa sane nɛɛ pɛ haa esa akɛ wɔjá lɛ. Ni kɛ́ wɔjwɛŋ ehewalɛ ni yɔɔ naakpɛɛ lɛ nɔ lɛ, wɔnáa nɔmimaa akɛ ebaanyɛ ejie wɔ kɛjɛ wɔkaai lɛ fɛɛ amli. Shi kɛlɛ, Satan kɛ saji ni ato he gbɛjianɔ koni eha adesai ayiŋ afee amɛ kɔshikɔshi akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ yɛ lɛ eyi je nɛɛ mli obɔ. Eko ɔ, je mi si kpali ma de ke wami ba lɛ nitsɛ e he. Ke wa be nɔ mi mami kulaa kaa Yehowa ji Bɔlɔ ɔ, nɛ e suɔ wɔ ɔ, eko ɔ, wa yi mi maa pee wɔ enyɔɔnyɔ kaa Yehowa ji lɛ, nɛ e maa ye bua wɔ ke waa kɛ wa hemi kɛ yemi he kahi kpe.​— Heb. 11:6 lɛ he.

8 Kɛ́ oyiŋ bɔi bo kɔshikɔshi akɛ Yehowa ji Bɔlɔ lɛ, tsu he nii amrɔ nɔŋŋ. Taomɔ saji amli jogbaŋŋ yɛ wɔwoji lɛ amli koni ona saji ni kɔɔ odaseyeli babaoo ni tsɔɔ akɛ Yehowa ji mɔ ni bɔ nibii fɛɛ lɛ he. Obaanyɛ ona saji babaoo ni wajeɔ mɔ hemɔkɛyeli yɛ sane nɛɛ he yɛ woji bibii ni ji Was Life Created? kɛ The Origin of Life—Five Questions (Saji Enumɔ).

7. Mɛni Kpojemi 4:11 ɔ kaiɔ wɔ, se mɛni ma nyɛ maa ba ke waa kɛ wa hemi kɛ yemi he ka kpe?

8-9 lɛ nɔ. Ke o bɔni o yi mi peemi kaa Yehowa ji Bɔlɔ ɔ, mɛni e sa kaa o pee? (Kwɛmɔ mfoniri lɛ hu.)

Ekolɛ okane wolo bibioo ni ji Was Life Created? Mɛni hewɔ okaneee ekoŋŋ? (Hyɛ kuku 8)

Esa akɛ Abi. Awie odaseyelii krokomɛi ni maa nɔ mi lɛ ahe yɛ saji ni nyiɛ sɛɛ ni ji “Ani Afee Lɛ?” ni yɔɔ jw.org lɛ nɔ lɛ. Nyɛmi yoo oblayoo ko ni yɔɔ United States, ni atsɛɔ lɛ Jessica lɛ kɛɛ akɛ: “Beni mikane Origin of Life wolo bibioo lɛ sɛɛ lɛ, miná hekɛnɔfɔɔ akɛ anɔkwalei ni yɔɔ mli lɛ tsɔɔ akɛ Bɔlɔ ko ni yɔɔ nilee ji mɔ ni fee nɔ fɛɛ nɔ. Agbɛnɛ hu, mɛi babaoo ená wolo ni ji Is There a Creator Who Cares About You? Ekolɛ okane sane nɛɛ momo. Shi mɛni hewɔ okaneee ekoŋŋ?

Kɛha oteemɔŋ nikasemɔ ni nyiɛ sɛɛ lɛ, obaanyɛ osusu anɔkwa saji ni maa nɔ mi akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ yɛ lɛ ahe. Jeee akɛ niiamlitaomɔ ni ooofee lɛ baaye abua bo koni ohemɔkɛyeli akɛ Yehowa yɛ shihilɛ mli lɛ mli awa kɛkɛ, shi moŋ ebaaha ole bɔ ni Yehowa ji lɛ jogbaŋŋ. ( Rom. 1:20 ) Kɛfata he lɛ, obaanyɛ okɛ nɔ ni okaseɔ lɛ aye abua mɛi krokomɛi ni ekolɛ amɛyiŋ feɔ amɛ kɔshikɔshi akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ yɛ lɛ. Oti titri: Yehowa eha wɔ odaseyelii babaoo ni tsɔɔ akɛ lɛ ji wɔ-Bɔlɔ.

YEHOWA HA AKƐ ŊMA BIBLIA LƐ

Kane 2 Timoteo 3:16, 17. Baiblo ɔ ji sɛ gbi nɛ Yehowa ngɔ kɛ ha adesahi. Ehaa shihilɛ mli sanebimɔi ni he hiaa lɛ ahetoo, ni etsɔɔ wɔ nɔ ni ji Yehowa suɔmɔnaa nii kɛ nɔ ni etaoɔ ni wɔfee. Wa he ye kaa Yehowa ji Baiblo ɔ Ngmalɔ, nɛ lɔ ɔ ha nɛ Baiblo ɔ pee womi nɛ he hia pe kulaa ngɛ wa si himi mi. Wa suɔ nɛ wa ná nɔ mi mami kaa wa nu e Munyu ɔ sisi, nɛ waa bɔ mɔde wawɛɛ nɛ waa kɛ tsu ní.

10. Kɛ wa naa Baiblo ɔ ha kɛɛ? ( 2 Timoteo 3:16, 17 )

Mɛni he je nɛ wa ngɛ nɔ mi mami kaa Yehowa lɛ ha nɛ a ngma Baiblo ɔ? Yiŋtoi babaoo ahewɔ. Nyɔŋmɔ Wiemɔ lɛ ja yɛ yinɔsane mli. ( Luka 3:1, 2 ) Eja hu kɛ́ eewie adebɔɔ mli nibii ahe. ( Hiob 26:⁠7 ) Biblia lɛ ji wolo ni aja fe fɛɛ, yɛ mɔdɛŋ ni Satan bɔ koni eha akpata hiɛ lɛ fɛɛ sɛɛ. Hii sɔrɔtoi aaafee 40 kɛ aaafee afii 1,600 ŋma Biblia lɛ; ni kɛlɛ, amɛshɛɛ saji lɛ kɛ amɛhe kpãa gbee. Ŋaawoi ni be ekaaa ni eye ebua mɛi akpekpei abɔ yɛ amɛ daa gbi shihilɛ mli lɛ yɛ mli. (Abɛ 13:20; 15:21; Mat. 7:12 ) Nɛ Baiblo gbamihi fuu hu ba mi, nɛ lɔ ɔ ji odase yemi kpa ko hu nɛ tsɔɔ kaa a ma nyɛ maa ngɔ wa he kɛ fɔ Baiblo ɔ nɔ. ( Yosh. 23:14 ) Hɛɛ, Biblia lɛ jɛ Nyɔŋmɔ mumɔ lɛ mli!

Ke wa yi mi pee wɔ enyɔɔnyɔ kaa Baiblo ɔ je Mawu mumi mi ɔ, mɛni e sa kaa waa pee? E sa nɛ waa kɛ lɛ nɛ pee sɔlemi sane, nɛ waa pee níhi nɛ kɛ wa sɔlemi ɔ kɔ. Kadimɔ nɔ ni nyɛmi nuu fioo ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Jordan, ni atsɔse lɛ akɛ Odasefonyo lɛ kɛɛ: “Miheee nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni akɛɔ mi lɛ miyeɔ. Ehe miihia ni masusu nibii ahe jogbaŋŋ. Ke wa ya nɔ nɛ wa ha nɛ nɔ mi mami nɛ wa ngɛ kaa Yehowa lɛ ha nɛ a ngma Baiblo ɔ mi waa a, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ wa hemi kɛ yemi ɔ sisi tomi ɔ mi maa wa.​— 1 Kor. 3:12, 13 lɛ he.

“Ma je ​​Yehowa yi kɛ ye tsui tsuo ngɛ jali a kuu ɔ mi.”​— LA. 111:1 lɛ he.

ANI okaiɔ gbi ni akpɛlɛ onɔ akɛ sanekpakpa lɛ shiɛlɔ lɛ? Yɛ nakai miishɛɛ gbi lɛ nɔ lɛ, ojie ohe kpo otsɔɔ jálɔi babaoo ni jieɔ Yehowa gbɛi lɛ yi lɛ. ( Lala 148:1, 2, 12, 13 ) Shi kɛlɛ, yɛ nakai gbi lɛ nɔ lɛ, ekolɛ obatsɔ kuu bibioo ko hu mlinyo, kuu ni ekolɛ nanemɛi jálɔi fioo ko pɛ fata he—shiɛmɔyaa kuu. Shiɛmɔyaa kui tsuɔ gbɛfaŋnɔ titri ko he nii koni amɛye amɛbua wɔ ni wɔtsu nitsumɔ ni akɛwo wɔdɛŋ akɛ wɔshiɛ Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ he sanekpakpa lɛ “yɛ je lɛŋ he fɛɛ he” lɛ he nii.— Mat. 24:14 lɛ he.

2 Te onuɔ shiɛmɔyaa kuu lɛ he gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ he ohaa tɛŋŋ? Nyɛmiyoo ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Wendy * lɛ wie akɛ: “No hewɔ lɛ gbɛjianɔtoo nɛɛ etsake misɔɔmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ mli babaoo: Mikase shiɛmɔ kɛ nitsɔɔmɔ he ŋaai heei kɛjɛ mɛi krokomɛi adɛŋ, eye ebua mi koni mikɛ mumɔŋ otii amamɔ mihiɛ ni minine ashɛ nɔ, ni miná nanemɛi ni kɛ miwala be fɛɛ ahi shi​—enɛɛmɛi jɛ gbɛjianɔtoo ni suɔmɔ yɔɔ mli nɛɛ mli kɛba.” Nyɛmi nuu ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Rich lɛ kɛfata he akɛ: “Mibuɔ sɔɔmɔ akɛ kuu nɔkwɛlɔ akɛ hegbɛ ko ni nɔ bɛ, yɛ be mli ni shiɛlɔi komɛi náa hegbɛ amɛyeɔ amɛbuaa mɔ ko koni eya ehiɛ kɛyashɛ baptisimɔ he lɛ, kuu nɔkwɛlɔ lɛ yɛ hegbɛi krɛdɛɛi ni ekɛyeɔ ebuaa nakai mɔ lɛ koni eya ehiɛ yɛ mumɔŋ afii babaoo.” Nyɛmi yoo ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Dinah lɛ wie akɛ: “Shiɛmɔyaa kuu lɛ he gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ hi waa, mibuɔ mikuu lɛ akɛ miweku.”

“Nyɛɛ hyɛ bɔ nɛ nyɛ buɔ tue ha a nɔ saminya.”— LUKA 8:18 .

MWƆNƐ Ɔ, Yehowa ngɛ mumi mi niye ní fuu hae. Wa ma nyɛ maa kplɛɛ David nɔ nɛ e la ke: ‘Yehowa ji ye hyɛlɔ, nɔ́ ko nɔ́ ko be ye hiae’ ɔ nɔ.— Kane La 23:1, 2, 5 .

2 Blɔ kake nɛ Yehowa guɔ nɔ kɛ haa wɔ mumi mi niye ní mwɔnɛ ɔ ji wa asafo mi kpehi nɛ wa yaa a. Yɛ kpeei nɛɛ ashishi lɛ, bei pii lɛ, wɔtĩɔ saji ni wɔle jogbaŋŋ lɛ amli. Ke wa “tu wa si saminya ngɛ anɔkuale ɔ mi” po ɔ, wa hia kaimi nɛ ɔmɛ. ( 2 Pet. 1:12 ) Shi kɛ́ mumɔŋ nifeemɔi lɛ amli saji ni asusu he shii abɔ fata he lɛ, ani ewaa kɛhaa bo akɛ ooobo toi? Munyu nɛ ɔ ma nyɛ maa ye bua mo. Wa ma susu nɔ́ he je nɛ e he hia nɛ waa bu tue saminya ngɛ asafo mi kpehi a sisi ɔ he. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, wa maa na bɔ nɛ nihi nɛ a tsɔɔ ní ngɛ asafo ɔ mi ɔ ma nyɛ maa ye bua ni kpahi konɛ a bu tue ha. Nɛ wa ma susu ga womi komɛ nɛ ma nyɛ maa ye bua wɔ konɛ waa bu tue bumi saminya a he. Se kekleekle ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ waa hyɛ nɔ nɛ kɛ wɔ ngɛ munyu tue ɔ nɛ waa hyɛ.

TAAKƐ GUSTAVO YOSEF KƐƐ

AFƆ mi yɛ afi 1947 yɛ Caribbean ŋshɔkpɔ fɛfɛo ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Cuba lɛ nɔ. Eyɛ he ni Caribbean Ŋshɔ lɛ kooyigbɛ nu ni mli edɔ lɛ kɛ Atlantik Ŋshɔ lɛ kpeɔ yɛ lɛ. Beni afɔ mi sɛɛ lɛ, mifɔlɔi fɔ minyɛmimɛi yei bibii enyɔ. Wɔyi enumɔ lɛ wɔhi akrowa ni atsɛɔ lɛ Esmeralda lɛ mli.

Mikaiɔ akɛ toiŋjɔlɛ yɛ wɔ akrowa bibioo lɛ mli. Wɔhi he ko ni bɛŋkɛ wɔwekumɛi komɛi, ni wɔtsɛkwɛmɛi, tsɛkwɛmɛi, kɛ wɔtsɛkwɛmɛi fata he. Wɔná niyenii ni fa bɔ ni sa ni wɔhi shi yɛ miishɛɛ mli.

Benɛ i ye maa pee jeha enuɔ ɔ, ye fɔli kɛ Walton Jones bɔni Baiblo ɔ kasemi. Eji sanekpakpa jajelɔ ni yɔɔ ekãa ni ebaanyiɛ aaafee ŋmɛlɛtswai nyɔŋma dani eshɛ wɔ akrowa lɛ he kɛkɛ. No mli lɛ, Yehowa Odasefoi nyɛɔ amɛjáa Nyɔŋmɔ yɛ Cuba yɛ heyeli mli. Ale wɔ jogbaŋŋ akɛ wɔshiɛɔ yɛ shia kɛ shia, ni wɔhiɛɔ baagi kɛ nine kotokui ni Biblia kasemɔ he woji ni jara wa eyi mli obɔ. Ni wɔfee nyiɛmɔ babaoo! Mikaiɔ bɔ ni misɔmɔ Yehowa yɛ nakai “bei kpakpai” ni mikɛtsu nii yɛ mishishijee mli lɛ jogbaŋŋ. Shi “jaramɔ bei” kã wɔhiɛ.​— 2 Tim. 4:2 lɛ he.

Kaa bɔ nɛ wa na ngɛ Galatia Bi 4:24 ɔ, mɛni somi enyɔ nɛ bɔfo Paulo tu he munyu ɔ?

◼ Beni bɔfo Paulo wie wekukpaa ni kã Abraham kɛ Sara kɛ Hagar teŋ lɛ he sɛɛ lɛ, ekɛɛ akɛ: “Ejaakɛ nɛkɛ yei nɛɛ ji kpaŋmɔi enyɔ.” ( Gal. 4:22-24 ) Be ko nɛ be ɔ, nihi nuɔ sisi kaa somi enyɔ ɔmɛ ji Mlaa somi ɔ kɛ somi ehe ɔ. Shi, kɛ́ wɔpɛi mli jogbaŋŋ lɛ, wɔheɔ wɔyeɔ akɛ ehe miihia ni afee tsakemɔ. Etamɔ nɔ ni jeee kpaŋmɔ hee lɛ he Paulo wieɔ, shi moŋ kpaŋmɔ ni akɛ Abraham fee lɛ he. Susumɔ saji fioo komɛi ahe okwɛ.*

Paulo kɛ tsulɔ yoo Hagar to Sinai Gɔŋ lɛ he. ( Gal. 4:25 ) Hagar tsɔɔ Mlaa somi ɔ nɛ a kɛ ha ngɛ Sinai Yoku ɔ nɔ ngɛ jeha 1513 D.Ŋ.B. (2 Mose 19:5, 6) Adesa ko be nɛ e yi mluku nɛ e ma nyɛ maa ye Mlaa nɛ ɔ nɔ kɛ pi si, enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e kai Yuda bi ɔmɛ kaa nyɔguɛhi ji mɛ kɛ ha yayami. Somi nɛ ɔ ma nyɛ maa ye bua Israel ma a nɛ a le Mesia a, nɔ nɛ ji adesa pɛ nɛ ma nyɛ maa ye Mlaa a nɔ kɛ pi si ɔ. Benɛ Mesia a ngɔ e wami nɛ ye mluku ɔ kɛ sã afɔle ɔ, e ha nɛ adesahi nɛ a yi mluku ɔ ma nyɛ maa ye a he kɛ je yayami kɛ gbenɔ mi. ( Gal. 3:19, 24, 25 ) Benɛ Yesu ha nɛ Mawu yi mi tomi ba mi ɔ, Mlaa a he hia we hu.​— Rom. 10:4 lɛ he.

Paulo kɛ srɔtofeemɔ ni yɔɔ Hagar to Sara, “yoo ni eye ehe” ni fɔ “kɛtsɔ shiwoo nɔ” lɛ he. ( Gal. 4:23 ) Si womi nɛ ɔ ji somi nɛ a kɛ Abraham so ɔ nɛ. ( Gal. 3:29; 4:28, 30 ) Afo mli kuku yɛ 1 Mose 22:18 akɛ: “O [Abraham] seshi lɛ nɔ aaatsɔ ajɔɔ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ jeŋmaji fɛɛ aha amɛhe, ejaakɛ obo migbee toi.”

* Enɛ jajeɔ nɔ ni je kpo yɛ March 15, 2006 Buu-Mɔɔ lɛ baafai 10-12 lɛ mli lɛ.

Nakai kpaŋmɔ ni akɛ Abraham fee lɛ fata shiwoo ni Yehowa wo yɛ Eden lɛ he. (1 Mose 3:15) E ha nɛ wa na kaa ‘nina’ nɛ a gba fɔ si ɔ maa pee Abraham nina. Paulo tsɔɔ mli akɛ nɛkɛ “seshi” nɛɛ damɔ shi kɛha Yesu Kristo titri. Mɛni he je nɛ Paulo ngɔ “okadi drama” nɛ ɔ kɛ tsu ní ɔ? Etsɔɔ akɛ kɛji Yudafoi ni hi shi yɛ egbii lɛ amli lɛ ma nɔ mi doo akɛ amɛbaaye Mla lɛ nɔ lɛ, amɛ hu amɛbaakã he amɛhi nyɔŋyeli mli tamɔ Hagar. Shi kɛlɛ, kɛ́ amɛkpɛlɛ Abraham “seshi” titri ni ji Yesu Kristo nɔ lɛ, amɛ hu amɛbaaye amɛhe tamɔ Sara. A ma ná he yemi nitsɛnitsɛ kɛ je yayami kɛ gbenɔ mi. ( Yoh. 8:32-34 ) Ke a kplɛɛ we Yesu nɔ ɔ, yi mi tomi titli nɛ ngɛ Mlaa somi ɔ he, nɛ ji kaa e maa kudɔ mɛ kɛ ya Kristo ɔ ngɔ ɔ maa laa mɛ.

Galatabii ni Paulo ŋma amɛ wolo lɛ ateŋ mɛi babaoo jeee Yudafoi, ni amɛbɛ Mla lɛ shishi kɔkɔɔkɔ. Yudafoi komɛi miima nɔ mi doo akɛ Jeŋmajiaŋbii Kristofoi lɛ abo Mla lɛ toi, ni nɔ ni fata he ji ketiafoo taomɔ nii lɛ. Paulo susu kaa e maa pee kuasiahi kaa nimli nɛ ɔmɛ maa kplɛɛ Mlaa a “nyɔguɛ yemi” ɔ nɔ ejakaa a ná “he yemi” nɛ ji Kristo nɔ́ ɔ momo.​— Gal. 5:1, 10-14 he.

BIBLIA MLI ANƆKWALE SANE

Wuoyaa Ŋshɔŋlooi yɛ Biblia Bei Amli

Eko ɔ, nihi nɛ a kaneɔ Baiblo ɔ ma kai kaa Yesu se nyɛɛli komɛ ji lo hɛli. ( Marko 1:16-20 ) Ke a ya hɛ lo ngɛ Galilea Wo ɔ mi ɔ, a maa ngɔ ya.

Akɛ kpãa loo klala kpãa fĩɔ ŋshɔŋlooi ahe koni akɛfee ŋshɔŋlooi. Bɔ ni ŋshɔŋloo lɛ da ha lɛ damɔ loo ni ataoɔ lɛ nɔ. Bei pii lɛ, ŋshɔŋlooi bibii lɛ yɛ dade loo tɛi ni akɛwoɔ ŋshɔ lɛ mli ni haa amɛboteɔ ŋshɔ lɛ mli, yɛ be mli ni ŋshɔŋlooi wuji lɛ yɛ ŋshɔŋlooi ni akɛwoɔ ŋshɔŋ lɛ shishigbɛ kɛ tsei loo ŋshɔŋlooi ni akɛwoɔ ŋshɔŋ lɛ yɛ ŋwɛigbɛ.

Wuo yaa ŋshɔŋlooi ahe jara wa waa. Tɛi ni yɔɔ ŋshɔ lɛ shishi lɛ baanyɛ afite amɛ loo loo babaoo ni amɔmɔ lɛ tsiimɔ baanyɛ agbala amɛ. ( Luka 5:⁠6 ) Biblia lɛ wieɔ Yakobo kɛ Yohane ni ‘miisaa amɛyaai’ lɛ he—ekolɛ amɛkɛ abui kɛ klala kpãa tsu nii. ( Mat. 4:21 ) Eko ɔ, wo hɛli ma dla ní nɛ ɔmɛ be fɛɛ be nɛ a kɛ tsu ní. Luka hu tsɔɔ bɔ ni wuolɛɛmɛi “fɔɔ amɛyaai lɛ ahe” koni amɛjie sũ loo jwɛi ni efite lɛ. ( Luka 5:2 ) Lɔ ɔ se ɔ, wo hɛli ɔmɛ ngɔɔ a ya amɛ kɛ tsoɔ konɛ a gbi—eko ɔ, konɛ a ko puɛ.​—Ezek. 47:10 he.

Yesu fɔ nyumuhi nɛ a le kaa kɛ yaa hɛɛmi ɔ nine konɛ a ba pee “nimli hɛli.”

Akɛ wolo nɛɛ hɔ̃ɔɔ. Efata jeŋ muu fɛɛ Biblia mli tsɔsemɔ nitsumɔ ko ni akɛ onia ni ajɛɔ suɔmɔ mli atsuɔ lɛ fiɔ sɛɛ lɛ he. Kɛ́ oosumɔ ni ofee onia lɛ, ofainɛ yaa donate.jw.org.
