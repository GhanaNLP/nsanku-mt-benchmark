Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

SƐNEA ƐBAE SƐNEA ATURUKUKU, AKWANKYERƐ-ABODAN NO, KƆKƆ AWURADE NO HO, NA ƆMAA AKOKO, AKWANKYERƐ NO, BA FIE NO MU

AKOKO, Akokɔ, ne Aturukuku, Turtle-dove, fi agya biako hɔ na efi ɛna biako hɔ. Afei Akokɔ no ka kyerɛɛ Turtle-dove no sɛ, “Kɔ kɔ Tall-grass-country, na me nso, mɛkɔ Thick-forest-country no mu, na sɛ monya aduane a, ɛnneɛ wobɛba abɛka akyerɛ me.” Afei wɔtetew mu. Mfeɛ mmiɛnsa na Aturukuku anhunu ne nuabea. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne kunu sɛ, “Kye me, na merekɔhwehwɛ me nuabea.” Ɛnna ne kunu kaa sɛ, “Sɛ wokasa wie a, mate, kɔ, na me ne wo nkɔ.” Na ɔne no sii kwan so duu kurow bi akyi (beae a ne nuabea te). Wohuu (atɛkyɛ) a ɛyɛ kɔntɔnkrɔn bi wɔ hɔ, na ɛhɔ na ɔne ne nuabea hyiae. Na Akokɔ no bisaa Turtle-dove no sɛ, “Bere a wokɔɔ Tall-grass-country no, wo nyaa aduane a wobɛdi?” Obuae sɛ, “Baabi a mekɔɔ no, na guinea-aburow nkutoo na ɛwɔ hɔ;na bere a metow me mma, bɛyɛ asia, na wɔde wɔn ho bataa me ho, na bere a na wɔn kɛse te sɛ eyi (a ɛkyerɛ wɔn kɛse) no, mede wɔn bɛn me, na ɔbɔmmɔfo bi tow ne tuo, pom! na wokum me mma, na sɛ manhu ntease a, anka wokum me nso.” Bere a ɔkasa wiei no, Akokɔ no kae sɛ, “Baabi a mekɔɔ no, mebɛyɛɛ obi a ne ho hia ankasa prɛko pɛ.” Aturukuku bisaa no, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Ka nea wopɛ sɛ woka wɔ wo ‘nipa a ne ho hia ankasa’ ho kyerɛ me.” Afei Akokɔ no kae sɛ, “Nea ‘onipa a ne ho hia ankasa’ kyerɛ ne sɛ, sɛ nhwɛso no, sɛ me wura trɛw mpa fɛfɛ bi mu a, t hen mekɔda so, na me nsuo gu so, na sɛ me wura fa nam de gu soup-stew mu a, afei meyi deɛ mepɛ; aduane biara a me wura bedi no, mekɔfa bi, na sɛ midi saa a, me wura ntumi mfa ne tuo anaa ne poma mmɔ me. Ɛno.ne nti na meka sɛ meyɛ obi a ne ho hia ankasa.” Akokɔ no kaa sɛ, “Baabi a wote hɔ no, ɛnyɛ beaeɛ pa, enti Kwasida bi a ɛdi Kwasida Adae anim no, fa wo mma, ne wo kunu bra, na ma wɔn nyinaa ntena yɛn fie.” Aturukuku kaa sɛ, “Mate, mɛkɔ, na Kwasida a edi Kwasida Adae bi anim no, mɛba.” Afei ɔsɔfo bi baa Hen’s akuraa ase, wosow ing ne dɔn nketewa no. Wobisaa no se, “Nana, ɛyɛ dɛn?” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Dente se woama no kwan ma ɔkɔm de no mfeɛ mmienu, enti ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ ɔpanin no nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛ no sɛ ɔmfa nnomaa (ne) nkesua dumienu mmra.” Afei Ɔpanyin no kɔkyeree nnomaa baanan, de nkesua dumien bae, faa na ɔbɔɔ eto, na ɔkɔde maa onyame Dente wɔ n’atɛkyɛ afɔremuka a ɛte sɛ kɔntɔnkrɔn no so, twaa nnomaa baanan no ti ne p wɔde hama bɛbɔ wɔn wɔ hɔ, na wɔafa wɔn ntakra ne wɔn ti ne wɔn nan, na wɔde apetepete atɛkyɛ afɔrebukyia no nkyɛn; na ɔde eto a wɔayam no nso bɔɔ so. Afei, da a edi Adae no anim no, na Aturukuku rekɔhyia ne nuabea. Bere a ɔrekɔ no ohui, wɔ afɔremuka a ɛyɛ kronkron ma Dente no so, ne nuabea no agyanom, ne ne ti, ne nan, ne ntaban, ne mmerɛbo, ne ne dwensɔtwaa. Aturukuku kaa sɛ, “Eyi nyinaa akyi no, merentumi nkɔ hɔ bio da.” Na Turtle-dove tu kɔeɛ na ɔsii fam wɔ silk-cotton dua bi so, na ɔkaa (too dwom sɛ): " Mesu me nuabea o, Awerɛhoɔ o."

Wo, Hen, a na woyɛ obi a ne ho hia bi no, so eyi ne wo ti?

Wo, Hen, a na woyɛ onipa a ne ho hia bi no, so eyinom yɛ wo ntaban?

Wo, Hen, a na woyɛ onipa a ne ho hia bi no, so eyinom yɛ wo nan?

Wo, Hen, a na woyɛ obi a ne ho hia bi no, so eyi ne wo mmerɛbo?

Wo, Hen, a na woyɛ obi a ne ho hia bi no, so eyinom yɛ wo dwensɔtwaa? "

Na Aturukuku kaa sɛ, " Merenkɔ Kwaeɛ man mu bio, ɛfiri sɛ kwaeɛ mu nnipa nyɛ papa." Saa na Aturukuku bɛtenaa Tall-grass-man, bere a Akokɔ no tenaa fie no mu.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebia wobɛfa ebinom sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no betumi akamfo me (sɛ maka ho asɛm).
