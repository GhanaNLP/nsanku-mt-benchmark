Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

SƐNEA ƐBAE SƐNEA ATURUKUKU, AKWANKYERƐ-ABODAN NO, KƆKƆ AWURADE NO HO, NA ƆMAA AKOKO, AKWANKYERƐ NO, BA FIE NO MU

AKOKO, Akokɔ, ne Aturukuku, Turtle-dove, fi agya biako hɔ na efi ɛna biako hɔ. Afei Akokɔ no ka kyerɛɛ Turtle-dove no sɛ, “Kɔ kɔ Tall-grass-country no so, na me nso, mɛkɔ Thick-forest-country no mu, na sɛ monya aduane a, ɛnneɛ wobɛba abɛka akyerɛ me.” Afei wɔtetew mu. Mfeɛ mmiɛnsa na Aturukuku anhunu ne nuabea. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne kunu se, “Ka me ho, na merekɔhwehwɛ me nuabea.” Ɛnna ne kunu kaa sɛ, “Sɛ wokasa wie a, mate, kɔ, na me ne wo nkɔ.” Na ɔne no sii kwan so duu kurow bi akyi (beae a ne nuabea te). Wohuu (atɛkyɛ) a ɛyɛ kɔntɔnkrɔn bi wɔ hɔ, na ɛhɔ na ɔne ne nuabea hyiae. Na Akokɔ no bisaa Turtle-dove no sɛ, “Bere a wokɔɔ Tall-grass-country no, wo nyaa aduane a wobɛdi?” Obuae sɛ, “Baabi a mekɔɔ no, na guinea-aburow nkutoo na ɛwɔ hɔ;na bere a metow me mma, bɛyɛ asia, na wɔde wɔn ho bataa me ho, na bere a na wɔn kɛse te sɛ eyi (a ɛkyerɛ wɔn kɛse) no, memaa wɔmmɛn me, na ɔbɔmmɔfo bi tow ne tuo, pom!, na okum me mma, na, sɛ minnim ntease a, anka wobekum me nso.” Bere a ɔkasa wiei no, Akokɔ no kae sɛ, “Baabi a mekɔɔ no, mebɛyɛɛ obi a ne ho hia ankasa prɛko pɛ.” Aturukuku bisaa no, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Ka nea wopɛ sɛ woka wɔ wo ‘nipa a ne ho hia ankasa’ ho kyerɛ me.” Afei Akokɔ no kaa sɛ, “Nea ‘onipa a ne ho hia ankasa’ kyerɛ ne sɛ, sɛ nhwɛso no, sɛ me wura trɛw mpa fɛfɛ bi mu a, ɛnde mekɔda so, na me nsu gu so, na sɛ me wura fa nam de hyɛ soup-stew no mu a, afei miyi nea mepɛ; aduan biara a me wura bedi no, mekɔfa bi, na sɛ midi saa a, me wura ntumi mfa ne tuo anaa poma na boro me.Ɛno nti na meka sɛ meyɛ obi a ne ho hia ankasa.” Akokɔ no kaa sɛ, “Baabi a wote hɔ no, ɛnyɛ beaeɛ pa, enti Kwasida bi a ɛdi Kwasida Adae anim no, fa wo mma, ne wo kunu bra, na ma wɔn nyinaa ntena yɛn fie.” Aturukuku kaa sɛ, “Mate, mɛkɔ, na Kwasida a edi Kwasida Adae bi anim no, mɛba.” Afei ɔsɔfo bi baa Hen’s akuraa ase, na ɔwosow ne dɔn nketewa no. Wobisaa no se, “Nana, ɛyɛ dɛn?” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Dente se woama no kwan ma ɔkɔm de no mfeɛ mmienu, enti ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ ɔpanin no nkyɛn kɔka kyerɛ no sɛ ɔmfa nnomaa (ne) nkesua dumienu mmra.” Afei Ɔpanyin no kɔkyeree nnomaa nnan, de nkesua dumien bae, faa na ɔbɔɔ eto, na ɔkɔde maa onyame Dente wɔ n’atɛkyɛ afɔremuka a ɛte sɛ kɔntɔnkrɔn no ho, twaa nnomaa baanan no ti na ɔde wɔn guu hɔ, na ɔfaa wɔn ntakra ne wɔn tiri, ne wɔn nan, na ɔde plaster guu atɛkyɛ afɔremuka no nkyɛn; na ɔde eto a wɔayam no nso bɔɔ so. Afei, da a edi Adae no anim no, na Aturukuku rekɔhyia ne nuabea. Bere a ɔrekɔ no ohui, wɔ afɔremuka a ɛyɛ kronkron ma Dente no so, ne nuabea no agyanom, ne ne ti, ne nan, ne ntaban, ne mmerɛbo, ne ne dwensɔtwaa. Aturukuku kaa sɛ, “Eyi nyinaa akyi no, merentumi nkɔ hɔ bio da.” Na Turtle-dove tu kɔeɛ na ɔsii fam wɔ silk-cotton dua bi so, na ɔkaa (too dwom sɛ): " Mesu me nuabea o, Awerɛhoɔ o."

Wo, Hen, a na woyɛ obi a ne ho hia bi no, so eyi ne wo ti?

Wo, Hen, a na woyɛ onipa a ne ho hia bi no, so eyinom yɛ wo ntaban?

Wo, Hen, a na woyɛ onipa a ne ho hia bi no, so eyinom yɛ wo nan?

Wo, Hen, a na woyɛ obi a ne ho hia bi no, so eyi ne wo mmerɛbo?

Wo, Hen, a na woyɛ obi a ne ho hia bi no, so eyinom yɛ wo dwensɔtwaa? "

Na Aturukuku kaa sɛ, " Merenkɔ Kwaeɛ man mu bio, ɛfiri sɛ kwaeɛ mu nnipa nyɛ papa." Saa na Aturukuku bɛtenaa Tall-grass-man no mu, bere a Akokɔ no tenaa fie no mu.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebia wobɛfa ebinom sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no betumi akamfo me (sɛ maka ho asɛm).
