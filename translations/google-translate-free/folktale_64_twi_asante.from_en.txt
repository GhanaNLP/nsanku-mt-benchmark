Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

SƐNEA ƐBAE SƐ ANANSE, AKWANKYERƐ NO, KƆƆROƆ WƆ AKWANKYERƐ NO SO

ANANSE O! ɔwaree Nsia babea, Wim-nyame no maame, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ Nyame (Wim-nyame) sɛ, "Da a wo maame, Nsia, bewu no, bra bɛka da a wɔapaw ama ayiyɛ amanne no kyerɛ me."

Bɛyɛ nnansa akyi no wɔbaa bɛka kyerɛɛ no ​​sɛ Nsia a ɔyɛ Wim-nyame no maame no awu. Afei Ananse mogya-abusuafoɔ ne ‘Berekuo, Ɔhene Jay, Kwakuo, Bonsu, ne Odwan, Nguan. Afei Ananse kasa kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Wɔaba bɛka ayiyɛ ho amanne bi akyerɛ me, enti ɛsɛ sɛ mo ne me kɔ." Enti Ananse maa wɔde (nkrasɛm bi) kɔmaa Wim-nyame no sɛ ɔreba guasodeyɛ no ase. Afei Wim-nyame no maa ne manfo nyinaa boaboaa wɔn ho ano, te sɛ Omanhene bi nhyiam no ara pɛ. Na Ananse kaa sɛ, "Sɛ anwummerɛ ayɛ nwini a, mɛba." Akɔre no yɛɛ kotokuo, na ɔde ‘Berekuo, Ɔhene Jay, Kwakuo, Bonsu, ne Odwan, Oguan guu mu, na ɔde kotokuo no guu ne mmati so, na ɔde ne ntama kyekyeree so. Afei ɔkasa kyerɛɛ abɔde no sɛ, "Sɛ midu Wim-nyame no nkyɛn a, ɛsɛ sɛ musu." Bere a oduu Wim-nyame no anim no, ‘Berekuo no maa so (ne nne):

"Kurukusu, ku! ku! ku! ku!

Kurukusu, ku! ku! ku! ni."

Na Bonsu no maa (ne nne) so sɛ:

"Kakum!"

Na Oguan no maa (ne nne so sɛ:

"Me!"

Eyi ne Kwaku Ananse kwadwom. Nkurɔfo no nyinaa kaa sɛ, “Woayɛ yie, ɔbarima baako nie a ɔtumi su na ɔdi awerɛhoɔ wɔ akwan ahodoɔ mmiɛnsa so.” Bere a owiee (ne kwadwom) no, Wim-nyame no hwehwɛɛ ofie pa bi a ɔbɛtra mu Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma daa adi no, Wim-nyame no noaa nnuan pa na ɔde maa Ananse. Bere a Kwaku Ananse huu saa aduan yi no, ɔkaa (kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ), "Meremfa bi mma 'Berekuo, meremfa bi mma Kwakuo, meremfa bi mma Odwan, efisɛ madi mmuada akyɛ." Enti Ananse dii, wamma ne mogya-abusuafoɔ biara. Afei, ɔne wɔn kɔɔ so kɔɔ so na wɔkɔɔ so, na Wim-nyame no kae sɛ, "Yɛbɛyɛ nnawɔtwe ayi afahyɛ no." Nanso Nguan no kaa (kyerɛ Ananse), "Ɔkɔm rekum me, menkɔ"; Kwakuo ne 'Berekuo nso kaa saa ara. Afei Wim-nyame no kɔtenaa nnipa a wɔahyiam no mu, na ɔmaa wɔbɔɔ Ananse amanneɛ sɛ adepa a ɔyɛɛ da bi no, ebia ɔbɛsan ayɛ bio, ɛfiri sɛ ɔne ne mpanimfoɔ no ahyiam (sɛ wɔretie). Ananse buae sɛ, "Me ho nyɛ me den." Ade kyee no, Wim nyame no ka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, "Woama m'ani ayɛ aniwu." Na 'Berekuo, ne Kwakuo, ne Odwan kaa sɛ, "Yɛn nso, woama ɔkɔm de yɛn, enti yɛn ne wo bɛtete yɛn ho afiri abusuakuo no ho." Enti Kwakuo ne 'Berekuo kɔɔ kwae no mu, nanso Odwan, Nguan no, kɔɔ so traa fie; na Akɔre no huruw yɛɛ ne ho petee wɔ nnua no so; ɛno te saa efisɛ na n’ani awu.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
