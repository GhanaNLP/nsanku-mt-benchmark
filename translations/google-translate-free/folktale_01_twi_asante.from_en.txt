SƐNEA ƐBAE SƐNEA OBONTO YA, MINNOW NO, "NANANTE" HO BERE BIARA WƆ NSU NO MU A ONNI ATIRIMKA

WƆKA sɛ na Akwakoraa-nanabea wɔ hɔ, na n’agyapade yerae. Ɔkaa sɛ ɛyɛ "nsu mu abɔdeɛ" na wɔfaa (nneɛma no). Na krak no te hɔ, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Ah, woama yɛn aniwa ayɛ mmerɛw (wɔ aniwu mu), na nea yɛhyɛ nyansa sɛ yɛnyɛ de nkyerɛ wo nea ɛwɔ yɛn tirim ne (eyi): kɔ na fa kɔbere kyɛnsee mmra na momma yɛn nyinaa mfa (yɛn) nusu nhyɛ mu ma, na nea ontumi nsu sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛhyɛ no ma no, ɛno ne nea wafa (nneɛma no)."

Ɔde kɔbere kyɛnsee no bae. Okoto, krak no, sui na ɔhyɛɛ mu ma; wɔguu mu guu hɔ; Mmobonse baa hɔ sui; Okawa baa hɔ bɛsui; Oyoyo, ɔkraman no baa hɔ bɛsui; Otidi baa hɔ bɛsui; Obonko, shrimp no baa hɔ bɛsui; Akorese baa hɔ bɛsu; Opitiri baa hɔ bɛsui; Okobo baa hɔ bɛsui; nsu mu abɔde nyinaa bae bɛsui. Nea etwa to (wɔ ne nyinaa mu) Obonto Ya, Minnow no, baa sɛ ɔrebɛsu; wannya nsuo amfi n’ani so; ɛno nti wɔhunuu sɛ Obonto Ya na wafa (nneɛma no).

Nanso Akwakoraa-nana kaa sɛ, "Merenyɛ wo hwee." Ɛno nti, abɔdeɛ no nyinaa kaa sɛ, “Yɛn deɛ, yɛbɛboro no, ɛfiri sɛ wahyɛ yɛn aniwuo.” Enti wɔboroo no.

Bere a wɔboroo no wiei no, Akwakoraa-nanabea kae sɛ, "Megyaw wo (daa) sɛ wobɛtu akɔ akyirikyiri a wunhia botae biara; obiara mpɛ da sɛ ɔne wo nya biribi yɛ."

Eyi nti na, sɛ mmea "kɔ splashing" (ma mpataa) a, wohu OBONTO YA sɛ ɔrekyinkyin (seesei) ha ne ha.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
