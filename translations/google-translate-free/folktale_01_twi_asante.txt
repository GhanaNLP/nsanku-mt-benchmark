YE that Nana Aberewa was there, and her things were lost, and she said asuom’ animals and ya take. He bows down and says, “A’, you have humiliated us, so what we will do to you when you see yetirim’ is: go and bring yawa, and let us stand and cry for her, and he who could not cry for her will do it.” He brought the pain; Okoto cried for, we poured out; Mmobonse cried; Okawa besui; This is the besui; Otidie cried; Obonko besui; Akorese cried; Opitire cried; Okobo besui; water’s animal mothers will cry. And afterwards Obonto Ya also came to cry, he did not have tears, and we saw that Obonto Ya had done it. And Grandma Aberewa said, “I ’m not going to kiss you.” And the beasts said, "We will not eat our bebo, and he has humiliated us. And we booed him. When we finished, Grandma Aberewa said, “I leave you patient, you’re walking around in vain, no one wants to make fun of you.”

That is, if the children go to the kiss, we will kiss and Obonto Ya walks here and there.
