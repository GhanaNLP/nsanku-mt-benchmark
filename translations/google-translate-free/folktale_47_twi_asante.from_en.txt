Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

SƐNEA NSƐM NE NNOMA BAA ABUSUE NO MU

WƆKA sɛ bere bi na kurow kɛse bi wɔ hɔ, na na anomaa bi sɔre da biara sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛba abɛyi nkurɔfo no anom. Afei Ananse, Akɔre no tee asɛm yi, na ɔsiim sɛ ɔrekɔ saa kuro no mu. Ɔduruu kuro no mu no, ɔka kyerɛɛ kuro no mufoɔ sɛ, “Adɛn nti na wɔ kuro kɛseɛ te sɛ yei mu no, nnipa a wɔwɔ mu nyinaa anom atwa?” Kurow no mu Titenani no kae sɛ, “Agya, anomaa ketewaa bi, bere a owia gyina hɔ dinn saa (ɔde ne nsa kyerɛ soro) bae bɛkyeree yɛn, na ɔtetew yɛn anom.” Kwaku Ananse, Akɔre no kaa sɛ, “Eyi deɛ, mempene adeɛ a ɛte saa so, na m’ankasa mɛkum no.” Akuraa no Titenani no kaa sɛ, “Oo, na kuro yi ntumi nyɛ hwee, na wo, kɔn a wo nko ara gyina hɔ no, dɛn na wobɛtumi ayɛ?” Ananse kaa sɛ, “Hwɛ yei, me sekan, sɛ mesem no ara pɛ a, mɛtwa ne ti saa, bam!” Bere a owia no gyinaa hɔ (i.e. awiabere) no, Anomaa no bae bɛtenaa dua bi so, na ɔmaa so (ne nne):

“E, e, anom, anom, e, E!

Jaws, merekɔ, E!

Jaws, maba, o!”

Na Akɔre no maa (ne nne) so sɛ:

“Jaws, n’agya, e, E!

Jaws, n’agya, E!

N’agya, n’agya, E!

N’agya, maba, o!

E! n’agya.”

Afei Anomaa no ba bɛgyinaa abɔnten no awieeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Hena nne ne saa a ɔbua me, na ɔyɛ me nananom ayayadeɛ?Mapaapae akuraa no asefoɔ afono nyinaa mu, obiako ntumi nkasa.” Na ɔmaa (ne nne) so bio sɛ:

“E, e, anom, anom, e, E!

Jaws, merekɔ, E!

Jaws, maba, o!”

Na Ananse buaa sɛ:

“Jaws, n’agya, e, E!

Jaws, n’agya, E!

N’agya, n’agya, E!

N’agya, maba, o!

E, n’agya.”

Afei Kwaku Ananse baa hɔ bɛtenaa (ne fie) apon ano. Afei nsɛm ne wɔn a wɔaka (i. e. me werɛ fii sɛ mɛka) sɛ kuro no mu nnipa nyinaa aguan na wɔafi Ananse. Na Anomaa no tu kɔduruu Kwaku Ananse’s (fie) ano; na Kwaku Ananse dwane na ɔhyɛn ne dan mu, na ɔtoo matting no (wɔ ɔpon no atifi), na Anomaa no maa so (ne nne):

“ E, e, anom, anom, e, E!

Jaws, merekɔ, E!”

Afei Kwaku Ananse dwane na ɔhyɛn calabash bi a wɔde di dwuma mu, na ɔmaa (ne nne) so brɛoo paa sɛ:

“ Jaws, n’agya, e, E!

Jaws, n’agya, E!

N’agya, maba, o!

E! n’agya.”

Afei na Anomaa no rebɛforo akɔ dan no mu sɛ ɔkɔ na wabue kalaba a wɔde di dwuma no, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛkyere Ananse, nanso Kwaku Ananse faa ne sekan na ɔtwaa ne ti, bam! na Anomaa no fã tu kɔɔ Edge-of-the-sea-man no so. Kwaku, Akɔre no, faa anom a ɛsensɛn Anomaa no so no, na ɔde too fam hɔ; na ɔde Anomaa no ti too so; na ɔkɔɔ sɛ ɔrekɔfrɛ kurom hɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, “Mobɛtumi aba, ɛfiri sɛ makum saa Anomaa no.” Wɔkaa sɛ, “Oh, ɛno ne Kwaku Ananse, ɔyɛ ɔtorofoɔ a wonim no yie, ebia ɔnka nokware.” Wɔkaa sɛ, “Paw abofra baako a ɔwɔ nhumu na ɔnkɔhwɛ.” Nteasefoɔ no kɔsiee baabi, na ɔhwɛeɛ, na ɔhunuu sɛ abɔdeɛ no ti da hɔ, na ɔkɔkaa sɛ, “Mommra, nnamfonom, ɛfiri sɛ Kwaku Ananse anni atoro.” Nnipa no nyinaa bae. Afei eyi, bere a ɔfaa ne hwene no, ɔde bataa ho. Nanso wo a woannya w’ankasa de no, wokɔfaa w’adamfo de no; ɛno nti na woyare a wo sẽ yɛ wo yaw no. Afei, saa Anomaa no deɛ, wɔkɔfaa no firii Po no ano-man no mu, na wɔtwitwaa no nketenkete paa, na wɔbɔɔ wɔn petee. Saa na nnomaa pii baa abusuakuw no mu.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebia wobɛfa ebinom sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no betumi akamfo me (sɛ maka ho asɛm).
