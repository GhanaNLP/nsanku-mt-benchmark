Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

ANANSE, AKWANKYERƐ NO KAA SƐ ƆREKƆHWEHWƐ ƆKWASƐM, BERE A BERE NYINAA NA ƆNNO HO YƐ ƆKWASƐM

Bere bi na ANANSE, Akɔre no te hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkwasea bi ne no bɛkɔ akɔto mpataa afiri. Ɔkyinkyin baabiara, nanso wantumi annye bi. Afei ohyiaa Osansa, Ɔkraman no. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Momma yɛnkɔ nkɔsi mpataa afiri.” Ɔkraman no buae sɛ, “Mewɔ nam (dodow) a mɛdi.” Ɛno akyi no, ohyiaa Anene, Ɔkɔre no, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Momma yɛnkɔ nkɔsi mpataa afiri.” Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, “Mate.” Afei, Ɔkraman no ka kyerɛɛ Ɔkraman no sɛ, “Ɛfa saa mpataa afiri-hyehyɛ akwantuo a wo ne Ananse rekɔ so yi ho no, nkɔ, ɛfiri sɛ Ananse se ɔrehwehwɛ ɔkwasea, sɛ berɛ a ɔne ɔkwasea no de mpataa afiri asi hɔ s, obenya sika no nyinaa.” Ɔkraman no buae sɛ, “Ɛho nhia sɛ woka bi bio, efisɛ mɛhwɛ ma manya sika no nyinaa.” Ɔkraman ne Akɔre no sii kwan so kɔɔ kwae no mu sɛ wɔrekɔtwa mmɛw nkorabata. Ɔkraman no ka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, “Ma me sekan no na mɛkɔ akɔtwitwa (wɔn), na w’adwuma bɛyɛ sɛ wobɛfa ɔbrɛ (a efi m’adwuma mu aba) no.” Akɔre no buae sɛ, “So wofa me sɛ ɔkwasea, ma menyɛ twitwa, bere a woregye ne brɛ.” Na Ananse retwa (nsateaa) bere a Anene, Ɔkraman no te hɔ resi apini. Bere a otwitwaa (wɔn) wiei no, wɔkyekyeree wɔn (wɔ kotoku mu). Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, “Akɔre, ma memfa na mensoa wɔn, na wo nso wobɛfa ne brɛ.” Akɔre no buae sɛ, “Fi adi, wofa me sɛ ɔkwasea?" Na ɔfaa wɔn de guu ne ti so. Ɔkraman no dii n’akyi na na ɔresi apini. Bere a woduu fie no, Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Ma menyɛ mpataa afiri no na wofa m'adwumaden no brɛ." Ananse buae se, "Da; ma mennyɛ wɔn na wofa ɔbrɛ no." Afei Ɔkɔre no kɔtenaa n’akyi, na na ɔresi apini. Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɔkwasea, woka sɛ wowɔ nteaseɛ, nanso worewuwu." Akɔre no wiee mpataa-afiri no. Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Ma mensoa mpataa afiri no na memfa nkɔ nsuo no ho, na wofa ne brɛ." Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "Dabi, me ne wɔn bɛkɔ, na wo nso wofa ne brɛ." Woduu nsu no ho; Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Agya Akɔre, aboa bi te nsuo no mu, enti ma mennyina nsuo no mu na memfa mpataa afiri no nsi hɔ, sɛ aboa no ka me a, wobɛfa ne wuo." Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ah, wofa me sɛ ɔkwasea a ɔfata; me ara mɛto afiri no, na sɛ ɔka me a, wobɛfa ne wuo." Ananse faa mpataa afiri no de guu nsuo no mu, na wɔsan baa fie. Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma daa adi no, wɔkɔhwehwɛɛ afiri no mu, na na wɔakyere mpataa abien. Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Akɔre, wofa saa mmienu yi, na sɛ ɔkyena yɛba, na (afifiri no) akyere anan a, me nso mɛfa wɔn." Akɔre no buae sɛ, "Ɔbarima yi! Wofa me sɛ ɔkwasea a ɔfata; wofa saa baanu yi, na ɔkyena, me nso mɛfa baanan no." Ɔkraman no faa wɔn. Ade kyee anɔpa no wɔkɔɔ (san kɔsraa afiri no), na wofii mu baanan (mpataa). Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Wo fa saa baanan yi, na sɛ ɔkyena yɛba na yɛkyere awotwe a, me nso mɛfa wɔn." Akɔre no buae sɛ, "Wofa me sɛ ɔkwasea; wowɔ saa anan yi, na ɔkyena me nso, mɛfa awotwe no." Ade kyee anɔpa no wɔkɔɔ (sɛ wɔrekɔsra afiri no) na wofii adi awotwe. Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Monnye (wɔn), na ɔkyena me nso, mɛfa dunsia no." Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "Wofa me sɛ ɔkwasea; so wofa awotwe no, na ɔkyena menya dunum no.” By now the fish-traps have become worn out, and the Crow addressed the Spider, saying, “Go and fetch these rotten fish-traps, and when one sells them, one will get ntanu (i. e. (£16) ma (afiri) baako biara, saa ara na wo nso, Agya Akɔre, fa mpataa yi, na fa mpataa afiri a aporɔw no de ma me.” Akɔre no kaa sɛ, “Dabi, so wofa mpataa no na wokɔtɔn wɔn, na mɛfa mpataa afiri a aporɔw no na mafa (sika a wonya no).” Wɔde (wɔn nneɛma) kɔɔ gua bi so. Ntɛm ara na na Ɔkraman no atow n’agyapade nyinaa agu; onyaa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma pii. Nanso na Spider’s (nneɛma) no da so ara wɔ hɔ. Ɛnna Ɔkraman no ka kyerɛɛ no ​​sɛ, “Fa mpataa afiri a aporɔw yi na fa ntwa mu na wɔbɛtɔ.” Akɔre no maa wɔn so; ɔkaa sɛ, “Meretɔn mpataa afiri a aporɔw!” Afei akuraa no so panyin no tee (no), na ɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛhe na onipa yi firi, kɔ na frɛ no sɛ ɔmmra.” Akɔre no kɔe. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, “So wususuw sɛ yɛn a yɛte akuraa a ɛwɔ ha no ase no yɛ nkwaseafo, afei w’adamfo (ha) ba bɛtɔn mpataa (a mfaso wɔ so), so woanhyɛ no nsow, nanso wobɛtow mpataa afiri a aporɔw agu ama yɛn?” Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, “Mommɔ no mpire.” Enti wɔboroo no. Afei Akɔre no kaa sɛ, “Akɔre, woahu, w’ani awu aniwu nti, wokaa sɛ worekɔhwehwɛ ɔkwasea bi a ɔne wo bɛyɛ adwuma, na (afei) woahu sɛ wo ara na woyɛ ɔkwasea.”

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
