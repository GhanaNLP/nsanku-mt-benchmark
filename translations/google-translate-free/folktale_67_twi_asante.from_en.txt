Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

ANANSE, AKWANKYERƐ NO KAA SƐ ƆREKƆHWEHWƐ ƆKWASƐM, BERE A BERE NYINAA NA ƆNNO HO YƐ ƆKWASƐM

Bere bi na ANANSE, Akɔre no te hɔ, na ɔkae sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkwasea bi ne no bɛkɔ akɔto mpataa afiri. Ɔkyinkyin baabiara, nanso wantumi annye bi. Afei ohyiaa Osansa, Ɔkraman no. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Momma yɛnkɔ nkɔsi mpataa afiri.” Ɔkraman no buae sɛ, “Mewɔ nam (dodow) a mɛdi.” Ɛno akyi no, ohyiaa Anene, Ɔkraman no, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Momma yɛnkɔ nkɔsi mpataa afiri.” Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, “Mate.” Afei, Ɔkraman no ka kyerɛɛ Ɔkraman no sɛ, “Ɛfa saa mpataa afiri a wɔde si hɔ akwantuo a wo ne Ananse rekɔ so yi ho no, nkɔ, ɛfiri sɛ Ananse se ɔrehwehwɛ ɔkwasea, sɛ sɛ ɔne ɔkwasea no si mpataa afiri a, ɔbɛnya sika no nyinaa.” Ɔkraman no buae sɛ, “Ɛho nhia sɛ woka bi bio, efisɛ mɛhwɛ ma manya sika no nyinaa.” Ɔkraman ne Ɔkraman no sii kwan so kɔɔ kwae no mu sɛ wɔrekɔtwa mmɛw nkorabata. Ɔkraman no ka kyerɛɛ Ananse sɛ, “Ma me sekan no na mɛkɔ akɔtwitwa (wɔn), na w’adwuma bɛyɛ sɛ wobɛfa ɔbrɛ (a efi m’adwuma mu aba) no.” Akɔre no buae sɛ, “So wofa me sɛ ɔkwasea, ma menyɛ twitwa, bere a woregye ne brɛ.” Na Ananse retwa (nsateaa) bere a Anene, Ɔkraman no te hɔ resi apini. Bere a ɔtwitwaa (wɔn) wiei no, wɔkyekyeree wɔn (wɔ kotoku mu). Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, “Akɔre, ma memfa wɔn na mensoa wɔn, na wo nso wobɛfa ne brɛ.” Akɔre no buae sɛ, “Fi adi, wofa me sɛ ɔkwasea?" Na ɔfaa wɔn de guu ne ti so. Ɔkraman no dii n’akyi na na ɔresi apini. Bere a woduu fie no, Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Ma menyɛ mpataa afiri no na wofa m'adwumaden no brɛ." Ananse buae se, "Da; ma mennyɛ wɔn na wofa ɔbrɛ no." Afei Ɔkɔre no kɔtenaa n’akyi, na na ɔresi apini. Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɔkwasea, woka sɛ wowɔ nteaseɛ, nanso worewuwu." Akɔre no wiee mpataa afiri no. Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Ma mensoa mpataa afiri no na memfa nkɔ nsuo no ho, na wofa ne brɛ." Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "Dabi, me ne wɔn bɛkɔ, na wo nso wofa ne brɛ." Woduu nsu no ho; Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Agya Akɔre, aboa bi te nsuo no mu, enti ma mennyina nsuo no mu na memfa mpataa afiri no nsi hɔ, sɛ aboa no ka me a, wobɛfa ne wuo." Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ah, wofa me sɛ ɔkwasea a ɔfata; me ara mɛto afiri no, na sɛ ɔka me a, wobɛfa ne wuo." Ananse faa mpataa afiri no de guu nsuo no mu, na wɔsan baa fie. Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma daa adi no, wɔkɔhwehwɛɛ afiri no mu, na na wɔakyere mpataa abien. Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Akɔre, wofa saa mmienu yi, na sɛ ɔkyena yɛba, na (afifiri no) akyere anan a, me nso mɛfa wɔn." Akɔre no buae sɛ, "Ɔbarima yi! Wofa me sɛ ɔkwasea a ɔfata; wofa saa baanu yi, na ɔkyena, me nso mɛfa baanan no." Ɔkraman no faa wɔn. Ade kyee anɔpa no wɔkɔɔ (san kɔsraa afiri no), na wofii mu baanan (mpataa). Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Wo fa saa baanan yi, na sɛ ɔkyena yɛba na yɛkyere awotwe a, me nso mɛfa wɔn." Akɔre no buae sɛ, "Wofa me sɛ ɔkwasea; wowɔ saa anan yi, na ɔkyena me nso, mɛfa awotwe no." Ade kyee anɔpa no wɔkɔɔ (sɛ wɔrekɔsra afiri no) na wofii adi awotwe. Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Monnye (wɔn), na ɔkyena me nso, mɛfa dunsia no." Akɔre no kaa sɛ, "Wofa me sɛ ɔkwasea; so wofa awotwe no, na ɔkyena menya dunum no.” Ebedu saa bere yi na mpataa afiri no asɛe, na Ɔkɔre no kasa kyerɛɛ Akɔre no sɛ, “Kɔ na kɔfa mpataa afiri a aporɔw yi, na sɛ obi tɔn a, obi benya ntanu (i. e. (£16) ama (afiri) biara, saa ara na wo, Agya Akɔre, fa mpataa yi, na fa mpataa afiri a aporɔw no na wode ma me” Akɔre no kae sɛ, “Dabi, wofa mpataa no na kɔ na kɔtɔn wɔn, na mɛfa mpataa afiri a aporɔw no na mafa (sika a wonya no).”Wɔsoaa (wɔn nneɛma) kɔɔ gua bi so.Ntɛm ara na Ɔkraman no atow ne nneɛma nyinaa agu, na onyaa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma pii Nanso Akɔre (nneɛma) no kaa hɔ.Afei Ɔkraman no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, “Fa mpataa afiri a aporɔw yi na wɔabɔ no maa wɔn so kae sɛ, “Meretɔn mpataa afiri a aporɔw!”Afei akuraa no ase nipa no tee (no), na ɔkae sɛ, “Ɛhe na saa ɔbarima yi fi ba; so bɛtow mpataa afiri a aporɔw agu yɛn?’ Ti-nipa no kaa sɛ, “Bɔ no mpire Enti wɔhwee no. Afei Ɔkɔre no kaa sɛ, “Akɔre, woahu, w’ani awu wɔ aniwu mu, na wokae sɛ worekɔhwehwɛ ɔkwasea sɛ ɔne wo bɛyɛ adwuma, na (afei) woahu sɛ wo ara na woyɛ ɔkwasea.”

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
