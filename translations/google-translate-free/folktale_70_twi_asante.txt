THERE are three young men; he's sister and they. The three young men from Bontuku or Odwa were going to the fields. We got to the Lamb, and one of them had a kiss. He lifted the kiss, and felt the medicine on the kiss and kissed' He said that ye'mpena was dead, and lay on the pato. He said, "Yanom ye' the mother is dead. One said, "I'll let us get to Bontuku now, and let's fight and bury him. Another said, "If you dare to get us there, I'll wake him up now. And the obako heard the medicine; he made our knees, and we' sat down. Right now we've reached it. And the other took medicine and put it on the wound'; and took his body and boiled the potion' and sprinkled it on the corpse. He said, "Wake up the corpse! wake up the corpse!" And he woke up. Which of these three did he believe?
