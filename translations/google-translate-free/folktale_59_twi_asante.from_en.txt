Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

SƐNEA ƐBAE SƐ 'KRAMAN, ƆKWANKYERƐ NO, NTUMI NNYA N'AKWANKYERƐ (AKWAN) DA .

BERE bi na Ɔkraman bi wɔ hɔ, na esiane korɔnbɔ su nti, wantumi annya ɔbea (a ofi n’ankasa akuraa ase) anware no. Enti osiim kɔɔ kurow pɔtee bi mu sɛ ɔnkɔhwehwɛ ɔbere bi ma ɔne no ware. (Ɔwaree, na ɔne no san kɔɔ n’akuraa ase.) Ɔne ne yere tenaa hɔ (bere bi), bere a ɔsii kwan so no, na ɔsan kɔɔ n’akuraa ase. Bere bi akyi no, ne kunu de nkra kɔmaa no, na ɔkae sɛ ɔreba hɔ. N’asew faa ne tuo no kɔɔ kwae no mu sɛ ɔrekɔkum ɔkraman bi de asiesie ne ho ama Ɔkraman no ba. Bɛyɛ nnansa akyi no, Ɔkraman no kɔɔ hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, ɔhunuu sɛ ɔkraman nam wɔ ogya no so, wɔ adiwo hɔ. Anadwo dui no, ɔne ne yere kɔhome. Na Ɔkraman no rebɛfiri abɔnten akɔyɛ nsuo, na ɔhunuu sɛ nam no a sradeɛ no retu akɔ ogya no mu. Ɔkraman no san bae na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔdaa, na ɔde ne ti too pillow no so (na ɔdaa) na ɔhwɛɛ nam no kyɛe. Afei nam no hua duu ne nkyɛn; ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ obegyina ano, nanso wantumi. Ow! soro ɔsɔre kɔɔ baabi a nam no wɔ; ɔwe ne nyinaa, na ɔsan kɔdaa hɔ. Ade kyee anɔpatutuutu, bere a nneɛma daa adi, bere a ne yere rekɔprapra no, ɔhwehwɛɛ nam no, na asinasin biako mpo da hɔ. Ne yere kaa sɛ, "Oh, maame! oh, maame! obi awe nam no nyinaa." Bere a Ɔkraman no tee eyi no, n’ani wui (i.e. n’ani wui), na ɔfaa ne ntama kataa ne ti, na ɔdaa hɔ komm. Afei prɛko pɛ na ɔsɔre faa kwan no so; oguan kɔɔ n’ankasa akuraa ase, na ɔkaa nsɛm, sɛnea ɔkɔɔ na ɔwe n’asew nam, ne sɛnea n’ani awu wɔ aniwu mu, ne sɛnea, esiane ɛno nti, ɔrekɔhwehwɛ obi a ɔwɔ akorɔmfo aduru, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wɔbɛma no bi. Nkurɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Adanko, Hare no, wɔ (aduru) bi a ɛwɔ tumi paa, paa, paa." Ɔkraman no sii kwan so, na ɔde ahoɔhare duu Hare akuraa ase. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya Hare, metu mmirika kɔ wo nkyɛn kɔhwehwɛ mmoa; meyɛ owifo, owifo, owifo, na wɔka sɛ wowɔ aduru (ɛno yɛ aduru) ma ɛno, enti mesrɛ wo sɛ ma me bi, na nea ɛbɛba biara no, mɛtua." Ɔkraman no buae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nokware sɛ mewɔ bi, enti kɔ na fa mprako nam bi brɛ me."

Ɔkraman no sii kwan so kɔɔ akuraa bi ase na onyaa akyi nan biako maa domafa mu duru a ɛyɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i.e. 3s. 6d.) na ɔde bae. Ɔkraman no noaa no, na ɔtwitwaa mu, na ɔteɛteɛɛm guu nnua nketewa a wɔde ayɛ akuturuku so, na ɔyam ɛmo, ne nkyene, ne ayɛyɛdeɛ, na ɔde petepetee nam no mu, na ɔde guu ogya no so. Bere a ogya no kaa no, bere bi akyi no, ɔno (Ɔkraman) tee sɛ ɛreyɛ hyew, hyee, e e e! Ɔkraman no hwɛɛ no ​​bere tenten. Afei Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Ɔkraman, wo kɔda dan no mu baako mu, na me nso, mɛkɔ akɔda foforo mu, na ɔkyena anɔpatutuutu, mɛyi nam yi afiri (ogya no so) na mede ayɛ aduru ama wo." Ɔkraman no kɔe sɛ ɔrekɔda. Ɔkraman no nso, ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔdaa. Afei Ɔkraman no da so na ɔdaa so (nanso wantumi nna), na nam no hua faa ne hwene mu baa ne so, nanso wankasa. Afei ɔtee ne hua bio. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya Hare." Ɔkraman no buae sɛ, "Yiw!" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mpɔtorɔ wɔ mo akuraa ase, enti merekɔfa nam no aba, na mede ato me nkyɛn." Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔgye; me deɛ, deɛ ɛhia me ara ne sɛ mede me nsa bɛto so ɔkyena, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a mede bɛyɛ aduru ama wo." Enti Ɔkraman no kɔfaa no. Afei Ɔkraman no da hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya Hare." Obuae se, "Yiw!" Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mpɔtorɔ no bɛwe, enti mede bɛto me pillow ase, na makɔda." Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Nea ɛhaw me ara ne sɛ ɔkyena, sɛ nneɛma mu da hɔ a, mede bɛyɛ nnuru ama wo." Ɔkraman no daa fam prɛko bio; mmɔden biara a ɔbɛbɔ no, wantumi anhyɛ ne ho so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya Hare, saa mpɔtorɔ yi aba me pillow ase, enti mɛfa nam no de ato me ti so, na ɛhɔ na mɛgyina kɔsi sɛ nneɛma bɛda adi." Hare no buae sɛ, "Oh, mekɔ so ka sɛ, nea ɛhaw me ara ne sɛ menya (nam no) ɔkyena de ayɛ aduru ama wo." Ɔkraman no faa nam no de too ne ti so, na ɛhɔ na ɔgyinaa hɔ. Afei gravy no kakra sen fa n’anim, na ɔtetew mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oo, Agya Hare, nam yi deɛ, saa mprako no aforo me so, na wɔaba me tiri so, enti merekɔkura (nam no) m'anom." Hare no buae se, "Oho o! Mekɔ so ka sɛ ɔkyena, sɛ nneɛma da adi a, mede bɛyɛ nnuru ama wo." Afei na ɛyɛ ntɛmntɛm wɔ n’anom, na ɔkyinkyin twaa dan no ho hyiae wɔ hɔ; ɔwosow ne dua (a ɔde nnyigyei a ɛsakra), fre! fre! fre! Ɔkaa sɛ, "Agya Hare, ma memfa wo ka wɔ saa nam yi ho." Hare no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na wopɛ sɛ wode ka wɔ ho?" Ɔkraman no buae sɛ, "Mekyerɛ sɛ, sɛ anka saa mprako yi bedi no, ma mentua wo ka." Hare no kaa sɛ, "Oh dabi! ma ɔkyena adekyee mmra." Ow! nanso afei Ɔkraman no maa (nam no) kɔ (siane ne menewam). Afei ɔhwee fam na ɔdae. Ade kyee anɔpa, ntɛm paa, Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Ɔkraman, fa saa nam no bra." Ɔkraman no buae sɛ, "Ho, Agya Hare, adɛn nti na, woante nea meka kyerɛɛ wo nnɛra no, sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mede wo ka (nam no ho)?" Hare no buae se, "Enti woadi no?" Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Yiw! Medii, ɛfiri sɛ metɔɔ." Ɔkraman no kaa sɛ, "Wo, Ɔkraman, korɔnbɔ a wɔfrɛ no 'korɔnbɔ' no rentwam wɔ wo nkyɛn da."

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
