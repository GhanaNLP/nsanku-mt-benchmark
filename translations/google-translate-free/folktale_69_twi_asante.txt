NWIADOWA and Nkuruma heard 'turn silent'; ye'pra gu half, and ye'da half. We've done 'one room drink' And Nwiadowa said to Nkuruma, "Ye'dan yim' is a shame, so let us go and dig a hole, and throw the grass agum' Nkuruma said, "I'm not going. Nwiadowa siim' is also amenable. O'bo'a, and Nkuruma burned him, and Nwiadowa took it. He said, "Come to the pot pit and let me kiss you. Nwiadowa said, "A! Odomankoma so, have you seen a man who has dug a hole pen?" Nkuruma said, "If you don't answer, I'll come and kiss you. Nwiadowa said, "Well, take sonee sa water and bring me a drink and my cups will not be there. Nkuruma said, "You've called me a 'fool; we're here in the 'manm', we're drinking water with sonee?" Nkuruma went from there to the pit drinking' to fwee Nwiadowa. And when he o'fwe him, God heard, and sent Kokosakyi to fetch the two men. The two people are koreye. Nwiadowa spoke with his mouth; Nkuruma touched his die. And God caused okyeame to answer Nkuruma; we've justified Nwiadowa. Ene that if we'd plant Nwiadowa, it would lie 'on the ground'; o'da God and his Mpan'infuo. And on Nkuruma, stand tall; he says he's fighting God and he's answered him injustice.
