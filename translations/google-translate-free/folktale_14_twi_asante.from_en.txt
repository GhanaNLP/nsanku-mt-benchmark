SƐNEA EFUO, BƆSƆ TUTUMI-COLOBUS NO DUA BƐYƐ FITAA

WƆKA sɛ (bere bi) na Esono, Asono no te; Kwakuo, Bonsu no; Anyinhima, Bonsu a ne hwene yɛ Putty; ne Efuo, Bonsu a Ɔyɛ Black-Colobus; na wɔkaa sɛ wɔbɛkɔ akuraa bi ase akɔhwehwɛ mmaa aware. Afei wosiim. Bere a wɔrekɔ no, wohuu ɔtare bi a atɛkyɛ wom. Bonsu a ne hwene yɛ Putty no kaa sɛ, "Gyina hɔ bere a merebisa wo biribi." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wɔ saa akwantuo yi a yɛrekɔ so yi mu no, hena (wɔ yɛn mu) a ne ho yɛ fɛ?" Asono no kaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ Efuo, Black-Colobus Bonsu." Kwakuo (bonsu foforo no) kaa sɛ, "Sɛ ɛte saa a, momma yɛnhwe no, na yɛmfa no nhyɛ ɔtare a atɛkyɛ wom no mu." Nokwarem no wɔboroo no, na wɔfaa no de no kɔtoo ɔtare a atɛkyɛ wom no mu, na wɔkɔɔ so. Afei Colobus Bonsu no sɔree (fii ɔtare no mu), na odii wɔn akyi sum ase, (ɔnantew) brɛoo, na ɔkɔtenaa akuraa no mfinimfini-aboaboa ano. Na nnipa a wɔaka no duu akuraa no ase no, wɔhunuu sɛ mmabaa nkumaa ahyiam wɔ Gyedua-dua bi ase. Mmabaa nkumaa no huu wɔn. Oyi kaa sɛ, "Mepɛ oyi"; eyi kaa sɛ, "Mepɛ oyi." Na wɔwaree mmabaa no nyinaa. Afei, na ababaa baako wɔ hɔ, a wakɔ afuo no mu, (nanso ansa na ɔretu kwan) na waka akyerɛ ne maame sɛ, "Sɛ mmeranteɛ no ba a, paw baako na fa no to nkyɛn ma me." Na ɔfiri afuo no mu san baeɛ no, ɔbisaa ne maame se, “Me deɛ wɔ he?” Obuae se, "Bere a mekɔɔ (sɛ merekɔhwɛ ho) no, na wɔakyekyɛ (mmerante no) mu dedaw." Abeawa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ hwee." Na ɔyii plantains bi nwoma, na na ɔrekɔtow nwi no agu midden-heap no so.

Afei, na saa Colobus Bonsu no aba bɛtena midden-heap no so. Na abeawa no rebɛtow nwura no agu so, na Colobus Bonsu no kae sɛ, “Ntow ngu me so.” Abeawa no kaa sɛ, “Hena ne saa?” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛyɛ me.” Abeawa no kaa sɛ, “Ɛyɛ dɛn?” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ná me ne me mfɛfoɔ agodie reba sɛ yɛrebɛware (mo) mmabaa, berɛ a wɔboroo me, na wɔmaa me nipadua nyinaa guu fĩ, na ɛno nti na maba sɛ merebɛtena ha.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Me deɛ, medɔ wo, enti bra na yɛnkɔ fie.” Ɔne ɔne no kɔe, na ɔnyaa nsuo hohoroo no. Bere a ɔfaa fiber sponge de guu ne honam ani kakra no, ohui sɛ ne honam ani yɛ hyew fɛfɛɛfɛ ankasa. Abeawa no kaa sɛ, “Maame, bra bɛhwɛ, na ahoɔfɛ te sɛ eyi nyɛ ade a wɔde di agoru.” Na ɛna no kaa sɛ, “Monhohoro no wie.” Na ɔhohoro no wiee, na ɔde ngo guu no, na ɔfaa ne mpaboa, na ɔfaa ne sankuo (nnwom agyan), na ɔkɔɔ tenaa Gyedua-dua no ase, na ɔbɔɔ. Afei mmeawa a na wɔaware dedaw no, bere a wohuu no ara pɛ no, oyi kae sɛ, “M’ani nnye me kunu bio,” saa biako no kae sɛ, “M’ani nnye me kunu bio, ɛyɛ Black-Colobus Bonsu a medɔ no no.” Black-Colobus Bonsu no kaa sɛ, “Ɔno a manya no, medɔ no.” Afei mmoa no nyinaa kyinkyin de wɔn ho dii akɔneaba, na wɔkae sɛ wɔrekɔfa wɔn nneɛma na wɔasan aba. Na bere a mmoa no kɔe no, sɛ anka wɔbɛkɔ (akɔ so) no, wɔkɔtenaa kwan no so, na wɔtwɛn Black-Colobus Bonsu no sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wɔbɛboro no na wɔakum no bere a waba no. Black-Colobus Bonsu no nso kyinkyin kɔdii nkra, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Me nso, merebɛboaboa me nneɛma ano na masan aba.” Na ɔsiim. Ɔduruu kwan no so no, mmoa no hunuu no; wɔkaa sɛ, “Abibifo-Kolobo Bonsu wɔ hɔ, Abibifo-Kolobo Bonsu wɔ hɔ.” Afei Black-Colobus Bonsu no soo (nkorabata bi) dua bi (na ohuruw kɔɔ soro), kra! kra! kra! Afei mmoa no faa dɔte fitaa tow guu no so, na ebinom bataa ne dua ho. Saa na ne dua yɛɛ fitaa; na ebinom bɔɔ ne hwene. Ɛno nti na ne hwene yɛɛ fitaa no. Bere tenten a atwam no na Black-Colobus Bonsu dua ne ne hwene yɛ tun, tun, tun, tuntum paa.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
