Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

SƐNEA ƐBAE SƐ SƐ NWANSANA, ƆKWANKYERƐ NO, TENA KRAMAN, ƆKRAMAN NO SO A, ƆDE NE HO, KAM! KAM!

NA ɔbɔmmɔfo bi wɔ akraman baanan; na wɔfrɛ biako Sniff-sniff; na wɔfrɛ foforo nso Lick-lick; na wɔfrɛ foforo nso Tie-in-knots; na wɔfrɛɛ foforo nso Gulp-down. Ná ɔne ne kraman bɔ abɔmmɔ. Bere nyinaa, sɛ ɔkɔ wuram a, ɔne wɔn bom kɔ. Da bi ɔkɔɔ kwae mu na ɔhunuu kola-dua bi; na nnuaba a ɛsensɛn fam akuwakuw te sɛ eyi akata so. Afei ɔsan kɔɔ fie, na ɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ ne yere, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Kola-dua bi, a mahu no, wɔde nnuaba a ɛsensɛn fam wɔ akuwakuw te sɛ eyi so akata so." Ne yere kaa sɛ, "Kɔ na kɔfa wɔn, ɛfiri sɛ wunim sɛ yenni kola-nut baako mpo a yɛbɛwe na yɛnni biara a yɛbɛtɔn." Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no sii kwan so, nanso ɔgyaa akraman no wɔ fie, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a kola-dua no wɔ. Afei ɔforoo so, na ɔtetew kola-nuts no bi. Afei ɔtee firii ha sɛ, "Twa! be! kukuru! kukuru! hu! pi!" Sasabonsam bi (gyinaa) n’anim. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Hena na watetew me kola-nuts?" Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Me." Sasabonsamfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Mekum wo ntɛm ara ɛnam saa adeɛ a woayɛ yi nti." Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wobɛkum me a, ma menni kan mfrɛ me akraman." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Frɛ, nanso sɛ wobɔ mmɔden mprɛnsa sɛ wobɛfrɛ wɔn na wɔamma a, ɛnde mɛkum wo." Afei ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no maa (ne nne) so :

"Sniff-sniff, (bra na) hyia me, Lick-lick, (bra na) hyia me, Tie-in-knots, (bra na) hyia me, Gulp-down, (bra na) hyia me."

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔreba akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔreba akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔreba akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔreba akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔreba akyɛ!”

Na akraman a wɔwɔ fie no tee no brɛoo paa, na wotuu mmirika kɔɔ kuro no akyi. Afei ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no maa (ne nne) so bio sɛ:

“Sniff-sniff, (bra na) hyia me, Lick-lick, (bra na) hyia me, Tie-in-knots, (bra na) hyia me, Gulp-down, (bra na) hyia me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔreba akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔreba akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔreba akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔreba akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔreba akyɛ!”

Afei akraman ate; wotuu mmirika, prada! prada! Sasabonsam no kaa sɛ, “Frɛ bio, na ɛno na ɛwie ne mprɛnsa so.” Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no maa (ne nne) so denden paa sɛ:

“Sniff-sniff, (bra na) hyia me, Lick-lick, (bra na) hyia me, Tie-in-knots, (bra na) hyia me, Gulp-down, (bra na) hyia me.

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔreba akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔreba akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔreba akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔreba akyɛ!

Wai, wai, wai, Wɔreba akyɛ!” Ná n’ano nnya nnyina hɔ, bere a na akraman no di n’anim no. Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Sniff-sniff, Sasabonsam bi nie, sniff no." Na Sniff-sniff hwim no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Lick-lick, lick no so." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Tye-n-knot, kyekyere no wɔ knot mu." Tie-in-knots kyekyeree ne menewa wɔ nhama mu; na Sasabonsam no hwee fam hɔ, kum! Afei Gulp-down faa no kɔe na ɔmenee no. Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no ne akraman no boaboaa kola-nuts no ano de baa fie. Bere a ɔbɔmmɔfo no san bae no, ɔbaa hɔ bɛnoaa aduan a wɔayam no yiye, na ɔde too nkyɛn wɔ hɔ maa akraman no; ɔde nsu petepetee so de guu hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ ne yere nkyɛn wɔ hɔ sɛ ɔrekɔdidi. Bere a odii wiei no, ofii adi kɔɔ abɔnten kɔtenaa gyedua (dua) bi ase. Afei ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no akoa, Fly, kɔhwɛɛ aduane no (a wɔde ato hɔ sɛ wɔasiesie ama akraman no), na ɔmaa so de too ɔdan no atifi. Akraman no fi abɔnten baa mu na ɔkɔm de wɔn. Lick-lick ka kyerɛɛ afoforo no sɛ, "Moahu nea yɛayɛ ama yɛn wura no, nanso ɛde besi nnɛ no, wamma yɛn aduan biara mpo sɛ yenni, enti momma yɛnkɔkum no." Wobuae se, "Monni atoro, monnni atoro." Lick-lick fii adi kɔɔ abɔnten kɔtetew ɔbɔmmɔfo no nipadua, na ɔne ɔbɔmmɔfo no dii agoru; na Sniff-sniff nso baeɛ; na ɔhwee ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no amu; na ɔsan nso kɔe. Afei bere a Tie-in-knots bae bɛkyekyeree ne menewa wɔ nhama mu no, na Gulp-down nso, bae bɛmenee no. Afei akraman no san baa fie hɔ; na wɔrebɛhwɛ pato no mu, na wohuu soup a ɛtɔ gu fam wɔ hɔ. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Fly, ebia ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no gyaw yɛn aduane na woadi?" Fly kaa sɛ, "Minhuu bi wɔ baabiara." Sniff-sniff, bere a ɔmaa n’ani so no, ohui sɛ aduan no asisi hɔ (wɔ ɔdan no atifi). Ɔkaa sɛ, “Oo, agya! yɛakum yɛn wura kwa, ɛfiri sɛ ampa ara aduane a ɔde asi hɔ ama yɛn no wɔ hɔ, na ɛnyɛ baako, na ɛnyɛ mmienu nso, ɛyɛ Nwansena no na ɔfaa no de too hɔ." Nwansena no guan firii hɔ kɔeɛ. Ɛno nti, sɛ ɔkraman te hɔ, na nwansena bi te ne so a, ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkyere no.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
