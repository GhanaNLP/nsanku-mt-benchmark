Yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa, (sɛ nea yɛrebɛka no yɛ nokware) .

SƐNEA OBI NYAA ƆYERE (NA NA ƆSAN GYE NE NKWA) Dnam AKWAN A ƆKYERƐKYERƐ NSƐNKYERƐMU SO

BERE bi na mmarima baasa te hɔ na na wɔpɛ adwuma. Wɔkɔ Tall-grass-country kɔtɔɔ nkyekyere ne nneɛma foforɔ. (Wɔn san kɔɔ Ashanti) wɔmaa wɔn nua barima soaa nneɛma no bi, na wɔkɔe tɔn. Wɔbaa kurow kɛse bi mu. Na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna babea bi wɔ kurow no mu a ɔpow obiara a wɔde no bɛma no aware no. Afei saa ahɔhoɔ yi (aguadifoɔ no) kɔɔ hɔ (kɔ ne fie), nanso na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba no akɔ " akɔboro " ahohoro no. Ɔhemmaa Ɛna (nanso) tɔɔ ntama mmienu de too nkyɛn maa ne babea. Bere a ne ba san bae no ohuu mmarima baasa no. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ne akwakoraa no se, " Ɛnnɛ mahu nea mepɛ no, enti kum oguan na ma memfa nkyerɛ wɔn." Bere a wɔnoa aduan no wiei no, abeawa kumaa no kɔfaa asono sare, kotoku, kola nut, ne nnuadewa a wɔayam no dua bi, na ɔde guu aduan no so sɛ ɔnkɔma mmarima no. Na ɔkaa sɛ, " Nea obetumi akyerɛkyerɛ wɔn ntease mu no, ɔno na mɛware no." Na wɔde aduane no kɔe. Mmarima no yii mu, na wokaa se, " Se ennye Ohemmaa Maame ba na ode saa aduane yi bae a, anka yenya nsem bi bisa no." Mmarima no wɔfase kaa sɛ, " Sɛ mansuro mo a, anka metumi aka nea saa nneɛma no kyerɛ akyerɛ mo." Wokaa se, " Kyerɛkyerɛ mu." Abofra no buae se, " Abeawa no se wama n'adwene ayɛ fitaa te sɛ kotoku, na sɛ obetumi anya yɛn mu biako a yɛaba ha no a, ɛnsɛ sɛ ɔware? Asono sare no asekyerɛ ne sɛ, da bi bɛba a ɔbɛyera; kola nut no nso kyerɛ sɛ da bi bɛba a ɔbɛma no (ne kunu) awe kola (i. e. ayɛ awerɛhow). Asase mu nnua no kyerɛ sɛ ɔpɛ." wo sɛ wobɛdua nnuaba a wɔayam ama no, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a afe biara, sɛ ɔkɔhwɛ ntini no a ɔbɛnya bi atwe akɔ soro." Enti Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba waree mmarima no nua barima. Bere a ɔwaree no wiei no, Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no ba bisaa no sɛ, " Wo din de dɛn? " Ɔkaa sɛ, " Wɔfrɛ me Kwaku Susufuo," kyerɛ sɛ, Kwaku-a-ɔfa-ɔhaw-sɛ-ɔdwene. Abeawa no buae sɛ, " Me nso, wɔfrɛ me Akua-a-ɔfa-ɔhaw-de-dwene." Ɔde guasodeyɛ a ɛfata waree no. Ade kyee anɔpa no, anuanom mmarima baasa no sii kwan so kɔɔ wɔn ankasa kurom. Kwaku-a-ɔfa-ɔhaw-de-dwene no kɔɔ so traa ne yere akuraa ase. Ɔtenaa hɔ afe baako, na sɛ ɔsɔre (anɔpa) a, na ɔne ne nnamfonom tena nte marbles so di agorɔ. Afei, da bi a na ɔredi agorɔ saa no, ɔbɔɔ marble mmienu wɔ tuo baako mu, nanso nnipa a ɔne wɔn redi agorɔ no, kaa sɛ wɔremfa mma no, na wɔpɛɛ mpo sɛ wɔboro no. Ɔhwɛɛ n’akyi, na ɔhunuu abofra bi sɛ ɔgyina hɔ; ɔfaa ɔbo bi de maa abofra no sɛ ɔmfa na ɔnkɔmfa mma ne yere. Afei bere a ne yere nsa kaa ɔbo no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, " Me kunu pɛ sɛ ɔsan kɔ ne kurom, efisɛ ɔka sɛ anomaa bi tra fam kyɛ dodo a, onya ɔbo." Na Ɔhemmaa Ɛna no kaa sɛ, " Sɛ ɔkɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ wo ne no kɔ." Nokwarem no, ɔne ɔne no kɔe. Na wohyiaa Kwaku-a-ɔfa-ɔhaw-de-dwene ne papa nua mmarima. Afei, yei akyi bere bi no, wɔka kyerɛɛ no ​​sɛ, " Kwaku-a-ɔfa-ɔhaw-de-dwene, gye hama bi na yɛakɔ wuram akɔto afiri sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a yɛbɛkyere mmorɔnoma ama yɛagye ahɔhoyɛ ama wo yere a ɔyɛ ɔhɔho no." Enti wosiim, nanso na saa mpanyimfo yi (nokwarem) apene so sɛ wobekum no. Wɔn ne Kwaku-a-ɔfa-ɔhaw-de-dwene no kɔe; woduu (beae bi a ɛyɛ ankonam) wɔ kwan no so; wɔkyekyeree no ka kyerɛɛ no ​​sɛ, “Yɛbɛkum wo.” Afei ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, “Bere a mekɔɔ me yere akuraa ase no, mihuu sika bebree, na sɛ moamma me kwan sɛ menkyerɛ mo baabi a ɛwɔ, na miwu a, morenhu.” Wobisaa no se, “Ɛhe na sika no da?” Obuae sɛ, “Ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ me yere nkyɛn kɔbɔ no amanneɛ sɛ matɔ nam, na (adetɔnfo no) ka sɛ ne bo yɛ peredwan du (£80), na ɛsɛ sɛ ɔhwehwɛ kotoku a wɔde nguan nwoma ayɛ no mu;sɛ ɔhwɛ hɔ na wanhu a, ma ɔnhwɛ Krampan kotoku no mu, sɛ wanhu wɔ hɔ a, ma ɔnhwɛ abɔso no mu a nnade no wɔ mu.” Enti wɔbaa hɔ bɛkaa nkrasɛm no maa ne yere. Na ɔno nso kaa sɛ, “Nneɛma no wɔ akuraa no fie, enti momma yɛnkɔfa.” Ɔne wɔn kɔɔ ɔpanyin no fie; na ɔbaa no kaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ ɔpanyin no, kyerɛkyerɛɛ mu sɛ, “Nhama kotokuo a wɔde nguan nwoma ayɛ no kyerɛ sɛ wɔakyekyere no; Krampan no kyerɛ sɛ akɔre no rebɔ mmɔden sɛ wɔbɛwe ne nam; abɔso a ɛkura nnade no kyerɛ sɛ wɔrebɛtwa ne menewam.Ntɛm ara na ti-nipa no bɔɔ gong-gong, sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wɔkɔ hɔ kɔgye nkwa Kwaku-a-ɔfa-ɔhaw-de-dwene Na wɔne no san bae. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, “Obi nam sɛnkyerɛnne kwan so nkyerɛase so nyaa ɔyere na (asan nso) ogyee ne nkwa.” Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
