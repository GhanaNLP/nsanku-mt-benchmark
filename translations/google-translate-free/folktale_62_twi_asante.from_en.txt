SƐNEA AWURADE BAA ABUSUE NO MU

BERE bi ɔbɔmmɔfo bi a wɔfrɛ no Kwasi Gyaba ne ne yere kɔtenaa nsraban bi mu sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wɔbɛbɔ abɔmmɔ. Afei, bere a Kwasi Gyaba kɔɔ wuram no, wamfa nam biara amma. Wamfa nam biara amma da.

Afei, da bi ne yere bisabisaa no nsɛm sɛ, "Megye di sɛ, sɛ wokum nam a, wotɔn, na wodi (sika a wonya) anaasɛ wode ma wɔ akyɛde mu, enti ɛnnɛ, sɛ wokɔ kwae mu, na woankɔfa nam ansan a, merekɔ me maame nkyɛn wɔ hɔ, efisɛ meyare sɛ mɛtra mpɔtorɔ, ne mpataa, ne ntontom, ne nea ɛtete saa so. Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ, Kwasi Gyaba, sii kwan so kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu. Ohuu duyker bi sɛ ɔretwam. Ɔkaa (kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ), "Duyker, baabi a woda no, ɛhɔ na me ne wo bɛhome." Duyker no kɔgyinaa dua bi ase. Ɔno (ɔbɔmmɔfo no) maa tuo no so, pam! Python bi —baabi a efi bae no na papayɛ nkutoo na wonim —baa Duyker no so, na Asono nso huruw baa so. Ɔbɔmmɔfo no kɔɔ so siei na ɔyɛɛ komm. Ɔkaa (kyerɛɛ ne ho sɛ), "Awerɛhosɛm! awerɛhosɛm bɛn ara na mahu." Python no kaa sɛ, "Agya ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ, bra, deɛ ti baako redwene, ɛtɔ da bi a ti foforɔ ate, enti, ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ, bra ha, na ma yɛnkɔ yɛn akuraa ase, na yɛnkyɛ saa duyker yi, ɛfiri sɛ yɛrenyɛ wo bɔne biara." Ɔbɔmmɔfo no faa ne tuo no dii wɔn akyi. Wɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔkyekyɛɛ nam no mu. Python no kaa sɛ, "Agya ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ, m'ani gye wo ho, enti da ha nnɛ, na mɛma wo aduru; afei saa aduru yi, sɛ wode to w'aso mu a, wobɛte nhaban, nnua, ne nsubɔnten nne, ne mmoa nyinaa kasa. Enti anadwo yi kɔ na kɔda, nanso nhome." Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kɔdaa; ogyaee ahomegye. Python no faa aduru de guu n’aso mu. Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma daa adi no, ɔbɔmmɔfo no tee nneɛma nyinaa kasa ase. Ɔsiim, na ɔbaa n’akuraa ase. Afei, ɔbaa hɔ no, ɔtee sɛ n’asew aba ne babaa (fie). Afei na n’asew anifuraefo wɔ n’ani nifa so. Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kɔtenaa dan no mu. Afei, kasa ne nea aka (i. e. me werɛ fii sɛ mɛka ho asɛm), kae sɛ bere a ohyiaa n’asew no, na ɔrebɔ atoko. Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no tenaa hɔ komm, na nnomaa mmienu a na wɔwɔ hɔ (firii aseɛ kasaeɛ); obiako bisaa ɔfoforo no sɛ, "Wobɛyɛ dɛn atumi anya atoko no, bere a, sɛ mekɔ (sɛ mekɔfa) a, ɔpam me?" Ɔfoforo no buae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na, woanhyɛ no nsow sɛ n'ani nifa so yɛ onifuraefo, enti bere a (wopɛ) sɛ wobɛfa atoko no, sɛ wotwam wɔ nea asɛe no ho a, ontumi nhu wo?" Kwasi Gyaba tee (eyi); ɔde ne ho too n’akyi na ɔserewee, twe! twe! twe! N'asew kaa sɛ, "Ah, m'asew, adɛn nti na woreserew me saa?" Nanso Kwasi Gyaba da fam serewee, na ɔkɔɔ so serewee. Ɛhɔ ne afei, n’asew kaa Wim-nyame no asɛm a wɔabara no guu ne so, na ɔkae sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ Kwasi Gyaba kyerɛ nea ɛde ne serew bae wɔ Wim-nyame no anim. Wim-nyame no somaa abɔfoɔ sɛ wɔmfa Kwasi Gyaba mmra. Bere a oduu kurow no awiei no, ɔmaa ne dwom so:

"Asei merekasa na mawu, Kwasi Gyaba e!

Merebɛkasa na mawu, sɛ eyi ba a, ɛnsɛ sɛ mosu mma me. Kwasi Mpuroko Gyaba, ɔrekɔ, Sɛ eyi ba a ɛnsɛ sɛ wosu ma me.”

Wim-nyame no mpanyimfo no kae sɛ, “Tie ɔdeduani no dwom.” Wɔfaa Kwasi Gyaba wɔ Wim-nyame no anim. Okyeame (kasamafoɔ) no sɔree na ɔkaa sɛ, “Kwasi Gyaba, ampa ara woserewee w’asew, anaasɛ ɔredi atoro?” Kwasi Gyaba buae sɛ, “Owura, ɛnam aduru bi, a mewɔ ne nkɛntɛnso ase nti, sɛ meda nea enti (me serew) adi a, mɛwu.” Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, “Esiane sɛ woyɛ m’abɔmmɔfoɔ nti, meremma wo nwu.” N’asew kaa sɛ, “Owura, ɔredi atoro, na ɔreserew me kɛkɛ, enti m’ani rennye biribi foforo biara ho gye nkyerɛkyerɛmu.” Ɔkasafo no kaa sɛ, “Kwasi Gyaba, woate?” Kwasi Gyaba buae sɛ, “Ɛnde mesrɛsrɛ sɛ wɔmma ɔdamoa, na sɛ mawu a, fa me to mu.” Ankyɛ na n’ano gyinaa hɔ ara, na Akokɔ ne Akokɔ bi twaam; na Akokɔ no ka kyerɛɛ Akokɔ no se, “Gyina hɔ na ma mentra wo so.” Akokɔ no kaa sɛ, “Dɛn, wɔ saa nnipa yi anim?” Akokɔ no kaa sɛ, “Sɛ woannyina hɔ a, mɛka asɛm a wɔabara no de akyerɛ sɛ wobɛtumi ayi wo ho afiri hɔ.” Afei Kwasi Gyaba tee saa. Ntɛm ara ɔkaa sɛ, “Owura, Wim-nyame, ma wonnyae ɔdamoa tutu, ɛfiri sɛ mewɔ asɛm bi ka.” Wim-nyame no kaa sɛ, “Kasa.” Kwasi Gyaba kaa sɛ, “Meka asɛm a wɔabara no, na m’asew bɛfa ne ba akɔ.” Mpanyimfo no nyinaa kaa sɛ, “Ɛno yɛ nokware, ɛyɛ nokware, asɛm no awie.” Ɛhɔ ne afei Wim-nyame no yɛɛ mmara bi a ɛkyerɛ sɛ, “obiara, w’adamfo mpo, sɛ ɔhaw wo a, ka asɛm a wɔabara no kyerɛ no, anaasɛ sɛ ɛyɛ wo yere na ɔyɛ saa a, ka asɛm a wɔabara no na gyae no.” Enti ɛbaa sɛ awaregyae baa abusuakuw no mu. Ɛyɛ Akokɔ a yɛyɛɛ no ​​mfonini no.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
