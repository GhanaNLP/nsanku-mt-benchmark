SƐNEA AKOKONINI, AKOKU NO, NYAA ANO A ƐYƐ NSƐM

NA AGYA ANANSE, Akɔre no, ne ne mma Afudotwedotwe (Yafunu-te sɛ-ɛpae), Nyiwankonfwea (Tin-hanks), (ne) Tikonokono (Big-big-head) te hɔ. Na Akɔre no ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ mokɔhwehwɛ borɔdɔma ma me di." Enti mmofra yi sii kwan so; wɔnantee na wɔnantee, na wɔnantee, na wohyiaa borɔdɔma dua bi, na (borɔdɔma no) abere, na mmofra no kɔɔ (dua no) ase. Afudotwedotwe (Yafunu-te sɛ-ɛpae) ka kyerɛɛ Nyiwankonfwea (Thin-shanks) sɛ, "Foro na tutu wɔn mu bi." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, sɛ meforo a me nan bɛbubu." Tikonokono (Big-big-head) kaa sɛ, "Wo, Afudotwedotwe, so woforo kɔ soro." Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, sɛ meforo kɔ soro a, me yafunu bɛpae; w'ankasa woforo." Enti Tikonokono (Ti kɛse-kɛse) foroo. Ɔtetew Borɔdɔma dua no mma no bi na ɔkɔɔ so tetew na ɔtetew, afei ɔkae sɛ, "So ɛno bɛyɛ?" Wɔn a wɔaka no kaa sɛ, "Ah, (yɛbɛyɛ dɛn ahu?)." Enti ɔkɔɔ so twee wɔn; afei ɔsan bisaa wɔn sɛ, “Ɛno bɛyɛ yie anaa?” Na wobuaa sɛ: “Bɔ fam na hwɛ.” Na bere a ɔrebɛbrɛ ne ti ase, sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ no, afei ne ti bɔɔ fam, na ɔkaa (asɛmfua bi te sɛ) "Kum!" Bere a anka yafunu a ɛte sɛ nea ɛpae bɛserew no, ne yafunu paee wɔ bang mu, ta! Thin-shanks, bere a na obeguan akɔka akyerɛ ne papa no, ne nan bubui na ɛkɔɔ so tra ban no akyi. Na Akokɔ no rehohoro ne kra, na ɔrepue sɛ ɔrekɔtow nkesua-akutu (ayɛyɛde no) agu, bere a ohui sɛ Thin-shanks ahwe ase na ne nan abubu, na wɔagyaw no wɔ ban no fã foforo. Bere a ɔrebɛserew no, n’ano yɛɛ den sɛ:

"Bere a na ɔrebɛserew no, N'ano bɛyɛɛ nsensanee."

Bere a ɔrebɛserew no, N'ano yɛɛ nsensanee."

Ɛno nti na Mpanyimfo no ka sɛ, "Sɛ obi hwe ase a, kɔ na monhwɛ no, na nserew." Afei Akokɔ deɛ, ɔyɛɛ n’ade saa, na ɔserewee, ɛno nti n’ano yɛɛ nsensanee; kan no na ɔwɔ ano fɛfɛ na ɛyɛ fɛ.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
