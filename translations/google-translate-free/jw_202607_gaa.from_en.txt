Kasemɔ Nii Kɛjɛ Gibeonbii lɛ Adɛŋ

“Gibeon bi ɔmɛ kɛ Israel so tue mi jɔmi, nɛ a hi a kpɛti.”​— YOS. 10:1 lɛ he.

AFI lɛ ji afi 1473 D.Ŋ.B. Israel maŋ lɛ eje Shiwoo Shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ kunimyeli shishi etsɛko. Israelbii lɛ eye Yeriko kɛ Ai maji lɛ anɔ kunim yɛ gbɛ ni yɔɔ naakpɛɛ nɔ. Shi sɛɛ mli lɛ, nɔ ko ni akpaaa gbɛ ba. Gbɛfalɔi akuu ko ba amɛŋɔɔ, ni amɛkɛɛ amɛjɛ shikpɔŋ shɔŋŋ ni amɛmiitao amɛkɛ Nyɔŋmɔ webii lɛ ato toiŋjɔlɛ kpaŋmɔ.

2 Nakai gbɛfalɔi lɛ ji Gibeonbii lɛ. Enɛ ji klɛŋklɛŋ be ni atsĩ Gibeonbii lɛ atã yɛ Biblia lɛ mli, shi jeee naagbee nɔ ni. Biblia mli saji babaoo tsɔɔ akɛ Gibeonbii lɛ kɛ Israelbii lɛ bɔ gbagbalii yɛ afii ohai ni nyiɛ sɛɛ lɛ amli fɛɛ. Nikasemɔi ni he hiaa yɛ saji nɛɛ amli kɛha wɔ, ni amɛhaa wɔleɔ Yehowa Nyɔŋmɔ he saji babaoo.

Amɛfata Hivbii amaŋ lɛ he, ni ji Kanaanbii amaji kpawo ni “mɛi ayi fa ni amɛhe wa” fe Israelbii lɛ ateŋ ekome. (5 Mose 7:1) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, Gibeon bi ɔmɛ he wa ngɛ ta hwumi mi. ( Yosh. 10:2 ) Shi amɛyɔse akɛ amɛte shi amɛwo .

Israel najiaŋdamɔlɔi lɛ kpɛlɛ odaseyeli ni Gibeonbii lɛ kɛba lɛ nɔ, ni amɛkɛ amɛ kpaŋ toiŋjɔlɛ kpaŋmɔ. ( Yosh. 9:14, 15 ) Enɛ ɔ se ɔ, e kɛ we kulaa nɛ a le kaa Gibeon bi ɔmɛ sisi mɛ, se Israel bi ɔmɛ kplɛɛ tue mi jɔmi kpaŋmɔ nɛ a kɛ Gibeon bi ɔmɛ so ɔ nɔ, ejakaa a “kã Yehowa kita kɛ ha mɛ.” ( Yosh. 9:16-19 ) Akɛ nitsumɔ ni baa shi wo Gibeonbii lɛ adɛŋ akɛ amɛsɔmɔ kɛya naanɔ akɛ “labualɔi kɛ nuholɔi kɛha asafo lɛ kɛ Yehowa afɔleshaa latɛ lɛ.”— Yosh. 9:27 lɛ he.

Gibeonbii lɛ kpa Yoshua fai koni eye ebua amɛ. ( Yosh. 10:3-7 ) Yoshua nyɛɛ Israel bi ɔmɛ a hɛ mi kɛ ya kpɔ̃ mɛ, nɛ a jɔɔ a mɔde bɔmi ɔ. Yehowa shwie nyɔŋmɔŋtɛi wuji po eshwie henyɛlɔi Amorbii asraafoi lɛ anɔ, ni eha je tsɛre yɛ naakpɛɛ gbɛ nɔ kɛha tawuu. ( Yosh. 10:9-14 ) Kɛtsɔ toiŋjɔlɛ kpaŋmɔ ni Gibeonbii lɛ taoɔ ni amɛkɛ Israelbii lɛ kpaŋ, ni amɛkpa Yoshua fai koni eye ebua amɛ kɛ́ awo amɛhe gbeyei lɛ nɔ lɛ, Gibeonbii lɛ jie hemɔkɛyeli ni amɛyɔɔ yɛ Yehowa mli lɛ kpo—hemɔkɛyeli yɛ Yehowa mli akɛ ebaanyɛ eha ewiemɔ lɛ aba mli ni ehere amɛ.

6 Mɛni sane nɛ ɔ tsɔɔ wɔ ngɛ Yehowa he? Yehowa baa ehe shi ni enaa mɔbɔ. Etsɔ hiɛ efã Israel koni ‘ashwie shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ bii lɛ fɛɛ kɛya,’ ni Gibeonbii lɛ hu fata he. ( 4 Mose 33:51, 52 ) Se ngɛ tue mi jɔmi somi nɛ Israel kɛ mɛ so ɔ se ɔ, Yehowa je mɔbɔ nami mi kɛ ha nɛ Gibeon bi ɔmɛ hi si. Yehowa pee jã, e ngɛ mi kaa Israel bi ɔmɛ bii lɛ ga loko a kɛ mɛ so somi ɔ mohu lɛɛ. Kɛkɛ ni eye ebua Israel ni amɛkpɛtɛ kpaŋmɔ ni amɛfee lɛ he, ni etsɔ naakpɛɛ gbɛ nɔ ekpɔ̃ Gibeonbii lɛ.— Yosh. 9:26; 11:19 lɛ he.

7 Kɛ wa ma plɛ kɛ kase Gibeon bi ɔmɛ ha kɛɛ? Kɛtsɔ hemɔkɛyeli ni mli wa ni wɔɔná yɛ Yehowa mli lɛ nɔ. Akɛnɛ wa le níhi fuu ngɛ Yehowa he pe bɔ nɛ Gibeon bi ɔmɛ le ngɛ jamɛ a be ɔ mi he je ɔ, wa ngɛ yi mi tomihi fuu po nɛ wa ma nyɛ maa da nɔ kɛ ngɔ wa hɛ kɛ fɔ Yehowa nɔ kulaa. (La 40:4, 5) Jehanɛ se hu ɔ, wa ma nyɛ maa kase Gibeon bi ɔmɛ konɛ wa je wa tsui mi nɛ wa tsu ní tsumihi nɛ a he hia we ngɛ Yehowa sɔmɔmi mi ɔ hulɔ. ( Yosh. 9:23, 27 ) Nyɛmi nuu fioo ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Luke lɛ hiɛ kpaŋ be ni ena nyɛmi nuu onukpa ko ni yɔɔ Betel weku lɛ mli lɛ ni efee nakai pɛpɛɛpɛ lɛ nɔ kɔkɔɔkɔ. Eyɛ mli akɛ nakai Betel nitsulɔ lɛ tsu nitsumɔ ko ni tsii ni ehe gbɛi yɛ gbɛ ko nɔ lɛ he nii moŋ, shi ekɛ ehe ha dioo koni eyakwɛ he ni amaa Maŋtsɛyeli Asa yɛ maŋ lɛ mli lɛ. Luke de ke: “I kase kaa he si bami pi he numi ko kɛkɛ, mohu ɔ, wa jeɔ he si bami kpo kɛ guɔ nɔ́ nɛ wa hlaa kaa wa maa pee aloo wa be peee ɔ nɔ.” Nyɛ ha nɛ wa je hemi kɛ yemi nɛ wa ngɛ ngɛ Yehowa mi ɔ kpo daa ligbi kɛ gu wa he nɛ waa kɛ maa fɔ e nɔ ke waa kɛ si fɔfɔɛhi nɛ a mi wa kpe, kɛ he si bami nɛ waa kɛ tsu teokrase ní tsumi fɛɛ ní tsumi nɛ wa ma ha wɔ ɔ nɔ.

( 2 Sam. 21:2, 5, 6 ) Enɛ ɔ ji dami sane nɛ a ye we! Saul ku toiŋjɔlɛ kpaŋmɔ ni kã Israelbii lɛ kɛ Gibeonbii lɛ ateŋ be kakadaŋŋ lɛ mli.

( 4 Mose 35:30, 31 ) Jehanɛ se hu ɔ, a yɔse kaa a be nyɛe maa gbe nɔmlɔ ke mlaa ha we he blɔ. Mɛi ni ekolɛ abaajie hiɛaŋkwɛmɔ kɛjɛ amɛhe lɛ po fata he, taakɛ bei pii lɛ ebaa yɛ maŋsɛɛbii kɛ mɛi fioo komɛi ahe lɛ. Yɛ ebe ni sa mli lɛ, Yehowa baajaje jalɛsane ni ayeee ni ekolɛ ejálɔi kɛkpe lɛ. Wa kaseɔ hu kaa Yehowa ngɛ blɔ hyɛe kaa wa maa ye wa si womi ɔmɛ a nɔ kaa bɔ nɛ e hyɛ blɔ kaa Israel bi ɔmɛ maa ye tue mi jɔmi si nɛ a kɛ Gibeon bi ɔmɛ wo mɛ ɔ nɔ ɔ.—Okɛ to Amos 1:9 he.

13 Maa pee jeha 500 ngɛ Matsɛ David be ɔ se ɔ, a ngma Gibeon bi ɔmɛ ekohu ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi. No mli lɛ, afii 70 ni akɛhi Babilon nomŋɔɔ mli lɛ eba naagbee, ni yɛ afi 537 D.Ŋ.B. ( Ezra 2:1, 2, 58 ) Yɛ afi 468 D.Ŋ.B., kuu kroko ku amɛsɛɛ shi Ezra ni ji woloŋmalɔ lɛ fata he. ( Ezra 7:1-7 ) Nakai nomŋɔɔ mli kui bibii enyɔ lɛ fɛɛ ji “Nethinim” lɛ awekui. (Hyɛ Ezra 2:58 kɛ 7:7 ɔ sisi ningma.) Mɛnɔmɛ ji Nethinim ɔmɛ? Amɛji “sɔlemɔtsu mli sɔɔlɔi loo sɔɔlɔi ni jeee Israelbii,” ni etamɔ nɔ ni amɛteŋ mɛi babaoo ji Gibeonbii lɛ aseshibii.—Kwɛmɔ Wiemɔi Ashishitsɔɔmɔ, “Nethinim” lɛ.

14 Kanemɔ 1 Kronika 9:2 kɛ shishigbɛ niŋmaa lɛ. “Sɔlemɔtsu lɛ mli tsuji” lɛ fata klɛŋklɛŋ mɛi ni aŋɔ amɛ nom ni ku amɛsɛɛ kɛba Israel lɛ ahe. No sa kadimɔ waa ejaakɛ jeee Yudafoi ni yɔɔ nomŋɔɔ mli lɛ fɛɛ ku amɛsɛɛ amɛba. Ŋwanejee ko bɛ he akɛ Yudafoi pii ena shweremɔ fioo ko yɛ Babilon, ni amɛsumɔɔɔ ni amɛshãa afɔlei ni he hiaa koni amɛku amɛsɛɛ ni amɛyaye amɛbua koni ama amɛshikpɔŋ ni akpãtã hiɛ lɛ ama shi ekoŋŋ. Shi anɔkwafoi ni aŋɔ amɛ nom, ni mɛi komɛi ni eeenyɛ efee akɛ amɛjɛ Gibeonbii lɛ aseshibii fata he lɛ shwe waa akɛ amɛaana ni ato anɔkwa jamɔ shishi ekoŋŋ yɛ sɔlemɔwe ni yɔɔ Yerusalem lɛ, ni amɛbɔ mɔdɛŋ fɛɛ ni amɛaanyɛ koni amɛku amɛsɛɛ amɛba. Ákɛ mɛi ni tamɔɔɔ Yudafoi lɛ, Gibeonbii lɛ aseshibii lɛ náaa shikpɔŋ gboshinii ko yɛ Israel. Se kɛ̃ ɔ, a kpale kɛ ba konɛ a ya tsu a ní tsumihi ngɛ sɔlemi we ɔ, nɛ a ya ye bua kɛ dla Yerusalem gbogbo ɔmɛ po.—Neh. 3:26 lɛ he.

15 Mɛni sane nɛ ɔ tsɔɔ wɔ ngɛ Yehowa he? Wɔkaseɔ akɛ wɔ-Nyɔŋmɔ lɛ hiɛ sɔɔ ejálɔi anɔkwafoi lɛ waa, ni ekwɛɔ amɛ be fɛɛ be. Beni Babilon nomŋɔɔyeli lɛ ba naagbee lɛ, no mli lɛ nɔ ni miihe ashɛ afii 1,000 eho kɛjɛ be mli ni Yehowa here Gibeonbii lɛ ayiwala yɛ be mli ni Israelbii lɛ ye Shiwoo Shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ kunim lɛ. Se kɛ̃ ɔ, e ya nɔ nɛ e jɔɔ mɛ. Gibeonbii lɛ aseshibii je Yuda kɛ Yerusalem hiɛkpatamɔ lɛ mli yɛ afi 607 D.Ŋ.B. Yɛ nomŋɔɔ mli lɛ sɛɛ lɛ, amɛtee nɔ amɛkɛ amɛhe wo anɔkwa jamɔ mli gbagbalii, ni amɛkɛ Levibii lɛ sɔmɔ yɛ sɔlemɔwe lɛ. Etamɔ nɔ ni mɛi komɛi hi he ko ni bɛŋkɛ sɔlemɔwe lɛ. ( Ezra 2:70; Neh. 11:21 ) Ni amɛnine shɛ hegbɛ nɔ akɛ amɛkawo too, onia, kɛ gbɛjegbɛ nyɔmɔ yɛ sɔlemɔwe nitsumɔ ni amɛtsuɔ lɛ hewɔ.— Ezra 7:24 .

ANI osusu bɔ ni Yehowa le nii waa ha, bɔ ni enilee lɛ da ha, loo bɔ ni etsu nibii babaoo eha lɛ he pɛŋ? Ke wa susu wa Bɔlɔ ɔ he ɔ, wa baa wa he si. Ye Obua Mɛi Krokomɛi ni Amɛle Yehowa

“Enɛ ɔ ji neneene wami, kaa a ma le mo, mo ji anɔkuale Mawu kake ɔ.”— YOH.

KƐ BIBLIA kaselɔ ko ba asafoŋ kpee klɛŋklɛŋ kwraa, ni ebɔi saji ahetoo hamɔ, egba mɛi krokomɛi ehemɔkɛyeli lɛ he sane, ni yɛ be ni sa mli lɛ ekpɛ eyiŋ akɛ ebaajɔɔ ehe nɔ eha Yehowa Nyɔŋmɔ lɛ, te onuɔ he ohãa tɛŋŋ? Ŋwanejee ko bɛ he akɛ omii shɛ ohe waa! Ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ, Yehowa ji nɔ nɛ sa yi jemi ngɛ mumi mi hɛ mi yami nɛ wa Baiblo kaselɔ ɔ náa a he. Ke wa na bɔ nɛ Yehowa yeɔ bua nɔ ko nɛ e waa ngɛ Mawu jami mi ɔ, wa jeɔ Yehowa yi.​— 1 Kor. 3:​5-9; 3 Yohane 3, 4 .

2 Mawu suɔ kaa “nimli tsuo nɛ a le anɔkuale ɔ.” (Kane 1 Timoteo 2:3, 4 .) Yehowa suɔ nɛ nihi nɛ a le lɛ kɛ e yi mi tomi ɔmɛ saminya bɔ nɛ a ma nyɛ. ( Yer. 9:24 ) Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa e sa nɛ wa ná anɔkuale nile. Shishijee wiemɔ ni atsɔɔ shishi akɛ “anɔkwa nilee” lɛ etsɔɔɔ anɔkwalei ni abua naa kɛkɛ ni mɔ ko kaseɔ, shi moŋ nilee ni “náa mɔ lɛ nɔ hewalɛ kɛmɔ shi,” taakɛ Biblia he nilelɔ ko kɛɛ lɛ. Kɛ́ Biblia mli nikaselɔ ko bale Yehowa lɛ, nakai nilee lɛ náa bɔ ni ehiɔ shi ehãa lɛ nɔ hewalɛ. Ni ehãa enáa “naanɔ wala” he gbɛkpamɔ hu.— Yohane 17:3 .

3 Yehowa ha wɔ he blɔ kaa wa maa ye bua nihi nɛ a ba le lɛ. Ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, wa ma susu sane bimihi nɛ nyɛɛ se nɛ ɔmɛ a he: Mɛni he je nɛ e he hia nɛ Baiblo kaseli nɛ a le Yehowa saminya a? Kɛ wa ma plɛ kɛ tsɔɔ mɛ bɔ nɛ Yehowa ngɛ ha kɛɛ? Ni te wɔɔfee tɛŋŋ wɔye wɔbua amɛ ni amɛkɛ amɛ

4 Yesu tsɔɔ kaa kita nɛ pe kulaa ji kaa waa suɔ Yehowa kɛ wa tsui tsuo, wa susuma tsuo, wa juɛmi tsuo, kɛ wa he wami tsuo. (Kane Marko 12:30 .) Ja Baiblo kaselɔ ko le Yehowa loko e ma nyɛ maa suɔ lɛ. Wa suɔ nɛ wa Baiblo kaselɔ nɛ jɔɔ e he nɔ—pi wɔ, pi tsɔɔmi ko, pi blɔ nya tomi ko—se mohu Yehowa. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, ke waa kɛ nɔ ko ngɛ Baiblo mi ní kasee ɔ, oti titli nɛ waa kɛ ma wa hɛ mi ji kaa wa maa ye bua wa ní kaselɔ ɔ nɛ e ba le Yehowa nɛ e suɔ lɛ.

5 Tsakpaa ko yɛ suɔmɔ kɛ toiboo teŋ. Ke o Baiblo kaselɔ ɔ ba le Yehowa nɛ e ba suɔ lɛ ɔ, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ e maa bu lɛ tue. Yesu de ke: “Bɔ nɛ pee nɛ je ɔ nɛ le kaa i suɔ Tsɛ ɔ, i ngɛ pee kaa bɔ nɛ Tsɛ ɔ fã mi ke ma pee ɔ.” ( Yoh. 14:31 ) Nakai nɔŋŋ hu suɔmɔ ni Biblia kaselɔ ko yɔɔ kɛha Yehowa—fe nɔ fɛɛ nɔ—baatsirɛ lɛ koni ebo lɛ toi ni ekɛ ehe akpɛtɛ ehe.— 1 Yoh.

6 Baiblo kaseli nɛ a ba le Yehowa saminya a ma nyɛ maa pee juɛmi nɛ da. Ákɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ: Jeee kɔɔyɔŋ lɛlɛ kudɔmɔ he mlai ni ekaseɔ loo kaa ni eeefee kɛkɛ lɛ kaseɔ kɔɔyɔŋ lɛlɛ kudɔmɔ. Esa akɛ ekɛ ŋmɛlɛtswai babaoo hu awo kɔɔyɔŋ lɛlɛ kudɔmɔ he lɛ mli. Yɛ be ni sa mli lɛ, ekaseɔ bɔ ni kɔɔyɔŋ lɛlɛ lɛ feɔ enii ehaa yɛ shihilɛi srɔtoi amli, kɛ bɔ ni eyiŋkpɛɛi lɛ saa kɔɔyɔŋ lɛlɛ lɛ nitsumɔ he ehaa. Jã kɛ̃ nɛ Baiblo kaselɔ ko hu ma nyɛ maa le Yehowa kɛ gu e ní kasemi ɔ pɛ nɔ, mohu ɔ, e maa na bɔ nɛ níhi yaa nɔ ha ke e mwɔ e yi mi kpɔ ngɛ Ngmami ɔ sisi tomi mlaahi a he ɔ hu nɔ. Be mli ni Yehowa he nilee ni eyɔɔ lɛ yaa hiɛ, ni enaa bɔ ni eŋwɛi Tsɛ lɛ yeɔ ebuaa lɛ lɛ, ebaaha ehe asa koni enyɛ ekpɛ yiŋ kpakpai yɛ shihilɛ fɛɛ shihilɛ mli.

7 Yehowa he nile nɛ Yosef le ɔ ye bua lɛ benɛ Potifar yo ɔ bɔ mɔde kaa e maa sisi lɛ ɔ. Ŋwanejee ko bɛ he akɛ, kɛjɛ Yosef gbekɛbiiashi tɔ̃ɔ lɛ, enu saji ni kɔɔ Adam kɛ Hawa kɛ Abraham kɛ Sara he lɛ ahe. (1 Mose 2:24; 20:3, 6) Yosef nyɛ nɛ e na bɔ nɛ Yehowa buɔ gba si himi kɛ nike ní nɛ ji bɔmi nami ha a kɛ je jamɛ a sane ɔmɛ a mi. Yosef ba le Yehowa saminya, nɛ e kpoo Potifar yo ɔ hɛ mi nyami ɔ, nɛ e de lɛ ke: “Te ma plɛ kɛ pee yayami ngua nɛ ɔ nɛ ma pee yayami kɛ si Mawu ha kɛɛ?” (1 Mose 39:7-9) Yosef le nɔ́ nɛ hi kɛ nɔ́ nɛ dɛ ngɛ Yehowa hɛ mi. Enu nɔ ni baaye e-Nyɔŋmɔ lɛ awui lɛ shishi. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, Yosef mwɔ yi mi kpɔ nɛ da. Ke wa ye bua wa Baiblo kaseli ɔmɛ nɛ a le Yehowa saminya, nɛ a le bɔ nɛ e su ha a, mɛ hu a maa pee juɛmi nɛ maa sa Yehowa hɛ mi.

Mi kɛ Miwako bote gbalashihilɛ mli yɛ afi 1992, ni wɔná hegbɛ akɛ wɔfee ekome kɛsɔmɔ akɛ gbalashihilɛ mli hefatalɔi yɛ Brooklyn Betel. Miwako ji mɔ ko ni susuɔ mɔ he ni esusuɔ mɔ he, ni efee klalo be fɛɛ be akɛ ebaaye ebua mɛi krokomɛi. Nɔkwɛmɔnɔ ni efee lɛ eye ebua mi ni mitsake bɔ ni mikɛ mɛi krokomɛi yeɔ mihaa lɛ mli. Eji nikeenii ni jɛ Yehowa ŋɔɔ—miishɛɛ kɛ miishɛjemɔ jɛɛhe ni bɛ naagbee kɛha mi.

JAPAN Nitsumɔhe Nine LƐ KƐ TSƆƆMƆ LƐ

Beni be shwie mli lɛ, Miwako fɔlɔi enyɔ lɛ fɛɛ ni yɔɔ Japan lɛ he baye waa. No hewɔ lɛ, amɛjɛ mlihilɛ mli amɛŋmɛ wɔ gbɛ koni wɔfã kɛya nitsumɔhe nine ni yɔɔ Japan lɛ koni wɔhi he ko ni bɛŋkɛ ni wɔkwɛ amɛ. Wɔfã kɛtee jɛmɛ yɛ afi 1999 mli.

Etsɛɛɛ ni Betel weku ni yɔɔ Japan lɛ kpɛlɛ wɔnɔ, ni wɔtsake wɔhe wɔwo wɔshia hee lɛ mli. Atsake mi kɛtee hiɛ kɛtsɔ sui kpakpai ni nyɛmimɛi hii kɛ yei ni jɛ Japan lɛ yɔɔ, tamɔ ejurɔfeemɔ kɛ gbɔfeemɔ ni sa kadimɔ waa lɛ ni mikase lɛ nɔ. Kɛ́ eba nitsumɔ he lɛ, amɛkɛ hiɛdɔɔ tsuɔ nii ni amɛkɛ amɛjwɛŋmɔ maa nɔ. Amɛtsuɔ nii jogbaŋŋ hu akɛ kuu. Mɛɛ gbɛ nɔ? Japanbii akusum nifeemɔi maa nɔ mi akɛ amɛkɛ mɛi krokomɛi baatsu nii yɛ ekomefeemɔ kɛ ekomefeemɔ mumɔ mli kɛha mɛi fɛɛ ahilɛ-kɛhamɔ moŋ fe ni mɔ kome pɛ baatsu nii kɛha mɔ diɛŋtsɛ nibii ni eshɛɔ he lɛ. Nɔkwɛmɔnɔ kpakpa ni amɛfee lɛ wo mi hewalɛ koni mikɛ mɛi krokomɛi atsu nii yɛ heshibaa mli, ni mina bɔ ni migbɛfaŋnɔ bibioo lɛ yeɔ ebuaa Yehowa yiŋtoo muu lɛ fɛɛ lɛ.

Shi kɛlɛ, akɛni esoro wɔ kusumii hewɔ lɛ, bei komɛi ba ni ewa kɛha mi akɛ manu nɔ hewɔ ni atsuɔ saji ahe nii yɛ gbɛ pɔtɛɛ ko nɔ lɛ shishi. Ehe bahia ni makase tsuishitoo ni mikafee nɔ ko fe nine. Yɛ be ni sa mli lɛ, mitsake misusumɔ lɛ mli, ni miyɔse akɛ Yehowa baanyɛ ajɔɔ yiŋkpɛɛ fɛɛ yiŋkpɛɛ nɔ kɛji wɔba wɔhe shi wɔha gbɛtsɔɔmɔi ni etsɔɔ egbɛjianɔtoo lɛ nɔ ekɛhaa wɔ lɛ. Yɛ naagbee lɛ, Yehowa ji mɔ ni haa nibii yaa nɔ jogbaŋŋ.

Yɛ wɔgbalashihilɛ gbi lɛ nɔ

Bei pii lɛ, mikɛ bɔ ni atsuu mihe yɛ Betel lɛ toɔ bɔ ni atsuu gbalɔ Mose he lɛ he. Yehowa ye bua Mose nɛ e ná he si bami ngɛ jeha 40 nɛ e kɛ sɔmɔ kaa to hyɛlɔ ɔ mi. Taakɛ Mose ji lɛ, mi hu mijɛ weku ko ni ekase nii waa mli. No mli lɛ, miŋmɛ gbɛ ni nakai nikasemɔ lɛ eha miwo mihe nɔ ni mikɛ mihe fɔ̃ɔ mi diɛŋtsɛ mihe nɔ. Betel ha nɛ i kase kaa ma ba wa he si nɛ ma ngɔ ye hɛ kɛ fɔ Yehowa nɔ. Kaa bɔ nɛ Yehowa to e tsui si kɛ tsu Mose he ɔ, jã nɔuu nɛ Yehowa pee ha mi jehahi babauu nɛ be ɔ.

Wɔkwɛ Miwako fɔlɔi enyɔ lɛ fɛɛ aaafee afii 24 kɛyashi amɛgboi. Atswa tɛlifoŋ babaoo, bei ni haa mɔ haoɔ, kɛ gbɛfaa kɛmiiya helatsamɔhe lɛ ba. Miwako nyɛ, Masako hewalɛnamɔ ba shi kwraa

he ni ewaaa akɛ eteɔ shi koni enyiɛ lɛ. E be gbɔjɔɔ ha Masako kaa e maa ya asafo mi kpehi kɛ fiɛɛmi. Yɛ enaagbee otsii lɛ amli lɛ, ehe bahia ni ekɛ helatsɛmɛi ashwiili atsu nii. Shi kɛlɛ, yɛ enɛɛmɛi fɛɛ mli lɛ, suɔmɔ ni eyɔɔ kɛha Yehowa lɛ naaa shi kɔkɔɔkɔ. Ehiɛ kpɛɔ kɛ miishɛɛ be fɛɛ be ni ebaawie anɔkwale lɛ he. Masako nɔkwɛmɔnɔ ni tsɔɔ bɔ ni esɔmɔɔ Nyɔŋmɔ ni enaa tsui ni nyɔɔ mɔ mli kɛ miishɛɛ bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni shihilɛ lɛ ji lɛ ji nɔ ni kanyaa mi waa.

Yɛ afi 2024 lɛ, wɔna tsakemɔ kpele ko yɛ wɔshihilɛ mli. Beni wɔkɛ nɔ ni fe afii 30 sɔmɔ yɛ Betel sɛɛ lɛ, aha wɔ nitsumɔ yɛ kpokpaa lɛ nɔ. Amrɔ nɛɛ wɔmiisɔmɔ akɛ gbɛgbalɔi krɛdɛi yɛ Okinawa ŋshɔkpɔ lɛ nɔ, ni wɔná hegbɛ wɔye wekui ni yɔɔ asraafoi anitsumɔhe ni bɛŋkɛ jɛmɛ lɛ odase. Tsɔsemɔ kpakpa ni wɔnine shɛ nɔ yɛ Betel lɛ ye ebua ni nɛkɛ tsakemɔ kɛmiiya shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ nɛɛ tee nɔ jogbaŋŋ kwraa. Wɔbɔɔ mɔdɛŋ koni wɔhiɛ be he gbɛjianɔtoo kpakpa mli ni wɔkɛ wɔbe atsu nii jogbaŋŋ kɛtsɔ wɔjwɛŋmɔ ni wɔkɛaama wɔsɔɔmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ nɔ daa ni wɔtsuɔ nii waa lɛ nɔ. Yehowa je mi mi jɔmi mi nɛ e jɔɔ wɔ nɛ e ha wɔ Baiblo kaseli nɛ a ba pee kaa wa weku li. Wɔmiikase bɔ ni ehe hiaa ni wɔsumɔ gbɔmɛi lɛ diɛŋtsɛ. Mi kɛ Miwako naa Yehowa shi daa gbi yɛ nitsumɔ fɛfɛo nɛɛ hewɔ.

EMƐ EKƐ YEHOWA BAATSU ENƐ PII PO

Miweku lɛ, eyɛ mli akɛ amɛteee shi amɛwooo mi dɔŋŋ moŋ, shi amɛfatako mihe yɛ jamɔ krɔŋŋ mli lolo. Ke e ba jã po ɔ, Yesu wo si kaa nihi nɛ a ma je a weku mi kɛ ba pee e kaseli ɔ ma ná e bɔ ɔ toi 100 amlɔ nɛ ɔ kaa mumi mi weku. ( Marko 10:29, 30 ) Enɛ eba mli diɛŋtsɛ yɛ migbɛfaŋ. Wekui fɛɛ ni yɔɔ je lɛŋ fɛɛ ni sumɔɔ mi ni amɛkwɛ mi yɛ afii ni eho lɛ amli akɛ amɛ diɛŋtsɛ amɛwekui lɛ ayi fa tsɔ akɛ mikaiii.

Saamɔ Nɔ ni Efite lɛ Oya!

ANI nyɛmi nuu loo nyɛmi yoo ko edɔ bo aahu akɛ amrɔ nɛɛ nɔ ko efite wekukpaa ni kã nyɛteŋ lɛ? Bɔni afee ni osaa nɔ ni efite lɛ ni oya nɔ ohi shi akɛ nanemɛi lɛ, ehe miihia ni okɛ jwɛŋmɔ kpakpa atsu he nii oya.*

Ákɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, esa akɛ atsa wu ni ekumɔ lɛ koni yɛ naagbee lɛ enyɛ etsu nii jogbaŋŋ ni piŋmɔ ko bɛ he. Shi piŋmɔ lɛ baaya nɔ kɛji akɛ akwɛɛɛ fã ni etse lɛ nɔ oya. Nɔ ni ehiii fe fɛɛ lɛ, wu lɛ baanyɛ afee nɔ ni ehiii kwraa, ni enyɛŋ etsu nii jogbaŋŋ.

Taakɛ wu ni eku lɛ ji lɛ, esa akɛ atsu wekukpaa ni efite ni kã mɔ ko kɛ enaanyo Kristofonyo teŋ lɛ he nii oya. Kɛ jeee nakai lɛ, piŋmɔ ni jɛɔ naanyobɔɔ ni efite mli baa lɛ baaya nɔ. Mlifu baanyɛ aya hiɛ, ni ekolɛ onaanyo lɛ baaje odɛŋ. Yesu de ke: “Nyɛɛ dla sane ɔ oya.” ( Mat. 5:25 ) No hewɔ lɛ, yaa onaanyo lɛ ŋɔɔ ni okɛ lɛ agba sane lɛ he sane yɛ mlihilɛ mli, shi nyɛjea mli faŋŋ. Mɛni hewɔ enɛ baanyɛ afee nɔ ni wa lɛ? Susumɔ yiŋtoi enyɔ komɛi ahewɔ lɛ he okwɛ.

Klɛŋklɛŋ lɛ, ekolɛ ebaaka bo akɛ obaaku ohiɛ oshwie shihilɛ lɛ nɔ ni otsuuu he nii, ekolɛ akɛni ohe ejɔɔɔ bo akɛ ooowie naagbai ahe lɛ hewɔ.# Susumɔ Euodia kɛ Sintika, nyɛmimɛi yei Kristofoi ni etamɔ nɔ ni amɛnyɛŋ amɛtsu béi ko he nii lɛ he okwɛ. Eeenyɛ efee akɛ amɛnaagba lɛ tee nɔ ehi shi, ejaakɛ bɔfo Paulo ni yɔɔ Roma shɔŋŋ lɛ po nu he. E wo mɛ he wami kaa a pee nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ nɛ he maa hia konɛ a kɛ kake peemi nɛ hi a kpɛti ekohu. ( Filip. 4:2 ) Ke a ba a he si, nɛ a sɛɛ ní saminya, nɛ a ngɔ a tɔmihi kɛ pa mɛ nitsɛnitsɛ ɔ, a huɛ bɔmi ɔ ma nyɛ maa hi ekohu.

Nɔ ni ji enyɔ lɛ, ekolɛ obaakpa gbɛ akɛ mɔ kroko lɛ baakpɛlɛ etɔmɔ lɛ nɔ. Shi oosumɔ ni oná onaanyo lɛ ekoŋŋ, shi jeee ni okɛ fɔyeli ama shi. Datrɛfonyo ni tsaa wu ni ekumɔ lɛ kɛ ejwɛŋmɔ baama etsamɔ nɔ, shi jeee nɔ ni kɛ wu ni ekumɔ lɛ ba lɛ mli fitsofitso fɛɛ mlitaomɔ nɔ. Kɛ́ okɛ onaanyo lɛ miigba sane lɛ, kaasusu mɔ ni esa akɛ ashwa yɛ nɔ ni efite lɛ he lɛ he. Mohu ɔ, mo kai bɔ nɛ huɛ bɔmi ɔ he jua wa ha mo nɛ o ngɔ o juɛmi kɛ ma nɔ́ nɛ e sa nɛ o pee kɛ dla huɛ bɔmi ɔ nɔ.

Mɛni hewɔ okɛ onyɛmi nuu loo onyɛmi yoo lɛ egbaaa sane ŋmɛnɛ, ni okɛ yiŋtoo akɛ obaasaa nɔ ni efite lɛ ni okɛ naanyobɔɔ lɛ aba ekoŋŋ? “Kaaha hulu lɛ miinyɔ shi be mli ni omli efu lolo lɛ.” ( Efe. 4:26 ) Ke o pee nɔ́ ko kɛ dla níhi nɛ puɛ ɔ oya a, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ o maa po wa ‘tue mi jɔmi kpãa nɛ peeɔ wɔ kake’ ɔ he piɛ.​— Efe. 4:1-3 he.

* Esa akɛ atsu mlifu bibii ahe nii yɛ teemɔŋ ni akɛ eshaifaa aha lɛ kɛjɛ tsuiŋ. ( Mat. 5:23, 24 ) Tɔmi komɛ nɛ hɛdɔ ngɛ he ɔ ma bi nɛ asafo mi nikɔtɔmahi nɛ a ye bua lɛ. ( Mat. 18:17 ) Se Kristofo no ko ma hla yemi kɛ buami nɛ ɔ ke e kɛ blɔ tsɔɔmihi nɛ a tsɔɔ ngɛ Mateo 18:15, 16 ɔ tsu ní.

NIKASEMƆ HE GBƆMƆ Saamɔ Hetooi ni Tswaa Mɔ Maa Shi

Ke wa ya asafo mi kpehi ɔ, wa náa he blɔ kɛ gu wa hetohi a nɔ kɛ woɔ wa nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi he wami. ( Heb. 10:24, 25 ) Kɛ wa ma plɛ kɛ dla sanehi a hetohi nɛ maa wo ni kpahi he wami nitsɛnitsɛ ha kɛɛ?

• Moo sɔle kɛ ha Yehowa. Mo bi lɛ konɛ e ye bua mo konɛ o ha sanehi nɛ maa wo e hɛ mi nyami, nɛ e wo ni kpahi he wami.​— La 12:10 . 35:18 lɛ he.

• Okɛ oti awo mli akɛ ooofee babaoo fe wolo lɛ mli ni oookane kɛkɛ. Kɛ́ okɛ bo diɛŋtsɛ owiemɔi ha hetoo lɛ, mɛi krokomɛi baaná he sɛɛ be mli ni amɛnuɔ ohemɔkɛyeli kpojiemɔ lɛ.

• Ha ohetoo lɛ afee kuku. Bei pii lɛ, saji ahetoi kukuji ni yɔɔ mlɛo ji nɔ ni woɔ mɔ hewalɛ fe fɛɛ, ni ehaa mɛi babaoo kɛ amɛhe woɔ kpee lɛ mli.​— Abɛi. 10:19 lɛ he.

• Ha ohetoo lɛ afee nɔ ni hi. Tsi ohe kɛjɛ ohetoo lɛ ni okɛaawo asafo lɛ loo onyɛmi Kristofonyo ko ŋaa loo owie eshi lɛ lɛ he.

Kɛ́ atsɛɛɛ bo koni oha hetoo po lɛ, obaanyɛ otswa mɛi krokomɛi oma shi. Moo bu mɛ tue saminya, nɛ o je hɛsa kpo ngɛ a heto ɔmɛ a he.​— Abɛ 10:10 . 10:21 lɛ he.
