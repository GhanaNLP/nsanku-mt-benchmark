MPITRIPI NE MPATRAPA

MPITRIPI ne Mpatrapa ne Anyankama-Kyerebi ne Kyerebi-Koso-Adawurampon kɔtuu mmɛw-nsã nnua aduasa, na wɔkaa sɛ, "Mommma obiara nka mmɛw-nsã no nsuo na ɔnteɛteɛ (ne nsateaa)."

Afei Paapa Akɔre kɔkaa nnua-nsã no nsuo no na ɔtetee (ne nsateaa), na nkurɔfoɔ (lit. wɔn) kaa sɛ, "Fa ɛka no." Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛka bɛn na mabɔ?" Wobuae se, "Mode ka efiri se, Mpitripi ne Mpatrapa ne Anyankama-Kyerebi ne Kyerebi-Koso-Adawurampon akɔ na wɔatutu mmɛ-nsã nnua aduasa, na wɔkaa sɛ mma obiara nka mmɛ-nsã no nsuo na ɛntete (ne nsateaa), na wode wo nsa aka mmɛ-nsã no nwene na woateɛteɛ (wo nsateaa), ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, 'fa ɛka no'" (i.e. ɛsɛ sɛ wutua asotwe no); na wɔde Duned Spider no sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wobenya (akatua no), enti ohuruw bataa ɔdan no atifi, (a dede te sɛ) Kado!

Ɛno nti na wɔka sɛ daa no, obi bɛhunu sɛ wɔde plaster abɔ Ananse wɔ ɔdan no atifi, ɛwom sɛ bere tenten a atwam no na ɔnam fam te sɛ Okoto, Krak.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
