NKYERƐKYERƐMU

Akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ afe yi bɛkɔ fam sɛ afe a ɛyɛ den sen biara wɔ yɛn man yi sikasɛm abakɔsɛm mu no mu biako. Te sɛ wiase nyinaa no, COVID-19 ɔyaredɔm no bɔɔ yɛn wɔ afe 2020 bosome mmiɛnsa a ɛdi kan no mu, na ɛde sikasɛm mu dwumadiɛ brɛɛ aseɛ, na ɛmaa yɛn sika a yɛnya no so tew ne ɛka foforɔ a yɛanhyehyɛ a ɛfa ɔyareɛ no a yɛbɛko atia ho.

Bere a yɛsusuu sɛ, na yɛrenya nkɔsoɔ kɛseɛ wɔ yɛn ahotɔ mu afiri ɔyaredɔm no nsunsuansoɔ a ɛsɛeeɛ no mu denam yɛn nsakraeɛ nhyehyɛeɛ no a wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ de dii dwuma no so no, nsɛm ahodoɔ a ɛsisiiɛ a wɔ berɛ no mu, asɛe sikasɛm mu nkonimdie akɛseɛ a yɛyɛɛ no chalked firi 2017 kɔsi 2019. Nea ɛdi kan no na ɛyɛ Russia-Ukraine ɔko no, afei ɛkyɛe wɔ key no twa mu sika a wonyae wɔ afe 2022 sikasɛm nhyehyɛe mu, a ɛmaa wɔtew boseabɔ ho nhyehyɛe so. Mpofirim ara, nea edi kan wɔ mfe du mu no, wɔtoo Amanaman Ntam Kapital Guadi no mu maa yɛn, ɔman no mu sikasɛm tebea mu yɛɛ den na boseabɔ ho ka kɔɔ soro, na ɛboaa ma wɔbɔɔ bosea kɛse na cedi bo kɔɔ fam ntɛmntɛm.

Enti, bere mpo a amannɔne sika mmoa a efi kapital gua so ne yɛn amannɔne sika a yenya no so tew no, yɛn nneɛma a yɛde fi amannɔne ba ho ka a ɛkɔɔ soro no kɔɔ soro fi yɛn ahwehwɛde a ɛda adi sɛ yentumi nnyae wɔ nneɛma a wɔde fi amannɔne ba ho sɛ ɔman, a yɛn bosea a yɛde tua ho ka a ɛkɔɔ soro no ka ho no maa yɛn sika bo a ɛkɔ soro no sɛee yɛn sika no bo bio. Eyi ama yɛn nyinaa asetra ho ka ayɛ den, sɛ Aban ne ankorankoro nyinaa. Petrol boɔ a ɛrekɔ soro no ama nneɛma ne nnwuma dodoɔ no ara boɔ akɔ soro, na nneɛma boɔ akɔ soro 40.4% wɔ Ɔkɔtɔberɛ 2022 mu.

Wɔasɔ yɛn ahwɛ ankasa sɛ nkurɔfo, na Aban wɔ tema kɛse ma mo wɔ nhyɛso a ɛnsɛ a eyi de aba no ho. Yɛpɛ sɛ yɛda yɛn aseda sɛ Aban ma yɛn ayɔnkofoɔ ahodoɔ, a Mmarahyɛ Bagua, Adwumawura Fekuo, Adwumayɛfoɔ Kuo, Ɔmanfoɔ Kuo, Gyidie Ahyehyɛdeɛ, Association of Ghana Industries, Ghana Union of

NNIANIMU

1. Afe 2023 Ɔmanfoɔ Budget no yɛ deɛ Aban no atumi ayɛ wɔ afe 2022 mu ne dodoɔ a abɔ ne tirim sɛ ɔbɛboaboa ne sika a ɔnya no ano na ɔde adi dwuma de adi nhyehyɛeɛ ne nhyehyɛeɛ ho dwuma. Afe 2023 sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ no asɛmti ne "Sɛbɛsan de Sikasɛm Kɛseɛ a Ɛgyina pintinn na wɔatumi agyina ano ama Nkɔsoɔ a ɛka obiara & Botaeɛ a wɔde bɛka ho".

2. Mfeɛ pii mu no, sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ foforɔ biara kura nnwuma foforɔ pii a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔhyɛ aseɛ a wɔbɛbɔ ka wɔ nnwuma a ɛrekɔ so no ho. Ɛnyɛ sɛ eyi de nhyɛso ba yɛn sikasɛm so nko na mmom abakɔsɛm mu no ɛde akatua a ɛma nnwuma ho ka nyinaa kɔ soro bere a sikatua a wɔatu ahyɛ da no twetwe mfɛntom ho ka nso.

3. Wɔ afe 2023 mu no, Aban de nhyehyɛɛ ne nhyehyɛeɛ foforɔ bedi dwuma, nanso ɛbɛhyɛ amammerɛ a ɛne sɛ wɔbɛwie nnwuma a ɛwɔ hɔ dada nyinaa nso ho nkuran.
