SƐNEA KWATIMA, RAM NO, BAABEA ODWANINI EDIN

NA (bere bi) Ram bi wɔ hɔ, Kwatima, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Merekɔhwehwɛ asase bi a mɛsi dan wɔ so." Na Asono nso, bere koro no ara mu no, na ɔwɔ adwene koro no ara pɛpɛɛpɛ. Afei Kwatima, Ɔkraman no sii kwan so sɛ ɔrekɔhwɛ asase a ɔpɛ no; na ɔtetew mu, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obesi fie. Na Osebo, Asono no nso kɔeɛ (na berɛ a ɔduruu baabi a Ram no aprapra mu no), ɔkaa sɛ, "Adom me! Akuraa a na merebɛsi, nanso na menyɛɛ adwuma biara wɔ so de besi nnɛ no, me honhom nananom ahyɛ aseɛ dedaw wɔ so." Na ɔno nso, yɛɛ clearing kakra na ɔkɔɔ. Ade kyee no, bere a nneɛma fii ase puei (i.e. anɔpatutuutu) no, Ɔkraman no san kɔɔ hɔ bio. Ɔduruu beaeɛ hɔ no, ɔkaa sɛ, “Adom me!” Nokwarem no me honhom nananom rebɛboa me, (hwɛ) asase a wɔaprapra so no." Na ɔno nso, ɔprapraa (nwura no) kɔe. Na Asono no nso, bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh! eyi yɛ asɛm bi; Ɛkame ayɛ sɛ mesuro wɔ ho." Na ɔno nso, ɔkɔɔ kwae no mu; otwitwaa nnua na ɔde too fam wɔ hɔ (wɔ beae no). Na Ɔkraman no nso bae, na ɔno nso hwɛɛ bere tenten a ne ho adwiriw no. Ɔsan nso kɔɔ wuram, sɛ ɔrekɔtwa ahama a ɛwea, na ɔtow wɔn guu fam hɔ kɔe. Na bio Asono no bae. Ɔno nso, ɔhwɛɛ kyɛe (nkɔso a wɔanya), na ɔde pegged out (ofie no) na off ɔkɔɔ. Kwatima bae bio, na ɔtutuu ntokuru de nnua no guu mu, na ɔkɔɔ. Na bio Asono no baeɛ, na ɔkyekyeree asɛnnua nnua no, na ɔkɔɔ. Na bio Kwatima bae, na ɔkɔɔ na ɔboaboaa sare no ano de guu hɔ na ɔkɔɔ. Na Asono no san bae, na ɔfaa atɛkyɛ no na ɔyɛɛ plastering no; na ɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔe. Na bio Kwatima baa hɔ bɛyɛɛ ɔdan no atifi, na ɔkɔɔ. Na bio Asono no baeɛ, na ɔbɔɔ fam, na ɔfirii. Afei Kwatima nso bae, na ɔtetew dɔte fitaa na ɔde plaster guu (afasu no so), na ɔkɔɔ fie. Bere a ɔkɔe no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ne ba no sɛ, "Ɛnnɛ mawie fie no, na sɛ Kwasida Adae no tɔ a, Memeneda (a edi hɔ) no yɛbɛma ofie foforo no ayɛ hyew, enti hwehwɛ nnipa a wokurakura atuo a wɔbɛkɔ akɔtow atuo akɔ dan no mu." Afei Asono nso baa (na ɔhunuu ofie a wɔawie no). Ɔkaa sɛ, “Eh! Minni biribiara a mɛka, na mmom sɛ mɛkɔ akɔboaboa me nneɛma ano na masan aba kɛkɛ." Afei ɔkɔɔ, na ɔkɔka kyerɛɛ ne ba no, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Tu mmirika ntɛmntɛm sɛnea wubetumi na ka kyerɛ Honhom-bɔmmɔfo no sɛ sɛ Adae no hwe ase Kwasida bi a, (a edi hɔ) Memeneda no mɛdi ofie foforo a ɛreyɛ hyew no ho afahyɛ, enti ma no mmra mmɛtow tuo mma me." Na ne ba no kɔɔ hɔ kɔka kyerɛɛ no. Ɔno (Honhom-bɔmmɔfoɔ) kaa sɛ, "Mate." Afei da a edi Adae no anim no, Kwatima boaboaa ne nneɛma ano, na ɔfaa de guu fie hɔ wɔ hɔ, na ɔsan bae. Na Asono nso, boaboaa ne de ano, na ɔfaa wɔn (hɔ). Bere a ɛdaa adi pefee sɛ wobehu nneɛma no, Kwasida Adae no, Kwatima kɔɔ baabiara kɔkaa nnipa a ɔne wɔn te no kwan; na Asono nso, ne no gyaee. Memeneda, bere a nneɛma puei (i.e. anɔpatutuutu) no, Kwatima ne ne ba no kɔɔ (kɔ fie foforo no mu); afei ɔkɔtoo tuo guu dan no mu. Na Asono nso baeɛ; ɔtee tuo no; ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛnnɛ nyɛ da pa, ɛnnɛ ahonhom no reto atuo." Na ɔno nso, ɔbaa; na Honhom-bɔmmɔfo no nso kɔɔ fie a ɛwɔ hɔ no mu, na ɔmaa tuo no teɛɛm sɛ, pomm! Asono no baa hɔ bɛhunuu Kwatima ne ne ba sɛ wɔte hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn ne w'adwuma?" Kwatima nso kaa sɛ, "Wo nso, w'asɛm ne dɛn?" Asono no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ wunni biribiara (a wobɛba ha) a, me nso, minni bi." Na Kwatima kaa sɛ, "Sɛ ɛte saa a, ɛnde yɛn baanu nyinaa bɛtena ha." Enti wɔtenaa hɔ. Da biara, sɛ Asono sɔre a, na ɔkɔ abɔmmɔ, na ɔde nam san ba. Bere nyinaa na ɛte saa. Nanso Kwatima deɛ, wamfa biribiara amma. Afei da koro bi a osuro no kɔɔ wuram; ɔyɛɛ nea obetumi biara sɛ ɔbɛkyere biribi, nanso wantumi. Ɔsan kɔɔ fie, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Kwatima, sɛ ɛnnɛ woankɔ kwae mu na woamfa nam bi amma a, nea mɛyɛ wo no, w'ani rennye ho." Kwatima sii kwan so, na ɔkɔɔ wura no ho, na ɔde bush-buck bi san bae na ɔtow guu fam hɔ. Bere a Asono huu aboa no, ɛmaa ne ho dwiriw no te sɛ biribiara. Bere a nneɛma puei (i.e. anɔpatutuutu) no, Kwatima kɔe, na ɔsan de foforo (antelope) bae. Asono no kaa sɛ, "E!" He said to his child, "Look here, I am going to the bush, and I am taking Kwatima along with me as well, and the young duyker that I brought you the other day, when we have gone, call Kwatima's child and ask him, saying, 'When your father goes to the bush to catch meat, demonstrate for us on this duyker how he does it.'" And the people (Osebo and Kwatima) sii kwan so. Na Asono ba no kaa sɛ, "Kwatima ba, bra ma yɛndi agorɔ." Ɔbaa; ɔkaa sɛ, "Sɛ agya kɔ kwaeɛ mu a, sei na ɔfa so kyere mmoa." Na ohuruw, na ɔkyeree duyker kumaa no na ɔfaa ne menewa. Kwatima ba no kaa sɛ, "Wokɔ na wokɔfa ɔboɔ kɛseɛ bi." Asono ba no kɔeɛ na ɔde baako baeɛ. Ɔde sii fam hɔ. Kwatima ba no san n’akyi fii ho na ɔbaa na afei ɔde ne ti atifi bɔɔ ɔbo no, pim! na ɛbubuu asinasin. Asono ba no kaa sɛ, "Ah." And their fathers came in from the bush, and the Leopard's child told his father what had happened, and Kwatima's child, too, told his father what had happened. Afei na wɔwɔ hɔ, bere a da bi biribi bataa Kwatima nan ho, na ɔrebɛbɔ no brush, nanso Asono susuwii sɛ ɔresan n’akyi sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obekum no. Ntɛm ara na Asono no de ahopere baa Kwatima so sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔbɛkyere no. Na Kwatima tuu mmirika firii kiri, kiri, kiri. Ɔkɔɔ ɔtare bi a ɛda hɔ mu, a na Python bi wɔ mu. Kwatima kaa sɛ, "Agya Python, meguan kɔ wo nkyɛn kɔhwehwɛ ahobammɔ sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a Asono no renkyere me." Ɛhɔ na afei Python no kaa sɛ, "Wo, yɔnko, woayɛ no yie (sɛ wobɛba me nkyɛn), enti fa me nsa." Kwatima bɔɔ mmɔden paa na ɔde ne nsa bɔɔ no. Saa na Kwatima nyaa din Odwa-nini (lit. Fa Python kyekyere nsa). Efii ase wɔ ne nsa a ɔne Python rekyiakyia no no mu.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebia wobɛfa ebinom sɛ ɛyɛ nokware, na nea aka no betumi akamfo me (sɛ maka ho asɛm).
