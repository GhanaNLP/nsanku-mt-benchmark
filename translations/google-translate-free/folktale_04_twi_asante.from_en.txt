SƐNEA ƐBAE SƐNEA YƐNNYA ABOFRA WƆ FIE BIARA MU

WƆKA sɛ ɔbɔmmɔfo bi ne ne ba kɔsii atrae ketewaa bi, na wɔtenaa mu. Na ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ akum mmoa, wakum, wakum, wakum. Na aboa biara ka wɔn ho. Afei, sɛ ɔkumm saa mmoa yi a na ɔdi nam no, na ɔtwitwa wɔn ti, na ɔde gu hɔ; na mmoa ti ahyɛ dan biako mu ma, na na ayɛ ma te sɛ eyi. Afei ɛtɔ da bi a wogyaw nsɛmfua wɔ akyi (i.e. biribi wɔ hɔ a me werɛ afi sɛ mɛka); saa ɔbɔmmɔfo no, sɛ ɔkɔ kwae mu a, na ogyaw ne ba wɔ fie bere nyinaa. Da bi, berɛ a abofra no te hɔ no, ɔhunuu aboa Fitaa (a ɔwɔ nsensaneeɛ) no; na ɛyɛɛ mpofirim. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Me banana, anwummerɛ pa.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ɛhe na w’agya wɔ.” Ɔno (abofra no) kaa sɛ, “Agya akɔ wuram.” Asono no, berɛ a ɔrehwɛ ne ho no, ɔhunuu mmoa no ti, na wɔdɔɔso. Ɔkaa sɛ, “Ah!, worehyia ɔhaw.” Ɔkaa sɛ, “Momfa mmoa no nyinaa ti mmra mma menhwɛ.” Na nea osuro no gyinaa hɔ na ɔbɔɔ ne dua (a ɛyɛɛ nnyigyei a ɛyɛ swishing te sɛ) f’re! f’re! f’re! Na abofra no faa mmoa no ti de sisii hɔ wɔ ntonto tenten tenten bi a ɛkɔ (sɛ yɛbɛka no sɛ) fi ha kɔ akyirikyiri. Na Asono no kaa sɛ, “Bobɔ wɔn din ma me na mate.” Na ɔgyinaa abofra no kɔn ho, na ɔkaa sɛ, “Momfrɛ wɔn din;oyi, aboa bɛn na ɔyɛ?”

Ɔno (abofra no) kaa sɛ:

“Oyi ne ntontom ketewa no, Adowa, Adowa, Adowa, Kwadwo Berefi Adowa, Agya kumm saa Adowa yi, sɛ tuo no to a, meyi Paapa, damirifa!” (Awerɛhosɛm ne sɛ).

“Aboa bɛn na eyi yɛ?”

“Eyi ne Otwe, Duyker, Duyker, Duyker (a n’abodin ne), agile-one-sɛ-n’ani nnye-mfomso-da-ɔbɔmmɔfo.Papa na okum Duyker yi, sɛ tuo no to a, mekamfo Paapa, damirifa!”

“Aboa bɛn na eyi yɛ?”

“Oyi ne duyker tuntum no, Oyuo, Oyuo, Oyuo, Oyuo Kwabi, Paapa na okum Oyuo yi, sɛ tuo no to a, mekamfo Paapa, damirifa!”

“Aboa bɛn na eyi yɛ?”

“Oyi ne duyker kɔkɔɔ no, Abedie, Abedie, Abedie, Paapa na okum Abedie yi, sɛ tuo no to a, mekamfo Paapa, damirifa!”

“Aboa bɛn na eyi yɛ?”

“Eyi ne Otromo, Bongo, Bongo, Bongo, Sakwa Bongo, Agya kum Bongo yi, sɛ tuo no to a, me hai Paa, damirifa!”

“Aboa bɛn na eyi yɛ?”

“ Eyi ne Wansane, Bush-buck, Bush-buck, Bush-buck, Mmomire Bush-buck, Agya kum Bush-buck yi, sɛ tuo no to a, me hail Pather, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn na eyi yɛ? ”

“ Eyi ne Kotoko, Ɔkraman, Ɔkraman, Ɔkraman, Gyanbibi, Ɔkraman, Agya kum Ɔkraman yi, sɛ tuo no to a, meyi Paapa, damirifa!”

“ Aboa bɛn na eyi yɛ? ”

“ Oyi ne Ekuo, Buffalo, Buffalo, Asempi, Buffalo, Agya kumm Buffalo yi, sɛ tuo no to a, me hail Pather, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn na eyi yɛ? ”

“ Oyi ne Oforotie, Cob, Cob, Cob, Pantan, Cob, Agya kumm Cob yi, sɛ tuo no to a, me hail Pather, damirifa!”

“ Aboa bɛn na eyi yɛ? ”

“ Oyi ne Osonson, Hartbeest, Hartbeest, Hartbeest, Ofuntum Kwoa, Hartbeest, Agya na okum Hartbeest yi, sɛ tuo no to a, me hail Agya, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn na eyi yɛ? ”

“ Oyi ne Oko, Roan, Roan, Roan, Agya na okum saa Roan yi, sɛ tuo no to a, me hail Agya, damirifa!”

“ Aboa bɛn na eyi yɛ? ”

“ Eyi ne Aboa Kesie, Aboa Kɛse, Aboa Kɛse, Aboa Kɛse, ’Sai, Hene a ɔte sare-tenten-man, Agya kum Aboa Kɛse yi, sɛ tuo no to a, mekamfo Agya, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn na eyi yɛ? ”

“ Eyi ne Esono, Asono, Asono, Asono, Nantwinini Kɛse a ɔno nkutoo nantew, nea ɔbubu agyan, Agya kum Asono yi, sɛ tuo no to a, mekamfo Agya, damirifa! ”

“ Aboa bɛn na eyi yɛ? ”

“ Oyi ne Susono, Hippo, Hippo, Hippo, Agya kumm Hippo yi, sɛ tuo no to a, me hail Pather, damirifa!”

“ Aboa bɛn na eyi yɛ? ”

“ Oyi ne Ebie, Kwae-Kwae-Krako, Ɔbran-kwae-Ɔkraman, Ɔbran-kwae-Ɔkraman, Agya kum saa Ɔbran-kwae-Ɔkraman yi, sɛ tuo no to a, mekamfo Agya, damirifa!”

“ Aboa bɛn na eyi yɛ? ”

“ Eyi ne Kokotee, Bush mprako, Bush mprako, Bush mprako, Asamoa, Bush mprako, Agya kum Bush mprako yi, sɛ tuo no to a, mekamfo Paapa, damirifa! ” "Aboa bɛn na eyi yɛ?"

"Oyi ne Ofosuo, Nsuo-buck, Nsu-buck, Water-buck, Agya kum Nsu-buck yi, sɛ tuo no to a, me hail Agya, _damirifa!_"

"Aboa bɛn na eyi yɛ?"

"Oyi ne Kwaduo, Yellow-backed-duyker, Yellow-backed-duyker, Yellow-backed duyker, Boapampa, Agya kumm Yellow-backed-duyker yi, sɛ tuo no bɔ a, me hail Agya, _damirifa!_"

"Aboa bɛn na eyi yɛ?"

"Oyi ne Apese, Brush-tailed-porcupine, Brush-tailed-porcupine, Brush-tailed-porcupine, Agya kumm Brush-tailed-porcupine yi, sɛ tuo no kɔ a, meyi Paapa, _damirifa!_"

"Aboa bɛn na eyi yɛ?"

"Oyi ne Ebuo, Colobus Bonsu, Colobus Bonsu, Colobus Bonsu, Ya, Colobus Bonsu, Agya kum Colobus Bonsu yi, sɛ tuo no to a, me kamfo Paapa, _damirifa!_"

Na afei ɔduruu baabi a White-(spotted)-biako no ti da, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Na oyi nso, eh?" Ɔno (abofra no) kaa sɛ:

"Ma Agya mmra anim (meto din), efisɛ eyi yɛ hu."

Asono no kaa sɛ, "Mate; merekɔ; ɔkyena mɛsan aba, na sɛ w'Agya ba fie a, bisa no ne din." Agya no firii kwaeɛ no mu san baeɛ, na ɔno (abofra no) kaa sɛ: "Ɛyɛ awerɛhoɔ a mahunu." Agya no kaa sɛ: “Ɛdeɛn na ɛwɔ hɔ?” Ɔno (abofra no) kaa sɛ: "Wogyaw me wɔ ha sɛ menkɔ wura mu, na Asono no bae, na ɔmaa mede mmoa no nyinaa ti too hama ne n." ame wɔn mmiako mmiako, kɔsii sɛ (yɛ)duruu n’abusuafoɔ deɛ no ho, na ɔkaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ meka ne din, nanso mekaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ ɔma wo di kan ba, sɛdeɛ na yei yɛ adeɛ a ɛyɛ hu." Agya no kaa sɛ: "Oh, ɛno nyɛ hwee, yɛbɛhunu deɛ ɛbɛsi bio ɔkyena." Na nokwarem no, bio berɛ a nneɛma hyɛɛ aseɛ da adi (i. e. anɔpatutuutu) Agya no kɔɔ kwaeɛ no mu. Asono no san bae bio na ɔmaa no (abofra no) de ti no too abɔnten hɔ wɔ nhama mu prɛko bio, na ɔmaa ɔtoo mmoa no ti din mmiako mmiako. Bere a bio, ne mprenu so no, oduu Asono no ti no, ɔkae sɛ:

"Ma Agya mmra anim (meto din), efisɛ eyi yɛ hu."

Asono no kaa sɛ: "Ɛyɛ yie, nanso ɔkyena sɛ mɛba na sɛ woanka ankyerɛ me a, mɛtwa wo ti nso." Abofra no kaa sɛ: "Mate!" Anwummere no yɛɛ nwini, na Agya no san kɔɔ fie na (abofra no) kae sɛ: "Ɛdefa ɛnnɛ ho —ɔkyena sɛ woanhyɛ wo ho den na woahu nea wobɛyɛ wɔ ho a, ɛnde makɔ na megyaw wo wɔ ha wɔ saa atrae yi mu." Agya no kaa sɛ: “Nea ɛwɔ.” asɛm no bio?" Abofra no kaa sɛ: "Asono no bae ne mprenu so, na ɔka sɛ sɛ ɔkyena mankasa a, nea ɔbɛyɛ ama me no bɛyɛ nea ɛboro me yafunu (lit. didi)." Agya no kae sɛ: "Ɛyɛ, mate." Afei ɔkyinkyin kɔhwehwɛɛ nhaban ne nnuru ntini, a ɔde beguare ne tuo. Ɔde nneɛma guu mu, na ɔde sii hɔ. Sɛnea nneɛma sii no ara pɛ a wotumi hu no, ɔkɔɔ hɔ kohintaw baabi. Ɛno akyi bere tiaa bi no, ohui sɛ Asono no de ahopere reba.

Ɔsono no duu fie hɔ ara pɛ na ɔne ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no ba no kasae sɛ, “Ɛnnɛ deɛ, deɛ ɛbɛsi no bɛsi.” Abofra no kae sɛ: “Ah, ɛno ne sɛ wunnim (biribiara ankasa).” Asono no kaa sɛ: “Ɛdeɛn na woka, mante?” Abofra no kae sɛ: “Woka kyerɛɛ me sɛ memfa ntonto (ti ahorow no), na dɛn na metumi aka (akyerɛ saa)?” Asono no kaa sɛ: “Monkɔ so (yɛ adwuma no).” Abofra no nso k mmoa no ti na ɔtrɛw mu wɔ hama so, na ɔkaa (to nkyɛn kyerɛ n’Agya sɛ), “Ɛhe na mede (oyi) bɛto?” Asono no kaa sɛ: “Dɛn na woka?” Abofra no kae sɛ: “Ná merekae aboa yi din ara kwa.” Asono no kɔgyinaa abofra no kɔn ho na ɔkaa sɛ, “Kɔ so, na oyi nso, aboa bɛn na ɔyɛ?”

“Eyi ne Otwe, Bush-buck, Bush-buck, Bush-buck, Agya kum Bush-buck yi, sɛ tuo no to a, me hail Pather, damirifa!”

Na ɔtoo mmoa no ti nyinaa din saa ara, na ɔduruu Asono de no ho. Ɔno (Asono) kaa sɛ: “Na oyi, eh?” Ɔno (abofra no) kaa sɛ: “Oyi ne Etwie, Ɔsebɔ, Ɔsebɔ, Ɔsebɔ, Agya Ɔsebɔ, Agya kum Ɔsebɔ yi, sɛ tuo no to a, meyi Paapa, damirifa!”

Asono no kaa sɛ: “Oo, nanso woahu amanehunu, m’aso mu no, obiako ate, ɔfoforo no da so ara wɔ hɔ.” Na ɔsan kɔɔ bisaa no bio, na abofra no san kaa no bio, na ɔno (Asono no) kae sɛ: “M’aso abien ate afei, me calabash ketewa a me fetish a mede ato m’anim no wom nkutoo na aka.” Afei Agya no bɔɔ abɛn, na ɔfrɛɛ ne ba no ka kyerɛɛ no ​​se, “Twe wo ho f aside,” na abofra no huruw gyinaa nkyɛn Na Asono no kaa sɛ: “Ɛhe na worekɔ?” Na abofra no kae sɛ: “Ná meretu akɔ akyirikyiri kakra.” Asono no kaa sɛ: “Me banana, wowɔ nteaseɛ.” Na ɔno (Asono) kaa sɛ: “Na eyi nso, ne din de dɛn?” Ɔno (abofra no) kaa sɛ:

“Oyi ne Etwie, Ɔsebɔ, Ɔsebɔ, Ɔsebɔ, Agya Ɔsebɔ, Agya kum Ɔsebɔ yi, sɛ tuo no to a, meyi Paapa, damirifa!”

N’ano gyinaa hɔ, na na Ɔsebɔ no rehuruhuruw abɛkyere no, na abofra no Agya maa tuo no teɛɛm sɛ, Tomm! Na Asono no da hɔ, na n’ahweaseɛ nnyigyei ne sɛ, Tim! Na Agya ba bɛtwaa Asono nam na ɔtow twaa ne ho hyiae.

Afei ɛno nti na mpanyimfo no ka sɛ ɛnsɛ sɛ yegyaw abofra nkutoo wɔ fie hɔ, efisɛ ɔbɔmmɔfo no yɛɛ saa, na ɛkaa kakraa bi na ɔmaa Asono no kyeree ne ba no.

Eyi, m’asɛm, a maka, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebi nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
