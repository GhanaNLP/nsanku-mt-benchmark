Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBƐYƐ WƆ ƆKRAMMAN KRAMAN NO, BETUMI AYƐ NE AKORƆMƆ (AKWAN) NO AYARESA AYA

NA ɔyɛ Kraman pɛn, na akorɔmɔ suban nti, ɔrentumi nnya ɔbaa (ɔfa ne akuraa no ara pɛ sɛ ɔware no. Enti, ɔtoo ne bo sɛ kuro bi bɛkɔ akɔtena ne nkyɛn na wanya ɔbaa a ɔne no bɛkɔ. (Ɔ ware, na ɔsan ne no san baa nakuraa hɔ. Ɔne ne yere no tenaa hɔ (berɛ a ɔbɛdii berɛ bi, berɛ a ofii adi no, na ɔsan kɔɔ nakuraa no so. Wɔ berɛ bi akyi, ne kunu de nkra baeɛ sɛ ɔreba hɔ. Ne agya-in-mmara no de ne tuo kɔɔ nwura no mu kɔkum adwowtwa bi a ɛwɔ ahoboa mu maa Kraman no baeɛ. Ɛbɛyɛ nnansa yi, Kraman no kɔɔ hɔ, na bere a ɔkɔɔ no, ɔhunuu sɛ mmaa honam no da ogya no so, adiwo no mu. Berɛ a anadwo no baeɛ no, na ɔne ne yere no kɔ ahomegyeɛ. Na Kraman no refiri adi ayɛ nsuo wɔ abɔnten, bere a ohuu nam a ɛbɛhyerɛn ogya no mu. Kraman no san bae na wɔtoo afiase, na ɔde ne retire no too tumiɛ (al) so na ɔhwɛɛ nam no tenten. Mprempren nam no hwam no duruu no, ɔbɔɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛkora so, nanso wantumi. Ofii soro kɔɔ soro koduu baabi a nam no wɔ; ɔtɔn ne nyinaa na ɔsan bae na ɔda. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛda adi pefee no, bere a ne yere no rekye no, ɔhwehwɛɛ nam no, na ɛnyɛ sin biako mpo na ɛda hɔ. Ne yere no kae sɛ, "Oh, ɛna! oh, ɛna!! Ebinom anom nam no nyinaa. " Bere a Kraman tee eyi no, naniwa wui. Ná nani awu, na ɔde ne ntama kɔkaa ne etire no ho, na ɔdaa hɔ komm. Afei ɔsɔre fii adi wɔ kwan no so pɛ, guan kɔɔ nankasa akuraa, na ɔkaa amaneɛbɔ no, sɛnea ɔkɔe na ɔwe ne papa-in mmara nam, ne sɛnea naniwa awu aniwu, ne sɛnea ɛte saa, na ɛno nti, na ɔreyɛ nhwehwɛmu wɔ obi a ɔwɔ aawuduru ano aduro a ɔde bɛyɛ akorɔmɔ no bi, ebia wɔde bi ama no. Nnipa no kae sɛ, "Adanko, Hare, wɔ tumi paa (medicine)". Kraman no de sii hɔ, na ahoɔhare a ɔduruu Hare akuraa no ase no yareɛ bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Avery Hare, I run to you for mmoa; me yɛ ɔmmɔfoɔ, ɔmmɔmmɔfoɔ, ɔmmɔmmɔfoɔ, na wɔka sɛ wo wɔ aduro (a ɛneɛ) ma me ayaresa, nti me bo afu me paa, mema me bi, na biribiara a ɛyɛ boɔ no, metua. " Hare no buae sɛ, "It I is is is here, enti fa me kraman no nam bi mena. "

Kraman no bɔɔ ne bo fii adi kɔɔ akuraase bi na wonyaa akyi nan biako maa kyɛfa a ɛwɔ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma mu (i.e. 3.s. a. 3d.) na ɛbɛbae. Hare no noaa no, twitwaa no, na fa guu nnua nketewa so, na ɔde ɛmo ne nkyene guu fam, na ɔde guu nam no mu, na ɔde guu ogya no so, na ɔde guu egya no so. Bere a ogya no kaa no, berɛ bi akyi no, (Kraman) tee sɛ suzzling, hyiae, e stress! Kraman no de berɛ tenten na ɛhwɛɛ no. Afei Hare no kae sɛ, "Kraman, wo kɔ bɛda wɔ dan ahodoɔ no bi mu, na me nso, me nso, yɛbɛkɔ akɔtena ɔkyena foforɔ mu, na me kɔ ɔkyena ntɛm, mebɛyi saa nam yi (the ogya) na me ne no ayɛ aduro ama wo. " Kraman no fii hɔ kɔɔ nna. Hare no nso, wɔkɔtoo abɔnten. Afei Kraman da so si ananmu (ntam hɔ a ontumi nna), na nam no hwam nam ɛhwe so ba ne so, nanso wankasa. Seesei ɔsan frɛ no bio. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Avery Hare. " Hare no buae sɛ, "Aane! " Na ɔkae sɛ, "nkuraa wɔ wo akuraa, enti meretwe nam no, na mehyɛ no nkyɛn. " Hare no kae sɛ, "Go na monya; me deɛ, me werɛ how nyinaa ne sɛ mede me nsa bɛto so ama ɔkyena, wɔ ahyɛdeɛ a metumi de ayɛ aduro ama wo. " Enti Kraman no kɔeɛ na wɔtua no ka. Mprempren Kraman no da hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Avery Hare. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Aane! " Na ɔkae sɛ, "The nkura no bɛwe, enti memfa bɛhyɛ mtumiɛ ase, na kɔ ada. " Hare no kaa sɛ, "All I interest is by-ɔkyena, sɛ nneɛma mu da hɔ a, me ne no bɛyɛ aduro ama wo. " Kraman no da ase bio; ɔbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛkora so no, ɔrentumi nni ne ho so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Avery Hare, saa nkuraa yi aba me tumiɛ ase, nti me bɛfa nam no de asi me etire so, na mebɛgyina hɔ akosi sɛ nneɛma bɛda adi. " Hare no buae sɛ, "Oh, I me kɔ so ka sɛ, nea ɛfa me ho nyinaa ne sɛ, menya (nam no kosi ɔkyena ma wo aduro. " Kraman no faa nnam no de sii ne etire so, na ɔgyinaa hɔ. Afei owue kakra bi ma ɔboroo nanim, na ɔboroo nani. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, Father Hare, de besi nam yi, saa nkuraa no akɔ soro wɔ me so, na adu me retire, nti mebɛkura (nam no wɔ me ano. " Hare no buae sɛ, "Oho o! Mekɔɔ so kae sɛ, wɔ ɔkyena, sɛ nneɛma bɛda adi a, me ne no bɛyɛ aduro ama wo. " Afei na ɛyɛ nnam wɔ nano, na ɔkyinkyini dan a ɛwɔ hɔ no, na wawia ne dua (a nsakraeɛ nnyigyei wom)! Ɔkaa sɛ, "Avery Hare, mma me nni ɛka mma wo wɔ nam yi ho. " Hare no kae sɛ, "Dɛn na wopɛ sɛ woka wɔ ho? " Kraman no buae sɛ, "Me kyerɛ sɛ, sen sɛ anka ɛsɛ sɛ nkuraa yi di no, momma mentua ho ka. " Hare no kae sɛ, "Oh no! Ma morphine no mmra. " Nanso seesei Kraman no mma nam (dow) nkɔ (ne mem). Ɛnna ɔhwee ase na ɔdae. Anɔpa bi, ntɛm ara, Hare no kaa sɛ, "Kraman, de saa nam no baeɛ. " Kraman no buae sɛ, "Ho, Father Hare, adɛn nti na woanka nea meka kyerɛɛ wo no ankyerɛ wo pɛn? " Hare no buaeɛ sɛ, "So woadi? " Kraman no kae sɛ, "Aane! Me dii no, metɔɔ. " Hare no kae sɛ, "Wo, Kraman, korɔno a wɔfrɛ no korɔnno no renntra wo so da.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
