Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ YAREƐ PII WƆ ABUSUAKUO NO MU

MPREM no, na Kwaku Ananse te hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ Nyankopɔn, Sky-anuonyampɔn, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Nanabarima, fa wo odwan a wɔfrɛ no Kra Kwame (i. e.. nea wode bɔ wo ɔkra bɔ afɔre wɔ Memeneda) na ma me nni no, na ma metumi akɔgye ababaawa fɛfɛ (wɔ nsesaeɛ mu). " Skyony-anuonyam no maa no odwan, na Kwaku Ananse bɔɔ ne tirim san baa nakuraa no so, na ɔkunkum odwan no dii. Afei Ananse no kɔɔ akuraase bi sɛ, akuraa a ɛwɔ hɔ no nni ɔbarima sɔfoɔ, na ne nyinaa yɛ mmaa. Kwaku Ananse waree wɔn nyinaa na ɔtenaa hɔ da bi. Bere a ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔeɛ no, ɔkɔkyerɛɛ Eome-anuonyampɔn no sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse, wo deɛ odan a ɔnyaeɛ no, wakum no na wama mmaa bi aduan na waware (thuth). " Ewiem-anuonyampɔn no kaa sɛ, "year saa? " ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Indressire no, ɛyɛ nokware. " Eome-anuonyampɔn no somaa asɛnkafoɔ kɔɔ saa akuraa no so na ɔde mmaa a wɔwɔ hɔ no nyinaa baa hɔ. Na asɛnkafoɔ no kɔ kɔeɛ, kɔhyiaa mmaa no, na wɔde ɔbaa bi a na wɔyare no ade a ɛda nkyɛn no faa wɔn nyinaa, na wɔde wɔn kɔɔ Skygod-god no. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Mo a moda so yi, dɛn na metumi ne mo ayɛ? " Na ɔbaa yarefo no kae sɛ, "So na monnye me koraa. " Kwaku Ananse kɔeɛ na ɔde nkoa baa hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Bathe me na megyee (nsuo a wode adi dwuma) na mehwie agu twa yi mu. " Kwaku Ananse dwarea ne nipadua na ɔhwiee (nsuo a na ɔde di dwuma) guu dwa no mu. Saa ɔbaa yi bɛyɛɛ fɛ paa, na ɔbaa biara nni hɔ te sɛ nabusuakuo no mu. Kwaku Ananse waree a na ɛyɛ ne dedaw. Seesei ɔbɔfoɔ no san baa bio, na ɔhunuu saa anwonwa yi. Ɔdwane kɔɔ abɔnten kɔbɔɔ Ewiem ne anuonyampɔn no amaneɛ sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse ayɛ wo kwae, ɔde wo mmaa a wɔn ho nyɛ fɛ no aba na wama wo akura nea ɛyɛ fɛ no mu. " Ewiem-anuonyam no somaa asɛnkafoɔ kɔɔ hɔ kɔsii nea Ananse no wɔ, kɔbɔɔ saa ɔbaa no. Wɔde saa nkra yi kɔmaa Kwaku Ananse. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Thund he na ɔnyɛ me nso? " Na asɛnkafoɔ no kae sɛ, "The Sky-god ka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ yɛde ɔbaa no kɔ ne nkyɛn. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Ɛno ne sɛ ɔte hɔ, gye no kɔ. " Seesei, Kwaku Ananse na ɔkɔgyee tow a ɔde yareɛ a ɔayi afiri ɔbaa no mu no nyinaa, na ɔprapraa dadwenɛ bi wɔ ano, na ɔde wedeneɛ too otwa foforo so de maa ne abofra Ntikuma, Ntikuma, de maa no. ne Kwaku Ananse boroo wɔn so (wɔn twene a na wɔayɛ no) na wɔtoo dwom:

"Y'odede den, Y'ode den.

Aso Yaw e!

Y'odede dende, Y'ode den.

Mo aniwa yɛ kɔkɔɔ kwa!

Y'odede dende, Y'ode den.

Woyɛ bandy a wo ho akokwaw!

Y'odede dende, Y'ode den.

So saa Aso Yaw?

Y'odede dende, Y'ode den.

Wo ho hia kɛse!

Y'odede dende, Y'ode den.

Ɛhwene yɛ wo anim ɔpɛ!

Y'odede dende, Y'ode den.

Wo anan yɛ wo yaw te sɛ akoa!

Y'odede dende, Y'ode den.

Wo retire te sɛ nantwi!

Y'odede dende, Y'ode den. "

Ntikuma (dade a ɛyɛ den ne dwom):

"Abawa fɛfɛɛfɛ, Abawa fɛfɛɛfɛ! "

Na Afuadotwewtwe (Yafunu-to-apueeɛ) ne Nyinnwɔtwe (Spinle-Shands) sii gyinaeɛ. Anene a ɔyɛ akwakwaaabi no de ahoɔhare tuu mmirika ka kyerɛɛ Ewiem-anuonyampɔn no sɛ, Kwaku Ananse wɔ asa bi a ɛfata wo nanso ɛnyɛ Ananse. Ntɛm ara na Ewiem-anuonyam no de asɛnkafoɔ kɔɔ hɔ sɛ wɔrekɔfa nasa aba. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Me asa yi, yɛn nko ara na yɛdi no wɔ ahema dan mu, na sɛ Ewiem-anuonyampɔn no gye to mu a, ɛnde me de me bɛba. Wɔkɔɔ hɔ bɛka kyerɛɛ Eome-anuonyampɔn no. Ɔkaa sɛ, ɛnyɛ hwee, momma ɔmfa no (it) mmra. Kwaku Ananse de akyene kɔɔ ahema dan mu, na Ewiem-anuonyampɔn no baeɛ na ɔbɛsii, na ne yerenom no nyinaa sane. Seesei deɛ, na ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ a wɔn yareɛ. Bere a ohuu sɛ Kwaku Ananse atrɛw dadwenɛ mu wɔ twa a na ɛyɛ ne yareɛ nyinaa ho no, ɛno nti, ɔkae sɛ ɔrensie. Na afei Sky-god no hyɛɛ no ma, na ɔbaa hɔ no, Kwaku Ananse maa ne tow so bɔɔ ɔbaa no, na yareɛ no abɔ apete (a nnyigyei bi akata so), ntome! Saa ara na akisikuru, yafunu mu akisikuru, akisikuru, akisikuru, adwenemhaw, nketewa-pax, ɛyaw, asikreyare, abɔda, baa abusuakuo no ntam. Bere a ayayade biara nni hɔ akyi no, na Kwaku Ananse yare biara nni hɔ, nanso na Skygod-god no ne nea ɛde ba abusuakuo no mu yareɛ.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
