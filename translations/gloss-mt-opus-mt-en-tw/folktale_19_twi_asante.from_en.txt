SƐNEA ƐKƆƆ SO WƆ MMOFRA NWOMASUA HO (FIRST)

NEA yɛka no, berɛ a ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bae, na saa Agya Ananse, Ananse, ne ne yere "Aso", ne ne mmofra, Ntikuma, Nyinnwɔtwe (Thin-shains), Afuadotwewtwe (Belly-Se-to-burst), ne Tikononono (Big-big) sii atenaeɛ ketewa bi na ɔtenaa mu. Da biara a Ananse bɛkɔ akɔfa aduane, wuram basere, na wɔanoa. Afei da bi, Agya Ananse kɔɔ nwura no mu, ɔhunuu sɛ aduan dɛɛ bi gyina hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. Aduane no kaa sɛ, Me din nni fɛ. Ananse no kaa sɛ, Dɛn na wɔfrɛ no? Me na merefrɛ no, Fill-up-eason-and. Ananse no kaa sɛ, Fille aduane no bi wɔ hɔ a ebia mehu. Aduane a wɔde pal-oil soup ahyɛ mu ma no, ɛne Kwaku Ananse dii ne nyinaa so. Berɛ a ɔwieeɛ no, ɔbisaa aduane no sɛ, Dɛn na wakyiwadeɛ? Mmuae a aduane no de maeɛ, Me tan tuo ketewaa, ne methuda badwa bi. Ananse no faa nkesee fie no, kɔtoo silin so. Afei Kwaku Ananse fii adi kɔɔ nwura mu de aduane baeɛ, na "Aso nso, berɛ a ɔwiee aduannoa a wɔfrɛ no Kwaku Ananse no, na ɔkae sɛ, Ohia, wahiade ankasa ne me, nea ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ ne aduane, wo ne mmofra yi yɛ ade a ɛho hia ankasa; sɛ woresɛe ade a, na me aso ayɛ dinn (efi wo kwadwom nnyigyeɛ mu). Me werɛ how wɔ aseseɛ no akyi, na ɔkɔɔ (and) wɔ silin a na aduane no wɔ no. Ɔkaa sɛ, Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Me din nnyɛ fɛ. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Mprempren, Ntikuma hyɛɛ no nsow sɛ nagya annyin wɔ nokwasɛm no nitan a ɛne sɛ ɔne no nnidi no, na Ntikuma de dɔn guu nagya so na ɔhunuu nea agya anya ho nsɔmu. Bere a agya no fii adi kɔɔ nwura no mu no, Ntikuma foro kɔɔ soro wɔ soro (oforo silin) na ohuu aduane no. Ɔfrɛɛ ne maame ne ne anuanom no, na wɔkɔɔ soro. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Me nyɛ obi a ne ho yɛ fɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Afei dɛn na woafrɛ no? Ɔkaa sɛ, Me din ne Fill-up-afe-season. Ɔkaa sɛ, Fills kakra bi a metumi ahu. Na aduane no hyɛɛ ma sɛe wɔ palm-oil so. Ntikuma ne ne maame ne ne anuanom dii kɔsii sɛ wɔwiee koraa. Na afei Ntikuma bisaa aduane no, Dɛn na wohyɛɛ no den? aduane no kaa sɛ, Mun tuo tiaa ne kora ketewa. Ntikuma ka kyerɛɛ Afudotwewwe (Belly-ell-like-burst) sɛ ɔmfa bi mmra mma me. Na Agya Ananse (mean-time) asan aba (fi nwura a wɔde wuram basere) no mu. "Aso wiee no. Wɔfrɛɛ no Kwaku Ananse; ɔbuaa sɛ, ebia na wo nte nea meka no; Me kae sɛ, efi da kosi da a, sɛ me ne aduane ba fie a, ebia wobɛdidi nsa, ɛfiri sɛ wo ne ebinom a ɛwɔ ahiadeɛ mu. "Aso ne ne mmofra dii. Agya Ananse hohoroo fam, ɔforoo soro wɔ silin hɔ; ɔkae sɛ, Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ! Kom koraa! Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ. Kom koraa! Agya Ananse kae sɛ, Ah! Ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ ntawntawto a ɛwɔ saa ntotam yi so no, ɛnyɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ; mɛkɔ de Oyokofoɔ kente baako abɛto so. Ɔde sii ne mpaboa so, ɔforo kɔɔ hɔ (akurgain). Ɔkaa sɛ, Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. Kommmra! Saa aduane yi yɛ fɛ. Komm koraa! Ɔde ne ho hwɛɛ round no na ɔhunuu tuo ketekete ne kora ketewa bi a ɛwɔ mu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Ɛnyɛ adeɛ baako, ɛnyɛ nneɛma mmienu (ii. Esiane sɛ akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho nti, ɛyɛ Ntikuma. Kwaku Ananse na ɛbubue aduane no, na ɔhwee ase; ofii Ɔpanyin kente nwoma no mu kɔtoo nwura no mu. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, ɔhunuu sɛ adeɛ bi a ɛyɛ fɛ paa (adeɛ) a wɔfrɛ no Mopere, abapra, wɔ hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, OhO, a ɛyɛ nwanwa! Saa adeɛ yi yɛ fɛ sen nea etwa to no, abofra yi ho yɛ fɛ. Annanse no kae sɛ, Mennyɛ aho yɛ fɛ. (Ananse) kaa sɛ, ɛnneɛ dɛn na worefrɛ no? Ɔkaa sɛ, Mene Abiridibrada (a ɛno ne sɛ yɛbɛka sɛ, Swish-and-raise-weals). Na ɔkae sɛ, Swash a ɛyɛ ma me kakra. Na abapɔn no bɔ no, Swash, swash, swaish. Agya Ananse suu, Pui! Apuei! Ɛhɔ na anomaa bi (tenaa ɛbɛn hɔ) ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse, Say Adwoabere (a ɛkyerɛ sɛ, adwo-sea-anaasɛ). Na Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Adwoabere. Na abapɔw no gyinaa hɔ hwee no. Na Kwaku Ananse de abapra yi baa fie; ɔkɔɔ de sii silin so. "Aso" no wiee (cooking) aduane no, ɔkaeɛ sɛ, Kwaku Ananse, ba bɛdidi. Ɔno na ɔbuaa sɛ, esiane sɛ woda so ara wɔ ha (asase so nti, ebia wunni ɛtokuro biara wɔ waso (nankasa nte nea mekae no, madidi. Kwaku Ananse foroo soro kɔtraa ase komm. Ɛnna ɔsan baa fam bio; afei ɔkɔɔ bio na ɔde ne ho kɔsiee baabi. Afei Ntikuma foroo abansoro atifi. Ɔkae sɛ, "Oh, saa agya (of mining) no asan de biribi aba bio. " Ntikuma kae sɛ, "Maame, Nyannkontinwe, Afudotwe, ba ha, ma nea agya de aba (berɛ yi) sen nea etwa to koraa. " Afei wɔn nyinaa foroo soro (wɔ silin no mu). Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Saa adeɛ yi yɛ fɛ. " Na ɔbuaa sɛ, "Mennyɛ 'undary'. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ wo din? " Na ɔkae sɛ, "Merefrɛ no 'Swish-and-raise-raweals'. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Swish kakra ma mihuu. " Na saa abapɔn no bɔɔ no so boroo wɔn so koraa. Kwaku Ananse gyinaa nkyɛn, na ɔfrɛɛ sɛ "Lay a ɛwɔ so no, ɛda so, titiriw wɔ Ntikuma, na ɛda ne so. " Mprempren a Kwaku Ananse ahwɛ na wɔahunu sɛ wɔrebɔ no yiye no, ɔkae sɛ, "Adwoabere" (Cool-and-sea-dasea-now). Kwaku Ananse na ɔbɛdii abapɔw no na ɔde guu nketenkete bɔɔ wɔn ho apete. Ɛno na ɛmaa abapra no baa abusuakuo no mu. Enti ɛba sɛ woka wo abofra no asɛm bi a, na wɔrentie wo, yɛbɔ no mpire.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
