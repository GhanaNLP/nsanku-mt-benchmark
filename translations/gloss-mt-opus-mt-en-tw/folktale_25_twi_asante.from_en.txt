YAW no ka sɛ, bere bi na me maame te hɔ a ne maame awu na ogyaw wɔn (one). Wɔyɛɛ afahyɛ bi, na atapanyin (Ata Panin) faa ahene a ɛsom bo ne sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ bi na ɔde wɔn too ata kumaa, Ata Kumaa kɔn mu. Ná ata kumaa ne ne nnamfoɔ no da asuawa mu sɛ wɔrebɛkɔ adwa adware. Bere a wɔrehorone no, hama a etwa to no twitwaa sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ a atwa Ata Kumaa kɔn ho ahyia no, na ahosiesieɛ no atɔ nsuo no mu. Ɔnhwehwɛ sɛ ɔbɛnya no, ɔrentumi anhu. Ɔbaa fie kɔbɔɔ ne nuabea no amanneɛ sɛ, "Sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ no ayera. " Ata 'Panin (the apapanyin) kae sɛ, Mennye nni sɛ wɔbɛhwere, na sɛ anka wɔwɔ hɔ mpo a, biribi wɔ hɔ a mɛka afi wo hɔ. Ata Kumaa (ata kumaa) kae sɛ, "Dɛn na wobɛka afi me hɔ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, " ɔbɛka afi wo abodin mmɔre, bereobuo a asasewaw ketewa no wɔ, Nsuo a gu gu no, No no bere so? gles ponponponponons, ahaban a ɛno nkutoo ne dua a wode abo abɔ mu no, ne Asono Ɔhemmaa no dua. Ata Kumaa kae sɛ, "Me ate. " Ata Kumaa fii adi sɛ ɔrebɛhwehwɛ saa nneɛma yi, na ɛbaa bea pɔtee bi a na dua baako wɔ hɔ. Bere a dua no hunuu no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Ataa Kumaa, (obi a nnipa ka sɛ) ɔda kuro no kuro akyi te sɛ osuo suo a ɛma ɔhome. Na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔkɔhyiaa Aserewa nwoma bi. Na Aserewa nwoma no kae sɛ, Sika ne Ata Kuma, sɛ ɔda kuro no kuro akyi a, obiara ka sɛ ɔte sɛ osuo osu a ɛma ɔhome. Kojo Nsa a.

Aserewa nwoma no kaa sɛ, Ɛno nti na wosu, wɔba bɛgye adeɛ. Na Ata Kumaa kɔɔ bɛgyee (Aserewa anoma berebuo), na ɔdaa no ase. Na ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ, na ɔkɔtoo nsuo, kɔhyiaa nsuo, gugluponponponponponons. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ata Kumaa, sɛ ɔda akuraase abɔnten awieeɛ a, nnipa akwaaba te sɛ osuo osu a ɛma ɔhome. Ata Kumaa ka kyerɛɛ no amaneɛbɔ a na waka akyerɛ afoforɔ no. Gurging-nsu no maa no bi (fi ne nsu a ɛsen mpɔn) na ɔde kɔɔ so. Afei ɔne Daboɔ no hyiaa bio. Bere a Daboɔ no hunuu no, ɔkaa sɛ, Ata Kuma, sɛ ɔda akuraase abɔnten awieeɛ a, ɔte sɛ osuo osu a ɛma ɔhome. Ata Kumaa ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ eRRAND. Ɔkaa sɛ, Bran na gye adeɛ bi to mu, (nding one one mmɔden). Afei Ata Kumaa kaa sɛ, Avery Tiger, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔyɛ na ama me nsa aka Asono Ɔhemmaa no dua? Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ah, sɛnea wokɔ no, mmoa no nyinaa bɛda fam, na Asono no Ɔhemmaa bɛda mfinimfini, na sɛ wokɔ a, woatwi wɔn denneennen, na sɛ wodu Asono Ɔhemmaa no nkyɛn a, twa ne dua, nanso sɛ wode ahwɛyiye tiatia wɔn so a, wɔbɛtumi atete wɔn. nkate bɔne kunkum wo. " Ata Kumaa kae sɛ, "Metaa meda mo ase. " Na bere a ɔne no kɔɔ hɔ no, ɔhunuu saa mmoa yi; ɔgyee wɔn so denneennen, na ɔduruu Ɔhemmaa no nkyɛn. Ɔtwaa ne dua no mu, na ɔsan nakyi. Wɔ kwan a ɔfaa so tɔɔ ad-glas no, na ɔde kɔɔ (fie). Ɔkae sɛ, "Ata 'Panyin, ha na woka sɛ wobɛfa afi me so. " Ata 'Panyin kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka wɔn. " Mprempren, da bi a na wɔte mu, na Ata Kumaa de ahwehwɛ no kɔhwɛɛ mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Saa ahwehwɛ yi, bere a ɛduru bi no, hu maame a ɔwɔ Nsaman-amansan asase so. " Ata 'Panyin kae sɛ, "Is a so, fa ba, na ma mehwɛ. " Ɔde maa no. Berɛ a na ɔrebɛhwɛ mu no, ɛbɛdwane afiri ne nsa, na ɛbubu. Ata Kumaa kae sɛ, "Na eyi deɛ, nea wunya firii me nkyɛn no, me hwehwɛ wo. " Afei Ata 'Panyin kɔgye nneɛma a Ata Kumaa de aba no. Ata Kumaa kaa sɛ, "Mempɛ (saa), nea mepɛ ne sɛ wobɛkɔ akɔfa afoforɔ aba. " Ata 'Panyin de ne ho sii hɔ, na me ne ahaban baako kɔhyiaa dua bi. Ɔkae sɛ, Ata Panyin, a, sɛ ɔda adi wɔ akuraase abɔnten awieeɛ a, nani gye sɛ osu asuguare. Ata 'Panyin buae sɛ, Meda mo maame! agya ne wo agya! Ɛde besi ha no, ma wo ahaban baako na ɔmma me nkɔ. (dua a ɛwɔ ahaban koro pɛ no kae sɛ, "Pass on, he na onim sɛ ɔne wo bedi no, ɛhɔ na ɔwɔ. " Ɔkɔɔ so kɔhyiaa Aserewa nwoma bi. Aserewa nwoma no kae sɛ, "Ata Panyin; Bere a ɔda akuraase abɔnten awieeɛ sɛ ɔma ahoɔden sɛ osuo asuguare. " Aserereamaa amaa no kae sɛ, "Pas on no, ɔno a ɔnim (ɛdeɛ ɔne wo bɛdi dwuma) no, ɔwɔ hɔ. " Ɛnna ɔduruu Gurging-nsu mu. Gruging-nsuo kaa sɛ, "Ata Panyin; Sɛ ɔda kuro no awieeɛ a, ɔte sɛ osuo osu a ɛma ahome. " Ata 'Panyin' kaa sɛ, "Bere A me aya wo maame no, bere a me agya agya no, mema me nsuo bi na ɔmma me so. " Gurging-nsuo no kaa sɛ, Pass a ɛwɔ anim no, ɔno a ɔnim (awerɛhow a ɔde bɛdi wo ho dwuma) no wɔ hɔ. Daboɔ no akyi no, ɛhɔ na ɔhyiaa. Dɔboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Hea Ata Panyin, a ɔda ne abɔnten awieeɛ no, ɛyɛ sɛ ɔma ahoɔden sɛ osuo asuguare. " Ata 'Panyin buae sɛ, 'Iaua' maame!' ne wo agya! Me deɛ, mema me mmɔre bi na mema me kwan. " Daboɔ no kaa sɛ, "Ɔno na onim sɛnea wɔbɛdi wo ho dwuma," na ɔhyiaa Asono mmoakuo bi, na Ahemmaa a ɔwɔ mfinimfini no, ɔkɔɔ so twitwaa ne dua no. Afei ɔkɔɔ so twitwaa ne mu. Asono no mmoakuo no nyinaa gyee no nsɔmu, na wɔgyee no so, na ɔwui. Ata Kumaa hwehwɛɛ ne nuabea wɔ kwan so. Na ɔmma.
