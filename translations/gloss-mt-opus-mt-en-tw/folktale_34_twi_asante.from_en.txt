Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ ONIMA BI BƐKYERƐ NO, MFITIASEƐ A ONIIPA FOFOFORƆ BƐN NO NKYERƐ

NA ɛyɛ bere bi a ɔbɔfoɔ bi wɔ hɔ. Bere a ɔsɔre kɔɔ nwura no mu kɔpɛɛ agorɔ sɛ ɔku, sɛ ɔbɛnya bi adi, na ebinom atɔn. Afei da bi a ɔkɔɔ nwura no mu no, ɔtee Kokotee, Nwura ne ɔPONKYERƐNI, too nsa frɛɛ nabusuani no sɛ, "Kokotee Asamoa e! " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Aane, nua, yiw. " (ɔPONKYERƐNI a odi kan no kae sɛ, "Yɛn mfuo mu berɛ a yɛde bɛyɛ adwuma no adu, enti momma yɛn kɔ akwanhyia no ho anigyeɛ mu, yɛmfa yɛn ho nhyɛ dadeɛ no fɛfɛɛfɛ mu na yɛmfa ano nhyɛ (twa nnwinnadeɛ) so, sɛ, sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ yɛtwa nnua biara so a, yɛbɛtumi atwa so. . ɔbɔɔfoɔ ahunnu, na ɔtee nkɔmmɔdie no nyinaa. Mprempren, Bush-pig nuabarima no bisaa ne nuabarima Bush-pig sɛ, "Na akuraa bɛn na yɛbɛkɔ na dadeɛ no abɔ? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Yɛbɛkɔ akuraa a wɔfrɛ no goal-the-asuwa no. " Na ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛda? " Na ɔbuaa sɛ, "Monday. Saa ɔbɔfoɔ no tiee nhyehyɛeɛ yi nyinaa, na ɔtwee ne ho sii hɔ maa fie. Afei, bere a ɔbaa fie no, ɔka kyerɛɛ nsɛm ho amanneɛbɔ okokoro a ɛwɔ mpoano atɔeɛ no sɛ, nea ɔtee bere a ɔkɔɔ nwura no mu no. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ akuraa no Titenani sɛ, "Mma mmofra no nkɔ ntwa nnyina mfa mmra, na ɛberɛ a Ɛdwoada no, Bush-pigs no sesa wɔn ho (wɔ nnipa mu na wɔba, yɛbɛfa wɔn na wɔabɔ wɔn sanku ama nnyina no. " Mmofra no kɔtwaa nnyina no, na wɔde wɔn baa hɔ. Ɔpanyin no kɔɔ akuraa hɔ na ɔka kyerɛɛ akuafoɔ no sɛ wɔnni dade stallity mma no. Na tantofoɔ no bisaa Head-man no sɛ, Na saa dade stabilitypo dodoɔ yi nyinaa, a wose ɛsɛ sɛ me ne wɔn bɔ no, dɛn na wode bɛbɔ? Na ɔka kyerɛɛ no amaneɛbɔ no, sɛdeɛ ɔbɔfoɔ bi akɔ nwura no mu, na ɔsan ba bɛbɔ amanneɛ sɛ Ɛdwoada mmabaa nwoma bi bɛdane wɔn ho ayɛ nnipa, ahyɛdeɛ a wɔde bɛba ne dɔnka sɛ ɔrebɛbɔ nnwinnadeɛ. Sɛnea dwomfoɔ kyerɛ no, tanmfoɔ no dwanee sɛ wɔbɛbɔ dade stabilitypo no so ntɛm ara. Bere a ɔhene no de ne nsa too nnyina ne stalosa dade mma so no, ɔbɔɔ kurow-crier no ma ɔbɔɔ dawuru no, kae sɛ, Ɛdwoada no, ɛyɛ ɔbaa, anaa sɛ ɛyɛ ɔbarima no, ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara kɔ baabiara. Ɛdwoada duu hɔ, na wɔ anɔpa a aberewa ka kyerɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no sɛ, Gor and di yam ɛmo, ne nkyene, ne gyene, ne baabi a mmanoma no bɛtwitwa wɔn nwoma ahoroɔ no gu; woyɛ ne mfɛfoɔ; woyɛ wo mfɛfoɔ; Enti, bere a wokɔ hɔ no, fa ɛmo no bu wɔn fɔ, na mmanoma no ba ha, na sɛ yɛkyere wɔn a, na ɛsɛ sɛ ebinom dwane na wɔde wɔn nwoma ahoroɔ kɔto wɔn so, bere a ɛmo no pira wɔn, na wɔgyaa wɔn no, wɔbɛyɛ nnipa (akund). ɔbɔɔfoɔ no kɔeɛ de siee hɔ. Na ɔtee sɛ wɔfrɛ wɔn sɛ, Kokote Asamoa, Ɛdwoada adu no, momma yɛn nyinaa nkɔ. Wɔyii wɔn nwoma ahoroɔ no, na wɔde sii hɔ. Afei, Mprako no mu baako, a na ɔyɛ ɔyaresafoɔ a ɔwɔ nhabankuro no mu, bɛkaa wɔn ho, na ɔde ne dadwenɛ too hɔ wɔ baabi foforɔ. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no hwɛ wɔn, na ɔma wɔn kɔ. Ɔkɔɔ kɔgyee nwoma ahoroɔ nyinaa, na ɔde ɛmo a ɛyɛ dhuwden guu wɔn so, na ɔde wɔn hyɛɛ asuwa no mu, na ɔmaa nsuo no yii wɔn. Nanso, nhabannuru ho ɔbenfo no de, woyii no fii ahomegyeɛ no mu, ɔbɔfoɔ no anhu. Ɔbɔfoɔ no baa fie, na Ɔhene no nso too nsa frɛɛ anokwafoɔ no sɛ wɔnhyiam mfa nnaadaa no mmra ne fie. Saa nnipa yi baa Ɔhene fie no, wɔbisaa Ɔhene no, na wɔka sɛ, Adɛn nti na woafrɛ yɛn? Ɔhene no kae sɛ, Wo na woyɛɛ mmarima, na wodwanee kɔtoo baabi foforɔ; kosi da a woasan aba no, ɛno ne asianeɛ a meka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ woba, ɛfiri sɛ meremma wo kwan mma wo nkɔ bio. Amadama no kaa no kyerɛɛ Ɔhene sɛ, Dɛn na woaka sɛ, yɛate, nanso yɛnim sɛ Ɔbɔadeɛ no nim hu amaneɛ. ternter baa nkyɛn bɛkaa yɛn ho asɛm nyinaa kyerɛɛ mo, nanso ɛno mfa ho, yɛn ne mo bɛtena abom; nanso yɛnim sɛ nea woka no yɛ atoro; ahobrɛaseɛ, ɛno nhia; yɛda saa ɔbɔfoɔ no ase. Enti, yɛn ne mo bɛtena abom; nanso adeɛ baako na ɛhyɛɛ, ɛne sɛ, mopae yɛn mfitiaseɛ; anaa sɛ wadeyɛɛ biara bɛda yɛn mfitiaseɛ adi; ɛsɛ sɛ ɛba no saa a, yɛbɛbu eyi, mo abusuakuo no, na yɛakɔ. Seesei, wɔ berɛ a Ɔhene no kɔɔ too nsa frɛɛ wɔn no, dunini no ne afoforo bi dwanee. Na dunini no kɔgyee ne dadwenɛ na ɔdwanee; nanso ahomegyeɛ no san baeɛ. Ɛno ne sɛ, wɔanhu wɔn nwoma ahoroɔ no. Ɔhene no penee tebea a momaoma no da so no so, na wɔne mmanoma no tenaa faako a ɛyɛ anigyeɛ. Mprempren akuraa no mmarima ware mmea binom; wɔmaa wɔn mmofra. Mprempren, da bi a ɛyɛ mmoama no mu baako ne nkuraasefoɔ no mu baako reko ne wɔn nkuturu no. Ɛhɔ no, akuraaseni no kaa aboa kɛseɛ no, Fa wo ho fi hɔ, aboa te sɛ wo a woyɛ Bush-pig adwumakuw no dea. Ɛnkyɛree biara na ɔkaa saa sen mmanoma no nyinaa aniwa, na wɔkɔɔ Ɔhene fie kɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, kwan a ɛbɛtumi ayɛ yiye a yɛaka akyerɛ wo sɛ afei na woaba no, enti dɛn na wobɛyɛ? Ɔhene no ma wɔkɔeɛ na ɔfrɛɛ nnipa no, na ɔfrɛɛ wɔn nnipa no. Wɔn a wɔyɛɛ somsotwe no. Ɔhene no hwɛɛ asɛm no mu yiye na ɔmaa atɛmmuo sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mmoma no gyae soboɔ no, efisɛ (ɔno) na ɔkaa sɛ ɛyɛ berɛ tenten a atwam na anisoadehunu yi, na ɛyɛ dwumadie a ɛdi kan a obiara anka nasɛm biara ankyerɛ no (wɔ ne mfitiaseɛ ho). Nanso mmanoma no kae sɛ, Yɛmpene so. Na Ɔhene no kae sɛ, Wobɛtie, na wosusuw sɛ nea ɔbarima yi kae sɛ ɛyɛ atoro no, ɛnyɛ Bush-pigs? Na mmanoma no kae sɛ, Oh, yɛtee. Ɛhɔ na mmanoma ne nsuo a ɛwɔ mpoano no bɔɔ nnipa bɛyɛ du pɛ. Mmoama no sɛee akuraa no, kosi sɛ na nnipa bɛyɛ du pɛ na wɔaka. Na wɔsrɛɛ sɛ wɔmma wɔn mmɔborɔhununu na wɔka kyerɛɛ mmmanoma no sɛ wɔn (mmanoma) no wɔ nifa so. Wɔde mmanoma no tiee, na wɔka kyerɛɛ nnipa no sɛ, asɛm bi a yɛadi kan aka no, ɛnyɛ den sɛ yɛbɛte ase, na sɛ yɛbɛka abom na yɛatena ase a, yɛsrɛ kasafonin a ɛtwetwe adwene nyinaa sɛ yɛn mfitiaseɛ. Sɛ wosusuu anaa woka (argain) ho asɛm a, ɛnneɛ, (yɛbɛbisa wo) sɛ, bere a yɛbaa saa baabi no, yɛbɛtumi asan de ɛhɔ aba. Na nnipa no kaa sɛ, "Yɛntumi nyɛ adeɛ a ɛte saa bio. " Nea yɛayɛ yi ama yɛn abusuakuo no asɛe, yɛnnyɛ saa bio. Enti wɔyɛɛ amansan dawurubɔ, na wɔbɔɔ dawuro no kɔɔ nsunsuansoɔ a ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara kyerɛ obi a ɔfiriri, sɛ ɛnyɛ nsɛm a wɔafa no adi a, ɛsɛ sɛ wɔsɛe kuro no. Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom betumi agye nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a wotumi yi me ayɛ (a asɛmka wɔ ho).
