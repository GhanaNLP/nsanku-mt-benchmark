Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBƐYƐ WƆ ATARROTS TAIL NO HO NO BƐYƐƐ NEA ƐREKƆ FAM KORAA

Ɔkae sɛ bere bi na Ɔhemmaa bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔdii baaayewa fɛfɛɛfɛ. Ná ɔwɔ hɔ; nanso bere a wɔmaa no awareɛ wɔ baako mu no, wampene sɛ ɔbɛware. Bere biara a nnipa (her) bɛkɔkasaa (to no), ɛfa awareɛ ho no, ɔkaa sɛ, akyinnye biara nni ho. Mprempren abɔdeɛ no, Ɔnani (Ɔnka) wɔ baabi wɔ nwura no mu, na ɔte ɔbɔ kɛse bi mu. da bi a ɔwɔ hɔ (wɔ ne ɔdan mu no, ɔtee abɔfoɔ bi a wɔwu, na wɔrekasa ho wɔ abawa fɛfɛ no asɛmti ho, sɛdeɛ wɔde no maa obiara wɔ awareɛ mu no, ɔkaa sɛ ɔmpɛ no. Ɔnka no da hɔ, na waka sɛ (to ne ho), I bɛdane me ho ayɛ aberante pa, na merekɔ ware Queen Mothers child. Wɔ nokware mu no, Ɔnka no danee ne ho bɛyɛɛ mmabunu; sɛ wobɛhwɛ a, na ɛnyɛ yiye. Ɔde sii hɔ maa akuraa no. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na ɔrewe koko-nuts. Berɛ a ɔduruu kuro no ano no, na Queen Mothers ba a ɔkɔdi ahohoro no asan aba na ɔretrɛ ntadeɛ no mu ma ɛayow. Ɔnkata nsuo no tɔeɛ no gyegyeegye wɔ Queen Mothers abofra no gyegyegyegye so. Queen Mothers ba no kae sɛ, Adɛn nti na woabobɔ wo din wɔ gyegyeegye so? Ɔkasa no kae sɛ, Woyɛ hokwan (in kasa so) Esono. Ɔkaa sɛ, Me ntɔn abodin no nyɛ Amu. Esono ff for (her) fie. Ntɛm ara na Ɔhemmaa no ba no kɔɔ hɔ kɔbɔɔ ne maame kae sɛ, Eh! Eh! eh! (Ashe de anigyeɛ a emu yɛ den retua no, mihuu nea mepɛ. Ɔhemmaa no kaa sɛ, Atwa toɔ! Ɛhe na ɔbabarima ne mmara wɔ? Ɔkaa sɛ, He I am, nanbea. Ɔhemmaa no de Ɔkasa no few nsa, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Ma wo ne wo yere no ntena faako a ɛyɛ anigyeɛ. Bere a anwummere no yɛɛ nwini no, ɔde ne yere gyee nahome. Enti ɛyɛɛ nna awotwe ni. Wɔ tenten hɔ na ɔkaa sɛ, Me yere, me kɔ me akuraa nanso merensan mma. Ne yere no kaa sɛ, "Afei na me ne wo bɛkɔ (together). " Ɔnka no kae sɛ, "Dabi. " Ɔnka no bɔɔ nkɔmmɔ na ɔkasaa (and) sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɔremma, nanso Queen Mothers abofra no antie (to). Bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, na ɔne ne kunu de sii hɔ. Ɔkasa no kɔɔ so no, Ɔkasa no baa abɔnten (kwan no so) na ɛde kɔɔ nwura no mu. Queen Mothers ba no kae sɛ, Dɛn na woreyɛ yi, ɛne kwan yi? Ɔnka no kae sɛ, Yɛn deɛ, momma yɛn nsi ano na yɛnni makyi. Ɔne ɔno na ɛkɔɔ so, na wɔkɔɔ so, na (ethense) Ɔnka no kɔeɛ bɛyɛɛ ɔdan. Ɔdanka no nso baa mu. Ntɛm ara na Ɔnanka danee ne ho yɛɛ no aboa kɛseɛ; afei ɔkasa kyerɛɛ Maame ba no, na ɔkaa sɛ, Eyi ne nea me ara me sesaa me ho wɔ ahyɛdeɛ a mede ware wo no mu, ɛfiri sɛ na wote wo maame ne wo papa laps so bɔ amanneɛ sɛ, Biribiara nni hɔ (mesuro); mebɛkum wo; me tenten me, na me akyere ntontom ama me; sɛ wo nsa ka bi a, wo rewie wo bi, wo awiei. Akoo Akoo Akoo Ɔnka no kae sɛ, "Scratch me retire. " Ɔhene Ɛnanom no ba no kae sɛ, "Me werɛ how sɛ mobɛbɔ no; sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mokum me a, ɛnneɛ mowu me. " Ɔnka no sii ɔpɛ a ɔwɔ sɛ ɔbɛtete ne retire so akyere Ɔhemmaa Ɛnanom ba no, bere a Akoo no bɔɔ tuo sɛ ɔsu a, pom! Akoo no kɔɔ ɔdan no mu kɔfaa Queen Mother ba no ne Ɔnanka no kɔɔ fie de amaneɛbɔ a ɛbaa sɛ wahu Queen Mothere ba no ne Ɔnanka no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛnea wakum Ɔnan. Ɔhemmaa Ɛnanom ba no nso de abakɔsɛm no ho nsekyerɛɛ nyinaa maa no. Ɔhemmaa no kaa sɛ, "Akoo, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mefa de meda mo ase? " Akoo no kae sɛ, "Grand Mother, mem Mempɛ biribiara, efisɛ me ara mepɛ pal-oil bi. " Ɔhemmaa no kɔe na ɔde pal-oil bae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Grand Mother, meregye me dua na me werɛ how wɔ saa palm-oil yi mu, na wɔnam saayɛ so ma ɛyɛ ma (akwantu a akokoɔduro wom). " Saa na Akoo bi dua bɛyɛɛ kɔkɔɔ. Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
