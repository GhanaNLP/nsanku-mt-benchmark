SƐNEA ƐBƐYƐ WƆ NEA ƐBAA SƐ NWANSANA, NWANSENA, KƆSRAMAN, KRAMAN NO, ƆHUNUU NO KAM! KAM!

Ná Ɔbɔfoɔ bi wɔ nkraman nnan; baako nso a wɔfrɛ no Siff-snffiri; foforo nso ne Luck-lick; foforo nso wɔfrɛ no Tie-in apam; na foforo nso wɔfrɛ no Mene kɛse. Bere biara a ɔbɛkɔ nwura no, na ɔde ne kraman no bɔ korɔn. Ɔtaa kɔ nwura no, na ɔne no kɔ bɔ mu. Da bi ɔkɔɔ nwura no mu na ohuu pila-tree bi; Wɔde aduaba a wɔde abɔ ho wɔ afã ahorow a ɛte sɛɛ akata so. Afei ɔsan baa fie, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne yere amaneɛbɔ no sɛ, "Akola-tree bi a mahu no, wɔde aduaba a wɔde abɔ afã ahorow a ɛte sɛɛ akata so. " Ne yere no kae sɛ, "So na fa wɔn, efisɛ wunim sɛ yɛnni kola-nut biako mpo a yɛwe na yɛntɔn. " Ɔbɔfo no fii adi wɔ ɔkwan no so, nanso ofii nkraman no fie, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a na kola-tree wɔ. Afei ɔforo kɔɔ soro, na ɔfaa kola-nuts no bi. Afei ɔtee fi baabi a ɛwɔ hɔ sɛ, "Twa! Huru! kuru! kukuru! pipe! " Sasbonsam (stood) bi wɔ nanim. Ɔkae sɛ, "Hwan na wabɔ matadeɛ? " Na ɔbɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, "Mekum wo ntɛm ara wɔ adeɛ a woayɛ yi ntawntawto ho. " Na ɔbɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, "Sɛ wobɛkum me a, momma me nni kan frɛ me nkraman. " Ɔkaa sɛ, " Nanso bere a woanya mmɔdenbɔ mmiɛnsa afrɛ wɔn na woammra no, ɛnde mekum wo. Afei ɔbɔfoɔ no yii nne (hi nne) so:

"Snriff-snffi, (come and) me, Lick-lick, (come ne) hyia me, Tie-in apɔ, (come) hyia me, Men kɛse (down, (come) hyia me.

Waii, wai, waii, wɔn ani beree sɛ wɔbɛba! Nano nnya nnyinaa hɔ, bere a na kraman no da nanim no. Nnipa no kae sɛ, "Sashiff-siff, ha yɛ Sasbonsam, ɔbɔɔ no animtiaa. " Na Siriff bɔɔ no ahohora. Na Sashiff bɔɔ no ahohora. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Lick-likick, ne natadeɛ bɔɔ no kɔn. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Tie-in-apɔ so, tie no wɔ ɔpɛ mu. " Tie-in apam no de ne mem no akyekyere wɔ apam no mu; na Sasbonsam no hwee ase wɔ hɔ, kum no! Afei Mene kɛse a wadi mfeɛ no de no kɔeɛ na nani bɔɔ no so. ɔbɔfoɔ no ne nkraman no hyiaa kola-nuts no na wɔde wɔn baa fie. Bere a ɔbɔfoɔ no san bae no, ɔbaa hɔ bɛwiee aduane a ɛyɛ asau a ɛyɛ nsaman no nyinaa, na ɔde sii hɔ maa nkraman no. Ɔde nsuo guu mu de siee hɔ, na ɔfiri hɔ kɔɔ ne yere bɛdii. Bere a ɔdidi wiei no, ɔkɔɔ abɔnten kɔtoo agyede (dua) ase. Afei Agyenkwa no akoa, Nwansena, kɔhwɛɛ aduane (place in nkraman ho boa) na ɔde too silin so. Na nkraman no fi abɔnten na ɔkɔm de wɔn. Lick-like kaa asɛm bi kyerɛɛ wɔn a aka no sɛ, "Woahu nea yɛayɛ ama yɛn wura no, nanso ɛde besi nnɛ, wama yɛn aduane biara mpo nni hɔ a yɛbɛdidi nti, momma yɛnni nkɔkum no. " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Woyɛ atoro, wunni atorɔ. " Lick-like fii adi kɔɔ abɔnten, na ɔbɔɔ ɔbɔfoɔ no amu ho, na ɔne ɔbɔfoɔ no bɔɔ bɔɔl. Afei nso, ɔkɔɔ Spori-sffiff amu no ho nkɔmmɔ, na ɔno nso kɔɔ nanim. Afei Tie-in-apɔ no mu biara ammɛkyekyere ne menem wɔ apɔɔ mu, sene Men kɛse adwa no nso, bɔɔ no akuturuku. Afei nkraman no san baa fie; na wɔrebɛhwɛ pato no mu, bere a wɔhunuu nkwan a ɛwɔ hɔ no ho nsunsuansoɔ. Wɔkaa sɛ, "Fly, anka ebia ɔbɔfoɔ no gyaa yɛn aduane na wɔadidi? "So wɔaka sɛ, "Minhuu no wɔ baabi. " Safe a etwaam no, bere a ɔmaa naniwa so no, ɔhunuu aduane a wɔde sii hɔ (o silin). Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, papa! Yɛakum yɛn wura kwa, ɛfiri sɛ, aduane a ɔde ama yɛn no, ɛwɔ hɔ ampa, na ɛnyɛ baako, na mmom mmienu, nwansena a wɔde no kɔtoo hɔ no. Nwansena no guan firii hɔ kɔdwane. Ɛno nti, bere a kraman bi te hɔ, na nwansena bi te ne so, ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkyere no.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
