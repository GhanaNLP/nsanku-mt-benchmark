" Sky-god kae sɛ, "Akud and power towns te sɛ Kokofu, Bekwai, Asumegya aba, nanso woantumi anntɔ, na woyɛ ɔbarima kɛkɛ a wɔn nni wo, (wose) anaa? " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Dɛn ne nsɛm boɔ? " Sky-god no kaa sɛ, "Worentumi ntɔ biribi kwa? Abɔdeɛ nnanmusini a ɛwɔ Ɔnka no mu nkoaa na Ɔnka no; Osebo, Daboɔ; Mmoatia, Mmoatia; (and) MMMborɔ, Nyawone. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Me de saa nneɛma yi nyinaa bi bɛba, na ( nea ɛka ho ne), me de me aberewa, Nasisia (The Sixth-child) bɛka bebree no ho. " Sky-god no kaa sɛ, "Go na fa wɔn ba. " Ananse no baeɛ, na ɔka kyerɛɛ ne maame no sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ me bɛtɔ Sky-god nsɛm, ne Skk no S. Ɛyɛ anuonyam na mekae sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mede Ɔnninini, Ɔnka; Osebo, Daboɔ; Mmoatia, Mmoatia; ne Moborɔ, Nyawone; na mekae sɛ mɛka wo ho ama bebree na moagye anuonyampɔn. " Mprempren Ananse bisabisaa ne yere "Aso no asɛm sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔyɛ na yɛanya Ɔni, Ɔnka? " Na "Aso kae sɛ, "Wokɔ twa ba a wɔde mmɛw nsa ayɛ no nso, na wotwa hama a ɛka bi nso, na fa ba. Kwaku Ananse, Ananse, kae sɛ, "Thathiadeth, me de wo bɛkɔ Ewiem anuonyampɔn na me nsa aka Ewiase ne anuonyampɔn anansesɛm (wɔ nsesaeɛ)". Kwaku Ananse de no kɔɔ Nigeriamey (Ewiem-anuonyampɔn). Ewiase-anuonyampɔn no kae sɛ, "Me nsa aka (i. e. Ɛhɔ na meda nea aka no. " Ananse no san bae, na ɔbɛka nea esii no kyerɛɛ ne yere sɛ, "Nyawone na ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ. " Ne yere no kae sɛ, "Hwɛ borɔdɔma, na fa nsuo hyɛ mu ma." Ananse no faa nwura no mu, bere a ohuu (Nwowone) dɔm wɔ hɔ, na ɔhwiee nsuo no bi guu Nyane so. Ananse no de deɛ nkaeɛ no guu ne ho so, na ɔtew borɔdeɛ no ahaban bi so na ɔde ne retire no ka ho. Na afei ɔka kyerɛɛ Nyawone no sɛ, "Sɛnea osuo no aba no, na wo ho nyɛ wo dɛ sɛ wobɛwura eyi mu, me tirim yɛ me yaw, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ na osu no remmɔ wo; worenhu sɛ mefaa borɔdeɛhahan anka me ho? " Afei Nyawone no kae sɛ, "Yɛda Aku ase, yɛda Wo Aku ase. " Nwoone no nyinaa tu kɔɔ soro, na ɛreyera abɛyɛ aboɔden abo, foforo! Agya Ananse kaa ano, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Phandsinds, me, me nsa aka wo, na meregye Ewiem-anuonyam anansesɛm (wɔ nsesaeɛ). " Na ɔde Nnwom no kɔɔ Enkokoɔpɔn no mu. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "So saa ara, mete aseɛ. " Afei Ananse no firii hɔ kɔhwehwɛɛ Daboɔ no anantuo, na (hua ebi wɔ hɔ) no, ɔtuu amoa a emu dɔ, na ɔde kataa so, na ɔsan baa fie.
