Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

KWAMENA, THE TERED-HILL, A ƐDA NNINI A AGYE DIN PINTINN NTAM

Ɔkae sɛ bere bi na ɔyɛ Ɔhemmaa bi baa, na saa asɛm no biara nni hɔ a wɔde maa no wɔ awareɛ mu, na ɔbɛka sɛ, ɔmpɛ no. Nanso (a etwa to no), Ɔman-aman no Ɔhene, Adu Gyamfi, baa waree saa Ɔhemmaa no abofra no. Queen Mothers ba no kaa ne kunu sɛ, "Me ne wo bɛkɔ wo kuro no mu. " Ɔbarima no buae sɛ, "Ma me nkɔ (first), na Adae no tɔ Kwasida bi, ɛdwoada a ɛdi hɔ yi na me ne mo bɛkɔ. " Na ɔbaa no kae sɛ, "Oh no, me ne wo bɛkɔ abɔ mu. " Ɛnneɛ na wɔrekɔ. Bere a wɔrekɔ no, wɔhunuu kuropɔn bi a wɔde dadeɛ pono ano kwan, na dadeɛ nyinaa twaa kuro no ho hyiaeɛ. Afei, wɔ kuro yi mu, sɛ mogya ntu wɔ wo suni biara a, (and) wogyinaa ɛpono akyi na wofrɛɛ no a, obiara annye wo so. Mprempren a ɛyɛ Queen Mother ba yi deɛ, suni biara nni hɔ a wawui. Ɔkasa ne benkum-behindi (E. e. Me werɛ fi sɛ, bere a Adu Gyamfi bɛduruu dadeɛ pono no, ɛnna ɔkɔɔ ne fie; ɔne Queen Mothere ba noankasa bio. Queen Mothers babea ne ne kunu Ɔkra tenaa hɔ, na wɔgyinaa dadeɛ pono no akyi, na wɔannya kwan nkɔ fie. Na Queen Mothers ba no ama ne nne so (and rape) ama no so:

"Adowa, me deɛ, me na mebaa, Adowa Kwasi Krampare, buee me kwan maa Me,dowa, yii me yam kyerɛɛ me. "

Nanso Adodowa ankasa ne ho. Ɔno nso antumi anhu kwan a ɔbɛfa so asan aba ne kuro no mu. Ɛneɛ na ɔne Ɔkra kɔɔ nhini a agyina pintinn ntam. Na Queen Mothere ba no faa kɔbere dadeɛ na ɔtenaa so, na ɔfaa foforo, na ɔtuu ne retire no ho ka; na Ɔkra te ne nkyɛn. Ɛno nti, sɛ wokɔ baabi a nhini a agyina pintinn (standard) no kɔ a, na Queen Mother maame ba ne natadeɛ soafoɔ ahofama te hɔ, na wɔbɛdane Kwamena a wɔn gyinabea kɔ soro wɔ gyinabea ho.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
