Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

YƐMFA ƐSONO NKO ARA NKO ARA NKO, NA YƐABƐN ƆBOƆ WƆ ASEREWA, WREENOMAA

KKWAKU AANANSE, Ananse no, na ɔte hɔ, na kwasida bi, ɔkɔɔ bɔɔ anɔpa pa kyerɛɛ Sky-god. Ɔno (the Sky-god) maa no nkataka fã. Kwaku Ananse de too gua na ɔkɔɔ so. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na mmaa wɔ adwa no mu boro wɔn ntadeɛ so. Kwaku Ananse ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ wɔnnye nkataka fã no ntom na wɔmfa mma wɔn abofra no. Wɔn nsa kaa no na wɔmaa abofra no. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Ma me biribi na mema me kɔ. Afei wɔguu nsuo mu twa, maa no, na ɔde kɔɔ. Sɛnea ɔkɔeɛ no, na nnipa bi wɔ hɔ a wɔreyɛ dɔte mmepɔw a wɔde bɛyɛ bakyerɛ, na Kwaku Ananse ama wɔn nsuo a wɔbɛnom. nnipa no maa no bayere. Ɔkɔɔ so kɔhyiaa tanmfoɔ, na ɔrenoa bidiɛ. Ɔmaa no ayeforɔ no sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔgye no to mu na ɔdi no, na ɔdi nidiɛ no anyɛ yie. Na Kwaku Ananse nso kae sɛ, Ma me biribi na ma me kɔ. Ɔmaa no nko ara (asɔre), na ɔde kɔeɛ. Ɔkɔɔ hunuu ɔbarima bi a na ɔde ne kɔteɛ retwa ne nsal-nuts. Na Kwaku Ananse ama no nko ara (asɔre), na dane no, ɔde Kwaku Ananse pal-oil, nsuo-po, ahyɛ mu ma. Na ɔkɔɔ so, na ohyiaa ɔbaa bi a ɔsoaa mmofra du, na ɔde nankasa ntasuo guguu wɔn nwoma ahoroɔ mu. Kwaku Ananse de ngo no kɔgyee ɔbaa no sɛ ɔmfa mma ne mmofra no. Ná ɔbaa no ma no abofra biako, na ɔde sii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔkɔhyiaa ɔbaa bi. Ná ɔnni abofra biara, na ogyee dua a abere na ɔde nnadewa bɔɔ ho ahweneɛ. Afei Agya Ananse de abofra no maa ɔbaa no, na ɔbaa a ɔwɔ dane no maa no dua arere, na ɔde sii hɔ. Ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔne nabatwɛn no hyiaa bi. Kwaku Ananse de nsenia no maa no, na ne wɔ dane no maa Kwaku Ananse atoko-folfour. Ɔne no kɔe, na ɔbaa Asono mmoakuo bi mu. Asono mmoakuo no mu mmoakuo no anom nsa wɔ atɛkyɛ amena bi mu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "TundOught this founds-flour to di, ɛfiri sɛ atɛkyɛ tadeɛ yi nyɛ yie. " Afei Asono mmoakuo no gye too mu na wɔnom. Bere a wɔawie nsonom no, Ananse no kae sɛ, Ma me biribi na ma menkɔ. Wɔkaa sɛ, Yɛnni hwee, nanso ɛtream yi a ɛda hɔ no, momma yɛn nyinaa ntwam na yɛnhuruw nk3 mu, na nea ɔhwe mu no, wotumi fa no. Seesei, Asono no mu biako bɛyɛ abofra. Bere a mmanoma no nyinaa huruw kɔɔ soro, nanso nea ɛwɔ abusua kwan no mu no guu nsuo no mu, na Kwaku Ananse kɔgyee no, na ɔde palm fiber hama bɔɔ Ɔsono nan, na ɔkuraa mu. Seesei huu sɛ aseresɛm anomaa bi retwam. Na Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "This is a ahomaa fɛfɛɛfɛ, mekɔkyere no de ama me abofra, Ntikuma. " Na Kwaku Ananse dii Ireland anomaa no akyi; ɔtoo aboɔ guu mu, wannya. Ɛsono no aba no, na Ɛsono no afiri hɔ. Ɛno nti na yɛwɔ ababufuo bi a ɛne sɛ, "Yɛn nnyaw Ɛsono sɛ yɛbɛkɔ akɔto ɔboɔ wɔ wawa asɔre bi ase. "

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
