Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

WƆFRƐ NO NWONWAASO SƐ KWAKU AANANSE, SPINER

KKWAKU AANANSE, Ananse no, na wɔte hɔ pɛn, bere a Sky-god maame no, yareɛ bɔɔ no. Wɔkɔɔ hɔ kɔbuu aduro bi a wɔde bɛba abɛhwɛ no. Ɔbaa no, ɔde sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (£8) perwee too gua sɛ ɔrekɔ ne yareɛ no. Afei, Kwaku Ananse kɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ Ewiem anuonyampɔn no sɛ, Saa yɔnkoɔ yi de sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma rekɔ kwa; ɔntumi nhwɛ no, nti ɔnhwɛ no, na sɛ mehwɛ no a, me retire betumi agye. Kwaku Ananse faa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (e. e. £4) na ɔkaa Sky-god no sɛ, "Sɛ metumi sa saa yareɛ yi a, me bɛma wo gyata a ɔte ase. " Afei anɔpa, bere a nneɛma bɛda adi no, na Nyankokɔn maame, Sky-god, aka kɔ. Wɔkae sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse, ne ho dɛn? " Kwaku Ananse fii saa baabi no, na wadwane. Seesei, ɔkasa ne benkum-behind ( e. e. Me werɛ afi sɛ: Kwaku Ananse adamfo kɛse ne Okusie, Okisi. Ɔka kyerɛɛ Okisi nea ɛkɔɔ so no, ɔkae sɛ, "Ɛno ne nea mahu no, enti afei ɛsɛ sɛ wo mmoa me. " Okisi no buae sɛ, "In kwan bɛn na metumi de aboa wo? " (Kwaku Ananse) buae sɛ, "Mepɛ sɛ wokɔ tutu ɔkwan no so kosi sɛ ebedu pemp a Sky-god no te so. " Okisi no kae sɛ, "Me bɛyɛ no ama wo. " Enti ɔyɛɛ no. Oki no baa bɛtoo nsa frɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, "Mewiee. " Kwaku Ananse kɔtoo ne ɔbarima Ntikuma; na watwa ne abenfo ama no; na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔnkɔ ɔkwan no so na ɔntra pempe no ase, na bere a ohui sɛ Skygod-god no de ne ho atra pepe no so no, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔbɔ nabenfo na ɔka sɛ:

" Sɛ wokunkum Kwaku Ananse a, abusuakuo no bɛsɛe!

Sɛ wode Kwaku Ananse kyɛ a, abusuakuo no de nne bɛyam! "

Ntikuma kɔɔ tenaa ahɔhoɔgyeɛ akongua no ase, na Kwaku Ananse bɔɔ dawuro sɛ ɔrekɔ Nyankopɔn, Ewiase anuonyam, hɔ wɔ nankasa apam no ho. Bere a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, afei Ewiem-anuonyampɔn no mu agumadifoɔ kyeree no nsɔmu; wɔde no kɔɔ Ewiase anuonyampɔn no anim. Afei kasafoɔ no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Yo, Kwaku Ananse, woka kyerɛɛ Nyankopɔn, Ewiem-anuonyampɔn no sɛ, sɛ ne maame, Nasia, wui a, ɔbɛtumi akum wo; nti Nasia afiri hɔ nti na yɛbɛkum wo. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Ɛsɛ sɛ moma me kwan ma meka masɛm. Nanso akyerɛwfoɔ no gyee ne nsɔmu wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ wɔbɛkɔ akum no. Ntikuma hyɛɛ aseɛ (ne nne) sɛɛ: "Sɛ wokunkum Kwaku Ananse a, abusuakuo no bɛsɛe!

Sɛ wode Kwaku Ananse kyɛ a, abusuakuo no de nne bɛyam! "

Wɔkae sɛ, "spee, tie! " Na bio aben no bɔɔ:

" Sɛ wokunkum Kwaku Ananse a, abusuakuo no bɛsɛe!

Sɛ wode Kwaku Ananse kyɛ a, abusuakuo no de nne bɛyam! "

Afei Krontihene kɔɔ soro kɔbɔɔ Eome-anuonyampɔn, abebuo, "saa abusuakuo yi yɛ wo dea, Ewiem-anuonyam, ne Ya, Old-Nana-bil. To-da a woanya Kwaku Ananse na woakum no. Yaa, Old-Nana-bi, ka sɛ, Ma no nkɔ, enti sɛ wogyae no a, ɛbɛyɛ yie. Ɛneɛ na Ewiem-anuonyam no maa akyerɛkyerɛfoɔ no yii no adi. Ɛno nti na abobuo bi wɔ hɔ a ɛne sɛ, "Wo ho yɛ nwanwa sɛ Kwaku Ananse".

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
