Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

SƐNEA AKEKYIRE, AKAKRAWI NO KYERƐ NO, NYAA ƐSE A AHYE

AKETEKYIRE, Sakrawi, ne Kwaku Ananse, Ananse, hyɛɛ aseɛ sɛ wɔrekɔyɛ aburoo hwehwɛ wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu. Na wɔnyaa aburoo aba mmienu pɛ. Na Kwaku Ananse nso kae sɛ, "Mefiri fam rekɔ mistructure. " Na Sakrawi no kae sɛ, "Mebɛsa me wɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ mewe no. " Kwaku Ananse kɔtoo ne dua. Sakrawi no nso de ne ho gyee mu, na ɔde too ogya no so. Na ɛyɛ adidibea, na ɔtraa hɔ (besie). Ananse no nso, ɔkɔɔ afuw no mu, na na ɔrekɔdwo wɔ nkranteɛbɔ a ɛfa aburoo no ho no ase. Aburoo no kae sɛ, "Done pep aniwa mu, behaust wo nsateaa kɔ maniwa mu. " Kwaku Ananse bɛdane naniwa ahwɛ ne Kraman; ne Kraman no kae sɛ, "Mohia sɛ wohwɛ me, ɛnyɛ me na meyɛɛ no. " Na Kwaku Ananse rebu poma bi de abɔ Kraman no so, bere a poma no kae sɛ, "Mommma me nntwa, ma (nifa) wo Kraman nka me. " Kwaku Ananse baa fie na ɔka kyerɛɛ Sakrawi no nyinaa. Bere a ɔreserew no, ɔhwee ogya mu. Saa na Sakrawi no nyaa ne se hyewee. Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me abata ho, sɛ ɛyɛ me dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
