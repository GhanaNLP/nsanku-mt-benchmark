SƐNEA BABADUA NO KYERƐ WƆ NSUAMU A ƐFA DUA KORO NO ARA HO

Ɔbaa bi gyee akyinnye, na ɔkɔɔ abosom nkumaa no mu biako kɔbisabisaa no (a ɛfa abofra no ho. Na anuonyampɔn no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Ɛyɛ blobable sɛ sɛ wotua abofra a ɔbɛwuwu no, nanso meyɛɛ nea metumi nyinaa de ama wo, nanso adeɛ a ɛsɛ sɛ meka ne sɛ, abofra no ne (alal) Ɔhemmaa bi abofra a ɔwɔ hɔ a ɔne Niamey, Ewiem-anuonyampɔn, ne bere a wokura no, mma ɛnyɛ hwee, nanso sɛ yɛsɔre a, ɛsɛ sɛ modware no, ngo fa, na fa si akonnwa so. (Likell) mmofra a wɔwɔ Anansesɛm mu no, ɛnkyɛ na wɔadua. Wɔfrɛɛ abofra abusua dentaa. Sɛnea na obom kumaa no kyerɛ no, maame no, bere a ɔsɔre, hohoroo no, na ɔboroo no, na mmofra no dii aduan, na afei wofii nafuo mu. Mprempren, da bi a ɔnni berɛ a ɔbɛnoa biribiara ama no adi no, ɔde ahyɛdeɛ maa nafanaa, Ɔkra, sɛ, "Merekɔ afuo, nti monyɛ biribi mfa nni aduan mma Akosua Dentaa nni. " Na maame no nso de sii hɔ. Afei, Ɔkra wiee borɔdeɛ no, ɔkae sɛ, "Akosua Dentaa, ba ha ne pound yi _fu_, wo ne wo Queen-Money no ho asɛm. " Ɔkra kae sɛ, "Mommra. " Come, wo ne wo ahantan. " Na Akosua Dentaa sɔre kɔɔ soro, ɔbɛgyee petle no; ɔde fu/fu_ a wɔahyɛ no den no. Bere a ɔwieeɛ pɛ no, ɔkɔɔ na ɔda dan no mu, de ne ntoma no kɔkaa ne etire ho. Ɔkra kɔɔ (party), ɔkaa sɛ, "Denta"; mmuae biara nni ho. Ɔyii ntoma no firii ne etire; Akosua Dentaa awu; obesi ntoma no ananmu wɔ ne so. Ɛnkyɛree na maame no san fii afuo no mu bae. Ɔbaa no, na ɔnyɛ (at) hwehwɛ ne abofra, na mmom ɔkɔɔ mukye ho kɔwiee aduannoa. Ɛnna ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔkra, he na Denta wɔ? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Grand Mother, a naduane a me noa de siee hɔ no mpo, ɛda so ara gyina hɔ. " Na maame no kae sɛ, "Me ate. " Afei maame no fii dan mu kɔɔ ahyɛdeɛ mu sɛ ɔrekɔfa ntoma (to betting) na wafiri abɔnten akɔhwehwɛ ne abofra. Berɛ a ɔkɔɔ dan no mu no, na akwanhyia bi da hɔ a ɛyɛ eobuo. Ɔdii ntoma no so, na ɔhunuu sɛ Akosua Dentaa awu. Ɔkae sɛ, "Ɔkra, woka sɛ Dentia afi adi wɔ abɔnten, na seesei a ɔwɔ ha no, wawu? " Ɔkra kae sɛ, "Grand Mother In anhu. " Afei ɔbaa no kyekyere ntoma abɔso wɔ ne asene no so na ɔfaa no (dead) abofra no wɔ nakyi, na ofirii ne ho, na osuu abɔnten, na akuraa no Etitire bae, na ɔkae sɛ, So wo abofra (foff wakyi) na fa sie. Ɔbaa no buae sɛ:

"And I ne abofrakosua dentaa ntam, na me ne no dii akasakasa, me ne no kɔɔ kɔhwɛɛ nsuo-gazer.

Wɔka sɛ, 'Is is A Queen Mother ba.'

Mepɛ sɛ obi kɔ ka sɛ, The Queen Mother's abofra, Siankwa Adamowa abofra, eyi, me abofra, merennyaw no wɔ ha. "

Na ɔtwee ne ho sii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ akuraase bi, na bere a ɔduruu hɔ no, akuraa no Etire kae sɛ, "Fa abofra no fi wakyi na bra ma me nsie no, nhyɛ no den (te sɛ adeɛ). " Nanso ɔbaa no kaa sɛ: "

"And I ne abofrakosua dentaa ntam, na me ne no dii akasakasa, me ne no kɔɔ kɔhwɛɛ nsuo-gazer.

Wɔka sɛ, 'Is is A Queen Mother ba.'

Mepɛ sɛ obi kɔ ka sɛ, The Queen Mother's abofra, Siankwa Adamowa abofra, eyi, me abofra, merennyaw no wɔ ha. "

Na ɔtwee ne ho fii hɔ bio. Ɔante, na ɔforoo, faa kwaebirentuo bi mu, na afei de, abofra no abubu, na ɔde kɔɔ (herback) na ɔsan de kɔɔ fie. Na Etire a ɔwɔ akuraa no bisaa no sɛ, "Ɛhe na abofra no wɔ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "I de no kɔeɛ na metoo no tuo. " Etire a akuraa no Etaire kaeɛ sɛ, "Wo ho adwiriw wo, sɛ woama ho kwan wɔ ha a, anka anka ɛsɛ sɛ yɛsie wɔ nsaman-aman no kwaebirentu no mu, ɛno bɛyɛ nea ɛfata. " Enti, mpanimfoɔ no ka sɛ, "Sɛ wo abofra wu a, ɛnneɛ di awerɛhoɔ wɔ "moderation" mu; mma ɛnntena wo ase. Saa ɔbaa no nam saayɛ so dii dwuma, na ɔtow ne abofra no kɔe bere a na ɛho nhia sɛ ɔyɛ saa no. " Na afei ɔbaa no kae sɛ, "A ma Ɔkra yi, fa no to mpata ne pound mu ma me. " Afei nkuraasefoɔ no kɔɔ hɔ (baabi a Ɔkra wɔ, na nnyigyei bi wɔ hɔ a wɔbɔ no! Pu! Na wɔbubu no kosii sɛ ɔbɛyɛɛ mmerɛw koraa. Na afei na aka "ear ayamye so"; bere a wɔde twitwaa no, na ɛyɛ den, na bere a wotwitwaa no, ɛhuruw kɔtoaa Babadua no so. Ɛno nti na Babadua no wɔ apɔɔ (wɔ ne dua mu no, kan no na ɛyɛ troom, trom, a ɛyɛ torotoro yiye.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom betumi agye nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a wotumi yi me ayɛ (a asɛmka wɔ ho).
