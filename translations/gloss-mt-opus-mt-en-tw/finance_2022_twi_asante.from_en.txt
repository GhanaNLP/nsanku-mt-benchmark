Adwumayɛ man a ɛtumi tena hɔ daa: Sikakora ho nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛhyɛ den ne Adwuma mmeaeɛ

FOREWORD

Covid-19 yaredom no nsunsuansoɔ kɛseɛ no, sikasɛm ahodoɔ a Ghana ka ho nyinaa ate nka. Wɔnam asetena mu nkogu so no, Ghanafoɔ nyaa ohia, asetena kwan ne nnwuma a wɔhwere wɔ Aban no so. Mani gye sɛ mɛka sɛ yɛn mmuaeɛ a yɛde di yareɛdom no nkɛntɛnsoɔ ho dwuma no, ɛyɛ akokoduru na ɛyɛ pintinn na ɛyɛ anigyeɛ wɔ ewiase nyinaa. Aban nso hyɛɛ aseɛ yɛɛ nhyehyɛeɛ de boaa nnwuma na ɔsan de yɛn asetena baa tebea a ɛfata mu.

Afei nso, wɔ mmuaeɛ a wɔde maa nnuhuam ne hokwan ahodoɔ a ɔyaredɔm no de ba no ho no, aban no hyɛɛ H.E. Ɔmanpanyin Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo akandifoɔ pa yɛɛ Nnwumakuo no GH¢ billion COVID-19 Allevation ne Revitation of Nnwumakuo, (GhanaCAREObaatan Pa nhyehyɛeɛ) ase. Eyi ama yɛn nnipa ho atɔ wɔn ntɛmntɛm na wɔn ho atɔ wɔn kɛse na wɔregye yɛn ɔman no.

Yɛn ani sɔ gyidie a wɔkura mu wɔ Aban mu bere mpo a yɛrebɔ mmɔden sɛ yɛbɛma ayaresa a emu yɛ den afi yaredom hɔ no. Saa berɛ yi, wɔsɔɔ yɛn pintinnyɛ a yɛde bɛtena ase sɛ ɔman no hwɛeɛ. Baebolo no ma akwankyerɛ a ɛho hia wɔ mmere a ɛte sɛɛ mu bere nyinaa. Titiriw no, sɛnea wɔaka wɔ Yesaia 9:10 mu no, nansa no ahwe ase, nanso yɛde aboɔ a wɔasen besisi hɔ; wɔatwitwa sikamore nnua no, nanso yɛde sida nnua bɛsi wɔn baabi. Saa ara nso na Aban asi ne bo sɛ ɔbɛyɛ bɔhyɛ no. Wɔ asianeɛ yi ho no, yɛbɛkɔ so de nhyehyɛeɛ ne dwumadi ahorow a wɔasiesie sɛ wɔbɛsan de mmeaeɛ a ayera nyinaa aba na yɛama ɔmanfoɔ abrabɔ gyinabea atu mpɔn.

2022 sika nhyehyɛeɛ no botaeɛ titire baako ne sɛ wɔbɛkyekyere adwumayɛ man a ɛbɛtumi atra hɔ daa a ɛbɛtumi de sikasɛm mu nkɔsoɔ a ɛyɛ den, ɛbɛtena hɔ daa na ɛka obiara ho aba ama adwuma pa ama obiara, titire mmabun. Aban nhyehyɛeɛ ne sɛ, wɔbɛtua wɔn ho ama mmabunu sɛ wɔde wɔn akyɛdeɛ ahodoɔ bedi dwuma na wɔakura wɔn ahoɔden mu de ahwɛ sɛ yiedie bɛfa akwanya pɛpɛɛpɛ so ama obiara.

Aban bɛhyɛ mmabun ahotɔ mu den na wɔakyekye wɔn adwumayɛ honhom, na ɛbɛma YouStart Programme no ayɛ adwuma wɔ 2022 mu de ama mmabun adwumawuranom kwan ma wɔasisi wɔn guadwa anaasɛ wɔatrɛw mu.

Wɔ 2022 sika nhyehyɛeɛ no mu no, yɛayɛ nhyehyɛeɛ sɛ yɛbɛkɔ so adi nsɛnnennen a ɛwɔ hɔ seesei a ɛfa sikasɛm ho no ho dwuma na yɛapue denneennen wɔ bere a atwam no mu. Aban de ne ho ahyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛkɔ akyiri na ɛyɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ mu de ama nimdeɛ ne adwumayɛ akɔ anim wɔ mmabun mu.

Sɛ nhwɛsoɔ no, abusua bi bɛtumi ayɛ sika nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛbɛkyerɛ sika dodoɔ a ɛwɔ hɔ ne sɛdeɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wɔde di kan wɔ nhwɛsoɔ, dan ka, amansan dwumadie nnoɔma ne aduane mu. Sɛ ɛho hia sika pii a, ɛsɛ sɛ abusua no susuw ho sɛ wɔbɛma akatua pii anaasɛ wɔbɛfa bɔ kɛseɛ.

Budget deficit: Eyi ba bere a Aban sikatua sen sika a ɛba.

Capital Expenditure: Eyi yɛ sika a wɔde di dwuma wɔ mpɛndwuma akɛseɛ te sɛ akwan, sukuu ahodoɔ, ayaresabea ahodoɔ, twe, akwantuo, nsuo nhyehyɛeɛ, afifideɛ ne mfidie a ɛkeka ho mu.

Adwumayɛfoɔ a wɔtua wɔn ka: Eyi yɛ akatua ne sika a wɔde boa a ɛfa akatua ho te sɛ asetena bɔne, anisɔ akyɛdeɛ sika ne pɛnhyen a wɔtua ma aban adwumayɛfoɔ.

Nkɔsoɔ ahokafoɔ (DPS): Wei kyerɛ aman ne/anaasɛ ahyehyɛdeɛ a wɔne aman a wɔrenya nkɔsoɔ yɛ adwuma de du wɔn nkɔsoɔ botaeɛ ho. DPS betumi ayɛ aman pii, e.g. Amanaman Ntam Sikakorabea (IMF), World Bank, African Development Bank (AfDB) anaa aman mmienu a wɔka ho ne aman ankorankoro te sɛ Germany, United Kingdom ne United States of Amerika (USA).

Afe awieeɛ nneɛma boɔ kɔ soro: gyinabea a boɔ kɔ soro wɔ wɔ Ɔpɛnimba, afe a ɛwɔ nsuhoɔ mu.

Expendecture Overrun: Eyi ba bere a Aban sikasɛmeɛ boro Sika nhyehyɛeɛ dodoɔ a Mmarahyɛbadwa no apene so no so.

FREE SHS: Free SHS (Ntoaso sukuu a wontua hwee) nhyehyɛe yɛ Ghana Aban a Ɔmanpanyin Nana Akufo-Addo de sii hɔ wɔ Ɛbɔ 2017 mu a ɛbɛma SSHS adesua anya kwan a ahofadi wom ama jHS ani a wɔfata wɔ adesua mu.

Grants: Iyi yɛ sika fam mmoa ahodoɔ a nkɔsoɔ ahokafoɔ ne deɛ ɛnsɛ sɛ Aban tua.

Mmoa sika a wɔde ma Government units afoforo: Saa akatua a wɔyɛ yi a mmara te sɛ District Assemblies Common Fund (DACF), Road Fund, Ghana Nhomasua Ahotoso Foto (GETFUND), Petroleum ho sika a wɔahyehyɛ ne Ɔman apɔwmuden sikakorabea koraa hwehwɛ.

Ɔman mu nnoɔma a wɔyɛ nyinaa boɔ (GDP): Wei kyerɛ boɔ a ɛsom nyinaa nnoɔma a etwa to a wɔhwehwɛ ne ɔsom a wɔyɛ wɔ ɔman no mu wɔ berɛ pɔtee bi akyi.

Amanaman ntam nneɛma akɛseɛ nyinaa: Eyi susuw ɔman bi sika a ɛwɔ amannɔne a ɛbɛtumi atɔ nneɛma ne ɔsom a ɛfiri amammerɛfoɔ aman hɔ wɔ berɛ mu; Ɛsan nso boa ahoɔden a ɔman no mu sika wɔ wɔ abusuabɔ mu ma aman foforo sika ahoroɔ.

Boɔ kɔ soro: mponoɔ dodow a ɛwɔ nneɛma boɔ nyinaa gyinabea wɔ berɛ a ɛhia ɔsom ne nneɛma mu.

IPEP (Infrastructure for Pothiewarding Program): Infrastructure for Positive Toum (IPEP) yɛ NPP aban no flagship programmes no flagudership programme no mu biako a ne botae ne sɛ wobetu ohia ase na ɛde pɛyɛ a enni hɔ koraa aba, titiriw wɔ nkuraase ne mpɔtam a wonni ahotɔ denam nneɛma a wɔde ma wɔ ahotɔ nnoɔma titiriw so.

McKeconomic botaeɛ: Eyi yɛ nsubɔ a wɔde di dwuma de nya sikasɛm mu mmɔdenbɔ. Nhwɛso ahorow bi ne sikasɛm mu nkɔsoɔ, boɔ kɔ soro, nsesaɛ wɔ aman foforɔ, dwadie mu mmɔdenbɔ, sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ ahorow nkonimdi kakra anaa nkogudi ne gyinaeɛ ho a Ghana Sikakorabea a ɛka sika ahwehwɛdeɛ ne nneɛma a wɔde ma wɔ sikasɛm mu no asi.

ADWUMA NKYEKYƐNKETENKETE, NKETEWA NE MFIMFININI (Ketewa ne Nnwuma nketewa ɔsatuo nhyehyɛeɛ): Wɔtaa kyerɛ ketewa-adwumakuo (anaasɛ adwuma ketewa) ase sɛ adwuma ketewaa bi a wɔde nnipa dodow a ɛsua koraa na ɛwɔ akontaabu krataa anaa adetɔn sika a ɛba a ennu pɔtee pɔtee dodow.

NBSSI (Ɔman mu badwa a ɔhwɛ Nnwuma nketewa So): Ɔman mu badwa a ɔhwɛ Nnwuma nketewa so (NBSSI) yɛ ɔman adwumayɛkuo a ɛnyɛ adwuma yie a ɛhyɛ Dwumadiɛ ne Nnwuma Asoɛeɛ ase na ɛyɛ adwuma sɛ wɔbɛhyɛ Ketewwa ne Nnwuma nketewa nkɔsoɔ ne nkɔsoɔ ho nkuran (Adwuma nketewa ne mfinimfini).

Wɔn a wɔnyɛ partic Real GDP: So aba a ɛfiri mu baeɛ nyinaa wɔ sikasɛm mu a ɛnka dwumadi ahodoɔ a ɛwɔ fango adwumayɛ mu, a ɛfa gyinabea a boɔ kɔ soro ho ntawntawntawto.

Sika a ɛfiri tow mu: Aban sika a wɔnya mfi toɔ mu no, nhwɛso ahorow ne tumi krataa anaa tumi nkrataa, dwumadifoɔ sika ne asodi foforɔ.

asɛdeɛ a aka: asɛdeɛ a aka ne MDAfo bɔhyɛ ahorow a ɛfa Sikasɛm Asoɛeɛ ne 2016 akatua a aka a ɛfa Mmara hyɛ sika ho.

Speciy balance : Eyi ne nsonsonoeɛ a ɛda Aban sikaya ne sikasɛm a mfɛntom ka ho (a ɛne ka tua hyia) ntam. Wobetumi asusuw eyi sɛ ɔman mu nnoɔma a wɔyɛ nyinaa boɔ (GDP) no ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu.

Nkɔso dodow a wɔhwɛ kwan: sɛnea Ghana sikasɛm bɛkɔ so anyin wɔ afe bi mu.

Sika a ɛba: Eyi ne sika dodoɔ nyinaa a Aban nya de yɛ ne dwumadi ahodoɔ a ɛfiri mpɔtam ne akyi.

RFJ (Reaving for Aduane and Nnwuma): bɛhyehyɛ mmoa adwuma a akansi ne mmɔdenbɔ wom, ama ɔman mu nnoɔma yɛ akɔ soro, atew nneɛma a wɔde firi amannɔne ba so, aboa ma mmoa nneɛma asetena kwan atu mpɔn, na ama mmoayɛn dwumadie mu adwumayɛfoɔ asetena kwan atu mpɔn.
