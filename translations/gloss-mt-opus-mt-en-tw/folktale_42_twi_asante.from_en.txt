Daboɔ a wɔde palm-winew. Adowa kɔɔ hɔ no, wɔnom palm-wine, na wɔbɛyɛɛ draw; ɔntumi sɔree, na ɔhyɛ aseɛ delm-tre. Wɔ anwummere no nwunu no, Daboɔ no kaa sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwɛ nagyapadeɛ no. Berɛ a ɔkɔeɛ no, na Adowa no anom ne nsa na ɔda hɔ wabow nsa.
