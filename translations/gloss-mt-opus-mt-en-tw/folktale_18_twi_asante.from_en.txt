Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

SƐNEA NNEƐMA A WƆDE DI DWUMA NO BAA ABUSUAKUO MU

Ɔkae sɛ, ɔbɔɔfoɔ bi (ence) kɔɔ nwura no mu, ɔbaa bɛhwɛɛ amena bi mu, na emu na na ɔgyina Onipa, Ɔbarima, Obarima, Osebo, Daaboɔ, ɔwɔ, ɔwɔ, ne Okusie, Okisi. Mprempren berɛ a saa nnipa yi hunuu Ɔbɔfoɔ no, wei kaa sɛ, Ather Ɔbɔfoɔ, ba bɛgye me firi adi. Ɔno na ɔkaa sɛ, Agya, bra bɛgye me. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Mma ha nkɔ ɔkyena, mpo sɛ anka mɛgye wo nkwa, na anka wobɛnya nea wobɛyɛ me (menim) no, ɛnsɛ sɛ me kɔn dɔ. (act so) (act so) Asɛm no ankasa ne sɛ, mebɛyi mo afiri. Na nokware a ɔde yii nnipa no nyinaa fii adi kɔɔ (his) akuraa. Da bi, berɛ a Ɔbɔfoɔ no te hɔ no, Osebo, Daboɔ, de nam pii baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Agya Ɔbɔfoɔ, eyi yɛ aseda ma ayamye a woyɛeɛ no, meda mo ase, Opiafo. Ɔbɔfoɔ no daa no ase na ɔne Daboɔ no kɔeɛ. Wɔ dwumadie foforɔ nso ase no, na Ɔbɔfoɔ no wɔ hɔ, na Okusie, Okisi, baeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ather call Ɔbɔfoɔ, wobɛpɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma bi? Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Ho! Ɛdɛn nti na ɛnnyɛ saa, ɛno ne adeɛ a ɛwɔ mmarima a wɔn werɛ ho hwehwɛ mu. Okisi no sii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ baabi a Nyankopɔn, Sky-god, kuraa ne sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma no. Ɔkɔɔ tutuu ɛtokuro bi wɔ dan no ase, na ɔkɔɔ dan no mu, na ɔkɔgyee Sky-god sika kɔkɔɔ no nyinaa, na ɔde maa Ɔbɔfoɔ no. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Me da wo ase, Akua. " Na Okisi no kɔeɛ. Ɛda foforɔ nso, na ɔwɔ hɔ, na ɔtee sɛ ebinom refɛm no, BERƐ a atwam. Ɔbɔfoɔ no buaa sɛ, "Ame" (come) a ɛwɔ hɔ no. Ɔbarima no baeɛ no, ɔkaa sɛ, Agya Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ, anɔpa pa. (Ɔbɔfoɔ no), buaeɛ sɛ, Aane, wanya adwenemhaw. Iyi yɛ palm-winew kukuo ketewa a medaa mo ase. Agya Ɔbɔmmɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Mempɛ, mani nnye ho; Ɔbarima deɛ, ɛyɛ sɛnea mekaeɛ no ara ne sɛ, anka mebɛyi wo afiri mu; hwɛ nea Daboɔ no de baeɛ no, hwɛ nea Okisi de baa me nkyɛn no. Ɔbarima no de ne mmɛw-winewns no kɔeɛ no. Na kɔduruu hɔ kyerɛɛ Ewiem anuonyampɔn no sɛ, wo sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma a ɛyerae no, akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ saa Oki no bae na ɔde kɔe na ɔde mae maa Ɔbɔfoɔ no. Ewiem-anuonyampɔn no kae sɛ, Is saa na ɔkae? Ɔkaa sɛ, Grandressire, it the nokorɛ pefee. Eome-anuonyampɔn no somaa asɛnkafoɔ sɛ wɔnkɔkyeree Ɔbɔfoɔ no, na wɔbɔɔ no hama soa. Afei bere a wɔne Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔduruu kwan no so no, ɔkae sɛ, Mpanimni, mepɛ sɛ me ho tɔ me. Dabi, ɔfefoɔ no kae sɛ, ɔmmɔmmɔfoɔ, wobɛdwane. Ɔkaa sɛ, Dabi, merendwane, na sɛ wopɛ a, mɛfa hama no akɔhyɛ me asune, abɔ me dusin, na woama me kwan ma menkɔ. Asɛmkafoɔ no tiee no, na wɔakɔ. Ɛhɔ na ɔwɔ, ɔma ne ho tɔɔ no, bere a abɔdeɛ a ɛwɔ hɔ bere tenten no baeɛ. Ɔkae sɛ, Hwɛ ha, me adamfo, gye aduro kɔn ketewa yi to mu, na fa no ka wo ho, na sɛ wokɔ a, na wɔnnyina asɛmfuabl no anim, na ɛba sɛ wobekum wo, me bɛkɔ Ɔhenes harem, sɛ ebia mesera ne yeres ba; makyi a me ne no aka no, wubehu sɛ obiara asɔre, na wo nso woaka, Mewɔ aduro, na sɛ menya aduro a, na sɛ mani gye, na sɛ me ne no bɛba a, mɛkɔ Ɔhene no nkyɛn. Wode bɛsi abofra a ɔbɛsa ne ho. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Me werɛ hyɛ mo, Akua. Ɔkasafoɔ no de Ɔbɔfoɔ no kɔeɛ na ɔbɛgyinaa Sky-god anim, (Bere a ɔne ne Mpɔtam bɔfoɔ (Kronti) Kronti, Akwum, Adonten, Gyase, ne Koydɔm ahemfo anim. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa nasɛm no, na berɛ a Kasafoɔ no sɔre gyaee atɛmmuo no, wɔtee awerɛhow, puei dede! Wɔ ahemfo nkurɔfo fie akyirikyiri. Wɔkaa sɛ, Dɛn na ɛyɛ? Wɔkaa sɛ, ɔwɔ aka yere a ɔdɔ no abofra. Obiara ankɔ. Afei Ɔbɔfoɔ no gyinaa hɔ; ɔkae sɛ, Sɛ me nni deduani a, anka menya aduro (a awuduru ano aduro ma awuduru no. Obi bi a ɔtee na ɔkɔkyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, Go na monnye deduani no mmra ha. Ɔbɔfoɔ no baeɛ no na bɔnefoɔ no baeɛ. Sky-god no kae sɛ, Sɛ wotumi hwɛ eyi a, me abofra no, sɛ onwu a, wode wo etire bɛma wo. Ɔbɔfofoɔ no kaa sɛ, Me ate nne, mate-one. Ɔbɔfofoɔ no kaa sɛ (argain), Nanabarima, nea merebɛkyerɛ wo, sɛ wotumi nya bi a, ɛnde mɛtumi ahwɛ wo abofra no. Wɔkae sɛ, Dɛn na ɛyɛ? Ɔkaa sɛ, Demotion of the Tell-tale to be afrafra the meduced kixed. ine. Wɔkaa sɛ, Ɛhe na yɛbɛnya mogya afi Tell-tale hɔ? Kasamafoɔ no mu baako, a na ɔyɛ ɔnyansani, kae sɛ, Na yɔnkoɔ a ɔbaeɛ no nso, ɔka kyerɛɛ Ewiem-anuonyam sɛ Ɔbɔfoɔ no aba abɛwia Ewiem-nkokoɔ sika no, ɛhe na ɔwɔ? Wɔkaa sɛ, Wo nni mfomsoɔ, wunni mfomsoɔ. Wɔkɔɔ de yɔnkoɔ no bae. Ɔbɔfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Bere a ɔbɛwa ne nsa aka, na ɔde ne mogya kɔ ma me. Sɛ wɔpae mu pɛ a, Ɔbɔfoɔ no de mogya no kɔyam aduro no na ɔde gu ɔhene ba no nan. Apa ara na ɔsɔreeɛ. Wɔkaa sɛ, Sing the Hunters beye. Wɔteɛm sɛ, Ee! Sky-god no kae sɛ, Me fa wo etire na fa no, na kum wo, na twa ne honam, na tow gu afã ahodoɔ no nyinaa. Wɔkum no, na wɔtwa ne honam no ma ɛyɛ nketenkete, na tow ne nyinaa gu. Saa ara na Tell-tales bebree baa abusuakuo no mu.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
