Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƆWEA (THERE-BAR), ABOSOM-AKOTERE (THE ABOOMAKOTERƐ), NE ƆPATUO (THE PATU) BAA WƆN OSU SORONKO HƆ

ƆWEA, Tree-bearer (a ɔwɔ abodin) Kwadupam Kyerers, Ɔpatuo, Patuo ne Abosom-Akotere, Abosomkorɛ (nea ɛne abodin bɔ abira) ------- () da biara, bere a Adube-tree foro kɔɔ soro no, ɔtwɛn wɔ eyi so; da a edi hɔ no, ɔtwɛn wɔn ho wɔn ho; ne da a edi hɔ no. Ɛyɛ da bi a ɛyɛ da bi a ɛyɛ onipa no owuo (ɔhyɛɛ Adobes dwumadie no na ɔhwehwɛɛ Adobe-tree no, ɛhe na obetumi ahu no? Na ɔkɔe sɛ ɔbɛka akyerɛ Treebeare ne Patu no amaneɛbɔ no. Na wɔkae sɛ, Go and call no. Na as-wum no bɔɔ ne nne: Adobe o! Adobe o!

Duabearefo di awerɛhow:

Bere a ɔkɔe no, ɔsan bae; ɔkɔɔ, ɔsan annsane, ɔsan annsane, ɔsan annsane, ɔsane!

Na Patu no afrɛ:

YƐHWƐɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma) na ebia yɛde too hɔ, m! Yɛn sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma) na ebia yɛde too hɔ, m!

Na ɛno ne nsuo a ɛwɔ abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ mmiɛnsa yi mu no mfitiaseɛ.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
