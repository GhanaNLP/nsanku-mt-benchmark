SƐNEA ABIRABƆ YI KYERƐ WƆ ABUSUAKUO HƆ.

Ɛdii bere bi a na obarima bi a wɔfrɛ no Ɔtan-to-contradicted, na ɛno nti, ɔkɔsii akuraa ketewa (a ne nkutoo) na ɔbɛtenatenaa mu. Na abɔdeɛ a wɔfrɛ no Ɔtwee no kɔɔ (visit), na ɔne no kɔɔ tenaa ase wɔ mmɛw bea bi nan. Na mmerɛ no bi hwee ase. Ɔtwee no kae sɛ, "Averyath Ɔtan-to-indicted, you asɛm no abere. " Ɔtan-to-contradicted kae sɛ, "Ɛno ne abɔdeɛ a ɛwɔ abɛn no mu; Bere a abere no, afã ahorow abiɛsa no gyina hɔ prɛko pɛ; bere a wɔrenyin no, wotwa mu, na sɛ mepae mu ma ngo no a, wɔyɛ ngo a ngo ahyɛ mu ma abiɛsa; na mefa ngo no kɔ Akase kɔtotɔ Akase bere; Akase atwa no na ɛde Akase bere ma. Akase atwa na ɛde ma. Me nanbea, a me maame, hena na odi me; sɛ maame di me so a, me na megyina hɔ dedaw. " Ɔtwee no kae sɛ, "A ma ɛno nyinaa, wo di atoro. " Na Ɔtan-to-inducted-tradicted faa poma bi na ɔbɔɔ Ɔtwee no wɔ retire no so, na okum no. Afei abede ketewa no nso baa hɔ. Na ɔne no kɔɔ adiansa-tree no, na saa ara na ɛbae. Na ɛnam so yɛ mmoa no nyinaa. Seesei, Kwaku Ananse, Ananse, kɔɔ hɔ kɔka ne ntoma, na ɔde ne kotokuo no bɔɔ no, ɔtwee no wɔ ne mari so, na ɔtwee ne ho kɔɔ Ɔtan-to-bin-conducted akuraa no ase. Ɔgyee no ka sɛ, "Asak, anɔpa pa. " Ɔtan-to-b-tradicted buae sɛ, "Y'Aku, ne ɛhe na worekɔ? " Obuae sɛ, "Mereba wo nkyɛn. " Na ɔde ne akonnwa no kɔtoo so wɔ mmɛw nsa-tree ase. Ɔtan-to-in-conducted kaa sɛ, "Cook aduane a ɛsɛ sɛ Ananse no di. " Na ɔrewie no, Kwaku Ananse ne Ɔtan-to-contranded na wɔbɛtenaa mmɛw dua no ase. Na nsɛm no bi hwee ase. Na Kwaku Ananse de wɔn kɔtoo ne kotokuo no mu. Na ɔkɔɔ so yɛɛ saa kosii sɛ ne kotokuo no yɛɛ ma. Wɔde aduane no bae, na Kwaku Ananse dii. Bere a odii naduan wiei no, asɛm no bi tɔe, na Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Avery Ɔtan-to-inconducted, you asɛm no abere. " Ɔtan-to-inconducted kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ wɔn abɔdeɛ sɛ wɔbɛgyina hɔ saa; Bere a wɔrenyin no, afã ahorow abiɛsa na ɛnyin prɛko pɛ; bere a wɔredua no, wotwa mu, na sɛ mepae mu ma wotwa ngo no, wɔyɛ nsu-pots abiɛsa a ngo ahyɛ mu ma, na mefa ngo no kɔ Akaks. e, sɛ ɔbɛtɔ Akase atwa a, Akase bere no ba na awoɔ ma me nanbea a ɔdi me maame no sɛ, ɔbɛtumi asoa me wɔ dane. Bere a maame di me so no, na megyina hɔ dedaw. " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Woanni atoro, nea woka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware; me deɛ, me wɔ nruma bi a megyina mafuo mu, na sɛ wɔn abere a, me ne nnua aduɔson nson a ɛnni hwee no bɔ mu kɔduru wɔn nkyɛn, nanso afei mpo wɔn nsa nka, meda makyi, na metumi de me kɔteɛ no ayɛ agudeɛ ama wɔn. Nnipa a wɔtan wɔn no kaa sɛ, "Oh, I understan I, I understantimed d. kosi ɔkyena a, mɛkɔ ahwɛ. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "thidenthieth. " Bere a Ananse no rekɔ (home) no, na ɔwe asɛm a ɔhyiaa no, na ɛda adi wɔ kwan so. Ade kyee anɔpa no, bere a nneɛma fii ase yɛɛ nea wotumi hu, ɛyɛ auwor-to-contracted a wɔde asi hɔ sɛ wɔbɛkɔ Ananse akuraa ase. Mprempren a Ananse no dii kan bae (da a edi kan no), na ɔkɔkyerɛɛ ne mmofra sɛ, Abofra bi bɛba ha, na sɛ ɔsɔre bisa me nsɛm a, ɛsɛ sɛ woka kyerɛ no sɛ, anka mɛfiri baabi bi, bere a me kɔteɛ bɔɔ mbeaeɛ nson, na ɛsɛ sɛ mefa no kyerɛ tomfoɔ ma ɔsiesie, na ɔntumi anwie wɔ hɔ berɛ a ɔanwie no. Bere kɔ so no, ɔka sɛ wawie adwuma no. Ɛnkyɛe biara na Ɔtan-to-boded no bɛkaa ho. Ɔkae sɛ, Ɛhe na wagya akɔ? Wɔbuaa sɛ, AMDA, agya kɔɔ baabi bio, na ne kɔteɛ a ɛbɔɔ no wɔ mmeae ahodoɔ nson no, ɔde kɔɔ togofoɔ bi nkyɛn, nanso wantumi anwie adwuma a ɛwɔ berɛ no, na agya akɔwie, na (yo, Agya) nso, wonhuu mogya a ɛwɔ kwan no so? Ɔtan-to-banded kaa sɛ, "I. " Ɔtan-to-inconducted bisabisaa And, Ɛhe na wo maame wɔ? (Thaidopy ba no buae sɛ, Maame nso, nnansa yi ara ɔkɔɔ asuwa, na anka nnsu-pot no bubui na ɔsɛee esiane sɛ ɔtɔɔ no berɛ ara pɛ na ɔtɔɔ no nti, nanso ɔanwie pii na wasan akɔ da a ɛresan ayɛ saa. Ɔtan-to-contracted no anka hwee. Mprempren Kwaku Ananse duu hɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, Coook aduane bi a Ɔtan-to-inconducted betumi adi no. Bere a mmofra no renoa aduane no, na wɔde atare ketewa baako pɛ na ɛyɛɛ aduane nanso ɛmo dodoɔ kɛse; wɔmaa kwan no so yɛɛ hye pa ara. Na wɔawie no, na wɔde asi hɔ ansa na Ɔtan-to-inconducted no reba. Ɔtan-to-conducted dii. Seesei ɛmo no ayɛ no yaw, na ɔpɛ sɛ owu. Ɔkaa sɛ, Ntikuma (Anansa abofra no, ɔtwee nsuo maa me sɛ metumi anom. Ntikuma kɔɔ nsusuabe no; ɔmfaa nsuo no biara amma da. Ɔtan-to-in-conducted kaa sɛ, Ntikuma, ɛhe na saa nsuo no wɔ? Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Ah, nsuo a yɛwɔ ha (wɔ yɛn nsuo-ku mu) no yɛ ahodoɔ mmiɛnsa; ɛno ne agya a ɛdi kan, sɛ me maame co-yere yere no wɔ mfinimfini, na me maame no yɛ me dea (fi kuku) fam; Na mepɛ sɛ me ne me maame no nsuo yɛ pɛ, sɛ me nsa nka me maame no nsuo a, gye sɛ me werɛ fi Ake ahwɛyie kɛseɛ berɛ a wɔreretwe no, ɛbɛma abusuakuw mu manso. Ɔtan-to-inconducted kaa sɛ, Yout brat, wo ho nna hɔ. Nsɛm ara, Kwaku Ananse kaeɛ sɛ, Beat no maa no wuiɛ. Ɔtan-to-indicted kaeɛ sɛ, Adɛn nti na wɔbɔɔ me ma mewuiɛ? Ananse no kaa sɛ, So woka sɛ ɔtan ne wo bɔ abira, nanso wo ne ebi bɔ abira, ɛno nti na meka sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wɔboro wo so na ama wo bo betumi ayɛ wo dɛ? Enti wɔdii Ɔtan-to-contracted so nkonim ma ɔwuiɛ. Afei Kwaku Ananse bubuu ne honam wɔ asinasin nketenkete mu na ɔde ne ho petepetee so. Ɛno nti na nnipa pii a ɔtan bɔ abira no, wɔrehu wɔn wɔ abusuakuo no mu.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
