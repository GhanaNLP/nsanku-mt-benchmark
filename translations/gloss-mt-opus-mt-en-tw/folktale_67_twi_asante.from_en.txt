Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

KWAKU AANANSE, ANANSE NO, KAA SƐ ƆREKƆ SO KWASA HWEHWƐ MU, BERE A ƐHƆ A ƆNE KWASA NYINAA YƐƐ KWAASE

KKWAKU AANANSE, Ananse no, na ɔte hɔ pɛn, na ɔkaa sɛ ɔpɛ kwae a ɔne no bɛkɔ akɔfa mpataa. Ɔhwehwɛɛ nneɛma nyinaa mu, nanso wantumi annya biako. Afei ohyiaa Osa, Akrɔma. Ɔkae sɛ, Momma yɛnkɔ na yɛnnya mpataa-trals. Akrɔma no buae sɛ, Mewɔ nam (thuod browth) a wobedi. Akyiri yi, ɔne Anane, Kwaakwaaabi, hyiae, na ɔkae sɛ, Momma yɛnkɔ site-trates. Kwaakwaaabi no kae sɛ, I. Mprempren, Akrɔma no kaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ Kwaakwaaabi, bɛyɛ mpataa-twimum adwuma a wo ne Kwaku Ananse rekɔ no, nkɔ, mma Kwaku Ananse sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ kwae, sɛ ɔne kwasea no de mpataa-trax si hɔ. Kwaakwaadabi no buae sɛ, Ɛnsɛ sɛ woka bio, efisɛ mɛhwɛ sɛ me nsa ka sika no nyinaa. Kwaakwaaba ne Ananse a wɔde asi hɔ sɛ nwura no bɛtwa mfehaha no. Kwaakwaaba a wɔka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse no, ma me kekan no na menkɔ na metwa (mith) no, na wadwuma ne sɛ wobɛfa brɛ (restrucing from madwumayɛ mu). Ananse no buae sɛ, So woyi me kwasea; yɛ atwam, bere a wobɛfa brɛ a ɛwɔ hɔ no. Kwaku Ananse yɛ atɛkyɛɛ (pOMALS) berɛ a Anene, Kwaakwadabi no, sii hɔ nwiinwiiɛ. Bere a ɔtwaa (them) no, wɔkyekyeree wɔn (wɔ babaso bi mu). Kwaakwaaba no kae sɛ, Annanse, fa no na fa no, bere a wobɛfa brɛ a ɛwɔ hɔ no. Ananse no buae sɛ, Gout, so wode me kɔ kwasea? Na ɔde wɔn kɔeɛ na ɔde wɔn too ne tenten. Kwawadabi no dii akyire na na ɔregu ahome. Ɛduruu fie no, Kwaakwaababi no kae sɛ, "Ma me ma mpataa-taraps no na wofa me nnwuma no brɛ. " Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "Mma wɔn nni nkae; monyɛ na monkɔfa brɛ no. " Afei Kwaakwadaabi no kɔɔ nakyi na ɔretwitwa nakyi, na ɔrehyehyɛ ahome. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "soya abonds, wose woyɛ adwene, na woda so rewu. " Ananse no wiee mpataa-raps no. Kwaakwaadadabi no kae sɛ, "Ma me soa mpataa-taraps no na fa kɔ nsuo no mu, na fa berɛ a ɛwɔ hɔ no. " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Dabi, me ne wɔn bɛkɔ, na wode brɛ a ɛwɔ hɔ no akɔ. " Wɔduruu nsuo no mu; Kwaakwaadadabi no kaa sɛ, "Asama Ananse, ɛhɔ na aboa kɛseɛ bi wɔ nsuo no mu, nti momma me nnyina nsuo no mu na fa mpataa-trals no, sɛ aboa kɛseɛ no nka me a, wode owuo no bɛkɔ hɔ. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Ah, wode me kɔ ɔkwasea ankasa; me ara na mede afidie no besi hɔ, na sɛ ɔbɔ me a, wobɛfa owuo no wɔ hɔ. " Kwaku Ananse faa apataa-afidie no de sii nsuo no mu, na wɔbaa fie. Ade kyee anɔpa, bere a nneɛma bɛda adi no, na wɔkɔ hwehwɛ afidie no mu, na wɔakyere apataa mmienu. Kwaakwadabi no kaa sɛ, "Ananse, woyi mmienu yi, na ɔkyena nso, na (afidie no akyere anan, me dane kɔfa wɔn. " Ananse no yii ano sɛ, Saa yɔnkoɔ yi! Fa me kɔ ɔkwase ankasa; wode mmienu yi kɔ, na kɔ ɔkyena, me dane no, me kɔfa anan no. Kwawadadabi nso de wɔn kɔeɛ. Anɔpa a edi hɔ no, wɔkɔɔ kɔsraa afidie no, na wɔnyaa anan (apataa). Kwaakwaaba no kaa sɛ, "Wokɔfa nnan yi, na ɛberɛ nso a ɔkyena a yɛfiri hɔ akɔ nwɔtwe no, ne da a yɛbɛfa wɔn. " Ananse no yii ano sɛ, "Mofa me kɔ kwasea; mowɔ nnan yi, na mɛkɔ ɔkyena nso a, me nso mebɛfa awotwe no. " Afei anɔpa (kɔɔ afidie no kɔsraa) na wɔnyaa awotwe. Kwaakwadabi no kaa sɛ, "Sak (them) ne to ɔkyena a, me nso mebɛfa dunsia no. " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Mogye me kɔ kwasea; so wofa awotwe no, na kɔ ɔkyena a mewɔ dunsia no. Eduu saa bere yi no, mpataa-taxs no ayɛ nea ɛfata, na Kwawadadabi no kaa Ananse no ho asɛm, na ɔkae sɛ, Go na fa saa mpataa a aporɔw yi kɔ, na sɛ wɔtɔn biako a, wubenya ntanu (i e. (£16) ma one biara (trap), saa na woyɛ, Agya Ananse, fa saa apataa yi, na fa apataa a aporɔw no na fa ma me. Ananse no kae sɛ, Dabi, fa apataa no kɔ kɔtɔn, na me de apataa a ɛakyinkyim no bɛkɔ akɔfa mfasoɔ (them-trals). Wɔde wɔn nneɛma kɔhyɛɛ dwaso. Ntɛm ara na Kwaakwadawa no yii ne nneɛma nyinaa guu hɔ; onyaa sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma pii. Nanso, na aka Ananses (nneɛma) no. Afei Kwaakwaaba no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, FA saa mpataa a aporɔw yi ne hama wosow no na wɔntɔ. Ananse no maa wɔn so; ɔkae sɛ, I retɔn mpataa-trals a aporɔw! Mprempren akuraa no panyin te asɛm no, na ɔkae sɛ, Ɛhe na yɔnkoɔ yi fi ba, na wɔkɔ frɛ no ba. Ananse no kɔeɛ. Ɔpanyin no kaa sɛ, So woka sɛ wodi akuraa a ɛwɔ ha no nkamfoɔ; afei wadamfo (here) baeɛ na wobɛtɔn apataa (a ɛwɔ mfasoɔ) nanso woanhyɛ no nsow, nanso wobɛpam apataa-taxs ama yɛn? head-man no kae sɛ, Flog. Enti wɔboroo no. Afei Kwaakwaaba no kae sɛ, Spider, woahunu sɛ, waniwa awu aniwu; wose wo ne wo reyɛ hwehwɛ kwae bi ho adwuma, na woahu sɛ wankasa na woyɛ kwasea.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
