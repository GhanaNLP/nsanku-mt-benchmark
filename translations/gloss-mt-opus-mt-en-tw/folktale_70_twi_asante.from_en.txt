Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

ABISAA

Na ɛyɛ mmeranteɛ mmiɛnsa; wɔn nyinaa dɔ baaayewa baako. Mprempren saa mmeranteɛ mmiɛnsa yi gyaw Bondonuku hɔ kɔɔ Oguaa, kyerɛɛ sɛ wɔre ɔbɛtɔ nneɛma. Bere a wɔduruu Oguaa no, na wɔn mu biako wɔ ahwehwɛ. Ɔfaa ahwehwɛ no, na oyii honam ani adan (ahaban asiane) bi de guu ahwehwɛ no so, na ɔhwɛɛ mu. Ohuu sɛ wɔn dɔfo awu, na ɔda mpa a ɛso yɛ kusuu mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Nnamfoɔ, yɛn dɔfo awu. " Wɔn mu baako kae sɛ, "Mebɛyɛ yɛn ma yɛadu Bondon ntɛm ara sɛ ebia yɛbɛkɔ asie no. " Nea odi hɔ no nso, ɔkae sɛ, "Sɛ wotumi du hɔ a, mɛnyan no ntɛm ara. " Seesei deɛ, nea ɔda aduro no ano no, ɔkɔtoo adduro, na wɔde no too wɔn nkotodwe so, na wɔde sii hɔ pɛ. Foforo nso kɔɔ na ɔnyaa aduro de guu kɔbere tiawa mu; na ɔde ne nantwi ne ne dua kɔhyɛɛ aduro no mu, na ɔde funu no fraa mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Funu, guare; fun, guare, na ɛma ɛyow. " Seesei nnipa baasa yi mu biako, na wɔn mu bɛn na wɔyɛ papa sen biara?
