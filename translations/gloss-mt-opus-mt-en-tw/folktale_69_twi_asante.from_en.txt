Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBƐYƐ WƆ BERƐ A SIKAKORABEA BI, GAREDEN-EGS, ROWROW ALONG FAM, W BERE A NKRUMA, NKRUMA, GYINA HƆ MA KOSI BƆƆL

GARDEN-EGS ne Nkruma tenaa ase dan baako, fa a wɔde nwura a wɔprapraa so no, fa a na wɔde yɛ nna-beaeɛ. Nea ɛsɛ sɛ wɔyɛ biara, wɔyɛɛ no saa dan biako mu. Mprempren Garden-eggags ka kyerɛɛ Nkruma sɛ, "M'ani awu wɔ ɔman yi ho, enti yɛnkɔ nni ɛtokuro bi, na yɛmfa nwura a ɛwɔ mu no nhyɛ mu. " Nkruma kaa sɛ, "I not go go agged it)". Garden-gress de too hɔ na wɔtu amena bi. Bere a ɔtutuu no no, Nkruma teɛɛ no so, na Garden-eggags san buae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Lift the ɛtokuro no mu, na fa ba sɛ ebia mɛhu. " Nkruma buae sɛ, "Ah! " Ɛfiri bere a Ɔbɔɔdeɛ no bae no, woahu sɛ onipa bi ama ɛtokuro bi so? " Nkruma kae sɛ, "Sɛ woannye wo ano a, anka me bɛba abɛpam wo. " Garden-egggs kae sɛ, "Afei na monya ɔnneɛ, de nsuo gu me so, na mema no so (ɛtokuro) na mema no so. " Nkruma buae sɛ, "Mogye me kɔ kwasea, ɛfiri sɛ yɛte ha sɛ abusuakuo, yɛde sɛdeɛ bi na ɛduru. Ná Nkruma aka baabi a ɔte (sitting) na ɔkɔɔ ɛtokuro no so kɔbɔɔ Garden-gress. Afei, bere a ɔreboro no, Sky-god no tee, na ɔsomaa Kɔkɔsrayi, Ɔpɛpɛ sɛ ɔmfa wɔn baanu no mmra. Wɔn baanu no kɔɔ. Garden-gress kaa nsɛmfua a ɛwɔ nano; Nkruma na ɔkasae. Mprempren, Ewiem-anuonyampɔn no ama nahokafoɔ anya atɛmmuo atia Nkruma, ne wɔ Garden-gress adom mu. Ɛno nti, sɛ yɛyɛ afifideɛ a, ɔda fam hɔ, ɔda Ewiem-anuonyam ne ne mpɔtam mmapɔmma ase. Na Nkruma deɛ, ɔgyina soro pii; ɔka sɛ ɔne Erome-anuonyam bɛko ɛfiri sɛ ɔmaa atɛnkyea.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
