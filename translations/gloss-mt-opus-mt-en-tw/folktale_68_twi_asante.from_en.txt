Ɛnkyerɛ ankasa sɛ, yɛnkyerɛ ankasa (sɛ yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware).

SƐNEA KWAKU AANANSE, ANANSE NO KYERƐ NO, BƐYƐƐ POPOR

KKWAKU AANANSE, Ananse, de ne ho kɔhyɛɛ hɔ kɔɔ Nyankopɔn, Ewiem-anuonyampɔn, na ɔkaa sɛ, Ma me aburoo aba baako na mema mo nyinaa yɛ nnipa akuraa (wɔ nsesaeɛ mu). Ewiem-anuonyampɔn no kae sɛ, Morentumi nyɛ yiye. Ananse buae sɛ, Merentumi nyɛ hwee, me maame, Nasia, nka ntam, afei metua mmara toɔ a me ne me etire wɔ no. Ewiem-anuonyam no kaa sɛ, Receive it (Rea aburoo nnɔbaeɛ aba) na ɛbɛyɛ nkunimo nnɔbaeɛ aba ne ntam. Ananse no bɔɔ dawuro kɔɔ akuraase bi. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔmanpanin no asɛm a ɛdi hɔ. Sky-god no asoma me; ɔka sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ mefa saa akuro yi aba baako na me ne no kɔ baabi, na sɛ meba (anyinam) a, ɛsɛ sɛ mema aburo nna nnɔbaeɛ aba yi ne nkokɔ. Sɛ nkokɔ ne aburoo da bom a, ɛbɛyɛ yie anaa? Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, Ne nyinaa ɛsɛ sɛ woyɛ de ma wɔn kwan ma wɔda. Ɔman ti no kae sɛ, Go and si hɔ. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ ɔrebɛhwehwɛ no, nanso na aburoo (aba baako) no nni hɔ bio. Ananse no kaa sɛ, Head-man, nkokɔ no adidi Sky-gods atoko no. head-man no kae sɛ, Merentumi nni eyi ho mansotwe biara, enti fa anwoma no kɔ. Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ kɔfaa akɔkɔɔ kɛseɛ bi, na wɔde too gua maa akuraa foforɔ. Bere a ɔduruu hɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔman a ɛwɔ anim no sɛ, Ewiem-anuonyampɔn no asoma me, na me bɛda ha, na saa anwoma yi a ɛsɛ sɛ me ne odwan no da no. Ɔman panin no kaa sɛ, "Thinids and bubuut it to baabi. " Wɔ anadwo no, Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ nguanban kɔkum anwoma no. Ɔfaa anwoma no na ɔkuraa odwennini kɛse bi nsɛm ho. Ɛda a ɛdi kan no, ɔkɔɔ head-man a ɛwɔ hɔ no kae sɛ, "Ma me anomaa. " Bere a anka wɔbɛkɔ akɔbɔ no, na aboa no awu. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Ah! Ɛno ne Sky-god maa a odwan no akum no no. " Ɔpanyin no kae sɛ, "Metumi nni ho mansotwe biara, enti gye odwan na kɔ. " Kwaku Ananse faa odwan no na ɔtwee ne ho sii hɔ. Ɔduruu akuraa foforo. Ɔkɔɔ ɔmanpanin no fie.head-man no kae sɛ, "What report? " Ananse no buae sɛ, "The Sky-gods somaa me, na me kuraa ahowan no; na anantwi no ne wɔn ho wɔn ho bɛda. " Ɔman no panyin no kae sɛ, "Ho!! Momma yɛn nkae odwa no. " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Dabi, wei ne Sky-god no nguanten soronko, ɛgyina anantwi no so. " Petrore no kae sɛ, "G na fa si hɔ, ɛnneɛ. " Ananse no kɔtoo hɔ. Ɛwɔ anadwo no, Kwaku Ananse kɔkum odwan no, na ɔde ne mogya sii anantwi no tenten so. Ɛda a ɛdi kan no, Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Head-man, ma me odwan. " Wɔn kɔɔ anantwinini hɔ, na ɛyɛ odan a wɔawuwu. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Head-man, it the Skygod-god no odwa a anantwi no awu no. " Titenani no kaa sɛ, "Metumi ne mo nni mansotwe biara wɔ saa asɛm yi ho, enti meyi nantwi a ɛkuma no no kɔ. " Kwaku Ananse de nantwi no kɔe, na ɔtwee ne ho kɔɔ akuraa foforo ase. Ogyee nantwi no na ɔhyɛɛ no den, na ɔdaee. Bere a ɔda hɔ no, wɔbaa bɛkaa kyerɛɛ ɔman a ɛwɔ anim no sɛ ne nkoa no mu biako awu. Head-man no kaa sɛ, "Wotwa no to, ɛfiri sɛ me nni adaka no. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, mepɛ, ma Sky-god no, sɛ me nsa ka funu a wɔrebɛpam no, sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ me sesa no ma nantwi. " nkuraasefoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Dabi. " (Bt) Kwaku Ananse (intisted), reka sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ wode ba me nkyɛn, na sɛ wotɔn nantwi a wunya no a, wobɛnya sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. £4), na wotumi fa (yi dodoɔ) na wotɔ odonkɔ foforɔ. " Nnipa no kae sɛ, "nokware wɔ mu. " Nti na wɔnyaa nsesaeɛ no. Kwaku Ananse na ɔyii funu no sii hɔ, na ɔbaa akuraa foforɔ ase. Bere a ɔduruu hɔ no, ɔkasa kyerɛɛ ɔbarima a ɔda ano no sɛ, "Permi a abofra yi kɔ ne wo mmofra da wɔ pato yi (abrannaa dan a ɛbue) mu; seesei (m) abofra yi nyɛ fɛ, ɔwɔ suban ahyɛ mu sɛ ɔrebɔ mframa anadwo, enti sɛ ɔbɔ mframa a, momma mmofra no mmɔ no so. " Mprempren ɔbarima no awu nna abiɛsa. mmofra no ne ɔno (fun) da hɔ. Afei deɛ, wɔtee nka sɛ adeɛ bi retwa nsuo. Wɔkae sɛ, "Saa abofra no abubu mframa, ma yɛn nni no so nkonim. " Wɔhwee no so, titim! tim! (wa wɔn akontotu ahodoɔ no nnyigyei). Afei anɔpatutuum no, Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ hɔ kɔtoo ne abofra no. Bere a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, na wawu. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Head-man, wo mmofra akum Ewiem ɔman panyin a na ɔka me ho no. " Titenani no kae sɛ, "Merentumi nni asɛm yi ho asotwe biara, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ na woatumi ayi mmofra no afi hɔ. " Ananse no buae sɛ, "Morentumi nnye ntom gye sɛ wo, wo ti, ne wo nnipa a wɔwɔ kuro yi mu nyinaa kɔ Ewiem-anuonyampɔn anim, ka wasɛm. " Ɔman panin no bɔɔ dawuro no, na ɔkasa kyerɛɛ sɛ, "All of All," na ɔbɔɔ no dawuru. Ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ ansa na Ewiem-anuonyampɔn oper no akɔ, akɔka yɛn asɛm. " Ananse no na ɔdii kwan no anim; nnipa no nyinaa dii nakyi. Bere a Kwaku Ananse duu kuro no kuro akyi no, ɔsomaa Emirays sɛ ɔrebɛbɔ anuonyampɔn sɛ ɔne kuro bi nnipa bi reba. Ewiem-anuonyampɔn frɛɛ ne asɛmbil no, na ɔtenaa ase (Wɔ nahemfiefo). Bere a Kwaku Ananse rekɔsɔre no, ɔforoo (ne dwom): "Kwaku Ananse, Alalas, nyaa aburoo aba baako sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔpɛ anomaa.

Kwaku Ananse, alas, mefaa anomaa no kɔfaa odwan.

Mefaa odwan no kɔgyee nantwi, megyee nantwi no kɔgyee funu, megyee funu no na me nyaa kuro bi nnipa. "

Wɔn mu biara kaa sɛ, "Wote dwom a Kwaku Ananse reto no? " Kwaku Ananse maa ne dwom so bio:

"Kwaku Ananse, alas, me na menyaa aburoo aba baako sɛ menkɔ akonya anwoma.

Kwaku Ananse, alas, mefaa anomaa no kɔfaa odwan.

Mefaa odwan no kɔgyee nantwi, megyee nantwi no kɔgyee funu, megyee funu no na me nyaa kuro bi nnipa. "

Kwaku Ananse duu Sky-god anim, na Sky-gogod bisaa Kwaku Ananse amaneɛbɔ no. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Me nsa ka kuro bi aba baako, na mekae sɛ me ne tefoɔ bɛba kuro bi tete; kuro bi nnipa wɔ hɔ a wɔregyina hɔ. " Skyky-god kae sɛ, "Tlang him. " (Wɔse, ") E! e! " Sky-god kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mede ma wo sɛ aseda? " Ananse no kae sɛ, "Mempɛ hwee; (baabi) wɔ aprannaa so no nyinaa yɛ me pɛ. " Skyky-god kae sɛ, "Oh, OHIA!! " Saa asɛm yi (mefoy) wu wɔ ohia, wo a wokɔɔ bebree no, nnya nkaeɛ biara. " Ɛno nti na ohia remfa Kwaku Ananse ntwa mu da.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
