FOREWORD

Akyinnye biara nni ho sɛ afe yi bɛkɔ so ayɛ asɛnnennen a ɛwɔ yɛn ɔman no sikasɛm abakɔsɛm mu no mu biako. Te sɛ ewiase ahomegyeɛ no, Kovid-19 yareɛ a ɛtrɛ wiase nyinaa bɔɔ yɛn wɔ afe 2020 nan mu baako, a ɛmaa sikasɛm dwumadie mu brɛooyɛ baeɛ, na ɛmaa yɛn sika a wɔnya no sɛee na yɛbuu sika a ɛbɛka ho a ɛne sɛ yɛreko atia yaredom no ho akontaa.

Bere a yɛsusuw ho no, na yɛreyɛ kɔ anim paa wɔ yɛn ayaresa mu wɔ nsunsuanso bɔne a ɔyaredɔm no de aba no mfitiaseɛ wɔ yɛn nsakraeɛ nhyehyɛeɛ no dwumadie no mu no, wɔde dwumadi ahodoɔ bi a ɛboro berɛ no so bɔɔ yɛn, na ɛsɛee sikasɛm kɛseɛ nkonimdiɛ a yɛkɔɔ so kuraa mu firi 2017 kɔsi 2019. Nea edi kan no, Russia ne Ukraine ɔko no, na afei wɔakyɛ wɔ sika a wɔboaboa ano akwan atitire 2022 sika nhyehyɛeɛ no mu, na ɛde mfeɛ du a atwam no, berɛ a ɛdi kan no, wɔtoo Amanaman ntam kɛseɛ no mu, ɛmaa ɔman mu sika a wɔde boa tebea no yɛɛ den na bosegye boɔ kɔɔ soro, na ɛmaa ɛka tua a emu yɛ duru ne sika boɔ a ɛrepere ntɛmntɛm wɔ sidi no ho.

Enti, bere a amannɔne sika mmoa a efi dwetɛ gua so ne yɛn amannɔne sika so no so tew mpo no, nnoɔma a wɔde ba man mu ho ka kɔ soro kɔɔ soro fi yɛn akɔnnɔ a ɛnnɔɔso da a ɛda adi ma nnoɔma a wɔde firi amannɔne ba sɛ ɔman no, na ɛka a wotua ho ka a ɛrekɔ soro no sɛe yɛn sika no mfasoɔ bio. Eyi ama yɛn nyinaa atra ase boɔ a ɛyɛ den sɛ Aban ne ankorankoro. Fango bo a ɛkɔ soro ama nkɔanim aba wɔ nneɛma ne ɔsom dodow no ara boɔ mu, na boɔ kɔ soro wɔ nea ɛkorɔn a ɛyɛ 12.4% wɔ Ahinime 2022 mu.

Wɔasɔ yɛn ahwɛ sɛ nnipa, na Aban de wo akɔ akyiri paa wɔ nhyɛsoɔ a ɛboro so yi ho. Yɛpɛ sɛ yɛda yɛn aseda sɛ Aban ma yɛn kyɛfafoɔ, a Mmarahyɛbadwa, Employers Association, Adwumayɛfoɔ nkabom, Ɔmanfoɔ fekuo, Nyamesom ahyehyɛdeɛ, Ghana nnwumakuo Fekuw, Ghana Aguadifoɔ ka ho.

NNANIAM

1. 2023 Citizens Budget no yɛ mmara a ɛkyerɛ nea Aban ayɛ wɔ afe 2022 mu ne sika a wɔnya de di dwuma ne nhyehyɛeɛ ne dwumadie ahorow. 2023 sika nhyehyɛeɛ no asɛmti ne "Revering and Sustainment and Sustainy for Nyini a ɛka obiara bom ne Mfasoɔ a wɔde ka ho".

2. Wɔ mfeɛ no mu no, sikasɛm nhyehyɛe foforo biara kura nnwuma foforɔ dwumadiwura a wɔde bɛhyɛ aseɛ wɔ nnwuma a ɛrekɔ so no ka. Ɛnyɛ sɛ eyi de nhyɛsoɔ ba yɛn sika nsɛm so nko, na mmom, abakɔsɛm mu no, ɛde ntua ba ka a aka a ɛma dwumadi ahodoɔ ne nyinaa boɔ sɔre berɛ a sikatua a wɔatwerɛ so no twetwe mfasoɔ boɔ.

3. Wɔ 2023 mu no, Aban de nhyehyɛeɛ foforɔ ne dwumadi ahorow bedi dwuma, nanso ɛbɛhyɛ amammerɛ a ɛne sɛ wɔbɛwie dwumadi ahorow a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw no nyinaa nkuran.
