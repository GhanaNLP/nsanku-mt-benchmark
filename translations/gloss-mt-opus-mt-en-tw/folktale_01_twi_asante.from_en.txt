SƐNEA ƐBƐYƐ NA ƆBONTOYA, MPATWA KETWA NO, ABA "WALKING" WƆ BASABASA HO WƆ NSUO NO MU

WƆBƆƆ sɛ na Old-grand maame wɔ hɔ, na nagyapadeɛ no yeraa. Ɔkaa sɛ ɛyɛ "nsuo mu mmoa" na ɛde no kɔɔ (nneɛma no. Na kɔɔtɔ no te hɔ, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Ah, woabɛma yɛn aniwa awetroop (in aniwu), na nea yɛhyɛ nyansa sɛ yɛbɛkyerɛ mo nea ɛwɔ yɛn retire no mu ne sɛ: kɔ na fa kɔbere dadeɛ na hyɛ mu ma (our) nsuo, na nea ɔntumi nsuo nhyɛ no ma no, ɛno na ɛde nneɛma no akɔ. "

Ɔde kɔbere dadeɛnne no bae. Okoto, kɔɔtɔ no, osuuo na ɛhyɛɛ no ma; wɔyii no adi; Mmborɔhunu baeɛ na ɔresu; Okawa baeɛ sɛ ɔresu; Oyoyo, ɔwɔ-nsuo, baeɛ, na osuu; Otidi baeɛ na osuuu; Ɔboko, mpɔtɔ, baeɛ, na osuo, Akɔrereeɛ baeɛ, Ohiiri baa hɔ na osuo; Ɔkɔkɔbɔ baeɛ, nsuo mu mmoa no nyinaa baa hɔ a ɔresu. Awiei koraa no, Ɔbontoya, Mpatawa ketewa no, baa nsuo, wannya nsuo amfi naniwa so; Nti, wɔbuu no sɛ Ɔbontoya abɛfa (nneɛma no.

Nanso, Old-woman-grand Motherak kae sɛ, "Merenyɛ wo hwee. " Ɛhɔupon, abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no nyinaa kae sɛ, "Yɛn deɛ, yɛbɛdi no so, ɛfiri sɛ ɔde yɛn ahyɛ aniwu. "

Bere a wɔadi nade awie no, Old-wonder-grand Motherak kae sɛ, "Megyaw wo hɔ (foreign biara) sɛ mebɛto wo mu wɔ adeyɛ a enni ano ho; obiara mpɛ sɛ onya biribi yɛ tia wo. "

Eyi ne asianeɛ a ɛwɔ hɔ sɛ, sɛ mmaa "go bring" (for apataa) a, wɔhunu Ɔbontoya nkyinkyim (mprempren) wɔ ha ne ɛhɔ.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
