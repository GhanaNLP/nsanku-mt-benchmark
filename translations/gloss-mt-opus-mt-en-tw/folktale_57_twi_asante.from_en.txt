MMOFRA-KWASI-GYINAMMOA

ƐDII da bi a na ɛyɛ abofra, Kwasi Gyanama, na ɔwɔ nuammaa awotwe, na emu biako nni ɔhokafoɔ, enti wɔyii wɔn ho adi sɛ wɔrekɔ baabi akɔhwehwɛ okununom hwehwɛ. Child-Kwasi-Gyanmoa kae sɛ, Me ne wo bɛkɔ. Wɔn mu panyin no kae sɛ, Momma ɔne yɛn nkɔ, na bere a yɛadu (well) keteke kwan so no, na yɛn mu duru tumi twe yɛn. Wɔyiyi wɔn ho, na Child-Kwasi-Ginyama kɔ (wɔn a wɔde wɔn ka ho). Wɔbaa asubɔnten bi so, na emu fa bi te sɛ dɔte fitaa; ebi yɛ dɔte kɔkɔɔ; ebi nso yɛ dɔte kɔkɔɔ; na ne fã bi (te sɛ) dɔteɛ tuntum. Wɔteɛm, Eh, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ na yɛatwa saa asuwa yi? Child-Kwasi-Gyanama de ne ho (magic) thin-tail-switch, bɔɔ nsuo no, na wɔyɛɛ nsuo no mu kyekyɛɛ mmienu, na wɔnam mu kɔɔ so. Wɔ baa kuro bi mu; na ɛyɛ kuro kɛseɛ paa. Child-Kwasi-Gyanama kaa sɛ, kuro no yɛ din (kyerɛ sɛ relief ma babadua nnomaa su), ɛnni mu onipa? Mprempren Anwoma bi akasa, akyerɛ sɛ, Obi wɔ fie hɔ a, ɔkɔ so. Wɔkɔɔ so, na aberewa nso, na ne retire gye nani wɔ ne anan so, na ɔrehwehwɛ ntontom. Bere a ohuu child-Kwasi-Gyanama no, afei ɔmaa ne retire so de sii ananmu; ɔkae sɛ, Me mmanana, dɛn nti na sɛ obi bɛba ha a, na woaba? Child-Kwasi-Gyanama buaa sɛ, Me nuammɔ awotwe ni, na wɔnnya okununom a wɔne wɔn bɛbɔ, na wɔaka sɛ wɔbɛba akɔhwehwɛ okununom a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔware.  Na child-Kwasi-Gyanama nso kɔeɛ ne aberewa. Ɛduruu anadwo no, aberewa no bɔɔ ne se, kɔkɔɔ fan, bɔɔ ahyɛdeɛ sɛ ɔne wɔn bɛkɔ akokum mmabaa no. child-Kwasi-Gyanama kaa sɛ, E! aberewa no kaa sɛ, Dɛn na ɛyɛ? Child-Kwasi-Gyanama buae sɛ, Bere a mewɔ mankasa me akuraa, na sɛ meda ahomegyeɛ nanso mtumi nna a, na maame twitwa slicee a ɛyɛ bayere no de gu ogya mu, na ɔde kora a ɛtrrokuro wom kɔ, na ɔkɔ de nsu gu mu ma me nom. lume_ a ofi aberewa no akɔnnɔ mu no faa wɔn de wɔn guu ne nuabea so. Bere a meresan afi nsuo mu aba no, ɔkaeɛ sɛ, Mebɔɔ mmɔden sɛ mepete nsuo (wɔ saaneɛ no mu), nanso mantumi anpete. Child-Kwasi-Gyanama buaa sɛ, Go na ɔnya atɛkyɛ bi, na mefaa nhaban de guu mu (sɔhwɛɛ no), na megyee no de guu mu. Ná aberewa no kɔyɛɛ saa, na ɔguu nsuo a ɛwɔ mu no ho fĩ, na na ne nyinaa yɛ pɛ. Ɔde baa no, na ɔde bayere no yii ogya no, na ɔde maa Child-Kwasi-Gyanama dii, na ɔsaa nsuo no nso. Afei ɔtow ne ho too fam na ɔdae. Amald-grand maame sɔree; ɔkae sɛ, Ɛyɛ me se. Ɔde ne kɛse nyinaa bɔɔ mu, ɔdane kɔkɔɔ, na ɔde wɔn fii dan no mu kɔɔ mu; ɔde ne nsa ka mmofra no akɔnnɔ, na nea ɛwɔ kɔn no mu dokum_suman no nni nokware no, ɔde ne se hyewee. Saa kwan yi so na ɔkum mmofra awotwe no. Na onnim sɛ ɛyɛ ne mmofra ankasa na wɔakum no. Bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, na ɛyɛ nea ɛda adi kɛse, na ɔpanyin paa, na Twenty-grand maame fii adi kɔɔ afuw no mu. Child-Kwasi-Ginyama nso kɔkaa ne nsam kyerɛɛ ne nuamma no na ɔmaa wɔn guanee. Formal-grand maame fi afuw mu. Child-Kwasi-Ginyama kae sɛ, Old-graminax! Manald-granger e! Mana a na anka woyɛ kwasea no, anka wobɛkum wo mmofra anaa? Me de, me ne me nkwaa nyinaa kɔ. Old-grand maame kae sɛ, Ɛnyɛ nsunsuansoɔ biara, nea ɔnim (sɛnea wɔbɛdi wo ho dwuma) ne baabi a ɛwɔ hɔ. Ɔfii hɔ, na Oldwon-grand maame nso dane ne ho yɛɛ no ababaawa fɛfɛ, na ɔkɔɔ Child-Kwasi-Ginyama akuraa ase. Ɔkaa sɛ, Obiara a ɔbɛtumi ayɛ agyan de ahyɛ nan ase atadeɛ a ɛyɛ borɔdɔma yi mu no, mɛware no. nnipa a na wɔwɔ akuraa no so nyinaa de wɔn ho bɛhyɛ mu, nanso na wɔntumi nyɛ. Child-Kwasi-Gyanama de akokoduru bɔɔ no hu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me ne you. " Child-Kwasi-Ginyama kaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, merennyaw wo. " Enti ne wofa no ware no. Ɔne no kɔɔ nna, nanso ɔboroo naniwa abien no, na afei ababaayewa kaa sɛ, "Child-Kwasi-Ginyama hɔ! Me mmati ayɛ me mmofra aniwa mmienu na me ne wɔn rekɔ. " Abofra-Kwasi-Gyamoa buae sɛ, Fa wɔn na fa wo ho, ma nea onim (sɛnea wɔne wo bedi dwuma) no, yɛ baabi a ɛwɔ hɔ. Childa Afei, na child-Kwasi-Gyanama nim sɛ Old-angwo badwaa kɛseɛ ne _enceint_, enti ɔno nso nyaa koraa na ɔbɔɔ no wɔ ne yafunu so, na ɔbɔɔ ne ntoma no so, na ɔtwee ne ho kɔsii Old-grand maame akuraa ase. Bere a ɔduruu kuro no awieeɛ no, osuuo. Old-woman-grand maame kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ asɛm? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Me ne me kunu na ɛreba, na ɛberɛ a ɔduruu bepɔw a ɔhwee ase no so na ɛmaa naniwa tɔɔ fam no, ɛmaa nabam buiiɛ mmienu. " Old-wonder-granda maame kae sɛ, "Ɛno deɛ, ha na ɛyɛ ɔsɛmmɔnedifo a na waba bio no ani benkum, child-Kwasi-Ginya papa nua, ne ne nifa ani no. Sɛ wose wkunu gyina hɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ woka sɛ, Maniwa so da hɔ, maniwa nna hɔ. ƐnkyɛE biara na Child-Kwasi-Gyanama nsa aka wɔn, sene nea ɔkaeɛ sɛ, Yora area-gragrame, this yɛ me wɔfase no aniwa na me ne wɔn kɔ. - Gyanama baa fie, na ɔde aniwa no sii wɔn ananmu wɔ ne wofa kanyae afidie bea no mu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Maniwa so te, maniwa da hɔ," na ne wofa ho aniwa da hɔ pefee. Afei anɔpa, bere a nneɛma bɛda adi no, na mmofra (wɔ akuraa no mu) nyinaa kɔdware wɔ adwa no mu. child-Kwasi-Gyanamoa kaa sɛ, "Mening moment. " Wɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Ebia wanya awotwaa dua? " Bere a wɔkaa saa no, na ne nufuɔ no mu ayɛ duru, na ɔda adagyaw. Ɛnkyɛree biara na ɔhuruw kɔɔ nsuo no mu sen Old-grande maame, mmienu! Ɔkaa sɛ, "Child-Kwasi-Gyanama, manya wo," na ɔde no kɔeɛ kɔɔ ne akuraa. Afei ɔpanyin Panyin-grand maame kɔɔ hɔ kɔtuaa kɛntɛn bi de kataɔ ne so, na ɔnyaa palm-nuts, na ɔde wɔn sii ogya no so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mefiri hɔ rekɔ gyenene abusua no, me kɔ nnamfomma abusuakuo no nkyɛn, na afei mebɛkum child-Kwasi-Gyanama. " Ɔhyɛɛ da, na child-Kwasi-Gyanama boaboaa abotan ano na ofii aseɛ kɔɔ Rrunch, pupruru! Old-grand maame nana kae sɛ, "Dɛn na wɔrewe no? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Tiger-nuts. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Let me bi bewe. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Uncover me. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Mobɛkɔ adwane. " Ɔgyee so sɛ, Dabi, merennyaw no. " Ɔbɔɔ no din, na ɔpueeɛ; Na aberewa no nana rebɔ me pal-nuts. Abofra-Kwasi-Gyanama na ɔdidi mmɛw nnua, ɔbɔɔ akwakoraa ne aberewa, na okum no. Afei oyii ne ho fii Old-grand maame dadwenɛ no mu na ɔhyɛɛ ne ho. Old-grand maame san bae; ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wo na wo biribiara yɛ fĩ saa? " Obuae sɛ, "Makum child-Kwasi-Gyamoa; mede no ato ogya so. " Saa amaneɛbɔ yi yɛɛ dɛ maa Old-grand maame, na osii gyinae na ɔma ne nne so (and): "Child, youakum child-Kwasi-Ginyama, ebi da a ɛbɛyɛ obi. "

Ɔwiee aduane no, ɔtenaa nkyɛn, na ɔdii. Old-grand maame de nkwan no guu nano dede kɛseɛ, fononoo! fweo! Child-Kwasi-Gyanmoa kae sɛ, "Worehyɛ kwan (wɔhyɛn wo ba no ano) a wo ba no ti. " Oldwoman-grand maame kae sɛ, "Dɛn na woka? " Na ɔbuaa sɛ," na ɔreka sɛ nkyene nni saa nkwan yi so. " Sonak-grand maame kae sɛ, "Go na ɔbɔɔ ne ho bi. " Enti ɔkɔɔ bi na ɔnyaa bi. Wɔdidie, na Old-grand maame ka sɛ, "Afei a makum child-Kwasi-Ginyama no, dane nani, me nana. " Ɔbaa no, ɔgyee sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ na ɔsiesiee no kama. Mprempren, Oldwold-gramina de nanim kyerɛɛ berɛ bi. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ah!! Saa abofra yi anim nkae me Abofra-Kwasi-Gyamoa. " A abofra no kae sɛ, "Nanabea, adɛn nti na wohuu sɛ Abofra-Kwasi-Gyanama baeɛ na okum me abusuafoɔ baawɔtwe (do a woka saa) nyinaa, ma wo werɛ how. " Madin-manabea panyin no kaa sɛ, "Shut you ano. " Afei abofra no yii ne ho wɔ fie hɔ. Akwafa no yii no kɔeɛ. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ma mɛkɔ na moato no ngu mid-dent so. " So child-Kwasi-Gyamoa na ɔde no kɔe. Bere a ɔduruu mid-dent no, ɔtwee sika kɔkɔɔ agude a na aberewa no de asiesie no nyinaa gui. Afei ɔkaa sɛ, "Ho, Old-grand maame, hora, Old-grand maame, me, me, me, child-Kwasi-Gyamoa, sesei wɔ mprɛnu so no, me ne mo nana aba abɛkum no, na me nso me de mo sika kɔkɔɔ agude no akɔ. " Old-grandame buae sɛ, "I kae sɛ mo anim te sɛ Child-Kwasi-Gyamoa deɛ no; baabiara a wobɛkɔ no, me ne mo kɔ. " Ɛnna wɔn mmienu nyinaa dwanee, kiri! kiri! ki!! kiri! Abofra-Kwasi-Gyanama bɛduruu ne akuraa no so, na ɔde sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ no hyɛɛ ne mogya mu, na ɔkɔɔ kwan, na ɔde ne ho siee nkyɛn, na akwampaefoɔ-grandame nso baa hɔ. Ɛno nti, bere a wonam kwan so no, wobɛte kasaakasakasa nnyigyei, "pe. " Ɛno ne Old-grande-graning Child-Kwasi-Gyamoa.
