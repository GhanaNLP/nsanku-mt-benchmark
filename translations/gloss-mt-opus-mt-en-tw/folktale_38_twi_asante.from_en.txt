Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

MPANIMFOƆ KYERƐ SƐ, "BƐDI BUSUANI, ANAA KUN, ANAA NEA ƆWƆ ADWUMA NYINAA BƐYƐ, SƐ ƆBISA WO A, ƐSƐ SƐ MoBOA HUM"

KWAKU AANANSE, Ananse, kae kyerɛɛ "Aso" (ne yere), "Momma yɛnkɔ na yɛnyɛ turo. " "Aso kae sɛ, "Me nkɔ. " Kwaku Ananse kɔkyerɛɛ fam. Bere a ɔsan baa nakyi no, ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nokware sɛ worenkɔ (to support me))" anaa? "Aso" buae sɛ, "ɔbea akyide bi a ɔreyi fam no. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "To-ɔkyena ma yɛn kɔhyew turo mu ahantan. " Aso kaa sɛ, "ɔbea adwinni bi a ɛrehyew turo mu moira, na me deɛ, mɛkɔ akɔhyew me ogya no anaa? " Kwaku Ananse san baeɛ. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, ɔkae sɛ, "Aso", me srɛ mo sɛ, momma yɛn nkɔ boaboa yɛn ho ano na yɛnyɛ nneɛma. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Anka wɔrenkɔ. " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Meahu mmusua. " Ɛnna ɔkɔtoo adeɛ nyinaa yie. Seesei wɔwɔ hɔ, na nnɔbaeɛ no baeɛ. Ɛda bi "Aso kɔɔ afuo hɔ. Ɔbaa bɛka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse, nnɔbaeɛ no aba turo no mu, na ɛhe na yɛbɛkɔ akɔboaboa wɔn ano? " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, " Ho! Nea saa Nkramoni no kae no, me nte aseɛ koraa. " "Aso" kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛbɛtumi aba sɛ mete aseɛ. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Me kɔ si hɔ san ba. " Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ turo hɔ, na ɛhɔ na ɔgyinaa hɔ pii, na ɔno ankasa (to ne ho), "Me, Kwaku the Ananse, me wɔ ha, na me ne me atena ama eyi, ɔbaa kɛkɛ ama me kwae? " Kwaku Ananse faa kekkan, agyan ne asɔre bi, na ɔtuu amena kɛse pa ara. Ɔfaa nnɔbaeɛ a ɛwɔ afuo no mu nyinaa de hyɛɛ mu, san kɔɔ nakyi. Ɔkaa sɛ, ""Aso", nsa a ɛdwirio, betumi aka amaneɛbɔ. " Aso bae. Ɔkae sɛ, "Mekɔɔ kɔbisabisa onipa bi asɛm wɔ me ahonyadeɛ ho; ɔka sɛ, nna nwɔtwe na ɛwɔ hɔ ma me ansa na mewui, nti me nkɔ afuo bio, na me nkɔ hɔ bio, na sɛ me wu a, mɛsie me. Metuu amena kɛseɛ wɔ hɔ, na emu na wode asie me. nna nwɔtwe na ɛdiɛ; Kwaku Ananse yɛɛ ne ho sɛ obewu. Nnipa no maa Ananse so kɔɔ soro kɔsiee no wɔ ne turo mu. Wɔsan baa afie na wɔyɛɛ ayiyie amanne pa paa. "Aso" nso, yɛɛ kunna ho amanneɛ. Bere a wɔreyɛ da a ɛtɔ so awotwe ayi no, "Aso kae sɛ, "Ah, ma me nkɔ kɔhwɛ me kunu turo. " "Aso kɔɔ hɔ; ɛhɔ na turo, dadeɛ! "Aso" kae sɛ, "O, turo a me kunu yɛe ne nea na maniwa da so (sɛ wode aduane bɛma me a, nnipa bi aba abɛdidi nea ɛwɔ mu nyinaa. " Afei osuu na ɔtow nnome (opp) guu wɔn so, na ɔkae sɛ, "Me (m) Kwaku Ananse obi a ɔwɔ honhom, wnnɔbaeɛ a wode ama me no nyinaa, nnipa aba abɛdi. "

Kwaku Ananse maa ne ho so (ne nne ne dwom):

"Gyae (complaining)! Meadi nea me pɛ!

Solves (complaining), "Aso tow hyɛ nira a wobeyi afi hɔ so.

Mesoya (copplaining), meadi nko ara nea ɛyɛ me dea!

Mesoya (complaining), Aso adwiniɛ a ɛrehyew (turo mu opira) adeɛ. Me tirim yɛ me yaw (complaining) nko ara!

Solves (complaining), "Aso wɔabara sɛ wɔnyɛ kua.

Mesoya (copplaining), meadi nko ara nea ɛyɛ me dea! "

Ɛwɔ hɔ no, "Aso" gyinaa hɔ, na ɛyɛ hu. Ɔkɔɔ fie kɔtoo Adanko, Hare, sɛ ebia ɔbɛte nsɛmfua bi a na ɛwɔ ne kunu afuo hɔ. Hare no ne no sii hɔ. "Aso duu afuo hɔ, na ɔsan kɔɔ su (her nne) mu bio sɛ:

" Me kunu ne Kwaku, Ananse, na ɔwui na ogyaw me afuo hɔ, na nnipa bae na wɔdii mu aduane.

Kwaku, Ananse, me na mekotow ma wo, me kɔn dɔ me, Mebɔɔ me asu, Mebɔɔ me asu. "

Kwaku Ananse maa ne so:

"Ciase (complaining) no, meadi nea ɛyɛ me dea!

Solves (complaining), "Aso tow hyɛ nira a wobeyi afi hɔ so.

Mesoya (copplaining), meadi nko ara nea ɛyɛ me dea!

Mesoya (compliting)), Aso adwiniɛ (turo mu opira).

Mesoya (copplaining) nyinaa, meadi nea ɛyɛ me dea nko ara!

Solves (complaining), "Aso wɔabara sɛ wɔnyɛ kua.

Mesoya (copplaining), meadi nea ɛyɛ me dea ara pɛ; metumi agyae (complaining)!

Ɛfiri Ɔbɔdeɛ a woyɛeɛ (nneɛma) no, woahu ɔbaa bi ammoa ne kunu?

Mesoya (copplaining), meadi nea ɛyɛ me dea ara pɛ; metumi agyae (complaining)! "

Hare no kae sɛ, "M'awie ntie, momma yɛnkɔ fie sɛ ebia mɛka nea ɛyɛ nyinaa akyerɛ mo. " Ɔne "Aso" kɔɔ fie. Hare kaa sɛ, "Kwaku Ananse kaeɛ sɛ, ɔka kyerɛɛ mo sɛ, 'Let's go and ear turo'; woka sɛ wɔrenkɔ; Momma yɛnkɔ na yɛnhyew (kutun a ɛyɛ dry no)'; na wose yɛnkɔ; Momma yɛnkɔ na yɛnkɔ, na moasɛ sɛ mo nkɔ; ɛnneɛ moawie osu, ɛfiri sɛ ɔno nko ara na ɔredi, ɛfiri sɛ Ɔbɔɔdeɛ no bɔɔ no, onhuu ɔbaa a ɔboa ne kunu; nti ntam ngyae dadie. ng (Ɔkasa) ɛfiri sɛ wadi nea ɛyɛ ne dea nyinaa nko ara. " Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, "Sɛ obiara de ne ho hyɛ adwuma biara mu na obisa wo sɛ, boa no. "

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
