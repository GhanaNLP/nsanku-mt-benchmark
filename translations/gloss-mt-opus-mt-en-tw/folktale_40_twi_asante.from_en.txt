Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

WUHYIA WƆ HƆ NA WOHU

ƐDA bi na ɔyɛ ɔbaa bi ne ne abofra. Bere a ɔsomaa no (anyinamrom) no, ɔkae sɛ, "I Khan no ankɔ. " Ná ne maame taa ka sɛ, "Wasie wɔ hɔ na wobehu. " Ɛnkyɛe na ɔde no kɔeɛ, bere a ɔsomaa no (anyinamwhere) no, ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Mennyɛ saa. " Afei ne maame no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Misiee wɔ hɔ na wubehu. " Ɛda bi a abofra no kae sɛ, "Apovers, maame reka akyerɛ me sɛ, awiabere wɔ hɔ na merebɛhwɛ ; ɛda yi ara na mɛkɔ akɔ hwehwɛ 'I-will-see," ' Ebia mehwɛɛ no. " Enti sɛnea ɔde sii hɔ no, na (re) Pea-tree a ɔdono sika kɔkɔɔ a wɔabɔ no asinasin. Pea-tree no bisaa no sɛ, "Dɛn nti na obiara mma ha da, seesei waba ha? " The abofra no kae sɛ, "Me te hɔ daa a me ne maame te, mekae sɛ, Mewɔ ahomegyee wɔ hɔ na wobehu, na meka sɛ menkɔ hwehwɛ 'I-will-see' a, metumi ahwɛ no. " Pea-tree no kae sɛ, "Me yɛ wɔn mu biako, nanso yɛn mu kɛse no ara wɔ akyirikyiri. " Na abofra no fi hɔ bio. Bere a ɔrekɔ no, na ohu sɛ bere tenten-pie-pie-pie-nnyinae reba abɛhyia no. Mfeɛ-pie-of-of nyinaa gyee no kyim sɛ, "Adɛn nti na obiara nfa saa kwan yi so mprempren? " Na abofra no buaa sɛ, "Asoply, maame kae sɛ, 'Wobɛhu, enti mekae sɛ meba hwehwɛ I-will-see mu, ɛno betumi ayɛ yiye. Me ne wɔn nnyina nyinaa buae sɛ, me nkwaa no bi yɛ me yaw paa. Me san kɔɔ abofra no anim. Me na abofra no san fii hɔ bio. Bere a ɔne no kɔɔ hɔ no, ɔboroo so wɔ abo a ɛyɛ borɔdɔma mu, na ɔda kuku a abubu sin bi so, na ɔreba (towards). Gadud-Sarks kae sɛ, Adɛn nti na sɛ obiara nni ha a, na woaba? Na abofra no abua sɛ, Bere a maame ne me te hɔ no, na ɔka sɛ, Mehu, enti mekae sɛ mɛkɔ I-will-see hwehwɛ mu, wɔ ahyɛdeɛ a metumi de ahwɛ no mu. Gur bload-Sarks kae sɛ, Me yɛ wɔn mu biako, me bo yɛ fɛ, ma nea ɛso sen biara (of Us). Bere a ɔkɔe no, (Na ɛwɔ hɔ) Nkoo a wɔde nhwigyeɛ bɛtoo fie bi so. Nkoo no kaa sɛ, Mprempren yɛbɛnya mogya bi a yɛde bɛsi fie yi. Mprempren a abofra no aduru hɔ no, wɔkae sɛ, Dɛn nti na obiara amma ha da, na woabɛka sɛ, "Metaa ne maame te ha anaa? " Ɔkaa sɛ, Wait wɔ hɔ na wobɛhunu, enti meka sɛ mɛkɔ I-will-see hwehwɛ a, ɛno betumi aba sɛ mehwɛ. Ɛhɔ na Nkoo no gyee abofra no, na ɔtwa no. Ad, na wɔgyee ne mogya kɔhwiee no wɔ kɔbere kuku mu; wɔde bi kɔtoo fie. Afei Nkoo no de ahoɔhare bɔɔ dua tenten, na ɛdii wɔn ho wɔn ho afua ahoroɔ wɔ mogya no mu, na wɔn aduasa ahoroɔ no bɛyɛɛ kɔkɔɔ. Nkoo no nyinaa faa wɔn aduasa ahoroɔ no, de wɔn kɔtoo mu, yɛɛ dwumadie no nkaedi ade. Afei da bi, Nkoo no mu baako kae sɛ ɔbɛdi. Ɔkɔɔ kɔhyiaa abofra nomaame. Na maame no bisaa Akoo no sɛ, Me abofra a na me ne no te no, na ɔyɛ ɔkwasea ade biara. Bere a na me sɔre kɔ soro no, na meka sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔtwɛn hɔ na ohu; Ɔte sɛ kwae, de me asɛmfua reyi me ano, kae sɛ ɔbɛkɔ I-will-see hwehwɛ mu a, ɛno betumi ahwɛ. Ɛyɛɛ da a ɛtɔ so nwɔtwe ni na menhunuu no, na me nso mema mo ani so ade, sɛ woahunu no a, ɛde no ba me nkyɛn. Akoo no kae sɛ, Oh, ɔbaa yɛn akuraa no so, na yɛn nso, yɛakum no. Ne mogya bi a yɛfaa so yɛɛ dwumadie no ho nkaeɛ no, ɛwɔ me dua yi so. Ɛno nti, bere a wokɔ (wo kwan so) no, kɔ ka kyerɛ wo nnipa nyinaa sɛ, sɛ obi ka kyerɛ no bi a, Wait wɔ hɔ na wubehu, ɛnde na ɔtoo nnome agu no so.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
