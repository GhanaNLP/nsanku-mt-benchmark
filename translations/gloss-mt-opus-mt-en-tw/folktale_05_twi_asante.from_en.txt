SƐNEA ƐBƐYƐ WƆ ƆPƐ A WONYAE WƆ FI MU NO HO.

ƐDII da bi a ɛyɛ Ɔpɛtɛ ne Kwaakwadabi, na Kwaakwaaba no kae sɛ, Momma yɛnkɔ ntwa twe na yɛnni so agoru. Wɔtu si hɔ ma nwura no, na wɔfiri altrall-cott dua bi so aba. Ɔpɛtɛ no ara na ɔhunuu bellall-cotton dua no, ɔkaa sɛ, Eyi deɛ, ɛyɛ me deɛ, ma me, enti me na metwa saa asɛm yi so kɛseɛ. Kwaakwadabi no kaa sɛ, Me nso metee. Kwaakwadabi no, sɛnea na ɔrehwɛ no, ɔhuu sɛ Ketue dua bi gyina hɔ a, ɔsan kɔɔ ne nkyene. Wɔn mmienu nyinaa dii amane. Ɔpɛpɛ no bɔɔ ne twe a ɛbɔɔ no saa ara pɛ na wɔbɔɔ no.

Futuro! Futuro! Me nsa aka (mine) ntɛm ara!

Ɔpɛt Kwakye! Me nsa aka (mine) ntɛm ara!

Bere a Kwaakwadabi dii no so nkonim no, ɔbɔɔ nea ɛte sɛɛ:

Kwaakwaaba, Kwa! Kwa!

Kwaakwaaba, Kwa! Kwa!

Wɔkɔɔ so bɔɔ, na Sky-god no tee, na ɔsomaa Akrɔma (to call wɔn; Na Akrɔma bae, (nanvoy wɔn) "Berɛ a atwam! " Na wɔbuaa sɛ, "Ame! " Ɔkaa sɛ, "The Sky-god reka sɛ wate asaw bi wɔ ha nti, ɛsɛ sɛ wode ka ho ma wotie." Abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no sii hɔ kɔɔ Sky-god anim, na wɔdii agorɔ, bɔɔ agorɔ, na Sky-god ne Skygod no dii agorɔ. Mmoa nka sɛ, "Me ate sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ; nnwom nnyigyei abien no yɛ dɛ, enti mema mo (ach)." Afei Sky-god no kɔe na mema no nnaka abien na mema no to hɔ, na ɛho akyi no, na ɛho akyi yɛ fɛ yiye sɛ wubehu; nneɛma a asɛe wɔ mma sɛe a ɛte sɛ eyi ho; ebi nso yɛ abounduder koraa; sika kɔkɔɔ wɔ ma sɛe a ɛte sɛɛ mu. Sky-god kasa no, ɛwɔ Twitter, (nsɛnnima) "wo mmienu biara ba abɛfa. " Ɔpɛpɛ no kae sɛ, "Mene ɔpanin, nti yɛnkɔ nni kan. " Berɛ a ɔkɔɔ no, ɔyii fɛfɛɛfɛ a ɛyɛ baako, berɛ a Kwaadadadabi nso faa aborosoɔ a ɛyɛ tan paa. Wɔmaa wɔn so kɔɔ wɔn akuraa hɔ. Bere a wɔbuee wɔn, Ɔpɛpɛ, bere a ɔhwɛɛ ne mu no, na atɛkyɛ fĩ. Wɔ Karwadadabi no mu no, bere a ɔhwɛɛ mu no, sika kɔkɔɔ hyerɛn sɛ hwee. Ɛnkyɛree koraa na JOughtstone bi bɛduruu hɔ na ɔka kyerɛɛ Ɔpɛtɛ (ONEONEth) sɛ, "Measoma sɛ mɛka sɛ wo maame-in mmara awu. " (Ɔpɛpɔn) kaa sɛ, "Kwaakwaaabi, maa me sikakɔkɔɔ baako sɛ mekyekyere me basaa a metumi akɔ ayie no mu. " Ɔnyaa sikakɔkɔɔ sini no na ɔtwee ne ho kɔɔ ayie no ase. Ntɛm ara na ɔkaa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ ɔkɔ na ne ho tɔ no; ɔte fam, nanso bere a ɔreforo no, sikakɔkɔɔ sini no hwee ase na ɛyerae. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, Kwaakwadabi agyapadeɛ no ayera, na ɛbɛyɛ dɛn na mesan aba fie? " Enti ohuruw kɔɔ fi no mu na ɔhwehwɛɛ (for sikakɔkɔɔ sini). Ɛno nti na wobɛhunu Ɔpɛtɛ no, bere nyinaa ɔbɔ kɔkɔɔ, ɔbɔ kɔkɔɔ, wɔ tiafi, sɛ ɔbɛhwehwɛ Krow abokia sɛ ɔde bɛma no.

Ɛno nti na Mpanimfoɔ kyerɛ sɛ, "Aniwaɛ nni mu. "
