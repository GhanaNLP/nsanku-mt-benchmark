SƐNEATHE TORO, A WƆFRƐ NO "AKYEM ABOADEDEƐ" KYERƐ NO, BAA ABUSUAKUO MU

ƐDII ababaawa fɛfɛɛfɛ bi akyi bere bi. Ɔbɔɔ ɔbarima biara a ɔkae sɛ ɔdɔ no, na ɔbɛka sɛ, "Mensom wo. " Seesei Daboɔ no adan ne ho ayɛ aberanteɛ fɛfɛɛfɛ, na ɔwoo no, na ɔde no kɔɔ nakuraa. (Bere a ɔduruu hɔ no, ɔkae sɛ, "Mene Daboɔ, na me ne mo bɛbɔ. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Agya, me dɔ mo, nea mowɔ biara, me ho dɔ mo. " Ɔne no nyaa ɔbarima; na ɔyɛ onipa ba. Nanso, ɔtwaa, na na ɛyɛ ababaaayewa. Seesei da bi, ɛna panin kae sɛ, "Mebɛhwɛ me babiara. " Nti, ne maame kɔe, na (babaa) faa no, na ɔde no siee. Na ne kunu no san bae; ɔkae sɛ, "Haasan bi ba ha? " Na ɔkae sɛ, "Obi nnya mma ha. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛboro so (silin) yi ma kuru, kuduru, ne abotereɛ, dwoɔ bɔ me. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Menice. " Afei anɔpa, ne kunu no kɔɔ nwura no mu, na ɔbaa no kaa kyerɛɛ ne maame sɛ, "Go, ne bere a wodu asennibea akwan no so no, nka asɛm, bere a wodu adwa no, wuduru asuwa, no, ka. Sɛ woka a, ɔbɛba abɛkyere wo. " Seesei ɔduruu baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ, na ɔntuaa adwene biara (kɔɔ nea ɔka kyerɛɛ no) nanso ɔfrɛɛ sɛ, "Me ɔbarima--mmara, me ɔbarima--babarima-mmara!" Daboɔ no bɛkyeree no de no kɔeɛ, na berɛ a ne yere huu ne ho (nipadua) a ɔhwee no na osuu no. Ne kunu kae sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛyɛ asɛm? " Na ɔbuaa sɛ, "Mewɔ yaw wɔ me yafunu mu. " Ɛda a edi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛda adi no, ɔbaa no kae sɛ, "Me kɔ me akuraa. " Ɔde ɛto ne nkosua na ɛbɔɔ no, na ɔde no too ne retire so, na ɔde ne mmofra baanu no kɔɔ, baako nantew kɔɔ nanim, foforo no akyi. Afei, bere a ɔpow ɛto no bi (yam a wɔayam, na ɔde maa nabofra no dii no, ɔkae sɛ, "S goe and di, ɔbarima ayamyefoɔ abofra. " Ɛnna ɔtwee ne ho maa ɔfoforɔ no sɛ, "Receive and di, ɔbarima a ɔyem no abofra. " Saa bere yi nyinaa na ne kunu nam ne nkyɛn wɔ kwan so. Afei, bere a na aka kakra ma wɔadu akuraa hɔ no, Daboɔ no kae sɛ, "Aso (his yere no din de, mani agye sɛ wo maame baa hɔ na woamfa ankyerɛ me, na wone nea ɛde ba sɛ mekyeree no, enti gyina hɔ ma me mmofra yi din: abofra a me ne no bɔ Ofori; abofra no, Oforiwa. " Daboɔ no yii sikakɔkɔɔ agudeɛ na ɔde maa ne mmofra no sɛ, Mome mo, wom m Yɛn nsa nka aboa biara a aboɔ bɛtɔ no; na bio, ɛsɛ sɛ wokum abodin, fa sponge (nhohoro no), na dɔte fitaa na fa petepete no so; na afei mema wo nhyira, kosɛ! Akyem Aboadeɛ koro no ara. "

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
