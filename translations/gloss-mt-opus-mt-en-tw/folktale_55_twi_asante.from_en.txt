SƐNEA SEKAR-GRASRAS BAA ABUSUAKUO MU

Ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bi bae na Ntikuma sii ne bo sɛ ɔbɛkɔ aduane ho hwehwɛ sɛ ɔde nagya, Agya Ananse, Ananse, bɛba. Ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ akuraase bi; (Nea ɛwɔ hɔ no) na ɔhyiaa Old-grande maame. Saa aberewa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Me banana ba no, adɛn nti na obiara mma ha? Ntikuma buae sɛ, Ɛyɛ ɛkɔm no nti, na me aba hwehwɛ sɛ biribi kɔdi. Ɛbaa no. Ntikuma fii hɔ. Wɔn bakyerɛ no mu bi kae sɛ, Puck me, board me; afoforo nso kae sɛ, Mommma mo ani beree me, na mani beree me. Afei Ntikuma tuu wɔn a wɔkae sɛ, Dony bload board me, nani beree me. Afei Old-grand maame a wadi mfeɛ no kaa sɛ, bɔɔ aduane no fã bi too asɛnka, na ɔde nwoma ahoroɔ guu kuku no mu na ɔde sii ogya no so. Ntikuma de nwoma ahoroɔ no guu ogya no so. Bere a wɔsiesiee wɔn ho no, oyii ogya no, ɛbɛdaa emu nsɛm a ɛwɔ kuku no mu, na biribiara (inside) adane aduane pa. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, Me nsa aka, nanso mema me nam nhyɛ (the stewing). Old-grand maame kae sɛ, Go ne Nkwakuo a wɔwɔ dua no, Asono no, sɛ wose, Old-grand-grame maame ka sɛ, mo nyinaa de, ɛnanom! Asono no kae sɛ, Sɛ ɛnyɛ ma Old-grandian maame a, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛbɔ wo so nkonim. Ɛbaa no na ɔboroo no. Me mmoa no sii wɔ ahyɛdeɛ a ɛkyerɛ sɛ meboro no so. Sharp-knifea a ɔyɛ ɔpanyin dada no kaa sɛ, Me Sharp na ɔbɔɔ mmoa yi atitirefoɔ. Afei sharp-One twaa mmoa no mu atitirefoɔ. Ntikuma boaboaa mmoa no nyinaa ano na ɔboroo nkyerɛkyerɛ bi wɔ nkaho, na ɔde ne nyinaa too hɔ maa ne akuraa. Agya Ananse huu (ne ɔbabarima) Ntikuma sɛ ɔregyina nanim, na ɔkae sɛ, Nkyene a ɛreba no, power-hill (i. e. nea edi kan) no. Ɔtoaa so sɛ, Ma menkura makyi. Ntikuma kɔtoo ɛhɔ. Mprempren Kwaku Ananse kɔyii ayeforɔ koro; ɔkae sɛ, Sɛ menya me abosonsomɔ a, mebɛdidi eyi adi. (N yere) "Aso kae sɛ, Ɔbɔdeɛ no yɛɛ nneɛma nti, woahunu ɔbarima bi a na ɔwɔ abosonsomɔ? Kwaku Ananse ne ne yere, ne ne mmofra no, dii bayerɛ no wieeɛ, na ɛhɔ na ɛkɔɔ so sɛ ɔyɛ Agya Ananse busuani. Anɔpa bi, bere a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Me nsa aka abosonsomɔ. Na ɔkɔɔ abɔnten kɔtoo (wɔ akwankyɛn tenten) wɔ fie hɔ, noa bayere no, na odii, kɔsii sɛ wɔwiee (all). Ntikuma kaa sɛ, me bɛsan akɔ. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Me ne wo bɛkɔ (together). Ntikuma kae sɛ, me ne no nkɔ pɛ. Bere a Nitiakuma kɔɔ nna no, afei Kwaku Ananse kɔɔ hɔ kɔgyee abɔmmɔfo were bag, de amena nketewa guu mu, na wɔde nso hyɛɛ mu ma, na wɔbɔɔ no borɔdɔma wɔ Ntikuma ntama ano. Bere a nneɛma bɛda adi no, ntɛm ara, na Ntikuma adi ntɛm paa wɔ kwan so. Bere a na (sum a wɔde ahintaw) anim (fi nneɛma) abubu, Anan, hwehwɛ Ntikuma sɛnea ɔbɛpɛ no, na ɔkɔ hwehwɛ (baabi) nso no. Bere a ɔhunuu sɛ wɔredi aboa anammɔn no akyi, na ɔrekɔ so. Na ɔrekɔ no, na Nitikuma ne Old-grande maame te hɔ. Amald-grand maame dada no kae sɛ, Agya barima, dɛn na wo guadwa no te? Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, Ɛkɔm akyere me, enti mema me biribi didi. Old-grandame kae sɛ, ɛhe na ɛsɛ sɛ menya AGU FA? Kwaku Ananse buaa sɛ, Adɛn nti na me yɛ kwasea, me abofra, Ntikuma, baeɛ, na wode bi maa no. Old-woman-grandgrame kaa sɛ, Go to afuw a ɛwɔ hɔ no; yams a Pull me soro, twe me, twe me, no. Ananse (relied) Berɛ a ɔduruu hɔ no, bakyerɛ no mu bi kae sɛ, Pull me soro, me so ntwe me, ebinom kae sɛ, Dont twe me so, nabɛn me. Na ɔtwee ebinom a wɔkae sɛ, Pull me soro, twe me, na ɔne wɔn baa fie no. Ɔbɛdii wɔn akyi wiee; Old-woman-grand Mother kae sɛ, Go na mobɛto aduane pa no fã, na ɔde nwoma ahoroɔ no akɔhyɛ kuku no mu, na ɔde asi ogya no so. Kwaku Ananse buaa sɛ, Ah, dɛn na ɛha nkasa yi a woka kyerɛ me; Ɔbɛndeɛ no yɛɛ nneɛma nti, yɛto aduane pa no mu, na yɛkura nsensanee no mu? Ananse no faa aduane no papa, na ɔde sii ogya no so, nanso ɔno deɛ, na ɔwɔ ogya no ho, nanso ɔno deɛ, ɔgyaee nneɛma no. Egyee dadwenɛ no kakra baako na ɔde too soro. Bere a wɔnoaa aduane no, ɔfaa no, na ne nyinaa adane aboɔ, nanso na wereeɛ sin baako a na ɔde asisi atifi hɔ no adane aduane pa. Agya Ananse kae sɛ, "Eh! Saa akwampamani akwahosan yi anni atorɔ (nsɛnne nyinaa). " Na Ananse no kɔɔ so tow nwoma ahoroɔ no guu wɔn so de wɔn baa ogya no so. Bere a wɔnoaa aduane no, ɔtwee ogya no, na ɛdane bakyerɛ a wɔanoa nso sɛnea wopɛ sɛ wuhu. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Old--grand maame, eyi nyinaa yɛ yiye paa, nanso afei ɛsɛ sɛ wode nam ma no ka ho. " Old-grand maame kae sɛ, "Go na bu dua no, Asono wɔ so; sɛ wokɔ a, wobɛka sɛ, Woyɛ mmooma. Ananse no kae sɛ, "ɔbaa yi!! Me ne Asono no nni nea ɛde ba wɔ manso ho, na dɛn nti na merekɔdi wɔn so dwuma wɔ ɔkwan yi so? " Oldwomen-grand maame kae sɛ, "Go, and sɛ ɔhaw ba a, ɛno ne mabusuabɔ. " Ananse no sii kwan so. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo Asono, wo a wo wɔ saa nua yi so, Old-wold-grand Motherak no kyerɛ sɛ woyɛ mmooma. " Mmoa no kae sɛ, "Ɛnyɛ ma Old-graven maame na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛbɔ wo so. " Abakɔsɛm kyerɛwfo-gragrame kae sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ na ɛba bɛbɔ no."

Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Aberewa, ɛno nni ntiatia. " Ɛnna mmoa no bɛtoo no so na ɛdii Kwaku Ananse so nkonim. Aberewa no kae sɛ, "Me Sekkan The-sharp-one, twaa wɔn atitirefoɔ no mu. " Tenten! (wa nnyigyei a kekan no yɛe sɛnea ɛsɛ na ɛyɛ adwuma no, ɛmmoa no baa no so. Old-granger-graminame no kaa sɛ, "Softly and three likelied. " Nanso Adanse no anka (her). Afei, Ananse no boaboaa nam no ano na ɔsan baa fie, na Aberewa no kɔɔ nkyɛn kakra. Afei Kwaku Ananse wiaa ne kkan no de tow hyɛɛ ne kotokuo no so, na ɔdwanee san baa nakuraa no so. Berɛ a ɔduruu ne akuraa hɔ no, ɔka kyerɛɛ (ne ɔbabarima), "Ntikuma, ha a me nsa aka no, momma yɛn nkɔ nwura no mu. " Enti wɔsii kwan, kɔhuu Wuram mprako bi. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Nitiakuma, kɔbu wɔn mmonnaa. Ntikuma kɔɔ na ɔkɔkaa sɛ, "Bush-pigs, wo ho yɛ fɛ. " Bush-pigs no buae sɛ, "Sɛ ɛnyɛ sɛ woyɛ Kwaku Ananse, wo busuani a worebɔ dama no ba a, anka ɛsɛ sɛ woba bɛbɔ wo. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Bra na modu akwan nyinaa so, ɔyɛ bɔne koraa, ne animtiaa no nni aseresɛm. " Na Bush-pigs no bɛbɔ no, bere a Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Old Womens Kanife, the-sharsh, twaa wɔn atitirefoɔ no mu maa me." h! na Sekkan no bɛtoo wɔn so, na wotwitwaa mmoa no nyinaa ti. Ɛnna ɔde sii hɔ sɛ watwa nnua no. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Hai! "Dɛn na woka ho asɛm yi? Mekae sɛ metwaa mmoa no ti, na wowiee ɛno, na afei dɛn koraa na eyi yɛ? " Mprempren Ntikuma hwɛɛ kwan kakra, ɛnneɛ ɔkae sɛ, "Softly and clized is them. " Kwaku Ananse anka ne ho asɛm, na Sekkan no baa fam, na Kwaku Ananse kɔgyee no. Wɔsan baa fie. Ɛda a edi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛda adi no, Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ wɔbɛkɔ Skygod-gold a ɛwɔ hɔ no. Ntikuma kaa sɛ, "Agya, nkɔ sɛ ɛyɛ Sekkan yi ntawntawto. " Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Get in justice. " Ntikuma kaa sɛ, fa wo kwan si hɔ. Kwaku Ananse sii hɔ, na ɔkɔɔ Sky-god a ɛwɔ hɔ no, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Agya, kɔ da a, sɛ obi wɔ abufuo a, me nkutoo, me, Kwaku Ananse, me bɛyi wɔn atitirefoɔ nyinaa afiri hɔ wɔ da koro pɛ na mema wo. Sky-god no kaa sɛ, "Hrea drivering is arever for one per perieng. " Yebetumi ayɛ saa, efisɛ atamfo asraafoɔ abakɔsɛm kɛseɛ gyina kuro no kuro akyi, enti wofiri hɔ kɔ. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Momma wɔmfa nsa ne odwan biako mmra. " Ntɛm ara na wɔde wɔn kɔe. Kwaku Ananse fii adi kɔɔ kwan so; Kwaku Ananse bisaa atamfo asraafo no sɛ, So wo ne Eomem-anuonyam no bɛko? Yiw, buae sɛ, ɛno ne nea yɛka. Mprempren Ewiem-anuonyam asraafo no gyinaa Kwaku Ananse akyi. Afei Kwaku Ananse ka kyerɛɛ Old Womens Sekkan, The virt-One, kae sɛ, Cut atamfoɔ a wɔwɔ atamfoɔ ma me. Na Sekkan no twaa wɔn atitirefoɔ, tan! Kaku Ananse, na owiee no. Ɛno yɛ nea eye, ɛnnyae. Nanso Sekkan no kɔtoo Eomem-anuonyampɔn asraafodom so na wɔretwa (wɔn atitirefoɔ). Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Oh, a ɛyɛ Erome-anuonyam asraafodom no, enti ɔgyae. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Sɛ wo nufuɔ mu akyere a, ma ɛdwo. O. O. Skyky-god asraafoɔ no nyinaa werɛ how. Mprempren Sekkan no tu kwan wɔ mframa no mu; ɛbaa nea Kwaku Ananse wɔ. Ɔkae sɛ, Hwɛ, wonnim me anaa? wonnim me? Kwaku Ananse, me na mede wo firi Old-manwoafoɔ hɔ. O. sesei! Kwaku Ananse ti bɔɔ fam de tan! Na ɛkɔɔ so no Sekkan no; ne baabi a ɛsie no danee ɛserɛ bi a yɛfrɛ no Etene (spar-grass); nano yɛ den pa ara, ɛyɛ nnam paa.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
