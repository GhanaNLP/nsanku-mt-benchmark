Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

SƐ WƆREBƐNYIE A, BERE A BUSUANI KYERƐ SƐ ƆBƐNTƐ WO NSO A, GORO ALONG A ƆWƆ HIM NO

NA ɛyɛ bere bi a ɔbaa bi wɔ hɔ, na ɔdii mmofra baasa. Ná wɔn mu panyin rehu amane wɔ yaw ho. Wɔn anuanom no mu panyin ka kyerɛɛ wɔn maame sɛ wɔmma wɔn nni sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma sɛ ebia wɔbɛkɔ dwadie. Wɔn mu nkumaa no nso kae sɛ ɔbɛpɛ sɛ ɔne wɔn kɔ. Wɔbɔɔ dawuro sɛ ɛnsɛ sɛ wɔne wɔn kɔ. Ɔmaame no sɛ ɔne wɔn kɔ. Afei wɔn maame maa ɔpanin no (mmabarima) sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma maa £5 mfasoɔ, ne sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma maa mmeranteɛ ne mmabaa mfasoɔ a ɛwɔ £2 so. Bere a nneɛma bɛda adi no, wɔdaa adi. Ná mpanimfoɔ no kɔ (in front) na wɔgyaa abofra (to) no hɔ. Ná abofra no aka nakyi nkakrankakra. mpanimfo no hyiaa obarima bi a na ɔde apataa reba. Ɔka kyerɛɛ mpanimfoɔ no sɛ, "Buy! " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Go on, na sɛ wuhyia abofra bi a, ma no ntɔ, na ɔnka sɛ ɔbɛtɔ no, ɔbɛbɔ no na ama no atɔ; na sɛ ɔda so ara sɛ ɔbɛtɔ a, fa no kɔ ne maame. " Na yɔnkoɔ no kɔɔ so, na ɛmaa no hunuu sɛ ɔno na ɔhyiaa abofra no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "mpanimfoɔ bi a mehyiaa wɔn no, wɔkae sɛ, wopɛ sɛ wotɔ saa apataa yi. " Na abofra no kae sɛ, "MuTWA berɛ a wɔn a wɔyɛ me mpanimfoɔ no antɔ? " Ná yɔnkoɔ no aka sɛ, "Wɔka sɛ wobɛtɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ me di wo so nkonim, na ɛsɛ sɛ wogye wo san na wode wo ma wo maame. " Na abofra no kae sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn na ɛbɛyɛ? " Na yɔnkoɔ no kae sɛ, "Annu Osua mu duru a ɔwɔ wɔ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. e. £2. 2)" Na abofra no de tuaa no na ne nsa kaa. Ɔkɔɔ so yɛɛ mpanimfoɔ no wɔ akuraase bi. Wɔn nsa aka atoasodeɛ, nanso wɔnni nam biara a wɔne wɔn bɛkɔ. abofra no kaa sɛ, "Sika yɛ apataa a metɔɔ no. " Wɔde hyɛɛ soup-stew mu. Na wɔawie (oking) no, mpanimfoɔ no de tata no tenten maa no. Bere a abofra no rebu no, ɔhunuu sika kɔkɔɔ dumm no, na ɔde hyɛɛ ne ntoma no ano. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛdaa adi no, mpanimfoɔ no sii hɔ, na berɛ a wɔde wɔn ho kɔbɔɔ mu no, wɔhyiaa ɔbarima bi, na akokɔninini bi daa nneɛma a na ɔkura no atifi. Bere a ɔne mpanimfoɔ no baeɛ no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Musi akokɔnini yi. " Wɔbuaa sɛ, "Moso, na mobɛhyia abofra; monyɛ no abofra, na sɛ ɔbɛtɔ a, ɔbɛboro no. " Ɛyɛ nokware a ɔhyiaa abofra no. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo abusuafo mpanyimfo kae sɛ, wobɛtɔ akokɔnini yi, na sɛ wotɔ a, me werɛ hyɛ wo so, na mefa wo kɔ wo maame. " Ɔgyee nea mewɔ no toom. " Yɛn ɔkoɔ no, wɔ ne fam no, wɔde no hyɛɛ akokɔni no nsa. Wɔduruu akuraase bi. Na akua nyinaa nni akokɔnini a ɛwɔ akwadan mu. Afei anɔpa, bere a nneɛma bɛda adi no, na akokɔnini no yɛ abofra, adwumayɛfo no dea. Ɛna a ɔyɛ akuraa no ɔbarima no kae sɛ, "Abofra, fa akokɔnini no ba na tɔ. " Na abofra no kae sɛ, boɔ no yɛ osuahu na sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (E. £3) mu duru (wa. . . £3). Ɔbarima a ɔda ano no tuaa sika no. Afei da a edi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛda adi pefee no, wofii ase bio. Bere a wɔne mpanimfoɔ rekɔ no, na ɔbarima bi a ɔwɔ aminamoa na ɛrehyia no. Ɔkae sɛ, "Buy this aminamoa yi. " mpanimfoɔ no kaa sɛ, "Fa no ka ho, na wo ne abofra bi atɔ no. Ɛsɛ sɛ ɔka sɛ ɔbɛtɔ, ɔbɔɔ no, na ɔde no asan akɔ ne maame. " yɔnkoɔ no faa so, na ɛne abofra no hyiaa. Ɔkae sɛ, "Wo mpanimfoɔ se wobɛtɔ saa aminamoa yi, na sɛ woantɔ a, ɛsɛ sɛ me di wo so nkonim na mefa wo san kɔ wo maame. " Na abofra no kae sɛ, "Ɛdeɛn ara? " yɔnkoɔ no kaa sɛ, A osua ne suru mu duru a ɛwɔ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (i. £3) mu no mu duru (i. £3). Na abofra no tuaa boɔ no; na ɔkɔɔ so, na ɔne ne mpanimfoɔ no baa akuraase bi. Mprempren nkuraa a wɔde guare no bɔ man no anan bere biara a ɔbɛbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛda. Bere a abofra no faa anyinamoa no kɔɔ hɔ no, na ɛkɔ ɔpanyin no fie na ɛtwe nkuraa a ɛwɔ hɔ no. Head-man no kaa sɛ, Mebɛtɔ saa amoa yi afiri onipa a ɔwɔ hɔ no hɔ. abofra no kae sɛ, It is mi. (£3) (5 Na mpanimfoɔ no rekɔ fa mu, wɔrehyia awifoɔ a ɔwiaa ɔhene bi funu. Sɛnea awifoɔ kyerɛ no, ɛsɛ sɛ wotɔ saa funu yi. mpanimfoɔ no kaa sɛ, Fa no, na kɔ, na wuhyia abofra bi; fa no na fa no ma no bɛtɔ. Sɛ ɔka sɛ ɔbɛtɔ no, mmɔ no so, na ɔmfa no mma no nsan mma ne maame. awifoɔ no kɔɔ so, na wɔhyiaa abofra no; wɔkaa sɛ, mpanimfoɔ no ka sɛ wobɛtɔ saa funu yi. abofra no kaa sɛ, Eh!  Afei ɔtwee ne ho, na ɔbaa akuraase bi, na ɔkɔɔ fie baako kɔsrɛɛ aduane. Nea fie no wura kae no, nni hɔ biara. abofra no kae sɛ, Grand Father, III wo abo. Sɛnea fie no wura kae no, biara nni hɔ. abofra no kae sɛ, Grand FathIAth, I yɛ me anibiannaso. fie no wura no kae sɛ, Adɛn nti na saa abofra yi haw me saa; yɛn Ɔhene awu, yɛdi mmuadadi; eyi ne da a ɛtɔ so nwɔtwe; nanso yɛhwehwɛ sɛdeɛ yɛbɛtumi anya ne funu. Ɛduruu nkyɛn hɔ no, ɔkaa sɛ, Mma no aduane ne nam. Bere a abofra no dii aduan wiei no, ɔkaa sɛ, Master of the fie no, mehuu Chiefs bodies; ɛnnora a na mereba no, awifoɔ bi tɔɔ no osuahu ne suru mu duru a ɛyɛ sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma. Wɔde abofra no kɔeɛ, na ɔkɔkyerɛɛ wɔn nipadua no. Wɔkɔɔ de no bae, na wɔsiee no yie. Wɔkaa sɛ, Afei na wubedi nkonim ama Ɔhene no. Enti, abofra no bɛyɛɛ Ɔhene. Seesei, bere a ne nuabarima mpanimfo tee ne ho asɛm no, wɔbae, na afei wɔbɔɔ no ka sɛ mogya retu no. abofra no kae sɛ, Ebia wobɛyi wo afi hɔ; me nim wo, enti me nni hɔ. Ɛno nti na yɛka sɛ, Sɛ worekɔ baabiara, na sɛ wo nuabarima kumaa kyerɛ sɛ ɔne wo bɛkɔ a, fa no kɔ.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
