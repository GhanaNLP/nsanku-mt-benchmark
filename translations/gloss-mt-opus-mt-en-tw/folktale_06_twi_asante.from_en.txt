Na wɔka sɛ ɛhɔ (cone) yɛ Ɔkɔtɔdeɛ, na ne akyikykyin a ɔkɔeɛ no hyiaa aberewa bi a na ɔwɔ kuru wɔ ne nan so. Na Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Gr Executive me! " Eyi ne kuru a ɛwɔ kɔlaase bi mu; wode baako a ɛte sɛɛ bɔ mmɔden, wotumi nantew anaa? " Berewa no kae sɛ, "Oh, a ɛyɛ kakra koraa. " Ɔkɔtɔdeɛ no kaeɛ sɛ, "So wo, ɛnnɛ, sɛ mɛyɛ biribi pa ama wo (to-da) a, gye sɛ woyɛ biribi bɔne tia me. " Saa aberewa no kae sɛ, "Oh, ITUMI anyɛ (thot). " Ɔkɔtɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Sɛ woantumi anyɛ saa a, metee. "Ɔkaa sɛ, "Shut waniwa na bue wɔn. " Na aberewa no too naniwa mu buee wɔn. Ɔkɔmdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Hwɛ wo kuru. " Na bere a aberewa no bae sɛ ɔrebɛhwehwɛ nkaedum no akyi. Afei ɔdanee ne ho (her aniwa) bio; obuee wɔn, na ɔhunuu sɛ wɔayi asase no nyinaa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Close wɔn bio. " Ɔtoo wɔn mu, na ɔbuee wɔn, na afie gyinaa hɔ pintinn. Na ɔsan sii ne bo sɛ ɔbɛhwehwɛ kuro bi a kɛseɛ yi wom; ɛhɔ na na ɛyɛ "huge". Ɔkɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Old ɔbea, it wo (lit. lit. Wɔ wo nsa mu no," aberewa no kae sɛ, "Thanks I for you. " Ɛnna aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mefa de meda wo ase? " Ɔkɔtɔdeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Mempɛ ade ketewa a ɛho nhia mpo, na me nso, (all) mani gye saa blood-cotton-tree a ɛwɔ hɔ no ho. " Saa aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Thising is that for they, take take. " Afei Ɔkɔtɔdeɛ no peree ne ho, na ɔde berebuo bɔɔ mu, na ɔde nkusua mmienu guu so, de nsa guu so. Ɔne wɔn dii. Na ɔboroo nkosua abien no, na ɔkɔhwehwɛɛ biribi ma ne mmofra no dii. Ɔbaabaa no banana, a na ɔne no te no, hyɛɛ aseɛ kɔɔ sikakyii, "Ehe! " aberewa no kae sɛ, "Dɛn ne asɛm no? " Na ɔkae sɛ, "Momma mewe a Eaglemmofra. " Na abere no kae sɛ, "Ɛhe na menya mmofra?" Ná nananom ketewa no fi bio, sikakyi! sikakyi! sikakyi! Ɛduruu aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Dɛn ne asɛm? " Na abofra no kae sɛ, Ma menwe a Eagle mmofra, sɛ me nsa nka bi a, me bɛwuu. Ah no kae sɛ, "Ah! Ɛsɛ sɛ eyi, me nana wu ma a Eagle mma asɛm nom? So wunya nkwaa, fa nkuma na kɔkɔbɔloll-cott-tree na fa wo mma no mmra. " Akuafoɔ no kɔɔ hɔ, (nkumaa), Pinpin! Ppin! Pinpin! Na ɛte sɛ nea dua no rehwe ase no, bere a Eagle mma no ɔpanin huruwi no, na ɔsɔre gyinaa berebuo no ano, na ɔmaa su bi so, ɔfrɛɛ maame, kae sɛ:

Sango, anomaa e!

Sango, the ahomaa, the Eagles child!

Sango, anomaa e!

Sango, sɛ ɔkɔdidi a, san ba!

Sango, anomaa e!

Sango, o!

Ɔbaa no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Sangri," na saa dua no (a na ɛreyɛ atwa mu koraa) san bae, na nnipa a na wɔakyere no nyinaa, wɔtwaa mu. Afei ɔfaa aduane a ɔde baeɛ no de maa ne mmofra. Afei ɔboroo wɔn yie, na ɔka sɛ, "Me kɔ, sɛ aberewa no ba sɛ ɔbɛgye wo a, fa wo. " Na aberewa no kae sɛ, "Go and strike the dua na fa abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no ba ma me nana mwe. " Na wɔkɔɔ hɔ mprenu, Pinpin! Ɔfrɛɛ ne maame, na ɔfrɛɛ no, na ɔfrɛɛ no mmuaeɛ na afei dua no kasae sɛ ɛrebɛbɔ fam sɛ "Ma sɛe! " (it). Wɔyii Eagle mma no; wɔde biako maa aberewa, nanso biako a na ɛda so ara wɔ kanea wɔ woawa-dua bi so. Wɔwosow biako, na wɔde maa aberewa banana no, na ɔde kaa abɔdeɛ a wɔato (she) ho. Ɛno akyi bere tiaa bi no, Ɔkɔdeɛ no baeɛ. Bere a ɔduruu dua a wɔhwee ase no, ɔhunuu ne mmofra no mu baako sɛ ɔte hɔ, ɔbisaa no nea ɛkɔɔ so, na ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ ɔnni amaneɛbɔ no nyinaa. Ɔkɔdeɛ no sii kwan sɛ ɔbɛnya aberewa no akuraa. Bere a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no, na aberewa no banana aba no redi ne mmofra no mu biako. Ɔkae sɛ, "Oldani, Im response you. " Ɔfiri ɔbaa dada no fie na ofii aseɛ (her nkonyaayi) wɔ kuro no kuro akyi. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sangri," na onipa biara ayera, na ɔsan kae sɛ, "Sangri," na fie biara paee prɛko pɛ na ɛnyɛ tenabea. "Sanguan," akuraa no san bɛyɛɛ kwaeɛ no bio. "Sangri," na aberewa no kɔn san bae.
