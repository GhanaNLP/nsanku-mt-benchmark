Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBAA SƐ KWAKU AANANSE, ANANSE NO, KƆSRAA APARANA

AANANE O! Ɔwaree Nasia Ewiem onyame anuonyampɔn no, na ɔkɔkaa Niamey (Ewiem-anuonyampɔn), ɛda a wo maame, Nasia, wuwu, na ɔka ɛda a wɔapaw ama ayi amanneɛ no kyerɛ me.

Ɛbɛyɛ nnansa akyi no, wɔba bɛka kyerɛɛ no sɛ Nesia, Ewiem ne anuonyampɔn maame no, wui. Mprempren Kwaku Ananse mogya a ɛwɔ mu no yɛ Berekumo, King Jay, Ɔkwakuo, Kwadua, ne Odan, Odan. Afei Kwaku Ananse ne wɔn dii nkitaho kae sɛ, Wɔabɛka ayi amanneɛ nti, ɛsɛ sɛ woka me ho. Enti Kwaku Ananse (nkra) de no kɔɔ Ewiem-anuonyam sɛ ɔreba dwumadie no ase. Afei Sky-god no maa nadesuadeɛ nyinaa boaboaa ano, te sɛ Omanhene asɛmbility. Na Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Sɛ anwummere tɔ dwo a, mɛpue. " Ananse no yɛɛ kotokuo bi, na emu na ɔde 'Berekumo, King Jay, Ɔkwakuo, Kwadua, ne Odan, Odan, ne kotokuo no too nabotiri, na ɔtwee ne ntokoo no kataa so. Afei ɔkasa kyerɛɛ abɔdeɛ ahodoɔ no sɛ, "Bere a meduruu Sky-gods no hɔ no, na ɛsɛ sɛ wogu osu. " Bere a ɔduruu Sky-god no, na "Berekuo no rema so (hi nne):

"Kurukusu, kuw! kuw!

Kurukusu, kuw! kuw! kuw! I.

Na Kwadu no nso asɔre atia (ne nne) sɛ:

"Kakum! "

Na Odwan no nso kaa (ne nne):

"Me! "

Eyi ne Kwaku Ananse awerɛhow. Nnipa no nyinaa kae sɛ, "Woadi yiye, ha yɛ ɔbarima biako a wotumi su na odi awerɛhow wɔ akwan ahodoɔ abiɛsa so. " Bere a odii awerɛhow wiei no, Sky-god no hwehwɛɛ ofie fɛfɛ a ɔbɛtenaa hɔ. Afei anɔpa, bere a nneɛma bɛda adi no, Skygod-god noaa aduan pa na ɔde maa Kwaku Ananse. Bere a Kwaku Ananse huu saa aduane yi, ɔkaeɛ sɛ, Mma obiara Berekuo, meremma Odan biara, ɛfiri sɛ meyɛɛ ankonam berɛ tenten. Kwaku Ananse dii nkogu, na wamma ne mogya ho nsɛm biara a ɛfa ho. Mprempren, ɔne wɔn kɔɔ so, na Sky-god kae sɛ, "Yɛbɛkura da no ayɛ awotwe. " Nanso, Odan no kae sɛ (to Kwaku Ananse), "Ɛkɔm rekum me, me nkɔ"; Ɔkwakuo ne Berekuo kaa adeɛ koro no ara. Mprempren Sky-god no kɔɔ tenaa nnipa a wɔhyia no ntam, na ɔmaa wɔbɔɔ Kwaku Ananse amanneɛ sɛ adeɛ pa a ɔyɛɛ no da a ɛtwa toɔ no, ɔbɛtumi ayɛ bio, efisɛ ɔne ne mpanimfoɔ no boaboaa wɔn ho ano (toee). Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, Mente nka yiye. 

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
