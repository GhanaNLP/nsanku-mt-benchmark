Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBƐYƐ WƆ NE ETIRE KAKU ANAN SII KWAN SO, ANANSE, BƐYƐƐ BEAEƐ KƐSEƐ WƆ KA A ƐTWA TOƆ WƆ KWAKE SO NO.

Ɛkɔm kɛseɛ bi baeɛ, na saa Kwaku Ananse no kaa sɛ ɔbɛkɔ akɔhwehwɛ nam ne nhabannuru na ɔde aba sɛ ɔne ne yere "Aso no betumi adidi. Ɔkɔɔ asuo bi mu na ɔkɔhyiaa nnipa; seesei saa nnipa yi a ohyiaa wɔn no, wɔn ho a wɔyii wɔn abufuo nti, na ɛyɛ ahonhom. Bere a Kwaku Ananse hyiaa Ahohonhom no, na wɔregyina nsuo no mu na wɔrekyekyere dry no de akyere apataa no. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "thoraderships, so metumi aba akɔbɔ akuturuku kakra? " Ahonhom no kae sɛ, "Bra. " Kwaku Ananse kɔe, na ohui sɛ wɔde wɔn ti nnompe resiesie stream no. Ahonhom no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse sɛ, Wo ahyɛ no nsow sɛ, nea yɛyi nsuo a ɛyɛ dry no, wo ma yɛyi wo tifi dompe no wɔ ahyɛdeɛ a wobɛtumi de ayɛ dɔteɛ nso mu? Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, "Mebɛma wo kwan, gye me. " Ahonhom no yii no fii hɔ maa no. Kwaku Ananse ne Ahonhom bɔɔ mu yɛɛ nsuo a ɛyɛ dry no mpa. Ahosanm no maa dwom bi sɔree sɛ:

"Yɛn, Ahonhom no, sɛ yɛpete asubɔnten no a, na yɛrekyere apataa no, na yɛde yɛn atitirefoɔ no yɛ nsuo no ho fĩ, de gu asuo no ho fĩ.

O, Spirits no, yɛregyigye nsuo no ho agu fĩ.

Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Saa dwom yi yɛ dɛ, so metumi ato ne bi? " Ahonhom no kae sɛ, "Sing ebi. " Na ɔmaa ne nne so:

"The Spirits, yɛregyigye nsuo no, yɛfa yɛn atitirefoɔ sɛ wɔregyegye nsuo no.

O, Honhom no, yɛregyigye nsuo no ayɛ fĩ.

Ɛfiri bere a Ɔbɔdeɛ no yɛɛ nneɛma no, so yɛfa yɛn atitirefoɔ sɛe nsuo no?

O, Ahonhom no, yɛn ho ayɛ nsuo no. Me de me retire ka nsuo a ɛdwo kɔ da da, O, Ahonhom no, yɛrebɔ nsuo no. "

Kwaku Ananse too nnwom wiei, na honhom ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Yɛadɛw, yɛanya apataa, wo kyɛfa yɛ kɛntɛn, gye na kɔdi, fa wo tifi apam, ka ho (wɔ wo nipadua ntam, na kɔ, nanso nea ɛsɛ sɛ yɛka paa ne sɛ, ɛda a wobɛto dwom biara, wo tifi dompe bɛbue so. " Ananse no kaa sɛ, "Ataa in borosoɔ a wode ama me no, nyinaa ne no, ne nyinaa yɛ pɛ Me kɔn dɔ, na me nso me dwom, asianeɛ bɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mede to no? " Ahonhom no kae sɛ, "This is well, gok out. " Annanse no de sii hɔ. Ahonhom no nso, wɔnyaa biribiara boom, na wɔn nso wɔkɔeɛ. Ahonhom no duu baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ sɛnea ɛteɛ no, afei wɔmaa wɔn dwom so:

"Yɛn, Ahonhom no, sɛ yɛpete asubɔnten no a, na yɛrekyere apataa no, na yɛde yɛn atitirefoɔ no yɛ nsuo no ho fĩ.

O, Spirits no, yɛregyigye nsuo no ho agu fĩ.

Na Ananse no tee dwom no, na ɔno nso faa no:

" Ɛfiri Ɔbɔdeɛ no nti, yɛde yɛn atitirefoɔ akɔ gu nsuo no ho fĩ? "

O, Spirits no, yɛregyigye nsuo no ho agu fĩ.

ƐnkyɛA biara na ɔwiee ne tifi dwam na ɔhwee ase. Kwaku Ananse maa so na ɔkuraa ne kokoɔ no ano. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ahohonhom, Ahoɔnm, me etire abɔ fam. " Ahonhom no tee, na wɔkae sɛ, "Ɛno ne Ananse no, ɔntee nea yɛka kyerɛɛ no, na ɔrefrɛ yɛn nyinaa sɛ, momma yɛnni n'akyi ntie nea ɔbɛka. " Ɛkame ayɛ sɛ ntɛm ara, Ananse no baa nkyɛn ntɛm ara. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Poo! Me agya mmofra, me retire abue na ɔhwee ase, nti me srɛ mo sɛ, sɛ me ayɛ wo opira biara a, fa kyɛ no; wo wɔ hokwan mu, nanso fa me retire san si baabi ma me. " Ahonhom no faa no, na wɔde sii ananmu. Wɔkae sɛ, "Afei sɛ woto dwom yi bio a, na ɛtɔ bio, bere a woafrɛ yɛn no, yɛremmɔ wo ho ban, nti wo ne wo nkɔ. " Ahonhom no si hɔ bio. Bere a wɔreto wɔn dwom no, na wɔreto dwom no. Na afei Kwaku Ananse fii ase too dwom bio, na ne retire no so tew na ɔhwee ase, kukum! Na oyii ano na oyii kasafĩ ne nsusuwii, ɔbɔɔ no akyi berɛmo no, na ne ho dwiriw no araa ma ne ho dwiriw no wɔ kwan no ho, faako a soro! (Bere a ɔhyɛɛ mu no, na wira mupae no ayɛ nnyigyei. Ɔkae sɛ, Kwan, gye me, na da a me ho yɛ me fɛ no, me bɛma wo bi. Ɛno nti na wobɛhunu Kwaku Ananse a ɔwɔ ti ketewaa bi a ne kɛse yɛ den pa ara; ne nyinaa fi naso yeɛ mu.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
