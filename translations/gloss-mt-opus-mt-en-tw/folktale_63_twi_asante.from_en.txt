Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ABOSOM, ADER-GODS KYERƐ NO, BAA ABUSUAKUO NO MU

Ɔbɔfoɔ bi ne ne yere tenaa ɔbɔɔfoɔ nsraban bi mu pɛn. Na wɔnni hia, na na wɔte hɔ reko ama wɔɔhyɛ. Na ɔbɔfoɔ no sɔre kɔ kwaeɛ muwɔ ahuhudeɛ mu. Ɔbaa fie de apataa-traps sii hɔ wɔ asubɔnten no mu, na ɔde apataa ne nsam nketewa no baa ne yere no nkyɛn. Mprempren wɔwɔ hɔ, na ne yere no bisaa no sɛ, Me gye di sɛ bere a wokunkum nam no, woyi no ma, anaasɛ wotɔn no na woadi (mfasoɔ no), enti kɔ da a wokɔ nwura no na woamfa nam biara amma a, me bɛsan akɔ me akuraa hɔ. Nnipa no de wɔn ani sii kwaeɛ no so. Ɔnam kwan so kɔeɛ, na wanhu ayamye biara aboa. Ɔkaa sɛ (to ne ho), Oh, me nsa aka mmuu. Seesei, bere a na ɔrehwɛ no, ɔhunuu Ɔtwee bi a ɛrekyinkyin wɔ baabi a ɛwɔ no hɔ. Ɔkaa sɛ, Wo deɛ, Ɔtwee, mekum wo. ɔbɔɔfoɔ no dii nakyi, akyirikyiri! nkyiri! (wa nnan nnyigyei). Ɔtwee no guanee na ɔkɔɔ nipadua no nhini a agyina pintinn no ntam. Ɔkaa sɛ, Grand Father Tano no, medwanee mo ho ahobammɔ ho bɔfoɔ no, ebia ɔbɛgya me. Me deɛ, me nim adeɛ a wɔfrɛ no Tano no, enti mefa no sɛ, mɛfa wo akɔgye me yere. Tano gyinaa hɔ, na ɔbuu ɔkasa no mu, sɛ, Ɔbɔfoɔ, gyae Ɔtwee hɔ kɔfa *fie, ɛfiri sɛ mfasoɔ pii bɛba ama wo wɔ me so. Tano Owuo Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɔbɔfoɔ, fitaah na wode me *ɔkyeame (Upsman)))) kɔeɛ? " Na ɔbɔfoɔ no buaa sɛ, "Agya, maniwa abere; na mepɛ sɛ nam kɔ ma me yere, bere a mehuu Ɔtwee no, na ɔreka sɛ wadwane ahobammɔ ama saa adeɛ yi; me kae sɛ menhunu hwee wɔ ho. Saa adeɛ yi kyerɛɛ sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mefa no fie, sɛnea na ɛwɔ hɔ (it) mfasoɔ pii wɔ so. Owuo bisaa Tano sɛ, "Worekɔ fie, na wunim sɛ bere a obi yareɛ, na mefa me mpa a wɔatɛn, na meba ne nkyɛn no, ɛnsɛ sɛ obiara siw ano, enti sɛ wokɔ fie a, dɛn na me ka kyerɛ wo: bere a obi yareɛ, na meremfa no nkɔ, me bɛbɔ yo akyerɛ yo uu, na woanya biribi wɔ akatua ama me, na saa akatua no, mogya nyinaa yɛ me pɛ, na ebia wo wɔ honam. " Governod a ɛba fam koraa no ka kyerɛɛ Owuo, Grastire, I ate, master. Nnipa no gyee Tano, na wɔne no baa fie. Tano gyinaa hɔ, ɔtoo dwom, na wɔde wɔn ho hyɛɛ kuw no mu. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, bere a nneɛma bɛda adi, na ɛberɛ a obi yareɛ paeɛ no, wɔde no kɔɔ Tano. (Tano) kaeɛ sɛ, "Wobɛwuwu. " Na ɛyɛ nokware, ɔwui. (To foforo) na ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Wonwuwu, enti wei na ɛde eyi baeɛ. " Berɛ a obi faa nneɛma no, ɔno (the couble-god) kaeɛ sɛ, "Me kɔ srɛ nkwa ma wo. " Na wo de saa afɔreɛ yi (afɛfɛdeɛ) ma Tano no, nokware bi na woyɛeɛ wo yie. Saa ara na yɛte nka sɛ aduan a wɔde di dwuma ketewaa bi no dɔɔso sen biara; enti nnipa kɔ hɔ kɔ Tano kɔfa ne mmofra binom fi mu.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
