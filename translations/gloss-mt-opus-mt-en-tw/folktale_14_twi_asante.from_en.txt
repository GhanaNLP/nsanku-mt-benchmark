Ɛneɛ na wɔyii wɔn sii hɔ. Sɛ wɔrekɔ no, na wɔafiri atɛkyɛ tadeɛ bi mu baeɛ. Putty-roomed Kwadua no kae sɛ, "Standa so bere a me rebisa mo biribi. Eyi kae sɛ, I I Afei, na ababaawa baako wɔ hɔ, a na wakɔ afuo hɔ, aka kakra ma wafiri hɔ no, aka akyerɛ ne maame sɛ, "Sɛ mmeranteɛ no ba a, fa baako to nkyɛn ma me. " Ɛnna bere a ɔsan firii afuo no mu no, ɔbisaa ne maame sɛ, "Ɛhe na me wɔ? " Obuae sɛ, "In He na mekɔɔ (to But) no, na wɔn mu akyekyɛ dedaw (mmeranteɛ) wɔn mu." Afei, na saa Efutuo no aba a wɔbɛtena ase a ne bo nyɛ den. Na ababaayewa no reretwe nwura no agu so, bere a Efutuo no kae sɛ, Dont betting me so. Asaayewa no kaa sɛ, Hwan ne saa? Ɔkaa sɛ, It. Asayewa no kaa sɛ, Dɛn na ɛyɛ asɛm? Ɔkaa sɛ, Me ne me agorɔ yɔnkonom reba aregyae (yo) mmabaawa, bere a wɔbɔɔ me, na wɔmaa me nipadua nyinaa guu fĩ, na ɛno nti na mebaa ha. Ɔkaa sɛ, Me deɛ, me kɔn dɔ wo, nti bra fie. Ɔne no kɔɔ, na ɔnyaa nsuo hohoroo no. Bere a ɔfaa suapɔn na ɔde guu ne dadwenɛ so kakra no, ɔhunuu sɛ ne dadwenɛ no yɛ nea ɛyɛ fɛ paa. Na ababaayewa no kae sɛ, Maame, ba bɛhwɛ, ahoɔfɛ a ɛte sɛɛ nyɛ adeɛ a wode bedi agoru. Na maame no kae sɛ, Finish hohoroo no. Black-Colob abɔmmɔfoa no nso de ahoɔden pa bɔɔ wɔn ho pɔw, na ɔkae sɛ, Me nso me ne no bɛbɔ de aboaboa nneɛma ne san ba ano.
