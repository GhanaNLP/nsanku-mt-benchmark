Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

SƐNEATHE SALUTATIONS) "KURONKORO" NE "BURU" KYERƐ NO

ƐDURUU hɔ bi no, na ɔyɛ ɔbɔfoɔ (cal) Kwaku, ne ne yere (local Buruwa), na ɛfiri sɛ na wɔn ho yɛ den nti, wɔhyɛɛ sɛ wɔbɛyɛ atenaeɛ foforɔ. Kwaku, ɔbɔfoɔ no, berɛ a ɔfiri nwura no mu no, ɔkunkum eku bi, nanso berɛ a ɔsan de baa fie ne asase so no, ɔkae sɛ, "This is noy (fore aduane). " Enti, ɔkɔɔ kɔtɔn no kwa. Bere a ɔkunkum adeɛ no, na ɛyɛ. Nanso, ne yere no, anhu sika no da, na ɔbisaa no sɛ, "Moamfa sika ansan amma, na moamfa nam ansan amma, na moamfa ne nyinaa aseɛ nso anfa he? " Obuae sɛ, "Bere a mɛkɔ, na nnipa rekɔtɔ (nam), na ɛbɛyɛ sɛ nnu no, mebubu no. " Da bi a ɔkunkum adeɛ bi, na ɔde sii kwan so, na ne yere no dii nakyi kɔhwɛɛ no. Afei ohu sɛ ɔtɔn saa dua no, faa sika no, na ɔtɔɔ nufuo kuku (a wɔfrɛ no dɔnto) na ɔbɛnom. Afei ne yere no san baa fie. Ɛno akyi bere tiaa bi no, Kwaku, ɔbɔfoɔ no nso baa hɔ. Ɔduruu ne fie hɔ ara pɛ na ɔkaa sɛ, "Anwum mmere pa, Buruwa. " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Yaa Ekudonto (Yesu, foundow for nfuafuo kuku)". Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na mobua me wɔ saa kwan yi so? " Obuae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuaeɛ a ɛfata wɔ nea ɛwɔ wo retire no ho. " ɔbɔɔfoɔ no anka hwee. Ɛda a ɛdi hɔ no, berɛ a nneɛma bɛda adi no, ɔkɔɔ afuo hɔ kɔsane; ɔkaa sɛ, "Anummmere pa. " Ya burecu buaeɛ sɛ, "Ya burecu. " Buruwa kaa sɛ, "Dɛn nti na wobua (my nkyea) wɔ saa ɔkwan yi so? " Na ɔbɔfoɔ no buae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ mmuae a ɛfata sɛ yɛbɛka nea ɛwɔ wo retire mu. " Saa na ɛbɛyɛɛ nkamfo wɔ abusuakuo no mu sɛ wɔmfa nkyene ahorow a "Yaa kurotia" ne "Ya burecu" no nni dwuma. Wɔde wɔn mfitiaseɛ ka ɔbɔfoɔ, Kwaku, ne ne yere Buruwa. Eyi ne me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, fa bi kɔ baabi foforo na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
