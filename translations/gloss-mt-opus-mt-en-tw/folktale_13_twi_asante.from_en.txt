SƐNEA AKOKONII, AKOKOKOƆNI NO KYERƐ NO, NYAA ANO A ANO YƐ NANAM

AGYA AANANSE, Ananse, ne ne mmofra Afudotwe (Belly-to-burst), Nyewankonfa (Thin-shhands), (and) Tikonono (Big-big) te hɔ. Na Ananse no ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ, "Ɛsɛ sɛ mo kɔ hwehwɛ borɔdɔma ma me di. " Nti, saa mmofra yi fii hɔ, na wɔtraa ase, na wɔbaa dua bi, na (borɔmma no abere, na mmofra no kɔɔ ase (dua). Afuadtwewtwe (Yafunu-to-apueeɛ) kaa sɛ Nyawntwitwa (Thin-shhans), "Climb ne foundu ebi afono. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Is, sɛ meforo me anan a, ɛbɛbubu." Tikonono (Big-big-head) kae sɛ, "Yo, Afudotwetwe, woforo. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Oh, sɛ meforo a, me yafunu no mpaeɛ; woforo wo ho. " Enti Tikonono (Big-bhead) foro kɔɔ soro. Ɔkotowo mfonini-tree mmofra no bi na ɔkɔɔ so bɔɔ akuturuku na ɔbɔɔ borɔdɔma, ɛnde ɔkae sɛ, "Anka ɛbɛyɛ dɛn? " Wɔn ahomegyeɛ no kaa sɛ, "Ah, (how a yɛnim)" no, ɔkaeɛ. Enti ɔkɔɔ so twetwee wɔn; afei obisaa wɔn bio sɛ, "Dabi? " Na wobuae sɛ, "Wo retire ne wo hwɛ. " Na bere a na ɔrekɔ fam wɔ ne retire ho no, na ɛsɛ sɛ ɔhwɛ, afei ne retire no bɔɔ fam, na ɔkae sɛ (a asɛmfua te sɛ "Kum! ") Bere a Belly-like bɔɔ bɔɔl no! e-to-mpaeɛ, yafunu a ɛpaee wɔ ɔtopae, ta! Thun-shes, bere a ɔbɛdwane akɔka akyerɛ nagya no, ne anan bɛbɔ na ɔda so ara wɔ ɛban no akyi. Na Akokɔnini no rehoro ne ɔkra, na ɔrefi adi akɔtow nkesua (fi afɔredeɛ no mu), bere a ohu sɛ Thin-shhains ahwe ase na ne anan abubu, na aka wɔ ɛban no nkyɛn. Berɛ a ɔrebɛse no, nano kyerɛɛ sɛ:

" Na ɔrekɔrese no, Nano kyerɛɛ aseɛ.

Bere a ɔrebese no, na ne ano aka "ase. "

Ɛno nti na Mpanimfoɔ no kyerɛ sɛ, "Sɛ obi hwe ase a, kɔ hwehwɛ no, na ɔnse. " Mprempren Akokɔnini no deɛ, ɔyɛɛ nade sɛɛ, na ɔfrɛɛ no, nti ne ano yɛɛ no agyiraehyɛdeɛ. Kan no na ɔwɔ ano a ɛyɛ fɛ, ɛyɛ fɛ.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
