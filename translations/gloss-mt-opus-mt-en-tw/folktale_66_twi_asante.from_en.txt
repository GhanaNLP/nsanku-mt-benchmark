Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

KƆKƆKƆƆKINAKA, A WƆKYERE WƆN KƆƆ KWAKU ASENE ANANSE WƆ ƆBƐSEƐ AKYI.

Ɛduruu berɛ a na Kkɔɔkyinaka nwoma no rekɔ Kwaku Ananse, Ananse, wɔ dwumaɛ a sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma (£4) no mu duru bɛreeɛ ho hwehwɛ. Seesei, bere a Kwaku Ananse nni biara no, ɔkɔɔ nadamfo, Ntontom, nkyɛn kɔfa bi kɔmaa nadamfo, Kɔkɔɔtinaka nwoma no. Ɛyɛɛ da a ɛtwaa mu a na Ntontom no ahyɛ bɛseɛ no ntua no, na Ntontom no ka kyerɛɛ Kwaku Ananse, Go na ɔnyaa me sika kɔkɔɔ mituma maa me. Kwaku Ananse buae sɛ, "Kɔkɔɔkyia nwoma no te atifi wɔ anɔpa abo a ɛyɛ dua, enti kɔgye wo sika kɔkɔɔ mfutuma. " Ntontom no bisaa sɛ, ɛbɛyɛ dɛn na mɛkɔ? Anase no buae sɛ, "abɔdeɛ no betumi atu kwan. " dua no bɛbu, na woanya Kɔkɔɔkyia nwoma, na woanya wo sika kɔkɔɔ mituma no akyi. Ntontom no kɔeɛ na akwantu no bɔɔ dua. Ɛberɛ a na ɛbɛyɛ sɛ wobɛbu no, Kɔkɔɔkyika nwoma no firii hɔ kɔeɛ, pa buu bubuu bu! Afei Ntontom no baa, na ɔkae sɛ, Christances, Kwaku Ananse. Ɛno nti na yɛwɔ abobuo a ɛtu mmirika, Abɔdeɛ a wɔfrɛ no Kɔkɔkyika nwoma no angyee amfiri Kwaku Ananse hɔ sɛ, ɛsɛ sɛ Ntontom no kɔ troth-cotton-tree no. Eyi, mabakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ me dɛ a, sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, (anaasɛ) fa bi, fa bi fa bi kɔ baabi foforo, na ebinom nso ba me nkyɛn.
