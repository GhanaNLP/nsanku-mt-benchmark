Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

ƐNO NTI, ANANSE, MMIRIKA AHODOƆ A ƆWƆ NSUO ANI SO NO

KWAKU AANANSE, Ananse, kɔkaa asɛm no kyerɛɛ Ɔkraman, Kraman no, anka mepɛ sɛ me ne mo kɔ si akuraa ketewa a ɛwɔ mu. Kraman no kae sɛ, Me nso, anka saa. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, bere a Sunday-dae ahwe ase no, ɛdwoada a ɛdi hɔ yi, kɔ na kɔgye hama a wɔde hama ayɛ; Me nso, me bɛnya hama-creeper na yɛbɛfiri adi. Bio nso, mɛtwa twa na ma nsuo ahyɛ mu ma, na wo nso, wo de, wo ho agu fĩ bi, ebia baabi a yɛrekɔ no, nsuo biara nni hɔ. Kwaku Ananse de nsuo bi guu mu; Kraman nso nyaa ɛwoɔ sɛ wɔde (his) towa bɛhyɛ mu. Ná wɔka wɔn ho, na wɔadu baabi a ɛbu kwan no, na wɔn ho akye. Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Momma yɛnnom yɛn nsuo no bi. Kraman no kaa sɛ, Mewɔ me ano bi a wɔbɛnom. Kwaku Ananse faa bi, na ɔde ne ano ka kyerɛɛ no; ɔkaa sɛ, Ohia, ɛhe na eyi firi baeɛ? Kraman no kaa sɛ, Ɛwɔ me yafunu so. Ananse no kaa sɛ, Aane! Sɛnea wɔtenaa hɔ sɛ wɔrebɛsan anya wɔn ahome no, Kwaku Ananse kaa sɛ, Kraman, momma yɛn nni agorɔ. Kraman no kaa sɛ, agorɔ bɛn? Ananse no buaeɛ sɛ, The cand-binding game; yy; Moraman no kae sɛ, Dabi, wode me di kan. Ananse no kae sɛ, Gye wo ho, bere a wo ɔpanin reka asɛm bi akyerɛ wo no, woyɛ no anaa? Kraman no kae sɛ, Who, ma no ntra hɔ sɛnea wopɛ. Aanse no kae sɛ, bransɛn me so. Na ɔde no kɔɔ ne retire so sɛ ɔrebɛtɔn no sɛ ɔbɛnya biribi adi. Bere a Kwaku Ananse tee sɛ no, ɔmaa awerɛhosu so kae sɛ:

I, Ananse Akuma, mpo me nsa nkaa nea ɛfata sɛ aboa kɛseɛ bɛtɔn me.

Kraman bi a ɛnsom hwee yi nko ara, wɔbɛtɔn me.

Metumi agye me?

O, kwan, gye me!

Kwaku Ananse betumi asu na osu nkutoo. Kraman soaa no kosii sɛ ɔduruu asuwa bi. Afei, Ɔdɛnkyɛm, Akokɔsrade no, tee nkra a ɛyɛ borɔdɔma no. Ɔbaa no, ɔbisaa Kraman nea na ɛyɛ asɛm no. Kraman no tow Kwaku Ananse guu fam, dwanee, na Akokɔsrade no mu yɛɛ hare Kwaku Ananse. Na Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Agya, adeɛ ayamye bɛn na metumi de ama wo de akyerɛ sɛ mani gye me ho? Akokɔsrade no kae sɛ, Yɛnni biribiara koraa. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, Sɛ wo wɔ mmofra a, ɛnneɛ ɔkyena bɛba abɛhyehyɛ wɔn ti nhwi ama wɔn koraa. Akokɔsrade no kae sɛ, "Me tie. " Kwaku Ananse baa fie; ɔkae sɛ, "Aso, kɔ hwehwɛ palm-nuts ne gyene, kosi ɔkyena a mebɛkum ɔkyena na me ne brinna. g akyi a yɛwɔ kwan ne asoɛe a yɛbɛdie. " "Aso kɔgyee bi, na Ananse bɔɔ ne sekan ne ɛtɔɔ, na ɔde kɔɔ asuawa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Dacodicold, the Nsuo-Amal, I mereba mo so. " Na ɔde fufuo no kɔtoo nsuo no mu. Akokoɔkɔsrade no baeɛ, na ɔrebɛfa fufuo no adi, berɛ a Ananse no de ne kkan bɛtwaɔ no, gyaya! Nanso wannya no (properly) na ɔfiri hɔ. Kwaku Ananse san baa fie. Aso bisaa no sɛ, "Ɛhe na saa aboa no wɔ? " Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Gye wo ho; onipa fi akwantu bi mu, na mpo nnyaa nahome, na wofiri aseɛ reha no nsɛmmisa. " "Aso kae sɛ, "Mehunuu sɛ wo nsa anka no. " Kwaku Ananse anka hwee. Afei anɔpa koraa, Aso paa, "Aso kae sɛ ɔrekɔ adwa no. Berɛ a ɔduruu hɔ no, na Akokoɔsrade a ɛda asubɔnten ano no, na sillle-flif no rebɔ kuru. "Aso" san baa fie bɛkaa ne kunu asɛm kyerɛɛ no. Kwaku Ananse kae sɛ, "Afei wuhu sɛnea aduru bi a mewɔ no, wɔyɛ; sɛ mekum nam (da koro) a, ɛnyɛ kosi sɛ nneɛma bɛda adi, da a edi hɔ no, mekɔ akɔfa, nanso mepɛn, na mmom mema mo akwaaba sɛ moahu. " Kwaku Ananse twaa poma bi na ɔde no kɔɔ asuwa a ɛwɔ hɔ no, na ɛhɔ na ɔbaa Akokoɔkɔsrade no mu. Ɔne poma no bɔɔ mu kae sɛ, "Akokɔsrade, mowu? Akokoɔkɔsrade, wuwuwu? " na ɔdanee no kɔɔ nanim ne akyi. Na Akokɔsrade no da so ara wɔ hɔ, nanso na onwu. Bere a Kwaku Ananse rebɛyɛ ne nsa aka ne ho no, na Akokɔsrade no nam ne so mpofirim, kuadwuma! Kwaku Ananse dii aperepere, petepeteee! pesewre, na ɔhwehwɛɛ kwan a ɔbɛfa so adwane. Ɛno nti, bere a Ananse twa nsuo no ho fa no, ɔde ahopereɛ ka ho; Ɔsuro sɛ Akokɔsrade no bɛba abɛkyere no bio.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
