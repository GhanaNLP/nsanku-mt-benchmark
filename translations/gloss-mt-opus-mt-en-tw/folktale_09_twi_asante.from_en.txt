SƐNEA ƆKƆTƆ, KƆTƆ NO KƆƆ SO YƐƐ HONO NE ABEREWA, ABEREWA, NYA DAN.

Ɔbaa bi kuraa ne abofra; bere a ɔsomaa no (anyinamwrow) no, ɔkae sɛ, "Me nkɔ. " Ɔno na ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Ɛnneɛ tena hɔ, wo ne wo nyansa. " Akwame bere a ɔsomaa no (RARRAD biara) no, ɔkaa sɛ, "Me nkɔ. " Afei da bi ɔkae sɛ, "Go ne mentetew nsuo mu mma me. " Na ɔkae sɛ, "Me nkɔ. " Na aberewa no boroo no kae sɛ, "Motwɛn wɔ hɔ, na wobehu. " Ɔkasaeɛ sɛ, "Mebɛkɔ hwehwɛ 'Intening' sɛ mebɛhunu (sɛnea ɛteɛ)'. Ɛnna mehyɛɛ kwan, na mekɔɔ mmirikatuo kɛseɛ (nanan nnyigyei a ɔde tu mmirika no, na ɛte sɛ nea..., titiriw, yiyedi. Wɔ tenten a ɔduruu akuraa bi ase, na ɔhunuu aberewa bi a ɔte hɔ. Bere a ɔhunuu no no, na ɔreretwe ne ho bio. Ɛduruu aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Me banana ba no, mabɛn wo. " Na abofra no kɔɔ anim; na aberewa no kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na nsuo afiri waniwa mu adwane wo saa? " Ɔkae sɛ, "Me maame ne me tenaa hɔ da biara, bere a ɔsɔree no, ɔkae sɛ, 'Justine w' twɛn hɔ na wubehu wo, na ɔrebɛbɔ me so, enti me kae sɛ, anka menkɔ akɔhwehwɛ 'hu sɛ dɛn na metumi ahu?' Ebi te sɛ). " Na aberewa no kaa sɛ, "I am is he. " Ɛnna ɔkaeɛ sɛ, "Go kɔ fie a ɛwɔ hɔ no akyi na fa bakyerɛ na fa wɔn ba; berɛ a wobɛkɔ akɔduru hɔ no, nea ɛbɛka sɛ 'Dig me so, di me so, nkɔ soro; nanso nea ɛka sɛ, Mommma me nni me so, agoro no, saa ara na ɔtuu fam. Obuae sɛ, Ho! Ko a ɔkɔɔ hɔ no nokware; bakyerɛ no mu bi ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Digi me wu no," p, di me so, fa me tu me fo;" afoforo nso kae sɛ, "Don do me di me so, nntu me, nntu mo ho so. " Na ebinomnom a wɔkae sɛ, "Don't di me so," na wɔne ebinom a ɔtutuui no. Na ɔne wɔn baa hɔ; na aberewa no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, "Skin wɔn na wɔde wɔn guu ogya no so. " Na ɔwui na ɔde wɔn guu (ogya) so, na sɛ wɔnoa wie a, ɔda aduane bi adi, na ɔde wɔn kɔtoo aberewa anim. Na ɔkae sɛ, "Go on the kuro akyi, na hwɛ asaw a ɛwɔ nam a ɛda so, hwɛ ntam (nam) na fa wapɛde. " Na abofra no faa bi, na ɔbae, na ɔde too ogya no so, na ɔyɛɛ no kwan. Bere a ɔwiee no, ɔde sii aberewa anim. Ɛduruu aberewa no kaa sɛ, "So wɔawie? " Ɔbuaa sɛ, "Aane, moboysieth. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Brame and Bund the fufuo. " Na aberewa no kɔtoo nakyi; ɔkae sɛ, "Gat yere a wɔanoa no, hwiee gu wɔ ɛhwene mu, ne pounds. " Na abofra no kae sɛ, "Ɛyɛ nea ɛyɛ nwonwa, sɛ yɛweund fufuo wɔ onipa ɛhwe mu; eyi ne nea maame kaa ho asɛm no. " Eyi nyinaa na ɔkae wɔ ne retire no mu. Ɔkɔɔ so twitwaa; nanso bere a ɔde twitwaa no, ɔde nkakrankakra paa. aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Pound it very, sɛ woanhyɛ no den a, wo kokoɔ no bɛbue. " Na ɔde twitwaa no, pipe pii! pipe! Na sɛ ɔyɛ wie a, ɔhwie gu nkwan so, na ɔde si hɔ aberewa. Ɛduruu aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Me nana, bɔ me din na gye na di. " Na ɔkae sɛ, "Wore are Old-grave-gramin. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ee! Ee nnhuu, sɛ ɔbɛtumi adidi. " Na aberewa no dii mfe awotwe no nyinaa. Wɔ nna awotwe no, ɔnam saayɛ so yɛɛ no daa. Na da bi abofra no kɔɔ adwa hɔ sɛ ɔrepete nsuo. Kɔtɔ no huu no, na ɔkae sɛ, "Adɛn nti na obi ba ayɛ teateaa saa? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Manald-graminal a me ne no te, na sɛ me ne noa aduane na mema no a, ɔka sɛ, 'Me din a me ne no bedidi'; meka sɛ, 'Wɔrefrɛ mo Old-wogravex'; na mese, 'Ee! Worenhu kwan a wode bedidi, na afei ɔadidi; ɛno nti na me ho yɛ me den no. " Kɔtɔ no kae sɛ, "She na ɔfrɛ no Old-woman-Mosono. " abofra no kae sɛ, "Ho. " Na ɔde ne nsu-pot sii ne retire so. Kɔɔtɔ no kaa sɛ, "Done werɛ mfi sɛ woresan aba. " Nabofra no fii hɔ; bere nyinaa a ɔbɛba no, na ɔkɔ so ka sɛ, "Old-women-Mosono, Mosono, Mosono. " Bere a ɔduruu kuro no kuro akyi no, ne werɛ firii, na ɔbɛdii nasuo na nsuo no tɔeɛ, na ɔkɔɔ fie. Ɛduruu aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Ɛhe na nsuo-pot no wɔ? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sɛnea na meba no, medii nano, na ɛyɛɛ basaa. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sta go foforo na ɛkɔeɛ. " Na ɔde kɔɔ, na Kɔtɔ no kae sɛ, "Wosane asane anaa? " Na ɔno nso kae sɛ, "So woka ne ho asɛm kyerɛ me, me werɛ aho, na me aba abɛbisa bio. " Kɔtɔ no kaa sɛ, "She na ɔyɛ Old-woman-Monsono. " Na ɔkae sɛ, " Na ɔkae sɛ, "Ho. " Ɔde ne nyinaa kuraa mu kae. " Ɛduruu fie, ne berɛ a ɔduruu fie a ɔde sii hɔ (kuku). Ɛduruu aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Go and dire leve bakyerɛ na fa wɔn ba. " Ɔyɛɛ ne nyinaa sɛnea ɛsen biara, na ɔde aduane no sii aberewa no anim, na aberewa no kaa sɛ, "Mention me din na mefaa no sɛ medidi. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo ne Old-worold-grandamother. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "I kyerɛ me din. " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Wo ankasa din? " Bere no kae sɛ, "Wo ankasa din anaa? " abere no kae sɛ, "Aane. Ɔkaa sɛ, " Wɔfrɛ no Old-wold-Monsono. " Saa aberewa no. aberewa no sii kwan so, na ɔkaa sɛ, Merekɔ onipa a ɔka kyerɛɛ mo sɛ me din de Mosono no hwehwɛ. Na ɔkɔɔ asuwa no, na ɔkɔhyiaa Ɔbontoya, ɔpanyin no, (nea ɔde yɛɛ no basabasa no, kae sɛ, Bondon, mo maame! Bonto, mo agya! So woka kyerɛɛ me banana akorae sɛ wɔfrɛɛ me Mosono? " Ɔkae sɛ, "It not. " A aberewa no huu sɛ Adwene (apam) no regyina abɛn; ɔnwiinwii sɛ, "Wo Adwene, wo maame! Maame! Adwene, wo agya! agya! agya kaa no kyerɛɛ me banana ankora a na wɔfrɛ no Mosono no? " Ɔkaa sɛ, "Na ɛnyɛ me. " Ɔhunuu sɛ Kɔtɔ no gyina hɔ. Ɔsrɛɛ no sɛ, "Kɔtɔ, wo maame! Maame! Kɔtɔ, wo agya! agya! Ɔno na ɔka kyerɛɛ me banana ankora sɛ mete Nkran, me yɛ Ɔdehye mogya, na wɔfrɛ me Mosono? " Kɔtɔ no kae sɛ, "Aane, meka kyerɛɛ no, na ɛdɛn nti na woama obi ba kwan ma wanyin a ɛyɛ tratraa saa? " Na aberewa no bo fuwii, na ne calabash a ɔkuraa no, ɔmaa no so tow guu Kɔtɔ no so. Ɛno nti na Kɔtɔ no hono wɔ nakyi no. Kɔtɔ a atwam no, na hono biara nni hɔ. Kɔtɔ no nso faa dɔte fitaa, na ɔtow guu aberewa no ti so. Ɛno nti na mmaa mpanyimfo atifi ntadeɛ no atifi ntadeɛ no wɔ ti n fitaa.
