Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

ABAYWA BIARA BETUMI AMA WHO AHWEHWƐ ADE NO WƆ AWAREƐ MU

Ɛdii bere bi a na ababaayewa bi a wɔfrɛ no Kwaaboaso bi wɔ hɔ. Obi a nani awu a wɔmaa no waree no kae sɛ, Mempɛ no. Wɔde no maa ɔbɔfoɔ bi, na ɔkaa sɛ, Ugh! ɔbarima yi wɔ ntontom wɔ ne ho, ɔmpɛ no.

Da bi a ofii hɔ kɔɔ afuw no mu no, ɔkae sɛ ɔrekɔtwa bɔɔrɔde no. Ɔfaa kkan bi bɔɔ borɔdeɛ no, kɔhwɛɛ! Sɛ nnipa nketewa no te ɔbrɛ no so a, wɔsii fam, na wɔtoo Kaboaboaso, wɔkaa sɛ, "Wo na woyɛ nea ɛsɛ sɛ woyi obi ma no, wowosow wo etire, pusu! pusu! " Na mmati no akye ne nsɔmu kae sɛ: "Mommra, momma yɛnnya ne so nkɛntɛnso.

Yɛn nsa ka no, O!

Yɛn Kawaso bɛtumi anya so nkɛntɛnsoɔ.

Ɛba no, momma yɛnnya ne so nkɛntɛnso.

Yɛn nsa ka no, O!

Afei, bere a saa ɔbɔfoɔ a wɔmaa no Kawaboaso no nne no, ɔkae sɛ, "Mebɛhwɛ nea ɛyɛ asɛm no, efisɛ yɛnni bɔne bi sɛ yɛbɛtua akane bɔne. " Bere a ɔkɔɔ no, na Kaboaboaso wɔ hɔ, na mmati no bɔɔ no. Afei ɔbɔfoɔ no bɔɔ tuo wɔ mmati hɔ, na baako hwee ase gui. Nea odi kan wɔ mmati so no kae sɛ: "Ɔno na ɔwɔ nufufuo a wɔnom, na ɔde nsa guu mu; ɔde no too saa berɛ yi, (sɛ afei ɔde natadeɛ bɔɔ ne ho). " Bio ɔbɔfoɔ no too tuo, na ɔfoforo no hwee ase. Wɔ mmati no so panyin kae sɛ, oduruyɛfoɔ no wɔ nsɛmfua a wɔnom na wɔdi no so nkonim, na ɔde no kɔtoo nkyɛn. ɔbɔɔfoɔ no kum mmati no nyinaa gye nea wadi mfeɛ nnum pɛ. Ɔpanyin no too nsa frɛɛ ɔbɔfoɔ no sɛ, Bra. ' ɔbɔɔfoɔ no mu panyin kae sɛ, Hwɛ me dan a ɛwɔ hɔ no, na wubehu aduro a ɛwɔ tuo no ho. akuduro no ne tuo ano aba a woayi no nyinaa, fa nea ɛyɛ wo dea, na fa waboaso nso, ansa na wobɛkɔ, kɔtwa banana, na wo kɔ a, tow gu, na ama mmoaa foforo no asɔre, na wɔakyere wo, afei wɔ berɛ a wɔagyae sɛ wɔbɛfa wɔn, biako no, woafiri hɔ akyɛ, na wadi kan tete. " Na ɔkɔɔ twaa Nana, na ɔfaa K, na ogyee banana. Woso nso, na bere a ɔduu kwan no so no, ɔtow kwadu biako nyinaa kɔe. Ɔkɔɔ so yɛɛ saa fie kwan no nyinaa. Na bere a berɛ tiawa bi pɛ na aka ansa na wɔadu fie, hwehwɛɛ mmoatia a wɔreyɛ no. Na ɔtow kwadu no nko ara guu hɔ, na mmati no kɔɔ akyi (du-baako). Na ɔbɔfoɔ no san de Kawaboaso baa ne mogya mu. Afei ɔbɔfoɔ no firii adi kɔɔ ne fie. Mprempren, na ɔbɔfoɔ no te hɔ, bere a ɔhunuu asɛnkafoɔ wɔ nbaabi, na ɔkaa sɛ, "Dɛn ne amaneɛbɔ? " Na asɛnkafoɔ no kae sɛ, "Kwaboaso se waka akyerɛ akuraa no Eretire sɛ ɔmma no adanmudeɛ, na ɔka sɛ seesei ɔwɔ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔbɛware wo. " Na ɔbɔfoɔ no kae sɛ, "Meda nsɛmfua a ɛfiri akuraa no Odikro ano no ase, nanso metumi ware ayawa no, ɛfiri sɛ me nsa da so ara aka bi. Me nipadua so ha. " Ɛno nti na mpanimfoɔ kyerɛ sɛ, "Bere a wɔde wo kɔ awareɛ mu ma obiara no, ware no, ɛfiri sɛ wonnim no gye sɛ da bi a wowɔ ahiadeɛ mu no, ɔbɛgye wo. "

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
