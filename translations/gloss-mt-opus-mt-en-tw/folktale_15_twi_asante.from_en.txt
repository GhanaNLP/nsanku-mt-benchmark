Yɛn nkyerɛ ankasa, yɛnkyerɛ sɛ yɛyɛ nokware, (sɛ nea yɛbɛka sɛ ɛyɛ nokware)

SƐNEA ƐBƐYƐ A, NNIPA BI REYƐ ANWUMENU, NA AFOFORO NKUTOO NKUTOO

Na wɔka sɛ ɛhɔ (concent) yɛ ɔbaa, na ɔkɔɔ Palm-tree bi a ɛgyina nsuo no mu sɛ ɔbɛtumi asusu mmofra-o ho. Na bere a ɔkɔɔ no, Palm-tree no kae sɛ, Mma wo nea wopɛ, nanso abofra a me ne no bɛbɔ a ɔbɛma wo no, sɛ ɔsɔre a, wɔrenyɛ adwuma biara. Ɔkaa sɛ, Mepene so (to nna abien ni. Ɛnyɛ nna abiɛsa, na ɛnyɛ nna abiɛsa, bere a ɔne wɔn dii awoɔ no. Te sɛ mmofra a wɔwɔ adan atifi a ɛhɔ yɛ kusuu no, na ɔnnyin ntɛm ara. Na ne maame ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, Awomen no mu biako koraa nni hɔ a wuhia sɛ wohwɛ Palm-tree no, ɛnsɛ sɛ woforo koro da. Ɔkaa sɛ, Maame, metee. Seesei, da bi a maame no fii hɔ kɔɔ afuo hɔ. Ɔkɔɔfrɛ m'adɔfo, na ofii ase dii agoru, na ɔkyeree nasen ne asen ho nsɔmu na ɔhwee ase hwee ase. Na ababaayewa no kae sɛ, Saa hama a etwa to no, kɔ de me Palm-tree fiber bi a metumi asan akanyan no brɛ me. Me abarimaa no fii adi bɛtɔɔ bi. ababaayewa no kae sɛ, Mempɛ sɛ wubenya eyi, na ebinom nso ahwehwɛ afi Palm-tree a ɛwɔ nsuo no mu. Ɛneɛ, abofra no de sii hɔ wɔ ebinom hwehwɛ mu. Ohuruw kɔɔ soro no, ɔkra! ɔkra! ɔkra! Ɛde kɔpem sɛ ɔbɛduruu dua no kɔn no mu. Ɔyii ne sekkan no, na ntɛm ara na ɔde ne nsa kaa no, Palm-dua no mu paapaee mmienu, na abɔfra no yafunu mu yɛɛ mmienu, na pen-dua no mu yɛɛ den bio. Ɛhɔ na ɛyɛɛ no ɔkra. Bere a ne maame duu nsuo no mu no, ankyɛ na ohuu sputements bi a ɛwɔ ne so, na ɔkaeɛ sɛ:

Rease quension yi da nsuo a ɛte sɛ me abofra Akwasi Kwasaman no so.

Ɔkaa sɛ, Maame, ɛyɛ me.

Ɔkae sɛ, Dɛn nti na Palm-tree kraw wo?

Ɔkae sɛ, I kɔtwitwa Palma fiber no ho de ma me dɔfo ne Palm-tree draw me.

Me maame no kae sɛ, Mo, Palm-tree yi gye no, nya ne so nkɛntɛnso, nya ne, me ba Kwasaman.

Palm-tree sɛe no, nya ne so nkɛntɛnso, nya ne so nkɛntɛnso, na ɛma Palm-eco nya ne so nkɛntɛnso.

Na maame no kɔe. Na agya no nso baeɛ, na ɔkaa sɛ:

Rease sputens bɛn na ɛda nsuo a ɛte sɛ me abofra kumaaman yi so?

Ɔkaa sɛ, Agya, ɛyɛ me.

Ɔno (agya) kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na Palm-tree kraw wo? "

Ɔkaa sɛ, "I kɔtwitwaa Palm fiber ho kɔbɔɔ me dɔfo ne Palm-tree draw me. "

Ɔno (The agya) kae sɛ, "Mo, Palm-tree yi gye no, nya ne so nkɛntɛnso, me ba Kusaman.

Palm-tree nya ne so nkɛntɛnso, na ɛma ɔde ne ho to ne so, na ɛno nya ne so nkɛntɛnso.

Na agya fii fie, na ɔka kyerɛɛ akuraa no Etitire, ne akua no Etire nso, frɛɛ nnipa no nyinaa, na wɔbaa Palm-tree nan, na akuraa no Etire yii (ne nne) so, na wɔkae sɛ:

"Nse sputension" yɛ nea ɛda nsuo a ɛte sɛ nanana Akwasi Kwasaman no so?

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Grand Fathur, it I. "

Ɔno (Etire of the akuraa) kae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na Palm-treggri wo? "

Ɔkaa sɛ, "I kɔtwitwaa Palm fiber ho kɔbɔɔ me dɔfo ne Palm-tree draw me. "

Etire a ofi akuraa no kae sɛ, "Mo, saa Palm-tree no, nya ne so nkɛntɛnso, nya ne so nkɛntɛnso, nya Me banana Kwasaman.

Palm-tree nya ne so nkɛntɛnso, na ɛma ɔde ne ho to ne so, na ɛno nya ne so nkɛntɛnso.

Na akua no Etitire no kɔɔ adi, na nkuraasefoɔ nyinaa too dwom koro no ara kyerɛɛ Palm-tre no sɛ ɔmmɛdaadaa no. Na afei ne dɔfo no bɛkaa ho, na ɔkae sɛ: "Hwan ne se sputens a ɛda nsuo so, te sɛ dɔm Akwasi Kwasaman? "

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ me, wo ɔdɔfo ankasa. "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na Palm-tree kraw wo? "

Ɔkaa sɛ, "I kɔbuu Palm fiber me nti, na Palm-tree no de me hyehyɛɛ fam. "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Palm-tree gyae no, gyae no.

Palm-tree gyae me dɔfo Akwasi Kwasaman.

Palm-tree gyae no, gyae no, wogyae no, Palm-tree gyae no. "

Bere a ɔkasae no, Palm-tree no buee kakra, na ɔdɔfo no yafunu mu puei kakra. Na ɔmaa ne nne so bio, kae sɛ:

"Nse sputension" yɛ nea ɛda nsuo so, te sɛ dɔfo Akwasi Kwasaman?

Ɔkaa sɛ, "Ɛyɛ me, wo ɔdɔfo ankasa. "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Dɛn nti na Palm-tree kraw wo? "

Ɔkaa sɛ, "I kɔbuu Palm fiber me nti, na Palm-tree no de me hyehyɛɛ fam. "

Ɔkae sɛ, "Palm-tree gyae no, gyae no.

Palm-tree gyae me dɔfo Akwasi Kwasaman.

Palm-tree gyae no, gyae no, wogyae no, Palm-tree gyae no. "

Na afei ne dɔfo baa adi prɛko pɛ, na ɔpueeɛ nadɔfo bosom no so, na wɔdwaneee wɔn ho, na wɔdanee ngo. Na nnipa a wɔkɔɔ hɔ ntɛm na wɔn ho dwiriw wɔn wɔn wɔ wɔn anim no, wɔn ho yɛ fɛ paa; nanso wɔn a wɔn nsa nka bi no, wɔn na wɔn ho yɛ fɛ. Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ, fa bi fa bi wɔ baabi foforo, na ma ebinom nsan mmra me nkyɛn.
