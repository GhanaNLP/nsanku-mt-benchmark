SƐNEA ANENE KƆN, KWAAKWAADABI KYERƐ NO, BƐYƐƐ CAME WENU NA NABA NO BƐYƐƐ TUNTUM

Ɔbaa bi a ne din de Ata (Nta) nam kwan so de ne abofra no kɔɔ baabi kɔbɔɔ ntasuo (for apam). Bere a wɔduruu asuawa hɔ no, nabofra no kae sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔ soro nsuo no so. " Nanso aberewa kae sɛ, "Momma yɛnkɔ fam, ɛfiri sɛ ɛwɔ fam a mmoawa nketewa wɔ. " Nabofra no kaa sɛ, "Me deɛ, me kɔ round, ɛwɔ sɛ wawa nketewa no wɔ hɔ. " Nti na ne maame kɔɔ nsuo ase. Wɔtenaa hɔ berɛ tenten, na ne abofra no nyaa bi, na ɔbaa nkyɛn bɛhwehwɛɛ ne maame. Sɛnea na ɔrekɔ no, (a na ɛwɔ hɔ) ne maame calabash floating wɔ nsuo no anim. abofra no kae sɛ, "Ah not not one adeɛ biako, ɛnyɛ mmienu (nneɛma), maame calabash. " Na ɔfrɛɛ no nne, na ɔfrɛɛ no maame sɛ:

"I kaeɛ sɛ, na mobɛtie, meka saa, na morenkyerɛ.

Me kae sɛ, Maame, momma yɛn nkɔ round, ɛfiri sɛ ɛwɔ atifi fam a mmoawa nketewa no te'!

Na ɔkae sɛ ɔrekɔ fam, efisɛ ɛwɔ asuwa fam ne baabi a mmoawa nketewa no te.

Na ɛdɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ? JObility me maame, Ata, Mehm! "

Na Akrɔma no baa nkyɛn; ɔkaa sɛ, "Abofra, wo dwom yɛ dɛ, ɔto nnwom na me bɛnya wo maame ama wo. " Na abofra no maa so (her nne):

"I kaeɛ sɛ, na mobɛtie, meka saa, na morenkyerɛ.

Me kae sɛ, Maame, momma yɛn nkɔ round, ɛfiri sɛ ɛwɔ atifi fam a mmoawa nketewa no te'!

Na ɔkae sɛ ɔrekɔ fam, efisɛ ɛwɔ asuwa fam ne baabi a mmoawa nketewa no te.

Na ɛdɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ? JObility me maame, Ata, Mehm! "

Na Akrɔma no de ne (magic) lail-switch kɔbɔɔ nsuo no anim, nanso nsuo no gyinaa baabi a ɛgyina. Na ɔno (Akrɔma) no kɔɔ so. Na Kɔkɔsakyi no, Ɔpɛt no de ne ho bae, na ɔno nso kae sɛ, "Sing saa dwom no ma mete sɛ, mɛtumi anya wo maame ama wo. " Na abofra no na ɔtoo dwom no:

"I kaeɛ sɛ, na mobɛtie, meka saa, na morenkyerɛ.

Me kae sɛ, Maame, momma yɛn nkɔ round, ɛfiri sɛ ɛwɔ atifi fam a mmoawa nketewa no te'!

Na ɔkae sɛ ɔrekɔ fam, efisɛ ɛwɔ asuwa fam ne baabi a mmoawa nketewa no te.

Na ɛdɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ? JObility me maame, Ata, Mehm! "

Na ɔno nso faa ne (magic) sinnua na ɔbɔɔ asubɔnten no anim, nanso hwee ankɔ so. Na ɔno nso kɔɔ so. Na (nnom) Ane, Kwaakwaadaabi no, nso bɛkaa ho. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Abofra, adɛn nti na woteɛm saa? Cury ne mo nte sɛ mɛnya wo maame ama wo. Na abofra no na ɔtoo dwom no:

"I kaeɛ sɛ, na mobɛtie, meka saa, na morenkyerɛ.

Me kae sɛ, Maame, momma yɛn nkɔ round, ɛfiri sɛ ɛwɔ atifi fam a mmoawa nketewa no te'!

Na ɔkae sɛ ɔrekɔ fam, efisɛ ɛwɔ asuwa fam ne baabi a mmoawa nketewa no te.

Na ɛdɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ meyɛ? JObility me maame, Ata, Mehm! "

Kwaakwadabi faa aduro cone too nsuo mu, na ɔde ne dua suman bɔɔ nsuo no anim, na nsuo no mu paapaee mmienu; ɔkaa sɛ, "Sing the dwom"; na abofra no too dwom. Na ohuu ne maame ti ti nhwi; na ɔboroo nani kakra. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sing rehaw," na ɔtoo dwom. Na ne maame no baa ahene hɔ. Kwaakwadabi no kae sɛ, "Go on dwomtoɔ. " Na abofra no te, na ne maame da ne nanpɔn ntam koraa. Ɔkaa sɛ, "Sing refood," na ɔtoo dwom. Ne maame da mpan prɛko pɛ na ɔgyinaa sikakorabea hɔ. Ata, Nta no, kae sɛ, "Kwaakwaadaabi, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mefa so meda wo ase? " Obuae sɛ, "Me deɛ, mempɛ hwee koraa, kɔtotɔ sum-blue ne fitaa ntama. " Ata tuu mmirika de bi baa Kwaakwaadabi, na Kwawaakwaaabi no de sum-blue no kata ne ho, ne fitaa a ɔde bɔɔ ne kɔn no. Ɛno nti na wobɛhunu Kwaakwadabi no, n'akyi ayɛ tuntum, nanso ne kɔn no yɛ fitaa. Ɛyɛ akyɛdeɛ a wɔnya firii Ata, Nta hɔ.

Eyi, me abakɔsɛm a me ne no wɔ abusuabɔ no, sɛ ɛyɛ dɛ a, (or) sɛ ɛnyɛ dɛ a, ebinom betumi agye nokware, na ahomegyeɛ a wotumi yi me ayɛ (a asɛmka wɔ ho).
