10 Kanne 2 Timoti 3:16, 17. Yɛlɛ naŋ be a Baabole poɔŋ na eɛ duoro naŋ yi Naaŋmen zie a ko nensaaleba. A Baabole iree zemmo soorebie mine naŋ e nimizeɛ nɔɛ. A Baabole lɛ wuli teŋ Jehoova poboɔbo, ane yɛlɛ oŋ boɔrɔ ka te erɛ. Te taaɛ sagediibu ka Jehoova la vɛŋ baŋ sɛge yɛlɛ zaa naŋ be a Baabole poɔŋ. Azuŋ, a Baabole la gane naŋ e tegroŋ yaga zaa ko te te zemmo poɔŋ. Te maŋ kannɛɛ a Baabole a woŋ a muni, a lɛ de yɛlɛ na teŋ kanne na meŋ tonɔ neŋ toma te zemmo poɔŋ.

10. Bo teeroŋ teŋ taa kyaare a Baabole? (2 Timoti 3:16, 17)
<<<P>>>
11 Bonso teŋ sagedi ka Jehoova la de o vooroŋ soŋ na soŋ dɔba mine baŋ sɛge a Baabole? Tɔɔ, yɛ vɛŋ ka te nyɛ yɛlɛ awala mine naŋ vɛŋ teŋ sage lɛ di. A dendɛŋ soba, ka a Baaboleŋ wa yele dakoreŋ saŋa yɛlɛ, a maŋ toriŋ. (Luk. 3:1, 2) Ka a Baaboleŋ wa yele lɛ a donɛɛ naŋ waa, a maŋ toriŋ. (Joob 26:7) Aneazaa ka Setaana mɔɛ o moɔbo zaa ka o sãã a Baabole, kyɛ a Baabole la gane ba naŋ de tɔre ko noba a gaŋ gane zaa naŋ be a teŋɛzuŋ. Dɔba naŋ ta 40 la sɛge a Baabole. A yi saŋa na baŋ piili sɛgrɛ a Baabole, a te ta saŋa na baŋ sɛgoo baare, a de ŋa yuomo 1,600. Kyɛ, yɛlɛ na baŋ sɛge na tue taa velaa zaa. Kawane naŋ be a Baabole poɔŋ na naŋ sonnɛɛ noba turituri zenɛ ŋa ba bebiri zaa yeltaree poɔŋ. (Yeng. 13:20; 15:21; Mat. 7:12) Daŋyelbo yaga naŋ be a Baabole poɔŋ nyɛɛ eebo. Lɛ eɛ sieree naŋ wuli ka te na baŋ dɛlɛɛ a Baabole. (Joos. 23:14) Yelmeŋɛ zaa, sieree zaa wuleeŋ ka Naaŋmen la de o vooroŋ soŋ na soŋ noba baŋ sɛge a Baabole.
