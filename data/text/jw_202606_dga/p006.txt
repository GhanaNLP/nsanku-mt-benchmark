(Mat. 24:14) A noɔre iribo pɛlɛɛŋ. Naaŋmen
sonnɛɛ o puorebiiri naŋ la Jehoova Sieree
Deme baŋ moɔlɔ a duorisoŋ korɔ noba. Te
moɔlɛɛ a Naaŋmen Naaloŋ duorisoŋ na a yi
tenne naŋ pare 240 poɔŋ. Naaŋmen Naaloŋ
na naŋ piili dire naaŋ a parɛɛ yuomo 100 pam-
pana, a na yuomo na zaa poɔŋ, te moɔlɛɛ a
duorisoŋ korɔ noba. Eɛ, te niŋe moɔ̃ yɛŋ teŋ
moɔlɔ a duorisoŋ korɔ noba, a wulo ba a Baa-
bole poɔ yɛlɛ meŋ. A toma ŋa eɛ toŋ tegroŋ
yaga zaa.—Mat. 28:19, 20.

Naaŋmen yuori Jehoova dɔglɛɛ te zuŋ. Azuŋ,
a eɛ apoɔsori naŋ e tegroŋ yaga ko te ka ba
boɔlo te Jehoova Sieree Deme. Noba yaga
naŋ be a teŋɛzuŋ ba baŋ ka Naaŋmen yuori
la Jehoova. Kyɛ, te mɔɔ yɛŋ a leɛ de Naa-
ŋmen yuori na eŋ ziiri na zaa aŋ seŋ ka o be
a Baabole poɔŋ na. Ka hooŋ kaa The New
World Translation of the Holy Scriptures poɔŋ,
ho na nyɛŋ ka Naaŋmen yuori na bebeŋ a pare
7,000.
