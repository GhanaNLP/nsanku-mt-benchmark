11 Ka hooŋ wa yeŋ tere yelyaga, teere nyɛ yɛlɛ naŋ na baŋ soŋ ho kyɛllekyɛlleba na ane lɛ baŋ na toɔ̃ de a tonɔ neŋ toma. Maŋ de a Baabole sɛgre a eŋ ho kyɛllekyɛlleba na faŋa, kyɛ soŋ ka ba nyɛ lɛ baŋ na toɔ̃ e a de a Baabole sɛgre na tonɔ neŋ toma. Maŋ vɛŋ ka ho yelyaga na tɔ kaŋa zaa sukyiri, a gaŋ hoŋ na yele a yɛlɛ aŋa neŋyaga la hoŋ yele korɔ. Aŋa mannoo poɔŋ, bitontona Pɔɔl da wulee Timoti yɛlɛ aŋ seŋ ka o e ka o neŋ Jehoova niiloŋ e kpeɛŋaa. O da wulee yɛlɛ oŋ na toɔ̃ erɛ a sonnɔ o yɔɔmine naŋ be Efesu. (Kanne 1 Timoti 4:13-16.) Maŋ teere yɛlɛ naŋ na baŋ soŋ a kyɛllekyɛlleba, ka ho pãã soŋ ba ka ba nyɛ lɛ baŋ na toɔ̃ e a de a tonɔ toma ba zemmo poɔŋ.
<<<P>>>
12 Ka hooŋ wa sɛgrɛ ho yelyaga na, maŋ yele a yɛlɛ gbɛɛ yaga ka a yire. Ka hooŋ pãã wa gaa te terɛ a yelyaga, ana e ŋa ho neŋ a kyɛllekyɛlleba la dire dama. Ka ho yelyaga naŋ maŋ e dama diibu, ana soŋɛɛ a kyɛllekyɛlleba ka ba woŋ a yɛlɛ soŋ a gaŋ hoŋ na sɛge a yelbie zaa biŋ, a wa kanna korɔ ho kyɛllekyɛlleba na. Apply Yourself to Reading and Teaching gambile na yeleeŋ ka: “Maŋ yele yɛlɛ dama diibu soriŋ, de ho teeroŋ eŋ a yelyaga ane ho kyɛllekyɛlleba na eŋɛŋ.” Ka ho yelbie naŋ maŋ yire ho sukyiri poɔŋ, ho kyɛllekyɛlleba na na kyɛllɛ wonɛɛ a yɛlɛ soŋ a boɔrɔ ka ba de yɛlɛ na hoŋ yele na tonɔ neŋ toma ba zemmo poɔŋ.

13 Maŋ bɔ sommo yi te yɔɔmine na naŋ de yuomo yaga tere yelyaga na zieŋ. Aŋa mannoo poɔŋ, sɛre hoŋ na gaa tere a yelyaga na, gaa te yɔɔ na kaŋa zie a te wuluu lɛ hoŋ sɛgre ho yelyaga na. Ka hooŋ de sowuli zaa oŋ na ko bo na toŋ neŋ toma, a na soŋeŋ ka ho tere a yelyaga soŋ zaa. Ane meŋ, ho na baŋ terɛɛ a yelyaga ka o woŋ a nyɛ ziiri na oŋ na baŋ soŋ bo ka ho nyɛ nimitɔɔre gaabo. (Yeŋg. 1:5) Saŋa kaŋa, te menne koŋ toɔ̃ baŋ ziiri na mine aŋ seŋ ka te nyɛ nimitɔɔre gaabo. Saŋa kaŋa meŋ, te koŋ toɔ̃ baŋ ka te yelee a yɛlɛ soŋ bee te ba yelaa soŋ. Azuŋ, ho yɔɔdɔɔ na na baŋ soŋ boŋ ka ho nyɛ lɛ hoŋ na baŋ e tere a yelyaga ka a kyɛllekyɛlleba woŋ a muni soŋ. Te zaa boɔrɔŋ ka te e wulwulbo naŋ kyile, ka lɛ vɛŋ ka noba maŋ toɔ̃ woŋ a Baabole poɔ yelmenne na muni soŋ a de a tonɔ neŋ toma.*

* Ho na baŋ nyɛɛ yɛlɛ mine a kyaare lɛ hoŋ na baŋ e nyɛ nimitɔɔre gaabo a wa e wulwulo naŋ kyile bee a baŋ yelyaga terebo. Kanne Apply Yourself to Reading and Teaching gambile na ane Benefit From Theocratic Ministry School Education gane na.

13. Ka hooŋ wa yeŋ tere yelyaga, ananso la wala aŋ na soma ka ho bɔ sommo yi te yɔɔdɔɔ kaŋa naŋ kyile yelyaga terebo poɔŋ zieŋ? (Kaa a foto.)
