7 Ka te naŋ wa kyɛllɛ yɛlɛ soŋ zaa te lammozien, lɛ wuleeŋ ka te boɔrɔŋ ka te taa popeeloŋ pampa-na ane nyɔvore naŋ ba taa baaraa nendaare kaŋa meŋ. Takoreŋ saŋa na, Jehoova da yelkoɛ o puo-rebiiri ka ka ba naŋ kyɛllɛ wono o yɛlɛ a de a tonɔ neŋ toma, ba na taaɛ popeeloŋ a zeŋ tegitegilɛ. Zenɛ ŋa, ka te naŋ e lɛ te meŋ na nyɛ la ana maa-loo na. (Dut. 32:44-47) Ka te naŋ maŋ kyɛllɛ yɛlɛ soŋ zaa te lammozien, te maŋ nyɛ la faŋa emmo, a la baŋ lɛ teŋ na baŋ e a toɔ̃ are te yelkpeɛne noɔ-reŋ kyɛ dire kannyiri a kyɛnlɛ a donpaalaa. (Uro. 8:25) Pampana aŋ e nimizeɛ yaga ka te kyɛllɛ wono Jehoova wulibo, kyɛ tonɔ yaga o puoruu toma poɔŋ. Bonso, a koŋ kɔɔre a donɛɛ ŋa waa-nɛɛ o baaroo.—Mat. 24:42-44; Yel. 1:3.

8 Te yɔɔdɔba na naŋ taa apoɔsori ka ba terɛ yelyaga a lammore zieŋ na seŋ ka ba maŋ tere a yelyaga ka noba nyɛ tɔnɔ yi a poɔŋ. Ka te yɔɔdɔ-ba naŋ maŋ sɛgre ba menne soŋ zaa, a vɛŋ ka ba yelyaga na are a Baabole eŋɛŋ, a na baŋ soŋeŋ ka noba naŋ ba puoro Jehoova zanne yelnimizeere mine yi a yelyaga poɔŋ. Lɛ eebo meŋ na soŋ yɛ te yɔɔmine ka ba woŋ a Baabole poɔ yelmenne muni soŋ a de a tonɔ neŋ toma ba zemmo poɔŋ. Azuŋ, ka hooŋ wa taa toma zaa a lammozien, de a nimizeɛ. Bonso, noba yaga na baŋ nyɛɛ tɔnɔ yi ho yelyaga na poɔŋ. Wagre zaa hoŋ wa taa yelya-ga, bee ka hooŋ wa taa sakuubiiri toma, teɛre a yɛlɛ ŋa gaa tɔɔre. A na soŋeŋ ka ho e wulwulo naŋ kyile. Aneazaa ka a sowuli ŋa na baŋ soŋɛɛ te yɔɔdɔba naŋ terɛ yelyaga na, kyɛ a na baŋ so-ŋɛɛ neɛzaa naŋ taa Baabole poɔ yelyaga kyɛbee a taa sakuubiiri toma ka o toŋ a lammo saŋa.—2 Tim. 4:2; Tit. 1:9.

9. Wala hoŋ na baŋ e ka ho kyɛllekyɛlleba maŋ woŋ ho yelyaga na muni soŋ zaa?
<<<P>>>
yelnimizeɛ na meŋɛ hoŋ boɔrɔ ka ho wuli. Ka hooŋ wa terɛ yelyaga, ta vɛŋ ka a e kpeɛŋaa ko a kyɛl-lekyɛlleba ka ba woŋ a yɛlɛ muni. Azuŋ, maŋ sɛg-raa a yelyaga ka o tutaa soŋ zaa. Kyɛ, ka hooŋ ba sɛgre ho yelyaga na soŋ, a na eɛ kpeɛŋaa ko ho kyɛllekyɛlleba na ka ba baŋ yɛlɛ na hoŋ boɔrɔ ka ho wuli. Lɛ na vɛŋeŋ ka a e kpeɛŋaa ka ba kyɛllɛ wono a yelyaga. A yɛlɛ ata ŋa na baŋ soŋ boŋ ka ho maŋ sɛgre ho yelyaga soŋ zaa. A dɛndeŋ soba, ka hooŋ wa piili a yelyaga, vɛŋ ka ho kyɛllekyɛlle-ba na baŋ ho yelyaga na yelnimizeere. Ayi soba, a yelyaga poɔŋ, yeli a yelnimizeere zaa ka a pɛle soŋ zaa a kyɛ tutaa aŋa lɛ hoŋ yelaa a piilee saŋa na. Ka hooŋ wa manne a yelnimizeɛ kaŋa zaa baa-re, maŋ mãã ho meŋɛ a wuli yelnimizeɛ na hoŋ lɛ waana ka ho manne o yɛlɛ. Ata soba, ka hooŋ wa yeŋ baare a yelyaga, lɛ maaŋ yeli a yelyaga yelni-mizeere ka a kyaaŋ soŋ zaa. Ho na baŋ eɛ a yɛlɛ ŋa meŋ poɔ, ka hooŋ wa manne a yelnimizeɛ kaŋa zaa yɛlɛ baare, vɛŋ ka a kyɛllekyɛlleba baŋ ka ho yeŋ lɛ yelee a yelnimizeɛ kaŋa yɛlɛ.

10 De eŋnyagroŋ manna neŋ ho yelyaga na yelni-mizeere. Ka hooŋ nɔŋ yelyaga na hoŋ waana ka ho tere na, ho na boɔrɔŋ ka ho yeli a yɛlɛ neŋ eŋnyagroŋ. A kyɛllekyɛlleba meŋ na nyɛɛ lɛ ho eŋɛŋ. (Yeng. 2:4, 5; Tom. 4:20) Bo yɛlɛŋ na baŋ soŋ bo ka ho maŋ nɔŋ yɛlɛ na hoŋ waana ka ho yeli na? Tɔɔ, maŋ teɛre a yelyaga yɛlɛ ane a Baa-bole poɔ sɛgre yɛlɛ gaa tɔɔre. Te yɔɔdɔɔ kaŋa be la United States paaloŋ, o nyɛɛ yuomo naŋ pare 70. Ŋaaŋ oŋ yeli a kyaare yelyaga kaŋa oŋ daaŋ woŋ o baapaaloŋ saŋa: “N maŋ teɛrɛɛ lɛ na a yel-yaga soba naŋ da eŋ te faŋa a kyaare lɛ teŋ na baŋ maŋ e a teɛre yɛlɛ gaa tɔɔre saŋa na teŋ wa kanna a Baabole. O da de la eŋnyagroŋ a wuli lɛ aŋ seŋ ka te maŋ e, N da nyɛɛŋ ka a yɛlɛ sereŋ eɛ nimi-zeɛ koo. A na yelyaga na leerɛɛ N zemmo yɛlɛ kpɛlɛŋkpɛlɛŋ. Azuŋ, ka maaŋ wa kanna a Baabo-le N maŋ zannɛɛ yɛlɛ yaga yi o poɔŋ. A lɛ soŋeeŋ ka N neŋ Jehoova niiloŋ meŋ e kpeɛŋaa.”
