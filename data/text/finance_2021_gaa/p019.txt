OTI: “Maŋ shika gbɛjianɔtoo kɛha nɔyaa nitsumɔi emuuyeli, naagbee kɛ amɛmli hewalɛwoo”
<<<P>>>
• Aha okwaafoi 1,736,510 na yelikɛbuamɔ kɛtsɔ fɛtilaizai kɛ dumɔ wui ni aja aha amɛ ni Amralofoi lɛ ewo ehe nyɔmɔ lɛ mli eko lɛ nɔ, ni enɛ ye ebua ni akpa niyenii babaoo yɛ nibaanii komɛi tamɔ abele kɛ ekrokomɛi lɛ amli, ni mɛi na nitsumɔ hu yɛ nɛkɛ nibii nɛɛ amli; ahe ni aja lɛɛtoi kpakpai 7,500 aha okwaafoi 750 yɛ Yiteŋ Anai, Yiteŋ, Oti kpokpai anɔ. Ahe ni aja wuɔi 30,000 aha okwaafoi 3000 yɛ Yiteŋ Anai, Boka, Ashante, Yiteŋ, kɛ Ga Kpokpai anɔ:
<<<P>>>
• Ahe ni aja kooloi bibii, kplotoo kɛ wuɔi 531,100 aha okwaafoi ni aje kooloi atsui 11 amaamɔ shishi kɛ nubui amɛha kooloi alɛɛhei kpawo;
<<<P>>>
• Atsa nidumɔ nibii anɔ nushwamɔhei ni aje shishi momo ni eshɛ ŋɛlɛ srɔtoi anɔ ni abaagbe naa lɛ nɔ: Tamne mlijaa I&II—atsu oha mlijaa 90 kɛ oha mlijaa 50; Mprumem mlijaa II— atsu oha mlijaa 95, ekrokomɛi asaamɔ, amɛhiɛtsakemɔ kɛ ekomɛi amligbelemɔ yɛ Tono— atsu oha mlijaa 95, Kpong nibaanii anɔ nushwamɔhe lɛ — atsu oha mlijaa 61 kɛ Kpong Faa lɛ naa abɛku gbɛ nii anɔ nushwamɔhe lɛ — atsu oha mlijaa 72;
<<<P>>>
• Agbe nu kpaakpoi 10 ni aamamɔ lɛ naa yɛ Yiteŋ Boka, Yiteŋ Anai, Yiteŋ Kpokpa kɛ Savana Kpokpa lɛ nɔ koni ana nu yɛ jɛmɛ ni akɛ eko adu nii afi muu lɛ fɛɛ;
<<<P>>>
Ŋmɔ kɛ emli nii anɔ nushwamɔ tsɔji
<<<P>>>
• Atsɔ Kpokpa Kome Nitsumɔ kome Gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ nɔ kɛgbe nitoohei 21 naa;
<<<P>>>
• Aha kookoo tɔŋ kome jara GH¢7,600 lɛ damɔ he kome eha kookoo okwaafoi yɛ 2017/2018 afi lɛ nɔ, ni akɛ oha mlijaa 8.42 ŋmɛ nɔ ni eha tɔn kome jara tee ¢8,240 yɛ 2018/2019 kookoo kpamɔbe lɛ mli koni kookoo okwaafoi lɛ akaye jaramɔ yɛ jara ni eba shi yɛ jeŋjeŋ jara lɛ nɔ lɛ he; ni eha nitsumɔi 110,000 kɛtsɔ nɛkɛ kookoo he gbɛjianɔtoi nɛɛ kɛnitsumɔ nɔ, koni shihilɛ atsake ni no amɛ akrowaiaŋbii ashimɔ kɛ maji wujiaŋ yaa lɛ naa kɛye abua shihilɛ kpakpa yɛ Ghana fɛɛ.
