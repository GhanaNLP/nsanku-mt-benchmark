Shika ni afite
<<<P>>>
5. Be ni shɛɔ Afuabe lɛ naagbee yɛ 2020 afi lɛ mli lɛ, Amralofoi lɛ efite shika ni naa shɛɔ GH¢100.1 biliɔŋi (nyɔmɔi memeji hu ni awo fata he), kɛji akɛto gbɛkpamɔ ni ji GH¢97.7 biliɔŋi lɛ he. Shika hekpai kɛ nyɔmɔbɔɔ krokomɛi ka he ni akɛŋmɛ lɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ. Nyɔmɔbɔɔ krokomɛi fɛɛ tee ŋwɛi fe gbɛkpamɔ taakɛ wɔnaa yɛ 2020 gbɛkpamɔ ni yɔɔ tɛŋmɔ ni ji 2 lɛ mli lɛ. tamɔ bɔ ni wɔnaa yɛ nitɛŋmɔ ni ji enyɔ 2 lɛ mli lɛ. Shika wuji ni wɔnaa yɛ nyɔmɔi lɛ ekomɛi amli lɛ saŋŋ yadamɔ nifeemɔi nɛɛ kɛ ekrokomɛi anɔ:
<<<P>>>
• Jɛ nitsumɔ mli hesalɔi babaoo ni ahe yɛ hewalɛnamɔ nitsumɔ lɛ mli lɛ, tako ni aha hewalɛnamɔ nitsulɔi kɛ agbɛnɛ hu afii anɔ ni aŋmɛ aha asraafoi dani amɛyaa hejɔɔmɔ lɛ anyɔmɔwoo.
<<<P>>>
• COVID-19 he nyɔmɔbɔɔ ni ba yɛ nibii kɛ sɔɔmɔi ahe; kɛ
<<<P>>>
• COVID-19 shihilɛhei ahe nyɔmɔbɔɔ ni afite kɛjɛ japadei amaamɔ mli lɛ.
<<<P>>>
6. Ehe miihia ni ale akɛ Amralofoi lɛ yafa shika ni shɛɔ GH¢44.9 biliɔŋ kɛfata shika ni ana kɛ kee shikai ni naa shɛɔ GH¢55.1 biliɔŋi lɛ he bɔ ni afee ni akɛwo nitsumɔi ni ato akɛ abaafee lɛ ahe nyɔmɔ yɛ 2020 afi lɛ mli.
