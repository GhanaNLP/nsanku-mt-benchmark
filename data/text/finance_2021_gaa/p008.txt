Shikataomɔ Gbɛjianɔtoo
<<<P>>>
4. Yɛ 2020 bɔjeti lɛ mli lɛ, Amralofoi lɛ to gbɛjianɔi komɛi ni amɛbaatsɔ nɔ kɛtao shika kɛ keenii ni shɛɔ GH¢53.7 biliɔŋi koni akɛgbele nifeemɔi, nitsumɔi kɛ mlai komɛi amli. Be ni shɛɔ Afuabe 2020 lɛ naagbee lɛ shika ni ana lɛ naakulɛ ji GH¢55.1 biliɔŋi, enɛ ho gbɛkpamɔ nɔ lɛ he kɛ GH¢1.5 biliɔŋi. Enɛ fɛɛ jɛ gbɛjianɔtoo kpakpai ni akɛtsu maŋsɛɛ jarayeli tooyeli he nii, no ji sɛɛnamɔ ni yɔɔ Integrated Customs Management System (ICUMS) ni agbele lɛ he, amɛto gbɛjianɔi ni mli wala kɛbua shikai anaa jogbaŋŋ. Nitɛŋmɔ 1 lɛ miitsɔɔ Shikataomɔ gbɛjianɔtoo yɛ gbɛi srɔtoi anɔ yɛ afi 2020 lɛ mli.
