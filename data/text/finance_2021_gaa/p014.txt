18. Kɛfata gbɛnaa nitsumɔi lɛ ahe lɛ, Amralofoi lɛ, baana piamɔ kɛjɛ jeŋjeŋ shikatoohe lɛ, kɛha US$100 miliɔŋ ni ji maŋ lɛ trukaa COVID-19 he gbɛjianɔtoo nitsumɔi ahewalɛwoo kɛ nɔyaa. (Emergency Preparedness and Response Plan (EPRP)) ni no aha anyɛ abote hela lɛ jɛɛhei amli jogbaŋŋ, kɛpɛi nii amli, koni anyɛ hela lɛ saji fɛɛ akula kɛŋmɛ sɛkɛ kɛha (3Ts) ni ji hela lɛ mlipɛimɔ (Testing), sɛɛjaa (Tracing) kɛ tsabaa (Treatment.) fɛɛ.
