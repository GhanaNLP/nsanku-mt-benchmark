7. Maŋ nyɔmɔbɔɔ yɛ afi 2020 lɛ mli
<<<P>>>
• He ni maŋ nyɔmɔbɔɔ yashɛ yɛ Afuabe 2020 lɛ naa yakpa GH¢291.6 biliɔŋi (US$50.8 biliɔŋi), ni edamɔ shi ha oha mlijaa 76.1 (76.1%) GDP. Enɛ fata shika ni Amralofoi lɛ yatao ni akɛwo kpɔmɔ shika saji ahe nyɔmɔi kɛ aŋkroaŋkro srawa hewalɛ hemɔ nyɔmɔi lɛ. Kɛji ajie shika he gbɛjianɔtoo nitsumɔhei akpɔmɔ nyɔmɔbɔɔ yɛ mli lɛ, 2020 guɔ nyɔmɔ lɛ fee oha mlijaa 71.5 (71.5%). Guɔ he nyɔmɔbɔɔ lɛ mlijaramɔi nɛ:
<<<P>>>
• Maŋsɛɛ nyɔmɔi: GH¢141.7 biliɔŋi ni ji (US$24.7 biliɔŋi) ni damɔ shi kɛha oha mlijaa 48.6 (48.6%) ni ji guɔ nyɔmɔbɔɔ shika tso muu lɛ; kɛ
<<<P>>>
• Jaku nyɔmɔ: GH¢149.8 biliɔŋi (US$26.1 biliɔŋi) no damɔ shi kɛha oha mlijaa 51.4 (51.4%) ni ji guɔ nyɔmɔbɔɔ shika tso muu lɛ.

8. Amralofoi lɛ ashika gbɛjianɔtoo he mlai kɛ nitsumɔi (toohemɔ kɛ nitsumɔ gbɛjianɔtoo) tsɔɔ bɔni shika he saji baaya lɛ aha yɛ bei kukuoo mli. 2021 Bɔjɛti lɛ damɔ gbɛkpamɔi otii ni baa nɛɛ anɔ:
<<<P>>>
• Shika fɛɛ ni abaana yɛ afi lɛ mli (GDP) lɛ aya ŋwɛi oha mlijaa 5.0 (5.0%);
<<<P>>>
• Shika ni abaana ni muŋmɔŋ shika bɛ mli (GDP) lɛ aya ŋwɛi oha mlijaa 6.7 (6.7%);
<<<P>>>
• Afi lɛ naagbee mli shika jarawalɛ ashɛ oha mlijaa 8.0 (8.0%);
<<<P>>>
• Bɔjɛti lɛ sɛɛ nyɔmɔbɔɔ ashɛ oha mlijaa 9.5 (9.5%) yɛ GDP lɛ mli;
<<<P>>>
• Shika ni baashwɛ yɛ GDP lɛ nɔ ashɛ oha mlijaa 1.3 (1.3%);
<<<P>>>
• Maŋsɛɛ shika ni akɛbaato lɛ anyɛ adamɔ kɛhooo kwraa lɛ nyɔji 4.0 shɛɛ nibii kɛ nitsumɔi anyɔmɔwoo naa.
