<<<P>>>
HIƐGBELEMƆ
<<<P>>>
1. 2021 Shika Gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ nɔdoomɔ ni miitsɔɔ sɛɛnamɔ ni Amralofoi lɛ na yɛ 2020 afi lɛ mli kɛ bɔ ni ato ni abaahehei yɛ shikataomɔ gbɛi komɛi anɔ kɛ bɔ ni akɛbaatsu gbɛjianɔtoi lɛ ahe nii yɛ 2021 afi lɛ mli. Oti ni ka 2021 shika gbɛjianɔtoo nɛɛ nɔ ji “Maŋ shika gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ emuuyeli, naagbee kɛ amɛmli hewalɛwoo”. Nɛkɛ oti nɛɛ maa nɔ mi kɛyeɔ he odase shiishi akɛ yɛ afii komɛi amli nɛɛ lɛ shika gbɛjianɔtoi hei srɔtoi fɛɛ hiɛɔ nɔyaa nitsumɔi hei ni bayeɔ nitsumɔi ni miiya momo lɛ anajiaŋ Gbo kɛ Gbiɛnaa fɛɛ. Jeee nɔnyɛɛ pɛ enɛfeemɔ kɛbaa shika saji amli, moŋ eha etsɔ nɔnyɛɛ tridii, ni no haaa anyɛ awo kɔntratafoi ni etsu nii amɛgbe naa lɛ anyɔmɔ mra. Enɛ kɛ shika he kpa too hu ba waa.
<<<P>>>
2. Yɛ 2021 mli lɛ, Amralofoi lɛ eŋmɔ amɛyiŋ kpɔ koni amɛtsɔ gbɛ hee kroko kwraa nɔ. Oti lɛ ji koni no aye abua ni amɛnyɛ amɛha agbe nitsumɔi srɔtoi ni agbeko naa lɛ fɛɛ anaa.

3. Shika he gbɛjianɔtoi ni yɔɔ nifeemɔi lɛ ahe yɛ 2020 mli lɛ nɛ:
<<<P>>>
• Klɛŋklɛŋ nyɔji nɛɛhu yɛ afi lɛ mli lɛ shika ni ana (GDP) yikulɛ naa yakpa oha mlijaa 0.2 (0.2%). Bɔ ni akpaa gbɛ akɛ abaana ji eya ŋwɛi kɛshɛ oha mlijaa 0.9 (0.9%) yɛ afi lɛ naagbee mli.
<<<P>>>
• Muŋmɔŋ Shika ni ana yɛ afi lɛ mli hu (GDP) naa yakpa oha mlijaa 0.4 (0.4%) yɛ klɛŋklɛŋ nyɔji nɛɛhu yɛ afi lɛ mli lɛ mli, shi aakpa gbɛ akɛ ebaaya ŋwɛi kɛshɛ oha mlijaa 1.6 (1.6%) yɛ afi lɛ naagbee mli.
<<<P>>>
• Afi lɛ naagbee ni ji Afuabe lɛ shika jarawalɛ yashɛ oha mlijaa 10.4 ( 10.4%) yɛ beiaŋ ni gbɛkpamɔ ji ebaayashɛ oha mlijaa 11.0 ( 11.0%)
<<<P>>>
• Bɔjeti tso muu lɛ fɛɛ sɛɛ nyɔmɔ yashɛ oha mlijaa 11.7 (11.7%) (GDP) kɛji akɛto gbɛkpamɔ nɔ ni ji oha mlijaa 11.4 (11.4%) (GDP) lɛ he.
