<<<P>>>
SHƐEWIEMƆ NI JƐ MAŊSƆƆLƆ NI KWƐƆ SHIKA GBƐJIANƆTOO LƐ DƐŊ
<<<P>>>
Ghanabii yafɔ oshiki yɛ Afuabe 2020 lɛ nɔ kɛha wɔmaŋhienyielɔ H.E. Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo ekoŋŋ koni etsa enitsumɔi kpakpai lɛ anɔ. Shifimɔ ni Ghanabii na yɛ nɛkɛ Amralofoi nɛɛ amli lɛ jeee shwɛmɔ nɔ ko.
<<<P>>>
Wɔ 2021 shika he gbɛjianɔtoo nɛɛ shikamɔ lɛ baasha wɔmaŋ shika saji lɛ amli kɛbaa wala yi, ni ewo nitsumɔi ni miigbee shi lɛ anɔ, kɛjie hewalɛwoo ko kpo ni no awa kɛtswa Ghana maŋ kpakpa lɛ kɛma shi shiŋŋ yɛ COVID-19 lɛ sɛɛ. Maŋ shika gbɛjianɔtoo nɛɛ baafee shifimɔ nɔ kɛha yiŋkpɔ fɛɛ ni wɔto ni baaye abua kɛtsake maŋ nɛɛ hiɛ kɛtswa maŋ lɛ, kɛbaa maŋ lɛ nitsumɔi fɛɛ ayi.
<<<P>>>
Wɔjaje shika saji fɛɛ kɛbadamɔ enɛ lɛ nɔ ekoŋŋ yɛ klɛŋklɛŋ kuku nɛɛ mli, no ji kɛtsɔ gbɛjianɔ kpakpai ni ato lɛ nɔ lɛ ajaje otii wuji ni aakpa gbɛ, akpa shikataomɔ he nifeemɔi komei ahe mama, atsɔɔ gbɛi srɔtoi anɔ ni amɔɔ nibii komei anaa, aŋkroaŋkro nitsumɔi ayelikɛbuamɔ, kɛ gbɛjianɔtoo kɛha naagbai anaa mɔmɔ nifeemɔi hu. Be ni 2019 lɛ ba naagbee lɛ, wɔbɔ mɔdɛŋ ni wɔtsake maŋ shika saji lɛ ahiɛ kɛtsɔ gbɛi otii wuji komei atsɔɔmɔ nɔ ni jeŋ muu lɛ fɛɛ ye enɛ he odase, kɛkɛ ni COVID-19 jie ehiɛ kpo. Bɔ ni hela nɛɛ bafite shika saji lɛ, jeŋjeŋ fɛɛ nu he jogbaŋŋ ni jeee Ghana pɛ.
<<<P>>>
COVID-19 hela gbonyo lɛ kpojee lɛ kɛ haomɔi ni naawala bafite wɔnitsumɔi kɛ daa shihilɛ saji fɛɛ. Amralofoi lɛ yɔse haomɔ kple ni enɛ kɛba maŋbii anɔ lɛ jogbaŋŋ ni eto gbɛjianɔ ni baaye abua kɛtee nitsumɔi fɛɛ ashi ekoŋŋ. Hiɛnɔkamɔ yɛ lolo ejaakɛ wɔna gbɛi srɔtoi anɔ ni atsɔɔ kɛmɔɔ nibii anaa. Amralofoi lɛ kɛ amɛhe esha afɔle akɛ amɛbaakwɛ koni wɔmaŋbii ana hewalɛ, no hewɔ lɛ eto gbɛjianɔ koni agbu Ghanabii kɛbɛje amɛ ni mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ana hewalɛ ni wɔtswa maŋ lɛ kɛma shi.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ nɛkɛ beiaŋ ni wa nɛɛ mli po wɔkɛ bo etsa mli kɛha nɔyaa nifeemɔi ni wɔto gbɛjianɔi wuji ni baaye abua kɛtswa shika saji lɛ wɔma shi kpɛŋŋ ekoŋŋ. Ghanabii aaafee 1.2 milioŋ baanyɛ ana sɛkɔndre sukuu tsɔsemɔ mli gbɛ kwraa, wɔto gbɛjianɔ kɛsaa shikatoohei anifeemɔi fɛɛ ni mɛi ni kɛ shikatoohei nɛɛ yeɔ jara lɛ aniji ashɛ amɛshikai ni amɛkɛyatotoi fɛɛ anɔ. Kɛfata he lɛ, afee tsɔji ahe gbɛjianɔtoo srɔto ko ni no eha wɔna klɛŋklɛŋ tsɔji ni atotoi yɛ jaji kɛ gbɛjegbei anɔ nɛɛ.
<<<P>>>
Wɔmaa nɔ mi nyɔŋma shiishi akɛ lɛɛlɛŋ wɔtsɔ naagbai komɛi amli yɛ beiaŋ ni wɔkpaa nɔyaa gbɛ lɛ. Amralofoi lɛ etswa fai shi akɛ amɛkɛ gbɛjianɔtoi komɛi baashwie jaraŋ ni no aye abua kɛmɔ nibii anaa kɛwo nitsumɔi anɔ adamɔ shi. Eko ji wɔkɛ shika ni shɛɔ GH¢750 milioŋi wo nitsumɔ nifeemɔi komɛi amli yɛ Agbiɛnaa 2020 lɛ mli ni enɛ eye ebua nitsumɔi wuji kɛ bibii ni ji Micro, Small scale, Medium-Sized Enterprises (MSMES) kɛ ekrokomɛi hu.
<<<P>>>
Wɔmiifɔmɔ niji kɛmiitsɛ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ koni ebafata wɔhe ni wɔtsa nɔ wɔkɛ susumɔi ni sa awo jaku lɛ ama shi koni mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ana he sɛɛ. No ji wɔ fɛɛ wɔwo shi akɛ wɔkɛ wɔtsui kɛ wɔnyɛmɔ fɛɛ baaye wɔbua kɛsɔmɔ Ghana.
