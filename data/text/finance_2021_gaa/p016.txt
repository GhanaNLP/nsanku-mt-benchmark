OTI: “Maŋ shika gbɛjianɔtoo kɛha nɔyaa nitsumɔi emuuyeli, naagbee kɛ amɛmli hewalɛwoo”
<<<P>>>
Tooyeli nɔshimɔ kadimɔ (stampu): Abaakpa too ni ajara mli fifio lɛ he mɔ yɛ afi lɛ mlijaa enyɔ, etɛ kɛ ejwɛ lɛ nɔ yɛ afi 2021 lɛ mli yɛ jarayelɔi bibii adɛŋ ni abaashi enɛ nɔ kɛ tooyeli nɔshimɔ kadimɔ (stamp) da;
<<<P>>>
Tsɔji ahe tooyeli: Abaakpa bɔ ni ajaraa tsɔji ahe too mli ejwɛ ni awoɔ yɛ afi mlijaramɔi ejwɛ naa yɛ tsɔji ahe yɛ afi lɛ mlijaa enyɔ, etɛ kɛ ejwɛ lɛ nɔ yɛ afi 2021 lɛ mli yɛ trɔtrɔi kɛ taxi tsɔji adɛŋ da koni no aye abua kɛtserɛ tsɔji amlitamɔ nyɔmɔwoi anɔ; kɛ
<<<P>>>
Kɔŋnɔtwaa yɛ toi ni etsɛtsɛi awoo mra he: Shikagbelei kɛ kpai ni abua naa, ni efafai awoo kɛyashi Afuabe nyɔɔŋ lɛ nɔ yɛ afi 2020 lɛ mli ahe hu abaatsɛ ewoo he mlai lɛ anɔ aha kɔmpenei kɛ aŋkroaŋkro nitsumɔi ni kɛ GRA baayata shi kɛtsɔɔ akɛ amɛbaawo amɛnyɔmɔi lɛ be ni 2021 Gbo nyɔɔŋ lɛ baashɛ lɛ. Mɛi ni baana enɛfeemɔ he sɛɛ lɛ esani amɛyaŋmala amɛgbɛi yɛ Ghana Revenue Authority bii lɛ aŋɔɔ, ni esa akɛ amɛnyɛ amɛye amɛklɛŋklɛŋ shiwoo lɛ nɔ.
