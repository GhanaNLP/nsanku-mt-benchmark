<<<P>>>
• ahe ni aja lɔlei (pickup) 840 kɛ bɔɔsui 350 ni aha sukuu nɔkwɛlɔi motoi 2000 kɛye abua amɛnyiɛmɔ kɛya nitsumɔ;
<<<P>>>

39. Yɛ 2021 mli lɛ, Amralofoi lɛ baa:
<<<P>>>
• tsɔse KG-P6 tsɔɔlɔi 11,997 yɛ sukuu hiɛtsakemɔ kudɔmɔ gbɛjianɔtoo (Standard-Based Curriculum) lɛ mli kɛ 95,000 hu yɛ Common Core Programme (CCP) lɛ mli.
<<<P>>>
• abaafee klɛŋklɛŋ kaa ( National Standard Assessment Test (NSAT)) yɛ klasi 2 kɛ 4;
<<<P>>>
• abaatsa nɔ ni amamɔ tsui koni awo mli nibii ni yeɔ buaa nikasemɔ, hei ni baawa ninenaa nitsumɔi kasemɔ yɛ Awooo Agbuteŋ SHS Gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ mli; ni
<<<P>>>
• Abaatsa nɔ amamɔ nikasemɔhei 20 ni baaye abua jeŋshikpamɔ nikasemɔ yɛ Ghana nɛɛ fɛɛ, ni abaagbele daa, taakɛ Amralofoi agbɛjianɔtoo lɛ kɛɔ yɛ Jeŋshikpamɔ, he lɛ Tsɔji amli hesalɛ, Nifeemɔi, kɛ Akɔntaa lɛ.
