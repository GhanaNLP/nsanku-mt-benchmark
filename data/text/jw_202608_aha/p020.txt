BƲTAYƖ
Dɩyɛzumanlɛ eyi kɔwʋwa yɛ ma yɛ anyɩ kɔzɔ ɔnwʋmɔ ɔhʋanɩ mɔɔ yɛwɔ kɛɛ yeke-nyia nnwʋanlɩ, yekɔzʋ̃mɩ Gyehova, na yɛahã odwokɔba yɩ be yɛahile mmenii mvʋlɔ nɩ.

Ma Ɔnwʋmɔ Ɔhʋanɩ Mɔɔ Ɔwɔ Kɛɛ Ɔsʋ̃mɩ Nyamɩnlɩ Nɩ Idú Bulɔ Lma Wʋ

‘Ima Devʋlɔ anwʋzɩzɩbɛvʋlɔ be yɛ mɔɔ ɩkɛma yekɔzʋ̃mɩ yɩ mɔɔ ahunlududulɛ bela íni numɔ.’—LK. 1:69, 74.
<<<P>>>
NƖ ZAKALIYIA nɩ yɩ yɩ Elizabɛtɩ bɛ alʋbɔ hɔ anyunumi, na nɩ béni ɛyalɛ. Naasʋ̃ Gyehova yelɩ nnwʋanlɩ dɩyɛ be, ɩmanlɩ bɔwʋlɩ ɛyalɛ benyia na bɔvʋlɔlɩ yɩ Dwɔɔnʋ. Odwokɔ eyi yelɩ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛdɩlɩ nɩ asibɔnwʋ paa. Na beyizialɩ kɛɛ: “Na mɩnla enii yɛ kuakula eyi ɩbayɛ a?” (Lk. 1:57-66) Gyehova luwalɩ Zakaliyia zʋ manlɩ odwokɔ eyi nwʋ munwalɩyɛ. Ihilehileli numɔ kɛɛ, Mɛzaya yɩ mɔɔ nɩ ɩlékye ɩbaya nɩ, Dwɔɔnʋ kɔwʋwa mmenii yi ma bɛkɛzɩnza bɛ adwɩnɩ na bɛayɛ Mɛzaya yɩ adʋwavʋlɔ. (Em. 3:7; Lk. 1:67-79) Naasʋ̃, kɛ mɔɔ nɩ Dwɔɔnʋ ɩbayɛ nkʋ̃ la yɛ Zakaliyia hanlɩ nwʋmɔ odwokɔ a.* Kɛmɔ yɛkɔnwʋ yɩ wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ eyi nu nɩ, nɩ Zakaliyia yi odwokɔ yɩ dɩ nkɔmɩhyɛ na nnɛ sʋ̃, yɛzaye sʋ̃ yenyia zʋlɔ mfasʋ. Akʋ̃ sʋ̃, yɛnwʋ ozumalɛdɩyɛ titili asã be wɔ Zakaliyia yi odwokɔ yi nu. Mɛyɩ yɛ lɛ ɔnwʋmɔ ɔhʋanɩ mɔɔ yɛwɔ kɛɛ: (1) yekenyia nnwʋanlɩ, (2) yekɔzʋ̃mɩ Gyehova, nɩɩ (3) asɛdɩ mɔɔ yɛwɔ kɛɛ yɛkɛhã odwokɔba yɩ.
