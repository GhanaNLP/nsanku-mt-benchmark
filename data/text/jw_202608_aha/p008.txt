Yɛkɔhɔ Zu Yekegyina Pindinyii

BƲTAYL
Eyile bela ise kɛɛ yɛkakɛ kɛɛ, Setanɩ nɩ yɩ awɔvʋlɔ tanɩ yɩ yɛ ɩsɛ kɛɛ yekũ yetiyia bɛ a, na kɛ ye mmeliyema a.
<<<P>>>
Sa Ɔkʋla Di Setanɩ Nɩ Yɩ Azunzuma Evinli Yi Zʋ Nkunimi!
<<<P>>>
‘Kɛ mmenii yɛ bɛ nɩ yɛ kũ a. Mfomi yekũũ yetiyia azunzuma atinuzɩbɛmaa mɔɔ bɔwɔ oyumi nɩ.’—ƐFI. 6:12.
<<<P>>>
GYEHOVA yɩ azɔ̃mɩvʋlɔ bɛáhɔ kʋnɛ. (Ayɩ. 2:4; Dwɔ. 18:36) Naasũ Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ ma yɛnwʋ kɛɛ, ɩsɛ kɛɛ yekũ yetiyia Setanɩ nɩ yɩ awɔvʋlɔ tanɩ yɩ. (Kɩnga Ɛfɩsɛsɩmaa 6:11, 12.) Naasũ awɔvʋlɔ tanɩ eyimmɔ bɔwɔ tumi bɛtela yɛ, meti kɛ ɩkɛyɛ na yɛahʋla yɛali bɔ zʋ nkunimi a? Mɔɔ ɩkɔwʋwa yɛ paa yɛ lɛ kɛɛ yɛkɛva yɛ nwʋ yekediã Gyehova zʋ. Akũ sʋ̃, ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛnwʋ ahvanɩ azʋlɔtʋ mɔɔ Setanɩ nɩ yɩ awɔvʋlɔ tanɩ yɩ duwa zʋlɔ sɩkɛ yɛ nɩ ye mmeliyema yɛ dedi nɩ.
<<<P>>>
2 Wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ mɔɔ ili eyi yɩ anyunu yi nu nɩ, yɛnwʋnlɩ kezimɔ Abɔnsamɩ bɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ɩkɛzɩkɛ anwʋlazʋ mɔɔ yɛlɛ wɔ Gyehova Nyamɩnlɩ nɩ Gyisɛsɩ Kɩlayɩsɩ nu nɩ. Na wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ eyi nu nɩ, yɛkɔnwʋ ahvanɩ mvʋlɔ anwĩ mɔɔ Setanɩ duwa zʋlɔ kũ tiyia yɛ. Mɔɔ ili bɔmʋnlɩ, ɩpɛ kɛɛ ɩsɩkɛ ɔkʋ̃yɛlɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ yɛ nɩ ye mmeliyema avinli nɩ. Mɔɔ ɩtɔ zʋlɔ anwĩ, ipinyiabinyia mmenii ma botwodwu betiyia yɛ kɛmɔ ɩkɛyɛ na yɛayɩ yɛ adwɩnɩ wɔ dasɩlɛlilɛ egyima yɩ zʋ anzɛnyʋ bɔlɔ ɩahyɛ yɛ zʋ ma yeayiaki dasɩlɛlilɛ egyima yɩ.

SETANƖ BƆ MMƆDƖNƖ KƐƐ ƖKƐZƖKƐ ƆKƲ̃YƐLƐ MƆƆ ƖWƆ YƐ NƖƖ YE MMELIYEMA AVINLI NƖ
<<<P>>>
3 Setanɩ bɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ɩkɛma nkyɩkyɛmi kɛya yɛ nɩɩ ye mmeliyema avinli. Setanɩ zi kɛɛ yɛbɔɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ yɛkɔhũ yekediyia yɩ, meti yɩ bʋtayɩ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ ɩkɛma yɛkɔhũ yekediyia yɛ nwʋ yɛ nwʋ mfomi. Kɛ ɩyɛ eyi ise a? Mmenii mɔɔ bɔwɔ Setanɩ yi oyuwadɩ yi nu nɩ, bo nu sɔ̃maa béni ɔlɔlɛ kʋlaa na mbɔmɩ sɔ̃maa nɩ bɛále bɔ nwʋ bedi. Setanɩ pɛ kɛɛ yesumazuma bɛ. Meti ɩpɛ kɛɛ yɛtɩndɛ yetiyia ye mmeliyema nɩ
<<<P>>>
1. Aninyiammɔ yɛ ɩsɛ kɛɛ ye munwala yekũ yetiyia bɛ a? (Ɛfɩsɛsɩmaa 6:11, 12)
2. Mɩnla yɛ yekozunzu nwʋmɔ wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ eyi nu a?
3. Mɩnla yɛ Setanɩ pɛ kɛɛ yɛyɛ a?
