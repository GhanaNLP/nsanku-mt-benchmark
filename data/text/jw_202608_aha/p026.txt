STÉPHANE LANDEROIN YI MUNWA NU NDWOKƆ
<<<P>>>
MMƖLƖ mɔ nɩ mʋkɔ sukuulu nɩ, nɩ mɩábɛ sukuuluhɔlɛ kʋlaa, na nɩ mízi dɩyɛ sɔ̃. Iluwakɛɛ nɩ mɩábɛ kɛɛ mmenii sũmaa adwɩnɩ ba mʋ zʋ nɩ tɩ, nɩ mɩta mɩtɩ mʋ nwʋ. Nɩ mɩánwʋ mʋ nwʋ kɛɛ abiye mɔɔ Nyamɩnlɩ kɛma mʋkɔnwʋ yɩ. Mɩnla ti mɔɔ mɩkãã zɔ a?
<<<P>>>
Minyinli wɔ France twonwa be mɔɔ bɔfʋlɔ yɩ Paris nu. Nɩ mɩ awʋvɔlɔ nɩ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɔdua mʋ nwʋ beyiyia nɩ munwatĩĩ bɛále bedi kɛɛ Nyamɩnlɩ wɔ kɛnɩ. Meti, ɔzɔ la yɛ nɩ mɩzamɩ sɔ̃ mide midi a. Nɩ mide midi kɛɛ nninyeni yi munwatĩĩ yalɩ la mɔɔ kɛ abiye yɛ ɩwɔlɩ a. Nɩ mide midi paa kɛɛ adwɩnɩ mɔɔ mɩlɛ yɩ nɩ dɩ baaba iluwakɛɛ muzumanlɩ wɔ sukuulu. Nɩ mɩtɩ nyanlɩ kɛɛ ntɩyasɩ wɔ mɔɔ mide midi nɩ nu, meti nɩ mɩáha mʋ nwʋ kɛɛ mikeɣiziaɣizia nwʋmɔ ndwokɔ bela.
<<<P>>>
Nɩ mɩ awʋvɔlɔ bɛádɩ sianti mɔɔ mmenii wɔ Nyamɩnlɩ nu dedi nɩ tʋ kʋlaa. Nɩ bɛkã kɛɛ sɛ Nyamɩnlɩ wɔ ɛkɛnɩ a, ankɩ nninyeni tanɩ ilésizi, na ankɩ mmenii sɔ̃ lɔ́nwʋ amanɩyɛ zɔ. Nɩ míni nyamɩnlɩzɔ̃mɩnlɛ nwʋ adwɩnɩba iluwa nninyeni tanɩ mɔɔ nɩ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɔsɔ̃mɩ Nyamɩnlɩ nɩ bɛyɛ nɩ tɩ. Meti mɩzamɩ sɔ̃ mɩɣa minyianlɩ adwɩnɩ kɛɛ, nɣɛlɛbɛ bela íni numɔ kɛɛ mikele keli kɛɛ Nyamɩnlɩ be wɔ kɛnɩ mɔɔ ɩdwɩnɩ enii nwʋ. Na nɩ midi bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bede Nyamɩnlɩ bedi nɩ nwʋ nwɔwɩ po.
<<<P>>>
Kakɩ yɩ mɛyɩ, mʋwɔ anwʋlazʋ kezile paa kɛɛ Dɩyɛwɔlɛnvɔlɔ be wɔ kɛ. Akũ sɔ̃, mʋnwʋ yɩ kɛɛ ɩávalɩ nwʋmɔ mɩ sinidɔlɛ munwatĩĩ mɛda nɩ, ɩdwɩnɩ mʋ nwʋ paa. Ebiye a ekeɣizia kɛɛ, mɩnla yɛ ɩmanlɩ mɩzɩnzanlɩ mɩ adwɩnɩ a, na mɩnla yɛ ɩma mɩ anyɩ de kakɩ yɩ a? Ma mɩhã nwʋmɔ odwokɔ mihile wʋ ɛ.
<<<P>>>
Minyianlɩ alʋbɔ 19 nɩ, nɩ ɩsɛ kɛɛ bɛtɩdɩ mɩ kɛɛ sogyianii, meti aɣanɩ yɩ manlɩ mʋhɔ milili ɔlʋbɔ ɔkʋ̃ wɔ French Guiana mɔɔ ɩwɔ South America ma bɛdɩdʋlɩ mɩ kɛɛ sogyianii. Ɔzɔ mmɩlɩ yɩ, nɩ mízi kɛɛ ɔzɔ dɩka mɔɔ ɩyɛ pɩkɛ nɩ kenyia mɩ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ zʋ tumi wɔ egile be.
<<<P>>>
Minyianlɩ alʋbɔ 20 nɩ, mizia mɩɣalɩ Paris. Ɛkɛnɩ mɛyɩ, nɩ mɩyɛ egyima ɔzʋmɩ Kizile kɔbʋmɩ Yalɛ, na Fʋlɔ nɩɩ Bɔɔlɔ mɛyɩ, anyɩnlʋlelɛ nkʋ̃. Nɩ dɩyɛ ɔkʋ̃ yɩ la yɛ mɩyɛ yɩ dapɛnɩ bela a, naasʋ̃ nɩ mɩ anyɩ ɩále. Na nɩ míni bʋtayɩ bela wɔ mɩ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu. Sɛ nɩ mʋnwʋ kɛɛ besizii mbiyemmɔ, bɛyɩyɩɩ mmenii nu anzɛnyɩɩ mmenii bedii ehyinyia a, nɩ mɩ anyɩ ɩáleli nwʋmɔ kʋlaa. Na sɛ mʋnwʋ abiye mɔɔ ɩlɛyɛlɩ tanɩ bela kɛɛ ɩnwʋʋ ama-
