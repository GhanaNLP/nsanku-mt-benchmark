Nyia wu mmeliyema nwv adwınɩba na kã bɔ nwv odwokɔba kɛmɔ Pɔɔlv yelı nı
(Kila nkyɩkyɛmi 8)
<<<P>>>
Onɛsɩmɛsɩ lili yı bʊwɔ paa. Ɔzɔ la yɛ amɩyɛ sʊ̃, ndwokɔ mɔɔ yɛkɛhã yɛkɛvalɩ yɛ ɔhɔnwʊmmɔ dedimaa nwv nɩ yɛ ikehile kezimɔ mmenii kowu bɛ anzɛnyɩɩ bɛ nɩɩ bɛ keli a. Sɛ yɛkã yɛ mmeliyema nwv odwokɔba a, ɩkɛma mmenii mvʊlɔ bɛ nɩɩ bɛ keli yi bʊwɔ. Ɔzɔ la yɛ sɛ yɛákã bɔ nwv odwokɔba a, ɩlɛ́ma mmenii kowu bɛ a. Naasʊ̃, sɛ yɛkɔhʊla yɛkɛhã abiye nwv odwokɔba a, gyɩsɛ yenyia yı nwv adwınɩba. Mɩnla yɛ ɩkɔhʊla ɩkɔwʊwa yɛ ma yɛkɛyɛ zɔ a? Bɛmá yehila dɩyɛ be mɔɔ nɩ Pɔɔlʊ ta yɛ.

8 Nɩ Pɔɔlʊ ta da Gyehova asɩ wɔ subanɩba mɔɔ nɩ Fɩlɩmɔnʊ wɔ nɩ nwv. (Flm. 4, 5) Kɛ Fɩlɩmɔnʊ yɩzayɩ nkʊ̃ yɛ nɩ Pɔɔlʊ da Gyehova asɩ wɔ yı subanɩba nwv a, mfomi mmenii mvʊlɔ sʊ̃ kɩnla nwvmɔ. (1 Kɔl. 1:4-7; Fɩl. 1:3-5; Kɔl. 1:3, 4; 1 Tɛs. 1:2, 3) Nɩ Pɔɔlʊ yı anyɩ dʊwa yı mmeliyema bɔ mfʊmɩ azvlɔtv nɩ, naasʊ̃ ɩwɔlɩ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ɩkɛva yı adwɩnɩ ikeziye bo subanɩba zv mfomi. Nɩ ɩta ɩda Gyehova asɩ kɛɛ ɩma inyia ɔhɔnwʊmmɔ mɔɔ bɛdɩ zɔ. Na wʊzawv sʊ̃ ɛ, asʊ̃ ɩyɛ sɩ ɩma wv kɛɛ ɛkɛhã abiyemmɔ nwv odwokɔba a? Mɛyɩ nɩ kakɛ kɛɛ, sɛ ɔbɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ekenyia mmenii mvʊlɔ nwv adwɩnɩba na ɔbɔ Gyehova mpayɩ kɛɛ ɩmá nninyeni thɔ bʊwɔ ɩma bɛ a, ɩkɛma ɩkɛyɛ mɛlɛkɛ kɛɛ ɛkɛhã bɔ nwv odwokɔba.

9 Iávalɩ nwvmɔ ndwokɔzɩzɩbɛ azvlɔtv mɔɔ nɩ Pɔɔlv iyiyiaa nɩ, ɩ nu Fɩlɩmɔnv lili yi bʊwɔ. Asʊ̃ iluwa ɛvɛlɩyɛ anzɛnyɩɩ nninyeni mɔɔ ɩhyɛ wv zv nɩ ti, ɛdɩndɛ basabasa hile abiye kyĩ a? Sɛ yeyiyiaa buwabɩlɛ be mɔɔ numɔ yɛ sɩ a, wʊákila a, sa ɩma yɛfa yɛ ɛya yɛkɔ yekua abiye zʊ. Naasʊ̃, ma yezunzú bʊwabɩlɛ mɔɔ nɩ Pɔɔlʊ wɔ numɔ wɔ mmɩlɩ mɔ ɩhɛlɛlɩ kɛlɛta thɔ ɩmanlɩ Fɩlɩmɔnʊ nɩ nwv yehila. Alvbɔ egyikĩĩ be ansaana ɩkɛlɛɛ yı kɛlɛta yı kɛma Fɩlɩmɔnʊ nɩ, ɔlɔmɩ be dʊlɩ hyɛlɩ Pɔɔlʊ zʊ, na bɛhytlɩ. Bɛvalɩ yı bedianlɩ fiyiadɩ wɔ Gyelusalamɩ nɩɩ Sizeliyia. Ayinzi yi, bɛ nɩɩ yı hɔlɩ Wulomi, na mmɩlɩ mɔ bɔkɔɔ nɩ, ɛlɛnɩ mɔɔ nɩ ɩda numɔ nɩ wutuli yi wɔ ngyemvile nu. Meti mmɩlɩ mɔ nɩ ɩkɛlɛɛ kɛlɛta kɔhɔ kɛma Fɩlɩmɔnʊ nɩ, nɩ ‘bediã yı fiyiadɩ’ wɔ yɩzayɩ yı awudo wɔ Wulomi. (Flm. 1) Asʊ̃ nɩ Pɔɔlʊ kɛma bʊwabɩlɛ mɔɔ nɩ numɔ yɛ sɩ mɔɔ nɩ iyiyiaa nɩ kɛhyɛ yı zʊ nkʊ̃ ma ɩkɛhɩnhã yı adwɩnɩ ikehile Fɩlɩmɔnʊ a? Anhã, ɩáyɛ zɔ kʊlaa. Mfomi ndwokɔ mɔɔ nɩ ɩwɔ kɛlɛta mɔɔ ɩhɔ ɩva ɩmanlɩ Fɩlɩmɔnʊ yi nu nɩ, nɩ ɩhyɛ ɛzɩzɩbɛ paa, na nɩ debela kile kɛɛ ɩwɔ annidi ɩma Fɩlɩmɔnʊ. Mɩnla ozumalɛdɩyɛ yɛ ɩwɔ eyi nu a? Sɛ ɛvɛlɩyɛ anzɛnyɩɩ nninyeni hyɛ yɛ zʊ, anzɛnyɩɩ yeyiyiaa ɔhawʊ be, na yɛbɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ yɛ nɩɩ mmenii mvʊlɔ keli yi bʊwɔ a, ɩkɛma azʊ̃nudwolɩyɛ kɔwʊwa asafo yı nu.
