Sɛ yɛdɔ ye mmeliyema na yɛma ɔkũyɛlɛ kɔ zolɔ bɔwa yɛ avinli a, sa ɩma yedi Setanɩ nɩɩ yɩ awɔvʋlɔ tanɩ yɩ zʋ nkunimi (Kila nkyɩkyɛmi 16)
<<<P>>>
mɔɔ ɩma nninyeni nu yɛ sɩ ma yɛ anzɛnyɩ̃ bɛfa salɛ betiã yɛ ɔzɔ̃mɩnlɛ egyima yɩ zʋ po a, ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛkɔ zʋlɔ yɛda annidi ali yekile bɛ. Mɩnla ti a? Iluwakɛɛ ɔzɔ yɛ Gyehova pɛ kɛɛ yɛyɛ a. (Wul. 13:1, 2) Kɛ mmɛla bela mɔɔ aɣanɩ yɩ kɛhyɛ mɔɔ itiyia yɛ nɩ yɛ Setanɩ da yɩ mɛda a. Naasʋ̃ Setanɩ ikilaa ɔhʋanɩ kɛɛ yɛkɛva bɔ nwʋ ɛya na yɛadɩndɛ yɛadiyia bɛ anzɛnyɩ̃ yɛadwodwu yɛadiyia bɛ. Nnɛ, sɛ aɣanɩ be hyɛ mmɛla anzɛnyɩ̃ isi pɔkɔ be na mmenii nu yɩ ɩáyɛ adwɩnɩ a, botwodwu betiyia yɩ. Naasʋ̃, ɩávata kɛɛ Kilisinyianii be kɛyɛ debe mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ! (Ɛyɛ. 24:21, 22, ad.; Odw. 8:2, 3) Sɛ yekyina ɔfvã be wɔ manlɩwulɛ ndwokɔ nu na yɛáfa yɛ nwʋ yetiã Nyamɩnlɩ yi Bulombunlililɛ yɩ zʋ a, sa yɛtɔ Setanɩ yɩ ɛhanɩ yi nu.—Dwɔ. 15:19.

14 Bɛzabɛ mɔɔ nɩ botwodwu betiyia Gyehova Dasɩvʋlɔmaa wɔ mmɩlɩ be mɔɔ ɩya ɩzĩɩhɔ nɩ, sikalɛyɩ bo nu be ya yɛ Gyehova yɩ azɔ̃mɩvʋlɔ mɔɔ bɔbɔ ɛhyɩmɛ paa. (1 Tim. 1:12, 13) Sɛ iluwa Gyehova yɩ zɔ̃mɩnlɛ ti mmenii wʋdʋ wʋ, bɛyɛ wʋ atɩtɩɩ, anzɛnyɩ̃ bɛkã awohwi ndwokɔ betiã wʋ zʋ a, kakɛ kɛɛ sa bɔkʋla bɛsɩnza na bɛaɣa bɛayɛ Gyehova yɩ azɔ̃mɩvʋlɔ. (Mt. 5:11) Bɔ mpayɩ na aɣanɩ mbanimbani yi bɛádwodwuli bɛádiyialɩ yɛ, na ama yɛahɔ zʋlɔ yɛava azũnudwolɩyɛ yɛazɔ̃mɩ Gyehova. (1 Tim. 2:1, 2) Akʋ̃ sʋ̃, bɔ mpayɩ ma wu mmusunwamaa, wu egyimanumaa, anzɛnyɩ̃ wu sukuulumaa mɔɔ botwodwu betiyia wʋ nɩ. Siã Gyehova kɛɛ ɩwuwá wʋ ma ɔnwʋ mɔɔ ɛyɛ a ɩkɔhʋla kɛzɩnza adwɩnɩ mɔɔ bɛlɛ wɔ Gyehova Dasɩvʋlɔmaa nwʋ nɩ. Akʋ̃ sʋ̃, abʋtalɩ nwʋ kehyinyia paa! Baalɛ be mɔɔ ɩvalɩ alʋbɔ sũmaa idwodwuli idiyialɩ yi hũ na ayinzi yi ɩya ɩyɛlɩ Dasɩvʋlɔ nɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ, “Sɛ yɛkɔ zʋlɔ yenyia bɛzabɛ mɔɔ botwodwu betiyia yɛ nɩ nwʋ abʋtalɩ sũmaa a, ɩkɛma bekenyia yɛ nwʋ adwɩnɩba.”
