17 Mmʋlɩ mɔ Pɔɔlʋ hanlɩ kɛɛ ‘yehɔ zʋlɔ yenyia yɛ nwʋ abʋtalɩ’ nɩ, mɩnla yɛ nɩ ɩpɛɛ ikehile a? Nɩ ɩhyɛɛ yɛ ɛzɩzɩbɛ kɛɛ, sɛ yɛ anlima be fʋmɩ yɛ anzɛnyɩɩ ɩyɛ debe ma yɛfa ɛya a, yenyıá yɩ nwʋ abʋtalɩ na yɛla kunlumiba ali yehile yi. Wɔ ɔzɔ nkyɩkyɛmɩ yi nu la, Pɔɔlʋ zia hanlɩ ndianwʋ kɩnlɩma be mɔɔ Gyehova ɩyɛ nɩ nwʋ odwokɔ. Gyehova tɩ yɛ tu, meti iáhila ɔhvanɩ kɛɛ yɛkɛyɛ yɛ dɩyɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ. Izi kɛɛ yeáli munli, naasʋ̃ ye subanɩba azʋlɔtu nɩ yɛ ɩfa yɩ adwɩnɩ isiye zʋlɔ a. (Ndw. 103:13, 14; 130:3) Eyi ti, ɩkɔ zʋlɔ ɩda kunlumiba nɩɩ nwɔkɛnwʋnlɛ ali ikile yɛ. 17. Mmʋlɩ mɔ Pɔɔlʋ hanlɩ kɛɛ, ‘yehɔ zʋlɔ yenyia yɛ nwʋ abʋtalɩ’ nɩ, mɩnla yɛ nɩ ɩpɛɛ ikehile a?
<<<P>>>
abʋtalɩ mmʋlɩ mɔ bɛyɛ debe bediyia yɛ nɩ. Iluwakɛɛ amɩyɛ sʋ̃, sɛ yɛyɛ debe yetiyia yɛ anlima na inyia yɛ nwʋ abʋtalɩ a, ɩma yɛ anyɩ de paa. Mɔwʋ dɩyɛ ma yehilá ndianwʋ be. Sɛ yeduwa owudo be mɔɔ iso fɩlawɛsɩ na yɛkakɛ kɛɛ sɛ inyi a yɩ fɩlawɛsɩ yɩ kɛyɛ pɩkɛ paa a, ɩma yɛ anyɩ kɔ nwʋmɔ paa. Yekugua zʋlɔ nzulo na yetudu ndibe bela mɔɔ ɩwɔ nwʋmɔ nɩ. Ɔzɔ la yɛ sɛ yɛkakɛ subanɩ ngʋnlɩma mɔɔ ye mmeliyema lɛ yɩ nɩ a, ɩkɔwʋwa yɛ ma yɛkɛva bɛ tanɩ yɛkɛhyɛ bɛ mmʋlɩ mɔ bɛyɛ debe ma ɩyɛ ɛyɛkɛ yɛ nɩ. Na mɛyɩ kɛma yɛ nɩɩ bɛ keli yi bʋwɔ s গুরুতর.

18 Gyehova yɩ anyɩ sɔ mmɔdɩnɩ bela mɔɔ ɔbɔɔ kɛɛ ɛkɛma azɔ̃nudwolɩyɛ kɔwʋwa asafo yɩ nu nɩ. Meti eyile bela, pɩbɛ ɔnwʋmɔ ɔhvanɩ mɔɔ ɩkɛma ɛkɛyɛ zɔ. Kɔ zʋlɔ ɛ nɩɩ mmenii mvʋlɔ dɩndɛ wɔ ɔhvanɩba zʋ na kã bɔ nwʋ odwokɔba. Suma kyɛbɛbalɛ. Sɛ ɔfʋmɩ abiye a, ɛ nɩɩ yɩ ziyeziyé bɛ avinli. Nyia ɔlɔlɛ kezile ma wu mmeliyema, na ama wuahʋla wʋava bɛ tanɩ wuahyɛ bɛ. Sɛ ɛyɛ nlinyeni eyimmɔ munwala a, ɩkɔwʋwa ma azɔ̃nudwolɩyɛ kɔwʋwa asafo yɩ nu. Na ɛkɛhɩnla kãnwʋazɩmaa mɔɔ ‘bɔkɔwʋwa azɩlɛ yɩ zʋ na bɛ anyɩ iale baaba wɔ azɔ̃nudwolɩyɛ s গুরুতরmaa nu’ nɩ nwʋ daabaa!—Ndw. 37:11.
