BOTAYI
Áma ɛma ɔhvanɩ ma Se-tanɩ ma enyia adwɩnɩ kɛɛ ɔlɔ́kʋla kenyia Gyehova Nyamɩnlɩ nɩɩ Gyisɛsɩ nu anwʋlazʋ.
<<<P>>>
Pʋ Setanɩ Yɩ Nnahɩlɩbɛ Na Nyia Gyehova Nɩɩ Gyisɛsɩ Nu Anwʋlazʋ
<<<P>>>
“Begyiná pindinyii wɔ Abɔnsamɩ yɩ anyunu na ɩzʋ̃ bɔ nwv ɩkeyiandi.” —GYE. 4:7.
<<<P>>>
SƐ YESI pɔkɔ kɛɛ yɛkɔzʋ̃mɩ Gyehova a, Setanɩ nɩɩ yɩ azunzuma evinli yi kɔ zvlɔ bɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ bɛkɛma ɔlɔlɛ mɔɔ yɛwɔ yɛma Gyehova nɩ kɛhã azɩ. Naasʋ̃, Setanɩ zi kɛɛ ɩlɔ́kʋla ɩkɛma ɔlɔlɛ mɔɔ yɛwɔ yɛma Gyehova nɩ kɛhã azɩ mmɩlɩ mɔ yeyike Gyehova na ɩbʋwaa yɛ nɩ. Meti Setanɩ bɔ mmɔdɩnɩ bela kɛɛ ɩkɛzɩkɛ ɔlɔlɛ mɔɔ yɛwɔ yɛma Gyehova nɩ na ama Gyehova iayiaki kɛɛ ɩkɔ-wʋwa yɛ. Ɔhvanɩ ɔkʋ̃ mɔɔ Setanɩ duwa zʋlɔ yɛ zɔ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ ɩkɛma yɛkenyia adwɩnɩ kɛɛ Gyehova nɩɩ yɩ Ɣa yɩ bɛádwɩnɩ yɛ nwv.—2 Kɔl. 11:14; Gye. 1:6.
<<<P>>>
2 Sɛ azogyiamaa wɔ kʋnɛ nu a, ihyinyia kɛɛ benyia bɛ mbani-mbani yi bo nu anwʋlazʋ. Na sɛ bɛányia bɛ mbanimbani yi bo nu anwʋlazʋ a, ɩkɛma ɩkɛyɛ sɩ kɛɛ bekediye bɛ, na eyi kɔhʋla kɛyɛ asianɩ kezile paa kɛma bɛ. Eyi kɔhʋla kɛma bɔ nnwvannlɩ kevi bɛ sa. Ɔzɔ la yɛ amɩyɛ sʋ̃, yɛ bʋwabʋlɛ yɩ dɩ kɛɛ azogyiamaa mɔɔ bɔwɔ kʋnɛ nu nɩ, yɛ nɩɩ “azunzuma atinuzɩzɩbɛmaa” yɛ yɛkɔ̃ʋ̃ a. (Ɛfɩ. 6:12) Setanɩ Abɔnsamɩ zi kɛɛ sɛ anwʋlazʋ mɔɔ yɛwɔ wɔ Gyehova nɩɩ Gyisɛsɩ nu nɩ kã azɩ a, ɩkɛyɛ sɩ kɛɛ yeke-diye bɛ, na yeléde bɔ mmʋwalɩyɛ po yekediã nu. Meti ɩyɛɛ mɔɔ ɩkɔhʋla bela kɛɛ ɩkɛma anwʋlazʋ mɔɔ yɛwɔ wɔ Gyehova nɩɩ Gyisɛsɩ nu nɩ kɛhã azɩ. Wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ eyi nu nɩ, yekozunzu kezimɔ Setanɩ luwalɩ nnahɩlɩbɛ ɔhvanɩ zʋ lahɩlɩlɩ abiyemmɔ wɔ ɔwɔkɩ mmɩlɩ yɩ nu, nɩɩ kezimɔ ɩyɛɛ ɔzɔ wɔ nnɛ. Akʋ̃ sʋ̃, yekehi-la kezimɔ yɛkɔhʋla yɛkɔbʋ yɩ nnahɩlɩbɛ yɩ wɔ nnɛ. Na wɔ dɩyɛ-zumanlɛ mɔɔ ili ɛkɛnɩ nu sʋ̃ nɩ, yɛahila ahvanɩ anwĩ mɔɔ Setanɩ bɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ikoluwa zʋlɔ ɩkɛzɩkɛ yɛ nɩɩ Gyehova avinli.*
<<<P>>>
* Odwohilalɛ mɔɔ yeyiyia nɩ, kɛ yi munwala yɛ ɩzʋ̃ Setanɩ a. Naasʋ̃ yɩzayɩ yɛ ipinyiabi-nyia mmenii ma botwodwu betiyia Nyamɩnlɩ yi mmenii a. Meti sɛ yeyiyiaa ndwokɔzɩzɩbɛ be, anzenyɩ iluwa Gyehova yɩ ɔzɔmɩnlɛ ti yeyiyiaa odwodwudiyialɛ a, yezi kɛɛ Setanɩ la yɛ ɩbɔɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ɩkɛzɩkɛ yɛ nɩɩ Gyehova avinli a.—1 Kɔl. 2:12; 1 Dwɔ. 5:19.
