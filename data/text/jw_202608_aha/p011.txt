Asũ ekeli nɔhʊanlɛ kɛma Gyehova nɩɩ yi Bulombunlililɛ yɩ nɩ a?
<<<P>>>
(Kila nkyɩkyɛmi 13)
<<<P>>>
nɩ bɛhyɛ yɩ nwʊ nkɔmɩ nɩ yalɩ azɩlɛ yɩ zʋ nɩ, mmenii sũmaa bɛ nɩɩ yɩ iádi yi buwɔ. Kɛ ndianwʊ nɩ, mmɩlɩ mɔ ɩwɔlɩ yɩ ɔzʋ̃mɩnlɛ egyima yɩ tʋ nɩ, yi mmusunwamaa yɩ be hanlɩ kɛɛ yi ti ɩzɩkɛ. (Mk. 3:21) Nɩ nyamɩnlɩzʋ̃mɩnlɛ nu mbanimbani yi ta kã kile yi kɛɛ íni nwʋmɔ ɔhʋanɩ kɛɛ ikilehile mmenii. (Lk. 11:15, 16) Gyisɛsɩ yɩ asũmavʋlɔ yɩ bo nu ɔkʋ̃ yɩlɩ yɩ manlɩ. (Mt. 26:14-16) Nyamɩnlɩzʋ̃mɩnlɛ nu mbanimbani nɩɩ aɣanɩ mbanimbani hanlɩ be ti wɔlɩ numɔ ma bowuwalɩ Gyisɛsɩ owulo fɔ. (Mk. 15:1, 13-15) Naasʋ̃, eyimmɔ munwala nu nɩ, Gyisɛsɩ hɔlɩ zʋlɔ yelɩ egyima mɔɔ nɩ Gyehova ɩva iwula yɩ sa nɩ. Na bɛzabɛ sʋ̃ mɔɔ bɛ nɩɩ yɩ iádi yi buwɔ nɩ, ɩáyɛ biye ituwa bɛ kakɛ kyĩ. Mmɩlɩ mɔ bɛyɛlɩ kɛɛ bɛbahyɩ Gyisɛsɩ bɔkɔhɔ bokohũ yi nɩ, Pita wɔlɩ mmɔdɩnɩ bela kɛɛ ɩkɔwɔ yɩ nwʋ banɩ. Meti, Pita dualɩ ɔsɔfʋ ebani yi yɩ sũmɩvʋlɔ yɩ azʋ̃. Naasʋ̃ Gyisɛsɩ yɩ anyɩ iádeli mɔɔ Pita yelɩ nɩ nwʋ, meti iluwalɩ nnwʋánlɩ ɔhʋanɩ zʋ ɩvalɩ benyia yɩ azʋ̃ ɩvʋãmɩnlɩ ɛkɛnɩ. (Lk. 22:50, 51) Wɔ bʊwabɩlɛ eyimmɔ mɔɔ Gyisɛsɩ yiyialɩ munwala nu nɩ, ɩáma yɩ ɛyɛɛlɛ fi egyima mɔɔ nwʋmɔ hyinyia mɔɔ Gyehova va wulalɩ yɩ sa nɩ. Na ɔzɔ la sʋ̃ yɛ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛ nɩɩ yɩ iádi yi buwɔ nɩ, ɩáyɛ biye ituwa bɛ kakɛ a.

12 Dɩkɛɛ Gyisɛsɩ nɩ, yɛzayɛ sʋ̃ yeyiyia ndwɔkɔzɩzɩbɛ azʋlɔtʋ mɔɔ ɩkʋla ikolɔ yɛ abɔkɔ. Setanɩ bɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ɩkɛma yekeyiaki dasɩlɛlilɛ egyima yɩ. Ye mmusunwamaa nɩɩ mmenii mvʋlɔ mɔɔ bɛázʋ̃mɩ Gyehova nɩ sʋ̃ twodwu tiyia yɛ. Naasʋ̃ Gyisɛsɩ hã hileli yɛ kɛɛ yelɔ́ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ botwodwu betiyia yɛ nɩ po. (Kɩnga Matiyu 5:43, 44.) Eyi kile kɛɛ, íni kɛɛ yɛtanɩ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ botwodwu betiyia yɛ nɩ, anzɛnyɩɩ yɛkã kɛɛ yɛyɛɛ biye yekoduwa bɛ kakɛ kyĩ. Mfomi yetiye Gyisɛsɩ yɩ ahyɛdɩ yɩ, na yɛbɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ yɛkɛla kunlumiba, abʋtalɩ, nɩɩ annidi ali yekehile mmenii munwatĩĩ mɔɔ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ botwodwu betiyia yɛ nɩ po kɩnla nwʋmɔ.—1 Kɔl. 13:4.
