Mɩmaa ndɛnɩ wɔ Chinese okuwu be nu wɔ 2006
<<<P>>>
Meti mɩya mʋnwʋnlɩ kɛɛ, sɛ mɩfa Gyehova yɩ zɔ̃mɩnlɛ yɩ midi bɔmʋnlɩ wɔ mɩ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu a, íni kɛɛ mʋdwinɩ mɩ ahyinyiadɩ nwʋ, iluwakɛɛ Gyehova kehila mɩ. Na mɩzamɩ sɔ̃ mʋnwʋnlɩ kɛɛ odwokɔ eyi dɩ nɔhʋanlɛ paa, iluwakɛɛ mʋnwʋnlɩ kezimɔ nɩ Gyehova ikilaa Michel bʋwɔ nɩ. Michel mɛyɩ, nɩ Gyehova yɩ apɛdɩyɛlɛ nwʋ hyinyia yɩ paa, naasʋ̃ nɩ mɔɔ ihyinyia bela nɩ, yɩ sa kã yɩ na yɩ anyɩ sɔ̃ de.
<<<P>>>
Mmɩlɩ mɔ minyianlɩ anyunumihɔlɛ wɔ mɩ Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛ yɩ nu nɩ, mɩyɛlɩ nzɩnzanlɩyɛ wɔ mɩ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu. Mizili pɔkɔ kɛɛ mulɔ̃nʋ nnuno tanɩ dɔkɔ̃. Na ɛkɛnɩ la, mɩyɩbalɩ mɩ nnuno tanɩ yɩ munwatĩĩ, na mɩánṽ dɔkɔ̃ kyĩ. Akʋ̃ mʋwɔlɩ yɩ tu kɛɛ mʋkɔɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ. Eyile ɔkʋ̃ be mɔɔ yɛhɔlɩ dɩyɛzumanlɛ nɩ, mɩhã mihileli eliyema benyia be kɛɛ ɩdɩdĩ mɩ eyinli yɩ ima mɩ. Nɩ mɩ eyinli yi walɩ paa, naasʋ̃ ɩdɩdɩlɩ ɩmanlɩ mɩ wɔ Kɩndɔm Hɔɔlʋ kɛnɩ la.
<<<P>>>
Dapɛnɩ egyikĩĩ be mɛda nɩ, mʋzɔ̃ French Guiana suwa yɩ zʋ mɩyɩlɩ mʋ nwʋ mʋhɔlɩ France. Nɩ Kɩndɔm Hɔɔlʋ wɔ kɛnɩ, meti mʋdʋwalɩ mɩ Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛ yɩ zʋ wɔ kɛ. Nɩ mɩ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ zɩnza kulaa, meti izili mu mmusunwamaa nwʋ baaba. Nɩ ɩyɛ bɛ nnwv́ánlɩ kɛɛ mɩánlʋ nnuno tanɩ nɩ̃ nzã, na mɩáhɔ paati mɔɔ bɛyɛ ɔzɔ nninyeni yi sʋ̃ wɔ yɩ tu nɩ dɔkɔ̃. Nɩ bɛ anyɩ deli nwʋmɔ kɛɛ mɩ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ zɩnza, naasʋ̃ nɩ bɛ anyɩ iáleli mi dedi fulɔ yɩ nwʋ. Akʋ̃ sɔ̃, ɔhʋanɩ mɔɔ nɩ muduwa zʋlɔ mɩkã mi dedi nwʋ odwokɔ mikile bɛ nɩ, nɩ ɩáhɔ bʋwɔ kulaa. Ayɩnzi yi, mɩya muzumanlɩ kɛɛ, sɛ mɩkãã Gyehova nwʋ odwokɔ mikehile bɛ a, ɩsɛ kɛɛ mɩda annidi ali na mʋdwinɩ bɛ nyanlɩdɩlɛ sʋ̃ nwʋ.
<<<P>>>
Wɔ 1996 nu nɩ, munlumanlɩ. Na mʋwɔlɩ Gyehova mpayɩ kɛɛ ɩwʋwá mɩ ma mɩya mɩyɛ odwokɔbadɛlɛlɛvʋlɔ dɩkɛɛ Michel. Nɩ eyi yɛ lɛ ɔhʋanɩba mɔɔ mukoluwa zʋlɔ mikehile kɛɛ mɩ anyɩ sɔ mɔɔ Gyehova ɩyɛ ɩma mɩ nɩ.

GYEHOVA MANLƖ MINYIANLƖ NYILALƐ SŨMAA
<<<P>>>
Nɩ eliyema baalɛ kɩnlɩma be mɔɔ bɔfʋlɔ yɩ Claudine wɔ mɩ asafo yɩ nu, na nɩ mɩ anyɩ deli yɩ nwʋ. Mɩyɛlɩ yɩ ndwĩ kɛɛ nɩ ɩdɩ abiye mɔɔ ɩbɔ ɛhyɩmɛ wɔ dasɩlɛlilɛ nu, na sɛ etiye munwalɩyɛ mɔɔ nɩ ɩma wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tu nɩ sɔ̃ a, nɩ ɔkʋla nwʋ yɩ kɛɛ ɩdɔ Gyehova paa. Nɩ mɩ anyɩ deli nwʋmɔ kɛɛ mɩ nu yɩ kɛyɛ agyiahʋnlɩ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ, naasʋ̃ nɩ mɩpɛ kɛɛ midi bɔmʋnlɩ munwu yɩ kɛɛ sɛ Claudine sɔ̃ yɩ anyɩ deli nwʋmɔ kɛɛ ɩkɛyɛ odwokɔbadɛlɛlɛ egyima yɩ be a. Anyɩnlɩlelɛ odwokɔ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ nɩ yɩzayɩ sɔ̃ yɩ anyɩ deli nwʋ! Meti yegyialɩ wɔ April 2000.
<<<P>>>
Wɔ ɔlʋbɔ mɔɔ ilili ɛkɛnɩ nu nɩ, yɛwɔlɩ numɔ yɛyɛlɩ ɔhʋanɩbukolɛ egyima yɩ. Na wɔ mmɩlɩ be mɛda nɩ, yezili pɔkɔ kɛɛ yekozuma Mandarin ndɛnɩ yɩ na yɛadu yɛahɔ yɛawʋwa Chinese okuwu be mɔɔ ibike yɛ nɩ. Nɩ íla azɩ ɩma mɩ kɛɛ mukozuma ndɛnɩ eyi. Iluwakɛɛ nɩ ɩsɛ kɛɛ musunzu nninyeni nwʋ kɛmɔ Chinese maa sunzu nninyeni nwʋ nɩ la, na ama mɩahʋla mɩahã ndɛnɩ yɩ buwɔ kɛmɔ ɩkɛyɛ a mukɔhʋla mikehilehile ma bɛkɛdɩ yɩ tu. Nɔhʋanlɛ odwokɔ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ mɩákʋla musuma Mandarin ndɛnɩ yɩ bʋwɔ. Naasʋ̃ mɩyɛlɩ mɔɔ mʋkɔhʋla, na yɛ ɛgɛɛlɛ léfi kezimɔ yɛ nu yɛ mmeliyema mɔɔ bɔwɔ Chinese asafo yɩ nu wɔlɩ numɔ yɛlɩ dasɩlɛlilɛ egyima yɩ nɩ kyĩ. Kɛ nyilalɛ nkɔ̃ la yɛ yenyianlɩ a, mfo-mi Gyehova luwalɩ eyi zʋ ziyeziyeli yɛ manlɩ egyimalilɛ be mɔɔ nɩ ɩla yɛ anyunu nɩ sɔ̃.

NDWOKƆZIZIBƐ MƆƆ YEYIYIALL

Nɩ miyiyiaa ndwokɔzizibɛ azʋlɔtu be mɔɔ nɩ ɩsɛ kɛɛ midi nwʋmɔ egyima. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, nɩ mɩtɩ nyanlɩ kɛɛ kɛ mɔɔ ɩsɛ kɛɛ mɩyɛ mɩma Gyehova nɩɩ mɩ mmeliyema munwala yɛ mɩyɛɛ yɩ a. Meti, nɩ mɩsɩkɛ mmɩlɩ sũmaa misiyeziye mɩ egyimalilɛ azʋlɔtu nɩ kɛmɔ ɩkɛyɛ a, mʋkɔhʋla mɩkɛma ndɛnɩ mɔɔ ɩkɔ bʋwɔ paa mɔɔ odwokɔ
