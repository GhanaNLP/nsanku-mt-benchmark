10 Sɛ yeyiyiaa ɔhawʊ a, Setanɩ pɛ kɛɛ yenyia adwɩnɩ kɛɛ Gyehova ɩádwɩnɩ yɛ nwʋ. Na sɛ ɔhawʊ yɩ kɔ zʋlɔ la bʋwa kɛ na yɩ tʋ iádu a, Setanɩ pɛ kɛɛ yɛfa Gyehova nwʋ ɛya iluwakɛɛ ɩlɛ́yʋlɩ yɛ ɔhawʊ yɩ wɔ kɛnɩ nɩ ti. (Ɛyɛ. 19:3) Bɛzabɛ mɔɔ benyia adwɩnɩ mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ nɩ kɔhʋla kɛhã kɛɛ: ‘Sɛ Gyehova ɩyɩ yɩ anyɩ ɩma mɩ a, mɛyɩ nɩ mɩnla ti mɔɔ ɩsɛ kɛɛ mɩyɛ yɩ apɛdɩ a?’ Naasʋ̃, sɛ yenyia adwɩnɩ mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ a, yɛlɛ́yɛ mɔɔ Gyehova pɛ yɩ, na mfomi sa yɛyɛ mɔɔ Setanɩ pɛ yɩ.

11 Setanɩ duwa intanɛtɩ yɩ zʋ ma yɛ adwɩnɩ ɩyɛ yɛ anwĩ anwĩ wɔ Gyehova yɩ mmɛla mɔɔ ɩfalɩ baaba nɩ tanɩ nwʋ nɩ nwʋ. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, wɔ intanɛtɩ yɩ zʋ nɩ, mmenii sũmaa bu mɔɔ Gyehova duwa Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ zʋ kilehile falɩ mɔɔ ɩdɩ baaba nɩ mɔɔ ɩdɩ tanɩ nwʋ nɩ kɛɛ ɩba nwʋ, na bɛkã kɛɛ ɩsɛ kɛɛ abiyela yɛ mɔɔ yɩzayɩ ɩpɛ yɩ. Setanɩ yɛ idii oyuwadɩ yɩ zʋ tumi a, na iluwa anyɩnlɩdɩlɛ ɔhwanɩ zʋ izi mmenii sũmaa bɛ adwɩnɩ anyɩ ma bɛánwʋ sʋnvnkũ mɔɔ ɩwɔ baaba nɩ tanɩ avinli.—Dwɔ. 14:30; Ayɩ. 5:20; 1 Tim. 4:1, 2.
<<<P>>>
12 Sɛ yetiye mɔɔ mmenii mɔɔ bɔwɔ Setanɩ yi oyuwadɩ yi nu nɩ kã falɩ mɔɔ ɩdɩ baaba nɩ tanɩ nwʋ nɩ a, sa ɩkʋla ɩtwĩ yɛ wɔ Gyehova nwʋ. Eliyema baalɛ be mɔɔ yɛkɔvʋlɔ yɩ kɛɛ Shannon mɔɔ inyia alʋbɔ 20 nɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ: “Ihyɛlɩ paa ansaana mʋnwʋʋ kɛɛ mɔɔ musumaa wɔ sukuulu nɩ mmenii mɔɔ mɩ nɩ bɛ bɔ nɩ ɩsɩnzaa adwɩnɩ mɔɔ mɩlɛ yɩ wɔ baaba nɩ tanɩ nwʋ nɩ. Akũ mɩ anyɩ ɣa lelili nninyeni mɔɔ Gyehova kyi nɩ nwʋ. Mɩzɩkɛlɩ mmɩlɩ sũmaa mɩyɩnganlɩ ndwokɔ mɔɔ mmenii bɛhɛlɛ bogua sohyiya midiya zʋ nɩ, akũ mʋwɔlɩ yɩ tʋ kɛɛ minyiaa bɛ adwɩnɩ yɩ be. Ngyɩkĩĩ ngyɩkĩĩ la mɩhwɩnlɩ mʋ nwʋ wɔ Gyehova, mu mmusunwamaa, nɩ mmeliyema yɩ nwʋ.” Naasʋ̃ ayinzi yi, Shannon yɛlɩ nzɩnzanlɩyɛ na izia ɩhyɛlɩ ɩ nɩ yɩ Zɩ Gyehova bɔ ɔhɔnwʋvalɛ yɩ nu ɛzɩzɩbɛ. Meti ɩáma ɔhwanɩ ma Setanɩ nyia yi nzunzunlɩyɛ yɩ zʋ tumi dɔkɔ̃. Kɛ ɩyɛlɩ yɩ izeeli a?

13 Shannon nwʋnlɩ yɩ kɛɛ ihyinyia kɛɛ ɩyɛ nzɩnzanlɩyɛ kezile na ama ɩ nɩ Gyehova nɩ Gyisɛsɩ bɔ ɔhɔnwʋvalɛ yɩ ɩayɛ baaba dɔkɔ̃. Ihanlɩ kɛɛ: “Mʋwɔlɩ Gyehova mpayɩ kɛɛ ɩwʋwá mɩ ma minyia ɔhɔnwʋmmɔba. Akũ sʋ̃ mɩyɛlɩ ɛbɩbɛlɛnu wɔ yɛ awuuku azʋlɔtu nu. Vidiyo mɔɔ yi odwokɔtile se Young People Ask—How Can I Make Real Friends? nɩ wʋwalɩ mɩ paa.* Eliyema baalɛ yɩ mɔɔ nɩ ɩwɔ vidiyo yi nu nɩ, nɩ yɩ bʋwabʋlɛ yɩ dɩkɛɛ mɩ dɩyɛ yɩ la. Izũ kãnwʋazɩ nu ɩbɩbɛlɩ mmʋwalɩyɛ wɔ yɩ awuvʋlɔ kɛ. Meti mizili pɔkɔ kɛɛ mɩzamɩ sʋ̃ mɩkɛyɛ ɔzɔ. Mɩ awʋvʋlɔ, asafʋ nu mbanimbani, nɩ mmeliyema mmaalɛ mɔɔ bɔ nwʋ wʋkwa nɩ wʋwalɩ mɩ ma mɩhwɩnlɩ mʋ nwʋ kulaa kulaa wɔ nninyeni mɔɔ Gyehova kyi nɩ nwʋ na mɩvalɩ ɔhɔnwʋmmɔ mɔɔ bɔdɔ yɩ. Mɩya mʋnwʋnlɩ yɩ kɛɛ, kɛ dɩyezumanlɛhɔlɛ nu mɩ awʋvʋlɔ mɔɔ mɩkɛyɛ sutiye mɩkɛma bɛ yɩzayɩ nkɔ̃ yɛ ikile kɛɛ mʋsũmɩ Gyehova bʋwɔ a. Mfomi ɩsɛ kɛɛ mɩ nɩ Gyehova nyia ɔhɔnwʋvalɛ baaba, na mɩtɩ sianti mɔɔ mide Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ midi nɩ tʋ bʋwɔ.”
