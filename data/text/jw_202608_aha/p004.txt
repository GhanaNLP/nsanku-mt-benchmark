Setanı yı oyuwadı yı bɔ mmɔdınl kɛɛ ɩkɛma yekenyia adwınl kɛɛ
Gyehova yı mmɛla mɔɔ ıfalɩ baaba nɩɩ tanɩ nwu nɩ, yelɔkʋla yekɛva yɛ nwu yekediã zʋlɔ
(Kila nkyɩkyɛmi 11)
<<<P>>>
(2 Kɔl. 4:3, 4) Lma azɔfʋ mbiyemmɔ kilehile kɛɛ Gyisɛsɩ yɛ lɛ Nyamɩnlɩ. Eyi ti, mmenii sũmaa bézi kɛɛ Nyamɩnlɩ yi dima yɛ lɛ Gyehova. Meti kɛ ankı bɔkɔzũmɩ Gyehova nɩ, Gyisɛsɩ mfomi yɛ bɔsũmɩ yɩ a. Naasʋ̃, Gyisɛsɩ nɩɩ Gyehova munwatĩĩ beále ɔzũmɩnlɛ mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ nɩ betiã nu. (Mt. 4:10; Dwɔ. 4:23) Mmenii sũmaa de mɔɔ bɛ azɔfʋ kilehile bɛ nɩ bedi, meti sɛ yekilehilee bɛ nɔhʋanlɛ yɩ po a, bɛádiye kʋlaa. Na ɩyɛ po a, botwodwu betiyia yɛ dasulɛlilɛ egyima yɩ.
<<<P>>>
9 Sɛ yɛpɛ kɛɛ yɛfa abiye ɔhɔnwʋ a, yedi bɔmʋnlɩ yɛnwu yi dima. Naasʋ̃ Setanı ɩábɛ kɛɛ mmenii kɛva Gyehova ɔhɔnwʋ, meti ɩbɔ mmɔdınl kɛɛ ɩléma bɔkɔnwu yi dima po. Alʋbɔ sũmaa ɛdɩ yɛ Setanı luwa awohwi ɔzũmɩnlɛ zʋ ma mmenii bɛ ɛyɛɛlɛ vi Nyamɩnlɩ yi dima a. (Kɩnga Gyɛlɩmaya 23:26, 27.) Bayɩbʋlʋ fa Nyamɩnlɩ yi dima yɩ tindiã banɩ ɛzɩzɩbɛ nwu. Na tamvʋlɔ eyi wɔ mmɔdınl kɛɛ ɩkɛma bɛkɛyɩ Nyamɩnlɩ yi dima yɩ wɔ Bayɩbʋlʋ tʋhilelɛ sũmaa nu. (Ɛɣɛ. 18:10) Akʋ̃ sʋ̃, Setanı luwa awohwi ɔzũmɩnlɛ zʋ ɩma mmenii nyia adwɩnɩ kɛɛ Nyamɩnlɩ yɛ ɩfa amanɩyɛnwʋnlɛ ɩba a. Iluwa eyi ti, ɩma mmenii sũmaa bo kunlu tuli Nyamɩnlɩ nwu na bɛábɛ kɛɛ bɛkɛya kɛyɛ yɩ ɔhɔnwʋ po. (Gye. 4:8) Akʋ̃ sʋ̃, Setanı wɔ mmɔdınl kɛɛ ɩkɛma mmenii keyiaki ɔzũmɩnlɛ mɔɔ bɛfa bɛma Gyehova nɩ.
