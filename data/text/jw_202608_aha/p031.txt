ASŨ EZI A?
<<<P>>>
Wɔ Bayɩbʋlʋ mmɩlɩ nu nɩ, mɩnla yɛ nɩ ɩkɔlɩ zʋlɔ wɔ twonwa yɩ anyɩnkɛ yi munwa a?
<<<P>>>
NƖ TWONWA yɩ anyɩnkɛ ídɩ ɔhʋanɩ mɔɔ mmenii duma numɔ kɔ twonwa nu la. Mfomi nɩ ɩdɩ dɩka mɔɔ nninyeni sũmaa kɔlɩ zʋlɔ wɔ kɛ, iluwakɛɛ nɩ mmenii sũmaa yɛ boduwa nu a. Bayɩbʋlʋ kã ahwonwa anyɩnkɛ mɔɔ lɓʋ 50 nwʋ odwokɔ na ikile nninyeni azulɔtʋ mɔɔ nɩ ɩkɔlɩ zʋ wɔ kɛ.
<<<P>>>
Nɩ mmenii yiyia nu wɔ twonwa yɩ anyɩnkɛ yi munwa. Hibulu odwokɔpɔkɔ “anyɩnkɛ” nɩ, nɩ ígyĩ kɛnɩ ɩma anyɩnkɛ mɔɔ boduwa nu bɔkɔ twonwa yɩ nu anzɛnyɩɩ boduwa nu befinde la. Mfomi, nɩ ɩdɩ dɩka mɔɔ bɔwʋwɔ pɛda sʋ̃ wɔ kɛnɩ. Wɔ nnɩka be nɩ, nɩ twonwa yɩ anyɩnkɛ mɔɔ boduwa nu befinde nɩ lɛ nnɩka pantanwʋʋ mɔɔ mmenii yiyia nu wɔ kɛ.—Ne. 8:1.
<<<P>>>
Nɩ kualɛlilɛmaa tɔnɩ be nninyeni wɔ twonwa yɩ anyɩnkɛ yi munwa. Wɔ Gyelusalamɩ nɩ, nɩ nyɩnkɛ be wɔ kɛ mɔɔ bɔfʋlɔ bɛ Egyeni Anyɩnkɛ, nɩɩ Mmuwuanɩ Anyɩnkɛ, na ɩkɛyɛ kɛɛ benyianlɩ bɛ anlima yi wɔ nninyeni mɔɔ nɩ bɔtɔnɩ wɔ kɛnɩ yi nu.—Zɛf. 1:10; Dwɔ. 5:2.
<<<P>>>
Nɩ mbiyemmɔ kɔdi kualɛ wɔ twonwa yɩ anyɩnkɛ yi munwa, na nɩ mbiyemmɔ sʋ̃ bɔkɔ bɛyɛ bɛzabɛ be nninyeni be wɔ kɛnɩ. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, Abilehamɩ nɩɩ Bowazɩ munwatĩĩ, bɛ nɩɩ mbiyemmɔ yɛlɩ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ wɔ mmɛla ɔhʋanɩ zʋ wɔ dasɩvʋlɔmaa bɛ anyunu wɔ twonwa yɩ anyɩnkɛ yi munwa. (Gyn. 23:10-15, 17, 18; Wu. 4:1, 10) Akɔ̃ sʋ̃, sɛ nɩ ngyɛvʋlɔ be bɛányia abiye ma ide bɛ ɩkɔ yɩ awudo a, wɔ nunuma nɩ, nɩ bɔkɔ bɛda punwuanu dɩka mɔɔ ibike twonwa yɩ anyɩnkɛ yɩ ma awulo kyĩ. (Ndɛ. 19:15-21) Nɩ mbiyemmɔ sʋ̃ kɔ kɛnɩ kɔ tiye odwokɔ fʋlɔ be mɔɔ izi nɩ.—2 Sa. 18:24, 25.
<<<P>>>
Nɩ ndɛnɩwʋwalɛmaa buwa ndɛnɩ wɔ dɩka mɔɔ ibike twonwa yɩ anyɩnkɛ yi munwa. Nɩ ndɛnɩwʋwalɛmaa nɩ di ndwokɔ yɩ wɔ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɔwɔ kɛnɩ yɩ munwatĩĩ bɛ anyunu. Na nɩ ɩma mmenii yi nyia ɔnwʋmɔ ɔhʋanɩ tiye odwokɔ yɩ nu bʋwɔ, na bɔnwʋ kezimɔ ndɛnɩwʋwalɛmaa nɩ kowuwa odwokɔ yɩ sʋ̃.
<<<P>>>
Nɩ nninyeni sũmaa kɔlɩ zʋlɔ wɔ twonwa yɩ anyɩnkɛ yi munwa
<<<P>>>
(Dit. 21:18-20) Nɩ bʋwabɩlɛ eyi kɛma ndɛnɩwʋwalɛmaa nɩ kowuwa odwokɔ yɩ nɩ bʋwɔ. Sɛ nɩ ndɛnɩwʋwalɛmaa nɩ si pɔkɔ wie a, mbanimbani yi fa pɔkɔzɩlɛ nɩ yɛ egyima ndɛndɛ zʋ la. (Dit. 25:2) Nɩ bekila ɔhʋanɩ kɛɛ, ndɛnɩwʋwalɛmaa mɔɔ bobuwa ndɛnɩ wɔ twonwa yɩ anyɩnkɛ yi munwa nɩ kehila ma pelɩpelɩlilɛ nɩɩ ɛbɩla baaba kɔhɔlɩ zʋlɔ wɔ Yizilaya manlɩ yɩ nu.—Dit. 16:18-20; Dwb. 29:7, 11-17.
<<<P>>>
Gyehova yɩ azũmɩvʋlɔ nɔhʋanlɛmaa be hyɛlɩ Yizilayamaa mɔɔ nɩ bɔwɔ twonwa yɩ anyɩnkɛ yi munwa nɩ ɛzɩzɩbɛ kɛɛ benyiá Gyehova nu anwʋlazʋ. Iluwakɛɛ nɩ mmenii ta yiyia numɔ wɔ twonwa yɩ anyɩnkɛ yi munwa nɩ ti, nɩ ɩyɛ a nkɔmhyɛmaa nɩɩ mmenii mvʋlɔ kyina kɛnɩ fa amanyɛwɔlɛ ma manlɩ yɩ. (1 Aw. 22:10; Gyɛ. 17:19, 20) Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, Gyihu hanlɩ wɔ mmenii yi munwatĩĩ bɛ anyunu kɛɛ mɔɔ Gyehova hanlɩ kɛɛ ɩkɛva kɛyɛ Ehabɩ yɩ awudomaa nɩ ya nu. (2 Aw. 10:8-10) Bulombunli Hɛzɩkaya sʋ̃ hunwahunwanlɩ yɩ azogyiamaa mbanimbani bo munwa wɔ twonwa yɩ anyɩnkɛ yi munwa, na ɩhyɛlɩ bɛ ɛzɩzɩbɛ kɛɛ bɛvá bɔ nwʋ bediã Gyehova zʋ.—(2 Mm. 32:6-8)
<<<P>>>
Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ kã kɛɛ “nyɛlɛbɛ” tɩnyɩa numɔ wɔ “twonwa yɩ anyɩnkɛ yi munwa.” (Ɛyɛ. 1:20, 21) Wɔ ɔwɔkɩ Yizilaya nɩ, nɩ twonwa yɩ anyɩnkɛ yi munwa yɛ nɩ mmenyia nɔhʋanlɛmaa ma fulɔdulɛba na bobuwa ndɛnɩ mɔɔ ɩtɩngɛ a. Nnɛ sʋ̃, yɛnwʋ Gyehova yɩ nyɛlɛbɛ nwu odwokɔ wɔ Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ nu. Na yɩ azũmɩvʋlɔ mɔɔ bɔwɔ oyuwadɩ dɩka bela nɩ bɔbuwaa mmenii ma bɛkɛbɩbɛ nyɛlɛbɛ ankasa yɩ wɔ Bayɩbʋlʋ yi nu.
