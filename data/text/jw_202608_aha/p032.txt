<<<P>>>
Wɔ Bayɩbʋlʋ mmɩlɩ nu nɩ, mɩnla yɛ nɩ ɩkɔlɩ zʋlɔ wɔ twonwa yɩ anyɩnkɛ yi munwa a?

BAYƖBƲLƲ NU NƆHƲANLƐ ODWO KƆ BE
<<<P>>>
Owudo Mɔɔ Itunwo Ndɛ
<<<P>>>
Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ kã owudo kɛyɛ 30 nwʋ odwokɔ. Numɔ ɔkʋ̃ yɛ lɛ almond owudo yi. Sɛ ɔkɔ Yizi-laya a, almond kɩnla nnuno mɔɔ bedi bɔmʋnlɩ bopuko fɩlawɛsɩ wɔ mmɩlɩ mɔ ɛyɛbɛ mmɩlɩ ɩkɔɔ yɩ awielɩyɛ nɩ nwʋ. Almond owudo yi yɛ ekeli bɔmʋnlɩ kɔnwʋ yɩ fɩlawɛsɩ pɩkɛ pɩkɛ yɩ mɔɔ ɩdɩ kɔla pɩnkɩ anzɛnyɩɩ fuvulo yi ansaana wʋanwʋ owudo fulɔ bela yɩ fɩlawɛsɩ a. Dima mɔɔ nɩ Yizilayamaa fa fulɔ owudo eyi nɩ, nɩ yɩ tu kile kɛɛ, “owudo mɔɔ itunwo ndɛ.” Asũ Dɩyɛwɔlɛnvʋlɔ yɩ yɩ subanɩ be da ali wɔ owudo eyi nu a?
<<<P>>>
Sɛ yekila Gyelɩmaya 7:25 yɩ azɩkɛnɩ odwokɔ yɩ nu a, Gyehova hanlɩ valɩlɩ yɩzayɩ yɩ nwʋ kɛɛ, ɩdɩ kɛɛ abiye mɔɔ itwodwu nwɔnɩ dɛkɛdɛkɛ. Wɔ March 15, 2011 Ɔwɩenlɛ Ayanɩ yɩ nu nɩ, bɛhanlɩ kɛɛ: “Gyehova fa yɩ nwʋ tindiã almond owudo nwʋ. Kɛmɔ owudo eyi tunwo ndɛ nɩ, ɔzɔ la yɛ Gyehova kã kɛɛ, eyile bela itunwo nwɔnɩ dɛkɛdɛkɛ ɩbɔ yi mmenii kɔkɔ wɔ ɔhawʋ mɔɔ bekeyiyia mmɩlɩ mɔ bɛyɛ nninyeni tanɩ nɩ nwʋ a. Akʋ̃ sɔ̃, lhanlɩ ihileli bɛ kɛɛ, sa ikila ma yi munwa nu ndwokɔ munwala ba numɔ.”—Gyɛ. 1:11, 12.
<<<P>>>
Iyɛ a, Bayɩbʋlʋ fa bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛ alʋbɔ hɔ anyunumi nɩ be ti zʋ odwoni tindiã almond owudo yi fɩlawɛsɩ fuvulo yɩ nwʋ. (Odw. 12:5) Na mmɩlɩ mɔ Bɛzalɛlɩ ɩyɛɛ kɩnlanɩyɛ owudo mɔɔ bɛkɛva keziye ndamɛ aliyia yi nu nɩ, ɩyɛlɩ kɩnlanɩyɛ owudo yɩ kɔɔpʋ mɔɔ botwonwɔ ngɔ-lɔ bokua numɔ nɩ dɩkɛɛ almond owudo yɩ fɩla-wɛsɩ.—Ɛkɩ. 37:17-20.
