Gyehova iluwa Gyisɛsɩ Kɩlayɩsɩ zʋ ile yɛ
(Kila nkyɩkyɛmi 6)
<<<P>>>
Amagadɔnʋ kʋnɛ yɩ nu. Wɔ Gyisɛsɩ yɩ Alʋbɔ Abʋmɩ Tumililɛ tʋ nɩ, ikodunwo awuvʋlɔ, na ngyɩkĩĩ ngyɩkĩĩ la ɩ nʋ mmenii 144,000 mɔɔ ɩ nʋ bɛ keli tumi nɩ kɛma adasamaa keli munli. (Dwɔ. 5:28; Yik. 22:1, 2) Alʋbɔ Abʋmɩ Tumililɛ yɩ kɛya yɩ awielɩyɛ nɩ, nɩ bɛyɩ tanɩ nʋ owulo wɔ kɛnɩ kʋlaa kʋlaa. (1 Kɔl. 15:26, 55-57) Akʋ̃ bɛkɛzɩkɛ Setanɩ nʋ yɩ awɔvʋlɔ tanɩ yɩ, nʋ enii bela mɔɔ thɔ ilili Setanɩ nʋ yi mmenii yi bɛ mɛda nɩ. (Yik. 20:7-10) Na eyi kɛma bɛzabɛ mɔɔ belili Gyehova nɔhʋanlɛ nɩ kɔhʋla kɔzʋ̃mɩ Gyehova mɔɔ tanɩ, owulo, anzɛnyʋ odwodwudiyialɛmaa bela ilédide bɛ dɔkʋ̃ kyĩ! Wɔ ɔzɔ mmɩlɩ nɩ, yɛkɛyɩ yɛ Nyamɩnlɩ ayɛlɛ daabaa kɛɛ iluwa Kɩlayɩsɩ Gyisɛsɩ zʋ ile yɛ nnwʋanlɩ.—2 Kɔl. 9:15.

YENYIA ƆNWƲMƆ ƆHƲANL YƐSƲ̃MƖƖ GYEHOVA
<<<P>>>
7 Kɩnga Luuku 1:74, 75. Gyisɛsɩ yɩ nnwʋanlɩ mɔɔ ɩva idianlɩ kɛnɩ ɩmanlɩ yɛ nɩ ma yenyia ɔnwʋmɔ ɔhʋanɩ titili be. Zakaliyia hanlɩ nwʋmɔ odwokɔ kɛɛ ɩma yenyia ɔnwʋmɔ ɔhʋanɩ mɔɔ yɛfa ‘yɛsʋ̃mɩ mɔɔ ahunlududulɛ bela íni numɔ.’ Mɩnla ɔhʋanɩ zʋ yɛ ‘yɛsʋ̃mɩ’ Gyehova a? Sɛ yɛkã odwokɔba yɩ, yɛbɔ mpayɩ, yesuma Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ, na yɛkɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ, mansini nʋ manlɩ-avinli ayiyialɩyɛ a, nɩ ikile kɛɛ yɛsʋ̃mɩɩ Gyehova. Akʋ̃ sʋ̃, sɛ yɛsʋ̃mɩ wɔ Betɩlɩ, yɛbʋwa ma bekyina na besiyeziye Kɩndɔm Hɔɔlʋ nʋ Ayiyialɩyɛ Nnɩka azulɔtu a, nɩ ikile kɛɛ yɛsʋ̃mɩɩ Gyehova.

8 Sɛ bɛkã kɛɛ yɛsʋ̃mɩɩ Gyehova mɔɔ “ahunlududulɛ bela íni numɔ” a, kɛ yɩ tʋ kile a? Mɔɔ ili bɔmʋnlɩ, ikile kɛɛ ye kunlu iáduli owulo nwʋ. Iluwakɛɛ yɛwɔ nnwʋanlɩ mɔɔ íni awielɩyɛ nwʋ anyɩnlɩlazʋ nɩ ti, yɛáva yɛ mmɩlɩ nʋ yɛ anwʋzɩzɩbɛ munwatĩĩ yɛyɛ mɔɔ yɛzayɛ yɛpɛ yɩ, mfomi yɛbɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ yɛkɛva Gyehova yekeli bɔmʋnlɩ wɔ yɛ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu. Sɛ yɛ adamvʋlɔ fa owulo nwunlanwunla yɛ po a yɛáma
