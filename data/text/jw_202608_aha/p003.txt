SETANƖ HANLƖ GYEHOVA NLL GYISƐSƖ NWƲ AWOHWI NDWOKƆ
<<<P>>>
3 Gyehova wɔlɩ Adamɩ nɩ Yifi nɩ, ɩákyɛ kʋlaa yɛ Setanɩ manlɩ bɛyɛlɩ Gyehova zʋ azɔ̃nzɩzɩbɛ a. Setanɩ yizialɩ Yifi kɛɛ: “Ɩdɩ amba kɛɛ Nyamɩnlɩ tʰã kɛɛ máma bedi toolo yi nu nnuno yɩ zʋ mma nɩ be a?” (Gyn. 3:1-5) Nɩ Setanɩ pɛ kɛɛ Yifi nyia adwɩnɩ kɛɛ Gyehova ɩlɛ nninyeni be isiyee bɛ. Nɩ Setanɩ idii awohwi, naasʋ̃ Yifi leli yi lili. Iwɔ nʋmɔ kɛɛ Gyehova yɛ ɩmanlɩ nnwʋanlɩ nu dɩyɛ bela wɔ Adamɩ nɩ Yifi dɩyɛ, naasʋ̃ alɔbɔ odwokɔ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ, bɛyɛlɩ yɩ zʋ azɔ̃nzɩzɩbɛ na bediyeli Setanɩ yi odwokɔ mfomi.—Gyn. 3:6.
<<<P>>>
4 Ɔzʋ̃mɩ Adamɩ nɩ Yifi bɛ mmɩlɩ zʋ yɛ Setanɩ hɔ zʋlɔ hɩnhã Gyehova nwʋ awohwi ndwokɔ a. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, Setanɩ luwalɩ Dwoobu yɩ ɔhɔnwʋmmɔ asã yɩ zʋ manlɩ Dwoobu dɩlɩ nyanlɩ kɛɛ Gyehova tálɔ yɩ, na sɛ idi Gyehova nɔhʋanlɛ o, ɩádi yi nɔhʋanlɛ o, ɩávalɩ yɩ nwʋ. (Dwb. 22:1-3) Setanɩ valɩ ɔhawʋ sʋ̃maa yalɩ Dwoobu zʋ. Nɩ ɩlɛ adwɩnɩ be kɛɛ sɛ Dwoobu iyiyiaa ɔhawʋ a, ɩkɛva nwʋmɔ ɔzʋnɩ kediã Gyehova zʋ, na ilényia ɔlɔlɛ ɩkɛma Gyehova dɔkʋ̃. Iwɔ nʋmɔ kɛɛ Setanɩ manlɩ Dwoobu nwʋnlɩ amanɩyɛ sʋ̃maa dɩyɛ, naasʋ̃ Dwoobu hɔlɩ zʋlɔ lili Gyehova mɔɔ ɩdɩ yɩ ɔhɔnwʋ nɩ nɔhʋanlɛ.—Dwb. 27:1, 5.
<<<P>>>
5 Mmɩlɩ mɔ Gyisɛsɩ yalɩ azɩlɛ yɩ zʋ nɩ, Setanɩ wɔlɩ mmɔdɩnɩ bela kɛɛ ɩlɛ́ma mmenii keli Gyisɛsɩ yɩ meda. Ɔhʋanɩ ɔkʋ̃ mɔɔ iluwalɩ zʋlɔ ɩyɛlɩ ɔzɔ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ, ɩmanlɩ nyamɩnlɩzʋ̃mɩnlɛ nu mbanimbani nɩ mmenii mvʋlɔ hɩnhanlɩ awohwi ndwokɔ valɩlɩ Gyisɛsɩ nwʋ. Mmenii sʋ̃maa leli ɔzɔ awohwi ndwokɔ nɩ lili, na mɛyɩ manlɩ bohunli Gyisɛsɩ kɛɛ tanɩvʋlɔ.—Kɩnga Matiyu 11:19; 27:27-31; Mk. 14:55, 56; Dwɔ. 8:41-45.
<<<P>>>
6 Wɔ Gyisɛsɩ yi owulo mɛda nɩ, Setanɩ wɔlɩ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ikogolɔ adʋwavʋlɔ yɩ bɛ abɔkɔ, na nɩ ɩábɛ kɛɛ bedi Gyisɛsɩ nwʋ dasɩlɛ sʋ̃. Mɔɔ ilili bɔmʋnlɩ ɩyɛlɩ yɛ dɩ, nɩ ɩábɛ kɛɛ mmenii de di kɛɛ Nyamɩnlɩ idunwo Gyisɛsɩ wɔ owulo nu. Meti Setanɩ manlɩ mmenii be lili awohwi kɛɛ Gyisɛsɩ yɩ adʋwavʋlɔ yɩ bɛya bowuwa yi funli yi bɔhɔ. (Mt. 28:11-15) Eyi mɛda nɩ, Setanɩ manlɩ azɔfʋ yɩ nɩ adumivʋlɔ yɩ valɩ odwodwudiyialɛ yalɩ adʋwavʋlɔ yɩ zʋ. Nɩ Setanɩ yɩ adwɩnɩ ɩyɛ yɩ kɛɛ eyi konwunlanwunla bɛ. Nɩ ɩpɛ kɛɛ itudu bo kunlu nkʋ̃ʋ̃ ma beyiaki egyima mɔɔ be Niibenyia ɩva iwula bɛ sa nɩ.—Egy. 7:57–8:1; 12:1-3; 28:22.
<<<P>>>
7 Iwɔ numɔ, bɔlɔ hanlɩ ɔwɔkɩ Kilisinyiamaa yɩ be ma beyiakili dasɩlɛlilɛ egyima yɩ. Naasʋ̃ bo nu sʋ̃maa hɔlɩ zʋlɔ lili Gyisɛsɩ nɔhʋanlɛ na bɛáyiaki dasɩlɛlilɛ egyima yɩ. Bɔnwʋnlɩ yɩ kɛɛ ɩdɩ Setanɩ nɩ azunzuma evinli yi yɛ bɛpɛ kɛɛ besi egyima yɩ ɔhʋanɩ a. (Egy. 4:18-20; 1 Pit. 5:8) Na nnɛ sʋ̃ ɛ? Mɩnla nnahɩlɩbɛ ɔhʋanɩ yɛ Setanɩ ɩbɔɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ikoluwa zʋlɔ ɩkɛma yekeyiaki ɔzʋ̃mɩnlɛ mɔɔ yɛfa yɛma Gyehova nɩ a?
<<<P>>>
SETANƖ KƆ ZƲLƆ KÃ AWOHWI NDWOKƆ FALI GYEHOVA NLƖ GYISƐSƖ NWƲ
<<<P>>>
8 Nnɛ, nnahɩlɩbɛ ɔhʋanɩ ɔkʋ̃ mɔɔ Setanɩ duwa zʋlɔ dahɩlɩ mmenii yɛ lɛ kɛɛ ɩkɛyɩda nɔhʋanlɛ mɔɔ ɩfalɩ Gyehova nɩ Gyisɛsɩ nwʋ nɩ zʋ. Abɔnsamɩ dɩ “awohwimaa munwala bɛ zɩ,” na iduwa awohwi ɔzʋ̃mɩnlɛ zʋ ɩpɩndɩ awohwi ndwokɔ ɩfalɩ Gyehova nɩ Gyisɛsɩ nwʋ. (Dwɔ. 8:44) Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, Abɔnsamɩ izi mmenii sʋ̃maa bɛ adwɩnɩ anyɩ, na bɛánwʋ nɔhʋanlɛ ndwokɔ mɔɔ ɩfalɩ Gyehova nɩ Gyisɛsɩ nwʋ nɩ.
