88. Arane vale menli mɔɔ lɛ aleɛdonlɛ nu sukoanwu nee sukoanyia yɛɛ aleɛtovoma 32,496, bɔbɔ mraalɛ gyima nu, amaa bɛ gya gyinlabelɛ ahɔ anwuma na bɛanyia bie bɛanlea bɛ mbusua. 92. Arane bahɔ zo yeayɛ moalɛ mɔɔ anu mia (di munli) kpalɛ la yeamaa menli mɔɔ ɛva ɛdɔnenlɛ (menli-nwo gualilɛ) nu na bɛlɛ ngoane la.
