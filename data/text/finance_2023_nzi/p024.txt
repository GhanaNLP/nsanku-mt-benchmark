<<<P>>>
40. Wɔ kookoo gyima ne anu, arane luale ngyehyeleɛ ngakyile mɔɔ bie a le ɛziezielɛ, ɛlualɛ bieko, ayile ɛkpululɛ, fɛtɛlaeza ɛgualɛ nee mbaka nwo ɛpɛpɛlɛ maanle mgbavolɛ nee mbɛlɛra nyianle gyima yɛle. Gyima ɛhye mɔ ɛboa ɛmaa bɛva anwosesebɛ mɔɔ bɛlɛ la bɛyɛ gyima mɔɔ nvasoɛ wɔ zo, ɔlua zo bɛde azua mgbole zo ɛhɔlɛ ne azo.
