57. Amaa yeahola yɛazɔ maanle nu ɛhakyilɛ nwo ngyegyelɛ ne anloa la, arane bahɔ zolɛ akpondɛ mbaka ngakyile mɔɔ bɛdua mɔɔ kola gyinla kpundii wɔ nzuwielɛ anzɛɛ maanle nu reserese ɛyɛlɛ mekɛ nloa la yeava yeazɔ ngyegyelɛ ne anloa. Eza ɔbava ezukoa yeawula nzule mɔɔ bɛfa bɛyɛ egyinli la anu wɔ maanle ye ɛleka biala. Ɔbazi menli adua ɛmaa bɛava bɛ nwo bawula nyɛmoa ɛzielɛ nu amaa bɛanyia ezukoa na yemɔ azi kakɛ mɔɔ ɔyɛ a bɛbɔ ye wɔ aleɛ aliɛ ɛlualɛ nwo la adenle.

58. Wɔ ɛvolɛ 2022 anu, Maanzinli Nnu Nzule Ɛmaanlɛ Gyima ne mɔɔ wɔ Anwɔnla Maanzinli nu (ngyɛnu 3 ne) mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye ‘STRABAG’ Nzule Gyima ne la, anwo gyimayɛlɛ ne ɛdwu ɛya-ngyɛnu 9 anzi kpɔkɛ 5 la maa Adaklu Anyigbe; Agotime-Ziope; Tongu Avinli; Ho Adɔleɛ yɛɛ Tongu Sɔlɔ Maanzinli ne mɔ nzule. Saa bɛwie nzule ɛhye yɛ a, ɔbayɛ nvasoɛ yeamaa azua 424 yɛɛ menli mɔɔ bɛ dodo bo 234,000 la.

59. Arane doale ɛnweanloa nwo banebɛlɛ gyima ne azo kɛ ɔfa yeabɔ ɛnweanloa azua azua ne mɔ anwo bane yeavi bɛ ɛnrinzinlɛ nee bɛ ɛyilɛ nwo. Ɔbɔle menli bɛ azedɛnlanlɛ nee ninyɛne nwo bane mekɛ ko ne ala anu ɔvile nyevile ɛzɛkyelɛ sa nu. Kɛkala, ɛnweanloa banebɛlɛ gyima ne anwo gyimayɛlɛ ɛleka mɔɔ ɛhɔ ɛdwu la a le Dansoman (ɛya-ngyɛnu 97), Komenda (ɛya-ngyɛnu 95), Elmina Ndɔzo III (ɛya-ngyɛnu 92), Cape Coast (ɛya-ngyɛnu 80) and Anomabu (ɛya-ngyɛnu 75), Dixcove (ɛya-ngyɛnu 40) Aboadze Ndɔzo II (ɛya-ngyɛnu 45) yɛɛ Ningo-Prampram (ɛya-ngyɛnu 50).
