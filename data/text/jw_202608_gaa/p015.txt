ekolɛ Paulo baabi ehe akɛ ‘Ani Filemon baagbala Onesimo toi? Kɛ́ efee nakai lɛ, te asafoŋbii lɛ baafee amɛnii yɛ he amɛhã tɛŋŋ? Ni te nuŋtsɔmɛi krokomɛi kɛ nyɔji krokomɛi ni yɔɔ asafo lɛ mli lɛ baanu he amɛhã tɛŋŋ?’ Paulo le akɛ sane nɛɛ baanyɛ afite toiŋjɔlɛ ni yɔɔ asafo lɛ mli lɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, eŋma wolo ni ekɛhã Tihiko koni ekɛyahã Filemon. (Efe. 6:21, 22; Kol. 4:7-9) Jeee Filemon pɛ wolo eŋma, eŋma asafo ni yɔɔ Kolose lɛ hu wolo. Yɛ nikasemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaasusu saji komɛi ni yɔɔ woji enyɔ nɛɛ amli lɛ ahe ni wɔkwɛ nibii ni amɛbaahã wɔkase yɛ Paulo, Onesimo, kɛ Filemon ŋɔɔ.

4 Mɛni wɔbaanyɛ wɔkase kɛjɛ nɔ ni Paulo fee lɛ mli? Bɔ ni ekɛ nyɛmimɛi lɛ ye ehã lɛ hã toiŋjɔlɛ hi asafo lɛ mli. Wɔ hu wɔbaanyɛ wɔkase lɛ ni wɔfee nakai nɔŋŋ.
<<<P>>>
5 Paulo kɛ Filemon ye akɛ enaanyo. (Flm. 17) Paulo efeee enii tamɔ eji Filemon nuŋtsɔ ni ebaanyɛ efamɔ lɛ. (2 Kor. 1:24) Moŋ lɛ, ena Filemon akɛ enyɛmi Kristofonyo, ni ekai lɛ akɛ nanemɛi ji amɛ. Nɔ ni Paulo fee lɛ baahã Filemon ana bɔ ni esa akɛ ekɛ Onesimo ni amrɔ nɛɛ ebatsɔ Kristofonyo lɛ aye ahã. Ni wɔ fɛɛ wɔbaanyɛ wɔkase nii kɛjɛ nɔ ni Paulo fee lɛ mli, titri lɛ, wɔteŋ mɛi ni miisɔmɔ akɛ asafoŋ onukpai lɛ. Kɛ́ asafoŋ onukpai lɛ kɛ nyɛmimɛi lɛ ye akɛ nanemɛi lɛ, ebaahã toiŋjɔlɛ ahi asafo lɛ mli. Akɛni asafoŋ onukpai lɛ sumɔɔ nyɛmimɛi lɛ hewɔ lɛ, amɛkɛ amɛ yeee tamɔ nɔ ni amɛ amɛji amɛnuŋtsɔmɛi. (1 Pet. 5:1-3) Moŋ lɛ, amɛnáa dekã amɛhãa asafoŋbii lɛ, amɛboɔ amɛ toi, ni akɛni amɛshwɛɛɛ amɛhe kwraa hewɔ lɛ, amɛyeɔ amɛbuaa amɛ kɛ yelikɛbuamɔ ko he miihia amɛ. Tamɔ Paulo fee lɛ, kɛ́ asafoŋ onukpai lɛ kɛ nyɛmimɛi lɛ ye akɛ nanemɛi lɛ, ehãa nyɛmimɛi lɛ hu kɛ amɛhe yeɔ yɛ nakai gbɛ nɔŋŋ nɔ, ni no hãa toiŋjɔlɛ yaa nɔ ehiɔ asafo lɛ mli.

6 Paulo jie Filemon yi. (Flm. 4, 5, 7) Kɛ́ ajie wɔyi lɛ, ewoɔ yibii kpakpai. Mɛni hewɔ wɔkɛɔ nakai? Ejaakɛ wɔteŋ mɛi pii yɛ ni bei komɛi lɛ, wɔyeɔ wɔtsui yɛ saji komɛi ahe. Ekolɛ wɔbaaye wɔtsui kɛji mɛi naa wɔ akɛ wɔhe yɛ fɛo loo wɔhe bɛ fɛo, mɛi sumɔɔ wɔsane loo amɛsumɔɔɔ wɔsane, loo kɛji sɛɛmɔ hegbɛ ko ni wɔyɔɔ lɛ baaje wɔdɛŋ. Naagba bɛ he akɛ wɔbaanu he nakai bei komɛi. Shi kɛ́ wɔkwɛɛɛ ni ahi lɛ, bɔ ni wɔnuɔ he wɔhãa lɛ baanyɛ ahã wɔhao loo saji agba wɔnaa mra, loo efee wɔ akɛ mɛi miiwie wɔsane loo wɔhã mɛi ana po akɛ wɔhi fe amɛ. Kɛ́ eba lɛ nakai lɛ, wɔkɛ mɛi ehiŋ shi yɛ toiŋjɔlɛ mli. Shi kɛ́ mɔ ko nuɔ he nakai ni ajie eyi lɛ, no baahã enu he akɛ ehe yɛ sɛɛnamɔ. (Abɛi 12:25) Ebaahã enu he hu akɛ hiɛ sɔɔ lɛ ni asumɔɔ lɛ. Ani osumɔŋ ni onifeemɔi ahã mɔ ko anu he nakai? Kɛ́ nakai lɛ, ŋɔɔ hegbɛ ni okɛjie mɛi ayi yɛ nɔ kpakpa ko ni amɛfeɔ lɛ he. Kaahã ohiɛ kpa nɔ akɛ, jeee be ni mɔ ko efee nɔ ko jogbaŋŋ pɛ esa akɛ ojie eyi. Moŋ lɛ, kɛ́ ebɔ mɔdɛŋ akɛ ebaafee nɔ ko kpakpa ko, shi ekolɛ eyayeee omanye po lɛ, no hu esa akɛ ojie eyi. Kɛ́ wɔbaafo lɛ kuku lɛ, kɛ́ ona nɔ ko kpakpa ko yɛ mɔ ko he lɛ, kɛɛmɔ lɛ.
