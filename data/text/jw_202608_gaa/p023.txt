wɔbaahi shi kɛya naanɔ hewɔ lɛ, wɔkɛ wɔbe kɛ wɔhewalɛ fɛɛ efeee nɔ ni wɔsumɔɔ pɛ. Mɔŋ lɛ, wɔhãa Yehowa sɔɔmɔ lɛ he hiaa wɔ fe nibii krokomɛi fɛɛ. Agbɛnɛ hu, kɛ́ mɛi ni he tsɛ̃ɔ wɔ lɛ kɛɛ wɔ akɛ amɛbaagbe wɔ lɛ, wɔhãaa no awo wɔhe gbeyei ni wɔkpa Yehowa sɔɔmɔ. Mɔŋ lɛ, wɔyaa nɔ wɔyeɔ Yehowa anɔkwa ejaakɛ wɔle akɛ kɛ́ wɔgboi lɛ, wɔsɛɛ lɛ, Yesu kɛ wɔ baaba wala mli ekoŋŋ. (Yoh. 6:39, 40) Nɔ ni ji enyɔ lɛ, etsɔɔ akɛ wɔsheee gbeyei akɛ eshafeelɔi ni wɔji lɛ hewɔ lɛ, Yehowa baakpoo wɔjeŋ ehã wɔ. Wɔyɛ nɔmimaa akɛ Yehowa sumɔɔ wɔ ni eyɛ wɔhe miishɛɛ, ni Yesu kpɔmɔ afɔle lɛ hewɔ lɛ, ekɛ wɔhe eshai lɛ baake wɔ.—1 Yoh. 4:9, 10.

9 Shi yɛ wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ ekomɛi agbɛfaŋ lɛ, ewa kɛhã amɛ akɛ amɛbaahe amɛye akɛ amɛsɔɔmɔ lɛ saa Yehowa hiɛ. Ekolɛ yɛ shihilɛ gbonyo ko ni amɛtsɔ mli be ko ni eho lɛ hewɔ lɛ, amɛnuɔ he akɛ amɛsaaa mɛi ni Yehowa baasumɔ amɛ. Aloo be ko ni eho lɛ amɛfee eshai komɛi ni yɔɔ hiɛdɔɔ ni amɛkpa Yehowa fai ni ekɛke amɛ, ni amɛhã asafoŋ onukpai lɛ ye amɛbua amɛ, shi loloolo lɛ, amɛhenilee yiɔ amɛ yɛ nakai eshai lɛ ahewɔ. Kɛ́ nakai onuɔ he lɛ, mɛni obaanyɛ ofee?

10 Susumɔ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ nɛɛ he okwɛ. Kɛ́ mɔ ko nane ku ni etee datrɛfonyo ŋɔɔ lɛ, datrɛfonyo lɛ tsaa enane lɛ, ni ekɛɔ lɛ ni efee nibii komɛi koni enyɛ enyiɛ jogbaŋŋ ekoŋŋ. Shi kɛ́ mɔ lɛ susuɔ akɛ enyɛŋ enyiɛ dɔŋŋ lɛ, mɛni esa akɛ efee? Esa akɛ ehe datrɛfonyo lɛ eye, ni eya nɔ efee nibii ni ekɛɛ lɛ ni efee lɛ. Kɛ́ efee nakai lɛ, yɛ be ni sa mli lɛ, ehe baawa lɛ ni ebaanyiɛ jogbaŋŋ ni ejo foi po.

11 Yɛ nakai gbɛ nɔŋŋ nɔ lɛ, kɛ́ esha ko ni ofee lɛ ekumɔ otsui, ni no hewɔ lɛ oonu he akɛ Yehowa náŋ ohe miishɛɛ lɛ, esa akɛ ohe Yehowa oye akɛ ebaawa bo. Ná ole akɛ Yehowa dromɔ lɛ kɛ emɔbɔnalɛ lɛ hewɔ lɛ, ebaatsɔ kpɔmɔnɔ lɛ nɔ ehã ohe awa bo ekoŋŋ. (Yoh. 1:16, 17; Rom. 3:23, 24; 1 Yoh. 3:19, 20) No hewɔ lɛ, feemɔ nibii ni ewo bo ŋaa ni ofee koni ohe awa bo ekoŋŋ lɛ. Nomɛi ji, sɔlemɔ, nikasemɔ, kɛ kpeei srɔtoi lɛ ni obaaya. (Lala 1:2; 1 Tes. 5:17; Heb. 10:25) Tamɔ bɔ ni kɛ́ mɔ ko nane ku lɛ, ekolɛ ebaahe be dani mɔ lɛ he baawa lɛ ekoŋŋ lɛ, nakai nɔŋŋ ebaahe be dani obaaná nɔmimaa akɛ Yehowa sumɔɔ bo. Shi obaanyɛ ohe oye akɛ ohe baawa bo ekoŋŋ. No hewɔ lɛ, onijiaŋ akaje wui. Yaa nɔ ofee nibii ni baahã okɛ Yehowa teŋ naanyobɔɔ lɛ mli awa. (Fip. 3:16) Ni daa gbi lɛ, daa Yehowa shi ejaakɛ ehãa bo hewalɛ ni he baahia bo koni onyɛ oya nɔ osɔmɔ lɛ. Yɛ anɔkwale mli lɛ, wɔ fɛɛ wɔdaa Yehowa kɛ Yesu shi ejaakɛ amɛhã wɔ hegbɛ ni wɔkɛ sɔɔmɔ krɔŋŋ ahã ni wɔsheee gbeyei!

12 Kanemɔ Luka 1:76-79. Zakaria kɛɛ ebinuu fioo lɛ akɛ: “Obaatsɔ Yehowa hiɛ oyasaa egbɛi lɛ oto.” Te enɛ ba mli ehã tɛŋŋ? Be ni ebinuu fioo ni ji Yohane Baptisilɔ lɛ da lɛ, ebatsɔ shiɛlɔ ni yɔɔ ekãa waa. Ewo Yudafoi lɛ hewalɛ koni amɛtsake amɛtsui yɛ amɛhe eshai lɛ ahe, amɛhã abaptisi amɛ, ni amɛkpa Mesia lɛ baa lɛ gbɛ. Be ni ebaptisi Yesu sɛɛ lɛ, ehã mɛi le akɛ Yesu jɛ Nyɔŋmɔ ŋɔɔ. Ewie akɛ: “Naa, Nyɔŋmɔ Gwantɛŋbi ni wóɔ je lɛ he esha lɛ kɛyaa lɛ!” No sɛɛ lɛ, Yohane wo ekaselɔi lɛ hewalɛ koni amɛnyiɛ Yesu, ni ji Mesia lɛ, sɛɛ.—Yoh. 1:29, 34-36, 40, 41.
