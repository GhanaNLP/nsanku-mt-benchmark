16 Kɛ́ wɔjwɛŋ bɔ ni Yehowa kɛ Yesu eye ebua wɔ yɛ wɔshihilɛ mli ehã lɛ nɔ lɛ, no baahã wɔhe wɔye akɛ amɛsumɔɔ wɔ waa. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔsusu be ko ni Yehowa hã wɔsɔlemɔ ko hetoo loo ewaje wɔ lɛ he. Kɛ́ wɔjwɛŋ bɔ ni eye ebua wɔ yɛ nakai be lɛ mli lɛ nɔ lɛ, no baahã efee faŋŋ kɛhã wɔ akɛ ebaaya nɔ ekwɛ wɔ. Wɔbaanyɛ wɔbi nyɛmimɛi lɛ hu ni amɛtsɔɔ wɔ bɔ ni Yehowa eye ebua amɛ ehã. (Abɛi 22:17) Kɛ́ wɔfee nakai lɛ, no baahã wɔná nɔmimaa akɛ be fɛɛ be ni yelikɛbuamɔ he baahia wɔ lɛ, Yehowa kɛ Yesu baaye amɛbua wɔ.—Kanemɔ Lala 118:5, 6.

17 Wɔ fɛɛ wɔmiisumɔ ni wɔkɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu aya nɔ abɔ naanyo kpaakpa. Shi bei abɔ ni akpãtãko Satan kɛ edaimonioi lɛ ahiɛ kwraa nɛɛ, amɛbaaya nɔ amɛbɔ mɔdɛŋ amɛfee nibii ni baanyɛ ahã hemɔkɛyeli ni wɔyɔɔ yɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu mli lɛ mli agbɔjɔ. (Kpo. 20:1-3, 10) Mɛni baahã wɔnyɛ wɔwu wɔshi amɛ? Esa akɛ wɔya nɔ wɔwaje naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ wɔ kɛ Yehowa teŋ lɛ, ni wɔhe wɔye waa akɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu fɛɛ sumɔɔ wɔ waa, ni amɛshwɛɛɛ wɔhe kwraa. Yesu wo shi akɛ kɛ́ wɔfee nakai lɛ, Yehowa baaya nɔ ebu wɔhe, ni mɔ ko mɔ ko nyɛŋ “ashɔ̃ [wɔ] yɛ Tsɛ lɛ dɛŋ.”—Kanemɔ Yohane 10:29; Yak. 4:7.

18 Yɛ nikasemɔ ni nyiɛ sɛɛ lɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaasusu nibii enyɔ krokomɛi hu ni Satan feɔ koni efite naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ wɔ kɛ Yehowa teŋ lɛ he. Wɔbaana bɔ ni eefee bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni ebaanyɛ koni ehã mligbalamɔ aba asafo lɛ mli, kɛ bɔ ni ekudɔɔ mɛi ni bɛ asafo lɛ mli koni amɛwa wɔyi lɛ. Kɛfata he lɛ, wɔbaakase bɔ ni wɔbafee wɔya nɔ wɔye Yehowa anɔkwa ekɔɔɔ he eko nɔ ni Satan baafee.
