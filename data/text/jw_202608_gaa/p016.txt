Dɔlɛ sane ji akɛ, ké wɔwieee nibii kpakpai yɛ nyɛmimɛi lɛ ahe lɛ, ehãaa mɛi kɛ amɛ aye jogbaŋŋ. Shi ja wɔhiɛ nyɛmimɛi lɛ ahe jwɛŋmɔ kpakpa dani wɔbaanyɛ wɔwie nibii kpakpai yɛ amɛhe. Mɛni baanyɛ aye abua wɔ ni wɔnyɛ wɔfee nakai? Nɔ ko ni Paulo fɔɔ feemɔ lɛ baanyɛ aye abua wɔ ni wɔfee nakai.

⁸ Paulo daa Yehowa shi daa yɛ Filemon sui kpakpai lɛ ahewɔ. (Flm. 4, 5) Efee nakai nɔŋŋ yɛ mɛi krokomɛi hu agbɛfaŋ. (1 Kor. 1:4-7; Fip. 1:3-5; Kol. 1:3, 4; 1 Tes. 1:2, 3) Eyɛ mli akɛ Paulo naa amɛfatɔi lɛ moŋ, shi ebɔ mɔdɛŋ akɛ ekɛ ejwɛŋmɔ baama amɛsui kpakpai lɛ anɔ. Ni edaa Yehowa shi daa akɛ eduro lɛ nanemɛi ni tamɔ nakai. Ké ewa kɛhã bo akɛ obaawie nibii kpakpai yɛ mɛi ahe lɛ, no lɛ kaimɔ akɛ: Ké obɔɔ mɔdɛŋ akɛ okɛ ojwɛŋmɔ baama nyɛmimɛi lɛ asui kpakpai lɛ anɔ, ni ofɔɔ sɔlemɔ ni Yehowa ajɔɔ amɛ lɛ, ewaŋ kɛhãŋ bo akɛ obaawie nibii kpakpai yɛ amɛhe.

⁹ Eyɛ mli akɛ no mli lɛ Paulo kɛ naagbai miikpe moŋ, shi ekɛ Filemon wie kɛ naajɔlɛ. Ani okaiɔ be ko ni etɔ bo waa loo ohao ni no hã okɛ mɔ ko wie bɔ ni esaaa? Eji anɔkwale akɛ, ké wɔkɛ naagbai miikpe lɛ, ewaaa akɛ mɛi ahe baawo wɔmli la ni wɔkɛ amɛ awie bɔ ni esaaa. Enɛ hewɔ bɔ ni Paulo kɛ Filemon ye ehã lɛ sa kadimɔ waa lɛ. Be ni eŋmaa Filemon wolo lɛ, no mli lɛ “awo lɛ tsuŋ” yɛ shĩa ni lɛ diɛŋtsɛ eyahai lɛ mli yɛ Roma. (Flm. 1) Shi afii fioo komɛi dani abaawo lɛ tsuŋ nɛɛ po lɛ, mɛi komɛi yi lɛ, ni no sɛɛ lɛ amɔ lɛ ni awo lɛ tsuŋ be ni efeko efɔŋ ko. Awo lɛ tsuŋ yɛ Yerusalem kɛ Kaisarea. No sɛɛ lɛ, afã ni akɛ lɛ aya Roma shi be ni akɛ lɛ yaa lɛ, meele ni amɛyɔɔ mli lɛ yasha shi. Shi Paulo hãaa shihilɛi srɔtoi ni etsɔ mli nɛɛ ahã ekɛ Filemon awie bɔ ni esaaa. Moŋ lɛ, ekɛ lɛ wie kɛ bulɛ kɛ naajɔlɛ. Mɛni wɔkaseɔ yɛ bɔ ni ekɛ Filemon ye ehã lɛ mli? Ké etɔ wɔ, wɔhao, loo wɔkɛ naagba ko miikpe po lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔbɔ mɔdɛŋ ni wɔkɛ nyɛmimɛi lɛ aye kɛ naajɔlɛ koni toiŋjɔlɛ aya nɔ ahi asafo lɛ mli. Ni ké toiŋjɔlɛ yɛ asafo lɛ mli lɛ, no baahã wɔnyɛ wɔtsu wɔnaagbai lɛ ahe nii.
