ANI MANYƐ MANÁ MIISHƐƐ YƐ KOO HAYII MLI?
<<<P>>>
Miku misɛɛ mitee French Guiana ni ji he ni atsɔɔ mi asraafoi anitsumɔ lɛ yɛ lɛ, ni miyahi akrowa ko ni etse ehe banee mli yɛ koo hayii ko mli. Miyasu-su akɛ kɛ́ miyahi koo mli, mishã wii, ni mitse mihe kɛje nibii gbohii ni mina yɛ Paris lɛ ahe lɛ, maná miishɛɛ. Shi be ko sɛɛ lɛ, miku misɛɛ kɛtee miyɛlɛ ŋmɔ lɛ mli ekoŋŋ. Mitsuɔ nii kɛjɛɔ Ju kɛyashiɔ So-haa, kɛkɛ lɛ, yɛ otsi lɛ naagbee lɛ, miyashwɛ mihiɛ waa.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ nakai beiaŋ lɛ, mɔ ko ke mi Biblia. Mibɔi kanemɔ kɛsusumɔ lɛ akɛ ebaaye ebua mi ni matsɔ gbɔmɔ kpakpa. Shi minuuu nɔ ni mikaneɔ lɛ shishi.
<<<P>>>
Miná nanemɛi komɛi ni amɛ hu amɛmiitao amɛ-le nɔ hewɔ ni adesai yɔɔ shihilɛ mli. Amɛyahi he ko yɛ koo lɛ mli ni amɛtsɛɔ jɛmɛ “Ekomefeemɔ.” Amɛ-tsɛ mi ni mikɛ amɛ abahi jɛmɛ.
<<<P>>>
Mikɛ amɛ yahi koo lɛ mli, ni minya shihilɛ yɛ jɛmɛ lɛ he ejaakɛ jɛmɛ shihilɛ bɛ haŋtsii ni naa-gbai fioo ko pɛ wɔná. Wɔfee nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni wɔsumɔɔ, ni mɔ ko bɛ jɛmɛ ni baakɛɛ wɔ nɔ ni esa akɛ wɔfee. Wɔteɔ shi be ni hulu eje, ni wɔyawɔɔ be ni enyɔ shi. Wɔdu wɔ diɛŋtsɛ wɔniyenii, wɔtswaa lalai, ni wɔshãa wii. Wɔfiteee koo ni wɔyɔɔ mli lɛ, ni wɔyeɔ wɔbuaa wɔhe. Wɔkpeee loo ejaakɛ wɔsumɔɔɔ ni wɔgbeɔ kooloi ni yɔɔ koo lɛ mli lɛ. Wɔbuuu mlai ni maŋ lɛ woɔ lɛ, ni bei komɛi lɛ, wɔkuɔ mli kɛ́ wɔsu-suɔ akɛ no hi kɛhã wɔ ni eyeŋ mɔ ko awui.
<<<P>>>
Wɔná kulɛ, mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni yɔɔ je lɛ mli lɛ hiɔ shi tamɔ bɔ ni wɔhiɔ shi lɛ. Be ni wɔyɔɔ koo lɛ mli lɛ, wɔhãaa mɔ ko anyiɛ wɔhiɛ, ni wɔkɛ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yeɔ jogbaŋŋ ekɔɔɔ he eko he ni mɔ lɛ jɛ. Shi be ko sɛɛ lɛ, mibayɔse akɛ, wɔteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ fɔɔ lɛ pɛ enɔ mli, ni wɔtɔ̃ɔ, ni wɔkɛ naagbai ni mɛi ni yɔɔ hei krokomɛi lɛ kɛkpeɔ lɛ nɔŋŋ kpeɔ. Minanemɛi ni mi-kɛyɔɔ koo lɛ mli lɛ ateŋ mɛi komɛi hi jɛmɛ gbii fioo komɛi; amɛteŋ mɛi komɛi hu hi shi otsii loo nyɔji komɛi. Mi lɛ, mihi jɛmɛ nɔ ni fa fe afii enyɔ.
<<<P>>>
Be ni miyɔɔ koo lɛ mli, mibayɔse akɛ, nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni he hiaa wɔ yɛ shihilɛ mli lɛ ebu yɛ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ. Gbi ko lɛ, mikpɛ miyiŋ akɛ makane wui ni yɔɔ akpakpa mli lɛ. Be ni mikaneɔ lɛ, mina akɛ, efa waa, ni no hewɔ lɛ, mikpa kanemɔ. Mibi mihe akɛ,

<<<P>>>
B. Mi nɛ; be ni miyɔɔ koo lɛ mli lɛ
<<<P>>>
