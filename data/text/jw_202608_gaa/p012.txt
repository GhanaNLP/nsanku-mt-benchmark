Kɛ́ wɔtee nɔ wɔsumɔ wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ ni wɔhã ekomefeemɔ hi wɔteŋ lɛ, wɔbaaye Satan kɛ daimonioi lɛ anɔ kunim
(Kwemɔ kuku 16)
<<<P>>>
kɛhã wɔ loo amɛtsĩ wɔnitsumɔ lɛ naa po lɛ, loloolo lɛ, esa akɛ wɔbu amɛ. Mɛni hewɔ? Ejaakɛ Nyɔŋmɔ Wiemɔ lɛ biɔ ni wɔfee nakai. (Rom. 13:1, 2) Wɔnyɛŋ wɔkɛɛ akɛ Satan hɔ mlai ni hãa wɔhe fiɔ wɔ ni nɔyelɔi lɛ woɔ lɛ eko fɛɛ eko sɛɛ. Fɛɛ sɛɛ lɛ, ebaasumɔ ni wɔmli afu nɔyelɔi lɛ ni wɔwie wɔshi amɛ loo wɔte shi wɔwo amɛ po. Ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, kɛ́ nɔyelɔi lɛ wo mlai ni maŋbii lɛ kɛ amɛ kpãaa gbee lɛ, maŋbii lɛ ateŋ mɛi babaoo tswaa grasin tsɛnsi amɛwoɔ nɔyelɔi lɛ. Shi wɔ akɛ Kristofoi lɛ, nifeemɔi ni tamɔ nakai lɛ esaaa wɔ! (Abɛi 24:21, 22, shn.; Jaj. 8:2, 3) Kɛ́ wɔshwe akɛ atsake nɔyeli ko, ni wɔkɛ wɔhiɛ efɔ̃ɔ Nyɔŋmɔ Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ nɔ lɛ, belɛ, wɔmiifee Satan suɔmɔnaa nii ji no.—Yoh. 15:19.

14 Mɛi komɛi ni be ko ni eho lɛ amɛte shi amɛwo Yehowa Odasefoi lɛ ebatsɔmɔ Odasefoi ni yɔɔ ekãa waa. (1 Tim. 1:12, 13) No hewɔ lɛ, ‘kɛ́ mɛi jɛ bo ni amɛwa oyi ni amɛmale amɛwie ohe efɔŋ fɛɛ efɔŋ lɛ,’ kaahã ohiɛ kpa nɔ akɛ amɛbaanyɛ amɛtsake ni amɛbasɔmɔ Yehowa. (Mat. 5:11) Sɔlemɔ koni nɔyelɔi lɛ kɛwɔ aye jogbaŋŋ ni amɛhã wɔya nɔ wɔsɔmɔ Yehowa yɛ toiŋjɔlɛ mli. (1 Tim. 2:1, 2) Sɔlemɔ ohã mɛi krokomɛi hu ni baanyɛ ate ashi amɛwo bo tamɔ oweku lɛ mli bii lɛ, mɛi ni okɛtsuɔ nii loo mɛi ni okɛyaa skul lɛ nɛkɛ. Bi Yehowa ni eye ebua bo ni ona nɔ ni obaanyɛ ofee koni amɛtsake amɛsusumɔ. Ni esa akɛ oto otsui shi waa hu. Yoo ko te shi ewo ewu ni ji Odasefonyo lɛ afii pii, shi amrɔ nɛɛ ebatsɔ Odasefonyo. Ewie akɛ, “Miwu to etsui shi waa ehã mi. Yɛ anɔkwale mli lɛ, no ni ye ebua mi. No hewɔ lɛ, tsuishitoo he hiaa waa.”
