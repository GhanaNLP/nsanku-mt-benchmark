Wɔbaaya Nɔ Wɔfi Shi Shiŋŋ
<<<P>>>
NƆ NI WƆBAAKASE
Esa akɛ wɔhã ehi wɔjwɛŋmɔ mli be fɛɛ be akɛ, Satan kɛ edaimonioi lɛ wɔkɛwuo lɛ shi jeee wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ.
<<<P>>>
Obaanyɛ Oye Satan Kɛ Daimonioi Lɛ Anɔ Kunim!
<<<P>>>
“Jeee heloo kɛ lá wɔkɛnɔɔ lɛ, shi moŋ wɔkɛ . . . mumɔi fɔji ni yɔɔ ŋwɛiniiaŋ lɛ.”—EFE. 6:12.
<<<P>>>
YEHOWA tsuji ji wɔ, no hewɔ lɛ, wɔkɛ wɔhe wooo tai ni awuɔ yɛ je lɛ mli lɛ amli. (Yes. 2:4; Yoh. 18:36) Shi Biblia lɛ wie akɛ, esa akɛ wɔteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ awu ashi Satan kɛ edaimonioi lɛ. (Kanemɔ Efesobii 6:11, 12.) Mumɔi fɔji nɛɛ yɛ hewalɛ kwraa fe wɔ. No hewɔ lɛ, te wɔbaafee tɛŋŋ wɔye amɛnɔ kunim lɛ? Ja wɔhã Yehowa eye ebua wɔ dani wɔbaanyɛ wɔye amɛnɔ kunim. Shi ehe hiaa hu ni wɔle nibii ni Satan kɛ edaimonioi lɛ feɔ koni no ahã wɔ kɛ wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ ahemɔkɛyeli lɛ mli agbɔjɔ.
<<<P>>>
2 Yɛ nikasemɔ ni tsɔ hiɛ lɛ mli lɛ, wɔbana bɔ ni Satan bɔɔ mɔdɛŋ ni efite wɔjwɛŋmɔ koni wɔkahe Yehowa kɛ Yesu wɔye. Yɛ nikasemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaasusu nibii enyɔ krokomɛi hu ni Satan feɔ koni no ahã wɔkpa Yehowa sɔɔmɔ lɛ ahe. Klɛŋklɛŋ lɛ, Satan bɔɔ mɔdɛŋ koni ehã mligbalamɔ aba asafo lɛ mli. Agbɛnɛ hu, akɛni eesumɔ ni wɔshe gbeyei ni wɔjie wɔjwɛŋmɔ kɛje shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ nɔ koni fiofio lɛ wɔkpa shiɛmɔ lɛ hewɔ lɛ, ekudɔɔ mɛi ni bɛ asafo lɛ mli lɛ koni amɛwa wɔyi.
<<<P>>>
SATAN MIIBƆ MƆDƐŊ KONI EHÃ MLIGBALAMƆ ABA WƆTEŊ
<<<P>>>
3 Satan bɔɔ mɔdɛŋ koni ehã naagbai aba wɔ kɛ wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ ateŋ koni no ahã mligbalamɔ aba asafo lɛ mli. Eetao ni
<<<P>>>
1. Namɛi esa akɛ wɔteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ awu ashi? (Efesobii 6:11, 12)
2. Mɛni he wɔbaasusu yɛ nikasemɔ nɛɛ mli?
3. Mɛni Satan miitao ni wɔfee?
