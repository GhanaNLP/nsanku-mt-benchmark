11 Be ni Yesu, ni ji Mesia lɛ, ba shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ lɛ, mɛi pii kɛ lɛ yeee jogbaŋŋ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, be ni eje shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ shishi lɛ, ewekumɛi komɛi kɛɛ ejwɛŋmɔ tsuuu nii jogbaŋŋ. (Mar. 3:21) Jamɔŋ hiɛnyielɔi lɛ ka shii abɔ akɛ amɛbaahã ekpa shiɛmɔ, ni amɛkɛ sane fɔ̃ enɔ po akɛ daimonioi aŋɔɔ enáa ehewalɛ lɛ kɛjɛɔ. (Luka 11:15, 16) Ebɔfoi lɛ ateŋ mɔ kome ni ekɛ lɛ yaa kɛbaa lɛ jie lɛ ehã. (Mat. 26:14-16) Osɔfoi hiɛnaanɔbii lɛ kɛ nɔyelɔi lɛ fee ekome koni agbe lɛ. (Mar. 15:1, 13-15) Shi Yesu hãaa nibii fɔji ni afee lɛ lɛ ahã ekpa nitsumɔ ni Yehowa kɛwo edɛŋ lɛ tsumɔ. Ni efeee mɛi ni ye lɛ saji fɔji lɛ eko kɛtseee. Be ni amɔ Yesu lɛ, bɔfo Petro ka akɛ ebaafã Yesu he. Anɔkwa, efo osɔfonukpa lɛ nyɔŋ ko toi po. Shi Yesu eyanyaaa nɔ ni Petro fee lɛ he kwraa, ni etsa nuu lɛ toi ehã lɛ.
<<<P>>>
(Luka 22:50, 51) Yɛ shihilɛi nɛɛ fɛɛ amli lɛ, Yesu ebɔɔɔ mɔdɛŋ akɛ ebaafee amɛ eko kɛtse. Moŋ lɛ, etee nɔ etsu nitsumɔ ni Yehowa ehã lɛ akɛ etsu lɛ.
<<<P>>>
12 Tamɔ bɔ ni eba lɛ yɛ Yesu gbɛfaŋ lɛ, wɔ hu wɔkɛ naagbai ni baanyɛ ahã wɔkpa Yehowa sɔɔmɔ lɛ kpeɔ. Satan miibɔ mɔdɛŋ koni ehã wɔkpa shiɛmɔ. Wɔwekumɛi kɛ mɛi krokomɛi ni sɔmɔɔɔ Yehowa lɛ baanyɛ ate shi awo wɔ loo amɛwa wɔyi po. Fɛɛ sɛɛ lɛ, Yesu fã wɔ koni wɔsumɔ mɛi ni waa wɔyi lɛ po. (Kanemɔ Mateo 5:43, 44.) Mɛni baatsɔɔ akɛ wɔmiiye Yesu famɔ lɛ nɔ? Esaaa akɛ wɔnyɛɔ mɛi ni waa wɔyi lɛ loo wɔfeɔ amɛ eko kɛtseɔ po. Moŋ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔfee bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni wɔbaanyɛ ni wɔkɛ mɛi fɛɛ, ni mɛi ni waa wɔyi lɛ po fata he lɛ, aye kɛ naajɔlɛ, wɔto wɔtsui shi wɔhã amɛ, ni wɔbu amɛ.—1 Kor. 13:4.
