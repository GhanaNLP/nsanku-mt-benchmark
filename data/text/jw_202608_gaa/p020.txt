Eji Hegbɛ Kpele Ni Wɔná Akɛ ‘Wɔkɛ Sɔɔmɔ Krɔŋŋ Baahã Yehowa Ni Wɔsheee Gbeyei’
<<<P>>>
LALA 62
Lala Hee Lɛ
<<<P>>>
NƆ NI WƆBAAKASE
Ebaaye ebua wɔ ni wɔhiɛ asɔ nikeenii ni ji yiwalahe-remɔ lɛ, hegbɛ ni wɔná akɛ wɔbaasɔmɔ Yehowa lɛ, kɛ nitsumɔ ni ekɛwo wɔdɛŋ akɛ wɔshiɛ lɛ.
<<<P>>>
“Etee yiwalaheremɔ akolonto shi . . . koni . . . ehã wɔ hegbɛ ni wɔkɛ sɔɔmɔ krɔŋŋ ahã lɛ ni wɔsheee gbeyei.”—LUKA 1:69, 74.
<<<P>>>
NUU ko hi blema Israel ni atsɛɔ lɛ Zakaria, ni eŋa gbɛi ji Eli-zabet. Amɛhi shi afii pii ni amɛfɔɔɔ. Shi be ko be ni amɛda-ra waa lɛ, Yehowa fee naakpɛɛ nii ko ehã amɛ. Ehã amɛfɔ bi-nuu ni amɛwo lɛ Yohane. Be ni amɛmaŋbii lɛ nu nɔ ni eba lɛ, amɛbi sane akɛ: “Mɛɛ gbɔmɔ nɛkɛ gbekɛ nɛɛ baatsɔ?” (Luka 1:57-66) Yehowa hã Zakaria hã sanebimɔ nɛɛ hetoo. Zaka-ria tsɔɔ mli akɛ, etsɛŋ Mesia ni amɛkɛ afii pii ekpa lɛ gbɛ lɛ baaba, shi dani ebaaba lɛ, Yohane baaye abua mɛi ni amɛ-tsake amɛtsui ni amɛnyiɛ Mesia lɛ sɛɛ. (Amos 3:7; Luka 1:67-79) Shi Zakaria ewieee nɔ ni ebinuu Yohane baafee lɛ pɛ he.* Yɛ nikasemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaana akɛ nɔ ni Zakaria wie lɛ kɔɔ nɔ ni baaba wɔsɛɛ hu he, ni akɛ nɔ ni ewie lɛ kɔɔ wɔhe ŋmɛnɛ. Yɛ nɔ ni Zakaria wie lɛ mli lɛ, wɔnaa nibii etɛ komɛi ni esa akɛ wɔhiɛ asɔ amɛ: (1) nikeenii ni ji yiwalaheremɔ lɛ, (2) hegbɛ ni wɔná akɛ wɔbaasɔmɔ Yehowa lɛ, kɛ (3) nitsumɔ ni akɛwo wɔdɛŋ akɛ wɔshiɛ lɛ.
