11 Ké ofee nɔ ko loo owie nɔ ko ni egba mɔ ko naa lɛ, yaa ni oyasaa okɛ lɛ teŋ. (Mat. 5:23-25) Ekolɛ onáŋ enɛ feemɔ mlɛo, shi ehi akɛ nyɛbaasaa nyɛteŋ, moŋ fe ni nyɛteŋ baafite. Ké nyɛkpe lɛ, kpa lɛ fai, shi kaakwa ofee nakai. Bo nɔ ni ebaawie lɛ toi, ni obɔ mɔdɛŋ akɛ obaanu nɔ hewɔ ni sane lɛ egba enaa lɛ shishi. Kpɛlɛmɔ otɔmɔ nɔ. Kaajie saji anaa ni okɛ shwamɔ akafɔ̃ mɛi krokomɛi anɔ yɛ nɔ ni ofee lɛ he. Kɛjeee nakai lɛ, sɛɛnamɔ baŋ ofaikpamɔ lɛ he, béi lɛ sɛɛ efoŋ, ni emli baanyɛ awo wu po. Ebɛ mlɛo akɛ wɔbaakpa fai, shi ké wɔbaa wɔhe shi lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔfee nakai. Ni bei pii lɛ, no ji gbɛ ni ké wɔtsɔ nɔ lɛ, ebaahã wɔsaa wɔ kɛ mɛi ateŋ oya.—Rom. 14:19.

12 Filemon sane lɛ hãa wɔnaa bɔ ni wɔbaafee wɔbaa toiŋjɔlɛ yi ké mɔ ko tɔ̃ wɔnɔ. Ani File-mon kɛ Onesimo he ke lɛ ni ehere lɛ hiɛmɛɛ? Biblia lɛ etsɔɔɔ. Shi Paulo ná nɔmimaa akɛ Fi-lemon baafee nakai. (Flm. 17, 21) Paulo le akɛ sui ni Filemon yɔɔ lɛ hewɔ lɛ, ewaŋ kɛhãŋ lɛ akɛ ekɛ Onesimo he baake lɛ. Mɛni ji sui nɛɛ ekomɛi?
<<<P>>>
13 Filemon sumɔ nyɛmimɛi lɛ. (Flm. 5) File-mon gbɛ́i lɛ shishi ji “mɔ ni sumɔɔ mɔ,” ni egbɛ́i lɛ sa lɛ. Mɛni hewɔ wɔkɛɔ nakai? Ale lɛ akɛ mɔ ko ni sumɔɔ mɛi waa, ni su nɛɛ ji nɔ ni baaye abua lɛ ni enyɛ ekɛ Onesimo he ake lɛ. Be ni Paulo ŋma wolo eyahã Kolose asafo lɛ,
