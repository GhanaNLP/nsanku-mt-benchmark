Kɛ́ wɔjweŋ nibii ni Yehowa tsɔɔ wɔ yɛ Biblia lɛ mli lɛ nɔ jogbaŋŋ lɛ,
wɔkɛ lɛ baabɔ naanyo waa, ni no baahã wɔhe lɛ wɔye
(Kwemɔ kuku 14)
<<<P>>>
sane ko ni ji amale loo ejeee anɔkwalei fɛɛ ni kɔɔ sane lɛ he. Kɛ́ wɔtee nɔ wɔkane loo wɔbo saji ni tamɔ nakai toi lɛ, wɔbaahe nibii ni bɛ mli wɔye. Shi kɛ́ wɔkase Biblia lɛ kɛ asafo lɛ woji kɛ vidioi lɛ, wɔyaa asafoŋ kpeei daa, ni wɔkɛ nyɛmimɛi ni hemɔkɛyeli mli wa bɔ lɛ, ebaawa akɛ abaalaka wɔ. Kɛ́ wɔle Biblia lɛ mli anɔkwalei lɛ jogbaŋŋ lɛ, kɛ́ wɔna amale lɛ wɔbaana, ni wɔŋmɛŋ Satan gbɛ ni ehã wɔhemɔkɛyeli lɛ mli agbɔjɔ.—Abɛi 13:20; Bɔf. 17:11.

15 Kɛ́ wɔtee nɔ wɔkase Biblia lɛ ni wɔbale Yehowa kɛ Yesu jogbaŋŋ lɛ, no baahã wɔna faŋŋ
<<<P>>>
15. Mɛni kɛ́ wɔfee lɛ, ebaahã wɔna faŋŋ akɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu miisumɔ ni wɔná miishɛɛ?
<<<P>>>
akɛ amɛmiisumɔ ni wɔná miishɛɛ. (Abɛi 22:19) Agba hii kɛ yei ni he Yehowa kɛ Yesu amɛye lɛ ahe saji yɛ Biblia lɛ mli. Kɛ́ wɔjweŋ bɔ ni Yehowa kɛ Yesu kwɛ amɛ lɛ nɔ jogbaŋŋ lɛ, wɔ hu wɔbaahe Yehowa kɛ Yesu wɔye waa. Yehowa kɛ Yesu ewie saji komɛi ni shɛjeɔ mɔ mii waa, ni aŋmala saji nɛɛ yɛ Biblia lɛ mli. Kɛ́ wɔkane saji nɛɛ ni wɔjweŋ nɔ lɛ, wɔbaana akɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu sumɔɔ wɔ waa. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, Yehowa wie akɛ esumɔɔ etsuji lɛ waa fe bɔ ni yoo ni miikwɛ ebi abifao lɛ sumɔɔ ebi lɛ. (Yes. 49:15) Ewie hu akɛ, eshwɛɛɛ mɛi ni sumɔɔ egbɛ́i lɛ ahe kwraa. (Mal. 3:16, 17) Kɛ́ wɔkane Biblia lɛ, wɔnaa bɔ ni Yesu nuɔ he
