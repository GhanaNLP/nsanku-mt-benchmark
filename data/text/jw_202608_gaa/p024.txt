(Kwemɔ kuku 16)
<<<P>>>
13 Yohane fee bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni ebaanyɛ koni ehã mɛi fɛɛ ale akɛ Yesu ji Mesia lɛ, ni ehi waa akɛ efee nakai. Mɛni hewɔ? Zakaria kɛ Mesia lɛ baa lɛ to “jetsɛremɔ” he. Enɛ tsɔɔ akɛ, Yesu baa lɛ baatsake mɛi ashihilɛ kwraa. Enɛ ji sane kpakpa diɛŋtsɛ, ni ehe miihia ni ashiɛ sane nɛɛ ahã mɛi fɛɛ. Shi Yohane kome too nyɛŋ ashiɛ sane nɛɛ ehã mɛi fɛɛ. Kɛ́ nakai lɛ, namɔ hu baashiɛ sane kpakpa nɛɛ?
<<<P>>>
14 Yesu shiɛ sane kpakpa nɛɛ waa. Lɛ ji mɔ ni Zakaria gba yɛ ehe akɛ ebaahã “la akpɛ ahã mɛi ni tara duŋ kɛ gbele hɔɔŋ mli lɛ.” Yesu sumɔɔ waa ni eye ebua mɛi ni akɛ amɛ yeee jogbaŋŋ lɛ. Tamɔ bɔ ni agba lɛ, Yesu hã “la kpɛ” ehã amɛ kɛtsɔ “Yehowa mlihilɛ afi lɛ” he sane ni eshiɛ lɛ nɔ. (Kanemɔ Yesaia 61:1, 2; Luka 4:16-21) Eshiɛ kɛ ekãa akɛ: “Ŋwɛi Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ eshɛ shi etã.” Fɛɛ sɛɛ lɛ, Yesu le akɛ, ehe miihia ni aná mɛi pii ni baashiɛ sane kpakpa nɛɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, etsɔse ekaselɔi lɛ koni amɛ hu amɛnyɛ amɛshiɛ eko.—Mat. 4:16, 17; 10:7; 28:19, 20.
<<<P>>>
15 Ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, wɔ akɛ Kristo lɛ sɛɛnyiɛlɔi lɛ, wɔkɛ hegbɛ ni wɔná akɛ wɔbaaye wɔbua mɛi ni amɛna akɛ abaanyɛ ahere amɛ lɛ shwɛɛɛ kwraa. Yɛ anɔkwale mli lɛ, Yesu kpɔmɔ afɔle lɛ kɛ Nyɔŋmɔ Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ he sane ni wɔbaashiɛ lɛ he ebahia waa fe bei fɛɛ ni eho. Mɛni hewɔ wɔkɛɔ nakai? Nɔ ni etsɛŋ ni ebaaba lɛ hewɔ. Etsɛŋ, “amanehulu kpeteŋkpele ni anako enɔ dã kɛjɛ je lɛ shishijee aahu kɛbashi amrɔ nɛɛ” baaba. (Mat. 24:21) Yɛ nakai beiaŋ lɛ, ekolɛ mɛi babaoo baabo yiwalaheremɔ he sane ni wɔshiɛɔ lɛ toi, ni amɛbaabɔi Yehowa jamɔ ejaakɛ amɛbaa-
