<<<P>>>
Tso Ko Ni Hiɛ Tsẽɔ Mra
<<<P>>>
Atsĩ tsei aaafee 30 atã yɛ Biblia lɛ mli. Almond tso lɛ fata tsei nɛɛ ahe. Efata tsei ni gbáa afoforo mra yɛ Israel kɛ fẽibe lɛ miihe aba naagbee lɛ ahe. Yɛ January nyɔɔŋ lɛ naa-gbeegbɛ lɛ, anaa ni egbá afoforo fɛfɛo. Efɔfɔii lɛ akɔla lɛ eko ji pinki loo eyɛŋ. Israelbii lɛ tsɛɔ almond tso lɛ yɛ amɛwiemɔ mli akɛ, “mɔ ni hiɛ tsẽɔ mra.” Ani nɔ ko yɛ ni mɔ ni bɔ tso nɛɛ feɔ ni tamɔ bɔ ni tso nɛɛ gbáa afoforo mra lɛ?
<<<P>>>
Yɛ Yeremia 7:25 lɛ shishigbɛ niŋmaa lɛ mli lɛ, awie akɛ Yehowa teɔ shi leebi maŋkpa ni etsuɔ egbálɔi lɛ. March 15, 2011, Buu-Mɔɔ lɛ wie akɛ: “Taakɛ almond tso ‘hiɛ tsẽɔ’ mra lɛ, nakai nɔŋŋ Yehowa ‘teɔ shi leebi maŋkpa’ yɛ mfonirifeemɔŋ [koni eyabɔ] ewebii lɛ kɔkɔ yɛ nɔ ni jɛɔ toigbele mli kɛbaa lɛ he. . . . Ejɔŋ ehe—‘ebaaya nɔ eshi kpe’—kɛyashi egbalɛ wiemɔ lɛ baaba mli.”—Yer. 1:11, 12.
<<<P>>>
Akɛ bɔ ni almond tso lɛ gbáa afoforo yɛŋ lɛ hu toɔ gbɔlɛ beiaŋ waŋ he yɛ Biblia lɛ mli. (Jaj. 12:5) Akɛni almond fɔfɔii lɛ yɛ fɛo hewɔ lɛ, be ni Bezalel feɔ sɔlemɔ buu lɛ kanetso lɛ, efee kanetso lɛ niji lɛ he kɔɔpoi lɛ tamɔ almond fɔfɔii.—2 Mo. 37:17-20.
<<<P>>>
Ahɔɔɔ wolo nɛɛ. Efata jeŋ muu fɛɛ Biblia mli tsɔsemɔ nitsumɔ ko ni ajɛɔ suɔmɔ mli atsuɔ onia kɛfiɔ sɛɛ lɛ he. Kɛ́ ootao otsu onia lɛ, ofainɛ yaa donate.jw.org.
