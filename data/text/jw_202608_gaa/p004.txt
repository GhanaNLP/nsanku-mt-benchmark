Satan je lɛ mli eyi kɛ saji ni baahã wɔnu he akɛ esaaa akɛ wɔheɔ nɔ ni Yehowa tsɔɔ wɔ lɛ wɔyeɔ
(Kwɛmɔ kuku 11)
<<<P>>>
(Mat. 4:10; Yoh. 4:23) Mɛi nɛɛ ateŋ mɛi pii heɔ nɔ ni atsɔɔ amɛ lɛ amɛyeɔ aahu akɛ, kɛ́ wɔmiitsɔɔ amɛ anɔkwale lɛ po lɛ, amɛbooo toi, ni bei komɛi po lɛ, amɛteɔ shi amɛwoɔ wɔ.
<<<P>>>
⁹ Kɛ́ okɛ mɔ ko bɔi naanyobɔɔ lɛ, nibii ni oleɔ yɛ ehe klɛŋklɛŋ kwraa lɛ ateŋ ekome ji egbéi. Satan sumɔɔɔ ni mɛi kɛ Yehowa bɔɔ naanyo kpaakpa, ni no hewɔ lɛ ehãaa amɛle Yehowa gbɛ́i po. Te efeɔ lɛ ehãa tɛŋŋ? Kɛjɛ blema tɔ̃ɔ lɛ, ehãa amale jamɔi lakaa mɛi koni amɛhiɛ akpa Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛ́i lɛ nɔ. (Kane-mɔ Yeremia 23:26, 27.) Agbɛnɛ hu, ehã ajie Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛ́i lɛ kɛje Biblia shishitsɔɔmɔi babaoo amli koni mɛi akale gbɛ́i nɛɛ ni amɛkɛtsɛ lɛ, tsɛbelɛ, akɛ gbɛ́i nɛɛ to mɔɔ ni wa ni buɔ mɔ he lɛ he. (Abɛi 18:10) Kɛfata he lɛ, ehã amale jamɔi etsɔɔ mɛi akɛ, Nyɔŋmɔ ji mɔ ni hãa wɔnaa nɔ lɛ. Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, mɛi babaoo sheɔ Nyɔŋmɔ gbeyei, ni amɛnaaa lɛ akɛ mɔ ko ni baasumɔ ni ekɛ amɛ abɔ naanyo. (Yak. 4:8) Nɔ kroko hu ni Satan feɔ ji, ebɔɔ mɔdɛŋ koni ehã Yehowa webii lɛ akpa Yehowa sɔɔmɔ.
