NƆ NI WƆBAAKASE
<<<P>>>
Satan miisumɔ ni onu he akɛ Yehowa Nyɔŋmɔ kɛ Yesu Kristo sumɔɔɔ bo, shi kaahe lɛ oye.
<<<P>>>
Hemɔ Yehowa Kɛ Yesu Oye Shi Jeee Satan
<<<P>>>
“Nyɛtsĩa Abonsam gbɛ, ni ebaajo nyɛnaa foi.”—YAK. 4:7.
<<<P>>>
KƐJƐ be ni obasumɔ Yehowa ni okpɛ oyiŋ akɛ obaasɔmɔ lɛ lɛ kɛbaa nɛɛ, Satan kɛ edaimonioi lɛ amli ewo la waa, ni amɛmiifee bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni amɛbaanyɛ koni amɛhã suɔmɔ ni oyɔɔ kɛhã Yehowa lɛ he ajɔ. Satan ná kulɛ, okpa Yehowa sɔɔmɔ. Shi ele akɛ, akɛni naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ bo kɛ Yehowa teŋ lɛ mli wa waa hewɔ lɛ, Yehowa miiye eebua bo, ni no hewɔ lɛ enine nyɛŋ ashɛ onɔ. Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, eebɔ mɔdɛŋ koni ehã suɔmɔ ni oyɔɔ kɛhã Yehowa lɛ he ajɔ bɔ ni afee ni Yehowa akpa bo yelikɛbuamɔ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, eefee bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni ebaanyɛ koni onu he akɛ Yehowa kɛ e-Bi lɛ jwɛŋŋ ohe.—2 Kor. 11:14; Yak. 1:6.
<<<P>>>
2 Naa nɔ ko ni baahã wɔnu sane nɛɛ shishi. Kɛ́ asraafoi miiwu ta lɛ, esa akɛ amɛhe amɛtatsɛmɛi lɛ amɛye. Kɛ́ amɛheee amɛtatsɛmɛi lɛ amɛyeee lɛ, amɛboŋ amɛtatsɛmɛi lɛ toi, ni no kɛ asraafoi lɛ baawo oshãra wulu mli loo ebaahã agbe amɛ po. Yɛ gbɛ ko nɔ lɛ, wɔji asraafoi ni kɛ “mumɔi fɔji ni yɔɔ ŋwei-niiaŋ lɛ” miiwu. (Efe. 6:12) Satan Abonsam le akɛ, kɛ́ hemɔkɛyeli ni wɔyɔɔ yɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu Kristo mli lɛ tã pɛ lɛ, wɔbaakpa amɛ toiboo. Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, efeɔ bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni ebaanyɛ koni ehã hemɔkɛyeli ni wɔyɔɔ yɛ amɛmli lɛ atã. Yɛ nikasemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaasusu bɔ ni efee nakai yɛ blema, kɛ bɔ ni eefee nakai lolo ŋmɛnɛ lɛ he. No sɛɛ lɛ, wɔbaasusu bɔ ni wɔbaafee wɔye enɔ kunim lɛ he. Yɛ nikasemɔ ni nyiɛ sɛɛ lɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaasusu nibii enyɔ krokomɛi ni Satan feɔ koni enine anyɛ ashɛ wɔnɔ lɛ ahe.*
<<<P>>>
* Naagbai fɛɛ ni wɔkɛkpeɔ lɛ, jeee eko fɛɛ eko ni Satan diɛŋtsɛ kɛbaa wɔnɔ. Shi ekudɔɔ mɛi ni yɔɔ je lɛ mli lɛ ni amɛsusu nii ahe ni amɛfee amɛnii tamɔ lɛ koni amɛte shi amɛwo Nyɔŋmɔ webii lɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, kɛ́ wɔkɛ naagbai miikpe, loo aawa wɔyi yɛ Yehowa ni wɔsɔmɔɔ lɛ hewɔ lɛ, belɛ, Satan ji mɔ ni wɔkɛwuɔ lɛ.—1 Kor. 2:12; 1 Yoh. 5:19.
