Bianɛ lɛ, wɔmiisɔmɔ yɛ New Caledonia akɛ kpokpaa nɔkwɛlɔ kɛ eŋa
<<<P>>>
fe fɛɛ ji nɔ ni mifeɔ mihãa Yehowa kɛ nyɛmimɛi lɛ. No mli lɛ, miitao ni wiemɔi ni mihãa lɛ ahi jogbaŋŋ ni sane ko kwraa bɛ he, no hewɔ lɛ mikɛ bei pii saa mihe. Loloolo lɛ, bei pii lɛ, miwerɛ hoɔ mihe ejaa-kɛ minyaaa nɔ ni minyɛɔ mifeɔ lɛ he. (Jaj. 7:16) Ehe bahia ni mana akɛ Yehowa ji Tsɛ ni yɔɔ suɔmɔ, enáa nɔ ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔfee lɛ he miishɛɛ, ni ekɛ wɔ etooo mɛi krokomɛi ahe loo ekpaaa emuuyeli gbɛ kɛjɛɛɛ wɔdɛŋ.
<<<P>>>
Be ni wɔyɔɔ Paris lɛ, ahã mibɔi sɔɔmɔ akɛ kpokpaa nɔkwɛlɔ yelikɛbualɔ. Minu he akɛ midako, ni mileee bɔ ni mafee matsu kpokpaa nɔkwɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ jogbaŋŋ. Shi mibana akɛ Yehowa mumɔ krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ baanyɛ ahã wɔtsu babaoo fe bɔ ni wɔ diɛŋtsɛ wɔbaanyɛ wɔtsu.
<<<P>>>
Mináaa nɔ kroko ko hu mlɛo. Kɛjɛ migbekɛ-biiashi tɔ̃ɔ lɛ, miiye hela ko ni hãa latsa tsaa mi fe nine, titri lɛ miniji. Kɛ́ mitsui fã yɛ nɔ ko hewɔ lɛ, latsa tsaa mi fe nine, tamɔ kɛ́ miihã wiemɔ yɛ mɛi pii ahiɛ yɛ kpokpaa nɔ kpee loo kpokpaa wulu nɔ kpee shishi nɛkɛ. Shi kɛ́ misɔle mihã Yehowa koni ehã mafee kpoo lɛ, ehiɔ. Hela nɛɛ eyako moŋ, shi kɛ́ mikɛ mihiɛ fɔ̃ Yehowa nɔ waa lɛ, mifeɔ kpoo ni latsa etsaaa mi pii.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ afi 2011 lɛ, Yehowa hã nɔ ko ni kã wɔtsui nɔ waa lɛ ba mli. Wɔbɔi sɔɔmɔ akɛ maŋsɛɛ sɔɔlɔi yɛ New Caledonia. Eji ŋshɔkpɔi komɛi ni yɔɔ Pacif-ic ŋshɔ lɛ nɔ. Kɛ́ mɔ ko ta ɛroplen mli kɛjɛ Paris kɛtee jɛmɛ lɛ, ebaahe ŋmɛlɛtswai 20 dani ebaashɛ jɛmɛ. Be ni wɔtee jɛmɛ lɛ, ehe bahia ni wɔkase ni-bii hei pii. Ehe bahia ni wɔkase niyenii hei yeli tamɔ duade, kookoo, kɛ yɛlɛ nɛkɛ. Ni leebi maŋkpa lɛ, wuɔi agbɛɛmɔi kɛ gbei abolɔmɔ hãaa wɔnyɛ wɔwɔ, shi be ni bei shwie mli lɛ, wɔnyɛɔ wɔwɔɔ. Nɔ kroko hu ni ehe bahia ni wɔkase ji, jeee be fɛɛ be mɛi ni yɔɔ ŋshɔkpɔi nɛɛ anɔ lɛ hereɔ mɔnɔ kɛ wiemɔi, moŋ lɛ amɛholeɔ amɛhiŋmɛii sɛɛ tsɔi anɔ.
<<<P>>>
Nɔ kroko hu ni Yehowa feɔ ehãa wɔ ji, eyeɔ ebuaa wɔ koni wɔnyɛ wɔtsake wɔhe wɔwo ni-tsumɔi ni ekɛwoɔ wɔdɛŋ lɛ amli. Be ni wɔsɔmɔ akɛ maŋsɛɛ sɔɔlɔi afii komɛi lɛ, ahã wɔyasɔmɔ yɛ Be-tel. No sɛɛ lɛ, ahã wɔtee Skul Kɛhã Maŋtsɛyeli Sanekpakpa Jajelɔi lɛ eko, ni amrɔ nɛɛ wɔmiisɔmɔ akɛ kpokpaa nɔkwɛlɔ kɛ eŋa. Yɛ nitsumɔ fɛɛ nitsu-mɔ ni Yehowa kɛwoɔ wɔdɛŋ lɛ eko fɛɛ eko mli lɛ, wɔnaa akɛ etsɔɔ emumɔ krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ nɔ eyeɔ ebuaa wɔ. Yehowa ehã mina akɛ kɛ́ mináaa nibii komɛi mlɛo po lɛ, loloolo lɛ, ebaaye ebua mi koni manyɛ matsu enitsumɔ lɛ eko. No hewɔ lɛ, be fɛɛ be lɛ, misɔleɔ kɛ hiɛdɔɔ koni eye ebua mi. Kɛbashi bianɛ po lɛ, ewa kɛhã mi akɛ mawie yɛ mɛi pii ahiɛ, shi Yehowa yeɔ ebua mi ni minyɛɔ miwoɔ nyɛmimɛi ni yɔɔ asafo lɛ mli lɛ hewalɛ, ni miyeɔ mibua amɛ hu koni amɛnyɛ amɛsɔmɔ lɛ yɛ gbɛ ni etaoɔ lɛ nɔ.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ naagbai fɛɛ ni wɔkɛkpe lɛ fɛɛ mli lɛ, Claudine ye ebua mi waa. Enáa tsui ehãa mi. Eyɛ he miishɛɛ akɛ ebaatsu nitsumɔ fɛɛ nitsumɔ ni Yehowa kɛ-baawo wɔdɛŋ lɛ, ni eshiɛɔ kɛ ekãa. Eyeee ŋkɔmɔ kɛ jeŋ dɔ yɛ ŋshɔkpɔi lɛ anɔ loo wɔfã gbɛ kɛjɛ asafo ko mli kɛtee asafo kroko mli ni ehe bahia ni wɔtsa-kɛ wɔhe wɔwo shihilɛ ni asafoŋbii lɛ yɔɔ mli lɛ mli.
<<<P>>>
Be ni mitee kpokpaa wulu nɔ kpee klɛŋklɛŋ kwraa lɛ, miná mile akɛ Yehowa, Bɔlɔ Kpele lɛ kwɛɔ ejálɔi lɛ be fɛɛ be. Akɛni mihã Yehowa sɔɔmɔ he hia mi fe nɔ fɛɛ nɔ yɛ mishihilɛ mli hewɔ lɛ, eke mi nibii kpakpai pii. Yehowa eye ebua mi, ni mina akɛ esa akɛ mabu amralofoi lɛ kɛ mɛi ni ekɛtsuɔ nii kɛtsɔɔ ewebii lɛ gbɛ lɛ. Eye ebua mi hu koni maba mihe shi. Mikɛ mɛi ni sumɔɔɔ ni amɛhiɔ shi yɛ Yehowa taomɔ nii anaa lɛ kpa bɔɔ koni manyɛ ma-sɔmɔ lɛ jogbaŋŋ, shi Yehowa ehã mi nanemɛi kpa-kpai pii. Ehã mi ŋa ni nɔ bɛ. Ni miná hiɛnɔkamɔ ni nɔ bɛ akɛ mahi shi kɛya naanɔ ni Yehowa baake mi nibii kpakpai pii.—Mar. 10:29, 30.
