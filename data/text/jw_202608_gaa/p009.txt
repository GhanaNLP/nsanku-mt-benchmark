Saul ye David he awuŋa ni eheee Yehowa eyeee dɔŋŋ,
shi Yonatan he Yehowa nɔ eye ni eye ebua David
(Kwɛmɔ kuku 4, 6)
<<<P>>>
wɔkɛ wɔhe atswa moŋ fe ni wɔbaawu wɔshi
lɛ. Mɛɛ gbɛ nɔ etsɔɔ efeɔ nakai? Mɛi ni yɔɔ
Satan je lɛ mli lɛ ateŋ mɛi babaoo ejieee suɔ-
mɔ kpo, ni bei pii po lɛ, amɛheee amɛhe
amɛyeee. Satan miisumɔ ni wɔfee wɔnii tamɔ
amɛ. Eesumɔ ni wɔwie wɔshi wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ
kɛ Yehowa asafo lɛ ni wɔnyɛ wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ
po.—Rom. 16:17.
<<<P>>>
4 Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, susumɔ bɔ ni fiofio lɛ
Maŋtsɛ Saul banyɛ David lɛ he okwɛ. Kulɛ,
esumɔɔ David sane. Ená David he miishɛɛ
ejaakɛ eji asraafonyo ni yɔɔ ekãa. (1 Sam.
18:5) Shi be ni Saul na akɛ mɛi jieɔ David yi
fe lɛ lɛ, eye David he awuŋa waa. No hã
Saul banyɛ David. No hewɔ ni eka shii abɔ
akɛ ebaagbe David lɛ. (1 Sam. 18:9-11) Eji
Saul gbe David kulɛ, Satan baanya waa. Mɛni
hewɔ wɔkɛɔ nakai? Ejaakɛ no mli lɛ, Yehowa
ehã afɔ David mu akɛ mɔ ni baaye Israel nɔ
maŋtsɛ yɛ Saul sɛɛ. (1 Sam. 16:13) Eji Yeho-
wa yiŋtoo nɛɛ baaa mli kulɛ, Satan tsui baa-
nyɔ emli waa. No hewɔ lɛ, be ni Satan naa
Saul ni eefee bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni eenyɛ koni egbe
David, mɔ ni Yehowa ehala koni eye maŋtsɛ
lɛ, ená miishɛɛ waa.
<<<P>>>
5 Satan le akɛ kɛ́ wɔhã ekomefeemɔ hi wɔ-
teŋ lɛ, Yehowa baaye abua wɔ ni ejɔɔ wɔ.
(Lala 133:1) Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, eesumɔ ni naagbai
aba asafo lɛ mli. Kɛ́ wɔye wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ ahe
awuŋa ni wɔhiɛ kɔ̃ amɛnɔ tamɔ bɔ ni Saul fee
yɛ David he lɛ, Satan baatsɔ no nɔ ehã wɔkɛ
wɔhe atswa. Satan le akɛ kɛ́ wɔye wɔnyɛmi-
mɛi lɛ ahe awuŋa lɛ, ebaawa akɛ wɔbaajie
suɔmɔ kpo wɔtsɔɔ amɛ. (Mar. 12:30, 31) Kɛ́
eba lɛ nakai lɛ, wɔjamɔ lɛ esaŋ Yehowa hiɛ.
Yakobo bɔ wɔ kɔkɔ kɛ hiɛdɔɔ yɛ bɔ ni eyɔɔ
oshãra waa akɛ wɔbaaye wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ ahe
awuŋa lɛ he.—Kanemɔ Yakobo 3:14-16.
