‘Mɛni hewɔ nibii ni he baahia wɔ yɛ shihilɛ mli lɛ ebu waa nakai lɛ?’ No hewɔ lɛ, mikɛɛ mihe akɛ, ‘Shikpɔŋ lɛ tamɔ shĩa ko ni asaa mli jogbaŋŋ ni akɛ nibii fɛɛ ni he baahia lɛ ewo mli koni mɔ ko abahi mli.’ Mibana akɛ nibii ni yɔɔ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ lɛ diɛŋtsɛ baaa shihilɛ mli ni mɔ ko hɔɔɔ sɛɛ. Shi namɔ ni fee nibii fɛɛ ni yɔɔ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ lɛ? Mɔ ko yɛ ni bɔ nibii fɛɛ. Shi mileee mɔ nɛɛ he nɔ ko nɔ ko.
<<<P>>>
Mitee nɔ mikane Biblia lɛ. Shi bianɛ lɛ, mikane koni mana nɔ ni Nyɔŋmɔ sumɔɔ ni mafee. Mibɔi sɔlemɔ, ni mifeɔ krɔs okadi lɛ ni Katolikbii lɛ feɔ lɛ, shi mileee nɔ hewɔ ni amefeɔ nakai okadi lɛ. Mibi mihe hu akɛ, ‘ani eeenyɛ efee akɛ atsɛɔ mɔ ni bɔ nibii fɛɛ lɛ akɛ Yah, ejaakɛ eji gbɛ́i ni minu yɛ la-lai ni wɔlá be ni wɔyɔɔ koo lɛ mli lɛ ekomɛi amli.’

MIBALE MI-TSƐ NI FEƆ EJURƆ WAA LƐ JOGBAŊŊ
<<<P>>>
Bianɛ lɛ, kɛ mijwɛŋ be ni mibɔi sɔlemɔ lɛ he lɛ, minuɔ he akɛ mi-Bɔlɔ lɛ na akɛ miisumɔ ni male lɛ jogbaŋŋ. Be ko lɛ, mɛi ni mikɛhi koo lɛ mli lɛ ateŋ mɔ kome kɛ Odasefoi komɛi kpe ni amɛhã lɛ wolo ni ji Obaanyɛ Ohi Shi Kɛya Naanɔ Yɛ Paradeiso Yɛ Shikpɔŋ Nɔ lɛ eko.* Be ni mikane wolo nɛɛ, minaa kpɛ mihe waa yɛ nɔ ni mikase yɛ mli lɛ hewɔ! Minya bɔ ni agbala mli akɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔhi shi kɛya
<<<P>>>
* Yehowa Odasefoi ni fee, shi akalaaa dɔŋŋ.
<<<P>>>
naanɔ yɛ shikpɔŋ ni etsɔ paradeiso nɔ lɛ he. Nɔ ni mishweɔ ji, ni mahi shi yɛ he ko ni nɔ fɛɛ nɔ miiya nɔ jogbaŋŋ, ni toiŋjɔlɛ yɛ mi kɛ mɛi ni misumɔɔ amɛsane lɛ ateŋ ni wɔgboiii. Mibasumɔ akɛ mikɛ Bɔlɔ ni feɔ ejurɔ waa ni miisumɔ ni wɔná shihilɛ ni nɔ bɛ lɛ abɔ naanyo.
<<<P>>>
Mina akɛ, esa akɛ matsake bɔ ni mibaa mijeŋ mihãa lɛ, shi mina hu akɛ ebaabi ni aye abua mi. No hewɔ lɛ, mibi minaanyo kroko ko ni ehã mikɛ Odasefonyo maŋsɛɛ sɔɔlɔ ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Michel, ni kɛ lɛ kaseɔ nii lɛ akpe.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ gbi ni mikɛ Michel kpe lɛ nɔŋŋ nɔ lɛ, efɔ̃ mini-ne ni miba kpokpaa wulu nɔ kpee ko ni abaafee yɛ Matoury lɛ. Maŋ nɛɛ bɛŋkɛ Cayenne ni ji French Guiana maŋtiase lɛ. Kɛ́ mita kar mli kɛtee Matoury lɛ, ebaahe mi ŋmɛlɛtswai etɛ dani mashɛ jɛmɛ. No mli lɛ, miyitsɔi etsa kakadaŋŋ, ni mito tsɛŋ ba-sabasa ko. Miwo trɔza pɛpol kɛ pinki basabasa ko, ni shɛɛti ni miwo lɛ da kwraa fe mi. Shi nyɛmimɛi lɛ kɛ amɛjwɛŋmɔ emaaa bɔ ni misaa mihe mihã lɛ nɔ, moŋ lɛ, amɛye amɛbua mi ni minyɛ mitee kpee lɛ. Amɛhã mi he ko miwɔ, ni amɛhã mi niyenii hu. Bɔ ni nyɛmimɛi lɛ jie suɔmɔ kpo amɛtsɔɔ mi, ni amɛ-fee mi ejurɔ lɛ hã miyiŋ tsɔ akɛ mina anɔkwale lɛ.
