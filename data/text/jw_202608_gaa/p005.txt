11 Satan sumɔɔ ni wɔheɔ nɔ ni Yehowa tsɔɔ wɔ yɛ nɔ ni ja kɛ nɔ ni ejaaa he lɛ wɔyeɔ, ni soshia midia ji gbɛi ni etsɔɔ nɔ efeɔ nakai lɛ ateŋ ekome. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, yɛ soshia midia lɛ nɔ lɛ, mɛi babaoo wieɔ akɛ saji ni yɔɔ Biblia lɛ mli lɛ abe eho, ni ehe ehiaaa ni Nyɔŋmɔ tsɔɔ wɔ nɔ ni ja kɛ nɔ ni ejaaa. Akɛni Satan ji “je lɛ nɔyelɔ” hewɔ lɛ enyɛ elaka mɛi babaoo, ni no hewɔ lɛ amɛnaa efɔŋ akɛ ekpakpa, kɛ ekpakpa akɛ efɔŋ.—Yoh. 14:30; Yes. 5:20; 1 Tim. 4:1, 2.

12 Kɛ́ wɔbo nɔ ni mɛi ni yɔɔ Satan je lɛ mli lɛ kɛɔ yɛ nɔ ni ja kɛ nɔ ni ejaaa he lɛ toi lɛ, fiofio lɛ, wɔbaanu he akɛ nɔ ni Yehowa tsɔɔ wɔ lɛ ejaaa. Nyɛmi yoo oblayoo ko ni wɔbaatsɛ lɛ akɛ Sharon lɛ wie akɛ: “Bei shwie mli dani mibayɔse akɛ nibii ni mikaseɔ yɛ skul kɛ mɛi ni mikɛbɔɔ lɛ miitsake mijwɛŋmɔ. Fiofio lɛ, mibanya nibii ni Yehowa etsɔɔ wɔ akɛ ejaaa lɛ ahe. Mifɔɔ nibii ni akɛwoɔ soshia midia lɛ nɔ lɛ kanemɔ, ni no hewɔ lɛ mibɔi niiahesusumɔ tamɔ mɛi ni kɛ saji lɛ woɔ nɔ lɛ nɔŋŋ. Ebashɛ he ko lɛ, Yehowa sɔɔmɔ lɛ hãaa maná miishɛɛ. Ni kɛ́ miyɛ miwekumɛi lɛ kɛ nyɛmimɛi lɛ ateŋ lɛ, mináaa miishɛɛ.” Sɛɛ mli lɛ, Sharon fee tsakemɔi, ni ewaje wekukpaa ni yɔɔ ekɛ Yehowa ni ji eŋwei Tsɛ lɛ teŋ lɛ. No hewɔ lɛ ehãaa Satan alaka lɛ dɔŋŋ. Mɛni efee ni wa lɛ ni enyɛ efee tsakemɔi lɛ?

13 Sharon yɔse akɛ, esa akɛ efee tsakemɔi wuji bɔ ni afee ni eya nɔ ekɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu abɔ naanyo. Ewie akɛ: “Misɔle koni Yehowa awa mi ni maná nanemɛi kpakpai, ni mitao nii amli hu yɛ asafo lɛ woji kɛ vidioi lɛ amli. Vidio ni ji Young People Ask—How Can I Make Real Friends? lɛ ye ebua mi waa.* Naagba ni nyɛmi yoo lɛ ni yɔɔ vidio lɛ mli lɛ kɛkpe lɛ tamɔ minɔ lɛ nɔŋŋ. Eba ehe shi ni ebi efɔlɔi ni amɛye amɛbua lɛ. Mikpɛ miyiŋ akɛ makase enɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ. Mifɔlɔi, asafoŋ onukpai lɛ, kɛ nyɛmimɛi yei komɛi ye amɛbua mi ni mibanyɛ nibii ni Yehowa nyɛɔ lɛ ekoŋŋ ni mikɛ mɛi ni sumɔɔ Yehowa lɛ kpa bɔɔ ni miná nanemɛi kpakpai. Mibana akɛ, jeee asafoŋ kpeei ni maya kɛ mifɔlɔi atoi ni mabo lɛ kɛkɛ ni baatswɔ akɛ miisɔmɔ Yehowa. Moŋ lɛ, esa akɛ mafee nibii ni baahã mi kɛ Yehowa teŋ naanyobɔɔ lɛ mli awa, ni agbɛnɛ hu, mana yiŋtoi ahewɔ ni miheɔ saji ni yɔɔ Biblia lɛ mli lɛ miyeɔ lɛ.”
