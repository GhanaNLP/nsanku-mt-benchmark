¹⁶ Zakaria wiemɔi ni yɔɔ Luka 1:68-79 lɛ hãa wɔkaiɔ nibii etɛ komɛi ni wɔshwɛɛɛ amɛ-he kwraa. Nomɛi ji: Yehowa nikeenii ni nɔ bɛ ni ji yiwalaheremɔ lɛ, hegbɛ ni wɔná akɛ wɔ-baasɔmɔ lɛ ni wɔsheee gbeyei lɛ, kɛ nitsumɔ ni ekɛwo wɔdɛŋ akɛ wɔshiɛ sane kpakpa lɛ. Ani oheɔ Kristo kpɔmɔ afɔle lɛ oyeɔ, ni owo Yehowa shi akɛ obaasɔmɔ lɛ kɛya naanɔ koni okɛtsɔɔ akɛ omɔ enikeenii lɛ mli? Ani oshwɛɛɛ hegbɛ ni oná akɛ obaasɔmɔ Yehowa lɛ he? Kɛ́ nakai lɛ, okɛ ekãa atsu nitsumɔ ni akɛwo odɛŋ, ni ji akɛ oshiɛ “Yehowa mlihilɛ afi lɛ.” Shiɛmɔ sane kpakpa ni ji akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ baakpãtã efɔŋfeelɔi fɛɛ ahiɛ, ni etsɛŋ, shikpɔŋ lɛ baatsɔ paradeiso lɛ. Fɔ̃ɔ mɛi fɛɛ ni sumɔɔ Yehowa lɛ nine ni amɛbafa-ta wɔhe ni wɔkɛ “sɔɔmɔ krɔŋŋ ahã lɛ ni wɔ-sheee gbeyei yɛ anɔkwayeli kɛ jalɛ mli yɛ ehiɛ wɔgbii fɛɛ.”—Luka 1:74, 75.

<<<P>>>
NƆ NI WƆNAA YƐ MFONIRI LƐ MLI Baafa 24: Yɛ klɛŋklɛŋ mfoniri lɛ mli lɛ, nyɛmimɛi yei enyɔ komɛi miishiɛ yɛ shinai anaa. Yɛ nɔ ni ji enyɔ lɛ mli lɛ, nyɛmi nuu ko kɛ mɔ ko ni ekɛtsuɔ nii lɛ miijɔɔ amɛhe ni eeshiɛ eehã lɛ. Yɛ nɔ ni ji etɛ lɛ mli lɛ, nyɛmi yoo ko miitsɔɔ nɔ hewɔ ni Yehowa ji mɔ ni bɔ nibii fɛɛ lɛ mli yɛ skul, ni etsɔɔlɔ lɛ kɛ eklasbii lɛ miibo lɛ toi jogbaŋŋ.
