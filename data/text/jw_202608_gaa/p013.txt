WƆBAANYƐ WƆYE SATAN KƐ DAIMONIOI LƐ ANƆ KUNIM!
<<<P>>>
16 Biblia lɛ kɛɔ akɛ wɔbaanyɛ wɔye Satan kɛ daimonioi lɛ anɔ kunim. (Yak. 4:7; 1 Yoh. 5: 4, 5) Shi dani wɔbaanyɛ wɔye amɛnɔ kunim lɛ, ebaabi ni wɔná hemɔkɛyeli ni mli wa yɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu mli. (Efe. 6:16) No hewɔ lɛ, yaa nɔ ofee nibii ni baahã hemɔkɛyeli ni oyɔɔ yɛ Yehowa Nyɔŋmɔ kɛ Yesu Kristo mli lɛ mli aya nɔ awa. Hemɔkɛyeli nɛɛ hu baaye abua bo ni osumɔ onyɛmimɛi lɛ ni ekomefeemɔ ahi nyɛteŋ.
<<<P>>>
17 Satan baaya nɔ efee nibii ni baahã wɔkɛ wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ atswa ni efee nibii hu ni baa-hã wɔnijiaŋ aje wui ni wɔkpa shiɛmɔ. Shi esaaa akɛ wɔhãa Satan yeɔ wɔnɔ kunim kɔ-kɔɔkɔ. Yehowa kɛ Yesu ewo wɔ shi akɛ, kɛ́ wɔtee nɔ wɔye amɛ anɔkwa lɛ, amɛ hu amɛ-baaye amɛbua wɔ koni wɔye Satan nɔ kunim. Wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ hu kɛ wɔ efee ekome kɛmii-wu kɛmiishi Satan. Kɛ́ mɛi te shi amɛwo wɔ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔkɛ Yesu ŋaawoo lɛ atsu nii ni wɔkɛ amɛ aye kɛ naajɔlɛ. (Luka 6:27, 28) No hewɔ lɛ, kaajo foi yɛ ta ni okɛ Satan wuɔ lɛ naa. Hemɔ oye waa akɛ obaanyɛ oye lɛ kɛ daimonioi lɛ anɔ kunim.
