6 Kɛ́ wɔsumɔɔ wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ lɛɛlɛŋ lɛ, wɔhe tsɛ̃ŋ amɛ loo wɔyeŋ amɛhe awuŋa, moŋ lɛ wɔbaaye amɛ anɔkwa. Susumɔ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ kpakpa ni Yonatan fee lɛ he okwɛ. Saul ye David he awuŋa, shi ebinuu Yonatan eyafeee nakai. Ena David akɛ enaanyo, ni ekɛɛ lɛ akɛ nɔ ni Yehowa ewie yɛ ehe lɛ baaba mli. Ekɛɛ David akɛ, “Obaaye Israel nɔ maŋtsɛ.” Yonatan ba ehe shi ni ekpɛlɛ nɔ akɛ, David ji mɔ ni Yehowa miisumɔ ni eye maŋtsɛ yɛ Saul sɛɛ, ni no hewɔ lɛ, eye ebua David.—1 Sam. 23:16, 17.

7 Gbɔmɛi amli yɛ srɔto, ni Satan baasumɔ ni etsɔ no hu nɔ ehã mligbalamɔ aba wɔteŋ. Eesumɔ ni wɔhe wɔye akɛ, akɛni esoro nɔ ni wɔteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ sumɔɔ, bɔ ni wɔteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ feɔ enii ehãa kɛ maŋ nɔ ni wɔteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ jɛ hewɔ lɛ, ekomefeemɔ nyɛŋ ahi wɔteŋ. Mɛni kɛ́ wɔfee lɛ, ehãŋ ená hegbɛ ni ekɛ mligbalamɔ aba wɔteŋ? Kɛ́ wɔkɛ wɔhe eyakpaaa gbee yɛ saji ahe loo naagbai ba wɔteŋ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔtsu he nii oya nɔŋŋ. (Kanemɔ Efesobii 4:25-27.) Feemɔ nibii ni baahã osumɔ nyɛmimɛi lɛ waa. Kaimɔ akɛ, tamɔ bɔ ni gbɔmɔtso lɛ henii lɛ eko fɛɛ eko he hiaa ni ehe yɔɔ sɛɛnamɔ lɛ, nakai nɔŋŋ Yehowa shwɛɛɛ wɔteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ he, ni enaa wɔteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ akɛ ehe yɛ sɛɛnamɔ. (1 Kor. 12:12) Wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ ji asraafoi ni fata wɔhe koni wɔkɛ Satan kɛ daimonioi lɛ awu.—1 Pet. 5:8, 9.

8 Kɛ́ onyɛmi Odasefonyo ko wie nɔ ko loo efee nɔ ko ni dɔ bo lɛ, kaimɔ akɛ eyeee emuu tamɔ bo nɔŋŋ. Akɛni wɔkɛ wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ feɔ ekome kɛtsuɔ nii hewɔ lɛ, bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni fee lɛ, wɔbaana amɛtɔ̃mɔi. (Jaj. 7:20) Shi kɛ́ eba lɛ nakai lɛ, esa akɛ wɔkɛ ŋaa ni Biblia lɛ wo lɛ atsu nii, ni wɔná tsui wɔhã wɔhe. Ni kɛ́ amɛtɔ̃ wɔnɔ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔhe afee oya ni wɔkɛke amɛ. (Kol. 3:12-14) Kɛ́ wɔjie suɔmɔ kpo wɔtsɔɔ wɔhe ni wɔhã ekomefeemɔ hi wɔteŋ lɛ, belɛ, wɔmiifee nɔ ni Yehowa kɛ Yesu sumɔɔ ni wɔfee lɛ.—Yoh. 17:20, 21.

9 Nɔ kroko ni Satan feɔ koni no ahã wɔkpa Yehowa sɔɔmɔ ji, ehãa awaa wɔyi. Bɔfo Paulo bɔ kɔkɔ akɛ, abaawa anɔkwa Kristofoi fɛɛ ayi. (2 Tim. 3:12) Kɛ́ awa mɔ ko yi lɛ, akɛ lɛ yeko jogbaŋŋ. No hewɔ lɛ, kɛ́ awa wɔyi ni wɔkwɛɛɛ ni ahi lɛ, wɔkɛ wɔjwɛŋmɔ fɛɛ baama bɔ ni wɔbaafee wɔhã agbala mɛi ni wa wɔyi lɛ toi lɛ nɔ. Shi kɛ́ wɔfee nakai lɛ, wɔbaaná bei fioo pɛ kɛtsu nitsumɔ ni he hiaa waa, ni ji shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ ni Yehowa kɛwo wɔdɛŋ lɛ.—Rom. 12:19-21.

10 Hã wɔsusu nɔ ni ba Abraham binuu Isak nɔ lɛ he wɔkwɛ. Isak batsɔ niiatsɛ ni ená kooloi pii. Enɛ hã Filistibii lɛ ye ehe awuŋa ni no hewɔ lɛ amɛtsimɔ enubui lɛ koni ekaná nu. (1 Mo. 26:12-18) Shi nu hu he baahia Isak, eweku lɛ kɛ ekooloi lɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, efã kɛtee he kroko ni jekɛ waa ni etsa nubui yɛ jɛmɛ. Shi Filistibii lɛ kɛɛ nu ni yɔɔ jɛmɛ lɛ hu lɛ amɛnɔ ni, ni enɛ hã Isak tookwɛlɔi lɛ kɛ amɛ bé. Eyɛ faŋŋ akɛ, Filistibii lɛ kɛ Isak yeee jogbaŋŋ, ni kulɛ no baanyɛ ahã Isak nijiaŋ aje wui.
