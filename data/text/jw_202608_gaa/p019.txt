17 Mɛni Paulo tsɔɔ be ni ewie akɛ, “nyɛyaa nɔ nyɛnáa tsui nyɛhãa nyɛhe” lɛ? No mli lɛ, eewo wɔ hewalɛ ni wɔto wɔtsui shi ni wɔkɛ nyɛmimɛi lɛ aye kɛ naajɔlɛ ké amɛwo wɔmli la loo amɛfee nɔ ko ni gba wɔnaa po. Yɛ nakai kuku lɛ mli nɔŋŋ lɛ, Paulo wie akɛ Yehowa feɔ enɛ he nɔkwɛmɔnɔ yɛ gbɛ ni taa mɔ tsuiŋ lɛ nɔ. Yehowa nuɔ nii ashishi, ni no hewɔ lɛ ekpaaa gbɛ akɛ ejálɔi lɛ baafee nɔ fɛɛ nɔ pɛ-pɛɛpɛ ni amɛtɔ̃ɔɔ. Eyɛ mli akɛ ele amɛgbɔjɔ-mɔi lɛ moŋ, shi ekɛ ejwɛŋmɔ maa amɛsui kpa-kpai lɛ anɔ. (Lala 103:13, 14; 130:3) Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, eyaa nɔ ejieɔ suɔmɔ ni tsakeee kpo etsɔɔ wɔ ni enaa wɔ mɔbɔ. (Yaf. 3:22, 23) Ké wɔfee bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni wɔbaanyɛ koni wɔna nyɛmimɛi lɛ tamɔ bɔ ni Yehowa naa amɛ lɛ, ké amɛtɔ̃ wɔnɔ lɛ, ewaŋ kɛhãŋ wɔ akɛ wɔbaaná tsui wɔhã amɛ, tamɔ bɔ ni ké wɔtɔ̃ amɛnɔ lɛ, amɛ hu amɛnáa tsui amɛhãa wɔ lɛ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ: Ké be shɛko ni tso ko ni wɔdu lɛ baagba afo-fro, shi wɔkai bɔ ni ké egba afofro lɛ efeɔ fɛo

17. Mɛni wiemɔ ni ji, “nyɛyaa nɔ nyɛnáa tsui nyɛhãa nyɛ-he” lɛ biɔ ni wɔfee?
<<<P>>>
ehãa lɛ, no baaye abua wɔ ni wɔya nɔ wɔtsu he nii kɛyashi ebaagba afofro ekoŋŋ. Nakai nɔŋŋ ké wɔkai sui kpakpai ni nyɛmimɛi lɛ yɔɔ lɛ, no baaye abua wɔ ni ké amɛwooo wɔdaan ŋmaa yɛ sane ko he po lɛ, wɔkɛ amɛhe ake amɛ, ni wɔkɛ amɛ aye kɛ naajɔlɛ.

18 Yehowa hiɛ sɔɔ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni ofeɔ koni toiŋ-jɔlɛ kɛ ekomefeemɔ aya nɔ ahi asafo lɛ mli lɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, daa gbi lɛ, kwɛmɔ akɛ mɛni obaa-nyɛ ofee koni obaa toiŋjɔlɛ yi. Yaa nɔ okɛ mɛi awie kɛ naajɔlɛ ni owie mɛi ahe ekpakpa. Ké otɔ̃ mɛi anɔ lɛ, kpa fai. Ké ofee nɔ ko ni egba mɔ ko naa lɛ, feemɔ bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni obaanyɛ ni osaa nyɛteŋ. Feemɔ nibii ni baahã suɔmɔ ni oyɔɔ kɛhã nyɛmimɛi lɛ mli awa waa, ejaakɛ no ni baahã efee mlɛo kɛhã bo akɛ okɛ amɛhe baake amɛ tswaa. Ké ofee nibii nɛɛ, no baaye abua waa koni toiŋjɔlɛ aya nɔ ahi asafo lɛ mli. Ni wɔsɛɛ lɛ, obaafata mɛi ni he jɔ ni “shikpɔŋ lɛ baatsɔ amɛnɔ” lɛ ahe, ni ‘omii baashɛ ohe waa yɛ toiŋjɔlɛ babaoo mli’ kɛya naanɔ!—Lala 37:11.
