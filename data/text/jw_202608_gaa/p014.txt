Nɔ Ni Hãa Anaa Akɛ Wɔyɛ Suɔmɔ

NƆ NI WƆBAAKASE
Wɔbaana bɔ ni wɔbaafee wɔkase bɔfo Paulo, Filemon, kɛ Onesimo, ni wɔfee nibii ni baahã toiŋjɔlɛ aya nɔ ahi asafo lɛ mli.
<<<P>>>
Mikɛ Suɔmɔ Miikpa Bo Fai
<<<P>>>
“Mikɛ suɔmɔ moŋ miikpa bo fai.”—FLM. 9.
<<<P>>>
WƆSHWƐƐƐ toiŋjɔlɛ ni yɔɔ wɔ kɛ wɔnyɛmimei lɛ ateŋ lɛ he kwraa. Ehãa wɔnaa bɔ ni shihilɛ baaŋɔɔ ahã yɛ Paradeiso lɛ mli. (Yes. 32:17, 18) Eji anɔkwale akɛ Yehowa ji mɔ ni ehã wɔná toiŋjɔlɛ nɛɛ moŋ, shi sɔ̃ kã wɔteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ nɔ akɛ ebaakwɛ ni ebaa toiŋjɔlɛ nɛɛ yi. (Rom. 12:18) Te esa akɛ wɔkɛ wɔnyɛmimei lɛ aye ahã tɛŋŋ koni toiŋjɔlɛ aya nɔ ahi asafo lɛ mli? Ké wɔtɔ̃ nyɛmi ko nɔ lɛ, mɛni wɔbaanyɛ wɔfee koni toiŋjɔlɛ aya nɔ ahi wɔ kɛ lɛ teŋ? Ni ké nyɛmi ko tɔ̃ wɔnɔ lɛ, mɛni wɔbaanyɛ wɔfee koni toiŋjɔlɛ aya nɔ ahi wɔ kɛ lɛ teŋ? Wɔbaana hetoi lɛ yɛ sane ko ni kɔɔ bɔfo Paulo, Filemon, kɛ Onesimo he lɛ mli. Yɛ nakai sane lɛ mli lɛ, bɔfo Paulo jɛ suɔmɔ mli ekpa fai koni ekɛbaa toiŋjɔlɛ yi.

MIKƐ SUƆMƆ MIIKPA BO FAI
<<<P>>>
2 Kanemɔ Filemon 8, 9, 17. Yɛ wolo ni Paulo ŋma eyahã Filemon lɛ mli lɛ, ewo lɛ hewalɛ ni ké Onesimo, ni ji enyɔŋ ni ejo foi lɛ, ku esɛɛ kɛba eŋɔɔ lɛ, ehere lɛ hiɛmɛɛ. Paulo kɛɛ lɛ ni efee nakai yɛ suɔmɔ ni eyɔɔ kɛhã Kristo kɛ enyɛmimei ni yɔɔ asafo lɛ mli lɛ hewɔ. Ekolɛ ole sane nɛɛ. No mli lɛ, Filemon yɛ Kolose asafo lɛ mli. Ale lɛ akɛ mɔ ko ni hemɔkɛyeli mli wa ni yɔɔ suɔmɔ. Eyɛ nyɔŋ ko ni jeee Kristofonyo ni atsɛɔ lɛ Onesimo. Be ko lɛ, Onesimo jo foi eshi Filemon ni etee Roma. Eeenyɛ efee akɛ, ewó Filemon shika kɛtee. (Flm. 18) Be ni Onesimo yɔɔ Roma lɛ, ebatsɔ Kristofonyo kɛ Paulo naanyo kpaakpa. (Kol. 4:9) Shi Roma mla lɛ biɔ ni nyɔji ni ejo foi kɛshi amɛnuŋtsɔmɛi lɛ aku sɛɛ kɛya amɛŋɔɔ. Bɔ ni afee ni Paulo aye mla nɛɛ nɔ lɛ, ewo Onesimo gbɛ kɛtee Filemon ŋɔɔ.—Flm. 12, 13.

3 Eeenyɛ efee akɛ Paulo susu bɔ ni Filemon baafee enii ehã ké Onesimo ku sɛɛ kɛtee eŋɔɔ lɛ he. Mla lɛ bi ni ké nyɔŋ ko ni ejo foi ku esɛɛ etee enuŋtsɔ ŋɔɔ lɛ agbala etoi waa. No hewɔ lɛ,
<<<P>>>
1. Mɛɛ sɔ̃ kã wɔteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ nɔ?
<<<P>>>
2. Gbaa Filemon kɛ Onesimo sane lɛ kuku. (Filemon 8, 9, 17)
<<<P>>>
3. Be ni Paulo woɔ Onesimo gbɛ kɛyaa Filemon ŋɔɔ lɛ, mɛɛ saji ekolɛ ebibii ehe? (Kwɛmɔ mfoniri ni yɔɔ wolo lɛ hiɛ lɛ hu.)
