No mli lɛ, bɔfo Gabriel ewie akɛ abaatsɛ Yesu “Nyɔŋmɔ Bi,” ni ebaaye nɔ akɛ Maŋtsɛ yɛ “etsɛ David maŋtsɛ sɛi lɛ” nɔ. (Luka 1:30-35) Be ni Yesu ye afii 30 lɛ, abaptisi lɛ. Be ni abaptisiɔ lɛ lɛ, akɛ mumɔ krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ fɔ lɛ mu ni ebatsɔ Mesia lɛ, ni ji ‘yiwalaherelɔ ni yɔɔ hewalɛ kpele’ lɛ. (Mat. 3:16, 17) Eji nɔ ko ni sa kadimɔ waa!

3 Ehe bahia ni ahere Yudafoi ni hi shi yɛ Yesu beiaŋ lɛ. Shi namɔ loo mɛni ehe bahia ni ahere amɛ kɛje mli? Zakaria hã hetoo akɛ: “Yiwalaheremɔ kɛmiijɛ wɔhenyɛlɔi kɛ mɛi fɛɛ ni he tsɛ̃ɔ wɔ lɛ adɛŋ.” (Luka 1:71) Ani enɛ tsɔɔ akɛ Yesu baahere amɛ kɛje Romabii ni yeɔ amɛnɔ ni amɛwaa amɛyi lɛ adɛŋ? Dabi. Moŋ lɛ, ehe baahia ni ahere Yudafoi lɛ kɛje Satan ni ji mɔ tuuntu ni hãa awaa amɛyi lɛ dɛŋ. (Heb. 2:14, 15) Satan hewɔ ni adesai fɛɛ, ni blema Yudafoi lɛ hu fata he lɛ, feɔ esha ni amɛgboiɔ lɛ. Ehe bahia ni ahere Yudafoi lɛ yɛ yiŋtoo kroko hu hewɔ. No ji, amɛnyɛɛɛ amɛye Mose Mla lɛ nɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ. Ni Biblia lɛ kɛɛ no hewɔ lɛ, “alomɔ” amɛ.

3. Mɛni hewɔ ehe bahia ni ahere Yudafoi ni hi shi yɛ Yesu beiaŋ lɛ?
<<<P>>>
(Rom. 3:20; Gal. 3:10, 13) Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, ehe bahia ni amɛná nɔ ni Yehowa tsɔ Zakaria nɔ ewo shi lɛ. No ji, “yiwalaheremɔ . . . kɛtsɔ amɛhe eshai ni akɛbaake amɛ lɛ nɔ.”—Luka 1:77.

4 Ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, wɔ hu mɛni ehe ebahia ni ahere wɔ kɛje mli? Tamɔ eba lɛ yɛ Yudafoi ni hi shi yɛ Yesu beiaŋ lɛ agbɛfaŋ lɛ, wɔ hu ehe ebahia ni ahere wɔ kɛje esha kɛ gbele dɛŋ. (Rom. 5:12) Kɛfata he lɛ, Satan kɛ daimonioi lɛ ehã wɔmiina nɔ waa. Amɛfeɔ bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni amɛbaanyɛ koni wɔgbo Yehowa nɔ toi ni wɔkpa lɛ sɔɔmɔ. Amɛhãa ‘mɛi ni he tsɛ̃ɔ wɔ lɛ’ teɔ shi amɛwoɔ wɔ akɛni wɔmiijá Yehowa lɛ hewɔ. Esa akɛ ahere wɔ kɛje wɔhenyɛlɔi nɛɛ fɛɛ adɛŋ.

5 Mɛɛ gbɛ nɔ Yesu ni ji wɔ-Yiwalaherelɔ lɛ hereɔ wɔ kɛjeɔ wɔhenyɛlɔi nɛɛ adɛŋ? Be ni Yesu yɔɔ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ lɛ, etsɔɔ esɛɛnyiɛlɔi lɛ bɔ ni esa akɛ amɛjá Yehowa amɛhã. Kɛfata he lɛ, ekɛ ewala fɔ̃ shi akɛ kpɔmɔnɔ “koni mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni heɔ enɔ eyeɔ lɛ hie akakpãtã shi moŋ ená naanɔ wala.” (Yoh. 3:16; Mat. 20:28) Wɔfeɔ bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni wɔbaanyɛ koni wɔjá Yehowa tamɔ bɔ ni Yesu etsɔɔ wɔ lɛ. Shi loloolo lɛ, wɔyeee emuu, no hewɔ lɛ, kɛ́ wɔtɔ̃ Yehowa nɔ lɛ, wɔkpaa lɛ fai ni ekɛke wɔ. Ni Yehowa hu kɛ wɔhe keɔ wɔ yɛ Yesu kpɔmɔ afɔle lɛ hewɔ, ni ekpɛlɛɔ wɔjamɔ lɛ nɔ. (1 Yoh. 2:1, 2) Yesu, ni ji wɔ-Yiwalaherelɔ lɛ, tsɔɔ gbɛi nɛɛ anɔ ehereɔ wɔ koni akɛ wɔhe eshai lɛ ake wɔ ni wɔhi shi kɛya naanɔ.
