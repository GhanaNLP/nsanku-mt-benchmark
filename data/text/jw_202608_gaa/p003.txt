SATAN FITE MƐI AJWƐŊMƆ YƐ YEHOWA KƐ YESU HE
<<<P>>>
3 Be ni Yehowa bɔ Adam kɛ Hawa lɛ sɛɛ etsɛɛɛ lɛ, Satan bɔ mɔdɛŋ akɛ ebaahã amɛgbo Yehowa nɔ toi. Satan laka Hawa ni ehe eye akɛ Yehowa ni ji e-Bɔlɔ lɛ sumɔɔɔ lɛ. Ebi Hawa akɛ: “Ani eji anɔkwale akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ kɛɛ nyɛkaye abɔɔ lɛ mli tsei lɛ eko fɛɛ eko yibii?” (1 Mo. 3:1-5) Eetao ni Hawa anu he akɛ Yehowa hãaa lɛ kɛ ewu Adam nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni he hiaa amɛ lɛ. Nɔ ni Satan wie lɛ ji amale. Shi Hawa he Satan amale lɛ eye. Dɔlɛ sane ji akɛ, Hawa kɛ Adam fee Satan ni ji Yehowa henyɛlɔ lɛ moŋ suɔmɔnaa nii. Eyɛ mli akɛ Yehowa ji mɔ ni hã amɛ wala kɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni amɛyɔɔ lɛ moŋ, shi amɛgbo enɔ toi.—1 Mo. 3:6.
<<<P>>>
4 Kɛjɛ Adam kɛ Hawa beiaŋ tɔ̃ɔ kɛbaa nɛɛ, Satan etee nɔ efite mɛi ajwɛŋmɔ yɛ Yehowa he. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, Satan hã hii etɛ komɛi yakɛɛ Hiob Yehowa he saji komɛi ni bɛ mli. Eesumɔ ni Hiob ahe aye akɛ Yehowa sumɔɔɔ lɛ, ni akɛ, nɔ ko nɔ ko bɛ ni Hiob baafee ni baasa Yehowa hiɛ. (Hiob 22:1-3) Satan hã Hiob piŋ waa hu. Eyasusu akɛ no baahã Hiob ashwa Yehowa koni suɔmɔ ni Hiob yɔɔ kɛhã Yehowa lɛ he ajɔ. Shi nɔ ni Satan fee lɛ hãaa Hiob kɛ Yehowa akpa naanyobɔɔ.—Hiob 27:1, 5.
<<<P>>>
5 Afii ohai abɔ komɛi asɛɛ be ni Yesu ba shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ lɛ, Satan bɔ mɔdɛŋ akɛ ebaahã mɛi akayanyiɛ Yesu sɛɛ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, ehã jamɔŋ hiɛnyiɛlɔi lɛ kɛ mɛi krokomɛi ni he tsɛ̃ɔ Yesu lɛ, gbɛ Yesu he amalei amɛshwã. Mɛi pii he amalei nɛɛ amɛye, ni no hã amɛfolɔ Yesu naa akɛ eji efɔŋfeelɔ, ni amɛgbe lɛ.
<<<P>>>
—Kanemɔ Mateo 11:19; 27:27-31; Mar. 14:55, 56; Yoh. 8:41-45.
<<<P>>>
6 Yɛ Yesu gbele lɛ sɛɛ lɛ, Satan bɔ mɔdɛŋ koni eje Yesu sɛɛnyiɛlɔi lɛ anijiaŋ wui koni amɛkpa Yesu he sane shiɛmɔ. Te efee lɛ ehã tɛŋŋ? Klɛŋklɛŋ lɛ, Satan sumɔɔɔ ni mɛi heɔ amɛyeɔ akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ etee Yesu shi. No hewɔ lɛ, ehã mɛi komɛi male akɛ Yesu kaselɔi lɛ ni eyaju Yesu gbonyo lɛ. (Mat. 28:11-15) No sɛɛ lɛ, Satan hã jamɔŋ hiɛnyiɛlɔi lɛ kɛ nɔyelɔi lɛ wa Yesu kaselɔi lɛ ayi. Eyasusu akɛ no baahã amɛshe gbeyei waa. Eesumɔ ni amɛshe gbeyei waa aahu akɛ amɛbaakpa nitsumɔ ni Yesu kɛɛ amɛtsu lɛ tsumɔ.—Bɔf. 7:57–8:1; 12:1-3; 28:22.
<<<P>>>
7 Kaselɔi lɛ ateŋ mɛi komɛi she gbeyei ni amɛkpa shiɛmɔ, shi amɛteŋ mɛi babaoo tee nɔ amɛye anɔkwa ni amɛtee nɔ amɛshiɛ. Amɛna akɛ Satan kɛ edaimonioi lɛ ji mɛi ni hɔ amɛyi ni awaa koni amɛkpa shiɛmɔ lɛ sɛɛ. (Bɔf. 4:18-20; 1 Pet. 5:8) Mɛni Satan feɔ ŋmɛnɛ koni ekɛje wɔnijiaŋ wui ni wɔkpa Yehowa sɔɔmɔ?
<<<P>>>
SATAN MIIYA NƆ EEFITE MƐI AJWƐŊMƆ YƐ YEHOWA KƐ YESU HE
<<<P>>>
8 Ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, Satan hãa ewaa kɛhãa mɛi akɛ amɛbaana Yehowa kɛ Yesu he anɔkwalei lɛ. Abonsam ji “malemɔ tsɛ” lɛ; ehã amale jamɔi lɛ miigbɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu he amalei amɛmiishwã. (Yoh. 8:44) Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, eshwila mɛi koni amɛkana mɛi tuuntu ni Yehowa kɛ Yesu ji. (2 Kor. 4:3, 4) Sɔlemɔi lɛ ekomɛi tsɔɔ akɛ, Yesu nɔŋŋ ji Yehowa, ni ji e-Tsɛ lɛ. Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, mɛi leee Nyɔŋmɔ gbéi ni ji Yehowa lɛ, ni amɛjáa Yesu moŋ fe ni amɛbaajá Yehowa. Shi Yehowa miisumɔ ni ajá ekome pɛ, ni Yesu hu miisumɔ ni ajá Yehowa kome pɛ.
<<<P>>>
