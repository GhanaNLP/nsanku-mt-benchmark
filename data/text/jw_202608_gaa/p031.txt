ANI OLE?
<<<P>>>
Mɛni tee nɔ yɛ maŋ agbói ni atsĩ tã yɛ Biblia lɛ mli lɛ anaa?
<<<P>>>
MAŊ agbói lɛ jeee he ko ni mɛi tsɔɔ kɛboteɔ maŋ ko mli kɛkɛ. Akɛnɔ mɛi pii tsɔɔ agbói nɛɛ amli hewɔ lɛ, afee nibii pii yɛ jɛmɛ. Atsĩ maŋ agbói atã shii abɔ yɛ Biblia lɛ mli, ni ehãa wɔnaa nibii ni tee nɔ yɛ jɛmɛ.
<<<P>>>
Mɛi bua amɛhe naa yɛ maŋ agbói anaa. Kɛ́ akɛ Hebri wiemɔ ni atsɔɔ shishi akɛ “agbó” lɛ tsu nii lɛ, ekɔɔɔ he ni atsɔɔ kɛboteɔ maŋ ko mli lɛ pɛ he. Akɛtsuɔ nii hu kɛhãa he ni mɛi mamɔɔ agbai yɛ, yɛ maŋ lɛ agbó lɛ masɛi lɛ. Yɛ maji komɛi amli lɛ, ayɛ henaabuamɔhei ni dara yɛ maŋ lɛ agbói lɛ amasɛi ni mɛi baanyɛ akpe yɛ jɛmɛ.—Neh. 8:1.
<<<P>>>
Jarayelɔi yawoɔ jara yɛ maŋ agbói lɛ amasɛi. Atsɛ agbói komɛi ni yɔɔ Yerusalem lɛ akɛ Loo Agbó kɛ Gwantɛŋi Agbó. Eeenyɛ efee akɛ, nibii ni ahɔ̃ɔ yɛ agbói nɛɛ amasɛi hewɔ awo amɛ nakai gbɛ́i lɛ.—Zef. 1:10; Yoh. 5:2.
<<<P>>>
Mɛi yayeɔ jara yɛ maŋ agbói lɛ amasɛi ni amɛfeɔ nibii krokomɛi hu yɛ jɛmɛ. Abraham ya-kpaŋ kpaŋmɔ yɛ maŋ agbó ko masɛi yɛ mɛi ahiɛ. Nakai nɔŋŋ Boaz hu fee. (1 Mo. 23:10-15, 17, 18; Rut 4:1, 10) Kɛ́ mɛi miifã gbɛ ni je na amɛ ni mɔ ko hãaa amɛ he ko amɛtɔ lɛ, amɛyayeɔ jena-mɔ lɛ yɛ maŋ henaabuamɔhe ni yɔɔ maŋ lɛ agbó lɛ masɛi lɛ. (Koj. 19:15-21) Mɛi komɛi hu yaa maŋ agbói anaa koni amɛyabo saji ni eba atoi. —2 Sam. 18:24, 25.
<<<P>>>
Kojolɔi yeɔ saji yɛ maŋ agbói lɛ amasɛi. Amɛ-yeɔ saji yɛ mɛi ahiɛ koni mɛi lɛ ana saji lɛ amli kɛ bɔ ni abua lɛ ahã. (5 Mo. 21:18-20) Akɛnɔ ayeɔ saji lɛ yɛ mɛi ahiɛ hewɔ lɛ, nɔ hãa kojolɔi lɛ kwɛɔ koni amɛye saji lɛ jogbaŋŋ. Kɛ́ kojolɔi lɛ bua sane ko lɛ, amɛkwɛɔ koni atsu he nii oya nɔŋŋ. (5 Mo. 25:2) Akpa gbɛ akɛ, kojolɔi ni kojoɔ saji yɛ maŋ agbói lɛ anaa lɛ baaba amɛjeŋ jogbaŋŋ ni amɛ-ye jalɛ sane koni no ahã maŋbii lɛ hu aba amɛjeŋ jogbaŋŋ.—5 Mo. 16:18-20; Hiob 29:7, 11-17.

Afee nibii srɔtoisrɔtoi yɛ maŋ agbói anaa
<<<P>>>
Blema hii anɔkwafoi lɛ yaa maŋ agbói lɛ anaa amɛyawoɔ Israelbii ni ebua amɛhe naa yɛ jɛmɛ lɛ hewalɛ koni amɛkɛ amɛhiɛ afɔ̃ Yehowa nɔ. Kɛ́ mɛi yɛ saji ni amɛbaasumɔ ni mɛi ale lɛ, amɛyaa maŋ agbói lɛ anaa ejaakɛ amɛle akɛ mɛi babuaa amɛhe naa yɛ jɛmɛ. (1 Maŋ. 22:10; Yer. 17:19, 20) Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, Yehu tee maŋ agbó ko naa eyakɛɛ maŋbii lɛ fɛɛ akɛ nɔ ni Yehowa wie eshi Ahab shĩa lɛ eba mli. (2 Maŋ. 10:8-10) Maŋ-tsɛ Hezekia hu bua tatsɛmɛi anaa yɛ maŋ agbó ko naa ni ewo amɛ hewalɛ koni amɛkɛ amɛhiɛ afɔ̃ Yehowa nɔ kɛhã yelikɛbuamɔ.—2 Kro. 32:6-8.

Biblia lɛ wie akɛ, “anɔkwa hiɛshikamɔ” miibo waa loo ewó egbee nɔ yɛ “maŋ lɛ agbói lɛ anaa.” (Abɛi 1:20, 21) Yɛ blema Israel lɛ, hii anɔkwafoi wo ŋaa kpakpa ni amɛkojo saji jogbaŋŋ yɛ maŋ agbói lɛ anaa. Ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, aŋmala hiɛshikamɔ wie-mɔi ni jɛ Yehowa ŋɔɔ lɛ yɛ Biblia lɛ mli, ni etsuji ni yɔɔ jeŋ fɛɛ lɛ miiye amɛmiibua mɛi koni amɛkase Biblia lɛ ni amɛná anɔkwa hiɛshikamɔ.
