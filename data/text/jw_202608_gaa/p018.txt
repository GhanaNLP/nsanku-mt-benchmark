Tamɔ bɔ ni Filemon fee lɛ, feemɔ gbɔ koni ekomefeemɔ aya nɔ ahi asafo lɛ mli
(Kwemɔ kuku 15)
<<<P>>>
ni ji, asafo ni Filemon yɔɔ mli lɛ, ewie akɛ suɔmɔ ji, “kpãa ni feɔ mɛi ekome yɛ gbɛ ni eye emuu nɔ.” (Kanemɔ Kolosebii 3:14.) Nɔ ni Paulo wie nɛɛ hãa wɔnaa akɛ, kɛ́ toinjɔlɛ kɛ ekomefeemɔ baahi asafo lɛ mli lɛ, ehe baahia waa ni wɔjie suɔmɔ kpo. Kɛ́ suɔmɔ ni wɔyɔɔ kɛhã mɛi lɛ mli wa lɛ, ewaŋ kɛhãŋ wɔ akɛ wɔkɛ amɛhe baake amɛ, ni wɔkɛ amɛ aye jogbaŋŋ. (1 Kor. 13:4-7) Hã wɔkwɛ bɔ ni suɔmɔ baahã Filemon kɛ mɛi ahe ake amɛ.
<<<P>>>
14 Filemon feɔ gbɔ waa, ni no hãa enáa hegbɛ ni ejie suɔmɔ kpo etsɔɔ mɛi. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, ekɛ eshĩa lɛ hã koni asafo lɛ akpe yɛ jɛmɛ. (Flm. 2) Agbɛnɛ hu, Paulo bi Filemon ni esaa he ko eto lɛ ni ebahi jɛmɛ, ni enɛ hãa wɔnaa akɛ ele akɛ Filemon feɔ gbɔ waa. (Flm. 22) Ŋwanejee ko bɛ he akɛ, gbɔ ni Filemon feɔ lɛ hã nyɛmimɛi lɛ hiɛ mɛ, ni amɛtsui nyɔ amɛmli.—Flm. 7.
<<<P>>>
15 Mɛni hewɔ kɛ́ wɔfeɔ gbɔ tamɔ Filemon fee lɛ, no baahã wɔná hegbɛ wɔjie suɔmɔ kpo wɔtsɔɔ mɛi waa lɛ? Kɛ́ wɔfee wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ gbɔ lɛ, ehãa wɔbaleɔ amɛ jogbaŋŋ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, kɛ́ wɔfee nyɛmimɛi ni wɔkɛshãraaa tsɔ loo mɛi ni efeee wɔ akɛ amɛbaanya nibii ni wɔnyaa he lɛ ahe lɛ gbɔ lɛ, wɔnaa sui fɛfɛji ni amɛteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɔɔ ni hãa esoroɔ amɛ lɛ, ni no hãa asafo lɛ mli hilɛ ŋɔɔ. Kɛ́ wɔbale mɛi nɛɛ ahe saji krokomɛi tamɔ he ni atsɔse amɛ yɛ, bɔ ni fee ni amɛba anɔkwale lɛ mli, kɛ́ shihilɛi ni amɛtsɔmɔ mli lɛ, no hãa wɔbasumɔɔ amɛ waa ni wɔhiɛ sɔɔ amɛ fe tsutsu lɛ po. Enɛ hãa wɔnaa nɔ hewɔ ni Yehowa gbala amɛ kɛbafata eweku lɛ he lɛ. (Yoh. 6:44) Kɛ́ wɔfeɔ wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ gbɔ lɛ, ehãa wɔbasumɔɔ amɛ waa, wɔnáa amɛhe jwɛŋmɔ kpakpa, ni kɛ́ amɛtɔ̃ wɔnɔ lɛ, ehãa wɔkɛ amɛhe keɔ amɛ.
<<<P>>>
16 Ani amrɔ nɛɛ ni wɔwieɔ lɛ, mɔ ko etɔ̃ onɔ ni ewa kɛhã bo akɛ okɛbaake lɛ? Kɛ́ wɔsumɔɔ wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ po lɛ, bei komɛi lɛ, ewaa kɛhãa wɔ akɛ wɔkɛ amɛhe baake amɛ. Kɛ́ eba lɛ nakai lɛ, mɛni baanyɛ aye abua wɔ? Susumɔ nɔ ni Paulo wie yɛ wolo ni eŋma eyahã asafo ni Filemon yɔɔ mli ni yɔɔ Kolose lɛ he okwɛ. (Kanemɔ Kolosebii 3:13.)
