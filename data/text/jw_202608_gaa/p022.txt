Yehowa etsɔ Yesu Kristo nɔ ekɛ nikeenii ni ji yiwalaheremɔ lɛ eduro wɔ
(Kwɛmɔ kuku 6)
<<<P>>>
kɛbaa lɛ, Yesu ta “etsɛ David maŋtsɛ sɛi lɛ” nɔ yɛ ŋwɛi eeye Maŋtsɛ. (Luka 1:32, 33) Etsɛŋ Yesu, mɛi 144,000 lɛ, kɛ ŋwɛibɔfoi anɔkwafoi lɛ baakpãtã adesai fɛɛ ni teɔ shi amɛwoɔ Nyɔŋmɔ lɛ ahiɛ yɛ Harmagedon. No sɛɛ lɛ, yɛ Afii Akpe Nɔyeli lɛ mli lɛ, Yesu baatee gbohii lɛ ashi kɛba wala mli, ni ekɛ mɛi 144,000 lɛ baaye abua adesai koni fiofio lɛ amɛye emuu. (Yoh. 5:28; Kpo. 22:1, 2) Be ni afii akpe lɛ baaba naagbee lɛ, amɛjie esha kɛ gbele kɛje jɛmɛ. (1 Kor. 15:26, 55-57) No sɛɛ lɛ, Yesu kɛ mɛi 144,000 lɛ baakpãtã Satan, edaimonioi lɛ, kɛ adesai fɛɛ ni baayanyiɛ amɛsɛɛ be ni afii akpe lɛ eba naagbee lɛ ahiɛ. (Kpo. 20:7-10) Naagbee kwraa lɛ, adesai fɛɛ ni tee nɔ amɛye Yehowa anɔkwa lɛ baasɔmɔ lɛ ni esha bɛ amɛhe, amɛgboiii, ni mɔ ko mɔ ko hu eteee shi ewooo amɛ dɔŋŋ! No hewɔ lɛ, wɔbaajie Nyɔŋmɔ yi kɛya naanɔ ejaakɛ etsɔ Kristo Yesu nɔ ehere wɔ.—2 Kor. 9:15.

HEGBƐ NI WƆNÁ AKƐ WƆBAASƆMƆ YEHOWA LƐ
<<<P>>>
7 Kanemɔ Luka 1:74, 75. Yehowa nikeenii ni ji kpɔmɔnɔ lɛ, hãa wɔnáa hegbɛ srɔto ko. Zakaria wie akɛ, “ehã wɔ hegbɛ ni wɔkɛ sɔɔmɔ krɔŋŋ ahã lɛ ni wɔsheee gbeyei.” Mɛɛ gbɛ nɔ wɔkɛ “sɔɔmɔ krɔŋŋ” hãa Yehowa? Be fɛɛ be ni wɔbaashiɛ, wɔbaasɔle, wɔbaakase Biblia lɛ, wɔbaaya asafoŋ kpee, kpokpaa nɔ kpee, kɛ kpokpaa wulu nɔ kpee lɛ, belɛ wɔkɛ sɔɔmɔ krɔŋŋ miihã Yehowa ji no. Agbɛnɛ hu, kɛ́ wɔmiisɔmɔ yɛ Betel, wɔmiiwa kɛmiimamɔ Maŋtsɛyeli Asai kɛ Kpeei Asai, ni wɔmiiye wɔmiibua kɛmiikura tsui nɛɛ, belɛ wɔkɛ sɔɔmɔ krɔŋŋ miihã Yehowa ji no.
