The apostles were convinced that Jehovah is worthy of worship because he is the Maker of all things; that Jesus is God’s Son, who has “sayings of everlasting life”; and that Jesus has Jehovah’s full support. Now rather than consider many specific ways our faith can be tested, let us focus on three truths that can help us stay firmly grounded regardless of what tests we may face. They are: (1) Jehovah is the Creator of all things. (2) Jehovah inspired the Bible. (3) Jehovah is backing his people today. If we are thoroughly convinced of these three truths, we will remain loyal when we undergo tests of faith.

7 Read Revelation 4:11. This text reminds us that Jehovah created all things. This fact alone makes him worthy of our worship.
