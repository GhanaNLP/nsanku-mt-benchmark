11 Ka te Baabole zannezannaŋ wa de yɛlɛ na oŋ zanna na tonɔ neŋ toma o zemmo poɔŋ, o na nyɛrɛɛ maaloo, ka lɛ vɛŋ ka o taa popeɛloŋ meŋ. Kyɛ, aseŋ ka te somoo ka o nyɛ ka ana maaloo na zaa oŋ nyɛrɛ na yiree te Wulwuli Kpoŋ Jehoova zieŋ. (Aiz. 30:20, 21) Aminekaŋaŋ, te Baabole zannezanna na pugro teŋ a kyaare boma na oŋ zanna na bee lɛ na teŋ baŋ wulibo seŋ. Aneazaa ka a ba e faa ka o e lɛ, kyɛ wagre zaa aseŋ ka te vɛŋ ka o baŋ ka Jehoova la neɛ naŋ koroo ana wulibo na, azuŋ o na yoŋ aŋ seŋ ka o de bareka puoruu korɔ.

12 Baabole zannezanna seŋ ka o de yɛlɛ na oŋ zanne kyaare Jehoova na tonɔ neŋ toma o zemmo poɔŋ. Azuŋ, sobuo hoŋ na baŋ tu kyɛ somoo ka o toɔ̃ e lɛ? Sori kaŋa hoŋ na baŋ tu kyɛ e lɛ la hoŋ na wuluu lɛ na aŋ seŋ ka o maŋ teɛre yɛlɛ na oŋ zanne a kyaare Jehoova na, agaŋaazaa saŋa na oŋ kanne a Baabole zie kaŋa naŋ e kpeɛŋaa ka o woŋ a muni. Aŋa mannoo poɔŋ, saŋa na oŋ wa kanna Jehoova sɛrɛɛdiibu yɛlɛ na, aminekaŋaŋ o na teɛreŋ ka, ananso la wala ka Jehoova da sãã noba mine kyɛ bare ba mine yɛŋ. Azuŋ, sobuo hoŋ na toɔ̃ tu a somoo ka o taa teɛroŋ soŋ a kyaare a yɛlɛ ŋa?
