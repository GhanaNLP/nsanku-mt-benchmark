Soŋ Baabole Zannezanneba Ka
Ba Wa Baŋ Jehoova Soŋ Zaa
<<<P>>>
“A nyɔvore naŋ ba taa baaroo la ŋa, ka ba baŋ [ho] yoŋ naŋ waa
Naaŋmen meŋɛ.”—GYN. 17:3.
<<<P>>>
KA HO Baabole zannezannaŋ wa piili de gbɛre wa a lammozie, a
piili ire yɛlɛ nɔɛ, a manna o sagediibu yɛlɛ korɔ noba, a e siri ka o
de suobu, wala aŋ maŋ erɛ bo? Yelmeŋɛ zaa, ho poɔ maŋ pelɛŋ
soŋ. Kyɛ, a yɛlɛ ama zaa poɔŋ, te baŋɛɛŋ ka Jehoova la aŋ seŋ ka
te puore bareka, bonso o naŋ soŋ a Baabole zannezanna oŋ de a
na gbɛɛ ŋa.—1 Kor. 3:5-9; 3 Gyn. 3, 4.
<<<P>>>
2 A Baabole yeleeŋ ka Naaŋmen boɔrɔŋ “ka neɛzaa” wa “nyɛ faa-
bo a kyɛ wa baŋ a yelmeŋɛ.” (Kanne 1 Timoti 2:3, 4.) Jehoova
boɔrɔŋ ka neɛzaa baŋoo soŋ ane o poboɔbo meŋɛmeŋɛ. (Jer. 9:
24) Lɛ wuleeŋ ka aseŋ ka o soba baŋ a yelmeŋɛ. Ka ba naŋ yeli ka
neɛ kaŋa baŋɛɛ a yelmeŋɛ, wala ka lɛ wuli? Baabole gaŋbaŋna
kaŋa yeleeŋ ka: “A soba seŋ ka o de yɛlɛ na oŋ zanne baŋ ka a eɛ
yelmeŋɛ a kyaare Naaŋmen na tonɔ neŋ toma o zemmo poɔŋ, kyɛ
a ba e ŋa o soba zanne baŋɛɛ a yɛlɛ yelmeŋɛ weɛ lɛ, a ba de a tonɔ
neŋ toma.” Azuŋ, ka Baabole zannezannaŋ wa zanne baŋ Jehoo-
va yɛlɛ a bamoo soŋ zaa, lɛ yeŋ yɛlɛ na oŋ zanne na na leɛrɛɛ o
zemmo yɛlɛ. Ka o naŋ e lɛ, o na baŋ taaɛ tamaa ka o na nyɛ la “nyɔ-
vore naŋ ba taa baaroo.”—Gyn. 17:3.
<<<P>>>
3 Te puoree Jehoova bareka oŋ ko te apoɔsori ka te soŋ noba ka
ba wa bamoo soŋ zaa. A zannoo ŋa poɔŋ, te na nyɛɛ a soorebie ŋa
noɔre iribo: Ananso la wala ka Baabole zannezanneba seŋ ka ba baŋ
Jehoova soŋ zaa? Wala teŋ na baŋ e wuli ba ka ba baŋ lɛ Jehoova
naŋ waa meŋɛmeŋɛ? Sobuo teŋ na toɔ̃ tu soŋ ba ka ba de yɛlɛ na
baŋ zanne a kyaare Jehoova na tonɔ neŋ toma ba zemmo poɔŋ?
