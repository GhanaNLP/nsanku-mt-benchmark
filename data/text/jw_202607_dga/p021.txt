Te zaa boɔbo la ka nɔmmo na te Baabole zannezanneba naŋ taa ko Jehoova na tɔ ba sukyie ka ba de gbɛre ka ba na deɛ suobu. Kyɛ a ba e ŋa baŋ boɔrɔ ka te ne ba nyɔge zɔmennoŋ bee ka ba neŋ Jehoova Sieree Deme e zɔmenne kyɛbee baŋ nɔŋ a Baabole poɔ wuluu kaŋa zuŋ la baŋ boɔrɔ ka ba de suobu. Azuŋ, ka te neŋ neɛ zaaŋ wa zanna a Baabole, a yelnimizeɛ la ka te soŋ o soba ka o wa baŋ Jehoova kyɛ nɔmoo meŋ.

5 Ka Baabole zannezannaŋ wa baŋ Jehoova soŋ zaa a nɔmoo meŋ, a na eɛ mɔlɔ koo ka o kyɛllɛ wono o yɛlɛ. Yezu yeleeŋ ka: “Kyɛ N erɛ la lɛ N Saa naŋ eŋ noɔre ko ma ka ka a teŋɛzu noba na baŋ ka N nɔŋ la a Saa.” (Gyn. 14:31) Azuŋ, aŋa lɛ na Yezu naŋ yeli na, ka Baabole zannezannaŋ nɔŋ Jehoova yaga zaa, o na maŋ boɔrɔŋ ka o kyɛllɛ wono o yɛlɛ a lɛ dire yelmennoŋ koroo wagre zaa meŋ.—1 Gyn. 5:3.

6 Ka Baabole zannezannebaŋ wa baŋ Jehoova soŋ zaa, ana soŋeŋ ka ba maŋ tõɔ̃ de gbɛ-sonne. Te de yelmannaa ŋa wuli a pare: Te de ka neɛ kaŋa boɔrɔŋ ka o zanne moto zɔmmo. A soba yeleeŋ ka ka o naŋ kanne a moto gane baare, lɛ yoŋ na tõɔ̃ vɛŋeŋ ka o baŋ moto zɔmmo. Ka a sobaŋ e lɛ baare, ka lɛ o na kyileŋ a maŋ tõɔ̃ zɔŋ moto bee? Ai. Ka o sobaŋ na tõɔ̃ baŋ moto zɔmmo, aseŋ ka o de aware yaga a zanne a moto zɔmmo. Ka o naŋ e lɛ, ana somooŋ ka o baŋ a moto ziiri mine aŋ seŋ ka o de toŋ neŋ toma wagre naŋ seŋ neŋ poɔŋ. Ana la somooŋ ka o nyɛ lɛ aŋ seŋ ka o zɔnnɔ a moto neŋyaga poɔŋ ane zie noba naŋ ba e yaga. A yɛlɛ ama na tõɔ̃ somooŋ ka o kyile moto zɔmmo eŋeŋ. Lɛ meŋ la, Baabole zannezanna koŋ tõɔ̃ baŋ Jehoova soŋ a tuuro a Baabole zannoo yoŋ eŋeŋ. Kyɛ, ka o naŋ maŋ de yɛlɛ na oŋ zanna na tonõ neŋ toma o zemmo poɔŋ, ana somooŋ ka o derɛ gbɛ-sonne, o na nyɛrɛɛ popeɛloŋ o zemmo poɔŋ saŋa zaa oŋ wa de gbɛre naŋ kyaare a Baabole sowuli na. Azuŋ, saŋa na o bammo na oŋ taa kyaare Jehoova naŋ duoro saa na, o na nyɛɛ lɛ o sazu Saa na naŋ na soŋoo. A yɛlɛ ŋa zaa na vɛŋeŋ ka o maŋ boɔrɔ ka o derɛ gbɛ-sonne o zemmo yelzaa poɔŋ.

7 Gyoosɛf naŋ da baŋ Jehoova soŋ zaa na, a da soŋooŋ saŋa na Pɔtifa pɔge naŋ da bɛnloo ka o ne o gaŋneŋ taa na. Saŋa na Gyoosɛf naŋ da e pɔlbile na, o da zannɛɛ Adama ane Hawaa, Abraham ane Saara zemmo yɛlɛ. (Mun. 2:24; 20:3, 6) A kyaare a noba ŋa zemmo yɛlɛ, Gyoosɛf da zanne nyɛɛ teeroŋ na Jehoova naŋ taa a kyaare kultaa ane gaŋneŋ taa yɛlsɛgraa naŋ e kyɔɔtaa ko kultaa deme na. Azuŋ, oŋ da baŋ Jehoova teeroŋ a kyaare a yɛlɛ ŋa na, a da somooŋ oŋ zagre yɛlɛ na Pɔtifa pɔge naŋ da boɔrɔ ka o e na. Gyoosɛf da yeleeŋ ka: “Wola ka N pãã na e a yelfa-kpoŋ ŋa a sãã Naaŋmen poɔ?” (Mun. 39:7-9) Azuŋ, Gyoosɛf da baŋɛɛ yɛlɛ Jehoova naŋ nɔŋ ane yɛlɛ oŋ ba nɔŋ. O da baŋɛɛ yɛlɛ oŋ na e ka Jehoova poɔ sãã. Azuŋ, o da deɛ gbɛ-soŋ. Ka te naŋ soŋ te Baabole zannezanneba ka ba baŋ Jehoova soŋ zaa, a baŋ yɛlɛ oŋ boɔrɔ ane yɛlɛ oŋ ba boɔrɔ, ba na baŋ deɛ gbɛ-sonne ka a pɛle o poɔ.
