YƐLƐ TE NAŊ NA ZANNE
<<<P>>>
A zannoo ŋa na soŋ teŋ ka te nyɛ yɛlɛ na teŋ na baŋ zanne yi a Giibiyoŋ deme zieŋ, aneŋ yɛlɛ na Jehoova naŋ da e ko ba.
<<<P>>>
Bo Yɛlɛ Teŋ Na Baŋ Zanne Yi A Giibiyoŋ Deme Zieŋ?
<<<P>>>
‘A Giibiyoŋ teŋɛ deme ane a Izerayɛl biiri da taa la emmaaroŋ ba kpekyageŋ a laŋ zeŋ.’—JOOS. 10:1.
<<<P>>>
A IZERAYƐL biiri naŋ da yi Igyipiti a gaa ta a Noɔre Emmo Teŋgane na, a da eɛ yuomo 40. Jehoova da soŋeeŋ baŋ zɔɔtoɔ̃ Gyɛreko neŋ Ai tenne na naŋ da be a Noɔre Emmo Teŋgane naŋ. Azuŋ, noba mine naŋ da yi Keenan paaloŋ na lomboe mine da woŋ la lɛ na a Izerayɛl biiri naŋ zɔɔrɔ toɔnɔ noba na yɛlɛ. Azuŋ ba da maalɛɛ ba teɛroŋ ka ba na wa yelee yɛlɛ mine ko a Izerayɛl biiri. Ba da wa yelko la a Izerayɛl biiri ka ba yi la teŋɛ kaŋa naŋ e tɔɔre yaga zaa a wa ba zie. Ba da yeleeŋ ka, ba boɔrɔŋ ka ba ne ba nyɔge zɔmennoŋ a gaŋ ba ne ba naŋ na zɔɔ, bozuŋ ba baŋeeŋ ka Naaŋmen be la ba zieŋ.
<<<P>>>
2 Ana noba ŋa da la a Giibiyoŋ deme. Ka hooŋ kaa a Baabole poɔŋ, a dɛndɛŋ saŋa la ŋa baŋ yeli a Giibiyoŋ deme yɛlɛ. Kyɛ, a puoriŋ a Baabole la yelee ba yɛlɛ. A Baabole la wulee lɛ na a Giibiyoŋ deme neŋ a Izerayɛl biiri naŋ da laŋ zeŋ yuomo yaga. A Giibiyoŋ deme da wulee gyog-sonne yaga teŋ na baŋ tɔgle. Te na baŋ zannɛɛ Jehoova gyog-sonne meŋ a yi yɛlɛ na oŋ da e ko a Giibiyoŋ deme na poɔŋ.

A GIIBIYOŊ DEME DA TAAƐ SAGEDIIBU JEHOOVA EŊEŊ KYƐ DA SIGRI BA MENNE MEŊ
<<<P>>>

A Giibiyoŋ deme da paalɛɛ a Hivi deme poɔŋ. Ba da boɔlɛɛ be ka Giibiyoŋ. Daŋkyini da ŋmaa gyeerɛɛ a teŋɛ, baŋ da taa sogya kpeɛne meŋ yaga.
