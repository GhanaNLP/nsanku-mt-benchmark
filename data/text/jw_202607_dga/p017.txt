12 Tɔɔ, a dɛndɛŋ soba, te pãã nyɛ yɛlɛ mine
teŋ na baŋ zanne a kyaare Jehoova, saŋa na
teŋ wa kanna a Baabole. Ka hooŋ nyɛ, saŋa
kaŋa a Baabole maŋ wulee Naaŋmen gyogri
na mine daadaalɛ. (Yiib. 34:6, 7) Kyɛ wagre
mineŋ, a Baabole maŋ yelee noba mine zem-
mo yɛlɛ ane gbɛɛ mine ba naŋ de yɛlɛ. Azuŋ,
ka hooŋ kanne a noba ŋa taa yɛlɛ, teɛre gaa
tɔɔre a nyɛ lɛ hoŋ na baŋ zanne yelkaŋa a
kyaare Jehoova a yi ana yɛlɛ na pooŋ.
