<<<P>>>
A Nyɛ Zeŋ Laafeɛ Tegitegile! gane na taaɛ soorebie yaga naŋ na baŋ soŋ Baabole zannezanneba ka ba baŋ Jehoova soŋ zaa. A mine la ŋa:
<<<P>>>
“Bo teɛroŋ ka Jehoova taa kyaare noba naŋ moɔlɔ a duorisoŋ na?” (zannoo 21)
<<<P>>>
“Te zannɛɛ Jehoova ane Yezu yɛlɛ yaga. Azuŋ, wala hoŋ e baŋ ka ba na tu la dantorii sori kyɛ e a yɛlɛ ŋa?” (zannoo 33)
<<<P>>>
“Ka te naŋ e yɛŋbanneba a sagra Jehoova nɔɛ, wala oŋ maŋ teɛre? Bonso hoŋ yel lɛ?” (zannoo 34)
<<<P>>>
“Ka teneeŋ kuli neɛ naŋ ba nɔŋ Jehoova, bo teɛroŋ ka Jehoova na taa kyaare te?” (zannoo 42)
<<<P>>>
“Bo ka a Baabole sɛgre ŋa wulibo a kyaare Jehoova sudi?” (zannoo 56)
<<<P>>>
“Sobuo ka lɛ na Jehoova naŋ maŋ moɔrɔ ka o sonnɔ yelfa-erebɛ na wuli ka o eɛ Neɛ naŋ maŋ teɛrɛ yɛlɛ gɛrɛ tɔɔre, a taa nembaalonzoe, ane nɔmmo meŋ?” (zannoo 57)
<<<P>>>
maale a Nyɛ Zeŋ Laafeɛ Tegitegile! gane na, ba deɛ soorebie yaga toŋ neŋ toma aŋ na baŋ soŋ Baabole zannezanna ka o zanne Jehoova gyogri yɛlɛ gaa tɔɔre ane lɛ oŋ waa meŋɛmeŋɛ. (Kaa a daga, “Soorebie Naŋ Na Baŋ Soŋ Te Te Wuluu Poɔŋ.”) Azuŋ maŋ mɔɔ ho moɔbo zaa a de a soorebie ŋa tonɔ neŋ toma ka ho neŋ ho Baabole zannezannaŋ wa zanna boma. De a soŋ ho Baabole zannezanna ka o wa baŋ Jehoova soŋ zaa.
<<<P>>>
⁹ Ka te neŋ nobaŋ wa piili zanna a Baabole, te maŋ eŋ baŋ faŋa ka ba maŋ kanne a Baabole bebiri zaa.* Wala teŋ na e soŋ ba ka ba toɔ̃ baŋ Jehoova soŋ zaa a yi ba Baabole kannoo na poɔŋ? Te na baŋ eŋ baŋ faŋa ka wagre zaa baŋ wa kanna a Baabole, ba maŋ teɛre nyɛ ananso na Jehoova naŋ veŋ baŋ sɛge ana yɛlɛ na eŋ be, ane lɛ ana sɛgre naŋ wulo neɛ na meŋɛmeŋɛ naŋ la Jehoova ane o gyogri. Aŋa mannoo poɔŋ, nyɛ yɛlɛ na bitontona Gyimisi naŋ da zanne yi Gyoob zemmo yɛlɛ poɔŋ. Gyimisi da zannɛɛ yɛlɛ yaga a kyaare Gyoob kannyiri diibu na, kyɛ o da lɛ zanne nyɛɛŋ ka Jehoova eɛ nimimaaroŋ ane nembazo Naaŋmen.—Kanne Gyimisi 5:11.

¹⁰ Te meŋ boɔrɔŋ ka te Baabole zannezanneba maŋ toɔ̃ nyɛ yelnimizeere mine yi ba Baabole kannoo poɔŋ. Ka ba naŋ wa kanne a Baabole, aseŋ ka ba nyɛ yɛlɛ Jehoova naŋ boɔrɔ ane yɛlɛ oŋ ba boɔrɔ. Aŋa mannoo poɔŋ, ho Baabole zannezanna na nyɛɛ yɛlɛ mine yi a Nyɛ Zeŋ Laafeɛ Tegitegile! gane na zie na naŋ yeli ka “Piili Kanna A Baabole” na. Oŋ yeli ko ba ka o boɔrɔŋ ka o kanne Daaniel naŋ da be a gbeŋme bɔgiŋ zie na yɛlɛ. Te na baŋ deɛ a soorebie ŋa eŋoo faŋa, ka o teɛ-

9-10. (a) Boŋ na baŋ soŋ Baabole zannezanneba ka ba baŋ Jehoova soŋ zaa saŋa na baŋ wa kanna a Baabole ba menneŋ na? (Gyimisi 5:11) (b) Ka ho Baabole zannezannaŋ wa boɔrɔ ka o kanne neɛ kaŋa zemmo yɛlɛ a Baabole poɔŋ, bo soorebie hoŋ na baŋ de emoo faŋa ka o teɛre a yɛlɛ gaa tɔɔre? Tere yelmannaa.
