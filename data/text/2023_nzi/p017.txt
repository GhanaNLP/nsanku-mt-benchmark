Ezukoa ngyehyɛleɛ ɛyɛlɛ
<<<P>>>
19. Arane bava ezukoa ɛnyianlɛ nwo ndenle mɔɔ ɔ muala bamaa yɛ ezukoa ne ɛya-ngyɛnu 1 anzi kpɔkɛ 35 yeado Mɛlalilɛ Ayialeka ne anyunlu ɛmaa bɛanleɛnlea nu na bɛalie bɛdo nu. Ɛhye baboa wɔ yɛ ezukoa ɛnyianlɛ nwo agyakɛ ɛdulɛ ne. Ndenle ne bie mɔ a le ɛhye mɔ;
<<<P>>>
• Sigalɛte nee ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛfa tɛba bɛyɛ la awo adwule ne nzenzanleɛ bara nu amaa ɔ nee ECOWAS ɛdeɛ ne ayɛ ko, bɛbavola adwule mɔɔ bɛdie wɔ nza anu ɛsesebɛ nwo bɛadɛla mɔɔ bɛdie ye wɔ bia (nza) nwo la.
<<<P>>>
• Bɛbado vaate anu bɛavi ɛya-ngyɛnu 12 anzi kpɔkɛ 5 bɛahɔ ɛya-ngyɛnu 15. Eza bɛbayɛ nzenzanleɛ wɔ mɔɔ ɔnle kɛ bɛtua vaate wɔ la anu.
<<<P>>>
• Bɛbahakyi adwule mɔɔ bɛdie wɔ gyima gyima ɛkɛ siane biala anu bɛayɛ ye mɔɔ bɛdie ye dahuu biala mɔɔawie biala yua la.
<<<P>>>
• Bɛbade adwule mɔɔ bɛdie wɔ ezukoa dodo biala mɔɔ bɛkɔye anzɛɛ bɛkɔfa bɛto ɛkɛ la azo yeavi ɛya -ngyɛnu 1 anzi kpɔkɛ 5 yeahɔ ɛya-ngyɛnu 1. Eza bɛbamaa adenle mɔɔ ɛkola ɛye ezukoa kenle biala a.
<<<P>>>
• Ndenle gyɛne bie mɔ noko mɔɔ bɛkpondɛ kɛ bɛnea la a le ɛhye mɔ:
<<<P>>>
○ Bɛbadɔne 5G anwoma nu anwosesebɛ ne;
<<<P>>>
○ Bɛbabɔ mɔdenle bɛanlea kɛ bɛkɛli sua nwo adwule ɛlielɛ mɛla ne azo;
<<<P>>>
○ Bɛbanlea kɛ bɛkɛnyia nvasoɛ bieko bɛaboka dɛba ne mɔɔ bɛlɛ wɔ Jubilee nwole ne anwo la anwo; yɛɛ
<<<P>>>
○ Bɛbaɛ nzezanleɛ wɔ bɛ adwule mɔɔ bɛnyia la anu amaa bɛaye kakɛbɔlɛ mɔɔ ba wɔ ninyɛne mɔɔ yɛangola yɛanyɛ la anwo na bɛabu mgbonda mɔɔ fale ninyɛne bɛyɛle la angome kye anwo.
