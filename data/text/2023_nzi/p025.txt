44. Arane ngyehyɛleɛ a le kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛsɔ ngɛnlaneɛ wɔ azua mɔɔ mɔɔ nzule ɛbɛnga bɛ ɛyia nee ɛlɛka ɛleka mɔɔ bɛ ɛhɔle yɛ se la azo. Ɛhye ati yeva ndenle fofolɛ mɔɔ bɛdua zo bɛsɔ kɛnlaneɛ mɔɔ tɛ lateleke ngɛnlaneɛ anwosesebɛ ne bie a ɔhyia la yedo gua wɔ azua ɛhye mɔ azo. Bɛwie gyima mɔɔ bɛlɛyɛ ye wɔ Azizkpe, Aflive yɛɛ Alorkpem azua mɔɔ wɔ azule nye wɔ ada Aduduleɛ Maanzikyi ne mɔɔ wɔ Ngɛnla Maanzinli ne anu la yɛ.
