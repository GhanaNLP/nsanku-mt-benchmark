ƆSOAFOƆ NO ANODISƐM
<<<P>>>
Akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho sɛ afe yi bɛyɛ afe a ɔman yi kɔɔ sikasɛm mu ahokyere pa ara wɔ yɛn abakɔsɛm mu. Sɛdeɛ ɛkɔɔ so wɔ ewiase aman ahodoɔ soɔ no, yadeɛ kontonkurowi COVID-19 to hyɛɛ yɛn so wɔ afe 2020 ɔfa a ɛdi kan no mu ma sikadwuma tɔɔ ape nam so ma sika a ɛba ɔman fotoɔ mu no so teeɛ na afei nso yɛbɔɔ ɛka a na yɛn ani nna de ko tiaa yadeɛ kontonkurowi yi.
<<<P>>>
Ɛberɛ a yɛhyɛɛ aseɛ tuu Nsakraeɛ Pa Nhyehyɛeɛ ho anammɔn na yɛsusuu sɛ afei deɛ, yɛrebɔ yɛn ho mmɔden ade yɛn ho afiri yadeɛ kontonkurowi yi ho nsunsuansoɔ mu ara pɛ na ɔhaw afoforɔ bɛbɔɔ mu a, ɛnam so ama nneɛma a ɛma sikasɛm gyina yie a yɛde totoo yie firi afe 2017 kɔsi 2019 mu no nyinaa asane agye agu. Deɛ ɛdi kan yɛ Russia ne Ukraine ɔko no, deɛ ɛdi hɔ nso yɛ ɛberɛ a yɛsɛee wɔ akwanhodoɔ a yɛbɛfa so anya sika wɔ 2022 sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ mu no nnyetom ho a, ɛnam so maa amansan boseabɔfoɔ de too edwa sɛ yɛmfata sɛ wɔbɔ yɛn bosea. Afei, bɛyɛ mfeɛ edu nie, prɛko pɛ Amansan Boseabɔ ho Edwadifoɔ mpɛ sɛ wɔbɛma yɛn bosea, ɔman yi ara mu boseabɔ ho nsɛm nso bɛyɛɛ basaa ma nsiho boɔ kɔɔ soro pa ara na ɛnam so ma boseabɔ ho ɛka kɔɔ soro a, ama yɛn sika mu duru no kɔ ara na ɛrekɔ fam.
<<<P>>>
Yei kyerɛ sɛ, ɛberɛ a amannɔne sika a yɛnya firi amansan boseabɔ edwa so ne sika a ɛfiri adwadeɛ a yɛde kɔ amannɔne mu no so reteɛ, na afei ɛsiane sɛdeɛ yɛpɛ amannɔne nneɛma pa ara enti, sika a yɛde kra nneɛma firi amannɔne no nso kɔ ara na ɛrekɔ soro, na amannɔne ɛka a yɛde no nso kɔ ara na ɛrekɔ soro no, na yɛn sika mu duru no so te ara na ɛreteɛ. Yei ama asetena mu ayɛ den ama Aban ne ankorankoro nyinaa. Fango boɔ a ɛgu so rekɔ soro enti, ama nneɛma boɔ ayɛ den a bosome Ahineme mu wɔ afe 2022 mu, nneɛma-boɔ-sorokɔ kɔduruu ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduanan ne akyire pɔ ɛnan (40.4% in October 2022).
