“Yɛreka abom adi dwuma de Mpontuo ne Adwadeɛyɛ asane ama ahoɔden aba Ɔman Sikasɛm u na yɛama aseɛ atim”
<<<P>>>
Afe Awieeɛ Nneɛma Boɔ-Sorokɔ (End Year Inflation): Yei yɛ mpɛmpɛnsoɔ a nneɛma boɔ kɔduru wɔ $pɛnimma bosome mu wɔ afe pɔtee bi mu.
<<<P>>>
Dwumadie ho sika a yɛadi aboro so (Expenditure Overrun): Yei si wɔ ɛberɛ a, sika a yɛde adi dwuma ahodoɔ no aboro deɛ yɛtwa too hɔ wɔ sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ mu a mmarahyɛbadwa gye too mu no so.
<<<P>>>
Amannɔne Edwadie Sikakorabea (EXIM - Export-Import) Bank: Ghana Amannɔne Edwadie Sikakorabea (Export-Import Bank - GEXIM) yɛ sikakorabea bi a wɔnam mmara a ɛtɔ so ahankron ne dubaako (Ghana Export-Import Bank Act 2016 - Act 911) so buee wɔ ɔman yi mu wɔ afe 2016 mu a, ɛbɛboa aban wɔ adwadeɛ a yɛde kɔ amannɔne kɔtɔn ho edwadie no ama ase atim.
<<<P>>>
Adwadeɛ A Yɛde Kɔ Amannɔne ho Edwadie Asoɛeɛ Ahodoɔ (Export Trade Houses -ETH): Yeinom yɛ edwadie ho nnwuma bi a wɔboa nnwumakuo a wɔhia mmoa ne nimdeɛ wɔ amannɔne edwadie mu fa sɛdeɛ wɔn nsa bɛka adwadeɛ bi afiri amannɔne anaa wɔde adwadeɛ bi bɛkɔ amannɔne.
<<<P>>>
Sikasɛm mu Sintɔ (Fiscal Deficit): Yei yɛ nnyinasoɔ a wɔhwɛ ho ansa na ɔman bi anya ɛkwan abɔ bosea de adi dwuma a bosea ho nsiho-tua ka ho bie.
<<<P>>>
Sikasɛm ho Anammɔntuo Ahodoɔ (Fiscal Operations): Yeinom yɛ sikasɛm ho akontabuo ne amaneɛbɔ, sika ho ntotoeɛ, dwetire ho dwumadie, sikatua, akatua ho nsɛm, agyapadeɛ, dwumadie so-hwɛ ne ɛkatua.
<<<P>>>
Sikasɛm ho Mmara (Fiscal Policy): Yei yɛ ɛkwan a aban fa so de sika di dwuma ne ɛtoɔ ho nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ a ɛboa ma sikasɛm mu mpontuo nnyinasoɔ anaa nneɛma a ɛma ɔman sikasɛm gyina yie no ase tim.
