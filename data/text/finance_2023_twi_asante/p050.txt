<<<P>>>
Ɔman mu Adwadeɛ nyinaa ankasa (Real GDP): Yei yɛ susudua a wɔde hunu ɔman mu adwadeɛ nyinaa bo a ɛsom a wɔde atoto sɛdeɛ nneɛma-boo-soroko no teɛ ho.
<<<P>>>
Sika A Aban Nya (Revenue): Yei yɛ sika dodoɔ a Aban nya firi ne dwumadie ahodoɔ a ɔdi wɔ ɔman yi mu ne amannɔne.
<<<P>>>
Mmoayɛn A Ɛde Nnuane ne Nnwuma Ba -RFJ (Rearing For Food and Jobs): Yei yɛ Ghana Aban mpontuo ho dwumadie foforɔ a ɔde aba a ɛnam so bɛma ntɔtemmoayɛn adwuma no adi mu na sika aba mu sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a, ntɔtemmoa nam bɛdɔɔso ama ɔmanfoɔ anya bi awe na yɛate ntɔtemmoa nam a yɛkra ba ɔman yi mu no so koraa, na ɛnam so ama nnwuma abu so a ɔmanfoɔ bɛnya bi ayɛ ama wɔn a wɔdi ntɔtemmoa ho dwuma nyinaa no asetenam nso atu mpɔn.
<<<P>>>
Nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛde nsakraeɛ Ba (Sensitization): Yei yɛ nkyerɛkyerɛ anaa dawubɔ a ne botaeɛ ne sɛ ɛbɛma nhunumu ne adwenesakra nam so de nsakraeɛ pa aba tebea bi a ɛnyɛ mu.
<<<P>>>
Nnwuma Nketewa ne Adantam (Small and Medium-Sized Enterprises - SMEs): TYeinom yɛ ankorankoro nnwuma a ɛnhyɛ aban ase a wɔtumi fa nnipa kakra bi de di wɔn dwuma.
<<<P>>>
Asetena mu Mmoa Sika (Social Benefits): Yei yɛ sika a Aban gyina ɔmanfoɔ anan mu tua ma nsuo, pɛtro ne nkanea nnwumakuo no de sɔ Ghanafoɔ asene.
<<<P>>>
Squadron: Yei yɛ ewiem asraafoɔ ekuo bi a wɔwɔ ewiemhyɛn a wɔde di wɔn dwuma no mmienu a anaa deɛ ɛboro saa ne asraafoɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔka ewienhyɛn no.
<<<P>>>
Asenesɔ Sika (Subsidy): Yei yɛ sika mu mmoa bi a Aban de ma ɔman mma ma ɛyi adesoa kakra firi wɔn so ma wɔn kɔn mu dwo wɔn.
<<<P>>>
Dwumadie akyiridie (Surveillance): Yei yɛ ɛkwan a yɛfa so de ani di biribi akyi na yɛhwɛ sɛdeɛ ɛsi teɛ.
<<<P>>>
Asorɔkye kunini (Tidal wave): Ɛyɛ ɛpo asorɔkye a ɛso pa ara a ɛba wɔ ɛberɛ a ɛpo ase asasewosoɔ bi asi anaa ɔbotan mu awosoɔ bi akɔ so wɔ ɛpo ase.
<<<P>>>
Fannyɛ (Transparency): Yei ne sɛ Aban bɛma ɔman mma ahunu deɛ ɛrekɔ so na wɔate amanneɛbɔ nyinaa wɔ ɛberɛ ano ɛberɛ ano.
<<<P>>>
Kwantempɔm (Trunk Road): Yei yɛ lbre ɛkwan titire a mpn pii no ɛka nkuro mmienu anaa deɛ ɛboro saa, suhyɛn gyinabea, ewiemhyɛn gyinabea
