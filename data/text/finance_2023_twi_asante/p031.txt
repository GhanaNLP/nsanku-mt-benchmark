51. Afe 2022 mu no, yɛgyinaa dwumadie a wɔato edin” Ghana Nnuadua Ɛda” no so kyekyɛɛ nnua nketewa dodoɔ ɔpepem aduonu nsia, mpem aduonum enum (26.55 million seedlings) na wɔatumi atintim emu dodoɔ ɔpepem aduonu ɛnan , mpem aduanan ɛnan (24.44 million). Bio, yɛkyekyɛɛ nnua nketewa a ofram, emire, wawa, kokrodua, teak, cedrela, gmelina, cassia, eucalyptus ne mahogany ka ho bie dodoɔ ɔpepem dummienu ne mpem nkron (12.9 million) ma wɔduaeɛ.
