NSƐMFUA ASEKYERƐ (SIKASƐM NHYEHYƐEƐ MU NSƐMFUA)
<<<P>>>
Mansini Baako Afididwuma Baako (1D1F): Yei yɛ mfidinnwuma ahodoɔ a yɛbɛbuebue wɔ mansini mu na ɛnam so ama mfidinnwuma atu mpɔn wɔ Ghana. Yei botaeɛ ne sɛ nnwuma bɛbu so wɔ ɔman yi mu na Ghanafoɔ a wɔwɔ ɔman yi afanan nyinaa anya adwuma ayɛ.
<<<P>>>
Abibirem Aman mu Edwadie A Akwansideɛ Nni ho (African Continental Free Trade Area – AfCFTA): Yei yɛ edwa kɛseɛ a wɔahyehyɛ ama Abibirem Adwdeɛyɛ Nnwumakuo a ɛbɛboa ama wɔde wɔn adwadeɛ akɔ Abibirem aman so akɔtɔn a wɔrentua edwadie mu ɛtoɔ ahodoɔ biara na wɔanya edwa kɛseɛ a ɛwɔ Abibirem ha no ho mfasoɔ.
<<<P>>>
Ɔhaw-anosie (Alleviation): Yei yɛ ɛkwan wɔfa so ma ɔhaw bi ho nsunsuansoɔ ano brɛ ase.
<<<P>>>
Sika Mu Nkyekyɛmu (Allocation): Yei yɛ ɛkwan a wɔfa so kyekyɛ ɔman sika mu ma Aban asoɛeɛ ahodoɔ no ne nnwumakuo ahodoɔ no ma wɔde di dwuma ma ɔmanfoɔ.
<<<P>>>
Aboadeɛ (Ancilliary): Yei yɛ mmoa anaa akadeɛ a ɛboa ma tebea bi kɔ yie.
<<<P>>>
Afadeɛ (Apparel): Afadeɛ anaa atadeɛ pɔtee bi a wɔtɔn wɔ sotɔɔ mu.
<<<P>>>
Amannɔne Edwadie ho Gyinapin (Balance of Payments): Yei yɛ edwadie a ɛkɔ so wɔ ɔman bi ne ewiase aman afoforɔ ntam wɔ ɛberɛ pɔtee bi mu sɛ ebia, abosome mmiɛnsa biara wɔ afe no mu anaa mpo afe no nyinaa mu no ho anodisɛm anaa nkontabuo.
<<<P>>>
Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ (Budget): Sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ gyina hɔ ma nhyehyɛeɛ a Aban ayɛ afa ɛkwan a ɔbɛfa so anya sika ne deɛ ɔde saa sika no bɛyɛ ho. Nhwɛsoɔ ne sɛ, abusua bi bɛtumi ayɛ sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ nam so akyerɛ sika dodoɔ a wɔwɔ ne nneɛma ahodoɔ a ɛhia sɛ wɔde sika no yɛ no nnidisoɔ nnidisoɔ. Ɛbɛtumi ayɛ ɛdan ho ɛka, nsuo ne nkanea ɛka enna aduane ho ɛka) Ɛkɔba sɛ wɔhia sika no dodoɔ a, ɛho bɛhia sɛ abusua no bɛhwehwɛ sika no bi aka ho anaa mpo wɔbɛkɔ akɔbɔ bosea.
