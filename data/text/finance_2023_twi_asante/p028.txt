Adwumanya ho Nhyehyɛeɛ
<<<P>>>
45. Ɛduruu Ahineme 2022 mu no, na Mmabunu Adwumanya Adwumakuo (Youth Employment Agency) no, agyina ne nkorabata ahodoɔ no so afa nnipa dodoɔ Mpem aduɔwɔtwe, ahanson aduasa enum (80,735).
<<<P>>>
46. Afe 2022 mu, Aban faa Ɔman Adwumanya ho ɛkwan foforɔ nhyehyɛeɛ (National Alternative Employment and Livelihood Programme (NAELP); no so tuu anammɔn a ɛdidi soɔ yi:
<<<P>>>
• Siesiee nsase a asɛe, sane duaa nnua wɔ asase dodoɔ hɛkta ahansia aduanan enum (645) so nam so maa adwumanya ho akwannya dodoɔ mpem mmienu ahasa.; na afei
<<<P>>>
• Guu nnua aba dodoɔ ɔpepem aduonu (20 million) de boaa Afuoyɛ Mpontuo Dwumadie (plantation development programme) no nam so maa mmabunu mpem aduɔwɔtwe aduɔson baako (80,071) a wɔwɔ Akotom, Adinkra, Techire, Akwatiakwaso, Fufuo ne Hohoe mu nyaa afebɔɔ ne animanim yi ara nnwuma (permanent and casual jobs) yɛeɛ.
<<<P>>>
47. Afe 2023 mu nso, Aban bɛkɔ so ayɛ deɛ ɛdidi soɔ yi:
<<<P>>>
• Bɛduadua nnua nam so asiesie asase hekta dodoɔ apem (1000) a asɛe wɔ mpɔtam pɔtee ahodoɔ bi mu, atintim nnua a wɔtumi nya mu sika sɛ ebia kube, abɛ, sitrɔs (ankaa) ne amango dodoɔ ɔpepem de aboa ama afuoyɛ mpontuo (plantation development) akɔ nkan asane de asi nnua dodoɔ a wɔatwitwa anan mu;
<<<P>>>
• Bɛbuebue beaeɛ ahodoɔ edu a wɔtintim nnua nketewa nam so ama nnipa dodoɔ mpem ɔha anya nnwuma ayɛ; na afei
<<<P>>>
• Yɛbɛma wɔn a wɔyɛ galamsey seesei ne wɔn a wɔnya bi a wɔbɛyɛ dodoɔ mpem aduonu (20,000) ne fam agudeɛtuo adwuma nketewa dodoɔ ahanu (200 small-scale mining companies) nteteeɛ afa fagudeɛtuo ho apɔmuden ho (mine health), ahobanbɔ nhyehyɛeɛ (safety standard) ne ɛkwan
