SIKA A ƐBAA ƆMAN FOTOƆ MU
<<<P>>>
8. Sika dodoɔ a ɛbaa aban fotoɔ mu ne deɛ wɔde kyɛɛ ɔman no kɔsii ɔpepem aduosia enum, ahasa ne aduɔkron nkron a ɛyɛ ɔman mu adwadeɛ nyinaa ɔha mu nkyɛmu dubaako (GH¢65,399 million - 11.0 percent of GDP), a na deɛ ɛsi ani soɔ sɛ nsa bɛka yɛ ɔpepem aduosia nson , ahasa ne nson a ɛyɛ ɔman mu adwadeɛ nyinaa mu aha mu nkyɛmu dubaako ne akyire pɔ ɛnan (GH¢67,307 - 11.4 percent of GDP) na wode toto saa ɛberɛ korɔ no ara ho wɔ afe 2021 mu a, yɛn nsa kaa ɔpepem aduanan nkron, ɔha ne nwɔtwe a ɛyɛ ɔman mu adwadeɛ nyinaa mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu edu ne akyire pɔ nson (GH¢49,108 million (10.7 percent of GDP).
<<<P>>>
9. Sɛ yɛde Sika Dodoɔ ankasa a ɛbaa fotoɔ mu toto deɛ yɛde sii yɛn ani so sɛ yɛbɛnya no ho a, na ɛda adi sɛ sika no so tee ɔpepem baako, ahankron ne nkron anaa ɔha mu nkyɛmu mmienu ne akyire pɔ nwɔtwe (GH¢1,909 million or 2.8 percent) ne sikasɛm mu mpontuo ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduasa mmiɛnsa ne akyire pɔ mmienu (33.2 percent). Sika nkamu yi a yɛn nsa anka no firi sika ahodoɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ ɛba wɔ ɔman no nkorabata ahodoɔ no mu a antumi amma no enti.
