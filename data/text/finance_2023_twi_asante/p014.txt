11. Sika dodoɔ a Aban de dii dwuma a ɛka a yɛde dada ne sika afoforɔ bi ka ho bio kɔduruu ɔpepem ɔha ne nkron, ahanan ne aduonu baako a ɛyɛ ɔman mu adwadeɛ nyinaa mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu dunwɔtwe ne akyire pɔ enum (GH¢109,421 million -18.5 percent of GDP), na yei boroo sika dodoɔ ɔpepem ɔha ne mmiɛnsa, ahankron aduɔkron mmienu (GH¢103,992 million) a ɛyɛ ɔman mu adwadeɛ nyinaa mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu dunson ne akyire pɔ nsia (17.6 percent of GDP) a ɛyɛ sika dodoɔ a yɛde sii yɛn ani so sɛ yɛde bɛdi dwuma no so ɔha mu nkyɛmu enum ne akyire pɔ mmienu. Dwumadie ho sika a ɛboroo soɔ kakra no farebae ne Nsiho ne Sika a yɛtua ma Aban Nwumakuo ahodoɔ.
