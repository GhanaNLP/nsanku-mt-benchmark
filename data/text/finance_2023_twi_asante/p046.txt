<<<P>>>
Sikasɛm mu Ahohiahia (Fiscal Stress): Yei yɛ tebea anaa susudua bi a yɛde hunu mpɛmpɛnsoɔ a mansisi bi akɔ mu wɔ sikasɛm mu ahohiahia anaa ahokyere mu a ɛho kɔhia sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ Ɔman no de mmoa sononko kɔ hɔ a ɛremma mansini korɔ no nyɛ deɛ wɔpɛ.
<<<P>>>
Fa-me-kwa Ntoasoɔ Sukuukorɔ (FREE SHS): Fa-me-kwa Ntoasoɔ Sukuukorɔ yi yɛ Aban nhyehyɛeɛ foforɔ a Ɔmanpanin Nana Akuffo-Addo aban de puee edwa wɔ Ɛbɔ bosome mu wɔ afe 2017 mu sɛ JHS osukuuni biara a ɔbɛbɔ ne ho mmɔden wɔ sɔhwɛ mu no, ɔbɛnya ɛkwan wɔ Ntoasoɔ Sukuu mu akɔsua adeɛ a ɔrentua hwee.
<<<P>>>
Ghana Ɔhwɛpa Dwumadie (Ghana Cares): Dwumadie yi wɔ nhyehyɛeɛ mmienu a ɛyɛ anammɔn ahodoɔ a Aban adi kan atu wɔ Coronavirus Nsunsuansoɔ Anosie Dwumadie ho no ntoasoɔ.
<<<P>>>
Sika a yɛde kyɛ Aban (Grants): Yeinom yɛ sika ho mmoa ahodoɔ a Mpontuo mu Aboafoɔ de boa Aban a ɛnyɛ adeɛ a Aban bɛtua.
<<<P>>>
Sika a wɔtua ma Aban Nnwuma ahodoɔ (Grants to other Government Units): Yei gyina hɔ ma sika a, mmara hyɛ sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wɔtua kɔgu Mansini Mmarahyɛbadwa Fotoɔ “DACF” no mu, Lɔrekwan Fotoɔ “Road Fund” mu, Nwomasua Fotoɔ “GETFUND” mu, Fango ho Dwumadie Fotoɔ “Petroleum Related Fund” ne Ɔman Apɔmuden ho Fotoɔ “National Health Fund” mu.
<<<P>>>
Ɔman mu Adwadeɛ Nyinaa Bo A Ɛsom (Gross Domestic Product (GDP): Yei kyerɛ nneɛma a yɛyɛ wɔ ɔman yi mu wɔ ɛberɛ pɔtee bi mu no nyinaa bo a ɛsom.
<<<P>>>
Amannɔne Sika dodoɔ A Ɔman Wɔ (Gross International Reserves):Yei yɛ susudua a yɛfa so hunu sɛdeɛ ɔman bi sikasɛm ahoɔden a ɔwɔ sɛ ɔbɛtumi atɔ nneɛma afiri amannɔne no teɛ wɔ ɛberɛ pɔtee bi mu. Yei boa ma yɛde ɔman bi fotoɔ toto ɔman foforɔ bi deɛ ho a yɛhunu sɛdeɛ ɔman no sikasɛm ahoɔden a ɔwɔ no teɛ.
