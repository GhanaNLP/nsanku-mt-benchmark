20. Afe 2023 mu no, sika dodoɔ a ɛsi Aban ani so sɛ ɔbɛgyegye wɔ sikanya nhyehyɛeɛ ne akyɛdeɛ ahodoɔ mu no ano som Ghana sidi ɔpepem ɔha aduanan mmiɛnsa ahankron aduonum nsia GH¢143,956 million (Ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ nyinaa bo a ɛsom mu ɔha nkyɛmu edu nwɔtwe akyiripɔ hwee(18.0% of GDP) na yei nyinasoɔ bɛyɛ sikanya ɛkwan a ɛbɛwɔ hɔ afebɔɔ-a ne titire ne ɛtogyegyeɛ nhyehyɛeɛ - na anoboɔ bɛsom Ɔman mu adwadeɛ nyinaa bo a ɛsom no mu ɔha nkyɛmu baako akyiri pɔ aduasa enum(1.35 percent of GDP. ).

21. Ɔman yi mu sika dodoɔ ne sika akyɛdeɛ a yɛbɛgyegye no ano ɛboɔ bɛsom Ghana sidi ɔpepem ɔha aduanan baako ahanum aduonum mmienu (GH¢141,552), na yei da afe mu sikasɛm kankorɔ a ɛyɛ ɔha nkyɛmu aduanan nsia akyiri pɔ nsia adi, na ɛwɔ soro sene deɛ yɛde ani bu faa afe 2022 ho no deɛ so. Mpontuo mu aboafoɔ sika akyɛdeɛ a wɔde bɛma no ano som Ghana sidi ɔpepem mmienu aha ɛnan ne mmiɛnsa (GH¢2,403 million) (Ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ nyinaa bo a ɛsom mu ɔha nkyɛmu hwee akyiri pɔ enum (0.5% of GDP).
