25. Saa ɔfa yi da mmɔden a Aban rebɔ fa dwumadie sononko (government flagship projects) a ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ wɔ asoɛeɛ ahodoɔ mu wɔ afe 2022 mu no ho ne mpontunnwuma a wahyehyɛ (planned activities) sɛ ɔbɛdi wɔ afe 2023 mu no adi.
<<<P>>>
26. Saa dwumadie ne anammɔn ahodoɔ yi nyinaa gyina Aban Animanim yi ara mu Ɔman Mpontuo Dwumadie Nhyehyɛeɛ (Government’s Medium-Term National Development Policy Framework, 2022-2026) no so. Dwumadie ahodoɔ yi botaeɛ ne sɛ ɛbɛboa ama Aban atumi aduru botaeɛ ahodoɔ a ɛfa Ghana COVID 19 ne Ankorankorɛ Nnwuma mu Nkanyan ho Mmoa (Ghanacares) ho no ne anammɔntuo a ɛwɔ Apam a wɔayɛ afa nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛwɔ Amanaman Nkabom Ekuo Mpontuo nhyehyɛeɛ (UN Agenda 2030, SDG’s) ne Abibirem Nkabom Kuo Botaeɛ 2023 (African Union Agenda 2063) mu ho no ho.
<<<P>>>
27. Saa ɔfa yi da Aban mmɔden a wabɔ wɔ Ɔman yi mmeaeɛ ne Asoɛeɛ ahodoɔ mu wɔ afe 2022 mu ne dwuma a ɔbɛdi wɔ afe 2023 mu adi.

28. Aban botaeɛ a ɛsi n’ani so sɛ ɔbɛma nnua mfudeɛ (tree crops) ho mfasoɔ adɔɔso enti no, ɔmaa akuafoɔ nsa kaa ɛho ntutɛ nnua nketewa dodoɔ Ɔpepem mmienu ne ahanson (2.7 million) wɔ afe 2022 wɔ kuayɛ ɛberɛ no mu.
