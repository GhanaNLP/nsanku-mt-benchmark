13. Aban sikasɛm ho dwumadie wɔ ɛberɛ a yɛreka ho asɛm yi mu no kɔsii sɛ yɛdii sika boroo deɛ na yɛde asi yɛn ani soɔ no so. Sika a yɛdi boroo soɔ no kɔsii ɔpepem aduanan ɛnan ne aduonu mmienu a ɛyɛ ɔman mu adwadeɛ nyinaa mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu nson ne akyire pɔ ɛnan (GH¢44,022 million - 7.4% of GDP) a na deɛ yɛde asi yɛn ani so sɛ yɛde bɛdi dwuma no yɛ ɔpepem aduasa nsia, ahansia ne aduɔwɔtwe ɛnan a ɛyɛ ɔman mu adwadeɛ nyinaa mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu nsia ne akyire pɔ mmienu (GH¢36,684 million - 6.2% of GDP). Yɛnyaa sika a na yɛbɛhia asɔ yɛn sika soɔ dodoɔ no ara firii ɔman yi mu. Amannɔne ɛka nso a yɛtuaa no kɔduruu ɔpepem nsia, ahanum ne aduasa baako a ɛyɛ ɔman mu adwadeɛ nyinaa mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu baako ne akyire pɔ baako (GH¢6,531 million - 1.1% of GDP).
