Megyina Ɔmanpanin Nana Addo Danquah Akuffo-Addo anan mu hyɛ Ghanafoɔ nyinaa bɔ sɛ Aban reyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ a ɛbɛboa ama nneɛma a ɛma ɔman sikasɛm gyina yie no ase atim na ama ɔman sikasɛm asane apagya, yɛatumi anya sika agu ɔman fotoɔ mu, yɛde aban nnwuma mu nsakraeɛ ho anammɔntuo adi dwuma, yɛatrɛ abɛɛfo mfidie ne dwumadie ho akwanhodoɔ mu, yɛate amannɔne nneɛma a ɛho nhia a yɛkra ba ɔman yi mu no so, yɛayɛ adwadeɛ a yɛde kɔ amannɔne dodoɔ, yɛatrɛ ɔman yi mu adwadeɛyɛ nnwuma mu a wɔbɛyɛ adwadeɛ a yɛkra firi amannɔne no bi wɔ ɔman yi ara mu na afei yɛabɔ mmɔborɔwafoɔ ne wɔn a wɔayi wɔn totwene wɔ asetenam no ho ban.
