16. Animanim Sikasɛm ho Nhyehyɛeɛ Botaeɛ Ahodoɔ mu Akwankyerɛ no enti, Aban de asi n’ani so sɛ ne nsa bɛka Sikasɛm mu Mpontuo Nnyinasoɔ a ɛdidi soɔ yi wɔ ɛberɛ tiawa a ɛdi yɛn anim (2023-2026) yi mu:
<<<P>>>
i. Ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ nyinaa kankorɔ ɔha mu nkyɛmu ɛnan ne akyire pɔ mmiɛnsa;
<<<P>>>
ii. Ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ a Fango nka ho bie kankorɔ ɔha mu nkyɛmu ɛnan;
<<<P>>>
iii. Afe awieeɛ nneɛma-boɔ-sorokɔ ɔha mu nkyɛmu nwɔtwe a ɛbɛtumi akɔ soro anaa ɛso ate aba fam ɔha mu nkyɛmu mmienu;
<<<P>>>
iv. Anyɛ bie koraa no, sika dodoɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ ɛka yɛn fotoɔ yɛ ɔman mu adwadeɛ nyinaa mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu baako ne akyire pɔ mmiɛnsa wɔ ɛberɛ a ɛdi yɛn anim (2023-2026) yi mu; ɛnna afei
<<<P>>>
v. Amannɔne Sika Dodoɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ yɛnya de kra nneɛma bɛyɛ abosome ɛnan.
<<<P>>>
17. Ɛsiane sikasɛm mu mpontuo nnyinasoɔ botaeɛ ne animanim yi ara mu sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ botaeɛ ahodoɔ enti, yɛde nneɛma ɛma ɔman sikasɛm gyina yie ho botaeɛ ahodoɔ a ɛdidi soɔ yi asi yɛn ani so sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ yɛn nsa ka wɔ afe 2023 mu:
<<<P>>>
i. Ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ nyinaa kankorɔ ɔha mu nkyɛmu mmienu ne akyire pɔ nwɔtwe;
<<<P>>>
ii. Ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ a Fango nka ho bie kankorɔ ɔha mu nkyɛmu mmiɛnsa;
<<<P>>>
iii. Ɔpɛnimma bosome awieeɛ nneɛma-boɔ-sorokɔ ɔha mu nkyɛmu dunwɔtwe ne akyire pɔ nkron;
<<<P>>>
iv. Anyɛ bie koraa no, sika dodoɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ ɛka yɛn fotoɔ yɛ ɔman mu adwadeɛ nyinaa mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu hwee ne akyire pɔ nson; ɛnna
<<<P>>>
v. Amannɔne Sika Dodoɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ yɛnya de kra nneɛma boro abosome mmiɛnsa ne nnansa.
