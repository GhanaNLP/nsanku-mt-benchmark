31. Afe 2023 mu, Aban bɛhwɛ ama wɔadua, na wɔakyekyɛ ateaa (cashew), nkuto (shea), kube(coconut), abɛ (oil palm), mamgoo (mango), ne rɔba (rubber) dodoɔ ɔpepem enum ne aduosia enum (5.65million) a ɛso dɔsɔɔ de ama akuafoɔ dodoɔ mpem ɔha aduɔson enum ( 175,000).

32. Afe 2022 mu no, Aban gyinaa “Mansini Baako Adekoradan Baako Nhyehyɛeɛ (“One District One Warehouse” programme) no so wiee mfudeɛ adekoradan dodoɔ aduosia enum (65 warehouses) nam so de mɛtrik tɔn dodoɔ mpem aduɔwɔtwe kaa ɔman ahoɔden a yɛde kora aburoo sɛso nnuane no ho, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyi nnɔbaeɛ a wɔtwa a ɛsɛe kwa no afiri hɔ, ama akuafoɔ nsa aka wɔn adwuma no ho mfasoɔ.

34. Ɛsiane sɛdeɛ nnansa yi ɔyɛasaseyie a wɔafra mu (inorgani fertiliser) ho yɛɛ den ma ne ɛboɔ nso kɔɔ soro pa ara ɛsiane ewiase nyinaa mu ɔhaw a ɛrekɔ so no enti no, Aban apagya adwadifoɔ a wɔtɔn ɔyɛasaseyie a wɔmfraa mu (Organiɔ fertiliser) no kyɛfa a wɔwɔ mu sɛdeɛ ɛmma dodoɔ a ɛbɛhia no ntɔ sini. Saa anammɔntuo yi aboa ama kuayɛ akɔ nkan, ayi ɛkɔm afiri hɔ.
