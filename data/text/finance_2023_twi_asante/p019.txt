Sɛdeɛ Yɛde Sika bɛdi Dwuma
<<<P>>>
19. Sikanya nhyehyɛeɛ no akyiri no, Aban bɛtu sɛdeɛ yɛde sika bɛdi dwuma ho nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ a ɛdidi soɔ yi ho anammɔn de aboa sikasɛm mu mpontuo:
<<<P>>>
• Bɛto Asetenam Mmoa A Ɛko Tia Ohia (LEAP) sika no mu afiri sidi ahanan aduonum (Ghs45) no a yɛtua no bosome biara no akɔduru Ghana sidi aduɔkron (Ghs90), na yɛapagya nnipa dodoɔ a yɛtua ma wɔn no mu afiri nnipa mpem ahasa aduanan ɛnan, ɔha ne aduɔwɔtwe enum (344,185). Saa nsakraeɛ ahodoɔ yi bɛpagya afe 2022 Mmoa A Ɛko Tia Ohia (LEAP) sika no mu afiri Ghana sidi ɔpepem ɔha aduɔkron nson ahanum (Ghs197.5) no akɔduru Ghana sidi ɔpepem ahasa aduɔkron enum (Ghs395 million);
<<<P>>>
• Bɛpagya sika a wɔtua ma wɔn a wɔnoa sukuu adidiekwa ho aduane no mu ama deɛ wɔde bɛka ho no enti akɔduru Ghana sidi ɔpepem ɔha aduasa nwɔtwe (Ghs138 million),
<<<P>>>
• Yɛbɛtoa so atete Ɔmanpanin, Ɔmanpanin Abadiakyire, Asoafoɔ, Asoafoɔ Abadiakyire, Nkuropɔn, Nkuro, ne Nkuro nketewa mu Asoafoɔ, ne afoforɔ bi a Aban dwumadie sononko hyehyɛ wɔn nsa, a ɔman nnwumakuo a ɛdi dwuma pɔtee bi (State Owned Enterprises) mu mpanimfoɔ ka ho bie akatua so ɔha nkyɛmu aduasa;
<<<P>>>
• Yɛde sikanya ho ɛtoɔ dodoɔ a yɛte firi sika a yɛtwa to hɔ de di dwuma bi so no bɛba fam afiri ɔha nkyɛmu aduonu enum a seesei yɛte no aba ɔha nkyɛmu edu nson akyiri pɔ enum;
<<<P>>>
• Enkɔsi afe 2023 awieeɛ no, yɛbɛtwe sika a yɛtwa to hɔ de di dwuma bi no nyinaa agu Abɛɛfo Sika ho Ntotoeɛ ne Amaneɛbɔ Ɛkwan so no so (GIFMIS) na yɛahwɛ sɛ wɔbɛfa Abɛɛfo Sika ho Ntotoeɛ ne amaneɛbɔ Ɛkwan no (GIFMIS) so
