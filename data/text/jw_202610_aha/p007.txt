Sɛ yekile anyinlɩzɔlɛ wɔ mɔɔ yelɛ yɩ nɩ nwu a, ɩma yɛ anyɩ de
(Kila nkyɩkyɛmi 1)
<<<P>>>
(1) Mɩnla ti mɔɔ ɩsɛ kɛɛ yekile anyinlɩzɔlɛ wɔ mɔɔ mmenii mvʋlɔ bɛyɛ bɛma yɛ nɩ nwu a? (2) Mɩnla yɛ ɩkɔwʋwa yɛ ma yekehile anyinlɩzɔlɛ wɔ mɔɔ yɛlɛ yɩ nɩ nwu a? (3) Mɩnla ɔhvanɩ zʋ yɛ yɛkɔhʋla yekehile anyinlɩzɔlɛ wɔ nninyeni mɔɔ Gyehova nɩ mmenii mvʋlɔ bɛyɛ bɛma yɛ nɩ nwu a?
<<<P>>>
MƖNLA TI MƆƆ ƖSƐ KƐƐ YEKILE ANYINLƖZƆLƐ WƆ NNINYENIBA MƆƆ YƐWƆ NƖƖ MƆƆ MMENII MVƲLƆ BƐYƐ BƐMA YƐ NƖ NWU A?
<<<P>>>
4 Sɛ yɛáhile nninyeni nwʋ anyinlɩzɔlɛ a, ɩdɩ asianɩ kezile ɩma yɛ. Bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛáhile nninyeni nwʋ anyinlɩzɔlɛ nɩ, eyile bela bɛ anyɩ iále. Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ kã kɛɛ: “Abiye mɔɔ ɩyɛ nwɔkɛ mɛyɩ, eyile bela ídɩ baaba ɩma yɩ.” (Ɛyɛ. 15:15) Iwɔ numɔ, itwu mmɩlɩ be a, ye munwatĩĩ yeyiyia ndwokɔzɩzɩbɛ wɔ yɛ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu mɔɔ ɩkʋla ikolɔ yɛ abɔkɔ. Kɛ mmɩlɩ munwatĩĩ yɛ yɛkɔhʋla yekeli yɛ ndwokɔzɩzɩbɛ mɔɔ yeyiyia nɩ nwu egyima a, naasʋ̃ kezimɔ yɛkɛyɛ yɛ dɩyɛ wɔ nwʋmɔ nɩ gyĩ yɛzayɛ yɛ zʋ. Meti sɛ yɛfa yɛ adwɩnɩ yesiye nninyeni tanɩ mɔɔ ɩkɔlʋ zʋlɔ wɔ yɛ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu nɩ zʋ a, ɩkɛma yɛkɛdɩ nyanlɩ kɛɛ eyile bela “ídɩ baaba” ɩma yɛ anzɛnyɩɩ ɩkɛma adwɩnɩndwɩnɩ kɛhyɛ yɛ zʋ. Sɛ ɩbayɩ zɔ a, ɩkɛma ɩkɛyɛ sɩ kɛɛ yɛkɛva yɛ adwɩnɩ yekeziye nninyeniba mɔɔ yɛ sa kã yɩ wɔ yɛ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu nɩ zũ na ɩkɔhʋla ɩkɛzɩkɛ yɛ anyɩnlɩlelɛ.
<<<P>>>
5 Sɛ yekile nninyeni nwʋ anyinlɩzɔlɛ a, yenyia zvlɔ mfasʋ. Sɛ yekile nninyeni nwʋ anyinlɩzɔlɛ a, ɩma yɛ anyɩ de paa. Ɛyɛlɛwulɛ 15:15 yɩ ɔfʋã mɔɔ itualɩ yɩ tʋ nɩ kã kɛɛ: “Enii mɔɔ ɩwɔ anyɩnlɩlelɛ ahʋnlɩnɩ mɛyɩ, eyile bela ɩwɔ ɔbʋnlɩdʋlɛ tʋ.” Meti, kɛmɔ Ɛhɛlɛledwokɔ yɩ ma yɛnwʋ yɩ nɩ, enii mɔɔ ɩfa yɩ adwɩnɩ isiye nninyeniba mɔɔ ɩkɔlʋ zʋlɔ wɔ yɩ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu nɩ zʋ, na ikile nwʋmɔ anyinlɩzɔlɛ nɩ, yɩ anyɩ de paa. Sianti yɛ lɛ kɛɛ, ɩáma ndwokɔzɩzɩbɛ mɔɔ iyiyiaa wɔ yɩ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu nɩ ide yɩ adwɩnɩ. Eyi ma yɛnwʋ yɩ kɛɛ ɩyɛ mɛlɛkɛ ɩma abiye mɔɔ ikile nninyeni nwʋ anyinlɩzɔlɛ nɩ kɛɛ ikenyia anyɩnlɩlelɛ, dɩkɛɛ abiye mɔɔ “eyile bela ɩwɔ ɔbʋnlɩdʋlɛ tʋ” nɩ.
