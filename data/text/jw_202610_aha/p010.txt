Kɛ ιkɛyɛ na wɔama mmenii mvulɔ beanwʋ yι kɛɛ wʋ anyι sɔ mɔɔ bɛyɛ bɛma wʋ nι a?
(Kila nkyɩkyɛmi 14)
<<<P>>>
yɛ nyɛlιyɛ zʋ yɛma Gyehova nwʋ kɛɛ yɛ anyι sɔ mɔɔ ιyɛ ιma yɛ nι. Akʋ̃ sʋ̃, ιsɛ kɛɛ yɛkakɛ kɛɛ Gyehova pɛ kɛɛ yekile anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ wɔ mɔɔ mmenii mvʋlɔ bɛyɛ bɛma yɛ nι nwʋ. Bɛmá yehila kezimɔ yɛkɔhʋla yekoluwa yɛ nyɛlɩyɛ nu yɛ munwa ndwokɔ zʋ yekehile kɛɛ yɛ anyι sɔ mɔɔ Gyehova nɩɩ mmenii mvʋlɔ bɛyɛ bɛma yɛ nι.
<<<P>>>
¹⁴ Duwa wʋ munwa ndwokɔ zʋ kile mɔɔ mmenii mvʋlɔ bɛyɛ bɛma wʋ nι nwʋ anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ. Mmɩlɩ bela mɔ mmenii mvʋlɔ zʋ̃ kunlumiba nu kɛyɛ debe anzɛnyɩɩ kɛhã odwokɔ be mɔɔ ιhyɛ ɛzιzιbɛ kehile wʋ nι, kakɛ kɛɛ ɛkɛla bɛ asι wɔ bo kunlumiba nι nwʋ. Itwu mmɩlɩ be a, sa ɔkʋla ɛsɛndɩ mɛsegyi, email, anzɛnyɩɩ ɛkɛlɛ kɛlɛta ɛfa da bɛ asι. Kɛ ndianwʋ nι, sa ɔkʋla ɛkɛlɛ odwokɔ be fa da asafo nu ebani be mɔɔ ιya thyɛlι ɛ nɩɩ wʋ abusunwa yι ɛzιzιbɛ nι asι. (1 Tɛs. 5:12, 13) lwɔ numɔ la a, sa ɔkʋla da asafo nu mbanimbani bɛ yɩmmɔ sʋ̃ asι. Bɛfata kɛɛ yɛda bɛ asι iluwakɛɛ, bɛma hvani ma bo hũmmɔ fa mmɩlɩ mɔ ankɩ bɛ nɩɩ bɛ kɔwɔ numɔ kɛyɛ nninye-ni be nι kɔ kyela mmeliyema yι na bɛhyɛ bɛ ɛzιzιbɛ.

14. Mɩnla ɔhuanɩ yɛ ɔkɔhʋla koluwa zulɔ kɛla mmenii mvulɔ asɩ wɔ mɔɔ bɛyɛ bɛma wʋ nι nwʋ a? (Kila mfonyini yi sõ.)
<<<P>>>
¹⁵ Enii mɔɔ ιsɛ kɛɛ yɛda yι asι paa yɛ lɛ Gyehova. Kɛ yɛkɛyɛ yι yekezia a? Yɛyɛ zɔ iluwa mpayɩ mɔɔ yɛbɔ nι zʋ. (Ɛfɩ. 6:18) Mbɔmɩ sũmaa nι, sɛ yɛbɔɔ mpayɩ a, yɛfa yɛ ahyinyiadɩ yetiã Gyehova anyunu anzɛnyɩɩ yesiã yι kɛnɩ mmʋwalɩyɛ. Iyɛ a, yesiã yι kɛɛ ιwʋwá yɛ ma yegyina odwodwudiyialɛ be mɔɔ yeyiaa nι yi munwa, ιmá nyɛlɛbɛ yɛ ma yɛva yezizi abɔkɔba, anzɛnyɩɩ ιvá yɛ tanɩ be mɔɔ yɛyɛ nι ιhyɛ yɛ. Naasʋ̃ íni kɛɛ yɛma yɛ ɛyɛlɛ fi kɛ yɛkɛla Gyehova asɩ wɔ nninyeniba mɔɔ ιyɛ ιma yɛ nι nwʋ. (Ndw. 95:2) Isɛ kɛ yekila kɛ yɛ mpayɩwɔlɛ ɩlɛyɛ dɩyɛzianlɛ nkʋ̃ la, mfomi ιsɛ kɛ yɛda Gyehova asι sʋ̃ wɔ yɛ mpayɩwɔlɛ nu.
<<<P>>>
¹⁶ Duwa wʋ nyɛlɩyɛ zʋ kile mɔɔ mmenii mvʋlɔ bɛyɛ bɛma wʋ nɩ nwʋ anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ. Sɛ abiye zʋ̃ kunlumiba nu yɛ debe ma wʋ a, duwa wʋ nyɛlɩyɛ zʋ kile kɛɛ wʋ anyι sɔ mɔɔ ιyɛ ιma wʋ nι. Gyisɛsɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ sɛ yɛyɛ baaba a, ιma yɛ anyι de, meti sɛ yɛyɛ debe yɛfa yekile kɛ yɛ anyι sɔ mɔɔ mmenii mvulɔ bɛyɛ bɛma yɛ nɩ a, sa ιma yɛzaye sʋ̃ yɛ anyɩ de. (Egy. 20:35) Ɔzɔ mɔɔ ɛkɛyɛ nɩ
