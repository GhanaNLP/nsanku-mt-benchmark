Bɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ eyile bela ekele mmɩlɩ ɔkɔdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ nninyeni mɔɔ Gyehova ɩyɛ ɩma wʊ nɩ nwʊ (Kila nkyɩkyɛmi 9-10)
<<<P>>>
mpayɩ na yɛda yɩ asɩ wɔ nninyeni mɔɔ ɩyɛ ɩma yɛ nɩ nwʊ a, ɩlɛ́ma adwɩnɩndwɩnɩ kɛhyɛ yɛ zʋ sɔ̃maa. Mɩnla ti mɔɔ yɛkãã zɔ a? Iluwakɛɛ, sɛ yekile anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ wɔ mɔɔ yɛlɛ yɩ nɩ nwʊ a, ɩlɛ́ma yɛkɔdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ mɔɔ ɩkɔdʋ yɛ wɔ ɛhyɩ̃ be nɩ nwʊ, na mfomi yɛkɛva yɛ adwɩnɩ yekeziye nninyeni mɔɔ Gyehova ɩyɛ ɩbʋwa yɛ nɩ zʋ. Sɛ yɛdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ nninyeni mɔɔ Gyehova ɩyɛ ɩma yɛ daada nɩ nwʊ a, ɩkɔwʋwa yɛ ma yekenyia yi nu anwʋlazʋ kʋlaa kɛɛ ɩkɔwʋwa yɛ wɔ eyile be sɔ̃. Na eyi ɩlɛ́ma adwɩnɩndwɩnɩ kɛhyɛ yɛ zʋ wɔ mɔɔ ɩkɔdʋ yɛ wɔ eyile be nɩ nwʊ. (Mt. 6:25-34) Akʋ̃ sɔ̃, Gyehova zi ye ozunɛ nu yɛ ɔhawʋ munwatĩĩ, meti sɛ yɛbɔ mpayɩ yɛda yɩ asɩ na yesiã yɩ kɛɛ ɩwʋwa yɛ a, ɩkɛma yekenyia “Nyamɩnlɩ azɔ̃nudwɔlɩyɛ.” Ɔzɔ azɔ̃nudwɔlɩyɛ nɩ ma yɛ ahʋnlɩnɩ tɔ yɛ kunlu na ɩma yɛ adwɩnɩndwɩnɩ zʋ tɩ kʋlaa.

7 Sɛ yekile nninyeni nwʋ anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ a, ɩma yɛtwɩ yebike Gyehova. Dɩyɛ baaba bela mɔɔ yɛlɛ yɩ nɩ zʋ̃ Gyehova kɛ. (Kɩnga Gyemisi 1:17.) Gyehova hyɛ nninyeni sɔ̃maa yɛ mɔɔ eleledɩyɛ yɩ nɩ nnwʋanlɩ mɔɔ íni awielɩyɛ nwʊ anyɩnlɩlazʋ po kɩnla nwʊ. Mɩnla ti a? Iluwakɛɛ ɩdɔ yɛ, na kɛ ebiye a yɛfata nɩ ti a. (Wul. 3:24; Ɛfɩ. 2:8) Meti sɛ yekile anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ wɔ ɔlɔlɛ mɔɔ Gyehova dɔ yɛ nɩɩ nninyeniba mɔɔ ɩyɛ ɩfa ɩma yɛ nɩ nwʊ a, ɩtwĩ yɛ ibike yi na ɩba ɩyɛ yɛ Zɩ nɩɩ yɛ Ɔhɔnwʋ mɔɔ yɛdɔ yɩ paa.

MƖNLA YƐ ƖKƆWƲWA YƐ MA YEKEHILE ANYƖNLƖZƆLƐ WƆ MƆƆ YƐLƐ YƖ NƖ NWƲ A?
<<<P>>>
8 Dwɩnɩndwɩnɩ nninyeniba mɔɔ Gyehova ɩyɛ ɩma wʋ daada nɩ nwʋ. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, dwɩnɩndwɩnɩ odwɔkɔzɩzɩbɛ be mɔɔ eyiyialɩ mɔɔ nɩ ɩsɛ kɛɛ ekyina yi munwa nɩ nwʊ. Ebiye a, nɩ ɩdɩ wʋ dɔvʋlɔ be mɔɔ iwuli, adimakɛlɛ ɛyɛlɛyɛlɛ be mɔɔ ɩɣa ɩdɔlɩ wʋ zʋ anzɛnyɩɩ odwodwudialɛ be mɔɔ eyiyialɩ iluwa wʋ dedi ti nɩ. ‘Mɩnla yɛ ɩwʋwalɩ wʋ ma egyinanlɩ yi munwa a?’ Sɛ ekila wʋ mɛda a, ɔnwʋ yɩ kɛɛ, kɛ wʋzawʋ wʋ anwʋzɩzɩbɛ yɛ ɛva gyinanlɩ yi munwa a, mfomi Gyehova yɛ ɩmanlɩ anwʋzɩzɩbɛ sʋnʋnkʋ̃ wʋ ma ɛva gyinanlɩ yi munwa a. (Kɩnga 2 Kɔlɛntɩmaa 4:7.) Meti sɛ ɔdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ mɔɔ Gyehova ɩyɛ ɩma wʋ ma idua numɔ nɩ nwʊ a, ɩkɛhã wʋ ma ekehile anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ kezile wɔ mɔɔ ɩyɛ ɩma wʋ nɩ munwatĩĩ nwʊ.—Ndw. 103:2.
