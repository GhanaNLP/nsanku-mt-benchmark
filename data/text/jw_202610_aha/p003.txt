Ɔwɩɛnɩlɛ Aɣanɩ mɔɔ ɩɣalɩ wɔ July 15, 1926 nɩ hanlɩ kɛlɛta eyi nwu odwokɔ. Kɛlɛta yɩ nu odwokɔ hanlɩ kɛɛ: “Dɩyɛzumanlɛ ndwokɔ mɔɔ ɩɣalɩ eɣile asã yɩ la mɔɔ ise, Character or Covenant—Which?’ nɩɩ ‘Sacrifice and Service’ nɩ ɣa yɩk yɩ ɣɛ ɛɣɛɛlɛ, na ɩhyɛ ɣɛ ɛzɩzɩbɛ paa. Kakɩ yɩ, ɣɛáɣinga Ɔwɩɛnɩlɛ Aɣanɩ yɩ la na mfomi yesuma ankasa.” Izia sũ lhanlɩ kɛɛ, “abiyela pɛ kɛɛ ɩma munwatĩĩ wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʋ, na ɣɛ asafo yɩ inyiaa zulɔ mfasʋ paa.”

<<<P>>>
Wɔ May 1926 yɩ awielɩyɛ nɩ, Bayɩbʋlʋ Zumanlɛmaa nɩ yiyialɩ numɔ wɔ ayiyialɩyɛ be tʋ wɔ London mɔɔ ɩwɔ England nɩ. Ɔzɔ mmɩlɩ nɩ, nɩ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛpɩlɩntɩ awuuku wɔ Britain nɩ munwatĩĩ hɔ strike, meti nɩ ɩɣɛ sɩ kɛɛ bɛkenyia mmenii ma bɛkɛbɩlɩntɩ ɣɛ awuuku yi. Izɔ ti, ye mmeliyema mɔɔ nɩ bɔwɔ Brooklyn Betɩlɩ nɩ valɩ dɔnɩhwɩlɩ sũmaa yɛlɩ egyima va bɩlɩntɩlɩ buuku mɔɔ yi odwokɔtile se The Standard for the People yɩ nu 117,000 mɔɔ nɩ bɛkɛhyɛ wɔ ayiyialɩyɛ yɩ tʋ nɩ.
<<<P>>>
Wɔ Fulɔ, May 29 nɩ, mmeliyema mmenyia nɩɩ mmaalɛ wɔlɩ yɩ tʋ kɛɛ bɛlɛ awuuku yi bedii egyima. Wɔ ɔzɔ eɣile nɩ egyimalilɛ mɔɔ ɩhɔlɩ zulɔ wɔ ayiyialɩyɛ yɩ tʋ nɩ hyɛlɩ mmeliyema yɩ munwatĩĩ ɛzɩzɩbɛ kɛɛ: “Bɔtʋʋ salɛ kɔvʋlɔ Bayɩbʋlʋ Zumanlɛmaa nɩ munwatĩĩ kɛɛ bɔwɔ́ Gyisɛsɩ nɩɩ Bulombunlililɛ yɩ nwu ɛlawulo. Bɔhɔ́ Alexandra Palace nwɔnɩ dɛkɛdɛkɛ, na mmeliyema yɩ mɔɔ bɔwɔ Service Department nɩ kehile bɛ mɔɔ bɛyɛ́.”
<<<P>>>
Awuuku mɔɔ bɛhyɛlɩ wɔ ɔzɔ eɣile nɩ bʋ 100,000. July 15, 1926 Ɔwɩɛnɩlɛ Aɣanɩ yɩ wɔlɩ amanɩyɛ kɛɛ: “Nɩ mmeliyema anyɩ le baaba. Iluwakɛɛ, nɩ bɛtɩ nɣanlɩ kɛɛ bediye Gyehova yɩ ahyɛdɩ mɔɔ ise bɔwɔ́ odwokɔ yɩ nwu ɛlawulo nɩ.”
<<<P>>>
Eɣile mɔɔ ili kɛnɩ nɩ, Eliyema Rutherford manlɩ ndɛnɩ be wɔ Royal Albert Hall, na ɩhanlɩ mɔɔ ɩkɔdʋ oyuwadɩ adumivʋlɔ nɩ nwu odwokɔ. Mmɩlɩ mɔ bowukoli ayiyialɩyɛ dɩka yɩ nɩ, ɩákyɛ bela ɣɛ mmenii 13,000 yɛlɩ aliyia yi nu kɛnɩ manlɩ a. Na bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛányia ɔnwʋmɔ ɔhʋanɩ bɔkɔ aliyia yi nu kɛnɩ nɩ diyeli egyimalilɛ yɩ wɔ punwuanu iluwa speakers nɣezile mɔɔ nɩ bɛva bɛdɩda punwuanu nɩ ti.
<<<P>>>
Wɔ yɩ ndɛnɩ yi nu nɩ, Eliyema Rutherford hilehileli numɔ kɛɛ, oyuwadɩ aɣanɩ yɩ bɛ sa zi azɩ iluwakɛɛ bɔbʋ Gyehova nɩɩ yi Bulombunlililɛ yɩ. Eliyema Rutherford dɩndɛlɩ diyialɩ League of Nations nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ yɩ, na ɩhanlɩ idianlɩ kɛnɩ kɛɛ sa ɩba awielɩyɛ.
