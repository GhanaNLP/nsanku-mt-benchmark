17 Ɩkɛyɛ baaba kɛɛ ɛkɛva mmɩlɩ pɔkyĩĩ be keziye ɛkɛnɩ mɔɔ ɛkɛva kɛyɛ awulohyɩnlɩyɛ odwokɔ yɩ, kɛ ɔzɔ a, elényia alagyɩ ɛkɛyɛ yɩ kyĩ. Eliyema be mɔɔ bɔfʋlɔ yɩ John mɔɔ inyia alʋbɔ 16 nɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ: “Sɛ wʋánwʋ awulohyɩnlɩyɛ odwokɔ yɩ kɛɛ dɩyɛ mɔɔ nwʋmɔ hyinyia paa a, ɩkɛyɛ mɛlɛkɛ kɛɛ wʋ ɛgɛɛlɛ kevi.” Eliyema baalɛ Sierra mɔɔ inyia alʋbɔ 25 sʋ̃ hanlɩ kɛɛ: “Midi bɔmʋnlɩ mɩfa awulo-hyɩnlɩyɛ odwokɔ yɩ nwɔnɩ ansaana mɩayɛ dɩyɛ fʋlɔ bela, na nninyeni táhyɛlɩ mʋ zʋ.” Ɔzɔ la sʋ̃ yɛ eliyema Rogelio mɔɔ inyia alʋbɔ 21 nɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ: “Sɛ mɩsɛtɩ alaamɩ wɔ mɩ foonu zʋ a, ɩkakɛ mɩ ma mɩyɛ awulohyɩnlɩyɛ odwokɔ yɩ eyile bela.”
<<<P>>>
18 Bɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ɛkɛva wʋ awulohyɩnlɩyɛ odwokɔ yɩ eyile bela wɔ mmɩlɩ mɔ ɛva ziye kɛnɩ kɛ ɛkɛva kɛyɛ nɩ. Na áma ɛma nninyeni twɩnhwĩ wʋ adwɩnɩ. Isabelle mɔɔ inyia alʋbɔ 19 nɩ hileli yɩ adwɩnɩ wɔ eyi nwʋ, lhanlɩ kɛɛ: “Kã kile wʋ nwʋ kɛɛ, ɛléfa wʋ sa kɛhã debela anzɛnyɩ ɔlɔsɔ wɔ foonu yi nu kɔbʋmɩ kɛɛ ɛkɛva wʋ awulohyɩnlɩyɛ odwokɔ yɩ kowie. Sɛ ɛfa wʋ awulohyɩnlɩyɛ odwo-kɔ yɩ wɔ wʋ foonu zʋ na nninyeni twɩnhwĩ wʋ adwɩnɩ a, fa mɔɔ bɛbʋlɩntɩ yɩ mfomi yɛ wʋ awulo-hyɩnlɩyɛ odwokɔ yɩ. Owie yi ansaana wʋava wʋ sa wʋahã wʋ foonu yi.” Olivia mɔɔ inyia alʋbɔ 19 sʋ̃ hanlɩ kɛɛ: “Mɔɔ ɩbʋwa mɩ paa yɛ lɛ kɛɛ mɩ nu mʋ ɔhɔnwʋmmɔ anzɛnyɩɩ mɩ mmusunwamaa kɔwɔ numɔ kɛva awulohyɩnlɩyɛ odwokɔ yɩ. Sɛ ɔwɔ abi-ye mɔɔ ɩkɛhakɛ wʋ ma ɛkɛyɛ awulohyɩnlɩyɛ odwo-kɔ yɩ a, ɩma ɩyɛ mɛlɛkɛ kɛɛ ɛkɛva yɩ eyile bela.”

BƲWA WƲ NNWUAKULA MA BƐBƖLƐLI ƐHƐLƐLƐDWOKƆ YƖ NU EYILE BELA
<<<P>>>
19 Awuvʋlɔ, sɛ ɩba ɩyɛ bo su kɛɛ bɛ nɩɩ bo nnwua-kula yɩ kozunzu awulohyɩnlɩyɛ odwokɔ yɩ nwʋ eyile bela a, sa bɛfa benyi. Sɛ bo nnwuakula yɩ dɩ ngyɩkĩĩ ngyɩkĩĩ na sɛ kɛ debela yɛ bɛkɛdɩ yɩ tu po a, ɔzɔ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ mɔɔ bɛyɛ kɛɛ bokozunzu awulo-hyɩnlɩyɛ odwokɔ yɩ nwʋ eyile bela nɩ kɔwʋwa bɛ wɔ eyile be. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, sɛ oduwa owudo ɛgyɩkĩĩ be na okugua zʋlɔ nzulo eyile bela a, ɩma inyi bʋwɔ na ɩba ɩyɛ owudo kezile. Ɔzɔ la sʋ̃ yɛ sɛ bɛfa mmɩlɩ ɛgyɩkĩĩ bɛ nɩɩ bo nnwuakula yɩ fa awulohyɩnlɩyɛ odwokɔ yɩ na bɔdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ nwʋmɔ eyile bela a, ɩkɔwʋwa bɛ ma bɛkɛyɛ sɩ, na bɛanyi wɔ sunzuma ɔfʋã nu.—Kɩnga Fɩlɩpɩmaa 1:9, 10.
<<<P>>>
20 Gyisɛsɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ: “Kɛ pʋanʋ nkʋ̃ yɛ sɛ enii idi a ikenyia nnwʋanlɩ a, na odwokɔ bela mɔɔ ɩzʋ̃ Nyamɩnlɩ yi munwa nu ikevinde nɩ sʋ̃ ɩma nnwʋanlɩ.” (Mt. 4:4) Ye nu bela íni kɛnɩ mɔɔ ɩkɛbɛ yɩ kɛɛ eyile kũ po kodua numɔ mɔɔ ilédi alɩyɛ. Sɛ ɔzɔ a, mɛyɩ nɩ nwʋmɔ hyinyia paa kɛɛ ye-keli sunzuma ɔfʋã nu alɩyɛ eyile bela iluwa awulo-hyɩnlɩyɛ odwokɔ yɩ mɔɔ yekozunzu nwʋmɔ eyile bela nɩ zʋ!
