MMƖLƖ mɔ ɛ nɩ Gyehova Dasɩvɔlɔmaa zumanlɩ
Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ nɩ, mɩnla yɛ ɩmanlɩ enyianlɩ anwʋlazʋ
kɛɛ ɔnwʋ “dedi ɔkʋ̃” nɩ anzɛnyɩɩ ɔhʋanɩ ɔkʋ̃ kyei-
nyii mɔɔ Nyamɩnlɩ pɛ kɛɛ yeduwa zʋlɔ yɛsũmɩ yɩ
nɩ a? (Ɛfɩ. 4:4-6) Asʋ̃ ɩdɩ ɔlɔlɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ Gyehova yɩ
mmenii bɛ avinlɩ nɩ yɛ ɩhwɩnlɩ wʋ ma ɛɣa yɛlɩ Gye-
hova Dasɩvɔlɔ a? Asʋ̃ ɩdɩ bɛ nɔhʋanlɛlilɛ nɩɩ bɛ
baabayɛlɛ yɛ ɩhanlɩ wʋ ahʋnlɩnɩ a? Ɔlɔlɛ kanyɩɩ nɩɩ
azũnudwolɩyɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ Gyehova yɩ ahyɩhyɛdɩ yɩ nu
nɩ, yɛ ɩtwĩ mmenii sũmaa ma bɛba bɛyɛ Gyehova
Dasɩvɔlɔmaa a. Na ntɩyasɩ wɔ eyi nu, iluwakɛɛ ɔlɔ-
lɛ nɩɩ subanɩba yɛ ɩma yɛnwʋ nɔhʋanlɛ Kilisinyia-
maa a.—Dwɔ. 13:35; 1 Pit. 2:12.
<<<P>>>
Naasʋ̃ sɛ eliyema be yɛ debe tiyia yɛ anzɛnyɩɩ
ɩ nɩɩ yɛ iádi yi buwɔ sʋ̃ ɛ? Anzɛnyɩɩ sɛ eliyema be
yɛ tanɩ kezile be dɩkɛɛ mɔɔ izili wɔ Kɔlɛntɩ asafo yi
nu mɔɔ Pɔɔlʋ hanlɩ nwʋmɔ odwokɔ nɩ sʋ̃ ɛ?
(1 Kɔl. 5:1) Sɛ ye dedi kɔhʋla kɛyɛ sɩ a, íni kɛɛ ikyi-
na ye mmeliyema bɛ nyɛlɩyɛ zʋ nkũ la. Kɛ ɩkɛyɛ na
wʋanwʋ yɩ kɛɛ wu dedi gyĩ fapɩmɩ baaba zʋ a?
<<<P>>>
KƐ ƖKƐYƐ NA WƲAVA WƲ DEDI WƲAGYINA
FAPƖMƖ BAABA ZƲ A?
<<<P>>>
Sɛ yɛkɔhʋla yɛkɛva ye dedi yekegyina fapɩmɩ
baaba zʋ a, ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛkɔ zʋlɔ yɛma anwʋlazʋ mɔɔ
yɛwɔ wɔ Bayɩbʋlʋ ɩtʋwɔlɛ ehilehilelɛ yɩ nu nɩ yɛ
kezi. Kɛ ɩkɛyɛ na eyi ɩayɛ bʋwɔ a? Sunzu eyi nwʋ
kila.
<<<P>>>
Eyile kũ be, Gyisɛsɩ hanlɩ odwokɔ be kɛɛ ɩsɛ
kɛɛ bedi yɩ nnwʋnlamɩ na bɔnʋ yi mmegyia. Naasʋ̃
odwokɔ eyi lileli yɩ adʋwavɔlɔ yɩ bo nu sũmaa,
meti beyiakili Gyisɛsɩ yɩ mɛdalɩlɛ. Bɛhanlɩ kɛɛ: “Ehi-
lehilelɛ eyi ɩyɛ sɩ.” Iluwakɛɛ mɔɔ Gyisɛsɩ hanlɩ nɩ li-
leli bɛ sũmaa nɩ ti, bɛátʋwa yɩ dɔkɔ̃.—Dwɔ. 6:
56-60, 66.
<<<P>>>
Mɛyɩ mɛda nɩ, Gyisɛsɩ yizialɩ yɩ asũmavɔlɔ yɩ kɛɛ:
“Na munlummɔ sʋ̃ ɛ, bɛkɛbɛ kɛɛ bɔkɔhɔ a?” Na
Pita wʋwalɩ yɩ kɛɛ “Niibenyia, aninyia kɛnɩ yɛ yɛ-
kɔɔ a? Mɔwʋ yɛ ɛlɛ odwokɔ mɔɔ ɩma nnwʋanlɩ mɔɔ
íni awielɩyɛ nɩ a. Sikalɛpɛyɩ sʋ̃ yede yedi; na yezi
kɛɛ mɔwʋ yɛ ɛdɩ Nwanzanwanza ɔkʋ̃ nɩ mɔɔ ɔzʋ̃
Nyamɩnlɩ kɛnɩ a.”—Dwɔ. 6:67-69.
<<<P>>>
Mɩnla ti mɔɔ Gyisɛsɩ yi odwokɔ yɩ iádide
Pita nɩɩ asũmavɔlɔ nɣalɩyɛ nɩ a? Iluwakɛɛ nɩ be
dedi gyĩ fapɩmɩ baaba zʋ na kɛ bɛ nɣanlɩdɩlɛ
zʋ a. Pita hanlɩ kɛɛ: “Yede yedi; na yezi kɛɛ
mɔwʋ yɛ ɛdɩ Nwanzanwanza ɔkʋ̃ nɩ mɔɔ ɔzʋ̃
Nyamɩnlɩ kɛnɩ a.” Meti nɩ asũmavɔlɔ nɔhʋanlɛmaa
eyimmɔ zi mɔɔ bede bedi, nɩɩ sianti mɔɔ beleli be-
lili nɩ. Nɩ bɛlɛ anwʋlazʋ paa kɛɛ Gyisɛsɩ yɛ lɛ Mɛ-
zaya nɩ, iluwakɛɛ bɛvalɩ mɔɔ Gyisɛsɩ hanlɩ nɩɩ
mɔɔ ɩyɛlɩ nɩ bedindianlɩ mɔɔ bozuma wɔ Ɛhɛlɛlɛ-
dwokɔ yɩ nu nɩ nwʋ. Anwʋlazʋ kezile mɔɔ benyianlɩ
wɔ Bayɩbʋlʋ ɩtʋwɔlɛ ehilehilelɛ nu nɩ manlɩ be dedi
yɛlɩ sɩ na mmɩlɩ mɔ mmenii mvʋlɔ bɛ padi golɔlɩ
iluwa mɔɔ Gyisɛsɩ hanlɩ yɩ ti nɩ, bɔnlummɔ mɛyɩ be
padi iákolɔ.
<<<P>>>
Mɩnla yɛ yɛkɔhʋla kozuma wɔ ndianwʋ eyi nu a?
Mɔɔ yezi nɩ wɔ tumi kezile paa tɛla kezimɔ yɛtɩ
nɣanlɩ nɩ. Mɩnla ti a? Iluwakɛɛ Bayɩbʋlʋ nu nɔhʋa-
nlɛ mɔɔ yezuma, dɩkɛɛ Nyamɩnlɩ yi dima, yi tile
nu pɔkɔ, nɩɩ eleledɩyɛ nɩ iázɩnza kyĩ, naasʋ̃ mmenii
mvʋlɔ bɛ nyɛlɩyɛ kɔhʋla kɛzɩnza kezimɔ yɛtɩ nɣanlɩ
