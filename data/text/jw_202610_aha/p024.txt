Pɩbɛlɩ Ɛhɛlɛlɛdwokɔ Yɩ Nu Eɣile Bela
<<<P>>>
“Bɛvalɩ bɛ adwɩnɩ munwala beleli odwokɔ yɩ na eɣile bela nɩ bɛpɩbɛlɩ Ɛhɛlɛlɛdwokɔ yɩ nu bvwo.”​—EGY. 17:11, NWT.
<<<P>>>
WƆ ƆLUBƆ 1905 nu nɩ, Gyehova yɩ ahyɩhyɛdɩ yɩ yelɩ buuku be. Nɩ ɛhɛlɛlɛndwokɔ nu numɔ ehilehilelɛ mɔɔ ɩzʊ Zion’s Watch Tower yi nu nɩ wɔ numɔ. Bɛyelɩ yɩ kɛɛ bɛyɩnga yɩ eɣile bela. Nɩ eyi bʊwa mmeli-yema yɩ ma bɛkɩnga na bɔdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ ɛhɛlɛlɛdwokɔ yɩ nwʊ eɣile bela wɔ ɔlʊbɔ munli yi munwala nu. Bɛvalɩ buuku eyi belili egyima nkʊʊ̃ kɔbʊmɩ ɔlʊbɔ 1927 nu. Mɛyɩ mɛda nɩ, bɛvalɩ ɛhɛlɛlɛndwokɔ yɩ nu numɔ ehilehilelɛ yɩ bɛhɩnlanlɩ Year Book * yɩ nwʊ. Nɩ betindimi Year Book yi ɔlʊbɔ bela meti nɩ ɩma mmeliyema yɩ nyia Bayɩbʊlʊ nu ndwokɔ sʊ̃maa nu numɔ ehilehilelɛ mɔɔ ɩzʊ Zion’s Watch Tower yi nu nɩ bɔdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ nwʊmɔ.
<<<P>>>
2 Wɔ 1986 nu nɩ, yɛ ahyɩhyɛdɩ yɩ wɔlɩ yɩ tʊ kɛɛ itindimii awulo-hyɩnlɩyɛ odwokɔ yɩ wɔ buuku eɣalɛ be mɔɔ bɔfʊlɔ yɩ Pɩbɛlɩ Ɛhɛlɛlɛ-dwokɔ Yi Nu Eɣile Bela nɩ nu. Sikalɛyɩ, bɛyɛ buuku eyi wɔ ndɛnɩ mɔɔ ɩbʊ 300 nu. Akʊ̃ sʊ̃, sɛ ɔbunko JW Library® app yɩ anzɛnyu Watchto-wer Library anzɛnyu Watchtower ONLINE LIBRARY™ (wol.jw.org) yɩ la a, sa ɔnwʊ awulohyɩnlɩyɛ odwokɔ yɩ wɔ kɛnɩ.
<<<P>>>
3 Iluwa kɛɛ yɛwɔ nninyeni sʊ̃maa yɛ eɣile bela nɩ ti, itwu mmɩlɩ be a, nɩ ɩyɛ sɩ kɛɛ yɛkenyia mmɩlɩ yɛkɛva awulohyɩnlɩyɛ odwokɔ yɩ. Wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ eyi nu nɩ, yekozunzu nninyeni asã be nwʊ. Mɔɔ ili bɔmunlɩ, yekozunzu Bayɩbʊlʊ nu ndianwʊ be mɔɔ ɩkɔhʊla ɩkɔwʊwa yɛ ma yɛkɔnwʊ sianti mɔɔ nwʊmɔ hyinyia kɛɛ yede mmɩlɩ yesuma Ɛhɛlɛlɛdwokɔ yɩ eɣile bela nɩ nwʊ. Mɔɔ ɩtɔ zʊlɔ anwĩ, yɛkɔnwʊ sia-nti mɔɔ nwʊmɔ hyinyia kɛɛ yɛfa awulohyɩnlɩyɛ odwokɔ yɩ eɣile
<<<P>>>
