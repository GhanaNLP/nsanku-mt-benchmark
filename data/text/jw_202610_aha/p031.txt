Amba, ɔlɔlɛ nɩɩ subanɩba mɔɔ yɛ mmeliyema da yɩ ali nɩ hyɛ yɛ ɛzɩzɩbɛ. Na ɔzɔ yɛ Gyehova sʋ̃ pɛ kɛɛ yɛtɩ nɣanlɩ a. Naasʋ̃ sɛ mbiyemmɔ bɛ nyɛlɩyɛ ma kezimɔ yɛtɩ nɣanlɩ nɩ sɩnza, na sɛ yɛkɔlɩ zʋlɔ yenyia dedi wɔ nɔhʋanlɛ mɔɔ yezuma wɔ Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ nu nɩ a, ɩkɛma yɛkɔhɔlɩ zʋlɔ yekegyina pindinyii wɔ nɔhʋanlɛ yɩ nu. Eyi ma yenyia anwʋlazʋ kɛɛ yɛkɩnla ahyɩhyɛdɩ ɔkʋ̃ kyeinyii mɔɔ bɔsʋ̃mɩ Gyehova wɔ nɔhʋanlɛ nu nɩ nwʋ. Meti nɔhʋanlɛ odwokɔ eyi lázɩnza mmɩlɩ mɔ abiye ɩyɛ debe idiyia yɛ nɩ.
<<<P>>>
Sɛ yenyia nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛba mɔɔ yɛfa yesuma Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ a, ɩhyɛ yɛ anwʋlazʋ mɔɔ yɛwɔ wɔ nɔhʋanlɛ yɩ mɔɔ yezuma wɔ Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ Odwokɔ yɩ nu nɩ ɛzɩzɩbɛ. Na eyi kɛma yɛkɔhɔ zʋlɔ yekenyia anwʋlazʋ kezile paa kɛɛ yeluwa ɔhʋanɩ ɔkʋ̃ kyeinyii mɔɔ Nyamɩnlɩ pɛ kɛɛ yeduwa zʋlɔ yɛsʋ̃mɩ yɩ nɩ zʋ. Sɛ ɩbayɩ zɔ a, ye dedi kegyina fapɩmɩ baaba zʋ, na ye padi ilókolɔ lávalɩ nwʋmɔ mɔɔ mmenii mvʋlɔ kɛhã anzɛnyɩɩ bɛkɛyɛ bela nɩ.—Ndw. 16:8; kila alɩka mɔɔ ise “Bʋwá Gyehova Yɩ Ahyɩhyɛdɩ Yɩ Nu” nɩ.

Bʋwá Gyehova Yɩ Ahyɩhyɛdɩ Yɩ Nu
<<<P>>>
Fa yɩ kɛɛ otuu ɔhʋanɩ kɔhɔ dɩka be mɔɔ ɩyɛ pɩkɛ paa mɔɔ ɔhʋanɩ yɩ walɩ. Na ɔvʋ baasɩ mɔɔ ɛlɛ ɔkɔ ɔzɔ dɩka nɩ. Sɛ boluwa ɔhʋanɩ yɩ zʋ bɔkɔɔ a, pɩlazunɛ bela íni nwʋmɔ kɛɛ wʋ anyɩ kele baaba.
<<<P>>>
Naasʋ̃ sɛ ɛda baasɩ yi nu a, ebiye a nninyeni be mɔɔ ɩkɔhɔlɩ zʋlɔ wɔ baasɩ yɩ nu nɩ kɔhʋla kɛma ɛkɛva ɛya. Sa ɩkʋla ɩbayɩ kɛɛ mmenii be mɔɔ bɛda baasɩ yɩ nu nɩ kɛyɛ dede, anzɛnyɩɩ dɩlayɩvanii yi po kɛdɩndɛ kediyia wʋ. Naasʋ̃ eyimmɔ munwatĩĩ láma enyia adwɩnɩ kɛɛ wʋáfu baasɩ mɔɔ ankɩ ɩsɛ kɛɛ ɔfu nɩ. Ise kɛɛ ɔbʋwa baasɩ yɩ nu nkʋ̃ kɔbʋmɩ kɛɛ okodwu dɩka mɔɔ ɔkɔ nɩ.
<<<P>>>
Sɛ yɛkɛhã a, ye munwatĩĩ yetuu ɔhʋanɩ kɔhɔ Gyehova yi oyuwadɩ fʋlɔ yɩ nu. Na yɛ anyɩ de paa kɛɛ yɛkɩnla Gyehova yɩ ahyɩhyɛdɩ yɩ nwʋ. Naasʋ̃ sa yɛkʋla yɛtɩ nɣanlɩ kɛɛ eliyema be yɛ debe diyia yɛ, ɔzɔ sʋ̃ la yɛ sɛ eliyema be mɔɔ yebu yɩ paa nɩ yɛ tanɩ kezile be a, sa ɩkʋla ɩyɛ yɛ asɩbɔnwʋ a. Naasʋ̃, lávalɩ nwʋmɔ mɔɔ mmenii mvʋlɔ kɛyɛ bela nɩ, yɛwɔ ahyɩhyɛdɩ ɔkʋ̃ kyeinyii tɔɔɩ ɩ nɩɩ yɛ kɔɔ Nyamɩnlɩ yi oyuwadɩ fʋlɔ yi nu. Meti sɛ yenyia anwulazʋ kezile paa wɔ nɔhʋanlɛ yɩ mɔɔ yezuma wɔ Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ nu, na yenyia anwʋlazʋ wɔ ahyɩhyɛdɩ mɔɔ ihilehile yɛ ɔzɔ nɔhʋanlɛ yɩ nɩ nu a, sa yɛtuwa yɛ ɔhʋanɩdulɛ yɩ zʋ. Na ɩbayɩ zɔ a, ye dedi kɛyɛ sɩ.
