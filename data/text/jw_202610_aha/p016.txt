13 Gyisɛsɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ ɔlɔlɛ mɔɔ yɩ mɛdalilɛmaa kɛla yɩ ali kehile bɔ nwu bɔ nwu nɩ kɛma mmenii kɔnwʋ kɛɛ bɛdɩ yɩ adʋwavʋlɔ. (Kınga Dwɔɔnʋ 13:35.) Kilisinyiamaa mɔɔ bɔwʋwalɩ azɩ wɔ asʋ̃mavʋlɔ yɩ bɛ mmılɩ zʋ nɩ lalɩ ɔzɔ ɔlɔlɛ yɩ be ali wɔ ahvanɩ sɔ̃maa zʋ. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, bɛ nɩɩ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ nɩ behyinyia mmʋwalɩyɛ nɩ hyɛlɩ nninyeni mɔɔ bɛlɛ yɩ nɩ. (Egy. 2:44, 45) Na bɔwʋlɩ mpayɩ bɛmanlɩ bɛ mmeliyema mɔɔ nɩ beyiyiaa odwodwudiyialɛ nɩ sɔ̃. (Egy. 12:5) Akʋ̃ sɔ̃ bɔwʋwalɩ ayinsamaa nɩɩ ahunavʋlɔ mɔɔ nɩ bɔwɔ bo nu nɩ. (Gye. 1:27) Na bɛzabɛ po mɔɔ nɩ bɛdɩ ahyinyiamaa nɩ valɩ bo ozukua nɩɩ nninyeni mɔɔ bɔwɔ nɩ wʋwalɩ bɔ ɔhɔnwʋmmɔ Kilisinyiamaa mvʋlɔ. (2 Kɔl. 8:1-4) Ɛhɛlɛvʋlɔ be mɔɔ bɔfʋlɔ yɩ Tertullian nɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ, nɩ mmenii kã kɛɛ, bɛyɛlɩ yɩ ndwɩ kɛɛ nɩ Kilisinyiamaa mɔɔ bɔwʋwalɩ azɩ wɔ asʋ̃mavʋlɔ yɩ bɛ mmılɩ zʋ nɩ ‘da ɔlɔlɛ ali kile bɔ nwu bɔ nwu na nɩ beziyeziye bɔ nwu po kɛɛ bɔkowu bɛkɛma bɛ mmeliyema.’

14 Nyamınlı yi mmenii mɔɔ bɔwɔ kɛnɩ nnɛ nɩ sumazuma Kilisinyiamaa mɔɔ bɔwʋwalɩ azɩ wɔ asʋ̃mavʋlɔ yɩ bɛ mmılɩ zʋ nɩ, na bɛda ɔlɔlɛ ali bekile bɔ nwu bɔ nwu. Na yeduwa dasɩlɛlilɛ egyima yɩ zʋ yɛda ɔlɔlɛ ali yekile bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛázũmɩ Gyehova nɩ po. Akʋ̃ sɔ̃, yɛyɩ fʋtulɔ yɛfa yɛbʋwa dasɩlɛlilɛ egyima mɔɔ yɛyɛɛ nɩ. Abiyela iáziã yɛ anzɛnyɩɩ bɛáhyɛ yɛ kɛɛ yɛyɩ fʋtulɔ na ɔzɔ la sʋ̃ yɛ yɛáduwa tayıtı a. Biyekɔ̃ sʋ̃, yɛma ɔlɔlɛ kã yɛ ma yɛbʋwa yɛ mmeliyema mmenyia nɩɩ mmaalɛ mɔɔ bɔ nwu hyɩlɩ bɛ nɩ.* Sɛ yɛfa mɛyɩ yetiã ahanlɩ a, yɛbʋwa yɛ nwu yɛ nwu wɔ sunzuma ɔfʋã nu nɩɩ nyanlıdılɛ ɔfʋã nu sɔ̃. Eyi nɩɩ odwokɔ mɔɔ sɔ̃mavʋlɔ Pɔɔlʋ hanlɩ nɩ yiyia. Lhanlɩ kɛɛ: “Bɛhyɛ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ be padi golɔ nɩ ɛzɩzıbɛ, bɔwʋwá enii mɔɔ ɩyɛ mɛlɛkɛ nɩ, na benyiá abiyela nwu abʋtalɩ.” (1 Tɛs. 5:14, NWT) Gyehova va yɛ hınla yɩ abusunwa mɔɔ bɔwɔ oyuwadɩ munwala, mɔɔ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɔwɔ numɔ nɩ da ɔlɔlɛ ali kile bɔ nwu bɔ nwu nɩɩ mmenii mvʋlɔ nɩ nwu.
