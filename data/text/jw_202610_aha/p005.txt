G. Ayiyialɩyɛ be mɔɔ bɛyɛlɩ wɔ Madison Square Garden wɔ New York, wɔ October 1926
<<<P>>>
Wɔ Bɔɔlɔ October 17, mmɩlɩ mɔ bɔwɔlɩ oyuwa 3:00 nɩ, mmenii kɛyɛ 12,000 yɛ bediyeli Eliyema Rutherford yɩ ndɛnɩ nɩ a. Na mmɩlɩ mɔ bowieli ndɛnɩ nɩ, nɩ anyɩnlɩlɛlɛmaa nɩ pɛ kɛɛ bɔnwʋ sɔ̃maa. Wɔ ɔzɔ ayiyialɩyɛ yɩ tʋ nɩ, bɛhyɛlɩ awuuku mɔɔ ɩbʋ 9,000, na ɔzɔ ayiyialɩyɛ yɩ paa yɛ bɛhyɛlɩ awuuku bɛdɛlalɩ ayiyialɩyɛ bela mɔɔ nɩ bɔhɔ kɣĩ wɔ ɔzɔ mmɩlɩ nɩ a.

ALIYIA MƆƆ ƖWƆ 117 ADAMS STREET
<<<P>>>
Iluwakɛɛ nɩ mmenii sɔ̃maa hyinyia yɛ ɔnwʋma azʋlɔtʋ yɩ be nɩ ti, mmeliyema mɔɔ nɩ bɔwɔ Brooklyn nɩ nwʋnlɩ kɛɛ ihyinyia kɛɛ benyia dɩka kezile mɔɔ bɛfa bɛpɩlɩntɩ ɔnwʋma. Nɩ bɛ rent aliyia mɔɔ ɩwɔ 18 Concord Street nɩ, naasɔ̃ nɩ aliyia yɩ dɩ ɛgyɩkĩĩ ma egyima yɩ mɔɔ inyiaa anyunumihɔlɛ nɩ. Meti mmeliyema yɩ dɔlɩ azɩlɛ be wɔ Adams Street, nɩ ibike bɛ aliyia yɩ mɔɔ nɩ bɛpɩlɩntɩ awuuku wɔ numɔ nɩ. Na bɔwɔlɩ yɩ tʋ begyinanlɩ bɔlɔnsanɩ mɔɔ ɩdʋwa zʋlɔ 8, nɩ eyi kɛma bekenyia dɩka kezile mɔɔ bɛkɛva kɛbʋlɩntɩ awuuku nɩ.
<<<P>>>
Nɩ bokowie aliyia eyi mɔɔ ɩwɔ 117 Adams Street nɩ wɔ 1927. Na nɩ eyi kɔwʋwa mmeliyema yɩ ma bekeziyeziye bɔ nwʋ kɛma anyunumihɔlɛ mɔɔ ɩbaa nɩ. Wɔ 1927 yɩ tuwɔlɛ pɛɛ nɩ, mmenii sɔ̃maa ɣa hɩnlanlɩ Bayɩbʋlʋ Zumanlɛmaa nɩ nwʋ, na ahyɩhyɛdɩ yɩ hɔlɩ zʋlɔ yelɩ nzɩnzanlɩyɛ azʋlɔtʋ mɔɔ nɩ ɩkɛma sɛ itwu 1931 a, bɔkɔhʋla bɛkɛvata kɛɛ bɔkɔvʋlɔ bɛ kɛɛ Gyehova Dasɩvʋlɔmaa.
