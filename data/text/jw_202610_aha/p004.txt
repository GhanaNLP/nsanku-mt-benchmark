E. George Wright (bɛnɩ zʋ) nɩɩ Edwin Skinner mmɩlɩ mɔ nɩ bɔsɔ̃mɩ wɔ India nɩ
<<<P>>>
Ɛ. George Draper
<<<P>>>
E
<<<P>>>
Ɛ
<<<P>>>
Mmɩlɩ mɔ bɔbɔlɩ ayiyialɩyɛ nɩ, Eliyema Rutherford hã hileli Edwin kɛɛ ɩgá ɩnwʋ́ yɩ wɔ London.
<<<P>>>
Eliyema Skinner hanlɩ kɛɛ: “Mmɩlɩ mɔ ɩhanlɩ zɔ nɩ, minyianlɩ adwɩnɩ kɛɛ ebiye a nɩ ɩpɛ kɛɛ mʋkɔ mʋsɔ̃mɩ wɔ manlɩ fʋlɔ zʋ. Awulohyɩnlɩ nɩ, mʋhɔlɩ London. Mʋkɔɔ munwatĩĩ nɩ miziyeziye mɩ adwɩnɩ kɛɛ sɛ mʋkɔ na ɩkã kɛɛ mʋhɔ́ mʋzɔ̃mɩ wɔ manlɩ fʋlɔ zʋ a, sa mɩkã mikile yi kɛɛ, mʋkɔhɔ” Amba sɔ̃, Eliyema Rutherford gizialɩ yɩ kɛɛ: “Asɔ̃ ɛkɛbɛyɩ kɛɛ ɔkɔhɔ India a?” Na Edwin sɔ̃ wʋwalɩ yɩ kɛɛ: “Eyile sina yɛ ɛkɛbɛ yɩ kɛɛ mʋkɔhɔ a?”
<<<P>>>
Alapɛnɩ asã mɛda wɔ July nʋ nɩ, Edwin nɩɩ odwokɔbahanlɛvʋlɔ George Wright vʋlɩ ɛlɛnɩ hɔlɩ India. Wɔ India nɩ, nɩ Eliyema Skinner kila ɔzɔ̃mɩnlɛ egyima yɩ mɔɔ nɩ bɛyɛ wɔ Afghanistan, Burma (Myanmar), Ceylon (Sri Lanka), India, nɩɩ Persia (Iran) nɩ zʋ. Eliyema Skinner hɔlɩ zʋlɔ wʋwalɩ India alʋbɔ 64 kɔbʋmɩ kɛɛ iwieli yɩ zɔ̃mɩnlɛ egyima yɩ wɔ azɩlɛ yɩ zʋ wɔ 1990.

EHILALƐMAA MƆƆ BOTUDU AHƲANL LILI DAZILƐLILƐ EGYIMA YƖ ANYUNU
<<<P>>>
Ɔzʋmɩ 1894 nɩ, nɩ ahyɩhyɛdɩ yɩ sɔ̃ma iagyianugyinalɛmaa ma bɔkɔ bekyelagyela Bayɩbʋlʋ Zʋmanlɛmaa okuwu azʋlɔtu nɩ. Wɔ July 1926 nɩ, egyima mɔɔ nɩ ahyɩhyɛdɩ yɩ iagyianugyinalɛmaa bɛyɛ nɩ zɩnzanlɩ. Nɩ bɛfa eyile ɔkɔ̃ yɛ bɛfa bekyela mmeliyema yɩ a, naasɔ̃ nɩ bɛkɛva ayile anwĩ bekegyela bɛ. Akʋ̃ sɔ̃, nɩ ɩsɛ kɛɛ bɛfa dɔnɩhwɩlɩ 100 bɛyɛ dasɩlɛlilɛ egyima yɩ wɔ eziyianɩ bela nu.
<<<P>>>
Eliyema George Draper mɔɔ nɩ ɩkɩnla bɛzabɛ mɔɔ nɩ botudu ahʋanɩ bɔkɔ bekyelagyela mmeliyema yɩ nɩ hilehileli numɔ kɛɛ, ɩsɛ kɛɛ mmeliyema yɩ munwatĩĩ mɔɔ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɔkɔ bekyelagyela mmeliyema yɩ sʋ̃ kɩnla nwʋmɔ nɩ, bɛkã odwokɔba yɩ. Sɛ bosuma Ɔwɩɛnɩlɛ Aɣanɩ yɩ a, sa isiyeziye bɛ ma bɛyɛ zɔ.” Eliyema Draper wieli yɩ zɔ̃mɩnlɛ egyima yɩ wɔ azɩlɛ yɩ zʋ wɔ 1938.

AYIYIALIҮƐ BE MƆƆ BƐYƐLI WƆ NEW YORK CITY
<<<P>>>
Bɛyɛlɩ ayiyialɩyɛ kezile mɔɔ itualɩ tʋ wɔ ɔzɔ ɔlʋbɔ yɩ nu wɔ October 14-18, 1926, wɔ New York City. Newspapers egyimayɛlɛ okuwu azʋlɔtu mɔɔ bɔwɔ twonwa yɩ nu kɛnɩ nɩ leli dianlɩ numɔ kɛɛ bɔkɔwɔ Eliyema Rutherford yɩ ndɛnɩ yɩ mɔɔ yi odwokɔtile se “Earth’s Greatest Conflict Near” nɩ nwʋ ɛlawulo. Naasɔ̃ ɩka ɛgyɩkĩĩ ma bɔbɔ yɩ tʋ nɩ, Newspapers egyimayɛlɛ okuwu nyɛzile sɔ̃maa hanlɩ kɛɛ bɛányia bɔbɔ ayiyialɩyɛ yɩ nwʋ ɛlawulo.
<<<P>>>
Meti mmeliyema yɩ zili pɔkɔ kɛɛ bɛkɛma mmenii kɛdɩ mɔɔ Newspapers egyimayɛlɛ okuwu azʋlɔtu bɛyɛ nɩ nwʋ odwokɔ. Wɔ Yalɛ, October 15 nɩ, R. J. Martin lalɩ kɛlɛta ɛyalɛ fʋlɔ be mɔɔ bɔfʋlɔ yɩ Against the People nɩ ali. Kɛlɛta ɛyalɛ eyi manlɩ Newspapers azʋlɔtu nɩ bɔ nwʋ lalɩ kɛ, na ɩwɔlɩ Eliyema Rutherford yɩ ndɛnɩ yɩ nwʋ ɛlawulo. Eliyema Martin hanlɩ kɛɛ: “Yɛwɔ yɩ tʋ kɛɛ amɩyɛzayɛ yɛpɩlɩntɩ yɛ awuuku. Na yɛ mmɩlantɩyɛ nɩ kɛyɛ egyima awulohyɩnlɩyɛ nɩɩ awulodwolɩyɛ kɔbʋmɩ kɛɛ yɛkɛbɩlɩntɩ kɛlɛta eyi nkɔ̃kʋ̃ nkɔ̃kʋ̃ 1,250,000. Yewie yi a, sa yɛfa yɛma mmeliyema yɩ mɔɔ bɔwɔ ayiyialɩyɛ yɩ tʋ nɩ ma bɛfa bɔkɔ punwuanu na bɛahyɩhyɛ bɛama mmenii.” Mmɩlɩ mɔ bowieli ɔzɔ ɔfʋã yɩ nɩ, mmeliyema yɩ wɔlɩ yɩ tʋ kɛɛ bɛkyɩkyɛɛ ahɛlɛta mmalɛ yɩ. Ɔzɔ nunuma yɩ nɩ, bɛhyɛlɩ 300,000.
<<<P>>>
Wɔ Fʋlɔ nɩɩ Bɔɔlɔ nɩ, bɛhyɩhyɛlɩ ahɛlɛta mmalɛ 1,000,000! Akʋ̃ sɔ̃, yɛ mmeliyema eyimmɔ mɔɔ bɔbɔ ɛhyɩmɛ nɩ hyɩhyɛlɩ Deliverance buuku yi 3,783 nɩɩ blohyʋɔ mvʋlɔ 35,140.
