BƲTAYƖ
Ikɔwʋwa yɛ ma yɛkɔnwʋ kezimo yekehile nninyeni nwʋ anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ, nɩɩ ɔhʋanɩ mɔɔ yekoluwa zʋlɔ yɛkɛyɛ zɔ.
<<<P>>>
Behilé Nninyeni Nwʋ Anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ
<<<P>>>
“Bɛyɛ́ mmenii mɔɔ bekile nninyeni nwʋ anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ.”—KOL. 3:15, NWT.
<<<P>>>
NƖ ELIYEMA benyia be mɔɔ bɔfʋlɔ yɩ John* nyia alʋbɔ 95 mɔɔ nɩ ládɩ pɔkɔ baaba. Meti nɩ ihyinyia kɛɛ mmenii mvʋlɔ kila yɩ. Sɛ abiye ba kyela yɩ anzɛnyɩɩ ɩba ɩkye dɩyɛ egyikĩĩ be po yɩ a, nɩ John kã kɛɛ, “Mɩla wʋ asɩ.” Na mɛyɩ mɛda nɩ, ɩkɛlɛ enii kɔ̃ nɩ yi dima wɔ yi buuku egyikĩĩ be nu. Sɛ itwu nunuma a, isiã enii mɔɔ ikila yɩ nɩ kɛɛ ɩwʋwá yɩ ma iwu ntwɛlɩ wɔ yɩ mba yɩ nwʋ na ama ɩahʋla ɩawɔ Gyehova mpayɩ. Akũ, John bɔ mpayɩ da Gyehova asɩ wɔ mɔɔ enii ɔkʋ̃ bela zῦ yɩ ahʋnlɩnɩ nu yɛlɩ manlɩ yɩ nɩ nwʋ. Bɛzabɛ mɔɔ nɩ bekila John nɩ, bo nu ɔkʋ̃ hanlɩ kɛɛ, “Iávalɩ nwʋmɔ kezimɔ yɩ adimakɛlɛ yɩ dɩ bela nɩ, nɩ ɩwɔ anyɩnlɩlelɛ paa, na nɩ yi kunlu twoli mɔɔ ɩlɛ yɩ nɩ nwʋ sɔ̃.” John yi ozumanlɛ-nwʋnlɛ yɩ ma yɛnwʋ kɛɛ, sɛ abiye kile nninyeni nwʋ anyɩnlɩzɔ-lɛ a, ɩma yɩ anyɩ de na ɩma yi kunlu twoli mɔɔ ɩlɛ yɩ nɩ nwʋ.
<<<P>>>
² Alɔbɔ odwokɔ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ, yɛda oyuwadɩ be mɔɔ mmenii iáhile nninyeni nwʋ anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ nu. Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ, mmenii lékile nninyeni nwʋ anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ wɔ oyuwadɩ yɩ awielɩyɛ mmɩlɩ yɩ nu. (2 Tim. 3:1, 2) Mmenii be wɔ kɛ a, bɛma bɛ ɛyɛɛlɛ fi kɛɛ be-kehile anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ wɔ mɔɔ mmenii mvʋlɔ bɛyɛ bɛma nɩ nwʋ. Mmenii be sʋ̃ wɔ kɛ a, bosunzu kɛɛ bɛsɛ bɛfata kɛɛ mmenii mvʋlɔ yɛ baaba bɛ, meti nwʋmɔ iáhyinyia kɛɛ bekehile nninye-ni nwʋ anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ. Sɛ abiye yɛ zɔ a, nɩ ɩdaa angʋmidi subanɩ ali.
<<<P>>>
³ Kɛ Gyehova yɩ azũmɩvʋlɔ nɩ, íni kɛɛ yɛyɛ yɛ dɩyɛ dɩkɛɛ mme-nii mɔɔ bɔwɔ oyuwadɩ yɩ nu nnɛ mɔɔ bɛáhile nninyeni nwʋ anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ nɩ. Sʋ̃mavʋlɔ Pɔɔlʋ hyɛlɩ yɛ ɛzɩzɩbɛ kɛɛ: ‘Yɛyɛ́ mme-nii mɔɔ yekile nninyeni nwʋ anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ.’ (Kol. 3:15) Meti sɛ mmenii mvʋlɔ zῦ bɛ ahʋnlɩnɩ nu yɛ debe ma yɛ a, ɩsɛ kɛɛ amɩyɛ sʋ̃ yɛzῦ yɛ ahʋnlɩnɩ nu yekile nwʋmɔ anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ. Wɔ dɩyɛzu-manlɛ eyi nu nɩ, yekozunzu ndwokɔyizialɛ asã eyimmɔ nwʋ:
