4 Ɛhɛlɛlɛdwokɔ mɔɔ yɛ dɩyɛzumanlɛ yɩ gyĩ zʋlɔ nɩ bʋwa yɛ ma yɛnwʋ kɛɛ nɩ Dwuumaa be wɔ Beliyia mɔɔ nɩ bɛpɛ kɛɛ bɔnwʋ Ɛhɛlɛlɛdwokɔ yɩ nu buwɔ. (Kɩnga Egyima 17:10, 11, NWT) Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ ma yɛnwʋ yɩ kɛɛ nɩ ɔzɔ Beliyiamaa yɩ wɔ ɛpɛ paa kɛɛ bɛkɛbɩbɛlɩ mɔɔ nɩ Pɔɔlʋ ikilehilee nɩ nu. Iluwa ɔzɔ ti, Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ kã bɔ nwʋ odwokɔ kɛɛ bɛdɩ mmenii mɔɔ “bɔwɔ adwɩnɩba” na “bediyeli odwokɔ yɩ” mɔɔ nɩ Pɔɔlʋ ikilehilee bɛ nɩ. Iluwa kɛɛ beleli mmɩlɩ bɛvalɩ mɔɔ nɩ Pɔɔlʋ ikilehilee bɛ nɩ bedindianlɩ Ɛhɛlɛlɛdwokɔ yɩ nwʋ nɩ ti, ɩmanlɩ benyianlɩ anwʋlazʋ paa kɛɛ mɔɔ nɩ ikilehilee bɛ nɩ dɩ nɔhʋanlɛ. Ɔzɔ la yɛ ɩdɩ wɔ amɩyɛ sʋ̃ yɛ ɔfʋã nu a. Sɛ yɛkɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ anzeyɩɩ ayiyialɩyɛ na enii mɔɔ ɩmaa ndɛnɩ nɩ bɔ Ɛhɛlɛlɛdwokɔ be a, yɛzayɛ sʋ̃ yɛfa yɛ Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ na yebuko yedi yɩ mɛda, na eyi ma yenyia anwʋlazʋ paa kɛɛ mɔɔ ikilehilee yɛ nɩ dɩ nɔhʋanlɛ.

5 Beliyiamaa yɩ bo nu sɔ̃maa ya yɛlɩ Kilisinyiamaa. (Egy. 17:12) Naasʋ̃ nɩ ndwokɔzɩzɩbɛ be ɩbʋbɔɔ ɩba. Dwuumaa mɔɔ nɩ bɔwɔ Tɛsalonika nɩ hɔlɩ Beliyia na “bɛzɩkɛlɩ ɔlɔmɩ yɩ bɛ adwɩnɩ” ma bodwodwuli bediyialɩ mmenii eyimmɔ mɔɔ bɛya bɛyɛlɩ Kilisinyiamaa a, ɩlɛkyɛlɩ nɩ. (Egy. 17:13) Naasʋ̃ Kilisinyiamaa eyimmɔ bɛáma mɔɔ mmenii nɩ yɛlɩ nɩ ma bɔlɔ kã bɛ. Iluwakɛɛ nɩ bɛpɩbɛlɩ Ɛhɛlɛlɛdwokɔ yɩ nu eyile bela nɩ ti, benyianlɩ anwʋlazʋ paa kɛɛ nɔhʋanlɛ yɩ la yɛ bɔwɔ numɔ yɩ a. Meti ɩáyɛ nnwʋanlɩ kulaa kɛɛ bɔ tʋ diminli wɔ dedi yi nu. Amɩyɛ sʋ̃, sɛ yede mmɩlɩ yɛpɩbɛlɩ Ɛhɛlɛlɛdwokɔ yɩ nu na yɛdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ nwʋmɔ eyile bela a, ɩkɔwʋwa yɛ ma yɛkɛva nɔhʋanlɛlilɛ yekegyina ndwokɔzɩzɩbɛ munwa pindinyii.

• Nɩ Mosisi Mmɛla yɩ pɩbɛ kɛɛ bɔkɔwɔ afɔlɩ eyile bela.—Ɛkɩ. 29:38-42; 30:7, 8.

• Gyehova hã hileli Dwohyuwa kɛɛ ɩyɩnga Mmɛla nɩ na ɩdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ nwʋmɔ “awulohyɩnlɩyɛ nɩɩ awulodwolɩyɛ.”—Dwh. 1:8, ad.

• Gyehova hyɛlɩ Yizilaya awulombunli yi kɛɛ behilá Nyamɩnlɩ yi Mmɛla yɩ zʋ na bɛhɛlɛ biye bɛma bɔ nwʋ, na bɛyɩngá yɩ eyile bela.—Dit. 17:18-20.

6 Yɛfa awulohyɩnlɩyɛ odwokɔ yɩ eyile bela iluwakɛɛ ɩbʋwa yɛ ma yɛfa yɛ adwɩnɩ yesiye Gyehova nɩ ɔzʋ̃mɩnlɛ mɔɔ yɛfa yɛma yɩ nɩ zʋ. Gyehova dɩ yɛ Nyamɩnlɩ, meti nwʋmɔ hyinyia paa kɛɛ yenyia mmɩlɩ yɛfa yɛsʋ̃mɩ yɩ. Idɔ yɛ na ɩpɛ kɛɛ yenyia nnwʋanlɩ mɔɔ íni awielɩyɛ. (Dwɔ. 3:16) Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ ma yɛnwʋ yɩ kɛɛ, sɛ yede mmɩlɩ yɛsʋ̃mɩ Gyehova eyile bela a, yɩ anyɩ sɔ yɩ paa. Mmeliyema
