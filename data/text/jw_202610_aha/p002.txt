ALƲBƆ YAALƐ MƆƆ IZĨ LHƆ
<<<P>>>
ƆWƖƐNƖLƐ Aɣanɩ yɩ mɔɔ ɩɣalɩ wɔ January 1, 1926 nɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ: “Llɛ́kyɛ, Nyamɩnlɩ kɛhyɛ yɩ dima yɩ animinyiamɩ. lkɛyɩ mbiyemmɔ ma bɛkɛyɛ yɩ dasɩvʋlɔmaa wɔ azɩlɛ yɩ zʋ.”
<<<P>>>
Wɔ 1926 nu nɩ, Gyehova lʋwalɩ Gyisɛsɩ mɔɔ ɩdɩ asafʋ yi ti nɩ zʋ wʋwalɩ Kilisinyiamaa nɩ ma bɔnwʋnlɩ kɛɛ, dasɩlɛlilɛ egyima yɩ yɛ ɩsɛ kɛɛ bɛma nwʋmɔ hyinyia bɛ paa wɔ bɛ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu a. Mɔɔ ɩkɛyɛ na dasɩlɛlilɛ egyima yɩ iahɔlɩ zʋlɔ nɩ, “sũmɩvʋlɔ nɔhʋanlɛvʋlɔ mɔɔ izi nɣɛlɛbɛ nɩ” yelɩ awuuku mvʋlɔ manlɩ mmeliyema yɩ, na bɛyɛlɩ nzɩnzanlɩyɛ wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛhɔlɛ nu sɔ̃. Akʋ̃ sɔ̃, benyianlɩ ntɩyasɩ fʋlɔ mɔɔ ɩfalɩ asɛdɩ mɔɔ Kilisinyianii bela wɔ nɩ nwʋ. (Mt. 24:45) Gyisɛsɩ wʋwalɩ bɛ ma bɛ sa hanlɩ nniyia nu mahyini azulɔtu mɔɔ bɛfa bɛpʋlɩntɩ awuuku na ama dasɩlɛlilɛ egyima iahɔlɩ zʋlɔ.

AWUUKU MVƲLƆ NƖƖ NZƖNZANLƖYƐ MƆƆ BƐYƐLƖ WƆ DƖYƐZUMANLƐHƆLƐ NU
<<<P>>>
Wɔ April eziyanɩ yɩ nu nɩ, The Golden Age mɔɔ kakɩ yɩ bɔfʋlɔ yɩ Awake! nɩ wɔlɩ amanɩyɛ kɛɛ bɛyɩ buuku fʋlɔ be ali mɔɔ bɔfʋlɔ yɩ Deliverance. Nɩ ɔzɔ buuku yi kɛɣa keziye Studies in the Scriptures buuku yi yɩ agyia nu na ɩaɣa ɩaɣɛ buuku titili mɔɔ bɛkɛva bɛkɛyɛ dasɩlɛlilɛ egyima yɩ. Mɔɔ ɩkɛyɛ na bɛanyia awuuku sũmaa mɔɔ nɩ behyinyia nɩ, mmeliyema mɔɔ bɔwɔ Bɛtɛlɩ nɩ dɔlɩ mahyini be mɔɔ nɩ ɩkʋla ɩpʋlɩntɩ awuuku 22 wɔ miniti ɔkʋ̃ nu. Nɩ Bayɩbʋlʋ Zumanlɛmaa yɩ fa buuku fʋlɔ yɩ be ma abiyela mɔɔ bekeli yi dasɩlɛ nɩ.
<<<P>>>
Nɩ Bayɩbʋlʋ Zumanlɛmaa yɩ va alʋbɔ sũmaa zuma Studies in the Scriptures buuku yi wɔ bɛ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʋ. Naasʋ̃, wɔ May eziyanɩ yɩ ahyɛasɩ nɩ, bɔwɔlɩ yɩ tu kɛɛ bosumaa dɩyɛzumanlɛ ndwokɔ mɔɔ ɩwɔ Ɔwɩɛnɩlɛ Aɣanɩ yɩ nu nɩ. Mɩnla yɛ ɩzʋ numɔ ɩɣalɩ a?
<<<P>>>
Asafʋ kʋ̃ be hɛlɛlɩ hɔ manlɩ Eliyema Rutherford kɛɛ: “Wɔ Bɔɔlɔ bela, yesuma dɩyɛzumanlɛ odwokɔ mɔɔ ahyɩhyɛdɩ yɩ hile yɛ kɛɛ yezuma wɔ ɔzɔ eɣile nɩ. Yɛla bɛ asɩ paa kɛɛ ndwokɔ mvʋlɔ bela mɔɔ ɩkɛɣa nɩ, bɔbʋwa yɛ ma yɛtɩ yɩ tʋ ndɛndɛ.”
<<<P>>>
Ɔzɔ ntɩyasɩ fʋlɔ nɩ wʋwalɩ Bayɩbʋlʋ Zumanlɛmaa nɩ ma bɛvalɩ bɛ adwɩnɩ beziyeli dasɩlɛlilɛ egyima yɩ zʋ. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, dɩyɛzumanlɛ odwokɔ kʋ̃ mɔɔ ise “Character or Covenant—Which?” (Subanɩ anzɛnyɩɩ Apamɩ?) nɩ hilehileli numɔ kɛɛ, kɛ Kilisinyianii yɩ asɛdɩ titili mɔɔ nwʋmɔ hyinyia paa yɛ lɛ kɛɛ ɩkɔwɔ mmɔdɩnɩ ɩkɛzɩnza yi subanɩ, ɩkɛla kunlumiba ali, na ɩ nɩɩ mmenii ali yi bowɔ a. Na ɩva ɩhɩnlanlɩ nwʋmɔ kɛɛ, Gyehova hyɛlɩ Kilisinyiamaa kɛɛ bɛyɛ yɩ dasɩvʋlɔmaa. Meti, iluwakɛɛ bɔdɔ yɩ nɩ ti, ɩsɛ kɛɛ Kilisinyianii bela tiye ahyɛdɩ eyi, na isiyeziye yɩ nwʋ kɛɛ ɩkɛhã odwokɔba yɩ be.
<<<P>>>
Dɩyɛzumanlɛ odwokɔ fʋlɔ mɔɔ ise “Sacrifice and Service” (Afɔlɩwɔlɛ nɩɩ Ɔzũmɩnlɛ) nɩ zili zʋlɔ owudo kɛɛ, Kilisinyiamaa sũmɩ Gyehova nɔhʋanlɛ nu fa da yɩ ali kɛɛ bɔdɔ yɩ. Dɩyɛzumanlɛ odwokɔ yɩ hyɛlɩ Bayɩbʋlʋ Zumanlɛmaa nɩ munwatĩĩ ezɩzɩbɛ kɛɛ bɛyɛ mɔɔ bɔkɔhʋla bela bɛva bɛhã odwokɔba yɩ behile mmenii dʋdʋ bela mɔɔ bɛ sa kɛhã bɛ nɩ. —Ayɩ. 43:10, 12; Mt. 24:14.
