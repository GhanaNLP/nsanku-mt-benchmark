BƆTAYI
Ikɔwʊwa yɛ ma yɛkɔnwu sianti mɔɔ ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛma yɛ anyɩ sɔ ɔnwʊmɔ ɔhʋanɩ mɔɔ yenyia kɛɛ yɛbʊwaa wɔ nnwʊánlıdɩyɛ asã mɔɔ ɩkɔlɩ zʋlɔ wɔ nnɛ nɩ nu.

Ma Wʊ Ɔfʋã Nu Dɩyɛ Mɔɔ Ɛyɛɛ Wɔ Nnwʊánlıdɩyɛ Mɔɔ Ɩkɔlɩ Zʋlɔ Wɔ Nnɛ Nɩ Idú Bulɔ Ima Wʊ
<<<P>>>
“Yɛ abiyela ɩkɔnwʋ kɛɛ mʋnlʋmmɔ bɛdɩ mɩ adʋwavʋlɔ a.”—DWƆ. 13:35.
<<<P>>>
MMƖLƖ be, Gyehova wukoli Ngyemvile Kɔkɔlɔ yı nu ma yi mmenii luwalɩ numɔ ɩva wɔlɩ bɔ nwʋ banɩ wɔ Igyibiti azogyiamaa nɩ bɔ nwʋ. Sɛ ɔwɔ ɛkɛnɩ yɛ odwokɔ eyi zili a, kila kezimɔ ankı wʊ anyɩ kele. Akʋ̃ sɔ̃, sɛ nɩ ɔwɔ ɛkɛnɩ be yɛ ɔnwʋnlɩ kɛɛ Gyisɛsɩ ɩsaa mme-nii adimakɛlɛ na itunwoo awuvʋlɔ a, ankı kila kezimɔ wʊ anyɩ kele! Eyimmɔ yɛ lɛ nnwʊánlı nninyeni mɔɔ Bayɩbʋlʋ yı hanlɩ nwʊmɔ odwokɔ nɩ numɔ be. Pɩlazunlɛ bela íni nwʊmɔ kɛɛ enii bela mɔɔ ɩwɔ ahʋnlɩnıba mɔɔ ɩvalɩ yı anyɩ ɩnwʋnlɩ nnwʊánlı nninyeni mɔɔ izizi-li nɩ kɔnwʋ kɛɛ Nyamɩnlɩ yɛ ɩmanlɩ izizili a. (Mt. 15:31) Naasʋ̃ nnwʊánlı nninyeni eyimmɔ zizili a íhyɛ. Asʋ̃ nnwʊánlı nninyeni be isizii wɔ nnɛ sʋ̃ a? Nyɛ! Mɩnla ti mɔɔ yɛkãã zɔ a?
<<<P>>>
2 Bekile nnwʊánlıdɩyɛ tʋ kɛɛ debe mɔɔ ɩbʋlʋ enii ntɩyası zʋ anzɛnyʋ debe mɔɔ enii ulɔkula ɩkɛyɛ kyĩ. Meti, nɩ ikile kɛɛ ɩdɩ debe mɔɔ Gyehova yɩzayɩ kɔ̃ la yɛ ɩkʋla ɩfa yi tumi ɩyɛ a. Wɔ dɩyɛ-zumanlɛ eyi nu nɩ, yekozunzu nnwʊánlıdɩyɛ asã be mɔɔ Gyehova ɩyɛɛ wɔ yı ahyɩhyɛdɩ nu wɔ nnɛ nɩ nwʋ. Yɛkɔhʋla yɛkɛhã kɛɛ ɩdɩ nnwʊánlıdɩyɛ iluwakɛɛ Gyehova yɩzayɩ nkʋ̃ la yɛ ɩkɔhʋla ɩkɛma eyimmɔ munwatĩĩ kezizi a. Sɛ yesunzuu nninyeni asã yı numɔ ɔkʋ̃ bela nwʋ a, yɛkɔnwu ahʋanɩ mɔɔ yekoluwa zʋlɔ yɛkɛyɛ Gyehova yı “ɔhɔnwʋ egyimayɛlɛmaa” wɔ nnwʊánlı nninyeni eyimmɔ mɔɔ ɩyɛɛ nɩ nu.—Kɩnga 1 Kɔlɛntɩmaa 3:9, NWT.
