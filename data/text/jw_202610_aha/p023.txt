20 Ɔhɔnwʊmmɔ baaba dɩ akyɛdɩ mɔɔ ɩzʊ Gye-
hova kɛ, na yehyinyia bɛ wɔ nnɛ tɛla mmılɩ
bela mɔ ɩya ɩzĩ thɔ. Meti, ɛ nɩ mmenii nyia
ɔhɔnwʊvalɛ kɛmɔ Danɩyɛlɩ yɛlɩ nɩ. Pɩbɛ ɔhɔ-
nwʊmmɔ mɔɔ bɛdɩ kɛɛ Gyelɩmaya wɔ wʊ asafo yi
nu, mɔɔ ɔkɔhʊla kozuma debe wɔ bɛ kɛnɩ na bɛa-
hyɛ wʊ ɛzɩzɩbɛ sʊ̃. Ɛ nɩ mmenii mɔɔ bɔkɔwʊwa
wʊ ma ekezi wʊ kɛnya kɛɛ ekeli Gyehova nɔ-
hvanlɛ nɩ bɔwɔ́. Sɛ nwʊmɔ ba hyinyia a, ɔzʊ̃
kãnwvazı nu siã mmenii mvʊlɔ ma bɔwɔ́ mpayɩ
bɛma wʊ. Mɔɔ ɩtɛla eyi munwala nɩ, kakɛ kɛɛ
Gyehova dɩ wʊ Ɔhɔnwʊba mɔɔ ibike wʊ paa,
na iléyiaki wʊ kyĩ. (Hib. 13:5) Sɛ ye munwatĩĩ
yɛfa Gyehova kɛɛ yɛ ɔhɔnwʊba mɔɔ ibike yɛ
paa a, ɩkɛma yɛkɔwʊwa yɛ nwʊ yɛ nwʊ ma yeke-
li nɔhvanlɛ kɛma Gyehova. Na ɩkɛma yekenyia
ɔnwʊmɔ ɔhvanɩ yɛkɛyɛ ɔhɔnwʊmmɔ daabaa.

• Yɛ́ azʊnlɩ tiye yi: Duwa Bayɩbʊlʊ ɛɣɩngalɛ zʊ
tiye Gyehova. De mmılɩ suma mɔɔ ɩpɛ yɩ nɩɩ
mɔɔ ɩábɛyɩ, kezimɔ ɩwʊwalɩ mmenii be wɔ
Bayɩbʊlʊ mmılɩ nu, nɩɩ kezimɔ ɩkɔhʊla ɩkɔ-

• Nyia abʊtalɩ: Kakɛ kɛɛ ide mmılɩ na ama
yɛahʊla yeanyia abiye yi nu anwʊlazʊ na
yɛava yɩ ɔhɔnwʊ. Meti, bɔ mmɔdɩnı kɛɛ
ɛkɛɣa kɛyɛ Gyehova yɩ ɔhɔnwʊ iluwakɛɛ
yɩzaɣɩ sʊ̃ ɩpɛ paa kɛɛ ɩyɛ wʊ ɔhɔnwʊ.
