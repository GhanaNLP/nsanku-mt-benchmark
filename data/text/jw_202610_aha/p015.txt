8 Mmɩlɩ bela mɔɔ yɛkɛyɛ dasɩlelilɛ egyima yɩ bɛ nɩ, nɩ yɛbʊwaa Gyehova nɩɩ Gyisɛsɩ wɔ nnwʊánlɩdɩyɛ mɔɔ bɛyɛɛ wɔ nnɛ nɩ nu. Nɔhʊanlɛ odwokɔ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ, Gyehova nɩɩ Gyisɛsɩ da yɛ mɛda. Gyisɛsɩ wɔlɩ anʊhʊba kɛɛ: “Mɩ nɩɩ bɛ wɔ ɛkɛ mmɩlɩ munwala kɔbʋmɩ oyuwadɩ eyi yɩ awielɩyɛ.” (Mt. 28:20, NWT) Akʊ̃ sɔ̃, sɛ yɛyɛɛ dasɩlelilɛ egyima yɩ a, awɔvɔlɔ yɩ kɩnla yɛ nwʊ.—Yik. 14:6, 7.

9 Nkɔmɩhyɛnii Mayɩka hyɛlɩ nnwʊánlɩdɩyɛ bɛ mɔɔ isii wɔ nnɛ nɩ nwʊ nkɔmɩ dianlɩ kɛ. Ɔzɔ nnwʊánlɩdɩyɛ nɩ yɛ lɛ ɔkũyɛlɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ mmenii bɛ avinli nɩ. Gyehova valɩ yɩ sunzuma yɩ hanlɩ Mayɩka ma thɛlɛlɩ kezimɔ Gyehova kohunwahunwa mmenii munwa na bɛawʊwa wɔ ɔkũyɛlɛ nu nɩ nwʊ odwokɔ. (Kɩnga Mika 2:12.) Yɛnwʊ kɛɛ ɔzɔ nkɔmɩhyɛ yɩ ya numɔ iluwakɛɛ Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ mmenii da wɔ ɔkũyɛlɛ nu távalɩ nwʊmɔ subanɩ mɔɔ bo nu bela lɛ yɩ anzɛnyɩ dɩka bela mɔɔ bo nu bela zũ nɩ. Yɛ nu ndwomidulɛvʊlɔ Devidi yɛ adwɩnɩ, mmɩlɩ mɔ thɛlɛlɩ kɛɛ, “Kila! Kezimɔ ɩdɩ baaba na ɩyɛ kɩnlɩma kɛɛ mmeliyema kɔwɔ numɔ kɔwʊwa wɔ ɔkũyɛlɛ nu!” nɩ.—Ndw. 133:1.
<<<P>>>
10 Nnɛ yɛnwʊ ɔkũyɛlɛ eyi wɔ ahvanɩ sʊ̃maa zɛ zʋ. Kɛ ndianwʊ nɩ, távalɩ nwʊmɔ dɩka bela mɔɔ yɛwɔ nɩ, yenyia sunzuma ɔfʋã nu alɩyɛ ɔkũ yɩ la wɔ yɛ dɩyɛzumanlɛ nɩɩ yɛ ayiyialɩyɛ nyezile nu mmalɛ tʊ. Mmeliyema mɔɔ ɩsʋnɩ dɩka nu manlɩ mɔɔ bo nu bela zũ numɔ nɩ fa ɔkũyɛlɛ bo numɔ yɛ egyima wɔ Bɛtɩlɩ nɩɩ dɩka mɔɔ bɛyɛ asafo aliyagyinanlɛ nwʊ egyima nɩ wɔ oyuwadɩ yɩ dɩka bela. Ɔkũyɛlɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ yɛ avinli nɩ, numɔ yɛ sɩ paa. Yɛáma ɔhvanɩ ma ozukua mɔɔ yɛwɔ anzɛnyɩ ehyinyia mɔɔ yedi, yɛ amamlɛ nu manlɩwulɛndwokɔ sɩkɛ ɔkũyɛlɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ yɛ avinli nɩ. Wɔ oyuwadɩ yɩ dɩka bela mɔɔ yɛkɔhɔ nɩ, yɛlɛ mmeliyema mɔɔ beziyeziye bɔ nwʊ kɛɛ bɛkɛva nnwʊnlamɩ ɔfʋã nu mmʊwalyɛ mɔɔ yehyinyia nɩ bɛkɛma yɛ, na bɛahyɛ yɛ ezɩzɩbɛ ma yeahɔ zʋlɔ yɛazʊ̃mɩ Gyehova. Ilésili wɔ abakɔsɛmɩ nu kyĩ kɛɛ mmenii mɔɔ bɔzʊ̃ amanlɩ sʊ̃maa nu mɔɔ ɩsʊnɩ bɛ amamlɛ nɩ kɛya kɔwɔ numɔ na bɛanyia adwɩnɩ ɔkʊ̃ wɔ nninyeni nwʊ na bɛayɛ mɔɔ Gyehova pɛ yɩ.

11 Ɔkũyɛlɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ yɛ avinli nɩ dɩ nnwʊánlɩdɩyɛ iluwakɛɛ kɛ ɔzɔ yɛ ɩdɩ wɔ oyuwadɩ yɩ nu a. Wɔ oyuwadɩ nu dɩka bela mɔɔ ɔkɔhɔ nɩ, ɔkɔnwʊ mmenii nu nkyɩkyɛmɩ. Eyile bela yɛtɩ kʊnɛ, atunwadɩlɛ nɩɩ basabasayɛlɛ nwʊ amanɩyɛwɔlɛ. Alɔbɔ odwokɔ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ, nnɛ mmenii nu hyɩhyɛ na ɔhawʊ sʊ̃maa hyɛ oyuwadɩ yɩ nu manlɩ. Kɛmɔ Bayɩbʊlʊ hanlɩ nɩ, mmenii ‘bɛáli nɔhvanlɛ, bɛáhʋla bɛ nɩɩ bɛ yɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ, bɛdɩ dimazɩkɛlɛmaa, bɛáhʋla bɛhyɛ bɔ nwʊ zʋ, bɔ nwʊ yɛ ɛyɛlɛ, bɛ anyɩ iáleli baabayɛlɛ nwʊ, bɛyɩ mmenii mvʊlɔ bɛma, bɛ azʊ̃ yɛ sɩ, bepigyiabigyia bɔ nwʊ sʋlɔ.’ (2 Tim. 3:2-4, NWT) lávalɩ nwʊmɔ kɛɛ Setanɩ nɩɩ yɩ azunzuma evinli yɛ bedii oyuwadɩ yɩ zʋ tumi nɩ, ɔkũyɛlɛ la nwʊ la wɔ yɛ avinli. (1 Dwɔ. 5:19; Yik. 12:9) Eyi dɩ nnwʊánlɩdɩyɛ amba!
