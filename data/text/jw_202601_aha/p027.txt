yɛkɛva Bayɩbʋlʋ nu nɔhvanlɛ odwokɔ yɩ yekehilehile mmenii. Sɛ mmenii anyɩ iáleli nwʋmɔ po a, yɛkɔ zʋlɔ yɛkã. Asũ eyi kile kɛɛ sɛ yɛkãã nɔhvanlɛ odwokɔ be anzɛnyɩɩ odwokɔba yɩ a, yɛkã la mɔɔ yɛádwɩnɩ kezimɔ yɛkɛhã yɩ nɩ mmɩlɩ mɔ yɛkɛhã yɩ nɩ nwʋ a? Ídɩ zɔ kʋlaa! Wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ eyi nu nɩ, yekeli bɔmʋnlɩ yekozunzu odwokɔyizialɛ eyi nwʋ: Ɛhɩnla dɩka yɛ yɛkɔnwu nɔhvanlɛ yɩ a? Akũ yekezia yekozunzu ndwokɔyizialɛ eyimmɔ nwʋ: Mɩnla ti mɔɔ ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛkã nɔhvanlɛ a? Mɩnla ɔhvanɩ yɛ yekoluwa zʋ yɛkɛhã a? Na mɩnla mmɩlɩ yɛ ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛkã Bayɩbʋlʋ nu nɔhvanlɛ odwokɔ yɩ yekile mmenii a? Ndwokɔyizialɛ eyimmɔ mɔɔ yekenyia nwʋmɔ munwalyɛ nɩ kɔwʋwa yɛ ma yekoluwa anyɩnlɩdɩlɛ ɔhvanɩ zʋ yɛkɛhã odwokɔba yɩ wɔ mmɩlɩba nu. Akũ sɔ̃, ɩkɔwʋwa yɛ ma yɛkɔhɔlɩ zʋlɔ yekeli nɔhvanlɛ wɔ debela nu.

ƐHƖNLA DƖKA YƐ YƐKƆNWU NƆHVANLƐ YƖ A?
<<<P>>>
4 Gyehova yɩ kɛnɩ yɛ nɔhvanlɛ zʋ a. Meti odwokɔ bela mɔɔ ɩkã nɩ dɩ nɔhvanlɛ. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, yi odwokɔ mɔɔ ɩfalɩ baaba nɩɩ tanɩ nwʋ nɩ munwala dɩ nɔhvanlɛ. (Ndw. 19:9; 119:142, 151) Dɩyɛ bela mɔɔ ɩhyɛ nwʋmɔ nkɔmɩ nɩ ba numɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ. (Ayɩ. 55:10, 11) Sɛ ɩbɔ anvhuba kɛɛ ɩkɛyɛ debe a, ididili zʋlɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ. (Nɔ. 23:19) Mɔɔ ɩdɩ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ, Gyehova lɔ́kʋla keli awohwi kyĩ! (Hib. 6:18) Sianti azʋlɔtʋ eyi ti mɔɔ Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ fʋlɔ Gyehova kɛɛ “nɔhvanlɛ Nyamɩnlɩ” yɩ a.—Ndw. 31:5.
<<<P>>>
5 Mbiyemmɔ kã kɛɛ, ɩyɛ sɩ kɛɛ yɛkɔnwu Gyehova, “nɔhvanlɛ Nyamɩnlɩ” nɩ. Naasũ mɔɔ bɛkã nɩ ídɩ nɔhvanlɛ kʋlaa, iluwakɛɛ abɔdɩ di dasɩlɛ kɛɛ Nyamɩnlɩ wɔ kɛnɩ. (Wul. 1:20) Mmɩlɩ mɔ sʋ̃mavʋlɔ Pɔɔlʋ ɩ nɩɩ Giliki nnimidimaa be mɔɔ bɔwɔ Atɛnsɩ tɩndɛɛ nɩ, ɩhanlɩ kɛɛ Nyamɩnlɩ pɛ kɛɛ ‘yɛnwʋ yɩ’ na ɩ “nɩɩ ye nu bela yɛ avinli iáwa.” (Kɩnga Egyima 17:27.) Nɔhvanlɛ odwokɔ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ, Gyehova yɩzayɩ ɩtwĩ kãnwʋazɩmaa mɔɔ bɛpɩbɛɛ nɔhvanlɛ yɩ nɩ ma bebike yi.—Dwɔ. 6:44.
<<<P>>>
6 Ɔhvanɩ ɔkũ mɔɔ yekoluwa zʋlɔ yɛkɔnwʋ Gyehova yɛ lɛ kɛɛ yekozuma Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ. Mmenyia mɔɔ bɛhɛlɛlɩ Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ nɩ, Nyamɩnlɩ yi sunzuma nwanzanwanza yɛ ɩva ɩhanlɩ bɛ ma bɛhɛlɛlɩ a. (2 Pit. 1:20, 21) Meti odwokɔ bela mɔɔ ɩwɔ Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ nu nɩ dɩ nɔhvanlɛ na sa yɛkʋla yede yedi paa. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ ma yɛnwʋ kɛɛ Gyehova yɛ ɩwɔlɩ amansɔnɩ yɩ nu dɩyɛ bela mɔɔ ɩwɔ numɔ nɩ a. (Gyn. 1:1, 26) Akũ sɔ̃, ɩma yɛnwʋ sianti mɔɔ ye munwala yɛyɛ tanɩ, nɩɩ sianti mɔɔ amanɩyɛnwʋnlɛ nɩɩ owulo dʋ bɩndɩnlɩ oyuwadɩ yɩ nu nɩ a. (Wul. 5:12; 6:23) Biyekũ sɔ̃, Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ ma yɛnwʋ kɛɛ atutɩɩ mɔɔ Setanɩ, “awohwimaa munwala bɛ zɩ” va ɣa adasamaa bɔ zʋ nɩ, Gyehova koluwa yɩ Ɣa yɩ zʋ kɛyɩ yɩ wɔ kɛ na iadindiã nninyeni buwɔ. (Dwɔ. 8:44; Wul. 16:20) Dɔkʋ̃ sɔ̃, Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ ma yɛnwʋ kɛɛ Gyisɛsɩ kezike tanɩyɛlɛmaa, ikodunwo awuvulɔ, ikeziyeziye azɩlɛ yɩ ma ɩkɛyɛ paladayɩsɩ, na ɩkɔwʋwa adasamaa ma bekeli munli dɔkʋ̃. (Dwɔ. 11:25, 26; 1 Dwɔ. 3:8) Gyehova va nɔhvanlɛ yɩ ihilehile yɛ, na ɩma ɔnwʋmɔ ɔhvanɩ yɛ kɛɛ yɛzayɛ sɔ̃ yɛvá yehilehile mmenii. Eyi dɩ ɔnwʋmɔ ɔhvanɩ kezile paa!—Mt. 28:19, 20.
