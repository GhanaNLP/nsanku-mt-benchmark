Yɛ Anyɩ Sɔ Elelɛdɩyɛ Nɩ
<<<P>>>
BƲTAYƖ
<<<P>>>
Yekehila kezimɔ yekehile kɛɛ yɛ anyɩ sɔ elelɛdɩyɛ yɩ nɩ.
<<<P>>>
Kɛ Ɩkɛyɛ Na Wʊahile Kɛɛ Wʊ Anyɩ Sɔ Elelɛdɩyɛ Nɩ a?
<<<P>>>
“Kɩlayɩsɩ yɩ ɔlɔlɛ nɩ yɛ isiye yɛ a.”—2 KƆL. 5:14.
<<<P>>>
FA YƖ kɛɛ aliyia be wu gua wʊ zʋ, na abiye va yɩ nnwʋanlɩ diã asianɩ nu ɩya ɩyɩ wʊ ivinde. Kɛ ɛkɛdɩ nyanlɩ ekezia a? Asʋ̃ ɛlɛ́fa anyɩnlɩlelɛ kɛla yɩ asɩ kɛɛ ile wʊ nnwʋanlɩ a? Pɩlazunɛ bela íni nwʋmɔ kɛɛ ɔzʋ̃ wʊ ahʋnlɩnɩ nu ɛkɛla yɩ asɩ paa, na wʊahile kɛɛ wʊ anyɩ sɔ mmɔdɩnɩ mɔɔ ɩwɔlɩ ɩva ileli wʊ nnwʋanlɩ nɩ, na wʊ ɛyɛɛlɛ iléfi mɔɔ ɩyelɩ ɩmanlɩ wʊ nɩ kyĩ.
<<<P>>>
2 Kɛmɔ dɩyɛzʋmalɛ mɔɔ ili eyi yɩ anyunu nu nɩ hilehileli numɔ nɩ, yehyinyia abiye na iale yɛ wɔ tanɩ nu owulo tʋ. Gyisɛsɩ yɩ elelɛdɩyɛ afɔlɩ nɩ yɛ ɩkɔhʋla ikele yɛ na ama (1) bɛava yɛ tanɩ mɔɔ yɛyɛ nɩ bɛahyɛ yɛ, (2) yɛanyia anyɩnlɩlazʋ kɛɛ eyile be yekeli munli, nɩɩ (3) yɛ nɩɩ Gyehova yɛanyia abusunwawɔlɛ mɔɔ numɔ yɛ sɩ. Iluwa elelɛdɩyɛ yɩ ti, yɛwɔ anyɩnlɩlazʋ paa kɛɛ eyile be yɛkɔwʋwa azɩ daabaa wɔ oyuwadɩ fʋlɔ yɩ nu. Nɔhʋanlɛ, Gyisɛsɩ hileli kɛɛ ɩdɔ yɛ mmɩlɩ mɔ ɩvalɩ yɩ nnwʋanlɩ idianlɩ ɛkɛnɩ ɩmanlɩ yɛ nɩ. Na ansaana ɩkɛya azɩlɛ yɩ zʋ po nɩ, nɩ ɩdɔ yɛ. (Ɛɣɛ. 8:30, 31) Sɔ̃mavʋlɔ Pɔɔlʋ hɛlɛlɩ kɛɛ “Kɩlayɩsɩ yɩ ɔlɔlɛ nɩ yɛ isiye yɛ a.” (Kɩnga 2 Kɔlɛntɩmaa 5:14, 15.) Eyi kile kɛɛ ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛda yɩ ali kɛɛ yɛ anyɩ sɔ ɔlɔlɛ mɔɔ Gyisɛsɩ lalɩ yɩ ali hileli yɛ nɩ, na yɛ ɛyɛɛlɛ iléfi mɔɔ ɩyelɩ ɩmanlɩ yɛ nɩ kyĩ.
<<<P>>>
3 Kɛ ɩkɛyɛ na wʊahile anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ wʊama elelɛdɩyɛ nɩ a? Wʊ munwatɩyɛ kɔhʋla kɛyɛ sʋnʋnkʋ̃ wɔ mmenii mvʋlɔ dɩyɛ nwʋ. Mɩnla ti a? Fa yɩ kɛɛ mmenii asã be botuu ɔhʋanɩ bɔkɔhɔ dɩka ɔkʋ̃, naasʋ̃ ɩsʋnɩ dɩka mɔɔ bo nu bela ɩzʋ̃ ɩbaa. Pɩlazunɛ bela íni nwʋmɔ kɛɛ ɩsʋnɩ ɔhʋanɩ mɔɔ bo nu bela koluwa zʋ. Ɔzɔ la sʋ̃ yɛ “ɔhʋanɩ” mɔɔ okoluwa zʋlɔ ekehile anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ ɛkɛma elelɛdɩyɛ nɩ kɔhʋla kɛyɛ sʋnʋnkʋ̃ wɔ mmenii mvʋlɔ bɛ dɩyɛ nwʋ a, na eyi gyĩ kezimɔ ɛ nɩɩ Gyehova avinli abusunwawɔlɛ dɩ nɩ zʋ. Dɩyɛzʋmalɛ eyi kɔwʋwa mmenii eyimmɔ ma be-
<<<P>>>
1-2. Gyisɛsɩ valɩ yɩ nnwʋanlɩ dianlɩ kɛnɩ manlɩ yɛ, mɩnla yɛ ɩsɛ kɛɛ eyi kã yɛ ma yɛyɛ a, na mɩnla ti a? (2 Kɔlɛntɩmaa 5:14, 15) (Kila mfonyini yi sɔ̃.)
<<<P>>>
