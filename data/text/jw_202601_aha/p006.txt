Isɛ kɛɛ yɛkɔ zʋlɔ yesuma Gyehova nwʋ dɩyɛ, yɛda subanɩba ali na yɛma Gyehova bɔ yɛ nwʋ banɩ (Kila nkyɩkyɛmi 18)

SUMAZUMA
DEVIDI NA MA GYEHOVA
WƆ́ WƲ NWƲ BANƖ
<<<P>>>
14 Sɛ yekenyia anyɩnlɩlelɛ mɔɔ idi munli a, mɛyɩ nɩ ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛma Gyehova bɔ yɛ nwʋ banɩ. Ebiye a ekeyizia wʋ kɛɛ, mɩnla ɔhwanɩ zʋ yɛ Gyehova bɔ yɛ nwʋ banɩ a? Mɩnla yɛ ɩsɛ kɛɛ mɩyɛ na ama Gyehova ɩawɔ mʋ nwʋ banɩ a?
<<<P>>>
15 Nɩ Belemgbunli Devidi wɔ anwʋlazʋ kɛɛ Gyehova kɔwɔ yɩ nwʋ banɩ mmɩlɩ mɔ nɩ iyiyiaa ndwokɔzɩzɩbɛ nɩ. (Kɩnga Ndwomi 27:5.) Mɩnla ɔhwanɩ zʋ yɛ nnɛ Gyehova bɔ yɩ asɔ̃mɩvʋlɔ nwʋ banɩ a? Ibɔ yɛ nwʋ banɩ wɔ dɩyɛ bela mɔɔ ɩkɔhʋla kɛzɛkɛ dedi mɔɔ yɛwɔ wɔ yi nu nɩ nwʋ. Gyehova bɔ yɛ anwʋhʋba kɛɛ akʋndɩ bela mɔɔ bɛkɛva kɛɣa yɛ zʋ nɩ, ilékyina. (Ndw. 34:7; Ayɩ. 54:17) Iwɔ numɔ kɛɛ Setanɩ, azunzuma evinli nɩ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ botwodwu betiyia yɛ nɩ wɔ tumi dɩyɛ, naasɔ̃ Gyehova wɔ tumi tɛla bɛ kʋlaa. Sɛ ɩbayɩ kɛɛ yewu po a, Gyehova kodunwo yɛ. (1 Kɔl. 15:55-57; Yik. 21:3, 4) Akɔ̃ sɔ̃, Gyehova bʋwa yɛ mmɩlɩ mɔ adwɩnɩndwɩnɩ hyɛ yɛ zʋ nɩ na ama yɛahɔ zʋ yɛazɔ̃mɩ yɩ. (Ɛɣɛ. 12:25; Mt. 6:27-29) Gyehova va yɛ mmeliyema ma yɛ kɛɛ bɛhyɛ yɛ ɛzɩzɩbɛ mmɩlɩ mɔ yeyiyiaa ndwokɔzɩzɩbɛ nɩ. Akɔ̃ sɔ̃, ɩva asafo nu mbanimbani ɩma yɛ kɛɛ behilehilé yɛ na bɔwʋwa yɛ ma yɛ dedi yɛ sɩ. (Ayɩ. 32:1, 2) Sɛ yɛkɔ dɩyɛzumalɛ a, yɛnwu ahwanɩ mvʋlɔ mɔɔ yɛkɔhʋla kɛma Gyehova kɔwʋwa yɛ na ɩawɔ yɛ nwʋ banɩ.—Hib. 10:24, 25.
<<<P>>>
16 Nɩ sɛ Devidi yɛ sutiye ma Gyehova a, Gyehova bɔ yɩ nwʊ banɩ. Mɩnla ɔhwanɩ zʋ a? Nɩ ɩbʋwa Devidi ma isizi abɔkɔba, na eyi manlɩ Devidi anyɩ leli. (Fa tindĩã Ɛɣɛlɛwulɛ 5:1, 2 nwʋ.) Naasɔ̃ mmɩlɩ bela mɔɔ Devidi kowuli Gyehova yɩ mmɛla zʋ nɩ, nɩ Gyehova ɩáwɔ yɩ nwʊ banɩ wɔ mɔɔ ɩzʋ̃ numɔ ɩkɛya nɩ nwʊ. (2 Sa. 12:9, 10) Nɩ itwu mmɩlɩ be a, mmenii mvʋlɔ tudu Devidi nkɔ̃ʋ̃ ma idi ɛyɛkɛ. Sɛ ɩbayɩ zɔ a, ɩkã mɔɔ iduwaa numɔ nɩ nwʊ odwokɔ ikile Gyehova, na Gyehova sɔ̃ bʋwa yɩ. Kɛ ɩyɛ yɩ ise a? Nɩ ɩma Devidi tɩ nyanlɩ kɛɛ ɩdɔ yɩ na ɩwɔ kɛnɩ ɩma yɩ. Nɩ eyi kyɩkyɩ Devidi yɩ ɛyɛlɛ paa.—Ndw. 23:1-6.
