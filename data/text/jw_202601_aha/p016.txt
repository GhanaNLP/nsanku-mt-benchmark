Ɔzɔ afɔlɩ azulɔtʋ nɩ lalɩ yɩ ali kɛɛ, mmenii hyinyia afɔlɩ be mɔɔ idi munli. Mɛyɩ yɛ lɛ Gyisɛsɩ yɩ afɔlɩ nɩ. Ɔzɔ afɔlɩ yɩ yɛ ɩkɛma mmenii munwala kenyia nyilalɛ eyile be a. Gyisɛsɩ yi owulo nɩ yɛ ɩkɛma Gyehova kenyia debe kegyina zʋlɔ kɛva yɛ tanɩ kɛhyɛ yɛ a. Nɩ Pɔɔlʋ zi mɔɔ Gyisɛsɩ yi owulo nɩ kɛyɛ kɛma adasamaa nɩ paa. Mmʋlɩ mɔ ɩhɛlɛlɩ kɛlɛta ɩhɔ ɩmanlɩ Kɔlɛntɩmaa nɩ, ɩhanlɩ tanɩ azulɔtʋ mɔɔ nɩ bɛyɛ ma idua numɔ nɩ nwʋ odwokɔ. Akʋ̃ ɩva ɩdʋwalɩ zʋlɔ kɛɛ: “Naasũ Boyuwa bɛ bʋwɔ; bɛdɩ bɔ nwʋ; bɔwu bɛ kɛɛ ɛdɩnyɛlɛmaa wɔ Niibenyia Gyisɛsɩ Kɩlayɩsɩ yi dima nu yɛ Nyamɩnlɩ yi sunzuma nu.”—1 Kɔl. 6:9-11, NWT.

8 Ansaana ɔkɔhɔ ɔlʋbɔ eyi Ɛhakɛlɛlilɛ yɩ nɩ, de mmʋlɩ dwɩnɩndwɩnɩ kezimɔ enyia Gyehova yɩ tanɩfakyɛ zʋ mfasʋ nɩ nwʋ. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, iluwa elelɛdɩyɛ nɩ ti, sɛ yɛyɛ tanɩ be na yenu yɛ nwʋ a, íni kɛɛ yɛma ye tiwʋwaba buwa yɛ fɔ sũmaa. Naasũ sɛ ɩyɛ sɩ kɛɛ ekele kediã numɔ kɛɛ Gyehova va tanɩ mɔɔ ɛyɛ dua numɔ nɩ hyɛ wʋ sũ ɛ? Ebiye a ɛhã hile wʋ nwʋ po kɛɛ, ‘Mizi kɛɛ Gyehova kɔhʋla kɛva mɩ tanɩ kɛhyɛ mɩ dɩyɛ, naasũ mʋlɔkʋla kɛva kɛhyɛ mʋ nwʋ kyɩ.’ Sɛ ɔzɔ yɛ ɛtɩ nyanlɩ a, mɛyɩ nɩ kakɛ kɛɛ, Gyehova yɛ ɩfa tanɩ ɩkyɛ a, na ɩma yɩ Ɣa yɩ tumi kɛɛ iwuwá ndɛnɩ. Gyehova léfalɩ lówulalɩ mɔwʋ anzɛnyɩ abiyela yɩ sa kɛɛ izí pɔkɔ ɩma yɩ wɔ enii mɔɔ ɩkɔnwʋ yɩ nwɔkɛ nɩ nwʋ. Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ kã kɛɛ: ‘Na sɛ yɛbʋwa hanyu nu kɛmɔ Nyamɩnlɩ sʋ̃ ɩda hanyu nu nɩ a, yɩ Ɣa Gyisɛsɩ Kɩlayɩsɩ yɩ mmɛgyia nɩ ɩtɩ yɛ nwʋ wɔ tanɩ munwala nwʋ.’ (1 Dwɔ. 1:6, 7) Kɛmɔ yede yedi kɛɛ Bayɩbʋlʋ nu ndwokɔ mvʋlɔ dɩ nɔhʋanlɛ nɩ, ɔzɔ la yɛ yɛkɔhʋla yekele eyi sʋ̃ yekeli a. Elelɛdɩyɛ yɩ ɩma Gyehova inyia debe igyina zʋlɔ ɩnwʋ yɛ nwɔkɛ, na yi Odwokɔ yɩ kã kɛɛ, ‘ɩwɔ ɛpɛ kɛɛ ɩkɛva tanɩ ɩkɛhyɛ.’—Ndw. 86:5.

9. Wɔ Bayɩbʋlʋ nu nɩ sɛ bɛkã “tanɩ” a, kɛ ɩtʋ kile a? (Ndwomi 51:5 nɩɩ azɩkɛnlɩ odwokɔ)
<<<P>>>
Nɩ ɩsɛ kɛɛ Yizilayamaa yɩ fa nnamɩ bɔ afɔlɩ na bɛava bɛabɩda bɛama bɛ tanɩ. Ɔzɔ afɔlɩ azulɔtʋ nɩ lalɩ yɩ ali kɛɛ, mmenii hyinyia Gyisɛsɩ yɩ afɔlɩ mɔɔ idi munli nɩ, na ama bɛava bɛ tanɩ bɛahyɛ bɛ na beanyia nyilalɛ mɔɔ ɩzʋ̃ numɔ ɩba nɩ be (Kila nkyɩkyɛmɩ 6-7)
