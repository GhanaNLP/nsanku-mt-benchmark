Nɩ ihyinyia kɛɛ Finihyianii baalɛ yɩ kã yɩ nwu azɩ, isi yɩ kɛɣa na ɩda dedi ali na ama ianyia mmuwalɩyɛ mɔɔ ɩpɛɛ yɩ nɩ (Kila nkyɩkyɛmi 5)

Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ ɔhwanɩhilelɛ nɩ wɔ ɛpɛ kɛɛ bekeyizia yɩ kɛnɩ mmuwalɩyɛ na bɛadiye yi sũ a.

3 Wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ eyi nu nɩ, sa yedi bɔmʋnlɩ yekila mɔɔ yɛkɔhʋla kozuma wɔ Finihyianii baalɛ be mɔɔ izianlɩ Gyisɛsɩ kɛɛ ɩwʋwá yɩ nɩ kɛnɩ. Yi odwokɔ yɩ ma yɛnwʋ subanɩ asã be mɔɔ ihyinyia kɛɛ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɔnwʋ kɛɛ behyinyia Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ ɔhwanɩhilelɛ nɩ da yɩ ali. Akũ sũ, sa yesunzu ndianwʋ mɔɔ sɔmavʋlɔ Pita, Pɔɔlʋ, nɩ Bulombunli Devidi mɔɔ bɛvalɩ bɔ nwʋ bedianlɩ Gyehova zʋ yelɩ nɩ nwʋ.

4 Eyile ɔkũ be, Finihyianii baalɛ be ɣalɩ Gyisɛsɩ kɛnɩ. Nɩ sunzuma evinli be ididee yɩ ɣa baalɛ ɛzɩzɩbɛ ɛzɩzɩbɛ. (Mt. 15:21-28) Baalɛ yɩ hɔ wuli ntwɛlɩ wɔ Gyisɛsɩ yɩ anyunu zianlɩ yɩ kɛɛ ɩwʋwá yɩ ɣa baalɛ yɩ. Mmɩlɩ mɔ baalɛ eyi hɔlɩ Gyisɛsɩ yɩ pɛ nɩ, ɩlalɩ subanɩ be mɔɔ nwʋmɔ hyinyia ali. Bɛmá yezunzu numɔ be nwʋ.

4. Mɩnla yɛ nɩ Finihyianii baalɛ be pɛ kɛɛ Gyisɛsɩ yɛ ma yɩ a?

5 Finihyianii baalɛ yɩ lalɩ kãnwʋazɩ ankasa ali. Mɩnla ti mɔɔ yɛkʋla yɛkã yɩ zɔ a? Iluwakɛɛ mmɩlɩ mɔ Gyisɛsɩ valɩ yɩ dindianlɩ ɔhyʋwa ɣalɛ nwʋ nɩ, ɩáfa ɛya. Ɩkɛyɛ kɛɛ nɩ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bédɩ Dwuumaa nɩ ta sũ nhyʋwa mmalɛ wɔ bɛ awudo. Sɛ mɔwʋ yɛ Gyisɛsɩ hanlɩ zɔ hileli wʋ a, ankɩ ɛkɛyɛ mɩnla a? Asũ ankɩ ɛkɛdɩ nɣanlɩ kɛɛ ɩwʋdʋ wʋ na enti ekeyiaki mmuwalɩyɛ mɔɔ ɛpɩbɛɛ yɩ wɔ yɩ kɛnɩ nɩ a? Kɛ ɔzɔ yɛ baalɛ nɩ yelɩ yɩ dɩyɛ a. Kɛ kãnwʋazɩ nkũ la yɛ ɩlalɩ yɩ ali a, mfomi nɩ izi yɩ kenɣa sũ. Yɛnwʋ yɩ zɔ iluwakɛɛ ɩhɔlɩ zʋlɔ izianlɩ Gyisɛsɩ kɛɛ ɩwʋwá yɩ. Mɩnla yɛ ɩhanlɩ yɩ ma ɩyɛlɩ zɔ a? Nɩ ɩwɔ dedi wɔ Gyisɛsɩ nu. Nɔhʋanlɛ nu nɩ, dedi mɔɔ baalɛ eyi lalɩ yɩ ali nɩ hanlɩ Gyisɛsɩ yɩ ahʋnlɩnɩ ma ɩyɛlɩ debe sʋnʋnkũ be ɩwʋwalɩ yɩ. Ɩávalɩ nwʋmɔ kɛɛ ahyɛasɩ yɩ Gyisɛsɩ hanlɩ hileli yɩ kɛɛ bɔzũma yɩ kɛɛ ɩɣa ɩwʋwá “Yizilayamaa nɩ mmɔ mɔɔ bɛdɩdɩ kɛɛ mmuwuanɩ mɔɔ beminli nɩ nkũ” la nɩ, Gyisɛsɩ duli sunzuma evinli nɩ wɔ Finihyianii baalɛ yɩ ɣa kuakula baalɛ nɩ yi nu.
