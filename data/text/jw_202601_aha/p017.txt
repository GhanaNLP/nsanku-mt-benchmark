51:5 nu azikɛnɩ odwokɔ yɩ.) Tanɩ mɔɔ bɛva bɔwulɩ yɛ nɩ yɛ ɩma yenyia adwɩnɩ tanɩ na yɛyɛ nyɛlɩyɛ tanɩ a. Mɛyɩ la ahanlɩ a, ɩáma yɛ nnwʊnlamɩ owudo yɩ yɛ egyima kɛmɔ Gyehova wɔlɩ nɩ. Meti mɔɔ yɛ nwʊ pa yɛ, yɛyɛ mbanimbani na yewu yɩ a. Eyi ma yɛnwʊ sianti mɔɔ nnwuakula mɔɔ bɔwʊ bɛ mɔɔ bɛléyɛlɩ tanɩ bela po nɩ, bɔ nwʊ kʊla pa bɛ na bowu nɩ a. Iluwa Adamɩ yɩ tanɩ yɩ ti, ye munwala yɛyɛ tanɩmaa. Meti mɔɔ mmenii mɔɔ bɛyɛ tanɩ nʊ mmenii mɔɔ bɛyɛ baaba munwala fɛ na bowu yɩ a.

10 Mɔwʊ dwɩnɩ kezimɔ Adamɩ nʊ Yifi dɩlɩ nyanlɩ mmɩlɩ mɔ bɛyɛlɩ tanɩ na bɔdɔlɩ sini nɩ nwʊ kila. Tanɩ mɔɔ bɛyɛlɩ nɩ manlɩ benyianlɩ nyanlɩdɩlɛ mɔɔ ɩtáye baaba. Mmɩlɩ mɔ Adamɩ nʊ Yifi yɛlɩ tanɩ wieli la pɛ, yɛ bɛ tiwʊwaba wɔlɩ yɩ tʊ kɛɛ ididee bɛ a, iluwakɛɛ nɩ bowuli Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ mmɛla zʊ. (Wul. 2:15) Ayilemvile nu la, bɛdʊlɩ nyanlɩ kɛɛ debe ɩáhɔ bʊwɔ wɔ bo nu. Meti bɛbɛlɩ yɩ kɛɛ bɛkɛgɩda bɔ nwʊ na bɛava bɔ nwʊ bɛaziye bɔ Wɔlɛvʊlɔ yɩ. (Gyn. 3:7, 8) Ɔzɔ mmɩlɩ yɩ yɛ lɛ mmɩlɩ mɔ ili bɔmʊnlɩ mɔɔ Adamɩ nʊ Yifi bɛ tiwʊwaba lileli bɛ, adwɩnɩndwɩnɩ hyɛlɩ bɔ zʊ, belili ɛyɛkɛ na bɛ anyɩ dʊnlɩ a. Mɔɔ nɩ ɩkɩnla nwʊmɔ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ ɔzɔ nyanlɩdɩlɛ eyi kelile bɛ kɔbʊmɩ kɛɛ bokowu.—Gyn. 3:16-19.

11 Kezimɔ tanɩ manlɩ Adamɩ nu Yifi dɩlɩ nyanlɩ nɩ, ɔzɔ la yɛ amɩyɛ sʊ̃ yɛtɩ nyanlɩ a. Iluwakɛɛ yɛáli munli nɩ ti, yɛtɩdʊ na yedi ɛyɛkɛ. Iwɔ numɔ kɛɛ yɛbɔɔ mmɔdɩnɩ bela kɛɛ yekeli yɛ ɔhawʊ azʊlɔtʊ nwʊ egyima dɩyɛ, naasʊ̃ ɔhawʊ be wɔ kɛnɩ a, yɛlɔ́kʊla kɛyɛ nwʊmɔ hwɩɩ. Mɩnla ti a? Iluwakɛɛ Bayɩbʊlʊ yɩ ma yɛnwʊ yɩ kɛɛ, “nninyeni munwatĩĩ mɔɔ Nyamɩnlɩ ɩwɔlɩ nɩ bɛya bɛyɛ mbyanlɩ.” (Wul. 8:20) Eyi hã adasamaa munwala. Kɛ ndianwʊ nɩ, mmenii sʊ̃maa bɔbɔɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ bekeziyeziye azɩlɛ yɩ, bɛkɛma basabasayɛlɛ, ehyinyia nɩ kʊnɛ tu kodua. Naasʊ̃ ɩtávalɩ nwʊmɔ mmɔdɩnɩ bela mɔɔ bɔbɔɔ nɩ, bɔlɔ́kʊla bɛléyɛlɩ ɔzɔ nninyeni yɩ nu bela wɔ kɛnɩ. Naasʊ̃ iluwa elelɛdɩyɛ yɩ ti, yɛwɔ anyɩnlɩlazʊ baaba be. Idɩ mɩnla anyɩnlɩlazʊ a?

12 Elelɛdɩyɛ yɩ ma yenyia anyɩnlɩlazʊ kɛɛ “nninyeni munwatĩĩ mɔɔ Nyamɩnlɩ ɩwɔlɩ nɩ bɛbanyia fanwʊdi.” (Wul. 8:21) Eyi kile kɛɛ, sɛ yedi munli wɔ Paladayɩsɩ a, yɛ nwʊ lépayɛ, yɛlétɩdʊ, ye tiwʊwaba lédide yɛ, adwɩnɩndwɩnɩ léhyɛ yɛ zʊ na yelédi ɛyɛkɛ dɔkɔ̃. Akʊ̃ sʊ̃, mmɔdɩnɩ mɔɔ yɛkɔwɔ kɛva keziyeziye azɩlɛ yɩ na yɛama azʊ̃nudwolɩyɛ ɩaya zʊlɔ nɩ léyɛ mbyanlɩ. Mɩnla ti a? Iluwakɛɛ “Azʊ̃nudwolɩyɛ Bulombunli,” Gyisɛsɩ Kɩlayɩsɩ mɔɔ ileli yɩ nɩ kɔwʊwa yɛ ma yɛkɛyɛ zɔ.—Ayɩ. 9:6, 7.

13 Sɛ bɛyɩ tanɩ mɔɔ ɩwɔ yɛ nwʊ nɩ wɔ kɛ na yɛba yedi munli a, kila kezimɔ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ kɛyɛ. Yekodwodwu nwɔnɩ bela nɩ, nɩ yɛwɔ anwʊzɩzɩbɛ na ye kunlu lótʊ kɛɛ kɔmɩ kohũ yɛ, yɛ nwʊ kɛba yɛ anzɛnyɩ yekowu po. Nnɛ po, sɛ yɛsɔ anyɩnlɩlazʊ eyi mɔɔ ɩdɩ kɛɛ sɩkyɛ ɩma yɛ ɛkɛla nɩ nu bʊwɔ a, yɛkenyia azʊ̃nudwolɩyɛ. (Hib. 6:18, 19) Kɛmɔ sɩkyɛ sɔ ɛlɛnɩ nu ma ikyina pindinyii nɩ, ɔzɔ la yɛ anyɩnlɩlazʊ mɔɔ yɛwɔ nɩ ma yɛsɔ ye dedi nu pindinyii a, na ɩma yɛkʊla yɛkyina ndwokɔzɩzɩbɛ bela munwa. Sa yɛkʊla yenyia anwʊlazʊ kɛɛ Gyehova koduwa “bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛ anyɩ bʊlʊ bɛpɛ yɩ nɩ kakɛ.” (Hib. 11:6) Elelɛdɩyɛ yɩ la yɛ ɩma yenyia ɛyɛɛlɛyɩkɩlɛ nnɛ nɩ eyile be nwʊ anyɩnlɩlazʊ a.
