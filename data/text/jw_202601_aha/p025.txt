16 San Bra Yehowa Nkyɛn brohyʊa yɩ lɛ ɛgɛɛlɛyɩkylɛ ndwɔkɔ be. Ɩkã kɛɛ: “Nyia anwʊlazʊ kɛɛ Gyihova kɔbʊwa wʊ ma ekezia kɛyɛ yɩ ɔhɔnwʊ dɔkũ. Ɩkɔbʊwa wʊ ma ekeli wʊ adwɩnɩndwɩnɩ nɩ ɛzɛkɛ bela mɔɔ abiye yɩ nyɛlɩyɛ ti edii nɩ zʊ. Na ɩkɛva wʊ tanɩ kɛhyɛ wʊ ma wʊ ahʊnlɩnɩ ɩadɔ wu kunlu na wʊanyia tiwʊvaba baaba. Eyi kɔbʊwa wʊ ma ekezia kegyina wʊ agyia zʊ dɔkũ na ɛ nɩ wʊ mmeliyema bɛawɔ numɔ bɛazũmɩ Gyihova.” Kakɛ sũ kɛɛ, asafo nu mbanimbani yɩ wɔ ɛkɛ kɛɛ bɔkɔbʊwa wʊ. Bɛdɩ kɛɛ “ɛgɩntanlɛdɩka wɔ anwʊma mmɩlɩ nu, nɩ eziyelɛdɩka wɔ ahũmidulɛ mmɩlɩ nu.” (Ayɩ. 32:2) Yezi kɛɛ wu anyɩ sɔ elelɛdɩyɛ nɩ na ɛpɛ kɛɛ ɛyɛ dɩyɛ bela fa kile kɛɛ wu anyɩ sɔ yɩ. Meti bizia wʊ nwu kɛɛ, ‘Mɩnla yɛ mʊkɔhʊla kɛyɛ na mɩava miaziyeziye mɩ nɩ Gyihova avinli a?’ (Ayɩ. 1:18; 1 Pit. 2:25) Kɛ ndianwʊ nɩ, sa ɔkʊla kɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ wɔ Kɩndɔm Hɔɔlʊ a? Asũ sa ɔkʊla kɔ pɩbɛ mmʊwalyɛ wɔ asafo nu mbanimbani yɩ bo nu ɔkũ kɛnɩ kɛɛ ɛpɛ kɛɛ esiã ba sũmɩ Gyihova dɔkũ a? Sa ɩyɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ ma abiye nɩɩ wʊ ba suma Bayɩbʊlʊ yɩ wɔ mmɩlɩ dʊdʊ be nu. Nyia anwʊlazʊ kɛɛ Gyihova keyila mmɔdɩnɩ bela mɔɔ ɔbɔɔ kɛɛ ekezia kɛya yɩ pɛ na wvahile anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ wvama elelɛd
