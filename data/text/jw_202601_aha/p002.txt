Yekehila mɔɔ ihyinyia kɛɛ yɛyɛ na ama yɛahɔ zʋ yɛanyia Gyehova yɩ ɔhʋanɩhilelɛ zʋ mfasʋ na yɛ anyɩ iale.
<<<P>>>
Kɔ Zʋlɔ Fa Wʋ Nwʋ Tiã “Nyamɩnlɩ Yɩ Ɔhʋanɩhilelɛ” Zʋ
<<<P>>>
YƐ ƆLƲBƆ ODWOKƆ MA 2026:
<<<P>>>
“Anyɩnlɩlelɛ yɛ lɛ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɔnwʋ yɩ kɛɛ behyinyia Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ ɔhʋanɩhilelɛ.”—MT. 5:3, NWT.
<<<P>>>
GYEHOVA zi mɔɔ adasamaa hyinyia. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, yehyinyia alɩyɛ, ntalɩ, nɩɩ dabɩlɛ na ama yɛahɔ zʋ yɛawʋwa nnwʋanlɩ nu. Sɛ yɛ sa ɩákã ɔzɔ nninyeni yi wɔ mmɩlɩ sikalɛ be po a, ɛbɩlawɔlɛ kɔhʋla kɛyɛ sɩ kɛma yɛ paa. Naasʋ̃ nnwʋnlamɩ ɔfʋã nninyeni eyimmɔ la ahanlɩ a, Gyehova wɔlɩ yɛ wɔ ɔhʋanɩ be zʋ mɔɔ ɩma yɛnyia ɛpɛ kɛɛ yɛkɔnwʋ yɩ na yɛava yɛ nwʋ yeadĩã yɩ ɔhʋanɩhilelɛ zʋ. (Kɩnga Matiyu 5:3, NWT.) Sɛ yɛ anyɩ kele paa a, ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛnwʋ kɛɛ yehyinyia yɩ ɔhʋanɩhilelɛ, na yɛkɔ zʋ yɛfa yɛ nwʋ yetiã yɩ zʋ.
<<<P>>>
2 Giliki odwokɔ mɔɔ bekile yɩ tʋ “bɔnwʋ yɩ kɛɛ behyinyia Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ ɔhʋanɩhilelɛ” nɩ, ɩyɛ sʋ̃ a bekile yɩ tʋ kɛɛ “bɛdɩ sunzuma nu ahyinyiamaa.” Mɔwʋ tua ehyinyianii be mɔɔ isinziãã dɩyɛ mɔɔ yɩ nwʋ yɛ evinli na mɔɔ ikeli po ɩyɛ sɩ ɩma yɩ nwʋ mfonyini kila. Sɛ oyuwa yɩ ifii ketee po a ɩda numɔ, na awulo two na ɛyɛbɛ ba sʋ̃ a ɩda numɔ. Mmenii nwʋ yɩ a bɛádɛnɩ yɩ nwʋ iluwa kezimɔ yɩ nwʋ dɩ nɩ ti. Ɔkʋla nwʋ kɛɛ ɩvɛ na yɩ anyɩ iále. Izi kɛɛ sɛ ɩkɔhʋla kenyia anwʋzɩzɩbɛ na ɩawʋwa nnwʋanlɩ nu a, ikehyinyia mmenii mvʋlɔ bɔ mmʋwalɩyɛ, meti ɩkɔ zʋ isinziã dɩyɛ. Ɔzɔ la yɛ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bezi kɛɛ behyinyia
