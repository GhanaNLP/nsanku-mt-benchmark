(Kila nkyɩkyɛmi 1-2)
<<<P>>>
mɔ iyizialɩ kɛɛ, ‘Aninyia yɛ ikele mɩ a?’ nɩ, yɩzayɩ ɩyʋlɩ yi munwa kɛɛ: “Mɩla Nyamɩnlɩ asɩ kɛɛ iluwa Gyisɛsɩ Kɩlayɩsɩ yɩ zʋ ɩyɛ eyi!” (Wul. 7:25) Nɩ Pɔɔlʋ ɩkãã Gyisɛsɩ yi elelɛdɩyɛ afɔlɩ nɩ nwʋ odwokɔ.* Ɔzɔ elelɛdɩyɛ yɩ ɩma (1) yenyia tanɩfakyɛ, (2) yenyia anyɩnlɩlazʋ kɛɛ yekeli munli eyile be, na(3) yɛ nʋ Gyehova avinli yɛ baaba dɔkɔ̃. Mmɩlɩ mɔ yesunzuu eyimmɔ nwʋ nɩ, ɩkɛma ɔlɔlɛ mɔɔ yɛwɔ yɛma Gyehova, “Nyamɩnlɩ mɔɔ mɛyɩ yɛ ɩma enii inyia anyɩnlɩlazʋ” nɩ kɛyɛ kezi. (Wul. 15:13) Akʋ̃ sʋ̃, ɩkɛma yekenyia anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ kezile yɛkɛma Gyisɛsɩ mɔɔ Gyehova luwa yɩ zʋ ile yɛ nɩ.—Kol. 1:14.

ELELƐDƖYƐ YƖ MA YENYIA TANƖFAKYƐ
<<<P>>>
4 Yehyinyia elelɛdɩyɛ yɩ na ama Gyehova ɩava yɛ tanɩ ɩahyɛ yɛ. Ye munwala yedi mfʋmɩ wɔ yɛ ndwokɔhanlɛ nʋ yɛ nyɛlɩyɛ nu. (Kɩnga Odwokɔhanlɛvʋlɔ 7:20.) Tanɩ be wɔ kɛnɩ a, numɔ yɛ ɔnlʋmɔ paa. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, Mosisi mmɛla nɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ, sɛ Yizilayanii be kũ abiye anzɛnyʋ ɩsɩkɛ agyiahʋnlɩ a, ɩsɛ kɛɛ bokũ yi. (Lɛv. 20:10; Nɔ. 35:30, 31) lwɔ numɔ kɛɛ tanɩ sʋ̃maa wɔ kɛnɩ a, numɔ ɩáyɛ ɔnlʋmɔ dɩyɛ, naasʋ̃ tanɩ bela dɩ tanɩ. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, Devidi hanlɩ kɛɛ: “Mikehila mɩ agyiadulɛ bʋwɔ na mɩávalɩ mɩ tavlɩma mɩáyɛlɩ tanɩ.” (Ndw. 39:1) Eyi kile kɛɛ, mɔɔ yɛkã nɩ po kʋla yɛ tanɩ.—Gye. 3:2.
<<<P>>>
5 Mɔwʋ dwɩnɩ nwʋmɔ kila. Asʋ̃ ɛhã odwokɔ be hile abiye kyĩ mɔɔ wu kunlu a ankɩ wʋãkã a? Asʋ̃ ɛyɛ debe ma onlu wu nwʋ kyĩ a? Pɩlazunɛ bela íni nwʋmɔ kɛɛ, debe mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ dʋ wu kyĩ. Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ kã kɛɛ: “Sɛ yɛkã kɛɛ yɛáyɛ tanɩ a, yɛdahɩlɩ yɛ nwʋ, na nɔhʋanlɛ nɩ íni ye nu.”—1 Dwɔ. 1:8.
<<<P>>>
6 Iluwakɛɛ Gyehova dɩ pelɩpelɩ nɩ ti, ɩsɛ kɛɛ inyia debe mɔɔ ikegyina zʋlɔ kɛva tanɩ kɛhyɛ. Elelɛdɩyɛ yɩ ma inyia debe kyina zʋ fa yɛ tanɩ kyɛ yɛ. (Ɛfɩ. 1:7) Naasʋ̃, eyi íáhile kɛɛ Gyehova bu yɩ anyɩ kua yɛ tanɩ zʋ kɛkɛ, anzɛnyʋ ibu yi kɛɛ ɩáyɛ hwɩɩ. Gyehova íáwu yɩ anyɩ kua tanɩ zʋ kʋlaa.—Ayɩ. 59:2.
<<<P>>>
7 Nɩ Mosisi mmɛla yɩ pɩbɛ kɛɛ, Yizilayamaa nɩ kɛva nnamɩ kɔwɔ afɔlɩ na bɛava bɛabɩda bɛama bɛ tanɩ. (Lɛv. 4:27-31; 17:11)
