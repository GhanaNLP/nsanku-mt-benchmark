Yuuni bahigu Nɛma daa shaara niŋbu (End of year inflation): Dimbɔŋɔ nyɛla nɛma daa ni duri shɛm yuuni kam Burinya goli ni.
<<<P>>>
Laɣidibo din yaɣi: Dimbɔŋɔ niŋdimi Gomnanti yi ti di n-yaɣi jintɔriba ni saɣi ti bajɛti laɣ’ shɛli zuɣu.
<<<P>>>
FREE SHS: Free SHS nyɛla Ghana Gomnanti zaligu shɛli Tiŋbanzuɣulana Nana Akuffo-Addo ni piligi o ʒɛmani puuni September 2017 ni, ni di che ka karimbia kam ŋun baŋsim simdi ni o chaŋ SHS chaŋ li yoli ka bi yo kɔbo.
<<<P>>>
Sɔŋsim: Dimbɔŋɔ nyɛla tinduya nima ban sɔŋdi lɛbiginsim tuma ni tiri Gomnanti laɣ’ shɛli ka di pala o ti yɛn yola di zuɣu.
<<<P>>>
Gomnanti Tuma Yaɣa sɔŋsim: Dimbɔŋɔ nyɛla zaligu ni saɣi ti ni Gomnanti yo laɣ’ shɛŋa n-niŋ tuma yaɣa kamani District Assembly Common Fund (DACF), Road Fund, Ghana Education Trust Fund (GETFUND), Petroleum Related Fund nti pahi National Health Fund.
<<<P>>>
Gross Domestic Product (GDP): Dimbɔŋɔ nyɛla nɛma mini tuun’ shɛli din be tiŋgbani ni saha shɛli, ni ŋa dariza ni nyɛ shɛli.
<<<P>>>
Gross International Reserves: Dimbɔŋɔ zahindila tiŋgbani laɣizuyu dariza bee yiko zaŋ chaŋ di ni yɛn tooi da nɛma bee tuma shɛm tinduya ni saha yini puuni. Dina n-lahi wuhiri tiŋgbani liɣiri dariza zaŋ mayisi tinduya liɣiri dariza.
<<<P>>>
Inflation (Nɛma mini tuma shaaa niŋbu): Nɛma mini tuma daa ni duri bee m-pahiri shɛm saha yini sunsuuni.
<<<P>>>
Fara Tuhi Ku Lɛbiginsim (Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme IPEP): Fara Tuhi Ku Lɛbiginsim nyɛla tuun’ suŋ shɛli NPP Gomnanti ni tahina ni di tuhi ku fara ka filim woliginsim din be fɔndi mini tiŋkpansi susuuni. Di nia nyɛla di ti tiŋkpansi bɛ yɛlibɔra ka di sɔŋ n-yihi ba fara puuni.
