Farigu Kpaŋmaŋa
<<<P>>>
4. Farigu laasabu mini liyiri pina January la ni hali nti to September 2021 daa yiyisila GH¢46.85 billion (GDP 10.7% m-bala), n-zaŋ mayisi tuun’ shɛŋa ŋan daa labi lihi (revised programme) ka bɛ lihiri ni di paai GH¢51.31 billion (GDP 11.7% ). Farigu din daa deei laasabu zaa daa nyɛ din pa kpam farigu (74%), din pa kpam farigu ka lahi pa farigu mi yiyisi (9%), kpam mini gaasi gbambihi daa yiyisila (9%), farigu din pahi mi yiyisi (6%) ka pina mi yiyisi (2%) kamani ti nya li shɛm Teebuli 1 ni maa.
<<<P>>>
5. Farigu mini pina boobu 2021 yuuni n-zaŋ mayisi di ni daa buyisi zali shɛm, daa yɛn siyila tiŋa gari di ni daa tɛhi di kpaŋmaŋa ni yɛn nyɛ shɛm feera duri nyɔri farigu (Corporate Income Tax), tuma yɔri farigu (Personal Income Tax), tiŋgbani niima farigu (Mineral Royalties), nɛma ŋan maali tingbani ŋɔ ni zuyu farigu (Domestic Excise Duty), ni pɔhim zuyu tuma farigu (Communications Service Tax).
