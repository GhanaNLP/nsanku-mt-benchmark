BOMMA NI NYAMMA TIYISIBU COVID-19 NYAAŊA

GhanaCARES “Pay’ suŋ” Program
<<<P>>>
17. Din yɛn kpaŋsi Ghana maŋmaŋa gama zuyu zaa, zaŋ hali ni Covid-19 nyaaŋa, Gomnanti pilila GH¢100 billion Ghana COVID-19 Alleviation mini daabiligu tuma yiya kpaŋsibu sɔŋsim sodoligu la, (the GhanaCARES “Masuŋ” programme). Dimbɔŋɔ nyɛla din yɛn kpaŋsi ka tiyisi ti tiŋgbani ŋɔ daabiligu kpaŋsibu mini sabiila yayili sɔŋsim.
<<<P>>>
18. Din dolina ŋɔ nyɛla program maa ni di nasar’ shɛŋa 2021 yuuni pulini ni 2022 yuuni pulini nianima.
<<<P>>>
Pukparilim so’ pala kpuyibu mini bindira ʒi n-kpe tiŋgbani na tayibu
<<<P>>>
19. Niriba ban yiyisi 579,662 ka bɛ puuni pukpariba yiyisi 547,126 nti pahi ban pukparilim mali yaa gba yiyisi 32,536 nyɛla ban sabi bɛ yuya m-pahi October 2021 pukpariba ni, din yɛn kpaŋsi adalichi ni Gomnanti sɔŋsim zaŋ chaŋ pukparilim yayili.
<<<P>>>
20. Gomnanti daa layimla ban be nɔhi gulibu daabiligu mini shiŋkaafa kɔbu ni salintuya nti pahi ban kɔri kamantoonsi ni o baŋ lala pukparilim yaya ŋɔ maa muysigu ni nyɛ shɛli, din daa dɔyi sɔŋsim sodol’ shɛli bɛ ni boli Interest Rate Subsidy (IRS) n-ti pukparilim daabiligunima din yɛn sɔŋsi bɛ muysigu ni liyiri polo. Lala IRS ŋɔ yɛn sɔŋmi m-booi layipaŋda zuyu farigu 50%, din yɛn sɔŋ ba ka bɛ tooi bo mazina ka yɛligi bɛ tuma layidoo.
<<<P>>>
21. Pukparilim tuma so’ pala kpuyibu puuni, sodola ŋan doli ŋɔ na ka Gomnanti yɛn kpaŋsi 2022 yuuni puli ni:
<<<P>>>
• di kpa Ghana Agricultural Land Information Bank (GhLIB) ka di tooi sɔŋ polo din ni ko bɔbu, balante pukparigu din jɛndi daabiligu;
<<<P>>>
• di yirigi ka ti bipola maa daliri pukparilim yayili la ni m-pahi social media zaŋ tum tuma zuyu; ka
<<<P>>>
• mali pukparilim daabilm ni din jɛndi binnɛma ni arizichi din yɛn ku n-niŋ binnɛma maa bahibu ni ka zaŋ di lahibali kɔhi ti bipola maa.
