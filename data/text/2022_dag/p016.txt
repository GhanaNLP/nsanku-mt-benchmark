22. Saamba gɔrim shee mini nuu ni tuumbaŋsim nti kaya ni taada yayili tiyisibu bomma ni nyamma din be GhanaCARES ni la, Gomnanti daa naala niriba ban kalinli yiyisi niriba kɔbisita (300) ka be saamba ni yɛn gɔri chani tiŋbani ŋɔ luɣ’ shɛŋa la ka bɛ boli li National Tourism Destination Single Window ni la, din yɛn che ka saamba gɔrim tuma niŋ yaa ni vielim.
<<<P>>>
23. 2022 yuuni ŋɔ, Gomnanti yɛn kpala sɔŋsim yaya, kamani nɛma bombu shitɔri (Pattern Development Shop), niriba bee binyɛra ŋan tumdi tuma viɛnyɛlinga biɛhigu shee (Powerhouse), sayiri bahibu shee (Scrapyard), ka lahi maali pala nti pahi niyilim buyim, kom, ni tangalimia zuyu yɛlibɔra bo n-niŋ saamba gɔrim tuma yaya ni.

Nɛma maalibu mini tinduya ni daabilim niŋbu yɛligibu
<<<P>>>
24. Gomnanti 10-Point Industrialization Agenda ni, di zaŋla niyilimbuyim maalibu n-kpɛhi jina ni, din yɛn che ka nɛma din yiri sambani ni n-kpɛrina baligi, ka yeligi kulim maalibu din yɛn kpaŋsi nɛma sambani ni yibu ni tuma. 2021 yuuni la, Gomnanti daa pilila tuma Bioequivalence Centre din be Noguchi la, ni di sɔŋ tiima maalibu tumayilinima din be Ghana ŋɔ la. Đan dolina ŋɔ nyɛla Gomnanti ni yɛn niŋ shɛli 2022 yuuni ŋɔ:
<<<P>>>
• Daabiligu zalisi maali n-zali (ni Registrar General Department Acceleration tumayili dijitalayizibu);
<<<P>>>
• M-pili tuun’ shɛli din yɛn che ka tuma yiya ŋan be bɔba ni yaya kam kpaŋsi ka ŋa mini Technical Yunivɛsitinima zaŋ bɛ kpalanzuya n-gbuni taba; ka
<<<P>>>
• yɛligi Ghana dundɔŋ ni tiima maalibu, ka zaŋ ti kpalanzuya n-gbuni tiima maalibu tuma yiya ŋan yɛn kpaŋsi tiima maalibu yɛlibɔra maalibu (Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (API))

Yintanɛti ni daabiligu vooi gurim mini yintanɛti daabiligu toontibo yomyom
<<<P>>>
25. Gomnanti nyɛla ŋun mali kpaŋmaŋa zaŋ chaŋ Ghana yintanɛti ni daabiligu la polo. Dimbɔŋɔ zuyu, Gomnanti tila Ghana niriba ban yaɣi 15 miliyɔŋ foto zuyuŋmahi, ka lahi zaŋ yiya ŋan kalinli yiyisi 7.5 miliyɔŋ n-niŋ tiŋgbani biɛhigu shee soya (National Digital Address System) maa ni.
