33. Ghana Medium-Term National Development Policy Framework (2018-2021) saha ni garila zuɣu, Ghana yi n-kpela Medium-Term National Development Policy Framework (MTNDPF) (2022-2025). Sodol’ palli ŋɔ yina la Long-Term National Development Framework (Ghana@100) la ni, Coordinated Programme of Economic and Social Development Policies (2017-2024) ni, Ghana Beyond Aid Agenda and Strategy nti pahi Ghana COVID-19 Alleviation and Revitalisation of Enterprises Support Programme (GhanaCARES) la ni. Lala sodola ŋɔ gba kamina nti maɣisi UN Agenda 2030 (SDS) mini Africa Union Agenda 2063 la.

35. Gomnanti kpammaŋa ni tuma kalinsi baligibu chɛmi ka di namdi sodola mini programnima kamani Ghana Productive Safety Net Project (GPSNP). Tuma ŋɔ puuni, Gomnanti yɛn:
<<<P>>>
i. bola tuma n-ti faranima ban kalinli yiɣisi 33, 952 MMDAs 80 puuni, ka di yɔri yiɣisi shiiri miliyɔŋ GH¢ 53.43; ka
<<<P>>>
ii. zaŋla liɣiri din yiɣisi shiiri miliyɔŋ GH¢5.82 n-tari dundɔna ni niriba kamani tinsi ŋan kalinli yiɣisi 128 ŋan gbaai 41 MMDAs shɛŋa ŋan be Tudu yaɣa anu la ni, ka bɛ zaŋ pili bee n-kpaŋsi bɛ daabiligu.

36. Gomnanti ni kpuɣi nia shɛli ni o vo paɣisara ni nachimba haŋkaya n-kpɛhi daabiligu ni ka di baligi bipola zo n-chaŋ nyɔŋ ni. Ka lahi ti bipola biɛrisuŋ zuɣu la zuɣu, Gomnanti daa zaŋla pu’ kura yiɣisibu (rehabilitation) mini tihi labi sa (replanting) ni pompibu (mass spraying) ni kulim bahibu ni hand pollination ni kookoo tihi pinibu ni kookoo puri ŋan be kookoo kɔbu yaɣili kom yɔɣibu ka di ti niriba ban kalinli yiɣisi 130,000 tuma.
