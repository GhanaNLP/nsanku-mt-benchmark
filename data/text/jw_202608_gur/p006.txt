15 To san kɔ’ɔn zamesa Baabule la, to
wan sirum sakɛ ti Yehowa la Yezu nɔŋɛ to
mɛ paa. (Mag. 22:19) Baabule la tɔɣɛ Ye-
howa yamesi basɛba n yuun dekɛ bamea
delum Yehowa la Yezu yele mɛ. To san
ti’isa tayesa Yehowa la Yezu n yuun soŋɛ
ba se’em la, la wan basɛ ti tomam me
kɔ’ɔn dekɛ tomea delum ba. Yɛla zo’e
zo’e n boi Baabule la poan n pa’alɛ ti Ye-
howa la Yezu nɔŋɛ to mɛ. Makerɛ, Yeho-
wa yeti eŋa nɔŋɛ a yamesi mɛ gana pɔka
n nɔŋɛ a bia se’em la. (Isa. 49:15) A le
yeti, nɛresɛba n nɔŋɛ eŋa la dela tuulum
bɔ’ɔra en. (Ma. 3:16, 17) Baabule la basɛ
ti to baŋɛ ti Yezu me nɔŋɛ to mɛ paa.
Bala n sɔi ti a ki bo to.—Yon 15:12, 13.
