9 Esi wokplɔ Daniel kple sɔhɛ Hebrivi bubuwo yi aboyo me la, wodo go tetekpɔ siwo manya va wo dzi kpɔ esime wonɔ wo de o. Tso gɔmedzedzea ko la, Babilontɔwo ƒoe ɖe wo nu be woawɔ nu abe yewo ene. Azɔ hã, wona hehe wo be woasubɔ le fiasã me. Ke hã, hehexɔxɔ sia ana nɔnɔme aɖewo nado mo ɖa si anye xɔse ƒe dodokpɔ na wo.
<<<P>>>

11 Le Hebrivi ɖekakpui siwo katã wotia be woaxɔ hehea dome la, Daniel, Sadrak, Mesak kple Abednego ko ŋue Ŋɔŋlɔawo gblɔ le be wowɔ nuteƒe na Yehowa. Xɔlɔ̃ ene siawo kpe ɖe wo nɔewo ŋu woɖu tetekpɔwo dzi hewɔ nuteƒe na Yehowa. Wo ame enea katã gbe be yewomaɖu fia ƒe nuɖuɖuawo o, si wɔe be womeda le Yehowa ƒe se dzi o. Eye Yehowa yra wo ɖe esia ta.—Dan. 1:6-12, 17.
<<<P>>>
12 Ne sɔhɛ ye nènye la, ɖe hawòtiwo fonɛ ɖe nuwò ɣeaɖewoɣi be nàwɔ nu si mesɔ oa? Daniel kple exɔlɔ̃awo kpe ɖe wo nɔewo ŋu wowɔ nu si sɔ. Abe woawo ene la, dze xɔlɔ̃ ame siwo ɖoe kplikpaa be yewoawɔ nuteƒe na Yehowa eye wotsri xexea ƒe nuwɔnawo. (1 Yoh. 2:15-17) Xɔlɔ̃ vavãwo akpe ɖe ŋuwò nàwɔ nuteƒe le “nu suetɔ” gɔ̃ hã me, eye esia awɔe be Yehowa nayra wò ahado ŋusẽ wò. (Luka 16:10) Eye nuteƒewɔwɔ le nu suesuewo me akpe ɖe ŋuwò nàwɔ nuteƒe ne dodokpɔ siwo nu sẽ wu la va dziwò.
