Boa Afoforɔ Ma Wɔnhu Yehowa Yie
<<<P>>>
DWOM 79
Boa Ma Wɔnnyina Hɔ Pintinn
<<<P>>>
“Sɛ obi benya daa nkwa a, ɛsɛ sɛ ohu wo a woyɛ nokware Nyankopɔn koro pɛ no.”—YOH. 17:3.
<<<P>>>
NEA ADESUA YI BƐKA HO ASƐM
<<<P>>>
Sɛnea wobɛboa wo Bible suani ama no ahu Yehowa, ne suban, ne nea ɔpɛ ne nea ɔmpɛ.
<<<P>>>
SƐ WO Bible suani bi fi ase ba adesua, ofi ase ma mmuae, ɔka nea ɔresua no bi kyerɛ afoforɔ na osi gyinae sɛ ɔbɛbɔ asu a, sɛn na wote nka? Ɛba saa a, ɛma w’ani gye! Nanso yenim sɛ, sɛ yɛn Bible suani bi nya nkɔso a ɛte saa a, ɛnyɛ yɛn ahoɔden, na mmom ɛyɛ Yehowa mmoa so. Sɛ yehu sɛ obi ase retim wɔ nokware no mu a, ɛho ayeyi nyinaa, yɛde ma Yehowa.—1 Kor. 3:5-9; 3 Yoh. 3, 4.
<<<P>>>
2 Onyankopɔn koma so adeɛ ne sɛ ‘obegye nnipa ahorow nyinaa na wɔanya nokware no ho nimdeɛ a edi mu.’ (Kenkan 1 Timoteo 2:3, 4.) Yehowa pɛ sɛ nkurɔfo hu no. Ɛnyɛ ɛno nko, biribiara a wabɔ ne tirim sɛ ɔbɛyɛ ama adasamma nso, ɔpɛ sɛ nnipa hu. (Yer. 9:24) Sɛ yebetumi ayɛ saa a, gye sɛ yenya nokware no ho nimdeɛ a edi mu. Ɛnneɛ yemmisa sɛ, sɛ yɛka sɛ obi wɔ nokware no ho nimdeɛ a edi mu a, ɛkyerɛ sɛn? Bible ho ɔbenfo bi kyerɛɛ mu sɛ, sɛ obi wɔ nokware no ho nimdeɛ a edi mu a, ɛnyɛ sɛ ɔwɔ Onyankopɔn ho nimdeɛ ara kɛkɛ, na mmom ɔma Onyankopɔn ho nimdeɛ a ɔwɔ no kyerɛ no kwan wɔ n’abrabɔ mu. Enti sɛ Bible suani bi hu Yehowa a, nimdeɛ a onya no, ɔma ɛsesa n’abrabɔ, na ɛma onya anidaso sɛ obetumi anya “daa nkwa.”—Yoh. 17:3.
<<<P>>>
3 Yehowa adom yɛn paa; wama yɛn hokwan sɛ yɛmmoa afoforɔ ma wɔmmehu no. Adesua yi mu no, yebesusu nsɛmmisa yi ho. Nea edi kan, adɛn nti na ɛsɛ sɛ Bible asuafo hu Yehowa yie? Nea ɛtɔ so mmienu, yɛbɛyɛ dɛn aboa Bible asuafo ama wɔahu sɛnea Yehowa te? Nea etwa to, yɛbɛyɛ dɛn aboa wɔn ama wɔde nea wɔresua no ayɛ adwuma wɔ wɔn asetena mu?
<<<P>>>
1. Sɛ wuhu sɛ wo Bible suani bi renya nkɔso a, sɛn na wote nka?
<<<P>>>
2. Yehowa koma so adeɛ ne sɛn? (1 Timoteo 2:3, 4)
<<<P>>>
3. Dɛn na yebesusu ho wɔ adesua yi mu?
