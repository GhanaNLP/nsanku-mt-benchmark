6 Joseph a yɛadi kan aka ne ho asɛm no ma yehu nea Yehowa yɛ de boaa ɔne ne yere bere a wofii Betel no. Ɔka sɛ: “Bere a yefii Betel no yehuu Yehowa nsa wɔ yɛn abrabɔ mu paa. Ɔnam yɛn nuanom so boaa yɛn. Biribiara a yehia wɔ asetena mu no, Yehowa maa yɛn nsa kae, na awerɛkyekye nso de, ɔmaa yɛn bi paa. Yehowa nnyaa yɛn mu da!” Wo nso, sɛ nneɛma sesa prɛko pɛ wɔ w’abrabɔ mu na sɛ emu yɛ den sɛ dɛn mpo a, nya awerɛhyem sɛ Yehowa bɛkyerɛ wo kwan na wahyɛ wo den. (Yes. 54:10; 58:11) Joseph de kaa ho sɛ: “Yɛn nnwom no baako mu nsɛm ka me koma paa. Eka sɛ: ‘Nea ɔkyena de bɛba, me deɛ minnim oo. Nea ebesi biara, ɔka me ho.’* Ewom, ebia nneɛma renkɔ sɛnea yɛhwɛ kwan, nanso ɛsɛ sɛ yɛkae sɛ biribiara a ebesi wɔ yɛn abrabɔ mu no, Yehowa ka yɛn ho.”

7. Sɛn na bɔne a ebinom ayɛ atwam betumi ama wɔate nka?
<<<P>>>
ho sɛ yɛrensan nkɔyɛ saa bɔne no bio. (Mmeb. 28:13; Yak. 5:14-16; 1 Yoh. 1:9) Nanso ɛmfa ho sɛ Yehowa de yɛn bɔne akyɛ yɛn nyinaa akyi no, yɛn ahonim betumi ateetee yɛn saa ara. Ebia wobɛka sɛ, ‘Saa bɔne a mayɛ yi deɛ, ɛbɛyɛ dɛn na Yehowa de akyɛ me?’ (2 Kor. 2:7) Sɛ wote nka saa nso a, ntease wom, efisɛ bɔne a yɛayɛ atwam no, ɛho adwendwen tumi hyɛ yɛn so, na etumi yɛ den sɛ ebefi yɛn tirim. Bere a Ɔhene Dawid yɛɛ bɔne no, ɔkaa sɛ: “Me bɔne nti, me nnompe mu nnwo me.” Ɔsan de kaa ho sɛ: “Madwudwo, na mabubu koraa.” (Dw. 38:3, 8) Wo nso, sɛ bɔne a wayɛ atwam da so ara reteetee wo a, ɛnneɛ Yesaia nwoma no bɛboa wo paa.

8 Kenkan Yesaia 1:18. Yehowa de mfatoho kamakama bi kyerɛ yɛn sɛnea ɔde yɛn bɔne kyɛ yɛn korakora. Ɔka sɛ, sɛ yɛn bɔne “te sɛ koogyan” mpo a, ɔbɛma no ayɛ “fitaa sɛ sukyerɛmma.” Yenim sɛ koogyan yɛ biribi a ɛyɛ den sɛ ɛbɛpa. Nanso Yehowa ma yɛn awerɛhyem sɛ, sɛ ɔde yɛn bɔne kyɛ yɛn a, na ɔde akyɛ yɛn koraa; ɔrenkae bio da!
