Otumfoɔ, Yehowa Nyankopɔn
<<<P>>>
NEA ADESUA YI BƐKA HO ASƐM
<<<P>>>
Yɛbɛhwɛ nea ɛkyerɛ sɛ obi ahu Yehowa anaa onim no, nea enti a ɛsɛ sɛ yehu Yehowa yie, ne ɔkwan a yɛbɛfa so ahu no yie.
<<<P>>>
Kɔ So Ara Hu Yehowa Yie
<<<P>>>
“Hu Onyankopɔn a wo papa som no no, na fa koma a edi mu som . . . no.”—1 BE. 28:9.
<<<P>>>
YEHOWA nimdeɛ ne ne nyansa mu dɔ, na n’anwanwadwuma nso deɛ bebree kwa. Sɛ yɛdwen sɛnea Ɔbɔadeɛ no te ho a, ɛma yɛn ho dwiri yɛn. Wei nti, ebia yɛn nso yɛbɛka asɛm a ɔsomafo Paul kae yi bi: “O hwɛ sɛnea Onyankopɔn ahonyade ne ne nyansa ne ne nimdeɛ mu dɔ! Yɛrentumi mpɛɛpɛɛ n’atemmu mu nwie da, na yɛrentumi nhwehwɛ n’akwan mu nwie da!”—Rom. 11:33, 34.
<<<P>>>
2 Ɛwom sɛ yɛrentumi nhu biribiara a ɛfa Yehowa ho, nanso nea ɛbɛboa yɛn ama yɛatumi ahu sɛnea ɔte deɛ, yebetumi ahu. Nea enti a yɛreka saa ne sɛ, Ɔhene Dawid ka kyerɛɛ ne ba Solomon sɛ: ‘Hu Onyankopɔn a wo papa som no no, na fa koma a edi mu som no. Sɛ wohwehwɛ no a, ɔbɛma woahu no.’ (1 Be. 28:9) Mfe pii akyi no, ɔsomafo Yohane nso ka kyerɛɛ ne nuanom Kristofo sɛ, ‘wɔbetumi anya ɔnokwafo no ho nimdeɛ.’ (1 Yoh. 5:20) Yesu ankasa nso kaa sɛ, obiara a ɔpɛ sɛ ohu “Agya no yie” no, ɔbɛboa no ma wahu no.—Mat. 11:27.
<<<P>>>
3 Yɛn a yɛahyira yɛn ho so ama Yehowa no, yɛasua ne ho adeɛ pii dada. Nanso ‘yɛrenhu’ no nwie da, anaa yɛrensua ne ho adeɛ nwie da. (Yoh. 17:3) Yɛbɛyɛ nhwɛso a, nea yɛyɛ a ɛbɛma Yehowa ani agye, ne sɛnea yebetumi anya ne mu ahotoso kɛse no, da biara yebetumi asua ho adeɛ. Sɛ yɛbaa asafo no mu akyɛ oo, sɛ ɛnkyɛe oo, momma yensusu nsɛmmisa mmiɛnsa bi ho. Ɛbɛboa yɛn nyinaa ama yɛahu Yehowa yie. Ɛne sɛ: Sɛ yɛka sɛ obi ahu Yehowa yie a, ɛkyerɛ sɛn? Adɛn nti na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛkɔ so hu Yehowa yie? Ɔkwan bɛn na yɛbɛfa so ahu no yie?
<<<P>>>
1. Sɛ yɛdwen Yehowa nyansa, ne nimdeɛ ne n’anwanwadwuma ho a, ɛma yɛte nka sɛn?
<<<P>>>
2. Dɛn na ɛkyerɛ sɛ yebetumi ahu Yehowa yie?
<<<P>>>
3. Dɛn na yebesusu ho wɔ adesua yi mu?
