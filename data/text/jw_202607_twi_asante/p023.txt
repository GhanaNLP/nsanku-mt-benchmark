ƆRESUA NO NYƐ ADWUMA

12 Sɛ wo Bible suani betumi ahu mfaso a ɛwɔ nea ɔresua no so a, gye sɛ ɔde yɛ adwuma. Ɛnneɛ, wobɛyɛ dɛn aboa no ama watu-mi de nea ɔresua no ayɛ adwuma? Nea edi kan, sɛ ɔkenkan Bible mu asɛm bi na ɔnte ase a, nea onim fa Yehowa ho dada no, boa no ma ɔndwendwen ho. Yɛbɛyɛ nhwɛso a, ebia wo suani no akan biribi afa Yehowa atemmu ho, na ɔredwen ho sɛ adɛn nti na ebinom wɔ hɔ a, Yehowa sɛee wɔn, na ebi-nom nso deɛ wanyɛ wɔn hwee. Sɛ wo Bible suani hyia tebea bi sei a, wobɛyɛ dɛn aboa no ama wate ase?
