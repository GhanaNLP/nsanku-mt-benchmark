Dwendwen nneɛma a Yehowa abɔ ho, na ɛbɛma woahu no yie
<<<P>>>
(Hwɛ nkyekyɛm 15)
<<<P>>>
asua Bible no. Ɛtɔ da a Bible hyɛ da ka Yehowa suban pɔtee bi ho asɛm. (Ex. 34:6, 7) Nanso mpɛn pii no, ɛka nneɛma a ɛkɔɔ so wɔ nkurɔfo asetenam ne gyinae a wɔsisii ho asɛm. Enti sɛ wokan Bible mu asɛm bi a ɛte saa a, to wo bo ase hwɛ nea ɛma wuhu fa Yehowa ho.
<<<P>>>
13 Yɛnhwɛ nhwɛso bi. Fa no sɛ worekenkan Yeremia 38:6-13, ɛnna wuhui sɛ Etiopiani Ebed-Melek yɛɛ biribi de gyee odiyifo Yeremia. Anhwɛ a nea w’adwene bɛkɔ so ntɛm ne nea wubetumi asua afi Ebed-Melek hɔ; n’ahofama, n’akokoduru ne ɔdɔ a na ɔwɔ. Na Yehowa suban nso ɛ, wuhu bi wɔ saa asɛm yi mu? (Kenkan Yeremia 39:15-18.) Sɛ wohwɛ saa tworɔsɛm yi a, nkyekyɛm bɛn na ɛma wuhu sɛ Yehowa kyerɛ nneɛma ho anisɔ, ɔnhwɛ nnipa anim na ɔne obiara di no yie? Yehowa suban foforɔ bɛn na wuhu wɔ saa asɛm yi mu? Bere biara a wobɛkenkan Bible mu asɛm bi no, bɔ mmɔden sɛ wubehu Yehowa suban a ɛwom, na da no ase sɛ wama woahu saa suban no.
<<<P>>>
14 Ade a ɛtɔ so mmienu a ɛbɛboa yɛn ama yɛahu Yehowa yie ne sɛ, yɛbɛdwendwen nneɛma a wabɔ ho. (Dw. 145:9, 10) Nanso ɛnyɛ sɛ yɛhwɛ abɔdeɛ ara kɛkɛ a, ɛbɛma yɛahu Yehowa yie. Ebinom de wɔn nkwa nna nyinaa asua abɔdeɛ ho adeɛ, nanso wɔnnye nni sɛ Onyankopɔn bi wɔ hɔ na kampɛsɛ wɔahu ne suban. Ɔsomafo Paul kaa sɛ Onyankopɔn “suban ahorow a nnipa ntumi nhu . . . no, ada adi pefee fi bere a ɔbɔɔ wiase, na wɔnam nneɛma a wayɛ so na ehu.” (Rom. 1:20) Bible nkyerɛase bi kyerɛ saa nkyekyɛm yi ase sɛ, “sɛ wudwen nneɛma ho yiye a,” wubetumi ahu Onyankopɔn suban. Sɛ yɛhwɛ nneɛma a Yehowa abɔ na yɛdwendwen ho a, ɛbɛma yɛahu ne suban.
