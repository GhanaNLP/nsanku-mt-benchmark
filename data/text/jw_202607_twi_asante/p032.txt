<<<P>>>
Nea Ɛbɛboa Yɛn Ama Yɛama Mmuae a Ɛhyɛ Nkuran
<<<P>>>
Sɛ yɛkɔ adesua a, yɛn mu biara wɔ hokwan sɛ ɔma mmuae de hyɛ ne nuanom nkuran. (Heb. 10:24, 25) Dɛn na ɛbɛboa yɛn ama yɛatumi ama mmuae a ɛbɛhyɛ afoforɔ nkuran?
<<<P>>>
• Bɔ Yehowa mpae. Srɛ no sɛ ɔmmoa wo na ma mmuae a ɛbɛhyɛ no anuonyam, na ahyɛ afoforɔ nso nkuran.—Dw. 35:18.
<<<P>>>
• Fa yɛ wo botae sɛ worenkenkan mmuae no mfi nwoma no mu tee. Sɛ wode wo ara wo nsɛm ma mmuae a, ɛbɛhyɛ afoforɔ den na ɛbɛma wɔahu sɛ wowɔ Yehowa mu gyidi.
<<<P>>>
• Ma wo mmuae nyɛ tiawa. Sɛ wokɔ adesua na woma mmuae ntiantiaa a emu da hɔ a, ɛka afoforɔ koma paa. Afei nso, ɛma nnipa pii nya kwan kasa.—Mmeb. 10:19.
<<<P>>>
• Ma wo mmuae nhyɛ afoforɔ den. Sɛ worema mmuae a, mfa ntu anuanom fo anaa mfa ntwi wɔn anim.
<<<P>>>
Sɛ woma wo nsa so na yɛamfrɛ wo mpo a, wubetumi ahyɛ afoforɔ nkuran. Wobɛyɛ no sɛn? Sɛ obi rema mmuae a, yɛ aso tie na akyiri yi woakamfo no.—Mmeb. 10:21.
