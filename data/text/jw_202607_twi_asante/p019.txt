(Heb. 4:16) Sɛ Yehowa yɛ biribi de boa wo a, ɛyɛ a tena ase bisa wo ho sɛ: ‘Enti nea Yehowa ayɛ de aboa me yi, dɛn na ɛma mihu fa ne ho? Ne suban bɛn na ɛma mihu?’

YƐRENTUMI NSUA YEHOWA HO ADEƐ NWIE DA
<<<P>>>
17 Sɛ yɛkɔ wiase foforɔ no mu a, nneɛma a yebesua afa Yehowa ho deɛ, ɛnyɛ agorɔ. Saa bere no, Yehowa bɛma yɛn nwoma mmobɔwee bi a ɛbɛma yɛahu nea ɔpɛ ne nea ɔmpɛ. (Adi. 20:12) Ɛhɔ deɛ, sɛ woto w’ani hwɛ benkum oo, woto w’ani hwɛ nifa oo, nneɛma a Onyankopɔn abɔ nko ara na wubehu. Enti ɛbɛma yɛasua nneɛma pii afa Yehowa ho. (Yes. 11:6-9) Wo deɛ, hwɛ sɛ wowɔ Paradise, na adeɛ rekye a adeɛ resa nyinaa, bɔne refi wo ho nkakrankakra, na w’adɔfo a wɔawuwu nso wɔanyan wɔn aba nkwa mu. Hwɛ sɛnea ɛno bɛma wahu Yehowa yie paa!
<<<P>>>
18 Yehowa deɛ, yɛrentumi nsua ne ho adeɛ nwie da! Ɔwɔ hɔ daa, enti “yɛrentumi nhwehwɛ ne ntease mu nwie.” (Dw. 90:2; 147:5) Nea ɛte ne sɛ, “adwuma a nokware Nyankopɔn no ayɛ fi mfiase kosi awiei no, nnipa renhu mu da.” (Ɔsɛnk. 3:11) Enti sɛ yɛbaa asafo no mu akyɛ oo, sɛ ɛnnɛra yi ara na yɛreba oo, momma yɛn nyinaa nyere yɛn ho nsua yɛn Agya a ɔwɔ ɔdɔ no ho adeɛ yie.
<<<P>>>
17-18. Adɛn nti na yebetumi aka sɛ yɛrentumi nsua Yehowa ho adeɛ nwie da?
<<<P>>>
MFONI HO NKYERƐKYERƐMU Anim: Ebed-Melek reyi Yeremia afi abura mu; nea ɔyɛe no kyerɛ sɛ na ɔwɔ akokoduru paa. Kratafa 18: Onua bi a dadwen ahyɛ ne so redwendwen sɛnea Yehowa boaa no ma otumi yɛɛ ne dwumadi wɔ nnawɔtwe mfinimfini adesua ase.
