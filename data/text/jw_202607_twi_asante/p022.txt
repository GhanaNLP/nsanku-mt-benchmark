Nsɛmmisa a Ɛboa Ma Yetumi Kyerɛkyerɛ Yie
<<<P>>>
Nsɛmmisa bebree wɔ Wubetumi Anya Anigye Daa! nwoma no mu, na ebetumi aboa wo Bible asuafo ma wɔahu Yehowa yie. Emu bi ne:
<<<P>>>
“Wɔn a wɔka asɛmpa no, Yehowa hu wɔn sɛn?” (adesua 21)
<<<P>>>
“Wohwɛ nea woasua afa Yehowa ne Yesu ho no a, wohwɛ a, sɛ wɔrebu nnipa atɛn a, wobebu atɛnkyea anaa wobebu atɛntrenee? Adɛn nti na woreka saa?” (adesua 33)
<<<P>>>
“Sɛ wutie Yehowa na woyɛ w’adeɛ nyansam a, ɛma ɔte nka sɛn? Adɛn ntia?” (adesua 34)
<<<P>>>
“Sɛ yɛware obi a ɔnnɔ Yehowa a, wohwɛ a Yehowa bɛte nka sɛn?” (adesua 42)
<<<P>>>
“Dɛn na ɛwɔ kyerɛwsɛm yi mu a ɛma yehu sɛ Yehowa de bɔne kyɛ a, na ɔde akyɛ?” (adesua 56)
<<<P>>>
“Sɛn na Yehowa ne abɔnefo di a ɛma yehu sɛ ɔte asɛm ase, ɔwɔ mmɔborɔhunu, na ɔwɔ ɔdɔ nso?” (adesua 57)
<<<P>>>
Yɛn nso, sɛ yɛne yɛn Bible asuafo resua ade a, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛde nsɛmmisa di dwuma. Sɛ wohwɛ Wubetumi Anya Anigye Daa! nwoma no sei a, nsɛmmisa a ɛwom no, nea enti a wɔyɛe ne sɛ ɛbɛboa Bible asuafo ama wɔasua nneɛma pii afa Yehowa ho, na wɔahu sɛnea ɔte. (Hwɛ adaka a yɛato din “Nsɛmmisa a Ɛboa Ma Yetumi Kyerɛkyerɛ Yie” no.) Enti sɛ moresua nwoma no a, saa nsɛmmisa no, ɛyɛ a fa di dwuma. Sɛ woyɛ saa a, ɛbɛboa wo suani no ama wahu sɛnea Yehowa te.
<<<P>>>
9 Sɛ yefi ase ne afoforɔ sua adeɛ a, ntɛm pa ara ɛsɛ sɛ yɛhyɛ wɔn nkuran sɛ wɔnkenkan Bible da biara.* Yɛbɛyɛ dɛn aboa wɔn ama wɔanya wɔn Bible akenkan so mfaso paa? Yebetumi ama wɔahu sɛ, sɛ wɔkenkan Bible mu asɛm bi wie a, wɔbetumi abisa wɔn ho sɛ, ‘Adɛn nti koraa na Yehowa ma wɔde saa asɛm yi baa Bible mu?’ ‘Yehowa suban bɛn na saa asɛm yi ma mihu?’ Ɔsomafo Yakobo ayɛ wei ho nhwɛso. Bere a ɔreka Hiob ho asɛm no, ɛnyɛ sɛnea Hiob miaa n’ani gyinaa sɔhwɛ mu nko ara na ɔkae, na mmom ɔkaa Yehowa suban nso ho asɛm. Ɔkaa sɛ Yehowa wɔ ayamhyehye ne mmɔborɔhunu.—Kenkan Yakobo 5:11.
<<<P>>>

<<<P>>>
* Hwɛ Wubetumi Anya Anigye Daa! nwoma no adesua a ɛtɔ so 05 no, ɔfã a yɛato din fa wei si w’ani so no. Eka sɛ: “Fa si w’ani so sɛ wobɛkenkan Bible da biara. Ɔfã a yɛato din ‘Kenkan Bible Da Biara’ no, sɛ wudi akyi a, ɛbɛboa wo ama woatumi ayɛ saa.”
<<<P>>>
9-10. (a) Dɛn na ɛbɛboa yɛn Bible asuafo ma wɔanya wɔn Bible akenkan so mfaso paa? (Yakobo 5:11) (b) Sɛnea Bible mu asɛm bi ma yehu nea Yehowa ani gye ho ne nea n’ani nnye ho no, ma ho nhwɛso.
