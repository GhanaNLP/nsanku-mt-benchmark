n’ayɔnkofa no mu ayɛ den. Yɛbɛyɛ nhwɛso a, sɛ da biara wokan Bible, wohwɛ Broadcasting dwumadi a ɛba bosome biara, anaa wokɔ nhyiam a, ɛyɛ a wote nka sɛn? Yɛyɛ saa a, yɛn ara yehu sɛ yɛabɛn yɛn soro Agya no. Adɛn ntia? Efi sɛ yɛasua nneɛma pii afa ne ho. Ɛno nti ɛma yeyi no ayɛ, na yɛda no ase. Yɛte nka te sɛ odwontofo Asaf. Mmɔden biara a yɛbɛbɔ sɛ yebehu Yehowa yie no, ɛbɛma yɛahu paa sɛ yɛne n’ayɔnkofa no yɛ biribi a ɛnsɛ sɛ yɛtoto no ase koraa.

9 Bible ka kyerɛ yɛn sɛ ‘yenhu’ Yehowa. (Mmeb. 3:5, 6) Nea wei kyerɛ ne sɛ, nneɛma a yɛasua afa Yehowa ho no, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛdwendwen ho, na yɛma ɛtena yɛn tirim sɛ sɛnea yɛbɔ yɛn bra no, ɛho hia Yehowa. Yɛyɛ saa a, ɛbɛma yɛasisi gyinae pa. Yɛbɛyɛ mfatoho a, anuanom bi wɔ hɔ a wɔde wɔn kunu anaa wɔn yere mfoni si wɔn pon so, wɔtumi de hyɛ wɔn walɛt mu, anaa wɔtumi de to wɔn fon so. Sɛ wɔyɛ saa a, ɛboa wɔn ma wɔtaa dwendwen wɔn hokafo no ho. Afei nso wɔhwɛ sɛ nea wɔbɛyɛ biara no, ɛbɛma wɔn hokafo no ani agye, na ɛnyɛ nea ɛbɛma wɔn werɛ aho anaa ɛbɛha wɔn. Yɛn nso, sɛ yɛtaa dwendwen Yehowa ho a, ɛbɛma yɛasisi gyinae a ɛbɛsɔ n’ani.—Dw. 19:13, 14.

11 Hena na ɔboaa wo ma wubehuu Yehowa? Ebia w’awofo na ɛde nokware no tetee wo. Ebia wɔkaa Bible mu nsɛm a ɛma wuhu Yehowa suban ho asɛm kyerɛɛ wo, na wɔma wuhui sɛ nneɛma a Yehowa abɔ nso, ɛma yehu ne suban. Anaasɛ ebia Yehowa Danseni bi na ɛbaa wo nkyɛn ne wo besuaa Bible. Sɛnea Bible mu nsɛm ne nneɛma a Yehowa abɔ ma yehu ne suban no, ebia nea ɔne wo sua ade no de Wubetumi Anya Anigye Daa! nwoma no boaa wo ma wodwendwen ho kɔɔ akyiri.* (Neh. 8:8; Aso. 14:17) Emu biara no, momma yɛnhwɛ nea yebetumi ayɛ na ama yɛakɔ so asua Yehowa ho adeɛ.
