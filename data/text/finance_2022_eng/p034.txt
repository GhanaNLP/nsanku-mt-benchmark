Population Census
<<<P>>>
83. The counting phase of the 2021 Population and Housing Census was completed on 8th August, 2021. The Draft Report, released on 22nd September, 2021, establised the total population of Ghana to be 30,792,608. Out of this, 15,610,149 million are females (50.7 percent) and 15,182,459 million are males (49.3 percent).
