ECONOMIC PERFORMANCE FOR 2021
<<<P>>>
Macroeconomic Performance
<<<P>>>
3. Provisional data on the performance of the economy in 2021 is as follows:
<<<P>>>
• Overall real GDP averaged 3.5 percent in the first half of 2021 against the 0.7 percent growth in same period 2020;
<<<P>>>
• Non-Oil real GDP grew at an average of 4.8 percent in the first half year of 2021 compared to 1.1 percent in the same period 2020;
<<<P>>>
• Inflation as at end-September was 10.6 percent, compared to the 10.4 percent same period in 2020;
<<<P>>>
• Overall budget deficit (cash) of 7.7 percent of GDP against the target of 7.4 percent of GDP;
<<<P>>>
• Primary balance recorded a deficit of 2.0 percent of GDP, compared to the target deficit of 1.7. percent;
<<<P>>>
• The Public debt (as percentage of GDP) stood at 77.7 percent at the end of September 2021, up from 76.1 percent at the end of December 2020;
<<<P>>>
• Gross International Reserves at end-August 2021 was US$11.44 billion, enough to cover 5.2 months of imports, compared to US$8.62 billion equivalent to 4.1 months of imports recorded at end-December 2020; and
<<<P>>>
• Exchange rates remained stable across the major international currencies for the three quarters of 2021. Specifically, the Ghana cedi depreciated cumulatively by 1.7 percent against the US Dollar by the end of September 2021, compared to a 2.9 percent depreciation in the same period in 2020.
