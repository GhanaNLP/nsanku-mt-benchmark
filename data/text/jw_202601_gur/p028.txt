Ho san paʼala yelemɛŋɛrɛ la ho wan base ti nɛreba putiʼirɛ bɔna la ani poan? (Bisɛ kaalegɔ zɛʼɛsi 7-8)
<<<P>>>
BEM ZUO TI TO TƆYERA YELEMƐŊƐRƐ LA?
<<<P>>>
7 Wuu to puyum baŋɛ seʼem la, la nari ti to tɔyera yelemɛŋɛrɛ to san bɔta ti to kelum bɔna Yehowa yizuo la poan. Ge to san bɔta ti a puurɛ pee, la ka nari ti to yeen tɔyera yelemɛŋɛrɛ maʼa. Sɛla n sɔi ti to tɔyera yelemɛŋɛrɛ la pakɛ Yehowa mɛ. To geele bisɛ Yezu n yuun boi tinparɛ wa zuo mɔɔla la. (Kaalɛ Mark 3:11, 12.) Yezu n yuun ta lɛm Galilee Mɔyerɛ la mɔɔla la, nɛreba zoʼe zoʼe n yuun paɛ a zɛʼan. Kolekpabɛʼɛsi n yuun doli basɛba ti ba luta Yezu nɛŋan ge kaasa ti: “Ho dela Yenɛ Dayɔa.” Beni n sɔi ti kolekpabɛʼɛsi la yuun tɔɣɛ yelemɛŋɛrɛ ti Yezu dela Naayinɛ Dayua? Daanseʼere ba boti la ba tɔɣɛ ti nɛreba la dekɛ bamea delum ba ge nyaa pãʼasɛ ba ti ba da le puʼusa Yehowa. Kolekpabɛʼɛsi la n yuun tɔɣɛ sɛla la dela yelemɛŋɛrɛ ge ba yuun tari la putɛbeʼo. Ba yuun ka pãʼasɛ Yezu bii ba eŋɛ sɛla ti a puurɛ pee. Hali, Yezu yuun yele kolekpabɛʼɛsi la ti ba da mɔɔlɛ a yele.
<<<P>>>
8 To wan zamesɛ la bem yele wa poan? Sɛla zuo ti to tɔyera yelemɛŋɛrɛ la pakɛ Yehowa mɛ. Bala la, la yele pakɛ mɛ ti to mɔɔla yelemɛŋɛrɛ la bɔʼɔra nɛreba ge ba me san pẽyera tɔ, to wan dekɛ pẽka la bo Yehowa!—Mt. 5:16; dekɛ makɛ Tm. 14:12-15.
