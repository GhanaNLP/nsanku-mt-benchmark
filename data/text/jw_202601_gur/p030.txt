Tɔyesa Yezu taʼan mina seʼem maʼa ti la nara ti ho mɔɔlɛ yelesoma la, la hon wan mɔɔla de seʼem ( Bisɛ kaalegɔ zɛʼan 15)
<<<P>>>
13 Paul yuun le yele a kɛleseriba la ti ba basɛ ti ba yetɔyum la zaʼasum wuu dia la. Bala ka paʼalɛ ti to basera yɛla aseʼa bii suyera yelemɛŋɛrɛ la. Ge a yuun boti a yeti to basɛ ti to yetɔyum la “eta nɛreba suunre me nyɛlom” lan wan eŋɛ seʼem ti to san mɔɔla yelemɛŋɛrɛ la bɔʼɔra nɛreba, la wan “zaʼasum” bo ba. (Yob 12:11) Bala taʼan kpeʼem bo to. La san dɛna dimɛnka to taʼan tiʼisa ti sɛla n zaʼasum bo tomam la me zaʼasum bo la nɛra woo. Bala mea nɔɔ la, to taʼan tiʼisa ti ton tɔyeri seʼem la ani soŋa bɔʼɔra la nɛra woo. Ge la dayɛ bala. Makerɛ, buuri aseʼa nɛreba san tɔyera sɛla bɔʼɔra nɛreba, ba ni kɔʼɔn tɔyɛ la sɛla n boi ba suuren, hali ba san tɔyera bɔʼɔra bunkureba. Ge buuri basɛba me nɛreba taʼan bisɛ ti ho san tɔyera bala la ka nari, la dela zɛɛsi. Paul yeti la dela to mina sɛla n nari taʼan “lebehe . . . sɛla n de maʼan.” Bala paʼalɛ ti to basɛ ti to yetɔyum ana nyɛlum bɔʼɔra nɛreba ge dayɛ tomam mea n boti seʼem.

MINA SEʼEM MAʼA TI LA NARA TI HO PAʼALƐ BAABULE YƐLA ASEʼA
<<<P>>>
14 Yezu yuun san tɔyera bɔʼɔra a podɔleba la, a ni mina la guŋerɛ ge ti a yetɔyum me zaʼasum. Bala n sɔi ti a yuun taʼan paʼala yɛla zoʼe zoʼe. (Mr. 6:34) Ge yɛla aseʼa n yuun boi ti ba nara ti ba zamesɛ paʼasɛ. Yezu yuun ka boti a paʼalɛ ba sɛla woo ti a mina. A yuun mi la ba tɔregɔ beene. A yuun bɔkɛ ti, ba kan taʼan zamesɛ yɛla aseʼa paʼasɛ sankaŋa. A yeti, ba kan taʼan bɔkɛ yele aŋa soŋa. (Yon 16:12) Bala paʼalɛ to la bem?
<<<P>>>
15 Yezu makerɛ la paʼalɛ ti, ton mi yelemɛŋɛrɛ la ka paʼalɛ ti to paʼala sɛla woo ti to mina. To wan tɔyesa Yezu la wani?
<<<P>>>
13. To wan eŋɛ la wani taʼan basɛ ti to yetɔyum ana nyɛlum?
<<<P>>>
14. Yezu n yuun boi tiŋgɔŋɔ wa zuo la, a yuun paʼalɛ a podɔleba la sɛla woo ti a mina? Tɔyɛ paʼalɛ.

15. La nari ti to paʼalɛ to Baabule zamesegɔdoma sɛla woo ti to mina? Tɔyɛ paʼalɛ. (Magaha 25:11) (Bisɛ foote la.)
