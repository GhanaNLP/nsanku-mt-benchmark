3 Wuu Yezu la, to me tiregɛ tara la yelemɛŋɛrɛ ge me tɔɣera yelemɛŋɛrɛ saŋa woo hali nɛreba san puyum ka bɔta bala. La boi bim ti nɛreba basɛba sunsɔa ta’an nyie la sɛla ti to tɔɣera la ge to kɔ’ɔn mɔɔla ge pa’ala Baabule yelemɛŋɛrɛ la mɛ. Bala pa’alɛ ti la ka nari ti to ni geele bisɛ to wan tɔɣɛ yelemɛŋɛrɛ la se’em? Dayɛ bala la fii me! Zaseŋɔ wa poan, to wan nyɛ sokere wa lebesego: Bɛ ti to wan ta’an nyɛ yelemɛŋɛrɛ la? To me wan le nyɛ soke ana wa lebesego: Bem zuo ti la nara ti to tɔɣera yelemɛŋɛrɛ la ge to wan tɔɣɛ dɛ la wani? Wani ma’a ti la nara ti to tɔɣera yelemɛŋɛrɛ la? Soke ana wa lebesego wan soŋɛ tɔ ti to ta’an tɔɣera yelemɛŋɛrɛ la soŋa soŋa ge me mina guŋerɛ la sansɛka n nari ti to tɔɣɛ.

5 Nɛreba basɛba yeti la kpe’em mɛ ti ho baŋɛ Yehowa, “sakerɛ sera Yenɛ” la. Yelese’a ti to nyɛta la pa’alɛ ti Naayinɛ boi. (Arom 1:20) Paul n yuun tɔɣeri bɔ’ɔra Greek gɔnmi’ileba n boi Atens la, a yuun yeti Naayinɛ boti la to ‘ɛɛra en’ ge “[a] ka zaen la to.” (Kaalɛ Tooma 17:27.) Yelemɛŋɛrɛ, Yehowa ve’eri la sɛba n siregeri bamea ge me bɔta yelemɛŋɛrɛ la paara amea.—Yon 6:44.
<<<P>>>
6 Sore deyima poan ti to wan ta’an baŋɛ Yehowa dela to san zamesa Baabule la. Naayinɛ dekɛ la a zilam vo’osum la soŋɛ buraasi ti ba gulesɛ Baabule la. (2 Pe. 1:20, 21) Bala la, sɛla woo n boi Baabule la poan la dela yelemɛŋɛrɛ ge ti to me wan ta’an sakɛ bo. Makerɛ, to sakɛ ti Baabule la n tɔɣɛ saazuo la tinparɛ wa n pi’ilum se’em ti vom bɔna bim la dela yelemɛŋɛrɛ. (Pin. 1:1, 26) To sakɛ Baabule la n pa’alɛ sɛla n sɔi ti be’em, kum la namesegɔ bɔna la. (Arom 5:12; 6:23) To tari suure lua la Baabule la n yeti Yehowa wan doose la a Dayua la poan maalum maalɛ sɛla woo ti Sitaana n de “ponpɔrɔŋdoma naba” sa’am la. (Yon 8:44; Arom. 16:20) To tari sakerɛ la Baabule la n pu’ulum biŋe ti Yezu wan sa’am putɔpa, isige sɛba n ki la, malum maalɛ tinparɛ wa ge me soŋɛ tɔ ti to ta ana kasi la. (Yon 11:25, 26; 1 Yo. 3:8) Bisɛ Yehowa n bo to yɔ’ɔ kātɛ se’em! A pa’alɛ tɔ yelemɛŋɛrɛ la ge me bo to yɔ’ɔ ti to me pa’ala nɛreba.—Mt. 28:19, 20.
