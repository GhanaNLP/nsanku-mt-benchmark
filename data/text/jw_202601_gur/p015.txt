Yezu lua bunɔ la yele.* Lua bunɔ la zuo (1) Yehowa diti la sugeri bɔ'ɔra tɔ, (2) to tari puti'irɛ ti to wan ta ana kasi beere sa'am, la (3) to wan ta'an tara bɔnsoŋa la to Naanɔdaana la. To san geele yele ana wa, la wan basɛ ti to tara nɔŋerɛ bɔ'ɔra Yehowa, “Yenɛ se'em n bahere te nɛreba din'inha sunya gura.” (Arom 15:13) To puurɛ n pee la Naayinɛ se're n sɔi la, “la dela a Dayɔa Yesu poan te eŋa Yenɛ fan' to bahɛ.”—Kol. 1:14.

* YELEBIRE VUURƐ: Lua bunɔ la dela hon dekɛ sɛla yɔ to'e nɛra yamenɛ poan basɛ. Yezu kum la dela lua bunɔ sɛla n sɔi la, la faaɛ la nɛresɛba n nɔŋi Naayinɛ la be'em la kum poan basɛ.

pɔyedisa'aŋɔ bii a ku nɛra, la nari ti ba me ku en. (Mal. 20:10; Kan. 35:30, 31) Be'em ase'a ka ba'ɛ dɛna yelekãtɛ ge ba kelum dɛna la be'em. Makerɛ, Yoomadaana David yeti: “N wan gu'ura n mɛŋa soŋa, la n da dekɛ n zeleŋa tom be'em.” (Yooma 39:1) Yelemɛŋɛrɛ, hali to yetɔyum ta'an dɛna be'em.—Yam. 3:2.

5 Ti'isɛ bisɛ hon tabelɛ tɔɣɛ bii eŋɛ sɛla ti la ka ana soŋa. Hon tabelɛ tɔɣɛ nɛra sɛla ge bɔta ti ho lebe luke? Hon tabelɛ eŋɛ sɛla ge yeti ho san baŋɛ ti hon kan eŋɛ ti? Nɔkpe'ene kai ti, ho lebesego la dela ẽɛ. Baabule la yeti: “To san yeti to ka tara yelebe'ero, to pa'asɛ la to mɛŋa ge me ka tɔgɔra yelemɛŋɛrɛ.”—1 Yo. 1:8.
