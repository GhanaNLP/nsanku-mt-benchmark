YUUNƐ 18
To Puurɛ Pee Mɛ La Lua Bunɔ La
<<<P>>>
YELEKPINE
To zaʼa waabi n wan eŋɛ seʼem paʼalɛ ti to puurɛ pee ya la lua bunɔ la.
<<<P>>>
Ho Wan Eŋɛ La Bem Paʼalɛ Ti Ho Puurɛ Pee Ya La Lua Bunɔ La?
<<<P>>>
‘Krista nɔŋerɛ la n kẽ to suure.’—2 KO. 5:14.
<<<P>>>
YIRE san tabelɛ lu dĩ ho ti nɛra faaɛ hɔ basɛ ho puurɛ kan pee la nɛra la? Hali ba san faaɛ nɛreba basɛba paʼasɛ homea poan ho wan bɔta ti ho eŋɛ paʼalɛ ti ho puurɛ pee ya.
<<<P>>>
2 Wuu zaseŋɔ seko n tole la n paʼalɛ seʼem la, to kan taʼan faaɛ tomea beʼem poan. Ge Yehowa n eŋɛ sɛla n wana: A dekɛ la a Dayua la bo to ti to (1) taʼan nyɛ to beʼem sugeri dia, (2) taʼan tara beere saʼam putiʼirɛ ti to wan ta ana kasi (3) ti to le tara bɔnsoŋa la Naayinɛ. Lua bunɔ la zuo ti to tara putiʼirɛ ti to wan ta vɔna daarewoo tinpaalega la poan. Yezu yuun eŋɛ paʼalɛ ti a tari la nɔŋerɛ zoʼe zoʼe bɔʼɔra nɛreba bala n sɔi ti a yuun ki bo to la. A yuun nɔŋɛ nɛreba mɛ ge ti ba nyaa dɔɣɛ en tinparɛ wa zuo. (Mag. 8:30, 31) Tuntuna Paul yeti, ‘Krista nɔŋerɛ la n kẽ to suure.’ (Kaalɛ 2 Korint 5:14, 15.) Bala vuurɛ dela to eŋɛ paʼalɛ ti to puurɛ pee ya la Yezu nɔŋerɛ la ge me da malum tamɛ eŋa n eŋɛ sɛla bo to la.
<<<P>>>
3 Ho wan eŋɛ la bem paʼalɛ ti ho puurɛ pee ya la lua bunɔ la? Ho lebesego la taʼan bɔna de toʼore la basɛba. To makɛ yeti nɛreba batã n yeti ba keŋɛ tiŋa ayima ge zeʼele tisi toʼore toʼore. Ba sarɔtɔ la wan bɔna la toʼore toʼore. Bala mea nɔɔ, “sarɔseko” ti ho wan eŋɛ paʼalɛ ti ho puurɛ pee ya la lua bunɔ la wan paʼalɛ la hon la Yehowa bɔŋa la n ani seʼem nananawa. Bala la, zaseŋɔ wa wan tɔɣɛ la nɛreba sugumnɔɔrɛ butã yele: (1) Baabule zamesegɔdoma, (2) sɛba n muse koʼom, la (3) mabiisi sɛba n buregɛ la.
<<<P>>>
1-2. Lua bunɔ la kẽ to suure ti to bɔta ti to eŋɛ la bem ge bem zuo? (2 Korint 5:14, 15) (Bisɛ foote la.)
<<<P>>>
3. Hon wan eŋɛ seʼem paʼalɛ ti ho puurɛ pee ya la lua bunɔ la taʼan bɔna de toʼore la basɛba, beni n sɔi?
