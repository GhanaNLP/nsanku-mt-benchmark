Yezu kum la maʼa n wan taʼan basɛ ti nɛresaaleba tara bɔnsoŋa la Yehowa (Bisɛ kaalegɔ zɛʼan 16)
<<<P>>>
LUA BUNƆ LA N BASƐ TI TO TARA BƆNSOŊA LA YEHOWA
<<<P>>>
14 Adam la Hawa n yuun tum beʼem la zuo, la basɛ ti asaala ka le tara bɔnsoŋa la Yehowa. Hali Baabule la yeti, asaala zaaɛ mɛ la Naayinɛ. (Arom 8:7, 8; Kol. 1:21) Beni n sɔi? Seʼere n sɔi la, Yehowa dela kasi ge me kisa beʼem. Baabule la tɔɣɛ Yehowa yele yeti: “Hom nini wa ba dɛna faʼa gaŋɛ i beha beʼem. Ho kan nyaŋɛ tɔʼɔha beʼem.” (Hab. 1:13) Beʼem n basɛ ti tomam la Naayinɛ bɔŋa la saʼam. To seʼem saʼam kan taʼan tara bɔnsoŋa la Naayinɛ, san dayena yele la maalɛ mɛ. Lua bunɔ la n basɛ ti yele la maalɛ ti to le tara bɔnsoŋa la Naayinɛ.
<<<P>>>
15 Baabule la yeti, “La dela Yesu Krista kum la zuo te Yenɛ wan taʼan bahɛ to yelebeʼero bo to.” (1 Yo. 2:2) Yezu kum la bo yɔʼɔ ti nɛresaaleba le dɛna Yehowa zɔdoma la wani? Yezu kum la basɛ ti sumaʼasum le bɔna Naayinɛ la nɛresaaleba tiŋasuka. (Arom 3:23-26) Hali Yezu n yuun nan ka waʼana ki la, Naayinɛ yuun yeti sɛba n yuun tari sakerɛ puʼusa en la dela tontɔɔdoma. (Pin. 15:1, 6) Bem zuo? Lua bunɔ la zuo. Yehowa yuun mi soŋa ti a Dayua Yezu wan ki faaɛ nɛresaaleba. (Isa. 46:10) Lua bunɔ la n bo yɔʼɔ ti nɛresaaleba le tara bɔnsoŋa la Naayinɛ.
<<<P>>>
16 Tiʼisɛ bisɛ hon tari bɔnsoŋa la Yehowa la n de yelesonɛ seʼem bo ho. Makerɛ, ho wan taʼan yi Yehowa ho “Sɔ,” wuu Yezu n yele seʼem la. (Mt. 6:9) Ho me taʼan le yi Yehowa ho “Zɔ.” To san yi Yehowa to “Sɔ” bii to “Zɔ,” la nari ti to siregera tomea ge tara zoolum bɔʼɔra en. Bem zuo? Sɛla n sɔi la to dela beʼemdoma ge me kan taʼan ana duʼa duʼa la Yehowa tomea paŋa poan. La dela lua bunɔ la zuo ti to wan nyaŋɛ tara bɔnsoŋa la Yehowa.
