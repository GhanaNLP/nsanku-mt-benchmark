<<<P>>>
SE EYOYE A OBI NKYERE OBI ASE
<<<P>>>
OBOFUO bi na owoo ho. Osore a, na oko wuram’ e’ko pe nam akum abedi, na waton bi. Na dakoro bi, okoo wuram’; otee se Kokotee free ne nua se, “Kokotee Asamoa e!” Osee, “Y’a nua e!” Osee, “Adwuma aduru so, na ma yenko ton’suom’ nko bo nnadee, na ye’nse ano, na ye’twa dua biara a, y’atimi de atwa.” Kokotee free onuanom no, na obofuo bata ho, na otee nsem no nnyina. Na Kokotee nua no bisaa onua Kokotee se, “Na kuro ben so na ye’beko akobo ye’nnadee?” Osee, “Ye’be ko Nsuakyi.” Na osee, “Da ben?” Osee, “Dwoada.” Obofuo ate nsem no nnyina, na osiim’ koo ’fie. Na obaa ’fie no, na okoboo Nsuakyi ’Dekuro amanee se, asem a okoo wuram’ wate no no. Na obofuo no ka kyeree Odekuro se, “Ma mmofra nko twitwa nnua mmera, na Dwoada Kokotee no dan ye’ho ba a, ye’de yen atoto nnua nom’.” Mmofra no ko twitwaa nnua no baye. Odekuro no ko see otomfuo a owo kurom’ ho maa no boo dayemfuo so maa no. Na otomfuo no bisaa Odekuro see, “Na nnayemfuo bebrebe yi a wo se me nko bobo yi dee, wo de’ye deen?” Na oboo no amanee se, “Obofuo koo wuram’ waba abebo me amanee se, Dwoada mmoa bi bedane ye’ho nnipa aba wo ton’suom’ abebo nnadee.” Otomfuo dii mmirika ko boo nnayemfuo no ntem. Ohene nsa kaa nnua ne nnayemfuo no wieye, na omaa esen boo ’dawuro se, “Dwoada, oba o, obarima o, obiara nnko babiara.” Dwoada soye, na anopa no, aberewa bi ka kyeree obofuo se, “Ko yam mmeko ne nkyene, ne gyene; na babi a mmoa no beworo ye'nwoma agu, na wo so wo ko a, na wo de mmeko no ayem', na se mmoa no ba ha, na se ye'kye yen, na se ye'dwane ako fa ye'nwoma ahye, na mmeko no te yen ya a, na y'aworo agu, na y'abe ye nnipa." Obofuo koreye, na oko bataa ho. Na otee se ye'fre se, " Kokotee Asamoa, Dwoada aso, mo mma yenko." Na ye'nnyina baye. Ye'be woroo ye'ho nwoma guu ho. Na odunsini bako a odim' dee, ode ne nwoma ako sie babi. Na obofuo no fweye maa mmoa no koreye. Oko faa nwoma no nnyina, na ode mmeko no a y'ayam yeem', na ode ko guu asuom', maa asuo no de koreye. Odunsini no dee a ode ko sieye no, obofuo no anhu. Obofuo no baa 'fie, na Ohene no soso ko free nkrofuo no wo ton'suom', maa ye'baa ne 'fie. Nkrofuo no baa ahenfie, na ye'bisaa Ohene se, "Aden nti na wo befree yen?" Ohene see, " Me nnipa ne mo, na mo dwane ko tenaa babi; nne na mo aba, nti na me se, mo mmera na me'mma mo nnko bio." Mmoa no ka kyeree Ohene se, " W'aka a, y'ate; na ye'nim se Odomankoma 'bofuo na wabeka nsem nnyina akyere wo nti nnye biribi, ye'ne wo betena; na ye'nim se dee wo'ka yi dee, ankra wo boa; nanso nnye biribi; obofuo na ye'da n'ase. Na ye'ne wo betena; na dee ye'kyire ne se: wo bekyere y'ase, anase wo nkoa biara so bekyere y'ase; enee ye'bebo wo man yi na y'ako." Ebere a Ohene ko free yen no, odunsini no ene ebinom dwaneye. Na odunsini no ko faa ne nwoma koreye; na nkae no dee ye'san baye. Enam se ye'anhu ye'nwoma. Ohene peneee mmoa no nhyehyeye no so, na mmoa no ne yen tenaa kama. Na mmarima a ye'wo kurom' ho waree mmoa no mma no bi ne yen woo mma. Na dakoro bi na mmoa no bi ene kurom'fuo no bi e'ko tokwa. Yei na kurom'ni no, ka kyeree aboa no se, " Fa sore ho, aboa te se wo Kokotee fekuo." Okaa sa pe, mmoa nnyina ani bereye, na ye'koo Ohene 'fie ko ka kyeree no se, "Asem a ye'ka kyeree wo, enna aduru ho no, na wo'ye no deen?" Ohene maa ye'ko free nkrofuo a ye'kaa asem no baye. Ohene fweem' fweem', na obuaye se, mmoa no nnyae mma enka, na ye'baye akye, obiara so nkaa bi da nkyeree yen. Na mmoa no see, "Ye'nntie." Na Ohene no see, "Na mo'nntie na mo dwene se dee 'barima yi kaye, oboa; mo nnye Nkokotee?" Na mmoa no see, "O! Y'ate." Na mmoa no ne Asuokyifuo no koye. Mmoa no boo kuro no maa no kaa nnipa beye se 'du. Na ye'dwane toaye, maa mmoa no dii bem. Mmoa no tieye, na ye'ka kyeree nkrofuo no se, "Asem dada nnye tiena, na se mo ne yen betena a, ye'kyi asekyere. Se mo dwen, na se mo beka a, enee mo nkyere yen 'hahan koro a ye'firim' baye na yenko.” Na nkrofuo no see, “Ye’ntimi nnye sa da. Adee a y’aye ama oman yi aseyee, ye’nnye bi da.” Nti ye’faa dawuro boom’ se, “Obiara nnkyere ’biara ase, na asekyere ma kuro bo.”
