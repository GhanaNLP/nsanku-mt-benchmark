16 Bible no aka ato hɔ sɛ ɛrenkyɛ koraa, Yehowa ne “asaase nyinaa so ahemfo” de besi ani. (Adi. 16:14) Edu saa bere no a, wɔn a wɔnim Yehowa na wɔde wɔn ho to ne so no, Yehowa bɛbɔ wɔn ho ban. (Dw. 9:10; kenkan Yohane 17:3.) Nanso wɔn a wɔnnye Yehowa ntom sɛ wɔn Agya ne wɔn Nyankopɔn no, sɛ edu bere a Yehowa rekeka ne ho no a, wɔbɛba abehu sɛ ɔno ne ade nyinaa so Tumfoɔ no. Yehowa aka ato hɔ sɛ: “Mɛhyɛ me ho anuonyam na matew me din ho, na mɛda me ho adi wɔ aman pii anim ma wɔahu sɛ mene Yehowa.” (Hes. 38:23) Saa bere no, Yehowa asomfo ani begye paa, efisɛ wɔnim Yehowa sɛ wɔn adamfo a obetwitwa agye wɔn!—Mal. 3:16.

17 Hokwan kɛse paa na yɛanya sɛ yɛreboa ama nkɔmhyɛ a ɛwɔ Sakaria 8:23 no aba mu. Ɛnyɛ yɛn nuanom nko ara na yɛanya hokwan ne wɔn abom reyɛ adwuma, na mmom abɔfo dɔm a wɔwɔ soro no nso. Yehowa nam wɔn so boa yɛn ma yɛka asɛmpa no kyerɛ nnipa a wɔka kasa horoɔ nyinaa wɔ asaase so baabiara. Biribiara a yehia na ama yɛatumi aka asɛmpa no akyerɛ nnipa a wɔfi aman ne mmusuakuw ne kasa nyinaa mu nso, Yehowa de ama yɛn. Enti momma yɛmmɔ mmɔden mfa saa nneɛma yi nni dwuma yie. Ɛno bɛma yɛaboa nnipa pii ama wɔabɛsom yɛn Nyankopɔn Yehowa a ɔwɔ mmɔborɔhunu ne aboterɛ, na onyiyi nnipa mu no. Enkosi sɛ Yehowa din ho bɛte no, momma yɛnyere yɛn ho nka asɛmpa no, na yɛmmɔ ne din no ho dawuro nkyerɛ nnipa nyinaa.—Luka 11:2.
