Asafo mu mpanyimfo tie Yehowa, asafo no, ne wɔn mfɛfo mpanyimfo (Hwɛ nkyekyɛm 16)
<<<P>>>
12 Yere wo ho sua Bible no. Mpae a Yehosafat bɔe no ma yehu sɛ na ɔyɛ obi a onim Onyankopɔn Asɛm no mu yie. Yehowa hyɛɛ Abraham bɔ sɛ ɔde bɔhyɛ asaase no bɛma n’asefo. Sɛnea saa bɔhyɛ no baa mu no, na Yehosafat nim. (Gen. 12:7) Mpae a Yehosafat bɔe no, na emu nsɛm no te sɛ mpae a Solomon bɔe bere a na wɔrehyira asɔrefie no so no. (2 Be. 6:28-30; 20:5-9) Nimdeɛ a na Yehosafat wɔ no boaa no ma ɔde ne ho too Yehowa so na osii ne bo sɛ obetie no. Yɛn nso, nimdeɛ a yɛwɔ no, ɛwɔ sɛ yɛkɔ so sua bi ka ho. (Mat. 13:52) Ɛba saa a, ɔhaw biara a yehyia no, yebesi yɛn bo sɛ yebetie Yehowa.
<<<P>>>
13 Brɛ wo ho ase. Yehosafat mpae no nso ma yehu sɛ na ɔbrɛ ne ho ase. Ná ɔyɛ ɔhene a ɔwɔ asraafo bebree, enti anka obetumi agye ne ho adi aka sɛ onhia Yehowa mmoa. Nanso ɔbrɛɛ ne ho ase bɔɔ mpae wɔ ɔman no nyinaa anim srɛɛ Yehowa sɛ, ɔmmoa wɔn, efisɛ wɔnhu nea wɔnyɛ. (2 Be. 20:12) Esiane sɛ na Yehosafat brɛ ne ho ase nti, bere a Yehowa maa no akwankyerɛ no, ɔyɛɛ ntɛm de yɛɛ adwuma. Yɛn nso, sɛ yɛbrɛ yɛn ho ase a, tebea biara mu no, yebetie Yehowa.—Filip. 2:5, 8.

12. Dɛn na yɛyɛ a ɛbɛma yɛasi yɛn bo sɛ yebetie Yehowa?

13. Sɛ yɛbrɛ yɛn ho ase a, ɛbɛboa yɛn sɛn?
<<<P>>>
14 Yɛ osetie, na afoforo bɛhwɛ asua. Yehowa faa odiyifo Yahasiel so buaa Yehosafat mpae no. Ɔde akwankyerɛ a emu da hɔ maa Yehosafat sɛ ɛnyɛ ɔmanfo no na wɔbɛko. Odiyifo Yahasiel ka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ: “Munkogyinagyina hɔ, monyɛ komm na monhwɛ sɛnea Yehowa begye mo nkwa.” Bible ka sɛ, ɛhɔ ara na Yehosafat “bɔɔ ne mu ase de n’anim butuw fam.” Ɔyɛɛ saa no, ɔmanfo no nyinaa nso “bɔɔ wɔn mu ase . . . som Yehowa.” Adeɛ kyee ara, Yehosafat ne ne manfo no fii adi sɛ wɔrekohyia atamfo no. Ɔka kyerɛɛ ɔman no sɛ, “munnya Yehowa” ne “n’adiyifo mu gyidi.”—2 Be. 20:14-20.
<<<P>>>
15 Esiane sɛ na Yehosafat wɔ gyidi paa nti, “ɔne ɔman no tuu agyina,” na oyiyii mmarima sɛ wɔnto nnwom ‘nni akofo no anim.’ (2 Be. 20:21) Hyɛ no nso sɛ, ɔne ɔmanfo no susuu ho ansa na ɔresi saa gyinae no. Nea ɔyɛe no hyɛɛ ne manfo no nkuran ma wɔde wɔn ho too Yehowa so, na wɔyɛɛ osetie. Yehowa nso hyiraa wɔn, enti atamfo no ara twaa wɔn ho koe, na wɔkunkum wɔn ho.—2 Be. 20:22-24.
