4 Xlẽ Yosua 9:8-13. Gibeontɔwo ble Yosua be anyigba didi aɖe ŋutɔ dzie yewotso. Wogblɔ be yewose be wofe Mawu Yehowae wɔ ava na wo woɖu Egipte kple Amoritɔwo fe fia siwo nye Sixon kple Og dzi. Gibeontɔwo dze aɖaŋu ale gbegbe be womefo nu tso nu si dzɔ ɖe Yeriko kple Ai dzi ya ŋu o, elabe esia ana Yosua nakpɔe be menye “anyigba didi” aɖeke dzie wotso o. Yosua kple Israel-metsitsiawo xɔ Gibeontɔwo fe nyaa dzi se eye wobla ŋutifafanu kpli wo, evɔ womebia gbe Yehowa hafi o. (Yos. 9:14, 15) Etefe medidi o Israel-metsitsiawo va kpɔe be ɖe Gibeontɔwo ble yewo, gake esi wònye be woka atam na Gibeontɔwo xoxo le “Yehowa, Israel fe Mawu la fe ŋkɔ me” ta la, wowɔ ɖe nubabla la dzi. (Yos. 9:16-19) Wona Gibeontɔwo zu “nakefɔlawo kple tsidulawo na ha la kple Yehowa fe vɔsamlekpui la.”—Yos. 9:27.
