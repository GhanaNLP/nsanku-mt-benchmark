TAƉODZINUA
<<<P>>>
Le nyati sia me la, míadzro kpɔɖeŋu siwo Gibeontɔwo ɖo kple ale si Yehowa wɔ nu ɖe wo ŋui la me.
<<<P>>>
Srɔ̃ Gibeontɔwo Ƒe Kpɔɖeŋu
<<<P>>>
“Gibeontɔwo wɔ ŋutifafa kple Israel, eye wole wo dome.”
—YOS. 10:1.
<<<P>>>
KPƆ nudzɔdzɔ sia ɖa le susu me. Ƒe 1473 Do Ŋgɔ Na Yesu (D.Y.) mee. Israel dukɔa le ava wɔm le Ŋugbedodonyigbaa xɔm. Yehowa kpe ɖe Israel-viwo ŋu woɖu du siwo nye Yeriko kple Ai dzi gbidii. Gake nane si womenɔ mɔ kpɔm na o la dzɔ. Amedzro aɖewo va do be anyigba didi aɖe dzie yewotso. Nya si wotsɔ vɛ ye nye be yewodi be yewoawɔ ŋutifafa kple Israel-viwo.
<<<P>>>
2 Gibeontɔwoe amedzroawo nye. Emae nye zi gbãtɔ si Biblia ƒo nu tso wo ŋu, gake menye emae anye zi mamlɛtɔ o. Biblia me nuŋlɔɖiwo ɖee fia be Gibeontɔwo kple Israel-viwo va nɔ anyi ɖekae ƒe alafa gbogbo aɖewo. Míate ŋu asrɔ̃ nu nyui geɖe tso Gibeontɔwo gbɔ, eye míade dzesi Yehowa ƒe nɔnɔmewo le ale si wòwɔ nu kpli woe me.
<<<P>>>
BƆBƆ ƉOKUIWÒ EYE NÀWƆ NU LE XƆSE ME
<<<P>>>
3 Gibeontɔwo nɔ wofe du sesẽ me le Gibeon esime Israel-viwo dze Kanaan-nyigbaa xɔxɔ gɔme. “Dukɔ adre” ye nɔ Kanaan-nyigba dzi eye wo me nɔlawo “sɔ gbɔ hesesẽ wu” Israel-viwo. Dukɔ mawo dometɔ ɖekae nye Hivitɔwo, eye woƒe dugã aɖee nye Gibeon. (5 Mose 7:1) Gibeontɔwo nye avakalẽtɔwo. (Yos. 10:2) Kanaan dukɔ bubuawo ɖoe be yewoawɔ ava kple Israel-viwo, gake Gibeontɔwo ya kpɔe be avawɔwɔ kple Israel makpa yewo o. Elabena wode
