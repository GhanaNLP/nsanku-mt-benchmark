KPE ĐE BIBLIA NUSRƆ̃VIA ŊU WÒANƆ YEHOWA NYAM ĐE EDZI
<<<P>>>
4 Yesu gblɔ be sedede gãtɔe nye be mía-lɔ̃ Yehowa kple dzi blibo, luʋɔ blibo, tamesusu blibo kple ŋusẽ blibo. (Xlẽ Marko 12:30.) Hafi Biblia nusrɔ̃vi nate ŋu alɔ̃ Yehowa la, ele be wòanyae nyuie. Míedi be Biblia nusrɔ̃vi naxɔ nyɔnyrɔ le esi wòlɔ̃ Yehowa ta, ke menye le esi wòdi be yeadze mía ŋu, yeanye Đasefo bubuwo xɔlɔ̃, alo esi Biblia ƒe nufiafia aɖewo dzɔa dzi nɛ ta o. Eya ta nu si koŋ wòle be wòanye míaƒe taɖodzinu ne míele Biblia srɔ̃m kple amewo ye nye be míakpe ɖe wo ŋu woanya Yehowa ahalɔ̃e.
<<<P>>>
5 Ne Biblia nusrɔ̃vi va le Yehowa nyam ɖe edzi helɔ̃e la, esia avãe wòaɖo toe. Yesu na eme kɔ be ame si lɔ̃ Mawu aɖo toe, esi wògblɔ be: “Be xexea nanya be melɔ̃ Fofo la ta la, mele nu si tututu Fofo la de se nam be mawɔ la wɔm.” (Yoh. 14:31) Le mɔ ma ke nua, Biblia nusrɔ̃vi si lɔ̃ Yehowa vevie la adi be yeaɖo toe ahaku ɖe eŋu bliboe.—1 Yoh. 5:3.
<<<P>>>
6 Zi geɖe la, Biblia nusrɔ̃vi siwo nya Yehowa nyuie tea ŋu tsoa nya me nyuie. Míate ŋu awɔ eƒe kpɔɖeŋu ale: Ne ame aɖe di be yeanye yameʋukula la, menye ɖeko wòasrɔ̃ mɔfiame siwo ku ɖe yameʋukuku ŋu alo aŋlɔ eŋuti dodokpɔwo ko evɔ o. Ke ele vevie hã be wòanɔ yameʋukula bibiwo gbɔ asrɔ̃ ale si wokunɛ eye eya ŋutɔ hã nakui hena ɣeyiɣi aɖe. Esia ana wòava nya ale si wòakui to yame ƒe nɔnɔme vovovowo me. Le mɔ ma ke nua, be nusrɔ̃vi nanya Yehowa nyuie la, menye ɖeko wòle be wòasrɔ̃ Biblia ko evɔ o. Ele vevie hã be wòawɔ nu si wòsrɔ̃ la ŋu dɔ le agbe me. Ne ewɔe alea, nu nyui siwo ado tso eme ana wòakpɔe be Biblia ƒe mɔfiamewo ɖea vi. Esia ana wòava nya Yehowa nyuie. Eye ne ede dzesi ale si Yehowa le kpekpem ɖe eŋui la, asu te atso nya me nyuie le nɔnɔme ɖe sia ɖe me.
<<<P>>>
7 Yehowa nyanya nyuie ye kpe ɖe Yosef ŋu esime Potifar srɔ̃ di be wòadɔ kpli ye. Đikeke mele eme o be le Yosef ƒe ɖevime la, anya se Adam kple Xawa, kpakple Abraham kple Sara ƒe ŋutinyawo. (1 Mose 2:24; 20:3, 6) Ŋutinya mawo anya kpe ɖe Yosef ŋu wòkpɔ Yehowa ƒe susu le srɔ̃ɖeɖe kple gbɔdɔdɔ ŋu. Yosef va nya Yehowa nyuie ale gbegbe be ete ŋu gbe Potifar srɔ̃ ƒe nyafoɖeamenuwo eye wògblɔ nɛ be: “Aleke gɔ̃ mawɔ vɔ̃ gã sia gbegbe ahawɔ nu vɔ̃ ɖe Mawu ŋu?” (1 Mose 39:7-9) Yosef nya nu siwo dzea Yehowa ŋu kple nu siwo medzea eŋu o. Enya be nuwɔna aɖewo ate ŋu ado vevesese na Mawu. Esia na Yosef tso nya me nyuie. Ne míekpe ɖe míaƒe Biblia nusrɔ̃viwo ŋu wonya Yehowa nyuie la, woawo hã woatso nya me wòadze eŋu.
