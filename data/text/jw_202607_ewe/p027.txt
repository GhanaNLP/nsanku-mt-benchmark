Menɔ yunivɛsiti xɔŋkɔ aɖe dem henɔ nu srɔ̃m be mava zu atikewɔla esime mexɔ nyɔnyrɔ. Dzinyelawo tsɔ nu geɖe sa vɔe ale be mate ŋu ade sukua. Anye ne mayi yunivɛsiti nusɔsrɔ̃awo dzi hafi, gake medi be matsɔ nye nu nyuitɔwo ana Yehowa ahasubɔe ɣeyiɣiawo katã.
<<<P>>>
Tɔnye gblɔ nam ŋkubiãtɔe be: “Ne èke atse dzudzɔ yunivɛsitia dedea, manyã wò le afe sia me!” Metɔtɔ eye melé blanui. Melɔ̃ dzinyelawo vevie eye nyemedi be mado vevesese na wo o. Eya ta zi geɖea, meyina ɖanɔa sukukpoa dzi le zã me hedoa gbe ɖa na Yehowa vevie gblɔna be: “Yehowa meɖe kuku, kpe ɖe ŋunye ne mate ŋu atso nya me nyuie.” Mlɔeba, meva tso nya me be madzudzɔ yunivɛsitia dede. Tɔnye nyãm le afea me. Eya ta meyɔ Wilson hegblɔ nu si dzɔa nɛ. Elɔ̃ faa be mava tsi ye gbɔ. Ale mefɔ nye nuwo de akplo ɖeka aɖe me heɖo bɔs yi egbɔ. Meɖo ŋku edzi be nye dzi koe dze eme enumake. Mekpɔe be metso nya me nyuie.
