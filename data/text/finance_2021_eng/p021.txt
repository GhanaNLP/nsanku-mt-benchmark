MINISTRY OF ENERGY
<<<P>>>
29. In an effort to provide safe and reliable supply of energy, Government in 2020:
<<<P>>>
• completed approximately 98 percent of the 200MW Amandi Power Project and is currently at the last phase of commissioning;
<<<P>>>
• Connected a total of 388 out of 560 communities to the national grid, increasing the National Electricity Access rate from 85.0 percent in January 2020 to 85.2 percent at end-December 2020, with over 10,000 communities so far connected to the national grid Under the Rural Electrification Programme; and
<<<P>>>
• distributed 1,500 LPG cook stoves under the Rural LPG Promotion Programme.
<<<P>>>
30. In 2021, Government will continue to monitor and facilitate the implementation of the following projects:
<<<P>>>
• complete Phase 1B of the Early Power Project that will bring the installed capacity to 202MW;
<<<P>>>
• continue the ongoing PPA Renegotiations;
<<<P>>>
• implement the Pwalugu Multipurpose Dam;
<<<P>>>
• repair /replace T3 Gas Turbines;
<<<P>>>
• complete Lot1 (Kumasi-Kintampo component) of the Kumasi-Bolgatanga Transmission Line Project;
<<<P>>>
• upgrade 161kV Volta-Achimota-Mallam Transmission Line Project; and
<<<P>>>
• complete the 330kV A4BSP (Pokuase Bulk Supply Point).
