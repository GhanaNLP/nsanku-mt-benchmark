13 Yohanes ku kutri vevie ɖe gbefã na ame geɖe ale si nu wòate ŋui be Yesue nye Mesia la, ame si dzi Yehowa ato ana amefomea nakpɔ ɖeɖe. Nya nyui sia nye nya si wòle be amewo katã nase. Nu ka tae? Elabe Zakariya gblɔe ɖi be Mesia si gbɔna la anye “agudzedze,” si fia be eƒe vava anye nudzɔdzɔ ɖedzesi aɖe si ahe tɔtrɔ gãwo vɛ na amefome bliboa! Esia na wòdze ƒãa be ehiã be nya nyuia nakpe xexe bliboa ɖo; Yohanes ɖeka mate ŋu awɔ esia o. Ke ame kawo hãe aɖe gbefã xɔxɔ ƒe gbedeasi vevi sia?

14 Yesu ɖe gbefã nya nyuia eteƒe mekɔ o. Eya ŋue Zakariya ƒo nu tsoe esi wògblɔ ɖi be ana “kekeli ame siwo bɔbɔ nɔ viviti kple ku ƒe vɔvɔli me.” Nyateƒee, Yesu ƒe dɔme trɔ ɖe ame tsɛ siwo wote ɖe anyi la ŋu vevie. Ena kekeli klẽ na amewo esi wòɖe gbefã “Yehowa ƒe amenuvefe” la. (Xlẽ Yesaya 61:1, 2; Luka 4:16-21) Eɖe gbefã dzonɔamemetɔe be: “Dzifo-fiaɖuƒe la gogo.” Ke hã, enyae be ahiã be ame geɖe naɖe gbefã nya nyuia. Eya ta, ena hehe eƒe nusrɔ̃lawo be woawɔ esia.—Mat. 4:16, 17; 10:7; 28:19, 20.
