NU SIWO ŊU BIBLIA ƑO NU TSOE
<<<P>>>
Ati Si Nyɔna Kaba
<<<P>>>
Ati vovovo blaetɔ̃ siwo ŋu Biblia ƒo nu tsoe la dometɔ ɖekae nye yevuziti. Enye ati siwo ƒoa se kaba le vuvɔŋɔli megbe dometɔ ɖeka. Yevuziti ƒoa se le January ƒe nuwuwu lɔƒo. Eƒe sefofowo biãna vie alo funa ɣie henya kpɔna ale gbegbe. Esɔ ŋutɔ be le Hebrigbe mea, ŋkɔ si wotsɔ na yevuziti gɔmee nye “nyɔna kaba.” Nu kae ɖe dzesi le ati sia kple Ewɔla la ŋu?
<<<P>>>
Le Yeremiya 7:25 ƒe etenuŋɔŋlɔa mea, Yehowa gblɔ be yefɔna ŋdi kanya dɔa yefe nyagblɔɖilawo ɖe yefe amewo. March 15, 2011 ƒe Gbetakpɔxɔ gblɔ be: ‘Abe ale si ko yevuziti nyɔna kaba ene la, Yehowa anyɔ kaba le kpɔɖeŋunyagbɔgblɔ nu be yeaxlɔ̃ nu yefe amewo tso tomaɖomaɖo me tsonuwo ŋu. Eye madzudzɔ o—ayi edzi “anɔ ŋute”—va se ɖe esime eƒe nyagblɔɖinya la nava eme keŋkeŋ.’—Yer. 1:11, 12.
<<<P>>>
Biblia tsɔ yevuziti ƒe sefofowo hã sɔ kple ame tsitsiwo ƒe ta fowɔ. (Nyagb. 12:5) Anɔ eme be wowɔ sikakaɖiti siwo le gbedoxɔa me la ƒe kpluwo ɖe yevuziti ƒe sefofo ƒe nɔnɔme me, le ale si sefofoawo nya kpɔnae ta.—2 Mose 37:17-20.
<<<P>>>
Womele agbalẽ sia dzram o. Enye xexea me katã ƒe Biblia-fiafiadɔ si wotsɔa lɔlɔ̃nu faa nudzɔdzɔwo doa alɔe la ƒe akpa aɖe. Ne èdi be yeadzɔ nu la, míeɖe kuku, ʋu donate.jw.org.
