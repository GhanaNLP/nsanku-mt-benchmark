STÉPHANE LANDEROIN LA MANNE O ZEMMO YƐLƐ
<<<P>>>
SAŊA na maŋ da e baapaalaa na, N da ba nɔŋ sakuu gaabo fẽẽ zaa. Lɛ da vɛŋɛɛŋ ka N sakuu boma zannoo yɛlɛ da ba taa nimi-ri. N da maŋ boɔrɔŋ ka N erɛ yɛlɛ N yoŋ tɛgɛŋ, a ba boɔrɔ ka noba nyɛrɛ yɛlɛ maŋ erɛ na. Kyɛ, N da ba baŋ ka Naaŋmen kaa-rɛɛ yelzaa maŋ da erɛ na. Te nyɛ ananso na zuŋ maŋ yeli lɛ?
<<<P>>>
France paaloŋ teŋkpɛŋ na baŋ boɔlɔ Paris na baŋ guoli ma. N dɔgreba neŋ noba naŋ kpeɛrɛ peɛle te na ba sa-gedi ka Naaŋmen bebeŋ. N meŋ da taaɛ ana sagediibu ŋa. N da ba sagedi ka neɛ kaŋaŋ maale boma zaa, N da sagedieŋ ka boma meŋɛ la wa bebe a meŋɛŋ. N zannɛɛ a yɛlɛ ŋa sakuu poɔŋ, noba yaga sagɛɛ a yɛlɛ ŋa di azuŋ N meŋ da sagɛɛŋ ka a eɛ yelmeŋɛ. N da ba peɛre yelzaa a kyaare a yɛlɛ ŋa. Lɛ na baŋ maŋ wuli a yɛlɛ na da vɛ-ŋɛɛŋ maŋ nyɛ ka a eɛ yelmenne.
<<<P>>>
N dɔgreba da ba nɔŋ ŋmenpuore yɛlɛ. Bozuŋ ba da teɛreŋ ka ka Naaŋmeneŋ da bebe, o daaŋ koŋ kaa-ra ka yelfaare pɔgrɔ noba ka ba dire dɔgɛɛ lɛ. Ane meŋ, N da nyɛɛŋ ka bampuori-kombampoɔ yeltaree be la ŋmenpuore poɔŋ, azuŋ N da nyɛɛŋ ka yɛŋ ba kyebe ka neɛ sagedi ka Naaŋmen taaɛ nɔmmo. N da maŋ erɛɛ noba naŋ sagedi ka Naaŋmen bebeŋ na seɛ.
<<<P>>>
Pampana yɛŋ, N sagedieŋ ka Naaŋmeneŋ maale nen-saaleba a teɛrɛ N yɛlɛ meŋ, aneazaa ka saŋa kaŋa N maŋ nyɛŋ ka N ba seŋ neŋ ka o erɛ lɛ korɔ ma. Kyɛ, boŋ leɛre N teɛroŋ maŋ leɛ taa popeɛloŋ pampana? Yɛ vɛŋ ka N manne ko yɛ.
<<<P>>>
A paaloŋ bege wuleeŋ ka ka neɛŋ wa nyɛ yuomo 19 a eɛ ferebo ka ba koo wuluu a yi sogya toma poɔŋ. Azuŋ saŋa na maŋ da nyɛ yuomo 19 na, ba da ko maŋ wuluu, kyɛ de ma gaa neŋ South America teŋɛ kaŋa baŋ boɔlɔ French Guiana ka N te bebe yuoni gbuli. Ana saŋa na, N daaŋ ba teɛre ka ana zie ŋa na eɛ zie naŋ na leɛre N zemmo poɔ yɛlɛ.
<<<P>>>
N da leɛ gaaɛ Paris saŋa na maŋ nyɛ yuomo 20 na. N da maŋ toŋɛɛ toma a yi Monday te tɔ Friday. Ka N ne N zɔmenne maŋ gaa te pɛle te niŋe a nyu boma Saturday neŋ Sunday daare. Lɛ maŋ da erɛ wagre zaa. Kyɛ, N da ba taa popeɛloŋ meŋɛmeŋɛ. N da nyɛɛŋ ka N zemmo ba taa tɔnɔ zaa. N da nyɛɛŋ ka noba maŋ ŋmaarɛɛ zi-rii, a erɛ taa yɛlɛ naŋ ba seŋ neŋ, agaŋaazaa ka o sobaŋ wa yi ziyuo kaŋa bee ka o engane waaloŋ wa e o yoŋ. N da nyɛɛŋ meŋ ka noba mine eɛ bondaaneba ka ba mine meŋ e nandaaneba. Ka noba naŋ ba e yelfaa zaa
