16 Ofainɛ susumɔ wiemɔi komɛi ni woɔ mɔ hewalɛ waa ni yɔɔ broshuɔ ni ji Kuu Osɛɛ Kɛba Yehowa Ŋɔɔ lɛ mli lɛ he. Aŋma yɛ mli akɛ: “Ná nɔmimaa akɛ, Yehowa baaye abua bo ni onyɛ oku osɛɛ kɛba eŋɔɔ. Ebaaye ebua bo ni okafee yeyeeye, okɛ mɛi ni efee bo dɔlɛ nii lɛ ahe ake amɛ, ni ohenilee akayi bo dɔŋŋ. Kɛkɛ lɛ, no baatsirɛ bo ni obafata Yehowa webii lɛ ahe ekoŋŋ kɛsɔmɔ lɛ.” Kaimɔ hu akɛ asafoŋ onukpai lɛ miisumɔ waa ni amɛye amɛbua bo. Amɛbaanyɛ amɛfee “tamɔ teemɔhe be ni kɔɔyɔɔ miitswa, abobaahe be ni nugbɔ miinɛ.” (Yes. 32:2) Wɔle akɛ ohiɛ sɔɔ kpɔmɔnɔ lɛ, ni oosumɔ ni ofee nɔ ko koni Yehowa ana nakai. No hewɔ lɛ, bi ohe akɛ, ‘Mɛɛ nibii manyɛ mafee amrɔ nɛɛ koni mikɛ Yehowa abɔ naanyo ekoŋŋ?’ (Yes. 1:18; 1 Pet. 2:25) Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, obaanyɛ oba asafoŋ kpeei yɛ Maŋtsɛyeli Asa lɛ nɔ. Agbɛnɛ hu lɛ, obaanyɛ otsi obɛŋkɛ asafoŋ onukpai lɛ ateŋ mɔ kome ni eye ebua bo. Ni nibii ni ebaanyɛ efee kɛye ebua bo lɛ eko ji, ebaato gbɛjianɔ koni mɔ ko kɛbo akase nii kɛyashi be ko. Hemɔ oye waa akɛ, kɛ́ ofee nibii nɛɛ fɛɛ koni onyɛ oku osɛɛ oba Yehowa ŋɔɔ ekoŋŋ ni okɛtsɔɔ akɛ ohiɛ sɔɔ kpɔmɔnɔ lɛ, Yehowa baajɔɔ bo.

17 Yesu kɛɛ Yehowa kɛ kpɔmɔnɔ lɛ hã “koni mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni heɔ enɔ eyeɔ lɛ hiɛ akakpãtã shi moŋ ená naanɔ wala.” (Yoh. 3:16) Akɛni Yesu gbo ehã wɔ hewɔ lɛ, Yehowa baanyɛ ajie wɔ kɛje esha kɛ gbele mli. Esaaa akɛ wɔteŋ mɔ ko mɔ ko hiɛ kpaa enɛ nɔ kɔkɔɔkɔ. (Rom. 3:23, 24; 2 Kor. 6:1) No hewɔ lɛ, dani Kaimɔ lɛ baashɛ lɛ, hemɔ be ni okɛsusu suɔmɔ ni Yehowa kɛ Yesu jie lɛ kpo lɛ he. Ni enɛ baanyɛ tsirɛ bo ni ofee nɔ ko kɛtsɔɔ akɛ ohiɛ esɔ nɔ ni amɛfee lɛ.

18 Mɛni obaanyɛ ofee kɛtsɔɔ akɛ ohiɛ sɔɔ kpɔmɔnɔ lɛ? Ekolɛ ebaasoro nɔ ni obaafee lɛ yɛ mɔ kroko nɔ lɛ he. Shi ná ole akɛ, kɛ́ ofata Yehowa jálɔi akpekpei abɔ krokomɛi lɛ ahe ni nyɛhi shi akɛ mɛi ni ‘hiɛ kãmɔɔ kɛhãaa nyɛ diɛŋtsɛ nyɛhe dɔŋŋ, shi moŋ nyɛhiɛ kãmɔ kɛhã mɔ ni gbo kɛhã nyɛ lɛ,’ no lɛ Yehowa baajɔɔ bo yɛ omɔdɛŋbɔɔ lɛ hewɔ.—2 Kor. 5:15.
