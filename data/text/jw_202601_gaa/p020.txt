Wɔhiɛ Sɔɔ Kpɔmɔnɔ Lɛ Waa
<<<P>>>
NƆ NI WƆBAAKASE
<<<P>>>
Nibii ni wɔteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ baanyɛ afee kɛtsɔɔ akɛ ehiɛ sɔɔ kpɔmɔnɔ lɛ.
<<<P>>>
Mɛni Obaafee Kɛtsɔɔ Akɛ Ohiɛ Sɔɔ Kpɔmɔnɔ Lɛ?
<<<P>>>
“Suɔmɔ ni Kristo lɛ yɔɔ lɛ nyeɔ wɔnɔ.”—2 KOR. 5:14.
<<<P>>>
ŊƆƆ lɛ akɛ tsũ ko kumɔ eshwie onɔ ni mɔ ko bajie bo. Obaanu he akɛ ohiɛ mɔ lɛ owala he nyɔmɔ, aloo jeee nakai? Kɛjeee bo pɛ po ajie lɛ, obaasumɔ ni bo diɛŋtsɛ oya mɔ lɛ ŋɔɔ ni oyafee nɔ ko kɛtsɔɔ akɛ ohiɛ esɔ jiemɔ ni ebajie bo lɛ waa, ni akɛ, ohiɛ kpaŋ nɔ kɔkɔɔkɔ.
<<<P>>>
² Nikasemɔ ni tsɔ hiɛ lɛ hã wɔna akɛ wɔ diɛŋtsɛ wɔnyɛŋ wɔjie wɔhe kɛje esha ni afɔ wɔ yɛ mli lɛ mli. Shi naa nɔ ni Yehowa fee. Ekɛ e-Bi lɛ hã akɛ kpɔmɔnɔ koni (1) enyɛ ekɛ wɔhe eshai lɛ ake wɔ, (2) wɔsɛɛ lɛ, ejie wɔhe eshai lɛ kɛje wɔhe, ni (3) wɔnyɛ wɔkɛ Yehowa abɔ naanyo. Kpɔmɔnɔ lɛ hewɔ lɛ, wɔyɛ hiɛnɔkamɔ akɛ wɔsɛɛ lɛ, wɔbaahi shi kɛya naanɔ yɛ Yehowa jeŋ hee lɛ mli. Yesu wala ni ekɛfɔ̃ shi ehã wɔ lɛ tsɔɔ akɛ esumɔɔ adesai waa. Shi jeee be ni eba shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ lɛ pɛ ebatsɔɔ akɛ esumɔɔ adesai, moŋ lɛ, be ni eyɔɔ ŋwɛi ni ebako shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ po lɛ, esumɔɔ adesai. (Abɛi 8:30, 31) Bɔfo Paulo ŋma akɛ, “suɔmɔ ni Kristo lɛ yɔɔ lɛ nyeɔ wɔnɔ.” (Kanemɔ 2 Korintobii 5:14, 15.) Enɛ tsɔɔ akɛ esa akɛ wɔfee nɔ ko kɛtsɔɔ akɛ wɔhiɛ sɔɔ suɔmɔ ni Yesu jie lɛ kpo lɛ, ni akɛ, wɔhiɛ kpaŋ nɔ kɔkɔɔkɔ.
<<<P>>>
³ Mɛni obaanyɛ ofee kɛtsɔɔ akɛ ohiɛ sɔɔ kpɔmɔnɔ lɛ? Ekolɛ esoro nɔ ni obaanyɛ ofee lɛ yɛ mɔ kroko nɔ lɛ he. Mɛni hewɔ wɔkɛɔ nakai? Ŋɔɔ lɛ akɛ mɛi etɛ komɛi yaafã gbɛ kɛya he kome too lɛ nɔŋŋ, shi esoro maŋ ni amɛteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ jɛɔ mli. No hewɔ lɛ, ebaasoro gbɛ ni amɛteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ baatsɔ nɔ kɛya he.
<<<P>>>
1-2. Mɛni esa akɛ Yesu kpɔmɔ afɔle lɛ anyɛ wɔnɔ ni wɔfee? Ni mɛni hewɔ? (2 Korintobii 5:14, 15) (Kwɛmɔ mfoniri lɛ hu.)
<<<P>>>
