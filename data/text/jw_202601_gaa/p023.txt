ekudɔ bɔ ni wɔbaa wɔjeŋ wɔhãa lɛ. Mɛni baahã eba lɛ nakai? Sane ni Yesu kɛmaje Kristofoi ni hi shi yɛ Efeso yɛ Yesu bɔfoi lɛ abeiaŋ lɛ baanyɛ ahã wɔná sanebimɔ nɛɛ hetoo. Kristofoi ni yɔɔ Efeso lɛ fi shi waa ni Yesu jie amɛyi yɛ no hewɔ. Shi ekɛɛ amɛ hu akɛ: “Sane ni miyɔɔ yɛ ohe nɛ, no ji akɛ oŋmɛɛ oklɛŋklɛŋ suɔmɔ lɛ he.” (Kpo. 2:3, 4) Yesu wiemɔi nɛɛ tsɔɔ akɛ kɛ́ bei shwie mli lɛ, ebaanyɛ eba lɛ akɛ Kristofonyo ko kɛ etsui fɛɛ jáŋ Nyɔŋmɔ. Mɔ lɛ baanyɛ afee mɔ ko ni sɔleɔ, eyaa asafoŋ kpeei, ni eyaa shiɛmɔ hu, shi efeɔ nibii nɛɛ kɛkɛ shi jeee kɛ etsui fɛɛ. Kɛ́ wɔkɛɛ lɛ, jeee suɔmɔ ni kudɔɔ lɛ dɔŋŋ. No hewɔ lɛ, kɛ́ oonu he akɛ suɔmɔ ni oyɔɔ kɛhã Yehowa lɛ mli waaa tamɔ tsutsu lɛ, mɛni obaanyɛ ofee?

10 Bɔfo Paulo kɛɛ Timoteo ni ejwɛŋ nibii ni efeɔ kɛjáa Yehowa lɛ anɔ ni ‘ekɛ ehe awo mli kwraa.’ (Kanemɔ 1 Timoteo 4:13, 15.) Bo hu obaanyɛ ojwɛŋ nibii ni ofeɔ kɛjáa Yehowa lɛ anɔ, ni okwɛ bɔ ni obaafee ni ‘ofee ekãa yɛ mumɔ lɛ naa.’ (Rom. 12:11) Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, obaanyɛ okɛ be ni fa fe bɔ ni tsutsu ko lɛ okɛsaa ohe kɛhãa asafoŋ kpeei lɛ asaa ohe. Kɛ́ ofee nakai lɛ, kɛ́ otee asafoŋ kpee lɛ, ojwɛŋmɔ baahi nɔ ni yaa nɔ lɛ nɔ jogbaŋŋ. Loo kɛ́ oofee aŋkro nikasemɔ lɛ, obaanyɛ otao he ko ni yɔɔ dioo ni baawa bo koni onyɛ ojwɛŋ nɔ ni okaseɔ lɛ nɔ jogbaŋŋ. Tamɔ bɔ ni akɛ lai woɔ la mli koni eya nɔ etso lɛ, nakai nɔŋŋ esa akɛ oya nɔ ofee nibii nɛɛ koni be fɛɛ be lɛ, ohiɛ asɔ kpɔmɔnɔ lɛ kɛ nibii krokomɛi fɛɛ ni Yehowa feɔ ehãa bo lɛ. Agbɛnɛ hu, obaanyɛ ojwɛŋ nibii kpakpai ni Yehowa efee ehã bo akɛ Yehowa Odasefonyo lɛ nɔ dani Kaimɔ lɛ ashɛ. Ŋwanejee ko bɛ he akɛ kɛ́ ofee nakai lɛ, ohiɛ baasɔ kpɔmɔnɔ lɛ waa, ejaakɛ no hewɔ ni onyɛ okɛ Yehowa miibɔ naanyo lɛ.

11 Kɛ́ eshɛ he ko ni oonu he akɛ ojáaa Yehowa kɛ ekãa tamɔ tsutsu lɛ, no etsɔɔɔ akɛ Yehowa mumɔ lɛ bɛ onɔ. Kaimɔ nɔ ni bɔfo Paulo kɛɛ Korintobii lɛ yɛ esɔɔmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ he. Ekɛɛ amɛ akɛ: “Kɛ́ mikɛ mligbigbilimɔ fee lɛ, no hu po lɛ, eji nitsumɔ ni atu awo midɛŋ akɛ shĩa nɔkwɛlɔ.” (1 Kor. 9:17) Mɛni nɔ ni ewie lɛ tsɔɔ?
<<<P>>>
12 Bei komɛi lɛ, Paulo nuuu he akɛ eshiɛ. Fɛɛ sɛɛ lɛ, etswa efai shi akɛ ebaaya nɔ eshiɛ ekɔɔɔ he eko bɔ ni enuɔ he ehãa. Bo hu obaanyɛ ofee nakai. Tswaa ofai shi akɛ obaafee nɔ ni ja kɛ́ bei komɛi lɛ onuuu he akɛ ofee nakai po. No hewɔ lɛ, sɔlemɔ obi Yehowa ni ewa bo ni oshwe akɛ obaafee nɔ ni ja, ni ehã bo hewalɛ hu koni onyɛ ofee nakai. (Fip. 2:13) Yaa nɔ ofee nibii ni baahã okɛ Yehowa abɔ naanyo waa. Kɛ́ ofee nakai lɛ, fiofio lɛ, suɔmɔ ni oyɔɔ kɛhã Yehowa lɛ mli baawa ekoŋŋ, ni obaajá lɛ kɛ ekãa.
