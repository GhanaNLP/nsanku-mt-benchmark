13 Awo Kristofoi ŋaa yɛ 2 Korintobii 13:5 lɛ akɛ: “Nyɛyaa nɔ nyɛpɛia nyɛmli nyɛkwɛa akɛ nyɛyɛ hemɔkɛyeli lɛ mli lo.” No hewɔ lɛ, yɛ be kɛ beiaŋ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔbi wɔhe saji tamɔ: ‘Kɛ́ mijwɛŋ bɔ ni mihiɔ shi mihãa lɛ he lɛ, ani manyɛ makɛɛ akɛ Yehowa sɔɔmɔ lɛ he hiaa mi fe nɔ fɛɛ nɔ?’ (Mat. 6:33) ‘Ani nibii ni mikɛjieɔ mihiɛtserɛ lɛ tsɔɔ akɛ minyɛɔ efɔŋ?’ (Lala 97:10) ‘Ani minifeemɔi hãa toiŋjɔlɛ kɛ ekomefeemɔ hiɔ minyɛmimɛi Kristofoi lɛ ateŋ?’ (Efe. 4:2, 3) Kaimɔ be lɛ ji be ni hi jogbaŋŋ ni obaanyɛ okɛsusu saji nɛɛ ahe. Kɛ́ wɔsusu nɔ ni Yehowa kɛ Yesu feɔ amɛhãa wɔ lɛ he lɛ, no baatsirɛ wɔ ni wɔfee nɔ ko kɛda Yehowa shi, ni agbɛnɛ hu, wɔkɛtsɔɔ akɛ wɔhiɔ shi wɔhãa Kristo shi jeee wɔ diɛŋtsɛ wɔhe.*

14 Wɔnyɛmimɛi hii kɛ yei komɛi ekpa asafoŋ kpeeibaa loo amɛkɛ asafo lɛ ekpa bɔɔ. Mɛi nɛɛ ateŋ mɛi komɛi sɔmɔ Yehowa jogbaŋŋ afii pii dani amɛkpa. Mɛni hewɔ eba lɛ nakai? Amɛteŋ mɛi komɛi lɛ, shihilɛ lɛ mli naagbai lɛ su amɛtsui ni no hã amɛnu he akɛ amɛnyɛŋ amɛsɔmɔ Yehowa dɔŋŋ. (Luka 21:34) Amɛteŋ mɛi komɛi hu lɛ, nɔ ko ni nyɛmi ko fee loo ewie lɛ gba amɛnaa. (Yak. 3:2) Ni amɛteŋ mɛi krokomɛi hu lɛ, amɛfee esha ko ni yɔɔ hiɛdɔɔ ni amɛsumɔɔ ni aye abua amɛ. Ekɔɔɔ he eko nɔ ni hã okpa asafoŋ kpeeibaa lɛ, mɛni obaanyɛ ofee? Mɛni kpɔmɔnɔ lɛ kɛ suɔmɔ ni Yehowa kɛ Yesu yɔɔ kɛhã bo lɛ baanyɛ anyɛ onɔ ni ofee?
<<<P>>>
15 Susumɔ sane nɛɛ he okwɛ. Kɛ́ okwɛ lɛ, te Yehowa nuɔ he ehãa tɛŋŋ yɛ mɛi ni ekpa asafoŋ kpeeibaa lɛ ahe? Anɔkwa sane lɛ ji, emli fuko amɛ ni ehiɛ kpako amɛnɔ hu. Etaoɔ etoi ni elaaje nɛɛ. Ni kɛ́ ena amɛ lɛ, eyeɔ ebuaa amɛ koni amɛku amɛsɛɛ kɛba etooku lɛ mli, ni elɛɔ amɛ hu. (Kanemɔ Ezekiel 34:11, 12, 16.) Ani ekolɛ Yehowa miifee nakai yɛ ogbɛfaŋ? Hɛɛ! Akɛni ookane sane nɛɛ hewɔ lɛ, etsɔɔ akɛ loloolo lɛ oosumɔ ni osa Yehowa hiɛ. Tamɔ bɔ ni Yehowa kwɛ otsui mli ni egbala bo kɛba anɔkwale lɛ mli lɛ, eeenyɛ efee akɛ amrɔ nɛɛ eegbala bo koni oku osɛɛ kɛba.

* Obaanyɛ okane sane ni ji “Mɛni Kanyaa Bo Koni Osɔmɔ Nyɔŋmɔ?” ni yɔɔ June 15, 1995, Buu-Mɔɔ lɛ mli lɛ, koni ona nibii ni obaanyɛ ofee kɛtsɔɔ akɛ ohiɛ sɔɔ nibii ni Yehowa feɔ ehãa bo lɛ.
