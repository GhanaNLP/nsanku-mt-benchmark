Ehãa David naa faŋŋ akɛ, esumɔɔ lɛ ni eshiŋ lɛ kɔkɔɔkɔ, ni enɛ yeɔ ebuaa lɛ koni ekahao fe nine.—Lala 23:1-6.

17 Wɔ hu kɛ́ wɔyaakpɛ yiŋ yɛ sane ko he ni wɔhã Yehowa tsɔɔ wɔ gbɛ lɛ, no tsɔɔ akɛ wɔmiikase David. Agbɛnɛ hu, esa akɛ wɔhã ehi wɔjwɛŋmɔ mli akɛ, kɛ́ wɔgbo Yehowa nɔ toi lɛ, ebuŋ wɔhe kɛjeŋ naagbai ni baanyɛ ajɛ nakai toigbele lɛ mli kɛba lɛ ahe. (Gal. 6:7, 8) Tamɔ eba lɛ yɛ David gbɛfaŋ lɛ, wɔ hu wɔkɛ naagbai baakpe ni jeee ni wɔgbo Yehowa nɔ toi loo wɔfee nɔ ko ni esumɔɔ. Kɛ́ eba lɛ nakai lɛ, esa akɛ wɔjɛ wɔtsuiŋ wɔsɔle waa wɔhã Yehowa. Kɛ́ wɔfee nakai lɛ, Yehowa baashɛje wɔmii ni ebaahã wɔna akɛ eshiko wɔ.—Fip. 4:6, 7.

YAA NƆ OTSƆƆ AKƐ OLE AKƐ NYƆŊMƆ HE MIIHIA BO
<<<P>>>
18 Ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, mɛi pii nuɔ he akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ kɛ egbɛtsɔɔmɔi lɛ ahe ehiaaa amɛ loo amɛjáa lɛ bɔ ni amɛ diɛŋtsɛ amɛsumɔɔ loo amɛkɛ amɛhiɛ fɔ̃ɔ adesai anɔ, ni enɛ hewɔ lɛ amɛbɛ miishɛɛ. Esa akɛ wɔkwɛ jogbaŋŋ ni wɔkakase amɛ. Moŋ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔbo Yesu wiemɔi ni ahala akɛ afi 2026 afi ŋmalɛ lɛ toi, akɛ: “Mɛi ni le akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ he miihia amɛ lɛ yɛ miishɛɛ.” Te wɔbaafee tɛŋŋ wɔfee nakai? Esa akɛ wɔye niyenii ni Yehowa ehoo ehã wɔ lɛ kɛtsɔ e-Wiemɔ lɛ ni wɔbaakase lɛ nɔ, ni wɔwo gbɔmɔ hee lɛ, ni ji sui kpakpai ni etsɔɔ wɔ lɛ, ni wɔbo lɛ toi daa koni wɔhi eteemɔhe lɛ be fɛɛ be.

18. Ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, te mɛi pii feɔ amɛnii amɛhãa tɛŋŋ? Ni mɛni wɔbaanyɛ wɔfee kɛtsɔɔ akɛ wɔle akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ he miihia wɔ? (Kwɛmɔ mfonirii lɛ hu.)

NƆ NI WƆNAA YƐ MFONIRII LƐ AMLI Wolo lɛ hiɛ: Nyɛmi yoo ko miisumɔ ni Yehowa abu ehe, no hewɔ lɛ, etee asafoŋ kpee ni ekɛ enyɛmimɛi lɛ efee ekome kɛmiikase Yehowa he nii. Baafa 6: Nyɛmi yoo lɛ miikase Nyɔŋmɔ Wiemɔ lɛ, ewo gbɔmɔ hee lɛ, ni eyaba abo yɛ Yehowa teemɔhe lɛ. Nɔ ni hãa wɔnaa nakai ji, eekase Buu-Mɔɔ lɛ, eefee mɛi ejurɔ, ni eŋmɛ gbɛ ni asafoŋ onukpai lɛ miiwo lɛ hewalɛ.
