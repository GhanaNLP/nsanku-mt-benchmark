KƐ́ OJI BIBLIA MLI NIKASELƆ LƐ, MƐNI OBAANYƐ OFEE?
<<<P>>>
4 Kɛ́ akɛ bo miikase Biblia lɛ, susumɔ sane nɛɛ he okwɛ: Oosumɔ ni okase Yehowa he nii kɛ Biblia lɛ mli anɔkwalei lɛ. Enɛ tsɔɔ akɛ Yehowa miisumɔ ni obatsɔ enaanyo ni eewa bo koni onyɛ ofee nakai. (Yoh. 6:44; Bɔf. 13:48) “Yehowa ji mɔ ni taoɔ tsuii amli.” Ni no tsɔɔ akɛ enaa mɔdɛŋ ni obɔɔ koni okase ehe nii lɛ, ni kɛ́ ena akɛ oofee tsakemɔi koni ofee esuɔmɔnaa nii lɛ, enáa miishɛɛ. (Abɛi 17:3; 27:11) Kpɔmɔnɔ lɛ hewɔ lɛ, obaanyɛ okɛ Yehowa abɔ naanyo waa. (Rom. 5:10, 11) Kaahã ohiɛ kpa anɔkwa sane nɛɛ nɔ kɔkɔɔkɔ.

4. Te Yehowa naa Biblia mli nikaselɔi ehãa tɛŋŋ?
<<<P>>>
5 Kɛ́ oji Biblia mli nikaselɔ lɛ, mɛni obaanyɛ ofee kɛtsɔɔ akɛ ohiɛ sɔɔ kpɔmɔnɔ lɛ? Nɔ kome ni obaanyɛ ofee ji ni okɛ ŋaa ni bɔfo Paulo wo Filipibii lɛ baatsu nii. Ekɛɛ amɛ akɛ: “He ni wɔtee hiɛ kɛshɛ lɛ, nyɛhãa wɔyaa nɔ wɔnyiɛa jogbaŋŋ yɛ nɛkɛ gbɛ nɛɛ nɔŋŋ nɔ.” (Fip. 3:16) Atsɔɔ ŋmalɛ nɛɛ shishi yɛ Biblia kroko ko mli akɛ: “Esa akɛ wɔya nɔ wɔnyiɛ gbɛ ni wɔnyiɛ nɔ amrɔ nɛɛ nɔŋŋ nɔ; esaaa akɛ wɔkuɔ wɔsɛɛ.” No hewɔ lɛ, kaahã nɔ ko loo mɔ ko hã okpa wala gbɛ ni onyiɛ nɔ lɛ nɔ nyiɛmɔ.—Mat. 7:14; Luka 9:62.
