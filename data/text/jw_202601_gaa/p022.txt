Yehowa biŋ wɔ kɔkɔɔkɔ ni wɔfee nɔ ko ni wɔnyɛŋ wɔfee. Ebaaye ebua wɔ koni wɔnyɛ wɔfee esuɔmɔnaa nii
<<<P>>>
(Kwemɔ kuku 6)
<<<P>>>
hewɔ lɛ, obaanyɛ ofee esuɔmɔnaa nii. (Kanemɔ 5 Mose 30:11-14.) Yehowa ewo shi akɛ ebaawa bo. (Yes. 41:10, 13; 1 Kor. 10:13) No hewɔ lɛ, onijiaŋ akaje wui. Okɛ ojwɛŋmɔ akama nɔ ni amrɔ nɛɛ ewa kɛhã bo lɛ nɔ tsɔ, moŋ lɛ, susumɔ nibii kpakpai fɛɛ ni Yehowa feɔ ehãa bo lɛ he, titri lɛ, e-Bi lɛ ni etsu koni ebagbo ehã bo lɛ. Be ni suɔmɔ ni oyɔɔ kɛhã Yehowa lɛ daa lɛ, obaana akɛ “ekitãi lɛ anɔyeli waaa.”—1 Yoh. 5:3.*

7 Ni kɛ́ oji oblanyo loo oblayoo ni fɔlɔi miitsɔse bo yɛ anɔkwale lɛ mli lɛ, ani sane nɛɛ kɔɔ ohe? Hɛɛ, ekɔɔ ohe ejaakɛ ofɔlɔi kɛ bo miikase Biblia lɛ. Yɛ anɔkwale mli lɛ, oji ofɔlɔi a-Biblia mli nikaselɔ ni he hiaa waa. Biblia lɛ kɛɛ: “Nyɛtsia nyɛbɛŋkɛa Nyɔŋmɔ, ni ebaatsi ebɛŋkɛ nyɛ.” (Yak. 4:8; 1 Kro. 28:9) Kɛ́ ofee nibii ni baahã okɛ Yehowa abɔ naanyo waa lɛ, lɛ hu ebaafee nibii ni baahã ekɛ bo abɔ naanyo waa. Yehowa enaaa bo akɛ mɔ ko ni aatsɔse lɛ yɛ anɔkwale lɛ mli kɛkɛ. Esusuɔ ohe ejaakɛ eesumɔ ni okɛ lɛ abɔ naanyo waa. Ni mɛni Yehowa efee koni onyɛ okɛ lɛ abɔ naanyo waa? Ekɛ e-Bi lɛ hã yɛ ohewɔ. Ohiɛ akakpa nikeenii ni nɔ bɛ nɛɛ nɔ kɔkɔɔkɔ! (Rom. 5:1, 2) No hewɔ lɛ, dani Kaimɔ lɛ baashɛ lɛ, susumɔ nɔ ni Yehowa kɛ Yesu fee amɛh
