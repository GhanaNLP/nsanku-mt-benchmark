7 Ke wa bu tue saminya ngɛ asafo mi kpe-hi a sisi ɔ, lɔ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa wa suɔ wami. Kaa bɔ nɛ Yehowa de e sɔmɔli nɛ a hi si blema a, ja wa bu lɛ tue loko wa ma ná bua jɔmi amlɔ nɛ ɔ, nɛ wa ná neneene wami hwɔɔ se. (5 Mose 32:44-47) Ke wa bu tue ngɛ wa kpe ɔmɛ a sisi ɔ, wa náa he wami womi, nɛ wa kaseɔ bɔ nɛ wa ma plɛ kɛ da wa nyagba amɛ a nya ha konɛ wa nyɛ nɛ waa to wa tsui si kɛ mlɛ je ehe ɔ. (Rom. 8:25) Jehanɛ hu ɔ, akɛnɛ wa ngɛ nyagbe li-gbi ɔmɛ a mi he je ɔ, amlɔ nɛ ɔ ji be nɛ e sa kaa waa bu Yehowa tue saminya konɛ wa nyɛ nɛ waa ya nɔ nɛ waa hwɔɔ.—Mat. 24:42-44; Kpo. 1:3.

BƆ NƐ WA MA PLƐ KƐ YE BUA NI KPAHI HA KONƐ A BU TUE SAMINYA
<<<P>>>
8 E sa nɛ nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi nɛ a tsɔɔ ní ngɛ kpoku ɔ nɔ ɔ nɛ a ha nɛ a munyu ɔ nɛ nu tso sa-minya, titli nihi nɛ a tuɔ magbɛ nɔ munyu ɔ. Ke nyɛminyumu ko ná be kɛ dla e he saminya, nɛ e ngɔ otihi nɛ a ngɛ e munyu ɔ mi ɔ kɛ da Bai-blo ɔ nɔ ɔ, lɔ ɔ maa ye bua nihi nɛ tsa pi Oda-sefohi ji mɛ ɔ konɛ a kase anɔkuale ko nɛ he hia ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, lɔ ɔ maa ye bua nihi nɛ a ji Odasefohi ɔ konɛ a nu anɔkualehi nɛ ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi ɔ sisi saminya konɛ a kɛ tsu ní. Ke o ngɛ ní peemi ko ngɛ asafo mi kpe sisi, nɛ o bɔ mɔde nɛ o tsu he ní saminya a, e ma nyɛ maa ye bua ni kpahi wawɛɛ nɛ́ mo po o be lee. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, mo susu bɔ nɛ ga womihi nɛ nyɛɛ se nɛ ɔ maa ye bua mo ha konɛ o pee tsɔɔlɔ kpakpa a he. E ngɛ mi kaa ga womi nɛ ɔmɛ kɔɔ nihi nɛ a tuɔ magbɛ nɔ munyuhi a he titli mohu lɛɛ, se e ma nyɛ maa ye bua nyɛ-mimɛ tsuo nɛ a tsuɔ ní peemi kpahi a he ní ngɛ asafo mi kpehi a sisi ɔ hulɔ.—2 Tim. 4:2; Tito 1:9.
<<<P>>>
9 Moo to o munyu ɔ nya saminya konɛ oti ɔmɛ nɛ a je kpo heii. Ke o ngɛ ní tsɔɔe ɔ, moo to o munyu ɔ nya saminya konɛ o tue buli ɔmɛ nɛ a nyɛ nɛ a nyɛɛ o munyu ɔ se. Ke o munyu ɔ pee huanyaii ɔ, o tue buli ɔmɛ be nó nɛ o ngɛ dee ɔ sisi nue. Ke jã a, lɛɛ mɛni blɔ nɔ o maa gu kɛ to o munyu ɔ nya saminya? Ní komɛ nɛ maa ye bua mo ji nɛ ɔ nɛ: (1) Ke o je o munyu ɔ sisi pɛ ɔ, ha nɛ o tue buli ɔmɛ nɛ a le otihi nɛ o ma he munyu tumi ɔ. (2) Moo tsɔɔ oti ɔmɛ a mi kakaaka. Ke o tsɔɔ oti ko mi ta a, moo mlɛ bɔɔ loko o tu oti kpa he munyu konɛ tue buli ɔmɛ nɛ a nyɛ nɛ a nyɛɛ o munyu ɔ se. (3) Ke o yaa gbe o munyu ɔ nya a, moo tĩ oti ɔmɛ a mi ekohu.
<<<P>>>
10 O bua nɛ jɔ nɔ́ nɛ o ma tsɔɔmi ɔ he. Ke o bua jɔ otihi nɛ o ma a he munyu tumi ɔ he ɔ, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ o kɛ gɛjɛmi maa tu munyu, nɛ e ma ná o tue buli ɔmɛ a nɔ he wami. (Abɛ 2:4, 5; Níts. 4:20) Ke o susu ngmami ɔmɛ kɛ oti-hi nɛ ngɛ o munyu ɔ mi ɔ he saminya a, lɔ ɔ maa ye bua mo konɛ o bua nɛ jɔ oti ɔmɛ a he. Nyɛ-minyumu ko nɛ e ngɛ United States nɛ e ye jeha 70 kɛ se ɔ hɛ ji munyu ko nɛ a tu be nɛ e yi jeha 20 lolo ɔ nɔ. E de ke: “Loloolo ɔ, i kaiɔ bɔ nɛ munyu tulɔ ɔ wo wɔ he wami ha kaa ke wa kane Mawu Munyu ɔ, wa susu he kɛ ya tsitsaa a he. E tu munyu ɔ kɛ nó mi mami, nɛ e tsɔɔ wɔ blɔ nɔ nɛ wa maa gu kɛ pee jã a heii. Jamɛ a munyu ɔ tsake ye si himi wawɛɛ. E ye bua mi nɛ ye Baiblo kanemi ɔ nu tso, nɛ huɛ bɔmi kpakpa ba ngɛ i kɛ Yehowa wa kpɛti.”
