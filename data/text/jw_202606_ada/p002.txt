OTI NƐ NGƐ NÍ KASEMI Ɔ MI
<<<P>>>
Wa ma nyɛ maa ya nɔ maa ye Yehowa anɔkuale ngɛ kahi nɛ waa kɛ maa kpe ɔ tsuo se.
<<<P>>>
Nɔ́ Nɛ Maa Ye Bua Wɔ Nɛ Waa Ye Yehowa Anɔkuale Kɛ Waa Kɛ Kahi Ngɛ Kpee
<<<P>>>
“Nyɔmtsɛ, mɛnɔ ngɔ lɛ wa maa je kɛ ya? Mo lɛ o ngɛ neneene wami munyu ɔmɛ ɔ nɛ.”—YOH. 6:68.
<<<P>>>
LIGBI KO Ɔ, Yesu ngɛ ni komɛ ní tsɔɔe ngɛ Kapernaum, nɛ e de mɛ ke: “Ke nyɛ yi nɔmlɔ Bi ɔ he lo ɔ nɛ nyɛɛ nu e muɔ ɔ, nyɛ be wami ngɛ nyɛ nitsɛmɛ nyɛ mi.” E de hu ke: “Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ nɛ e yeɔ ye he lo ɔ nɛ e nuɔ ye muɔ ɔ ngɛ neneene wami, nɛ ma tle lɛ si ngɛ nyagbe ligbi ɔ nɔ.” (Yoh. 6:53, 54) E se nyɛɛli ɔmɛ a kpɛti nihi hiɛhiɛɛ a bua jɔɛ nɔ́ nɛ e de ɔ he, nɛ a de ke: “Pi munyu nɛ a nuɔ kɛ tue ji nɛ ɔ; mɛnɔ ma nyɛ maa bu tue?”—Yoh. 6:60.
<<<P>>>
2 Kaseli ɔmɛ a kpɛti nihi fuu nyɛɛ we Yesu se hu, ejakaa nɔ́ nɛ e de ɔ hao mɛ wawɛɛ. Anɛ enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa a be hemi kɛ yemi ko kulaa ngɛ Yesu mi lo? Dɛbi. Ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ, loko Yesu maa tu munyu nɛ ɔ, e pee nyakpɛ nɔ́ ko, nɛ e se nyɛɛli ɔmɛ a bua jɔ wawɛɛ. Lɔ ɔ ha nɛ a de ke: “Niinɛ, Gbalɔ ɔ nɛ e maa ba je ɔ mi ɔ ji nɛ ɔ nɛ.” (Yoh. 6:14) Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa e se nyɛɛli ɔ nɛ a kua lɛ ɔ ngɛ hemi kɛ yemi ko kẽ ngɛ e mi. Se benɛ Yesu tu munyu nɛ ɔ nɛ e pee mɛ nyakpɛ ɔ, a nyɛ we nɛ a ya nɔ nɛ a nyɛɛ e se.
<<<P>>>
