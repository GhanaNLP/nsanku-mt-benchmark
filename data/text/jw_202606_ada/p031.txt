SANE BIMIHI NƐ JE NÍ KANELI A NGƆ
<<<P>>>
Mɛni somi enyɔ lɛ bɔfo Paulo ngɛ he munyu tue ngɛ Galatiabi 4:24 ɔ?
<<<P>>>
■ Benɛ bɔfo Paulo tu tsakpa nɛ ngɛ Abraham kɛ Sara kɛ Haga a kpɛti ɔ he munyu se ɔ, e de ke: “A ma nyɛ maa ngɔ ní nɛ ɔmɛ kaa nɔ́ he tomi níhi; ejakaa yi nɛ ɔmɛ daa si kɛ ha somi enyɔ.” (Gal. 4:22-24) Be ko nɛ be ɔ, wa tsɔɔ kaa somi enyɔ nɛ ɔmɛ ji Mlaa somi ɔ kɛ somi ehe ɔ nɛ. Se benɛ wa kpale hla níhi a mi ngɛ ngmami nɛ ɔ he ɔ, wa ba na kaa e sa nɛ waa pee tsakemi ngɛ bɔ nɛ wa nu ngmami nɛ ɔ sisi ha a he. E ma nyɛ maa ba kaa pi somi ehe ɔ he munyu nɛ Paulo ngɛ tue, mohu ɔ, e ngɛ somi nɛ Mawu kɛ Abraham so ɔ he munyu tue. Mɛni he je nɛ wa de jã a?*
<<<P>>>
Paulo ngɔ Mlaa somi ɔ nɛ a kɛ ha ngɛ Sinai Yoku ɔ nɔ ngɛ jeha 1513 loko mwɔnɛ ɔ be kanemi ɔ kɛ to Haga he. (2 Mose 19:5, 6) Yudabi ɔmɛ tsuo yi mluku, lɔ ɔ he ɔ, a nyɛ we nɛ a ye mlaa a tsuo nɔ kɛ pi si. Enɛ ɔ ha nɛ a na kaa a ji nyɔguɛ kɛ ha yayami. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, Mlaa somi ɔ maa ye bua Israelbi ɔmɛ konɛ a le Mesia a mi tso, ejakaa lɛ pɛ lɛ e ma nyɛ maa ye Mlaa a nɔ kɛ pi si saminya. Benɛ Mesia a ngɔ e wami nɛ ye mluku ɔ kɛ sã afɔle se ɔ, lɔ ɔ bli blɔ kɛ ha adesahi tsuo nɛ yayami ngɛ a he ɔ konɛ a nyɛ nɛ a yu a he ngɛ yayami kɛ gbenɔ he. (Gal. 3:19, 24, 25) Akɛnɛ Yesu tsu Mawu yi mi tomi ɔ he ní ta he je ɔ, Mlaa a he hia we hu.—Rom. 10:4.
<<<P>>>
Paulo tu slɔɔto nɛ ngɛ Haga kɛ Sara nɛ ji “yo nɛ e ye e he,” nɛ e fɔ kɛ “gu si womi nɔ” ɔ he munyu. (Gal. 4:23) Jamɛ a si womi ɔ ji somi nɛ Yehowa kɛ Abraham so ɔ nɛ. (Gal. 3:29; 4:28, 30) Ngɛ 1 Mose 22:18 ɔ, Yehowa de kɛ kɔ somi ɔ he ke: “Je mahi tsuo nɛ a ngɛ zugba a nɔ ɔ maa gu [Abraham] nina a nɔ kɛ ná jɔɔmi, ejakaa o bu ye gbi tue.”
<<<P>>>
* Enɛ ɔ ji tsakemi nɛ wa pee ngɛ munyu nɛ je kpo ngɛ March 15, 2006 Blɛfo gbi Hwɔɔmi Mɔ ɔ bf. 10-12 ɔ mi ɔ he.
<<<P>>>
Somi nɛ ɔ tsɔɔ ní kpahi nɛ kɔɔ si nɛ Yehowa wo ngɛ Eden abɔɔ ɔ mi ɔ he. (1 Mose 3:15) E tsɔɔ kaa “nina a” nɛ a wo e he si ɔ maa je Abraham weku ɔ mi. Paulo tsɔɔ mi heii kaa Yesu Kristo titli ji jamɛ a “nina a” nɛ. Se nihi 144,000 nɛ a pɔ mɛ nu, nɛ a ji ‘Kristo níhi,’ nɛ a maa piɛɛ e he kɛ ye matsɛ ɔ hu piɛɛ nina nɛ ɔ he. (Gal. 3:16, 29; Kpo. 14:1-3) Ngɛ somi nɛ Yehowa kɛ Abraham so ɔ nya a, nihi fuu nɛ a maa kplɛɛ Yesu nɔ, nɛ a maa bu lɛ tue ɔ, ma ná jɔɔmihi babauu.
<<<P>>>
Mɛni he je nɛ Paulo ngɔ ‘nɔ́ he tomi ní nɛ ɔmɛ’ kɛ tsu ní ɔ? Paulo pee jã konɛ e kɛ ha nɛ Yudabi ɔmɛ nɛ a ngɛ e be ɔ mi ɔ nɛ a na kaa ke a ya nɔ nɛ a kɛ Mose Mlaa a tsu ní ɔ, lɛɛ a maa pee nyɔguɛhi kaa bɔ nɛ e ji ngɛ Haga blɔ fa mi ɔ. Se ke a kplɛɛ Abraham “nina a,” nɛ ji Yesu Kristo ɔ nɔ ɔ, lɛɛ a be nyɔguɛ pee hu, nɛ a maa ye a he kaa Sara. Ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ, a maa ye a he ngɛ yayami kɛ gbenɔ he. (Yoh. 8:32-34) Se ke a kplɛɛ we Yesu nɔ ɔ, lɛɛ e tsɔɔ kaa a nui yi mi tomi he je nɛ a ngɔ Mlaa somi ɔ kɛ ha a sisi, nɛ ji kaa e maa tsɔɔ mɛ blɔ kɛ ya Kristo ngɔ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Galatiabi ɔmɛ nɛ a ji Kristofohi nɛ Paulo ngma sɛ womi ɔ kɛ ya ha mɛ ɔ, a kpɛti nihi fuu pi Yudabi ji mɛ, nɛ a be Mlaa a sisi. Se Yudabi komɛ nɛ a ngɛ asafo ɔ mi ɔ ngɛ Ma Jeli ɔmɛ nɛ a ba pee Kristofohi ɔ a nɔ nyɛɛ kaa a kɛ Mlaa a nɛ tsu ní, né nɔ pomi ɔ hu piɛɛ he. Se Paulo ha nɛ a na heii kaa nile be mi kaa a maa pee jã. Akɛnɛ a kplɛɛ Kristo nɔ he je ɔ, a ye a he, nɛ e he hia we nɛ a pee nyɔguɛhi kɛ ha Mlaa a hu.—Gal. 5:1, 10-14.
