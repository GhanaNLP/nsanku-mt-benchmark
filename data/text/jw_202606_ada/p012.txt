Huɛmɛ kpakpahi woɔ a sibi a bua, nɛ a jeɔ mi mi jɔmi kɛ mi mi sami kpo kɛ tsɔɔ a sibi (Hyɛ kuku 19)
<<<P>>>
Yesu yɔse hemi kɛ yemi nɛ a je kpo ɔ, nɛ e bua jɔ he. Ke wa hlaa ní kpakpahi ngɛ wa huɛmɛ a he ɔ, lɔ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa wa ngɛ Yesu kɛ Yehowa kasee.
<<<P>>>
15 Sɔlemi hu ji nó ko nɛ he hia wawɛɛ. Wa ma nyɛ maa sɔle ha Yehowa nɛ e ye bua wɔ konɛ waa ya nɔ nɛ waa kɛ nihi nɛ a susumi kɛ a ní peepee je ekpa ngɛ wa nó ɔ he ɔ nɛ bɔ gbagbanii. O ma nyɛ ma susu si fɔfɔɛ komɛ nɛ ma nyɛ maa ngɔ nyagba kɛ ba o kɛ nyɛmi ko nyɛ kpeti ɔ he, nɛ o sɔle ngɛ he konɛ o nyɛ nɛ o to o tsui si. Mo kai kaa Yehowa nɛ ji wa Bɔlɔ ɔ le kaa e slo bɔ nɛ wa kpɛti nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ ha. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, ke o sɔle ɔ, mo ná nɔ mi mami kaa Yehowa le nó nɛ o hia konɛ o ya nɔ nɛ o fĩ si. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, ke o yɔse kaa e he wa ha mo kaa o maa to o tsui si ngɛ si fɔfɔɛ ko mi ɔ, moo sɔle ha Yehowa nɛ e ha mo e mumi klɔuklɔu ɔ konɛ o nyɛ nɛ o ye o he nɔ.—Luka 11:13; Gal. 5:22, 23.

KE WA NYƐMIMƐ ƆMƐ NGƐ NÓ NAE
<<<P>>>
16 Nó he je nɛ e ji nyagba. Ke wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ be he wami aloo a ngɛ haoe ɔ, eko ɔ, a ní peepee komɛ ma nyɛ ma gba wa nya. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nó ɔ, eko ɔ, a ma tsɔ a he banee, níhi maa pee mɛ tai mlamlaamla, aloo a maa pee ní komɛ nɛ maa pee wɔ nyakpɛ po. E ma nyɛ maa ba kaa a ma de ní komɛ nɛ maa dɔ wɔ wawɛɛ. (Hiob 6:2, 3) Se eko ɔ, wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ kɛ nyagba komɛ ngɛ kpee nɛ wa li he nó ko, enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e ma nyɛ maa ba kaa a be a ní pee kaa bɔ nɛ wa ngɛ blɔ hyɛe ɔ.
<<<P>>>
17 Moo kase bɔ nɛ Yesu mi mi jɔ nɛ e mi mi sãã lɛ ha a. Yesu jeɔ mi mi jɔmi kɛ mi mi sami kpo kɛ tsɔɔ nihi nɛ a ngɛ nó nae ɔ, e le mɛ jio, e li mɛ jio. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nó ɔ, mo susu bɔ nɛ Yesu kɛ hɛ yuyuilɔ ko nɛ a tsɛɔ lɛ ke Batimeo ɔ ye ha a he nɛ o hyɛ. Benɛ e nu kaa Yesu bee ɔ, e bɔni ngmlaa kpami bɔ nɛ pee nɛ Yesu nɛ e hyɛ lɛ. Se ni ɔmɛ nɛ a ngɛ Yesu se nyɛɛe ɔ a kpɛti ni komɛ de lɛ ke e pee dii. Se e suɔ wawɛɛ kaa Yesu nɛ tsa lɛ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e kpa ngmlaa wawɛɛ po pe kekle ɔ. Eko ɔ, e ní peepee nɛ ɔ maa pee ni ɔmɛ a ti ni komɛ tai. Se e “he pee Yesu mɔbɔ,” nɛ tsɔɔ kaa e mi mi sã lɛ wawɛɛ, nɛ e suɔ nɛ e nɛ ye kɛ bua lɛ. (Mat. 20:34; Mar. 10:46-52) Yesu kɛ Batimeo tu munyu ngɛ mi mi jɔmi mi, nɛ e je e yi ngɛ hemi kɛ yemi nɛ e je kpo ɔ he, lɔ ɔ se ɔ, Yesu tsa lɛ.
