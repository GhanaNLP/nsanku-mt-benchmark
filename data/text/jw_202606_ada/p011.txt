(Luka 17:3, 4) Mo kai kaa pi nɛ o yaa tsɔɔ nɔ nɛ e nó da kɛ nɔ nɛ e nó dɛ, mohu ɔ, oti ɔ ji nɛ nyɛ ma tsu nyagba a he ní, konɛ nyɛɛ ya nɔ nɛ nyɛɛ bɔ gbagbanii ekohu.—Abɛ 18:24.

KƐ WA SUSUMI KƐ WA NÍ PEEPEE KƆ WE
<<<P>>>
10 Nɔ́ he je nɛ e ji nyagba. Be komɛ ɔ, e he waa ha wɔ kaa waa kɛ wa nyɛmimɛ nɛ a ní peepee kɛ a susumi je ekpa ngɛ wa nó ɔ he ɔ maa bɔ gbagbanii. Eko ɔ, ni komɛ a ní peepee komɛ ngɛ nɛ e peeɔ wɔ tai. Ni komɛ ngɛ nɛ akɛnɛ a wa mɛ yi mi be ko nɛ be loo bɔ nɛ a tsɔse mɛ ha he je ɔ, a ngɛ dii nɛ a nyɛ we nɛ a kɛ nihi bɔɔ. Ni komɛ hu ngɛ nɛ bɔ nɛ a tsɔse mɛ ha he je ɔ, a jeɔ si nɛ a tuɔ munyu fuu. Aloo eko ɔ, be mi nɛ wa suɔ nɛ waa ya he nɛ nihi bua a he nya ngɛ ɔ, mɛɛ lɛɛ a suɔ nɛ a tsɔ a he banee.
<<<P>>>
11 Moo kase Yesu tsui si tomi ɔ. Yesu kɛ nihi nɛ a ní peepee kɛ a susumi je ekpa ngɛ e nɔ ɔ he ɔ pee huɛmɛ. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nó ɔ, bɔfo Yakobo kɛ bɔfo Yohane suɔ nɛ a ná he blɔ nɛ nɔ kuɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, a ya bi Yesu kaa e ha nɛ a ti nɔ kake nɛ hi e hiɔ nɔ, nɛ nɔ kake hu nɛ hi e muɔ nɔ ngɛ Matsɛ Yemi ɔ mi. (Mar. 10:35-37) Se ngɛ Yesu blɔ fa mi ɔ, e ba e he si nɛ e ngmɛɛ he blɔ nɛ nɔ kuɔ nɛ e ngɛ ngɛ hiɔwe ɔ he nɛ e ba zugba a nɔ. (Flp. 2:5-8) Ke o ngɔ nó nɛ Yesu pee ɔ kɛ to nó nɛ Yakobo kɛ Yohane pee ɔ he ɔ, o naa heii kaa a ní peepee kɛ a susumi je ekpa kulaa ngɛ Yesu nó ɔ he! Se ngɛ enɛ ɔ tsuo se ɔ, Yesu to e tsui si kɛ ha Yakobo kɛ Yohane kɛ ni kpahi hulɔ.
<<<P>>>
12 Yesu hyɛ we blɔ kaa be fɛɛ be ɔ, e kaseli ɔmɛ nɛ a pee a ní ngɛ blɔ nɛ da nɔ. Yesu le kaa kaseli kpa amɛ hu suɔ nɛ a ná he blɔ nɛ nɔ kuɔ kaa bɔ nɛ e ji ngɛ Yakobo kɛ Yohane a blɔ fa mi ɔ. (Mar. 9:34) He nɛ a hi ɔ nɔuu lɛ Yesu hu hi kɛ wa. Ngɛ jamɛ a kpɔ ɔ mi ɔ, jami hɛ mi nyɛɛli ɔmɛ woɔ nihi he wami kaa a di biɛ hemi kɛ he blɔ nɛ nɔ kuɔ nɛ a ma ná a se. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, Yesu na nó he je nɛ e kaseli ɔmɛ peeɔ a ní jã a. Se kɛ̃ ɔ, Yesu to e tsui si kɛ ha mɛ. E le kaa su nɛ ji he nɔ womi kɛ hɛ mi nyami hlami ɔ tlɔ a he jehahi babauu, nɛ a be nyɛe maa ngmɛɛ he oya nɔuu, e ma he be.—Mar. 10:42-45.
<<<P>>>
13 Mɛni maa ye bua wɔ konɛ waa ya nɔ nɛ waa kɛ wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ nɛ bɔ gbagbanii? Moo bɔ mɔde nɛ o to o tsui si kɛ ha nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ ke a ní peepee komɛ peeɔ mo tai po. (Abɛ 14:29) Wɔ tsuo wa ngɛ su ekpaekpahi, nɛ behi fuu ɔ, su nɛ ɔmɛ fuu ngɛ nɛ tsa pi su yayahi, mohu ɔ, jã ji bɔ nɛ wa ngɛ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, ke ni kpahi to a tsui si kɛ ha wɔ ɔ, wa bua jɔɔ. (Kane Efesobi 4:2.) Jehanɛ hu ɔ, e se nɛ waa pee kpɛii tsɔ kaa doo ɔ, nihi nɛ a tsake a su kɛ a ní peepee komɛ. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nó ɔ, e he maa wa ha nɔ ko nɛ e gboɔ zo loo e ngɛ dii ɔ kaa e kɛ nihi maa bɔ nɛ a sɛɛ ní faa. Ngɛ jamɛ a be ɔ mi nɔuu ɔ, ke nɔ ko tuɔ munyu fuu hu ɔ, e sa nɛ waa to wa tsui si kɛ ha lɛ. E ngɛ mi kaa huɛ bɔmi gbagbanii ma nyɛ maa hi waa kɛ ni komɛ a kpɛti pe ni komɛ mohu lɛɛ, se kɛ̃ ɔ, e sa nɛ wa kai kaa ke wa ní peepee kɛ wa susumi kɔ we po ɔ, waa kɛ wa nyɛmimɛ ma nyɛ maa hi si saminya.
<<<P>>>
14 Tsui si tomi maa ye bua wɔ konɛ waa kɛ wa juɛmi nɛ ma wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ a su kpakpahi a nɔ. Ke wa pee jã, nɛ wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ a ní peepee kɛ a susumi je ekpa ngɛ wa nó ɔ he po ɔ, wa maa ya nɔ nɛ waa kɛ mɛ maa pee huɛ. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nó ɔ, e ngɛ mi kaa Yakobo kɛ Yohane fo mɛ pɛ a nó mi, nɛ a suɔ nɛ a ná he blɔ nɛ nɔ kuɔ mohu lɛɛ, se nó nɛ a pee ɔ hu tsɔɔ kaa a ngɛ hemi kɛ yemi kaa Matsɛ Yemi ɔ maa ba niinɛ.
