18 Mɛni maa ye bua wɔ konɛ waa ya nɔ nɛ waa kɛ wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ nɛ bɔ gbagbanii? Mo je mi mi jɔmi kɛ mi mi sami kpo. Su nɛ ɔmɛ maa ye bua mo konɛ o nu nyɛmimɛ nɛ a be he wami aloo a hao ɔ sisi, nɛ́ o “tu bua womi mu-nyu” kɛ tsɔɔ mɛ. (Kane 1 Tesalonikabi 5:14.) Mo kai kaa huɛ kpakpa peeɔ klaalo kaa e maa ye bua e huɛmɛ, titli ngɛ “haomi behi” a mi. (Abɛ 17:17) Huɛ kpakpa nuɔ he kɛ haa nihi nɛ a ngɛ nɔ́ nae ɔ. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, e woɔ a bua, e woɔ mɛ he wami, nɛ e peeɔ ní slɔɔtohi kɛ yeɔ buaa mɛ hulɔ.

19 Mɛni ji ní komɛ nɛ o ma nyɛ maa pee kɛ tsɔɔ kaa o jeɔ mi mi jɔmi kpo nɛ o mi mi sãã mo ngɛ nihi a he? Ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ, o be nyɛe ma je nyagba nɛ o huɛ ɔ kɛ ngɛ kpee ɔ kɛ je kulaa, se kɛ̃ ɔ, o ma nyɛ maa bɔ mɔde kaa o maa le bɔ nɛ e ngɛ he nue ha, kɛ nɔ́ nɛ e ngɛ mi gblee. (Mat. 7:12; 1 Pet. 3:8) Ke e ngɛ mo níhi nɛ e ngɛ mi gblee ɔ dee ɔ, moo bu lɛ tue saminya. Ke e maa hi ɔ, moo bɔ mɔde nɛ o de lɛ níhi nɛ maa wo e bua. (Abɛ 12:25) Hyɛ saminya nɛ o ko pee o ní kaa nɔ́ nɛ o le níhi tsuo nɛ e ngɛ mi gblee ɔ. (Abɛ 18:13) Mohu ɔ, moo to o tsui si kɛ ha lɛ, nɛ o ko hyɛ blɔ kaa e pee e ní kaa bɔ nɛ moo o ngɛ hlae ɔ.—Efe. 4:32.

20 Wa bua jɔɔ wawɛɛ kaa wa ngɛ huɛmɛ kpa-kpahi ngɛ Yehowa asafo ɔ mi. Se kɛ̃ ɔ, wɔ tsuo wa yi mluku, nɛ wa kpɛti nihi fuu kɛ si fɔfɔɛhi nɛ a mi wa kpe hyɛ. Enɛ ɔ he je nɛ be komɛ ɔ, wɔ tsuo wa deɔ ní komɛ aloo wa peeɔ ní komɛ nɛ gbaa ni kpahi a nya a nɛ. Se wa bua jɔɔ kaa wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ baa a he si, a toɔ a tsui si kɛ haa wɔ, a jeɔ mi mi jɔmi kɛ mi mi sami kpo kɛ tsɔɔ wɔ, nɛ a yaa nɔ nɛ a kɛ wɔ bɔɔ gbagbanii. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ wɔ tsuo waa bɔ mɔde nɛ wa je su nɛ ɔmɛ kpo kɛ tsɔɔ wa sibi.
