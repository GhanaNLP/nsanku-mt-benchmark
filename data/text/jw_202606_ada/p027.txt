Nɔ yelɔ ehe nɛ ɔ suɔ nɛ e ná tabuli fuu nɛ a he wa saminya. Enɛ ɔ ha nɛ si himi ɔ mi ba wa ha wɔ Yehowa Odasefo ɔmɛ, ejakaa waa kɛ wa he wui tabuli a ní tsumi mi, nɛ wa fĩ we ma kudɔmi kuu ko se ngɛ he fɛɛ he nɛ wa ngɛ hulɔ. Enɛ ɔ ha nɛ wa nyɛ we nɛ waa fiɛɛ, nɛ waa kpe nɛ wa ja Yehowa faa kaa bɔ nɛ wa pee be ko nɛ be ɔ. Bɔɔbɔɔbɔɔ ɔ, nɔ yelɔ ɔ tsi wa ní tsumi ɔ nya, nɛ e nuu nyɛmimɛ lafahi abɔ kɛ wo tsu. A pɔɔ a ti ni komɛ gbemi wawɛɛ, nɛ niye ní bɔɔ pɛ lɛ a haa mɛ hulɔ. Behi fuu ɔ, a haa mɛ niye ní nɛ muɔ ngɛ mi. Se Baiblo ɔ fã wɔ hu kaa wo ye muɔ.

Ngɛ nyagba nɛ ɔmɛ tsuo a se ɔ, wa yaa nɔ nɛ wa kpeɔ nɛ wa sɔmɔɔ Yehowa. (Heb. 10:25) Wa peeɔ kpehi po ngɛ ngmɔhi a mi, kɛ he kpahi nɛ nihi a juɛmi be he yae. I kai kaa be ko po ɔ, nyɛminyumu ko ngɔ he nɛ e lɛɔ jijɔhi ngɛ ɔ kɛ ha wɔ kaa wa kɛ pee kpe. Se wa nyɛ we po nɛ wa dla lejɛ ɔ, nɛ wa je to ɔmɛ ngɛ piɛɔ mi hulɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, waa kɛ jijɔ ɔmɛ tsuo bla kɛ pee kpe ɔ. Ja mo nitsɛ o ba na kɛ o hɛngmɛ.—Mika 2:12.

Wa naa nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi nɛ a bɔ mɔde nɛ a ye bua wɔ ngɛ jamɛ a be ɔ mi, nɛ wa nine su mumi mi niye ní nɔ ɔ si wawɛɛ nitsɛ. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, a record ɔ munyuhi nɛ a maa tu ngɛ kpe ɔ sisi ɔ tsuo kɛ fɔɔ klama nɔ, nɛ nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ ngɔɔ kɛ ya haa Odasefohi tsuo nɛ a ngɛ Cuba a. Behi fuu ɔ, a kɛ munyuhi tsuo nɛ a maa tu ngɛ kpe ɔ sisi ɔ woɔ nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi enyɔ pɛ a dɛ, nɛ a dlaa a he, nɛ a ba tuɔ munyu ɔmɛ, nɛ a record ɔ. Akɛnɛ nyɛmimɛ nɛ ɔmɛ record ɔ munyu ɔmɛ ngɛ laami mi he je ɔ, be komɛ ɔ, o maa nu nɛ kungwɔhi kpa nɔ́ kɛ wo mi, aloo o maa nu ní komɛ a pɛmi ngɛ mi nɛ e ngɛ muɔ. Ke kane be he nɛ a maa pee kpe ɔ ngɛ ɔ, nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ ngɔɔ baisikle ko nɛ a kɛ klama ko wo he ɔ kɛ tsuɔ ní kɛ náa electricity. Enɛ ɔ yeɔ buaa wɔ nɛ wa nyɛɔ nɛ waa fiaa munyu ɔmɛ nɛ a record ɔ, nɛ wa buɔ kpe ɔ tue. Akɛnɛ a tsi wa ní tsumi ɔ nya he je ɔ, wa nine sui womihi tsuo nɛ asafo ɔ peeɔ ɔ nɔ kaa bɔ nɛ e ji ngɛ nyɛmimɛ nɛ a ngɛ ma kpahi a nɔ ɔ blɔ fa mi ɔ, se kɛ̃ ɔ, wa nine suɔ níhi nɛ wa hia konɛ huɛ bɔmi kpakpa nɛ hi waa kɛ Yehowa wa kpɛti ɔ nɔ. Niinɛ, wa bua jɔ kaa wa pee kake kɛ sɔmɔ Yehowa.—Neh. 8:10.

WA BA PEE BLƆ GBALI KƐ FƆLI
<<<P>>>
Benɛ i ye jeha 18 ɔ, i je blɔ gbami ní tsumi ɔ sisi ngɛ ma ko mi ngɛ Florida. Maa pee jeha kake se ɔ, a ha nɛ i ya sɔmɔ kaa blɔ gbalɔ klɛdɛɛ ngɛ Camagüey, nɛ ji ma ngua nɛ ngɛ Cuba a mi. Lejɛ ɔ nɛ i kɛ nyɛmiyo ko nɛ e he ngɛ fɛu, nɛ e je Santiago de Cuba a kpe ngɛ. A tsɛɛ lɛ ke Emilia. Oya nɔuu nɛ wa bɔni nyɛɛmi, nɛ ngɛ jeha kake pɛ mi ɔ, wa sɛ gba si himi mi.
