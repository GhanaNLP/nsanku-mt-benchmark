(Níts. 17:2, 3; 18:24, 28) Mwɔnɛ ɔ hu ɔ, Yehowa Odasefo ɔmɛ ngɔɔ níhi nɛ a tsɔɔ ngɛ a womi ɔmɛ a mi, brɔɔdkastin ɔ mi, magbɛ nɔ munyuhi nɛ a tuɔ, kɛ video ɔmɛ a mi ɔ kɛ daa Baiblo ɔ nɔ. Kɛ ni hehi ba wa kpe ɔmɛ ɔ, a bua jɔɔ bɔ nɛ wa daa Baiblo ɔ nɔ kɛ tsɔɔ ní ha a he. Nihi nɛ a ngɛ ní peemi ngɛ wa kpe ɔmɛ a sisi ɔ daa Baiblo ɔ nɔ kɛ tsɔɔ ní. Pi nɛ a kaneɔ ngmami ɔ pɛ kɛkɛ, mohu ɔ, a yeɔ buaa wɔ nɛ wa nuɔ sisi, nɛ wa naa bɔ nɛ waa kɛ ma tsu ní ha.—Luka 24:32.

Nyɛmimɛ komɛ nɛ a kɛ jehahi babauu sɔmɔ Yehowa a tsɔɔ ní komɛ nɛ yeɔ buaa mɛ nɛ a buɔ tue saminya kɛ a ya asafo mi kpe:
<<<P>>>
• Nyɛmiyo Suefawn nɛ a baptisi lɛ ngɛ jeha 1986 mi ɔ de ke: “I sɔleɔ kɛ haa Yehowa konɛ e ye bua mi nɛ ye juɛmi nɛ ko hi si kpae. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, i deɔ ye he ke asafo mi kpehi nɛ ma ya, nɛ ma bu tue saminya, kɛ ye he nɛ ma kɛ wo kpe ɔ mi ɔ tsuo piɛɛ ye jami ɔ he.”
<<<P>>>
• David nɛ a baptisi lɛ ngɛ jeha 1983 ɔ mi ɔ de ke: “I sɔleɔ kɛ haa Yehowa konɛ e ye bua mi nɛ ye juɛmi nɛ ko hi si kpae. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, i deɔ ye he ke asafo mi kpehi nɛ ma ya, nɛ ma bu tue saminya, kɛ ye he nɛ ma kɛ wo kpe ɔ mi ɔ tsuo piɛɛ ye jami ɔ he.”
<<<P>>>
• Nicolas nɛ a baptisi lɛ ngɛ jeha 1982 ɔ mi ɔ de ke: “Ke nɔ ko tsu ní peemi ko he ní ngɛ asafo mi kpe sisi ɔ, moo bɔ mɔde kaa o ma kadi nɔ́ ko nɛ o bua jɔ he ngɛ e ní peemi ɔ mi, nɛ o je e yi ngɛ he.”
<<<P>>>
• Augustin nɛ a baptisi lɛ ngɛ jeha 1988 ɔ mi ɔ de ke: “Ke tsɔlɔ anɔkualetsɛ kɛ ní lelɔ ɔ to blɔ nya kaa waa kpale kase munyu ko nɛ wa kase kɛ be momo ɔ, lɔ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa jamɛ a munyu ɔ he hia wɔ ekohu.”
