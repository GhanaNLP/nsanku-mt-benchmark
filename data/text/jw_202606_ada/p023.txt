11 Mo susu níhi nɛ maa ye bua o tue buli ɔmɛ ɔ he, nɛ o tsɔɔ mɛ bɔ nɛ a kɛ nɔ́ nɛ o ngɛ mɛ tsɔɔe ɔ ma tsu ní ha. Ngɔɔ Baiblo ɔ kɛ wo o tue buli ɔmɛ he wami, nɛ o tsɔɔ mɛ blɔ pɔtɛɛ nɔ nɛ a maa gu konɛ a ngɔ nɔ́ nɛ o ngɛ mɛ tsɔɔe ɔ kɛ tsu ní, mohu pe nɛ o maa tu munyu ɔ gbaja a. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, bɔfo Paulo de Timoteo ní pɔtɛɛ komɛ nɛ e sa nɛ e pee konɛ huɛ bɔmi kpakpa nɛ ya nɔ nɛ e hi e kɛ Yehowa a kpɛti be mi nɛ e ngɛ nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ ye buae ngɛ Efeso ɔ. (Kane 1 Timoteo 4:13-16.) Mo susu níhi nɛ maa ye bua o tue buli ɔmɛ ɔ he, nɛ o tsɔɔ mɛ ní pɔtɛɛ komɛ nɛ e sa kaa a pee konɛ a nyɛ nɛ a ngɔ nɔ́ nɛ o ngɛ mɛ tsɔɔe ɔ kɛ tsu ní ngɛ a si himi mi.

12 Ke o ngɛ o he dlae ɔ, moo tu munyu ɔ nɛ e je kpo si abɔ konɛ o munyu ɔ nɛ pee kaa ní sɛɛmi. Ke o je o tsui mi kɛ tu munyu ɔ, lɔ ɔ maa ye bua nɛ o tue buli ɔmɛ maa pee totooto kɛ bu mo tue, nɛ a maa nu sisi hulɔ, mohu pe nɛ o maa ngma nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ kɛ fɔ si nɛ o ma ba kane. Womiyo nɛ ji, Yaa O Hɛ Mi Ngɛ Ní Kanemi Kɛ Ní Tsɔɔmi Mi ɔ de ke: “Moo je o tsui mi kɛ tu munyu konɛ o tue buli ɔmɛ nɛ a na bɔ nɛ o naa mɛ ha, kɛ bɔ nɛ o bua jɔ munyu ɔ he ha.” Ke o munyu ɔ pee ní sɛɛmi ɔ, o tue buli ɔmɛ maa pee totooto kɛ bu mo tue, a bua maa jɔ o munyu ɔ he, nɛ a maa suɔ kaa a kɛ otihi nɛ ngɛ o munyu ɔ mi ɔ ma tsu ní.

13 Mo bi ga womi kɛ je nyɛminyumu ko nɛ e he be kɛ ní tsɔɔmi ɔ ngɔ. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, loko o ma ba tu o munyu ɔ, o ma nyɛ maa ngɔ níhi nɛ o ngma, nɛ o suɔ kaa o maa tu a he munyu ɔ kɛ tsɔɔ nyɛminyumu ɔ, nɛ ke e wo mo ga ko ɔ, moo ngɔ kɛ tsu ní konɛ o munyu ɔ nɛ nu tso saminya. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, o ma nyɛ maa tu o munyu ɔ kɛ tsɔɔ lɛ konɛ e de mo hehi nɛ e sa kaa o ya o hɛ mi ngɛ. (Abɛ 1:5) Pi be fɛɛ be nɛ wa nyɛɔ leɔ níhi nɛ eko ɔ, e he maa hia nɛ waa tsɔɔ mi saminya. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, ke nɔ ko bu mo tue be mi nɛ o ngɛ o he dlae ɔ, e ma nyɛ maa de mo otihi nɛ e sa kaa o tsɔɔ mi saminya konɛ a sisi numi nɛ ko ye. Wɔ tsuo wa suɔ nɛ waa pee tsɔɔli nɛ a he be saminya bɔ nɛ pee nɛ wa nyɛ nɛ waa ye bua wa tue buli ɔmɛ konɛ a nu
