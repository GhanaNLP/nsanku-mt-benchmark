Se loko nyɛ maa ya a, o kɛ lɛ nɛ susu bɔ nɛ nyɛ ma plɛ kɛ wo nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ he wami ha a he, nɛ ke nyɛ je lejɛ ɔ hu ɔ, mo de lɛ hehi nɛ e bɔ mɔde ngɛ, kɛ hehi nɛ e ma nyɛ maa ya e hɛ mi ngɛ. Ke o tsɔse nɔ nɛ e yeɔ buaa mo ɔ saminya a, lɛ hu e he maa su, nɛ e ma ba pee fiɛɛmi yami kuu nɔ hyɛlɔ hwɔɔ se. (2 Tim. 2:2) O ma nyɛ ma ha nɛ nyɛmimɛ nɛ a nane pi si ngɛ o kuu ɔ mi ɔ nɛ a tsɔse fiɛɛli kpahi hulɔ. Blɔ gbali kɛ fiɛɛli kpahi nɛ a ngɛ níhi a si kpa-mi ɔ kɛ ni hehi ma nyɛ maa ya fiɛɛmi konɛ a tsɔse mɛ. A ma nyɛ maa tsɔɔ mɛ bɔ nɛ a jeɔ ní sɛɛ-mi sisi ha, bɔ nɛ a ma plɛ kɛ tsa ní sɛɛmi ɔ nɔ ha pee se, kɛ bɔ nɛ a kɛ nihi maa kase Baiblo ɔ ha. Amlɔ nɛ ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ wa susu ní etɛ komɛ nɛ wa kpɛti nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ma nyɛ maa pee konɛ e fiɛɛmi yami kuu ɔ nɛ ya nɔ saminya a he.

8 O kɛ o fiɛɛmi yami kuu ɔ nɛ ya fiɛɛmi. Ke o kɛ nyɛmimɛ nɛ a ngɛ o fiɛɛmi yami kuu ɔ mi ɔ ya fiɛɛmi ɔ, nyɛ ‘woɔ nyɛ sibi he wami.’ (Kane Romabi 1:12.) Ke nyɛ bla kɛ ya fiɛɛmi ɔ, e haa nɛ nyɛ nɔ gbagba teɔ, nɛ nyɛ nuɔ he kaa nyɛ ngɛ slɔkee ke nyɛ ngɛ fiɛɛe ngɛ he komɛ. Ke ní tsu-mi nɛ o ngɛ tsue ɔ ngmɛ́ mo blɔ nɛ o kɛ o fiɛɛ-mi yami kuu ɔ nɛ bla kɛ ya fiɛɛmi ɔ, lɛɛ o kɛ o ni-tsumitsɛ ɔ nɛ susu he konɛ e pee tsakemi ngɛ o ní tsumi ɔ mi kɛ ha mo. Eko ɔ, e maa ngmɛ mo blɔ nɛ o tsu ní ngɛ ligbi kpa nɔ. (Neh. 2:4-6) Ke o wa ngɛ jeha mi aloo hiɔ ha nɛ o nyɛ we nɛ o kɛ nihi nɛ a ngɛ o kuu ɔ mi ɔ nɛ ya fiɛɛmi ɔ, lɛɛ o kɛ o kuu nɔ hyɛlɔ ɔ nɛ sɛɛ he ní. O kuu nɔ hyɛlɔ ɔ ma nyɛ maa to blɔ nya konɛ a tsɛ mo ngɛ fon nɔ ke a ngɛ fiɛɛmi yami kpe pee, aloo ke a kɛ nɔ ko ngɛ Baiblo ɔ kasee. Ke o ji niheyo loo yiheyo nɛ o ngɛ nɔmlɔ tso mi he wami kpakpa, se e he waa

ha mo kaa o maa ya fiɛɛmi be fɛɛ be hu nɛɛ? Ke jã a, lɛɛ daa otsi ɔ, o kɛ nɔ ko nɛ ngɛ o kuu ɔ mi ɔ nɛ to blɔ nya konɛ o kɛ lɛ nɛ ya fiɛɛmi. Eko ɔ, e he maa hia nɛ o susu níhi nɛ o peeɔ ɔ he eko-hu, konɛ o hyɛ kaa o ma nyɛ maa pee tsakemi-hi ngɛ bɔ nɛ o kɛ o be tsuɔ ní ha a he lo. (Rom. 12:11; Kol. 4:5) Ke o sɔle ha Yehowa a, e ma ha nɛ o ma ná “suɔmi ɔ kɛ he wami ɔ” konɛ o kɛ pee jã. Ke o pee jã a, o ma ná he se wawɛɛ.—Flp. 2:13.

9 Moo pee nihi nibwɔ. Ke wa peeɔ nihi nɛ a ngɛ wa fiɛɛmi yami kuu ɔ mi ɔ nibwɔ ɔ, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ suɔmi kɛ bua jɔmi maa hi wa kuu ɔ mi. (Kane Romabi 12:13.) Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, o ma nyɛ ma ha nɛ fiɛɛmi yami kuu nɛ o ngɛ mi ɔ nɛ ba hi kpee ngɛ o we ɔ mi kɛ ha fiɛɛmi yami. Ngɛ asafo komɛ hu a mi ɔ, ke nyɛminyumu ko ba tu magbɛ nɔ munyu ngɛ a we asafo ɔ mi ɔ, nyɛmimɛ nɛ a ngɛ fiɛɛmi yami kuu ko mi ɔ toɔ he nya buami he blɔ nya konɛ a kɛ pee lɛ nu-bwɔ. Se ke nyɛminyumu ɔ be nyɛe maa ba he nya buami ɔ sisi po ɔ, nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ nɛ a ngɛ kuu ɔ mi ɔ yaa nɛ a ya yeɔ ní, nɛ a woɔ a sibi he wami. Blɔ gbalɔ klɛdɛɛ ko nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Sarah de ke: “I yeɔ gbeye kaa ma pee nihi nibwɔ, lɔ ɔ ke i tsɛ nihi ní yemi ɔ, i peeɔ lɛ kpokploo. I bɔɔ mɔde kaa ma ngɔ ye juɛmi kɛ ma nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ nɛ ma le mɛ ɔ nɔ, mohu pe nɛ ma hi tsui yee ngɛ niye ní ɔ he.” Ke wa ha nɛ suɔmi kɛ kake peemi ngɛ wa fiɛɛmi yami kuu ɔmɛ a mi ɔ, e haa nɛ huɛ bɔmi kpakpa nɛ ngɛ waa kɛ Yehowa kɛ wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ a kpɛti ɔ mi waa.—Neh. 8:10; Níts. 20:35.
