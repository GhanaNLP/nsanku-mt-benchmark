(Hyɛ kuku 16)
<<<P>>>
YEHOWA ODASEFOHI JI NIHI NƐ MAWU KPLƐƐ A NƆ MWƆNƐ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
13 Ke wa be nɔ mi mami kaa Yehowa Odasefohi ji nihi nɛ Mawu kplɛɛ a nɔ mwɔnɛ ɔ, lɛɛ e sa nɛ waa pee he nó ko oya nɔuu. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nó ɔ, wa ma nyɛ ma bi wa he sane nɛ ɔmɛ: ‘Mɛnɔmɛ lɛ a bɔɔ mɔde kaa a kɛ nó nɛ Baiblo ɔ de ɔ ma tsu ní ngɛ a si himi mi? Jami te nɛɛ lɛ a kɛ a tsɔɔmihi daa Baiblo ɔ pɛ nɔ?’ (2 Tim. 4:3, 4) ‘Mɛnɔmɛ lɛ a ngɛ Yehowa suɔmi nya ní pee mwɔnɛ ɔ?’—Mat. 7:20, 21.
<<<P>>>
14 Mawu suɔ nɛ a fiɛɛ sane kpakpa a kɛ tsɔɔ nihi ngɛ he fɛɛ he loko nyagbe ɔ nɛ ba, enɛ ɔ he ɔ, wa ma nyɛ ma bi wa he ke: ‘Mɛnɔmɛ lɛ a ngɛ sane kpakpa a fiɛɛe mwɔnɛ ɔ?’ (Mat. 24:14) Heto ɔ ngɛ heii. Mɛ ji Yehowa Odasefo ɔmɛ. Wɔ pɛ lɛ wa ngɛ Mawu Matsɛ Yemi ɔ he sane kpakpa a fiɛɛe ngɛ mahi nɛ hiɛ pe 240 a nɔ. Waa kɛ jeha 100 kɛ se lɛ tsu ní tsumi nɛ ɔ, nɛ loloolo ɔ, wa kpa we. E ngɛ heii kaa fiɛɛmi ní tsumi ɔ ji ní tsumi ko nɛ waa kɛ fiɛ we kulaa.—Mat. 28:19, 20.
<<<P>>>
15 Kane Mateo 6:9 kɛ Yesaya 43:10. Akɛnɛ Mawu biɛ ji Yehowa he je ɔ, wa bua jɔ kaa a tsɛɔ wɔ ke e we Odasefohi. Nihi fuu nɛ a ngɛ je ɔ mi mwɔnɛ ɔ li kaa Mawu biɛ ji Yehowa. Se wa bɔ mɔde nɛ waa tsɔɔ Baiblo ɔ sisi. Ngɛ Baiblo ɔ nɛ wa tsɔɔ sisi ɔ mi ɔ, a kɛ Mawu biɛ ɔ tsu ní ngɛ mi kaa bɔ nɛ e ji ngɛ blema Baiblo nɛ a kɛ nine ngma a mi ɔ. Mawu biɛ nɛ ji Yehowa a je kpo ngɛ Je Ehe Sisi Tsɔɔmi ɔ mi maa pee si 7,000 kɛ se.
<<<P>>>
16 Kane Yohane 13:34, 35. Mwɔnɛ ɔ, nihi fuu nɛ a ngɛ je ɔ mi ɔ kɛ a sibi sãã nya ngɛ níhi a he, nɛ a nyɛɔ a sibi. Se Yehowa we bi lɛɛ a be jã. A suɔ a he wawɛɛ nitsɛ nɛ kake peemi hu ngɛ a kpɛti. E ngɛ mi kaa wa je ma slɔɔtoslɔɔtohi a mi, nɛ e slo wa ti nɔ fɛɛ nɔ si fɔfɔɛ mohu lɛɛ, se wa bɔɔ mɔde kaa wa ma ha nɛ suɔmi kɛ tue mi jɔmi nɛ hi wa kpɛti.
<<<P>>>
13. Ke wa be nɔ mi mami kaa Yehowa Odasefohi ji nihi nɛ Mawu kplɛɛ a nɔ mwɔnɛ ɔ, mɛni e sa nɛ waa pee?
<<<P>>>
14. Mɛni odasehi lɛ maa nɔ mi kaa Yehowa Odasefo ɔmɛ ha nɛ gbami nɛ ngɛ Mateo 24:14 ɔ ngɛ mi bae?
<<<P>>>
15. Mɛni Yehowa Odasefo ɔmɛ pee kɛ wo Mawu biɛ ɔ hɛ mi nyami? (Mateo 6:9; Yesaya 43:10)
<<<P>>>
16. Anɛ Yehowa Odasefohi kɛ fami nɛ ngɛ Yohane 13:34, 35 ɔ ngɛ ní tsue lo? Moo tsɔɔ nya. (Hyɛ foni ɔ hulɔ.)
