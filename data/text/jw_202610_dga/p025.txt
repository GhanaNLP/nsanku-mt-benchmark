4 Ka hooŋ nyɛ, a zannoo ŋa arɛɛ a toma gane na eŋɛŋ. Be yelee lɛ na Gyuuda biiri mine naŋ da e ba yɛlɛ saŋa na bitontona Pɔɔl naŋ da moɔlɔ a duori-soŋ korɔ ba Bɛroya teŋɛ naŋ. (Kanne Toma 17:10, 11.) A noba naŋ da kyɛllɛ wono a yɛlɛ a Bɛroya teŋɛ na da taaɛ popeɛloŋ a kyaare yɛlɛ na Pɔɔl naŋ da yele na, azuŋ ba da boɔrɔŋ ka ba zanne yɛlɛ yaga yi a sɛgre poɔŋ a de tono neŋ toma. A Baabole gba ye-leeŋ ka ba “da e la noba naŋ kyile.” Ba da bɔɛ wag-re a peɛre a sɛgre yɛlɛ gaa tɔɔre a nyɛ ka yɛlɛ na Pɔɔl naŋ wuli ba na eɛ yelmeŋɛ. Zenɛ ŋa meŋ, te meŋ maŋ tɔglɛɛ a Bɛroya teŋɛ deme saŋa na teŋ wa kyɛllɛ wono yɛlɛ a yi te lammore zie bee te lammo-bɛrɛ neŋ te lammobilii zieŋ na. Te maŋ yuo la te Baabole a tuuro a kannoo saŋa na a yelyaga soba naŋ wa kanna a Baabole na. Te maŋ lɛ nyɛŋ ka yɛlɛ na zaa teŋ kyɛllɛ wono na eɛ yelmenne.
<<<P>>>
5 A Baabole yeleeŋ ka: “Ba yaga zie da sage de la a yɛlɛ.” (Tom. 17:12) Kyɛ, a da ba kɔɔre ka yelkpɛɛ-ŋaa kaŋa da wa. A Gyuuda biiri na mine naŋ da iri zɔɔrɔ neŋ Pɔɔl na da yi la Tɛsalonika a wa Bɛroya teŋɛ na a wa yele yelfaare kyaara Pɔɔl wuluu na. (Tom. 17:13) Kyɛ, a Kristabiiri na naŋ da zanne a yelmeŋɛ na da ba vɛŋ ka ana yɛlɛ ŋa tɔ dabeɛ eŋ ba bee a sãã ba sagediibu. Kyɛ boŋ da soŋ ba baŋ toɔ̃ are kpeeŋaa? Ba da maŋ peɛrɛɛ a sɛgre poɔ yɛlɛ be-biri zaa, a nyɛ ka yɛlɛ na a sɛgre naŋ yele na eɛ yel-menne. Azuŋ, lɛ da soŋɛɛŋ baŋ are kpeeŋaa a naŋ dire yelmennoŋ. Zenɛ ŋa, ka teneeŋ maŋ bɔ wagre bebiri zaa a peɛre a Baabole sɛgre kaŋa yɛlɛ gaa tɔɔre, ana soŋeŋ ka te naŋ kyɛnɛ dire yelmennoŋ korɔ Jehoova haali wagre kpeɛne saŋa.

5. Sobuo ka a Bɛroya teŋɛ deme na da nyɛ tono saŋa na baŋ da maŋ peɛre a sɛgre poɔ yɛlɛ bebiri zaa na?

Yɛlɛ Mine Naŋ Be A Baabole Poɔŋ A Wuli Ka Asɛŋ Ka Te Maŋ Peɛre A Baabole Sɛgre Bebiri Zaa
<<<P>>>
A Moosis Bege na da yeleeŋ ka asɛŋ ka a ŋmenpuore wedeɛreba maŋ iri sarekare ko Jehoova bebiri zaa.—Yiib. 29:38-42; 30:7, 8.
<<<P>>>
Jehoova da yelkoɛ Joosua ka o maŋ kanne a Bege poɔ yɛlɛ a teɛre a yɛlɛ “ŋmenaa ane teŋsogo.”—Joos. 1:8.
<<<P>>>
Jehoova da yelkoɛ a Izerayɛl namine na ka ba sɛge yɛlɛ na baŋ kanne a yi a Bege poɔŋ na biŋ, a maŋ kannaa bebiri zaa.—Dut. 17:18-20.
<<<P>>>
A yieloŋ yiele da yeleeŋ ka o maŋ taaɛ popeɛloŋ ka o naŋ wa kanne Naaŋmen yelbi-ri na “ŋmenaa ane zisoɔree.”—Yiem. 1:1-3.
<<<P>>>
Daaniel da maŋ puoree gbɛri-bota bebiri zaa.—Daan. 6:10.
<<<P>>>
A Yel-iriwulaa gane na yelee neŋsugu yaga mine naŋ de puoruu korɔ Jehoova o ŋmen-puoredie na poɔŋ “ŋmenaa ane teŋsogo” yɛlɛ.—Yel. 7:15.

6 Ka teneeŋ maŋ kanne kyɛ peɛre a Baabole sɛgre na bebiri zaa, lɛ na wuliŋ ka te nɔŋɛɛ Jehoova kyɛ koroo gyeremɛ meŋ. Jehoova la te Naaŋmen, azuŋ a eɛ nimizeɛ ka te maŋ bɔ wagre a kanne o Yelbiri na kyɛ peɛre o yɛlɛ gaa tɔɔre. Jehoova nɔŋ teŋ a boɔrɔ ka te nyɛ nyɔvore naŋ ba taa baaraa. (Gyn. 3:16) Yɛlɛ yaga be la a Baabole poɔŋ a wuli ka Je-hoova poɔ maŋ pɛleŋ ka teneeŋ wa bɔ wagre bebi-ri zaa a de e yelkaŋa wuli ka te taaɛ nɔmmo aneŋ
