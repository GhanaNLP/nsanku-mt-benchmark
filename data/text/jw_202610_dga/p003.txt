A LAMMORE DA EŊƐƐ NAAŊMEN NOBA FAŊA KA BA TONƆ A NAAŊ MOƆLOO TOMA NIMIZEẼ
<<<P>>>
May kyuu na baaraa seŋ, a Baabole zannezanneba kpɛŋ zie zaa da gaaɛ lammo kaŋa baŋ da e London, England paaloŋ. Ana saŋa na, ziiri na baŋ da maŋ printɛrɛ gama na da ba tonɔ toma ana bebiri naŋ. Azuŋ, a da eɛ kpeɛŋaa ka te yɔɔmine nyɛ zie a printi te gama na. Azuŋ, te yɔɔdɔbɔ naŋ da tonɔ toma a Brooklyn Bɛɛtiliŋ na da deɛ aware yaga toŋ neŋ toma a printi gampɛlɛ 117,000. A gampɛlee ŋa da taaɛ sowuli mine a ko Naaŋmen noba. A gane yuori da la The Standard for the People.
<<<P>>>
May 29, Saturday daare, te yɔɔdɔbɔ neŋ te yɔɔpɔgeba da piileeŋ a de a gampɛlee ŋa tonɔ neŋ toma, a da yi yeŋe a piili tɔrɔ a gampɛlɛ korɔ noba. Ana bebiri na a lammo da eŋ baŋ faŋa yaga zaa. A lammo yelyaga da eŋ baŋ faŋa ka: “Baabole zannezanna zaa naŋ be kyɛ, te tonɔ boɔlɛɛ yɛ zaa ka yɛ de yɛ menne eŋ a naaŋ moɔloo toma poɔŋ, a moɔlɔ te Naa Yezu ane o Naaloŋ na yɛlɛ. Yɛ daŋ iribu a gaa Alexandra Palace, te yɔɔdɔbɔ naŋ bebe na na wuli yeŋ yɛlɛ naŋ seŋ ka yɛ e.”
<<<P>>>
Te yɔɔmine na da tɔrɛɛ gampɛlɛ noɔre naŋ gaŋ 100,000. July 15, 1926 A Kaakaara Ziduoruu na da yeleeŋ ka: “Te yɔɔdɔbɔ neŋ te yɔɔpɔgeba na da taaɛ popeeloŋ naŋ ba taa mannoo. Yelmeŋɛ zaa, ba da nyɛɛŋ ka ba kyɛlle woŋee Jehoova noɔre. Jehoova yeleeŋ ka: ‘Yɛ na la N sieree deme, maaŋ la yɛ Naaŋmen.’”
<<<P>>>
Zie naŋ da nyaa na, te Yɔɔdɔɔ Rutherford da terɛɛ yelyaga kaŋa zie na baŋ da lantaa na, Royal Albert Hall. A yelyaga da kyaarɛɛ yɛlɛ naŋ na pɔge a donɛɛ naan diibu kpeẽo na. A yelyaga yelzu da la “Why World Powers Are Tottering—The Remedy.” A da ba kɔɔre ka a yelyaga zie zaa da pa ne noba. Noba 13,000 da paɛ a zie na baŋ terɛ a yelyaga na, kyɛ ka noba naŋ waana. Azuŋ, noba mine da arɛɛ a zie yeŋeŋ kyɛ kyɛllɛ a yelyaga, azuŋ ba da deɛ speeka bɛrɛ kɔnɛt a yeŋeŋ ka a noba zaa na tõɔ̃ kyɛlle woŋ a yelyaga soŋ zaa.
<<<P>>>
A yi te Yɔɔdɔɔ Rutherford yelyaga na poɔŋ, o da wuleeŋ ka a donɛɛ naan diibu kpeẽo na mɔɔrɔ fããnaŋ, bozuŋ ba zagrɛɛŋ ka ba sage Jehoova ane O Naaloŋ na. Te Yɔɔdɔɔ Rutherford da la wulee yɛlɛ naŋ na pɔge a League of Nations, a yeli ka ba koŋ la wa bebe.
