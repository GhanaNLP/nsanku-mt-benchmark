Daaraata puoriŋ, July kyuu na poɔŋ, te yɔɔdɔɔ Edwin ane te yɔɔdɔɔ kaŋa baŋ boɔlɔ George Wright naŋ da e naaŋ moɔlo naŋ e siri ka na gaaɛ zie zaa baŋ boɔrɔ sommo da kpɛ la maŋbori a gaa India paaloŋ. BA DA MAŊ TOŊƐƐ TE YƆƆDƆBƆ MINE BAŊ TE SOŊ KA A NAAŊ MOƆLOO TOMA YƐLƐ NYƐRƐ NIMITƆƆRE GAABO
<<<P>>>
Yuoni 1894 a de waana 1926, a lantaapuoruu maŋ to-ŋɛɛ te yɔɔmine baŋ gaa te eŋ a Baabole zannezanneba faŋa. Kyɛ a yi July 1926 a de waana, a lantaapuoruu da eɛ leɛroo mine a kyaare te yɔɔmine na baŋ maŋ toŋ baŋ te are a lantaapuoruu gbɛbɔgriŋ na. Ba toma na da leɛ waa ŋa gbaŋdige zukaareba toma. Saŋa na, ba da maŋ gaa puoree ba yɔɔmine na bebi-yenaa, kyɛ a yi ana saŋa na a waana, ba maŋ deɛ bebie ayi a te puore ba yɔɔ-mine na. Ane meŋ, a da fɛrɛɛŋ ka ba maŋ de aware 100 toŋ neŋ a naaŋ moɔloo toma kyuu gbuli zaa.
<<<P>>>
A yi lɛtɛ kaŋa naŋ da wa a A Kaakaara Ziduoruu poɔŋ, te Yɔɔdɔɔ George Draper naŋ da e te yɔɔdɔɔ na kaŋa naŋ kaara te yɔɔmine zu na da yeleeŋ ka: “A eɛ ni-mizeɛ ka te yɔɔmine naŋ kaara te yɔɔmine zu ane te yɔɔdɔbɔ zaa a e siri ka ba na toŋɛɛ a naaŋ moɔloo toma na, a maŋ kanna A Kaakaara Ziduoruu kyɛ derɛ kpããmo naŋ seŋ neŋ a korɔ ba yɔɔmine. [Aiz. 56:10] A yɛlɛ ŋa neŋ a zu maŋ meŋ mɔɔrɔ N moɔbo zaa ka N erɛ.” Yuoni 1938 poɔŋ, te Yɔɔdɔɔ Draper da kpieŋ a baare o donɛɛ zu toma na. O da eɛ dɔɔ baŋ sɔ naaloŋ kãã, azuŋ te taaɛ sagediibu ka pampana o be la sazuŋ.

LAMMOKPEŊ KAŊA BAŊ DA E NEW YORK CITY POƆŊ
<<<P>>>
Yuoni 1926 naŋ da waana o baaroo na, ba da eɛ lammokpeŋ kaŋa October o bebie pie neŋ anaare a te tɔ bebie pie neŋ anii New York City poɔŋ. A lammo poɔŋ, te Yɔɔdɔɔ Rutherford da terɛɛ yelyaga kaŋa yelzu naŋ la “Earth’s Greatest Conflict Near.” Azuŋ, a teŋɛ duori-saamma toma ziiri yaga zie zaa da sagɛɛŋ ka ba na deɛ a yelyaga eŋ ba duorisaamma gama poɔŋ ka noba yaga toɔ̃ kanne. Kyɛ, saŋa na a lammo naŋ da waana ka o pii-li na, a duorisaamma toma ziiri kpɛŋ zie zaa da zagrɛɛŋ ka ba e yɛlɛ na baŋ yelka ba na eŋ na.
<<<P>>>
A yɛlɛ ŋa da veŋeeŋ ka te yɔɔdɔbɔ na da yi yeŋe a yeli yɛlɛ na a duorisaamma toma ziiri na naŋ e na yɛlɛ ko noba. Azuŋ, Friday daare, October 15, te Yɔɔdɔɔ R. J. Martin da maalɛɛ gampɛlee kaŋa yuori naŋ la Against the People. A gampɛlee ŋa da yelee potu-yɛlɛ na a duo-risaamma toma ziiri naŋ e, ka lɛ veŋ ka noba yaga koŋ woŋ a duorisoŋ. A gampɛlee da la taaɛ yɛlɛ a kyaare te Yɔɔdɔɔ Rutherford yelyaga na mine, ka noba na baŋ kanne. Te Yɔɔdɔɔ Martin da yeleeŋ ka: “Pampana te taaɛ mansin a na baŋ printi te gampɛlɛ. Te yɔɔdɔbɔ naŋ be Bɛɛtil zie ŋa na toŋɛɛ tonkpeɛne ŋmenaa neŋ zisoɔree a te ta saŋa na teŋ na printi gampɛlɛ noɔre naŋ ta 1,250,-000. Ka teneeŋ wa printaa baare, te na yelkoɛ te yɔɔmi-ne naŋ be a lammo ŋa pareŋ ka ba de a gampɛlɛ yi yeŋe a te tɔre ko noba.” Azuŋ, saŋa na baŋ da baare a lammo kyɛlee kaŋa na, te yɔɔmine da piileeŋ a tɔrɔ a gampɛlɛ. Zie da wa maana ka ba tɔrɛɛ gampɛlɛ 300,000.
<<<P>>>
