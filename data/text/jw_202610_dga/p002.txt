YUOMO KƆƆ NAŊ PARE LA ŊA
<<<P>>>
YUONI 1926 January 1, A Kaakaara Ziduoruu na yeleeŋ ka: “A koŋ kɔɔre Naaŋmen na vɛŋeŋ ka o yuori na pɛgeyi ka neɛzaa baŋ ana yuori na. Noba mine na bebeŋ a e o sieree deme a donɛɛ zuŋ.”
<<<P>>>
A yi yuoni 1926 a de gɛrɛ, Jehoova tue a lantaa-puoruu zudaana Yezu Krista eŋeŋ a soŋ Kristabiiri baŋ nyɛ ka toma naŋ e toŋ tegroŋ nensaalaa zemmo poɔŋ la a naaŋ moɔloo toma na. Azuŋ, ka a naaŋ moɔloo tomaŋ na baŋ nyɛrɛ nimitɔɔre gaabo aseŋ ka “a gbaŋgba-bive-laa ane yɛmbaŋna” soŋ ka Naaŋmen puorebiiri nyɛ gaŋ-paala, a e leɛroo a kyaare te lammore, kyɛ wɛl yɛlɛ muni ka a kyaaŋ soŋ zaa ka a Kristabiiri baŋ lɛ aŋ seŋ ka ba erɛ ba yɛlɛ. (Mat. 24:45) Yezu da la ko baŋ sommo zaa yɛlɛ naŋ fɛre ba. A mine la, deri aneŋ mansinhi naŋ na soŋ ka a toma nyɛrɛ nimitɔɔre gaabo.

GAŊ-PAALAA KAŊA ANEŊ LEƐROO MINE BAŊ DA E A KYAARE TE LAMMORE NA
<<<P>>>
April kyuu na poɔŋ, gane na baŋ boɔlɔ ka The Gol-den Age (pampana ona teŋ boɔlɔ ka Awake!) da yeleeŋ ka ba iree gaŋ-paalaa kaŋa. A gane yuori la Deliverance. Ana gaŋ-paalaa ŋa baŋ na leɛ de tonɔ neŋ toma a naaŋ moɔloo toma poɔŋ kyɛ bare a Studies in the Scriptures gane na. Kyɛ a da fɛrɛɛŋ ka ba maale ana gaŋ-paalaa ŋa yaga. Azuŋ, te yɔɔdɔbɔ naŋ da be a Bɛɛtil poɔŋ na da da la mansin kaŋa naŋ printerɛ gama. Minti yenaa zaa, a mansin da maŋ tõ printerɛ gama 22. Te yɔɔmine nu da kpɛɛ a gama ŋa, azuŋ ba da maŋ deɛ vuo zaa baŋ nyɛ a de tɔrɔ neŋ a gama korɔ noba a naaŋ moɔloo poɔŋ.
<<<P>>>
A Baabole zannezanneba da deɛ yuomo yaga a zanne yɛlɛ yaga yi a Studies in the Scriptures gane na ba lam-more ziiriŋ. Kyɛ a yi May kyuu na poɔŋ, ba da ba la zan-na a gane ŋa ba lammo ziiriŋ, A Kaakaara Ziduoruu gane na baŋ da leɛ zanna a lammorezieŋ. Bo maaloŋ da yi ana leɛroo ŋa poɔ wa?
<<<P>>>
Lantaapuoruu kaŋa da sɛge koɛ te Yɔɔdɔɔ Rutherford ka: “Sunday bebiri zaa te maŋ zannɛɛ A Kaakaara Zi-duoruu gane poɔ yɛlɛ. Te poɔ pɛleɛŋ yaga zaa, bozuŋ wagre zaa te maŋ zannɛɛ yelpaala wagre naŋ seŋ neŋ poɔŋ. A waa ŋa kyaaŋ paalaa kaŋa naŋ kyaana.”
<<<P>>>
“Ana kyaaŋ paalaa” na da wuleeŋ ka a Baabole zan-nezanneba da nyɛɛ yɛlɛ pare bammo soŋ zaa, a nyɛ ka aseŋ ka ba de ba menne eŋ a naaŋ moɔloo toma poɔŋ, bozuŋ ana la toŋ nimizeɛ. Ana mannoo poɔŋ, zannoo yɛlɛ kaŋa da be la A Kaakaara Ziduoruu gane kaŋa poɔŋ a yelka a yelnimizeɛ meŋɛmeŋɛ naane baŋ na moɔrɔ ka ba e Kristabiiri naŋ taa gyog-sonne, a taa nimimaaroŋ aneŋ gyeremɛ ko noba. Ka ba naŋ erɛ lɛ yoŋ, ba na iree ba teɛroŋ yi a naaŋ moɔloo toma eŋeŋ. A zannoo yɛlɛ da tɔnneɛŋ a yelka Jehoova koɛ a Kristabiiri bege ka ba e o sieree deme. Ba naŋ noŋ Jehoova zuŋ, Kristabie zaa seŋ ka o kyelle woŋ a bege ŋa a kyɛ e siri ka o na toŋɛɛ a naaŋ moɔloo toma na.
<<<P>>>
Zannoo yɛlɛ kaŋa meŋ da taaɛ a yelzu “Sacrifice and Service.” A zannoo yɛlɛ da manne wuleeŋ ka a eɛ nimizeɛ ka yelmeŋɛ Kristabiiri de yelmennoŋ puoro neŋ Jehoova a de wuli ka ba nɔmooŋ. O da la yeli poɔ yɛŋ ka aseŋ ka a Baabole zannezanneba zaa mõ niŋe a moɔlɔ a naaloŋ duorisoŋ lɛ zaa baŋ na tõ, a lɛ moɔlɔ a duori-soŋ korɔ noba yaga meŋ.—Aiz. 43:10, 12; Mat. 24:14.
<<<P>>>
Baŋ da e ana leɛroo ŋa a da ba kɔɔre kyɛ ka noba naŋ be a lantaapuoree poɔŋ da nyɛrɛ tonɔ. Aŋa daaraa-wala mine poɔŋ, ba da sɛgɛɛ lɛta kaŋa eŋ a July 15, 1926 A Kaakaara Ziduoruu na poɔŋ. A lɛta yeleeŋ ka: “A zan-noo yɛlɛ ‘Character or Covenant—Which?’ ane ‘Sacrifice and Service’ soŋ teŋ paa. A sereŋ dii la te sukyie kyɛ eŋ te faŋa meŋ. Pampana, te maŋ kanneŋ kyɛ lɛ zannaa meŋ poɔ. Te yɔɔmine eŋɛ nyagraŋ neŋ ana yɛlɛ ŋa. Bo-zuŋ, a zannoo saŋa ba maŋ nyɛɛ apoɔsori a iri yɛlɛ nɔɛ
