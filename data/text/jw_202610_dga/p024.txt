YƐLƐ TE NAŊ NA ZANNE
A zannoo ŋa na eŋ teŋ faŋa ka te nyɛ ananso aŋ e nimizeɛ ka te maŋ peɛre a Baabole sɛgre kaŋa bebi-ri zaa. Te na la nyɛɛ sowuli mine a kyaare lɛ teŋ na baŋ tu kyɛ maŋ e lɛ.
<<<P>>>
YUONI 1905 poɔŋ, Jehoova lantaapuoruu na maalɛɛ gane kaŋa yuo-ri naŋ la Daily Heavenly Manna for the Household of Faith. A gane ŋa da taaɛ Baabole sɛgre aneŋ yɛlɛ mine baŋ iri yi a Zion’s Watch Tower na poɔŋ, ana yɛlɛ ŋa da maŋ wɛlɛɛ a Baabole sɛgre na muni aŋ pɛle soŋ zaa. Azuŋ, te yɔɔdɔbɔ neŋ te yɔɔpɔgeba da maŋ tõɔ̃ kannɛɛ a Baabo-le sɛgre kaŋa bebiri zaa a teɛre o yɛlɛ gaa tɔɔre. Bebiri zaa maŋ taaɛ o Baabole sɛgre. Azuŋ, ba da maŋ kannɛɛ a bebiri zaa sɛgre a te ta a yuoni baaraa. Jehoova puorebiiri deɛ a gane ŋa toŋ neŋ toma a yi yuoni 1905 te ta yuoni 1927. A puoriŋ, ba da leɛ deɛ a yɛlɛ ŋa poɔ te yuoni zaa gane na baŋ boɔlɔ ka Year Book na poɔŋ.* Yuoni zaa ba da maŋ maalɛɛ a Year Book, azuŋ yuoni zaa te yɔɔmine da maŋ nyɛɛ Baa-bole sɛgre tɛɛtɛɛ aneŋ yɛlɛ mine baŋ maŋ iraa yi A Kaakaara Ziduoruu poɔŋ. Azuŋ, te yɔɔmine da maŋ nyɛɛ Baabole sɛgre baŋ na maŋ kan-ne kyɛ teɛre o yɛlɛ gaa tɔɔre bebiri zaa.
<<<P>>>
2 Kyɛ a yi yuoni 1986 ba maalɛɛ gane kaŋa yuori naŋ la, Baabole Sɛgre Hoŋ Na Maŋ Peɛre A Yɛlɛ bebiri zaa. Pampana ŋa, a gane ŋa be la kɔkɔɛ naŋ pare 300 poɔŋ. Ba maŋ printɛɛ a gane ŋa, kyɛ o be la JW Library® app, Watchtower ONLINE LIBRARY™ ane Watchtower Library zuŋ.
<<<P>>>
3 Te kaŋa zaa maŋ taaɛ yɛlɛ yaga ka te e bebiri zaa, azuŋ saŋa kaŋa ana baŋ eɛ kpeɛŋaa ka te maŋ nyɛ wagre a kanne a bebiri zaa Baabo-le sɛgre na. Te na nyɛɛ soorebie ata mine nɔɛ iribo a zannoo ŋa poɔŋ, ka a soŋ te ka te maŋ tõɔ̃ kanne a bebiri zaa Baabole sɛgre na a teɛre
<<<P>>>
* A Year Book ba kye kɔkɔ yaga poɔŋ, A Kaakaara Ziduoruu naŋ da maŋ wa kyuu zaa poɔŋ na zuŋ, ba da maŋ deɛ a Baabole sɛgre mine eŋ o poɔŋ. Bebiri zaa da maŋ taaɛ o Baabole sɛgre aneŋ A Kaakaara Ziduoruu mine naŋ wɛle ana sɛgre na muni soŋ zaa hoŋ na baŋ kannaa.

<<<P>>>
