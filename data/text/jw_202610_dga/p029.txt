18 Maŋ mɔɔ kyɛ kanne a bebiri zaa Baabole sɛgre a yi wagre na hoŋ sɛgre biŋ ka ho na eɛ lɛ na. Ta vɛŋ ka yelzaa pigi bo sori. Te yɔɔpɔge Isabelle naŋ nyɛ yuomo 19 wulee yɛlɛ mine naŋ na baŋ soŋ. O ye-leeŋ ka: “Maŋ yelko ho meŋɛ ka ho koŋ e yelzaa bee ho gba koŋ kaa yelzaa ho fone zuŋ ka hooŋ ba kan-ne a bebiri zaa Baabole sɛgre na baare. Ka hooŋ wa de ho fone erɛ neŋ a zannoo, aminekaŋaŋ yɛlɛ mine na tagrɛɛ ho teɛroŋ a fone zuŋ, ho na baŋ deɛ a Baabole Sɛgre Hoŋ Na Maŋ Peɛre A Yɛlɛ bebiri zaa gane na baŋ printi na aneŋ a Baabole e neŋ a zannoo sɛre kyɛ derɛ ho fone.” Te yɔɔpɔge Olivia nyɛɛ yuomo 19, ŋaaŋ oŋ meŋ yeli: “A maŋ somaŋ yaga zaa ka ho neŋ noba mineŋ wa laŋ kanne a be-biri zaa Baabole sɛgre na aŋa ho booree deme bee ho zɔmenne. A maŋ eɛ mɔlɔ ka ba teɛre bo ka yɛ e o bebiri zaa.”

19 Bidɔgreba, ka yɛ neŋ yɛ biiriŋ maŋ laŋ kanne a bebiri zaa Baabole sɛgre na, a na soŋeŋ ka a leɛ ba gyogo ka ba meŋ erɛ lɛ saŋa na baŋ wa e ba yoŋ bee a wa leɛ nembɛrɛ na. Aminekaŋaŋ, ho biiri na yɛlɛ bammo naŋ ba do saa, kyɛ ka ho ne baŋ maŋ erɛ lɛ, a daaŋ na soŋ baŋ nendaare kaŋa. Aŋa mannoo poɔŋ, ka bon-biriŋ wa nyɛrɛ koɔ̃ yire tɛne poɔŋ, o maŋ baaŋ fẽẽfẽẽ fẽẽfẽẽ lɛɛŋ te leɛ te-kpoŋ. Lɛ meŋ la, ka te bibiiri naŋ maŋ nyɛrɛ wuluu bebiri zaa a tuuro a bebiri zaa Baabole sɛgre na kannoo eŋɛŋ, fẽẽfẽẽ fẽẽfẽẽ ba na baaŋ a wa leɛ Kristabiiri naŋ kyile.—Kanne Filipae 1:9, 10.

20 Yezu yeleeŋ ka: “Bondirii yoŋ naane korɔ nen-saalaa nyɔvore, kyɛ yelbiri zaa naŋ yire Jehoova noɔreŋ la korɔ nyɔvore.” (Mat. 4:4) Bebiri zaa te maŋ di la bondirii ka ana ko te faŋa ka te toɔnɔ erɛ yɛlɛ. Nyɛ lɛ meŋ aŋ e nimizeɛ yaga zaa ka bebiri zaa te maŋ zanne Naaŋmen Yelbiri na soŋ zaa, a baŋ o teɛroŋ. Te nyɔvore arɛɛ teŋ na zanna Jehoova yɛlɛ bebiri zaa. Azuŋ, daaŋ ta vɛŋ ka yelzaa pigi bo sori ka ho koŋ toɔ̃ kanne a bebiri zaa Baabole sɛg-re na.
