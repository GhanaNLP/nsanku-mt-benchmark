<<<P>>>
9 Daaniel yoŋ naane pɔlbile baŋ da nyɔge gaa- neŋ Baabilɔŋ, kyɛ ba da nyɔgɛɛ Hiburu biiri pɔl- bilii mine poɔ a gaaneŋ. Aŋa lɛ na teŋ yel a piilee saŋa na, ba kaŋa zaa da ba la pɛɛle o yiri. Azuŋ yɛlɛ yaga da erɛɛ a Baabilɔŋ paaloŋ poɔŋ naŋ na baŋ e pigrinyɛ ko ba. A da ba kɔɔre kyɛ ka a Baa- bilɔŋ deme da boɔrɔ ka ba wuli a pɔlbilii ŋa yɛlɛ mine, ka ba meŋ erɛ yɛlɛ aŋa lɛ na a Baabilɔŋ deme naŋ erɛ na. A puoriŋ ka ba ko ba wuluu ka ba tonɔ korɔ a naa o nayiri poɔŋ. Kyɛ ana wuluu na mine baŋ na ko ba na na vɛŋɛŋ ka a e kpeɛ- ŋaa ka ba naŋ kyɛnɛ dire yelmennoŋ korɔ Je- hoova. Azuŋ a da seŋ ka ba kaara ba menne soŋ a yi a yɛlɛ ŋa poɔŋ.
<<<P>>>
10 A piilee saŋa na, ba da tuoree yelkaŋa naŋ waa ŋa yelfaa zaa ba kyebe ka ba naŋ e ana yɛlɛ na. A naa da yeleeŋ ka ba maŋ de a nayiri bondi- rii na mine ko Daaniel aneŋ o pɔlbil-taa na. Kyɛ Jehoova Bege da ba tere sori ka ba dire ana bon- dirii na mine. (Daan. 1:5, 8) Kyɛ a pɔlbilii ŋa da na baŋ deɛ yɛlɛ mine iri neŋ yɛlɛ noɔre kyɛ sãã Jehoova Bege na. Bozuŋ ba da nyɔge baŋ gaaneŋ paaloŋ yuo kaŋa, baŋ ba lɛ so ba menne, a ba lɛ pɛɛle ba yiri meŋ. Azuŋ ba da na teɛreŋ ka yelzaa a naa naŋ yeli anaŋ seŋ ka ba e. Aminekaŋaŋ meŋ gba, ba da nyɛɛŋ ka ka ba naŋ zagre a naa noɔre o suuri na iri ne baŋ, ka lɛŋ wa e boŋ na pɔge ba? A yi a yɛlɛ ŋa zaa poɔ, wala baŋ pãã na e?

10. Bo gbɛre la aŋ da seŋ ka a pɔlbilii ŋa de ka lɛ vɛŋ ka ba naŋ kyɛnɛ dire yelmennoŋ korɔ Jehoova?
<<<P>>>
11 A Hiburu pɔlbilii na baŋ da korɔ wuluu Baa- bilɔŋ poɔŋ na da eɛ yaga, kyɛ a Baabole yelee ba poɔ banaare yoŋ yɛlɛ. Aminekaŋaŋ ba na yoŋ la da di yelmennoŋ ko Jehoova. Ba na la Daaniel, Hananaiya, Miiseyel ane Azaraiya. Nɔkpeɛne zaa kyebe ka a pɔlbilii ŋa da soŋɛɛ taa, ka lɛ vɛŋ baŋ toɔ̃ are pigrinyɛ noɔreŋ kyɛ dire yelmennoŋ korɔ Jehoova. Ba da zagrɛɛŋ ka ba di a nayiri bondi- rii na Naaŋmen bege naŋ yeli ka a ba seŋ ka ba di na. Ba da eɛ lɛ a de wuli ka ba diree yelmennoŋ korɔ Jehoova. Lɛ da vɛŋɛɛŋ ka Je- hoova poɔ pɛle, oŋ da ko ba maaloo.—Daan. 1: 6-12, 17.
<<<P>>>
12 Saŋa kaŋa ho taaba maŋ boɔrɔŋ ka ho e yel- kaŋa naŋ ba soma bee? Daaniel neŋ o zɔmenne na da sonnɛɛ taa a derɛ gbɛ-sonne. Azuŋ, tɔglɔ ba ewuli soŋ na, a maŋ nyɔgrɔ zɔmenne naŋ maŋ e siri ka ba na diree yelmennoŋ korɔ Je- hoova na. Ta nyɔgrɔ noba naŋ ba nɔŋ Jehoova zɔmenne. (1 Gyn. 2:15-17) Zɔmeŋ-sonne na baŋ soŋ boŋ ka ho dire yelmennoŋ korɔ Jehoova haali saŋa na hoŋ ba nyɛ ka yelkaŋa eebo eɛ yelfaa na. Ka hooŋ e a yɛlɛ ŋa zaa, Jehoova na ko boŋ maaloo yaga. (Luk. 16:10) Ka hooŋ maŋ kyɛnlɛ wono Jehoova yɛlɛ haali yelbilii bi- lii poɔŋ gba, ana soŋ boŋ saŋa na hoŋ wa tuoro yelnimizeere mine nendaare kaŋa na.

Naa Nebukadineezer da gaŋ zanne la zannoo kaŋa yɛlɛ naŋ taa dabeẽ yaga zaa, oŋ da boɔrɔ ka o baŋ a zannoo muni.
