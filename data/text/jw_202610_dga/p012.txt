YƐLƐ TE NAŊ NA ZANNE
<<<P>>>
A zannoo ŋa na soŋ teŋ ka te nyɛ yelseɛlɛ ata mine Jehoova naŋ erɛ zenɛ ŋa, aneŋ lɛ aŋ seŋ ka te de apɔɔsori na teŋ taa ka te soŋ ka ana yɛlɛ na nyɛ eebo na tegroŋ yaga zaa.
<<<P>>>
ŊMAA foto ho teɛroŋ poɔ a nyɛ ho naŋ poɔ a Izerayɛl biiri poɔŋ a are a Manzeɛ noɔreŋ kyɛ kaara lɛ Jehoova naŋ wɛle a kõɔ̃, ka ho neŋ o puorebiiri zaa kyɛnɛ a kõɔ̃ seŋsooreŋ ka Igyipiti sogya-re na koŋ toɔ̃ nyɔge yɛ. Nyɛ popeɛloŋ neŋ nɔmãã naŋ da na erɛ bo seŋ lɛ. Ŋmaa foto ho teɛroŋ poɔŋ a nyɛ Yezu naŋ sanna baa-leba a kyɛ sennɛ kũũni meŋ, nyɛ lɛ ho poɔ naŋ da naan pɛle seŋ. Nɔkpeene zaa kyebe ka sukyiri-sonne deme na naŋ nyɛ ana yel-seɛlɛ ŋa sagedieŋ ka Naaŋmen la vɛŋ aŋ e. (Mat. 15:31) Kyɛ ka hooŋ nyɛ, ana yelseɛlɛ ŋa zaa da eɛŋ dakoreŋ saŋa na. Azuŋ, Naaŋmen naŋ kyɛnɛ erɛɛ yelseɛlɛ mine zenɛ ŋa bee? Ɛɛ. Wala teŋ e baŋ lɛ? Yɛ vɛŋ ka te nyɛ.
<<<P>>>
2 A yelbiri “yelseɛlee” maŋ are koɛ yelkaŋa naŋ e tuo yaga a ko nensaaleba ka ba e bee ka ba baŋ ana yɛlɛ na pare te baare. A eɛ yɛlɛ Jehoova naŋ maŋ de o kpẽõ na e neŋ. Azuŋ, a zannoo ŋa poɔŋ, te na zanne nyɛɛ yelseɛlɛ ata mine Jehoova naŋ vɛŋ aŋ nyɛrɛ eebo o lantaapuoruu naŋ zenɛ ŋa. Te na baŋ yeliŋ ka a eɛ yelseɛlɛ bozuŋ Jehoova meŋe la vɛŋ aŋ erɛ. A yɛlɛ ata ŋa kaŋa zaa poɔŋ, te na maŋ nyɛɛ lɛ teŋ meŋ sonnɔ Jehoova aŋa o “ton-toŋtaaba” ka ana yelseɛlɛ na nyɛrɛ eebo.—Kanne 1 Korinti 3:9.

LƐ NA JEHOOVA PUOREBIIRI NAŊ MAALEŊ ERƐ YAGA NA EƐ YELSEƐLEE
<<<P>>>

A yi yuoni 1914, Jehoova puorebiiri da eɛ 5,000. Kyɛ a yi ana saŋa na waana zenɛ, a noba ŋa Aizeeya naŋ yeli ba yɛlɛ o daŋ-yelbo na poɔŋ ka ba eɛ “bile bile soba” na eɛ yaga ka ba noɔre te
