yelmennoŋ poɔŋ. Kyɛ a ba maŋ e mɔlɔ ka te erɛ lɛ. Bozuŋ a donɛɛ
yeltaree maŋ tagra teŋ yire Jehoova zieŋ. Azuŋ, aseŋ ka te kaara
soŋ a ta erɛ te yɛlɛ aŋa lɛ a donɛɛ noba naŋ erɛ na. Yelkaŋa aŋ seŋ
ka te kaara soŋ la zɔmenne na teŋ maŋ nyɔgrɔ na, agaŋaazaa baa-
paalba. Baapaalba mine maŋ teereŋ ka zɔmeŋɛ zaa eɛ zɔmeŋɛ, ka
ba na tõɔ̃ sagɛɛ o soba di bee ka ba koŋ tõɔ̃ sagoo di. Baapaalba
mine maŋ nyɛŋ ka aseŋ ka ba nyɔge noba ba ne ba yuomo naŋ seŋ
taa zɔmennoŋ, ka lɛ vɛŋ ka ba ne ba maŋ dire dama a kyaare yel-
nimizeere mine. Ba mine meŋ maŋ teereŋ ka a ba fɛre ka ba taa zɔ-
menne, bozuŋ ba maŋ boɔrɔŋ ka ba erɛ yɛlɛ ba yoŋ tegɛŋ. Kyɛ bo
teeroŋ ka Jehoova neŋ Yezu taa kyaare zɔmenne nyɔgebo?
