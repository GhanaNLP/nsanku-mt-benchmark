<<<P>>>
• Woviã agblemenuku siwo wodzrana la siwo fe xexlẽme nye miliɔn 20 la tsɔ do alɔ Agbledenyawo Dodo ɖe Ngɔ fe Đoɖo la. Esia wɔe be wodi dɔ na sɔhɛ 80,071 siwo nye ame siwo woxɔ ɖe dɔ me koŋ kple esiwo womexɔ ɖe dɔ me koŋ o la fe xexlẽme le Akotom, Adinkra, Techire, Akwatiakwaso, Fufuo kple Hohoe.
