LALA 12
Yehowa Ji Nyɔŋmɔ Ofe
<<<P>>>
NƆ NI WƆBAAKASE
Yɛ nikasemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaahã sanebimɔi ni nyiɛ sɛɛ nɛɛ ahetoo: Mɛni tsɔɔ akɛ mɔ ko le Yehowa? Mɛni hewɔ esa akɛ wɔya nɔ wɔkase ehe nii koni wɔle lɛ jogbaŋŋ? Ni te wɔbaafee tɛŋŋ wɔle lɛ jogbaŋŋ?
<<<P>>>
14
<<<P>>>
Yaa Nɔ Okase Yehowa He Nii Koni Ole Lɛ Jogbaŋŋ
<<<P>>>
“Bɔɔ mɔdɛŋ ni ole otsɛ Nyɔŋmɔ lɛ ni okɛ otsui muu fɛɛ asɔmɔ lɛ.”
—1 KRO. 28:9.
<<<P>>>
YEHOWA, wɔ-Nyɔŋmɔ lɛ, le nɔ fɛɛ nɔ, ehiɛ kã shi fe mɔ fɛɛ mɔ, ni efee nibii pii ni yɔɔ naakpɛɛ. Kɛ́ wɔjwɛŋ enɛɛmɛi fɛɛ ahe lɛ, wɔnaa kpɛɔ wɔhe. Bɔfo Paulo wie akɛ: “Kwɛ bɔ ni nii ni Nyɔŋmɔ yɔɔ lɛ fa kɛteke nɔ hã, kɛ bɔ ni ehiɛ kã shi waa, kɛ bɔ ni enilee lɛ mli kwɔ hã! Eyiŋkpɛi lɛ, anyɛɛɛ naa ana, ni egbɛi lɛ hu, anyɛɛɛ sɛɛ ana!” Ekolɛ bo hu onuɔ he tamɔ bɔ ni Paulo nu he lɛ.—Rom. 11:33, 34.
<<<P>>>
2 Eyɛ mli akɛ, wɔnyɛŋ Yehowa he nɔ fɛɛ nɔ wɔle moŋ, shi wɔbaanyɛ wɔle mɔ ni eji. Mɛni tsɔɔ nakai? Maŋtsɛ David kɛɛ ebinuu Solomon akɛ: “Bɔɔ mɔdɛŋ ni ole otsɛ Nyɔŋmɔ lɛ ni okɛ otsui muu fɛɛ asɔmɔ lɛ . . . Kɛ́ otao esɛɛ gbɛ lɛ, ebaahã ona lɛ.” (1 Kro. 28:9) Afii ohai komɛi asɛɛ lɛ, bɔfo Yohane kɛɛ nyɛmimɛi lɛ akɛ, amɛbaanyɛ amɛná “anɔkwafo lɛ he nilee.” (1 Yoh. 5:20) Yesu diɛŋtsɛ hu wie akɛ, ebaaye ebua mɛi ni ‘amɛle Tsɛ lɛ jogbaŋŋ.’—Mat. 11:27.
<<<P>>>
3 Mɛi fɛɛ ni sɔmɔɔ Yehowa bianɛ lɛ ekase ehe nibii pii momo. Fɛɛ sɛɛ lɛ, be fɛɛ be lɛ, nɔ ko yɛ kɛ̃ ni esa akɛ wɔkase yɛ Yehowa he. (Yoh. 17:3; kwɛmɔ shishigbɛ niŋmaa lɛ.) Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔkase ehe nii koni wɔna nɔ ni hãa enáa miishɛɛ kɛ nɔ ni baaye abua wɔ koni wɔhe lɛ wɔye waa.
<<<P>>>
1. Kɛ́ wɔjwɛŋ bɔ ni Yehowa le nɔ fɛɛ nɔ, ni efee nibii pii ni yɔɔ naakpɛɛ lɛ nɔ lɛ, te wɔnuɔ he wɔhãa tɛŋŋ?
<<<P>>>
2. Mɛni tsɔɔ akɛ wɔbaanyɛ wɔle mɔ ni Yehowa ji?
<<<P>>>
3. Mɛni he wɔbaasusu yɛ nikasemɔ nɛɛ mli?
