15 Wɔteŋ mɛi babaoo yɛ maji wuji ni tsei kɛ kooloi pii bɛ amɛmli lɛ amli. Kɛ́ oyɛ maŋ ko ni tamɔ nɛkɛ mli lɛ, te obaafee tɛŋŋ otsɔ adebɔɔ nibii anɔ okase Yehowa he nii? Nyɛmi nuu ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Patrick, ni yɔɔ Brazzaville, ni ji maŋ wulu ko ni yɔɔ Republic of the Congo lɛ mli lɛ wie akɛ: “Daa gbi lɛ, mibɔɔ mɔdɛŋ akɛ makwɛ nɔ ko ni Yehowa bɔ. Bei pii lɛ, minaa kooloi bibii tamɔ tsatsubii kɛ abɛi loo loofɔji bibii ni miitao niyenii loo he ni amɛbaafee amɛtsũ yɛ. Gbi ko lɛ, mina loofɔji ni amɛmiilo amɛtsũ. Amɛtaoɔ nibii ni sa yɛ akutso lɛ mli ni amɛkɛloɔ tsũ amɛhãa amɛbii bibii lɛ.” Shi Patrick kwɛɛɛ loofɔji nɛɛ kɛkɛ; ejwɛŋ Bɔlɔ lɛ he waa. Ewie akɛ: “Mina akɛ, Yehowa jwɛŋɔ ebɔɔ nii bibii lɛ po ahe. Enɛ wa mi ni mina akɛ, ejwɛŋɔ mi hu mihe, ni be fɛɛ be lɛ, ebaawa mi koni maná nibii ni he hiaa mi lɛ.”

16 Nɔ ni ji etɛ lɛ, kɛ́ wɔsusu gbɛi srɔtoi anɔ ni Yehowa eye ebua wɔ lɛ he lɛ, ebaahã wɔle lɛ jogbaŋŋ. Nakai ji bɔ ni Yesu mami Maria fee. No mli lɛ, ele Yehowa jogbaŋŋ ni ‘ehiɛ eba nyam yɛ Yehowa hiɛ.’ (Luka 1:30) Shi be ko lɛ, Yehowa bi lɛ ni etsɔ e-Bi lɛ mami. Enɛ ji nɔ ko ni he hiaa waa ni efeemɔ bɛ mlɛo. Yehowa tsɔ bɔfo Gabriel kɛ Maria wekunyo Elizabet nɔ ehã ena akɛ, eheɔ lɛ eyeɔ ni ebaaye
