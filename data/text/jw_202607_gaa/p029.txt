Yɛ nyɔji fioo komɛi asɛɛ lɛ, mishi kɛtee Japan wiemɔ asafo ko ni yɔɔ New York lɛ mli. Japan wiemɔ lɛ ni mikase lɛ hã minyɛ misɔmɔ Yehowa yɛ gbɛi srɔtoi anɔ. Yɛ 1989 lɛ, mitee kpokpaa nɔ kpee ko ni afee yɛ Japan wiemɔ mli lɛ yɛ Los Angeles, yɛ California. No ji klɛŋklɛŋ kpokpaa nɔ kpee ni mitee ni afee yɛ Japan wiemɔ mli. Mitsui mɔ nyɛmi yoo gbɛgbalɔ ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Miwako Onami ni fata mɛi ni fee drama lɛ ahe lɛ.
<<<P>>>
Mikɛ Miwako bote gbãla mli yɛ 1992, ni ahã efata mihe kɛsɔmɔ yɛ Brooklyn Betel. Miwako susuɔ mɔ he waa, enáa mɔ he tsui ni be fɛɛ be lɛ, eŋɔɔ hegbɛ kɛyeɔ ebuaa mɛi. Mikase bɔ ni akɛ mɛi yeɔ jogbaŋŋ ahãa yɛ edɛŋ. Minaa lɛ akɛ nikeenii ni jɛ Yehowa ŋɔɔ, ni ehãa mináa miishɛɛ be fɛɛ be.

Be ko sɛɛ lɛ, helai ni naa wa waa bamɔmɔ Miwako fɔlɔi ni no mli lɛ amɛyɔɔ Japan lɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, ahã wɔyasɔmɔ yɛ Japan nitsumɔhe nine lɛ koni wɔbɛŋkɛ amɛ ni wɔnyɛ wɔkwɛ amɛ. Wɔhiɛ sɔ suɔmɔ ni ajie kpo atsɔɔ wɔ nɛɛ waa. Wɔfà kɛtee jɛmɛ yɛ 1999.
<<<P>>>
Betel weku lɛ ni yɔɔ Japan lɛ hã wɔhiɛ mɛ, ni fiofio lɛ jɛmɛ shihilɛ baŋɔɔ wɔnaa. Nyɛmimɛi ni yɔɔ Japan lɛ yɛ sui kpakpai pii. Ekomɛi ji, amɛhãa mɔ nɔ waa, ni amɛfeɔ gbɔ hu waa, ni mikase amɛsui nɛɛ. Amɛkɛ nitsumɔ shwɛɛɛ kwraa, ni amɛhãaa nɔ ko agbala amɛjwɛŋmɔ. Agbɛnɛ hu, amɛle bɔ ni afeɔ ekome kɛtsuɔ nii. Nyɛle nɔ hewɔ ni miwieɔ nakai? Japanbii lɛ naa lɛ akɛ, ehi akɛ abaafee ekome kɛtsu nii moŋ fe ni mɔ kome baatsu fɛɛ kɛfee gbéi ehã ehe. Amɛsu kpakpa nɛɛ hã mina akɛ ehi waa akɛ maba mihe shi ni mikɛ mɛi afee ekome kɛtsu nii, ni agbɛnɛ hu, mihiɛ asɔ nɔ ko fioo ni minyɛɔ mifeɔ kɛyeɔ buaa koni Yehowa yiŋtoo aba mli lɛ.

Be ni be shwie mli lɛ, mitsake bɔ ni minaa amɛnifeemɔi komɛi mihãa lɛ, ni mibayɔse akɛ, kɛ́ wɔkɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔi ni Yehowa tsɔɔ esafo lɛ nɔ ekɛhãa wɔ lɛ tsu nii kome too lɛ, ebaahã yiŋ fɛɛ yiŋ ni wɔkpɛɔ lɛ aye omanye. Sane lɛ kɛkɛ ji, Yehowa ji mɔ ni hãa nitsumɔ ni wɔtsuɔ lɛ fɛɛ yeɔ omanye.
<<<P>>>
Bei pii lɛ, mikɛ tsɔsemɔ ni miná yɛ Betel lɛ toɔ tsɔsemɔ ni Mose ná lɛ he. Mose tsu nii akɛ tookwɛlɔ afii 40, ni Yehowa tsɔ no nɔ eye ebua lɛ koni eba ehe shi. Mi kɛ lɛ fɛɛ lɛ, wɔkase wolo kɛtee hiɛ. Wolo ni mikase kɛtee hiɛ lɛ hewɔ lɛ, miwo mihe nɔ ni mikɛ mihe fɔ̃ mi diɛŋtsɛ mihe nɔ. Shi Betel hã mina akɛ, esa akɛ maba mihe shi ni mikɛ mihe afɔ̃ Yehowa nɔ. Yehowa to etsui shi ni etsɔse Mose, ni nakai nɔŋŋ efee yɛ mi hu migbɛfaŋ yɛ afii ni eho nɛɛ fɛɛ amli.
