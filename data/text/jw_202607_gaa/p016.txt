8 Be ni wɔyaa nɔ wɔkaseɔ Yehowa he nii ni wɔbaleɔ lɛ jogbaŋŋ lɛ, naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ wɔ kɛ lɛ teŋ lɛ mli waa. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, kɛ́ onyiɛ gbɛjianɔ ni oto akɛ obaakane Biblia lɛ daa gbi lɛ sɛɛ, okwɛ JW Broadcasting nifeemɔi ni baa daa nyɔɔŋ nɔ lɛ eko, otee kpokpaa nɔ kpee loo kpokpaa wulu nɔ kpee lɛ, te no hãa onuɔ he ohãa tɛŋŋ yɛ Yehowa he? Bei pii lɛ, wɔnuɔ he akɛ, naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ wɔ kɛ wɔ-Tsɛ ni yɔɔ ŋwɛi lɛ teŋ lɛ mli ewa fe tsutsu lɛ. Mɛni hewɔ? Ejaakɛ wɔkaseɔ ehe nibii krokomɛi kɛfataa nɔ ni wɔle momo lɛ he, ni no tsirɛɔ wɔ ni wɔjieɔ eyi ni wɔdaa lɛ shi. Asaf susu bɔ ni Yehowa sumɔɔ lɛ waa lɛ he ni ewie akɛ: “Nyɔŋmɔ ji mitsui tɛsaa kɛ migbɛfaŋnɔ kɛmiiya naanɔ.” (Kanemɔ Lala 73:24-28.) Nakai nɔŋŋ ji bɔ ni wɔ hu wɔnuɔ he. Be fɛɛ be ni wɔbaabɔ mɔdɛŋ ni wɔle Yehowa jogbaŋŋ fe bɔ ni wɔle lɛ lɛ, wɔnaa akɛ eji naanyo ni nɔ bɛ kwraa, ni no hãa wɔhiɛ sɔɔ naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ wɔ kɛ lɛ teŋ lɛ waa.

9 Biblia lɛ kɛɔ wɔ akɛ, wɔjwɛŋ Yehowa he. (Abɛi 3:5, 6) Mɛni no tsɔɔ? Nɔ ni etsɔɔ ji, esa akɛ wɔsusu nibii ni wɔkase yɛ ehe kɛ bɔ ni enuɔ he ehãa yɛ wɔnifeemɔi ahe lɛ ahe. Kɛ́ wɔfee nakai lɛ, no baahã wɔkpɛ yiŋ kpakpai. Naa nɔ ko ni baanyɛ awa wɔ ni wɔnu enɛ shishi: Bei pii lɛ, nyɛmimɛi pii kɛ amɛwumɛi loo amɛŋamɛi mfonirii maa okpɔlɔ ni amɛtaa sɛɛ yɛ amɛ-ɔfis lɛ nɔ loo amɛkɛwoɔ amɛwalɛtii loo pɛɛsii amli loo amɛfɔŋ nɔ. No hãa amɛjwɛŋɔ amɛwumɛi lɛ loo amɛŋamɛi lɛ ahe daa, ni ekaiɔ amɛ akɛ amɛsumɔɔ amɛ waa. Ni no hewɔ lɛ, amɛkpɛɔ yiŋ ni baahã amɛwumɛi lɛ loo amɛŋamɛi lɛ aná miishɛɛ, ni amɛfeŋ nɔ ko ni baadɔ amɛ. Nakai nɔŋŋ kɛ́ wɔfɔɔ Yehowa hesusumɔ lɛ, ewaaa akɛ wɔbaakpɛ yiŋ ni baasa ehiɛ.—Lala 19:13, 14.

10 Yehowa bɔ wɔ tamɔ bɔ ni eyɔɔ lɛ, no hewɔ lɛ wɔbaanyɛ wɔkase lɛ. (1 Mo. 1:26) Be ni wɔyaa nɔ wɔkaseɔ Yehowa sui lɛ ahe nii lɛ, wɔbaana bɔ ni wɔbaafee wɔkase lɛ jogbaŋŋ. Enɛ ji nɔ ko ni woloŋmalɔi lɛ kɛ Farisifoi lɛ eyafeee. Amɛle Mla lɛ he saji fitsofitso. Shi Yesu kɛɛ, ‘amɛkuɔ amɛhiɛ amɛshwieɔ Mla lɛ mli nibii ni he hiaa titri lɛ anɔ, ni nomɛi ji jalɛsaneyeli kɛ mɔbɔnalɛ kɛ anɔkwayeli.’ (Mat. 23:23) Esaaa akɛ wɔfeɔ wɔnii tamɔ amɛ. Moŋ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔsusu nɔ ni mlai kɛ shishitoo mlai ni wɔkaseɔ yɛ Biblia lɛ mli lɛ hãa wɔleɔ yɛ Yehowa he lɛ he. Kɛ́ wɔfee nakai lɛ, no baahã wɔnyɛ wɔkase Yehowa.—1 Yoh. 4:8, 11.
