<<<P>>>
MƆ NI GBA JI KEN KIUCHI
<<<P>>>
MIHIƐ kpaŋ klɛŋklɛŋ gbi ni mibɔi nitsumɔ yɛ he ni awoɔ woji asɛɛ yɛ lɛ ni yɔɔ Brooklyn Betel yɛ New York, yɛ U.S.A lɛ nɔ kɔkɔɔkɔ. Be ni mibote he ni awoɔ woji lɛ asɛɛ yɛ lɛ, mihiɛ fee mi yaa yɛ bɔ ni tsɔne ni akɛwoɔ woji asɛɛ lɛ tsuɔ nii kɛ bɔ ni egbɛɔ ehãa lɛ he. Woji lɛ ehã mulu eyi kɔɔyɔɔ lɛ mli, ni mu ni akɛwoɔ tsɔji lɛ amli lɛ ehe jɛmɛ fɛɛ.
<<<P>>>
Mina nibii pii yɛ jɛmɛ, shi nɔ ni mɔ mijwɛŋmɔ waa ji, mɛi ni tsuɔ nii lɛ. Mina nyɛmimɛi oblahii kɛ oblayei ni miitsu nitsumɔi ni ewaaa akɛ etsumɔ baaje otsine. Eyɛ mli akɛ anaaa nɔ ni amɛtsuɔ lɛ moŋ, shi fɛɛ sɛɛ lɛ, amɛhiɛ sɔ nitsumɔ ni amɛtsuɔ lɛ ni amɛyɛ miishɛɛ. Amɛnɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ hã mina bɔ ni ajɛɔ heshibaa mli asɔmɔɔ Yehowa.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ afii ni eho nɛɛ amli lɛ, Yehowa etee nɔ etsɔse mi koni manyɛ masɔmɔ lɛ jogbaŋŋ. Shi dani maba nɔ nɔ lɛ, nyɛhãa magbaa nyɛ bɔ ni mishwe akɛ mana anɔkwale lɛ be ni miyɔɔ Buddha jamɔ mli lɛ.

MITAO YEHOWA SƐƐGBƐ BE NI MIDAKO
<<<P>>>
Afɔ mi yɛ Chicago, Illinois, yɛ U.S.A. Miyɛ nyɛmimɛi etɛ, ni miji onukpa. Kulɛ mifɔlɔi yɛ Japan, shi amɛyasusu akɛ United States baahã amɛná ogbɔjɔ shihilɛ, no hewɔ lɛ, amɛshi kɛtee jɛmɛ. Agbɛnɛ hu, amɛmiisumɔ ni amɛbii lɛ aya skul kpakpai ni amɛye omanye yɛ shihilɛ mli.
<<<P>>>
Mifɔlɔi kɛ Buddha jamɔ lɛ shwɛɛɛ kwraa. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, shĩa ni mimami da yɛ mli lɛ, Buddha sɔlemɔtsu yɛ jɛmɛ. Enyɛmimɛi hii enyɔ kɛ epapa sɔmɔ akɛ Buddha osɔfoi. Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, mi hu be ni midaa lɛ, mikɛ Buddha jamɔ lɛ shwɛɛɛ kwraa. Kɛ́ mijɛ skul miba lɛ, nɔ klɛŋklɛŋ ni mifeɔ ji, mishãa tsofa-kɛ-ŋma mihãa wɔ-Buddha amaga lɛ, ni mihãa lɛ omɔ kɛ nu. Kɛ́ eshɛ Hɔgbaa lɛ, wɔyaa Buddha sɔlemɔtsu ko mli ni wɔyajáa amaga ni yɔɔ jɛmɛ lɛ. Amaga nɛɛ da fe wɔnɔ lɛ, ni akɛ shika tsuru eha he fɛɛ.
<<<P>>>
Mikaiɔ akɛ, be ko be ni miye aaafee afii kpawo lɛ, miyadamɔ Buddha amaga lɛ ni yɔɔ wɔshĩa lɛ hiɛ, ni mibi mihe akɛ, ‘Kɛ́ migbo lɛ, nɛgbɛ maya?’ No mli lɛ mikase akɛ, kɛ́ migbo lɛ matsɔ mumɔ, ni mayahi shi yɛ mumɔi ajeŋ ko mli. Enɛ hã mina akɛ, be ko baashɛ ni miwala akɛ adesa lɛ baaba naagbee, ni kɛ́ mitsɔ mumɔ po lɛ, minyɛŋ mafee nibii ni adesai feɔ lɛ eko dɔŋŋ. Enɛ hã miwerɛ ho mihe waa ni minu he akɛ minyɛŋ nɔ ko mafee yɛ mishihilɛ lɛ he.
<<<P>>>
