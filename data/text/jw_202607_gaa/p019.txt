Sɛɛ mli lɛ, Maria susu nibii nɛɛ ni Yehowa fee kɛye ebua lɛ kɛ nɔ ni no hãa eleɔ yɛ Yehowa he lɛ ahe, ni ebana faŋŋ akɛ Yehowa yɛ hewalɛ waa, ekɛ mɛi fɛɛ yeɔ jogbaŋŋ, ni eshwɛɛɛ mɛi ni abuuu amɛ lɛ po ahe. (Luka 1:46-55) Maria hiɛ sɔ nɔ ni Yehowa fee ehã lɛ lɛ. Be fɛɛ be ni Yehowa baaye ebua wɔ lɛ, efeɔ nakai “yɛ be ni sa mli,” ni wɔ hu wɔhiɛ sɔɔ waa. (Heb. 4:16) Kɛ́ Yehowa ye ebua bo lɛ, bi ohe akɛ: ‘Mɛɛ sui Yehowa ejie kpo etsɔɔ mi yɛ sane nɛɛ mli?’

WƆBAAYA NƆ WƆKASE YEHOWA HE NII
<<<P>>>
17 Yɛ jeŋ hee lɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaakase Yehowa he nii babaoo. Ebaahã wɔ wolokpoi hei ni baahã wɔna bɔ ni esumɔɔ ni wɔhi shi wɔhã. (Kpo. 20:12) Agbɛnɛ hu, wɔkɛ nibii ni ebɔ lɛ baashãra jogbaŋŋ ni no baahã wɔkase ehe nii pii. (Yes. 11:6-9) Wɔbaale ehe nibii hei hu.
<<<P>>>
Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, wɔbaana be ni eteɔ wɔnanemɛi kɛ wɔwekumɛi lɛ ashi lɛ. Kɛfata he lɛ, wɔbaana bɔ ni ebaafee eye ebua wɔ koni wɔye emuu.
<<<P>>>
18 Be fɛɛ be lɛ, nɔ ko kɛ̃ yɛ ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔkase yɛ Yehowa he. Eyɛ kɛjɛ naanɔ, no hewɔ lɛ, “eniiashishinumɔ lɛ, anyɛɛɛ shishi ana.” (Lala 90:2; 147:5) Wɔnyɛŋ wɔná wɔle “nitsumɔ ni anɔkwa Nyɔŋmɔ lɛ tsuɔ kɛjɛɔ shishijee kɛyaa naagbee lɛ.” (Jaj. 3:11) No hewɔ lɛ, ekɔɔɔ he eko afii abɔ ni wɔkɛhi anɔkwale lɛ mli lɛ, esa akɛ wɔya nɔ wɔkase wɔ-Tsɛ ni yɔɔ suɔmɔ lɛ he nii.

17-18. Mɛni hewɔ be fɛɛ be lɛ, nɔ ko kɛ̃ yɛ ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔkase yɛ Yehowa he lɛ?

NƆ NI WƆNAA YƐ MFONIRII LƐ AMLI Wolo lɛ hiɛ: Ashɛ Yeremia afɔ̃ bu ko mli, shi Ebed-melek fee ekãa ni eyajie lɛ. Baafa 18: Nyɛmi nuu ko ni sheɔ gbeyei waa kɛ́ ená nifeemɔ lɛ miijweŋ bɔ ni Yehowa ye ebua lɛ ni enyɛ efee nifeemɔ ko ni akɛhã lɛ yɛ otsi teŋ kpee shishi lɛ he.
