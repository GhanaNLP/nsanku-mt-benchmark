“Oshɛrɛ mihe eshai lɛ fɛɛ oshwie osɛɛ”
<<<P>>>
Bɔ ni wɔji bianɛ lɛ ni Yehowa kwɛɔ, shi jeee bɔ ni wɔji be ko ni eho (Kwɛmɔ kuku 11)
<<<P>>>
lɛ. Nakai nɔŋŋ wɔ hu kɛ́ wɔfee esha ni wɔtsake wɔtsui lɛ, Yehowa kɛkeɔ wɔ, ni eyeɔ ebuaa wɔ ni wɔya nɔ wɔsɔmɔ lɛ kɛ henilee ni he tse. Mɛni ekoŋŋ awie yɛ Yesaia wolo lɛ mli ni baanyɛ ashɛje wɔmii kɛ́ wɔhenilee miiyi wɔ waa yɛ esha ko ni wɔfee be ko ni eho lɛ hewɔ?
<<<P>>>
10 Kanemɔ Yesaia 38:17 kɛ shishigbɛ niŋmaa. Abaanyɛ atsɔɔ wiemɔ ni ji “oshɛrɛ mihe eshai lɛ fɛɛ oshwie osɛɛ” lɛ shishi hu akɛ, “ojie mihe eshai lɛ fɛɛ kɛje ohiɛ.” Yɛ ŋmalɛ nɛɛ mli lɛ, wɔnaa akɛ, kɛ́ mɔ ko ni efee esha lɛ tsake etsui lɛ, Yehowa shɛrɛɔ mɔ lɛ he eshai lɛ eshwieɔ he ko ni enaŋ loo ejwɛŋmɔ baŋ nɔ dɔŋŋ. Wolo ko tsɔɔ wiemɔ nɛɛ shishi akɛ: “Ohã efee tamɔ nɔ ni mifeko esha ko.” Enɛ tsɔɔ akɛ, kɛ́ Yehowa kɛ wɔhe eshai ke wɔ lɛ, enaa wɔ tamɔ nɔ ni wɔfeko esha ko. No hewɔ lɛ, egbalaaa wɔtoi yɛ he ejaakɛ
<<<P>>>
Kaahã tɔmɔi komɛi ni otɔ̃ be ko ni eho lɛ hã omiishɛɛ tã
<<<P>>>
ekaiii dɔŋŋ. Ewo shi akɛ, ‘ekaiŋ wɔhe eshai lɛ dɔŋŋ.’ (Yes. 43:25) Enɛ shɛjeɔ mɔmii waa, aloo jeee nakai? Kɛ́ Yehowa, Kojolɔ Ni Fe Fɛɛ lɛ, kaiii wɔhe eshai lɛ, no lɛ, esa akɛ wɔ hu wɔjie yɛ wɔjwɛŋmɔ mli, ni esaaa akɛ wɔhãa wɔhenilee yiɔ wɔ yɛ he.
<<<P>>>
11 Kɛ́ wɔhenilee yi wɔ waa yɛ esha ko ni wɔfee be ko ni eho lɛ he lɛ, esa akɛ wɔkai shi ni Yehowa ewo wɔ lɛ. Kɛ́ ekɛ wɔhe eshai ke wɔ lɛ, ekɛke wɔ kwraa. Yehowa enaaa wɔ akɛ mɛi ni hiɛ eshwie shi yɛ esha ko ni wɔfee be ko ni eho lɛ hewɔ, ni efeee enii tamɔ adesai ni yeee emuu ni bɛɔ mɔ amɛwoɔ amɛmli lɛ. (Yes. 57:16) Jeee bɔ ni wɔji be ko ni eho lɛ he hiaa Yehowa, moŋ lɛ, nɔ titri ni he hiaa lɛ ji
