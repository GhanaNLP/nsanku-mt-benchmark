4 Wɔshihilɛ baanyɛ atsake be ni wɔkpaaa gbɛ. (Jaj. 9:11; 1 Kor. 7:31) Hela baanyɛ amɔ wɔ, wɔhe baanyɛ afi wɔ, basabasafee-mɔ baanyɛ aba he ni wɔyɔɔ lɛ, aloo abaanyɛ atsake nɔ ni wɔtsuɔ yɛ Yehowa sɔɔmɔ lɛ mli lɛ. Kɛ́ eba lɛ nakai lɛ, ebaanyɛ ehã wɔye wɔ-tsui loo wɔfee basaa. Ekolɛ wɔbaabi wɔhe akɛ: ‘Mɛni mafee?’ Be ni nyɛmi nuu ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Joseph lɛ esɔmɔ yɛ Betel aaafee afii 17 sɛɛ lɛ, ahã eyasɔmɔ akɛ gbɛgbalɔ. Ewie akɛ: “Mifee basaa. No mli lɛ, mileee he ni miyaje shishi kɛjɛ loo nɔ ni baaba minɔ, ejaakɛ misɔmɔ yɛ Betel afii pii.” Kɛ́ wɔshihi-lɛ tsake be ni wɔkpaaa gbɛ lɛ, mɛɛ saji Yeho-wa ehã aŋma yɛ Yesaia wolo lɛ mli ni baanyɛ ashɛje wɔmii?

4. Kɛ́ wɔshihilɛ tsake be ni wɔkpaaa gbɛ lɛ, te ekolɛ wɔ-baanu he wɔhã tɛŋŋ?
<<<P>>>
5 Kanemɔ Yesaia 42:16. Yehowa ewo shi akɛ, ebaanyiɛ mɛi ni yɛ gbɛ ko nɔ lɛ amɛta-mɔ shwilafoi lɛ ahiɛ kɛtsɔmɔ gbɛi ni amɛtsɔ-mɔ ko nɔ dã lɛ anɔ, ni ebaahã amɛhiɛ duŋ lɛ atsɔ la. Kɛ́ wɔshihilɛ tsake be ni wɔkpaaa gbɛ lɛ, ekolɛ wɔbaanu he tamɔ “shwilafoi” ni yaatsɔ ‘gbɛ ni nɔ ewo duŋ ni yɔɔ gɔŋ kɛ jɔɔ gɔŋ kɛ jɔɔ ni amɛtsɔko nɔ dã’ lɛ nɔ. Wɔbaa-nyɛ wɔnu he hu tamɔ nɔ ni wɔnyiɛ koo hayii ko mli ni wɔnaaa gbɛ ni esa akɛ wɔtsɔ nɔ. Shi Yehowa ewo shi akɛ, ekɔɔɔ he eko shihilɛ ni wɔkɛbaakpe lɛ, eshiŋ wɔ ni ebaatsɔɔ wɔ gbɛ hu. Ebaamɔ wɔnine mli ni enyiɛ wɔhiɛ kɛtsɔ duŋ lɛ mli, ni ehã gbɛ ni wɔnyiɛ nɔ lɛ nɔ akpé. Agbɛnɛ hu, ebaahã gbɛ ni yɔɔ gɔŋ kɛ jɔɔ gɔŋ kɛ jɔɔ ni wɔnyiɛ nɔ lɛ afee trɛnɛɛ. Mɛɛ gbɛ nɔ ebaatsɔ efee enɛ? Ebaatsɔ e-Wie-mɔ lɛ kɛ esafo lɛ nɔ ewo wɔ ŋaa kpakpa.
