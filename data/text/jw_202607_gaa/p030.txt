Agbɛnɛ hu, wɔyaa helatsamɔhe lɛ kpitiokpitio. Masako, ni ji Miwako mami hela lɛ mli wo wu aahu akɛ eshɛ he ko ni nyiɛmɔ po bawa kɛhã lɛ. Enɛ hã ebawa waa kɛhã lɛ akɛ ebaaya asafoŋ kpeei kɛ shiɛmɔ. Otsii fioo dani egbo lɛ, ebɔi helatsɛmɛi ashwiili mlitamɔ. Yɛ shihilɛi nɛɛ fɛɛ amli lɛ, suɔmɔ ni eyɔɔ kɛhã Yehowa lɛ nɔ gbɔɔɔ. Be fɛɛ be ni ebaawie anɔkwale lɛ he lɛ, ekɛ miishɛɛ ko wieɔ. Ekɔɔɔ he eko shihilɛ ni Masako baatsɔ mli lɛ, eyeee etsui fe nine ni ehãaa emiishɛɛ atã hu, ni enɛ woɔ mi hewalɛ waa.

Yɛ 2024 lɛ, wɔshihilɛ tsake kwraa. Be ni wɔkɛ nɔ ni fa fe afii 30 esɔmɔ yɛ Betel sɛɛ lɛ, ahã wɔyasɔmɔ akɛ gbɛgbalɔi krɛdɛi. Bianɛ lɛ, wɔmiisɔmɔ akɛ gbɛgbalɔi krɛdɛi yɛ ŋshɔkpɔ ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Okinawa lɛ nɔ, ni wɔyeɔ wekui ni yɔɔ asraafoi nsara ko ni bɛŋkɛ wɔ lɛ mli lɛ odase. Tsɔsemɔ ni wɔná yɛ Betel lɛ ye ebua wɔ ni wɔnyɛ wɔtsake wɔhe wɔwo shihilɛ hee nɛɛ mli jogbaŋŋ. Wɔto wɔbe he gbɛjianɔ jogbaŋŋ. Ni bɔ ni afee ni wɔkɛ wɔbe atsu nii jogbaŋŋ lɛ, wɔkɛ wɔjwɛŋmɔ yaa shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ nɔ waa ni wɔtsuɔ lɛ kɛmɔɔ shi. Yehowa edro wɔ Biblia mli nikaselɔi ni ebatsɔmɔ tamɔ wɔwekumi. Wɔle akɛ ehe hiaa waa ni wɔsumɔ mɛi. Shi bianɛ lɛ, shihilɛ ni wɔtsɔɔ mli lɛ hãa anɔkwa sane nɛɛ feɔ faŋŋ fe tsutsu lɛ po. Mi kɛ Miwako daa Yehowa shi daa yɛ nitsumɔ ni nɔ bɛ nɛɛ ni ekɛwo wɔdɛŋ lɛ hewɔ.

MITSWA MIFAI SHI AKƐ, MAHÃ YEHOWA AYA NƆ ATSƆSE MI
<<<P>>>
Miwekumɛi lɛ eteee shi amɛwooo mi dɔŋŋ, shi amɛbatsɔmɔko Yehowa jálɔi hu. Shi Yesu wo shi akɛ, mɛi ni shiɔ amɛwekumɛi ni amɛbatsɔmɔɔ ekaselɔi lɛ, baaná nanemɛi kaselɔi toi 100 ni baabatsɔmɔ tamɔ amɛwekumɛi. (Mar. 10:29, 30) Enɛ eba mli yɛ migbɛfaŋ. Minyɛŋ makane wekui abɔ fɛɛ ni yɔɔ heihei yɛ jeŋ fɛɛ ni ejie suɔmɔ kpo amɛtsɔɔ mi ni amɛkɛ mi eye tamɔ amɛwekumɛi lɛ.

Miyɛ Anɔkwale wolo lɛ ni Wilson kɛhã mi lɛ yɛ miwoji lɛ amli lolo. Nakai wolo bluu lɛ hãa mikaiɔ bɔ ni Yehowa kɛ mi ye jogbaŋŋ ni ehã mibale lɛ lɛ. (1 Kro. 28:9) Mitswa mifai shi akɛ, mahã Yehowa aya nɔ atsɔse mi. Mitswa mifai shi hu akɛ, maye mabua mɛi ni amɛbakase anɔkwale ni baahã amɛná naanɔ wala lɛ.
