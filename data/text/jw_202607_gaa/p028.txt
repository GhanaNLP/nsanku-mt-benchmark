MIKASE NIBII KOMƐI NI HE HIAA YƐ BROOKLYN BETEL
<<<P>>>
Be ni mikɛ Wilson ehi shi afii enyɔ sɛɛ lɛ, afɔ̃ mi nine ni mibasɔmɔ yɛ Brooklyn Betel yɛ 1985. Tamɔ bɔ ni miwie kɛjɛ shishi lɛ, ahã miyatsu nii yɛ he ni awoɔ woji asɛɛ yɛ lɛ. Be ko be ni mikɛ tsɔne ko miitsu nii lɛ, mifee nɔ ko ni kulɛ esaaa akɛ mifeɔ. Enɛ hã woji ni akɛwoɔ woji asɛɛ lɛ hai abɔ fite ni ebabi ni ashɛrɛ fɛɛ ashwie. Nyɛmimɛi lɛ kɛɛ miyakɛɛ wɔnɔkwɛlɔ lɛ nɔ ni eba lɛ. Mitee ni miyagbala nɔ ni ba lɛ mli fitsofitso mitsɔɔ lɛ, shi eshwɛ mi nɔ ko ni mafee. No hewɔ lɛ, wɔnɔkwɛlɔ lɛ kɛ naajɔlɛ kai mi akɛ, “Be fɛɛ be lɛ, ehi akɛ ké otɔ̃ lɛ okpa fai.” No hewɔ lɛ, yɛ nakai ŋmɛlɛtswaa lɛ nɔ nɔŋŋ lɛ, mikase akɛ, ké mitɔ̃ lɛ, ehe miihia ni makpɛlɛ nɔ ni makpa fai.
<<<P>>>
Agbɛnɛ hu, bɔ ni nyɛmimɛi ni edara ni etsɛ yɛ Betel lɛ fee amɛnii amɛhã lɛ hã mikase bɔ ni asɔmɔɔ mɛi jogbaŋŋ ahãa. Gbi ko lɛ, Nyɛminuu Milton Henschel, ni eji Gbɛtsɔɔmɔ Kuu lɛ mli nyo lɛ ta wɔkpɔlɔ lɛ he kɛhã shwane niyeli. Nakai gbi lɛ, mɛi babaoo ba niyeli fe bɔ ni nyɛmimɛi lɛ kpa gbɛ, ni no hã mɛi ni jaa niyenii lɛ gbɛ. Wɔ, ni ji oblahii ni tara okpɔlɔ lɛ he lɛ bɔi tɔkemɔ bibiibibii yɛ bɔ ni niyenii lɛ sɛɛ etsɛ lɛ hewɔ. Be ni wɔyaana lɛ, Nyɛminuu Henschel ewo ehe nɔ, ni eyafata mɛi ni jaa niyenii lɛ ahe kɛmiija niyenii lɛ. Mihiɛ kpaaa bɔ ni eba ehe shi ni esɔmɔ lɛ nɔ kɔkɔɔkɔ. Enɛ hã mikai bɔ ni Yesu sɔmɔ ebɔfoi lɛ.—Yoh. 13:3-5.
