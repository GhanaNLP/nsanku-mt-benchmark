12 Klɛŋklɛŋ lɛ, kɛ́ wɔkase Yehowa Wiemɔ lɛ, no baahã wɔle ehe saji krokomɛi. Bei komɛi lɛ, Biblia lɛ tsɔ̃ɔ Yehowa sui lɛ atã. (2 Mo. 34:6, 7) Shi bei pii lɛ, Biblia lɛ wieɔ saji ni kɔɔ mɛi ahe kɛ yiŋ ni mɛi nɛɛ kpɛ lɛ ahe. Kɛ́ okane saji ni tamɔ nakai lɛ, susumɔ he jogbaŋŋ koni no ahã okase nɔ ko yɛ Yehowa he.
<<<P>>>
13 Hã wɔkɛ amaniɛ ni abɔ yɛ Yeremia 38:6-13 lɛ afee enɛ he nɔkwɛmɔnɔ. Ŋmalɛ nɛɛ hãa wɔnaa akɛ, Ebed-melek, Etiopianyo lɛ, hã ajie gbalɔ Yeremia kɛje bu ko mli. Ŋwanejee ko bɛ he akɛ, kɛ́ okane sane nɛɛ, ewaaa akɛ obaana akɛ Ebed-melek kɛ ehe shã afɔle, efee ekãa, ni eyɛ suɔmɔ kɛhã Yehowa jálɔi, ni obaasumɔ ni okase lɛ. Shi ani oona Yehowa sui komɛi yɛ Ebed-melek sane lɛ mli? (Kanemɔ Yeremia 39:15-18.) Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, mɛni yɔɔ Yeremia 39:15-18 lɛ mli ni hãa onaa akɛ Yehowa hiɛ sɔɔ nɔ ni wɔfeɔ wɔhãa lɛ lɛ, eyeɔ jalɛ sane loo ejieee nii amli? Mɛɛ Yehowa sui krokomɛi sane nɛɛ hãa onaa? Be fɛɛ be ni obaakane sane ko yɛ Nyɔŋmɔ Wiemɔ lɛ mli lɛ, kwɛmɔ akɛ mɛɛ Yehowa sui nakai sane lɛ hãa onaa, ni oda lɛ shi, ni变革 jie eyi yɛ nakai sui lɛ ahewɔ.
<<<P>>>
14 Nɔ ni ji enyɔ lɛ, kɛ́ wɔsusu nibii ni Yehowa ebɔ lɛ ahe jogbaŋŋ lɛ, ebaahã wɔkase Yehowa he nɔ ko. (Lala 145:9, 10) No hewɔ lɛ, esaaa akɛ wɔkwɛɔ nibii ni Yehowa ebɔ lɛ kɛkɛ, moŋ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔsusu amɛhe jogbaŋŋ. Mɛi kɛ afii pii kaseɔ kooloi, tsei, kɛ adebɔɔ nibii krokomɛi ahe nii, shi amɛnaaa akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ ko yɛ, lɛ ebɔ nibii nɛɛ, ni eyɛ sui fɛfɛji. Bɔfo Paulo ŋma akɛ, Nyɔŋmɔ sui ni anaaa lɛ, “kɛjɛ jeŋbɔɔ mli beebe lɛ anaa faŋŋ, ejaakɛ atsɔɔ nibii ni efee lɛ anɔ ayɔseɔ enɛɛmɛi.” (Rom. 1:20) Atsɔɔ ŋmalɛ nɛɛ shishi yɛ Biblia ko mli akɛ, “mɔ ni jwɛŋɔ jogbaŋŋ lɛ baanyɛ ana Nyɔŋmɔ sui lɛ.”
