14 Kanemɔ Yesaia 26:19. Jeee gbele sɛɛ pɛ kɛkɛ Yehowa baafo, shi moŋ ebaahã mɛi ni wɔsumɔɔ amɛsane ni egboi lɛ aba wala mli ekoŋŋ. Naa nɔ ni ewie yɛ mɛi ni egboi lɛ ahe. Ewie akɛ: “Migbohii lɛ baate shi.” Enɛ tsɔɔ akɛ, Yehowa naa amɛ akɛ enii, ehiɛ kpaŋ amɛnɔ, ni yɛ ebe ni eto lɛ mli lɛ, ebaatee amɛ shi kɛba wala mli. Bɔ ni nu hãa tsei ni egboi lɛ tsɛ̃ɔ ekoŋŋ lɛ, nakai nɔŋŋ Yehowa, Wala Fɛɛ Jɛɛhe lɛ, baatsĩɛ mɛi ni wɔsumɔɔ amɛsane ni egboi lɛ ahiɛ kɛba wala mli ekoŋŋ. Kwɛ bɔ ni wɔmii baashɛ wɔhe waa wɔhã be ni wɔhereɔ amɛ atuu lɛ!—Okɛto Marko 5:42 lɛ he.

15 Eyɛ mli akɛ wɔheɔ shi ni Nyɔŋmɔ ewo akɛ ebaatee gbohii ashi lɛ wɔyeɔ moŋ, shi kɛ́ mɔ ko ni wɔsumɔɔ esane lɛ gbo lɛ, edɔɔ wɔ waa. Shi wɔle akɛ Yehowa hiɛ kpako amɛnɔ, ni enɛ shɛjeɔ wɔmii waa. Hiɛnɔkamɔ nɛɛ shɛje Michael ni wɔtsĩ etã yɛ kuku 12 lɛ mli lɛ mii. Ewie akɛ: “Yesaia 26:19 lɛ ye ebua mi ni mina akɛ Yehowa shwɛɛɛ mifɔlɔi lɛ ahe kwraa. Ehiɛ kpaŋ amɛnɔ, ni ebaatee amɛ shi wɔsɛɛ. Enɛ woɔ mi hewalɛ ni eshɛjeɔ mimii.” No hewɔ lɛ, ehi waa akɛ yɛ be kɛ beiaŋ lɛ, wɔbaafee gbohiiashitee lɛ he mfoniri yɛ wɔjwɛŋmɔ mli. Kɛ́ wɔfee nakai lɛ, ebaashɛje wɔmii.
<<<P>>>
16 Yɛ nikasemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ, wɔna ŋmalɛi fioo komɛi yɛ Yesaia wolo lɛ mli ni baanyɛ ashɛje wɔmii kɛ́ wɔshihilɛ tsake be ni wɔkpaaa gbɛ, wɔhenilee miiyi wɔ waa yɛ esha ko ni wɔfee be ko ni eho lɛ hewɔ, kɛ be ni mɔ ko ni wɔsumɔɔ esane lɛ egbo. Eŋɔɔ wɔnaa waa akɛ Yehowa tsɔɔ Biblia lɛ nɔ eshɛjeɔ wɔmii! (Lala 94:19) Ani oyɛ ŋmalɛ ko ni ona akɛ eshɛjeɔ omii kɛ́ ohao? Kɛ́ nakai lɛ, kpe owo oyitsɔŋ loo ŋmaa owo wolo ko nɔ ni okɛkpɛtɛ he ko ni ewaaa akɛ obaana. Eba akɛ, wiemɔi nɛɛ ni jɛ Yehowa, wɔ-Tsɛ ni yɔɔ ŋwɛi ni sumɔɔ wɔ lɛ, ŋɔɔ lɛ baaya nɔ ashɛje omii.
