nyɛ ahã sanebimɔi ni miyɔɔ yɛ Nyɔŋmɔ, wala, kɛ gbele he lɛ ahetoo. No hewɔ lɛ, mihé King James Version Biblia momo ko. Ani ole he ni mihé yɛ? Mihé yɛ Buddhabii agbijurɔyeli ko shishi; mibɔleee kwraa akɛ abaahɔ̃ɔ Biblia yɛ he ko ni tamɔ nakai.
<<<P>>>
Be ni miye afii 17 lɛ, mikaiɔ akɛ, gbi ko lɛ, mikɛ migbee lɛ miishara shi yɛ gbɛ lɛ he kɛkɛ ni nuu ko tsi ebɛŋkɛ mi. Atsɛɔ nuu nɛɛ Wilson Bashou. Ebi mi akɛ, “Ani ole nɔ ni Nyɔŋmɔ ewo shi akɛ wɔsɛɛ lɛ ebaafee?” Sanebimɔ nɛɛ hã wɔsusu ŋmalɛi komɛi ahe. Wilson kane Kpojiemɔ 17:1 lɛ, ni ebi mi akɛ, “Mɛni ‘nui babaoo’ lɛ ni ashawo kpetenkpele lɛ ta nɔ lɛ damɔ shi kɛhã?” Shi minyɛɛɛ matsɔɔ lɛ. Kɛkɛ ni ekane kuku 15 lɛ ehã mi. Awie yɛ jɛmɛ akɛ, nui lɛ damɔ shi kɛhã mɛi. Minya bɔ ni kɛ́ Yehowa Odasefoi lɛ kane ŋmalɛ ko ni shishinumɔ wa lɛ, amɛkɛ ŋmalɛi krokomɛi gbálaa mli koni anyɛ anu shishi lɛ he waa. Be ni wɔgba sane lɛ wɔtã lɛ, Wilson hã mi wolo ko, ni ji Anɔkwale Ni Kɛ Mɔ Yaa Naanɔ Wala Mli Lɛ. Nakai wolo bluu bibioo lɛ tsake mishihilɛ kwraa.
<<<P>>>
Gbii nyɔŋma sɛɛ lɛ, mitee asafoŋ kpee klɛŋklɛŋ kwraa yɛ Maŋtsɛyeli Asa lɛ nɔ. Mihiɛ kpaŋ bɔ ni nyɛmimɛi lɛ here mi hiɛmɛɛ lɛ nɔ kɔkɔɔkɔ. Kɛjɛ nakai gbi lɛ nɔ kɛbaa lɛ mikwɛko misɛɛ dɔŋŋ. Sɛɛ mli lɛ, Wilson kɛ mi bɔi Biblia lɛ kasemɔ. Minya Biblia lɛ ni mikaseɔ lɛ he aahu akɛ, bei komɛi lɛ, wɔkɛ ŋmɛlɛtswai kpaanyɔ sɔŋŋ kaseɔ nii. Enɛ gba mifɔlɔi anaa waa, ni amɛbɔ mɔdɛŋ akɛ amɛbaahã makpa nii lɛ kasemɔ. Shi be ni mitee nɔ mikase nii lɛ, mibahe miye waa akɛ anɔkwale lɛ nɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, yɛ 1983 lɛ, mihã abaptisi mi.

MIKPA YUNIVƐSITI KONI MANYƐ MATSU GBƐGBAMƆ NITSUMƆ LƐ
<<<P>>>
Be ni abaptisi mi lɛ, no mli lɛ miiya yunivɛsiti ko ni ehe gbɛ́i waa koni mibatsɔ datrɛfonyo. Mifɔlɔi kɛ nibii pii shã afɔle koni manyɛ maya skul nɛɛ. Kulɛ manyɛ maya kɛyashi magbe naa, shi no mli lɛ miitao ni mikɛ minyɛmɔ fɛɛ kɛ mibe fɛɛ asɔmɔ Yehowa.
<<<P>>>
Be ko lɛ, mipapa kɛɛ mi akɛ, “Kɛ́ okpa yunivɛsiti lɛ yaa lɛ, mashwie bo kɛshi shĩa!” Enɛ hã mihao ni mifee basaa. Misumɔɔ mifɔlɔi waa, ni misumɔɔɔ ni mifeɔ nɔ ko ni baakumɔ amɛtsui. No hewɔ lɛ, bei pii kɛ́ je na lɛ, mijeeɔ kpo kɛyaa skul lɛ yale lɛ mli ni miyasɔleɔ mikɛɔ Yehowa akɛ, “Yehowa, ofainɛ, ye obua mi ni mana nɔ ni mafee.” Be ni agbɛnɛ lɛ, mikpɛ miyiŋ akɛ miyaŋ yunivɛsiti lɛ dɔŋŋ lɛ, mipapa shwie mi kɛshi shĩa lɛ. Mitswa Nyɛminuu Bashou ni mikɛɛ lɛ nɔ ni eba lɛ. Ekɛɛ mi akɛ manyɛ mabahi emasɛi. Mito minibii fɛɛ miwo baagi kome mli ni mifo tsɔne kɛtee he ni eyɔɔ lɛ. Minu he tamɔ nɔ ni jatsu wulu ko eje minɔ. Mina akɛ mikpɛ yiŋ kpakpa.
<<<P>>>
Minu he tamɔ loofɔlɔ ko ni eje akpa ko mli! Agbɛnɛ lɛ, manyɛ mikɛ mibe fɛɛ asɔmɔ Yehowa ni nɔ ko gbalaaa mijwɛŋmɔ. No hewɔ lɛ, yɛ 1984 lɛ, mije gbɛgbamɔ nitsumɔ lɛ shishi.
<<<P>>>
Be ni misɔmɔɔ akɛ gbɛgbalɔ lɛ, Yehowa ye ebua mi ni miná sui kpakpai pii. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, eye ebua mi ni minijiaŋ akaje wui oya kɛ́ nibii eyaaa lɛ jogbaŋŋ.
