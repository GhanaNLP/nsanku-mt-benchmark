6 Joseph, ni wɔtsĩ etã yɛ kuku ejwɛ lɛ mli lɛ
wie bɔ ni Yehowa tsɔɔ akɛ esumɔɔ lɛ kɛ eŋa
lɛ ehãa, kɛ bɔ ni eye ebua amɛ be ni amɛshi
Betel lɛ he. Ewie akɛ: “Be ni wɔshiɔ Betel lɛ,
wɔna akɛ Yehowa sumɔɔ wɔ, ni eye ebua wɔ.
Etsɔ wɔnyɛmimei ni nɔ bɛ lɛ anɔ eye ebua
wɔ. Ehã wɔná nibii ni he baahia wɔ lɛ, ni
esheje wɔmii hu yɛ be ni sa mli, ni eeya nɔ ee-
fee nakai lolo.” Bo hu kɛ́ oshihilɛ tsake be ni
okpaaa gbɛ lɛ, ná ole akɛ, Yehowa baawaje
bo ni etsɔɔ bo gbɛ kɛ́ etamɔ nɔ ni tsakemɔ lɛ
naa wa po. (Yes. 54:10; 58:11) Joseph wie
hu akɛ: “Misumɔɔ asafo lɛ lalai krokomɛi lɛ
ekome ko waa. Emli wiemɔi lɛ eko kɛɛ, ‘Gbɔ-
mɔ ko bɛ ni le wɔsɛɛ sane. Shi Nyɔŋmɔ kɛ mi
yɛ; mitsui efaŋ.’* Yɛ anɔkwale mli lɛ, ekolɛ
nibii eyaŋ lɛ bɔ ni wɔbaakpa gbɛ lɛ mɔŋ, shi
sane ko yɛ ni tsakeee, no ji akɛ: Ekɔɔɔ he eko
shihilɛ ni wɔkɛbaakpe lɛ, Yehowa shiŋ wɔ kɔ-
kɔɔkɔ!”

7. Te mɛi komɛi ni fee esha be ko ni eho lɛ nuɔ he amɛhãa
tɛŋŋ?
<<<P>>>
ejaakɛ amɛsumɔɔ wɔ. No sɛɛ lɛ, wɔfeɔ bɔ fɛɛ
bɔ ni wɔbaanyɛ koni wɔkayafee nakai esha lɛ
ekoŋŋ. (Abɛi 28:13; Yak. 5:14-16; 1 Yoh. 1:9)
Kɛ́ Yehowa kɛ esha ni wɔfee lɛ ke wɔ lɛ, ekolɛ
fɛɛ sɛɛ lɛ, wɔhenilee baaya nɔ ayi wɔ. Ekolɛ
wɔbaakɛɛ wɔhe akɛ, ‘Nɔ ni mifee nɛɛ Yeho-
wa kɛkeŋ mi kɔkɔɔkɔ.’ (2 Kor. 2:7) Naagba
bɛ he akɛ wɔbaanu he nakai, ejaakɛ ebaawa
waa akɛ wɔhiɛ baaje esha ni wɔfee be ko ni
eho lɛ nɔ, ni ekolɛ no baahã wɔhenilee ayi wɔ
be kpalaŋŋ. Maŋtsɛ David tsɔ shihilɛ ni tamɔ
nakai mli; ewie akɛ: “Miwui lɛ amli ejɔɔɔ mi
yɛ mihe esha lɛ hewɔ.” Ekɛfata he akɛ: “Mihe
ejɔ ni mikumɔ kwraa.” (Lala 38:3, 8) Kɛ́ na-
kai ji bɔ ni onuɔ he bei komɛi lɛ, no lɛ, ŋma-
lɛi komɛi ni yɔɔ Yesaia wolo lɛ mli lɛ baanyɛ
ashɛje omii.

8 Kanemɔ Yesaia 1:18. Kɛ́ Yehowa kɛ
wɔhe eshai ke wɔ lɛ, ekɛkeɔ wɔ kwraa ni
ekaiii dɔŋŋ. Ekɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ ko tsɔɔ mli
yɛ Yesaia wolo lɛ mli. Ekɛɛ kɛ́ wɔhe eshai
lɛ “tsuɔ tamɔ lá po,” ebaahã amɛyɛ “tamɔ
snoo.” Kɛ́ nɔ ko ni tsuɔ tamɔ lá ka wɔtade
yɛŋ mli lɛ, ejiemɔ bɛ mlɛo kwraa. Kɛ̃lɛ, Ye-
howa kɛɛ, kɛ́ wɔhe eshai lɛ tsuɔ tamɔ lá po
lɛ, ebaahã amɛyɛ tamɔ snoo; ebaajie wɔhe
eshai lɛ yɛ wɔhe kwraa, ni ebaafee tamɔ nɔ ni
wɔfeko esha po.
