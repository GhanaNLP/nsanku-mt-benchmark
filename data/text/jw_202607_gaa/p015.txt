Yɛ nikasemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaasusu sanebimɔi etɛ komɛi ahe. Amɛbaaye amɛbua wɔ fɛɛ ekɔɔɔ he eko afii abɔ ni wɔkɛsɔmɔ Yehowa koni wɔle lɛ jogbaŋŋ. Sanebimɔi lɛ ji: Mɛni tsɔɔ akɛ mɔ ko le Yehowa? Mɛni hewɔ esa akɛ wɔya nɔ wɔbɔ mɔdɛŋ wɔle lɛ jogbaŋŋ fe bɔ ni wɔle lɛ lɛ? Ni te wɔbaafee tɛŋŋ wɔle lɛ jogbaŋŋ?

MƐNI TSƆƆ AKƐ MƆ KO LE YEHOWA?
<<<P>>>
4 Mɛni ji gbɛ kome ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔtsɔ nɔ wɔle Yehowa? Yehowa wo Yudafoi ni anɔ amɛ nom yɛ Babilon ni baaku amɛsɛɛ kɛba Israel lɛ shi akɛ: “Mahã amɛ tsui ni amɛkɛle mi, akɛ miji Yehowa.” (Yer. 24:7) Yudafoi ni ku amɛsɛɛ kɛjɛ Babilon kɛba Israel lɛ, le Yehowa he nibii pii momo. Shi be ni amɛku amɛsɛɛ kɛjɛ Babilon lɛ, ehe bahia ni amɛkɛ Yehowa abɔ naanyo waa. Nilelɔ ko wie akɛ: “Esoro Nyɔŋmɔ he nii ni abaale lɛ kwraa yɛ le ni abaale lɛ jogbaŋŋ koni akɛ lɛ abɔ naanyo waa lɛ he. Wɔbaanyɛ wɔkɛto srɔto ni yɔɔ mɔ ko ni miikane nuu kɛ yoo teŋ suɔmɔ he sane kɛ mɔ ko ni ená suɔmɔ ehã mɔ ko lɛ teŋ lɛ he.” Le ni wɔbaale akɛ Yehowa yɛ gbéi, ni akɛ, lɛ ji Nyɔŋmɔ lɛ kɛkɛ faaa. Esa akɛ wɔle esui fɛfɛji lɛ hu.
<<<P>>>
5 Gbɛ kroko ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔtsɔ nɔ wɔle Yehowa ji, ni wɔbaakase nɔ ni esumɔɔ kɛ nɔ ni esumɔɔɔ. Nɔ ko ni Maŋtsɛ Yosia fee lɛ hãa wɔnaa enɛ. Biblia lɛ wie akɛ: “Yɛ enɔyeli afi ni ji 8 lɛ nɔ, be ni edako lɛ, ebɔi etsɛ David Nyɔŋmɔ lɛ sɛɛgbɛtaomɔ.” (2 Kro. 34:3) Yosia kase Yehowa he nii pii kɛjɛ Ŋmalɛ lɛ mli, shi jeee no pɛ kɛkɛ efee. Ekɛ nibii ni ekase lɛ tsu nii. Ebɛ naakpɛɛ akɛ, Yehowa hã gbalɔ Yeremia ŋma akɛ, Yosia ye “jalɛ sane ni efee jalɛ nii, ni eye mɔ ni naa nɔ kɛ ohiafo sane ehã lɛ” lɛ. Kɛkɛ ni Yehowa kɛshi sɛɛ akɛ: “Ani jeee enɛ ji nɔ ni tsɔɔ akɛ mɔ ko le mi?”—Yer. 22:15, 16.
<<<P>>>
6 Kanemɔ Lala 9:10. Kɛ́ wɔbale Yehowa jogbaŋŋ lɛ, wɔbaahe lɛ wɔye waa hu. Nakai eba lɛ yɛ bɔfo Paulo gbɛfaŋ. Be ni awo lɛ tsuŋ nɔ ni ji naagbee nɔ ni eshwɛ fioo ni abaagbe lɛ lɛ, etsɔɔ nɔ hewɔ ni ehiɛ gbooo akɛ ebaana amanehulu akɛ Kristofonyo. Eŋma akɛ: “Mihiɛ gbooo. Ejaakɛ mile mɔ ni mihe enɔ miye lɛ.” (2 Tim. 1:12) Paulo he Yehowa eye waa ejaakɛ ekase Yehowa he nii, ni ena akɛ Yehowa ye ebua lɛ yɛ gbɛi pii anɔ. Ehe eye akɛ, bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni fee lɛ, Yehowa hiɛ kpaŋ enɔ ni ebaatee lɛ shi ejaakɛ eye lɛ anɔkwa.

MƐNI HEWƆ ESA AKƐ WƆYA NƆ WƆBƆ MƆDƐŊ WƆLE YEHOWA JOGBAŊŊ FE BƆ NI WƆLE LƐ LƐ?
<<<P>>>
7 Be ni wɔkaseɔ Yehowa he nii klɛŋklɛŋ kwraa lɛ, wɔkase nii waa koni wɔle ehe nibii pii bɔ ni wɔbaanyɛ. Shi kɛ́ wɔsɔmɔ Yehowa afii pii lɛ, ekolɛ wɔbɔŋ mɔdɛŋ akɛ wɔbaale lɛ jogbaŋŋ fe bɔ ni wɔle lɛ lɛ. Mɛni hewɔ? Ejaakɛ kɛ́ wɔmiikane Biblia lɛ yɛ shĩa loo yɛ asafoŋ kpee shishi lɛ, bei pii lɛ, wɔsusuɔ bɔ ni wɔbaaná nɔ ni wɔkaneɔ lɛ he sɛɛ, kɛ bɔ ni ebaaye ebua wɔ loo mɛi krokomɛi lɛ he. Kɛ́ wɔfee nakai lɛ ehi, ejaakɛ nakai ji nɔ ni Biblia lɛ woɔ wɔ hewalɛ ni wɔfee. (2 Tim. 3:16, 17) Shi Biblia lɛ hãa wɔleɔ mɔ ni Yehowa ji hu, ni enɛ ji nɔ ni he hiaa fe fɛɛ ni esa akɛ wɔsusu he. (Lala 25:8-10) No hewɔ lɛ, kɛ́ wɔmiikane Biblia lɛ, esaaa akɛ wɔsusuɔ bɔ ni wɔ loo mɛi krokomɛi baaná nɔ ni wɔkaneɔ yɛ Biblia lɛ
