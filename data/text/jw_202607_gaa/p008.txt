LALA 41
Ofainɛ Bo Misɔlemɔ Toi
<<<P>>>
NƆ NI WƆBAAKASE
Wɔbaana bɔ ni kɛ́ wɔhao loo wɔwerɛ ho wɔhe ni wɔkane ŋmalɛi komɛi ni yɔɔ Yesaia wolo lɛ mli ni wɔjwɛŋ amɛnɔ lɛ, amɛbaashɛje wɔmii.
<<<P>>>
ŊƆƆ LƐ AKƐ, gbekɛ ko werɛ eho ehe ni eefó waa. Mɛni hewɔ ni ewerɛ eho ehe ni eefó lɛ? Ekolɛ, amɛmiifã kɛyahi he kroko ni no hewɔ lɛ, ebaashi enanemɛi lɛ ni enaŋ amɛ dɔŋŋ. Aloo ekolɛ, eyafeee nɔ ko jogbaŋŋ ni no ehã ewerɛ eho ehe lɛ. Mɛni nyɛ ni yɔɔ suɔmɔ lɛ baafee yɛ shihilɛ ni tamɔ nɛkɛ mli? Ebaafua ebi lɛ lɛ, ebaabi lɛ nɔ ni feɔ lɛ, ni ebaahã ele akɛ esumɔɔ esane waa.
<<<P>>>
2 Yɛ Biblia lɛ mli lɛ, Yehowa kɛ ehe toɔ nyɛ ni yɔɔ suɔmɔ ni shɛjeɔ ebii amii lɛ he. Ewie akɛ: “Bɔ ni bi nyɛ shɛjeɔ ebi mii lɛ, nakai maya nɔ mashɛje nyɛmii.” (Yes. 66:13) Yehowa sumɔɔ wɔ kwraa fe wɔfɔlɔi. (Yes. 49:15) Ehã aŋmala saji yɛ e-Wiemɔ ni ji Biblia lɛ mli koni kɛ́ wɔwerɛ ho wɔhe loo wɔhao lɛ, amɛshɛje wɔmii ni amɛwo wɔ hewalɛ hu.—Rom. 15:4.
<<<P>>>
3 Yehowa naawiemɔi pii yɛ Yesaia wolo lɛ mli ni baanyɛ ashɛje wɔmii. (Yes. 40:1) Be ko yɛ Yuda shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ lɛ, Yehowa webii lɛ tsɔmɔ shihilɛi babaoo ni mli wawai amli. No hewɔ lɛ, Yehowa hã Yesaia ŋma wolo kɛshɛje amɛmii. Wɔ hu ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, wɔyɛ “bei krɛdɛi ni mli shihilɛ wa” amli, ni miishɛjemɔ he ehia wɔ. (2 Tim. 3:1) Yɛ wɔnikasemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaasusu ŋmalɛi komɛi ni yɔɔ Yesaia wolo lɛ mli lɛ ahe. Ŋmalɛi nɛɛ baanyɛ ashɛje wɔmii ni amɛwo wɔ hewalɛ kɛ́ (1) wɔshihilɛ tsake be ni wɔkpaaa gbɛ, (2) wɔhenilee miiyi wɔ waa yɛ esha ko ni wɔfee be ko ni eho lɛ hewɔ, kɛ (3) be ni mɔ ko ni wɔsumɔɔ esane lɛ egbo.
