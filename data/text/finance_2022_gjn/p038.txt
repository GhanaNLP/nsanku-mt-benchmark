“A pɔr jimanɛ kikɛ be efuli; amanshɛrbisheŋ be kelɔŋɛ n yili nɛ ashuŋ be kebuwi”
<<<P>>>
Mmalgabakpra (Kejimanshɛrbiwol be mmalgaba)
<<<P>>>
1D1F (Distriti koŋwule, Faktɔri koŋwule): Kepɔr afaktɔtri nɛ indastri-ana monɛ amo alɛ beeŋ ŋin efuli na a yɔ kinishibuwi be efuli nɛ ku kɔ ashuŋgboŋ damta. Kumobe tama ela fanɛ ku bra ashuŋ damta m ba sa Ghana-ebi kikɛ efuli na to.
<<<P>>>
Akanwutabiliti: Kede ela saŋɛ nɛ Gominanti daga ketuwe asheŋ n sa efuliebi a ŋini kumobe ashuŋ n saa kute loŋ be ashuŋ be asɔ monɛ a bra. Kede beeŋ tiŋ a la kananɛ Gomananti kata amanshɛrbi nɛ mputɛ kama nɛ a wɔ kumobe enɔ to.
<<<P>>>
Alokeshin (Kachige): Ekpa nɛ baa bulɔso a chige asɔ a sa Gominanti be ayabi nɛ adipatimɛnti.
<<<P>>>
Alivieshin: Kede ela ekpa monɛ baa bulɔso a duwɔ kesheŋ be tɔrɔ so.
<<<P>>>
Kemanshɛrbijisowol: Kemanshɛrbijiswol ela nkre monɛ bu nase nɛ ku bee ŋini mboŋ monɛ baaŋ nyɛ amanshɛrbi nɛ asɔ monɛ baaŋ jajɛ amo so. Anye e keni fanɛ, keyali beeŋ tiŋ n nase bumobe kejimanshɛrbiwol nɛ ku beeŋ ŋini amanshɛrbi monɛ bu kɔ nɛ kananɛ baaŋ nase bumobe asheŋtirso m bɛ abarso (fanɛ ebu be kukɔ, nchu nɛ edɛ nɛ ajibi). Nɛ bu kaŋ wu fanɛ a daga bu naŋ nyɛ amanshɛrbi n daŋɛso, keyali na beeŋ fɛ kenaŋ nyɛ amanshɛrbi n daŋɛso nko n kuu asɔ monɛ baaŋ tɔ so nko n ya fin mpaŋ.
<<<P>>>
Kejimanshɛrbi be kejimbaŋso: Kede bee ba jimanɛ monɛ Gominanti be amanshɛrbijiso chɔ amanshɛrbi chalaso.
<<<P>>>
Kapital Ɛspɛndicha: Ade la amanshɛrbi monɛ baa ta a shuŋ apurojɛtikpra fanɛ abɔrbi be kekuu, asukuru, ashibitiana, abriji be kepɔr, enite, nchu be kesa, aplanti nɛ amanshin nɛ adamta.
<<<P>>>
Beshumpo be kakɔka: Kede ela kakɔka nɛ kakɔka be adaŋɛsobi fanɛ, soshia sikiriti, agrandiuti nɛ apɛnshin nɛ baa ka a sa abani be beshumpo.
<<<P>>>
Divɔlɔpmɛnt Patnɛs: Kede bee yili a sa efuli pɔɛana nko ashuŋgboŋ monɛ amo nɛ efuli monɛ a kraa nyalɛ na bee chɛ abar a keni kananɛ loŋ be efuliana na be kafulilɔŋɛ be nkre beeŋ tiŋ yili nɛnɛ. DPS-ana ere beeŋ tiŋ a la ashuŋboŋ be ntuŋ fanɛ Intanashinal Mɔnitri Fand (IMF), Wɛld Banki, Afrika Divɔlɔpmɛnt Banki (AfDB) nɛ efuli anyɔ chɛ-abar to be bechɛtopo be efuliana fanɛ Germany, United Kingdom nɛ United State of America.
