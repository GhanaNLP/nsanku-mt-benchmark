65. Gominanti bee chɛso a lɔŋɛ a wɔrɔ n kaa kilgi nchu be kewurwe adɔjibiso be apurojɛti ere monɛ bu kaŋ luwe amo a beeŋ buwi ekpa n sa kafuli 6,766ha monɛ baaŋ tiŋ n wurweso nchu nɛ ku chɛto nɛ bu tiŋ n dɔɔ kafɛ to kikɛ; Kpong Irigeshin Sikim (2,176ha), Tono Irigeshin Sikim (2,490ha) nɛ Kpong Lɛft Bank Irigeshin Purojɛti (2,100ha). Bu luwe Irigeshin be apurojɛti ko ashi Tamne, Upper East Rigin nɛ Mprumem ashi Central Rigin monɛ a beeŋ buwi ekpa n sa kafuli monɛ kumobe kɔnɔ beeŋ fo 375ha nɛ bu baa kɔ a dɔɔ amalo.
