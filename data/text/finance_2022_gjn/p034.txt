Durnyaŋ kikɛ be kepre anishi a fin COVID-19 be vasin bra kumu lɛla, COVID-19 be avasin be kejɔɔ baa kii daŋɛso nna n sa ekpa monɛ bu daa bulɔso a kuŋ kumo nɛ bu daa tere apurotɔkɔl na, bande sososo be kekuŋ be ekpaana nɛ bu daa kɔ a kuŋ kumo nɛ amobe kenishi deŋ kemaa nuu adur a kuŋ be ekpaana fanɛ, kewɔtɔ kakuŋ anishi, abar be kebarga, nɛ enɔ be kefor be asheŋ.

79. COVID-19 be avasin monɛ bu fara a ta a jɔɔ baasa fara March 2021 to nna nɛ amobe sososo la 600,000 be AstraZeneca be avasin nɛ bu ta n jɔɔ baasa nɛ bumobe kɔnɔ baŋso 2.5 miliɔn nɛ a baa naa du kanaŋkama bumo to be ekama nyɛ doos koŋwule 8th November, 2021 be jimanɛ to.
