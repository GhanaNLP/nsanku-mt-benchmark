Pokuase be Intachenji
<<<P>>>
57. 2022 to, ekpaana be kelɔŋɛ be ashuŋ nɛ amobe ako la kelɔŋɛso, amaŋ be ketii, ekpakar be kelɔŋɛ nɛ ekpakar be kedɔɔ beeŋ shuŋ abɔrbigboŋ be 25,000km so, abɔrbi wurbiana be 16,000km so nɛ ndegboŋto be abɔrbi be 3,500km so.
<<<P>>>
58. N naŋ ta n daŋɛso, jimanɛ jimanɛ be kelɔŋɛ be ashuŋ fanɛ (epulto be kelɔŋɛso nɛnɛ, kenaŋ ta ajambi n wurweso, asfati be kenase, ekpa be kelɔŋɛ n kuu jewulebi nɛ abriji nɛ akɔlbɛti be kelɔŋɛ) beeŋ shuŋ abɔrbigboŋ be 23km, abɔrbi wurbiana be 800km nɛ ndegboŋ to be abɔrbi be 300km so. Amobe boshi, abɔrbigboŋ be 75km, abɔrbi wurbiana be 150km nɛ ndegboŋto be abɔrbi 30km be kelɔŋɛ beeŋ luwe.
