“A pɔr jimanɛ kikɛ be efuli; amanshɛrbisheŋ be kelɔŋɛ n yili nɛ ashuŋ be kebuwi”
<<<P>>>
RFJ (Rɛrin Fɔ Fud And Jɔbs): Kede beeŋ bra asɔbɔaya be kebla be ashuŋgboŋ na be kebla n kasar abar be keler anishi, nɛ ku beeŋ shinɛ efuli to be asɔblaso be kushuŋ na a yɔ anishito, n shin duwɔ asɔblaso be kekela efuli pɔtɛana so, n chɛto nɛ ashuŋ be kenyɛ, n shin shinɛ asɔbɔayablapoana nɛ baasa nɛ baa shuŋ nɛ amobe eblaŋ be kebawɔtɔ a nyalɛ.
<<<P>>>
Soshiyal Bɛnifits: Gomananti bee ka asɔbsidi a sa akɔmpene nɛ a bee sa nchu nɛ edɛ nɛ pɛtro be asɔ wurbi nkpal efuliebi so.
<<<P>>>
Sovelans: Kede la ekpa monɛ baa bulɔso a ta kekenito nɛnɛ nɛ kenyi a dara kesheŋ.
<<<P>>>
Transperensi: Kede la jimanɛ monɛ gominannti bee sa efuliebi baru kashiteŋto nɛ manaŋmanaŋ ta n laŋɛ asɔ monɛ a bee wɔrɔ na so.
<<<P>>>
Yus ɔf Guds and Sɛvisisi: Amanshɛrbi monɛ gominanti bee ka ta n laŋɛ kumobe ashuŋ kikɛ nɛ amonɛ ku bee wɔrɔ a sa efuli na be baasa.
<<<P>>>
Valu Adɛd Taks (VAT): Amanshɛrbi monɛ baa ta a deŋi asɔ nɛ ashuŋ be yawu so jimanɛ nɛ bu naŋ wɔtɔ kela amo to na.
