67. Kadɔɔ be jimanɛ nɛ ku baŋ luwe 2020/21 be jimanɛ to na kokoo nɛ bu dɔɔ n nyɛ kikɛ be kɔnɔ dii m baŋ saŋɛ kama peya so m fo 1,045,500 atɔn. Bu daŋɛso kedɔɔ n nyɛ be amanshɛrbi kalfa to be 28 n ya fo GH¢10, 560 tɔn koŋwule 2019/20 be kadɔɔ be jimanɛ to n yige kumo ndoŋ 2021/22 be kudɔɔ be jimanɛ to nkpal bu ka nyishi kokoo be yawu m ba kaseto ashi efuli pɔtɛana be abɛto so.

68. Kushuŋ bu ka fara ARIBRO (Animal Risɛchi Institu Burɔila) na, kede ela sososo nɛ CSIR ebi tiŋ n lara efuli nkoshinyɛn nɛ nkoshiche monɛ amo kikɛ la aburɔila. Bu tiŋ n lara efuli nkoshiche 1,300 nɛ nkoshinyɛn 300 abɔkwɛ anu nɛ ashunu be kefɛto nɛ a daŋ n luwe. N ta n daŋɛso, bu tiŋ n duu abɛ be aduuso 6,023,930 nɛ a kɔr nɛ bu ta n chige n sa Planti fɔ Ɛspɔt nɛ Rural Divɔlɔpmɛnti (PERD) be purogram na, nɛ bu fa abɛ be aduuso fɛaso 55,727 nɛ kpakpa be aduuso fɛaso 34, 252 n sa bedɔpo.
