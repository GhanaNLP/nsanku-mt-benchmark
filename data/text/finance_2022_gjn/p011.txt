7. Gomananti be amanshɛrbisheŋ be kananɛ a nite kikɛ baa yili amanshɛrbi be kejimbaŋso GH¢33.86 biliɔn (GDP be 7.7%), ta n kesar amanshɛrbi tamaso be nchɛrga ka yili GH¢32.58 biliɔn (GDP be 7.4%). Efuli ere nɛ efuli pɔtɛso be amanshɛrbi nɛ bu ta n ka amanshɛrbi nɛ bu daŋ ji m baŋso na. Efuli ere to be amanshɛrbi nɛ bu ta n ka loŋ be kukɔ na daa la GH¢ 20.39 biliɔn, nɛ efuli pɔtɛ-ana to peya la GH¢13.47 biliɔn.
