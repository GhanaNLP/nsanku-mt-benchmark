“A pɔr jimanɛ kikɛ be efuli; amanshɛrbisheŋ be kelɔŋɛ n yili nɛ ashuŋ be kebuwi”
<<<P>>>
Ghana kikɛ be banki be amanshɛrbi be kananɛ a bee nite efuli na be amanshɛrbisheŋ be ekpa so.
<<<P>>>
MSME (Maikuro And Smɔl Bizinɛs Divɔlɔpmɛnt Purogram): Maikuro-ɛntapais (nko Maikuroɛntaprais) la kusɔ monɛ baa ŋinito fanɛ ku la kushuŋ fimbi nna nɛ ku bee ta beshumpo gberɛbi n saa kɔ amanshɛrbi be dede nko a nyɛ amanshɛrbi monɛ a maŋ fo kananko.
<<<P>>>
NABCo (Nashinal Bildɛs Kɔɔ): La kushuŋ monɛ baa tere NABCO nɛ ku la Ghana Gominanti be kusɔ pupɔr monɛ bu bra nɛ ku sa yunivɛsiti be bebiipo monɛ be luwe ashuŋ nɛ ku tiŋ n sa kebawɔtɔ to be tɔrɔ be yulwe ashi baasa gbagba be ashuŋ nɛ gomananti be ashumkpaana n shin tii bumobe kenyi so nɛ ku ŋin efuli ere be amanshɛrbisheŋ be ndaŋ n yɔ esoso.
<<<P>>>
NBSSI (Nashinal Bɔd Fɔ Smɔl And Midiɔm Skel Indastri): Nashinal Bɔd fɔ Smɔl Skel Indastri (NBSSI) na la efuli be kaba be eshumkpa monɛ ku bee shuŋ a maa fin tɔnɔ nɛ ku wɔ Minisri ɔf Tred nɛ Indastri be kaseto, nɛ bu sa kumo eleŋ fanɛ ku ŋin Maikuro nɛ Smɔl Ɛntapraisis (MSEs) n yɔ anishito.
<<<P>>>
GDP monɛ nku be amanshɛrbi maŋ daŋɛso: Kede ela Ghana to be asɔ kikɛ be kɔnɔ ashi kumobe amanshɛrbisheŋ to nɛ nku be amanshɛrbi maŋ daŋɛso nɛ ku bee liɛ Infuleshin.
<<<P>>>
Amanshɛrbi Chalaso nɛ a maŋ la taks peya: Ade ela gomanati be amanshɛrbi chalaso monɛ baa nyɛ nɛ a maŋ la taks peya, amobe ako ela afiis nɛ baa sɔ pɔɛ a sa apɛmiti nɛ alansisi, gominanti be asɔ be keta n shuŋ be afiis nɛ adamta.
<<<P>>>
Autstandin Obligeshins (Asɔ nɛ a kraŋ daga kewɔrɔ): Asɔ nɛ a kraŋ daga kewɔrɔ ela amanshɛrbi nɛ Ministri ɔf Fainans beeŋ ka n sa MDA-ebi nɛ amanshɛrbi nɛ a daŋ daga bu kaa ka 2016 to n sa mboŋ monɛ mbra ŋini fanɛ bu yɛra amanshɛrbi.
<<<P>>>
Praimere Balans: Kede la mbarga monɛ ku wɔ gominanti be amanshɛrbi chalaso nɛ amanshɛrbijiso monɛ bu maŋ wɔtɔ nchu (akɔ be keka maŋ tiiso). Kede beeŋ tiŋ n ler efuli fanɛ ku kaa la Gurɔs Dɔmɛstik Purodakt (GDP) be asɔ be kalfato.
<<<P>>>
Purojɛktɛd Grofu Reti (Efuli be ndaŋ tamaso): Kananɛ Ghana be amanshɛrbsheŋ beeŋ daŋ kafɛto.
<<<P>>>
Amanshɛrbichalaso: Kede ela amanshɛrbi nɛ gomananti bee sɔ ta n laŋɛ efuli to nɛ efuli pɔtɛana so be ashuŋ to kikɛ.
