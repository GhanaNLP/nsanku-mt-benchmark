“A pɔr jimanɛ kikɛ be efuli; amanshɛrbisheŋ be kelɔŋɛ n yili nɛ ashuŋ be kebuwi”
<<<P>>>
Ashi 2022 be kejimanshɛrbiwol na to, anyeeŋ nase ekpaana monɛ anyeeŋ chɛso a wɔrɔ epulto be ania monɛ anyeeŋ tiŋ n ta n kɔ amanshɛrbisheŋ be akpaltobi nɛ anye a tiŋ n nyɛ eleŋ pupɔr kulɔ mɔɔso na be kaman. Gominanti kraŋ kata to nɛ kagbenekoŋwule kananɛ ku beeŋ tiŋ n lara efuli ekpa pupɔrana buwitoso nɛ a bɛ abarso nɛnɛ nɛ a tiŋ n sa kenyi n shin chɛto nɛ mbiafolbi a ba kii ashuŋwuraana. Anye ka kraa bɛ Prɛsidɛnti be afɛsheŋ monɛ a la keŋin “Ghana Beyond Aid” so na, mee sha nɛ n kule Ghanaebi kikɛ, fɔŋfɔŋ mbiafolbi nɛ bu fara a ta kebargato a -wɔ be adabi, a fɛ n kaa bra asɔ pupɔr be kewɔrɔ be mfɛra n kaa pɛto kpakpa nɛ kepɔr ashuŋwuraana be efuli nɛ ku beeŋ baa wɔtɔ saŋɛkama.
<<<P>>>
Mee sha fanɛ anyebe ekama na e wɔtɔ enɔ nɛ anye a pɔr Ghana monɛ anyee sha. Ebɔrɛ e nyina anyebe kenanasawule Ghana!!!!
