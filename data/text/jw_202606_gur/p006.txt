Yehowa nɛreba kɔ’ɔn tara la nɔyinɛ, la nɔŋerɛ bɔ’ɔra taaba
(Bisɛ kaalegɔ zɛ’an 16)
<<<P>>>
YEHOWA N TARI A
TIGERE LA TUNA
<<<P>>>
13 To san pɔsɛ ti’isa ti la dayɛ Yehowa Sɛɛradoma tigere ti Naayinɛ dɔla pa’ala a nɛreba, la nari ti to eŋɛ sɛla kalam. La nari ti to ti’isɛ soke ana wa yele: ‘Bani n tiregeri tara Baabule la n pa’alɛ sɛla la tuna tuuma? Tigere kani sakerɛ yɛla n makɛ la Baabule la?’ (2 Tim. 4:3, 4) ‘Bani n tuni Yehowa n boti sɛla zina wa?’—Mt. 7:20, 21.
<<<P>>>
14 Naayinɛ boti la nɛra woo wum yelesoma la ge ti ba’asegɔ la nyaa paɛ. Bala la, to wan ta’an soke tomea yeti, ‘Bani n mɔɔli yelesoma la zina beere wa?’ (Mt. 24:14) La niɛ peelumi ti la dela Yehowa Sɛɛradoma n mɔɔli yelesoma la. Yehowa Sɛɛradoma ma’a n mɔɔli Naayinɛ Na’am yelesoma la tisi n gaŋɛ 240 poan. Ton pɔsɛ muula yelesoma la, la gaŋɛ yuun 100 n wana. Na’am muulegɔ tuuma la n kɔ’ɔn dɛna ninmu’urɛ tuunɛ bɔ’ɔra Naayinɛ nɛreba.—Mt. 28:19, 20.
<<<P>>>
15 Kaalɛ Matu 6:9 la Isaiah 43:10. To zuo zẽkɛ mɛ la ban yi’iri to Yehowa Sɛɛradoma la. Nɛreba zo’e zo’e ka mi ti Naayinɛ yu’urɛ n de Yehowa. Ge tomam tiregɛ mɛ lerege Baabule la ge dekɛ Naayinɛ yu’urɛ la eŋɛ zẽse’esi ti la nara ti la bɔna la. Ban yuun pɔsɛ gulesɛ Baabule la, Naayinɛ yu’urɛ la yuun boi bim gaŋɛ sugumnɔɔrɛ 7,000 mɛ.
<<<P>>>
16 Kaalɛ Yon 13:34, 35. Zina beere wa, nɛreba zo’e zo’e ka tari nɔyinɛ la taaba ge me dina taaba. Ge Yehowa nɛreba kɔ’ɔn tara la nɔyinɛ, la nɔŋerɛ bɔ’ɔra taaba. To ze’ele la tisi to’ore to’ore ge ti to bɔŋa me bɔna to’ore to’ore. Hali la bala za’a, to tari la suma’asum la nɔŋerɛ bɔ’ɔra taaba. Hon tabelɛ keŋɛ tansugere kayima zamesegɔ? La san dɛna bala,
<<<P>>>
13. To wan eŋɛ la wani to san pɔsɛ tara puti’ira ayi ayi la tigere sɛka ti Naayinɛ dɔla ka poan pa’ala a nɛreba la?
<<<P>>>
14. Sore kani poan ti Yehowa Sɛɛradoma basɛ ti Matu 24:14 tuna tuuma?
<<<P>>>
15. Yehowa Sɛɛradoma iti la bem ti la pa’alɛ ti ba pẽyeri Naayinɛ yu’urɛ la mɛ? (Matu 6:9; Isaiah 43:10)
<<<P>>>
16. Beni n sɔi ti to wan ta’an yeti Yehowa Sɛɛradoma n tari Yon 13:34, 35 la tuna tuuma? (Bisɛ foote la.)
