13) Mabisɛba n boi to gurup la poan la ni soŋɛ to mɛ yeledaaŋɔ poan mayesɛ wuu to san bɛ̃’ɛra, to suure san sa’am bii to nɛra san ki. (1 Tes. 5:14) Mɛlesekãtɛ la n kɔ’ɔn pɔ̃ra la, to wan ɛɛra to mabiisi la soŋerɛ paa. (Mt. 24:21) To wan ta’an ŋmibe to yeledaaŋɔ poan se’ere n sɔi la, to mi ti to mabiisi la nɔŋɛ to mɛ ge me wan soŋɛ tɔ. —Mag. 17:17.
<<<P>>>
14 Ho wan nyɛ soŋerɛ yeledaaŋɔ la yeledi’ire saŋa. To na’am mɔɔlegɔ gurup la ni soŋɛ to mɛ yeledi’ire poan, yaarɛ poan, nɔŋɔ poan, bã’asi san wi’ira, la to san bɛ̃’ɛra. Sankaŋa la, to na’am mɔɔlegɔ gurup bisegadoma la ni maasum mɛ ti ba soŋɛ tɔ. Makerɛ, yeledi’ire saŋa, na’am mɔɔlegɔ gurup bisegadoma la ni sɔsɛ mɛ la yizuo woo n boi ba gurup la poan ti ba baŋɛ yelese’a n pakɛ ba mayesɛ wuu tiim, dia, ko’om, kɛ̃’a zɛ’an bii lɔyeyɛɛtɔ. Ba san baŋɛ bala, ba ni eŋɛ la sɛla soŋɛ mabiisi la. Leyɛ’ɛsa, gurup bisegadoma la ni basɛ ti Tansugere Na’am Tuuma Bisegadoma la baŋɛ sɛba n boi ba gurup la poan ɛɛra soŋerɛ paa. Ba ni layum sɔsɛ mɛ ge maasum soŋɛ ba. Bisɛ to na’am mɔɔlegɔ gurup la n soŋeri to se’em!
<<<P>>>
15 Wuu ton baŋɛ se’em la, Yehowa tigere la kãregɛ mɛ. Yehowa tigere bɔbese’ere n boi saazuo la tari la malesi zo’e zo’e. A tigere la bɔbese’ere n boi tingɔŋɔ wa zuo la me tari la nɛreba n gaŋɛ tusetusa awei ge ti nɛreba kelum kena pa’asa dabeserɛ woo poan. (Zek. 8:23; Lil. 5:11) Sɛba n pu’useri Yehowa la za’a boti la ba mɔɔla Naayinɛ Na’am yelesoma la bɔ’ɔra nɛreba! (Lil. 14:6, 7) To za’a boti la to nyɛta nyuurɔ to na’am mɔɔlegɔ gurup la poan. Maaseŋɔ wa soŋeri to mɛ ti to ta’an pẽyera Yehowa la to suure za’a.—Ym. 111:1
