la yuun sakɛ ti Yehowa n nari ti ba puʼu-sa, sɛla n sɔi la eŋa n naam sɛla woo. Ba yuun le sakɛ ti Yezu n de Naayinɛ Dayua la n tari “yetɔgom sebo n boʼore vom sebo n ka tare baʼahegɔ.” Ba me yuun le mina ti Yehowa puurɛ pee mɛ la Yezu n iti sɛla la. To mi yeleseʼa n wan taʼan soŋɛ tɔ ti to taʼan tara yelemɛŋɛrɛ bɔʼɔ-ra Yehowa yeledaaŋɔ san paɛ tɔ. Ba dela to sakɛ ti: (1) Yehowa n naam sɛla woo. (2) Yehowa n basɛ ti ba gulesɛ Baabule la. (3) Yehowa n tari a tigere la tuna. To san sirum sakɛ yɛla atã wa soŋa soŋa, la wan soŋɛ tɔ ti to tara yelemɛŋɛrɛ bɔʼɔra Yehowa hali yeledaaŋɔ san paɛ tɔ.

YEHOWA N NAAM SƐLA WOO
<<<P>>>
7 Kaalɛ Liligere 4:11. Gulesegɔ wa tĩɛ tɔ ti Yehowa n naam sɛla woo. Wana paʼalɛ ti Yehowa kɔʼɔn nara mɛ ti to puʼusa en. To san tiʼisa tayesa eŋa n tari paŋa seʼem la, to ni baŋɛ ti a wan taʼan soŋɛ tɔ yeledaaŋɔ poan. Ge Sitaa-na pãʼaseri la nɛreba ti ba tiʼisa ti Naayi-nɛ ka boi. Naanɔ lɔyɔrɔ zamesegɔdoma basɛba yeti Naanɔdaana ka boi ge sɛla woo kɔʼɔn diʼe me na bɔna. La yele pakɛ mɛ ti to mina sɛla n sɔi ti to sakɛ ti Yeho-wa n naam sɛla woo, la sɛla n sɔi ti to sakɛ ti a nɔŋɛ tɔ. Bala la, yeledaaŋɔ san puyum paɛ tɔ, to wan kelum sakɛ ti Naa-yinɛ boi mɛ ge me wan soŋɛ tɔ.—Heb. 11:6.

8 Ho san pɔsɛ tara putiʼira ayi ayi ti Naanɔdaana ka boi, la nari ti ho eŋɛ sɛla. Eŋɛ viisegɔ to tigere gɔnɔ la poan ti la soŋɛ hɔ ti ho sirum sakɛ ti Yehowa n naam sɛla woo. Gɔnlɛmlɛm la zuo n de Was Life Created? la The Origin of Life—Five Questions Worth Asking wan soŋɛ hɔ ti ho nyɛ yeleseʼa n wan basɛ ti ho sa-kerɛ kpeʼem. Sɛla n le basɛ ti to baŋɛ ti Naayinɛ n naam sɛla woo boi la zaseŋɔ yele la zuo n de “Ba Naam Ku Mɛ?” n boi
