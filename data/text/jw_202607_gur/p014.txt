<<<P>>>
YELEKPINE
<<<P>>>
To wan zamesɛ baŋɛ ton wan mina Yehowa vuurɛ n de sɛla, sɛla n sɔi ti la yele pakɛ ti to mina Yehowa soŋa, la ton wan eŋɛ seʼem taʼan ita bala.
<<<P>>>
Kɔʼɔn Zamesa Yehowa Yɛla Paʼasa
<<<P>>>
“Nan ho sɔ Yenɛ la ho suunre zanʼan, ge naʼaha en.”
—1 YEL. 28:9.
<<<P>>>
YEHOWA miʼilum ka tari makerɛ. A dela yɛm Zaʼadaana ge me kɔʼɔn tuna tunkiresi. To san ni tiʼisa to Naaŋɔdaa-na la yele, ɔmiʼisa n ni tara tɔ. La me taʼan ana ho wuu lan yuun ani tuntuna Paul seʼem la. A yuun yeti: “Yenɛ kɔʼɔn ete la som bɔʼɔra nɛreba. A me le kɔʼɔn dɛna la yamdaana ge me tara potɛnʼsonɛ. Bala, ane n wan taʼan baŋɛ Yenɛ potɛnʼɛrɛ? Ane n wan taʼan bɔkɛ Yenɛ n tɛnʼɛ-here seʼem?”—Arom 11:33, 34.
<<<P>>>
2 La boi bim ti to kan taʼan kɔʼɔn mina Yehowa yɛla zaʼa zaʼa ge to wan taʼan kelum baŋɛ eŋa n ani seʼem. Naba David yuun yele a dayua Solomon yeti: “Nan ho sɔ Yenɛ la ho suunre zanʼan, ge naʼaha en . . . Ho han ɛ en, ho wan nyɛ en.” (1 Yel. 28:9) Yuuma zoʼe zoʼe pooren, tuntuna Yon yuun yele mabiisi la ti, ba wan taʼan “mina yelemɛŋɛrɛ Yenɛ la.” (1 Yo. 5:20) Yezu me yuun yeti a wan paʼalɛ “sɛba te a bɔta te a lilige Sɔ la bo ba” la.—Mt. 11:27.

1. La ni ana to la wani to san tiʼisa Yehowa yɛm, a miʼilum la a tunkiresi la yele?
<<<P>>>
2. Bem n paʼalɛ ti to wan taʼan baŋɛ Yehowa?
