BUILDING AT 117 ADAMS STREET
<<<P>>>
Because of the increase in demand for literature, responsible brothers in Brooklyn recognized the need to expand our printing facilities. For years, they had rented the property at 18 Concord Street, but this building could not accommodate the needed expansion. The brothers purchased a plot of land on Adams Street, a short walk from the existing building. They began to construct an eight-story concrete building, nearly doubling their floor space.
<<<P>>>
That building, located at 117 Adams Street, would be occupied in 1927, setting the stage for even more growth. The year ahead would see a rapid expansion and a continued cleansing of the organization, preparing God’s people to be worthy of accepting the name Jehovah’s Witnesses.
