MIRACULOUS UNITY
<<<P>>>
9 The prophet Micah foretold another miracle we see in our time—the remarkable unity of God’s people. Under inspiration, Micah described men and women whom Jehovah would gather and who would dwell together in unity. (Read Micah 2:12.) In fulfillment of that prophecy, God’s people today live in harmony despite any differences they may have in personality or background. They agree with the psalmist David, who wrote: “Look! How good and how pleasant it is for brothers to dwell together in unity!”—Ps. 133:1.
<<<P>>>
10 Today our unity is evident in many ways. For example, wherever we live, we share the same spiritual program at meetings, assemblies, and conventions. Witnesses from different backgrounds and nationalities work side by side on building projects and in Bethel facilities across the globe. Our bond is close.
<<<P>>>
We are not divided by culture, economic status, or political beliefs. Think about it: Our loving family is there for us wherever we go—ready to offer spiritual and practical help. Never before in history have so many people from so many lands and cultures been united in thought and in conduct.
<<<P>>>
11 Our unity is miraculous because it is in striking contrast to the world’s widespread disunity. Across the globe, people are deeply divided. The news is filled with reports about fights, riots, protests, rebellions, and wars—painting a grim picture of division and strife. As foretold in the Bible, people are “disloyal, . . . not open to any agreement, slanderers, without self-control, fierce, without love of goodness, betrayers, headstrong, puffed up with pride.” (2 Tim. 3:2-4) Despite the fact that “the whole world is lying in the power of the wicked one” and his demons, we continue to thrive as a united people. (1 John 5:19; Rev. 12:9) Truly, that is miraculous!
<<<P>>>
12 We can contribute to the bond of unity in Jehovah’s organization. How? By carefully listening to and applying what Jehovah teaches us. The teachings of his Word help us to show patience, to extend forgiveness, to avoid arguments, and to be kind to others. (Eph. 4:2, 31, 32) Each time we overlook a flaw in a fellow believer, each time we control the urge to gossip, and each time we extend forgiveness—we are working along with Jehovah as he accomplishes a modern-day miracle within his organization.
