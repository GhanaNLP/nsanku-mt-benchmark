Treasure Your Share in Modern-Day Miracles
<<<P>>>
“By this all will know that you are my disciples.”—JOHN 13:35.
<<<P>>>
IMAGINE how thrilling it would have been to stand on the shore of the Red Sea and watch as Jehovah divided its waters, providing his people with a path of escape from the Egyptian army. And just think of the joy you would have had to see Jesus cure the sick and even raise the dead! These are just some of the miracles recorded in the Bible. Any righteous-hearted person who witnessed such miracles would have had no doubt that God was behind those events. (Matt. 15:31) But those amazing events occurred a long time ago. Are miracles happening today? Yes! Why can we say that?
