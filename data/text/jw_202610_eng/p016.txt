MIRACULOUS LOVE
<<<P>>>
13 Jesus said that his followers would be identified by their love for one another. (Read John 13:35.) Such love was central in the lives of first-century Christians. For example, they shared their belongings with those in need. (Acts 2:44, 45) They supported those who were persecuted. (Acts 12:5) They cared for orphans and widows. (Jas. 1:27) And despite their own poverty, they sent relief aid to other Christians. (2 Cor. 8:1-4) Second-century writer Tertullian quoted what others said about the early Christians: “See . . . how they love one another [and] how they are ready even to die for one another.”
<<<P>>>
