20 True friends are a gift from Jehovah, and we need them more than ever. So like Daniel, make friends with others. Seek out those in your congregation who, like Jeremiah, can inspire and guide you. Surround yourself with those who can strengthen your resolve to stay loyal to Jehovah. Humbly ask others to pray for you, when needed. Above all, remember who your best Friend is—Jehovah. He will never abandon you. (Heb. 13:5) With Jehovah’s help, together we can take our stand against anything that threatens us today and enjoy friendships that will last forever.

Jehovah—Our Father, Our God and Friend
<<<P>>>
What if you struggle to see Jehovah as a Father or as Someone you can trust? Try making small but consistent efforts to draw close to Jehovah each day in the following areas.
<<<P>>>
● Communicate: Talk to Jehovah in prayer; he wants to hear what you have to say.
<<<P>>>
● Spend time together: Listen to Jehovah through your Bible reading and study. Learn more about who he is—his qualities and actions.
<<<P>>>
● Be patient: Remember that building trust and a relationship takes time. You can be sure that Jehovah wants a relationship with you.
