7 A thankful attitude draws us closer to Jehovah. Every good thing that we have comes from Jehovah. (Read James 1:17.) He has given us many gifts, including the ransom and the hope of salvation. Why? Not because we deserve them, but because he loves us. (Rom. 3:24; Eph. 2:8) When we are grateful for Jehovah’s undeserved kindness, we draw closer to him as our beloved Father and best Friend.
