Edwɛne Fofolɛ Ne
<<<P>>>
BODANE
<<<P>>>
Edwɛkɛ ɛhye baboa yɛ yeamaa yɛahile ngoanelielɛ ahyɛlɛdeɛ ne, nwolɛ adenle mɔɔ yɛnyia yɛlɛsonle Gyihova yɛɛ edwɛkɛhanlɛ gyima ne mɔɔ bɛva bɛmaa yɛ la anwo anyezɔlɛ.
<<<P>>>
Maa Ɛ Nye Ɛzɔ Adenle Mɔɔ Wɔnyia Ɛlɛsonle Nyamenle Mɔɔ Ɛnzulo La
<<<P>>>
‘Yekpogya ngoanelielɛ mɛne bie zo kɛ ɔbamaa yɛanyia adenle yɛazonle ye dahuu mɔɔ yɛnzulo.’—LUKU 1:69, 74.
<<<P>>>
GYIHOVA luale nwanwane adenle zo maanle ɛsɔfo Zɛkalaya nee ɔ ye Ɛlezabɛte mɔɔ bɛyɛ mrelera la wole Dwɔn. Enee Ɛlezabɛte le mota. Mɔɔ bɛwole la, ɛnee menli mɔɔ wɔ ɛkɛ ne la nee bɛ mbusuafoɔ kpondɛ kɛ bɛnwu mɔɔ kakula ne bayɛ la. Ɔti bɛbizale bɛ nwo kɛ: “Duzu a kakula ɛhye bayɛ a?” (Luku 1:57-66) Gyihova luale Zɛkalaya anwo zo buale kpuya zɔhane. Ɔhilehilele nu kɛ ɔnrɛhyɛ Mɛzaya ne mɔɔ bɛva mekɛ tendenle bɛhendɛ ye la bara, na Dwɔn baboa menli yeamaa bɛanlu bɛ nwo amaa bɛanwu ahenle zɔhane na bɛadoa ye. (Emɔso 3:7; Luku 1:67-79) Noko tɛ mɔɔ Dwɔn bayɛ la ala a Zɛkalaya hanle nwolɛ edwɛkɛ a.* Kɛ mɔɔ yɛbanwu ye wɔ edwɛkɛ ɛhye anu la, Zɛkalaya edwɛkɛ ne le ngapezo na ɔfale yɛdayɛ noko yɛ nwo ɛnɛ. Ye edwɛkɛ ne bamaa yɛanwu ninyɛne nsa mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkile nwolɛ anyezɔlɛ la. Bɛmɛ a le (1) ngoanelielɛ ahyɛlɛdeɛ ne, (2) nwolɛ adenle mɔɔ yɛnyia yɛlɛsonle Nyamenle yɛɛ (3) yɛ edwɛkɛhanlɛ gyima ne.
