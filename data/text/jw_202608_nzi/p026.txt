ƐBƐLABƆLE NU EDWƐKƐ
<<<P>>>
Me Bɔvolɛ Kpole Ne Nwunle Me
<<<P>>>
STÉPHANE LANDEROIN ANLOA EDWƐKƐ
<<<P>>>
MELE kakula la ɛnee me nye ɛnlie sukulu ɛhɔlɛ nwo yɛɛ ɛnee menze debie. Ɛnee mekulo kɛ me ngomekye meyɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ mekulo la mɔɔ awie biala adwenle ɛmba me nwo zo a. Ɔti ɛnee me nye ɛnla kɛ Nyamenle banwu me. Duzu ati ɔ?
<<<P>>>
Bɛtetele me wɔ Paris mɔɔ wɔ France la. Ɛnee me awovolɛ nee menli mɔɔ wɔ ɛkɛ la ɛnlie Nyame-nle ɛnli, ɔti medame noko meanlie ye meanli. Ɛnee medie medi kɛ tɛ awie biala a bɔle ninyɛne mɔɔ yɛnwu bɛ la a, bɛdabɛ mumua a bɛrale a. Bɛhilehi-lele me ɛhye wɔ sukulu, na ɛnee mesuzu kɛ ɔle nɔ-halɛ kɛ mɔɔ awie biala noko nwunle ye zɔ la. Ɔyɛle me kɛ ndelebɛbo wɔ nu ɔti meambiza nwolɛ kpuya biala.
<<<P>>>
Ɛnee me awovolɛ ɛnlɛ Nyamenle ɛzonlenlɛ nwo adwenle kpalɛ. Bɛhanle kɛ saa Nyamenle bie wɔ ɛkɛ a, anrɛɛ amaneɛnwunlɛ nee nzisi ɛtɛbule ewia-de ye zɛhae. Kɛmɔ medame noko menwunle kɛ menli dɔɔnwo mɔɔ sonle Nyamenle la le nabala-ba la ati, menyianle adwenle kɛ nrɛlebɛ ɛnle nu kɛ awie balie ali kɛ Nyamenle bie mɔɔ lɛ ɛlɔlɛ la wɔ ɛkɛ. Ɛnee megolo bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛdie Nyamenle bɛdi bɔbɔ la anwo.
<<<P>>>
Noko ɛnɛ, medie medi kɛ Bɔvolɛ bie wɔ ɛkɛ yɛɛ ɔdwenle me nwo ɔnva nwo kɛ metɔ sinli la. Maa menga deɛmɔti mengakyile me adwenle nee kɛzi yehakyi me ɛbɛlabɔle la anwo edwɛkɛ mengile wɔ.
<<<P>>>
Mɔɔ menlile ɛvolɛ 19 la, arane ne hanle kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛtete me bɛmaa meyɛ sogya. Ɔti bɛmaanle mengɔle French Guiana mɔɔ wɔ South America la ɛvolɛ ko. Me rɛle ɛnrɛvi kɛzi ɛleka kɛnlɛma ɛhye mɔɔ wɔ moamoa la hanle me ɛbɛlabɔle la ɛlɛ.
<<<P>>>
Menlile ɛvolɛ 20 la, menziale mengɔle Paris. Noko yeangyɛ, ɛnee mɔɔ meyɛ la ala a le kɛ me-bayɛ gyima ɛsesebɛ wɔ dapɛne ne anu na ɔdwu dapɛne awieleɛ a, ɛnee yedo me nee nzanlonlɛ yɛɛ party mgbole ɛhɔlɛ. Noko ɛnee ɛbɛlabɔle zɛhae ɛmmaa anyelielɛ biala. Ɛnee me ɛbɛlabɔle ne ɛngyi kpɔkɛ biala azo. Menwunle kɛ menli ɛnli nɔhalɛ, bɛyɛ nyeyenu yɛɛ bie mɔ di ehyia kpalɛ, na ɛhye gyegyele me kpalɛ. Menli mgbalɛ nwu amaneɛ.
