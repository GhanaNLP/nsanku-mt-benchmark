15 Saa yɛnyia adwenle kɛ yɛbɔ mɔdenle yɛaye nzisi mɔɔ yɛyia anzɛɛ nzisi mɔɔ wɔ ewiade la yɛavi ɛkɛ a, yɛbazɛkye yɛ mekɛ nee yɛ anwosesebɛ ala. (Nol. 1:15) Gyihova ze mɔɔ yɛgyi ɔ nloa la. (Edw. 37:18) Ɔnrɛhyɛ biala, Gyihova balua ye Belemgbunlililɛ ne azo ayɛ debie biala mɔɔ Seetan nee ye sunsum ɛtane ne mɔ ɛzɛkye ye la boɛ. (1 Dwɔn 3:8; Yek. 21:5) Noko kɛkala ɛdeɛ, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkɔ zo yɛnyia menli nwo abotane na yɛkulo bɛ. Ɔwɔ kɛ yɛfa yɛ mekɛ nee yɛ anwosesebɛ yɛboa menli dodo biala mɔɔ yɛbahola la yɛmaa bɛbayɛ Gyihova agɔnwo mɔ.

16. Duzu a baboa yɛ yeamaa yɛali konim wɔ yɛ nee Seetan nee sunsum ɛtane ne mɔ konle ne anu a? (Eza nea nvoninli ne mɔ.)
<<<P>>>
sunsum ɛtane ne mɔ konle ne anu. (Gye. 4:7; 1 Dwɔn 5:4, 5) Noko diedi a le debie titili mɔɔ ɔbamaa yɛali konim a. (Ɛfɛ. 6:16) Ɔti kɔ zo mia diedi mɔɔ ɛlɛ wɔ Gyihova Nyamenle nee Gyisɛse Kelaese anu la anu. Eza diedi bamaa wɔahɔ zo wɔahulo ɛ mediema na ɛ nee bɛ adɛnla koyɛlɛ nu.
<<<P>>>
17 Bɛmaa yɛbɔ kpɔkɛ kɛ yɛnrɛdɔ ɛhane biala mɔɔ Abɔnsam babɔ mɔdenle kɛ ɔdua zo yeazɛkye yɛ koyɛlɛ ne na yeado yɛ sa nu la anu ɛlɛ. Gyihova nee Gyisɛse ɛbɔ ɛwɔkɛ kɛ saa yɛkɔ zo yɛbeta bɛ a, bɛ nee yɛ balua dahuu. Bieko, yɛ nee yɛ mediema diedima amuala a ɛlɛbɔ nu aho atia Abɔnsam yɛ kpɔvolɛ kpole ne a. Eza yɛ nee bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛdwazo bɛtia yɛ la bali ye kɛnlɛma kɛ mɔɔ Gyisɛse dule yɛ folɛ la. (Luku 6:27, 28) Ɔti mmamaa ɛ sa nu to wɔ yɛ nee Seetan konle ne anu. Nyia anwodozo kɛ ɛbahola wɔali konim wɔ ɛ nee Seetan nee ye sunsum ɛtane ne mɔ konle ne anu.
