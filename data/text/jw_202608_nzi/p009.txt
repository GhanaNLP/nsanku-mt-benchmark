Dwɔnatan boale Devidi noko Sɔɔlo anye bolole Devidi, na yemɔ a zɛkyele diedi mɔɔ Sɔɔlo lɛ wɔ Gyihova anu la a
<<<P>>>
(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 4, 6)
<<<P>>>
Gyihova ahyehyɛdeɛ ne na bɔbɔ yɛakpɔ bɛ.—Wlo. 16:17.
<<<P>>>
4 Maa yɛnlea neazo bie. Belemgbunli Sɔɔlo dwazole tiale Devidi mɔɔ ɛnee ɔdi nɔhalɛ la. Mɔlebɛbo ne ɛnee Sɔɔlo kulo Devidi edwɛkɛ kpalɛ. Ɔ nye liele kɛzi Devidi ze konle nye zo la anwo kpalɛ. (1 Sa. 18:5) Noko nzinlii mɔɔ Sɔɔlo nwunle kɛ menli ne kulo Devidi edwɛkɛ tɛla ye la, ɔmaanle ɔnyianle ɔ nwo adwenle ɛtane. Ɛhye ati, Sɔɔlo bɔle mɔdenle fane dɔɔnwo kɛ ɔku Devidi. (1 Sa. 18:9-11) Saa ɔholale ɔhunle Devidi a, anrɛɛ ɔbahile kɛ ɔlɛyɛ mɔɔ Seetan kpondɛ la. Duzu ati ɔ? Ɛnee Gyihova ɛmaa bɛlimoa bɛkpokpa Devidi amaa kenle bie yeayɛ Yizilayɛma belemgbunli. (1 Sa. 16:13) Saa zɔhane edwɛkɛ ne amba nu a, anrɛɛ Seetan anye balie kpole. Sɔɔlo hɔle zo vale Devidi anwo ɛya kpole na ɛnee ɔkulo kɛ ɔku ye wɔ adenle biala azo. Mɔɔ Seetan nwunle ye zɔhane la, ɔbayɛ kɛ ɔ nye liele kpalɛ.
<<<P>>>
4. Duzu ati a Belemgbunli Sɔɔlo dwazole tiale Devidi a? (Eza nea nvoninli ne.)
<<<P>>>
5 Seetan ze kɛ saa koyɛlɛ tɛnla yɛ avinli a, ɔbamaa Gyihova ayila yɛ na yeaboa yɛ. (Edw. 133:1) Kɛmɔ Seetan kulo kɛ ɔsɛkye zɔhane koyɛlɛ ne la ati, ɔkulo kɛ yɛtwe nungule na yɛ nye bolo yɛ mediema Kilisienema kɛ mɔɔ Sɔɔlo anye bolole Devidi la. Seetan ze kɛ saa yɛnyia adiema bie anwo adwenle ɛtane a, ɔnrɛmaa yɛnrɛla ɛlɔlɛ ali yɛnrɛhile ye. (Maa-ke 12:30, 31) Saa yɛanla ɛlɔlɛ ali yɛangile yɛ mediema a, asoo Gyihova balie yɛ ɛzonlenlɛ ne ado nu ɔ? Gyemise bɔle yɛ kɔkɔ ɛsesebɛ kɛ esiane kpole wɔ nu kɛ yɛbanyia yɛ mediema anwo adwenle ɛtane.—Kenga Gyemise 3:14-16.
