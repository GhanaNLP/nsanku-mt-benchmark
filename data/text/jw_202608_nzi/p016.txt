Nyia ɛ mediema diedima anwo adwenle kpalɛ na ka bɛ nwo edwɛkɛ kpalɛ kɛ mɔɔ Pɔɔlo yɛle la
<<<P>>>
(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 8)
<<<P>>>
ɛtane a, ɔnrɛmaa awie mɔ nee bɛ ɛnrɛli ye boɛ. Noko akee amaa yɛaha awie mɔ anwo edwɛkɛ kpalɛ la, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛnyia bɛ nwo adwenle kpalɛ. Duzu a baboa yɛ yeamaa yɛahola yɛayɛ zɔ a? Maa yɛzuzu debie mɔɔ ɛnee Pɔɔlo ta yɛ la anwo.
<<<P>>>
8 Ɛnee Pɔɔlo ta yɛ Gyihova mo wɔ Faelimɔn subane ngɛnlɛma ne mɔ anwo. (Fae. 4, 5) Eza ɔyɛle ye mo wɔ menli gyɛne dɔɔnwo subane kpalɛ nwo. (1 Kɔl. 1:4-7; Fel. 1:3-5; Kɔl. 1:3, 4; 1 Tɛs. 1:2, 3) Ɛnee Pɔɔlo nwu subane mɔɔ ɛnle kpalɛ mɔɔ ɔ mediema diedima da ye ali la, noko ɔbɔle mɔdenle kɛ ɔbanwu bɛ subane mgbalɛ ne mɔ. Na ɛnee ɔle ye subane kɛ ɔbayɛ Gyihova mo wɔ agɔnwolɛma ɛhye mɔ anwo. Na ɛdawɔ noko ɛ? Ɔyɛ se ɔmaa wɔ kɛ ɛbaha awie mɔ anwo edwɛkɛ kpalɛ ɔ? Ɛnee kakye kɛ saa ɛbɔ mɔdenle kɛ ɛbanwu ɛ mediema subane ngɛnlɛma ne mɔ na wɔayɛ asɔne kɛ Gyihova ɛmaa ninyɛne ɛhɔ boɛ ɛmaa bɛ a, ɔnrɛmaa ɔnrɛyɛ se kɛ ɛbaha bɛ nwo edwɛkɛ kpalɛ.
<<<P>>>
9 Ɔnva nwo kɛ ɛnee Pɔɔlo wɔ ngyegyelɛ nu la, ɔhɛlɛle edwɛkɛ mɔɔ yɛ fɛ la wɔ Faelimɔn kɛlata ne anu. Asoo yera ye ɛlɛ kɛ kɛmɔ ɛnee wɔnve
<<<P>>>
8. Duzu bahola aboa yɛ yeamaa yɛanyia yɛ mediema diedima anwo adwenle kpalɛ na yɛaha bɛ nwo edwɛkɛ kpalɛ a? (Eza nea nvoninli ne.)
<<<P>>>
9. Duzu ati a edwɛkɛ agbɔkɛ mɔɔ Pɔɔlo vale hɛlɛle Faelimɔn kɛlata ne la yɛ anyelielɛ a?
<<<P>>>
kpalɛ la ati ɔmaanle ɛdeɛdeanle awie nu ɔ? Saa yɛwɔ tɛnlabelɛ mɔɔ ɛnle kpalɛ la anu a, ɔnyɛ se kɛ yɛbava awie mɔ anwo ɛya na yɛadendɛ ɛtane yɛahile bɛ. Ɛnee Pɔɔlo noko wɔ tɛnlabelɛ mɔɔ ɛnle kpalɛ la anu wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɔkɛlɛ Faelimɔn kɛlata ne la. Ɔka ɛvolɛ ekyii kolaa na yeahɛlɛ zɔhane kɛlata ne la, menli bie yɛle ye basabasa na bɛhyele ye. Bɛlimoale bɛhyele ye wɔ Gyɛlusalɛm yɛɛ bɛhyele ye bieko wɔ Sizalia. Nzinlii mɔɔ bɛ nee ye ɛlɛkɔ Wulumu la, meli ne butule adenle nu. Mekɛ mɔɔ ɔkɛlɛ kɛlata yeahɔmaa Faelimɔn la ɛnee ‘ɔla efiade’ wɔ ɔdaye mumua ne ye sua nu wɔ Wulumu. (Fae. 1) Noko asoo Pɔɔlo maanle ngyegyelɛ ɛhye mɔ mɔɔ ɔhɔle nu la hanle kɛzi ɔhɛlɛle Faelimɔn kɛlata ne la ɔ? Kyɛkyɛ. Pɔɔlo vale edwɛkɛ agbɔkɛ mɔɔ da atiakunlukɛnlɛma nee ɛbulɛ ali la a hɛlɛle ye kɛlata ne a. Duzu a yɛsukoa yɛfi nu a? Saa yɛnɛ anzɛɛ yɛgyi ngyegyelɛ bie anloa bɔbɔ a, yɛbahola yɛ nee awie mɔ ali ye kɛnlɛma na yemɔ bamaa anzodwolɛ ara yɛ avinli. Na saa yɛde anzodwolɛ nu a, ɔbaboa yɛ yeamaa yɛahola yɛagyinla yɛ ngyegyelɛ ne mɔ anloa kpalɛ.
