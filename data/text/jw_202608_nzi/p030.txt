Yɛlɛyɛ maangyebakyi nu gyima ne bie wɔ New Caledonia
<<<P>>>
me mediema ne mɔ la le ekyi somaa. Ɔti ɛnee mefa mekɛ dɔɔnwo a mesiezie gyimalilɛ mɔɔ mebanyia la a, ɔluakɛ ɛnee mekulo kɛ debie biala yɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ. Noko ɛnee me sa nu ta to ɔluakɛ me nye anzɔ biala mɔɔ mengolale menyɛle la. (Nol. 7:16) Ɛnee ɔhyia kɛ mesukoa kɛ Gyihova le Selɛ mɔɔ lɛ ɛlɔlɛ na ɔ nye ɛnla kɛ yɛbayɛ debie biala pɛpɛɛpɛ. Ɔ nye sɔ mɔdenle biala mɔɔ yɛbɔ la.
<<<P>>>
Mɔɔ yewɔ Paris la, bɛkpale me kɛ maangyebakyi zo neavolɛ ne agyakɛnugyinlavolɛ. Mendele nganeɛ kɛ mele kakula somaa yɛɛ menze ɛhwee. Noko menwunle kɛ Gyihova sunsum nwuanzanwuanza ne boa yɛ maa yɛkola yɛyɛ ninyɛne dɔɔnwo mɔɔ ɔhale yɛ ngomekye a, anrɛɛ yɛnrɛhola yɛnrɛyɛ la.
<<<P>>>
Ɛnee melɛ ngyegyelɛ bieko. Ɔvi me ngakula nu amuala, ɛnee melɛ ewule bie mɔɔ maa mete ɛhɛlɛnra dɔɔnwo a, na me sa nu a te ɛhɛlɛnra kpalɛ a. Saa me ahonle tu, titili megyi menli nyunlu melɛtendɛ wɔ nyianu bo a, ɛnee akee edwɛkɛ ne ɛzɛkye bɔkɔɔ. Noko saa mesɛlɛ Gyihova kɛ ɔmaa me adwenle ɛgyinla a ɔmaa ɔka aze ekyi. Ngyegyelɛ ne ɛtefile ɛkɛ ɛdeɛ, noko saa mefa me nwo meto Gyihova anwo zo a ɔmaa mesie me nwo dii na mende ɛhɛlɛnra dɔɔnwo.
<<<P>>>
Wɔ 2011, Gyihova maanle yɛ sa hanle nwolɛ adenle bie mɔɔ ɛnee yɛ nye ɛla ye ade mekɛ tendenle la. Bɛmaanle yɛhɔyɛle edwɛkpatɛlɛvolɛma wɔ New Caledonia. Ɔle mbɔra ngyikyi bie mɔɔ wɔ Pacific Nyevile ne bɛne zo nehane a. Ɔvi Paris
<<<P>>>
mɔɔ kɔ ɛkɛ ne la wale kpalɛ. Ɛfa alopile a ɛbali dɔnehwele 20 kolaa na wɔadwu. Ɛnee gyimalilɛ ɛhye lɛ ye ngyegyelɛ ɛdeɛ. Ɛnee ɔwɔ kɛ yɛsukoa kɛ yɛbali bɛdɛ, daboase yɛɛ elue. Ɔdwu aleɛbahye a ɛte kɛ ngɔkɔlɛ ɛlɛbɔ yɛɛ ahwea noko ɛlɛkpo na ɔmmaa yɛngola da, noko ɛnee ɔwɔ kɛ yɛsukoa kɛ bɛleyɛ dede bɔbɔ a yɛbala. Eza ɛnee ɔwɔ kɛ yɛsukoa kɛzi ɛkɛ ne amra di adwelie la. Kɛ neazo la, saa ɛbiza sua ne azo amra edwɛkɛ a bɛtwe bɛ nye anzi a bɛfa bɛdie zolɛ a. Ɛnee ɔwɔ kɛ yɛsukoa ɛhye mɔ amuala.
<<<P>>>
Eza Gyihova boale yɛ maanle yɛyɛle gyimalilɛ fofolɛ biala mɔɔ bɛvale bɛmaanle yɛ la. Yɛyɛle edwɛkpatɛlɛvolɛ gyima ne bie yɛɛ yɛyɛle gyima ɛvolɛ ekyii wɔ Bɛtɛle. Nzinlii yɛhɔle Belemgbunlililɛ Edwɛkpakavolɛma Sukulu ne bie na kɛkala mele maangyebakyi zo neavolɛ. Yɛnwu kɛzi Gyihova ɛlua ye sunsum nwuanzanwuanza ne azo ɛboa yɛ wɔ gyimalilɛ biala anu la. Gyihova ɛmaa yɛnwu ye kɛ yɛ sinlidɔlɛ ne mɔ ɛngile kɛ yɛ nwo ɛngyia ye. Ɛhye ati a meyɛ asɔne dahuu kɛ ɔboa me ɔmaa menyɛ mɔɔ hyia la. Megyi menli nyunlu melɛtendɛ a me ahonle tɛtu ɛdeɛ, noko ɔlua Gyihova moalɛ zo mekola memaa mediema mɔɔ yɛkɔkpɔla bɛ la anwosesebɛ na meboa bɛ memaa bɛdi Gyihova adehilelɛ zo.
<<<P>>>
Yɛyiale ngyegyelɛ dɔɔnwo ɛdeɛ noko Claudine boale me wɔ ɔ muala anu. Ɔlɛ me nwo abotane. Ɔ nye die nwo kɛ ɔbayɛ gyima biala mɔɔ bɛbava bɛamaa yɛ la yɛɛ dahuu ɔlɛ ɛhulolɛ kɛ ɔbali awie mɔ daselɛ. Ɔndendɛ ɔ nzo abo wɔ wuluwulu anzɛɛ ngyegyelɛ gyɛne mɔɔ yɛyia wɔ maangyebakyi gyima ne anu la anwo.
<<<P>>>
Wɔ nyianu mɔɔ menlimoale mengɔle la abo, menwunle kɛ Gyihova, yɛ Bɔvolɛ Kpole ne nea ye azonvolɛ dahuu. Ɛhye ati yemaa yɛ nyilalɛ dɔɔnwo. Yeboa me yemaa mede deɛmɔti ɔwɔ kɛ yɛbu arane mgbanyima ne mɔ la abo. Eza yeboa me yemaa menzukoa mɛlɛbɛnwoaze. Mendwenle me nwo menvile agɔnwolɛma dɛba mɔɔ melɛ la anwo amaa meahola meava Gyihova ngyinlazɔ ne mɔ meabɔ me ɛbɛla. Noko Gyihova ɛmaa me agɔnwolɛma dɔɔnwo. Menyia yelɛ kpalɛ. Na me nye la mekɛ mɔɔ yɛbadɛnla aze dahuu na Gyihova ahɔ zo amaa yɛ ninyɛne dɔɔnwo la ade.—Maake 10:29, 30.
