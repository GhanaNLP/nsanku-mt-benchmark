Gyihova dua Gyisɛse Kelaese anwo zo die yɛ
(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 6)
<<<P>>>
Gyisɛse ɛdɛnla “Devidi belemgbunli ebia ne” azo ɛli tumi ɔvi 1914. (Luku 1:32, 33) Kɛ Nyamenle Belemgbunlililɛ ne azo Belemgbunli la, ɔ nee bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛ nee ye bali tumi wɔ anwuma yɛɛ ye anwumabɔvolɛ nwuanzanwuanzama ne baye Nyamenle agbɔvolɛ mɔɔ wɔ azɛlɛ ye azo la avi ɛkɛ ne bɔkɔɔ wɔ Amagɛdɔn ne anu. Akee wɔ Ɛvolɛ Apenle Ko Tumililɛ ne anu, Gyisɛse badwazo mowuamra na ɔ nee menli 144,000 ne mɔɔ bɛ nee ye bali tumi la bamaa alesama ali munli bieko ngyikyi ngyikyi. (Dwɔn 5:28; Yek. 22:1, 2) Ɛvolɛ apenle ko tumililɛ ne kara awieleɛ la, ɛnee bɛye ɛtane mɔɔ bɛvale bɛwole yɛ nee ewule bɛvi ɛkɛ ne bɔkɔɔ. (1 Kɔl. 15:26, 55-57) Akee bɛbazɛkye Seetan, ye sunsum ɛtane ne mɔ yɛɛ alesama mɔɔ bɛlile bɛ nzi wɔ sɔnea mɔɔ li awieleɛ la anu la bɔkɔɔ. (Yek. 20:7-10) Akee nɔhavoma bahola azonle Gyihova mɔɔ ɛtane, ewule anzɛɛ dwazotiama biala ɛndenda bɛ gyakɛ anu a. Yɛbaye yɛ Nyamenle ne ayɛlɛ na yɛahile “ye ahyɛlɛdeɛ ne mɔɔ bɛngola bɛngilehile nu,” mɔɔ bie a le Kelaese Gyisɛse mɔɔ ɔbalua ɔ nwo zo yealie yɛ ngoane la anwo anyezɔlɛ dahuu.—2 Kɔl. 9:15.

NWOLƐ ADENLE MƆƆ YƐNYIA YƐLƐSONLE NYAMENLE LA
<<<P>>>
7 Kenga Luku 1:74, 75. Ɛkpɔnedeɛ ahyɛlɛdeɛ ne mɔɔ Gyihova ɛva ɛmaa yɛ la maa yɛnyia debie titili bie. Zɛkalaya hanle nwolɛ edwɛkɛ kɛ, “ɔbamaa yɛanyia adenle yɛazonle ye dahuu mɔɔ yɛnzulo.” Ndenle boni a yɛdua zo yɛsonle Gyihova a? Saa yɛbɔ edwɛkpa ne nolo, yɛyɛ asɔne, yɛsukoa ye Edwɛkɛ ne na yɛkɔ debiezukoalɛ nee nyianu ngakyile a, ɛnee yɛlɛsonle Gyihova. Eza saa yɛyɛ gyima wɔ Bɛtɛle anzɛɛ yɛboa yɛmaa bɛsisi anzɛɛ bɛsiezie Belemgbunlililɛ Asalo nee Nyianu Asalo ngakyile a, ɛnee yɛlɛsonle Gyihova.
