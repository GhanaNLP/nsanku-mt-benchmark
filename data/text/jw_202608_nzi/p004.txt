Seetan ewiade ne soa ninyɛne mɔɔ ɔbahola yeamaa yeayɛ se kɛ yɛbali Nyamenle ngyinlazo ne mɔ azo la gua yɛ nwo zo
<<<P>>>
(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 11)
<<<P>>>
4:3, 4) Kilisiene asɔne asɔne ne mɔ ta kilehile kɛ Selɛ ne nee Ralɛ ne le ko. Ɛhye ati ɔmaa menli ye bɛ adwenle fi Selɛ ne mɔɔ bɛnze ye duma bɔbɔ la anwo zo na bɛsonle ɔ Ra ne. Gyihova nee Gyisɛse ɛnlie ɛzonlenlɛ zɛhae ɛndo nu. (Mat. 4:10; Dwɔn 4:23) Saa ɔsi menli adwenle ne anye zɛhae a, ɔmaa ɔyɛ se kɛ ɛ nee bɛ bazuzu edwɛkɛ nwo na bɛade ɔ bo anzɛɛ bie bɔbɔ a ɔmaa bɛdwazo bɛtia yɛ ɛzonlenlɛ gyima ne.
<<<P>>>
9 Saa yɛkulo kɛ yɛfa awie agɔnwolɛ a, ninyɛne mɔɔ yɛdimoa yɛyɛ la anu ko a le kɛ yɛbabi-za ahenle duma. Kɛmɔ Seetan ɛngulo kɛ menli fa Gyihova agɔnwolɛ la ati, ɔmkpondɛ kɛ bɛnwu Ye duma. Ɔti yelua adalɛ ɛzonlenlɛ zo yemaa menli arɛle ɛvi Nyamenle duma ne wɔ ɛvolɛ dɔɔnwo anu. (Kenga Gyɛlɛmaya 23:26, 27.) Kpɔvolɛ zɔhane ɛbɔ mɔdenle ɛmaa bɛye Nyamenle duma ne mɔɔ Baebolo ne fa toto aranemgbole mɔɔ anwo yɛ se na ɔbɔ yɛ nwo bane anwo la bɛvi Baebolo ngilebɛbo dɔɔnwo anu. (Mrɛ. 18:10) Eza Abɔnsam a ɛmaa menli ɛnyia adwenle kɛ Gyihova a maa yɛnwu amaneɛ a. Yemɔti ɔmaa ɔyɛ se kɛ menli mɔɔ bɛkulo Gyihova la babu ye kɛ ɔle bɛ Gɔnwo mɔɔ bɛbahola bɛabikye ye a. (Gye. 4:8) Bieko, Seetan kulo kɛ ɔmaa Gyihova menli bɔbɔ diedi ka aze.
