13 Saa arane nu mgbanyima di mɛla mɔɔ ɔmmaa yɛnnyia fanwodi yɛnzonle yɛ Nyamenle ne anzɛɛ ɔmaa ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nu yɛ se maa yɛ wɔ adenle bie azo bɔbɔ a, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛbu bɛ. Duzu ati ɔ? Ɔluakɛ Nyamenle Edwɛkɛ ne se ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ ye zɔ. (Wlo. 13:1, 2) Tɛ Seetan a gyi kpɔkɛ ɛtane biala mɔɔ arane mgbanyima ne mɔ basisi la anzi a. Noko ɔ nye la kɛ yɛbadwazo yɛatia bɛ kpɔkɛzilɛ zɔhane mɔ anzɛɛ bɛbamaa yɛadendɛ yɛ nzo abo. Saa menli anye ɛnlie mɛla anzɛɛ kpɔkɛ bie mɔɔ arane nu mgbanyima sisi la anwo a, bɛto nwolɛ mgbonde. Ɔnle kɛ Kilisienema fa bɛ nwo wulowula ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ anu. (Mrɛ. 24:21, 22, ɔbdw.; Nol. 8:2, 3) Saa yɛfa yɛ nwo yɛwulowula maanyɛlɛ edwɛkɛ nu na yɛ rele fi kɛ yɛngyi afoa biala a, ɔbamaa Seetan anye alie.—Dwɔn 15:19.

14 Menli mɔɔ dɛba ne ɛnee bɛdwazo bɛtia Gyihova Alasevolɛ la bie mɔ ɛrayɛ yɛ mediema diedima mɔɔ bɔ mɔdenle ɛnɛ. (1 Tim. 1:12, 13) Ɔti saa ‘menli bɔ wɔ aholoba na bɛyɛ wɔ kpɔdekpɔde na bɛbɔ adalɛ bɛfa edwɛkɛ bɛto ɛ nwo zo’ a, kakye kɛ bɛbahola bɛahakyi bɛarayɛ diedima. (Mat. 5:11) Yɛ asɔne kɛ Gyihova ɛmaa menli mɔɔ lɛ tumi la ɛmaa yɛ adenle amaa yɛahɔ zo yɛazonle ye wɔ anzodwolɛ nu.
