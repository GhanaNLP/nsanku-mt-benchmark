16 Yɛdwenledwenle moalɛ mɔɔ Gyihova nee Gyisɛse fa maa yɛ ɛnɛ la anwo a, eza ɔbahola yeaboa yɛ yeamaa yɛalie yɛali kɛ bɛkulo yɛ. Kɛ neazo la, saa yɛkakye mekɛ bie mɔɔ Gyihova buale yɛ asɔneyɛlɛ bie anzɛɛ ɔmaanle yɛ anwosesebɛ mɔɔ yɛhyia nwo la a, ɔbamaa yɛanyia anwodozo kɛ ɔbahɔ zo yeanlea yɛ. Eza yɛbahola yɛabiza yɛ mediema yɛamaa bɛaha kɛzi bɛnwu kɛ Gyihova ɛboa bɛ la anwo edwɛkɛ bɛahile yɛ. (Mrɛ. 22:17) Saa yɛyɛ zɔ a, ɔbamaa yɛanyia anwodozo kɛ ngyegyelɛ biala mɔɔ yɛbanyia la Gyihova nee Gyisɛse nee yɛ lua.—Kenga Edwɛne 118:5, 6.

17 Yɛ muala yɛkulo kɛ yɛyɛ Gyihova Nyamenle nee Gyisɛse Kelaese agɔnwo mɔ dahuu. Noko kolaa na bɛaye Seetan nee ye sunsum ɛtane ne mɔ bɛavi ɛkɛ ne bɔkɔɔ la, bɛkulo kɛ bɛdabɛ noko bɛsɛkye anwodozo mɔɔ yɛlɛ wɔ Gyihova nee Gyisɛse anu la. Ɔti ɔwɔ kɛ yɛmia yɛ nye yɛko yɛtia bɛ. (Yek. 20:1-3, 10) Amaa yɛandɔ yɛ agbɔvolɛ ɛhye mɔ ɛhane ne anu la, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛfa Gyihova agɔnwolɛ kyengye na yɛdie yɛdi kɛ ɔ nee ye kulovolɛ Ralɛ ne kulo yɛ yɛɛ yɛ nwo hyia bɛ. Na kɛ mɔɔ Gyisɛse hanle la, “awie biala ɛnrɛhola ɛnrɛlie [wɔ] ɛnrɛvi Selɛ ne asa anu.”—Kenga Dwɔn 10:29; Gye. 4:7.

18 Wɔ edwɛkɛ bieko anu, yɛbazuzu ndenle gyɛne nwiɔ mɔɔ Seetan fa di gyima wɔ yɛ nee ye konle ne anu la. Bɛmɛ a le mɔdenle mɔɔ ɔbɔ kɛ ɔfa mgbakyemgbakyenu yeara asafo ne nu yɛɛ menli mɔɔ bɛnle asafo ne anu mɔɔ ɔmaa bɛdwazo bɛtia yɛ la. Eza yɛbanlea mɔɔ yɛyɛ a yɛbahɔ zo yɛali nɔhalɛ yɛamaa Gyihova ɔnva nwo mɔɔ Seetan bayɛ la.
