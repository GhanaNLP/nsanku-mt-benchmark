16 Zɛkalaya edwɛkɛ mɔɔ wɔ Luku 1:68-79 la maa yɛkakye ninyɛne nsa mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkile nwolɛ anyezɔlɛ la. Bɛmɛ a le Gyihova ngoane-lielɛ ahyɛlɛdeɛ ne mɔɔ yɛ nwanwane la, nwolɛ adenle mɔɔ yɛnyia yɛlɛsonle Nyamenle mɔɔ yɛnzulo yɛɛ edwɛkɛhanlɛ gyima ne mɔɔ kye-kye menli arɛle la. Wɔnyia Kelaese ɛkpɔnedeɛ ne anu diedi na wɔyila ɛ nwo zo wɔmaa Gyiho-va wɔva wɔhile kɛ wɔlie ye ahyɛlɛdeɛ ne wɔdo nu ɔ? Asoo ɛ nye sɔ nwolɛ adenle mɔɔ wɔnyia ɛlɛsonle ye la? Saa ɛ nye sɔ ye a, ɛnee bɔ mɔ-denle yɛ gyima mɔɔ bɛva bɛmaa wɔ kɛ ɛbɔ “Gyihova ɛlolɛ mekɛ ne” nolo la. Ka Gyihova “nzenralɛlilɛ kenle ne,” mekɛ mɔɔ ɔbava yea-zɛkye yɛ nee ye agbɔvolɛ na yeamaa azɛlɛ ne ayɛ Paladaese la anwo edwɛkɛ kile awie mɔ. To ɛsalɛ fɛlɛ menli mɔɔ kulo Gyihova la amuala maa yɛbɔ nu yɛzonle “ye dahuu mɔɔ yɛnzulo wɔ nɔhalɛlilɛ nee tenleneyɛlɛ nu wɔ ɔ nyunlu wɔ yɛ ngoane mekɛ kɔsɔɔti anu.”—Luku 1:74, 75.

NVONINLI NE ANWO NGILENU Mukelɛ 24: Nvoninli mɔɔ limoa la anu, mediema mraalɛ nwiɔ ɛlɛyɛ azua nu daselɛ-lilɛ. Nvoninli mɔɔ tɔ zo nwiɔ la anu, adiema nrenyia bie ɛlɛdi ɔ gɔnwo gyimayɛvo bie daselɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛlɛdie bɛ menle la. Mɔɔ tɔ zo nsa la anu, adiema bɛlɛra bie ɛlɛ-kilehile daselɛ mɔɔ kile kɛ bɛbɔle ninyɛne la anu na ye ki-lehilevolɛ ne nee ɔ gɔnwo mɔ sukuluma ɛlɛtie ye kpalɛ.
