Zɛkalaya hanle kɛ: ‘Gyihova ɛkpogya ngoanelielɛ mɛne [anzɛɛ, ngoanedievolɛ mɔɔ lɛ tumi] bie zo yemaa yɛ wɔ ye sonvolɛ Devidi sua nu.’ (Luku 1:69) Zɔhane ngoanedievolɛ ne mɔɔ lɛ tumi la a le Mɛzaya ne. Enee Zɛkalaya mekɛ zo Dwuuma ne anye la Mɛzaya ne ɛsesebɛ, yɛɛ enee bɛze kɛ ɔbayɛ Ebileham abozonli na ɔvi Devidi dehelɛ abusua ɛlɔnwɔne ne anu a ɔbara a. (Gyn. 22:17, 18; Mat. 22:42) Enee akee duzu ati a Gyihova maanle Zɛkalaya hanle ngoanedievolɛ ɛhye mɔɔ lɛ tumi la anwo edwɛkɛ a? Oluakɛ enee Mɛzaya ne ɛralɛ ɛbikye kpalɛ! Ɔkadwu siane nsia la, bɛlɛra bie mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Mɛle la bawo Gyisɛse. Anwumabɔvolɛ Geebeleɛle hanle kɛ bɛbavɛlɛ ye “Nyamenle Ara” na ɔbadɛnla “ɔ ze Devidi belemgbunli ebia” zo yeali Belemgbunli. (Luku 1:30-35) Mɔɔ Gyisɛse lile ɛvolɛ 30 na bɛzɔnenle ye la yɛɛ ɔrayɛle ngoanedievolɛ ne mɔɔ lɛ tumi la a. Mekɛ zɔhane a Gyihova vale ye sunsum nwuanzanwuanza ne kpokpale ye na ɔrayɛle Mɛzaya ne a. (Mat. 3:16, 17) Ɛhye le anyelielɛ edwɛkɛ kpalɛ!

3 Enee ɔhyia kɛ bɛdie Zɛkalaya mekɛ zo Dwuuma ne. Noko nwane anzɛɛ duzu debie a ɔwɔ kɛ bɛdie bɛ bɛfi ɔ sa nu a? Zɛkalaya buale kɛ: “Ɔbalie yɛ yeavi yɛ agbɔvolɛ nee bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛkyi yɛ la amuala asa anu.” (Luku 1:71) Asoo ɛhye kile kɛ Gyisɛse balie bɛ avi Wulomuma nzisi tumililɛ ne abo ɔ? Kyɛkyɛ. Emomu enee ɔwɔ kɛ bɛdie Dwuuma ne bɛfi Seetan, nzisima kpanyinli ne asa nu. (Hib. 2:14, 15) Seetan a ɛmaa alesama, mɔɔ tete Dwuuma boka nwo la ɛyɛ ngɛkɛlɛ ɛmaa ɛtane nee ewule a. Eza enee Dwuuma ne ɛngola ɛnli Mosisi Mɛla ne azo pɛpɛɛpɛ. Ɛhye ati enee munzule ɛha bɛ na ɔwɔ kɛ bɛdie bɛ. (Wlo. 3:20; Gal. 3:10, 13) Enee bɛhyia debie mɔɔ Gyihova luale Zɛkalaya anwo zo vale bɔle bɛ ɛwɔkɛ la—“ɔbava bɛ ɛtane yeahyɛ bɛ na yealie bɛ ngoane.”—Luku 1:77.

4 Agbɔvolɛ boni mɔ a ɔwɔ kɛ bɛdie yɛ bɛfi bɛ sa nu ɛnɛ a? Yɛdayɛ noko yɛyɛ ngɛkɛlɛ yɛmaa ɛtane nee ewule kɛ Gyisɛse mekɛ zo Dwuuma ne la. (Wlo. 5:12) Eza Seetan nee sunsum evinli ne mɔ maa yɛnwu amaneɛ. Bɛbɔ mɔdenle kɛ bɛbɛlɛbɛla yɛ bɛamaa yɛahwe yɛ nwo yɛavi nɔhalɛ ɛzonlenlɛ nwo. Bɛsi menli nee ahyehyɛdeɛ ngakyile mɔɔ bie mɔ boka “bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛkyi yɛ” anwo la bɔbɔ adua bɛmaa bɛdwazo bɛtia yɛ ɛzonlenlɛ ne. Ɔhyia kɛ bɛdie yɛ bɛfi yɛ agbɔvolɛ ɛhye mɔ asa nu.

5 Duzu a yɛ Ngoanedievolɛ ne yɛ die yɛ fi agbɔvolɛ ɛhye mɔ asa nu a? Maa yɛnlea mɔɔ Gyisɛse yɛ die yɛ fi ɛtane sa nu la. Mɔɔ Gyisɛse wɔ azɛlɛ ye azo la, ɔhilehilele ye ɛdoavolɛma ne kɛzi bɛbazonle Gyihova wɔ adenle mɔɔ ɔdie ɔto nu la azo la. Akee ɔvale ye ngoane ɔyɛle ɛkpɔnedeɛ “amaa awie biala mɔɔ kɛla ye ali kɛ ɔle diedi wɔ ye nu la bɛanzɛkye ye na yeanyia dahuu ngoane.” (Dwɔn 3:16; Mat. 20:28) Ɔti yɛyɛ biala mɔɔ yɛbahola la yɛsonle Gyihova wɔ adenle mɔɔ ɔdie ɔto nu la azo kɛ mɔɔ Gyisɛse hilehilele la. Noko kɛmɔ yɛnli munli la ati, saa yɛangola yeanli Gyihova ngyinlazo ne mɔ azo a, yɛsɛlɛ ye kɛ ɔva yɛ ɛtane ɔhyɛ yɛ. Na ɔlua ɛkpɔnedeɛ ne ati, Gyihova balie yɛ ɛzonlenlɛ ado nu. (1 Dwɔn 2:1, 2) Adenle zɛhae a yɛ Ngoanedievolɛ Gyisɛse dua zo die yɛ fi ɛtane sa nu na ɔmaa yɛnyia anyelazo kɛ yɛbadɛnla aze dahuu a.
