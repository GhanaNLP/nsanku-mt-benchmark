7 Eza Seetan dua adenle gyɛne zo kɛ ɔmaa mgbakyemgbakyenu ara yɛ nee yɛ mediema Kilisienema avinli. Kɛmɔ yevi ɛleka ngakyile la ati, yɛlɛ subane ngakyile yɛɛ asolo mɔɔ awie biala kulo la. Seetan kulo kɛ yɛnyia adwenle kɛ, kɛmɔ yɛvi ɛleka ngakyile la ati yɛnrɛhola yɛnrɛyɛ ko. Ɔkulo kɛ yɛnyia yɛ mediema nwo adwenle ɛtane na yɛfa bɛ nwo ɛya. Duzu a ɛyɛ a ɔnrɛmaa Seetan ɛnrɛnyia adenle ɛnrɛva mgbakyemgbakyenu ɛnrɛra yɛ avinli a? Ɛ nee ɛ mediema ɛziezie akɛsakɛsa biala mɔɔ bara bɛ avinli la ndɛndɛ. (Kenga Ɛfɛsɛsema 4:25-27.) Nyia ɛ mediema anwo ɛlɔlɛ kpole. Eza nyia bɛ nwo adwenle kpalɛ. Ɔnva nwo kɛ yɛle ngakyile la, yɛ muala yɛle sonlabaka ko anwo nvɛyeba. (1 Kɔl. 12:12) Yɛ nee yɛ mediema Kilisienema kɔsɔɔti a ɛlɛbɔ nu aho atia Seetan nee sunsum ɛtane ne mɔ mɔɔ bɛle yɛ agbɔvolɛ la a.—1 Pita 5:8, 9.

8 Saa ɛ diema diedinli bie ka edwɛkɛ anzɛɛ yɛ debie ɔmaa ɔgyegye wɔ a, kakye kɛ ɔdaye noko ɔnli munli kɛ ɛdawɔ ala la. Kɛmɔ ɛ nee ɛ mediema ta bɔ nu yɛ ninyɛne la ati, ɔwɔ kɛ ɛmaa ɔtɛnla wɔ adwenle nu kɛ edwɛkɛ ngyikyingyiki bie mɔ bahola ara bɛ avinli. (Nol. 7:20) Edwɛkɛ zɛhae bie ba a, fa Baebolo ne anu folɛdulɛ ne mɔɔ se bɛhɔ zo bɛnyia abotane bɛmaa bɛ nwo la di gyima. Saa awie yɛ debie maa ɔgyegye wɔ noko a fa kyɛ ye ndɛndɛ. (Kɔl. 3:12-14) Gyihova nee Gyisɛse kulo kɛ yɛyɛ ko, ɔti ɛdawɔ noko yɛ ye zɔ.—Dwɔn 17:20, 21.

9 Adenle bieko mɔɔ Seetan dua zo maa yɛ sa nu to la a le kpɔdekpɔdeyɛlɛ. Ɛzoanvolɛ Pɔɔlo bɔle kɔkɔ kɛ bɛbayɛ nɔhalɛ Kilisienema kɔsɔɔti kpɔdekpɔde. (2 Tim. 3:12) Kpɔdekpɔdeyɛlɛ kile kɛ bɛ nee yɛ ɛnrɛli ye boɛ. Saa bɛ nee yɛ anli ye boɛ a, bie a yɛbava yɛ adwenle yɛazie kɛzi yɛkulo kɛ bɛtwe zɔhane menli ne mɔ anzo, kɛzi ɔwɔ kɛ bɛbua yɛ benle anzɛɛ kɛzi yɛbali bɛ nzenralɛ la azo somaa. Saa yɛyɛ ye zɔ a, ɔnrɛmaa yɛnrɛnyia mekɛ yɛnrɛyɛ gyima mɔɔ anwo hyia kpalɛ mɔɔ Gyihova ɛva ɛmaa yɛ kɛ yɛyɛ, mɔɔ yemɔ a le kɛ yɛbabɔ Belemgbunlililɛ ne anwo edwɛkpa ne nolo la.—Wlo. 12:19-21.
