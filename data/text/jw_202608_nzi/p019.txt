17 Mɔɔ Pɔɔlo hanle kɛ ‘yɛhɔ zo yɛnyia abota-ne yɛmaa yɛ nwo’ la, duzu a ɛnee ɔlɛkile a? Ɛnee ɔlɛmaa yɛ anwosesebɛ kɛ saa yɛ mediema diedima ka yɛ ɛya anzɛɛ bɛfo yɛ bɔbɔ a, yɛla atiakunlukɛnlɛma ali yɛhile bɛ. Pɔɔlo eza hanle neazo kɛnlɛma mɔɔ Gyihova ɛyɛ wɔ ɛhye anwo la anwo edwɛkɛ wɔ ngyɛnu ko ne ala anu. Gyi-hova anye ɛnla kɛ ye azonvolɛ bayɛ bɛ debie biala pɛpɛɛpɛ. Ɔze yɛ sinlidɔlɛ ne mɔ noko ɔfa ye adwenle ɔsie yɛ subane mgbalɛ ne mɔ azo. (Edw. 103:13, 14; 130:3) Ɛhye ati ɔkɔ zo ɔda ye ɛlɔlɛ kpundii ne ali ɔkile yɛ na ɔse yɛ anwunvɔ-ne. (Awo. 3:22, 23) Saa yɛyɛ biala mɔɔ yɛbaho-la la yɛbu yɛ mediema diedima kɛ mɔɔ Gyiho-va bu bɛ la a, ɔnrɛyɛ se kɛ yɛbahɔ zo yɛanyia bɛ nwo abotane wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛbayɛ nvonleɛ la kɛ mɔɔ bɛdabɛ noko bɛnyia yɛ nwo abotane la. Wɔmɔ maa yɛyɛ ndonwo bie. Saa yɛkakye kɛzi baka bie felawɔse ne mɔ le ngɛnlɛma la a, ɔba-maa yɛanlea ye boɛ wɔ mekɛ bɔbɔ mɔɔ felawɔ-se ɛnle zo la. Zɔhane ala a saa yɛkakye subane mgbalɛ mɔɔ yɛ mediema lɛ la a, ɔbaha yɛ yea-maa yɛava bɛ ɛtane yɛahyɛ bɛ na yɛ nee bɛ ali ye kɛnlɛma wɔ mekɛ bɔbɔ mɔɔ bɛbaye yɛ rɛle bɛado yɛ sa nu la.

18 Gyihova anye sɔ mɔdenle biala mɔɔ ɛbɔ kɛ ɛboa wɔamaa anzodwolɛ ara asafo ne anu la. Ɔti kenle ko biala kpondɛ ndenle mɔɔ ɛbalua zo wɔaboa wɔamaa yɛadɛnla anzodwolɛ nu la. Te-ndɛ kɛnlɛma kile awie mɔ na ka bɛ nwo edwɛkɛ kpalɛ. Sukoa kɛ ɛbakpa kyɛlɛ. Saa ɛyɛ debie ɛmaa ɔgyegye awie a, ɛ nee ye ɛziezie bɛ avinli. Nyia ɛ mediema anwo ɛlɔlɛ kpole na yemɔ ba-boa wɔ yeamaa wɔava ɛtane wɔahyɛ bɔkɔɔ. Saa ɛyɛ zɔ a ɛbaboa asafo ne anu amra wɔamaa bɛadɛnla anzodwolɛ nu. Na ɛbaboka bɛlɛvo-ma mɔɔ ‘bɛbanyia azɛlɛ ne, na bɛ nye alie kpa-lɛ wɔ anzodwolɛ dɔɔnwo anu’ la anwo dahuu!—Edw. 37:11.
