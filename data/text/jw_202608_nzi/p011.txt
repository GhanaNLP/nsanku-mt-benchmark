Asoo ɛnrɛgyinla afoa biala amaa wɔali nɔhalɛ wɔamaa Gyihova ɔ?
(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 13)
<<<P>>>
anli ye boɛ a, asoo yɛbazi abotane na yɛakpondɛ anzodwolɛ ɔ?
<<<P>>>
11 Evolɛ ɛya dɔɔnwo anzi mɔɔ Gyisɛse, Mɛzaya ne mɔɔ bɛbɔle ɔ nwo ɛwɔkɛ la rale azɛlɛ ye azo la, ɔdaye noko menli dɔɔnwo nee ye anli ye boɛ. Kɛ neazo la, Gyisɛse mbusuafoɔ bie mɔ nyianle adwenle kɛ yezɛ. (Maake 3:21) Enee ɛzonlenlɛ nu mgbanyima bɔ mɔdenle dahuu kɛ bɛbamaa yeagyakyi edwɛkɛhanlɛ gyima ne. (Luku 11:15, 16) Ɔ gɔnwo kpalɛ ko yele ye maanle. (Mat. 26:14-16) Ɛsɔfo mgbanyima ne mɔ nee maanyɛlɛ nu mgbanyima bɔle adwenle ko na bɛbuale ye ewule fɔlɛ. (Maake 15:1, 13-15) Noko Gyisɛse ammaa dwazotia ne anzi ye adenle kɛ ɔbamaa ɔ nye abolo wɔ Gyihova ɛzonlenlɛ nu, yɛɛ yeanli bɛ nzenralɛ ɛlɛ. Mekɛ mɔɔ bɛhyele ye la, ɛzoanvolɛ Pita bɔle mɔdenle kɛ ɔko yealie ye. Pita vale dadeɛ bɔbɔ pɛle ɛsɔfo kpanyinli ne akɛlɛ bie anzo ko. Noko ɛnee Gyisɛse ɛngulo kɛ Pita yɛ zɔ, ɔti ɔluale nwanwane adenle zo ɔyɛle nrenyia ne ayile. (Luku 22:50, 51) Gyisɛse ammaa nzisi ɛhye mɔ amuala andwehwe ye adwenle anzɛɛ yeammaa yeambɔ mɔdenle kɛ ɔbali nzenralɛ.

12 Zɔhane ala a ɛnɛ noko yɛkɔ tɛnlabelɛ ngakyile mɔɔ bahola amaa yɛ sa nu ado la anu a. Seetan bɔ mɔdenle kɛ ɔnrɛmaa yɛnrɛyɛ edwɛkɛhanlɛ gyima ne. Kɛ neazo la, ɔmaa yɛ mbusuafoɔ mɔɔ bɛnle diedima anzɛɛ awie mɔ gyɛne dwazo tia yɛ anzɛɛ bɛyɛ yɛ kpɔdekpɔde. Noko Gyisɛse dule yɛ folɛ kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkulo bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛyɛ yɛ kpɔdekpɔde bɔbɔ la. (Kenga Mateyu 5:43, 44.) Ɛhye kile kɛ ɔnle kɛ yɛyɛ menli mɔɔ bɛyɛ yɛ kpɔdekpɔde la ɛtane. Emomu kɛ mɔɔ Gyisɛse hanle la, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ biala mɔɔ yɛbahola la yɛ nee menli kɔsɔɔti di ye wɔ atiakunlukɛnlɛma, abotane yɛɛ ɛbulɛ nu saa bɔbɔ bɛdwazo bɛtia yɛ anzɛɛ yɛ gyima ne a.—1 Kɔl. 13:4.
