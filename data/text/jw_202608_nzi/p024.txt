13 Dwɔn bɔle mɔdenle hanle hilele menli dɔɔnwo kɛ Gyisɛse a le Mɛzaya ne a, na ɛnee ɛhye hyia kpalɛ. Duzu ati ɔ? Zɛkalaya hanle kɛ Mɛzaya ne ɛralɛ ne bayɛ “alehyenlɛ wienyi.” Ɛhye kile kɛ saa Gyisɛse ba a ɔbamaa menli kɔsɔɔti ɛbɛlabɔlɛ ahola ahakyi! Ɛnee ɔwɔ kɛ bɛbɔ edwɛkpa ɛhye nolo bɛkile awie biala, noko Dwɔn angomekye ɛnrɛhola ɛnrɛyɛ gyima ɛhye. Nwane bieko a baboka Dwɔn anwo yeamaa bɛabɔ ngoanelielɛ edwɛkɛ ɛhye nolo a?
<<<P>>>
14 Gyisɛse bɔle edwɛkpa ɛhye nolo. Ɔdaye a Zɛkalaya hanle ɔ nwo edwɛkɛ kɛ ɔbamaa “bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛde awozinli nee ewule alubo bo la anwu wienyi” la. Ɛnee ɔ kunlu yelɛyela ye maa bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛ nee bɛ ɛnli ye boɛ la. Ɔti ɔhanle “Gyihova ɛlolɛ mekɛ ne” mɔɔ ɔbakyekye bɛ rɛle la anwo edwɛkɛ ɔhilele bɛ. (Kenga Ayezaya 61:1, 2; Luku 4:16-21) Ɔbɔle nolo kɛ: “Anwuma Belemgbunlililɛ ne ɛbikye.” Noko ɛnee ɔze kɛ menli dɔɔnwo mɔɔ bɛbaha edwɛkpa ne la anwo bahyia. Ɔti ɔtetele ye ɛdoavolɛma ne ɔmaanle bɛnwunle kɛzi bɛbabɔ edwɛkpa ko ne ala nolo la.—Mat. 4:16, 17; 10:7; 28:19, 20.
<<<P>>>
15 Ɛnɛ, yɛdayɛ noko yɛ nye sɔ gyima mɔɔ bɛva bɛmaa yɛ kɛ yɛdoa zo yɛbɔ Gyihova ngoanelielɛ ne nolo la. Nɔhalɛ nu, Gyisɛse ɛkpɔnelɛ afɔlebɔlɛ ne nee Nyamenle Belemgbunlililɛ ne anwo edwɛkpa ne mɔɔ yɛbɔ ye nolo la anwo hyia kpalɛ ɛnɛ tɛla mekɛ biala. Duzu ati ɔ? Ɔlua edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ɔnrɛhyɛ ɔbazi la ati ɔ. Ɔnrɛhyɛ, alesama bahɔ “anwongyelelɛ kpole mɔɔ sumunli kɛ ewiade bɔle ɔ bo badwu ɛnɛ bie ɛtɛsile ɛlɛ” la anu. (Mat. 24:21) Mekɛ zɔhane, bie a menli dɔɔnwo badie edwɛkpa
