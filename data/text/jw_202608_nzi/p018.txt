Die nyɛvolɛ fa boa maa yɛdɛnla koyɛlɛ nu kɛ mɔɔ Faelimɔn yɛle la
(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 15)
<<<P>>>
Onɛsemɛse ɛtane yeahyɛ ye a. Mɔɔ Pɔɔlo kɛlɛ Kɔlɔsae asafo ne, asafo ne mɔɔ ɛnee Faelimɔn wɔ nu la kɛlata la, ɔhanle kɛ ɛlɔlɛ maa “menli koyɛlɛ di munli.” (Kenga Kɔlɔsaema 3:14.) Ɛhye kile kɛ subane gyɛne biala ɛnle ɛkɛ mɔɔ maa yɛtɛnla anzodwolɛ nee koyɛlɛ kpole nu kɛ ɛlɔlɛ la a. Saa yɛkulo awie mɔ kpalɛ a, ɔmmaa ɔnyɛ se kɛ yɛbava bɛ ɛtane yeahyɛ bɛ na yɛ nee bɛ ali ye boɛ. (1 Kɔl. 13:4-7) Maa yɛnlea kɛzi ɛhye boale Faelimɔn la.
<<<P>>>
14 Ɛnee eza Faelimɔn lɛ subane bie mɔɔ boa ye ɔmaa ɔda ɛlɔlɛ kpole ali a. Yemɔ a le nyɛvolɛlielɛ. Ɛnee ɔmaa mediema ne mɔ bayɛ asafo ayia ngakyile wɔ ye sua nu ɛkɛ. (Fae. 2) Eza Pɔɔlo hanle hilele Faelimɔn kɛ ɔziezie ɛleka mɔɔ ɔbadɛnla la ɔmaa ye, na yemɔ maa yɛnwu kɛ ɛnee Pɔɔlo ze kɛ ɔdie nyɛvolɛ. (Fae. 22) Na ɔbayɛ kɛ kɛmɔ ɛnee ɔdie nyɛvolɛ la ati awie mɔ nyianle ɛhulolɛ kɛ bɛbahɔ ye sua nu yɛɛ ɔmaanle bɛ nye liele.—Fae. 7.
<<<P>>>
15 Saa yɛsukoa Faelimɔn nyɛvolɛlielɛ subane ne a, kɛzi ɔkola ɔmia ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛ yɛmaa awie mɔ la anu ɛ? Saa yɛdie ye mediema wɔ yɛ sua nu a, ɔmaa yɛnwu bɛ kpalɛ. Saa yɛ nee bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛnle yɛ gɔnwo mɔ, bɔbɔ bɛdabɛ mɔɔ yɛmbikye bɛ kpalɛ la bɔ nu yɛ ninyɛne a, ɔbamaa yɛanwu kɛ Gyihova sonvolɛ ko biala lɛ subane ngakyile na yemɔ maa Gyihova ahyehyɛdeɛ ne anu yɛ fɛ. Saa yɛnwu kɛzi bɛtetele bɛ, kɛzi bɛrale nɔhalɛ ne anu anzɛɛ kɛzi bɛgyinla sɔnea ngakyile nloa k pundii la a, ɔmaa yɛkulo bɛ na yɛbu bɛ kpole. Eza ɔmaa yɛnwu deɛmɔti Gyihova hwenle bɛ rabokale ye azonvolɛ ne mɔ anwo la. (Dwɔn 6:44) Ɔti saa yɛdie ye mediema nyɛvolɛ a, ɔboa yɛ ɔmaa yɛkulo bɛ kpole, yɛnyia bɛ nwo adwenle kpalɛ na yɛfa bɛ ɛtane yɛkyɛ bɛ.

16 Ɔyɛ se kɛ ɛbava awie bie mɔɔ ɛyɛ wɔ ɛtane la ɛtane wɔahyɛ ye ɔ? Yɛkulo yɛ mediema ɛdeɛ, noko ɔyɛ a ɔyɛ se kɛ yɛbava bɛ ɛtane yeahyɛ bɛ. Duzu a bahola aboa yɛ a? Suzu edwɛkɛ mɔɔ Pɔɔlo hɛlɛle hɔmaanle mediema mɔɔ wɔ Kɔlɔsae asafo ne anu mɔɔ ɛnee Faelimɔn boka nwo la anwo. Edwɛkɛ ne wɔ Kɔlɔsaema 3:13. (Kenga.) Nea kɛzi Faelimɔn dele nganeɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɔdele kɛ bɛlɛkenga kɛlata zɔhane bɛahile asafo ne mɔɔ yia ye sua nu ɛkɛ la. Ɛsuzu kɛ nwane anwo adwenle a bara ɔ ti anu a? Asoo ɛnee Onɛsemɛse anwo ɛya tɛbɔ ye nu ɔ? Saa ɛnee ɔtɛfa ɔ nwo ɛya a, ɔkile kɛ ɔnwunle mɔɔ
