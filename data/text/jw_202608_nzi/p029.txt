Yɛ atɔfolɛlielɛ kenle ne
<<<P>>>
Melɛmaa ɛdendɛlɛ meahile Chinese ekpunli bie, 2006
<<<P>>>
Yɛhɔle nyianu ne la, bɛhilehilele 1 Timote 5:8 anu. Ngyɛnu zɔhane hanle kɛ abusua tile kpalɛ nea ye sua nu amra. Bɛhanle kɛ Gyihova noko fa ngyinlazo ɛhye di gyima na ɔnea ye abusua ne anu amra dahuu. Ɔti menwunle kɛ saa mefa Gyihova ɛhulolɛdeɛ medimoa me ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nu a, Gyihova banlea me. Nzinlii mɔɔ meneanle Michel ɛbɛlabɔlɛ la, menliele menlile kɛ edwɛkɛ zɔhane le nɔhalɛ. Ɔvale ye adwenle ɔziele Gyihova ɛhulolɛdeɛyɛlɛ zo, noko ɛnee ɔ sa ka debie biala mɔɔ ɔhyia nwo la yɛɛ ɔ nye die.
<<<P>>>
Mɔɔ menzukoale ninyɛne dɔɔnwo la, akee menyɛle nzenzaleɛ. Menzile kpɔkɛ kɛ mebagyakyi sigalɛte nee wii ɛnlonlɛ. Ɔti menvuandinle sigalɛte nee wii mɔɔ metɛdɛ la amuala menguale senle nu na meanno bieko. Membɔle debiezukoalɛ ɛhɔlɛ bo. Kenle ko bie mɔɔ mengɔle debiezukoalɛ la, menganle mengilele adiema nrenyia bie kɛ ɔpɛpɛ me enrinli ndendenle ne, na ɔpɛpɛle ye wɔ Belemgbunlililɛ Asalo ɛkɛ ne ala.
<<<P>>>
Dapɛne ekyii anzi, menvile French Guiana namule zɔhane anu na menziale mengɔle France. Mengɔle debiezukoalɛ na mendoale me Baebolo ɛzukoalɛ ne azo. Ɛnee me ɛbɛlabɔlɛ ne ɛyɛ kpalɛ ɛdeɛ, noko kɛzi ɛnee mehakyi la zinle me abusua ne anwo. Ɛnee menno sigalɛte, wii anzɛɛ nza yɛɛ mengɔ party mgbole bieko. Noko kɛmɔ meanlua adenle kɛnlɛma zo meanga me diedi ne anwo edwɛkɛ meangile bɛ la ati, bɛ nye anlie nwo. Nzinlii membanwunle kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ meda ɛbulɛ ali na mesuzu kɛzi bɛte nganeɛ la anwo wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ melɛka Gyihova anwo edwɛkɛ meahile bɛ la.
<<<P>>>
Bɛzɔnenle me wɔ 1996. Menzɛlɛle Gyihova kɛ ɔmaa menyɛ edwɛkpatɛlɛvolɛ kɛ Michel la. Ɛnee ɔzɔho kɛ ɛhye a le adenle kpalɛ mɔɔ mebalua zo meayɛ Gyihova mo a.
<<<P>>>
ME ANWUMA SELƐ NE YILALE ME KPOLE
<<<P>>>
Ɛnee adiema raalɛ anwo fɛlɛko bie wɔ me asafo ne anu. Bɛfɛlɛ ye Claudine na me nye liele ɔ nwo. Menwunle kɛ ɛnee ɔbɔ mɔdenle wɔ daselɛlilɛ nu yɛɛ mualɛ mɔɔ ɔmaa wɔ debiezukoalɛ bo la kile kɛ ɔkulo Gyihova kpalɛ. Ɛnee mekulo kɛ megya ye noko ɛnee mekpondɛ kɛ medimoa menwu ye saa ɔ nye die edwɛkpatɛlɛvolɛ gyima ne anwo a. Ɛnee ɔ nye die nwo, ɔti yɛgyale wɔ April 2000.
<<<P>>>
Ɛvolɛ ko anzi, yɛbɔle nu yɛyɛle adekpakyelɛ gyima ne bie. Nzinlii yɛzile kpɔkɛ kɛ yɛbazukoa Mandarin aneɛ amaa yɛahɔboa Chinese ekpunli ne mɔɔ wɔ asafo bie anu la. Ɛnee aneɛ zɔhane ɛzukoalɛ yɛ me se kpalɛ. Ɔwɔ kɛ medwenle kɛ Chinesema na mesukoa ndenle fofolɛ mɔɔ mebalua zo meaha edwɛkpa ne meamaa bɛ nye alie nwo la. Nɔhalɛ nu, meannwu Mandarin aneɛ ne ka kpalɛ ɛlɛ. Noko membɔle me nwo mɔdenle na yɛ rɛle ɛnrɛvi kɛzi yɛ nee Chinese mediema ne mɔ bɔle nu yɛle edwɛkɛhanlɛ gyima ne anyelielɛ nu la ɛlɛ. Gyihova yilale yɛ wɔ mekɛ zɔhane na ɔtetele yɛ ɔmaanle gyima mɔɔ la yɛ nyunlu la.

KƐZI MENGYINLANLE NGYEGYELƐ NLOA LA
<<<P>>>
Ɛvolɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ ɛpɛ nu la anu, ɛnee ɔwɔ kɛ medi ngyegyelɛ dɔɔnwo anwo gyima. Kɛ neazo la, ɛnee mesuzu kɛ mɔɔ melɛyɛ meamaa Gyihova nee
