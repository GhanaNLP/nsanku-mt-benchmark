BODANE
Kakye ye dahuu kɛ nɔhalɛ Kilisienema nee sunsum ɛtane ɛlɔne a ɛlɛko a na tɛ bɛ mediema Kilisienema ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Ɛbahola Wɔali Konim!
<<<P>>>
‘Tɛ nwonane nee mogya a yɛ nee bɛ ɛlɛko a, emomu yɛ nee sunsum ɛtane ɛlɔne mɔɔ bɛwɔ anwuma lɔ la a ɛlɛko a.’—ƐFƐ. 6:12.
<<<P>>>
GYIHOVA azonvolɛ ɛngɔ konle. (Aye. 2:4; Dwɔn 18:36) Noko Baebolo ne ka kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛ muala yɛ nee Seetan nee ye sunsum ɛtane ne mɔ ko. (Kenga Ɛfɛsɛsema 6:11, 12.) Kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na yɛahola yɛali konim wɔ yɛ nee yɛ agbɔvolɛ ɛhye mɔ mɔɔ anwo yɛ se la konle ne anu ɛ? Debie mɔɔ hyia kpalɛ la a le kɛ yɛbava yɛ nwo yɛado Gyihova anwo zo yɛamaa yeaboa yɛ. Noko eza ɔwɔ kɛ yɛnwu ndenle mɔɔ Seetan nee ye sunsum ɛtane ne mɔ dua zo teta yɛ nee yɛ mediema diedima la.
<<<P>>>
2 Wɔ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ li ɛhye anyunlu la anu, yɛzuzule kɛzi Abɔnsam bɔ mɔdenle kɛ ɔsɛkye anwodozo mɔɔ yɛlɛ wɔ Gyihova Nyamenle nee Gyisɛse Kelaese anu la anwo. Wɔ edwɛkɛ ɛhye anu, yɛbazuzu ndenle gyɛne nwiɔ mɔɔ Seetan dua zo nee yɛ ko la anwo. Mɔɔ limoa, ɔbɔ mɔdenle kɛ ɔsɛkye koyɛlɛ mɔɔ wɔ yɛ nee yɛ mediema avinli la. Yɛɛ mɔɔ tɔ zo nwiɔ, ɔmaa menli dwazo tia yɛ amaa yɛ ahonle adu anzɛɛ yɛaye yɛ adwenle yɛavi gyima mɔɔ Gyihova ɛva ɛmaa yɛ kɛ yɛyɛ la azo.

SEETAN BƆ MƆDENLE KƐ ƆSƐKYE YƐ KOYƐLƐ NE
<<<P>>>
3 Seetan bɔ mɔdenle kɛ ɔfa mgbakyemgbakyenu yeara yɛ nee yɛ mediema avinli. Ye bodane a le kɛ ɔmaa yɛaho yɛatia yɛ nwo amaa yɛango yɛantia ɔdaye mumua ne. Adenle boni a ɔdua zo ɔyɛ ɛhye amaa ɔ sa aha yɛ a? Menli dɔɔnwo mɔɔ wɔ Seetan ewiade ye anu la ati yɛ se yɛɛ bɛnlɛ ɛlɔlɛ. Bɛ nuhua biala ɛnlie ɔ gɔnwo ɛnli. Seetan anye la kɛ yɛbanyia ewiade ne subane ne bie na yɛadendɛ yɛatia yɛ mediema nee
<<<P>>>
1. Nwane mɔ a ɔwɔ kɛ yɛ muala yɛko yetia bɛ a? (Ɛfɛsɛsema 6:11, 12)
2. Duzu a yɛbazuzu nwo wɔ edwɛkɛ ɛhye anu a?
3. Adenle boni a Seetan bɔ mɔdenle kɛ ɔdua zo yeazɛkye yɛ koyɛlɛ ne a?
