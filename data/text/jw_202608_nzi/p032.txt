<<<P>>>
Baka Bie Mɔɔ Bɔ Ndotolo Ndɛ
<<<P>>>
Alemɔndo baka ne boka mbaka ngakyile kɛyɛ 30 mɔɔ bɛbobɔ bɛ aluma wɔ Baebolo ne anu la anwo. Saa ɛyɛlɛ mekɛ ne pɛ nu a, ɔboka mbaka mɔɔ bɛdimoa bɛbɔ ndotolo la anwo. January kahɔ ye awieleɛ la, menli kola nwu ye kɛ yeye felawɔse penke anzɛɛ fufule. Hibulu duma mɔɔ bɛfa bɛfɛlɛ alemɔndo baka ne la abo kile “baka mɔɔ dwazo ndɛ,” na ɔfɛta ye bɔkɔɔ. Asoo mɔɔ baka ɛhye yɛ nee mɔɔ ye Bɔvolɛ ne yɛ la zɔho bɛ nwo ɔ?
<<<P>>>
Saa ɛnea Gyɛlɛmaya 7:25 abodwɛkɛ ne a, Gyihova hanle ɔ nwo edwɛkɛ kɛ ɔdwazo ndɛ na ɔsoa ye ngapezoma. March 1, 2011, Ezinzale Arane ne hanle kɛ: “Kɛmɔ alemɔndo baka ‘dwazo’ ndɛ la, zɔhane ala a Gyihova noko ‘dwazo nwonlomɔ mangyee’ wɔ sɛkɛlɛ-neɛ adenle zo kɔbɔ ye menli kɔkɔ wɔ mɔɔ vi anzosesebɛyɛlɛ nu ba la anwo a. Ɛnee ɔnrɛlie ɔ menle—‘ɔbahɔ zo yeamaa ɔ nye ala ɛkɛ’—kɔkpula kɛ ye ngapezo edwɛkɛ ne bara nu.” —Gyɛ. 1:11, 12.
<<<P>>>
Eza Baebolo ne fa mgbanyinli egyɛne toto alemɔndo felawɔse fufule ne anwo. (Nol. 12:5) Yɛɛ ɔbayɛ kɛ kɛmɔ ye felawɔse ne le kɛnlɛma la ati, mɔɔ bɛlɛyɛ ndanlɛsua ne anu ninyɛne ne mɔ la, bɛyɛle nya ne mɔ mɔɔ wɔ kɛnlanɛebaka ne anwo mɔɔ bɛfa nwole bɛgua nu la kɛ alemɔndo felawɔse la.—Ɛzɛ. 37:17-20.
