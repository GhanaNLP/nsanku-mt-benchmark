Ɔliele ɔdole nu kɛ yeyɛ ɛtane na ɔyɛle ye afoa nu ɛdeɛ kɛ ɔsiezie ɔ nee Faelimɔn avinli. Kɛ neazo la, ɛnee Onɛsemɛse ze ye bɔkɔɔ kɛ Wulomu mɛla ne kile kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ ɔsia ɔkɔ ɔ menle ɛkɛ. Ɔti ɔbɔle kakɛ na ɔpɛle adenle mɔɔ ye tendenle bo kilomita 1,400 (anzɛɛ mayelɛ 900) la ɔziale ɔhɔle Faelimɔn ɛkɛ. Ɛnee ɔlɛ ɛhulolɛ kɛ ɔbayɛ akɛlɛ bieko na yealie anzohwenlɛ biala yeado nu. Ɔbayɛ kɛ ɛnee ɛhye ɛyɛlɛ ɛnla aze fee. Bie a Onɛsemɛse dele nganeɛ kɛ Faelimɔn fɛta mɔɔ ɔvale ɔyɛle ye la. Noko saa ɔdele nganeɛ zɔ bɔbɔ a, yemɔ anzi ye adenle kɛ ɔbazia yeahɔ ɔ menle ne ɛkɛ na yeayɛ biala mɔɔ ɔbahola la yeaziezie ɔ nee ɔ diema diedinli ne avinli.

11 Saa wɔyɛ debie wɔmaa yegyegye awie a, dimoa tudu nwolɛ agyakɛ kpondɛ anzodwolɛ. (Mat. 5:23-25) Nɔhalɛ nu, bie a ɛhye ɛyɛlɛ bayɛ se, noko anzodwolɛ mɔɔ bara bɛ avinli bieko la le debie mɔɔ ɔhyia. Dimoa kpa ye kyɛlɛ. Eza nyia ɛhulolɛ kɛ ahenle ɛlɛtendɛ a ɛbadie ye kpalɛ na bɔ mɔdenle kɛ ɛbade kɛzi ɔte nga-
<<<P>>>
neɛ la abo. Akee die to nu kɛ wɔanyɛ ye boɛ. Mmafa edwɛkɛ ɛyeye ɛ nloa anzɛɛ mmabɔ awie gyɛne somolɛ wɔ mɔɔ ɛyɛle la anwo. Saa ɛyɛ zɔ a ɔnrɛmaa kyɛlɛkpalɛ ne ɛnrɛli munli, ɔbamaa edwɛkɛ ne ahyɛ anzɛɛ ɔbazɛkye edwɛkɛ ne bɔbɔ. Ɔhyia mɛlɛbɛnwoaze na wɔahola wɔakpa kyɛlɛ noko yemɔ a fa anzodwolɛ ba ndɛndɛ a.—Wlo. 14:19.

12 Faelimɔn neazo ne maa yɛnwu kɛzi awie yɛ yɛ ɛtane a yɛbava yeahyɛ ye na yɛ nee ye adɛnla anzodwolɛ nu la. Asoo amgba Faelimɔn vale Onɛsemɛse ɛtane hyɛle ye na ɔliele ye kɛnlɛma ɔ? Bɛanga ye wɔ kɛlɛtokɛ ne anu. Noko ɛnee Pɔɔlo die di kɛ ɔbayɛ ye zɔ. (Fae. 17, 21) Ɔluakɛ ɔze Faelimɔn kɛ ɔlɛ subane mɔɔ ɔbamaa yeanyia ɛhulolɛ kɛ ɔbava Onɛsemɛse ɛtane yeahyɛ ye la. Subane boni mɔ ɔ?
