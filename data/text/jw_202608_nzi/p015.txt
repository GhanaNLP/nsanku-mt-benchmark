3 Bie a mɔɔ enee ɔgyegye Pɔɔlo la a le kɛ saa Onɛsemɛse kɔdwu a, duzu a Faelimɔn bava ayɛ ye a? Enee mɛla maa Faelimɔn adenle kɛ ɔbahwe Onɛsemɛse anzɔ. Asoo ɔdaye noko ɔbayɛ ye zɔ ɔ? Duzu a asafo ne anu amra noko bayɛ a? Kɛzi ɔbaha mediema gyɛne mɔɔ lɛ ngɛlɛlɛ yɛɛ ngɛlɛlɛ gyɛne mɔɔ wɔ asafo ne anu la ɛ? Enee Pɔɔlo ze kɛ tɛnlabelɛ ɛhye bahola azɛkye anzodwolɛ mɔɔ wɔ asafo ne anu la. Ɔti ɔhɛlɛle Faelimɔn kɛlata na ɔmaanle Tekekɛse vale hɔmaanle ye. (Ɛfɛ. 6:21, 22; Kɔl. 4:7-9) Mekɛ ko ne ala eza Pɔɔlo hɛlɛle kɛlata hɔmaanle Kɔlɔsae asafo ne. Maa yɛzuzu ngɛlata nwiɔ zɔhane mɔ anwo na yɛnlea mɔɔ yɛbahola yɛazukoa yɛavi Pɔɔlo, Onɛsemɛse yɛɛ Faelimɔn neazo ne anu la.

4 Duzu a yɛsukoa yɛfi Pɔɔlo neazo ne anu a? Pɔɔlo nee awie mɔ lile wɔ adenle mɔɔ maa anzodwolɛ tɛnla asafo ne anu la azo. Ye neazo ne maa yɛnwu kɛzi yɛdayɛ noko yɛbaboa yɛamaa anzodwolɛ adɛnla asafo ne anu la.
<<<P>>>
5 Pɔɔlo nee Faelimɔn lile kɛ ɔ gɔnwo. (Fae. 17) Pɔɔlo annyia adwenle kɛ ɔbahola yeaha mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ Faelimɔn yɛ la yeahile ye kɛ asɛɛ ɔvea ɔ bo la. (2 Kɔl. 1:24) Emomu ɔbule Faelimɔn kɛ ɔ diema diedinli yɛɛ ɔhakyele ye kɛ bɛle agɔnwolɛma. Mɔɔ Pɔɔlo yɛle la maanle Faelimɔn nwunle kɛzi ɔwɔ kɛ ɔ nee Onɛsemɛse, mɔɔ kɛkala ɛrayɛ ɔ diema Kilisienenli la di la. Eza ɔle neazo kɛnlɛma ɔmaa yɛ muala, titili asafo nu mgbanyima. Mgbanyima mɔɔ bɛ nee awie mɔ di kɛ bɛ gɔnwo mɔ la maa anzodwolɛ tɛnla asafo ne anu. Kɛmɔ bɛle mboaneneavolɛma mɔɔ lɛ ɛlɔlɛ la ati, bɛnli mboane ne anwo zo tumi. (1 Pita 5:1-3) Emomu bɛnyia mekɛ bɛmaa Nyamenle mboane ne, bɛtie bɛ edwɛkɛ, bɛbu bɛ kɛ bɛ nwo

6 Pɔɔlo hanvole Faelimɔn. (Fae. 4, 5, 7) Yɛ muala yɛhyia nganvolɛ. Duzu ati ɔ? Ɔluakɛ ɔyɛ a yɛ nuhua dɔɔnwo adwenle nu yɛ yɛ kesee kɛ saa yɛ nwo hyia a. Bie a yɛbadwenle kɛzi yɛde la anwo, anzɛɛ saa awie mɔ kulo yɛ edwɛkɛ anzɛɛ saa nwolɛ adenle bie mɔɔ yɛlɛ la bavi yɛ sa a. Saa yɛte nganeɛ zɔ a, nvonleɛ biala ɛnle nwo. Noko ɔdwu mekɛ ne bie a nganeɛdelɛ zɛhae bahola amaa adwenleadwenle adɔ yɛ nwo, yɛava ɛya ndɛndɛ, yɛabu awie mɔ kɛ bɛlɛbɔ yɛ somolɛ anzɛɛ yɛ nee awie mɔ azu bɔbɔ. Nyɛleɛ zɛhae mɔ ɛmmaa yɛngola yɛ nee awie mɔ ɛndɛnla anzodwolɛ nu. Noko saa yɛkanvo awie a, ɔmmaa ɛnde nganeɛ zɔ (Mrɛ. 12:25) Ɔmaa ɔnyia anwodozo kɛ awie mɔ kulo ye edwɛkɛ yɛɛ ɔ nwo hyia bɛ. Ɛkulo kɛ ɛmaa awie mɔ te nganeɛ kɛ bɛ nwo hyia ɔ? Enee kpondɛ nwolɛ ndenle ngakyile fa kanvo bɛ nɔhalɛ nu. Tɛ ninyɛne mgbalɛ mɔɔ awie mɔ yɛ la ala a ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkanvo bɛ wɔ nwo a, eza ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkanvo bɛ wɔ mɔdenle mɔɔ bɛbɔle wɔ nwo la anwo. Yɛbahola yɛaha ye sikalɛ kɛ, saa ɛnwu debie kpalɛ bie wɔ awie anwo a, ka kile ye.

7 Pɔɔlo hanle Onɛsemɛse anwo edwɛkɛ kpalɛ. (Kenga Faelimɔn 10-12.) Ɛsuzu kɛ Onɛsemɛse anwo edwɛkɛ kpalɛ mɔɔ Pɔɔlo hanle la hakyile kɛzi Faelimɔn bu Onɛsemɛse, kɛzi ɔtendɛ ɔkile ye nee kɛzi ɔ nee ye lile la ɔ? Ɛhɛe, ɔbahola yeara ye zɔ! Zɔhane ala a mɔɔ yɛka wɔ yɛ mediema diedima anwo la ka kɛzi awie mɔ bu bɛ nee kɛzi bɛ nee bɛ di la a. Saa yɛka bɛ nwo edwɛkɛ kpalɛ a ɔboa awie mɔ ɔmaa bɛ nee bɛ di ye kɛnlɛma.
