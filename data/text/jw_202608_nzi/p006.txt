Saa yɛdwenle ninyɛne mɔɔ yɛyɛ a ɔbamaa yɛava Gyihova agɔnwolɛ kyengye dahuu la anwo a, ɔnrɛmaa Seetan asa ɛnrɛha yɛ
(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 14)
<<<P>>>
yɛanli. Kɛ neazo la, saa adalɛ ekyi fonlɔfonla bɛ edwɛkɛ nu anzɛɛ bɛ edwɛkɛ ne amuala le adalɛ a, ɔbazɛkye yɛ adwenle. Noko saa yɛsukoa Baebolo ne nee yɛ mbuluku ne mɔ na yɛ nee Kilisienema mɔɔ bɛnyi sunsum nu la tu dahuu a, ɔbayɛ se kɛ awie bahola abɛlɛbɛla yɛ. Saa yɛte nɔhalɛ ne abo kpalɛ a, ɔbamaa yeayɛ se kɛ Seetan bahola azɛkye yɛ diedi. —Mrɛ. 13:20; Gyi. 17:11.
<<<P>>>
Gyihova nee Gyisɛse dwenle alesama anwo kpalɛ la anwo a, ɔbamaa yɛava bɛ agɔnwolɛ kpalɛ. Edwɛkɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ kile kɛ Gyihova nee Gyisɛse kulo yɛ la ɛyi Baebolo ne anu. Kɛ neazo la, Gyihova vale kɛzi ɔkulo ye menli la totole kɛzi ɛrɛladane kulo ɔ ra mɔɔ no nyɛfonlɛ la anwo, na ɔhanle kɛ ye ɛlɔlɛ ɛdeɛ ne le kpole tɛla ɛrɛladane bɔbɔ ɛdeɛ ne. (Aye. 49:15) Bieko, ɔhanle kɛ menli mɔɔ kulo ye duma ne la sonle bolɛ maa ye kɛ ‘agyapadeɛ mɔɔ ɔ nye die nwo’ la. (Mal. 3:16, 17, ɔbdw.) Eza yɛbahola yɛagenga mɔɔ Gyisɛse hanle hilele yɛ la wɔ Baebolo ne anu. Ɔhanle kɛ ɔkulo yɛ kpole kpalɛ ɔti a ɔvale ye ngoane ɔbɔle afɔle ɔmaanle yɛ la.—Dwɔn 15:12, 13.
