SEETAN HANLE GYIHOVA NEE GYISƐSE ANWO ADALƐ EDWƐKƐ WƆ TETE NE
<<<P>>>
3 Mɔɔ Gyihova bɔle Adam nee Yive la, yea-ngyɛ biala yɛɛ Seetan nyianle adwenle kɛ ɔmaa bɛahwe bɛ nwo bɛavi Gyihova anwo a. Ɔbɛlɛbɛlale Yive ɔmaanle ɔdele nganeɛ kɛ ye Bɔvolɛ ne ɛndwenle ɔ nwo. Seetan bizale ye kɛ: “Asoo amgba Nyamenle se bɛmmadi baka biala mɔɔ wɔ tola ye anu la ma ne bie ɔ?” (Gyn. 3:1-5) Seetan maanle Yive liele lile kɛ Gyihova ammaa ɔ nee ɔ hu asa anga debie biala mɔɔ ɔhyia kɛ ɔfa ɔmaa bɛ la. Alɔbɔlɛ edwɛkɛ ne a le kɛ Yive nee Adam liele kpɔvo-lɛ zɔhane adalɛ ne lile na bɛyɛle Gyihova, Ahenle mɔɔ vale debie biala maanle bɛ la anwo anzosesebɛ.—Gyn. 3:6.
<<<P>>>
4 Ɔvi Adam nee Yive mekɛ zo mɔɔ ba la, Seetan tɛkɔ zo tɛka Gyihova anwo ada-lɛ edwɛkɛ. Kɛ neazo la, ɔluale Dwobu agɔ-nwɔlɛma adalɛma nsa ne anwo zo kɛ ɔmaa Dwobu anyia adwenle kɛ Gyihova ɛngulo ye yɛɛ ye nɔhalɛlilɛ nwo ɛngyia ye. (Dwobu 22:1-3) Abɔnsam nyianle adwenle kɛ saa Dwobu kɔ ngyegyelɛ nu a, ɔbamaa yeakpo Gyihova. Mɔɔ Seetan yɛle la gyegyele Dwobu kpole ɛdeɛ, noko ɔhɔle zo ɔlile nɔhalɛ na yeanyɛ de-bie mɔɔ ɔbazɛkye ɔ nee Nyamenle agɔnwɔlɛ-valɛ ne la.—Dwobu 27:1, 5.
<<<P>>>
5 Ɛhye anzi ɛvolɛ ɛya dɔɔnwo mɔɔ Gyisɛse rale azɛlɛ ye azo la, Seetan bɔle mɔdenle kɛ ɔsi menli adenle amaa bɛandoa Gyisɛse. Ade-nle ko mɔɔ ɔluale zo ɔyɛle zɔ la a le kɛ ɔmaa-nle ɛzonlenlɛ nu mgbanyima mɔɔ bɛkpɔ Gyi-sɛse la nee menli gyɛne salole Gyisɛse anwo adalɛ edwɛkɛ. Awieleɛ bɔkɔɔ ne, bɛhunle ye kɛ ɛtanevolɛ bie la.—Kenga Mateyu 11:19; 27:27-31; Maake 14:55, 56; Dwɔn 8:41-45
<<<P>>>
6 Mɔɔ Gyisɛse wule la, akee Seetan vale ɔ nye ziele gyima mɔɔ Gyisɛse ɛdoavɔlɛma ne ɛlɛyɛ la azo. Debie mɔɔ Seetan limoale yɛle la a le kɛ ɔbɔle mɔdenle kɛ ɔmaa bɛ adwenle nu ayɛ bɛ kesee kɛ saa amgba bɛdwazole Gyisɛ-se a. Kɛ neazo la, ɔluale menli bie anwo azo ɔbɔle adalɛ kɛ bɛandwazo Gyisɛse emomu ye ɛdoavɔlɛma ne a rawuale ye funli ne vile nla ne anu a. (Mat. 28:11-15) Yemɔ anzi, ɔbɔle mɔdenle kɛ ɔdua kpɔdekpɔdeyɛlɛ zo yeanwu-nlonwunla bɛ. Enee ɔkulo kɛ ɛdoavɔlɛma ne te nganeɛ kɛ gyima mɔɔ bɛ Menle ne ɛva ɛmaa bɛ kɛ bɛyɛ la yɛ se somaa.—Gyi. 7:57–8:1; 12:1-3; 28:22.
<<<P>>>
7 Alimoa Kilisienema bie mɔ maanle ɛhye hanle bɛ ɛzulolɛ na bɛgyakyile edwɛkɛhanlɛ gyima ne ɛdeɛ, noko bɛ nuhua dɔɔnwo gyi-nlanle k pundii na bɛanga bɛ nloa bɛando nu. Bɛnwunle kɛ Seetan nee sunsum ɛtane ne mɔ a bɛngulo kɛ bɛmaa bɛsonle Gyihova a. (Gyi. 4:18-20; 1 Pita 5:8) Na ɛnɛ noko ɛ? Duzu a Seetan yɛ kɛ ɔfa yeazi yɛ adenle amaa yɛa-nzonle Gyihova a?

8 Ɛnɛ, ndenle titili mɔɔ Seetan dua zo la anu ko a le kɛ ɔnrɛmaa menli ɛnrɛnwu nɔha-lɛ ne mɔɔ fale Gyihova nee Gyisɛse anwo la. Kɛmɔ ɔle “adalɛ kɔsɔɔti bɛ ze” la ati, ɔdua adalɛ ɛzonlenlɛ zo ɔka bɛ nwo adalɛ edwɛkɛ ɔkile menli dɔɔnwo. (Dwɔn 8:44) Kɛ neazo la, yezi menli adwenle ne anye ɔti bɛnze sonla holɛ mɔɔ Gyihova nee ɔ Ra ne de la. (2 Kɔl.
