BODANE
Mmadie Gyihova Nyamenle nee Gyisɛse Kelaese anwo adalɛ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ Seetan ka la ɛdi.
<<<P>>>
Mmadie Seetan Ɛdi, Fa Ɛ Nwo To Gyihova Nee Gyisɛse Anwo Zo
<<<P>>>
“Bɛzi Abɔnsam adenle, na ɔvi bɛ nwo ɔbanriandi.”—GYE. 4:7.
<<<P>>>
SAA yɛsi kpɔkɛ kɛ yɛsonle Gyihova ala a, ɛnee Seetan nee ye sunsum ɛtane ne mɔ ɛrayɛ yɛ agbɔvolɛ. Noko Seetan ze kɛ, mekɛ ala mɔɔ Gyihova gyi yɛ nzi la ɔnrɛhola ɔnrɛli yɛ nwo zo konim. Ɔti ɔbɔ mɔdenle kɛ ɔmaa yɛahwe yɛ nwo yɛavi Gyihova anwo amaa Gyihova amboa yɛ bieko. Na adenle ko mɔɔ ɔdua zo ɔyɛ zɔ la a le kɛ ɔbamaa yɛ adwenle nu ayɛ yɛ kesee kɛ saa amgba Gyihova nee ɔ Ra ne nee yɛ lua a.—2 Kɔl. 11:14; Gye. 1:6.
<<<P>>>
2 Saa sogyama kɔ konle a, ɔwɔ kɛ bɛnyia mgbanyima mɔɔ gyigyi bɛ ti la anu anwodozo. Saa anwodozo zɔhane sɛkye a, ɔbamaa bɛ nuhua ko biala ali ɔ ti anwo na yemɔ noko bamaa bɛawu. Yɛdayɛ noko yɛ nee “sunsum ɛtane ɛlɔne” ɛlɛko wɔ sunsum nu. (Ɛfɛ. 6:12) Na Seetan Abɔnsam ze kɛ saa yɛnlɛ Gyihova Nyamenle nee Gyisɛse Kelaese anu anwodozo bieko a, ɔnrɛmaa yɛnrɛdie bɛ na bɛaboa yɛ. Ɔti ɔlɛyɛ biala mɔɔ ɔbahola la amaa yeazɛkye anwodozo mɔɔ yɛlɛ wɔ bɛ nu la. Wɔ edwɛkɛ ɛhye anu, yɛbanlea kɛzi ɔvale adenle ɛhye ɔlile gyima wɔ tete ne nee kɛzi ɔlɛfa yeali gyima ɛnɛ noko la. Akee yɛbazuzu kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na yɛandɔ ye ngane ne mɔ anu la anwo. Wɔ edwɛkɛ bieko anu, yɛbazuzu ndenle nwiɔ bieko mɔɔ Seetan fa di gyima wɔ yɛ sunsum nu konle ne anu la anwo.*
<<<P>>>
* Tɛ Seetan a fa ngyegyelɛ kɔsɔɔti mɔɔ yɛyia la ba yɛ nwo zo fɔɔnwo a. Noko ɔdaye a ɔgyi “ewiade sunsum” ne mɔɔ maa awie mɔ yɛ Nyamenle menli kpɔdekpɔde la anzi a. Ɔti saa ɔfa ngyegyelɛ ne ɔba yɛ nwo zo fɔɔnwo o, saa ɔdua awie mɔ anwo zo o, ɔdaye a ɔdi ngyegyelɛ kɔsɔɔti mɔɔ yɛlɛyia wɔ yɛ sunsum nu konle ne anu la anwo ɛzonle a.—1 Kɔl. 2:12; 1 Dwɔn 5:19.
