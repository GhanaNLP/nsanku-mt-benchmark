Bɛmaa Yɛnyia Ɛlɔlɛ Subane
<<<P>>>
BODANE
<<<P>>>
Yɛbazuzu kɛzi ɛzoanvolɛ Pɔɔlo, Faelimɔn yɛɛ Onɛsemɛse neazo ne baboa yɛ yeamaa yɛakpondɛ anzodwolɛ wɔ asafo ne anu la anwo.
<<<P>>>
Megyinla Ɛlɔlɛ Zo Meazɛlɛ Wɔ
<<<P>>>
“Mekulo kɛ megyinla ɛlɔlɛ zo mesɛlɛ wɔ.”—FAE. 9.
<<<P>>>
YƐ MUALA yɛ nye die anzodwolɛ mɔɔ wɔ Gyihova menli avinli la anwo. Anzodwolɛ ɛhye maa yɛnwu kɛzi asetɛnla wɔ Paladaese bayɛ fɛ la. (Aye. 32:17, 18) Gyihova a fa anzodwolɛ ɛhye maa yɛ ɛdeɛ, noko ɔle yɛ nuhua ko biala ɛzonlelilɛ kɛ ɔbaboa yeamaa yɛabɔ nwolɛ bane na yeadɛnla ɛkɛ ne dahuu. (Wlo. 12:18) Ɔti kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na yɛ nee awie mɔ ali wɔ adenle mɔɔ ɔbaboa yeamaa yɛadɛnla anzodwolɛ nu wɔ asafo ne anu la azo ɛ? Na saa yɛyɛ debie yɛmaa ɔgyegye awie ɛ? Anzɛɛ awie yɛ debie maa ɔgyegye yɛ noko ɛ? Saa yɛsuzu edwɛkɛ bie mɔɔ zile mɔɔ bɛha nwolɛ edwɛkɛ wɔ Baebolo ne anu la anwo a, yɛbanwu mualɛ ne. Edwɛkɛ zɔhane fale ɛzoanvolɛ Pɔɔlo, Faelimɔn yɛɛ Onɛsemɛse anwo.
<<<P>>>
MƆƆ PƆƆLO ZƐLƐLE FAELIMƆN KƐ ƆYƐ LA
<<<P>>>
2 Kenga Faelimɔn 8, 9, 17. Pɔɔlo hanle ye wɔ kɛlata mɔɔ ɔhɛlɛle Faelimɔn la anu kɛ ɔzia ɔlie ye akɛlɛ Onɛsemɛse. Na Pɔɔlo zɛlɛle Faelimɔn kɛ kɛmɔ ɔkulo Kelaese nee ɔ mediema diedima la ati ɔlie ye. Noko ɛze edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ɛnee ɛzi la ɔ? Ɛnee Faelimɔn wɔ Kɔlɔsae asafo ne anu, na ɛnee mediema ne mɔ ze ye kɛ ɔlɛ diedi kpole yɛɛ ɔkulo menli kpalɛ. Ɛnee Onɛsemɛse le ye akɛlɛ, na ɔnriandile ɔvile ɔ menle ne anwo ɛkɛ ɔhɔle Wulomu. Na ɔbayɛ kɛ ɔkɔ la ɔwuale ye a ɔhɔle a. (Fae. 18) Mɔɔ Onɛsemɛse wɔ Wulomu la, ɔyiale Pɔɔlo na bɛrayɛle agɔnwolɛma kpalɛ; eza ɔrayɛle Kilisienenli. (Kɔl. 4:9) Noko wɔ Wulomu mɛla bo, ɛnee ɔwɔ kɛ akɛlɛ ɛhye mɔɔ ɛnriandi la sia kɔ ɔ menle ɛkɛ. Ɔti Pɔɔlo hanle kɛ Onɛsemɛse ɛzia ɛhɔ Faelimɔn anwo lɔ.—Fae. 12, 13.
