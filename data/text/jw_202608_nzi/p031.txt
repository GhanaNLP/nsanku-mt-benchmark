Wɔ tete ne, duzu a ɛnee bɛyɛ ye wɔ azuamgbole nlenkɛ mgbole ne mɔ anloa a?
<<<P>>>
ƐNEE suakpole ne nlenkɛ mgbole ne mɔ ɛnle ade-nle mɔɔ bɛfa nuhua ala. Kɛmɔ menli dɔɔnwo fa ɛkɛ ne la ati, ɛnee ɔmaa bɛyɛ ninyɛne ngakyile dɔɔnwo wɔ ɛkɛ ne alehyenlɛ biala. Baebolo ne ka suakpole nlenkɛ mgbole nwo edwɛkɛ mɔɔ bo 50, yɛɛ ɔka ninyɛne mɔɔ ɛnee bɛyɛ ye wɔ ɛkɛ la anwo edwɛkɛ.
<<<P>>>
Ɛnee menli yia ɛkɛ. Hibulu edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ mɔɔ bɛkile ɔ bo “anlenkɛ kpole” la ɛngile anlenkɛ mɔɔ la suakpole ne anyunlu la angomekye. Eza ɔkola ɔkile suakpole ne anlenkɛ kpole ne mɔɔ bɛfa nu bɛwolo la ɛleka mɔɔ bɛsisi kpɔda ngakyile la. Wɔ ɛleka bie mɔ, saa ɛfa anlenkɛ kpole ne anu ɛwolo ala a, bɛyɛ ɛleka kpole wɔ ɛkɛ mɔɔ menli dɔɔnwo kola yia ɛkɛ a.—Nih. 8:1.

Ɛnee gualilɛma di gua wɔ ɛkɛ. Ɛnee bɛfɛlɛ nlenkɛ mgbole mɔɔ wɔ Gyɛlusalɛm la bie mɔ Fɛlɛ Anlenkɛ Kpole yɛɛ Mboane Anlenkɛ Kpole. Bie a ninyɛne mɔɔ ɛnee bɛtɔne bɛ wɔ ɛkɛ ne la ati a bɛ-vɛlɛle bɛ zɔhane a.—Zɛf. 1:10; Dwɔn 5:2.
<<<P>>>
Ɛnee menli di gua wɔ ɛkɛ yɛɛ bɛyɛ bɛdabɛ mumua ne bɛ ninyɛne gyɛne wɔ ɛkɛ. Ebileham nee Bowaze nee awie mɔ yɛle ngyekyeleɛ wɔ ala-sevolɛ anyunlu wɔ suakpole nlenkɛ mgbole ne mɔ anloa. (Gyn. 23:10-15, 17, 18; Wut. 4:1, 10) Ɛnee saa adenduvoma annyia awie anva bɛ angɔ sua nu wɔ nɔe ne a, bɛda gua ne azo. (Ndɛ. 19:15-21) Ɛnee eza menli kɔ suakpole ne nlenkɛ mgbole ne mɔ anloa kɔtie edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ɛlɛsisi la.—2 Sa. 18:24, 25.

Ɛnee ndɛnebuavolɛma di edwɛkɛ wɔ ɛkɛ. Ɛnee bɛdi edwɛkɛ wɔ menli mɔɔ bɛyia ɛkɛ la anyu-nlu amaa bɛade edwɛkɛ bie mɔɔ ɛzi nee mɔɔ bavi nu ara la. (Dit. 21:18-20) Kɛmɔ menli dɔɔnwo wɔ ɛkɛ ne la ati, ɛnee ɔboa ɔmaa ndɛnebuavolɛma ne bua ndɛne fɔɔnwo. Saa ndɛnebuavolɛma ne si

Nɔhavoma maanle Yizilayɛma mɔɔ yia sua-kpole ne nlenkɛ mgbole ne mɔɔ anloa la anwo-sesebɛ kɛ bɛva bɛ nwo bɛdo Gyihova anwo zo. Kɛmɔ ngapezoma nee menli gyɛne ze kɛ menli kɔyia nlenkɛ mgbole ne mɔ anloa la ati, ɛnee bɛka bɛ edwɛkɛ ne wɔ ɛkɛ. (1 Arl. 22:10; Gyɛ. 17:19, 20) Kɛ neazo la, Gyihu hanle ye wɔ menli ne amuala anyunlu kɛ edwɛkɛ biala mɔɔ Gyihova hanle ye wɔ Ehabe sua nu amra anwo la ɛra nu. (2 Arl. 10:8-10) Belemgbunli Hɛzikaya boɔboale ye sogya mgbanyima ne mɔ anloa wɔ suakpole ne anle-nkɛ kpole bie anloa ɛkɛ ne kɛ ɔmaa bɛ anwosese-bɛ yeamaa bɛava bɛ nwo bɛado Gyihova anwo zo.—2 Ek. 32:6-8.

Baebolo ne ka kɛ “nɔhalɛ nrɛlɛbɛ” ɛlɛtea nu yɛɛ ɔmaa ɔ ne zo “wɔ suakpole ne nlenkɛ mgbole ne mɔ anloa.” (Mrɛ. 1:20, 21) Wɔ tete Yizilayɛ, ɛnee mrenyia nɔhavoma tu folɛ kpalɛ yɛɛ bɛbua ndɛne fɔɔnwo wɔ suakpole ne nlenkɛ mgbole ne mɔ anloa. Ɛnɛ, Gyihova ɛmaa bɛhɛlɛ ye nrɛlɛbɛ ne anwo edwɛkɛ wɔ Baebolo ne anu na ye azonvolɛ mɔɔ bɛwɔ ewiade ye ɛleka biala la ɛlɛboa menli bɛamaa bɛanwu nɔhalɛ nrɛlɛbɛ ɛhye.
