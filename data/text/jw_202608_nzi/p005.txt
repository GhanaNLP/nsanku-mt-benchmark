10 Saa yɛlɛyia ngyegyelɛ a, Seetan kulo kɛ yɛdie yedi kɛ Gyihova ɛndwenle yɛ nwo. Ɔ nye la kɛ saa yɛ ngyegyelɛ ne kyɛ a yɛbava Gyihova anwo ɛya. (Mrɛ. 19:3) Bie a bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛbade nganeɛ zɛhae bie la bɛaha ye bɛ ti anu kɛ: ‘Duzu ati bɔbɔ a metɛkɔ zo me-tɛdi Gyihova mɛla nee ye ngyinlazo ne mɔ azo a? Mennyia zolɛ nvasoɛ biala!’ Adwenle zɛhae bahola amaa Seetan anyia yɛ nwo zo tumi.

11 Seetan dua sohyia media zo maa menli adwenle nu yɛ bɛ kesee wɔ Gyihova ngyinla-zo ne mɔ anwo. Kɛ neazo la, menli dɔɔnwo ka wɔ ye wɔ sohyia media zo kɛ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nwo ngyinlazo mɔɔ wɔ Baebolo ne anu la ɛkpa nwo na ɔwɔ kɛ awie biala ɔdaye ɔsi ɛbɛla mɔɔ ɔkulo kɛ ɔbɔ la anwo kpɔkɛ. Ɔda ali bɔkɔɔ kɛ ahenle “mɔɔ nea ewiade ye azo la” ɛbe kpalɛ wɔ kɛzi ɔmaa menli nyia adwenle kɛ kpalɛ le ɛtane yɛɛ ɛtane le kpalɛ la anu.—Dwɔn 14:30; Aye. 5:20; 1 Tim. 4:1, 2.

13 Shannon nwunle kɛ ɔhyia kɛ ɔyɛ nzenza-leɛ amaa yeahola yeayɛ Gyihova nee Gyisɛse agɔnwo dahuu. Ɔhanle kɛ: “Menyɛle asɔne na menyɛle neɛnleanu wɔ yɛ mbuluku ne mɔ anu. Vidio ne mɔɔ ɔ ti se Young People Ask—How Can I Make Real Friends? noko boale me kpalɛ.* Wɔ vidio zɔhane anu, adiema bɛlɛ-ra bie mɔɔ ɛnee ɛlɛyia me ngyegyelɛ ne bie la bɛlɛle ɔ nwo aze hanle ye edwɛkɛ hilele ye awovolɛ na ɔmaanle bɛboale ye. Medame noko menyɛle ye zɔ. Me awovolɛ, asafo nu mgbanyima nee mediema mraalɛ mɔɔ bɛnyi sunsum nu la boale me maanle mendwenle me nwo menvile agɔnwolɛ ɛtane nwo. Me-mbandele ɔ bo kɛ, saa mewɔ nɔhalɛ ne anu a, tɛ mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ meyɛ la ala a le kɛ mebahɔ de-biezukoalɛ na meayɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ me awovo-lɛ anye die nwo la. Emomu ɔwɔ kɛ medame mumua ne mesukoa Baebolo ne na medie meto nu kɛ ɔle nɔhalɛ na mefa Gyihova agɔ-nwolɛ kyengye.”
