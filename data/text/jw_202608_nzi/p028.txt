‘Duzu ati a azɛlɛ ne maa yɛ sa ka ninyɛne dɔɔnwo zɛhae a? Ole kɛ awuke bie mɔɔ bɛziezie nu na bɛtoto debie biala boɛ bɛmaa awie mɔɔ ɔlɛba yearadɛnla nu la.’ Menwunle kɛ debie biala amba mgbane. Noko nwane a hyehyɛle debie biala maanle yɛ a? Ɔbayɛ kɛ Bɔvolɛ bie wɔ ɛkɛ. Noko ɛnee menze ɔ nwo ɛhwee.

Mendoale zo mengengale Baebolo ne. Noko mekɛ ɛhye ɛdeɛ ɛnee mekulo kɛ menwu mɔɔ Nyamenle kpondɛ kɛ meyɛ la. Membɔle ɔ bo kɛ meyɛ asɔne na meayɛ meanubaka sɛkɛlɛneɛ ne wɔ me nwoma azo kɛ mɔɔ Kateleke asɔnema ne yɛ la, ɔnva nwo kɛ ɛnee mende deɛmɔti bɛyɛ zɔ la abo la. Wɔ yɛ namule ne azo, ɛnee yɛlɛto edwɛne a yɛbɔ duma Jah wɔ nu, ɔti ɛnee mekulo kɛ menwu ye saa Bɔvolɛ ne duma a le Jah a.

KƐZI MENWUNLE ME ZE NE MƆƆ Ɔ TI AKUNLU LE KƐNLƐMA LA

Saa mendwenle mekɛ mɔɔ membɔle ɔ bo kɛ meyɛ asɔne la anwo a, ɔmaa mete nganeɛ kɛ Bɔvolɛ ne nwunle kɛ mekulo kɛ menwu ɔ nwo edwɛkɛ dɔɔnwo. Me gɔnwo raalɛ bie mɔɔ wɔ namule ne azo la yiale Gyihova Alasevolɛ na bɛmaanle ye You Can Live Forever in Paradise on Earth* buluku ne ko. Na mɔɔ mengengale ye wɔ buluku

* Gyihova Alasevolɛ a yɛle a noko bɛmpenlente ye bieko ɛnɛ.

ne anu la zinle me nwo. Me nye liele kɛ menwunle kɛ mebahola meadɛnla aze dahuu wɔ paladaese azɛlɛ zo la. Na mɔɔ ɛnee melɛkpondɛ la ala ɛne, kɛ mebadɛnla azɛlɛ ye azo ɛleka kɛnlɛma na me nee menli mɔɔ mekulo bɛ la adɛnla anzodwolɛ nu mɔɔ menrɛwu ɛlɛ a. Ɛnee mekulo kɛ mebikye Bɔvolɛ ɛhye mɔɔ ɔ ti akunlu le kɛnlɛma mɔɔ ɛyɛ kenle bie asetɛnla kɛnlɛma ɛhye anwo ngyehyɛleɛ ɛmaa yɛ la.
<<<P>>>
Mendele ɔ bo kɛ ɔhyia kɛ mekakyi me subane bie mɔ, noko eza menwunle kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ menyia awie boa me. Ɛnee edwɛkpatɛlevolɛ bie mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Michel la nee me gɔnwo nrenyia bie sukoa Baebolo ne, ɔti menganle mengilele ye kɛ ɔha ɔhile Michel ɔmaa ɔ nee medame noko ɛrazukoa debie.
<<<P>>>
Kenle ko ne ala mɔɔ menyiale Michel la, ɔhanle kɛ membɛla nyianu bie mɔɔ bɛbayɛ wɔ Matoury la bie. Saa mefa kale a, mebapɛ adenle kɛyɛ dɔnehwele nsa kolaa na meadwu lɔ. Ɛnee me enrinli ne wale kpalɛ yɛɛ me kɛsa noko ɛyɛ buzuu zɛhae. Menwulale telawoza mɔɔ ɔ nye le kɛ buluwu yɛɛ penke mɔɔ bɛpentipenti nu fufule la yɛɛ anwuma adɛladeɛ mgbole mgbole bie a mengɔle nyianu ne a. Ɔnva nwo kɛzi ɛnee meziezie me nwo la, mediema ne mɔ duale me kale kakɛ, bɛmaanle me aleɛ yɛɛ dabelɛ. Ɛlɔlɛ nee nyele mɔɔ mediema ne mɔ lale ye ali la maanle menliele menlile kɛ menwu nɔhalɛ ne.
