Saa yɛkenga Baebolo ne dahuu a ɔmaa anwodozo mɔɔ yelɛ la yɛ kpole
<<<P>>>
(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 10)
<<<P>>>
11 Ɔboa yɛ ɔmaa yɛnyia subane ngɛnlɛma. Pɔɔlo hanle hilele kpavolɛ Timote kɛ ɔhɔ zo ɔnyia anyuhɔlɛ wɔ ɛdendɛlɛ, ɛbɛlabɔlɛ, ɛlɔlɛ, diedi nee anwodelɛ nu. (1 Tim. 4:12, 15) Saa yɛkenga alehyenlɛ ne anu tɛkese ne a, ɔmaa yɛnyia adenle yɛdwenle kɛzi yɛbanyia subane kpalɛ la anwo. Ɛhye bamaa Gyihova ava ye sunsum ne ahile yɛ adenle na yɛazukoa kɛ yɛ-banyia adwenle mɔɔ ɔlɛ wɔ ninyɛne nwo la bie na yɛayɛ mɔ sɔ ɔ nye la.—Wlo. 8:5, 6.
<<<P>>>
12 Ɔbaboa yɛ yeamaa yɛaziezie yɛ nwo yɛa-maa gyimalilɛ ngakyile wɔ Gyihova ɛzonle-nlɛ nu kenle bie. Kɛmɔ Gyihova ahyehyɛdeɛ ne ɛlɛnyia anyuhɔlɛ la ati, yɛhyia mediema mrenyia nee mraalɛ mɔɔ bɛnyi sunsum nu la amaa bɛaboa wɔ gyima kpole mɔɔ yɛlɛ yɛyɛ la anu. Yɛhyia mediema mrenyia nee mraalɛ mɔɔ bɛbayɛ adekpakyelɛma nee edwɛkpatɛ-lɛvolɛma la. Eza yɛhyia mediema mɔɔ bɛba-yɛ gyima wɔ Bɛtɛle nee edwɛkɛbohilelɛ ɔfese
<<<P>>>
11. Saa yɛyɛ alehyenlɛ ne anu tɛkese ne a, kɛzi ɔboa yɛ ɔmaa yɛnyia subane ngɛnlɛma ɛ?
<<<P>>>
12. Nwane mɔ a bɛhyia bɛ wɔ Gyihova ahyehyɛdeɛ ne anu a, na duzu a ɔhyia kɛ yɛyɛ amaa bɛava yɛ bɛali gyi-ma a?
<<<P>>>
ngakyile ne mɔ anu yɛɛ bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛbaboa suazilɛ gyima ne la. Bieko, yɛhyia mgbavo-lɛ mɔɔ bɔ mɔdenle na bɛbɛlɛ bɛ nwo aze la amaa bɛazonle kɛ asafo nu azonvolɛ, asafo nu mgbanyima nee maangyebakyi zo neavolɛma. Gyihova ɛlɛkpondɛ mediema mrenyia mɔɔ bɛ-kola bɛdie bɛ bɛdi, mrenyia mɔɔ bɛfa Baebolo ngyinlazo bɛbɔ bɛ ɛbɛla la yeamaa bɛali ye ahyehyɛdeɛ ne anyunlu. (Dit. 1:13-15) Ɔti ale-hyenlɛ ne anu tɛkese ne baboa yɛ yeamaa yɛa-nyia debie mɔɔ yɛbayɛ ye kenle ko biala amaa yɛabikye Gyihova na yeamaa yɛanyia subane ngakyile mɔɔ ɔbaboa yɛ wɔ gyimalilɛ biala anu la.
