9 Mɔɔ bɛvale Daneɛle nee Hibulu mgbavolɛ gyɛne bɛhɔle Babɛlɔn la, bɛyiale sɔnea ngakyi-le mɔɔ bɛanyia bie wɔ bɛ sua zo ɛlɛ la. Bɛdwu-le Babɛlɔn ala la, Babɛlɔnoma bɔle mɔdenle kɛ bɛmaa bɛabɔ bɛ ɛbɛla kɛ bɛdabɛ la na bɛali bɛ amaamuo ngakyile ne mɔ azo. Eza bɛzile kpɔ-kɛ kɛ bɛtete bɛ bɛamaa bɛayɛ gyima wɔ bele-mgbunli sua nu ɛkɛ. Noko ɛnee ndetelɛ ɛhye bamaa bɛahɔ tɛnlabelɛ mɔɔ ɔbamaa yeayɛ se kɛ bɛbali nɔhalɛ bɛamaa Gyihova la anu.

10 Wɔannea a ɛbaha kɛ sɔnea mɔɔ bɛlimoale bɛyiale la ɛnyɛ ɛzulolɛ biala: Belemgbunli ne hanle kɛ bɛmaa Daneɛle nee mgbavolɛ gyɛne mɔɔ ɛha la ɛli aleɛ mɔɔ bɛfa bɛmaa ye la ala bie. Ɛnee aleɛ mɔɔ Mɔsisi Mɛla ne ka kɛ nwolɛ ɛnde la bie boka nwo. (Dan. 1:5, 8) Anrɛɛ mgbavolɛ ne mɔ bahola anyia adwenle kɛ saa bɛanli Mɛla ne azo a ɔnzɛkye debie ɔluakɛ bɛwɔ akɛlɛzonlenlɛ nu, ɔti tɛ mɔɔ bɛkulo la a bɛbayɛ a. Eza anrɛɛ bɛbahola bɛanyia adwenle kɛ nrɛlɛbɛ ɛnle nu kɛ bɛbayɛ debie mɔɔ ɔba-maa belemgbunli ne ava ɛya la. Ɔti duzu a bɛ-bayɛ a?

bɛ bɛmaa bɛzonle belemgbunli ne la anu nna ala a bɛbobɔle bɛ aluma kɛ bɛhɔle zo bɛlile nɔ-halɛ bɛmaanle Gyihova a. Bɛmɛ a le Daneɛ-le, Hyeedelake, Mihyake yɛɛ Abɛdenɛgo. Agɔ-nwɔlɛma nna ɛhye mɔ boale bɛ nwo ngoko amaa bɛaho bɛatia sɔnea na bɛahɔ zo bɛali nɔ-halɛ. Bɛ muala bɛkpole kɛ bɛbali belemgbunli ne aleɛ ne. Ɛhye ati Gyihova yilale bɛ.—Dan. 1:6-12, 17.

12 Asoo ɔyɛ a ɛ tipɛnema tinlitinli ɛ nwo zo kɛ ɛyɛ debie mɔɔ ɛnle kpalɛ la ɔ? Daneɛle nee ɔ gɔnwo mɔ boale bɛ nwo ngoko maanle bɛzile kpɔkɛ kpalɛ. Kɛ mɔɔ mgbavolɛ zɔhane mɔ yɛle la, ɛdawɔ noko fa menli mɔɔ bɛbɔ kpɔkɛ kye-ngye kɛ bɛbali nɔhalɛ bɛamaa Gyihova dahuu na bɛnrɛmaa ewiade ɛhye ɛnrɛzɛkye bɛ subane la agɔnwɔlɛ. (1 Dwɔn 2:15-17) Agɔnwɔlɛma kpalɛ bahola aboa wɔ bɛamaa wɔali nɔhalɛ wɔ “debie ekyi” bɔbɔ anu na bɛaboa wɔ bɛamaa wɔayɛ mɔɔ sɔ Gyihova anye la amaa ɔ nee wɔ alua dahuu. (Luku 16:10) Saa edi nɔhalɛ wɔ ni-nyɛne ngyikyi zɛhae mɔ anu a ɔbaboa wɔ yea-maa wɔali nɔhalɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɛbayia sɔnea mgbole la.
