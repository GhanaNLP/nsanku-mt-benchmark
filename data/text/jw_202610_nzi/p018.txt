EDWƐNE 135
Gyihova Modolɛ: “Me Ra, Nwu Nrɛlɛbɛ”
<<<P>>>
BODANE
Saa mgbavolɛ nee mbɛlɛra fa agɔnwolɛ kpalɛ a, ɔbahola yeaboa bɛ yeamaa bɛagyinla kpundii wɔ nɔhalɛ ɛzonlenlɛ nu.
<<<P>>>
Yɛyɛ Ko A Yɛbahola Yɛagyinla
<<<P>>>
“Awie bahola ali sonla ko anwo zo konim, noko menli nwiɔ bahola abɔ nu aho atia ye.”—NOL. 4:12.
<<<P>>>
KƐMƆ yɛle Gyihova alasevolɛ la ati, yɛze ye wienyi kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛgyinla nɔhalɛ ɛzonlenlɛ nzi. Noko ewiade ɛhye twehwe yɛ dahuu kɛ ɔmaa yɛavi Gyihova anwo. Ɔti ɔwɔ kɛ yɛbɔ mɔdenle kpalɛ amaa yɛannyia adwenle mɔɔ bɛlɛ la bie. Adwenle ɛhye mɔ bie a le kɛzi bɛbu agɔnwolɛvalɛ la. Kɛ neazo la, bie mɔ te nganeɛ kɛ mɔɔ hyia kpalɛ la a le kɛ bɛbanyia agɔnwolɛma dɔɔnwo, saa bɛle agɔnwolɛma kpalɛ o, saa bɛnle kpalɛ o, yemɔ ɛngyia bɛ. Mgbavolɛ nee mbɛlɛra dɔɔnwo kpondɛ folɛdulɛ nee arɛlekyekyelɛ fi bɛ tipɛnema ɛkɛ. Menli bie mɔ noko die di kɛ bɛngyia agɔnwolɛ biala anwo. Noko adwenle boni a Gyihova nee Gyisɛse lɛ wɔ agɔnwolɛvalɛ nwo a?
<<<P>>>
2 Gyihova kulo kɛ yɛfa agɔnwolɛ mɔɔ ɔbaboa yɛ yeamaa yɛazonle ye nɔhalɛ nu la. Agɔnwolɛ zɔhane bie a ɔ Ra Gyisɛse vale a. Ɔka ekyii Gyisɛse wu la, ɔhanvole ye ɛzoanvolɛma ne kɛ: “Bɛmɛ a bɛbeta me nwo wɔ me sɔnea ne mɔ anu a.” Ɔnwunle bɛ kɛ bɛle ɔ gɔnwo mɔ. (Luku 22:28; Dwɔn 15:15) Agɔnwolɛ kpalɛ a le awie mɔɔ ɔbeta wɔ kyengye wɔ mekɛ kpalɛ nee “anwongyelelɛ mekɛ” nu la. (Mrɛ. 17:17) Nolobɔvo 4:9-12* maa yɛnwu kɛ agɔnwolɛma kpalɛ kola maa yɛ anwosesebɛ na bɛkyekye yɛ rɛle. (Kenga.) Eza bɛboa yɛ bɛmaa yɛyɛ mɔɔ le kpalɛ la. Saa yɛlɛ agɔnwolɛma zɛhae bie a, yɛbahola yɛaho yɛatia debie biala mɔɔ ɔbahola yeazɛkye yɛ nee Gyihova agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne la. Eza saa yɛ nee yɛ gɔnwo mɔ bɔ nu yɛ ninyɛne boa Gyihova ɛzonlenlɛ a,
<<<P>>>
* Saa yɛlɛka agyalɛma anwo edwɛkɛ a yɛɛ yɛta yɛfa Baebolo tɛkese ɛhye yɛdi gyima, noko edwɛkɛ ɛhye fale nvasoɛ mɔɔ wɔ agɔnwolɛvalɛ zo la anwo titili.
<<<P>>>
1. Adwenle boni a menli dɔɔnwo lɛ wɔ agɔnwolɛvalɛ nwo a?
<<<P>>>
2. (a) Duzu a Gyisɛse yɛle hilele kɛ ɔ nye die agɔnwolɛ kpalɛ nwo a? (b) Kɛ mɔɔ Nolobɔvo 4:9-12 kile la, duzu a agɔnwolɛma kpalɛ bahola ayɛ aboa yɛ a?
