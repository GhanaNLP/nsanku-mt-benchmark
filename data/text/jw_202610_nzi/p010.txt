Duzu a ɛbahola wɔayɛ wɔava wɔahile mɔɔ awie mɔ ɛyɛ ɛmaa wɔ la anwo anyezɔlɛ a?
(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 14)
<<<P>>>
ɛlɔlɛ la aye mɔ noko maa bɛ hu mɔ fa mekɛ mɔɔ anrɛɛ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛfa bɛ nee bɛ yɛ ninyɛne bɔ nu la boa mediema ne mɔ na bɛmaa awie mɔ anwosesebɛ. Ɛhye ati ɔyɛ a ɛbahola wɔahile mɔɔ bɛyɛ la anwo anyezɔlɛ.
<<<P>>>
15 Eza yɛkulo kɛ yɛdua edwɛkɛ mɔɔ yɛka la azo yɛkile mɔɔ Gyihova ɛyɛ ɛmaa yɛ la anwo anyezɔlɛ. Kɛzi yɛbayɛ ye ɛ? Yemɔ a le kɛ yɛbavi yɛ ahonle nu yɛayɛ asɔne. Noko kakye kɛ asɔneyɛlɛ gua nu ngakyile. (Ɛfɛ. 6:18) Bie a yɛta yɛyɛ nzelɛlɛ anzɛɛ modolɛ asɔne. Saa yɛlɛyɛ asɔne zɛhae a yɛsɛlɛ debie yɛfi Gyihova ɛkɛ. Bie a yɛka yɛkile ye kɛ ɔmaa yɛ anwosesebɛ ɔmaa yɛva yɛgyinla sɔnea bie anloa anzɛɛ ɔmaa yɛ nrɛlɛbɛ mɔɔ yɛbava yɛazi kpɔkɛ kpalɛ anzɛɛ ɔva yɛ ɛtane ɔhyɛ yɛ. Noko saa yɛdwenle ninyɛne mgbalɛ mɔɔ Gyihova ɛyɛ ɛmaa yɛ la anwo a, eza ɔka yɛ ɔmaa yɛyɛ moyelɛ asɔne. (Edw. 95:2) Ɔyɛ a yɛbahola yɛadwenle yɛ asɔneyɛlɛ nwo na yɛabiza yɛ nwo kɛ, ‘Asoo ɔle me subane kɛ mebayɛ Gyihova mo wɔ mɔɔ yeyɛ yemaa me la anwo anzɛɛ dahuu mɔɔ mehyia nwo la ala a meka mekile ye ɔ?’
<<<P>>>
16 Dua wɔ nyɛleɛ zo. Saa awie yɛ debie maa ɛ nye die kpole a, kpondɛ ndenle dua zo kile mɔɔ yeyɛ yemaa wɔ la anwo anyezɔlɛ. Ɛbahola wɔayɛ ahenle mo ɛdeɛ, noko eza ɔwɔ kɛ ɛdua wɔ nyɛleɛ zo ɛkile mɔɔ yeyɛ la anwo anyezɔlɛ. Saa ɛyɛ zɔ a ɔbamaa wɔanyia anyelielɛ mɔɔ yɛkye awie mɔ debie a yɛnyia la bie. (Gyi. 20:35) Eza ɛbamaa awie mɔ ade nganeɛ kɛ bɛ nwo hyia yɛɛ menli anye sɔ mɔɔ bɛyɛ la. Ɛhye bamaa bɛ agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne anu amia.
<<<P>>>
17 Yɛbahola yɛalua yɛ nyɛleɛ zo yɛahile mɔɔ Gyihova ɛyɛ ɛmaa yɛ la anwo anyezɔlɛ. Yɛ nye die debie biala mɔɔ yeva yemaa yɛ la anwo kpole kpalɛ, ɛhye ati ɔka yɛ ɔmaa yɛdayɛ noko yɛyɛ debie yɛmaa ye. Duzu a yɛbahola yɛayɛ yɛamaa Gyihova a? Ɔwɔ kɛ yɛfa yɛ ‘sonlabaka ne yɛmaa Nyamenle kɛ afɔlebɔlɛ mɔɔ de ngoane nu.’ (Kenga Wulomuma
