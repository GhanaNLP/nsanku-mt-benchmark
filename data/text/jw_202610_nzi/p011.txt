18 Yɛyila yɛ nwo zo yɛmaa Gyihova na yɛ-
maa bɛsɔne yɛ yɛfa yɛkile mɔɔ yeyɛ yemaa yɛ
la anwo anyezɔlɛ. Bieko, yɛfa ninyɛne mgba-
lɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛ, mɔɔ bie a le yɛ mekɛ, yɛ anwose-
sebɛ, yɛ ezukoa nee ninyɛne gyɛne mɔɔ yɛlɛ
la yɛboa wɔ Gyihova ɛzonlenlɛ nu. Ɛhye ati
yɛyɛ biala mɔɔ yɛbahola la yɛkenga ye Edwɛ-
kɛ ne kenle ko biala na yɛdwenledwenle nwo-
lɛ, yɛbɔ edwɛkpa ne nolo, yɛkɔ debiezukoalɛ
ngakyile na yɛmaa mualɛ yɛɛ yɛye ndoboa
yɛboa Belemgbunlililɛ gyima ne. (Edw. 1:2, 3;
2 Kɔl. 8:4; Hib. 10:24, 25; 13:15) Saa yɛfa yɛ
ɛkɛla muala yɛsonle Gyihova a, ɛnee yɛlɛda
ye ali kɛ yɛ nye sɔ mɔɔ ɔyɛ ɔmaa yɛ la kpalɛ.
—Kɔl. 3:23, 24.

19 Yɛde ewiade mɔɔ menli le boniayɛ la anu
ɛdeɛ, noko yɛmɛ yɛze kɛ ɔle kpalɛ kɛ yɛbahile
anyezɔlɛ. Yɛze kɛ saa yɛkile anyezɔlɛ a ɔmaa
yɛ nye die, adwenleadwenle ɛndɔ yɛ nwo yɛɛ
mɔɔ hyia kpalɛ la ɔmaa yɛbikye Gyihova. Saa
yɛdwenle mɔɔ Gyihova ɛyɛ ɛmaa yɛ dɛba nee
mɔɔ ɔlɛyɛ yeamaa yɛ ɛnɛ la anwo a, ɔmaa yɛ-
nyia ɛhulolɛ kpole kɛ yɛbayɛ ye mo. Maa yɛbɔ
kpɔkɛ kɛ yɛbalua yɛ edwɛkɛ nee yɛ nyɛleɛ zo
yɛahile anyezɔlɛ. Noko mɔɔ hyia kpalɛ la, bɛ-
maa yɛhile mɔɔ Gyihova ɛyɛ la anwo anyezɔ-
lɛ. Saa yɛyɛ zɔ a yɛ Nyamenle ne mɔɔ kile
anyezɔlɛ la bayila yɛ molozo ɛnɛ nee mekɛ
mɔɔ ɛnlɛ awieleɛ la.—Edw. 106:1; Hib. 11:6.
