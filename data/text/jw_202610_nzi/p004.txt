Skinner mɔɔ ɔvi Sheffield mɔɔ wɔ England la. Mɔɔ bɛwiele nyianu ne la, Adiema Rutherford hanle hilele Edwin kɛ anrɛɛ ɔranwu ye wɔ London.
<<<P>>>
Adiema Skinner hanle kɛ: “Mɔɔ rale me adwenle nu la ala a le kɛ bɛkulo kɛ mekɔ maanle fofolɛ zo. Aleɛ hyenle la yɛɛ mengɔle London a. Na ɛnee mebɔ kpɔkɛ kɛ saa bɛka kɛ mengɔ maanle gyɛne nu a menrɛgyinlegyinla nwolɛ.” Amgba noko Adiema Rutherford bizale ye kɛ: “Ɛ nye balie nwo kɛ ɛbahɔ India ɔ?” Edwin buale ye kɛ: “Kenlenzu a ɛkulo kɛ mekɔ a?”
<<<P>>>
Wɔ dapɛne nsa anzi wɔ July, Edwin nee George Wright mɔɔ ɔdaye noko ɔle edwɛkpakavolɛ mɔɔ bɔ mɔdenle la vale meli hɔle India. Adiema Skinner yɛle ɔfese sonvolɛ na ɔnleanle gyima mɔɔ bɛyɛ wɔ Afghanistan, Burma (Myanmar), Ceylon (Sri Lanka), India yɛɛ Persia (Iran) la azo. Adiema Skinner dɛnlanle India too ɛvolɛ 64 kɔkpula kɛ ɔwiele ye azelɛ ye azo gyima wɔ 1990 na ɔwule la.

NEAVOLƐ ADENDUVOMA LILE EDWƐKƐHANLƐ GYIMA NE ANYUNLU
<<<P>>>
Ɛnee ahyehyɛdeɛ ne soa mediema bie mɔ maa bɛkɔkpɔlɔkpɔla Baebolo Sukoavoma ekpunli ngakyile ne mɔ ɔvi 1894. Wɔ July 1926, gyima mɔɔ adenduvoma ɛhye mɔ yɛ la hakyile. Ɛnee bɛfa kenle ko a bɛkpɔla mediema ne mɔ a noko bɛhanle kɛ bɛva kenle nwiɔ. Eza bɛmaanle bɛvale dɔnehwele 100 bɛyɛle daselɛlilɛ gyima ne siane biala.
<<<P>>>
Adiema George Draper mɔɔ le neavolɛ adenduvo la hanle ye wɔ kɛlata bie mɔɔ ɔhɛlɛle The Watch Tower ne la anu kɛ, ɔwɔ kɛ mediema nee neavolɛ adenduvoma kɔsɔɔti siezie bɛ nwo kɛ bɛbabɔ menli kɔkɔ. Na bɛbahola bɛayɛ zɔ a kyesɛ bɛsukoa The Watch Tower ne. Adiema Draper wiele ye azelɛ ye azo gyima ne wɔ 1938.

NEW YORK CITY NYIANU NE
<<<P>>>
Nyianu kpole mɔɔ li awieleɛ wɔ 1926 la a le mɔɔ bɛyɛle wɔ New York City ɔvi October 14-18 la. Menli mɔɔ kɛlɛ adwelielilɛ ngɛlata la liele dole nu kɛ bɛbabɔ Adiema Rutherford ɛdendɛlɛ ne mɔɔ ɔ ti ne se “Earth’s Greatest Conflict Near” la nolo. Noko akee ɔka ekyii nyianu ne dwu zo la, bɛnuhua dɔɔnwo hanle kɛ bɛnrɛbɔ ye nolo.
<<<P>>>
Mediema ne mɔ yɛle trate bie kɛ bɛdua zo bɛaha mɔɔ adwelielilɛ ngɛlata ne mɔ ɛyɛ la anwo edwɛkɛ bɛahile maanlema ne. Ɔti wɔ Yalɛ, October 15, Adiema R. J. Martin yele zɔhane trate ne mɔɔ ɔ ti ne se Against the People la. Adiema Martin hanle kɛ, “Yɛdayɛ mumua ne yɛbɔ ɔ bo yɛlɛpelente yɛ mbuluku, na mediema mɔɔ bɛyɛ gyima wɔ Bɛtɛle la bayɛ gyima alehyenlɛ nee aledwolɛ apelente trate 1,250,000. Yɛbamaa mediema mɔɔ wɔ nyianu ɛhye abo la ava ahɔ daselɛlilɛ na bɛahyɛ bɛamaa awie mɔ.” Mɔɔ bɛkpɔnenle nyianu ne la, mediema ne mɔ vale trate ne hɔle daselɛlilɛ. Bɛhyɛle trate ngoko 300,000 nɔsolɛ zɔhane ne.
<<<P>>>
Folɛ nee Molɛ kahɔ awieleɛ la ɛnee mediema ne mɔ ɛhyɛ trate kɛyɛ mgbe ko. Eza bɛhyɛle Deliverance buluku ne 3,783 yɛɛ The Standard for the People buluku ekyi ne 35,140.
<<<P>>>
