<<<P>>>
Kile Anyezɔlɛ Dahuu
<<<P>>>
“Bɛhile anyezɔlɛ.”—KƆL. 3:15.
<<<P>>>
ADIEMA bie mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye John* la ɛli ɛvolɛ 95; kɛmɔ yeyɛ
ɛrelera na ɔnlɛ anwosesebɛ la ati ɔlɛ menli mɔɔ bɛnea ye a.
Kenle ko biala saa awie bakpɔla John anzɛɛ awie yɛ debie ekyi
bie bɔbɔ boa ye a, ɔka ɔkile ahenle kɛ, “Meda wɔ ase.” Akee
ɔkɛlɛ ahenle duma wɔ ye buluku ekyi bie anu. Ɔdwu nɔsolɛ
nu a, ɔka ɔkile ahenle mɔɔ ɛlɛnea ye la kɛ ɔboa ye ɔmaa ɔho-
to ye ɛkpa ne anwo amaa yeayɛ asɔne. Akee John yɛ Gyiho-
va mo wɔ atiakunlukɛnlɛma mɔɔ bɛ nuhua ko biala lale ye ali
hilele ye wɔ kenle zɔhane la anwo. Menli mɔɔ nea ye la anu ko
hanle kɛ, “John ɛnde kpɔkɛ kpalɛ ɛdeɛ noko ɔ nye die dahuu
yɛɛ ɔ kunlu dwo ɔ nwo.” John anwubielɛ ne maa yɛnwu kɛ saa
yɛkile ninyɛne nwo anyezɔlɛ a, ɔmaa yɛ nye die na yɛ kunlu
dwo yɛ nwo.
<<<P>>>
2 Alɔbɔlɛ edwɛkɛ ne a le kɛ yɛde ewiade mɔɔ menli ɛngile
anyezɔlɛ la anu. Baebolo ne hanle dole ɛkɛ ne kɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ
li awieleɛ la anu, menli bayɛ boniayɛma. (2 Tim. 3:1, 2) Wɔ
menli bie mɔ afoa nu, saa awie yɛ debie maa bɛ a bie a bɛ rɛle
fi la ati a bɛnyɛ ye mo a. Noko menli dɔɔnwo ɛdeɛ bɛnnwu kɛ
ɔhyia kɛ bɛkile anyezɔlɛ a. Saa awie yɛ bɛ boɛ a, bɛte nganeɛ
kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛyɛ bɛmaa bɛ la ati a bɛyɛ a. Bɛle angomedi kpalɛ.
<<<P>>>
3 Ɔwɔ kɛ Gyihova azonvolɛ yɛ ngakyile fi ewiade ɛhye anwo.
Ɛzoanvolɛ Pɔɔlo maanle yɛ anwosesebɛ kɛ: “Bɛhile anyezɔ-
lɛ.” (Kɔl. 3:15) Ɔti saa awie yɛ debie maa yɛ a, ɔnle kɛ yɛmaa
