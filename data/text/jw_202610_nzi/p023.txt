Gyihova ɛkɛ, na yɛhyia bɛ nwo ɛnɛ yɛtɛla mekɛ biala. Ɔti kɛ mɔɔ Daneɛle yɛle la, ɛdawɔ noko fa menli agɔnwolɛ. Nea mediema mɔɔ wɔ wɔ asafo ne anu mɔɔ le kɛ Gyɛlɛmaya mɔɔ ɛbaho-la wɔazukoa bɛ neazo ne na bɛamaa wɔ anwo-sesebɛ bɛamaa wɔayɛ mɔɔ tenrɛ la na fa bɛ agɔnwolɛ. Ɛ nee menli mɔɔ bɛbaboa wɔ bɛa-maa wɔamia kpɔkɛ mɔɔ wɔbɔ kɛ ɛbali nɔhalɛ wɔamaa Gyihova la anu la ɛdu. Bɛlɛ ɛ nwo aze na ka kile awie mɔ kɛ bɛyɛ asɔne bɛmaa wɔ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ nwolɛ bahyia la. Mɔɔ tɛla bia-la la, kakye ye dahuu kɛ Gyihova a le ɛ gɔ-nwo kpalɛ kpalɛ a. Ɔnrɛgyakyi wɔ ɛlɛ. (Hib. 13:5) Ɔlua Gyihova moalɛ zo, yɛbahola yɛabɔ nu yɛaho yɛatia debie biala mɔɔ ɔbamaa yɛa-yɛ se kɛ yɛbava awie mɔ agɔnwolɛ la amaa yɛanyia agɔnwolɛma mɔɔ yɛ nee bɛ badu da-huu la.

Gyihova Le Yɛ Ze, Yɛ Nyamenle Nee Yɛ Gɔnwo
<<<P>>>
Na saa ɔyɛ se ɔmaa wɔ kɛ ɛbabu Gyihova kɛ ɛ Ze anzɛɛ awie mɔɔ ɛbahola wɔalie ye wɔali la ɛ? Bɔ mɔdenle kenle ko biala kɛ ɛbada wɔayɛ ninyɛne ngyikyi wɔ subane ɛhye mɔ anu wɔa-va wɔabikye Nyamenle.
<<<P>>>
• Adwelielilɛ: Dua asɔneyɛlɛ zo ka wɔ edwɛkɛ kile Gyihova; ɔkulo kɛ ɔtie wɔ.
<<<P>>>
• Nyia mekɛ maa Gyihova: Tie mɔɔ Gyihova ɛlɛka ahile wɔ la wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɛlɛkenga Baebolo ne anzɛɛ ɛlɛsukoa la. Sukoa ye su-bane ngakyile nee ninyɛne mɔɔ yeyɛ la anwo debie amaa wɔanwu ye kpalɛ.
<<<P>>>
• Nyia abotane: Kakye kɛ ɔdie mekɛ na wɔalie awie wɔali na ɛ nee ye ava agɔnwolɛ. Ɔti kɔ zo bɔ mɔdenle kɛ ɛbabikye Gyihova ɔlua-kɛ ɔkulo kɛ bɛyɛ agɔnwolɛma.
