anu menli ne anwo a, yɛndwu bɛ bie. Noko yɛbahola yɛaha ye wienyi kɛ ngapezo mɔɔ se yɛ nwo bazo la ɛra nu. Ɔluakɛ wɔ 1914, ɛnee bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛlɛsonle Gyihova la dodo le kɛyɛ 5,000. Noko ɔvi ɛvolɛ zɔhane mɔɔ ba la, menli “ekyi” zɔhane anwo ɛzo ɛrayɛ nolobɔlɛma mɔɔ bo mgbe ngɔnla. Saa ɛtia maanle mɔɔ bo 130 mɔɔ wɔ ewiade la anu menli a, bɛ nuhua ko biala anu menli ne dodo ɛndwu mgbe ngɔnla. Ɛhye kile kɛ yɛ nwo zonle tɛla maanle bie mɔ anu menli. Kɛkala menli mɔɔ ɛranwu Gyihova na bɛlɛsonle ye la anwo zonle tɛla dɛba ne yɛɛ bɛwɔ maanle ngakyile 240 anu. Amgba “mɔɔ le ekyi la” ɛrayɛ “maanle kpole.”

4 Ezonlenlɛ dɔɔnwo anu menli ne anwo zonle ɔluakɛ bɛsɔne bie mɔ mbɔdɔma nu, noko yɛmɛ yɛnyɛ zɔ. Eza yɛndo Nyamenle ɛbɛlabɔlɛ kpalɛ nwo ngyinlazo ne mɔ anu amaa menli dɔɔnwo ara bie. Emomu, yɛzukoale Baebolo ne na yɛvale mɔɔ ɔkilehile la yɛbɔle ye ɛbɛla. Yɛ nuhua dɔɔnwo ɛdeɛ, ɛnee ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ nzenzaleɛ mgbole wɔ adwenle mɔɔ yɛlɛ wɔ ninyɛne bie mɔ anwo la anu na yɛkakyi yɛ subane. (1 Kɔl. 6:9-11) Yɛyɛle nzenzaleɛ zɔhane mɔ yɛwiele la, akee yɛholale yɛbɔle edwɛkpa ne nolo na yɛdole ɛsalɛ yɛvɛlɛle awie mɔ kɛ bɛrɛla nɔhalɛ ɛzonlenlɛ nu. Nzinlii yɛyilale yɛ nwo zo yɛmaanle Gyihova anzɛɛ yɛbɔle ye ɛwɔkɛ kɛ yɛbayɛ ye ɛhulolɛdeɛ ɔnva nwo mɔɔ bazi biala la na yɛmaanle bɛzɔnenle yɛ.—Mat. 28:19, 20.
