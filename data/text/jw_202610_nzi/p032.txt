Gyihova Maa Yɛ Diedi Nwo Ahatualɛ
<<<P>>>
Ɛnee adiema raalɛ Emma fa kenle nsa wɔ dapɛne ne anu yɛ gyima wɔ hotel bie anu yɛɛ ɔyɛ gyima dɔnehwele ngɔnla kenle ko biala. Kenle ko bie, ɔzelɛle ye gyima nu kpanyinli ne kɛ ɔmaa ye adenle kenle ko ɔmaa ɔva ɔhɔ maanzinli nyianu. Noko ye gyima nu kpanyinli ne anyɛ emomu, ɔhanle kɛ ɔvi mekɛ zɔhane mɔɔ kɔ la ɔbava kenle nnu yeayɛ gyima wɔ dapɛne ne anu. Akee ɔhanle kɛ saa ɔnrɛhola ngyehyeleɛ fofolɛ ne azo di a ɔgyakyi gyima ne. Emma zile kpɔkɛ kɛ ɔbagyakyi gyima ne amaa yeahola yeahɔ nyianu ne, na ɛnee ɔlɛ anwodozo kɛ Gyihova banlea ye.
<<<P>>>
Kenle mɔɔ Emma hɔle hotel ɛkɛ ne kɛ ɔkabɔ gyima nu kpanyinli ne amaneɛ kɛ ɔlɛgyakyi gyima la, ɛnee nrenyia ne mɔɔ nea gyimayɛvoma ne amuala anwo zo la wɔ ɛkɛ. Mɔɔ ɔdele edwɛkɛ ne la, ɔhanle ɔhilele Emma kɛ ɔbali nwolɛ gyima. Noko wɔ dapɛne nwiɔ anzi, bɛyele Emma ali. Kenle ko bie, eza ɔyiale nrenyia ne mɔɔ nea gyimayɛvoma ne amuala anwo zo la na ɔhanle ɔhilele ye kɛ bɛye ye ali. Nrenyia ne vale ɛya kɛ gyima nu kpanyinli ne anyɛ mɔɔ ɔhanle la na ɔyele Emma ali la. Ɔhanle ɔhilele Emma kɛ ɔzia ɔhɔ gyima na bɔbɔ ɔhakyile mekɛ mɔɔ ɔbava yeayɛ gyima la. Ɔbayɛ gyima kenle nnu wɔ dapɛne ne anu yɛɛ ɔbava dɔnehwele nna a yeayɛ gyima kenle ko biala a. Ɛnee yemɔ le kpalɛ tɛla dɛba ne na ɔmaanle ɔnyianle mekɛ ɔyɛle sunsum nu ninyɛne. Gyihova lile ye ngyianlɛ wɔ gyima maanle ye wɔ adenle mɔɔ ɛnee ɔ nye ɛnla la azo.
