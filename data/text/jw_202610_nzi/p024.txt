BODANE
Edwɛkɛ ɛhye bamaa yɛ anwosesebɛ yeamaa yɛazuzu kenle ko biala anu tɛkese ne anwo yɛɛ ɔbamaa yɛanwu ndenle bie mɔ mɔɔ yɛbahola yɛalua zo yɛazuzu nwo la.
<<<P>>>
Neɛnlea Ngɛlɛlera Ne Anu Kenle Ko Biala
<<<P>>>
“Bɛliele edwɛkɛ ne na dahuu bɛnleɛnleanle Ngɛlelera ne anu kpalɛ.”—GYI. 17:11.
<<<P>>>
WƆ 1905, Gyihova ahyehyɛdeɛ ne yɛle buluku bie mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Daily Heavenly Manna for the Household of Faith la. Saa ɛbuke buluku ne anu a, ɛbanwu kɛ ɛvolɛ ne anu kenle ko biala lɛ tɛkese ko mɔɔ bɛbazuzu nwo yɛɛ ɛnee edwɛkɛ mɔɔ Zion’s Watch Tower ne noko ka wɔ tɛkese ko biala anwo la boka nwo. Gyihova menli vale buluku ko zɔhane ala lile gyima too 1927, ɛvolɛ mɔɔ bo 20 la. Yemɔ anzi ɛnee akee bɛfa alehyenlɛ biala anu tɛkese ne nee nwolɛ ngilenu ne bɛboka Year Book* ne anwo. Kɛmɔ ɛnee bɛyɛ Year Book fofolɛ ɛvolɛ biala la ati, ɛnee akee ɛvolɛ biala mediema ne mɔ nyia Baebolo tɛkese nee ngilenu ngakyile mɔɔ The Watch Tower ne ka wɔ ngɛlɛlera ko biala anwo la.
<<<P>>>
2 Wɔ 1986, ahyehyɛdeɛ ne hanle alehyenlɛ ko biala anu tɛkese mɔɔ yɛbazuzu nwo la bɔle nu yɛle ye buluku ekyi ko mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Examining the Scriptures Daily (Neɛnlea Ngɛlɛlera Ne Anu Dahuu) la. Ɛnɛ, bɛyɛ buluku ekyi ɛhye wɔ aneɛ mɔɔ bo 300 la anu. Eza saa ɛbuke JW Library, Ɛzinzalɛ Arane YINTANƐTE ZO MBULUKUZIELELEKA ne (wol.jw.org) yɛɛ Watchtower Library ne ala a, ɛbanwu alehyenlɛ ne anu tɛkese ne wɔ ɛkɛ.
