Anyezɔlɛ maa yɛ nye die na yɛ kunlu dwo yɛ nwo
(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 1)
<<<P>>>
yɛ nye die nwo ala, emomu eza ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkpo-ndɛ ndenle yɛdua zo yɛkile nwolɛ anyezɔ-lɛ. Ɛhye ati maa yɛzuzu kpuya nsa ɛhye mɔ anwo: (1) Duzu ati a ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkile anyezɔlɛ a? (2) Duzu a baboa yɛ yeamaa yɛahile anyezɔ-lɛ a? (3) Ndenle boni mɔ a yɛbalua zo yɛahile anyezɔlɛ a?
<<<P>>>
DUZU ATI A ƆWƆ KƐ YƐKILE ANYEZƆLƐ A?
<<<P>>>
4 Saa yɛangile mɔɔ yɛlɛ la anwo anyezɔlɛ a ɔbamaa yɛali nyane. Menli mɔɔ bɛngile mɔɔ bɛlɛ la anwo anyezɔlɛ la anye ɛnlie. Baebolo ne ka kɛ: “Sonla mɔɔ ɛlɛdi nyane la mekɛ kɔ-sɔɔti le ɛtane.” (Mrɛ. 15:15) Ɔyɛ a yɛ muala yɛyia ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ kola maa yɛ sa nu to la. Tɛ dahuu a yɛbahola yɛali ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ yɛ-yia la anwo gyima a, noko subane mɔɔ yɛba-la ye ali wɔ nwo la gyi yɛ nwo zo. Saa yɛfa yɛ adwenle yɛsie ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ yɛyia ye la ala azo a ɔbamaa yɛali nyane kenle ko biala. Ɔnrɛmaa yɛnrɛnwu ninyɛne mgbalɛ mɔɔ ɛlɛ-kɔ zo wɔ yɛ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nu la na ɛhye bahola azɛkye yɛ anyelielɛ bɔkɔɔ.
<<<P>>>
4. Saa yɛangile anyezɔlɛ a ngyegyelɛ boni a yɛbahola yɛayia a?
<<<P>>>
5 Saa yɛkile anyezɔlɛ a ɔmaa yɛ nye die. Mrɛ-lɛbulɛ 15:15 foa mɔɔ li awieleɛ la ka kɛ: “So-nla mɔɔ lɛ anyelielɛ ahonle la wɔ ɛkponledolɛ nu dahuu.” Awie mɔɔ lɛ “anyelielɛ ahonle” la fa ye adwenle sie ninyɛne mgbalɛ mɔɔ ɔnyia la azo na ɔkile nwolɛ anyezɔlɛ. Ɛhye boa ye maa ɔye ye adwenle ɔfi ye ngyegyelɛ ne mɔ azo ngyikyingyikyi na yemɔ maa ɔ nye die kɛ asɛɛ ɔwɔ “ɛkponledolɛ nu dahuu” la.
<<<P>>>
6 Kenga Felepaema 4:6, 7. Saa yɛyɛ asɔne na yɛyɛ Gyihova mo wɔ mɔɔ yeyɛ yemaa yɛ la anwo a, ɔnrɛmaa adwenleadwenle ɛnrɛdɔ yɛ nwo. Duzu ati ɔ? Ɔluakɛ ɔbamaa yɛava yɛ adwenle yɛazie ninyɛne mgbalɛ mɔɔ Gyihova ɛyɛ ɛmaa yɛ la azo. Ɔnrɛmaa mɔɔ bazi kenle bie la anwo adwenleadwenle ɛnrɛdɔ yɛ nwo. Saa yɛdwenledwenle kɛzi Gyihova ɛboa yɛ la anwo a, ɔbamaa anwodozo mɔɔ yɛlɛ kɛ ɔbanlea yɛ kenle bie la ayɛ kpole. Ɛhye kile kɛ ɔbaboa yɛ amaa yɛandwenledwenle mɔɔ bado yɛ kenle bie la anwo. (Mat. 6:25-34) Eza
