ƐLƆLƐ MƆƆ YƐLƐ YƐMAA YƐ NWO LA YƐ NWANWANE
<<<P>>>
13 Gyisɛse hanle kɛ saa ye ɛdoavɔlema kulo bɛ nwo ngoko a yɛɛ menli banwu kɛ bɛli ɔ nzi a. (Kenga Dwɔn 13:35.) Alimoa Kilisienema luale ndenle ngakyile zo lale ɛlɔlɛ ɛhye bie ali. Kɛ neazo la, bɛ nee awie mɔ hyɛle ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛlɛ la. (Gyi. 2:44, 45) Bɛboale bɛdabɛ mɔɔ ɛnee bɛlɛyɛ bɛ kpɔdekpɔde la. (Gyi. 12:5) Bɛnleanle nrisa nee ahunlanvolɛ. (Gye. 1:27) Ɔnva nwo kɛ ɛnee bɛdabɛ mumua ne bɛdi ehyia la, bɛvale moalɛ bɛhɔmaanle bɛ mediema diedima gyɛne mɔɔ bɛwɔ ngyianlɛ nu la. (2 Kɔl. 8:1-4) Abɔlɔba bie mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Tertullian la hanle kɛ ɛnee menli ka alimoa Kilisienema anwo edwɛkɛ kɛ: “Bɛkulo bɛ nwo ngoko yɛɛ bɛlɛ ɛhulolɛ kɛ bɛbawu bɛamaa bɛ nwo.”
<<<P>>>
14 Ɛnɛ, Nyamenle menli kulo bɛ nwo ngoko kɛ mɔɔ alimoa Kilisienema ne yɛle la. Yɛka edwɛkɛ ne bie yɛkile bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛnle asafo ne anu la yɛfa yɛkile kɛ yɛkulo bɛ. Eza yɛye ndoboa yɛboa ɛzonlenlɛ gyima ne. Yɛnzɛlɛzɛlɛ ezukoa yɛɛ awie biala ɛntinlitinli ye nwo zo kɛ yɛye ndoboa anzɛɛ yɛdua taete. Bieko, ɛlɔlɛ ka yɛ maa yɛboa yɛ mediema wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛhyia moalɛ la.* Saa eye moalɛ ɛhye mɔ esie ahane a, eza yɛboa yɛ mediema yɛmaa bɛbikye Gyihova yɛɛ bɛlɛdi nyane a yɛkyekye bɛ rɛle. Saa yɛyɛ ɛhye a ɛnee yɛlɛdi Pɔɔlo folɛdulɛ ɛhye azo. Ɔse: “Bɛkyekye menli mɔɔ arɛle ɛbɔ la arɛle, bɛboa mɔɔ ɛyɛ bɛtɛɛ la, bɛnyia awie biala anwo abotane.” (1 Tɛs. 5:14) Gyihova ɛva yɛ ɛboka abusua kpole bie mɔɔ wɔ ewiade amuala na bɛda ɛlɔlɛ ali bɛkile bɛ nwo ngoko nee awie mɔ la anwo.
<<<P>>>
15 Ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛ yɛmaa yɛ nwo la yɛ nwanwane ɔluakɛ yɛde ewiade mɔɔ menli dɔɔnwo le angomedi la anu. Ɛzoanvolɛ Pɔɔlo hanle kɛ wɔ awielee mekɛ ɛhye anu menli ‘bahulo bɛ ngome bɛ nwo, bɛbahulo ezukoa yɛɛ bɛbahulo anyelielɛ.’ Bɛnrɛhulo Nyamenle yɛɛ bɛbayɛ “menli mɔɔ ɛnlɛ sonla nwo ɛlɔlɛ,” bɛnrɛhulo bɛdabɛ mumua ne bɛ abusua ne anu amra bɔbɔ. (2 Tim. 3:2-4) Gyisɛse hanle dole ɛkɛ ne kɛ menli baye bɛ nwo amaa na bɛakpɔ bɛ nwo. Ɔhanle ɔzɔle zolɛ bɔbɔ kɛ “menli dɔɔnwo ɛlɔlɛ baha aze.” (Mat. 24:10, 12) Saa ɛnea mɔɔ
