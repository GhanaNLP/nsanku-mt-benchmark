<<<P>>>
4 Yɛ edwɛkɛtile tɛkese ne maa yɛnwu subane mɔɔ Dwuuma ekpunli bie mɔɔ ɛnee de Masidonea suakpole Bɛlia azo la lale ye ali wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ Pɔɔlo hanle edwɛkpa ne hilele bɛ la. (Kenga Gyima ne 17:10, 11.) Ɛnee tɛ bɛ adwenle a le kɛ bɛkpondɛ nvonleɛ wɔ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ Pɔɔlo ɛlɛka la anu. Baebolo ne ka kɛ ‘ɛnee menli ɛhye mɔ kulo debiezukoalɛ na bɛvale bɛ adwenle amuala bɛliele edwɛkɛ ne’ mɔɔ ɛnee Pɔɔlo ɛlɛka la. Mekɛ mɔɔ bɛvale mɔɔ Pɔɔlo ɛlɛka la bɛtotole mɔɔ bɛgenga wɔ Ngɛlɛlera ne anu anwo la, bɛnwunle kɛ ɔlɛkilehile nɔhalɛ ne. Zɔhane ala a yɛkɔ debiezukoalɛ anzɛɛ nyianu na tendɛvolɛ ne ɛlɛkenga Ngɛlɛlera bie a yɛdaye noko yɛbuke yɛ Baebolo ɛdeɛ ne na yɛnea nu a. Ɛhye maa yɛnyia anwodozo kɛ mɔɔ tendɛvolɛ ne ɛlɛka la le nɔhalɛ.

5 Bɛliama ne anu “dɔɔnwo rayɛle diedima.” (Gyi. 17:12) Noko ɛnee ngyegyelɛ ɛlɛba. Dwuuma mɔɔ wɔ Tɛsalonaeka la rale Bɛlia “na bɛlile bɛwulale menli dɔɔnwo ne” bɛmaanle bɛvale ngilehilelɛ fofolɛ ɛhye anwo ɛya. (Gyi. 17:13) Noko Bɛliama mɔɔ bɔ zo mɔɔ bɛrayɛ diedima la ahonle andu. Ngɛlɛlera ne mɔɔ bɛnleɛnleanle nu dahuu la maanle bɛliele bɛlile bɔkɔɔ kɛ bɛnwu nɔhalɛ ne na yemɔ maanle bɛgyinlanle kpundii. Saa yɛdaye noko yɛneɛnlea Nyamenle Edwɛkɛ ne anu kenle ko biala a,

Kɛzi Yɛsonle Gyihova Kenle Ko Biala La Anwo Neazo
<<<P>>>
• Ɛnee ɔwɔ Mosisi Mɛla ne anu kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ ɛsɔfo ne mɔ bɔ afɔle kenle ko biala.—Ɛzɛ. 29:38-42; 30:7, 8.
<<<P>>>
• Gyihova hanle hilele Dwɔhyua kɛ ɔgenga Mɛla ne na ɔdwenledwenle nwolɛ “alehyenlɛ nee aledwolɛ.”—Dwɔh. 1:8, ɔbdw.
<<<P>>>
• Gyihova hanle hilele Yizilayɛ arelemgbunli ne mɔ kɛ bɛhɛlɛ Nyamenle Mɛla ne bie na bɛkenga ye kenle ko biala.—Dit. 17:18-20.
<<<P>>>
• Edwɛndolɛnli ne hanle kɛ yɛmaa yɛ nye ɛlie Nyamenle mɛla ne anwo “alehyenlɛ nee aledwolɛ.”—Edw. 1:1-3.
<<<P>>>
• Ɛnee Daneɛle yɛ asɔne fane nsa kenle ko biala.—Dan. 6:10.
<<<P>>>
• Yekile buluku ne ka ekpunli kpole ne mɔɔ bɛlɛsonle Nyamenle “alehyenlɛ nee aledwolɛ” la anwo edwɛkɛ.—Yek. 7:15.
