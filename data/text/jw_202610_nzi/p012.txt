BODANE
<<<P>>>
Nwanwane ninyɛne nsa bie ɛlɛsisi ɛnɛ, na edwɛkɛ ɛhye baboa yɛ yeamaa yɛahile nwolɛ adenle mɔɔ yɛnyia yɛlɛboa yɛamaa yeasisi la anwo anyezɔlɛ.
<<<P>>>
Maa Ɔyɛ Wɔ Fɛ Kɛ Ɛboka Nwo A Nwanwane Ninyɛne Ɛlɛsisi Ɛnɛ A
<<<P>>>
“[Ɛhye ati] menli kɔsɔɔti banwu kɛ bɛle me ɛdoavolɛma.”
—DWƆN 13:35.
<<<P>>>
MEKƐ mɔɔ Yigyibiti sogyama ne doa Yizilayɛma la, Gyihova yele adenle dole Nyevile Kɔkɔlɛ ne anu maanle bɛhɔle. Saa ɛnee ɛgyi ɛkɛ ne bie a, anrɛɛ nea kɛzi ɛ nye balie la. Yɛɛ saa ɛnwunle mekɛ mɔɔ Gyisɛse yɛle wuliravolɛma bie ayile na ɔdwazole menli bie mɔɔ bɛwu la a, anrɛɛ ɛ nye balie kpalɛ! Ɛhye mɔ le nwanwane ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛhɛlɛ wɔ Baebolo ne anu la anu ekyi bie ala. Awie biala mɔɔ ka nɔhalɛ mɔɔ nwunle ni-nyɛne ɛhye mɔ la balie ali bɔkɔɔ kɛ Nyamenle a maanle ɔsisi-le a. (Mat. 15:31) Noko edwɛkɛ zɔhane mɔ sisile la ɛhyɛ kpalɛ. Asoo nwanwane ninyɛne tɛsisi ɛnɛ ɔ? Ɛhee! Duzu ati a yɛkola yɛka ye zɔ ɛ?
<<<P>>>
2 Saa debie bie mɔɔ sonla ɛngola ye yɛ anzɛɛ ɔbo sonla nde-lebɛbo zo la si a, yɛbahola yɛaha kɛ ɔle nwanwane debie. Ɔle debie mɔɔ Gyihova a fa ye tumi ne yɛ a. Wɔ edwɛkɛ ɛhye anu, yɛbazuzu nwanwane ninyɛne nsa mɔɔ Gyihova ɛlɛmaa yeasisi wɔ ye ahyehyɛdeɛ ne anu ɛnɛ la anwo. Ɔyɛ nwanwane ɔluakɛ Gyihova ala a maa ɔkola ɔba ye zɔ a. Yɛlɛsuzu ko biala anwo a, yɛbanlea kɛzi yɛbahola yɛ nee Gyihova abɔ nu ayɛ gyima yɛa-maa yeara nu la.—Kenga 1 Kɔlentema 3:9.

YƐ NWO MƆƆ ƐLƐSO LA YƐ NWANWANE
<<<P>>>

<<<P>>>
