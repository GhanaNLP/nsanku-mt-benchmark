7 Saa yɛkile anyezɔlɛ a ɔbamaa yɛabikye Gyihova. Gyihova a ɛva ninyɛne mgbalɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛ la amuala ɛmaa yɛ a. (Kenga Gyemise 1:17.) Yemaa yɛ ahyɛlɛdeɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ bie a le ɛkpɔnedeɛ ne yɛɛ ngoanelielɛ nwo anyelazo ne. Duzu ati a yeva ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ amuala yemaa yɛ a? Kɛmɔ ɔkulo yɛ la ati ɔ, na tɛ kɛ asɛɛ yɛyɛ nwolɛ gyima bie la ati a ɔwɔ kɛ ɔfa ɔmaa yɛ a. (Wlo. 3:24; Ɛfɛ. 2:8) Saa yɛkile Gyihova ɛlɔlɛ kpundii ne anwo anyezɔlɛ a, ɔbamaa agɔnwolɛvalɛ mɔɔ yɛ nee yɛ Ze nee yɛ Gɔnwo kpalɛ Gyihova lɛ la anu amia.

DUZU A BABOA YƐ YEAMAA YƐAHILE ANYEZƆLƐ A?
<<<P>>>
8 Dwenle mɔɔ Gyihova ɛyɛ ɛmaa wɔ dɛba la anwo. Kɛ neazo la, asoo ɛhɔle tɛnlabelɛ bie mɔɔ anu yɛ se la anu wɔ mekɛ bie mɔɔ ɛze ɛhɔ la ɔ? Bie a wɔ busuanli anzɛɛ ɛ gɔnwo bie wule, ɛ nwo dole wɔ kpole bie anzɛɛ eyiale dwazotia. Na bie a nzinlii ɛbizale ɛ nwo kɛ, ‘Kɛ ɔyɛle mɔɔ mengolale mengyinlanle tɛnlabelɛ zɔhane anloa ɛ?’ Noko saa ɛdwenle mɔɔ zile la anwo ɛnɛ a, ɛbanwu kɛ tɛ ɛdawɔ wɔ anwosesebɛ a maanle ɛholale ɛgyinlanle ɔ nloa a. Emomu kɛmɔ Gyihova maanle wɔ anwosesebɛ kpole la ati ɔ. (Kenga 2 Kɔlentema 4:7.) Saa ɛdwenle kɛzi Gyihova boale wɔ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɛze la anwo a, ɔbamaa wɔahile mɔɔ yeyɛ yemaa wɔ la anwo anyezɔlɛ kpole. —Edw. 103:2.
<<<P>>>
9 Dwenle mɔɔ Gyihova ɛlɛyɛ amaa wɔ ɛnɛ la anwo. Baebolo ne maa yɛ anwosesebɛ kɛ: “Bɛyɛ mo wɔ debie biala anwo.” (1 Tɛs. 5:18) Nyia mekɛ kenle ko biala dwenle ninyɛne mgbalɛ mɔɔ Gyihova yɛle maanle wɔ wɔ kenle ne anu la anwo. Ɛbahola wɔayɛ ye nwonlomɔ nu, ɔbamaa ɛ nye alie alehyenlɛ ne anu amuala. Anzɛɛ bie a nɔsolɛ nu, ɔbamaa wɔala kpalɛ. Anzɛɛ mekɛ biala mɔɔ ɔbaboa wɔ la. (Edw. 92:1, 2) Kɛ mɔɔ John mɔɔ yɛlimoa yɛha ɔ nwo edwɛkɛ la yɛ ye la, ɛdawɔ noko ɛbahola wɔa-
