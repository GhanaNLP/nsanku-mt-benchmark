17 Duzu ati a ɔle kpalɛ kɛ yɛbaye mekɛ fɔɔnwo bie yɛazie ɛkɛ mɔɔ yɛbava yɛaye alehyenlɛ ne anu tɛkese ne a? Ɔluakɛ saa yɛanyɛ ye zɔ a, bie a yɛ rele bɛavi ye bɔkɔɔ. John mɔɔ ɛli ɛvolɛ 16 la hanle kɛ: “Saa wɔanva alehyenlɛ ne anu tɛkese ne mɔɔ ɛbayɛ la wɔamboka ninyɛne mɔɔ hyia wɔ la anwo a, wɔannea a akee ɛnrɛyɛ bɔbɔ.” Adiema raalɛ Sierra mɔɔ ɛli ɛvolɛ 25 la hanle kɛ: “Alehyenlɛ ne anu tɛkese ne a le debie mɔɔ medimoa meyɛ wɔ kenle ne anu kolaa na meayɛ gyima gyɛne biala a.” Yɛɛ adiema nrenyia Rogelio mɔɔ ɛli ɛvolɛ 21 la noko hanle kɛ: “Saa mesɛte alarm wɔ me fonu ne azo a, ɔkakye me dahuu.”
<<<P>>>
18 Fa alehyenlɛ ne anu tɛkese ne mɔɔ ɛbagenga la boka wɔ ngyehyɛleɛ nwo na bɔ mɔdenle di zo dahuu. Mmamaa ninyɛne twehwe wɔ adwenle. Adiema raalɛ Isabelle mɔɔ ɛnyia ɛvolɛ 19 la hanle kɛ: “Fa yɛ wɔ bodane kɛ ɛnrɛyɛ anzɛɛ ɛnrɛnlea debie biala wɔ wɔ fonu ne azo kɔkpula kɛ ɛbayɛ alehyenlɛ ne anu tɛkese ne wɔawie. Saa ɛlɛfa wɔ fonu ne wɔayɛ a ninyɛne twehwe wɔ adwenle a, ɛnee die mɔɔ bɛpelente la bie.” Adiema raalɛ Olivia mɔɔ ɛnyia ɛvolɛ 19 la hanle kɛ: “Saa ɛ nee wɔ abusua ne anzɛɛ ɛ gɔnwo mɔ bɔ nu yɛ alehyenlɛ ne anu tɛkese ne a ɔboa kpalɛ. Saa ɛnyia awie mɔɔ ɛ nee ye babɔ nu ayɛ anzɛɛ ɔbahakye wɔ a, ɔnrɛmaa ɛnrɛtoto ye nu fee.”

BOA Ɛ MRA MAA BƐYƐ ALEHYENLƐ NE ANU TƐKESE NE KENLE KO BIALA
<<<P>>>
19 Awovolɛ, saa bɛ nee bɛ mra ne mɔ bɔ nu yɛ alehyenlɛ ne anu tɛkese ne kenle ko biala a, subane ɛhye baha bɛ mra ne mɔ anwo. Saa ngakula ne mɔ ɛnde alehyenlɛ ne anu tɛkese ne bie abo bɔbɔ a, kɛmɔ bɛyɛ ye dahuu la ati ɔbaboa bɛ kenle bie. Kɛ neazo la, saa baka ekyi bahola anyi ayɛ kpole a kyesɛ ɔnyia nzule kenle ko biala. Zɔhane ala a saa ngakula nee Gyihova banyia agɔnwolɛvalɛ kpalɛ na bɛdiedi nu amia a, kyesɛ bɛfa mekɛ ekyii kenle ko biala bɛsuzu alehyenlɛ ne anu tɛkese ne anwo a.—Kenga Felepaema 1:9, 10.
<<<P>>>
20 Gyisɛse hanle kɛ: “Tɛ paano angome a maa sonla tɛnla ngoane nu a, emomu edwɛkɛ biala mɔɔ vi Gyihova anloa anu la.” (Mat. 4:4) Yɛdi aleɛ kenle ko biala. Zɔhane ala a ɔhyia kɛ yɛsukoa Nyamenle Edwɛkɛ ne kenle ko biala a, ɔluakɛ yɛ ngoane gyi zo!
