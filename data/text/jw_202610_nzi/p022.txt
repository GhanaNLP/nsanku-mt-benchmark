Nyamenle ɛngyakyi ɔ gɔnwo mɔ ɛlɛ
(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 18)
<<<P>>>
belemgbunli ne adwenle bɛmaanle ɔlile mɛla kɛ ɔnle kɛ awie biala sɛlɛ debie fi nyamenle bia-la ɛkɛ kyesɛ ɔdaye belemgbunli ne ala. Daneɛ-le anli mɛla zɔhane azo ɔti bɛvuandinle ye bɛ-dole awenade tunli nu. Mɔɔ ɔla tunli ne anu la, ɛnee ɔzɔho kɛ yeha ɔ ngome ɔluakɛ ɛnee ɔ gɔ-nwo mɔ biala ɛnle ɛkɛ mɔɔ ɔbaboa ye yea-maa yeagyinla zɔhane sɔnea ne anloa a. Noko asoo ɛnee amgba yeha ɔ ngome ɔ?—Dan. 6:4-16.
<<<P>>>
17 Daneɛle 6:19-23 ka nwanwane adenle mɔɔ Gyihova luale zo liele Daneɛle la anwo edwɛkɛ. (Kenga.) Mɔɔ Daneɛle la awenade tunli ne anu la, Gyihova Nyamenle, Daneɛle Agɔnwo kpalɛ mɔɔ tɛla biala la liele ye vile awenade ne mɔ anloa anu. Aleɛ hyenle mɔɔ be-lemgbunli ne hɔle tunli ne anloa la, Daneɛ-le hanle hilele ye kɛ: “Me Nyamenle ne zoanle ye anwumabɔvolɛ maanle ɔratotole awenade ne mɔ anloa anu, . . . ɔluakɛ me nwo ɛnlɛ edwɛ-kɛ wɔ ɔ nyunlu.” (Dan. 6:22) Diedi kpole mɔɔ ɛnee Daneɛle lɛ wɔ ɔ Gɔnwo Gyihova anu la maanle ɔnyianle akɛnrasesebɛ.

18. Duzu ati a ɔhyia kɛ yɛfa Gyihova agɔnwɔlɛ a, na kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na yɛahola yɛayɛ zɔ ɛ? (Eza nea nvoninli ne nee ɛlɛ-ka “Gyihova Le Yɛ Ze, Yɛ Nyamenle Nee Yɛ Gɔnwo.”)
<<<P>>>
suafoɔ avinli bahola atete. Bie a bɛmaa wɔ gyi-malilɛ bie wɔ moa, wɔdu wɔvi ɛleka mɔɔ ɛde la arɛlevilɛ nu, anwodolɛ anzɛɛ ɛrelerayelɛ ɛmaa wɔ anwosesebɛ ɛwie anzɛɛ bɛdo wɔ efiade. Tɛ-nlabelɛ ɛhye mɔ bahola amaa ɛ nwo ayɛ wɔ koonwu anzɛɛ wɔade nganeɛ kɛ yeha ɛ ngome. Saa ɔba ye zɛhae a, ɔhyia kɛ ɛkakye kɛ Nyame-nle ɛngyakyi ɔ gɔnwo mɔ ɛlɛ, dahuu ɔwɔ bɛ nwo ɛkɛ. Gyihova mumua ne bɔ bɛ nwo bane na ɔnea bɛ ɔnva nwo ɛleka mɔɔ bɛwɔ la. (Edw. 139:7-10) Nyia anwodozo kɛ Gyihova nwu wɔ na ɔbaboa wɔ.

AGƆNWƆLƐMA MƆƆ BƐ NEE WƆ BADU DAHUU
<<<P>>>
19 Ɔhyia kpalɛ kɛ yɛbanyia agɔnwɔlɛma kpalɛ ɛnɛ. Duzu ati ɔ? Ɔluakɛ agɔnwɔlɛma kpalɛ boa yɛ maa yɛsisi kpɔkɛ kpalɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ “Gyi-hova kenle” ne ɛlɛbikye la. Bɛmaa yɛ anwose-sebɛ na bɛboa yɛ. (Kenga 1 Tɛsalonaekama 5:2, 11.) Kɛmɔ ɛzoanvolɛ Pita nwunle kɛ “ni-nyɛne kɔsɔɔti awieleɛ ɛbikye” la ati, ɔhanle kɛ: “Mɔɔ hyia kpalɛ la, bɛva ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ anu yɛ se la bɛhulo bɛ nwo ngoko.”—1 Pita 4:7, 8.
