3 Kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na agɔnwolɛma kpalɛ aboa kpavo-lɛ anzɛɛ bɛlɛra bie yeamaa yeali nɔhalɛ yeamaa Gyihova ɛ? Kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na awovolɛ nee mediema gyɛne mɔɔ bɛkulo Gyihova la ayɛ agɔnwolɛma kpalɛ amaa bɛ ɛ? Maa yɛzuzu ngapezonli Da-neɛle neazo ne anwo na yɛnlea kɛzi ɔlile ngye-gyelɛ mɔɔ ɛnee ɔzɔho kɛ ɔnrɛhola ɔ nloa gyinla la anwo gyima la. Eza yɛbanwu kɛzi Daneɛle Agɔnwo kpalɛ Gyihova boale ye wɔ mekɛ bɔbɔ mɔɔ ɛnee yeha ɔ ngome la.

4 Bɛwole Daneɛle wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɛnee Nyame-nle menli ne wɔ anwongyelelɛ kpole bie anu la. Ɛnee Dwuda belemgbunlililɛ ne nee Gyiho-va avinli ɛzɛkye ɔluakɛ menli ne anli nɔhalɛ ammaa ye. Ɛhye ati wɔ ɛvolɛ 620 K.Y.M. anu, Gyihova maanle Babelɔn Belemgbunli Nɛbu-kadɛnɛza lile bɛ nwo zo konim. (Dan. 1:2) Mekɛ zɔhane ɔbayɛ kɛ ɛnee Daneɛle le kakula kpavolɛ. Ɛvolɛ ekyii guale nu la, bɛvale ye bɛ-vile ye sua zo na bɛ nee ye hɔle Babelɔn kɛ bɛ-katete ye bɛamaa yeazonle belemgbunli ne. (Dan. 1:3-6) Ɔti bɛ nee kakula Daneɛle hɔle moamoa na ɛnee ɔmbikye ye abusua ne fee.

5 Duzu a boale Daneɛle maanle ɔholale ɔgyi-nlanle ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ ɔyiale wɔ Babelɔn la anloa a? Baebolo ne ka kɛ “Daneɛle zile pi wɔ ye ahonle nu” kɛ ɔbali Nyamenle mɛla ne mɔ azo. (Dan. 1:8) Awie a hilehilele Daneɛle mɛla zɔhane mɔ na ɔboale ye ɔmaanle ɔzile kpɔkɛ kɛ ɔbazonle Gyihova wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɔle kakula bɔbɔ la a. Ye awovolɛ a boale ye ɔ? Anyelielɛ edwɛkɛ ne a le kɛ Daneɛle duma mumua ne abo kile “Nyamenle Le Me Ndɛnebuavolɛ.” Ɔti duma mɔɔ Daneɛle awovolɛ vale donle ye la maa yɛnwu kɛ ɛnee bɛ nye la kɛ ɔbazonle Gyi-hova na yeali Ye adehilelɛ zo.—Dit. 6:6, 7.

6 Eza ɔbayɛ kɛ gyima mɔɔ ngapezonli Gyɛ-lɛmaya yɛle la maanle Daneɛle anwosesebɛ. Duzu ati a yɛkola yɛka ye zɔ a? Wɔmɔ maa yɛ-nlea debie: Gyihova kpale Gyɛlɛmaya kɛ nga-pezonli wɔ ɛvolɛ 647 K.Y.M., ɔti ɔbɔle ye gyi-ma ne abo kolaa na bɛawo Daneɛle. (Gyɛ. 1:2) Menli mɔɔ ɛnee Gyihova ta soa Gyɛlɛmaya wɔ bɛ ɛkɛ ne la a le alehelɛ abusua ne yɛɛ menli ti-tili gyɛne mɔɔ wɔ Dwuda la, na ɔbayɛ kɛ ɛnee Daneɛle abusua ne boka nwo. (Gyɛ. 1:17, 18; Dan. 1:3, 6) Yɛnrɛhola yɛnrɛzi ye pi kɛ ɛnee Gyɛlɛmaya nee Daneɛle le agɔnwolɛma ɛdeɛ, noko bie a mɔɔ Daneɛle le kakula kpavolɛ la, ɛnee ɔze Gyɛlɛmaya ngapezo ne mɔ kpalɛ. Mɔɔ ɔyɛle ɛrelera bɔbɔ la, ɛnee ɔkenga Gyɛlɛmaya ngapezo ne mɔ. (Gyɛ. 25:11; 29:10; Dan. 9:1, 2) Bie a Daneɛle nwunle Gyɛlɛmaya kɛ ɔle awie mɔɔ ɔle akɛnrasesebɛ na yebɔ kpɔkɛ kɛ ɔbahɔ zo yeali nɔhalɛ ɔnva nwo kɛ menli mɔɔ bɛnzonle Gyihova la ɛbɔ ye eyia la.
