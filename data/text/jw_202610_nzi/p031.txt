bɛka la bahola aha kɛzi yete nganeɛ la, noko ɔnga nɔhalɛ ne mɔɔ wɔ Baebolo ne anu la. Ɔti saa yɛdie yɛdi kɛ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ wɔ Baebolo ne anu la le nɔha-lɛ yɛɛ Gyihova ahyehyɛdeɛ ne kilehile yɛ nɔhalɛ ne a, yɛnrɛmaa awie mɔ subane ɛnrɛdenda yɛ gyakɛ anu.
<<<P>>>
Nɔhalɛ nu, ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ yɛ mediema da ye ali la maa yɛ anwosesebɛ. Yɛɛ bɛda subane kpalɛ ali a ɔmaa yɛ nye die. Noko saa yɛ mediema bie mɔ nyɛleɛ ti yɛlɛdi nyane a, anwodozo mɔɔ yɛlɛ kɛ mɔɔ yɛzukoa wɔ Baebolo ne anu la le nɔhalɛ la kola maa yɛ diedi si pi. Ɔbaboa yɛ yeamaa yɛanwu kɛ yɛwɔ nɔhalɛ ɛzonlenlɛ nu ɔnva nwo mediema ekyi bie nyɛleɛ mɔɔ ɛnle kpalɛ la.
<<<P>>>
Saa yɛyɛ ngyehyɛleɛ kpalɛ yɛfa yɛsukoa Baebo-lo ne dahuu a, ɔbamaa anwodozo mɔɔ yɛlɛ kɛ mɔɔ yɛzukoa wɔ Nyamenle Edwɛkɛ ne anu la le nɔhalɛ la ayɛ kpole. Akee anwodozo zɔhane ba-boa yɛ yeamaa yɛalie yɛali dahuu kɛ yɛnwu adenle kpalɛ mɔɔ bɛdua zo bɛsonle Nyamenle la. Na ɔba-maa yɛ diedi ne agyinla adiabo kpundii zo na awie mɔ nyɛleɛ anzɛɛ mɔɔ bɛka la ɛnrɛmaa ɔnrɛkpusu.—Edw. 16:8; nea ɛlɛka “Kɔ Zo Dua Adenle Kpalɛ Ne Azo.”

Kɔ Zo Dua Adenle Kpalɛ Ne Azo
<<<P>>>
Fa ye kɛ wɔvo kale mɔɔ ɔlɛkɔ ɛleka kɛnlɛma bie mɔɔ wɔ moa la. Saa kale ne ɛlɛkɔ a, ɛ nye balie kpalɛ ɔluakɛ ɛdawɔ noko ɛlɛkɔ bie.
<<<P>>>
Noko saa bɛlua adenle nu a, ninyɛne bie mɔ ba-hola aha wɔ ɛya. Bie a adendulɛma ne anu nwiɔ ɛlɛbɔ adawu mɔɔ bɛ ne wɔ anwuma anzɛɛ bie a delava ne anla ɛbulɛ ali angile wɔ. Noko ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ biala ɛngile kɛ tɛ kale mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ ɛfo la a wɔvo la ɔti ɔwɔ kɛ ɛdwu adenle nu.
<<<P>>>
Zɔhane ala a yɛ muala yɛlɛtu adenle yɛahɔ Nya-menle ewiade fofolɛ ne mɔɔ yeva yebɔ yɛ ɛwɔ-kɛ la anu a, na yɛ nye die kɛ yɛboka ye ahyehyɛdeɛ ne mɔɔ ɛlɛkile yɛ adenle la anwo. Noko ɔdwu mekɛ ne bie a yɛ diema diedinli bie bayɛ debie mɔɔ gyegye yɛ anzɛɛ adiema bie mɔɔ yɛbu ye la bayɛ ɛtane kpole bie mɔɔ yɛ azi-bɛnwo la. Noko yemɔ ɛngile kɛ yɛnle ahyehyɛdeɛ kpalɛ nu. Ahyehyɛdeɛ mɔɔ ɔbamaa yɛadwu Nya-menle ewiade fofolɛ ne anu la a yɛwɔ nu la. Saa yɛdie yɛdi kɛ mɔɔ yɛzukoa wɔ Baebolo ne anu la le nɔhalɛ na Nyamenle ahyehyɛdeɛ ne a hilehile-le yɛ nɔhalɛ ɛhye mɔ a, ɔbamaa yɛahɔ zo yɛalua adenle kpalɛ ɛhye ala azo. Na yɛ diedi ne bazi pi dahuu.
