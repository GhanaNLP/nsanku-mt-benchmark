Maa Wɔ Diedi Ɛgyinla Adiabo Mɔɔ Gyi Kpundii La Azo
<<<P>>>
MEKƐ mɔɔ ɛ nee Gyihova Alasevolɛ ɛlɛsukoa Bae-bolo ne la, duzu a maanle ɛliele ɛlile kɛ wɔnwu “diedi ko” ne anzɛɛ adenle kpalɛ mɔɔ bɛdua zo bɛsonle Nyamenle la ɛ? (Ɛfɛ. 4:4-6) Asoo ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ wɔ Gyihova menli avinli la a maanle ɛrale nɔ-halɛ ne anu ɔ? Asoo subane kpalɛ mɔɔ bɛda ye ali la a maanle ɛ nye liele bɛ nwo ɔ? Debie mɔɔ maa-nle menli dɔɔnwo rale nɔhalɛ ne anu la a le kɛzi yɛmaa bɛ nwo tɔ bɛ na yɛkulo bɛ nɔhalɛ nu la, na ndelebɛbo wɔ nu. Ɔluakɛ ɛlɔlɛ nee subane kpalɛ a le ninyɛne mɔɔ maa bɛnwu nɔhalɛ Kilisienema a.—Dwɔn 13:35; 1 Pita 2:12.
<<<P>>>
Na saa adiema diedinli bie yɛ debie maa ɔgye-gye wɔ anzɛɛ ɔyɛ wɔ debie mɔɔ ɔngile ɛlɔlɛ la noko ɛ? Anzɛɛ saa adiema bie yɛ ɛtane kpole bie, ɛtane mɔɔ yɛ ɛzulolɛ kɛ mɔɔ nrenyia ne mɔɔ ɛnee wɔ Kɔ-lente asafo ne anu mɔɔ Pɔɔlo hanle ɔ nwo edwɛ-kɛ la yɛle la ɛ? (1 Kɔl. 5:1) Saa yɛ diedi gyi awie mɔ nyɛleɛ nee mɔɔ bɛka la azo a, ɛnee ɔngyi adia-bo kpalɛ zo, nuhua ɛnrɛyɛ se. Asoo adiabo bie mɔɔ gyi kpundii mɔɔ bamaa wɔ diedi nu ayɛ se la wɔ ɛkɛ ɔ?

ADIABO MƆƆ GYI KPUNDII
<<<P>>>
Saa ɛkɔ zo ɛmia anwodozo mɔɔ ɛlɛ kɛ, mɔɔ wɔ-zukoa wɔ Baebolo ne anu la le nɔhalɛ la anu a, ɔbamaa wɔ diedi ne agyinla adiabo mɔɔ gyi kpu-ndii la azo. Kɛzi ɛhye baboa wɔ ɛ? Maa yɛnlea nea-zo bie.
<<<P>>>
Kenle ko bie Gyisɛse ɛdoavolɛma ne anu dɔɔ-nwo gyakyile kɛ bɛbali ɔ nzi. Boni a zile a? Edwɛkɛ bie mɔɔ Gyisɛse hanle la a dendanle bɛ gyakɛ anu a; ɔse ɔwɔ kɛ bɛdi ye nwonane na bɛno ye mogya ne. Bɛbuale ye kɛ: “Edwɛkɛ ɛhye anloa yɛ se.” Edwɛkɛ zɔhane mɔɔ Gyisɛse hanle la gyegyele bɛ too ɔmaanle bɛzile kpɔkɛ kɛ bɛnrɛdoa ye bieko.—Dwɔn 6:56-60, 66.
<<<P>>>
Nzinlii Gyisɛse bizale ye ɛzoanvolɛma ne kɛ: “Na bɛdabɛ noko ɛ, bɛkpondɛ kɛ bɛkɔ ɔ?” Pita buale ye kɛ: “Awulae, nwane ɛkɛ a yɛkɔ a? Wɔmɔ a ɛlɛ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ maa dahuu ngoane a. Yɛlie yɛli yɛɛ yɛnwu kɛ wɔmɔ a ɛle Nwuanzanwuanzanli ne mɔɔ vi Nyamenle ɛkɛ a.”—Dwɔn 6:67-69.
<<<P>>>
Duzu a maanle Pita nee ɛzoanvolɛma mɔɔ ɛha la yɛle ngakyile vile bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛhɔle la anwo a? Ɛnee bɛ diedi ne gyi debie mɔɔ gyi ye kpundii la azo, ɔngyi kɛzi bɛte nganeɛ la azo. Pita hanle kɛ: “Yɛlie yɛli yɛɛ yɛnwu kɛ wɔmɔ a ɛle Nwuanza-nwuanzanli ne mɔɔ vi Nyamenle ɛkɛ a.” Ɔti ɛnee nɔhavoma ɛhye mɔ ze debie mɔɔ bɛlie bɛli nee deɛmɔti bɛliele bɛlile la. Kɛmɔ bɛvale Gyisɛse edwɛkɛ nee ye nyɛleɛ bɛtotole mɔɔ bɛze wɔ Ngɛ-lɛlera ne anu la anwo la ati, bɛliele bɛlile bɔkɔɔ kɛ Gyisɛse a le Mɛzaya ne a. Anwodozo kpole zɔhane maanle bɛgyinlanle kpundii wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ Gyisɛse edwɛkɛ ne dendanle bie mɔ agyakɛ anu la.
<<<P>>>
Duzu a yɛsukoa yɛfi edwɛkɛ zɔhane anu a? Mɔɔ yɛze la kola nyia yɛ nwo zo tumi kpole kpalɛ tɛla kɛzi yɛte nganeɛ la. Duzu ati a yɛkola yɛka ye zɔ a? Baebolo nu ngilehilelɛ mɔɔ bie a le Nyamenle duma ne, bodane mɔɔ ɔlɛ ɔmaa alesama yɛɛ ɛkpɔ-nedeɛ ne la ɛngakyi. Awie mɔ nyɛleɛ anzɛɛ mɔɔ
