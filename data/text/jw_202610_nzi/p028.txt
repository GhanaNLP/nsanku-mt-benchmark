Duzu a baboa yɛ amaa ninyɛne andwehwe yɛ adwenle wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛyɛ alehyenlɛ ne anu tɛkese ne la ɛ?
(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 18)
<<<P>>>
wɔazɛlɛ Gyihova kɛ ɔboa wɔ ɔmaa ɛnyia mɔɔ ɛba wɔagenga la azo nvasoɛ. (Gye. 1:5) Akee kenga Ngɛlɛlera ne mɔɔ edwɛkɛ ne gyi zo la nee ngilenu ne. Ewie a, biza ɛ nwo kɛ: ‘Kɛzi tɛkese ɛhye bahola aboa me yeamaa me diedi nu amia ɛ? Kɛzi ɔbahola yeaboa me yeamaa meabikye Gyihova ɛ? Saa meyia ngyegyelɛ bie wɔ alehyenlɛ ne anu a kɛzi mebava meali gyima ɛ?’ Saa ɛ nee wɔ mbusuafoɔ anzɛɛ ɛ gɔnwo mɔ bɔ nu yɛ alehyenlɛ ne anu tɛkese ne a, anrɛɛ bɛ nuhua ko biala ɛha debie mɔɔ ɔnwunle ye la bɛva bɛmaa bɛ nwo anwosesebɛ. (Wlo. 1:12) Mediema bie mɔ ɛnwu ye kɛ saa bɛkye edwɛkɛ bie mɔɔ bɛ nye liele nwo la bɛgua bɛ ti anu a, ɔmaa bɛkakye ye wɔ alehyenlɛ ne anu amuala.

14 Kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na wɔanyia alehyenlɛ ne anu tɛkese ne azo nvasoɛ ɛ? Bɔ mɔdenle kɛ yeanyɛ boɛ fee la ɛbava nzuzulɛ ɛhye mɔ anu ko wɔali gyima: (1) Nea kɛ ɛbanwu ɛzukoalɛdeɛ titili mɔɔ wɔ ngilenu ne anu la na dwenledwenle nwolɛ. (2) Kenga Baebolo tɛkese ngakyile mɔɔ wɔ ngilenu ne anu la. (3) Kenga tile ne mɔɔ bɛyele alehyenlɛ ne anu tɛkese ne bɛvile nu la amuala. (4) Kpondɛ nwolɛ edwɛkɛ gyɛne wɔ Gyihova Alasevolɛ Neɛnleanu Buluku ne anu. (5) Kpondɛ dɔɔnwo wɔ Ezinzalɛ Arane ne mɔɔ bɛyele edwɛkɛ ne bɛvile nu la anu. Saa ɛlɛfa fonu anzɛɛ tabelɛte a wɔali gyima a, ɛbahola wɔanwu Ezinzalɛ Arane ne mɔɔ bɛyele edwɛkɛ ne bɛvile nu la wɔ ngilenu ne abo nehane.*
<<<P>>>
15 Adiema raalɛ Mackenzie mɔɔ ɛli ɛvolɛ 19 la hanle kɛzi ɔyɛ ye ɛdeɛ ne la anwo edwɛkɛ kɛ: “Saa mekenga alehyenlɛ ne anu tɛkese ne mewie a eza mefa mekɛ ekyii, bie a mitini kɛyɛ nnu metɔ alagye mekenga ye bieko na medwenledwenle nwolɛ. Saa meyɛ zɔ a, ɔmaa mekakye nuhua edwɛkɛ ne amuala.”

16 Mekɛ boni a ɔwɔ kɛ ɛyɛ alehyenlɛ ne anu tɛkese ne a? Kpa mekɛ mɔɔ baboa wɔ nee wɔ abusua ne la. Kɛ neazo la, bie mɔ yɛ kolaa na bɛali aleɛ nwonlomɔ anzɛɛ bɛdi bɛwie a. Yɛɛ bie mɔ yɛ kolaa na bɛava bɛ mra ne mɔ bɛahɔ sukulu. Bie mɔ fa Neɛnlea Ngɛlɛlera Ne Anu Dahuu ne bie to bɛ ɛkponle ne azo amaa bɛahakye na bɛayɛ ye dahuu. Edwɛkɛ mɔɔ yɛ nye ɛnla la kola si ɔti ɔdwu mekɛ ne bie a ɔbahyia kɛ ɛkakyi mekɛ mɔɔ ɛfa ɛyɛ la anu.
