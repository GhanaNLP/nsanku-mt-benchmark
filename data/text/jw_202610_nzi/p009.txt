10 Mɔɔ limoa, dwenle ninyɛne mɔɔ Gyihova ɛyɛ ɛmaa wɔ wɔ kenle ne anu la anwo. (Edw. 40:5) Ɛbahola wɔayɛ ye mo kɛ ɔdiele asɔne biala mɔɔ ɛyɛle, ɔmaanle ngɛlɛlera bie mɔɔ ɛgengale la kyekyele ɛ rɛle yɛɛ ɔluale edwɛkɛ anzɛɛ vidio bie mɔɔ wɔ jw.org azo la ɔboale wɔ wɔ kenle ne anu la. (Edw. 65:2; Mat. 24:45; Wlo. 15:4) Eza ɛbahola wɔadwenle ninyɛne ngɛnlɛma mɔɔ Gyihova ɛva ɛmaa yɛ amaa yɛ nye alie la anwo na wɔayɛ ye mo wɔ nwolɛ. Ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ bie a le kɛzi ewia ne ɛlɛkɔ ahɔdɔ a ɔmaa maanle nu yɛ kɛnlɛma nee aleɛ fɛlɛko yɛɛ agɔnwolɛma kpalɛ mɔɔ bɛkulo wɔ edwɛkɛ la. Na kɛmɔ Nyamenle ɛmaa yɛ “ngoane nee ɛnwomenle” la ati a yɛnwu ninyɛne ngɛnlɛma ɛhye mɔ na yɛ nye die nwo a. (Gyi. 17:25) Gyihova nyilalɛ boni bieko a ɛbahɛlɛ ye ɛne a?—Ɛfɛ. 5:20.

11 Eza kɛmɔ ɛ nee mediema mɔɔ wɔ ewiade amuala la bɔ nu sonle Gyihova la ati, ɔmaa ɛnyia nyilalɛ bieko. (Edw. 133:1; Mrɛ. 18:24) Asoo adiema nrenyia anzɛɛ raalɛ bie ɛyɛ wɔ nyele anzɛɛ ɛha edwɛkɛ bie ɛmaa wɔ anwosesebɛ ɔ? (Mrɛ. 12:25; 16:24) Ɛbahola wɔahile ninyɛne ngyikyi zɛhae mɔ bɔbɔ anwo anyezɔlɛ. Maa yɛnlea neazo bie. Mɔɔ adiema raalɛ bie ara raalɛ wule la, mɔlebɛbo ne ɛnee ɔyɛ se kɛ ɔkɔ debiezukoalɛ a ɔbado edwɛne mɔɔ bɛlɛto la bie. Noko ɔhanle kɛ: “Kenle ko bie, adiema raalɛ bie nwunle kɛ melɛsu. Ɔrale me nwo ɛkɛ na ɔvale ɔ sa ɔdole me kɔme na ɔ nee me bɔle nu dole edwɛne ne mɔɔ ɛha la. Ɛhye maanle mengulole mediema ne mɔ kpalɛ.” Debie ekyi zɛhae mɔɔ adiema raalɛ ne yɛle la maanle ɛrɛladane ne mɔɔ ɔlɛdi nyane la anye liele kpole.

12 Saa yɛdwenle mɔɔ Gyihova ɛyɛ ɛmaa yɛ dɛba nee mɔɔ ɔlɛyɛ yeamaa yɛ ɛnɛ la anwo a, ɔmaa yɛnyia ɛhulolɛ kɛ yɛbahile nwolɛ anyezɔlɛ. 13 Duzu a yɛbahola yɛayɛ yɛava yɛahile anyezɔlɛ a? Ngɛlɛlera mɔɔ edwɛkɛ ɛhye gyi zo la se ‘yɛhile anyezɔlɛ.’ Mɔɔ ɛzoanvolɛ Pɔɔlo dule yɛ folɛ ɛhye wiele la, eza ɔhanle kɛ: “Wɔ bɛ edwɛkɛ nee bɛ nyɛleɛ biala anu, bɛyɛ debie biala wɔ Awulae Gyisɛse duma nu na bɛlua ɔ nwo zo bɛyɛ Nyamenle Selɛ ne mo.” (Kɔl. 3:15, 17) Əti yɛkola yɛdua yɛ edwɛkɛ nee yɛ nyɛleɛ zo yɛkile anyezɔlɛ. Wɔ ɛke ɛnee Pɔɔlo ɛlɛka Gyihova mɔɔ yɛyɛ ye mo la anwo edwɛkɛ ɛdeɛ, noko saa yɛkile mɔɔ awie mɔ yɛ maa yɛ la anwo anyezɔlɛ a, yɛ Nyamenle ne anye die. Maa yɛnlea kɛzi yɛbahola yɛalua yɛ edwɛkɛ nee yɛ nyɛleɛ zo yɛahile anyezɔlɛ la bie mɔ.
