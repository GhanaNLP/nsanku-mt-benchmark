14 Daneɛle hɔle belemgbunli ne ɛkɛ na ɔvile ɛbulɛ nu ɔzɛlɛle ye kɛ ɔmaa ye mekɛ ekyii ɔmaa ɔkpondɛ mɔɔ ye ɛlalɛ ne abo kile la. Akee Daneɛle hɔle ɔ gɔnwo mɔ ɛkɛ. Kɛlɛtokɛ ne hanle kɛ: “Ɔzele bɛ kɛ bɛzɛlɛ anwuma Nyamenle ne ɛkɛ ne anwunvɔnezelɛ wɔ fealeranu edwɛkɛ ɛhye anwo, amaa bɛangu Daneɛle nee ɔ gɔnwo mɔ.” (Dan. 2:18) Gyihova diele bɛ nzɛlɛlɛ ne na ɔmaanle Daneɛle nwunle ɛlalɛ ne nee mɔɔ ɔ bo kile la. Ɛhye ati bɛangu Daneɛle nee ɔ gɔnwo mɔ, emomu belemgbunli ne maanle bɛ dibilɛ titili wɔ Babelɔn.—Dan. 2:19, 47-49.

15 Yɛbahola yɛazukoa ninyɛne dɔɔnwo mɔɔ anwo hyia la yɛavi kɛlɛtokɛ ɛhye anu. Mɔɔ limoa, ɔngyia kɛ ɛ ngomekye ɛgyinla sɔnea mɔɔ ɛleyia la anloa. Mmamaa anwomemaazo anzɛɛ nyiane si wɔ adenle kɛ ɛbakpondɛ moalɛ wɔavi awie mɔ ɛkɛ. Mɔɔ tɔ zo nwiɔ, ka kile awie mɔ kɛ bɛyɛ asɔne bɛmaa wɔ wɔ tɛnlabelɛ bie anwo. (2 Kɔl. 1:11) Kakye kɛ tumi kpole wɔ menli dɔɔnwo asɔneyɛlɛ nu. “Asɔneyɛlɛ Tievolɛ” ne bahola aboa yɛ yeamaa yɛali ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ ɔzɔho kɛ yɛnrɛhola ɔ nloa gyinla la azo konim. (Edw. 65:2; Ɛfɛ. 3:20) Saa yɛlɛ agɔnwolɛma mɔɔ bɛdabɛ noko bɛsonle Gyihova a, yɛbahola yɛanyia anwodozo kɛ bɛbamaa yɛ anwosesebɛ nee moalɛ mɔɔ yɛhyia amaa yɛahola yɛagyinla yɛ sɔnea ne mɔ anloa kpundii la.—Kenga Galeehyeama 6:2.

16 Gyihova le Agɔnwolɛ kpalɛ mɔɔ ɔbahola yeaboa yɛ wɔ tɛnlabelɛ biala anu, saa bɔbɔ yeha yɛ ngome a. Kɛ neazo la, mɔɔ Daneɛle kayɛ ɛrelera la—bie a mɔɔ enee yebo ɛvolɛ 90 la—enee Mideama nee Pɛhyeama ɛli Babelɔn anwo zo konim ɔti enee Belemgbunli Dalayɛse a ɛlɛdi tumi a. Mgbanyima mɔɔ yɛ gyima wɔ belemgbunli sua nu ɛkɛ la bie mɔ anye bolole Daneɛle ɔti bɛbɔle adwenle kɛ bɛku ye. Bɛlile
