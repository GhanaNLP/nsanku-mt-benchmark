8 Mekɛ biala mɔɔ yɛbayɛ ɛzonlenlɛ gyima ne afoa bie la ɛnee yɛ nee Gyihova nee Gyisɛse ɛlɛbɔ nu ayɛ gyima amaa yɛ nwo azo. Bɛgyi yɛ nzi yɛɛ Gyisɛse noko ɛbɔ yɛ ɛwɔkɛ kɛ: “Me nee bɛ lua dahuu kɔkpula mekɛ ɛhye awieleɛ.” (Mat. 28:20) Eza anwumabɔvolɛ ne mɔ boa yɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛdi daselɛ la.—Yek. 14:6, 7.

KOYƐLƐ MƆƆ WƆ YƐ AVINLI LA YƐ NWANWANE
<<<P>>>
9 Ngapezonli Maeka hanle nwanwane debie bieko mɔɔ ɛlɛsi ɛnɛ la anwo edwɛkɛ dole ɛkɛ. Yemɔ a le koyɛlɛ mɔɔ wɔ Nyamenle menli avinli la. Wɔ Maeka ngapezo ne anu, ɔhanle mrenyia nee mraalɛ mɔɔ Gyihova baboɔboa bɛ nloa na bɛadɛnla koyɛlɛ nu la anwo edwɛkɛ. (Kenga Maeka 2:12.) Ngapezo zɔhane ɛra nu bɔkɔɔ ɔluakɛ ɛnɛ Nyamenle menli de koyɛlɛ nu ɔnva nwo kɛ asolo bɛ subane anzɛɛ kɛzi bɛtetele bɛ la. Bɛ nee edwɛndolɛnli Devidi mɔɔ hanle edwɛkɛ ɛhye la yɛ adwenle bɔkɔɔ. Ɔse: “Nea! Kɛzi ɔle kpalɛ nee kɛnlɛma kɛ mediemabɔ nu adɛnla wɔ koyɛlɛ nu a!”—Edw. 133:1.
<<<P>>>
10 Ɛnɛ, yɛlɛ daselɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ kile kɛ yɛde koyɛlɛ nu la. Kɛ neazo la, ɔnva nwo ɛleka biala mɔɔ yɛwɔ la saa yɛkɔ debiezukoalɛ anzɛɛ nyianu ngakyile a, debie ko ne ala a yɛsukoa a. Mediema mɔɔ asolo bɛ amaamuo nee maanle mɔɔ bɛvi nu la bɔ nu yɛ gyima wɔ Bɛtɛle na bɛsisi azua wɔ ewiade amuala. Yɛbikye yɛ nwo kpalɛ. Yɛmmaa amaamuo, ezukoa mɔɔ yɛlɛ anzɛɛ maanyɛlɛ ɛmkpakyekpakye yɛ nu. Wɔmɔ nea o, ɛleka biala mɔɔ yɛbahɔ la, yɛ mediema mrenyia nee mraalɛ mɔɔ bɛkulo yɛ edwɛkɛ la lɛ ɛhulolɛ kɛ bɛbava mɔɔ yɛhyia nwo la bɛamaa yɛ yɛɛ bɛmaa yɛ anwosesebɛ bɛmaa yɛkɔ zo yɛsonle Gyihova. Ɔvi tete too badwu ɛnɛ, bɛtɛnwunle menli dɔɔnwo mɔɔ bɛvi maanle nee amaamuo ngakyile nu mɔɔ bɛlɛ adwenle ko na bɛyɛ bɛ ninyɛne wɔ adenle ko azo zɛhae ɛlɛ.
<<<P>>>
11 Yɛ koyɛlɛ ne yɛ nwanwane ɔluakɛ ɔle ngakyile ɔfi ewiade ye anu menli mɔɔ bɛ nu ɛkpakyekpakye la anwo. Menli nu ɛkpakyekpakye wɔ ewiade amuala. Kɛ neazo la, konle, basabasayɛlɛ, atuadelɛ nee menli mɔɔ dwazo tia tumililɛ la anwo edwɛkɛ a yɛta yɛte wɔ amaneɛbɔlɛ nu a. Na yemɔ da ye ali kɛ mgbakyemgbakyenu nee ɛkpɔlɛ wɔ menli avinli. Kɛ mɔɔ Baebolo ne hanle dole ɛkɛ ne la, menli ‘ɛnli nɔhalɛ, bɛ nee bɛ ɛnyɛ ngyehyɛleɛ, bɛle dumasɛkyevoma, bɛngomo bɛ nwo zo, bɛ nwo yɛ ɛzulolɛ, bɛngulo kpalɛyɛlɛ, bɛye awie mɔ bɛmaa, bɛle tufoande yɛɛ bɛtu bɛ nloa anu.’ (2 Tim. 3:2-4) Noko yɛmɛ yɛkɔ zo yɛsonle Gyihova wɔ koyɛlɛ nu ɔnva nwo kɛ “ewiade ye amuala wɔ amumuyɛnli ne” nee ye sunsum ɛtane ne mɔ tumi bo la. (1 Dwɔn 5:19; Yek. 12:9) Amgba ɛhye le nwanwane debie!
<<<P>>>
12 Yɛbahola yɛaboa yɛamaa koyɛlɛ mɔɔ wɔ Gyihova ahyehyɛdeɛ ne anu la adɛnla ɛkɛ. Kɛzi yɛbayɛ ye ɛ? Ɔwɔ kɛ yɛtie mɔɔ Gyihova kilehile yɛ la kpalɛ na yɛdi zo. Ɔluakɛ mɔɔ ɔkilehile yɛ wɔ ye Edwɛkɛ ne anu la boa yɛ maa yɛsi abotane, yɛfa ɛtane yɛkye, ɔmmaa yɛnli butule yɛɛ ɔmaa yɛ nee awie mɔ di ye kɛnlɛma nu. (Ɛfɛ. 4:2, 31, 32) Ɔti mekɛ biala mɔɔ yɛbabu yɛ nye yɛagua yɛ diema bie nvonleɛ zo anzɛɛ yɛanye awie mɔ anwo ndɛne anzɛɛ yɛbava ɛtane yɛahyɛ la, ɛnee yɛ nee Gyihova ɛlɛbɔ nu ayɛ gyima yɛamaa nwanwane debie ɛhye azi ye ahyehyɛdeɛ ne anu ɛnɛ.
