BƐZILE EDWƐKƐHANLƐ GYIMA NE AZO WƆ NYIANU NE MƆ ABO
<<<P>>>
Wɔ May, 1926 awieleɛ, Baebolo Sukoavoma dɔɔnwo hɔle nyianu wɔ London, England. Mekɛ zɔhane, ɛnee gyimayɛvoma mɔɔ wɔ Britain la ɛhɔ strike, ɔti ɛnee ɔyɛ se kɛ yɛbanyia awie mɔ mɔɔ bɛbapenlete ninyɛne bɛamaa yɛ na yɛadua bɛ kakɛ a. Ɛhye ati mediema mɔɔ yɛ gyima wɔ Brooklyn Bɛtɛle la vale dɔnehwele dɔɔnwo yɛle gyima amaa bɛahola bɛapenlete buluku ekyi bie mɔɔ ɔ ti se The Standard for the People la 117,000 bɛamaa mediema ne mɔ wɔ nyianu ne abo.
<<<P>>>
Wɔ Folɛ, May 29, mediema ne mɔ bɔle ɔ bo kɛ bɛfa zɔhane buluku ekyi ne bɛali gyima. Kenle zɔhane, bɛmaanle mediema ne mɔ anwosesebɛ wɔ nyianu ne abo kɛ: “Bɛlɛto ɛsalɛ bɛavɛlɛ Baebolo Sukoavo biala mɔɔ wɔ ɛke la kɛ ɔbɔ Belemgbunli ne nee Belemgbunlililɛ ne anwo edwɛkpa ne nolo. Awie biala ɛhɔ Alexandra Palace ɛkɛ ne ndɛ na mediema mɔɔ wɔ ɛkɛ ne la bahile ye mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ ɔyɛ la.”
<<<P>>>
Mediema ne mɔ hyɛle buluku ekyi zɔhane dodo mɔɔ bo 100,000. July 15, 1926 Watch Tower ne hanle kɛ: “Mediema ne mɔ anye liele bole zo. Bɛdele nganeɛ kɛ bɛyɛ biala mɔɔ bɛbahola la bɛli Gyihova mɛla mɔɔ ɔse: ‘Bɛle me alasevolɛ yɛɛ mele Nyamenle la’ azo.”
<<<P>>>
Aleɛ hyenle la, Adiema Rutherford maanle edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ɔbado ewiade tumililɛ la anwo ɛdendɛlɛ wɔ sua bie mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Royal Albert Hall la anu. Mɔɔ bɛbukele sua ne nlenkɛ ne mɔ la, ɔkadwu mitini ekyii bie ala anu la, ɛnee menli 13,000 ɛyi ɛkɛ. Bɛvale speaker mgbole bie bɛzieziele alienwo ɔti bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛannyia ɛleka wɔ nyianu asalo ɛkɛ ne la dɛnlanle alienwo diele ɛdendɛlɛ ne.
<<<P>>>
Adiema Rutherford hanle wɔ ye ɛdendɛlɛ ne anu kɛ, kɛmɔ ewiade tumililɛ ngakyile ne mɔ ɛkpo Gyihova nee ye Belemgbunlililɛ ne la ati bɛbade bɛagua. Ɔkpale Maanle Maanle Ngyekyeleɛ ne anwo ɛdanlɛ na ɔhanle kɛ ɔnrɛhyɛ ɔ bo zo baminli.
