Yɛ Nwo Ɛlɛso Ɔnva Nwo Kpɔdekpɔdeyɛlɛ
<<<P>>>
Gyihova Alasevolɛ mɔɔ wɔ ewiade amuala la dodo ne hɔle ɔ nyunlu wɔ Ewiade Konle II la anu ɔnva nwo kɛ bɛyiale kpɔdekpɔdeyɛlɛ la. Nazi sogya kpanyinli ko hanle kɛ: “Ɛlɛyɛ Gyihova Alasevolɛ kpɔdekpɔde la ɛnee bɔ zo mɔɔ bɛ nwo ɛlɛso a.”
<<<P>>>
Suzu mɔɔ zile Netherlands wɔ Ewiade Konle II anu la anwo. Yɛ 1986 Yearbook ne hanle kɛ: “Bɛhyele mediema mɔɔ wɔ Netherlands la bie mɔ bɛguale efiade, bɛvale bie mɔ bɛhɔle maanle gyɛne zo na bɛhunle bie mɔ bɔbɔ. Noko wɔ ɛvolɛ nwiɔ ala anu, nolobɔlɛma mɔɔ wɔ ɛkɛ la dodo ne rayɛle mɔnwo nwiɔ. Wɔ 1943 bɛ dodo dwule 1,379. Gyihova boale ye azonvolɛ maanle bɛ dodo ne hɔle ɔ nyunlu ɔnva nwo kɛ ɛnee bɛwɔ tɛnlabelɛ mɔɔ anu yɛ se la anu la.”

6. Duzu ati a dwazotiama angola anzi alimoa Kilisienema adenle a?
<<<P>>>
gyima mɔɔ ye ɛdoavolɛma ne ɛlɛyɛ la adenle. Bɛhyele ɛzoanvolɛma ne na bɛ nee bɛ hɔle Sanhedelin ne, mɔɔ le Dwuuma kɔɔto kpole ne la anyunlu. Ɛnee zɔhane ɛzonlenlɛ nu mgbanyima ne mɔ ‘kpondɛ kɛ bɛku’ ɛzoanvolɛma ne. Noko Falasiinli bie mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Gameeleɛle la bɔle bɛ kɔkɔ kɛ: “Bɛmmafa bɛ nloa bɛwulowula menli ɛhye mɔ edwɛkɛ nu, bɛgyakyi bɛ. Ɔluakɛ saa ngyehyɛleɛ ɛhye anzɛɛ gyima ɛhye vi sonla a ɔbazɛkye; noko saa ɔvi Nyamenle a, bɛnrɛhola bɛnrɛzɛkye bɛ. Emomu, bɛannea boɛ a ɔbayɛ kɛ bɛ nee Nyamenle mumua ne a ɛlɛko a.” (Gyi. 5:33, 34, 38, 39) Bie a ɛnee Gameeleɛle ɛnze kɛ ye edwɛkɛ zɔhane bazi. Mɔɔ bɛgyakyile ɛzoanvolɛma ne bɔbɔ la ɛzonlenlɛ nu mgbanyima ne mɔ hɔle zo gyegyele bɛ. Noko nɔhavoma zɔhane mɔ ammaa bɛanzi bɛ adenle, emomu Kilisiene ɛzonlenlɛ hɔle zo dɛlɛle. (Gyi. 6:7) Duzu ati ɔ? Ɔluakɛ ɛnee Nyamenle ɛlɛboa ye menli ne yeamaa bɛagyinla k pundii na bɛ nwo azo ɔnva nwo kɛ bɛlɛdwazo bɛatia bɛ la.—Gyi. 1:8.

7 Ɛnɛ menli apenle dɔɔnwo ɛlɛba nɔhalɛ ne anu ɔnva nwo kɛ bɛ mbusuafoɔ, bɛ gɔnwo mɔ gyimayɛvoma nee menli gyɛne ye bɛ kpɔdekpɔde la. Duzu ati a ɛdoavolɛma fofolɛ ɛhye mɔ ɛkpa kɛ bɛbava bɛ “amaneɛnwunlɛ baka ne” a? (Mat. 16:24) Ɔluakɛ bɛkulo Gyihova yɛɛ bɛlɛ anwodozo kɛ ɔbali ewɔkɛ mɔɔ yebɔ kɛ ɔbanlea bɛ la azo. Na Gyihova di ye ewɔkɛ zɔhane azo na eza ɔmaa bɛ “tumi bedevinli ne” ɔmaa bɛgyinla k pundii. (2 Kɔl. 4:7-9) Saa bɛ diedi ne ati bɛ mbusuafoɔ dwazo tia bɛ a, bɛte nganeɛ kɛ edwɛndolɛnli ne mɔɔ hanle kɛ, “saa me papa nee me mame bɔbɔ kpo me a, Gyihova ɛdeɛ, ɔbalie me” la.
