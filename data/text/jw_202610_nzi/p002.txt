ƐVOLƐ ƐYA KO MƆƆ ƐRAZE
<<<P>>>
“MEKƐ mɔɔ Nyamenle bawula ye duma ne anyunlunyia la ɛlɛbikye. Nyamenle bakpa menli bie yeamaa bɛayɛ ye alasevolɛ wɔ azɛlɛ ye azo.” Bɛhanle edwɛkɛ ɛhye wɔ January 1, 1926 The Watch Tower ne anu.
<<<P>>>
Wɔ 1926 anu amuala, Gyihova luale Gyisɛse Kelaese mɔɔ le asafo ne ati la anwo zo boale Kilisienema maanle bɛdele ɔ bo kɛ edwɛkɛhanlɛ gyima ne a ɔwɔ kɛ ɔkyia bɛ kpalɛ wɔ bɛ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nu a. Ɛhye ati, “akɛlɛ nɔhavo nee badwema” ne maanle Nyamenle azonvolɛ mbuluku fofolɛ, bɛyɛle nzenzaleɛ wɔ yɛ debiezukoalɛ ne mɔ anu na bɛhilehilele gyima mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ Kilisienema amuala yɛ la anu kpalɛ bɛmaanle bɛdele ɔ bo. (Mat. 24:45) Gyisɛse boale bɛ maanle bɛnyianle azua nee milahyinli mɔɔ bɛbava bɛapenlete mbuluku mɔɔ bɛbava bɛayɛ edwɛkɛhanlɛ gyima ne la.
<<<P>>>
BƐYƐLE BULUKU FOFOLƐ YƐƐ BƐYƐLE NZENZALEƐ WƆ YƐ DEBIEZUKOALƐ NE MƆ ANU
<<<P>>>
Wɔ April, bɛhanle ye wɔ The Golden Age (ɛnɛ bɛfɛlɛ ye Awake!) ne anu kɛ bɛbaye buluku fofolɛ bie mɔɔ ɔ ti ne se Deliverance la. Ɛnee bɛbava buluku ɛhye bɛazie Studies in the Scriptures ne mɔɔ bɛfa bɛkilehile menli wɔ daselɛlilɛ nu la agyakɛ anu. Amaa Baebolo Sukoavoma ne asa aha buluku ne dɔɔnwo la, mediema mɔɔ yɛ gyima wɔ Bɛtɛle la dɔle milahyinli fofolɛ bie mɔɔ bɛfa bɛpenlete mbuluku la. Ɛnee ɔkola ɔpenlente mbuluku 22 wɔ mitini ko ala anu. Na awie biala mɔɔ Baebolo Sukoavoma ne yiale ye wɔ daselɛlilɛ nu la, bɛmaanle ye buluku ne bie.
<<<P>>>
Wɔ ɛvolɛ dɔɔnwo anu, ɛnee Baebolo Sukoavoma ne sukoa Studies in the Scriptures ne wɔ debiezukoalɛ bo. Noko ɔvi May, 1926, mɔɔ ba la, bɛbɔle ɔ bo kɛ bɛsukoa The Watch Tower ne. Duzu a vile nu rale a?
<<<P>>>
Asafo bie hɛlɛle Adiema Rutherford kɛlata. Edwɛkɛ mɔɔ wɔ bɛ kɛlata ne anu la bie ɛne: “Yɛbiza wɔ ahye. Molɛ biala yɛsukoa edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ahyɛhyɛdee ne ɛkpa kɛ yɛzukoa la. Ɔyɛ yɛ fɛ kɛ kɛkala yɛ muala yɛte ndelebɛbo fofolɛ biala mɔɔ ɔbara ye mekɛ nloa la abo ndɛndɛ la.”
<<<P>>>
Ndelebɛbo fofolɛ boale Baebolo Sukoavoma maanle bɛvale bɛ adwenle bɛziele edwɛkɛhanlɛ gyima ne azo. Kɛ neazo la, edwɛkɛ bie mɔɔ ɔ ti ne se “Character or Covenant—Which?” la hilehilele nu kɛ tɛ nɔhalɛ Kilisienema gyima titili a le kɛ bɛbabɔ mɔdenle kɛ bɛbanyia subane kpalɛ na bɛadendɛ kɛnlɛma bɛahile awie mɔ. Edwɛkɛ ne hilehilele nu kɛ Gyihova se ɔwɔ kɛ Kilisienema yɛ ye alasevolɛ. Ɔti kɛmɔ bɛkulo ye la ati, ɔwɔ kɛ bɛ nuhua ko biala di ye mɛla ɛhye azo na ɔfa anyelielɛ ɔka edwɛkɛ ne.
<<<P>>>
Edwɛkɛ bieko mɔɔ ɔ ti ne se, “Sacrifice and Service” la zile zolɛ kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ nɔhalɛ Kilisienema di nɔhalɛ maa Gyihova fa da ye ali kɛ bɛkulo ye. Eza edwɛkɛ ne maanle Baebolo Sukoavoma anwosesebɛ kɛ bɛmaa bɛ nye ɛbolo daselɛlilɛ nu na bɛha edwɛkɛ ne bɛhile menli dodo biala mɔɔ bɛbahola.—Aye. 43:10, 12; Mat. 24:14.
