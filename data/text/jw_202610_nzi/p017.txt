16 Saa yɛfi yɛ ahonle nu yɛyɛ yɛ mediema mo wɔ neazo kpalɛ mɔɔ bɛyɛ la anwo na yɛbɔ mɔdenle yɛ nee bɛ tɛnla anzodwolɛ nu a ɔkile kɛ yɛkulo bɛ. (Maake 9:50) Eza saa yɛdua yɛ edwɛkɛ nee yɛ nyɛleɛ zo yɛmaa bɛnwu kɛ yɛkulo bɛ a ɔkile kɛ amgba yɛle Nyamenle menli. Saa yɛda ɛlɔlɛ ali a ɛnee amgba yɛ nee Gyihova ɛlɛbɔ nu ayɛ nwanwane gyima ɛnɛ wɔ ye ahyehyɛdeɛ ne anu.

17. Duzu a maa ɛdie ɛdi kɛ Gyihova ɛlɛyɛ nwanwane ninyɛne ɛnɛ a?
<<<P>>>
nyunlu ɔnva nwo ɛkpɔlɛ mɔɔ yɛyia la. Mgbakyemgbakyenu ɛbu zo wɔ ewiade ye anu ɛdeɛ, noko yɛmɛ koyɛlɛ wɔ yɛ avinli. Ɔwɔ nu kɛ yɛde ewiade mɔɔ menli ɛnlɛ ɛlɔlɛ la anu, noko ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛ yɛmaa Gyihova nee yɛ mediema la maa ɔda ali kɛ yɛle Gyisɛse nɔhalɛ ɛdoavolɛma. (Dwɔn 13:34) Tɛ kɛ yɛgyi ɛleka bie a yɛlɛnea nwanwane ninyɛne mɔɔ Gyihova ɛlɛmaa yɛasisi la, emomu yɛ nee ye a ɛlɛbɔ nu ayɛ gyima a.—1 Kɔl. 3:9.

18 Nwane bɔbɔ a lale hanle ɛlalɛ kɛ kenle ko dedee menli mɔɔ vi maanle ngakyile nu wɔ azɛlɛ ye azo amuala la bayɛ ko, bɛahulo bɛ nwo ngoko na bɛ nwo azo wɔ ewiade mɔɔ ngyegyelɛ ɛyi nuhua la anu a? Noko yɛmɔ ala a ɛlɛsi ɛnɛ a! Nwanwane ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ mɔɔ ɛlɛsisi la maa yɛnyia anwodozo kpole kɛ kenle bie nwo ɛwɔkɛ mɔɔ Gyihova ɛbɔ yɛ la bara nu bɔkɔɔ. (2 Pita 3:13) Maa ɔyɛ wɔ fɛ dahuu kɛ ɛboka nwo a nwanwane ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ ɛlɛsisi ɛnɛ a.
