GhanaJUƐƆ O HE “Binyɛ Kpakpa” Blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ.
<<<P>>>
17. Bɔ nɛ pee nɛ wa yi mi tomi kɛ ha Ghana tsakemi nɛ wa hyɛ we yemi kɛ buami ko dɛ ɔ nɛ e ye emu ɔ (Ghana Beyond Aid) kɛ jehanɛ ɔ se hu ngɛ KƆVID -19 hiɔ yaya a se ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ je sisi po sika nɛ ji sidi yɔɔyi lafa fɔ si konɛ a kɛ wo ni tsumihi a mi kɛ hue a nɔ aloo kɛ tle mɛ si ekohu, (GhanaJUƐƆ O HE “Binyɛ Kpakpa” blɔ nya tomi ɔ). A pee enɛ ɔ konɛ e nyɛ nɛ e hue aloo e wo ma a e ni tsumihi a nɔ nɛ e bli ni tsumi slɔɔtohi a mi titli ɔ, ni tsumihi nɛ a be Amlaalo ɔ sisi ɔmɛ a nɔ.
<<<P>>>
18. Nihi nɛ nyɛɛ se nɛ ɔmɛ ji nɔ yami otihi nɛ ba ngɛ 2021 mi kɛ blɔhi a nɔ nɛ a to kɛ ha 2022.
<<<P>>>
Mwɔnɛɔmwɔnɛ ɔ Aloo Tsakemi Ehehi kɛ ha Akua Peemi kɛ Niye ni Sɛmi Nya Tsimi.
<<<P>>>
19. Kɛ ha akua peemi blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ emu yemi ɔ, a bua nimli kakaaka 579,662 nya nɛ a ngma a biɛ kɛ gu kɔɔhiɔ mi klama blɔ nɔ tomi ko nɔ. Ngɛ nimli hiɛhiɛɛ nɛ ɔmɛ a kpɛti ɔ 547,126 ji akuafohi, 32,536 ji nihi nɛ a ngɔ a he kɛ wo akuafo ni tsumi ɔ mi ngɛ blɔ slɔɔtohi a nɔ. A to blɔ nya nɛ ɔ ngɛ Kɔle hlami ɔ mi ngɛ jeha 2021 mi konɛ e ha nɛ hɛ nɛ e hi akuafo ni tsuli slɔɔto nɛ ɔmɛ a se nɛ e kɛ nɔ yami , anɔkuale yemi aloo pɛpɛɛpɛ peemi nɛ ba akuafo ni tsumi ɔ mi.
<<<P>>>
20. Amlaalo ɔ kɛ lohwe lɛli titli ɔ kungwɔ lɛli, omɔ, soya aloo anyingmaloku, tomatosi aloo amoo ngmɔ peeli kɛ nihi slɔɔto nɛ a tsuɔ ni kɛ kɔ ni nɛ ɔmɛ a he ɔ, na ni sɛɛmi konɛ a kɛ tsɔɔ mɛ nya nɛ a nu nyagbahi nɛ ngɛ a ni tsumi ɔ he ɔmɛ a sisi. A pee enɛ ɔ kɛ tsɔɔ nɔ he je nɛ a ngɔ blɔ nya tomi ko kɛ fɔ si kɛ gbɔ sika papɛɛ he kpa nɛ baa ngɛ a ni tsumi ɔ mi ɔ ɔmɛ a nɔ. Sika papɛɛ he kpa nɔ gbɔmi blɔ nɔ tomi nɛ ɔ maa ba he kpa a si kɛ ba da lafa mi gbami 50 konɛ a na sika bɔ nɛ sa kɛ he ni tsumi ni ɔmɛ nɛ a kɛ bli a ni tsumi sika amɛ a mi.

<<<P>>>

<<<P>>>
