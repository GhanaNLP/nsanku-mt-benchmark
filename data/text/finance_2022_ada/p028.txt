64. A ma nɛ a gbe lohwehi a lɛhe nyɔngma (10) ngɛ nyɔngma kɛ kake (11) nɛ a to yi mi kaa a maa pee ɔmɛ a nya. Ngɛ enɛ ɔ se ɔ, a tsua aloo a je vu nɛ a kɛ klama gblaa e mi nyu ɔ nyɔngma kɛ kake (11) kɛ ha lohwe lɛhe ɔmɛ. Kɛ piɛɛ he ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ gba lohwe ɔmɛ a hapohapo pomi loo simi tsopa 7,700,500 kɛ ha kungwɔhi kɛ lohwe ekpa amɛ a lɛhe ɔmɛ.

65. Amlaalo ɔ tsa takuhi kɛ vuhi a mi tsuami aloo a mi tami kɛ ekomɛ ehe peemi ɔ nɔ, bɔ nɛ pee nɛ a na nyu kɛ pue ngmɔ nihi ɔmɛ a nɔ. Ni tsumi nɛ ɔmɛ a nya gbemi ɔ ma wa kɛ na ngmɔ nihi a nɔ nyu puemi he nɛ klemi maa su 6,766ha nɛ maa ye bua kɛ ha ngmɔhi a peemi ngɛ jeha a tsuo mi. Bɔ nɛ nihi a nɔ nyu puemi he ɔmɛ a klemi ngɛ ha ji nɛ ɔ nɛ: Kpong Ngmɔ Nihi a Nɔ Nyu Puemi He ɔ (2,176ha), Tono Ngmɔ Nihi a Nɔ Nyu Puemi He ɔ (2,490ha) kɛ Kpong Muɔ Nɔ Ngmɔ Nihi a Nɔ Nyu Puemi He ɔ (2,100ha). Ngmɔ nihi a nɔ nyu puemi he ɔ ekomɛ nɛ a gbe a nya ji Tamne ngɛ Yiti Beleku Kpo ngua a mi kɛ Mprumem ngɛ Kpɛti Kpo ngua a mi. Enɛ ɔmɛ hu ma ha wɔ ngmɔ nihi a nɔ nyu puemi he nɛ a klemi su 375ha kɛ ha omɔ kɛ ngmɔ ni tsɔwi a peemi.

66. Kɛ ba ta Lemɔ hlami ɔ nyagbe ngɛ 2021 jeha a mi ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ gbe ngmɔ nihi a tohe nyingmi enyɔ kɛ etɛ (23) nya, ngɛ nyingmi etɛ (30) nɛ a to kaa a maa pee ɔ mi. Ni tsumi nɛ ɔ ya nɔ ngɛ juɛmi kpɔ nɛ a tsɛɔ kɛ: Kpo Wayo Kake Ngmɔ Nihi Tohe Kake ɔ sisi. A to he blɔ nya kɛ ha kpaago (7) nɛ piɛ ɔmɛ a nya gbemi ngɛ 2021 jeha a nyagbe ɔ mi. Blɔ nɔ tomi nɛ ɔ ma bli blɔ kɛ ha ngmɔ ni tohe ɔmɛ nɛ a nyɛ nɛ a to ngmɔ nihi nɛ a ka nya su 30,000mt ngɛ ngmɔ ni hiɛhiɛɛ a bami blɔ hyɛmi ngɛ PFJ blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ sisi.
