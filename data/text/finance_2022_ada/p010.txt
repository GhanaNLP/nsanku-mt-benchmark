Bɔjɛti Mi Sɔmi kɛ Hiɔ Womi Blɔ Nɔ Tomi
<<<P>>>
7. Amlaalo ɔ sika he ni tsumi blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ ngɔ hiɔ nɛ ji Ghana sika a yɔɔyi 33.86 nɛ daa si kɛ ha lafa mi gbami (7.7 GDP) kɛ ba, ke waa kɛ to yi mi tomi nihi tsakemi nɛ ji Ghana sika a yɔɔyi 32.58 nɛ daa si kɛ ha lafa mi gbami (7.4 GDP) ɔ he. A da ma a kɛ ma se yemi kɛ buami nihi a nɔ kɛ wo hiɔ nɛ ɔ. Ma a hiɔ womi blɔ nya tomi kulaa ya da Ghana sika yɔɔyi 20.39, be mi nɛ maje hiɔwo yemi kɛ buami ɔ ba pee Ghana sika yɔɔyi 13.47 nɛ daa si kɛ ha lafa mi gbami 60.2 kɛ lafa mi gbami 39.8 kaa bɔ nɛ a to a nya a.

Hiɔ nɛ Ma a Ye He Mɔde Bɔmi kɛ ha 2021
<<<P>>>
8. Hiɔ nɛ ma a ye maje mahi ya su Ghana sika yɔɔyi 341.76 nɛ peeɔ Amɛlika dɔla yɔɔyi 58.348 benɛ Lemɔ hlami ɔ ke baa nyagbe ngɛ jeha 2021 ɔ mi. Enɛ ɔ daa si kɛ ha lafa mi gbami 77.8 GDP. Hiɔhi kulaa nɛ ma a ye ɔ ji maje hiɔhi nɛ ji Ghana sika yɔɔyi 163.65 nɛ ya peeɔ Amɛlika dɔla yɔɔyi 27.88 kɛ ma a nitsɛ e mi hiɔhi nɛ ji Ghana sika a yɔɔyi 178.11 nɛ lɔ ɔ hu ya peeɔ Amɛlika dɔla yɔɔyi 30.35 nɛ daa si kɛ ha lafa mi gbami 47.9 kɛ lafa mi gbami 52.1 ke a bua mɛ tsuo nya kaa bɔ nɛ a to a nya a.
