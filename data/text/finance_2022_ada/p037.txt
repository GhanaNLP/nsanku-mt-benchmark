“Oti: Ma nɔ yami kɛ gu hɛ mi ga kɛ dɛhe ni tsumi nɔ: Sika si pimi kɛ ni tsumi blimi. ”
<<<P>>>
Ma a Sika Blɔ Nya Tomi ɔ Nɔ Yami Nɛ Zu Mi Nu Piɛɛ We He: Enɛ ɔ ji ma a sika blɔ nya tomi ɔ nɔ yami /yɛmi nɛ zu mi nu he ni tsumi nihi piɛɛ we he. Enɛ ɔmɛ hyɛɔ bɔ nɛ nihi a he jua wami aloo a jua si bami ngɛ ha.
<<<P>>>
Sika Nɛ A Wui Nɔ To: Enɛ ɔ ji sika nɛ Amlaalo ɔ naa nɛ pi to sika. Ekomɛ ji hiɔhi nɛ a woɔ kɛ hlaa blɔ ko nya loo kɛ biɔ he blɔ nami womi, hiɔ nɛ a sa womi ngɛ Amlaalo ɔ ni komɛ a ngɔmi kɛ tsu ni he.
<<<P>>>
Hiɔ Nɛ Piɛ Womi: Hiɔ nɛ piɛ womi ji si womi hiɔhi nɛ ngɛ MDA kɛ Amlaalo ni tsumi he ngua nɛ hyɛɔ ma a sika blɔ nɔ tomi nɔ ɔ a kpɛti kɛ hiɔ kpa komɛ nɛ a sa womi.
<<<P>>>
Nɔ Da Oti (Primary balance): Nɔ da Oti ji sika nɛ piɛɛ ngɛ Amlaalo ɔ nihi nɛ e na kɛ nihi nɛ a pee nɛ mi womi sika ko be nɔ ɔ a kpɛti. (enɛ ɔ piɛɛ we hiɔ nɛ a ye womi he) a ma nyɛ maa da ma a weto ni nɛ a naa nɔ kɛ susu nɔ da nɛ baa ngɛ lafa mi gbami mi.
<<<P>>>
Ni Tsumi Nɔ Yami: Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ bɔ nɛ Ghana sika he ni tsumi maa ya nɔ ha ngɛ jeha ko mi.
<<<P>>>
Sika nɛ Baa Amlaalo ɔ dɛ: Enɛ ɔ ji sikahi tsuo nɛ Amlaalo ɔ naa kɛ jeɔ ma a kɛ mase ni tsumihi a blɔ nya tomi mi.
<<<P>>>
Lohwehi a Lɛmi kɛ Niye ni Nami (Rearing For Food and Jobs): Enɛ ɔ maa ngɔ kedimi kpakpa kɛ ba lohwe lɛmi ni tsumi ɔ mi nɛ e ma hue ni tsumi ɔ nya nɔ nɛ e gbɔ lohwehi a sɛmi kɛ ba ma a mi ɔ nɔ. Enɛ ɔ maa ngɔ ni tsumi kɛ ba ha nihi slɔɔto nɛ a ngɔ a he kɛ wo lohwe lɛmi ni tsumi ɔ mi nɛ maa ngɔ nɔ yami hu kɛ ba a si himi mi.
<<<P>>>
Ma Nihi a He Se Nami: Amlaalo ɔ woɔ hiɔ aloo to tsɔwitsɔwi ngɛ nyu, pɛtloo kɛ oslaba he wami kane kɛ ni ɔ ekomɛ a peemi he ɔmɛ a nɔ kɛ ha ma bi aloo ngɛ ma bi a nane mi.
<<<P>>>
Hiɔ Nɔ Gbɔmi aloo Jemi Sika: Sika nɛ ɔ ji hiɔ nɔ gbɔmi sika nɛ Amlaalo ɔ kɛ haa ma bi nɛ a kɛ je ohia nyagbahi a nɔ.
<<<P>>>
Hɛ Ngmɛ kɛ Nɔ Mi Womi: Blɔ nya tomi nɛ ɔ ji nihi a he akɔtaa bumi pɛpɛɛpɛ aloo hɛ ngmɛ kɛ nihi a mi womi fitsofitso kɛ nihi a he hɛ tomi ngɛ juɛmi nɛ mi kuɔ mi.
<<<P>>>
Ni Tsumi Mi Heii Peemi: Nɔ nɛ ɔ yaa nɔ ke Amlaalo ɔ to blɔ nya kɛ ha amaniɛbɔ mlamlaamla ngɛ nihi nɛ e ngɛ pee ɔmɛ a he.
<<<P>>>
Nihi a He Ni Tsumi Sika: Enɛ ɔ ji sika nɛ Amlaalo ɔ woɔ kɛ ha e ni tsumi ɔmɛ a blɔ nya tomi kɛ ha ma bi.
<<<P>>>
Mi Jiimi To (VAT): Enɛ ɔ ji to nɛ a kɛ fɔɔ kpɔ mi kpɔ mi nɛ nihi gbleɔ ngɛ a peemi mi ɔ nɔ.
