Sika He Ni Tsumi Blɔ Nɔ Tomi
<<<P>>>
15. Bɔ nɛ pee nɛ wa nyɛ nɛ waa nu nihi a nya saminya kɛ nu sika he ni tsumi blɔ nya tomi ɔ kɛ da si ɔ, wa je blɔ slɔɔtohi fuu kɛ pue si kɛ ha sika he blɔ nya tomi ɔ he ni tsumi nɛ ɔ ngɛ 2022 sika he blɔ nya tomi ɔ mi ngɛ be kpiti mi:
<<<P>>>
a. Amlaalo ɔ ma tsa nɔ nɛ e maa po piɛ kɛ wo ni tsuli a hiɔ womi blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ he bɔ nɛ pee ha nɛ nihi nɛ a ngɛ Amlaalo ni tsumi mi pɛ lɛ a biɛhi nɛ hi Amlaalo hiɔwo womi ngua a mi;
<<<P>>>
b. Amlaalo ni tsuli a mi hlami ɔ maa ya nɔ ngɛ 2022 jeha nɛ ɔ mi konɛ a kɛ hyɛ kaa Amlaalo ni tsumi he ɔmɛ ngɔ ni tsuli nɛ maa hia amɛ kɛ nihi nɛ sa mɛ ngɔmi ɔmɛ pɛ;
<<<P>>>
c. Ni tsumi ehehi nɛ a wo ta ngɛ 2022 bɔjɛti ɔ kɛ nɔ nɛmɛ nɛ a kɛ wo MDA, MMDA Kɛ SOE ni tsumi he/ kuu ɔmɛ tsuo a ni tsumi blɔ nɔ tomi ɔmɛ a mi ɔ, blɔ slɔɔtohi a nɔ nɛ a guɔ kɛ naa sika amɛ ɔ, a bɔ mɔde nɛ a ye blɔ nya tomihi nɛ a kɛ fɔ si ngɛ mlaa aloo L.I. 2411 mi ɔmɛ a nɔ saminya ngɛ a blɔ nya slɔɔto ɔmɛ a tsumi mi.
<<<P>>>
16. Sika he ni tsumi kpakami aloo kudɔmi blɔ nɔ tomi ɔmɛ a mi miami aloo a hyɛmi konɛ blɔ nɔ tomi ɔmɛ nɛ a ko gbede kɛ je sika he ni tsumi blɔ nɛ a je kɛ fɔ si ɔ nɔ:
<<<P>>>
• Blɔ ngmɛmi maa ba kɛ ha nihi pɛ nɛ MDA ɔmɛ ngɔ kɛ wo a nɔ hemi blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ nɛ a kɛ wo aloo a kɛ fɔ PPA kɔɔhiɔ mi kpa nɔ ni hemi blɔ nɔ tomi (PPA website) ɔ mi;
<<<P>>>
• Blɔ bimi aloo blɔ bami womihi tsuo kɛ ha nihi a hemi ngɛ jeha a mi ɔ, e ya da a bɔjɛti nɛ a pee kɛ ha a nihi a hemi kɛ a he blɔ nya tomi ɔ kɛ ha jeha a nɔ. Ngɛ enɛ ɔ se ɔ, a pee aloo a kudɔ blɔ bimi blɔ ɔmɛ nɛ a ya da a sika nɛ a ngɛ kɛ sikahi a blɔ nɛ a ngɛ hyɛe tutuutu ɔmɛ a nɔ;
<<<P>>>
• Ni tsumi nya dalɔ aloo e nɔkɔtɔma ko nɛ ko ngɔ e nine kɛ wo MOU aloo gbi kplɛɛmi ko nɛ maa fi Amlaalo ɔ nine ngɛ sika blɔ fami sisi be mi nɛ e bi we blɔ kɛ je Amlaalo ma sɔmɔlɔ nɔkɔtɔma nɛ hyɛɔ ma a sika he blɔ nya tomihi a nɔ ɔ dɛ.
