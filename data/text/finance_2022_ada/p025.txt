53. Kɛ ba ta Lemɔ nyagbe ligbi ɔ nɔ ɔ, a ngɔ Ghana fɔmi bimɛ nɛ a hiɛmi su 15,656,160 kɛ fɔ Ma Bi A he So Tomi Womi nɛ ji “National Identity Register (NIR)” ɔ nɔ. Ngɛ yibɔ nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, Ghana fɔmi bimɛ nɛ a yibɔ su 11,648,391 nɛ a ye kɛ je jeha nyɔngma kɛ enuɔ (15) kɛ ya a, a nine su Ghana fɔmi bi a he so tomi womi loo kaadi ɔ nɔ. Maje li nɛ mɛ hu a yibɔ su 163,695 ɔ, a gbla mɛ kɛ wo mi nɛ a ha mɛ Ghana maje he so tomi womi aloo kaadihi.
