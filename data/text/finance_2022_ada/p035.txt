MUNYUNGU PƆTEƐ KOME A SISI TSƆƆMI (BƆJƐTI MI MUNYUNGUHI)
<<<P>>>
Kɔpe Kake Ni Tsumi Kake 1D1F: Ni tsumi hehi a mami loo a blimi ngɛ kpo wawi ɔmɛ a mi ɔ maa ngɔ ni tsumi fuu kɛ ba ha Ghana ma a nɔ yami. E ma wa kɛ bli ni tsumi slɔɔto kɛ kpa Ghana ma a tsuo mi. Enɛ ɔ ma bli ni tsumi slɔɔto ha Ghana ma bi tsuo.
<<<P>>>
Nihi a He Akɔtaa Bumi: Enɛ ɔ ji blɔ nɔ nɛ Amlaalo ɔ guɔ kɛ buɔ nihi tsuo kɛ blɔnyahi nɛ a kɛ wo e nine nɔ he akɔtaa kɛ ha ma bi. Ni nɛ ɔ ekomɛ ji: sika, zugba kɛ mi nihi, kɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ko nɛ a kɛ wo e nine nɔ.
<<<P>>>
Nihi a Gbami: Enɛ ɔ ji blɔ nya tomi nɛ Amlaalo guɔ nɔ kɛ gbaa weto ni slɔɔto kɛ ha ni tsumi hehi nɛ ngɛ Amlaalo ɔ sisi ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Nɔ Gbɔmi: Enɛ ɔ ji blɔ nɔ tomi nɛ Amlaalo ɔ guɔ nɛ e kɛ jeɔ ma bi a haomi kɛ a nyagba slɔɔto ɔmɛ a nɔ.
<<<P>>>
Bɔjɛti: E ji blɔ nya tomi nɛ tsɔɔ he nɛ a ma na sika ngɛ kɛ nihi nɛ a maa ngɔ sika kɛ tsu. Nɔhyɛ nɔ: Weku ko ma nyɛ maa to blɔ nya nɛ ɔ eko kɛ tsɔɔ sika abɔ nɛ e ngɛ kɛ bɔ nɛ e ma tsu jamɛ a sika a kɛ a hiami a nya ni ha. A maa hyɛ a hiami nihi pɔteɛ komɛ kaa tsu hiɔwo, nyu he hiɔwo, kane he hiɔwo kɛ niye ni kɛ ekpa komɛ. Ke weku nɛ ɔ maa hiaa yi gbemi sika ko ɔ, a ma nyɛ ma tsu ni aloo a ya pa sika kɛ ha a blɔ nya ni tsumi nɛ ɔmɛ.
<<<P>>>
Bɔjɛti mi hiɔ (Defisiti): Hiɔ nɛ ɔ baa ke Amlaalo ɔ tsu sika he ni pe bɔ nɛ tohi nɛ a ngɛ blɔ hyɛeɔ ngɔɔ kɛ baa a.
<<<P>>>
Sika Titlii nɛ Amlaalo ɔ kɛ tsuɔ ni: Enɛ ɔ ji sika titlii nɛ Amlaalo ɔ kɛ woɔ ni komɛ kaa sukuu tsuhi, blɔhi, hiɔ tsami hehi, asaunɔ kɛ bkuhi, nyu, lɔle kɛ klamahi a peemi mi.
<<<P>>>
Ni Tsuli a Kpatami aloo Tɔkemi Sika: Enɛ ɔ ji hiɔhi nɛ Amlaalo ɔ woɔ e nine nɔ ni tsuli kɛ ni tsuli nɛ a ba he jɔɔmi mi ngɛ Amlaalo ɔ nine nɔ.
<<<P>>>
Yemi Kɛ Buami Kuuhi (DPs): Mɛ ji mahi, ni tsumi hehi kɛ kuuhi nɛ a haa mahi nɛ a wɛ tɛ ɔ yemi kɛ buami nɛ a kɛ tsu nɔ yami ni tsumihi nɛ ma ngɔ wami kɛ ba. DPs nɛ ɔmɛ ma nyɛ maa pee ni tsumi hehi nɛ tsuɔ ni kɛ guɔ blɔ munomunohi a nɔ kaa: Je kɛ Wɛ Sika To He Ngua a (IMF), Je ɔ Sika To He Ngua a (World Bank), Afrika Mahi a Nɔ Yami Sika To He ɔ (AFDB) loo mahi kaa Jaamani (Germany), Ngleesi Ablotsi (United Kingdom) kɛ Amerika (USA).
<<<P>>>
Nihi a He Jua Wami Ngɛ Jeha a Nyagbe Mi: He nɛ nihi a he jua kuɔmi ya su ngɛ Bloonya hlami ɔ mi ngɛ jeha nɛ a ngɛ he munyu tue ɔ mi.
<<<P>>>
Sika He Ni Tsumi nɛ Be Nɔ: Enɛ ɔ baa ke Amlaalo ɔ tsu sika he ni pe nɔ nɛ a kɛ wo bɔjɛti ɔ mi nɛ Mlaa Womi Kpe he ɔ ngmɛ blɔ ɔ.
