10. Tohi kɛ he dooli a yemi kɛ buami tsuo kɛ ha 2022 ɔ, e ya da Ghana sika yɔɔyi 100.52 nɛ ji lafa mi gbami 20.0 ke e da ma a weto nihi tsuo a nɔ ɔ( GDP). Hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi ngɛ kaa ma a mi tohi kɛ nihi nɛ gblaa sika kɛ ba a, e ya da sidi yɔɔyi 99.55. Ngɛ tohi kɛ nihi tsuo nɛ gblaa ma a sika kɛ ba a, nihi nɛ zu mi nu piɛɛ we he a nɔ to ɔmɛ peeɔ lafa mi gbami 77.5 nɛ ngɛ sika mi ɔ, e ya peeɔ Ghana sidi ayɔ 77.136. Blɔ hyɛmi ji kaa, nihi nɛ gblaa sika kɛ ba haa ma a, a nya buami ɔ kɛ ha 2022 ɔ ma ya da tohi a ngɔmi blɔ nya tomi ɔ mi bangemi kɛ a nɔ yemi, tohi a hemi blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ kɛ mlaa amɛ a mi tsakemi nɔ.
