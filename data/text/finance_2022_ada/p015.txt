Sika Gblami Blɔ Nɔ Tomihi
<<<P>>>
14. Amlaalo ɔ maa ngɔ blɔ nɔ tomi nihi nɛ ma tsake klama kɛ nihi a bɔmi aloo a peemi blɔ nya tomi ɔ ngɛ ma a kɛ maje aloo mase jua amɛ a nɔ. Ngɛ enɛ ɔ peemi mi ɔ, e maa hiaa wawɛɛ kaa wa maa pee tsakemi nɛ ɔmɛ:
<<<P>>>
• Bɔ nɛ pee nɛ se nami nɛ ba nɛ a bli klama kɛ nihi a peemi mi ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ maa tsiɛ kɛ ya se bɔɔ vati (VAT) to nɔ gbɔmi blɔ nya tomi ɔ nɛ a kɛ fɔ Aflika bohi kɛ tade bohi a peemi nɛ a kɛ fɔ si ngɛ 2019 mi ɔ kɛ ya ta jeha enyɔ;
<<<P>>>
• Ni tsumi nɛ ɔ nɔ yami ngɛ jeha enyɔ kɛ fa se ɔ, nihi a jua nɔ jemi kami tso ɔ nɛ a kɛ fɔ nihi nɛ a sɛɔ ɔmɛ a nɔ nɛ e ngɔ he jɔmi bɔɔ kɛ ba a, a ma plɛ e hɛ mi bɔ nɛ pee nɛ e tsu ni saminya nɛ a kɛ nɔ yami kɛ tsakemi nɛ ba hɛnɔ mami blɔ nya tomi ɔ mi;
<<<P>>>
• A ma gbɔ tohi nɛ a kɛ fɔ sika tsu nɛ a fɔ we he tsuahe tsɔwi ɔmɛ a nɔ kɛ je lafa mi gbami 3 kɛ ba da lafa mi gbami 1.5;
<<<P>>>
• Kɛ maa je Jɔne hlami ɔ nɔ ngɛ 2022 jeha a mi ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ maa gu Ghana sika gblami aloo nya buami ni tsumi he ɔ ( Ghana Revenue Authority) nɔ nɛ e nyɛ nɛ ye kɛ bua MMDA ɔmɛ konɛ a nyɛ nɛ a gu blɔ kake ko nɔ kɛ he aloo a ngɔ weto nihi nɛ a pee nɔ to ɔmɛ konɛ a nyɛ bu a he akɔtaa saminya;
<<<P>>>
• Amlaalo ɔ maa hyɛ nɛ e tsake tohi nɛ MDA ɔmɛ heɔ ɔmɛ a mi kɛ je Jɔne 2022 kekle ligbi ɔ nɔ. A maa je blɔ ko kɛ fɔ si konɛ to kɛ hiɔ nɛ ɔmɛ a nɔ nɛ hi huemi aloo womi ngɛ mɛ nitsɛmɛ a dɛhe kaa bɔ nɛ jeha a mi nihi a he jua kuɔmi aloo e si bami ngɛ yae ha a;
<<<P>>>
• A ma je aloo a ma kpa gbɛjegbɛ kɛ lɔkuhi a nɔ to ɔmɛ hemi nɔuu ke mlaa womi kpe he ɔ pi bɔjɛti ɔ nɔ nine.
<<<P>>>
• Wa maa je sisi bɔni tohi hemi ngɛ klama nɔ jua yemi nihi a nɔ, nɛ ji “Electronic Transaction Levy” aloo “E-Levy”, konɛ e bli to hemi ya a mi kɛ su ni tsumihi nɛ a be Amlaalo ɔ sisi tutuutu ɔmɛ a nɔ. Nihi nɛ to ehe nɛ ɔ ma kɔ a he titlii ji sika manemi kɛ e jemi kɛ guu kɔɔhiɔ mi kpa a nɔ, sika tohe ɔmɛ a sika manemi, kɔɔhiɔ mi kpa nɔ sika manemi kɛ e jemi ni tsumi hehi, nɔ nɛ mane aloo yaa je sika a, maa wo to nɛ ji lafa mi gbami 1.75 ngɛ a je juami blɔ nɔ tomi ɔmɛ a nɔ. To nɛ ɔmɛ a hemi ɔ maa je sisi ngɛ Lami kekle ligbi ɔ nɔ ngɛ jeha 2022 mi. A be to nɛ ɔ hee aloo ngɔe kɛ fɔ sika nɛ e bi sidi lafa ( GH¢100 ) nɔ.
