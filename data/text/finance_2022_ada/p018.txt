22. Bɔ nɛ pee ha nɛ wa nyɛ hue sikpa jemi aloo si slami, hɛ mi ga ni tsumi kɛ kusumi blɔ nya tomi ni tsumi he ɔ nɛ ngɛ GhanaJUƐƆ O HE (GhanaCARES) sisi ɔ nɔ ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ gbe kpa nɔ ni tsumi blɔ nɔ tomi kakaaka lafa etɛ (300) nya nɛ e kɛ ba wo ma mi slaami blɔ nya nɛ a bli ɔ sisi konɛ e nyɛ nɛ e kudɔ si slali ɔmɛ ngɛ a si slami hiami nihi ɔmɛ a he konɛ e bli mi kɛ ha e gbugbuugbu peemi kɛ e nɔ yami.
<<<P>>>
23. Ngɛ jeha 2022 mi ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ maa ma yemi kɛ buami hehi kaa nɔ yami tsuapohi, la sumi klamahi a tohe, ni kokoe a tohe. A ma dla blɔhi nɛ a tsa mɛ nɛ a gbla kane he wami, nyu kɛ ni sɛɛmi nihi kɛ ya si slami he ɔmɛ.

24. Bɔ nɛ pee ha nɛ Amlaalo ɔ yi mi tomi oti nyɔngma (10) komɛ nɛ e po fɔ si ɔ nɛ e kɛ hue ma a klama nihi a bɔmi nɔ yami nɔ ɔ nɛ ba mi ɔ, a ngɔ he hiami titlii ko kɛ fɔ nihi nɛ a mi jiɔ we tsɔ ɔ a nɔ konɛ e ku nihi a sɛmi ɔ nya. Enɛ ɔ ma kanya wɔ kɛ bli wa ni tsɔwitsɔwi a bɔmi ɔ mi he wami nɛ a fu konɛ e kɛ ni tsumi nɛ ba. Ngɛ 2021 jeha a mi ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ je sisi bɔni tsopa tsɔwi a peemi hehi blimi ngɛ Noguchi konɛ e pee se kpo kɛ ha tsopahi a peemi he ɔmɛ. Blɔ nya tomi titlii aloo oti nɛ ɔmɛ ma bɔni ni tsumi ngɛ 2022 jeha a mi:
<<<P>>>
• Ni tsumi blɔ nɔ tomi ɔmɛ a mi miami kɛ ha a hɛ mi tsakemi (Enɛ ɔ piɛɛ juɛmi klama blɔ nya tomi ɔmɛ a he) ngɛ he nɛ a ngmaa ni tsumihi kɛ ma a nɔ fɛɛ nɔ kɛ woɔ ma a womi ngua a mi ngɛ ɔ;
<<<P>>>
• Amlaalo ɔ maa je blɔ nya tomi komɛ a sisi nɛ a ma nihi a bɔmi he aloo a peemi he ngɛ kpo ngua amɛ a nɔ konɛ a kɛ tsa Yunivɛsiti ɔmɛ nɛ tsɔɔ hɛ mi ga ni tsumi ɔmɛ; nɛ
<<<P>>>
• A kɛ bli ma a mi tsopa peemi blɔ nɔ tomi ɔmɛ a mi konɛ e ye kɛ bua tsopa gbugbuugbu a peemi nihi ɔmɛ a nami.
<<<P>>>
Blɔ nɛ ngɛ juɛmi klama he ni lemi mi ɔ kɛ e mi tsakpa kɛ eka womi
<<<P>>>
25. Amlaalo ɔ nane hiami ngɛ juɛmi klama mi nile he ɔ ngɛ nɔ yae bɔ nɛ sa. Kɛ ha enɛ ɔ emu yemi ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ pee he si jemi kaadihi hɛ nɔ kake kɛ ha ma bi nɛ a hiɛhiɛɛ su ayɔ 15 nɛ a nu wehi ayɔ 7.5 kɛ wo hihe tsɔɔmi kɔɔhiɔ mi kpa a nɔ.
<<<P>>>
26. Nane nɛ Amlaalo ɔ hia kaa e maa ngɔ ma a sika he ni tsumi blɔ nya tomi ɔ kɛ fɔ kɔɔhiɔ mi juɛmi klama kpa a nɔ ɔ, ngmɛ blɔ nɛ Ghana Sika Tohe Ngua a ngɔ kɔɔhiɔ mi kpa nɔ sika blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ kɛ bɔni ni tsumi. Wa bli blɔ ko nɛ ji ‘Ghana.gov’ magbɛ ɔ nɔ kɛ piɛɛ ekomɛ a he nɛ e wo ni tsumihi a blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ mi eka.
