82. A gbe 2021 nimli kɛ wehi a kanemi ni tsumi ɔ nya ngɛ Maya ligbi kpaanyɔne (8) ɔ nɔ ngɛ 2021 jeha a mi. Ni tsumi nɛ ɔ he amaniɛbɔ womi nɛ a je kpo ngɛ Lemɔ 22, 2021 ɔ mi ɔ, tsɔɔ kaa Ghana bi a he hiɛmi ji 30,729, 608. Ngɛ ma bi a yibɔ nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, yihi a he hiɛmi ji ayɔ 15,610,149 nɛ e daa si kɛ ha lafa mi gbami (50.7) kɛ nyumuhi hu a hiɛmi ayɔ 15,182,459 nɛ daa si kɛ ha lafa mi gbami (49.3).
