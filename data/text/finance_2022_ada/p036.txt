“Oti: Ma nɔ yami kɛ gu hɛ mi ga kɛ dɛhe ni tsumi nɔ: Sika si pimi kɛ ni tsumi blimi. ”
<<<P>>>
Sɛkɔndle Sukuu Yami Faa: Sɛkɔndle sukuu yami faa nɛ hiɔwo be he ɔ, e ji Ghana Amlaalo ɔ blɔ nya tomi ehe ko nɛ Ma nɔ yelɔ Nana Akuffo-Addo nɔ yemi ɔ kɛ ba ngɛ Lemɔ hlami ɔ mi ngɛ 2017. Blɔ nya tomi nɛ ɔ ngmɛ blɔ faa kɛ ha JHS sukuu bi nɛ a na he blɔ kaa a maa ya sɛkɔndle sukuu ɔ nɛ a ya lɛ faa nɛ hiɔwo ko be he.
<<<P>>>
Yemi Kɛ Buami Sikahi: Enɛ ɔ ji yemi kɛ buami sika nɛ he dooli aloo he doomi kuuhi nɛ a yeɔ kɛ buaa ma nɔ yami ni tsumi ɔ haa Amlaalo ɔ. Sika nɛ ɔmɛ ji sika kekeehi nɛ a hiaa we a se kpalemi aloo a nɔ to womi.
<<<P>>>
Yemi kɛ Buami Sika Kɛ Ha Amlaalo Ni Tsumi Hehi: E ji sika nɛ mlaa ngmɛ blɔ nɛ Amlaalo ɔ haa kpo wawi kɛ kpo ngua amɛ (DACF), Blɔhi a he sika, Ghana Sukuu Tsɔsemi Blɔ Nya Tomi Sika (GETFUND), Zu mi nu sika kɛ Ma Bi A Nɔmlɔ tso He Wami Nami Sika.
<<<P>>>
Ma a Weto ni (GDP): Enɛ ɔ ji nihi tsuo nɛ a pee ngɛ ma a mi nɛ peeɔ ma a weto ni ngɛ be kpɔ loo be pɔtɛɛ ko mi.
<<<P>>>
Ma a Mase Sika Kpɔ (GIR): Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ ma ko sika he wami nɛ e ngɛ ngɛ ma se sikahi a nɔ ngɛ nihi a hemi kɛ ni tsumihi a he hiɔ womi mi ngɛ be pɔtɛɛ ko mi.
<<<P>>>
Nihi A He Jua Wami: Nihi kɛ ni tsumihi a he jua wami ngɛ be pɔtɛɛ ko mi.
<<<P>>>
Blɔ Nɔ Tomi kɛ ha Ohia Nɔ Gbɔmi (IPEP): Enɛ ɔ ji blɔ nya tomi ehe aloo muno ko nɛ NPP Amlaalo ɔ ngɔ kɛ ba nɛ e ba gbɔ ohia nɔ nɛ ha yi sɔmi aloo pɛpɛɛpɛ peemi nɛ ba ngɛ ohiafohi kɛ niatsɛmɛ a kpeti. Titli ɔ, ngɛ ma tsɔwi ɔmɛ a mi kɛ gu he hiami nihi kaa blɔ, nyu kɛ wehi a he blɔ nya tomi mi.
<<<P>>>
Ma a Sika Blɔ Nya Tomihi Susumi Tso: Enɛ ɔ ji blɔhi a nɔ nɛ a guɔ kɛ hyɛɔ ma a sika he ni tsumi mɔdebɔ kaa e nyɛ tsu nɔ nɛ a po fɔ si kaa e tsu ɔ lo. Nihi nɛ blɔ nɔ tomi nɛ ɔ hyɛɔ ekomɛ ji; sika he ni tsumi nɔ yami, nihi a he jua wami, tsakemi slɔɔto nɛ baa ngɛ ni tsumi nami mi, jua yemi nɔ yami ngɛ ma kpahi a nɔ, Amlaalo ɔ blɔ nɔ tomi komɛ a nɔ yami kɛ a si gbeemi kɛ hɛ mi mami ni komɛ nɛ Ghana Sika To He Ngua a pee nɛ kɔ ma a sika nɔ yami he.
<<<P>>>
Ni Tsumi Tsɔwi a Blɔ Nya Tomi (MSBDP): Enɛ ɔ ji ni tsumi tsɔwi nɛ a ngɔɔ nimli bɔbɔyoo nɛ a ni tsumi se da a yɛ tsitsaa tsɔ.
<<<P>>>
Ni Tsumi Ha Yunivɛsiti Nyagbe li (NABCo): E ji blɔ nya tomi nɛ Ghana Amlaalo ɔ ngɔ kɛ fɔ si nɛ e kɛ hla ni tsumi kɛ ha nihi nɛ a gbe Yunivɛsiti nya konɛ e kɛ gbɔ ma mi himi nyagba komɛ a nɔ. Enɛ ɔ hu maa ye kɛ bua kɛ tsɔse mɛ aloo fiaa mɛ nane kɛ ha hɛ mi ga nihi a peemi nɛ e kɛ nyɛ wo aloo hue ma a sika he ni tsumi nɔ.
<<<P>>>
Ma a Ni Tsumi He nɛ Hyɛeɔ Ni Tsumi Tsɔwi A Nɔ (NBSSI): Ni tsumi kuu nɛ ɔ, e ji ma a ni tsumi he ko nɛ hyɛɔ ni tsumi tsɔwi nɛ a di ni tsumi nɔ vide se aloo a hyɛ we ni tsumi mi vide ko blɔ. Ni tsumi kuu nɛ ɔ ngɛ Amlaalo Ni Tsumi He Ngua a Nɛ Hyɛɔ Jua Yemi kɛ Nihi a Peemi kuu ɔ sisi nɛ e blɔ nya ji e hyɛ ni tsɔwi nɛ ɔmɛ a wami kɛ a nɔ yami nɔ.
