67. Benɛ 2020/21 kokoo ɔ nya kɛ ta a, kokoo tsuo nɛ a na ngɛ be nɛ a tsɔɔ nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, nya ya su 1,045,500 tɔni (Tonnes). A fɔ kokoo jua a nɔ lafa mi gbami nyingmi enyɔ kɛ kpaanyɔ (28). Enɛ ɔ hue aloo wo kokoo tɔni jua a nɔ kɛ ba da Ghana sika sidi 10,560 ngɛ 2019/20 kokoo hemi be ɔ mi. E ngɛ mi kaa kokoo jua a nɔ gbɔ ngɛ maje jua amɛ a nɔ lɛɛ se Amlaalo ɔ je we kokoo jua a nɔ ngɛ 2021/2022 kokoo hemi jeha a mi.
