37. Ghana ma a mi hue tso ɔmɛ a si tlemi blɔ nya tomi ɔ nɛ ji (The National Afforestation Programme -NAP) ɔ bli blɔ nɛ a ngɔ nihi nɛ a yibɔ ya su 72,000. Yibɔ nɛ ɔ be kekle yibɔ nɛ a po fɔ si nɛ ji 60,000 ɔ nɔ. A pee enɛ ɔ kɛ ha yemi kɛ buami nɛ a kɛ du tsohi ngɛ he slɔɔtohi. Blɔ nya tomi nɛ ɔ gbla nihi nɛ a hiɛmi su 35,000 kɛ ba wo Nihe kɛ Yihe ngɛ TSO DUMI BLƆ NYA TOMI ɔ sisi. Ekohu ɔ, e kpale ngɔ nihi nɛ a hiɛmi su 17,000 ngɛ blɔ nya tomi ko nɛ a tsɛɔ ke: “Modified Taungya System (MTS)” ɔ sisi nɛ kɛ piɛɛ he ɔ, e ngɔ nihi nɛ a hiɛmi su 2,000 kɛ wo ngmɛdu bubuii peemi mi. Ni tsumi he nɛ be Amlaalo ɔ sisi ɔ hu ngɔ nihi nɛ a hiɛmi su 18,000.

38. Ni tsumi he nɛ ngɔɔ nihe kɛ yihe kɛ woɔ ni tsumi mi nɛ ji “The Youth Employment Agency (YEA)” ɔ, ngɔ nihi nɛ a hiɛmi su 82,869 ngɛ a blɔ nɔ tomi slɔɔto ɔmɛ a sisi. Ni tsumi hlami he nɛ ji “The Job Centre” nɛ gblaa ni tsumi hlali kɛ haa ni tsumi hehi aloo ni tsumitsɛmɛ ɔ hu je blɔ fɔ si nɛ e ha nihi maa pee 1,185 na ni tsumi.

39. Ngɛ nyagba slɔɔtohi nɛ KƆVID-19 hiɔ yaya a kɛ ba a se ɔ, nɔ yami komɛ ba ngɛ kpo kake ni tsumi kake nɛ ji 1D1F blɔ nya tomi ɔ sisi. Kɛ je jeha 2017 se kɛ ba ta amlɔ nɛ ɔ, kpo kake ni tsumi kake (1D1F) blɔ nya tomi ɔ pee ni tsumi slɔɔtohi nɛ a hiɛmi su 278. Enɛ ɔ ya nɔ ngɛ he munomuno kɛ a nɔ kuɔmi hu munomuno. Ngɛ enɛ ɔmɛ a kpɛti ɔ, ni tsumi hehi nɛ a yibɔ su 106 ngɛ nane nya ngɛ ni tsue amlɔ nɛ ɔ. Ngɛ enɛ ɔmɛ a se ɔ, a je ni tsumi kaa 148 hu sisi nɛ a ngɛ a nɔ tsae be mi nɛ ni tsumi 24 komɛ lɛɛ kpaloko a maa ho a sisi jemi ya. Kɛ ba ta amlɔ nɛ ɔ, ni tsumi hehi nɛ a ngɛ ni tsue ɔ nyɛ ngɔ nihi nɛ a hiɛmi su 156, 782 pɔtɛɛ ngɛ ni tsumi mi nɛ nihi ekomɛ lɛɛ a ngɔ mɛ ngɛ “ yaa nɛ o ba” blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ nya. Ngɛ yibɔ nɛ ɔmɛ a mi ɔ, ni tsumihi nɛ a bli ngɛ 2021 jeha a mi ji 17,451.
