77. Je ɔ tsuo a he wami kɛ kake peemi nɛ a kɛ hla tsopa kɛ ha KƆVID-19 hiɔ ɔ ngɛ yiblii kpakpa woe. KƆVID-19 hapohapo tsopa a ba piɛɛ sisije blɔhi a nɔ nɛ a to kɛ ha hiɔ ɔ nya tsimi ɔ nɛ ji tsopahi a peemi, gugwɔ nya kɛ nya he bohi a womi, Katso aloo blɔ womi ngɛ nimli a kpɛ kɛ falefale peemi ni ɔmɛ a he.
<<<P>>>
78. KƆVID-19 he tsopa peemi ɔ je sisi ngɛ Maale hlami ɔ mi ngɛ 2021 jeha a mi kɛ tsopa kakaaka 600,000 nɛ ji AstraZeneca tsopa a. A je sisi bɔni tsopa nɛ ɔ kakaaka gbumi aloo simi ha nimli maa pee ayɔ 2.5 benɛ e ke su ɔ Sɛklɛ ligbi kpaanyɔne ɔ nɔ ngɛ 2021 jeha a mi. A maa gbu aloo gu hapohapo tsopa nɛ ɔ ha nihi nɛ a ye jeha 18 kɛ yaa nɛ yihɔ yihi piɛɛ we he ɔ, nɛ enɛ ɔ ngɛ nɔ yae amlɔ nɛ ɔ ngɛ kpo ngua 16 kɛ kpo wawi 261 kulaa mi ngɛ Ghana ma a tsuo mi.

79. Kɛ piɛɛ mɔde nɛ Amlaalo ɔ ngɛ bɔe kaa e ma bli dloni (drone) kɛ tsopa nɛ a he hia gbami blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ mi ɔ, e kɛ ni tsumi he nɛ ji Zipline pee kake nɛ a bli tsopa jemi he ehe enyɔ ngɛ Anum kɛ Abujuro nɛ a ngɛ Beleku kɛ Oti kpo ngua amɛ a mi ɔ. Tsopa jemi he nɛ ɔmɛ ma gba tsopa amɛ kɛ ya 366 hiɔ tsami hehi. Amlɔ nɛ ɔ, tsopa gbami he yibɔ nɛ a tsɔɔ nɛ ɔ e mi lafa mi gbami 54 lɛɛ a he sumi peeɔ dengme.

80. Amlaalo ɔ maa wo e blɔ nɔ tomi ko nɛ ji Ajɛnda 111 (Agenda 111) ɔ mi la nɛ e kɛ ma tsami he 101 ngɛ kpo wawi ɔmɛ a mi. Kɛ piɛɛ enɛ ɔmɛ a he ɔ, a maa ma tsami he nguahi kpaago (7) kɛ yi puɛmi hiɔ tsami he hu etɛ (3) ngɛ kpo ngua a ekomɛ a mi. Amlɔ nɛ ɔ, a gbe kpo wawi a tsami he mami nɔ womi 91 a peemi nya kɛ ha tsu ɔmɛ a mami sisi jemi.
