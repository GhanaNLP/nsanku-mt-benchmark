35. Afe 2023 mu no, Aban bɛgye mmoa afiri EXIM sikakorabea (EXIM bank) no hɔ nam so asisi mfidie a wɔde yɛ ɔyɛasaseyie a wɔmfaa mu no bi afoforɔ, asane atrɛtrɛ deɛ ɛwɔ hɔ dada no mu.

36. Afe 2022 mu no, Aban gyinaa dwumadie a wɔato edin Nsuomnamyɛn a ɛma Nnuane ne Nnwuma nhyehyɛeɛ (Aquaculture for Food and Jobs, AFJ) no so, hyehyɛɛ nsuomnam adekoradeɛ dodoɔ ɔha aduonu ɛnan (124 holding facilities)- nsuomnam asutaeɛ ɔha ne nwɔtwe ne nsuomnam ebuo edu nsia (108 ponds and 16 cages), maa nsuomnam aduane baage dodoɔ mpem Dunson, ahanan aduosia (17, 460,) sane de nsuomnam mma guguu akuo ne nnwuma ahodoɔ dodoɔ aduonu enum asutaeɛ ne akoradeɛ mu. Bio, yɛmaa nnipa dodoɔ ahanu aduonu ɛnan (294) nteteeɛ fa ɛkwan pa a yɛfa so yɛ kua ho, ɛnna nsuomnam akuafoɔ ahanan ne nwɔtwe (408) nso, yɛmaa wɔn nteteeɛ faa ɛkwan a yɛfa so yɛ nsuomnam aduane wɔ ɔman yi mu ho.

37. Afe 2023 mu no, Aban bɛma akuhodoɔ bi nsa aka asutaeɛ a wɔde kley na anwono dodoɔ ahasa (300 earthen ponds), nsuomnam adekoradeɛ aduanan (40 cages) tapolin tanke aduonum mmienu (52 tarpaulin tanks) na wasane de nsuomnam aduane baage dodoɔ mpem aduasa enum ( 35,000), koobi (tilapia) ne adwene mma (catfish) ɔpepem mmiɛnsa (3 million) ama wɔn.

38. Kookoo dwumadie mu no, yɛmaa mmabunu dii dwuma ahodoɔ sɛ ebia nsiesie (rehabilitation), kookoo a yɛtwa gu sane dua (replanting), kookoo so adupeteɛ (mass spraying), ɔyɛasaseyie peteɛ (fertilizer application), ne kookoo nnua dodoɔ ho twitwa (mass pruning) so maa mmabunu dodoɔ mpem ɔha aduanan nson (147,000) nyaa adwuma yɛeɛ de sii akuraase kɔ kuro kɛseɛ mu akwantuo no ano.
