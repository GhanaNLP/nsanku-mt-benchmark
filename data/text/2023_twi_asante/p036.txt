64. Aban botaeɛ a ɛne sɛ ɔbɛma sukuu ahodoɔ ne Nwomasua ɔfese nyinaa nsa aka akadeɛ dodoɔ a wɔbɛhia ama wɔn dwumadie akɔ nkan enti no, Aban tɔɔ 66 sita bɔɔs ɔha aduɔson enum (66-seater Buses), Pick-Up ahyɛn aduɔson baako (71 Pick-Ups) ne trakta mmienu (2 Tractors) kyekyɛ maa Ntoasoɔ sukuu (Senior High Schools) ne Nsaanodwumasua ne Nteteeɛ sukuu ahodoɔ (Technical and Vocational Education and Training Institutes (a wɔwɔ Ɔman yi mu no.

65. Afe 2022 mu no, Aban faa Mpɔtam Mpontuo ne Nsaanodwumasua sukuu ahodoɔ aduonu nkron so ( 29 Community Development and Vocational Technical Institutes) tetee mmarima mpem mmiɛnsa ahanson ne aduonu ɛnan (3,724) ne mmaa mpem mmiɛnsa ahanson ne dunum (3,715) wɔ nsaanodwuma mu sɛdeɛ wɔn nimdeɛ bɛkɔ nkan ama wɔanya nnwuma ayɛ anaa abuebue wɔn ara wɔn nnwuma afa so abrɛ ohia ase.
<<<P>>>
66. Bio, yɛtetee mpɔtam adekyerɛfoɔ dodoɔ ahanu aduanan nwɔtwe (248 community educators) wɔ mpɔtam mpontuo ne Asetena mu banbɔ ho nimdeɛ mu wɔ Kwaso Mpɔtam Mpontuo kɔlegyi hɔ sɛdeɛ wɔbɛnya nimdeɛ de aboaboa mpɔtamfoɔ ano ama wɔatu mpontuo ho anammɔn.
