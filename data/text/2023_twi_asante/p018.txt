<<<P>>>
Sikanya Ho Nhyehyɛeɛ
<<<P>>>
18. Sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na sikanya ho anammɔntuo no ase bɛtim enti, Aban de sikanya ho nhyehyɛeɛ a ano ɛboɔ som ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ nyinaa ɔha mu nkyɛmu baako ne akyiri pɔ aduasa enum (1.35 percent of GDP) bɛba abɛto Mmarahyɛbadwa yi anim ama moadwennwene ho, agye atom. Nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ no mu bi na ɛdidi soɔ yi:
<<<P>>>
• Bɛsakra sigarɛte ne tawa adwadeɛ ho ɛtoɔ mu ama ɛne ECOWAS ɛtoɔ ho nhyehyɛeɛ asiae so, na yɛapagya nsaden ho ɛtoɔ mu ama aboro bia (beer) ahodoɔ deɛ so.
<<<P>>>
• Bɛpagya adwadeɛ ho ɛtoɔ (VAT) no mu afiri ɔha nkyɛmu dumienu ne ɛfa de akɔ ɔha nkyɛmu dunum na yɛatrɛ nneɛma a yɛgye ɛho Adwadeɛ ɛtoɔ(VAT) no mu, asane atu anammɔn ahodoɔ afa adwadeɛ a ɛnni sɛ yɛgye ɛho adwadeɛ ho ɛtoɔ (VAT) no ho.
<<<P>>>
• Bɛsesa Ɔman Sikasɛm Nsiesie Ho Ɛtoɔ (National Fiscal Stabilisation Levy,NFSL) no ayɛ no Mpontuo ne Nsiesie ho ɛtoɔ (Growth and Sustainability Levy,GSL)ama adi nneɛma ahodoɔ no nyinaa ho dwuma.
<<<P>>>
• Bɛte Abɛɛfo afidie so sikamane ho ɛtoɔ (Electronic Transfer Levy) no so afiri ɔha nkyɛmu baako akyiri pɔ enum (1.5) akɔduru ɔha nkyɛmu baako (1) na yɛayi daadaa sikateɛ no afiri hɔ.
<<<P>>>
• Nhyehyɛeɛ afoforɔ nso na ɛdidi soɔ yi:
<<<P>>>
○ Bɛtɔn deɛ wɔfrɛ no 5G Electromagnetic Spectrum no;
<<<P>>>
○ Bɛhwɛ ama ɛdanhane ho ɛtoɔtua adi mu;
<<<P>>>
○ Bɛtu anammɔn afa fangotubea (“Jubilee Field”) no ho nam so anya fango ho akwannya afoforɔ (Additional Oil Entitlement) (AEO) ; na afei
