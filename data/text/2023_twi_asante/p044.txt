“Yɛreka abom adi dwuma de Mpontuo ne Adwadeɛyɛ asane ama ahoɔden aba Ɔman Sikasɛm u na yɛama aseɛ atim”
<<<P>>>
Sika A Aka Mu Wɔ Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ mu (Budget deficit): Yei si wɔ ɛberɛ a sika a Aban de bɛdi dwuma no kɔ anim sene deɛ Aban nsa aka no ankasa.
<<<P>>>
Asɛ Ɔkraman (Canine): Deɛ ɛfa ɔkraman ho anaa ɛsɛ ɔkraman.
<<<P>>>
Mpontunnwuma ho Sika (Capital Expenditure): Yei yɛ sika a yɛde yɛ mpontunnwuma sɛ ebia akwanyie, sukuudansie, ayaresabea dansie, atwene-sie, akwantusɛm, nsuo, mfidie akɛseɛ ne deɛ ɛkeka ho.
<<<P>>>
Abɔnta Lɔre Ɛkwan (Carriageway): Ɛyɛ lɔre ɛkwan no ɔfa a ahyɛn tumi fa so.
<<<P>>>
Adwumayɛfoɔ akatua (Compensation of Employees): Yei yɛ adwumayɛfoɔ bosome akatua, Sika ahodoɔ, asetenam ahotosoɔ sika, akwankɔgya sika ne deɛ ɛkeka ho a wɔtua ma Aban adwumayɛfoɔ.
<<<P>>>
Onipadua mu nhwehwɛmu (Endoscopy): Iyei yɛ nhwehwɛmu bi a wɔyɛ wɔ onipadua no mu.
<<<P>>>
Ɛka ne Ɔman mu Adwadeɛ Nyinaa Ntam Ntotoho (Debt-to-GDP ratio): Yei yɛ sikasɛm mu gyinaberɛ a ɛda adi wɔ ɛberɛ a yɛde ɔman bi ɛka a ɔde no atoto ɔman no mu adwadeɛ nyinaa bo a ɛsom no ho na yɛahunu sɛdeɛ ɔman no gyinaberɛ no teɛ.
<<<P>>>
Biribi bo a ɛsom so-teɛ (Depreciation): Yei yɛ sɛdeɛ agyapadeɛ bi bo a ɛsom no so teɛ wɔ ɛberɛ pɔtee bi mu anaa sɛ sɛdeɛ sɛ wode ɔman bi sika toto aman afoforɔ deɛ ho a, emu duru no wɔ fam.
<<<P>>>
Mpontuo mu Aboafoɔ (Development Partners): Yei gyina hɔ ma Aman anaa nnwumakuo a wɔboa Aman a afei na wɔretu mpɔn ma wɔduru wɔn mpontuo botaeɛ ho. Yei bɛtumi ayɛ akuhodoɔ bi te sɛ Amansan Fotoɔ Adwumakuo (International Monetary Fund- IMF),Ewiase Sikakorabea (World Bank), Abibirem Mpontuo Sikakorabea (African Development Bank) ne deɛ ɛkeka ho. !tumi nso yɛ ankorankorɛ aman bi te sɛ Germany, Nyiresi Aburokyire, Amɛrika, ne wɔn a wɔkeka ho.
