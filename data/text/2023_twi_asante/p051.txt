Adwadeɛ ne Dwumadie ho sika (Use of Goods and Services): Yei yɛ sika dodoɔ a Aban tua wɔ dwuma a ɔdi ne dwumadie a ɔdi ma ɔmanfoɔ ho ho.
<<<P>>>
Adwadeɛ ho ɛtoɔ (Value Added Tax (VAT): Yei yɛ ɛtoɔ bi a yɛde to adwadeɛ ahodoɔ so wɔ adwadeɛyɛ mpɛmpɛnsoɔ biara mu.
<<<P>>>
Mmɔborɔwafoɔ Aduonu (V20 - Vulnerable Twenty): Yeinom yɛ aman bi a wɔsusu sɛ ewiem nsakraeɛ ho nsuansuansoɔ a ano yɛ den no ka wɔn pa ara.
<<<P>>>
Ɔman nkorabata (Subsector) – Ɛgyina hɔ ma ɔman no asoɛeɛ ahodoɔ no mu baako a dwumadie ahodoɔ kɔ so wɔ hɔ.
<<<P>>>
Wo-Deɛ-Hyɛ-Aseɛ (YouStart): Ɛyɛ ɛkwan a Aban pɛ sɛ ɔfa so boa mmabunu a wɔadi firi mfeɛ 18 kɔ 40 wɔ sikasɛm ne mfidie ho ma wɔde hyɛ wɔn nnwuma ase anaa sɛ nnwuma a ɛyɛ mmabunu dea na ɛnam so ama wɔahyɛ aseɛ na wɔatumi atrɛ wɔn ankasa nnwuma mu.
<<<P>>>
Mmabunu Adwumanya ho Adwumakuo (Youth Employment Agency - YEA): Wɔnam mmara a ɛtɔ so ahanwɔtwe ne aduɔwɔtwe nson a ɛyɛ Mmabunu Adwumanya ho Mmara a wɔhyɛɛ no afe 2015 mu (Youth Employment Act 2015 - Act 887) no so buee Mmabunu Adwumanya ho Adwumakuo yi sɛdeɛ ɛbɛhwɛ ahyehyɛ, atoto, ahwɛ na waboa wɔ mmabunu adwumanya ne nsɛm a ɛfa wɔn ho wɔ Ghanaman mu.
