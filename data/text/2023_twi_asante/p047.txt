Edwa-so Nneɛma-boɔ-sorokɔ (Headline Inflation): Yei yɛ nneɛma-boɔ-sorokɔ ho amaneɛbɔ a ɛfiri Adetɔfoɔ Nsusuiɛ a ɛfa nneɛma boɔ ho a Adwumayɛfoɔ ho Nkontabuo Ɔfese (Bureau of Labor Statistics) de to edwa bosome biara.
<<<P>>>
Ɔman Mu Adwadeɛyɛ (Import Substitution): Yei yɛ ɛkwan a yɛfa so yɛ adwadeɛ wɔ ɔman yi ara mu de hyɛ adwadeɛ ahodoɔ bi a yɛkra firi amannɔne no anan mu na mmom ɛnyɛ sɛ yɛbɛyɛ adwadeɛ de akɔ amannɔne akɔtɔn na ɛnam saa ɔman yi ara mu adwadeɛyɛ yi so ma ɔman yi mu mfidinnwuma tu mpɔn.
<<<P>>>
Nneɛma boɔ-sorokɔ (Inflation): Yei gyina hɔ ma sɛdeɛ dwumadie ahodoɔ ne adwadeɛ boɔ si kɔ soro wɔ ɛberɛ pɔtee bi mu.
<<<P>>>
Nsiho ho Eka (Interest cost): Yei yɛ bosea ho nsiho dodoɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ ɔkafoɔ tua wɔ bosea a wabɔ ho anaa biribi a ɔde ho ɛka wɔ ɛberɛ a ɔntuaa sika botene no ankasa nwieeɛ.
<<<P>>>
Nsiho-tua (Interest payments): Yei yɛ bosea anaa agyapadeɛ bi ho nsiho a ɔkafoɔ anaa deɛ wafiri agyapadeɛ no tua.
<<<P>>>
Mpontunneɛ A Yɛde Bɛtu Ohia Ase Dwumadie - IPEP (Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme): Mpontunneɛ A Yɛde Bɛtu Ohia Ase Dwumadie yi yɛ dwumadie ahodoɔ a ɛdi akotene wɔ NPP Aban nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ mu no baako a botaeɛ a ɛtaa akyire ne sɛ, yɛde mpontunneɛ bɛkɔ nkuraase, titire mpɔtam ahodoɔ a ohia wɔ hɔ no na ɛnam so aboa atu ohia ase na asetenam yiedie mu nsonsonoeɛ a ɛda nkuraasefoɔ ne kuromfoɔ ntam no asesa.
<<<P>>>
Ɔman Sikasɛm mu Mpontuo Nnyinasoɔ anaa Nneɛma a ɛboa ma ɔman sikasɛm gyina a ɛsi ani so (Macroeconomic targets): Yeinom yɛ susudua a wɔgyina so kyerɛ ɔman bi sikasɛm ho mmɔdenmmɔ. Yei ho nhwɛsoɔ ne sikasɛm mpontuo, nneɛmaboɔ sorokɔ, adwumayɛ mu mpontuo mpɛmpɛnsoɔ, ɔman yi ne aman afoforɔ ntam edwadie mu mmɔdemmɔ, sɛdeɛ aban no sikasɛm ntotoeɛ kankorɔ anaa sintɔ teɛ ene sɛdeɛ gyinaeɛ ahodoɔ a Ghana Sikakorabea Baatan bɛsie no bɛka nneɛma boɔ sorokɔ wɔ ɔman no mu.
