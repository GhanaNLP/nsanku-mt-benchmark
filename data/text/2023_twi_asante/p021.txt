<<<P>>>
22. Ani da ɛkwan sɛ sika dodoɔ a yɛde bɛdi dwuma (a ɛka a yɛbɛtua ka ho bi) bɛyɛ Ghana sidi ɔpepem ahanu ne enum ahanan aduasa baako (GHC205,431) a ɛyɛ ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ nyinaa bo a ɛsom mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduonu enum akyiri pɔ nsia. Sɛ yɛde deɛ na ani da ɛkwan sɛ yɛbɛnya wɔ afe 2022 mu no toto ho a, nkontabuo yi da no adi sɛ sika a yɛde dii dwuma (primary ependiture) so tee wɔ ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ nyinaa bo a ɛsom ho ɔha nkyekyɛmu hwee akyiri pɔ mmiɛnsa (deɛ ɛyɛ asɛdeɛ) na yei da Aban krado a wayɛ sɛ ɔbɛtu ɔman yi sikasɛm ho anammɔn pa ama agyina yie no adi. Afe 2023 sika a yɛde bɛdi dwuma no ho nkontabuo na yɛabɔ no tɔfa wɔ hyehyɛpono 5 so no.
