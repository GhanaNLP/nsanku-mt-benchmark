Nteaseɛ Apam (Memorandum of Understanding - MoU): Ɛyɛ krataa so nnyetom a ɛfa gyinaeɛsie bi ho anammɔntuo bi ho a afanu anaa nnipa a wɔboro saa agye atom sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wɔde di dwumat.
<<<P>>>
Nkorabata/Ɔfa (Module): Ɛyɛ biribi nkorabata a yɛtumi keka bom ma ɛyɛ adekorɔ no kɛseɛ.
<<<P>>>
Sikasɛm ho Anammɔntuo Nhyehyɛeɛ (Monetary policy): Ɛyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ a ɔman sikasɛm so adwumakuo baatan no fa so toto boseabɔ ho nsiho wɔ ɛberɛ tiawa bi mu anaa sɛdeɛ ɔde sika foforɔ bɛpue edwa, nsiho dodoɔ nyinaa ho nsɛm sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a ɛbɛboa ama nneɛma boɔ agyina faako.
<<<P>>>
Nnwuma Nketewa Mu Mpontuo Dwumadie - MSME (Micro And Small Buisness Developmet Programme): Nnwuma Nketewa yɛ ankorankorɛ nnwuma a wɔtumi fa nnipa kakra bi a dwetire a adwuma no gyina soɔ no mmoro sika dodoɔ pɔtee bi so.
<<<P>>>
Ɔman-kyekyere Atuhoakyɛfoɔ- NABCo (Nation Builders Corps): Yei yɛ adwene foforɔ koraa a Ghana Aban de puee edwa a ɔnam so bɛma mmeranteɛ ne mmabaawa a wɔawie asuapɔn no bɛnya adwuma ayɛ wɔ ankorankorɛ anaa Aban nnwuma mu na wɔaboa de wɔn nimdeɛ apɛ asetenam ɔhaw ahodoɔ nnannuaguberɛ, na yei aboa wɔn ma wɔanya osuahunu a ɛbɛboa Ɔman sikasɛm mpontuo.
<<<P>>>
Ɔman Adwumakuo A Ɛhwɛ Mfidinnwuma Nketwa So- (National Board for Small Scale Industries- NBSSI): Ɔman Adwumakuo a Ɛhwɛ Mfidinnwuma Nketewa so no yɛ ɔman adwumakuo a ɛnyɛ mfasoɔ enti na wɔwɔ hɔ na mmom, ɛyɛ adwumakuo a ɛhyɛ Asoɛeɛ a Ɛhwɛ Edwadie ne Mfidinnwuma so no ase, a mmara ma wɔn ho ɛkwan sɛ wɔnhwɛ sɛ Mfidinnwuma Nketewa ne Adantam (Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) bɛtu mpɔn.
<<<P>>>
Ɔman Nnuadua Dwumadie (National Afforestation Programme-NAP): Ɛyɛ kwaeɛ a yɛatɔre aseɛ no ananmuhyɛ sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a kwaeɛ mu nnepa a ahwere yɛn no bɛsane aba na ɛnam so ama mpɔtam ahodoɔ a wɔtete bɛn kwaeɛ, titire ahiafoɔ no asetenam atu mpɔn.
