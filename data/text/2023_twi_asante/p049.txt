“Yɛreka abom adi dwuma de Mpontuo ne Adwadeɛyɛ asane ama ahoɔden aba Ɔman Sikasɛm u na yɛama aseɛ atim”
<<<P>>>
Ɔman Atoyerɛnkyɛm ho Ntotoeɛ Adwumakuo (National Disaster Management Organisation - NADMO): Yei yɛ ɔman adwumakuo a ɛhwɛ toto atoyerɛnkyɛm ne mpofirim asianeɛ ahodoɔ ho nsɛm wɔ ɔman Ghana mu.
<<<P>>>
Ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ bo a ɛsom a Fango Sika nka ho (Non-Oil Real GDP): Yei yɛ ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ nyinaa bo ɛsom a sika a ɛfiri fango ho dwumadie ahodoɔ mu nka ho bi, a wɔagyina nneɛma boɔ soroko so no asese.
<<<P>>>
Ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ bo a ɛsom kankorɔ a Fango Sika nka ho (Non-Oil Real GDP Growth): Ɛyɛ nnyinasoɔ a yɛhwɛ so de hunu ɔman sikasɛm mu mpontuo wɔ ɔman no nkorabata biara a fango sika nka ho bie.
<<<P>>>
Sika a ɛmfiri ɛtoɔ mu (Non Tax Revenue): Yei yɛ Aban sika a ɔnya a ɛmfiri ɛtoɔ mu. Ebi ne sika a obi tua ansa na wanya tumi krataa bi adi dwuma ne sika a yɛtua wɔ nneɛma afoforɔ ho sɛ ebia nsiho ne mfantum ho.
<<<P>>>
Ɛka Ahodoɔ A Aban De (Outstanding Obligations): Ɛka ahodoɔ a Aban de no yɛ sika a ɛsɛ sɛ Asoɛeɛ a Ɛhwɛ Sikasɛm so no tua ma Aban Nnwuma ahodoɔ no ɛnna afei, ɛka ɛda hɔ firi afe 2016 mu a mmara hyɛ sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ Aban tua gu Mmara ho Fotoɔ ahodoɔ mu.
<<<P>>>
Nsunsuansoɔ (Outturn): Yei yɛ deɛ ɛsi wɔ tebea bi akyi anaa sɛ tebea ahodoɔ bi akyi.
<<<P>>>
Sika Dodoɔ A Aka A Yɛbɛhia De Adi Dwuma (Primary balance): Yei yɛ sika dodoɔ a aka wɔ ɛberɛ a Aban agyegye deɛ n'ani da ɛkwan sɛ ɔbɛnya no nyinaa akyi de adi dwuma, a bosea ho nsiho nka ho bie, (kyerɛ sɛ bosea a Aban bɛtua nka ho). Na ɛkwan a yɛfa so bu yei ho nkonta ne sɛ yɛsese no wɔ Ɔman Adwadeɛ nyinaa bo a ɛsom no mu ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu.
<<<P>>>
Sika a yɛadi aboro deɛ yɛwɔ so (Primary surplus - deficit): Yei kyerɛ sikasɛm mpɛmpɛnsoɔ a ɛgyina hɔ ma sika a yɛadi aboro deɛ yɛwɔ so a yɛagyegyeɛ afiri ɛtoɔ mu a, nsiho a yɛatua wɔ ɛka a ɔman no de nka ho.
<<<P>>>
Sikasɛm Mpontuo a ɛsi ani soɔ (Projected Growth Rate): Yei yɛ sɛdeɛ ani da ɛkwan sɛ Ghana sikasɛm bɛtu mpɔn wɔ afe pɔtee bi mu.
