85. Aban faa aduanenoafoɔ (caterers) ne aduanenoa aboafoɔ (cooks) mmaa mpem aduasa mmienu, ahanan aduɔkron nsia (32,496 caterers and cooks) sɛdeɛ wɔn sikasɛm bɛyɛ yie ama wɔatumi ahwɛ wɔn abusua. Bio Aban buee mfudeɛtɔ ho akwannya maa akuafoɔ nam so maa wɔn sikasɛm nyaa nnyinasoɔ.

86. Sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na botaeɛ a ɛne sɛ yɛbɛma onibiefoɔ aduane a nnuanennuro ahyɛ mu ma enti no, Aban tuaa Asetena mu Nkuranhyɛ a ɛko Ɛtia Ohia (Livelihood Empowerment Against Poverty (LEAP) ho sika no mmɔho enum maa fidua ahodoɔ mpem mmiɛnsa aduanan ɛnan, ahasa aduɔwɔtwe nkron (344,389).
