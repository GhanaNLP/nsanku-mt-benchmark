EDWUMA A ASƐNKA KUW DO HWƐFO NYE NO BOAFO YƐ
<<<P>>>
4 Asɛnka kuw do hwɛfo yɛ asafo mu panyin* bi a mpanyimfo kuw no eyi no dɛ ɔmboa enuanom a wɔwɔ n’asɛnka kuw no mu no ma wɔnyer hɔnho nsom Jehovah. Ɔyɛ no dɛn? Odzi kan, ɔma enuanom a wɔwɔ no kuw no mu no hu dɛ ɔdwen hɔn ho paa. (Mbɛ. 27:23) Ɔma n’enyi ku dɛm enuanom yi hɔn ho na ɔtaa nye hɔn bɔ nkɔmbɔ hwɛ mbrɛ ndzɛmba rokɔ wɔ hɔn abrabɔ mu. Iyi ma otum boa hɔn ma wɔkɔ do som Jehovah, na ɔma otum hu hɔn a wɔrefa atseetsee mu no na ɔyɛ ndzɛmba ahorow bi dze boa hɔn. (Mbɛ. 12:25; Isa. 32:2; Jas. 2:15-17) Otsia ebien, ɔboa enuanom a wɔwɔ no kuw no mu no ma wɔyer hɔn ho yɛ asɛnka edwuma no. Ɔtaa nye enuanom a wɔwɔ n’asɛnka kuw no mu no kɔ asɛnka, nkanka Memenda na Kwesida. Iyi ma otum hyɛ enuanom no nkuran, na ɔboa hɔn so ma wotum kyerɛkyerɛ yie wɔ asɛnka mu. Sɛ biribi ntsi orunntum nnkɔ asɛnka a, ɔma no boafo anaa onua fofor a ɔfata kɔhwɛ asɛnka nhyiamu no do ama enuanom no etum akɔ asɛnka. Otsia ebiasa, ɔboa enuanom mbarimba a woenuma a wɔwɔ no kuw no mu no ma wotum dze dwumadzi ahorow hyehyɛ hɔn nsa wɔ asafo no
<<<P>>>
* Sɛ asafo mu mpanyimfo ho nndɔɔ so a, wobotum ama ɔsomfo bi a ɔbɔ noho mbɔdzen ahwɛ kuw bi do kesi dɛ wobenya ɔpanyin bi a obotum ahwɛ kuw no do. Asafo mu somfo a wɔma ɔhwɛ asɛnka kuw bi do no, wɔfrɛ no kuw somfo osiandɛ ɔnnyɛ asafo mu panyin. Odzi akwankyerɛ biara a mpanyimfo dze bɛma no no ekyir.

4. (a) Asɛnka kuw do ahwɛfo no, hɔn edwuma ankasa nye dɛn? (b) Ebɛnadze na asɛnka kuw do hwɛfo yɛ dze boa enuanom a wɔwɔ no kuw no mu no? (Hwɛ adakaba a wɔato dzin “Nyansahyɛ A Obotum Aboa Kuw Do Ahwɛfo.”)
<<<P>>>
Nyansahyɛ A Obotum Aboa Kuw Do Ahwɛfo
<<<P>>>
• Mma mma asɛnka nhyiamu no nnyɛ tsen-tsenntsen, wɔakyɛr araa minitsi esuon pɛ, ama enuanom no etum ahyɛ asɛnka ase ntsɛm.
<<<P>>>
• Bɔ mbɔdzen nye obiara a ɔwɔ wo kuw no mu no nkɔ asɛnka. Hɔn a biribi ntsi wo-nntum mmba asɛnka nhyiamu no so ka ho. Ibotum nye enuanom a wɔtse dɛm no akɔ asɛnka ber fofor anaa ewimbir.
<<<P>>>
• Taa serasera enuanom a wɔwɔ wo kuw no mu no, na hyɛ hɔn nkuran. Ibotum nye hɔn edzi nkɔmbɔ wɔ hɔn efie, Ahendzi Asa do, anaa beebi fofor biara a obeye ama hɔn. Ibotum so afrɛ hɔn wɔ fon do anaa akyerɛw hɔn krataa dze ahyɛ hɔn nkuran.
<<<P>>>
• Tsetse enuanom mbarimba no, na boa hɔn ma wɔnyer hɔnho wɔ Jehovah no som mu, ama woetum dze dwumadzi ahorow ahye-hyɛ hɔn nsa.
<<<P>>>
• Sɛ wo kuw na ekuw afofor robɔ mu akɔ asɛnka mpo a, ɔsɛ dɛ ehwɛ hu dɛ wo kuw no munyi biara enya obi a ɔnye no bɔkɔ.
