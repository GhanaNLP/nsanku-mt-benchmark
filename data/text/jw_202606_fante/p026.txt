Medze Bɛyɛ Mfe 70 Asom Jehovah Wɔ Cuba
<<<P>>>
GUSTAVO JOSEPH NANKASA N’ANOMU ASƐM
<<<P>>>
WƆWOO me 1947 wɔ Caribbean supɔw fɛfɛɛfɛw bi a wɔfrɛ no Cuba do. Cuba supɔw no hyɛ Caribbean Po no nye Atlantic Po no ne finimfin. Yɛyɛ enuanom baasa; emi nye panyin, na beenu a aka no yɛ mbaa. Nna yɛnye hɛn awofo nyina tse ekuraase bi a wɔfrɛ no Esmeralda.
<<<P>>>
Mekaa paa dɛ nna hɛn ekuraase hɔ dwe paa. Nna yɛtse bɛn hɛn ebusuafo binom. Nna edziban nnyɛ na koraa, na dza yehia biara so, nna hɛn nsa ka ntsi nna ɔma hɛn enyi gye.
<<<P>>>
Aber a midzii bɛyɛ mfe enum no, onua bi a wɔfrɛ no Walton Jones hyɛɛ ase nye m’awofo suaa Bible no. Nna ɔmmfa n’asɛnka nndzi agor koraa; nna ɔdze bɛyɛ ndɔnhwer du nantsew twa kwan ba hɛn ekuraase hɔ bɛka asɛmpa no. Sɛ onya ara bodur a, nna yɛnye hɛn ebusuafo beberee kehyia mu wɔ hɛn nanabanyin na hɛn nanabasia hɔn fie hɔ, na otum nye hɛn bɔ Bible mu nkɔmbɔ ndɔnhwer pii. M’awofo na me papa kakraba bi a wɔfrɛ no Pedro, nye me aunt bi a wɔfrɛ no Ela enyi gyee dza nna worusua no ho. Ntsi annkyɛr koraa na wɔmaa wonumaa hɔn na wɔbɛyɛɛ Jehovah Adasefo. Merekasa yi, me aunt no wɔ Cuba ha na aka kakra ma oedzi mfe 100, na ɔda ho ara yɛ ɔkwampaafo.
<<<P>>>
Aber a m’awofo bɛgyee nokwar no, nna Cuba aban no mmfaa ne nsa nntoo Jehovah Adasefo hɔn dwumadzi do, ntsi nna yetum kɔ asɛnka na adzesua. Nna nkorɔfo nyim hɛn yie paa wɔ Cuba man no mu, osiandɛ nna yɛkɔ nkorɔfo hɔn efie kɛka asɛmpa no. Na aber biara a wobohu hɛn no, nna yekitsakitsa hɛn briefcase nye hɛn baag, na nna asafo nwoma ayɛ mu ma twom. Na nantsew so dze, nna yɛfa no kwa! Nna dɛm mber no yɛ ‘mber pa,’ na sɛ mekaa mbrɛ yesii yeer hɛnho soom Jehovah no a, ɔma m’enyi gye paa. Naaso, nna ‘mber bɔn’ da hɛn enyim rotweɔn hɛn.—2 Tim. 4:2.

WƆHYƐƐ ASE DƐ WƆREHAW HƐN WƆ CUBA
<<<P>>>
Midzii bɛyɛ mfe enum no ara so, me papa nye no nua kakraba Pedro kɔr ehyiadzi bi wɔ kurow bi do wɔ supɔw no ara do. Awerɛhosɛm nye dɛ, wɔkɔree no wɔkɔnoom nsu bi ma wonyaa typhoid fever. Yarba no dzeen me papa kakraba Pedro araa ma ne tsinhwi nyina tutui. Naaso me papa dze, yarba no kum no. Dɛm aber no, nna oedzi mfe 32 pɛr.
<<<P>>>
Me papa no wu ekyir no, me maame maa yetui kɛtsenaa no nuabanyin nkyɛn wɔ ekuraase bi a wɔfrɛ no Lombillo. Nna yɛpɛ hɛn nanabanyin na hɛn nanabasia hɔn asɛm paa, naaso yegyaa wɔnye me papa n’ebusuafo no nyina hɔ. Aber a yɛkɛtsenaa hɛn wɔfa ne nkyɛn no, yɛkɔr do ara soom Jehovah.
<<<P>>>
Wonumaa me August 26, 1957 wɔ nsutaae bi a ɔbɛn Lombillo mu. Nna medzi mfe du. Mfe ebien mpo enndu, Jehovah Adasefo a nna yɛwɔ Cuba no
