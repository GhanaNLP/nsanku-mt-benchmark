8 Sɛ inya ara nya adwen dɛ ɔnnyɛ Jehovah na ɔbɔɔ ndzɛmba nyina a, mma nntsena-tsena ho, yɛ ho biribi ntsɛmara. Sɛ eken-kan hɛn mbuukuu ahorow no bi a, ibe-nya dase pii a ɔbɔboa wo ma agye edzi paa dɛ Jehovah na ɔbɔɔ biribiara. Ibenya dɛm dase ahorow no bi wɔ Was Life Created? nye The Origin of Life—Five Questions Worth As-king nwomaba no mu. Ibotum so enya bi wɔ nsɛm nye video a ɔba toatoa do a wɔato dzin “Aso Wɔbɔe?” a ɔwɔ jw.org do no mu. Jessica yɛ akyerɛbaa bi a ɔwɔ United Sta-tes. Ɔkaa dɛ: “Aber a mekenkaan Origin of Life nwomaba no wiei no, mebɛgyee dzii dɛ, ampa Ɔbɔadze bi a onyim nyansa wɔ hɔ. Nna minyim dada dɛ Ɔbɔadze bi wɔ hɔ, naa-so aber a mekenkaan nwomaba no wiei no, ɔmaa mebɛgyee dzii paa.” Enuanom bebe-ree so akenkan Is There a Creator Who Cares About You? buukuu no, na aboa hɔn ma wɔa-gye edzi paa dɛ Ɔbɔadze bi wɔ hɔ. Bi a woa-ra so akenkan dɛm buukuu yi da, naaso sɛ esan kenkan no bio a, ɔbɔboa wo paa.

11 Ebɛnadze na ɔma yɛgye dzi paa dɛ Jeho-vah na ɔmaa wɔkyerɛɛw Bible no? Dza ɔma yɛgye dzi no dze, ɔdɔɔ so. Bi nye dɛ, nsɛm ahorow a Bible no ka dɛ osisii wɔ tsetse mber mu no nyina yɛ nokwar. (Luke 3:1, 2) Afei so, dza ɔka fa hɛn asaase yi ho no, dɛm pɛpɛɛpɛr na ɔtse. (Job 26:7) Satan ayɛ dza obotum biara dɛ nkyɛ ɔremma nkorɔfo nsa nnka Bible no bi. Naaso ndɛ, wɔakyerɛ Bible no ase akɔ kasa ahorow pii mu, ntsi beebi a ebɛfa biara no, ibohu bi wɔ hɔ. Mbarimba bɛyɛ 40 na wɔkyerɛɛw Bible no, na odzii bɛyɛ mfe 1,600 ansaana wɔrekyerɛw ewie, naaso no mu nsɛm no hyia pɛpɛɛpɛr. Wɔ-kyerɛɛw Bible no nnyɛ ndɛ, naaso afotu-sɛm a ɔwɔ mu no da ho ara roboa nkorɔfo wɔ hɔn abrabɔ mu. (Mbɛ. 13:20; 15:21; Matt. 7:12) Sɛ yɛdze dza yɛaka yi nyina to nkyɛn a, nkɔm ahorow a ɔwɔ Bible no mu beberee aba mu. (Josh. 23:14) Iyi nyina ma yehu dɛ, Nyankopɔn na ɔmaa wɔkyerɛɛw Bible no.
