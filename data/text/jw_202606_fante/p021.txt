Dza Obotum Aboa Wo Ma Etsie Asɛm Yie Wɔ Adzesua Ase
<<<P>>>
Wobisaa enuanom bi a wɔdze mfe pii asom Jehovah no dɛ, “Ebɛnadze na ɔboa wo ma itsie asɛm yie wɔ adzesua ase?” Dza wɔkae nye yi:
<<<P>>>
• Akyerɛbaa Suefawn a wonumaa no afe 1986 no kaa dɛ, “Sɛ ekɔ adzesua a, kɛtsena enyim ama etum dze w’adwen esi ɔkasafo no do na ehu vi-deo a wobeyi no so yie.”
<<<P>>>
• Wonumaa onua bi a wɔfrɛ no David wɔ afe 1983 mu. Ɔno so kaa dɛ: “Meserɛ Jehovah mpɛn pii dɛ ɔmboa me ma muntum mfa m’adwen ndzi dwumadzi no ekyir. Mema ɔtsena m’adwen mu dɛ, adzesua a mobɔkɔ, dwumadzi no a mibetsie no yie nye mbuae a mebɛma no nyina ka ɔsom a medze rema Jehovah no ho.”
<<<P>>>
• Onua bi a wɔfrɛ no Nicolas a wonumaa no wɔ 1982 no kaa dɛ: “Fa si w’enyi do dɛ, ebɛkam-fo obiara a ɔbɛyɛ assignment no. Ntsi gyen w’e-nyi hwɛ kwan a ɔfaa do yɛɛ no dwumadzi no, na tsie asɛm bi a ɔkyerɛkyerɛɛ mu yie anaa mfato-ho bi a ɔdze yɛɛ edwuma na gyina do fa kamfo no.”
<<<P>>>
• Wonumaa onua bi a wɔfrɛ no Augustin wɔ 1988 mu. Ɔno so kaa dɛ, “Da biara meka dɛ sɛ ako-waa nokwafo no ma yɛsan sua biribi a yeesua no dada no bio a, ɔma muhu dɛ ɔyɛ biribi a ye-hia.”
