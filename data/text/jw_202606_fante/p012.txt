Sɛ obi yɛ nyɛnko pa a, ohu dɛ obi refa yaw mu a, ɔkyekye no werɛ, ɔda ayamuyie edzi kyerɛ no na onya ayamuhyehye ma no
(Hwɛ nkyekyɛmu 19)
<<<P>>>
Jesus hun dɛ n’esuafo beenu yi gye no dzi. Ntsi hɛn so, sɛ yɛdze hɛn adwen si suban pa a hɛn nuanom no wɔ no do a, nna yerusuasua Jesus na ne papa Jehovah.
<<<P>>>
15 Yebotum aserɛ Jehovah dɛ, ɔmboa hɛn ma yɛntum mfa enuanom a ɔsor hɔn suban no nyɛnko. Afei hwɛ ndzɛmba ahorow bi a sɛ enuanom yɛ a obotum ahyɛ wo ebufuw, na serɛ Jehovah dɛ ɔmboa wo ama sɛ biribi a ɔtse dɛm si a, enya hɔnho abotar. Mma mma wo werɛ mmfir dɛ Jehovah na ɔbɔɔ hɛn, ntsi onyim dɛ ɔsor hɛn mu biara na no suban. Ntsi sɛ ebɔ mpaa a, nya awerɛhyɛmu dɛ Jehovah nyim dza obotum aboa wo ma akɔ do ara enya wo nuanom ho abotar. Sɛ ihu dɛ adze a wo nua bi reyɛ no rehyɛ wo ebufuw a, serɛ Jehovah dɛ ɔmfa no sunsum krɔnkrɔn no mboa wo amma annyɛ adze bi a ekyir yi ibonu woho. —Luke 11:13; Gal. 5:22, 23.

ABER A HƐN NUANOM RUHU AMANDZE
<<<P>>>
16 Ebɛnadze ntsi na obotum asɛɛ hom ntamu? Sɛ hɛn nuanom reberɛ anaa wɔrefa ɔhaw bi mu a, bi a wɔbɛyɛ biribi ma ahaw hɛn. Bi a wɔ-bɛtsew hɔnho, anaa biribi ketseketse bi nna hɔn bo efuw. Binom so botum ayɛ biribi ma edwen ho mpo a erenntse ase, anaa wɔbɛka asɛm bi anaa wɔbɛyɛ biribi ma wɔayɛ hɛn yaw paa. (Job 6:2, 3) Osiandɛ yennyim dza wɔrefa mu ntsi, annhwɛ a yebenya adwen dɛ gyama wɔahyɛ da ara na wɔreyɛ dɛm, anaa mpo dɛ wɔkyerɛ hɔnho.
<<<P>>>
17 Suasua ayamuyie na ayamuhyehye a Jesus wɔ no. Sɛ nna Jesus hu dɛ obi reberɛ anaa oruhu amandze na onnyim no mpo a, ohu no mbɔbɔr na ɔyɛ biribi dze boa no. Hom mma yɛnhwɛ dza Jesus yɛe aber a ohyiaa onyifurafo bi a nna wɔfrɛ no Bartimaeus. Aber a Bartimaeus tsee dɛ Jesus rutwa mu wɔ beebi a ɔtsena no, ɔhyɛɛ ase dɛ ɔrekeka mu mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a Jesus bohun no. Nyimpa pii tseatseaa Bartimaeus dɛ ɔnka n’ano nto mu, naaso nna ɔpɛ dɛ Jesus sa no yarba, ntsi ɔannkasa hɔnho koraa. Afei koraa mpo na nna ɔrekeka mu kɛse. Obotum aba dɛ dza Bartimaeus yɛe no yɛɛ nkorɔfo ehi, naaso Jesus dze, ‘no ho yɛɛ no mbɔbɔr.’ (Matt. 20:34; Mark 10:46-52) Osiee abotar nye no kasae, na ɔkamfoo no dɛ ɔagye no edzi. Owiei no, ɔsaa no yarba.
