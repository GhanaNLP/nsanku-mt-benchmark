NDWOM 122
Hom Ntsintsim, Mma Hom Nnhinhim!
<<<P>>>
DZA YEBOSUA
Yɛbɔhwɛ dza obotum aboa hɛn ma yɛakɔ do ara asom Jehovah ɔmmfa ho dza ɔbɔto hɛn biara no.
<<<P>>>
DA KOR bi, nna Jesus rekyerɛkyerɛ nyimpadɔm bi wɔ Capernaum, na ɔkaa kyerɛɛ hɔn dɛ: “Sɛ hom enndzi nyimpa Ba no no honam na hom annom no bɔgyaa a, hom nnyi nkwa wɔ hom mu.” Ɔsanee so kaa kyerɛɛ hɔn dɛ: “Obiara a odzi mo honam na ɔnom mo bɔgyaa no wɔ onnyiewiei nkwa, na mibenyan no wɔ da a odzi ekyir no.” (John 6:53, 54) Aber a Jesus n’esuafo no tsee dɛm asɛm yi no, annyɛ hɔn mu beberee noara dɛw, ntsi wɔkaa dɛ: “Asɛm yi dze n’ano yɛ hyew ae; woana botum etsie?”—John 6:60.
<<<P>>>
2 Asɛm a Jesus kae no haaw n’esuafo no mu pii, ntsi wogyaa n’ekyir dzi. Iyi kyerɛ dɛ nna wɔnngye no nndzi anaa? Oho, nna wɔgye no dzi. Osiandɛ ansaana Jesus rebɛka dɛm asɛm yi mpo no, nna oedzi kan ayɛ anwanwadze bi ma hɔnho edwuruw hɔn, ma wɔkae mpo dɛ: “Oyi nye nkɔnhyɛnyi a ɔreba wiadze no.” (John 6:14) Ntsi ɔda edzi dɛ nna wɔgye Jesus dzi, naaso aber a asɛm a ɔkae no haaw hɔn no, wogyaa n’ekyir dzi.
<<<P>>>
3 Naaso asomafo no dze, wɔkɔr do ara dzii Jesus n’ekyir.
<<<P>>>
1-2. Ebɛn asɛm na osii aber a nna Jesus rekyerɛkyerɛ wɔ Capernaum no?
<<<P>>>
