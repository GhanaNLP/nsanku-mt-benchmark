18 Dza obotum aboa ma hom akɔ do ara atsena asomdwee mu. Hɛn nuanom a woruhu amandze no, sɛ yɛma hɔn asɛm yɛ hɛn asɛm a, ɔbɛma yeetum ‘akyekye hɔn werɛ.’ (Kenkan 1 Thessalonicafo 5:14.) Kaa dɛ nyɛnko pa dze, ɔwɔ hɔ ma ne nyɛnko aber nyina, nkanka aber a oruhu amandze no. (Mbɛ. 17:17) Ntsi ma wo nuanom nhu dɛ eyɛ nyɛnko pa. Sɛ asɛm to hɔn a, fa no dɛ ɔwo na dɛm asɛm no ato wo. Ka hɔn akoma to hɔn yamu, kyekye hɔn werɛ, na yɛ ndzɛmba ahorow bi fa boa hɔn.

19 Ebɛnadze na ibotum ayɛ dze akyerɛ dɛ edwen wo nuanom ho na hɔn asɛm so hia wo? Yaw a wɔrefa mu no dze, irunntum nnyi mmfi hɔ, naaso ibotum ama woehu dɛ etse hɔn ase na dza wɔrefa mu no yɛ wo yaw paa. (Matt. 7:12; 1 Pet. 3:8) Sɛ wɔreka dza wɔrefa mu ho asɛm akyerɛ wo a, yɛ dzinn na tsie hɔn. Sɛ ɔba no dɛ ɔsɛ dɛ ekasa a, hwɛ hu dɛ dza ebɛka no bɛkyekye hɔn werɛ. (Mbɛ. 12:25) Hwɛ yie na annka asɛm bi anaa annyɛ biribi a ɔbɛkyerɛ dɛ inyim dza wɔrefa mu no nyina. (Mbɛ. 18:13) Nya wo nuanom ho abotar na mma nnhwɛ kwan dɛ aber nyina wɔbɛyɛ hɔn adze pɛpɛɛpɛr.—Eph. 4:32.

20 Ɔyɛ hɛn dɛw paa dɛ hɛn nuanom Christianfo yɛ hɛn anyɛnkofo. Naaso bɔn wɔ hɛn nyina hɛnho, na dza hɛn mu beberee afa mu wɔ abrabɔ mu no nnyɛ agor. Iyi ntsi ɔtɔ da a, yetum ka asɛm bi anaa yɛyɛ biribi ma ekyir yi yenu hɛnho. Ntsi sɛ yɛyɛ biribi ma ɔhye hɛn nuanom naaso wobu hɔn enyi gu do, wonya hɛn ho abotar, wonya ayamuhyehye ma hɛn, wɔda ayamuyie edzi kyerɛ hɛn na wɔnye hɛn tsena kama a, ɔma hɛn enyi gye ankasa. Hom mma hɛn nyina nyɛ dza yebotum biara mfa hɛn nuanom nyɛnko na yɛnye hɔn ntsena asomdwee mu.
