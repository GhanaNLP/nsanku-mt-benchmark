<<<P>>>
Eboa A Nna Wɔdze Pa Nam Wɔ Tsetse Mber Mu
<<<P>>>
Bible no ma yehu dɛ nna Jesus n’esuafo no mu bi yɛ afarfo. (Mark 1:16-20) Nna wɔdze eboa pa nam wɔ Galilee Po no do.
<<<P>>>
Nna wɔdze ahoma na wɔdze wen eboa no, na nna wɔwen ma ntokura deda mu. Eboa a n’enyiwa yɛ akɛse no, wɔdze pa nam akɛse, na dza n’enyiwa yɛ nkakraba no so, wɔdze pa nam nkakraba. Nna wɔtaa dze mbobaa anaa sumbui hyehyɛ eboa a n’enyi yɛ nkakraba no ano, mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a sɛ wɔtow a ɔbɔkɔ po no ase. Dza n’enyi akɛse no so, nna wɔdze mbobaa anaa sumbui hyehyɛ ne fa bi na wɔdze putiisii anaa ndua hyehyɛ ne fa bi so. Ntsi sɛ wɔtow a, ne fa a ɔyɛ dur no kɔ po no ase na ne fa a ɔyɛ har no tsetsew po no enyi.
<<<P>>>
Nna eboa no bo yɛ dzen paa. Nna mbobaa a ɔwɔ po no ase tum tsetsew eboa no. Sɛ nna wɔpa nam beberee so a, otum tsetsew eboa no. (Luke 5:6) Bible no ka dɛ mber bi nna James na John “resa hɔn eboa,” na ɔbɛyɛ dɛ nna wɔdze buyaaba na ahoma na wɔdze resa. (Matt. 4:21) Nna ɔbɛyɛ dɛ afarfo fi po mu ba biara a, wɔsa hɔn eboa. Luke so kaa afarfo bi ho asɛm dɛ nna ‘wɔrohohor hɔn eboa mu’ eyi fi biara a ɔwɔ mu no efi mu. (Luke 5:2) Sɛ wowie a nna wɔhata mu ma ɔwow mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a eboa no renntsetsew.—Ezek. 47:10.
<<<P>>>
Jesus frɛɛ mbarimba bi a wonyim mbrɛ wɔdze eboa pa nam dɛ ‘wombeyi nyimpa.’
<<<P>>>
