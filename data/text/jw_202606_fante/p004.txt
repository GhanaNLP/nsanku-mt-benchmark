6 Aber a nkorɔfo beberee gyaa Jesus n’ekyir dzi no, ebɛnadze na ɔboaa asomafo no ma wɔkɔr do dzii n’ekyir? Ndzɛmba ahorow bi a nna asomafo no gye dzi no na ɔboaa hɔn ma wɔyɛɛ dɛm. Nna wɔgye dzi dɛ Jehovah na ɔbɔɔ ndzɛmba nyina ntsi ɔsɛ fata dɛ wɔsom no. Afei so, nna wɔgye dzi dɛ Jesus yɛ Nyankopɔn ne Ba, na sɛ wotsie no a wobenya “onnyiewiei nkwa.” Dɛmara so na nna wonyim dɛ Jehovah n’enyi gye dza Jesus reyɛ no ho. Adzesua yi mu no, ɔnnyɛ ndzɛmba a obotum ama ayɛ dzen ama hɛn dɛ yɛbɔsom Jehovah no ho asɛm na yɛrebɛka. Mbom yɛbɔhwɛ nokwasɛm ebiasa bi a obotum aboa hɛn ma yɛakɔ do asom Jehovah ɔmmfa ho ɔhaw biara a yebehyia no. Dɛm nokwasɛm ahorow no nye dɛ: (1) Jehovah na ɔbɔɔ ndzɛmba nyina. (2) Jehovah na ɔmaa wɔkyerɛɛw Bible no. (3) Jehovah Adasefo hɔn som nye nokwar som. Sɛ yɛgye dɛm nokwasɛm ebiasa yi dzi paa a, ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yeemia hɛn enyi afa ɔhaw biara a yebehyia no mu.

JEHOVAH NA ƆBƆƆ NDZƐMBA NYINA
<<<P>>>
7 Kenkan Nyikyerɛ 4:11. Dɛm kyerɛwsɛm yi ma yehu dɛ Jehovah na ɔbɔɔ ndzɛmba nyina. Dɛm nokwasɛm yi nkotsee mpo ma yehu dɛ ɔno na ɔfata dɛ yɛsom no. Afei so, sɛ yɛdwendwen tum a ɔwɔ no ho a, ɔma yenya awerɛhyɛmu paa dɛ obotum agye hɛn efi nsɔhwɛ biara a yebehyia no mu. Naaso ndɛ, Satan adaadaa nkorɔfo pii ma woenya adwen dɛ Nyankopɔn biara nnyi hɔ. Abɔdzenyansahufo binom mpo se, biribiara a ɔwɔ wiadze yi mu no bae ara kwa, na ɔnnyɛ obiara na ɔbɔe. Dɛm ntsi sɛ yeennsi hɛn adwen pi dɛ Jehovah nye Ɔbɔadze no na ɔdɔ hɛn a, annhwɛ a sɛ yerihyia nsɔhwɛ a, hɛn adwen bɛyɛ hɛn dɛ Nyankopɔn biara nnyi hɔ na akantam ɔaboa hɛn.—Heb. 11:6.
