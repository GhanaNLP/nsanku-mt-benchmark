16 Kenkan John 13:34, 35. Ndɛ, ɔtan hun ahyɛ wiadze yi mu ma. Naaso ɔdɔ na koryɛ a ɔwɔ hɛn ntamu no dze, ɔsɛ w’enyi. Yemmfi fakor, na ɔsor beebi a wɔtsetsee hɛn mu biara, naaso yɛdodɔ hɛnho na asom-dwee so wɔ hɛn ntamu. Etu kwan akɔ beebi ma akɔ adzesua wɔ hɔ da a? Aber a ihun dɛ enuanom no dodɔ hɔnho na asomdwee wɔ hɔn ntamu no, etsee nka dɛn? Yɛgye dzi dɛ ɔmaa w’enyi gyee paa. Iyi nyina ma yehu dɛ Jehovah Adasefo hɔn som nye nokwar som, na Jehovah taa hɔn ekyir. Sɛ yɛdwendwen ɔdɔ na koryɛ a ɔwɔ hɛn ntamu no ho a, ɔbɛ-ma yɛakɔ do ara asom Jehovah ɔmmfa ho ɔhaw biara a yebehyia no.

KƆ DO ARA SOM JEHOVAH
<<<P>>>
17 Osiandɛ yɛtse nda a odzi ekyir no mu ntsi, ndzɛmba mu yɛ dzen ma hɛn nyina. (2 Tim. 3:1, 13) Nsɛm ahorow bi so botum esisi a obotum ama ayɛ dzen ama hɛn dɛ yɛ-bɔkɔ do ara asom Jehovah. Bi a yɛrenntse akwankyerɛ bi a ofi Nyankopɔn n’ahyehyɛ-dze no hɔ ase papa, ntsi ɔbɛyɛ dzen ama hɛn dɛ yɛdze bɛyɛ edwuma. Hɛn nua Christian-nyi bi so botum ayɛ adze ma ayɛ hɛn yaw paa. Sɛ nsɛm bi a ɔtsetse dɛm sisi a, ann-hwɛ a yerunnhu dza yɛnyɛ anaa mpo dɛ obotum ama hɛn adwen etu afora.
<<<P>>>
18 Naaso iyi nyina mu no, sɛ yɛma ɔtsena hɛn adwen mu dɛ Jehovah na ɔbɔɔ biribia-ra, ɔno na ɔmaa wɔkyerɛɛw Bible no, na Je-hovah Adasefo hɔn som nye nokwar som no a, obotum aboa hɛn ma yɛakɔ do asom Jehovah. Na dɛ mbrɛ Peter kaa kyerɛɛ Je-sus no, hɛn so yebotum aka akyerɛ Jehovah dɛ: “Woana ne nkyɛn na yɛbɔkɔ? Ɔwo na ewɔ nsɛm a ɔma onnyiewiei nkwa.”
