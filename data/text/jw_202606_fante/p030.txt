Cuba Nkorbata Egyinatukuw no mufo, 2013
<<<P>>>
na ɔmaa hɛnho dwuruuw hɛn paa. Enuanom no kaa kyerɛɛ hɛn dɛ wɔpɛ dɛ wɔdze hɛn edzin ma aban mpanyimfo no. Ntsi yebisaa dɛ, “Ah, dɛn ntsi na wɔpɛ dɛ wɔyɛ dɛm?”
<<<P>>>
Wɔkyerɛɛ mu kyerɛɛ hɛn dɛ, wɔnye aban mpanyimfo no ehyia mu mpɛn pii, na wɔrohwɛ ndzɛmba ahorow bi a wobotum ayɛ mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a Jehovah Adasefo nye aban no ntamu bɛyɛ papa. Na aban mpanyimfo no se wɔmfa ahwɛfo akwantufo a wɔwɔ ɔman no mu no nyina hɔn edzin mma hɔn. Ntsi hɛn nyina gyee too mu dɛ wɔmfa hɛn edzin mma hɔn. Na ofitsi dɛm aber no reba no, aban no hyɛɛ ase dɛ ɔretse hɛn ase nkakrankakra.
<<<P>>>
Ewie koraa no, wɔmaa hɛn kwan dɛ yɛnkɔ adzesua na asɛnka. Naaso dɛm aber no nyina nna aban no nngyee hɛn som no nntoo mu wɔ ɔman no mu. Ekyir koraa na yebohun dɛ saana aban no nyim ahwɛfo akwantufo no binom hɔn edzin dada na nna wɔpɛ dɛ wɔhwɛ dɛ edzin a wɔwɔ no noara nye no anaa.
<<<P>>>
September 1994 no, aban no kaa kyerɛɛ hɛn dɛ yebotum ebue hɛn nkorbata no bio. Ntsi, dan no ara a nna wɔdze nsa ato do bɛyɛ mfe 20 no na yɛsanee kobuei na yɛyɛɛ edwuma wɔ hɔ.
<<<P>>>
Afe 1996 mu no, da kor yɛwɔ hɔ ara na Bethel frɛɛ hɛn, na wɔkaa dɛ wɔpɛ dɛ menye Emilia kɔsom wɔ hɔ. Ahyɛse no moho dwuruuw me paa, na mekaa kyerɛɛ enuanom no dɛ yɛda ho ara rohwɛ mbofra beenu. Enuanom no susuu m’asɛm no ho, na ekyir yi wɔkaa kyerɛɛ me dɛ mubotum nye hɔn aba abɛtsena Bethel. Ntsi yɛgyee too mu, na yɛboaboaa hɛnho nye hɛn mba no tui kɔr Havana.
<<<P>>>
Nokwasɛm nye yi, ahyɛse no nna Bethel nnyɛ me dɛw. Nna medze mfe pii ayɛ ɔmansin edwuma no, ntsi nna ɔnoara na ɔda m’akoma do. Ntsi nna ɔyɛ dzen ma me dɛ mebɛyɛ edwuma wɔ ɔfese. Naaso enuanom binom a nna menye hɔn rosom wɔ Bethel no, nye me yer boaa me paa ma mesesaa m’adwen. Ntsi nkakrankakra no, m’enyi bɛgyee edwuma a nna mereyɛ no ho, na merekasa yi medze enyigye reyɛ edwuma wɔ Bethel.
<<<P>>>
Seseiara menye Emilia nyina hɛn enyi efir. Sɛ yɛkaa enuanom a ber nyina som edwuma no ntsi yehyiaa hɔn na yɛdze mfe pii soom hɔn no a, ɔma hɛn enyi gye paa. Ɔyɛ hɛn dɛw paa dɛ hɛn mba nye hɛn mbanana nyina rosom Jehovah. Aber a ɔsomafo John tsee dɛ binom a ɔboaa hɔn ma wobohun nokwar no da ho ara rosom Jehovah no, ɔkaa dɛ: “Minnyi dɛw a ɔkyɛn yi, dɛ metse dɛ me mba nam nokwar no mu.” Na dɛmara na hɛn so yɛtse nka.—3 John 4.
<<<P>>>
Menye Emilia dze bɛyɛ mfe 30 asom wɔ Bethel, na ɔmmfa ho dɛ mpanyinyɛ nye yarba retseetsee hɛn no, yɛbɔ mbɔdzen dɛ yɛbɛyɛ edwuma a wɔdze ahyɛ hɛn nsa no. Yɛbɔsoom Jehovah no, ɔhaw dze yeehyia bi paa, naaso ɔyɛ hɛn dɛw paa dɛ yeetum dze bɛyɛ mfe 70 asom “enyigye Nyankopɔn no” wɔ Cuba supɔw yi do!—1 Tim. 1:11; Ndw. 97:1.
