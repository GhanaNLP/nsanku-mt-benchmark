kuw do hwɛfo no ma ɔaboa wo. Ibotum so ama ɔkwampaafo bi anaa ɔdawurbɔfo bi a noho akokwaa a ɔwɔ kuw no mu no aboa wo. (Kenkan Mbɛbusɛm 1:5; 27:17.) Dɛmara na akyerɛbaa bi a ɔfɛr adze a wɔfrɛ no Heather yɛe. Ɔkaa dɛ: “Mekaa kyerɛɛ akyerɛbaa kwampaafo bi dɛ ɔmboa me ma muntum nyɛ sankɔhwɛ. Ntsi nna ɔtaa nye me kɔ asɛnka na ofii n’akoma mu boaa me. Hɔn a nna metaa kɔ hɔn nkyɛn nye hɔn kɔbɔ nkɔmbɔ no, ɔboaa me ma mutumii nye hɔn mu kor hyɛɛ ase suaa Bible no. Sɛ mamma ɔammboa me a, nkyɛ mayɛ moho. Sesei asɛnka edwuma no asan ayɛ me dɛw.” Asɛm no ara nye dɛ, yebotum afa akwan pii do aboa hɛn asɛnka kuw no.

MFASO A HƐN ASƐNKA KUW NO MA YENYA
<<<P>>>
11 Ɔboa hɛn ma yɛsom Jehovah yie. Hɛn nyina botum enya asɛnka kuw ho nhyehyɛɛ a wɔayɛ ama hɛn no do mfaso. Dɛ mfatoho no, Jehovah fa dɛm nhyehyɛɛ yi do dze hyɛ ekunafo, ngyanka, mberwa nye nkɔkora nkuran na ɔdze kyekye hɔn werɛ osiandɛ wɔda n’akoma do paa. (1 Thess. 2:8; Jas. 1:27) Afei so, sɛ yɛnye hɛn kuw no bɔ mu kɔ asɛnka a, ɔboa hɛn ma yehu mbrɛ yebesi aka asɛm no na yɛakyerɛkyerɛ yie. Enuanom mberantsɛ so nya ntsetsee mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a daakye bi wobotum dze dwumadzi ahorow ahyehyɛ hɔn nsa wɔ asafo no mu.—1 Tim. 3:10.
<<<P>>>
12 Akyerɛbaa Wendy a yeedzi kan aka no ho asɛm no, hom mma yɛnhwɛ mbrɛ asɛnka kuw ho nhyehyɛɛ no esi aboa no. Ɔkaa dɛ: “Nna me papa n’enyi nngye nokwar no ho koraa, ntsi sɛ nna odu Memenda na Kwesida a nna me maame nntaa nnye n’asɛnka kuw no mmbɔ mu nnkɔ asɛnka. Naaso emidze nna ɔma me kwan ma mokɔ. Aberwa kwampaafo bi faa me nyɛnko ntsi nna ɔtaa nye me kɔ asɛnka. Ɔboaa me ma muhun mbrɛ mibesi akyerɛkyerɛ yie wɔ asɛnka mu nye mbrɛ mibesi ayɛ pii wɔ asafo no mu. Afotu a ofi Bible no mu a mo kuw do hwɛfo no so dze maa me no aboa me araa dze ebesi ndɛ.”
<<<P>>>

(Phil. 1:27) Afei so, osiandɛ hɛn asɛnka kuw mufo nndɔɔ so ntsi, ɔnnyɛ dzen koraa dɛ enuanom a wɔfɛr adze anaa wɔmmpɛ kasa no mpo benya nyɛnko.
