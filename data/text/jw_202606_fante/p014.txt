<<<P>>>
NDWOM 65
Yer Woho Kɔ W’enyim!
<<<P>>>
DZA YEBOSUA
Yɛbɔhwɛ mbrɛ yebesi enya asɛnka kuw ho nhyehyɛɛ a wɔayɛ ama hɛn no do mfaso.
<<<P>>>
‘Medze m’akoma nyina mebɛda Jehovah ase, wɔ atseneneefo bagua mu.’—NDW. 111:1.
<<<P>>>
EKAA da a wɔkaa kyerɛɛ wo dɛ ibotum akɔ asɛnka no a? Dɛm da no w’enyi gyee paa, osiandɛ ebɛkaa Jehovah n’asomfo beberee a wɔrobɔ ne dzin no ho dawur wɔ wiadze beebiara no ho. (Ndw. 148:1, 2, 12, 13) Na dɛm da no ara so bi a ebɛkaa kuw kakraba bi ho, ɔno nye w’asɛnka kuw no. Mbrɛ ɔtse nye dɛ, edwuma a wɔdze ahyɛ hɛn nsa dɛ yɛnka asɛmpa no wɔ “wiadze nyinara” no, asɛnka kuw ahorow no boa hɛn ma yetum yɛ no yie.—Matt. 24:14.
<<<P>>>
2 Ihu asɛnka kuw a ewɔ mu no dɛn? Akyerɛbaa bi a wɔfrɛ no Wendy* kaa dɛ: “Enuanom a wɔwɔ m’asɛnka kuw no mu no dze, wɔaboa me paa. Wɔama mehu akwan afofor pii a mubotum afa do aka asɛm no akyerɛ nkorɔfo na wɔama me nkyerɛkyerɛ so etu mpon. Afei so, wɔaboa me ma medze botae ahorow bi esisi m’enyi do wɔ Jehovah no som mu, na metum mpo edu bi ho. Na anyɛnkofo a menya so dze, beberee kwa. Asɛnka kuw ho nhyehyɛɛ no yɛ kama paa.” Onua bi a wɔfrɛ no Rich so kaa dɛ: “Sɛ obi yɛ asɛnka kuw do hwɛfo a, ɔyɛ hokwan kɛse paa. Siantsir a mereka dɛm nye dɛ, adawurbɔfo botum aboa obi ma wɔabɔsom Jehovah. Naaso asɛnka kuw do hwɛfo no botum ayɛ ndzɛmba ahorow bi dze aboa nyimpa no ma ɔakɔ do ara nye Jehovah ne ntamu ayɛ papa.” Akyerɛbaa Dinah so se: “Enuanom a wɔwɔ m’asɛnka kuw no mu no ma m’enyi gye paa. Mafa hɔn nyina dɛ m’ebusuafo.”
<<<P>>>
