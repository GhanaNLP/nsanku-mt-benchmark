mpanyimfo kuw no hwɛ asafo mu panyin a obotum ayɛ dɛm edwuma no yie na wɔdze hyɛ ne nsa.
<<<P>>>
⁵ Ndzɛmba pii na ɔma ɔyɛ dzen ma kuw do ahwɛfo dɛ wɔbɛyɛ hɔn edwuma yie. Onua Jörg yɛ kuw do hwɛfo wɔ asafo bi a ɔwɔ Germany mu. Ɔkaa dɛ: “Medze esi m’enyiwa do dɛ menye enuanom a wɔwɔ m’asɛnka kuw no mu no bedzi nkitaho. Naaso osiandɛ ndwuma a ogu mo do wɔ asafo no mu no dɔɔ so ntsi, ɔtɔ da a, ɔyɛ dzen ma me dɛ mebɛyɛ dɛm.” Onua Abel fi Uganda na oedzi mfe 70. Ɔkaa dɛ: “Asafo a yɛwɔ mu no, enuanom no dɔɔ so naaso mpanyimfo na asomfo no suar. Ntsi sɛ efa asɛnka kuw kor a, ibohu dɛ enuanom a wɔwɔ mu no dɔɔ so. Ne dɛm ntsi munntum nnserasera adawurbɔfo a wɔwɔ m’asɛnka kuw mu no dɛ mbrɛ nkyɛ ɔsɛ dɛ meyɛ no.” Onua Obed yɛ asafo mu panyin a ɔwɔ Suriname. Ɔno so kaa dɛ: “M’enyi gye ho paa dɛ mebɛtsetse enuanom. Naaso iyi gye mber nye mbɔdzembɔ, na ɔtɔ da so a, ɔkyɛr ana nyimpa no a erekyerɛ no adze no ehu. Osian iyi ntsi ɔyɛ a ɔyɛ dzen ma me dɛ mebɛtsetse enuanom.” Ebɛnadze na obotum aboa kuw do ahwɛfo ama woetum edzi ɔhaw ahorow a ɔtsetse dɛm do?

⁶ Sɛ ɔbɛyɛ yie a, ɔsɛ dɛ enuanom a wɔwɔ asɛnka kuw biara mu no yɛ kakraabi. Iyi bɛma kuw do hwɛfo no ehu enuanom a wɔwɔ no kuw mu no yie na ɔaboa hɔn ma wɔasom Jehovah yie. Na sɛ asafo mu mpanyimfo a wɔwɔ asafo bi mu no ho suar so ɛ? Asafo mu mpanyimfo no botum akyekyɛ asɛnka kuw no nkakraba na wɔama asafo mu somfo bi ahwɛ ekuw ahorow no bi do kesi dɛ wobenya ɔpanyin a ɔfata ma ɔabɔhwɛ do. Iyi botum

ama asɛnka kuw do ahwɛfo no ayɛ hɔn edwuma no yie.
<<<P>>>
⁷ Sɛ eyɛ kuw do hwɛfo na asafo a ewɔ mu no, mpanyimfo no nndɔɔ so a, fa si w’enyi do dɛ ebɛtsetse wo boafo no ama oetum aboa wo ma ahwɛ Nyankopɔn no nguankuw no do yie. (Kenkan 1 Peter 5:2.) Fa dwumadzi ahorow bi hyehyɛ ne nsa. Ɔyɛ a ma ɔnhwɛ asɛnka nhyiamu do. Afei so, enye no mbɔ mu mbɔ mpaa na hom nsusu kuw no munyi biara ho, na hom nhwɛ mbrɛ hom besi aboa hɔn mu biara ma oetu mpon wɔ asɛnka edwuma no mu. Sɛ erekɛserasera wo kuw no mufo so a, enye wo boafo no ntaa nkɔ. Ansaana hom bɔkɔ no, hom nsusu dza hom bɛka ho. Na sɛ hom kɔ ba so a, beebi a ɔbɔɔ no ho mbɔdzen no kamfo no, na beebi a ohia mboa no so, boa no. Sɛ eyɛ iyinom a, daakye bi wo boafo no so botum abɛyɛ kuw do hwɛfo. (2 Tim. 2:2) Ibotum so ama enuanom afofor
