Yɛnhyehyɛ Hɛnho Nkuran
<<<P>>>
DZA YEBOSUA
Yɛbɔhwɛ dza yebotum ayɛ aber a akasakasa bi asoɛr yɛnye hɛn nua bi ntamu no.
<<<P>>>
WIADZE yi mu tsena yɛ yaw dodow. Sɛ innyi nyɛnko mpo dze a, nna asɛm no asɛɛ koraa. Jehovah nyim iyi, ntsi ɔama yeenya anyɛnkofo wɔ Christianfo asafo no mu. (Ndw. 119:63) Mbrɛ yesi dodɔ hɛnho no dze, sɛ yɛse yɛreka ho asɛm a, adze bɛkye hɛn.
<<<P>>>
2 Ɔnnyɛ dɛ yɛnye hɛn nuanom Christianfo bɔ ara kɛkɛ o, mbom wɔyɛ hɛn anyɛnkofo ankasa. Nokwasɛm nye dɛ, yɛpɛ hɔn asɛm paa. Adze a ɔma yɛdodɔ hɛnho nye dɛ, hɛn nyina dɔ Jehovah na Jesus. Na dɛm dɔ yi ara so na ɔma yɛyɛ kor no. (John 13:35) Naaso hɛn nuanom Christianfo no nnyɛ abɔfo, hɔnara so bɔn wɔ hɔnho, ntsi ɔtɔ da a wobotum ayɛ adze ma ahaw hɛn. Naaso ɔsɛ dɛ yɛyɛ dza yebotum biara nye hɔn tsena kama.
<<<P>>>
3 Nokwasɛm nye dɛ, yɛbɛn hɛn nuanom binom kyɛn binom. Ebɛnadze ntsi a? Enuanom binom wɔ hɔ a, dza hɔn enyi gye ho no, ɔno ara na hɛn so hɛn enyi gye ho. Anaa mpo dɛ mbrɛ wosi yɛ hɔn adze no, dɛmara na hɛn so yɛyɛ hɛn adze. Naaso enuanom binom so dze, ɔyɛ dzen ma hɛn dɛ yɛbɛfa hɔn nyɛnko. Ɔtɔ da a, bi a yɛnye hɔn rennyɛ adwen wɔ ndzɛmba bi ho, na iyi botum asɛɛ yɛnye hɔn ntamu. Enuanom binom so wɔ hɔ a, ɔsor mbrɛ wosi yɛ hɔn adze koraa, ntsi ɔyɛ a nna yɛmmpɛ dɛ yɛdze hɛn asɛm mpo kɔ hɔn do. Afei so, ɔtɔ da a sɛ hɛn nua bi yar paa anaa ɔrefa ɔhaw bi mu a, mbrɛ obesi ayɛ n’adze no botum ama ayɛ dzen ama hɛn dɛ yɛbɔkɔ do ara abɛn no. Adzesua yi mu no, yɛbɔhwɛ ndzɛmba ahorow bi a Jesus yɛe a obotum aboa hɛn ma yɛakɔ do ara nye hɛn nuanom atsena kama. Naaso, hom
