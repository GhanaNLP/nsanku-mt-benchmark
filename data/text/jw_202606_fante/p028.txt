<<<P>>>
Menye Emilia hyɛɛ nantsew a odzi awar enyim ase, na afe kor mpo enndu na yɛwaree.
<<<P>>>
Minyaa edwuma wɔ aban no ne factory bi a wɔyɛ esikyire mu, na nna edwuma no gye me ber nyina. Dɛm ntsi, menye Emilia nyina dze akwampaa edwuma no too hɔ; naaso nna yɛpɛ dɛ yɛkɔ do ara dze hɛn ber pii som Jehovah. Ahanamakye soɛr dze, nna m’enyi nngye ho, naaso meyɛɛ dɛ mobɔkɔ edwuma ahanamakye ndɔnebiasa na mapon ana-pa dubiako. Sɛ nna mokɔ edwuma dɛm aber no a, nna ɔma metaa nya ber kɔ asɛnka, na menye Emi-lia so anntoto adzesua mu da.
<<<P>>>
Afe 1969 mu no, yɛwoo hɛn abakan Gustavo. Dɛm aber no mu ara so na wɔkaa kyerɛɛ me dɛ, monsom dɛ ɔmansin do hwɛfo. Dɛm aber no, nna amansin do ahwɛfo a wɔwɔ mba nnyɛ na wɔ Cuba. Mese, nna menye Emilia nnyi hɛn bra mu adagyer koraa, naaso hɛn enyi gyee paa. Osiandɛ ɔmaa ye-nyaa hokwan bio dze soom hɛn nuanom, na yehun dɛ ɔyɛ Jehovah na orihyira hɛn. Yegu do reyɛ ɔmansin edwuma no ara so na yɛwoo hɛn mbaba-nyin Obed na Abner. Na mfe kakra ekyir no, yɛwoo hɛn ba basia Mahely.
<<<P>>>
Sɛ mekaa aber a nna yɛyɛ ɔmansin edwuma no na mohwɛ mbrɛ Jehovah sii boaa n’asomfo a nna wɔwɔ Cuba dɛm aber no a, ɔma m’enyi gye paa. Afei so, Jehovah boaa hɛn ma yetumii kyerɛ-kyerɛɛ hɛn mba no ma wɔbɔsoom no. Ɔwodze, ɔmansin edwuma a menye Emilia yɛe no, ma mom-bɔ hom mu ɛ.

ABER A YƐREYƐ ƆMANSIN EDWUMA NO, NNA WƆABRA HƐN SOM
<<<P>>>
Wɔ 1960 nye 1970 mfe no mu no, osiandɛ nna aban no abra hɛn som no ntsi, ndzɛmba mu yɛɛ dzen maa hɛn paa. Wɔtotoow Kingdom Halls no mu, na nna yenntum nnkɔ adzesua na asɛnka. Wɔ-paam asɛmpatserɛwfo a nna wɔwɔ ɔman no mu no nyina. Afei so, wɔkyekyee enuanom mberantsɛ be-beree koguu efiadze, na wɔtoow Bethel dan a ɔwɔ Havana no so mu.
<<<P>>>
Osiandɛ nna wɔdze nsa ato hɛn dwumadzi do ntsi, nna Memenda na Kwesida nkotsee na yetum kɛsera nsafo ahorow no. Dɛm ntsi nna yɛdze ada-pɛn ebien som asafo biara. Sɛ nna yerutu kwan a, nna yɛmmfa ndzɛmba pii na nna yɛtaa dze baese-kel na yɛdze kɔ, mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a aporisifo nye afofor hɔn adwen remmba hɛn do. Mbrɛ ɔtse nye dɛ, sɛ nna yɛrekɛsera asafo bi a, nna wonndzi kan mmbɔ asafo no amandzɛɛ ansaana. Nna yɛma ɔyɛ tse dɛ yɛrekɛsera hɛn ebusuafo, na ɔno so dze nna ne yɛ nnyɛ dzen koraa. Nokwasɛm nye yi, yɛfaa enuanom no dɛ hɛn ebusuafo araa ma sɛ nna yɛkɔ asafo bi mu a, nna hɛn werɛ efir mpo dɛ yɛkɔr dɛ yɛrokɔsom hɔn. (Mark 10:29, 30) Naaso, nna
