MBRƐ YEBESI ETSIE ASƐM YIE WƆ ADZESUA ASE
<<<P>>>
14 Sɛ hɛn enyi sɔ dza Jehovah kɔ do ara kaa-kaa hɛn wɔ adzesua ase no a, ɔbɛma yɛakyɛn hɛn aso etsie. ‘Mber a yɛtse mu yi no mu yɛ dzen na no mu tsena so yɛ yaw,’ ntsi yehia dɛm nkaakaasɛm yi ama yeetum egyina mu. (2 Tim. 3:1) Sɛ ɔtɔ da na sɛ ɔyɛ wo dɛ dza wɔ-reka wɔ adzesua ase no atse araa nko a, ibo-tum esuasua dza obi a ɔkyerɛɛw Ndwom 119 no yɛe a ɔmaa ɔkɔr do ara kyerɛɛ Nyankopɔn ne nkaakaasɛm no ho enyisɔ no.—Kenkan Ndwom 119:24, 111, 167.
<<<P>>>
15 Ɔsɛ dɛ yɛkɔ do ara kyerɛ ndzɛmba a Jeho-vah dze kyerɛkyerɛ hɛn no ho enyisɔ. Ɔwodze ma yensusu dza Israelfo no yɛe no ho nhwɛ.
<<<P>>>
* Sɛ epɛ dɛ ihu ndzɛmba ahorow bi a obotum aboa wo ma abɛyɛ ɔkasafo anaa ɔkyerɛkyerɛfo pa a, hwɛ nwomaba a wɔato dzin Ma W’enyi Nku Akenkan Na Nkyerɛkyerɛ Ho nye Nya Theocracy Ɔsom Skuul Adzesua No Do Mfaso buukuu no.

14. Ebɛnadze ntsi na ɔsɛ dɛ yesuasua obi a ɔkyerɛɛw Ndwom 119 no? (Ndwom 119:24, 111, 167)
<<<P>>>
15. Yebesi dɛn akɔ do ara akyerɛ dza yesua wɔ adzesua ase no ho enyisɔ?
<<<P>>>
Aber a nna wɔwɔ sar no do no, Jehovah maa hɔn manna. (Ex. 16:15, 31) Ahyɛse no dze nna hɔn enyi sɔ. Nna ɔyɛ hɔn dɛw, na ɔno ara so na ɔmaa wonyaa ahoɔdzen dze tsenaa sar no do. Naaso odur beebi no, nna manna no afon hɔn mu binom, ntsi nna hɔn enyi nnsɔ bio. (Nka. 21:5) Esuadze no nye dɛn? Ɔnnsɛ dɛ ye-suasua Israelfo no koraa. Onnyi dɛ yɛka dɛ nsɛm a yɛtse wɔ adzesua ase no, yɛatse aberɛ. Mbom no ɔsɛ dɛ yehu dɛ Jehovah na ɔrekye-rɛkyerɛ hɛn. Afei so, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛma ɔtsena hɛn adwen mu dɛ, dza yesua wɔ adzesua ase no, Jehovah n’ahyehyɛdze no tɔ bo ara siesie ma hɛn. Nokwasɛm nye dɛ, dza yesua wɔ adzesua ase no botum aboa hɛn ma yeenya onnyiewiei nkwa.—Mbɛ. 3:13, 16-18; John 17:3.
