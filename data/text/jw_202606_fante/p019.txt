14 Ɔboa hɛn aber a atoyerɛnkyɛm bi esi anaa asɛm bi ato hɛn awerɛfir mu no. Sɛ yarba bi tsew si beebi a yɛtse, atoyerɛnkyɛm bi si, yar-ba bi bɔ hɛn mpofirmu, wɔretan hɛn enyi anaa hɛn sikasɛm yɛ basaa a, hɛn asɛnka kuw mufo boa hɛn. Sɛ yɛbɛyɛ mfatoho a, asafo biara mu no, Asafo Ɔsom Egyinatukuw no mufo nye asɛnka kuw do ahwɛfo no hyia mu na wɔhwɛ dza wobotum ayɛ dze aboa hɔn a wobehia mboa soronko aber a biribi a ɔtse dɛm esi no. Afei so, sɛ atoyerɛnkyɛm bi si a kuw do ahwɛfo no dzi enyim hwɛ dɛ wobohu beebi a kuw no munyi biara wɔ, na wɔahwɛ mboa a obehia. Sɛ wohu dɛ obehia dɛ wɔnye hɔn mu bi kɔ ayarsabea, wɔma obi edziban, nsu, beebi a ɔbɛda anaa ntar a, wɔyɛ no dɛm.

15 Dɛ mbrɛ yeedzi kan aka no, Jehovah n’a-hyehyɛdze no so paa. N’asomfo binom wɔ sor na binom so wɔ asaase yi do. Abɔfo a wɔwɔ sor no nkotsee mpo, ɔnnyɛ asɛm kakraba. Na asaase yi do so, nyimpa bɔbor ɔpepem akron na wɔrosom Jehovah. Adze rekye a adze resa nyina, nyimpa ba ara na wɔreba n’ahyehyɛ-dze no mu. (Zech. 8:23; Nyi. 5:11) Jehovah n’asomfo a wɔwɔ sor nye asaase yi do no nyi-na aka abɔ mu, na wɔrobɔ mbɔdzen dɛ wɔ-bɛma obiara atse Nyankopɔn n’Ahendzi no ho asɛmpa no bi! (Nyi. 14:6, 7) Hom mma hɛn nyina mfa hɛnho nhyɛ asɛnka kuw nhye-hyɛɛ a wɔayɛ ama hɛn no mu. Sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm a, ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yeefi hɛn akoma nyina mu eyi Jehovah ayɛw.—Ndw. 111:1.
