Nna communist aban no pɛ asogyafo pii wɔ ɔman no mu, ntsi wɔhyɛɛ dɛ obiara nkɔ sogya. Iyi haaw Jehovah n’asomfo a nna wɔwɔ ɔman no mu no hɔn adwen paa, osiandɛ Jehovah Adasefo mmfa hɔnho nnhyɛ amanyɛsɛm mu na wɔnnkɔ sogya so. Dɛm ntsi nkakrankakra no, hokwan a nna yɛdze som Jehovah dɛ mbrɛ yɛpɛ wɔ ɔman no mu no fii hɛn nsa. Owiee ase no, aban no dze ne nsa too hɛn dwumadzi nyina do, na wɔkyekyee enuanom mbarimba beberee koguufiadze. Nna wɔtaa bor hɔn mu binom, na nna wɔkyen hɔn kɔm so. Ɔtɔ da mpo a, nna edziban a wɔdze ma hɔn no bɔgya wɔ mu, ntsi nna enuanom no nndzi osiandɛ Bible no kasa tsia.
<<<P>>>
Ɔmmfa ho dɛ nna aban no dze nsa ato hɛn dwumadzi do no, yɛkɔr do ara hyiaa mu soom Jehovah. (Heb. 10:25) Yɛyɛɛ ehyiadzi ahorow bi mpo wɔ binom hɔn haban mu, nye mbea afofor bi a nna aban no runnhu hɛn. Mber bi mpo, onua bi dze beebi a ɔnyɛn no nguan maa hɛn dɛ yɛmfa nyɛ ehyiadzi. Naaso, yeenntum ennyi nguan no emmfi hɔ na akantam yeesiesie hɔ, ntsi owiee ase no, yɛnye nguan no nyina bɔɔ mu yɛɛ ehyiadzi no.—Mic. 2:12.
<<<P>>>
Edwumadzen a enuanom no yɛe dɛm aber no dze boaa hɛn ma yɛkɔr do soom Jehovah no, hɛn enyi sɔ paa. Dza wɔyɛe no bi nye dɛ, nna wɔrekɔɔd ehyiadzi dwumadzi no nyina gu cassette do na wɔkyekyɛ ma enuanom a wɔwɔ ɔman no mu no nyina. Ɔtɔ da a, nna wɔma enuanom beenu pɛ ma ɔkasa ahorow no nyina na wɔrekɔɔd gu cassette do. Nna enuanom no suma hɔnho na wɔyɛ dɛm edwuma yi, ntsi nna ɔtɔ da a etse dɛ nkokɔ robɔn wɔ ekyir, na nna etse dede nketsenketse bi so wɔ ekyir. Sɛ nna enyinam ahoɔdzen nnyi beebi a wɔbɛyɛ ehyiadzi no a, nna dza enuanom no yɛ nye dɛ, wɔdze efir bi bɔ baesekel bi a ogyina fakor ho na wɔma onua kor tsena do twuw mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a wobenya enyinam ahoɔdzen. Nna iyi ma wotum bɔ cassette no ma enuanom no tsie dwumadzi no. Ɔwɔ mu, nna yennya fahodzi nnsom Jehovah dɛ mbrɛ yɛpɛ no, na asafo nwoma a nna hɛn nuanom Christianfo a wɔwɔ nsaase ahorow bi do nya no, nna hɛn nsa nnka bi. Naaso, dza nna yehia biara ama yɛnye Jehovah ntamukɔ do ayɛ papa no, hɛn nsa kae. Na iyi boaa hɛn ma yɛkɔr do dze enyigye bɔɔ mu soom Jehovah.—Neh. 8:10.
