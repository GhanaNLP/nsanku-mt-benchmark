<<<P>>>
ɔsɛ dɛ yɛma hɛn enyi da hɔ. Osiandɛ nna aporisifo taa tsetsɛw hɛn na ɔreyɛ ara nye yi nna woribisabi-sa hɛn nsɛm. Enuanom a nna yɛkɔsoɛ hɔn no, nna yesuro ma hɔn paa, osiandɛ sɛ nna aporisifo hu dɛ yɛwɔ hɔn nkyɛn a, wobotum akyer hɔn.—Rom. 16:4.
<<<P>>>
Nsafo ahorow a nna yɛsom hɔn no, nna enua-nom pii nnyi bi, naaso dza nna wɔwɔ biara no nna wɔnye hɛn kyɛ. Mbea ahorow bi a nna yɛkɔ no, nna ntonton wɔ hɔ paa, naaso nna enuanom no dze hɔn mosquito net ma hɛn, na hɔn mbom da nton-ton no mu mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a yebotum ada yie. Enua-nom binom so wɔ hɔ a, nna dza wobedzi mpo yɛ dzen ma hɔn, naaso nna wɔma yɛkɔsoɛ hɔn. Ntsi nna ɔtɔ da a, hɛnankasa dze hɛn edziban kɔ na yɛ-nye hɔn kyɛ.
<<<P>>>
Sɛ yɛrekɛsera nsafo ahorow no a, nna yenntum nye hɛn mba no nyina nnkɔ. Ntsi dza nna yɛyɛ nye dɛ, nna yɛnye hɔn mu kor pɛ kɔ na yegya nkaa no ma me maame na me sista. Asɛm no nye dɛ, sɛ nna yɛnye hɛn abofra bi rutu kwan a, nna ɔbɔ hɛn-ho ban paa, osiandɛ nna yetum dze asafo nwoma hyɛ baag a yɛdze ne diapers a wɔayɛ fi hyɛ mu no mu. Ntsi sɛ aporisifo hyia hɛn na wɔrohwehwɛ hɛn ndzɛmba mu a, nna wɔnnhwɛ dɛm baag no mu, ɔno ntsi nna yɛfa hɛnho dzi.
<<<P>>>
Mo dɔfo Emilia dze, metse no do a mosoɛr. Ɔboae ma yetumii tsetsee hɛn mba no, na ɔmaa yetumii yɛɛ ber nyina som edwuma no so. Iyi boaa me ma mokɔr do ara yɛɛ edwuma wɔ fac-tory hɔ, na ber kor no ara so, mutumii yɛɛ ɔman-sin edwuma no. Ebɛnadze ntsi na mereka dɛm? Ɔtɔ da a, nna ɔsɛ dɛ meyɛ overtime dapɛn kor anaa ebien bi mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a moronnkɔ edwuma Memenda na Kwesida. Ekyir yi, wɔtoo mo post mu wɔ edwuma mu. Wɔdze me too m’edwuma-mufo bi hɔn ano na nna ɔsɛ dɛ mohwɛ hɔn ma wɔyɛ edwuma fitsi Dwowda araa kesi Kwesida. Nna meyɛ iyi ho hwee a ɔrennyɛ yie. Naaso mubo-hun dɛ, sɛ mehyehyɛ edwuma no yie na mema hɔn edwuma ma wɔyɛ no Memenda na Kwesida a, nna wobotum ahwɛ m’ekyir ama me aber a merekɛsera nsafo ahorow no. Dze besi ndɛ no, menngye nndzi dɛ mpanyimfo a nna mehyɛ hɔn ase no hun dɛ nna memmba edwuma Memenda na Kwesida!
