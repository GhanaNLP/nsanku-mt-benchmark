atsetse adawurbɔfo a wɔwɔ wo kuw no mu no. Sɛ yɛbɛyɛ mfatoho a, ibotum ama daa akwampaafo nye adawurbɔfo a hɔnho ako-kwaa nye hɔn a wɔahyɛ asɛnka ase fofor na enuanom a hɔnho nnkokwaae no akɔ asɛnka. DZA IBOTUM AYƐ DZE ABOA W’ASƐNKA KUW MUFO
<<<P>>>
8 Kɔ asɛnka nhyiamu. Sɛ enye onua anaa akyerɛbaa bi bɔ mu yɛ asɛnka edwuma no a, “obiara dze ne gyedzi bɛhyɛ ne nyɛnko nku-ran.” (Kenkan Romefo 1:12.) Sɛ enye obi nam asɛnka mu a, ɔboa wo ma inya akokodur dze dzi dase. Afei so, sɛ hom nam beebi a hɔ yɛ hu a, ɔbɔ homho ban. Sɛ w’edwuma ntsi nntaa nnye wo kuw no mmbɔ mu nnkɔ asɛn-ka a, ibotum nye w’edwumawura aka ahwɛ dɛ obotum ayɛ nsesa wɔ mber anaa nda a edze kɔ edwuma no mu ama wo a. Bi a obetsie wo na ɔasesa ama wo. (Neh. 2:4-6) Sɛ yar-ba anaa mpanyinyɛ ntsi inntum nnkɔ asɛnka nhyiamu a, ka kyerɛ wo kuw do hwɛfo no. Bi a ɔbɛfrɛ wo wɔ tɛlefon anaa Zoom do ma etsie asɛnka nhyiamu no bi, na ɔama wo obi a enye no bɛyɛ tɛlefon do adasedzi anaa hom bɛkyerɛw nkrataa dze edzi dase. Sɛ eyɛ abe-rantsɛ anaa akataasia a ewɔ ahoɔdzen naa-so w’enyi nngye asɛnka ho so ɛ? Sɛ dɛm a, ɔno enye obi a ɔwɔ w’asɛnka kuw no mu nyɛ nhyehyɛɛ nkɔ asɛnka dapɛn biara. Bi a obe-hia dɛ esan hwɛ ndzɛmba a edze dzi kan wɔ w’asetsena mu no bio, ama enya mber pii dze asom Jehovah yie. (Rom. 12:11; Col. 4:5) Sɛ eyɛ dɛm a, ɔbɔboa wo paa na ɔbɔboa wo kuw no mufo so. Ntsi bɔ Jehovah mpaa na serɛ no dɛ ɔmma nya ‘ɔpɛ na ahoɔdzen a edze bɛyɛ dɛm.’—Phil. 2:13.

9 Da ahɔhoyɛ edzi. Sɛ ‘yɛhwehwɛ hokwan dze da ahɔhoyɛ edzi’ a, ɔbɛma hɛn asɛnka kuw no mu ayɛ dɛw. (Kenkan Romefo 12:13.) Dza yebotum ayɛ no bi nye dɛ yɛbɛma wɔayɛ asɛnka nhyiamu wɔ hɛn fie. Dza nsafo bi so yɛ nye dɛ, aber biara a onua bi befi asa-fo bi mu abɛma ɔkasa wɔ hɔ no, wɔma asɛnka kuw kor nye no bɔ mu dzidzi na wɔgye hɔn enyi. Sɛ ɔkasafo no enntum annkɔ aponto no ase mpo a, enuanom a wɔwɔ kuw no mu no ara hyia mu gye hɔn enyi na wɔhyɛ hɔn-ho nkuran. Akyerɛbaa kwampaafo tsitsir bi a wɔfrɛ no Sarah kaa dɛ: “Sɛ moroto nsa afrɛ enuanom ma wɔaba me fie a, ɔyɛ a me yamu hyehye me, ntsi mefrɛ enuanom kakraabi. Sɛ edziban a mebɛyɛ no bɛyɛ dɛw o, sɛ ɔrennyɛ dɛw o, ɔnnyɛ ɔno na medze m’adwen si do, mbom mo nuanom a menye hɔn bɔbɔ nkɔm-bɔ na mehu hɔn yie no ara na ohia me.” Sɛ yɛyɛ dza yebotum biara dze da ahɔhoyɛ edzi wɔ hɛn asɛnka kuw no mu a, ɔbɛma yɛabɛn Jehovah nye hɛn nuanom kɛse.—Neh. 8:10; Andw. 20:35.

10 Ka kyerɛ enuanom dɛ wɔmboa wo. Sɛ ye-tum ka asɛmpa no na yɛkyerɛkyerɛ yie wɔ asɛnka mu a, hɛn enyi bɛgye ho dɛ yɛnye hɛn kuw no bɛtaa abɔ mu akɔ asɛnka. Ntsi sɛ ihu dɛ ɔsɛ dɛ itu mpon wɔ asɛnka edwu-ma no ne fa bi mu a, ka ho asɛm kyerɛ wo kuw no munyi bi ma ɔmboa wo. Ibotum aka mboa pɔtsee a ihia no ho asɛm akyerɛ wo
