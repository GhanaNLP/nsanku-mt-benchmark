Nna ebɛn apam ebien na ɔsomafo Paul reka ho asɛm wɔ Galatiafo 4:24 no?
<<<P>>>
◼ Aber a ɔsomafo Paul kaa Abraham na ne yer Sarah nye n’abaawa Hagar ho asɛm wiei no, ɔkaa dɛ: “Iyinom nyina gyina hɔ ma biribi; osiandɛ mbaa yi gyina hɔ ma apam ahorow ebien.” (Gal. 4:22-24) Nkan no, nna yɛtse ase dɛ dɛm apam ebien no nye Mbra apam na apam fofor no. Naaso aber a yɛsanee yɛɛ dɛm asɛm yi mu nhwehwɛmu bio no, yebohun dɛ ohia dɛ yɛyɛ nsesa wɔ mu. Ɔbɛyɛ dɛ nna Paul reka apam a Jehovah nye Abraham yɛe no ho asɛm, na ɔnnyɛ apam fofor no. Siantsir a yɛayɛ dɛm nsesa yi no, hom mma yɛndɔ mu esukɔ kakra.*
<<<P>>>
Paul dze abaawa Hagar totoo Bepɔw Sinai ho. (Gal. 4:25) Ɔkaa dɛ Mbra apam a Jehovah nye Israelfo no yɛe wɔ Bepɔw Sinai do wɔ afe 1513 ansaana wɔrobɔwo Jesus no, ogyina hɔ ma Hagar. (Ex. 19:5, 6) Nna nyimpa biara nnyi hɔ a obotum edzi dɛm Mbra no do pɛpɛɛpɛr. Ntsi aber biara nna Mbra no kaa Israelfo no dɛ wɔyɛ bɔn no nkowaa. Afei so, nna dɛm apam no botum aboa Israelman no ama sɛ Messiah no ba a woehu no. Nna Messiah no nkotsee nye obi a obotum edzi Mbra no do pɛpɛɛpɛr. Aber a Messiah no dze no nkwa a odzi mu no bɔɔ afɔr no, afei dze nna nyimpa a bɔn wɔ hɔnho no botum adze hɔnho efi bɔn na owu nsamu. (Gal. 3:19, 24, 25) Ntsi Jesus wui no, nna no ho nnhia dɛ wodzi mbra no do bio.—Rom. 10:4.
<<<P>>>
Paul dze Sarah totoo Hagar ho. Na ɔkaa dɛ Sarah yɛ “ɔbaa a ɔdze noho” na ɔno dze “ɔnam bɔhyɛ do na ɔwoo ne ba no.” (Gal. 4:23) Dɛm bɔhyɛ no nye apam a Jehovah nye Abraham yɛe no. (Gal. 3:29; 4:28, 30) Dɛm bɔhyɛ yi ho asɛm wɔ Genesis 22:18. Hɔ no Jehovah kaa kyerɛɛ Abraham dɛ: “W’asefo mu na wobehyira asaase amanaman nyinara; osiandɛ etsie me ndze.”

* Iyi yɛ nkyerɛkyerɛmu fofor a yeenya. Yɛdze risi dza ɔwɔ Ɔweɔn-Aban a ɔbae wɔ March 15, 2006, nkr. 10-12 mu no ananmu.
<<<P>>>
Apam a Jehovah nye Abraham yɛe yi buee bɔ a Jehovah hyɛe wɔ Eden ture no enyi do kakra. (Gen. 3:15) Dɛm apam yi maa no mu daa hɔ dɛ, asenyi a wɔkaa no ho asɛm no befi Abraham mu aba. Paul kyerɛkyerɛɛ mu dɛ Jesus Christ nye ‘asenyi’ no ankasa. Naaso hɔn a “wɔyɛ Christ nedze no,” kyerɛ dɛ, 144,000 a wɔnye no bɔbɔ mu edzi adze wɔ n’Ahendzi no mu no, hɔn so ka dɛm “asenyi” no ho. (Gal. 3:16, 29; Nyi. 14:1-3) Osian bɔ a Nyankopɔn hyɛɛ Abraham ntsi, hɔn a wɔgye Jesus to mu na wotsie no no benya nhyira pii.
<<<P>>>
Ɔnodze, nna ebɛnadze na Paul pɛ dɛ ɔkyerɛ aber a ɔkaa dɛ “iyinom gyina hɔ ma biribi” no? Nna ɔpɛ dɛ ɔkyerɛ dɛ sɛ Jewfo a wɔwɔ n’aber do no si nketse dɛ mbra no ara na wobedzi do a, nna ɔno wɔbɛyɛ dɛ Hagar; wɔbɛyɛ nkowaa daa. Naaso sɛ wɔgye Jesus Christ a ɔyɛ Abraham ‘n’asenyi’ no ankasa no to mu a, ɔno wɔbɛyɛ dɛ Sarah, na wɔadze hɔnho afebɔɔ. Wobenya fahodzi ankasa efi bɔn na owu mu. (John 8:32-34) Naaso sɛ wɔanngye Jesus annto mu a, ɔkyerɛ dɛ wɔnntse Mbra apam no ase, osiandɛ nna Mbra apam no na ɔbɔboa hɔn ma woehu Christ no.
<<<P>>>
Galatiafo a nna Paul rekyerɛw krataa yi akɛma hɔn no, nna hɔn mu beberee nnyɛ Jewfo, ntsi nna Mbra no mmfa hɔnho anaa ɔnnkyekyer hɔn. Naaso nna Jewfo binom agye egyina mu dzendzendzen dɛ Christianfo a wɔnnyɛ Jewfo no ndzi Mbra no do. Sɛ yɛbɛyɛ mfatoho a, nna wɔse kyenkyen ara a wontwa hɔn bɔtɔ. Paul kyerɛɛ mu dɛ, ntseasee nnyi mu koraa dɛ wodzi Mbra a ɔdze hɔn ayɛ ‘nkowaa’ no do, osiandɛ nna Christ ama woenya “fahodzi.”—Gal. 5:1, 10-14.
