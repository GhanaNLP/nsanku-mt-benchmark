4 Sɛ yɛdze hɛn adwen si suban pa a hɛn nua-
nom no wɔ no do a, obotum aboa hɛn ma yɛa-
kɔ do ara nye hɔn atsena kama. Naaso, sɛ yɛ-
nye hɛn nuanom no ammbɔ a, yebesi dɛn
ehu suban pa ahorow a wɔwɔ no? Nna ɔsoma-
fo Paul n’enyi gye ho paa dɛ ɔnye no nuanom
bɔbɔ. Ntsi ɔkaa kyerɛɛ Christianfo a wɔwɔ
Thessalonica no dɛ: “Yɛyɛɛ dza yebotum bia-
ra dɛ yebohu hom enyim bio.” (1 Thess. 2:
17) Enuanom beberee se, sɛ wohyia hɔn nua-
nom Christianfo na wɔnye hɔn robɔ nkɔm-
bɔ a, ɔma wɔbɛn hɔnho paa. Ntsi woara so,
enye wo nuanom mbɔ nkɔmbɔ atse. Sɛ hom
nam asɛnka mu, hom hyia wɔ adzesua anaa
ehyiadzi ase a, enye hɔn mbɔ nkɔmbɔ. Naaso,
sɛ etaa nye wo nuanom Christianfo bɔ a, ɔno
ara nnkyerɛ dɛ enye hɔn ntamu bɔkɔ do ara
ayɛ kama.
<<<P>>>
5 Sɛ yɛwɔ suban pa a, ɔmmfa ho dza obesi
wɔ yɛnye hɛn nuanom ntamu biara no, ye-
botum esiesie. (Kenkan Colossaefo 3:12.)
Sɛ yɛbrɛ hɛnho adze, na yɛwɔ ayamuhyehye,
ayamuyie na abotar a, obotum aboa hɛn ma
yɛakɔ do afa hɛn nuanom nyɛnko aber mpo a
wɔayɛ adze ma ahaw hɛn no. Dɛ mbrɛ kaar hia
engine oil ama engine no etum ayɛ edwuma yie
no, dɛmara so na sɛ yɛwɔ suban pa a, obotum
aboa hɛn ma yɛnye hɛn nuanom atsena asom-
dwee mu. Afei, hom mma yɛnhwɛ ndzɛmba
ebiasa bi a obotum asɛɛ anyɛnkofa a yɛnye
hɛn nuanom wɔ no. Sɛ yerususu no mu biara
ho a, yebohu dɛ sɛ yesuasua ahobrɛadze, abo-
tar, ayamuhyehye nye ɔdwe a nna Jesus wɔ
no a, obotum aboa hɛn ma yɛakɔ do ara afa
hɛn nuanom nyɛnko.

6 Ebɛnadze ntsi na obotum asɛɛ hom ntamu?
Sɛ akasakasa bi si enuanom beenu bi ntamu a,
mpɛn pii no ibohu dɛ hɔn mu biara susu dɛ
ɔno na n’asɛm yɛ dɛw. Ɔwodze yɛmfa no dɛ
enuanom beenu bi adwen nnhyia wɔ asaasesin
pɔtsee a wɔpɛ dɛ hɔn asɛnka kuw no kɛyɛ mu
edwuma. Hɔn mu biara susu dɛ dza ɔreka no
na ɔbɔboa. Dɛm asɛm yi botum akɛfa akasa-
kasa aba hɔn ntamu. Ntsi sɛ wɔammbrɛ hɔnho
adze na wɔannhwɛ annka asɛm no, na obiara
dze n’asɛm hyɛ ne tsirmu a, obotum asɛɛ hɔn
ntamu. Afei ibohu dɛ asɛm a ɔnnyɛ asɛm bia-
ra no, nna afei dze abɛyɛ asɛm.
