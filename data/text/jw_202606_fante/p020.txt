DZA YEBOSUA
Yɛbɔhwɛ siantsir a ohia dɛ yɛkɔ do ara tsie dza Jeho-vah dze kyerɛkyerɛ hɛn wɔ adzesua nye nhyiamu afo-for ase no yie nye kwan a yebotum afa do ayɛ dɛm.
<<<P>>>
Sɛ Jehovah Rekyerɛkyerɛ Hɛn A, Yɛ Aso Tsie
<<<P>>>
“Hom nhwɛ mbrɛ hom si tsie asɛm no yie.”—LUKE 8:18.
<<<P>>>
NDƐ, Jehovah kyerɛkyerɛ hɛn ndzɛmba pii mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a ye-botum ehu no yie. Dɛm ntsi yɛnye David yɛ adwen paa wɔ asɛm a ɔkae yi ho. Ɔkaa dɛ: ‘Jehovah nye mo hwɛfo; na hwee nnkehia me.’—Kenkan Ndwom 23:1, 2, 5.
<<<P>>>
2 Jehovah kyerɛkyerɛ hɛn wɔ hɛn asafo nhyiamu ahorow no ase. Mpɛn pii no, dza yɛtse wɔ hɔ no yɛ biribi a yenyim no da-da. Sɛ ‘hɛn ase etsim wɔ nokwar no mu’ mpo a, ɔno nnkyerɛ dɛ yennhia nsɛm a ɔtse dɛm no. (2 Pet. 1:12) Naaso sɛ dza ye-rusua no yɛ biribi a atse atse araa, ɔyɛ a ɔyɛ dzen ma wo dɛ ebɛyɛ aso etsie anaa? Sɛ dɛm dze a, nna ɔno dɛm adzesua yi bɔboa wo. Yɛbɔhwɛ siantsir a ohia dɛ yetsie asɛm yie aber a yɛakɔ asafo nhyiamu bi ase no. Yɛbɛsan so ahwɛ dza akasafo yɛ a ɔbɛma etsiefo no akyɛn hɔn aso etsie dza wɔreka no. Afei so, yɛbɔhwɛ nyansahyɛ ahorow bi a obotum aboa hɛn ma yee-tsie asɛm yie wɔ adzesua ase. Naaso, hom mma yendzi kan nhwɛ siantsir a ɔsɛ dɛ yɛkaa obi a ɔkyerɛkyerɛ hɛn wɔ hɛn nhyiamu ase no.

KAA DƐ JEHOVAH NA ƆREKASA KYERƐ HƐN
<<<P>>>
3 Jehovah fa n’Asɛm Bible no do nye hɛn kasa. (2 Tim. 3:16, 17) Osiandɛ nna Jesus nyim dɛm asɛm yi ntsi, ɔdze Nyanko-pɔn n’Asɛm no kyerɛkyerɛɛ nkorɔfo. Na iyi kaa hɔn akoma araa ma wobisaa dɛ: “Ɔyɛ dɛn na ɔbarimba yi nyim Kyerɛw-nsɛm no mu dɛm aber a ɔnnkɔr skuul biara yi?” (John 7:15) Hɔn a nna wɔpɛ dɛ wosua Jehovah ho adze no enyi gyee mbrɛ Jesus kyerɛkyerɛɛ Nyankopɔn n’Asɛm no mu no kyerɛɛ hɔn no ho paa. Christianfo a wɔtsenaa ase wɔ asomafo hɔn aber do
<<<P>>>
1-2. (a) Ebɛnadze na Jehovah yɛ ma n’asomfo ndɛ? (Ndwom 23:1, 2, 5) (b) Ebɛnadze na yebosusu ho wɔ adzesua yi mu?
<<<P>>>
3. Ebɛn kwan na Jehovah fa do nye hɛn kasa, na n’asomfo so si dɛn dze n’A-sɛm Bible no dzi dwuma?
