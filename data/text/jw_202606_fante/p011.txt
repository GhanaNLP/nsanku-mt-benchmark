ABER A ƆSOR MBRƐ WO NUA NO SI YƐ N’ADZE KORAA
<<<P>>>
10 Ebɛnadze ntsi na obotum ayɛ dzen ama hɛn dɛ yɛbɛfa enuanom binom nyɛnko? Hɛn nua-nom binom wɔ hɔ a, hɔn suban anaa mbrɛ wosi yɛ hɔn adze ntsi, ɔyɛ a yɛmmpɛ dɛ yɛfa hɔn nyɛnko mpo. Binom wɔ hɔ a, mbrɛ wosi yɛ hɔn adze no botum ama hɔnho ayɛ hɛn ehi. Binom so wɔ hɔ a, nsɛm ahorow bi a woesisi wɔ hɔn abrabɔ mu anaa beebi a wɔtsetsee hɔn ntsi, ɔyɛ dzen ma hɔn dɛ wɔbɛka hɔnho akɔ nyimpa mu. Binom so dze, beebiara wɔwɔ hɔ bi. Yɛreka yi, bi mpo a efɛr adze na emmpɛ ka-sa, naaso hɔn dze wɔpɛ kasa paa na beebi a wɔnam biara so obiara tse hɔn nka.
<<<P>>>
11 Suasua abotar a nna Jesus wɔ no. Jesus faa hɔn a nna ɔsor hɔn suban no nyɛnko. Mber bi, Jesus n’asomafo beenu, James na John kɛkaa kyerɛɛ no dɛ sɛ ɔkɔ n’Ahendzi mu a, ɔmma hɔn mu kor ntsena ne nsa nyimfa na kor so ntsena ne bankum. Dza wɔyɛe yi ma yehu dɛ nna wobu hɔnho dɛ wɔkyɛn asomafo nkaa no. (Mark 10:35-37) Naaso Jesus dze, nna ɔbrɛ noho adze. Hokwan na dzibew a nna ɔwɔ wɔ sor no mpo, ogyaa ne nyina hɔ baa asaase yi do. (Phil. 2:5-8) Nna ɔsor Jesus no suban, nna ɔsor James nye no nua John so hɔn suban. Naaso Jesus kɔr do nyaa n’asoma-fo yi na afofor ho abotar.

12. Ebɛnadze na ɔboaa Jesus ma onyaa n’esuafo no ho abotar?
<<<P>>>
pɛr aber nyina. Nna onyim dɛ hɔn mu dodow noara pɛ dzibew, ntsi aber a James na John dze hɔn adzeserɛ no kɔtoo n’enyim no, annyɛ no nwanwa koraa. (Mark 9:34) Beebi a Jesus nye n’esuafo no nyinii no, nna sɛ obi wɔ dzi-bew anaa ɔyɛ nyimpa tsitsir a, nna wobu no paa. Ntsi nna ɔtse hɔn ase. Naaso ne nyina mu no, onyaa hɔnho abotar, osiandɛ nna onyim dɛ ɔnnyɛ da kor na wobotum dze asesa hɔn su-ban.—Mark 10:42-45.
<<<P>>>
13 Dza obotum aboa ma hom akɔ do ara atse-na asomdwee mu. Sɛ wo nua bi no suban bi hyɛ wo ebufuw mpo a, kɔ do ara nya no ho abotar. (Mbɛ. 14:29) Ɔsor obiara na mbrɛ osi yɛ n’a-dze, ntsi sɛ w’enyi nngye mbrɛ obi si yɛ n’a-dze no ho a, ɔno nnkyerɛ dɛ no suban nnyɛ. Mbom dɛmara na noara so tse. Sɛ nkorɔfo nya woho abotar a, ɔma w’enyi gye. (Kenkan Ephesusfo 4:2.) Ntsi woara so, sɛ dza wo nua bi yɛ no hyɛ wo ebufuw mpo a, kɔ do ara nya no ho abotar na tse no ase. Sɛ wo nua bi mmpɛ kasa anaa ɔfɛr adze a, tse ase dɛ obo-tum ayɛ dzen ama no dɛ ɔnye nkorɔfo bɔbɔ nkɔmbɔ. Sɛ wo nua bi so pɛ kasa paa a, nya no ho abotar. Ɔyɛ nokwar dɛ yɛbɛn hɛn nuanom binom kyɛn binom. Naaso enuanom mpo a ɔsor mbrɛ wosi yɛ hɔn adze no, yebotum nye hɔn atsena ma wɔayɛ yie.
<<<P>>>
14 Sɛ yɛwɔ hɛn nuanom ho abotar a, ɔbɛma yɛdze hɛn adwen esi suban pa ahorow a wɔwɔ no do. Yɛyɛ dɛm a, yɛremma mbrɛ wosi yɛ hɔn adze no nnsɛɛ yɛnye hɔn ntamu. Sɛ ehwɛ Ja-mes na John hɔn asɛm no a, ibohu dɛ nna wɔyɛ pɛsɛankonya na nna wɔpɛ dzibew so. Naaso dza wɔyɛe no so maa ɔdaa edzi dɛ wɔ-gye dzi dɛ Ahendzi no bɛba ɔkwan biara do.
