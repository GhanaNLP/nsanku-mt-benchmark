Hɛn Enyi Sɔ Werdam No
<<<P>>>
DZA YEBOSUA
<<<P>>>
Yɛbɔhwɛ dza hɛn nyina botum ayɛ dze akyerɛ dɛ hɛn enyi sɔ werdam no.
<<<P>>>
Ebɛnadze Na Ebɛyɛ Dze Akyerɛ Werdam No Ho Enyisɔ?
<<<P>>>
“Ɔdɔ a Christ wɔ no hyɛ hɛn.”—2 COR. 5:14.
<<<P>>>
SƐ ƆWO na ehyɛ dan bi a ebu egu mu na obi ebeyi wo a, nkyɛ erennkyerɛ dza nyimpa no ayɛ ama wo no ho enyisɔ anaa? Sɛ ɔnnyɛ wankotsee mpo na nyimpa no gyee wo a, bi a wankasa so bɛpɛ dɛ eda no ase na ema ohu dɛ w’enyi sɔ dza ɔayɛ ama wo no paa.
<<<P>>>
2 Dɛ mbrɛ yehunii wɔ adzesua a odzi iyi enyim mu no, hɛnankasa runntum nngye hɛnho mmfi bɔn na owu nsamu. Naaso Jesus no werdam afɔrbɔ no botum agye hɛn na wɔama (1) yeenya bɔnfakyɛ, (2) yeenya enyidado dɛ daakye sintɔ a wɔdze awo hɛn no befi hɔ, na (3) yɛasan nye Nyankopɔn ntamu ayɛ kama bio. Iyi ntsi yɛwɔ enyidado dɛ yebotum atsena ase daa wɔ Jehovah no wiadze fofor no mu. Werdam no ma yehu dɛ Jesus dɔ hɛn paa. Mbrɛ ɔtse nye dɛ, ansaana ɔreba asaase yi do mpo no, nna ɔdɔ nyimpa. (Mbɛ. 8:30, 31) Ɔsomafo Paul kyerɛɛw dɛ “ɔdɔ a Christ wɔ no hyɛ hɛn.” (Kenkan 2 Corinthfo 5:14, 15.) Dza iyi kyerɛ nye dɛ, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛma ɔdɔ a Jesus ada no edzi akyerɛ hɛn no som bo ma hɛn na yɛkyerɛ ho enyisɔ.
<<<P>>>
3 Ebɛnadze na werdam afɔrbɔ no bɛhyɛ wo ma ayɛ? Ɔsor dza ebɛyɛ na ɔsor dza obi fofor so bɛyɛ. Ma yɛmfa mfatoho nkyerɛkyerɛ asɛm yi mu: Fa no dɛ nyimpa baasa bi pɛ dɛ wotu kwan kɔ kurow bi do, naaso ɔsor beebi a hɔn mu biara wɔ. Osiandɛ hɔn nyina nnyi fakor ntsi, ɔbɔsor “kwan” a hɔn mu biara bɛfa do ekodu kurow no mu. Dɛmara na hɛn so, yɛpɛ dɛ yɛkyerɛ werdam no ho enyisɔ, naaso ɔsor anyɛnkofa a hɛn mu biara nye Jehovah wɔ,
<<<P>>>
1-2. Ebɛnadze na ɔsɛ dɛ Jesus no werdam afɔrbɔ no hyɛ hɛn ma yɛyɛ, na ebɛnadze ntsi a? (2 Corinthfo 5:14, 15) (Ibotum ahwɛ mfonyin no so.)
<<<P>>>
3. Ebɛnadze ntsi na ɔsor dza hɛn mu biara bɛyɛ dze akyerɛ werdam no ho enyisɔ no?
