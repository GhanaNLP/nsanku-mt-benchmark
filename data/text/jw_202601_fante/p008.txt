<<<P>>>
DZA YEBOSUA
<<<P>>>
Yɛbɔhwɛ dza yebotum ayɛ aber a awerɛhow anaa dadwen ahyɛ hɛn do no.
<<<P>>>
Jehovah Botum Aboa Wo Aber A Wo Werɛ Ahow No
<<<P>>>
“Ao m’asɛm yɛ mbɔbɔr!”—ROM. 7:24.
<<<P>>>
SƐ ETSE ɔsomafo Paul ho asɛm a, ebɛn asɛm na ɔba w’adwen mu? Bi a ebɛka dɛ nna ɔyɛ asɛmpatserɛwnyi a ɔwɔ akokodur, obi a onyim nkyerɛkyerɛ yie, anaa obi a ɔkyerɛɛw Bible mu nwoma beberee. Sɛ eka dɛm a, inntsii. Naaso nna Paul so yɛ nyimpa tse dɛ hɛnara, ntsi ɔtɔ da a, nna no werɛ tum how, n’abaw mu bu na dadwen so tum hyɛ no do.
<<<P>>>
² Kenkan Romefo 7:21-24. Krataa a Paul kyerɛwee kɛmaa Romefo no, ɔkaa mbrɛ nna ɔtɔ da a osi tse nka no ho asɛm wɔ mu, na ndɛ so hɛn mu piinara tum tse nka dɛmara. Ɔwɔ mu, nna Paul yɛ Christiannyi a ɔsom Nyankopɔn yie na nna ɔpɛ paa dɛ ɔyɛ Nyankopɔn n’apɛdze, naaso osian ne sintɔ ntsi, nna ɔnnyɛ aber nyina na ɔyɛ mberɛw ma no dɛ ɔbɛyɛ dɛm. Iyi da nkyɛn a, nna ɔtɔ da a ndzɛmba bɔn bi a ɔayɛ etwa mu ho asɛm tum haw no, na nna ɔhaw bi a wɔabɛgye egua wɔ no do so retseetsee no, na iyinom maa no werɛ howee.
<<<P>>>
³ Nna ɔtɔ da a dza Paul refa mu no ma no werɛ tum how na n’abaw mu so bu, naaso ɔamma iyi ho adwendwen annhyɛ no do.* Adzesua yi mu no, yebosusu nsɛmbisa a odzidzi do yi ho: Ebɛnadze na ɔmaa Paul kaa dɛ ‘n’asɛm yɛ mbɔbɔr’ no? Ebɛnadze na ɔboaa Paul ma dadwen anaa awerɛhow annhyɛ no do? Hɛn so, ebɛnadze na obotum aboa hɛn amma dadwen anaa awerɛhow annhyɛ hɛn do?
<<<P>>>
* ASƐM BI A WƆAKYERƐKYERƐ MU: Adzesua yi mu no, yɛreka hɔn a ɔwɔ mu ara a hɔn werɛ tum how anaa dadwen hyɛ hɔn do no ho asɛm. Ɔnnyɛ hɔn a wɔwɔ adwen mu yarba ma ɔno ntsi ɔsɛ dɛ wokohu dɔkta no ho asɛm na yɛreka.
<<<P>>>
1-2. Nna ɔtɔ da a ɔsomafo Paul tse nka dɛn, na ebɛnadze ntsi na dza ɔkyerɛwee faa noho no botum aboa hɛn? (Romefo 7:21-24)
<<<P>>>
3. Ebɛnadze na yebosusu ho wɔ adzesua yi mu? (Ibotum ahwɛ “Asɛm Bi A Wɔakyerɛkyerɛ Mu” no so.)
