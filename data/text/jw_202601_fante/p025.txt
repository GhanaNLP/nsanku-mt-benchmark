16 Sɛ ehwɛ nwomaba a wɔato dzin San Bra Jehovah Nkyɛn no mu a, ibohu dɛm nkuranhyesɛm yi: “Nya awerɛhyɛmu dɛ, sɛ esan ba Jehovah ne nkyɛn a, ɔbɛgye wo. Ɔbɔboa wo ma egyina wo dadwen ano, na eyi yaw biara efi wo mu. Afei ibenya tsibowa pa, na ɔbɛma w’asomu adwe wo na w’akoma so atɔ wo yamu. Ɔno bekenyan wo ma enye wo nuanom Christianfo asan asom Jehovah bio.” Ma ɔntsena w’adwen mu so dɛ, asafo mu mpanyimfo pɛ dɛ wɔboa wo. Wɔtse dɛ “huntsabea wɔ mframa ano, na guandɔbea wɔ ehum ano.” (Isa. 32:2) Yenyim dɛ w’enyi sɔ werdam no, ntsi bisa woho dɛ, ‘Ebɛn ndzɛmba na mubotum ayɛ no dɛm aber yi dze esiesie menye Jehovah ntamu?’ (Isa. 1:18; 1 Pet. 2:25) Ibotum akɔ asafo nhyiamu wɔ Ahendzi Asa do. Ibotum so akɔ asafo mu panyin bi nkyɛn na aka akyerɛ no dɛ ihia mboa ama asan abɔsom Jehovah bio. Bi a ɔbɛyɛ nhyehyɛɛ ma obi nye wo esua Bible no ekesi ber bi. Nya awerɛhyɛmu dɛ mbɔdzen a erobɔ ama etum abɔsom Jehovah bio no kyerɛ dɛ w’enyi sɔ werdam afɔrbɔ no, na Jehovah behyira do.

17 Jesus kaa dɛ Jehovah somaa no dɛ ɔmfa no nkwa mbɔbɔ afɔr “ama obiara a ɔbɛgye no edzi no annsɛɛ, na mbom oeenya onnyiewiei nkwa.” (John 3:16) Ntsi Jehovah nam werdam no do bɛgye hɛn efi bɔn na owu nsamu. Osian iyi ntsi ɔsɛ dɛ hɛn mu biara ma werdam no som no bo. (Rom. 3:23, 24; 2 Cor. 6:1) Sɛ ɔka nda kakra ma yɛakɔ Nkaadzi no a, ɔyɛ a hom mma yɛngye ber ndwendwen ɔdɔ a Jehovah na Jesus ada no edzi akyerɛ hɛn no ho, na hom mma yɛmma dɛm ɔdɔ no nkenyan hɛn ma yɛnkyerɛ dza wɔayɛ ama hɛn no ho enyisɔ.

18 Ebɛnadze na ebɛyɛ dze akyerɛ dɛ w’enyi sɔ werdam no? Dza ebɛyɛ dze akyerɛ ho enyisɔ no, bi ɔnnyɛ ɔno na obi fofor so bɛyɛ. Naaso kaa dɛ, Jehovah n’asomfo pii robɔ mbɔdzen dɛ ‘wɔrenntsena ase mma hɔnho bio, na mbom wɔbɛtsena ase ama obi a owui maa hɔn no.’ Ntsi nya awerɛhyɛmu dɛ mbɔdzen a woara so robɔ dze ayɛ dɛm no, Jehovah behyira do.—2 Cor. 5:15.
