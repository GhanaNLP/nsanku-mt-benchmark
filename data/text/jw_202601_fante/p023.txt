10 Ɔsomafo Paul kaa kyerɛɛ Timothy dɛ ɔntaa ndwendwen ndzɛmba a ɔyɛ dze som Jehovah no ho na ɔmfa n’akoma nhyɛ dza ɔyɛ biara mu. (Kenkan 1 Timothy 4:13, 15.) Woara so botum dze dɛm afotu yi ayɛ edwuma, na adwendwen ndzɛmba a eyɛ a ɔbɔboa wo ma asom Jehovah yie no ho, mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a ebɔkɔ do ara ‘ayɛ hyew wɔ sunsum mu.’ (Rom. 12:11; hwɛ “Be aglow with the spirit” no ho nkyerɛkyerɛmu wɔ nwtsty-E no mu.) Bi a obehia dɛ idzi kan sua dza yebosua wɔ asafo nhyiamu ase no yie ama sɛ ekɔ a etum ayɛ aso etsie dwumadzi no yie. Anaa sɛ wankasa rusua adze na epɛ dɛ inya do mfaso paa a, bi a obehia dɛ epɛ beebi a hɔ yɛ dzinn ama etum adwendwen dza ibosua no ho yie. Sɛ esɔ gya na sɛ orudum a, ibokua mu ama woetum adzɛw yie. Dɛmara so na sɛ ihu dɛ enyisɔ a ewɔ ma werdam no nye ndzɛmba afofor a Jehovah dze ama wo no rokɔ famu a, ibotum ayɛ dza yɛaka ho asɛm yi, na obekenyan wo ma akɔ do ara akyerɛ ho enyisɔ tse dɛ nkan no. Afei so, sɛ ɔka kakra ma yɛakɔ Nkaadzi no a, ibotum adwendwen nhyira ahorow a Jehovah dze ama n’Adasefo no ho. Mbrɛ ɔtse nye dɛ, sɛ eyɛ dɛm a, ɔbɛma enyisɔ a ewɔ ma werdam no ayɛ kɛse, osiandɛ sɛ ɔnnyɛ werdam no a, nkyɛ yerunntum mmfa Jehovah nyɛnko.

12 Ɔtɔ da a nna biribiara mmpusuw Paul dɛ ɔnka asɛmpa no. Naaso, sɛ nna ɔtse nka dɛm mpo a, nna ɔmmpa abaw mbom nna omia n’enyi yɛ no som edwuma no. Ntsi woara so botum esuasua Paul. Sɛ ɔtɔ da na biribiara mmpusuw wo dɛ yɛ dza Jehovah pɛ mpo a, ɔyɛ a mia w’enyi na yɛ. Ibotum aserɛ Jehovah dɛ ɔmma nya ɔpɛ nye ahoɔdzen a edze bɛyɛ dɛm. (Phil. 2:13) Kɔ do ara yɛ ndzɛmba a ɔbɔboa wo ma asom Jehovah yie no. Sɛ eyɛ dɛm a, ibotum enya awerɛhyɛmu dɛ ber rokɔ do no, ɔdɔ a ewɔ ma Jehovah no mu bɛsan ayɛ dzen na ahokeka a nna edze som no so bɛsan aba bio.
