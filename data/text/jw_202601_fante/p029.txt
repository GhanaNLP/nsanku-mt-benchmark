MBRƐ YEBESI AKA NOKWAR NO
<<<P>>>
10 Kenkan Colossaefo 4:6. Ɔsomafo Paul kaa Christianfo a wɔwɔ Colossae no dɛ, ɔsɛ dɛ wɔma hɔn anomu kasa yɛ dɛw aber nyina. Dɛm asɛm yi kyerɛ dɛn? Greek asɛmfua a wɔdze dzii dwuma wɔ ha no ma yehu dɛ sɛ yɛrekasa a, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛka nsɛm a hɛn etsiefo no benya do mfaso, yɛnye hɔn kasa yie, na yɛka nsɛm so a ɔbɛka hɔn akoma.
<<<P>>>
11 Sɛ yɛrekyerɛkyerɛ nkorɔfo nokwar no a, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛdze Paul n’afotu no yɛ edwuma na yɛma hɛn anomu kasa yɛ dɛw. Bible no ka dɛ nokwasɛm a ɔwɔ Nyankopɔn n’Asɛm no mu no tse dɛ sekan a n’ano yɛ nam, na otum paapaa hɛn kra nye hɛn sunsum mu. Dza dɛm asɛm yi kyerɛ nye dɛ, Nyankopɔn n’Asɛm no tum ma dza ɔwɔ hɛn akoma mu nye hɛn adwen mu no da edzi. (Heb. 4:12) Naaso, sɛ yɛammfa Bible no enndzi dwuma yie a, obotum ahyɛ nkorɔfo ebufuw na wɔakɛfa ekyingye hun aba. Biribi a ɔtse dɛm besi dɛn esi?
<<<P>>>
12 Yɛmfa no dɛ yɛnam asɛnka mu na yɛakɔto papa bi. Yeehu dɛ ɔbɔ ehonyi mpaa, na ɔnye n’ebusua so dzi Borɔnya na Easter. Yebotum dze Bible no aboa no ma oehu dɛ nyansa biara nnyi mu dɛ ɔbɔbɔ ehonyi mpaa, na Borɔnya na Easter nyina so fi abosonsɔr mu. (Isa. 44:14-20; 2 Cor. 6:14-17) Naaso, sɛ yɛka dɛm asɛm yi kyerɛ papa no da a yehyiaa no no ara a, ɔwɔ mu dɛ yɛbɛka nokwar no dze, naaso nna yɛremmfa Nyankopɔn n’Asɛm no nndzi dwuma yie.
<<<P>>>
13 Paul sanee so kaa kyerɛɛ hɛn dɛ ɔsɛ dɛ yɛma hɛn kasaa yɛ dza nkyen wɔ mu. Nna ɔnnyɛ dza ɔpɛ dɛ ɔkyerɛ nye dɛ yenkyinkyim nokwar no anaa yɛmfa nsuma. Mbom nna
<<<P>>>
10. Sɛ wɔka dɛ obi n’anomu kasa yɛ dɛw a, ɔkyerɛ dɛn? (Colossaefo 4:6)
<<<P>>>
11-12. Ebɛnadze ntsi na sɛ yɛrekyerɛkyerɛ nkorɔfo nokwar no a, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛhwɛ hɛn kasaa yie? Fa mfatoho kyerɛkyerɛ mu. (Ibotum ahwɛ mfonyin ahorow no so.)
<<<P>>>
13. Yebesi dɛn ama hɛn kasaa ayɛ dza nkyen wɔ mu?
<<<P>>>
Ibesi dɛn akyerɛkyerɛ nokwar no yie?
(Hwɛ nkyekyɛmu 11-12)
