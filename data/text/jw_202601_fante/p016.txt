8 Ɔrennkyɛr ma yɛakɔ afe yi Nkaadzi no, ntsi ɔyɛ a gye ber dwendwen nhyira ahorow a Jehovah no bɔnfakyɛ ama enya no ho. Nhyira no bi nye dɛ, osian werdam no ntsi, onnyi dɛ ema wo tsibowa kɔ do ara bu wo fɔ wɔ bɔn bi a ayɛ etwa mu no ho. Naaso, sɛ ɔyɛ dzen ma wo dɛ ebɛgye ato mu dɛ Jehovah dze wo bɔn akyɛ wo so ɛ? Bi a ebɛka dɛ, ‘Minyim dɛ Jehovah botum dze mo bɔn akyɛ me, naaso mankasa dze, murunntum mmfa nnkyɛ moho.’ Sɛ enya adwen a ɔtse dɛm dze a, nna ɔno kaa dɛ: Jehovah na ɔdze bɔn kyɛ, na ɔnoara so na ɔdze ahyɛ ne Ba no ne nsa dɛ ombu atsɛn. Jehovah mmfaa nnhyɛɛ ɔwo anaa obi fofor biara ne nsa dɛ ɔnkyerɛ obi a obotum dze no bɔn akyɛ no anaa ɔremmfa nnkyɛ no. Bible no ka dɛ: “Sɛ yɛnantsew kan mu dɛ mbrɛ [Jehovah] ɔwɔ kan mu no a, . . . ne Ba Jesus Christ bɔgya no tsew hɛn ho fi bɔn nyina mu.” (1 John 1:6, 7) Dɛm ntsi dɛ mbrɛ yɛgye Bible mu nkyerɛkyerɛ ahorow dzi no, dɛmara so na ɔsɛ dɛ yɛgye dzi dɛ Jehovah dze hɛn bɔn bɛkyɛ hɛn. Werdam no ma Jehovah nya biribi gyina do hu hɛn mbɔbɔr na ɔdze hɛn bɔn kyɛ hɛn. N’Asɛm Bible no mpo ma yehu dɛ ofi n’akoma mu ‘dze bɔn kyɛ.’—Ndw. 86:5.

9 Sɛ Bible no ka “bɔn” a, obotum akyerɛ ndzeyɛbɔn anaa sintɔ a wɔdze awo hɛn no. (Kenkan Ndwom 51:5.) Dɛm sintɔ a wɔdze awo hɛn no ma yɛyɛ bɔn, yɛyar, yɛbɔ nkɔkora na mberwa na yewu. Iyi ma yehu siantsir a mbofra a wɔawo hɔn fofor a wɔnnyɛɛ bɔn da no mpo tum yar na wowu no. Afei so, ɔma yehu siantsir a abɔnfo na apapafo nyina hu amandze na wowu no. Asɛm no ara nye dɛ, yɛyɛ Adam n’asefo yi ara dze, wɔdze bɔn awo hɛn nyina.
