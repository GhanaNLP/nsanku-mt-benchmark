Ɔwɔ dɛ yɛkɔ do sua Nyankopɔn n’Asɛm no yie, yɛhyɛ suban fofor no tse dɛ atar, na yɛma Jehovah bɔ hɛn ho ban
<<<P>>>
(Hwɛ nkyekyɛmu 18)
<<<P>>>
suban dadaw no gu, na yɛhyɛ suban fofor no dɛ atar. Dza iyi kyerɛ nye dɛ, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛdze dza Nyankopɔn kyerɛkyerɛ hɛn no bɔ hɛn bra. Ɔwɔ mu ara a, bi a suban bɔn bi a nna yɛwɔ no beyi ne tsir; bi a hɛn bo bofuw obi anaa yɛbɛka asɛm bi anaa yɛayɛ biribi ma wɔayɛ obi yaw. Sɛ ɔba no dɛm a, ɔnnsɛ dɛ yɛma hɛn abaw mu bu na yenya adwen dɛ yerunntum nnsɔ Jehovah n’enyi ara da. Mbom ɔsɛ dɛ yɛkɔ do yer hɛnho dɛ yɛbɛyɛ nsesa biara a ohia dɛ yɛyɛ. (Rom. 12:1, 2; Eph. 4:24) Aber a yɛrobɔ mbɔdzen dɛ yɛbɛyɛ dɛm no, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛkaa dɛm nokwasɛm yi: Sɛ yɛpɛ dɛ yɛhyɛ atar bi na ɔso anaa ɔkyekyer hɛn a, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛyɛ ho biribi ana yeetum ahyɛ. Naaso suban fofor no dze, sɛ yebotum ahyɛ a, ɔsɛ dɛ hɛnankasa mbom na yɛyɛ nsesa na yɛdze Nyankopɔn n’akwankyerɛ bɔ hɛn bra mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a yɛbɔsɔ n’enyi.

SUASUA DAVID—KƆ DO ARA MA JEHOVAH MBƆ WO HO BAN
<<<P>>>
14 Yeebohu dɛ sɛ yɛpɛ dɛ hɛn enyi gye paa a, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛkɔ do ara sua Nyankopɔn n’Asɛm no yie na yɛkɔ do ara hyɛ suban fofor no tse dɛ atar. Naaso ɔnnyɛ ɔno nkotsee, yehia Jehovah ne bambɔ so. Sɛ dɛm a, nna ɔno ebɛn akwan na Jehovah fa do bɔ hɛn ho ban, na ebɛnadze na ohia dɛ yɛyɛ ama ɔakɔ do ara ayɛ dɛm?
<<<P>>>
15 Nna Ɔhen David gye dzi paa dɛ sɛ ɔto ma ɔyɛ dzen a, Jehovah bɔbɔ no ho ban. (Kenkan Ndwom 27:5.) Ebɛn kwan na Jehovah fa do bɔ n’asomfo ho ban ndɛ? Kor nye dɛ, ɔbɔ hɛn ho ban fi obiara anaa biribiara a obotum asɛɛ yɛnye no ntamu no ho. Dɛm ntsi sɛ nkorɔfo soɛr tsia hɛn dɛ dɛn mpo a, ɔahyɛ hɛn bɔ dɛ akodze biara a wɔbɔbɔ etsia hɛn no, ɔrennyɛ yie. (Ndw. 34:7; Isa. 54:17) Ɔwɔ mu, Satan nye n’esunsum fi nye nkorɔfo a wɔsoɛr tsia hɛn no wɔ tum, naaso worunntum nntseetsee hɛn afebɔɔ. Sɛ woku hɛn mpo a, Jehovah benyan hɛn aba nkwa mu bio. (1 Cor. 15:55-57; Nyi. 21:3, 4) Afei so, sɛ dadwen hyɛ hɛn do a, Jehovah boa hɛn ma yemia hɛn enyi gyina ano mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a ɔrennsɛɛ yɛnye no ntamu afebɔɔ. (Mbɛ. 12:25; Matt. 6:27-29) Iyinom da nkyɛn a, hɛn Egya Jehovah ama hɛn enuanom beberee a wɔboa hɛn, nye asafo mu mpanyimfo a wɔserasera hɛn hyɛ hɛn nkuran. (Isa. 32:1, 2) Sɛ yɛkɔ asafo nhyiamu so a, yɛtaa sua ndzɛmba pɔtsee a yɛyɛ a ɔbɛma Jehovah abɔ hɛn ho ban.—Heb. 10:24, 25.
