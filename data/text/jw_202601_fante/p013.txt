18 Eliza a yeedzi kan aka no ho asɛm no suaa Job ho adze kɔr ekyir. Ɔkaa dɛ: “Mara so, ɔhaw a mihyiae no, nna ɔtoatoa do ntsi musuaa Job ho adze. Ɔhaw a Job faa mu no, nna ɔnnyɛ agor koraa na awerɛhosɛm so nye dɛ, nna onnyim beebi mpo a ofi. Naaso ne nyina mu no, ɔanntwe noho emmfi Jehovah ho da.” (Job 42:1-6) Diane a yeedzi kan aka no ho asɛm no so kaa dɛ: “Menye mo kun rusua Bɛn Jehovah buukuu no. Dɛ mbrɛ ɔwemfo dze dɛtse wen adze ma ɔyɛ fɛw no, dɛmara so na yehu dɛ Jehovah rowen hɛn ma yeenya suban ahorow a ɔyɛ fɛw, na iyi ma hɛn enyi gye. Ntsi sɛ yɛfom na hɛn abaw mu bu a, yɛmmfa hɛn adwen nnsi ɔno do, mbom yɛdze hɛn adwen si mbrɛ Jehovah roboa hɛn ma yɛabɛyɛ nyimpa pa no do. 19. Ɔwɔ mu ara a yɛbɛtse nka dɛn, naaso ebɛn awerɛhyɛmu na yebotum enya?
<<<P>>>
awerɛhow anaa dadwen a ahyɛ hɛn do no botu ayew korakora. Ɔwɔ mu ara a hɛn werɛ botum ahow. Naaso sɛ ndzɛmba a ɔkɛfa adwendwen ba no besi hɛn tsirmu a, Jehovah botum aboa hɛn ma yeeyi hɛn adwen efi do, na yɛdze hɛn adwen esi ndzɛmba pa do. Ntsi yebotum enya awerɛhyɛmu dɛ yebotum dze tsibowa pa asom Jehovah ma hɛn enyi agye wɔ asetsena mu.
<<<P>>>
20 Hom mma yensi hɛn bo dɛ yɛremma kwan ma bɔn bi a yɛayɛ etwa mu, ɔhaw ahorow bi a yerihyia nye hɛn sintɔ nnhyɛ hɛn do ma hɛn werɛ nnhow. Sɛ hɛn werɛ how anaa dadwen hyɛ hɛn do mpo a, Jehovah botum aboa hɛn na ɔaka hɛn akoma ato hɛn yamu. (Ndw. 143:10) Sɛ yɛkɔ wiadze fofor no mu a, yebotum ahwɛ kwan dɛ ndzɛmba a ɔbɛma hɛn enyi agye nkotsee na yɛbɔdwendwen ho. Dɛm aber no biribiara nnyi hɔ a obegya hɛn adwendwen, na yɛdze enyigye bɔsom hɛn Nyankopɔn Jehovah afebɔɔ!
