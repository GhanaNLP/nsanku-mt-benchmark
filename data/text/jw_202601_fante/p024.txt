nkɔ do nsɔ nhwɛ dɛ hom wɔ gyedzi no mu anaa; hom nkɔ do mpɛnsapɛnsa hom mu nhwɛ nyimpakor a hom yɛ ankasa.” Dɛm ntsi dɛ mbrɛ Paul kae no, ɔwɔ mu ara a ɔsɛ dɛ yɛhwehwɛ hɛnho mu hwɛ dɛ hɛn abrabɔ tse dɛn na yɛda ho ara nam gyedzi no mu anaa. Osiandɛ sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm a, ɔbɛma yɛadwen dza Jehovah na Jesus ayɛ ama hɛn no ho paa na obekenyan hɛn ma yɛakyerɛ werdam no ho enyisɔ. Na dɛ mbrɛ Paul kae no, sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm so a, nna yɛreda no edzi dɛ yɛnntse ase mma hɛnho bio, na mbom yɛtse ase ma Christ.*

* Sɛ epɛ dɛ ihu ndzɛmba bi a ibotum ayɛ dze akyerɛ dɛ w’enyi sɔ dza Jehovah ayɛ ama wo no a, hwɛ asɛm a wɔato dzin “Ebɛnadze Na Okenyan Wo Ma Esom Nyankopɔn?” Ɔwɔ June 15, 1995 Ɔweɔn-Aban no mu.
<<<P>>>
14. Ebɛnadze na obotum ama Christianfo binom atwe hɔnho efi asafo no ho?
<<<P>>>
Jehovah, na binom so dze, wɔbɔsoom Jehovah no abosoom bi ekyir na wogyae. Ebɛnadze na ɔmaa wogyae? Binom wɔ hɔ a “ɔbra yi mu dadwen” na ɔhyɛɛ hɔn do. (Luke 21:34) Binom so dze, asɛm bi a hɔn nua Christiannyi bi kae anaa adze bi a ɔyɛe no na ɔtoo hɔn fintsidua. (Jas. 3:2) Binom so wɔ hɔ a, wɔyɛɛ bɔn kɛse bi naaso osiandɛ nna wɔfɛr ntsi wɔamma asafo mu mpanyimfo ammboa hɔn. Naaso, ɔmmfa ho dza wɔama atwe woho efi asafo no ho biara no, ebɛnadze na ibotum ayɛ wɔ ho? Ebɛnadze na ɔsɛ dɛ werdam no nye ɔdɔ a Jehovah na Jesus ada no edzi akyerɛ wo no ka wo ma eyɛ?

15 Hɔn a woegyaa Jehovah som no, Jehovah eyi hɔn tokyen anaa? Oho. Mbom ɔhwehwɛ hɔn a wɔtse dɛm no hɔn ekyir kwan, na sɛ ohu hɔn a, ɔkyerɛkyerɛ hɔn na ɔboa hɔn ma wɔsan ba ne nkyɛn. (Kenkan Ezekiel 34:11, 12, 16.) Ehwɛ a, Jehovah roboa wo ma asan abɔsom no bio a? Nyew! Adzesua asɛm a irusua yi mpo kyerɛ dɛ epɛ dɛ eyɛ dza ɔsɔ n’enyi. Kaa dɛ, Jehovah hun adze papabi wɔ w’akoma mu, ntsi na ɔtwee wo baa nokwar no mu no. Na ɔda ho ara hu adze papabi wɔ w’akoma mu, ntsi na ɔpɛ dɛ ɔsan twe wo ba ne nkyɛn no.
