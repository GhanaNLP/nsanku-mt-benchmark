WERDAM NO MA YEHU DƐ JEHOVAH WƆ EHUMBƆBƆR
<<<P>>>
17 Werdam no ma yehu nokwasɛm fofor bi so. Ɔno nye dɛ Jehovah “no mbɔbɔrhu dɔɔ so.” Bible no ka dɛ, ‘ɔmaa yɛbaa nkwa mu, aber mpo a nna yeewu wɔ hɛn mfom mu no.’ (Kenkan Ephesusfo 2:4, 5.) “Hɔn a wɔwɔ akoma pa a obotum ama woeenya onnyiewiei nkwa” no, wɔtse dɛ ɔbarimba no a ɔhyɛ dan no a woebu egu no mu. Wɔgye to mu dɛ bɔn na sintɔ a wɔdze awo hɔn no ama wɔreberɛ paa na wonntum nngye hɔnho, ntsi wɔreserɛ dɛ obi mbɛgye hɔn. (Andw. 13:48) Jehovah so etsie hɔn sufrɛ no, na ɔama wɔatse Ahendzi no ho asɛmpa no bi, mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a wɔbɛba ebohu ɔno nye ne Ba Jesus yie. (John 17:3) Sɛ nna Satan n’adwen yɛ no dɛ ɔrema Adam na Eve ayɛ bɔn na ɔdze asɛɛ Nyankopɔn ne nhyehyɛɛ dze a, nna ɔnodze ne nsa esi famu.
<<<P>>>
17. Werdam no si dɛn ma yehu dɛ Jehovah wɔ ehumbɔbɔr? (Ephesusfo 2:4, 5)
<<<P>>>
18 Sɛ yɛrodwendwen nhyira ahorow a werdam no ama yeenya no ho no a, onnyi dɛ yɛma hɛn werɛ fir asɛm a no ho hia paa no so. Ɔnnsɛ dɛ yenya adwen dɛ mfaso a yebenya no nkotsee ntsi na Jehovah dze werdam no maa hɛn, mbom ɔsɛ dɛ yɛkaa dɛ Jehovah nam do rubua sombo a Satan dze bɔɔ no wɔ Eden ture no mu no so. (Gen. 3:1-5, 15) Jehovah nam werdam no do bɛtsew ne dzin ho na wɔama ada edzi dɛ nsɛm a Satan akeka afa no ho no nyina nnyɛ nokwar. Afei so, osiandɛ Nyankopɔn dɔ hɛn ntsi, ɔnam werdam no do bɛgye hɛn efi bɔn na owu nsamu. Bio so, ɔwɔ mu dɛ bɔn wɔ hɛnho dze, naaso Jehovah n’adom ara kwa ntsi, ɔama yeenya hokwan dɛ yɛbɛma nkorɔfo ehu dɛ Satan yɛ ɔtorfo. (Mbɛ. 27:11) Ebɛnadze na ibotum ayɛ dze akyerɛ dɛ w’enyi sɔ werdam no paa? Yebenya dɛm asɛmbisa yi ho mbuae wɔ adzesua a odzi hɔ no mu.
<<<P>>>
18. Sɛ yɛrodwendwen nhyira a werdam no ma yenya no ho a, ebɛn asɛm a no ho hia paa na onnyi dɛ yɛma hɛn werɛ fir?
