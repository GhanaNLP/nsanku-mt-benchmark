WERDAM NO MA YENYA BƆNFAKYƐ
<<<P>>>
4 Yehia werdam afɔrbɔ no ama Jehovah dze hɛn bɔn akyɛ hɛn. Yɛyɛ nyimpa a bɔn wɔ hɛnho yi ara dze, yɛfom wɔ hɛn kasaa nye hɛn ndzeyɛɛ mu. (Kenkan Ɔsɛnkafo 7:20.) Bɔn ahorow no bi yɛ akɛse kyɛn binom. Dɛ mfatoho no, Moses Mbra no kaa dɛ sɛ Israelnyi bi sɛɛ awar anaa oku obi a, wonku no. (Lev. 20:10; Nka. 35:30, 31) Naaso bɔn ahorow no bi wɔ hɔ a, ɔnnhyɛ da ara nnyɛ hu dɛm naaso ɔda ho ara yɛ bɔn. Dɛ mfatoho no, ɔdwontofo David kaa dɛ: “Mobɔhwɛ m’akwan yie, amma maammfa me tɛkyerɛma annyɛ bɔn.” (Ndw. 39:1) Mbrɛ ɔtse nye dɛ, ɔtɔ da a hɛn anomu kasa mpo tum ma yɛyɛ bɔn.—Jas. 3:2.

4-5. Ebɛnadze ntsi na hɛn nyina hia werdam afɔrbɔ no? (Ɔsɛnkafo 7:20)
<<<P>>>
5 Aka asɛm bi anaa ayɛ biribi ma enu woho pɛn? Nokwasɛm nye dɛ, biribi a ɔtse dɛm ato hɛn nyina bi pɛn. Ɔnnyɛ nwanwa dɛ Bible no ka dɛ: ‘Sɛ yɛka dɛ bɔn nnyi hɛnho a, yɛdaadaa hɛnho, na nokwar no nnyi hɛn mu.’—1 John 1:8.
<<<P>>>
6 Werdam no ntsi, nna Jehovah botum enya biribi egyina do dze hɛn bɔn akyɛ hɛn. (Eph. 1:7) Naaso, iyi nnkyerɛ dɛ sɛ yɛyɛ bɔn a Jehovah bu n’enyi gu do tse dɛ mbrɛ ɔmmfa noho bi. Jehovah mmpɛ dɛ yɛyɛ bɔn. (Isa. 59:2) Osiandɛ Jehovah bu atsɛn tsenenee ntsi, nna ɔsɛ dɛ ɔyɛ biribi ama oetum dze hɛn bɔn akyɛ hɛn korakora.
<<<P>>>
7 Nna Moses Mbra no hwehwɛ dɛ Israelfo no dze mbowa bɔ afɔr dze pata ma hɔn bɔn. (Lev. 4:27-31; 17:11) Dɛm afɔrbɔ ahorow no ma yehu dɛ nna adasamba behia biribi a ɔsom bo kyɛn mbowa hɔn nkwa. Ɔno nye Jesus no werdam afɔrbɔ no, na nna ɔdze nhyira pii bɛbrɛ adasamba. Ntsi nna Jesus n’afɔrbɔ no bɛma Jehovah enya biribi egyina do dze hɛn bɔn akyɛ hɛn. Krataa a Paul kyerɛwee kɛmaa Christianfo a nna wɔwɔ Corinth no ma yehu dɛ, nna Paul nyim mfaso ahorow a Jesus
