Yehia Nyame N’Asɛm

DZA YEBOSUA
Jehovah ama hɛn ndzɛmba bi a ɔbɛma yɛnye no ntamu ayɛ papa na hɛn enyi agye, na yɛbɔhwɛ dza ɔsɛ dɛ yɛyɛ ama yeetum enya dɛm ndzɛmba no do mfaso.
<<<P>>>
Kɔ Do Ara Hwehwɛ “Nyankopɔn N’akwankyerɛ”
<<<P>>>
2026 AFE ASƐM:
“Enyigye nye hɔn a wonyim dɛ wohia Nyankopɔn n’akwankyerɛ.”
—MATT. 5:3.
<<<P>>>
JEHOVAH nyim dɛ ndzɛmba etsitsir bi wɔ hɔ a, sɛ nyimpa ennya a, worunntum nntsena nkwa mu. Dɛm ntsi sɛ yebotum atsena nkwa mu a, ɔsɛ dɛ yedzidzi, yɛhyɛ atar, na yenya beebi a yɛdze hɛn tsir bɔto. Sɛ dɛm ndzɛmba yi mu kor mpo bɔ hɛn anaa no mu bi fi hɛn nsa ber tsiabaa bi mpo a, obotum ama asetsena mu ayɛ dzen ama hɛn paa anaa hɛn nkwa mpo botum efi hɛn nsa. Sɛ yɛdze dɛm asetsena mu ndzɛmba a nyimpa hia yi to nkyɛn a, Jehovah nyim dɛ yehia biribi fofor so; onyim dɛ yehia n’akwankyerɛ. (Kenkan Matthew 5:3.) Sɛ yɛpɛ dɛ hɛn enyi gye paa a, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛgye to mu dɛ yehia Nyankopɔn n’akwankyerɛ na yɛkɔ do ara hwehwɛ n’akwankyerɛ na yɛdze yɛ edwuma.
<<<P>>>
² Sɛ wɔka dɛ obi ‘nyim dɛ ohia Nyankopɔn n’akwankyerɛ’ a, ɔkyerɛ dɛn? Greek asɛmfua a wɔakyerɛ ase dɛm no, adwen a ɔtaa ekyir nye obi a oehia no paa ma ɔserɛserɛ adze. Owodze ma yɛmfa obi a ɔtse dɛm no dɛ mfatohɔ. Dza obedzi abɔ no, na atar a ɔhyɛ no no atsetsew ayɛ fi pɔtɔɔ. Ewia bɔ a, osi no do, na sɛ odu anafua so a onnya beebi nnda. Edziban a onnya nndzi ntsi ɔayɛ hatuu dɛ ahwernsaw. Na ma noho so ayɛ ntsi, obiara mpo mmpɛ n’enyim ahwɛ. Onyim paa dɛ sɛ oennya biribi enndzi a ɔkɔm boku no, naaso ɔboafo wɔ hen. Yebotum dze hɔn a wonyim dɛ wohia Nyankopɔn n’akwankyerɛ no atoto dɛm nyimpa yi ho. Dɛm nkorɔfo no nyim dɛ sɛ Nyame ammba hɔn abrabɔ mu a, ɔrennyɛ yie. Dɛm ntsi nhyehyɛɛ a Jehovah dze egu akwan mu ama hɔn a wɔdɔ no no nye ne ntamu aayɛ papa no, wonndzi ho agor koraa.
