BIBLE ESUAFO
<<<P>>>
4 Sɛ wɔnye wo rusua Bible no a, ma ɔntsena w’adwen mu dɛ Bible no a irusua no ara mpo kyerɛ dɛ Jehovah pɛ dɛ ɔfa wo nyɛnko ntsi na ɔrotwe wo aba ne nkyɛn no. (John 6:44; Andw. 13:48) Bible no ka dɛ ‘Jehovah sɔ akoma nyina hwɛ.’ Dɛm asɛm yi kyerɛ dɛ ohu w’akoma mu na ohu mbɔdzen a erobɔ dɛ ibosua no ho adze no, na sɛ ohu dɛ ereyer woho dɛ ebɛsesa w’abrabɔ na edze n’asɛm abɔ wo bra a, ɔma n’enyi gye bor do. (Mbɛ. 17:3; 27:11) Mbrɛ ɔtse nye dɛ, werdam no ntsi ibotum afa Jehovah nyɛnko ma ayɛ yie. (Rom. 5:10, 11) Mma mma wo werɛ mmfir dɛm nokwasɛm yi da.
<<<P>>>
5 Sɛ wɔnye wo rusua Bible no a, ebɛnadze na ibotum ayɛ dze akyerɛ dɛ w’enyi sɔ werdam no? Adze kor a ibotum ayɛ nye dɛ edze afotu a ɔsomafo Paul dze maa Philippifo no bɛyɛ edwuma. Otuu hɔn fo dɛ: “Dza yeedu ho dada no, noara so do na hom mma yendua nnantsew.” (Phil. 3:16) Bible nkyerɛase fofor bi so ka dɛ: “Beebi a yɛdze hɛn enyi akyerɛ no, hom mma yɛnkɔ do ara nhwɛ hɛn enyim tsenn.” Kae dɛ, nkwa kwan no ara na enam do no, ntsi onnyi dɛ ema kwan ma biribiara ma eman fi do da.—Matt. 7:14; Luke 9:62.
<<<P>>>
6 Na sɛ ndaansa yi ara na isuaa Bible mu nokwasɛm bi, naaso ɔyɛ dzen ma wo dɛ ebɛgye ato mu so ɛ? Sɛ dɛm dze a, nna yɛ dɛm asɛm no ho nhwehwɛmu, na serɛ Jehovah dɛ ɔmboa wo ma tse ase yie. (Ndw. 86:11) Sɛ iyi nyina ekyir no, ɔda ho ara yɛ dzen ma wo dɛ ebɛgye dɛm nkyerɛkyerɛ no ato mu a, mma mma ɔnnhaw wo pii mbom fa to nkyɛn kakra, na kɔ do ara sua Bible no. Bi a epɛ dɛ igyaa suban bɔn bi so, naaso eyɛ dzen ma wo ara. Sɛ dɛm a, kae dɛ Jehovah remma yɛnyɛ biribi a yerunntum nnyɛ da. Ntsi dza ɔaka wɔ n’Asɛm no mu no, ibotum dze abɔ wo bra. (Kenkan Deuteronomy 30:11-14.) Ɔahyɛ bɔ mpo dɛ ɔbɔboa wo. (Isa. 41:10, 13; 1 Cor. 10:13) Ntsi mma mmpa abaw. Onnyi dɛ edze w’adwen si dza ɔhaw wo no do pii, mbom dwendwen ndzɛmba pa a Jehovah ayɛ ama wo no ho na kyerɛ ho enyisɔ.
