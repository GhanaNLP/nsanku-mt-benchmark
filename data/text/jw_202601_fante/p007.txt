16 Aber biara a nna David betsie Jehovah no, nna ɔboa no ma osisi gyinae pa na iyi bɔɔ no ho ban fii ɔhaw ahorow a ɔto hɔn a wɔgye enyim ara bu ne mbra do no ho. (Fa toto Mbɛbusɛm 5:1, 2 ho.) Naaso aber a David ammfa Jehovah ne mbra ammbɔ ne bra no, dza ofii mu bae no, Jehovah ammbɔ no ho ban emmfi ho. (2 Sam. 12:9, 10) Na aber a nkorɔfo nye David enndzi no yie na wɔmaa ohun amandze no so ɛ? David fii n’akoma mu kaa dza nna ɔhaw no no kyerɛɛ Jehovah, na Jehovah so bɔɔ no ho ban. Ebɛnadze na ɔyɛe dze bɔɔ no ho ban? Ɔkyekyee David no werɛ, na ɔmaa onyaa awerɛhyɛmu dɛ ɔdɔ no paa na ɔbɔhwɛ no.—Ndw. 23:1-6.

17 Hɛn so, sɛ yɛbɔ Jehovah mpaa na yɛhwehwɛ n’akwankyerɛ ansaana yeesisi gyinae a, nna ɔkyerɛ dɛ yerusuasua David. Naaso, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛkaa dɛ sɛ yeenntsie Jehovah na yesisi gyinae bɔn a, bi a yebohu amandze, na ɔnodze Jehovah remma ɔmmpar hɛn. (Gal. 6:7, 8) Afei so, sɛ afofor ma yehu amandze a, yebotum efi hɛn akoma mu aka ho asɛm akyerɛ Jehovah, na yeenya awerɛhyɛmu dɛ ɔbɔbɔ hɛn akoma nye hɛn adwen ho ban.—Phil. 4:6, 7.

KƆ DO ARA HWEHWƐ JEHOVAH N’AKWANKYERƐ
<<<P>>>
18 Afe 2026 afe asɛm no ka dɛ: “Enyigye nye hɔn a wonyim dɛ wohia Nyankopɔn n’akwankyerɛ.” Dɛm asɛm yi yɛ nokwar paa ndɛ. Ebɛnadze ntsi na yɛreka dɛm? Osiandɛ nyimpa piinara a woetwa hɛnho ehyia ndɛ no nngye nnto mu dɛ wohia Nyankopɔn n’akwankyerɛ. Na hɔn a wɔse wɔsom Nyankopɔn no mpo, wɔnnsom no wɔ kwan a ɔpɛ do. Afofor so ma nyimpa nyansaa kyerɛ hɔn kwan, naaso iyinom nyina ekyir no, hɔn enyi nngye. Onnyi dɛ yesuasua hɔn. Sɛ dɛm dze a, nna ɔno ebɛnadze na ɔsɛ dɛ yɛyɛ? Ɔsɛ dɛ yɛkɔ do ara sua Jehovah n’Asɛm no yie, yɛhyɛ suban fofor no dɛ atar, na yetsie Jehovah na yɛma ɔbɔ hɛn ho ban.
