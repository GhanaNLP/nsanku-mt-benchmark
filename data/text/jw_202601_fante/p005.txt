9 Dɛ mbrɛ Peter yɛe no, hɛn so ɔsɛ dɛ yɛkɔ do ara sua Nyankopɔn n’Asɛm no yie. Bi a nokwasɛm a ɔnnyɛ dzen dɛ yɛbɛtse no na yɛbɛtaa esua ho adze. Naaso, ɔsɛ dɛ hɛn kɔn dɔ Bible mu nkyerɛkyerɛ a no mu dɔ paa anaa n’ase tse yɛ dzen no so na yesua ho adze. Ɔsɛ dɛ yɛgye ber na yɛbɔ mbɔdzen dɛ yebosua Bible mu nokwasɛm a no mu dɔ no yie na yɛatse ase. Na sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm a, yebenya do mfaso paa. Ebɛn mfaso na yebenya? Annyɛ yie koraa no, ɔbɔboa hɛn wɔ akwan ebien bi do: Kor nye dɛ, yebosua ndzɛmba pii afa Jehovah ho na ɔbɛma yɛadɔ no na yeebu no paa; na otsia ebien, dza yebosua no botum ekenyan hɛn paa ma yɛaka hɛn sor Egya no no suban ahorow a ɔyɛ fɛw no ho asɛm akyerɛ nkorɔfo. (Rom. 11:33; Nyi. 4:11) Yebotum esua biribi fofor efi Peter n’asɛm no mu. Ɔno nye dɛ: Sɛ yenya Nyankopɔn n’Asɛm no mu nkyerɛkyerɛmu fofor a, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛgye to mu ntsɛmara na yɛdze yɛ edwuma. Sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm a, Jehovah bɔkɔ do ara akyerɛ hɛn kwan na ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yɛdze nokwardzi asom no ekesi ase.

SUASUA PAUL—KƆ DO ARA HYƐ SUBAN FOFOR NO TSE DƐ ATAR
<<<P>>>
10 Sɛ yɛpɛ dɛ hɛn enyi gye na yɛma Jehovah kyerɛ hɛn kwan a, ɔsɛ dɛ yenya suban ahorow a ɔsɔ n’enyi. Jehovah kyerɛ hɛn mbrɛ yebesi enya suban ahorow a ɔtse dɛm. Ɔsomafo Paul kaa dɛ, ɔsɛ dɛ ‘yeyi suban dadaw no gu’ na yɛhyɛ ‘suban fofor no’ tse dɛ atar. (Kenkan Colossaefo 3:8-10.) Hom mma yɛnka, ɔnnda famu dɛ yɛbɔkɔ do ara ahyɛ suban fofor no. Hom mma yɛmfa Paul n’asɛm no dɛ mfatohɔ. Ofitsi no mbofrase no, ɔbɔɔ mbɔdzen dɛ ɔbɛyɛ dza ɔsɔ Nyankopɔn n’enyi. (Gal. 1:14; Phil. 3:4, 5) Naaso, nna onnyi Nyankopɔn ho nyimdzee a odzi mu, ntsi nna onnyim dɛ kwan a ɔrefa do asom no no nnyɛ papa. Afei so, nna onnyim nokwasɛm a Christ kyerɛkyerɛe no, na nna ahantan so ruku no. Ntsi yɛbɛka a, nna onnyi suban koraa. Nankasa mpo kaa dɛ nna ɔyɛ enyiammbɔho.—1 Tim. 1:13.
<<<P>>>
11 Ansaana Paul rebɛyɛ Christiannyi no, nna n’abofuw haw no paa. Asomafo Ndwuma nwoma no ka dɛ Paul no bo fuuw Jesus n’esuafo no paa, ntsi nna ‘ohunahuna hɔn, na nna oesi no bo dɛ obokuku hɔn.’ (Andw. 9:1) Paul bɛyɛɛ Christiannyi no, ɔda edzi dɛ ɔyeer noho dɛ obeyi dɛm suban bɔn yi egu. (Eph. 4:22, 31) Nna Paul robɔ mbɔdzen paa dɛ obegyaa dɛm suban bɔn yi, naaso mber bi akasakasa baa ɔnye Barnabas hɔn ntamu na ɔmaa ‘no bo fuuw dzendzenndzen.’ (Andw. 15:37-39) Dɛm aber no, Paul no suban bɔn yi sanee yii ne tsir, naaso ɔammpa abaw. Ɔkɔr do ‘hwee ne nyimpadua,’ kyerɛ dɛ, ɔyeer noho dɛ obegyaa dɛm suban bɔn no mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a ɔbɔkɔ do ara ayɛ dza ɔsɔ Jehovah n’enyi.—1 Cor. 9:27.
<<<P>>>
12 Paul ammfa noho annto nankasa n’ahoɔdzen do, na iyi boaa no ma otumii yii suban dadaw no gui na ɔhyɛɛ suban fofor no dɛ atar. (Phil. 4:13) Dɛ mbrɛ Peter yɛe no, Paul so dze noho too “ahoɔdzen a Nyankopɔn dze ma” no do. (1 Pet. 4:11) Iyi nyina ekyir no, nna ɔtɔ da a Paul fom na nna ɔma n’abaw mu bu. Naaso sɛ n’abaw mu bu a, nna ɔdze n’adwen si ndzɛmba pa a no sor Egya no ayɛ ama no no do, na iyi hyɛɛ no dzen ma osii no bo dɛ ɔbɔkɔ do ayɛ dza ɔsɔ Jehovah n’enyi.—Rom. 7:21-25.
