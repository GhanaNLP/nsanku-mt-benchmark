Phoenicianyi ɔbaa no brɛɛ noho adze, ɔkɔr do serɛɛ Jesus dɛ ɔmboa no, na onyaa gyedzi dɛ obotum aboa no
(Hwɛ nkyekyɛmu 5)
<<<P>>>
3 Adzesua yi mu no, yebedzi kan ahwɛ dza yebotum esua efi Phoenicianyi ɔbaa bi a ɔserɛɛ Jesus dɛ ɔmboa no no hɔ. Ɔbaa yi n’asɛm no ma yehu suban ebiasa bi a ɔsɛ dɛ hɔn a wonyim dɛ wohia Nyankopɔn n’akwankyerɛ no nya. Yewie a, yɛbɔhwɛ dza yebotum esua efi mbarimba baasa bi a wɔmaa Nyankopɔn kyerɛɛ hɔn kwan no hɔ. Hɔn nye ɔsomafo Peter, ɔsomafo Paul na Ɔhen David.
<<<P>>>
DZA YEBOTUM ESUA EFI PHOENICIANYI ƆBAA NO HƆ
<<<P>>>
4 Mber bi, Phoenicianyi ɔbaa bi kɔr Jesus ne nkyɛn. Nna ‘esunsum fi retseetsee ne babaa paa.’ (Matt. 15:21-28) Ntsi ɔkɔkotoow Jesus n’enyim na ɔserɛɛ no dɛ ɔmboa no. Dɛm Phoenicianyi ɔbaa yi daa suban fɛfɛɛfɛw ahorow bi edzi na yebotum esuasua no. Hom mma yensusu no mu bi ho.
<<<P>>>
5 Nna Phoenicianyi ɔbaa no brɛ noho adze paa. Ebɛnadze ntsi na yebotum aka dɛm? Tsetse no, nna Amanaman mufo taa nyɛn mbɔdɔm wɔ hɔn efie, na Jesus dze ɔbaa no totoo bɔdɔmba ho. Naaso aber a ɔyɛɛ dɛm no, ɔbaa no no bo emmfuw. Sɛ ɔwo na Jesus kaa dɛm asɛm yi kyerɛɛ wo a, nkyɛ ebɛyɛ dɛn? Bi a nkyɛ ebɛka dɛ Jesus ahyehyɛ wo ahorba ma ɔno ntsi eremma ɔmmboa wo, na akɛpɛ wo beebi atsena. Naaso Phoenicianyi ɔbaa yi annyɛ dɛm. Mbom ɔbrɛɛ noho adze, na ɔsanee so yɛɛ biribi fofor so. Ɔammpa abaw, ɔkɔr do serɛɛ Jesus dɛ ɔmboa no. Ebɛnadze na ɔmaa ɔyɛɛ dɛm? Nna ɔwɔ Jesus no mu gyedzi. Nokwasɛm nye dɛ, Jesus hun dɛ maame no ne gyedzi so paa na iyi kaa n’akoma ma ɔboaa no. Jesus edzi kan aka kyerɛɛ maame no dɛ wɔsomaa no dɛ ɔmbɔboa “Israel fie nguan a wɔayew no” nkotsee, naaso ekyir yi ɔboaa maame yi a nna ɔyɛ amanaman munyi no. Otutuu esunsum fi a nna ɔretseetsee maame no ne babaa no fii no mu.
