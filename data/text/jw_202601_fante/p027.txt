3 Dɛ mbrɛ Jesus yɛe no, hɛn so yɛbɔ mbɔdzen dɛ yebedzi nokwar aber nyina, na sɛ yɛka nokwar no na ɔbɛma hɛnho ayɛ nkorɔfo ehi mpo a, yenngyaa ka. Afei so, sɛ binom mmpɛ dɛ yɛka Bible mu nokwasɛm kyerɛ hɔn a, ɔno mma yɛnnka hɛn ano nnto mu. Naaso ehwɛ a iyi kyerɛ dɛ ɔnnsɛ dɛ yɛdwen mbrɛ yebesi aka Bible mu nokwasɛm akyerɛ nkorɔfo nye ber a yɛdze bɛyɛ dɛm no ho anaa? Oho, ɔnntse dɛm koraa! Adzesua yi mu no, yebenya dɛm nsɛmbisa yi ho mbuae: Henfa na yebotum ehu nokwar no? Yewie a, yebosusu dɛm nsɛmbisa yi so ho: Ebɛnadze ntsi na ɔsɛ dɛ yɛka nokwar no? Yebesi dɛn aka nokwar no? Ebɛn aber na ɔsɛ dɛ yɛka nokwar no? Dɛm nsɛmbisa yi ho mbuae bɔboa hɛn ma yeehu mbrɛ yebesi aka nokwar no ho asɛm ma wɔayɛ dɛw, nye aber a obehia dɛ yɛyɛ dɛm.

HENFA NA YEBOTUM EHU NOKWAR NO?
<<<P>>>
4 Nokwar a ɔwɔ hɔ biara fi Jehovah hɔ. Asɛm biara a ɔka no yɛ nokwar. Dza ɔaka afa papa na bɔn ho no nyina yɛ nokwar. (Ndw. 19:9; 119:142, 151) Dza ɔaka afa daakye ho biara so bɛba mu. (Isa. 55:10, 11) Odzi no bɔhyɛ do aber nyina. (Nka. 23:19) Nokwasɛm nye dɛ, Jehovah runntum nndzi ator da! (Heb. 6:18) Sɛ Bible no ka dɛ Jehovah yɛ “nokwar Nyame” a, ɔmmboa.—Ndw. 31:5.
<<<P>>>
5 Binom se ɔyɛ dzen dɛ wobohu Jehovah a ɔyɛ “nokwar Nyame” no yie, naaso wɔboa. Ndzɛmba a etwa hɛnho ehyia no ma yehu dɛ ɔwɔ hɔ na yebotum ehu no. (Rom. 1:20) Aber a nna ɔsomafo Paul nye Greekfo enyimdzefo bi rekasa wɔ Athens no, ɔkaa dɛ Nyankopɔn pɛ dɛ ‘yehu no’ na “ɔnye hɛn mu biara nntamu kwan nnwar.” (Kenkan Asomafo Ndwuma 17:27.) Nokwasɛm nye dɛ, Jehovah nankasa twe ahobrɛadzefo a wɔrohwehwɛ nokwar no ba ne nkyɛn.—John 6:44.
<<<P>>>
6 Kwan kor a yebotum afa do ehu Jehovah nye dɛ yebosua Bible no. Mbarimba a wɔkyerɛɛw Bible no, Nyankopɔn na ɔdze no sunsum krɔnkrɔn no boaa hɔn ma wotumii kyerɛwee. (2 Pet. 1:20, 21) Dɛm ntsi asɛm biara a ɔwɔ Bible no mu no yɛ nokwar na yebotum dze hɛnho ato do. Dɛ mfatoho no, Bible no ma yehu dɛ Nyankopɔn na ɔbɔɔ hɛn amansan yi, na ɔnoara so na ɔbɔɔ nyimpa na mbowa wɔ asaase yi do. (Gen. 1:1, 26) Afei so, ɔma yehu siantsir a yɛyɛ bɔn nye siantsir a amandzehu na owu ebu do wɔ wiadze yi mu dɛm. (Rom. 5:12; 6:23) Bio so, ɔma yehu dɛ Jehovah nam ne Ba no do besiesie ɔhaw a Satan a ɔyɛ “ator egya” no akɛfa aba no nyina. (John 8:44; Rom. 16:20) Iyinom da nkyɛn a, Bible no ahyɛ bɔ dɛ, Jesus bɛsɛɛ abɔnfo, obenyan ewufo, obesiesie asaase yi, na ɔaboa hɛn ma yɛabɛyɛ nyimpa a bɔn nnyi hɛnho. (John 11:25, 26; 1 John 3:8) Yebotum agye dza Bible no ka no nyina edzi. Jehovah ama yeenya hokwan kɛse paa. Ɔakyerɛkyerɛ hɛn nokwar no, na ɔdze ahyɛ hɛn nsa so dɛ yɛmboa afofor ma wombohu no ho nokwasɛm.—Matt. 28:19, 20.
