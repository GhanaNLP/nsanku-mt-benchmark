(Fa toto James 1:5-7 ho.) Afei, hom mma yɛnhwɛ dza yebotum esua efi ɔsomafo Peter, ɔsomafo Paul na Ɔhen David hɔ. Ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yeehu nhyehyɛɛ a Jehovah dze egu akwan mu a ɔdze kyerɛ hɛn kwan, mbrɛ osi boa hɛn ma hɛn enyi gye, nye dza yɛyɛ a ɔbɛma yeenya dɛm nhyehyɛɛ no do mfaso.

SUASUA PETER—KƆ DO ARA SUA NYANKOPƆN N’ASƐM NO YIE
<<<P>>>
7 Hom mma yensusu ɔsomafo Peter ho kakra. Jewfo a wodzii kan hun dɛ Jesus nye Messiah no, nna ɔka ho. Ohun dɛ Jesus nye obi a Jehovah nam no do dze “nsɛm a ɔma onnyiewiei nkwa” anaa sunsum afamu edziban ma n’asomfo. (John 6:66-68) Ansaana Jesus robɔkɔ sor no, ɔdze edwuma bi hyɛɛ Peter ne nsa. Ɔkaa kyerɛɛ no dɛ ‘ɔmma no nguan nkakraba edziban.’ (John 21:17) Peter gyee dɛm edwuma no too mu na ɔkyerɛkyerɛɛ Jesus n’ekyirdzifo no. Jehovah mpo maa ɔkyerɛɛw nkrataa ebien a ndɛ ɔka Bible no ho. Naaso dza Peter yɛe nyina ekyir no, nna ɔsɛ dɛ nankasa so dzidzi yie, kyerɛ dɛ, osua Nyankopɔn n’Asɛm no yie. Dɛ mfatohɔ no, Peter gyee ber suaa nkrataa a ɔsomafo Paul kyerɛwee no. Ɔgyee too mu dɛ nsɛm a Paul kyerɛwee no bi wɔ hɔ a, nna “ne ntseasee yɛ dzen.” (2 Pet. 3:15, 16) Naaso Peter ammpa abaw, mbom ɔkɔr do suae. Nna ɔgye dzi dɛ ‘edziban a ɔyɛ dzen,’ kyerɛ dɛ, nsɛm a n’ase tse yɛ dzen a Jehovah maa Paul kyerɛwee wɔ ne nkrataa no mu no, Jehovah bɔboa no ma ɔatse ase na ɔdze ayɛ edwuma. —Kenkan Hebrewfo 5:14–6:1.
<<<P>>>
8 Nna Peter gye Jehovah dzi, ntsi otsiee akwankyerɛ bi a Jehovah dze maa no no. Mber bi, nna Peter wɔ Joppa na onyaa enyido-adzehu bi. Enyido-adzehu no mu no, Nyankopɔn no bɔfoe kaa kyerɛɛ no dɛ onku mbowa a Moses Mbra no se mma wonndzi no bi na ondzi. Ofitsi no mbofrase nyina, nna ɔnnwee mbowa a wɔtse dɛm bi da, ntsi nna ne tsirmu nntsen no wɔ dza ɔbɔfo no reka akyerɛ no dɛ ɔnyɛ no ho. Ntsi ɔbɔfo no n’ano sii ara pɛr na Peter kaa dɛ: “Oho Ewuradze, murunntum nnyɛ dɛm, osiandɛ minndzii biribiara a no ho egu fi anaa no ho nntsew da.” Ɔno ekyir no, ɔbɔfo no kaa kyerɛɛ no dɛ: “Ndzɛmba a Nyankopɔn atsew ho no, mma nnka dɛ no ho egu fi.” (Andw. 10:9-15) Dza nna ɔbɔfo no pɛ dɛ ɔkyerɛ no, Peter tsee ase na ɔsesaa n’adwen. Yesi dɛn hu dɛm? Onyaa enyido-adzehu no ekyir no, wɔannkyɛr biara na mbarimba baasa bi baa ne nkyɛn. Wɔkaa kyerɛɛ no dɛ hɔn wura Cornelius a ɔyɛ Amanaman munyi no na ɔasoma hɔn dɛ wɔmbra mbɛfrɛ no nkɔ, na ɔpɛ dɛ ɔnye no kasa. Nkan no a nkyɛ Peter runntu ne nan nnsi Amanaman munyi ne fie da. Osiandɛ nna Jewfo no bu Amanaman mufo no dɛ hɔnho nntsew. (Andw. 10:28, 29) Naaso aber a Jehovah maa Peter hun dɛ ofi dɛm aber no rokɔ no ɔpɛ dɛ obiara tse asɛmpa no bi no, ɔgyee too mu ntsɛmara. (Mbɛ. 4:18) Ntsi ɔkaa asɛmpa no kyerɛɛ Cornelius na ne fiefo, na wɔgyee Jesus dzii. Ɔno ekyir no, wonyaa sunsum krɔnkrɔn na wonumaa hɔn, na iyi maa Peter n’enyi gyee paa.—Andw. 10:44-48.
