(Hwɛ nkyekyɛmu 9-10)

Obisaa noho dɛ, ‘Aberantsɛ paa na menya dɛm yarba yi a?’ Dɛm aber no, nna ɔwɔ ba banyimba a oedzi mfe ebiasa pɛ. Ebɛnadze na ɔboaa John ma ɔdze n’adwen sii ndzɛmba a nna ɔhyɛ nkuran do? Nankasa kaa dɛ: “Ɔwɔ mu dɛ nna meberɛ dze, naaso menye m’ebusua ammfa hɛn som enndzi agor koraa. Yɛanntoto asafo nhyiamu mu da, na dapɛn biara so, nna yɛkɔ asɛnka. Afei so, nna yɛyɛ hɛn ebusua som aber biara. Nna ɔtɔ da a ɔyɛ dzen dze, naaso nna yemia hɛn enyi yɛ dɛm ndzɛmba yi.” John sanee so kaa dɛ: “Sɛ wɔka kyerɛ wo dɛ enya yarba bi a, annhwɛ a ahyɛse no wo yamu bɛhyehye wo, na ebɛtse nka dɛ wo wiadze nyina aba n’ewiei. Naaso ekyir yi, ibohu dɛ Jehovah rehyɛ wo dzen na ɔroboa wo. Dɛ mbrɛ Jehovah ahyɛ me dzen no, ɔbɛhyɛ woara so dzen.”

14 Eliza a yeedzi kan aka no ho asɛm no so kaa dɛ: “Dza metse aber a makɔ asafo nhyiamu ase, nye dza mankasa gye ber sua ho adze no ma muhu dɛ Jehovah tsie me na ɔdɔ me paa. Iyi ma m’enyi gye.” Onua bi so a wɔfrɛ no Nolan yɛ ɔmansin do hwɛfo wɔ Africa. Ɔno so kaa dza ɔboa ɔnye ne yer Diane ho asɛm. Ɔkaa dɛ: “Sɛ hɛn abaw mu bu mpo a, ndzɛmba a yɛyɛ dze som Jehovah no dze, yeenngyaa yɛ. Sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm a, yehu dɛ Jehovah so roboa hɛn ma hɛn enyi agye. Yɛma ɔtsena hɛn adwen mu aber nyina dɛ Jehovah bɔboa hɛn na obehyira hɛn. Yennyim kwan a ɔbɛfa do ayɛ dɛm dze, naaso yenyim dɛ ɔbɛyɛ.”
