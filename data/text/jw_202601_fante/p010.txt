9 Nna ɔtɔ da a Paul n'abaw mu bu, naaso ndzɛmba a ɔmaa n'enyi gyee dze, nna ɔdɔɔ so. Dɛ mfatohu no, sɛ nna n'anyɛnkofo asɛmpatsɛrɛwfo bɔbɔ no amandzɛɛ dɛ ndzɛmba rokɔ yie wɔ nsafo ahorow no mu a, nna ɔma n'enyi gye. (2 Cor. 7:6, 7) Afei so, anyɛnkofa a nna ɔda ɔnye no nuanom Christianfo ntamu no maa n'enyi gyee. (2 Tim. 1:4) Adze fofor so a ɔmaa Paul n'enyi gyee nye dɛ, nna onyim dɛ ɔnye Jehovah ntamu yɛ papa na iyi boaa no ma ɔdze “tsibowa krɔnn” soom no. (2 Tim. 1:3) Aber mpo a nna ɔda efiadze wɔ Rome mu, Paul hyɛɛ no nuanom Christianfo nkuran dɛ ‘wondzi dɛw wɔ Ewuradze mu.’ (Phil. 4:4) Iyi ma yehu dɛ Paul amma dadwen anaa awerɛhow annhyɛ no do. Ɔda edzi dɛ, sɛ nna Paul wɔ hɔ ara na ɔhyɛ ase dɛ ɔrodwendwen ndzɛmba a ɔma no werɛ how ho a, nna oyi n'adwen fi do ntsɛmara na ɔdze n'adwen si ndzɛmba a obotum ama n'enyi agye do.

10 Adze fofor so a ɔmaa Paul n'enyi gyee nye dɛ, ɔgyee dzii dɛ werdam no yɛ akyɛdze a Jehovah dze ama narankasa. (Gal. 2:20; kenkan Ephesusfo 1:7.) Dɛm ntsi nna ɔwɔ awerɛhyɛmu paa dɛ osian Jesus Christ no werdam afɔrbɔ no ntsi, Jehovah botum dze no bɔn akyɛ no korakora. (Rom. 7:24, 25) Osian iyi ntsi, ɔmmfa ho ndzɛmba bɔn a nna Paul ayɛ etwa mu nye sintɔ a nna ɔwɔ noho nyina ekyir no, otumii kɔr do dze ‘ɔsom a ɔyɛ krɔnkrɔn’ maa Nyankopɔn, na iyi maa n'enyi gyee paa.—Heb. 9:12-14.
