NNA EBƐNADZE NA ƆMA PAUL NO WERƐ HOW NO?
<<<P>>>
4 Bɔn a nna ɔayɛ etwa mu. Ansaana Paul rebɛyɛ Christiannyi no, ɔyɛɛ ndzɛmba bɔn ahorow bi naaso ekyir yi, onuu noho. Dɛm aber no, nna wɔfrɛ no Saul. Dza ɔyɛɛ no bi nye dɛ, ɔpeen do dɛ wonsiw Nyankopɔn no somfo Stephen abo, na woruku no no nyina nna n’enyi tua. (Andw. 7:58; 8:1) Afei so, ayakayakadze a wɔdze yɛɛ Christianfo no nyina, nna Saul dzi mu akotsen.—Andw. 8:3; 26:9-11.
<<<P>>>
5 Ɔsomafo Paul bɛyɛɛ Christiannyi no, nna ɔtɔ da a ndzɛmba bɔn a ɔayɛ etwa mu no ho asɛm haw no. Ayakayakadze a ɔdze yɛɛ Christianfo no, ɔbɛyɛ dɛ aber a ɔbɛyɛɛ Christiannyi no, sɛ nna obesi ne tsirmu a nna ɔyɛ no yaw paa. Dɛ mfatoho no, wɔ bɛyɛ afe 55 mu no, ɔkyerɛɛw krataa a odzi kan kɛmaa Corinthfo no, na ɔkaa no wɔ mu dɛ: “Mennsɛ mmfata mpo dɛ wɔfrɛ me ɔsomafo, osiandɛ metaan Nyankopɔn n’asafo no enyi.” (1 Cor. 15:9) Bɛyɛ mfe enum ekyir so, ɔkyerɛɛw krataa kɛmaa Ephesusfo no, na ɔkaa no ho asɛm dɛ ‘osuar kyɛn obi a osuar koraa wɔ akrɔnkrɔmfo no nyina mu.’ (Eph. 3:8) Krataa a Paul kyerɛwee kɛmaa Timothy no so, ɔkaa no wɔ mu dɛ nkan no nna ɔkeka ebususɛm, ɔtan nkorɔfo enyi na nna ɔyɛ enyiammbɔho. (1 Tim. 1:13) Ɔwodze fa no dɛ Paul akɛsera asafo bi na oehyia hɔn a ɔtaan hɔn enyi no bi anaa hɔn ebusuafo. Ehwɛ a, ɔbɛtse nka dɛn?
<<<P>>>
6 Honam mu nsoɛ. Paul kaa biribi fofor bi so a nna ɔretseetsee no ho asɛm. Ɔkaa dɛ ɔtse dɛ “honam mu nsoɛ bi.” (2 Cor. 12:7) Ɔsomafo Paul annka adze pɔtsee a nna ɔretseetsee no ho asɛm, naaso mbrɛ osii kaa ho asɛm no ma yehu dɛ nna ɔyɛ biribi a ɔma ɔtse yaw wɔ no wer mu. Obotum aba dɛ nna ɔyɛ yarba bi anaa ɔhaw bi na woegya no adwendwen.*
<<<P>>>
7 Ne sintɔ. Nna sintɔ a ɔwɔ Paul ho no so haw no paa. (Kenkan Romefo 7:18, 19.) Nna ɔpɛ paa dɛ ɔyɛ dza ɔtsen, naaso ne sintɔ ntsi nna ɔyɛ dzen ma no dɛ ɔbɛyɛ dɛm. Nankasa kaa n’anomu asɛm dɛ adze papa a ɔpɛ dɛ ɔyɛ no da biara no, onntum nnyɛ, na bɔn a ɔmmpɛ dɛ ɔyɛ no mbom na ɔyɛ. Naaso iyi nyina ekyir no, ɔbɔɔ mbɔdzen dɛ ɔbɛyɛ dza ɔtsen. (1 Cor. 9:27) Ntsi ɔbɛyɛ dɛ sɛ nna suban bɔn bi a ɔrobɔ noho mbɔdzen dɛ obegyaa no yi ne tsir a, nna ɔma no werɛ tum how paa!
<<<P>>>
DZA ƆMAA PAUL NO WERƐ HOWEE NO, EBƐNADZE NA ƆYƐE WƆ HO?
<<<P>>>
8 Nkrataa a Paul kyerɛwee no ma yehu dɛ, mbrɛ nna Nyankopɔn no sunsum krɔnkrɔn no botum aboa ɔnye no nuanom Christianfo ma woedzi hɔn sintɔ na hɔn mberɛwyɛ ahorow bi do no, nna ɔtaa dwendwen ho. (Rom. 8:13; Gal. 5:16, 17) Nna Paul taa ka suban bɔn nye ndzeyɛɛ bi a ɔsɛ dɛ Christianfo yi no akwa ho asɛm. (Gal. 5:19-21, 26) Ɔda edzi dɛ nna Paul taa dwendwen mbrɛ obesi asesa no suban no ho, ntsi nna ɔkenkan Kyerɛwnsɛm no mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a obotum ayɛ dɛm. Yebotum aka dɛ nankasa so dze afotu a ɔdze maa nkorɔfo no yɛɛ edwuma.

6. Nna ebɛnadze bio na ɔretseetsee Paul? (Ibotum ahwɛ ase hɔ asɛm no so.)
<<<P>>>
* Nsɛm bi a Paul kae wɔ ne nkrataa no mu no ma yehu dɛ, ɔbɛyɛ dɛ nna onnhu adze papa. Ntsi obotum aba dɛ nna ɔnnda famu mma no dɛ ɔbɛkyerɛw nkrataa akɛma nsafo ahorow no, na ɔayɛ no som edwuma no so. (Gal. 4:15; 6:11) Anaaso dɛ obotum aba dɛ akɔhwisɛm a akyerɛkyerefo atorfo binom kekae faa no ho no na ogyaa no adwendwen. (2 Cor. 10:10; 11:5, 13) Yenyim adze pɔtsee a nna ɔretseetsee Paul no, naaso yenyim dɛ nna dɛm adzekor no ma no werɛ how.
