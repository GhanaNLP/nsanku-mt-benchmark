Sɛ dadwen hyɛ wo do a, sua nsɛm ahorow bi a obotum aboa wo
(Hwɛ nkyekyɛmu 17)
<<<P>>>
adwendwen taa hyɛ hɛn do anaa hɛn abaw mu taa bu a, bi a obehia dɛ yɛyɛ ho nhwehwɛmu wɔ Bible no anaa asafo nwoma mu ama yeehu mbrɛ yebesi ayɛ hɛn adze. Bi mpo a obehia dɛ yɛka ho asɛm kyerɛ Christiannyi bi a noho akokwaa. Hom mma yɛnhwɛ ndzɛmba afofor bi so a yɛyɛ a obotum aboa hɛn.
<<<P>>>
16 Serɛ Jehovah dɛ ɔmboa wo ma hu adze pɔtsee a ɔma dadwen hyɛ wo do no. Nna Ɔhen David nyim dɛ Jehovah nyim no yie paa. Ntsi ɔserɛɛ no dɛ ɔmboa no ma onhu adze pɔtsee a wɔama ‘adwendwen’ ahyɛ no do no. (Kenkan Ndwom 139:1-4, 23, 24.) Woara so, ibotum aserɛ Jehovah dɛ ɔmboa wo ma hu adze pɔtsee a ɔma dadwen hyɛ wo do no, nye dza ibotum ayɛ wɔ ho. Ibotum ebisa woho nsɛm bi tse dɛ: ‘Ebɛnadze paa na wɔato me adwendwen yi? Ebɛnadze na ɔkɛfa dɛm adwendwen no ba? Dza ɔkɛfa adwendwen no ba no besi me tsirmu a, ɔyɛ a miyi fi mu ntsɛmara anaa mokɔ do ara dwendwen ho?’
<<<P>>>
17 Nsɛm a obotum aboa wo ma edzi wo haw no ho dwuma no, sua ho adze. Ɔwɔ mu ara a, ɔyɛ a fa Jehovah no suban ahorow no bi na sua ho adze. Eyɛ dɛm a, ɔbɔboa wo paa. Ɔsomafo Paul dɛm yi, nna ɔtaa dwendwen werdam no nye Jehovah no bɔnfakyɛ nye no nokwar dɔ ho adze akɔ ekyir. Ibotum dze Buukuu A Jehovah Adasefo Dze Yɛ Nhwehwɛmu, Watch Tower Publications Index anaa asafo nwoma fofor biara a ɔwɔ wo kasaa mu no ayɛ dɛm. Sɛ inya nsɛm bi a obotum aboa wo a, ɔyɛ a kyerɛw to hɔ. Ibotum dze afam beebi a ibohu, na sɛ ewɔ hɔ ara na w’abaw mu reyɛ ebu anaa wo werɛ reyɛ ahow a, nna afa dɛm nsɛm no bi esua. Sɛ irusua asɛm no a, ɔyɛ a bɔ mbɔdzen fa dza irusua no toto

16. Ebɛnadze na obotum aboa wo ma ehu adze pɔtsee a ɔma dadwen hyɛ wo do no? (Ndwom 139:1-4, 23, 24) (Ibotum ahwɛ mfonyin no so.)
