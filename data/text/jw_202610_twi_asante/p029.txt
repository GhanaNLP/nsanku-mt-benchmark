18 Nhyehyɛe a woayɛ sɛ wode bɛkenkan daa asɛm no da biara no, yɛ nea wubetumi biara sɛ wubedi so; mma biribiara nntwetwe woadwen. Isabelle yɛ onuawa abaabaa bi a wadi mfeɛ 19, na nea ɔka nie: “Ka kyerɛ wo ho sɛ, sɛ wonyɛɛ daa asɛm no nwiei a, woremfa wo fon mmiamia so, anaa worenyɛ biribiara wɔ so. Sɛ wohwɛ na wode wo fon yɛ daa asɛm no na sɛ ɛbɛgyegye wo a, fa nea wɔatintim no bi mmom yɛ.” Olivia nso yɛ onuawa a wadi mfeɛ 19. Ɔka sɛ: “Ade baako a ɛboa paa ne sɛ wubenya obi ne no ayɛ daa asɛm no abom; ebetumi ayɛ w’abusuafo anaa wo nnamfo. Sɛ obi wɔ hɔ a bere biara ɔbɛkae wo ama woayɛ daa asɛm no a, ɛremma wontoto mu.”

BOA MMERANTE NE MMABAA MA WƆNYƐ DAA ASƐM DA BIARA
<<<P>>>
19 Awofo, sɛ mode yɛ mo su sɛ da biara mo ne mo mma bɛyɛ daa asɛm no a, ne yɛ bɛka wɔn hɔ, na sɛ wɔnyin mpo a, wɔbɛtoa so ayɛ no da biara. Sɛ wɔsusua sɛ wɔbɛte biribiara ase mpo a, esiane sɛ moyɛ no da biara nti, wɔbesua sɛ wɔn nso wɔbɛyɛ no daa. Yɛbɛyɛ nhwɛso a, ɛte sɛ nea wode aba ketewa bi ahyɛ fam. Sɛ da biara wugugu so nsuo a, ebetumi anyin abɛyɛ dua a ɛyɛ den. Saa ara nso na sɛ mmofra sua Yehowa ho ade kakra da biara a, wɔbenyin abɛyɛ nkurɔfo a wɔwɔ Yehowa mu gyidi paa.—Kenkan Filipifo 1:9, 10.
