Nnawɔtwe mmiɛnsa akyi, wɔ July mu no, Edwin Skinner ne George Wright faa po so hyɛn kɔɔ India. Ná Onua Wright yɛ ɔsɛmpakafo a ɔno nso bɔ ne ho mmɔden. Onua Skinner kɔsom wɔ Betel, na ɔsom adwuma a na wɔyɛ wɔ ɔman no mu nyinaa, ɔno na na ɔhwɛ so. Adwuma na wɔyɛ wɔ Afghanistan, Burma (Myanmar), Ceylon (Sri Lanka), ne Persia (Iran) nyinaa nso, ɔno na na ɔhwɛ so. Onua Skinner tenaa India mfe 64 kosii sɛ owiee ne som adwuma wɔ asaase so wɔ afe 1990 mu.

AHWƐFO AKWANTUFO DII ASƐNKA ADWUMA NO ANIM
<<<P>>>
Efi bɛyɛ afe 1894 reba no, na Yehowa asafo no soma n’ananmusifo ma wɔkɔsrasra Bible Asuafo no, te sɛ nea ɛnnɛ amansin so ahwɛfo yɛ no. Wɔ July 1926 mu no, sɛnea wɔyɛ wɔn adwuma no sesae. Kane no, na wɔde da koro na ɛyɛ, nanso afei deɛ na wɔde nnanu na ɛbɛkɔ akɔsra ekuo biara. Afei nso, na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔde nnɔnhwere 100 kɔ asɛnka bosome biara.
<<<P>>>
Onua George Draper a na ɔyɛ ɔhwɛfo kwantufo no tworɔɔ krataa kɔmaa asafo no. Ɔkaa sɛ: “Yehowa asomfo te sɛ nkraman a wɔwɛn fie, enti ɛsɛ sɛ yɛbɔ nkurɔfo kɔkɔ ma wɔhu asiane a ɛreba. [Yes. 56:10] Ɔwɛn-Aban a yebesua no na ɛbɛboa yɛn ama yɛatumi ayɛ saa, na ɛno na esi m’ani so.” Onua Draper wiee ne som adwuma wɔ asaase so wɔ afe 1938 mu.

NHYIAM BI A YƐYƐE WƆ NEW YORK CITY
<<<P>>>
Nhyiam kɛse a etwa to a yɛyɛe wɔ afe no mu no, yɛyɛɛ no October 14-18, 1926, wɔ New York City. Ná Onua Rutherford bɛma ɔkasa bi a n’asɛmti ne “Ɔko Kɛse Bi a Ɛreba Wiase Nyinaa Abɛn.” Nwumakuo a ɛtworɔ atesɛm nkrataa gye toom sɛ wɔbɛbɔ Onua Rutherford kasa no ho dawuro. Nanso, aka kakra ama bere no aso no, wɔn mu pii a wɔagye din twee wɔn ho.
<<<P>>>
Nea nwumakuo a ɛtworɔ atesɛm nkrataa no yɛe no, anuanom yɛɛ sɛ wɔbɛma amanfo ate ho asɛm. Fiada, October 15 no, Onua R. J. Martin yii kratawa foforɔ a yɛato din Against the People adi. Ná ne botae ne sɛ, ɛbɛpa nea nwumakuo no ayɛ no ho ntama, na abɔ Onua Rutherford kasa no ho dawuro. Onua Martin kaa sɛ: “Yɛafi ase de yɛn ara yɛn mfidie retintim yɛn nwoma. Yɛn nuanom a ɛwɔ Betel no bɛyɛ adwumaden awia ne anadwo akosi sɛ wɔbetintim kratawa no 1,250,000, na yɛbɛma anuanom a ɛwɔ nhyiam yi ase no akɔkyekyɛ ama nkurɔfo.” Bere a wɔwiee nhyiam no Fiada no, anuanom fii ase kyekyɛɛ nkratawa no. Eduu anwummere no, na wɔakyekyɛ nkratawa no 300,000.
<<<P>>>
