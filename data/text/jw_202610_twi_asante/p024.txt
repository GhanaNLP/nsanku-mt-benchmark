DWOM 162
N’Asɛm No Bɛyɛ M’aduane
<<<P>>>
NEA ADESUA YI BƐKA HO ASƐM
<<<P>>>
Nea enti a ɛho hia sɛ yɛyɛ daa asɛm da biara, ne akwan a yɛbɛfa so ayɛ no.
<<<P>>>
Kyerɛwnsɛm No Mu a Wobɛhwehwɛ Daa
<<<P>>>
‘Wɔgyee asɛm no, na na wɔhwehwɛ Kyerɛwnsɛm no mu yiye da biara.’—ASO. 17:11.
<<<P>>>
AFE 1905 mu no, Yehowa asafo no tintim nwoma bi a yɛato din Ɔsoro Mana a Wɔde Ma Gyidi Fiefo Da Biara. Wɔtintim no wɔ kasa pii mu. Wɔde tworɔnsɛm ahoroɔ ne Zion’s Watch Tower no mu nsɛm na ɛyɛɛ nwoma yi. Wei maa anuanom nyaa tworɔnsɛm bi, ne ɛho nkyerɛkyerɛmu a wɔbɛkenkan no da biara. Yehowa nkurɔfo de saa nwoma koro yi ara dii dwuma kosii afe 1927 mu a wɔansesa emu nsɛm. Efi saa bere no rekɔ no, wɔde daa asɛm no mu nsɛm baa Year Book no mu.* Ɛno boaa anuanom ma wɔnyaa tworɔnsɛm pii ne Ɔwɛn-Aban mu nsɛm a ɛkyerɛkyerɛ tworɔnsɛm no mu susuu ho.
<<<P>>>
² Afe 1986 mu no, tworɔnsɛm a anuanom besusu ho ne ɛho nkyerɛkyerɛmu no, asafo no fii ase tintim no wɔ nwomawa bi mu. Wɔtoo din Kyerɛwnsɛm No Mu a Wobɛhwehwɛ Daa. Seesei, yetintim saa nwomawa yi wɔ kasa bɛboro 300 mu. Afei nso, ɛwɔ JW Library® app, Ɔwɛn-Aban INTANƐT SO NHOMAKORABEA™ (wol.jw.org), ne Watchtower Library so.
<<<P>>>
³ Esiane sɛ yenni adaagye nti, yɛn mu bi wɔ hɔ a ɛyɛ den sɛ yɛbɛyɛ daa asɛm da biara. Ɛno nti, momma yensusu nsɛmmisa mmiɛnsa bi ho. Nea edi kan, Bible mu nhwɛso bɛn na ebetumi akanyan yɛn ama yɛayɛ daa asɛm da biara? Nea ɛtɔ so mmienu, sɛ yɛyɛ daa asɛm a, ɛboa yɛn sɛn? Nea etwa toɔ, nyansahyɛ bɛn na ɛbɛboa yɛn na yɛn daa asɛm a yɛyɛ no atu mpɔn?
<<<P>>>
* Kasa ahoroɔ a na wɔnkyerɛɛ Year Book no ase nkɔɔ mu no, na wɔde tworɔnsɛm ahoroɔ a daa asɛm no gyina so no ba Ɔwɛn-Aban a ɛba bosome biara no mu, na na wɔkyerɛ baabi a wubenya tworɔnsɛm no ho nkyerɛkyerɛmu no afi.
<<<P>>>
1-2. (a) Nwoma bɛn na Yehowa asafo no yɛe a ɛmaa anuanom nyaa tworɔnsɛm bi a na wɔtumi susu ho da biara? (b) Ɛnnɛ, ɛhe na yebetumi anya daa asɛm no?
<<<P>>>
3. Dɛn na ebetumi ama ayɛ den sɛ yɛbɛyɛ daa asɛm no da biara, na nsɛmmisa bɛn na yebesusu ho wɔ adesua yi mu?
