10 Nea edi kan no, dwendwen nea Yehowa ayɛ ama wo saa da no ho. (Dw. 40:5) Sɛnea Yehowa boa wo wɔ honhom fam no, dwendwen ho. Sɛnea otie wo mpaebɔ, sɛnea n’Asɛm Bible no kyekye wo werɛ, sɛnea Bible ho nwoma, video, ne sɛnea JW Broadcasting dwumadi no ma wuhu sɛ ɔdwen wo ho no, da no ase wɔ ho. (Dw. 65:2; Mat. 24:45; Rom. 15:4) Nneɛma pa a ɔyɛ ma wo honam fam no nso, dwendwen ho. Ebi ne sɛnea awia rekɔtɔ a ɛyɛ fɛ fa, sɛnea yedidi a yɛte ne dɛ, ne sɛnea ɔma yenya nnamfo a wɔdɔ yɛn. Esiane “nkwa ne ahome” a Onyankopɔn de ama yɛn nti na yenya saa nhyira yi nyinaa. (Aso. 17:25) Dɛn bio na Yehowa de ama wo a wubetumi ada no ase wɔ ho?—Efe. 5:20.

11 Esiane sɛ woda abusua kɛse bi mu na wo ne wo nuanom abom resom Yehowa nti, wunya nhyira pii. Bɔ mmɔden dwendwen saa nhyira no ho. (Dw. 133:1; Mmeb. 18:24) Ebetumi aba sɛ onua anaa onuawa bi ayi ne yam ayɛ biribi aboa wo, anaa waka awerɛkyekyesɛm bi akyerɛ wo. (Mmeb. 12:25; 16:24) Nneɛma nketenkete a yɛn nuanom yɛ ma yɛn no mpo, ne nyinaa ka nhyira a Yehowa de ma yɛn no ho, enti ɛsɛ sɛ yɛda no ase wɔ ho. Ɛho nhwɛso bi ni: Ná onuawa bi babaa awu, na esiane awerɛhoɔ a na ahyɛ ne so nti, sɛ ɔkɔ adesua a, na ontumi nto dwom no bi. Ɔkyerɛ mu a, “da koro, onuawa bi twaa n’ani hui sɛ meresu. Enti ɔyɛɛ ntɛm baa me nkyɛn de ne nsa bɛtoo me kɔn mu, na ɔne me too dwom no wiei. Wei maa ɔdɔ a mewɔ ma anuanom no yɛɛ kɛse.” Onuawa no nsa kɛkɛ a ɔde kɔtoo maame a ne ba awu no kɔn mu no maa ne werɛ kyekyee, na n’ani sɔe paa.

12 Nneɛma pa a Yehowa ayɛ ama wo, ne nea ogu so reyɛ ma wo no, sɛ wudwendwen ho a, ɛbɛma anisɔ a ɛwɔ wo komam no ayɛ kɛse. Sɛnea yɛadi kan aka no, sɛ obi yɛ biribi ma yɛn anaa ɔboa yɛn a, ɛnsɛ sɛ ɛho anisɔ no ka yɛn komam, mmom no, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛda no ase anaa yɛyɛ biribi de kyerɛ sɛ yɛn ani sɔ nea wayɛ ama yɛn no. 13 Nneɛma bɛn na yebetumi ayɛ de akyerɛ anisɔ? Wo deɛ, momma yɛnhwɛ tworɔsɛm a adesua yi gyina so no. Bere a ɔsomafo Paul tuu yɛn fo sɛ yɛnkyerɛ nneɛma ho anisɔ wiei no, ɔtoaa so sɛ: “Na biribiara a moyɛ no, kasa oo, adwuma oo, monyɛ ne nyinaa wɔ Awurade Yesu din mu, na momfa no so nna Agya Onyankopɔn ase.” (Kol. 3:15, 17) Wei ma yehu sɛ kasa ne nneyɛe na yɛde kyerɛ anisɔ. Ɛha yi, na Paul reka aseda a yɛde bɛma Yehowa ho asɛm. Nanso nea afoforɔ yɛ ma yɛn no nso, sɛ yɛkyerɛ ho anisɔ a, ɛma Yehowa ani gye. Ɛnneɛ, yɛbɛyɛ dɛn de yɛn kasa anaa yɛn nsɛm, ne yɛn nneyɛe akyerɛ sɛ yɛn ani sɔ nea afoforɔ yɛ ma yɛn no? Momma yensusu emu bi ho.

14 Fa wo kasa anaa wo nsɛm kyerɛ sɛ w’ani sɔ nea afoforɔ yɛ ma wo no. Ma ɛnyɛ wo su sɛ, sɛ obi yɛ biribi ketewaa bi mpo ma wo a, wobɛda no ase. Sɛ ɛyɛ pono na obue maa wo, nkuranhyɛsɛm bi na ɔka kyerɛɛ wo, anaa nkamfo bi na ɔde maa wo no, wubetumi ada no ase. Ɛtɔ da a, wubetumi asɛnde tɛs mɛsegyi, email, anaa wubetumi atworɔ lɛtɛ akɔda no ase. Yɛbɛyɛ nhwɛso a, asafo mu panyin bi abɛsra wo anaa w’abusua ahyɛ mo nkuran nnansa yi anaa?
