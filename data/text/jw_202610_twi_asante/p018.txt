Yehowa Adesrɛ Ne Sɛ: “Me Ba, Yɛ Onyansafo”
<<<P>>>
NEA ADESUA YI BƐKA HO ASƐM
<<<P>>>
Sɛ mmerante ne mmabaa fa wɔn a wɔdɔ Yehowa nnamfo a, ɛbɛboa wɔn ama wɔakɔ so adi Yehowa nokware.
<<<P>>>
Baanu Sõ a, Emmia
<<<P>>>
“Obi betumi adi ɔbaakofo so, nanso baanu de, wobetumi ne no de asi ani.”—ƆSƐNK. 4:12.
<<<P>>>
YEHOWA asomfo deɛ, yenim hia a ehia paa sɛ yɛyere yɛn ho di Yehowa nokware. Nanso wei yɛ nna fam. Efisɛ nnipa a wɔwɔ wiase no awe ahinam so sɛ wɔbɛma yɛatwe yɛn ho afi Yehowa ho. Ɔkwan baako a wɔfa so yɛ saa ne sɛ, adwene a wɔwɔ wɔ nnamfofa ho no, wɔpɛ sɛ yɛn nso yenya bi. Yɛbɛyɛ nhwɛso a, ebinom susu sɛ, nnamfo a wɔwɔ no, sɛ wɔn suban yɛ oo, sɛ wɔn suban nyɛ oo, ɛnyɛ ɛno na ehia, na mmom nea ehia ne nnamfo dodoɔ a wɔwɔ. Mmerante ne mmabaa bebree wɔ hɔ a, sɛ wɔrepɛ afotu anaa awerɛkyekye a, wɔn atipɛnfo mu nko ara na wɔhwehwɛ no. Ebinom nso wɔ hɔ a, wɔte nka sɛ wɔnhia obiara, enti wɔmfa nnamfo. Yehowa ne Yesu nso ɛ? Wɔpɛ sɛ yenya nnamfo anaa?
<<<P>>>
2 Yehowa pɛ sɛ yenya nnamfo a wɔbɛboa yɛn ama yɛasom no yie. Bere a ne Ba Yesu wɔ asaase so no, nnamfo a ɛte saa na ɔfae. Aka kakra ama Yesu awu no, ɔkamfoo n’asomafo anokwafo no sɛ: “Mo na moda so bata me ho wɔ me sɔhwɛ mu.” (Luka 22:28; Yoh. 15:15) Ɔyɔnko berɛbo deɛ, ɛnyɛ bere a nneɛma rekɔ yie nko ara na ɔwɔ hɔ ma wo, na mmom “amanehunu bere” mu nso, ɔwɔ hɔ ma wo. (Mmeb. 17:17) Ɔsɛnkafo ti 4 nkyekyɛm 9-12* ma yehu sɛ nnamfo pa deɛ, obiara boa ne yɔnko kyekye ne yɔnko werɛ. (Kenkan.) Afei nso obiara hyɛ ne yɔnko nkuran ma ɔyɛ adepa. Sɛ wowɔ nnamfo pa sei na sɛ biribi retwetwe wo sɛ kɔyɛ nea Yehowa mpɛ a, wɔboa wo
<<<P>>>
* Ɛwom sɛ yɛtaa de tworɔnsɛm yi ka aware ho asɛm, nanso nea ɛfa ho paa yɛ mfaso a ɛwɔ adamfofa so.
<<<P>>>
1. Adwene bɛn na wiase no wɔ wɔ nnamfofa ho?
<<<P>>>
2. (a) Nnipa bɛn saa na Yesu faa wɔn nnamfo? (b) Sɛ wohwɛ asɛm a ɛwɔ Ɔsɛnkafo 4:9-12 no a, nnamfo pa boa yɛn sɛn?
