4 Tworɔnsɛm a adesua yi gyina so no, ɛma yehu nea Yudafo bi a na wɔte Makedonia kuro Beroia mu yɛe bere a ɔsomafo Paul kyerɛkyerɛɛ wɔn no. (Kenkan Asomafo Nnwuma 17:10, 11.) Beroiafo a na wɔretie ɔsomafo Paul no, na wɔayɛ krado sɛ nea ɔbɛka biara wɔbetie. Ɛno nti, Bible kaa wɔn ho asɛm sɛ wɔwɔ “adwempa” na “wɔde wɔn adwene nyinaa gyee asɛm no.” Nea Paul kyerɛkyerɛɛ wɔn no, wɔde totoo nea Tworɔnsɛm no ka ho, na wɔhui sɛ ɛyɛ nokware. Sɛ yɛretie ɔkasa wɔ adesua anaa nhyiam ase a, biribi saa na yɛyɛ. Sɛ ɔkasafo no rekenkan Bible no a, yɛhwɛ yɛn deɛ mu di akyire. Ɛno ma yehu sɛ nea ɔkasafo no reka no yɛ nokware.

5 Beroiafo no mu “pii bɛyɛɛ gyidifo.” (Aso. 17:12) Nanso na ɔhaw bi rebɔbɔ ba. Yudafo bi fi Tesalonika baa Beroia bɛkekaa ɔsomafo Paul ho nsɛmmɔne, na ɛmaa ɔmanfo no sɔre tiaa Beroiafo a afei na wɔabɛyɛ Kristofo no. (Aso. 17:13) Nanso ɛno ammɔ wɔn hu koraa. Tworɔnsɛm no a na wɔhwehwɛ mu daa nti, na wɔgye di paa sɛ wɔahu nokware no. Wei amma wɔn gyidi anhinhim. Yɛn nso, sɛ yɛhwehwɛ Tworɔnsɛm no mu daa a, ɛbɛboa yɛn ma sɔhwɛ a yebehyia biara no, yɛatumi agyina ano.

DAA ASƐM DA BIARA?
<<<P>>>
6 Yɛyɛ daa asɛm da biara de kyerɛ Yehowa sɛ yɛdɔ no, na ne som ho hia yɛn. Yehowa ne yɛn Nyankopɔn, enti ɛho hia paa sɛ yɛsom no.
<<<P>>>
Bible Mu Nhwɛso a Ɛma Yehu Nea Na Ebinom Yɛ De Som Yehowa Da Biara
<<<P>>>
• Ná Mose Mmara no hwehwɛ sɛ Yehowa nkurɔfo bɔ afɔre da biara.—Ex. 29:38-42; 30:7, 8.
<<<P>>>
• Yehowa ka kyerɛɛ Yosua sɛ ɔkenkan Mmara nwoma no na ɔndwendwen ho “awia ne anadwo.”—Yos. 1:8, ase hɔ asɛm.
<<<P>>>
• Yehowa ka kyerɛɛ Israel ahemfo sɛ, wɔnhwɛ Mose Mmara no so na wɔntworɔ bi mfa, na wɔnkenkan ndwendwen ho da biara.—Deut. 17:18-20.
<<<P>>>
• Odwontofo no tuu yɛn fo sɛ, yɛnkenkan Onyankopɔn mmara na yɛndwendwen ho “awia ne anadwo.”—Dw. 1:1-3.
<<<P>>>
• Ná Daniel bɔ mpae mprɛnsa da biara.—Dan. 6:10.
<<<P>>>
• Adiyisɛm nwoma no ka sɛ nnipakuo kɛse bi de ɔsom kronn ma Onyankopɔn “awia ne anadwo.”—Adi. 7:15.
<<<P>>>
Ɔdɔ yɛn na ɔpɛ sɛ yenya daa nkwa. (Yoh. 3:16) Nhwɛso ahoroɔ a ɛwɔ Bible mu ma yehu sɛ, sɛ yɛsom Yehowa a, ɛma ne koma tɔ ne yam. Anuanom pii ahu sɛ, sɛ wɔyɛ daa asɛm ansa na wɔakɔ wɔn nnwuma so a, ɛma wɔnya kwan bɔ mpae, kenkan Bible no na wɔnya bere dwendwen ho.
