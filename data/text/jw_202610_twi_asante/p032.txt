Yehowa Hyira Wɔn a Wɔwɔ Gyidi
<<<P>>>
Nnawɔtwe biara, na Emma de nnansa yɛ adwuma wɔ hotɛl bi. Ɔkɔ nso a, ɔde nnɔnhwere nkron na ɛyɛ adwuma. Bere a ɔmantam nhyiam duu so a ɛsɛ sɛ ɔkɔ no, ɔkɔe sɛ ɔrekɔsrɛ da koro afi n’adwumam panyin hɔ na ama watumi akɔ. Ɛnyɛ sɛ adwumam panyin no ampene kɛkɛ, na mmom ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ, efi saa da no rekɔ no, sɛ anka ɔde nnansa bɛyɛ adwuma no, ɔde nnanum mmom na ɛbɛyɛ adwuma. Afei ɔde kaa ho sɛ, sɛ wampene nhyehyɛe foforɔ no so a, ɛnneɛ ɔmfa no sɛ wayi ne ho adi. Emma yɛɛ n’adwene sɛ obegyae adwuma no sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obetumi akɔ nhyiam no, na ɔde ne ho too Yehowa so sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ no.
<<<P>>>
Nhyiam no akyi, bere a Emma de krataa a ɔde regyae adwuma no kɔe no, ɔkɔtoo ɔpanyin paa a ɔhwɛ hotɛl no so no. Bere a ɔpanyin no tee nea ɛkɔɔ so no, ɔhyɛɛ Emma bɔ sɛ ɔno ara ne adwumam panyin no bɛkasa na wama watoa so ayɛ n’adwuma. Nanso nnawɔtwe mmienu akyi no, Emma adwumam panyin no ka kyerɛɛ no sɛ wayi no adi. Bere a Emma anni nna no, ɔsan hyiaa ɔpanyin paa a ɔhwɛ hotɛl no so no, na ɛhyee no sɛ adwumam panyin no antie no, na wayi Emma adi. Ɛhɔ ara na ɔsan de Emma adwuma no maa no, na ɔkaa sɛ ɔbɛyɛ adwuma nnanum nnawɔtwe biara. Anikasɛm ne sɛ, sɛ anka ɔde nnɔnhwere nkron na ɛbɛyɛ adwuma no, ɔde nnɔnhwere nnan pɛ na na ɛbɛyɛ adwuma da biara. Ɛbɛdaa adi sɛ nhyehyɛe foforɔ yi yɛ koraa sen dada no, efisɛ ɛmaa no kwan ma ɔyɛɛ n’akwampae adwuma no ne nnwuma afoforɔ wɔ Yehowa som mu. Sɛnea Yehowa maa ne nsa kaa nea ohia no, ɔno koraa na ɛyɛ no nwanwa.
