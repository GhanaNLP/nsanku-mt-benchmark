<<<P>>>
Hokwan a Woanya Sɛ Yehowa Nam Wo So Reyɛ Anwanwadeɛ No, Ntoto No Ase
<<<P>>>
“Ɛno na ɛbɛma nnipa nyinaa ahu sɛ moyɛ m’asuafo.”—YOH. 13:35.
<<<P>>>
FA NO sɛ bere a Yehowa paee Po Kɔkɔɔ no mu maa Israelfo no faa mu a Egyptfo no nsa anka wɔn no, na wowɔ hɔ bi. Hwɛ sɛnea anka w’ani bɛgye afa! Afei nso, fa w’adwene bu sɛ w’ani tua sɛ Yesu resa ayarefo yare, na ɔrenyane awufo. Wunhu sɛ anka ɛbɛma w’ani agye paa? Anwanwadeɛ ahoroɔ a Bible ka ho asɛm no, wei yɛ emu kakraa bi. Obiara a ɔnyɛ nyaatwom na ɔyɛ ɔnokwafo a ɔde n’ani huu anwanwadeɛ ahoroɔ no, na ɔbɛgye atom sɛ ɛyɛ Onyankopɔn na ɔhyɛ akyire. (Mat. 15:31) Nanso saa anwanwadeɛ yi sisii no, ɛnyɛ nnɛ. Ɛnnɛ nso ɛ, wohwɛ a anwanwadeɛ resisi anaa? Aane, ebi resisi. Nanso yɛka anwanwadeɛ koraa a, ɛkyerɛ sɛn?
<<<P>>>
2 Anwanwadeɛ yɛ biribi a onipa dasani ahoɔden ntumi nyɛ, anaa biribi a ontumi nte ase nwie. Ɛyɛ biribi a Yehowa de ne tumi yɛ. Adesua yi mu no, yɛbɛhwɛ anwanwadeɛ mmiɛnsa a Yehowa reyɛ wɔ ne nkurɔfo mu nnɛ. Yebetumi aka sɛ saa nneɛma no yɛ anwanwadeɛ, efisɛ, Yehowa nko ara na obetumi ama no asisi. Emu biara no, yɛbɛhwɛ nea yɛbɛyɛ de akyerɛ sɛ, yɛyɛ Onyankopɔn “mfɛfo adwumayɛfo.”—Kenkan 1 Korintofo 3:9.
