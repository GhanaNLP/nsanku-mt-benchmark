Sɛ wokyerɛ nneɛma ho anisɔ a, ɛma w’ani gye, na w’asom dwo wo
(Hwɛ nkyekyɛm 1)
<<<P>>>
sɛ yɛda no ase anaa yɛyɛ biribi de kyerɛ sɛ yɛn ani sɔ nea wayɛ ama yɛn no. Nea ɛbɛboa yɛn ama yɛatumi ayɛ wei no, momma yensusu nsɛmmisa mmiɛnsa yi ho: (1) Adɛn nti na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛkyerɛ nneɛma ho anisɔ? (2) Yɛbɛyɛ dɛn na anisɔ a yɛwɔ no ayɛ kɛse? (3) Nneɛma bɛn na yebetumi ayɛ de akyerɛ anisɔ?
<<<P>>>
ADƐN NTI NA ƐSƐ SƐ YƐKYERƐ NNEƐMA HO ANISƆ?
<<<P>>>
4 Sɛ yɛankyerɛ nneɛma ho anisɔ a, yɛn ara na ɛha yɛn. Bonniayɛfo nni anigye. Bible ka sɛ: “Mmɔborɔwa de, da biara nye mma no.” (Mmeb. 15:15) Ɛtɔ da a, yɛn nyinaa hyia ɔhaw bi a etumi ma yɛn aba mu bu. Ɔhaw a yehyia no, ebi wɔ hɔ a yɛrentumi nsi ano, nanso nea yɛbɛyɛ wɔ ho no deɛ, egyina yɛn so. Nea ɛnkɔ yie wɔ yɛn asetena mu no, sɛ ɛno na daa yɛde yɛn adwene si so deɛ a, anhwɛ a da biara bɛyɛ da bɔne ama yɛn. Suban a ɛte saa nso deɛ, ɛbɛsɛe yɛn anigye, efisɛ ɛremma yenhu nneɛma pa a ɛrekɔ so wɔ yɛn asetena mu no.
<<<P>>>
4. Sɛ yɛankyerɛ nneɛma ho anisɔ a, ɔhaw bɛn na ebetumi akɔfa aba?
<<<P>>>
5 Sɛ yɛkyerɛ nneɛma ho anisɔ a, ɛboa yɛn. Obi a ɔkyerɛ nneɛma ho anisɔ no, n’ani gye. Mmebusɛm 15:15 no fã a etwa toɔ no ka sɛ: “Nea ɔwɔ anigye koma de, daa ɔwɔ apontow mu.” Obi a “ɔwɔ anigye koma” no, sɛ ɔrehyia ɔhaw mpo a, ɔde n’adwene si nneɛma pa a ɛrekɔ so wɔ n’asetena mu no so, na ɔkyerɛ ho anisɔ. Ɛba saa a, eyi n’adwene fi nneɛma a ɛnkɔ yie wɔ n’asetena mu no so. Wei ma n’ani gye, na daa ɔte nka sɛ “ɔwɔ apontow mu.”
<<<P>>>
6 Kenkan Filipifo 4:6, 7. Sɛ yɛbɔ mpae da Yehowa ase wɔ nea ɔyɛ ma yɛn ho a, ɛmma “dadwen nnhyɛ” yɛn so. Adɛn nti na yɛreka saa?
