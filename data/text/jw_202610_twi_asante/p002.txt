MFE ƆHA A ATWAM
<<<P>>>
ƆWƐN-ABAN a ɛbaa January 1, 1926 kaa sɛ, “ɛrenkyɛ koraa Onyankopɔn bɛma obiara ahu sɛ ɔno ne Otumfoɔ no.” Ɛsan kaa sɛ, “Onyankopɔn beyi nnipa bi ama wɔadi ne ho adanse.”
<<<P>>>
Afe 1926 mu no, Yehowa nam Yesu Kristo a ɔyɛ asafo no ti so maa Kristofo no huu pefee sɛ, asɛnka adwuma no na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔde di kan wɔ wɔn asetena mu. Ɛno nti, “akoa nokwafo ne ɔbadwemma” no yɛɛ nwoma afoforɔ, na ɔkwan a na wɔfa so yɛ asafo nhyiam nso, wɔyɛɛ mu nsakrae. Afei nso, wɔmaa anuanom huu nea wɔyɛ a ɛbɛkyerɛ paa sɛ wɔyɛ Kristofo. (Mat. 24:45) Adan a na wɔbɛyɛ mu adwuma, ne mfidie a wɔde betintim nwoma ahoroɔ na ama wɔatumi ayɛ asɛnka adwuma no nso, Kristo boaa wɔn maa wɔn nsa kae.
<<<P>>>
WƆYII NWOMA FOFORƆ ADI, NA SƐNEA WƆYƐ ASAFO NHYIAM NSO WƆYƐƐ MU NSAKRAE
<<<P>>>
April mu no, The Golden Age a seesei yɛfrɛ no Nyan! no de too dwa sɛ, wɔayi nwoma foforɔ a yɛfrɛ no Deliverance adi. Ná ɛno na ɛbɛba abesi Studies in the Scriptures nwoma no ananmu wɔ asɛnka adwuma no mu. Nea ɛbɛyɛ na anuanom anya nwoma no dodoɔ a wɔhia de akɔ asɛnka no, anuanom a wɔwɔ Betel no tɔɔ afidie a wɔde tintim nwoma foforɔ, na na etumi tintim nwoma 22 wɔ simma baako ntam. Obiara a na Bible Asuafo no bedi no adanse no, sɛ ɛbɛyɛ yie a, na wɔma no nwoma no bi.
<<<P>>>
Mfe pii a abɛsen kɔ no, na Bible Asuafo no sua Studies in the Scriptures nwoma no wɔ adesua ase, nanso efi May 1926 no, wɔfii ase suaa Ɔwɛn-Aban no. Ɛboaa wɔn sɛn?
<<<P>>>
Little Bunker Hill asafo a ɛwɔ Illinois no tworɔɔ krataa bi kɔmaa Onua Rutherford. Wɔkaa sɛ: “Little Bunker Hill asafo no kyia wo. Sɛ edu Kwasida a, nsɛm a Yehowa asafo no akyerɛ sɛ yensua no, ɛno na yesua. Ɛyɛ yɛn dɛ paa sɛ, sɛ hann foforɔ biara ba a, yɛn nyinaa hu no ntɛm wɔ Ɔwɛn-Aban no mu.”
<<<P>>>
“Hann foforɔ,” anaa nsakrae foforɔ a na ɛba no boaa Bible Asuafo no ma wɔde wɔn adwene sii asɛnka adwuma no so. Yɛbɛyɛ nhwɛso a, Ɔwɛn-Aban adesua baako kyerɛkyerɛɛ mu sɛ, ɛwom sɛ ɛfata sɛ yɛda suban pa adi, yɛne nkurɔfo di no yie, na yɛda obuo adi kyerɛ wɔn, nanso ɛnsɛ sɛ yebu yɛn ani gu adwuma a ɛho hia paa no so. Saa adesua asɛm no kaa sɛ: ‘Sɛ obi dɔ Awurade a, ɛsɛ sɛ odi ne mmara nsɛm so. Yehowa ka sɛ: Moyɛ m’adansefo, na mene mo Nyankopɔn. Sɛ obi yɛ Kristoni a, ɛsɛ sɛ ne ho pere no sɛ obedi Onyankopɔn ho adanse.’
<<<P>>>
Ɔwɛn-Aban adesua foforɔ nso sii so dua sɛ, nea nokware Kristofo yɛ de kyerɛ sɛ wɔdɔ Yehowa paa ne sɛ, wɔbefi wɔn koma nyinaa mu asom no. Saa adesua asɛm no hyɛɛ Bible Asuafo no nkuran sɛ, nsuo mu oo, awia mu oo, wɔnyere wɔn ho nka asɛmpa no nkyerɛ obiara.—Yes. 43:10, 12; Mat. 24:14.
