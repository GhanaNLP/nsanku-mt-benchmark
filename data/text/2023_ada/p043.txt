Nihi A He Jua Wami Mi Oti: E ji nihi a he jua wami kpo jemi nɛ jeɔ kpo kɛ guɔ nihi a he jua tsɔɔmi he ɔ nɛ ni tsumi nɔ amaniɛbɔ he ɔ jeɔ kpo daa oslami aloo hlami gbogboe ɔ.

Nihi A Sɛmi Nine Mi Dami Nɔ: E ji blɔ nɔ tomi ko nɔ nɛ a guɔ nɛ a ngɔɔ ma a nitsɛ e mi nihi nɛ a pee kɛ daa nihi nɛ a sɛ ɔmɛ a nanemi pe a ngɔmi kɛ ya mase aloo maje konɛ a kɛ hue aloo wo ma a nitsɛ e mi nihi nɛ a pee ɔmɛ a nɔ.

Nɔ Da aloo Vide Nɔ Hiɔ: E ji videhi a nɔ hiɔ nɛ baa ngɛ sika papɛɛhi nɛ a wui lolo ɔmɛ a nɔ.
<<<P>>>
Nɔ Da aloo Videhi A Womi: E ji hiɔhi nɛ baa ngɛ sika papɛɛhi aloo sika pami nɔ aloo videhi a womi ngɛ sika pami aloo awaba tomi nihi a nɔ.

Blɔ Nɔ Tomi kɛ ha Ohia Nɔ Gbɔmi (IPEP): Enɛ ɔ ji blɔ nya tomi ehe aloo muno ko nɛ NPP Amlaalo ɔ ngɔ kɛ ba nɛ e ba gbɔ ohia nɔ nɛ ha yi sɔmi aloo pɛpɛɛpɛ peemi nɛ ba ngɛ ohiafohi kɛ niatsɛmɛ a kpɛti. Titli ɔ, ngɛ ma tsɔwi ɔmɛ a mi kɛ gu he hiami nihi kaa blɔ, nyu kɛ wehi a he blɔ nya tomi mi.
