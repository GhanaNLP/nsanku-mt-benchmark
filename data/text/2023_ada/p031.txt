La Wo Nya Blɔ ɔ He Ni Tsumi ɔ
<<<P>>>
61. Kɛ piɛɛ mɔde nɛ Amlaalo ɔ ngɛ bɔe kaa e ma bli blɔ ɔmɛ a mi nɛ e gba a mi nɛ e wa kɛ gbɔ oslahi a nɔ ngɛ blɔ ngua amɛ a nɔ ɔ, a je sisi bɔni Tɛma kɛ yaa Aflao kɛ, kɛ je Tɛma kɛ yaa Akɔsɔmo blɔ ɔmɛ a he ni tsumi. A je sisi hu bɔni Assin Fosu kɛ yaa Assin Praso blɔ hu dlami. A ma bli blɔ nɛ ɔ nɛ e kami ji 1.2km ngɛ Assin Fosu ma a mi ɔ mi nɛ a maa pee lɛ lɔle eywiɛ a nyɛɛmi he ngɛ be kake mi. A ma tsa nɔ hu ma pee lɔku nɛ tlee maa gu e sisi nɛ a ma gba gɔtahi ngɛ blɔ ɔ he kɛ je Assin Fosu kɛ ya su Assin Praso.
