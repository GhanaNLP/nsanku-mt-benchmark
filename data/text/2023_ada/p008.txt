“Ma a Sika He Blɔ Nya Tomi Susumi Tso Ɔmɛ Kpalemi Kɛ Ba Da A Glɛ Nɔ, A He Piɛ Pomi Kɛ Da Tɛ Kake Nɔ Kɛ Gu Blɔnya Tomi Kpakpahi A Nɔ”

ha nɔ fɛɛ nɔ, Mlaa Womi Kpe Bi, Ni Tsumitsɛmɛ A Kuu ɔ, Ni Tsumihi Mi Kuu Slɔɔto Ɔmɛ, Ma Bi Tsuo, Jami He Kuuhi, Nihi A Peemi He Kuuhi ngɛ Ghana, Ghana Ni Juali A Kake Peemi Kuu ɔ, Womi Mi Leli, Nɔ He Juɛmi Tsɔɔli kɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ Ghana kaa a ngɔ tsui si tomi kɛ si fimi nɛ waa kɛ ngɛ haomi kɛ nyagba nɛ ɔmɛ tsuo a mi gblee ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Ngɛ Ma nɔ yelɔ pe kulaa a nɛ ji Nana Addo Danquah Akuffo-Addo nanemi ɔ, i ngɛ hlae nɛ ma ha nɔ fɛɛ nɔ buasɛ gbi aloo ma wo Ghana ma bi tsuo a bua nɛ i ngɛ mɛ si woe kaa Amlaalo ɔ ngɔ blɔ nya tomi komɛ kɛ ngɛ si puee nɛ e maa kpale ngɔ wa ma sika he blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ kɛ ba da e kla aloo e glɛ nɔ ekohu nɛ e gbla sika kɛ tsake nihi babauu a hɛ mi nɛ e bli kɔmpiuta kɛ zugba nɔ nihi slɔɔto a peemi ɔmɛ a mi, konɛ e gbɔ nihi nɛ a he hiaa we ɔmɛ a sɛmi ɔ nɔ konɛ e hue nihi a ngɔmi kɛ ya mase ɔ nɔ. Nɛ jahanɛ se hu ɔ, e bli nihi nɛ wa sɛ ɔmɛ a peemi kɛ a bɔmi mi ngɛ ma a mi konɛ wa kɛ baa nihi nɛ aklama kpakpa ko be a he ɔmɛ a yi.
<<<P>>>
Ni slɔɔto nɛ wa kase kɛ je ni nɛ ɔmɛ nɛ a ba amɛ a mi, kaa ma, ngɛ be nɛ ɔ mi ɔ ma tsɛ wa juɛmi se nɛ wa kɛ tsake wa nihi a peemi hɛ mi mi nɛ e pee gbugbuugbu nɛ wa kɛ ngɔ wa ma he sika blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ kɛ ba da kla gbugbuugbu nɔ. Wa ma nyɛ maa pee lɛ nɛ e hi. Wa maa je kpo ngɛ haomi aloo nyagba nɛ ɔmɛ a mi kɛ he wami muno ko. Tsɛ Opemɛ ɔ maa baa wa yi nɛ E ma ha wɔ he wami nɛ wa kɛ pee ni nɛ ɔmɛ tsuo kulaa.
