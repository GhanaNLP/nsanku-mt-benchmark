14. Amlaalo ɔ sika blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ kɛ be nɛ a tsɔɔ ngɔ bɔjɛti ɔ mi hiɔ nɛ nya su sidi ayɔ 44,022 nɛ daa si kɛ ha lafa mi gbami 7.4 (ngɛ GDP nɔ) kɛ ba, ke waa kɛ to blɔ hyɛmi tɛ nɛ a po fɔ si nɛ ji sidi ayɔ 36,684 nɛ tsɔɔ lafa mi gbami 6.2 ngɛ GDP nɔ (6.2% of GDP). A da ma a nitsɛ e juɛtɛɛ komɛ a nɔ kɛ wo hiɔ nɛ ɔ. Wa da mase sika ko nɛ e yibɔ su sidi ayɔ 6,531 nɛ ji lafa mi gbami 1.1 ngɛ GDP nɔ (1.1% of GDP) kɛ wo hiɔ nɛ ɔ ekomɛ.

15. Nihi a mi hyɛmi blɔ nɔ tomi ko nɛ a pee ngɛ Lemɔ hlami ɔ e nyagbe ngɛ jeha 2022 mi nɛ a mɛ nɔ mi lolo ɔ je kpo aloo tsɔɔ kaa hiɔ kluduu nɛ ma a ye ji sidi ayɔ 467,371.32 nɛ ji Amɛlika dɔla a 48,871.34 nɛ daa si kɛ ha lafa mi gbami 75.9 ngɛ GDP nɔ. Ngɛ hiɔ nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, ma a nitsɛ e mi hiɔ nɛ e ye ji sidi ayɔ 195,657.61, nɛ daa si ha lafa mi gbami 31.8 ngɛ GDP nɔ, be mi nɛ mase hiɔ nɛ Ghana ma a ye ɔ ya su sidi ayɔ 271,713.71, nɛ e daa si kɛ ha lafa mi gbami 44.1 ngɛ GDP nɔ.
