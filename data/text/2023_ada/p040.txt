MUNYUNGU PƆTƐƐ KOMƐ A SISI TSƆƆMI (BƆJƐTI Ɔ MI MUNYUNGUHI)
<<<P>>>
Kɔpe Kake Ni Tsumi Kake (1D1F): Ni tsumi hehi a mami loo a blimi ngɛ kpo wawi ɔmɛ a mi ɔ maa ngɔ ni tsumi fuu kɛ ba ha Ghana ma a nɔ yami. E ma wa kɛ bli ni tsumi slɔɔto kɛ kpa Ghana ma a tsuo mi. Enɛ ɔ ma bli ni tsumi slɔɔto ha Ghana ma bi tsuo.
<<<P>>>
Aflika Mahi A Jua Yemi Nɛ To Be Nɔ He: E ji jua nɛ a pee nɛ e ma je tohi ngɛ Aflika mahi kɛ a sibi a jua yemi mi, nɛ e ma ha nɛ jua yemi ngɛ Aflika ma amɛ a kpɛti ɔ e he be wae nɛ a na he se.
<<<P>>>
Nɔ Gbɔmi: Enɛ ɔ ji blɔ nɔ tomi nɛ Amlaalo ɔ guɔ nɛ e kɛ jeɔ ma bi a haomi kɛ nyagba slɔɔto ɔmɛ a nɔ.
<<<P>>>
Nihi a Gbami: Enɛ ɔ ji blɔ nya tomi nɛ Amlaalo guɔ nɔ kɛ gbaa weto ni slɔɔto kɛ ha ni tsumi hehi nɛ ngɛ Amlaalo ɔ sisi ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Se Kpo: Yemi kɛ buami nɛ a kɛ ma fi aloo pi nɔ ko se.
<<<P>>>
Henɔ Tadehi aloo Bohi: Tade henɔ aloo hanɔ kakehi nɛ a ngɛ juae ngɛ tsuapo mi.
<<<P>>>
Jua Yemi Nɔ Womi: E ji jua yemi nɔ womi nɛ a pee ngɛ ni juami kuu komɛ a kpɛti ngɛ ma ko mi kɛ nɔ nɛ ya nɔ ngɛ ma kpahi a kpɛti ngɛ je ɔ mi ngɛ be pɔtɛɛ ko mi.
<<<P>>>
Bɔjɛti: E ji blɔ nya tomi nɛ tsɔɔ he nɛ a ma na sika ngɛ kɛ nihi nɛ a maa ngɔ sika kɛ tsu. Nɔhyɛ nɔ: Weku ko ma nyɛ maa to blɔ nya nɛ ɔ eko kɛ tsɔɔ sika abɔ nɛ e ngɛ kɛ bɔ nɛ e ma tsu jamɛ a sika a kɛ a hiami a nya ni ha. A maa hyɛ a hiami nihi pɔtɛɛ komɛ kaa tsu hiɔwo, nyu he hiɔwo, kane he hiɔwo kɛ niye ni kɛ ekpa komɛ. Ke weku nɛ ɔ maa hiaa yi gbemi sika ko ɔ, a ma nyɛ ma tsu ni aloo a ya pa sika kɛ ha a blɔ nya ni tsumi nɛ ɔmɛ.
<<<P>>>
Bɔjɛti mi hiɔ (Defisiti): Hiɔ nɛ ɔ baa ke Amlaalo ɔ tsu sika he ni pe bɔ nɛ tohi nɛ a ngɛ blɔ hyɛeɔ ngɔɔ kɛ baa a.
<<<P>>>
Gbe Henɔ Komɛ: Lohwe komɛ nɛ a ngɛ kaa gbe aloo a je gbehi.
