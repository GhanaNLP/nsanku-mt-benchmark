NYA TSƆƆMI
<<<P>>>
2. 2023 ma bi a bɔjɛti ɔ aloo ma a sika he blɔ nya tomi ɔ do nɔ nɛ Amlaalo ɔ pee ngɛ 2022 mi ɔ nɔ nɛ e kɛ tsɔɔ tohi a abɔ nɛ e ma he konɛ e kɛ tsu e yi mi tomi ɔmɛ a nya ni ngɛ 2023 jeha a mi. Oti kɛ ha 2023 bɔjɛti ɔ ji: “Ma a Sika He Blɔ Nya Tomi Susumi Tso Ɔmɛ Kpalemi Kɛ Ba Da A Glɛ Nɔ, A He Piɛ Pomi Kɛ Da Tɛ Kake Nɔ Kɛ Gu Blɔnya Tomi Kpakpahi A Nɔ”
<<<P>>>
3. Daa jeha a, ma sika blɔ nya tomi ehe ɔ toɔ blɔnya kɛ ha ni ehehi a sisi jemi aloo a peemi be mi nɛ nɔ nɛ a je sisi momo jeha nɛ be ɔmɛ tlɔ he nɛ a kɛ mɛ su ɔmɛ. Tsa pi nɛ blɔnya tomi nɛ ɔ ngɔɔ nɔ nyɛmi kɛ fɔɔ wa sika blɔ nya tomi ɔmɛ a nɔ kɛkɛ pɛ se e gblaa se hiɔhi kɛ baa nɛ enɛ ɔ hu ya hueɔ aloo woɔ nihi nɛ a maa pee ɔmɛ a jua amɛ a nɔ ejakaa hiɔhi nɛ a tsɛ a womi ɔmɛ to ɔ gblaa he kpahi kɛ baa.
<<<P>>>
4. Ngɛ 2023 jeha a mi ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ maa je blɔnya tomi kɛ mlaahi ehehi kpo konɛ a nyɛ nɛ a kanya nɛ a wa kɛ gbe ni tsumihi nɛ a je a sisi momo ɔmɛ a nya.
