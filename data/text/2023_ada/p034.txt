Sukuu Tsɔsemi
<<<P>>>
65. Bɔ nɛ pee ha nɛ Amlaalo ɔ yi mi tomi kaa e ma ha nɛ nihi nɛ a he hiaa kɛ ha sukuu tsɔsemi kɛ a he mɔde bɔmi nɛ e su sukuu tsɔsemi hami he ɔmɛ tsuo ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ he (nɛ e gba Bɔɔsuhi nɛ eko fɛɛ ko woɔ nimli 66) kakaaka 175, “Pick Up” 71 kɛ Tlatɔ 2 kɛ ha Siniɔ Hai, Hɛ mi ga kɛ Nine Nguɛ Nya Ga Tsɔsemi Sukuu ɔmɛ, kɛ Tsɔsemi Hami He ekpa komɛ ngɛ Ghana ma a tsuo mi.
<<<P>>>
66. Ngɛ jeha 2022 mi ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ tsɔse nyumuhi 3,724 kɛ yihi hu 3,715 nɛ mɛ tsuo a ji Hɛ mi ga kɛ nine nguɛ nya ni tsuli. A ha mɛ tsɔsemi nɛ ɔ ngɛ “Community Development kɛ Vocational Technical Institutes” sukuuhi 29 kɛ duɔ a ga kɛ nine nguɛ nya he kpɔmi nile kɛ juɛmi ɔmɛ a nya kɛ ha ni tsumi blimi kɛ ohia nɔ gbɔmi.
<<<P>>>
67. Kɛ piɛɛ enɛ ɔ he ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ kpale tsɔse ma bi a tsɔseli 248 ngɛ tsɔsemi he nɛ a tsɛɔ lɛ ke “Rural Development College” ɔ nɛ ngɛ Kwaso ɔ. Tsɔsemi nɛ ɔ ma ha ma bi ɔmɛ ma nya buami kɛ ma he bumi su kɛ nile.
<<<P>>>
68. Ngɛ jeha 2023 mi ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ ma tsɔse nihi 500 ngɛ ma bi a tsɔsemi, a nɔ yami kɛ ma he bumi juɛmi kɛ nile mi nɛ a maa pee kudɔmi ni komɛ ha mɛ nɛ ma ha mɛ tsɔsemi ngɛ jale peemi, kuau lomi kɛ lɛda he ni tsumi nɛ e gbla sika ba wo a dɛ konɛ e je mɛ ngɛ ohia yemi sisi.
