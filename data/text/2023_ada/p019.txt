Sika Gblami Blɔ Nya Tomihi
<<<P>>>
19. Bɔ nɛ pee nɛ wa nyɛ pi sika blɔ nya tomihi nɛ a to kɛ fɔ si ɔmɛ tso saminya a, Amlaalo ɔ maa ngɔ sika gblami blɔ nɔ tomi komɛ nɛ ma nyɛ gbla sika kaa lafa mi gbami 1.35 ngɛ GDP nɔ kɛ ba fɔ Mlaa Womi Kpe Bi ɔmɛ a hɛ mi kɛ ha a mi hyɛmi kɛ a nɔ nine pimi. Blɔ nya tomi nɛ ɔ ekomɛ ji:
<<<P>>>
• Sigaleti kɛ taba nɔ to ɔmɛ a mi hyɛmi aloo a mi tsakemi nɛ a kɛ ECOWAS blɔ nɔ tomi ni ɔmɛ nɛ a kɔ, a hue aloo a wo da nɛ nya wa to ɔmɛ a nɔ pe Biɛɛ nɔ to ɔmɛ.
<<<P>>>
• A hue aloo a wo mi jiimi to nɛ ji VAT susumi tso ɔ nɔ lafa mi gbami enyɔ kɛ fa ngɛ blema nɔ lafa mi gbami 12.5 nɔ ɔ nɔ konɛ e ba da lafa mi gbami 15. Enɛ ɔ se ɔ, a tsake mi jiimi to (VAT) ya a e klemi ɔ aloo e yi blimi ɔ mi konɛ a pee tsakemi titlii komɛ kɛ ha e mi ni komɛ a he ngmɛɛmi.
<<<P>>>
• A tsake To Nɛ A Pee Nɛ Maa Nu Ma A Sika He Ni Tsumi Blɔ Nɔ Tomi Ɔ Kɛ Da Si Ɔ “National Fiscal Stabilisation Levy (NFSL)” mi kɛ ba pee Ma Nɔ Yami He Bumi To “Growth and Sustainability Levy (GSL)” konɛ e su he fɛɛ he.
<<<P>>>
• A ma gbɔ Klama Nɔ Jua Yemi Susumi Tso ɔ nɔ kɛ je lafa mi gbami 1.5 kɛ ba da lafa mi gbami kake nɛ a ma je daa ligbi sika nɛ a po fɔ si nɛ a ma he nɔ to ɔ ngɛ blɔ mi.
<<<P>>>

o A maa jua Oslaba Kane He Wami Nɛ E He Wami aloo Ka su 5G “5G Electromagnetic Spectrum”;
<<<P>>>
o We Nɔ Tohi A Nɔ Womi Kɛ A Nɔ Yemi;
<<<P>>>
o Amlaalo ɔ maa ja e blɔ nya nɛ sa lɛ ɔ se ngɛ zu mi nutsua he ɔ nɛ ji Jubilee Field ɔ; nɛ
<<<P>>>
o A ma tsake sika nɛ ba amɛ a nɔ to ɔmɛ nɛ e je sika tsakemi si gbeemi nɛ a yo we ɔmɛ ngɛ blɔ mi konɛ a nyɛ nɛ a gbla nɔ nɛ a maa na amɛ kɛ ba.
