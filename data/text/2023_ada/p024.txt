31. Kɛ piɛɛ he ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ sɛ lohwe koklototsɛmɛ kaa na, jijɔ, apletsi kɛ ekpa komɛ nɛ a yibɔ su akpe 3,750 nɛ a gba kɛ ha lohwe lɛmi he kɛ huli 45 komɛ nɛ e ye bua ha mɛ nɛ a kɛ gbɔ a ni tsumi mi nyagba komɛ a nɔ konɛ a na he wami hulɔ kɛ tsu a blɔ nya ni tsumi ɔmɛ.
<<<P>>>
32. Ngɛ jeha 2023 mi ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ maa ye bua kɛ ma tso nɛ a bange a yiblii ɔmɛ a mi nɛ a hiɛmi maa pee kaa ayɔ 5.65 ɔ mi nɛ e wo mi sɔmi kɛ ha a dumi kɛ a he ni tsumi. A ma gba tso yiblii nɛ ɔmɛ nɛ ji atia tso, ayɔ aloo oku tso, agɔle ngmɛ tso, ta, mangoo tso kɛ palao hlɔɔba tso kɛ ha ngmɔ huli nɛ a yibɔ su akpe 175.
<<<P>>>
33. Ngɛ jeha 2022 mi ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ gbe ngmɔ nihi a tohe nyingmi ekpa kɛ enuɔ (65) nya. Blɔ nya tomi nɛ ɔ bli ngmɔ ni tohe ɔ mi nɛ e ngɔ 80,000mt kɛ ba piɛɛ nɔ nɛ ngɛ momo ɔmɛ a he. A pee enɛ ɔ ngɛ nɔ nɛ Amlaalo ɔ tsɛɔ ke Kpo Wayo Kake Ngmɔ Ni Tohe Kake ɔ sisi konɛ e wa kɛ tsi ngmɔ nihi a puɛmi ngɛ a kpami se ɔ nya bɔ nɛ pee ha nɛ ngmɔ huli ɔmɛ nɛ a na a ni tsumi ɔ he se.
<<<P>>>
34. Ngɛ jeha 2023 mi ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ maa gbe nyu gblami kɛ ha ngmɔ nihi a nɔ puemi ni tsumi kpɔ mi etɛne ɔ nya ngɛ Tamne. A ma tsa nɔ maa pee ngmɔ nihi a nɔ nyu puemi taku nɛ ɔ ekomɛ ngɛ Sunyeri, Tousal/Jeyiri, Tasundo kɛ Gilang nɛ a maa kpale tsa nɔ nɛ a maa pee he komɛ nɛ a ma nyɛ ma pue ngmɔ nihi a nɔ nyu puemi he ngɛ Vunania kɛ Gilang.

Adebɔ Zu Mi Zɔ Womi Nɔ He Gɔgɔ Fiami
<<<P>>>
35. Je ɔ tsuo mi haomi kɛ nyagba nɛ ba na zu mi zɔ womi nihi nɛ a kɛ klama pee ni tsumi ɔ, ha nɛ Amlaalo ɔ bli adebɔ zu mi zɔ womi nɔ peeli a he blɔ ɔ mi konɛ a nyɛ nɛ a pee fuu kɛ da nɔ nɛ klama pee ɔmɛ a nane mi nɛ e nyɛ nɛ e tsi zu mi zɔ womi ni a he hiami ɔ nya. Blɔ nya tomi nɛ ɔ ye bua kɛ fu ngmɔ ni bami nɛ e ye bua hulɔ kɛ gbɔ niye ni he hiami ɔ nɔ.
