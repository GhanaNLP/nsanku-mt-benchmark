“Ma a Sika He Blɔ Nya Tomi Susumi Tso Ɔmɛ Kpalemi Kɛ Ba Da A Glɛ Nɔ, A He Piɛ Pomi Kɛ Da Tɛ Kake Nɔ Kɛ Gu Blɔnya Tomi Kpakpahi A Nɔ”

ngɛ ma kpahi a nɔ, Amlaalo ɔ blɔ nɔ tomi komɛ a nɔ yami kɛ a si gbeemi kɛ hɛ mi mami ni komɛ nɛ Ghana Sika To He Ngua a pee nɛ kɔ ma a sika nɔ yami he.

Sisi Nine Womi Gbi Kplɛɛmi Womi: E ji gbi kplɛɛmi blɔ nɔ tomi womi pɔtɛɛ ko nɛ tsɔɔ nɔ ko nɔ kplɛɛmi ngɛ nihi nɔ aloo hiɛhiɛɛ a kpɛti.
<<<P>>>
Kpɔ Nya Kpɔ Nya: E ji kpɔ nya kpɔ nya munomunohi a kpɛti kake nɛ a ma nyɛ kɛ tsa aloo bla nɔ ko kɛ pee nɔ nɛ kle.
<<<P>>>
Sika He aloo Nɔ Mlaa: E ji sika nɔ mlaa nɛ ma ko sika nɔ hyɛmi he pee kɛ fɔ si nɛ a kɛ kpaka videhi a womi ngɛ be kpiti nɔ sika papɛɛ nɔ aloo sika nɛ a peeɔ kɛ ba a, nɔ da aloo vide konɛ a kɛ hyɛ nihi a jua he kake dami.
<<<P>>>
Ni Tsumi Tsɔwi a Blɔ Nya Tomi (MSBDP): Enɛ ɔ ji ni tsumi tsɔwi nɛ a ngɔɔ nimli bɔbɔyoo nɛ a ni tsumi se da a yɛ tsitsaa tsɔ.
<<<P>>>
Ni Tsumi Ha Yunivɛsiti Nyagbe li (NABCo): E ji blɔ nya tomi nɛ Ghana Amlaalo ɔ ngɔ kɛ fɔ si nɛ e kɛ hla ni tsumi kɛ ha nihi nɛ a gbe Yunivɛsiti nya konɛ e kɛ gbɔ ma mi himi nyagba komɛ a nɔ. Enɛ ɔ hu maa ye kɛ bua kɛ tsɔse mɛ aloo fiaa mɛ nane kɛ ha hɛ mi ga nihi a peemi nɛ e kɛ nyɛ wo aloo hue ma a sika he ni tsumi nɔ.
<<<P>>>
Ma a Ni Tsumi He nɛ Hyɛɛɔ Ni Tsumi Tsɔwi A Nɔ (NBSSI): Ni tsumi kuu nɛ ɔ, e ji ma a ni tsumi he ko nɛ hyɛɔ ni tsumi tsɔwi nɛ a di ni tsumi nɔ vide se aloo a hyɛ we ni tsumi mi vide ko blɔ. Ni tsumi kuu nɛ ɔ ngɛ Amlaalo Ni Tsumi He Ngua a Nɛ Hyɛɔ Jua Yemi kɛ Nihi a Peemi kuu ɔ sisi nɛ e blɔ nya ji e hyɛ ni tsɔwi nɛ ɔmɛ a wami kɛ a nɔ yami nɔ.
<<<P>>>
Ma a Mi Hue tsohi A He Piɛ Pomi Blɔ Nɔ Tomi (NAP): E ji je ɔ mi fɔmi peemi se kpalemi kɛ ba da e kla nɔ nɛ a kɛ dla hue tsohi nɛ a puɛ ɔmɛ kɛ a mi ni slɔɔto ɔmɛ kɛ gu ma bi a he ngɔmi kɛ mi womi nɔ, konɛ wa kɛ dla hi hehi nɛ hue tso bɔle mɛ ɔmɛ.
