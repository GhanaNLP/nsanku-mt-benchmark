12. Amlaalo ɔ sika he ni tsumi kulaa nɛ a bua nya a (hiɔhi nɛ piɛ womi kɛ hiɔhi nɛ a je glɛ nɔ) kɛ ha be nɛ a tsɔɔ ɔ yibɔ ya su sidi ayɔ 109,421 nɛ peeɔ lafa mi gbami 18.5 ngɛ GDP nɔ. Enɛ ɔ be blɔ hyɛmi yi mi tɛ nɛ a po fɔ si nɛ ji sidi ayɔ 103,992 nɛ peeɔ lafa mi gbami 17.6 ngɛ GDP nɔ ɔ he kɛ lafa mi gbami 5.2. Sika he ni tsumi blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ ya be nɔ nɛ wa hyɛ blɔ ɔ nɔ bɔɔ ejakaa ekomɛ ya wo hiɔ komɛ nɛ ji videhi nɛ a puɛ ngɛ hiɔ komɛ nɛ a sa womi ha Amlaalo ɔ kɔni komɛ a nɔ.
