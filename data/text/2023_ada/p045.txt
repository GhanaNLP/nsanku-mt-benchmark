“Ma a Sika He Blɔ Nya Tomi Susumi Tso Ɔmɛ Kpalemi Kɛ Ba Da A Glɛ Nɔ, A He Piɛ Pomi Kɛ Da Tɛ Kake Nɔ Kɛ Gu Blɔnya Tomi Kpakpahi A Nɔ”
<<<P>>>
Ma a Mi Adebɔ Nyagba kɛ Oslaa He Piɛ Pomi Ni Tsumi He ɔ (NADMO): E ji Amlaalo ɔ ni tsumi kɔni ko nɛ hyɛɔ adebɔ nyagbahi kɛ oslaahi nɛ baa ma a nɔ ɔmɛ.
<<<P>>>
Ma Sika Blɔ Nya Tomi Tutuutu Nɛ Zu Mi Nu Piɛɛ We He: Enɛ ɔ ji ma a sika blɔ nya tomi ɔ nɔ yami aloo yɛmi nɛ zu mi nu he ni tsumi nihi piɛɛ we he. Enɛ ɔmɛ hyɛɔ bɔ nɛ nihi a he jua wami aloo a jua si bami ngɛ ha.
<<<P>>>
Ma Sika He Blɔ Nya Tomi Nɔ Yami Tutuutu nɛ Zu Mi Nu Piɛɛ We He: E ji blɔ nɔ tomi ko nɛ a kɛ susuɔ aloo tsɔɔ daa jeha sika blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ nɔ yami ngɛ he fɛɛ he nɛ zu mi nu nɔ ko piɛɛ we he.
<<<P>>>
Sika Nɛ A Wui Nɔ To: Enɛ ɔ ji sika nɛ Amlaalo ɔ naa nɛ pi to sika. Ekomɛ ji hiɔhi nɛ a woɔ kɛ hlaa blɔ ko nya loo kɛ biɔ he blɔ nami womi, hiɔ nɛ a sa womi ngɛ Amlaalo ɔ ni komɛ a ngɔmi kɛ tsu ni he.
<<<P>>>
Hiɔ Nɛ Piɛ Womi: Hiɔ nɛ piɛ womi ji si womi hiɔhi nɛ ngɛ MDA kɛ Amlaalo ni tsumi he ngua nɛ hyɛɔ ma a sika blɔ nɔ tomi nɔ ɔ a kpɛti kɛ hiɔ kpa komɛ nɛ a sa womi.
<<<P>>>
Bɔ Nɛ E Ba Ha: Bɔ nɛ blɔ nɔ tomi komɛ kɛ ni komɛ a nya tomi ya nɔ ha.
<<<P>>>
Nɔ Da Oti (Primary balance): Nɔ da Oti ji sika nɛ piɛɛ ngɛ Amlaalo ɔ nihi nɛ e na kɛ nihi nɛ a pee nɛ mi womi sika ko be nɔ ɔ a kpɛti. (enɛ ɔ piɛɛ we hiɔ nɛ a ye womi he) a ma nyɛ maa da ma a weto ni nɛ a naa nɔ kɛ susu nɔ da nɛ baa ngɛ lafa mi gbami mi.
<<<P>>>
Si Gbeemi Oti (Primary surplus -deficit): E tsɔɔ si gbeemi aloo hiɔ nɛ ma a ye nɛ tsɔɔ Amlaalo ɔ sika he ni tsumi ngɛ amlɔ be nɛ ɔ mi nɛ sika nɛ baa Amlaalo ɔ ngɔ ɔ nɔ tohi nɛ videhi piɛɛ we he.
<<<P>>>
Ni Tsumi Nɔ Yami: Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ bɔ nɛ Ghana sika he ni tsumi maa ya nɔ ha ngɛ jeha ko mi.
<<<P>>>
Ma a Weto Ni Tutuutu: E ji ma ko weto ni nɛ a gbɔjɔ mi ngɛ nihi a he jua kuɔmi he.
<<<P>>>
Sika nɛ Baa Amlaalo ɔ dɛ: Enɛ ɔ ji sikahi tsuo nɛ Amlaalo ɔ naa kɛ jeɔ ma a kɛ mase ni tsumihi a blɔ nya tomi mi.
