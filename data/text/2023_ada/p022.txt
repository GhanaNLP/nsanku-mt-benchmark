Bɔjɛti Mi Sɔmi kɛ Hiɔ Womi Blɔ Nɔ Tomi Kɛ Ha 2023
<<<P>>>
24. Ke wa da sikahi nɛ wa po kɛ ha he ni tsumi kɛ he dooli a sika kɛ sika nɛ a je kluduu (hiɔhi nɛ piɛ a womi piɛɛ he), Bɔjɛti ɔ e mi se daimi kluduu ɔ ba pee hiɔ aloo si gbeemi nɛ e yibɔ ji ghana sika a sidi ayɔ 61,475 nɛ e daa si kɛ ha lafa mi gbami 7.7 ngɛ GDP nɔ. Se daimi pɔtɛɛ nɛ sa bami ngɛ sika mi ɔ ya pee hiɔ nɛ nya ya su Ghana sidi ɔ ayɔ 8,925 nɛ daa si kɛ ha lafa mi gbami 1.1 ngɛ GDP nɔ.

25. Wa ngɛ hyɛe kaa wa maa gu blɔ slɔɔtohi a nɔ kɛ wo si gbeemi aloo hiɔ nɛ ba nɛ ɔmɛ blɔ, nɛ blɔ nɛ ɔ ekomɛ ji mase aloo maje kɛ ma a nitsɛ e mi blɔ komɛ a nɔ. Blɔ ekpa komɛ a nɔ nɛ wa maa gu kɛ wo hiɔ nɛ ɔmɛ maa je huɛ bɔmi nɛ ngɛ waa kɛ mahi aloo ma komɛ a kpɛti ɔ nɔ nɛ e maa da IMF blɔ nya tomi ɔmɛ nɛ wa ngɛ a he ni sɛɛɛ ɔmɛ a nɔ kɛ blɔ titlii komɛ a nɔ nɛ wa maa gu kɛ to wa hiɔ ɔmɛ a he blɔ nya a nɔ.
