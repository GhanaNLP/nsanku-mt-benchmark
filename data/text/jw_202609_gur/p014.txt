<<<P>>>
YUUNƐ 84
To Wan Soŋɛ
<<<P>>>
YELEKPINE
To wan zamesɛ baŋɛ Ze-karia 8:23 n tuni tuuma se’em, la Yehowa nɛreba n iti se’em ta’an mɔɔla yelesoma la bɔ’ɔra nɛreba yetɔɣum woo poan.
<<<P>>>
“Boraahe pia wan yehe teŋa wa zuo buuri woo poan. [Ba] han nyɛ Yuda teŋa nɛra, ba wan gin a fuo vɛ’ɛra.”—ZEK. 8:23.
<<<P>>>
YEHOWA yuun burum nɛresɛba n yuun eŋɛ kpenkpasi la en ge tiregɛ ti ba mɛ Babel Dewoko la yetɔɣum mɛ. Bala la, nɛreba yuun pɔsɛ tɔɣera la yetɔɣum buuri buuri ge me yiregɛ tiŋgɔŋɔ wa zɛ’an woo. (Pin. 11:6-9) Ge Yehowa puti’irɛ dela a layesɛ “nɛrekooŋɔ . . . [n yese] nɛreba woo poan, la buuri woo, la teŋhe woo, la yetɔgom woo poan” ti ba pu’usa en. A me boti la a soŋɛ ba ti ba nyɛ fãarɛ mɛlesekãtɛ la poan ta’an ta bɔna tinpaalega la poan. (Lil. 7:9, 10, 14) Ge la wan eŋɛ la wani ti nɛreba n tɔɣeri yetɔɣum buuri buuri ta’an ta tara sakerɛ bɔ’ɔra Yehowa mɛlesekãtɛ la poan? La dela to mɔɔlɛ yelesoma la bo ba, bamea yetɔɣum poan. (Kaalɛ Arom 10:13, 14.) La dela yɔ’ɔ bɔ’ɔra tɔ ti to mɔɔlɛ yelesoma la bo nɛreba ti ba ta’an nyɛ fãarɛ.
