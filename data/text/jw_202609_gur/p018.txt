12 Nɛreba kɔ’ɔn lɛregera Baabule la mɛ
yuuma zo’e zo’e n wana.* Leregeredoma
kɔ’ɔn tiregera mɛ ti ba lerege Baabule la,
sɛla n sɔi la ba boti la nɛreba ta’an kaa-
la Naayinɛ yelesum la bamea yetɔyum
poan. Nananawa, to za’a waabi ta’an kaa-
lɛ Baabule la yetɔyesebo poan ti to wan
ta’an bɔkɛ. Ba lerege New World Transla-
tion of the Holy Scriptures la yetɔyum zo’e
zo’e poan. Bala soŋɛ Yehowa Sɛɛradoma
n boi tiŋgɔŋɔ wa poan ti ba zamesɛ yele-
mɛŋɛrɛ la Baabule la poan. Geele kee-
ma ayima n ze’ele Nepal tiŋa n tɔɣɛ New
World Translation la ti ba lerege amea ye-
tɔyum poan se’em. A yeti: “Mam san kaa-
la Baabule la n mea yetɔyum poan, la ni
ana mam wuu Yehowa n tɔɣeri bɔ’ɔra
mam. Bala soŋɛ mam mɛ ti n ana du’a
du’a la Yehowa ge me basɛ ti n sake-
rɛ kpe’em.” To tari New World Transla-
tion wa za’a bii ku bɔba ase’a yetɔyum n
gaŋɛ 300 poan. Baabule wa kɔ’ɔn makɛ
la ma’a. Ku bɔkerɛ me ka tɔi ge me basɛ ti
to bɔkera Baabule yelemɛŋɛrɛ la. Bala n
sɔi ti Yehowa Sɛɛradoma n boi tiŋgɔŋɔ
wa za’a poan sakerɛ yɛla, la ba pa’alegɔ
dɛna buyina la. (1 Ko. 1:10) Ge yɛla ase’a
me n basɛ ti New World Translation la di
mi’a paa. Bem yɛla n bala?

13 Naayinɛ yu’urɛ la ka boi Baabula
zo’e zo’e poan zina beere wa. Ge Naayi-
nɛ yu’urɛ la boi New World Translation la
poan mɛ sugumnɔɔrɛ zo’e zo’e. La paɛ
wuu yuun 100 n tole, Yehowa yu’urɛ la
yuun boi Baabula zo’e zo’e poan mɛ yele
yele wuu Hebru Gulesegɔ la. Ge pu’use-
gɔ nɛŋadoma n boi zina beere wa n yeti
bamam dela Kristakɔma la, ka tari Yeho-
wa yu’urɛ la tuna tuuma ge me ka bɔta ti
nɛreba mina dɛ. Makerɛ, Katolik Pu’u-
segɔ nɛŋadoma yese Naayinɛ yu’urɛ ba
Baabula, la ba gɔnɔ* poan mɛ. Pu’use-
gɔdoma basɛba me yese Naayinɛ yu’urɛ
la mɛ ba Baabula poan. Ge ban lerege
New World Translation la yetɔyesebo woo
poan la, Naayinɛ yu’urɛ la boi bim mɛ su-
gumnɔɔrɛ zo’e zo’e wuu ban yuun gulesɛ
Baabule la ti la ana se’em la. Beni n sɔi
ti la yele pakɛ ti nɛreba mina Naayinɛ
yu’urɛ?
<<<P>>>
14 Baabule la pa’alɛ ti la yele pakɛ mɛ ti
to mina Naayinɛ yu’urɛ la ge tara de tuna
tuuma. Yuumadaana la yele Naayinɛ nɛ-
reba yeti, “pɛngɛ [ya] Na’ayenɛ yu’urɛ
la.” (Ym. 135:1) Yezu n yuun pa’ali a pɔ-
dɔleba la ban wan zusa Yehowa se’em la,
a yuun yele ba ti ba ni yeti, “Ho yu’urɛ
nara pɛnka.” Pooren, se’em ma’a ti Yezu
zusa bɔ’ɔra a Sɔ la, a yuun yeti, “N bahɛ
te ba mina” ho yu’urɛ la mɛ. (Mt. 6:9;
Yon 17:26) Nɔtɔ’ɔsa Yoel la tuntuna Paul
me yuun gulesɛ yeti, “Se’em woo n [yi’i-
ri] Na’ayenɛ yu’urɛ la wan nyɛ faanrɛ.”
(Arom 10:13) Leregeredoma n yese Naa-
yinɛ yu’urɛ la Baabula poan la zuo, nɛre-
