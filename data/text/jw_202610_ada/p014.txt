Hɛ Mi Yami Ngɛ Yi Mi Wami Tsuo Se
<<<P>>>
E ngɛ mi kaa benɛ je mi ta enyɔne ɔ ngɛ nɔ yae ɔ, a wa Yehowa Odasefo ɔmɛ yi mi wawɛɛ mohu lɛɛ, se a yibɔ ya hɛ mi wawɛɛ nitsɛ. Nazi tabuli ɔmɛ a kpɛti nɔ kake de ke: “Ke o ngɛ Yehowa Odasefo ɔmɛ yi mi wae ɔ, kɛkɛ a yibɔ ngɛ hɛ mi yae mohu.”
<<<P>>>
Mo susu nɔ́ nɛ ya nɔ ngɛ Netherlands ngɛ jamɛ a be ɔ mi ɔ he nɛ o hyɛ. Jeha 1986 Yearbook ɔ bɔ amaniɛ ke: “E ngɛ mi kaa a nuu Yehowa Odasefo ɔmɛ, a wo mɛ tsu, a kɛ mɛ ho nya tsimi hehi ya, nɛ a gbe a ti ni komɛ nɛ a gbo mohu lɛɛ, se ngɛ jeha enyɔ se ɔ, fiɛɛli nɛ a ngɛ jamɛ a ma a mi ɔ a yibɔ ɔ bɔ he si enyɔ kɛ se. Ngɛ jeha 1943 ɔ mi ɔ, fiɛɛli nɛ a ngɛ Netherlands a yibɔ ɔ ya hɛ mi kɛ ya su 1,379, nɛ enɛ ɔ ji yibɔ nɛ ya hɛ mi pe kulaa nɛ a ná ngɛ jamɛ a be ɔ mi. . . . E ngɛ heii kaa Yehowa jɔɔ e we bi nɛ a yibɔ ɔ ya hɛ mi ngɛ ka nɛ nya wa wawɛɛ nɛ a kɛ kpe ɔ tsuo se.”
<<<P>>>
se nyɛɛli nɛ a pee klaalo kaa a maa na nɔ́, aloo a ma gbo po ngɛ hemi kɛ yemi nɛ a ngɛ ngɛ Yesu mi ɔ he je. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, Yesu de kaa a maa ngɔ e se nyɛɛli ɔmɛ kɛ ba amlaalohi kɛ matsɛmɛ a hɛ mi. (Mat. 10:17, 18) E bɔ e se nyɛɛli ɔmɛ kɔkɔ hu kaa jami hɛ mi nyɛɛli maa te si kɛ wo mɛ. (Yoh. 16:2) Jehanɛ hu ɔ, Yesu de kaa anɔkuale jami ma ha nɛ mi gbami maa ba weku mi bimɛ komɛ a kpɛti.—Mat. 10:34-36.

6 Kaa bɔ nɛ Yesu gba kɛ fɔ si ɔ, nihi babauu wa Yesu kaseli ɔmɛ yi mi bɔ nɛ pee nɛ a kpa e se nyɛɛmi. Benɛ Yesu kpale kɛ ho hiɔwe ya be bɔɔ se ɔ, Yuda jami hɛ mi nyɛɛli ɔmɛ bɔ mɔde kaa a maa tsi Yesu kaseli ɔmɛ a nya konɛ a kpa fiɛɛmi. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, a nuu e kaseli ɔmɛ nɛ a kɛ mɛ ba Sanhedrin ɔ, nɛ ji Yudabi ɔmɛ a kojomi he ngua a hɛ mi. Jami hɛ mi nyɛɛli nɛ ɔmɛ suɔ nɛ a kpata kaseli ɔmɛ a he mi. Se Farisi bi ɔmɛ a kpɛti nɔ kake nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Gamaliel ɔ bɔ mɛ kɔkɔ ke: “Nyɛ ko pee nyumu nɛ ɔmɛ nɔ́ ko, mohu ɔ, nyɛɛ ngmɛɛ a he. Ejakaa ke yi mi tomi nɛ ɔ loo ní tsumi nɛ ɔ je adesahi a ngɔ ɔ, e maa gu; se ke Mawu ngɔ lɛ e je ɔ, lɛɛ nyɛ be nyɛe maa gu lɛ. Ke nyɛ pee kaa nyɛ maa gu lɛ ɔ, lɛɛ e ma nyɛ maa ba po kaa nyɛɛ kɛ Mawu nitsɛ lɛ ngɛ hwue ɔ nɛ.” (Níts. 5:33, 34, 38, 39) Munyu nɛ Gamaliel tu ɔ ba mi niinɛ. Benɛ a ngmɛɛ kaseli ɔmɛ a he ɔ, jami hɛ mi nyɛɛli ɔmɛ ya nɔ nɛ a te si kɛ wo mɛ. Se a nyɛ we nɛ a ye kaseli ɔmɛ a nɔ kunimi, mohu ɔ, nihi babauu ba kplɛɛ anɔkuale ɔ nɔ, nɛ a ba pee Kristofohi. (Níts. 6:7) Mɛni he je nɛ a nyɛ we nɛ a ye a nɔ kunimi ɔ? Ejakaa Yehowa fĩ e we bi ɔmɛ a se, nɛ a yibɔ ya hɛ mi ngɛ si temi kɛ womi tsuo se.—Níts. 1:8.

7 Mwɔnɛ ɔ, nimli akpehi abɔ kɛ bua jɔmi ngɛ Baiblo ɔ kasee nɛ a ba ngɛ Yehowa sɔmɔe ngɛ si temi kɛ womi kɛ yi mi wami nɛ a wekuli, nihi nɛ a kɛ mɛ tsuɔ ní, kɛ ni kpahi kɛ ngɛ a nɔ bae ɔ tsuo se. Mɛni he je nɛ kaseli ehe nɛ ɔmɛ fia a pɛɛ si kaa a ma “wo [a] sɛumi tso ɔ”? (Mat. 16:24) Ejakaa a suɔ Yehowa nɛ a ngɛ hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi ngɛ e mi kaa e maa hyɛ a nɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, Yehowa hu ha mɛ “he wami nɛ pe adesahi a nɔ́ ɔ” konɛ a nyɛ nɛ a fĩ si. (2 Kor. 4:7-9) Ke a wekuli te si kɛ wo mɛ ɔ, a naa kaa munyu nɛ la polɔ ɔ tu ɔ ngɛ mi bae ngɛ a blɔ fa mi.
