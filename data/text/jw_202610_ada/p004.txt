Otsi etɛ se, ngɛ July nyɔhiɔ ɔ mi ɔ, Nyɛminyumu Edwin kɛ George Wright, nɛ lɛ hu e kɛ kã fiɛɛɔ ɔ ngɔ mele nɛ a ho India ya. Nyɛminyumu Edwin sɔmɔ kaa asafo ɔ ní tsumi kɔni ɔ sɔmɔlɔ, nɛ e hyɛɔ fiɛɛmi ní tsumi ɔ nɛ a tsuɔ ngɛ Afghanistan, Burma (Myanmar), Ceylon (Sri Lanka), India, kɛ Persia (Iran) a nɔ. Nyɛminyumu Edwin hi India jeha 64 kɛ ya si benɛ e gbe e zugba a nɔ sɔmɔmi ní tsumi ɔ nya ngɛ jeha 1990 ɔ mi.

NYƐMIMƐ NYUMUHI NƐ A YA SLAA KUUHI NƐ NYƐMIMƐ ƆMƐ NGƐ MI Ɔ NYƐƐ FIƐƐMI NÍ TSUMI Ɔ HƐ MI
<<<P>>>
Kɛ je maa pee jeha 1894 ɔ, asafo ɔ tsɔɔ nyumuhi komɛ nɛ a ya slaa kuu komɛ nɛ Baiblo Kaseli ɔmɛ ngɛ mi ɔ. Ngɛ July 1926 mi ɔ, nyumu nɛ ɔmɛ nɛ a tsɛɔ mɛ ke pilgrims (blɔ hiali) ɔ, a ní tsumi ɔ tsake. Jinɛ sisije ɔ, a kɛ ligbi kake lɛ slaa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ, se jehanɛ lɛɛ, a kɛ ligbi enyɔ lɛ maa slaa mɛ, nɛ a hyɛɔ blɔ hu kaa a maa ngɔ ngmlɛfia 100 kɛ fiɛɛ daa nyɔhiɔ.
<<<P>>>
Nyɛminyumu George Draper nɛ e ji blɔ hiali ɔmɛ a kpɛti nɔ kake ɔ ngma sɛ womi ko kɛ ya ha asafo ɔ. E tsɔɔ kaa e sa nɛ nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ tsuo, nɛ mɛ blɔ hiali ɔmɛ hu a piɛɛ he ɔ nɛ a pee klaalo kaa a maa bɔ nihi kɔkɔ. Se loko a ma nyɛ maa pee jã a, ja a kase Hwɔɔmi Mɔ ɔ saminya. Nyɛminyumu Draper gbe e zugba a nɔ sɔmɔmi ní tsumi ɔ nya ngɛ jeha 1938 ɔ mi.

KPE KO NƐ A PEE NGƐ NEW YORK CITY
<<<P>>>
A pee nyagbe kpe ngua ko ngɛ jeha a mi ngɛ October 14-18, 1926 ngɛ New York City. Adafi fiami womi peemi he nguahi nɛ a ngɛ ma a mi ɔ
<<<P>>>
kplɛɛ nɔ kaa a maa fia Nyɛminyumu Rutherford magbɛ nɔ munyu nɛ e yi ji, “Earth’s Greatest Conflict Near” ɔ he adafi ngɛ a womi ɔmɛ a mi. Se benɛ e piɛ bɔɔ nɛ kpe ɔ maa je sisi ɔ, adafi fiami he nɛ ɔmɛ hiɛhiɛɛ kua kaa a maa fia kpe ɔ he adafi.
<<<P>>>
Nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ ma nya si kaa a ma ha nɛ nihi nɛ a le nɔ́ nɛ adafi fiami womi ɔmɛ pee ɔ. Ngɛ Soha, October 15 ɔ nɔ ɔ, R. J. Martin je dɛ womi ehe ko nɛ e yi ji, Against the People ɔ kpo. Dɛ womi nɛ ɔ tu yiwutso ní nɛ adafi fiami womi ɔmɛ pee ɔ he munyu. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, e fia munyu nɛ Nyɛminyumu Rutherford maa tu ɔ he adafi. Nyɛminyumu Martin de ke: “Wɔ nitsɛmɛ waa kɛ wa womi fiami klamahi bɔni wa womi ɔmɛ fiami, nɛ wa nyɛmimɛ nɛ a tsuɔ ní ngɛ Betel ɔ maa bɔ mɔde wawɛɛ nɛ a maa fia dɛ womi nɛ ɔ kakaaka 1,250,000, nɛ lɔ ɔ se ɔ, wa ma de nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ nɛ a ba kpe ɔ kaa a ya gba dɛ womi nɛ ɔ kɛ ha nihi nɛ a ngɛ ma a mi ɔ.” Benɛ a kpa kpe ɔ Soha a, nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ bɔni dɛ womi ɔ gba mi, nɛ benɛ e ke suu gbɔkuɛ ɔ, a gba dɛ womi ɔ kakaaka 300,000.
<<<P>>>
Loko Hɔgba ke suu ɔ, a gba dɛ womi ɔ maa pee ayɔ kake sɔuu! Jehanɛ hu ɔ, nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ kɛ kã gba Deliverance womi ɔ kakaaka 3,783 kɛ womiyo kpahi kakaaka 35,140.
<<<P>>>
Ngɛ Hɔgba piani ngmlɛ 3, ngɛ October 17 ɔ nɔ ɔ, nihi maa pee 12,000 bu Nyɛminyumu Rutherford magbɛ nɔ munyu ɔ tue. Benɛ Nyɛminyumu Rutherford tu e munyu ɔ ta a, nihi nɛ a ba bu e munyu ɔ tue, nɛ a bua jɔ he ɔ suɔ nɛ a le babauu kɛ kɔ e munyu ɔ he. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, a gba womihi nɛ hiɛ pe akpe nɛɛ ngɛ kpe ɔ sisi.
