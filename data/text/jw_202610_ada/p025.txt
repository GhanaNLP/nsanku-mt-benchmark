BAIBLO MI NƆ HYƐMI NÓ NƐ WOƆ NƆ HE WAMI
<<<P>>>
4 Ngmami nɛ a ngɔ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ kɛ da nɔ ɔ ha nɛ wa na kaa Beroyabi ɔmɛ nɛ bɔfo Paulo fiɛɛ kɛ ha mɛ ɔ suɔ nɛ a kase Baiblo ɔ mi anɔkuale ɔ. (Kane Ní Tsumi 17:10, 11.) Pi nɛ Beroyabi ɔmɛ ngɛ Paulo tue bue konɛ a te si kɛ wo lɛ, mohu ɔ, a “pɛtɛ munyu ɔ he” nɛ a suɔ nɛ a kase babauu hulɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, Baiblo ɔ de ngɛ a he kaa “a susuɔ níhi a he kɛ yaa tsitsaa.” Akɛnɛ a ngɔ nó nɛ Paulo ngɛ mɛ tsɔɔe ɔ kɛ to nó nɛ a kase ngɛ Ngmami ɔ mi ɔ he he je ɔ, a yɔse kaa nó nɛ Paulo ngɛ mɛ tsɔɔe ɔ ji anɔkuale. Wɔ hu wa ngɛ nɔ mi mami kaa níhi nɛ a tsɔɔ wɔ ngɛ asafo mi kpehi, kpɔ mi kpehi kɛ kpokpa nɔ kpehi a sisi ɔ ji anɔkuale, ejakaa wa bliɔ wa Baiblo ɔ mi nɛ wa nyɛɛɔ se ngɛ mi ke munyu tulɔ ɔ ngɛ ngmami ko kanee.
<<<P>>>
5 Beroyabi ɔmɛ “a ti nihi babauu ba he ye.” (Níts. 17:12) Se e kɛ we kulaa nɛ Yudabi komɛ nɛ a wa Paulo yi mi ngɛ Tesalonika a ba Beroya nɛ a ba de ní yayahi kɛ kɔ Paulo he. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, nihi babauu nɛ a ngɛ ma a mi ɔ bɔni Kristofo ɔmɛ yi mi wami. (Níts. 17:13) Se Kristofo nɛ ɔmɛ yi gbeye. Akɛnɛ a kaseɔ Ngmami ɔ daa ligbi he je ɔ, a ná nɔ mi mami kaa a na anɔkuale ɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, a nyɛ nɛ a fĩ si kpɛii ngɛ anɔkuale ɔ mi. Ke wɔ hu wa kaseɔ Mawu Munyu ɔ daa ligbi ɔ, e maa wo wɔ he wami konɛ waa da nyagbahi nɛ waa kɛ kpeɔ daa ligbi ngɛ si himi mi ɔ nya.

Baiblo Mi Nɔ Hyɛmi Níhi Nɛ Kɔɔ Mawu Nɛ A Ma Ja Lɛ Daa Ligbi ɔ He
<<<P>>>
• Mose Mlaa biɔ nɛ a sã afɔlehi daa ligbi.—2 Mose 29:38-42; 30:7, 8.
<<<P>>>
• Yehowa fã Yoshua kaa e kane Mlaa “nyɔ kɛ pia.”—Yosh. 1:8.
<<<P>>>
• Yehowa fã matsɛmɛ nɛ a ngɛ Israel ɔ kaa a ngma Mlaa a mi munyu ɔmɛ eko kɛ ha a he nɛ a kane daa ligbi.—5 Mose 17:18-20.
<<<P>>>
• La polɔ ɔ wo wɔ he wami kaa wa bua nɛ jɔ Mawu Munyu ɔ kanemi he nɛ wa pue wa yi mi tɛ ngɛ he “nyɔ kɛ pia.”—La 1:1-3.
<<<P>>>
• Daniel sɔleɔ si etɛ ngɛ ligbi ɔ mi.—Dan. 6:10.
<<<P>>>
• Kpojemi womi ɔ tu asafo kuu ngua nɛ e mi bimɛ ngɛ Mawu sɔmɔe ngɛ blɔ klɔuu nɔ “nyɔ kɛ pia” a he munyu.—Kpo. 7:15.
<<<P>>>
Yehowa kɛ e sɔmɔmi nɔ. Akɛnɛ Yehowa ji wa Mawu he je ɔ, e sa nɛ wa sɔmɔ lɛ daa ligbi. E suɔ wɔ, nɛ e suɔ nɛ wa ná neneene wami. (Yoh. 3:16) Baiblo mi nɔ hyɛmi níhi ha nɛ wa na kaa, ke wa sɔmɔɔ Yehowa daa ligbi ɔ, e bua jɔɔ. Nyɛmimɛ fuu yɔse kaa ke a susu ngmami ɔ he daa ligbi ɔ, lɔ ɔ haa nɛ a ngɔɔ be ɔ eko kɛ sɔleɔ, a kaneo Baiblo ɔ, nɛ a pueɔ a yi mi tɛ ngɛ nó nɛ a kase ɔ he hulɔ.
