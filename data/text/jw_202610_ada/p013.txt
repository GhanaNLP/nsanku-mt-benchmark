Jeha 1914
Maa pee 5,000
<<<P>>>
mi ɔ a yibɔ pɛ ji 5,000. Se kɛ je jamɛ a be ɔ mi kɛ ma nɛ ɔ, “nɔ nyafii ɔ” nɛ Yesaya tu he munyu ɔ ba hiɛ pe nimli ayɔ nɛɛ. Mahi nɛ hiɛ pe 130 ngɛ mwɔnɛ ɔ nɛ nimli nɛ a ngɛ eko fɛɛ eko mi ɔ a yibɔ sui ayɔ nɛɛ. Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa nihi nɛ a ngɛ Yehowa sɔmɔe mwɔnɛ ɔ a he hiɛ pe nihi nɛ a ngɛ ma komɛ a mi ɔ. Nihi babauu ba le Yehowa nɛ a je suɔmi mi kɛ ngɛ lɛ sɔmɔe ngɛ mahi nɛ hiɛ pe 240 a nɔ. E ngɛ mi kaa jami komɛ ngɛ nɛ a mi bimɛ hiɛ pe Yehowa Odasefohi mohu lɛɛ, se e ngɛ heii kaa Yesaya gbami nɛ ngɛ Yesaya 60:22 nɛ kɔɔ Yehowa we bi a he ɔ ba mi ngɛ wa blɔ fa mi. Niinɛ, “nɔ nyafii ɔ” ba pee “nimli akpe.”
<<<P>>>
4 Jami fuu ngɛ nɛ a mi bimɛ hiɛ babauu ejakaa a baptisiɔ bimwɔwi, se wɔɔ lɛɛ wa pee we jã. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, wa ngmɛ́ blɔ konɛ nihi nɛ a sume nɛ a kɛ Mawu je mi bami he mlaahi nɛ tsu ní ɔ nɛ a hi wa kpɛti bɔ nɛ pee nɛ wa he nɛ hiɛ kɛkɛ, aloo nɛ wa maa tsɔɔ nihi kaa e he hia we nɛ a kɛ Mawu mlaahi nɛ tsu ní kɛ pi si.
<<<P>>>
Mohu ɔ, wa kpɛti nɔ fɛɛ nɔ kase Baiblo ɔ, nɛ wa bɔ mɔde nɛ wa pee tsakemihi ngɛ wa je mi bami mi, konɛ wa je mi bami kɛ níhi nɛ Baiblo ɔ tsɔɔ ɔ nɛ kɔ. Ngɛ wa kpɛti nihi fuu wa blɔ fa mi ɔ, enɛ ɔ ba biɔ nɛ waa pee tsakemi nguahi ngɛ wa susumi kɛ wa ní peepee mi. (1 Kor. 6:9-11) Benɛ wa nyɛ nɛ wa pee tsakemi nɛ ɔmɛ ɔ, wa he su kɛ ha fiɛɛmi yami, nɛ amlɔ nɛ ɔ, wa ngɛ sane kpakpa a fiɛɛe kɛ ngɛ ni kpahi hae konɛ mɛ hu a ba piɛɛ wa he kɛ sɔmɔ Yehowa. Be bɔɔ se ɔ, wa jɔɔ wa he nɔ kɛ ha Yehowa, nɛ wa wo lɛ si kaa wa maa pee lɛ pɛ e suɔmi nya ní ngɛ si fɔfɔɛ fɛɛ ko mi, nɛ lɔ ɔ se ɔ, a baptisi wɔ.—Mat. 28:19, 20.
