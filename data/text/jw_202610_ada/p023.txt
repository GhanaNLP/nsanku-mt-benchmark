20 Huɛmɛ kpakpahi ji nike ní nɛ je Yehowa ngɔ, nɛ wa hia mɛ wawɛɛ nitsɛ amlɔ nɛ ɔ pe be ko nɛ be ɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, kaa bɔ nɛ Daniel pee ɔ, mo hu o kɛ nihi nɛ pee huɛ. Moo hla nihi nɛ a peeɔ a ní kaa Yeremia, nɛ́ a ma nyɛ maa ye bua mo, nɛ́ a maa wo mo he wami ngɛ o we asafo ɔ mi ɔ nɛ o kɛ mɛ nɛ bɔ. O kɛ nihi nɛ a maa ye bua mo konɛ o ya nɔ nɛ o ye Yehowa anɔkua-le ɔ nɛ bɔ gbagbanii. Moo je he si bami mi nɛ o de ni kpahi nɛ a sɔle kɛ ha mo ke e he ba hia. Nɔ́ nɛ pe kulaa a, mo kai kaa Yehowa ji o Huɛ nɛ se be, nɛ e be mo kuae kɔkɔɔkɔ. (Heb. 13:5) Ke wɔ tsuo waa kɛ Yehowa pee wa huɛ ɔ, wa ma nyɛ maa ye bua wa sibi konɛ waa ya nɔ nɛ waa ye lɛ anɔkuale, nɛ wa huɛ bɔmi ɔ maa hi si kɛ ya neneene.

FONI Ɔ MI TSƆƆMI
Womi ɔ hɛ mi foni: Daniel kɛ e huɛmɛ ɔmɛ ngɛ a sibi ye buae konɛ a nyɛ nɛ a da ka nɛ a kɛ kpe ɔ nya, nɛ a ya nɔ nɛ a ye anɔkuale.

Yehowa Ji Wa Tsɛ,
Wa Mawu Kɛ Wa Huɛ
<<<P>>>
Anɛ e he waa ha mo kaa o maa na Yehowa kaa Tsɛ loo Nɔ Ko nɛ o ma nyɛ maa ngɔ o hɛ kɛ fɔ e nɔ lo? Ke jã a, lɛɛ moo bɔ mɔde kaa o maa pee níhi nɛ nyɛɛ se nɛ ɔmɛ daa ligbi konɛ o nyɛ nɛ o hɛ kɛ su Yehowa he.
<<<P>>>
• Moo sɔle: O kɛ Yehowa nɛ sɛɛ ní ngɛ sɔlemi mi, ejakaa e suɔ nɛ e maa bu mo tue.
<<<P>>>
• Moo kase Yehowa he ní: Moo bu Yehowa tue kɛ gu Baiblo ɔ nɛ o maa kane nɛ o maa kase ɔ nɔ. Moo kase Yehowa he ní babauu konɛ o le nɔ nɛ e ji, suhi nɛ e ngɛ, kɛ bɔ nɛ e peeɔ e ní ha.
<<<P>>>
• Moo to o tsui si: Mo kai kaa e ma he be loko o ma nyɛ ma ná hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi ngɛ nɔ ko mi, nɛ o kɛ lɛ maa pee huɛ. O ma nyɛ ma ná nɔ mi mami kaa Yehowa suɔ nɛ e kɛ mo nɛ pee huɛ.
<<<P>>>
Ke o suɔ nɛ o kase babauu ɔ, hyɛ munyu nɛ ji “Mɛni Ma Pee Konɛ Ma Nyɛ Nɛ Ma Pee Mawu Huɛ?” nɛ ngɛ jw.org ɔ nɔ ɔ.
