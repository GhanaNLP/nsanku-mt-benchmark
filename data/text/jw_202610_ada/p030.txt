Mo Ha Nɛ O Hemi Kɛ Yemi ɔ Mi Nɛ Wa
<<<P>>>
BENƐ Yehowa Odasefo ɔmɛ kɛ mo ngɛ Baiblo ɔ kasee ɔ, mɛni lɛ ha nɛ o ná nɔ mi mami kaa o na “hemi kɛ yemi kake” ɔ, nɛ ji blɔ nɛ Yehowa suɔ kaa a gu nɔ kɛ ja lɛ ɔ? (Efe. 4:4-6) Anɛ o he kɛ su Yehowa we bi a he akɛnɛ o yɔse kaa suɔmi ngɛ a kpɛti ɔ he je lo? Aloo o bua jɔ a he akɛnɛ a baa a je mi saminya a he je lo? Ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ, nihi babauu suɔ kaa a kɛ Yehowa asafo ɔ maa bɔ akɛ-nɛ wa haa nɛ a nuɔ he kaa a ngɛ slɔkee, nɛ wa jeɔ suɔmi nitsɛnitsɛ kpo kɛ tsɔɔ mɛ ɔ he je, nɛ nii-nɛ hu ɔ, jã nɛ e sa kaa e hi. Ejakaa suɔmi nɛ wa jeɔ kpo, kɛ wa je mi bami kpakpa a ji nɔ́ nɛ kadiɔ wɔ kaa anɔkuale Kristofohi.—Yoh. 13:35; 1 Pet. 2:12.
<<<P>>>
Se ngɔɔ lɛ kaa nyɛmi ko tɔ̃ o nɔ, aloo e je we suɔmi kpo kɛ tsɔɔ mo ngɛ blɔ ko nɔ. Aloo nyɛmi ko pee yayami ko nɛ hɛdɔ ngɛ he kaa nɔ́ nɛ ya nɔ ngɛ Korinto asafo ɔ mi nɛ bɔfo Paulo tu he munyu ɔ hu nɛɛ? (1 Kor. 5:1) Ke hemi kɛ yemi nɛ o ngɛ ɔ daa si ngɛ bɔ nɛ ni kpahi a ní peepee ha a nɛ o nuɔ he ɔ pɛ nɔ ɔ, lɛɛ lɔ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa o hemi kɛ yemi ɔ be gbu-gbuugbu. Ke jã a, mɛni maa ye bua mo konɛ o fia o hemi kɛ yemi ɔ kɛ ma sisi tomi nɛ he wa nɔ?

MƐNI MAA YE BUA MO KONƐ O HEMI KƐ YEMI Ɔ MI NƐ WA?
<<<P>>>
Ke o ya nɔ nɛ o susu Baiblo ɔ sisije tsɔɔmi ɔmɛ nɛ o kase ɔ he, nɛ o ná nɔ mi mami nɛ mi wa kaa a ji anɔkuale ɔ, lɔ ɔ maa wo o hemi kɛ yemi ɔ mi he wami wawɛɛ nitsɛ. Enɛ ɔ he nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ lɛ nyɛɛ se ɔ nɛ.
<<<P>>>
Be ko ɔ, Yesu se nyɛɛli ɔmɛ a kpɛti nihi fuu kpa e se nyɛɛmi. Nɔ́ nɛ Yesu de kaa e sa nɛ a ye e he lo ɔ, nɛ a nu e muɔ ɔ ha nɛ a nane tɔ̃tɔ̃, nɛ a de ke: “Pi munyu nɛ a nuɔ kɛ tue ji nɛ ɔ.” Nɔ́ nɛ Yesu de ɔ gba a nya wawɛɛ, enɛ ɔ he ɔ, a kpa e se nyɛɛmi.—Yoh. 6:56-60, 66.
<<<P>>>
Lɔ ɔ se ɔ, Yesu bi kaseli nyɔngma kɛ enyɔ ɔmɛ ke: “Anɛ nyɛ hu nyɛ suɔ kaa nyɛ maa je lo?” Simon Petro ha lɛ heto ke: “Nyɔmtsɛ, mɛnɔ ngɔ lɛ wa maa je kɛ ya? Mo lɛ o ngɛ neneene wami mu-nyu ɔmɛ ɔ nɛ. Wa he ye, nɛ wa ba le kaa mo ji Mawu we Nɔ Klɔuklɔu ɔ nɛ.”—Yoh. 6:67-69.
<<<P>>>
Mɛni lɛ ha nɛ Petro kɛ bɔfo kpa amɛ je ekpa ngɛ nihi nɛ a nane tɔ̃tɔ̃ ɔ a he ɔ? Petro kɛ bɔfo kpa amɛ fia a hemi kɛ yemi ɔ kɛ ma sisi tomi nɛ he wa nɔ, se pi a he numi nɔ. Petro de ke: “Wa he ye, nɛ wa ba le kaa mo ji Mawu we Nɔ Klɔuklɔu ɔ nɛ.” Nyumuhi anɔkualetsɛmɛ nɛ ɔmɛ le nɔ́ nɛ a he ye ɔ, kɛ nɔ́ nɛ he je nɛ a he ye ɔ. Akɛnɛ a ngɔ Yesu ní peepee kɛ e munyu tutui kɛ to níhi nɛ a kase ngɛ Ngmami ɔmɛ a mi ɔ he he je ɔ, a ngɛ nɔ mi mami kaa Yesu ji Mesia a nɛ. Lɔ ɔ he je nɛ a hemi kɛ yemi ɔ ngɛ gbugbuugbu, nɛ a kpa we Yesu se nyɛɛmi be mi nɛ ni kpa amɛ lɛɛ a nane tɔ̃tɔ̃ nɛ a kpa Yesu se nyɛɛmi ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Mɛni wa ma nyɛ maa kase ngɛ sane nɛ ɔ mi? Níhi nɛ wa ba kase nɛ wa le ɔ ngɛ he wami wawɛɛ pe bɔ nɛ wa nuɔ he ha a. Mɛni he je nɛ wa de jã a? Ejakaa Baiblo ɔ tsɔɔmihi kaa Mawu biɛ ɔ, sihi nɛ Mawu wo, kɛ kpɔmi nɔ́ ɔ nɛ wa ba kase ngɛ Baiblo mi ɔ be tsakee kɔkɔɔkɔ. Se wa he numi lɛɛ e ma nyɛ ma tsake.
