kɛ susu jɔɔmihi nɛ o nine
su nɔ ngɛ ligbi ɔ mi ɔ he
(Hyɛ kuku 9-10)
<<<P>>>
sɔle kɛ ha Yehowa a, e haa wɔ “Mawu tue mi jɔmi” ɔ. Tue mi jɔmi nɛ ɔ poɔ wa tsui kɛ wa juɛmi he piɛ, nɛ wa yi tsui tsɔ.
<<<P>>>
7 Ke wa jeɔ hɛsa kpo ɔ, e hɛɔ wɔ kɛ suɔ Yehowa he. Ní kpakpahi tsuo nɛ wa ngɛ ɔ je Yehowa ngɔ. (Kane Yakobo 1:17.) Ní kpakpahi nɛ Yehowa ha wɔ ɔ ekomɛ ji kpɔmi nó ɔ kɛ hwɔɔ se he hɛnɔkami nɛ wa ngɛ ɔ. Mɛni he je nɛ Yehowa ha wɔ ní kpakpa nɛ ɔmɛ ɔ? Pi kaa wa sa kɛ ha ní nɛ ɔmɛ, mohu ɔ, suɔmi nɛ Yehowa ngɛ kɛ ha wɔ kɛ ní kpakpahi nɛ e peeɔ kɛ haa wɔ ɔ he ɔ, lɔ ɔ haa nɛ wa hɛɔ kɛ suɔ e he kaa wa Tsɛ, nɛ wa ngɔɔ wa hɛ kɛ fɔɔ e nɔ.

8 Mo susu bɔ nɛ Yehowa ye bua mo ha kɛ be ɔ he. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ ɔ, mo susu si fɔfɔɛ ko nɛ mi wa nɛ o kɛ kpe be ko nɛ be ɔ he nɛ o hyɛ. Eko ɔ, o suɔlɔ ko gbo, aloo hiɔ nɛ nya wa ko nu mo, aloo a te si kɛ wo mo akɛnɛ o suɔ nɛ o sɔmɔ Yehowa a he je. Se mɛni lɛ ye bua mo kɛ da nya? E ngɛ heii kaa pi mo nitsɛ o he wami nya nɛ o kɛ da si fɔfɔɛ nɛ o kɛ kpe ɔ nya, mohu ɔ, Yehowa ji nɔ nɛ ha mo he wami nɛ o hia nɛ o kɛ da nya a nɛ. (Kane 2 Korintobi 4:7.) Ke wa susu bɔ nɛ Yehowa ye bua wɔ ha kɛ be ɔ he ɔ, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ wa ma je hɛsa kpo wawɛɛ.—La 103:2.
<<<P>>>
9 Mo susu bɔ nɛ Yehowa ngɛ mo ye buae amlɔ nɛ ɔ ha a he. Baiblo ɔ wo wɔ he wami kaa waa “na si ngɛ nó fɛɛ nó he.” (1 Tes. 5:18) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, daa ligbi ɔ, mo susu ní kpakpahi nɛ haa nɛ o bua jɔɔ ɔ he. O ma nyɛ maa pee jã mɔtu, ejakaa lɔ ɔ ma nyɛ maa ye bua mo konɛ o bua nɛ jɔ ngɛ ligbi ɔ mi, aloo o ma nyɛ maa pee jã gbɔkuɛ ke o yaa hwɔ si, ejakaa lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ o tsui maa nɔ o mi, nɛ o ma nyɛ maa hwɔ mahe fuaa. Aloo o ma nyɛ maa pee jã ngɛ be nɛ maa da mo ngɛ ligbi ɔ mi ɔ mi. (La 92:1, 2) Kaa bɔ nɛ John nɛ wa tu e he munyu kɛ sɛ hlami ɔ pee ɔ, mo hu o ma nyɛ maa ngma ní kpakpahi nɛ ya nɔ ngɛ ligbi ɔ mi ɔ kɛ fɔ si. Kɛ o ma plɛ kɛ le ní kpakpahi nɛ o ma nyɛ maa ngma kɛ fɔ si ɔ ha kɛɛ?
