(Hyɛ kuku 18)
<<<P>>>
kpa ko kpe nɛ e he ko nyɛ ko wa ha lɛ kaa e maa ye Yehowa anɔkuale. Jamɛ a be ɔ mi ɔ, Mediabi kɛ Persiabi ɔmɛ ye Babilon nɔ kunimi, nɛ Matsɛ Darius ba ngɛ ma a nɔ yee. Nyumu komɛ nɛ a sɔmɔɔ ngɛ matsɛ we ɔ hɛ kɔ Daniel nɔ, ejakaa Daniel hɛɛ blɔ nya ngua ngɛ matsɛ we ɔ. Lɔ ɔ he ɔ, a to kaa a maa gbe lɛ. A sisi matsɛ ɔ nɛ e wo mlaa kaa nɔ ko nɔ ko nɛ ko sɔle kɛ ha mawu kpa ko kaa ja lɛ matsɛ ɔ pɛ. Se Daniel kɛ mlaa nɛ ɔ tsu we ní, enɛ ɔ he ɔ, a nu lɛ kɛ ya wo jatahi a muɔ mi. Benɛ Daniel ngɛ muɔ ɔ mi ɔ, e ngɛ kaa nó nɛ nɔ ko nɔ ko piɛ we e he ngɛ lejɛ ɔ. Se anɛ e ji anɔkuale kaa lɛ nɔ kake pɛ lɛ e ngɛ lejɛ ɔ lo?—Dan. 6:4-16.

17 Daniel 6:19-23 ɔ tsɔɔ nyakpɛ blɔ nɔ nɛ Yehowa gu kɛ kpɔ̃ Daniel. (Kane.) Yehowa Mawu, nɛ ji Huɛ nɛ se be nɛ nɔ ko ma nyɛ ma ná a gu nyakpɛ blɔ nɔ kɛ kpɔ̃ Daniel kɛ je jata amɛ a nya mi. E nɔ jena benɛ matsɛ ɔ ya jata amɛ a muɔ ɔ he ɔ, Daniel kɛ matsɛ ɔ tu munyu nɛ e de lɛ ke: “Ye Mawu ɔ tsɔ e bɔfo ɔ nɛ e ba ma jata amɛ a nya, . . . ejakaa ngɛ e hɛ mi ɔ, i pee we nó ko nɛ dɛ.” (Dan. 6:22) Benɛ Daniel wami ya je oslaa mi po ɔ, e ya nɔ nɛ e ngɔ e hɛ kɛ fɔ e Huɛ nɛ se be nɛ ji Yehowa a nɔ, nɛ lɔ ɔ ha nɛ e pee kã.

18 Anɛ o nu he hiɛ kaa e piɛ mo pɛ akɛnɛ o be o huɛmɛ kɛ o wekuli a kasa mi ɔ he je lo? Eko ɔ, sɔmɔmi ní tsumi ɔ he je ɔ, e ma bi nɛ o ya je he ko tsitsaa, aloo mo nitsɛ o hia kɛ ya je he kpa ko, aloo eko ɔ, o gbɔjɔ akɛnɛ o be he wami aloo a wo mo tsu ngɛ Yehowa nɛ o ngɛ sɔmɔe ɔ he je. Ngɛ si fɔfɔɛhi kaa kikɛ nɛ ɔmɛ a mi ɔ, eko ɔ, o maa nu he kaa nɔ ko nɔ ko be nɛ o kɛ maa sɛɛ ní aloo e maa ye bua mo. Se o hɛ nɛ ko je nɔ kaa o ngɛ Huɛ ko nɛ be fɛɛ be ɔ, e ngɛ o kasa nya. Lɛ ji Yehowa. E le nó tutuutu nɛ ngɛ mo pee, nɛ e ma nyɛ maa po o he piɛ nɛ e maa ye bua mo ngɛ he fɛɛ he nɛ o ngɛ ɔ. (La 139:7-10) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, mo ná nɔ mi mami kaa Yehowa naa mo, nɛ e maa ye bua mo.
