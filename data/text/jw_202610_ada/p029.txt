18 Moo to be nɛ o kɛ susu daa ligbi ngmami ɔ he, né o bɔ mɔde nɛ o ye be nɛ o to ɔ nɔ daa ligbi. Mo ko ha nɛ níhi nɛ a bɔle mo ɔ nɛ a gbla o juɛmi. Nyɛmiyo ko nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Isabelle nɛ e ye jeha 19 ɔ de ke: “Moo bɔ mɔde kaa o be nó kpa ko pee aloo o be nó kpa ko hyɛɛ ngɛ o fon ɔ nɔ kɛ yaa si be nɛ o maa gbe daa ligbi ngmami ɔ he susumi nya. Ke o to he hɛ kaa o hyɛɔ ní kpa-hi ngɛ o fon ɔ nɔ be mi nɛ o ngɛ daa ligbi ngmami ɔ he susue ɔ, lɛɛ moo ngɔ Mo Susu Ngmami ɔ He Daa Ligbi womiyo ɔ, nó nɛ a print ɔ kɛ tsu ní.” Nyɛmiyo ko nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Olivia nɛ e ye jeha 19 ɔ de ke: “Nó kake nɛ yeɔ buaa ji ke o kɛ o wekuli aloo o huɛmɛ bla kɛ susu daa ligbi ngmami ɔ he. Ke o ngɛ nɔ ko nɛ e ma kai mo aloo o kɛ lɛ ma bla kɛ susu ngmami ɔ he daa ligbi ɔ, e yeɔ buaa nɛ o hɛ ji nɔ.”

19 Fɔli, ke nyɛ bɔɔ mɔde nɛ nyɛɛ kɛ nyɛ bimɛ ɔmɛ susuɔ ngmami ɔ he daa ligbi ɔ, e maa ye bua mɛ nɛ mɛ hu ke a wa a, a maa pee jã daa ligbi. Eko ɔ, o bimɛ ɔmɛ wɛ lolo nɛ a nui níhi tsuo a sisi. Ke jã po nɛ e ji ngɛ a blɔ fa mi ɔ, mo susu nó he tomi nó nɛ ɔ he nɛ o hyɛ. Tso nyafii ma nyɛ maa wa ba pee tso ngua ke ji kaa e naa nyu bɔɔ daa ligbi. Jã a kɛ̃ nɛ ke o kɛ o bimɛ ɔmɛ hu ngɔɔ hɛngmɛfia bɔɔ ko kɛ susuɔ daa ligbi ngmami ɔ kɛ e mi tsɔɔmi ɔ he ɔ, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ huɛ bɔmi kpakpa maa hi a kɛ Yehowa a kpɛti ke a ngɛ wae.—Kane Filipibi 1:9, 10.
<<<P>>>
20 Yesu de ke: “Pi abolo pɛ nɛ haa nɛ adesa hiɔ wami mi, mohu ɔ, munyu fɛɛ munyu nɛ jeɔ Yehowa nya a.” (Mat. 4:4) Kaa bɔ nɛ wa bɔɔ mɔde kaa wa maa ye niye ní nitsɛ daa ligbi ɔ, jã nɔuu kɛ̃ lɛ e sɛ nɛ ligbi kake ngmelu po nɛ be nɛ wa susuu we daa ligbi ngmami ɔ he aloo wa kase we Baiblo ɔ, ejakaa wa wami daa si ngɛ nɔ!
