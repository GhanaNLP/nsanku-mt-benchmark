LA 135
Yehowa Ke: “Ye Bi,
Pee O He Ní Lelɔ”
<<<P>>>
OTI NƐ NGƐ NÍ
KASEMI Ɔ MI
<<<P>>>
Huɛmɛ kpakpahi nɛ nihewi
kɛ yihewi ma ná a maa ye
bua mɛ konɛ a kɛ anɔkuale
yemi nɛ sɔmɔ Yehowa.
<<<P>>>
Huɛmɛ Kpakpahi Yeɔ Buaa
Wɔ Nɛ Waa Kɛ Anɔkuale
Yemi Sɔmɔɔ Yehowa
<<<P>>>
“Nɔ ko ma nyɛ maa ye nɔ kake ngmelu nɔ, se ke ni enyɔ pee kake ɔ,
a ma nyɛ maa te si kɛ wo lɛ.”—FIƐƐ. 4:12.
<<<P>>>
AKƐNƐ wa ji Yehowa Odasefohi he je ɔ, wɔ tsuo wa suɔ nɛ wa
sɔmɔ Yehowa ngɛ anɔkuale yemi mi. Se pi be fɛɛ be nɛ enɛ ɔ
peemi ngɛ gbɔjɔɔ, ejakaa nihi nɛ a ngɛ je ɔ mi ɔ bɔɔ mɔde kaa a
ma je wɔ kɛ je Yehowa he. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e sa nɛ waa bɔ mɔde wa-
wɛɛ nɛ wa ko ha nɛ je ɔ susumi nɛ ná wa nɔ he wami. He kake
nɛ e he ba hia nɛ nihewi kɛ yihewi titli nɛ a hyɛ nɛ hi wawɛɛ ngɛ
ji, bɔ nɛ je ɔ naa huɛ bɔmi ha a. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, nihewi kɛ yi-
hewi komɛ susuɔ kaa nɔ́ nɛ he hia pe kulaa ji nihi abɔ nɛ a kɛ
maa pee huɛ, se tsa pi bɔ nɛ a ngɛ ha a nɛ he hia, kpakpahi jio,
yayahi jio. Nihewi kɛ yihewi babauu ngɔɔ a he kɛ fɔɔ a juami
bimɛ pɛ a nɔ konɛ a wo mɛ ga, nɛ a wo a bua. Ni komɛ hu nuɔ
he kaa e he hia we nɛ a ná huɛmɛ kulaa, ejakaa mɛ nitsɛmɛ a
ma nyɛ ma tsu a nyagbahi a he ní. Se kɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu hu naa
huɛ nɛ wa maa pee ɔ ha kɛɛ?
<<<P>>>
2 Yehowa suɔ nɛ wa ná huɛmɛ nɛ a maa ye bua wɔ konɛ waa
kɛ anɔkuale yemi nɛ sɔmɔ lɛ. Yesu hu kɛ nihi nɛ a suɔ Yehowa a
pee huɛ. Benɛ e piɛ bɔɔ nɛ e ma gbo ɔ, e de e kaseli ɔmɛ nɛ a
ye lɛ anɔkuale ɔ ke: “Nyɛ ji nihi nɛ nyɛ mɛtɛ ye he ngɛ ye
ka amɛ a mi.” Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa e kaseli ɔmɛ ji e huɛmɛ. (Luka 22:
28; Yoh. 15:15) Huɛ kpakpa ji nɔ ko nɛ e mɛtɛɔ nɔ he ngɛ be
kpakpahi kɛ “haomi behi” tsuo a mi. (Abɛ 17:17) Fiɛɛlɔ 4:
9-12* ɔ tsɔɔ kaa huɛmɛ kpakpahi maa wo wɔ he wami nɛ waa
pee nɔ́ nɛ da, nɛ a maa wo wa bua ke wa hao hulɔ. (Kane.)
<<<P>>>
* E ngɛ mi kaa waa kɛ ngmami nɛ ɔ pɔɔ ní tsumi ke wa ngɛ gba si himi he munyu tue
mohu lɛɛ, se ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi lɛɛ e kɔɔ se nami nɛ ngɛ huɛ bɔmi he titli.
<<<P>>>
1. Kɛ nihi fuu naa huɛ bɔmi ha kɛɛ mwɔnɛ ɔ?
<<<P>>>
2. (a) Mɛnɔmɛ lɛ Yesu kɛ pee huɛ? (b) Kaa bɔ nɛ Fiɛɛlɔ 4:9-12 ɔ tsɔɔ ɔ, kɛ
huɛmɛ kpakpahi yeɔ buaa wɔ ha kɛɛ?
