8 Be fɛɛ be nɛ wa maa ye Yehowa he odase kɛ tsɔɔ nɔ ko ɔ, waa kɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu pee kake kɛ ha nɛ nyakpɛ nɔ́ ko ngɛ nɔ yae. Lɔ ɔ ji, Yehowa we bi nɛ a ngɛ hɛ mi yae ɔ nɛ. Yehowa kɛ Yesu fĩ wa se. Yesu wo wɔ si ke: “I kɛ nyɛ ngɛ ligbi ɔmɛ tsuo kɛ yaa si je ɔ nyagbe be ɔ.” (Mat. 28:20) Jehanɛ hu ɔ, ke wa ngɛ fiɛɛe ɔ, bɔfo ɔmɛ fĩɔ wa se nɛ a yeɔ kɛ buaa wɔ hulɔ. —Kpo. 14:6, 7.

KAKE PEEMI NƐ NGƐ NYAKPƐ
<<<P>>>
9 Gbalɔ Mika gba gbami ko nɛ ngɛ mi bae ngɛ wa be nɛ ɔ mi ɔ kɛ fɔ si. Lɔ ɔ ji, kake peemi nɛ se be nɛ ngɛ Mawu we bi a kpɛti ɔ. Yehowa ngɔ e mumi klɔuklɔu ɔ kɛ wo Mika he wami nɛ e gba kaa Yehowa ma bua nyumuhi kɛ yihi nɛ a je he slɔɔtohi ɔ a nya, nɛ e ma ha nɛ kake peemi maa hi a kpɛti. (Kane Mika 2:12.) Gbami nɛ ɔ ngɛ mi bae ngɛ Yehowa we bi a kpɛti mwɔnɛ ɔ, e ngɛ mi kaa Yehowa we bi ngɛ su munomunohi, nɛ a je he slɔɔtohi kɛ si fɔfɔɛ slɔɔtohi a mi mohu lɛɛ, se kake peemi ngɛ a kpɛti. A kɛ nɔ́ nɛ la polɔ David de ɔ kpaa gbi. E de ke: “Hyɛ bɔ nɛ e hi kɛ bɔ nɛ e ngɛ fɛu ha kaa nyɛmimɛ maa hi si ngɛ kake peemi mi!” —La 133:1.
<<<P>>>
10 Mwɔnɛ ɔ, kake peemi ngɛ wa kpɛti ngɛ blɔ slɔɔtohi a nɔ. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, e ngɛ mi kaa wa ngɛ he slɔɔtohi mohu lɛɛ, se wa kaseɔ nɔ́ kake ɔ nɔuu ngɛ asafo mi kpehi, kpɔ mi kpehi kɛ kpokpa nɔ kpehi a sisi. Nyɛmimɛ nɛ a je he slɔɔtohi nɛ a tuɔ gbi slɔɔtohi ɔ peeɔ kake kɛ maa asafo ɔ tsuhi, nɛ a peeɔ kake kɛ tsuɔ ní ngɛ Betel hulɔ. Niinɛ, kake peemi nɛ ngɛ wa kpɛti ɔ se be. Wa ha we nɛ kusumi, sika nɛ nɔ ko ngɛ, aloo ma kudɔmi sanehi nɛ gba wa kpɛti, kaa bɔ nɛ e ngɛ nɔ yae ngɛ je ɔ mi mwɔnɛ ɔ. Mo susu he nɛ o hyɛ: He fɛɛ he ko nɛ wa maa ya ngɛ je ɔ mi ɔ, wa nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi pee klaalo kaa a maa ye bua wɔ ngɛ he lo nya, né a wo wɔ he wami konɛ waa ya nɔ nɛ wa sɔmɔ Yehowa. Ngɛ je ɔ mi tsuo ɔ, Yehowa we bi bɔɔ mɔde kaa a ma ha nɛ a susumi kɛ Yehowa susumi nɛ kɔ, né a pee níhi nɛ maa sa Yehowa hɛ mi.
<<<P>>>
11 Kake peemi nɛ ngɛ wa kpɛti mwɔnɛ ɔ ngɛ nyakpɛ wawɛɛ ejakaa tsa pi jã nɛ e ji ngɛ je ɔ mi kulaa. Kake peemi be nihi a kpɛti kulaa ngɛ je ɔ mi mwɔnɛ ɔ. Daa ligbi ɔ, wa nuɔ amaniɛ bɔmihi nɛ kɔɔ kunɔ nɔmi, basabasa peemi, atuã tsɔmi kɛ tahi a he. Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ bɔ nɛ mi gbami kɛ ninyɛ ngɛ nihi a kpɛti ha. Kaa bɔ nɛ Baiblo ɔ gba kɛ fɔ si ɔ, nihi maa pee a he ‘nihi nɛ a yi anɔkuale, nihi nɛ a hí kɛ somi ko somi, nɔ he guɛ gbeli, nihi nɛ a be he nɔ yemi, nihi nɛ a hɛ mi jɔɛ, nihi nɛ a sume nɔ́ kpakpa, nɔ se blɔ tsɔɔli, nihi nɛ a peeɔ a tsui nya ní, kɛ pupuupu peeli.’ (2 Tim. 3:2-4) E ngɛ mi kaa ‘nɔ yayami ɔ kɛ e daimonio ɔmɛ lɛ a ngɛ je ɔ tsuo kudɔe’ mohu lɛɛ, se Yehowa we bi pee kake kɛ ngɛ Yehowa sɔmɔe. (1 Yoh. 5:19; Kpo. 12:9) Niinɛ, enɛ ɔ ji nyakpɛ nɔ́!

12 Mɛni blɔ nɔ wa maa gu kɛ ha nɛ kake peemi nɛ ngɛ Yehowa we bi a kpɛti ɔ mi nɛ wa? Kɛ gu Yehowa blɔ tsɔɔmi tue nɛ wa maa bu, nɛ waa kɛ ma tsu ní ɔ nɔ. Níhi nɛ wa kaseɔ ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi ɔ yeɔ buaa wɔ nɛ wa toɔ wa tsui si, waa kɛ wa nyɛmimɛ a tɔmihi keɔ mɛ, waa kɛ wa sibi pé, nɛ wa jeɔ mi mi jɔmi kpo kɛ tsɔɔ wa sibi hulɔ. (Efe. 4:2, 31, 32) Be fɛɛ be nɛ wa ma má wa hɛ kɛ fɔ wa nyɛmi ko gbɔjɔmi nɔ, loo wa maa bɔ mɔde kaa wa be sieku yee, loo wa maa bɔ mɔde kaa waa kɛ wa nyɛmi ko tɔmi
