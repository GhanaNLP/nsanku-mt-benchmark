Mɛni maa ye bua mo konɛ nɔ́ ko nɛ ko gbla o juɛmi ke o ngɛ daa ligbi ngmami ɔ mi hyɛe?
(Hyɛ kuku 18)
<<<P>>>
o kɛ o huɛmɛ loo o wekuli lɛ ngɛ daa ligbi ngmami ɔ he susue ɔ, nyɛ ti nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ma nyɛ maa tsɔɔ nɔ́ nɛ e kase nɛ e wo lɛ he wami ɔ. (Rom. 1:12) Nyɛmimɛ komɛ bɔɔ mɔde kaa a ma kadi oti kake loo enyɔ ko ngɛ daa ligbi ngmami ɔ mi konɛ a hɛ ko je nɔ aloo a nyɛ nɛ a susu he ngɛ ligbi ɔ mi tsuo.
<<<P>>>
14 Ga womihi nɛ nyɛɛ se nɛ ɔ ma nyɛ maa ye bua mo konɛ o ná daa ligbi ngmami ɔ nɛ o maa hyɛ mi ɔ he se wawɛɛ. (1) Mo kadi otihi nɛ a he hia wawɛɛ nɛ a ngɛ Hwɔɔmi Mɔ ɔ mi tsɔɔmi ɔ mi ɔ, nɛ o pue o yi mi tɛ ngɛ a he. (2) Moo kane ngmamihi nɛ a tsɛ se ngɛ mi tsɔɔmi ɔ mi ɔ. (3) He nɛ a je jamɛ a ligbi ɔ ngmami ɔ ngɛ ɔ, moo kane ngmami ɔ tsuo, kɛ je e kuku kake ɔ kɛ ya su nyagbe. (4) Moo ngɔ Yehowa Odasefohi A Níhi A Mi Hlami Womi ɔ kɛ hla níhi a mi kɛ kɔ jamɛ a ligbi ɔ ngmami ɔ he konɛ o kase babauu. (5) Mo bli Hwɔɔmi Mɔ ɔ nɛ a je daa ligbi ngmami ɔ mi tsɔɔmi ɔ kɛ je mi ɔ, nɛ o kane e fã komɛ kɛ piɛɛ he. Ke o ngɛ daa ligbi ngmami ɔ mi hyɛe ngɛ o fon loo o tablɛti ɔ nɔ ɔ, o ma nyɛ maa nyɛ Hwɔɔmi Mɔ ɔ he link ɔ nɛ e ngɛ mi tsɔɔmi ɔ sisi ɔ nɔ.*
<<<P>>>
* Hwɔɔmi Mɔ nɛ a tsɛ se ngɛ mi tsɔɔmi ɔ sisi ɔ tsɔɔ jeha kɛ nyɔhiɔ nɛ a kɛ pee hwɔɔmi mɔ ɔ. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, e tsɔɔ bafa loo bafahi, kɛ kuku loo kukuhi nɛ a je daa ligbi ngmami ɔ kɛ je mi ɔ.
<<<P>>>
14-15. Mɛni ji nɔ́ kpa ko hu nɛ wa ma nyɛ maa pee, nɛ mɛni lɛ ma nyɛ maa ye bua wɔ konɛ ke wa hyɛ daa ligbi ngmami ɔ mi ɔ, wa nyɛ nɛ wa kai nɔ́ nɛ wa kase ɔ?
<<<P>>>
15 Nyɛmiyo ko nɛ e ye jeha 19 nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Mackenzie ɔ tsɔɔ nɔ́ nɛ ye bua lɛ nɛ e kaiɔ otihi nɛ ngɛ daa ligbi ngmami ɔ mi ɔ. E de ke: “Ke i ngɔ hɛngmɛfia enuɔ pɛ po kɛ piɛɛ be nɛ i kɛ hyɛɔ daa ligbi ngmami ɔ mi ɔ he ɔ, e haa nɛ i toɔ ye tsui si kɛ kaneɔ daa ligbi ngmami ɔ nɛ i susuɔ he. Jã nɛ i peeɔ ɔ yeɔ buaa mi nɛ i nyɛɔ kaiɔ nɔ́ nɛ i kase ɔ.”
<<<P>>>
16 Mɛni be lɛ e sa nɛ o hyɛ daa ligbi ngmami ɔ mi? Moo hyɛ be nɛ maa hi kɛ ha o kɛ o weku ɔ. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, ni komɛ peeɔ loko a yeɔ mɔtu niye ní aloo ke a ye mɔtu ní ta aloo loko a kɛ a bimɛ ɔmɛ maa ya sukuu. Bɔ nɛ pee nɛ ni komɛ a hɛ nɛ ko je nɔ kaa a maa hyɛ daa ligbi ngmami ɔ mi ɔ, a ngɔɔ Mo Susu Ngmami ɔ He Daa Ligbi womiyo ɔ kɛ fɔɔ he nɛ a maa na ngɛ mla. Se ke ligbi nɛ ní komɛ nɛ a hyɛ we blɔ ɔ ba a, o ma nyɛ ma susu daa ligbi ngmami ɔ he ngɛ be kpa mi ngɛ jamɛ a ligbi ɔ nɔuu mi.
