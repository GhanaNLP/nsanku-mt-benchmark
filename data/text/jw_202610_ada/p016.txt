13 Yesu tsɔɔ kaa suɔmi ji nó nɛ ma kadi e se nyɛɛli ɔmɛ. (Kane Yohane 13:35.) Kristofohi nɛ a hi si ngɛ kaseli ɔmɛ a be ɔ mi ɔ je suɔmi nɛ ɔ kpo ngɛ blɔ slɔɔtohi a nɔ. Kaa nɔ hyɛ-mi nó ɔ, a kɛ a he lo nya níhi ye bua nihi nɛ a be eko ɔ. (Níts. 2:44, 45) A ye bua nihi nɛ a ngɛ mɛ yi mi wae ɔ. (Níts. 12:5) Jehanɛ hu ɔ, a ye bua awusahi kɛ yalɔyihi. (Yak. 1:27) Kris-tofohi nɛ a be nó ko tsɔ po ɔ ngɔ sika kɛ ha konɛ a kɛ ye bua ni kpahi. (2 Kor. 8:1-4) Womi ngmalɔ ko nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Tertullian nɛ e hi si ngɛ bɔfo ɔmɛ a gbenɔ se ɔ, tsɛ munyu nɛ ni komɛ tu kɛ kɔ Kristofo ɔmɛ a he ɔ se ngɛ womi ko nɛ e ngma a mi. Munyu nɛ e tsɛ se ɔ de ke: ‘Hyɛ bɔ nɛ a suɔ a he, nɛ́ a pee klaalo kaa a ma gbo kɛ ha a sibi ha.’

13. Mɛni blɔ nɔ nɛ Kristofohi nɛ a hi si ngɛ kaseli ɔmɛ a be ɔ mi ɔ gu kɛ je suɔmi kpo kɛ tsɔɔ a sibi? (Yohane 13:35)
<<<P>>>
14. Mɛni blɔhi a nɔ nɛ Mawu we bi guɔ kɛ jeɔ suɔmi kpo kɛ tsɔɔ a sibi mwɔnɛ ɔ? (Hyɛ foni ɔmɛ hulɔ.)
<<<P>>>
mi kpo kɛ tsɔɔ a sibi mwɔnɛ ɔ. Wa jeɔ suɔmi nɛ ɔ kpo kɛ tsɔɔ nihi nɛ pi Odasefohi po ji mɛ ɔ kɛ gu sane kpakpa a nɛ wa fiɛɛɔ kɛ haa mɛ ɔ nɔ. Bɔ nɛ pee nɛ wa nyɛ nɛ wa tsu ní tsumi nɛ ɔ, wa jeɔ wa tsui mi kɛ haa yemi kɛ buami hulɔ. Nɔ ko nyɛ we wa nɔ, nɛ nɔ ko hu kpa we wɔ pɛɛ loko wa haa yemi kɛ buami, nɛ wa wui mi dlami nyɔngma hulɔ. Suɔmi lɛ woɔ wɔ he wami nɛ wa yeɔ buaa wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ ngɛ fimi behi a mi ɔ nɛ.* Jehanɛ hu ɔ, wa yeɔ buaa wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ konɛ a bua nɛ jɔ, nɛ huɛ bɔmi kpakpa nɛ hi a kɛ Yehowa a kpɛti. Ní nɛ ɔmɛ nɛ wa peeɔ ɔ kɛ munyu nɛ bɔfo Paulo tu ɔ ngɛ tsakpa. E de ke: “Nyɛɛ tu bua womi munyu kɛ tsɔɔ ni nɛmɛ nɛ a hao ɔ, nyɛɛ ye bua ni nɛmɛ nɛ a gbɔjɔ ɔ, nɛ́ nyɛɛ to nyɛ tsui si kɛ ha nihi tsuo.” (1 Tes. 5:14) Yehowa ha nɛ wa ba piɛɛ nyɛmimɛ nɛ a ngɛ je kɛ wɛ nɛ a ngɛ kaa weku ɔ a he. Nyɛmimɛ nɛ ɔmɛ jeɔ suɔmi nitsɛ-nitsɛ kpo kɛ tsɔɔ a sibi kɛ ni kpahi hulɔ.
