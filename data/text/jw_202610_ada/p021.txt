Se enɛ ɔ ji nɔ́ ko nɛ adesa ko be nyɛe maa pee gblegbleegble! (Dan. 2:3, 9-11) Ke a nyɛ we nɛ a pee jã a, lɛɛ a maa gbe mɛ. Se a nyɛ we nɛ a tsɔɔ matsɛ ɔ nlami ɔ sisi. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e mi mi fu nɛ e fã kaa a gbe ní leli ɔmɛ tsuo nɛ a ngɛ Babilon ɔ, né Daniel kɛ e huɛmɛ ɔmɛ hu piɛɛ he ɔ. Se ngɛ si fɔfɔɛ nɛ mi wa nɛ ɔ tsuo mi ɔ, Daniel kɛ e huɛmɛ ɔmɛ pee kake.

14 Daniel ya matsɛ ɔ ngɔ nɛ e ya kpa lɛ pɛɛ kaa e ha lɛ be bɔɔ konɛ e ba tsɔɔ lɛ e nlami ɔ. Lɔ ɔ se ɔ, Daniel kpale kɛ ho e huɛmɛ ɔmɛ a ngɔ ya. Ngmami ɔ tsɔɔ wɔ nɔ́ nɛ ya nɔ pee se. E de ke: “[Daniel] de mɛ ke a sɔle kɛ ha Mawu nɛ ngɛ hiɔwe ɔ nɛ e na mɛ mɔbɔ ngɛ laami sane nɛ ɔ he, bɔ nɛ pee nɛ a ko kpata Daniel kɛ e huɛ ɔmɛ a hɛ mi.” (Dan. 2:18) Yehowa ha sɔlemi nɛ a je a tsui mi kɛ sɔle ɔ heto, nɛ e tsɔɔ Daniel nlami ɔ kɛ nlami ɔ sisi hulɔ. Daniel ya tsɔɔ matsɛ ɔ nlami ɔ kɛ e sisi. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, a gbi a ti nɔ ko nɔ ko, mohu ɔ, a ha Daniel kɛ e huɛmɛ ɔmɛ blɔ nya nɛ nɔ kuɔ ngɛ Babilon.—Dan. 2:19, 47-49.

15 Wa ma nyɛ maa kase ní komɛ nɛ a he hia wawɛɛ ngɛ sane nɛ ɔ mi. Kekleekle ɔ, e he hia we nɛ mo nɔ kake pɛ o da kahi nɛ o kɛ ngɛ kpee ɔ a nya. Ko ha nɛ he nɔ womi loo zo nɛ tsi o nya nɛ o ko bi yemi kɛ buami. Enyɔne ɔ, mo ko to tsle kaa o ma de ni kpahi nɛ a sɔle ha mo ngɛ nyagba ko nɛ o kɛ ngɛ kpee ɔ he. (2 Kor. 1:11) Mo pue o yi mi tɛ ngɛ bɔ nɛ sɔlemi nɛ a jeɔ tsui mi kɛ sɔleɔ ɔ tsuɔ ní wawɛɛ ha a he. Yehowa nɛ ji “sɔlemi Tue Bulɔ” ɔ maa ye bua wɔ konɛ wa nyɛ nɛ waa pee níhi nɛ jinɛ wa susu kaa wa be nyɛe maa pee ɔ. (La 65:2; Efe. 3:20) Ke wa ngɛ huɛmɛ nɛ a sɔmɔɔ Yehowa a, wa ma nyɛ maa ngɔ wa hɛ kɛ fɔ a nɔ konɛ a wo wɔ he wami, nɛ a ye bua wɔ kɛ da nyagbahi nɛ waa kɛ kpeɔ ngɛ si himi mi ɔ a nya.—Kane Galatiabi 6:2.
