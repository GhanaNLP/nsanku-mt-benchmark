10 Kekleekle ɔ, mo susu ní kpakpahi nɛ Yehowa pee ha mo ngɛ ligbi ɔ mi nɛ o ma nyɛ maa na lɛ si ngɛ he ɔ he. (La 40:5) O ma nyɛ maa na lɛ si akɛnɛ e buɔ o sɔlemihi tue ɔ he je. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, o ma nyɛ maa na lɛ si ngɛ Baiblo ngmami ko nɛ o kane nɛ e wo o bua a he, aloo o ma nyɛ maa na lɛ si ngɛ video ko nɛ o hyɛ, loo munyu ko nɛ o kane ngɛ jw.org ɔ nɔ nɛ e ta o tsui he ɔ he. (La 65:2; Mat. 24:45; Rom. 15:4) Jehanɛ hu ɔ, mo susu níhi nɛ a ngɛ fɛu nɛ Yehowa ha wɔ, nɛ haa nɛ wa bua jɔɔ ngɛ si himi mi ɔ he nɛ o hyɛ. O ma nyɛ maa na lɛ si ngɛ bɔ nɛ ke pu ɔ yaa nɔ si nɛ e ngɛ fɛu ha a he, aloo niye ní ngɔngɔɛ ko nɛ o ye nɛ o bua jɔ ɔ he, aloo o huɛ kpakpa ko nɛ e susuɔ o he ɔ he. Wɔ tsuo wa náa ní kpakpa nɛ ɔmɛ nɛ Yehowa haa wɔ ɔ he se akɛnɛ e ha wɔ “wami kɛ mumi” ɔ he je. (Níts. 17:25) Mɛni jɔɔmi kpahi lɛ Yehowa ha nɛ o nine su nɔ mwɔnɛ ɔ, nɛ o maa suɔ nɛ o ngma kɛ fɔ si?—Efe. 5:20.

11 Mo susu jɔɔmihi nɛ o nine su nɔ akɛnɛ o ba piɛɛ nyɛmimɛ nɛ a ngɛ je kɛ wɛ nɛ suɔmi ngɛ a kpɛti ɔ he ɔ he nɛ o hyɛ. (La 133:1; Abɛ 18:24) Anɛ nyɛminyumu ko aloo nyɛmiyo ko je mi mi jɔmi kpo kɛ tsɔɔ mo aloo e de nó ko nɛ e wo o bua wawɛɛ hyɛ lo? (Abɛ 12:25; 16:24) Ke nyɛmi ko je mi mi jɔmi kpo kɛ tsɔɔ wɔ ngɛ blɔ nyafii ko nɔ po ɔ, e sa nɛ waa na lɛ si ngɛ he. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ ɔ, nyɛmiyo ko nɛ e biyo gbo ɔ tsɔɔ kaa sisije ɔ, ke a ya asafo mi kpe ɔ, e he waa kɛ haa lɛ wawɛɛ kaa e ma la. Nyɛmiyo ɔ nɛ e biyo ɔ gbo ɔ de ke: “Ligbi ko ɔ, nyɛmiyo ko na kaa i ngɛ ya foe benɛ a ngɛ lae ɔ, enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e ba ye kasa nya nɛ e ngɔ e nine kɛ fɔ ye kuɛ mi, nɛ e kɛ mi la la a tsuo nɛ piɛ ɔ kɛ gbe nya. 12 Ke wa susu ní kpakpahi nɛ Yehowa pee ha wɔ kɛ be kɛ ní kpakpahi nɛ e ngɛ pee ha wɔ amlɔ nɛ ɔ he ɔ, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ wa maa je wa tsui mi kɛ na lɛ si. Se kaa bɔ nɛ ní kasemi ɔ sisije ɔ tsɔɔ ɔ, ke nihi pee ní kpakpahi kɛ ha wɔ ɔ, e sa nɛ waa na mɛ si aloo waa pee nó ko kɛ tsɔɔ kaa wa hɛ sa nó nɛ a pee kɛ ha wɔ ɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, mɛni wa ma nyɛ maa pee kɛ tsɔɔ kaa wa hɛ sa níhi tsuo nɛ Yehowa kɛ ni kpahi peeɔ kɛ haa wɔ ɔ?

13 Mɛni wa maa pee kɛ tsɔɔ kaa wa le sina? Mo susu ngmami nɛ a ngɔ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ kɛ da nɔ ɔ he nɛ o hyɛ. Bɔnɛ bɔfo Paulo wo wɔ he wami kaa waa pee wa he nihi nɛ a le sina se ɔ, e ya nɔ nɛ e de ke: “Nó fɛɛ nó nɛ nyɛ ngɛ pee ɔ, munyu tumi mi jio, ní peepee mi jio, nyɛɛ pee kulaa ngɛ Nyɔmtsɛ Yesu biɛ mi, nɛ nyɛɛ gu e nɔ kɛ na Mawu nɛ ji Tsɛ ɔ si.” (Kol. 3:15, 17) Wa ma nyɛ maa ngɔ wa munyu tutui kɛ wa ní peepee kɛ tsɔɔ kaa wa le sina. Ngɛ ngmami nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, Paulo ngɛ tsɔɔe kaa e he hia nɛ waa na Yehowa si. Se Yehowa hu bua jɔɔ wawɛɛ ke wa na ni kpahi si ngɛ níhi nɛ a peeɔ kɛ haa wɔ ɔ he. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ wa susu blɔ nɔ nɛ wa maa gu kɛ ngɔ wa munyu tutui kɛ wa ní peepee kɛ tsɔɔ kaa wa le sina a he.
