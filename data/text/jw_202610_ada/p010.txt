Kɛ o ma plɛ kɛ je hɛsa kpo kɛ ha ni kpahi ha kɛɛ?
<<<P>>>
(Hyɛ kuku 14)
<<<P>>>
o maa ngma, aloo sɛ gbi nɛ o maa mane lɛ ngɛ fon nɔ ɔ nɔ. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, ke asafo mi nɔ-kɔtɔma ko ba slaa o kɛ o weku ɔ, nɛ e wo nyɛ he wami ɔ, nyɛ ma nyɛ maa mane lɛ sɛ gbi kɛ na lɛ si. (1 Tes. 5:12, 13) Be komɛ hu ɔ, o ma nyɛ maa na asafo mi nikɔtɔma amɛ a yi ɔmɛ si wawɛɛ, ejakaa be nɛ jinɛ a huno ɔmɛ ko ná ha mɛ ɔ, a huno ɔmɛ ngɔɔ jamɛ a be ɔ eko kɛ ya slaa asafo mi bi ɔmɛ nɛ a woɔ mɛ he wami.

15 Yehowa ji nɔ nɛ e sa kaa waa na lɛ si wawɛɛ nitsɛ. Wa peeɔ jã kɛ guɔ sɔlemi nɔ. (Efe. 6:18) Behi fuu ɔ, ke wa ngɛ sɔlee ɔ, wa biɔ Yehowa nɛ e pee nɔ́ ko kɛ ha wɔ aloo wa kpaa lɛ pɛɛ nɛ e ye bua wɔ. Eko ɔ, wa deɔ Yehowa nɛ e ye bua wɔ nɛ wa fĩ si ngɛ ka ko nɛ waa kɛ ngɛ kpee ɔ mi, aloo e ha wɔ nile nɛ wa kɛ mwɔ yi mi kpɔhi nɛ da, aloo wa deɔ lɛ nɛ e ngɔ wa tɔmihi kɛ ke wɔ. Se kɛ̃ ɔ, e sɛ nɛ wa hɛ nɛ je nɔ kaa Yehowa pee ní kpakpahi fuu kɛ ha wɔ nɛ e sa nɛ waa na lɛ si ngɛ he. (La 95:2) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e sa nɛ waa hyɛ nɛ hi nɛ wa sɔlemihi nɛ ko pee ní bimihi sɔuu, mohu ɔ, e sa nɛ waa na Yehowa si ngɛ níhi nɛ e peeɔ kɛ haa wɔ ɔ he.
<<<P>>>
16 Moo ngɔ o ní peepee kɛ tsɔɔ kaa o le sina. Ke nɔ ko pee nɔ́ ko ha mo nɛ e ta o tsui he ɔ, moo ngɔ o ní peepee kɛ ha nɛ nɔ ɔ nɛ le kaa o bua jɔ nɔ́ nɛ e pee kɛ ha mo ɔ he. Ke o ngɔ o ní peepee kɛ o munyu tutui kɛ tsɔɔ kaa o hɛ sa nɔ́ nɛ nɔ ko pee kɛ ha mo ɔ he ɔ, lɔ ɔ haa nɛ mo nitsɛ hu o bua jɔɔ wawɛɛ. (Níts. 20:35) Jehanɛ hu ɔ, e haa nɛ ni kpahi a bua jɔɔ, nɛ a nuɔ he kaa a he hia nɛ o suɔ mɛ. Enɛ ɔ haa nɛ huɛ bɔmi gbagbanii hiɔ nyɛ kpɛti.
<<<P>>>
17 Nɔ́ ko ngɛ nɛ e he hia nɛ wa ma nyɛ maa pee kɛ tsɔɔ kaa wa hɛ sa níhi tsuo nɛ Yehowa pee kɛ ha wɔ ɔ. Mɛni ji jamɛ a nɔ́ ɔ? Lɔ ɔ ji nɛ wa maa ngɔ wa “nɔmlɔ tso ɔmɛ kɛ ha kaa afɔle nɛ e hɛ ngɛ.” (Kane Romabi 12:1.) Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa wa maa ya nɔ maa ngɔ nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ nɛ wa ngɛ, nɛ ji, wa susumi, wa he wami, kɛ wa wami tsuo kɛ pee níhi nɛ sa Yehowa hɛ mi. Mɛni ji níhi nɛ sa Yehowa hɛ mi ɔ ekomɛ?
