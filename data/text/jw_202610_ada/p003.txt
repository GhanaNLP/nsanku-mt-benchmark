KPEHI NƐ A PEE Ɔ WO MAWU WE BI HE WAMI KAA A FIƐƐ WAWƐƐ
<<<P>>>
Ngɛ May nyɔhiɔ ɔ nyagbe ɔ, Baiblo Kaseli ɔmɛ pee kpe ko ngɛ London ngɛ England. Ngɛ jamɛ a be ɔ mi ɔ, hehi tsuo nɛ a print ɔ womihi ngɛ ngɛ Britain ɔ ho strike ya. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, nyɛmimɛ nyu-muhi nɛ a ngɛ Brooklyn Betel ɔ ngɔ ngmlɛfiahi babauu kɛ print womiyo ko nɛ e yi ji The Standard for the People ɔ kakaaka 117,000. A gba womiyo nɛ ɔ kɛ ha nihi nɛ a ba kpe ɔ tsuo.
<<<P>>>
Ngɛ Hɔ May 29 ɔ nɔ ɔ, nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ ngɔ womi-yo ɔ kɛ bɔni ní tsumi oya nɔuu. Jamɛ a ligbi ɔ kpe ɔ blɔ nya tomi ɔ de ke: “Baiblo Kaseli ɔmɛ tsuo nɛ a ba kpe ɔ nɛ á fiɛɛ Yesu kɛ Matsɛ Yemi ɔ he sane kpakpa a kɛ tsɔɔ nihi. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, moo te si mɔtu mla, nɛ o ya Alexandra Palace ɔ, nɛ nyɛmimɛ nɛ a ngɛ lejɛ ɔ maa tsɔɔ mo nɔ́ nɛ e sa kaa o pee.”
<<<P>>>
A gba womihi nɛ hiɛ pe 100,000. Blɛfo gbi Hwɔɔmi Mɔ ɔ nɛ a pee ngɛ July 15, 1926 de ke: “Nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ tsuo a bua jɔ wawɛɛ nitsɛ, ejakaa a nu he kaa a bu fami nɛ Yehowa fã kaa a fiɛɛ sane kpakpa a tue.”
<<<P>>>
E nɔ jena a, Nyɛminyumu Rutherford tu magbɛ nɔ munyu ko ngɛ Royal Albert Hall ɔ, nɛ e tsɔɔ nɔ́ nɛ maa ba nɔ yemihi nɛ a ngɛ he wami ngɛ je ɔ mi ɔ a nɔ. Benɛ a bli sinya amɛ nɔuu pɛ ɔ, nihi 13,000 sɛ kpe peemi asa a nɔ, nɛ nihi nɛ a né blɔ ngɛ asa a nɔ bu munyu ɔ tue ngɛ kpo ɔ nɔ kɛ gu speaker slɔɔtohi a nɔ.
<<<P>>>
Ngɛ Nyɛminyumu Rutherford munyu ɔ nɛ e tu ɔ mi ɔ, e tsɔɔ kaa nɔ yeli ɔmɛ yi manye ejakaa a kua Yehowa kɛ e Matsɛ Yemi ɔ. Nyɛminyumu Ruther-ford ya nɔ nɛ e tu munyu kɛ si Je Ma Amɛ A Somi ɔ, nɛ e tsɔɔ kaa e se maa po kaa bɔ nɛ a gba kɛ fɔ si ɔ.

A TSƆ NYƐMIMƐ KƐ YA INDIA KONƐ A YA FIƐƐ
<<<P>>>
Benɛ a pee kpe ɔ ngɛ London ɔ, Nyɛminyu-mu Rutherford kɛ Nyɛminyumu Edwin Skinner nɛ e ji colporteur (amlɔ nɛ ɔ, a tsɛɔ mɛ ke blɔ gbali) ngɛ Sheffield ngɛ England ɔ kpe ngɛ kpe ɔ sisi. Pee se ɔ, Nyɛminyumu Rutherford ngma sɛ womi kɛ ya Nyɛminyumu Edwin kaa e ba na lɛ ngɛ London.
<<<P>>>
Nyɛminyumu Skinner de ke: “Oya nɔuu nɛ i yɔse kaa eko ɔ, e maa tsɔ mi kɛ ya ma kpa nɔ. Lɔ ɔ he ɔ, i ya London e nɔ jena a, nɛ i pee klaalo kaa ma kplɛɛ ní tsumi fɛɛ ní tsumi nɛ e kɛ maa wo ye dɛ ɔ nɔ.” Niinɛ hu ɔ, jã pɛpɛɛpɛ lɛ níhi ya nɔ. Nyɛminyumu Rutherford bi lɛ ke: “Anɛ o maa suɔ nɛ o ya India lo?” Nyɛminyumu Edwin ha lɛ heto ke: “Mɛni be lɛ o suɔ nɛ ma ya lejɛ ɔ?”
