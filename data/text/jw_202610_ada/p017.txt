tsɔɔ kaa ngɛ wa be nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, nihi maa pee a he ‘nihi nɛ a suɔ mɛ nitsɛmɛ a he, sika suɔli, kɛ nihi nɛ a suɔ níhi nɛ haa nɔ bua jɔmi.’ A be suɔmi náe ha Mawu, ‘nɛ a be suɔmi náe kɛ ha a weku mi bimɛ’ hulɔ. (2 Tim. 3:2-4) Yesu gba kaa nihi maa tsɔɔ a sibi a se blɔ, nɛ a maa nyɛ a sibi hulɔ. E de hu ke: “Nihi babauu a suɔmi ɔ nya maa jɔ.” (Mat. 24:10, 12) Anɛ ke o hyɛ je ɔ mi ɔ, o nɛ kaa ní nɛ ɔmɛ ngɛ nɔ yae lo? Se Yehowa he wami ɔ lɛ ha nɛ e we bi yaa nɔ nɛ a jeɔ suɔmi kpo kɛ tsɔɔ a sibi ɔ nɛ. Niinɛ, enɛ ɔ ji nyakpɛ nɔ́ nɛ ngɛ nɔ yae mwɔnɛ ɔ!

16 Ke wa je wa tsui mi nɛ wa je nihi a yi ngɛ nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ kpakpa nɛ a peeɔ ɔ he, nɛ wa bɔ mɔde wawɛɛ nɛ wa ha nɛ tue mi jɔmi hi waa kɛ wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ wa kpɛti ɔ, lɔ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa wa ngɛ suɔmi kpo jee. (Mar. 9:50) Ke wa ya nɔ nɛ wa je suɔmi kpo ngɛ wa munyu tutui kɛ wa ní peepee mi ɔ, lɔ ɔ maa nɔ mi heii kaa wa ji Mawu we bi. Ke wa jeɔ suɔmi kpo ɔ, lɔ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa waa kɛ Yehowa pee kake kɛ ngɛ ní tsue be mi nɛ e ha nɛ nyakpɛ nɔ́ ko ngɛ nɔ yae ngɛ e we bi a kpɛti mwɔnɛ ɔ.

<<<P>>>
17 Bɔ nɛ wa yibɔ ngɛ hɛ mi yae ha kɛ kake peemi kɛ suɔmi nɛ ngɛ wa kpɛti ngɛ asafo ɔ mi ɔ tsɔɔ kaa Yehowa ngɛ nyakpɛ níhi pee ngɛ wa be nɛ ɔ mi. E ngɛ mi kaa nihi babauu teɔ si kɛ woɔ wa fiɛɛmi ní tsumi ɔ mohu lɛɛ, se ngɛ jamɛ a be ɔ mi nɔuu ɔ, wa yibɔ ngɛ hɛ mi yae. E ngɛ mi kaa kake peemi be nihi a kpɛti ngɛ je ɔ mi mohu lɛɛ, se wɔɔ lɛɛ kake peemi ngɛ wa kpɛti. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, e ngɛ mi kaa wa ngɛ je ko nɛ nihi je we suɔmi kpo ngɛ mi ɔ mi mohu lɛɛ, se suɔmi nɛ wa ngɛ kɛ ha Mawu kɛ wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ ɔ maa nɔ mi kaa wa ji anɔkuale Kristofohi. (Yoh. 13:34) Pi nɛ wa ngɛ hyɛe nɛ nyakpɛ ní nɛ ɔmɛ ngɛ nɔ yae kɛkɛ, mohu ɔ, wa “piɛɛ Mawu he kɛ ngɛ ní tsue.”—1 Kor. 3:9.
<<<P>>>
18 E ngɛ nyakpɛ kaa ngɛ je nɛ nyagbahi sɔuu lɛ ngɛ mi nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, Yehowa we bi lɛɛ a yibɔ ngɛ hɛ mi yae daa ligbi, kake peemi ngɛ a kpɛti nɛ a suɔ a sibi hulɔ. Ní nɛ ɔmɛ ji nyakpɛ níhi nɛ a ngɛ nɔ yae mwɔnɛ ɔ. Nyakpɛ ní nɛ ɔmɛ haa nɛ wa náa nɔ mi mami kaa níhi tsuo nɛ Yehowa wo si kaa e maa pee hwɔɔ se ɔ maa ba mi kokooko. (2 Pet. 3:13) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, o bua nɛ jɔ he blɔ nɛ o ná kaa o piɛɛ Yehowa he kɛ ngɛ ní tsue kɛ ha nɛ nyakpɛ ní nɛ ɔmɛ ngɛ nɔ yae ɔ he.
