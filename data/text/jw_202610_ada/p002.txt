JEHA LAFA NƐ BE Ɔ
<<<P>>>
BLƐFO gbi Hwɔɔmi Mɔ ɔ nɛ a pee ngɛ January 1, 1926 de ke, “E be kɛe nɛ Mawu maa wo e biɛ ɔ hɛ mi nyami, nɛ e maa hla ni komɛ ngɛ zugba a nɔ kaa e we odasefohi.”
<<<P>>>
Ngɛ jeha 1926 mi ɔ, Yehowa gu Yesu Kristo, nɛ e ji asafo ɔ yi ɔ nɔ kɛ ha nɛ Kristofo ɔmɛ ba nu sisi kaa fiɛɛmi ní tsumi ɔ ji ní tsumi nɛ he hia wawɛɛ nɛ e sa kaa a tsu. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, “tsɔlɔ anɔkualetsɛ kɛ ní lelɔ ɔ” pee womi ehehi kɛ ha Mawu we bi, nɛ a pee tsakemi ngɛ bɔ nɛ a peeɔ a kpe ɔmɛ ha a he, nɛ a tsɔɔ nɔ́ tutuutu nɛ e sa nɛ Kristofohi tsuo nɛ a pee. (Mat. 24:45) Yesu ye bua mɛ nɛ a na tsuhi kɛ klamahi nɛ a kɛ maa pee womihi konɛ a nyɛ nɛ a tsu fiɛɛmi ní tsumi ɔ.

WOMI EHE KƐ TSAKEMIHI NƐ A PEE NGƐ ASAFO MI KPE ƆMƐ A HE
<<<P>>>
Ngɛ April nyɔhiɔ ɔ mi ɔ, The Golden Age (nɛ amlɔ nɛ ɔ, a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Awake!) ɔ fia adafi kaa a maa pee womi ehe ko nɛ e yi ji Deliverance ɔ. Womi nɛ ɔ ma ba da womi nɛ ji Studies in the Scriptures ɔ nane mi, nɛ womi ehe nɛ ɔ ma ba pee womi titli nɛ a kɛ ma tsu ní ngɛ fiɛɛmi mi. Bɔ nɛ pee nɛ a na womihi fuu he je ɔ, nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi nɛ a ngɛ Betel ɔ ya he womi fiami klama ehe ko, nɛ e ma nyɛ maa print womi nɛ ɔ kakaaka 22 ngɛ hɛngmɛfia kake pɛ mi. Baiblo Kaseli ɔmɛ ngɔ womi nɛ ɔ kɛ ha nɔ fɛɛ nɔ nɛ a maa fiɛɛ kɛ ha lɛ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Jehahi babauu nɛ be ɔ, Baiblo Kaseli ɔmɛ kaseɔ womi nɛ ji Studies in the Scriptures ɔ ngɛ a kpe ɔmɛ a sisi. Se kɛ je May nyɔhiɔ ɔ nɔ kɛ yaa a, a ma bɔni munyuhi nɛ ngɛ Hwɔɔmi Mɔ ɔ mi ɔ kasemi. Mɛni lɛ je tsakemi nɛ ɔ mi kɛ ba?
<<<P>>>
Asafo ko ngma sɛ womi kɛ ya ha Nyɛminyumu Rutherford, nɛ a de ke, “Daa Hɔgba a, wa kaseɔ Hwɔɔmi Mɔ ɔ mi munyuhi nɛ asafo ɔ to he blɔ nya kaa waa kase ɔ. Wa bua jɔ kaa la a ngɛ kpɛe ha wɔ tsuo, nɛ wa nuɔ tsakemi ehehi nɛ a peeɔ ɔ sisi oya nɔuu.”
<<<P>>>
“La a nɛ ngɛ kpɛe” aloo tsakemi ehehi nɛ a peeɔ ɔ ye bua Baiblo Kaseli ɔmɛ nɛ a kɛ fiɛɛmi ní tsumi ɔ pee nɔ́ titli ngɛ a si himi mi. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, Hwɔɔmi Mɔ ɔ mi munyu ɔ eko tsɔɔ kaa mɔde nɛ Kristofono ko maa bɔ kaa e maa pee tsakemi ngɛ e je mi bami mi, nɛ e ma je mi mi jɔmi kɛ bumi kpo kɛ tsɔɔ ni kpahi ɔ pɛ pi, mohu ɔ, e sa nɛ e fiɛɛ. Hwɔɔmi Mɔ ɔ tsa nɔ ke, “Yehowa fã Kristofohi kaa a pee e we odasefohi. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, akɛnɛ Kristofohi tsuo suɔ Yehowa he je ɔ, e sa nɛ a bu fami nɛ ɔ tue nɛ a pee klaalo kaa a maa fiɛɛ.”
<<<P>>>
Hwɔɔmi Mɔ ɔ mi munyu kpa ko nɛ e yi ji “Sacrifice and Service” ɔ tsɔɔ kaa anɔkuale Kristofohi jeɔ suɔmi kpo kɛ tsɔɔ Yehowa kɛ gu sɔmɔmi nɛ a sɔmɔɔ lɛ ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ nɔ. Hwɔɔmi Mɔ ɔ mi munyu ɔ wo Baiblo Kaseli ɔmɛ he wami kaa a pee nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ nɛ a ma nyɛ konɛ a kɛ kã nɛ fiɛɛ sane kpakpa a kɛ ha nihi babauu.—Yes. 43:10, 12; Mat. 24:14.
