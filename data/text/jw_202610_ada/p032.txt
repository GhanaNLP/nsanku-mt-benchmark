Mo Ha Nɛ O Hemi Kɛ Yemi ɔ Mi Nɛ Wa | 30
<<<P>>>
NÍHI A SI KPAMI
<<<P>>>
Yehowa Jɔɔ Wɔ Ke Wa Ngɔ Wa Hɛ Kɛ Fɔ E Nɔ
<<<P>>>
Emma tsuɔ ní ligbi etɛ daa otsi ngɛ hotel ko, nɛ e kɛ ngmlɛfia nɛɛ lɛ tsuɔ ní daa ligbi. Ligbi ko ɔ, e bi e nɔkɔtɔma nɛ e hyɛɔ ní tsumi ɔ nɔ ɔ kaa e ha lɛ ligbi kake pɛ ngɛ otsi ɔ mi konɛ e nyɛ nɛ e ya kpokpa nɔ kpe nɛ a maa pee ɔ. Emma nɔkɔtɔma a kplɛɛ we e ní bimi ɔ nɔ, mohu ɔ, e de Emma kaa kɛ je jamɛ a ligbi ɔ kɛ yaa a, e ma tsu ní ligbi enuɔ ngɛ otsi ɔ mi. E tsɔɔ kaa ke Emma pee we jã a, lɛɛ e kpa ní tsumi ɔ. Se Emma ngɔ e hɛ kɛ fɔ Yehowa nɔ, nɛ e hla kaa e ma kpa ní tsumi ɔ, lɔ ɔ he ɔ, e ya kpokpa nɔ kpe ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Benɛ Emma ngma sɛ womi kɛ ya kaa e ngɛ hlae nɛ e kpa ní tsumi ɔ, e ya na hotel manager nitsɛ hu ngɛ lejɛ ɔ. Benɛ e de mana-ger nɔ́ nɛ ba a, manager a wo lɛ si kaa e ma tsu sane ɔ he ní kɛ ha lɛ. Se otsi enyɔ se ɔ, a de Emma kaa a fiee lɛ ngɛ ní tsumi ɔ mi. Se tlukaa, Emma kɛ hotel manager kpale kpe ekohu, nɛ benɛ e bɔ lɛ nɔ́ nɛ ba a, e mi mi fu wawɛɛ kaa Emma nɔkɔtɔma a kɛ e blɔ tsɔɔmi tsu we ní nɛ e fiee Emma ngɛ ní tsumi ɔ mi. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e tsu sane ɔ he ní oya nɔuu, nɛ a to blɔ nya kpa kɛ ha Emma. Amlɔ nɛ ɔ lɛɛ, e ma tsu ní ligbi enuɔ ngɛ otsi ɔ mi, se e kɛ ngmlɛ-fia eywiɛ pɛ lɛ ma tsu ní ɔ daa ligbi. Blɔ nya tomi ehe nɛ ɔ hi saminya kɛ ha Emma, ejakaa e ná be fuu nɛ e kɛ ma tsu blɔ gbami ní tsu-mi ɔ kɛ ní kpahi a he ní ngɛ Yehowa sɔmɔmi mi. Yehowa tsu e hiami níhi a he ní kɛ ha lɛ pe bɔ nɛ e ngɛ blɔ hyɛe ɔ po.

Womi nɛ ɔ, wa jua we. A pee kɛ ha je kɛ wɛ Baiblo tsɔsemi ní tsumi ɔ, nɛ nihi jeɔ a suɔmi mi nɛ a haa nɔ́ ko nɛ a kɛ fío se. Ke o ngɛ hlae nɛ o ha yemi kɛ buami ɔ, wa kpa mo pɛɛ nɛ o ya donate.jw.org.
