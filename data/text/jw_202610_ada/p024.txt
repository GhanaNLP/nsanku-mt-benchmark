Mo Susu Ngmami ɔ He Daa Ligbi
<<<P>>>
“A kɛ a juɛmi tsuo pɛtɛ munyu ɔ he, nɛ daa ligbi ɔ, a kpaa Ngmami ɔ mi saminya.”—NÍTS. 17:11.
<<<P>>>
NGƐ jeha 1905 ɔ mi ɔ, Yehowa asafo ɔ pee womi ko nɛ e biɛ ji Daily Heavenly Manna for the Household of Faith, nɛ a ngɔ daa li-gbi ngmami ɔ kɛ Zion’s Watch Tower a mi munyu ko nɛ e tsɔɔ ngmami ɔ mi ɔ kɛ wo mi. Enɛ ɔ yeɔ buaa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ nɛ a ka-neɔ ngmami ɔ daa ligbi, nɛ a pueɔ a yi mi tɛ ngɛ he ngɛ jeha a mi tsuo. Yehowa we bi ngɔ womi nɛ ɔ nɔuu kɛ tsu ní hluu kɛ ya si jeha 1927 ɔ mi. Kɛ je lɔ ɔ se ɔ, a kɛ daa ligbi ngmami ɔ kɛ Hwɔɔ-mi Mɔ ɔ mi munyu ko nɛ tsɔɔ ngmami ɔ mi ɔ bɔni Year Book ɔ mi womi.* A peeɔ Year Book nɛ ɔ daa jeha, nɛ a kɛ daa ligbi ngma-mi ɔ kɛ Hwɔɔmi Mɔ ɔ mi munyu nɛ tsɔɔ ngmami ɔ mi ɔ woɔ mi, enɛ ɔ ha nɛ nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ kaseɔ ngmami ɔ nɛ a susuɔ he be fɛɛ be.
<<<P>>>
2 Kɛ je jeha 1986 kɛ ma nɛ ɔ, Yehowa asafo ɔ bɔni womiyo ko nɛ e yi ji Mo Susu Ngmami ɔ He Daa Ligbi ɔ peemi. Amlɔ nɛ ɔ, a pee womiyo nɛ ɔ ngɛ gbihi nɛ hiɛ pe 300 mi. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, o ma nyɛ maa na daa ligbi ngmami ɔ ngɛ JW Library app ɔ kɛ Watchto-wer ONLINE LIBRARY (wol.jw.org) ɔ kɛ Watchtower Library ɔ nɔ.
<<<P>>>
3 Akɛnɛ wa be deka kulaa he je ɔ, e he ma nyɛ maa wa ha wɔ kaa wa ma susu daa ligbi ngmami ɔ he. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, ngɛ ní kase-mi nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, wa ma susu sane bimi etɛ nɛ ɔmɛ a he. (1) Mɛni Baiblo mi nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ lɛ ma nyɛ maa ye bua wɔ konɛ wa susu ngmami ɔ he daa ligbi? (2) Mɛni he je nɛ e sa nɛ wa susu ngma-mi ɔ he daa ligbi ɔ? kɛ (3) Mɛni blɔ nɔ wa maa gu kɛ susu ngma-mi ɔ he daa ligbi konɛ wa ná he se wawɛɛ?
<<<P>>>
* Gbi komɛ ngɛ nɛ a be Year Book ɔ eko, se daa nyɔhiɔ ɔ, a ngɔɔ daa ligbi ngmami ɔ kɛ e mi tsɔɔmi ɔ kɛ woɔ Hwɔɔmi Mɔ ɔ nɛ a peeɔ daa nyɔhiɔ ɔ mi konɛ nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ nɛ a na nɛ a kane.
<<<P>>>
1-2. (a) Mɛni womihi lɛ Yehowa asafo ɔ pee blema, nɛ mɛni he je? (b) Jije wa ma nyɛ maa na daa ligbi ngmami ɔ ngɛ mwɔnɛ ɔ?
<<<P>>>
3. Mɛni lɛ ma nyɛ ma ha nɛ e he nɛ wa ha wɔ kaa wa maa hyɛ daa ligbi ngma-mi ɔ mi be fɛɛ be ɔ, nɛ mɛni he wa ma susu ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi?
