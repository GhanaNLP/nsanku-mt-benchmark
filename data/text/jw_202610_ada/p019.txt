Huɛmɛ kaa jã a maa ye bua wɔ konɛ waa kɛ anɔkuale yemi nɛ sɔmɔ Yehowa ke e he wa ha wɔ kaa wa maa pee jã po. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, ke waa kɛ huɛmɛ kpakpahi bla kɛ tsu ní ha Yehowa a, wa bua maa jɔ ngɛ si himi ɔ mi, nɛ wa huɛ bɔmi ɔ maa hi si kɛ ya neneene.
<<<P>>>
3 Ke o ji niheyo loo yiheyo ɔ, kɛ huɛmɛ kpakpahi maa ye bua mo konɛ o kɛ anɔkuale yemi nɛ sɔmɔ Yehowa ha kɛɛ? Mɛni blɔ nɔ nɛ o fɔli kɛ nihi nɛ a suɔ Yehowa a ma nyɛ maa gu kɛ pee huɛmɛ kpakpahi kɛ ha mo? Nyɛ ha nɛ wa susu gbalɔ Daniel nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ he, nɛ waa hyɛ nɔ́ nɛ ye bua lɛ nɛ e ya nɔ nɛ e pee nɔ́ nɛ da, nɛ e ngɔ e hɛ kɛ fɔ Yehowa nɔ be mi nɛ e kɛ si fɔfɔɛ ko nɛ mi wa a kpe ɔ. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, waa maa hyɛ bɔ nɛ Daniel Huɛ nɛ pe kulaa nɛ ji Yehowa a ye bua lɛ ha benɛ e pee kaa nɔ́ nɛ e piɛ lɛ pɛ ɔ.

HUƐMƐ KPAKPAHI MAA YE BUA MO KONƐ O SƆMƆ YEHOWA
<<<P>>>
4 Benɛ Daniel ngɛ wae ɔ, jamɛ a be ɔ mi ɔ, Mawu we bi kɛ si fɔfɔɛ ko nɛ mi wa ngɛ kpee. Israelbi ɔmɛ nɛ a ngɛ Yuda a kpa Yehowa sɔmɔmi, nɛ Yehowa hu kua mɛ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, ngɛ jeha 620 loko mwɔnɛ ɔ be kanemi je sisi ɔ, Yehowa ngmɛ blɔ nɛ Matsɛ Nebukadnezar ba tua Yudabi ɔmɛ. (Dan. 1:2) Ngɛ jamɛ a be ɔ mi ɔ, Daniel ji niheyo wayo. Jeha bɔɔ komɛ a se ɔ, a nu Yudabi ɔmɛ kɛ ho Babilon ya, nɛ Daniel weku ɔ hu piɛɛ he. Lɔ ɔ se ɔ, a ngɔ Daniel kɛ ho matsɛ we ɔ ya konɛ a ya tsɔse lɛ. (Dan. 1:3-6) Enɛ ɔ ha nɛ Daniel ya je ma kpa nɔ, nɛ e be e wekuli ɔmɛ a kasa nya hu.
<<<P>>>
5 Mɛni lɛ ye bua Daniel nɛ e ya nɔ nɛ e ye Yehowa anɔkuale ngɛ Babilon ɔ? Baiblo ɔ tsɔɔ kaa Daniel “ma nya si ngɛ e tsui mi” kaa e kɛ Mawu mlaa amɛ ma tsu ní. (Dan. 1:8) Kɛ Daniel plɛ kɛ kase mlaa nɛ ɔmɛ ha kɛɛ? Mɛnɔmɛ lɛ a ye bua lɛ loko e nyɛ nɛ e mwɔ e yi mi kpɔ kaa e ma sɔmɔ Yehowa benɛ e ji jokuɛ ɔ? E ngɛ heii kaa e fɔli ɔmɛ lɛ a ye bua lɛ. E sa kadimi wawɛɛ kaa Daniel biɛ ɔ sisi ji “Ye Kojolɔ Ji Mawu.” Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa Daniel fɔli ɔmɛ ngɛ hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi kaa ke Daniel ba wa a, e ma sɔmɔ Yehowa, nɛ e kɛ e blɔ tsɔɔmi ma tsu ní. —5 Mose 6:6, 7.
<<<P>>>
6 E ma nyɛ maa ba lɛ hu kaa Yeremia nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ kpakpa a ná Daniel nɔ he wami. A fɔ Daniel be mi nɛ Yehowa tsɔ Yeremia kaa e ngɔ sɛgbi kɛ ya ha Yuda Matsɛ ɔ kɛ odehehi a weku komɛ nɛ a ngɛ Yuda a ngɛ jeha 647 loko mwɔnɛ ɔ be kanemi. (Yer. 1:2, 17, 18; Dan. 1:3, 6) E ma nyɛ maa ba kaa Daniel weku ɔ piɛɛ odehehi a weku ɔ he. E ngɛ mi kaa wa be nyɛe ma de kɛ nɔ mi mami ke ji Yeremia kɛ Daniel pee huɛ mohu lɛɛ, se e ngɛ heii kaa e nu sɛ gbi nɛ Yeremia ba de Mawu we bi ɔ. Benɛ Daniel wa po ɔ, e ya nɔ nɛ e kase munyu nɛ Yeremia ngma kɛ fɔ si ɔ. (Yer. 25:11; 29:10; Dan. 9:1, 2) Daniel kase Yeremia nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ kpakpa a. Daniel na kaa Yeremia pee kã nɛ e ya nɔ nɛ e ye Yehowa anɔkuale, e ngɛ mi kaa nihi nɛ a bɔle lɛ ɔ yi Yehowa anɔkuale.
<<<P>>>
7 Wa ma nyɛ ma de kɛ nɔ mi mami kaa Daniel fɔli ɔmɛ, gbalɔ Yeremia, kɛ Yehowa sɔmɔli anɔkualetsɛmɛ komɛ pee nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ kpakpa kɛ ha Daniel. A ye bua lɛ nɛ e kɛ Yehowa pee huɛ.
