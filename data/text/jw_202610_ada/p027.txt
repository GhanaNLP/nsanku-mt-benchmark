(Hyɛ kuku 10)

12 Ke wa susuɔ ngmami ɔ he daa ligbi, lɔ ɔ ma dla wɔ kɛ ha ní tsumi fɛɛ ní tsumi nɛ asafo ɔ kɛ ma nyɛ maa wo wa dɛ hwɔɔ se ɔ. Ní tsumihi babauu ngɛ Yehowa asafo ɔ mi mwɔnɛ ɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, Yehowa asafo ɔ hia nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi nɛ a nane pi si konɛ a tsu ní tsumi nɛ ɔmɛ. Wa hia nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi nɛ a ma sɔmɔ kaa blɔ gbali, ma se sane kpakpa fiɛɛli, nihi nɛ a ma sɔmɔ ngɛ Betel loo ngɛ gbi sisi tsɔɔmi he nɛ be Betel, kɛ nihi nɛ a maa ye bua kɛ ma asafo ɔ tsuhi. Jehanɛ se hu ɔ, wa hia nihewi nɛ a he su nɛ a baa a he si konɛ a sɔmɔ kaa asafo mi sɔmɔli, asafo mi nikɔtɔmahi kɛ kpɔ mi nɔ hyɛli. Yehowa suɔ kaa nyumuhi nɛ a ma nyɛ maa fĩa kɛ fɔ a nɔ, nɛ a le bɔ nɛ a kɛ Baiblo sisi tomi mlaahi ma tsu ní ha a nɛ a nyɛɛ hɛ mi ngɛ e we asafo ɔ mi. (5 Mose 1:13-15) Ke wa susuɔ ngmami ɔ he daa ligbi ɔ, lɔ ɔ maa ye bua wɔ nɛ huɛ bɔmi kpakpa maa hi waa kɛ Yehowa wa kpɛti. Enɛ ɔ ma ha nɛ wa ma ná suhi nɛ wa hia a konɛ wa nyɛ nɛ waa pee klaalo kɛ ha ní tsumi fɛɛ ní tsumi nɛ a kɛ maa wo wa dɛ hwɔɔ se ɔ.

<<<P>>>
13 Kɛ o ma ple kɛ susu ngmami ɔ he daa ligbi ha kɛɛ? Loko Betel weku ɔ mi bimɛ ngɛ je kɛ wɛ ɔ maa je ní tsumi sisi ngɛ ligbi ɔ mi ɔ, a susuɔ daa ligbi ngmami ɔ he kekle. Se loko a maa pee jã a, a sɔleɔ kpiti kɛ jeɔ sisi. Mo hu o ma nyɛ maa sɔle kpiti kɛ je sisi nɛ o bi Yehowa nɛ e ye bua mo konɛ o ná daa ligbi ngmami ɔ nɛ o ma he susumi ɔ he se. (Yak. 1:5) Ke o sɔle ta a, moo kane jamɛ a ligbi ɔ ngmami ɔ kɛ mi tsɔɔmi ɔ. Lɔ ɔ se ɔ, o ma nyɛ ma bi o he sane nɛ ɔmɛ: ‘Kɛ mwɔnɛ ɔ ngmami ɔ ma nyɛ maa ye bua mi ha kɛɛ konɛ ye hemi kɛ yemi ɔ mi nɛ wa? Mɛni i kase kɛ kɔ Yehowa he nɛ maa ye bua mi konɛ ma hɛ kɛ su e he? Kɛ mwɔnɛ ɔ ngmami ɔ maa ye bua mi konɛ ma nyɛ ma da nyagbahi nɛ ma kɛ kpe ngɛ ligbi ɔ mi ɔ nya ha kɛɛ?’ Ke
