OTI NƐ NGƐ NÍ KASEMI Ɔ MI
E maa ye bua wɔ konɛ wa bua nɛ jɔ he blɔ nɛ wa ná kaa wa piɛɛ Yehowa he kɛ ha nɛ nyakpɛ ní etɛ komɛ ngɛ nɔ yae ngɛ wa be nɛ ɔ mi ɔ he.
<<<P>>>
O Kɛ Yehowa Nɛ E Ngɛ Nyakpɛ Níhi Pee Mwɔnɛ ɔ Nɛ Pee Kake Kɛ Tsu Ní
<<<P>>>
“Lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ nihi tsuo maa le kaa ye kaseli ji nyɛ.”—YOH. 13:35.
<<<P>>>
NGƆƆ lɛ kaa o piɛɛ Israelbi ɔmɛ a he ngɛ Wo Tsu ɔ nya, nɛ o na nɛ Yehowa gba Wo Tsu ɔ mi enyɔ nɛ nyɛ nyɛɛ zugba gbigbli nɔ kɛ be slɔkee, nɛ Egipt tabo ɔ nine sui nyɛ nɔ. Kɛ o maa nu he ha kɛɛ? E ngɛ heii kaa o bua maa jɔ wawɛɛ nitsɛ. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, moo hyɛ bɔ nɛ o bua maa jɔ ha ke ji kaa o ngɛ Yesu hyɛe nɛ e ngɛ hiɔtsɛmɛ tsae né e ngɛ gbogboehi si tlee ɔ! Nyakpɛ níhi nɛ a tu he munyu ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi ɔ a kpɛti enyɔ pɛ ji nɛ ɔ nɛ. E ngɛ heii kaa ke tsui kpakpatsɛ ko na nyakpɛ ní nɛ ɔmɛ kɛ e hɛngmɛ ɔ, e maa na kaa Yehowa lɛ ha nɛ a ya nɔ. (Mat. 15:31) Se nyakpɛ ní nɛ ɔmɛ ya nɔ blema lokoo. Anɛ nyakpɛ níhi ngɛ nɔ yae ngɛ wa be nɛ ɔ hu mi lo? Ee! Mɛni he je nɛ wa ma nyɛ ma de jã a?
<<<P>>>
2 Nyakpɛ nɔ́ ji nɔ́ ko nɛ adesa ko be nyɛe maa pee ngɛ e he wami nya, aloo e maa nu sisi kɛ pi si. E ji nɔ́ ko nɛ Yehowa pɛ lɛ ma nyɛ maa ngɔ e he wami kɛ pee. Ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, wa ma susu nyakpɛ níhi etɛ nɛ Yehowa ngɛ pee ngɛ e we asafo ɔ mi mwɔnɛ ɔ he. A ji nyakpɛ níhi niinɛ, ejakaa Yehowa pɛ ji nɔ nɛ ha nɛ ní nɛ ɔmɛ ngɛ nɔ yae mwɔnɛ ɔ. Ngɛ eko fɛɛ eko mi ɔ, wa maa hyɛ blɔ nɔ nɛ wa maa gu kɛ ‘piɛɛ Yehowa he kɛ tsu ní,’ be mi nɛ e ha nɛ nyakpɛ ní nɛ ɔmɛ ngɛ nɔ yae ɔ.—Kane 1 Korintobi 3:9.
