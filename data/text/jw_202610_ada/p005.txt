Akɛnɛ nihi babauu a bua jɔ wa womi ɔmɛ a he he je ɔ, nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi nɛ a tsuɔ ní ngɛ Brooklyn Betel ɔ na kaa e he hia nɛ a na he ko nɛ kle saminya nɛ a ma nyɛ maa fia wa womi ɔmɛ fuu ngɛ. Jeha babauu nɛ be ɔ, a hai tsu ko ngɛ he ko nɛ a tsɛɔ ke Concord Street ɔ, se tsu nɛ ɔ klee we tsɔ kɛ ha mɛ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ he zugba ko ngɛ Adams Street, nɛ e kɛ kekleekle tsu ɔ he kɛ we.
