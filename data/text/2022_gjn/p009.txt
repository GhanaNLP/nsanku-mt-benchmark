Amanshɛrbichaliso be Kananɛ a Nite
<<<P>>>
4. Amanshɛrbichalaso nɛ kakɛ kikɛ ta ashi January n ya fo September be kɔnɔ yili GH₵ 46.85 biliɔn (GDP be 10.7%), ta n kesar amanshɛrbi tamaso GH₵ 51.31 biliɔn (GDP be 11.7%). Amanshɛrbichalaso na kikɛ daŋ shi asɔ monɛ a maŋ la nku be lampo chaliso (74%), Asɔ monɛ a maŋ la nku a maŋ kɔ lampo be amanshɛrbichalaso (9%), Nku nɛ Gasi be arisiti (9%), Amanshɛrbichalaso pɔtɛ (6%) nɛ Kakɛ (2%) fanɛ kananɛ ku wɔ kekeniso 1 to na.
<<<P>>>
5. Asɔ nɛ a shinɛ amanshɛrbichalso nɛ kakɛ duwɔso 2021 to n ta a kesar amanshɛrbi chalaso tamaso na shi asɔ ere to nna, Koporet Inkam be Lampo, Baasa gbagba be kakɔka be Lampo, Asɔ nɛ baa kur a nyɛ kasawuleto be Aroyalti, Efuli to be asɔ lɔŋɛso be Lampo nɛ Abaru nɛ Abomboŋ be kesa be Lampo ka maŋ fo kananɛ bu daŋ tama.
