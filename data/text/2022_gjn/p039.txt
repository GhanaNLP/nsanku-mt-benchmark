<<<P>>>
Kafɛ be lalaluwe Be Infuleshin: Kede bee ŋini kafɛ to be kufol December be lalaluwe be asɔ be yawu be kedii be kananɛ a sa.
<<<P>>>
Amanshɛrbijiso Be Kejimbaŋso: Kede bee ba saŋɛ nɛ Gominanti ji m baŋso kejimanshɛrbiwol to be amanshɛrbi monɛ Palimɛnt sɔ n wɔtɔ.
<<<P>>>
Frii SHS: Frii SHS (Frii Sinia Hai Sukuru) be asheŋkataso ela Ghana Gominanti be nkre pupɔr monɛ Prɛsidɛnt Nana Akufo-Addo be abani bra September 2017 nɛ ku bee sa JHS be ebiipo kama nɛ e jigi kasɔbii to ekpa nɛ e nyɛ SHS be kasukurubla jaga.
<<<P>>>
Kakɛ: Ade la amanshɛrbi be kechɛto nɛ kafulilɔŋɛ be bechɛtopo bee sa Gominanti nɛ a maŋ daga bu ka naŋ ka n sa bumo.
<<<P>>>
Kakɛ nɛ bu kɔ a sa Gominanti be ashumpkawurbi: Ade la amanshɛrbi monɛ mbra sa ekpa fanɛ bu ka fanɛ Distrit Asɛmbliana be Kɔmɔn Fand (DAKF), Rod Fand, Ghana Edukeshin Tras Fand (GETFUND), Pɛturoliɔm Riletɛd Fand nɛ Nashinal Hɛlf Fand.
<<<P>>>
Gurɔs Dɔmɛsti Purodact (GDP): Kede ela efuli ere to be asɔ nɛ ashuŋ nɛ a bee bra amanshɛrbi kikɛ be kɔnɔ jimanɛ koŋwule ko to.
<<<P>>>
Gurɔs Intanashinal Risɛvs (Amanshɛrbi nɛ efuli ere bee yili efuli pɔtɛ so): Kede la ekpa monɛ efuli na bee wɔrɔ nɛ kumobe efuli pɔtɛana so be amanshɛrbi bee nyɛ eleŋ a tiŋ a tɔ asɔ nɛ ashuŋ efuli pɔtɛana so jimanɛ ko, ku naa chɛto nɛ efuli na be amanshɛrbi be eleŋ bee tiŋ a kasar efuli pɔtɛana peya so.
<<<P>>>
Infuleshin (Amanshɛrbi be yawu be kedii): Kede ela kananɛ asɔ nɛ ashuŋ be yawu bee dii jimanɛ ko.
<<<P>>>
IPEP (Infrastrakcha fɔ Pɔvɛti Eradikeshin Purogram): Infrastrakcha fɔ Pɔvɛti Eradikeshin Purogram (IPEP) na la NPP be kinishipre be apurogram na be kuko nna nɛ kumobe asheŋfɛso la fanɛ bu mur ketir n shin duwɔ beko be keba chɔ beko so, fɔŋfɔŋ ndekarso nɛ mboŋ monɛ a paŋ kinishibuwi ta n laŋɛ kesa bumo asɔ pɔrso monɛ a daga.
<<<P>>>
Amanshɛrbisheŋkpra be eyilikpaana: Ade ela ekpa monɛ baa yiliso a keni kananɛ efuli na be amanshɛrbisheŋ bee nite.
