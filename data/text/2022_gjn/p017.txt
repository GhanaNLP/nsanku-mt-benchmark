17. Ketiŋ n nyɛ kekilgi Ghana Biyɔd Eid nɛ Biyɔnd COVID-19 be Tɔrɔ be nkre na m bɔɔ enɔ so, Gominanti fara kushuŋ nɛ GH¢ 100 biliɔn be Ghana COVID-19 be Alivieshin nɛ Rivaitalaizeshin ɔf Ɛntaprraisi Sɔpɔt Purogram, (Ghanakɛs “Obaatam Pa” Purogram). Kede e naaŋ bra kagbenefuli, kesa eleŋ nɛ nkilgi anyebe efuli ere to ta n laŋɛ ashuŋ be kelɔŋɛ n yili amobe ayaso nɛ kechɛto n sa baasa koko be ashuŋana.

19. Baasa koko nɛ bumobe kɔnɔ beeŋ fo 579,662 nɛ bumo to 547,126 la bedɔpo nɛ 32,536 la baasa nɛ baa shuŋ nɛ adɔjibi la baasa monɛ bu sibɛ bumobe atere n wɔtɔ bedɔɔpo be Ilɛtrɔnik deta besi to fanɛ bu la bedɔɔpo ashi October 2021 to nɛ ku chɛto nɛ bu tiŋ m bɛso m pin bumo a ta anishi a yuu bumo so, kushuŋ nɛnɛ nɛ asheŋ be kebuwito nɛnɛ ashi adɔtoshuŋ be kechɛto be kaplɛa so.
<<<P>>>
20. Gominanti shɛr baasa damta fanɛ mbuibi blapoana, amalo be bedɔpo, chɛbi nɛ kamantosi be bedɔpo nɛ baasa monɛ baa shuŋ nɛ adɔjibi a sha kepin tɔrɔ monɛ ku wɔ bumobe ashuŋ na to, nɛ ku shinɛ bu nase ekpa monɛ baaŋ bulɔso n sa bumo Intrɛs Reti Subsidi (IRS) nɛ ku chɛto n lara amanshɛrbi nɛ bu kɔ a shuŋ na be tɔrɔ. IRS be kechɛto na beeŋ sa kalfato be 50 subsidi ashi mpaŋ be nchu nɛ a deŋi amoso na nɛ baasa monɛ baa shuŋ adɔtoshuŋ e ta n tɔ ashuŋsɔ n shin tii amanshɛrbi monɛ bu kɔ a shuŋ na be ndaŋ so.
