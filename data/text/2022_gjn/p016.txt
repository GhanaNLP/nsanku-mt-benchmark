Amanshɛrbijiso Be Ekpa Naseso
<<<P>>>
15. Nkpal nɛ bu tiŋ n tintɛ amanshɛrbijiso n shin shinɛ amanshɛrbisheŋ malɛ a yili amobe ayaso nɛnɛ so amanshɛrbijiso be ekpaana ko beeŋ deŋi kelɛfaso 2022 be amanshɛrbisheŋ be kafɛto ta n ya fo kumobe kafɛ be mfrinto:
<<<P>>>
a. Gominanti beeŋ chɛso a kuŋ kakɔka be kawol na be ekpa lɛla monɛ ku daga, saŋɛ na so baasa monɛ bu la abani be bshumpo nɛ bu bulɔ ekpa lɛla so n ta bumo ashuŋ to nawule be atere a naaŋ ba wɔ kakɔka be kawol na to;
<<<P>>>
b. Baaŋ nite n keni baasa monɛ baa shuŋ abani be ashuŋ kikɛ 2022 to nɛ kumobe kafɛ be mfrinto nɛ ku shinɛ baasa a baa shuŋ kananɛ a daga bu ka shuŋ nɛ baasa monɛ baa shuŋ abani be ashumkpa nɛ ku tiŋ shinɛ beshumpo na e saaŋ baŋso kananɛ ku daga;
<<<P>>>
c. Ashuŋ pupɔr kama monɛ amobe atere ler 2022 be kejimanshɛrbi na to n ta MDA-ana, MMDA-ana nɛ SOE-ana be kafɛ be bargato to be Adivɔlɔpmɛnt Plan beeŋ bɛ ekpaana nɛ bu nase L. I. 2411 na to, bande kakpa nɛ amobe amanshɛrbi shi nɛ ku tiŋ m bra keshuŋ nɛnɛ nɛ ekpa lɛla monɛ baaŋ bulɔso a pɔr asɔ nɛ kananɛ baasa bee shuŋ a sa efuli na;
<<<P>>>
16. Amanshɛrbijiso be ekpaana monɛ baaŋ bɛso nɛ a bulɔ ekpa lɛla so nɛ ku shinɛ bu sa maŋ ler kafɛ be mfrinto be amanshɛrbisheŋ be keyili nɛnɛ be ekpa lɛla na to be ako nde:
<<<P>>>
• Asɔ monɛ bu nase n kaŋ sɔ n wɔtɔ ashi MDA-ana be Prokiɔmɛnti be Aplan nɛ bu ta n nase PPA be “website” na nawule nɛ baaŋ shuliso fanɛ bu ta n tɔ ashuŋsɔ;
<<<P>>>
• Prokiɔmɛnti be ashumkpa kama monɛ baa sha ketɔ asɔ ta n laŋɛ amanshɛrbisheŋ be kafɛ kama to daga bu ka kɔ amanshɛrbi monɛ bu kuu n sa bumo loŋ be kafɛ na be kejimanshɛrbiwol na to. Kede be boshi, loŋ be asɔ monɛ baa sha na daga a ka shi kakpanɛ amanshɛrbi monɛ baaŋ ta n shuŋ loŋ be kuhuŋ na ka wɔtɔ to nna;
<<<P>>>
• Kushuŋ to be enimu kama maaŋ tiŋ n nyishi enɔ n sa MOU kama nko kesɔnwɔtɔ kama nɛ ku beeŋ ŋini Ghana be gomananti be amanshɛrbi be kelara nɛ e maŋ nyɛ keshuliso ashi Lɔkɔ be Minista kutɔ.
