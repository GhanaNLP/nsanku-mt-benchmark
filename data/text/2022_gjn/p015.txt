Amanshɛrbichalaso be Ekpa monɛ a beeŋ bɛso
<<<P>>>
14. Gominanti beeŋ bra ekpa pupɔr monɛ a beeŋ shinɛ asɔ lɔŋɛso be mboŋ gboŋ e ler anishi n shin shinɛ amobe asɔ lɔŋɛso e tiŋ n kesar efuli ere to nɛ kowuso be abɛto so. Nkpal loŋso ku kii kprɛmɛ nna nɛ kebra mbra be nchɛrga-ana ere:
<<<P>>>
• Nɛ ashungboŋ ere a tiŋ yili amobe ayaso n shin pargato, Gomanati beeŋ shinɛ ashungboŋ kama nɛ a bee lo awajɛ na a ler Vat be keka to fanɛ kananɛ Afrika Tɛkstal Printi-ebi daŋ nyɛ 2019 to na mfɛ anyɔ monɛ a wɔ anishit ere to;
<<<P>>>
• Ku kaŋ shuŋ mfɛ anyɔ nɛ bargato be kamanto, kusɔ monɛ baa tere tempura bɛnchi mak na(efuli pɔtɛana so be yawu be keduwɔso be mbra monɛ bu kraŋ nase na), beeŋ nyɛ kekenito monɛ ku beeŋ shinɛ ku ler anishi a kɔ kayɛlga;
<<<P>>>
• Baaŋ duwɔ shuwa monɛ bu maŋ daŋ ŋelga nɛ ashuwakurpo wurbiana na bee fa be lampo so ashi kalfa to be 3% n kii 1.5% kalfa to;
<<<P>>>
• Ashi January 2022 to, Gominanti beeŋ shinɛ Ghana Revenue Atɔriti-ebi a chɛ MMDA-ana to nɛ bu nase ekpa lɛla koŋwule moso nɛ bumo kikɛ beeŋ bulɔso a sɔ kapitɛ be lampo nɛ ku chɛto n yɛra kapitɛ be lampo be kesɔ anishito nɛ ku ler afuli nɛnɛ;
<<<P>>>
• Gominanti beeŋ lɔŋɛ n keni MDA-ana be afiis nɛ achaji nɛ baa sɔ to nɛ bu fara a ta amo a shuŋ 1st January 2022. Afiis nɛ achaji na beeŋ baa kɔ kananɛ a bee tiiso nko a duwɔso be ekpa monɛ a bee dii nko a duwɔso a bɛ kafɛ na kikɛ be infuleshin be enite so nna;
<<<P>>>
• Efuli ere be kaplɛkama nɛ bu yili ebu a sɔ amanshɛrbi, n ta agbi be mboŋ n daŋɛso, maŋ naa kɔ amanshɛrbi be kesɔ baŋ shi jimanɛ monɛ bu nyishi enɔ n sa kejimanshɛrbiwol ere.
<<<P>>>
• Baaŋ bra “Ilɛtrɔnik Tranzakshin Levi” nko “E-Levi” nɛ ku beeŋ deŋi ilɛtrɔnik be ashuŋ kikɛ so nɛ ku parga lampo be keshewu na to nɛ ku bra bekama monɛ bu maa shuŋ abani be ashuŋ na m ba wɔtɔ.
