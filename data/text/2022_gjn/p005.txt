“A pɔr jimanɛ kikɛ be efuli; amanshɛrbisheŋ be kelɔŋɛ n yili nɛ ashuŋ be kebuwi”
<<<P>>>
NJUWE
<<<P>>>
Tɔrɔ damta nɛ COVID-19 be kulɔ mɔɔso na bra na la kusɔnɛ ku tɔrɔ efuli kama n ta Ghana n daŋɛso. Kadigal nɛ ku tu Ghanaebi ta n laŋɛ baasa be aluwu, asɔbɔaya nɛ ashuŋ be kejajɛ maŋ la asɔ nɛ gominanti be lakal foeso. Ku wɔrɔ ma kagbenefuli nɛ n kaŋɛ fanɛ anyebe kananɛ anye yili ayaso n kɔ kulɔ mɔɔso na daa la kusɔnɛ anye ta gbenshe nɛ kagbenekoŋwule n kɔ nɛ durnyaŋ be efuli kikɛ keni m bulɔ anishi. Gomananti nase ekpa pupɔrana monɛ a beeŋ tiŋi ashuŋana n shin shinɛ anyebe kebawɔtɔ a bɛta n yili fanɛ dra na.
<<<P>>>
N naŋ ta n tiiso, nkpal nɛ anye a tiŋ n nyɛ kulɔ na be tɔrɔ be yulwe nɛ asɔ lɛla nɛ a beeŋ ler kumo to so, Gominanti nɛ H.E. Prɛsidɛnti Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo nyɛ manaŋ n fin ekpa monɛ ku bra GH₵ 100 biliɔn COVID-19 be Alivieshin nɛ Rivaitalaizeshin ɔf Entapraisis be purogram, (GhanaCares “Obaatan Pa” purogram). Kede bra epulto be kayurwushi damta n sa anyebe baasa n shin naŋ shinɛ efuli na fara a yili kumobe ayaso.
<<<P>>>
Ku bulɔ anyebe anishi kananɛ menye kraŋ sɔ Gominanti n ji n kraa bɛ kumo so hali anye ka bee kɔ eleŋso nɛ anye a ler kulɔ mɔɔso na be kurfɛ to. Jimanɛ ere kikɛ to anyebe keduu nchin n yili a sha nɛ anye kraŋ pite fanɛ efuli na la kusɔnɛ ku luri kechɔnkeni to. Fanɛ kananɛ Issiah kaŋɛ na, “Abrikisi na tɔr, ama anyeeŋ pɔr nɛ ajombu monɛ bu lɔŋɛso;bu kuu asikamɔn na n lɛ, ama anyeeŋ ta alarbeŋ n yili amobe ayilikpa”(Isaiah 9:10)
<<<P>>>
Ku la Gominanti be nkre nna fanɛ baaŋ wɔrɔ asɔ kama monɛ bu sa kɔnɔ fanɛ baaŋ wɔrɔ na, nkpal loŋso baaŋ chɛso nɛ bumobe ashuŋ, ashɛŋnaseso nɛ apurogram monɛ a beeŋ shinɛ bu tiŋ n ju m pɛ asɔ monɛ bu da maŋ tiŋ n wɔrɔ na n shin bra kebawɔtɔ lɛla m baa sa efuliebi kikɛ.
<<<P>>>
Kesheŋkreso tinini koŋwule nɛ ku baa la 2022 be Kejimanshɛrbiwol na nɛ kashinteŋto mfɛ asa (3) nɛ a bɛso na ela, kepɔr ashuŋwuraana be efuli monɛ ku beeŋ chɛso a wɔtɔ nɛ ku beeŋ tiŋ m bra amanshɛrbisheŋ be ndaŋ n saa wɔtɔ saŋɛkama n tiŋ m bra ashuŋ ler anishi m ba sa ekama, fɔŋfɔŋ mbiafolbi. Gominanti be ashɛŋkataso beeŋ baa la asɔ nɛ a beeŋ sa nɛ bu tiŋ n chɛ mbiafolbi to nɛ bu fin bumobe kenyi be eyilikpaana n shin ta bumobe eleŋ kikɛ n shuŋ nɛ ku chɛto m bra kebawɔtɔ lɛla nɛ ekama be kenyɛ kesheŋkama nɛ nkpiɛto maŋ wɔtɔ.
<<<P>>>
Nkpal nɛ bu tiŋ n daŋɛ mbiafolbi be ketiŋ n wɔrɔ be kebawɔtɔ nɛ bu nyɛ ashuŋwuraana be gbenshe so, 2022 be Kejimanashɛrbiwol na to, Gominanti beeŋ fara nɛ YouStart purogram na nɛ ku buwi ekpa n sa mpaŋ be kenyɛ n sa mbiafolbi be ashuŋ nɛ bu tiŋ n ta m fara nko n ta m kpaŋɛ bumobe ashuŋ to.
