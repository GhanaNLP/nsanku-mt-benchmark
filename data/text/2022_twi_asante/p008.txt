Sɛdeɛ na Ɔman sikasɛm mu mpontuo nnyinasoɔ no teɛ
<<<P>>>
3. Deɛ ɛdidi soɔ yi yɛ nkontabuo ho amaneɛbɔ a yɛn nsa aka a ɛkyerɛ sɛdeɛ na ɔman sikasɛm teɛ wɔ afe 2021 mu:
<<<P>>>
• Abosome nsia a na ɛdi kan wɔ afe 2021 mu no, ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ nyinaa mu mpontuo duruu ɔha mu nkyɛmu mmiɛnsa ne akyire pɔ mmiɛnsa (Overall real GDP averaged 3.5 %) wɔ ɛberɛ a na saa ɛberɛ korɔ no ara mu wɔ afe 2020 mu no, adwadeɛ mu mpontuo duruu ɔha mu nkyɛmu hwee ne akyire pɔ nson (0.7 % growth);
<<<P>>>
• Adwadeɛ a ɛma yɛnya sika a Fango nka ho (Non-Oil real GDP) kɔɔ anim ɔha mu nkyɛmu ɛnan ne akyire pɔ nwɔtwe (4.8 %) wɔ abosome nsia a ɛdi kan wɔ afe 2021 mu, na saa ɛberɛ korɔ no ara mu wɔ afe 2020 mu no, saa sika yi kɔɔ anim ɔha mu nkyɛmu baako ne akyire pɔ baako pɛ (1.1 %);
<<<P>>>
• Na nneɛma boɔ-sorokɔ wɔ Ɛbɔ bosome awieeɛ yɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu edu ne akyire pɔ nsia (10.6 %), na saa ɛberɛ korɔ no ara wɔ afe 2020 mu no, na nneɛma boɔ-sorokɔ yɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu edu ne akyire pɔ ɛnan (10.4 %);
<<<P>>>
• Sika dodoɔ a na aka mu a yɛhia de asɔ yɛn sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ mu sika nyinaa so (Overall budget deficit -cash) wɔ abosome nsia a ɛdi kan wɔ afe 2021 mu no yɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu nson ne akyire pɔ nson (7.7 % of GDP), na saa ɛberɛ korɔ no ara mu wɔ afe 2020 mu no, na deɛ ɛsi yɛn ani so yɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu nson ne akyire pɔ ɛnan (7.4 % of GDP);
<<<P>>>
• Sika dodoɔ a yɛbɛhia de atua dwumadie ho ɛka (Primary balance) no yɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu mmienu ne akyire pɔ hwee (2.0 % of GDP), na saa ɛberɛ korɔ no ara wɔ afe 2020 mu no, na ɛyɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu baako ne akyire pɔ nson (1.7. %);
<<<P>>>
• Ɛka a na ɔman no de a wode ba ɔman adwadeɛ nyinaa mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu (%age of GDP) a, ɛyɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduoson nson ne akyire pɔ nson (77.7 %) wɔ Ɛbɔ bosome awieeɛ wɔ afe 2021 mu, wɔ ɛberɛ a Ɔpɛnimma bosome baa awieeɛ wɔ afe 2020 mu no, na ɛyɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduoson nsia ne akyire pɔ baako (76.1 %);
<<<P>>>
• Amannɔne Sika Dodoɔ a Ɔman no wɔ (Gross International Reserves) nso kɔduruu Amɛrika sika ɔpepepem dubaako ne ɔpepem aduanan ɛnan (US$11.44 billion) wɔ Ɔsanaa bosome awieeɛ wɔ afe 2021 mu, a ɛbɛtumi akra nneɛma abosome enum ne nnanu na wode toto Ɔpɛnimma bosome awieeɛ wɔ afe 2020 mu ho a, na ɛyɛ Amɛrika sika ɔpepepem nwɔtwe ne ɔpepem aduosia mmienu (US$8.62 billion) a na ɛbɛtumi akra nneɛma abosome ɛnan ne da koro pɛ; ɛnna afei
