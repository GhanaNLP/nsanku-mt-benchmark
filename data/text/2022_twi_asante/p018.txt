Nhyehyɛeɛ A Ɛfa Dwumadie Ho Sikasɛm Ho
<<<P>>>
15. Sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na yɛde sika bɛdi dwuma a ɛhia mapa na aboa ama yɛanya sikasɛm mu ahohyɛsoɔ enti, yɛbɛtu dwumadie ho sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ ho anammɔn wɔ afe 2022 ne mmerɛ kakra a ɛdi yɛn anim yi mu:
<<<P>>>
a. Aban bɛkɔ so abɔ aban adwumayɛfoɔ akatua ho nhyehyɛeɛ no ho ban na yei mu no, yɛbɛhwɛ sɛ aban adwumayɛfoɔ a wɔnam ɛkwan pa so afa wɔn wɔ adwumam no nko ara na wɔde wɔn din bɛkɔ Aban akatua nwoma mu;
<<<P>>>
b. Afe 2022 mu ne mmerɛ kakra a ɛdi yɛn anim yi mu no, yɛbɛyɛ Aban adwumayɛfoɔ dwumadie nnyinasoɔ mu nhwehwɛmu nam so de ahunu adwumayɛfoɔ dodoɔ a wɔn ho bɛhia wɔ Aban nnwuma ahodoɔ mu sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a yɛbɛnya Aban adwumayɛfoɔ dodoɔ a ɛfata na wɔn dwumadie adi mu;
<<<P>>>
c. Mpontunnwuma afoforɔ biara a yɛaka ho asɛm wɔ afe 2022 Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ yi mu sɛ yɛbɛyɛ, ne deɛ Aban Nnwuma (MDAs), Kuropɔn, Nkuro ne Mansini Mmarahyɛbadwa (MMDAs) ne Aban Nnwumakuo ahodoɔ (SOEs) no Animanim Mpontuo Nhyehyɛeɛ (Medium-Term Development Plans) mu no ho anammɔntuo nyinaa a ɛmfa ho ne baabi a dwumadie no ho sika firi no, yɛde bɛfa mmara (L.I. 2411) ɛkwan no so sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a yɛbɛyi sikasɛeɛ akwa na aboa ama ɔman mpontunneɛ ho dwumadie a Aban nnwuma di ma ɔmanfoɔ no adi mu ama mfasoɔ aba ho.
<<<P>>>
16. Yɛbɛdi sikasɛm ho anammɔntuo ahodoɔ a ɛdidi soɔ yi so sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a yɛremfom wɔ mmerɛ kakra a ɛdi yɛn anim yi mu wɔ sikasɛm ahohyɛsoɔ ɛkwan a yɛatwa ato hɔ no:
<<<P>>>
• Nneɛma a Aban Nnwuma agye atom wɔ wɔn Nneɛmatɔ Nhyehyɛeɛ (Procurement Plans) mu a apue edwa wɔ Aban Nneɛmatɔ Mpenesoɔ Adwumakuo (PPA) no abɛɛfo amaneɛbɔbea (website) no nko ara na yɛbɛdi ho dwuma de ama nneɛmatɔ ho kɔntraagye;
<<<P>>>
• Ɛsɛ sɛ Nneɛmatɔ ho abisadeɛ a Aban adwumakuo biara bɛyɛ wɔ afe biara mu no yɛ deɛ wɔatwa ɛho sika ato hɔ wɔ saa afe korɔ no mu Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ mu. Yei da nkyɛn a, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛhunu baabi a saa nneɛmatɔ yi ho sika firi reba ɛnna sika dodoɔ a ɛwɔ hɔ ma saa nneɛmatɔ yi;
<<<P>>>
• Aban adwumam panin biara nni ho ɛkwan sɛ ɔde ne nsa hyɛ Nteaseɛ Apam (MoU) biara ase de kyekyere Ghana Aban wɔ sikasɛm mu wɔ ɛberɛ a ɔnnyee mpenesoɔ mfirii Ɔsoafoɔ a Ɔhwɛ Sikasɛm so no nkyɛn.
