“Yɛrekyekyere Ɔman a Ankorankorɛ Nnwumabue ase bɛtim wɔ mu a ɛbɛfiri Sikasɛm Ntotoeɛ pa ne Nnwumabue ho Nhyehyɛeɛ mu”
<<<P>>>
Nnwuma Nketewa Mu Mpontuo Dwumadie - MSME (Micro And Small Buisness Developmet Programme): Nnwuma Nketewa yɛ ankorankorɛ nnwuma a wɔtumi fa nnipa kakra bi a dwetire a adwuma no gyina soɔ no mmoro sika dodoɔ pɔtee bi so.
<<<P>>>
Ɔman-kyekyere Atuhoakyefoɔ- NABCo (Nation Builders Corps): Yei yɛ adwene foforɔ koraa a Ghana Aban de puee edwa a ɔnam so bɛma mmeranteɛ ne mmabaawa a wɔawie asuapɔn no bɛnya adwuma ayɛ wɔ ankorankorɛ anaa Aban nnwuma mu na wɔaboa de wɔn nimdeɛ apɛ asetenam ɔhaw ahodoɔ nnannuaguberɛ, na yei aboa wɔn ma wɔanya osuahunu a ɛbɛboa Ɔman sikasɛm mpontuo.
<<<P>>>
Ɔman Adwumakuo A Ɛhwɛ Mfidinnwuma Nketwa So- (National Board for Small Scale Industries- NBSSI): Ɔman Adwumakuo a Ɛhwɛ Mfidinnwuma Nketewa so no yɛ ɔman adwumakuo a ɛnyɛ mfasoɔ enti na wɔwɔ hɔ na mmom, ɛyɛ adwumakuo a ɛhyɛ Asoɛeɛ a Ɛhwɛ Edwadie ne Mfidinnwuma so no ase, a mmara ma wɔn ho ɛkwan sɛ wɔnhwɛ sɛ Mfidinnwuma Nketewa ne Adantam (Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) bɛtu mpɔn.
<<<P>>>
Ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ bo a ɛsom a Fango Sika nka ho (Non-Oil Real GDP): Yei yɛ ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ nyinaa bo ɛsom a sika a ɛfiri fango ho dwumadie ahodoɔ mu nka ho bi, a wɔagyina nneɛma boɔ sorokɔ so no asese.
<<<P>>>
Sika a ɛmfiri ɛtoɔ mu (Non Tax Revenue): Yei yɛ Aban sika a ɔnya a ɛmfiri ɛtoɔ mu. Ebi ne sika a obi tua ansa na wanya tumi krataa bi adi dwuma ne sika a yɛtua wɔ nneɛma afoforɔ ho sɛ ebia nsiho ne mfantum ho.
<<<P>>>
Ɛka Ahodoɔ A Aban De (Outstanding Obligations): Ɛka ahodoɔ a Aban de no yɛ sika a ɛsɛ sɛ Asoɛeɛ a Ɛhwɛ Sikasɛm so no tua ma Aban Nnwuma ahodoɔ no ɛnna afei, ɛka ɛda hɔ firi afe 2016 mu a mmara hyɛ sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ Aban tua gu Mmara ho Fotoɔ ahodoɔ mu.
<<<P>>>
Sika Dodoɔ A Aka A Yɛbɛhia De Adi Dwuma (Primary balance): Yei yɛ sika dodoɔ a aka wɔ ɛberɛ a Aban agyegye deɛ n'ani da ɛkwan sɛ ɔbɛnya no nyinaa akyi de adi dwuma, a bosea ho nsiho nka ho bie, (kyerɛ sɛ bosea a Aban bɛtua nka ho). Na ɛkwan a yɛfa so bu yei ho nkonta ne sɛ yɛsese no wɔ Ɔman Adwadeɛ nyinaa bo a ɛsom no mu ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu.
<<<P>>>
Sikasɛm Mpontuo a ɛsi ani soɔ (Projected Growth Rate): Yei yɛ sɛdeɛ ani da ɛkwan sɛ Ghana sikasɛm bɛtu mpɔn wɔ afe pɔtee bi mu.
<<<P>>>
Sika A Aban Nya (Revenue): Yei yɛ sika dodoɔ a Aban nya firi ne dwumadie ahodoɔ a ɔdi wɔ ɔman yi mu ne amannɔne.
<<<P>>>
Mmoayɛn A Ɛde Nnuane ne Nnwuma Ba -RFJ (Rearing For Food and Jobs): Yei yɛ Ghana Aban mpontuo ho dwumadie foforɔ a ɔde aba a ɛnam so bɛma ntɔtemmoayɛn adwuma no adi mu na sika aba mu sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a, ntɔtemmoa nam bɛdɔɔso ama ɔmanfoɔ anya bi awe na yɛate ntɔtemmoa nam a yɛkra ba ɔman yi mu no so koraa, na ɛnam so ama nnwuma abu so a ɔmanfoɔ
