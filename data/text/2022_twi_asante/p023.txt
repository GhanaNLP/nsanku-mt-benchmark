33. Afe 2022-2025 Animanim yi ara mu Ɔman Mpontuo Nhyehyɛeɛ ( Medium-Term National Development Policy Framework (MTNDPF) no na ɛbɛsii nhyehyɛeɛ a wɔato edin sɛ Ghanaman yi mu animanim yi mpontuo botaeɛ (2018-2021) (Ghana’s Medium-Term National Development Policy Framework -2018-2021) no anan mu. Mpontuo ho nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ a ɛdidi soɔ yi ne Nhyehyɛeɛ foforɔ yi nnyinasoɔ: Ghanaman yi Daakye mu Mpontuo Botaeɛ (Long-Term National Development Framework - Ghana@100), Sikasɛm ne Asetenam Mpontuo Nhyehyɛeɛ Ahodoɔ (Coordinated Programme of Economic and Social Development Policies (2017-2024), Mpɛmpɛnsoɔ a Ghana nhia mmoa, (Ghana Beyond Aid) ɛnna GhanaCOVID-19 asetɔre ne ankorankorɛ nnwuma nkanyan ho mmoa (GhanaCARES). Saa nhyehyɛeɛ yi botaeɛ foforɔ ne sɛ ɛbɛma yɛatumi aduru Amanaman Nkabom Ekuo nhyehyɛeɛ a wɔato edin “Agenda 2030” (SDGs) ne Abibirem Nkabom Botaeɛ a wɔato edin “Africa Union Agenda 2063” no ho.

Adwumanya Ho Nhyehyɛeɛ
<<<P>>>
35. Sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na Aban bɛtumi ama ɔmanfoɔ anya nnwuma ayɛ enti no, ɔde nhyehyɛeɛ ne dwuma ahodoɔ bi te sɛ “Ɔmanfoɔ Adwumanya Nhyehyɛeɛ (Ghana Productive Safety Net Project” (GPSNP) agu akwan mu. Dwumadie yi mu no, Aban:
<<<P>>>
i. maa ɔmanfoɔ a wɔwɔ Nkuropɔn, Nkuro, ne Mansini, aduɔwɔtwe (80 MMDAs) a wɔyɛ ahiafoɔ pa ara mu dodoɔ mpem aduasa mmiɛnsa aha nkron aduonum mmienu (33,952 extreme poor persons) nnwuma yɛeɛ na wɔn ho akatua nyinaa dodoɔ som sika ɔpepem aduonum mmiɛnsa ne mpem aduanan mmiɛnsa (GH¢53.43 million total wage payout);
<<<P>>>
ii. Kyekyɛɛ sika dodoɔ ɔpepem enum ne mpem aduɔwɔtwe mmienu GH¢5.82) maa fidua ahodoɔ ɔpepem nkron ahanu ne adunan ɛnan (9,244) a wɔwɔ mpɔtam ahodoɔ ɔha ne aduonu nwɔtwe mu (128 communities) wɔ Nkuropɔn, Nkuro ne Mansini aduanan baako (41 MMDAs) a ɛwɔ Atifi Amantam enum mu sɛ wɔmfa nhyɛ nnwuma ase anaa wɔmfa ntrɛ wɔn nnwuma no mu.
<<<P>>>
36. Aban nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛwɔ hɔ sɛ ɔbɛma mmeranteɛ ne mmabaawa adi dwuma ahodoɔ, nam so de ate kuro kɛseɛm atukɔtena (rural-urban) so, na ama wɔn asetena mu atu mpɔn enti no, ɔnam nnuadua, kookoo mfuo so adupeteɛ, ɔyɛasaseyie-peteɛ, nnuabaguo, kookoo nnua mman a yadeɛ aka no ho twitwa ne kookoo mfuo-so-nsusuguo so maa nnipa dodoɔ mpem ɔha ne aduasa nyaa nnwuma yɛeɛ (130,000 jobs were created).
