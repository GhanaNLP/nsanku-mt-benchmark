1. Afe 2022 Ɔman Mma Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ yi yɛ dwuma a Aban dii wɔ 2021 mu no ho tɔfabɔ ne sika dodoɔ a Aban hwɛ anim sɛ ɔbɛnya de atu ne nhyehyɛeɛ ne ne dwumadie ahodoɔ ho anammɔn wɔ afe 2022 mu ne mmerɛ kakra a ɛdi yɛn anim yi mu.
<<<P>>>
2. Sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na anammɔn a yɛretu ama asetenam ne sikasɛm mpontuo asane apagya na ɔman mpontuo botaeɛ ahodoɔ abam no enti, afe 2022 Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ yi botaeɛ ne sɛ yɛbɛfa Sikasɛm Ntotoeɛ pa so bɛkyekyere Ɔman a Ankorankorɛ Nnwumabue ase bɛtim wɔ mu na Nnwuma abu so ama ɔman mma anya bi ayɛ.
