67. Afe 2020/21 kuayɛ ɛberɛ a akɔ awieeɛ yi mu no, kookoo dodoɔ a yɛteeɛ kɔɔ soro boroo deɛ yɛate pɛn nyinaa so kɔduruu tɔnn ɔpepem baako, ne mpem aduanan enum ne ahanum (1,045,500 tonnes) . Yɛpagyaa sika a Aban de tɔ kookoo mu kɔduruu ɔha nkyɛmu aduonu nwɔtwe ma ɛkɔduruu sidi mpem edu ahanum aduasa (GH¢10,560) tɔnn baako biara wɔ afe 2019/20 kuayɛ ɛberɛ no mu na ɛboɔ no kɔɔ so gyinaa saa ara wɔ afe 2021/22 kuayɛ ɛberɛ no mu mpo wɔ ɛberɛ a na kookoo ɛboɔ aba fam wɔ ewiase nyinaa edwaman so.

68. Wɔde Mmoayɛn Nhwehwɛmu Beaeɛ a ɛfa nkokɔ a yɛbɛwe (ARIBRO) (Animal Research Institute Broiler) ho dwumadie baeɛ no, ɛberɛ a ɛdi kan nie a CSIR de brɔla nkokɔ abedeɛ ne anini apue edwa. Wɔyɛn Nkokɔbedeɛ dodoɔ a na wɔadi nnawɔtwe enum apem baako ne ahasa ne nkokɔnini a wɔbɛforo abedeɛ no ahasa a na wɔadi nnawɔtwe nson. Bio, wɔhwɛ dii abɛ nkumaa a yɛtu tim dodoɔ ɔpepem nsia ne mpem aduonu mmiɛnsa ahankron aduasa (6,023,930 oil palm germinated seeds) a wɔbɛdua wɔ “Kuayɛ mu Nnɔbaeɛ a yɛde kɔ Amannɔne kɔtɔn ne Nkuraaseɛ Mpontuo dwumadie (PERD) no mu no ho dwuma ɛnna wɔtɔnn abɛ nkumaa a wɔtu tintim no mu dodoɔ mpem aduonum enum ahanson aduonu nson (55, 727 oil palm seedlings) ne kube a yɛtu tim dodoɔ mpem aduasa ɛnan ahanu aduonum mmienu (34,252 coconut seedlings) maa akuafoɔ.
