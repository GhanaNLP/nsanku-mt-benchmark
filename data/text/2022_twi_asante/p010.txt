Sika A Ɛbaa Ɔman Fotoɔ Mu
<<<P>>>
4. Sika a Aban gyegyee ne deɛ wɔde kyɛɛ ɔman no firi Ɔpɛpɔn kɔsi Ɛbɔ bosome mu wɔ afe 2021 mu no nyinaa kɔsii ɔpepepem aduanan nsia ne ɔpepem aduɔwɔtwe enum (GH¢46.85 billion) a ɛyɛ ɔman mu adwadeɛ nyinaa mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu edu ne akyire pɔ nson (10.7% of GDP), na yɛyɛɛ dwumadie mu nsakraeɛ no, na sika dodoɔ a yɛde yɛn ani asi so sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ yɛnya no yɛ ɔpepepem aduonum baako ne ɔpepem aduasa baako (GH¢51.31 billion) a ɛyɛ ɔman mu adwadeɛ nyinaa mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu dubaako ne akyire pɔ nson (11.7% of GDP). Sika dodoɔ a ɛbaa ɔman fotoɔ mu no na ɛdidi soɔ yi: Ɛtoɔ a ɛmfiri fango dwumadie ho sika mu (Non-oil Tax Revenue) a ɛyɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduoson ɛnan (74%), sika a ɛmfiri fango mu na ɛnyɛ ɛtoɔ sika (Non-oil-non-Tax Revenue) a ɛyɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu nkron (9%), sika a ɛfiri Fango ne Ogyaframa mu (Oil and Gas Receipts) a ɛyɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu nkron (9%), sika afoforɔ (other Revenue) a ɛyɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu nsia (6%) ɛnna sika a wɔde kyɛɛ ɔman no (Grants) a ɛyɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu mmienu (2%) sɛdeɛ yɛada no adi wɔ Hyehyɛpono 1 mu no.
<<<P>>>
5. Afe 2021 Sika dodoɔ a Aban gyegyee no annuru deɛ yɛde sii yɛn ani so sɛ yɛn nsa bɛka no ɛsiane sɛ Nnwumakuo Mfasoɔ ho Ɛtoɔ (Corporate Income Tax), Ankorankorɛ Sika ho Ɛtoɔ (Personal Income Tax), Fagudeɛ ho Ɛtoɔ, Ɔman yi mu Adwadeɛ ho Ɛtoɔ (Domestic Excise Duty) ne Nkratoɔ Dwumadie ho Ɛtoɔ (Communications Service Tax) nyinaa so teeɛ.
