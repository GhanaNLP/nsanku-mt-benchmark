Aban ayɛ adwene sɛ sika dodoɔ a ɔde bɛdi dwuma (a ɛka a yɛbɛtua ka ho bi) bɛduru Ghana sika sidi ɔpepepem ɔha aduasa nson ne ɔpepem aduonum mmiɛnsa (GH¢137.53 billion) a ɛyɛ ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduonu nson ne akyire pɔ ɛnan (27.4 % of GDP). Nneɛma a ɛbɛdi akotene wɔ Aban dwumadie mu no ne Mpontunneɛ ho dwumadie, Aban dwumadie atitire, adwumayɛfoɔ akatua ɛnna bosea ho nsiho-tua.
