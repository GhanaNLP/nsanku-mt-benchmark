53. Ɛduruu Ɛbɔ bosome da a ɛtɔ so aduasa wɔ afe 2021 mu no, na wɔatwerɛ Ghanafoɔ dodoɔ ɔpepem dunum, mpem ahansia aduonum nsia, ɔha ne aduosia (15,656,160 Ghanaians) edin wɔ Ɔmanbayɛ ho Adansedie Nwoma (National Identity Register (NIR) no mu, na wɔmaa saa nnipa yi mu dodoɔ ɔpepem dubaako, mpem ahansia aduanan nwɔtwe ne ahasa aduɔkron baako (11,648,391 Ghanaians) a na wɔadi boro mfeɛ dunum nyinaa Ɔman Kaade no bi (Ghana Cards). Bio, yɛtwerɛɛ amannɔnefoɔ dodoɔ mpem ɔha aduosia mmiɛnsa ne ahansia aduɔkron enum (163,695 foreigners) edin memaa wɔn ahɔhoyɛ ho adansedie kaade (Non-Citizen Identity Cards).
