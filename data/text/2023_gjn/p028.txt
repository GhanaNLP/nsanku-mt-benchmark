55. 2022 to, Adistriti Wata Sɔplai Sikim Anu nɛ a wɔ Volta Rigin (Phase 3), nɛ baa tere kumo STRABAG Wata Projɛti nɛ ku bee yɛra nchu Adaklu Anyigbe; Agotime-Ziope; Central Tongu; Ho West nɛ North Tongu Adistriti na luweto n ya fo 95 kalfa to. Ku kaŋ luwe cheche, nde 424 nɛ baasa nɛ baaŋ baŋso 234,000 e naaŋ ji projɛti na be tɔnɔ.

56. Gomananti beeŋ chɛso nɛ tekunɔ be kekuŋ be ashuŋ n tiŋ kuŋ achinakpa nɛ a mata teku ashi tekunɔ be kasawule be kewe nɛ nchu be kebulɔ n kaa kuŋ baasa be efute, ashuŋ nɛ mpitɛ ashi teku be keda be kaplɛa. Dansoman (97%), Komenda (95%), Elmina Phase III (92%), Cape Coast (80%) nɛ Anomabu (75%), Dixcove (40%) Aboadze Phase II (45%) nɛ Ningo-Prampram (50%) be tekunɔ be kekuŋ be aprojɛti na kra la asɔ nɛ baa shuŋ nɛ a baa luweto.
