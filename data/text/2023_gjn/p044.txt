1D1F (Distrit koŋwule, Faktɔri koŋwule): Kepɔr afaktɔtri nɛ indastriana monɛ amoalɛ beeŋ, ŋin efuli na a yɔ kinishibuwi be efuli nɛ ku kɔ ashungboŋ damta. Kumobe tama ela fanɛ ku bra ashuŋ damta m ba sa Ghana-ebi kikɛ efuli na to.

Gurɔs Dɔmɛsti Purodact (GDP): Kede ela efuli ere to be asɔ nɛ ashuŋ nɛ a bee bra amanshɛrbi kikɛ be kɔnɔ jimanɛ koŋwule ko to.

Gurɔs Intanashinal Risɛvs (Amanshɛrbi nɛ efuli ere bee yili efuli pɔtɛ so): Kede la ekpa monɛ efuli na bee wɔrɔ nɛ kumobe efuli pɔtɛana so be amanshɛrbi bee nyɛ eleŋ a tiŋ a tɔ asɔ nɛ ashuŋ efuli pɔtɛana so jimanɛ ko, ku naa chɛto nɛ efuli na be amanshɛrbi be eleŋ bee tiŋ a kasar efuli pɔtɛana peya so.

Hɛdilaiyi Infuleshin: Kumo ela infuleshin be kebi monɛ bu maŋ daŋ tiseto nɛ “Consumer Price Index” (CPI) be kushuŋ na lara efuli nɛ Bureau ɔf Leba Sitatitis (BLS) bee lara efuli kufol kama.

Impɔti Sabtutushin: Ku la ekpa monɛ bu wɔrɔ nɛ ku bee ŋini kananɛ baaŋ ta efulito be asɔwɔrɔso n talato efuli pɔtɛso be asɔklaso nɛ ku chɛto nɛ efuliso be ashungboŋ a nyɛ eyilikpa lɛla.

Ifuleshin (Amanshɛrbi be yawu be kedii): Kede ela kananɛ asɔ nɛ ashuŋ be yawu bee dii jimanɛ ko to.
