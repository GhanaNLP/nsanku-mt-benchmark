78. Gomananti beeŋ bulɔ Ghana Tɔɔrisim Atɔriti (GTA), so n leŋto kenishipreso kelaŋɛ baasa be mfɛra n yɔso be program na so ta n laŋɛ “See Ghana, Eat Ghana”, Wɛɛ Ghana, nɛ Fiil Ghana be kampein na so nɛ ku leŋ baasa to nɛ bu baa sha kewɔrɔ Ghana to be asɔ ashi Tɔɔrizim be kushun na to.
<<<P>>>
79. Gomannti beeŋ wɔrɔ shawɔra nɛ ahotɛlebi ajibifapogbonwuraana nɛ bu baa fa Ghana to be ajibi nɛ anuuso, bu baa shuŋ nɛ Ghana be asɔ shelso, asɔ chinaso so nɛ asɔ monɛ baa ta a shiga nko a ta a lɔŋɛ bumobe mboŋ nɛ ku chɛto nɛ bu kuuso amo nɛ baa kla a ta a shuŋ bumobe kaba na.
<<<P>>>
80. Nkpal Gomananti ka bee sha nɛ ku niŋ amanshɛrbi be kebawɔtɔ to n naa sha baasa be kechɛto nɛ kasheŋkeni be mboŋ a lɔŋɛ so, GTA nɛ “Ghana Investment Promotion Centre” (GIPC) beeŋ ta amu n gbasa abar n shin nase keleŋto be asɔ nɛ a beeŋ shinɛ efuli ebi nɛ efuli pɔtɛso ebi e bra bumobe kechɛto.

82. Ghana CARES be Phase 2 na to, Gomananti beeŋ lara mboŋ ko ashi bumonɛ bu buwi bumo gbagba be ashuŋ be kaba na so n sa bumo kechɛto nɛ ku leŋ bumo to nɛ bu baa kesar abar a duwɔ asɔklaso so a talato nɛ efuliso be asɔlɔŋɛso nɛ efuli pɔtɛso be kasɔfa be kedaŋɛso nɛ ku wɔrɔ ekpa lɛlaana monɛ a beeŋ chɛ ashuŋ na to nɛ a baa tiŋ a wɔrɔ loŋ be asɔ.N naŋ ta n tiiso, Gomananti beeŋ buwi ekpa n sa nɛ ashuŋ nɛ a tiya wɔtɔ nɛ amo nɛ bu kraa sha kebuwi a bir abarso n wɔrɔ kɔnɔ konwule n chɛto nɛ loŋ be ashuŋ na e paragato, lɔŋɛ n yili ayaso nɛ kepɔr ashuŋ nɛ a bee lɔŋɛ asɔ nɛ a baa tiŋ a lɔŋɛ asɔ monɛ bu wu fanɛ baasa bee nyɛ a ya ka fa efuli pɔtɛana so na.
