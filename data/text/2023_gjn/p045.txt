IPEP (Infrastracha fɔ Pɔvɛti Eradikeshin Purogram): Kede la NPP be gomananti be kechɛto be program nɛ kumobe nia la kemur kitir n shin duwɔ buko ka kɔ a chɔ buko be kebawɔtɔ so, fɔŋfɔŋ ndekarso nɛ mboŋ nɛ bu paŋ cheche be kesa asɔpɔrso monɛ a beeŋ chɛ bumobe kebawɔtɔ to.

Amanshɛrbisheŋkpra be atamaso: Ade ela ekpa monɛ baa yiliso a keni kananɛ efuli na be amanshɛrbisheŋ bee nite. Amobe ako ela amanshɛrbisheŋ kataso be ndaŋ, asɔ be yawu be kedii, ashuŋ be kenyɛ be nchɛrga, efuli pɔtɛana be kayawuji, kananɛ gomanati be amanshɛrbisheŋ be ekpaaana bee yɔ anishito nko bee laŋɛ kaman nɛ Ghana kikɛ be banki be amanshɛrbi be kananɛ a bee nite efuli na be amanshɛrbisheŋ be ekpa so.
<<<P>>>
Memorandum of Understanding (MoU): Ku la kenu nsa abar gbagba nna nɛ ku chaŋ nkre n nase nɛ ku wɔ baasa be ntuŋ anyɔ nko ntuŋ damta be mfrinto nɛ bu bulɔso n wɔrɔ kesheŋ.
<<<P>>>
Modul: Ku la kaba ko nna nɛ ku barga ashi mba be ntuŋ ko to nɛ a beeŋ tiŋ n chɛ abarso n kii kegboŋi.
