Sɛ yɛkenga Bible ne daa a, ɔboka yɛ maa yenya akokoduro
(Nea ngyekyɛmuɛ 10)
<<<P>>>
yé a, odikyɛ ɔbɔ yenwo mmɔden maa ye dwudwolɛ yɛ fɛ, ɔbɔ bra pá, ɔdɔ mmenia, onya gyidie, na ɔmaa ye nwo te. (1 Tim. 4:12, 15) Sɛ yɛyɛ daa asɛm daa biala a, ɔboka yɛ maa yenwu bɔ yɛyɛ a, yɛ soen kɔsɔ Yehowa nye, na yahɔ yɛ nyunu wɔ ye soen nu ɔ. Ebie bɔ Yehowa soen nwo hia ye ne, ɔmaa Yehowa sunzum krongron ne kyire ye atee. Ɛhe maa ɔkora fa Yehowa akwangyerɛ bɔ ye bra.—Rom. 8:5, 6.

12 Sɛ yɛyɛ daa asɛm ne daa biala a, okosiesie yɛ kɔma adwuma biala bɔ asafo ne kɔfa kowura yɛ sa ne. Adwuma pee yɛɛ ɔwɔ Yehowa asafo nanu ɔ. Yeti yehia mmrienzua ne mmrasua bɔ bɛkɔyɛ sɔ adwuma ne ɔ. Nakoso odikyɛ bɛyɛ mmenia bɔ bɛne Yehowa afĩa te kama. Yehia mmenia maa bɛsõ kyɛ ateepakyelɛfoɛ, nzɛmbatrɛlɛfoɛ, ɔne bɛbɔ bɛkɔyɛ adwuma wɔ Bɛtɛl. Afei ebiemɔ koso kosisi asafo sua, ɔne bɛbɔ bɛkɔyɛ adwuma wɔ nekaa bɔ bekyire dwudwolɛ bo ɔ. Ebieku koso, yehia mmrandeɛ bɔ bɛbrɛ bɛnwo ase maa bɛsõ kyɛ asafo nu asomvoɛ, mbanyi, ɔne manzini soneafoɛ. Yehowa koso de, mmenia bɔ okuro kyɛ bedi ye asafo ne nyunu ne, yɛɛ ole bɛbɔ bedi nahorɛ, na yɛfa yɛnwo yɛto bɛ so a ɔyɛ yé ɔ. Afei odikyɛ bɛyɛ mmrienzua bɔ bɛfa Bible nu dwirɛ bɛbɔ bɛbra ɔ. (Deut. 1:13-15) Sɛ ɛyɛ daa asɛm daa biala a, ɔkɔboka wɔ maa ɛkɔte Bible nanu dwirɛ nabo yé. Ɔba sɔ a, okosiesie wɔ, na sɛ asafo ne ma wɔ adwuma biala a, ɛkɔhora ye yɛ.

13 Yeliemamɔ bɔ bɛyɛ adwuma wɔ Bɛtɛl ne, kora bɔ bɛkɔhyɛ adwuma bo ne, bɛ bɔ nu bɛyɛ daa asɛm. Bɛhyɛ yebo a, bedimoa bɛbɔ mbaeɛ na basusu daa asɛm nanwo. Wɔ koso ɛ, atee beni yeɛ ɛkɔhora kɔfa so kɔyɛ wɔ daa asɛm ne ɔ? Ɛkɔhora kɔbɔ mbaeɛ kɔhyɛ yebo, na asrɛ Yehowa kyɛ, ɔboka wɔ ma ɛte bɔ ɛbaasua ne bo. (Yak. 1:5) Afei kenga tworɔnzɛm ne, ɔne ngyerɛkyiremuɛ bɔ ɔboka so ne. Eyie a, bisa wɔnwo kyɛ: ‘Ɛnnɛ daa asɛm ne, sɛɛ yeɛ ɔkɔboka me maa me gyidie kɔyɛ se ɔ? Sɛɛ yeɛ ɛhe kɔmaa ngopingye Yehowa ɔ? Sɛ meyia ɔhaw bie koso a, sɛɛ yeɛ ɛhe kɔboka me maa ngogyina ye noa ɔ?’ Sɛ ɛne w’abusuafoɛ anaa wɔ damvomɔ yeɛ ɛmɔ bɔ nu bɛyɛ daa asɛm ne a, bɔ ebiala hyɛle ye nzorɛ ne, ɛmɔbɔ yenwo ngɔmmɔ na
