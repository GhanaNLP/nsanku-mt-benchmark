Sona bɔ ɔtte boniayɛ ne, daa ole anigye
<<<P>>>
(Nea ngyekyɛmuɛ 1)
<<<P>>>
bɛ a, bɛngae po né bala ye ase. Nakoso mme-ni pee wɔ berɛ a, benwu ye kyɛ sɛ ebie yɛ bɛ pá a, ɔte sona ne asɛdeɛ kyɛ ɔyɛ sɔ. Ebiemɔ koso nwu ye kyɛ bɛ bɔbɔ yee bafɛ banya ye ɔ, yeti yenwo nhia kyɛ bɛkɔda ebie ase. Sɛ ebie yɛ sɔ a, okyire kyɛ ɔte pɛsɛmangomenya.
<<<P>>>
3 Yehowa asomvoɛ de, sɛ ebie yɛ nikyee ma yɛ a, yɛnye sɔ paa, ofikyɛ yetie afutue bɔ Pɔɔ-lo famane yɛ ne. Ɔhane kyɛ: “Nnɛma ɛmɔyɛ boniayɛ.” (Kol. 3:15) Amba, sɛ ebie yɛ nikyee ma yɛ a, ɛyɛ a ne ɔyɛ yɛ fɛ. Nakoso nzu yee yɛ-kɔyɛ yɛkɔfa yekohyire kyɛ yɛnye sɔ ɔ? Adesua he anu ne, yekosusu nningyein nza bɔ ɔkɔbo-ka yɛ nwo. (1) Nzuati yee ɔnzɛkyɛ yɛyɛ bonia-yɛ ɔ? (2) Bɔ yele ye ne, yɛkɔyɛ sɛɛ né yahyire kyɛ yɛnye sɔ? (3) Nzu yee yɛyɛ a, okyire kyɛ yɛtte boniayɛ ɔ?
<<<P>>>
NZUATI YEƐ ƆNZƐKYƐ YƐYƐ BONIAYƐ Ɔ?
<<<P>>>
4 Sɛ ebie te boniayɛ a, opira ye bɔbɔ. Ebie bɔ ɔte boniayɛ de, daa olle anigye. Nzuati ɔ? An-yandera 15:15 kã kyɛ: “Sona bɔ onwu amanee ne, kyẽa biala ɔtte mayee mma ye.” Ɛhe maa yenwu kyɛ ɔhaw bɔ yɛfa nu ne, ɔkora bubu yɛ. Nahorɛ dwirɛ yee ole kyɛ, nna daa namu yee yɛ haw ne kɔhora kofi berɛ ɔ. Nakoso ɔba sɔ a, kyɛbɔ yɛkɔyɛ yɛ nikyee ne, yenwo hia paa. Yeti sɛ yamva yɛ adwene yangɔ yɛ haw naso, na yɛdwenedwene nningyein pa nwo a, ɔkɔ-boka yɛ. Nakoso sɛ yɛyɛ boniayɛ, na sɛ yam-va yɛnye yando bɔ Yehowa yɛ ma yɛ ne a, yɛ-nye ngɔgye. Na annea a, né wamaa yɛ haw ne ayɛ piri.
<<<P>>>
5 Ebie nye sɔ bɔ ole ye a, ɔmaa yenye gye. Nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ sona bɔ ɔte sɔ ne, ɔmva ye adwene nzie ye haw naso, na mmom nikyee bɔ ɔkɔmaa yenye kɔgye ne, yee ɔfa y'adwene kɔ so ɔ. Yeti, ɛyɛ a ne wayɛ kyɛ olle haw biala ne. Sɔ yee Anyandera 15:15 ne maa yenwu ye ɔ. Ɛberɛ nwa: “Bɔ ye nye agye de, mmerɛ biala te ɛpoentolɛ ma ye.” Ɔte “ɛpoentolɛ” amba, ofikyɛ ɔmva yenwo ne bɔ sona ne fa nu ne, sɛ ɔtte boniayɛ a, ɔmaa ye ahone tɔ ye kunu.
