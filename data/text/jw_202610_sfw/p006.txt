DWEIN 46
Yɛda Wo Ase, Yehowa
<<<P>>>
BƆ ADESŨA NE KƆHÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Yekonwu bɔ ɔkɔboka yɛ na yannyɛ boniayɛ, ɔne bɔ yɛkɔyɛ yɛkɔfa yekohyire kyɛ yɛtte boniayɛ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Ɛkɔyɛ Sɛɛ Na Anyɛ Boniayɛ?
<<<P>>>
“Nnɛma ɛmɔyɛ boniayɛ.”—KOL. 3:15.
<<<P>>>
JOHN* te aliemaa bɔ wanya afoɛ 95. Na ɔnate kyɛ kesaalae wanyi nati, wayɛ mmerɛ, afei anwonyerɛ koso teetee ye. Daabiala, sɛ ebie basra ye, anaa sɛ sona ne yɛ nikyee kãa bie ma ye po a, né yenye agye. Yeti ɛyɛ a ɔkã kyire sona ne kyɛ, “Meda wɔ ase.” Oyie a, né wahworɔ ye dumaa wagua ye buuk kaamba bie nu. Afei odwu nɔsoa na ɔkwaala a, ɔkã kyire bɔ ɔnea ye ne kyɛ, ɔsɔ yenu maa obu ndwerɛ wɔ ye mba ne nyunu, na ɔbɔ mbaeɛ. Afei ɔda Yehowa ase kyɛ wamaa aliemaamɔ aaboka ye ɔ. Bɛbɔ bɛnea ye nanu kõ hane kyɛ: “Bɔ John fa nu amukoraati si ne, ɔmaa yenye sɔ bɔ Yehowa ne aliemaamɔ yɛ ma ye ne, yeti daa yenye gye.” John dwirɛ ne, ɔmaa yenwu kyɛ, sona bɔ ɔtte boniayɛ ne, daa ole anigye.
<<<P>>>
2 Bible ne kã kyɛ, ayieleɛ mmerɛ nanu ne, subaen kõ bɔ okobu so yeɛ ole boniayɛ. (2 Tim. 3:1, 2) Na amba-ne-amba, bɔ ɔyɛ yea paa yeɛ ole kyɛ, ɛnnɛ ɛkɔ nekaa biala a, mmenia lle ayɛ. Ɔwɔ nu, ebiemɔ wɔ berɛ a, aworafire ti ebie yɛ nikyee ma
