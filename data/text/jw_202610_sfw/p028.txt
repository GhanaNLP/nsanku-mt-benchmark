Sɛ ɛ́yɛ daa asɛm ne a, nzu yee ɛkɔyɛ na amma hwee andwetwe wɔ ɔ? (Nea ngyekyɛmuɛ 18)
<<<P>>>
ɛmɔfa bɛhyɛ ɛmɔ nwo ngunaen. (Rom. 1:12) Bɔ aliemaa biemɔ yɛ ɛhe: beyie bɛ daa asɛm a, bɛye edwirɛ porɛ nza anaa nna bie befi nu, na bafa bagua betia nu, afei bɛdwenedwene yenwo wɔ kyẽa nanu.
<<<P>>>
14 Atee foforɛ beni yeɛ yɛkɔhora yɛkɔfa so yɛkɔyɛ yɛ daa asɛm ɔ? Nea sɛ ɛkɔhora kɔfa ɛhemɔ nu bie kɔyɛ adwuma a: (1) Edwirɛ biemɔ bɔ ahyɛ ye nzorɛ wɔ daa asɛm nanu ne, pɛ yebo na dwenedwene yenwo. (2) Kenga tworɔnzɛm nemɔ bɔ ɔwɔ nu ne. (3) Tworɔnzɛm bɔ daa asɛm ne gyi so ne, kenga ye ngyekyɛmuɛ bɔ waha ne mukoraati. (4) Fa Nhoma a Yehowa Adansefo De Yɛ Nhwehwɛmu ne sua pee boka ye. (5) Ɛwɛnelɛ Waen adesua bɔ bɛyele daa asɛm ne befili nu ne, kenga nu dwirɛ ne bie boka so. Sɛ ɛfa fon anaa tablɛt yeɛ ɛyɛ daa asɛm ne a, ekonwu Ɛwɛnelɛ Waen bɔ bɛyele daa asɛm ne befili nu nanwo link wɔ yebo. Emia so a, ɔkɔbukye Ɛwɛnelɛ Waen ne kɔma wɔ.*

so. Sɛ minit 5 po yeɛ mefa me boka ye a, ɔmaa meto meboase me kenga nu. Afei me kenga tworɔnzɛm bɔ daa asɛm ne gyi so ne, ɔne ngyerɛkyiremuɛ ne. Ɛhe boka me maa nu dwirɛ ne ka me tianu.”

16 Mmerɛ beni yeɛ ɛkɔhora kɔyɛ wɔ daa asɛm ne ɔ? Sɛ ɔba mmerɛ bɔ ɛkɔfa kɔyɛ daa asɛm ne de a, ogyi mmerɛ bɔ ɔkɔboka ɛne wɔ abusua naso. Bɔ ebiemɔ yɛ ɛhe: Kora bɔ bekodidi ngyerɛmɔ anaa bedidi beyie a, né bayɛ bɛ daa asɛm. Ebiemɔ koso, bɛne bɛ mmaamɔ bɔ nu yɛ daa asɛm kora ne bafa bɛ bahɔ sukuu. Bɔ ɔkɔyɛ na bakae ye ne, bɛfa daa asɛm ne bɛto bɛ table so. Ɔwɔ nu, mmerɛ bɔ ɛfa yɛ wɔ daa asɛm ne, nikyee bie kɔhora kɔɔbɔ nu. Nakoso ɔba sɔ a, ɛkɔhora kɔyɛ ye mmerɛ foforɛ wɔ sɔ kyẽa nala nu.
