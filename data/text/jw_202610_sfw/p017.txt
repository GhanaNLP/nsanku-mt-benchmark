okyire kyɛ amba yɛsõ Nyameɛ. Yeti bɛyɛ ni-kyee pá a, ɛmɔma yɛkamvo bɛ, na yɛyɛ bɔ yɛ-kɔhora biala kyɛ yɛne bɛ kɔtena asomdwee nu. (Mak. 9:50) Sɛ yɛmaa aliemaamɔ nwu kyɛ yɛdɔ bɛ a, okyire kyɛ anwanwadeɛ bɔ Ye-howa yɛ ye wɔ asafo nanu ne, yɛboka maa ɔyɛ yé.

MA ƆYƐ WƆ FƐ KYƐ ƐYƐ W’AFANU DEƐ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
17 Bɔ ɔkɔso wɔ asafo nanu ne, ɔmaa ye-nwu kyɛ ɛnnɛ Yehowa té yɛ anwanwadeɛ. Ɔwɔ nu mmenia tane yɛnye, nakoso yasone pee mbaen. Mmenia afĩa ndwo wɔ wiase nanu, koso yɛ de yahã yabonu, na yɛte berɛ kama. Ewiase he anu de, ebie ngyɛke ebie nako-so yɛdɔ Yehowa, na yɛdodɔ yɛnwo. Ɛhe maa
<<<P>>>
ebiala nwu kyɛ yɛte Yesu asuafoɛ. (Yoh. 13:34) Nna kyɛ yabobɔ yɛ sa yagua yɛ hue so yeɛ yɛnea Yehowa maa ɔyɛ ɛhe mukoraati, na mmom yɛ koso yɛyɛ yɛ fanu deɛ, ofikyɛ ‘yɛne ye yeɛ yɛyɛ adwuma ɔ.’—1 Kor. 3:9.
<<<P>>>
18 Kyɛbɔ nningyein ngɔ yé wɔ wiase he anu nati, sɛ ebie hãne kyɛ Yehowa mmenia bɔ bɛwɔ aseɛ ye aso kɔsone pee mbaen, bɛkɔhã bɛkɔbɔ nu, na badodɔ bɛnwo a, ahaa nwa yeɛ okole koli ɔ? Nakoso bɔ ɔkɔso hɔ ɛne, na ɔyɛ nwanwa. Ɛhe maa yenya gyidie kyɛ bɔ Yeho-wa nwa ɔkɔyɛ ye kyẽabie ne koso, bɔ ɔte biala ɔkɔwa nu. (2 Pet. 3:13) Yeti daa biala ma ɔyɛ wɔ fɛ kyɛ anwanwadeɛ bɔ Yehowa yɛ ne, ɛbo-ka maa ɔyɛ yé ɔ.
