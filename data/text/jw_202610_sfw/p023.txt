20 Adamvoa pá de, ɔte akyɛdeɛ bɔ ofi Yehowa berɛ ɔ. Yeti sɛ wɔ nnamvo yeɛ ole bɛbɔ bɛdɔ Yehowa a, yede né ayie koraa. Nnamvoa sɔ mɔ yeɛ yehia ɔ. Ɛhe ati, mmenia bɔ bɛkɔboka wɔ maa ɛkɔyɛ bɔ Yehowa kuro ne, fa wɔnwo bɔ bɛ. Ɛyɛ sɔ a né esuesua Daniel. Ɛnea wʼasafo nu a, ebiemɔ wɔ berɛ bɔ bɛte kyɛ Yeremia anaa? Yede fa bɛ adamvoa, na ekosua nningyein pee kofi bɛ berɛ. Sɛ nningyein ngɔ yé a, ka kyire aliemaamɔ kyɛ bɛbɔ mbaeɛ bɛma wɔ. Kae kyɛ wɔ damvo paa yeɛ ole Yehowa, na ɔngɔye yenye ngɔma wɔ lle. (Heb. 13:5) Yehowa kɔboka yɛ maa yɛkɔhõ yekotia sɔhwɛ biala bɔ yekoyia ɔ. Afei okosa kɔboka yɛ maa yekonya nnamvoa pá, kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a, yɛne bɛ afĩa ngɔtete lle ɔ.

Yehowa Te Yɛ Baba Ne Yɛ Nyameɛ, Na Osa Te Yɛ Damvo
<<<P>>>
Ebiaa ɔyɛ se kyɛ ekonwu kyɛ Yehowa te wɔ Baba, anaa wɔ damvo bɔ ɛkora kã wɔ dwirɛ kyire ye ɔ. Sɛ sɔ a, nzu yeɛ ɔkɔboka wɔ ɔ? Daa biala yɛ nikyeebie bɔ ɔkɔmaa ekopingye ye. Ebie yeɛ ole kyɛ,
<<<P>>>
• Ɛne ye dwudwo: Ɔpena kyɛ ɛkã wɔ dwirɛ kyire ye, yeti ɛyɛ a bɔ mbaeɛ.
<<<P>>>
• Ma odwudwo hyire wɔ: Bible bɔ ekosua de, nnɛfa di angorɛ. Ɛyɛ sɔ a, né ɛmaa Yehowa dwudwo kyire wɔ. Afei ɔkɔmaa ekonwu kyɛbɔ osi te ɔ.
<<<P>>>
• Nya aboterɛ: Sɛ ekole ebie koli na ayɛ ye damvo a, ɔtte ɛlɛhomapɛ dwirɛ. Yeti nikyee biala bɔ ɛyɛ na wamaa Yehowa ayɛ wɔ damvo ne, nnpɛra aba, ofikyɛ Yehowa koso ɔpena kyɛ ɔyɛ wɔ damvo.
