Sɛ ebie yɛ nikyee ma wɔ a, nzu yɛɛ ɛkɔyɛ kɔfa kohyire kyɛ wɔnye asɔ ɔ?
<<<P>>>
(Nea ngyekyɛmuɛ 14)
<<<P>>>
nemɔ sɔa, bɛyɛ nningyein pee bɛboka yɛ. Ebie ole kyɛ bɛbasra yɛ, na banea kyɛbɔ yɛnwo te ɔ. Yeti sɛ bɛbasra wɔ a, ɛkɔhora kɔhworɔ nikyeebie kɔfa kɔla bɛ ase. (1 Tɛs. 5:12, 13) Afei bɛye mɔ koso, ɛkɔhora kɔla bɛ ase, ofikyɛ mmerɛ bɔ ahaa behu mɔ kɔfa kɔtena awuro ne, ɛhene bie yeɛ bɛfa bɛsrasra aliemaamɔ ne, nakoso bɛnga hwee.
<<<P>>>
¹⁵ Yehowa koso, yekuro paa kyɛ yɛda ye ase. Na atee bɔ yɛfa so yɛyɛ sɔ yeɛ ole kyɛ, yɛkɔbɔ ye mbaeɛ. (Ɛfe. 6:18) Mmerɛ pee ne, sɛ yɛbɔ Yehowa mbaeɛ a, ɛyɛ a yɛsrɛ ye kyɛ ɔyɛ nikyeebie ma yɛ. Nzuati yeɛ yɛbɔ sɔ mbaeɛ ne ɔ? Ebiaa ne yehia anwoserɛ bɔ yɛkɔfa yekogyina yɛ haw noa ɔ, yɛpena kyɛ yesi gyinayɛɛ bie, anaa ebie po a yali mvomsoɛ bie yeɛ yɛpa ye kyerɛ kyɛ ɔfa hyɛ yɛ ɔ. Nakoso, nnɛmɔmaa yɛ wora fi kyɛ yɛkɔla ye ase, ofikyɛ wayɛ bie wama yɛ. (Edw. 95:2) Yede nzu yeɛ ɔkɔboka wɔ maa ɛkɔyɛ sɔ ɔ? Ɔwɔ nu alaa a, bisa wɔnwo kyɛ: ‘Ɛyɛ a метаa meda Yehowa ase wɔ me mbaeɛ nu, anaa daabiala bɔ ohia me nala yeɛ mekã ɔ?’
<<<P>>>
¹⁶ Yɛ nikyeebie fa kyire kyɛ ɛtte boniayɛ. Sɛ ebie yɛ nikyee ma wɔ, na ɛfa wɔnoa da ye ase a, né oyielle. Ɛyɛ a yɛ nikyee foforɛ bie ma onwu ye kyɛ wɔnye asɔ. Ɛyɛ sɔ a, ekonwu kyɛ edwirɛ bɔ Yesu hane ne, ɔte nahorɛ. Yenwa sɛ yɛkyɛ nikyee a, ɔmaa yɛnye gye paa tra kyɛ yekole ɔ. (Aso. 20:35) Ɛhe kɔmaa sona ne konwu kyɛ ɛdɔ ye, na ye koso okohuro wɔ dwirɛ.
<<<P>>>
¹⁷ Nningyein nworanwora bɔ Yehowa ayɛ ama yɛ ne, nzu paa yeɛ yɛyɛ a okyire kyɛ yɛnye asɔ ɔ? Yeɛ ole kyɛ ‘yekotu yɛ nipadua yekosie berɛ kyɛ afɔleɛ bɔ ɔte ase ɔ.’ (Kenga Romanfoɛ 12:1.) Bɔ sɔ dwirɛ he kyire yeɛ ole kyɛ, yɛkɔfa yɛnwo amu yɛkɔma Yehowa. Afei yɛkɔfa bɔ yɛle ye biala yɛkɔsɔ̃ ye. Yeɛ bɔ yɛdwene yenwo ne koso, yɛkɔnea kyɛ Yehowa nye gye yenwo. Yede nzu bieku yeɛ okyire kyɛ yafa yɛnwo yama Yehowa ɔ?
<<<P>>>
¹⁸ Yɛnwo so bɔ yehyirale na yɛbɔle asu ne, okyire kyɛ bɔ Yehowa ayɛ ama yɛ ne, yɛnye
