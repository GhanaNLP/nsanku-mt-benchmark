BƆ WASI ALIEMAA BIEMƆ ABRABƆ NU Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Ɛfa Wɔnwo To Yehowa So A, Ɔngɔmaa Wɔ Sa Ngosi Ase
<<<P>>>
Né Emma yɛ adwuma wɔ hotɛl. Dapɛen biala né ɔkɔ adwuma ɛlɛsa, na daa biala ɔyɛ adwu-ma dɔnhwere 9. Né nhyiamu adwu so, yeti ɔhɔle ye bɔss berɛ kyɛ ɔma ye kyẽa kɔ̃. Nako-so wande yebo, na ɔhane kyɛ bɔ wayɛ nati, dapɛen biala ɔkɔwa adwuma ɛlɛnnu; ɔngɔho-ra a, ɔyakyi adwuma ne. Nakoso né Emma le gyidie kyɛ Yehowa kɔnea ye. Yeti ɔyɛle kyɛ ɔkɔhɔ nhyiamu ne, ná wayakyi adwuma ne.
<<<P>>>
Emma hworɔle krataa kyɛ ɔyakyi adwuma ne. Ɛlɛho bɔ ɔfa hɔle ne, ɔɔtole hotɛl berɛ panyi paa ne. Baba ne tele Emma dwirɛ ne, ɔha hyi-rele ye kyɛ ɔne bɔss ne kodwudwo kɔma ye. Nakoso dapɛen nyɔ si ne, bɔss ne ha hyirele Emma kyɛ waye ye ali. Emma annwu bɔ ɔyɛle ɔ; osa yiale panyi ne bieku. Panyi ne fɛle yaa paa, ofikyɛ ɔne bɔss ne anga ye sɔ. Panyi ne maa besa bɛfale Emma adwuma. Bɛhane kyɛ ɔbra adwuma ɛlɛnnu dapɛen biala, nakoso daa biala ɔkɔyɛ adwuma dɔnhwere nna pɛ. Ɛhe de né wato wali, ofikyɛ ne ɔte pá tra daa deɛ ne. Afei koso, ne okonya atee kɔhɔ ade-sua ne asɛnga. Bɔ Yehowa yɛ mane ye ne, né ɔbo ye adwene ne ye ndeaseɛ so.
