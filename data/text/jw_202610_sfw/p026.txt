5:15, 16.) Sɛ kyẽa biala, yɛfa mmerɛ kãa yesua Yehowa nwo nikyee a, ɔkɔboka yɛ maa yekosisi gyinayɛɛ pa. Afei adwene bɔ Yehowa le ye wɔ nningyein nwo ne koso, yekonwu ye.
<<<P>>>
8 Sɛ yɛyɛ daa asɛm daa biala a, ɔboka yɛ maa yegyina ɔhaw bɔ yekoyia ye wɔ kyẽa nanu ne noa. Yesu hane kyɛ ‘kyẽa biala le ye haw.’ (Mat. 6:34) Yesu hane a ɔnnaka, ofikyɛ sɛ aleɛ nya kyẽ wɔ ngome de a, ɔhaw de ɔngɔpa wɔnwo lle. Nakoso wɔ sɔ tworɔnzɛm nala nu ne, Yesu maa yenwuni kyɛ ɔhaw biala bɔ yɛfa nu ne, Yehowa se. Yeɛ bɔ yehia koso ɔse. (Mat. 6:25-32) Afei koso, sɛ yɛyɛ daa asɛm daa biala a, ɔmaa yɛmva y’adwene yenzie yɛ haw naso. Na mmom bɔ Yehowa yɛ fa boka yɛ ne, yeɛ yɛfa y’adwene yesie so ɔ. (Edw. 68:19; 1 Pet. 5:6, 7) Wɔde ma yɛnea bɔ ɔtole aliemaa brasua bie. Wanya afoɛ 19. Mmerɛ bie ɔyɛle bɔne pirikua bie. Daa biala ne ye tiboa teetee ye kyɛ, ebiaa Yehowa falle ye bɔne hyɛlle ye. Yeti ɔbɔle Yehowa mbaeɛ kyɛ, ɔma onwu ye kyɛ wafa ye bɔne wahyɛ ye amba. Ɔbɔle mbaeɛ ne yiele ne, ɔyɛle ye daa asɛm ne. Tworɔnzɛm bɔ né daa asɛm ne gyi so ne, yeɛ ole Edwein 86:5. Ɛberɛ ka kyɛ: “Ao AWURADE, ɛte paa, na ɛfa bɔne kyɛ; wɔ ɔdɔ bɔ onni ngasi ne, ɔsone ma bɛ bɔ besu bɛfrɛ wɔ ɔ.” Ɔkenga yiele ne, ɔhane kyɛ, “Ɛberɛ ala yeɛ m’ahone tɔle mekunu ɔ, na nwuni kyɛ, awerɛkyekyerɛ bɔ né mehia ala yeɛ Yehowa afa ama me ne. Yeti menye gyele paa. Ofi sɔ mmerɛ ne bɔ ɔba ne de, daa meyɛ me daa asɛm.”
<<<P>>>
9 Daa asɛm ne de, ɔboka yɛ paa, ofikyɛ sɛ yeyia ɔhaw a, ɔmaa yɛfa ‘yɛwora amukoraa yewura AWURADE nu.’ (Any. 3:5, 6) Sɛ yɛnea Russia ne Ukraine ngondii ne sɔa a, mmerɛ bɔ bɛhyɛle yebo ne, na ɔlla ase mma aliemaamɔ. Nakoso sɔ kyẽa ne, tworɔnzɛm bɔ ne daa asɛm ne gyi so ne, ɔbokale bɛ paa. Na ɔwɔ Yesaya 30:
<<<P>>>
15, NWT. Ɛberɛ ka kyɛ: “Sɛ ɛmɔfa ɛmɔnwo bɛto me so na ɛmɔanzuro a, ɛmɔkonya anwosere.” Sɔ tworɔnzɛm ne, ɔbokale aliemaamɔ bɔ ne bɛwɔ Ukraine ne, na ɔmaa benyane awerɛkyekyerɛ. Ɛmɔma yetie bɔ aliemaa Eduard hane ɔ. Ɔnwa: “Ngyerɛmɔ tutuutu yeɛ mene me ye yɛle daa asɛm ne ɔ. Yeyiele ne, yeɛ yɛbɔle mbaeɛ ɔ. Na menwa, ɔmaa yenyane anwosere bɔ yɛkɔfa yekogyina bɔ ne ɔkɔ so ne noa. Afei né wayɛ tekyɛ bɔ bahwie nzue forɔlɔɔ bie bagua yɛnwo ne.” Na aliemaa Svitlana koso ɛ, nzu yeɛ ɔhane ɔ? Yenwa: “Tworɔnzɛm bɔ ɔwɔ Yesaya 30:15 ne, ɛhene ala yeɛ né yehia ɔ. Mmerɛ bɔ né ngondii ne kɔso namu, sɔ tworɔnzɛm nala yeɛ ne ɔwɔ yɛ tianu ɔ, na né daa yɛdwenedwene yenwo.”
<<<P>>>
10 Sɛ yɛyɛ daa asɛm daa biala a, ɔboka yɛ maa yedi nahorɛ. Sɛ yɛpena kyɛ yɛda nzorɛ wɔ yɛ klassmate, ɔne bɛbɔ yɛne bɛ kɔ adwuma nanwo a, odikyɛ yenya akokoduro. Afei sɛ yɛkɔhora yɛkɔhã nzɛmba ne bie yekohyire mmenia, na yali nahorɛ mmerɛ bɔ yeyia ɔhaw koso a, sana yenya akokoduro. Mmerɛ bie, Yehowa yele Yosua kyɛ oli Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ nyunu. Ɔwale sɔ ne, Yehowa hane ye pɛen nza so hyirele ye kyɛ, ogyina pindinn ná ɔma yenwo yɛ se. Afei Yehowa sa hane kyɛ, bɔ ɔkɔyɛ na wanya akokoduro ne, odikyɛ daa ɔkenga Mmraa nwomaa ne. (Kenga Yosua 1:6-9.) Yeti sɛ yɛyɛ daa asɛm, na yɛkenga Bible ne daa biala a, ɔkɔboka yɛ maa yekogyina pindinn na yahɔso yali nahorɛ.
