10 Daabiala, bɔ Yehowa yɛ ma wɔ ne, dwene- dwene yenwo na da ye ase. (Edw. 40:5) Ebie yeɛ ole kyɛ, mmerɛ biala bɔ ɛkɔbɔ mbaeɛ ne, otie. Sɛ w’abanu bu na ɛkenga Bible nanu dwi- rɛ bie a, né wɔnwo ayɛ wɔ fɔnwoo. Odwu mmerɛ bie a, ɛkora kenga asafo nwomaa, ɛnea vidio anaa brɔdkastin bie, na eyie a bɔ ɛkã yeɛ ole kyɛ, ‘Aa ɛhe de, me dwirɛ yeɛ babɔ so ye oo.’ (Edw. 65:2; Mat. 24:45; Rom. 15:4) Afei wama wɔ nningyein pee bɔ ɔmaa wɔnye gye ɔ. Ebie yeɛ ole kyɛ, sɛ edidi a, ɛte ye fɛ. Ɔmaa yɛkora yenwu nningyein bɔ ɔyɛ nyeme- ne tekyɛ eyia bɔ ɔkwaatɔ ɔ. Wɔ nnamvɔmɔ bɔ bɛdwene wɔnwo ne koso, bɛboka so. Sɔ nnin- gyein he amukoraati, ngoa bɔ Yehowa afa ama yɛ ne, yeɛ ɔmaa yɛkora yenya so mvasoɛ ɔ. (Aso. 17:25) Yede nzu kyɛ bieku yeɛ Yehowa ayɛ ama wɔ ɛnnɛ, bɔ ekuro kyɛ ɛtworɔ ɔ? —Ɛfe. 5:20.

11 Aliemaamɔ bɔ ɛne bɛ wɔ asafo nu ne, be- kuro wɔ dwirɛ kyɛ bɛ bɔbɔ b’abusuafoɛ. (Edw. 133:1; Any. 18:24) Yɛkã ye ebiaa aliemaa bie yɛle nikyeebie mane wɔ, anaa ɔhãne dwirɛ bie maa ɔtɔle w’ahone so. (Any. 12:25; 16:24) Bɔ aliemaamɔ yɛ ne, sɛ ɔte kãa po a, kyire kyɛ wɔnye sɔ. Yɛnea bɔ ɔtole yeliema brasua bie bɔ ɔwa wuli ɔ. Ɔwa ne wue nati, sɛ ɔkɔ adesua na bɛto dwein a, ɔngora ye to, ofikyɛ ɛyɛ a ne ye wora abɔ. Elɛho bie aliemaa brasua kõ nwuni ye, na ɔhɔle yenwo berɛ, yeɛ ɔfale ye sa tole ye kɔme anu ɔ. Afei ɔne ye bɔle nu tole dwein ne. Bɔ ɔwa awu ne hãne kyɛ: “Bɔ alie- maa ne yɛle ne, me wora ngofi ye lle.” Aliemaa brasua ne dwirɛ ne, ɔmaa yenwu kyɛ, nikyee- biala bɔ yɛkɔyɛ yɛkɔma yeliemamɔ ne, ɔkɔho- ra kɔtɔ b’ahone so paa.

13 Tworɔnzɛm bɔ adesua ne gyi so ne, ɔkã kyɛ nnɛmaa yɛyɛ boniayɛ. Mmerɛ bɔ Pɔɔlo kyirekyire sɔ dwirɛ nanu ne, ɔhãne kyɛ: “Ni- kyee biala bɔ ɛmɔyɔ wɔ edwudwolɛ anaa yɔleɛ nu ne, ɛmɔyɔ ye mukoraa wɔ Awurade Yesu dumaa nu, na ɛmɔfa asennaa bɛfa ye so bɛma Baba Nyameɛ.” (Kol. 3:15, 17) Bɔ Pɔɔlo hane ne kyire kyɛ, yɛ nneyɛɛ ɔne yɛ dwudwolɛ, yeɛ yɛfa yɛda Yehowa ase ɔ. Afei Yehowa kuro kyɛ ebie yɛ nikyee ma yɛ a, yɛda ye ase. Yede yɛ- kɔyɛ sɛɛ ne yafa yɛ nneyɛɛ ɔne yɛ dwudwo- lɛ yahyire kyɛ yɛtte boniayɛ?
