Aliemaa Rutherford hahyirele ye kyɛ ɛyɛ a ɔbra London ma beli ngɔmmɔ bie.
<<<P>>>
Aliemaa Skinner hane kyɛ: “Bɔ Aliemaa Rutherford hane ne, ɔmaa nyane adwene kyɛ annea a, né wasoma me kyɛ ngɔ maen foforɛ so. Aleɛ hyene nala yee nzili nu kyɛ mekɔ. Mekɔ namu, bɔ na owura me tianu yee ole kyɛ sɛ onya soma me a, ngɔhɔ. Ɛnwa nzɛ né mese bɔ ɔwɔ ye tianu ɔ. Ndwuli berɛ ala pɛ yee obisale me kyɛ: ‘Mepena kyɛ ɛkɔ India, ɛkɔhora kɔhɔ anaa?’ Yenoa ambe, yee mmisale ye kyɛ: ‘Kyinzu yee ngɔ ɔ?’”
<<<P>>>
Yeti ye dapɛen nza si wɔ July bɔsome nanu ne, bɛmaa Aliemaa George Wright bokale Aliemaa Skinner so, na bɛfole ship bɛhɔle India. Aliemaa George Wright koso, né ɔbɔ yenwo mmɔden wɔ asɛnga adwuma nanu paa. Bɛhɔle ne, bɛhane kyɛ Aliemaa Skinner nea Bɛtɛl bɔ ɔwɔ berɛ naso. Yeti asɛnga adwuma bɔ né bɛyɛ ye wɔ Afghanistan, Burma (Myanmar), Ceylon (Sri Lanka), India, ɔne Persia (Iran) ye yee né ɔnea so ɔ. Aliemaa Skinner fale afoɛ 64 sone wɔ India, na oyiele ye aseɛ so adwuma wɔ 1990 nu.

ALIEMAAMƆ BƆ BƐKƆSRA BIBLE ASUAFOƐ NE, BELILI ASƐNGA ADWUMA NE NYUNU
<<<P>>>
Ofi 1894 bɔ ɔba ne, né asafo ne ye aliemaa biemɔ maa bɛkɔnea Bible Asuafoɛ nemɔ boso. Aliemaamɔ bɔ né bɛsoma bɛ ne, né bɛfrɛ bɛ pilgrims. Na bɛ yee ɛnnɛ bɛfrɛ bɛ manzini soneafoɛ ne. Wɔ July 1926 nu ne, kyɛbɔ né bɛyɛ b’adwuma ne, bɛsesãne nu. Dáa ne, sɛ bɛkɔsra asafo bie a, né bedi ɛlɛhomapɛ. Nakoso siɛ ye, né bedi elenyɔ. Afei né bɛfa dɔnhwere 100 yee bɛyɛ asɛnga adwuma ne bɔsome biala ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Né Aliemaa George Draper boka pilgrims naso. Ɔhworɔle krataa ɔɔmane asafo ne, na ɔhane kyɛ: “Nikyee kõ bɔ mahyɛ ye nzorɛ bɔ mekuro kyɛ ɔtena me tianu paa yee ole kyɛ, kyɛbɔ ngyea bɔ b’awuro nwo waen ne, sɔ ala yee Ɛwɛnelɛ Waen bɔ yesua ne koso te ɔ. Nakoso sɛ yanzua a, né waayɛ kyɛ ngyea bɔ begua awuro nu koso bɛngora po nala.” (Yes. 56:10) Aliemaa Draper yiele ye aseɛ so adwuma wɔ 1938 nu.

<<<P>>>
Nhyiamu pirikua bɔ ɔpɛ yebo bɔ bɛyɛle wɔ 1926 nu ne, bɛhyɛle yebo October 14, na October 18 yee beyiele ɔ. Bɛyɛle ye wɔ New York City. Bɛbɔ bɛyɛ news pepɛs ne, bele bɛtole nu kyɛ bɛkɔbɔ Aliemaa Rutherford tɔɔk nanwo dawure wɔ bɛ grafik nu. Né ye tɔɔk ne dwireti yee ole “Koɛ Kandinga Bie Bɔ Ɔbaasi Wiase Ne Apingye.” Nakoso waha kaa maa odwu so ne, benu pee nala hane kyɛ bɛngɔbɔ dawure ne kõ.
<<<P>>>
