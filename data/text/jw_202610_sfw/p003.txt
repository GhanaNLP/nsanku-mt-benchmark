NHYIAMU BƆ BƐYƐLE NE, ƆMAA ALIEMAAMƆ YERELE BƐNWO YƐLE ASƐNGA ADWUMA NE
<<<P>>>
May bosome ne ɔkɔ ye ayieleɛ ne, bɛyɛle nhyia-mu bie wɔ London bɔ ɔwɔ England, na aliemaamɔ pee yeɛ bɛwale bie ɔ. Né bayɛ nwomaa bie bɔ bɛ-frɛ ye The Standard for the People, na nhyiamu nabo yeɛ né bɛkɔhyɛ bɛkɔma aliemaamɔ ɔ. Nako-so dodoɔ bɔ bɛkɔfa bɛkɔhɔ nhyiamu nabo ne, né benyanne ye, ofikyɛ sɔ mmerɛ ne, né adwu-mayɛfoɛ ahɔ strike wɔ Britain, yeti né prenten prɛss nemɔ nyɛ adwuma. Ɔwale sɔ ne, aliemaa-mɔ bɔ bɛwɔ Brooklyn Bɛtɛl ne, bɛfale bɛnwo belɛ-le nu. Begyinane bɛ gya so bɛprentele nwomaa ne ngokongoko 117,000.
<<<P>>>
Fue, May 29 ne, aliemaamɔ nyane nwomaa ne bie wɔ nhyiamu nabo. Sɔ ɛlɛho ne, bɔ bɛhane ye wɔ nhyiamu nabo ɛhe: “Ebiala bɔ ɔwɔ wa ne, ɔbra maa yɛbɔ ɔhene ne, ɔne y’ahennie nanwo dawu-re. Ɛhema ngyerɛmɔ tutuutu, ɛmɔma yeyia nu wɔ Alexandra Palace, na yɛyɛ asɛnga nhyiamu.”
<<<P>>>
Nwomaa bɔ bɛhyɛle ne, ɔbo 100,000. Ɛwɛnelɛ Waen bɔ ɔwale July 15, 1926 ne, ɔhane yenwo dwirɛ kyɛ: “Aliemaamɔ nye gyele paa, ofikyɛ be-nwuni kyɛ adwuma bɔ Yehowa afa ama bɛ ne, ba-yere bɛnwo bayɛ.”
<<<P>>>
Aleɛ hene ne, Aliemaa Rutherford mane tɔɔk bie wɔ Royal Albert Hall. Né ye dwirɛti yeɛ ole, “Nzuati Yeɛ Awaen Nemɔ Ngora Mma Nningyein Ngɔ Yé Ɔ?” Bebukyele anoa ne bɔ bɛbaahyɛ yebo nala, mmenia 13,000 wale berɛ kyɛ bɛbaatie bie. Bɛwale dee maa ɛberɛ yili sɔ ala bo. Yeti bɛ-fale speaker besiele aluo berɛ, na ɛhene ati, bɛbɔ bangɔ sua nu berɛ ne, bɛ koso betiele bie.
<<<P>>>
Aliemaa Rutherford tɔɔk nanu ne, ɔhane bɔ yeti yeɛ awaen nemɔ ayɛ dee koso nningyein ngɔ yé ne. Yeɛ ole kyɛ bɛmva Yehowa, ɔne ye ahennie ne bɛnyɛ fuu. Osa hane kyɛ Maenmaen Apam bɔ ɔwɔ berɛ ne, bɛnwa bɛkɔmaa asomdwee kɔwa, nakoso ɔngɔyɛ yé, na mmom okogu.
