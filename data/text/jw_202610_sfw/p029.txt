18 Mmerɛ bɔ aye asie berɛ kyɛ, ɛkɔfa kɔyɛ wɔ daa asɛm ne, bɔ mmɔden na di so. Nnɛmaa hwee twetwe wɔ. Aliemaa brasua Isabelle bɔ wali afoɛ 19 ne hane kyɛ: “Kã kyire wɔnwo kyɛ, sɛ nnyɛle daa asɛm ne a, mengɔfa me fon ne ngomiamia so, na nikyee biala bɔ ɔkɔwa so ne koso, mengɔnea. Afei sɛ fon yeɛ ɛfa yɛ daa asɛm, na enwu ye kyɛ ɔkɔtwetwe wɔ adwene a, yede ɔkɔyɛ pá kyɛ, ɛkɔfa bɔ baprinte ne bie.” Olivia koso bɔ wanya afoɛ 19 ne, ɔhane kyɛ: “Nikyee kõ bɔ ɔboka paa yeɛ ole kyɛ, ɛne wɔ damvo mɔ anaa w’abusuafoɛ kɔbɔ nu kɔyɛ daa asɛm ne. Sɛ ele bie bɔ daa ɔkɔkae wɔ maa ɛkɔyɛ daa asɛm a, ɛngɔtõ nu lle.”

19. Awofo, sɛ ɛmɔ ne ɛmɔ mmaa nemɔ yɛ daa asɛm daa biala a, ɔkɔboka bɛ sɛ? Fa mvandoho kyirekyire nu. (Filipifoɛ 1:9, 10)
<<<P>>>
yɛ sɔ a, ɔkɔha bɛ berɛ, na bafa sɔ subaen ne banyi. Yedwudwo ye, ebiaa wɔ mmaa nemɔ te mmaa mmaa. Yeti ɔkɔyɛ se kyɛ bɛkɔte daa asɛm nanu dwirɛ bie bo. Ɛhene po ne, wɔde ɛmɔyɛ. Ofikyɛ, sɛ ɛmɔkɔ so bɛyɛ a, ɔkɔboka bɛ, na sɛ benyi a bɛ koso bayɛ ye daa. Wɔde dwene edwirɛ he anwo nea. Sɛ ebie dua flaws a, sana oguegue so nzue tonn kodwu kyɛ yebo kotimu ɔ. Afei okodwu mmerɛ bie ne, ne wayɛ pirikua kama. Ɛhe fa ngwalaa nwo sɛ? Sɛ kyẽa biala, yɛpena mmerɛ kaa, na yɛne ngwalaa ne yɛ daa asɛm a, ɔkɔboka bɛ, na ngakangaka bekonyi bɛkɔɔyɛ mmenia bɔ bele Yehowa nu gyidie paa ɔ.—Kenga Filipifoɛ 1:9, 10.

20 Yesu hane kyɛ: “Nna paanoo ngome so yeɛ sona nate nya ngoa ɔ, na mmom ɔnate edwirɛ biala bɔ ofi Nyameɛ noa ba so.” (Mat. 4:4) Sɛ yedidi daa biala a, ɔboka yɛ maa yenya anwoserɛ na wamaa yahora yafa yayɛ adwuma. Sɛ sɔ a, Nyameɛ dwirɛ ne bɔ ɔkɔmaa yekonya daa ngoa ne koso ɛ? Ɔnzɛkyɛ yɛmaa kyẽa kõ po pɛ nu, bɔ yɛngɔyɛ daa asɛm ɔ. Yɛyɛ sɔ a, ɔkɔboka yɛ maa yɛ gyidie kɔyɛ se, na yɛne Yehowa afia ayɛ kama.
