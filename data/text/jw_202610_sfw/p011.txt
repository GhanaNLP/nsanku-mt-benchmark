19 Ewiase bɔ yɛte nu ne, mmenia lle ayɛ. Nakoso yɛde yenguro kyɛ yekosuesua bɛ, yeti ebie yɛ nikyee ma yɛ a, yɛda ye ase. Ofikyɛ ebie bɔ yenye sɔ nikyee ne, ɔte ye adwenedwene so, na ɔmaa yenye gye. Afei bɔ ɔtra yemu yeɛ ole kyɛ ɔbayɛ Yehowa damvo. Bɔ Yehowa ayɛ ama yɛ ne, sɛ yɛdwenedwene yenwo a, ɔkɔmaa yɛnye kɔsɔ bɔ wayɛ ne, na yala ye ase. Yeti sɛ ebie yɛ nikyee ma yɛ a, ɛmɔma yɛbɔ mmɔden yɛla ye ase. Na sɛ yɛbɔ mbaeɛ koso a, yɛkae kyɛ yɛkɔla Yehowa ase. Afei ɛmɔma yɛfa yɛ nneyɛɛ yehyire kyɛ, bɔ Yehowa yɛ ma yɛ ne, yɛnye sɔ. Yɛyɛ sɔ a, Yehowa nye kɔgye yɛnwo, na okohyira yɛ, ofikyɛ yede ɔtte boniayɛ.—Edw. 106:1; Heb. 11:6.
