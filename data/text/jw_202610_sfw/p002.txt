BƆ ƆHƆLE SO AFOƐ ƐYAA ƐHE
<<<P>>>
Ɛwɛnɛlɛ Waen bɔ ɔwale January 1, 1926 ne hane kɛ: “Ɔngɔhyɛ Nyamɛɛ kɔmaa ebiala konwu ye dumaa. Yeti yenwo hia kɛɛ onya mmenia maa bedi yenwo adanzeɛ.”
<<<P>>>
Yesu bɔ ɔte asafo ne ti ne, Yehowa nate ye so maa aliemaamɔ nwuni nikyeebie wɔ 1926 nu. Yeɛ ole kɛɛ Kristofoɛ de, bɔ odikyɛ bɛmaa yenwo hia bɛ paa yeɛ ole asɛnga adwuma ne. Ɛhene ati, “akoaa nahorɛniɛ ne nworɛniɛ” ne yɛle nwomaa foforɛ bɔ né bɛkɔfa bɛkɔhɔ asɛnga. (Mat. 24:45) Afei nikyee deinn bɔ odikyɛ Kristoniɛ yɛ ne, bɛmaa aliemaamɔ tele yebo paa. Kyɛbɔ bɛyɛ adesua ne koso, bɛsesãne ye. Na nekaa bɔ bɛkɔprente nwomaa, ɔne ngaen bɔ bɛkɔfa bɛkɔprente ne, ɛhene koso Yesu bokale bɛ maa benyane bie.
<<<P>>>
BƐYƐLE NWOMAA FOFORƐ, NA KYƐBƆ BƐYƐ ADESUA NE KOSO, BƐSESÃNE YE
<<<P>>>
Nwomaa bɔ né aliemaamɔ fa kɔ asɛnga ne, né bɛfrɛ ye Studies in the Scriptures. Nakoso wɔ April 1926 nu ne, The Golden Age bɔ kesaalae bɛfrɛ ye Awake! ne, ɔhane kyɛ bɛbaayɛ nwomaa foforɛ, na ɛhene yeɛ bɛkɔfa bɛkɔhɔ asɛnga ɔ. Dumaa bɔ ɔla so yeɛ ole Deliverance. Bɔ ɔkɔyɛ ná bapprente ye pee ne, aliemaamɔ bɔ bɛwɔ Bɛtɛl ne, bɛtɔle prenta foforɛ. Ɛhene de, minit biala né ɔkora prente nwomaa 22. Bɛprente beyiele ne, aliemaamɔ koso, bɛyerele bɛnwo bɛhyɛle.
<<<P>>>
Bible asuafoɛ ne, né besua Studies in the Scriptures ne wɔ adesua bo, na ɛhe hɔle so afoɛ pee. Nakoso wɔ May nu ne, bɛhyɛle yebo kyɛ bɛfa Ɛwɛnɛlɛ Waen ne bɛyɛ adesua wɔ Ahennie Asa so. Bɛyɛle sɔ ne, oosili sɛɛ?
<<<P>>>
Aliemaamɔ nye gyele yenwo paa, na asafo bie de, bɛhworɔle lɛtɛ bɛfa bɛɛlale Aliemaa Rutherford ase. Bɔ bɛhane ɛhe: “Kwasida biala yesua Ɛwɛnɛlɛ Waen ne, kyɛbɔ asafo ne nwa yɛyɛ nala. Ɛhene ati, kyɛbɔ né yɛte Bible nu dwirɛ bie bo ne, bɛsesã ye a ɔmaa yenwu ye ndɛ.”
<<<P>>>
Ndeaseɛ foforɛ bɔ benyane ne, ɔmaa aliemaamɔ nwuni kyɛ asɛnga adwuma ne de, onnikyɛ bɛfa bedi angorɛ. Adesua kɔ bɔ né ɔwɔ Ɛwɛnɛlɛ Waen bie nu sɔa, né ye dwirɛti yeɛ ole “Subaen Pá, Ɔne Yehowa Adwuma, Beni Yeɛ Yenwo Hia Ɔ?” Ɔhane kyɛ: “Ebiemɔ wɔ berɛ a, bɔ yenwo hia bɛ paa yeɛ ole kyɛ, bekonya subaen pá, na baboka mmenia. Yeti asɛnga adwuma bɔ odikyɛ Kristoniɛ yɛ ne, yenwo nhyeɖa nhia bɛ. Nakoso adwuma bɔ Yehowa afa ama yɛ ne, yeɛ ole kyɛ yeli yenwo adanzeɛ. Yeti ebiala bɔ ɔdɔ Yehowa ne, odikyɛ otie sɔ dwirɛ ne, na ɔyere yenwo yɛ asɛnga adwuma ne bie.”
<<<P>>>
Ɛwɛnɛlɛ Waen bie koso, adesua bɔ né ɔwɔ nu ne, edwirɛti bɔ ɔla so ɛhe: “Tu Wɔnwo Sie Berɛ Na Sõ Yehowa.” Ɔhane kyɛ, sɛ Kristoniɛ bie nwa ɔdɔ Yehowa paa a, sana ɔyɛ nikyeebie. Yeɛ ole kyɛ, okofi ye ahone nu kɔsõ Yehowa. Yeti asɛnga adwuma ne de, onnikyɛ aliemaamɔ fa di angorɛ. Ɔba sɔ a, mmenia konwu Yehowa nwo dwirɛ, na nningyein bɔ ɔbaayɛ ne koso bɛkɔte.—Yes. 43:10, 12; Mat. 24:14.
