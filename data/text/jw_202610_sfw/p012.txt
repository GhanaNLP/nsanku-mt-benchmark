DWEIN 143
Kɔ So Yɛ Adwuma no, Wɛn na Twɛn
<<<P>>>
BƆ ADESŨA NE KƆHÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Ɛnnɛ, Yehowa yɛ nningyein nza bie, na ɔyɛ nwanwa paa. Yekonwu bɔ yɛyɛ a okyire kyɛ, ɔyɛ yɛ fɛ kyɛ yɛboka maa ɔyɛ yé ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Anwanwadeɛ Bɔ Osisi Ɛnnɛ Ne, Ma Ɔyɛ Wɔ Fɛ Kyɛ Ɛboka Maa Ɔyɛ Yé Ɔ
<<<P>>>
“Ɔnate ɛhe aso mmenia amukoraa bekonwu kyɛ ɛmɔte m’asuafoɛ.”—YOH. 13:35.
<<<P>>>
YƐNEA Bible nu a yenwu kyɛ tete ne, anwanwadeɛ pee sisili. Ebie yeɛ ole kyɛ, Yehowa pakyele Ɛpo Kɔkorɛ nanu maa ye mmenia fale nu, yeti Egyptfoɛ nemɔ annya bɛ. Yesu koso wale aseɛ so ne, ɔsale mmenia anwonyerɛ, na bɛbɔ bawu ne po otingyele bɛ. Sɛ né ɛwɔ berɛ bie a, ahaa wɔnye ngɔgye anaa? Né ebiala di yenwo adanzeɛ kyɛ Nyameɛ tumi yeɛ ɔyɛ adwuma ɔ, na ɔtte mbaen; sana sona ne nga nahorɛ de. (Mat. 15:31) Edwirɛ hemɔ sili ahyɛ, nakoso yebisa oo, Yehowa té yɛ anwanwadeɛ anaa? Yóo. Nzu yeɛ ɔmaa yenwu ye sɔ ɔ?
<<<P>>>
² Bɛkã anwanwadeɛ a, ɔte nikyee bɔ ɔbo sona adwene ne ye ndeaseɛ so, anaa nikyee bɔ sona ngɔhora ye yɛ lle ɔ. Adesua he anu ne, yɛkɔnea anwanwadeɛ nza bie bɔ Yehowa yɛ ye wɔ ye asafo nanu ɛnnɛ ɔ. Sɔ nningyein nza bɔ yɛbaahã yenwo dwirɛ ne, sɛ nna Yehowa a, ebiala ngɔhora ye yɛ lle. Ɛhene ati yeɛ yɛkã kyɛ ɔte anwanwadeɛ ne. Yɛfa kõ biala a, yɛkɔnea bɔ yɛ koso yɛyɛ a, okyire kyɛ yɛboka maa ɔyɛ yé ɔ. Yɛyɛ sɔ a, ne ‘yɛ ne Yehowa bɔ nu yɛ adwuma.’—Kenga 1 Korintofoɛ 3:9.
<<<P>>>
1-2. (a) Anwanwadeɛ beni yeɛ Bible ne kã yenwo dwirɛ ɔ? (b) Bɛkã anwanwadeɛ a, okyire sɛɛ, na yɛkɔyɛ sɛɛ na yaboka maa wayɛ yé? (1 Korintofoɛ 3:9)
