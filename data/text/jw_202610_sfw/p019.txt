Yeti ɔka kãa maa bekũ ye ne, ɔhahyirele bɛ kyɛ: “Ɛmɔ yeɛ ɛmɔ ne me banate alaa wɔ me nzɔhwɛ nu ɔ.” (Luka 22:28; Yoh. 15:15) Ebie bɔ ɔte wɔ damvo de, nningyein kɔ yé a, ɔne wɔ ala, na sɛ edwirɛ to wɔ a, ɔngɔye yenye ngɔma wɔ. (Any. 17:17) Sɔ sona ne yeɛ Nworɛ Dwirɛha-nɛniɛ 4:9-12* kã yenwo dwirɛ ɔ. (Kenga.) Ɔboka wɔ maa ɛyɛ bɔ ɔte pá, na nikyeebie hyɛ wɔ so a, ɔkã edwirɛ bɔ ɔyɛ fɛ kyire wɔ. Ele sɔ adamvoa ne bie a, nikyefee nne berɛ bɔ ɔkɔ-hwe wɔ kofi Yehowa nwo ɔ. Afei nningyein bɔ yɛyɛ ye wɔ Yehowa soen nu ne, ɛne ye bɔnu yɛ a, adwuma bɔ ɛmɔkɔbɔ ne, ɔtte angorɛ, na ɛmɔ afĩa ngɔtete lle.

3 Bɛbɔ bɛdɔ Yehowa ne, sɛ abrandeɛ anaa ta-luwa bie fa bɛ adamvoa a, sɛɛ yeɛ bɛkɔboka ye maa ɔkɔsõ Yehowa ɔ? Awofo, sɛ ɛmɔfa ɛmɔ mmaamɔ adamvoa a, ɔkɔboka bɛ sɛ? Aliemaa-mɔ bɔ bɛnwo akokwa ne, bɛkɔyɛ sɛɛ na babo-ka mmrandeɛ ne ndaluwa nemɔ? Yɛbaanea Daniel nwo dwirɛ, na ɔkɔboka yɛ maa yeko-nya edwirɛ hemɔ nwo mmuayɛɛ. Afei bɔ ɔyɛ-le mmerɛ bɔ né wayɛ kyɛ ye anyelasoɛ ayie ne, yekonwu ye. Ebieku koso, mmerɛ bɔ né waha ye ngome ne, yekonwu kyɛbɔ ye damvo Yeho-wa bokale ye ɔ.

4. Daniel te abɛɛmaa ne, nzu yeɛ osili ɔ?
<<<P>>>
mmerɛ ne, ɔkɔyɛ kyɛ né Daniel te abɛɛmaa. Afoɛ kãa si ne, Babilɔnfoɛ nemɔ hyele mmeni pee bɛhɔle, na né Daniel ne ye abusua boka so. Bedwuli dɔ ne, bɛfale ye bɛhɔle bɛ ahem-vie berɛ kyɛ bɛkwaatete ye maa ɔyɛ adwuma ma ɔhene ne. (Dan. 1:3-6) Nekaa bɔ bɛfale ye bɛhɔle ne, né ombingye ye kuro, yeɛ né ɔnza mbingye ye abusuafoɛ; ɛmɔma yɛhã Daniel de, ne wali amia pa.

5 Bɛfale Daniel bɛhɔle Babilɔn ne, ɔyɛle sɛɛ bɔ wanyakyi Yehowa soen ɔ? Bible ne kã kyɛ né ‘wayɛ ye adwene kyɛ’ ɔkoli Nyameɛ mmraa so. (Dan. 1:8) Sɔ mmraa ne, nwa yeɛ ohyirele ye ɔ? Gya ye maame ne ye baba ɛne. Dumaa Daniel bɔ bɛfa bɛtole ye ne sɔa, yebo kyire “Nyameɛ Yeɛ Okoli Me Dwirɛ Kɔma Me Ɔ.” Ɛhe maa yenwu kyɛ né bɛpena kyɛ bɛwa ne sõ Yehowa, na otie ye dwirɛ.—Deut. 6:6, 7.
<<<P>>>
6 Diyifoɛ adwuma bɔ né Yeremia yɛ ne, ɛhe-ne koso bokale Daniel paa. Afoɛ 647 Kora Yeɛ Yesu Ba ne, Yehowa hahyirele Yeremia kyɛ ɔɔyɛ diyifoɛ. Sɔ mmerɛ ne, né bɛwolle Daniel. (Yer. 1:2) Bɛbɔ né Yeremia kã Yehowa dwirɛ kyire bɛ ne, né Daniel abusuafoɛ boka ye, ofi-kyɛ nnehyeɛ ne mmeni titire yeɛ né ɔtaa kã edwirɛ ne kyire bɛ ɔ. (Yer. 1:17, 18; Dan. 1:3, 6) Yeti né Daniel te ye damvo anaa? Ɛhene de yɛnze. Nakoso Daniel te abɛɛmaa ne, ɔte-le ye dwirɛ ne, na siɛ ye bɔ ɔyɛle panyi ne po, né ɔkenga. (Yer. 25:11; 29:10; Dan. 9:1, 2) Ɔwɔ nu kyɛ né mmenia ne mva Yehowa soen nyɛ fuu, nakoso Yeremia nyane akokoduro, na wanyakyi Yehowa soen. Bɔ ɔyɛle ne, Dan-iel suane nikyee fili nu.
