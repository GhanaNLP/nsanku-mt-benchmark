8 Anwanwadeɛ bɔ Yehowa ne Yesu yɛ ne, yeɛ ɔmaa mmeni pee ba asafo nanu ɔ. Yeti mmerɛ biala bɔ yɛkɔhã nzɛmba ne, okyire kyɛ yɛboka maa bɔ bɛyɛ ne, ɔyɛ yé. Ɛmɔma yɛkae kyɛ begyi yɛ si. Yesu nwa: “Me ne ɛmɔ wɔ berɛ mmerɛ biala kodwu ewiase ayieleɛ.” (Mat. 28:20) Afei yɛkae kyɛ abɔfo nemɔ koso, bɛboka yɛ maa yɛkã nzɛmba ne.—Nye. 14:6, 7.

KYƐBƆ YAYƐ KÕ NE, ƆYƐ NWANWA
<<<P>>>
9 Diyifoɛ Mika hã tole berɛ kyɛ nikyeebie bɔ ɔyɛ nwanwa kosi wɔ yɛ mmerɛ he aso. Yeɛ ole kyɛ Yehowa kɔhã ye mmenia kɔbɔ nu, na batena asomdwee nu. (Kenga Mika 2:12.) Sɔ dwirɛ ne awa nu, ofikyɛ ɛnnɛ bɛbɔ bɛsõ Yehowa ne, ebiala le ye subaen, ɔne nekaa bɔ ofi ɔ, nakoso bɛ mukoraati bɛte berɛ kama. Dawide hãne a ɔnnaka. Yenwa: “Nea kyɛbɔ osi te paa ná ɔyɛ nyemene ɔ, kyɛ aliɛmaa mɔ bɛkɔtena bɛkɔyɛ ko ɔ!”—Edw. 133:1.
<<<P>>>
10 Nzu bieku yeɛ ɔmaa yenwu kyɛ yɛte kõ ɔ? Yɛkɔ adesua ne nhyiamu a, bɔ yesua ne, ɛhene ala yeɛ aliemaamɔ sua ye wɔ ewiase nekaa biala ɔ. Bɛbɔ besi asafo sua, ɔne bɛbɔ bɛyɛ adwuma wɔ Bɛtɛl ne, ebiala le nekaa bɔ ofi ɔ, nakoso bahã babɔ nu, na bɛyɛ adwuma asomdwee nu. Kyɛbɔ yahã yabɔ nu ne, nikyefee nne berɛ bɔ ɔkɔmaa y’afĩa kɔtete ɔ. Yeti sɛ ebie fi ni oo, ɔte sɛɛ oo, ole bie oo, olle bie oo, yɛmma ɛhene ndete y’afĩa. Yɛnyɛ pɔletis, na ɛhene amaa yayɛ kõ. Nekaa biala bɔ yɛkɔhɔ ne, yɛse kyɛ yeliemamɔ wɔ berɛ. Yehia mmokale a, bɛngɔbɔ yɛ atianuserɛ, na bekosa bɛkɔfa Bible ne bɛkɔhyɛ yɛ ngunaen. Ɔwɔ nu, ebiala le nekaa bɔ ofi ɔ, koso bɔ yedé yedi ne, ɔte kõ, na yɛnu biala bɔ mmɔden kyɛ ɔkɔfa Bible nu dwirɛ kɔbɔ ye bra. Ɛhe de, sɛ nna Yehowa asafo ne a, ahaa ebie silli lle.

11 Kyɛbɔ yahã yabɔ nu ne, nzuati yeɛ ɔyɛ nwanwa ɔ? Ofikyɛ asomdwee nne wiase he anu. Yeti etie news a, banga kyɛ bɛkõ ngondii a, bɛkɔhã kyɛ bɛyɛ ɔyɛkyerɛ anaa dɛmɔnstrehyɛn. Banga ɛhene koso a, bɛkɔhã kyɛ edwirɛ bie ayɛ twe-ma-ndwe. Afei mmenia ‘nni nahorɛ, belle sona nwo dɔ, bɛne bɛ yɛ apam a ɔnyɛ yé, bɛsɛkye mmenia dumaa, bɛngora bɛnhyɛ bɛnwo so, bɛ ti anu te awosii, bɛpo nikye paa biala, bɛye bɛ mmango bɛma, bɛ so yɛ se, na bɛmoma bɛnwo so.’ Bible ne hãtole berɛ kyɛ sɔ yeɛ mmenia kɔyɛ ɔ. (2 Tim. 3:2-4) Ewiase bɔ yɛte nu ye, owura Satan ne ye sunzummɔne mɔ sa. Nakoso bɔ ɔyɛ nwanwa yeɛ ole kyɛ, ye bɔ yɛsõ Yehowa ne de, asomdwee bɔ ɔwɔ yɛ nu ne, oté wɔ berɛ.—1 Yoh. 5:19; Nye. 12:9.
<<<P>>>
12 Asomdwee bɔ ɔwɔ yɛ nu ne, sɛ yɛnu biala yɛ ye afanu deɛ a, daa biala ɔkɔtena berɛ. Yɛkɔyɛ ye sɛ? Yeɛ ole kyɛ bɔ Bible kã ne, yekotie na yafa yayɛ adwuma. Yɛyɛ sɔ a, yekonya yeliemamɔ nwo aboterɛ na yɛne bɛ ngɔyeye so. Afei bɛyɛ yɛ bɔne koso a, yɛkɔfa yɛkɔhyɛ bɛ. (Ɛfe. 4:2, 31, 32) Yeti yeliemamɔ subaen bɔ yɛnye ngye yenwo ne, nnɛmɔmaa yɛfa y’adwene yɛkɔ so. Afei nnɛmɔmaa yɛkekã bɛnwo dwirɛ. Bɛyɛ nikyee maa ɔyɛ yɛ yea koso a, ɛmɔma yɛyakyi nu ma ɔha. Yɛyɛ ɛhemɔ a, okyire kyɛ anwanwadeɛ bɔ Yehowa yɛ ye wɔ asafo nanu ne, yɛboka maa ɔyɛ yé.
