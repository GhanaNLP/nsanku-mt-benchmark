Nyameɛ Akwangyerɛ
<<<P>>>
BƆ ADESŨA NE KƆHÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Yɛbaanea bɔ yeti yeɛ ye-nwo hia kyɛ, yɛyɛ daa asɛm ne daa biala, ɔne bɔ ɔkɔ-boka yɛ maa yɛkɔyɛ sɔ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ Daa Asɛm Ne Daa Biala
<<<P>>>
‘Betiele edwirɛ ne; na daa biala, ne bɛneanea Tworɔnzɛm ne anu.’—ASO. 17:11.
<<<P>>>
WƆ 1905 nu ne, asafo ne yele nwomaa bie ali. Bɛfale tworɔn-zɛm ahoroɛ, ɔne Zion’s Watch Tower nanu dwirɛ yeɛ bɛyɛle nwo-maa ne ɔ. Ɛhe maa aliemaamɔ nyane tworɔnzɛm bie, ɔne yenwo ngyerɛkyiremuɛ bɔ bɛkɔkenga ye daa biala ɔ. Aliemaamɔ fale sɔ nwomaa ne yɛle adwuma tonn oodwuli 1927 nu. Ofi sɔ mme-rɛ ne bɔ ɔba ne, bɛfale daa asɛm nanu dwirɛ ne wale Year Book nu.* Ɛhene koso bokale aliemaamɔ maa benyane tworɔn-zɛm pee, ɔne Ɛwɛnɛlɛ Waen nanu dwirɛ bɔ daa bekosusu ye-nwo ɔ.
<<<P>>>
2 Daa asɛm bɔ yesusu yenwo ne, asafo ne yele ye ali wɔ 1986 nu. Ɔte nwomaa kãamba bie. Na ɛhene yeɛ bɛfrɛ ye Tworɔnzɛm Ne Bɔ Ekosusu Yenwo Daa Ɔ. Kesaalae, bayɛ nwomaa ne wɔ dwu-dwolɛ kɔbo 300 nu. Ekonya bie wɔ JW Library® app, Ɛwɛnɛ-nɛ Waen INTANƐT SO NWOMAA AKORAEƐ ™ (wol.jw.org) ɔne Watchtower Library naso.
<<<P>>>
3 Ɛnnɛ, mmenia lle alagye koraa. Yeti ɛyɛ a, né wayɛ se kyɛ yenu biemɔ kɔyɛ daa asɛm daa biala ɔ. Yede, ɛmɔma yesusu nzɛmmisa nza biemɔ nwo. Bɔ olimoa, Bible nu dwirɛ beni yeɛ ɔkɔhora kɔboka yɛ maa yɛkɔyɛ daa asɛm daa biala ɔ? Bɔ ɔtɔ so nyɔ, nzuati yeɛ yenwo hia kyɛ daa biala yɛyɛ daa asɛm ne ɔ? Bɔ ɔpɛ yebo ɔ, nzu yeɛ ɔkɔboka na yahora yayɛ yɛ daa asɛm ne daa biala ɔ?
<<<P>>>
* Dwudwolɛ ahoroɛ bie wɔ berɛ a, né belle Year Book. Yeti bɔ ɔkɔyɛ na bahora bayɛ daa asɛm ne bie ne, né bɛfa tworɔnzɛm ahoroɛ bɔ daa asɛm ne gyi so ne, bɛba Ɛwɛnɛlɛ Waen nu bosome biala. Afei daa asɛm nanwo ngyirekyiremuɛ ne koso, ne bɛfa bɛbo-ka so.
