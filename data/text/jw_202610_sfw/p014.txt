Ɛtane Bɛnye Po A, Né Bɛsone
<<<P>>>
Wiase Kõe Bɔ Ɔtɔ So Nyɔ nanu ne, kyɛbɔ bɛtanene Yehowa Adanzefoɛ nye ne, ɔtte angorɛ. Nakoso sɔ mmerɛ ne po mmenia pee wale asafo nanu. Né ɔyɛ nwanwa paa. German sogyaniɛ bie hane kyɛ: “Yehowa Adanzefoɛ de, ɛtane bɛnye ne, bekye bɔ né mmenia ba nu ɔ.”
<<<P>>>
Netherlands koso ɛhene bie ala yeɛ osili ɔ. Yearbook bɔ ɔwale 1986 ne, ɔhane yenwo dwirɛ kyɛ: “Bɔ wafa aliemaamɔ nye so ne, nna ebie ɛhe. Bɛkye bie bɛto nu ne, na bekũ bie. Nakoso ɛhe mukoraati si ne, mmeni pee bɛɛyɛle Yehowa Adanzefoɛ. Wɔ 1941 nu ne, né bɛte 642 nakoso afoɛ nyɔ si ne, ne badwu 1,379. Né ebie silli lle.... Ɔwɔ nu, né bɛtane bɛnye, koso Yehowa adaworoma bɛyɛle pee mbaen.”
<<<P>>>
Yesu suaniɛ a, odikyɛ onwu kyɛ, bɔ ɔkɔfa yenye so ne, nna ebie ɛhe, na ebiaa bekohũ ye po. Yesu sa hane kyɛ bɛkɔfa ye asuafoɛ nemɔ bɛkɔhɔ kɔɔt, na bafa bɛ bagyina ahemvo mɔ nyunu. (Mat. 10:17, 18) Osa hane kyɛ Yehowa soen ti, asɔre mbanyi nemɔ kɔtane bɛnye. (Yoh. 16:2) Na bɛne b’abusuafoɛ afĩa kɔhora kɔtete.—Mat. 10:34-36.
<<<P>>>
6 Kyɛbɔ Yesu hane ne, bɛtanene Kristofoɛ nemɔ nye paa. Owu hɔle ne, wangyɛ Yudafoɛ asɔfo nemɔ tuli bɛnye siele Kristofoɛ naso, ofikyɛ né bɛpena kyɛ bɛyakyi asɛnga adwuma ne. Yeti bɛhyele bɛ, na bɛfale bɛ bɛhɔle bɛ kɔɔt kandinga bɔ bɛfrɛ ye Sanhedrin ne. Asɔfo nemɔ yɛle b’adwene kyɛ ‘bekohũ bɛ,’ nakoso Farasiiniɛ kɔ̃ bɔ bɛfrɛ ye Gamaliɛl ne, ɔhane edwirɛ bie hyirele bɛ. Ɔhane kyɛ: “Nnɛma ɛmɔyɛ nikye fee betia sɔ mmenia hemɔ; émɔyakyi bɛ. Ofikyɛ sɛ bɛ nhyehyɛleɛ he anaa b’adwuma he ofi mmenia berɛ a, ongogyina. Nakoso sɛ ofi Nyameɛ berɛ a, ɛmɔngɔhora bengogu ye, na sɛ ɛmɔannea yé a, Nyameɛ yeɛ ɛmɔkɔho bekotia ye ɔ.” (Aso. 5:33, 34, 38, 39) Ɔwale sɔ ne, bɛyakyili bɛ, na ɛhene si po ne, né beté tane bɛnye. Nakoso Kristosom angu, na mmom mmeni pee bɛɛyɛle Kristofoɛ. (Aso. 6:7) Ɛhe maa yenwu kyɛ Gamaliɛl dwirɛ ne, né ɔte nahorɛ, ofikyɛ bɔ bɛyɛle ne, wangɔ anzi fuu. Mmenia bɔ né bɛtane bɛnye sɔa de, ɔyɛle sɛɛ bɔ bɛyɛle pee sɔ ɔ? Ofikyɛ né Nyameɛ gyi bɛsi.—Aso. 1:8.
<<<P>>>
7 Mmeni pee wɔ berɛ a, bɛnwa bɛkɔsɔ̃ Yehowa nati, b’abusuafoɛ tane bɛnye. Ebiemɔ po de, abusua apo bɛ. Ebiemɔ koso, bɛbɔ bɛne bɛ yɛ adwuma mma bɛnwo ndɔ bɛ. Yeɛ bɛ mannifoɛ koso, sɔ ala yeɛ bɛyɛ ɔ. Nakoso bɛnwa Yehowa ala yeɛ bɛkɔsɔ̃ ye ɔ. Nzuati ɔ? (Mat. 16:24) Bɛdɔ Yehowa, na bede bedi kyɛ, wahã kyɛ ɔkɔnea bɛ ne, bɔ ɔte biala ɔkɔyɛ. Amba-ne-amba Yehowa koso, ɔma bɛ tumi bɔ ɔbo sona deɛ so, na ɛhene maa bɛkora begyina nu. (2 Kor. 4:7-9) Bɛbɔ b’abusuafoɛ tane bɛnye ne, bɛne edweintolɛniɛ ne yɛ adwene. Ɔhane kyɛ: ‘Sɛ me baba ne me maame po me po a, AWURADE de, okole me.’ (Edw. 27:10) Wɔde nea oo, sɛ ebie di Yesu si a, ebie fa yenye so paa. Nakoso mmeni pee yeɛ bɛba asafo nanu ɔ. Ɛhe de, ɔbo yɛ adwene ne yɛ ndeaseɛ so.
