Nahorɛ Ne Bɔ Anwu Ye Ne, Ɛkɔyɛ Sɛɛ Na Asɔ Nu Yé?
<<<P>>>
ƐHYƐLE yebo kyɛ esua Bible ne, nzu yeɛ ɔmaa enwuni kyɛ Yehowa Adanzefoɛ yeɛ bekã nahorɛ ɔ? (Ɛfe. 4:4-6) Ɔdɔ bɔ ɔwɔ benu ne anaa? Anaa b’abrabɔ pá ne? Mmeni pee wɔ berɛ a, sɔ nningyein nyɔ ne, yeɛ wamaa bawa asafo nanu ɔ. Ofikyɛ Yehowa asafo ne amaa banwu kyɛ saa bɛ koso, bɛ so le mvasoɛ. Ɛhe nyɛ nwanwa, ofikyɛ ɛhene yeɛ ɔmaa benwu nahorɛ Kristofoɛ ɔ.—Yoh. 13:35; 1 Pet. 2:12.
<<<P>>>
Nakoso yɛfa ye kyɛ aliemaa bie ne wɔ anni ye yé, anaa wayɛ nikyee bie bɔ okyire kyɛ onguro wɔ dwirɛ. Anaa aliemaa bie ayɛ bɔne, na ye bɔne ne, ɔmvata Kristoniɛ. Wayɛ kyɛ akoaa bie bɔ ɔɔfale ye baba aye wɔ Korinto asafo nanu ne. (1 Kor. 5:1) Sɛ ebie subaen ngome yeɛ ɛnea so sõ Yehowa a, yede annea yé a ɛkɔyakyi asafo ne. Ɛkɔyɛ sɛɛ na ɛhe bie ando wɔ?

Ma Wɔ Gyidie Yɛ Se
<<<P>>>
Nningyein pee yeɛ asua afi Bible nanu ɔ. Sɛ ede to nu kyɛ ɛhene si ne, nahorɛ biala nne berɛ kɔ a, ɔkɔmaa wɔ gyidie kɔyɛ se. Ɔba sɔ a, ɔkɔboka wɔ paa. Wɔ de, nea bɔ yeti yeɛ yekã sɔ ne.
<<<P>>>
Mmerɛ bie Yesu hane edwirɛ bie, na ye asuafoɛ nemɔ ande yebo. Yenwa sana bedi ye nwonaen na bɛno ye mogya. Asuafoɛ nemɔ bie hane kyɛ: “Ngyerɛkyerɛ he de, ɔyɛ se.” Yeti benu pee anni ye si kɔ̃.— Yoh. 6:56-60, 66.
<<<P>>>
Yesu bisale ye asuafoɛ 12 ne kyɛ: “Ɛmɔ koso, ɛmɔpena kyɛ ɛmɔkɔ bie anaa?” Petro hane kyɛ: “Awurade, nwa berɛ yeɛ yɛkɔhɔ ɔ? Wɔ yeɛ ele daa ngoa dwirɛ ɔ; na yale yali, na yanwu ye kyɛ wɔ ole Nyameɛ ye Krongronniɛ ne.”—Yoh. 6:67-69.
<<<P>>>
Nzuati yeɛ asuafoɛ 12 ne de, bangɔ ɔ? Né bede bedi paa kyɛ Yesu yeɛ ole Mɛsaya ne. Nnaa ɛkae bɔ Petro hane ne? Yenwa “yale yali, na yanwu ye kyɛ wɔ ole Nyameɛ ye Krongronniɛ ne.” Nzu yeɛ ɔmaa benyane sɔ gyidie ne? Né banwu Yesu subaen, yeɛ batie ye ngyerɛkyerɛ koso. Sɛ bɛfa bɛtoto bɔ basua ye wɔ Bible nu nanwo a, ne ɔmaa benya adanzeɛ bɔ okyire kyɛ Yesu yeɛ ole Mɛsaya ne. Ɛhene yeɛ ɔmaa benyane ye nu gyidie ɔ. Yeti sɛ wahã edwirɛ bie na bɛnde yebo a, nna ɛhene ati yeɛ bengolli ye si kɔ̃ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Ngɔmmɔ kãa bɔ yabɔ ye, nzu yeɛ yesua yefi nu ɔ? Mmenia subaen de, ɔkora sesa. Nakoso nahorɛ bɔ ɔwɔ Bible nu ne de, ɔnzesa. Ebie yeɛ ole kyɛ, Bible nwa Nyameɛ dumaa li Yehowa, na ɔnate Yesu wue naso kɔma yɛ Paradise. Sɔ dwirɛ bɔ wahã ne, ɔngɔsesa yenoa lle. Yeti sɛ asafo nanuniɛ bie yɛ nikyee bɔ ɔttemaye a, sona ne yeɛ ye subaen asesa ɔ. Ɛhene ngyire kyɛ Bible nu dwirɛ bɔ asua ne, ɔtte nahorɛ kɔ̃. Ɛhe ati, sɛ ede bɔ Bible kã ne di, na ede to nu kyɛ Yehowa asafo yeɛ bekyirekyire sɔ nahorɛ ne a, ɛngɔhã kyɛ aliemaa bie subaen ti ɛyakyi asafo ne.
