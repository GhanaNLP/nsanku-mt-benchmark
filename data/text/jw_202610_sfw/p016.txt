Nyameɛ mmenia dodɔ bɛnwo, na bɛbɔ bɛnne asafo nanu po, bɛdɔ bɛ
(Nea ngyekyɛmue 14)
<<<P>>>
dodɔ bɛnwo paa. Bɛbɔ wahia bɛ ne, bɛyɛle bɛ adɔyɛ. (Aso. 2:44, 45) Bɛbɔ ne bɛtane bɛnye ne koso, bɛbɔle mbaeɛ bɛmane bɛ. (Aso. 12:5) Besa bɛneane nyisiaa ne akunafɔɛ. (Yak. 1:27) Benu bie wɔ berɛ a né belle bie, nakoso beliemamɔ bɔ bɛnwo ahyere bɛ ne, bɛyele ndoboa bɛfa bɛbokale bɛ. (2 Kor. 8:1-4) Bɛfrɛ baba bie Tertullian, na ɔtenane ase kɔyɛ afɔɛ 155-220 Yɛ Aberɛso. Ɔhworɔle tete Kristofoɛ nemɔ nwo dwirɛ kyɛ: “Bɔ bɛkã bɛfa bɛnwo yee ole kyɛ, mmenia hemɔ de, bɛdodɔ bɛnwo paa, na odikyɛ bewu bɛma bɛnwo po a bekowu.”
<<<P>>>
14 Kyɛbɔ tete Kristofoɛ nemɔ yɛle ne, yɛ bɔ yɛsɔ Yehowa ɛnnɛ ne, yɛdodɔ yɛnwo paa. Bɛbɔ bɛnne asafo nanu po, yɛdɔ bɛ. Ɛhene ati yee yɛka nzɛmba ne yekyire bɛ ne. Sɔ adwuma ne, yɛye ndoboa yɛfa yɛboka. Ebiala nhyɛ yɛ, yee bɛmbɛ sikaa biala bɛmma yɛ kyɛ yetua. Yeliemamɔ nwo kyere bɛ a, yɛdɔ bɛ nati yɛboka bɛ.* Nna ɛhene ngome yee yɛyɛ ɔ; yesa yɛfa Bible nu dwirɛ yɛhyɛ bɛ ngunaen. Ɛhe kyire kyɛ yɛfa somafoɛ Pɔɔlo afutue ne yɛyɛ adwuma. Yenwa ‘bɛ bɔ b’aba nu abu ne, yɛkyekye bɛ wora, bɛ bɔ bayɛ mmerɛ ne, yɛboka bɛ, ná yenya sona biala nwo aboterɛ.’ (1 Tɛs. 5:14) Yehowa alo yɛ paa kyɛ wafa yɛ wawa asafo nanu ɔ. Ofikyɛ ɔdɔ bɔ ɔwɔ alie-maamɔ nu ne, engonya ye nekafee. Wamaa yayɛ kyɛ abusua ne, na bɛbɔ bɛnne asafo nanu po yɛdɔ bɛ.
<<<P>>>
15 Ɔdɔ bɔ ɔwɔ yɛnu ne, nzuati yee ɔyɛ nwanwa ɔ? *Ofikyɛ ewiase he anu de, ebie ngyɛke ebie.* Somafoɛ Pɔɔlo hãne kyɛ yɛ mmerɛ he aso ne, mmenia kɔyɛ ‘pɛsɛmangomenya, na bɛ nye kɔboro sikaa.’ Afei ‘bekohuro anigye bekɔtra Nyameɛ,’ na bengonya ɔdɔ bɛngɔma ye. ‘Bengonya sona nwo dɔ,’ na bɛ bɔbɔ b’abusuafoɛ po bengonya ɔdɔ biala ngɔma bɛ. (2 Tim. 3:2-4) Yesu koso hãne kyɛ mmenia kɔye bɛ nwo bɛkɔma, ná bapɔ bɛ nwo. Afei “ɔdɔ bɔ mmeni pee le ye ne, ye noa kodwo.” (Mat. 24:10, 12, *NWT*) Ɛnea bɔ ɔkɔso wɔ wiase he anu a, nna ebie yee ɔkɔhã kohyire wɔ kyɛ bɛ dwirɛ ne te nahorɛ ɔ. Ɛhe maa yenwu kyɛ ɔdɔ bɔ ɔwɔ yɛnu ne, sɛ nna Yehowa a, ahaa ɔngɔyɛ yé lle.
