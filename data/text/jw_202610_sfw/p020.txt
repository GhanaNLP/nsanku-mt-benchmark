8 Sɛ awofo boka bɛ mmaamɔ a, bɛkɔhora bɛkɔsõ Yehowa. Yeti ele mmaa a, bisa wɔnwo kyɛ: ‘Bɔ yeti yeɛ yɛnga antɛm ɔne plɛgye, na yɛngyea flag ne, mahyire nu mahyire bɛ? Bɛkɔ sukuu na ebiemɔ kã kyire bɛ kyɛ bɛyɛ nikyeebie bɔ ɔttemaye a, aso bɛse bɔ bɛkɔyɛ ɔ? Benwu foto anaa vidio bɔ ɔttemaye wɔ intanɛt so a, bɛkɔyɛ ye sɛ? Sɛ amaneɛnwunɛ piri ne ba a, bekogyina pindinn kyɛ Yehowa ala yeɛ bɛkɔsõ ye anaa?’ Nningyein bɔ ɔfa Yehowa soen nwo ne, ɛne bɛ bɔ yenwo ngɔmmɔ. Kyire bɛ kyɛbɔ bɛkɔma mmuayɛɛ wɔ adesua bo, na ɛne bɛ hɔ asɛnga. Ɛyɛ sɔ a, ɔdɔ bɔ bele bɛma Yehowa ne, ɔkɔyɛ piri, na bekonya ye nu gyidie. Kyɛbɔ Daniel suane nikyee fili Yeremia berɛ ne, sɔ ala yeɛ bɛbɔ bɛnwo akokwa ne, sɛ mmrandeɛ ne ndaluwa fa bɛnwo bɔ bɛ a, ɔkɔboka bɛ ɔ.

9 Bɛsoale Daniel ne mmɛɛmaa bɔ bɛte Yudafoɛ ne bɛhɔle Babilɔn ne, né ɔyɛ se kyɛ bɛkɔyɛ bɔ Yehowa kuro ɔ. Nzuati ɔ? Bedwuli nala, Babilɔnfoɛ nemɔ yɛle alaa kyɛ bɛkɔmaa bekosuesua bɛ. Bɔ né bɛpena yeɛ ole kyɛ okodwu nekaa bie ne, né bɛ wora afi kyɛ bɛte Yudafoɛ po. Afei bɛfale bɛ bɛhɔle ahemvie berɛ kyɛ bɛkwaatete bɛ maa bɛyɛ adwuma bɛma ɔhene ne. Ɛberɛ de bammia bɛnye a, ahaa bɛngɔsõ Yehowa kõ.
<<<P>>>
10 Beedwuli ahemvie berɛ ne, ɔhene ne hane kyɛ, ye aleɛ bɔ odi ne, ɛhene bie ala yeɛ bɛfa bɛma Daniel ne mmɛɛmaa nemɔ ɔ. Ɛhe de, né ahaa ɔtte gidigidi biala, nakoso né ɔkɔmaa bɛkɔyɛ nikyee bɔ Yehowa nguro ɔ. Nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ ne Yehowa mmraa ne nwa sɔ aleɛ ne, nnɛmaa bedi, ofikyɛ yenwo nde. (Dan. 1:5, 8) Nzu yeɛ né bɛkɔyɛ ɔ? Bekuro a ahaa bɛkɔhã kyɛ, ‘Yemia yɛ masta yeɛ ole bɛ; bɛkã kyɛ yɛyɛ nikyeebie a, yelle hwee nga. Yeɛ yanni b’aleɛ ne bie a, ɔhene ne kɔfɛ yaa. Ɔba sɔ a, né yɛyɛ ye sɛ? Yeti yedi a ɔtte hwee.’ Yede belili anaa banni?
<<<P>>>
11 Mmɛɛmaa bɔ né bɛkwaatete bɛ wɔ ahemvie berɛ ne, benu nna pɛ yeɛ Bible ne bɔ bɛ dumaa kyɛ betiele Yehowa dwirɛ ɔ. Bɛ yeɛ ole Daniel, Sadrake, Mɛsake, yeɛ Abedinego. Benu biala bokale ye mmango maa ɔyɛle bɔ Yehowa kuro ɔ. Yeti bɛhane kyɛ beli ɔhene ne aleɛ ne, banni. Ɛhe maa yenwu kyɛ Yehowa ngome yeɛ ne bɛpena kyɛ bɛsõ ye ɔ. Bɔ bɛyɛle nati Yehowa hyirale bɛ.—Dan. 1:6-12, 17.
<<<P>>>
12 Ɛte abrandeɛ anaa taluwa a, ɛyɛ a né ebiemɔ piepia wɔ kyɛ ɛyɛ bɔ Yehowa nguro anaa? Yede, sɛ esuesua Daniel ne ye nnamvo nza ne a, ɔkɔboka wɔ. Benu biala bokale ye mmango maa ɔyɛle bɔ Yehowa kuro ɔ. Yeti wɔ koso bɛbɔ betie Yehowa dwirɛ ne, fa bɛ adamvoa. Mmenia bɔ bɛte sɔ ne, bɔ wiase ne yɛ ne, bɛnyɛ bie. (1 Yoh. 2:15-17) Nningyein bie wɔ berɛ a, wayɛ kyɛ ɔte kãamba bie, yeti andie Yehowa po a ɔtte hwee. Nakoso sɛ wɔ nnamvo yeɛ ole bɛbɔ bɛdɔ Yehowa a, ɛhene anu po bɛkɔboka wɔ maa ɛkɔyɛ bɔ okuro ɔ. Ɛyɛ sɔ a Yehowa kohyira wɔ. (Luka 16:10) Na sɛ siɛ ye, edwirɛ pirikua bie si a, ɔnate kyɛ nningyein bɔ ‘ɔte kãa’ nu po, etiele Yehowa nati, ɔngɔyɛ se kyɛ ɛkɔyɛ bɔ okuro ɔ.
