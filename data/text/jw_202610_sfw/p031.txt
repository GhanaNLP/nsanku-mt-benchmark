aliemaa bie subaen ti sɔ anigye ne te kɔ koso ɛ? Ɔba sɔ a, gyidie bɔ yele ye kyɛ Bible nu dwirɛ bɔ yasua ne si ne, nahorɛ biala nne berɛ kṍ ne, yeɛ ɔkɔboka yɛ ɔ. Ɔkɔmaa yɛkonwu kyɛ, sɛ yɛ nu bie yɛ nikyee bɔ ɔttemaye po a, nahorɛ ne de ye ala yeɛ yanwu ye ne.
<<<P>>>
Sɛ yesua Bible daa biala a, ɔkɔmaa yɛkonwu kyɛ nahorɛ nala yeɛ yanwu ye ne. Ɛhene kɔmaa yɛkonya gyidie kyɛ Yehowa asafo ne si ne, na-horɛ biala nne berɛ kṍ. Yeti sɛ aliemaa bie yɛ ni-kyee bɔ ɔttemaye po a, yɛngɔyakyi asafo ne.—Edw. 16:8; nea alaka nanu dwirɛ ne koso. Ye dwirɛti yeɛ ole “Nnefi Yehowa Asafo Nanu.”
<<<P>>>
Nnefi Yehowa Asafo Nanu
<<<P>>>
Fa ye kyɛ etu atee kɔ nekaabie bɔ ɛberɛ yɛ nyemene, yeti ɛɛfole bass bɔ ɔkɔ berɛ ɔ. Atee ne wale paa. Atee tendemaa koso de, ɔne ɛfɛɛ yeɛ bɛnate ɔ, na ebiaa ɛkɔla kãa po. Nakoso wɔ-nye agye, ofikyɛ ɛkɔ nekaa bɔ ɛberɛ yɛ nyemene.
<<<P>>>
Nakoso yɛfa ye kyɛ drava ne kɔ amirika soma; ɛkwaahã a wafɛ yaa. Anaa sona bɔ ɔte wɔnwo ne, ɔkora da alaa a né ɔfa ye ti bato wɔ so. Ɔwɔ nu kyɛ bɛnwo yɛ ahyi de, nakoso ɛkɔtena bass nanu sɔ ala. Ofikyɛ sɔ bass ne, yeɛ ɔfa wɔ kɔ ne-kaa bɔ ɛberɛ yɛ nyemene ne.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ koso, ɔte kyɛbɔ yetu atee yɛkɔ Paradise. Yɛ bass yeɛ ole Yehowa asafo ne, na yɛne alie-maamɔ yeɛ yɛte nu ɔ. Ɔyɛ yɛ fɛ paa kyɛ yɛne bɛ wɔ asafo nanu ɔ. Nakoso ebiaa benu bie kɔyɛ ni-kyee ma ɔkɔyɛ yɛ yea, anaa aliemaa bie bɔ yebu ye paa kɔyɛ bɔne pirikua. Ɔba sɔ a, ongyire kyɛ yamini. Sɛ yekonya ngoa a, Yehowa asafo nala yeɛ odikyɛ yɛtena nu ɔ. Sɛ yede yɛto nu kyɛ na-horɛ nala yeɛ yanwu ye ne, na Yehowa asafo ne yeɛ bekyirekyire sɔ nahorɛ ne a, ɔkɔmaa yɛ gyi-die kɔyɛ se. Ɔba sɔ a, bɔ okosi biala yengofi asa-fo nanu.
