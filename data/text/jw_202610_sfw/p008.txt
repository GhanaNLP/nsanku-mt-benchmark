Isaiah 33:6
"He is the stability of your times" ♡

6 Kenga Filipifoɛ 4:6, 7. Adwenedwene de, nikyee kɔ̃ bɔ ɔkɔboka yɛ maa yekoli so yeɛ ole mbaebɔ. Nzuati yeɛ yekã sɔ ɔ? Ofikyɛ sɛ yɛbɔ mbaeɛ yɛda Yehowa ase wɔ bɔ ɔyɛ ma yɛ kyẽa biala nanwo a, okyire kyɛ yɛnye sɔ bɔ wayɛ wama yɛ ne. Afei ɔboka yɛ maa yɛfa y’adwene yesie bɔ wayɛ wama yɛ naso, na bɔ yelle ye ne, yɛmva yɛnha yɛnwo. Sɛ yenwu bɔ Yehowa ayɛ ama yɛ ne a, ɔkɔmaa yekonya gyidie kyɛ, bɔ ɔte biala ɔkɔnea yɛ, na yɛngɔfa ɛhema nwo adwenedwene yɛngɔha yɛnwo. (Mat. 6:25-34) Afei koso, sɛ yefi yɛ ahone nu yɛka yɛ haw yekyire Yehowa a, onwu ye, na otie. Na ɔkɔmaa “Nyameɛ asomdwee” bɔ ɔbo ndeaseɛ amukoraa so ne, ɔkɔnea y’ahone ne y’adwene so.

7 Sɛ yanyɛ boniayɛ a, Yehowa nye kɔgye yɛnwo. Yɛse kyɛ akyɛdeɛ pá, ɔne akyɛdeɛ bɔ odi mu biala de, Yehowa berɛ yeɛ ofi ɔ. (Kenga Yakobo 1:17.) Yehowa dɔ yɛ paa, ofikyɛ wahyɛ yɛ nningyein pee. Ebie yeɛ ole ɔwa bɔ ɔmaa oowu lele yɛ ne. Afei wama yɛ anyelasoɛ kyɛ, kyẽabie yɛkɔtena ase daa. Nna mmɔden bie bɔ yɛbɔ ti yeɛ wafa wahyɛ yɛ ɔ, na mmom ɔdɔ yɛ ti ɛne. (Rom. 3:24; Ɛfe. 2:8) Yeti sɛ yɛkɔso yɛfa Yehowa adamvoa, na yɛmaa yɛnye sɔ bɔ wayɛ wama yɛ ne a, ɔkɔmaa yekopingye ye.

8 Bɔ Yehowa ayɛ ama wɔ ne, dwenedwene yenwo. Sɛ ɛnea w’abrabɔ nu a, ɛkae ɔhaw bie bɔ ɛfale nu bɔ ɔmaa ɔnye so tãne wɔ anaa? Ebiaa w’abusuaniɛ anaa wɔ damvo bie wuli, ɛbɔle anwonyerɛ kandinga bie, anaa Yehowa bɔ ɛsö ye nati, bɛmaa ebie fale wɔ nye so. Ɛhemɔ amukoraati ɔyɛle sɛɛ bɔ ɛhora gyinane nu ɔ? Wɔ bɔbɔ deinn ɛse ye kyɛ sɛ nna Yehowa yeɛ ɔbokale wɔ a, ahaa angora yenoa gyina. (Kenga 2 Korintofoɛ 4:7) Sɛ ɛdwenedwene ɛhe anwo a, ekonwu kyɛ Yehowa ayɛ bie ama wɔ pá, yeti daabiala ɛkɔla ye ase.—Edw. 103:2.
