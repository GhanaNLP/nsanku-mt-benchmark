DWEIN 135
Yehowa Adesrɛ Ne Sɛ: “Me Ba, Yɛ Onyansafo”
<<<P>>>
BƆ ADESŨA NE KƆHÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Bɛbɔ bɛdɔ Yehowa ne, sɛ mmrandeɛ ne ndaluwa fa bɛ adamvoa a, ɔkɔboka bɛ maa bɛkɔhɔso bɛkɔsõ ye.
<<<P>>>
Adamvoa Pá De, Mvasoɛ Wɔ So
<<<P>>>
‘Sɛ ebie fa ngondii toa sona horɛ a, ɔkɔhora koli ye so ngunim, nakoso mmenia nyɔ de, ɔngɔhora ngoli bɛ so ngunim.’—NWO. 4:12.
<<<P>>>
YƐ BƆ yɛsõ Yehowa ne, yenyane a ahaa bɔ Yehowa kuro ngo-me yee daa bĩala yɛkɔyɛ ɔ. Nakoso bɔ ɔkɔso wɔ wiase nanu ne, ɔmaa ɔyɛ se ma yɛ, ofikyɛ annea a, né yasuesua bɛ. Ebie yeɛ ole bɔ begyina so bɛfa adamvoa ne. Mmeni pee wɔ berɛ a, b’a-dwene yɛ bɛ kyɛ, ebie nnamvo mɔ sone a, ɛhene yeɛ ɔte pa ɔ. Nakoso bɔ ɔyɛ aworabɔ yeɛ ole kyɛ, bɔ bɛfa nu ne, ekonwu ye ne, né bɛ nnamvo nemɔ po nze. Ebiemɔ koso belle nnamvoa, ofikyɛ bɛnwa yenwo nhia. Mmrandeɛ ne ndaluwa po de, benu pee wɔ berɛ a, bɛ mmango mmrandeɛ ne ndaluwa yeɛ betu bɛ fo ɔ. Yehowa ne Yesu koso ɛ, mmenia bɔ yɛfa bɛ adamvoa ne, benwu ye sɛ?
<<<P>>>
2 Mmenia bɔ bɛkɔboka yɛ maa yɛkɔsõ Yehowa ne, bɛ yeɛ ɔpena kyɛ yɛfa bɛ adamvoa ɔ. Sɔ yeɛ ɔwa Yesu yɛle ɔ. Ye aso-
<<<P>>>
1. Kyɛbɔ bɛfa adamvoa ne, mmenia nwu ye sɛ?
<<<P>>>
2. (a) Nwamɔ yeɛ Yesu fale bɛ adamvoa ɔ? (b) Kyɛbɔ Nworɛ Dwirɛhanɛnie 4:9-12 ka ne, ɛfa sona pá adamvoa a, ɔkɔboka wɔ sɛ?
