3 “Yudatɔ” si ŋu Zakariya ƒo nu tsoe le eƒe nyagblɔɖia me la le tsitre ɖi na ame siwo Mawu tsɔ eƒe gbɔgbɔ kɔkɔea si ami na. Ame siawo kee Biblia yɔ be “Mawu ƒe Israel la.” (Gal. 6:16) Woaɖu fia kple Yesu le dzifo. (Heb. 3:1) ‘Ame ewoawo’ le tsitre ɖi na Yehowa subɔla siwo le mɔ kpɔm be yewoava nɔ agbe tegbee le Paradiso me le anyigba dzi. Ame siawo nye gbe vovovo dola siwo tso dukɔ vovovowo me.
<<<P>>>
4 Le ƒe 1919 me la, Kristo tia nɔviŋutsu amesiaminawo ƒe ƒuƒoƒo aɖe wonye “kluvi nuteƒewɔla kple aɖaŋudzela.” Nɔvi siawo de dzesii be ele be nya nyuia nade dukɔwo katã me, eye esia bia be woaɖe Biblia me gbedeasia gɔme ɖe gbegbɔgblɔ geɖe me. (Mat. 24:45) Ɣemaɣi la, Biblia Nusrɔ̃viawo nɔ “Xexemewɔwɔ Ƒe Foto-Drama” ɖem na amewo le dukɔ vovovowo me xoxo, gake kluvi nuteƒewɔlaa kpɔe be ehiã be yewoagawɔ geɖe wu. Le kpɔɖeŋu me, wota Biblia-srɔ̃gbalẽ si ƒe tanya e nye Mawu Ƒe Saŋku le ƒe 1921 me, eye wova ɖe egɔme ɖe gbe vovovo 36 me. Le ƒe 1935 me la, kluvi nuteƒewɔlaa se egɔme be “amehã gã” aɖee anɔ agbe tegbee le anyigba dzi, eye esia na wokpɔ ale si gbegbe dɔa keke tae. Aleae wòdzɔe be le ƒe mawo me la, Yehowa ɖo gɔmeɖoanyi na gbeƒãɖeɖedɔ si ava keke ta aɖo xexea me godoo ale be Zakariya ƒe nyagblɔɖia nava eme.

“WÒ DUKƆ ATSƆ WO ƉOKUIWO ANA FAA”
<<<P>>>
5 Esi nya nyuia yi edzi nɔ ta kekem la, woke ɖe ame geɖe siwo si dzi nyui le la ŋu le gbe vovovo dolawo dome. Abe ale si Biblia gblɔe ɖi ene la, ame siwo tso xexea ƒe akpa vovovowo va tsɔ ɖe le nya nyuia me eye wodi be yewoasubɔ Yehowa. (Xlẽ Yesaya 2:2-4.) Be woate ŋu akpe ɖe ame siawo katã ŋu la, Mawu ƒe amewo ‘tsɔa wo ɖokuiwo nana faa’ kpɔa gome le gbeƒãɖeɖedɔa me. Gake menye ema ɖeɖe ko o, woɖea míaƒe agbalẽwo gɔme ɖe gbe vovovowo me, wotaa wo, eye woɖoa wo ɖe xexea ƒe akpa vovovowo. (Ps. 68:11; 110:3) Azɔ hã, nɔvi siwo nye kɔmpiuta dzi dɔɖowo ŋuti nunyalawo kple aɖaŋudɔwɔla bibiwo trɔ asi le dɔwɔnuwo abe jw.org kple JW Library® ene ŋu, eye esia kpena ɖe gbegbɔgblɔ vovovo dolawo ŋu wosrɔ̃a nu geɖe tso Biblia me.

6 Gbɔgbɔmenuɖuɖu si kluvi nuteƒewɔla kple aɖaŋudzelaa naa mí la ɖea vi na ame siwo tso dukɔwo kple gbegbɔgblɔwo katã me. Eye mɔ aɖe si nu woawo hã ɖenɛ fiana le be yewokpɔa ŋudzedze ɖe gbɔgbɔmenuɖuɖua ŋue nye be wodzɔa nu faa tsɔ kpea asi ɖe xexea me katã ƒe gbeƒãɖeɖedɔa ŋu. Esia wɔe be míete ŋu taa míaƒe agbalẽwo ɖe gbe siwo wu 1,100 me. Eye ƒe sia ƒe la, ame akpe alafa geɖe xɔa nyɔnyrɔ.
