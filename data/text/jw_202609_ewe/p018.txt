MÍELE GBEFÃ ƉEM YEHOWA
ƑE ŊKƆ LE DUKƆWO DOME
<<<P>>>
12 Dɔ siwo gbegɔmeɖelawo wɔna la ɖea vi na mí katã. Tso gbe aɖe gbe ke la, gbegɔmeɖelawo ku kutri vevie ɖe Biblia gɔme ɖe gbe vovovowo me ale be ame geɖe nate ŋu axlẽ Mawu ƒe Nyaa le wo degbe me.* Le míaƒe ŋkekeawo me la, woɖe Ŋɔŋlɔ Kɔkɔeawo—Xexe Yeye Gɔmeɖeɖe Biblia gɔme ɖe gbegbɔgblɔ geɖe me eye esia le kpekpem ɖe Yehowa subɔlawo ŋu le xexea me godoo be woasrɔ̃ nya-teƒe si le Mawu ƒe Nyaa me. De dzesi nya si hamemetsitsi aɖe si le Nepal gblɔ tso Biblia gɔmeɖeɖe sia ŋu. Egblɔ be: “Ne mexlẽ Biblia sia le mía degbe me la, ɖeko wòwɔna nam abe Yehowa le nu ƒom nam ene. Ewɔe be nye kple Mawu dome va le kplikplikpli eye nye xɔse gasẽ ɖe edzi.” Woɖe Xexe Yeye Gɔmeɖeɖe Biblia bliboa alo eƒe akpa aɖewo gɔme ɖe gbegbɔgblɔ siwo wu 300 me. Woɖe egɔme wòde pɛpɛpɛ, emenyawo le bɔbɔe eye wòfia nyateƒe si ku ɖe Yehowa ŋu. Esia hã le eme tae Yehowa Ɖasefo siwo le xexea me godoo la ƒe dzixɔsewo kple nufiafiawo le ɖeka. (1 Kor. 1:10) Ke hã, nu nyui bubuwo gaɖe dzesi le Biblia gɔmeɖeɖe sia ŋu.
<<<P>>>
13 Mawu ƒe ŋkɔ Yehowa medze le Biblia gɔmeɖeɖe geɖe siwo li egbea la me o, gake edze le Xexe Yeye Gɔmeɖeɖe Biblia ya me zi akpe geɖe. Anɔ abe ƒe alafa ɖeka-e nye esi va yi la, àkpɔ Mawu ƒe ŋkɔa le Biblia geɖe me, vevietɔ Hebri Ŋɔŋlɔawo me. Ke hã egbea, subɔsubɔhakplɔla siwo gblɔna be yewonye Mawu teƒenɔlawo mezãa Mawu ƒe ŋkɔa o, eye womedi be amewo nanyae hã o. Le kpɔɖeŋu me, Katolikohaa ƒe ŋgɔnɔlawo ɖe Mawu ƒe ŋkɔa ɖa le wofe Bibliawo kple agbalẽ bubuwo me.* Sɔlemeha bubu geɖewo hã ɖe Mawu ƒe ŋkɔa ɖa le wofe Biblia gɔmeɖeɖewo me. Gake le Xexe Yeye Gɔmeɖeɖe Biblia me ya la, gbegɔmeɖelawo gbugbɔ Mawu ƒe ŋkɔa ɖe afi siwo katã wòdze le le Biblia ƒe asinunɔŋlɔgbalẽ gbãtɔawo me. Eya ta àkpɔ Mawu ƒe ŋkɔa le gbegbɔgblɔ siwo katã me woɖe Xexe Yeye Gɔmeɖeɖe Biblia gɔme ɖo la me. Na míakpɔ nu si tae esia le vevie ɖa.
<<<P>>>
14 Ŋɔŋlɔawo gblɔ nya vovovo siwo te gbe ɖe ale si wòle vevie be míanya Mawu ƒe ŋkɔa ahanɔ ezãm la dzi. Le kpɔɖeŋu me, wode dzi ƒo na mawusubɔlawo le Psalmowo me be ‘woakafu Yehowa ƒe ŋkɔ.’ (Ps. 135:1) Esi Yesu nɔ eƒe nusrɔ̃lawo fiam ale si woado gbe ɖa na Yehowa la, efia wo be woagblɔ be: “Wò ŋkɔ ŋuti nakɔ.” Emegbe esi Yesu nɔ gbe dom ɖa na Fofoa la, egblɔ be: “Mena wonya wò ŋkɔ la.” (Mat. 6:9; Yoh. 17:26) Apostolo Paulo kple nyagblɔɖila Yoel siaa gblɔ be: “Ame sia ame si yɔa Yehowa ƒe ŋkɔ la, woaɖee.” (Rom. 10:13) Ame siwo zãa Biblia gɔmeɖeɖe siwo me woɖe Mawu ƒe ŋkɔa ɖa le la mate ŋu awɔ Ŋɔŋlɔawo me nya vovovo siawo dzi o.
<<<P>>>
* Woɖe Hebri Ŋɔŋlɔawo gɔme zi gbãtɔ ɖe Helagbe me le ƒe 150 Do Ŋgɔ Na Yesu (D.Y.) me.
<<<P>>>
* Le ƒe 2008 me la, Katolikohaa ƒe ŋgɔnɔla si woyɔna be Papa Benedict Wuiadelia gblɔ be ame aɖeke “naga-zã” Mawu ƒe ŋkɔ le Katoliko subɔsubɔwɔnawo, hadzidzi-wo alo gbedodoɖawo me o, eye “womegayɔ ŋkɔa” hã o. Kpɔ October 1, 1997 ƒe Gbetakpɔxɔ, axa 16-18, memama 4-8 hã.
