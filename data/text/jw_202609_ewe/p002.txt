<<<P>>>
Ka Ðe Edzi Be Yehowa Akpɔ Tawò
<<<P>>>
“Ègblɔ be: ‘Yehowae nye nye sitsofe.’”—PS. 91:9.
<<<P>>>
MÍELE ŋkeke mamlɛawo ƒe yɔyrɔe nu. Eye etefe madidi o, nu ɖedzesiwo adzɔ le xexe bliboa me. Yehowa adee dzi me na dukɔwo be woawɔ “nu si yeɖo ɖe tame la dzi,” si nye be “woatsɔ woƒe fiaɖuƒe ana lã wɔadã la,” si le tsitre ɖi na Dukɔ Ƒoƒuawo.* (Nyaɖ. 17:17) Eyome, lã wɔadã la si va kpɔ ŋusẽ geɖe kpakple dukɔ siwo katã wɔ ɖeka kplii la atsrɔ̃ alakpasubɔsubɔhawo katã ɖa keŋkeŋ. (Nyaɖ. 17:16) Emae anye xaxa gã la ƒe gɔmedzedze. Nu kae akplɔe ɖo?
<<<P>>>
2 Eyae nye be dziɖuɖu siwo le xexea me godoo awɔ ɖeka aho ɖe Yehowa ƒe amewo ŋu. (Eze. 38:14-16) Nu ka tae? Agbalẽ si nye Yehowa Ƒe Tadedeagu Dzadzɛa—Wogbugbɔe Ðo Anyi Mlɔeba! gblɔ be: “Satana afoe ɖe dukɔwo nu be woabu mɔ na tadedeagula vavãwo kple tadedeagu dzadzɛa siaa le anyigba dzi.” Le ema megbe la, nudzɔdzɔ vevitɔ afe ati; eyae nye Armagedon-ʋaa. Yesu axɔ ŋgɔ na ame akpe alafa ɖeka blaene vɔ eneawo kpakple mawudɔlawo woatsrɔ̃ ame vɔ̃ɖiwo eye woaxɔ na nutefewɔlawo. Satana ƒe xexea nu ayi eye míadze agbe yeye gɔme le xexe yeyea me.—Nyaɖ. 16:14-16; 19:11-21.
<<<P>>>
