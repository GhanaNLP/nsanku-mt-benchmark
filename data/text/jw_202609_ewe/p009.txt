Israel-viwo wɔ ɖe Yehowa ƒe mɔfiame dzi togbo esia ate ŋu ana Egiptetɔwo nado dziku gɔ̃ hã
<<<P>>>
(Kpɔ memama 3)
<<<P>>>
alẽtsui alo gbɔ̃tsui eye woatsɔ eƒe ʋu asisi ɖe wofe aƒe ƒe ʋɔtrutiwo ŋu ale be woanɔ dedie. (2 Mose 12:5, 7) Ekema Yehowa ƒe dɔlaa ato aƒe siwo ƒe ʋɔtrutiwo ŋu wosisi ʋu ɖo la ŋu eye mawu amegbetɔwo alo lãwo ƒe ŋgɔgbevi aɖeke le aƒe mawo me o. Gake anya sesẽ na Israel-viwo be woaɖo to. Nu ka tae? Nu si tae nye be Egiptetɔwo bua alẽtsui be ele kɔkɔe eye wosubɔnɛ. Eya ta Israel-viwo anya vɔ̃ be ne Egiptetɔwo kpɔ yewo la, woatu nu kpli yewo. (2 Mose 8:25-27) Ke hã, Israel-viwo kpɔe be ele vevie be yewoaɖo to, eye ‘wowɔ abe ale si tututu Yehowa de se na wo ene.’ (2 Mose 12:28) Le esia ta, Yehowa kpɔ woƒe ŋgɔgbeviwo katã ta.
<<<P>>>
4 Zã ma tututu mee Israel-viwo dzo le Egipte. Gbã, Yehowa na woɖo ta Fudzĩa ƒe dziehe gome, gake eyome ena mɔfiame aɖe wo si wɔ moyaa na wo. Egblɔ na wo be woatrɔ aɖo ta anyiehe eye woafu asaɖa anyi ɖe teƒe aɖe si le atsiaƒua kple towo dome. Teƒe si Yehowa be woafu asaɖa anyi ɖo le Fudzĩa nu la ana woaxaxa eye Farao ƒe asrafowo ate ŋu ava dze wo dzi bɔbɔe. Le esia hã me la, Israel-viwo ɖo to nya si Yehowa gblɔ to Mose dzi. (2 Mose 14:1-4) Gake esi Israel-viwo kpɔ Egipte-ʋakɔa gbɔna la, ‘dzidzi ƒo wo’ eye wohe nya ɖe Mose ŋu be ekplɔ yewo va afi ma be yewoaku! (2 Mose 14:5-11) Nu ka tae wòve Israel-viwo be yewoɖo to Yehowa? Eye aleke Yehowa bu woƒe nuwɔna mae?
<<<P>>>
5 Biblia gblɔ be le Fudzĩa nu la, Israel-viwo ‘mekpɔ ŋudzedze ɖe nukudɔ siwo Yehowa wɔ’ le Egipte la ŋu o; “womeɖo ŋku” eƒe amenuveve geɖeawo dzi o. (Xlẽ Psalmo 106:7.) Togbɔ be wokpɔ ale si Yehowa xɔ na woƒe ŋgɔgbeviwo esi wòhe fuwɔame ewolia va Egiptetɔwo dzi hã la, womete ŋu de dzesi lɔlɔ̃ si gbegbe wòɖe fia wo le nudzɔdzɔ ma me la o, eye womeka ɖe edzi be agate ŋu aɖe yewo o. Yehowa de dzesi dzi ƒe nɔnɔme si le wo si hegblɔ be “wodze aglã . . . le Fudzĩ la nu.” Togbɔ be wodze Mose yome to Fudzĩa me hã la, wometrɔ woƒe agbenɔnɔ o. Le yeyiɣi aɖe megbe la, Yehowa va gbe nu le dzidzime ma gbɔ eye meɖe mɔ woyi Ŋugbedodonyigbaa dzi o. (Ps. 106:21, 24-26) Nufiame kae le eme na mí?
