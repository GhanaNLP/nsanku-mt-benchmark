BƲTAYI
Kezimɔ ɔkɔwʊwa wʊ Bayɩbʊlʊ dɩyɛzumanlɛmaa ma bɔkɔnwʊ Gyehova bʊwɔ, mɔɔ yɩ anyɩ deli nwʊmɔ nɩɩ mɔɔ yɩ anyɩ iáleli nwʊmɔ.
<<<P>>>
Bʊwa Mmenii Mvʊlɔ Ma Bɛɣá Bɔnwʊ Gyehova Bʊwɔ
<<<P>>>
“Nnwʊanlɩ mɔɔ íni awielɩyɛ nɩ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ bɔkɔnwʊ mɔwʊ, nɔhwanlɛ Nyamɩnlɩ ɔkʊ̃ kyeinyii nɩ.”—DWƆ. 17:3.
<<<P>>>
SƐ BAYƖBƲLƲ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʊlɔ be bɔ yɩ tʊ kɛɛ ɩbaa dɩyɛzumanlɛ, ɩmaa munwalɩyɛ, ɩkãã yi dedi nwʊ odwokɔ ikehile mmenii, na ayinzi yi inuma a, kɛ ɛtɩ nyanlɩ se a? Pɩlazʊnɛ bela íni nwʊmɔ kɛɛ, wʊ anyɩ de paa! Naasʊ̃ yezi kɛɛ, Gyehova yɛ ɩwʊwalɩ Bayɩbʊlʊ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʊlɔ yɩ ma inyianlɩ anyunumihɔlɛ mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ a, meti yɛfa nwʊmɔ ayɛlɛyɩlɛ yɛma yɩ.—1 Kɔl. 3:5-9; 3 Dwɔ. 3, 4.
<<<P>>>
2 Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ apɛdɩ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ ‘mmenii munwatĩĩ bɔkɔnwʊ ehilehilelɛ baaba nɩ mɔɔ ɩfalɩ Gyisɛsɩ Kɩlayɩsɩ yɩ nwʊ nɩ, na beanyia nnwʊanlɩ.’ (Kɩnga 1 Timoti 2:3, 4.) Meti Gyehova pɛ kɛɛ mmenii nwʊ yɩ bʊwɔ, na bɛba bɔnwʊ nninyeni ngɩnlɩma mɔɔ ɩyɛɛ nɩɩ mɔɔ ɩbayɛ munwatĩĩ. (Gyɛ. 9:24) Sɛ abiye kɛyɛ zɔ a, ɩsɛ kɛɛ ɩnwʊ ehilehilelɛ baaba nɩ. Eyi iáhile kɛɛ ekenyia Nyamɩnlɩ nwʊ nnimidi kɛkɛ. Bayɩbʊlʊ nwʊ nnimidivʊlɔ be kã eyi nwʊ odwokɔ kɛɛ “abiye kɛma Nyamɩnlɩ nwʊ nnimidi mɔɔ inyia nɩ kɛzɩnza yɩ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ.” Sɛ Bayɩbʊlʊ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʊlɔ be ba nwʊ Gyehova bʊwɔ a, ɩkã kezimɔ ɩbɔ yɩ ɛbɩla nɩ, na eyi ɩma inyia “nnwʊanlɩ mɔɔ íni awielɩyɛ” nwʊ anyɩnlɩlazʊ.—Dwɔ. 17:3.
<<<P>>>
3 Gyehova ma yenyia ɔnwʊmɔ ɔhvanɩ be, mɛyɩ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ yɛkɔwʊwa mmenii mvʊlɔ ma bɛkɛɣa kɔnwʊ yɩ bʊwɔ. Wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ eyi nu nɩ, yekozunzu ndwokɔyizialɛ eyimmɔ nwʊ: Mɩnla ti mɔɔ ɩsɛ kɛɛ Bayɩbʊlʊ dɩyɛzumanlɛmaa nwʊ Gyehova bʊwɔ a? Kɛ ɩkɛyɛ na yeahilehile bɛ ma bɛanwʊ kezimɔ Gyehova dɩ ankasa a? Yɛɛ, kɛ ɩkɛyɛ na yɛawʊwa bɛ ma bɛava Gyehova nwʊ nnimidi mɔɔ benyia nɩ bɛawɔ bɛ ɛbɩla a?
<<<P>>>
1. Sɛ ɔnwʊ kɛɛ Bayɩbʊlʊ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʊlɔ be inyiaa anyunumihɔlɛ a, kɛ ɛtɩ nyanlɩ se a?
<<<P>>>
2. Mɩnla yɛ Gyehova pɛ kɛɛ Bayɩbʊlʊ dɩyɛzumanlɛmaa bɔnwʊ yɩ a? (1 Timoti 2:3, 4)
