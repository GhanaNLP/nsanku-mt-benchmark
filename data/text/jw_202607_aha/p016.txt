8 Sɛ yɛnwʋ Gyehova buwɔ a, anwʋma ɩávalɩ yɛ nɩɩ yɩ avinli kʋlaa. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, sɛ ɛkɩnga wɔ Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ eyile bela owie a, kɛ ɩma ɛtɩ nyanlɩ se a? Na sɛ ekila broadcasting egyimalilɛ, anzɛnyɩi ɔkɔ ayiyialɩyɛ sʋ̃ a, kɛ ɩma ɛtɩ nyanlɩ se a? Mbɔmɩ sũmaa nɩ, ɩma yɛtɩ nyanlɩ kɛɛ yeyike yɛ sʋlɔ Ezilɛ nɩ paa. Mɩnla ti a? Iluwakɛɛ yezuma Gyehova yɩ nwʋ dɩyɛ sũmaa. Na eyi kã yɛ ma yɛyɩ yɩ ayɛlɛ na yeda yɩ asɩ. Yɛtɩ nyanlɩ dɩkɛɛ ndwomidulɛvʋlɔ mɔɔ ɩdwɩnɩndwɩnɩnlɩ kezimɔ Gyehova dwɩnɩ yɩ nwʋ nɩ nwʋ nɩ. Ihanlɩ kɛɛ, “Nyamɩnlɩ dɩ mɩ ahʋnlɩnɩ bʋdanlɩ nɩɩ mɩ kyɛfʋã daabaa.” (Kɩnga Ndwomi 73:24-28.) Mmɔdɩnɩ bela mɔɔ yɛkɔwɔ kɛɛ yɛkɔnwʋ Gyehova buwɔ nɩ, ɩhyɛ anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ mɔɔ yɛwɔ yɛma yɛ nɩɩ yɩ ɔhɔnwʋvalɛ mɔɔ ɩtʋ bʋlɔ baaba nɩ nu ɛzizibɛ.
<<<P>>>
9 Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ tu yɛ fʋlɔ kɛɛ ‘yɛnwú’ Gyehova. (Ɛyɛ. 3:5, 6) Eyi kile kɛɛ, yɛkɔdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ nninyeni mɔɔ yezuma yɛvalɩ Gyehova nwʋ nɩ nwʋ na yɛanwʋ kezimɔ ɩtɩ nyanlɩ wɔ yɛ nyelɩyɛ azʋlɔtʋ nwʋ. Ɔzɔ mɔɔ yɛkɛyɛ nɩ kɔwʋwa yɛ ma yekezizi abɔkɔba. Mɔwʋ dɩyɛ sunzu ndianwʋ eyi nwʋ kila: Agyiahʋnlɩmaa sũmaa wɔ kɛnɩ mɔɔ bɛfa bɔ nwʋkãvʋlɔ yɩ mfonyini bɛta dɩka mɔɔ bɔkɔnwʋ yɩ. Mbiyemmɔ po fa wula bɛ walɛtɩ nu, anzɛnyɩi bɛfa betiã bo foonu zʋ. Eyi bʋwa bɛ ma bɛta bɔdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ ɔlɔlɛ mɔɔ bɔwɔ bɛma bɔ nwʋkãvʋlɔ yɩ nɩ nwʋ. Sɛ agyiahʋnlɩvʋlɔ be yɛ zɔ a, ɩbʋwa yɩ ma isizi abɔkɔ mɔɔ ɩkɛma yɩ nwʋkãvʋlɔ yɩ anyɩ kele. Akʋ̃ sʋ̃, ɩbɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ɩlɛ́yɛ nninyeni mɔɔ ɩkɛma yɩ ɛyɛɛlɛ kɔwɔ. Ɔzɔ la yɛ sɛ amɩyɛ sʋ̃ yɛdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ ɔlɔlɛ mɔɔ yɛwɔ yɛma Nyamɩnlɩ nɩ nwʋ a, ɩkɔwʋwa yɛ ma yekezizi abɔkɔ mɔɔ ɩsɔ yɩ anyɩ a.—Ndw. 19:13, 14.

10 Gyehova wɔlɩ yɛ wɔ yɩ subanɩ zʋ, eyi ti yɛkʋla yɛda yɩ subanɩ ngʋnlɩma yɩ be ali. (Gyn. 1:26) Sɛ yɛta yɛdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ Gyehova yɩ subanɩ azʋlɔtʋ yɩ nwʋ a, ɩkɛma ɩkɛyɛ mɛlɛkɛ kɛɛ yekozumazuma yɩ. Falasiimaa nɩɩ mmɛla ahilehilevʋlɔ mɔɔ nɩ bɔwɔ Gyisɛsɩ yɩ mmɩlɩ zʋ nɩ beáda Gyehova yɩ subanɩ ngʋnlɩma yɩ be ali, meti Gyisɛsɩ dɩndɛlɩ diyialɩ bɛ ɛzizibɛ ɛzizibɛ. Nɩ bezí Mmɛla yɩ buwɔ paa, naasũ Gyisɛsɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ, “bobu bɛ anyɩ bokua ehilehilelɛ mɔɔ ihyinyia baaba wɔ mmɛla nɩ nu nɩ, mɔɔ ɩdɩ kɛɛ pelɩpɛlɩlilɛ, ngokenwunlɛ nɩɩ nɔhuanlɛlilɛ nɩ zʋ.” (Mt. 23:23) Amɩyɛ mɛyɩ, yɛábɛ kɛɛ yɛkɛyɛ kɛɛ Falasiimaa nɩɩ mmɛla ahilehilevʋlɔ nɩ mmɔ. Mfomi sɛ yesuma Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ a, yɛpɛ kɛɛ yɛdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ mɔɔ ɩma yɛnwʋ wɔ Gyehova nwʋ nɩ nwʋ. Ɔzɔ mɔɔ yɛkɛyɛ nɩ kɛma yɛkɛla yi subanɩ ngʋnlɩma nɩ be ali.—1 Dwɔ. 4:8, 11.
