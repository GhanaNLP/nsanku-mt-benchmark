Minyianlɩ alʊbɔ 17 nɩ, eɣile ɔkʊ̃ be, nɩ mɩ nɩɩ mʊ ɔhyʊwa yetuu mpasa, na eliyema benyia be mɔɔ bɔfʊlɔ yɩ Wilson Bashou nɩ ɣikeli mɩ na iɣizialɩ mɩ kɛɛ, “Ezi Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ anvʊhʊba mɔɔ ɩfalɩ eɣile be nwʊ nɩ a?” Odwokɔyizialɛ eyi manlɩ yɛwɔlɩ Bayɩbʊlʊ nu nkɔmmɔ yɛhyɛlɩ. Wilson ɣɩnganlɩ Yikile 17:1, na iɣizialɩ mɩ kɛɛ, “Mɩnla yɛ ‘baka sõmaa’ mɔɔ adwomanlɩwɔlɛnii nɩ da nwʊ nɩ kile a? Nɩ mízi nwʊmɔ hwɩɩ, meti ɩɣɩnganlɩ nkyɩkyɛmi 15 nɩ na ɩhanlɩ kɛɛ baka sõmaa nɩ, iɣyĩ kɛnɩ ɩma mmenii. Mɩ anyɩ leli paa, iluwakɛɛ mɩɣa mʊnwʊnlɩ kɛɛ sɛ Gyehova Dasɩvɔlɔmaa kɩnga Bayɩbʊlʊ nu odwokɔ be mɔɔ yɩ tʊdɩlɛ yɛ sɩ a, Bayɩbʊlʊ yɩ la yɛ bɛfa bekilehile nu a. Mmɩlɩ mɔ yɛ nkɔmmɔ yɩ ɣalɩ awielɩyɛ nɩ, Wilson valɩ The Truth That Leads to Eternal Life buuku yi manlɩ mɩ. Na ɔzɔ buuku blue yi zɩnzanlɩ mɩ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ kolaa kolaa.

Ayile bunlu mɛda nɩ, mʊhɔlɩ mɩ dɩyɛzumanlɛ mɔɔ ili bɔmʊnlɩ wɔ Kɩndɔm Hɔɔlʊ. Na mɩ ɛɣɛɛlɛ iléfi kezimɔ mmeliyema yɩ dɩlɩ bɛ anyunu manlɩ mɩ nɩ kyĩ. Na ɔzʊ̃mɩ ɔzɔ mmɩlɩ yɩ mɔɔ ɩkɔɔ yɩ, milétianlɩ dɩyɛzumanlɛ nu kyĩ. Mɛyɩ mɛda nɩ, Wilson wɔlɩ yɩ tʊ kɛɛ ɩ nɩɩ mɩ sumaa Bayɩbʊlʊ yɩ. Nɩ mɩ anyɩ deli Bayɩbʊlʊ yɩ mɔɔ nɩ musumaa yɩ nwʊ paa, meti nɩ ɩtwʊ mmɩlɩ be po a, yɛfa dɔnɩhwɩlɩ awɔtwɛ yesuma dɩyɛ. Nɩ mɩ awʊvɔlɔ bɛ anyɩ iáleli mɔɔ nɩ musumaa yɩ nwʊ, meti bodwodwuli bediyialɩ mɩ ɛzɩzɩbɛ ɛzɩzɩbɛ. Naasʊ̃, eɣile bela mɔɔ mukozuma dɩyɛ nɩ, nɩ mɩtɩ nɣanlɩ kɛɛ nɔhʊanlɛ yɩ ɛdɩ. Meti wɔ 1983 nu nɩ, munlʊmanlɩ.

MIYIAKILI YUNIVƐSITI NA MIƔA MIƔELI ƆHƲANƖBUKOLƐVƆLƆ
<<<P>>>
Mmɩlɩ mɔ munlʊmanlɩ nɩ, nɩ mʊkɔ yunivɛsiti be mɔɔ ile dima, na nɩ mɩɣɛɛ course be mɔɔ ɩkɛma mɩkɛɣa mɩkɛɣɛ dɔkɩta. Mɩ awʊvɔlɔ valɩ nninyeni sõmaa wɔlɩ afɔlɩ na ama mɩahʊla mɩahɔ sukuulu eyi. Ankɩ mukɔhʊla mʊkɔdʊwa mɩ sukuulu yi zʊ, naasʊ̃ nɩ mɩpɛ kɛɛ mɩɣɛ mɔɔ mʊkɔhʊla bela wɔ Gyehova yɩ ɔzʊ̃mɩnlɛ nu, na mɩfa mɩ mmɩlɩ munwala musũmɩ yɩ.
<<<P>>>
Mɩ papa wɔlɩ mʊ kɔkɔ kɛɛ, “Sɛ wʊzawʊ ɔbɔ wʊ nwʊ mmʊzulɔ na eyiaki Yunivɛsiti a, sa mutu wʊ wɔ awʊdɔ kɩhyĩ!” Eyi manlɩ mɩ ɛɣɛɛlɛ wɔlɩ, na nɩ mɩánwʊ mɔɔ mɩɣɛ. Nɩ mʊdɔ mɩ awʊvɔlɔ paa, meti nɩ mɩábɛ kɛɛ mɩɣɛ dɩyɛ bela ma bɛ sa si azɩ. Meti nɩ mɩta mifinde mʊkɔ mubʊwa sukuulu kɛnɩ ohunluma na mʊzɔ̃ mɩ ahʊnlɩnɩ nu mubɔ mpayɩ kɛɛ, “Gyehova, misiãã wʊ, bʊwa mɩ wɔ mʊ pɔkɔzilɛ eyi nu.” Mmɩlɩ mɔ mizili pɔkɔ kɛɛ mikeyiaki sukuulu nɩ, mɩ papa hã hileli mɩ kɛɛ mʊzɔ̃ awʊdo kɛnɩ mudú. Iɣalɩ yɩ zɔ nɩ, mʊvʊlɔlɩ Wilson, na mɩhanlɩ mɔɔ izili nɩ mihileli yi, na ɩhã ihileli mɩ kɛɛ mɩɣá mʊwʊwa yɩ awʊdo. Meti mɩzɩzalɩ mɩ nninyeni yɩ munwala mugualɩ baagɩ ɔkʊ̃ nu, na mʊhɔ mʊvʊlɩ baasɩ mʊhɔlɩ yɩ awʊdo. Mʊhɔ mudwuli yɩ awʊdo la yɛ mɩdɩlɩ nɣanlɩ kɛɛ mɩ ahʊnlɩnɩ dɔ mu kunlu a. Mʊnwʊnlɩ kɛɛ, amba mizi pɔkɔba.
