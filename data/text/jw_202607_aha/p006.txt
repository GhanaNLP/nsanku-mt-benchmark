Ɔzɔ mɔɔ yɛkɛyɛ nɩ kɛla yɩ ali kɛɛ yɛwɔ Gyehova nu anwʋlazʋ kɛɛ ikeli bʋwabɩlɛ bela nwʋ egyima wɔ yɩzayɩ yɩ mmɩlɩ nu. Sunzu Eliyema baalɛ Laura French yɩ ndianwʋ yɩ nwʋ kila. Wɔ ɔlʋbɔ 1926 nu nɩ, nɩ ɩsɔ̃mɩ wɔ Canada Bɛtɩlɩ. Kɛyɛ alʋbɔ bunlu mɛda nɩ, bɛ nɩ yɩ iádi yɩ buwɔ kʋlaa. Bowuli ndɛnthyɩyɩalɛ bediyialɩ yɩ. Bɛhanlɩ kɛɛ ɩva yɩ nwʋ ɩhɔ ɩwɔ ɔvʋakɩlɛmaa be, eyi ti bɛmanlɩ ɩzɔ̃ Bɛtɩlɩ ɩyɩlɩ yɩ nwʋ. Kɛ ɩyɛlɩ yɩ dɩyɛ izeli wɔ kezimɔ bɛ nɩ yɩ lili nɩ nwʋ a? Iwɔ nʋmɔ kɛɛ ɩyɛlɩ yɩ eyekɛ paa dɩyɛ, naasʋ̃ tákã mmeliyema yɩ anzɛnyɩ ahyɩhyɛdɩ yɩ nwʋ ndwokɔ tanɩ kyĩ. Mfomi, ɩhɔlɩ zʋlɔ ɩvalɩ ɛhyɩmɛ ɩyɛlɩ ɔhʋanlɩkʋlɛlɛ egyima yɩ alʋbɔ anla. Na wɔ ɔlʋbɔ 1940 nu nɩ, bɛzabɛ mɔɔ beli nninyeni anyunu nɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ iziá ɩya ɩzɔ̃mɩ wɔ Bɛtɩlɩ, na eyi yɛlɩ yɩ asibɔnwʋ paa. Kɛ ɩyɛlɩ yɩ dɩyɛ izeli a? Ivalɩ anyɩnlɩlelɛ ileli idianlɩ nʋmɔ. Eliyema baalɛ French valɩ alʋbɔ 50 ɩzɔ̃mɩnlɩ wɔ Bɛtɩlɩ na ɩhɔlɩ zʋlɔ ɩvalɩ nɔhʋanlɛlilɛ ɩzɔ̃mɩnlɩ Gyehova nkʋ̃ʋ̃ kɔbʋmɩ kɛɛ iwuli. Dɩkɛɛ Eliyema baalɛ French nɩ, sɛ bɛ nɩ yɛ iádi yɩ buwɔ po a, sa yɛkʋla yɛyɛ mɔɔ ɩtɩnyɛ na yɛdɩkɛ kɛɛ Gyehova keli bʋwabɩlɛ yɩ nwʋ egyima wɔ yɩzayɩ yɩ mmɩlɩ nu.—Ayɩ. 26:3, 4.

13 Wɔ Bulombunli Devidi yɩ wulo mɛda kɛyɛ alʋbɔ 500 nɩ, Gibiyɔnʋmaa yɩ be dima zia vindenlili Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ nu dɔkɔ̃. Dwuumaa hɔlɩ alɔngɔzɔ̃mɩnlɛ nu wɔ Babilɔnʋ alʋbɔ 70. Naasʋ̃ wɔ ɔlʋbɔ 537 A.Y.M. nu mɔɔ bɛ alɔngɔzɔ̃mɩnlɛ yɩ yalɩ awielɩyɛ nɩ, bo nu be nɩ Amɩnladʋ Zɛlubabɛlɩ zia hɔlɩ Gyelusalamɩ. (Ɛzɩ. 2:1, 2, 58) Na wɔ ɔlʋbɔ 468 A.Y.M. nu sɔ̃ nɩ, Dwuumaa mɔɔ bɛhalɩlɩ Babilɔnʋ nɩ be nɩ Ɛzɩla mɔɔ ɩdɩ ɛhɛlɛvʋlɔ nɩ sɔ̃ hɔlɩ Gyelusalamɩ. (Ɛzɩ. 7:1-7) Bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛ nɩ Zɛlubabɛlɩ nɩ Ɛzɩla hɔlɩ Gyelusalamɩ nɩ, nɩ bɔ nwʋ ɩázʋnɩ. Naasʋ̃ asɔlɩ pika yɩ nu azɔ̃mɩvʋlɔ be sɔ̃ hɩnlanlɩ bɔ nwʋ ma bɔhɔlɩ. Nɩ ɔzɔ mmenii yi bédɩ Yizilayamaa, na ɩkɛyɛ kɛɛ bo nu sɔ̃maa dɩ Gibiyɔnʋmaa bɔ tʋmaa.
<<<P>>>
14 Kɩnga 1 Mmʋlɩzʋdwokɔ 9:2 nu azɩkɛnɩ odwokɔ yɩ. Nɩ “asɔlɩ pika yɩ nu azɔ̃mɩvʋlɔ” kɩnla okuwu mɔɔ beli bɔmʋnlɩ mɔɔ bezia bɔhɔlɩ Gyelusalamɩ nɩ nwʋ. Nɩ eyi yɛ nnwʋánlɩ paa, iluwakɛɛ kɛ Dwuumaa munwatĩĩ yɛ bezia bɔhɔlɩ Gyelusalamɩ a. Nɩ Dwuumaa sɔ̃maa nyia abika nɩɩ ozukua sɔ̃maa wɔ Babilɔnʋ. Meti nɩ bɛábɛ kɛɛ betiã ɛbɩlawɔlɛ baaba mɔɔ nɩ benyia wɔ Babilɔnʋ nɩ ɛkɛnɩ na besia bɔkɔ Gyelusalamɩ bɔkɔ bɔbuwa egyima yɩ. Naasʋ̃ Gibiyɔnʋmaa bɔ tʋmaa mbiyemmɔ nɩɩ Dwuumaa nɔhʋanlɛmaa bɛlɩ yɩ kɛɛ bɔkɔhɔ bekegyina asɔlɩ pika fulɔ bɛkɛma Gyehova na bɛazɔ̃mɩ wɔ ɛkɛnɩ. Iwɔ nʋmɔ kɛɛ nɩ bʋwabɩlɛ nu yɛ sɩ dɩyɛ, naasʋ̃ bezia bɔhɔlɩ Gyelusalamɩ. Nɩ Dwuumaa lɛ azɩlɛ kɛɛ bɛ agyiapadɩ wɔ Yizilaya naasʋ̃ Gibiyɔnʋmaa mɛyɩ, nɩ béni agyiapadɩ bela wɔ Yizilaya. Naasʋ̃, bezia bɔhɔ bɔdʋwalɩ be egyima mɔɔ nɩ bɛyɛ wɔ asɔlɩ pika yɩ nu nɩ zʋ na bɔwʋwalɩ po ma beziyeziyeli Gyelusalamɩ banɩ yɩ.—Ne. 3:26.

15 Mɩnla yɛ odwokɔ eyi ma yɛnwʋ falɩ Gyehova nwʋ a? Eyi buwa yɛ ma yɛnwʋ kɛɛ Gyehova dɔ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɔkɔ zʋlɔ bɔsɔ̃mɩ yɩ nɩ, na eyile bela ɩbʋwa bɛ. Mmɩlɩ mɔ Gyehova leli Gibiyɔnʋmaa wɔ Dwohyuwa yɩ mmɩlɩ zʋ mɛda nɩ, ɩhɔlɩ zʋlɔ iyilalɩ bɔ mmusunwa yɩ alʋbɔ sɔ̃maa. Mmɩlɩ mɔ Babilɔnʋmaa zɩkɛlɩ Gyelusalamɩ wɔ ɔlʋbɔ 607 A.Y.M. nu nɩ, Gyehova leli Gibiyɔnʋmaa bɔ tʋmaa yɩ. Mmɩlɩ mɔ bɔzʋ̃ alɔngɔzɔ̃mɩnlɛ nu bɛyalɩ Yizilaya mɛda nɩ, Gyehova hɔlɩ zʋlɔ valɩ Gibiyɔnʋmaa yɩ lili egyima wɔ asɔlɩ pika yɩ nu. Na ɩkɛyɛ kɛɛ nɩ bo nu be bɛda bebike asɔlɩ pika yɩ po.
