BUWA BAYIBULU DƖYƐZUMANLƐVOLƆ YI MA ƖNWÚ GYEHOVA BUWƆ
<<<P>>>
4 Gyisɛsɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ, Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ ahyɛdɩ mɔɔ ɩdɩ kezi nɩ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ yɛkɛva yɛ ahunlɩnɩ munwatĩĩ, yɛ ɛkɛla munwatĩĩ, yɛ adwɩnɩ munwatĩĩ, nɩ yɛ anwʊzɩzɩbɛ munwatĩĩ yɛkɔlɔ Nyamɩnlɩ. (Kɩnga Maakɩ 12:30.) Sɛ Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ be ba nwʋ Gyehova buwɔ a, ɩma inyia ɔlɔlɛ ɩma Gyehova. Gyehova yɩ nwʋ ɔlɔlɛ yɛ ɩsɛ kɛɛ ɩkã Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ be ma inuma a, na kɛ ebiye a ɩrɛ kɛɛ ɩyɛ yɛzayɛ anzɛnyɩɩ mmeliyema yɩ bɔ ɔhɔnwʋ, anzɛnyɩɩ ebiye a Bayɩbʋlʋ ehilehilelɛ pɔkyɩı be mɔɔ yɩ anyɩ deli nwʋmɔ nɩ ti a. Meti yɛ bʋtayɩ titili yɛ lɛ kɛɛ yɛkɔwʊwa Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ yɩ ma ɩkɛya kɔnwʊ Gyehova buwɔ na ɩalɔ yɩ.
<<<P>>>
5 Ɔlɔlɛ mɛyɩ, ɩ nɩ sutiye yɛ boluwa a. Meti sɛ wʊ Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ yɩ ba nwʋ Gyehova na inyia ɔlɔlɛ ɩma yɩ a, ɩkɛhã yɩ ma ɩkɛyɛ sutiye ɩkɛma Gyehova. Gyisɛsɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ: “Ɩwɔ kɛɛ oyuwadɩ nwʋ yɩ kɛɛ mudɔ Ezilɛ nɩ; meti mɩyɛ dɩyɛ bela mɔɔ Ezilɛ nɩ ikeze mɩ nɩ.” (Dwɔ. 14:31) Meti kɛmɔ Gyisɛsɩ yelɩ nɩ, ɔzɔ la yɛ Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ mɔɔ ɩdɔ Gyehova paa nɩ, ɩbɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ɩkɛyɛ sutiye ɩkɛma Gyehova na iali yɩ nɔhvanlɛ a.—1 Dwɔ. 5:3.
<<<P>>>
6 Sɛ Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ be ba nwʋ Gyehova buwɔ a, ɩkɛyɛ mɛlɛkɛ kɛɛ ikezizi abɔkɔba. Kila ndianwʋ eyi: Sɛ abiye pɛ kɛɛ ɩsuma kaalɩ hwilɛ, na ikila kezimɔ abiyemmɔ fatwu kaalɩ anzɛnyɩɩ ɩkɩnga kaalɩ hwilɛ nwʋ mmɛla kɛkɛ la a, ɩlɔnwʋ kaalɩ hwilɛ kyĩ. Mfomi ɩsɛ kɛɛ ɩta ɩsɔ stiya yi nu na yɩzayɩ itwu kaalɩ yɩ na ama ɩaya ɩayɛ dɩlayɩva mɔɔ yɩ nwʋ wʊkwa. Ɔzɔ la yɛ sɛ Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ be kyina mɔɔ ɩsumaa wɔ Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ nu nɩ zʋ isizi abɔkɔ wɔ yɩ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu na ɩnwʋ kezimɔ ɩbuwaa yɩ a, ɩma ɩba ɩnwʋ Gyehova buwɔ a. Sɛ Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ nɩ nyia Gyehova nwʋ nnimidi sõmaa na ɩnwʋ kezimɔ yɩ sʋlɔ Ezilɛ nɩ ɩbuwaa yɩ a, ɩkɛhã yɩ ma ikezizi abɔkɔ baaba wɔ buwabɩlɛ bela nu.
<<<P>>>
7 Nɩ Dwosefi zi Gyehova buwɔ, na eyi wʊwalɩ yɩ mmɩlɩ mɔ nɩ Pɔtifa yɩ yɩ pɛ kɛɛ ɩ nɩ yɩ da nɩ. Mmɩlɩ mɔ nɩ ɩdɩ kuakula benyia nɩ, ɩzumanlɩ kɛɛ mmɩlɩ mɔ Gyehova wɔlɩ Adamɩ nɩ, ɩmanlɩ baalɛ ɔkũ pɛ yɩ kɛɛ yɩ yɩ, mɛyɩ yɛ lɛ Yifi. Akũ sɔ̃, ɩdʋlɩ mɔɔ izili wɔ mmɩlɩ mɔ abiye valɩ Abilehamɩ yɩ yɩ Sɛla kɛɛ ɩrɛ kɛɛ ikyia yɩ nɩ nwʋ odwokɔ. (Gyn. 2:24; 20:3, 6) Ndwokɔ eyimmɔ wʊwalɩ Dwosefi ma ɩnwʊnlɩ Gyehova yɩ adwɩnɩ wɔ agyiahʋnlɩ nu nna nu nkitanwʋlilɛ nwʋ. Iluwakɛɛ nɩ Dwosefi zi Gyehova buwɔ nɩ ti, ɩáyɛ mɔɔ nɩ Pɔtifa yɩ yɩ pɛ kɛɛ ɩyɛ nɩ. Dwosefi hanlɩ hileli yi kɛɛ: “Kɛ ɩkɛyɛ na miali amumuyɛ odwokɔ mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ, na mɩayɛ tanɩ miadiyia Nyamɩnlɩ a?” (Gyn. 39:7-9) Nɩ Dwosefi zi mɔɔ ɩtɩnyɛ nɩɩ mɔɔ ɩádɩnyɛ wɔ Gyehova yɩ anyɩ zʋ. Nɩ izi mɔɔ ɩyɛ a, ɩkɛma Gyehova yɩ ɛyɛɛlɛ kɔwɔ. Eyi manlɩ Dwosefi zili pɔkɔ baaba. Meti sɛ yɛbʊwa yɛ Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛmaa ma bɔnwʊ Gyehova buwɔ, na bɔnwʊ kezimɔ Gyehova dɩ ankasa a, ɩkɛma bɛzabɛ sɔ̃ bekezizi abɔkɔba.

KILEHILE YƖ MA ƖNWÚ KEZIMƆ GYEHOVA DƖ ANKASA
<<<P>>>
8 Kɛ ɩkɛyɛ na wʊawʊwa wʋ Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ ma ɩanwʊ kezimɔ Gyehova dɩ ankasa a? Gyehova wʊwalɩ Dwoobu ma ɩnwʊnlɩ kezimɔ ɩdɩ ankasa, iluwa ndwokɔyizialɛ mɔɔ ɩbʋ 50 mɔɔ ɩva iyizialɩ yɩ nɩ zʋ. (Dwb. 38:1–41:34) Sɛ yɛzayɛ sɔ̃ yɛyɛɛ Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛ a, sa yɛkʋla yɛfa ndwokɔyizialɛ yedi egyima. Sɛ yekila ndwokɔyizialɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ Sa Ɔkʋla Enyia Anyɩnlɩlelɛ Daabaa! buuku yi nu a, bɛyɛ yɩ kɛɛ
