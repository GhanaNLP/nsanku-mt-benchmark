nwu buwɔ paa, iluwakɛɛ pɔkɔ bela mɔɔ ikezi nɩ, ɩkɔhʋla ɩkɛma Gyehova yɩ anyɩ kele anzɛnyɩɩ yɩ ɛyɛɛlɛ kɔwɔ? Akʋ̃, mɩhyelɩ yɩ ɛzɩzɩbɛ kɛɛ, ɩwɔ bʋwabɩlɛ yɩ nwʋ mpayɩ na ihilá kezimɔ Gyehova keli nwʋmɔ egyima. Izili pɔkɔ kɛɛ ɩlɛ́yɛ egyima mɔɔ yɩ mmusunwamaa bɛhyɛɛ yɩ kɛɛ ɩyɛ́ nɩ, na tákyɛ bela, inyianlɩ egyima mɔɔ ɩkɛma ɩkɔhʋla ɩkɔhɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ eyile bela.” Yɛ yɩ ndwĩ kɛɛ, Marianne íási pɔkɔ bela ɩma yɩ Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ yɩ nɩ, mfomi mɔɔ nɩ baalɛ yɩ izi wɔ Gyehova nwʋ daada nɩ, yɛ ɩwʋwalɩ yɩ ma ɩdwɩnɩndwɩnɩnlɩ nwʋmɔ a. Mɔɔ ɩzʋ̃ eyi nu ɩyalɩ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ, Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ yɩ nɩɩ Marianne bɔ ɔhɔnwʋvalɛ nu yelɩ sɩ. Akʋ̃ sʋ̃, ɩnɩɩ Gyehova avinli yɛlɩ sɩ. Marianne hanlɩ yɩ Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ yɩ nwʋ odwokɔ kɛɛ: “Ɩya ɩnwʋ kɛɛ, sɛ yɛfa Gyehova yedi bɔmʋnlɩ wɔ yɛ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu a, yɩzayɩ sʋ̃ ɩlɛ́yɩ yɩ anyɩ ɩkɛma yɛ kyĩ.”

NYILALƐ MƆƆ BƐZABƐ MƆƆ BƐBA BƆNWU GYEHOVA BUWƆ NƖ BENYIA
<<<P>>>
17 Sɛ yɛ nɩɩ mmenii sumaa Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ a, yɛbʋwa bɛ ma bɛba bɔnwu nninyeni sũmaa mɔɔ nwʋmɔ hyinyia. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, bɛba bɔnwu bʋwabɩlɛ mɔɔ awuvʋlɔ wɔ numɔ, elelɛdɩyɛ yɩ, nɩɩ Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ Bulombunlililɛ yɩ. Akʋ̃ sʋ̃, yɛbʋwa bɛ ma bɔnwu kezimɔ bɛhyɩhyɛ asafo yɩ. Dɩkɛɛ mɔɔ ɩkɔlɩ zʋlɔ wɔ yɛ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʋ, egyima mɔɔ asafo nu mbanimbani bɛyɛ, nɩɩ kezimɔ Ɔhʋanɩhilelɛ Okuwu yi di asafo yɩ anyunu nɩ. Naasʋ̃ kɛmɔ yɛya yezunzu nwʋmɔ nɩ, yɛ Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ kenyia ndwokɔ eyimmɔ nwʋ nnimidi dɩyɛ, naasʋ̃ dɩyɛ mɔɔ nwʋmɔ hyinyia titili yɛ lɛ kɛɛ ɩkɛya ɩkɔnwu Gyehova buwɔ.
<<<P>>>
18 Kila ɔnwʋmɔ ɔhʋanɩ kezile ɔkʋ̃ mɔɔ yenyia kɛɛ yɛkɔwʋwa mmenii ma bɛkɛya bɔkɔnwu Gyehova! Nnɛ, bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛya bɔnwu Gyehova na bɔsʋ̃mɩ yɩ nɩ wɔ anyɩnlɩlelɛ kezile paa wɔ bɛ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu. (Ndw. 25:12-15) Na wɔ eyile be sʋ̃ nɩ, bɛzabɛ mɔɔ benyia nɔhʋanlɛ Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ nwʋ nnimidi nɩ kenyia nnwʋanlɩ mɔɔ íni awielɩyɛ. (1 Dwɔ. 5:20) Naasʋ̃, kɛ mɛyɩ yɩzayɩ nkʋ̃ o, Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ kã kɛɛ sɛ abiye bɔ mmɔdɩnɩ ba nwu Gyehova buwɔ na ɩdɔ yɩ a, mɛyɩ “sʋ̃ izi yi.” (1 Kɔl. 8:3) Amba, Dɩyɛ Munwala Zʋ Tumi-vʋlɔ yɩ nɩ zi yɛ paa na ɩnwʋ yɛ kɛɛ yɛdɩ yɩ ɔhɔnwʋmmɔ. Kila kezimɔ ɩdɩ nyilalɛ kezile kɛɛ yɛkɛya yɛkɔnwu nɔhʋanlɛ Nyamɩnlɩ ɔkʋ̃ kyeinyii nɩ!
