11 Sɛ Bayɩbulu dɩyɛzumanlɛvɔlɔ be nwʋ kɛɛ inyiaa mɔɔ isumaa nɩ zʋ mfasʋ, na ɩkã nwʋmɔ odwokɔ ikile yɛ a, yɛ anyɩ de paa. Naasɔ̃, ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛbʋwa yɩ ma ɩnwʋ kɛɛ, mɔɔ isumaa nɩ munwala zũ Gyehova, mɔɔ ɩdɩ ye Kilehilevɔlɔ Kezile Baaba nɩ yɩ kɛnɩ. (Ayɩ. 30:20, 21) Ebiye a, yɛ Bayɩbulu dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ be kɛla yɛ asɩ wɔ mɔɔ isumaa nɩ nwʋ anzɛnyɩɩ ebiye a, ɩkɛhã ikehile yɛ kɛɛ yɩ anyɩ deli kezimɔ yɛfa yekilehile nɩ nwʋ. Iwɔ numɔ kɛɛ yɛyɛɛ yɛ ɔfʋã nu dɩyɛ yɛkɔwʋwa yɩ dɩyɛ, naasɔ̃ ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛhyɛ yɩ ɛzɩzɩbɛ ma ɩfa nnasɩ nɩɩ ayɛlɛyɩlɛ yɩ ɩma Gyehova mfomi.

11. Kɛ yɛkɛyɛ na yɛawʋwa yɛ Bayɩbulu dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ ma ɩanwʋ kɛɛ mɔɔ isumaa nɩ dɩ akyɛdɩ mɔɔ ɩzɔ̃ Gyehova kɛnɩ a?

12 Sɛ Bayɩbulu dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ be kɔhʋla kenyia mɔɔ isumaa nɩ zʋ mfasʋ a, gyɩsɛ ɩfa mɔɔ isumaa nɩ ɩyɛ egyima wɔ yɩ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu. Meti kɛ ɛkɛyɛ na wʋawʋwa wʋ Bayɩbulu dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ ma ɩava mɔɔ isumaa nɩ ɩayɛ egyima a? Mɔɔ ili bɔmʋnlɩ, sɛ ɩkɩnga Bayɩbulu nu odwokɔ be na ɩádɩ yɩ tʋ a, bʋwa yɩ ma ɩdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ mɔɔ izi ɩfalɩ Gyehova nwʋ daada nɩ nwʋ. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, ebiye a, nɩ ɩyɩnga Gyehova yɩ ndɛntwuwale nwʋ odwokɔ be, na ɩánwʋ sianti mɔɔ Gyehova hunhunli mbiyemmɔ na iyiakili mbiyemmɔ nɩ. Sɛ Bayɩbulu dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ be fa odwokɔ mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ tiã wʋ anyunu a, kɛ ɛkɛyɛ na wʋawʋwa yɩ ma ɩadɩ yɩ tʋ a?
