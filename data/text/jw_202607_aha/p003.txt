Gibiyɔnʊmaa lahɩlɩlɩ Dwohyuwa ma ɩ nɩɩ bɛ yelɩ azɔ̃nudwolɩyɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ
(Kila nkyɩkyɛmi 4)
<<<P>>>
Gyɛlɩko nɩɩ Ayɩ bɔ zʋ nkunimi nɩ, Gibiyɔnʊmaa zʋ̃manlɩ mmenii be* ma bɔhɔlɩ Dwohyuwa yɩ ɛkɛnɩ kɛɛ ɩ nɩɩ bɛ yé azɔ̃nudwolɩyɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ.
<<<P>>>
4 Kɩnga Dwohyuwa 9:8-13. Gibiyɔnʊmaa yɩ lahɩlɩlɩ Dwohyuwa, na bɛhã behileli yi kɛɛ bɔzʋ̃ ayinzi ayinzi paa. Bɛhã behileli bɛ kɛɛ bezi kɛɛ Gyehova yɛ ɩwʋwalɩ yi mmenii Yizilayamaa ma belili Igyibitimaa nɩɩ Amɔlɩmaa bɛ Awulombunli Sayɩhɔnʊ nɩɩ Ɔgɩ bɔ zʋ nkunimi a. Gibiyɔnʊmaa yɩ hileli kɛɛ bezi nyɛlɛbɛ paa, iluwakɛɛ bɛákã mɔɔ nɩ bezi kɛɛ ɩdʋ Gyɛlɩko nɩɩ Ayɩ nɩ nwʋ odwokɔ. Sianti yɛ lɛ kɛɛ nɩ ɔzɔ odwokɔ yɩ zili a ɩlɛ́kyɛlɩ, na nɩ ɩkɛyɛ sɩ kɛɛ ikodwu ayinzi ayinzi zɔ. Meti sɛ bɛhanlɩ a, ankɩ Yizilayamaa yɩ kɔnwʋ yɩ kɛɛ bɔ́zʋ̃ ayinzi ayinzi kɛmɔ bekilee nɩ. Kɛ ankɩ Dwohyuwa nɩɩ Yizilayamaa yɩ keyizia Gyehova yɩ adwɩnɩ wɔ nwʋ nɩ, bɛáyɛ yɩ zɔ na mfomi beleli ndwokɔ mɔɔ Gibiyɔnʊmaa bɛhanlɩ nɩ belili na bɔwɔlɩ bɛ anvhʋba kɛɛ bɛ nɩɩ bɛ lɔ́kʋ̃. (Dwh. 9:14, 15) Naasʋ̃ ɩákyɛ kʋlaa yɛ Yizilayamaa ya nwʋnlɩ yɩ kɛɛ Gibiyɔnʊmaa yɩ lahɩlɩ bɛ a, na iluwakɛɛ nɩ ‘bɛva Gyehova bɛhã ndamɩ bɛ’ nɩ ti, belili bɛ anvhuba nɩ zʋ. (Dwh. 9:16-19) Yizilayamaa manlɩ ɔhʋanɩ ma Gibiyɔnʊmaa wʋwalɩ bo nu na bɛvalɩ egyima be mɔɔ ɩba azɩ bowulalɩ bɛ sa. Nɩ be egyima yɛ lɛ kɛɛ bɔkɔhɔ nzulo nɩɩ ɛyɛnɩ bɛkɛma Yizilayamaa na bɛava biye sʋ̃ bɛahɔ Gyehova yɩ asɔlɩ pika yɩ nu bɔ nnwʋanlɩ eyile yi munwala.—Dwh. 9:27.

5 Mmɩlɩ mɔ Amɔlɩmaa dɩlɩ kɛɛ Gibiyɔnʊmaa nɩɩ Yizilayamaa bɛyɛ azɔ̃nudwolɩyɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ nɩ, bɛ awulombunli anlu hanlɩ bɔ nwʋ wɔlɩ numɔ kɛɛ bɛ nɩɩ Gibiyɔnʊmaa kɔhʋ̃. Gibiyɔnʊmaa yɩ bo kunlu duli paa. Meti, bezianlɩ Dwohyuwa kɛɛ bɛyɩlá bɛawʋwa bɛ ma bɛ nɩɩ bɛ
<<<P>>>
* Mmenyia mɔɔ bɛ nɩɩ Yizilayamaa hɔ dɩndɛlɩ ma bɛyelɩ azɔ̃nudwolɩyɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ nɩ zʋ̃ Gibiyɔnʊ, Kefayɩla, Biyalɔtʋ, nɩɩ Kiliyatɩgyilamɩ.—Dwh. 9:17.
