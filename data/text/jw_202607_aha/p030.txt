Mɩ nɩɩ Miwako yɛvalɩ alʊbɔ kɛyɛ 24 yehilalɩ yɩ awʊvʊlɔ kɔbʊmɩ kɛɛ bowuli. Nɩ yɛta yɛyɛ kɔɔlʊ paa, na nɩ kɔɔlʊ bela mɔɔ yekenyia nɩ kʋla ma ye kunlu yɩba yɩba yɛ paa. Nɩ yɛta yɛkɔ hospital sʊ̃. Miwako yɩ maamɩ mɔɔ bɔfʋlɔ yɩ Masako nɩ yɩ adimakɛlɛ yɩ nu yɛlɩ sɩ paa, na nɩ ɩáhʊla itiyia po. Nɩ ɩáhʊla ɩkɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ nɩɩ dasɩlɛlilɛ. Lha alapɛnɩ ɛgyikĩĩ be ma iwu yi po mɛyɩ, nɩ ɩbʊwa hwiili nu. Naasʊ̃, lávalɩ nwʊmɔ kɛɛ nɩ ɩwɔ ɔzɔ bʊwabɩlɛ yɩ nu nɩ, nɩ ɩdɔ Gyehova paa. Nɩ sɛ ɩkãã nɔhʊanlɛ yɩ nwʊ odwokɔ a, nɩ yɩ anyunu ɩyɛ ɛzɩlɩkɛ ɛzɩlɩkɛ. Nɩ Masako yɩ anyɩ sɔ mɔɔ ɩwɔ bela, na lávalɩ nwʊmɔ ɔhawʊ azʊlɔtʊ mɔɔ iyiyialɩ nɩ, ɩhɔlɩ zʋlɔ ɩvalɩ anyɩnlɩlelɛ ɩzʊ̃mɩnlɩ Gyehova. Na mɩpɛ kɛɛ musumazuma yi ndianwʊba nɩ.
<<<P>>>
Wɔ 2024 nɩ, nzɩnzanlɩyɛ kezile be zili wɔ yɛ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu. Behã behileli yɛ kɛɛ, yɛhɔ yɛyɛ ɔhʋanɩbukoelɛmaa titili. Ɔzɔ mmɩlɩ yɩ, nɩ yɛva alʊbɔ 30 nɩɩ yɩ mɛda yɛzʊ̃mɩ wɔ Betɩlɩ. Meti sikalɛyɩ, yɛsʊ̃mɩɩ wɔ pamɩ be zʋ wɔ Okinawa. Na yenyia ɔnwʊmɔ ɔhʋanɩ yedii mmusunwa mɔɔ bɛdɩda azogyiamaa bɛ azʊkɔlɩyɛ mɔɔ ibike ɛkɩhyl nɩ dasɩlɛ. Ɛdɩdɩlɛ mɔɔ yenyianlɩ wɔ Betɩlɩ nɩ wʊwalɩ yɛ paa, meti mmɩlɩ mɔ ye egyimalilɛ zɩnzanlɩ nɩ, yɛáfɛ kʋlaa. Yɛbɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ yɛkɛyɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛba, na yɛfa yɛ mmɩlɩ sʊ̃ yedi egyima bʊwɔ na ama yɛahʊla yɛava ehyimɛ yɛayɛ dasɩlɛlilɛ egyima yɩ. Gyehova ɩwʊwa yɛ ma yenyia Bayɩbʊlʊ dɩyɛzumanlɛmaa mɔɔ yɛva bɛ yɛyɛ kɛɛ ye mmusunwamaa. Sikalɛyɩ, yɛya yɛnwʊ yɩ kɛɛ, dɩyɛ mɔɔ nwʊmɔ hyinyia paa yɛ lɛ kɛɛ yɛkɔlɔ mmenii. Mɩ nɩɩ Miwako yɛda Gyehova yɩ asɩ eyile bela kɛɛ ɩva egyimalilɛ mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ iwula yɛ sa.

MIZI MƖ KƐNYA KƐƐ MƲKƆHƆ ZƲLƆ MƖKƐMA GYEHOVA KƐDƖDƖ MƖ
<<<P>>>
Ɩwɔ numɔ kɛɛ sikalɛyɩ, mu mmusunwamaa bɛádwodwu betiyia mɩ dɩyɛ, naasʊ̃ bo nu bela lébalɩ lɔ́sʊ̃mɩnlɩ Gyehova. Naasʊ̃, kɛmɔ Gyisɛsɩ hanlɩ nɩ, abiyela mɔɔ ikediã yi mmusunwa kɛnɩ ɩkɛya ikeli yɩ mɛda nɩ, bɔkɔwɔ nwʊmɔ 100 bɛkɛma yɩ. (Mk. 10:29, 30) Mɔɔ ɩdʋlɩ mɩ nɩ ma mʊnwʊ kɛɛ, odwokɔ eyi dɩ nɔhʊanlɛ paa. Mmeliyema mɔɔ minyia bɛ wɔ oyuwadɩ yɩ ɔfvã dɩka bela mɔɔ bɔdɔ mɩ na bɔdwɩnɩ mʊ nwʊ nɩ, sɛ mɩkã kɛɛ mitinyiaa bɛ a, mulówie kyĩ.
<<<P>>>
Truth buuku yi mɔɔ Wilson va manlɩ mɩ nɩ, ɩlanwʊ la ɩwɔ mi nninyeni nu. Eyile bela mɔɔ mikehila ɔzɔ buuku yi nɩ, ɩkakɛ mɩ ma mʊnwʊ kezimɔ Gyehova manlɩ mʊnwonlɩ nɔhʊanlɛ yɩ nɩ. (1 Mm. 28:9) Mizi mɩ kɛnya kɛɛ, mɩkɛma Gyehova kɔhɔ zʋlɔ kɛdɩdɩ mɩ. Na mɩzamɩ sʊ̃ mʊkɔwʊwa mmenii mvʋlɔ ma bɛkɛya bɔkɔnwʊ yɩ na ama bɛanyia nnwʊanlɩ mɔɔ íni awielɩyɛ.
