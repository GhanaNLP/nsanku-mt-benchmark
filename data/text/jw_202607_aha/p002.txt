Suma Debe Wɔ Gibiyɔnʊmaa Nɩ Bɛ Ɛkɛnɩ
<<<P>>>
“Gibiyɔnʊmaa nɩ Yizilayamaa hɔ yelɩ azɔ̃nudwolɩyɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ, na bɛ nɩɩ bɛ wʊwalɩ.”—DWH. 10:1.
<<<P>>>
WƆ ƆLUBƆ 1473 A.Y.M. nu nɩ, Yizilayamaa wɔlɩ yɩ tʊ kɛɛ bɔkɔ̃ʊ̃ bekele Anvhonba Azɩlɛ yɩ. Nɩ Gyehova wʊwa Yizilayamaa yɩ ma bɔhʊ̃ beli Gyɛlɩko nɩɩ Ayɩ ahwonwa yɩ zʊ nkunimi. Naasʊ̃ ayinzi yi, debe mɔɔ ɩyɛ nnwʊánlɩ paa zili mɔɔ nɩ bɛáhila yɩ ɔhʊanl. Mmenii be yalɩ Yizilayamaa bɛ ɛkɛnɩ ya hanlɩ kɛɛ bɔzʊ̃ ayinzi ayi-nzi paa na bɛpɛ kɛɛ bɛ nɩɩ bɛ yɛ azɔ̃nudwolɩyɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ.
<<<P>>>
2 Mmenii mɔɔ yɛkãã bɔ nwʊ odwokɔ yɩ yɛ lɛ Gibiyɔnʊmaa yɩ. Eyi yɛ ɩdɩ mmɩlɩ mɔ ili bɔmʊnlɩ mɔɔ Bayɩbʊlʊ yɩ hanlɩ bɔ nwʊ odwokɔ a, naasʊ̃ ayinzi yi Bayɩbʊlʊ yɩ zia hanlɩ bɔ nwʊ odwokɔ. Bayɩbʊlʊ yɩ da yɩ ali kɛɛ Gibiyɔnʊmaa nɩɩ Yizilayamaa hɔlɩ zʊlɔ wʊwalɩ alʊbɔ sɔ̃maa. Gibiyɔnʊmaa lalɩ subanɩba sɔ̃maa ali mɔɔ yɛkɔhʊla yekozuma. Na mɔɔ ɩdʊdʊlɩ bɛ nɩ sʊ̃ ma yɛnwʊ nninye-ni sɔ̃maa falɩ Gyehova Nyamɩnlɩ nwʊ.
<<<P>>>
ƖSƐ KƐƐ YENYIA GYEHOVA NU DEDI NA YEKÃ YƐ NWƲ AZƖ
<<<P>>>
3 Mmɩlɩ mɔ Yizilayamaa yɩ wɔlɩ yɩ tʊ kɛɛ bɔkʊ̃ʊ̃ bede Keenanɩ azɩlɛ yɩ nɩ, nɩ Gibiyɔnʊmaa yɩ sʊ̃ da bo twonwa Gibiyɔnʊ nu. Nɩ twonwa eyi yɛ pɩkɛ paa, na nɩ bɔwɔ nwʊmɔ banɩ sʊ̃. Nɩ Gibiyɔnʊ kɩnla Havayɩ manlɩ yɩ nwʊ. Nɩ Havayɩ kɩnla amanlɩ asunwa mɔɔ bɔwɔ Keenanɩ mɔɔ “bɔ nwʊ zʊnlɩ na bɔ nwʊ yɛ sɩ” tɛla Yizilaya-maa nɩ bɔ nwʊ. (Dit. 7:1) Meti, nɩ Gibiyɔnʊmaa lɛ azogyiamaa mɔɔ bɔ nwʊ yɛ sɩ paa. (Dwh. 10:2) Nɩ Keenanɩ ahwonwa nyalɩyɛ yɩ pɛ kɛɛ bɛ nɩɩ Yizilayamaa kʊ̃, naasʊ̃ Gibiyɔnʊmaa mɛyɩ, bɛáyɛ zɔ. Mɩnla ti a? Iluwakɛɛ bɔnwʊnlɩ kɛɛ bɔlɔ́kʊla bɛ nɩɩ Yizilayamaa kɔhʊ̃. Nɩ bezi paa kɛɛ Gyehova yɛ ɩkɔ̃ʊ̃ ɩma yi mmenii a. Na nɩ bezi sʊ̃ kɛɛ Gyehova ɩwɔ Yizilayamaa anvhonba kɛɛ ikodu Keena-nɩmaa wɔ bɛ azɩlɛ yɩ zʊ na ɩava bɛ azɩlɛ yɩ ɩama yi mmenii Yizi-layamaa. (Ɛkɩ. 34:11; Dwh. 9:24) Meti, mmɩlɩ mɔ Yizilayamaa lili
