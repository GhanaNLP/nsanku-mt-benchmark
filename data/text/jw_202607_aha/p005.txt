9 Gyehova ɩátwĩ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛ nɩ Gibiyɔnumaa iádi yi buwɔ nɩ azɔ̃ wɔ ɛkɛnɩ la. Mfomi Bulombunli Devidi yɩ mmʋlɩ zʋ ansaana ibali nwʋmɔ egyima. Gyehova ɩáma nzulo twu nkũɔ̃ alʋbɔ asã, na eyi manlɩ kɔmɩ yalɩ Yizilaya. Meti mmʋlɩ mɔ Devidi yizialɩ Gyehova sianti mɔɔ kɔmɩ ya Yizilaya nɩ, Gyehova hã hileli yi kɛɛ inyia ɛya iluwakɛɛ Sɔɔlʋ nɩ mmenyia be mɔɔ bɔwɔ yɩ abusunwa nu nɩ bohũhũ Gibiyɔnumaa yɩ.—2 Sa. 21:1.
<<<P>>>
10 Kɩnga 2 Samʋwɛlɩ 21:3-6. Devidi nɩ Gibiyɔnumaa yɩ dɩndɛlɩ, na iyizialɩ bɛ mɔɔ ɩkɔhʋla ɩkɛyɛ ɩkɛva ɩkɛbɩda bɛ. Asʋ̃ nɩ Gibiyɔnumaa yɩ kɛhã kehile Devidi kɛɛ iluwakɛɛ iádi odwokɔ yɩ nwʋ egyima ndɛ nɩ ti, iduwá ozukua kezile ɩma bɛ a? Anhã. Gibiyɔnumaa hã hileli yi kɛɛ odwokɔ eyi mɛyɩ, “ídi dwɛtɛ nɩ goodu odwokɔ” iluwakɛɛ kɛmɔ Mmɛla yɩ kile nɩ, bɔlɔ́kʋla bokoduwa dɩyɛ be bɛkɛva bekele kodiyiawu be yɩ nnwʋanlɩ. (Nɔ. 35:30, 31) Akɔ̃ sʋ̃, nɩ bez̃i kɛɛ bɔlɔ́kʋla bokohũ enii mmʋlɩ mɔ mmɛla ɩlémanlɩ ɔhʋanɩ bɛ. Meti awielɩyɛ kʋlaa nɩ, Devidi hanlɩ kɛɛ bɛyí Sɔɔlʋ yi nnwuakula mmenyia asunwa bɛma bɛ ma bohũ bɛ. Mɩnla ti a? Lkɛyɛ kɛɛ awulilɛ mɔɔ ɩhɔlɩ zʋlɔ nɩ, Sɔɔlʋ yɩ nnwuakula mmenyia yɩ valɩ bɔ nwʋ wulalɩ numɔ wɔ ɔhʋanɩ be zʋ. Eyi mɛda nɩ, nzulo wɔlɩ yɩ tʋ kɛɛ itwuu wɔ Yizilaya, na tákyɛ yɛ alɩyɛ wululi zʋlɔ a. Na eyi lalɩ yɩ ali kɛɛ kezimɔ Yizilayamaa nɩ Gibiyɔnumaa lili nɩ, Gyehova ɩyɩ yɩ ɛya wɔ bɔ zʋ.—2 Sa. 21:9, 10, 14.

11 Mɩnla yɛ odwokɔ eyi ma yɛnwʋ falɩ Gyehova nwʋ a? Eyi da yɩ ali kɛɛ Gyehova dɩ pɛlɩpɛlɩlɩlɛ Nyamɩnlɩ. (Ndw. 37:28) Mmenii sɔ̃maa wɔ kɛnɩ a, bɛ nɩ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɔzʋ̃ manlɩ fʋlɔ zʋ anzɛnyɩɩ ɩsʋnɩ kezimɔ bɛyɛ bɛ dɩyɛ nɩ iáli yi buwɔ kʋlaa. Naasʋ̃ Gyehova pɛ kɛɛ yɛ nɩ abiyela di yi buwɔ. Wɔ Gyehova yɩzayɩ yɩ mmʋlɩba nu nɩ, ikeziyeziye nsisi bela mɔɔ yɩ azɔ̃mɩvʋlɔ luwa numɔ kyĩ nɩ. Akɔ̃ sʋ̃, Gyehova ikilaa ɔhʋanɩ kɛɛ yekelili yɛ anvhʋba zʋ kɛmɔ nɩ ikilaa ɔhʋanɩ kɛɛ Yizilayamaa kɔhɔlɩ zʋlɔ kelili azɔ̃nudwolɩyɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ mɔɔ bɛ nɩ Gibiyɔnumaa yɛlɩ nɩ zʋ nɩ.—Fa tindiã Emɔsʋ 1:9 nwʋ.
