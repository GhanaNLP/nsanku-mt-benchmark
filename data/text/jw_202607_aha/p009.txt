MMƖLƖ MƆ YƐ BƲWABƖLƐ ZƖNZA AƔILEMVILE NU
<<<P>>>
4 Yɛ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ kɔhʋla kɛzɩnza mmɩlɩ mɔ yɛá-hila yɩ ɔhʋanɩ. (Odw. 9:11; 1 Kɔl. 7:31) Sa yɛ nwʋ kʋla pa yɛ aɣilemvile nu, yeyiyia ozukua-sɛmɩ nu anwʋkyɩlɩ, kʋnɛ kɔhʋla kezi wɔ yɛ manlɩ nu anzɛnyu ye egyimalilɛ kɔhʋla kɛ-zɩnza. Nzɩnzanlɩyɛ mɔɔ ɩdɩdɩ ɔzɔ kɔhʋla kɛma adwɩnɩndwɩnɩ kɛhyɛ yɛ zʋ anzɛnyu yɛ adwɩnɩ kodu kɔvʋla. Eyi kɔhʋla kɛma yekeyizia yɛ nwʋ kɛɛ: ‘Mɩnla yɛ mɩyɛ a?’ Eliyema be mɔɔ bɔfʋlɔ yɩ Joseph mɔɔ ɩhɔ ɩzʋ̃mɩnlɩ wɔ Bɛtɩlɩ alʋbɔ kɛyɛ 17 mɔɔ aɣinzi yi, bɛhã behileli kɛɛ ɩhɔ ɩyɛ ɔhʋanɩbukoelɛvʋlɔ nɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ: “Nɩ mɩ anyɩ zʋ dianlɩ mɩ. Iluwakɛɛ nɩ mɩva alʋbɔ sɔ̃maa muz̃ũmɩ wɔ Bɛtɩlɩ nɩ ti, nɩ mɩánwʋ ke-zimɔ mʋkɔwɔ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ fʋlɔ eyi tʋ.” Mɩnla anwʋlazʋ yɛ Gyehova duwa Ayɩzaya buuku yi zʋ fa ma yɛ mmɩlɩ mɔ yɛ bʋwabɩlɛ zɩnza aɣile-mvile nu nɩ a?
<<<P>>>
5 Kɩnga Ayɩzaya 42:16. Gyehova bɔ anvʋhʋba kɛɛ ɩkɛma bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛ anyɩ izũ wɔ sɛnkyulɛdɩ ɔhʋanɩ zʋ nɩ kɔnwʋ ɔhʋanɩ, na ɩama bɛ anwunzumi ɩalanɩ kɩnlanɩyɛ. Odwo-kɔpɔkɔ ‘anyɩnlɩsunliya,’ “ɔhʋanɩ mɔɔ bolódu-walɩ zʋlɔ kyĩ,” “anwunzumi,” nɩɩ “ahʋanɩ mɔɔ ɩhyɩyahyɩya” mɔɔ bɛva beli egyima wɔ ɛkɩ nɩ, ɩma yɛnwʋ kezimɔ yɛkɔhʋla yɛkɔdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ ye eyile be nwʋ mmɩlɩ mɔ yɛ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ zɩnza aɣilemvile nu nɩ. Yɛkɔhʋla yɛkɛdɩ nyanlɩ dɩkɛɛ mɔɔ yeluwa ɔhʋayɩlɛ be nu mɔɔ yézi dɩka mɔɔ yɛkɔɔ nɩ. Naasʋ̃ Gyehova ma yenyia anwʋlazʋ kɛɛ, ɩávalɩ nwʋmɔ bʋwabɩlɛ bela mɔɔ yeyiyiaa nɩ, ɩkɔwɔ yɛ nwʋ banɩ, na ɩléyiaki yɛ kyĩ. Gye-hova kɔzɔ yɛ sa na ɩava yɛ ialuwa dɩka mɔɔ
<<<P>>>
4. Sɛ yɛ bʋwabɩlɛ sɩnza aɣilemvile nu a, mɩnla ɔhʋanɩ zʋ yɛ ɩkɔhʋla ɩkɛhã yɛ a?
<<<P>>>
5. Mɩnla anwʋlazʋ yɛ Gyehova fa ma yɛ wɔ Ayɩzaya 42:16 a? (Kila mfonyini yi sõ.)
<<<P>>>
Sɛ wʋ bʋwabɩlɛ sɩnza aɣilemvile nu po a, Gyehova iléyiaki wʋ kyĩ
(Kila nkyɩkyɛmi 5)
<<<P>>>
‘Sɛ oluwa ɔhʋanɩ mɔɔ olóduwalɩ zʋlɔ kyĩ zʋ a, miléyiaki wʋ.’
