“Wʋ awuvɔlɔ kenyia nnwʋanlɩ”
<<<P>>>
Dɩyɛ bela lɔ́kʋla kezi awudunwolɩyɛ nwʋ anyɩnlɩlazʋ mɔɔ yɛwɔ nɩ yɩ ɔhʋanɩ kyĩ (Kila nkyɩkyɛmi 14)
<<<P>>>
anyɩnlɩlelɛ anzɛnyɩ ɩma eyiaki Gyehova yɩ ɔzɔ̃mɩnlɛ ma nyilalɛ mɔɔ ekenyia wɔ eyile be nɩ bɔ wʋ.

MMƖLƖ MƆ YƐ DƆVƆLƆ BE IWU
<<<P>>>
12 Sɛ yɛ dɔvɔlɔ be wu a, nwʋmɔ ɛyɛkɛ nɩ kɔhʋla kɛyɛ kezi paa na ɔzɔ ɛyɛkɛ nɩ kɔhʋla kɔwʋwa kɛnɩ kɛhyɛ paa. Itwu mmɩlɩ be a, ebiye a, yɛkɛhã kɛɛ mɩnla ti mɔɔ Gyehova manlɩ ɔhʋanɩ ma yɛ dɔvɔlɔ yɩ iwuli a. Sɩnɩ yɩbalɩ eliyema benyia be mɔɔ bɔfʋlɔ yɩ Michael yɩ awʋvɔlɔ ma bowuli. Michael hanlɩ kɛɛ: “Mɩ ahʋnlɩnɩ wuwuli paa, na mɩvalɩ Gyehova nwʋ ɛya kɛɛ ɩmanlɩ nwʋmɔ ɔhʋanɩ ma bowuli. Mizi kɛɛ, kɛ Gyehova yɛ ɩmanlɩ bowuli a, naasʋ̃ mɩádɩ yɩ tʋ kɛɛ ɩáyɛ nwʋmɔ debe.” Ebiye a, ɔzɔ la yɛ wʋzawʋ sʋ̃ etɩ nyanlɩ a. Mmɩlɩ mɔ yedii ɛyɛkɛ mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ nɩ, mɩnla ɛyɛɛlɛyɩkyulɛ nɩɩ anyɩnlɩlazʋ yɛ Gyehova fa ma yɛ a?
<<<P>>>
12. Sɛ yɛ dɔvɔlɔ be wu a, mɩnla ɔhʋanɩ zʋ yɛ yɛkɔhʋla yɛkɛdɩ nyanlɩ a?
<<<P>>>
13 Kɩnga Ayɩzaya 25:8. Gyehova bɔ anʋhʋba kɛɛ “ɩkɛmɩ owulo daabaa.” Mɔwʋ dɩyɛ fa yɩ kɛɛ ɛda oyuwadɩ be nu mɔɔ owulo íni kɛnɩ dɔkɔ̃! Pɩlazunɛ bela íni nwʋmɔ kɛɛ wʋ anyɩ kele paa, iluwakɛɛ wʋ dɔvɔlɔ bela ilówu ma ekeli nwʋmɔ ɛyɛkɛ. (Ayɩ. 35:10) Na ɔlɔ́nwʋ wʋ dɔvɔlɔ be yi siye na wʋali nwʋmɔ ɛyɛkɛ sʋ̃. Mfomi ekenyia anyɩnlɩlazʋ kɛɛ, wʋ alɔvɔlɔ kɔwʋwa nnwʋanlɩ nu daabaa na bekenyia pɔkɔdɩlɛ sʋ̃. Ɔzɔ anʋhʋba eyi ídɩ awohwi ndwokɔ kʋlaa iluwakɛɛ Gyehova yɩzayɩ yɛ ɩwɔlɩ ɔzɔ anʋhʋba yɩ a. Na Gyehova sʋ̃ mɛyɩ, yɩ anʋhʋba munwatĩĩ ba numɔ.—Yik. 21:4, 5.
