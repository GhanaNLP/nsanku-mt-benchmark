kɛɛ ιyɛ debe mɔɔ nwvmɔ ihyinyia na ιyɛ sɩ, mɔɔ mɛyɩ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ ɩkɛyɛ Nyamınlɩ yalɛ mɔɔ bɛbawu yɩ nɩ yɩ maamɩ. Gyehova luwalɩ bɔ-vvlɔ Gebila nɩɩ Mɛɛlɩ yɩzayɩ yi busunwanii Elizabɛtɩ zʋ manlɩ Mɛɛlɩ nwʋnlɩ kɛɛ ɩwɔ yi nu anwʋlazʋ na ɩkɔwʋwa yɩ sʋ̃. Ayinzi yi, Mɛɛlɩ dwɩnɩndwɩnɩnlɩ ndwɔkɔ mɔɔ bɛhanlɩ bɛhileli yi nɩ nwʋ. Na ɩya ɩnwʋnlɩ yɩ petee kɛɛ Gyehova wɔ tumi, ɩ nɩɩ mmenii munwatĩĩ di yi buwɔ, na ɩdwɩnɩ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ mmenii bɛáwu bɛ nɩ nwʋ baaba. (Lk. 1:46-55) Kezimɔ Mɛɛlɩ hileli anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ wɔ mɔɔ Gyehova yɛlɩ manlɩ yɩ nwʋ nɩ, ɔzɔ la yɛ amɩyɛ sʋ̃ sɛ Gyehova bʋwa yɛ ‘wɔ mmʋlɩ mɔ yehyinyia’ mmʋwalɩyɛ nɩ a, yekile nwʋmɔ anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ a. (Hib. 4:16) Mmʋlɩ bela mɔɔ ekenyia mmʋwalɩyɛ mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ nɩ, ɩyɛ a bizia wʋ nwʋ kɛɛ: ‘Mɩnla yɛ eyi kilehile mɩ falɩ Gyehova nwʋ a? Gyehova yi subanɩ ɛhɩnla dɩyɛ yɛ eyi ma mʋnwʋ yɩ a?’

YELƆKƲLA YEKOZUMA GYEHOVA NWƲ DƖYƐ YEKOWIE KYĨ
<<<P>>>
17 Wɔ paladayɩsɩ nɩ, Gyehova kɛma yɛ ɔnwʋma mɔɔ bɛyɩnyɩa fʋlɔ mɔɔ ɩkɛma yɛkɔnwʋ yɩ apɛdɩ. (Yik. 20:12) Na wɔ ɛkɛnɩ sʋ̃ nɩ, yɛkɔnwʋ Gyehova yɩ abɔdɩ sũmaa, na eyi kɛma yekozuma yɩ nwʋ nninyeni sũmaa zɛ. (Ayɩ. 11:6-9) Akũ sʋ̃, yɛ alɔvʋlɔ mɔɔ Gyehova kodunwo bɛ, nɩɩ sinidɔlɛ mɔɔ ɩzʋ̃ yɛ nwʋ ɩkɛyɩ ngyɩkĩĩ ngyɩkĩĩ nɩ kɛma yekozuma nninyeni mvʋlɔ yɛkɛvalɩ Gyehova nwʋ.
<<<P>>>
18 Gyehova mɛyɩ, yelɔkʋla yekozuma yɩ nwʋ dɩyɛ yekowie kyĩ. Izi nninyeni sũmaa ɩtɛla yɛ, meti “yelɔkʋla yɛkɛbɩbɛ yɩ ntɩyasɩ nu yekowie yɩ kyĩ.” (Ndw. 90:2; 147:5) Na “egyima mɔɔ nɔhʋanlɛ Nyamınlɩ yɩ ɩyɛ ɔzʋ̃mɩ ahyɛasɩ kɔbʋmɩ awielɩyɛ nɩ, yelɔnwʋ numɔ kyĩ.” (Odw. 3:11) Meti kɛ yɛyalɩ asafo yɩ nu ahyɛ anzɛnyɩɩ ɩlékyɛlɩ nɩ, bɛmá yɛva nwʋmɔ ɔhʋanɩ bela mɔɔ yekenyia nɩ yezuma yɛ Zɩ mɔɔ ɩwɔ ɔlɔlɛ nɩ yɩ nwʋ dɩyɛ sũmaa.
