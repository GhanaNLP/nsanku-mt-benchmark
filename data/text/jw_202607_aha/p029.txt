Aziyianɩ egyɩkĩĩ be mɛda nɩ, mʋhɔlɩ asafo be mɔɔ bɛkã Japan ndɛnɩ nu wɔ New York. Japan ndɛnɩ mɔɔ muzumanlɩ nɩ wukoli ɔnwʋmɔ ɔhʋanɩ sũmaa manlɩ mɩ wɔ Gyehova yɩ ɔzɔ̃mɩnlɛ nu. Ɔlʋbɔ 1989 nɩ, mʋhɔlɩ ayiyialɩyɛ be wɔ Los Angeles, mɔɔ ɩwɔ California nɩ. Mɛyɩ yɛ ɩdɩ ayiyialɩyɛ mɔɔ ili bɔmʋnlɩ mɔɔ mʋhɔlɩ wɔ Japan ndɛnɩ nu a. Dɩlama mɔɔ bɛyɛlɩ wɔ ayiyialɩyɛ yɩ tʋ nɩ, nɩ eliyema baalɛ be mɔɔ ɩdɩ ɔhʋanɩbukoelɛvolɔ nɩ kɩnla nwʋmɔ. Nɩ bɔfʋlɔ yɩ kɛɛ Miwako Onami. Mɩ anyɩ ɣa lelili yɩ nwʋ, meti mɩdʋlɩ mʋdɔlɩ yɩ ɔlɔlɛ nu.

Mɩ nɩɩ Miwako yegyialɩ wɔ 1992. Na yenyianlɩ ɔnwʋmɔ ɔhʋanɩ yɛhɔ yɛzɔ̃mɩnlɩ wɔ Brooklyn Bɛtɩlɩ. Miwako dwɩnɩ mmenii nwʋ paa, na eɣile bela ɩpɛ kɛɛ ɩyɛ debe ɩbʊwa mmenii mvʋlɔ. Mɩ yɩ Miwako mɛyɩ, mukɔhʋla mɩkɛhã paa kɛɛ ɩdɩ akyɛdɩ mɔɔ ɩzʋ̃ Gyehova kɛ, iluwakɛɛ eɣile bela ɩma mɩ anyɩ de, na ɩkyɩkyɩ mɩ ɛɣɛɛlɛ sʋ̃.

Nɩ Miwako yɩ awuvulɔ da Japan, na idwuli dɩka be nɩ, bɔ nwu balɩ be ɛzɩzɩbɛ ɛzɩzɩbɛ. Meti mmeliyema mɔɔ bedi nninyeni anyunu wɔ Brooklyn Bɛtɩlɩ nɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ, sa yɛkʋla yɛkɔ yɛyɛ egyima wɔ Japan Bɛtɩlɩ. Nɩ eyi kɛma yekeyike Miwako yɩ awuvulɔ na ama yɛahʋla yɛahila bɛ. Meti yeduli yɛhɔlɩ kɛnɩ wɔ 1999.

Yɛhɔlɩ Japan Bɛtɩlɩ nɩ, mmeliyema mɔɔ bɔwɔ kɛnɩ nɩ manlɩ yɛ anyɩ leli paa. Akʋ̃ sʋ̃, nɩ mmeliyema mɔɔ bɔwɔ Japan nɩ bɔwɔ subanɩba sũmaa zɛ. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, nɩ bo kunlu ɩdɩyɛnɛ, na bɛpɛ ngyɛnvʋlɔyɛlɛ sʋ̃. Eyi hanlɩ mɩ ahʋnlɩnɩ paa na mʋwɔlɩ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ muko-zumazuma bɛ. Nɩ bɛáva be egyimayɛlɛ bedi eɣelɛ kʋlaa, na nɩ bɔbɔ numɔ sʋ̃ bɛyɛ egyima. Mɩnla ɔhʋanɩ zʋ a? Wɔ Japan bɛ amamɩlɛ nu nɩ, nɩ bosunzu kɛɛ sɛ mmenii okuwu be bɔ numɔ yɛ egyima be a, mfasʋ sũmaa wɔ zʋlɔ tɛla kɛɛ enii ɔkɔ̃ kɛdɩ yɩ nwʋ kɛyɛ egyi-ma, na ɩava ɩabɩbɛ animinyiamɩ ɩama yɩ nwʋ. Naasʋ̃ iluwakɛɛ ɩsʋnɩ dɩka mɔɔ bɛdɩdʋlɩ mɩ nɩ ti, itwu mmɩlɩ be a, kezimɔ mmeliyema mɔɔ bɔwɔ Japan bɛyɛ be nninyeni be nɩ, nɩ mɩádɩ yɩ tʋ. Meti nɩ ɩsɛ kɛɛ musuma kɛɛ mɩkɛla abʋtalɩ ali mmɩlɩ mɔ mɩ nɩɩ bɛ láyɛ adwɩnɩ nɩ. Idwuli dɩka be nɩ, akʋ̃ mɩɣa mɩdʋlɩ bɔ tʋ, na mʋnwʋnlɩ kɛɛ, pɔkɔ bela mɔɔ yekezi nɩ, sɛ yɛfa ahyɩhyɛdɩ yɩ ɔhʋanɩhilelɛ yɛyɛ egyima a, Gyehova kɔ-hʋla keyila zʋlɔ. Iluwakɛɛ, debela mɔɔ yekezi pɔkɔ kɛɛ yɛkɛyɛ nɩ, Gyehova yɛ ɩma ɩyɛ buwɔ a.
