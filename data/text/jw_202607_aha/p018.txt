Sɔ̃mavolɔ Pɔɔlʋ helɛlɩ kɛɛ: “Ɔzʋmɩ kɛɛ Nyamɩnlɩ ɩwɔlɩ oyuwadɩ nɩ, yɛ ɩwɔlɩ yɩ tʋ kɛɛ ɩyɩlɩ yɩ subanɩ nɩ mɔɔ bɛánwʋ yɩ nɩ ikehile mmenii a.” (Wul. 1:20) Bayɩbʋlʋ tʋhilelɛ be kile nkyɩkyɛmi eyi tʋ kɛɛ, “abiye mɔɔ ɩkʋla ɩdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ nninyeni nwʋ bʋwɔ nɩ kɔnwʋ Nyamɩnlɩ yi subanɩ azʋlɔtʋ nɩ.” Meti, ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ abɔdɩ nwʋ na ama yɛanwʋ Nyamɩnlɩ yi subanɩ ngɩnlɩma nɩ.

15 Mmenii sũmaa da nnɩka mɔɔ ɩáma bɔnwʋ abɔdɩ sũmaa. Sɛ ɔzɔ yɛ wʋ bʋwabɩlɛ dɩ a, mɛyɩ po nɩ, ɔkɔhʋla koluwa abɔdɩ zʋ kozuma Gyehova nwʋ dɩyɛ sũmaa. Ma yezunzu Patrick mɔɔ ɩzʋ Republic of the Congo yɩ twonwa kezile mɔɔ bɔfʋlɔ yɩ Brazzaville nɩ yi odwokɔ yɩ nwʋ. Ikã kɛɛ: “Eyile bela mʋbɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ mʋkɔnwʋ Gyehova yɩ abɔdɩ. Mbɔmɩ sũmaa nɩ, mɩta mʋnwʋ mmʋwaba anzɛnyɩɩ nnʋma mɔɔ bɛ anyɩ wʋlʋ mɔɔ bɛpɩbɛɛ alɩyɛ anzɛnyɩɩ dɩka mɔɔ bɛkɛla. Eyile ɔkʋ̃ be, mʋnwʋnlɩ nnʋma be kɛɛ bɛyɛɛ bo obuwu. Nɩ bɛsɩza nninyeni be wɔ yɛ apaa yi nu kɛnɩ na bɛfa bɛyɛ bɛ obuwu yi, na ama bɛava bo nnwuakula bɛagua nu.” Patrick iákila nnʋma eyi la, na mfomi ɩdwɩnɩndwɩnɩnlɩ Dɩyɛwɔlɛvʋlɔ yɩ nwʋ. Iva thɩnlanlɩ nwʋmɔ kɛɛ: “Mɩya mʋnwʋnlɩ yɩ kɛɛ Gyehova dwɩnɩ yɩ abɔdɩ munwatĩĩ nwʋ, mɔɔ yɩ abɔdɩ ngyɩkĩĩ ngyɩkĩĩ po kɩnla nwʋ. Eyi ma mʋnwʋ kɛɛ ɩdwɩnɩ mʋ nwʋ na ɩkɛva mɔɔ mihyinyia bela sʋ̃ ɩkɛma mɩ.”
