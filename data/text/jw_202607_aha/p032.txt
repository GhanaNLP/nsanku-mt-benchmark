Siyeziye Munwalɩyɛ Mɔɔ Lhyɛ Ɛzɩzɩbɛ
<<<P>>>
Wɔ yɛ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʋ nɩ, yenyia ɔnwʋmɔ ɔhʋanɩ yɛma munwalɩyɛ yɛfa yɛhyɛ yɛ mmeliyema mmenyia nɩɩ mmaalɛ ɛzɩzɩbɛ. (Hib. 10:24, 25) Kɛ ɩkɛyɛ na yɛaziyeziye munwalɩyɛ mɔɔ ɩkã ahʋnlɩnɩ na ɩhyɛ mmenii mvʋlɔ ɛzɩzɩbɛ a?
<<<P>>>
• Bɔ Gyehova mpayɩ. Siã Gyehova kɛɛ ɩwʋwá wʋ ma ɛma munwalɩyɛ mɔɔ ɩkɛhyɛ yɩ animinyiamlɩ na ɩahyɛ mmenii mvʋlɔ ɛzɩzɩbɛ.—Ndw. 35:18.
<<<P>>>
• Fa siye wʋ anyɩ zʋ kɛɛ elékinga mɔɔ ɩwɔ buuku yi nu la kɛkɛ. Sɛ ɛfa wʋzawʋ wʋ ndwokɔ ɛma munwalɩyɛ a, mmenii mvʋlɔ nyia ɔzʋlɔ mfasʋ paa na ɩbʋwa bɛ ma bɔnwʋ kezimɔ elé Gyehova nu dedi.
<<<P>>>
• Ma wʋ munwalɩyɛ yɩ lyɛ sikalɛ. Munwalɩyɛ mɔɔ ɩyɛ sikalɛ na ɩáyɛ kyɩngyĩĩ nɩ ta hyɛ ɛzɩzɩbɛ paa, na ɩbʋwa ma mmenii sũmaa fa bɔ nwʋ wula dɩyɛzumanlɛ yɩ nu.—Ɛɣɛ. 10:19.
<<<P>>>
• Ma munwalɩyɛ mɔɔ ɩhyɛ ɛzɩzɩbɛ. Máfa wʋ munwalɩyɛ yɩ tu wu mmeliyema fʋlɔ anzɛnyɩɩ ɛfa twu bɛ anyunu anzɛnyɩɩ ɛfa tɩndɛ tiyia bɛ.
<<<P>>>
Sɛ wʋányia nwʋmɔ ɔhʋanɩ ma munwalɩyɛ po a, sa ɔkʋla hyɛ mmenii mvʋlɔ ɛzɩzɩbɛ. Ɔhʋanɩ mɔɔ okoluwa zʋlɔ kɛyɛ eyi yɛ lɛ kɛɛ, ekɛyɛ azunlɩ mmɩlɩ mɔ abiye ɩmaa munwalɩyɛ nɩ, na aɣinzi yi, wʋahã mɔɔ wʋ anyɩ lellili nwʋmɔ wɔ bo munwalɩyɛ nɩ nwʋ wʋahile bɛ.—Ɛɣɛ. 10:21.
