16 Mɩnla ɔhvanɩ zʋ yɛ yɛkɔhʋla yekozumazuma Gibiyɔnʋmaa nɩ a? Dɩkɛɛ Gibiyɔnʋmaa yɩ nɩ, yɛzaye stilde{ʋ} yɛpɛ kɛɛ yɛyɛ mɔɔ yɛkɔhʋla bela yɛsʋ̃mɩ Gyehova na yɛbʋwa mmenii mvʋlɔ stilde{ʋ} ma bɛyɛ zɔ. Naasʋ̃ ɩyɛ a, ikehyinyia kɛɛ yɛkɛyɛ nzɩnzanlɩyɛ wɔ yɛ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu. Naasʋ̃ yɛfa anyɩnlılelɛ yɛyɛ zɔ iluwakɛɛ yɛdɔ Gyehova. Na ɔzɔ yɛ eliyema benyia Alwin, mɔɔ ɩwɔ Philippines nɩ yɛlɩ a. Nɩ ɩyɛ egyima wɔ oil kɔmpeni be nu, na nɩ ide akatuwa kezile paa. Naasʋ̃, nɩ ɩpɛ kɛɛ ɩyɛ sʋ̃maa wɔ Gyehova yɩ ɔzʋ̃mɩnlɛ nu. Meti mmɩlɩ bela mɔ mansini zʋ ehilalɛvʋlɔ kɛya kegyela bɛ asafʋ yɩ nɩ, nɩ isiã ɔhvanɩ wɔ egyima nu na ama ɩahʋla ɩava mmɩlɩ sʋ̃maa ɩahɔ dasʋlelilɛ wɔ ɔzɔ dapɛnɩ yɩ nu. Mmɩlɩ be mɛda nɩ, nɩ Alwin pɛ kɛɛ iyia-ki yi egyima yɩ na ɩyɛ ɔhvanɩbukolɛvʋlɔ. Meti ɩwɔlɩ Gyehova mpayɩ kɛɛ ɩwʋwá yɩ ma inyia egyima fʋlɔ mɔɔ ɩkɛma ɩkɔhʋla ɩkɛyɛ ɔhvanɩbukolɛ egyima yɩ be. Nɩ Alwin zi kɛɛ sɛ iyiaki oil kɔmpeni egyima yɩ a, sa yi ozukuasɛmɩ kʋla kɔ azɩ. Naasʋ̃, mmɩlɩ mɔ inyianlɩ egyima be mɔɔ ɩkɛma ɩkɔhʋla ɩkɔwɔ yɩ ɔhvanɩbukolɛ egyima yɩ tu nɩ, yɩ anyɩ leli paa. Ayinzi yi, yɩ yı ya hɩnlanlɩ yɩ nwʋ ma bɛyɛlɩ ɔhvanɩbukolɛ egyima yɩ, na kɛ agyiahʋnlɩmaa nɩ, bɔhʋlalɩ bɔwʋwalɩ mmenii 21 ma bɛya bɔzʋ̃mɩnlɩ Gyehova. Pʋlazunɛ bela íni nwʋmɔ kɛɛ Gyehova yɩ anyɩ zɔlɩ agyiahʋnlɩmaa eyimmɔ bɔ mmɔdɩnɩwɔlɛ wɔ yɩ ɔzʋ̃mɩnlɛ nu nɩ baaba. Yɛzayɛ stilde{ʋ} yɛkɔhʋla yekenyia anwʋlazʋ kɛɛ Gyehova yɩ anyɩ sɔ afɔlɩ mɔɔ yɛbɔ wɔ yɩ ɔzʋ̃mɩnlɛ nu nɩ, na yezi kɛɛ ɩkɛva yɛ ahyinyiadɩ ɩkɛma yɛ eyile bela.—Mt. 6:33.

17 Yezuma Gyehova yi subanɩ azʋlɔtʋ mɔɔ ɩyɛ pɩkɛ nɩ nwʋ nninyeni sʋ̃maa wɔ kezimɔ ɩ nu Gibiyɔnʋmaa lili nɩ nu. Gyehova kã yɩ nwʋ azɩ, ɩnwʋ nwɔkɛ, ɩdɩ pelɩpelɩ, na ɩdɩ nɔhvanlɛvʋlɔ stilde{ʋ}. Akʋ̃ stilde{ʋ}, iyila bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɔsʋ̃mɩ yɩ nɔhvanlɛ nu nɩ. Mɛyɩ la ahanlɩ a, yezuma nninyeni aditili be stilde{ʋ} wɔ Gibiyɔnʋmaa bɛ kɛnɩ. Dɩkɛɛ Gibiyɔnʋmaa nɩ, sɛ yeyiyiaa bʋwabɩlɛ be mɔɔ numɔ yɛ sɩ a, yɛpɛ kɛɛ yenyia Gyehova nu dedi kezile, na yɛfa yɛ nwʋ yetiã yɩ zʋ. Akʋ̃ stilde{ʋ}, dedi kɔwʋwa yɛ ma yɛkɛhã yɛ nwʋ azɩ na yɛayɛ egyima bela mɔɔ bɛkɛva kowula yɛ sa wɔ asafʋ yi nu nɩ. Sɛ bɛ nɩɩ yɛ iádi yi buwɔ stilde{ʋ} a, yɛpɛ kɛɛ yɛda abʋtalɩ ali, na yenyia anwʋlazʋ kɛɛ Gyehova keli bʋwabɩlɛ nɩ nwʋ egyima na iayila yɛ wɔ mmɩlɩ baaba nu. Akʋ̃ stilde{ʋ}, yɛpɛ kɛɛ yɛyɛ mɔɔ yɛkɔhʋla bela yɛfa yɛsʋ̃mɩ Gyehova wɔ nɔhvanlɛ nu. Lyɩla kɛɛ, yɛkɛma mɔɔ Gibiyɔnʋmaa yɩ yɛlɩ nɩ kɔwʋwa yɛ adwɩnɩ nu na yɛazumazuma be ndianwʋba nɩ.
