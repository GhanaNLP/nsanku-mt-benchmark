BƆTAYI
Sɛ yɛdwenendwenɩ ndwokɔ mɔɔ wɔ Ayɩzaya buuku yi nu nɩ nwʋ a, yɛkɔhʋla yekenyia ɛgɛɛleyikyele wɔ mmɩlɩ mɔ ye padi golɔ nɩ.
<<<P>>>
Nyia Ɛgɛɛleyikyele Wɔ Ayɩzaya Buuku Yi Nu
<<<P>>>
“Mɔɔ Gyehova ɩhã ye edɩ: ‘Kɛmɔ baalɛ kyekye yɩ ya benyia yɩ ɛgɛɛlɛ nɩ, ɔzɔ la ye mʋkɔhɔ zʋlɔ mɩkeyikye be ɛgɛɛlɛ a.’ ”
—AYI. 66:12, 13.
<<<P>>>
TUA kuakula be mɔɔ isũũ ezizibɛ ezizibɛ nwʋ mfonyini kila. Ebiye a, yɩ awʋvʋlɔ botuu bɔkɔhɔ dɩka fʋlɔ, na eyi kɛma ikediã yɩ ɔhɔnwʋmmɔ kɛnɩ. Anzɛnyɩɩ ebiye a, idii ɛyɛkɛ wɔ mfʋmɩ be mɔɔ ili nɩ nwʋ. Wɔ bʋwabɩlɛ mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ nu nɩ, mɩnla yɛ ɛdanɩ mɔɔ ɩwɔ ɔlɔlɛ nɩ ɩkɛyɛ a? Sa ɩzũ ɔlɔlɛ nu ɩtɔ yɩ ya yɩ nwʋ na iadiye yi. Akũ ɩkɛma kuakula yɩ kɔnwʋ kɛɛ ɩdɔ yɩ.
<<<P>>>
2 Sɛ yekila Bayɩbʋlʋ yi nu a, Gyehova fa yɩ nwʋ tindiã ɛdanɩ be mɔɔ ɩdwɩnɩ yɩ ya nwʋ na ɩkyekye yɩ ɛgɛɛlɛ nɩ nwʋ. Gyehova kã kɛɛ, “Kɛmɔ baalɛ kyekye yɩ ya benyia yɩ ɛgɛɛlɛ nɩ, ɔzɔ la ye mʋkɔhɔ zʋlɔ mɩkeyikye be ɛgɛɛlɛ a.” (Ayɩ. 66:13) Ɔlɔlɛ mɔɔ Gyehova ɩwɔ ɩma yɛ nɩ, ɩdɩ kezile ɩtɛla kezimɔ ɔwʋvʋlɔ bela dɔ yɩ ya nɩ. (Ayɩ. 49:15) Mmɩlɩ mɔ yeyiyiaa ndwokɔzizibɛ anzɛnyɩɩ yɛ ɛgɛɛlɛ wɔ nɩ, Gyehova duwa ndwokɔ mɔɔ ɩwɔ Bayɩbʋlʋ nu nɩ zʋ ɩkyekye yɛ ɛgɛɛlɛ na ɩhye yɛ ezizibɛ.—Wul. 15:4.
<<<P>>>
3 Gyehova manlɩ bɛhɛlɛlɩ ndwokɔ sɔ̃maa wɔ Ayɩzaya buuku yi nu mɔɔ ɩkyekye yɛ ɛgɛɛlɛ. (Ayɩ. 40:1) Yɛ Zɩ mɔɔ ɩwɔ ɔlɔlɛ nɩ manlɩ Ayɩzaya hɛlɛlɩ ɛgɛɛlɛyikyule ndwokɔ hɔ manlɩ yi mmenii Dwuudamaa mɔɔ nɩ beyiyiaa ɔhawʋ nɩ. Yɛzayɛ sʋ̃ yɛda “anwʋhyɩlɩlɛ” mmɩlɩ nu, meti yehyinyia ɛgɛɛlɛyikyele. (2 Tim. 3:1) Bɛmá yehila nkyɩkyɛmi azʋlɔtʋ be wɔ Ayɩzaya buuku yi nu mɔɔ ɩkɔhʋla ɩkeyikye yɛ ɛgɛɛlɛ na ɩahye yɛ ezizibɛ mmɩlɩ mɔ (1) yɛ bʋwabɩlɛ zɩnza ayilemvele nu, (2) yɛyɛ tanɩ be ma ye ti-
