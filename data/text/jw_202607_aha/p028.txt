Mɩ nɩɩ Michiko Oda nɩɩ yi hũ
<<<P>>>
nɩ, mʋhɔlɩ pika be nu. Nɩ pika yi dɩ bɔlɔnsanɩ, na mɩhanlɩ kɛɛ, sɛ midi dasɩlɛ wɔ kɛnɩ muwie la pɛ a, sa mʋkɔ awudo. Iluwakɛɛ nɩ mɩvɛ paa, na nɩ nzulo yi sõ pɛ kɛɛ itwi. Meti nɩ mɩáhula mʋnwʋ yi kɛɛ sɛ mʋbɔ o, sɛ áma mʋpɔ o. Naasʋ̃ mmɩlɩ mɔ mʋvʋlɩ bɔlɔnsanɩ mɔɔ ɩtɔ zvlɔ asã nɩ, mʋnwʋnlɩ kɛɛ benyia be gyĩ kɛnɩ. Nɩ ɩzɔ̃ Philippines, na mɩhanlɩ wɔ mɩ ti nu kɛɛ, ‘Benyia eyi mɛyɩ, miále midi kɛɛ ikediye odwokɔ yɩ.’ Naasʋ̃ adwɩnɩ mɔɔ nɩ mɩlɛ nɩ, nɩ ídɩ zɔ kʋlaa! Mʋwɔlɩ yɩ tʋ kɛɛ mɩ nɩɩ benyia yɩ sumaa Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ, na ɩɣa ɩyɛlɩ mɩ anlɩma wɔ sunzuma ɔfʋã nu.

MUZUMANLƖ NNINYENI SŨMAA WƆ BROOKLYN BƐTƖLƖ
<<<P>>>
Mɩ nɩɩ Wilson hɔ wʋwalɩ alʋbɔ anwĩ mɛda nɩ, bɔdʋlɩ salɛ bɔvʋlɔlɩ mɩ wɔ Brooklyn Bɛtɩlɩ wɔ 1985. Kɛmɔ mʋbɔɔ mu odwokɔ yɩ tʋ yɩ mɩhanlɩ nɩ, bɛhanlɩ kɛɛ mʋhɔ mɩyɛ egyima wɔ dɩka mɔɔ bɛpɩlɩntɩ awuuku. Eɣile ɔkʋ̃ be, nɩ mɩlɛ mahyini be mɔɔ bɛfa bɛkɛlɛ buuku be yi odwokɔtile mɩyɛɛ egyima. Na mɩánwʋ mɔɔ ɩyɛlɩ, nɩ mɩzɩkɛ buuku yɩ cover yɩ nu sũmaa. Mmeliyema mɔɔ mɩ nɩɩ bɛ yɛ egyima nɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ, mʋhɔ mɩhã nwʋmɔ odwokɔ mihile eliyema mɔɔ ikila ɔzɔ azʋkɔlɩyɛ yɩ zʋ nɩ. Mmɩlɩ mɔ mʋhɔlɩ nɩ, mihilehileli mu ndwokɔ nu mihileli yi, naasʋ̃ dɩyɛ ɔkʋ̃ be wɔ kɛnɩ mɔɔ mɩáyɛ. Na eliyema mɔɔ ikila yɛ zʋ nɩ ɩzɔ̃ annidi nu ɩhã ihileli mɩ kɛɛ, “Mmɩlɩ bela mɔɔ, ekeli mfʋmlɩ be nɩ, ɩyɛ a, pa kyɛbɛ.” Mɔɔ izili nɩ manlɩ muzumanlɩ kɛɛ, sɛ midi mfʋmlɩ be a, ɩsɛ kɛɛ mide mitiã numɔ na mɩpa kyɛbɛ.
<<<P>>>
Mmeliyema mɔɔ bɛ alʋbɔ hɔ anyunumɩ, mɔɔ nɩ bɔsũmɩ wɔ Bɛtɩlɩ nɩ manlɩ muzumanlɩ kezimɔ mvʋzɔ̃ mɩ ahunlɩnɩ nu mʋkɔzɔ̃mɩ mmenii mvʋlɔ. Eɣile ɔkʋ̃ be, nɩ yekelii yɛ oyuwa alɩyɛ, na nɩ mɩ nɩɩ Eliyema Benyia Milton Henschel mɔɔ nɩ ɩkɩnla Ɔhʋanɩhilelɛ Okuwu yi nwʋ nɩ da ɔbʋnlɩ ɔkʋ̃ nwʋ. Ɔzɔ eɣile yi, mmenii sũmaa ɣalɩ dɩka mɔɔ bedi alɩyɛ nɩ paa, na nɩ ɩma mmeliyema mɔɔ bɛkyɛɛ alɩyɛ yɩ nɩ beánwʋ bɛ anyɩ zʋ. Mmɩlantɩyɛ mɔɔ nɩ yɛdɩda ɔbʋnlɩ yɩ nwʋ nɩ, nɩ yɛtɩndɛlɩɩ yɛ avũ tʋ kɛɛ, bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛkyɛɛ alɩyɛ yɩ, bɔ nwʋ ɩáyɛ ndɛ kʋlaa. Eliyema Henschel dʋlɩ mɔɔ yɛhanlɩ nɩ, naasʋ̃ iábuko yi munwa. Na idwodwuli ɩhɔ ɩhɩnlanlɩ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛkyɛɛ alɩyɛ yɩ nwʋ ma bɛhyɛlɩ. Kãnwʋazɩ subanɩ mɔ Eliyema Henschel lalɩ yɩ ali nɩ, mɩ ɛɣɛɛlɛ iléfi kyĩ. Imanlɩ mɩhakɛlɩ kezimɔ Gyisɛsɩ zɔ̃mɩnlɩ yɩ asɔ̃mavʋlɔ yɩ nɩ.—Dwɔ. 13:3-5.
