Gyehova Dɩdɩ Mɩ Ma Mɩɣa Mɩɣɛ Yɩ Sʋ̃mɩvʋlɔ Baaba
<<<P>>>
KEN KIUCHI YI MUNWA NU NDWOKƆ
<<<P>>>
MƖ LANWƲ la mɩkakɛ mmɩlɩ mɔ ili bɔmʋnlɩ mɔɔ mʋhɔlɩ dɩka mɔɔ bɛpɩlɩntɩ awuuku wɔ Brooklyn Bɛtɩlɩ mɔɔ ɩwɔ New York, U.S.A. nɩ paa. Mmɩlɩ mɔ mʋhɔ mu-wulalɩ ɔzɔ dɩka yɩ na mʋnwʋnlɩ kezimɔ mahyini azulɔtʋ mɔɔ bɛfa bɛpɩlɩntɩ dɩyɛ nɩ ɩyɛɛ dede na ituonɩ yɩ nwʋ nɩ, ɩyɛlɩ mɩ nnwʋánlɩ paa. Nɩ kɛlɛta yɩ nwʋ ndudulubo va aliyia yɩ nu kɛnɩ munwala na oil mɔɔ bɛfa bokua mahyini yi nu sɔ̃ leli kɛnɩ.
<<<P>>>
Mʋnwʋnlɩ nninyeni sɔ̃maa wɔ kɛnɩ, naasʋ̃ mɔɔ ɩhanlɩ mɩ paa yɛ lɛ mmeliyema mmulantɩyɛ nɩɩ nyɛ-dɛkɛ mɔɔ nɩ bɛyɛ egyima wɔ kɛnɩ nɩ. Nɩ egyima ɔkʋ̃ yɩ nɩ la yɛ bɛyɛ yɩ eyile bela, na nɩ mmenii bɛánwʋ egyima mɔɔ bɛyɛ wɔ kɛnɩ po na bɛahã kɛɛ bɛkamvʋʋ bɛ. Naasʋ̃ mmeliyema mɔɔ nɩ bɛyɛ egyima wɔ kɛnɩ nɩ mɛyɩ, nɩ bɛ anyɩ deli bɛ egyima yɩ nwʋ paa. Be ndianwʋ yɩ wʋwalɩ mɩ ma mʋnlɩ kezimɔ ɩse kɛɛ mʋzʋ̃ kãnwʋazɩ nu mʋsɔ̃mɩ Gyehova.
<<<P>>>
Gyehova ɩva alubɔ sɔ̃maa ɩdɩdɩ mɩ ma mɩɣa mɩyɛ yɩ sʋ̃mɩvʋlɔ baaba. Naasʋ̃ ansaana mɩbanwʋ nɔhuanlɛ yɩ nɩ, nɩ mʋwɔ Buddha ɔzɔ̃mɩnlɛ nu. Ma mili bɔmʋnlɩ mhã mɔɔ ɩyɛlɩ mɔɔ mɩɣa mʋnwʋnlɩ nɔhuanlɛ yɩ mihile wʋ.
<<<P>>>
MƖBƖBƐLƖ NYAMƖNLƖ WƆ MU NNWUAKULA NU
<<<P>>>
Bɔwʋlɩ mɩ wɔ Chicago, Illinois, mɔɔ ɩwɔ U.S.A. nɩ, na ɛkɛnɩ sʋ̃ la yɛ bɛdɩdɩlɩ mɩ a. Mɩ awʋvʋlɔ wulɩ nnwuakula anla, na mɩzamɩ yɛ mɩdɩ ɛɣalɛ ebani a. Mɩ awʋvʋlɔ duli wɔ Japan hɔ wʋwalɩ Chicago, iluwakɛɛ nɩ bosunzu kɛɛ sɛ bɔkɔ bɔbʋwa America a, nninyeni kɔhɔ bʋwɔ kɛma bɛ paa. Nɩ bɛpɛ kɛɛ bo nnwuakula kɔ sukuulu baaba na ama iazi bɛ bʋwɔ wɔ eyile be.
<<<P>>>
Nɩ mɩ awʋvʋlɔ dɩ Buddhamaa mɔɔ bɛáva bɔ ɔzɔ̃mɩnlɛ bedi ɛɣɛlɛ kulaa. Pika mɔɔ bɛdɩdɩlɩ mɩ maamɩ wɔ numɔ nɩ, nɩ Buddhamaa bɛ asɔlɩ pika gyĩ numɔ. Nɩ mɩ maamɩ yɩ papa nɩɩ yi mmenlima mmenyia anwĩ dɩ Buddha azɔfʋ. Meti mɩdɩ kuakula nɩ, nɩ yɛ abusunwa yɩ íádindiã Buddha ɔzɔ̃mɩnlɛ azɩ kulaa. Nɩ sɛ mʋzʋ̃ su-kuulu mɩba awudo bela a, midi bɔmʋnlɩ mɩyɩba incense mɩma Buddha buzumɩ mɔɔ iziye yɛ awudo nɩ, na mɩfa sɛka nɩɩ nzulo mɩma yɩ. Na sɛ itwu Bɔɔlɔ eyile a, nɩ yɛkɔ Buddha asɔlɩ aliyia nu yɛkɔ yɛsʋ̃mɩ buzumɩ be mɔɔ ɩdɩ kɛɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ yɛ awudo nɩ, naasʋ̃ eyi mɛyɩ nɩ ɩdɩ kezi na nɩ yɩ nwʋ munwala dɩ goodu.
<<<P>>>
Mmɩlɩ mɔ minyianlɩ kɛyɛ alubɔ asunwa nɩ, mɩkakɛ kɛɛ mihilalɩ buzumɩ mɔɔ iziye yɛ awudo nɩ la dinyii zɛ, na miɣizialɩ mʋ nwʋ kɛɛ, ‘Sɛ muwu a, mɩnla yɛ ɩkɔdʋ mɩ a?’ Nɩ Buddha ɔzɔ̃mɩnlɛ ma mʋnwʋ kɛɛ, sɛ muwu a, sa mukɔ mʋtʋwa mɩ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ zʋ wɔ dɩka mɔɔ azunzuma nkʋ̃ la yɛ bɔwɔ kɛnɩ a. Eyi ti, mʋnwʋnlɩ kɛɛ sɛ eyile ɔkʋ̃ eyile ɔkʋ̃ la na muwu a, mɩkɛlanɩ sun-zuma, na mulɔkula mikele mɩ anyɩ kɛmɔ mmenii yɛ nɩ. Eyi manlɩ mɩ ɛɣɛɛlɛ wɔlɩ baaba, na ɛkɛnɩ la mɩdɩlɩ nyavlɩ kɛɛ míni bʋwavʋlɔ bela.
<<<P>>>
Mmɩlɩ mɔ minyianlɩ kɛyɛ alubɔ 15 nɩ, mɩdɩlɩ nyaanlɩ kɛɛ mfasʋ bela íni nninyeni mɔɔ mɩyɛ wɔ Bu-ddha ɔzɔ̃mɩnlɛ nu nɩ zʋ. Mɩɣa mʋnwʋnlɩ kɛɛ mihyi-nyia debe wɔ mɩ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu. (Mt. 5:3) Minyianlɩ nya-nlɩdɩlɛ be kɛɛ Nyamɩnlɩ tɩyasɩvʋlɔ be mɔɔ ɩlɛ tumi
