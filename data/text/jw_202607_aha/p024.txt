14 Kila dɩyɛ mɔɔ ɩtɔ zʋlɔ anwĩ mɔɔ ɩkɔhʋla ɩkɔwʋwa Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ be ma ɩkɛva mɔɔ ɩsumaa nɩ ɩkɛyɛ egyima. Fa yɩ kɛɛ wʋ Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ be ɩwɔ yɩ tʋ ɩbaa dɩyɛzumanlɛ na ɔnwʋ kɛɛ ɩ nɩ eliyema be bɛ avinli láyɛ baaba. Ebiye a, eliyema yɩ ɩyɛ debe idiyia yɩ. Sa ɔkʋla bʋwa yɩ ma ɩnwú mmeliyema yɩ kezimɔ Gyehova nwʋ bɛ nɩ la. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, sa ɩkʋla ibizia yɩ nwʋ kɛɛ: ‘Mɩnla afɔlɩ yɛ Gyehova ɩwɔ ɩva ihile kɛɛ ɩdɔ ye mmeliyema yɩ a?

14. Kɛ ɛkɛyɛ na wʋawʋwa Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ be mɔɔ ɩ nɩɩ eliyema be bɛ avinli láyɛ baaba nɩ a?

(Dwɔ. 3:16) Kɛ Gyehova bu ɔkɔ̃yɛlɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ yɛ avinli nɩ ise a? (Ndw. 133:1) Sɛ Gyehova nwʋ kɛɛ yɛyɛɛ mɔɔ yɛkɔhʋla bela yɛkɔwɔ ɔkɔ̃yɛlɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ yɛ avinli nɩ nwʋ banɩ a, kɛ ɩtɩ nyanlɩ ise a?’ (2 Kɔl. 13:11) Bʋwa wʋ Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ yɩ ma ɩnwú kɛɛ, sɛ yɛfa yɛ adwɩnɩ yesiye ye mmeliyema bo subanɩba zʋ tɛla kɛɛ yɛkɛva yɛ adwɩnɩ yekeziye bɔ mfʋmɩ zʋ a, ɩma Gyehova yɩ anyɩ de paa.

15 Ɔhʋanɩ fʋlɔ mɔɔ ɔkɔhʋla koluwa zʋlɔ kɔwʋwa wʋ Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ ye lɛ kɛɛ, ɔkɔwʋwa yɩ ma ɩkɛva mɔɔ ɩsumaa nɩ ikezizi abɔkɔba. Sa ɔkʋla di bɔmʋnlɩ fa Sa Ɔkʋla Enyia Anyɩnlɩlelɛ Daabaa! buuku yi ozumalɛdɩyɛ 35 nɩ kile yi. Yi odwɔkɔtile se, “Kezimɔ Yekezizi Abɔkɔ Baaba.” Naasʋ̃ sɛ ebiye a, wʋ Bayɩbʋlʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛvʋlɔ yɩ ɩwɔ odwɔkɔ yɩ nwʋ mpayɩ, na ɩyɛ ɛbɩbɛlɛnu sʋ̃ nkʋ̃ʋ̃, naasʋ̃ ɩyɛ sɩ ɩma yɩ kɛɛ ikezi pɔkɔ sʋ̃ ɛ? Ebiye a, ɩánwʋ Bayɩbʋlʋ nu egyinalɛzʋ-ndwɔkɔ pɔkyɩɩ mɔɔ ɩfalɩ yi odwɔkɔ yɩ nwʋ. Sɛ ɩba yɩ zɔ a, sa ɔkʋla hyɛ yɩ ezɩzɩbɛ ma ɩdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ mɔɔ ɩwʋwalɩ Gyisɛsɩ ma izizili abɔkɔba nɩ nwʋ. Ihanlɩ kɛɛ: “Eyile bela mɩyɛ mɔɔ [mɩ Zɩ] ɩpɛ yɩ.” (Kɩnga Dwɔɔnʋ 8:29.) Sa ɔkʋla hyɛ yɩ ezɩzɩbɛ ma ibizia yɩ nwʋ kɛɛ, ‘Ɛbɩbɛlɛnu mɔɔ mɩyɛ, nɩɩ mɔɔ muzuma mɩvalɩ Gyehova nwʋ munwala nɩ, mikila a, mɩnla pɔkɔ yɛ misi a, ɩkɛma Gyehova yɩ anyɩ kele a?’ Bʋwa yɩ ma ɩnwʋ kɛɛ, sɛ isi pɔkɔ mɔɔ ɩkɛma Gyehova yɩ anyɩ kele a, mɛyɩ yɛ ikile kɛɛ izi pɔkɔ baaba a.
