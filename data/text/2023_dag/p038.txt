Lɛbiginsim Sɔŋdiba (Development partners (DOs)):Dimbɔŋɔ nyɛla tiŋgbana bee laɣiŋgunima ban pahiri Gomnanti zuyu ni bɛ pali tiŋgbaŋ lɛbiginsim nia. DPs shɛhira n-nyɛ Dunia ŋɔ totopɔŋ shɛli bɛ ni booni 'International Monetary Fund (IMF)', World Bank, African Development Bank (AfDB) bee tɔyisigu di be tiŋgbana ayiyi sunsuuni kamani Germany, United Kingdom n-ti pahi United States of America (USA).
