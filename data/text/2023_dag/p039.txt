Nɛma ʒi n-yi Daabiligu Ya (Export Trade Houses) ETH: Di nyɛla daabiligu ya shɛŋa ŋan bɔri tuma tiri daabiligu shɛŋa ŋan bɔri tinduya ni daabigɔnaate ni bɛ deeri bee yihiri nɛma bee n-tumdi tiri ba.
<<<P>>>
Fiscal Deficit:Di wuhirila Gomnanti laɣipaŋbo neembɔra din yɛn yo o laɣidibo ni nti pahi nyɔri yɔbu zuyu.
<<<P>>>
Fiscal Operations: ŋannima n-nyɛ kunta mini laɣidibo laasabu maalibu lahibali tibu, Liɣiri gbubbu (Cash managrement), liɣiri zaŋ kpɛhi daabiligu ni (investment), laɣiyɔbo (accounts payable), daabiligu ni neen' shɛŋa ŋan bi kɔhira (Fixed assets), tiŋbaŋ puuni gbibibu (Internal Control), ni samli yɔbu lɛbigibu.
<<<P>>>
Fiscal Policy: Di nyɛla Gomnanti ni kpuɣi laɣidibo mini farigu zal' shɛŋa ŋan ni kpaŋsi bomma ni nyamma biɛhigu, di bahi bamdi, dagu' kara biɛhigu .
<<<P>>>
Fiscal Stress: Di nyɛla tɛha din bɔri ni di zahim dundɔŋ ni Gomnanti ni be sababu / muyisigu ni yuun' shɛli din ni bɔri tiŋbaŋ maa zaa sɔŋsim, di bahi bamdi tuun' shɛŋa din ni baligi zilɛli ni yubu niŋbu.
<<<P>>>
Free SHS:Free SHS nyɛla zal' shɛli Ghana Gomnanti ni zali ka di nyɛ Tiŋgbaŋzuyulana, Nana Akuffo-Addo ʒɛmani ni pili shɛli 2017, September goli ni. Din yɛn che ka karimbia kam ŋun baŋsim simdi ni o chaŋ SHS chaŋ li ka bi yo kɔbo.
<<<P>>>
Ghana Cares: Di nyɛla yaɣa ayi tuun' shɛli din tuyiri Gomnanti ni pun tum shɛli koronavarɔsi tuhi ku (Coronavirus Alleviation Programme (CAP))” la.

Sɔŋsim: (Grants): Dimbɔŋɔ nyɛla liɣiri sɔŋsim shɛŋa liɣiri tuma duri ni tiri Gomnanti ka di pa la o ti lahi yɛn yo li mi yaha .
<<<P>>>
Sɔŋsim din tiri Gomnanti tuma Yaɣ' shɛŋa (Grants to other Government Units): Dimbɔŋɔ nyɛla zaligu ni sayi ti ni Gomnanti yo laɣ'shɛŋa n-niŋ tuma yaɣa kamani Asabuli pɔŋ ni (District Assemblies Common Fund' (DACF)), Pala pɔŋ ni (Road Fund), Ghana Education Trust Fund (GETFUND), Petroleum Related Fund mini Alaafee Bomma Guzuli (National Health Fund).
