25. Yaɣili ŋɔ yɛrimi kpaŋsiri gomnanti tuun' gahinda kpaŋmaŋa yaɣa ŋan yaai 2022 yuuni la mini tuun' shɛŋa ŋan gbaai ni ŋa tum 2023 yuuni puuni.
<<<P>>>
26. Tuma ŋan gbaai ni ŋa tum ŋɔ mini niŋsim ŋɔ zaa jɛndila Gomnanti Tiŋgbani lɛbiginsim nia ŋan gbaai saha saɣisima (Government's Medium-Term National Development Policy Framework (2022-2026)). Tuma ŋan gbaai ni ŋa tum ŋɔ nia nyɛla ŋan yɛn pali Gomnanti CARES tuma la nti pahi nia ŋan yi tinduya ni nima ni gbaai alikauli shɛŋa zaŋ jɛndi UN Agenda 2030 (SDG's) mini African Union Agenda 2063.
<<<P>>>
27. Yaɣili ŋɔ yɛrimi kpaŋsiri Gomnanti kpaŋmaŋa yaɣa ŋan yaai bee gahim mini bomma ni nyamma yaɣa ni 2022 yuuni la, nti pahi tuma ŋan gbaai ni ŋa tum mini niŋsim ŋan yɛn niŋ 2023 yuuni.

28. Gomnanti, zaŋ dalim o sodoligu nia din nyɛ di taɣi liyiri din nyari tihi sabbu yaɣili la ni na, yimina ni tibihi miliyɔŋ 2.7 n-ti pukpariba 2022 yuuni la kɔbu saha.
<<<P>>>
29. Nɔhi ban nyɛri gala ka bɛ kalinli yiyisi 120,000 daa nyɛla bɛ ni da shɛba n-tari paɣiba ban guli nɔhi ka bɛ kalinli yiyisi 1,000 ka bɛ yi bɔba ni yaɣa awei ni na (Savelugu, Sagnarigu, Wa tiŋa puuni, Nandom, Sissala East, Krachi East, Nkwanta South, Techiman North and Nkoranza) ka di lu zahim Rearing for Food and Jobs module (Binnɛma bahibu bindirigu mini tuma zuɣu). Ban daa di anfaani ŋɔ daa lahi deei napɔri din ni deei nɔhi 120 din mali baatiri.
<<<P>>>
30. Din lahi pahi, binkɔb' baligu ban kalinli yiyisi 3,750 daa lahi nyɛla bɛ ni da shɛba n-tari ni bɛ dɔɣi yeligi ka pukpariba 45 nya kpaŋsibu zaŋ chaŋ bindir' pooli suhudamli polo ka ban di anfaani maa tooi nyaŋ bahi mɔni ka nyaŋ yili yɛlimuɣisira.
