Gross Domestic Product (GDP):Dimbɔŋɔ nyɛla nɛma ni tuun' shɛŋa din be tiŋbaŋ ni saha yinga dariza ni nyɛ shɛli.
<<<P>>>
Gross International Reserves:Dimbɔŋɔ zahindila tiŋgbani laɣizuɣu dariza bee yiko zaŋ chaŋdi ni yɛn tooi da nɛma bee tuma shɛm tiŋduya ni saha yini puuni. Dina n-lahi wuhiri tiŋgbani liɣiri dariza zaŋ mayisi tinduya ni liɣiri dariza.
<<<P>>>
Headline Inflation: Di nyɛla Bureau of Labor Statistics ni deeri lahibal'shɛŋa Consumer Price Index (CPI) nima sani n-tiri ti goligoli zaŋ chaŋ nɛma daa ni duri shɛm .
<<<P>>>
Nɛma dahi dubu (Inflation): Nɛma mini tuma daa ni pahiri shɛm saha yini ni.
<<<P>>>
Import substitution: Di nyɛla sodoligu din kpaŋsiri nɛma nɛma maalibu ka di zaani nɛma da n-kpena zaani ni di kpaŋsi dundɔŋ ni feera duri.
<<<P>>>

<<<P>>>
Interest payments: Di nyɛla din tahiri laɣipaŋbu bee n-yɔri nyɔri samli bee talima zuɣu.

IPEP (Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme): Fara Tuhiku Yamɛbo (Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme): Fara tuhiku yamɛbo nyɛla NPP Gomnanti ni tahi tuun'shɛli na ni di tuhi ku fara ka filim woliginsim din be fɔndi ni mini tiŋkpansi sunsuuni. Di nia nyɛla di ti tiŋkpansi bɛ yɛlibɔra ka di sɔŋ n-yihi ba fara ni.
