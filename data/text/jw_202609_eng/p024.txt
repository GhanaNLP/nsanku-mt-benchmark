14 The priesthood shows that Jehovah wants us to have life. (Ezek. 33:11) The animal sacrifices offered in ancient times did not completely atone for the sins of God’s worshippers. (Heb. 7:19) However, those sacrifices pointed to the perfect sacrifice of Christ Jesus. His sacrifice makes true forgiveness possible. It opens up the way for obedient humans to have everlasting life. How grateful we are that Jehovah took the initiative to arrange for our salvation!—1 John 4:10.
<<<P>>>
15 We see Jehovah’s love in his choice of individuals who will serve as priests. Like their Master, the anointed must prove faithful even to death. (Phil. 2:8; Rev. 2:10) Their loyalty to Jehovah must be proved beyond all doubt. Thus, we can trust that they will lovingly apply the benefits of the ransom sacrifice, which will help us to overcome sin and grow to perfection. That remarkable work will continue throughout Christ’s Thousand Year Reign until Jehovah’s purpose with regard to the earth and the healing of mankind has been accomplished.—Rev. 22:1, 2.
<<<P>>>
16 Christ, our High Priest, is sympathetic to our challenges and experiences because he himself lived as a human on earth. (Read Hebrews 4:15.) Consider: Do you feel tired at times? Jesus did too. (John 4:6) Have you felt the effects of hunger and thirst? Jesus knows what that feels like. (Matt. 4:2; John 19:28) Do you face opposition and ridicule for your faith? Jesus did too. (Isa. 50:6; John 15:20) Have you lost a loved one in death? Jesus lost his friend Lazarus and apparently his stepfather, Joseph. We can rest assured that our High Priest, Jesus, will care for us with understanding and empathy that come from experience.
<<<P>>>
17 The 144,000 will have fellow feeling and insight as they help Jesus lift mankind to perfection. They were once imperfect and sinful as men or women on earth. They know what it feels like to make mistakes and to struggle with guilt. They understand what it is like to suffer from health problems, both physical and emotional. They come from a variety of backgrounds, races, cultures, time periods, and personal circumstances. Whatever your life experience has been, someone from among the 144,000 can relate.
