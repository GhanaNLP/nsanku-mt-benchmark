17 What have we considered in this article? Clearly, we must reject the spirit of this world. That includes shunning unwholesome speech, rejecting immoral conduct, and avoiding online dangers. Although the spirit of this world is powerful, do not despair. You can resist its influence. After all, Jehovah is far more powerful than Satan and his wicked world. (Read 1 John 4:4.) Furthermore, Jehovah will provide you with his holy spirit—the most powerful force in the universe—to help you avoid this world's influence. (Luke 11: 13) May we all be determined to continue "walking by [God's] spirit" and guard against the spirit of the world!—Gal. 5:16.
