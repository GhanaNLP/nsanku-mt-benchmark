WHAT IS “THE SPIRIT OF THE WORLD”?
<<<P>>>
⁴ In the Bible, the expression “the spirit of the world” refers to the way people who are not friends of God think and act. Those who are infected by the spirit of the world tend to be selfish. They may also reject Jehovah's moral standards. For example, many today take a permissive view of ungodly conduct. They follow “the will of the nations,” not the will of the Creator.—1 Pet. 4:3.
