Guard Against “the Spirit of the World”
<<<P>>>
“Everything in the world . . . does not originate with the Father.”—1 JOHN 2:16.
<<<P>>>
WE LIVE in a world that is ruled by Satan. (John 14:30; 1 John 5:19) He especially wages a bitter fight against Christ’s followers. (Rev. 12:12, 13, 17) This does not mean that Satan is in every place at all times. In fact, the Bible does not indicate that any angel—wicked or righteous—has that ability. But Satan has a large number of wicked angels on his side. (Rev. 12:9) And the spirit of the world, which Satan promotes, is everywhere. Most people yield to its influence whether they realize it or not. (Read 1 John 2:15, 16.) Even if people do not believe that Satan exists, they please him when they act according to the world’s spirit. In a sense, they are “caught alive by him to do his will.”—2 Tim. 2:26.
