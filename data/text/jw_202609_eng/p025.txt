18 All that we have learned about the priesthood teaches us that love, mercy, compassion, and fellow feeling are aspects of Jehovah's personality. Satan is our constant accuser; Jehovah is not. (Rev. 12:10) We have considered in this study that Jehovah has done much to draw us to him and to pave the way for us to feel assured of his approval and friendship. Does that not draw you closer to Jehovah? Does it not make it easier to accept his love and forgiveness?
<<<P>>>
19 Our study of the priesthood also assures us that even when we feel unworthy because of our sins, we can trust that Jehovah understands our struggle. He knows that we are made of dust. (Ps. 103:14) Nonetheless, he continues to see past our sins and focuses on the good in us and on our potential for improvement. When our sins and mistakes are all we can see, we need to remember that Jehovah “is greater than our hearts and knows all things.”—1 John 3:20.
<<<P>>>
20 As we have seen, Jehovah has provided the priesthood so that we can worship him acceptably. It is his heartfelt desire that we do so. Thanks to the gift of the priesthood, the gap between Jehovah and his imperfect but faithful worshippers has been closed. We may thus do as Hebrews 4:16 encourages: “Let us, then, approach the throne of undeserved kindness with freeness of speech, so that we may receive mercy and find undeserved kindness to help us at the right time.”
