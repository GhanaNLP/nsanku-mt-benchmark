10 Those who want to please Jehovah must hate what he hates. (Ps. 97:10) With that in mind, we can ask ourselves: ‘Do I feel that Jehovah’s moral laws are keeping me from living as I would really like to? Does my choice of entertainment indicate that I am drawn to things that Jehovah hates? Do I wish that Jehovah would somehow adjust his standards to conform to my desires?’
<<<P>>>
11 What if you feel drawn to something that Jehovah hates? Do not conclude that it is impossible for you to live by Jehovah’s standards. Remember that all imperfect humans are inclined to sin. (Ps. 51:5; 103:14) However, you can choose not to act on wrong desires, not “[to carry] out the will of the flesh.” (Eph. 2:3) Also, you should refuse to dwell on wrong desires. If at some point you sense that a wrong desire has come up in your heart, immediately turn your eyes and your mind away from things that Jehovah hates. (Gen. 39:9, 12) And reflect on how such things could damage your relationship with Jehovah and with others. Be determined not to let that happen. (Eph. 5:5) With Jehovah’s help, you can learn to hate what is bad.
