MAKING JEHOVAH'S NAME KNOWN AMONG THE NATIONS
<<<P>>>
12 All of us depend on the work done by translators. Bible translation is not new.* Translators have worked tirelessly to ensure that the message of God's Word reaches more people. In recent years, a remarkable tool for unifying Jehovah's people has been the New World Translation of the Holy Scriptures. Note what an elder who serves in Nepal says about this translation: "Reading this translation in my language has made Jehovah more real to me. It has helped me draw closer to God and has strengthened my faith." The New World Translation is available, in whole or in part, in well over 300 languages. That is one reason why Jehovah's people are able to think and speak in agreement—they use this clear, reliable Bible translation that conveys the same message regardless of language. (1 Cor. 1:10) However, this translation does much more.
<<<P>>>
13 The New World Translation is one of the few Bibles being distributed in the 21st century that continues to honor Jehovah's name by using it throughout the Bible text. At the beginning of the last days, it was common to find the name Jehovah in many translations of the Bible, especially in the Hebrew Scriptures. However, the leaders of Christendom, who claim to represent God, have become increasingly opposed to making known or to using God's name, Jehovah. The leaders of the Catholic Church have removed it from their Bibles and from related literature.* Most Protestant denominations likewise omit the name from their translations of the Bible. In contrast, the New World Translation consistently restores the divine name to its rightful place in the Scriptures, regardless of language. Why is this significant?
<<<P>>>
14 The Scriptures repeatedly highlight the importance of knowing and using God's name. The psalmist urges God's servants to "praise the name of Jehovah." (Ps. 135:1) When teaching his disciples how to pray to Jehovah, Jesus directed them to say: "Let your name be sanctified." Later, while praying to his Father, Jesus said: "I have made your name known." (Matt. 6:9; John 17:26) The apostle Paul quoted the words of the prophet Joel, who said: "Everyone who calls on the name of Jehovah will be saved." (Rom. 10:13) People who use Bible translations that omit the divine name cannot obey these inspired exhortations.
