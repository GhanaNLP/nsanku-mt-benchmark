SE YOYE A NWANSANA KO SI ’KRAMAN SO A, NA WABO NO KAM! KAM!
<<<P>>>
OBOFUO bi na owoo akraman nnan; obako de Ahuahua; obako so de Ntaferentafere; obako so de Abopo; obako so de Amenekom. Na one n’akraman yee ’ha. Da oko wuram’ a, one yen na ekoro. Dakoro bi okoo wuram’ na oko hunoo bese dua bi; na aso se yi’a, kyen!
<<<P>>>
Na osan baa ’fie beboo ne yere amanee se, “Bese bi a m’ako hunu, aso se yi’a, kyen!” Ne yere see, “Ko te na wo nim se ye’nnya bese fua mpo nwe, na y’anya bi aton.” Obofuo sii ’kwan so, na ogyaa akraman no ’fie koo die bese no wo. Na oforoye tetee bese no bi. Efei otee noho’a se, “Twa! be! kukuru! kukuru! hu! pi!” Sasabonsam anim’ ni. Osee, “Hwan na watete me bese yie?” Obofuo see, “Mea!” Sasabonsam see, “Adie a w’aye yi, meku wo seisei.” Obofuo see, “Wo beku me de a, ma me mfre m’akraman ansa.” Osee, “Fre, na se etia mprensa na y’amma a, enee meku wo.” Na obofuo ama so:
<<<P>>>
“Ahuhuya e hyia me, Ntaferentafere e hyia me. Abopo e hyia me, Amenekom’ e hyia me.
<<<P>>>
Wai, wai, wai, Otekyem’!
<<<P>>>
Wai, wai, wai, Otekyem’!
<<<P>>>
Wai, wai, wai, Otekyem’!
<<<P>>>
Wai, wai, wai, Otekyem’!
<<<P>>>
Wai, wai, wai, Otekyem’!”
<<<P>>>
Na akraman no tee no ’fie kete kete kete, na ye’de mmirika koo kurotia. Na obofuo ama so bio:
<<<P>>>
“Ahuhuya e hyia me, Ntaferentafere e hyia me.
<<<P>>>
Abopo e hyia me, Amenekom’ e hyia me.
<<<P>>>
Wai, wai, wai, Otekyem’!
<<<P>>>
Wai, wai, wai, Otekyem’!
<<<P>>>
Wai, wai, wai, Otekyem’!
<<<P>>>
Wai, wai, wai, Otekyem’!
<<<P>>>
Wai, wai, wai, Otekyem’!”
<<<P>>>
Efei na akraman no ate; ye’de mmirika agum’ prada! prada! Sasabonsam see, “Fre bio, efei die mprensa no aso.” Obofuo maa so dendenden se:
<<<P>>>
“Ahuhuya e hyia me, Ntaferentafere e hyia me.
<<<P>>>
Abopo e hyia me, Amenekom’ e hyia me.
<<<P>>>
Wai, wai, wai,
<<<P>>>
Otekyem’!
<<<P>>>
Wai, wai, wai, Otekyem’!
<<<P>>>
Wai, wai, wai, Otekyem’!
<<<P>>>
Wai, wai, wai, Otekyem’!
<<<P>>>
Wai, wai, wai, Otekyem’!”
<<<P>>>
N’ano ansi, na akraman no anim’ ni. Obofuo see, “Ahuahua, Sasabonsam ni, huahua no ho.” Na Ahuahua huahuaa ne ho. Osee, “Ntaferentafere, taferetafere ne ho.” Osee, “Abopo, bo no po.” Na Abopo abo ne menem’ po; na Sasabonsam fwee ho, kum! Na Amenekom’ afa no amen’. Obofuo ne akraman no sesaa bese no baa ’fie. Obofuo baye benoaa nkate’kwan ayowa ma, na ode sii akraman no ho; osaa nsuo de sii ho, na okoo ne yere ho ko didiye. Odidi wieye na okoo abonten ko tenaa gyedua’se. Na obofuo akoa nwansana oko hunoo aduane no na wama so asi pata. Akraman no firii abonten baye na okom de yen. Ntaferentafere ka kyeree nkrofuo no se, “Mo ahunu se adie a y’aye ama ye’wura, aduane mpo ato sese omma yen bi nni, nti mo mma yenko kum no.” Ye’see, “Wo mmoa, wo mmoa.” Ntaferentafere koo abonten na wako taferetafere obofuo ho, na obofuo ne no e’go’; na Ntaferentafere koye. Na Ahuahua so aba; na ohuahuaa obofuo ho; na o’nso koye. Na Abopo baye’a, na wabo ne menem’ po, na Amenekom so aba na wamene no. Efei akraman no baa ’fie; ye’se ye’fwe patom’ ho, ye’fwe se nkwan soso ho. Ye’see, “Nwansana, dabere obofuo gyaa yen aduane na w’adie?” Nwansana see, “Me nhunoye kora.” Ahuahua se o’pagya n’ani, ofwe se aduane si ho. Osee, “Agya! y’akum ye’wura kwa, na se aduane a ode sii yen ho ni, na nye ’ko, nye ’nu, Nwansana na ofa ko sii ho.” Nwansana firii ho na okoye. Ene se, se ’kraman te ho, na se nwan-sana ko si ne so a, na o’pe no akye no.
