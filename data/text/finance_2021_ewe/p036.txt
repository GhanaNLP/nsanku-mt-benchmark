Nyati: Gakpɔmɔnunyawo fɔfɔ ɖe te to ŋgɔyidɔwo dzi yiyi, wo nu wuwu kple wofe kelili me.
<<<P>>>
Ŋgɔyidɔwo wɔwɔ ƒe Adzɔdolawo: Esia nye dukɔwo kple habɔbɔ siwo naa kpekpeɖeŋu dukɔ bubuwo to ganana wo me be woate ŋu akpɔ ta na woƒe ŋgɔyidɔwo wɔwɔ ƒe taɖodzinuwo. Adzɔdolawo ate ŋu anye dɔwɔƒe alo dukɔ aɖewo ƒe ƒuƒoƒo. Le kpɔɖeŋu me, “International Monetary Fund” (IMF), Xexemedukɔwo ƒe Gadzraɖoƒegã (World Bank), African Development Bank (AfDB) kple bubuawo. Adzɔdola la ate ŋu anye dukɔ ɖeka aɖe ko. Le kpɔɖeŋu me, Germany, United Kingdom, United States of America (USA) kple bubuawo.
