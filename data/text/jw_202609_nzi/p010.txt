6 Yizilayɛma neazo ne maa yɛnwu kɛ ɔhyia kɛ yɛneɛnlea adwenle mɔɔ ɔti yɛyɛ tieyɛ yɛmaa Gyihova nee menli mɔɔ yekpa bɛ kɛ bɛli ye menli ne anyunlu la anu. Yɛbahola yɛabiza yɛ nwo kɛ, ‘Saa menwu kɛ adehilelɛ bie baboa me a yɛɛ medi zo ɔ?’ Bie a kɛmɔ ɛnee Yizilayɛma bie mɔ ɛngulo kɛ bɛ belamunli mralɛ ne mɔ wu la ati a bɛvale boane mogya ne bɛhanle bɛ nlenkɛ felɛmo ne mɔ anwo a. Eza yɛbahola yɛabiza yɛ nwo kɛ, ‘Asoo kɛmɔ awie mɔ yɛ tieyɛ la ati a medame noko meyɛ tieyɛ ɔ?’ Kenle mɔɔ Yizilayɛma fi Yigyibiti la, Gyihova maanle ‘bɛnyianle Yigyibitima anyunlu ɛlolɛ.’ (Ɛzɛ. 12:36) Noko nzinlii mɔɔ Yigyibitima rale bɛ nwo zo la bɛanva bɛ nwo bɛando Gyihova anwo zo, emomu bɛyɛle anzosesebɛ. (Ɛzɛ. 14:10-12) Noko yɛmɛ, ɔnle kɛ yɛyɛ kɛ Yizilayɛma ne la, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛfa yɛ nwo yɛto Gyihova nee menli mɔɔ yekpa bɛ la anwo zo. (Kenga Gyɛlɛmaya 23:4.) Saa bɛmaa yɛ adehilelɛ bie na yɛnwie ɔ bo te kpalɛ anzɛɛ yɛdi zo a ɔbaha Gyihova agbɔvolɛ ɛya bɔbɔ a, yɛbali zo. Kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na yɛanyɛ nvonleɛ ko ne ala mɔɔ Yizilayɛma yɛle la bie na yɛanyia ahonle mɔɔ yɛ tieyɛ la ɛ?

7. Kɛzi ɛlɔlɛ kpole mɔɔ yɛbanyia yɛamaa Gyihova la bamaa yɛayɛ tieyɛ ɛ?
<<<P>>>
anzɛɛ saa yɛdi zo a ɔbamaa yɛayia ngyegyelɛ a, bie a ɔbayɛ se kɛ yɛbayɛ tieyɛ. Saa ɛhye bie to wɔ a kakye kɛ, kɛmɔ yɛkulo Gyihova na yɛfa yɛ nwo yɛ to ɔ nwo zo la ati a yɛtie ye a. (1 Dwɔn 5:3) Gyihova anwo ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛ la maa yɛnyia abotane yɛkendɛ ye na yɛdie yɛto nu kɛ bie a ɔbalie mekɛ kolaa na yɛanyia yɛ tieyɛ ne azo nvasoɛ.—Nol. 7:8; Maeka 7:7.

8 Wɔ yɛ mekɛ ye azo noko, Nyamenle menli yɛ tieyɛ nwo neazo kɛnlɛma wɔ mekɛ bɔbɔ mɔɔ bɛnze kɛzi adehilelɛ bie baboa bɛ la. Kɛ neazo la, wɔ COVID-19 ewulenzane ne anu, bɛhanle bɛhilele asafo ne mɔ kɛ bɛlua video-conference zo bɛyɛ debiezukoalɛ ne mɔ. Asafo nu kpanyinli bie mɔɔ wɔ Peru la hanle kɛ mɔlebɛbo ne ɛnee ɔsuzu kɛ adehilelɛ zɔhane ɛnrɛboa wɔ bɛ ɛkɛ. Ɔnva nwo kɛ yeanwie ɔ bo te la, ɔbɔle kpɔkɛ kɛ ɔbayɛ tieyɛ na yeava ɔ nwo yeado Nyamenle anwo zo. (Mrɛ. 3:5, 6) Duzu a vile nu rale a? Ɔhanle kɛ: “Menwunle kɛzi Gyihova yila bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛyɛ tieyɛ la. Mediema mɔɔ wɔ asafo ne anu la anwo yɛle se yɛɛ yɛbikyele yɛ nwo kpalɛ.” Yɛnlea neazo bieko. Wɔ 2020, ahyehyɛdeɛ ne vale asafo nee ekpunli mɔɔ ka aneɛ gyɛne la anwo adehilelɛ fofolɛ rale. Mɔɔ adiema raalɛ bie mɔɔ wɔ Japan la dele kɛ bɛbagu ekpunli mɔɔ ɔwɔ nu la, ɔhanle kɛ: “Mekulo kɛ medi adehilelɛ fofolɛ ɛhye azo, mengulo kɛ meka nwolɛ edwɛkɛ ɛtane. Me nye la kɛ mebanwu mɔɔ vi nu bara la.”

9 Siezie ɛ nwo maa dwazotia. Ɛhye anwo hyia kpalɛ wɔ yɛ daselɛlilɛ gyima ne anu. Wɔ ewiade ɛleka dɔɔnwo, arane ne mɔ ɛtɛfale bɛ sa ɛtɛtole yɛ daselɛlilɛ gyima ne azo. Yemɔ bɔbɔ saa yɛlɛbɔ edwɛkpa ne nolo a ɔwɔ kɛ yɛmaa menli ne nwu ye kɛ ɔnrɛhye bɛbazɛkye ewiade ɛhye, na yɛbahɔ zo yɛaha edwɛkɛ zɔhane kɔkpula kɛ ɔka ekyii Amagɛdɔn ne ba la.
