GYIHOVA DUMA NE

12 Gyima mɔɔ edwɛbohilelɛma yɛ la boa yɛ muala. Nuhua ko a le Baebolo ne mɔɔ bɛkile ɔ bo la, na ɔnle debie fofolɛ.* Bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛkile edwɛkɛ bo la yɛ gyima ɛsesebɛ amaa menli dɔɔnwo asa aha Nyamenle Edwɛkɛ ne. Kenlensa ye, Ngɛlɛlera Nwuanzanwuanza—Ewiade Fofolɛ Ngilebɛbo ne ɛboa Gyihova menli ne ɛmaa koyɛlɛ ɛra bɛ avinli. Asafo nu kpanyinli ko mɔɔ wɔ Nepal la hanle Ewiade Fofolɛ Ngilebɛbo ne anwo edwɛkɛ kɛ: “Baebolo ngilebɛbo ɛhye mɔɔ mekenga wɔ medame mumua ne me aneɛ nu la ɛmaa menwu Gyihova kpalɛ. Yeboa me yemaa mebikye ye na me diedi ne ɛyɛ kpole.” Bɛyɛ Ewiade Fofolɛ Ngilebɛbo ne amuala anzɛɛ ye foa bie wɔ aneɛ mɔɔ bo 300 la anu. Kɛzi bɛhile Baebolo ɛhye abo la anu la ɛkɛ, ɔnyɛ se kɛ ɛbade ɔ bo yɛɛ ɔka Gyihova anwo nɔhalɛ ne. Ɛhye ati a Gyihova menli mɔɔ wɔ ewiade amuala la lɛ diedi ko na bɛka edwɛkɛ ko ne ala bɛkile awie mɔ la. (1 Kɔl. 1:10) Noko tɛ ɛhye angome a maa Ewiade Fofolɛ Ngilebɛbo ne sonle bolɛ a.
<<<P>>>
13 Ɛnɛ, Nyamenle duma ne ɛnle Baebolo dɔɔnwo anu. Noko ɔfinde Ewiade Fofolɛ Ngilebɛbo ne anu fane dɔɔnwo. Kɛyɛ ɛvolɛ 100 mɔɔ eze ɛhɔ la ɛnee Nyamenle duma ne finde Baebolo ngilebɛbo dɔɔnwo anu, titili wɔ Hibulu Ngɛlɛlera ne anu. Noko Kilisiene asɔne asɔne ne anu mgbanyima mɔɔ bɛse bɛgyi Nyamenle agyakɛ anu la maa ɔyɛ se kɛ menli banwu Nyamenle duma ne na bɛava bɛali gyima. Kateleke asɔne ne anu mgbanyima ɛye Nyamenle duma ne ɛvi Baebolo mɔɔ bɛdabɛ a bɛhile ɔ bo la nee bɛ mbuluku biala anu.* Eza ɛzonlenlɛ gyɛne dɔɔnwo noko ɛye Nyamenle duma ne ɛvi bɛ Baebolo ngilebɛbo ne mɔ anu. Noko saa bɛlɛkile Ewiade Fofolɛ Ngilebɛbo ne abo bɛahɔ aneɛ biala anu a, bɛmaa Nyamenle duma ne finde ɛleka mɔɔ ɔfɛta kɛ ɔfinde la kɛ mɔɔ ɛnee ɔde ye wɔ mɔlebɛbo Ngɛlɛlera ne anu la. Ɔhyia kɛ yɛmaa menli nwu Nyamenle duma ne. Maa yɛnlea deɛmɔti ɔle zɔ la.
<<<P>>>
14 Baebolo ne ta ka deɛmɔti ɔhyia kɛ yɛnwu Nyamenle duma ne na yɛfa yɛdi gyima la. Edwɛndolɛnli ne maanle Nyamenle azonvolɛ anwosesebɛ kɛ “bɛye Gyihova duma ne ayɛlɛ.” (Edw. 135:1) Mɔɔ Gyisɛse ɛlɛkile ye ɛdoavolɛma ne kɛzi ɔwɔ kɛ bɛyɛ asɔne la, ɔse anrɛɛ bɛha kɛ: “Wɔ duma nwo ɛde.” Nzinlii mɔɔ Gyisɛse ɛlɛyɛ asɔne la, ɔhanle ɔhilele Gyihova kɛ: “Memaa bɛnwu wɔ duma.” (Mat. 6:9; Dwɔn 17:26) Ɛzoanvolɛ Pɔɔlo noko hwenle adwenle hɔle ngapezonli Dwowɛle edwɛkɛ ɛhye azo. Ɔse: “Awie biala mɔɔ kɛbɔ Gyihova duma la, bɛbalie ye ngoane.” (Wlo. 10:13) Bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛkenga Baebolo ngilebɛbo mɔɔ Nyamenle duma ɛnle nu la ɛnrɛhola ɛnrɛli folɛdulɛ ɛhye mɔ azo.
