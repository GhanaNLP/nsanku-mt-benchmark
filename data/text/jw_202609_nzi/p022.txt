Wɔ tete ne, kolaa na bɛakyekye Yizilayɛ maanle ne la ɛnee abusua tile biala bɔ afɔle maa ɔ nee ye abusua ne. Kɛ neazo la, mɔɔ Nowa nee ye abusua ne vi ɛlɛne ne anu vindele la ɔbɔle moyɛlɛ afɔle. (Gyn. 8:20) Ebileham sisile afɔlemokyea amaa yeabɔ afɔle yeamaa Gyihova. (Gyn. 12:7, 8) Yɛɛ Dwobu noko bɔle ɛyelalɛ afɔle maanle ɔ mra ne mɔ. (Dwobu 1:5) Ɛhye ati a kolaa na bɛaye Livae ɛsɔfolilɛ nwo ngyehyɛleɛ ne bɔbɔ la, ɛnee Baebolo ne ta ka ɛsɔfoma anwo edwɛkɛ la. Gyihova hanle hilele Yizilayɛma kɛ: “Maa ɛsɔfo ne mɔ [mbusua tile] mɔɔ dahuu bikye Gyihova la ɛde bɛ nwo amaa Gyihova angu bɛ.”—Ɛzɛ. 19:22.

7 Ɛsɔfo mɔɔ limoa mɔɔ Baebolo ne hanle ɔ nwo edwɛkɛ la a le Mɛlekezidɛke. (Gyn. 14:18) Ɛnee ɔle ɛsɔfo yɛɛ belemgbunli. Nzinlii Gyihova bɔle ɛwɔkɛ kɛ Mɛzaya ne noko bayɛ ɛsɔfo yɛɛ belemgbunli kɛ Mɛlekezidɛke la. Noko ninyɛne mgbalɛ mɔɔ Mɛzaya ne bayɛ la baboa alesama dahuu.—Edw. 110:4; Hib. 7:17.
<<<P>>>
8 Nzinlii mɔɔ Gyihova kyekyele Yizilayɛ maanle ne la, ɔvale Mosisi Mɛla ne ɔmaanle bɛ. Ɔhanle mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ ɛsɔfo ne mɔ yɛ la wɔ Mɛla zɔhane anu. Ɔkpale Mosisi adiema Aalɔn nee ɔ bo zo amra kɛ bɛdabɛ a bɛbayɛ ɛsɔfoma a. (Ɛzɛ. 40:12-15) Ɛnee ɔwɔ kɛ Livaema boa ɛsɔfo ne mɔ maa bɛgyinla maanle ne amuala nee bɛ nuhua ko biala agyakɛ anu bɔ afɔle. Gyihova vale afɔle holɛ ko mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛbɔ, deɛmɔti ɔwɔ kɛ bɛbɔ afɔle zɔhane mɔ nee kɛzi ɔwɔ kɛ bɛbɔ ye la anwo adehilelɛ maanle bɛ. Ɛnee ngyehyɛleɛ ɛhye le mɔɔ bara kenle bie la sɔhovolɛ.

9 Mɔɔ bɛzɔnenle Gyisɛse la, bɛkpale ye kɛ Ɛsɔfo Kpanyinli mɔɔ ɔbaboa alesama amaa bɛahola bɛazonle Nyamenle a. (Hib. 8:1) Nzinlii, Gyisɛse vale ye ngoane mɔɔ di munli la bɔle afɔle kpɔnenle alesama vile ɛtane nu. Ɛnee ye afɔle ne kpogya tɛla nane afɔle mɔɔ ɛnee Yizilayɛ ɛsɔfo ne mɔ bɔ la. Ɛnɛ noko, Gyisɛse le yɛ Ɛsɔfo Kpanyinli mɔɔ boa yɛ ɔmaa yɛsonle Gyihova a. (Hib. 9:24) Wɔ Nyamenle Belemgbunlililɛ ne anu, menli 144,000 ne mɔɔ bɛkpokpa bɛ la nee Gyisɛse babɔ nu ayɛ gyima kɛ arelemgbunli nee ɛsɔfoma. (1 Pita 1:3, 4; 2:9) Saa bɛkɔ anwuma ala a bɛ nee Nyamenle yɛɛ Gyisɛse babɔ nu ayɛ gyima bɛamaa alesama ali munli na azɛlɛ ne ayɛ paladaese. Yɛ nye la ye ade kɛ yɛbanwu kɛ bɛwie bɛ gyima ne yɛ bɔkɔɔ!
<<<P>>>
10 Kɛ mɔɔ yɛnwu ye la, ɔvi tete ne amuala Gyihova kpale ɛsɔfoma kɛ bɛboa alesama bɛmaa bɛbikye ye. Afɔle ngakyile mɔɔ ɛnee ɛsɔfo ne mɔ bɔ la maanle Yizilayɛma mɔɔ di nɔhalɛ la hakyele kɛ bɛhyia ɛkpɔnedeɛ. Gyihova bahɔ zo ava ɛsɔfoma ali gyima wɔ Kelaese Ɛvolɛ Apenle Ko Tumililɛ ne amuala anu kɔkpula kɛ alesama bɛali munli mɔɔ bɛnrɛhyia ɛsɔfoma gyima ne anwo bieko a. Duzu bieko a yɛbahola yɛazukoa yɛavi ahyɛledeɛ kɛnlɛma ɛhye anu a, na kɛzi ɔbamaa yɛanyia ɔ nwo ɛlɔlɛ kpole ɛ? Maa yɛnlea.
