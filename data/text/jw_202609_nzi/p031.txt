16 Eza suzu Robert anwubielɛ ne anwo. Agɔnwolɛ ɛtane nee anyelielɛ mgbane mgbane maanle ɔnlonle sigalɛte nee nza na ɔ nee ɔ gɔnwo mɔ mrenyia lale. Ɔnyianle adwenle kɛ ɛnee ɔlɛ fanwodi ɔyɛ debie bia-la mɔɔ ɔkulo la. Noko nzinlii ɔnwunle kɛ amuanrɛɛ ɔnlɛ fanwodi fee, emomu yeyɛ akɛlɛ yemaa ewiade ne. (2 Pita 2:19) Duzu a ɔyɛle a? Robert hanle kɛ: “Menvale menli mɔɔ kulo Gyihova la agɔnwolɛ. Menyɛle nzenzaleɛ wɔ me ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nu, me adɛladeɛ ɛwulalɛ, me anwoziezielɛ yɛɛ kɛzi metendɛ la anu.” Kɛkala Robert anye die kenle ko biala kɛ ɔlɛbɔ ɛbɛla mɔɔ sɔ Gyihova anye la. Ye ɛbɛlabɔlɛ ɛhakyi bɔkɔɔ. Ɛhye maa ɔ nye die kpole kpalɛ.

17 Duzu a yɛzuzu nwo wɔ edwɛkɛ ɛhye anu a? Yɛnwu ye wienyi kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛtwe yɛ nwo yɛfi ewiade sunsum ne anwo. Ɛhye mɔ bie a le kɛ yɛbamaa yɛ ɛdendɛlɛ ayɛ fɛ, yɛa-hwe yɛ nwo yɛavi ɛbɛlatane nwo na yɛanlea boɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛfa yintanɛte ne yɛali gyima la. Ɔwɔ nu kɛ ewiade sunsum ne anu yɛ se ɛdeɛ noko mmamaa wɔ ahonle tu. Ɛbahola wɔali zolɛ konim ɔluakɛ Gyihova lɛ tumi tɛla Seetan nee ye ewiade ne. (Kenga 1 Dwɔn 4:4.) Bieko, Gyihova bava ye sun-sum nwuanzanwuanza ne mɔɔ bo tumi bia-la mɔɔ wɔ aleɛabo la anwo zo la aboa wɔ yeamaa wɔali ewiade ye azo konim. (Luku 11:13) Bɛmaa yɛ muala yɛbɔ kpɔkɛ kɛ yɛba-maa Nyamenle ‘sunsum ne ahile yɛ adenle’ na yɛahwe yɛ nwo yɛavi ewiade sunsum ne anwo!—Gal. 5:16.
