18 Ɛsɔfolilɛ ngyehyɛleɛ ne maa yɛnwu kɛ Gyihova le ɛlɔlɛ, ɔse anwunvɔne, ɔlɛ ahunluyelɛyelalɛ yɛɛ ɔte yɛ edwɛkɛ bo. Seetan a bɔ yɛ somolɛ dahuu a, tɛ Gyihova ɔ. (Yek. 12:10) Edwɛkɛ ɛhye ɛmaa yɛnwu kɛ Gyihova ɛyɛ ngyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ ɔbamaa yɛahola yɛabikye ye, yɛayɛ mɔɔ sɔ ɔ nye na yɛayɛ ɔ gɔnwo mɔ dahuu a. Kɛzi ɛhye ka yɛ ɛ? Ɔmaa yɛnyia ɛlɔlɛ kpole yɛmaa Gyihova. Eza ɔmaa yɛnwu kɛ ɔkulo yɛ kpole kpalɛ yɛɛ ɔlɛ ɛhulolɛ kɛ ɔbava yɛdaye noko yɛ ɛtane yeahyɛ yɛ.

19 Ɔdwu mekɛ ne bie a yɛte nganeɛ kɛ yɛ ɛtane ne mɔ ati yɛ nwo ɛngyia. Noko ɛsɔfolilɛ ngyehyɛleɛ ne maa yɛnwu kɛ Gyihova te yɛ edwɛkɛ bo. Ɔze kɛ yɛle ndɛtɛlɛ. (Edw. 103:14) Noko ɔnva ye adwenle ɔnzie yɛ ɛtane ne mɔ azo, emomu ɔnea ninyɛne mgbalɛ mɔɔ yɛkola yɛyɛ nee mɔɔ yɛbahola yɛayɛ ye kenle bie la. Saa ɛtane nee nvonleɛ mɔɔ yɛyɛ la ala a wɔ yɛ ti anu a, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkakye kɛ “Nyamenle le kpole tɛla yɛ ahonle yɛɛ ɔze debie biala.”—1 Dwɔn 3:20.
<<<P>>>
20 Kɛ mɔɔ yɛnwu ye la, Gyihova ɛyɛ ɛsɔfolilɛ ngyehyɛleɛ ne amaa yɛahola yɛazonle ye wɔ adenle mɔɔ ɔdie ɔto nu la azo. Gyihova nee alesama avinli tetele, noko yelua ngyehyɛleɛ kɛnlɛma ɛhye azo yeziezie yɛ nee ye avinli amaa yɛabikye ye. Kɛ ɛnee ɔkulo kɛ ɔba ye la ɛne. Ɔti yɛkulo kɛ yɛyɛ mɔɔ Hibuluma 4:16 ka la yɛfa yɛkile kɛ yɛ nye sɔ ahyɛlɛdeɛ kɛnlɛma ɛhye. Ɔse: “Zɔhane ati, bɛmaa yɛva anwodozo yɛbikye ɛlɔlɛ kpole belemgbunli ebia ne, amaa bɛanwu yɛ anwunvɔne na yɛanyia ɛlɔlɛ kpole mɔɔ baboa yɛ wɔ mekɛ kpalɛ nu la.”
