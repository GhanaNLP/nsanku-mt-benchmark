Alesama anwo yɛ Gyihova alɔbɔlɛ ɛhye ati a yeyɛ ngyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ yɛbahola yɛalua zo yɛabikye ye na yɛanyia ɔ nyunlu ɛlolɛ la a. Na ɛhye ka yɛ maa yɛkulo ye kpole.

12 Gyisɛse noko kulo kɛ yɛ nee Gyihova nyia agɔnwolɛvalɛ kpalɛ. Kɛmɔ ɔle yɛ Ɛsɔfo Kpanyinli la ati, ɔle yɛ “boavolɛ wɔ Selɛ ne anwo lɔ.” (1 Dwɔn 2:1; kenga Hibuluma 7:25.) Ninyɛne boni mɔ a ɔbayɛ kɛ Gyisɛse gyinla zo sɛlɛ maa wɔ a? Bie a ɔnea ɛleka mɔɔ ɛvi, kɛzi bɛtetele wɔ, wɔ kpɔkɛdelɛ nee ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ ɛlɛfa nu la. Eza bie a ɔnea subane ngenlema mɔɔ ɛlɛ, wɔ nyɛleɛ yɛɛ nɔhalɛlilɛ mɔɔ ɛlɛfa wɔazonle Gyihova la. Kɛmɔ Gyisɛse kola nwu mɔɔ wɔ ahonle nu la ati, kɛ ɔde ye biala la, ɔnwu asɔne mɔɔ ɛyɛ ye dahuu ɛsɛlɛ ɛtanefakyɛ nee mɔdenle mɔɔ ɛbɔ kɛ ɛbayɛ mɔɔ ɔle kpalɛ la. Yɛbahola yɛanyia anwodozo kɛ Gyihova mɔɔ ɔlɛ ahunluyelɛyelalɛ na ɔlɛ ɛhulolɛ kɛ ɔbava ɛtane yeahyɛ la badie nzɛlɛlɛ mɔɔ Gyisɛse yɛ maa yɛ la. (Edw. 86:5) Duzu ati a yɛkola yɛka ye zɔ a? Ɔluakɛ Gyihova mumua ne a ɛkpa Gyisɛse kɛ ɔyɛ yɛ boavolɛ na ɔzɛlɛ ɔmaa yɛ a.

13. Kɛ mɔɔ Hibuluma 4:16 ka la, nyilalɛ boni bieko a ɛsɔfolilɛ ngyehyɛleɛ ne maa yɛnyia a, na kɛzi ɔboa yɛ ɛ?
