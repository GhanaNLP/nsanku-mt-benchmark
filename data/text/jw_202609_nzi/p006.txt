(Mat. 4:3; 6:13) Ɛhye ati Gyihova ahyehyɛdeɛ ne ɛmaa yɛ mbuluku nee vidio ngakyile mɔɔ maa yɛnyia anwodozo kɛ yeva yɛ ɛtane yehyɛ yɛ yɛɛ yɛsonle bolɛ yeamaa ye la. Eza ɔmaa anwodozo mɔɔ yelɛ kɛ ɔbamaa yɛ sa aha mɔɔ yɛhyia nwo la yɛ kpole. (Edw. 86:5; Mat. 10:29-31; Luku 12:28; Hib. 13:5) Ɛkakye mekɛ bie mɔɔ ɛnleanle vidio anzɛɛ ɛgengale debie wɔ yɛ mbuluku ne mɔ anu na ɔhanle wɔ ahonle la ɔ? Bie a ɛhanle kɛ, ‘Medame ti a bɛhɛlɛle edwɛkɛ ɛhye a!’ Kakye kɛ Gyihova fa sunsum nu aleɛ ɛhye mɔ maa yɛ wɔ ye mekɛ nloa amaa yeabɔ yɛ nee ye agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne anwo bane.

16 Gyihova ɛnrɛmaa ye agbɔvolɛ ɛnrɛhu ye azonvolɛ kɔsɔɔti. Noko ɔdwu mekɛ ne bie a ɔmaa dwazotiama adenle ɔmaa bɛyɛ ye azonvolɛ bie mɔ kpɔdekpɔde na bɔbɔ bɛku bie mɔ amaa yeala ali kɛ Seetan le adalɛ. (Yek. 2:10; 6:9) Noko Seetan akunlu ɛndwo menli ekyi zɔhane mɔɔ bɛwu la anwo. Kɛmɔ yeva ɛya la ati, ɔkulo kɛ bɛbɔ Nyamenle azonvolɛ nɔhavoma kɔsɔɔti abo zo. (Mat. 24:9; Yek. 12:9-12) Ɛhye ɛnrɛzi ɛlɛ! Yɛbahola yɛalie yɛali kɛ Gyihova ɛlɛdua ye anwumabɔvolɛma anwo zo yeabɔ ye menli ne kɔsɔɔti anwo bane amaa bɛazonle ye nɔhalɛ nu dahuu.—Hib. 1:7, 14.

17. Duzu ati a ɔnle kɛ yɛmaa yɛ nye da kɛ Gyihova babɔ yɛ nwo bane avi debie biala mɔɔ ɔbahola yeamaa yɛadɔ esiane nu ɛnɛ la anwo a?
<<<P>>>
bane ɔfi nyɛleɛ nee tɛnlabelɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ bahola amaa yɛadɔ esiane nu la anwo. (Mrɛ. 14:29, 30; 22:3; 2 Kɔl. 7:1) Noko ɔnle kɛ yɛmaa yɛ nye da kɛ Gyihova babɔ yɛ nwo bane avi debie biala mɔɔ bahola ava yɛ ngoane ado esiane nu ɛnɛ la anwo. Saa ɔyɛ ye zɔ a, ɔbamaa Seetan awudu adalɛ mɔɔ ɔbɔ kɛ kɛmɔ Gyihova bɔ yɛ nwo bane la ati a yɛsonle ye la anu. (Dwobu 1:10, 11) Yɛze kɛ, kɛmɔ Gyihova kulo kɛ ɔte ye duma nwuanzanwuanza ne anwo la ati a yezi amumuyɛma mɔɔ dwazo tia ye azonvolɛ na bɛyɛ bɛ kpɔdekpɔde la anwo “abotane kpalɛ” a. (Wlo. 9:22) Noko ye abotanezilɛ ne ɛlɛba ye awieleɛ. Na yɛlɛ anwodozo kɛ ɔbaboa yɛ yeamaa yɛagyinla sɔnea biala anloa k pundii amaa yeabɔ yɛ nee ye agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne anwo bane.—1 Kɔl. 10:13; 1 Pita 5:10.

18 Wɔ anwongyelelɛ kpole ne anu, Gyihova bahɔ zo abɔ yɛ nee ye agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne anwo bane amaa yɛ avinli anzɛkye. Eza ɔbabɔ yɛ nwo bane wɔ adenle gyɛne zo. Saa maanle maanle ne mɔ bateta Gyihova azonvolɛ a, Gyihova ɛnrɛmaa bɛnrɛhu ye azonvolɛ kɔsɔɔti. (Yiz. 38:1, 2, 10-16) Ɔbazoa Gyisɛse nee ye anwuma ɛlɔne ne kɛ bɛhɔlie ye menli ne, na yemɔ a bava Amagɛdɔn konle ne ara a. Gyisɛse nee ye ɛlɔne ne nee Nyamenle agbɔvolɛ baho. (Yek. 17:12-14; 19:11, 14, 15) Noko kɛzi Gyihova babɔ ye menli ne anwo bane wɔ Amagɛdɔn ne anu ɛ?
