Twe Ɛ Nwo Fi “Ewiade Sunsum” Ne Anwo
<<<P>>>
‘Ewiade ye anu debie biala ɛnvi Selɛ ne.’—1 DWƆN 2:16.
<<<P>>>
YƐDE ewiade mɔɔ Seetan a nea zo la anu. (Dwɔn 14:30; 1 Dwɔn 5:19) Menli mɔɔ ɔ nye sa bɛ kpole kpalɛ la a le Kelaese ɛdoavolɛma. (Yek. 12:12, 13, 17) Noko ɛhye ɛngile kɛ ɛleka biala mɔɔ yɛwɔ la ɛnee Seetan wɔ ɛkɛ. Baebolo ne bɔbɔ ɛnga kɛ anwumabɔvolɛ amumuyɛma nee nɔhavoma ne anu bie lɛ tumi kola yɛ zɔ. Noko akee Seetan lɛ anwumabɔvolɛ atisesebɛma dɔɔnwo mɔɔ bɛli ɔ nzi a. (Yek. 12:9) Yɛɛ ewiade sunsum ne mɔɔ Seetan a gyi ɔ nzi la noko wɔ ɛleka biala na yelua zo ɔ sa ɛha menli dɔɔnwo mɔɔ bɛnnwu ye bɔbɔ a. (Kenga 1 Dwɔn 2:15, 16.) Saa menli mɔɔ bɛnlie bɛnli kɛ Seetan wɔ ɛkɛ bɔbɔ la yɛ mɔɔ ewiade ne anu amra yɛ la bie a, ɛnee bɛlɛyɛ mɔɔ Seetan kulo la. Wɔ adenle bie azo ‘yehye bɛ kɛ bɛyɛ ye ɛhulolɛdeɛ.’—2 Tim. 2:26.
<<<P>>>
2 Mekɛ bie, ɛnee yɛ nuhua dɔɔnwo noko li ewiade sunsum ne anzi. Noko mekɛ mɔɔ yɛranwunle mɔɔ ewiade sunsum ne kile nee ahenle mɔɔ gyi ɔ nzi la, yɛvale Baebolo ne anu folɛdulɛ mɔɔ ɔse “yɛkpo ɛbɛlatane nee ewiade atiakunluwɔzo” la yɛyɛle gyima. (Tae. 2:11, 12) Kɛmɔ yɛdi zɔhane folɛdulɛ ne azo la ati, ɔda ali kɛ yɛkulo kɛ Gyihova boa yɛ ɔnva nwo kɛ Seetan lɛ tumi na ɔ sa ɛha menli dɔɔnwo la. (Gye. 4:7, 8) Nɔhalɛ nu, saa Gyihova nwu kɛ yɛlɛbɔ mɔdenle yɛakpo ewiade sunsum ne a ɔ nye die kpalɛ!

2-3. (a) Ngakyile boni a wɔ Gyihova menli nee bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛli “ewiade sunsum” ne anzi la avinli a? (b) Esiane boni a ɔwɔ kɛ yɛnea boɛ wɔ nwo a?
