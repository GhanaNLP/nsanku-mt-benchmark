Kɛzi ɛsɔfolilɛ ngyehyɛleɛ ne ka yɛ maa yɛkulo Gyihova kpole la.
<<<P>>>
Ɛsɔfolilɛ Ngyehyɛleɛ Ne Maa Yɛnwu Kɛ Gyihova Kulo Yɛ
<<<P>>>
“Ɛsɔfo kpanyinli biala mɔɔ bɛye ye bɛfi menli avinli la, bɛkpa ye kɛ ɔyɛ Nyamenle gyima ɔmaa menli.”—HIB. 5:1.
<<<P>>>
SAA yɛze o, yɛnze o, alesama amuala hyia moalɛ kpole kpalɛ. Duzu ati ɔ? Ɔvi mekɛ mɔɔ Adam nee Yive yɛle Nyamenle anwo anzosesebɛ la bɛvale ɛtane bɛwole yɛ muala. Ɛhye ati yɛ nee Nyamenle avinli ɛtete na yɛnrɛhola yɛnrɛbikye ye. (Ɛfɛ. 2:12; Kɔl. 1:21) Ɔle kɛ asɛɛ kuma kpole bie ralale yɛ nee Nyamenle avinli mɔɔ yɛngola ye pɛ na yɛahɔ Nyamenle anwo ɛkɛ la. Saa awie amboa yɛ a, yɛnrɛnyia nɔhalɛ anyelielɛ ɛlɛ yɛɛ yɛnrɛnyia anyelazo biala. Ngyehyɛleɛ boni a Gyihova ɛyɛ kɛ ɔfa yeaboa yɛ a?
<<<P>>>
2 Gyihova yɛle ɛsɔfolilɛ ngyehyɛleɛ ne amaa yealie yɛ yea-vi ɛtane nu. Ɔkpale ɛsɔfoma nee ɛsɔfo kpanyinli ɔmaanle Yizilayɛma. Nzinlii ɔkpale Gyisɛse kɛ Ɛsɔfo Kpanyinli ɔmaanle Kilisiene asafo ne na ɔtɛde yɛ Ɛsɔfo Kpanyinli ɛnɛ. Wɔ edwɛkɛ ɛhye anu, yɛbazukoa kɛzi ɛsɔfolilɛ ngyehyɛleɛ ne boale mrenyia nee mraalɛ wɔ tete ne la anwo debie. Eza yɛbanlea kɛzi yɛ Ɛsɔfo Kpanyinli Gyisɛse ɛlɛboa yɛ ɛnɛ nee kɛzi ɔ nee menli 144,000 ne baboa yɛ kenle bie la. Akee yɛbanwu kɛzi ngyehyɛleɛ kɛnlɛma ɛhye kile kɛ Gyihova kulo yɛ la.

KƐZI ƐSƆFOLILƐ NGYEHYƐLEƐ NE BOALE YIZILAYƐMA LA
<<<P>>>
3 Ezoanvolɛ Pɔɔlo hilehilele deɛmɔti Gyihova yɛle ɛsɔfolilɛ ngyehyɛleɛ ne maanle Yizilayɛma la anu. (Kenga Hibulu-
<<<P>>>
1. Duzu ati a alesama hyia moalɛ ndɛndɛ a?
2. Duzu a yɛbazuzu nwo wɔ edwɛkɛ ɛhye anu a?
3. Kɛ mɔɔ Hibuluma 5:1 kile la, kɛzi ɛsɔfolilɛ ngyehyɛleɛ ne boale menli mɔɔ ɛnee bɛkulo kɛ bɛbikye Gyihova la ɛ? (Eza nea nvoninli ne.)
