Edwɛkɛ ɛhye bahilehile gyima mɔɔ Gyihova menli ɛlɛyɛ amaa edwɛkɛ mɔɔ wɔ Zɛkalaya 8:23 ara nu la anu.
<<<P>>>
Yɛka Edwɛkpa Ne Yɛkile Menli Wɔ Bɛ Aneɛ Nu
<<<P>>>
‘Mrenyia bulu mɔɔ bɛvi aneɛ mɔɔ maanle maanle ne mɔ ka la amuala anu la barazɔ Dwuunli tɛladeɛ nu.’—ZƐK. 8:23.
<<<P>>>
GYIHOVA yɛle atuadelɛma ne mɔɔ ɛnee bɛlɛsi Babɛle Tawa ne la aneɛ ne basabasa ɔti yeammaa bɛangola sua ne si. Ɛhye maanle alesama hanle aneɛ ngakyile, bɛ nu hyehyɛle ngyikyi ngyikyi na bɛdele bɛkpondenle azɛlɛ ye azo amuala. (Gyn. 11:6-9) Noko Gyihova bodane a le kɛ ɔbaha ‘menli ekpunli kpole bie mɔɔ bɛvi maanle maanle nee mbusua nee menli nee aneɛ ngakyile nu la’ yeabɔ nu na yeava bɛ yeapɛ anwongyelelɛ kpole ne yeahɔ ewiade fofolɛ ne anu. (Yek. 7:9, 10, 14) Noko saa menli ɛhye mɔ bahola anyia diedi wɔ Gyihova anu wɔ anwongyelelɛ kpole ne anu a, kyesɛ yɛka edwɛkpa ne yɛkile bɛ wɔ bɛdabɛ mumua ne bɛ aneɛ nu yɛmaa bɛte ɔ bo. (Kenga Wulomuma 10:13, 14.) Ɔyɛ yɛ fɛ kɛ yɛlɛyɛ gyima mɔɔ ɔbamaa menli anyia ngoane kenle bie la bie la!
