Yizilayɛma diele Gyihova ɔnva nwo kɛ bie a ɔmaanle Yigyibitima vale ɛya la
(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 3)
<<<P>>>
BƐYƐLE TIEYƐ NOKO NZINLII
BƐYƐLE ANZOSESEBƐ
<<<P>>>
3 Kolaa na Gyihova alie Yizilayɛma avi Yigyibiti la, ɔhanle ɔhilele bɛ kɛ bɛhu boane anzɛɛ abɔnkye nyinli na bɛva ye mogya ne bie bɛha bɛ nlenkɛ felɛmo ne mɔ anwo amaa ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ li awieleɛ la bie anga bɛ. (Ɛzɛ. 12:5, 7) Bɛyɛ zɔ a Gyihova babɔ bɛ azua ne mɔ adɛla amaa belamunli ralɛ biala, sonla o, nane o, mɔɔ wɔ sua nu ɛkɛ ne la anwu. Noko ɔbayɛ kɛ ɛnee ɔyɛ se kɛ bɛbali mɛla ɛhye azo. Duzu ati ɔ? Bie a ɛnee bɛsulo kɛ Yigyibitima bava konle adoa bɛ ɔluakɛ ɛnee Yigyibitima awozonle ne mɔ ko le boane nyinli, ɔti bɛbu mboane nyinli kɛ bɛle nwuanzanwuanza. (Ɛzɛ. 8:25-27) Noko Yizilayɛma nwunle kɛ ɔhyia kɛ bɛyɛ tieyɛ wɔ mekɛ zɔhane ɔti ‘bɛyɛle mɔɔ Gyihova hanle’ la. (Ɛzɛ. 12:28) Na Gyihova angu bɛ belamunli mralɛ ne mɔ.
<<<P>>>
4 Zɔhane nɔe ne, Yizilayɛma vile Yigyibiti. Mɔlebɛbo ne, Gyihova hanle kɛ bɛhɔ Nyevile Kɔkɔlɛ ne sɔlɔ afoa nu. Noko nzinlii ɔmaanle bɛ adehilelɛ bie mɔɔ wɔ sonla nye zo ɛnee ɔzɔho kɛ nrɛlɛbɛ ɛnle nu la. Ɔhanle kɛ bɛhakyi bɛ nye bɛhɔ nyiakɛ na bɛhɔzokoɛ Nyevile Kɔkɔlɛ ne nee awoka ne mɔ avinli. Mekɛ zɔhane ɛnee Falo nee ye sogyama ne doa bɛ, ɔti saa bɛsia a bɛbaha Nyevile Kɔkɔlɛ ne nee Falo sogyama ne avinli. Noko Yizilayɛma yɛle tieyɛ na bɛzialɛ kɛ mɔɔ Gyihova luale ye sonvolɛ Mosisi anwo zo hanle la. (Ɛzɛ. 14:1-4) Nzinlii mɔɔ akee bɛnwunle kɛ Yigyibiti sogyama ne ɛlɛba la, bɛ “ahonle dule” na bɛhanle kɛ Mosisi vale bɛ rale ɛkɛ ne kɛ bɛrawu. (Ɛzɛ. 14:5-11) Duzu ati a akee bɛanyɛ tieyɛ a? Na kɛzi Gyihova bule subane mɔɔ bɛlale ye ali la ɛ?
<<<P>>>
5 Baebolo ne ka kɛ mɔɔ Yizilayɛma dwule Nyevile Kɔkɔlɛ ne anloa la bɛangile Gyihova nwanwane gyima ne mɔ mɔɔ ɔyɛle ye wɔ Yigyibiti la anwo anyezɔlɛ yɛɛ “bɛangakye” ye ɛlɔlɛ kpundii ne. (Kenga Edwɛne 106:7 yɛɛ ɔbodwɛkɛ.) Bɛnwunle kɛzi Gyihova bɔle bɛ belamunli mralɛ nwo bane wɔ ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ tɔ zo bulu la anu la. Noko yemɔ bɔbɔ ammaa bɛande kɛzi Gyihova kulo bɛ kpole la abo yɛɛ bɛannyia anwodozo kɛ ɔbahola yealie bɛ bieko. Kɛmɔ Gyihova nwunle mɔɔ wɔ bɛ ahonle nu la ati, ɔhanle kɛ ‘bɛdele atua wɔ Nyevile Kɔkɔlɛ ne anloa.’ Ɔwɔ nu
