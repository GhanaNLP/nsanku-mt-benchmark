<<<P>>>
“Bɛmaa Kelaese Anzodwolɛ Ne Ɛhile Bɛ Ahonle Mɔɔ Ɔwɔ Kɛ Ɔyɛ La”
<<<P>>>
Kɔlɔsaema 3:15 ka “Kelaese anzodwolɛ ne” anwo edwɛkɛ. Duzu a “Kelaese anzodwolɛ ne” kile a? Ɔkile anzodwolɛ mɔɔ awie nyia wɔ ye ahonle nu ɔluakɛ ɔle Kelaese ɛdoavolɛ la ati. Saa yɛnyia daselɛ mɔɔ kile kɛ Gyihova nee ɔ Ra ne kulo yɛ a, ɔmaa yɛnyia anzodwolɛ ɛhye. —Dwɔn 14:23, 27.
<<<P>>>
Ɛzoanvolɛ Pɔɔlo maanle Kilisienema mɔɔ wɔ Kɔlɔsae asafo ne anu la anwosesebɛ kɛ bɛmaa Kelaese anzodwolɛ ne ɛhile bɛ ahonle mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ ɔyɛ la anzɛɛ ɛmaa bɛnwu mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛyɛ la. Adenle boni azo? Giliki edwɛkɛkpɔkɛ mɔɔ Pɔɔlo hilele ɔ bo ‘ɛhile mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ ɔyɛ’ la nee kɛzi bɛfɛlɛ awie mɔɔ bɛlɛbɔ bɔlo a ɔdi referee la zɔho bɛ nwo. Ɛhye kile kɛ, kɛ mɔɔ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ referee ne ka la a bɔlobɔlɛma ne di zo la, zɔhane ala a ɔwɔ kɛ yɛmaa “Kelaese anzodwolɛ ne” kile yɛ mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ la a.
<<<P>>>
Kɛ neazo la, saa yɛ nee yɛ diema diedinli bie nyia edwɛkɛ a, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛmaa “Kelaese anzodwolɛ ne” kile yɛ adenle, kɛ asɛɛ yɛmaa yeali referee amaa yɛanwu mɔɔ yɛyɛ a yɛbahɔ zo yɛadɛnla anzodwolɛ nee koyɛlɛ nu la. Amaa yɛahola yɛayɛ ɛhye la, bie a ɔbahyia kɛ yɛfa ninyɛne mɔɔ yɛkulo la yɛsie ahane anzɛɛ yɛfa awie mɔ ɛtane yɛkyɛ bɛ. Saa yɛyɛ zɔ a ɔkile kɛ yɛlɛmaa “Kelaese anzodwolɛ ne” ahile yɛ ahonle ne mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ ɔyɛ la.—Kɔl. 3:12-14.
<<<P>>>
Bɛndɔne buluku ɛhye. Ɔboka ewiade amuala Baebolo ngilehilelɛ gyima mɔɔ bɛfa ndoboa mɔɔ vi ahonle nu a bɛdi nwolɛ gyima la anwo. Saa ɛkulo kɛ ɛye ndoboa a, yɛsɛlɛ wɔ kɔ donate.jw.org.
