Saa yɛyɛ zɔ a ɔkile kɛ yɛdie ye yɛdi yɛɛ yɛkola yɛfa yɛ nwo yɛto ɔ nwo zo. Ɔdwu mekɛ ne bie a yɛnnwu kɛzi yɛkile yɛ edwɛkɛ nu bɔbɔ yɛahile Gyihova a. Bie a yɛlɛdi nyane kpole la ati ɔ, na ɔmmaa yɛnnwu debie mɔɔ yɛyɛ nwolɛ asɔne a. (Wlo. 8:26) Noko Gyihova tie yɛ, ɔte yɛ edwɛkɛ bo yɛɛ ɔyɛ nwolɛ debie. Ɔnle kɛ ɔyɛ se ɔmaa yɛ kɛ yɛbaha debie biala mɔɔ ɛlɛgyegye yɛ la yɛahile yɛ anwuma Selɛ ne mɔɔ lɛ ɛlɔlɛ na ɔse anwunvɔne la.

14 Ɛsɔfolilɛ ngyehyɛleɛ ne maa yɛnwu kɛ Gyihova kulo kɛ yɛnyia dahuu ngoane. (Yiz. 33:11) Nane mɔɔ ɛnee bɛfa bɛbɔ afɔle wɔ tete ne la angola amkpɔne Nyamenle azonvolɛ anvi bɛ ɛtane nwo bɔkɔɔ. (Hib. 7:19) Noko afɔlebɔlɛ zɔhane mɔ boale bɛ maanle bɛnwunle kɛ bɛhyia afɔlebɔlɛ mɔɔ di munli na yemɔ a le Gyisɛse afɔlebɔlɛ ne. Gyisɛse afɔlebɔlɛ ne maa bɛkola bɛfa ɛtane bɛkyɛ bɔkɔɔ. Ɔmaa alesama mɔɔ yɛ tieyɛ la nyia anyelazo kɛ bɛbadɛnla aze dahuu. Ɔyɛ yɛ fɛ kɛ Gyihova limoale yɛle ngyehyɛleɛ ɛhye mɔɔ ɔbalua zo yealie yɛ ngoane la!—1 Dwɔn 4:10.

15 Menli mɔɔ Gyihova ɛkpa bɛ kɛ bɛyɛ ɛsɔfoma wɔ ye Belemgbunlililɛ ne anu la maa yɛnwu kɛ ɔkulo yɛ. Ɔwɔ kɛ bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛkpokpa bɛ la di nɔhalɛ kɔ ewule nu kɛ bɛ Menle ne la. (Fel. 2:8; Yek. 2:10) Saa yɛnwu kɛ bɛlile nɔhalɛ bɛmaanle Gyihova a, ɔbamaa yɛanyia anwodozo kɛ wɔ Evolɛ Apenle Ko Tumililɛ ne anu bɛbaboa yɛ bɛamaa yɛaye mɔɔ tenrɛ la na yɛali munli ngyikyingyikyi. Bɛbalie yɛ bɛavi ɛtane mɔɔ bɛvale bɛwole yɛ la anu. Gyisɛse afɔlebɔlɛ ne a bamaa ɛhye mɔ amuala ayɛ boɛ a. Gyisɛse nee bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛ nee ye bali tumi la bayɛ nwanwane ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ kɔkpula kɛ bodane mɔɔ Gyihova lɛ maa alesama nee azɛlɛ ne la amuala bara nu bɔkɔɔ la.—Yek. 22:1, 2.

16 Yɛ Ɛsɔfo Kpanyinli Gyisɛse te ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ yɛfa nu la amuala abo ɔluakɛ ɔdaye noko ɔvale ngyegyelɛ ko ne ala bie anu wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɔwɔ azɛlɛ ye azo la. (Kenga Hibuluma 4:15.) Kɛ neazo la, asoo ɔyɛ a ɛte nganeɛ kɛ wɔvɛ somaa ɔ? Gyisɛse noko vɛle. (Dwɔn 4:6) Ɔyɛ a ɛhɔne nee nzuhɔne ku wɔ ɔ? Gyisɛse eva tɛnlabelɛ ɛhye bie anu ɛlɛ. (Mat. 4:2; Dwɔn 19:28) Ɔlua wɔ diedi ne ati bɛyɛ wɔ kpɔdekpɔde ɔ? Zɔhane ala a bɛyɛle Gyisɛse a. (Aye. 50:6; Dwɔn 15:20) Asoo wɔ kulovolɛ bie ewu ɔ? Gyisɛse agɔnwo Lazalɛse nee ye mame ahu Dwosefi noko wule. Yɛbahola yɛanyia anwodozo kɛ Gyisɛse mɔɔ le yɛ Ɛsɔfo Kpanyinli ne la bala ahunluyelɛyelalɛ ali yɛɛ ɔbaze yɛ anwunvɔne ɔluakɛ yeva tɛnlabelɛ biala mɔɔ yɛfa nu la bie anu ɛlɛ.
