EDWƐNE 91 KA ANWODOZO NEE ANWOBANEBƆLƐ NWO EDWƐKƐ
<<<P>>>
4 Yɛbahola yɛapɛ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ wɔ Edwɛne 91 ne anu la sikalɛ kɛ: Nyia anwodozo kɛ Gyihova babɔ ɛ nwo bane. Ngyehyɛnu 1 nee 2 ne maa yɛnwu kɛ Gyihova babɔ bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛfa bɛ nwo bɛto ɔ nwo zo la anwo bane. (Kenga Edwɛne 91:1, 2.) Kɛmɔ yɛze kɛ yɛvea Anwuma Anwumanli ne, Tumivolɛ Bedevinli ne andɛba abo la ati, yɛte nganeɛ kɛ yɛlɛ anwobanebɔlɛ. (Wut. 2:12; Edw. 17:8) Edwɛkɛ agbɔkɛ “ɛvealeka” yɛɛ “aranemgbole” maa yɛkakye kɛzi ɛnee bɛdie suakpole bie anwo bane wɔ tete ne la. Ɛnee bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛde suakpole ne azo la nyia anwobanebɔlɛ yɛɛ ɛnee ɔwɔ kɛ bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛnde ɛkɛ ne la noko nriandi ba ɛkɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛ agbɔvolɛ doa bɛ la. Wɔ ngyehyɛnu mɔɔ doɔdoa zo la anu, edwɛndolɛnli ne vale ndonwo ngakyile lile gyima amaa yɛanyia anwodozo kɛ Gyihova babɔ yɛ nwo bane ɔnva nwo ngyegyelɛ biala mɔɔ yɛbayia la.—Edw. 91:3-13.
<<<P>>>
5 Edwɛkɛ mɔɔ wɔ Edwɛne 91:14-16 ne la kyekye yɛ rɛle tɛla edwɛkɛ biala mɔɔ wɔ Edwɛne 91 ne anu la. Wɔ ɛkɛ ne, ɛnee tɛ edwɛndolɛnli ne a ɛlɛtendɛ a, emomu Gyihova mumua ne ɔ. Gyihova bɔle ye azonvolɛ ɛwɔkɛ bie. (Kenga Edwɛne 91:14-16.) Ɔhanle ye fane dɔɔnwo wɔ ngyehyɛnu zɔhane mɔ anu kɛ ɔbabɔ ye menli nwo bane ɔluakɛ bɛfa bɛ nwo bɛto ɔ nwo zo, bɛze ye duma yɛɛ bɛfɛlɛ ye wɔ asɔneyɛlɛ nu. Yɛbahola yɛalie Gyihova yɛali bɔkɔɔ ɔluakɛ ɔdi ye ɛwɔkɛ zo dahuu. (Dwɔh. 23:14) Ɔmbɔ adalɛ!—Nɔm. 23:19.

6 Gyihova maanle edwɛndolɛnli ne hɛlɛle edwɛkɛ mɔɔ wɔ Edwɛne 91 ne la amaa yɛade nganeɛ kɛ yɛlɛ anwobanebɔlɛ na yɛava yɛ nwo yɛado ɔ nwo zo. (Wlo. 15:4) Yɛdayɛ noko yɛbahola yɛaha edwɛkɛ mɔɔ edwɛndolɛnli ne hanle la bie. Ɔse: “Wɔmɔ a ɛle me ɛvealeka nee me aranemgbole” a. (Edw. 91:2) Noko ndenle boni mɔ a Gyihova balua zo abɔ yɛ nwo bane a?

7 Dɛba ne yɛ mbuluku ne mɔ hilele nu kɛ, Edwɛne 91 le ɛwɔkɛ mɔɔ Gyihova ɛbɔ kɛ ɔbabɔ yɛ nee yɛ agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne anwo bane a. Ɛhye kile kɛ yɛbahola yɛanyia anwodozo kɛ Gyihova bamaa yɛ sa aha mɔɔ yɛhyia nwo amaa debie biala anzɛkye yɛ nee ye agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne la dahuu. Noko asoo yɛ nee ye agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne ala a ɔbabɔ nwolɛ bane ɔ? Kyɛkye. Eza Gyihova ɛbɔ ɛwɔkɛ wɔ Edwɛne 91 ne kɛ ɔbabɔ yɛ ngoane nwo bane yɛɛ ɔbamaa yɛanyia anzodwolɛ wɔ mekɛ bɔbɔ mɔɔ yɛlɛnwu amaneɛ la. Duzu ati a yɛkola yɛka ye zɔ a?
