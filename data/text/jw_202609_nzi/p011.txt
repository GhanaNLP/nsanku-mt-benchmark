(Mat. 24:14) Eza wɔ anwongyelelɛ kpole ne anu edwɛkɛ mɔɔ yɛbaha yɛahile menli la anloa bayɛ se kpalɛ na ɔbamaa bɛakpa kɛ bɛbazonle Gyihova anzɛɛ bɛnrɛzonle ye. Yɛ edwɛkɛhanlɛ gyima ne bayɛ kɛ ‘ngyegyelɛ kpole’ amaa Gyi-hova agbɔvolɛ.* (Yek. 16:21) Ɔnyɛ nwanwane kɛ bɛbava ɛya kɛ mɔɔ Falo vale ɛya wɔ Nyevile Kɔkɔlɛ ne anloa la. Mɔɔ baboa yɛ yeamaa yɛa-gyinla dwazotia mɔɔ la yɛ nyunlu anloa la a le kɛ yɛbayɛ tieyɛ ɛnɛ na yɛayɛ daselɛlilɛ gyima ne ɔnva nwo mɔɔ awie mɔ bava ayɛ yɛ la.

SUKOA KƐZI BELEMGBUNLI GYƐHƆHYAFATE YƐLE TIEYƐ LA
<<<P>>>
10 Baebolo ne eza ka menli mɔɔ yɛle tieyɛ la anwo edwɛkɛ. Kɛ neazo la, maanle nsa bie yɛle adwenle ko kɛ bɛfa konle bara Belemgbu-nli Gyɛhɔhyafate nee Dwudama anwo zo. Gyɛ-hɔhyafate nee ye menli ne amuala ahonle dule kpalɛ na ɛnee ndelebɛbo wɔ nu. (2 Ek. 20:1-3) Noko belemgbunli ne ammaa ɛzulolɛ anvo ɔ nwo zo, ɔyɛle nwolɛ debie. Maa yɛnlea kɛzi mɔɔ Gyɛhɔhyafate yɛle ye bieko la baho-la aboa yɛ yeamaa yɛayɛ tieyɛ la.
<<<P>>>
11 Bɔ kpɔkɛ kɛ ɛbakpondɛ adehilelɛ wɔavi Gyi-hova ɛkɛ na wɔali zo. Mɔɔ ɛzulolɛ hanle Gyɛ-hɔhyafate la “ɔbɔle kpɔkɛ kɛ ɔkpondɛ Gyihova abo zo.” (2 Ek. 20:3) Ɛnee ɔle Gyɛhɔhyafate subane kɛ ɔbakpondɛ adehilelɛ yeavi Gyihova ɛkɛ. (2 Ek. 19:3) Noko wɔ tɛnlabelɛ ɛhye anu ɛdeɛ “ɔbɔle kpɔkɛ” anzɛɛ ɔzile pi kɛ ɔbakpo-ndɛ Gyihova na yeali adehilelɛ biala mɔɔ ɔba-va yeamaa ye ala azo. Akee ɔvile ye ahonle nu ɔyɛle nwolɛ asɔne. (Kenga 2 Edwɛkɛsisilɛ 20:5, 6, 10-12.) Saa yɛgyi ngyegyelɛ nloa a, bɛmaa yɛli ye neazo ne anzi. Yɛbahola yɛabɔ kpɔkɛ kɛ yɛbadie Gyihova, yɛayɛ asɔne na yɛazukoa debie amaa yeahile yɛ mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ la.—Edw. 119:112.
<<<P>>>
12 Kɔ zo sukoa debie. Asɔne mɔɔ Gyɛhɔhya-fate yɛle la maa ɔda ali kɛ ɛnee ɔsukoa de-bie kpalɛ. Ɔnwunle kɛzi Gyihova lile ɛwɔkɛ mɔɔ ɔbɔle Ebileham kɛ ɔbava azɛlɛ ne yea-maa Yizilayɛma la azo la. (Gyn. 12:7) Edwɛkɛ
