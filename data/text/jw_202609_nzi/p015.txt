barazɔ . . . Dwuunli tɛladeɛ nu ɛsesebɛ na bɛaha kɛ: “ ‘Yɛkpondɛ kɛ yɛ nee bɛ kɔ, ɔluakɛ yɛde kɛ Nyamenle nee bɛ lua.’ ” ” (Zɛk. 8:23) Ngapezo ɛhye ɛlɛba nu ɛnɛ. Maa yɛnlea kɛzi yɛ nuhua ko biala bahola aboa amaa ngapezo ɛhye ara nu la.
<<<P>>>
KƐZI YƐLƐKA EDWƐKPA NE WƆ ANEƐ NGAKYILE NU LA
<<<P>>>
3 “Dwuunli” ne mɔɔ bɛha ɔ nwo edwɛkɛ wɔ Zɛkalaya ngapezo ne anu la gyi ɛkɛ ne maa bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛkpokpa bɛ la. Eza bɛfɛlɛ bɛ “Nyamenle Yizilayɛ ne.” (Gal. 6:16) Bɛkpa menli ɛhye mɔ kɛ “bɛbahɔ anwuma.” (Hib. 3:1) “Mrenyia bulu” ne gyi ɛkɛ ne maa bɛlɛvoma mɔɔ bɛlɛ anyelazo kɛ bɛbadɛnla azɛlɛ ye azo dahuu wɔ Paladaese la. Bɛvi maanle ngakyile nu yɛɛ bɛka aneɛ ngakyile.
<<<P>>>
4 Mɔɔ Gyisɛse kpale “akɛlɛ nɔhavo nee badwema ne” wɔ 1919 la, mediema zɔhane mɔ nwunle kɛ bɛ gyima ne bie a le kɛ bɛbahile edwɛkpa ne abo bɛahɔ aneɛ ngakyile nu. (Mat. 24:45) Ɔwɔ nu kɛ ɛnee Baebolo Sukoavoma ne ɛhile “Photo-Drama of Creation” ne abo ɛhɔ aneɛ ngakyile nu ɛdeɛ, noko akɛlɛ nɔhavo ne nwunle kɛ tɛ vidio zɔhane ala a ɔwɔ kɛ bɛkile ɔ bo a. Kɛ neazo la, bɛhilele The Harp of God buluku ne mɔɔ bɛyele ye wɔ 1921 la abo bɛhɔle aneɛ 36 anu. Wɔ 1935, bɛdele menli mɔɔ bayɛ “menli ekpunli kpole” ne la abo. Ɛhye ati akɛlɛ nɔhavo ne nwunle kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛka edwɛkɛ ne bɛkile menli mɔɔ wɔ maanle ngakyile nu na bɛka aneɛ ngakyile la. Yemɔti mediema mɔɔ ɛnee bɛkpokpa bɛ wɔ zɔhane mekɛ ne la yɛle edwɛkɛhanlɛ gyima ne anwo ngyehyɛleɛ kpalɛ amaa bɛabɔ edwɛkpa ne nolo wɔ azɛlɛ ye azo amuala amaa Zɛkalaya ngapezo ne ara nu bɔkɔɔ.
<<<P>>>
“WƆ MENLI NE BAVI BƐ ƐHULOLƐ NU ADU BƐ NWO AMAA”
<<<P>>>
5 Mekɛ mɔɔ Nyamenle menli ɛlɛbɔ edwɛkpa ne nolo wɔ ewiade ɛleka dɔɔnwo la, bɛnwunle menli dɔɔnwo mɔɔ ka aneɛ ngakyile mɔɔ bɛkulo kɛ bɛsonle Gyihova la. Na kɛ mɔɔ Ayezaya hanle la, bɛvɛlɛle menli ɛhye mɔ kɛ bɛrɛla Gyihova sua nu amaa bɛahilehile bɛ ye ndenle ne mɔ. (Kenga Ayezaya 2:2-4.) Amaa Nyamenle azonvolɛ aboa menli ɛhye mɔ bɛamaa bɛazukoa Gyihova anwo debie na bɛanlea bɛ la, ‘bɛfi bɛ ɛhulolɛ nu bɛtu bɛ nwo bɛmaa bɔkɔɔ’ bɛbɔ edwɛkpa ne nolo. Eza bɛkile yɛ mbuluku nee vidio ne mɔ abo bɛkɔ aneɛ ngakyile nu, bɛpenlete mbuluku na bɛfa bɛkɔmaa bɛ wɔ ewiade amuala. (Edw. 68:11; 110:3) Bieko, akɛlɛ nɔhavo ne ɛmaa mediema bie mɔ ɛyɛ jw.org nee JW Library® ne wɔ aneɛ dɔɔnwo anu. Ɛhye boa menli dɔɔnwo mɔɔ wɔ ewiade mɔɔ ka aneɛ ngakyile la maa bɛsukoa Baebolo ne anu nɔhalɛ ne.
<<<P>>>
6 Menli mɔɔ vi maanle ngakyile nu na bɛka aneɛ ngakyile la nyia sunsum nu aleɛ mɔɔ akɛlɛ nɔhavo nee badwema ne fa maa yɛ la azo nvasoɛ. Adenle ko mɔɔ bɛdua zo bɛkile ɛhye anwo anyezɔlɛ la a le kɛ bɛfi bɛ ahonle nu bɛye ndoboa bɛboa ewiade amuala edwɛkɛhanlɛ gyima ne. Ɛhye ɛboa ɛmaa bɛyɛ yɛ mbuluku nee vidio ne mɔ wɔ aneɛ mɔɔ bo 1,100 anu la. Ɔti ɛvolɛ biala, bɛsɔne menli apenle dɔɔnwo.
