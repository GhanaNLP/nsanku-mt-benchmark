10 Ɔwɔ kɛ bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛkulo kɛ bɛyɛ mɔɔ sɔ Gyihova anye la kyi mɔɔ ɔkyi la. (Edw. 97:10) Ɛhye ati yɛbahola yɛabiza yɛ nwo kɛ: ‘Asoo mete nganeɛ kɛ Gyihova ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nwo ngyinlazo ne mɔ ɛmmaa mengola me-nyɛ mɔɔ mekulo la ɔ? Asoo ninyɛne mɔɔ mefa medielie me nye la kile kɛ mekulo ni-nyɛne mɔɔ Gyihova kyi la ɔ? Asoo me kunlu a anrɛɛ Gyihova ɛhakyi ye ngyinlazo ne mɔ wɔ adenle bie azo yemaa ɔ nee mɔɔ mekulo kɛ meyɛ la ɛyɛ ko ɔ?’

11 Na saa ɛnyia ɛhulolɛ kɛ ɛyɛ debie mɔɔ Gyihova kyi la ɛ? Mmanyia adwenle kɛ ɛnrɛhola ɛnrɛli Gyihova ngyinlazo ne mɔ azo ɛlɛ. Kakye kɛ adwenle mɔɔ ɔbahola yea-maa yɛayɛ ɛtane la ba alesama mɔɔ ɛnli mu-nli la kɔsɔɔti ati anu. (Edw. 51:5; 103:14) Noko yɛbahola yɛakpa kɛ saa adwenle zɛ-hae bie ba yɛ ti anu a, yɛnrɛsosɔ zo, kile kɛ yɛnrɛyɛ “mɔɔ yɛ sonlabaka ne kulo la.” (Ɛfɛ. 2:3) Eza ɔnle kɛ yɛdwenledwenle atia-kunluwɔzo ɛtane nwo. Saa atiakunluwɔzo ɛtane ba ɛ ti nu a, ye ɛ nye nee wɔ adwenle fi ninyɛne mɔɔ Gyihova kyi la azo ndɛndɛ. (Gyn. 39:9, 12) Akee suzu kɛzi zɔhane ɛta-ne ne bahola azɛkye ɛ nee Gyihova yɛɛ awie mɔ avinli la anwo. Bɔ kpɔkɛ kɛ ɛnrɛmaa de-bie zɛhae ɛnrɛzi ɛlɛ. (Ɛfɛ. 5:5) Ɔlua Gyiho-va moalɛ zo ɛbahola wɔahyi ɛtane.

NEA BOƐ WƆ YINTANƐTE NE AZO

12 Yintanɛte ne a boa yɛ nuhua dɔɔnwo maa yɛkola yɛyɛ ninyɛne ngakyile nee ni-nyɛne mɔɔ fale yɛ ɛzonlenlɛ nwo la a. Noko akee ninyɛne dɔɔnwo mɔɔ yɛnwu ye wɔ yi-ntanɛte ne azo la da ewiade sunsum ne ali. Kɛ neazo la, nlanwonvoninli ɛbu zo na ɔnyɛ se kɛ ngakula nee mgbanyinli amuala bahɔ ɛkɛ ahɔnlea bie. Menli dɔɔnwo, titili mgbavolɛ nee mbɛlɛra fa nvoninli, vidio yɛɛ mɛsegyi mɔɔ fale nla nwo la sɛnde bɛ gɔnwo mɔ. Eza menli ka adalɛ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ kola bɛlɛbɛla awie mɔ la wɔ yintanɛte ne azo. Menli dɔɔnwo noko ta fa mɔɔ ɛlɛkɔ zo wɔ bɛ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nu la gua sohyia media ne azo. Nvoninli, edwɛkɛ nee vidio nzinre-nzinra ɛhye mɔ kola maa yɛte nganeɛ kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛfa mekɛ dɔɔnwo yɛdielie yɛ nye na yɛtudu adenle saa bɔbɔ ɔnrɛmaa yɛnrɛ-nyia mekɛ yɛnrɛzonle Gyihova a.—Hib. 10:24, 25.

13 Ɔwɔ kɛ yɛnea boɛ wɔ ninyɛne mɔɔ wɔ yintanɛte ne azo mɔɔ bahola azɛkye yɛ mekɛ nee yɛ adwenle la anwo. Amaa yɛaho-la yɛayɛ zɔ la ɔwɔ kɛ yɛbiza yɛ nwo kɛ: ‘Mekɛ tendenle nyɛ a mefa meduolua yintanɛte ne azo menɛɛnlea ninyɛne a?’ (Ɛfɛ. 5:15, 16) ‘Ninyɛne boni mɔ a meta menea a? Saa me-nwu debie mɔɔ ɛnle kpalɛ la a, asoo meye mefi ɛkɛ ne ndɛndɛ?’ (Edw. 119:37) ‘Asoo medie debie biala mɔɔ menwu ye wɔ yinta-nɛte ne azo la medi ɛkɛ ne ala anzɛɛ menea kɛ ɔle nɔhalɛ?’—Mrɛ. 14:15.
