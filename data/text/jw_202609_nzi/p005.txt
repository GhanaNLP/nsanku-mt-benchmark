14 Ɔdua ye sunsum nwuanzanwuanza ne azo. Eza Gyihova dua ye sunsum ne azo boa yɛ maa yɛko yɛtia yɛ sinlidɔlɛ ne mɔ. Ɛhye ati saa yɛkɔ zo yɛsɛlɛ ye kɛ ɔva sunsum nwuanza-nwuanza ne ɔmaa yɛ a, ɔbayɛ ye zɔ. (Luku 11:9-13) Kɛzi sunsum nwuanzanwuanza ne bɔ yɛ nwo bane ɛ? Ɛzoanvolɛ Pɔɔlo hanle kɛ: “Bɛhɔ zo bɛmaa sunsum ne ɛhile bɛ adenle na bɛnrɛli nwonane nu atiakunluwɔzo nzi ɛlɛ.” (Gal. 5:16) Ngyɛnu ɛhye ɛlɛka awie mɔɔ ɔyɛ biala mɔɔ ɔbahola la kɛ ɔbadie Gyihova na yeava ye ngyinlazo ne mɔ yeabɔ ye ɛbɛla la anwo edwɛkɛ. Yɛyɛ ye zɔ a, Nyamenle bava ye sunsum ne aboa yɛ yeamaa yɛadwenle na yɛayɛ yɛ ninyɛne nrɛlɛbɛ nu. Ɔti mekɛ bia-la mɔɔ yɛbanyia atiakunluwɔzo ɛtane la, ye sunsum ne bahola aboa yɛ yeamaa yɛaye yɛ adwenle yɛavi zo ndɛndɛ. Kakye kɛ Gyihova sunsum ne ɛnye atiakunluwɔzo ɛtane ɛnvi yɛ nu. Noko ɔbahola yeaboa yɛ yeamaa yɛamia kpɔkɛ mɔɔ yɛbɔ kɛ yɛbayɛ mɔɔ tenrɛ la anu. Na yemɔ ɛnrɛmaa yɛ nee Gyihova avinli ɛnrɛ-zɛkye.
