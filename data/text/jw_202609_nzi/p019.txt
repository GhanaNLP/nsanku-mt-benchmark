16 Baebolo ne ka kɛ ɔnrɛhyɛ konle badɔ “arelemgbunli mɔɔ wɔ azɛlɛ ye azo amuala la” nee Gyihova avinli. (Yek. 16:14) Mekɛ zɔhane, bɛbalie bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛze Gyihova na bɛdi ye duma ne eni la. (Edw. 9:10; kenga Dwɔn 17:3.) Noko ɔbamaa bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛannwu ye kɛ ɔle bɛ Ze nee bɛ Nyamenle la anwu ye wɔ adenle gyɛne zo. Gyihova ɛha kɛ: “Mebawula me nwo anyunlunyia na meahile me nwuanzanwuanzayɛlɛ ne na meamaa bɛanwu me wɔ maanle maanle dɔɔnwo anyunlu; na bɛbanwu kɛ medame a mele Gyihova a.” (Yiz. 38:23) Mekɛ zɔhane Gyihova menli anye balie kɛ bɛnwunle ye kɛ bɛ Gɔnwo na tɛ bɛ kpɔvolɛ la!—Mal. 3:16.

17 Ɔyɛ yɛ fɛ kɛ yɛnyia nwolɛ adenle yɛlɛboa ekyi yɛamaa ngapezo mɔɔ wɔ Zɛkalaya 8:23 la ara nu la. Eza yɛnyia nwolɛ adenle kɛ yɛ nee yɛ mediema yɛɛ anwumabɔvolɛ ne mɔ ɛlɛbɔ nu aha edwɛkpa ne ahile menli mɔɔ ka aneɛ ngakyile la. Gyihova maa yɛ sa ka debie biala mɔɔ yɛhyia nwo amaa yɛaha edwɛkpa ne yɛahile maanle nee mbusua nee aneɛ nee menli kɔsɔɔti. Maa yɛva ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ yɛli gyima kpalɛ yɛboa menli dodo biala mɔɔ yɛbahola la yɛmaa bɛbikye Gyihova, yɛ Nyamenle ne mɔɔ ɔnnea sonla nyunlu, ɔse anwunvɔne na ɔsi abotane la. Ɔle enililɛ kpole kɛ yɛbayɛ gyima ɛhye bie kɔkpula kɛ bɛbade Gyihova duma nwo wɔ ewiade amuala la.—Luku 11:2.
