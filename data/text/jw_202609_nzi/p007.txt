ahonle tu. Duzu ati ɔ? Yɛdie yɛdi kɛ Gyihova le yɛ ɛvealeka nee yɛ aranemgbole. Ɔti yɛlɛ anwodozo bɔkɔɔ kɛ Gyisɛse nee ye anwuma ɛlɔne ne bazɛkye Nyamenle agbɔvolɛ kɔsɔɔti wɔ Amagɛdɔn konle ne anu mɔɔ ko bɔbɔ ɛnrɛha a. (Gyɛ. 25:33; Zɛk. 14:12) Noko ye agbɔvolɛ ɛnrɛhola ɛnrɛhu Nyamenle mboane ne mɔɔ sonle bolɛ maa ye la anu ko bɔbɔ.—Luku 21:28; 2 Pita 2:9.

20 Akee edwɛndolɛnli ne edwɛkɛ ne bara nu bɔkɔɔ wɔ Amagɛdɔn konle ne anu. Ɔse: “Apenle badu agua wɔ foa ko yɛɛ apenle bulu badu agua ɛ sa fema zo, noko wɔmɔ ɔnrɛbikye wɔ. Ɛ nye ala a ɛbava wɔanlea a na wɔanwu amumuyɛma anzohwenlɛ ne. Ɔluakɛ wɔha kɛ: ‘Gyihova le me ɛvealeka,’ wɔva Anwuma Anwumanli ne wɔyɛ wɔ tɛnlabelɛ.” (Edw. 91:7-9) Saa Amagɛdɔn ne bɔ ɔ bo a, yɛbahola yɛamaa yɛ nye ala kɛ Gyihova babɔ ye menli ne kɔsɔɔti nee yɛ nuhua ko biala ngoane nwo bane, ɔbamaa yɛazie yɛ nwo dii yɛɛ ɔbabɔ yɛ nee ye agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne anwo bane. Kɛmɔ wɔha kɛ “Gyihova le me ɛvealeka” la ati, yebɔ ɛwɔkɛ kɛ ngyegyelɛ ne ‘ɛnrɛbikye wɔ.’ Ɔti ɔnle kɛ yɛmaa mɔɔ bazi kenle bie la maa yɛ ahonle tu, ɔluakɛ yɛlɛ anwodozo bɔkɔɔ kɛ Gyihova babɔ yɛ nwo bane.
