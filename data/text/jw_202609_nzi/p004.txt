Kɛmɔ ɛnee Seetan ze kɛ Gyihova ɛbɔ ɛwɔkɛ wɔ Edwɛne 91:11, 12 kɛ ɔbabɔ ye ngoane nwo bane la ati, ɔhanle ɔhilele Gyisɛse kɛ ɔvi ɛzonlenlɛsua ne ati zo ɔvuandi ɔ nwo ɔde aze ɔnlea saa anwumabɔvolɛma babɔ ɔ nwo bane a. 9 Kɛzi Gyisɛse buale Seetan la maa yɛnwu kɛ Edwɛne 91 kile kɛ Gyihova babɔ ye menli nwo bane wɔ adenle biala azo. Ɔbabɔ ɔ nee bɛ agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne nee bɛ ngoane nwo bane na yeamaa bɛanyia anzodwolɛ wɔ ngyegyelɛ mekɛ nu. Wɔ tete ne, Gyisɛse nwunle kɛ ɔ Ze luale anwumabɔvolɛma anwo zo bɔle Ye menli ngoane nwo bane wɔ mekɛ ngakyile nu. (2 Arl. 6:15-17; 19:34, 35; Dan. 3:19, 20, 26-28; 6:16-22) Noko ɛnee Gyisɛse ze kɛ saa ɔyɛ mgbane mgbane na ɔnyia adwenle kɛ anwumabɔvolɛma babɔ ɔ nwo bane amaa yeamboda bɔbɔ a, ɛnee ɔlɛsɔ ɔ Ze yeanlea. Ɔti mɔɔ Seetan hanle kɛ ɛwɔkɛ mɔɔ wɔ Edwɛne 91 la kile kɛ Gyihova babɔ ye ngoane nwo bane la ɛnee ɔle nɔhalɛ. Noko mɔɔ ɔhanle kɛ Gyisɛse ɛvuandi ɔ nwo ɛde aze la ɛnee ɔkulo kɛ ɔsɔ ɔ Ze ɔnea.*

KƐZI GYIHOVA BƆ YƐ NWO BANE ƐNƐ Ɛ?
<<<P>>>
11 Gyihova bɔ yɛ nwo bane wɔ sunsum nu. Ɔluakɛ ɔze kɛ yɛle ɛtanevolɛma na yɛnrɛhola yɛnrɛyɛ mɔɔ ɔkulo la dahuu. Ɔti ɔboa yɛ ɔmaa yɛko yɛtia debie biala mɔɔ bahola azɛkye yɛ nee ye agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne la. (Wlo. 6:16; 7:21-23) Maa yɛzuzu ndenle ngakyile mɔɔ ɔdua zo ɔboa yɛ wɔ yɛ konle ɛhye anu la bie mɔ anwo.
<<<P>>>
12 Ɔdua ye Edwɛkɛ ne azo. Baebolo ne ɛmbɔ yɛ kɔkɔ kɛ yɛhwe yɛ nwo yɛvi atiakunluwɔzo ɛtane nwo ala, emomu eza ɔmaa yɛnwu kɛzi yɛbali zolɛ konim la. Maa yɛva Gyisɛse edwɛkɛ mɔɔ wɔ Mateyu 5:28, 29 la kɛ neazo. (Kenga.) Ɛnee Gyisɛse ɛlɛka nrenyia mɔɔ “kɔ zo nea raalɛ na ɔnyia ɔ nwo ɛhulolɛ la” anwo edwɛkɛ. Ɔhanle kɛ, nrenyia zɛhae “ɛzɛkye agyalɛ wɔ ye ahonle nu dɛbadɛba.” Duzu a yɛsukoa yɛfi nu a?
<<<P>>>
13 Saa yɛkɔ zo yɛnea nlanwonvoninli a ɔbamaa yɛanyia nla nwo atiakunluwɔzo wɔ yɛ ahonle nu. Akee saa atiakunluwɔzo ne yɛ kpole na nzinlii yɛyia ɛbɛlatane nwo sɔnea bie a, ɔbamaa yɛabɔ adwɔmane ɛbɛla. (Gye. 1:14, 15) Amaa yɛahola yɛakpo atiakunluwɔzo ɛtane la ɔwɔ kɛ yɛfa Gyisɛse folɛdulɛ ɛhye yɛyɛ gyima ndɛndɛ. Ɔhanle kɛ, “saa ɛ nye fema ɛlɛmaa wɔayɛ ɛtane a, tu ye to ye lɔ.” (Mat. 5:29) Yemɔti ɔnle kɛ yɛnea nvoninli biala mɔɔ twehwe yɛ adwenle kɔ nla zo la. (Kɔl. 3:5) Saa yɛyɛ zɔ a ɔbabɔ yɛ nee Gyihova agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne anwo bane.
