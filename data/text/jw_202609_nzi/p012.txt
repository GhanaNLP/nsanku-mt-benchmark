Asafo nu mgbanyima bɔ mɔdenle kɛ bɛbadie Gyihova, ahyehyɛdeɛ ne yɛɛ bɛ gɔnwo mɔ mgbanyima
(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 16)
<<<P>>>
mɔɔ Gyɛhɔhyafate hanle nee asɔne mɔɔ Sɔlɔmɔn yɛle wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛyila ɛzonlenlɛsua ne azo la zɔho bɛ nwo. (2 Ek. 6:28-30; 20:5-9) Ninyɛne mɔɔ Gyɛhɔhyafate zukoale la maanle ɔnyianle Gyihova anu anwodozo kpole na ɔbɔle kpɔkɛ kɛ ɔbadie Ye. Amaa yɛdayɛ noko yɛamia kpɔkɛ mɔɔ yɛbɔ la anu la, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛbuke ‘ɛleka mɔɔ yɛfa yɛ ninyɛne yɛsie la’ anu, kile kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛsukoa nɔhalɛ ne anwo ninyɛne fofolɛ yɛboka mɔɔ yɛze ye dɛba la anwo. (Mat. 13:52) Saa yɛyɛ zɔ a ɔbamaa yɛadie Gyihova dahuu wɔ tɛnlabelɛ mɔɔ anu yɛ se bɔbɔ la anu.
<<<P>>>
13 Sukoa kɛ ɛbapɛlɛ ɛ nwo aze. Eza Gyɛhɔhyafate asɔneyɛlɛ ne kile kɛ ɛnee ɔbɛlɛ ɔ nwo aze. Kɛmɔ ɔle belemgbunli la ati, anrɛɛ ɔbahola yeava ɔ nwo yeado ɔ nwo zo na yeade nganeɛ kɛ ɔbahola yeali tɛnlabelɛ ne anwo gyima. Noko mekɛ mɔɔ ɔyɛ asɔne wɔ menli ne anyunlu la, ɔbɛlɛle ɔ nwo aze ɔliele ɔdole nu kɛ Dwudama hyia Gyihova moalɛ. (2 Ek. 20:12) Kɛmɔ ɛnee ɔbɛlɛ ɔ nwo aze la ati yeammaa yeanyɛ se kɛ ɔbali Nyamenle adehilelɛ zo. Zɔhane ala a saa yɛbɛlɛ yɛ nwo aze a ɔbamaa yɛanyia ahonle mɔɔ yɛ tieyɛ wɔ tɛnlabelɛ biala anu a.—Fel. 2:5, 8.

14 Suzu kɛzi wɔ nyɛleɛ baha awie mɔ la anwo. Gyihova luale ngapezonli Gyeheezayɛle anwo zo buale Gyɛhɔhyafate asɔneyɛlɛ ne. Ɔmaanle ɔnwunle ye wienyi kɛ ɔngyia kɛ menli ne ko. Emomu ɔhanle ɔhilele bɛ kɛ: “Bɛgyinla bɛ bo zo ne, bɛmmatu bɛ bo, na bɛnlea kɛzi Gyihova balie bɛ ngoane la.” Baebolo ne hanle kɛ akee ‘Gyɛhɔhyafate hotole na ɔbutule ɔ nyunlu aze, na menli ne amuala butubutule bɛ nyunlu aze na bɛzonlenle Gyihova.’ Mɔɔ aleɛ hyenle mɔɔ menli ne ɛlɛkɔ la, belemgbunli ne maanle bɛ anwosesebɛ kɛ “bɛnyia Gyihova” nee “ye ngapezoma ne mɔ anu diedi.”—2 Ek. 20:14-20.
<<<P>>>
15 Gyɛhɔhyafate kpale menli maanle ‘bɛlile menli mɔɔ ɛziezie bɛ nwo ɛmaa konle ne la anyunlu’ kɛ bɛdo ayɛlɛyelɛ edwɛne. Ɛhye kile kɛ ɛnee Gyɛhɔhyafate lɛ diedi kpole. (2 Ek. 20:21) Mɔɔ Gyɛhɔhyafate nee “menli ne vale adwenle wiele la” yɛɛ ɔzile kpɔkɛ zɔhane a. Ɔyɛle neazo kɛnlɛma ɔmaanle ye maanlema ne na bɛzukoale kɛ bɛbava bɛ nwo bɛado Gyihova anwo zo na bɛayɛ tieyɛ. Gyihova yilale bɛ kpɔkɛzilɛ ne azo na ɔmaanle bɛ agbɔvolɛ honle tiale bɛ nwo.—2 Ek. 20:22-24.
