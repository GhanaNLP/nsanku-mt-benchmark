MAA WƆ ƐDENDƐLƐ ƐYƐ FƐ
<<<P>>>
6 Baebolo ne ka kɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ li awieleɛ la anu, menli bayɛ “adubɛnwo, anwomemaazo” yɛɛ ‘bɛ nee bɛ ɛnrɛyɛ ngyehyɛleɛ.’ (2 Tim. 3:1-3) Menli da subane zɔhane mɔ bie ali wɔ bɛ ɛdendɛlɛ nu ɛnɛ. Kɛ neazo la, ɔyɛ a yɛte wɔ amaneɛbɔlɛ bie mɔ anu kɛ menli bie mɔ bɔ ɔ bo kɛ bɛdi butule na nzinlii akee bɛko. Eza ɛkɔ awuke dɔɔnwo anu a ɛte kɛ bɛlɛbɔ bɛ nwo aholoba. Zɔhane ala a ɛte kɛ mgbanyinli nee ngakula ɛlɛbɔ nla nwo ngoayɛ a. (Ɛfɛ. 5:3, 4) Saa awie tendɛ wɔ adenle zɛhae azo a, ɔkile kɛ ɔlɛmaa ewiade sunsum ne ahile ye adenle.
<<<P>>>
7 Kɛmɔ yɛle Kilisienema la ati, saa yɛ nee awie mɔ ɛlɛtendɛ anzɛɛ yɛlɛsende mɛsegyi a, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ neazo kpalɛ yɛmaa bɛ. (Gye. 1:26, 27) Amaa yɛanwu kɛ yɛ ɛdendɛlɛ yɛ fɛ anzɛɛ ɔnyɛ fɛ la, yɛbahola yɛabiza yɛ nwo kɛ: ‘Saa mefa ɛya a meka mgbole ɔ, titili saa ahenle mɔɔ me nee ye ɛlɛtendɛ la ɛnze kɛ mele Gyihova Dasevolɛ a? Me nee me mediema diedima ɛlɛbɔ adawu a asoo meka ɛtane, metendɛ metia bɛ anzɛɛ megolo bɛ nwo ɔ? Saa me nee me busuanli bie ɛlɛtendɛ a asoo me edwɛkɛ wowɔ ɔ? Boni a bɛbaha wɔ me ɛdendɛlɛ nwo a?’
<<<P>>>
8 Saa yɛnwu kɛ yɛ ɛdendɛlɛ ɛnyɛ fɛ a, duzu a ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ a? Tɛ edwɛkɛ agbɔkɛ mɔɔ yɛfa yɛdi gyima la ala a ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkakyi a. Kakye kɛ Gyisɛse hanle kɛ: “Debie biala mɔɔ vi sonla nloa anu finde la vi ye ahonle nu, na ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ a gua sonla nwo evinli a.” (Mat. 15:18) Ɛnee Gyisɛse ɛlɛkile kɛ yɛ ɛdendɛlɛ da mɔɔ wɔ yɛ ahonle nu la ali. Ɛhye ati a Baebolo ne ka kɛ “aholoba” nee ɛya, ɛya kpole yɛɛ ɛkpɔlɛ lua adenle ko azo la. (Ɛfɛ. 4:31) Saa ɛnwu kɛ ɔhyia kɛ ɛyɛ nzenzaleɛ a, sɛlɛ Gyihova kɛ ɔboa wɔ ɔmaa ɛwula “subane fofolɛ ne.” (Kenga Kɔlɔsaema 3:8-10.) Eza ɛbahola wɔayɛ neɛnleanu wɔ yɛ mbuluku ne mɔ anu wɔakpondɛ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ ɔbaboa wɔ la. Bɔ kpɔkɛ kyengye kɛ ɛnrɛmaa ewiade sunsum ne ɛnrɛzɛkye wɔ subane nee wɔ ɛdendɛlɛ. —Ɛfɛ. 4:29.

TWE Ɛ NWO FI ƐBƐLATANE NWO
<<<P>>>
9 Eza yɛnwu ye ɛnɛ kɛ menli dɔɔnwo ɛmbu Gyihova ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nwo ngyinlazo ne mɔ fee. Menli dɔɔnwo ɛnnwu nvonleɛ wɔ ɛkolɛyelɛ, nrenyia mɔɔ fa ɔ gɔnwo nrenyia anzɛɛ raalɛ mɔɔ fa ɔ gɔnwo raalɛ la anwo. Eza bɛdie bɛto nu kɛ menli mɔɔ bɛtɛgyale la bahola ava bɛ nwo awula nla nu. (Aye. 5:20) Saa yɛannea boɛ a, ngyikyingyiki yɛbanyia adwenle zɔhane bie na yɛnrɛhyi mɔɔ le ɛtane la bieko. (2 Kɔl. 11:3) Wɔannea bɔbɔ a yɛbadwenle kɛ Baebolo ngyinlazo ɛkpa nwo na mɔɔ ewiade ne ka la a ɛdwu zo a. Noko ɔnle nɔhalɛ fee! Gyihova ndenle le kpalɛ maa yɛ dahuu. Bɛkpogya bɛtɛla sonla ndenle yɛɛ bɛmkpa nwo ɛlɛ.—Aye. 48:17, 18; 55:9.
