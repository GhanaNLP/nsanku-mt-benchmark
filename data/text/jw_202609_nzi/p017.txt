10 Maa yɛnlea kɛzi yɛbahola yɛalua yɛ mbuluku ne mɔ nee JW Library ne azo yɛaha edwɛkpa ne yɛahile awie mɔ wɔ aneɛ gyɛne nu la. Yɛva Robert mɔɔ ka Nrelenza la kɛ neazo. Mekɛ bie mɔɔ ɔlɛdi daselɛ wɔ habɔ bie la, ɔyiale nrenyia bie mɔɔ vi Türkiye mɔɔ yɛ gyima wɔ meli nu la. Ɛnee nrenyia ne te Nrelenza ekyi noko ɔliele Edwɛkpa Mɔɔ Vi Nyamenle Ɛkɛ! brohyua ne ko wɔ Turkish aneɛ nu. Kɛmɔ Robert ze brohyua ne anu edwɛkɛ ne kpalɛ la ati, ɔhilele nrenyia ne ɛleka fɔɔnwo bie mɔ kɛ ɔgenga wɔ ye aneɛ ne anu. Akee nrenyia ne bizale ye mɔɔ ɛlɛkɔ zo wɔ ewiade ye la anwo kpuya dɔɔnwo na Robert luale JW Library app ne azo vale Baebolo ne buale ye wɔ Turkish aneɛ nu. Ɔzile nrenyia ne anwo ɔti ɔhanle kɛ: “Eeii, ɛbaha kɛ ɛte me aneɛ ne!” Robert hanle kɛ: “Yɛbɔle adawu asɛɛ dɔnehwele ko. Wɔ yɛ adawubɔlɛ ne anu, menwunle kɛ ɛnee ɛbulɛ mɔɔ nrenyia ne lɛ maa Baebolo ne nee ndelebɛbo mɔɔ ɔlɛ la ɛyɛ kpole.” Ɔle nwolɛ adenle kpole mɔɔ yɛnyia kɛ yɛbaboa ahonlekpalɛma yeamaa bɛazukoa Nyamenle anwo debie wɔ bɛdabɛ mumua ne bɛ aneɛ nu!

Raalɛ Bie Nyianle Anyelazo
<<<P>>>
Ɛnee asetɛnla nu yɛ se maa raalɛ bie mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Youa la wɔ ye sua zo, ɔti ɔdule ɔhɔle United States. Ɛnee ɔnde Nrelenza, Hmong aneɛ ne ala a ɔka a. Eza ɛnee ɔ hu ɛmmaa ɔngenga buluku biala nee Baebolo ne bɔbɔ. Ɔhanle kɛ: “Anzodwolɛ nee anyelielɛ ɛdeɛ, meannyia bie ɛlɛ.” Kenle ko bie, adiema raalɛ bie mɔɔ ɔka Nrelenza la vale trate ne mɔɔ ɔ ti ne se Azedɛnlalɛ wɔ Anzondwolɛ Ewiade Fofolɛ Nu la bie maanle ye wɔ Hmong aneɛ nu. Ɛnee Youa ɛtɛtele Nyamenle ewɔkɛ ne mɔ mɔɔ bɛha nwolɛ edwɛkɛ wɔ trate ne anu la anwo edwɛkɛ ɛlɛ. Edwɛkɛ ne yɛle ye fɛ na ɔmaanle ɔnyianle anyelazo kɛ ninyɛne bayɛ boɛ kenle bie. Ɔvale trate ne ɔveale ye kɛndɛne bie anu ɛvolɛ dɔɔnwo. Ɛnee ɔta ɔfea ɔ nwo ɔye ɔkenga. Nzinlii, Youa amra mraalɛ ne mɔ ko rale nɔhalɛ ne anu na ɔvale ye ɔhɔle debiezukoalɛ mɔɔ bɛyɛ ye wɔ Hmong aneɛ nu la bie. Akee bɛ nee ye zukoale Baebolo ne na ngyikyingyiki ɔ nee menli mɔɔ bɛlɛ anyelazo ko ne ala mɔɔ trate kɛnlɛma zɔhane maanle ɔnyianle bie la bɔle nu zonlenle Gyihova.
