Ɛtane ɛmaa yeyɛ kɛ kuma la yɛ nee Nyamenle avinli; ɛsɔfolilɛ ne le kɛ belegye
(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 3)
<<<P>>>
ma 5:1.) Enee Gyihova kulo kɛ Yizilayɛma bikye ye. Ɛhye ati a Gyihova kpale ɛsɔfo kpanyinli ne na ɔmaanle ye gyima kɛ ɔboa menli ne wɔ bɛ ɛzonlenlɛ nu la a. Ɛsɔfo ne mɔ boale Nyamenle azonvolɛ maanle bɛbikyele ye. Bɛyɛle kɛ belegye mɔɔ alesama bava zo apɛ kuma ne ahɔ Nyamenle anwo ɛkɛ a.
<<<P>>>
4 Kɛzi ɛsɔfo ne mɔ boale Yizilayɛma maanle bɛbikyele Nyamenle na bɛzonlenle ye wɔ adenle mɔɔ ɔdie ɔto nu la azo ɛ? Wɔ ndenle titili nwiɔ azo. Mɔɔ limoa, bɛhilehilele menli ne Nyamenle anwo debie. Kɛzi bɛyɛle ye ɛ? Malakae 2:7 hanle kɛ: “Ɔwɔ kɛ ɛsɔfo kilehile menli ne Nyamenle anwo edwɛkɛ, na ɔwɔ kɛ menli kola biza ye mɛla ne anwo edwɛkɛ, ɔluakɛ ɔle ɛlɔne Gyihova ɛbɔfoɔ.” Yemɔti ɛnee ɛsɔfo ne mɔ gyima a le kɛ bɛbahile Mosisi Mɛla ne anu bɛahile Yizilayɛma na bɛaboa bɛ bɛamaa bɛade ɔ bo na bɛali zo. Kɛ neazo la, wɔ Nihɛmaya mekɛ zo, Livaema 13 boale ɛsɔfo Ɛzela maanle ɔhilehilele Mɛla ne anu ɔhilele menli ne na bɛboale bɛ bɛmaanle bɛdele mɔɔ bɛlɛkenga la abo. Yemɔ amaa yeanyɛ se kɛ bɛbava Nyamenle mɛla ne mɔ bɛali gyima na bɛabikye Nyamenle.—Nih. 8:2, 3, 6-8.
<<<P>>>
5 Mɔɔ tɔ zo nwiɔ, bɛgyinlanle menli ne agyakɛ anu bɛzɛlɛle Nyamenle na bɛbɔle afɔle. Enee Yizilayɛma ze kɛ bɛyɛ ɛtane kɛnle ko biala yɛɛ bɛtɔ sinli. Enee bɛze kɛ ɔhyia kɛ bɛkyikyi bɛ ɛtane ne mɔ. (Edw. 51:9) Ɛhye ati a Gyihova kpale ɛsɔfoma kɛ bɛgyinla bɛ gyakɛ anu bɛbɔ afɔle ngakyile la. Nea kɛzi ɛhye maanle Yizilayɛma mɔɔ bɛnlunle bɛ nwo la anye liele kɛ bɛvale bɛ ɛtane bɛhyɛle bɛ la! Ɛsɔfolilɛ ngyehyɛleɛ ne maa yɛnwu kɛ Gyihova lɛ ɛlɔlɛ, ahunluyelɛyelalɛ yɛɛ ɔdwenle ye menli ne boɛyɛlɛ nwo, titili bɛ ngoanelielɛ nwo.
