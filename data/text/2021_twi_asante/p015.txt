14. Ghanaman mu no, na nnipa dubaako na wɔnya yadeɛ no bi nnawɔtwe biara, na yei kɔɔ soro kɔduruu nnipa ahanson ne aduɔwɔtwe baako wɔ Kutawonsa bosome mu wɔ nnawɔtwe a ɛtwa toɔ no mu wɔ 2020 mu, na ɛso te kɔduruu ɔha ne ɛnan wɔ Ɔpɛnimma bosome mu wɔ nnawɔtwe a ɛtwa toɔ no mu wɔ 2020 mu. Afe 2021 yi mu no, yadeɛ no foforɔ sane yii ne tiri a na nnwawɔtwe biara no, nnipa aduɔkron nkron na wɔnya bi wɔ Ɔpɛpɔn bosome mu, na yei kɔɔ soro kɔduruu nnipa ahanwɔtwe ne aduɔwɔtwe wɔ Ɔgyefuo da a ɛtɔ so enum wɔ 2021 mu.
<<<P>>>
15. Ɛsiane sɛ Aban pɛ sɛ ɔko tia yadeɛ mmoawa no enti, ɔde akɔneabadie ho ɔhyɛ too ɔmanfoɔ so sɛ obiara nhyɛ ɛdan mu, na yei nyaa nsunsuansoɔ bɔne wɔ Ɔman no asoɛɛ titire mu dwumadie dodoɔ no ara so, titire ahɔhoyɛ, kuadwuma, apɔmuden, akwantuo, adwadeɛyɔ, adansie, sikakorabea ne nwomasua nnwuma so.
<<<P>>>
16. Akwantuo mu adwumayɛfoɔ, ahyɛnkafoɔ ne “tro-tro” mu ameetifoɔ ɛnna ankorankorɛ nnwuma mu adwumayɛfoɔ a wɔnni Aban nwoma mu na wɔnni asetenam aboadeɛ biara no ho kyeree wɔn yie wɔ saa akɔneabadie ho ɔhyɛ a Aban de too ɔmanfoɔ soɔ no mu. Ɛwom sɛ na ɔhyɛ no bɛboa ama yadeɛ mmoawa no asetrɛ ano abrɛ ase, nanso ɛmaa adwadeɛ ne asetenam akadeɛ ahodoɔ a ɔmanfoɔ hia de abɔ wɔn ɔbra no so tee pa ara.
<<<P>>>
17. Yɛde yɛn ho toto Abibirem Atɔeɛ aman ahodoɔ no ho a, Ghana Aban de akokoɔduru tuu anammɔn pa a ɔde sɔɔ yadeɛ mmoawa no asetrɛ ano na ɛnam so maa amansan kamfoo Aban no wɔ saa anammɔn ahodoɔ a ɔtuu wɔ yadeɛ yi ho na yei hyɛɛ Aban nkuran ma ɔyɛɛ bi kaa ho.
