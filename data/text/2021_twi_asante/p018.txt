Adwadeɛ so ɛtoɔ: Aban bɛyi ɛtoɔ a nnwumakuo nketewa tua wɔ wɔn adwadeɛ a wɔtɔn so no afiri hɔ wɔ afe yi mu wɔ abosome mmiɛnsa a ɛtɔ so mmienu, mmiɛnsa ne ɛnan no mu ama wɔn kɔn mu adwo wɔn;
<<<P>>>
Ahyɛn ho Mfasoɔ so Ɛtoɔ: Aban bɛyi abosome mmiɛnsa biara ɛtoɔ a tro-tro ne taksi wuranom tua no afiri hɔ wɔ afe yi mu wɔ abosome mmiɛnsa a ɛtɔ so mmienu, mmiɛnsa ne ɛnan no mu sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a ahyɛntuadeɛ so bɛte kakra; ɛnna
<<<P>>>
Ɛberɛ a wɔde ayi nsiho-tua afiri hɔ no mu ntwemu sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ nkuranhyɛ ama adwadifoɔ na wɔatua ɛtoɔ a wɔntuaeɛ: Aban ayi asotwe ho sikatua ne nsiho a ɛwɔ ɛtoɔ a nnwumakuo ne ankorankorɛ ntuaeɛ bɛsi Ɔpɛnimma bosome wɔ 2020 mu no afiri hɔ. Yei yɛ nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛbɛboa nnwumakuo ne ankorankorɛ ama wɔatumi atua wɔn sika botene no nko ara wɔ afe yi mu wɔ bosome Ɛbɔ mu. Saa nhyehyɛeɛ yi wɔ hɔ ma wɔn a wɔbɛyɛ wɔn nkrataasɛm yie wɔ Ghana Ɛtoɔgyeɛ Adwumakuo (GRA) no ɔfese na wɔatua wɔn ɛtoɔ wɔ abosome mmiɛnsa a ɛdi kan wɔ afe yi mu no.

Ghana COVID-19 ASETƆRE NE ANKORANKORƐ NNWUMA MU NKANYAN HO MMOA (OBAATAN PA) DWUMADIE NO
<<<P>>>
21. Ghana Aban daa dwumadie bi a yɛato edin sɛ Ghana COVID-19 Asetɔre Ne Ankorankorɛ Nnwuma Mu Nkanyan ho Mmoa (Ghana COVID-19 Alleviation and Revitalization of Enterprises Support- Ghana CARES) no adi wɔ Obubuo bosome da a ɛtɔ so 20 wɔ afe 2020 mu. Saa dwumadie yi botaeɛ ne sɛ ɛbɛbrɛ yadeɛ kontonkurowi ho nsunsuansoɔ no ase na aboa nnwuma ama wɔde nhyehyɛeɛ pa adi dwuma ama wɔatumi agyina wɔn nan so ntɛm pa ara na ɛnam so ama Ɔman yi sikasɛm nso atu mpɔn. Nokwasɛm ne sɛ, yei yɛ akokoɔduru ɛkwan a yɛbɛtumi afa so asakra Ghanaman sikasɛm mu ama agyina yie, ama mfidinnwuma aba, yiedie bɛba na Ɔman yi ade ne ho nam so atumi asɔ Ghanafoɔ nyinaa ahiadeɛ mapa ano ama wɔn.
