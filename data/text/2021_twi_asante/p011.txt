8. Aban sikasɛm ho nhyehyɛeɛ ne amammɔntuo ahodoɔ (ɛtoɔ ne sika a yɛde di dwuma ho gyinaeɛsie ahodoɔ) no wɔ nsunsuansoɔ pa ara wɔ sɛdeɛ yɛn sikasɛm bɛyɛ wɔ animanim yi ara mu no so. 2021 Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ yi gyina nneɛma a ɛma Ɔman sikasɛm gyina a yɛde ani abu sɛ yɛbɛnya anaa nnyinasoɔ ahodoɔ ɛdidi soɔ yi so:
<<<P>>>
• Ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ nyinaa kankorɔ ɔha mu nkyɛmu enum ne akyire pɔ hwee (Real GDP growth of 5.0%);
<<<P>>>
• Ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ a Fango nka ho kankorɔ ɔha mu nkyɛmu nsia ne akyire pɔ nson (Non-Oil Real GDP growth of 6.7%);
<<<P>>>
• Ɔpɛnimma bosome mu nneɛma boo-sorokɔ- ɔha mu nkyɛmu nwɔtwe (8.0%);
<<<P>>>
• Sika a yɛadi aboro deɛ yɛwɔ so- ɔha mu nkyɛmu nkron ne akyire pɔ enum (9.5 % of GDP);
<<<P>>>
• Sika dodoɔ a yɛbɛhia de asɔ deɛ yɛagyegyeɛ so- ɔha mu nkyɛmu baako ne akyire pɔ mmiɛnsa (1.3 % of GDP); ɛnna
<<<P>>>
• Amannɔne Sika Dodoɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ yɛnya de kra nneɛma bɛyɛ abosome ɛnan.
