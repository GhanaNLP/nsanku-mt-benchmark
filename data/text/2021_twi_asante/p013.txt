Asɛntire: “Yɛbɛwie dwumadie ama aseɛ atim na yɛatoa deɛ aka so nam so akanyan ɔman sikasɛm”
<<<P>>>
Ahonnidie ne Efi Ho Ɛtoɔ (Sanitation and Pollution Levy-SPL) a ɛyɛ mpɛsewa edu (10 pesewas) wɔ Pɛtro/Desɛl biara so a ɛhyɛ Ogya- Ahoɔden Ɛtoɔ ho Mmara (Energy Sector Levies Act - ESLA) no ase, na yɛnam saa ɛtoɔ yi so bɛtoto ahonnidie ho nsɛm ama adi mu, mpontuo bɛba asetenam na yɛate nnipa dodoɔ a wɔyare anaa wɔwuwuo ɛsiane efi enti no so;
<<<P>>>
Ogya-Ahoɔden Nkanyan ho Ɛtoɔ (Energy Sector Recovery-Delta Fund) a ɛyɛ mpɛsewa aduonu wɔ lita/desɛl biara so, na yei hyɛ ESLA nhyehyɛeɛ no ase a ɛbɛboa ama yɛatua ogya-ahoɔden ho ɛka a ɛfiri Ogya-Ahoɔden ho Apam ahodoɔ a ɛkɔɔ so wɔ ɛberɛ a na mfidie a ɛka saa Apam ho no nyɛ adwuma;
<<<P>>>
Sikakorabea Nnwuma mu nsiesie ho ɛtoɔ- ɔha mu nkyɛmu enum wɔ sikakorabea ahodoɔ no mfasoɔ so ansa na yɛate ɛtoɔ (5 % on profit-before-tax) na yɛde saa sika yi bɛtua ɔmanfoɔ sika dodoɔ a aka mu wɔ sikakorabea nnwuma a aguo no mu. Yɛbɛyɛ nsakraeɛ wɔ yei mu wɔ afe 2024 mu;
<<<P>>>
Sikasɛm Asoɛeɛ ne Amannwoeɛ Asoɛeɛ no bɛka abom ahwɛ akradeɛ ho dwumadie so na wɔne nnwumakuo a wɔdi akotene wɔ saa dwumadie yi mu bɛdi nsawɔsoɔ nam so ahyehyɛ akwanhodoɔ a wɔbɛfa so anya sika wɔ akradeɛ ho dwumadie yi mu; ɛnna afei
<<<P>>>
Yɛbɛyɛ nsakraeɛ wɔ sika a yɛgyegye wɔ Lɔrekwan so ɛtoɔ mu ama ɛne ɛnnɛ mmerɛ yi mu nneɛma boɔ ayɛ pɛ sɛdeɛ Sikatua Ahodoɔ ho Mmara (Fees and Charges -Miscellaneous Provisions)- Act, 2018 (Act 983) a ɛma ɛkwan ma yɛhyehyɛ Lɔrekwan so ɛtoɔ kyerɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ no, na ɛnam yei so ama yɛanya afe biara mu sikatua mu nsakraeɛ a ɛfata a ɛne afe biara mu nneɛma-boɔ sorokɔ a Ghana Asetenam Nkontabuo Adwumakuo (Ghana Statistical Service) de bɛto edwa no siae soɔ.

<<<P>>>
11. Aban ayɛ sɛ afe 2021 yi mu no, ɔde Ghana sika dodoɔ sidi ɔpepepem ɔha ne dumiɛnsa ne ɔpepem nwɔtwe (GH₵113.8 billion) na ɛbɛdi dwuma sɛdeɛ yɛakyerɛkyerɛ mu wɔ Hyehyɛpono 4 a ɛwɔ aseɛ ha yi mu no. Sika a aka a ɛsɛ sɛ Aban nya de sɔ deɛ ɔbɛgyegye no so no bɛfiri amannɔne ne ɔman yi ara mu boseabɔ.
