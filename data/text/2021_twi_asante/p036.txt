Asɛntire: “Yɛbɛwie dwumadie ama aseɛ atim na yɛatoa deɛ aka so nam so akanyan ɔman sikasɛm”
<<<P>>>
Sika A Aka Mu Wɔ Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ mu (Budget deficit): Yei si wɔ ɛberɛ a sika a Aban de bɛdi dwuma no kɔ anim sene deɛ Aban nsa aka no ankasa.
<<<P>>>
Mpontunnwuma ho Sika (Capital Expenditure): Yei yɛ sika a yɛde yɛ mpontunnwuma sɛ ebia akwanyie, sukuudansie, ayaresabea dansie, atwene-sie, akwantusɛm, nsuo, mfidie akɛseɛ ne deɛ ɛkeka ho.
<<<P>>>
Adwumayɛfoɔ akatua (Compensation of Employees): Yei yɛ adwumayɛfoɔ bosome akatua, Sika ahodoɔ, asetenam ahotosoɔ sika, akwankɔgya sika ne deɛ ɛkeka ho a wɔtua ma Aban adwumayɛfoɔ.
<<<P>>>
Mpontuo mu Aboafoɔ (Development Partners): Yei gyina hɔ ma Aman anaa nnwumakuo a wɔboa Aman a afei na wɔretu mpɔn ma wɔduru wɔn mpontuo botaeɛ ho. Yei bɛtumi ayɛ akuhodoɔ bi te sɛ Amansan Fotoɔ Adwumakuo (International Monetary Fund- IMF), Ewiase Sikakorabea (World Bank), Abibirem Mpontuo Sikakorabea (African Development Bank) ne deɛ ɛkeka ho. Ɛtumi nso yɛ ankorankorɛ aman bi te sɛ Germany, Nyiresi Aburokyire, Amɛrika, ne wɔn a wɔkeka ho.
<<<P>>>
Afe Awieeɛ Nneɛma Boɔ Sorokɔ (End Year Inflation): Yei yɛ mpɛmpɛnsoɔ a nneɛma boɔ kɔduru wɔ Ɔpɛnimma bosome mu wɔ afe pɔtee bi mu.
<<<P>>>
Dwumadie ho sika a yɛadi aboro so (Expenditure Overrun): Yei si wɔ ɛberɛ a, sika a yɛde adi dwuma ahodoɔ no aboro deɛ yɛtwa too hɔ wɔ sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ mu a mmarahyɛbadwa gye too mu no so.
<<<P>>>
Ogya- Ahoɔden Ɛtoɔ ho Mmara -ESLA: (Energy Sector Levies Act).
<<<P>>>
Nnipasesa (Evacuation): Yei yɛ ɛkwan a wɔfa de nnipadɔm firi beaeɛ bi kɔ beaeɛ foforɔ.
