“Amɛmɔ wiemɔ lɛ mli, ni daa gbi lɛ, amɛtaoɔ ŋmalɛi lɛ amli jogbaŋŋ.”—BƆF. 17:11.
<<<P>>>
LALA 162
Esa Akɛ Male Nyɔŋmɔ
<<<P>>>
NƆ NI WƆBAAKASE
<<<P>>>
Nikasemɔ nɛɛ baaye abua wɔ ni wɔna nɔ hewɔ ni ehe miihia ni wɔhe be ni wɔkane daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ, ni ebaawo wɔ ŋaa komɛi yɛ bɔ ni esa akɛ wɔfee lɛ wɔhã lɛ he.
<<<P>>>
YƐ AFI 1905 lɛ, Yehowa asafo lɛ fee wolo ko ni ŋmalɛ kɛhã gbi fɛɛ gbi kɛ mligbalamɔ ni jɛ Zion’s Watch Tower lɛ mli lɛ yɔɔ mli. Enɛ ye ebua nyɛmimɛi lɛ koni gbi fɛɛ gbi yɛ afi lɛ mli lɛ, amɛkane ŋmalɛ kome ko ni amɛjwɛŋ nɔ. Yehowa webii lɛ kɛ wolo nɛɛ tsu nii kɛyashi 1927. No sɛɛ lɛ, akɛ daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ kɛ mligbalamɔ ni jɛ Buu-Mɔɔ mli lɛ fataa Year Book lɛ he.* Afeɔ Year Book lɛ daa afi. No hewɔ lɛ, daa afi lɛ, akɛ ŋmalɛi srɔtoi kɛ mligbalamɔi ni jɛ Buu-Mɔɔ mli lɛ woɔ mli, ni enɛ hã nyɛmimɛi lɛ ná ŋmalɛi babaoo ni amɛbaajwɛŋ nɔ.
<<<P>>>
2 Kɛjɛ 1986 lɛ, asafo lɛ bɔi wolo bibioo ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Ŋmalɛi Lɛ Amli Ni Wɔɔkwe Daa lɛ feemɔ, ni daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ yɛ mli. Amrɔ nɛɛ lɛ, atsɔɔ wolo nɛɛ shishi kɛtee wiemɔi ni fa fe 300 amli. Obaanyɛ ona daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ hu kɛ́ ogbele JW Library® app lɛ, Buu-Mɔɔ INTANET NƆ WOJIATOOHE™ (wol.jw.org) lɛ, loo Watchtower Library lɛ.
<<<P>>>
3 Akɛni wɔbɛ dekã kwraa hewɔ lɛ, bei komɛi lɛ ebaanyɛ ewa kɛhã wɔ akɛ wɔbaakane daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, yɛ nikasemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaahã sanebimɔi etɛ komɛi ahetoo. Nomɛi ji: Mɛɛ sane ko yɔɔ Biblia lɛ mli ni baanyɛ awo wɔ hewalɛ ni wɔkane daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ? Mɛni hewɔ ehi akɛ wɔbaakane daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ? Ni te wɔbaanyɛ wɔfee lɛ wɔhã tɛŋŋ?
<<<P>>>
* No mli lɛ, abɛ Year Book yɛ wiemɔi komɛi ni Ga fata he lɛ amli. Shi daa nyɔɔŋ nɔ lɛ, aŋmalaa ŋmalɛi kɛhã gbi fɛɛ gbi yɛ nyɔɔŋ lɛ mli kɛ Buu-Mɔɔ mli ni abaana amɛmligbalamɔi lɛ yɛ lɛ, yɛ nakai nyɔɔŋ lɛ Buu-Mɔɔ mli.
