18 Be ni wɔkase nibii ni Yehowa efee ehã wɔ lɛ, wɔhiɛ sɔ lɛ waa, ni no hewɔ lɛ wɔjɔɔ wɔhe nɔ wɔhã lɛ ni wɔhã abaptisi wɔ. Yehowa náa miishɛɛ waa kɛ́ wɔtee nɔ wɔkɛ wɔbe, wɔhewalɛ, wɔshika kɛ nibii krokomɛi ni wɔyɔɔ lɛ tsu nii jogbaŋŋ kɛsɔmɔ lɛ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, wɔkaneɔ Biblia lɛ daa gbi ni wɔjwɛŋɔ nɔ, wɔshiɛɔ Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ he sane kpakpa lɛ, wɔyaa asafoŋ kpeei daa ni wɔkɛ wɔhe woɔ mli, ni wɔtsuɔ onia kɛfiɔ Yehowa asafo lɛ kɛ shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ sɛɛ. (Lala 1:2, 3; 2 Kor. 8:4; Heb. 10:24, 25; 13:15) Kɛ́ wɔfee bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni wɔbaanyɛ koni wɔsɔmɔ Yehowa jogbaŋŋ lɛ, no tsɔɔ akɛ wɔhiɛ sɔɔ nɔ ni Yehowa feɔ ehãa wɔ lɛ waa.—Kol. 3:23, 24.

19 Eyɛ mli akɛ ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, mɛi babaoo hiɛ esɔɔ nii moŋ, shi wɔle akɛ ehi akɛ wɔhiɛ baasɔ nii ni wɔjie hiɛsɔɔ kpo kɛtsɔ wɔwiemɔ kɛ wɔnifeemɔ nɔ. Kɛ́ wɔfee nakai lɛ, wɔbaaná miishɛɛ waa, wɔyeŋ wɔtsui tsɔ, ni nɔ ni fe fɛɛ lɛ, naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ wɔ kɛ Yehowa teŋ lɛ mli baawa. Kɛ́ wɔjwɛŋ nibii ni Yehowa efee ehã wɔ be ko ni eho, kɛ nibii ni efeɔ ehãa wɔ bianɛ lɛ ahe lɛ, wɔhiɛ baasɔ lɛ waa. Yehowa hu baahã wɔna akɛ ehiɛ sɔɔ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni wɔfeɔ kɛsɔmɔɔ lɛ lɛ ni ebaajɔɔ wɔ waa ŋmɛnɛ kɛ wɔsɛɛ fɛɛ.—Lala 106:1; Heb. 11:6.
