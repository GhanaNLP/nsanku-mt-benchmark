Yehowa Yeɔ Ebuaa Mɛi Ni Kɛ Amɛhiɛ Fɔ̃ɔ Enɔ Lɛ
<<<P>>>
Nyɛmi yoo ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Emma lɛ tsuɔ nii yɛ hotɛl ko. Ekɛ gbii etɛ tsuɔ nii yɛ otsi lɛ mli, ni yɛ gbi lɛ eko fɛɛ eko mli lɛ, ekɛ ŋmɛlɛtswai nɛɛhu tsuɔ nii. Be ko lɛ, eyabi mɔ ni etsuɔ nii yɛ eshishi lɛ gbɛ ni ehã efo nitsumɔ mli gbi kome koni enyɛ eya kpokpaa wulu nɔ kpee ko. Shi ekpɛlɛɛɛ enibimɔ lɛ nɔ, moŋ lɛ, ekɛɛ lɛ akɛ kɛ́jɛ nakai gbi lɛ nɔ kɛyaa lɛ, ebaatsu nii gbii enumɔ yɛ otsi lɛ mli, ni kɛ́ enyɛɛɛ lɛ, no lɛ, ekpa nitsumɔ lɛ. Emma he eye akɛ Yehowa baakwɛ lɛ, hewɔ lɛ ekpɛ mli akɛ ebaakpa ni-tsumɔ lɛ, ni etee kpee lɛ.
<<<P>>>
Be ni Emma tee hotɛl lɛ koni ekɛ lɛta ni tsɔɔ akɛ eekpa nitsumɔ lɛ ayahã lɛ, no mli lɛ, hotɛl lɛ nɔkwɛlɔ lɛ yɛ jɛmɛ. Be ni enu nɔ ni eba lɛ, ewo Emma shi akɛ, ebaatsu he nii ehã lɛ. Shi otsii enyɔ sɛɛ lɛ, akɛɛ Emma akɛ ashwie lɛ. Be ko sɛɛ lɛ, Emma kɛ hotɛl lɛ nɔkwɛlɔ lɛ kpe ekoŋŋ. Be ni ená ele akɛ ashwie Emma lɛ, emli fu waa. Ekwɛ ni ahe Emma yɛ nitsumɔ lɛ mli ekoŋŋ. Yɛ be nɛɛ mli lɛ, ahã etsu nii gbii enu-mɔ yɛ otsi lɛ mli, shi gbi fɛɛ gbi lɛ, ebaatsu nii ŋmɛlɛtswai ejwɛ pɛ. Enɛ hi fe bɔ ni tsutsu lɛ etsuɔ nii lɛ, ejaakɛ no hã ená hegbɛ etsu gbɛgbamɔ nitsumɔ lɛ kɛ nitsumɔi krokomɛi ehã Yehowa. Hɛɛ! Yehowa tsu esane lɛ he nii fe bɔ ni ekpaa gbɛ lɛ kwraa.
