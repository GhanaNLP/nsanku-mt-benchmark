Daa gbi lɛ, ŋɔɔ be kɛsusu nibii kpakpai ni oyɔɔ lɛ ahe
(Kwɛmɔ kuku 9-10)
<<<P>>>
wɔyeee wɔtsui tsɔ. Kɛ́ wɔkɛ wɔjwɛŋmɔ ma bɔ ni Yehowa kwɛɔ wɔ lɛ nɔ lɛ, wɔbaaná nɔmimaa kwraa akɛ wɔsɛɛ lɛ ebaakwɛ wɔ, ni no hewɔ lɛ, wɔyeŋ wɔtsui tsɔ. (Mat. 6:25-34) Agbɛnɛ hu, kɛ́ wɔsɔle wɔhã Yehowa ni wɔda lɛ shi, ni wɔbi lɛ ni eye ebua wɔ lɛ, enaa wɔyaafonui lɛ kɛ wɔpiŋmɔi lɛ, ni ehãa wɔnáa “Nyɔŋmɔ toiŋjɔlɛ” lɛ eko. Toiŋjɔlɛ nɛɛ hãa wɔfeɔ kpoo ejaakɛ naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ wɔ kɛ Yehowa teŋ lɛ mli wa ni no hewɔ lɛ, wɔyeee wɔtsui tsɔ.
<<<P>>>
7 Kɛ́ wɔhiɛ sɔɔ nii lɛ, naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ wɔ kɛ Yehowa teŋ lɛ mli baawa. Nibii kpakpai fɛɛ ni wɔyɔɔ lɛ, Yehowa ŋɔɔ ejɛ. (Kanemɔ Yakobo 1:17.) Eke wɔ nibii pii. Nibii nɛɛ ekomɛi ji, kpɔmɔnɔ lɛ kɛ hiɛnɔkamɔ ni wɔyɔɔ akɛ wɔbaahi shi kɛya naanɔ lɛ. Mɛni hewɔ eke wɔ nibii nɛɛ? Suɔmɔ ni eyɔɔ kɛhã wɔ lɛ hewɔ, shi jeee nɔ ko ni wɔfee hewɔ. (Rom. 3:24; Efe. 2:8) Kɛ́ wɔhiɛ sɔɔ suɔmɔ ni Yehowa jieɔ lɛ kpo etsɔɔ wɔ, kɛ nibii kpakpai ni ekɛkeɔ wɔ lɛ, no baahã wɔsumɔ lɛ ni wɔhe lɛ wɔye waa.

8 Susumɔ nibii kpakpai ni Yehowa fee ehã bo be ko ni eho lɛ ahe. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, susumɔ be ko ni okɛ shihilɛ ko ni mli wa kpe lɛ he. Ekolɛ mɔ ko ni osumɔɔ esane gbo, hela mɔ bo waa, loo awa bo yi akɛni osɔmɔɔ Yehowa lɛ hewɔ. Mɛni ye ebua bo yɛ nakai beiaŋ? Ekã shi faŋŋ akɛ, jeee bo diɛŋtsɛ ohewalɛ naa onyɛ odamɔ shihilɛ lɛ naa. Moŋ lɛ, ole akɛ, eji Yehowa hãaa bo ehewalɛ ni fa kɛteke nɔ kulɛ, onyɛŋ ofi shi. (Kanemɔ 2 Korintobii 4:7.) No hewɔ lɛ, kɛ́ osusu nibii kpakpai ni Yehowa efee ehã bo be ko ni eho lɛ ahe lɛ, no baahã ohiɛ asɔ lɛ waa.—Lala 103:2.
<<<P>>>
9 Susumɔ nibii kpakpai ni oyɔɔ bianɛ lɛ ahe. Biblia lɛ woo wɔ hewalɛ akɛ: “Nyɛdaa shi yɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ he.” (1 Tes. 5:18) Daa gbi lɛ, susumɔ nibii kpakpai ni oyɔɔ lɛ ahe. Obaanyɛ okɔ be ko yɛ gbi lɛ mli kɛfee nakai. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, obaanyɛ ofee nakai leebi be ni ote shi, koni okɛ nɔ ko kpakpa aje gbi lɛ shishi.
