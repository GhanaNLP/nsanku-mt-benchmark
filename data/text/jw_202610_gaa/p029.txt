18 Daa gbi lɛ, bɔɔ mɔdɛŋ ni okane daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ yɛ be ni oto akɛ obaakane lɛ mli. Kaahã nɔ ko gbala ojwɛŋmɔ. Nyɛmi yoo ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Isabelle ni eye afii 19 lɛ wie akɛ: “Tswaa ofai shi akɛ, okwɛŋ nɔ ko yɛ ofoŋ lɛ nɔ loo ofeŋ nɔ ko yɛ nɔ kɛyashi obaakane daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ ogbe naa. Kɛ́ oyɔse akɛ kɛ́ ookane daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ yɛ ofoŋ lɛ nɔ lɛ, nibii krokomɛi ni yɔɔ ofoŋ lɛ nɔ lɛ gbalaa ojwɛŋmɔ lɛ, no lɛ okɛ wolo lɛ diɛŋtsɛ afee.” Nyɛmi yoo ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Olivia ni eye afii 19 lɛ wie akɛ: “Nɔ kome ni yeɔ ebuaa mi ni makane daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ daa ji, mikɛ mɛi krokomɛi tamɔ miwekumɛi loo minanemɛi feɔ ekome kɛkaneɔ. Kɛ́ oyɛ mɔ ko ni baakai bo loo mɔ ko ni okɛ lɛ baafee ekome kɛkane lɛ, ewaaa kwraa akɛ obaafee lɛ daa.”

19 Fɔlɔi, kɛ́ nyɛkɛ nyɛbii lɛ feɔ daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ daa lɛ, ebaaka amɛhe, ni amɛbaafee amɛwala beiaŋ fɛɛ. Ekolɛ obii lɛ darako ni no hewɔ lɛ amɛnuŋ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ shishi jogbaŋŋ, shi akɛni okɛ amɛ feɔ daa hewɔ lɛ, amɛbaana lɛ akɛ nɔ ko ni he hiaa ni esa akɛ amɛfee daa. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, kɛ́ adu nɔ ko ni ashwieɔ nɔ nu daa lɛ, ebaada ni ebatsɔ tso agbo. Nakai nɔŋŋ kɛ́ okɛ obii lɛ feɔ daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ daa lɛ, fiofio lɛ ebaahã naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ amɛ kɛ Yehowa teŋ lɛ mli awa waa.—Kanemɔ Filipibii 1:9, 10.
