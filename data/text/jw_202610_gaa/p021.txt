Daniel kɛ enanemɛi etɛ lɛ anɔ lɛ maa enɛ nɔ mi. Be ko lɛ, Maŋtsɛ Nebukadnezar la lamɔ ko ni no hã eshe gbeyei waa. No hewɔ lɛ, eshwe akɛ enu nɔ ni lamɔ lɛ tsɔɔ lɛ shishi. Enɛ hã efã ni ayatsɛ majikfeelɔi lɛ kɛ ŋkunyaayelɔi lɛ kɛba ehiɛ. Ebi amɛ ni amɛgba lɛ lamɔ lɛ ni amɛtsɔɔ lɛ shishi hu. Adesa ko adesa ko bɛ ni baanyɛ afee nɔ ni ebi amɛ lɛ! (Dan. 2:3, 9-11) Maŋtsɛ lɛ kɛɛ amɛ akɛ ké amɛgbaaa lɛ lamɔ lɛ ni amɛbaatsɔɔ lɛ shishi lɛ, ebaahã agbe amɛ kɛ Babilon hiɛshikalɔi krokomɛi lɛ fɛɛ. Daniel kɛ enanemɛi lɛ fata Babilon hiɛshikalɔi lɛ ahe. No hewɔ lɛ, be ni sane nɛɛ yaa nɔ lɛ, mɛni amɛfee?

14 Daniel jɛ bulɛ mli ebi maŋtsɛ lɛ ni ehã lɛ be fioo koni etsɔɔ maŋtsɛ lɛ elamɔ lɛ shishi. No sɛɛ lɛ, Daniel yana enanemɛi lɛ. Egba amɛ nɔ ni eba lɛ, ni “ekɛɛ amɛ ni amɛsɔle amɛkpa Nyɔŋmɔ ni yɔɔ ŋwɛi lɛ fai kɛhã mɔbɔnalɛ yɛ nɛkɛ teemɔŋ sane nɛɛ he,” koni akakpãtã lɛ kɛ amɛ hiɛ. (Dan. 2:18) Yehowa bo sɔlemɔ ni amɛjɛ amɛtsuiŋ amɛsɔle lɛ toi. Ejie lamɔ lɛ kpo etsɔɔ Daniel, ni etsɔɔ lɛ shishi. Daniel tsɔɔ maŋtsɛ lɛ elamɔ lɛ shishi, ni no hewɔ lɛ, agbeee amɛteŋ mɔ ko. Maŋtsɛ lɛ hã Daniel kɛ enanemɛi lɛ gbɛhei ni nɔ kwɔlɔ yɛ Babilon. —Dan. 2:19, 47-49.

15 Obaanyɛ okase nibii kpakpai komɛi kɛjɛ sane nɛɛ mli. Klɛŋklɛŋ lɛ, ké ootsɔ shihilɛ ko ni mli wa ko mli lɛ, ehe ehiaaa ni okome too pɛ odamɔɔ naa. Kaahã efee bo hiɛgbele akɛ obaabi yelikɛbuamɔ. Nɔ ni ji enyɔ lɛ, kaashashao shi akɛ obaakɛɛ mɛi ni amɛsɔle amɛhã bo yɛ sane ko he. (2 Kor. 1:11) Jwɛŋmɔ gbɛi ni yɔɔ naakpɛɛ ni Yehowa baanyɛ atsɔ nɔ ehã bo kɛ mɛi krokomɛi asɔlemɔi hetoo lɛ ahe. Yehowa, “Sɔlemɔ Toibolɔ” lɛ, baanyɛ aye abua bo ni ofee nibii ni osusuɔ akɛ ebaawa kɛhã bo akɛ ofee lɛ. (Lala 65:2; Efe. 3:20) Kɛ́ wɔyɛ nanemɛi ni sɔmɔɔ Yehowa lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔná nɔmimaa akɛ amɛbaaye amɛbua wɔ yɛ shihilɛi ni mli wawai amli.—Kanemɔ Galatiabii 6:2.
