LALA 135
Yehowa Kɛɛ: “Mibi, Hã Ohiɛ Akã Shi”
<<<P>>>
NƆ NI WƆBAAKASE
Kɛ́ oblahii kɛ oblayei bɔ nanemɛi kpakpai lɛ, no baanyɛ ahã amɛsɔmɔ Yehowa jogbaŋŋ.
<<<P>>>
Nanemɛi Kpakpai Baaye Abua Bo Ni Osɔmɔ Yehowa Jogbaŋŋ
<<<P>>>
“Mɔ ko nine baanyɛ atsɔ mɔ ni ekome too eyɔɔ lɛ nɔ, shi mɛi enyɔ ni efee ekome baanyɛ ate shi awo mɔ lɛ.”—JAJ. 4:12.
<<<P>>>
WƆ FƐƐ wɔmiisumɔ ni wɔsɔmɔ Yehowa, wɔ-Nyɔŋmɔ lɛ, jogbaŋŋ. Shi yɛ anɔkwale mli lɛ, jeee be fɛɛ be enɛ feemɔ yɔɔ mlɛo. Mɛni hewɔ? Ejaakɛ mɛi ni yɔɔ je lɛ mli lɛ miibɔ mɔdɛŋ koni amɛtse wɔ kɛje Yehowa he. No hewɔ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔbɔ mɔdɛŋ waa koni wɔkajwɛŋ tamɔ amɛ. Nɔ ko ni esa akɛ wɔkwɛ jogbaŋŋ yɛ he, titri lɛ, wɔ oblahii kɛ oblayei lɛ ji, susumɔ ni mɛi ni yɔɔ je lɛ mli lɛ hiɛ yɛ naanyobɔɔ he lɛ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, oblahii kɛ oblayei komɛi ni leee Yehowa lɛ susuɔ akɛ, ehe miihia waa ni amɛná nanemɛi babaoo kɛ́ nanemɛi nɛɛ jeee mɛi ni amɛkɛbɔɔ waa loo mɛi ni amɛbaanyɛ amɛmu amɛfɔ̃ amɛnɔ po. Amɛteŋ mɛi komɛi susuɔ akɛ amɛtipeŋfoi pɛ ni amɛbaanyɛ amɛhe amɛ amɛye. Ni amɛteŋ mɛi krokomɛi po susuɔ akɛ nanemɛi ahe ehiaaa amɛ. Te Yehowa kɛ Yesu naa naanyobɔɔ amɛhãa tɛŋŋ?
<<<P>>>
2 Yehowa miisumɔ ni wɔná nanemɛi ni baaye amɛbua wɔ ni wɔsɔmɔ lɛ jogbaŋŋ. Ebi, Yesu, hala nanemɛi ni tamɔ nakai. Be ni eshwɛ fioo ni ekɛ ewala baahã akɛ kpɔmɔnɔ lɛ, ejie ebɔfoi anɔkwafoi lɛ ayi akɛ: “Nyɛji mɛi ni ekpɛtɛ mi yɛ mijaramɔ shihilɛi lɛ amli.” Enaaa amɛ akɛ esɛɛnyiɛlɔi kɛkɛ; ena amɛ akɛ enanemɛi. (Luka 22:28; Yoh. 15:15) Naanyo kpakpa kpɛtɛɔ mɔ be fɛɛ be, jeee be ni nibii miiya lɛ jogbaŋŋ pɛ, shi “yɛ haomɔ beiaŋ” hu. (Abɛi 17:17) Nanemɛi kpakpai yeɔ amɛbuaa wɔ ni
