Kɛ́ wɔkaneɔ Biblia lɛ daa gbi lɛ, ebaahã wɔfee ekãa waa
<<<P>>>
(Kwɛmɔ kuku 10)
<<<P>>>
gbaŋŋ. Enɛ baahã Yehowa kɛ emumɔ lɛ akudɔ wɔ ni wɔsusu nii ahe tamɔ lɛ, ni wɔsa ehiɛ yɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni wɔfeɔ lɛ mli.—Rom. 8:5, 6.
<<<P>>>
¹² Wɔkaneɔ daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ ejaakɛ eyeɔ ebuaa wɔ koni wɔná sui ni baahã wɔnye wɔtsu nitsumɔi ni atsuɔ yɛ asafo lɛ mli lɛ eko. Nitsumɔi babaoo yɛ ni esa akɛ atsu yɛ Yehowa asafo lɛ mli, ni ehe baahia ni aná nyɛmimɛi ni hemɔkɛyeli mli wa, ni miisumɔ ni amɛtsu nitsumɔi nɛɛ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, ehe miihia ni aná daa gbɛgbalɔi, maŋsɛɛ sɔɔlɔi, mɛi ni yeɔ amɛbuaa yɛ Betel, mɛi ni yeɔ amɛbuaa ni amamɔɔ asafo lɛ tsũi, kɛ mɛi ni yeɔ amɛbuaa yɛ wiemɔ shishitsɔɔmɔ ɔfisii. Agbɛnɛ hu, ehe miihia ni aná oblahii ni sa, ni baa amɛhe shi ni baanyɛ asɔmɔ akɛ asafoŋ sɔɔlɔi, asafoŋ onukpai, kɛ kpokpaa nɔkwɛlɔi. Yehowa miitao nyɛmimɛi hii ni abaanyɛ amu afɔ̃ amɛnɔ, ni feɔ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ kpakpa, ni le bɔ ni akɛ Biblia mli shishitoo mlai tsuɔ nii koni amɛnyiɛ hiɛ yɛ esafo lɛ mli. (5 Mo. 1:13-15) Kɛ́ okane daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ kɛfata Nyɔŋmɔjamɔ nifeemɔi krokomɛi ni ofeɔ daa gbi lɛ ahe lɛ, no baahã oná sui ni baahã onyɛ otsu nitsumɔi ni atsuɔ yɛ Yehowa asafo lɛ mli lɛ eko.

<<<P>>>
¹³ Naa ŋaawoi komɛi ni baaye abua bo be ni okaneɔ daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ. Dani nyɛmimɛi ni yɔɔ Betel lɛ baaje amɛnitsumɔ shishi lɛ, Betel weku lɛ fɛɛ feɔ ekome kɛsusuɔ daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ he. Amɛteŋ mɔ kome damɔɔ amɛnajiaŋ ejeɔ etsuiŋ esɔleɔ kuku dani amɛjeɔ shishi. Bo hu dani obaaje daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ shishi lɛ, obaanyɛ osɔle kuku ni obi Yehowa ni eye ebua bo ni oná nɔ ni ooba obakane lɛ he sɛɛ. (Yak. 1:5) No sɛɛ lɛ, kanemɔ ŋmalɛ lɛ kɛ emligbalamɔ lɛ. Kɛ́ ogbe naa lɛ, obaanyɛ obi ohe saji tamɔ: ‘Mɛɛ gbɛ nɔ ŋmalɛ nɛɛ baanyɛ ahã mihemɔkɛyeli lɛ mli awa? Mɛni mikase yɛ Yehowa he ni baahã mikɛ lɛ abɔ naanyo waa? Mɛɛ gbɛ nɔ ebaanyɛ eye ebua mi ni matsu naagbai ni ekolɛ mikɛbaakpe ŋmɛnɛ lɛ ahe nii?’ Kɛ́ ekolɛ okɛ oweku loo onanemɛi feɔ ekome kɛkaneɔ daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ, no lɛ, nyɛ fɛɛ nyɛbaanyɛ nyɛwie he, ni nyɛteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ atsɔɔ nɔ ni enyaa he
