Mɛni Nɔ Ohemɔkɛyeli Lɛ Damɔ?
<<<P>>>
BE NI Yehowa Odasefoi kɛbo kase nii lɛ, mɛni hã oyiŋ tsɔ akɛ ona “hemɔkɛyeli kome” lɛ, ni ji gbɛ kome pɛ ni Nyɔŋmɔ sumɔɔ ni wɔtsɔ nɔ wɔjá lɛ lɛ? (Efe. 4:4-6) Ani okɛ Yehowa webii lɛ babɔ akɛni ona akɛ suɔmɔ yɛ amɛteŋ lɛ hewɔ? Ani oná amɛhe miishɛɛ akɛni amɛbaa amɛjeŋ jogbaŋŋ lɛ hewɔ? Mɛi pii sumɔɔ ni amɛkɛ Yehowa webii lɛ abɔ ejaakɛ Yehowa webii lɛ hãa amɛnuɔ he akɛ amɛyɛ shweshweeshwe ni asumɔɔ amɛ. Nakai esa akɛ eba lɛ ejaakɛ nɔ ni akɛbaale anɔkwa Kristofoi ji suɔmɔ kɛ jeŋba kpakpa.—Yoh. 13:35; 1 Pet. 2:12.
<<<P>>>
Kɛ́ bɔ ni Yehowa webii lɛ hã onu he lɛ titri hewɔ obafata amɛhe lɛ, no lɛ kɛ́ onyɛmi Odasefonyo ko fee bo nɔ ko ni gba onaa, loo efee bo nɔ ko ni etsɔɔɔ suɔmɔ, aloo efee esha ko ni yɔɔ hiɛdɔɔ waa tamɔ nuu ni Paulo wie ehe ni yɔɔ blema Korinto asafo lɛ mli lɛ, ani obaaya nɔ okɛ Yehowa webii lɛ abɔ? (1 Kor. 5:1) Kɛ́ bɔ ni mɛi hãa onuɔ he ohãa lɛ ji nɔ titri ni ohemɔkɛyeli lɛ damɔ nɔ lɛ, no lɛ sane yɛ he. Kɛ́ nakai lɛ, mɛni nɔ esa akɛ ohemɔkɛyeli lɛ adamɔ?
<<<P>>>
TE OBAAFEE TƐŊŊ OHÃ OHEMƆKƐYELI LƐ MLI AWA?
<<<P>>>
Kɛ́ anɔkwalei ni yɔɔ Biblia lɛ mli lɛ nɔ ohemɔkɛyeli lɛ damɔ lɛ, be ni oyaa nɔ okaseɔ amɛhe nii babaoo lɛ ohemɔkɛyeli lɛ mli baawa. Naa sane ko ni tsɔɔ nakai.
<<<P>>>
Be ko lɛ, Yesu kaselɔi lɛ ateŋ mɛi babaoo kpa esɛɛnyiɛmɔ. Mɛni ba? No mli lɛ, Yesu wie akɛ, esa akɛ amɛye eheloo lɛ ni amɛnu elá lɛ dani amɛbaaná wala, ni amɛyanyaaa nɔ ni ewie lɛ he. Amɛwie akɛ: “Wiemɔ ni wa nɛ.” Nɔ ni Yesu wie lɛ gba amɛnaa aahu akɛ amɛkpa esɛɛnyiɛmɔ.—Yoh. 6:56-60, 66.
<<<P>>>
Sɛɛ mli lɛ, Yesu bi ebɔfoi lɛ akɛ: “Ani nyɛ hu nyɛmiitao ni nyɛya?” Petro hã lɛ hetoo akɛ: “Nuŋtsɔ, namɔ ŋɔɔ wɔbaaya? Bo oyɔɔ naanɔ wala wiemɔi lɛ. Wɔ lɛ, wɔhe wɔye ni wɔná wɔle hu akɛ bo ji Mɔ Krɔŋkrɔŋ ni jɛ Nyɔŋmɔ ŋɔɔ lɛ.”—Yoh. 6:67-69.
<<<P>>>
Mɛni hewɔ Petro kɛ bɔfoi krokomɛi lɛ amli fuuu ni amɛkpa Yesu sɛɛnyiɛmɔ lɛ? Nɔ hewɔ lɛ ji, amɛhemɔkɛyeli lɛ damɔ nɔ ko kpakpa nɔ, jeee bɔ ni amɛnuɔ he amɛhãa lɛ kɛkɛ. Petro wie akɛ: “Wɔ lɛ, wɔhe wɔye ni wɔná wɔle hu akɛ bo ji Mɔ Krɔŋkrɔŋ ni jɛ Nyɔŋmɔ ŋɔɔ lɛ.” Amɛle nɔ ni amɛhe amɛye kɛ nɔ hewɔ ni amɛhe amɛye lɛ. Amɛkɛ Yesu wiemɔi kɛ enifeemɔi lɛ to nɔ ni amɛkase yɛ Ŋmalɛ lɛ mli lɛ he, ni amɛyiŋ tsɔ akɛ Yesu ji Mesia lɛ. Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, amɛhemɔkɛyeli lɛ mli bawa, ni no hewɔ lɛ be ni mɛi lɛ kpa Yesu sɛɛnyiɛmɔ lɛ, amɛ lɛ, amɛfeee nakai.
<<<P>>>
Mɛni wɔkaseɔ yɛ sane nɛɛ mli? Nɔ ni wɔle lɛ yɛ hewalɛ kwraa fe bɔ ni wɔnuɔ he wɔhãa. Mɛni hewɔ? Ejaakɛ nibii ni wɔkaseɔ yɛ Biblia lɛ mli, tamɔ Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛ́i lɛ, eshiwoi lɛ, kɛ kpɔmɔnɔ lɛ nɛkɛ lɛ, tsakeee. Mɛi awiemɔi
