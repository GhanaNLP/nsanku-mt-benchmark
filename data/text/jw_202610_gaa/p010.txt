(Kwɛmɔ kuku 14)

kɛda amɛ shi. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, obaanyɛ osɛndi asafoŋ onukpa ko ni basara bo kɛ oweku lɛ ni ewo nyɛ hewalɛ lɛ mɛseji kɛda lɛ shi. (1 Tes. 5:12, 13) Agbɛnɛ hu, yɛ be kɛ beiaŋ lɛ, obaanyɛ oda asafoŋ onukpai lɛ aŋamɛi lɛ ashi ejaakɛ amɛŋmɛɔ gbɛ ni amɛwumɛi lɛ kɛ be ni amɛkɛ amɛfeɔ ekome kɛfeɔ nii lɛ eko yasaraa nyɛmimɛi lɛ ni amɛwoɔ amɛ hewalɛ.

15 Mɔ ni fe fɛɛ ni esa akɛ wɔda lɛ shi ji Yehowa, ni wɔfeɔ nakai kɛ́ wɔmiisɔle. (Efe. 6:18) Bei pii kɛ́ wɔmiisɔle lɛ, wɔbiɔ Yehowa nɔ ko loo wɔkpaa lɛ fai ni eye ebua wɔ. Ekolɛ wɔbiɔ lɛ ni eye ebua wɔ ni wɔnyɛ wɔdamɔ shihilɛi komɛi ni mli wala lɛ anaa, ehã wɔ nilee koni wɔkpɛ yiŋ kpakpa, loo ekɛ esha ko ni wɔfee lɛ ake wɔ. Shi esaaa akɛ wɔhiɛ kpaa nɔ akɛ wɔbaada lɛ shi yɛ nibii pii ni efeɔ ehãa wɔ lɛ hewɔ. (Lala 95:2) Bei komɛi lɛ, esa akɛ wɔbi wɔhe akɛ, ‘Kɛ́ miisɔle lɛ, ani mikaiɔ akɛ mada Yehowa shi yɛ nibii kpakpai ni efee ehã mi lɛ ahe, aloo nii sɔŋŋ mibiɔ?’

16 Tsɔ onifeemɔ nɔ otsɔɔ akɛ ohiɛ esɔ. Kɛ́ mɔ ko fee nɔ ko kɛtsɔɔ akɛ esusuɔ ohe lɛ, feemɔ nɔ ko ohã mɔ lɛ kɛtsɔɔ akɛ ohiɛ esɔ. Yesu wie akɛ kɛ́ wɔfee nɔ ko wɔhã mɔ ko lɛ ehãa wɔnáa miishɛɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, kɛ́ ofee nɔ ko ohã mɛi kɛda amɛ shi lɛ, obaaná miishɛɛ. (Bɔf. 20:35) Agbɛnɛ hu, ebaahã mɛi lɛ hu aná miishɛɛ ejaakɛ amɛbaana akɛ osumɔɔ amɛ. Enɛ baahã amɛsumɔ bo waa, ni naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ okɛ amɛ teŋ lɛ mli baawa.

17 Wɔbaanyɛ wɔtsɔ wɔnifeemɔ nɔ wɔhã Yehowa ana akɛ wɔhiɛ sɔɔ nibii ni efeɔ ehãa wɔ lɛ waa. Mɛni wɔbaanyɛ wɔfee wɔhã Yehowa? Wɔbaanyɛ wɔkɛ ‘wɔgbɔmɔtsei lɛ ahã lɛ akɛ afɔle ni hiɛ kã.’ (Kanemɔ Romabii 12:1.)
