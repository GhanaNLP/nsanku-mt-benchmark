4 Ŋmalɛ ni nikasemɔ lɛ damɔ nɔ lɛ hãa wɔnaa akɛ, Yudafoi ni yɔɔ Beroia lɛ sumɔ ni amɛnu nɔ ni ŋmalɛ lɛ tsɔɔ lɛ shishi. (Kanemɔ Bɔfoi 17:10, 11.) Biblia lɛ hãa wɔnaa akɛ Yudafoi nɛɛ ná Paulo shiɛmɔ lɛ he miishɛɛ waa. No hewɔ lɛ, Biblia lɛ kɛɛ amɛyɛ “su kpakpa.” Amɛkɛ Paulo shiɛmɔ lɛ to nɔ ni amɛkane yɛ Ŋmalɛ lɛ mli lɛ he, ni no hã amɛna akɛ nɔ ni Paulo tsɔɔ lɛ ji anɔkwale. Wɔ hu wɔfeɔ nakai kɛ́ wɔtee asafoŋ kpee, kpokpaa nɔ kpee, loo kpokpaa wulu nɔ kpee. Yɛ kpeei nɛɛ ashishi lɛ, kɛ́ aahã wiemɔ ni wielɔ lɛ miikane ŋmalɛi lɛ, wɔnyiɛɔ esɛɛ yɛ wɔ-Biblia lɛ mli. Wɔfeɔ nakai koni wɔná nɔmimaa akɛ nɔ ni wielɔ lɛ wieɔ lɛ ji anɔkwale.
<<<P>>>
5 “[Beroiabii lɛ ateŋ] mɛi babaoo bahe Nuŋtsɔ lɛ nɔ amɛye.” (Bɔf. 17:12) Shi etsɛɛɛ ni Yudafoi komɛi ni wa Paulo yi yɛ Tesalonika lɛ ba Beroia ni amɛbawie nibii gbohii yɛ Paulo he, ni enɛ hã maŋbii lɛ ateŋ mɛi pii bɔi Beroiabii ni ebatsɔmɔ Kristofoi lɛ ayi waa. (Bɔf. 17:13) Shi no hãaa Beroiabii ni ebatsɔmɔ Kristofoi lɛ atsui afã. Akɛni amɛkaseɔ ŋmalɛi lɛ daa gbi hewɔ lɛ, amɛná nɔmimaa akɛ amɛna anɔkwale lɛ, ni no ye ebua amɛ ni amɛfi shi. Wɔ hu, kɛ́ wɔkase ŋmalɛi lɛ daa gbi lɛ, no baahã wɔhemɔkɛyeli lɛ mli awa ni wɔnyɛ wɔfi shi yɛ shihilɛi ni mli wawai amli.
