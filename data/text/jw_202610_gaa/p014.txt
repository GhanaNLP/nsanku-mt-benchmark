Awa Amɛyi Shi Amɛtee Nɔ Amɛshwere
<<<P>>>
Awa Yehowa Odasefoi lɛ ayi waa be ni awuɔ Jeŋ Ta Ni Ji Enyɔ lɛ, shi naakpɛɛ nɔ ko ba; amɛyibɔ lɛ tee nɔ efa. Nazinyo ko ni wa Odasefoi lɛ ayi lɛ wie akɛ: “Be ni oowa amɛyi lɛ, belɛ amɛyi miifa moŋ.”
<<<P>>>
Naa nɔ ko ni ba yɛ nakai beiaŋ nɔŋŋ yɛ Netherlands. Abɔ amaniɛ yɛ 1986 Yearbook lɛ mli akɛ: “Eyɛ mli akɛ amɔmɔ Nyɔŋmɔ webii lɛ, awo amɛtsuŋ, akɛ amɛ tee yiwalɛ nsarai amli, ni agbe amɛteŋ mɛi komɛi moŋ, shi shiɛlɔi lɛ ayibɔ lɛ ku ebɔ he yɛ afii enyɔ pɛ mli. Yɛ afi 1943 lɛ, no mli lɛ shiɛlɔi 1,379 ni yɔɔ Netherlands. Enɛ ji yibɔ ni fa fe fɛɛ kɛbashi nakai beiaŋ. No hewɔ lɛ, eyɛ mli akɛ nyɛmimɛi lɛ kɛ shihilɛ ni mli wa waa kpe moŋ, shi Yehowa jɔɔ amɛnitsumɔ lɛ nɔ ni ehã eshwere.”
<<<P>>>
foi ni hi shi yɛ blema lɛ ayi, koni no ahã amɛkpa Yesu sɛɛnyiɛmɔ. Be ni Yesu tee ŋwɛi sɛɛ etsɛɛɛ nɔŋŋ lɛ, jamɔŋ hiɛnyiɛlɔi lɛ ka akɛ amɛbaahã Yesu kaselɔi lɛ akpa shiɛmɔ. Amɛhã amɔmɔ bɔfoi lɛ ni akɛ amɛ tee Sanhedrin lɛ, ni ji Yudafoi akɔɔtu ni fe fɛɛ lɛ, hiɛ. Jamɔŋ hiɛnyiɛlɔi nɛɛ miitao ni amɛgbe bɔfoi lɛ; shi Gamaliel, ni ji Farisifonyo lɛ, bɔ amɛ kɔkɔ akɛ: “Nyɛkɛ nyɛhe akawaa hii nɛɛ asane mli, shi nyɛshia amɛ. Ejaakɛ kɛ́ gbɔmɛi adɛŋ yiŋtoo nɛɛ loo nitsumɔ nɛɛ jɛ lɛ, ebaagu; shi kɛ́ Nyɔŋmɔ dɛŋ ejɛ lɛ, nyɛnyɛŋ amɛ nyɛgu. Kɛjeee nakai lɛ, no lɛ abaana nyɛ po akɛ mɛi ni kɛ Nyɔŋmɔ diɛŋtsɛ miinɔ.” (Bɔf. 5:33, 34, 38, 39) Gamaliel wiemɔ lɛ ba mli pɛpɛɛpɛ. Be ni aŋmɛɛ Yesu kaselɔi lɛ ahe lɛ, jamɔŋ hiɛnyiɛlɔi lɛ tee nɔ amɛte shi amɛwo amɛ, shi amɛnyɛɛɛ amɛgu nitsumɔ ni kaselɔi anɔkwafoi lɛ tsuɔ lɛ. Moŋ lɛ, mɛi babaoo bo amɛ toi ni amɛbatsɔmɔ Kristofoi! (Bɔf. 6:7) Mɛni hã eba lɛ nakai? Eba lɛ nakai ejaakɛ Nyɔŋmɔ ye ebua amɛ ni amɛtee nɔ amɛshiɛ ni amɛshwere yɛ amɛyi ni awa lɛ fɛɛ sɛɛ.—Bɔf. 1:8.

7 Ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, mɛi pii miiba anɔkwale lɛ mli, eyɛ mli akɛ no hãa amɛwekumɛi, mɛi ni amɛkɛtsuɔ nii kɛ mɛi krokomɛi kɛ amɛ fɔɔ tako mli loo amɛteɔ shi amɛwoɔ amɛ po. Mɛni hewɔ kaselɔi hei nɛɛ hala akɛ ‘amɛbaawó amɛpiŋmɔ tso’ lɛ? Amɛfee nakai ejaakɛ amɛsumɔɔ Yehowa, ni amɛheɔ amɛyeɔ akɛ ebaaye shi ni ewo akɛ ebaakwɛ amɛ lɛ nɔ. Yehowa yeɔ eshiwoo lɛ nɔ, ni ehãa amɛ “hewalɛ ni fa kɛteke nɔ lɛ” koni amɛnyɛ amɛdamɔ naa. (2 Kor. 4:7-9) Kɛ́ amɛwekumɛi lɛ te shi amɛwo amɛ yɛ nibii ni amɛheɔ amɛyeɔ lɛ hewɔ lɛ, amɛnuɔ he tamɔ bɔ ni lalatsɛ lɛ nu he lɛ nɔŋŋ be ni ewie akɛ: “Kɛ́ mi diɛŋtsɛ mipapa kɛ mimami shi mi po lɛ, Yehowa diɛŋtsɛ baahere mi” lɛ.
