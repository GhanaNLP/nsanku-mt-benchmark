lɛ, ekɛ shihilɛ ko kpe ni ka ehemɔkɛyeli lɛ. No mli lɛ, Mediabii lɛ kɛ Persiabii lɛ ni yeɔ Babi-lon nɔ. Ni maŋtsɛ hee ni atsɛɔ lɛ Dario lɛ ehã Daniel gbɛhe ni nɔ kwɔ. No hewɔ lɛ, hii komɛi ni sɔmɔɔ maŋtsɛ lɛ ye ehe awuŋa waa, ni amɛ-tsɔ ŋaa koni amɛgbe lɛ. Amɛshwɛ maŋtsɛ lɛ akpa ni ewo mla akɛ mɔ ko mɔ ko akasɔle ehã nyɔŋmɔ ko akɛ ja lɛ maŋtsɛ lɛ. Shi Daniel tee nɔ esɔle ehã Yehowa, ni no hewɔ lɛ, ashɛ lɛ afɔ̃ jatai abu mli. Naa Daniel yɛ jatai abu mli ni enaanyo ko kwraa bɛ emasɛi koni eye ebua lɛ. Shi ani eji anɔkwale akɛ mɔ ko mɔ ko bɛ ema-sɛi?—Dan. 6:4-16.

17 Daniel 6:19-23 lɛ hãa wɔnaa akɛ, no mli lɛ eshwɛko Daniel kome too. (Kanemɔ.) Ye-howa Nyɔŋmɔ, ni ji naanyo ni he wa fe fɛɛ ni mɔ ko baanyɛ aná lɛ, hã ebɔfo ko yahere Da-niel kɛje jatai lɛ adɛŋ. Be ni je tsɛre ni Maŋ-tsɛ Dario tee jatai abu lɛ naa lɛ, Daniel kɛɛ lɛ akɛ: “Mi-Nyɔŋmɔ lɛ tsu ebɔfo ni ebaŋamɔ ja-tai lɛ anaabui, . . . ejaakɛ yɛ ehiɛ lɛ, anaaa efɔŋ ko yɛ mihe.” (Dan. 6:22) Be ni Daniel wala yaje oshãra mli lɛ, Daniel tee nɔ ekɛ ehiɛ fɔ̃ e-Naanyo ni fe fɛɛ ni ji Yehowa lɛ nɔ, ni no hã efee ekãa.

18 Ani onuɔ he akɛ eshwɛ okome akɛni okɛ onanemɛi kɛ oweku lɛ teŋ jekɛ waa lɛ hewɔ? Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, ekolɛ otee oosɔmɔ yɛ he ko ni jekɛ waa, loo ehe bahia ni ofã ni oyahi he kroko. Aloo ekolɛ hela ehã ogbɔjɔ, loo awo bo tsuŋ yɛ Yehowa ni osɔmɔɔ lɛ hewɔ. Shihilɛi ni tamɔ nɛkɛ lɛ baanyɛ ahã ohe afee shoo loo onu he akɛ mɔ ko bɛ ni baanyɛ aye abua bo. Shi kaahã ohiɛ kpa nɔ kɔkɔɔkɔ akɛ oyɛ naanyo ko ni shiŋ bo. Yehowa le nɔ tuuntu mli ni otsɔɔ lɛ, ni ebaanyɛ eye ebua bo ni ebu ohe ekɔɔ he eko he ni oyɔɔ. (Lala 139:7-10) Hemɔ oye akɛ Yehowa naa bo, ni ebaaye ebua bo.
