(Kwemɔ kuku 14)
<<<P>>>
akɛ wɔbaaye mɔ ko he osheku, loo wɔkɛ mɔ ko he baake lɛ lɛ, belɛ wɔkɛ Yehowa efee ekome kɛmiitsu nii koni efee naakpɛɛ nii yɛ esafo lɛ mli.

SUƆMƆ NI JI NAAKPƐƐ NII
<<<P>>>
13 Yesu wie akɛ, nɔ titri ni mɛi kɛbaale esɛɛ-nyiɛlɔi lɛ ji suɔmɔ ni amɛsumɔsumɔɔ amɛhe lɛ. (Kanemɔ Yohane 13:35.) Kristofoi ni hi shi yɛ Yesu bɔfoi lɛ abeiaŋ lɛ sumɔsumɔɔ amɛ-he yɛ gbɛi srɔtoi anɔ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, amɛ-kɛ mɛi ni ehia amɛ lɛ ja nibii ni amɛyɔɔ lɛ. (Bɔf. 2:44, 45) Amɛsɔle amɛhã mɛi ni awa amɛyi lɛ. (Bɔf. 12:5) Amɛkwɛ awusãi kɛ yei okulafoi. (Yak. 1:27) Ni Kristofoi ni ji ohia-foi lɛ po tsu onia koni akɛyaye abua Kristo-foi krokomɛi. (2 Kor. 8:1-4) Yɛ Yesu bɔfoi lɛ abeiaŋ lɛ sɛɛ lɛ, niŋmalɔ ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Tertu-llian lɛ ŋma nɔ ni mɛi wie yɛ blema Kristofoi lɛ ahe. Naa nɔ ni ekɛɛ mɛi wie: ‘Kwɛ bɔ ni amɛsu-mɔɔ amɛhe, ni amɛmiisumɔ ni amɛgbo amɛhã amɛhe po.’

13. Mɛɛ gbɛi anɔ Kristofoi ni hi shi yɛ Yesu bɔfoi lɛ abeiaŋ lɛ sumɔsumɔɔ amɛhe? (Yohane 13:35)
<<<P>>>
14. Mɛɛ gbɛi anɔ Yehowa webii lɛ jieɔ suɔmɔ kpo ŋmɛnɛ? (Kwemɔ mfonirii lɛ hu.)
<<<P>>>
waa. Wɔshiɛɔ sane kpakpa lɛ wɔhãa mɛi ni jeee Odasefoi lɛ kɛjieɔ suɔmɔ kpo wɔtsɔɔ amɛ hu. Ni wɔtsuɔ onia kɛfiɔ shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ sɛɛ. Mɔ ko mɔ ko kpaaa wɔfai loo enyɛɛɛ wɔnɔ ni wɔtsu onia, ni abiii ni wɔkɛ nibii ni wɔyɔɔ lɛ amli mlijai nyɔŋma mli ekome ahã. Suɔmɔ hu tsirɛɔ wɔ ni wɔwaa wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ yɛ amɛfimɔ beiaŋ.* Kɛfata he lɛ, wɔbɔɔ mɔdɛŋ koni wɔye wɔbua wɔhe koni wɔya nɔ wɔfee kpoo, wɔná miishɛɛ, ni naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ wɔ kɛ Yehowa teŋ lɛ mli awa. Enɛ kɛ ŋaa ko ni bɔfo Paulo wo lɛ kpãa gbee. Ewie akɛ: “Nyɛshɛjea mɛi ni ehao lɛ amii, nyɛyea nyɛbuaa mɛi ni he waaa lɛ, nyɛtoa nyɛtsui shi nyɛhãa mɛi fɛɛ.” (1 Tes. 5:14) Yehowa ehere wɔ kɛba weku ko ni yɔɔ jeŋ fɛɛ lɛ mli. Weku nɛɛ mli bii lɛ jieɔ suɔmɔ krɔŋŋ kpo amɛtsɔɔ amɛhe kɛ mɛi krokomɛi.
<<<P>>>
15 Suɔmɔ ni Yehowa webii lɛ jieɔ lɛ kpo lɛ ji naakpɛɛ nii ejaakɛ amɛmiifee enɛ yɛ jeŋ ko ni mɛi babaoo foɔ amɛ pɛ amɛnɔ mli lɛ mli. Bɔfo Paulo ŋma akɛ, yɛ wɔgbii nɛɛ amli lɛ, mɛi baatsɔmɔ “hesulɔi, shikasulɔi, [kɛ] mɛi ni sumɔɔ shwɛmɔ-kɛ-ŋɔɔmɔ.” Amɛsumɔɔɔ
