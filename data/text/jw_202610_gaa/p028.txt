Mɛni baaye abua bo koni nibii akagbala ojwɛŋmɔ be ni okaneɔ daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ?
(Kwɛmɔ kuku 18)
<<<P>>>
yɛ mli. (Rom. 1:12) Mɛi komɛi ena akɛ, kɛ́ amɛkpe oti ko ni amɛna yɛ daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ mli amɛwo amɛyitsoŋ lɛ, eyeɔ ebuaa amɛ ni amɛsusuɔ he gbi muu lɛ fɛɛ.

14 Naa ŋaawoi krokomɛi hu ni baanyɛ aye abua bo ni oná daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ he sɛɛ jogbaŋŋ kɛmɔ shi: (1) Folɔmɔ otii ni yɔɔ mligbalamɔ lɛ mli lɛ ashishi ni ojwɛŋ nɔ. (2) Kanemɔ ŋmalɛi ni atsɛ yisɛɛ yɛ mligbalamɔ lɛ mli lɛ. (3) Gbelemɔ ŋmalɛ ni akɔ daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ kɛjɛ mli lɛ, ni okane nakai yitso muu lɛ fɛɛ. (4) Kanemɔ saji ni atsɛ yisɛɛ yɛ Niiamlitaomɔ Wolo Kɛhã Yehowa Odasefoi lɛ mli ni kɔɔ nakai gbi lɛ ŋmalɛ lɛ he lɛ. (5) Kanemɔ Buu-Mɔɔ nikasemɔ ni ajie daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ kɛjɛ mli lɛ kukuji krokomɛi lɛ. Kɛ́ ookane daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ yɛ ofoŋ nɔ lɛ, obaanyɛ okliki Buu-Mɔɔ nikasemɔ ni atsɛ yisɛɛ yɛ mligbalamɔ lɛ naagbeegbɛ lɛ koni egbele nikasemɔ lɛ ehã bo.*

16 Mɛɛ be esa akɛ okane daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ? Halamɔ be ni baahi ahã bo loo oweku lɛ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, wekui komɛi feɔ daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ dani amɛyeɔ amɛleebi niyenii, be ni amɛye amɛleebi niyenii lɛ amɛgbe naa, loo amɛfeɔ dani gbekɛbii lɛ shiɔ kɛyaa skul. Amɛkɛ Ŋmalɛi Lɛ Amli Ni Wɔɔkwɛ Daa wolo lɛ eko fɔ̃ɔ he ko ni ewaaa akɛ amɛbaana, koni amɛhiɛ akakpa nɔ akɛ amɛbaakane. Yɛ anɔkwale mli lɛ, bei komɛi lɛ, nibii ni akpaaa gbɛ nyɛɔ ebaa. Kɛ́ eba lɛ nakai lɛ, ehe baahia ni akane daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ be kroko yɛ gbi lɛ mli.
