loo amɛnifeemɔi baanyɛ agba wɔnaa, shi no tsakeee anɔkwalei ni yɔɔ Biblia lɛ mli lɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, kɛ́ wɔheɔ anɔkwalei ni yɔɔ Biblia lɛ mli lɛ wɔyeɔ lɛɛlɛŋ ni wɔyɛ nɔmimaa akɛ, Yehowa asafo lɛ tsɔɔ wɔ anɔkwalei lɛ, no lɛ, nɔ ni mɔ ko baawie loo ebaafee hãŋ wɔkpa Yehowa sɔɔmɔ.
<<<P>>>
Eji anɔkwale akɛ, suɔmɔ ni wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ jieɔ lɛ kpo kɛ amɛjeŋ ni amɛbaa jogbaŋŋ lɛ hãa wɔnáa miishɛɛ. Shi kɛ́ mɔ ko nifeemɔ gba wɔnaa lɛ, Biblia mli anɔkwalei ni wɔkase lɛ ni wɔbaajweŋ nɔ lɛ baahã wɔhemɔkɛyeli lɛ mli awa, ni ebaahã wɔhe wɔye akɛ asafo ni wɔyɔɔ mli lɛ pɛ ji asafo ni mli bii lɛ jáa Yehowa yɛ gbɛ ni esumɔɔ akɛ ajá lɛ lɛ nɔ. Ni tɔmɔi ni mɛi fioo komɛi baatɔ̃ lɛ tsakeee enɛ kɔkɔɔkɔ.
<<<P>>>
Kɛ́ wɔkaseɔ Biblia lɛ daa lɛ, wɔbaahe anɔkwalei ni wɔkase yɛ mli lɛ wɔye waa. Ni no baahã efee faŋŋ kɛhã wɔ akɛ wɔmiijá Yehowa yɛ gbɛ ni ja nɔ. Kɛ́ eba lɛ nakai lɛ, wɔhemɔkɛyeli lɛ mli baawa waa ni mɛi awiemɔi loo amɛnifeemɔi hãŋ ehoso.—Lala 16:8; kwɛmɔ akrabatsa ni ji “Ota Bɔɔsu Ni Esa Akɛ Ota Mli Lɛ Mli” lɛ.

Ota Bɔɔsu Ni Esa Akɛ Ota Mli Lɛ Mli
<<<P>>>
Ŋɔɔ lɛ akɛ, ota bɔɔsu ko mli kɛmiifã gbɛ kakadaŋŋ kɛmiiya he ko ni yɔɔ fɛo. Ŋwanejee ko bɛ he akɛ, be ni nyɛje gbɛfaa lɛ shishi lɛ, omii shɛ ohe waa.
<<<P>>>
Shi be ni nyɛyaa lɛ, mɛi ni okɛyɔɔ bɔɔsu lɛ mli lɛ ateŋ mɛi komɛi fee nibii komɛi ni gba onaa. Ekolɛ amɛteŋ mɛi komɛi fee dede loo draivafonyo lɛ kɛbo yeee jogbaŋŋ. Shi no etsɔɔɔ akɛ jeee bɔɔsu ni esa akɛ ota mli lɛ mli ota lɛ. Nɔ ni he hiaa ni ofee ji ota mli kɛyashi nyɛbaashɛ he ni nyɛyaa lɛ.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ nakai gbɛ nɔŋŋ nɔ lɛ, wɔ fɛɛ wɔmiifã gbɛ kɛmiiya jeŋ hee ni Yehowa ewo he shi lɛ mli, ni eŋɔɔ wɔnaa waa akɛ wɔyɛ esafo lɛ mli. Shi bei komɛi lɛ, nyɛmimɛi lɛ ateŋ mɛi komɛi baafee nɔ ko ni baagba wɔnaa loo nyɛmi ko ni wɔbuɔ lɛ waa lɛ baafee nɔ ko ni ehiii kwraa, ni no baahã wɔnaa akpɛ wɔhe. Fɛɛ sɛɛ lɛ, kɛ́ wɔɔkɛɛ lɛ, wɔta bɔɔsu ni kɛwɔ baaya jeŋ hee lɛ mli lɛ mli, ni tsɔɔ akɛ, wɔyɛ asafo pɛ ni kɛwɔ baaya jeŋ hee lɛ mli lɛ mli. No hewɔ lɛ, kɛ́ wɔheɔ anɔkwalei ni wɔkase yɛ Biblia lɛ mli lɛ wɔyeɔ waa, ni wɔyɛ nɔmimaa akɛ Yehowa asafo lɛ ni etsɔɔ wɔ anɔkwalei nɛɛ lɛ, wɔbaatsa wɔgbɛfaa lɛ nɔ. Ni nɔ ko nɔ ko hosoŋ wɔhemɔkɛyeli lɛ.
