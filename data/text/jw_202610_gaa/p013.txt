<<<P>>>
lɛ, mɛi aaafee 5,000 ni sɔmɔɔ Yehowa yɛ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ. Shi kɛjɛ 1914 kɛbaa nɛɛ, mɛi fioo nɛɛ ni atsɛ amɛ yɛ Yesaia wolo lɛ mli akɛ “mɔ bibioo lɛ” ayi etee nɔ efa, ni amrɔ nɛɛ amɛyi fa fe miliɔn nɛɛhu. Kɛ́ wɔkɛbaato he lɛ, maji ni fa fe 130 sɔŋŋ yɛ ni mɛi ni yɔɔ amɛmli lɛ ayibɔ shɛɛɛ miliɔn nɛɛhu po. Ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, ayɛ mɛi pii ni le Yehowa ni sumɔɔ lɛ yɛ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ fe bei fɛɛ ni eho. Amɛyɛ shikpɔji 240 anɔ. Eji anɔkwale akɛ, kɛ́ akɛ Yehowa Odasefoi to jamɔi pii amli bii ahe lɛ, Odasefoi lɛ ayi faaa. Fɛɛ sɛɛ lɛ, eyɛ faŋŋ akɛ, gbalɛ ni yɔɔ Yesaia 60:22 ni kɔɔ Yehowa webii lɛ ahe lɛ eba mli. Hɛɛ! “Mɔ bibioo lɛ” ebatsɔ “maŋ ni he wa” lɛɛlɛŋ.

4 Sɔlemɔi pii amli bii fa ejaakɛ amɛbaptisiɔ abifabii, shi wɔ lɛ, wɔfeee nakai. Kɛfata he lɛ, wɔhãaa mɛi ni ehiii shi yɛ Yehowa gbɛtsɔɔmɔi anaa lɛ abatsɔmɔ Odasefoi loo wɔtsɔɔɔ akɛ mɛi baanyɛ amɛhala gbɛtsɔɔmɔi nɛɛ ateŋ nɔ ni amɛbaasumɔ ni amɛhi shi yɛ naa koni no ahã mɛi pii abatsɔmɔ Odasefoi. Moŋ lɛ, dani wɔbaatsɔmɔ Odasefoi lɛ, wɔteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ kaseɔ Biblia lɛ, ni wɔfeɔ tsakemɔi ni baahã wɔnyɛ wɔhi shi yɛ nɔ ni Biblia lɛ kɛɔ lɛ naa. Yɛ wɔteŋ mɛi pii agbɛfaŋ lɛ, enɛ bi ni wɔfee tsakemɔi wuji yɛ wɔsusumɔi kɛ wɔnifeemɔi amli. (1 Kor. 6:9-11) No hãa akpɛlɛɔ wɔnɔ akɛ shiɛlɔi, ni wɔnyɛɔ wɔshiɛɔ wɔhãa mɛi koni amɛ hu amɛbale Yehowa ni amɛjá lɛ. No sɛɛ lɛ, wɔjɔɔ wɔhe nɔ wɔhãa Yehowa, ni tsɔɔ akɛ, wɔwoɔ lɛ shi akɛ wɔbaafee esuɔmɔnaa nii ekɔɔɔ he eko nɔ ni baaba. Kɛkɛ lɛ, abaptisi wɔ.—Mat. 28:19, 20.

5 Wɔshweremɔ lɛ ji naakpɛɛ nii ejaakɛ Yehowa pɛ ji mɔ ni baanyɛ ahã eba. Nɔ ko ni Yesu wie lɛ tsɔɔ akɛ ebɛ mlɛo akɛ mɔ ko baatsɔ ekaselɔ. Ewie akɛ: “Kɛ́ mɔ ko miisumɔ akɛ enyiɛ misɛɛ lɛ, ekwa ehe ni ewó epiŋmɔ tso lɛ ni eya nɔ enyiɛ misɛɛ.” (Mat. 16:24) Enɛ tsɔɔ akɛ, kɛ́ mɔ ko miitao etsɔ Yesu sɛɛnyiɛlɔ lɛ, esa akɛ ená he miishɛɛ akɛ, ebaana nɔ loo ebaagbo po yɛ hemɔkɛyeli ni eyɔɔ yɛ Yesu mli lɛ hewɔ.
