KPEEI NI WO NYƆŊMƆ WEBII LƐ HEWALƐ NI AMƐSHIƐ WAA
<<<P>>>
Yɛ May naagbeegbɛ lɛ, afee kpee ko yɛ London, yɛ England, ni Biblia Kaselɔi lɛ ateŋ mɛi pii tee eko. No mli lɛ, nitsumɔhei ni ka-laa woji yɛ Britain lɛ fɛɛ eŋamɔ amɛnitsumɔ-hei lɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, nyɛmimɛi ni yɔɔ Brooklyn Betel lɛ tsu gbee mi magbe bo koni amɛnyɛ amɛkala broshuɔ ko ni wie Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛtsɔɔ-mɔ kɛhã ewebii lɛ he. Ajara broshuɔ nɛɛ ahã mɛi ni tee kpee lɛ.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ Hɔɔ, May 29 lɛ, nyɛmimɛi lɛ kɛ broshuɔ lɛ bɔi nitsumɔ. Aŋma yɛ kpee lɛ program kɛhã nakai gbi lɛ mli akɛ: “Wɔmiifɔ̃ Biblia Kaselɔ fɛɛ Biblia Kaselɔ ni yɔɔ biɛ lɛ nine ni eshiɛ Yesu kɛ Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ etsɔɔ mɛi. Leebi mra lɛ, nyɛyaa Alexandra Palace, ni nyɛmi-mɛi ni yɔɔ jɛmɛ lɛ baakɛɛ nyɛ nɔ ni nyɛfee.”
<<<P>>>
Ajara woji ni fa fe 100,000 sɔŋŋ. July 15, 1926 Buu-Mɔɔ lɛ bɔ amaniɛ akɛ: “Nyɛmimɛi lɛ ná miishɛɛ waa. Amɛnu he akɛ, amɛbo Yehowa famɔ akɛ amɛye ehe odase lɛ toi.”
<<<P>>>
Enɔ jetsɛremɔ lɛ, Nyɛminuu Rutherford hã wiemɔ ko yɛ Royal Albert Hall lɛ, ni ewie nɔ ni baaba je lɛŋ nɔyelii ni yɔɔ hewalɛ waa lɛ anɔ lɛ he. Be ni agbele shinai lɛ sɛɛ minitii fioo pɛ lɛ, mɛi 13,000 bayi asa lɛ nɔ obɔ. Mɛi ni náaa gbɛ yɛ asa lɛ nɔ lɛ bo toi yɛ agbo lɛ naa.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ Nyɛminuu Rutherford wiemɔ lɛ mli lɛ, ewie akɛ, adesai miina nɔ ejaakɛ nɔyelii lɛ ekpoo mɔ ni Yehowa efɔ lɛ mu akɛ Maŋtsɛ lɛ kɛ e-Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ. Nyɛminuu Rutherford wie eshi Jeŋmaji Akpanŋmɔ (League of Nations) lɛ ni ewie akɛ abaagu lɛ.

AYASHIƐ YƐ INDIA
<<<P>>>
Be ni Nyɛminuu Rutherford yɔɔ London yɛ kpee lɛ hewɔ lɛ, ekɛ Edwin Skinner, ni ji gbɛ-gbalɔ ko ni jɛ Sheffield, yɛ England lɛ kpe. Be ni agbe kpee lɛ naa lɛ, Nyɛminuu Skinner ku esɛɛ etee Sheffield. Shi Nyɛminuu Ruth-erford kɛɛ lɛ ni ebana lɛ yɛ London.
<<<P>>>
Nyɛminuu Skinner wie akɛ: “Minu he akɛ ebaakɛɛ mi akɛ miya maŋ kroko nɔ ni miya-shiɛ.” Ewie hu akɛ, “Be ni minine shɛ eshɛɛ lɛ nɔ lɛ, enɔ jetsɛremɔ nɔŋŋ lɛ, mitee London. . . . No mli lɛ, mifee mijwɛŋmɔ akɛ ké ekɛɛ miyasɔmɔ yɛ maŋsɛɛ lɛ, mishashaooo shi kwraa, makɛɛ lɛ akɛ, ‘Yoo maya.’ ” Ni nakai pɛpɛɛpɛ Nyɛminuu Rutherford kɛɛ lɛ. Ebi lɛ
