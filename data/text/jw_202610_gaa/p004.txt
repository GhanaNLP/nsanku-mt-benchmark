Ahala nyɛmi nuu ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ George Wright lɛ ni efata Edwin he kɛya India, ni yɛ otsii etɛ pɛ sɛɛ yɛ July mli lɛ, amɛta mee-le mli kɛtee. Nyɛminuu Skinner sɔmɔ akɛ ni-tsumɔhe nine sɔɔlɔ, ni ekwɛ shiɛmɔ nitsu-mɔ lɛ nɔ yɛ Afghanistan, Burma (Myanmar), Ceylon (Sri Lanka), India, kɛ Persia (Iran). Nyɛminuu Skinner ji Kristofonyo ni afɔ lɛ mu, ni esɔmɔ yɛ India afii 64 sɔŋŋ kɛyashi egbo yɛ afi 1990.

PILGRIMS LƐ NYIƐ HIƐ YƐ SHIƐMƆ NITSUMƆ LƐ MLI
<<<P>>>
Kɛjɛ aaafee 1894 lɛ, asafo lɛ tsuɔ najiaŋ-damɔlɔi komɛi koni amɛkɛ Biblia Kaselɔi akui srɔtoi lɛ ayakpe. Yɛ July 1926 lɛ, nitsumɔ ni najiaŋdamɔlɔi nɛɛ ni atsɛɔ amɛ pilgrims lɛ tsuɔ lɛ tsake. Pilgrims lɛ anitsumɔ lɛ tamɔ nitsumɔ ni kpokpaa nɔkwɛlɔi tsuɔ ŋmɛnɛ lɛ nɔŋŋ. No mli lɛ, amɛkɛ gbi kome saraa kui lɛ, shi kɛjɛ nakai nyɔɔŋ lɛ nɔ kɛyaa lɛ, amɛ-kɛ gbii enyɔ baasara kui lɛ, ni abi ni kɛhooo kwraa lɛ, amɛkɛ ŋmɛlɛtswai 100 atsu nii yɛ shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ mli daa nyɔɔŋ.
<<<P>>>
Nyɛminuu George Draper ni eji pilgrim lɛ ŋma wolo kɛyahã asafo lɛ. Ewie akɛ esa akɛ nyɛmimɛi lɛ fɛɛ, ni pilgrims lɛ hu fata he lɛ afee klalo akɛ amɛbaabɔ mɛi kɔkɔ. Nɔ ni baaye abua amɛ ni amɛfee nakai ji ni amɛ-baakase Buu-Mɔɔ lɛ. Nyɛminuu Draper ji Kristofonyo ni afɔ lɛ mu, ni egbo 1938.

KPEE WULU NI AFEE YƐ NEW YORK CITY LƐ
<<<P>>>
Afee naagbee kpee wulu ni afee yɛ afi lɛ mli lɛ yɛ October 14-18, 1926, ni afee yɛ New York City. Adafitswaa nitsumɔhei ni yɔɔ maŋ lɛ mli lɛ kpɛlɛ akɛ amɛbaatswa wiemɔ ni yitso ji, “Etsɛŋ, Abaawu Shikpɔŋ Lɛ Nɔ Ta Ni Fe Fɛɛ Lɛ” ni Nyɛminuu Rutherford baahã lɛ he adafi yɛ amɛdafitswaa woji lɛ amli. Shi be ni fɔmɔ eshɛ lagbaŋ et̃ã lɛ, adafitswaa ni-tsumɔhei wuji lɛ ekomɛi kɛɛ amɛtswaŋ kpee lɛ he adafi dɔŋŋ.
<<<P>>>
Nyɛmimɛi lɛ kpɛ mli akɛ amɛbaahã mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ale nɔ ni adafitswaa nitsumɔhei lɛ fee lɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, yɛ Sohaa, October 15 lɛ, Nyɛmi-nuu R. J. Martin jie trati hee ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Against the People lɛ kpo. Afee trati nɛɛ koni akɛhã mɛi ale nibii gbohii ni adafi-tswaa nitsumɔhei lɛ fee lɛ, ni agbɛnɛ hu akɛ-tswa Nyɛminuu Rutherford wiemɔ lɛ he ada-fi. Nyɛminuu Martin wie akɛ, “Wɔ diɛŋtsɛ wɔbɔi wɔwoji lɛ kalamɔ, ni wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ baatsu nii waa kɛyashi abaakala trati nɛɛ ekomekomei 1,250,000 sɔŋŋ. No sɛɛ lɛ, wɔ-baakɛɛ nyɛmimɛi ni eba kpee lɛ ni amɛyaja-ra amɛhã mɛi.” Be ni akpa leebi kpee lɛ, nyɛ-mimɛi lɛ bɔi trati lɛ jaa. Ni be ni shɛɔ gbɛkɛ lɛ, amɛjara tratii 300,000.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ otsi lɛ naagbee mli lɛ, nyɛmimɛi lɛ jara tratii lɛ pii fe nɔ ni amɛjara Sohaa lɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, kɛ́ abu fɛɛ naa lɛ, amɛjara tratii ni fa fe miliɔn kome! Kɛfata he lɛ, shiɛlɔi ni yɔɔ ekãa nɛɛ jara Deliverance wolo lɛ 3,783 kɛ woji bibii 35,140.
