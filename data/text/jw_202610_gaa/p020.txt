‘Ani miiye miibua mibii lɛ koni amɛle nɔ ni esa akɛ amɛfee tamɔ be ni abi amɛ ni amɛŋa aflaŋaa nɛkɛ? Ani miitsɔse amɛ koni kɛ́ amɛkɛkai ni baanyɛ ahã amɛfee efɔŋ kpe lɛ amɛkpɛ yiŋ kpakpai? Ani miitsɔɔ amɛ nɔ ni esa akɛ amɛfee kɛ́ amɛkɛ nibii ni esaaa kpe yɛ Intanɛt lɛ nɔ? Ani miye mibua amɛ koni amɛhemɔkɛyeli lɛ mli awa ni amɛya nɔ amɛye Yehowa anɔkwa yɛ amanehulu kpeteŋkpele lɛ mli?’ Yaa nɔ owa obii lɛ ni amɛsumɔ Yehowa waa, ni amɛkɛ amɛhiɛ afɔ̃ enɔ. Te obaafee enɛ ohã tɛŋŋ? Okɛ obii lɛ afɔ saji ni kɔɔ Yehowa he lɛ gbaa. Wo amɛ hewalɛ ni amɛhã saji ahetoo yɛ asafoŋ kpeei ashishi, ni okɛ amɛ afɔ shiɛmɔyaa hu. Tamɔ bɔ ni Yeremia fee nɔkwɛmɔnɔ kpakpa ehã Daniel lɛ, nakai nɔŋŋ wɔ mɛi ni wɔsumɔɔ Yehowa lɛ baanyɛ afee nɔkwɛmɔnɔ kpakpa wɔhã mɛi ni darako lɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, ná dekã ohã amɛ ni okɛ amɛ afee onanemɛi.

NANEMƐI KPAКPAI YEƆ AMƐBUAA WƆ KONI WƆYA NƆ WƆFEE NƆ NI JA
<<<P>>>
9 Be ni amɔmɔ Daniel kɛ oblahii bibii krokomɛi lɛ kɛtee Babilon lɛ, amɛkɛ shihilɛi ni baanyɛ ahã amɛfee efɔŋ yakpe. Be ni amɛyashɛ Babilon nɔŋŋ lɛ, Babilonbii lɛ bɔ mɔdɛŋ koni amɛhã oblahii bibii nɛɛ afee amɛnii tamɔ amɛ. Agbɛnɛ hu, atsɔse oblahii bibii nɛɛ koni amɛsɔmɔ yɛ maŋtsɛ lɛ shĩa lɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, ehe bahia ni amɛkwɛ jogbaŋŋ koni amɛkafee nɔ ko ni Yehowa sumɔɔɔ.
<<<P>>>
10 Shishijee lɛ, nɔ ko ni abi ni amɛfee lɛ tamɔ nɔ ni ejeee nɔ ko. Maŋtsɛ lɛ fã koni ajɛ eniyenii srɔtoi lɛ amli ahã Daniel kɛ oblahii bibii krokomɛi lɛ niyenii. Amɛle akɛ esaaa akɛ amɛyeɔ niyenii nɛɛ ekomɛi ejaakɛ nakai niyenii lɛ ahe tseee yɛ Yehowa hiɛ. (Dan. 1:5, 8)
<<<P>>>
Kulɛ, amɛbaanyɛ amɛkɛɛ akɛ, ‘Enɛ jeee nɔ ko. Wɔ lɛ, maŋtsɛ lɛ nyɔji ji wɔ, ni no hewɔ lɛ esa akɛ wɔfee nɔ ni ekɛɔ lɛ. Agbɛnɛ hu, kɛ́ wɔfeee nɔ ni maŋtsɛ lɛ kɛɛ lɛ, emli baafu wɔ, ni wɔsane lɛ baafite kwraa.’ Ehe bahia ni amɛkpɛ amɛyiŋ kɛ́ji amɛbaabo maŋtsɛ lɛ toi loo amɛboŋ lɛ toi.
<<<P>>>
11 No mli lɛ, aatsɔse oblahii bibii pii ni jɛ Yuda lɛ yɛ Babilon. Shi amɛteŋ mɛi ejwɛ pɛ Biblia lɛ wie akɛ amɛtee nɔ amɛye Yehowa anɔkwa. Amɛji Daniel, Hanania, Mishael, kɛ Azaria. Nanemɛi ejwɛ nɛɛ ye amɛbua amɛhe koni amɛya nɔ amɛfee nɔ ni ja ni amɛbo Yehowa toi. Amɛtee nɔ amɛye Yehowa anɔkwa ejaakɛ amɛyeee maŋtsɛ lɛ niyenii lɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, Yehowa jɔɔ amɛ.—Dan. 1:6-12, 17.
<<<P>>>
12 Akɛ oblanyo loo oblayoo lɛ, kɛ́ oblahii loo oblayei krokomɛi kɛɛ bo ni ofee efɔŋ lɛ, ani bei komɛi lɛ onuɔ he akɛ obo amɛtoi? Daniel kɛ enanemɛi lɛ ye amɛbua amɛhe ni amɛkpɛ yiŋ kpakpai. Tamɔ bɔ ni amɛfee lɛ, bo hu halamɔ nanemɛi ni etswa amɛfai shi akɛ amɛbaaya nɔ amɛye Yehowa anɔkwa ni sumɔŋ ni je lɛ aná amɛnɔ hewalɛ. (1 Yoh. 2:15-17) Nanemɛi kpakpai baanyɛ aye abua bo ni oye anɔkwa yɛ “nɔ ko bibioo kwraa” mli po koni Yehowa ajɔɔ bo ni eye ebua bo. (Luka 16:10) Ni kɛ́ obo Yehowa toi yɛ nibii ni etamɔ nɔ ni amɛjeee nɔ ko lɛ amli lɛ, no baaye abua bo ni oye anɔkwa yɛ shihilɛi ni mli wawai amli.
