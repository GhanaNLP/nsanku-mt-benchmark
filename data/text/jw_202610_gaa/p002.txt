AFII 100 NI EHO NƐ
<<<P>>>
ABƆ amaniɛ yɛ January 1, 1926 Buu-Mɔɔ lɛ mli akɛ: “Etsɛŋ, Nyɔŋmɔ baawo egbéi lɛ hiɛ nyam. Ebaaná mɛi ni baaye ehe odase.”
<<<P>>>
Yɛ afi 1926 mli fɛɛ lɛ, Yehowa tsɔ Yesu Kristo ni ji asafo lɛ yitso lɛ nɔ eye ebua Kris-tofoi koni amɛnu shishi akɛ esa akɛ shiɛmɔ ni amɛbaashiɛ lɛ afee nitsumɔ ni he hiaa fe fɛɛ yɛ amɛshihilɛ mli. No hewɔ lɛ, “nyɔŋ anɔ-kwafo kɛ hiɛshikalɔ lɛ” fee woji hee, etsake bɔ ni afeɔ asafoŋ kpeei ahãa, ni egbála ni-bii amli jogbaŋŋ koni Kristofoi fɛɛ ana nɔ ni esa akɛ amɛfee. (Mat. 24:45) Yesu Kristo ye ebua amɛ koni amɛná tsũi kɛ woji akala-mɔ tsɔji ni baaye abua amɛ koni amɛshiɛ.

AFEE WOLO HEE KO, NI ATSAKE BƆ NI WƆFEƆ ASAFOŊ KPEEI WƆHÃA LƐ
<<<P>>>
Yɛ April mli lɛ, atswa adafi yɛ The Golden Age (ni amrɔ nɛɛ lɛ atsɛɔ lɛ Awake!) lɛ mli akɛ afee wolo hee ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Deliverance. No mli lɛ, wolo titri ni akɛtsuɔ nii yɛ shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ mli ji Studies in the Scriptures, shi akɛ wolo hee nɛɛ baye no najiaŋ. Bɔ ni afee ni anyɛ akala wolo hee nɛɛ pii lɛ, nyɛmi-mɛi ni yɔɔ Betel lɛ he woji akalamɔ tsɔne hee ko. Tsɔne nɛɛ nyɛɔ ekalaa woji 22 yɛ mi-niti kome pɛ mli. Biblia Kaselɔi lɛ ja wolo nɛɛ amɛhã mɛi fɛɛ ni amɛshiɛ amɛtsɔɔ amɛ lɛ.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ afii ni eho lɛ amli lɛ, Biblia Kaselɔi lɛ kase wolo ni ji Studies in the Scriptures lɛ yɛ amɛsafoŋ kpeei ashishi. Shi kɛjɛ May lɛ, amɛkase saji komɛi ni yɔɔ Buu-Mɔɔ mli. Mɛɛ yibii tsakemɔ nɛɛ wo?
<<<P>>>
Asafo ko ŋma Nyɛminuu Rutherford akɛ: ‘The little Bunker Hill asafo ni yɔɔ Illinois lɛ miiŋa bo. Hɔgbaa fɛɛ Hɔgbaa lɛ, wɔkaseɔ sane ni asafo lɛ ejie yi akɛ wɔkase nakai Hɔgbaa lɛ. Eŋɔɔ wɔnaa akɛ wɔnuɔ saji hei ni akɛhãa wɔ lɛ shishi oya nɔŋŋ.’
<<<P>>>
Sane hee loo shishinumɔ hee ni akɛhã amɛ yɛ nakai beiaŋ lɛ ye ebua amɛ ni amɛkɛ hiɛdɔɔ shiɛ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, Buu-Mɔɔ mli sane ko tsɔɔ mli akɛ mɔdɛŋ ni wɔbaabɔ koni wɔná su kpakpa, ni agbɛnɛ hu wɔkɛ mɛi aye jogbaŋŋ ni wɔjie bulɛ kpo wɔtsɔɔ amɛ lɛ jeee nɔ ni he hiaa fe fɛɛ. Atsɔɔ mli yɛ nakai sane lɛ mli akɛ, Yehowa efã Kristofoi koni amɛye ehe odase. Kristofoi sumɔɔ Yehowa, no hewɔ lɛ, esa akɛ amɛteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ aye famɔ nɛɛ nɔ ni ená he miishɛɛ akɛ ebaashiɛ.
<<<P>>>
Buu-Mɔɔ lɛ mli sane kroko ko hu tsɔɔ mli akɛ, gbɛ ni anɔkwa Kristofoi tsɔɔ nɔ amɛ-tsɔɔ akɛ amɛsumɔɔ Yehowa ji, ni amɛbaa-sɔmɔ lɛ jogbaŋŋ. Yɛ nakai sane lɛ mli lɛ, awo Biblia Kaselɔi lɛ fɛɛ hewalɛ ni amɛshiɛ waa bɔ ni amɛbaanyɛ.—Yes. 43:10, 12; Mat. 24:14.
<<<P>>>
Biblia Kaselɔi lɛ fee tsakemɔi nɛɛ amrɔ nɔŋŋ. Be ni Buu-Mɔɔ lɛ mli saji nɛɛ je kpo lɛ sɛɛ otsii komɛi lɛ, asafo ko ŋma lɛta kɛ-maje Betel. Naa saji ni aŋmala yɛ mli lɛ eko-mɛi: “Saji ni je kpo yɛ Buu-Mɔɔ lɛ ekomɛi amli nyɛsɛɛ nɛɛ shɛje wɔmii jogbaŋŋ ni ewo
