G. Kpee ni afee yɛ Madison Square Garden yɛ New York, yɛ October 1926 lɛ
<<<P>>>
Yɛ Hɔgbaa, October 17 shwane be ni atswa ŋmɛji etɛ lɛ, mɛi aaafee 12,000 bo wiemɔ ni Nyɛminuu Rutherford hã lɛ toi. Be ni egbe ewiemɔ lɛ naa lɛ, mɛi komɛi ni nya sane lɛ he lɛ bibii saji. No hewɔ lɛ, ajara woji ni fa fe 9,000, ni kɛbashi nakai beiaŋ lɛ, no ji woji ni fa fe fɛɛ ni aja yɛ kpee shishi.

AMA TSŨ HEE YƐ ADAMS STREET
<<<P>>>
No mli lɛ, mɛi babaoo miitao wɔwoji lɛ, ni no hewɔ lɛ, nyɛmimɛi ni nyiɛ hiɛ ni yɔɔ Brooklyn lɛ na akɛ ehe baahia ni aná he ko ni da ni akɛkala woji lɛ. Yɛ afii ni eho lɛ amli lɛ, amɛhai tsũ ni yɔɔ Concord Street lɛ, shi amɛna akɛ tsũ nɛɛ edaaa hãaa nitsumɔ lɛdɔŋŋ. No hewɔ lɛ, nyɛmimɛi lɛ hé shikpɔŋ ko yɛ Adams Street ni kɛ tsũ ni yɔɔ Concord Street lɛ teŋ jekɛɛɛ. Amɛbɔi ŋwɛitsu nsɔɔdoi kpaanyɔ maa yɛ shikpɔŋ ni amɛhé lɛ nɔ. Tsũ nɛɛ baahã amɛná he ko ni da waa ni amɛkɛbaakala woji lɛ.
<<<P>>>
Ama tsũ ni yɔɔ Adams Street lɛ agbe naa yɛ 1927, ni eye ebua nyɛmimɛi lɛ waa. Kɛjɛ 1927 kɛyaa lɛ, mɛi babaoo bafata Biblia Kaselɔi lɛ ahe ni atee nɔ afee tsakemɔi yɛ asafo lɛ mli, ni no hã yɛ 1931 lɛ, amɛfee mɛi ni sa akɛ amɛbaaŋɔ gbéi ni ji Yehowa Odasefoi lɛ amɛfɔ̃ amɛnɔ.
