3 Ké oblanyo loo oblayoo ji bo lɛ, mɛɛ gbɛ nɔ nanemɛi kpakpai baanyɛ awa bo ni osɔmɔ Yehowa jogbaŋŋ? Mɛni hewɔ ofɔlɔi kɛ mɛi krokomɛi ni sumɔɔ Yehowa lɛ baanyɛ afee nanemɛi kpakpai kɛhã bo? Amrɔ nɛɛ wɔbaasusu gbalɔ Daniel sane lɛ he kɛ bɔ ni enyɛ etsu naagbai komɛi ni etamɔ nɔ ni anyɛŋ atsu amɛhe nii lɛ ahe nii. Agbɛnɛ hu, wɔbaasusu bɔ ni Daniel naanyo ni fe fɛɛ ni ji Yehowa lɛ ye ebua lɛ be ni efee tamɔ nɔ ni eshwɛ ekome too lɛ he.

NANEMƐI KPAKPAI BAAWA BO KONI OSƆMƆ YEHOWA
<<<P>>>
4 Be ni Daniel ji oblanyo fioo lɛ, no mli lɛ shihilɛ ni yɔɔ Yuda maŋtsɛyeli lɛ mli lɛ ehiii kwraa. Yehowa náaa Yuda maŋtsɛyeli lɛ he miishɛɛ ejaakɛ emli bii lɛ ateŋ mɛi babaoo ekpa lɛ sɔɔmɔ. No hewɔ lɛ, yɛ afi 620 D.Ŋ.B. lɛ, Yehowa ŋmɛ gbɛ ni Nebukadnezar, ni ji Babilon maŋtsɛ lɛ, ye amɛnɔ. (Dan. 1:2) Eenyɛ efee akɛ be ni enɛ ba lɛ, no mli lɛ Daniel eye afii 13 kɛmiimɔ afii 19. Aaafee afii etɛ sɛɛ lɛ, Babilon asraafoi lɛ bamɔmɔ Yudabii pii kɛtee Babilon, ni Daniel kɛ eweku lɛ fata he. No sɛɛ lɛ, akɛ Daniel tee Babilon maŋtsɛ lɛ shĩa lɛ koni ayatsɔse lɛ. (Dan. 1:3-6) No hewɔ lɛ, Daniel kɛ emaŋ lɛ teŋ bajekɛ, ni ekɛ eweku lɛ ehiii shi dɔŋŋ.

5 Mɛni ye ebua Daniel ni etee nɔ eye Yehowa anɔkwa yɛ Babilon? Biblia lɛ wie akɛ, Daniel “kpɛ mli yɛ etsui mli” akɛ ebaaye Yehowa mlai lɛ anɔ. (Dan. 1:8) Te fee tɛŋŋ ni Daniel le mlai nɛɛ? Namɛi ye amɛbua lɛ ni ekpɛ eyiŋ akɛ ebaasɔmɔ Yehowa be ni edako lɛ? Ekã shi faŋŋ akɛ, efɔlɔi. Mɛni hewɔ wɔkɛɔ nakai? Amɛwo lɛ Daniel, ni eshishi ji “Mikojolɔ Ji Nyɔŋmɔ.” Gbɛi Daniel ni amɛwo lɛ lɛ tsɔɔ akɛ, amɛmiisumɔ ni etsɔ Yehowa jálɔ.—5 Mo. 6:6, 7.
<<<P>>>
6 Agbɛnɛ hu, ekolɛ Yeremia nɔkwɛmɔnɔ kpakpa lɛ ná Daniel nɔ hewalɛ. Mɛni hewɔ nilɛe yɔɔ mli akɛ wɔbaawie nakai? Be ni afɔ Daniel lɛ, no mli lɛ Yeremia ji Yehowa gbalɔ, ni Yehowa tsuɔ lɛ kɛyaa Yuda maŋtsɛ lɛ kɛ odehei awekui ni yɔɔ Yuda lɛ aŋɔɔ koni eyakɛɛ amɛ ewiemɔ lɛ. Yeremia je enɛ shishi yɛ afi 647 D.Ŋ.B., ni ekã shi faŋŋ akɛ, Daniel weku lɛ fata wekui ni atsu lɛ amɛŋɔɔ lɛ ahe. (Yer. 1:2, 17, 18; Dan. 1:3, 6) Eyɛ mli akɛ wɔnyɛŋ wɔwie kɛ nɔmimaa akɛ Yeremia kɛ Daniel kpe loo amɛbatsɔmɔ nanemɛi moŋ, shi ŋwanejee ko bɛ he akɛ Daniel le Yeremia gbalɛi lɛ. Be ni Daniel gbɔ po lɛ, ekane Yeremia gbalɛi lɛ. (Yer. 25:11; 29:10; Dan. 9:1, 2) Ekã shi faŋŋ akɛ, Yeremia nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ hã Daniel na akɛ ebaanyɛ efee ekãa, ni eya nɔ eye Yehowa anɔkwa ké eyɛ mɛi ni sɔmɔɔ Yehowa lɛ ateŋ po.
