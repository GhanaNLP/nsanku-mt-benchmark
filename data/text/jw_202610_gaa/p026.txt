7 Wɔkaneɔ daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ ejaakɛ kɛ́ wɔfee nakai lɛ etsɔɔ akɛ wɔkɛ wɔbe miitsu nii jogbaŋŋ. (Kanemɔ Efesobii 5:15, 16.) Ehi waa akɛ wɔbaahe be fioo daa gbi ni wɔkɛkase bɔ ni Yehowa, wɔ-Tsɛ ni yɔɔ suɔmɔ lɛ, susuɔ nii ahe ehãa, ni wɔhã no aná wɔnɔ hewalɛ. Nɔ ko bɛ ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔfee daa gbi ni hi fe no.
<<<P>>>
8 Wɔkaneɔ daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ ejaakɛ eyeɔ ebuaa wɔ koni wɔnyɛ wɔdamɔ naagbai ni wɔkɛkpeɔ daa gbi lɛ anaa. Yesu wie akɛ “gbi fɛɛ gbi kɛ emli naagbai.” (Mat. 6:34) Yesu le akɛ wɔkɛ naagbai pii baakpe. Shi ekɛɛ wɔ hu akɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔná nɔmimaa akɛ Yehowa le shihilɛ ni wɔtsɔɔ mli lɛ kɛ nɔ ni he hiaa wɔ. (Mat. 6:25-32) Kɛ́ wɔkane daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ, eyeɔ ebuaa wɔ ni wɔkajwɛŋ wɔnaagbai lɛ ahe fe nine. Moŋ lɛ, ehãa wɔkɛ wɔjwɛŋmɔ maa nibii ni Yehowa miifee kɛmiiye eebua wɔ lɛ nɔ waa. (Lala 68:19; 1 Pet. 5:6, 7) Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, nyɛmi yoo ko ni eye afii 19 lɛ hao waa yɛ esha ko ni efee be ko ni eho lɛ hewɔ, ejaakɛ ebɛ nɔmimaa akɛ Yehowa kɛke lɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, esɔle ni ebi Yehowa ni ehã ená nɔmimaa akɛ ekɛke lɛ. Kɛkɛ ni ekane daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ. Ŋmalɛ lɛ ji Lala 86:5, ni jɛmɛ kaneɔ akɛ: “Bo Yehowa, ohi ni osumɔɔ waa akɛ okɛ esha ake; osuɔmɔ ni tsakeee lɛ fa babaoo hã mɛi fɛɛ ni boɔ tsɛɔ bo lɛ.” Ewie akɛ: “Amrɔ nɔŋŋ lɛ, mitsui nyɔ mimli, ni mida Yehowa shi waa akɛ ehã mina akɛ esumɔɔ mi. Nakai niiashikpamɔ lɛ wo mi hewalɛ ni makane daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ daa gbi.”
<<<P>>>
9 Bei pii lɛ, daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ kaiɔ wɔ ni wɔkɛ wɔhiɛ afɔ̃ Yehowa nɔ kɛ́ wɔkɛ naagbai miikpe. (Abɛi 3:5, 6) Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, yɛ gbi ni aje Russia kɛ Ukraine ta lɛ shishi lɛ, daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ kɔ Yesaia 30:15 lɛ fã ko he, ni ewieɔ akɛ: “Kɛji nyɛhi shi kpoo ni nyɛkɛ nyɛhiɛ fɔ̃ minɔ lɛ, nyɛbaaná hewalɛ.” Ŋmalɛ nɛɛ shɛje nyɛmimɛi ni yɔɔ Ukraine lɛ amii. Nyɛmi nuu ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Eduard lɛ wie akɛ: “Ŋmalɛ nɛɛ hã wɔná hewalɛ hee. Mi kɛ miŋa fee ekome kɛkane nakai leebi lɛ. No sɛɛ lɛ, wɔsɔle, ni wɔnu he akɛ awaje wɔ koni wɔkaye wɔtsui tsɔ.” Nyɛmi yoo ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Svitlana lɛ wie akɛ: “Yesaia 30:15 lɛ ye ebua wɔ waa. Wɔtee nɔ wɔsusu he be ni ta lɛ yaa nɔ lɛ.”

10 Wɔkaneɔ daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ ejaakɛ eyeɔ ebuaa wɔ koni wɔfi shi ni wɔya nɔ wɔye anɔkwa. Akɛ Yehowa tsuji lɛ, esa akɛ esoro wɔ yɛ mɛi ni wɔkɛyaa skul kɛ mɛi ni wɔkɛtsuɔ nii lɛ ahe. Agbɛnɛ hu, esa akɛ wɔshiɛ Biblia mli anɔkwalei lɛ wɔhã mɛi, ni wɔya nɔ wɔsɔmɔ Yehowa kɛ́ mɛi te shi amɛwo wɔ po. Ni ebaabi ni wɔná ekãa dani wɔbaanyɛ wɔfee enɛɛmɛi fɛɛ. Be ni Yehowa hala Yoshua ni enyiɛ Israelbii lɛ ahiɛ lɛ, ekɛɛ lɛ shii etɛ sɔŋŋ ni efee ekãa ni ewaje ehe. Agbɛnɛ hu, ekɛɛ Yoshua ni ekane Mla wolo lɛ daa gbi ejaakɛ no baaye abua lɛ ni efee ekãa waa. (Kanemɔ Yoshua 1:6-9.) Kɛ́ wɔkane daa gbi ŋmalɛ lɛ, kɛ agbɛnɛ hu Biblia lɛ daa gbi lɛ, ebaawaje wɔ ni wɔya nɔ wɔye anɔkwa.
