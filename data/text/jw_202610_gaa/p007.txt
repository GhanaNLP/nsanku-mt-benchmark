Mɔ ni hiɛ sɔɔ nii lɛ náa miishɛɛ waa, ni eyeee etsui tsɔ
<<<P>>>
(Kwɛmɔ kuku 1)
<<<P>>>
3 Esaaa akɛ Yehowa jálɔi náa su gbonyo nɛɛ ni mɛi babaoo jieɔ lɛ kpo lɛ eko. Bɔfo Paulo wie akɛ: “Nyɛfea nyɛhe mɛi ni le shidaa.” (Kol. 3:15) No hewɔ lɛ, kɛ́ mɔ ko fee wɔ ejurɔ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔfee nɔ ko kɛtsɔɔ akɛ wɔhiɛ esɔ nɔ ni efee ehã wɔ lɛ, shi jeee ni wɔbaaná he miishɛɛ kɛkɛ. Yɛ nikasemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaasusu sanebimɔi etɛ nɛɛ ahe: (1) Mɛni hewɔ esa akɛ wɔhiɛ asɔ nii? (2) Mɛni baaye abua wɔ ni wɔhiɛ asɔ nii waa? (3) Mɛni wɔbaanyɛ wɔfee kɛtsɔɔ akɛ wɔhiɛ sɔɔ nii?
<<<P>>>
MƐNI HEWƆ ESA AKƐ WƆHIƐ ASƆ NII?
<<<P>>>
4 Kɛ́ wɔhiɛ esɔɔɔ nii lɛ, wɔyeɔ wɔ diɛŋtsɛ wɔhe awui. Bei pii lɛ, mɛi ni hiɛ esɔɔɔ nii lɛ bɛ miishɛɛ. Biblia lɛ wie akɛ: “Nɔnalɔ gbii fɛɛ lɛ, gbii fɔji ni.” (Abɛi 15:15) Wɔ fɛɛ wɔkɛ naagbai kpeɔ ni hãa wɔnijiaŋ jeɔ wui. Eyɛ mli akɛ, jeee naagbai fɛɛ ni wɔkɛkpeɔ lɛ wɔbaanyɛ wɔfee nɔ ko yɛ he moŋ, shi wɔbaanyɛ wɔfee nɔ ko yɛ bɔ ni wɔnaa amɛ wɔhãa lɛ he. Kɛ́ wɔnaagba lɛ nɔ sɔŋŋ wɔkɛ wɔjwɛŋmɔ maa lɛ, daa nɛɛ wɔnii baafee wɔhe mɔbɔ. Kɛ́ eba lɛ nakai lɛ, wɔnaŋ nɔ ko kpakpa yɛ wɔshihilɛ mli, ni wɔmiishɛɛ baatã.
<<<P>>>
5 Kɛ́ wɔhiɛ sɔɔ nii lɛ, wɔ diɛŋtsɛ wɔnáa he sɛɛ. Kɛ́ wɔhiɛ sɔɔ nii lɛ, wɔnáa miishɛɛ. Abɛi 15:15 lɛ kɛɔ hu akɛ: “Mɔ ni yɔɔ miishɛɛ tsui lɛ, eyeɔ gbi jurɔ daa.” “Mɔ ni yɔɔ miishɛɛ tsui lɛ” kɛ ejwɛŋmɔ maa nibii kpakpai ni miiya nɔ yɛ eshihilɛ mli lɛ anɔ, ni ehiɛ sɔɔ nibii nɛɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, bei pii lɛ eye miishɛɛ. Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, enaagbai lɛ esuuu etsui tsɔ. Ewaaa akɛ “mɔ ni yɔɔ miishɛɛ tsui lɛ,” hiɛ baasɔ nii, ni no hãa enáa miishɛɛ, loo kɛ́ wɔɔkɛɛ lɛ, “eyeɔ gbi jurɔ daa.”
