Nyɔŋmɔ, ni ‘amɛbɛ suɔmɔ kɛhã amɛweku mli bii’ po. (2 Tim. 3:2-4) Yesu gba akɛ, mɛi baajiejie mɛi amɛhã. Ni ekɛfata he akɛ “mɛi pii asuɔmɔ lɛ he baajɔ.” (Mat. 24:10, 12) Nakai pɛpɛɛpɛ nibii miiya lɛ ŋmɛnɛ, aloo jeee nakai? Yehowa hewalɛ lɛ hewɔ lɛ, ewebii lɛ yaa nɔ amɛjieɔ suɔmɔ krɔŋŋ kpo amɛtsɔɔ amɛhe. Ŋwanejee ko bɛ he akɛ, eji naakpɛɛ nii ni miiya nɔ ŋmɛnɛ!

16 Kɛ́ mɛi ba amɛjeŋ jogbaŋŋ ni wɔjɛ wɔtsuiŋ wɔjie amɛyi, ni wɔbɔɔ mɔdɛŋ hu koni toiŋjɔlɛ aya nɔ ahi wɔ kɛ amɛteŋ lɛ, belɛ wɔmiijie suɔmɔ kpo ji no. (Mar. 9:50) Kɛ́ wɔyaa nɔ wɔjieɔ suɔmɔ kpo yɛ wɔwiemɔi kɛ wɔnifeemɔi amli lɛ, no hãa efeɔ faŋŋ akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ webii ji wɔ. Kɛ́ wɔjie suɔmɔ kpo lɛ, yɛ anɔkwale mli lɛ wɔkɛ Nyɔŋmɔ miifee ekome kɛmiitsu nii koni efee naakpɛɛ nii yɛ esafo lɛ mli ŋmɛnɛ.

17 Bɔ ni asafo lɛ shwereɔ, kɛ agbɛnɛ hu, ekomefeemɔ kɛ suɔmɔ ni yɔɔ wɔteŋ lɛ hãa anaa akɛ Yehowa miifee naakpɛɛ nii ŋmɛnɛ. Eyɛ mli akɛ mɛi babaoo teɔ shi amɛwoɔ nitsumɔ ni wɔtsuɔ yɛ jeŋ fɛɛ lɛ moŋ, shi fɛɛ sɛɛ lɛ, mɛi babaoo miitsɔmɔ Odasefoi. Mɛi amli egbala waa yɛ gbɛi srɔtoi anɔ yɛ jeŋ fɛɛ, shi wɔ lɛ, wɔfee ekome. Eyɛ mli akɛ mɛi babaoo asuɔmɔ lɛ he ejɔ yɛ jeŋ fɛɛ moŋ, shi wɔyaa nɔ wɔjieɔ suɔmɔ krɔŋŋ kpo wɔtsɔɔ Nyɔŋmɔ kɛ wɔhe, ni no tsɔɔ akɛ wɔji Kristo sɛɛnyiɛlɔi lɛɛlɛŋ. (Yoh. 13:34) Jeee akɛ wɔmiina naakpɛɛ nii nɛɛ kɛkɛ, moŋ lɛ, wɔhe yɛ mli, ejaakɛ “Nyɔŋmɔ nanemɛi nitsulɔi ji wɔ.”—1 Kor. 3:9.

18 Eyɛ naakpɛɛ akɛ, yɛ jeŋ ni naagbai eyi mli obɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ, Nyɔŋmɔ tsuji ni yɔɔ jeŋ fɛɛ lɛ ayi miiya nɔ eefa, amɛfee ekome, ni amɛjieɔ suɔmɔ krɔŋŋ kpo amɛtsɔɔ amɛhe. Eji naakpɛɛ nii lɛɛlɛŋ! Naakpɛɛ nii nɛɛ hãa wɔnáa nɔmimaa akɛ nibii fɛɛ ni Yehowa ewo shi akɛ ebaafee wɔsɛɛ lɛ baaba mli. (2 Pet. 3:13) Kaahã ohiɛ kpa nɔ akɛ, eji hegbɛ kpele ni oná akɛ okɛ Nyɔŋmɔ baafee ekome kɛtsu nii koni efee naakpɛɛ nii ŋmɛnɛ.
