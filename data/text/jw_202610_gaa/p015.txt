8 Be fɛɛ be ni wɔbaaye Yehowa he odase lɛ,
belɛ wɔkɛ lɛ kɛ Yesu miifee ekome kɛmiifee
naakpɛɛ nii ni ji shweremɔ lɛ. Yehowa kɛ Yesu
yeɔ amɛbuaa wɔ yɛ shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ mli.
Yesu wo wɔ shi akɛ: “Mikɛ nyɛ yɛ gbii lɛ fɛɛ kɛ-
yashi je lɛ naagbee gbii lɛ amli.” (Mat. 28:20)
Agbɛnɛ hu, ŋwɛibɔfoi lɛ yeɔ amɛbuaa wɔ yɛ
shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ mli.—Kpo. 14:6, 7.

EKOMEFEEMƆ KO NI JI NAAKPƐƐ NII
<<<P>>>
9 Gbalɔ Mika gba naakpɛɛ nii kroko ni miiya
nɔ yɛ wɔbei nɛɛ amli, ni no ji, ekomefeemɔ ni
nɔ bɛ ni yɔɔ Yehowa webii lɛ ateŋ lɛ. Yehowa
kɛ emumɔ lɛ tsirɛ Mika ni ewie hii kɛ yei komɛi
ni Yehowa baabua amɛnaa ni ehã amɛfee eko-
me kɛhi shi lɛ ahe. (Kanemɔ Mika 2:12.) Te
gbalɛ nɛɛ baa mli ŋmɛnɛ ehãa tɛŋŋ? Ŋmɛnɛ lɛ,
eyɛ mli akɛ esoro bɔ ni Yehowa webii lɛ ateŋ
mɔ fɛɛ mɔ yɔɔ kɛ he ni amɛteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ jɛ
moŋ, shi ekomefeemɔ yɛ amɛteŋ. Amɛnuɔ he
tamɔ bɔ ni David nu he lɛ. Ewie akɛ: “Kwɛ bɔ
ni ehi, kwɛ bɔ ni eyɔɔ fɛo akɛ nyɛmimɛi baafee
ekome kɛhi shi!”—Lala 133:1.
<<<P>>>
10 Ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, nibii pii yɛ ni tsɔɔ akɛ wɔfee
ekome. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, ekɔɔɔ he eko he ni
wɔyɔɔ lɛ, wɔkaseɔ Biblia mli sane kome too lɛ
nɔŋŋ yɛ asafoŋ kpeei, kpokpaa nɔ kpeei, kɛ
kpokpaa wulu nɔ kpeei ashishi. Yɛ jeŋ fɛɛ lɛ,
Odasefoi ni jɛ hei srɔtoi kɛ maji srɔtoi anɔ lɛ
feɔ ekome kɛmamɔɔ tsũi, ni agbɛnɛ hu, amɛ-
feɔ ekome kɛtsuɔ nii yɛ nitsumɔhe niji srɔtoi
amli. Wɔsumɔɔ wɔhe waa. Yɛ je lɛ mli lɛ, bei
pii lɛ, shikatsɛmɛi kɛ ohiafoi ebɔɔɔ, mɛi kɛ
mɛi ni esoro he ni amɛjɛ lɛ ebɔɔɔ, ni mɛi kɛ
mɛi ni esoro paati mli ni amɛyɔɔ lɛ hu ebɔɔɔ.
Shi nɔ ko ni tamɔ nakai bɛ Yehowa webii lɛ
ateŋ. He fɛɛ he ni wɔbaaya yɛ jeŋ fɛɛ lɛ, wɔ-
nyɛmimɛi ni jieɔ suɔmɔ kpo lɛ miisumɔ ni
amɛye amɛbua wɔ, ni agbɛnɛ hu, amɛwo wɔ
hewalɛ ni wɔya nɔ wɔsɔmɔ Yehowa. Ŋmɛnɛ
lɛ, mɛi babaoo ni jɛ hei srɔtoi miibɔ mɔdɛŋ
ni amɛsusu nii ahe tamɔ Yehowa, ni amɛfee
esuɔmɔnaa nii. Ni nɔ ko ni tamɔ nɛkɛ bako dã.

11 Ekome ni wɔfee lɛ ji naakpɛɛ nii ejaakɛ he
fɛɛ he ni obaaya yɛ je lɛ mli lɛ, mɛi amli egbala
waa. Yɛ je lɛ mli he fɛɛ he lɛ, mɛi amli egbala
yɛ gbɛi srɔtoi anɔ. Daa gbi lɛ, atswaa adafi akɛ
aanɔ, aafee basabasa, aatswa grasin tsɛnsi, aa-
tse atua, loo aawu ta yɛ heihei. Mɔbɔ sane ji
akɛ, mɛi amli egbala waa yɛ je lɛ mli, ni naa-
gbai ni yɔɔ gbeyeigbeyei eyi je lɛ mli obɔ.
Tamɔ bɔ ni Biblia lɛ gba lɛ, mɛi etsɔmɔ “mɛi
ni yeee anɔkwa, . . . mɛi ni anyɛɛɛ akɛ amɛ
afee kpaŋmɔ, mɛi ni wieɔ mɛi krokomɛi ahe
efɔŋ, mɛi ni nyɛɛɛ amɛhe nɔ amɛye, mɛi ni hiɛ
yɔɔ la, mɛi ni sumɔɔɔ ekpakpa, mɛi ni jieɔ
mɛi amɛhãa, kuɛŋtilɔi, mɛi ni naa amɛhe tsɔ.”
(2 Tim. 3:2-4) Eyɛ mli akɛ “jeŋ muu lɛ fɛɛ kã
mɔ fɔŋ lɛ [kɛ edaimonioi lɛ] hewalɛ mli” moŋ,
shi wɔyaa nɔ wɔsɔmɔɔ Yehowa kɛ miishɛɛ akɛ
weku ni efee ekome. (1 Yoh. 5:19; Kpo. 12:9)
Lɛɛlɛŋ, enɛ ji naakpɛɛ nii!
