Gana DWENLE Ɛ NWO (ƐRƐLADANE KPALƐ) NGYEHYƐLEƐ NE
<<<P>>>
21. Arane lale Gana Dwenle Ɛ nwo (ƐRƐLADANE KPALƐ) Ngyehyɛleɛ ne ali wɔ Siane Bulu nee ko kenle ne mɔɔ tɔ zolɛ abulanwiɔ wɔ ɛvolɛ 2020 ne anu. Bodane ne a le kɛ bɛbava ngyehyɛleɛ ɛhye bɛazɔ anwongyelelɛ mɔɔ Kolona Vaelɔso ewulenzane ne ɛva ɛra la anloa. Eza ɔbaboa yeamaa yɛ ezukoasɛm ayɛ boɛ na gyima gyima agyinla bɛ gyakɛ azo na Ganama anwo adɔ bɛ.
<<<P>>>
22. Gana DWENLE Ɛ NWO bodane titili ko a le kɛ ɔbaboa yeamaa menli anyia gyima ayɛ na eza arane ezukoasɛm agyinla boɛ yɛɛ gyima gyima bahɔ zo na gyimayɛvoma anyia ahatualɛ kpalɛ, bɔbɔ bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛyɛ bɛ ti anwo gyima la.
<<<P>>>
23. Gyima titili nee ngyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ bɛbayɛ wɔ Gana DWENLE Ɛ NWO Ngyehyɛleɛ ne abo la, bie a le ɛhye mɔ:
<<<P>>>
• bɛbaboa bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛyɛ egyinli mgbole la na bɛazi mgbavolɛ nee mbɛlɛra mɔɔ ɛwie sukulu na bɛnyɛ gyima la adua bɛamaa bɛava bɛ nwo bɛawula egyinli mgbole ɛyɛlɛ nu;
<<<P>>>
• ɔbaboa yeamaa bɛabukebuke milahyinli gyima gyima;
<<<P>>>
• ɔbaboa yeamaa bɛava milahyinli bɛayɛ adwinli nwo ninyɛne na yɛava kɔmputa yɛali gyima gyima dɔɔnwo wɔ maanle ye anu;
<<<P>>>
• ɔbaboa yeamaa kɔmputa mɔɔ bɛfa bɛdi gyima la azo maanle ye anu ndɛndɛ zo kpalɛ;
<<<P>>>
• ɔbaboa yeamaa Azuazilɛ Gyima ne ali munli;
<<<P>>>
• ɔbaboa yeamaa Gana ayɛ maanle mɔɔ Abibile Adɔleɛ ezuavolɛ kɔsɔɔti kɛhulo kɛ bɛkɛrayɛ gyima wɔ ɛke la;
<<<P>>>
• bɛbayɛ ngakyile wɔ gyima titili mɔɔ gyi Arane anye zo la anu bɛamaa yeali munli;
<<<P>>>
• bɛbabuke Gana Anyunluhɔlɛ Bange mɔɔ ɔbanlea yeamaa bɛanyia ezukoa bɛali gyima ngakyile mɔɔ wɔ Gana DWENLE Ɛ NWO Ngyehyɛleɛ ne abo la.
