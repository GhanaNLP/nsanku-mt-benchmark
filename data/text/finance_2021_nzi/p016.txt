<<<P>>>
• Eza arane nee Kelesɛɛnema nee Ngɛnlamo bie mɔ bɔle nu hyɛle aleɛ aleɛ mɔɔ bɛtɛtonle la bɛmaanle mbusua 470,000, wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛduale maanlema kɛ bɛmmafinde gua zo la;

<<<P>>>
• Arane boale Ganama mɔɔ ɛnee wɔ adendulɛ nu mɔɔ anwo hyelele bɛ la ɔmaanle bɛrale sua nu anzondwolɛ nu;

• Arane vale Gana Sidi mgbe GH¢750 boale Gyima Ngyikyi nee Mgbole ekyi bie mɔ. Bɛvale ezukoa ɛhye anu Gana Sidi mgbe GH¢412.88 bɛzɔle gyima gyima 277,511 avinli;
<<<P>>>
• Arane bukele Kolona Vaelɔso ne anwo Gya mɔɔ bɛnyianle Gana Sidi mgbe GH¢ 57.13 wɔ nu a. Bɛ mgbondabulɛ ne kile kɛ wɔ Siane Bulu nee Nwiɔ ne awieleɛ, ɛnee bɛva ezukoa ne anu Gana Sidi mgbe GH¢45.22 bɛli kolona vaelɔso ne anwo gyima. Gyima ne bie mɔ a le kɛ bɛhyehyɛle aleɛ bɛmaanle mgbanyinli nee ehyiavolɛma. Eza bɛtotɔle ahale bɛmaanle kpɔkɛdelɛ ɛleka ɛleka mɔɔ ɛnee bɛnea Kolona Vaelɔso wuliravolɛma ne mɔ la.
