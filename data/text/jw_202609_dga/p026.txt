Go Ho Meŋɛ Yi “A Donɛɛ Vooroŋ” Na Eŋɛŋ
<<<P>>>
“A boma zaa naŋ be a donɛɛ poɔ . . . ba yi a Saa zie.”
—1 GYN. 2:16.
<<<P>>>
A BAABOLE yeleeŋ ka Setaana la a donɛɛ ŋa naa, azuŋ o maŋ bɛnlɛɛ noba naŋ be a donɛɛ poɔŋ. (Gyn. 14:30; 1 Gyn. 5:19) A Baabole la yeleeŋ ka o suuri ireeŋ neŋ Yezu potuuribo. (Yel. 12:12, 13, 17) Kyɛ lɛ ba wuli ka Setaana maŋ be la zie zaaŋ a iri zɔɔrɔ neŋ te. A Baabole zie zaa ba yeli ka maleke soŋ bee maleke faa kaŋa taaɛ kpeẽo ka o maŋ baŋ be zie zaa a erɛ yɛlɛ oŋ boɔrɔ. Kyɛ a Baabole yeleeŋ ka Setaana taaɛ maleke faare yaga aŋ tono koroo. (Yel. 12:9) Kyɛ yelkaŋa bebeŋ ka Setaana maŋ toɔ̃ vɛŋ oŋ nyɛ kpeẽo noba naŋ be a donɛɛ zu zie zaa. O maŋ vɛŋeŋ ka noba teɛrɛ yɛlɛ aŋa lɛ na oŋ boɔrɔ na. Ana teɛroŋ ŋa maŋ nyɛɛ kpeẽo noba enneŋ saŋa na gba baŋ ba baŋ. (Kanne 1 Gyɔɔn 2:15, 16.) Noba mine bebeŋ ba ba sagedi ka Setaana bebeŋ, kyɛ ba erɛɛ yɛlɛ Setaana naŋ boɔrɔ a vɛŋ ka a donɛɛ vooroŋ nyɛ kpeẽo ba eŋɛŋ. A Baabole yeleeŋ ka ana noba ŋa taa Setaana “nyɔge baŋ eŋ gbaŋbaaloŋ poɔ ka ba erɛ o poboɔbo yɛlɛ.”— 2 Tim. 2:26.
<<<P>>>
2 Saŋa kaŋa naŋ pare te yaga zie da maŋ teɛrɛɛ bee a erɛ yɛlɛ lɛ na a donɛɛ noba naŋ erɛ na. Lɛ wuleeŋ ka a donɛɛ vooroŋ da nyɛrɛɛ kpeẽo te zuŋ. Kyɛ teŋ wa zanne nyɛ neɛ na boɔbo ka a donɛɛ noba erɛ na, wala teŋ e te yɛlɛ? Te deɛ a Baabole yelbie ŋa toŋ neŋ toma, “ka te bare yelfaare ane vɔlfaare.” (Tit. 2:11, 12) Aneazaa ka Setaana taaɛ kpeẽo a maŋ de bɛnlɛ noba, kyɛ te maŋ arɛɛ kpeɛŋaa a zagre o donɛɛ vooroŋ na bare. Lɛ wuleeŋ ka te zagrɛɛ o bɛnlɛ bare kyɛ are Jehoova puoreŋ.
