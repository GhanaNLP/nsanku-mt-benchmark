Nansaalee kɔkɔre yoŋ oŋ wono, kyɛ o da gaaɛ zie maŋgboe naŋ maŋ are a te moɔlɔ a duorisoŋ. O da nyɛɛ dɔɔ kaŋa naŋ da yele Turkey kɔkɔre a tonɔ toma a maŋgbo-re kaŋa poɔŋ. A dɔɔ nansaalee yelbo da ba taa nimiri, azuŋ Robert da deɛ a Good News From God! gambile naŋ be Turkish kɔkɔre poɔŋ na ko a dɔɔ. Robert naŋ da baŋ a gambile ŋa poɔ yɛlɛ soŋ zaa zuŋ, o da soorɛɛ a dɔɔ ka o kanne a gane poɔ wɛlbo mine a Turkish kɔkɔre poɔŋ. A dɔɔ naŋ da kanne baare na, o da soorɛɛ Ro-bert soorebie mine. Robert da deɛ a JW Lib-rary app na somoo oŋ nyɛ a soorebie nɔɛ iribo yi Turkish Baabole na poɔŋ. Tamaa Naŋ Bebe A Ko Noba Naŋ Bɔ Zo-Lirizie
<<<P>>>
Pɔge kaŋa yuori naŋ la Youa, da tuoree yelkpeɛne yaga o zemmo poɔŋ. A da fɛrɛɛŋ ka o bare o paaloŋ kyɛ te be United States paaloŋ. O da ba wono nan-saalee kɔkɔre. A Hmong kɔkɔre na yoŋ oŋ da wono. Ane meŋ, o serɛ da ba koo sori ka o kanne gane zaa aneŋ a Baabole meŋ gba. Ŋaaŋ ka a pɔge yeli: “Wagre zaa, eŋkyere da maŋ be la N eŋɛŋ, N daaŋ ba taa popeɛloŋ zaa.” Bebiri kaŋa, pɔge kaŋa naŋ e Jehoova Sieree Deme kaŋa a yele nansaalee kɔkɔ-re da wa ko oŋ gampɛlee kaŋa yuori naŋ la Life in a Peaceful New World. A gane ŋa be la a Hmong kɔ-kɔre poɔŋ. Youa da zanne nyɛɛ yelsonne na zaa Naaŋmen naŋ eŋ noɔre ka o na eŋ nendaare kaŋa na yɛlɛ a yi a Baabole poɔŋ. O zemmo zaa poɔŋ, o daaŋ ba woŋ a yɛlɛ ŋa zaa. Yɛlɛ na oŋ zanne nyɛ a gampɛlee poɔŋ na da vɛŋɛɛŋ ka o poɔ zaa pɛle. A da vɛŋɛɛŋ oŋ taa tamaa ka nendaare kaŋa zemmo na somaŋ. O da deɛ a gampɛlee sɔgle zie na o serɛ naŋ daaŋ koŋ nyɛ o zaa. Wagre yaga o da maŋ deɛ a gampɛlee sɔgle kanne kyɛ la de te sɔgle. Youa da taaɛ bidɔba banaare, bipɔgeba meŋ banaare. O bipɔge-bilbaaraa soba na da zannɛɛ a yelmeŋɛ a leɛ Jehoova Sieree Deme kaŋa. O da deɛ o ma gaaneŋ te lammozie na kaŋa baŋ yele Hmong kɔkɔre. A zan-noo naŋ wa baare na, ba da sɛgrɛɛ yɛlɛ ka Youa piili a Baabole zannoo neŋ Jehoova Sieree Deme. A baaraa zaa, o da toɔnɔ laŋna neŋ la Jehoova puore-biiri, baŋ zaa puoro Jehoova kyɛ taa nendaare kaŋa tamaa naŋ soma. Kyɛ yɛlɛ naŋ da somoo la a gam-pɛlee na oŋ da kanne yi o meŋɛ kɔkɔre poɔŋ na.
