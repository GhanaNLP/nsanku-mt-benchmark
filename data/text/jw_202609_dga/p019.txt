15 Jehoova Sieree Deme kpoŋ zie zaa maŋ toɔ̃ kannɛɛ a New World Translation Baabole na ba menne kɔkɔɛ pooŋ. A Baabole ŋa maŋ soŋeŋ baŋ zanne baŋ yelmenne naŋ bebe kyaare Jehoova, a taa nɔmmo naŋ e zuluŋ koo, a dɛloo kyɛ pugro o yuori na.—Yeng. 18:10; Aiz. 56:6.
<<<P>>>
16 A Baabole yeleeŋ ka a koŋ kɔɔre Jehoova na zɔɔ neŋ la a “namine naŋ be a teendaa zaa zu ziiri.” (Yel. 16:14) Ana saŋa na, Jehoova na go la neɛ zaa naŋ bamoo, a kyɛllɛ wono o yɛlɛ, a pugro o yuori na meŋ. (Yiem. 9:10; kanne Gyɔɔn 17:3.) Ka Jehoovaŋ wa saana noba naŋ ba kyɛllɛ wono o yɛlɛ bee a ba dɛloo na, ba zaa na wa nyɛŋ ka o na la Naaŋmen naŋ taa kpeɛ̃o ka o tɔ zaa ba kyebe. Jehoova yeleeŋ ka: ‘Lɛ la ka N na wuli N gandaaloŋ ane N sonnoŋ, a e ka ba baŋ ma tenne yaga niŋesogɔŋ. Lɛ ka ba pãã na baŋ ka maaŋ la Jehoova.’ (Izik. 38:23) Ana saŋa na, Jehoova puorebiiri na taaɛ popeɛloŋ yaga, bonso Naaŋmen na eɛ ba zɔmeŋɛ, o koŋ e ba dɔndɔma.—Maal. 3:16.
<<<P>>>
17 A eɛ apoɔsori naŋ e tegroŋ yaga teŋ nyɛ ka te soŋ moɔle a naaloŋ duorisoŋ ko neɛ zaa naŋ be a donɛɛ lombori zaa aŋa lɛ Zekaraiya 8:23 naŋ yeli na. Te la taaɛ apoɔsori ka te neŋ te yɔɔdɔba neŋ te yɔɔpɔgeba, aneŋ a malekere laŋ tonɔ a naaŋ moɔloo toma.Te naŋ nyɛrɛ ana sommo ŋa zaa zuŋ, te maŋ moɔlɛɛ a duorisoŋ korɔ noba kɔkɔ-tɛɛtɛɛ pooŋ. Jehoova ko teŋ bonzaa naŋ na toɔ̃ soŋ ka te moɔle a duorisoŋ a ko noba naŋ yi tenne zaa, bale zaa, kɔkɔɛ zaa pooŋ. Azuŋ te moɔ̃ niŋe a de te tontoŋ boma na sonnɔ noba yaga ka ba leɛ Jehoova zɔmenne. Te na baŋ soŋ baŋ ka ba nyɛ ka Jehoova eɛ Naaŋmen naŋ taa nimibaaloŋ, kannyiri, a nɔŋ nembooro zaa meŋ. A eɛ apoɔsori naŋ e kpoŋ ka te moɔ̃ niŋe a moɔlɔ a duorisoŋ a te ta saŋa na neɛ zaa naŋ be a donɛɛ zuŋ naŋ na baŋ yelmeŋɛ naŋ bebe a kyaare Jehoova aneŋ o yuori na.—Luk. 11:2.
