TE SOŊNEƐ NOBA NAŊ BE A DONƐƐ LOMBORI ZAA BAŊ NA BAŊ JEHOOVA YUORI NA
<<<P>>>
12 Ka te zaaŋ na toɔ̃ nyɛ tono yi a Baabole poɔŋ, aseŋ ka ba leɛre a Baabole eŋ kɔkɔ-tɛɛtɛɛ poɔŋ.* Yuomo yaga la ŋa ka noba leɛrɛ a Baabole enne kɔkɔ-yobo poɔŋ. A kɔkɔ leɛreba naŋ boɔrɔ ka noba yaga maŋ kanne Naaŋmen Yelbiri na ba menne kɔkɔre poɔ zuŋ, ba mɔɔ la ba moɔbo zaa a leɛre a Baabole eŋ kɔkɔ-yobo poɔŋ. Azuŋ, zenɛ te na baŋ kannɛɛ a Baabole te menne kɔkɔɛ poɔŋ a woŋ o muni. A ba kɔɔre kyɛ baŋ leɛre a New World Translation of the Holy Scriptures Baabole na eŋ kɔkɔ yaga poɔŋ ka lɛ soŋ Jehoova puorebiiri naŋ be a donɛɛ lombori zaa baŋ zanna a yelmeŋɛ yire Naaŋmen Yelbiri na poɔŋ. A lantaapuoruu neŋkpoŋ kaŋa naŋ be Nepal yeleeŋ ka: “Saŋa zaa maŋ wa kanne a Baabole ŋa, N maŋ nyɛŋ ka Jehoova yelee yelɛ korɔ ma N meŋɛ kɔkɔre poɔŋ, lɛ vɛŋeeŋ ka N ne o niiloŋ e kpeeŋaa yaga.” Zenɛ ŋa, a New World Translation of the Holy Scriptures Baabole na gbuli zaa bee kyɛlee be la kɔkɔɛ naŋ gaŋ 300 poɔŋ, ka noba yaga na baŋ kanne. A Baabole ŋa kannoo ba e tuo, ka a yelbie zaa tori a wulo yelmenne a kyaare Jehoova. Azuŋ, Jehoova puorebiiri naŋ be a donɛɛ lombori zaa sagɛɛ yelyenaa di, baŋ zaa moɔlɔ a duorisoŋ korɔ neɛ zaa. (1 Kor. 1:10) Yɛ vɛŋ ka te nyɛ ananso mine a New World Translation of the Holy Scriptures Baabole na naŋ taa tono yaga zaa lɛ.

12. Ka Naaŋmen puorebiiriŋ wa kanna bee a zanna a New World Translation Baabole na, bo tono baŋ maŋ nyɛ yi o poɔŋ?
<<<P>>>
13 Baabolehi yaga bebeŋ ka Naaŋmen yuori ba kye a poɔŋ. Kyɛ a New World Translation taaɛ Naaŋmen yuori na turituri yaga o poɔŋ. Ka hooŋ kaa yuomo 100 mine naŋ pare, a Baabolehi yaga naŋ da bebe na da taaɛ Naaŋmen yuori Jehoova a poɔŋ, agaŋaazaa a Hiburu sɛgre na poɔŋ. Kyɛ zenɛ ŋa, zirii puoruu wedeereba naŋ boɔlɔ ba menne Kristabiiri ba boɔrɔ ka ba de Naaŋmen yuori na tono neŋ toma bee a boɔrɔ ka neɛ zaa baŋ ana yuori na. Aŋa mannoo poɔŋ, a Katolik puoruu wedeereba iree Naaŋmen yuori yi ba Baabolehi aneŋ gama mine pooreŋ.* A puoruu kyɛlɛɛ na meŋ eɛ lɛ neŋ a zu. Kyɛ kɔkɔre zaa poɔŋ, ka hooŋ kaa a New World Translation Baabole na, Naaŋmen yuori be la zie zaa aŋ seŋ ka o be a gaŋ turituri yaga aŋa lɛ aŋ da waa a dakoreŋ saŋa Baabolehi pooreŋ na. Kyɛ ananso la wala aŋ e nimizeɛ yaga zaa ka noba baŋ Naaŋmen yuori na?
<<<P>>>
14 A Baabole wuli teŋ ka a eɛ nimizeɛ ka te baŋ Naaŋmen yuori a de o tono toma. Aŋa mannoo poɔŋ, a yieli yiele da yelkoɛ Naaŋmen puorebiiri ka ba pugro Jehoova yuori na. (Yiem. 135:1) Saŋa na Yezu naŋ da wulo lɛ o potuuribo naŋ na maŋ puore ko o Saa Jehoova na, o da yeleeŋ ka: “Vɛŋ ka ho yuori pɛgeyi.” A puoriŋ o meŋɛ da puoreeŋ a yelko o Saa ka: “N vɛŋeeŋ baŋ baŋ ho yuori na.” (Mat. 6:9; Gyn. 17:26) Danyele Jooele ane bitontona Pɔɔl da sɛgeɛŋ ka: “Neɛzaa naŋ boɔlɔ Jehoova yuori na na nyɛ la faabo.” (Uro. 10:13) Kyɛ kɔkɔ leɛreba mine iree Naaŋmen yuori yi Baa-
