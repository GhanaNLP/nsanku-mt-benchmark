<<<P>>>
na ka ba wa go o puorebiiri zaa. Yezu neŋ o malekere na pãã na zɔɔ neŋ la Naaŋmen dɔn-dɔnne zaa, a sãã ba kpɛleŋkpɛleŋ. (Yel. 17:12-14; 19:11, 14, 15) Sobuoŋ ka Jehoova na tu kyɛ go o noba Hammagedon zɔɔre na pooŋ?
<<<P>>>
19 A ba seŋ ka te zoro dabeẽ ka Yezu neŋ o malekere naŋ wa sããna Naaŋmen dɔndɔnne a Hammagedon zɔɔre na pooŋ. Ananso la wala? Bozuŋ te dɛlɛɛ Jehoova a lɛ sagedi meŋ ka o na go teŋ lɛ zaa yɛlɛ naŋ na waa. Azuŋ te ba-ŋɛɛŋ pɛɛlaaŋ ka Yezu neŋ o malekere na na wa sããŋɛɛ Naaŋmen dɔndɔnne zaa kpɛleŋkpɛleŋ, bonyeni gba koŋ kyɛre. (Jer. 25:33; Zek. 14:12) Te la baŋɛɛŋ ka a zɔɔre pooŋ, Naaŋmen dɔndɔnne na koŋ ko o yelmeŋɛ puorebie bo-nyeni gba.—Luk. 21:28; 2 Pit. 2:9.
<<<P>>>
19-20. Ananso la wala aŋ ba seŋ ka te zoro dabeẽ ka Yezu ne o malekere naŋ wa sããna Naaŋmen dɔndɔnne a Hammagedon zɔɔre na pooŋ? (Kaa a gane niŋe foto.)
<<<P>>>
20 A Hammagedon zɔɔre na saŋa, yelbie naŋ be a Yieme gane pooŋ na nyɛɛ eebo. Be yeleeŋ ka: “Noba turi na le la ho lamboriŋ, ka tur-pie le a ho nuduloŋ seŋ, kyɛ a na waa la tɔɔre ne bo. Ho nimiri yoŋ la ka ho na kaa nyɛ ne a dɔg-roŋ a neŋfaare naŋ na nyɛ. Ho naŋ vɛŋ ka [Je-hoova] e ho zosɔgloo zie zuiŋ, ka a Soba Naŋ Do Saa e ho kpeɛbozie.” (Yiem. 91:7-9) Ka a Hammagedon zɔɔre naŋ wa piili, Jehoova na go la o puorebiiri zaa ka ba koŋ nyɛ sããmmo sori kaŋa pooŋ. O na go la o neŋ ba kaŋa zaa niiloŋ na, ba nyɔvoɛ, a kyɛ lɛ ko ba maaroŋ ka ba tõɔ are yelkpeɛne noɔreŋ ana saŋa na. Aŋa a yieloŋ yiele, ho meŋ na yeleŋ ka: “Jehoo-va la N zosɔgloo zie,” Jehoova meŋ eŋ bon noɔre ka: “Yelfaa zaa koŋ pɔge bo.” Azuŋ a ba seŋ ka te zoro yɛlɛ naŋ na e nendaare kaŋa dabeẽ. Aseŋ ka te sagedi kpɛleŋkpɛleŋ ka Je-hoova na go teŋ lɛ zaa yɛlɛ naŋ waa. Azuŋ dɛle Jehoova wagre zaa.
