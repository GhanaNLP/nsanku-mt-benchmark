YIEME 91 SOŊEEŊ TEŊ NYƐ KA TE NA BAŊ DELEƐ JEHOOVA KA O NA GO TEŊ
<<<P>>>
4 Yelbie naŋ be Yieme 91 o wɛlbo 1 ane 2 na vɛŋeeŋ aŋ pɛle ka Jehoova na go la neɛzaa naŋ dɛloo na. Azuŋ a eɛ nimizeɛ ka te dɛle Jehoova ka o na go teŋ. (Kanne Yieme 91:1, 2.) Jehoova la Kpeɛo Zaa Daana, azuŋ te na baŋ dɛlooŋ kpɛleŋkpɛleŋ ka o na go teŋ. (Ruut 2:12; Yiem. 17:8) A Baabole pooŋ, ba deɛ yelbiri aŋa “zo-kpeebo zie” toŋ neŋ toma. Dakoreŋ saŋa na, ba da maŋ mɛ la ziiri mine aŋ korɔ noba gomeŋɛ. Noba da maŋ zo gaa sɔglɛɛ ana ziiri naŋ saŋa na ba dɔndɔnne naŋ wa waana na. Noba naŋ da be a tenne pooreŋ da maŋ nyɛ la gomeŋɛ. Ka bana na naŋ kpeɛrɛ peɛle be na da maŋ zo wa bɔ gomeŋɛ a yi ba dɔndɔnne nuuriŋ. Ka hooŋ lɛ kaa a wɛlbore naŋ tɔnne na, ho na nyɛŋ ka neɛ na naŋ sɛge a yelɛ deɛ yelmannee mine naŋ soma yaga zaa a wuli lɛ Jehoova naŋ maŋ tu kyɛ go o puorebiiri yi yelkpeɛne pooŋ.—Yiem. 91:3-13.
<<<P>>>
5 Ka hooŋ kaa Yieme 91 baaraa seŋ na, ho na nyɛɛ yelbie mine naŋ toɔrɔ sukyiri yaga zaa beŋ. Be ba yeli sori na Jehoova naŋ maŋ tu kyɛ go o noba yelɛ, kyɛ o yelee Jehoova meŋɛ noɔre yelbie yelɛ. Jehoova eŋɛɛ noɔre beŋ ka o na go la o puorebiiri. A yelbie ŋa eɛ yelɛ naŋ na nyɛ eebo meŋɛmeŋɛ. (Kanne Yieme 91:14-16.) Ka hooŋ kaa nyɛ, Jehoova maŋ yeliŋ ka “N na”. Lɛ wuleeŋ ka Jehoova meŋɛ la ŋmɛ o nyaa a yeli ka o na go la o puorebiiri, bozuŋ ba dɛlooŋ, a baŋ o yuori na meŋ muni, a kyɛ maŋ puoro sɔroo wagre zaa meŋ. Jehoova maŋ paalɛɛ o nɔ-ennee zaa, azuŋ te na baŋ dɛlooŋ kpɛleŋkpɛleŋ. (Joos. 23:14) Ka Jehoovaŋ wa eŋ noɔre zaa, bonnoo zaa ba maŋ kye a eŋɛŋ, lɛ zaa naŋ la a na nyɛ eebo!—Sor. 23:19.

6 Jehoova la vɛŋ baŋ sɛge a Yieme gane na ko te, ka lɛ na vɛŋ ka te dɛloo kpɛleŋkpɛleŋ ka o na go teŋ. (Uro. 15:4) Aŋa lɛ a yieloŋ yiele naŋ sɛge a yelɛ ŋa na, te meŋ na baŋ de la nyaa a yeli ko Jehoova ka: “Hooŋ la N zo-kpeɛbo zie ane N kpeɛo.” (Yiem. 91:2) Kyɛ bo soe ka Jehoova eŋ te noɔre ka o na maŋ tuŋ kyɛ ko te gomeŋɛ?

AŊA LƐ YIEME 91 NAŊ YELI NA BO SOE LA KA JEHOOVA MAŊ TU KYƐ KORƆ TE GOMEŊƐ?
<<<P>>>
7 Saŋa kaŋa naŋ pare, te puoruu gama na wuleeŋ ka yelbie na naŋ be Yieme 91 na paree la ka Jehoova eŋɛɛ noɔre ka o na maŋ go la o neŋ o puorebiiri zɔmennoŋ na baŋ taa na. Azuŋ te baŋɛɛ ka Jehoova na maŋ deɛ sommo naŋ seŋ neŋ ko te ka te toɔ̃ go te ne o niiloŋ na soŋ zaa. Kyɛ a sɛgre la yelee soe mine Jehoova naŋ maŋ lɛ tu kyɛ korɔ te gomeŋɛ. A la eɛ yelɛ oŋ eŋ-noɔre ka o na maŋ eŋ. Ka hooŋ kaa a sɛgre pooŋ, Jehoova lɛ eŋɛɛ noɔre ka o daaŋ koŋ tere sori ka o dɔndɔnne sãã o yelmeŋɛ puorebiiri zaa a yi a donɛɛ pooŋ. Ka o yelmeŋɛ puorebiiriŋ wa dire dɔgɛɛ meŋ, o maŋ ko baŋ a Naaŋmen maaroŋ na baŋ toɔ̃ are ba yelkpeɛne na noɔreŋ. Kyɛ wala teŋ e baŋ ka lɛ ka Jehoova eŋ noɔre ka o na maŋ eŋ a ko o puorebiiri a yi a sɛgre ŋa pooŋ?

8 Ho teɛrɛɛ saŋa na Setaana naŋ da wa Yezu zie a wa pigruu nyɛ na? Setaana da iree yelbie mine naŋ be Yieme 91 na yeli neŋ yelɛ. (Kanne Matio 4:5-7.) Setaana da tagɛɛ Yezu teɛroŋ gaa neŋ yelbie naŋ be Yieme 91:11, 12 na a wuli ka Jehoova na go la Yezu ka yelfaa zaa koŋ pɔgoo.
