**8** Mekɛ mɔɔ ɛlɛsiezie ɛ nwo wɔamaa ɛvolɛ ɛhye Ngakyelɛlilɛ ne la, nyia mekɛ dwenledwenle kɛzi ɛbanyia Gyihova ɛtanefakyɛ ne azo nvasoɛ la anwo. Kɛ neazo la, ɔlua ɛkpɔnedeɛ ne ati ɔngyia kɛ ɛbadwenledwenle ɛtane bie mɔɔ ɛyɛle na wɔnlu ɛ nwo wɔ nwolɛ la anwo. Na saa ɔyɛ se ɔmaa wɔ kɛ ɛbalie wɔado nu ɛ? Bie a wɔha wɔhile ɛ nwo kɛ, ‘Meze kɛ Gyihova fa ɛtane kyɛ noko ɔyɛ se kɛ mebava meahyɛ _me nwo._’ Saa zɔhane a ɛte nganeɛ a, kakye kɛ: Gyihova a fa ɛtane kyɛ a, na yemaa ɔ Ra ne noko tumi kɛ ɔbua ndɛne. Gyihova ɛtɛmaanle wɔ anzɛɛ awie biala nwolɛ adenle kɛ ɔhile awie mɔɔ ɔbaze ye anwunvɔne anzɛɛ ɔnrɛze ye anwunvɔne la. Baebolo ne ka kɛ: ‘Saa yɛlua wienyi nu kɛ mɔɔ [Gyihova] mumua ne wɔ wienyi nu la a, **_ɔ Ra Gyisɛse mogya ne te yɛ nwo fi ɛtane muala anwo.’_** (1 Dwɔn 1:6, 7) Yɛbahola yɛalie edwɛkɛ ɛhye yeali kɛ mɔɔ yɛdie Baebolo nu ngilehilelɛ gyɛne biala yɛdi la. Ɛkpɔnedeɛ ne maa Gyihova nyia debie gyinla zo se yɛ anwunvɔne, na ye Edwɛkɛ ne ka kɛ ‘ɔlɛ ɛhulolɛ kɛ ɔbava ɛtane yeahyɛ.’—Edw. 86:5.
