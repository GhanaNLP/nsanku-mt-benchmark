YE' se nta na ye wo ho, na ye'ni wu gyaa yen. Ye'hye fa na Ata 'Panin faa afwenee ne sika de yan Ata Kuma. Ata Kuma ne no mfefuo se ye ko dware asuom'. Ye'dware, Ata Kuma sika a eyane no bu guu nsuom'. Wape ape, wanhu. Obaa 'fie be ka kyeree onua se, " Sika no ayera." Ata 'Panin see, " Me nnim se ayera, na se ayera dee a, megye wo do." Ata Kuma see, "Wo begye me deen? " Osee, " Megye wo Etwie bowere, Aserewa perebuo, Ponponponponsa, Ahahan koro, Eson' Hema dua." Ata Kuma see, " Mate." Ata Kuma siim' be duruu babi na dua koro na esi ho yi. Dua hunoo no, osee, "Ata Kuma ni o, wafiti k'otia ama osuo amuna." Osee, " Nye biribi nti na maduru ha, na agya 'ba ne Ata 'Panin, ena 'ba ne Ata 'Panin, na watoto afwenee ayan me na ate ayera.

Aserewa see, “Yei nti na wo'su, bra begye bi.” Na Ata Kuma ko gyeye, na. odaa n’ase. Na osiim’ koye, na okotoo Ponponponponsa. Osee, “Ata Kuma ni o wafiti k’otia ama osuo amuna.” Na oboo amanee no sa’ra. Na Ponponponponsa maa no bi, na ode koye. Na oko too Etwie. Etwie hunoo no osee, “Ata Kuma ni o, wafiti k’otia ama osuo amuna.” Ata Kuma boo n’amanee kyeree no. Osee, “Begye bi.” Na efei na Ata Kuma see, “Agya ’Twie, na meye den na manya Eson’ Hema dua?” Osee, “O! wo be koro, na mmoa nnyina ada, Eson’ Hema da mfinfini, na wo koro a, tiatia ye’so den, na wo ko to Eson’ Hema so a, na w’atwa no dua, na se wo tiatia ye’so berebere dee a, ye beku wo.” Ata Kuma see, “Me da’se.” Okoreye ko hunoo mmoa yi; otiatiaa ye’so dendenden, na okotoo Eson’ Hema. Otwaa ne dua na osane baye. Na otoo afwefwe ’kwanm’ de baye. Na oduruu ’fie. Osee, “Ata ’Panin, nnooma a wo se wo’gye me no, eni.” Ata ’Panin see, “Me nsa aka.” Dakoro bi, ye’te ho, na Ata Kuma faa afwefwe na o’fwem’. Osee, “Afwefwe yi wo fwem’ a wo huno ena a owo asamandoo.” Ata ’Panin see, “Ampa, fa ma me nfwe.” Ode maa no. Osee o’fwem’, na efiri ne nsa abo. Ata Kuma see, “Yedee, dee wo gyee me na me’gye wo bi.” Na Ata ’Panin ko faa dee Ata Kuma de baye de bree no. Ata Kuma see, “Mempe gye se wo ko gye foforo bre me.” Ata ’Panin siim’ kotoo Ahan ko’. Osee, “Ata ’Panin ni o, wafiti k’otia ama osuo amuna.” Ata ’Panin see, “Mafiti wo ’ni ne wo ’se, me dee ma me w’ahahan bi me nko.” A’han ko’ see, “Ko w’anim’, na dee onim wo do.” Oko too Aserewa. Aserewa see, “Ata ’Panin ni o, wafiti k’otia ama osuo amuna.” Ata ’Panin see, “Mafiti wo ’ni ne wo ’se, me dee ma me wo perebuo no bi me nko.” Aserewa see, “Ko na dee onim wo do.” Na wako to Ponponponponsa. Ponponponponsa see, “Ata ’Panin ni o, wafiti k’otia ama osuo amuna.” Ata ’Panin see, “Mafiti wo ’ni, mafiti wo ’se, me dee ma me wo nsuo bi ma me nko.” Ponponponponsa see, “Ko w’anim ne dee onim wo do.” Na wako to Etwie. Etwie see, “Ata ’Panin ni o, wafiti k’otia ama osuo amuna.” Ata ’Panin see, “Mafiti wo ’ni ne wo ’se, me dee ma me wo bowere no bi me nko.” Etwie see, “Dee onim wo do.” Osiim’ koye ko too Eson’kuo, na Eson ’Hema da mfinfini. Enee oko twaa ne dua. Eson’kuo no nnyina soo nom’ tiatiaa no so, na owuye. Ata Kuma afwe no nua ’kwan. Wamma. Ene se ye se, “Wo ne wo bogya bi te ho, na se osee w’adee a, nnye n’akatua, na Ata ’Panin yee sa ko wuye.
