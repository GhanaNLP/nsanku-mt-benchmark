NU SIWO ŊU BIBLIA ƑO NU TSOE
<<<P>>>
Ɖɔ Siwo Wotsɔ Ɖe Lã Le Blema
<<<P>>>
Biblia xlẽla geɖe nyae be Yesu yomedzela aɖewo nye tɔƒodelawo. (Marko 1:16-20) Wozãa ɖɔwo ne wole lã ɖem le Galilea-ƒua me.
<<<P>>>
Wogbia ka helɔ̃nɛ ɖe wo nɔewo ŋu wòzua ɖɔ. Ale si ɖɔa ƒe ŋkuwo ƒe lolome nɔna la nɔa te ɖe lã si ƒomevi woatsɔe aɖe dzi. Zi geɖe la, wodea tsumi alo kpe ɖɔ suewo toga ale be woate ŋu akpe ayi tsi gɔme, gake ɖɔ gãwo ya kpena eye wòtea ŋu yia tsia gɔme bɔbɔe eya ta wotsia kɔkwo ɖe wo toga be woaho ɖe tsia dzi.
<<<P>>>
Ɖɔ siwo wotsɔ ɖea lã yemayi la xɔ asi ŋutɔ. Ke hã, ɖɔ siawo tea ŋu vuvuna ne woku kpe siwo le tsia me alo ne wotsɔ wo ɖe lã gbogbo aɖe. (Luka 5:6) Biblia gblɔ tso Yakobo kple Yohanes ŋu be wonɔ “woƒe ɖɔ vuvuwo sam.” Anye be abui kple kae wotsɔ nɔ esia wɔm. (Mat. 4:21) Anɔ eme be tɔƒodelawo gbugbɔ saa woƒe ɖɔwo ɣesiaɣi si wogbɔ tso lãɖeƒe. Luka hã gblɔ be ɖɔkplɔlawo nɔ “woƒe ɖɔwo kpalam.” Wowɔa esia kple susu be woaɖe ba kple ɖinu bubuwo le wo ŋu. (Luka 5:2) Eyome wosiaa ɖɔawo be woaƒu ale be woaganyunyɔ kaba o.—Eze. 47:10.
<<<P>>>
Yesu kpe ŋutsu siwo zãa ɖɔ tsɔ ɖea lãwo la be woava nye ‘amedelawo.’
<<<P>>>
