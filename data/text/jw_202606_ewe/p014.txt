TAÐODZINUA
Nyati sia ana míakpɔ ale si gbeadzifufofo ƒe ɖoɖoa ɖea vi na míi.
<<<P>>>
Na Gbeadzifufofo Ƒe Ɖoɖoa Naɖe Vi Na Wò
<<<P>>>
“Makafu Yehowa tso dzi blibo me le ame dzɔdzɔewo ƒe fufofo... la dome.”—PS. 111:1.
<<<P>>>
ÈƉO ŋku gbe si gbe woɖe mɔ na wò be nàzu gbefãɖela maxɔnyɔnyrɔ anɔ gbefã ɖem nya nyuia dzia? Dzi dzɔ wò gbe ma gbe ŋutɔ, elabe èva nye ameha gã siwo ɖea gbefã Yehowa ƒe ŋkɔ hekafunɛ la me tɔ. (Ps. 148:1, 2, 12, 13) Anɔ eme be èva nye ƒuƒoƒo sue aɖe si woyɔna be gbeadzifuƒoƒo, si me nɔvi vɛ aɖewo le hã me tɔ. Gbeadzifuƒoƒo ƒe ɖoɖoa kpena ɖe mía ŋu míewɔa dɔ si Yesu de asi na mí eyomedzelawo be míaɖe gbefã Fiaɖuƒe ŋuti nya nyuia “le anyigba blibo la katã dzi” la nyuie.—Mat. 24:14.
<<<P>>>
2 Aleke gbeadzifuƒoƒo ƒe ɖoɖo sia le vi ɖem na wòe? Nɔvinyɔnu aɖe si ŋkɔe nye Wendy* gblɔ be: “Gbeadzifuƒoƒo ƒe ɖoɖoa ɖe vi nam le mɔ geɖe nu. Ena mesrɔ̃ gbefãɖeɖe kple nufiafia ƒe mɔnu yeyewo tso nɔvi bubuwo gbɔ. Nɔviawo kpe ɖe ŋunye meɖo gbɔgbɔmetaɖodzinuwo heɖo wo gbɔ eye medze xɔ̃lɔ̃ nyui geɖewo.” Nɔviŋutsu Rich hã gblɔ be: “Agbanɔamedzi vevi aɖee wònye be ame nanye gbeadzifuƒofodzikpɔla. Ƒuƒofodzikpɔlaa ƒe dɔe nye be wòakpe ɖe gbefãɖela siwo le eƒe ƒuƒoƒoa me ŋu be woayi ŋgɔ le Yehowa subɔsubɔ me ahayi edzi ali ke le xɔse me le woƒe nyɔnyrɔxɔxɔ megbe gɔ̃ hã.” Nɔvinyɔnu aɖe si ŋkɔe nye Dinah gblɔ be: “Nu nyui aɖe ŋutɔe gbeadzifuƒofo ƒe ɖoɖoa nye! Nye ƒuƒoƒoa me tɔwo va zu nye ƒometɔwo.”
<<<P>>>
* Míetrɔ ŋkɔ aɖewo.
