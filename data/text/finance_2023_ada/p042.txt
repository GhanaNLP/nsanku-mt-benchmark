“Ma a Sika He Blɔ Nya Tomi Susumi Tso Ɔmɛ Kpalemi Kɛ Ba Da A Glɛ Nɔ, A He Piɛ Pomi Kɛ Da Tɛ Kake Nɔ Kɛ Gu Blɔnya Tomi Kpakpahi A Nɔ”
<<<P>>>
Nihi Ngɔmi kɛ Ya Mase Jua Yemi We: A ji mase juami ni tsumi komɛ nɛ haa ni juami mi yemi kɛ buami kɛ nihi nɛ a hiaa maje jua yemi he ga woli a yemi kɛ buami.
<<<P>>>
Sika Pami Blɔ Nya: Enɛ ɔ ji blɔ he aloo blɔ nya nɛ Amlaalo ko ngɛ kɛ ha sika pami nɛ a kɛ wo sika he ni tsumi mi hiɔ kɛ a nɔ da aloo vide.
<<<P>>>
Sika He Ni Tsumi: Enɛ ɔ ji sika he akɔtaa bumi aloo amaniɛ bɔmi, e nɔ hyɛmi, e ngɔmi kɛ nɔ ko tsumi mi, hiɔhi nɛ a hiaa womi, ni tsuli a hiɔwo blɔ nɔ tomi, weto ni nɛ a po fɔ si kɛ hiɔ womi blɔ nya tomi.
<<<P>>>
Sika He Ni Tsumi Mlaa: Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ Amlaalo ɔ sika jemi kɛ ni tsumi kɛ tohi a he mlaahi nɛ a ma wa ma a sika si fɔfɔɛ kɛ ma sika mu ɔ tsuo he ni tsumi.
<<<P>>>
Sika He Nyagba: E ji juɛmi ko nɛ a kɛ susuɔ aloo a kɛ kaa bɔ nɛ Amlaalo ɔ sika he ni tsumi sɛ haomi aloo fikɔfikɔ ko mi nɛ e hiaa haomi ɔ he ngmlaa jemi kɛ hla blɔhi a nɔ nɛ a maa gu kɛ hla nyagba a he tsaba.
<<<P>>>
Sɛkɔndle Sukuu Yami Faa: Sɛkɔndle sukuu yami faa nɛ hiɔwo be he ɔ, e ji Ghana Amlaalo ɔ blɔ nya tomi ehe ko nɛ Ma nɔ yelɔ Nana Akuffo-Addo nɔ yemi ɔ kɛ ba ngɛ Lemɔ hlami ɔ mi ngɛ 2017. Blɔ nya tomi nɛ ɔ ngmɛ blɔ faa kɛ ha JHS sukuu bi nɛ a na he blɔ kaa a maa ya sɛkɔndle sukuu ɔ nɛ a ya lɛ faa nɛ hiɔwo ko be he.
<<<P>>>
GhanaJuɛɔ O He: E ji blɔ nɔ tomi ko nɛ hɛɛ hɛ mi enyɔ nɛ a pee kɛ da blɔ nya komɛ nɛ Amlaalo ɔ to ngɛ Kolona hiɔ ɔ jemi blɔ nya tomi ɔ sisi nɔ.
<<<P>>>
Yemi Kɛ Buami Sikahi: Enɛ ɔ ji yemi kɛ buami sika nɛ he dooli aloo he doomi kuuhi nɛ a yeɔ kɛ buaa ma nɔ yami ni tsumi ɔ haa Amlaalo ɔ. Sika nɛ ɔmɛ ji sika kekeehi nɛ a hiaa we a se kpalemi aloo a nɔ to womi.
<<<P>>>
Yemi kɛ Buami Sika Kɛ Ha Amlaalo Ni Tsumi Hehi: E ji sika nɛ mlaa ngmɛ blɔ nɛ Amlaalo ɔ haa kpo wawi kɛ kpo ngua amɛ (DACF), Blɔhi a he sika, Ghana Sukuu Tsɔsemi Blɔ Nya Tomi Sika (GETFUND), Zu mi nu sika kɛ Ma Bi A Nɔmlɔ tso He Wami Nami Sika.
<<<P>>>
Ma a Weto ni (GDP): Enɛ ɔ ji nihi tsuo nɛ a pee ngɛ ma a mi nɛ peeɔ ma a weto ni ngɛ be kpɔ loo be pɔtɛɛ ko mi.
