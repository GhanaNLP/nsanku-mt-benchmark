“Ma a Sika He Blɔ Nya Tomi Susumi Tso Ɔmɛ Kpalemi Kɛ Ba Da A Glɛ Nɔ, A He Piɛ Pomi Kɛ Da Tɛ Kake Nɔ Kɛ Gu Blɔnya Tomi Kpakpahi A Nɔ”

Mi Dlami Setsɔ - E ji sika blɔ nya tomi he ko nɛ piɛɛ ma ko e he nɛ a hyɛɔ e sika blɔ nya tomi he ngua a nɔ ngɛ ɔ he.
<<<P>>>
MooJe sisi: E ji blɔ nya ko nɛ Amlaalo ɔ to kɛ fɔ si nɛ e gu nɔ kɛ ha sika, ga womi aloo nya numi nɔ ko kɛ ha nihe kɛ yihe nɛ a ye jeha kɛ je 18 kɛ ya su 40 nɛ a ngɛ jeha kla nɛ a tsɔɔ nɛ ɔ mi ɔ konɛ a nyɛ da nɔ kɛ bli a dɛhe ni tsumihi nɛ a ya nɔ.

Nihe kɛ Yihi A Ngɔmi kɛ Ni Tsumi Mi He (YEA): A da nihe kɛ yihe a ngɔmi mlaa 2015 (Acts 2015) kɛ mlaa 887 (Acts 887) nɔ nɛ a kɛ to nihe kɛ yihe a ngɔmi he nɛ ɔ si konɛ e ba hyɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ nɛ kɔ nihe kɛ yihe a ngɔmi kɛ wo ni tsumi slɔɔtohi a mi ngɛ Ghana a nɔ.
