Sika Titlii nɛ Amlaalo ɔ kɛ tsuɔ ni: Enɛ ɔ ji sika titlii nɛ Amlaalo ɔ kɛ woɔ ni komɛ kaa sukuu tsuhi, blɔhi, hiɔ tsami hehi, asaunɔ kɛ lɔkuhi, nyu, lɔle kɛ klamahi a peemi mi.
<<<P>>>
Lɔle Gbɛjegbɛ: E ji gbɛjegbɛ mi he nɛ lɔlehi guɔ.
<<<P>>>
Ni Tsuli a Kpatami aloo Tɔkemi Sika: Enɛ ɔ ji hiɔhi nɛ Amlaalo ɔ woɔ e nine nɔ ni tsuli kɛ ni tsuli nɛ a ba he jɔɔmi mi ngɛ Amlaalo ɔ nine nɔ.
<<<P>>>
Nɔmlɔ tso Mi Hyɛmi Klama: E ji blɔ ko nɔ nɛ a guɔ nɛ a kɛ klama hyɛɔ nɔmlɔ tso ɔ mi.
<<<P>>>
Hiɔ Nɛ A kɛ To Ma Weto Ni He: E je pa mi kami tso nɛ a kɛ hyɛɔ ma a e hiɔ nɛ e ye ngɛ e weto nihi tsuo a he aloo a nɔ.
<<<P>>>
Sika Mi Jiimi Nɔ Gbɔmi: E ji nɔ gbɔmi nɛ baa ngɛ weto ni aloo sika mi jiimi nɔ ke a kɛ to ma kpahi a sika aloo a weto hi a he.
<<<P>>>
Yemi Kɛ Buami Kuuhi (DPs): Mɛ ji mahi, ni tsumi hehi kɛ kuuhi nɛ a haa mahi nɛ a wɛ tɛ ɔ yemi kɛ buami nɛ a kɛ tsu nɔ yami ni tsumihi nɛ ma ngɔ wami kɛ ba. DPs nɛ ɔmɛ ma nyɛ maa pee ni tsumi hehi nɛ tsuɔ ni kɛ guɔ blɔ munomunohi a nɔ kaa: Je kɛ Wɛ Sika To He Ngua a (IMF), Je ɔ Sika To He Ngua a (World Bank), Afrika Mahi a Nɔ Yami Sika To He ɔ (AFDB) loo mahi kaa Jaamani (Germany), Ngleesi Ablotsi (United Kingdom) kɛ Amerika (USA).
<<<P>>>
Nihi a He Jua Wami Ngɛ Jeha a Nyagbe Mi: He nɛ nihi a he jua kuɔmi ya su ngɛ Bloonya hlami ɔ mi ngɛ jeha nɛ a ngɛ he munyu tue ɔ mi.
<<<P>>>
Sika He Ni Tsumi nɛ Be Nɔ: Enɛ ɔ baa ke Amlaalo ɔ tsu sika he ni pe nɔ nɛ a kɛ wo bɔjɛti ɔ mi nɛ Mlaa Womi Kpe he ɔ ngmɛ blɔ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
EXIM Sika Tohe aloo Banki: Ghana nihi sɛmi kɛ a ngɔmi kɛ ya mase sika tohe aloo banki ɔ, da mlaa tso 2016 aloo mlaa tso 911 nɔ kɛ tsɔ Ghana Nihi A Sɛmi Kɛ A Ngɔmi Kɛ Ya Mase Sika Tohe ɔ konɛ ye kɛ bua Ghana Amlaalo ɔ ngɛ e nihi ngɔmi kɛ ya mase aloo maje blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ.
