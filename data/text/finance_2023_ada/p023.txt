26. Mi dlami nɛ ɔ tsɔɔ nɔ yami komɛ nɛ a ba ngɛ Amlaalo ɔ blɔ nya tomi ehe titlii komɛ nɛ e kɛ ba ngɛ he oti komɛ ngɛ jeha 2022 mi kɛ ni kpa komɛ a he blɔ nya nɛ a to kɛ fɔ si kɛ ha a peemi ngɛ jeha 2023 mi.
<<<P>>>
27. Blɔ nya tomi kɛ nihi nɛ a ma tsu nɛ ɔmɛ daa si titli ngɛ Amlaalo ɔ E Be Kpiti Nɛ E To Kɛ Ha Ma a Nɔ Yami Blɔ Nya Tomi ɔ nɛ a pee kɛ je 2022 kɛ ya si 2026 nɛ e ji “Government’s Medium-Term National Development Policy Framework (2022-2026)” ɔ nɔ. Nɔ nɛ a kɛ ma blɔ nya tomi nɛ ɔ hɛ mi ji, e tsu yi mi tomi nɛ a pee kɛ ha blɔ nya tomi nɛ ji Amlaalo ɔ JUƐƆ O HE Ɔ nya ni. Kɛ tsa nɔ ɔ, blɔ nya tomi nɛ ɔ hu ma tsu nihi nɛ a to a he blɔ nya kɛ fɔ si nɛ wa nine maa su a nɔ kɛ je maje blɔ nya tomi komɛ a mi nɛ e kɛ UN Ajɛnda 2030 nɛ ji SDG’s ɔ kɛ Aflika Kake Peemi Ajɛnda 2063 nihi ɔmɛ kɔ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
28. Mi dlami nɛ ɔ tsɔɔ nɔ yami nihi nɛ Amlaalo ɔ pee ngɛ he titlii komɛ ngɛ ma a sika he blɔ nya tomi ɔ mi ngɛ jeha 2022 mi, blɔ nya tomi komɛ kɛ ni ekpa komɛ nɛ a maa pee ngɛ 2023 jeha a mi.

29. Ngɛ Amlaalo ɔ yi mi tomi nya kaa e ma plɛ se nami slɔɔto nɛ tso dudui kɛ baa a hɛ mi ɔ, e gba tso bubui nɛ a wo mi sɔmi nɛ a yibɔ ya su ayɔ 2.7 kɛ ha akuafohi ngɛ jeha 2022 ngmɔ peemi be ɔ mi.
<<<P>>>
30. Amlaalo ɔ sɛ kungwɔ yihi akpe 120 nɛ a maa ngmɛ nɛ a gba kɛ ha yihi nɛ a ngɛ kungwɔ lɛe ɔmɛ ngɛ Kpo Ngua ma nɛɛ (9) komɛ a nɔ. Ma nɛ ɔmɛ ji: Savelugu, Sagnarigu, Wa Municipal, Nandom, Sissala Beleku blɔ, Krachi Beleku blɔ, Nkwanta Leneyi blɔ, Techiman Omleyi blɔ kɛ Nkoranza. A pee nɔ nɛ ɔ ngɛ blɔ nya tomi ko nɛ a tsɛɔ ke: Lohwe Lɛmi Kɛ Ha Niye Ni Ni Tsumi ɔ sisi – “Rearing for Food and Jobs module”. Ngɛ blɔ nya tomi nɛ ɔ mi ɔ ni komɛ a nine su kungwɔ tsuhi nɛ a wo a mi batle nɛ a ma nyɛ kɛ lɛ kungwɔhi nɛ a yibɔ su 120 ngɛ be kake mi nɔ.
