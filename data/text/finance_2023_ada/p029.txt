52. Ngɛ jeha 2022 mi ɔ, a gba tso bubui ayɔ 26.55 kɛ kpa Ghana ma a tsuo mi ngɛ blɔ nya tomi ko nɛ a wo lɛ biɛ ke “Pee Ghana Ba Mumu” (Green Ghana Day) blɔ nya tomi ɔ sisi. Ngɛ blɔ nya tomi nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, a nyɛ nɛ a du tso bubui ayɔ 24.44. Kɛ piɛɛ he ɔ, a du tso bubui ayɔ 12.9 nɛ ekomɛ ji Ofram, Emire, Wawa, Kokrodua, Teak, Cedrela, Gmelina, Cassia, Eucalyptus kɛ Mahogany.

55. Je ɔ tsuo mi la dɔmi ɔ ngɔɔ nyagba aloo haomi slɔɔto kɛ ba haa je ɔ tsuo sika he blɔ nya tomi ɔ nɛ e ngɔ nyagba nɛ ɔmɛ hu kɛ ba saa adesa he wami nami, niye ni nami kɛ mahi nɛ a sika he blɔ nya tomi yi emu kpakpa ko kaa Ghana a.
