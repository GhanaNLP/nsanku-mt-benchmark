Sika He Ni Tsumi Blɔ Nɔ Tomihi
<<<P>>>
20. Kɛ piɛɛ sika gblami blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ he ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ ngɔ sika he ni tsumi titlii nɛ ɔmɛ kɛ fɔ si konɛ e ye kɛ bua sika blɔ nɔ tomi ɔmɛ.
<<<P>>>
• Amlaalo ɔ maa bɔ nikɔtɔmahi a nɔ hyɛmi sika nɛ ji LEAP ɔ he kɛ ha nihi nɛ a sa hami ɔmɛ. A ma hue aloo wo amlɔ nɛ ɔ sika a nɔ kɛ je sika sidi 45 nɔ ɔ nɔ kɛ ya su sika sidi 90. Jehanɛ ɔ se hu a ma hue aloo wo nihi nɛ a sa sika nɛ ɔ hami ɔ a yibɔ ɔ nɔ nɛ a hiɛmi nɛ e be amlɔ nɛ ɔ yibɔ 344,185 ɔ nɔ. Blɔ nɔ tomi nɛ ɔ ma bli LEAP sika nɛ a po fɔ si ɔ mi kɛ je sika sidi ayɔ 197.5 ngɛ jeha 2022 mi ɔ kɛ ba da sidi ayɔ 395;
<<<P>>>
• A maa bli sika nɛ a po fɔ si kɛ ha nihi nɛ a hooɔ ni ha sukuu bi ɔmɛ a mi nɛ a maa ngɔ sika sidi ayɔ 138 kɛ gbe yi ha mɛ;
<<<P>>>
• A ma tsa ma ni tsuli nɛ ji Ma nɔ yelɔ ɔ, Ma nɔ Yelɔ Se Yelɔ ɔ, Minista amɛ kɛ a se yeli ɔmɛ, MMDCE ɔmɛ kɛ Ma nɔ hyɛmi ni tsuli ekpa amɛ ngɛ Amlaalo ni tsumi he ɔmɛ a hiɔwo nɔ gbɔmi lafa mi gbami nyingmi etɛ (30%) ɔ nɔ;
<<<P>>>
• A ma gbɔ sikahi nɛ a pi a nɔ nine kɛ ha ni komɛ a tsumi ɔmɛ a nɔ kɛ je amlɔ nɛ ɔ lafa mi gbami 25 kɛ ba da lafa mi gbami 17.5;
<<<P>>>
• A maa ngɔ sikahi tsuo nɛ a pi a nɔ nine kɛ ha ni komɛ a tsumi ɔ kɛ ba wo GIFMIS blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ sisi konɛ a hyɛ kaa a maa da GIFMIS blɔ nɔ tomi nɛ ɔ nɔ kɛ tsu a sika gblami kɛ a sika he ni tsumi blɔ nɔ tomi ɔmɛ tsuo a he ni ke jeha 2023 ke e baa nyagbe ɔ.
<<<P>>>
• A maa ngɔ kpangmi kɛ fɔ Ma Ni Tsumi He komɛ (SOEs) a ni tsuli a hiɔwo nɔ, nɛ eko ya hiɔwe nɛ e ba be ma ni tsuli nɛ a ngɛ Hiɔ Womi Kpa Kake Blɔ Nɔ Tomi ɔ (Single Spine Salary Structure) nɔ ɔ nɔ ɔ, a daa jeha sika nɔ nɛ a daa kɛ hyɛɔ a hiɔwo hiɔwe yami ɔ;
