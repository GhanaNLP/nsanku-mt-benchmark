9. Sikahi tsuo nɛ to hemi gblaa kɛ baa kɛ He dooli a nike sika tsuo nɛ a bua a nya a ya su Ghana sidi ayɔ 65,399 nɛ daa si kɛ ha lafa mi gbami 11.0 ke waa kɛ to blɔ hyɛmi nɔ nɛ a po fo si nɛ ji Ghana sidi ayɔ 67,307 nɛ daa si ha lafa mi gbami 11.4 ngɛ GDP nɔ kɛ nɔ nɛ nine su nɔ nɛ yibɔ su sidi ayɔ 49,108 nɛ lɔ ɔ hu peeɔ lafa mi gbami 10.7 ngɛ GDP nɔ ngɛ jeha 2021 ɔ mi.

10. Ke waa kɛ to blɔ hyɛmi tɛ nɛ a po fɔ si ɔ he ngɛ be nɛ a tu he munyu ɔ mi ɔ, tohi a sika kɛ he dooli a sika nɛ ba amɛ tsuo a nɔ gbɔmi ɔ pee Ghana sidi ɔ ayɔ 1,909 nɛ daa si kɛ ha lafa mi gbami 2.8 nɛ e daa jeha nɔ yami ɔ ko su lafa mi gbami 3.2. Nɔ gbɔmi nɛ ba ngɛ sikahi nɛ to hemi kɛ baa blɔ fa mi ɔ ya nɔ ejakaa tohi a sika nɔ yami ɔ ba si ngɛ nihi nɛ a kɛ sika ba amɛ tsuo a blɔ fa mi.
