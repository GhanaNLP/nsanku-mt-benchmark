“Ma a Sika He Blɔ Nya Tomi Susumi Tsɔ Ɔmɛ Kpalemi Kɛ Ba Da A Glɛ Nɔ, A He Piɛ Pomi Kɛ Da Tɛ Kake Nɔ Kɛ Gu Blɔnya Tomi Kpakpahi A Nɔ”

Lohwehi a Lɛmi kɛ Niye ni Nami (Rearing For Food and Jobs): Enɛ ɔ maa ngɔ kedimi kpakpa kɛ ba lohwe lɛmi ni tsumi ɔ mi nɛ e ma hue ni tsumi ɔ nya nɔ nɛ e gbɔ lohwehi a sɛmi kɛ ba ma a mi ɔ nɔ. Enɛ ɔ maa ngɔ ni tsumi kɛ ba ha nihi slɔɔto nɛ a ngɔ a he kɛ wo lohwe lɛmi ni tsumi ɔ mi nɛ maa ngɔ nɔ yami hu kɛ ba a si himi mi.
<<<P>>>
Hɛ Ngmɛ Blimi: Hɛ blimi kɛ fɔ nɔ ko nɔ nɛ ma tle he numi ngɛ nɔ ko he aloo nɔ ko mi si.
<<<P>>>
Ni Tsumi Kpokploo kɛ Nɔ Nɛ Kle Bɔɔ (SMEs): A ji dɛhe ni tsumi tsɔwi nɛ a nyɛ we ni tsuli babauu ngɔmi.
<<<P>>>
Ma Nihi a He Se Nami: Amlaalo ɔ woɔ hiɔ aloo to tsɔwitsɔwi ngɛ nyu, pɛtloo kɛ oslaba he wami kane kɛ ni ɔ ekomɛ a peemi he ɔmɛ a nɔ kɛ ha ma bi aloo ngɛ ma bi a nane mi.
<<<P>>>
Skuadlɔ: E ji kɔɔhiɔ mi aslaafohi a ni tsumi kɔni nɛ ngɛ hiɔwe lɛ kaa enyɔ kɛ se kɛ kɔɔhiɔ mi lɛ nɛ ɔmɛ a mi kɛ he ni tsuli.
<<<P>>>
Hiɔ Nɔ Gbɔmi aloo Jemi Sika: Sika nɛ ɔ ji hiɔ nɔ gbɔmi sika nɛ Amlaalo ɔ kɛ haa ma bi nɛ a kɛ je ohia nyagbahi a nɔ.
<<<P>>>
Hɛ Ngmɛ kɛ Nɔ Mi Womi: Blɔ nya tomi nɛ ɔ ji nihi a he akɔtaa bumi pɛpɛɛpɛ aloo hɛ ngmɛ kɛ nihi a mi womi fitsofitso kɛ nihi a he hɛ tomi ngɛ juɛmi nɛ mi kuɔ mi.
<<<P>>>
Wo Oslɔke: E ji wo nɔ oslɔke ngua nɛ wo mi gbigbi simi kɛ vokano ngɔɔ kɛ baa.
<<<P>>>
Ni Tsumi Mi Heii Peemi: Nɔ nɛ ɔ yaa nɔ ke Amlaalo ɔ to blɔ nya kɛ ha amaniɛbɔ mlamlaamla ngɛ nihi nɛ e ngɛ pee ɔmɛ a he.
<<<P>>>
Blɔ Ngua: E ji gbɛjegbɛ nɛ tsaa ma ngua enyɔ aloo fuu, nyu nɔ lɛ dahehi, kɔɔhiɔ mi lɛ dahehi kɛ he ekpa komɛ nɛ a pee kɛ ha lɔle gagahi kɛ nɔ nɛmɛ nɛ a wo tlomi nɛ mi jiɔ.
<<<P>>>
Nihi a He Ni Tsumi Sika: Enɛ ɔ ji sika nɛ Amlaalo ɔ woɔ kɛ ha e ni tsumi ɔmɛ a blɔ nya tomi kɛ ha ma bi.
<<<P>>>
Mi Jiimi To (VAT): Enɛ ɔ ji to nɛ a kɛ fɔɔ kpɔ mi kpɔ mi nɛ nihi gbleɔ ngɛ a peemi mi ɔ nɔ.
<<<P>>>
V20 (Mahi Nɛ Adebɔ Nyagba Tsɔ We A He): A ji mahi nɛ adebɔ je mi tsakemi nyagba pɔɔ a sika blɔ nya tomi jemi ngɛ kla aloo glɛ nɔ.
