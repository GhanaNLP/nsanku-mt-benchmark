NYA BLIMI
<<<P>>>
Nɔ jemi be he kaa jeha nɛ ɔ pee jeha ko nɛ nyagba ba sɛ wa ma sika he blɔ nya tomi ɔ mi wawɛɛ pe jehahi nɛ a be ɔmɛ. Kaa bɔ nɛ e ba ngɛ maje mahi tsuo a nɔ ɔ, Covid-19 hiɔ yayami ɔ ba na wo hu benɛ wa sɛ jeha 2020 mi bɔɔ pɛ. Nyagba nɛ ɔ ba ba wa ma sika blɔ nya tomi ɔ si wawɛɛ nɛ enɛ ɔ ha nɛ wa ma sika blɔ nya tomi ɔmɛ a nɔ ba gbɔ nɛ e kɛ hiɔ nɛ wa dla we wa he kɛ to kɛ ha hiɔ ehe nɛ ji Covid-19 nɛ e ba nɛ ɔ e tsami ɔ ba piɛɛ wa hiɔ yemi ɔmɛ a he.
<<<P>>>
Be mi nɛ wa susu kaa wa ngɛ wa he jee kɛ maa je hiɔ yayami nɛ ɔ sisi kɛ gu nihi nɛ waa kɛ puɛ si kɛ ha hiɔ nɛ ɔ e blɔ womi ɔ, yobu ekpa komɛ hu ba na wɔ nɛ e ba je wa ma sika blɔ nɔ tomi mɔde nɛ wa bɔ kɛ je jeha 2017 kɛ ba su jeha 2019 ɔ tsuo kɛ je glɛ nɔ. Kekleekle nɔ ji Russia kɛ Ukraine a ta nɛ ba a, kɛ jahanɛ ɔ se hu ni komɛ a se nɛ wa kpɛ ngɛ a nɔ kplɛɛmi mi ngɛ 2022 ma he bumi kɛ sika blɔ nya tomi mi ɔ hu ba ba bumi aloo bu nɛ ngɛ sika he ni tsumi blɔ nya tomi ɔ he ɔ si. He ni bemi nya a, maje maje jua nɛ Ghana hu woɔ nga waa kɛ mɛ wa jua yemi sinya a nɛ ma a mi sika he blɔ nya tomi ɔmɛ ba mia. Enɛ ɔ ha sika pami blɔ nya tomi ɔ ho hiɔwe ya nɛ wa hiɔ yemi ɔ mi ba bli nɛ enɛ ɔ tsɛ wa sidi ɔ yi se nɛ e he wami ɔ nɔ bɔni gbɔmi kɛ fo.
<<<P>>>
Ekohu ɔ, yemi kɛ buami nɛ maje sika mi he wami nami nɛ wa naa kɛ jeɔ maje jua yemi ɔ mi ɔ nɔ ba gbɔ. Nɛ jehanɛ se ɔ, wa kuɔ si nɛ jɔɛ kɛ ha mase nihi a sɛmi kaa ma a kɛ wa mase hiɔhi nɛ wa nyɛ we womi ɔ tsa nɔ kɛ gbee wa sika a he wami ɔ si. Enɛ ɔ ha nɛ ma a mi si himi kɛ nihi tsuo a he jua ba wa wawɛɛ ha Amlaalo ɔ kɛ ma bi tsuo. Pɛtloo kɛ lble mi nu a jua ba kuɔ kɛ ho hiɔwe ya nɛ enɛ ɔ ba hue nijua ni kɛ jua yemi ni kpahi tsuo a juahi a nɔ nɛ a he jua wami hiɔwe yami ɔ ya su lafa mi gbami 40.4 ngɛ jeha 2022 ngɛ Kɔle hlami ɔ mi.
