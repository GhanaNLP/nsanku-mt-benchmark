46. Kɛ ba to Kɔle hlami ɔ nyagbe ngɛ 2022 mi ɔ, Blɔ He Nɛ E Hyɛɔ Nihewi Kɛ Yihewi A Ni Tumi Hami Nɔ ɔ “Youth Employment Agency (YEA)” nyɛ ngɔ nihi akpe 80,735 kɛ wo ni tsumi slɔɔtohi a mi.
<<<P>>>
47. Ngɛ jeha 2022 mi ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ da blɔ nɔ tomi ko nɛ a tsɛɔ lɛ ke “National Alternative Employment and Livelihood Programme (NAELP)” ɔ nɔ nɛ e kɛ;
<<<P>>>
• Kpɔ aloo a kpale dla hue tso komɛ nɛ a klemi su hɛkta 645 nɛ a puɛ be ko nɛ be ɔ. Enɛ ɔ ngɔ ni tsumi nɛ be akpe 2,300 nɔ kɛ ba; nɛ
<<<P>>>
• Ni tsumi nɛ ɔ ha nɛ a bu ni bubui ayɔ 20 nɛ a kɛ wo ni dumi blɔ nya tomi ɔ mi eka. Blɔ nɔ tomi nɛ ɔ pee “a ya nɛ a ba” ni tsumi kɛ ni tsumi pɔtɛɛ kɛ ha nihe kɛ yihe akpe 80,071 kɛ se ngɛ mahi kaa Akotom, Adinkra, Techire, Akwatiakwaso, Fufuo kɛ Hohoe ma amɛ a nɔ.
<<<P>>>
48. Ngɛ jeha 2023 mi ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ ma tsa nɔ nɛ e maa:
<<<P>>>
• E ma kpɔ nɛ e ma dla zugbahi nɛ a puɛ nɛ a klemi su hɛkta akpe 1000 ngɛ ma pɔtɛɛ komɛ a nɔ. A maa bu tsohi nɛ a he maa ba se nami kaa agɔle ngmɛ tso, ta, akutu tso kɛ e henɔ ɔmɛ kɛ mangoo tso kaa ayɔ kake nɛ a kɛ bli tso dumi kɛ hue tso mi blimi ni tsumi ɔ mi;
<<<P>>>
• A ma bli ni bubui hehi nyɔngma (10) ngɛ kpo ɔ ekomɛ a mi nɛ enɛ ɔ maa ngɔ ni tsumi tutuutu aloo pɔtɛɛ akpe lafa (100,000) kɛ ba; nɛ
<<<P>>>
• A ma tsɔse nihi nɛ a juɔ si ya tsuaa zu mi juɛni kɛ nihi nɛ a maa ju si ya tsua zu mi juɛni akpe nyingmi enyɔ (20,000).
