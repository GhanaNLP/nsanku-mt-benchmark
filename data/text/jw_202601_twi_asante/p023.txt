10 Ɔsomafo Paul tuu Timoteo fo sɛ ɔsom a ɔde ma Yehowa no ‘ɔmfa n’adwene nyinaa nsi so,’ na nea ɔbɛyɛ ama ne som atu mpɔn nso, ‘onwinnwen ho.’ (Kenkan 1 Timoteo 4:13, 15.) Enti bɔ mmɔden fa saa afotu no kɔ adwinnwen mu, na hwehwɛ akwan a wubetumi afa so asom Yehowa yiye, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ‘wobɛyɛ hyew honhom mu.’ (Rom. 12:11) Yɛbɛyɛ nhwɛso a, nea yebesua wɔ adesua ase no, wubetumi asua no yiye ansa na woakɔ, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wubetumi de w’adwene adi adesua no akyi. Anaa sɛ woresua ade a, wubetumi apɛ baabi a ɛhɔ yɛ dinn akɔhyɛ na woatumi adwinnwen nea woresua no ho. Woyɛ saa a, ɛbɛma woanya nea woresua no so mfaso kɛse. Sɛ wosɔ gya na wopɛ sɛ ɛkɔ so dɛw a, wobɛkɔ so ara de nnyina ahyehyɛ mu. Nneɛma a Yehowa ayɛ ama yɛn a agyede no ka ho no nso, sɛ wopɛ sɛ wokyerɛ ho anisɔ a, nea yɛaka ho asɛm yi nyinaa ɛsɛ sɛ woyere wo ho yɛ no kɛse. Afei nso, sɛ aka nnawɔtwe kakra ma yɛakae Yesu wu no a, nhyira a Yehowa hwie gu n’Adansefo so no, wubetumi adwinnwen ho. Sɛ woyɛ saa a, ɛbɛboa wo ma woanya anisɔ kɛse ama agyede no, efisɛ ɛno na abue kwan ama woatumi ne Yehowa afa adamfo.
