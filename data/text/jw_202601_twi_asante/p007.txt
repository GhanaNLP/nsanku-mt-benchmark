16 Bere biara a Dawid betie Yehowa no, na ɔboa no ma osisi gyinae pa, na na ɛma Dawid ani gye. Enti Yehowa faa saa kwan yi so bɔɔ ne ho ban. (Fa toto Mmebusɛm 5:1, 2 ho.) Nanso bere a Dawid buu Yehowa mmara so no, ɛso nsunsuanso no de, Yehowa ammɔ ne ho ban amfi ho. (2 Sam. 12:9, 10) Ɛtɔ mmere bi nso a, na nkurɔfo teetee Dawid ma ohu amane. Ɛba saa a, na Dawid fi ne komam bɔ Yehowa mpae. Dɛn na Yehowa yɛe de bɔɔ ne ho ban? Ɔkyekyee Dawid werɛ, na ɔmaa no awerɛhyem sɛ ɔdɔ no paa, na ɔbɛhwɛ no.—Dw. 23:1-6.
<<<P>>>
17 Sɛ yɛbɔ mpae hwehwɛ Yehowa akwankyerɛ de sisi gyinae a, na yɛresuasua Dawid. Nanso ɛsɛ sɛ yɛkae sɛ, sɛ yɛantie Yehowa na yesisi gyinae bɔne a, ebia yebehu amane, na ɛno de Yehowa renyi yɛn mfi mu. (Gal. 6:7, 8) Afei nso, sɛ nkurɔfo ma yehu amane a, yebetumi aka yɛn komam asɛm akyerɛ Yehowa, na yɛanya awerɛhyem sɛ ɔbɛbɔ yɛn adwene ne yɛn koma ho ban.—Filip. 4:6, 7.

MA ONYANKOPƆN NKƆ SO NKYERƐ WO KWAN
<<<P>>>
18 Afe 2026 afe asɛm no ka sɛ: “Anigye ne wɔn a wohu sɛ wohia Onyankopɔn akwankyerɛ.” Ennɛ paa na wuhu sɛ saa asɛm no yɛ nokware. Adɛn nti na yɛreka saa? Efisɛ ɛnnɛ nnipa bebree nnye ntom sɛ wɔhia Onyankopɔn akwankyerɛ. Afoforo nso gye Onyankopɔn di, nanso wɔwɔ atorosom mu anaa nnipa nyansa na ɛkyerɛ wɔn kwan. Wei nti wɔn ani nnye. Ɛnsɛ sɛ yesuasua wɔn. Ende, dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ? Ɛsɛ sɛ yɛkɔ so sua Onyankopɔn Asɛm no yiye, yɛhyɛ suban foforo no, na yetie Yehowa ma ɔbɔ yɛn ho ban.
<<<P>>>
18. Ɛnnɛ, dɛn na nnipa bebree nnye ntom, na dɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ na ama Yehowa akɔ so akyerɛ yɛn kwan? (Hwɛ mfoni no nso.)
<<<P>>>
MFONI HO NKYERƐKYERƐMU Anim: Onuawa bi pɛ sɛ Yehowa bɔ ne ho ban, enti ɔne ne nuanom Kristofo ahyiam wɔ adesua ase. Kratafa 6: Onuawa koro yi ara resua Ɔwɛn-Aban, ɔreda ayamye adi akyerɛ afoforo de akyerɛ sɛ wahyɛ suban foforo no. Afei nso, asafo mu mpanyimfo akɔsra no rehyɛ no nkuran, na wayɛ aso retie wɔn. Wei nyinaa kyerɛ sɛ wama Yehowa rekyerɛ no kwan.
