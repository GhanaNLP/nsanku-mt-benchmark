Ɛsɛ sɛ yɛkɔ so sua Onyankopɔn Asɛm no yiye, yɛhyɛ suban foforo no, na yɛma Yehowa bɔ yɛn ho ban (Hwɛ nkyekyɛm 18)
<<<P>>>
afuw obi anaa yɛakeka yɛn adwene akyerɛ no mpo. Ɛba saa a, ɛnsɛ sɛ yɛma yɛn abam bu. Mmom ɛsɛ sɛ yɛkɔ so yere yɛn ho yɛ nsakrae biara a ehia. (Rom. 12:1, 2; Efe. 4:24) Bere a yɛreyɛ saa no, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛkae saa asɛm yi: Ataade de, sɛ ɛso sen yɛn anaa ɛkyere yɛn a, gye sɛ yɛyɛ ho biribi ansa na yɛatumi ahyɛ. Nanso suban foforo no de, sɛ yebetumi ahyɛ a, gye sɛ yɛn mmom yɛyɛ nsakrae, na yɛde Yehowa akwankyerɛ bɔ yɛn bra na ama yɛasɔ n’ani.

SUASUA DAWID—MA YEHOWA MMƆ WO HO BAN
<<<P>>>
14 Yɛabehu sɛ, sɛ yɛn ani betumi agye a, ɛsɛ sɛ yesua Onyankopɔn Asɛm no, na yɛhyɛ suban foforo no. Nanso ɛnyɛ ɛno nko ara; yehia Onyankopɔn ahobammɔ nso. Ɛnde, akwan bɛn na Yehowa fa so bɔ yɛn ho ban, na dɛn na ehia sɛ yɛyɛ na wabɔ yɛn ho ban?
<<<P>>>
14-15. Ɔkwan bɛn na Yehowa fa so bɔ ne nkurɔfo ho ban nnɛ? (Dwom 27:5) (Hwɛ mfoni a ɛwɔ Ɔwɛn-Aban yi anim no nso.)
<<<P>>>
15 Ná Ɔhene Dawid gye di paa sɛ mmere bɔne mu mpo Yehowa bɛbɔ ne ho ban. (Kenkan Dwom 27:5.) Ɔkwan bɛn na Yehowa fa so bɔ yɛn ho ban nnɛ? Baako ne sɛ, obiara anaa biribiara a ebetumi asɛe yɛn gyidi no, Yehowa bɔ yɛn ho ban fi ho. Enti sɛ nkurɔfo sɔre tia yɛn sɛ dɛn mpo a, wahyɛ yɛn bɔ sɛ, akode biara a wɔbɛyɛ de aba yɛn so no renyɛ yiye. (Dw. 34:7; Yes. 54:17) Ɛwom, Satan ne n’apamfo no wɔ tumi, nanso wɔrentumi nteetee yɛn afebɔɔ. Sɛ wɔkum yɛn mpo a, Yehowa benyan yɛn aba nkwa mu bio. (1 Kor. 15:55-57; Adi. 21:3, 4) Afei nso, sɛ dadwen hyɛ yɛn so a, Yehowa boa yɛn ma yetumi gyina ano, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɛrensɛe yɛne ne ntam. (Mmeb. 12:25; Mat. 6:27-29) Wei da nkyɛn a, wama yɛn anuanom bebree a wɔboa yɛn, ne asafo mu mpanyimfo a wɔsrasra yɛn hyɛ yɛn nkuran. (Yes. 32:1, 2) Sɛ yɛkɔ adesua nso a, Yehowa kyerɛkyerɛ yɛn ma yehu nneɛma pɔtee a yɛyɛ a ɛbɛma wabɔ yɛn ho ban.—Heb. 10:24, 25.
