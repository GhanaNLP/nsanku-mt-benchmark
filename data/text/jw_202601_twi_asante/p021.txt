4 Sɛ woresua Bible no a, kae sɛ pene a woapene so resua Bible no ara mpo kyerɛ sɛ Yehowa na ɔretwe wo aba ne nkyɛn. (Yoh. 6:44; Aso. 13:48) Bible ka sɛ “nea ɔhwehwɛ komam ne Yehowa.” Wei kyerɛ sɛ, sɛ Yehowa hu mmɔden biara a worebɔ sɛ wubesua ne ho ade, ne nsakrae biara a woreyɛ na woatumi adi ne mmara so a, ɛyɛ ne fɛ kwa. (Mmeb. 17:3; 27:11) Agyede no nti, wubetumi ne Yehowa afa adamfo denneennen. (Rom. 5:10, 11) Mma wo werɛ mfi saa nokwasɛm yi da.
<<<P>>>
5 Sɛ woyɛ obi a wo ne Yehowa adansefo resua ade a, ɛnde, dɛn na wobɛyɛ de akyerɛ sɛ w'ani sɔ agyede no? Ade baako a wubetumi ayɛ ne sɛ wubetie afotu a ɔsomafo Paul de maa Filipifo no. Ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ: “Baabi a yɛanya nkɔso akodu no, momma yɛnkɔ so nnantew pɛpɛɛpɛ wɔ saa kwan yi ara so.” (Filip. 3:16) Bible nkyerɛase foforo nso kyerɛ saa kyerɛwsɛm yi ase sɛ: “Baabi a yɛde yɛn ani akyerɛ no, yɛnhwɛ so nkɔ yɛn anim tee.” Enti, kae sɛ nkwa kwan no ara na wonam so no, enti mma biribiara ntwe wo nsan.—Mat. 7:14; Luka 9:62.
<<<P>>>
6 Nnansa yi woasua biribi wɔ Bible mu a wonte ase anaa? Sɛ saa a, yɛ ho nhwehwɛmu na bɔ mpae srɛ Yehowa sɛ ɔmmoa wo na te ase yiye. (Dw. 86:11) Sɛ ne nyinaa akyi woante ase a, wo de fa to hɔ ansa, nanso nnyae Bible no sua. Ebi nso a, subammɔne bi a Bible kasa tia na ayɛ den ama wo sɛ wubegyae. Sɛ saa a, kae sɛ Yehowa nhwɛ kwan sɛ yɛbɛyɛ nea ɛboro yɛn so. Enti nea ɔka sɛ yɛnyɛ biara, wubetumi ayɛ. (Kenkan Deuteronomium 30:11-14.)
