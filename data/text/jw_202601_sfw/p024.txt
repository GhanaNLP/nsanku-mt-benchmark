13 2 Korintofoɛ 13:5 kã kyɛ: “Émɔneanea ɛmɔ ahone nu bɛnea sɛ ɛmɔtɛtira gyidie ne anu a.” Kyɛbɔ sɔ tworɔnzɛm ne kyire ne, ɔwɔ nu alaa a odikyɛ yebisa yɛnwo dwirɛ biemɔ. Ebie yeɛ ole kyɛ: ‘Aso Yehowa som nwo té hia me anaa?’ (Mat. 6:33) ‘Bɔ mefa megye menye ne, okyire kyɛ mekyi bɔne anaa?’ (Edw. 97: 10) ‘Aso subaen bɔ meda ye ali ne, okyire kyɛ mepena kyɛ asomdwee tena asafo nanu hɔ?’ (Ɛfe. 4:2, 3) Odwu mmerɛ bɔ yɛkae Yesu wue ne a, ɛyɛ a yɛdwenedwene bɔ Yehowa ne Yesu ayɛ ama yɛ nanwo. Yeti odwu sɔ mmerɛ ne a, ɔte pá kyɛ yekobisa yɛnwo sɔ dwirɛ nemɔ. Ofikyɛ ɔkɔmaa yekonwu ye kyɛ yɛyɛ bɔ yɛ bɔbɔ yekuro, anaa yɛyɛ bɔ Kristo nye gye yenwo ɔ.*

14. Nzu yeɛ ebiaa wamaa aliemaa biemɔ ayakyi asafo ne ɔ?
<<<P>>>
na nu dadwene’ yeɛ ne wahyɛ bɛ so ɔ. (Luka 21:34) Ebiemɔ koso, edwirɛ bie bɔ aliemaa bie hane, anaa nikyeebie bɔ ɔyɛle ne, yeɛ ɔtole bɛ sintidua ɔ. (Yak. 3:2) Yeɛ ebiemɔ wora bɛyɛle bɔne pirikua bie, na ne ɔyɛ se ma bɛ kyɛ bɛkɔbɔ mbanyi nemɔ amaneɛ. Ɔmva yenwo ne bɔ ɔmaa ɛyakyili asafo ne, nzu yeɛ ɔkɔboka wɔ maa ekosa kɔwa ɔ, na sɛɛ yeɛ Yesu wue ne bɔ ɛkɔdwenedwene yenwo ne koso kɔboka wɔ maa ɛkɔyɛ sɔ ɔ?

15 Bɛbɔ bayakyi asafo ne, Yehowa yelle bɛ nu, na mmom onwu ye kyɛ bɛte kyɛ mmoaen bɔ bamini ɔ. Yeti ɔpena bɛ boso, na sɛ onwu bɛ a, ɔboka bɛ maa besa bɛba asafo nanu. Sɛ ɛdwenedwene sɔ dwirɛ nanwo a ɔkɔboka wɔ paa. (Kenga Hesekiel 34:11, 12, 16.) Bɛbɔ Yehowa pena bɛ boso ne, ɛboka bɛ so hɔ? Yóo. Ɛwɛnelɛ Waen he bɔ ɛkenga ne, ɛhene ngome po kyire kyɛ eté le ɔdɔ ma Yehowa. Wɔde nea oo, mmerɛ bɔ ne esuanne Bible ne po Yehowa nwuni kyɛ ele subaen pá bie, yeti ɔhwene wɔ wale asafo nanu. Yede ɛnea a sɔ mmerɛ he yeɛ ɔkɔyakyi wɔ nu anaa?
