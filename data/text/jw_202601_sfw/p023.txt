10 Somafoɛ Pɔɔlo tuli Timoteo foɛ kyɛ nningyein bɔ ɔyɛ ye wɔ Yehowa som nu ne, ‘ɔdwenedwene yenwo’ nea kyɛbɔ osi yɛ ye ne, na afei ‘otu yenwo sie berɛ bɔkɔɔ’ ɔma sɔ nningyein nemɔ. (Kenga 1 Timoteo 4:13, 15.) Wɔ koso sɛ ɛfa sɔ afutue ne yɛ adwuma a ɔkɔboka wɔ. Yeti nningyein bɔ ɛyɛ ye wɔ Yehowa som nu ne, sa dwenedwene yenwo, na nea kyɛbɔ ɛkɔfa anwokeka kɔyɛ ɔ. Sɛ ɛyɛ sɔ a ɔkɔmaa ‘ɛkɔyɛ hye wɔ sunzum nu.’ (Rom. 12:11) Ebie yeɛ ole kyɛ, koraka ɛkɔhɔ adesua ne, dimoa sua bɔ yekosusu yenwo ne. Ɛyɛ sɔ a ɔkɔboka wɔ maa sɛ ɛkɔ a, ɛkɔhora kɔfa w’adwene kosie dwumadie naso. Sɛ wɔ bɔbɔ ésua nikyee koso a, ɛkɔhora kɔpena nekaabie bɔ ɛberɛ te dinn kɔtena kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a nikyefee ngɔtwetwe w’adwene ɔ. Ɛhene kɔmaa ɛkɔhora kɔdwenedwene bɔ esua nanwo na anya so mvasoɛ. Sɛ ɛyɛ sɔ nningyein hemɔ a ɔkɔyɛ kyɛ ebie bɔ okunya see nu maa ɔsɔ ne, kyire kyɛ ɔdɔ bɔ ele ma Yehowa ne, ɔkɔyɛ piri. Ɔba sɔ a, bɔ wayɛ wama wɔ ne mukoraati bɔ ebie yeɛ ole Yesu wue ne, wɔnye kɔsɔ. Yeti sɛ ɔka kãa maa yɛkae Yesu wue ne a, nhyiraa bɔ Yehowa afa alo wɔ bɔ ebie yeɛ ole kyɛ ahoraa aayɛ ye damvo ne, ɛyɛ a dwenedwene yenwo.
