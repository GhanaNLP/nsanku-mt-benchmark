YESU WUE NATI, YEHOWA KƆHORA KƆFA YƐ BƆNE KƆHYƐ YƐ
<<<P>>>
4 Ɔnate kyɛ bɔne wɔ yɛnwo nati, yedi mvomsoɛ wɔ yɛ dwudwolɛ ne yɛ nneyɛɛ nu. (Kenga Nworɛ Dwirɛhanɛniɛ 7:20.) Ɛhene ati ne odikyɛ Yesu wu ma yɛ, kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a yekonya bɔne fakyɛ ɔ. Bɔ ɔte yeɛ ole kyɛ bɔne bie wɔ berɛ a, ɔte piri paa na sɛ ebie yɛ bie a, ne bɛkora bekũ ye. Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ aberɛ so sɔa, sɛ ne ebie sɛkye agyaa, anaa okũ sona a, ne Mose Mmraa ne kyire kyɛ odikyɛ bekũ ye. (Lew. 20:10; Num. 35:30, 31) Nakoso, yɛkae kyɛ bɔne biala te bɔne, ɔmva yenwo kyɛ ɔte piri anaa ɔte kãa ne. Sɛ yɛkɔyɛ nneasoɛ a, Dawide hane kyɛ, “Ngɔnea m’atee bɔ nnate so ɔ yé, amaa mamva me noa mangekã edwirɛ bɔ ɔte bɔne ɔ.” (Edw. 39:1) Ɛhe maa yenwu ye kyɛ, yɛnoa dwudwolɛ po kora maa yɛyɛ bɔne. —Yak. 3:2.
