BƆ ADESŨA NE KƆHÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Sɛ yɛ wora bɔ anaa y’abanu bu a, yekonwu bɔ yɛkɔhora yɛkɔyɛ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Sɛ Nningyein Hyɛ Wɔ So Maa W’abanu Bu A, Nzu Yeɛ Ɔkɔboka Wɔ Ɔ?
<<<P>>>
“Aa, meyɛ anwumvo!”—ROM. 7:24.
<<<P>>>
SƐ YƐTE somafoɛ Pɔɔlo nwo dwirɛ a, ebiaa bɔ ɔba yɛ tianu yeɛ ole kyɛ, ne ole akokoduro yeti ɔhane nzɛmba ne wɔ maen pee so. Ne osa se ngyerɛkyerɛ, na ɔhworɔle Bible nu nwomaa pee nala. Nakoso, ne Pɔɔlo te sona tekyɛ yɛ ala. Yeti odwu mmerɛ bie a, ne nningyein pee kora maa y’abanu bu.
<<<P>>>
2 Kenga Romanfoɛ 7:21-24. Pɔɔlo hane bɔ ɔmaa ye abanu buli nanwo dwirɛ wɔ ye krataa bɔ ɔhworɔ ɔɔmane Romanfoɛ nanu. Ne Pɔɔlo yere yenwo paa kyɛ ɔkɔyɛ bɔ Nyameɛ kuro ɔ. Nakoso ɔnate kyɛ ne ɔte sona bɔ bɔne wɔ yenwo nati, odwu mmerɛ bie a ne ɔkora yɛ se ma ye paa kyɛ ɔkɔyɛ sɔ. Afei koso sɛ ne ɔkae nningyein biemɔ bɔ ɔyɛle kora yeɛ ɔbaayɛ Kristoniɛ, ɔne ɔhaw bɔ ne ɔfa nu wɔ ye asetena nu ne a, ne ɔmaa ye wora bɔ paa. Yenu pee nala wɔ berɛ a, sɔ yeɛ ɔto yɛ ɔ. Yeti bɔ ɔtole Pɔɔlo ne, yɛkɔhora yɛkɔte yebo.
<<<P>>>
3 Ɔwɔ nu, odwu mmerɛ bie a ne Pɔɔlo abanu abu,* nakoso wamma ɛhene nwo adwenedwene anhyɛ ye so. Yede yebisa
<<<P>>>
* Bɛbɔ ɔwɔ nu alaa a ne b’abanu abu anaa bɛ wora abɔ ne, bɛ yeɛ adesua he kã bɛnwo dwirɛ ɔ, na mmom ɔtte bɛbɔ daa ne adwenedwene ahyɛ bɛ so ne. Bɛde, odikyɛ bɛfa bɛkɔ hɔspitel.
<<<P>>>
1-2. Odwu mmerɛ bie a, ɔhaw beni yeɛ ne Pɔɔlo fa nu ɔ, na bɔ ne ɔfa nu ne, nzuati yeɛ yɛkɔhora yɛkɔte yebo ɔ? (Romanfoɛ 7:21-24)
<<<P>>>
