18 Eliza bɔ yahã yenwo dwirɛ ne yɛle Yob dwirɛ nanwo nhwehwɛmuɛ. Ɔhane kyɛ: “Bɔ ɔtole Yob ne, ne ɔtte angorɛ; ɔhaw he ba a ne foforɛ li so. Ɛhe mukoraati maa ye abanu buli, nakoso wanyakyi Yehowa som, ɔmva yenwo kyɛ ne ɔnze bɔ wamaa onwu sɔ amaneɛ ne. Me koso edwirɛ pee yeɛ wato me ɔ, yeti ɔmaa mete bɔ Yob fale nu nabo paa. Amba ye dwirɛ ne bɔ nzuane ne, ɔbokale me paa.” (Yob 42:1-6) Diane koso yalimoa yahã yenwo dwirɛ, na ɔhane kyɛ: “Me ne me hu yesua Bɛn Yehowa nwomaa ne, na waboka yɛ maa yanwu ye kyɛ Yehowa mva ye adwene ngɔ mvomsoɛ bɔ yedi naso. Na mmom ɔte kyɛ ebie bɔ ɔfa nnɛterɛ nwo nningyein ɔ. Kyɛbɔ nnɛterɛ ne te biala ne, ɔkora fa nwo nikyee maa ɔyɛ yé. Sɔ ala yeɛ Yehowa koso ɔboka yɛ maa yenya subaen pa ɔ, na ɛhe amaa yɛnye agye.”—Yes. 64:8.

19 Afutue bɔ ɔwɔ adesua he anu ne, sɛ yɛfa yemu yɛyɛ adwuma po a, ɔyɛ sɛɛ alaa a adwenedwene kɔhora kɔhyɛ yɛ so, anaa y’abanu kɔhora kobu. Nakoso Yehowa kɔboka yɛ maa y’adwenedwene so kɔte. Yeti sɛ yɛne ye afia te kama na yɛyere yɛnwo yɛyɛ bɔ okuro a, yedé yedi kyɛ yɛnye kɔhora kɔgye.
<<<P>>>
20 Ebiaa bɔne bie bɔ yɛyɛle ne, yenwo dwirɛ té ha yɛ, yɛfa ɔhaw bie nu, anaa ɔyɛ se ma yɛ kyɛ yɛkɔyɛ bɔ ɔte pá ɔ. Sɛ ye sɔ po a, ɛmɔma yɛyere yɛnwo kyɛ yɛngɔma sɔ nningyein nemɔ ngobu y’abanu. Yɛkɔhora koso yɛkɔsrɛ Yehowa kyɛ ɔboka yɛ na y’abanu ammu. (Edw. 143:10) Ɔmva yenwo ne bɔ yɛfa nu biala ne, sɛ yɛkɔ ewiase foforɛ nanu a, yɛse kyɛ nikyefee biala nne berɛ bɔ ɔkɔmaa y’abanu kobu ɔ. Sɔ mmerɛ ne de, daa biala bɔ yekodwɔso ne, yekonwu ye kyɛ yele anigye bɔ yɛfa yɛsõ Yehowa ɔ.
