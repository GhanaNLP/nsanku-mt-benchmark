Sɛ ɛne ebie sua nikyee a, nwa yeɛ ɛmaa sona ne adwene kɔ ye so ɔ? (Nea ngyekyɛmuɛ 7-8)
<<<P>>>
yɛkã nahorɛ. Nakoso, odikyɛ yɛhyɛ ye nzorɛ kyɛ, nna nahorɛ bɔ yɛkɔhã ne ngome yeɛ ole edwirɛ ne, na mmom bɔ ɔmaa yɛkã nahorɛ ne koso, yenwo hia. Ofikyɛ sɛ Yehowa kole yɛ kɔto nu a, ogyi ɛhene aso. Wɔde ma yɛnea edwirɛ bie bɔ osili mmerɛ bɔ ne Yesu yɛ ye asɛnga adwuma ne. Mmerɛ bie ne ɔwɔ Galilea po ne noa, na ne okyirekyire mmeni lɔen bie. Ɛberɛ ala benu biemɔ bɔ bele sunzummɔne fifini ase wɔ ye nyunu, na bɛteateane nu kyɛ: “Wɔ ole Nyameɛ Awa ne.” (Kenga Marko 3:11, 12.) Yede, nzu yeɛ ɔmaa sunzummɔne nemɔ hane sɔ nahorɛ dwirɛ ne fale Yesu nwo ɔ? Ebiaa ne b’adwene yeɛ ole kyɛ ɔkɔmaa mmenia bɔ bɛwɔ berɛ ne kole bɛ koli, na sɛ siɛ ye bɛkã edwirɛ bie bɔ ɔtte nahorɛ po a, b’adwene ngɔhɔso. Sɛ ɔba ye sɔ a, ne bɛnate ɛhene aso bɛkɔhwe bɛ bekofi Yehowa nwo. Bɔ sunzummɔne nemɔ hafale Yesu nwo ne, ne ɔte nahorɛ, nakoso ne bettira adwene pá. Yesu de, ne ɔse adwene bɔ otira bɛ ɔ, yeti wanzosɔ bɛso kyɛ beli yenwo adanzeɛ.

8 Nzu yeɛ yesua yefi sɔ dwirɛ nanu ɔ? Yeɛ ole kyɛ, nna nahorɛ bɔ yɛkɔhã ne ngome yeɛ yenwo hia Yehowa ɔ, na mmom adwene bɔ ɔta ye si bɔ yɛfa yɛyɛ sɔ ne koso boka ye. Ɛhe maa yenwu ye kyɛ yenwo hia paa kyɛ yɛkɔmaa mmenia konwu nahorɛ bɔ ɔfa Yehowa nwo ne. Nakoso, odikyɛ yɛfa yenwo animnyam mukoraati yɛma Yehowa, na ɔnzɛkyɛ yedé yɛma yɛnwo.—Mat. 5:16; fatoto Asomafoɛ 14:12-15 nanwo.
<<<P>>>
9 Ɛmɔma yesa yɛnea nikyee foforɛ bie bɔ yɛyɛ a, okyire kyɛ yedé animnyam yɛma yɛnwo ɔ. Yɛfa ye kyɛ asafo nu panyi bie ahã edwirɛ bie ahyire yɛ, nakoso ɔnzɛkyɛ yɛbɔ so. Sɛ yɛ koso yɛkekã yekyire mmeni foforɛ, na siɛ ye bɛbanwu ye kyɛ sãa bɔ ne yɛkã ne te nahorɛ a, ebiaa ɔkɔmaa bekobu yɛ paa, na bahoahoa yɛ po. Ofikyɛ bekonwu ye kyɛ yɛse edwirɛ pee bɔ aliemaamɔ pee nala nze ɔ. Nakoso sɛ yɛyɛ sɔ a, Yehowa nye ngɔgye yɛnwo. (Any. 11:13) Nzuati ɔ?
