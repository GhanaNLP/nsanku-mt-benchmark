NZU YEE NE ƆMAA PƆƆLO WORA BƆ Ɔ?
<<<P>>>
4 Nningyein biemɔ bɔ ɔyɛle kora yee ɔbaayɛ Kristoniɛ ɔ. Ɔyɛle nningyein pee bɔ ne ɔttemaye ɔ. Ebie yee ole kyɛ mmerɛ bɔ ne beku Setefano ne, ne ɔwɔ berɛ bie, na ne ɔpene so po. Sɔ mmerɛ ne, ne bɛfrɛ ye Sɔɔlo. (Aso. 7:58; 8:1) Nna ɛhene ngome, na mmom Kristofoɛ nemɔ koso, ɔtanene bɛnye paa.—Aso. 8:3; 26:9-11.
<<<P>>>
5 Pɔɔlo ɔɔyɛle Kristoniɛ afoɛ pee si po ne, sɛ ne ɔkae kyɛbɔ olili Kristofoɛ nemɔ nye ne a, ne ɔyɛ ye yea paa. Bɔ ɔmaa yekã sɔ yee ole kyɛ, wɔ afoɛ 55 Y.B. (Y’aberɛ so) ne, ɔhworɔle krataa ɔɔmane Korintofoɛ nemɔ, na ɔhane ye wɔ nu kyɛ: “Memvata kyɛ bɛfrɛ me somafoɛ, ɔnate kyɛ ndanene Nyamɛɛ asɔre ne nye.” (1 Kor. 15:9) Afoɛ nnu si ne, ɔhworɔle krataa ɔɔmane Efesofoɛ nemɔ, na ɛhene koso ɔhane ye wɔ nu kyɛ, ‘ye yee ɔte kãa paa wɔ anwoteefoɛ ne amukoraa nu ɔ.’ (Efe. 3:8) Mmerɛ bɔ ne ɔtworɔ krataa kɔma Timoteo ne koso, ɔhane kyɛ ‘daa ne, ne ɔkeka musue dwirɛ, ɔtanene Kristofoɛ nemɔ nye, na ne ɔte patapaa.’ (1 Tim. 1:13) Wɔde nea kyɛ Pɔɔlo aasra asafo bie na wanwu bɛbɔ ɔtanene bɛnye ne bie, anaa b’abusuafoɛ wɔ berɛ ɔ. Nea kyɛbɔ ne ɔkɔyɛ basaa ɔ.
<<<P>>>
6 Ne ole ɔhaw bie bɔ ne ɔteetee ye paa ɔ. Pɔɔlo hane kyɛ sɔ haw ne, ne wayɛ kyɛ “bowue bie bɔ owura ye nwonaen nu ɔ.” (2 Kor. 12:7, NWT) Nikyee deinn bɔ ne ɔha ye ne de wanga. Nakoso yɛnea kyɛbɔ osi hane ye dwirɛ ne a, ebiaa anwonyerɛ anaa nikyee foforɛ bie yee ne ɔha ye ɔ.*
<<<P>>>
7 Ne ɔyɛ se ma Pɔɔlo kyɛ ɔkɔyɛ bɔ ɔte pá ɔ. (Kenga Romanfoɛ 7:18, 19.) Ɔwɔ nu kyɛ ne ɔpena kyɛ ɔyɛ bɔ ɔte pá, nakoso ɔnate bɔne bɔ ɔwɔ yenwo nati, ne ɔyɛ se ma ye. Ye bɔbɔ ole tole nu kyɛ odwu mmerɛ bie a bɔ onguro kyɛ ɔkɔyɛ ne, ɛhene ala yee ne ɔyɛ ɔ. Ehe mukoraati si ne, ɔyerele yenwo kyɛ ɔkɔyɛ bɔ ɔte pá ɔ. (1 Kor. 9:27) Sɛ ne Pɔɔlo nwu ye kyɛ subaen mmɔne bie bɔ ɔwɔ yenwo ne, ɔwɔ nu alaa a ne wasa waye ye ti a, wɔde nea kyɛbɔ ne y’abanu kosi kobu ɔ.
<<<P>>>
SƐ NE PƆƆLO WORA BƆ A, NZU YEE NE ƆYƐ Ɔ?
<<<P>>>
8 Ne Pɔɔlo sua tworɔnzɛm ne, na ɛhene bokale ye maa onwuni bɔ ɔyɛ a ɔkɔhora koli subaen bɔ ne ɔpena kyɛ ɔyakyi naso ɔ. Ɛhene ati, wɔ ye ngrataa ahoroɛ bɔ ɔhworɔ ɔɔmane Kristofoɛ nanu ne, ɔmaa benwuni kyɛ Nyameɛ sunzum ne kɔhora kɔboka bɛ maa bekoli subaen bie bɔ bɛpena kyɛ bɛyakyi naso, kyɛbɔ waboka ye ne. (Rom. 8:13; Gal. 5:16, 17) Ɔnate ɛhe ati nningyein biemɔ bɔ sɛ Kristoniɛ yɛ a ɔngɔma Yehowa nye ngɔgye yenwo ne, ɔmaa benwuni ye pefee. (Gal. 5:19-21, 26)
<<<P>>>
* Sɛ yɛnea edwirɛ biemɔ bɔ Pɔɔlo hworɔle ye wɔ ye ngrataa nanu ne a, ɔmaa yenwu ye kyɛ ebiaa yenye yee ne ɔha ye ɔ. Sɛ sɔ yee ɔte a, yede sɛ ne ɔtworɔ ngrataa kɔma aliemaamɔ, anaa otu atee kɔyɛ asɛnga adwuma ne a, ne ɔkɔyɛ se kɔma ye. (Gal. 4:15; 6:11) Ɔkɔhora koso kɔwa kyɛ, edwirɛ biemɔ bɔ bɛbɔ ne bɛpena kyɛ bɛfa ngondombo ngyerekyerɛ bɛba asafo nanu ne kekã fale yenwo ne, yee ne yenwo dwirɛ ha ye ɔ. (2 Kor. 10:10; 11:5, 13) Ɔmva yenwo ne kyɛbɔ ne edwirɛ ne te biala ne, ɔkɔyɛ kyɛ ne ɔmaa Pɔɔlo wora bɔ paa.
