dwudwolɛ yɛ fɛ tekyɛ ngyee. Nakoso nná bɔ ne okyire yeɛ ole kyɛ, yɛkɔfa nahorɛ ne yɛkɔfea. Na mmom né ɔpena kyɛ yenwu ye kyɛ sɛ yɛkã nahorɛ nanwo dwirɛ a, odikyɛ yɛmaa yɛnoa dwudwolɛ “yɛ fɛ,” kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a ɔkɔhã sona ne ahone ɔ. (Yob 12:11) Nakoso odwu mmerɛ bie a, ɛyɛ a ne ɔlla ase kyɛ yɛkɔyɛ sɔ. Nzuati ɔ? Sɛ yɛnea aleɛ sɔa a, ebie wɔ berɛ a ne yekuro ye paa yeti ɔhale yɛ ngome a, ahaa ebiala koso kuro sɔ aleɛ ne. Sɔ ala yeɛ sɛ yannea yé a yekonya adwene kyɛ kyɛbɔ yedwudwo ne, ebiala nye kɔgye yenwo ɔ. Nakoso nna sɔ koraa yeɛ ɔte ɔ. Sɛ ɛkɔ maen biemɔ so a, ebie kyire ye adwene wɔ edwirɛ bie nwo a, ɔkora kã ye dwirɛ pein-pein ɔmva yenwo kyɛ ebiaa ɔne panyi po yeɛ bedwudwo ne. Nakoso sɛ ɛkɔ maen foforɛ so a, sɛ ebie yɛ sɔ a, bebu sɔ sona ne kyɛ ommu nikye, anaa ɔte mbaninzɛm. Pɔɔlo hane kyɛ odikyɛ yenwu ‘mmuayɛɛ pá bɔ yɛkɔfa yɛkɔma ebiala ɔ.’ Bɔ ɛhe kyire yeɛ ole kyɛ, sɛ yɛkã edwirɛ yekyire ebie a, odikyɛ yɛkã ye wɔ atee bie aso bɔ ɔkɔmaa sona ne nye kɔgye yenwo ɔ. Na mmom ɔnzɛkyɛ yɛkã kyɛ nekaa bɔ yefi nati yekodwudwo kyɛbɔ yekuro ɔ.
