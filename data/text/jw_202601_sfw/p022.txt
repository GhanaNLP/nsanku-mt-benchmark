Yehowa ngɔhã kyɛ yɛyɛ nikyeebie bɔ ɔbo y’anwoserɛ so lle. Ɛhene ati, ɔkɔboka wɔ maa ɛkɔfa ye mmraa kɔbɔ wɔ bra
(Nea ngyekyɛmuɛ 6)
<<<P>>>
yɛ ne, sɛ eyere wɔnwo a ɛkɔhora kɔfa kɔbɔ wɔ bra. (Kenga Deuteronomi 30:11-14.) Bɔ ɔkɔboka wɔ paa yeɛ ole kyɛ ɛkɔdwenedwene nningyein pee bɔ Yehowa ayɛ ama wɔ nanwo. Ebie yeɛ ole Ɔwa ne bɔ ɔmaa oowu mane wɔ ne. Sɛ eyɛ sɔ a, ɔkɔmaa ɔdɔ bɔ ele ye ma Yehowa ne ɔkɔyɛ piri, na ekonwu ye kyɛ sãa ‘ye ahyedeɛ ne, ɛso lilɛ nyɛ se.’—1 Yoh. 5:3.*

7 Ebiaa w’awofoɛ te Yehowa Adanzefoɛ, yeti bɛne wɔ yeɛ esua nikyee ɔ. Sɛ sɔ a, yede nwu ye kyɛ b’adwene wɔ wɔ so paa, na wɔnwo hia bɛ. Yehowa kuro paa kyɛ ɔkɔfa w’adamvoa, nakoso sana ɛmaa onwu ye kyɛ ɛpena kyɛ ɛyɛ ye damvo. Ɛhene ati yeɛ Bible ne nwa: “Ɛmɔhwe bepingye Nyameɛ, na ye koso ɔkɔhwe kopingye ɛmɔ” ne. (Yak. 4:8; 1 Abe. 28:9) Kae kyɛ nna kyɛ w’awofoɛ te Yehowa Adanzefoɛ nati yeɛ wamaa Yehowa pena kyɛ opingye wɔ ɔ, na mmom onwu mmɔden bɔ ɛbɔ kyɛ ɛkɔfa ye adamvoa ne, na ɛhene ati yeɛ ɔpena kyɛ opingye wɔ ne. Yesu wue ne yeɛ wamaa ɛne Yehowa afora afa sɔ adamvoa ne ɔ. Yeti bɔ Yesu ayɛ ama wɔ ne, nnɛmaa wɔ wora fi ye lle. (Rom. 5:1, 2) Ɛhene ati kora bɔ yeɛ ɛkɔhɔ Yesu wue ngaeɛ nabo ne, bisa wɔnwo kyɛ, ‘Mvasoɛ beni yeɛ ye wue ne amaa manya ye ɔ?’ Afei mmerɛ bɔ anwu ye sɔ ne, fa nikyeebie sie wɔnye so wɔ Yehowa som nu, na yere wɔnwo kyɛ ɛkodwu yenwo.
