SUESUA PƆƆLO NA NYA SUBAEN BƆ ƆKƆMAA NYAMEƐ NYE KƆGYE WƆNWO Ɔ
<<<P>>>
10 Sɛ yɛpena kyɛ Yehowa kyire yɛ atee, na wamaa yɛnye agye a, odikyɛ yenya subaen biemɔ bɔ yenye gye yenwo ɔ. Somafoɛ Pɔɔlo hworɔle kyɛ: ‘Ɛmɔgyi ɛmɔ subaen daa ne begua, na ɛmɔnya subaen foforɛ.’ (Kenga Kolosefoɛ 3:8-10.) Sɛ yele subaen dáa bie na yɛpena kyɛ yɛsesã yefi yenwo a, ɔtte ɛlɛhomapɛ dwirɛ na ɔlla ase koso. Somafoɛ Pɔɔlo sɔa, sɔ bie yee ɔtole ye ɔ. Ofi ye ngwalaa nu yeɛ ne ɔpena kyɛ ɔyɛ bɔ Nyameɛ kuro ɔ. (Gal. 1:14; Flp. 3:4, 5) Nakoso, atee pá bɔ Nyameɛ pena kyɛ yɛfa yɛsõ ne de, ne ɔnze yenwo bali. Afei koso ne onné Kristo ngyerɛkyerɛ ndonu, na ne osa te anwomasoɛ. Sɔ subaen ne, ne Yehowa nye ngye koraa. Pɔɔlo bɔbɔ hane kyɛ ne ɔte “patapaa.”—1 Tim. 1:13.
<<<P>>>
11 Koraka Pɔɔlo baayɛ Kristoniɛ ne, ne ole ahone paa. Asomafoɛ nwomaa ne maa yenwu kyɛ, ne ‘onwunanwuna’ Yesu asuafoɛ nemɔ, na né ‘ɔpena kyɛ okũ bɛ.’ (Aso. 9:1) Yeti siɛ ye bɔ ɔɔyɛle Kristoniɛ ne, ne odikyɛ ɔyere yenwo gyi sɔ subaen dáa ne mukoraati gua, na wamaa waahuro mmenia dwirɛ. (Ɛfe. 4:22, 31) Ɛhe mukoraati si ne odwu mmerɛ bie a, ne Pɔɔlo di mvomsoɛ. Mmerɛ bie sɔa, ɔne Banaba suli aporowaa serɛserɛ, na ɛhe maa ɔfɛle yaa paa. (Aso. 15:37-39) Nakoso, Pɔɔlo amma bɔ osili ne ammu ye abanu, na mmom ɔhɔleso hyɛle ye “nipadua ne anyeborolɛ” maa ɔyɛle bɔ Yehowa kuro ɔ.—1 Kor. 9:27.
<<<P>>>
12 Tekyɛ Petro ne, Pɔɔlo fale yenwo tole ‘anwoserɛ bɔ Nyameɛ fa mane ye’ naso. (1 Pet. 4:11) Na ɛhene yeɛ ɔbokale ye maa ɔhora gyili ye subaen dáa nemɔ guale ɔ. Sɔ bɔ ɔyɛle ne kyire kyɛ ne ɔbrɛ yenwo ase. (Flp. 4:13) Yeti ɔwɔ nu kyɛ ne odi mvomsoɛ de, nakoso wamma ɛhene ammu ye aba nu. Na mmom ɔfale ye adwene siele nningyein pá bɔ Yehowa ayɛ ama ye naso. Ɛhene bokale ye maa ɔyerele yenwo yɛle bɔ Nyameɛ kuro ɔ.—Rom. 7:21-25.
<<<P>>>
13 Sɛ yasɔ̃ Yehowa afoɛ pee po a, odikyɛ yɛyere yenwo yegyi subaen dáa biemɔ bɔ ɔwɔ yɛnwo ne yegua. Yɛyɛ sɔ a, ne yesuesua somafoɛ Pɔɔlo, na ɛhene kɔmaa Nyameɛ nye kɔgye yɛnwo. Odikyɛ yenwu ye kyɛ yaayɛ sona ye de, yɛkɔhora yekoli mvomsoɛ. Tekyɛ ebiaa yɛkɔfɛ ebie so yaa, anaa yɛkɔkekã edwirɛ yeayea yekohyire ye. Sɛ ɔba sɔ a, ɔnzɛkyɛ yenya adwene kyɛ yɛ de Yehowa fa yɛ yɛ hwee a, ɔnyɛ yé. Na mmom odikyɛ yɛyere yɛnwo kyɛ yɛkɔsesã. (Rom. 12:1, 2; Ɛfe. 4:24) Mmerɛ bɔ yɛyɛ sɔ ne, odikyɛ yɛkae sɔ dwirɛ bɔ yenwo hia he: Sɛ yɛkɔhora yɛkɔsesã yɛ subaen a, sana yedimoa yɛte kyɛbɔ Yehowa pena kyɛ yɛbɔ yɛbra nabo yé. Bɔ ɔte yee ole kyɛ, Nyameɛ ngɔsesã ye mmraa ne, yɛ mmom yeɛ odikyɛ yɛsesã yɛ subaen maa ɔne bɔ okuro ne yia ɔ.
