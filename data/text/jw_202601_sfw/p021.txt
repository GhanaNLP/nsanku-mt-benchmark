4 Sɛ ɛne Yehowa Adanzefoɛ sua nikyee a, nwu ye kyɛ bɔ ɛyɛ ne kyire kyɛ ɛpena kyɛ ɛbayɛ Yehowa damvo, yeti ye koso ɔkɔboka wɔ. (Yoh. 6:44) Nakoso sɛ nna Yesu wue ne ahɔ a, ahaa ɛne Yehowa ngɔhora ngɔfa adamvoa lle. (Rom. 5:10, 11) Yeti nnɛmaa wɔ wora fi bɔ wayɛ wama wɔ ne. Ɔnate kyɛ Yehowa ‘nea ahone nu’ nati, mmɔden bɔ ɛbɔ kyɛ ekosua yenwo nikyee ne, onwu ye mukoraati. Kyɛbɔ ɛyere wɔnwo kyɛ ɛkɔfa bɔ esua ne kɔbɔ wɔ bra ne koso onwu ye, na ɛhene maa yenye gye.—Any. 17:3; 27:11.
<<<P>>>
5 Sɛ ɛne Yehowa Adanzefoɛ sua nikyee a, nzu yee ɛyɛ a okyire kyɛ wɔnye sɔ Yesu wue ne ɔ? Nikyee kɔ̃ bɔ ɛkɔhora kɔyɛ yeɛ ole kyɛ ɛkɔfa afutue bɔ somafoɛ Pɔɔlo famane Filipifoɛ nemɔ ne kɔyɛ adwuma. Ɔhane kyɛ: “Bɔ yanya ye dada ne, émɔma yehɔ so yɛsɔ nu bum.” (Flp. 3:16) Bible bie koso kã ye kyɛ: “Nekaa bɔ yafa yɛnye yahyire ne, ɛmɔma yehɔ yɛ nyunu sɔ ala.” Sɔ dwirɛ ne kyire kyɛ ngoa atee bɔ ɛnate so ne, nnɛmaa nikyefee si w’atee maa ɛyakyi so falɛ.—Mat. 7:14; Luka 9:62.
<<<P>>>
6 Ebiaa bɛne wɔ asua Bible nu dwirɛ bie, nakoso ɛnhyɛ da nde yebo, yeti ɔyɛ se ma wɔ kyɛ ekole kɔto nu ɔ. Sɛ sɔ a yede ɛkɔyɛ ye sɛ? Yɛ sɔ dwirɛ nanwo nhwehwɛmuɛ, na srɛ Yehowa kyɛ ɔboka wɔ ma ɛte yebo yé. (Edw. 86:11) Sɛ ɛhe mukoraati si ne, oté yɛ se ma wɔ kyɛ ɛkɔte yebo a, nzu yee ɛkɔyɛ ɔ? Nnɛmaa ɔha wɔ, na mmom wɔde kɔso ma bɛne wɔ yɛ adesua ne, na ebiaa siɛ ye ɛkɔte yebo. Na sɛ ɛbɔ bra bie bɔ enwu ye kyɛ Yehowa nguro, nakoso ɔyɛ se ma wɔ kyɛ ɛkɔyakyi koso ɛ? Sɛ sɔ a nnɛmaa w’abanu bu. Kae kyɛ Yehowa de ɔse kyɛ yɛte sona, yeti ɔngɔhã kyɛ yɛyɛ nikyeebie bɔ ɔbo y’anwoserɛ so ɔ. Ye bɔbɔ deinn po wahã kyɛ ɔkɔboka wɔ. (Yes. 41:10, 13; 1 Kor. 10:13) Ɛhene ati ye mmraa bɔ wafa wama
