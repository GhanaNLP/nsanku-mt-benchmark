BƆ ADESŨA NE KƆHÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Nahorɛ bɔ ɔwɔ Bible nanu ne, sɛ yɛkã yenwo dwirɛ yekyire mmenia a, yekonwu kyɛbɔ yɛkɔhã ye maa bɛnye kɔgye yenwo ɔ. Yekosa yekonwu bɔ yeti yɛɛ odikyɛ yɛkã nahorɛ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Yɛkɔyɛ Sɛɛ Ne Yahã Nahorɛ Nanwo Dwirɛ Maa Wahã Mmenia Ahone?
<<<P>>>
‘AWURADE te nahorɛ Nyameɛ.’—EDW. 31:5.
<<<P>>>
SƐ YEYIA aliemaa bie bɔ yɛnze ye a, edwirɛ bɔ yɛtaa yebisa ye yeɛ ole kyɛ, “Ɔyɛle sɛɛ yeɛ enwuni nahorɛ ne ɔ?” Ebie wɔ berɛ a ɔkã kyɛ yede ye awofoɛ yeɛ bɛfale nahorɛ ne bɛtetele ye ɔ. Ebie koso kã kyɛ ebie yeɛ ɔne ye suane nikyee maa oonwuni nahorɛ ne ɔ. Nzu yeɛ ɔmaa bɛkã sɔ ɔ? Bɔ ɔmaa bɛkã sɔ yeɛ ole kyɛ, yɛ Yehowa Adanzefoɛ de, nahorɛ bɔ ɔwɔ Bible nanu bɔ yasua ne, yɛnye gye yenwo, yeti yɛfa yɛbɔ yɛ bra. Ofikyɛ yɛse kyɛ sɛ yɛkɔhora yɛkɔboka Yehowa abusua naso a, sana yɛyɛ sɔ. Ɛhene ati yɛkã nahorɛ wɔ nikyeebiala nu.—Edw. 15:1-3.
<<<P>>>
2 Ne Yesu kã nahorɛ mmerɛ biala. Ɛhene ati, bɛbɔ benguro ye dwirɛ ne po, ne bɛse ye kyɛ nahorɛ nanwo dwirɛ bɔ ɔkã ne, sɛ mmenia nye ngɔgye yenwo po a, ɔngɔkata-kata so. (Mat. 22:16) Yesu bɔbɔ deinn hane kyɛ nahorɛ ne bɔ ɔfa kyirekyire mmenia ne, nna ebiala yeɛ yenye kɔgye yenwo ɔ. Yeti ɔhane kyɛ: “Mmale kyɛ mebaatete nu ma baa bienzua dwɔso tia ɔse; ná baa brasua adwɔso atia oni, ná sia brasua adwɔso atia ohyireni,” anaa ye sia brasua. (Mat. 10:35) Nna bɔ ne Yesu ne ye asuafoɛ nemɔ kuro yeɛ ole kyɛ nahorɛ bɔ bɛkã yenwo dwirɛ ne kɔmaa abusua nu kɔtete ɔ. Nakoso ne ɔse kyɛ ɔyɛ sɛɛ alaa a ɔkɔwa ye sɔ. (Mat. 23:37) Ne ɔse kyɛ ye dwirɛ ne kɔmaa mmenia nu kɔhyɛ nyɔ; bɛbɔ bɛnye gye nahorɛ bɔ ɔwɔ Bible
