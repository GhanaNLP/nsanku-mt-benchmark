Sɛ aliemaa bie ayɛ nikyee maa wayɛ wɔ yea koso a, ɔkɔboka wɔ maa ɛkɔye sona nanwo dwirɛ kofi wɔ nu. Yɛɛ sɛ bɔne bie bɔ ayɛ ne yee wamaa wɔ kunu tete wɔ a, ɔkɔfa kɔhyɛ wɔ, na wamaa anya asomdwee. Ɛhene kɔmaa ɛne aliemaamɔ mukoraati kosa kɔfa anigye kɔsõ ye bieku.” Kae kyɛ mbanyi nemɔ koso, bɛte kyɛ “afealeɛ bɔ mvrama piri tu a sona fea nu ɔ, nekaa bɔ mvrama piri tu a sona bɔ ye nwo waen ɔ.” Yeti bɛkɔboka wɔ. (Yes. 32:2) Yɛse ye paa kyɛ wɔnye sɔ Yesu wue ne. Yeti ɛkɔhora kobisa wɔnwo kyɛ, ‘Nzu yee meyɛ a ɔkɔmaa mene Yehowa ‘kosiesie yɛ afia’ ɔ?’ (Yes. 1:18; 1 Pet. 2:25) Bɔ ɛkɔhora kɔyɛ ne bie yeɛ ole kyɛ ɛkɔhɔ adesua. Sɛ ɛkɔ koso a ɛkɔhora ne mbanyi nanu kɔ kɔbɔ ngɔmmɔ, na ma onwu ye kyɛ ɛpena kyɛ esa ba asafo nanu yeti bɛboka wɔ. Bɔ mbanyi nemɔ kɔyɛ ne bie yeɛ ole kyɛ, ebiaa bɛkɔmaa ebie ne wɔ kosua nikye. Sɛ ɛyɛ sɔ nningyein hemɔ a, okyire kyɛ wɔnye sɔ Yesu wue ne paa, na Yehowa koso kɔboka wɔ.

NZU YEE ƐKƆYƐ KƆFA KOHYIRE KYƐ WƆNYE SƆ YESU WUE NE Ɔ?
<<<P>>>
17 Yehowa nate Yesu wue naso ale yɛ afi bɔne ne ewue sanu. Ɛhene ati yeɛ Yesu hane kyɛ ‘ebiala bɔ okole ye koli ne, ongomini, na mmom okonya daa ngoa’ ne. (Yoh. 3:16) Bɔ Yesu ayɛ ama yɛ ne, nnɛmɔmaa yɛ wora fi ye lle. (Rom. 3:23, 24; 2 Kor. 6:1) Yesɔ nati, ɔdɔ bɔ Yehowa ne Ɔwa ne aye ye ali ahyire yɛ ne, sɛ ɔka kãa maa yɛkae Yesu wue ne a, odikyɛ yɛdwenedwene yenwo. Sɛ yɛyɛ sɔ a, ɔkɔboka yɛ maa yɛkɔyɛ nikyeebie yekohyire kyɛ yɛnye asɔ bɔ bayɛ bama yɛ ne.
<<<P>>>
18 Yede nzu paa yeɛ ɛkɔyɛ kɔfa kohyire kyɛ wɔnye sɔ Yesu wue ne ɔ? Ebiaa bɔ ɛkɔyɛ ne, nna ɛhene ala yeɛ sona foforɛ kɔyɛ ɔ. Nakoso nikyeebiala bɔ ɛkɔyɛ kɔfa kohyire kyɛ wɔnye sɔ Yesu wue ne, Yehowa konwu ye na okoyira wɔ paa. Kae kyɛ aliemaamɔ bɔ bɛwɔ ewiase mukoraati ne koso bɛyɛ sɔ ala. Ofikyɛ ɛne bɛ mukoraati ɛmɔ mbena kyɛ ‘ɛmɔtena ase bɛmma bɛ nwo ko, na mmom ɛmɔpena kyɛ ɛmɔtena ase bɛma Yesu bɔ ɛmɔ ti owuli ne.’—2 Kor. 5:15.
