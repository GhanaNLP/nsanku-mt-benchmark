Yesu wue ne ngome yeɛ ne Yehowa kɔhora kogyina so kɔmaa ɔne sona afia kosa kɔyɛ kama ɔ
(Nea ngyekyɛmuɛ 16)
<<<P>>>
ne po kã kyɛ mmenia mukoraati aayɛ Nyameɛ pɔfoɛ. (Rom. 8:7, 8; Kol. 1:21) Nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ bɔne biala nne Yehowa nwo. Yeti sɛ ebie yɛ bɔne a, onguro kyɛ okopingye ye koraa. Bible ne kã Yehowa nwo dwirɛ kyɛ: “Wɔ nye te krongron paa bɔ yiti ɛngora nnea bɔne, ná ɛngora nnea amumuyɛɛ ɔ.” (Hab. 1:13) Atee bie aso ne, bɔne aayɛ tekyɛ kumaa pirikua bie bɔ waala sona ne Nyameɛ afia ɔ. Yeti, sɛ yɛkɔhora yekopingye Nyameɛ a, sana yenya nikyeebie tekyɛ breegye bie bɔ ɔkɔɔla sɔ kumaa naso, bɔ yɛkɔfa so yekopingye ye ɔ. Yesu ngoa bɔ ɔfa bɔle afɔleɛ ne, ɔtekyɛ sɔ breegye bɔ yɛkã yenwo dwirɛ ne.
<<<P>>>
15 Bible ne kã kyɛ Yesu wue ne, “ɔte yɛ bɔne nwo mbata.” (1 Yoh. 2:2) Yede nzuati yeɛ yɛkɔhora yɛkɔhã kyɛ Yesu patale yɛne Yehowa afia ɔ? Bɔ ɔte yeɛ ole kyɛ, ɔnzɛkyɛ yenya adwene kyɛ ewue bɔ Yesu oowuli ne, gyamia ɔmaa Yehowa nwo tɔle ye wɔ atee bie aso. Na mmom ɔnate kyɛ ɔte tenenee Nyameɛ nati, ɛhene ngome yeɛ ne ɔkɔhora kogyina so kole kɔtonu kyɛ ɔne sona afia kosa kɔyɛ kama ɔ. (Rom. 3:23-26) Bɛbɔ bewuli kora yeɛ Yesu baafa ye ngoa bɔ afɔleɛ bɔ belili nahorɛ bɛmane Yehowa ne po, ɔhora hane kyɛ bɛte teneneefoɛ. (Gye. 15:1, 6.) Nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ ne odé di paa kyɛ atee biala so, Ɔwa Yesu kɔfa ye ngoa kɔbɔ afɔleɛ. (Yes. 46:10) Amba, Yesu wue ne abukye yenwo atee amaa yɛne Nyameɛ afia asa ayɛ kama.
<<<P>>>
16 Yɛne Yehowa afia bɔ wasa wayɛ kama ne, aso anya so mvasoɛ anaa? Wɔde susu yenwo nea o, ɔnate yenwo atee bɔ ɔte sɔ nati, ɛkora frɛ Yehowa kyɛ wɔ “Baba” kyɛbɔ Yesu hyirele yɛ kyɛ yɛyɛ ne. (Mat. 6:9) Odwu mmerɛ bie po a, yɛkora yɛkã kyɛ Yehowa te yɛ “damvo.” Nakoso, mmerɛ biala bɔ yɛkɔyɛ sɔ ne, odikyɛ yɛda ye ali kyɛ yɛ bu ye, na yɛbrɛ yenwo ase. Nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ bɔne bɔ ɔwɔ yɛnwo nati, ɔte yenwo atee bie mbaen bɔ yanya ye kyɛ yɛkora yepingye Yehowa ɔ, na ɔnte kyɛ yɛ tipɛen yeɛ ole ye. Yesu wue ne, yeɛ wamaa ɛhe ahora ayɛ yé ɔ. Bible ne kã kyɛ, Yehowa nate Yesu so ‘wahwe nningyein mukoraa wabata ye bɔbɔ ye nwo; na ɔnate ye asɛnnua nu mogya ne aso yeɛ wamaa sɔ asomdwee he awa ɔ.’ (Kol. 1:19, 20) Ɛhene ati yeɛ kesaalae bɔ bɔne té wɔ yɛnwo ne po ne, yɛne Yehowa afia ahora ayɛ kama ne.

15. Yesu wue ne, sɛɛ yeɛ wabukye yenwo atee amaa yɛne Nyameɛ afia asa ayɛ kama ɔ?
<<<P>>>
16. Nningyein benimɔ koso yeɛ ɛkɔhora kɔdwenedwene yenwo, mmerɛ bɔ ɔka ngyẽa kãa maa yɛkae Yesu wue ne? (Nea foto ne koso.)
