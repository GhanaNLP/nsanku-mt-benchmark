BƆ ADESŨA NE KƆHÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Yekonwu kyɛ Yesu wue nati, Yehowa kɔhora kɔfa yɛ bɔne kɔhyɛ yɛ, yɛne ye afia kɔhora kɔyɛ kama, na yasa yanya anyelasoɛ kyɛ yɛkɔtena ase daa.
<<<P>>>
Nzuati Yeɛ Ne Odikyɛ Yesu Bawu Ma Yɛ Ɔ?
<<<P>>>
“Nwa yeɛ ɔkɔpɛpɛ kole me kofi sɔ nipadua bɔ ɔkɔ ewue nu he anu ɔ?”—ROM. 7:24.
<<<P>>>
WƆDE dwene sɔ dwirɛ he anwo nea. Ne abrandeɛ bie wura sua bie nu yeɛ obu guale ye so ɔ. Ɔwɔ nu wanwu, nakoso ne ɔngora fite. Yeti bɔ ne ɔkɔyɛ alaa yeɛ ole kyɛ ɔkɔteatea nu kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a, sɛ ebie pɛ nu a, ɔkɔte ye nga na waaye ye ɔ.
<<<P>>>
2 Mmerɛ bɔ Adam yɛle Yehowa so asoserɛ ne, ɔɔyɛle bɔnefoɛ. Siɛ ye bɔ ɔwole mmaa ne, ɔfale sɔ bɔne ne wole bɛ, yeti yɛ koso yɛ boka so. Ɔnate ɛhene ati mmenia amukoraa ayɛ bɔne, na wamaa yɛ dwirɛ aayɛ tekyɛ abrandeɛ bɔ ne sua abu agua ye so ne. Yeti odikyɛ ebie boka yɛ kora ne yahora yale yɛnwo. Wɔ krataa bɔ Pɔɔlo hworɔ ɔɔmane Romanfoɛ nanu ne, ɔhane kyɛbɔ bɔne afa yɛ ayɛ ngoaa nanwo dwirɛ. (Kenga Romanfoɛ 7:22-24.) Ne Pɔɔlo se kyɛ bawo ye bato bɔne nu, yeti ɔyɛ sɛɛ alaa okowu. (Rom. 6:23) Ɛhene ati ɔsrɛle kyɛ, ‘bɛpɛpɛ bɛle ye befi sɔ nipadua bɔ ɔkɔ ewue nu nanu.’ Sɔ tebea nanu ala yeɛ yɛ koso yɛwɔ. Yeti yehia ebie bɔ okole yɛ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
3 Pɔɔlo hane kyɛbɔ ne ye dwirɛ yɛ anwumvo yiele ne, osa hane edwirɛ bie bɔ ɔmaa yenya anyelasoɛ ɔ. Ɔhane kyɛ: “Mefa y’Awurade Yesu Kristo so meda Nyameɛ ase!” (Rom. 7:25) Wɔ ɛwa ne, Yesu wue bɔ ne Yehowa nate so wale yɛ ngoa nanwo dwirɛ yeɛ ne ɔkã ɔ. Ɔnate Yesu wue nati, (1) Yehowa kɔhora kɔfa yɛ bɔne kɔhyɛ yɛ, (2) yanya anyelasoɛ kyɛ bɔne bɔ ɔwɔ yɛnwo ne, Yehowa kɔye kofi berɛ. Yeɛ afei (3) wabukye yenwo
