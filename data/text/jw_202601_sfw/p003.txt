Maame bɔ ne ofi Fonike ne, ne ɔbrɛ yenwo ase. Afei ɔhɔle so srɛle Yesu kyɛ ɔboka ye, ofikyɛ ne odé ye di
<<<P>>>
(Nea ngyekyɛmuɛ 5)
<<<P>>>
3 Maame bie bɔ ne ofi Fonike lale subaen nza bie ali mmerɛ bɔ ne ɔsrɛ Yesu kyɛ ɔboka ye ne. Yekosusu bɔ ɔyɛle nanwo wɔ adesua he anu, na yanea bɔ yekosua yekofi nu mmerɛ bɔ yɛpena kyɛ yɛfa Nyameɛ akwangyerɛ yɛyɛ adwuma ne. Afei yɛkɔnea mmrienzua nza biemɔ bɔ ne bɛfa Nyameɛ akwangyerɛ bɛyɛ adwuma nwo dwirɛ. Bɛ yeɛ bele somafoɛ Petro, Pɔɔlo, ɔne Ɔhene Dawide.
<<<P>>>
ODIKYƐ YƐBRƐ YƐNWO ASE, YENYA GYIDIE, NA YƐKƆSO YƐSRƐ NYAMEƐ KYƐ ƆBOKA YƐ
<<<P>>>
4 Maame bie bɔ ne ofi Tiro ne Sidon mandanu berɛ ne, ne ‘sunzummɔne ha ɔwa brasua paa.’ Yeti ɔwale Yesu berɛ oobuli ndwerɛ srɛle ye kyɛ ɔboka ye. (Mat. 15:21-28) Atee bɔ maame ne fale so ne Yesu dwudwole ne, ɛmɔma yɛnea bɔ yɛkɔhora yekosua yekofi nu ɔ.
<<<P>>>
5 Mmerɛ bɔ ne Yesu ne maame ne dwudwo ne, ɔfale ye totole kyea ba nwo. Nakoso maame ne amvɛ yaa, nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ sɛ ne ɛkɔ b’awuro mɔ nu a, ne ngyea mmaa nyɛ nna. Sɛ wɔ yeɛ Yesu hane sɔ dwirɛ ne hyirele wɔ a, ahaa nzu yeɛ ɛkɔyɛ ɔ? Ahaa ɛkɔfɛ yaa na ahwi afa ye so anaa? Maame bɔ ofi Fonike ne de, nna sɔ koraa yeɛ ɔyɛle ɔ. Ɛhene maa yenwu ye paa kyɛ ne ɔbrɛ yenwo ase. Maame ne sa yɛle nikyee foforɛ bie koso bɔ ne ɔyɛ nyemene paa. Yeɛ ole kyɛ ɔhɔle so srɛle Yesu kyɛ ɔboka ye. Yede, nzu yeɛ ɔmaa ɔyɛle sɔ ɔ? Ne ole Yesu nu gyidie. Ɔnate sɔ gyidie bɔ onyane ye nati, ɔhane Yesu maa otuli sunzummɔne bɔ ɔha ɔwa brasua ne fili ye so. Ne ɛhe yɛ nwanwa paa, ofikyɛ ne Yesu alimoa ahã ahyire ye kyɛ ɔwale kyɛ ɔbaaboka Yisraelfoɛ. Nakoso, bɔ maame ne yɛle nati ɔbokale ye.
<<<P>>>
6 Akwangyerɛ bɔ Yehowa fama yɛ ne, sɛ yekonya so mvasoɛ a, sana yesuesua bɔ Fonike braa ne yɛle ne. Odikyɛ yenya gyidie bɔ nu yɛse, na yɛkɔso yɛsrɛ Nyameɛ kyɛ ɔboka yɛ. Sɛ yɛyɛ sɔ a, okohyire kyɛ yɛbrɛ yenwo ase.
