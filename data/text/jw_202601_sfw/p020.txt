<<<P>>>
BƆ ADESŨA NE KƆHÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Yesu ngoa bɔ ɔfa tole berɛ mane yɛ ne, yɛkɔnea bɔ yɛnu biala kɔyɛ kɔfa kohyire kyɛ yenye sɔ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Nzu Yeɛ Ɛkɔyɛ Kɔfa Kohyire Kyɛ Wɔnye Sɔ Yesu Wue Ne Ɔ?
<<<P>>>
“Na Kristo dɔ ne di yɛ so,” anaa ɔhyɛ yɛ.—2 KOR. 5:14.
<<<P>>>
FA YE kyɛ sua abu agua ɛne ebiemɔ so, yeɛ ebie aaye ɛmɔ ɔ. Sɔ sona ne, aso ɛkɔfa ye asenaa koli anaa? Ɛngɔyɛ sɔ lle, ofikyɛ ɔnne nu a ahaa awu. Yeti bɔ ɔyɛ mane wɔ ne, wɔ wora ngofi ye lle na mmom ofi sɔ mmerɛ ne bɔ ɔkɔ ne, ɛkɔyɛ bɔ ɛkɔhora biala kɔfa kohyire kyɛ wɔnye asɔ bɔ wayɛ wama wɔ ne.
<<<P>>>
2 Kyɛbɔ yenwuni ye wɔ adesua ko hene anu ne, bɔne bɔ bafa bawo yɛ ne, yɛ bɔbɔ yɛngɔhora yengole yɛnwo yengofi nu lle. Nakoso Yesu wue ne ale yɛ, na ɛhene ati, (1) Yehowa kɔhora kɔfa yɛ bɔne kɔhyɛ yɛ, (2) yanya anyelasoɛ kyɛ bɔne bɔ ɔwɔ yɛnwo ne, Yehowa kɔye kofi berɛ. Yeɛ afei (3) wabukye yenwo atee maa yɛne Nyameɛ afia ayɛ kama. Ɔnate ɛhe ati, yɛnye la so kyɛ yɛkɔhora yɛkɔtena ase daa wɔ ewiase foforɛ bɔ ɔba nanu. Bɔ Yesu ayɛ ama yɛ ne, ɔmaa yenwu ye kyɛ okuro yɛ dwirɛ paa. Nahorɛ dwirɛ yeɛ ole kyɛ ɔhyɛle yebo kyɛ okuro yɛ dwirɛ ne, nna ɛnnɛ ɔ. Ofikyɛ ɔwɔ anwuro dɔ namukoraati ne okuro yɛ dwirɛ. (Any. 8:30, 31) Ɔdɔ bɔ Yesu le ma yɛ ne, somofoɛ Pɔɔlo hane kyɛ ‘odi yɛ so,’ anaa ɔhyɛ yɛ. (Kenga 2 Korintofoɛ 5:14, 15.) Sɔ dwirɛ ne kyire kyɛ bɔ Yesu ayɛ ama yɛ ne, ɔnzɛkyɛ yɛmaa yɛ wora fi ye lle, na mmom daa biala odikyɛ yɛyɛ nikyeebie yɛfa yekyire kyɛ yɛnye sɔ.
<<<P>>>
3 Yɛ mukoraati yɛpena kyɛ yɛmaa yɛnye sɔ Yesu wue ne, nakoso ole bɔ yɛnu biala kɔyɛ kɔfa kohyire sɔ ɔ. Nzuati yeɛ yɛkã sɔ ɔ? Wɔde yɛfa mvandoho he yehyirehyire nu. Fa ye kyɛ mmenia nza bie kɔ kuro bie aso, nakoso ɔsono nekaa bɔ benu biala wɔ ɔ. Ɛnea a, atee komapɛ nala yeɛ bemu bɛkɔfa so be-
<<<P>>>
1-2. Yesu ngoa bɔ ɔfa bɔle afɔleɛ mane yɛ ne, nzu yeɛ odikyɛ ɔhyɛ yɛ maa yɛyɛ ɔ, na nzuati ɔ? (2 Korintofoɛ 5:14, 15) (Nea foto ne koso.)
<<<P>>>
