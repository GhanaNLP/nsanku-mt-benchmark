14 Eliza bɔ yalimoa yahã ye dwirɛ ne hane kyɛ: “Sɛ mekɔ adesua anaa mebɔbɔ mesua nikyee a, ɔmaa menwu kyɛ Yehowa dwene menwo, na otie me mbaeɛ. Sɛ ɔba sɔ a ne menye agye sɔa.” Nolan koso te manzini soneafoɛ wɔ Africa, na ɔye dumaa li Diane. Ɔhane edwirɛ bie fale ɔne ɔye nanwo kyɛ: “Sɛ y’aba nu bu sɛɛ po a, nningyein biemɔ tekyɛ mbaeɛ, asafo nhyiamu, ɔne Bible bɔ yɛkɔkenga ne de, yɛnyakyi ye yɛlɛ. Ofikyɛ ɛhene yeɛ ɔmaa yepingye Yehowa na yɛnye gye ɔ. Afei koso ɔmaa yenwu kyɛ bɔ ɔkɔto yɛ biala ne, Yehowa kɔboka yɛ. Ɔwɔ nu kyɛ yɛnze atee deinn bɔ ɔkɔfa so kɔyɛ sɔ ɔ, nakoso yele gyidie kyɛ ɔngɔyakyi yɛ nu lle.”
