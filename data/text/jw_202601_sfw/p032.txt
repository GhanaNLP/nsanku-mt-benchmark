BƆ ƆKƆBOKA WƆ MAA EKOSŨA NIKYEE YÉ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Sɛ Ɛkenga Bible Nu Dwirɛ Bie A, Fa Ye Kyɛ Edwirɛ Ne Sili Ne, Ne Ɛwɔ Berɛ Bie
<<<P>>>
Bible ne kã edwirɛ pee bɔ ɔte nahorɛ bɔ osisili ɔ. Na yɛkɔhora yekosua nningyein pee yekofi sɔ dwirɛ nemɔ nu. Sɛ yɛkenga sɔ dwirɛ nemɔ na yɛfa ye kyɛ ne yɛwɔ berɛ bie yee osili a, ɔkɔboka yɛ paa maa yɛkɔte yebo yé. Yede ma yɛnea nningyein biemɔ bɔ ɔkɔboka yɛ maa yɛkɔyɛ sɔ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
• Ɛyɛ a kenga ye serɛ. Sɛ ɛkenga Bible ne, na ɛkenga ye serɛ a, ɔboka wɔ paa maa ɛkora fa w’adwene bu bɔ ne ɔkɔso wɔ berɛ ne. Sɛ ɛne w’abusua yee ɛmɔkenga Bible nu dwirɛ bie bɔ ɔfa mmeni soma bie nwo a, ɛmɔkɔhora bekɔyɛ ye sɔa: Ɛmɔhyɛ bɛma bɛnwo, na ɛmɔ nu biala ɔkenga sona kɔ̃ deɛ. Ɛhene kɔmaa edwirɛ ne kɔhã ɛmɔ ahone paa.
<<<P>>>
• Fa w’adwene sie bɔ ɛkenga yenwo dwirɛ naso. Sona bɔ ɛkenga yenwo dwirɛ ne, sɛ ɔyɛle nikyeebie a, bɔ mmɔden kyɛ ekonwu adwene bɔ ɔmaa ɔfa yɛle sɔ ne. Ɛkɔhora kobisa wɔnwo kyɛ: Nzu paa yee ɔmaa sona ne dwudwole wɔ sɔ atee naso, anaa ɔyɛle ye nikyee sɔ ɔ? Sɛ me yee sɔ dwirɛ ne tole me a, nzu yee ahaa ɔkɔwa metianu ɔ, na sɛɛ yee ahaa ngɔyɛ me nikye ɔ?
<<<P>>>
• Yɛ bɔ akenga nanwo drɔyen. Sɛ ɛnze drɔyen bɔ po a, bɔ wɔnwo mmɔden kyɛ edwirɛ bɔ akenga afi Bible nanu ne, ɛkɔdrɔ yenwo nikyee kãa bie kɔto berɛ. Sɛ ɛyɛ sɔ a, ɔkɔmaa ɔkɔyɛ tekyɛ ɛwɔ berɛ bie yee edwirɛ ne sili ne, na ɛhene kɔmaa ɔkɔha wɔ tianu.
