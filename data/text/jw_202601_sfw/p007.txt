17 Sɛ yɛbaasi gyinayɛɛ bie a, odikyɛ yedimoa yɛbɔ mbaeɛ yɛfa yɛpena Yehowa akwangyerɛ. Yɛyɛ sɔ a, ne yesuesua Dawide. Nakoso, odikyɛ yɛkae kyɛ, sɛ yandie Yehowa na yesisi gyinayɛɛ biemɔ bɔ ɔttemaye a, yede yɛ bɔbɔ yeɛ yɛkɔfɛ ɔ. Ofikyɛ Yehowa ngɔbɔ yɛnwo waen. (Gal. 6:7, 8) Nakoso, sɛ yedi nahorɛ yɛma Yehowa sɛɛ po a, mmerɛ bie wɔ berɛ a edwirɛ kɔhora kɔto yɛ. Ɔba sɔ a, odikyɛ yɛkã bɔ ɔha yɛ namu yekyire Yehowa wɔ mbaeɛ nu. Na ye koso, ɔkɔkyekye yɛ wora na waboka yɛ.—Flp. 4:6, 7.

KƆSO FA WƆNWO TO YEHOWA SO MA OHYIRE WƆ ATEE
<<<P>>>
18 2026 afoɛnoa dwirɛ ne kã kyɛ: “Anigye yeɛ ole bɛbɔ benwu ye kyɛ behia Nyameɛ akwangyerɛ ɔ.” Ɛnnɛ paa yeɛ odikyɛ yetie sɔ dwirɛ bɔ Yesu hane ne, na yɛpena Yehowa akwangyerɛ ɔ. Nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ mmeni pee nala nné Nyameɛ nni, yeti bɛmva ye akwangyerɛ bɛnyɛ húu. Ebiemɔ koso de, bedé ye bedi, nakoso bɛpena kyɛ bɛsõ ye kyɛbɔ bɛ bɔbɔ bekuro ɔ. Yeɛ ebiemɔ wora, bɔ mmenia kã ne, ɛhene yeɛ begyina so bɛbɔ bɛ bra ɔ. Ɛhe mukoraati amaa bɛnle anigye. Yede, nzu yeɛ ɔkɔhora kɔboka yɛ na yanzuesua bɛ ɔ? Odikyɛ yɛyere yɛnwo yesua Nyameɛ Dwirɛ ne yé, yenya subaen bɔ ɔkɔmaa Yehowa nye kɔgye yɛnwo, ná yɛkɔso yɛyɛ nningyein bɔ ɔkɔmaa ɔkɔbɔ yɛnwo waen ɔ.

18. Sɛɛ yeɛ mmeni pee nwu akwangyerɛ bɔ Nyameɛ fa ma yɛ ne ɔ? Na nzu yeɛ yɛyɛ a, okyire kyɛ yɛfa yɛnwo yɛto Yehowa so ɔ? (Nea foto ne koso.)
<<<P>>>
BƆ FOTO NE KÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ Ɛwɛnɛlɛ Waen ne nyunu: Aliemaa brasua bie ahɔ adesua na ɔma mmuayɛɛ. Ɛberɛ bɔ ɔwɔ ne kyire kyɛ ɔma atee maa Yehowa fa oliemamɔ so bɔ yenwo waen. Krataa bɔ ɔtɔ so 6: Sɔ aliemaa brasua nala, osua Ɛwɛnɛlɛ Waen kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a ye gyidie kɔye se ɔ. Afei ɔkwaahyɛ aliemaa bie ne ɔye nikye. Ɛhene kyire kyɛ ɔpena kyɛ onya subaen bɔ Yehowa nye gye yenwo ɔ. Asafo nu mbanyi nemɔ aasra ye, na otie edwirɛ bɔ bɛkã bekyire ye ne.
