16 Sɛ yɛkora yɛboka mmenia maa bɛbanwu nahorɛ bɔ ɔfa Yehowa nwo, na bɛkɔso ‘bɛnate nahorɛ ne anu’ a, ɔmaa yɛnye gye paa. (3 Yoh. 3, 4) Nakoso sɛ yɛkɔhora yɛkɔyɛ sɔ a, sana yɛyɛ nningyein bɔ oliliso hemɔ: Ma benwu ye kyɛ nahorɛ ne bɔ asua afi Bible nanu ne, ɛfa bɔ wɔ bra, na edé di kyɛ Nyameɛ bɔhyɛ mukoraati koso kɔwa nu. Sɛ ɛyɛ sɔ a, bekosuesua wɔ. Afei sɛ ɛne bɛ sua nikyee a, ma benwu ye kyɛ bɔ ekyirekyire bɛ ne, ofi Bible nanu, na offi wɔ bɔbɔ wɔ tianu. Ma wɔnoa dwudwolɛ yɛ fɛ, na nwu mmerɛ bɔ ɔfata kyɛ ɛkã edwirɛ bie ɔ. Sɛ sona ne pena kyɛ ɔkã edwirɛ bie koso fa hoahoa wɔ a, nnɛmaa wɔ tianu yɛ wɔ fɛ, na mmom fa yenwo animnyam ne ma Yehowa. Sɛ ɛyɛ sɔ a, okyire kyɛ ɛsõ Yehowa bɔ ɔte nahorɛ Nyameɛ ne.

BƆ FOTO NE KÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ Krataa bɔ ɔtɔ so 29: Foto bɔ olimoa nanu ne, aliemaa bie anwu ye kyɛ biaa bie atotɔ Bronya nwo nningyein asie ye awuro. Yeti ɔma onwu ye kyɛ Bronya fi abosonsom nu. Nakoso foto bɔ ɔtɔ so nyɔ nanu ne, mmerɛ bɔ aliemaa ne nwuni Bronya nwo nningyein wɔ awuro berɛ ne, wanga yenwo dwirɛ. Na mmom ɔma biaa ne nwu nningyein biemɔ bɔ ɔyɛ a, okyire kyɛ ɔdwene ye abusua nwo ɔ. Ɛnea a, ɛhe anu beni yeɛ biaa ne nye kɔgye yenwo ɔ?
