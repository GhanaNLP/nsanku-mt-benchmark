<<<P>>>
10 Kenga Kolosefoɛ 4:6. Kristofoɛ bɔ ne bɛwɔ Kolose ne, somafoɛ Pɔɔlo tuli bɛ foɛ kyɛ: “Émɔma ɛmɔ noa dwudwolɛ yɛ fɛ.” Yede, sɔ dwirɛ ne kyire sɛɛ? Greek dwirɛ bɔ behyirele yebo kyɛ ‘yɛma yɛnoa dwudwolɛ yɛ fɛ’ ne, okyire kyɛ yɛkɔhã edwirɛ bɔ ɔkɔhyɛ mmenia ngunaen, ná waboka bɛ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
11 Edwirɛ bɔ ɔwɔ Bible nanu ne, ɔte kyɛ dadeɛ bie bɔ yenoa yɛ nna ɔ, na ɔkora pɛɛ tonn kodwu kyɛ okowura ɛkra ne sunzum b’afia ɔ. Kyire kyɛ, Bible ne kora maa bɔ ɔwɔ y’ahone ne y’adwene nu mukoraati da ali. (Heb. 4:12)
<<<P>>>
10. Sɛ bɛkã kyɛ yɛma yɛnoa dwudwolɛ “yɛ fɛ” a, okyire sɛɛ? (Kolosefoɛ 4:6)
<<<P>>>
11-12. Sɛ yɛpena kyɛ yɛboka mmenia maa benwu nahorɛ bɔ ɔwɔ Bible nanu ne a, odikyɛ yɛnea yé paa, nzuati ɔ? Ma yenwo nhwɛsoɛ. (Nea foto ne koso.)
<<<P>>>
Nakoso, sɛ yannwu kyɛbɔ yɛkɔhã Bible nu dwirɛ yekohyire mmenia a, ebiaa ɔkɔmaa bɛkɔfɛ yɛso yaa, anaa bɛne yɛ kosu aporowaa. Yeti odikyɛ yɛfa somafoɛ Pɔɔlo afutue ne yɛyɛ adwuma, na yɛmaa yɛnoa dwudwolɛ yɛ fɛ mmerɛ bɔ yɛkã Bible nanu dwirɛ yekyire mmenia ne.
<<<P>>>
12 Wɔ de yɛnea sɔ dwirɛ he. Fa ye kyɛ yahɔ asɛnga yeɛ yaato biaa bie ɔ. Ɔhahyirele yɛ kyɛ, ɛyɛ a ɔkoto statuo nyunu bɔ mbaeɛ, na ɔne ɔye ne ye mmaamɔ di Bronya ne Esta. Aso ɛberɛ nala yɛkɔkenga Bible ne yekogua ye so, na yamaa wanwu ye kyɛ ɔttemaye kyɛ ebie kɔkoto statuo nyunu kɔbɔ mbaeɛ, na Bronya ne Esta koso ofi abosonsom nu anaa? (Yes. 44:14-20; 2 Kor. 6:14-17) Ɔwɔ nu, sɛ yɛkã sɔ a ɔte nahorɛ, nakoso sɛ yɛkã ye sɔ mmerɛ ne a, yɛ dwirɛ ne ngɔhã ye ahone.
