10 Mmerɛ bɔ Adam ne Hawa yɛle bɔne ne, wɔde susu ɔhaw bɔ ɔfa wale bɛ so nanwo nea. Benwuni kyɛ banni Nyameɛ mmraa bɔ ne ‘bahyerɛ bagua b’ahone nu’ naso. Yeti bɛ tiboa buale bɛ fɔ, na bɛyɛle basaa. (Rom. 2:15) Afei bɛnye wale bɛnwo so komapɛ, na benwuni kyɛ bɛnwo la so. Yeti bɛfale nyaa bɛkatale bɛnwo, na bɛɛfeale Nyameɛ. (Gye. 3:7, 8) Ɔwale sɔ ne benwu ye kyɛ bayɛ bɔne piri paa bati Nyameɛ. Ɛhene maa ɛfɛlelɛ hane bɛ, esuronyi tɔle bɛ so, na ne bɛnye so atã. Ɛhe yeɛ ɔte mmerɛ bɔ olimoa koraa bɔ bɛhɔle sɔ haw nanu ɔ. Na ne sɔ nningyein nemɔ kɔha bɛ tonn kodwu kyɛ bekowu ɔ.—Gye. 3:16-19.

11 Ɔhaw bɔ bɔne fawale Adam ne Hawa so ne, ebie ala yeɛ yɛfa nu ne. Ɛhene ati yeɛ yenwu amaneɛ sɔ ne. Na sɛ yɛtwe yɛ nu yɛne bɛtene yɛ pɛ po a, nningyein biemɔ wɔ berɛ a yɛngɔhora yengotu yebo lle. Nzuati? Ofikyɛ Bible ne kã kyɛ bafa yɛ ‘bawura nikyee mbaen bie abo.’ (Rom. 8:20) Sɔ dwirɛ ne kɔ ma mmenia mukoraa bɔ yɛwɔ aseɛ he aso ne. Sɛ yɛkɔyɛ nneasoɛ a, ɛnnɛ awaen pee nala yere bɛnwo kyɛ bɛkɔbɔ aseɛ nanwo waen, bekotu nzɛmmɔne bo, bɛkɔboka mmenia maa bɛsa kɔhã bɔ behia, na ye po bamaa koɛ bo apɛ. Nakoso, ye mukoraati ongonzi húu. Ofikyɛ bɛ mmɔden ne mukoraati si ne, ɔtekyɛ bɔ bɛkɔ nzue begua kɛndɛen nu ne. Nakoso, Yesu wue ne maa yenya anyelasoɛ. Atee beni aso?

12 Ɔnate Yesu wue nati, yele anyelasoɛ kyɛ ‘bɛkɔpɛpɛ bekole abɔdeɛ ne bɔbɔ deinn bekofi ngoaasolɛ porɔlowaa nu.’ (Rom. 8:21) Ɛhe kyire kyɛ sɛ yɛkɔ ewiase foforɛ nanu a, yɛngɔfokyɛ na adwenedwene ngɔhyɛ yɛ so kɔ̃. Afei, ɔnate kyɛ bɔne kofi yɛnwo nati, yɛ tiboa ngobua yɛ fɔ, esuronyi ngɔhã yɛ, na yɛnye so ngɔtã. Ebieku koso, sɔ mmerɛ ne, Yesu Kristo bɔ ɔte “Asomdwee Hene” bɔ ɔfale ye ngoa bɔle afɔleɛ mane yɛ ne, yeɛ ne odi yɛ so hene ɔ. Yeti ɔhaw bɔ ɛnnɛ mmenia nnya yenoa ayire ne, ɔkɔboka bɛ maa bekoli so.—Yes. 9:6, 7.
<<<P>>>
13 Sɛ bɔne fi sona nwo koraa a, ɛnea a sɛɛ yeɛ asetena kɔyɛ ɔ? Wɔ de susu yenwo nea kyɛ ekodwɔso kofi wɔ bɛen so ngyerɛmɔ biala bɔ wɔ nekafee ndutu wɔ, anaa wɔ kunu ndete wɔ kyɛ w’abusuaniɛ bie kɔfokyɛ, okowu, anaa ɛhɔen kohũ ye ɔ. Kesaalae po bɔ sɔ mmerɛ ne walle ne, sɛ ‘yɛsɔ anyelasoɛ bɔ yele nanu yé a, ɔkɔyɛ tekyɛ sɛkyɛ bɔ otira yɛ kra nu bɔ nu nde ɔ.’ (Heb. 6:18, 19) Sɛ ɛlɛen nate ɛpo so a, sɛkyɛ boka ye maa mvrama mva ye nni akɔneaba. Sɔ ala yeɛ anyelasoɛ bɔ yele ne boka maa yɛ gyidie yɛ se, na yahora yagyina ɔhaw biala bɔ ɔkɔto yɛ ne noa. Na sɛ enya gyidie bɔ ɔte sɔ a, Yehowa koyira wɔ, ofikyɛ “oyira bɛ bɔ bɛ nye aboro bɛpena ye boso” ne. (Heb. 11:6) Amba, ɔnate Yesu wue nati yele anyelasoɛ kyɛ nningyein kɔyɛ yé kyẽabie, na ɛhene kyekye yɛwora paa.
