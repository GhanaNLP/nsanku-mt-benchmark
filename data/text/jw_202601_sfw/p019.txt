YESU WUE NE MAA YENWU KYƐ YEHOWA SE ANWUMVOIN
<<<P>>>
17 Yesu ngoa bɔ ɔfa bɔle afɔleɛ ne, ɔmaa yenwu ye kyɛ amba, Yehowa “anwumvoinnwunɛ sone.” Ofikyɛ ‘wamaa yɛ bɔ ahaa yɛte awufoɛ wɔ mvomsoɛ nu ne yanya ngoa.’ (Kenga Ɛfesofoɛ 2:4, 5.) Tekyɛ abrandeɛ bɔ sua bu guale ye so bɔ ne osũ pena mmokalɛ ne, bɛbɔ bɛbrɛ bɛnwo ase ne nwu ye kyɛ bɔne afa bɛ ayɛ ngoaa. Yeti bɛkoso, besũ begua Nyameɛ so kyɛ ole bɛ. (Aso. 13:48) Yehowa koso nate Ahennie nanwo nzɛmba bɔ yɛkã naso tie bɛ sufrɛ. Ofikyɛ ɛhene boka bɛ maa benya ɔne Ɔwa Yesu nwo nimdeɛ bɔ odi mu ɔ. (Yoh. 17:3) Kyɛbɔ yalimoa yanwu ye ne, Satan hora maa Adam ne Hawa yɛle Nyameɛ so asoserɛ. Ebiaa ɛhene ati, ye adwene yɛle ye kyɛ bɔ Nyameɛ abɔ ye tianu kyɛ ɔkɔyɛ ne, wayɛ mbaen. Sɛ onyane sɔ adwene ne a, yede ne ɔlakalaka yenwo!
<<<P>>>
18 Mvasoɛ pee wɔ Yesu wue naso bɔ yɛkɔhora yɛkɔdwenedwene yenwo ɔ. Ebie yeɛ ole daa ngoa bɔ yekonya ye kyẽabie ne. Nakoso, nikyee titire paa bie wɔ berɛ bɔ yeti yeɛ Nyameɛ maa Yesu oowuli ɔ, na ɔnzɛkyɛ yɛmaa yɛwora fi ye llé. Ɛhene yeɛ ole kyɛ, edwirɛ bɔ Satan maa osili wɔ Eden turo nu ne, ne Yehowa nate Yesu wue naso kɔmaa yekonwu nahorɛ bɔ ɔwɔ nu ɔ, na ɔnate so wate ye dumaa nanwo. (Gye. 3:1-5, 15) Yehowa sa nate Yesu wue naso lɛle yɛ fili bɔne ne ewue sa nu. Sɔ bɔ ɔyɛle ne maa yenwu ye kyɛ okuro yɛ dwirɛ paa. Afei koso, ɔwɔ nu kyɛ bɔne wɔ yɛnwo, nakoso Yehowa ama yɛ atee kyɛ yɛfa yɛ abrabɔ so yehyire kyɛ Satan te ngondomboniɛ. (Any. 27:11) Yede, ɛkɔyɛ sɛɛ ne ahyire kyɛ wɔnye sɔ Yesu wue ne? Adesua bɔ oliberɛ ne kɔmaa yekonwu ye.
<<<P>>>
17. Yesu ngoa bɔ ɔfa bɔle afɔleɛ ne, sɛɛ yeɛ ɔmaa yenwu ye kyɛ Yehowa se anwumvoin paa ɔ? (Ɛfesofoɛ 2:4, 5)
<<<P>>>
18. Mmerɛ bɔ yɛdwenedwene Yesu wue nanwo ne, nzu yeɛ ɔnzɛkyɛ yɛmaa yɛwora fi ɔ?
