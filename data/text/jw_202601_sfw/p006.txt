SUESUA DAWIDE NA MA YEHOWA BƆ WƆNWO WAEN
<<<P>>>
14 Sɛ yɛnye kɔgye paa a, nikyee foforɛ bie koso wɔ berɛ bɔ yehia ɔ. Ɛhene yeɛ ole kyɛ yɛkɔma atee maa Yehowa kɔbɔ yɛnwo waen. Yede, atee beni yeɛ Yehowa fa so bɔ yɛnwo waen ɔ? Na yɛkɔyɛ sɛɛ ne yamaa wabɔ yɛnwo waen?
<<<P>>>
15 Ne Ɔhene Dawide le gyidie paa kyɛ sɛ ɔkɔ amaneɛ nu a, Yehowa kɔbɔ yenwo waen. (Kenga Edwein 27:5.) Yede ɛnnɛ koso, sɛɛ yeɛ Yehowa bɔ yɛnwo waen ɔ? Bɔ ɔte yeɛ ole kyɛ, sona anaa nikyeebiala bɔ ɔkɔhora kɔmaa gyidie bɔ yele ye wɔ Yehowa nu ne kɔhɔ ase ne, ɔbɔ yɛnwo waen fi yenwo. Wahyɛ bɔ kyɛ, akodeɛ biala bɔ bɛkɔhyehyɛ bekotia ye mmenia ne, ɔngɔyɛ adwuma lle. (Edw. 34:7; Yes. 54:17) Ɔwɔ nu, Satan ne ye sunzummɔne, ɔne bɛbɔ ɔmaa bɛtane yɛnye ne, bɛnwo yɛ se. Nakoso sɛ ɛfa bɛ toto Yehowa nwo a, bɛte ngyee po a, bamini turoo nu. Yeti sɛ bekũ yɛ po a, Yehowa kosa kɔfa yɛ kɔwa ngoa nu bieku

14-15. Atee beni yeɛ Yehowa fa so bɔ ye mmenia nwo waen ɔ? (Edwein 27:5) (Nea foto bɔ ɔwɔ Ɛwɛnɛlɛ Waen ne nyunu ne koso.)
