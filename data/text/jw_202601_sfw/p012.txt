Sua nningyein bɔ ɔkɔboka wɔ maa ekoli w'adwenedwene so ɔ
(Nea ngyekyɛmuɛ 17)
<<<P>>>
rɛ ne a, sana ɛkɔ hɔspitel maa dɔkta nea wɔ. Sɔ ala yeɛ yɛ koso sɛ y'abanu taa bu a, odikyɛ yɛyɛ nhwehwɛmuɛ wɔ Bible ne, ɔne asafo nwomaa ahoroɛ nu yenwu bɔ yeti yeɛ ɔmaa ɔba ye sɔ ɔ. Afei koso, yɛkɔhora yɛkɔhã yenwo dwirɛ yekohyire aliemaa bie maa ɔkɔboka yɛ. Ɛmɔma yɛnea nikyee foforɛ bieku bɔ ɔkɔhora kɔboka yɛ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
16 Kã w'ahone nu dwirɛ kyire Yehowa, na srɛ ye kyɛ ɔboka wɔ. Ne Ɔhene Dawide dé di kyɛ Yehowa se ye paa. Ɛhene ati ɔsrɛle ye kyɛ ɔneanea ye nu, na onwu ‘ye ti anu adwene.’ (Kenga Edwein 139:1-4, 23, 24.) Wɔ koso sɛ adwenedwene hyɛ wɔ so a, ɛkɔhora kɔsrɛ Yehowa kyɛ ɔboka wɔ ma enwu bɔ waafa sɔ adwenedwene ne waato wɔ so ɔ, ɔne bɔ ɛyɛ a ɛkɔhora kogyina yenoa ɔ. Ɛkɔhora koso kobisa wɔnwo kyɛ, ‘Nzu deinn yeɛ me dwenedwene yenwo ɔ? Nzu yeɛ ɔfa sɔ adwenedwene ne ba ɔ? Sɛ ɔba sɔ a, ɛyɛ a mekora mefa m'adwene mesie nikyee foforɛ bɔ ɔkɔmaa menye kɔgye naso anaa?’
<<<P>>>
17 Edwirɛ bɔ ɔfa wɔ haw nanwo ne, sua yenwo nikyee kɔ siɛ. Sɛ yɛnea somafoɛ Pɔɔlo sɔa a, mmerɛ bɔ ne ye abanu abu ne, ɔdwenedwenene Yesu wue ne, ɔne kyɛbɔ Yehowa fa bɔne kyɛ nanwo. Sɔ bɔ ɔyɛle ne bokale ye paa. Wɔ koso odwu mmerɛ bie a, ɛkɔhora kosua Yehowa subaen bie bɔ sɛ w'abanu bu a ɔkɔhyɛ wɔ ngunaen nanwo nikye. Ebie yeɛ ole ye anwumvoin, ye bɔne fakyɛ, ɔne ye dɔ bɔ onni ngasi ne. Ɛkɔhora kɔnea Nhoma a Yehowa Adansefo De Yɛ Nhwehwɛmu nanu, na ekonwu edwirɛ bie bɔ ɔkɔboka wɔ ɔ. Bɔ ekonwu ye ne, tworɔ na fa ta nekaabie, na sɛ w'abanu bu a akenga. Mmerɛ bɔ esua ɛhemɔ ne, fatoto bɔ ɛfa nu nanwo, na nea kyɛ-
