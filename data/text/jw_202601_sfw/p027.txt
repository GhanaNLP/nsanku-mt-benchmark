3 Kyɛbɔ Yesu yɛle ne, yɛ koso yɛbɔ mmɔden kyɛ yɛkɔhã nahorɛ mmerɛ biala, ɔmva yenwo kyɛ ebiaa ɔkɔmaa mmenia nye ngɔgye yenwo ne. Afei koso, sɛ mmenia kɔfɛ yɛso yaa po a, yɛngata nahorɛ bɔ ɔwɔ Bible nanu naso, na mmom yɛmaa benwu ye. Nakoso ɛhene kyire kyɛ, ɔnate kyɛ yɛse kyɛ yɛkã nahorɛ nati, odikyɛ yɛkã yɛ dwirɛ pein-pein anaa? Ɔnzɛkyɛ yɛyɛ sɔ llé! Wɔ adesua he anu ne, yekonya sɔ dwirɛ hemɔ nwo mmuayɛɛ: Nisua yee yɛkɔhora yekonwu nahorɛ ne ɔ? Nahorɛ bɔ ɔwɔ Bible nanu ne, nzuati yee yɛmaa mmenia nwu ye ɔ, na atee beni yee yɛkɔfa so yɛkɔhã yenwo dwirɛ ɔ? Afei mmerɛ beni yee odikyɛ yɛyɛ sɔ ɔ? Sɔ dwirɛ nemɔ nwo mmuayɛɛ kɔboka yɛ maa yekonwu atee pá bɔ yɛkɔfa so yɛkɔhã nahorɛ nanwo dwirɛ maa ɔkɔhã mmenia ahone ɔ.

NISUA YEE YƐKƆHORA YEKONWU NAHORƐ NE Ɔ?
<<<P>>>
4 Sɛ ɛpena nahorɛ paa a, ɛngɔhora ngosã Yehowa nwo llé. Ofikyɛ ɔkã nahorɛ mmerɛ biala. Ye mmraa bɔ ɔfa bɔ ɔte pá, ɔne bɔ ɔttemaye nwo ne sɔa, yenwu ye kyɛ ɔte nahorɛ turoloo. (Edw. 19:9; 119:142, 151) Edwirɛ biala bɔ ɔkɔhã kyɛ okosi ne koso, ɔba nu. (Yes. 55:10, 11) Afei koso, ɛbɔ biala bɔ ɔkɔhyɛ ne, odi so. (Num. 23:19) Bible ne po kã kyɛ Yehowa ngɔhora ngɔpɛ ngondombo lle! (Heb. 6:18) Ɛhene ati, ɔnyɛ nwanwa kyɛ bɛfrɛ ye ‘nahorɛ Nyameɛ’ ɔ.—Edw. 31:5.
<<<P>>>
5 Mmeni pee kã kyɛ, ɔyɛ se paa kyɛ yɛkɔpena Yehowa bɔ ɔte ‘nahorɛ Nyameɛ’ ne si atee. Nakoso ɔtte nahorɛ, ofikyɛ nningyein bɔ wabɔ ne di adanzeɛ kyɛ ɔwɔ berɛ. (Rom. 1:20) Mmerɛ bie, somafoɛ Pɔɔlo ne Greekfoɛ biemɔ bɔ basua nwomaa bɔle ngɔmmɔ wɔ Atense, na ɔmaa benwuni kyɛ Nyameɛ pena kyɛ ‘yɛpena ye si atee,’ ofikyɛ “ɔne yɛ nu biala afia atee anwa.” (Kenga Asomafoɛ 17:27.) Nahorɛ nu, mmenia bɔ bɛbrɛ bɛnwo ase, ná bɛpena Yehowa si atee ne, ye bɔbɔ ɔtwe bɛ ba nahorɛ nanu.—Yoh. 6:44.
<<<P>>>
6 Atee kõ bɔ yɛkɔhora yɛkɔfa so yɛkɔpena Yehowa si atee yee ole kyɛ yekosua Bible ne, ofikyɛ ye sunzum krongron yee ɔmaa bɛfa bɛhworɔle ɔ. (2 Pet. 1:20, 21) Ɛhene ati nikyeebiala bɔ ɔwɔ Bible nanu ne, ɔte nahorɛ na yɛkɔhora yekole yekoli. Ebie yee ole kyɛ, ɔkã kyɛ Yehowa yee ɔbɔle nningyein bɔ ɔwɔ anwuro ne aseɛ he aso mukoraati ɔ. (Gye. 1:1, 26) Bible ne sa maa yenwu bɔ ɔyɛle a bɔne wale wiase, ɔne bɔ yeti yee yenwu amaneɛ na yewu ɔ. (Rom. 5:12; 6:23) Afei koso osa maa yenwu kyɛ Yehowa nate Ɔwa naso kosiesie nikyeebiala bɔ Satan bɔ ɔte “ngondombo baba” ne asɛkye ye ne mukoraati. (Yoh. 8:44; Rom. 16:20) Edwirɛ foforɛ koso wɔ Bible nanu bɔ odikyɛ yedé yedi kyɛ ɔkɔwa nu ɔ. Yee ole kyɛ, Yesu kɔsɛkye abɔnefoɛ, okotingye bɛbɔ bawu ne, ɔkɔmaa aseɛ ne kɔyɛ paradise, ná waye bɔne bɔ ɔwɔ yɛnwo namu wafi berɛ. (Yoh. 11:25, 26; 1 Yoh. 3:8) Amba, Yehowa alo yɛ paa, ofikyɛ wamaa yanwu nahorɛ ne, na wasa wafa wawura yɛsa kyɛ, yɛhã yenwo dwirɛ yehyire mmenia.—Mat. 28:19, 20.
