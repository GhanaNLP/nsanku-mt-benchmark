NDWOM 55
<<<P>>>
Mma Hom Nnsuro Hɔn!
<<<P>>>
DZA YEBOSUA
<<<P>>>
Mma nngye atorsɛm a Satan keka fa Jehovah Nyankopɔn nye Jesus Christ ho no nndzi da.
<<<P>>>
“Hom nsiw Abɔnsam kwan, na oboguan efi hom nkyɛn.”
—JAS. 4:7.
<<<P>>>
YESI gyinae dɛ yɛbɔsom Jehovah ara pɛr a, nna Satan nye n’esunsum fi no hɔn akoko apaa. Naaso Satan nyim paa dɛ Jehovah taa hɛn ekyir, ntsi ɔyɛ dɛn ara mpo a orunntum nndzi hɛn do konyim da. Ntsi dza ɔpɛ ara nye dɛ ɔbɛma yeegyaa Jehovah som mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a Jehovah rommboa hɛn bio. Adze kor a ɔyɛ nye dɛ ɔpɛ dɛ yenya adwen dɛ Jehovah nye ne Ba no mmpɛ hɛn asɛm na wɔnndwen hɛn ho.—2 Cor. 11:14; Jas. 1:6.
<<<P>>>
2 Sɛ asogyafo wɔ akono a, ɔsɛ dɛ wotsie dza hɔn mpanyimfo no bɛka no. Sɛ woenntsie hɔn mpanyimfo no na obiara yɛ dza ɔpɛ a, obotum dze hɔn nkwa ato esian mu. Dɛmara na hɛn so yɛnye Satan na ‘n’esunsum fi’ ridzi ako. (Eph. 6:12) Satan Abɔnsam nyim dɛ sɛ yɛnngye Jehovah Nyankopɔn nye Jesus Christ nndzi a, yerinntsie hɔn. Ntsi ɔyɛ araa dɛ ɔremma yɛnngye hɔn nndzi. Adzesua yi mu no, yɛbɔhwɛ dza Satan yɛe a amma nkorɔfo anngye Jehovah enndzi wɔ tsetse mber mu nye mbrɛ osi yɛ dɛm wɔ hɛn aber yi mu so. Afei, yɛbɔhwɛ mbrɛ yebesi ayɛ hɛn adze amma yɛanngye ne ndaadaasɛm no enndzi. Adzesua a odzi hɔ no mu so, yɛbɔhwɛ akwan afofor ebien a Satan fa do dze daadaa hɛn mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a yebegyaa Jehovah som.*
<<<P>>>
* Ɔnnyɛ aber nyina na Satan nankasa dze ɔhaw ahorow a yehyia no ba hɛn do tsenn. Naaso “wiadze no no sunsum” a opiapia nkorɔfo ma wɔsoɛr tsia Nyankopɔn n’asomfo na wɔyɛ hɔn adze gyangyan no, Satan noara na ɔhyɛ ekyir. Dɛm ntsi dɛ yɛrefa ɔhaw bi mu o, dɛ wɔretan hɛn enyi o, ne nyina Satan na ɔhyɛ ekyir.—1 Cor. 2:12; 1 John 5:19.
