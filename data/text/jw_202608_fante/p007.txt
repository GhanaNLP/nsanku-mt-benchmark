16 Sɛ yɛdwendwen ndzɛmba a Jehovah na Jesus yɛ ma hɛn ndɛ no ho a, ɔno so bɔboa hɛn ma yɛagye edzi paa dɛ wɔdɔ hɛn ampa. Afei so, sɛ yɛkaakaa ndzɛmba bi a Jehovah yɛe dze boaa hɛn mber bi a abɛsen kɔ no a, ɔbɛma yɛagye edzi paa dɛ ɔbɔkɔ do ara aboa hɛn. Yebotum nye hɛn nuanom Christianfo abɔ nkɔmbɔ na yeebisa hɔn mbrɛ Jehovah esi aboa hɔn no ho asɛm. (Mbɛ. 22:17) Sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm a, ɔbɛma yeenya awerɛhyɛmu paa dɛ ɔmmfa ho ɔhaw biara a yebehyia no, Jehovah na Jesus bɔboa hɛn.—Kenkan Ndwom 118:5, 6.

17 Hɛn nyina, yɛpɛ dɛ yɛkɔ do ara yɛ Jeho-
<<<P>>>
vah Nyankopɔn na Jesus Christ hɔn anyenkofo. Naaso kesi dɛ wɔbɛsɛɛ Satan nye n’esunsum fi no korakora no, obehia dɛ yɛkɔ do ara nye hɔn dzi esi amma wɔannsɛɛ yɛnye Jehovah na Jesus hɔn nntamu. (Nyi. 20:1-3, 10) Mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a yɛremma hɛn atamfo yi kwan ma wɔnndaadaa hɛn no, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛma anyɛnkofa a ɔda yɛnye Jehovah ntamu no mu yɛ dzen, na yɛgye dzi paa dɛ Jehovah na no Dɔba no pɛ hɛn asɛm paa na yɛsom bo ma hɔn so. Sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm a, dɛ mbrɛ Jesus kae no, obiara ‘runntum nnhwim hɛn mmfi Egya no ne nsamu.’—Kenkan John 10:29; Jas. 4:7.
