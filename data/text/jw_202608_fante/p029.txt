Merema ɔkasa akyerɛ kuw bi a wɔdze China kasa yɛ asafo nhyiamu, 2006
<<<P>>>
Mubohun dɛ, Jehovah yɛ hɛn Egya, ntsi ɔbɔkɔ do ara ahwɛ hɛn na ɔama hɛn nsa aka dza yehia aber biara. Ntsi mekaa no wɔ me tsirmu dɛ, sɛ medze Jehovah n’apɛdze dzi kan wɔ m’abrabɔ mu a, no ho rinnhia dɛ modwendwen biribiara ho dze haw moho; ɔbɔhwɛ me. Michel dɛm yi, nna dza ohia no ara nye dɛ ɔbɛyɛ dza Jehovah pɛ, ntsi Jehovah so hwɛɛ no kama na iyi maa n’enyi gyee. Ntsi mubohun dɛ, sɛ mara so meyɛ dɛm a, Jehovah bɔhwɛ me.
<<<P>>>
Mugu do rusua Bible no, mubohun dɛ ɔsɛ dɛ meyɛ nsesa. Ntsi meyɛɛ dɛ mibegyaa towa na wii nom. Misii dɛm gyinae no wiei ara pɛr na medze towa nye wii biara a nna mowɔ no kogu u gya mu hyeew ne nyina, na monnoom bio ara dze besi ndɛ a merekasa yi. Ɔno ekyir no, mehyɛɛ ase dɛ morokɔ adzesua. Da kor bi, aber a mokɔr adzesua no, mekaa kyerɛɛ onua bi dɛ ontwitwa me tsinhwi no mma me, na Kingdom Hall hɔ ara na otwitwaa ne nyina.
<<<P>>>
Adapɛn kakra ekyir no, migyaa kuw a nna modɔm wɔ French Guiana ekuraase hɔ no, na mesanee kɔr France. Mokɔhwehwɛɛ beebi a Kingdom Hall wɔ na motoaa me Bible adzesua no do. Nna megyaa towa nye wii nom, megyaa nsa we, na nna monnkɔ apon-to a n’ase yɛ gyigyeegye bio. Nna ɔyɛ m’ebusuafo mpo nwanwa dɛ metum asesa dɛm. Naaso nna hɔn enyi nngye kwan a mefa do ka asɛmpa no kyerɛ hɔn no ho, osiandɛ nna meka me nsɛm penpen. Ekyir yi, mubohun dɛ, sɛ mepɛ dɛ midzi hɔn dase a, ɔsɛ dɛ menye hɔn kasa yie na mema wɔtse nka dɛ mubu hɔn.
<<<P>>>
Wonumaa me afe 1996. Mobɔɔ Jehovah mpaa dɛ ɔmboa me ma mara so membɛyɛ asɛmpatserɛw-nyi tse dɛ Michel. Nna ɔyɛ me dɛ iyi nye kwan pa a mubotum afa do akyerɛ dza Jehovah ayɛ ama me no ho enyisɔ.

M’EGYA JEHOVAH AKYƐ ME NDZƐMBA PII
<<<P>>>
M’enyi bɛgyee akyerɛbaa hoɔfɛwfo bi a nna ɔwɔ m’asafo mu no ho. Nna wɔfrɛ no Claudine. Nna ɔbɔ noho mbɔdzen wɔ asɛnka edwuma no mu, na nna mbuae a ɔma wɔ adzesua ase no ma muhu dɛ ɔdɔ Jehovah paa. Ntsi meyɛɛ dɛ mobɔwar no, naaso nna mepɛ dɛ muhu dɛ n’enyi gye asɛmpatserɛw edwuma no ho anaa. Ɔmaa mubohun dɛ n’enyi gye ho paa! Ntsi yɛwaree wɔ April 2000.
<<<P>>>
Afe a odzi hɔ no, yɛhyɛɛ akwampaa edwuma no ase. Mber kakra ekyir no, yɛyɛɛ dɛ yebosua Chinese Mandarin kasa na yɛdze akɔboa kuw bi a wɔdze dɛm kasa no yɛ adzesua. Nna dɛm kuw no bɛn hɛn asafo no. Nna kasa no no sua yɛ me dzen paa. Odzi kan no, nna ɔsɛ dɛ musua mbrɛ Chinafo si susu ndzɛm-ba ho. Afei, nna ɔsɛ dɛ mohwehwɛ kwan a mubotum afa do aka asɛm no akyerɛ hɔn ma hɔn enyi agye ho. Nokwasɛm nye yi, mennhu mbrɛ wosi ka Manda-rin kasa no yie da. Naaso meyɛɛ dza mubotum. Mbrɛ yɛnye hɛn nuanom Chinafo yi dze enyigye bɔɔ mu yɛɛ asɛnka edwuma no dze, hɛn werɛ rimmfir da. Jehovah hyiraa hɛn paa, na ntsetsee a ɔnam hɔn do dze maa hɛn no so, nna ɔbɔboa hɛn wɔ Jehovah no som mu daakye.
