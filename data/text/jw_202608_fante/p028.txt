Nna mibisaa moho dɛ, ‘Ebɛnadze ntsi na asaase no ma yenya ndzɛmba a yehia no pii dɛm?’ Sɛ ehwɛ asaase no a, ɔtse dɛ fie bi a obi ahyɛ da ara esiesie mu kamakama dze rotweɔn obi dɛ ɔmbɛtsena mu. Iyi maa muhun dɛ ndzɛmba a ɔwɔ wiadze yi mu no ammba kwa. Sɛ dɛm dze a, nna ɔno woana na osiesiee dɛm ndzɛmba yi nyina maa hɛn? Mubohun dɛ ɔnyɛ dɛn ara a Ɔbɔadze bi wɔ hɔ, naaso nna minnyim no ho hwee.
<<<P>>>
Mokɔr do kenkaan Bible no. Naaso dɛm aber yi dze, nna mepɛ dɛ muhu dza Nyankopɔn pɛ dɛ meyɛ. Mehyɛɛ ase bɔɔ no mpaa na nna meyɛ sign of the cross naaso nna minnyim dza ɔkyerɛ mpo. Afei so, nna yebɔ dzin Jah wɔ hɛn ndwom ahorow a yɛtow no mu, ntsi mibisaa moho dɛ, ‘Ɔbɔadze no ne dzin dze Jah anaa?’

MUBOHUN M’EGYA A NE YAMU YE NO
<<<P>>>
Ɔyɛ me dɛ aber a mehyɛɛ ase bɔɔ mpaa no, Ɔbɔadze no hun dɛ mepɛ dɛ muhu no yie. Da kor bi, me nyɛnko basia bi a nna ɔwɔ hɛn ekuraase hɔ no hyiaa Jehovah Adasefo bi na wɔmaa no buukuu bi. Nna wɔato dzin You Can Live Forever in Paradise on Earth (Ibotum Atsena Ase Daa wɔ Paradise Asaase Do).* Aber a mehyɛɛ ase dɛ merekenkan buukuu no, moho boow me. Mubohun dɛ hɛn asaase
<<<P>>>
* Jehovah Adasefo na wotsintsimii naaso seseiara wonntsintsim no bio.

yi bɛdan Paradise na mubotum atsena ase daa na iyi maa m’enyi gyee paa. Dza nna morohwehwɛ ara nye no. Kyerɛ dɛ nna mepɛ dɛ metsena beebi a hɔ yɛ fɛw na menye m’adɔfo tsena asomdwee mu a obiara runnwu. Dɛm ntsi Ɔbɔadze a ne yamu ye na ɔpɛ dɛ yenya daakye papabi a ɔtse dɛm no, nna mepɛ dɛ muhu no yie na mefa no nyɛnko.

Mubohun dɛ ɔsɛ dɛ meyɛ nsesa ahorow bi wɔ m’abrabɔ mu, naaso nna mibehia mboa. Nna me nyɛnko banyin bi nye Ɔdasefo asɛmpatserɛwnyi bi a wɔfrɛ no Michel sua Bible no. Ntsi mekaa kyerɛɛ me nyɛnko no dɛ mepɛ dɛ mihyia Michel.
<<<P>>>
Da a mihyiaa Michel no ara na ɔtoo nsa frɛɛ me dɛ membra ehyiadzi bi wɔ Matoury, nna ɔbɛn ɔman no n’ahenkurow Cayenne. Nna ofi beebi a mowɔ no rokɔ hɔ yɛ bɛyɛ akwansin 155 anaa kilomita 240. Nna me tsinhwi efuw ayɛ tsentsen, na nna megya abɔdwesa basaa bi. Nna mehyɛ trousers colours colours bi, na shirt so a nna mehyɛ no, nna ɔso kyɛn me koraa. Atar a mehyɛe no annhaw enuanom no, mbom wɔnye me foow kaar kɔr ehyiadzi no ase, wɔmaa me edziban na wɔmaa me beebi so dae. Ɔdɔ na ayamuyie a enuanom no daa no edzi kyerɛɛ me no maa mebɛgyee dzii dɛ nokwar no ara nye yi.
