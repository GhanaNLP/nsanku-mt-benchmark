Hom Mma Yennya Ɔdɔ

DZA YEBOSUA
Yebosua biribi efi ɔsomafo Paul, Philemon na Onesi-mus hɔ, na ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yeehu mbrɛ yebesi aboa ma asomdwee atsena asa-fo no mu.
<<<P>>>
‘Medze Ɔdɔ Rototɔ Aserɛ Wo’
<<<P>>>
“Medze ɔdɔ mbom rototɔ aserɛ wo.”—PHILEM. 9.
<<<P>>>
ƆYƐ hɛn dɛw paa dɛ asomdwee wɔ yɛnye hɛn nuanom Chris-tianfo ntam u. Dɛm asomdwee yi ma yehu dɛ Paradise bɛyɛ dɛw paa. (Isa. 32:17, 18) Ɔyɛ nokwar dɛ Jehovah na ɔma yenya dɛm asomdwee yi, naaso ɔyɛ hɛn mu biara n’asodzii dɛ ɔhwɛ hu dɛ ɔremma biribiara nnsɛɛ dɛm asomdwee no. (Rom. 12:18) Sɛ dɛm dze a, nna ɔno yebesi dɛn aboa ma asomdwee akɔ do atse-na asafo no mu? Sɛ yɛfom hɛn nua bi anaa sɛ hɛn nua bi fom hɛn a, ebɛnadze na yebotum ayɛ amma yɛnnye no ntam u ann-sɛɛ? Ma yensusu adzeserɛdze bi a ɔsomafo Paul dze too Phile-mon n’enyim no ho. Nna dɛm adzeserɛ no fa Onesimus ho, na ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yeenya asɛm a yeebisa yi ho mbuae.
<<<P>>>
PAUL N’ADZESERƐ NO
<<<P>>>
2 Kenkan Philemon 8, 9, 17. Paul kyerɛɛw krataa kɛmaa Philemon, na ɔhyɛɛ no nkuran dɛ ɔnsan ngye n’akowaa Onesi-mus bio. Paul serɛɛ Philemon dɛ ɔnyɛ dɛm osiandɛ nna onyim dɛ ɔdɔ Christ nye no nuanom Christianfo. Nna woana koraa nye Philemon? Nna Philemon yɛ onua bi a ɔwɔ Colossae asafo no mu. Nna obiara nyim no dɛ ne gyedzi so paa na nna ɔdɔ no nuanom so. Nna ɔwɔ akowaa bi a wɔfrɛ no Onesimus na nna ɔnnyɛ Christiannyi. Onesimus guanee fii Philemon ne nkyɛn kɔr Rome, na ɔbɛyɛ dɛ ɔrokɔ no so owiaa no kaa ho. (Philem. 18; hwɛ no ho nkyerɛkyerɛmu no.) Aber a nna Onesimus wɔ Rome no, ɔbɛyɛɛ Christiannyi na ɔfaa Paul nyɛnko pampee. (Col. 4:9) Naaso nna Rome mbra no hwehwɛ dɛ Onesimus san kɔ no wura Philemon ne nkyɛn, ntsi Paul maa ɔsanee kɔree.—Philem. 12, 13; hwɛ “I am sending him back to you” no ho nkye-rɛkyerɛmu.
<<<P>>>
3 Bi a nna Paul rodwen ho dɛ, ‘Sɛ Onesimus san kɔ a, Phile-mon bɛyɛ no dɛn? Nna mbra ma Philemon kwan dɛ ɔtwe
<<<P>>>
1. Ebɛn asodzi na hɛn mu biara wɔ?
<<<P>>>
2. Ebɛn asɛm na osii Philemon na Onesimus hɔn ntam u? (Philemon 8, 9, 17)
<<<P>>>
3. Aber a Paul kaa kyerɛɛ Onesimus dɛ ɔnsan nkɔ Philemon hɔ no, ɔbɛyɛ dɛ nna ebɛn nsɛm na Paul rodwendwen ho? (Hwɛ mfonyin a ɔwɔ Ɔweɔn-Aban yi enyim no so.)
