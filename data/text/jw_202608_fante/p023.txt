Sɛ hɔn a wɔsoɛr tsia hɛn no huna-huna hɛn dɛ woboku hɛn mpo a, yɛmmbɔ hu. Mbom yɛkɔ do ara dze nokwardzi som Jeho-vah osiandɛ yenyim dɛ sɛ yewu mpo a, Jesus bɛsan enyan hɛn aba nkwa mu bio. (John 6:39, 40) Otsia ebien, ɔkyerɛ dɛ ɔmmfa ho dɛ bɔn wɔ hɛnho no, onnyi dɛ yesuro osiandɛ Jeho-vah rinnyi hɛn tokyen. Mbom no Jehovah ma yehu dɛ ɔdɔ hɛn, na ɔgye hɛn to mu. Na osian werdam afɔrbɔ no so ntsi, yɛwɔ awerɛ-hyɛmu dɛ sɛ yɛyɛ bɔn na yɛserɛ Jehovah hɔ bɔnfakyɛ a, obohu hɛn mbɔbɔr na ɔdze akyɛ hɛn.—1 John 4:9, 10.

9 Naaso hɛn nuanom Christianfo binom wɔ hɔ a wɔtse nka dɛ dza wɔbɛyɛ wɔ Jehovah no som mu biara ronnsɔ n’enyi. Bi a wɔtse nka dɛ wɔnnsɛ hwee na Jehovah so nndɔ hɔn osian tsebea bi a wɔafa mu wɔ abrabɔ mu ntsi. Anaadɛ bɔn bi a wɔayɛ etwa mu ho asɛm re-tseetsee hɔn ɔmmfa ho dɛ wɔaserɛ bɔnfakyɛ efi Jehovah hɔ na wɔama mpanyimfo no aboa hɔn mpo no. Sɛ etse nka dɛm a, ebɛnadze na obotum aboa wo?

10 Ma yɛnyɛ mfatoho bi. Sɛ obi ne nan mu bu na okohu dɔkta a, dɔkta no bɛka mbiew no esi enyim na ɔakyerɛ no ndzɛmba kor anaa ebien bi a ɔbɔboa no ma oetum asan anantsew bio. Naaso sɛ nyimpa no nya adwen dɛ mbrɛ oesi epira no orunntum nnantsew bio so ɛ? Ɔsɛ dɛ ɔgye dɔkta no dzi na ɔyɛ dza ɔaka akyerɛ no dɛ ɔnyɛ no. Sɛ ɔyɛ dɛm a, nkakra-nkakra no, obotum anantsew bio na oetum etu mbirika mpo.

11 Dɛmara na woara so, sɛ bɔn bi a ayɛ etwa mu ho asɛm da ho ara retseetsee wo ma etse nka dɛ Jehovah nndɔ wo bio a, gye dzi dɛ Jeho-vah dze wo bɔn akyɛ wo. Kaa dɛ ɔdɔ wo paa, ɔno ntsi na ɔmaa no Dɔba no bowui maa wo no. (John 1:16, 17; Rom. 3:23, 24; 1 John 3:19, 20) Kɔ do ara bɔ Jehovah mpaa, sua n’A-sɛm Bible no na kɔ adzesua, na sɛ eyɛ dɛm ndzɛmba yi a, ɔbɔboa wo ma agye ato mu dɛ Jehovah dze wo bɔn akyɛ wo. (Ndw. 1:2; 1 Thess. 5:17; Heb. 10:25) Dɛ mbrɛ ɔbɛgye mber ana obi a ne nan mu ebu no noho asan atɔ no bio no, dɛmara so na bi a ɔbɛgye mber ana agye ato mu dɛ Jehovah dze wo bɔn akyɛ wo. Naaso sɛ eyɛ ndzɛmba a yɛaka ho asɛm yi a, nkakrankakra no ebɛba abɛgye edzi paa dɛ ɔdɔ wo. Dɛm ntsi mma mma mbrɛ isi tse nka no mmbu w’abaw mu ma nngyaa Jehovah som. Mbom kɔ do ara yɛ ndzɛmba a ɔbɔboa wo ma atwe abɛn Jehovah paa. (Phil. 3:16) Afei so, da Jehovah ase da biara dɛ ɔma wo ahoɔdzen ma edze nokwardzi som no. Ho-kwan soronko a Nyankopɔn nam Christ do ama yeenya dze rosom no a yennsuro hwee no, hɛn enyi sɔ papaapa!
