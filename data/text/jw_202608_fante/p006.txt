Sɛ yɛdze hɛn adwen si ndzɛmba a ɔbɛma yɛakɔ do nye Jehovah ntamuyɛ papa no do a, yɛremma kwan ma Satan nndaadaa hɛn
(Hwɛ nkyekyɛmu 14)
<<<P>>>
no nyɛnko. (Jas. 4:4) Afei so, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛyɛ ahwɛyie paa na yɛanngye asɛm biara a yɛbɛkenkan anaa yɛbɛtse no enndzi. Ɔtɔ da a, yɛbɛkenkan asɛm bi anaa yɛbɛtse asɛm bi a ɔnnyɛ nokwar koraa anaa nokwar ketseketse bi na ɔwɔ mu. Sɛ yɛannyɛ ahwɛyie a ndzɛmba ahorow a ɔtsetse dɛm botum adaadaa hɛn. Naaso sɛ yɛgye ber sua Bible no nye asafo nwoma ahorow na yɛfa hɔn a wɔdɔ Jehovah paa no nyɛnko a, obiara runntum nndaadaa hɛn. Sɛ yɛtse dza Bible no kyerɛkyerɛ no ase yie a, yɛremma kwan ma Satan nnsɛɛ yɛnye Jehovah ntamu.—Mbɛ. 13:20; Andw. 17:11.

15 Sɛ yesua Bible no yie a, ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yɛagye edzi dɛ Jehovah na Jesus pɛ hɛn asɛm paa na wɔpɛ dɛ hɛn enyi gye. (Mbɛ. 22:19) Sɛ yɛkenkan Bible mu nsɛm a ɔma yehu mbrɛ Jehovah na Jesus si dwen nyimpa ho na yɛdwendwen ho kɔ ekyir a, ɔbɛma yeenya awerɛhyɛmu dɛ sɛ yɛfa hɔn nyɛnko a, nna yɛnntoow hɛnho nnkyenee. Afei so, awerɛkyekyesɛm pii wɔ Bible no mu a ɔma yehu mbrɛ Jehovah na Jesus si dɔ hɛn. Sɛ yɛbɛyɛ mfatoho a, Jehovah se ɔdɔ n’asomfo kyɛn
