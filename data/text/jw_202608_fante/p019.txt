wo nua bi no bɔn bɛkye no. Nokwasɛm nye dɛ yɛdɔ hɛn nuanom, naaso ɔtɔ da a, ɔyɛ dzen ma hɛn dɛ yɛdze hɔn bɔn bɛkye hɔn. Ebɛnadze na obotum aboa hɛn ma yɛdze hɔn bɔn akyɛ hɔn? Ma yɛnhwɛ asɛm a Paul kyerɛwee kɛmaa Colossae asafo a nna wohyia mu wɔ Philemon ne fie no. Asɛm no wɔ Colossaefo 3:13. (Kenkan.) Ɔwodze fa no dɛ Philemon tse hɔ bi na wɔrekenkan krataa no, ehwɛ a woana no ho adwen na ɔbɛba ne tsir mu? Nna Onesimus no ho ebufuw da ho hyɛ no mu anaa? Sɛ dɛm dze a, ɔno nna krataa no rema oehu dza ɔsɛ dɛ ɔyɛ. Nna ɔsɛ dɛ ɔkɔ do nya Onesimus ho abotar na ofi n’akoma mu dze no bɔn kyɛ no. Hɛn so sɛ ɔyɛ dzen ma hɛn dɛ yɛdze obi no mfom bɛkye no a, Paul n’afotu yi besi dɛn aboa hɛn?

17 Aber a Paul kaa dɛ yɛnkɔ do ara nnya hɛn nuanom ho abotar no, nna ɔpɛ dɛ ɔkyerɛ dɛn? Nna ɔrehyɛ hɛn nkuran dɛ sɛ hɛn nua bi fom hɛn anaa ɔhyɛ hɛn ebufuw a, ɔsɛ dɛ yenya no ho abotar na yɛnye no dzi no yie. Dɛm kyerɛwsɛm no ara so ma yehu dɛ Jehovah dɔ hɛn na ɔdze bɔn kyɛ ntsi ɔsɛ dɛ yesuasua no. Jehovah nnhwɛ kwan dɛ yɛbɛyɛ dza ɔbor hɛn ahoɔdzen do. Onyim dɛ mfom wɔ hɛn ho, naaso ɔdze n’adwen si suban pa a yɛwɔ no do. (Ndw. 103:13, 14; 130:3) Na osian iyi ntsi ɔkɔ do ara da nokwar dɔ edzi kyerɛ hɛn na ohu hɛn mbɔbɔr.

(Kwa. 3:22, 23) Sɛ yɛbɔ mbɔdzen hu hɛn nuanom dɛ mbrɛ Jehovah hu hɔn no a, ɔrennyɛ dzen mma hɛn koraa dɛ yɛbenya hɔn ho abotar dɛ mbrɛ hɔnara so wonya hɛn ho abotar no. Sɛ yɛbɛyɛ mfatoho a, sɛ yedua dua bi na yenyim dɛ sɛ ɔpaa nhyiren a ɔbɛyɛ fɛw paa a, aber a ɔmmpaa nhyiren mpo no, yebenya ho abotar na yɛakɔ do ara adodɔw ho egugu do nsu. Dɛmara na hɛn so sɛ yɛdze hɛn adwen si suban pa a hɛn nuanom wɔ no do a, ɔbɔboa hɛn ma sɛ wɔfom hɛn a, yeenya hɔn ho abotar, na yɛdze akyɛ hɔn na yɛnye hɔn edzi no yie.

18 Biribiara a ereyɛ dze aboa ma asomdwee akɔ do atsena asafo no mu no, Jehovah hu na n’enyi sɔ paa. Ntsi da biara yɛ ndzɛmba a ɔbɛma enye wo nuanom ntamu ayɛ kama. Enye hɔn nkasa yie na ka hɔn ho nsɛmpa. Sɛ efom wo nua bi a, pa no kyɛw na yɛ dza ibotum biara na enye no nsiesie hom ntamu. Afei fi w’akoma mu dɔ wo nuanom, osiandɛ ɔno na ɔbɛma edze hɔn bɔn akyɛ hɔn. Sɛ eyɛ dɛm ndzɛmba yi nyina a, ɔbɔboa ma asomdwee akɔ do atsena asafo no mu na wɔbɛkan wo aka ahobrɛadzefo a “wobenya asaase no edzi” no ho na w’enyi so bɛgye afebɔɔ.—Ndw. 37:11.
