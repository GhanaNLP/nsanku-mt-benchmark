BIBLE MU ASƐM BI A W’ENYI BƐGYE HO
<<<P>>>
Dua Bi A Onyan Ntsɛm
<<<P>>>
Almond anaa aborɔfo nkatse dua ka ndua bɛyɛ 30 a Bible no ka ho asɛm no ho. Sɛ awɔwber rubotwa mu a, ndua a wɔpaa nhyiren no, almond dua no ka ho. Ɔntɔ araa dɛ January bosoom no bɛba n’ewiei no dze, nna dua yi apaa nhyiren dada. Otum paa nhyiren kɔkɔɔ anaa fitaa. Hebrew kasa mu no, dza dua yi ne dzin kyerɛ nye “nyia oenyan.” Aso dɛm dua yi botum ama yeehu biribi afa Ɔbɔadze no ho anaa?
<<<P>>>
Sɛ yɛhwɛ asɛm a ɔwɔ Jeremiah 7:25 no a, Jehovah kaa nankasa noho asɛm dɛ da biara nna ɔsoɛr ahanamakye soma no nkɔnhyɛfo wɔ no nkorɔfo nkyɛn. Ɔwɛɔn-Aban a ɔbae wɔ March 15, 2011 no kyerɛkyerɛɛ mu dɛ: “Tse dɛ mbrɛ aborɔfo nkatse dua ‘nyan’ anaa ɔpaa nhyiren ntsɛm no, dɛmara so na ɔtse dɛ mbrɛ Jehovah so ‘soɛr ahanamakye’ . . . kɔbɔ no nkorɔfo kɔkɔ wɔ dza asoɔdzen a wɔreyɛ no dze bɛba hɔn do no ho. . . . Nna Jehovah renngye n’ahom na nna ɔbɛma ‘n’enyi ada hɔ’ kesi dɛ nkɔm a ɔahyɛ no bɛba mu.”—Jer. 1:11, 12.
<<<P>>>
Nna ɔtɔ da a Bible no dze nkɔkora na mberwa hɔn tsir do dwen fitaa no toto aborɔfo nkatse ne nhyiren fitaa no ho. (Ɔsɛnk. 12:5) Afei so, osiandɛ ne nhyiren no yɛ fɛw paa ntsi, ɔbɛyɛ dɛ nkɔɔpow a nna osisi kandzeadua a ɔwɔ ntamadan no mu no nkorbata do no, Jehovah maa wɔyɛɛ no tse dɛ aborɔfo nkatse dua no ne nhyiren.—Ex. 37:17-20.
