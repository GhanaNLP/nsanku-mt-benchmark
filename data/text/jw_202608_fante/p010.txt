7 Satan pɛ dɛ yenya adwen dɛ osiandɛ ɔsor mbrɛ yesi tse, ebusua a yefi mu nye mbrɛ wosii tsetsee hɛn no ntsi, yerunntum nye hɛn nuanom nntsena asomdwee mu ma ɔnnyɛ yie. Ɔfa iyi do dze sɛɛ asomdwee a ɔwɔ hɛn ntamu no. Ebɛnadze na yebotum ayɛ amma yɛamma Satan kwan ma ɔannsɛɛ koryɛ a ɔwɔ hɛn ntamu no? Sɛ akasakasa ba yɛnye hɛn nuanom ntamu a, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛbɔ mbɔdzen nye hɔn siesie no ntsɛmara. (Kenkan Ephesusfo 4:25-27.) Fi w’akoma mu dɔ wo nuanom na nya hɔn ho adwen pa. Ɔwɔ mu dɛ ɔsor mbrɛ hɛn mu biara si tse dze, naaso hɛn mu biara hia ne nyɛnko. (1 Cor. 12:12) Yɛnye hɛn nuanom Christianfo nyina roko etsia Satan nye n’esunsum fi no.—1 Pet. 5:8, 9.

9 Kwan kor a Satan fa do bu hɛn abaw mu nye dɛ ɔma nkorɔfo tan hɛn enyi. Ɔsomafo Paul kaa dɛ nkorɔfo bɛtan nokwar Christianfo nyina hɔn enyi. (2 Tim. 3:12) Sɛ nkorɔfo tan hɛn enyi a, ɔyɛ yaw. Ntsi sɛ yɛannhwɛ no yie a, yɛbɛyɛ araa dɛ yɛbɛma obiara ehu dɛ hɛn asɛm yɛ dɛw anaa mpo dɛ yɛbɛka dɛ mbra no nye dɛm nkorɔfo no ndzi. Obowie ase no, nna yɛasɛɛ hɛn mber pii ma asɛmpaka edwuma a ohia paa a Jehovah dze ahyɛ hɛn nsa no, yeepia ato nkyɛn.—Rom. 12:19-21.

10 Ma yɛnhwɛ asɛm bi a ɔtoo Abraham ne ba Isaac. Isaac bɛyɛɛ sikanyi no, Philistiafo no enyi beer no. Dɛm ntsi mbura a ne papa Abraham no nkowaa tutui no nyina, Philistiafo no dze dɛtse sisiiw no. (Gen. 26:12-18) Nna Isaac nye n’ebusua nye n’anyɛmbowa nyina behia nsu, ntsi Isaac tui fii hɔ kotutuu mbura no bi wɔ beebi fofor. Beebi a ɔkɔree no so, Philistiafo no bɛkaa dɛ mbura no yɛ hɔn dze a. Wɔnye Isaac enndzi no yie koraa. Nna nkyɛ iyi botum ama n’abaw mu ebu. Naaso Isaac amma kwan ma no nguanhwɛfo no nye Philistiafo no ammper mbura no ho, mbom ɔyɛɛ dza ɔdze asomdwee bɛba. Ɔsanee tui fii hɔ kɔr beebi fofor na Jehovah hyiraa no. (Gen. 26:19-25) Hɛn so, sɛ nkorɔfo nye hɛn enndzi no yie a, yebotum esuasua Isaac na yɛahwehwɛ asomdwee na yeenya hɔn ho abotar.
