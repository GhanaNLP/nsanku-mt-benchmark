Jonathan ne papa Saul ammfa noho annto Jehovah do, ntsi n’enyi beer David. Naaso Jonathan dze ɔannye dɛm, mbom ɔtaa David n’ekyir
(Hwɛ nkyekyɛmu 4, 6)
<<<P>>>
dwen hɔn nyɛnko ho. Ntsi hɔnankasa mpo wɔnngye hɔnho nndzi. Dza Satan pɛ nye dɛ yebosuasua dɛm nkorɔfo yi. Onyim dɛ sɛ yesuasua hɔn a, hɛn so yɛbɛkasa etsia hɛn nuanom nye Jehovah n’ahyehyɛdze no, na obotum mpo ama yɛafa hɔn ho tan.—Rom. 16:17.
<<<P>>>
⁴ Ma yɛmfa Ɔhen Saul na David hɔn asɛm no nyɛ dza yɛreka yi ho mfatoho. Ahyɛse no, nna Ɔhen Saul pɛ David n’asɛm paa, osiandɛ nna ɔyɛ ɔkofo a ɔwɔ akokodur. (1 Sam. 18:5) Naaso odur beebi no, nna Saul kyir David kɔkɔɔkɔ. Ɔbaa no dɛn? Aber a Saul tsee dɛ amamfo no robɔ gye David do no, ɔyɛɛ no yaw paa. Dɛm ntsi Saul tuu n’enyi sii David do na ɔyɛɛ araa dɛ oboku no. (1 Sam. 18:9-11) Sɛ Saul tumii kum David a, nkyɛ Satan n’enyi bɛgye paa. Ebɛnadze ntsi na yɛreka dɛm? Siantsir no nye dɛ, nna Jehovah ama wɔasera David dɛ ɔno na ɔbɛyɛ Israelhen daakye. (1 Sam. 16:13) Ntsi sɛ David ammbɛyɛ ɔhen a nkyɛ Satan n’enyi bɛgye paa. Ɔbɛyɛ dɛ nna ɔyɛ Satan dɛw paa dɛ ahoɔyaw ntsi Saul n’enyi aber rohwehwɛ obi a Jehovah nankasa eyi no dɛ ɔbɛyɛ ɔhen no eku no.
<<<P>>>
⁵ Satan nyim paa dɛ koryɛ a ɔwɔ hɛn ntamu no na ɔma Jehovah hyira hɛn na ɔboa hɛn. (Ndw. 133:1) Ntsi ɔpɛ dɛ ahoɔyaw dzidzi hɛn mu ma hɛn enyi ber hɛn nuanom tse dɛ mbrɛ Saul n’enyi beer David no, mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a yɛnye hɔn ntamu bɛsɛɛ. Onyim dɛ sɛ ɔba no dɛm a, ɔbɛyɛ dzen paa dɛ yɛbɔdɔ hɔn. (Mark 12:30, 31) Sɛ hɛn enyi ber hɛn nuanom a ehwɛ a Jehovah bɛgye hɛn som ato mu anaa? Osuanyi James kasae tsiaa ahoɔyaw a ɔtse dɛm no dzendzenndzen.—Kenkan James 3:14-16.
