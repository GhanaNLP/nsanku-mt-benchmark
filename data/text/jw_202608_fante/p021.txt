ɔbɛyɛ dɛm ‘agyenkwa a ɔwɔ tum’ no. Jewfo a nna wɔwɔ Zechariah n’aber do no, nna wɔrohwɛ Messiah a wɔahyɛ no ho bɔ no kwan dada. Nna wonyim dɛ ɔbɛyɛ Abraham n’asenyi, na obefi ɔhen David n’ebusua mu. (Gen. 22:17, 18; Matt. 22:42) Sɛ dɛm dze a, nna ɔno ebɛnadze ntsi na Jehovah maa Zechariah kaa dɛm agyenkwa yi ne mbae ho asɛm? Siantsir nye dɛ nna Messiah no ne mbae no abɛn paa. Nna wɔaka bɛyɛ abosoom esia ma akataasia bi a wɔfrɛ no Mary awo Jesus. Na dɛ mbrɛ ɔbɔfo Gabriel kaa no ho asɛm no, nna wɔbɛfrɛ no “Nyankopɔn ne Ba.” Nna ɔbɛtsena “n’egya David n’ahengua” do edzi hen. (Luke 1:30-35) Wonumaa Jesus no, nna oedzi mfe 30 na dɛm aber no na ɔbɛyɛɛ agyenkwa a ɔwɔ tum no. Dɛm aber no ara so na Jehovah dze no sunsum krɔnkrɔn no seraa no ma ɔbɛyɛɛ Messiah a nna wɔahyɛ no ho bɔ no. (Matt. 3:16, 17) Nna iyi yɛ enyigyesɛm ankasa.

3 Nna ohia dɛ wɔgye Jewfo a wɔwɔ hɔ dɛm aber no. Naaso nna woana ne nsamu anaa ebɛnadze mu na ɔsɛ dɛ wɔgye hɔn fi? Zechariah ma yenya dɛm asɛmbisa yi ho mbuae. Ɔkaa dɛ: “Ɔbɛgye hɛn efi hɛn atamfo nye hɔn a wokyir hɛn no nyina nsamu.” (Luke 1:71) Dɛm aber no, nna Rome aban no na odzi Jewfo no hɔn do tum, na nna wɔretseetsee Jewfo no paa. Ntsi nna Zechariah repɛ akyerɛ dɛ Jesus bɛgye Jewfo no efi hɔn nsamu anaa? Oho. Mbom no, nna ɔbɛgye Jewfo no efi hɔn tamfo kɛse Satan ne nsamu osiandɛ amandze beberebe a nna woruhu no nyina, nna ɔno na ɔhyɛ ekyir. (Heb. 2:14, 15) Satan kor yi ara so na ɔma adasamba nyina, a nna Jewfo no so ka ho no yɛ bɔn na wowuwu no. Iyi da nkyɛn a, nna Jewfo no nntum nndzi Moses Mbra no do pɛpɛɛpɛr, dɛm ntsi nna wɔabɛhyɛ ndom ase ma ɔno ntsi ohia dɛ wɔgye

3. Nna ebɛnadze ntsi na ɔsɛ dɛ wɔgye Jewfo no?
<<<P>>>
hɔn. (Rom. 3:20; Gal. 3:10, 13) Jehovah nam Zechariah do hyɛɛ hɔn bɔ dɛ “ɔdze hɔn bɔn bɛkyɛ hɔn na ɔaagye hɔn nkwa.”—Luke 1:77.
<<<P>>>
4 Ndɛ so, ebɛn atamfo na ohia dɛ wɔgye hɛn fi hɔn nsamu? Tse dɛ Jewfo a nna wɔwɔ Jesus n’aber do no, hɛnara so yɛyɛ bɔn na yewu. (Rom. 5:12) Ɔno da nkyɛn a, Satan nye n’esunsum fi no so retseetsee hɛn paa na wɔama yɛreberɛ. Wɔreyɛɛ ara dɛ yɛbɛyɛ Jehovah do asoɔdzen na yeegyaa Jehovah som. Hɔnara so na wɔhyɛ nyimpa binom nye ahyɛhyɛdze binom hɔn ekyir ma wɔtan hɛn enyi no. Hɔm mma yɛnka, yehia ɔgyefo bi a ɔbɛgye hɛn efi dɛm atamfo yi nyina hɔn nsamu.
<<<P>>>
5 Ma yɛnhwɛ dza hɛn agyenkwa Jesus ayɛ mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a ɔbɛgye hɛn efi bɔn mu. Aber a nna Jesus reyɛ no som edwuma no, ɔkyerɛɛ n’esuafo no kwan pa a ɔsɛ dɛ wɔfa do som Jehovah. Ɔno ekyir no, ɔdze no nkwa too hɔ maa hɛn “ama obiara a ɔbɛgye no edzi no aannsɛɛ, na mbom oeenya onnyiewiei nkwa.” (John 3:16; Matt. 20:28) Ɔmmfa ho dɛ yɛbɔ mbɔdzen som Jehovah dɛ mbrɛ Jesus kyerɛkyerɛɛ hɛn no, bɔn da ho ara wɔ hɛnho. Naaso sɛ yɛfom na yɛserɛ Jehovah dɛ ɔmfa nkyɛ hɛn a, osian werdam afɔrbɔ no ntsi, ɔdze bɛkyɛ hɛn, na ɔagye hɛn som ato mu. (1 John 2:1, 2) Dɛm ndzɛmba yi a hɛn agyenkwa Jesus ayɛ ama hɛn no ma yetum nya bɔnfakyɛ na obotum so ama yeenya onnyiewiei nkwa daakye.
