SATAN KEKAA ATORSƐM FAA JEHOVAH NYE JESUS HO
<<<P>>>
3 Jehovah bɔɔ Adam na Eve no, wɔannkyɛr koraa na Satan maa wɔyɛɛ Jehovah do asoɔdzen. Satan daadaa Eve dɛ no Bɔfo no mmpɛ n’asɛm na ɔnndwen no ho, na Eve so gyee dzii. Satan bisaa Eve dɛ: “Aso ampaara dɛ Nyankopɔn ase hom dɛ, Mma hom nndzi ture yi mu ndua nyinara bi?” (Gen. 3:1-5) Satan daadaa Eve dɛ ɔnnyɛ ndzɛmba a ɔnye no kun hia nyina na Jehovah dze ama hɔn no. Awerɛhosɛm nye dɛ Eve tsiee Jehovah ne tamfo Satan, na ekyir yi Adam so kɛkaa hɔn ho. Nna Jehovah dze dza wohia nyina ama hɔn, naaso woenntsie no.—Gen. 3:6.
<<<P>>>
4 Ofitsi Adam na Eve hɔn aber do dze besi ndɛ, Satan akɔ do akeka atorsɛm afa Jehovah ho. Sɛ yɛbɛyɛ mfatoho a, nna Satan pɛ dɛ Job nya adwen dɛ Jehovah nndɔ no na ɔsom a ɔrosom no no nyina yɛ kwa. Ntsi ɔmaa Job n’anyɛnkofo bi kekaa atorsɛm faa Jehovah ho kyerɛɛ Job. (Job 22:1-3) Nna Abɔnsam n’adwen yɛ no dɛ sɛ ɔsɔ Job hwɛ a, obegyaa Jehovah som, ntsi ɔmaa Job berɛe papaapa. Naaso Abɔnsam ne nsa sii famu, osiandɛ Job kɔr do dzii Jehovah nokwar na hɔn anyɛnkofa no mu yɛɛ dzen.—Job 27:1, 5.
<<<P>>>
5 Satan amma annso hɔ ara. Aber a Jesus so baa asaase yi do no, ɔdze n’asɛm kɔr no do, na ɔyɛɛ araa dɛ ɔremma nkorɔfo nndzi n’ekyir. Adze kor a ɔyɛɛ nye dɛ ɔmaa nyamesom mpanyimfo na afofor a wɔmmpɛ Jesus n’asɛm no kekaa atorsɛm faa no ho. Owiee ase no, nkorɔfo piinara gyee dzii dɛ Jesus yɛ ɔdzebɔnyɛnyi na wɔmaa wokum no.—Kenkan Matthew 11:19; 27:27-31; Mark 14:55, 56; John 8:41-45.
<<<P>>>
6 Jesus wui n’ekyir no, Satan tuu n’enyi sii n’ekyirdzifo no do na ɔyɛɛ dza obotum biara mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a wobegyaa Jesus ho asɛmpa a nna wɔreka no. Nna ɔmmpɛ dɛ nkorɔfo gye dzi dɛ Nyankopɔn enyan Jesus, ntsi opiapiaa nyimpa binom ma wodzii akɔhwi kaa dɛ Jesus n’esuafo no ebowia no fun no, na ɔmaa dɛm asɛm yi tahyee. (Matt. 28:11-15) Ɔno ekyir no, Satan maa nyamesom mpanyimfo nye etumfo no taan Jesus n’ekyirdzifo no hɔn enyi dzendzenndzen. Nna n’adwen ara nye dɛ sɛ ɔyɛ dɛm a, wɔbɔbɔ hu. Nna Satan pɛ dɛ otu hɔn akoma na ɔma wonya adwen dɛ edwuma a hɔn Wura no dze ahyɛ hɔn nsa no yɛ dzen dodow na wogyaa yɛ.—Andw. 7:57–8:1; 12:1-3; 28:22.
<<<P>>>
7 Ɔwɔ mu, aber a wɔtaa Jesus n’ekyirdzifo no, hɔn mu binom bɔɔ hu, na iyi maa wogyaa asɛmpa no ka. Naaso hɔn mu beberee so annka hɔn ano annto mu, mbom wɔkɔr do ara kaa Jesus ho asɛmpa no. Nna wonyim paa dɛ ɔtaa beberebe a wɔdze reba hɔn do no nyina, ɔyɛ Satan na n’esunsum fi no na wɔhyɛ ekyir. (Andw. 4:18-20; 1 Pet. 5:8) Ndɛ so ɛ? Ebɛnadze na Satan reyɛ mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a obobu hɛn abaw mu ma yeegyaa Jehovah som?
