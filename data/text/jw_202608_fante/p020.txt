DZA YEBOSUA
Nhyehyɛɛ a Jehovah ayɛ dze agye hɛn nkwa, hokwan a yeenya dze rosom no, nye edwuma a ɔdze ahyɛ hɛn nsa dɛ yɛnka asɛmpa no, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛkyerɛ ho enyisɔ.
<<<P>>>
Ma ‘Hokwan A Enya Dze Rosom Nyankopɔn’ No Nsom Wo Bo
<<<P>>>
‘Ɔama nkwagye ambɛn do dɛ ɔbɛma yeenya hokwan dze asom no a yerunnsuro hwee.’—LUKE 1:69, 74.
<<<P>>>
ABER a nna ɔsɔfo Zechariah abɔ akɔkora, na ne yer Elizabeth so abɔ aberwa no, Jehovah yɛɛ anwanwadze bi. Ɔmaa wɔwoo ba banyin, na wɔtoo no dzin John. Aber a hɔn apaamu hɔfo nye hɔn ebusuafo tsee asɛm yi no, ɔyɛɛ hɔn nwanwa. Ntsi wobisaa dɛ: “Ebɛnadze na abofra yi benyin abɛyɛ?” (Luke 1:57-66) Jehovah nam Zechariah do yii hɔn ano. Ɔkyerɛɛ mu kyerɛɛ hɔn dɛ, ɔrennkyɛr Messiah no a wɔdze mfe pii ahwɛ no kwan no bɛba, na John bɔboa nkorɔfo ma woenu hɔnho, ama sɛ Messiah no ba a woekedzi n’ekyir. (Amos 3:7; Luke 1:67-79) Naaso, ɔnnyɛ dza ne ba John bɛyɛ nkotsee na ɔkaa ho asɛm.* Asɛm a Zechariah kae no dze, nna ɔyɛ nkɔnhyɛ, na wɔ adzesua yi mu no, yebohu dɛ dɛm nkɔnhyɛ no fa hɛnara so hɛnho. Zechariah n’asɛm no botum aboa hɛn ma yeehu ndzɛmba ebiasa bi a ɔsɛ dɛ yɛkyerɛ ho enyisɔ. Ɔno nye: (1) nhyehyɛɛ a ɔayɛ dze agye hɛn nkwa, (2) hokwan a yeenya dze rosom Jehovah, na (3) asɛmpaka edwuma a wɔdze ahyɛ hɛn nsa no.
