Suasua Philemon na yɛ wo nuanom ahɔho na ɔbɔboa ma asomdwee atsena asafo no mu
<<<P>>>
(Hwɛ nkyekyɛmu 15)
<<<P>>>
Naaso nna Paul gye dzi paa dɛ Philemon dze bɛkyɛ no. (Philem. 17, 21) Nna Paul nyim mbrɛ Philemon si tse ntsi nna onyim dɛ ɔrennyɛ dzen mma no koraa dɛ ɔdze Onesimus no bɔn bɛkyɛ no. Nna Philemon tse dɛn?
<<<P>>>
13 Nna Philemon dɔ no nuanom. (Philem. 5) Nna Philemon ne dzin n’asekyerɛ nye “Ɔdɔ,” na nokwasɛm nye dɛ nna ne dzin nye no honam sɛ. Nna hɔn a wɔwɔ asafo no mu no nyim dɛ Philemon dɔ hɔn paa. Nna dɛm ɔdɔ yi ara so na ɔbɛma ɔdze Onesimus no bɔn akyɛ no. Krataa a ɔsomafo Paul kyerɛwee kɛmaa Colossae asafo a nna Philemon wɔ mu no, ɔkaa dɛ “ɔdɔ tum ka nyimpa bɔ mu ma ɔyɛ yie.” (Kenkan Colossaefo 3:14.) Iyi ma yehu dɛ suban a ohia ama yeetum nye enuanom atsena asomdwee mu nye ɔdɔ. Sɛ yɛdɔ afofor a, ɔrennyɛ dzen koraa dɛ yɛdze hɔn mfom bɛkyɛ hɔn na yɛnye hɔn edzi no yie. (1 Cor. 13:4-7) Hom mma yɛnhwɛ mbrɛ ɔdɔ a nna Philemon wɔ no sii boaa no ma ɔdze Onesimus no bɔn kyɛɛ no.
<<<P>>>
14 Nna Philemon pɛ ahɔhoyɛ, ntsi nna ɔnnyɛ dzen koraa dɛ ɔbɛda ɔdɔ edzi akyerɛ afofor. Nna ɔma wɔyɛ asafo nhyiamu mpo wɔ ne fie. (Philem. 2) Paul kaa kyerɛɛ Philemon dɛ onsiesie beebi a ɔba a ɔbɛtsena mma no. Iyi so ma yehu dɛ nna ɔpɛ ahɔhoyɛ. (Philem. 22) Osiandɛ nna Philemon pɛ ahɔhoyɛ ntsi, ɔmaa nkorɔfo akoma tɔɔ hɔn yamu, na ɔmaa ɔyɛɛ mberɛw dɛ wɔbɛbɛn no.—Philem. 7.
<<<P>>>
15 Sɛ yesuasua ahɔhoyɛ su a Philemon daa no edzi no a, obesi dɛn aboa hɛn ma yɛadɔ afofor paa? Sɛ yɛda ahɔhoyɛ edzi kyerɛ hɛn nuanom a, ɔbɛma yeehu hɔn yie. Enuanom binom wɔ hɔ a, yɛnnhyɛ da ara mmbɛn hɔn dɛm, binom so wɔ hɔ a, ɔyɛ hɛn dɛ yerunntum mmfa hɔn nyɛnko. Naaso sɛ yɛnye dɛm enuanom yi nyina bɔ a, ɔbɛma yeehu dɛ wɔwɔ suban ahorow a ɔyɛ fɛw. Dɛm suban ahorow yi so na ɔma asafo no mu yɛ dɛw. Sɛ yehu ebusua a wofi mu, mbrɛ wosii baa asafo no mu nye dza wɔafa mu wɔ asetsena mu a, ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yɛadɔ hɔn na yeebu hɔn paa. Ɔbɛma yeehu siantsir a Jehovah twee hɔn baa n’ahyehyɛdze no mu. (John 6:44) Asɛm no nye dɛ, sɛ yɛda ahɔhoyɛ edzi kyerɛ hɛn nuanom a, ɔma yɛdɔ hɔn paa, yenya hɔn ho adwen pa na sɛ wɔfom hɛn so a, yɛdze kyɛ hɔn.
