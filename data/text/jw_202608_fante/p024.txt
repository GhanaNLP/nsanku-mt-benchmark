13 Ɔwɔ mu dɛ John maa nkorɔfo hun dɛ Je-sus nye Messiah no dze, naaso nna wobehia nkorɔfo beberee ma woedzi Messiah no ho dase. Ebɛnadze ntsi na yɛreka dɛm? Siantsir no nye dɛ, Zechariah kaa dɛ Messiah no a ɔre-ba no bɛyɛ “adzekyee ne kan,” kyerɛ dɛ, ɔdze nsesa bɛba nyimpa pii hɔn abrabɔ mu! Dɛm enyigyesɛm yi dze, nna ɔsɛ dɛ obiara tse bi. Naaso mbrɛ ɔtse nye dɛ, nna John nanko-tsee runntum nnka ho asɛm nnkyerɛ nyimpa nyina. Ɔnodze, yembisa dɛ, ‘Sɛ yeyi John si nkyɛn a, nna woananom bio na wɔbɔbɔ dɛm asɛmpa yi ho dawur akyerɛ nkorɔfo?
<<<P>>>
14 Jesus kaa asɛmpa no kyerɛɛ nkorɔfo be-beree. Nna Zechariah ahyɛ nkɔm dɛ Messiah no ‘bɛma hɔn a wɔtsena sum nye owu no sunsu-ma mu no enya kan.’ Jesus maa dɛm nkɔnhyɛ yi baa mu. Nna Jesus wɔ ayamuhyehye paa ma hɔn a wommbu hɔn bi na wɔyɛ hɔn adze gyangyan no. Ɔkaa ‘Jehovah n’enyisɔ afe’ ho asɛm kyerɛɛ hɔn. Iyi kyekyee hɔn werɛ, na nna ɔtse dɛ mbrɛ ɔama woehu kan. (Ken-kan Isaiah 61:1, 2; Luke 4:16-21) Ɔyeer noho bɔɔ asɛmpa no ho dawur, na ɔkaa dɛ: “Ɔsor Ahendzi no abɛn.” Naaso, nna Jesus nyim dɛ wobehia adawurbɔfo beberee ma wɔayɛ dɛm edwuma yi. Dɛm ntsi ɔtsetsee n’esuafo no mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a hɔnara so botum aka dɛm asɛm-pa yi bi.—Matt. 4:16, 17; 10:7; 28:19, 20.
