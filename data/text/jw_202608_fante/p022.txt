Jehovah nam Jesus Christ do agye hɛn nkwa (Hwɛ nkyekyɛmu 6)
<<<P>>>
(Luke 1:32, 33) Jesus a ɔyɛ Nyankopɔn n’Ahendzi no do Hen na 144,000 nye abɔfo a wɔwɔ sor no bɛsɛɛ Nyankopɔn n’atamfo a wɔwɔ asaase yi do no nyina wɔ Amagedon ko no mu. Afei wɔ Mfrenhyia Apem Tumdzi no mu no, Jesus benyan ewufo, na nkakrankakra no, ɔnye 144,000 no bɔboa adasamba ma wɔabɛyɛ nyimpa a bɔn biara nnyi hɔnho. (John 5:28; Nyi. 22:1, 2) Ɔntɔ araa dɛ Mfrenhyia Apem no mbra n’ewiei no, nna woeyi bɔn na owu a Adam kɛfae bae no efi hɔ. (1 Cor. 15:26, 55-57) Ɔno ekyir no, wɔbɛsɛɛ Satan na n’esunsum fi no, nye nyimpa biara a ɔbɔkɔ akɛtaa hɔn ekyir wɔ nsɔhwɛ a otwa tun no mu no korakora. (Nyi. 20:7-10) Afei, hɔn a wɔdze nokwardzi som Jehovah no dze enyigye bɔsom no. Wɔrennyɛ bɔn, worunnwu, na ɔtamfo biara so renntseetsee hɔn bio. Yebeyi hɛn Nyankopɔn no ayɛw afebɔɔ, na yɛakɔ do ara ada no ase wɔ “n’akyɛdze a ɔyɛ nwanwa” a ɔdze ama hɛn no ho. Dɛm akyɛdze no mu kor nye nkwa a ɔnam Christ Jesus do ama yeenya no.—2 Cor. 9:15.

HOKWAN A YEENYA DZE ROSOM JEHOVAH
<<<P>>>
7 Kenkan Luke 1:74, 75. Wɛrdam afɔrbɔ no yɛ akyɛdze a ofi Jehovah hɔ na ɔma yenya hokwan soronko bi. Zechariah kaa iyi ho asɛm dɛ ‘ɔma yenya hokwan dze som Jehovah a yennsuro hwee.’ Sɛ yɛkɔ asɛnka, yɛbɔ mpaa, yesua Bible no, na yɛkɔ asafo nhyiamu nye ehyiadzi akɛse na nkakraba a, nna ‘yɛrosom’ Jehovah. Ndwuma afofor bi so wɔ hɔ a yɛyɛ a ɔkyerɛ dɛ yɛrosom Jehovah. Bi nye dɛ yɛbɔkɔ akɛyɛ edwuma wɔ Bethel, yɛbɔboa ma woesisi anaa woesiesie Kingdom Halls nye Ehyiadzi Asa ahorow.
