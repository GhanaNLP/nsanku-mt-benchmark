Suasua Paul na nya wo nuanom Christianfo ho adwen pa na ka hɔn ho nsɛmpa so
(Hwɛ nkyekyɛmu 8)
<<<P>>>
pa na wɔnye hɔn edzi no yie. Dɛmara na sɛ yɛka hɔn ho nsɛmbɔn so a, ɔremma wommbu hɔn bi na wɔnye hɔn rinndzi no yie. Naaso sɛ yebotum aka afofor ho nsɛmpa a, ɔsɛ dɛ yenya hɔn ho adwen pa. Ebɛnadze na ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yeetum ayɛ dɛm? Ma yɛnhwɛ biribi a nna Paul taa yɛ.
<<<P>>>
8 Nna Paul taa da Jehovah ase wɔ suban pa a nna Philemon wɔ no ho. (Philem. 4, 5) Afei so, nna ɔtaa da Jehovah ase wɔ suban pa a afofor so wɔ no ho. (1 Cor. 1:4-7; Phil. 1:3-5; Col. 1:3, 4; 1 Thess. 1:2, 3) Nna Paul hu mfom aho-row a no nuanom no dzi no paa, naaso ɔnnyɛ ɔno na ɔdze n’adwen sii do. Mbom suban pa a nna wowɔ no na ɔdze n’adwen sii do, na nna ɔtaa da Jehovah ase dɛ ɔama oenya anyɛnko-fo a wɔtse dɛm. Sɛ ɔyɛ dzen ma wo dɛ ebɛka afofor ho asɛmpa a, bɔ mbɔdzen yɛ iyi: Fa w’a-dwen si ndzɛmba pa a afofor yɛ no do, na fi w’akoma mu bɔ mpaa ma hɔn na serɛ Jehovah dɛ onhyira hɔn. Sɛ eyɛ dɛm a, ɔrennyɛ dzen mma wo koraa dɛ ebɛka hɔn ho asɛmpa.

9. Ebɛnadze ntsi na yebotum aka dɛ Paul yɛɛ adze paa dɛ ɔkyerɛɛw nsɛm a ɔhyɛ nkuran kɛmaa Philemon?
<<<P>>>
wo do anaa aberɛ ma ɔno ntsi etutu obi no ho ama no pɛn anaa? Sɛ yɛafa atseetsee mu araa nko a, ɔrennyɛ dzen dɛ yɛdze hɛn ebufuw bɔkɔ afofor do. Sɛ ayɛ biribi a ɔtse dɛm pɛn a, nna ɔno ma yɛnhwɛ Paul n’asɛm no. Aber a nna ɔrekyerɛw krataa akɛma Philemon no, nna ebɛn nsɛm na woesisi wɔ n’abrabɔ mu? Mfe ka-kraabi a nna etwa mu no, nyimpadɔm bi boor Paul basabasa na asogyafo so kyeer no aber a nna ɔnnyɛɛ bɔn biara. Ɔno ekyir no, wɔdze no kɔtoo efiadze wɔ Jerusalem na Caesarea. Afei, wɔnye no rokɔ Rome no, po do hɛn a nna wɔ-tse mu no bobɔe, na okodur hɔ no so, Rome aban no kaa no hyɛɛ ne fie. Aber a nna ɔrekye-rɛw Philemon krataa no, nna asogyafo rowɛn no wɔ ne fie, ntsi nna ɔtse dɛ mbrɛ ‘ɔda efia-dze.’ (Philem. 1) Naaso ɔhaw beberebe a Paul faa mu no maa ɔkyerɛɛw nsɛm a ɔyɛ yaw kɛ-maa Philemon anaa? Oho. Mbom nsɛm a Paul kyerɛwee kɛmaa Philemon no, nna ɔhyɛ nku-ran na nna enyidzi so wɔ mu. Esuadze a ɔwɔ asɛm yi mu no nye dɛn? Ɔno nye dɛ, sɛ yɛabe-rɛ, ndzɛmba ahyɛ hɛn do anaa yɛrefa ɔhaw bi mu mpo a, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛbɔ mbɔdzen nye hɛn nua-nom kasa yie na yɛnye hɔn dzi no yie. Sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm a, yɛbɛma asomdwee atsena asafo no mu. Na sɛ asomdwee so wɔ yɛnye hɛn nuanom nta-mu a, ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yeetum emia hɛn enyi afa ɔhaw biara a yerihyia no mu.
