Onesimus n’aso, ntsi nna ɔnye no bɛfa mbra kwan do anaa? Na enuanom a wɔwɔ asafo no mu so ɛ, wɔbɛyɛ hɔn adze dɛn? Enuanom a wɔwɔ nkowaa nye enuanom a wɔyɛ nkowaa no so ɛ, ɔdze mpaapaamu bɛba hɔn mu anaa?’ Nna Paul nyim dɛ dɛm asɛm yi botum asɛɛ asomdwee a ɔwɔ asafo no mu no. Ntsi ɔkye-rɛɛw krataa maa Tychicus dɛ ɔmfa nkɛma Phi-lemon. (Eph. 6:21, 22; Col. 4:7-9; hwɛ Colos-saefo 4:7 no ho nkyerɛkyerɛmu.) Dɛm aber no ara so, ɔkyerɛɛw krataa kɛmaa enuanom a nna wɔwɔ Colossae asafo no mu no. Adzesua yi mu no, yebosusu nkrataa a Paul kyerɛwee kɛmaa Philemon nye Colossae asafo no, na yɛahwɛ dza yebotum esua efi dza Paul, Onesimus nye Philemon yɛe no mu.

4 Ebɛnadze na yebotum esua efi mbrɛ Paul sii yɛɛ n’adze no mu? Kwan a Paul faa do nye afofor dzii no boae ma asomdwee tsenaa asafo no mu. Ntsi sɛ yesuasua no a, yɛbɛma asom-dwee atsena asafo no mu.
<<<P>>>
5 Paul hun Philemon dɛ ne nyɛnko. (Philem. 17) Paul ennya adwen dɛ ɔwɔ tum kyɛn Phile-mon, ntsi dza ɔbɛka akyerɛ no biara ɔsɛ dɛ otsie no. (2 Cor. 1:24) Paul buu Philemon dɛ ne nyɛnko a ɔnye no yɛ edwuma, na ɔkaa anyɛnkofa a nna ɔda ɔnye no ntamu no mpo ho asɛm. Adze a Paul yɛe yi, nna ɔbɔboa Phile-mon paa ma oehu mbrɛ ɔsɛ dɛ ɔnye Onesi-mus a nna sesei ɔabɛyɛ no nua Christiannyi no tsena. Hɛn nyina so botum esuasua Paul, nka-nka asafo mu mpanyimfo. Sɛ asafo mu mpa-nyimfo fa enuanom a wɔwɔ asafo no mu no nyɛnko a, ɔboa ma asomdwee tsena asafo no mu. Osiandɛ wɔdɔ Jehovah no nguan no ntsi ‘wɔnnhyɛ hɔn do.’ (1 Pet. 5:1-3) Mbom wonya mber ma hɔn, wotsie hɔn na wɔboa hɔn aber a hɔnho akyer hɔn no. Sɛ mpanyimfo no suasua Paul na wɔfa enuanom no nyɛnko a, enuanom no so bosuasua hɔn na wɔbɔboa ma asomdwee atsena asafo no mu.
<<<P>>>
6 Paul kamfoo Philemon. (Philem. 4, 5, 7) Hɛn mu biara hia nkamfo. Ebɛnadze ntsi a? Siantsir kor nye dɛ, hɛn mu piinara wɔ hɔ a, ɔyɛ a yɛtse nka dɛ mfaso biara nnyi hɛn do. Bi a ɔyɛ hɛn dɛ hɛnho nnyɛ fɛw, nkorɔfo mmpɛ hɛn asɛm, anaa ɔyɛ hɛn dɛ dwumadzi bi a ɔhyɛ hɛn nsa wɔ asafo no mu a hɛn enyi gye ho paa no befi hɛn nsa. Yɛyɛ nyimpa yi ara dze, ɔwɔ mu ara a yɛbɔdwen ndzɛmba a ɔtsetse dɛm ho. Naaso sɛ yɛannhwɛ no yie a, iyi botum egya hɛn adwendwen anaa obotum ama asɛm bi a ɔnnyɛ asɛm biara no ahyɛ hɛn ebufuw. Obo-tum mpo ama yɛatwe nsɛm mu anaa yeebu hɛnho dɛ yɛkyɛn afofor. Iyinom nyina botum asɛɛ asomdwee a ɔwɔ yɛnye hɛn nuanom nta-mu no. Naaso sɛ etaa kamfo afofor a, ɔbɛma wɔatse nka dɛ mfaso wɔ hɔn do. (Mbɛ. 12:25) Iyi botum aka hɔn akoma ato hɔn yamu na ɔbɛ-ma woehu dɛ yɛdɔ hɔn na wɔsom bo ma hɛn. Emmpɛ dɛ ebɛma afofor atse nka dɛm anaa? Sɛ dɛm dze a, nna ɔno hokwan biara a ibenya no, fi w’akoma mu kamfo afofor. Ɔnnyɛ aber a obi etum ayɛ biribi no nkotsee na ɔsɛ dɛ yɛ-kamfo no, mbom sɛ yehu dɛ obi robɔ no ho mbɔdzen dɛ ɔbɛyɛ biribi so a, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛkamfo. Asɛm no ne tsentsen na ne tsiatsia ara nye dɛ, sɛ ihu biribi papabi wɔ wo nua ho a, ka kye-rɛ no.
