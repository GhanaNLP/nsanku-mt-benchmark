NDWOM 129
Yebemia Hɛn Enyi Daa
<<<P>>>
DZA YEBOSUA
Ma ɔntsena w’adwen mu aber nyina dɛ Satan nye n’esunsum fi no na enye hɔn roko na nnyɛ wo nua-nom Christianfo bi a.
<<<P>>>
JEHOVAH n’asomfo nndzi ako. (Isa. 2:4; John 18:36) Naaso Bible no se ɔsɛ dɛ yɛko tsia Satan nye n’esunsum fi no. (Ken-kan Ephesusfo 6:11, 12.) Hɛn atamfo a yɛnye hɔn roko no wɔ tum kyɛn hɛn koraa. Ntsi yebesi dɛn nye hɔn ako na yeedzi hɔn do konyim? Jehovah ara na obotum aboa hɛn ma yeedzi hɔn do konyim, ntsi ɔsɛ dɛ yɛdze hɛnho to no do. Naaso ɔsɛ dɛ yehu ndzɛmba ahorow bi a Satan na n’esunsum fi no yɛ mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a yɛnye Jehovah nye hɛn nuanom Christianfo nta-mu bɛsɛɛ.
<<<P>>>
2 Adzesua a odzi iyi enyim mu no, yehun atorsɛm bi a Abɔn-sam keka fa Jehovah Nyankopɔn na Jesus Christ ho mbrɛ ɔbɛ-yɛ a yɛrenngye hɔn nndzi. Adzesua yi mu so, yɛbɔhwɛ akwan afofor ebien a Satan fa do ko tsia hɛn. Ɔno nye dɛ ɔbɔ mbɔ-dzen dɛ ɔbɛsɛɛ koryɛ a ɔwɔ yɛnye hɛn nuanom ntamu no. Afei so, ɔma afofor soɛr tsia hɛn ama yeabɔ hu anaa yeeyi hɛn adwen efi edwuma a Jehovah dze ahyɛ hɛn nsa dɛ yɛnyɛ no do, na nkakrankakra yeegyaa yɛ.

SATAN PƐ DƐ ƆSƐƐ KORYƐ A ƆWƆ HƐN NTAMU NO
<<<P>>>
3 Satan reyɛ biara dɛ ɔdze mpaapaamu bɛba yɛnye hɛn nua-nom ntamu. Siantsir a ɔyɛ dɛm nye dɛ onyim dɛ sɛ asomdwee nnyi hɛn ntamu a, yɛronnko nntsia no mbom yɛbɔko etsia hɛnankasa hɛnho. Osi dɛn dze mpaapaamu ba hɛn mu? Nyim-pa a wɔwɔ Satan no wiadze no mu no, hɔn ano yɛ yaw na wɔnn-
<<<P>>>
1. Woananom na ɔsɛ dɛ hɛn mu biara ko tsia hɔn? (Ephesusfo 6:11, 12)
2. Ebɛnadze na yebosusu ho wɔ adzesua yi mu?
3. Ebɛn kwan na Satan fa do dze mpaapaamu ba hɛn ntamu?
