16 Zechariah no nkɔnhyɛ a ɔwɔ Luke 1:68-79 no ama yeehu ndzɛmba ebiasa bi a onnyi dɛ yɛtoto no adze koraa. Ɔno nye nhyehyɛɛ a Je-hovah ayɛ a ɔdze bɛgye hɛn nkwa, hokwan a yeenya dze rosom no a yennsuro hwee nye asɛmpaka edwuma a ɔdze ahyɛ hɛn nsa dɛ yɛn-yɛ no. Ntsi nhyehyɛɛ a Jehovah ayɛ dze agye hɛn nkwa no agye ato mu anaa? Nna ɔno nya Christ no werdam afɔrbɔ no mu gyedzi na hyi-ra woho do ma Jehovah. W’enyi sɔ hokwan a enya dze rosom Jehovah no anaa? Nna ɔno yer woho bɔ ‘Jehovah n’enyisɔ afe’ no ho dawur kyerɛ nkorɔfo ma wonhu dɛ Jehovah “n’awerdzi da” no reba. Ka kyerɛ hɔn dɛ ɔre-nnkyɛr koraa Nyankopɔn n’Ahendzi no bɛsɛɛ atamfo a wɔsoɛr tsia hɛn no nyina efi hɔ na obesiesie asaase no ma adan Paradise. To nsa frɛ hɔn a ‘wɔdɔ Jehovah nyina dɛ wɔmbɛka hɛn ho ma yɛmfa nokwardzi nye tsenenee nsom Jehovah a yerunnsuro hwee hɛn nkwa nda nyina.’—Luke 1:74, 75.
