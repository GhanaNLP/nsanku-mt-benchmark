Kezimɔ Azɔfʋlilɛ Nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ Nɩ Da Gyehova Yɩ Ɔlɔlɛ Ali
<<<P>>>
NDWOMI 107
Ɔlɔlɛ Nwv Ndianwʋ
<<<P>>>
BƲTAYI
Yekozuma kezimɔ azɔfʋlilɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ nɩ twĩ yɛ bike Gyehova.
<<<P>>>
‘Bɛyɩ Ɔsɔfʋ Ebani wɔ mmenii bɛ avinli kɛɛ igyiná bɛ agyia nu.’—HIB. 5:1.
<<<P>>>
DESANII bela hyinyia mmʋwalɩyɛ paa! Kɛ bezĩ anzɛnyʋ̃ bézi nɩ, abiyela hyinyia mmʋwalɩyɛ. Mɩnla ti a? Iluwakɛɛ ɔzʋmɩ mmɩlɩ mɔ Adamɩ nʋ Yifi yɛlɩ Nyamɩnlɩ zʋ azɔ̃nzɩzɩbɛ nɩ, bɔwʋ desanii bela wɔ tanɩ nu. Eyi ɩáma yɛkʋla yebike Nyamɩnlɩ bʋwɔ. (Ɛfɩ. 2:12; Kol. 1:21) Ɔhvanɩ be zʋ nɩ, ɩdɩ kɛɛ ɔhvanɩ kezile be ɣa la yɛ nɩɩ Nyamɩnlɩ avinli, meti sɛ yɛányia mmʋwalɩyɛ a, yelókʋla yekeyike yi anzɛnyʋ̃ yelókʋla yekenyia anyɩnlɩlelɛ ankasa wɔ yɛ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu. Na yelényia eyile be nwʋ anyɩnlɩlazʋ bela sʋ̃. Eyi ti, nɩ Gyehova pɛ kɛɛ ɩbʋwa adasamaa. Mɩnla yɛ ɩyɛlɩ a?
<<<P>>>
2 Mɔɔ ɩkɛyɛ na Gyehova iale yɛ wɔ yɛ tanɩ nu na yɛahʋla yɛaɣike yi nɩ, ɩyɛlɩ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ kɩnlɩma be. Mɛyɩ yɛ lɛ azɔfʋlilɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ nɩ. Iyɛlɩ ɔzɔ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ yɩ be ɩmanlɩ ɔwɔkɩ Yizilayamaa nɩ. Na aɣinzi yi, ɩyɛlɩ biye sʋ̃ ɩmanlɩ Kilisinyianɩ asaafʋ yɩ. Ɔzɔ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ nɩ, ɩkɔlɩ zʋlɔ wɔ nnɛ sʋ̃. Wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ eyi nu nɩ, yekozuma kezimɔ azɔfʋlilɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ nɩ wʋwalɩ ɔwɔkɩmaa nɩ. Akʋ̃ sʋ̃, sa yesunzu kezimɔ yekɔhʋla yekenyia azɔfʋlilɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ kɛnɩ nnɛ nɩ zʋ mfasʋ, nɩɩ kezimɔ yekɔhɔ zʋlɔ kenyia zʋlɔ mfasʋ wɔ eyile be nɩ nwʋ. Awielɩyɛ kʋlaa nɩ, sa yekila kezimɔ azɔfʋlilɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ nɩ da yɩ ali kɛɛ Gyehova dɔ yɛ paa.

KEZIMƆ AZƆFƲLILƐ NHYƖHYƐLƖYƐ YI WƲWALI ƆWƆKƖ YIZILAYAMAA NƖ
<<<P>>>
3 Sɔ̃mavʋlɔ Pɔɔlʋ hilehileli sianti mɔɔ Gyehova yɛlɩ azɔfʋlilɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ nɩ manlɩ Yizilayamaa nɩ nu. (Kɩnga Hibulumaa 5:1.) Nɩ Gyehova pɛ kɛɛ ɩbʋwa bɛ ma bebike yi. Mɛyɩ ti mɔɔ ɩyɩlɩ Ɔsɔfʋ Ebani kɛɛ “igyiná bɛ agyia nu wɔ yɩzayɩ Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ anyunʋ” nɩ a. Nɩ azɔfʋ yɩ bʋwa bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛpɛ kɛɛ bɔzʋ̃ bɛ ahʋnlɩnɩ
<<<P>>>
1. Mɩnla ti mɔɔ desanii bela hyinyia mmʋwalɩyɛ paa a?
<<<P>>>
2. Mɩnla yɛ yɛbazunzu nwʋmɔ wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ eyi nu a?
<<<P>>>
3. Kɛmɔ Hibulumaa 5:1 kile nɩ, mɩnla ɔhvanɩ zʋ yɛ azɔfʋ yɩ wʋwalɩ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ nɩ bɛpɛ kɛɛ bɔzʋ̃ bɛ ahʋnlɩnɩ nu bɔsʋ̃mɩ Gyehova nɩ a? (Kila mfonyini yi sʋ̃.)
