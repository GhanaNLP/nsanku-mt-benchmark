4 Mɩnla ɔhʋanɩ zʋ yɛ azɔfʋ mɔɔ nɩ bɔwɔ ɔwɔkʋ Yizilaya nɩ wʋwalɩ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ nɩ bɛpɛ kɛɛ bɔsʋ̃mɩ Gyehova ma bɛyikeli yi na bɔzʋ̃mɩnlɩ yɩ wɔ ɔhʋanɩ mɔɔ ide itiã numɔ nɩ zʋ a? Iluwa ahʋanɩ aditili anwĩ eyimmɔ zʋ. Mɔɔ ili bɔmʋnlɩ, nɩ azɔfʋ yɩ kyina Gyehova yɩ agyia nu kilehile mmenii yi Gyehova nwʋ odwokɔ. Mɩnla ɔhʋanɩ zʋ a? Malaki 2:7 kã kɛɛ: “Isɛ kɛɛ ɔsɔfʋ yi munwa bɔ nnimidi nwʋ banɩ, na mmenii pɩbɛ mmɛla wɔ yi munwa nu. Sianti yɛ le kɛɛ, ɩdɩ asafo Gyehova yɩ sʋ̃mavʋlɔ.” Meti nɩ ɩdɩ azɔfʋ yɩ bɛ asɛdɩ kɛɛ bɛkehilehile Yizilayamaa nɩ Mosisi yɩ mmɛla yɩ, bɔkɔwʋwa bɛ ma bɛkɛdɩ yɩ tʋ, na bɛava bɛawɔ bɛ ɛbɩla. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, wɔ Nehemaya yɩ mmɩlɩ zʋ nɩ, Levimaa 13 wʋwalɩ ɔsɔfʋ Ɛzɩla ma bɛhilehileli Mmɛla yɩ nu bɛhileli mmenii yi. Behilehileli numɔ wɔ ɔhʋanɩ be zʋ mɔɔ ɩmanlɩ ɩyɛlɩ mɛlɛkɛ kɛɛ mmenii nɩ kɛdɩ yɩ tʋ. Eyi manlɩ Yizilayamaa nɩ valɩ mɔɔ bɔzumanlɩ nɩ bɛyɛlɩ egyima, na ɩmanlɩ bɛyikeli Gyehova.—Ne. 8:2, 3, 6-8.

5 Mɔɔ ɩtɔ zʋlɔ anwĩ, nɩ azɔfʋ yɩ kyina mmenii nɩ bɛ agyia nu siã tanɩfakyɛ wɔ Gyehova kɛnɩ. Nɩ bɔduwa afɔlɩ mɔɔ nɩ bɔbɔ bɛma mmenii yi zʋ yɛ nɩ bɛyɛ zɔ a. Nɩ Yizilayamaa nɩ zi kɛɛ bedi mfʋmɩ eyile bela. Meti nɩ bezi kɛɛ behyinyia mmʋwalɩyɛ na ama bɛanyia tanɩfakyɛ wɔ Gyehova kɛ. (Ndw. 51:9) Eyi ti yɛ Gyehova manlɩ bɛnyianlɩ azɔfʋ mɔɔ bɔkɔwɔ afɔlɩ kɛma bɛ yɩ a. Pɩlazunɛ bela íni nwʋmɔ kɛɛ Yizilayamaa mɔɔ nɩ bɛpɛ kɛɛ bɔsʋ̃mɩ Gyehova nɩ bɛ ahʋnlɩnɩ dɔlɩ bo kunlu baaba kɛɛ benyia tanɩfakyɛ! Azɔfʋlilɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ nɩ da yɩ ali kɛɛ Gyehova dɔ mmenii mɔɔ bɛpɛ kɛɛ bɔsʋ̃mɩ yɩ nɩ, na ɩpɛ kɛɛ benyia tiwʋwaba baaba na bɛ nɩɩ yɩ nyia ɔhɔnwʋvalɛ daabaa.
