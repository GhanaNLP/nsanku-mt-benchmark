6 Yizilayamaa bo odwokɔ yɩ ma yɛnwʋ kɛɛ ɩsɛ
kɛɛ yɛpɩbɛlɩ sianti mɔɔ yɛye sutiye yɛma Gyehova
nɩ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ ɩyɩ bɛ kɛɛ belí nninyeni anyunu nɩ
nu bʋwɔ. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, yɛkɔhʋla yekeyizia yɛ
nwʋ kɛɛ, asʊ̃ mmɩlɩ mɔ mɩtɩ nyanlɩ kɛɛ ɩdɩ baaba
ɩma mɩ nkɔ̃ la yɛ mɩyɛ sutiye mɩma Gyehova a?
Lkɔhʋla ɩkɛya kɛɛ Yizilayamaa yɩ be valɩ owuanɩ
anzɛnyɩɩ ɛzɩkɛ mmegyia bɩndɩnlɩ bɛ anyɩnkɛ yɩ
munwa na ama bɛawɔ bɛ mmakanɩ nwʋ banɩ na kɛ
ebiye a, bɛlɛ bɛyɛ sutiye bɛkɛma Gyehova ti a.
Metɩ yɛkɔhʋla yekeyizia yɛ nwʋ kɛɛ, asʊ̃ mʋ suti-
yeyelɛ gyĩ kezimɔ mmenii mvʋlɔ yɛ bɛ dɩyɛ nɩ
zʋ a? Mmɩlɩ mɔ Yizilayamaa yɩ zʋ Igyibiti bɛyʋ bɔ
nwʋ nɩ, Gyehova hanlɩ Igyibitimaa yɩ bɛ ahʋnlɩnɩ
ma bɛhyɛlɩ Yizilayamaa yɩ nninyeni sʊ̃maa. (Ɛkɩ.
12:36) Naasʊ̃, mmɩlɩ mɔ Yizilayamaa yɩ nwʋnlɩ yɩ
kɛɛ Igyibitimaa dʋwa bɛ mɔɔ bɛbahũhũ bɛ nɩ,
bɛányia Gyehova nu anwʋlazʋ na bɛdɩlɩ atunwa.
(Ɛkɩ. 14:10-12) Yeábɛ kɛɛ yɛyɛ yɛ dɩyɛ kɛɛ Yizila-
yamaa yɩ. Mfomi, yɛpɛ kɛɛ yenyia Gyehova nu
anwʋlazʋ na yenyia bɛzabɛ mɔɔ iduwa bɔ zʋ ɩfa yɩ
ɔhʋanɩhilelɛ ɩma yɛ nɩ sʊ̃ nu anwʋlazʋ. (Kɩnga
Gyɛlɩmaya 23:4.) Sɛ bɛfa ɔhʋanɩhilelɛ be bɛma
yɛ na yɛádɩ yɩ tʋ anzɛnyɩɩ yɛfa yɛyɛ egyima a
mmenii kɛdanɩ yɛ po a, ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛbɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ
yɛkɛyɛ sutiye yɛkɛma Gyehova. Kɛ ɩkɛyɛ na yea-
nyia ahʋnlɩnɩ mɔɔ ɩkɛma yɛkɛyɛ sutiye na yɛálili
mfʋmɩ mɔɔ Yizilayamaa yɩ lili nɩ be a?

7. Mɩnla ɔhʋanɩ zʋ yɛ ɔlɔlɛ mɔɔ yɛwɔ yɛma Gyehova mɔɔ
yɛkɛhyɛ numɔ ɛzɩzɩbɛ nɩ kɔwʋwa yɛ ma yɛkɛyɛ sutiye yɛ-
kɛma yɩ a?
<<<P>>>
Gyehova yɩ ahyɩhyɛdɩ yɩ fa ɔhʋanɩhilelɛ be ma yɛ
na yɛádɩ sianti mɔɔ ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛfa yɛyɛ egyima nɩ
tʋ a, ɩkʋla ɩma ɩyɛ sɩ ɩma yɛ kɛɛ yɛkɛyɛ sutiye.
Ɔzɔ la sʊ̃ yɛ sɛ yɛfa ɔhʋanɩhilelɛ yɩ yɛyɛ egyima
na ɩkɛma yekeyiyia ndwokɔzɩzɩbɛ wɔ yɛ ɛbɩlawɔ-
lɛ nu a, ɩkɔhʋla ɩkɛma ɩkɛyɛ sɩ kɛɛ yɛkɛyɛ suti-
ye a. Sɛ ɩbayɩ zɔ a, ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛkakɛ kɛɛ yɛyɛ sutiye
yɛma Gyehova iluwakɛɛ yɛdɔ yɩ na yɛwɔ yɩ nu
anwʋlazʋ. (1 Dwɔ. 5:3) Ɔlɔlɛ mɔɔ yɛwɔ yɛma Gye-
hova nɩ bʋwa yɛ ma yɛfa abʋtalɩ yɛdɩkɛ yɩ, na
yɛzʊ̃ kãnwʋazɩ nu yede yetia nu kɛɛ wɔ mmɩlɩ ba
nu nɩ, yɛkɔnwʋ kɛɛ mfasʋ wɔ zʋ kɛɛ yɛyɛlɩ sutiye
yɛmanlɩ yɩ.—Odw. 7:8; Mik. 7:7.

8 Nnɛ sʊ̃, ye mmeliyema mmenyia nu mmaalɛ
sʊ̃maa yɛ sutiye nwʋ ndianwʋba ma yɛ, na bɛva
ɔhʋanɩhilelɛ bɛyɛ egyima mmɩlɩ po mɔ beádɩ yɩ tʋ
nɩ. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, wɔ COVID-19 adimakɛlɛ mmɩlɩ
yɩ nu nɩ, Ɔhʋanɩhilelɛ Okuwu yi hã hileli asaƒʋ
azʋlɔtʋ nɩ kɛɛ boluwa videoconferencing anzɛnyɩɩ
foonu zʋ bɛyɛ dɩyezumanlɛ. Asaƒʋ nu ebanɩ be
mɔɔ ɩwɔ Peru nɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ: “Ahyɛasɩ nɩ, nɩ mɩtɩ
nyanlɩ kɛɛ ɔhʋanɩhilelɛ eyi mɛyɩ, ɩlɔkʋla ɩkɛyɛ
egyima wɔ yɛ apaa yɩ nu kɩ.” Naasʊ̃, távalɩ nwʋmɔ
kɛɛ nɩ tádɩ ɔhʋanɩhilelɛ yɩ tʋ nɩ, nɩ izi yɩ kɛnya
kɛɛ ɩkɛyɛ sutiye ɩkɛma Gyehova na ianyia yi nu
anwʋlazʋ. (Ɛɣɛ. 3:5, 6) Mɩnla yɛ ɩzʊ̃ numɔ ɩyalɩ a?
Ihanlɩ kɛɛ: “Munwʋnlɩ kezimɔ Gyehova yila bɛza-
bɛ mɔɔ bɛyɛ sutiye nɩ.” Na ɩdʋwalɩ zʋlɔ kɛɛ, “yɛ
asaƒʋ yɩ ga yelɩ sɩ na ɔkʋyɛlɛ hɔlɩ zʋlɔ wʋwalɩ
yɛ avinli.” Sunzu ndianwʋ eyi sʊ̃ nwʋ kila. Wɔ
2020 nu nɩ, ahyɩhyɛdɩ yɩ yelɩ nzɩnzanlɩyɛ wɔ asaƒʋ
azʋlɔtʋ mɔɔ bɛfa manlɩ fʋlɔ zʋ ndenɩ bɛyɛ dɩyezu-
manlɛ nɩɩ okuwu azʋlɔtʋ nu. Mmɩlɩ mɔ eliyema
baalɛ be mɔɔ ɩwɔ Japan nɩ ya nwʋnlɩ kɛɛ bokogu
okuwu mɔɔ ɩwɔ numɔ nɩ, ɩhanlɩ kɛɛ: “Nɩ mɩábɛ
kɛɛ mɩtɩndɛ mitiyia nzɩnzanlɩyɛ eyi, na mfomi nɩ
mɩpɛ kɛɛ mɩyɛ sutiye. Mikilaa ɔhʋanɩ paa kɛɛ mi-
kenyia nzɩnzanlɩyɛ eyi zʋ mfasʋ.”
