Yizilayamaa yı diyeli Gyehova lávalı nwʋmɔ kɛɛ ankı sa Igyibitimaa kʋla twodwu tiyia bɛ nɩ
(Kila nkyɩkyɛmi 3)
<<<P>>>
ahyɛdɩ be mɔɔ nwʋmɔ hyinyia paa naasʋ̃ ɩkɔhʋla ɩkɛyɛ sɩ kɛɛ bekediye nɩ ɩmanlɩ bɛ. Lhanlɩ ihileli bɛ kɛɛ bohũ owuanɩ nyini anzɛnyɩɛ ɛzɩkɛ nyini, na bɛvá yi mmegyia nɩ bɛbɩndɩ bɛ anyɩnkɛ anyunu. (Ɛkɩ. 12:5, 7) Sɛ nɩ Gyehova yı bɔvʋlɔ yı ibaduaa numɔ nunuma nɩ wɔ bɛ anyɩnkɛ anyunu a, iluwa mmegyia yı ti, nɩ ilókũ abakanɩ bela anzɛnyɩɛ ɛnlamɩ be mɔɔ ɩdɩ abakanɩ wɔ ɔzɔ pika yi nu. Naasʋ̃, nɩ ankı ahyɛdɩ eyi kɔhʋla kɛyɛ sɩ kɛma Yizilayamaa nɩ kɛɛ bekelili zʋlɔ. Mɩnla ti a? Iluwakɛɛ, nɩ Igyibitimaa sɔ̃mɩ owuanɩ nyini. Meti nɩ Yizilayamaa kɔhʋla kenyia adwɩnɩ kɛɛ, sɛ Igyibitimaa nwʋ kɛɛ bokũũ owuanɩ nyini anzɛnyɩɛ ɛzɩkɛ nyini a, ɩkɛma bɛkɛva bɔ nwʋ ɛya na bɛahũhũ bɛzabɛ bɛ sʋ̃. (Ɛkɩ. 8:25-27) Naasʋ̃, eyi munwatĩĩ nu nɩ, Yizilayamaa nɩ nwʋnlɩ sianti mɔɔ ɩsɛ kɛɛ bɛyɛ sutiye, na “bɛyɛlɩ mɔɔ Gyehova hyɛlɩ bɛ kɛɛ bɛyɛ nɩ munwatĩĩ pɛpɛɛpɛ.” (Ɛkɩ. 12:28) Iluwa sutiye mɔɔ bɛyɛlɩ nɩ ti, Gyehova yilalɩ bɛ, na bɛ abakanɩ bela iáwu.

4 Yizilayamaa yı zɔ̃ Igyibiti yɩlɩ bɔ nwʋ ɔzɔ nunuma nɩ la. Na Gyehova hã hileli bɛ kɛɛ bɛvá bɛ anyɩ behile Ngyemvile Kɔkɔlɔ yı nwʋ. Naasʋ̃ ɩákyɛ kʋlaa yɛ Gyehova hã hileli bɛ kɛɛ bɛyɛ debe mɔɔ wɔ enii yı anyɩ zʋ nɩ sɛ ekila a, ɩdɩ kɛɛ mɔɔ ntɩyası bela íni numɔ. Gyehova hã hileli bɛ kɛɛ bezialɩ bɛ mɛda na bɔhɔ bohunwanlɩ bo munwa wɔ ngyemvile yi nu avile yi bɛ avinli. Wɔ bʋwabɩlɛ mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ nu nɩ, nɩ sɛ Yizilayamaa yı tua bɛ anyɩ kila bɛ mɛda a, bɔnwʋ kɛɛ Falo nɩ yı azogyiamaa yı ɛdɩ na sɛ bekila bɛ anyunu sʋ̃ a, Ngyemvile Kɔkɔlɔ yı yɛ ɩla ɛkɛnɩ yı a. Meti nɩ ɩdɩ kɛɛ mɔɔ bɛhalɩ avinli. Naasʋ̃ wɔ ɔzɔ mmɩlɩ eyi sʋ̃ nu nɩ, Yizilayamaa yı valɩ ɔhvanlhilelɛ mɔɔ Gyehova luwalɩ Mosisi zʋ va manlɩ bɛ nɩ yɛlɩ egyima. (Ɛkɩ. 14:1-4) Naasʋ̃, mmɩlɩ mɔ bɔnwʋnlɩ kɛɛ Igyibitimaa bebikee bɛ nɩ, “bo kunlu duli” na bɛhanlɩ po kɛɛ Mosisi va bɛ hɔ ɛkɛnɩ kɛɛ bɔhɔ bowuwu! (Ɛkɩ. 14:5-11) Mɩnla ti mɔɔ akʋ̃ dɩyɛ nɩ bonlu bɔ nwʋ kɛɛ bɛyɛlɩ sutiye yı a? Na kɛ Gyehova nwʋnlɩ nyɛlɩyɛ mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ mɔɔ bɛlalɩ yı ali nɩ izeli a?

5 Bayɩbʋlʋ ma yɛnwʋ yı kɛɛ mmɩlɩ mɔ Yizilayamaa yı wɔ Ngyemvile Kɔkɔlɔ yı nwʋ nɩ, “beákile anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ wɔ nnwʋánlɩ nninyeni mɔɔ [Gyehova] yɛlɩ” wɔ Igyibiti nɩ nwʋ, na “bɛákakɛ” ɔlɔlɛ mɔɔ nnahɩlɩbɛ íni numɔ mɔɔ ɩlalɩ yı ali ihileli bɛ nɩ sʋ̃. (Kɩnga Ndwomi 106:7.) Nɩ Yizilayamaa yı nwʋ kezimɔ Gyehova wɔlɩ bɛ mmakanɩ nu bɛ nnamɩ mmakanɩ nwʋ banɩ mmɩlɩ mɔ ɩvalɩ ɔhawʋ mɔɔ ɩtɔ zʋlɔ bunlu ɩyalɩ Igyibitimaa bɔ zʋ nɩ. Naasʋ̃, Yizilayamaa yı bɛátɩ ɔlɔlɛ kezile mɔɔ nɩ Gyehova ɩwɔ ɩma bɛ nɩ yı tʋ, na bɛáde bedi kɛɛ wɔ ɔzɔ mmɩlɩ eyi nu sʋ̃ nɩ, Gyehova kele bɛ.
