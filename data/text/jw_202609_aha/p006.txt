15 Yɩ ahyɩhyɛdɩ yɩ. Gyehova duwa yɩ ahyɩhyɛdɩ yɩ zʋ ma yɛ sa kã ndwɔkɔ azʋlɔtʋ wɔ yɩ mmʋlɩ nu mɔɔ ɩbɔ yɛ nwʋ banɩ wɔ sunzuma ɔfʋã nu. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, iluwakɛɛ yɛali munli nɩ ti, ɩyɛ a mfʋmɩ be mɔɔ yeli ma idua numɔ nwʋ adwɩnɩndwɩnɩ kɔhʋla kɛhyɛ yɛ zʋ anzɛnyɩɩ sa yɛkʋla yɛtɩ nyanlɩ kɛɛ abiyela ɩádwɩnɩ yɛ nwʋ. Sɛ yɛtɩ nyanlɩ zɔ a, Setanɩ sũ kɔwɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ɩkɛma yekenyia adwɩnɩ kɛɛ ɩdɩ baaba kɛɛ yɛkenyia nyanlɩdɩlɛ mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ. Na ɩkɛma yekenyia adwɩnɩ po kɛɛ Gyehova ɩále yɛ ɔzɔ̃mɩnlɛ tiã numɔ dɔkɔ̃. Sɛ ɩbayɩ zɔ a, ɩma yɛ adwɩnɩndwɩnɩ yɛ kezile paa. (Mt. 4:3; 6:13) Naasʋ̃, Gyehova yɩ ahyɩhyɛdɩ yɩ ma yɛ sa kã ndwɔkɔ azʋlɔtʋ mɔɔ ɩma yenyia anwʋlazʋ kɛɛ Gyehova va yɛ tanɩ ɩhyɛ yɛ, ɩdwɩnɩ yɛ nwʋ, na ɩkɛva yɛ ahyinyiadɩ bela ɩkɛma yɛ. (Ndw. 86:5; Mt. 10:29-31; Lk. 12:28; Hib. 13:5) Ebiye a, ɛkakɛ mmʋlɩ be mɔ ɛyɩnganlɩ odwɔkɔ be wɔ yɛ ɔnwʋma be nu mɔɔ ɩhanlɩ wʋ ahʋnlɩnɩ ma ɛhanlɩ kɛɛ, ‘Odwɔkɔ eyi mɛyɩ bɛhɛlɛlɩ bɛmanlɩ mɩ.’ Eyi da yɩ ali kɛɛ Gyehova duwa yɩ ahyɩhyɛdɩ yɩ zʋ fa ndwɔkɔ mɔɔ yehyinyia nɩ ma yɛ wɔ yɩ mmʋlɩ nu, na eyi bɔ yɛ nu yɩ avinli abusunwawɔlɛ yɩ nwʋ banɩ.

16 Gyehova ɩlɛ́ma ɔhʋanɩ ma yɩ adamvʋlɔ kohũhũ yɩ azɔ̃mɩvʋlɔ munwatĩĩ. lwɔ numɔ, ɩyɛ a Gyehova ma ɔhʋanɩ ma bɛyɛ yɩ azɔ̃mɩvʋlɔ be atutɩɩ anzɛnyɩɩ bokũ bɛ po. Gyehova yɛ zɔ na ama ɩala ali kɛɛ Setanɩ dɩ awohwiʋvlɔ. (Yik. 2:10; 6:9) Setanɩ anyɩ ɩáleli nwʋmɔ kɛɛ Gyehova yɩ azɔ̃mɩvʋlɔ nu ɛgyɩkĩĩ be la yɛ bɛ adamvʋlɔ bɔhʋla bohũ bɛ a. Yi kunlu a ankɩ odwodwudiyialɛmaa nɩ hũhũ Gyehova yɩ azɔ̃mɩvʋlɔ munwatĩĩ. (Mt. 24:9; Yik. 12:9-12) Naasʋ̃, nɔhwanlɛ odwɔkɔ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ, Gyehova ɩlɛ́ma ɔhʋanɩ ma dɩyɛ mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ kezi kyĩ! Sa yɛkʋla yenyia anwʋlazʋ kɛɛ Gyehova koluwa yɩ awɔvʋlɔ yɩ zʋ kɔwɔ yi mmenii nwʋ banɩ na ama bɛahɔ zʋlɔ beazɔ̃mɩ yɩ na bɛahã odwɔkɔba yɩ.—Hib. 1:7, 14.

17 Kɛ dɩyɛ bela mɔɔ ɩkɔhʋla ikebila yɛ wɔ nnwʋnlamɩ ɔfʋã nu nɩ yɛ Gyehova bɔ ye nu kũ bela nwʋ banɩ wɔ nwʋmɔ a. Sɛ yɛfa Bayɩbʋlʋ nu mmela nɩ egyinalɛzʋ-ndwɔkɔ yɛbɔ yɛ ɛbɩla a, ɩbɔ yɛ nwʋ banɩ wɔ bʋwabɩlɛ azʋlɔtʋ mɔɔ ɩkɔhʋla ikebila yɛ wɔ nnwʋnlamɩ ɔfʋã nu nɩ nwʋ. (Ɛɣɛ. 14:29, 30; 22:3; 2 Kɔl. 7:1) Naasʋ̃, íni kɛɛ yekila ɔhʋanɩ kɛɛ Gyehova kɔwɔ yɛ nwʋ banɩ wɔ dɩyɛ bela mɔɔ ɩkɔhʋla ikebila yɛ wɔ nnwʋnlamɩ ɔfʋã nu nɩ nwʋ. Sɛ Gyehova yɛ zɔ a, ɩkɛma mmenii kele Setanɩ yɩ awohwi yi keli. Na bekenyia adwɩnɩ kɛɛ, iluwakɛɛ Gyehova bɔ yɩ azɔ̃mɩvʋlɔ nwʋ banɩ wɔ nnwʋnlamɩ ɔfʋã nu asianɩ bela nwʋ nɩ ti yɛ bɔsɔ̃mɩ yɩ a. (Dwb. 1:10, 11) Naasʋ̃, sɛ yɛkɔ zʋlɔ yedi nɔhvanlɛ yɛma Gyehova mmʋlɩ mɔ bɛyɛɛ yɛ atɩtɩɩ nɩ po a, yɛma ɩda ali kɛɛ Setanɩ dɩ awohwivʋlɔ. Na mɛyɩ sũ ma ɩda ali kɛɛ yɛsũmɩ Gyehova iluwakɛɛ mɛyɩ yɛ ɩdɩ nɔhvanlɛ Nyamɩnlɩ nɩ a, na yɛdɔ yɩ. (Wul. 9:22) Llɛ́kyɛ kʋlaa, Gyehova kɛma amanyɛnwʋnlɛ mɔɔ yi mmenii boduwaa numɔ nɩ munwatĩĩ kɛɣa awielɩyɛ kʋlaa kʋlaa. Naasʋ̃, kɔbʋmɩ kɛɛ eyimmɔ munwatĩĩ kɛɣa awielɩyɛ nɩ, Gyehova kɔwuwa yɛ ma yɛkɛyɛ sutiye yekɛma yɩ, na ama yɛahɔ zʋlɔ yɛayɛ yɩ ɔhɔnwʋmmɔ.—1 Kɔl. 10:13; 1 Pit. 5:10.
