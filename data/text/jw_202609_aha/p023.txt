12 Gyisɛsɩ sʋ̃ pɛ kɛɛ yɛ nɩ Gyehova nyia ɔhɔnwʋvalɛ baaba. Kɛ yɛ Ɔsɔfʋ Ebani nɩ, Gyisɛsɩ yɛ egyima kɛɛ “bʋwavʋlɔ wɔ Ezɩlɛ nɩ yı pɛ” mɔɔ isiã tanɩfakyɛ ɩma yɛ. (1 Dwɔ. 2:1, NWT; kɩnga Hibulumaa 7:25.) Sɛ Gyisɛsɩ isiãã tanɩfakyɛ kɛma wʋ a, mɩnla nninyeni be yɛ isunzu nwʋmɔ a? Ebiye a, sa isunzu kezimɔ bɛdɩdɩlɩ wʋ nɩ kezimɔ adimakɛlɛ be ididee wʋ nɩ nwʋ. Sa ɩkʋla sʋ̃ isunzu adwɩnɩndwɩnɩ be mɔɔ ɩhyɛ wʋ zʋ anzɛnyʋ ɔhawʋ be mɔɔ oduwaa numɔ nɩ nwʋ. Akʋ̃ sʋ̃, ebiye a, ikozunzu wʋ subanɩba nɩ nninyeniba mɔɔ ɛyɛ nɩ nwʋ, nɩ kezimɔ ɛva nɔhʋanlɛlilɛ zũmɩ Gyehova nɩ nwʋ. Iluwakɛɛ Gyisɛsɩ nwʋ mɔɔ ɩwɔ yɛ ahʋnlɩnɩ nu nɩ ti, sa isunzu kezimɔ ɔkɔ zʋlɔ siã Gyehova kɛɛ ɩvá wʋ tanɩ ɩhyɛ wʋ, nɩɩ mmɔdɩnɩ mɔɔ ɔbɔɔ kɛɛ ɛkɛyɛ mɔɔ ɩtɩnyɛ nɩ nwʋ. Sa yɛkʋla yenyia anwʋlazʋ kɛɛ Gyehova kele ezianlɛ mɔɔ Gyisɛsɩ isiãã ɩkɛma yɛ nɩ ikediã numɔ, iluwakɛɛ Gyehova yi kunlu ɩdɩyɛnɛ, na iziyeziye yı nwʋ kɛɛ ɩkɛva tanɩ kɛhyɛ. (Ndw. 86:5) Mɩnla ti mɔɔ yɛwɔ ɔzɔ anwʋlazʋ yı a? Iluwakɛɛ Gyehova yɩzayɩ yɛ ɩyɩlɩ Gyisɛsɩ kɛɛ ɩhã ye odwokɔ ɩmá yɛ a.
