12 Kɔ zʋlɔ suma Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ. Mpayɩ mɔɔ Gyehohyafatɩ wɔlɩ nɩ ma yɛnwʋ yɩ kɛɛ nɩ ɩdɩ abiye mɔɔ ɩsuma Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ paa. Nɩ izi kɛɛ Gyehova li yɩ anvhʋba mɔɔ ɩwɔlɩ Abilehamɩ kɛɛ ɩkɛva azɩlɛ kɩnlɩma ɩkɛma Yizilaya nɩ zʋ. (Gyn. 12:7) Ndwokɔ mɔɔ Gyehohyafatɩ hanlɩ wɔ yɩ mpayɩwɔlɛ yɩ nu nɩ ma yɛnwʋ yɩ kɛɛ nɩ izi Sɔlɔmɔnʋ yɩ mpayɩ mɔɔ ɩwɔlɩ mmɩlɩ mɔ ɩyilaa asɔlɩ pika yɩ zʋ nɩ. Na ɔzɔ ndwɔkɔ yɩ be la yɛ yɩzayɩ sʋ̃ ɩva ilili egyima wɔ yɩ mpayɩwɔlɛ yɩ nu a. (2 Mm. 6:28-30; 20:5-9) Nninyeni mɔɔ nɩ Gyehohyafatɩ zi falɩ Gyehova nwʋ nɩ wʋwalɩ yɩ ma inyianlɩ yi nu anwʋlazʋ kʋlaa, na izili yɩ kɛnya kɛɛ ɩkɛyɛ sutiye ɩkɛma yɩ. Ɔzɔ la yɛ amɩyɛ sʋ̃, sɛ yɛkɔ zʋlɔ yesuma Gyehova nwʋ dɩyɛ na yɛyɛ mɔɔ iduwa Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ zʋ ɩkã ikile yɛ nɩ a, ɩkɛma yekezi yɛ kɛnya kɛɛ yɛkɛyɛ sutiye yɛkɛma yɩ mmɩlɩ po mɔ yeyiyiaa ndwɔkɔzɩzɩbɛ nɩ.—Mt. 13:52.

13 Da kãnwvazɩ ali. Gyehohyafatɩ yɩ mpayɩ da yɩ ali sʋ̃ kɛɛ nɩ ɩdɩ abiye mɔɔ ɩkã yɩ nwʋ azɩ. Kɛ bulombunli nɩ, ankɩ sa ɩkʋla ɩyɩ Gyehova isiye ahanlɩ na ɩfa yɩ nwʋ itiã yɩ azogyiamaa sʋ̃maa mɔɔ ɩlɛ bɛ nɩ zʋ na ɩ nɩ yɩ adamvʋlɔ yɩ bɛahɔ bɛahʋ̃, naasʋ̃ ɩáyɛ zɔ. Wɔ yɩ mpayɩ mɔɔ igyinanlɩ yɩ manlɩmaa yɩ bɛ anyunu ɩwɔlɩ nu nɩ, ɩhanlɩ kɛɛ ɩánwʋ mɔɔ ɩyɛ meti Gyehova ɩwʋwá yɩ. (2 Mm. 20:12) Kãnwʋazɩ subanɩ mɔɔ Gyehohyafatɩ lalɩ yɩ ali nɩ wʋwalɩ yɩ ma ileli Gyehova yɩ ɔhʋanɩhilelɛ idianlɩ numɔ. Ɔzɔ la yɛ amɩyɛ sʋ̃, sɛ yɛka yɛ nwʋ azɩ a, ɩkɔwʋwa yɛ ma yɛkɛyɛ sutiye yɛkɛma Gyehova wɔ bʋwabɩlɛ bela nu a.—Ftl. 2:5, 8.

14 Sunzu kezimɔ wʋ nyɛlɩyɛ kã mmenii mvʋlɔ nɩ nwʋ. Gyehova wʋwalɩ Gyehohyafatɩ yɩ mpayɩ. Iluwalɩ nkɔmɩhyɛnii Gyehazɩyɛlɩ zʋ ɩvalɩ ɔhʋanɩhilelɛ pɔkyĩĩ be ɩmanlɩ Gyehohyafatɩ. Gyehazɩyɛlɩ hã hileli Gyehohyafatɩ nɩɩ yi mmenii nɩ kɛɛ nwʋmɔ léhyinyia kɛɛ bɔkɔhʋ̃. Mfomi, bɔhɔ́ na “begyina-gyina ɛkɛnɩ na bɛyɛ dinyii na behila kezimɔ Gyehova kele bɔ nnwʋanlɩ.” Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ kã kɛɛ Gyehohyafatɩ dɩlɩ ɔzɔ la pɛ yɛ ‘ɩhanlɩ yi nu azɩ na ɩvalɩ yɩ anyunu iwutuli azɩ a.’ Na ɩyɛlɩ zɔ nɩ, mmenii yi munwala sʋ̃ hanlɩ bo nu azɩ na bɛlalɩ Gyehova asɩ. Eyile mɔɔ ili ɛkɛnɩ mɔɔ bekeyiyiaa bɛ adamvʋlɔ nɩ, Gyehohyafatɩ hyɛlɩ yɩ mmenii yi munwatĩĩ ɛzɩzɩbɛ kɛɛ benyiá Gyehova nu yɩ nkɔmɩhyɛmaa nɩ nu dedi.—2 Mm. 20:14-20.

15 Gyehohyafatɩ yʋlɩ mmenyia be kɛɛ “beli azogyiamaa nɩ bɛ anyunu” na bɔdʋ ayɛlɛyʋlɛ ndwomi. Lyɛlɩ zɔ ɩva ihileli kɛɛ ɩwɔ dedi kezile paa wɔ Gyehova nu (2 Mm. 20:21) Gyehohyafatɩ yɛlɩ ɔzɔ “mmɩlɩ mɔ ɩ nɩɩ manlɩ yɩ nɩ valɩ adwɩnɩ mɛda nɩ.” Kila kezimɔ Gyehohyafatɩ ɩyɛ Gyehova mɔɔ yekenyia yi nu anwʋlazʋ na yɛayɛ sutiye yɛama yɩ nɩ nwʋ ndianwʋba!
