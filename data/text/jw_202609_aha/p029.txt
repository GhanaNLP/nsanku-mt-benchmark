10 Sɛ yɛkɔhʋla yɛkɔzɔ Gyehova anyɩ a, mɛyɩ nɩ ɩsɛ kɛɛ yekyi nninyeni mɔɔ ikyi nɩ. (Ndw. 97:10) Eyi ti, ɩsɛ kɛɛ yebizia yɛ nwʋ kɛɛ: ‘Asʋ̃ mɩtɩ nyanlɩ kɛɛ Gyehova yɩ mmɛla ɩáma mʋbɔ mɩ ɛbɩla kɛmɔ mɩpɛ yɩ a? Asʋ̃ nninye-ni mɔɔ mɩfa midide mɩ anyɩ nɩ kile kɛɛ mɩ anyɩ deli nninyeni mɔɔ Gyehova kyi nɩ nwʋ a? Asʋ̃ ɩyɛ a, minyianlɩ a ankɩ Gyehova zɩnza yɩ mmɛla nu ma ɩ nu mɔɔ mɩ anyɩ deli nwʋmɔ nɩ yiyia a?’

11 Sɛ ɛba nwʋ yɩ kɛɛ wʋ anyɩ deli debe be mɔɔ Gyehova kyi nwʋ a, mɩnla yɛ ɩsɛ kɛɛ ɛyɛ a? Áma enyia adwɩnɩ kɛɛ ɔlɔ́kʋla kɛyɛ mɔɔ Gyehova pɛ yɩ kyĩ. Kakɛ kɛɛ yezayɛ munwatĩĩ yɛtɔ sini meti ɩyɛ a, yenyia adwɩnɩ tanɩ. (Ndw. 51:5; 103:14) Naasʋ̃ sa ɔkʋla si pɔkɔ kɛɛ eléyɛ mɔɔ ɩga wu ti nu nɩ na elédi “nnwʋnlamɩ nu akɔnɔ” mɛda. (Ɛfɩ. 2:3) Ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛpʋ sũ kɛɛ yɛkɔdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ nninyeni tanɩ nwʋ. Ɩyɛ na sɛ adwɩnɩ tanɩ ba wu ti nu a, ndɛndɛ la ɩsɛ kɛɛ ɛyɩ wʋ adwɩnɩ anzɛnyɩɩ wʋ anyɩ wɔ nninyeni mɔɔ Gyehova kyi nɩ zʋ. (Gyn. 39:9, 12) Akʋ̃ dwɩnɩndwɩnɩ kezimɔ ɔzɔ nninyeni nɩ kɔhʋla kɛzɩkɛ ɛ nɩɩ Gyehova nɩɩ mmenii mvʋlɔ bɛ avi-nli nɩ nwʋ. Si wʋ kenyia kɛɛ eléma debe mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ kezi. (Ɛfɩ. 5:5) Wɔ Gyehova yɩ mmʋwa-lɩyɛ zʋ nɩ sa ɔkʋla kyi nninyeni tanɩ.

12 Ye nu sʋ̃maa wɔ kɛnɩ mɔɔ dɩyɛ bela mɔɔ yɛyɛ wɔ yɛ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu nɩ, yɛfa Intanɛtɩ yedi egyima mɔɔ yɛ ɔzʋ̃mɩnlɛ po kɩnla nwʋ. Naasʋ̃ mɔɔ yɛnwʋ yɩ wɔ Intanɛtɩ zʋ nɩ numɔ sʋ̃maa da oyuwadɩ yɩ sunzuma yɩ ali. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, ponoglafi wulu zvlɔ wɔ Intanɛtɩ yɩ zʋ na ɩáyɛ sɩ kɛɛ mbanimbani nɩɩ nnwuakula kehila biye. Sexting dɩ nna nu ɛbɩlazɩkɛlɛ nwʋ mfonyini, vidiyo, nɩɩ ndwɔkɔ mɔɔ abiye fa sɛndɩ abiye. Ldɩ nyɛlɩyɛ be mɔɔ mmʋlantɩyɛ nɩɩ nyɛdɛkɛ fa bɔ nwʋ wula numɔ paa. Ndwɔkɔ sʋ̃maa wulu zvlɔ wɔ Intanɛtɩ yɩ zʋ mɔɔ ídɩ nɔhʋanlɛ mɔɔ bɛfa bɛdahɩlɩdahɩlɩ mmenii sʋ̃maa. Wɔ sohyi-ya midiya zʋ nɩ, mmenii ta fa nninyeni mɔɔ bɛfa bedide bɛ anyɩ anzɛnyɩɩ nnɩka mɔɔ bɔkɔ nwʋ mfonyini anzɛnyɩɩ vidiyo bokua kɛ. Sɛ yɛnwʋ yɩ zɔ a, sa yɛkʋla yɛtɩ nyanlɩ kɛɛ ɩsɛ yɛ-zayɛ sũ yɛfa yɛ mmɩlɩ sʋ̃maa yɛyɛ nninyeni mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ na ama yɛ anyɩ iale. Sɛ ɩbayɩ zɔ a, sa yɛbɔ yɩ tʋ kɛɛ yetindiãã dɩyɛzumanlɛhɔlɛ nɩɩ dasʋlɛlilɛ nu.—Hib. 10:24, 25

13 Ɩsɛ kɛɛ yekila yɛ nwʋ bʋwɔ wɔ debela mɔɔ ɩwɔ Intanɛtɩ yɩ zʋ mɔɔ ɩkɔhʋla ɩkɛzɩkɛ yɛ mmɩlɩ anzɛnyɩɩ yɛ adwɩnɩ nɩ nwʋ. Sa yɛkʋla yebizia yɛ nwʋ kɛɛ: ‘Asʋ̃ mɩsɩkɛ mmɩlɩ sʋ̃maa mikila nninyeni mɔɔ mmenii fa kua sohyiya midiya zʋ mmɩlɩ mɔ míni sianti baaba bela a?’ (Ɛfɩ. 5:15, 16) ‘Mɩnla nninyeni yɛ mikila wɔ Intanɛtɩ yɩ zʋ a? Sɛ mʋnwʋ debe mɔɔ ɩdɩbɛ a, asʋ̃ mɩyɩ mɩ anyɩ ndɛndɛ zʋ la a?’ (Ndw. 119:37) ‘Sɛ mʋnwʋ debe wɔ Intanɛtɩ yɩ zʋ a, ɛkɛnɩ la nɩ mile mili anzɛnyɩɩ mide mmɩlɩ mɩpɩbɛlɩ nɔhʋanlɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ nu nɩ a?’—Ɛɣɛ. 14:15.
