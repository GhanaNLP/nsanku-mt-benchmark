hyinyia paa kɛɛ yesiyeziye yɛ nwu yɛma odwo-dwudiyialɛ mmilɩ mɔ yɛkãã Bulombunlililɛ yɩ nwu odwokɔba yɩ nɩ. Wɔ amanlɩ sɔ̃maa nu nɩ, ayanɩ yɩ lɛfalɩ salɛ létianlɩ yɛ dasɩlɛlilɛ egyima yɩ zʋ, meti yɛkʋla yɛfa anyɩnlɩlelɛ yedi dasɩlɛ. Naasʋ̃ wɔ mɛyɩ po nu nɩ, yehyinyia akɛnyazızıbɛ na ama yɛahʋla yɛahã yɛahile mmenii kɛɛ ɩlékye bela, Nyamɩnlɩ Bulombunlililɛ yɩ ɩbazɩkɛ oyuwadɩ tanɩ eyi. Na odwokɔ eyi sʋ̃ mɛyɩ, yɛkɔhɔ zʋlɔ yɛkɛhã nkʋ̃ʋ̃ kɔbʋmɩ kɛɛ Amagadɔnʋ kɛya. (Mt. 24:14) Akʋ̃ sʋ̃, wɔ anwʋhyɩlɩyɛ kezile yi nu nɩ, yɛkɛhã ndwokɔ mɔɔ yi munwa yɛ ɛhyɩmɛ na eyi kɛma mmenii kezi pɔkɔ kɛɛ bɛkɛda Nyamɩnlɩ Bulombunlililɛ yɩ anzɛnyɩɩ enii ayanɩ mɛda. Na wɔ Gyehova yɩ ada-mvʋlɔ bɛ ɔfʋã nu mɛyɩ, odwokɔ yɩ nwu ɛyɛkɛ nɩ ‘kɛyɛ kezile’ wɔ bɔ zʋ.* (Yik. 16:21) láyɛ nnwʋánlɩ kɛɛ bɛkɛva ɛya kezimɔ Falo valɩ ɛya wɔ Ngyemvi-le Kɔkɔlɔ yɩ nwʋ nɩ. Sa yɛkʋla yesiyeziye yɛ nwu yɛma odwodwudiyialɛ mɔɔ ɩla yɛ anyunu nɩ iluwa sutiye mɔɔ yɛkɛyɛ na yɛahã odwokɔba yɩ ɩávalɩ nwʋmɔ kezimɔ mmenii kɛyɛ bɛ dɩyɛ bela nɩ.

10 Sɛ yekila Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ nu a, yɛnwʋ mmenii sɔ̃maa mɔɔ bɛyɛ sutiye nwʋ ndianwʋba sɔ̃maa

* Kila “Ndwokɔyizialɛ Mɔɔ Ɩzʋ̃ Ɛyɩngalɛmaa Bɛ Ɛkɛnɩ” mɔɔ ɩwɔ August 2025 Ɔwɩɛnɩlɛ Ayanɩ yi nu nɩ.
<<<P>>>
10. Kɛ Bulombunli Gyehohyafatɩ dɩlɩ nyanlɩ zeli mmilɩ mɔ amanlɩ asã be hanlɩ bɔ nwʋ wɔlɩ numɔ kɛɛ bɛ nɩɩ Dwuuda bɛahũ nɩ a?
<<<P>>>
bɛma yɛ. Bo nu ɔkʋ̃ yɛ lɛ Bulombunli Gyehohya-fatɩ. Nɩ ɩdɩ Dwuuda bulombunli, na ɔzɔ mmilɩ yɩ nu yɛ amanlɩ asã be hanlɩ bɔ nwʋ wɔlɩ numɔ kɛɛ bɛ nɩɩ Dwuuda bɛahũ a. Nɔhvanlɛ odwo-kɔ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ Gyehohyafatɩ nɩɩ yɩ manlɩmaa yɩ bo kunlu duli. Na ntɩyası wɔ numɔ sʋ̃ kɛɛ bo kunlu duli. (2 Mm. 20:1-3) Naasʋ̃ ɩávalɩ nwʋmɔ kɛɛ Gyehohyafatɩ yi kunlu iduli nɩ, izili pɔkɔ-ba. Bɛmá yehila kezimɔ Gyehohyafatɩ yɛlɩ sutiye na inyianlɩ Gyehova nu anwʋlazʋ nɩ, na yezuma debe wɔ nu.
<<<P>>>
11 Pɩbɛ́ Gyehova yɩ ɛkɛnɩ mmʋwalɩyɛ na yɛ sutiye ma yɩ. Mmilɩ mɔ Gyehohyafatɩ yi kunlu duli nɩ, ɩbɩbɛlɩ Gyehova yɩ ɛkɛnɩ ɔhvanɩhilelɛ. (2 Mm. 20:3) Nɩ ɩdɩ Gyehohyafatɩ yi subanɩ kɛɛ ɩkɛbɩbɛ Gyehova yɩ ɛkɛnɩ mmʋwalɩyɛ. (2 Mm. 19:3) Meti, mmilɩ mɔ bʋwabɩlɛ yɩ nu ya yɛlɩ sɩ nɩ, nɩ izi kɛɛ ihyinyia Gyehova yɩ mmʋwalɩyɛ. Gyehohyafatɩ zili yɩ kɛnya kɛɛ ɩkɛyɛ sutiye na ɩava ɔhvanɩhilelɛ bela mɔɔ Gyehova kɛva kɛma yɩ nɩ ɩayɛ egyima. Na akʋ̃ ɩzʋ̃ yɩ ahʋnlɩnɩ munwala nu ɩwɔlɩ Gyeho-va mpayɩ kɛɛ ɩwʋwa yɩ. (Kɩnga 2 Mmılɩzʋdwokɔ 20:5, 6, 10-12.) Sa yɛkʋla yesumazuma Gyeho-hyafatɩ yi ndianwʋ yɩ mmilɩ mɔ yeyiyiaa ndwokɔ-zɩzɩbɛ nɩ. Yɛkɔhʋla yekezi yɛ kɛnya kɛɛ yɛkɛyɛ su-tiye yɛkɛma Gyehova, yɛkɛbɩbɛ yɩ ɔhvanɩhilelɛ iluwa mpayɩ nɩɩ Bayɩbʋlʋ mɔɔ yekozuma nɩ zʋ, na yenyia yi nu anwʋlazʋ kɛɛ ɩkɔwʋwa yɛ.—Ndw. 119:112.
