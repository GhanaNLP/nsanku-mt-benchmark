6 Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ wɔ awielɩyɛ mmɩlɩ nu nɩ mmenii ‘kebigyia begyia bɔ nwʋ sʋlɔ, bɛkɛyɛ ahantanɩ,’ na bɛkɛyɛ mmenii mɔɔ “bɛáhʋla bɛ nu bɛ yɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ.” (2 Tim. 3:1-3) Nnɛ mmenii sũmaa da ɔzɔ subanɩ yɩ be ali wɔ bo ndwɔkɔhanlɛ nu. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, ɩyɛ a yɛtɩ news mɔɔ ɩfalɩ mbevilelilɛ be mɔɔ ɩhɔ iwieli kʋnɛ nu. Wɔ mmusunwa sũmaa nu nɩ, bɛtaa bɔwʋdʋwʋdʋ bɔ nwʋ. Ndɛnɩ evinli wulu ɔzʋlɔ nkɔ̃ɔ̃ ma nnwuakula nɩɩ mbanɩmbanɩ munwala kã ebiye. (Ɛfɩ. 5:3, 4) Mmenii mɔɔ bɛkã ɔzɔ ndɛnɩ evinli nɩ da oyuwadɩ yɩ sunzuma nɩ ali.

7 Kɛ Kilisinyiamaa nɩ ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛyɛ ndianwʋba wɔ yɛ ndwɔkɔhanlɛ nu wɔ mmɩlɩ mɔ yɛ nɩɩ abiye tɩndɛɛ anzɛnyɩɩ yɛkɛlɛɛ debe wɔ yɛ foonu nɩɩ yɛ kɔmpiyuta zʋ yɛkɛma abiye nɩ. (Gye. 1:26, 27) Sa yɛkʋla yebizia yɛ nwʋ ndwɔkɔ eyimmɔ mɔɔ belili ɔzʋlɔ yɩ yɛfa yɛpɩbɛlɩ yɛ ndwɔkɔhanlɛ nu: ‘Sɛ abiye yɛ debe ma mɩfa ɛya, na sɛ ɔzɔ enii ɔkɔ̃ nɩ ízi kɛɛ mɩdɩ Gyehova Dasɩvʋlɔ a, asʋ̃ mɩkã ndɛnɩ evinli mikile yɩ a? Sɛ mɩ nɩɩ mmeliyema bɔɔ nkɔmmɔ a, asʋ̃ mɩkã mmenii mvʋlɔ nwʋ ndwɔkɔ tanɩ anzɛnyɩɩ midi bɔ nwʋ nwɔwɩ wɔ ɔhvanɩ mɔɔ ɩávata zʋ a? Asʋ̃ mɩtɩndɛ basabasa mikile mu mmusunwamaa a? Sɛ bɛkã kɛɛ mu mmusunwamaa bɛhã debe bɛvalɩ mu munwa ndɛnɩ nwʋ a, mɩnla yɛ bɛkɛhã a?’

8. Kɛ ɩkɛyɛ na yeahwĩ yɛ nwʋ wɔ ndɛnɩ evinli nwʋ a? (Kolosiyiamaa 3:8-10)
<<<P>>>
ma yɩ ali wɔ yɛ ndwɔkɔhanlɛ nu a, mɩnla yɛ yɛkɔhʋla yɛkɛyɛ a? Ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛkã ndwɔkɔ mɔɔ ɩdɩ baaba. Naasʋ̃ nɩ yelɛwieli yi. Kakɛ mɔɔ Gyisɛsɩ hanlɩ nɩ. Lhanlɩ kɛɛ: “Mɔɔ ɩzʋ̃ enii yi munwa nu kevinde nɩ, ɩzʋ̃ yɩ ahʋnlɩnɩ nu. Na ɔzɔ nninyeni nɩ mmɔ yɛ bokua enii nwʋ evinli a.”(Mt. 15:18) Kɛmɔ Gyisɛsɩ hanlɩ nɩ, ye munwa ndɛnɩ yɛ ɩma kezimɔ yɛdwĩ na yɛtɩ nyanlɩ nɩ da ali a. Eyi ti yɛ Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ fa “mmuzulodwɔkɔ” kʋnla ɛya nɩɩ ɛdanɩlɛ nwʋ yɩ a. (Ɛfɩ. 4:31, NWT) Sɛ ɛba nwʋ kɛɛ ɔwɔ ɔzɔ subanɩ nɩ be wɔ wu nu a, siã Gyehova kɛɛ ɩwʋwá wu ma ɔhʋla wula “subanɩ fʋlɔ” nɩ. (Kɩnga Kolosiyiamaa 3:8-10.) Sa ɔkʋla yɛ ɛbɩbɛlɛnu wɔ yɛ awuuku azʋlɔtʋ yɩ nu falɩ ɔzɔ odwɔkɔtile yɩ nwʋ. Si wu kɛnya kɛɛ eléma oyuwadɩ yɩ sunzuma yɩ kenyia wu subanɩ anzɛnyɩɩ wu munwa ndɛnɩ zʋ tumi.—Ɛfɩ. 4:29.

9 Dɩyɛ fʋlɔ mɔɔ mmenii yɛ mɔɔ ɩma yɛnwʋ kɛɛ oyuwadɩ yɩ sunzuma yɩ yɛ ikilee ɔhvanɩ bɛ wɔ nnɛ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ bɛáva Gyehova yɩ mmɛla mɔɔ ɩfalɩ baaba nɩɩ tanɩ nwʋ nɩ bɛyɛ hwɩɩ. Na mmenii sũmaa bu yi kɛɛ mfʋmɩ bela íni nwʋmɔ kɛɛ abiye kɛyɩ ɛyɩnzɛlɛ kogua, benyia nu benyia anzɛnyɩɩ baalɛ nu baalɛ kɛla anzɛnyɩɩ kɛɛ abiye nɩɩ abiye kɛla ansaana bɛagyia. (Ayɩ. 5:20) Sɛ yɛákila yɛ nwʋ bʋwɔ a, ngyɩkĩĩ ngyɩkĩĩ la yɛkenyia adwɩnɩ mɔɔ bɛlɛ yɩ nɩ be na ɩléma yɛkehyi tanɩ. (2 Kɔl. 11:3) Yɛkɔhʋla yɛkɛdɩ nyanlɩ po kɛɛ, mɔɔ oyuwadɩmaa bɛyɛ nɩ yɛ idwu zʋ a, na Bayɩbʋlʋ nu ndwɔkɔ mɛyɩ ɩba nwʋmɔ. Naasʋ̃ eyi ɩáyɛ nɔhvanlɛ!
