ɩvalɩ Gyisɛsɩ ɩmanlɩ yɛ kɛɛ Ɔsɔfʊ Ebani yɩ a. Sɛ yɛbɔ Gyehova mpayɩ a, yɩ anyɩ de baaba. Sɛ yɛbɔ mpayɩ a, ikile kɛɛ yɛwɔ Gyehova nu anwʊlazʊ na yɛfa yɛ nwʊ yetĩã yɩ zʊ. Iyɛ a yɛánwʊ kezimɔ yɛhã ye odwokɔ, na ɩyɛ sʊ̃ a adwɩnɩndwɩnɩ kʊla hyɛ zʊ nkɔ̃ɔ̃ ma yɛánwʊ mɔɔ yɛhã yehile Gyehova po. (Wul. 8:26) Naasʊ̃ Gyehova tiye yɛ, ɩtɩ yɛ tʊ, na ɩbʊwa yɛ. Íni kɛɛ yɛma debela si yɛ ɔhwanɩ kɛɛ yɛ nɩ yɛ sʊlɔ Ezilɛ mɔɔ ɩdɔ yɛ na yi kunlu ɩdɩyɛnɛ nɩ kɔwɔ yɛ ɔhawʊ nwʊ nkɔmmɔ.

14 Azɔfʊlilɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ nɩ ma yɛnwʊ kɛɛ Gyehova pɛ kɛɛ yenyia nnwʊanlɩ mɔɔ íni awielɩyɛ. (Izi. 33:11) Nnamɩ mɔɔ bɛva bɔwɔlɩ afɔlɩ wɔ Bayɩbʊlʊ mmɩlɩ nu nɩ, tákʊla ɩyɩ Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ azɔ̃mɩvʊlɔ bɛ tanɩ wɔ kɛnɩ kʊlaa kʊlaa. (Hib. 7:19) Naasʊ̃ Gyehova luwalɩ ɔzɔ afɔlɩwɔlɛ yɩ zʊ wʊwalɩ yɩ azɔ̃mɩvʊlɔ ma bɔnwʊnlɩ kɛɛ behyinyia afɔlɩwɔlɛ mɔɔ idi munli. Na mɛyɩ yɛ lɛ Gyisɛsɩ Kɩlayɩsɩ yɩ nnwʊanlɩ mɔɔ idi munli nɩ. Iluwa yɩ afɔlɩwɔlɛ yɩ zʊ nɩ, ɩma yenyia tanɩfakyɛ ankasa. Iwuko ɔnwʊmɔ ɔhwanɩ ɩma adasamaa mɔɔ bɛyɛ sutɩye nɩ kɛɛ bekenyia nnwʊanlɩ mɔɔ íni awielɩyɛ. Yɛla Gyehova asɩ paa kɛɛ ɩyɛlɩ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ mɔɔ ɩkɛma yɛkenyia nnwʊanlɩ mɔɔ íni awielɩyɛ nɩ!—1 Dwɔ. 4:10.

15 Sɛ yekila mmenii mɔɔ Gyehova ɩyɩ bɛ kɛɛ azɔfʊ nɩ a, ɩma yɛnwʊ kɛɛ Gyehova dɔ yɛ paa. Isɛ kɛɛ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɔvɔ ngɔlɔ bɛ nɩ kɔ zʋlɔ di nɔhvanlɛ kɔpʊmɩ owulo nu dɩkɛɛ be Niibenyia Gyisɛsɩ. (Fɩl. 2:8; Yik. 2:10) Ɔzɔ mɔɔ bɛkɛyɛ nɩ kɛma abiyela kɔnwʊ kɛɛ belili nɔhvanlɛ bɛmanlɩ Gyehova. Meti sa yɛkʊla yenyia anwʊlazʊ kɛɛ wɔ Alvɓɔ Abʋmɩ Tumililɛ yɩ nu nɩ, sa bɔzʊ̃ ɔlɔlɛ nu bɔbʊwa yɛ ma yɛyɛ mɔɔ ɩtɩngɛ na ngyɩkĩĩ ngyɩkĩĩ la bɛawʊwa yɛ ma yɛali munli. Akʊ̃ sʊ̃, sa bɔbʊwa yɛ ma yede yɛ nwʊ wɔ tanɩ mɔɔ yenyianlɩ wɔ Adamɩ nɩ Yifi bɛ kɛnɩ nɩ nu. Nninyeniba eyimmɔ munwala kɛya numɔ iluwa Gyisɛsɩ yɩ afɔlɩwɔlɛ yɩ ti. Gyisɛsɩ nɩɩ mmenii 144,000 mɔɔ bɛkɩnla yɩ nwʊ nɩ kɛyɛ nnwʊánlɩ nninyeni eyimmɔ kɔbʊmɩ kɛɛ Gyehova yɩ anvhyba mɔɔ ɩwɔ ɩvalɩ adasamaa nɩɩ paladayɩsɩ nwʊ nɩ munwatĩĩ kɛya numɔ.—Yik. 22:1, 2.

16 Yɛ Ɔsɔfʊ Ebani Gyisɛsɩ zi kezimɔ yɛtɩ nyanlɩ nɩ buwɔ paa, iluwakɛɛ ɩya ɩwʊwalɩ azɩlɛ yɩ zʊ kɛɛ enii. (Kɩnga Hibulumaa 4:15.) Kɛ ndianwʊ nɩ, asʊ̃ ɩyɛ a ɛtɩ nyanlɩ kɛɛ ɛvɛ a? Gyisɛsɩ sʊ̃ dɩlɩ nyanlɩ zɔ. (Dwɔ. 4:6) Asʊ̃ kɔmɩ anzɛnyɩɩ ohobe hũ wʊ kyĩ a? Ebiye dʊ Gyisɛsɩ kyĩ. (Mt. 4:2; Dwɔ. 19:28) Iyɛ a abiyemmɔ sɩlɩ wʊ anzɛnyɩɩ botwodwu betiyia wʊ iluwa wu dedi ti a? Gyisɛsɩ sʊ̃ yiyialɩ bʊwabɩlɛ mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ. (Ayɩ. 50:6; Dwɔ. 15:20) Asʊ̃ wʊ dɔvʊlɔ be wu kyĩ a? Gyisɛsɩ sʊ̃, yɩ ɔhɔnwʊ Lazalɔsʊ wuli, na ɩkɛyɛ kɛɛ yɩ papa Dwosefi sʊ̃ wuli. Meti sa yɛkʊla yenyia anwʊlazʊ kɛɛ yɛ Ɔsɔfʊ Ebani Gyisɛsɩ kɛdɩ yɛ tʊ na ɩawʊwa yɛ iluwakɛɛ iluwa bʊwabɩlɛ eyimmɔ munwatĩĩ nu kyĩ.

17 Mmenii 144,000 yɩ sʊ̃ kɛla kunlumiba nɩɩ ntɩyası ali mmɩlɩ mɔ bɛ nɩɩ Gyisɛsɩ kɔwɔ numɔ kɔwʊwa ma enii keli munli nɩ. Nɩ bɛzabɛ sʊ̃ bɛáli munli kɛmɔ amɩyɛ sʊ̃ yeáli munli na tanɩ wɔ yɛ nwʊ nɩ la. Bezi kezimɔ yɛtɩ nyanlɩ mmɩlɩ mɔ yɛyɛ tanɩ be ma yɛ tiwʊwaba ididee yɛ nɩ. Bezi kezimɔ anwʊbalɛ dɩ nɩɩ kezimɔ abiye tɩ nyanlɩ mmɩlɩ mɔ adwɩnɩndwɩnɩ hyɛ yɩ zʊ nɩ. Akʊ̃ sʊ̃, bo munwatĩĩ beábuwa azɩ wɔ mmɩlɩ kʊ̃ nu, na bɔzʊ̃ nnɩka azvlvtv nɩɩ mmusunwa azvlvtv nu. Na ɩsʊnɩ bo nu bela nɩ yɩ bʊwabɩlɛ sʊ̃. Kezimɔ wʊ bʊwabɩlɛ yɩ dɩ bela nɩ, abiye wɔ mmenii 144,000 yɩ nu mɔɔ ɩkɛdɩ wʊ tʊ.
