15. Mɩnla yɛ osuma wɔ Gloria yɩ ozumanlɛnwʋnlɛ yɩ nu a? (Kila mfonyini yi sɔ̃.)
<<<P>>>
manlɛnwʋnlɛ yɩ. Izumanlɩ nɔhʋanlɛ yɩ wɔ yɩ nnwuakula nu, naasʋ̃ ɩmanlɩ dimalelɛ nɩ ozu-kuabɛlɛ nyianlɩ yɩ zʋ tumi. Ihanlɩ kɛɛ: “Nɩ mɩpɛ kɛɛ mɩyɛ ozukuavʋlɔ. Iluwa yɩ zɔ ti, mɩwɔlɩ yɩ tʋ kɛɛ mitindiãã dɩyɛzumanlɛhɔlɛ nu na idwuli dɩka be po nɩ, nɩ mɩábɛ yɩ kɛɛ mɩyɛ dasɩvʋlɔ dɔkʋ̃.” Naasʋ̃ ayinzi yi, Gloria ɣa nwʋnlɩ yɩ kɛɛ ɩma oyuwadɩ yɩ sunzuma yɩ inyia yɩ zʋ tumi, meti mɔɔ ɩva ɔzɔ nninyeni izi-ye yɩ anyɩ zʋ yɩ a. Mɩnla yɛ ɩyɛlɩ a? Ihanlɩ kɛɛ: “Mɩwɔlɩ yɩ tʋ kɛɛ mɩfaa mɩ Bayɩbʋlʋ ɛyɩnga-lɛ nɩ mpayɩwɔlɛ anyɩnlɩbʋlʋ na mmenii mɔɔ bɛázʋ̃mɩ Gyehova nɩ, mɩ nɩɩ bɛ dualɩ ɔhɔ-nwʋvalɛ nu.
