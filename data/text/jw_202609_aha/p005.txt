14. Mɩnla ɔhvanɩ zʊ yɛ sunzuma nwanzanwanza yɩ kɔ-hʊla kɔwʊwa yɛ a?
<<<P>>>
yɛ sunzuma nwanzanwanza yɩ kɔhʊla kɔwʊwa yɛ a? Sɔ̃mavʊlɔ Pɔɔlʊ hanlɩ kɛɛ: ‘bɛmá Nyamɩnlɩ yi sunzuma nɩ ihile bɛ ɔhvanɩ, na máma bedi bɔ nnwʊnlamɩ akɔnɔ yɩ mɛda.’ (Gal. 5:16) Nkyɩkyɛmi eyi falɩ abiye mɔɔ ɩbɔɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ikediye Nyamɩnlɩ na ɩava yɩ ɔhvanthilelɛ ɩawɔ yɩ ɛbɩla nɩ nwʊ. Meti sɛ yɛzayɛ sʊ̃ yɛyɛ zɔ a, Gyehova kɛva yi sunzuma yɩ kɔwʊwa yɛ. Na sa ɩkʊla ɩbʊwa yɛ ma yɛdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ nninyeniba nwʊ na yɛawɔ ɛbɩla baaba sʊ̃. Meti sɛ yenyia akɔ-nɔ tanɩ a, Nyamɩnlɩ yi sunzuma yɩ kɔwʊwa yɛ ma yɛkɔbʊ yɩ ndɛndɛ zʊ la. Iwɔ numɔ, Gyehova táva yi sunzuma yɩ ɩyí akɔnɔ tanɩ wɔ yɛ nu. Naasʊ̃ sa ɩkʊla ɩfa ɩbʊwa yɛ ma yenyia anwʊzɩzɩbɛ nɩɩ ɛpɛ mɔɔ yɛkɛva kɛyɛ mɔɔ ɩtɩnyɛ na ɩahwĩ yɛ nwʊ wɔ tanɩ nwʊ. Sɛ yɛyɛ zɔ a, ɩkɔwɔ yɛ nwʊ banɩ wɔ sunzuma ɔfʊã nu.
