mɔɔ Gyisɛsɩ va manlɩ yɩ nʊ kɩla. (Kɩnga Matiyu 4:5-7.) Setanɩ hwɩnlɩ adwɩnɩ gyinanlɩ odwokɔ mɔɔ ɩwɔ Ndwomi 91:11, 12 yɩ zʊ kɛɛ ɩdɩ nnwʊnlamɩ ɔfvã nʊ banɩwɔlɛ nwʊ anyhʊba. Meti ɩhanlɩ ihileli Gyisɛsɩ kɛɛ, ɩzɔ̃ asɔlɩ aliyia nɩ yi ti svlɔ dɛkɛdɛkɛ nɩ zʊ ɩdʊ́ yɩ nwʊ idiã azɩ na ihila kɛɛ awɔvvlɔ yɩ kɔwɔ yɩ nwʊ banɩ a, iluwakɛɛ bɔwɔ anyhʊba kɛɛ bɔkɔwɔ yɩ nwʊ banɩ na iábilalɩ. Mɩnla odwokɔ yɛ Gyisɛsɩ hanlɩ a? lákã kɛɛ: ‘Setanɩ, mɔɔ ɛkãã yɩ, kɛ ɔzɔ yɛ ɩdɩ a! Kɛ nnwʊnlamɩ ɔfvã nʊ banɩwɔlɛ yɛ ndwomi yi ɩkãã nwʊmɔ odwokɔ a.’ Mfomi Gyisɛsɩ wʊwalɩ yɩ kɛɛ: ‘Ɛhɛlɛlɛ nɩ se, íni kɛɛ otwe Gyehova wʊ Nyamɩnlɩ nɩ ekila.’ Mɩnla yɛ yesuma wɔ munwalɩyɛ mɔɔ Gyisɛsɩ va manlɩ yɩ nu a?

9 Nɩ Gyisɛsɩ zi paa kɛɛ ɩyɛ a yɩ Zɩ duwa yɩ awɔvvlɔ zʊ bɔ yi mmenii nwʊ banɩ wɔ asianɩ mɔɔ bɛ adamvvlɔ fa ba bɔ zʊ nɩ nwʊ. (2 Aw. 6:15-17; 19:34, 35; Da. 3:19, 20, 26-28; 6:16-22) Naasʊ̃ Gyisɛsɩ iátwe yɩ Zɩ ikila, iluwakɛɛ nɩ izi kɛɛ ɩlɛ́yɛ baaba kɛɛ ɩkɛhã kehile yɩ Zɩ kɛɛ ɩwɔ yɩ nwʊ banɩ wɔ mmɩlɩ mɔ iáye yɩ dɩyɛ wɔ nyɛlɛbɛ nu nɩ. Meti mmɩlɩ mɔ Setanɩ hwɩnlɩ adwɩnɩ gyinanlɩ Ndwomi 91 zʊ va hanlɩ kɛɛ Gyehova bɔ yi mmenii nwʊ banɩ wɔ nnwʊnlamɩ ɔfvã nʊ nɩ, nɩ ilédi awohwi. Naasʊ̃ mfʊmɩ mɔɔ ilili yɛ lɛ ɛhanlɛ mɔɔ tĩa ihileli Gyisɛsɩ kɛɛ ɩyɛ́ debe mɔɔ nyɛlɛbɛ íni numɔ ɩva idwe Gyehova ihila nɩ.* Munwalɩyɛ mɔɔ Gyisɛsɩ va manlɩ Setanɩ nɩ kile kɛɛ Gyehova wɔ anyhʊba kɛɛ ɩkɔwɔ yi mmenii nwʊ banɩ kɛɛ okuwu mmɩlɩ munwala. Meti, ɩkɔwɔ yɛ nwʊ banɩ wɔ sunzuma ɔfvã nu, nnwʊnlamɩ ɔfvã nu, nʊ nyanlɩdɩlɛ ɔfvã nu.

* Mmɩlɩ be mɔ ɩɣa ɩzĩ ɩhɔ nɩ, yɛ awuuku yi be hilehileli numɔ kɛɛ, Ndwomi 91 nɩ falɩ kezimɔ Gyehova bɔ anyhʊba kɛɛ ɩkɔwɔ yɛ nwʊ banɩ wɔ sunzuma ɔfvã nu nkʊ la nwʊ. Akʊ̃ sɔ̃ ɩhanlɩ kɛɛ, kezimɔ Setanɩ hwɩnlɩ adwɩnɩ ziyeli Ndwomi 91 zʊ kɛɛ Gyehova ɩwɔ anyhʊba kɛɛ ɩkɔwɔ yɩ azũmɩvvlɔ nwʊ banɩ wɔ nnwʊnlamɩ ɔfvã nu asianɩ nwʊ nɩ, ídɩ nɔhuanlɛ. Kila Ɔwɛnlele Aɣanɩ mɔɔ ɩɣalɩ wɔ December 1, 1974, owu. 722.

11 Gyehova bɔ yɛ nwʊ banɩ wɔ sunzuma ɔfvã nu. Izi kɛɛ yɛáli munli, na íla azɩ ɩma yɛ kɛɛ yɛkɛyɛ nninyeni mɔɔ yɩ anyɩ deli nwʊmɔ na yeahyi nninyeni mɔɔ ikyi nɩ. (Wul. 6:16; 7:21-23) Meti ɩyɛ nninyeni pɔkyɩɩ be ɩbʊwa yɛ. Eyi bʊwa yɛ ma yɛyɛ nninyeni mɔɔ yɩ anyɩ deli nwʊmɔ na ama yeahɔ zʊlɔ yɛayɛ yɩ ɔhɔnwʊmmɔ. Bɛmá yezunzu ɔzɔ nninyeni yi be nwʊ.
<<<P>>>
12 Yi odwokɔ Bayɩbʊlv yɩ. Sɛ yekila Bayɩbʊlʊ yi nu a, Gyehova bɔ yɛ kɔkɔ wɔ nninyeni tanɩ mɔɔ ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛtwĩ yɛ nwʊ wɔ nwʊmɔ nɩ ɔhvanɩ mɔɔ yekoluwa zʊlɔ yɛkɛyɛ zɔ nɩ nwʊ. Kɛ ndianwʊ nɩ, kila mɔɔ Gyisɛsɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛyɛ wɔ Matiyu 5:28, 29, NWT. (Kɩnga.) Wɔ nkyɩkyɛmi eyimmɔ nu nɩ, Gyisɛsɩ hileli yɛ kɛɛ, benyia bela mɔɔ ɩkɔ zʊlɔ ikila baalɛ mɔɔ ídɩ yɩ yɩ na ikenyia akɔnnɔ ɩkɛma yɩ nɩ, ɩ nu yɩ zikɛ agyiahʊnlɩ wɔ yɩ ahʊnlɩnɩ nu daada. Mɩnla yɛ yesuma wɔ odwokɔ eyi nu a?
