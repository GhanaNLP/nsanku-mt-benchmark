12 Ye munwala yenyia mmenii mɔɔ bekile ndɛnɩ be tʋ bɔkɔ ndɛnɩ fʋlɔ nu be egyima zʋ mfasʋ. Bayɩbulu tʋhilelɛ ídɩ dɩyɛ fʋlɔ.* Ndɛnɩ tʋhilelɛmaa yɛ egyima ɛzɩzɩbɛ na ama mmenii munwala beadɩ Nyamɩnlɩ yi odwɔkɔ yɩ. Ayile asã yɩ, iluwakɛɛ behile New World Translation of the Holy Scriptures yɩ tʋ bɔhɔ ndɛnɩ sũmaa nu nɩ ti, ɩma Gyehova yi mmenii bɛ adwɩnɩ yiyia wɔ Bayɩbulu nu nɔhwanlɛ odwɔkɔ nwʋ. Kila mɔɔ asafo nu ebani be mɔɔ ɩwɔ Nepal hã valɩ Bayɩbulu mɔɔ behile yɩ tʋ nɩ nwʋ. Ihanlɩ kɛɛ: “Bayɩbulu mɔɔ mɩkɩnga wɔ mɩzamɩ mɩ ndɛnɩ nu nɩ ma mʋnwʋ kɛɛ Gyehova wɔ kɛ ankasa. Ima miyike yi na ɩhyɛ mɩ dedi ɛzɩzɩbɛ sɔ̃.” Sa yɛkula yenyia New World Translation munli yi anzɛnyɩɩ yɩ ɔfʋã be wɔ ndɛnɩ mɔɔ ɩbʋ 300 nu. Iluwakɛɛ Gyehova yi mmenii fa Bayɩbulu eyi mɔɔ numɔ la kɛ, bɔkʋla bɛfa bɔ nwʋ betiã zʋ, na numɔ ndwɔkɔ dɩ pɛpɛɛpɛ ɩávalɩ nwʋmɔ ndɛnɩ mɔɔ behile yɩ tʋ bɔhɔ numɔ nɩ di egyima nɩ ti, ɩma bɛyɛ ɔkʋ̃ wɔ be ehilehilelɛ nu. (1 Kɔl. 1:10) Bɛmá yehila nni-nyeni mvʋlɔ mɔɔ Bayɩbulu eyi kula yɛ.

13 Bɛyɩ Nyamɩnlɩ yi dima yɩ wɔ Bayɩbulu sũmaa mɔɔ bɔwɔ kɛnɩ nnɛ nɩ nu. Naasũ New World Translation nɩ fa Nyamɩnlɩ yi dima Gyehova di egyima mbɔmɩ sũmaa. Wɔ alʋbɔ yaalɛ mɔɔ idua nu nɩ, nɩ ɩáyɛ sɩ kɛɛ abiye kɔnwʋ Nyamɩnlɩ yi dima Gyehova wɔ Bayɩbulu sũmaa nu. Nɩ ɩta ifinde wɔ Hibulu Ɛhɛlɛlɛdwɔkɔ yi nu. Naasũ nnɛ, nyamɩnlɩzʋ̃mɩnlɛ nu mbanimbani yɩ mɔɔ bese bɛdɩ Kilisinyiamaa nɩ beábe kɛɛ bɛfa Nyamɩnlɩ yi dima Gyehova nɩ bedi egyima na beábe sũ kɛɛ mmenii kɔnwʋ dima yɩ. Katɩlɩkɩ Asɔlɩ yɩ nu mbanimbani yi bɛyɩ Nyamɩnlɩ yi dima yɩ wɔ bɛ Bayɩbulu nu bɛ awuuku azʋlɔtʋ nu.* Ɔzɔ sũ la yɛɛ nsɔlɩ nsɔlɩ azʋlɔtʋ sũ bɛyɩ Nyamɩnlɩ yi dima wɔ bɛ Bayɩbulu yɩ nu a. Naasũ, wɔ New World Translation yi nu mɛyɩ, bɛva Gyehova yi dima bowula nnɩka mɔɔ ɩsɛ kɛɛ bɛfa bowula nɩ wɔ ndɛnɩ bela mɔɔ behile yɩ tʋ bɔhɔ numɔ nɩ nu. Mɩnla ti mɔɔ eyi nwʋ hyinyia a?
