BAYIBƲLƲ NU ODWOKƆ BE
<<<P>>>
‘Bɛmá Azũnudwolɩyɛ Nɩ Mɔɔ Gyisɛsɩ Kɩlayɩsɩ ɩva ɩma Bɛ Nɩ Ili Bulombunli Wɔ Bɛ Ahʋnlɩnɩ Nu’
<<<P>>>
Kolosiyiamaa 3:15 kã ‘azũnudwolɩyɛ nɩ mɔɔ Gyisɛsɩ Kɩlayɩsɩ ɩva ɩma yɛ’ nɩ nwʋ odwokɔ. Mɩnla yɛ lɛ ‘azũnudwolɩyɛ nɩ mɔɔ Gyisɛsɩ Kɩlayɩsɩ ɩva ɩma yɛ’ nɩ a? Idí azũnudwolɩyɛ be mɔɔ abiye nyia wɔ mmɩlɩ mɔ ɩɣa ɩyɛ Kɩlayɩsɩ yɩ tʋwavʋlɔ nɩ. Yenyia ɔzɔ azũnudwolɩyɛ yɩ mmɩlɩ mɔ yɛɣa yɛnwʋ kɛɛ Gyehova nɩɩ yɩ Ɣa yɩ dɔ yɛ nɩ.—Dwɔ. 14:23, 27.
<<<P>>>
Sɔ̃mavʋlɔ Pɔɔlʋ hyɛlɩ Kilisinyiamaa mɔɔ nɩ bɔwɔ Kolosiyia asafo yi nu ɛzɩzɩbɛ kɛɛ bɛmá ɔzɔ azũnudwolɩyɛ nɩ ili bulombunli wɔ bɛ ahʋnlɩnɩ nu anzɛnyɩɩ bo nu. Mɩnla ɔhʋanɩ zʋ a? Giliki odwokɔpɔkɔ “ilí bulombunli” mɔɔ Pɔɔlʋ va lili egyima wɔ ɛkɩ nɩ, bɛfa betindĩã abiye mɔɔ idii referee wɔ ɛɣɛlelilɛ be nu nɩ nwʋ. Kɛmɔ referee be wɔ tumi wɔ ɛɣɛlelilɛ be zʋ nɩ, ɔzɔ la sʋ̃ yɛ ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛma ‘azũnudwolɩyɛ nɩ mɔɔ Gyisɛsɩ Kɩlayɩsɩ ɩva ɩma yɛ’ nɩ nyia yɛ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ zʋ tumi a.
<<<P>>>
Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, sɛ akasakasa be twodwu wɔ yɛ nɩɩ yɛ anlima Kilisinyianii be avinli a, ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛma ‘azũnudwolɩyɛ nɩ mɔɔ Gyisɛsɩ Kɩlayɩsɩ ɩva ɩma yɛ’ nɩ nyia yɛ zʋ tumi. Sɛ yɛyɛ zɔ a, ɩkɔwʋwa yɛ ma yɛkɔnwʋ mɔɔ yɛkɛyɛ na azũnudwolɩyɛ nɩɩ ɔkʋ̃yɛlɛ ɩawʋwa yɛ nɩɩ yɛ mmeliyema avinli. Mɔɔ ɩkɛyɛ na yɛahʋla yɛayɛ zɔ nɩ, ɩtwu mmɩlɩ be a, ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛfa mɔɔ yɛpɛ nɩ yetia ahanlɩ, anzɛnyɩɩ yɛzũ yɛ ahʋnlɩnɩ nu yɛfa tanɩ yɛkyɛ. Eyi kehile kɛɛ yɛmaa ‘azũnudwolɩyɛ nɩ mɔɔ Gyisɛsɩ Kɩlayɩsɩ ɩva ɩma yɛ’ nɩ keli bulombunli wɔ yɛ ahʋnlɩnɩ nu.—Kol. 3:12-14.
