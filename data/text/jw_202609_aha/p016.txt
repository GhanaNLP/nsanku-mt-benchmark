7 Gyehova yi mmenii lɛ nninyeni sũmaa mɔɔ ɩbʋwa bɛ ma bedi mmenii munwatĩĩ dasɩlɛ ɩávalɩ nwʋmɔ ndɛnɩ mɔɔ bɛkã nɩ. Awɔvʋlɔ yɩ bʋwa Gyehova yi mmenii yi ma bɛfa ɔkʋ̃yɛ-lɛ ‘bɔbɔ odwokɔba yɩ ɛlawulo bekile manlɩ bela nɩɩ abusunwa bela nɩɩ ɛdɩndɛlɛ bela nɩɩ mmenii bela.’ (Kɩnga Yikile 14:6.) Sɛ yɛnwʋ kezimɔ nkɔmɩhyɛ mɔɔ Zakaliyia nɩɩ Dwɔɔnʋ hɛlɛlɩ nɩ ɩbaa numɔ a, ɩma yenyia dedi kezi-le kɛɛ nkɔmɩhyɛ fʋlɔ bela mɔɔ bɛhɛlɛ yɩ wɔ Bayɩbʋlʋ yi nu nɩ kɛya nu. Eyi kã yɛ ma yɛkɔ zʋlɔ yɛfʋãmɩ Gyehova yɩ ahyɩhyɛdɩ yɩ nwʋ iluwakɛɛ yɛnwʋ kɛɛ mɛyɩ yɛ Gyehova luwa zʋlɔ ɩmaa mmenii munwatĩĩ kɛdɩ odwokɔba yɩ a.

8 Wɔ Pɛntɩkɔsʋ 33 Y.M. nu nɩ, Gyehova yɛlɩ dɩyɛ be mɔɔ ɩma yɛnwʋ kɛɛ mɔɔ ɩmanlɩ izili mmɩlɩ mɔ nɩ bekyinaa Babɛlɩ Bɔlɔnsanɩ Tɩndɩnlɩ nɩ, sa ɩkʋla ɩyɛ nwʋmɔ debe. Iluwalɩ nnwʋánlɩ ɔhʋanɩ zʋ ɩmanlɩ Gyisɛsɩ yɩ mɛdali-lɛmaa yɩ hanlɩ ndɛnɩ azʋlɔtʋ mɔɔ nɩ bɛádɩ. (Egy. 2:4-10) Mɩnla ti mɔɔ ɩyɛlɩ zɔ a? Iluwakɛɛ nɩ ɩpɛ kɛɛ ɩma mmenii mɔɔ bɔzʋ̃ oyuwadɩ yɩ nnɩka azʋlɔtʋ mɔɔ bɛya beyiyia numɔ wɔ Gye-lusalamɩ asɔlɩ pika yi nu kɛɛ bɛbɛazũmɩ yɩ nɩ tɩ odwokɔba yɩ wɔ bɛzabɛ bɛ ndɛnɩ nu. (Egy. 2:11) Nnɛ, yɛ awuuku azʋlɔtʋ nɩ bʋwa yɛ ma yɛkã odwokɔba mɔɔ ide nnwʋanlɩ nɩ yekile ahʋnlɩnɩbamaa wɔ bɛzabɛ bɛ ndɛnɩ nu.
