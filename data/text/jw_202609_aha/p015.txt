amanlɩ amanlɩ bɛ ndɛnɩ azvlɔtv nɩ kɔzvzɔ Dwuunii ɔkʊ̃ yɩ atalɩ nu kɛhã kɛɛ: “Yɛpɛ kɛɛ yɛ nɩ bɛ kɔ iluwakɛɛ yɛdɩ kɛɛ Nyamɩnlɩ kĩnla bɔ nwʊ.”’” (Zak. 8:23) Nkɔmɩhyɛ eyi ɩbaa numɔ wɔ yɛ mmɩlɩ yɩ nu. Bɛmá yehila mɔɔ ye nu ɔkʊ̃ bela kɔhʊla kɛyɛ kɔwʊwa.

3 “Dwuunii” mɔɔ bɛhanlɩ yɩ nwʊ odwokɔ wɔ Zakaliyia yɩ nkɔmɩhyɛ nu nɩ gyĩ kɛnɩ ma bɛzabɛ mɔɔ Nyamɩnlɩ va sunzuma nwanzanwanza vɔ ngɔlɔ bɛ nɩ. Besia sʊ̃ bɔfʊlɔ bɛ kɛɛ “Nyamɩnlɩ Yizilaya.” (Gal. 6:16, NWT) Mmenii eyi nɩ Gyisɛsɩ keli bulombunli wɔ sulɔ. (Hib. 3:1) “Mmenii bunlu” yɩ sʊ̃ gyĩ kɛnɩ ma kãnwʊazɩmaa mɔɔ bɔwɔ anyɩnlɩlazʊ kɛɛ bɔkɔwʊwa azɩ daabaa wɔ Paladayɩsɩ azɩlɛ yɩ zʊ nɩ. Mmenii eyi zʊ̃ amanlɩ azʊlɔtv nu, na bɛkã ndɛnɩ azvlɔtv sʊ̃.

4 Wɔ 1919 nu nɩ, Gyisɛsɩ yɩlɩ mmenyia nɔhvanlɛmaa mɔɔ bɔvɔ ngɔlɔ bɛ nɩ bo nu be kɛɛ “sʊ̃mivʊlɔ nɔhvanlɛvʊlɔ nɩ mɔɔ izi nyɛlɛbɛ nɩ.” Ɔzɔ mmenyia yɩ ya nwʊnlɩ kɛɛ egyima mɔɔ bɛva bowula bɛ sa nɩ, ɩsɛ kɛɛ bɛyɛ yɩ wɔ oyuwadɩ nu dɩka bela. Meti bɛya bɔnwʊnlɩ kɛɛ nɩ ɩsɛ kɛɛ bekile Bayɩbʊlʊ yɩ tu bɔkɔ ndɛnɩ sũmaa nu. (Mt. 24:45) Ansaana bɛbayɩ ɔzɔ mmenyia yɩ nɩ, nɩ Bayɩbʊlʊ Zumanlɛmaa yɩ wɔ yɩ tu kɛɛ bɛlɛ “Photo-Drama of Creation” bedii dasɩlɛ wɔ oyuwadɩ yɩ nu dɩka bela. Naasʊ̃ sʊ̃mivʊlɔ nɔhvanlɛvʊlɔ nɩ ya nwʊnlɩ kɛɛ ɩsɛ kɛɛ bɛyɛ sũmaa bekĩnla nwʊ. Kɛ ndianwʊ nɩ, wɔ 1921 nu nɩ bɛyɩlɩ The Harp of God buuku yi mɔɔ ɩkɔwʊwa mmenii ma bokozuma Bayɩbʊlʊ yɩ nɩ, na ayinzi yi behileli yi tu bɔhɔlɩ ndɛnɩ 36 nu. Akʊ̃ sʊ̃ wɔ 1935 nu nɩ, bɛya bɛdɩlɩ yɩ tυ

kɛɛ “ɔlɔmɩ sʊ̃maa sʊ̃maa” mɔɔ bɔzʊ̃ oyuwadɩ nu dɩka bela kɔzʊ̃mɩ Gyehova wɔ azɩlɛ yɩ zʊ daabaa. Eyi manlɩ bɔnwʊnlɩ kɛɛ ɩsɛ kɛɛ bɛkã odwokɔba yɩ bekile mmenii munwala wɔ oyuwadɩ yɩ dɩka bela wɔ ndɛnɩ sʊ̃maa nu. Meti Gyehova luwalɩ Kilisinyiamaa mɔɔ bɔvɔ ngɔlɔ bɛ mɔɔ nɩ bɔwɔ kɛnɩ ɔzɔ mmɩlɩ nɩ zʊ yelɩ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ mɔɔ ɩkɛma bɛkɛhã odwokɔba yɩ wɔ oyuwadɩ yɩ dɩka bela na ama Zakaliyia yɩ nkɔmɩhyɛ yɩ ɩaya nu.

“WƲ MMENII KODU BƆ NWƲ KƐMA KƲLAA KƲLAA”

5 Kɛmɔ Ayɩzaya hanlɩ nɩ, mmenii sʊ̃maa mɔɔ bɔzʊ̃ oyuwadɩ dɩka bela nɩ kɛbɛ yɩ kɛɛ bokozuma Gyehova yɩ nwʊ dɩyɛ na bɛazʊ̃mɩ yɩ. Iluwa yɩ zɔ ti, mmɩlɩ mɔ Nyamɩnlɩ yi mmenii bɛkãã odwokɔba yɩ nɩ, bɔnwʊnlɩ mmenii mɔɔ bɛlɛ ahʊnlɩnɩba sʊ̃maa mɔɔ ɩsʊnɩ ndɛnɩ mɔɔ bo nu bela kã. (Kɩnga Ayɩzaya 2:2-4.) Kɛmɔ ɩkɛyɛ na Nyamɩnlɩ yi mmenii bɛahʊla bɛawʊwa mmenii mɔɔ bɛdɩ ɔzɔ nɩ, ‘botu bɔ nwʊ bɛma kʊlaa kʊlaa’ bɛkã odwokɔba yɩ bekile mmenii, bekile yɛ awuuku azʊlɔtυ tυ bɔkɔ ndɛnɩ sʊ̃maa nu, bɛpɩlɩntɩ na bɛfa bɔkɔ oyuwadɩ dɩka bela. (Ndw. 68:11; 110:3) Akʊ̃ sʊ̃, mmeliyema be mɔɔ bezi kɔmputa nu nɩ yɛ jw.org wɛbsayɩtɩ nu JW Library.® Na eyi bʊwa mmenii sʊ̃maa mɔɔ bɛkã ndɛnɩ azvlɔtυ nɩ ma bosuma Bayɩbʊlʊ yɩ.
