BƲTAYI
Yekehila kezimɔ Gyehova yi mmenii yi bʋwa ma odwo-kɔ mɔɔ ɩwɔ Zakalıya 8:23 nɩ ba numɔ.
<<<P>>>
“Mmenii bunlu mɔɔ bekã amanlɩ amanlɩ bɛ ndɛnɩ azvlotv nɩ kɔzvzɔ Dwuunii ɔkʋ̃ yɩ atalı nu.”—ZAK. 8:23.
<<<P>>>
GYEHOVA zɩnzanlɩ atunwadɩlɛmaa mɔɔ nɩ bekyinaa Babɛlɩ Bɔlɔnsanɩ Tɩndɩnlɩ nɩ bɛ ndɛnɩ va zɩkɛlɩ be egyima yɩ. Iluwa ɔzɔ ti, mmenii mɔɔ bɛtɩ ndɛnɩ kʋ̃ nɩ hɔ wʋwalɩ dɩka kʋ̃, na mɛyɩ yɛ ɩmanlɩ mmenii nu hyɩhyɛlɩ wɔ oyuwadɩ yɩ dɩka bela yɩ a. (Gyn. 11:6-9) Naasʋ̃ ɩdɩ Gyehova yi tile nu pɔkɔ kɛɛ ɩkɛhã “ɔlɔmɩ sʋ̃maa sʋ̃maa mɔɔ bɔzʋ̃ amanlɩ bela nu, abusunwa bela nu, yɛɛ edɩndɛlɛ bela nu” nɩ ɩkɔwɔ numɔ, na ɩawʋwa bɛ ma bɛa-dua anwʋhyɩlɩyɛ kezile yɩ beahɔ oyuwadɩ fʋlɔ yɩ nu. (Yik. 7:9, 10, 14) Ɔlɔmɩ sʋ̃maa sʋ̃maa nɩ kɔhʋla kenyia Gyehova nu dedi wɔ anwʋhyɩlɩyɛ kezile yɩ nu, iluwakɛɛ bɛdɩlɩ Bulombu-nlililɛ nwʋ odwokɔba yɩ wɔ bɛzabɛ ankasa bɛ ɛdɩndɛlɛ nu. (Kɩnga Wulomimaa 10:13, 14.) Kila ɔnwʋmɔ ɔhvanɩ kezile mɔɔ yenyia kɛɛ yɛkɩnla bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛkãã odwokɔba mɔɔ ide nnwʋanlɩ nɩ nwʋ!
