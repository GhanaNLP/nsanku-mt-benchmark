16 Bɛmá yehila Robert sɔ̃ yɩ ozumanlɛ-nwvnlɛ yɩ. Iluwa ɔhɔnvwʋmmɔ tanɩ mɔɔ Robert valɩ wɔ sukuulu nu nninyeni mɔɔ láyɛ baaba mɔɔ nɩ ikila nɩ ti, ɩwɔlɩ yɩ tʋ kɛɛ ɩnvʋ nnuno tanɩ nɩ nzã sṹmaa na ɩvalɩ yɩ nwʋ iwulalɩ mmenyia nu mmenyia nna nu. Lhanlɩ kɛɛ: “Mɩdɩlɩ nyanlɩ kɛɛ mʋkʋla mɩyɛ mɔɔ mɩpɛ bela.” Naasṹ mmɩlɩ ɩkɔlɩ zʋlɔ nɩ, Robert ɣa nwʋnlɩ yɩ kɛɛ ílɛ yɩ nwʋ, mfomi ɩyɛ akɛlɛ ɩma oyuwadɩ yɩ. (2 Pit. 2:19) Mɩnla yɛ ɩyɛlɩ a? Lhanlɩ kɛɛ: “Mɩvalɩ mʋ nwʋ mʋwɔlɩ mmenii mɔɔ bɔdɔ Gyehova. Na mɩyɛlɩ nzɩnzanlɩyɛ wɔ mɩ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu mɔɔ kezimɔ misiyeziye mʋ nwʋ nɩ kezimɔ mɩtɩndɛ po kɩnla nwʋ.” Sikalɛyɩ, kɛ Robert tɩ nyanlɩ se a? Lhanlɩ kɛɛ: “Sɛ mutwodwu nwɔnɩ bela a, mʋnwʋ kɛɛ mɩ ahʋnlɩnɩ dɔ mu kunlu iluwakɛɛ mizi kɛɛ mʋbɔɔ ɛbɩla mɔɔ nwʋmɔ tɩ mɔɔ ɩdɩ baaba ɩtɛla mɩ ɛbɩla dadampɔ nɩ. Na eyi ma mɩ anyɩ de paa.”

17 Mɩnla yɛ yezunzu nwʋmɔ wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ eyi nu a? Yɛɣa yezuma kɛɛ ɩsɛ kɛɛ yekila yɛ nwʋ bʋwɔ na ama kezimɔ mmenii mɔɔ bɔwɔ oyuwadɩ nu nɩ dwĩ na bɛyɛ bɛ dɩyɛ nɩ iányianlɩ yɛ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ zʋ tumi. Eyi pɩbɛ kɛɛ yɛkɔhwĩ yɛ nwʋ wɔ ndɛnɩ evinli nu ɛbɩla tanɩ nwʋ, na yɛahila yɛ nwʋ bʋwɔ sṹ mmɩlɩ mɔ yɛlɛ Intanɛtɩ yedii egyima nɩ. Iwɔ numɔ kɛɛ oyuwadɩ yɩ sunzuma yɩ nwʋ yɛ sɩ dɩyɛ, naasṹ máma wu kunlu tu. Sa ɔkʋla edi zʋlɔ nkunimi. Sianti yɛ lɛ kɛɛ Gyehova lɛ tumi kʋlaa tɛla Setanɩ nɩɩ yɩ oyuwadɩ tanɩ yɩ. (Kɩnga 1 Dwɔɔnʋ 4:4.) Aktilde; sṹ, Gyehova kɛva yɩ sunzuma nwanzanwanza mɔɔ ɩtɛla tumi bela wɔ amansɔnɩ yɩ nu nɩ kɔwʋwa wʋ ma ɔkɔhṵ kediyia oyuwadɩ yɩ sunzuma nɩ. (Lk. 11:13) ‘Bɛmá yɛma Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ sunzuma yɩ ihile ɔhʋanɩ’ yɛ na ama yɛahʋla yɛahṵ yɛadiyia oyuwadɩ yɩ sunzuma yɩ!—Gal. 5:16.
