mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ mɔɔ bɔwɔlɩ nɩ, na nɔhvanlemaan mɔɔ bedɩ zɔ nɩ yɛ Gyehova pɛ kɛɛ ɩfa bɛ ɔhɔnwʊ a. Kɛ ndianwʊ nɩ, mmɩlɩ mɔ Nowa zʋ alɩka yɩ nu vindenli nɩ, ɩvalɩ nnamɩ ɩwɔlɩ afɔlɩ ɩva ihileli kezimɔ Gyehova le ɩ nʋ yɩ mmusunwamaa yɩ nnwʊanlɩ nɩ nwʋ anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ. (Gyn. 8:20) Ɔzɔ la yɛ Abilehamɩ sɔ̃ wɔlɩ afɔlɩ azʋlɔtʋ manlɩ Gyehova a. (Gyn. 12:7, 8) Dwoobu sɔ̃ wɔlɩ afɔlɩ manlɩ yi nnwuakula yɩ, na sɛ bɛyɛ tanɩ be a Gyehova tawa tahyɛ bɛ. (Dwb. 1:5) Eyi ti yɛ Gyehova vʋlɔlɩ mmenyia be kɛɛ azɔfʋ ansaana ɩyɛɛ azɔfʋlɩlɛ nwʋ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ kɛma Yizilayamaa nɩ a. Gyehova zeli Yizilayamaa nɩ kɛɛ: “Azɔfʋ yɩ [mmusunwa adile] mɔɔ bɛba bɛkyina Gyehova anyunu nɩ, kã kile bɛ kɛɛ bedí bɔ nwʊ na Gyehova iáhunli bɛ.”—Ɛkɩ. 19:22.

7 Mmɩlɩ mɔ ili bɔmʋnlɩ mɔ Bayɩbʋlʋ valɩ odwokɔ pɔkɔ “ɔsɔfʋ” lili egyima yɛ lɛ mmɩlɩ mɔ ɩhanlɩ Mɛlkizɛdɛkɩ nwʋ odwokɔ nɩ. (Gyn. 14:18) Kɛ ɔsɔfʋ nkɔ̃ la yɛ nɩ ɩdɩ a, mfomi nɩ ɩdɩ bulombunli sɔ̃. Ayinzi yɩ, Gyehova wɔlɩ anvhʋba kɛɛ Mɛzaya yɩ kɛyɛ bulombunli nu ɔsɔfʋ kɛmɔ nɩ Mɛlkizɛdɛkɩ dɩ nɩ la. Naasɔ̃ nɩ nninyeni baaba mɔɔ Mɛzaya yɩ kɛyɛ nɩ, adasamaa kenyia zʋlɔ mfasʋ daabaa.—Ndw. 110:4; Hib. 7:17.

8 Kɛmɔ yezi nɩ, mmɩlɩ mɔ Gyehova yʋlɩ Yizilayamaa kɛɛ yi mmenii nɩ, iluwalɩ Mosisi zʋ ɩvalɩ Mmɛla yɩ ɩmanlɩ bɛ. Nɩ azɔfʋlɩlɛ nwʋ ɔhvanthilelɛ sɔ̃ kɩnla Mmɛla yɩ nwʋ. Gyehova yʋlɩ Mosisi yi anlima Elɔnʋ nu yɩ tʋmaa kɛɛ bɔnlʋmmɔ la yɛ bɔzɔ̃mí kɛɛ azɔfʋ a. (Ɛkɩ. 40:12-15) Levimaa nɩ wʋwalɩ azɔfʋ yɩ ma bɔwɔlɩ afɔlɩ bɛmanlɩ Yizilaya manlɩ yɩ kɛ okuwu nu Yizilayanii bela sɔ̃. Gyehova valɩ afɔlɩwɔlɛ yɩ nwʋ ɔhvanthilelɛ pɔkyɩɩ manlɩ bɛ. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, ihileli afɔlɩ azʋlɔtʋ mɔɔ bɔkɔhʋla kɔwɔ, sianti mɔɔ ɩsɛ kɛɛ bɔbɔ afɔlɩ yɩ, nu kezimɔ bɔkɔwɔ yɩ. Gyehova luwalɩ afɔlɩwɔlɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ yɩ zʋ manlɩ Yizilayamaa nɩ nwʋnlɩ kɛɛ ɩkɛyɛ debe titili be ɩkɛma adasamaa wɔ eyile be.

9 Mmɩlɩ mɔ Gyisɛsɩ nlumanlɩ nɩ, ɩya ɩyɛlɩ Ɔsɔfʋ Ebani wɔ Gyehova yi sunzuma ɔfʋã nu asɔlɩ aliyia kezile yi nu. (Hib. 8:1) Alʋbɔ asã nu ɔfʋã mɛda nɩ, ɩvalɩ yɩ nnwʊanlɩ mɔɔ idi munli nɩ ɩwɔlɩ afɔlɩ. Nɩ yɩ afɔlɩwɔlɛ nɩ tv bʋlɔ kʋlaa tɛla nnamɩ mɔɔ nɩ azɔfʋ yɩ fa bɔ afɔlɩ wɔ Yizilaya nɩ. Nnɛ, Gyisɛsɩ ɩsɔ̃mʋ kɛɛ Ɔsɔfʋ Ebani, na ɩya ɩyɛ kɛɛ bʋlɩgyɩ mɔɔ ɩla Nyamɩnlɩ nu enii avinli. (Hib. 9:24) Sɛ Gyisɛsɩ mɔɔ ɩdɩ yɛ Ɔsɔfʋ Ebani nɩ yɛ Bulombunli nɩ idii azɩlɛ yɩ zʋ tumi a, ɩ nu mmenii 144,000 mɔɔ bɔkɔzɔ̃mɩ kɛɛ awulombunli nu azɔfʋ nɩ sɔ̃ kɔwɔ numɔ kɛyɛ egyima. (1 Pit. 1:3, 4; 2:9) Na Gyehova, Gyisɛsɩ, nu mmenii 144,000 nɩ kɔwʋwa bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɔwɔ ɛrɛ kɛɛ bɛkediye Nyamɩnlɩ munwatĩĩ ma bɛkeli munli, na bɛayɛ azɩlɛ yɩ paladayɩsɩ. Yekilaa ɔzɔ mmɩlɩ yɩ ɔhvanɩ ezɩzɩbɛ ezɩzɩbɛ!

10 Yɛga yɛnwʋ yɩ kɛɛ, azɔfʋlɩlɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ mɔɔ Gyehova yɛlɩ ɔzʋmɩ ɔwɔkɩ mmɩlɩ nu munwatĩĩ nɩ, nɩ ɩpɛ kɛɛ enii duwa zʋlɔ fa bike yi. Afɔlɩ azʋlɔtʋ mɔɔ azɔfʋ yɩ wɔlɩ nɩ hakɛlɩ Yizilayamaa nɔhvanlemaanɩ kɛɛ behyinyia eleledɩyɛ nɩ. Wɔ Alʋbɔ Abʋmɩ Tumililɛ yɩ nu nɩ, Gyisɛsɩ nɩ mmenii 144,000 nɩ kɔzɔ̃mɩ kɛɛ azɔfʋ kɔbʋmɩ kɛɛ enii keli munli. Mɛyɩ mɛda nɩ, enii léhyinyia azɔfʋlɩlɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ bela ansaana ɩahʋla iayike Nyamɩnlɩ. Mɩnla ɔhvanɩ zʋ yɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ kʋnlʋma eyi mɔɔ yɛga yezuma nwʋmɔ dɩyɛ nɩ twĩ yɛ bike Gyehova a? Ma yezunzu egyikĩĩ be nwʋ.
