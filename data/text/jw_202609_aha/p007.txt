Gyisɛsɩ nɩɩ yɩ awɔvɔlɔ mɔɔ bɛkɩnla yɩ nwʋ nɩ koluwa Amagadɔnʋ kunɛ yɩ zʋ kɛyɩ Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ adamvʋlɔ munwatĩĩ wɔ ɛkɛnɩ. Na bɛkele Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ azũmɩvʋlɔ munwatĩĩ nnwʋanlɩ
<<<P>>>
Amagadɔnʋ kunɛ yɩ kɛya a. Gyisɛsɩ Kɩlayɩsɩ nɩɩ yɩ awɔvɔlɔ yɩ bɛ nɩɩ Gyehova yɩ adamvʋlɔ yɩ kɔhʋ̃. (Yik. 17:12-14; 19:11, 14, 15) Mɛti, nɩ mɩnla ɔhʋanɩ zʋ yɛ Gyehova kɔwɔ yɩ mmenii nwʋ banɩ wɔ Amagadɔnʋ kunɛ yɩ nu a?
<<<P>>>
19 Sɛ Gyisɛsɩ nɩɩ yɩ awɔvɔlɔ yɩ mɔɔ bɛkɩnla yɩ nwʋ nɩ bɛsɩkɛɛ tanɩyɛlɛmaa wɔ Amagadɔnʋ kunɛ yɩ nu a, íni kɛɛ yɛma ye kunlu tu. Mɩnla ti a? Iluwakɛɛ yɛwɔ Gyehova nu anwʋlazʋ na yezi kɛɛ ɩkɔwɔ yɛ nwʋ banɩ. Na yɛkɔhʋla yekenyia anwʋlazʋ kʋlaa kɛɛ, Gyisɛsɩ nɩɩ yɩ awɔvɔlɔ mɔɔ bɛkɩnla yɩ nwʋ nɩ koluwa Amagadɔnʋ kunɛ yɩ zʋ kɛyɩ Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ adamvʋlɔ munwatĩĩ wɔ ɛkɛnɩ. (Gyɛ. 25:33; Zak. 14:12) Akʋ̃ sʋ̃, yɛwɔ anwʋlazʋ paa kɛɛ Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ azũmɩvʋlɔ nu ɔkʋ̃ po íni ɛkɛnɩ mɔɔ adamvʋlɔ yɩ kohũ yi wɔ ɔzɔ eyile yɩ.—Lk. 21:28; 2 Pit. 2:9.
<<<P>>>
20 Lkɔhʋla kɛya kɛɛ wɔ Amagadɔnʋ kunɛ yɩ nu nɩ, odwokɔ mɔɔ sunzuma yɩ hanlɩ ndwomidulɛvʋlɔ yɩ ma thɩlɛlɩ nɩ kɛya numɔ kɛɛ: “Abʋmɩ kɔdʋdɔ azɩ wɔ wʋ ɔfʋã, na abʋmɩ bunlu sʋ̃ kɔdʋdɔ azɩ wɔ wʋ sa nyini zʋ. Naasʋ̃ mɔwʋ mɛyɩ, ilébike wʋ. Sɛ bɔtwĩĩ tanɩyɛlɛmaa bɛ azʋ̃ a, wʋ anyɩ yɛ ɛkɛva kehila bɛ a. Iluwakɛɛ ɛhanlɩ kɛɛ: ‘Gyehova yɛ lɛ mɩ eyiandinlɛhɔlɛdɩka’ nɩ ti, ɛva Sʋlɔsʋlɔnii nɩ ɛyɛ wʋ wʋwalɛ dɩka.” (Ndw. 91:7-9) Sɛ Amagadɔnʋ kunɛ yɩ bɔ yɩ tʋ a, yɛkɔhʋla kenyia anwʋlazʋ kɛɛ Gyehova kɔwɔ yi mmenii nwʋ banɩ wɔ nnwʋnlamɩ ɔfʋã nu, nyanlɩdɩlɛ ɔfʋã nu nɩɩ sunzuma ɔfʋã nu. Gyehova ɩlɔbɔ yɛ nwʋ banɩ kɛɛ okuwu kɛkɛ na mfomi ɩkɔwɔ ye nu ɔkʋ̃ bela nwʋ banɩ sʋ̃. Iluwakɛɛ, ye nu ɔkʋ̃ bela kã kɛɛ: “Gyehova yɛ lɛ mɩ eyiandinlɛhɔlɛdɩka,” Na Gyehova sʋ̃ bɔ yɛ anvhʋba kɛɛ: “Mɔwʋ mɛyɩ, ilébike wʋ.” Mɛti, bɛmá yɛva anwʋlazʋ yehila eyile be ɔhʋanɩ na yenyia Gyehova nu dedi kɛɛ ɩkɔwɔ yɛ nwʋ banɩ.
