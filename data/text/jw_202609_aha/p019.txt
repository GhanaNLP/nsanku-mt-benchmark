Bayɩbulu mɔɔ bɛyɩ Gyehova yi dima wɔ nu bedi egyima nɩ, bɔlɔkʊla bɔkɔnwʊ Nyamɩnlɩ yi dima yɩ na bɛava bɛali egyima.

15 Gyehova Dasɩvɔlɔmaa bo nu sɔ̃maa lɛ New World Translation wɔ ndɛnɩ mɔɔ bɔkʊla bɛkɩnga na bɛtɩ yɩ tʊ nu. Ɔzɔ Bayɩbulu tʊhilelɛ yɩ bʊwa bɛ ma bɔnwʊ nɔhvanlɛ mɔɔ ɩfalɩ Gyehova nwʊ, na bɛfa bɔ nwʊ betiã yɩ zʊ. Eyi ma benyia ɔlɔlɛ bɛma yɩ na bɛyɩ yi dima yi ayɛlɛ. —Ɛɣɛ. 18:10; Ayɩ. 56:6.
<<<P>>>
16 Bayɩbulu yɩ ma yɛnwʊ kɛɛ ɩlékye Gyehova nɩ “awulombunli munwatĩĩ mɔɔ bɔwɔ oyuwadɩ yɩ nu nɩ” kɔhʊ̃. (Yik. 16:14) Ɔzɔ mmɩlɩ yɩ nɩ, Gyehova kɔwɔ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bezi yi na bedi yi dima nni nɩ nwʊ banɩ. (Ndw. 9:10; kɩnga Dwɔɔnʊ 17:3.) Bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛátiye yɩ na bɛáfa yɩ bɛyɛ bɛ Zɩ nɩ kɔnwʊ enii ɔkʊ̃ mɔɔ ɩdɩ mmɩlɩ mɔ ibuwaa bɛ ndɛnɩ nɩ. Gyehova kã kɛɛ: “Mɩkɛhyɛ mʊ nwʊ animinyiamɩ na mɩadɩ mi dima yɩ nwʊ, na mɩkɛla mʊ nwʊ ali wɔ amanlɩ sɔ̃maa anyunu na ama bɛanwʊ kɛɛ mamɩ yɛ lɛ Gyehova.” (Izi. 38:23) Wɔ ɔzɔ mmɩlɩ nɩ, kila kezimɔ Gyehova yi mmenii anyɩ kele kɛɛ ɩdɩ bɔ Ɔhɔnwʊ na kɛ bɛ tamvʊlɔ a! —Mal. 3:16.

17 Kila ɔnwʊmɔ ɔhvanɩ mɔɔ yenyia kɛɛ yɛbʊwaa ma nkɔmɩhyɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ Zakalɩya 8:23 nɩ kɛɣa nu! Yenyia ɔnwʊmɔ ɔhvanɩ fʊlɔ sʊ̃ kɛɛ yɛ nɩɩ ye mmeliyema mmenyia nɩɩ mmaalɛ nɩɩ awɔvʊlɔ mɔɔ bekilee dasɩlelilɛ egyima mɔɔ yɛyɛɛ wɔ ɛdɩndɛlɛ azvlotʊ nu ɔhvanɩ nɩ, yɛ yɛbɔ numɔ yɛyɛ egyima a. Gyehova ma yɛ sa hã nninyeni mɔɔ yehyinyia na ama yɛahʊla yɛ nɩɩ mmenii mɔɔ bɔwɔ manlɩ bela nɩɩ abusunwa bela nɩɩ ɛdɩndɛlɛ bela nu nɩ yɛawɔ nkɔmmɔ. Bɛmá yɛva nninyeni eyi yeli egyima bʊwɔ na yɛwʊwá mmenii sɔ̃maa ma bɔhwĩ beyike Gyehova, Nyamɩnlɩ mɔɔ ɩáyɩyɩ mmenii nu, ɩnwʊ nwɔkɛ, na ɩwɔ abʊtalɩ nɩ. Yɛ anyɩ de kɛɛ yɛkɔhɔ ɔzʊlɔ yɛkɛhã odwokɔba yɩ yekehile mmenii mɔɔ bɔwɔ azɩlɛ yɩ zʊ munwala ma bɔkɔnwʊ Gyehova yi dima nɩɩ nɔhvanlɛ odwokɔ mɔɔ ɩfalɩ yɩ nwʊ nɩ.—Lk. 11:2.
