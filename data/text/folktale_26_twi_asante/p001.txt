APUPUO A OBOO N’ASUO, DA NSU’KAKYE
<<<P>>>
YE se Okawa, Opitire, Bonko ne asuom’ mmoa nnyina na ope kesie bi asie na nsuo nnyina awe ama mmoa nnyina hunoo amane. Ye deda anwea so. Efei na ye see, “Amane a ye’huno yi dee, mo mma yenko Nyankonpon ho na yenko su nkyere no se obema yen nsuo.” Mmoa nnyina siim’ koye. Na Okawa maa so se:
<<<P>>>
“Nye me Oka’ Abena, apimpim-a-ko-ha, apimpim-aba-ha?
<<<P>>>
Mahuri magyina datahin so, masu mafre wo.
<<<P>>>
Mate me nanase aburobia.
<<<P>>>
Aberefi Sempa me yere no, Abuya.”
<<<P>>>
Na osuo anto. Na Opitire so aba, osee:
<<<P>>>
“Nye me Opitire Abena, apimpim-a-ko-ha, apimpim-aba-ha?
<<<P>>>
Mahuri magyina datahin so, masu mafre wo.
<<<P>>>
Mate me nanase aburobia.
<<<P>>>
Aberefi Sempa me yere no, Abuya.”
<<<P>>>
Na osuo anto. Na Bonko so aba, osee:
<<<P>>>
“Nye me Bonko Abena, apimpim-a-ko-ha, apimpim-aba-ha?
<<<P>>>
Mahuri magyina datahin so, masu mafre wo. Mate me nanase aburobia.
<<<P>>>
Aberefi Sempa me yere no, Abuya.”
<<<P>>>
Na osuo anto. Mmoa nnyina besuye, na eko kaa Apupuo. Osee, “Mo mma me mmesu bi.” Na ye see, “So’ ho y’asu annye yie, na wo dee na ebeye den?” Osee, “Mo mma me nsu.” Na yeboroo no se ye ka kyeree no pen se onnsu. Na Apupuo see, “Mesu.” Na ye see, “Su!” Na omaa so:
<<<P>>>
“Nye me Apupuo Abena, apimpim-a-ko-ha, apimpim-aba-ha?
<<<P>>>
Mahuri magyina datahin so, masu mafre wo.
<<<P>>>
Mate me nanase aburobia.
<<<P>>>
Aberefi Sempa me yere no, Abuya.”
<<<P>>>
Na Onyame boom’, kukuru! mmoa nnyina see, “Ko so, ko so.” Na osuu bio na ’Nyame munaa tumm! Efei dee mmoa no kukuruu no sii akonkon, see, “Ko so bio.” Na omaa so sa’ra. Efei dee Nyankonpon gyaam’ ta! ta! ta! ta! nsuo nnyina yiyii. Efei dee aboa ’yi soreye a, nsuom’ twom! Mmoa nnyina koom’. Apupuo se o’kom’ na ye kaa no, na odaa nkokonoa. Ene se ye se, “Apupuo a oboo n’asuo, da nsu’kakye.”
<<<P>>>
M’anansesem a metooye yi, se eye de o, se ennye de o, momfa bi nko, na momfa bi mmera.
