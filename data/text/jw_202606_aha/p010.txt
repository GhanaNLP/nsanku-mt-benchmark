Kãnwʋazɩ kɔwʋwa wʋ ma okowu wʋ anyɩ kogua akasakasa ngyɩkĩĩ ngyɩkĩĩ mɔɔ ɩkɛya ɛ nɩɩ mmenii mvʋlɔ bɛ avinli nɩ zʋ na wʋazɔ̃ wʋ ahʋnlɩnɩ nu wʋava bɛ tanɩ wʋahyɛ bɛ
<<<P>>>
(Kila nkyɩkyɛmi 8)
<<<P>>>
ɩmanlɩ yɩ adʋwavʋlɔ yɩ, ɩwʋwʋlɩ bɛ agyia nwʋ. Nɩ nkɛlɛ yɛ beta bɛyɛ egyima eyi a. (Dwɔ. 13:3-5, 12-16) Gyisɛsɩ yelɩ zɔ va hileli adʋwavʋlɔ yɩ ma bɔnwʋnlɩ kezimɔ bɛkɛma ɔhɔnwʋvalɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ bɛ nɩɩ mmenii mvʋlɔ avinli nɩ kɔhɔ zʋlɔ kɔwʋwa kɛnɩ. Nɩ sɛ bobu mmenii mvʋlɔ kɛɛ bɛtɛla bɛ a, ɩkɛma bekele bɛ adwɩnɩhilelɛ bekediá numɔ mmɩlɩ po mɔ bɛ nɩɩ bɛ iáyɛ adwɩnɩ nɩ. Na sɛ odwokɔ be ba bɛ avinli sʋ̃ a, ɩlɛ́sɩkɛ ɔhɔnwʋvalɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ bɛ avinli nɩ.
<<<P>>>
⁸ Kɛ ɛkɛyɛ na wʋama ɔhɔnwʋvalɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ ɛ nɩɩ abiye avinli nɩ iahɔ zʋlɔ iawʋwa kɛ a? Kãnwʋazɩ kɔwʋwa wʋ ma okowu wʋ anyɩ kogua akasakasa ngyɩkĩĩ ngyɩkĩĩ mɔɔ ɩkɛya ɛ nɩɩ mmenii mvʋlɔ bɛ avinli nɩ zʋ na ɔzɔ̃ wʋ ahʋnlɩnɩ nu wʋava bɛ tanɩ wʋahyɛ bɛ. (Kɩnga Kolosiyiamaa 3:13.) Sɛ ɛ nɩɩ abiye bɛ adwɩnɩ iáyiyia wɔ debe nwʋ sʋ̃ a, kãnwʋazɩ kɔwʋwa wʋ ma ɛlɛ́fa yɩ nwʋ ɛya, na eyi kɛma bɔ ɔhɔnwʋvalɛ yɩ kɔhɔ zʋlɔ kɔwʋwa kɛnɩ. (Ndw. 4:4) Na sɛ bɛhanlɩ ndwokɔ be mɔɔ iávata behileli bɔ nwʋ po a, kakɛ kɛɛ ɩyɛ a, ye munwala

8. Sɛ akasakasa be ba ɛ nɩɩ wʋ anlima Kilisinyianii be bɛ avinli a, mɩnla ɔhʋanlɩ zʋ yɛ kãnwʋazɩ kɔwʋwa ma bɔkɔhɔ zʋlɔ bɛkɛyɛ ɔhɔnwʋmmɔ a? (Kolosiyiamaa 3:13) (Kila mfonyini yi sɔ̃.)
<<<P>>>
yɛkã ndwokɔ be mɔɔ ayinzi yi, yenu yɛ nwʋ. (Odw. 7:21, 22) Bizia wʋ nwʋ kɛɛ, ‘Ɔnwʋnlɛ mɔɔ mɩmaa mʋ ɔhɔnwʋ yɩ kɔnwʋ kɛɛ mʋ odwokɔ yɛ fɛ, nɩɩ ɔhɔnwʋvalɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ yɛ avinli nɩ, ɛhɩnla dɩyɛ yɛ nwʋmɔ hyinyia mɩ paa a?’ Kɛ ankɩ ɔkɔhwĩ odwokɔ yɩ nu nɩ, sa ɔkʋla de mɔɔ enii ɔkɔ̃ nɩ ɩkãã nɩ etiã numɔ. Akɔ̃ sʋ̃, máfa mɔɔ izili nɩ wula wʋ nu anzɛnyɩɩ ɔkɔ fa nwʋmɔ odwokɔ ba dɔkʋ̃.
<<<P>>>
⁹ Sɛ ɩyɛ sɩ ɩma wʋ kɛɛ okodualɩ akasakasa be mɔɔ ɩya ɛ nɩɩ abiye bɛ avinli nɩ zʋ sʋ̃ a, kãnwʋazɩ kɔwʋwa wʋ. Áma ɛ nu yɩ sũ pɩla kɛɛ mɔwʋ yɛ wʋ odwokɔ yɛ fɛ a. (Kɩnga Ɛyɛlɛwulɛ 17:9; 1 Kɔl. 6:7) Eyi kɔhʋla kɔwʋwa wʋ ma ɛkɛva wʋ adwɩnɩ keziye kezimɔ bɔ ɔhɔnwʋvalɛ yɩ kɔhɔ zʋlɔ kɔwʋwa kɛnɩ nɩ zʋ. Mmenii sɔ̃maa nwʋ yɩ kɛɛ sɛ bɛ nɩɩ abiye keziyeziye bɛ avinli odwokɔ na bɛfa adwɩnɩ mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ bɔkɔ a, ɩbʋwa paa. (Ndw. 34:14) Sa ɔkʋla kã kɛɛ: ‘Yɛyɛlɩ ɔhɔnwʋmmɔ a, kɛ nnɛ a. Meti íni kɛɛ yɛma debela sɩkɛ yɛ avinli kʋlaa. Sa yekʋla yɛkã mɔɔ izili yi nwʋ odwokɔ a?’ Siyeziye wʋ nwʋ kɛɛ ekele wʋ ɔfʋã nu dɩyɛ mɔɔ
