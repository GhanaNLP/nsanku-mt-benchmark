7 Yɛyɛ azʊnlɩ wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʊ iluwakɛɛ yɛpɛ kɛɛ yɛ anyɩ de wɔ nnɛ nu eyile be. Gyehova hã hileli yɩ azɔ̃mɩvʊlɔ mɔɔ bɔwʊwalɩ azɩ wɔ ɔwɔkɩ mmɩlɩ nu nɩ kɛɛ, sɛ bɛpɛ kɛɛ bɛ anyɩ de na bɔbʊwa azɩ daabaa a, gyɩsɛ bɛyɛ sutiye bɛma yɩ. Na ɔzɔ la yɛ ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛzayɛ s体系 yɛyɛ a. (Dit. 32: 44-47) Sɛ yɛyɛ azʊnlɩ wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʊ a, yɛnyia ɛzɩzɩbɛhyɛlɛ mɔɔ ɩkɔwʊwa yɛ ma yekegyina pindinyii wɔ ye odwokilalɛ nu na ɩma yɛfa abʊtalɩ yɛdɩkɛ oyuwadɩ fʊlɔ nɩ. (Wul. 8: 25) Nnɛ paa yɛ ihyinyia kɛɛ yɛma yɛ anyɩ da kɛnɩ na yɛyɛ sutiye yɛma Gyehova a, iluwakɛɛ yɛwɔ awielɩyɛ mmɩlɩ nu.—Mt. 24:42-44; Yik. 1:3.

8 Mmeliyema mmenyia mɔɔ bekilehile wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʊ nɩ, titili bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛma bagua nu ndɛnɩ azʋlɔtʋ nɩ, ɩsɛ kɛɛ bɔbɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ bokodu mpʊnɩ wɔ be ehilehilelɛ nu paa. Sɛ eliyema benyia be siyeziye yɩ ndɛnɩ bʊwɔ na ɩfa numɔ ndwokɔ yɩ ikyina Bayɩbʊlʊ yɩ zʊ a, ɩbʊwa bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛdɩ Dasɩvʊlɔ nɩ ma bɔnwʊ Bayɩbʊlʊ nu nɔhʊanlɛ ndwokɔ be mɔɔ nwʊmɔ hyinyia. Akʊ̃ sʊ̃, ɩbʊwa yɩ ɔhɔnwvʊmmɔ dedimaa ma bɛtɩ nɔhʊanlɛ ndwokɔ mɔɔ ɩwɔ Bayɩbʊlʊ nu nɩ yɩ tʊ bʊwɔ paa na bɔkɔ zʋlɔ bɛfa bedi egyima wɔ bɛ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu. Sɛ abiyie nyia egyimalilɛ na isiyeziye yɩ nwʊ bʊwɔ a, ɩkʊla inyia mvʊlɔ zʊ tumi paa. Meti sɛ enyia egyimalilɛ be a, kila kezimɔ adwɩnɩhilelɛ mɔɔ ilili zʋlɔ yɩ kɔhʊla kɔwʊwa wʊ ma ɛkɛyɛ ehilehilelɛvʊlɔ baaba. Ɩwɔ numɔ kɛɛ ɔzɔ ndwokɔ yɩ falɩ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛma bagua nu ndɛnɩ nwʊ dɩyɛ, naasʊ̃ sɛ ye nu bela nyia egyimalilɛ be a, sa yɛkʊla yɛfa yedi egyima. —2 Tim. 4:2; Tay. 1:9.

9 Hyɩhyɛ wu ndwokɔ yɩ bʊwɔ. Sɛ ɛmaa ndɛnɩ a, ɩsɛ kɛɛ ɛkã ndwokɔ mɔɔ wu ediyelɛmaa kɛdɩ yɩ tʊ. Sɛ ndwokɔ mɔɔ ɛkãã nɩ dɩ nwʊnwʊnlanvɩɩ a, ɩkɛma wu ediyelɛmaa nɩ bɛ adwɩnɩ kodu kɔvʊla. Meti mɩnla yɛ ɩkɔwʊwa wʊ a? (1) Wɔ wu ndɛnɩ yɩ ahyɛasɩ la nɩ, ma wu ediyelɛmaa bɔnwʊ ndwokɔ aditili mɔɔ ɛbahã nwʊmɔ odwokɔ nɩ. (2) Akʊ̃ sɛ ɛfa ndwokɔ aditili nɩ numɔ ɔkʊ̃ bela a, nɩ ɛhã nwʊmɔ odwokɔ. Na sɛ ɛkã odwokɔ titili be nwʊ odwokɔ wie na ɔkɔɔ fʊlɔ zʊ a, bɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ekegyina egyikĩĩ na ama wu ediyelɛmaa nɩ bɛanwʊ kɛɛ ɛbahã dɩyɛ fʊlɔ nwʊ odwokɔ. (3) Sɛ ɛlɛ wu ndɛnɩ yɩ sʊ̃ ɛbaa awielɩyɛ a, bɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ɔkɔwɔ yɩ munwala dɔkɔ.

10 Ma wv anyɩ leli wv ndɛnɩ yɩ nwv. Sɛ wʊzawʊ wʊ anyɩ deli mɔɔ ɛkɛhãã nwʊmɔ odwokɔ nɩ nwʊ a, ɩkɛma ɛkeva ɛhyɩmɛ kɛhã nwʊmɔ odwokɔ. (Ɛyɛ. 2:4, 5; Egy. 4:20) Sɛ ɔdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ ɛhɛlɛlɛndwokɔ nɩɩ ndwokɔ aditili mɔɔ ɛkɛhãã nwʊmɔ odwokɔ nɩ nwʊ a, ɩkɛma wʊ anyɩ keleli wʊ ndɛnɩ yɩ nwʊ. Eliyema benyia be mɔɔ yɩ alʊbɔ hɔ anyunumi mɔɔ ɩwɔ United States nɩ lanwʊ la kakɛ ndɛnɩ be mɔɔ idiyeli mmɩlɩ mɔ nɩ ɩdɩ abɩlantɩyɛ nɩ. Lhanlɩ kɛɛ: “Mɩlanwʊ la mɩkakɛ kezimɔ ndɛnɩmanlɛvʊlɔ yɩ hyɛlɩ yɛ ɛzɩzɩbɛ kɛɛ yɛdwɩnɩndwɩnĩ Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ Odwokɔ yɩ nwʊ yɛhɔ ayinzi nɩ. Ɩzʊ̃ anwʊlazv nu ihilehileli numɔ ma yɛnwʊnlɩ kezimɔ yɛkɛyɛ zɔ. Ɔzɔ ndɛnɩ yɩ hanlɩ mɩ paa. Lmanlɩ muduli mpʊnɩ wɔ mɩ Bayɩbʊlʊ ɛyɩngalɛ nu na mɩ nɩɩ Gyehova avinli abusunwawɔlɛ nu sʊ̃ yɛlɩ sɩ.”
