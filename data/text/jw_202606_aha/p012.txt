(Kila nkyɩkyɛmi 19)
<<<P>>>
nu, naasʊ̃ mɔɔ bɛyɛlɩ nɩ lalɩ ali kɛɛ bede bedi paa kɛɛ Bulombunlililɛ yɩ wɔ kɛnɩ. Gyisɛsɩ nwʊnlɩ dedi mɔɔ bɛlalɩ yɩ ali nɩ, na yɩ anyɩ zɔlɩ yɩ paa. Meti sɛ yɛzayɛ sʊ̃ yɛfa yɛ adwɩnɩ yesiye yɛ ɔhɔnwʊmmɔ bo subanɩba zʋ a, nɩ yesumazumaa Gyisɛsɩ nɩɩ yɩ Zɩ Gyehova.
<<<P>>>
¹⁵ Mɔɔ ihyinyia paa nɩ, sa yɛkʋla yɛbɔ Gyehova mpayɩ kɛɛ ɩwʋwá yɛ ma yɛhɔ zʋlɔ yɛva bɛzabɛ mɔɔ kezimɔ bɛyɛ bɛ dɩyɛ nɩ dɩ sʋnʋnkʋ̃ wɔ yɛ dɩyɛ nwʋ nɩ ɔhɔnwʋ. Dwɩnɩndwɩnɩ bʋwabʋlɛ azʋlɔtʋ mɔɔ ɩkɔhʋla ɩkɛma ɛkɛva abiye nwʋ ɛya nɩ nwʋ na bɔ nwʋmɔ mpayɩ. Kakɛ kɛɛ Gyehova mɔɔ ɩdɩ yɛ Wɔlɛvʋlɔ nɩ zi paa kɛɛ ɩsʋnɩ kezimɔ ye nu bela dɩ. Meti ɔkɔhʋla kenyia anwʋlazʋ kɛɛ, sɛ ɔbɔ yɩ mpayɩ a, ɩkɛva mmʋwalɩyɛ mɔɔ ehyinyia nɩ ɩkɛma wʋ ma ekegyina yi munwa. Na sɛ ɩyɛ sɩ ɩma wʋ kɛɛ ɛkɛla abʋtalɩ ali a, siã Gyehova kɛɛ ɩvá yi sunzuma nwanzanwanza yɩ ɩwʋwa wʋ ma ɛhyɛ wʋ nwʋ zʋ.—Lk. 11:13; Gal. 5:22, 23.

MMƖLƖ MƆ YE MMELIYEMA BƆNWUƲ AMANƖYƐ
<<<P>>>
¹⁶ Mɩnla ti mɔɔ ɩkɔhʋla ɩkɛyɛ ɔhawʋ a? Sɛ ye mmeliyema beyiyiaa ɔhawʋ anzɛnyɩɩ bɔnwʋʋ amanɩyɛ be a, ebiye a, bɛkɛhɩnhã ndwokɔ be anzɛnyɩɩ bɛkɛyɛ nninyeni be mɔɔ ɩkɛma yɛkɛva ɛya. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, ebiye a, bɛkɛyɛ dinyii, debe egyikĩĩ nɩ bɛva ɛya, anzɛnyɩɩ bɛkɛyɛ nninyeni be mɔɔ ɩkɛyɛ yɛ asɩbɔnwʋ. Sa bɔkʋla bɛkã ndwokɔ be anzɛnyɩɩ bɛyɛ nninyeni be mɔɔ ɩkɛma yekeli ɛyɛkɛ paa. (Dwb. 6:2, 3) Ɔhawʋ nɩɩ amanɩyɛnwʋnlɛ mɔɔ ye mmeliyema beyiyiaa nɩ, kɛ yi munwala yɛ yezi a. Meti ebiye a, yɛ adwɩnɩ kɛyɛ yɛ kɛɛ bɛyɛɛ bɛ dɩyɛ ma ɩkɛdɛla zʋlɔ sʊ̃maa.
<<<P>>>
¹⁷ Sumazuma Gyisɛsɩ yi kunlumiba nɩɩ kunlumiyɩbalɛ yɩ. Gyisɛsɩ lalɩ kunlumiba nɩɩ kunlumiyɩbalɛ ali hileli bɛzabɛ mɔɔ nɩ bɔnwʋʋ amanɩyɛ nɩ, mɔɔ nɩ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ iléyiyialɩ bɛ kyĩ po kɩnla nwʋmɔ. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, sunzu kezimɔ ɩ nɩɩ anyɩnlɩsunliyaniibe mɔɔ bɔfʋlɔ yɩ Batimiyɔsʋ lili nɩ nwʋ kila. Eyile ɔkʋ̃ be mɔɔ nɩ Gyisɛsɩ ituaa numɔ nɩ, ɩdɩnyɩadɩnyɩanlɩ numɔ, mɔɔ ɩkɛyɛ na Gyisɛsɩ yɩ adwɩnɩ ɩaya yɩ zʋ. Nɩ mmenii mɔɔ bɔwɔ kɛnɩ nɩ be bɛtɩnyɩadɩnyɩaa yɩ kɛɛ ɩyɛ dinyii, naasʊ̃ iluwakɛɛ nɩ ɩpɛ paa kɛɛ Gyisɛsɩ sa yɩ adimakɛlɛ nɩ ti, iátiye bɛ kʋlaa. Mfomi thɔlɩ zʋlɔ ɩdɩnyɩadɩnyɩanlɩ numɔ kezile paa. Ebiye a, mmenii mɔɔ nɩ bɔwɔ kɛnɩ nɩ be bɛ anyɩ iádeli mɔɔ nɩ Batimiyɔsʋ ɩyɛɛ nɩ nwʋ na yɩ nwʋ yɛlɩ bɛ ahyĩ po.
