NDWOMI 90
Bɛmá Yehye Yɛ Nwu Yɛ Nwu Ɛzızɩbɛ
<<<P>>>
BOTAYL
Kezimɔ yekeli ndwokɔ-zızɩbɛ mɔɔ ɩkɔhʋla ɩkɛzıkɛ ɔhɔnwʋvalɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ yɛ nɩɩ ye mmeliyema avinli nɩ zʋ.
<<<P>>>
Mɔɔ Ɩkɛma Ɔhɔnwʋvalɛ Mɔɔ Ɩwɔ Yɛ Nɩɩ Ye Mmeliyema Avinli Nɩ Kɔhɔ Zʋlɔ Kɔwʋwa Kɛnɩ
<<<P>>>
“Bowulá kunlumiyɩbalɛ, kunlumiba, kãnwʋazı, odwokɛ, nɩɩ abʋtalı kɛɛ atalɩ.”—KOL. 3:12, NWT.
<<<P>>>
ƐBILAWƆLƐ nu kɔhʋla kɛyɛ sɩ wɔ oyuwadɩ tanɩ eyi nu paa. Naasʋ̃ sɛ éni ɔhɔnwʋmmɔ a, mɛyɩ sɛ odwokɔ yɩ sɩkɛ kʋlaa. Gyehova zi kɛɛ yekehyinyia ɔhɔnwʋmmɔ mɔɔ bɔkɔwʋwa yɛ wɔ mmɩlɩ eyi nu. Meti, ɩma yenyia mmeliyema mɔɔ yɛkɔhʋla yɛkɛva bɛ ɔhɔnwʋ. (Ndw. 119:63) Yɛya yɛhʋnla abusunwa mɔɔ numɔ maa sʋ̃mɩ Gyehova na bɔdʋdɔ bɔ nwu nɩ nwu.
<<<P>>>
2 Yɛábɛ yɩ kɛɛ ɔhɔnwʋvalɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ yɛ nɩɩ ye mmeliyema avinli nɩ yɛ kɛɛ mɔɔ yezi bɛ kɛkɛ la. Mfomi yɛpɛ kɛɛ yɛyɛ ɔhɔnwʋmmɔ mɔɔ yedi nɔhʋanlɛ na yebike yɛ nwu paa. Yɛdɩ abusunwa mɔɔ yɛdɔ Gyehova nɩɩ Gyisɛsɩ, na ɔzɔ ɔlɔlɛ yɩ yɛ ɩkã yɛ ɩbɔ nu a. (Dwɔ. 13:35) Naasʋ̃ ɔhɔnwʋvalɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ yɛ nɩɩ ye mmeliyema avinli nɩ láya zʋzɔ la. Ide mmɔdɩnɩwɔlɛ paa na yɛahʋla yɛayɛ zɔ. Na iluwakɛɛ yɛáli munli nɩ ti, ɩyɛ a yeyiyia ndwokɔzɩzɩbɛ be mɔɔ ɩma ɩyɛ sɩ kɛɛ yɛkɔhɔ zʋlɔ yɛkɛyɛ ɔhɔnwʋmmɔ.
<<<P>>>
3 Nɔhʋanlɛ odwokɔ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ yɛta yebike yɛ mmeliyema mbiye-mmɔ yɛtɛla mbiyemmɔ. Mɩnla ti a? Ebiye a, yɛtɩ nyanlɩ kɛɛ yɛ nɩɩ bɛ adwɩnɩ yiyia wɔ nninyeni be nwu anzɛnyɩ̃ nninyeni mɔɔ yɛ anyɩ deli nwʋmɔ nɩ, mɛyɩ sʋ̃ la yɛ bɛ anyɩ deli nwʋmɔ a. Naasʋ̃ mmeliyema be wɔ kɛnɩ sʋ̃ mɔɔ yɛtɩ nyanlɩ kɛɛ yelókula yɛkɛva bɛ ɔhɔnwʋ. Mɩnla ti a? Itwu mmɩlɩ be a, yɛ adwɩnɩ iléyiyia wɔ nninye-ni be nwu na sa eyi kʋla ma ɩyɛ sɩ kɛɛ yɛkeyike bɛ. Ebiye a, iluwa-kɛɛ ɩsʋnɩ mɔɔ ye nu ɔkʋ̃ bela yɩ anyɩ deli nwʋmɔ na yɛáda yɛbɔ numɔ yede yɛ anyɩ nɩ ti, ɩkɔhʋla ɩkɛyɛ sɩ kɛɛ yɛ nɩɩ yɛ nwu kɔwɔ. Na sɛ eliyema be iyiyiaa ndwokɔzɩzɩbɛ anzɛnyɩ̃ atutu wɔ yɩ ɛbɩla-wɔlɛ nu a, mɛyɩ sʋ̃ ɩkʋla ma ɩyɛ sɩ kɛɛ yɛkɔhɔlɩ zʋlɔ yɛkɛva yɩ ɔhɔnwʋ. Wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ eyi nu nɩ, yekehila kezimɔ Gyisɛsɩ yɩ
