Kɛ ankɩ yekozunzu ahyanɩ pɔkyɩɩ mɔɔ bɔkɔhʋla kodwe ye dedi kehila nɩ nwʋ nɩ, bɛmá yɛva yɛ adwɩnɩ yeziye nɔhʋanlɛ ndwɔkɔ asã be mɔɔ ɩkɔwʋwa yɛ ma yekegyina pindinyii távalɩ nwʋmɔ odwohilalɛ bela mɔɔ yekeyiyia nɩ. Ɔzɔ nɔhʋanlɛ ndwɔkɔ yɩ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ: (1) Gyehova yɛ ɩwɔlɩ nninyeni munwatĩĩ yɩ a. (2) Gyehova yɛ ɩmanlɩ bɛhɛlɛlɩ Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ a. (3) Gyehova yɛ ɩbʋwaa yi mmenii wɔ nnɛ a. Sɛ yɛkɔ zʋlɔ yenyia anwʋlazʋ kʋlaa kʋlaa wɔ ɔzɔ nɔhʋanlɛ ndwɔkɔ asã eyi nu a, ɩkɔwʋwa ma yɛkɔhɔlɩ zʋlɔ yɛkɛva nɔhʋanlɛlilɛ yekegyina ye dedi nwʋ odwohilalɛ bela mɔɔ yekeyiyia nɩ munwa.

7 Kĩnga Yikile 4:11. Bayɩbʋlʋ nu odwokɔ eyi kakɛ yɛ kɛɛ Gyehova yɛ ɩwɔlɩ nninyeni munwala a. Eyi ma yɛnwʋ yɩ kɛɛ ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛsɔ̃mɩ yɩ. Sɛ yɛdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ yi tumi mɔɔ ɩlɛ nɩ nwʋ a, ɩma yenyia anwʋlazʋ kɛɛ ɩkɔhʋla ikele yɛ wɔ yɛ odwohilalɛ bela nu. Naasɔ̃ Setanɩ duwa nnahtlɩbɛ ɔhvanɩ zʋ ma mmenii tɩ nyanlɩ kɛɛ Nyamɩnlɩ bela íni ɛkɛnɩ. Sayɩnsɩmaa be po kã kɛɛ oyuwadɩ yalɩ la, na kɛ abiyela yɛ ɩwɔlɩ a. Sɛ yɛáhyɛ anwʋlazʋ mɔɔ yɛwɔ wɔ Gyehova nu kɛɛ mɛyɩ yɛ lɛ Dɩyɛwɔlɛvʋlɔ na ɩdɔ yɛ nɩ nu ɛzɩzɩbɛ a, ɩkɛma sɛ yeyiyiaa odwohilalɛ a, yɛkɛdɩ nyanlɩ kɛɛ íni kɛnɩ anzɛnyɩɩ ɩlɔ́kʋla kɔwʋwa yɛ.—Hib. 11:6.
