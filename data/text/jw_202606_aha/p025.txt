17 Adwɩnɩhilelɛ mvʋlɔ eyimmɔ sʋ̃ kɔhʋla kɔ-wʋwa wʋ ma ɛkɛyɛ azʋnlɩ bʋwɔ paa wɔ dɩyɛ-zumanlɛ tʋ. (1) Sɛ ekeli alɩyɛ ansaana wʋahɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ a, áma edi yɩ sũmaa ama wʋálalɩ. (2) Twu kɛnɩ ndɛ na pɛ dɩka bʋwa ansaana egyi-malilɛ yɩ tawɔ yɩ tʋ. (3) Fa wu foonu anzɛnyɩɩ tabɩlɛtɩ yɩ tiã silent, na áma ɛma ɩtwɩnhwĩ wu adwɩnɩ mmɩlɩ mɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ yɩ ɩkɔlɩ zʋlɔ nɩ. (4) Sunzu mɔɔ ndɛnɩmanlɛvʋlɔ yɩ ɩbahãã nwʋmɔ odwokɔ nɩ nwʋ. (5) Fa wʋ adwɩnɩ siye ndɛnɩmanlɛvʋlɔ yɩ zʋ. (6) Sa ɔkʋla kɛlɛ ndwo-kɔ nzikalɛ be wɔ kɛlɛta zʋ anzɛnyɩɩ ɔdʋlɔdʋlɔ nninyeni be mɔɔ ɩkɛma ɛkɛhakɛ ndwokɔ aditili mɔɔ bɛkãã nwʋmɔ odwokɔ nɩ. (7) Bɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ɔkɔnwʋ kezimɔ ɛhɛlɛlɛndwokɔ azʋlɔtʋ nɩ falɩ mɔɔ bɛkãã nwʋmɔ odwokɔ nɩ nwʋ. (8) Bi-zia wʋ nwʋ kɛɛ, ‘Mɩnla ɔhʋanɩ zʋ yɛ mɩkɔhʋla mɩkɛva eyi mɩkɔwʋwa abiye wɔ asaafʋ yɩ nu anzɛnyɩɩ azɩlɛ-sini yi nu a?’ (9) Ansaana ɔkɔ-hɔ mansini anzɛnyɩɩ manlɩ-avinli ayiyialɩyɛ nɩ, kila nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ kɛlɛta yɩ nu na kɩnga ɛhɛlɛlɛ-dwokɔ mɔɔ ɔzɔ eyile yi egyimalilɛ yɩ gyĩ zʋlɔ, ndɛnɩ bela yi odwokɔtile, nɩɩ ɛhɛlɛlɛndwokɔ mɔɔ ndɛnɩ bela gyĩ zʋlɔ nɩ. (10) Bʋwa azɩ dinyii na yɛ azʋnlɩ tiye ndwomi mɔɔ bɛyɛ yɩ vidiyo nɩ na ama wʋahʋla wʋaziyeziye wʋ ahʋnlɩnɩ wʋama egyimalilɛ yɩ nɩ.

18 Gyehova duwa ehilehilelɛ mɔɔ yɛ sa kã yɩ wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʋ nɩ zʋ fa yɛ “duwa ɛdɩnyɛlɛ ɔhʋanɩ zʋ.” Eyi ti, bɛmá yɛhɔ zʋlɔ yɛyɛ azʋnlɩ mmɩlɩ mɔ yɛhɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ nɩ. (Ndw. 23:3; 31:3) Yehyinyia Gyehova yɩ ɔhʋanɩhilelɛ na ama yɛahɔ zʋlɔ yɛayɛ mɔɔ ɩtɩnyɛ. Meti, bɛmá ‘yehila bʋwɔ wɔ kɛ yesi yetiye odwokɔ nɩ’ nwʋ na yɛva mɔɔ yɛtɩ wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʋ nɩ munwa-la yeli egyima wɔ yɛ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu. (Lk. 8:18) Bɛ-zabɛ mɔɔ betiye Gyehova nɩ kenyia anyɩnlɩlelɛ wɔ nnɛ na bɛanyia nnwʋanlɩ mɔɔ íni awielɩyɛ wɔ eyile be sɔ̃.—Ndw. 119:2, 14.
