dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʋ. Yehyinyia Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ ɛhakɛlɛ ndwokɔ na ama yɛahʋla yɛagyina ndwokɔzɩzɩbɛ sʋ̃maa mɔɔ yeyiyiaa wɔ anwʋhyɩlɩlɛ mmɩlɩ eyi nu nɩ yɩ munwa. (2 Tim. 3:1) Sɛ mɔɔ yetiyee nɩ, yɛtɩ nyanlɩ kɛɛ yɛdɩ nwʋmɔ odwokɔ mbɔmɩ sʋ̃maa po a, ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛbɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ yɛkenyia adwɩnɩ mɔɔ nɩ ndwomiduledivolɔ mɔɔ thɛlɛlɩ Ndwomi 119 ɩlɛ wɔ Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ ɛhakɛlɛ ndwokɔ nwʋ nɩ be.—Kɩnga Ndwomi 119:24, 111, 167.

15 Nwʋmɔ hyinyia paa kɛɛ yɛkɔ zʋlɔ yebu ehilehilelɛ mɔɔ yɛ sa kã yɩ nɩ kɛɛ akyɛdɩ mɔɔ ɩzɔ̃ Gyehova kɛnɩ. Sa yɛkʋla yesuma debe wɔ mɔɔ ɩdʋlɩ Yizilayamaa yɩ nu. Mmɩlɩ mɔ nɩ boluwa ɛsɛlɛ yɩ zʋ nɩ, Gyehova manlɩ mana bɛ. (Ɛkɩ. 16:15, 31) Na ahyɛasɩ yɩ, nɩ bɛ anyɩ deli nwʋmɔ paa. Nɩ mana yɩ yɛ fɛ, na ɩmanlɩ bɔhɔlɩ zʋlɔ bɔwʋwalɩ nnwʋanlɩ nu. Naasʋ̃ mmɩlɩ kɔlɩ zʋlɔ nɩ, ɩyɩlɩ mbiyemmɔ meti nɩ bɛ anyɩ iáleli nwʋmɔ dɔkʋ̃. (Nɔ. 21:5) Mɩnla yɛ yɛkɔhʋla yekozuma wɔ odwokɔ eyi nu a? Ndwokɔ mɔɔ yɛtɩ wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʋ nɩ, ebiye wɔ kɛnɩ mɔɔ yɛdɩ nwʋmɔ odwokɔ mbɔmɩ sʋ̃maa, naasʋ̃ íni kɛɛ yenyia adwɩnɩ kyĩ kɛɛ nwʋmɔ iáhyinyia. Yɛábɛ kɛɛ yɛyɛ kɛɛ Yizilayamaa yɩ mɔɔ bɛákile nninyeni nwʋ anyɩnlɩzɔlɛ nɩ. Mfomi yɛpɛ kɛɛ yɛkakɛ kɛɛ, nninyeni mɔɔ yesuma wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʋ nɩ dɩ akyɛdɩ mɔɔ ɩzɔ̃ Gyehova kɛ. Meti ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛma nninyeni mɔɔ yesuma wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʋ nɩ nwʋ hyinyia yɛ paa. Akʋ̃ sʋ̃, ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛkakɛ kɛɛ Gyehova yɩ ahyɩhyɛdɩ yɩ siyeziye nninyeni mɔɔ yesuma nɩ bʋwɔ paa ansaana bɛava bɛayɩlɛ yɛ ma yɛazuma. Sɛ yɛfa nninyeni mɔɔ bɛfa bekilehile yɛ nɩ yedi egyima wɔ yɛ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu a, ɩkɔhʋla ɩkɛma yɛkenyia nnwʋanlɩ mɔɔ íni awielɩyɛ.—Ɛyɛ. 3:13, 16-18; Dwɔ. 17:3.

16 Mɔɔ ɩkɔwʋwa yɛ ma yɛkɛyɛ azʋnlɩ paa wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʋ. Kila mɔɔ ɩwʋwa Gyehova yɩ azʋ̃mɩvʋlɔ be mɔɔ bɔzʋ̃mɩ bɛhyɛ nɩ ma bɔhɔ zʋlɔ bɛyɛ azʋnlɩ wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʋ. Ashley, mɔɔ ɩva alʋbɔ 30 nu yɩ mɛda ɩzʋ̃mɩ Gyehova nɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ: "Sɛ misiyeziye mʋ nwʋ ansaana mɩahɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ a, ɩbʋwa mɩ ma mɩyɛ azʋnlɩ bʋwɔ paa. Naasʋ̃ sɛ miásiyeziye mʋ nwʋ a, mɩáhʋla mɩma munwalɩyɛ na mɩáhʋla mɩfa mɩ adwɩnɩ misiye egyimalilɛ yɩ zʋ sõ."
