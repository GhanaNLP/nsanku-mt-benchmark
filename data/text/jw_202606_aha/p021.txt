bɛfa be ehilehilelɛ bekyina Ɛhɛlɛlɛdwokɔ yɩ zʋ. (Egy. 17:2, 3; 18:24, 28) Ɔzɔ la yɛ nnɛ sʋ̃, kɛ Gyehova Dasɩvʋlɔmaa nɩ, yɛfa ndwokɔ mɔɔ ɩwɔ yɛ awuuku azʋlɔtʋ nu, broadcasting egyimalilɛ azʋlɔtʋ, ndɛnɩ azʋlɔtʋ mɔɔ yetiye, nɩɩ ye vidiyo azʋlɔtʋ yekyina Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ zʋ a. Sɛ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛba yɛ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʋ nɩ ba nwʋ kezimɔ yɛfa Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ yekilehile ndwokɔ nu nɩ a, ɩyɛ bɛ asɩbɔnvʋ paa. Meti sɛ abiye wɔ egyimalilɛ wɔ ayiyialɩyɛ mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ tʋ a, láyɩnga Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ kɛkɛ la, mfomi ɩbɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ɩkɔwʋwa ediyelemaa yɩ ma bɔkɔnwʋ kezimɔ bɛkɛva bekeli egyima wɔ bɛ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu.—Lk. 24:32.

4 Sɛ Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ fa odwokɔpɔkɔ “tiye” nɩɩ “yɛ azʋnlɩ” di egyima a, mbɔmɩ sʋ̃maa nɩ ikile kɛɛ ɛkɛyɛ sutiye kɛma abiye. Meti, sɛ Gyehova duwa yi Odwokɔ yɩ zʋ kilehile yɛ wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʋ na yetɩ mɔɔ ɩpɛ kɛɛ yɛyɛ nɩ tʋ a, ɩma ɩyɛ mɛlɛkɛ kɛɛ yekediye yi. Yezi paa kɛɛ, sɛ yɛyɛ azʋnlɩ yetiye Gyehova wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʋ na yɛfa mɔɔ ikilehile yɛ nɩ yedi egyima a, ɩma yɩ anyɩ de baaba paa.—Ɛyɛ. 27:11.

Adwɩnɩhilelɛ Azʋlɔtʋ Mɔɔ Ɩkɛma Yekediye Odwokɔ Bʋwɔ
<<<P>>>
Beyiziaɣizialɩ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛva alʋbɔ sʋ̃maa bɔzũmɩ Gyehova nɩ be ma bɛhanlɩ mɔɔ ɩma bɔkʋla bɛyɛ azʋnlɩ wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʋ. Na mɔɔ bɛhanlɩ yɩ yɛ ilili zʋlɔ yɩ a:
<<<P>>>
• Suefawn, mɔɔ inlumanlɩ wɔ 1986 nɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ, “Sɛ ɔkɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ a, bʋwa anyunumi na ama wʋ adwɩnɩ ɩahɔ egyimalilɛ yɩ zʋ na wʋanwʋ vidiyo azʋlɔtʋ nɩ sɔ̃ bʋwɔ.”
<<<P>>>
• David, mɔɔ inlumanlɩ wɔ 1983 nɩ sʋ̃ hanlɩ kɛɛ, “Mʋbɔ Gyehova mpayɩ mbɔmɩ sʋ̃maa kɛɛ ɩwʋwá mɩ ma mɩva mɩ adwɩnɩ miziye dɩyɛzumanlɛ yɩ zʋ. Akɔ̃ mɩkakɛ mʋ nwʋ kɛɛ, dɩyɛzumanlɛ mɔɔ mʋkɔhɔ, mɩ adwɩnɩ mɔɔ mɩkɛva mikeziye egyimalilɛ yɩ zʋ, nɩɩ mʋ nwʋ mɔɔ mɩkɛva mukowula egyimalilɛ yɩ nu munwala kɩnla mʋ ɔzũmɩnlɛ nwʋ.”
<<<P>>>
• Nicolas, mɔɔ inlumanlɩ wɔ 1982 nɩ sʋ̃ hanlɩ kɛɛ, “Sɛ abiye di ɔfʋã be nwʋ egyima na ɩkã odwokɔ be, ɩfa ndianwʋ anzɛnyɩɩ ndindianwʋ be idi egyima na wʋ anyɩ deli nwʋmɔ a, fa siye wʋ anyɩ zʋ kɛɛ ɛkɛhamvʋ yɩ.”
<<<P>>>
• Augustin, mɔɔ inlumanlɩ wɔ 1988 nɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ, “Sɛ Ɔhʋanɩhilelɛ Okuwu nɩ fa debe mɔɔ yezuma yɩ kyĩ ba kɛɛ yeziá yezuma a, mɩkã mikile mʋ nwʋ kɛɛ, ɩdɩ sunzuma ɔfʋã nu alɩyɛ mɔɔ ɩɣa wɔ yɩ mmɩlɩ nu.”

hyʋɔ nɩ wʋwa yɛ ma yɛnwʋ subanɩ azʋlɔtʋ be mɔɔ ɩkɔhʋla ɩkɔwʋwa yɛ wɔ dasɩlɛlilɛ nu. Asʋ̃ ɩyɛ sɩ ɩma wʋ kɛɛ ɛ nɩɩ mmenii kɔwɔ nkɔmmɔwɔlɛ tʋ a? Mɛyɩ nɩ sɛ wʋ mmeliyema bedii ɔzɔ ɔfʋã yɩ nwʋ egyima wɔ dapɛnɩ avinli dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʋ a, yɛ azʋnlɩ tiye yɩ bʋwɔ paa.
