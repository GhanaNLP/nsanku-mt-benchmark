NDWOMI 89
Tiye Nyamınlı Odwokɔ Na Nyia Nyilalɛ
<<<P>>>
BƆTAYI
Yɛkɔnwu sianti mɔɔ sɛ ye-yiyia numɔ kɛɛ yesumaa Gyehova nwu dɩyɛ a, ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛyɛ azunlɩ yetiye egyi-malilɛ yɩ nɩ, nɩɩ kezimɔ yɛ-kɔhula yɛkɛyɛ zɔ.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ Azunlɩ Mmɩlɩ Mɔ Gyehova Ikilehilee Wʋ Nɩ
<<<P>>>
“Bɛhilá bʋwɔ wɔ kɛ besi betiye odwokɔ nɩ.”—LK. 8:18.
<<<P>>>
NNƐ, Gyehova fa sunzuma ɔfʋã nu alɩyɛ sũmaa ma yɛ. Meti yɛ nɩɩ Devidi yɛ adwɩnɩ mmɩlɩ mɔ tɩanlɩ kɛɛ, “Gyehova ɩdɩ mɩ Kilavʋlɔ. Dɩyɛ bela iléhyinyia mɩ” nɩ.—Kɩnga Ndwomi 23:1, 2, 5.
<<<P>>>
2 Gyehova duwa yɛ asafo ayiyialɩyɛ azulɔtu zʋ fa sunzuma ɔfʋã nu alɩyɛ ma yɛ wɔ nnɛ. Lye a, ndwokɔ be mɔɔ yɛtɩ nɩ, ídɩ debe fʋlɔ kʋlaa. Naasũ sɛ yɛ tu dimi wɔ nɔhuanlɛ yɩ nu kɛ mɩnla la po a, ihyinyia kɛɛ bɔkɔ zʋlɔ bɛkakɛ kakɛ yɛ. (2 Pit. 1:12) Nninye-ni be mɔɔ yɛdɩ nwʋmɔ odwokɔ daada wɔ yɛ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʋ nɩ, sɛ ɩbayɩ kɛɛ mɛyɩ la yɛ beta bekã nwʋmɔ odwokɔ a, asũ ɩyɛ sɩ ɩma wʋ kɛɛ ɛkɛyɛ azunlɩ kediye a? Mɛyɩ nɩ dɩyɛzumanlɛ eyi kɔwʋwa wʋ paa. Yɛkɔnwu sianti mɔɔ ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛyɛ azunlɩ wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʋ. Yekezia yɛkɔnwu mɔɔ mmeliyema mɔɔ bekilehile wɔ asafo yɩ nu nɩ kɛyɛ kɔwʋwa mmenii mvʋlɔ ma bɛkɛyɛ azunlɩ wɔ dɩyɛzu-manlɛ tʋ. Akʋ̃ yekehila nninyeni azulɔtʋ be mɔɔ ɩkɔhula ɩkɔ-wʋwa ye nu bela ma ɩkɛyɛ ediyelɛvʋlɔba. Naasũ ehilehilelɛ mɔɔ yɛ sa kã yɩ wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʋ nɩ, ɩzɔ̃ aninyia kɛnɩ a? Bɛmá yehi-la nwʋmɔ munwalɩyɛ yɩ.

KAKƐ KƐƐ GYEHOVA YƐ IKILEHILEE YƐ A
<<<P>>>
3 Gyehova duwa yi odwokɔ Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ zʋ kilehile yɛ. (2 Tim. 3:16, 17) Nɩ Gyisɛsɩ zɩ eyi, meti nɩ ɩkʋla ɩfa Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ Odwokɔ yɩ idi egyima bʋwɔ paa. Eyi hanlɩ yi ediyelɛmaa nkɔ̃ɔ̃ ma bɛhanlɩ kɛɛ: “Benyia eyi mɔɔ ɩákɔ sukuulu donwuu yɩ, kɛ ɩyɛ mɔɔ izi ɔnwʋma kezile ɔzɔ yɩ a?” (Dwɔ. 7:15) Naasũ mmɩlɩ mɔ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɔwɔ ahʋnlɩnɩba bɛdʋlɩ Gyisɛsɩ yi ehilehilelɛ nɩ, bɛ anyɩ leli paa. Kilisinyiamaa mɔɔ bɔwʋwalɩ azɩ wɔ ɔwɔkɩ mmɩlɩ nu dɩkɛɛ sũmavʋlɔ Pɔɔlʋ nɩɩ Apɔlɔsʋ zumazumanlɩ Gyisɛsɩ. Nɩ bɛzabɛ sʋ̃
<<<P>>>
1-2. (a) Mɩnla ɔhʋanlɩ zʋ yɛ Gyehova fa sunzuma ɔfʋã nu alɩyɛ ma yɛ wɔ nnɛ a? (Ndwomi 23:1, 2, 5) (b) Mɩnla yɛ yɛbazunzu nwʋmɔ wɔ dɩyɛzumanlɛ eyi nu a?
<<<P>>>
3. Mɩnla ɔhʋanlɩ zʋ yɛ Gyehova kilehile yɛ a, na mɩnla ɔhʋanlɩ zʋ yɛ yɩ azũmɩvʋlɔ fa yi Odwokɔ yɩ di egyima a?
