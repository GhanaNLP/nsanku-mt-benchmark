18 Kɛ ɛkɛyɛ na wʊama ɔhɔnwʊvalɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ ɛ nɩ abiye avinli nɩ iahɔ zʊlɔ ɩawʊwa kɛ a? Da kunlumiba nɩ kunlumiyɩbalɛ ali. Ɔzɔ mɔɔ ɛkɛyɛ nɩ kɛma ɔzʊ̃ wʊ ahʊnlɩnɩ nu kɛyɩk yı wu mmeliyema mɔɔ bɔnwʊv amanyɛ anzɛnyɩı beyiyiaa ɔhawʊ azʊlɔtʊ be nɩ bɛ ɛyɛɛlɛ. (Kɩnga 1 Tɛsalonikamaa 5:14, NWT.) Kakɛ kɛɛ ɔhɔnwʊba mɛyı, iziyeziye yı nwʊ eyile bela kɛɛ ɩkɔwʊwa yı ɔhɔnwʊ, titili wɔ “anwʊhyɩlɩyɛ mmɩlɩ” nu. (Ɛyɛ. 17:17) Ɔhɔnwʊ mɔɔ ɩdɩ zɔ nɩ iányia nyanlɩdɩlɛ kɛkɛ ma yı ɔhɔnwʊ, mfomi ɩbɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ɩkɛyɩk yı ɛyɛɛlɛ na ɩayɛ nninyeni pɔkyɩɩ be ɩawʊwa yı.

19 Mɩnla nninyeni azʊlɔtʊ be yɛ ɔkɔhʊla kɛyɛ kɛva kɛla kunlumiba nɩ kunlumiyɩbalɛ ali a? Iwɔ numɔ, ɔlɔkʊla kɛyı wu mmeliyema bɛ ɛyɛkɛ nu bɛ atɩtɩɩ wɔ kɛnɩ dɩyɛ, naasʊ̃ sa ɔkʊla ɛyɛ nninyeni be mɔɔ wʊ anwʊzɩzɩbɛ kɔhʊla fa kile kɛɛ ɛtɩ kezimɔ bɛtɩ nyanlɩ nu mɔɔ boduwaa numɔ nɩ tʊ. (Mt. 7:12; 1 Pit. 3:8) Sɛ wʊ ɔhɔnwʊ yı ɩkãã yı ɔhawʊ yı nwʊ odwokɔ ikehile wʊ a, yɛ azʊnlɩ tiye yi na bɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ɛkɛhã ndwokɔ be mɔɔ ɩkɛyɩk yı ɛyɛɛlɛ. (Ɛyɛ. 12:25) Máyɛ wʊ adwɩnɩ kɛɛ ezi ɔhawʊ mɔɔ iduwaa numɔ nɩ munwala mmɩlɩ mɔ ézi. (Ɛyɛ. 18:13) Bɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ekenyia yı nwʊ abutalɩ na mákila ɔhvanɩ kɛɛ ɩkɛyɛ yı dɩyɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ.—Ɛfɩ. 4:32.

20 Ɔhɔnwʊmmɔba mɔɔ yenyia bɛ wɔ Gyehova yı ahyɩhyɛdɩ yı nu nɩ, bɔtʊ bulɔ bɛma yɛ paa. Naasʊ̃ ye munwala yɛáli munli na yeyiyiaa ɔhawʊ azʊlɔtʊ be wɔ yɛ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu. Eyi ti, ɩyɛ a, yɛyɛ nninyeni azʊlɔtʊ be anzɛnyɩɩ yɛkɩnhã ndwokɔ be mɔɔ idide ye mmeliyema. Meti sɛ ye mmeliyema da kãnwvazɩ ali, benyia yɛ nwʊ abʊtalɩ, bɛda kunlumiba nɩ kunlumiyɩbalɛ ali bekile yɛ, na bɔkɔ zʊlɔ bɛyɛ yɛ ɔhɔnwʊmmɔ a, yɛ anyɩ de paa. Eyi ti, bɛmá yɛzayɛ sʊ̃ yɛhɔ zʊlɔ yɛyɛ ɔhɔnwʊmmɔba yɛma ye mmeliyema na yɛla subanɩ eyimmɔ ali yehile bɛzabɛ sʊ̃.
