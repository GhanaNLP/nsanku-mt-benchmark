Nɩ aɣanɩ fulɔ nɩ pɛ kɛɛ inyia azogyiamaa sũmaa mɔɔ bɔ nwu yɛ sɩ. Eyi manlɩ nninyeni nu yɛlɩ sɩ manlɩ Gyehova Dasɩvɔlɔmaa paa, iluwakɛɛ bɛáhɔ kunɛ na bɛáva bɔ nwu bowula manlɩwulɛ ndwokɔ nu. Eyi ti, aɣanɩ yɩ valɩ yɩ sa dianlɩ ye egyima yɩ zʋ. Nɩ yéni fanwudi mɔɔ yɛkɛva kɔzũmɩ Gyehova dɔkɔ̃, na bɛvalɩ mmeliyema sũmaa sɔ̃ bogualɩ fiyiadɩ. Nɩ bɛta bɛyɛ bo nu be ayɛkwa na alɩyɛ twɔkɩlɩ be la nɩ bɛfa bɛma bɛ a. Itwu mmɩlɩ be po a, nɩ mmegyia wɔ alɩyɛ mɔɔ bɛfa bɛma bɛ nɩ nu, naasũ kɛmɔ yezi nɩ, Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ kã kɛɛ áma yedi mmegyia.

Lávalɩ nwʋmɔ kɛɛ nɩ bɛva salɛ bediã ye egyima yɩ zʋ nɩ, nɩ yɛlanwʋ la yeyiyia numɔ yɛsũmɩ Gyehova. (Hib. 10:25) Nɩ yeyiyia numɔ wɔ ɔwʋlɔtʋ nu nnɩka mvʋlɔ wɔ manlɩ yɩ nu yɛyɛ ayiyialɩyɛ. Mɩkakɛ paa kɛɛ eyile ɔkʋ̃ be, eliyema be manlɩ yɛvalɩ yɩ mmuwuanɩ obuwu yɛyɛlɩ ayiyialɩyɛ. Yɛákula yɛyɩyɩ mmuwuanɩ yɩ wɔ obuwu yɩ nu na yɛahã kɛɛ yesiyeziyee obuwu yɩ nu po. Meti yɛ nɩɩ mmuwuanɩ yɩ munwala wɔlɩ numɔ yɛlɩ ayiyialɩyɛ yɩ!—Mik. 2:12.

Yɛ anyɩ sɔ egyimazɩzɩbɛ mɔɔ mmeliyema yɛlɩ wʋwalɩ yɛ ma yɛhɔlɩ zʋlɔ yenyianlɩ sunzuma ɔfʋã nu alɩyɛ wɔ ɔzɔ mmɩlɩ nɩ paa. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, nɩ bɔlʋkɔdʋ ayiyialɩyɛ egyimalilɛ yɩ munwala bokua kasɛtɩ zʋ na bɛkyɩkyɛ bɛma mmeliyema mɔɔ bɔwɔ Cuba nɩ munwala. Iyɛ po a, nɩ mmeliyema anwĩ la yɛ nɩ bɛma besiyeziye bɔ nwu bɛma ndɛnɩ azʋlɔtʋ mɔɔ bɛkɛma yɩ wɔ ayiyialɩyɛ yɩ tu nɩ munwala a. Sɛ besiyeziye bɔ nwu bowie a, akʋ̃ bɛma ndɛnɩ yɩ ma bɔlʋkɔdʋ bokua kasɛtɩ zʋ. Iluwakɛɛ nɩ mmeliyema yɩ bɔlʋkɔdʋ ndɛnɩ azʋlɔtʋ nɩ wɔ anwunzumi tʋ nɩ ti, nɩ ɩyɛ a yɛtɩ nnamɩ bo ozunɛ nɩɩ dede azʋlɔtʋ be mɔɔ ɩyɛ ɛzɩlɩkɛ wɔ nu. Nɩ sɛ yɛyɛɛ ayiyialɩyɛ yɩ wɔ dɩka mɔɔ layɩtɩ íni kɛnɩ a, mmeliyema yɩ fa mahyini be bɔ baskɩlɩ mɔɔ igyĩ dɩka ɔkʋ̃ nɩ nwʋ, na bɛma abiye padʋlɩ baskɩlɩ yɩ. Na sɛ bɛpadʋlɩɩ baskɩlɩ yɩ a, ɩma yɛ sa kã layɩtɩ wɔ mahyini mɔɔ bɛva bɔwɔ baskɩlɩ yɩ nwʋ nɩ nu. Eyi má bɔkula bɔbɔ egyimalilɛ yɩ ma ye munwala yetiye. Iwɔ numɔ kɛɛ nɩ yéni fanwʋdi mɔɔ yɛkɛva yɛkɔzũmɩ Gyehova, nɩɩ Bayɩbʋlʋ nwʋ awuuku sũmaa dɩkɛɛ mmeliyema mɔɔ bɔwɔ amanlɩ mvʋlɔ zʋ nɩ dɩyɛ, naasũ nɩ mɔɔ ɩkɛma yekeyike Gyehova mɛyɩ, nɩ yɛ sa kã yɩ. Amba, yɛ anyɩ leli kɛɛ yezũmɩnlɩ Gyehova yɛwɔlɩ numɔ.—Ne. 8:10.

Minyianlɩ alʋbɔ 18 nɩ, mʋwɔlɩ yɩ tʋ kɛɛ mɩyɛɛ ɔhʋanɩbukolɛ egyima yɩ wɔ Florida twonwa yɩ nu. Kɛyɛ ɔlʋbɔ ɔkʋ̃ mɛda nɩ, bɛmanlɩ mɩhɔ mʋzõmɩnlɩ kɛɛ ɔhʋanɩbukolɛvʋlɔ titili wɔ twonwa kezile be mɔɔ bɔfʋlɔ yɩ Camagüey nɩ nu. Ɛkɛnɩ yɛ miyiyialɩ eliyema baalɛ be mɔɔ bɔfʋlɔ yɩ Emilia mɔɔ ɩzʋ̃ Santiago de Cuba nɩ a. Nɩ yɩ nwʋ yɛ pɩkɛ paa. Yɛyɛlɩ agyiahʋnlɩ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ na wɔ aziyianɩ egyikĩĩ be mɛda nɩ, yegyialɩ.
