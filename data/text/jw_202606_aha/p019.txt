14 Odwokɔ tʋ yɛ a, yɛ okuwu zʋ ehilalɛmaa nɩ bʋwa yɛ. Sɛ adimakɛlɛ be ba anzɛnyɩ asianɩ be si a, yɛ dasɩlɛlilɛ okuwu zʋ ehilalɛmaa fa mmʋwalɩyɛ mɔɔ yehyinyia nɩ ma yɛ ndɛndɛ. Ɔzɔ la yɛ sɛ yehyinyia mmʋwalɩyɛ be wɔ hospital, ayanɩ twodwu tiyia yɛ, anzɛnyɩ manlɩ mɔɔ yɛda numɔ nɩ yi ozukuasɛmɩ yɛ basaa a, yɛ dasɩlɛlilɛ okuwu zʋ hilalɛmaa nɩ bʋwa yɛ ndɛndɛ a. Kɛ ndianwʋ nɩ, sɛ asianɩ be si a, yɛ dasɩlɛlilɛ okuwu zʋ hilalɛmaa nɩ fʋlɔ abusunwa bela anzɛnyɩ abi-yela mɔɔ ɩwɔ yi okuwu yi nu nɩ, na ibizia yɩ kɛɛ sɛ ikehyinyia mmʋwalɩyɛ be a. Sɛ bɔnwu yɩ kɛɛ eliyema be hyinyia nnwʋnlamɩ ɔfʋã nu nninyeni be a, bɛyɛ mɔɔ bɔkɔhʋla bela kɛɛ bɛkɛma yɩ sa kɛhã yɩ. Ɔzɔ la yɛ sɛ okuwu zʋ hilalɛmaa nɩ bɛba bɔnwu yɩ kɛɛ abiye wɔ bo okuwu yi nu mɔɔ ikehyinyia mmʋwalɩyɛ sũnvnkʋ be a, bɛ nɩ Asafo Ɔzũmɩnlɛ Kɔmitii nɩ sunzu nwʋmɔ, na bɛyɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ bɛfa mmʋwalɩyɛ bela mɔɔ ihyi-nyia nɩ bɛma yɩ. Nɔhʋanlɛ, sɛ odwokɔ tu yɛ a, yɛ okuwu zʋ hilalɛmaa nɩ bɛáli yɛ nwu ɛyɛlɛ kʋlaa!

15 Kɛmɔ yezi nɩ, Gyehova yɩ azũmɩvʋlɔ dɩ sũmaa zɛ. Wɔ sʋlɔ yɩzayɩ nkʋ̃ po nɩ, ɩlɛ awɔ-vʋlɔ abʋmɩ abʋmɩ sũmaa mɔɔ bɛlɛ nɔhʋanlɛlilɛ bɔsũmɩ yɩ. Na azɩlɛ yɩ zʋ sʋ̃ nɩ, ɩlɛ azũmɩvʋlɔ mɔɔ bɔbʋ miliyɔnʋ ahɔnla mɔɔ bɔ dʋdʋ nɩ, anyunumi la yɛ ɩkɔɔ a. (Zak. 8:23; Yik. 5:11) Naasʋ̃ Gyehova yɩ azũmɩvʋlɔ mɔɔ bɔwɔ sʋlɔ nɩɩ mɔɔ bɔwɔ azɩlɛ yɩ zʋ nɩ munwala bɔwɔ numɔ bɛlɛ ɔkʋ̃yɛlɛ bɛkãã Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ Bulombunlililɛ yɩ nwʋ odwokɔba yɩ! Egyima eyi mɔɔ bɔwɔ nu bɛyɛɛ nɩ kã bɛ bɔ numɔ paa. (Yik. 14:6, 7) Bɛmá yɛyɛ mɔɔ yɛkɔhʋla bela yenyia dasɩlɛlilɛ oku-wu nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ yɩ zʋ mfasʋ. Iluwakɛɛ mɛyɩ yɛ ɩbʋwa yɛ ma yɛzũ yɛ ahʋnlɩnɩ nu munwala yɛyɩ Gyehova ayɛlɛ a.—Ndw. 111:1
