<<<P>>>
Mfasʊ Mɔɔ lwɔ Dasɩlɛlilɛ Okuwu Nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ Yɩ Zʊ
<<<P>>>
“Mʊkɔzʊ̃ mɩ ahʊnlɩnɩ munwala nu mɩkɛyɩ Gyehova ayɛlɛ wɔ ɛdɩnyɛlemaa bɛ ayiyialɩyɛ tʊ nu asafo yɩ nu.”—NDW. 111:1.
<<<P>>>
ASŨ ɛkakɛ mmɩlɩ mɔ beleli wʊ bedianlɩ numɔ kɛɛ ɛyɛ́ ɛlawulowɔlɛvʊlɔ nɩ a? Pɩlazunlɛ bela íni nwʊmɔ kɛɛ wʊ anyɩ leli na ɔnwʊnlɩ wʊ nwʊ kɛɛ ɛkɩnla ɔlɔmɩ sʊ̃maa mɔɔ bɔsʊ̃mʊ Gyehova na bɛyɩɩ yi dima yɩ ayɛlɛ nɩ nwʊ. (Ndw. 148:1, 2, 12, 13) Naasʊ̃, ebiye a ɛya nwʊnlɩ yɩ sʊ̃ kɛɛ ɛkɩnla dasɩlɛlilɛ okuwu be nwʊ. Dasɩlɛlilɛ okuwu dɩ okuwu be mɔɔ mmeliyema mɔɔ bɔwɔ numɔ nɩ bɛásʊ̃. Bɛhyɩhyɛlɩ okuwu eyi na ɩawʊwa yɛ ma yɛahã Bulombunlililɛ odwokɔba yɩ mɔɔ Gyisɛsɩ va wula yɛ sa nɩ wɔ “oyuwadɩ dɩka bela.”—Mt. 24:14.
<<<P>>>
2 Kila kezimɔ mmeliyema mbiyemmɔ nwʊ dasɩlɛlilɛ okuwu mɔɔ bɛhyɩhyɛ nɩ. Eliyema baalɛ be mɔɔ bɔfʊlɔ yɩ Wendy* hanlɩ kɛɛ: “Dasɩlɛlilɛ okuwu nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ nɩ wʊwa mɩ ma mɩ dasɩlɛlilɛ du mpʊnɩ paa. Iluwakɛɛ ɩwʊwa mɩ ma muzuma nninyeni mvʊlɔ mɩvalɩ dasɩlɛlilɛ nwʊ. Iwʊwa mɩ sʊ̃ ma mʊnwʊ ahvanɩ mvʊlɔ mɔɔ mukoluwa zʊ mikeli mmenii dasɩlɛ. Ima mʊhʊla mɩva bʊtayɩ azʊlɔtʊ miziye mɩ anyɩ zʊ na mudwu nwʊmɔ na ɩma minyia ɔhɔnwʊmmɔba sʊ̃.” Eliyema benyia be mɔɔ bɔfʊlɔ yɩ Rich sʊ̃ hanlɩ kɛɛ: “Mʊnwʊ okuwu zʊ hilalɛvʊlɔ mɔɔ mɩdɩ nɩ kɛɛ ɔnwʊmɔ ɔhvanɩ sʊnvnkʊ̃ be mɔɔ minyia. Iluwakɛɛ sɛ ɛlawulowɔlɛvʊlɔ be bʊwa abiye ma inuma a, ɩdɩ okuwu zʊ hilalɛvʊlɔ yɩ asɛdɩ kɛɛ ɩkɔwʊwa enii mɔɔ ɩnluma nɩ ma ɩkɔhɔlɩ zʊlɔ ɩkenyi wɔ sunzuma nu.” Eliyema baalɛ be sʊ̃ mɔɔ bɔfʊlɔ yɩ Dinah hanlɩ kɛɛ: “Mamɩ mɛyɩ, dasɩlɛlilɛ okuwu nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ yɩ nɩ kã mɩ ahʊnlɩnɩ paa iluwakɛɛ mʊnwʊ mɩ dasɩlɛlilɛ okuwu yi kɛɛ mɩ abusunwa!”
