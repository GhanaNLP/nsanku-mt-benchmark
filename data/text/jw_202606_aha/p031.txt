Galatiyiamaa 4:24 yɩ a?
<<<P>>>
◼ Mmelɩ mɔ sɔ̃mavɔlɔ Pɔɔlʋ hanlɩ abusunwawɔlɛ
mɔɔ ɩwɔ Abilehamɩ, Sɛla, nɩɩ Hega bɛ avinli nwʋ
odwokɔ iwieli nɩ, izia lhanlɩ kɛɛ: “Ɔzɔ dɩyɛ nɩ ɩkã
odwokɔ mɔɔ yɛkʋla yɛtɩ yɩ tu ikile yɛ. Mmaalɛ anwĩ
nɩ begyĩ ɛkɛnɩ bɛma nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ anwĩ.” (Gal. 4:22-
24) Mmelɩ be mɔɔ ɩɣa ɩzĩ lhɔ nɩ, nɩ yɛtɩ yɩ tu kɛɛ
nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ anwĩ nɩ yɛ lɛ Mmɛla nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ yɩ nɩɩ
nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ fʋlɔ nɩ. Naasʋ̃ mmelɩ mɔ yezia yezu-
nzunli odwokɔ eyi nwʋ yɛhɔlɩ aɣinzi nɩ, yɛɣa
yɛnwʋnlɩ kɛɛ ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛyɛ numɔ nzɩnzanlɩyɛ. Ikɛyɛ
kɛɛ nɩ Pɔɔlʋ ɩlékã nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ fʋlɔ yɩ nwʋ odwokɔ,
mfomi nɩ ɩkãã Abilehamɩ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ yɩ nwʋ odwo-
kɔ. Sianti mɔɔ yɛyɛlɩ ɔzɔ nzɩnzanlɩyɛ yɩ be yɛ ilili
zulɔ yɩ a.*

Pɔɔlʋ hanlɩ kɛɛ abaawa Hega gyĩ kɛnɩ ma File
Sɩnayɩ. (Gal. 4:25) Eyi kile kɛɛ Mmɛla nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ
nɩ mɔɔ Gyehova nɩɩ Yizilayamaa yɛlɩ wɔ File Sɩnayɩ
zu wɔ ɔlʋbɔ 1513 A.Y.M nu nɩ, igyĩ kɛnɩ ɩma Hega.
(Ɛkɩ. 19:5, 6) Na enii bela sʋ̃ íni kɛnɩ mɔɔ ɩkɔhʋla
ikeli Mmɛla yɩ zʋ pɛpɛɛpɛ. Meti ɔzɔ mmɛla yɩ ha-
kɛlɩ Yizilayamaa yɩ kɛɛ bɛdɩ alɔngɔ bɛma tanɩ. Akʋ̃
sʋ̃, nɩ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ eyi kɔwʋwa Yizilayamaa ma bɔ-
kɔnwʋ Mɛzaya yɩ nɩ. Mɛyɩ la yɛ ɩdɩ enii mɔɔ ɩkɔhʋla
ikeli Mmɛla yɩ zʋ pɛpɛɛpɛ a. Mmelɩ mɔ Mɛzaya yɩ
valɩ yɩ nnwʋanlɩ mɔɔ idi munli ɩwɔlɩ afɔlɩ nɩ, ɩmanlɩ
adasamaa mɔɔ bɛáli munli nɩ nyianlɩ nwʋmɔ
ɔhʋanɩ mɔɔ bɛkɛva bɛkele bɔ nwʋ wɔ tanɩ nɩɩ
owulo tʋ. (Gal. 3:19, 24, 25) Mmelɩ mɔ Gyisɛsɩ
manlɩ Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ tile nu pɔkɔ ɣalɩ numɔ nɩ, akʋ̃
dɩyɛ nɩ yɛáhyinyia Mmɛla yɩ dɔkɔ̃.—Wul. 10:4.

Ɔzɔ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ yɩ mɔɔ Gyehova nɩɩ Abilehamɩ
yɛlɩ nɩ hanlɩ anʋhʋba mɔɔ Gyehova wɔlɩ wɔ Edɩnɩ
nɩ nwʋ odwokɔ sũmaa. (Gyn. 3:15) Imanlɩ ɩlalɩ ali
kɛɛ, “yɩ tunii” mɔɔ bɛhanlɩ yɩ nwʋ odwokɔ nɩ, ɩkɔzũ
Abilehamɩ nu yɛ ɩkɛɣa a. Pɔɔlʋ hilehileli numɔ kɛɛ,
“yɩ tunii” paa nɩ yɛ lɛ Gyisɛsɩ Kɩlaysɩ. Naasʋ̃ mme-
nii 144,000 mɔɔ bɛ nɩɩ Kɩlaysɩ keli dɩyɛ wɔ yi
Bulombunlililɛ nu mɔɔ “bɛdɩ Kɩlaysɩ yɩ dɩyɛ” nɩ sʋ̃
kɩnla sɛnkyɩlɛdɩ ɔhʋanɩ zʋ “yɩ tunii” nɩ nwʋ. (Gal.
3:16, 29; Yik. 14:1-3) Iluwa anʋhʋba mɔɔ Gyehova
va wɔlɩ Abilehamɩ nɩ ti, mmenii mɔɔ bede Gyisɛsɩ
betiã numɔ na bɛyɛ sutiye bɛma yɩ nɩ kenyia nyila-
lɛ sũmaa zɛ.

Mɩnla ti mɔɔ Pɔɔlʋ hanlɩ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ anwĩ
eyi nwʋ odwokɔ a? Nɩ ɩmaa ɩkɛla ali kɛɛ, sɛ Dwuumaa mɔɔ bɔwɔ yɩ mmelɩ zʋ nɩ kɔ zʋlɔ fa Mosisi
Mmɛla yɩ kyɩkyɩ bɔ nwʋ a, bɔkɔhɔ zʋlɔ bɛkɛyɛ alɔ-
ngɔ dɩkɛɛ Hega. Naasʋ̃ sɛ bede Abilehamɩ “yɩ
tunii” ankasa mɔɔ mɛyɩ yɛ lɛ Gyisɛsɩ Kɩlaysɩ nɩ
betiã nu a, bekenyia fanwʋdi dɩkɛɛ Sɛla. Bekenyia
fanwʋdi kʋlakʋla wɔ tanɩ nɩɩ owulo tu. (Dwɔ. 8:32-
34) Naasʋ̃ sɛ bɛáde Gyisɛsɩ betiã nu a, nɩ ikile kɛɛ
bɛádɩ Mmɛla nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ nɩ yi tile nu pɔkɔ anka-
sa yɩ tu, iluwakɛɛ nɩ Mmɛla nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ nɩ yɛ ɩkɛma
bɔkɔnwʋ Kɩlaysɩ nɩ a.
<<<P>>>
Galatiyiamaa mɔɔ Pɔɔlʋ hɛlɛlɩ odwokɔ eyi hɔ
manlɩ bɛ nɩ, nɩ bo nu sũmaa bɛdɩ Dwuumaa, meti
nɩ béni Mmɛla yɩ tʋ. Naasʋ̃ nɩ Dwuumaa mɔɔ bɛɣa
bɛyɛ Kilisinyiamaa yɩ be bɛkãã yɩ ezɩzɩbɛ ezɩzɩbɛ
kɛɛ ɩsɛ kɛɛ Kilisinyiamaa mɔɔ bɛdɩ Dwuumaa nɩ
didili Mmɛla yɩ zʋ na bɛma botua be mmenyia zʋ.
Pɔɔlʋ hilehileli numɔ kɛɛ, nɣɛlɛbɛ íni numɔ kɛɛ bɔ-
kɔhɔ zʋlɔ bekelili Mmɛla nɩ mɔɔ ɩma bɛyɛ “alɔngɔ”
nɩ zʋ, iluwakɛɛ nɩ Kɩlaysɩ ɩma benyia “fanwʋdi.”
—Gal. 5:1, 10-14.
