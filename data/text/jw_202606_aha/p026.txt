ƐBƖLAWƆLƐ NU NDWOKƆ
<<<P>>>
Yɛva Kɛyɛ Alʊbɔ 70 Yezũmɩ Gyehova Wɔ Cuba
<<<P>>>
GUSTAVO JOSEPH YI MUNWA NU NDWOKƆ
<<<P>>>
BƆWƲLƖ mɩ wɔ ɔlʊbɔ 1947 wɔ pamɩ pɩkɛ pɩkɛ be mɔɔ ɩwɔ Cuba nɩ zʋ. Cuba wɔ dɩka mɔɔ Caribbean Ngyemvile yi nɩɩ Atlantic Ngyemvile yi yiyia nɩ. Mʋwɔ mmenlima nnwuakula mmaalɛ anwĩ. Na nɩ yɛ nɩɩ yɛ awʋvʋlɔ da suwa be mɔɔ ɩwɔ Esmeralda nɩ zʋ.
<<<P>>>
Mɩkakɛ kɛɛ nɩ azɔ̃nudwolɩyɛ wɔ suwa yɩ zʋ kɛnɩ paa. Nɩ yɛ nɩɩ ye mmusunwamaa yɩ be yɛ yɛda kɛnɩ a. Nɩ yɛwɔ mɔɔ yekeli nɩɩ ozukua egyɩkĩĩ mɔɔ yekɛva yekodudua yɛ nwʋ.
<<<P>>>
Ɩka egyɩkĩĩ ma minyia alʊbɔ anlu nɩ, eliyema Walton Jones wɔlɩ yɩ tʋ kɛɛ ɩ nɩɩ mɩ awʋvʋlɔ bosumaa Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ. Nɩ ɩdɩ odwokɔhanlɛvʋlɔ mɔɔ ɩbɔ ɛhyɩmɛ paa. Nɩ ɩfa dɔnɩhwɩlɩ kɛyɛ bunlu itiyia ɩba ye suwa yɩ zʋ. Eyile bela mɔɔ ɩkɛya nɩ, nɩ mu mmusunwamaa bo nu sɔ̃maa beyiyia numɔ wɔ mɩ nlabenyia nɩɩ mɩ nlabaalɛ bɛ awudo ma bɛfa dɔnɩhwɩlɩ sɔ̃maa bosuma Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ. Mɩ awʋvʋlɔ, mɩ ankʋlɩ Pedro nɩɩ mɩ antɩ Ela bɛ anyɩ lelili mɔɔ nɩ bosumaa nɩ nwʋ, na ɩákyɛ yɛ bonlumanlɩ bɛɣa bɛyɛlɩ Gyehova Dasɩvʋlɔmaa bo nu ɔkɔ̃ a. Ɩka egyɩkĩĩ ma Ela inyia alʊbɔ 100, naasʋ̃ ɩlanwʋ la ɩsɔ̃mɩ kɛɛ ɔhʋanɩbukoelɛvʋlɔ wɔ Cuba.
<<<P>>>
Ɔzɔ mmɩlɩ yɩ, nɩ bɛlɛfalɩ salɛ belétianlɩ Gyehova Dasɩvʋlɔmaa be egyima yɩ zʋ wɔ Cuba nɩ ti, nɩ yɛwɔ fanwʋdi mɔɔ yɛfa yɛsɔ̃mɩ Gyehova. Nɩ Cuba maa zi Gyehova Dasɩvʋlɔmaa bʋwɔ paa. Nɩ bezi bɛ kɛɛ bɛyɛ abika abika nu dasɩlelilɛ na ɔkɔnwʋ bɛ bela nɩ, nɩ bɛlɛ Bayɩbʋlʋ nwʋ awuuku sɔ̃maa wɔ bɛ baagɩ nu mɔɔ bɛfa bɛma mmenii. Nɩ yɛta yetiyia paa! Sɛ ɩyɛ na mɩkakɛ kezimɔ mu nnwuakula nu nɩ yɛfa ɛhyɩmɛ yɛsɔ̃mɩ Gyehova wɔ ɔzɔ “mmɩlɩ baaba” yɩ nu nɩ a, ɩma mɩ anyɩ de paa. Naasʋ̃ nɩ “mmɩlɩ tanɩ” be sɔ̃ luwa ɔhʋanɩ yɩ zʋ ɩbaa.—2 Tim. 4:2.
<<<P>>>
MMƖLƖ TANƖ ƔALƖ
<<<P>>>
Ɩka egyɩkĩĩ ma minyia alʊbɔ anlu nɩ, mɩ papa nɩɩ mɩ ankʋlɩ hɔlɩ ayiyialɩyɛ be wɔ pamɩ yɩ ɔfʋã dɩka be. Alɔbɔ odwokɔ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ bɔhɔ́ bɔnlʋnlɩ nzulo be mɔɔ mmʋwaba wɔ numɔ ma bɔ nwʋ balɩ bɛ. Mmɩlɩ mɔ bezia bɛɣalɩ awudo nɩ, mɩ ankʋlɩ yi ti zʋ eɣinli munwatĩĩ ɩzanlɩ naasʋ̃ ɩáwu. Naasʋ̃ mɩ papa mɛyɩ, iwu-li. Nɩ ili alʊbɔ 32 pɛ.
<<<P>>>
Mɩ papa yi wulo mɛda nɩ, mɩ maamɩ zili pɔkɔ kɛɛ yɛhɔ́ yɛwʋwa yɩ anlima benyia be mɔɔ ɩwɔ Lombillo suwa nɩ yɩ pɛ. Nɩ eyi kile kɛɛ, ɩsɛ kɛɛ yetiã ye mmusunwamaa nɩɩ ye nlabenyia nɩɩ ye nlabaalɛ mɔɔ yɛdɔ bɛ paa nɩ kɛnɩ. Naasʋ̃, mɩ nɩɩ mɩ maamɩ nɩɩ mɩ mmenlima mmaalɛ yɩ hɔlɩ zʋlɔ zɔ̃mɩnlɩ Gyehova.
<<<P>>>
Wɔ August 26, 1957 nɩ, bonlumanlɩ mɩ wɔ nzulo be mɔɔ ibike Lombillo suwa yɩ nɩ nu. Nɩ minyia alʊbɔ bunlu. Mɩákũ ɛlalɩyɛ kyĩ kɛɛ ilótwu alʊbɔ anwĩ po na nninyeni nu ɩazɩnza wɔ Gyehova Dasɩvʋlɔmaa mɔɔ bɔwɔ Cuba nɩ bɛ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu. Wɔ 1959 nɩ, boduli aɣanɩ yɩ wɔ biyia zʋ na communist aɣanɩ yɩ ɣa lili tumi.
