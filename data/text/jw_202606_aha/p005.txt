zʋlɔ wɔ jw.org zʋ mɔɔ ise, “Asɔ̃ Bɔwɔlɩ a?” nɩ s体 ma yenyia dasɩlɛ mvʋlɔ mɔɔ ikile kɛɛ Gyehova yɛ lɛ Dɩyɛwɔlɛvʋlɔ nɩ. Eliyema kuakula baalɛ be mɔɔ ɩwɔ United States mɔɔ bɔfʋlɔ yɩ Jessica nɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ: “Mmɩlɩ mɔ mɩyɩnganlɩ Origin of Life blohyʋɔ yɩ muwieli nɩ, minyianlɩ dasɩlɛ azʋlɔtʋ mɔɔ idi munli paa mɔɔ ikile kɛɛ abiye mɔɔ izi nyɛlɛbɛ yɛ ɩwɔlɩ nninyeni munwala a. Nɩ mizi eyi daada, naasɔ̃ blohyʋɔ yɩ mɔɔ mɩyɩnganlɩ nɩ manlɩ mɩ adwɩnɩ zili mɩ pi.” Akɔ̃ sɔ̃, mmenii sũmaa nyia Sa Ɔkʋla Enyia Anyɩnlɩlelɛ Daabaa! buuku yi, dɩyɛzumanlɛ 6 nɩɩ 7 nɩɩ buuku mɔɔ ise Is There a Creator Who Cares About You? nɩ zʋ mfasʋ paa. Sɛ ɛyɩnga ɔzɔ awuuku yi be kyĩ po a, sa ɔkʋla esiã kɩnga yɩ dɔkɔ̃.

9 Gyehova ma yenyia dasɩlɛ sũmaa mɔɔ ikile kɛɛ ɩdɩ yɛ Wɔlɛvʋlɔ. Wɔ wʋzawʋ wʋ dɩyɛzumanlɛ nu nɩ, sa ɔkʋla fa siye wʋ anyɩ zʋ kɛɛ ɛkɛbɩbɛ dasɩlɛ azʋlɔtʋ mɔɔ ikile kɛɛ Nyamɩnlɩ wɔ kɛnɩ nɩ nu. Ɔzɔ mɔɔ ɛkɛyɛ nɩ kɔwʋwa wʋ ma ekenyia dedi kezile kɛɛ Gyehova wɔ kɛnɩ na ɩkɔwʋwa wʋ sɔ̃ ma ɔkɔnwʋ yɩ bʋwɔ. (Wul. 1:20) Akɔ̃ sɔ̃, mɔɔ okozuma nɩ kɛma ekenyia debe mɔɔ ɛkɛva kɔwʋwa bɛzabe mɔɔ bɛ adwɩnɩ iázi bɛ pi kɛɛ Nyamɩnlɩ wɔ kɛnɩ nɩ. Amba, Gyehova ɩwɔ nninyeni sũmaa na yi munwala di dasɩlɛ kɛɛ ɩwɔ kɛnɩ paa.

10 Kɩnga 2 Timoti 3:16, 17. Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ dɩ Gyehova yi munwa nu ndwɔkɔ mɔɔ ɩva ma yɛ. Lma yenyia ndwɔkɔyizialɛ mɔɔ nwʋmɔ hyinyia dɩkɛɛ, kɛ ɩyɛlɩ mɔɔ nnwʋanlɩ yalɩ a, mɩnla ti mɔɔ yɛda azɩ a, nɩɩ mɩnla yɛ Gyehova pɛ kɛɛ yɛyɛ a nwʋ munwalyɛ. Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ dɩ buuku mɔɔ nwʋmɔ hyinyia tɛla buuku bela, sianti yɛ lɛ kɛɛ yede yedi kɛɛ Gyehova yɛ ɩdɩ yɩ
<<<P>>>
10. Kɛ yebu Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ yese a? (2 Timoti 3:16, 17)
<<<P>>>
Ɛhɛlɛlɛvʋlɔ a. Iluwa yɩ zɔ ti, yɛpɛ kɛɛ yɛtɩ mɔɔ Bayɩbʋlʋ kilehile nɩ tʋ bʋwɔ na yɛfa yɛyɛ egyima.
<<<P>>>
11 Yɛwɔ sianti baaba ti mɔɔ yede yedi kɛɛ Gyehova yɛ lɛ Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ Ɛhɛlɛlɛvʋlɔ nɩ a. Mɩnla ti a? Sianti yɛ lɛ kɛɛ ndwɔkɔ mɔɔ ɩkã ɩfalɩ abakɔsɛmɩ nwʋ nɩ dɩ nɔhvanlɛ. (Lk. 3: 1, 2) Akɔ̃ sɔ̃, sɛ ɩkãã abɔdɩ nwʋ odwokɔ a, ɩkã yɩ pɛpɛɛpɛ. (Dwb. 26:7) lávalɩ nwʋmɔ mmɔdɩnɩ bela mɔɔ Setanɩ wɔ kɛɛ ɩkɛzɩkɛ Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ nɩ, mɛyɩ yɛ ɩdɩ buuku mɔɔ bɛhyɩhyɛ yɩ sũmaa bɛdɛla buuku bela a. Mmenii kɛyɛ 40 yɛ bɛvalɩ alʋbɔ kɛyɛ 1,600 bɛhɛlɛlɩ Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ a, naasɔ̃ numɔ ndwɔkɔ munwatĩĩ yiyia. lwɔ numɔ kɛɛ bɛhɛlɛlɩ a thyɛ dɩyɛ, naasɔ̃ numɔ fʋlɔdulɛ ɩbʋwaa mmenii abʋmɩ abʋmɩ sũmaa wɔ nnɛ. (Ɛyɛ. 13:20; 15:21; Mt. 7:12) Na numɔ nkɔmɩhyɛ sũmaa mɔɔ ɩya numɔ nɩ ma yɛkʋla yefa yɛ nwʋ yetiã zʋ. (Dwh. 23:14) Eyimmɔ munwatĩĩ ma yɛnwʋ kɛɛ Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ zʋ̃ Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ ɛkɛnɩ amba!

12 Sɛ yɛ adwɩnɩ iázi yɛ pi kɛɛ Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ dɩ Nyamɩnlɩ yɩ odwokɔ a, mɩnla yɛ ɩsɛ kɛɛ yɛyɛ a? Lsɛ kɛɛ yɛbɔ nwʋmɔ mpayɩ na yɛyɛ nwʋmɔ egyima. Abʋlantɩyɛ be mɔɔ bɔfʋlɔ yɩ Jordan, mɔɔ bɛdɩdʋlɩ yɩ kɛɛ Dasɩvʋlɔ nɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ: “Kɛ dɩyɛ bela mɔɔ mɩ awuvʋlɔ anzɛnyɩi mmenii mvʋlɔ bɛkã bekile mɩ nɩ yɛ mide midi wɔ ɛkɛnɩ la. Mfomi, gyɩsɛ mɩnwʋ kɛɛ ɩdɩ nɔhvanlɛ. Itwu mmɩlɩ be a, mɩ adwɩnɩ yɛ mɩ anwĩ anwĩ. Na sɛ ɩba yɩ zɔ a, mɩyɛ ɛbɩbɛlɛnu wɔ yɛ awuuku nu mɩfa mɩpɩbɛ mu ndwokɔyizialɛ nwʋ munwalyɛ.” Sɛ yɛbɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ yɛkɛhyɛ dedi mɔɔ yɛlɛ yɩ kɛɛ Gyehova yɛ lɛ Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ Ɛhɛlɛlɛvʋlɔ nɩ nu ezizɩbɛ a, ye dedi nu kɛyɛ sɩ.—1 Kɔl. 3:12, 13.
