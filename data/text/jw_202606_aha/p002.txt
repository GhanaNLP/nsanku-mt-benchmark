BƆTAYƖ
Sa yɛkʋla yedi nɔhʋanlɛ yɛma Gyehova iávalɩ nwʋmɔ ye dedi nwʋ odwohilalɛ bela mɔɔ yekeyiyia nɩ.
<<<P>>>
Kezimɔ Yɛkɔhɔ Zʋ Keli Nɔhʋanlɛ Mmɩlɩ Mɔ Yeyiyiaa Ye Dedi Nwʋ Odwohilalɛ Nɩ
<<<P>>>
‘Niibenyia, aninyia yɩ ɛkɛnɩ yɛ yɛkɔɔ a? Mɔwʋ yɛ ɛlɛ odwokɔ mɔɔ ɩma nnwʋanlɩ mɔɔ íni awielɩyɛ nɩ a.’—DWƆ. 6:68.
<<<P>>>
EYƖLE ɔkʋ̃ be, nɩ Gyisɛsɩ ikilehilea wɔ Kapɛnyɔmɩ. Na wɔ yi ehilehilelɛ yɩ nu nɩ, ɩhanlɩ kɛɛ: “Sɛ mʋnlʋmmɔ bɛádi mɩzamɩ, Enii Ɣalɛ nɩ mʋ nnwʋnlamɩ, bɛánʋ mi mmegyia a, belényia nnwʋanlɩ mɔɔ íni awielɩyɛ.” Na akʋ̃ sɔ̃ ɩhanlɩ kɛɛ: “Mɔɔ ikeli mʋ nnwʋnlamɩ nɩ be na ɩkɔnlʋ mi mmegyia nɩ be nɩ ɩlɛ nnwʋanlɩ mɔɔ íni awielɩyɛ; na mɩkodunwo yɩ mɩkɛya nnwʋanlɩ nu wɔ mmɩlɩ mɔ ili awielɩyɛ nɩ nu.” (Dwɔ. 6:53, 54) Na odwokɔ yɩ nɩ yelɩ yɩ adʋwavʋlɔ yɩ bo nu sɔ̃maa nnwʋánlɩ ma bɛhanlɩ kɛɛ: “Ehilehilelɛ eyi ɩyɛ sɩ, aninyia yɛ ɩkɔhʋla eyi zʋ di a?”—Dwɔ. 6:60.

2 Bɛzabɛ mɔɔ Gyisɛsɩ yɩ odwokɔ eyi lileli bɛ nɩ yiakili yɩ mɛdalilɛ. Asɔ̃ nɩ eyi kile kɛɛ béni Gyisɛsɩ nu dedi kʋlaa a? Anhã, kɛ ɔzɔ kʋlaa. Mɔɔ ɩdɩ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ mmɩlɩ mɔ mmenii nɩ bɔnwʋnlɩ nnwʋánlɩ nninyeni nyezile mɔɔ Gyisɛsɩ yelɩ nɩ, bɛhanlɩ kɛɛ: “Nɔhʋanlɛ eyi yɛ lɛ nkɔmɩhyenii nɩ mɔɔ Ɛhɛlɛlɛ nɩ ise ɩbaa oyuwadɩ nɩ.” (Dwɔ. 6:14) Meti eyi di dasɩlɛ kɛɛ nɩ Gyisɛsɩ yɩ adʋwavʋlɔ yɩ wɔ yɩ nu dedi. Naasɔ̃ mmɩlɩ mɔ Gyisɛsɩ hanlɩ debe mɔɔ bɛádɩ yɩ tʋ nɩ, beyiakili yɩ mɛdalilɛ.

Mɔwʋ yɛ ɛlɛ odwokɔ mɔɔ ɩma nnwʋanlɩ mɔɔ íni awielɩyɛ nɩ a. Sikalɛpɛyɩ sɔ̃ yede yedi; na yezi kɛɛ mɔwʋ yɛ ɛdɩ Nwanzanwanza ɔkʋ̃ nɩ mɔɔ ɔzɔ Nyamɩnlɩ ɛkɛnɩ a.’ (Dwɔ. 6:68, 69) Yɛ yɩ ndwɩ kɛɛ
