Iluwakɛɛ mmenii egyikĩĩ be la yɛ bɔwɔ dasɩlɛlilɛ okuwu yi nu nɩ ti, ɩbʊwa ma yɛnwʊ mmeliyema mvʊlɔ bɔwɔ na yɛbɔ numɔ yɛyɛ dasɩlɛlilɛ egyima yɩ (Kila nkyɩkyɛmi 10)
<<<P>>>
mɔɔ yɩ nwʊ wʊkwa mɔɔ ɩwɔ wʊ okuwu yi nu nɩ bʊwa wʊ. (Kĩnga Ɛyɛlɛwulɛ 1:5; 27:17.) Bʊtayɩ mɔɔ ɛpɛ kɛɛ otwʊ nwʊmɔ nɩ, kã nwʊmɔ odwɔkɔ kile wʊ okuwu zʊ hilalɛvʊlɔ yɩ. Ebiye a, sa ɩkʊla ɩbʊwa wʊ ma otwʊ nwʊmɔ. Na sa ɔkʊla sɔ̃ bizia mmenii mvʊlɔ bɔ mmʊwalɩyɛ. Eliyema baalɛ be mɔɔ bɔfʊlɔ yɩ Heather mɔɔ yɩ anyɩ tʊ dɩyɛ nɩ yɛlɩ zɔ. Ihanlɩ kɛɛ: “Miyizialɩ ɔhvanɩbukolɛvʊlɔ be yɩ mmʊwalɩyɛ wɔ kezimɔ bɛyɛ siakehila nwʊ. Nɩ eliyema baalɛ yɩ mɔɔ ɩdɩ ɔhvanɩbukolɛvʊlɔ yɩ wɔ ɔlɔlɛ na yi kunlu dɩyɛnɛ. L nɩɩ mɩ yɛlɩ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ mɔɔ nɩ ɩma yɛkɔ yɛkɔ dasɩlɛlilɛ. Iwʊwalɩ mɩ po ma mɩhʊlalɩ mɩvalɩ mɩ siakehila ɔkɔ̃ mʊwɔlɩ Bayɩbʊlʊ dɩyɛzumanlɛ tʊ. Nɔhvanlɛ odwɔkɔ lɛ kɛɛ, mɩ anyɩ le paa kɛɛ miyizialɩ mmʊwalɩyɛ iluwakɛɛ sikalɛyɩ, mɩ anyɩ le wɔ dasɩlɛlilɛ nu dɔkɔ̃.” Mɔɔ ɩdɩ yɛ lɛ kɛɛ, ye nu ɔkɔ̃ bela kɔhʊla koluwa ahvanɩ sʊ̃maa zɛ zʊ kɔhʊla kɔwʊwa yi okuwu yi.

11 Ɩbʊwa yɛ ma yɛnyi wɔ sunzuma ɔfʊã nu. Yɛ dasɩlɛlilɛ okuwu nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ yɩ kɔhʊla kɔwʊwa ye nu ɔkɔ̃ bela ma ikenyi wɔ sunzuma ɔfʊã nu. Kɛ ndianwʊ nɩ, mmenii be mɔɔ bɛla Gyehova yɩ ahʊnlɩnɩ zʊ dɩkɛɛ ahʊnavʊlɔ, ayinsamaa nu bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛ alʊbɔ hɔ anyunumi nɩ, sɛ beyiyiaa ndwɔkɔzɩzɩbɛ be a, okuwu zʊ hilalɛvʊlɔ yɩ kɔhʊla kɛhyɛ bɛ ɛzɩzɩbɛ. (1 Tɛs. 2:8; Gye. 1:27) Akʊ̃ sɔ̃, sɛ yɛ nɩɩ ye okuwu nu maa kɔ dasɩlɛli-
<<<P>>>
lɛ a, sa bɔbʊwa yɛ ma yetu mpʊnɩ wɔ ye ehilehilelɛ nu. Dasɩlɛlilɛ okuwu yi bʊwa ma bɛfa ɛdɩdɩlɛ bɛma mmeliyema mmenyia mɔɔ bonluma nɩ ma bɛfata bɛma asɛdɩ azʊlɔtʊ wɔ asafo yɩ nu.—1 Tim. 3:10.

12 Bɛmá yehila kezimɔ Wendy, mɔɔ yeli bɔmʊnlɩ yɛhã yɩ nwʊ odwɔkɔ nɩ nyianlɩ dasɩlɛlilɛ okuwu nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ yɩ zʊ mfasʊ. Ihanlɩ kɛɛ, “Iluwakɛɛ nɩ mɩ papa twodwu tiyia nɔhvanlɛ yɩ ti, nɩ ɩáma mɩ maamɩ ɔhvanɩ ma ɩta ɩkɔ dasɩlɛlilɛ ayiyialɩyɛ wɔ dapɛnɩ yɩ awielɩyɛ. Meti nɩ ɩáda ɩ nɩɩ yɩ dasɩlɛlilɛ okuwu yi bɔ numɔ kɔ dasɩlɛlilɛ. Naasɔ̃, mamɩ mɛyɩ nɩ ɩma ɔhvanɩ mɩ ma mʊkɔ. Eliyema baalɛ ebani be mɔɔ ɩdɩ ɔhvanɩbukolɛvʊlɔ valɩ mʊ ɔhɔnwʊ na ɩwʊwalɩ mɩ ma muduli mpʊnɩ wɔ mɩ ehilehilelɛ nu na muzumanlɩ kezimɔ mɩkɛyɛ sʊ̃maa mɩkɛma mɩ asafo yɩ. Mɩlanwʊ la mɩkakɛ Ɛhɛlɛlɛndwɔkɔ azʊlɔtʊ be mɔɔ mu okuwu zʊ hilalɛvʊlɔ va manlɩ mɩ mɔɔ ɩwʊwa mɩ babʊmɩ nnɛ nɩ.”

13 Ɩbʊwa ma yenyia ɔhɔnwʊmmɔ mɔɔ bɔdɔ yɛ na bɔbʊwa yɛ. Sɛ yɛ nɩɩ yɛ okuwu nu maa bɔ numɔ yɛ dasɩlɛlilɛ egyima yɩ a, ɩma yɛ nɩɩ bɛ nyia ɔhɔnvwvalɛ mɔɔ numɔ yɛ sɩ. (Fɩl. 1:27) Akʊ̃ sɔ̃, iluwakɛɛ mmenii mɔɔ bɔwɔ dasɩlɛlilɛ okuwu yi nu nɩ bɔ nwʊ ɩázʊ̃nɩ nɩ ti, ɩkʊla ɩbʊwa mmeliyema mɔɔ bɛ anyɩ tʊ dɩyɛ anzɛnyɩ ɩyɛ sɩ ɩma bɛ kɛɛ bɔkɔhɔ mmenii nu nɩ ma benyia ɔhɔnwʊmmɔ.
