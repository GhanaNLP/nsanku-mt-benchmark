okuwu zʊ hilalɛvʊlɔ. (2 Tim. 2:2) Sɛ ehyinyia mmʊwalɩyɛ na ama wʊahʊla wʊadɩdɩ ɛlawulowɔlɛmaa mɔɔ bɔwɔ wʊ okuwu yi nu nɩ a, sa ɔkʊla bizia mmeliyema mɔɔ bɔ nwʊ wʊkwa ma bɔbʊwa wʊ. Kɛ ndianwʊ nɩ, ɔhvanɩbʊkolɛmaa nɩ ɛlawulowɔlɛmaa mɔɔ bɛlɛ ozumanlɛnwʊnlɛ sɔmaa nɩ kɔhʊla kɔwʊwa ɛlawulowɔlɛmaa mɔɔ bɛɣa fʊlɔ nɩ bɛzabɛ mɔɔ bɛni ozumanlɛnwʊnlɛ sɔmaa nɩ. Sɛ ɛlawulowɔlɛmaa be wɔ kɛnɩ mɔɔ bɛzi kezimɔ bɔbɔ nkɔmmɔwɔlɛ tʊ, bɛɣɛ siakehila nɩ Bayɩbʊlʊ dɩyɛzumanlɛ a, sa ɛlawulowɔlɛmaa mɔɔ bɔ nwʊ wʊkwa nɩ kʊla bʊwa bɛ. Sikalɛyɩ, bɛmá yehila ahvanɩ asã mɔɔ ye nu ɔkɔ̃ bela kɔhʊla koluwa zʊ kɔwʊwa yɩ dasɩlɛlilɛ okuwu yi.

BƲWA WƲ DASILƐLILƐ OKUWU YI

8 Ɛ nɩɩ wʊ okuwu yi wó numɔ hɔ dasɩlɛlilɛ. Sɛ ɛ nɩɩ mmenii mvʊlɔ kɔ dasɩlɛlilɛ a, ɩbʊwa ma ‘bɛhyɛ bɔ nwʊ ɛzɩzɩbɛ.’ (Kɩnga Wulomimaa 1:12, NWT.) Sɛ yɛ nɩɩ mmenii mvʊlɔ bɔ numɔ kɔ dasɩlɛlilɛ a, ɩbʊwa ma ye kunlu iádu sɔmaa mmɩlɩ mɔ yɛ nɩɩ mmenii bɔɔ nkɔmmɔ nɩ. Akɔ̃ sʊ̃, ɩma yɛtɩ nyanlɩ kɛɛ yɛwɔ banɩwɔlɛ mmɩlɩ mɔ yedii dasɩlɛ wɔ nnɩka be nɩ. Sɛ iluwa wʊ egyima ti, wʊáhʊla ɛ nɩɩ wʊ okuwu nu maa bɔ numɔ kɔ dasɩlɛlilɛ a, mɩnla ti mɔɔ ɛ nɩɩ wʊ egyima diibenyia yɩ iádɩndɛ ma ɩyɛ nzɩnzanlɩyɛ wɔ eyile anzɛnyɩɩ mmɩlɩ mɔ ɛfa ɛyɛ egyima nɩ nu a? Ebiye a, ikele kediã numɔ. (Ne. 2:4-6) Na sɛ iluwa wʊ pɔkɔdɩlɛ mɔɔ táɣɛ baaba anzɛnyɩɩ wʊ alʊbɔ mɔɔ ɩhɔ anyunumɔ nɩ ti, wʊáhʊla ɛ nɩɩ wʊ okuwu nu maa yiyia numɔ kɔ dasɩlɛlilɛ sʊ̃ ɛ, mɩnla ti mɔɔ ɛ nɩɩ wʊ okuwu zʊ hilalɛvʊlɔ yɩ iáhã nwʊmɔ odwokɔ a? Sa ɩkʊla ɩyɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ ma oduwa vidiyo kɔɔlʊ zʊ ɛ nɩɩ bɛ kɔ dasɩlɛlilɛ, ɛkɛlɛ kɛlɛta anzɛnyɩɩ ɛyɛ yɩ wɔ foonu zʊ. Asʊ̃ ɛdɩ abɩlantɩyɛ anzɛnyɩɩ kɛdɛkɛ mɔɔ ɛlɛ pɔkɔdɩlɛ baaba naasʊ̃ ɩyɛ sɩ ɩma wʊ kɛɛ ɛkɛda kɔhɔ dasɩlɛlilɛ a? Mɛyɩ, nɩ sa ɔkʊla ɛ nɩɩ abiye mɔɔ ɩwɔ wʊ okuwu yi nu yɛ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ kɔ dasɩlɛlilɛ dapɛnɩ bela, na mɛyɩ kɔhʊla kɔwʊwa wʊ. Nwʊ nninyeni mɔɔ nwʊmɔ hyinyia wʊ

8. Kɛ ɩkɛyɛ na yɛawʊwa yɛ dasɩlɛlilɛ okuwu yi a? (Wulomimaa 1:12)
<<<P>>>
paa wɔ wʊ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu anzɛnyɩɩ kila kezimɔ ɛfa wʊ mmɩlɩ di egyima nɩ. (Wul. 12:11; Kol. 4:5) Sɛ ɔbɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ɛ nɩɩ wʊ okuwu yi nu maa kɛda kɔwɔ numɔ kɔhɔ dasɩlɛlilɛ a, ɩkɛma abiyela mɔɔ ɩwɔ numɔ nɩ kenyia wʊ zʊ mfasʊ. Meti siã Gyehova kɛɛ ɩwʊwá wʊ. Na ɩkɛma ‘ɛkenyia ɛpɛ nɩɩ anwʊzɩzɩbɛ mɔɔ ɛkɛva kɛyɛ yɩ apɛdɩ.’—Fil. 2:13, NWT.

9 Da ngyevʊlɔyɛlɛ ali. Sɛ yɛda ngyevʊlɔyɛlɛ ali a, ɩkɛma ye okuwu nu maa yɩ kɛdɩ nyanlɩ kɛɛ yɛdɔ bɛ. (Kɩnga Wulomimaa 12:13.) Kɛ ndianwʊ nɩ, sa yɛkʊla yɛma bɛyɛ dasɩlɛlilɛ ayiyialɩyɛ wɔ yɛ awudo. Wɔ asafo be nu nɩ, bɔwɔ nhyɩhyɛlɩyɛ mɔɔ ɛyɛvʊlɔ bela mɔɔ ɩkɛɣa kɛma ndɛnɩ wɔ bɛ asafo yɩ nu nɩ, bɛma dasɩlɛlilɛ okuwu ɔkɔ̃ yɛ alɩyɛ ma yɩ, na okuwu nu maa nɩ yɩ bɔ numɔ di alɩyɛ. Wɔ ɔzɔ dapɛnɩ yɩ nu nɩ, sɛ ndɛnɩmanlɛvʊlɔ yɩ tába po a, okuwu yi yiyia numɔ di alɩyɛ na bɛhyɛ bɔ nwʊ bɔ nwʊ ɛzɩzɩbɛ. Eliyema baalɛ be mɔɔ bɔfʊlɔ yɩ Sarah mɔɔ ɩdɩ ɔhvanɩbʊkolɛvʊlɔ titili nɩ hanlɩ kɛɛ: “Sɛ ɩsɛ kɛɛ mɩda ngyevʊlɔyɛlɛ ali a, ɩma adwɩnɩndwɩnɩ tɔ mɩ zʊ. Iluwa yɩ zɔ ti, mʊbɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ mɩlɛ́yɛ nniyenii kunkuntanyɩɩ. Na mɩfa mɩ adwɩnɩ misi ye mɩ mmeliyema mɔɔ mɩbanwʊ bɛ nɩ zʊ tɛla kɛɛ mʊkɔdwɩnɩndwɩnɩ alɩyɛ nyezile mɔɔ ɩsɛ kɛɛ mɩyɛ nɩ nwʊ.” Sɛ yɛda ngyevʊlɔyɛlɛ ali a, ɩma yɛtwɩ yebike Gyehova nɩɩ yɛ nwʊ yɛ nwʊ na eyi ma yɛnwʊ ye okuwu yi kɛɛ ye abusʊnwa.—Ne. 8:10; Egy. 20:35.

10 Bɔ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ ɛkɛyɛ ɛlawulowɔlɛvʊlɔ nu kilehilevʊlɔ baaba. Sɛ yɛbɔ mmɔdɩnɩ yɛma yɛ nwʊ kʊkwa wɔ ɛlawulowɔlɛ nɩɩ ehilehilelɛ egyima yɩ nu a, ɩkɛma yɛ anyɩ kele mmɩlɩ mɔ yɛ nɩɩ yɛ okuwu nu maa yɛɣɛɛ dasɩlɛlilɛ egyima yɩ nɩ.
