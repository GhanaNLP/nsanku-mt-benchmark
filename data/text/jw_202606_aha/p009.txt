KILISINYIANƖ SUBANƖ KƐMA YƐ
ƆHƆNWƲVALƐ YƖ KƆHƆ ZƲLƆ KƆWƲWA KƐNƖ
<<<P>>>
4 Sɛ yɛfa yɛ adwɩnɩ yesiye yɛ mmeliyema bo
subanɩba zʊ a, ɩkɛma ɔhɔnwʊvalɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ yɛ
avinli nɩ nu kɛyɛ sɩ. Na sɛ yɛ nɩɩ yɛ mmeliyema ta
bɔ a, ɩkɛma yɛkɔnwʊ bo subanɩba nɩ. Nɩ sɔ̃ma-
vʊlɔ Pɔɔlʊ pɛ paa kɛɛ ɩ nɩɩ yɩ mmeliyema keyiyia
numɔ. Yɛnwʊ eyi nwʊ dasɩlɛ wɔ yɩ kɛlɛta mɔɔ
ɩhɛlɛlɩ thɔ ɩmanlɩ Tɛsalonikamaa nɩ nu. Lhanlɩ
kɛɛ: “Yewɔlɩ mmɔdɩnɩ kɛɛ yɛkɔnwʊ mʊnlʊmmɔ
bɛ anyunu ebiyekɔ̃.” (1 Tɛs. 2:17) Mmenii sʊ̃maa
nwʊ yɩ kɛɛ, sɛ bɛpɛ kɛɛ bɛ nɩɩ abiye bɔ nkɔmmɔ
na bɛ nɩɩ enii ɔkʊ̃ nɩ yiyia a, ɩhyɛ bɔ ɔhɔnwʊvalɛ
yɩ nu ɛzɩzɩbɛ paa. Meti sɛ yɛkɔ dasɩlelilɛ, dɩyɛzu-
manlɛ, yɛɛ mansini nu manlɩ-avinli ayiyialɩyɛ a,
ɩma yenyia nwʊmɔ ɔhʊanɩ yɛ nɩɩ yɛ mmeliyema
bɔ. Naasʊ̃ sɛ yɛ nɩɩ abiye wɔ odwɔkɔ na yɛ nɩɩ yɩ
bɔ a, mɛyɩ yɩzayɩ nkʊ̃ lɔ́kʊla keziyeziye odwɔ-
kɔ yɩ.
<<<P>>>
5 Sɛ ɔhɔnwʊvalɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ yɛ nɩɩ yɛ anlima be
avinli nɩ ɩsɩkɛɛ a, Kilisinyianɩ subanɩ kɔwʊwa
ma yɛkɛhyɛ numɔ ɛzɩzɩbɛ. (Kɩnga Kolosiyia-
maa 3:12.) Sɛ yɛda subanɩ be dɩkɛɛ kãnwʊazɩ,
abʊtalɩ, kunlumiyɩbalɛ, nɩɩ kunlumiba ali a, ɩkɔ-
wʊwa yɛ ma yekeli ndwokɔzɩzɩbɛ mɔɔ ɩkɔhʊla
ɩkɛzɩkɛ ɔhɔnwʊvalɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ yɛ nɩɩ yɛ mmeliye-
ma avinli nɩ zʊ. Kɛ ndianwʊ nɩ, kɛmɔ ngɔlɔ mɔɔ
bɛfa bokua kaalɩ yɩ engine nu ma kaalɩ yɛ egyima
bʊwɔ nɩ, ɔzɔ la yɛ Kilisinyianɩ subanɩ azʊlɔtʊ
buwa yɛ ma yedi ndwokɔzɩzɩbɛ bela mɔɔ ɩkɔhʊla
ɩkɛzɩkɛ yɛ avinli nɩ zʊ a. Sikalɛyɩ, bɛmá yehila
bʊwabɩlɛ azʊlɔtʊ be mɔɔ ɩkɔhʊla ɩkɛzɩkɛ ɔhɔ-
nwʊvalɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ yɛ nɩɩ yɛ mmeliyema avinli nɩ.
Na wɔ numɔ bela nu nɩ, yekehila kezimɔ Gyisɛsɩ
yɩ subanɩ be dɩkɛɛ kãnwʊazɩ, abʊtalɩ, kunlumi-
ba, nɩɩ kunlumiyɩbalɛ mɔɔ yekozumazuma nɩ
kɔwʊwa yɛ ma yɛkɛyɛ ɔhɔnwʊmmɔba yɛkɛma yɛ
mmeliyema.

6 Mɩnla ti mɔɔ ɩkɔhʊla ɩkɛyɛ ɔhawʊ a? Sɛ akasa-
kasa be ba mmeliyema be bɛ avinli a, ebiye a,
bo nu bela kɛdɩ nyanlɩ kɛɛ mɛyɩ yɛ yi odwo-
kɔ yɛ fɛ. Kɛ ndianwʊ nɩ, fa yɩ kɛɛ mmeliyema
anwĩ be bɛ adwɩnɩ iáyiyia wɔ kezimɔ bɛ dasɩleli-
lɛ okuwu yi kɛyɛ egyima wɔ bɛ azʊlɛ-sini yi nu nɩ
nwʊ. Na ebiye a, apigyabigyabɔnwʊ subanɩ kɔ-
hʊla kɛma bo nu bela kɛdɩ nyanlɩ kɛɛ mɛyɩ yɩ
adwɩnthilelɛ yɛ ɩdɩ baaba a. Na bʊwabɩlɛ eyi kɔ-
hʊla kɛva akasakasa kɛya bɛ avinli na ɩazɩkɛ ɔhɔ-
nwʊvalɛ mɔɔ ɩwɔ bɛ avinli nɩ. Sɛ bɔkɔ zʊlɔ bɔ-
bʊwa bʊwa ɔzɔ odwɔkɔ yɩ nwʊ na bɛásiyeziye a,
ɩkɔhʊla ɩkɛma bo nu bela kɛva yɩ ɔhɔnwʊ nwʊ
ɛya ikowula yi nu. Na mmɩlɩ kɔlɩ zʊlɔ nɩ, odwo-
kɔ ɛgyɩkĩĩ eyi kɔhʊla kele salɛ nu na ɔhɔnwʊvalɛ
mɔɔ ɩwɔ bɛ avinli nɩ kɛzɩkɛ kʊlaa.

7 Sumazuma Gyisɛsɩ yɩ kãnwʊazɩ yɩ. Gyisɛsɩ
manlɩ yɩ adʊwavʊlɔ yɩ nwʊnlɩ kɛɛ, sɛ akasa-
kasa be ba bɛ avinli a, kãnwʊazɩ yɛ ɩkɔwʊwa
bɛ ma bekeziyeziye a. Nɩ adʊwavʊlɔ yɩ beta
bosũ enii mɔɔ ɩdɩ kezile wɔ bo nu nwʊ pɩla,
na nɩ subanɩ eyi ɩyɛɛ ikele ndini wɔ bo nu. Meti,
mɔɔ ɩkɛyɛ na Gyisɛsɩ ɩáwʊwa bɛ ma bɛazɩnza bo
subanɩ nɩ, ɩmanlɩ bɔnwʊnlɩ kɛɛ ɩsɛ kɛɛ bɛkã
bɔ nwʊ azɩ na bobu mmenii mvʊlɔ kɛɛ bɛtɛla
bɛ. (Mt. 20:25-28) Nunuma mɔɔ ansaana Gyi-
sɛsɩ ibawu po nɩ, ɩyɛlɩ kãnwʊazɩ nwʊ ndianwʊ
