12 Sɛ esiyeziyee wʊ ndɛnɩ a, bʋbɔ zʋlɔ mbɔmɩ sʊ̃maa ma ivindenli wu munwa na ama wʋahvla wʋadɩndɛ kɛmɔ ɛtɩndɛ eyile bela nɩ. Sɛ ɛmaa ndɛnɩ a, kɛ ankɩ ɛkɛhɛlɛ ndwokɔ yɩ kogua kɛlɛta zʋ na wʋayɩnga wʊahile ediyelɛmaa yɩ nɩ, fa wʊzawʊ wʊ ndwokɔ tɩndɛ na ama wu ediyelɛmaa nɩ bɛayɛ azʋnlɩ bɛama wʊ na bɛadɩ mɔɔ ɛkãã nɩ tʋ. Má Ɛyɩngalɛ Nu Ehilehilelɛ Nwʋ Hyinyia Wʊ blohyʊɔ yɩ kã kɛɛ: “Tɩndɛ kɛmɔ ɛtɩndɛ eyile bela nɩ la, eyi ɩkɛma ɛkɛla kezimɔ ɛtɩ nyanlɩ wɔ wʊ odwɔkɔtile nɩɩ wʊ ediyelɛmaa nɩ bɔ nwʊ nɩ ali.” Sɛ ɛmaa ndɛnɩ na ɔzʊ̃ wʊ ahʋnlɩnɩ nu tɩndɛ a, ɩkɛma wʊ ediyelɛmaa bɛkɛyɛ azʋnlɩ bɛkediye wʊ, bɛ anyɩ keleli mɔɔ ekilehileee nɩ nwʊ na bɛava bɛayɛ egyima wɔ bɛ ɛbɩlawɔlɛ nu.

13 Ma ndɛnɩmanlɛvʋlɔ be mɔɔ yɩ nwʊ wʊkwa ɩwʊwá wʊ. Kɛ ndianwʊ nɩ, ansaana ɔkɔhɔ kɛma ndɛnɩ be nɩ, sa ɔkʊla fa kɛlɛta mɔɔ ekehila zʋlɔ kɛma wʊ ndɛnɩ yɩ nu ɛbɩbɛlɛnu bela mɔɔ ɛkɛyɛ nɩ ma yɩ. Akɔ̃ sʊ̃, sɛ ɩfa adwɩnɩhilelɛ be ɩma wʊ a, sa ɔkʊla fa kɩnla wʊ ndɛnɩ yɩ nwʊ. Sa ɔkʊla sʊ̃ kã kile yɩ kɛɛ ɛpɛ kɛɛ ɛma ndɛnɩ yɩ kile yi na sɛ debe wɔ numɔ mɔɔ táhɔ bʊwɔ a, ɩawʊwa wʊ wɔ nwʊmɔ na ama wʊ ehilehilelɛ iadu mpʋnɩ. (Ɛyɛ. 1:5) Enii mɔɔ ipukoo ɔhvanɩ nɩ, ízi kɛɛ yɩ mɛda ɩhyɩya, meti gyɩsɛ abiye na ɩahʊla ɩahã kɛɛ mɔɔ yɛkãã yɩ tɔ azʋnlɩ nu anzɛnyɩɩ ɩádɔ azʋnlɩ nu. Yɛpɛ kɛɛ yetu mpʋnɩ wɔ ye ehilehilelɛ nu na ama yɛawʊwa ye ediyelɛmaa ma bɛanyia nninyeni ngɩnlɩma mɔɔ ɩwɔ Bayɩbʋlʋ yɩ nu nɩ zʋ mfasʊ.*
