Yehowa kɛ e juɛmi mɛ yayamihi nɛ wa pee kɛ be ɔ nɔ
(Hyɛ kuku 11)
<<<P>>>
10 Kane Yesaya 38:17 kɛ sisi ningma a. A ma nyɛ maa tsɔɔ munyuza nɛ ji, “O sake ye he yayami ɔ tsuo ngɔ pue o se” ɔ sisi hu ke, “O je ye he yayami ɔmɛ tsuo kɛ je o hɛ mi.” Ngmami ɔ tsɔɔ kaa ke yayami peelɔ ko tsake e tsui ɔ, Yehowa sakeɔ nɔ ɔ yayami ɔ kɛ fɔɔ e se, he nɛ e be nae ngɛ aloo e be he susue po. Womi ko hu tsɔɔ kaa a ma nyɛ maa tsɔɔ munyuza nɛ ɔ hu sisi ke: “O ha nɛ e pee kaa nó nɛ [i pee we yayami] ko hyɛ.” Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa ke Yehowa ngɔ wa tɔmihi kɛ ke wɔ ɔ, e ngɛ kaa nó nɛ wa pee we yayami ko gblegbleegble. Ke e ba jã a, Yehowa be kaie hu, nɛ e be wa tue gblae ngɛ jamɛ a tɔmi ɔmɛ a he hu. E wo si ke: “I be o tɔmi ɔmɛ kaie hu.” (Yes. 43:25) Enɛ ɔ woɔ wa bua wawɛɛ nitsɛ! Ke Yehowa nɛ ji Jeɔ Tsuo Kojolɔ ɔ po kaii we wa yayami ɔmɛ hu ɔ, lɛɛ e sɛ nɛ wɔ hu wa kai konɛ wa he nile nɛ ko hi wɔ fɔ bue.

Ko ha nɛ yayami ko nɛ o pee kɛ be ɔ nɛ
puɛ bua jɔmi nɛ o ma ná amlɔ nɛ ɔ
<<<P>>>
11 Be fɛɛ be nɛ wa he nile maa bu wɔ fɔ ngɛ yayami ko nɛ wa pee kɛ be ɔ he ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ wa kai si nɛ Yehowa wo wɔ ɔ. Ke Yehowa ngɔ wa he yayamihi kɛ ke wɔ ɔ, e kɛ ke wɔ kulaa. E na we nihi nɛ a pee yayami kɛ be ɔ a he juɛmi yaya, nɛ e be kaa wɔ adesahi nɛ wa yaa nɔ nɛ wa kaiɔ wa sibi a tɔmihi ɔ hulɔ. (Yes. 57:16) Pi yayami ko nɛ wa pee kɛ be ɔ ji nó nɛ Yehowa kɛ e juɛmi maa nɔ, mohu ɔ, nó nɛ wa ngɛ pee amlɔ nɛ ɔ, kɛ nó nɛ wa maa pee hwɔɔ se ɔ ji nó nɛ he hia lɛ wawɛɛ. (Yes. 55:7) Ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ, Yehowa suɔ wɔ wawɛɛ, nɛ e pee klaalo kaa e maa ngɔ wa tɔmihi kɛ ke wɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ waa ngɔ wa juɛmi kɛ ma suɔmi nɛ Yehowa ngɛ kɛ ha wɔ ɔ nɔ, nɛ́ wa
