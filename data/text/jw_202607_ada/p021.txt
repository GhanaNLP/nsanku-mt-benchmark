MOO YE BUA O BAIBLO KASELƆ Ɔ
KONƐ E BA LE YEHOWA SAMINYA
<<<P>>>
4 Yesu de ke waa suɔ Yehowa kɛ wa tsui tsuo kɛ wa klaa tsuo kɛ wa juɛmi tsuo kɛ wa he wami tsuo. (Kane Marko 12:30.) Loko Baiblo kase-lɔ ko ma ná suɔmi kɛ ha Yehowa a, ja e le lɛ saminya. Wa suɔ nɛ Baiblo kaselɔ ko nɛ jɔɔ e wami nɔ kɛ ha Yehowa, pi kɛ ha wɔ loo Baiblo tsɔɔmi ko aloo kɛ ha asafo ɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, oti nɛ waa kɛ maa wa hɛ mi ke waa kɛ nɔ ko ngɛ Bai-blo ɔ kasee ji kaa wa maa ye bua lɛ nɛ e ba le Yehowa saminya konɛ e ná suɔmi kɛ ha lɛ.
<<<P>>>
5 Tsakpa ngɛ suɔmi kɛ tue bumi a kpɛti. Ke Baiblo kaselɔ ko ba le Yehowa saminya nɛ e ná suɔmi kɛ ha lɛ ɔ, lɔ ɔ maa wo lɛ he wami konɛ e bu lɛ tue. Yesu de ke: “Bɔ nɛ pee nɛ je ɔ nɛ e le kaa i suɔ Tsɛ ɔ, i peeɔ kaa bɔ nɛ Tsɛ ɔ fã mi ke ma pee ɔ.” (Yoh. 14:31) Jã a nɔuu kɛ̃ nɛ ke Bai-blo kaselɔ ko hu ba ná suɔmi kɛ ha Yehowa a, lɔ ɔ maa wo lɛ he wami konɛ e bu lɛ tue nɛ e ye lɛ anɔkuale.—1 Yoh. 5:3.
<<<P>>>
6 Ke Baiblo kaselɔ ko ba le Yehowa sami-nya a, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ e mwɔ yi mi kpɔhi nɛ da. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, ke nɔ ko maa le lɔle hɛɛ mi ɔ, pi nɛ e maa hi hyɛɛ nɛ ni kpahi ngɛ hɛɛ aloo nɛ́ e maa kane womi nɛ kɔɔ bɔ nɛ a hɛɛ lɔle ha a he, kɛkɛ lɛ hu e le hɛɛ mi. Mohu ɔ, e sa nɛ lɛ nitsɛ e hɛɛ lɔle ɔ si abɔ, kɛkɛ bɔɔbɔɔbɔɔ ɔ, e he ma ba be saminya nɛ e maa le bɔ nɛ e ma hɛɛ lɔle ɔ ha ngɛ si fɔfɔɛ slɔɔtohi a mi. Jã nɔuu kɛ̃ nɛ loko Baiblo kaselɔ ko maa le Yehowa a, pi Baiblo ɔ pɛ nɛ e maa hi kasee, mohu ɔ, e sa nɛ e da Baiblo sisi tomi mlaahi a nɔ kɛ mwɔ yi mi kpɔhi, nɛ ke e na bɔ nɛ jã nɛ e pee ɔ ha nɛ e si himi pee kpakpa ha a, lɔ ɔ lɛ ma ha nɛ e ma ba le Yehowa saminya a nɛ. Ke ji kaa e ba le Yeho-wa he ní fuu nɛ e na blɔ slɔɔtohi a nɔ nɛ Yeho-wa gu kɛ ye bua lɛ ɔ, lɔ ɔ maa ye bua lɛ konɛ e mwɔ yi mi kpɔhi nɛ da ngɛ si fɔfɔɛ slɔɔtohi a mi.
<<<P>>>
7 Yosef le Yehowa saminya, nɛ enɛ ɔ ye kɛ bua lɛ be nɛ Potifar yo ɔ ngɛ hlae nɛ e kɛ lɛ ma ná bɔmi ɔ. E ngɛ heii kaa benɛ Yosef ji jokuɛyo ɔ, e kase Adam kɛ Hawa, kɛ jehanɛ se hu Abra-ham kɛ Sara a he ní. (1 Mose 2:24; 20:3, 6) Ni-mli nɛ ɔmɛ a he ní nɛ e kase ɔ ye kɛ bua lɛ nɛ e na Yehowa susumi ngɛ gba si himi kɛ bɔmi nami he. Akɛnɛ Yosef le Yehowa saminya he je ɔ, be nɛ Potifar yo ɔ ngɛ hlae nɛ e kɛ lɛ nɛ ná bɔmi ɔ, e kua oya nɔuu nɛ e de lɛ ke: ‘Mɛni he je nɛ ma tɔ̃ tɔmi agbo nɛ ɔ, nɛ ma pee yayami kaa kikɛ nɛ ɔ kɛ si Mawu ɔ?’ (1 Mose 39:7-9) Yosef le nɔ́ nɛ Yehowa suɔ kɛ nɔ́ nɛ e sume. E le nɔ́ nɛ maa dɔ Mawu. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e mwɔ yi mi kpɔ nɛ da. Ke wa ye bua wa Baiblo kaseli ɔmɛ nɛ a ba le Yehowa saminya a, lɔ ɔ maa ye bua mɛ nɛ a maa mwɔ yi mi kpɔhi nɛ maa sa e hɛ mi.

TSƆƆ O BAIBLO KASELƆ Ɔ
BƆ NƐ YEHOWA NGƐ HA
<<<P>>>
8 Mɛni blɔ nɔ o maa gu kɛ tsɔɔ o Baiblo kase-lɔ ko bɔ nɛ Yehowa ngɛ ha? Yehowa bi Hiob sa-nehi maa pee 50 kɛ se, nɛ lɔ ɔ ye bua lɛ nɛ e ba le Yehowa saminya! (Hiob 38:1–41:34) Wɔ hu wa ma nyɛ ma bi sanehi. Sane bimihi nɛ a ngɛ Ná Bua Jɔmi Kɛ Ya Neneene! womi ɔ mi ɔ maa ye bua Baiblo kaselɔ ko konɛ e le bɔ nɛ Ye-howa ngɛ ha tutuutu. (Moo hyɛ daka nɛ ji “Sane Bimihi Nɛ Maa Ye Bua Wɔ Konɛ Waa Tsɔɔ Ní Saminya.”) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, ngɔɔ sane bimi

5. Ke Baiblo kaselɔ ko ba ná suɔmi kɛ ha Yehowa a, mɛni lɔ ɔ maa wo lɛ he wami nɛ e pee?
<<<P>>>
