NÍ KOMƐ NƐ MAA YE BUA MO
<<<P>>>
Mo Dla O He Konɛ O Ha Sane Heto Nɛ Maa Wo Ni Kpahi He Wami
<<<P>>>
Ke wa ya asafo mi kpe ɔ, wa náa he blɔ kɛ woɔ wa nyɛmimɛ he wami kɛ guɔ sane heto nɛ wa haa a nɔ. (Heb. 10:24, 25) Se mɛni ma nyɛ maa ye bua wɔ konɛ wa ha sane heto nɛ maa fia ni kpahi kɛ ma si?
<<<P>>>
• Moo sɔle ha Yehowa. Mo de Yehowa nɛ e ye bua mo konɛ sane heto nɛ o ma ha a nɛ wo e hɛ mi nyami, nɛ e wo ni kpahi he wami. —La 35:18.
<<<P>>>
• Bɔɔ mɔde kaa o be heto ɔ kanee tɛɛ kɛ je womi ɔ mi. Ke o ngɔ mo nitsɛ o munyu kɛ ha sane heto ɔ, ní kpahi maa na bɔ nɛ o suɔ Yehowa ha a, nɛ a ma ná he se.
<<<P>>>
• Mo ha nɛ o heto ɔ nɛ pee kpiti. Behi fuu ɔ, ke o ha sane heto kpiti ɔ, e woɔ ni kpahi he wami wawɛɛ. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, e haa nɛ ni kpahi náa he blɔ nɛ mɛ hu a tuɔ munyu ngɛ asafo mi kpe ɔ sisi.—Abɛ 10:19.
<<<P>>>
• Mo ha nɛ o heto ɔ nɛ wo nihi he wami. Ke o ngɛ sane heto hae ɔ, koo ngɔ o munyu ɔ kɛ kã nyɛmimɛ loo asafo ɔ hɛ mi.
<<<P>>>
Ke o wo o nine nɔ nɛ a tsɛ we mo po ɔ, o ma nyɛ maa wo ni kpahi he wami. O ma nyɛ maa pee jã ke o buɔ ni kpahi tue, nɛ o jeɔ a yi ngɛ hetohi nɛ a ha a he.—Abɛ 10:21.
<<<P>>>
Womi nɛ ɔ, wa jua we. A pee kɛ ha je kɛ wɛ Baiblo tsɔsemi ní tsumi ɔ, nɛ nihi jeɔ a suɔmi mi nɛ a haa nό ko nɛ a kɛ fío se. Ke o ngɛ hlae nɛ o ha yemi kɛ buami ɔ, wa kpa mo pɛɛ nɛ o ya donate.jw.org.
