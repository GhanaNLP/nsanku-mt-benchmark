10 Kane 2 Samuel 21:3-6. David bi Gibeonbi ɔmɛ nɔ́ nɛ a suɔ nɛ e pee ha mɛ konɛ e kɛ kpata mɛ ngɛ yiwutso ní nɛ Saul pee mɛ ɔ he. Jinɛ Gibeonbi ɔmɛ ko nyɛ ngɔ jamɛ a he blɔ ɔ kɛ ko ngɔ sika fuu ngɛ David dɛ, se a pee we jã. Mohu ɔ, a tsɔɔ kaa “tsa pi sika hiɔ, loo sika tsu” nɛ a kɛ ma tsu sane nɛ ɔ he ní, ejakaa ngɛ Mlaa nya a, ke nɔ ko gbe nɔ ko ɔ, e sa nɛ a gbe lɛ kɛ to nane mi. (4 Mose 35:30, 31) Jehanɛ hu ɔ, Gibeonbi ɔmɛ le kaa a be nyɛe maa gbe nɔ ko kaa ja matsɛ ɔ fã mɛ kaa a pee jã. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, David ngmɛ Gibeonbi ɔmɛ blɔ kaa a gbe Saul binyumuhi kpaago, nɛ́ eko ɔ, a piɛɛ Saul he kɛ gbe Gibeonbi babauu ɔ. Pee se ɔ, Yehowa ha nɛ hiɔmi bɔni nɛmi ngɛ Israel ekohu, nɛ hwɔ nɛ ba a se po. Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa Yehowa mi mi fumi ɔ se po ngɛ sane yaya nɛ a ye Gibeonbi ɔmɛ ɔ he.—2 Sam. 21:9, 10, 14.

11 Mɛni nɛ sane nɛ ɔ tsɔɔ wɔ ngɛ Yehowa he? Sane nɛ ɔ ha nɛ wa na heii kaa Yehowa yeɔ dami sane be fɛɛ be. (La 37:28) Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, nihi fuu kɛ nihi nɛ a je ma kpahi a nɔ aloo e slo a he womi nɔ su ɔ hí si saminya. Se Yehowa suɔ nɛ waa kɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ nɛ hi si saminya. Akɛnɛ a yi Yehowa sɔmɔli fuu dami sane, nɛ a waa mɛ yi mi he je ɔ, Yehowa wo si kaa hwɔɔ se ɔ, e maa jɔɔ mɛ nɛ e maa ye dami sane kɛ ha mɛ. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, wa ba kase kaa Yehowa suɔ nɛ waa ye wa si womihi a nɔ, kaa bɔ nɛ e hyɛ blɔ kaa Israelbi ɔmɛ nɛ a ye tue mi jɔmi somi nɛ a kɛ Gibeonbi ɔmɛ so ɔ nɔ ɔ.—Kɛ to Amos 1:9 ɔ he.

12 Mɛni blɔ nɔ wa maa gu kɛ kase Gibeonbi ɔmɛ? Ke wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ ye wɔ sane yaya po ɔ, wa ma nyɛ maa to wa tsui si kɛ hyɛ Yehowa blɔ. Ke wa pee jã a, lɔ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa wa ngɔ wa he kɛ fɔ Yehowa nɔ konɛ e tsu sane ɔ he ní ha wɔ ngɛ be nɛ sa mi. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, mo susu nyɛmiyo ko nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Laura French ɔ he nɛ o hyɛ.
