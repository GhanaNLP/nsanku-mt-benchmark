<<<P>>>
Yaa Nɔ Nɛ O Le Yehowa
Saminya
<<<P>>>
“Moo le o tsɛ ɔ Mawu ɔ, nɛ o sɔmɔ lɛ kɛ o tsui tsuo.”—1 KRON. 28:9.
<<<P>>>
KE WA susu nile nɛ Yehowa ngɛ, bɔ nɛ e juɛmi mi kuɔ ha, kɛ
ní slɔɔtoslɔɔtohi nɛ e bɔ ɔmɛ a he ɔ, e haa nɛ wa nya kpɛɔ wa
he wawɛɛ nitsɛ. Wa nuɔ he kaa bɔ nɛ bɔfo Paulo nu he ɔ.
E ngma ke: “Ní babauu nɛ Mawu ngɛ, kɛ e juɛmi ɔ, kɛ e nile ɔ
mi kuɔ wawɛɛ nitsɛ! A be nyɛe ma hla e kojomi ɔmɛ a mi, nɛ
e blɔ ɔmɛ hu, a be nyɛe maa nu kulaa sisi!”—Rom. 11:33, 34.
<<<P>>>
2 E ngɛ mi kaa wa be nyɛe maa le nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ ngɛ Yehowa he
kɛ pi si mohu lɛɛ, se wa ma nyɛ maa le nɔ tutuutu nɛ e ji.
Mɛni he je nɛ wa de jã a? Matsɛ David de e bi Salomo ke:
“Moo le o tsɛ ɔ Mawu ɔ, nɛ o sɔmɔ lɛ kɛ o tsui tsuo . . . Ke
o hla e se blɔ ɔ, e ma ha nɛ o na lɛ.” (1 Kron. 28:9) Jeha lafa
komɛ a se ɔ, bɔfo Yohane de e nyɛmimɛ Kristofo ɔmɛ nɛ a hi
si ngɛ e be ɔ mi ɔ kaa a ma nyɛ ma “ná anɔkualetsɛ ɔ he nile”
nitsɛnitsɛ. (1 Yoh. 5:20) Yesu nitsɛ hu tsɔɔ kaa e maa ye bua
nihi konɛ a “le Tsɛ ɔ saminya.”—Mat. 11:27.
<<<P>>>
3 Wɔ tsuo wa kase níhi fuu kɛ kɔ Yehowa he, nɛ wa jɔɔ wa
he nɔ kɛ ha lɛ, nɛ wa ba ngɛ lɛ sɔmɔe. Se loloolo ɔ, ní hehi
fuu ngɛ nɛ wa ma nyɛ maa kase ngɛ Yehowa he. (Yoh. 17:3)
Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, wa ma nyɛ maa kase ní fuu kɛ kɔ níhi nɛ
haa nɛ Yehowa bua jɔɔ he, kɛ bɔ nɛ wa ma plɛ kɛ ná hɛ kɛ nɔ
fɔmi nɛ mi wa ngɛ e mi ha. Ke wa ngɛ Yehowa sɔmɔe jeha
<<<P>>>
1. Ke wa susu nile nɛ Yehowa ngɛ, bɔ nɛ e juɛmi mi kuɔ ha, kɛ ní slɔɔtoslɔɔto-
hi nɛ e bɔ ɔmɛ a he ɔ, kɛ wa nuɔ he ha kɛɛ?
<<<P>>>
2. Mɛni he je nɛ wa ngɛ nɔ mi mami kaa wa ma nyɛ maa le Yehowa a?
<<<P>>>
3. Mɛni he wa ma susu ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi?
