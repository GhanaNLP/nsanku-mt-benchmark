Mo susu níhi nɛ Yehowa bɔ ɔ he konɛ o le lɛ saminya
(Hyɛ kuku 15)
<<<P>>>
ha nɛ wa susu nɔ́ nɛ wa ma nyɛ maa pee konɛ waa ya nɔ nɛ waa kase Yehowa he ní ɔ he.
<<<P>>>
12 Kekleekle ɔ, e sa nɛ waa kase Baiblo ɔ sa-minya konɛ waa le Yehowa he níhi fuu. Be komɛ ɔ, Baiblo ɔ tuɔ Mawu su ɔmɛ ekomɛ a he munyu tɛɛ. (2 Mose 34:6, 7) Se be komɛ hu ɔ, e pee we jã, mohu ɔ, e tuɔ nimli kɛ yi mi kpɔ komɛ nɛ a mwɔ ɔ he munyu. Ke o ngɛ sa-nehi kaa jã a kanee ɔ, o ma nyɛ ma bi o he ke, ‘Mɛni sane nɛ ɔ tsɔɔ mi ngɛ Yehowa he?’
<<<P>>>
13 Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, ke o kane Yeremia 38:6-13 ɔ, o maa na kaa Etiopia nyumu ko nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Ebedmelek ɔ lɛ ha nɛ a kpɔ̃ gbalɔ Yeremia a nɛ. E ngɛ heii kaa e he be wae kaa o maa na blɔhi a nɔ nɛ o maa gu kɛ kase he kɛ afɔle sami su, kã, kɛ suɔmi nɛ Ebedmelek je kpo ɔ. Se kɛ̃ ɔ, o ma nyɛ maa kase su kpakpa-hi nɛ Yehowa je kpo ɔ ngɛ sane nɛ ɔ hu mi. (Kane Yeremia 39:15-18.) Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, mɛni lɛ ngɛ ngmami nɛ ɔ mi nɛ tsɔɔ kaa Yehowa jeɔ hɛsa kpo, e yeɔ dami sane, nɛ e hyɛ we nɔ hɛ mi? Jehanɛ hu ɔ, mɛni ji nɔ́ kpa ko nɛ o ma nyɛ maa kase ngɛ Yehowa he ngɛ sane nɛ ɔ mi? Be fɛɛ be nɛ o maa kane sane ko ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi ɔ, moo hyɛ kaa o maa yɔse suhi nɛ Yehowa je kpo ɔ lo, nɛ o je Yehowa yi ngɛ he, nɛ o na lɛ si ngɛ jamɛ a su ɔmɛ a he.
<<<P>>>
14 Enyɔne, wa ma nyɛ maa kase Yehowa he ní fuu ke wa pue wa yi mi tɛ ngɛ níhi nɛ e bɔ ɔmɛ a he. (La 145:9, 10) Enɛ ɔ biɔ ba-bauu pe adebɔ níhi pɛ nɛ wa maa hyɛ. Ni komɛ ngɔ jeha babauu kɛ kpa adebɔ níhi a si, se kɛ̃ ɔ, a he we yi kaa Mawu lɛ bɔ níhi, nɛ a nyɛ we nɛ a na su kpakpahi nɛ e ngɛ ɔ hulɔ! Bɔfo Paulo tsɔɔ kaa a “naa [Mawu] suhi nɛ a nɛ kɛ hɛngmɛ, nɛ ji e he wami nɛ ngɛ daa a kɛ Mawu nɛ e ji ɔ heii. Ejakaa a guɔ níhi nɛ e bɔ ɔ nɔ kɛ yɔseɔ, lɔ ɔ he ɔ, a be nya jemi ko.” (Rom. 1:20) Baiblo sisi tsɔɔmi ko de kaa, “ke nɔ ko susuɔ níhi a he kɛ yaa tsi-tsaa a, e ma nyɛ ma na Mawu su ɔmɛ.” Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa e sa nɛ wa ná tsui bleuu kɛ susu níhi
