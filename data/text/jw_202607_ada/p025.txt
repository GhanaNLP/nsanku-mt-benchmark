Yehowa bua nɛ jɔ aloo e ma ha nɛ e hao? I wo lɛ he wami hu kaa e sɔle kɛ ha Yehowa ngɛ sane ɔ he, nɛ́ e hyɛ blɔ nɔ nɛ Yehowa maa gu kɛ ha e sɔlemi ɔ heto. Nyagbe ɔ, e kplɛɛ we ní tsu-mi ɔ nɔ. Se e kɛ we kulaa nɛ e ná ní tsumi kpa nɛ ma ha nɛ e nyɛ nɛ e ya asafo mi kpe ɔmɛ tsuo.” Mo kadi kaa Marianne de we e Baiblo kaselɔ ɔ yi mi kpɔ né e sa kaa e mwɔ, mohu ɔ, e wo lɛ he wami konɛ e puɛ e yi mi tɛ ngɛ nó nɛ e le kɛ kɔ Yehowa he ɔ he. Enɛ ɔ ha nɛ huɛ bɔmi kpakpa ba ngɛ e kɛ Marianne a kpɛti, nɛ nó nɛ pe kulaa a, e hɛ kɛ su Yehowa he wawɛɛ. Mari-anne de kɛ kɔ e Baiblo kaselɔ ɔ he ke: “E ba yɔse kaa ke wa ngɔ Yehowa sɔmɔmi kɛ pee nó titli ngɛ wa si himi mi ɔ, e be wɔ kuae kɔkɔɔkɔ.”

YEHOWA MAA JƆƆ MO KƐ O BA LE LƐ SAMINYA
<<<P>>>
17 Ke waa kɛ wa Baiblo kaseli ɔmɛ ngɛ Bai-blo ɔ kasee ɔ, a ba kase ɔ níhi fuu nɛ a he hia. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nó ɔ, a ba kase ɔ Baiblo sisije tsɔɔmihi komɛ. Ekomɛ ji, si fɔfɔɛ mi nɛ gbo-gboe ɔmɛ ngɛ, nó he je nɛ Yesu gbo, kɛ nó nɛ Mawu Matsɛ Yemi ɔ maa pee. Jehanɛ se hu ɔ, a ba leɔ bɔ nɛ a to asafo ɔ he blɔ nya ha. Ekomɛ ji níhi nɛ yaa nɔ ngɛ asafo mi kpe ɔmɛ a sisi, níhi nɛ asafo mi nikɔtɔmahi peeɔ, kɛ nó nɛ Blɔ Tsɔɔmi Ajla Toli ɔmɛ tsuɔ. Se kaa bɔ nɛ wa na ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, pi ní nɛ ɔmɛ pɛ nɛ wa suɔ nɛ wa Baiblo kaseli ɔmɛ nɛ a kase. Mohu ɔ, wa suɔ nɛ a ba le Yehowa saminya.
<<<P>>>
18 E ji he blɔ ngua nɛ wa ná kaa wa maa tsɔɔ ni kpahi ní konɛ a ba le Yehowa! Amlɔ nɛ ɔ po ɔ, nihi nɛ a ba leɔ Yehowa nɛ a sɔmɔɔ lɛ ɔ náa bua jɔmi wawɛɛ nɛ a si himi peeɔ kpakpa hulɔ. (La 25:12-15) Hwɔɔ se hu ɔ, nihi tsuo nɛ a maa le Yehowa, nɛ “e ji anɔkualetsɛ ɔ” ma ná neneene wami. (1 Yoh. 5:20) Se pi lɔ ɔ pɛ. Bai-blo ɔ ha nɛ wa na kaa nihi nɛ a bɔɔ mɔde kaa a maa le Yehowa, nɛ a maa ngɔ a ní peepee kɛ tsɔɔ kaa a suɔ lɛ ɔ, ‘Mawu hu maa le mɛ.’ (1 Kor. 8:3) Ee, Yehowa Mawu hɛngmɛ maa hi a he be fɛɛ be nɛ e ma tsɛ mɛ ke e huɛmɛ. Nii-nɛ, lemi nɛ wa maa le anɔkuale Mawu ɔ ji jɔɔ-mi nɛ se be!
