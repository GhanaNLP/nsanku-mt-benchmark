‘Kɛ nɔ́ nɛ o kase ngɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu he ɔ ha nɛ o na kaa a maa ye dami sane ngɛ Harmagedon ta a mi ha kɛɛ?’ (ní kasemi 33)
<<<P>>>

<<<P>>>

9 Ke waa kɛ nɔ ko je Baiblo ɔ kasemi sisi pɛ ɔ, wa woɔ lɛ he wami konɛ e kane Baiblo ɔ daa ligbi.* Mɛni blɔ nɔ wa maa gu kɛ ye bua wa Baiblo kaselɔ ɔ konɛ e ba le Yehowa saminya ke e ngɛ Baiblo ɔスーパーkanee ɔ? Wa ma nyɛ maa de lɛ kaa ke e ngɛ Baiblo mi sane koスーパーkanee ɔ, e bɔ mɔde kaa e maa na nɔ́ he je nɛ Yehowa ha nɛ a ngma jamɛ a sane ɔ kɛ fɔ si, kɛ nɔ́ nɛ lɔ ɔ tsɔɔ lɛ ngɛ Yehowa he. Yakobo pee enɛ ɔ he nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ be nɛ e ngɛ Hiob sane ɔ he munyu tue ɔ. Pi Hiob si fimi ɔ pɛ he munyu nɛ Yakobo tu, mohu ɔ, e tu suhi nɛ Yehowa ngɛ ɔ hu he munyu, nɛ lɔ ɔmɛ ji, mi mi himi kɛ mɔbɔ nami.—Kane Yakobo 5:11.
<<<P>>>
10 Wa suɔ nɛ ke wa Baiblo kaseli ɔmɛ kane Baiblo mi sane ko ɔ, a nyɛ nɛ a yɔse níhi nɛ Yehowa suɔ kɛ níhi nɛ e sume ɔ ngɛ jamɛ a sane ɔ mi. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, eko ɔ, benɛ wa Baiblo kaselɔ ɔ ya hyɛ daka nɛ e yi ji “Moo Je Baiblo ɔ Kanemi Sisi” nɛ ngɛ Ná Bua Jɔmi Kɛ Ya Neneene! womi ɔ mi ɔ, e de kaa e suɔ nɛ e kane Daniel nɛ a nu lɛ kɛ wo jatahi a muɔ mi ɔ he ní. Ke e ba jã a, wa ma nyɛ maa wo lɛ he wami nɛ e susu sane bimi nɛ ɔmɛ a he: ‘Mɛni he je nɛ Yehowa he Daniel yi wami ɔ? Mɛni lɛ sane nɛ ɔ tsɔɔ mi ngɛ níhi nɛ Yehowa suɔ ɔ he?’ (Dan. 6:10, 22) E ma nyɛ ma bi e he hu ke: ‘Mɛni lɛ ba nyumu ɔmɛ nɛ a po Daniel nya a, a nɔ? Mɛni lɛ sane nɛ ɔ tsɔɔ mi ngɛ níhi nɛ Yehowa sume ɔ he?’ (Dan. 6:4, 5, 24) Nyagbe ɔ, wa ma nyɛ maa wo lɛ he wami nɛ e susu ní kpahi nɛ e ma nyɛ

9-10. (a) Mɛni maa ye bua Baiblo kaseli konɛ a le níhi fuu kɛ kɔ Yehowa he ke a ngɛ Baiblo ɔスーパーkanee? (Yakobo 5:11) (b) Ngɛ Daniel sane ɔ nya a, mɛni sane bimihi lɛ ma nyɛ maa ye bua Baiblo kaselɔ ko konɛ e le níhi nɛ Yehowa suɔ kɛ níhi nɛ e sume?
