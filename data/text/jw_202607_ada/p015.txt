MƐNI NƐ LEMI NƐ WA MAA LE YEHOWA A TSƆƆ?
<<<P>>>
4 Mɛni ji blɔ kake nɛ wa ma nyɛ maa gu nɔ kɛ le Yehowa saminya? Yehowa wo Yudabi nɛ a maa je nyɔguɛ tso mi ngɛ Babilon kɛ ba a si ke: “Ma ha mɛ tsui nɛ a kɛ maa le mi, kaa imi ji Yehowa.” (Yer. 24:7) E ngɛ heii kaa Yudabi nɛ ɔmɛ le níhi fuu kɛ kɔ Yehowa he momo. Se be nɛ a je nyɔguɛ tso mi kɛ ba a, e he ba hia nɛ a hɛ kɛ su Yehowa he wawɛɛ, nɛ jã peemi daa si ngɛ a tsui ɔ nɔ. Baiblo he ní lelɔ ko de ke: “Slɔɔto agbo ngɛ Mawu he ní nɛ wa maa le kɛ Mawu nɛ wa maa le lɛ saminya nɛ waa kɛ lɛ maa pee huɛ ɔ a kpɛti. Wa ma nyɛ maa ngɔ enɛ ɔ kɛ to bɔ nɛ nɔ ko peeɔ e ní ha ke e kane ní kɛ kɔ suɔmi he, kɛ bɔ nɛ e peeɔ e ní ha ke lɛ nitsɛ e ná nɔ ko he suɔmi ɔ.” Lemi nɛ wa maa le kaa Mawu ngɛ, kɛ e biɛ nɛ ji Yehowa nɛ wa maa le ɔ pɛ pi. E sa kaa waa le nɔ tutuutu nɛ e ji.
<<<P>>>
5 Ke wa le níhi nɛ Yehowa suɔ kɛ níhi nɛ e sume ɔ, lɔ ɔ hu tsɔɔ kaa wa ngɛ lɛ lee. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, mo susu Matsɛ Yosia he nɛ o hyɛ. Baiblo ɔ de ke: “Ngɛ e nɔ yemi jeha nɛ ji 8 ɔ mi be mi nɛ e ji nyumuyo loloolo ɔ, e bɔni e nɛnɛ David Mawu ɔ se blɔ hlami.” (2 Kron. 34:3) Yosia kase Mawu he níhi fuu ngɛ ngmami ɔ mi, se pi lɔ ɔ pɛ, e kɛ níhi nɛ e kase ɔ hu tsu ní. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, Yehowa de kɛ gu gbalɔ Yeremia nɔ kaa Yosia “ye dami sane nɛ e pee nɔ́ nɛ da,” nɛ e ye nɔ́ nali kɛ ohiatsɛmɛ a sane kɛ ha mɛ. Yehowa de hu ke: “Anɛ pi enɛ ɔ nɛ tsɔɔ kaa nɔ ko le mi ɔ lo?”—Yer. 22:15, 16.
<<<P>>>
6 Kane La 9:10. Ke wa le Yehowa saminya a, lɔ ɔ haa nɛ hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi nɛ wa ngɛ ngɛ e mi ɔ mi waa. Jã nɛ e ji ngɛ bɔfo Paulo blɔ fa mi. Benɛ a wo lɛ tsu, nɛ e be kɛe nɛ a maa gbe lɛ ɔ, e ngma ke: “Zo pee we mi. Ejakaa i le Nɔ nɛ i he lɛ ye ɔ.” (2 Tim. 1:12) Níhi nɛ Paulo kase ngɛ Yehowa he kɛ bɔ nɛ Yehowa ye bua lɛ ngɛ e si himi mi ha a, ha nɛ e ná hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi nɛ mi wa ngɛ Yehowa mi. Bɔfo Paulo ngɛ nɔ mi mami kaa Yehowa hɛ be anɔkuale nɛ e ye lɛ ɔ nɔ jee, nɛ ke e gbo po ɔ, e ma tle lɛ si kɛ ba wami mi ekohu.

MƐNI HE JE NƐ E SA NƐ WAA YA NƆ NƐ WAA LE YEHOWA SAMINYA A?
<<<P>>>
7 Benɛ wa ba bɔni Yehowa he ní kasemi pɛ ɔ, wa ba le níhi fuu kɛ kɔ e he, nɛ lɔ ɔ ha nɛ wa ba ngɛ lɛ sɔmɔe. Se ke be ngɛ mi puee ɔ, eko ɔ, mɔde nɛ wa bɔɔ kaa wa maa le Yehowa saminya a ma bɔni si bami. Mɛni ma ha nɛ e ba lɛ jã a? Behi fuu ɔ, ke wa ngɛ Baiblo ɔ kanee ngɛ we mi loo ngɛ asafo mi kpe sisi ɔ, waa kɛ wa juɛmi maa bɔ nɛ nɔ́ nɛ wa ngɛ kasee ɔ maa ye bua wɔ aloo ni kpahi ha a nɔ. Tɔmi ko be he kaa wa maa pee jã, ejakaa Baiblo ɔ woɔ wɔ he wami kaa waa pee jã. (2 Tim. 3:16, 17) Se nɔ́ kpa ko hu ngɛ nɛ e he hia wawɛɛ nɛ e sa kaa wa susu he. (La 25:8-10) E sɛ nɛ waa ngɔ wa juɛmi kɛ ma bɔ nɛ nɔ́ nɛ wa ngɛ kanee ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi ɔ maa ye bua wɔ kɛ ni kpahi ha a
