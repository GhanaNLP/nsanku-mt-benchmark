Moo tsɔɔ o Baiblo kaselɔ ɔ konɛ e kai nó nɛ e le ngɛ Yehowa he ke e kane Baiblo mi sane ko nɛ e nui sisi ɔ
(Hyɛ kuku 12-13)
<<<P>>>
maa kase kɛ kɔ Yehowa he ngɛ sane nɛ ɔ mi ɔ he.—Dan. 6:26, 27.
<<<P>>>
11 Ke wa na kaa wa Baiblo kaselɔ ɔ ngɛ níhi nɛ e ngɛ kasee ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi ɔ he se náe ɔ, e haa nɛ wa bua jɔɔ. Se e sa nɛ waa ye bua lɛ nɛ e na kaa Yehowa nɛ ji wa Tsɔɔlɔ Ngua a lɛ ngɛ lɛ ní ɔ tsɔɔe ɔ nɛ. (Yes. 30:20, 21) Eko ɔ, wa Baiblo kaselɔ ɔ maa na wɔ si ngɛ ní nɛ waa kɛ lɛ ngɛ kasee ɔ he, aloo eko po ɔ, e maa je wa yi ngɛ bɔ nɛ wa plɛɔ kɛ tsɔɔ lɛ ní ha a he. E ngɛ mi kaa tɔmi ko be he kaa e ma je wa yi mohu lɛɛ, se e sa nɛ be fɛɛ be ɔ, wa kai lɛ kaa Yehowa ji nɔ nɛ ngɛ lɛ ní ɔ tsɔɔe, lɔ ɔ he ɔ, e sa nɛ e na lɛ mohu si, se pi wɔ.

YE BUA O BAIBLO KASELƆ Ɔ NƐ E NGƆ NÓ NƐ E NGƐ KASEE NGƐ YEHOWA HE Ɔ KƐ TSU NÍ
<<<P>>>
12 E sa nɛ Baiblo kaselɔ ko nɛ e ngɔ níhi nɛ e kase kɛ kɔ Yehowa he ɔ kɛ tsu ní ngɛ e si himi
<<<P>>>
11. Mɛni he je nɛ e he hia kaa waa ye bua wa Baiblo kaseli ɔmɛ nɛ a na kaa Yehowa ji nɔ nɛ ngɛ mɛ ní tsɔɔe ɔ?
<<<P>>>
12-13. Ke Baiblo kaselɔ ko le Yehowa saminya a, kɛ lɔ ɔ maa ye bua lɛ ha kɛɛ ke e ngɛ Baiblo mi sane ko kanee nɛ e nui sisi ɔ? (Hyɛ foni ɔ hulɔ.)
<<<P>>>
mi. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, mɛni blɔ nɔ o maa gu kɛ ye bua o Baiblo kaselɔ ɔ konɛ e nyɛ nɛ e pee jã? Blɔ kake nɛ o maa gu nɔ kɛ ye bua lɛ ji, nɛ o maa tsɔɔ lɛ nɛ e susu nó nɛ e le kɛ kɔ Yehowa he ɔ he ke e kane Baiblo mi sane ko nɛ e nui sisi. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nó ɔ, ke e ngɛ Baiblo mi sane ko kanee kɛ kɔ bɔ nɛ Yehowa tsu sane komɛ a he ní ha blema a, eko ɔ, e yi mi maa pee lɛ enyɔɔnyɔ ngɛ nó he je nɛ Yehowa kpata ni komɛ a hɛ mi, se e baa ni komɛ a wami ɔ yi ɔ he. Ke e ba lɛ jã a, mɛni blɔ nɔ o maa gu kɛ ye bua lɛ konɛ e yi mi nɛ ko pee lɛ enyɔɔnyɔ hu?
<<<P>>>
13 O ma nyɛ maa wo o Baiblo kaselɔ ɔ he wami konɛ e susu níhi nɛ e kase kɛ kɔ Yehowa he ɔ he. O ma nyɛ maa wo lɛ he wami nɛ e bi e he ke: Kɛ Yehowa naa wami ha kɛɛ? (2 Pet. 3:9) Mɛni ji nó ko nɛ he jua wa nɛ Yehowa kɛ sã afɔle konɛ e kɛ he wa yi wami be mi nɛ wa ji yayami peeli ɔ? (Efe. 2:4, 5) Anɛ Yehowa gblaa nihi nɛ a pee we nó ko ɔ a tue lo? Ke nɔ ko sa tue gblami ɔ, anɛ e be e tue gblae lo? (2 Mose 34:6, 7) Be komɛ ɔ, Baiblo ɔ de we wɔ níhi fuu nɛ maa ye kɛ bua wɔ konɛ waa le nó he je nɛ Yehowa tsu sane komɛ a he ní ngɛ
