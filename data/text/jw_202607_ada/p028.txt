I NÁ TSƆSEMIHI FUU NGƐ BROOKLYN BETEL
<<<P>>>
Benɛ i kɛ Wilson hi si jeha enyɔ se ɔ, a fɔ mi nine kaa ma ba sɔmɔ ngɛ Brooklyn Betel ngɛ jeha 1985 ɔ mi. Kaa bɔ nɛ i de kɛ sɛ hlami ɔ, a ha nɛ i ya tsu ní ngɛ he nɛ a peeɔ womihi ngɛ ɔ. Ligbi ko nɛ i kɛ womi peemi klama a eko ngɛ ní tsue ɔ, i pee tɔ̃tɔ̃mi ko, nɛ lɔ ɔ ha nɛ womi se toto lafahi abɔ puɛ. Nyɛmimɛ nɛ i kɛ mɛ tsuɔ ní ngɛ Womi Peemi He ɔ de mi kaa ma ya bɔ nɔ nɛ hyɛɔ jamɛ a ní tsumi ɔ nɔ ɔ amaniɛ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, i ya de lɛ anɔkuale nɛ ngɛ sane ɔ mi ɔ tsuo, se nó kake ko ngɛ nɛ e he hia wawɛɛ nɛ i pee we. I kpa we pɛɛ. Lɔ ɔ he ɔ, nɔ nɛ hyɛɔ ní tsumi ɔ nɔ ɔ je mi mi jɔmi mi nɛ e de mi ke, “Be fɛɛ be nɛ o ma tɔ̃ ɔ, e maa hi kaa o ma kpa pɛɛ.” Enɛ ɔ ha nɛ i kase kaa e sa nɛ ma kplɛɛ ye tɔ̃-mihi a nɔ nɛ ma kpa he pɛɛ.
<<<P>>>
Nɔ hyɛmi nó kpakpa nɛ nyɛmimɛ anɔkualetsɛmɛ nɛ a wa ngɛ jeha mi ngɛ Betel ɔ pee ɔ ye kɛ bua mi nɛ i kase bɔ nɛ e he hia kaa ma sɔmɔ ni kpahi ha. Ligbi ko piani nɛ wa yaa ye ní ngɛ Betel ní yemi he ɔ, i kɛ nyɛminyumu Milton Henschel nɛ e piɛɛ Blɔ Tsɔɔmi Ajla Toli ɔmɛ a he ɔ hi okplɔɔ kake he. Jamɛ a ligbi ɔ, nihi babauu ba ní yemi he ɔ pe bɔ nɛ a hyɛ blɔ ɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ nɛ a ngɛ niye ní ɔ gbae ɔ ngɛ mɔde bɔe kaa a ma ha nɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ nɛ ná niye ní ɔ eko. Se wɔ nihewi ɔmɛ nɛ wa hii si ngɛ okplɔɔ he ɔ bɔni munyu tumi kaa ní gbali ɔmɛ a he sɔɛ. Nyɛminyumu Henschel lɛɛ e de we nó ko, mohu ɔ, e te si nɛ e ya piɛɛ ní gbali ɔmɛ a he kɛ gba niye ní ɔ. Ye hɛ be he si bami su nɛ nyɛminyu-mu Henschel je kpo ɔ nɔ jee gblee. Enɛ ɔ ha nɛ i kai benɛ Yesu fɔ e kaseli ɔmɛ a nane he kɛ ha mɛ ɔ. —Yoh. 13:3-5.
