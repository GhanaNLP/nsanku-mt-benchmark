wa ngɛ kɛ ha Yehowa a yeɔ buaa wɔ nɛ wa peeɔ jã. Jã ji bɔ nɛ nyɛminyumu ko nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Alwin nɛ e je Philippines ɔ pee. Ní tsumi nɛ e tsuɔ ɔ haa nɛ e náa sika fuu. Se e suɔ nɛ e ngɔ e be fuu kɛ sɔmɔ Yehowa. Be fɛɛ be nɛ kpɔ mi nɔ hyɛlɔ ɔ ma ba slaa a we asafo ɔ, Alwin biɔ blɔ ngɛ e nitsumitsɛ ɔ ngɔ konɛ e nyɛ nɛ e ngɔ e he kɛ wo fiɛɛmi ní tsumi ɔ mi ngɛ jamɛ a otsi ɔ mi. Enɛ ɔ haa nɛ e bua jɔɔ wawɛɛ. Se ní tsumi nɛ Alwin ngɛ tsue ɔ be lɛ blɔ ngmɛɛ nɛ e tsu blɔ gbami ní tsumi ɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e sɔle ha Yehowa nɛ́ e ye bua lɛ konɛ e ná ní tsumi kpa. E ngɛ mi kaa Alwin le kaa ke e kpa ní tsumi nɛ ɔ, e be sika fuu náe mohu lɛɛ, se kẽ ɔ, e pee jã. Enɛ ɔ ye bua lɛ nɛ e je blɔ gbami ní tsumi ɔ sisi. Pee se ɔ, e yo ɔ hu je blɔ gbami ní tsumi ɔ sisi, nɛ a nyɛ nɛ a ye bua nihi 21 nɛ a ba kase anɔkuale ɔ, nɛ a baptisi mɛ. Atsinyɛ jemi ko be he kaa Yehowa bua jɔ a mɔde bɔmi ɔ he wawɛɛ nitsɛ. Ke wɔ hu wa pee nó fɛɛ nó nɛ wa ma nyɛ konɛ waa pee babauu ngɛ Yehowa sɔmɔmi mi ɔ, wa ma nyɛ ma ná nɔ mi mami kaa Yeho-wa bua maa jɔ wa he wawɛɛ. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, e ma ha wɔ wa hiami níhi be fɛɛ be.—Mat. 6:33.

17 Bɔ nɛ Yehowa kɛ Gibeonbi ɔmɛ hi si ha a, haa nɛ wa naa su kpakpahi nɛ Yehowa ngɛ ɔ. Wa ba na kaa Yehowa baa e he si, e naa nɔ mɔbɔ, e yeɔ anɔkuale, e yeɔ dami sane, nɛ e jɔɔ e sɔmɔli nɛ a yeɔ lɛ anɔkuale ɔ be fɛɛ be. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, Gibeonbi ɔmɛ a sane ɔ ha nɛ wa kase kaa, e sa nɛ wa ná hemi kɛ yemi nɛ mi wa ngɛ Yehowa mi, nɛ waa ngɔ wa hɛ kɛ fɔ e nɔ ke waa kɛ si fɔfɔɛhi nɛ a mi wa kpe. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, e sa nɛ waa ba wa he si, nɛ waa kplɛɛ ní tsumi fɛɛ ní tsumi nɛ Yehowa kɛ ma ha wɔ kaa wa tsu ɔ nɔ. Ke a ye wɔ sane yaya a, e sa nɛ waa to wa tsui si kɛ mlɛ Yehowa konɛ e tsu sane ɔ he ní kɛ ha wɔ ngɛ be nɛ sa mi. Wa suɔ nɛ waa pee nó fɛɛ nó nɛ wa ma nyɛ konɛ waa pee babauu ngɛ Yehowa sɔmɔmi mi. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ wa kai nɔ hyɛmi ní kpakpahi nɛ Gibeonbi ɔmɛ pee ɔ, nɛ waa kase mɛ.
