<<<P>>>
4 Wa si fɔfɔɛ ma nyɛ ma tsake tlukaa. (Fiɛɛ. 9:11; 1 Kor. 7:31) E ma nyɛ maa ba lɛ kaa hiɔ maa fia wɔ tlukaa, waa kɛ sika he nyagba maa kpe, loo basabasa peemi ma nyɛ maa te si ngɛ he nɛ wa ngɛ ɔ. Aloo eko ɔ, asafo ɔ ma ha nɛ wa ya sɔmɔ ngɛ he kpa. Nyagbahi kaa jã a ma nyɛ ma ha nɛ waa hao loo wa juɛmi nɛ gbɛ. Eko ɔ, wa ma bi ke: ‘Mɛni ma pee amlɔ nɛ ɔ?’ Se tɔmi ko be he kaa wa ma bi wa he jã. Nyɛminyumu ko nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Joseph ɔ sɔmɔ ngɛ Betel maa pee jeha 17. Pee se benɛ a de lɛ ke e ya sɔmɔ kaa blɔ gbalɔ ɔ, e de ke: “I li nó nɛ maa pee aloo bɔ nɛ ma je si himi ɔ sisi ha, ejakaa i kɛ jehahi babauu sɔmɔ ngɛ Betel. Enɛ ɔ ha nɛ ye juɛmi gbɛ.” Ke waa kɛ si fɔfɔɛhi nɛ wa hyɛ we blɔ ɔ kpe ɔ, mɛni nɔ mi mami nɛ Yesaya womi ɔ ma nyɛ ma ha nɛ wa ná?

5 Kane Yesaya 42:16. Yehowa wo si kaa e maa nyɛɛ nihi nɛ ngɛ okadi peemi blɔ nɔ ɔ, a ngɛ kaa hɛ yuyuili ɔ a hɛ mi kɛ gu blɔhi nɛ a gui nɔ hyɛ ɔ a nɔ, nɛ e ma ha nɛ diblii nɛ ngɛ a hɛ mi ɔ ma plɛ pee la. A kɛ munyuhi nɛ ji “hɛ yuyuili,” “blɔhi nɛ a gui nɔ hyɛ,” “diblii,” kɛ “zugba nɛ e nɔ ngɛ googagooga” a ma nyɛ maa to bɔ nɛ wa naa si fɔfɔɛhi nɛ waa kɛ kpeɔ tlukaa ha a he. Si fɔfɔɛhi kaa kikɛ ma nyɛ ma ha nɛ waa nu he kaa wa ngɛ nyɛɛe ngɛ diblii kpii ko mi, nɛ wa li nó nɛ ngɛ wa hɛ mi. Se Yehowa ha wɔ nɔ mi mami kaa ke waa kɛ si fɔfɔɛhi nɛ a mi wa ngɛ kpee ɔ, e maa tsɔɔ wɔ blɔ, nɛ e be wɔ kuae kɔkɔɔkɔ.
