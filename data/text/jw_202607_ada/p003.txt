Gibeonbi ɔmɛ mlɔɔ Yoshua nɛ e kɛ mɛ so tue mi jɔmi somi
(Hyɛ kuku 4)
<<<P>>>
nɛ hwu ta, se Gibeonbi ɔmɛ lɛɛ a pee we a ní jã, a suɔ nɛ a kɛ Israelbi ɔmɛ nɛ ya pee kake, ejakaa a yɔse kaa a be nyɛe maa ye a nɔ kunimi. A le kaa Yehowa ngɛ ta hwue kɛ ngɛ Israelbi ɔmɛ hae. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, a le kaa Yehowa wo Israelbi ɔmɛ si kaa e maa ngɔ Kanaan zugba a tsuo kɛ ha mɛ. (2 Mose 34:11; Yosh. 9:24) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, akɛnɛ Israelbi ɔmɛ ye Yeriko kɛ Ai nɔ kunimi he je ɔ, Gibeonbi ɔmɛ tsɔ nyumu komɛ* kɛ ya Yoshua ngɔ ngɛ Gilgal konɛ a kɛ Israelbi ɔmɛ nɛ ya so tue mi jɔmi somi.
<<<P>>>
ha a he. Se Gibeonbi ɔmɛ kɛ nile tsu ní, nɛ a wui nɔ́ nɛ ba Yeriko kɛ Ai ma a nɔ ɔ nɔ ta, ejakaa ke a pee jã a, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ Israelbi ɔmɛ maa na kaa a ngɛ mɛ sisie kaa a je he ko tsitsaa. Jinɛ e sa kaa Yoshua kɛ Israel ma nikɔtɔma amɛ ko bi Yehowa blɔ tsɔɔmi, se a pee we jã. Mohu ɔ, a kplɛɛ Gibeonbi ɔmɛ a ní bimi ɔ nɔ nɛ a kɛ mɛ so tue mi jɔmi somi. (Yosh. 9:14, 15) E kɛ we kulaa nɛ Israelbi ɔmɛ ba na kaa mamoo Gibeonbi ɔmɛ sisi mɛ. Se akɛnɛ Israelbi ɔmɛ ngɔ ‘Yehowa kɛ kã mɛ kita’ he je ɔ, a ye a si womi ɔ nɔ. (Yosh. 9:16-19) Se Israelbi ɔmɛ ha nɛ Gibeonbi ɔmɛ tsu nyɔguɛhi a ní tsumi. A ha nɛ a ba pee ‘nihi nɛ a buaa lé nya, nɛ a saa nyu kɛ haa asafo ɔ kɛ Yehowa afɔle sami latɛ ɔ’ hulɔ.—Yosh. 9:27.
