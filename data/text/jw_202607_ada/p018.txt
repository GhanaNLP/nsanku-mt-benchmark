15 Ke o ngɛ ma ngua mi po ɔ, o ma nyɛ ma pue o yi mi tɛ ngɛ adebɔ níhi a he. Mo susu nyɛminyumu Patrick nɛ e ngɛ Republic of the Congo ma ngua nɛ ji Brazzaville mi ɔ he nɛ o hyɛ. E de ke: “Daa ligbi ɔ, i bɔɔ mɔde kaa ma hyɛ adebɔ nó ko. Be komɛ ɔ, i naa lohwe tsɔwi komɛ kaa tatuhi kɛ lohwe pɛlitsɛmɛ nɛ a hɛ dɔ kɛ ngɛ niye ní aloo he nɛ a maa pee tsu ngɛ hlae. Ligbi ko ɔ, i na lohwe pɛlitsɛ komɛ nɛ a ngɛ tsu pee. E ngɛ mi kaa wa ngɛ ma ngua mi mohu lɛɛ, se lohwe pɛlitsɛmɛ nɛ ɔmɛ na ní komɛ, nɛ a kɛ pee tsu kɛ ha a kɛ a bimɛ ɔmɛ.” Nyɛminyumu Patrick hyɛ we lohwe nɛ ɔmɛ pɛ kɛkɛ, mohu ɔ, e susu nó nɛ enɛ ɔ ma nyɛ maa tsɔɔ lɛ ngɛ Bɔlɔ ɔ he ɔ he kɛ ya tsitsaa. E de ke: “I kase kaa Yehowa susuɔ níhi nɛ e bɔ ɔ tsuo a he, a kle jio, a klee we jio. Enɛ ɔ ha nɛ i ná nɔ mi mami kaa bɔ fɛɛ bɔ nɛ ji ɔ, Yehowa hu maa hyɛ ye nɔ, nɛ e ma ha mi níhi nɛ i hia a.”

16 Nɔ́ etɛne nɛ wa ma nyɛ maa pee konɛ waa le Yehowa saminya ji nɛ wa ma susu níhi nɛ Yehowa pee kɛ ha wɔ be ko nɛ be ɔ he. Jã pɛ lɛ Yesu mami Maria pee.
