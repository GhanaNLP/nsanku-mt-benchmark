14 Mɛni blɔ kpa nɔ o ma nyɛ maa gu kɛ ye bua o Baiblo kaselɔ ɔ konɛ e ngɔ níhi nɛ e kase ngɛ Yehowa he ɔ kɛ tsu ní? Ngɔɔ lɛ kaa o Baiblo kaselɔ ɔ bɔni asafo mi kpehi yami, se e he wa kɛ ha lɛ kaa e kɛ nyɛmi ko ngɛ asafo ɔ mi ɔ maa bɔ. Eko ɔ, nyɛmi ɔ pee nɔ́ ko nɛ e gba e nya wawɛɛ. Ke jã a, lɛɛ mɛni o maa pee kɛ ye bua lɛ? Moo wo lɛ he wami nɛ e susu bɔ nɛ Yehowa naa nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi nɛ a ngɛ asafo ɔ mi ɔ ha a he. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, e ma nyɛ ma bi e he ke: Mɛni Yehowa kɛ sã afɔle kɛ tsɔɔ kaa e suɔ mɛ? (Yoh. 3:16) Kɛ kake peemi nɛ maa hi wa kpɛti ɔ he hia Yehowa ha kɛɛ? (La 133:1) Ke wa bɔɔ mɔde kaa wa maa baa kake peemi nɛ ngɛ wa kpɛti ɔ yi ɔ, kɛ Yehowa maa nu he ha kɛɛ? (2 Kor. 13:11) Moo ye kɛ bua o Baiblo kaselɔ ɔ nɛ e na kaa Yehowa bua jɔɔ wa he wawɛɛ ke wa ngɔ wa juɛmi kɛ ma su kpakpahi nɛ wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ ngɛ ɔ nɔ mohu pe a gbɔjɔmihi a nɔ.

Moo ye bua o Baiblo kaselɔ ɔ nɛ e mwɔ yi mi kpɔhi nɛ maa sa Yehowa hɛ mi (Hye kuku 15-16)

15 Jehanɛ hu ɔ, o ma nyɛ maa ye kɛ bua o Baiblo kaselɔ ɔ konɛ e da níhi nɛ e kase kɛ kɔ Yehowa he ɔ nɔ kɛ mwɔ yi mi kpɔhi nɛ da. O ma nyɛ maa gbla e juɛmi kɛ ya ní kasemi 35 nɛ e yi ji “Blɔ Nɔ Nɛ Wa Maa Gu Kɛ Mwɔ Yi Mi Kpɔhi Nɛ Da” nɛ ngɛ Ná Bua Jɔmi Kɛ Ya Neneene! womi ɔ mi ɔ nɔ. Se ngɔɔ lɛ kaa o Baiblo kaselɔ ɔ sɔle nɛ e hla níhi a mi kɛ kɔ sane ko he, se kɛ̃ ɔ, e he wa kɛ ha lɛ kaa e maa mwɔ yi mi kpɔ ngɛ sane ɔ he hu nɛɛ? Eko ɔ, e na Baiblo sisi tomi mlaahi fuu nɛ kɔɔ sane ɔ he, se e li a kpɛti nɔ́ nɛ kɔɔ e si fɔfɔɛ ɔ he tutuutu. O ma nyɛ maa ye kɛ bua lɛ konɛ e susu Yesu nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ he. Mɛni nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ Yesu pee ke e maa mwɔ yi mi kpɔ ko? E de ke: “I peeɔ níhi nɛ [ye Tsɛ ɔ] bua jɔɔ he be fɛɛ be.” (Kane Yohane 8:29) Moo wo o Baiblo kaselɔ ɔ he wami nɛ e bi e he ke, ‘Ngɛ níhi a mi nɛ i hla, kɛ nɔ́ nɛ i kase kɛ kɔ Yehowa he ɔ nya a, mɛni yi mi kpɔ lɛ ke i mwɔ ɔ, e maa sa Yehowa hɛ mi?’ Yi mi kpɔ fɛɛ yi mi kpɔ nɛ maa sa Yehowa hɛ mi ɔ ji yi mi kpɔ mwɔmi nɛ da.
