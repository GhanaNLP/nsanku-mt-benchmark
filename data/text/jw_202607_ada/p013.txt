Yehowa ngɛ nihi nɛ a gbo ɔmɛ a he munyu tue ɔ, e de ke: “Ye gbogboe ɔmɛ maa te si.” Munyungu nɛ ji “ye” nɛ Yehowa kɛ tsu ní ɔ, tsɔɔ kaa a ji e níhi. E hɛ ji a nɔ kɔkɔɔkɔ, nɛ ngɛ be nɛ e to ɔ mi ɔ, e ma tle mɛ si kɛ ba wami mi. Kɛ nga gbli nɛ hiɔmi nɛ ɔ, e tsɛ̃ɔ ekohu, jã kɛ̃ nɛ Yehowa, nɔ nɛ ngɔ nɛ wami tsuo je ɔ ma tle wa suɔli nɛ a gbo ɔ si kɛ ba wami mi eko-hu. Hyɛ bɔ nɛ waa kɛ bua jɔmi ma nya a he ha!—Kɛ to Mar. 5:42 ɔ he.

15 E ngɛ mi kaa wa he ye kaa Yehowa ma tle nihi nɛ a gbo ɔ si kɛ ba wami mi ekohu mohu lɛɛ, se kɛ̃ ɔ, ke wa suɔlɔ ko gbo ɔ, wa yeɔ aywi-lɛho. Se lemi nɛ wa le kaa Yehowa hɛ ji wa suɔli nɛ a gbo ɔmɛ a nɔ ɔ woɔ wa bua wawɛɛ. Si womi nɛ ɔ wo Michael nɛ wa wo e ta kɛ sɛ hlami ɔ bua. E de ke: “Yesaya 26:19 ɔ ye bua mi nɛ i ná nɔ mi mami kaa Yehowa suɔ ye fɔli ɔmɛ wawɛɛ. E hɛ ji a nɔ, nɛ e maa ya nɔ ma kai mɛ kɛ ya si be nɛ e ma tle mɛ si. Enɛ ɔ woɔ ye bua nɛ e haa nɛ ye nɔ gbagba teɔ.” Nyɛ ha nɛ waa ya nɔ nɛ waa po be nɛ a ma tle wa suɔli nɛ a gbo ɔ si kɛ ba wami mi ɔ he foni. Ke wa peeɔ jã a, e maa wo wa bua wawɛɛ nitsɛ.

16 Wa susu ngmami bɔɔ komɛ a he ngɛ Yesa-ya womi ɔ mi nɛ maa wo wa bua ke wa si fɔ-fɔɛ tsake tlukaa, aloo ke wa he nile ngɛ wɔ fɔ bue ngɛ yayami ko nɛ wa pee kɛ be ɔ he, kɛ jehanɛ hu ke wa suɔlɔ ko gbo nɛ wa ngɛ aywi-lɛho yee ɔ. Wa bua jɔɔ kaa Yehowa guɔ Bai-blo ɔ nɔ kɛ woɔ wa bua! (La 94:19) Anɛ ngma-mi ko ngɛ nɛ woɔ o bua ke o kɛ si fɔfɔɛhi nɛ a mi wa ngɛ kpee lo? Ke jã a, lɛɛ mo kase e mi munyu ɔmɛ kɛ wo o yi mi. Moo ngma kɛ fɔ womi ko nɔ nɛ o kɛ mɛtɛ si ngɛ he ko nɛ o ma nyɛ maa na be fɛɛ be. Mo ha nɛ munyu nɛ ɔmɛ nɛ wa hiɔwe Tsɛ nɛ e ngɛ suɔmi nɛ ji Yehowa a tu ɔ nɛ wo o bua.
