bua mɛ ngɛ fiɛɛmi ní tsumi ɔ mi, se i nui Japan gbi ɔ. Nyɛmiyo ko nɛ e ngɛ Betel nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Michiko Oda a hyɛ ye hɛ mi too nɛ e de mi ke, “E sa nɛ o kase Japan gbi ɔ!” Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, i bɔ mɔde nɛ i pee jã. Mamoo e ga womi nɛ ɔ ma tsake ye si himi.

Nyɔhiɔ bɔɔ komɛ a se ɔ, i hia kɛ ya je asafo ko nɛ a tuɔ Japan gbi ngɛ mi ɔ mi ngɛ New York. Japan gbi ɔ nɛ i kase ɔ bli he blɔhi fuu ha mi ngɛ Yehowa asafo ɔ mi. Ngɛ jeha 1989 ɔ mi ɔ, i ya kpe ko nɛ a pee ngɛ Los Angeles ngɛ California a. Enɛ ɔ ji kekleekle be nɛ i ya kpe nɛ a pee ngɛ Japan gbi mi ɔ eko. I ná nyɛmiyo ko nɛ e ji blɔ gbalɔ nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Miwako Onami nɛ e piɛɛ nihi nɛ a pee drama a he ɔ he bua jɔmi.
<<<P>>>
I kɛ Miwako sɛ gba si himi mi ngɛ jeha 1992 ɔ mi, nɛ e ba piɛɛ ye he nɛ wa pee kake kɛ sɔmɔ ngɛ Brooklyn Betel. Miwako susuɔ nɔ he, nɛ e mi mi jɔ. Be fɛɛ be nɛ ɔ, e pee klaalo kaa e maa ye bua ni kpahi. E nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ ye bua mi nɛ i kase bɔ nɛ i kɛ ni kpahi maa hi si saminya ha. Miwako ji nike ní nɛ je Yehowa ngɔ. Be fɛɛ be ɔ, e haa nɛ ye bua jɔɔ nɛ ye tsui hu nɔ ɔ ye mi.

WA SƆMƆ NGƐ JAPAN BETEL, NƐ WA SƆMƆ KAA BLƆ GBALI KLƐDƐƐ HULƆ
<<<P>>>
Benɛ be ngɛ mi puee ɔ, Miwako fɔli ɔmɛ nɛ a ngɛ Japan ɔ ba nu hiɔ wawɛɛ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, ngɛ jeha 1999 ɔ mi ɔ, a ha nɛ wa ya sɔmɔ ngɛ asafo ɔ ní tsumi kɔni nɛ ngɛ Japan ɔ, konɛ wa nyɛ nɛ waa hyɛ a nɔ saminya.
<<<P>>>
Nyɛmimɛ nɛ a ngɛ sɔmɔe ngɛ Japan Betel ɔ ha nɛ wa nu he kaa wa ba we mi, nɛ lɔ ɔ ha nɛ wa hɛ mwɔ si. Nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ nɛ a ngɛ Japan ɔ jeɔ mi mi jɔmi kpo, nɛ a peeɔ nɔ nubwɔ wawɛɛ. Su kpakpa nɛ ɔmɛ nɛ a jeɔ kpo ɔ ná ye nɔ he wami nitsɛ. A kɛ a ní tsumi fiɛ we kulaa. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, a peeɔ kake kɛ tsuɔ ní. Ejakaa ngɛ Japanbi a kusumi nya a, ke a ma tsu nɔ́ ko he ní ɔ, a blaa kɛ peeɔ jã ngɛ tue mi jɔmi mi mohu pe nɛ nɔ kake pɛ maa pee jã kɛ hla hɛ mi nyami. Enɛ ɔ haa nɛ a nyɛɔ tsuɔ níhi fuu a he ní. A nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ kpakpa a wo mi he wami nɛ i na kaa e sa nɛ ma je he si bami mi nɛ i kɛ ni kpahi nɛ pee kake kɛ tsu ní. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, e ha nɛ i na kaa e sa nɛ ye bua nɛ jɔ ní nyafinyafihi nɛ i nyɛɔ peeɔ ngɛ Yehowa sɔmɔmi mi ɔ he, ejakaa lɔ ɔ yeɔ buaa nɛ Yehowa yi mi tomi baa mi.

Ligbi nɛ wa sɛ gba si himi mi
<<<P>>>
Be komɛ ɔ, bɔ nɛ nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ nɛ a ngɛ lejɛ ɔ tsuɔ sane komɛ a he ní ha he je ɔ, e yeɔ ha mi kaa ma nu sisi ejakaa e slo bɔ nɛ wa peeɔ wa ní ha. Se i ba kase kaa ke i nui nɔ́ ko sisi po ɔ, e he hia nɛ ma to ye tsui si, nɛ ma ko pee nɔ́ ko nɛ ma gba a nya. Bɔɔbɔɔbɔɔ ɔ, i ba tsake ye susumi, nɛ i ba yɔse kaa Yehowa ma nyɛ maa jɔɔ blɔ tsɔɔmi fɛɛ ko nɛ e we asafo ɔ kɛ ma ha a nɔ ke wa ba wa he si nɛ waa kɛ tsu ní, ejakaa Yehowa pɛ lɛ haa nɛ níhi yeɔ manye.
<<<P>>>
Behi fuu ɔ, i ngɔɔ tsɔsemi nɛ i ná ngɛ Betel ɔ kɛ toɔ tsɔsemi nɛ Mose ná a he. Benɛ Mose tsu ní kaa to hyɛlɔ jeha 40 sɔuu ɔ, Yehowa gu lɔ ɔ nɔ kɛ tsɔse lɛ nɛ e ba pee nɔ ko nɛ e he jɔ. Kaa bɔ nɛ e ji ngɛ Mose blɔ fa mi ɔ, imi hu i ya yunivɛsiti nɛ he biɛ. Yunivɛsiti ɔ nɛ i ya a ha nɛ i woɔ ye he nɔ, nɛ i ngɔɔ ye he kɛ fɔɔ imi nitsɛ ye nɔ. Se Betel nɛ i ya sɔmɔ ngɛ ɔ ye bua mi nɛ i na kaa e sa nɛ ma ba ye he si, nɛ ma ngɔ ye hɛ kɛ fɔ Yehowa nɔ. Kaa bɔ nɛ Yehowa to e tsui si nɛ e tsɔse Mose ɔ, jã nɔuu kɛ lɛ Yehowa pee kɛ ha mi jehahi babauu nɛ be ɔ.
