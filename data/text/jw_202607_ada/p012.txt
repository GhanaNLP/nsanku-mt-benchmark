Wa ngɛ nɔ mi mami kaa bɔ fɛɛ bɔ nɛ ji ɔ, wa maa na wa suɔli nɛ a gbo ɔ ekohu
(Hyɛ kuku 14)
<<<P>>>
ko ha nɛ yayami ko nɛ wa pee kɛ be ɔ nɛ ha nɛ wa kpa Yehowa sɔmɔmi. Ke wa pee jã a, Yehowa maa jɔɔ wɔ, nɛ wa maa hi si kɛ ya neneene.

KE WA SUƆLƆ KO GBO NƐ WA NGƐ AYWILƐHO YEE
<<<P>>>
12 Ke wa suɔlɔ ko gbo ɔ, e dɔɔ wɔ nɛ wa yeɔ aywilɛho wawɛɛ, nɛ be komɛ ɔ, wa nuɔ dɔdɔɛ ɔ he be kɛkɛɛ. Eko ɔ, wa ma bi ke, ‘Mɛni he je nɛ Yehowa ngmɛ blɔ nɛ wa suɔlɔ ɔ gbo ɔ?’ Mo susu nyɛminyumu ko nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Michael, nɛ la sã e fɔli nɛ a gbo ɔ he nɛ o hyɛ. E de ke: “E dɔ mi wawɛɛ nitsɛ, nɛ ye mi mi fu Yehowa kaa e ngmɛ blɔ nɛ ye fɔli ɔmɛ gbo. I le kaa pi lɛ nɛ e gbe mɛ mohu lɛɛ, se e pee we he nɔ́ ko hulɔ.” Eko ɔ, mo hu o nu he kaa bɔ nɛ nyɛminyumu Michael nu he ɔ. Ngɛ si fɔfɔɛ kaa kikɛ mi ɔ, mɛni Yehowa peeɔ kɛ woɔ wa bua, nɛ mɛni he kɛ nɔ fɔmi e haa nɛ wa náa?
<<<P>>>
13 Kane Yesaya 25:8. Yehowa wo si kaa “e maa mi gbenɔ kɛ maa ya neneene.” Mo susu be nɛ gbenɔ be si hie hu ɔ he nɛ o hyɛ! O be aywilɛho yee hu gblegbleegble ngɛ o suɔlɔ ko nɛ e gbo ɔ he. (Yes. 35:10) E be lɛ bae gblee hu kaa o maa na nɛ o suɔlɔ ko hwɔɔ si ngɛ daka mi, nɛ a wo e nɔ kɛ yaa nɛ a yaa pu lɛ. Mohu ɔ, waa kɛ wa suɔli tsuo maa hi si kɛ ya neneene, nɛ wa ma ná nɔmlɔ tso mi he wami kpakpa. Si womi nɛ ɔ pi nyazia ko kɛkɛ, eja-kaa Yehowa nitsɛ lɛ wo si ɔ nɛ, nɛ e si womi ɔ maa ba mi kokooko.—Kpo. 21:4, 5.
