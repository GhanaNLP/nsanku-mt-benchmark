Yehowa Tsɔse Mi Nɛ I Ba Pee Nɔmlɔ Kpakpa
<<<P>>>
KEN KIUCHI NYA MI MUNYU
<<<P>>>
YE HƐ ji kekleekle be nɛ i ya tsu ní ngɛ he nɛ a peeɔ womi ngɛ ngɛ Brooklyn Betel nɛ ngɛ New York, U.S.A ɔ nɔ. Benɛ i sɛ womi peemi he ɔ nɛ i na bɔ nɛ klama amɛ nɛ a kɛ ngɛ ní tsue ɔ ngɛ pɛe wawɛɛ, kɛ bɔ nɛ klama amɛ ngɛ ní tsue ha a, ye nya kpɛ ye he nitsɛ. Womihi a nya nɛ a poo ɔ gbɛ fia kɔɔhiɔ mi tsuo, nɛ nu ɔ nɛ a kɛ wo klama amɛ a mi ɔ hu he fu kpe tsu ɔ mi tsuo.
<<<P>>>
Benɛ i ya Betel ɔ, i na níhi fuu nɛ pee mi nyakpɛ, se nɔ́ ko nɛ ná ye nɔ he wami wawɛɛ ji nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ nɛ a ngɛ ní tsue ngɛ lejɛ ɔ. Nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi nɛ ɔmɛ ngɛ ní tsumi kake ɔ nɔuu tsue daa ligbi. E ngɛ mi kaa ní tsumi nɛ a ngɛ tsue ɔ be hae nɛ a he biɛ mohu lɛɛ, se kɛ̃ ɔ, a ngɛ bua jɔmi wawɛɛ nitsɛ. A nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ ha nɛ i na kaa e sa nɛ i kɛ he si bami nɛ sɔmɔ Mawu.
<<<P>>>
Yehowa kɛ jeha babauu tsɔse mi konɛ ma ba pee nɔmlɔ kpakpa. Se kekleekle ɔ, ha nɛ ma de mo bɔ nɛ i plɛ kɛ hla anɔkuale ɔ se blɔ ha benɛ i ngɛ Buda jami ɔ mi ɔ.
<<<P>>>
I HLA ANƆKUALE MAWU Ɔ SE BLƆ BENƐ I JI JOKUƐ
<<<P>>>
A fɔ mi ngɛ Chicago ngɛ Illinois, U.S.A., nɛ lejɛ ɔ lɛ i hi kɛ wa. I ngɛ senihi etɛ, nɛ imi ji nɔkɔtɔma. Jinɛ Japan lɛ ye fɔli ɔmɛ ngɛ, se akɛnɛ a susu kaa a si himi maa pee kpakpa ngɛ United States he je ɔ, a hia kɛ ba lejɛ ɔ. A suɔ nɛ wɔ, nɛ ji a bimɛ ɔmɛ ɔ waa ya sukuu nɛ se be konɛ waa ye manye ngɛ si himi mi.
<<<P>>>
Ye fɔli ɔmɛ kɛ Buda jami fiɛ we kulaa. Ngɛ ye mami blɔ fa mi po ɔ, we nɛ e wa ngɛ mi ɔ, Buda sɔlemi tsu ngɛ lejɛ ɔ. E papaa kɛ e nyɛminyumu enyɔ ɔmɛ sɔmɔ kaa hɛ mi nyɛɛli ngɛ Buda jami ɔ mi. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, wɔ tsuo ngɛ weku ɔ mi ɔ, waa kɛ Buda jami ɔ fiɛ we kulaa. Benɛ i we ɔ, ke i kpa sukuu kɛ ba a, nɔ́ kekleekle nɛ i peeɔ ji kaa i sãã tsopa kɛ e he via kɛ haa Buda amaga a nɛ ngɛ wa we ɔ mi ɔ, nɛ i haa lɛ niye ní kɛ nyu hulɔ. Daa Hɔgba a, wa yaa Buda jami he ɔ, nɛ wa ya tuɔ munyu pɔtɛɛ komɛ kɛ tsɔɔ amaga a nɛ ngɛ lejɛ ɔ. Amaga nɛ ɔ kle pe nɔ́ nɛ ngɛ wa we ɔ mi ɔ, nɛ a ngɔ sika tsu kɛ ha he.
<<<P>>>
I kai kaa benɛ i ye maa pee jeha kpaago ɔ, i ya da si ngɛ amaga a nɛ e ngɛ wa we ɔ mi ɔ hɛ mi nɛ i bɔni ye yi mi susumi. I bi ye he ke: ‘Mɛni maa ba ye nɔ ke i gbo?’ A tsɔɔ mi ngɛ Buda jami ɔ mi kaa ke i gbo ɔ, ma ya tsa ye si himi ɔ nɔ ngɛ mumi a je. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, i yɔse kaa ma gbo ligbi ko kɛ̃. Se ke i gbo nɛ i ba pee mumi po ɔ, i be nyɛe nɛ ma ye ngɔɔmi ngɛ si himi ɔ mi kaa adesa. Enɛ ɔ he nɛ́ i susuɔ ɔ dɔɔ mi wawɛɛ, nɛ i nuɔ he kaa se nami ko be ye he!
<<<P>>>
