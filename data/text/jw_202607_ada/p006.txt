13 Maa pee jeha 500 ngɛ Matsɛ David nɔ yemi ɔ se ɔ, Baiblo ɔ kpale tu Gibeonbi ɔmɛ kɛ nó ko nɛ ba a nɔ ɔ he munyu ekohu. A nuu Yudabi ɔmɛ kɛ ho Babilon ya jeha 70. Se pee se ɔ, a kpale a se kɛ ba Yerusalem ekohu. Yudabi nɛ a kpale kɛ ba kekleekle ɔ kɛ Amlaalo Zerubabel lɛ ba. (Ezra 2:1, 2, 58) Maa pee jeha 70 se ɔ, Ezra nɛ ji womi ngmalɔ ɔ kɛ Yudabi fuu hu kpale a se kɛ je Babilon kɛ ba Yerusalem. (Ezra 7:1-7) ‘Netinimbi ɔmɛ’ hu piɛɛ kuu enyɔ nɛ ɔmɛ nɛ a kpale kɛ je Babilon kɛ ba a he. (Hyɛ sisi ningma nɛ ngɛ Ezra 2:58 ɔ kɛ 7:7 ɔ he.) Mɛnɔmɛ ji Netinimbi ɔmɛ? Mɛ ji “Sɔlemi we ɔ sɔmɔli” nɛ tsa pi Israelbi ji mɛ ɔ. E ma nyɛ maa ba kaa Netinimbi ɔmɛ a kpɛti nihi fuu ji Gibeonbi ɔmɛ a sisi bimɛ.—Hyɛ Baiblo Mi Munyu Komɛ A Sisi Tsɔɔmi, “Netinim.”

14 Kane 1 Kronika 9:2 kɛ sisi ningma a. “Sɔlemi we ɔ sɔmɔli ɔmɛ” piɛɛ kuu nɛ a kpale a se kɛ ba Yerusalem kekleekle ɔ a he. Enɛ ɔ sa kadimi wawɛɛ, ejakaa tsa pi Yudabi ɔmɛ tsuo nɛ a kpale a se kɛ ba Yerusalem. Yudabi ɔmɛ a kpɛti nihi fuu ma wehi nɛ a ngɛ fɛu ngɛ Babilon, nɛ a ngɛ sika fuu hulɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, a sume nɛ a maa je ní kpakpahi nɛ a ya ná ngɛ Babilon ɔ he konɛ a ya po Yerusalem ma a ekohu. Se Gibeonbi ɔmɛ a sisi bimɛ ɔmɛ kɛ Yudabi komɛ nɛ a yeɔ Yehowa anɔkuale ɔ suɔ nɛ a ya ma sɔlemi we ɔ ekohu kɛ ha Yehowa, nɛ a sɔmɔ lɛ ngɛ lejɛ ɔ. E ngɛ mi kaa Gibeonbi ɔmɛ a sisi bimɛ ɔmɛ be zugba ngɛ Israel, nɛ a be si himi ɔ nae gbɔjɔ ɔ mohu lɛɛ, se kɛ̃ ɔ, a pee klaalo kaa a maa kpale a se konɛ a ya tsu ní tsumihi nɛ a tsuɔ ngɛ sɔlemi we ɔ, nɛ a ye bua kɛ fia Yerusalem gbogbo ɔmɛ ekohu.—Neh. 3:26.

15 Mɛni nɛ sane nɛ ɔ tsɔɔ wɔ ngɛ Yehowa he? Sane nɛ ɔ ha nɛ wa na kaa Yehowa bua jɔɔ nihi nɛ a kɛ anɔkuale yemi sɔmɔɔ lɛ ɔ a he, nɛ e hyɛɔ a nɔ be fɛɛ be. Kɛ je be nɛ Yehowa po Gibeonbi ɔmɛ a he piɛ ngɛ Yoshua be ɔ mi ɔ se ɔ, e ya nɔ nɛ e jɔɔ mɛ jehahi babauu. Benɛ Babilonbi ɔmɛ kpata Yerusalem ma a hɛ mi ɔ, Yehowa po Gibeonbi ɔmɛ a sisi bimɛ ɔmɛ a he piɛ, nɛ benɛ a kpale a se kɛ ba Yerusalem hu ɔ, Yehowa ya nɔ nɛ e kɛ mɛ tsu ní ngɛ sɔlemi we ɔ. E ma nyɛ maa ba kaa hehi nɛ Gibeonbi ɔmɛ a sisi bimɛ ɔmɛ hi ɔ kɛ sɔlemi we ɔ he kɛ we. (Ezra 2:70; Neh. 11:21) Jehanɛ hu ɔ, akɛnɛ a tsuɔ ní ngɛ sɔlemi we ɔ mi he je ɔ, Persia matsɛ ɔ ngmɛ́ blɔ kaa a he tóhi nɛ ma bi ɔmɛ woɔ ɔ ngɛ a dɛ.—Ezra 7:24.
