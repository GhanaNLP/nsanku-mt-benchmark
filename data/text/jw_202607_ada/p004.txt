hwu ta. Gibeonbi ɔmɛ ye gbeye wawɛɛ nitsɛ, enɛ ɔ ha nɛ a ya kpa Yoshua pɛɛ kaa e ba ye bua mɛ. (Yosh. 10:3-7) Yoshua nyɛɛ Israelbi ɔmɛ a hɛ mi kɛ ya Gibeon konɛ a kɛ Kanaanbi ɔmɛ nɛ ya hwu ta a, nɛ Yehowa ye bua mɛ nɛ a ye kunimi. Yehowa ha nɛ gbiɛ ngo ngu nɛ kɛ pue Amor tabuli ɔmɛ a nɔ, nɛ e ha nɛ pu ɔ da si kɛ ya si be nɛ Israelbi ɔmɛ ye a he nyɛli ɔmɛ tsuo a nɔ kunimi. (Yosh. 10:9-14) Gibeonbi ɔmɛ hla kaa a kɛ Yehowa ma nɛ ji Israel ɔ maa so tue mi jɔmi somi. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, a hla yemi kɛ buami kɛ je Yoshua ngɔ benɛ mahi nɛ bɔle mɛ ɔ ba tua mɛ ɔ. Ní nɛ ɔmɛ tsuo nɛ a pee ɔ tsɔɔ kaa a ngɛ hemi kɛ yemi nitsɛnitsɛ ngɛ Yehowa mi kaa e maa po a he piɛ.

⁶ Mɛni nɛ sane nɛ ɔ tsɔɔ wɔ ngɛ Yehowa he? Yehowa baa e he si, nɛ e naa nɔ mɔbɔ. E sɛ hlami momo nɛ e de Israelbi ɔmɛ kaa a “fie nihi nɛ a ngɛ zugba a nɔ ɔ tsuo,” nɛ Gibeonbi ɔmɛ hu piɛɛ he ɔ kɛ je a hɛ mi. (4 Mose 33:51, 52) Se e ngɛ mi kaa benɛ Israelbi ɔmɛ kɛ Gibeonbi ɔmɛ so tue mi jɔmi somi ɔ, a bi we Yehowa blɔ tsɔɔmi mohu lɛɛ, se kɛ̃ ɔ, Yehowa kplɛɛ nɔ nɛ e ha we nɛ a kpata Gibeonbi ɔmɛ a hɛ mi hu. E ye bua Israelbi ɔmɛ nɛ a ye si nɛ a wo ɔ nɔ, nɛ e gu nyakpɛ blɔ nɔ kɛ he Gibeonbi ɔmɛ a yi wami.—Yosh. 9:26; 11:19.

⁷ Mɛni blɔ nɔ wa maa gu kɛ kase Gibeonbi ɔmɛ? E sa nɛ wa ná hemi kɛ yemi nɛ mi wa ngɛ Yehowa mi. Akɛnɛ wa le Yehowa su kpakpa amɛ wawɛɛ pe Gibeonbi ɔmɛ he je ɔ, e sa nɛ lɔ ɔ nɛ ye bua wɔ nɛ waa ngɔ wa hɛ kɛ fɔ Yehowa nɔ kulaa. (La 40:4, 5) Jehanɛ hu ɔ, wa ma nyɛ maa kase Gibeonbi ɔmɛ ke wa bɔ mɔde nɛ wa kplɛɛ ní tsumi nɛ ni kpahi susuɔ kaa e ba si ngɛ Yehowa asafo ɔ mi ɔ nɔ. (Yosh. 9:23, 27) Nyɛminyumu wayo ko nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Luke ɔ
<<<P>>>
tsɔɔ kaa nyɛminyumu ko nɛ e kɛ jehahi babauu sɔmɔ ngɛ Betel ɔ pee enɛ ɔ he nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́, nɛ e hɛ be nɔ jee gblee. Luke tsɔɔ kaa nyɛminyumu nɛ ɔ tsuɔ ní tsumihi nɛ a he hia wawɛɛ ngɛ Betel, se benɛ nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ ngɛ Matsɛ Yemi Asa ko mae ɔ, e hla kaa e maa pee watchman konɛ e kɛ po níhi nɛ a kɛ ngɛ tsu ɔ mae ɔ a he piɛ nyɔ ko mi. Luke de ke: “I ba na kaa he si bami ji su ko nɛ jeɔ kpo ngɛ wa ní peepee mi, se pi he numi ko kɛkɛ nɛ wa náa.” Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ waa ngɔ wa hɛ kɛ fɔ Yehowa nɔ daa ligbi ke waa kɛ haomihi ngɛ kpee, nɛ waa bɔ mɔde nɛ waa ba wa he si, nɛ waa kplɛɛ ní tsumi fɛɛ ní tsumi nɛ a kɛ maa wo wa dɛ ngɛ Yehowa asafo ɔ mi ɔ nɔ. Ke wa pee jã a, lɔ ɔ maa tsɔɔ kaa wa ngɛ hemi kɛ yemi nɛ mi wa ngɛ Yehowa mi.
