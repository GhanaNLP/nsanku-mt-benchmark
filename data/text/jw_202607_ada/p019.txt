tsu ní tsumi nɛ e kɛ wo e dɛ ɔ. Pee se ɔ, Maria susu nó nɛ ya nɔ ngɛ e si himi mi, kɛ nó nɛ lɔ ɔ tsɔɔ lɛ ngɛ Yehowa he ɔ he. Enɛ ɔ ha nɛ e na heii kaa Yehowa ngɛ he wami, e kɛ níhi hiɔ si ngɛ mi mi jɔmi mi, nɛ e susuɔ níhi nɛ ni kpahi naa mɛ kaa a ba si ɔ a he. (Luka 1:46-55) Kaa bɔ nɛ Maria bua jɔ nó nɛ Yehowa pee kɛ ha lɛ ɔ he, nɛ e je hɛsa kpo ɔ, jã kɛ̃ nɛ ke Yehowa ye bua wɔ “ngɛ be nɛ sa mi” ɔ, wɔ hu wa bua jɔɔ nɛ wa jeɔ hɛsa kpo. (Heb. 4:16) Ke o nine su yemi kɛ buami ko kaa kikɛ nɔ ɔ, mo bi o he ke: ‘Mɛni enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ mi ngɛ Yehowa he? Mɛni Yehowa suhi lɛ jeɔ kpo ngɛ nó nɛ e pee kɛ ha mi nɛ ɔ mi?’

WA MAA YA NƆ MAA KASE YEHOWA HE NÍ DAA
<<<P>>>
17 Ngɛ je ehe ɔ mi ɔ, wa maa ya nɔ maa kase Yehowa he ní fuu. ngɛ jamɛ a be ɔ mi ɔ, Yehowa ma ha wɔ womi kpo ehehi nɛ maa tsɔɔ wɔ níhi nɛ e suɔ nɛ waa pee. (Kpo. 20:12) Wa ma kpa adebɔ níhi a si saminya, nɛ wa maa kase níhi fuu kɛ je a mi. (Yes. 11:6-9) Jehanɛ se hu ɔ, ke Yehowa tle wa huɛmɛ kɛ wa wekuli si kɛ ba wami mi ekohu, nɛ wa na bɔ nɛ e ngɛ wɔ ye buae ha bɔɔbɔɔbɔɔ konɛ waa ye mluku ɔ, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ wa maa le Yehowa saminya ngɛ blɔ ehehi a nɔ.
<<<P>>>
18 Yehowa he ní nɛ wa maa kase ɔ nya be tae gblee, ejakaa e ngɛ kɛ je neneene. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, “e nó sisi numi ɔ hí kalemi.” (La 90:2; 147:5) Jehanɛ se hu ɔ, “ní nɛ anɔkuale Mawu ɔ tsu kɛ je sisije kɛ ya si nyagbe ɔ, adesa be nyɛe maa le kɔkɔɔkɔ.” (Fiɛɛ. 3:11) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, wa se kɛ ngɛ anɔkuale ɔ mi jio, wa se kɛ we jio, nyɛ ha nɛ wɔ tsuo waa ngɔ he blɔ fɛɛ he blɔ nɛ wa ma ná a kɛ kase níhi fuu kɛ kɔ wa hiɔwe Tsɛ nɛ ngɛ suɔmi ɔ he.

FONI ƆMƐ A MI TSƆƆMI
Womi ɔ hɛ mi foni: Ebedmelek nɛ e pee kã nɛ e ngɛ Yeremia nɛ a nu lɛ kɛ wo vu ko mi ɔ jee. Bafa 18: Nyɛminyumu ko nɛ e ngɛ he susue bɔ nɛ Yehowa ye bua lɛ ha nɛ e nyɛ tsu ní peemi ko nɛ a kɛ wo e dɛ ngɛ otsi mi kpe ɔ mi ɔ he.
