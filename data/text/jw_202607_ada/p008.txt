Moo Bu Ye Sɔlemi Tue
<<<P>>>
OTI NƐ NGƐ NÍ KASEMI Ɔ MI
<<<P>>>
Ke wa kane Yesaya womi ɔ nɛ wa pue wa yi mi tɛ ngɛ he ɔ, e ma nyɛ maa wo wa bua.
<<<P>>>
Yesaya Womi ɔ Ma Nyɛ Maa Wo O Bua
<<<P>>>
“Nɔ́ nɛ Yehowa ngɛ dee ji nɛ ɔ nɛ: . . . ’Kaa bɔ nɛ binyɛ woɔ e binyumu bua a, jã nɛ ma ya nɔ nɛ ma wo nyɛ bua.’ ”—YES. 66:12, 13.
<<<P>>>
MOO po jokuɛ ko nɛ e hao wawɛɛ nɛ e ngɛ ya foe ɔ he foni nɛ o hyɛ. Eko ɔ, e ngɛ ya foe akɛnɛ a weku ɔ ngɛ hiae kɛ yaa he kpa, nɛ e hɛ ma ja ngɛ e huɛmɛ ɔmɛ a he ɔ he je. Aloo eko ɔ, e kɔni mi jɔ̃ akɛnɛ e nyɛ we nɛ e pee nɔ́ ko saminya a he je. Ngɛ si fɔfɔɛ kaa kikɛ nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, mɛni lɛ binyɛ ko nɛ e suɔ e bi ɔ maa pee kɛ ye bua lɛ? E ngɛ heii kaa binyɛ ɔ maa fua e bi ɔ, e maa bu lɛ tue, nɛ e ma kai lɛ kaa e suɔ lɛ.
<<<P>>>
² Ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi ɔ, Yehowa ngɔ e he kɛ to binyɛ ko nɛ e suɔ e bi, nɛ e woɔ e bua a he. E de ke: “Kaa bɔ nɛ binyɛ woɔ e binyumu bua a, jã nɛ ma ya nɔ nɛ ma wo nyɛ bua.” (Yes. 66:13) Suɔmi nɛ Yehowa ngɛ kɛ ha wɔ ɔ nɔ kuɔ kulaa pe suɔmi nɛ binyɛ ko ngɛ kɛ ha e bi. (Yes. 49:15) Yehowa guɔ e Munyu nɛ ji Baiblo ɔ nɔ kɛ woɔ wa bua ke wa hao loo wa ngɛ aywilɛho yee.—Rom. 15:4.
<<<P>>>
³ Yehowa ha nɛ a ngma munyuhi nɛ woɔ nɔ bua fuu kɛ wo Yesaya womi ɔ mi. (Yes. 40:1) Yehowa ha nɛ Yesaya ngma womi nɛ ɔ be mi nɛ e we bi nɛ ji Yudabi ɔmɛ kɛ haomihi nɛ a mi wa ngɛ kpee ɔ konɛ e kɛ wo a bua. Wɔ hu wa ngɛ ‘behi nɛ a mi wa, nɛ a nya dami yee’ ɔ mi. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, wa hia bua womi. (2 Tim. 3:1) Nyɛ ha nɛ wa susu ngmami komɛ nɛ ngɛ Yesaya womi ɔ mi, nɛ maa wo wa bua ke; (1) wa si fɔfɔɛ tsake tlukaa, (2) ke wa he nile buɔ wɔ fɔ ngɛ yayami ko nɛ wa pee kɛ be ɔ he, kɛ (3) ke wa suɔlɔ ko gbo nɛ wa ngɛ aywilɛho yee ɔ he.
<<<P>>>
1-2. Ke wa hao loo wa ngɛ aywilɛho yee ɔ, mɛni Yehowa peeɔ kɛ yeɔ buaa wɔ?
<<<P>>>
3. Mɛni he wa ma susu ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi?
