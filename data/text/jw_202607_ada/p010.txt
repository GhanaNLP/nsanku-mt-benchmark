6 Joseph nɛ wa wo e ta kɛ sɛ hlami ɔ tsɔɔ bɔ nɛ Yehowa ye bua e kɛ e yo ɔ ha benɛ a je Betel ɔ. E de ke: “Wa na kaa Yehowa je suɔmi kpo kɛ tsɔɔ wɔ, nɛ e ye bua wɔ wawɛɛ benɛ tsakemi ɔ ba a. E gu nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ a nɔ kɛ ye bua wɔ. E ye bua wɔ nɛ wa nine su wa hiami níhi kɛ bua womi nɔ ngɛ be nya, nɛ loloolo ɔ, e ngɛ jã a pee.” Ke wɔ hu wa si fɔfɔɛ tsake tlukaa po ɔ, wa ma nyɛ ma ná nɔ mi mami kaa Yehowa maa wo wɔ he wami, nɛ e ma ha wɔ blɔ tsɔɔmi nɛ wa hia kɛ tsakemi ɔ he wa kaa mɛni po. (Yes. 54:10; 58:11) Joseph tsɔɔ hu kaa nɔ́ kpa ko nɛ ye bua lɛ ji Brɔɔdkastin la a eko. La a mi munyu ɔ ha nɛ e ná nɔ mi mami kaa si fɔfɔɛ fɛɛ ko nɛ e ma ya je mi ɔ, Yehowa maa je ngmlaa kɛ ha lɛ, nɛ e maa ye bua lɛ.* Ke níhi yɛ nɔ kaa bɔ nɛ wa ngɛ blɔ hyɛɛ ɔ po ɔ, wa le kaa bɔ fɛɛ bɔ nɛ ji ɔ, Yehowa maa ye bua wɔ.

7. Kɛ nihi nɛ a pee yayami kɛ be ɔ ma nyɛ maa nu he ha kɛɛ be komɛ?
<<<P>>>
behi fuu ɔ, ke Yehowa kɛ wa yayami ɔ ke wɔ po ɔ, wa he nile yaa nɔ nɛ e buɔ wɔ fɔ. Eko ɔ, wa ma susu ke, ‘Anɛ Yehowa kɛ yayami nɛ i pee nɛ ɔ maa ke mi niinɛ lo?’ (2 Kor. 2:7) Tɔmi be he kaa nɔ ko maa nu he jã, ejakaa e he waa kɛ haa wɔ kaa wa hɛ maa je yayami ko nɛ wa pee kɛ be ɔ nɔ. Enɛ ɔ ma nyɛ ma ha nɛ waa hao be kɛkɛɛ. Matsɛ David de ke: “Ye yayami ɔ he je ɔ, i be tue mi jɔmi. . . Ye nɔmlɔ tso ɔ nui nó he hu, nɛ i kuɔ kulaa.” (La 38:3, 8) Ke yayami ko nɛ wa pee kɛ be ɔ ha nɛ wa ngɛ he nue jã a, lɛɛ Yesaya womi ɔ ma nyɛ maa wo wa bua.

8 Kane Yesaya 1:18. Yehowa ngɔ nɔ́ he tomi nɔ́ ko kɛ tsu ní kɛ tsɔɔ bɔ nɛ e kɛ tɔmi keɔ kulaa ha. E de kaa, ke wa “yayami ɔmɛ tsuɔ tsutsuutsu” kaa mɛni po, e ma ha nɛ a ma “hiɛ futaa kaa snoo.” E ngɛ mi kaa ke nɔ́ tsutsuutsu ko sɛ tade futaa ko mi ɔ, e jemi yeɔ wawɛɛ mohu lɛɛ, se Yehowa wo si kaa ke wa tɔmi ɔ tsuɔ kaa mɛni po ɔ, e ma ha nɛ e ma hiɛ futaa, nɛ tsɔɔ kaa ke e kɛ jamɛ a tɔmi ɔ ke wɔ ɔ, e be kaie hu gblegbleegble.

9 Mo susu Matsɛ David nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ he nɛ o hyɛ. E ngɛ mi kaa e puɛ gba, nɛ e gbe nɔmlɔ mohu lɛɛ, se Yehowa kɛ e tɔmi ɔ ke lɛ, nɛ pee se ɔ, e tsɛ lɛ ke nyumu nɛ yeɔ anɔkuale. (2 Sam. 11:3, 4, 14, 15; 12:13; 1 Ma. 9:4, 5) Benɛ Yehowa ngɛ David he munyu tue ɔ, e tu anɔkuale nɛ David ye lɛ ɔ he munyu, se pi tɔmihi nɛ e tɔ̃ ɔ. Jã nɔuu kɛ̃ nɛ ke wa tsake wa tsui ɔ, Yehowa maa je suɔmi mi nɛ e kɛ wa tɔmi ɔ maa ke wɔ kulaa, nɛ e maa ye bua wɔ konɛ waa yaa nɔ nɛ waa kɛ bua jɔmi nɛ sɔmɔ lɛ. Mɛni ji nɔ́ kpa ko nɛ wa maa na ngɛ Yesaya womi ɔ mi, nɛ maa ye bua wɔ ke wa he nile ya nɔ nɛ e ngɛ wɔ fɔ bue?
