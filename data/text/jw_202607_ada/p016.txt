8 Ke wa ba ngɛ Yehowa lee saminya a, lɔ ɔ haa nɛ huɛ bɔmi nɛ ngɛ waa kɛ lɛ wa kpɛti ɔ mi waa. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, ke o kane Baiblo ɔ fã ko, o hyɛ brɔɔdkastin ɔ nɛ baa daa nyɔhiɔ ɔ eko, aloo o ya kpɔ mi kpe loo kpokpa nɔ kpe ko ɔ, kɛ o nuɔ he ha kɛɛ? Behi fuu ɔ, wa nuɔ he kaa wa hɛ kɛ su wa hiɔwe Tsɛ ɔ he wawɛɛ nitsɛ. Mɛni he je? Ejakaa wa kase níhi fuu kɛ kɔ e he. Enɛ ɔ haa nɛ wa jeɔ Yehowa yi, nɛ wa naa lɛ si. Wa nuɔ he kaa bɔ nɛ la polɔ ɔ nu he be nɛ e susu bɔ nɛ Yehowa suɔ lɛ ha a he ɔ. E de ke: “Mawu ji tɛsa kɛ ha ye tsui, kɛ ye blɔ fa mi nɔ́ kɛ yaa neneene.” (Kane La 73:24-28.) Be fɛɛ be nɛ wa maa bɔ mɔde kaa wa maa le Yehowa saminya a, lɔ ɔ haa nɛ wa bua jɔɔ wawɛɛ kaa e ji wa huɛ nɛ se be.

9 Baiblo ɔ wo wɔ he wami kaa ‘wa kai’ Yehowa be fɛɛ be. (Abɛ 3:5, 6) Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa e sa nɛ wa susu níhi nɛ wa kaseɔ kɛ kɔ Yehowa he ɔ he, né jehanɛ hu wa susu bɔ nɛ wa ní peepee haa nɛ e nuɔ he ha a he. Jã nɛ wa maa pee ɔ ma ha nɛ waa mwɔ yi mi kpɔhi nɛ da. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi fuu ngɔɔ a hunomɛ loo a yi ɔmɛ a foni kɛ fɔɔ he nɛ a ma nyɛ maa na be fɛɛ be. Enɛ ɔ yeɔ buaa mɛ nɛ a susuɔ a hunomɛ loo a yi ɔmɛ a he be fɛɛ be, nɛ e haa nɛ a mwɔɔ yi mi kpɔhi nɛ ma ha nɛ a huno ɔmɛ loo a yi ɔmɛ a bua maa jɔ. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, a bɔɔ mɔde kaa a be nɔ́ ko pee nɛ ma gba a sibi nya. Jã nɔuu kɛ̃ nɛ ke wa suɔ Mawu nɛ wa suɔ lɛ ɔ he be fɛɛ be ɔ, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ wa maa mwɔ yi mi kpɔhi nɛ ma ha nɛ e bua maa jɔ.—La 19:13, 14.

10 Yehowa bɔ wɔ ngɛ e subai nɔ, nɛ enɛ ɔ yeɔ buaa wɔ nɛ wa jeɔ e su ɔmɛ kpo. (1 Mose 1:26) Ke wa ya nɔ nɛ wa kase Yehowa su ɔmɛ a he ní ɔ, e be yee ha wɔ kaa wa maa kase lɛ. Yesu kã Farisi bi ɔmɛ kɛ Saduki bi ɔmɛ a hɛ mi, ejakaa a je we Mawu su kpakpa amɛ kpo. E ngɛ mi kaa a le Mlaa a saminya mohu lɛɛ, se Yesu de ke a “bui níhi nɛ a he hia ngɛ Mlaa a mi ɔ. Ní nɛ ɔmɛ ji dami sane yemi, mɔbɔ nami, kɛ anɔkuale yemi.” (Mat. 23:23) Wa sume nɛ waa pee wa ní kaa mɛ, mohu ɔ, wa suɔ nɛ waa na bɔ nɛ Yehowa su kpakpa amɛ jeɔ kpo ngɛ mlaahi kɛ sisi tomi mlaahi nɛ wa kaseɔ ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi ɔ mi ha. Jã nɛ wa maa pee ɔ maa ye bua wɔ konɛ wa je Yehowa su kpakpa amɛ kpo.—1 Yoh. 4:8, 11.

11 Kɛ o plɛ kɛ ba na anɔkuale ɔ ha kɛɛ? Eko ɔ, o fɔli ɔmɛ lɛ a tsɔɔ mo Yehowa he ní. E ma nyɛ maa ba kaa a kɛ mo sɛɛ Baiblo mi sanehi a he ní, aloo a de mo bɔ nɛ níhi nɛ Yehowa bɔ ɔ jeɔ e su kpakpa amɛ kpo ha. Aloo eko ɔ, Yehowa Odasefo ɔmɛ a kpɛti nɔ kake lɛ e kɛ mo kase Baiblo ɔ.
