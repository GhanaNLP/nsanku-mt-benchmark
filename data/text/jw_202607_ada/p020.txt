Nyɛɛ Tsɔɔ Mɛ Ní Konɛ A Fĩ Si Daa
<<<P>>>
OTI NƐ NGƐ NÍ KASEMI Ɔ MI
Blɔ nɔ nɛ o maa gu kɛ ye bua o Baiblo kaselɔ ɔ konɛ e le Yehowa saminya, nɛ jehanɛ se hu, e le suhi nɛ Yehowa ngɛ kɛ níhi nɛ e suɔ kɛ níhi nɛ e sume.
<<<P>>>
Moo Ye Bua Ni Kpahi Nɛ A Ba Le Yehowa
<<<P>>>
“Ke nɔ ko ma ná neneene wami ɔ, e he hia nɛ e ya nɔ nɛ e le mo anɔkuale Mawu kake pɛ ɔ.”—YOH. 17:3.
<<<P>>>
KE O na nɛ o Baiblo kaselɔ ko bɔni asafo mi kpe bami, nɛ pee se ɔ, e bɔni sane heto hami, nɛ lɔ ɔ se ɔ, e bɔni ni kpahi níhi nɛ e kase ɔ de mi, nɛ bɔɔbɔɔbɔɔ ɔ, e jɔɔ e wami nɔ kɛ ha Yehowa nɛ a baptisi lɛ ɔ, kɛ o nuɔ he ha kɛɛ? E ngɛ heii kaa o bua jɔɔ saminya! Ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ, wa le kaa Yehowa ji nɔ nɛ ye kɛ bua o Baiblo kaselɔ ɔ nɛ e nyɛ nɛ e pee ní nɛ ɔmɛ ɔ nɛ, enɛ ɔ he ɔ, wa naa Yehowa si wawɛɛ nɛ wa jeɔ e yi.—1 Kor. 3:5-9; 3 Yoh. 3, 4.
<<<P>>>
2 Yehowa suɔ kaa “a he nimli slɔɔtohi tsuo a yi wami nɛ a ba ná anɔkuale ɔ he nile nitsɛnitsɛ ɔ.” (Kane 1 Timoteo 2:3, 4) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, Yehowa suɔ nɛ nihi tsuo nɛ a ba le lɛ, nɛ a le yi mi nɛ e to kɛ ha adesahi ɔ he ní saminya. (Yer. 9:24) Loko nɔ ko ma nyɛ maa pe jã a, e sa nɛ e ba ná anɔkuale ɔ he nile nitsɛnitsɛ. Mɛni ji “nile nitsɛnitsɛ ɔ?” E tsɔɔ we kaa nɔ ko maa le níhi fuu kɛ kɔ Mawu he pɛ kɛkɛ. Baiblo he ní lelɔ ko de ke, nɔ ko nɛ e ngɛ nile nitsɛnitsɛ ɔ ji nɔ ko nɛ e bɔɔ mɔde kaa e ma ha nɛ nó nɛ e ngɛ kasee ɔ nɛ ná e nɔ he wami konɛ e hɛ kɛ su Mawu he. Ke Baiblo kaselɔ ko ba le Yehowa saminya a, jamɛ a nile ɔ haa nɛ e tsakeɔ e ní peepee, nɛ lɔ ɔ bliɔ blɔ kɛ haa lɛ konɛ e ná “neneene wami.”—Yoh. 17:3.
<<<P>>>
3 Yehowa ha wɔ he blɔ kaa waa ye bua ni kpahi nɛ a ba le lɛ saminya. Ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, wa ma susu sane bimi nɛ ɔmɛ a he: Mɛni he je nɛ e sa nɛ Baiblo kaseli nɛ a le Yehowa saminya a? Kɛ wa ma plɛ kɛ tsɔɔ mɛ bɔ nɛ Yehowa ngɛ ha kɛɛ? Mɛni blɔ nɔ wa maa gu kɛ ye bua mɛ konɛ a ngɔ nó nɛ a ngɛ kasee ngɛ Yehowa he ɔ kɛ tsu ní ngɛ a si himi mi?
<<<P>>>
1. Ke o Baiblo kaselɔ ko ba bɔni Yehowa sɔmɔmi ɔ, kɛ o nuɔ he ha kɛɛ?
<<<P>>>
2. Mɛni Yehowa suɔ kaa e maa pee kɛ ha Baiblo kaseli? (1 Timoteo 2:3, 4)
<<<P>>>
3. Mɛni he wa ma susu ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi?
