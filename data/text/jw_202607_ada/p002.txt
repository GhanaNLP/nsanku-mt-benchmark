OTI NƐ NGƐ NÍ KASEMI Ɔ MI
Wa ma susu níhi nɛ wa ma nyɛ maa kase kɛ je Gibeonbi ɔmɛ a ngɔ, kɛ bɔ nɛ Yehowa kɛ mɛ ye ha a he.
<<<P>>>
Moo Kase Gibeonbi Ɔmɛ
<<<P>>>
“Nihi nɛ a ngɛ Gibeon ɔ kɛ Israel so tue mi jɔmi somi, nɛ a ngɛ a kpɛti.”—YOSH. 10:1.
<<<P>>>
JEHA 40 se benɛ Israelbi ɔmɛ je Egipt nɛ a ya su Si Womi Zugba a nɔ ɔ, Yehowa ye bua mɛ nɛ a kɛ Yeriko kɛ Ai ma a hwu ta, nɛ a ye a nɔ kunimi. Se pee se ɔ, nɔ́ ko nɛ a hyɛ we blɔ ɔ ya nɔ. Nihi nɛ a ngɛ Kanaan zugba kpahi a nɔ ɔ, nu nɔ́ nɛ Isrealbi ɔmɛ pee ɔ he, nɛ a kpɛti ni komɛ hia blɔ kɛ ba Israelbi ɔmɛ a sito he ɔ. A tsɔɔ kaa a je he tsitsaa, nɛ a ba konɛ a kɛ Israelbi ɔmɛ nɛ ba so tue mi jɔmi somi.
<<<P>>>
2 Nimli nɛ ɔmɛ ji Gibeonbi. Enɛ ɔ ji kekleekle be nɛ a wo Gibeonbi ɔmɛ a ta ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi, nɛ pee se ɔ, a kpale tu a he munyu ekohu. Baiblo ɔ ha nɛ wa na kaa Gibeonbi ɔmɛ kɛ Israelbi ɔmɛ bla kɛ hi si jehahi babauu. Su kpakpahi fuu ngɛ nɛ wa ma nyɛ maa kase kɛ je Gibeonbi ɔmɛ a he. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, bɔ nɛ Yehowa tsu nɔ́ nɛ ba a nɔ ɔ he ní ha a maa ye bua wɔ nɛ waa kase ní kpakpahi fuu ngɛ Yehowa he.
<<<P>>>
MO JE HEMI KƐ YEMI KPO NƐ O BA O HE SI
<<<P>>>
3 Benɛ Israelbi ɔmɛ ya Si Womi Zugba a nɔ ɔ, e he ba hia nɛ a kpata mahi kpaago komɛ nɛ a ngɛ Kanaan zugba a nɔ ɔ a hɛ mi. Nihi nɛ a ngɛ ma nɛ ɔmɛ a mi ɔ a he hiɛ kulaa pe Israelbi ɔmɛ, nɛ a ngɛ tabuli babauu pe Israelbi ɔmɛ hulɔ. (5 Mose 7:1) Ma kpaago nɛ ɔmɛ a kpɛti kake ji Hiv ma a. Mahi komɛ nɛ a bla lɛ peeɔ Hiv ma a nɛ, nɛ Gibeon ji ma nɛ ɔmɛ a kpɛti kake. Se Gibeon ma a lɛɛ, a fia gbogbo kɛ wo he, nɛ a ngɛ tabuli babauu hulɔ.
