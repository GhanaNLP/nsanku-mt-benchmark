Michiko Oda ne ohũ yeɛ mene bɛ gyi berɛ ne
<<<P>>>
ne menwu ye kyɛ mafɛ. Afei koso me ngome yeɛ ne nnate nu ɔ, na né m’adwene yɛ me kyɛ nzue ne baatɔ, ofikyɛ ne wamuna. Nakoso, ne mesa mebisa menwo kyɛ, ‘Yeti mbɔno anaa?’ Ne awuro ne te aborɔsaen, yeti nikyeebie yɛle me kyɛ mvo ngɔ awuro dɔ. Mme-rɛ bɔ ndwuli bɔ ɔtɔ so nza naso ne, nyiale biaa bie, na ne ofi Philippines. Ngane ye wɔ metianu kyɛ: ‘Kyi-re ma ngɔ m’atee, ofikyɛ biaa he de, ongotie.’ Nako-so, sãa ne ɔtte sɔ koraa! Mene ye suane nikye, na siɛ ye, ɔɔyɛle nniema Yehowa Adanzeniɛ.

NZUANE SUBAEN PÁ BIEMƆ WƆ BROOKLYN BƐTƐL
<<<P>>>
Mene Wilson tenane afoɛ nyɔ, na bɛtole bɛsa bɛfrɛle me kyɛ mmra Brooklyn Bɛtɛl wɔ 1985 nu. Kyɛ-bɔ nnimoa ngane ne, ne meyɛ adwuma wɔ nekaa bɔ bɛkekã ngrataa bɛbɔnu maa ɔbayɛ nwomaa ne. Ɛlɛho bie bɔ ne meyɛ adwuma ne, mannwu bɔ ɔyɛle ɔ, mesa oomiane ngaen ne nekabie. Ɔwale sɔ ne, ɔp-rentele nwomaa ne si dwirɛ ne pee mbaen bɔ ne bɛn-hia ɔ. Aliemaamɔ bɔ mene bɛ wɔ berɛ ne, bɛhane kyɛ mmɔ aliemaa bɔ ɔla yɛnoa ne amaneɛ. Yeti ndworɔ-le krataa ngɔmane ye, na ngyirekyirele bɔ ne wasi ne mukoraati nu. Nakoso nikyee kɔ̃ bie wɔ berɛ bɔ mma-nyɛ ɔ. Yeɛ ole kyɛ maamba kyerɛ. Yeti bɔ ɔla yɛnoa ne frɛle me, na ɔtole yeboase ɔne me dwudwole. Na ɔha-hyirele me kyɛ: “Sɛ ɛyɛ nikyee na anyɛ ye yé a, ɔte pá kyɛ ɛkɔpa kyerɛ.” Ofi sɔ mmerɛ ne, masua kyɛ sɛ medi mvomsoɛ a, ngɔpa kyerɛ.
<<<P>>>
Aliemaamɔ bɔ bayɛ mbanyi bɔ ne bɛwɔ Bɛtɛl berɛ ne, bɛ koso nzuane nikyee foforɛ bie mvili bɛ berɛ. Ɛhene yeɛ ole kyɛbɔ ngɔsõ nniemamɔ ɔ. Ɛlɛho eyia bie, ne yɛbaadidi, na ne Aliemaa Milton Henschel bɔ ɔte Akwangyerɛ Kue nanuniɛ ne te yɛnwo berɛ. Ɛlɛho sɔ ne, ne mmenia bɔ bɛbaadidi ne sone paa, yeti ne wamaa aleɛ ne hyɛlɛ ne, ɔngɔ ndɛndɛ. Ɔwale sɔ ne, mmrandeɛ bɔ ne yɛte sɔ table nanwo ne, ne yedwu-dwo-dwudwo. Afei komapɛ yɛkwaanea ɔ, Aliemaa Henschel yeɛ wadwɔso bɔ ɔboka maa bɛkyekyɛ aleɛ bɛma aliemaamɔ ye. Sɔ anwobrɛaseɛ bɔ ɔlale ye ali ne, me wora ngofi ye lle, na osa kaele me kyɛbɔ Yesu nwunzini ye asomafoɛ nemɔ gyabo ne.—Yoh. 13:3-5.
