BOKA WƆ SUANIƐ NE
MAA ONWU YEHOWA YÉ
<<<P>>>
4 Yesu hane kyɛ mmraa bɔ ɔte piri paa yeɛ ole kyɛ, odikyɛ yɛfa y’ahone mukoraati, yɛ kra mukoraati, y’adwene mukoraati, ɔne y’anwoserɛ mukoraati yɛdɔ Yehowa. (Kenga Marko 12:30.) Sɛ yɛ Bible suaniɛ bie konya sɔ ɔdɔ ne kɔma Yehowa a, sana odimoa nwu ye. Bɔ yekuro yeɛ ole kyɛ, yɛ Bible asuafoɛ nemɔ konya ɔdɔ kɔma Yehowa, na ɛhene amaa babɔ asu. Na mmom nna kyɛ bɛnye gye asafo nanwo mbaen, anaa bɛpena kyɛ yɛne aliemaamɔ nye gye nati yeɛ bɛbɔ asu ɔ. Yeti sɛ yɛne ebie sua nikyee a, bɔ yenwo hia yɛ paa yeɛ ole kyɛ yɛkɔboka ye maa okonwu Yehowa yé, na wanya ɔdɔ wama ye.
<<<P>>>
5 Sɛ yɛdɔ ebie a, ɔnyɛ se kyɛ yekotie ye dwirɛ ɔ. Yeti wɔ Bible suaniɛ ne koso, sɛ onwu Yehowa yé, na onya ɔdɔ pirikua ma ye a, ɛhene yeɛ kɔmaa okotie ye dwirɛ ɔ, na ɔngɔhwe yenwo ngofi yenwo lle. (1 Yoh. 5:3) Sɔ yeɛ Yesu yɛle ɔ, yeti ɔhane kyɛ: “Kyɛbɔ Baba ne fale ahyɛdeɛ mane me ne, sɔ ala yeɛ meyɔ ɔ, amaa ɔnate so ewiase anwu kyɛ mekuro Baba ne dwirɛ.”—Yoh. 14:31.
<<<P>>>
6 Sɛ yɛ Bible asuafoɛ nemɔ nwu Yehowa yé a, ɔkɔmaa bekosi gyinayɛɛ bɔ yenye kɔgye yenwo ɔ. Wɔde ma yɛfa sɔ mvandoho he yehyire nu. Sɛ ebie pena kyɛ osua kyɛbɔ betwi kar a, ebiaa ɔkɔmaa ebie kohyire ye, anaa ɔkɔhɔ dravin sukuu po. Nakoso nna ɛhene ngome

4. Sɛ yɛne ebie sua nikyee a, nzu yeɛ odikyɛ yɛmaa yenwo hia yɛ paa ɔ? (Marko 12:30)
<<<P>>>
5. Sɛ wɔ Bible suaniɛ ne nya ɔdɔ pirikua paa ma Yehowa a, nzu yeɛ ɔkɔyɛ ɔ?
<<<P>>>
6. Sɛ wɔ Bible suaniɛ nwu Yehowa yé a, sɛɛ yeɛ ɔkɔboka ye maa okosi gyinayɛɛ pá ɔ? Ma yenwo mvandoho.
<<<P>>>
yeɛ ɔkɔmaa okonwu ye ɔ, na mmom sana ye bɔbɔ deinn ɔhyɛ yebo kyɛ otwi kar ne bie. Sɔ ala yeɛ yɛne ebie sua nikyee a, ɔte ɔ. Ɔmaa sona ne nya Yehowa nwo nimdeɛ. Nakoso sɛ ɔfa bɔ osua ne bɔ ye bra a, ɛhene yeɛ ɔmaa ɔbanwu ye yé paa ɔ. Ofikyɛ ye bɔbɔ deinn onwu ye kyɛ sɔ nikyee bɔ ɔyɛ ne, ɔmaa nningyein kɔ yé ma ye. Yeti mmerɛ bɔ ogu so osua nikye, na ɔnea kyɛbɔ ye Baba bɔ ɔwɔ anwuro ne boka ye ne, ɔkɔmaa ɔkɔyere yenwo kyɛ okosi gyinayɛɛ bɔ yenye gye yenwo ɔ.
<<<P>>>
7 Ne Yosef se Yehowa paa. Ɛhene ati yeɛ mmerɛ bɔ ne Pɔtifa aye ne pena kyɛ ɔne ye da ne, wambene ne. Ɔkɔyɛ kyɛ ye ngwalaa nu ne, bɛha behyirele ye kyɛ Yehowa bɔle Adam ne, ɔfale Hawa mane ye kyɛ ɔye. Afei bɔ osili mmerɛ bɔ ebie lɛle Abraham aye Sara ne koso, ɔkɔyɛ kyɛ bɛhane yenwo dwirɛ behyirele ye. (Gye. 2:24; 20:3, 6) Ɛhene maa onwuni kyɛ Yehowa nwu agyaa kyɛ yenwo hia, na sɛ egyalle ebie a, onguro kyɛ ɛne ye kɔla. Yeti ɔha hyirele Pɔtifa aye ne kyɛ: “Nzu ati yeɛ nyɔ sɔ amumuyɛɛ piri ye na nyɔ bɔne ndia Nyameɛ ɔ?” (Gye. 39:7-9) Ne Yosef se bɔ ɔyɛ a, ɔkɔmaa Yehowa nye kɔgye, ɔne bɔ ɔkɔmaa ye wora kɔbɔ ɔ. Yeti ne onguro kyɛ ɔkɔmaa ye wora kɔbɔ, na ɛhene yeɛ ɔbokale ye maa ɔyɛle bɔ Yehowa kuro ne. Sɔ ala yeɛ yɛ Bible asuafoɛ nemɔ koso, sɛ yɛboka bɛ maa benwu Yehowa yé a, ɔkɔmaa bekɔyɛ bɔ okuro ɔ.
