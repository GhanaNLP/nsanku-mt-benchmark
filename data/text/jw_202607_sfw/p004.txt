⁵ Mmerɛ bɔ Amorifoɛ ahemvo nnu tele kyɛ Gibeonfoɛ nemɔ aafa bɛnwo abɔ Yisraelfoɛ ne, wanyɛ bɛ fɛ koraa. Yeti bɛhane bɛnwo bɛbɔle nu kyɛ bɛne Gibeonfoɛ nemɔ kwaahɔ. Ɔwale sɔ ne, esuronyi hane bɛ, yeti bɛnwati bɛɛtoale Yosua kyɛ ɔɔboka bɛ. (Yos. 10:3-7) Ye koso otiele, na olili Yisrael sogyafoɛ nemɔ nyunu maa bɛhɔle Gibeon. Yehowa koso bokale bɛ maa belili Amorifoɛ nemɔ so ngunim. Ɔmaa asukɔtwea mbiri-mbiri tɔ guale bɛ so, na eyia ne koso, ɔmaa ogyinane nekahorɛ toonn oodwuli kyɛ behuni bɛmukoraati ɔ. (Yos. 10:9-14) Ne Gibeonfoɛ nemɔ dé di paa kyɛ Yehowa kole bɛ, na ɛbɔ biala bɔ wahyɛ Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ koso, ɔkɔwa nu. Ɛhene yee ɔmaa bɛfale bɛnwo bɛɛbɔle bɛ, na besa bɛsrɛle bɛ kyɛ bɛboka bɛ, mmerɛ bɔ ne bɛnwo ahyere bɛ ne.

⁶ Bɔ sɔ dwirɛ ne maa yenwu ye yɛfa Yehowa nwo ɔ. Né Yehowa alimoa ahã ahyire Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ kyɛ behũhũ aseɛ ne aso mmenia mukoraa, na ne Gibeonfoɛ nemɔ koso boka sɔ mmenia naso. (Num. 33:51, 52) Ɔwɔ nu, Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ ammisa Yehowa kora yee bɛne Gibeonfoɛ nemɔ yɛ apam ɔ. Nakoso ɔnate sɔ apam bɔ ne bɛne bɛ ayɛ nati, Yehowa nwuni bɛ anwumvoin na wamma bangu bɛ. Ɛhene si ne, Yehowa hɔle so bokale Yisraelfoɛ maa belili sɔ apam naso, na ɔnate so bɔle Gibeonfoɛ nanwo waen. (Yos. 9:26; 11:19) Ɛhe mukoraa-

⁷ Sɛɛ yee yɛkɔhora yekosuesua Gibeonfoɛ nemɔ ɔ? Yee ole kyɛ yekonya Yehowa nu gyidie paa. Yɛse Yehowa nwo dwirɛ pee yɛtra Gibeonfoɛ nemɔ. Yeti yele bɔ yɛkɔhora yekogyina so yɛkɔfa yɛnwo yɛkɔto ye so koraa ɔ. (Edw. 40:4, 5) Sɛ yɛba asafo nanu koso a, yele adwuma biemɔ bɔ mmenia bu ye kyɛ sɛ ebie yɛ bie a, okyire kyɛ sɔ sona ne ba ase soma. Nakoso, sɛ yɛbrɛ yɛnwo ase na yɛyɛ adwuma bɔ ɔte sɔ a, ne yesusesua Gibeonfoɛ nemɔ. (Yos. 9:23, 27) Luke te abrandeɛ bie bɔ ɔyɛ adwuma wɔ Bɛtɛl. Mmerɛ bie, aliemaa bie bɔ yenye afi bɔ ye koso ɔyɛ adwuma wɔ Bɛtɛl berɛ yɛle nikyeebie bɔ ye wora ngofi ye lle ɔ. Adwuma bɔ ne owura sɔ aliemaa ne sa wɔ Bɛtɛl berɛ ne, ne ɔtte ba. Nakoso mmerɛ bɔ ne besi Ahennie Asa bie ne, otuli yenwo mane ɔɔwɛnene berɛ kɔngɔen. Luke hane kyɛ: “Bɔ ɔyɛle ne maa nwuni kyɛ, sɛ ebie brɛ yenwo ase a, nna yenoa mbaen yee ɔfa kã ɔ, na mmom ogyi nikyee horɛ deinn bɔ ɔkɔyɛ naso.” Sɛ ɔhaw bie to yɛ na yɛfa yɛnwo yɛto Yehowa so kyɛ ɔboka yɛ a, okyire kyɛ yele ye nu gyidie. Afei koso odikyɛ yɛbrɛ yenwo ase, na yɛyɛ adwuma biala bɔ bɛkɔfa bekowura yɛsa wɔ asafo nanu ne. Sɛ yɛyɛ sɔ a, ne yesuesua Gibeonfoɛ nemɔ.
