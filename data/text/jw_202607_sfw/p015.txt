4 Sɛ yekonwu Yehowa yé a, nna ye dumaa ne ngome yeɛ odikyɛ yenwu ye ɔ. Yeɛ sɛ yedé yedi kyɛ ɔwɔ berɛ koso a, ne oyielle. Na mmom kyɛbɔ osi te deinn ne koso, odikyɛ yenwu ye. Yede yɛkɔyɛ ye sɛ? Edwirɛ bɔ Yehowa ha hyirele Yudafoɛ bɔ ne bekofi ngoasom nu bɛkɔwa ne, ɔmaa yenwu kyɛbɔ yɛkɔyɛ ye ɔ. Ɔhane kyɛ: “Ngɔma bɛ nimdeɛ bɔ ɔkɔmaa bekonwu me kyɛ me ole AWURADE ɔ.” (Yer. 24:7) Ne Yudafoɛ nemɔ se Yehowa nwo dwirɛ dada. Nakoso sɛ ne bekonwu ye yé a, ne odikyɛ bepingye ye paa. Nwomaa bie hyirele nu kyɛ: “Ɔsono kyɛ ele Nyameɛ nwo nimdeɛ, yeɛ ɔsono kyɛ ese ye ɔ. Waayɛ kyɛ ebie bɔ wakenga edwirɛ bie wafa mmenia nyɔ bɔ bekuro bɛnwo ne. Ebiaa ɔngɔhyɛ da ngɔte yebo, sana ye bɔbɔ wate watɔ ebie dɔ nu kora.”
<<<P>>>
5 Sɛ yenwu bɔ Yehowa kuro ne bɔ onguro a, ɛhene koso kyire kyɛ yɛse ye paa. Sɔ yeɛ Ɔhene Yosia yɛle ɔ. Osuane Yehowa nwo nikyee pee. Yeti Bible ne kã yenwo dwirɛ kyɛ: ‘Olili hene ne, ye afoɛ mmɔtwɛ ne anu bɔ né ɔtɛte abɛɛmaa ne, ɔhyɛle ye bo kyɛ ɔpena ye nana Dawide Nyameɛ si atee.’ (2 Abe. 34:3) Nakoso nna ɛhene ngome yeɛ ɔyɛle ɔ. Na mmom bɔ osua fale Yehowa nwo ne, ɔfayɛle adwuma. Ɛhene ati Yehowa nate diyifoɛ Yeremia so hane yenwo dwirɛ kyɛ, ‘obuale ndɛen turoloo, na ɔbɔle bra bɔ ɔtengye ɔ.’ Bɛbɔ belle bie ne koso, olili bɛ dwirɛ mane bɛ. Afei Yehowa maa Yeremia fabokale so kyɛ: ‘Ɛhene yeɛ ebie yɛ a, okyire kyɛ ɔse me ɔ.’—Yer. 22:15, 16.
<<<P>>>
6 Kenga Edwein 9:10. Sɛ yenwu Yehowa yé a, ɔmaa yedé ye yedi. Somafoɛ Pɔɔlo nwuni kyɛ sɔ dwirɛ ne te nahorɛ. Mmerɛ bɔ ɔla prizin bɔ ne ɔka kaa maa beku ye ne, ɔhane kyɛ: ‘Ɔnyɛ me fɛle, ofikyɛ mese bɔ male ye mali ne.’ (2 Tim. 1:12) Bɔ Pɔɔlo sua fale Yehowa nwo, ɔne kyɛbɔ Yehowa bokale ye nati yeɛ ɔmaa onyane yenu gyidie sɔ ne. Ne Pɔɔlo dé di paa kyɛ Yehowa wora ngofi nahorɛ bɔ wali wama ye ne, yeti sɛ ne beku ye po a, okotingye ye.

NZUATI YEƐ ODIKYƐ YƐKƆSO YENWU YEHOWA YÉ Ɔ?
<<<P>>>
7 Mmerɛ bɔ yɛhyɛle yebo kyɛ yesua nikyee ne, yɛyerele yɛnwo paa kyɛ yekonwu Yehowa yé. Nakoso sɛ yɛbɔ asu yeyie a, okodwu nekaa bie ne, yannea yé a, sɛ yɛkenga Bible anaa yɛkɔ adesua na bɛkenga a, ebiaa yɛngɔha yɛnwo kyɛ yɛkɔpena nikyee foforɛ bɔ ɔkɔmaa yekonwu Yehowa yé ɔ. Nzuati ɔ? Ebiaa bɔ ne y’adwene wɔ so alaa yeɛ ole kyɛbɔ ɔkɔboka yɛ, anaa yɛkɔfa yɛkɔboka mmenia ɔ. Yɛyɛ sɔ a ɔtte bɔne, ofikyɛ sɔ yeɛ Bible ne nwa yɛyɛ ɔ. (2 Tim. 3:16, 17) Nakoso yɛnea Bible nanu a, ɔmaa yenwu edwirɛ pee yɛfa Yehowa nwo. (Edw. 25:8-10) Yeti sɛ yɛkenga a, nnɛmɔmaa yɛ wora fi kyɛ yekobisa yɛnwo kyɛ: ‘Bɔ makenga ye, nzu yeɛ ɔmaa menwu ye mefa Yehowa nwo ɔ?’
