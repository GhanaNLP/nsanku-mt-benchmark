(Nea ngyekyɛmuɛ 12-13)

kɔhyɛ ye ngunaen kyɛ sɛ ɔkenga yie a obisa yenwo kyɛ: ‘Nzuati yeɛ Yehowa lɛle Daniel ngoa ɔ? Sɛɛ yeɛ ye dwirɛ ne maa menwu bɔ meyɛ a ɔkɔmaa Yehowa nye kɔgye ɔ?’ (Dan. 6:10, 22) Ɔkɔhora kobisa yenwo bieku kyɛ: ‘Bɛbɔ ne bɛbɔ Daniel somoɛ ne, nzu yeɛ ɔtole bɛ ɔ? Nzu yeɛ ɛhe maa menwu ye mefa bɔ Yehowa nguro nanwo ɔ?’ (Dan. 6:4, 5, 24) Ɛhene si ne, ma osusu yenwo nea subaen foforɛ bɔ sɔ dwirɛ ne maa onwu ye fa Yehowa nwo ɔ.—Dan. 6:26, 27.

11 Sɛ yenwu kyɛ yɛ Bible asuafoɛ nemɔ fa bɔ besua ne bɛbɔ bɛ bra a, ɔmaa yɛnye gye paa. Nakoso odikyɛ yɛmaa onwu ye kyɛ Yehowa yeɛ waboka ye ɔ, ofikyɛ ye yeɛ ɔte Kyerɛkyerɛniɛ Pirikua ɔ. (Yes. 30:20, 21) Ebiaa yɛ Bible suaniɛ bie kɔkamvo yɛ wɔ atee bɔ yɛfa so yɛne ye sua nikyee nanwo, na ebie po a ɔkɔhyɛ yɛ animnyam. Ɔwɔ nu kyɛ sɛ bɛkamvo yɛ sɔ a ɔtte bɔne, nakoso odikyɛ yɛhyɛ bɛ ngunaen maa bɛfa animnyam ne nnaseɛ namu bɛma Yehowa.

11. Nzuati yeɛ yenwo hia kyɛ yɛkɔmaa yɛ Bible asuafoɛ nemɔ konwu kyɛ Yehowa yeɛ okyirekyire bɛ ɔ?

ƆFA BƆ OSUA NE ƆYƐ ADWUMA
<<<P>>>
12 Nimdeɛ bɔ yɛ Bible asuafoɛ nemɔ anya ye wɔ Bible nanu ne, odikyɛ yɛboka bɛ maa bɛfa bɛyɛ adwuma. Yede ɛkɔyɛ ye sɛ? Bɔ olimoa, sɛ wɔ Bible suaniɛ ne kenga edwirɛ bie wɔ Bible nanu na ɔnde yebo a, boka ye ma ɔfa ye adwene sie kyɛbɔ Yehowa si te naso, na ɛhene kɔboka ye paa. Yɛfa ye kyɛ wakenga edwirɛ bie wafa ndɛen bie bɔ Yehowa buale ebiemɔ nanwo. Nakoso ɔnde yebo kyɛ Yehowa huni ebiemɔ, na ebiemɔ de wangu bɛ ɔ. Sɛ ɔba sɔ a, ɛkɔyɛ sɛɛ na aboka ye maa wate sɔ dwirɛ nabo?
<<<P>>>
13 Bɔ wɔ Bible suaniɛ ne asua afa Yehowa nwo ne, ɛkɔhora kɔhyɛ ye ngunaen maa ɔkɔdwenedwene yenwo. Ɔkɔhora kobisa yenwo kyɛ: ‘Sɛɛ yeɛ Yehowa bu ngoa ɔ? (2 Pet. 3:9) Ɔmva yenwo kyɛ yɛte abɔnefoɛ ne, nzu yeɛ Yehowa ayɛ kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a okole yɛ ngoa ɔ? (Ɛfe. 2:4, 5) Aso Yehowa kɔhwẽ ebie so
