4 Ewiase he anu de, edwirɛ bie bɔ yɛnnea atee kɔhora kɔto yɛ mmerɛ biala. (Nwo. 9:11; 1 Kor. 7:31) Ebiaa yengonwu bɔ ɔkɔyɛ ɔ, ne anwonyerɛ bie abɔ yɛ. Yɛ sikasɛm kɔhora kɔyɛ basaa, anaa ebiaa ɔnate amanyɔnzɛm ti ngondii kɔhora kosi wɔ nekaa bɔ yɛwɔ ne. Ebie koso kɔhora kɔyɛ mmerɛ bɔ yɛyɛ mmerɛ mukoraati soen adwuma ne, na basesã dwumadie bɔ owura yɛsa ne. Ɔba sɔ a, ne yɛnye so atã na ɔmaa yɛkora yebisa yɛnwo kyɛ: ‘Kesaalae paa nyɛ menwo sɛ?’ Aliemaa bie bɔ bɛfrɛ ye Joseph sɔa, ne wayɛ adwuma wɔ Bɛtɛl toonn afoɛ 17. Nakoso siɛ ye, bɛhane kyɛ ofi Bɛtɛl na ɔɔyɛ ateepakyelɛniɛ. Ɔhane kyɛ: “Edwirɛ ne funi me nu. Ne mafa afoɛ pee mayɛ adwuma wɔ berɛ, yeti ɔwale sɔ ne, ne mennwu bɔ nyɛ menwo ɔ. Ofikyɛ ne menze kyɛbɔ asetena ne kɔyɛ mmerɛ bɔ mafi Bɛtɛl ne.” Ye de, sɛ edwirɛ bie bɔ yɛnnea atee to yɛ a, sɛɛ yeɛ edwirɛ bɔ Yehowa nate Yesaya so hane ne, ɔkɔhora kɔkyekye yɛ wora ɔ?
<<<P>>>
5 Kenga Yesaya 42:16. Sɛ edwirɛ bie bɔ yɛnnea atee to yɛ a, ɛyɛ a ne yɛ dwirɛ aayɛ tekyɛ ebie bɔ yenye asi bɔ onnwu nekaa bɔ ɔnate ne. Ɔba sɔ a, ne yɛnye atutu aha yɛsa nu tekyɛ ebie bɔ wamini wɔ ɛhɔaeɛ nu, anaa ebie bɔ ɔnate awosii nu wɔ atee bɔ so te mmɔnga mmɔnga ɔ. Mmerɛ bɔ ɔte sɔ nu ne, Yehowa ahyɛ bɔ kyɛ okoli yɛ nyunu na wahyire yɛ atee, na ɔngɔyakyi yɛnu lle. Ɔba sɔ a, ne wayɛ kyɛ yɛnate awosii nu bɔ Yehowa asɔ yɛ sa ná wasa wasɔ tɔɔkye bɔ ɔfa nea atee naso ma yɛ ne. Afei otuatua mmɔnga mmɔnga nemɔ maa ɔda so kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a, yɛngofĩ ase ɔ. Ye de, atee beni yeɛ Yehowa fa so yɛ sɔ ɔ? Ɔnate ye dwirɛ Bible ne, ɔne ye asafo naso ma yɛ afutue bɔ
