Sɛ ɛdwenedwene nningyein bɔ Yehowa abɔ nanwo a, ɔkɔboka wɔ maa ekonwu ye yé
(Nea ngyekyɛmuɛ 15)
<<<P>>>
kyɛ yɛkɔkenga Bible ne. Bible ne taa kã edwi-rɛ biemɔ bɔ osili, ɔne bɔ mmenia biemɔ yɛle nanwo dwirɛ. Sɛ ɛkenga edwirɛ bɔ ɔte sɔ a, susu bɔ ɔmaa enwu ye fa Yehowa nwo na-nwo.
<<<P>>>
13 Wɔde ma yɛnea sɔ nneasoɛ he. Fa ye kyɛ ɛkenga Yeremia 38:6-13. Sɔ tworɔnzɛm ne kã kyɛbɔ Yitiopianiɛ Ebede Meleke lile diyifoɛ Yeremia nanwo dwirɛ. Sɛ ɛdwene sɔ dwirɛ nanwo a, ɛberɛ nala ekonwu kyɛ ne Ebede Meleke tu yenwo sie berɛ boka mmenia, ne ole akokoduro, na ɔyele ɔdɔ ali hyirele Yere-mia, yeti odikyɛ esuesua ye. Okosa koso kɔ-maa ekonwu Yehowa subaen ahoroɛ bɔ ɛkɔ-hora kosuesua ɔ. (Kenga Yeremia 39:15-18.) Ebie yeɛ ole kyɛ, nzu yeɛ ɔwɔ sɔ tworɔn-zɛm nanu bɔ ɔmaa yenwu kyɛ Yehowa nye sɔ bɔ ye mmenia yɛ ma ye ne, obua ndɛen turo-loo, na ɔnyɛ nyeyemuɛ ɔ? Aso enwuni nikyee foforɛ bieku bɔ ɛkɔhora kosua kɔfa Yehowa nwo wɔ sɔ dwirɛ nanu anaa? Mmerɛ biala bɔ ɛkenga Bible ne, bɔ mmɔden kyɛ ekonwu Ye-howa subaen ahoroɛ ne bie, na da ye ase kyɛ wamaa anwu sɔ subaen ne ɔ.
<<<P>>>
14 Nikyee bɔ ɔtɔ so nyɔ bɔ ɔkɔboka yɛ maa yekonwu Yehowa yé yeɛ ole kyɛ yɛkɔdwe-nedwene nningyein bɔ wabɔ nanwo. (Edw. 145:9, 10) Nahorɛ dwirɛ yeɛ ole kyɛ, sɛ ebie nea abɔdeɛ mbaen a, ne oyielle. Ofikyɛ ebie-mɔ wɔ berɛ a, bafa mmerɛ pee basua nnin-gyein bɔ Nyameɛ abɔ nanwo nikyee, nakoso bennwu ye subaen ahoroɛ bɔ ɔyɛ nyemene ne, na bɛkã po kyɛ ɔnne berɛ. Nakoso soma-foɛ Pɔɔlo hane kyɛ: “Nyameɛ subaen te ni-kyee bɔ yɛmva yɛ nye nnwu ye ɔ, nakoso waye bɛ ali wahyire yɛ wɔ ye abɔdeɛ nu.” (Rom. 1:20) Bible foforɛ bie hyirele sɔ dwi-rɛ nabo kyɛ, “ebie bɔ ɔkora susu nningyein nwo yé ne, yeɛ ɔkɔhora konwu Nyameɛ su-baen ahoroɛ ne ɔ.” Yeti odikyɛ yenya mme-rɛ yesusu nningyein bɔ Yehowa abɔ nanwo, na yɛnea bɔ ɛhene maa yenwu ye yɛfa ye-nwo ɔ.
