Mvili Japan mmandwuli New York ne, né mene aliemaa brasua bie bɔ bɛfrɛ ye Michiko Oda yɛbɔ ngɔmmɔ wɔ Brooklyn Bɛtɛl berɛ, na ngane bɔ osili nanwo dwirɛ ngyirele ye. Yeɛ ɔhã sili me nyunu pein kyɛ: “Odikyɛ esua Japanfoɛ dwudwolɛ ne!” Na sɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ yeɛ nyɛle ɔ, na menwa, maangyẽ yenwo nnaleɛ lle kyɛ ne sɔ nikyee ne kɔboka me ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Afei wangyɛ biala ngɔle asafo bɔ befa Japanfoɛ dwudwolɛ bɛyɛ adesua nu wɔ New York. Japanfoɛ dwudwolɛ bɔ nzuane ne, obukyele atee pee mane me wɔ Yehowa soen nu. Nu kõ yeɛ ole kyɛ, wɔ 1989 nu ne, ngɔle me nhyiamu bɔ olimoa wɔ Japanfoɛ dwudwolɛ nu. Bɛyɛle sɔ nhyiamu ne wɔ Los Angeles, bɔ ɔwɔ California ne. Ɛberɛ yeɛ ngonyiale taluwa bie bɔ bɛfrɛ ye Miwako Onami ɔ. Ne ɔte ateepakyelɛnie, na bɛbɔ bɛyɛle drama wɔ sɔ nhyiamu nabo ne, né ɔboka bɛso. Menwa, ye anwonyemene ne tɔle me so, yeti ndwe mane ye.
<<<P>>>
Mene Miwako yegyale wɔ 1992 nu, na yenyane yenwo atee yɛɛsone wɔ Brooklyn Bɛtɛl. Miwako kuro sona kyɛ, na menwa, sɛ owura yenoanu po a, ɔkɔye kɔma wɔ. Wafa ye subaen ne wasá me, yeti kesaalae ɔmaa me koso medwene sona nwo paa. Me ye de, mekã kyɛ, ɔte akyɛdeɛ bɔ Yehowa afa ama me ɔ. Sɛ mangya ye a, ahaa mawu, ofikyɛ ye yeɛ ɔkyekye me wora ɔ, na ɔmaa menye gye paa.

Odwuli mmerɛ bie ne, ne Miwako maame ne ye baba nde apɔ koraa. Yeti bɛmane yɛ atee kyɛ yɛhɔ Japan Bɛtɛl, kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a yekopingye bɛ, na yahora yanea bɛ boso ɔ. Ɛhene ati yetu yɛhɔle Japan Bɛtɛl wɔ 1999 nu.
<<<P>>>
Yeedwuli berɛ ne, aliemaamɔ lɛle yɛ kama, na ɛhene maa yenwuni kyɛ yawa awuro. Afei koso, subaen pá bɔ né aliemaamɔ bɔ bɛwɔ Japan ne da ye ali ne, nzuane nningyein pee mvili nu. Ebie yeɛ ole kyɛ, ne bekuro sona, bebukye bɛsa nu, na ne bɛmva b’adwuma koso benni angorɛ. Wɔ Japan koso ne, b’amammerɛ kyire kyɛ ɔte pá kyɛ mmeni pee kɔbɔnu kɔyɛ nikyeebie tra kyɛ sonahorɛ ngome kɔyɛ kɔfa kole dumaa ɔ. Ɛhene ati sɛ bɛ́yɛ adwuma a, ɛyɛ a bɛyɛ bɛbɔnu kyɛ kue. Bɛ nneasoɛ ne maa nwuni kyɛbɔ ngɔbrɛ menwo ase, na mene mmenia abɔnu ayɛ adwuma ɔ. Afei koso, ɔmaa ndele yebo kyɛ sãa nningyein mmaammaa biemɔ bɔ meyɛ mema Yehowa ne, mvasoɛ wɔ so, ofikyɛ ɔboka maa ye atirimbɔ ba nu.

Y’ayeforɔ kyẽa
<<<P>>>
Nakoso, ɔnate kyɛ bandete me wɔ Japan berɛ nati, sɛ odwu mmerɛ bie a, kyɛbɔ ne aliemaamɔ bɔ bɛwɔ berɛ ne si yɛ bɛ nikyee ne, ne ɔyɛ se ma me kyɛ ngole ngɔto nu ɔ. Ɔwale sɔ ne, ne odikyɛ mesua kyɛbɔ ngonya aboterɛ ɔ. Ne ɛhene kɔmaa ngɔhora ngɔhyɛ menwo so mmerɛ bɔ bayɛ nikyeebie bɔ mene bɛ nyɛ adwene ne. Mmerɛ kɔso ne, nzesane me subaen, na nwuni kyɛ sɛ yetie akwangyerɛ bɔ Yehowa nate asafo naso fama yɛ ne a, okoyira gyinayɛɛ biala bɔ yekosi naso. Ofikyɛ yɛkã ye, bɔ yɛkora yɛyɛ biala ne, Yehowa yeɛ ɔmaa ɔyɛ yé ɔ.

Ndeteyɛɛ bɔ nyane ye wɔ Bɛtɛl ne, ɛyɛ a metaa mefa metoto ndeteyɛɛ bɔ diyifoɛ Mose nyane ye nanwo. Afoɛ 40 bɔ Mose fa ɔɔneane mmoaen ne, Yehowa nate so bokale ye maa osuane kyɛbɔ ɔkɔbrɛ yenwo ase ɔ. Tekyɛ Mose ne, me koso ɔnate Univɛsiti kãa bɔ ngɔle nati, ne wamaa mayɛ anwomasoɛ. Afei ne ɔmaa menwu menwo kyɛ menhia ebiala mmokalɛ mmerɛ bɔ meyɛ adwuma bie ne.
