nu, yeti nningyein bɔ Yehowa abɔ ne, ɛyɛ a yɛndaa yennwu bie. Nakoso sɛ ɛte kuro piri bɔ ɔte sɔ nu po a, ɛkɔhora kɔdwenedwene nningyein bɔ Yehowa abɔ nanwo. Atee beni aso? Ɛmɔma yɛnea aliemaa bie bɔ bɛfrɛ ye Patrick bɔ ɔwɔ Republic of the Congo ne. Kuro bɔ ɔte so ne, bɛfrɛ ye Brazzaville, na ɔte kuro pirikua paa. Ɔhane kyɛ: “Bɔ ɔboka me maa daa menwu nningyein bɔ Yehowa abɔ ne yeɛ ole kyɛ, ɛyɛ a mekogyina me valanda naso. Meyɛ sɔ a ɔmaa mekora menwu ngaka mmaammaa biemɔ te kyɛ ngyiregyira ne nnomaa bɔ bɛpena aleɛ bedi ɔ. Ɛlɛho bie po de, nwuni nnomaa biemɔ bɔ ne bɛyɛ bɛ bue ɔ. Nningyein bɔ bɛfa bɛyɛle ne, benyane ye wɔ mandanu berɛ ala. Nakoso bɛhora bɛfa bɛyɛle bue kama, bɔ ɔkɔmaa sɛ bɛbobɔ bɛmmaamɔ a bekonya nekaa bɛkɔla ɔ. Menwa, ne ɔyɛ nyemene po mbaen.” Nakoso Patrick annea ye mbaen, na mmom bɔ ɔmaa onwu ye fa Yehowa nwo ne, ɔdwenedwenene yenwo. Ohyirele nu kyɛ: “Mmanwuni kyɛ Yehowa nye wɔ nningyein bɔ wabɔ naso, na ngaka mmaammaa po, ɔmaa benya bɔ behia ɔ. Yeti me koso nikyeebiala bɔ mehia ne, ɔkɔmaa mesa kɔha ye.”

16 Nikyee bɔ ɔtɔ so nza bɔ ɔkɔmaa yekonwu Yehowa yé yeɛ ole kyɛ, yɛkɔdwenedwene bɔ wayɛ wama yɛ nanwo. Sɔ yeɛ Yesu maame Maria yɛle ɔ. Ne ɔse Yehowa, na ne Yehowa koso ‘alo ye.’ (Luka 1:30) Nakoso mmerɛ bɔ ɔmane ye adwuma kandinga bie ne, yeɛ onwuni ye yé paa ɔ. Yeɛ ole kyɛ ne ɔkɔwo Ɔwa Yesu.
