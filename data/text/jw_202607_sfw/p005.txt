Suesua Gibeonfoɛ nemɔ, na brɛ wɔnwo ase yɛ adwuma bɔ mmenia bu ye kyɛ ɔba ase wɔ asafo nanu ne
(Nea ngyekyɛmuɛ 7)
<<<P>>>
hũhũ Gibeonfoɛ nemɔ maa bɛ boso konua.* Ɛhene maa Gibeonfoɛ nanu pee wuli. (2 Sam. 21:2, 5, 6) Yeti apam bɔ ne Yisraelfoɛ ne bɛ ayɛ ne, Sɔɔlo de wanni so. Ne bɛ dwirɛ ne yɛ yea paa.
<<<P>>>
9 Sɔ bɔne bɔ Sɔɔlo fayɛle Gibeonfoɛ ne, nna ɛberɛ nala yeɛ Yehowa buli yenwo atɛn ɔ. Na mmom siɛ ye bɔ Dawide oolili bia ne, yeɛ Yehowa yɛle sɔ ɔ. Bɔ ɔyɛle yeɛ ole kyɛ, ɔmaa ɛhɔen pirikua bie wale Yisrael afoɛ nza mumua. Ɔwale sɔ ne, Dawide bisale Yehowa bɔ yeti yeɛ sɔ hɔen ne awa ɔ. Yeɛ Yehowa koso hane kyɛ, Sɔɔlo ne ye abusuafoɛ ahã mogya agua, ofikyɛ behuni Gibeonfoɛ nemɔ, na ɛhene ati yeɛ wamaa sɔ hɔen ne awa ne.—2 Sam. 21:1.
<<<P>>>
10 Kenga 2 Samuel 21:3-6. Dawide bisale Gibeonfoɛ nemɔ kyɛ, nzu yeɛ ɔfa pata bɛ ɔ? Ne ahaa bɛkɔhora bɛkɔhã kyɛ ɔma bɛ sikaa, nakoso banga sɔ, na mmom bɛhane kyɛ, ɛhe de “nna ɛdwetɛ anaa sikaa kɔkorɛ dwirɛ ɔ.” Bɔ ɔmaa bɛhane sɔ yeɛ ole kyɛ, ne Mmraa ne nwa, sɛ ebie kũ sona a, nnɛmaa bedé mbata biala, na mmom behũ ye bie. (Num. 35:30, 31) Afei koso, ne bɛse kyɛ bɛngɔhora bengohũ ebie mmerɛ bɔ ɔhene manne b’atee ɔ. Yeti Dawide hane kyɛ behũ Sɔɔlo bosofoɛ nanu mmrienzua nzoo. Ɔkɔyɛ kyɛ ne bɛ koso bɛboka so bie yeɛ behuni Gibeonfoɛ nemɔ ɔ. Ye si ne, Yehowa maa nzue tɔle, yeti ɛhɔen ne wale ayieleɛ. Ne ɛhene kyire kyɛ afei siɛ ye de, bɔ ɔtole Gibeonfoɛ nemɔ ne, Yehowa ahã bie ama bɛ.—2 Sam. 21:9, 10, 14.
<<<P>>>
11 Bɔ sɔ dwirɛ ne maa yenwu ye yɛfa Yehowa nwo ɔ. Gibeonfoɛ nemɔ dwirɛ ne maa yenwu ye kyɛ Yehowa nguro asisie. (Edw. 37:28) Ɛwɔfoɛ mɔ bɔ mmenia mva bɛ nyɛ hwee, ɔne bɛbɔ belle bie ne po, ɔpena kyɛ yɛfa obuo yɛma bɛ. Yɛnu pee wɔ berɛ a, ɔnate kyɛ yɛsõ Yehowa nati bɛtane yɛnye. Nakoso sɛ Yehowa mmerɛ ne sõ a, ɔkɔye sɔ haw namu kofi berɛ maa yɛnwo kɔtɔ yɛ. Afei koso, mmerɛ bɔ Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ hyɛle Gibeonfoɛ nemɔ bɔ kyɛ bengohũ bɛ ne, ne Yehowa pena kyɛ bedi so. Ɛhene maa yenwu ye kyɛ, yɛ koso sɛ yɛhyɛ
