Nzɛmmisa Bɔ Yɛfa Yɛboka Yɛ Bible Asuafoɛ Maa Benwu Yehowa Yé Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Nzɛmmisa pee yeɛ ɔwɔ Nya Asetena Nu Anigye Daa! nwomaa nanu ɔ. Na ɔboka Bible asuafoɛ nemɔ maa benwu Yehowa yé. Sɔ nzɛmmisa ne bie ɛhe:
<<<P>>>
“Sɛɛ yeɛ Yehowa tengã wɔ bɛbɔ bɛkã nzɛmba nanwo ɔ?” (adesua 21)
<<<P>>>
“Sɛɛ yeɛ bɔ asua afa Yehowa ne Yesu nwo ne amaa anya awerɛhyemuɛ kyɛ bekobu atɛn-tenenee [wɔ Armagedon] ɔ?” (adesua 33)
<<<P>>>
“Sɛ etie Yehowa na ɛda nworɛ adi a, sɛɛ yeɛ ɔte ngã ɔ? Nzuati ɔ?” (adesua 34)
<<<P>>>
“Sɛ yegya ebie bɔ ɔnnɔ Yehowa a, ɛnea sɛɛ yeɛ ɔkɔte ngã ɔ?” (adesua 42)
<<<P>>>
“Sɛɛ yeɛ Yehowa fa yɛ bɔne kyɛ yɛ koraa ɔ?” (adesua 56)
<<<P>>>
“Kyɛbɔ Yehowa boka abɔnefoɛ ne, sɛɛ yeɛ ɔmaa yenwu kyɛ ye dwirɛ tte twaenn, ɔte ɔdɔ, na onwu anwumvoin ɔ?” (adesua 57)
<<<P>>>
ye dwirɛ kɔbo 50. (Yob 38:1–41:34) Sɛ yɛnea Nya Asetena Nu Anigye Daa! nwomaa ne a, nzɛmmisa bɔ ɔwɔ nu ne, bayɛ ye kyɛ ɔboka yɛ Bible asuafoɛ ma benwu kyɛbɔ Yehowa si te ɔ. (Nea “Nzɛmmisa Bɔ Yɛfa Yɛboka Yɛ Bible Asuafoɛ Maa Benwu Yehowa Yé Ɔ” bɔ ɔwɔ alaka nanu ne.) Yeti sɛ ɛne ebie sua nikyee a, fa sɔ dwirɛ hemɔ boka ye ma onwu Yehowa yé.
<<<P>>>
9 Sɛ yɛne ebie hyɛ yebo kyɛ yesua nikyee ala pɛ, odikyɛ yɛhyɛ ye ngunaen maa ɔkenga Bible ne kyẽa biala.* Yede yɛkɔyɛ sɛɛ na yaboka bɛ maa banwu kyɛbɔ Yehowa si te ɔ, mmerɛ bɔ bɛkenga Bible ne? Yɛkɔhora yɛkɔhyɛ bɛ ngunaen kyɛ, sɛ bɛkenga Bible nu dwirɛ bie a, besusu yenwo bɛnea bɔ yeti yeɛ Yehowa maa bɛhworɔle sɔ dwirɛ ne ɔ, ɔne bɔ sɔ dwirɛ ne maa benwu bɛfa Yehowa nwo ɔ. Somafoɛ Yakobo sɔa, mmerɛ bɔ ne ɔkã Yob amanehunu nanwo dwirɛ ne, sɔ yeɛ ɔyɛle ɔ. Nna kyɛbɔ omiane yenye gyinane nu ne ngome yeɛ ɔhane yenwo dwirɛ ɔ, na mmom ɔhwene adwene hɔle Yehowa subaen biemɔ so. Yeɛ ole kyɛ ɔse anwumvoin na osa dwene sona nwo.—Kenga Yakobo 5:11.
<<<P>>>
10 Yɛpena kyɛ yɛboka yɛ Bible asuafoɛ nemɔ maa benwu kyɛ, Bible nu dwirɛ biala bɔ bɛkɔkenga ne, bɛbɔ mmɔden kyɛ bekonwu bɔ Yehowa kuro ne bɔ onguro wɔ sɔ dwirɛ nanu. Yɛfa ye kyɛ wɔ Bible suaniɛ bie anea nekaa bɔ yenwa, “Hyɛ Yebo Kenga Bible Ne” bɔ ɔwɔ Nya Asetena Nu Anigye Daa! nwomaa nanu ne, na ɔha hyirele wɔ kyɛ ɔpena kyɛ ɔkenga Daniel bɔ ne ɔla gyata kumaa nu nanwo dwirɛ. Ekɔhora
<<<P>>>
* Nya Asetena Nu Anigye Daa! nwomaa ne adesua 05 ne, ye nekaa bɔ bahworɔ berɛ kyɛ Botaeɛ ne kã kyɛ: “Hyɛ yebo kenga Bible ne kyẽa biala. Sɛ ɛnea nekaa bɔ yenwa ‘Hyɛ Yebo Kenga Bible Ne’ a, ɔkɔboka wɔ maa ɛkɔyɛ sɔ.”
<<<P>>>
9-10. (a) Wɔ Bible suaniɛ ne kenga Bible ne a, nzu yeɛ ɔkɔboka ye maa okonya so mvasoɛ paa ɔ? (Yakobo 5:11) (b) Sɛ wɔ Bible suaniɛ ne kenga Daniel dwirɛ ne yie a, edwirɛ beni yeɛ ɔkɔhora kobisa yenwo ɔ?
