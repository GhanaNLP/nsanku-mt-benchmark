DWEIN 12
Otumfoɔ, Yehowa Nyankopɔn
<<<P>>>
BƆ ADESŨA NE KƆHÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ
Sɛ bɛkã kyɛ yenwu Yehowa yé a, yekonwu bɔ okyire ɔ. Afei bɔ yeti yeɛ odikyɛ yɛkɔso yenwu ye yé, ɔne atee bɔ yɛkɔfa so yɛkɔyɛ sɔ ne koso, yekonwu ye.
<<<P>>>
Aso Ɛbɔ Mmɔden Kyɛ Ekonwu Yehowa Yé?
<<<P>>>
‘AWURADE wɔ baba Nyameɛ ne, odi kyɛ enwu ye yé, ná ɛfa ahone bɔ nu te ɛso ye.’—1 ABE. 28:9.
<<<P>>>
YƐ NYAMEƐ bɔ yɛsõ ye ne, ole nimdeɛ tra ebiala, na kyɛbɔ osi se nworɛ ne de, bɛnga. Wasa wayɛ nningyein pee bɔ ɔyɛ nwanwa ɔ. Sɛ yɛdwenedwene ɛhemɔ mukoraati nwo a, ɔbo y’adwene ne yɛ ndeaseɛ so. Somafoɛ Pɔɔlo sɔa, sɔ yeɛ ne onwu ye ɔ, yeti ɔhane kyɛ: “O! Nyameɛ anwonya ne ye nworɛ ne ye nimdeɛ nu pi! Nwa yeɛ ɔkɔhora kohyirehyire ye porɛ bɔ wabɔ nu ɔ? Nwa yeɛ ɔse ye atee bɔ ɔfa so yɔ ye nningyein, anaa nwa yeɛ ɔkɔte ye bo koyie ɔ?” (Rom. 11:33, 34) Yɛ koso yɛne ye yɛ adwene.
<<<P>>>
2 Ɔwɔ nu, yɛngɔhora yengonwu Yehowa nwo dwirɛ mukoraati. Nakoso, kyɛbɔ osi te ne de, yɛkɔhora yekonwu yenwo dwirɛ kaa. Yɛyɛ sɛɛ yeɛ yenwu ye sɔ ɔ? Sɛ yɛnea Ɔhene Dawide sɔa a, ɔha hyirele ɔwa Solomon kyɛ: ‘AWURADE wɔ baba Nyameɛ ne, odi kyɛ enwu ye yé, ná ɛfa ahone bɔ nu te ɛso ye. Sɛ ɛpena ye si atee a, ekonwu ye.’ (1 Abe. 28:9) Afoɛ pee si ne, somafoɛ Yohane ha hyirele oliemamɔ Kristofoɛ kyɛ, Yesu ‘abukye b’adwene nu, na wamaa banwu nahorɛniɛ ne.’ (1 Yoh. 5:20) Yesu koso hane kyɛ, ebiala bɔ okuro kyɛ onya Yehowa nwo nimdeɛ ne, ‘Baa ne kɔla ye ali kohyire ye.’—Mat. 11:27.
<<<P>>>
3 Yɛ bɔ yɛsõ Yehowa ne de, yasua yenwo nikyee pee dada. Nakoso nningyein foforɛ pee wɔ berɛ bɔ yɛkɔhora yekosua yɛkɔfa yenwo, na wamaa ‘yanwu ye’ yé. (Yoh. 17:3) Ebie yeɛ ole kyɛ, bɔ yɛyɛ a ɔkɔmaa yenye kɔgye, ɔne bɔ ɔkɔboka
<<<P>>>
1. Sɛ yɛnea Yehowa nimdeɛ, kyɛbɔ ɔse nworɛ, ɔne bɔ wahora wayɛ ne a, sɛɛ yeɛ ɔmaa yɛte nga ɔ?
<<<P>>>
2. Nzu yeɛ ɔmaa yenwu ye kyɛ yɛkɔhora yekonwu Yehowa yé ɔ?
<<<P>>>
3. Nzu yeɛ yekosusu yenwo wɔ adesua he anu ɔ?
