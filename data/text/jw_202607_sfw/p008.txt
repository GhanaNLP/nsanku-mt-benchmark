<<<P>>>
“AWURADE nwa nyoo: ‘Kyɛbɔ baa oni si kyekye ye wora ne, sɔ ala yeɛ me koso ngɔkyekye ɛmɔ wora ɔ.’”—YES. 66:12, 13.

DWEIN 41
Tie Me Mpaebɔ
<<<P>>>
BƆ ADESŨA NE KƆHÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Yekonwu kyɛbɔ sɛ yɛ wora bɔ na yɛdwenedwene Yesaya nwomaa nanu dwirɛ nanwo a, ɔkɔkyekye yɛ wora ɔ.
<<<P>>>
YƐFA ye kyɛ maame bie ne ohu ne bɛwa betu befi nekaa bɔ bɛwɔ ne. Sɛ akwalaa ne nea kyɛ ɔkɔ yakyi ye damvomɔ nemɔ a, ne ye wora abɔ. Ɔba sɔ a, ye maame kɔkrɔkrɔ ye. Anaa yɛfa ye kyɛ akwalaa bie odi angorɛ yeɛ wapira ɔ. Sɛ ye maame nwu ye kyɛ ole suɛ ɔba awuro a, nzu yeɛ ɔkɔyɛ ɔ? Ɔnate kyɛ okuro ɔwa ne dwirɛ nati, ɔkɔfa yesa kɔto ye kɔme anu, na wakrɔkrɔ ye kyɛ ɔyakyi suɛ. Afei ɔkɔpena ayire bie kɔyɛ so kɔma ye.
<<<P>>>
2 Sɛ yɛnea Bible nanu a, Yehowa maa yenwu kyɛ ɔte kyɛ maame bie bɔ ɔkrɔkrɔ ɔwa bɔ okuro ye dwirɛ ɔ. Ɔkã kyɛ: “Kyɛbɔ baa oni si kyekye ye wora ne, sɔ ala yeɛ me koso ngɔkyekye ɛmɔ wora ɔ.” (Yes. 66:13) Ɔdɔ bɔ Yehowa le ma yɛ ne, ɔtra ɔdɔ bɔ maame anaa baba biala konya kɔma ɔwa ɔ. (Yes. 49:15) Yeti sɛ yɛ wora bɔ anaa y’abanu bu a, Yehowa nate Bible naso maa yenya edwirɛ pee bɔ ɔkɔkyekye yɛ wora ɔ, na ɛhene boka yɛ maa yɛkora yegyina yɛ gya so.—Rom. 15:4.
<<<P>>>
3 Edwirɛ bɔ Yehowa fa kyekye ye mmenia wora ne, yenwu nu pee wɔ Yesaya nwomaa nanu. (Yes. 40:1) Mmerɛ bɔ Yehowa maa Yesaya hworɔle ye nwomaa ne, ɔnate Yudafoɛ nemɔ asoserɛ ti, né nningyein ngɔ yé koraa wɔ maen nanu. Yeti ne nu dwirɛ ne kɔkyekye bɛ wora. Yɛ koso yɛte mmerɛ bɔ nu yɛ se paa nu, yeti yehia awɛrɛkyekyerɛ. (2 Tim. 3:1) Ebiaa edwirɛ bie bɔ yɛnnea atee ato yɛ, bɔne bie bɔ yayɛ ne, yenwo adwenedwene té hyɛ yɛso, anaa yɛnwoniɛ bie awu maa yedi yealɛ. Sɛ sɔ a, ye de yɛbaanea edwirɛ biemɔ bɔ ɔwɔ Yesaya nwomaa nanu, bɔ ɔkɔhora kɔkyekye yɛ wora maa yekosa yekogyina yɛgya so ɔ.
