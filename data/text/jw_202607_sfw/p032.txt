<<<P>>>
Ɛkɔye Sɛɛ Na Ama Mmuayeɛ Bɔ Ɔkɔhyɛ Aliemaamɔ Ngunaen Ɔ?
<<<P>>>
Sɛ yɛkɔ adesua a, yenya yenwo atee bɔ yɛkɔfa yɛkɔma mmuayeɛ bɔ ɔkɔhyɛ aliemaamɔ ngunaen ɔ. (Heb. 10:24, 25) Ye de sɛ yɛbaahɔ adesua a, nzu yee yɛyɛ a, ɔkɔmaa sɛ yɛkɔ a, yɛkɔhora yɛkɔma mmuayeɛ bɔ ɔte sɔ ɔ?
<<<P>>>
• Bɔ Yehowa mbaeɛ. Srɛ ye kyɛ ɔboka wɔ ma sɛ ɛkɔ adesua a, ɛhora ma mmuayeɛ ɛfa ye ye ayɛ, na afei koso ɔhyɛ aliemaamɔ ngunaen.—Edw. 35:18.
<<<P>>>
• Bɔ mmɔden kyɛ ɛngɔkenga wɔ mmuayeɛ ne ngofi nwomaa nanu. Sɛ angenga wɔ mmuayeɛ ne amvi nwomaa nanu, na mmom egyina kyɛbɔ esi te edwire nabo naso ɛma mmuayeɛ a, ɔkɔhyɛ aliemaamɔ ngunaen paa.
<<<P>>>
• Ma wɔ mmuayeɛ ne ɔyɛ tikaape. Mmuayeɛ bɔ ɔte tikaape, na nu la berɛ ne, ɛyɛ a ɔhyɛ ngunaen paa. Afei ɔmaa mmeni foforɛ koso nya atee dwudwo bie.—Any. 10:19.
<<<P>>>
• Ma wɔ mmuayeɛ ne ɔhyɛ aliemaamɔ ngunaen. Bɔ mmɔden kyɛ ɛngɔfa wɔ mmuayeɛ ne ngohwi aliemaa bie nyunu anaa ɛngɔbɔ ye akutia.
<<<P>>>
Sɛ ɛma wɔsa so na bamvrɛ wɔ po a, ɛkɔhora kɔhyɛ aliemaamɔ ngunaen. Ɛkɔyɛ ye sɛɛ? Yeɛ ole kyɛ bɛma mmuayeɛ a, ekotie ye yé na sɛ ɛmɔpɔno a, na amaa banwu nikyee pɔtee bɔ wɔnye gyele yenwo wɔ bɛ mmuayeɛ nanwo ɔ.—Any. 10:21.
