16 Sɛɛ yee yɛkɔhora yekosuesua Gibeonfoɛ nemɔ ɔ? Yɛpena kyɛ yɛyere yɛnwo yɛsõ Yeho-wa kyɛbɔ bɛyɛle ne. Yesɔ nati nningyein biemɔ bɔ ahaa ɔkɔmaa yɛnwo kɔtɔ yɛ ne, yɛfa yɛto ahameɛ. Nakoso ɔnate kyɛ yɛdɔ Yehowa nati, ɔnha yɛ kyɛ yɛkɔyɛ sɔ ɔ. Sɔ yee aliemaa bie bɔ bɛfrɛ ye Alwin bɔ ofi Philippines ne yɛle ɔ. Adwuma bɔ ne ɔyɛ ne, ne akatuaa ne te kama paa. Nakoso ne ɔpena kyɛ ɔfa ye mmerɛ pee yɛ asɛnga adwuma ne. Yeti mmerɛ biala bɔ man-zini soneafoɛ kɔwa b’asafo nanu ne, ne ɔsrɛ atee fi adwuma nu na wamaa wahora wahɔ asɛnga sɔ dapɛɛn nanu. Sɛ ne ɔyɛ sɔ a, ne ye-nye gye paa. Odwuli nekaabie ne, ne ɔpena kyɛ ɔbayɛ ateepakyelɛniɛ. Nakoso, ɔnate ye adwuma nati, ne ɔkɔyɛ se kɔma ye. Yeti ɔyɛ-le ye adwene kyɛ ɔkɔyaki, na ɔsrɛle Yeho-wa kyɛ ɔboka ye ma onya adwuma foforɛ bɔ ɔkɔmaa ɔkɔhora kɔyɛ ateepakyelɛ adwuma ne ɔ. Amba-ne-amba Yehowa adaworoma onyane adwuma ne bie. Ɔwɔ nu, ne onnya esikaa kyɛ dáa ne, nakoso yenye gyele ofikyɛ ɔmaa ɔho-ra ɔɔyɛle ateepakyelɛniɛ. Siɛ ye, ɔye ɔɔbokale ye so maa bɛyɛle ateepakyelɛ adwuma ne, na Yehowa nate bɛ so waboka mmenia 21 maa babɔ asu. Yɛdé yedi kyɛ sɔ gyinayɛɛ bɔ besili ne, ɔmaa Yehowa nye gyele paa. Yɛ koso yedé yedi kyɛ nikyeebiala bɔ yɛkɔfa yɛkɔbɔ afɔleɛ na wamaa yahora yafa yɛ mmerɛ pee yasõ Yehowa ne, ye wora ngofi ye lle. Afei ɔkɔ-maa yɛsa kɔhã bɔ yehia mmerɛ biala.—Mat. 6:33.

17 Gibeonfoɛ nemɔ nwo dwirɛ bɔ ɔwɔ Bible nanu ne, ɔmaa yenwu Yehowa subaen biemɔ bɔ ɔyɛ nyemene paa ɔ. Yɛɛ ole kyɛ ɔbrɛ yenwo ase, onwu anwumvoin, obua ndɛen turoloo, na odi nahorɛ. Bɛbɔ bedi nahorɛ bɛma ye ne koso, ɔmma bɛsa nzi ase. Bɛ dwirɛ na amaa yasa yasua nningyein pee. Ebie yee ole kyɛ, sɛ yɛfa ɔhaw nu po a, yekonya Yehowa nu gyidie na yafa yɛnwo yato ye so. Ɔba sɔ a, ɔkɔmaa yɛ-kɔbrɛ yenwo ase, na adwuma biala bɔ Yehowa kɔfa kɔma yɛ ne koso, yekole yɛkɔto nu yɛkɔ-yɛ. Sɛ mmenia ne yɛ anni ye yé koso a, odikyɛ yenya aboterɛ na yenya gyidie kyɛ Yehowa mmerɛ bɔ ɔsɛ nu ne, okoli yɛ dwirɛ kɔma yɛ. Afei koso, odikyɛ yɛyere yɛnwo yɛsõ Yehowa. Yɛyɛ sɔ a, okyire kyɛ yedi nahorɛ yɛma ye. Ɔba sɔ a, okohyire kyɛ amba, yesuesua bɔ Gi-beonfoɛ nemɔ yɛle ne.
