“Agyi me bɔne amukoraa agua wɔ si dɔ”
<<<P>>>
Yehowa de, nna bɔne biemɔ bɔ yayɛ ne, yee ɔfa ye adwene sie so ɔ
(Nea ngyekyɛmuɛ 11)
<<<P>>>
10 Kenga Yesaya 38:17. Edwırɛ bɔ yeɛ ole kyɛ, “agyi me bɔne amukoraa agua wɔ si dɔ” ne, ɔkora koso kyire kyɛ “aye me bɔne mukoraati afi wɔ nyunu.” Tworɔnzɛm he maa yenwu ye kyɛ, sɛ ebie yɛ bɔne na onu yenwo a, Yehowa gyi ye bɔne ne to nekaabie koraa, yeti onnwu ye kṍ. Nwomaa bie koso kyire sɔ dwirɛ nanu kyɛ: “Amaa wayɛ tekyɛ bɔ nyɛlle bɔne biala ne.” Yeti Yehowa de, sɛ yɛyɛ bɔne na onya fa kyɛ yɛ a, ɔye fi ye tianu koraa bɔ ɔngae ye kṍ ɔ. Yeti ɔtte nikyee bɔ siɛ ye ɔkɔhwe yɛso wɔ yenwo ɔ. Wahyɛ bɔ kyɛ: ‘Mengɔkaye wɔ bɔne kṍ.’ (Yes. 43:25) Edwırɛ he de, ɔmaa yɛnwo yɛ yɛ fɔnwoo paa. Yɛse kyɛ sɛ Yehowa bua ndɛen a, ebiala ngora nga yenwo dwirɛ kṍ. Yeti sɛ yenwa wafa yɛ bɔne wahyɛ yɛ a, yɛ de yɛnwo nyɛɛ bɔ odikyɛ yɛfa yenwo dwirɛ yɛha yɛnwo ɔ?
<<<P>>>
10. Edwırɛ beni yeɛ ɔwɔ Yesaya 38:17 bɔ ɔmaa yɛnwo yɛ yɛ fɔnwoo ɔ?
<<<P>>>
Bɔne biemɔ bɔ ayɛ ne, nnɛmaa ɔsɛkye anigye bɔ ɛfa sõ Yehowa ne
<<<P>>>
11 Sɛ bɔne bie bɔ yayɛ ne, yenwo dwirɛ té ha yɛ a, ɛmɔma yɛkae edwirɛ fɛɛfɛɛ bɔ Yehowa ahã ahyire yɛ ne. Yehowa de, sɛ ɔfa bɔne kyɛ a, ne wafa wahyɛ koraa. Ɔtte kyɛ sona bɔ yenwa wafa bɔne wahyɛ po a, ebiaa ne yenwo dwirɛ té wura ye nu ne. Anaa ɔnga kyɛ ɔnate bɔne bɔ yayɛ nati, sɛ ɔfa yɛ yɛ hwee a ɔnyɛ yé. (Yes. 57:16) Bɔ yenwo hia Yehowa paa yeɛ ole bɔ yɛyɛ ye kesaalae, ɔne bɔ yɛyere yɛnwo kyɛ yɛkɔyɛ ye kyẽabie ne, na ɔtte bɔne bie bɔ yayɛ ɔ. (Yes. 55:7) Ɔyɛ yɛ fɛ paa kyɛ yɛsõ Nyameɛ bɔ ɔfa bɔne kyɛ, na ɔdwene yɛnwo sɔ ɔ. Ɛhene ati nnɛmɔmaa bɔne bie bɔ yayɛ ne, yenwo adwenedwene hyɛ yɛso maa anigye bɔ yɛfa yɛsõ Yehowa ne kɔ ase. Ofikyɛ sɛ ɔba sɔ a, ebiaa
