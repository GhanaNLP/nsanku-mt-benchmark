EDWIRƐ BƆ KEN KIUCHI HANE Ɔ
<<<P>>>
NGƆLE Brooklyn Bɛtɛl bɔ ɔwɔ New York, U.S.A ne, na bɛhane kyɛ ngɔnyɛ adwuma wɔ nekaa bɔ bɛkekã ngrataa bɛbɔnu maa ɔbayɛ nwomaa ne. Kyẽa bɔ olimoa bɔ ngɔle berɛ ne, né menye so atã. Ofikyɛ ne ngaen nemɔ yɛ dede, ngrataa mmaammaa koso to petẽ ewienu, na né patro bɔ bɛfa begua ngaen nanu ne koso pambaen ale berɛ mukoraati.
<<<P>>>
Nningyein pee yeɛ menye gyele yenwo wɔ Bɛtɛl berɛ ɔ. Nu kɔ̃ bɔ ɔhane m’ahone paa yeɛ ole, aliemaamɔ mmrandeɛ ne ndaluwa bɔ ne bɛyɛ adwuma wɔ berɛ ne. Adwuma kɔ̃ nala yeɛ ne daa bɛyɛ ye ɔ. Afei koso ne mmenia nhoahoa bɛ, anaa bɛmmɔ b’abaso wɔ bɔ bɛyɛ nanwo. Nakoso, daa ekonwu bɛ ne, ne begye gua so. Yeti annea a, ɛkɔhã kyɛ gyamia belle haw po. Sɛ bɛkã kyɛ ebie brɛ yenwo ase, na ɔsõ Nyameɛ nahorɛ nu a, sɔ mmerɛ ne paa yeɛ mmandele yebo ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Afoɛ pee ɛhe yeɛ Yehowa atete me maa maayɛ ebie bɔ ɔbrɛ yenwo ase ɔ. Nakoso, ɛmɔma nnimoa nga bɔ ɔyɛle a, ebie bɔ ne nde Buddaniɛ ne, mmanwuni nahorɛ ne ɔ.

MBENANE NAHORƐ NYAMEƐ NE SI ATEE MMERƐ BƆ NE NDE KÃA NE
<<<P>>>
Bɛwole me wɔ Chicago, Illinois, U.S.A. Ne yɛte ngwalaa nna, na me yeɛ nde panyi ɔ. Me maame ne me baba tu fili Japan bɛɛtenane America, ofikyɛ ne besusu kyɛ ɛberɛ yeɛ asetena kɔhɔ yé kɔma bɛ, ná bɛ mmaamɔ koso anya sukuu pá ahɔ maa wasi bɛ yé kyẽabie ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Né me maame ne me baba mva Budda soen nni angorɛ. Maame po de, ne Budda asɔre sua gyi b’awuro, na né ye baba ne oliemamɔ mmrienzua nyɔ te Budda asɔfo. Yeti Budda soen nu ala yeɛ bɛwole yɛ bɛtole ɔ. Sɛ mepɔno sukuu mebaadwu awuro a, bɔ ne me d проверьте moa meyɛ yeɛ ole kyɛ, ngɔyera insɛnse wɔ Budda bosoen ne nyunu, na masa mama bosoen ne aleɛ ne nzue. Sɛ odwu Kwasida biala a, né yɛkɔ Budda asɔre sua nu yɛkɔbɔ mbaeɛ wɔ bosoen ne nyunu. Bosoen bɔ ɔwɔ asɔre sua nanu ne, ɛyɛ a ɔte piri tra bɔ yele ye wɔ awuro ne, na sikaa kɔkorɛ (gold) yeɛ ne bafa bayɛ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Ne bahyirehyire me wɔ Budda soen nu kyɛ, sɛ mewu a ngɔkakyi ɛnwomee na maatena nekaabie bɔ mengɔwa ngoa nu kɔ̃ kyẽabie ɔ. Ɔba sɔ a, asetena fɛɛfɛɛ bɔ ɔwɔ aseɛ he aso ne, ne mengonya kɔ̃. Sɛ medwenedwene ɛhe anwo a, ne ɔmaa mewora bɔ paa. Ofikyɛ ne menwu ye kyɛ sɛ mewu a, nné anyelasoɛ biala. Yeti, mekae kyɛ ɛlɛho bie ngogyinane bosoen ne nyunu nneane ye hãa, na mmisale menwo kyɛ: ‘Yeti mewu a, nzu yeɛ ɔkɔto me ɔ?’ Sɔ mmerɛ ne, ne nwɔ afoɛ nzoo.
<<<P>>>
Nyane kɔyɛ afoɛ 15 ne, nwuni kyɛ Buddafoɛ amaneɛ biemɔ bɔ ne mefa menwo mewura nu ne, ndeaseɛ biala nne nu. Yeti ne menwu ye kyɛ mehia nikyeebie wɔ m’asetena nu. (Mat. 5:3) Yeɛ ole kyɛ, ne medé medi kyɛ Nyameɛ bie bɔ ole tumi wɔ berɛ, na ne mepena kyɛ menwu ye. Edwirɛ biemɔ bɔ ne ɔkye m’adwene ne, ne mese kyɛ Bible ne kɔhora kɔye yeni oa.
