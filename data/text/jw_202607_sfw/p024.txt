Afei koso, bɛbɔ bɛyɛ bɔne bɔ odikyɛ Yehowa twẽ bɛ so ne, ɔmaa bɛfa bɛnwo bedi anaa?’ (Ɛks. 34:6, 7) Odwu mmerɛ bie a, bɔ yeti yeɛ Yehowa yɛle nikyeebie ne, Bible ne ngyirekyire nu mukoraati ngyire yɛ. Nakoso yɛmma ɔnha yɛ, ofikyɛ yɛse kyɛ yɛ Nyameɛ ne de, ɔyɛ bɔ ɔtengyɛ mmerɛ biala.

14 Yede, ɛmɔma yɛnea nikyee foforɛ bɔ ɔkɔboka wɔ Bible suaniɛ ne, maa ɔkɔfa bɔ wasua wafa Yehowa nwo ne kɔyɛ adwuma ɔ. Ebiaa wɔ Bible suaniɛ bie ahyɛ yebo kyɛ ɔba adesua, nakoso aliemaa bie ayɛ ye nikyee maa wayɛ ye yea, yeti sɔ aliemaa ne nwo yɛ ye ahi. Hyɛ ye ngunaen ma osusu kyɛbɔ Yehowa si nwu aliemaamɔ nanwo. Ɔkɔhora kobisa yenwo kyɛ: ‘Nzu yeɛ Yehowa ayɛ afa ahyire kyɛ okuro aliemaamɔ dwirɛ ɔ? (Yoh. 3:16) Kɔyɛlɛ bɔ ɔwɔ asafo nanu ne, sɛɛ yeɛ yenwo hia Yehowa ɔ? (Edw. 133:1) Sɛ Yehowa nwu ye kyɛ yɛbɔ yɛnwo mmɔden maa asomdwee tena asafo nanu a, sɛɛ yeɛ ɔte nga ɔ?’ (2 Kor. 13:11) Ɛyɛ a boka wɔ Bible suaniɛ ne ma onwu ye kyɛ, sɛ ɔfa ye adwene sie subaen pá bɔ ɔwɔ aliemaamɔ nwo naso, tra kyɛ ɔkɔfa kosie bɔ ɔttemaye naso a, ɔmaa Yehowa nye gye paa.

15 Ɛkɔhora koso kɔboka wɔ Bible suaniɛ ne maa okogyina bɔ wasua wafa Yehowa nwo naso, okosi gyinayɛɛ ahoroɛ wɔ ye asetena nu. Ɛkɔhora kɔmaa ɔkɔnea edwirɛ bɔ yenwa, “Kyɛbɔ Ekosisi Gyinaeɛ Pá Ɔ,” bɔ ɔwɔ Nya Asetena Nu Anigye Daa! nwomaa ne adesua 35 ne. Ebiaa ne wɔ Bible suaniɛ ne abɔ edwirɛ nanwo mbaeɛ na wayɛ yenwo nhwehwɛmuɛ. Nakoso onwuni kyɛ tworɔnzɛm bɔ ɔfa ye dwirɛ nanwo ne, ɔsone soma yeti wayɛ ye nye so nwonworaan, na onnwu gyinayɛɛ po bɔ osi ɔ. Ɔba sɔ a, ɛkɔyɛ sɛɛ ne aboka ye? Ɛkɔhora kɔhyɛ ye ngunaen maa okosusu atee bɔ ne Yesu fa so si ye gyinayɛɛ nanwo. Yede ne ɔyɛ ye sɛ? Yebɔbɔ ɔhane kyɛ: ‘Meyɔ bɔ ɔsɔ me Baba nye mmerɛ biala.’ (Kenga Yohane 8:29.) Hyɛ wɔ Bible suaniɛ ne ngunaen ma obisa yenwo kyɛ, ‘Sɛ menea bɔ masua mafa Yehowa nwo, ɔne nhwehwemuɛ bɔ mayɛ ne a, gyinayɛɛ beni yeɛ mesi a ɔkɔmaa Yehowa nye kɔgye ɔ?’ Ma onwu ye kyɛ gyinayɛɛ biala bɔ Yehowa nye gye yenwo ne, ɔte gyinayɛɛ pá.
