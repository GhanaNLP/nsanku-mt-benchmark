Ɛne Eliema Ne Besiesie B’afĩa Ndɛ!
<<<P>>>
ASO aliemaa bie ayɛ wɔ nikyee maa wayɛ wɔ yea paa maa yesɔ nati, ɛne ye b’afĩa asɛkye anaa? Sɛ sɔ a, ye de bɔ mmɔden kyɛ ɛne ye kosiesie ɛmɔ afĩa ndɛ. Sɛ ɛkwaayɛ sɔ a, ɛyɛ a fa w’adwene sie asomdwee bɔ ɛpena kyɛ ɔba ɛne sona ne afĩa naso mmom.*
<<<P>>>
Wɔde ma yɛnea mvandoho he. Sɛ ebie gya nu bu na sɛ ɔkɔ dɔktaniɛ berɛ a, ɔkɔhora kɔnea ye maa yegya ne kɔtɔa. Nakoso sɛ waangɔ na ɔtenatena yenwo a, ɔkɔhora kɔha ye paa. Sɛ wannea yé a, ɔngɔhora nate kɔ̃ po.
<<<P>>>
Kyɛbɔ sɛ yegya nu bu a yɛkɔ dɔktaniɛ berɛ maa ɔnea yɛ ndɛ ne, sɔ ala yeɛ sɛ yenwu ye kyɛ yɛne yeliema bie afĩa asɛkye a odikyɛ yɛne ye siesie y’afĩa ndɛ ɔ. Sɛ yanyɛ sɔ a, ɔkɔmaa yɛkɔfa aliemaa nanwo dwirɛ yekowura yɛ nu. Ɛhene kɔmaa yɛne ye afĩa kɔsɛkye koraa. Nakoso Yesu hane kyɛ: “Ɛne ye bésiesie ɛmɔ afĩa ndɛndɛ.” (Mat. 5:25) Yeti kɔ wɔ damvo ne berɛ na kã bɔ ɔha wɔ nanwo dwirɛ kyire ye, na sɛ ɛne ye bedwudwo a, ɛyɛ a to wɔ boase. Nakoso ɛyɛ a ɔtaa yɛ se ma yɛ paa kyɛ yɛkɔyɛ sɔ ɔ, nzuati ɔ? Ɛmɔma yɛnea nningyein nyɔ biemɔ bɔ yeti ɔmaa yɛkã sɔ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Bɔ olimoa ne, ebiaa ne enguro kyɛ ɛkɔhã edwirɛ bɔ wasi nanwo hwee. Ɔba sɔ a, ɔkɔhora kɔyɛ se kyɛ ɛkɔhɔ sona ne berɛ maa ɛmɔkosiesie ɛmɔ afĩa ɔ.# Sɔ dwirɛ bɔ yɛkã ye, ebie yeɛ ɔtole Yiwodia ne Sintike ne. Ne bɛte Kristofoɛ wɔ asomafoɛ nemɔ aberɛ so. Edwirɛ bie wale b’afĩa bɔ ne bɛmbena kyɛ besiesie ɔ. Yeti bɛtenatena yenwo alaa maa somafoɛ Pɔɔlo tele yenwo dwirɛ, mmerɛ po bɔ ne ɔwɔ Rome ne. Ɛhene ati, otuli bɛ foɛ kyɛ bɛyɛ bɔ bɛkɔhora biala kyɛ bɛkɔmaa asomdwee kɔwa b’afĩa. (Flp. 4:2) Sɛ ne bɛbrɛ bɛnwo ase, ebiala fi ye ahone nu kã bɔ ɔha ye nanwo dwirɛ, ná bɛfa bɛkyɛ bɛnwo nwo a, ne ɔkɔmaa bekosa bɛkɔyɛ adamvoa bieku.
<<<P>>>
Bɔ ɔtɔ so nyɔ, ebiaa w’adwene kɔyɛ wɔ kyɛ nna wɔ yeɛ mvomsoɛ ne fi wɔ. Yeti wɔ damvo ne mmom yeɛ odikyɛ ɔba wɔ gya so ɔ. Nakoso nwu ye kyɛ, bɔ yenwo hia yeɛ ole kyɛ ɛne wɔ damvo ne afĩa kosa kɔyɛ kama ɔ, na mmom nna bɔ odi fɔ, anaa bɔ odi bẽ ɔ. Sɛ ebie bowue nu bu na dɔktaniɛ nea ye a, bɔ ɔkɔyɛ a sona ne nwo kɔtɔ ye ne, ɛhene yeɛ dɔktaniɛ ne fa ye adwene kɔ so ɔ. Na mmom nna bɔ ɔyɛle a sona ne purale ne, yeɛ okobisabisa yenwo dwirɛ ɔ. Sɔ ala yeɛ sɛ ɛkɔ sona ne berɛ a, nna bekye bɔ yeɛ ɛkwaahyire bɔ odi fɔ ɔ. Na mmom bɔ ohia wɔ yeɛ ole kyɛ ɛne ye kosiesie ɛmɔ afĩa maa asomdwee kɔwa ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Bible ne tu yɛ foɛ kyɛ, “Nnɛmɔma eyia kɔtɔ ɛmɔ yaafɛlɛ so.” (Ɛfe. 4:26) Yeti sɛ ɛne aliemaa bie le edwirɛ a, ɛne ye behã ye ndɛ, kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a ɛmɔ afĩa ngɔsɛkye ɔ. Sɛ ɛkora yɛ sɔ a, ne ɛboka maa “asomdwee” tena asafo nanu.—Ɛfe. 4:1-3.
<<<P>>>
* Edwirɛ mmaammaa biemɔ wɔ berɛ a, ebiaa yɛkɔhora yɛne sona ne kɔhã yenwo dwirɛ, na yayakyi maa waha. (Mat. 5:23, 24) Nakoso edwirɛ ne bie wɔ berɛ a, ɔte piri. Ɛhene de sɛ ɔba ye sɔ a, odikyɛ ɛfa edwirɛ bɔ ɔwɔ Mateo 18:15, 16 ne yɛ adwuma. Sɛ ɛhene si ne, edwirɛ ne ambɛ a, ɛkɔhora kɔmaa mbanyi nemɔ kɔboka wɔ.—Mat. 18:17.

# Sɛ yeliema bie yɛ yɛ nikyeebie, na yenwu ye kyɛ ɔnhyɛda nha adwene a, ɔnate kyɛ ɔte yɛ damvo nati, yɛkɔhora yekobu yɛnye yekogua so.—Any. 26:20.
