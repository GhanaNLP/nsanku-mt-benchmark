BƆ ADESŨA NE KƆHÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ
Yekonwu kyɛbɔ yɛkɔboka yɛ Bible asuafoɛ nemɔ maa bekonwu Yehowa yé ɔ. Yekosa yekonwu kyɛbɔ yɛkɔboka bɛ maa bekonwu ye subaen ahoroɛ ne, bɔ okuro, ɔne bɔ onguro ɔ.
<<<P>>>
SƐ ƐNE ebie sua nikye, na ɔhyɛ yebo kyɛ ɔba adesua a, ɔmaa wɔnye gye paa. Na sɛ ɔhyɛ yebo ɔma mmuayɛɛ, ɔkã bɔ osua ne bie kyire mmenia, na siɛ ye ɔbɔ asu a, kyɛbɔ wɔnye si gye ne de bɛnga. Ɔba sɔ a, ɛfa yenwo animnyam ma Yehowa, ofikyɛ ɛse kyɛ ye yeɛ waboka wɔ suaniɛ ne maa wayɛ ɛhe mukoraati ɔ.—1 Kor. 3:5-9; 3 Yoh. 3, 4.
<<<P>>>
2 Yehowa ‘kuro kyɛ mmenia amukoraa benya daa ngoa ná benya nahorɛ nimdeɛ bɔ odimu ɔ.’ (Kenga 1 Timoteo 2:3, 4.) Ɔpena kyɛ ebiala nwu ye yé, na besa benwu bɔ wabɔ ye tianu kyɛ ɔkɔyɛ kɔma yɛ ɔ. (Yer. 9:24) Sɛ ɛhe kɔyɛ yé a, sana benya yenwo nimdeɛ bɔ odimu. Yede nzu yeɛ ole sɔ ‘nimdeɛ bɔ odimu’ ne? Yeɛ ole kyɛ ebie kosua Yehowa nwo nikye, na wafa bɔ osua ne wabɔ ye bra, na mmom nna kyɛ okosua na wanya yenwo nimdeɛ mbaen. Sɛ yɛ Bible asuafoɛ banwu Yehowa yé a, ɔmaa bɛkora bepingye ye, na bɛsesa bɛ subaen. Ɛhene boka bɛ maa benya anyelasoɛ kyɛ bekonya “daa ngoa.”—Yoh. 17:3.
<<<P>>>
3 Yehowa afa awura yɛsa kyɛ yɛboka mmenia ma benwu ye yé. Yeti wɔ adesua he anu ne, yekosusu sɔ dwirɛ hemɔ nwo. Yeɛ ole kyɛ, nzuati yeɛ yenwo hia kyɛ yɛ Bible asuafoɛ nemɔ nwu
<<<P>>>
1. Sɛ enwu ye kyɛ wɔ Bible suaniɛ nya ngɔsoɛ a, sɛɛ yeɛ ɛte nga ɔ?
<<<P>>>
2. Nzu yeɛ Yehowa pena kyɛ yɛ Bible asuafoɛ nemɔ nya ye ɔ? (1 Timoteo 2:3, 4)
<<<P>>>
3. Nzu yeɛ yekosusu yenwo wɔ adesua he anu ɔ?
