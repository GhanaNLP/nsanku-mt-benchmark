12 Sɛ y’abusuaniɛ anaa yɛ damvo bie wu a, ɛyɛ a ɔyɛ yea paa, na ɔkora maa yedi aworabɔlɛ yɛkyɛ. Odwu mmerɛ bie po a, yɛkora yebisa yɛnwo kyɛ: ‘Nzuati yeɛ Yehowa ama atee maa sona ne awu ɔ?’ Bɔ yɛkã ye, ebie tole aliemaa bie bɔ bɛfrɛ ye Michael. Sẽe tɔle nekaabie maa ye maame ne ye baba wuli. Ɔhane kyɛ: “Bɛ wue ne yɛle me yea paa. Ɔwɔ nu, mese kyɛ nna Yehowa yeɛ ɔmaa bewuli ɔ. Nakoso, ne mende yebo kyɛ wammɔ bɛnwo waen ɔ. Yeti mvɛle ye so yaa.” Ebiaa wɔ koso anya sɔ adwene ne bie le. Ye de, mmerɛ bɔ yɛ wora abɔ sɔ ne, nzu yeɛ Yehowa fa kyekye yɛ wora ɔ, na anyelasoɛ beni yeɛ ɔmaa yenya ye ɔ?
<<<P>>>
13 Kenga Yesaya 25:8. Yehowa ahyɛ bɔ kyɛ ‘okonunu ewue boso, anaa ɔkɔye kofi berɛ afebɔɔ.’ Wɔde nea kyɛ baye ewue bafi berɛ ɔ. Ɔba sɔ a, w’abusuaniɛ anaa wɔ damvo bie po ngowu na wamaa wɔ wora abɔ. (Yes. 35:10) Sɛ yɛnwoniɛ bie wu na bɛdeda ye, anaa bɛkwaasie ye a, ɛyɛ a yedé yɛnwo a onne. Nakoso sɛ ewue fi berɛ a, yengonwu sɔ nningyein nemɔ kɔ̃. Na mmom daa biala yekonwu kyɛ y’abusuafoɛ ne yɛ damvomɔ wɔ yɛnwo bɔ bele anwoserɛ ɔ. Ɛhene kɔmaa yɛnye kɔgye paa. Yedé yedi paa kyɛ sɔ dwirɛ ne kɔwa nu, ofikyɛ Nyameɛ bɔbɔ deinn yeɛ wahã ɔ, na ye koso de, bɔ ɔkɔhã biala ɔkɔyɛ.—Nye. 21:4, 5.
