12 Sɛɛ yee yɛkɔhora yekosuesua Gibeonfoɛ
nemɔ ɔ? Sɛ yeliemamɔ ne yɛ anni ye yé a, odi-
kyɛ yenya aboterɛ, na yɛfa yɛnwo yɛto Yeho-
wa so. Yɛyɛ sɔ a, okyire kyɛ yedé yedi paa kyɛ,
ye mmerɛ bɔ ɔsɛ nu ne, okoli yɛ dwirɛ kɔma
yɛ. (Yes. 26:3, 4) Sɔ yeɛ yeliema brasua bie bɔ
bɛfrɛ ye Laura French yɛle ɔ. Wɔ 1926 nu ne,
bɛmaa ɔɔyɛle adwuma wɔ Canada Bɛtɛl. Ɔyɛ-
le adwuma wɔ berɛ kɔyɛ afoɛ buru, na ɛhene si
ne bɛfale edwirɛ bie bɛtole ye so. Yeɛ ole kyɛ,
ne bɛnwa wafa yenwo wabɔ awaefoɛ. Ɛhene
ati bɛmaa ofili Bɛtɛl berɛ. Edwirɛ ne yɛle ye
yea paa, nakoso nzu yeɛ ɔyɛle ɔ? Wanga asafo
nanwo dwirɛ tɛɛ, na mmom ɔyerele yenwo
yɛle ateepakyelɛ adwuma ne afoɛ nna. Afei wɔ
1940 nu ne, besa bɛtole bɛsa bɛfrɛle ye kyɛ
ɔɔyɛ adwuma wɔ Bɛtɛl berɛ, na ɛhene maa ye-
nye gyele paa. Ɔyɛle adwuma wɔ berɛ afoɛ 50
oodwuli kyɛ owuli ɔ. Kyɛbɔ aliemaa brasua ne
yɛle ne, yɛ koso sɛ bɛne yɛ anni ye yé a, nnɛ-
mɔmaa y’abanu bu, na mmom ɛmɔma yɛyere
yɛnwo wɔ Yehowa soen nu.

13 Ɔhene Dawide wuli kɔyɛ afoɛ 500 si ne,
Bible ne sa hane Gibeonfoɛ nemɔ nwo dwirɛ.
Sɔ mmerɛ ne, afoɛ 70 bɔ ne Yudafoɛ nemɔ afa
aayɛ ngoaa wɔ Babilɔn ne, ne wasɔ̃. Yeti wɔ
afoɛ 537 K.Y.B ne, Yudafoɛ ne biemɔ ne Seru-
babɛl fili Babilɔn sa wale Yerusalem. (Ɛsra 2:
1, 2, 58) Ɛhene si kɔyɛ afoɛ 70 ne, bɛnu biemɔ
sa wale Yerusalem. Nakoso sɔ mmerɛ he de,
bɛne Ɛsra yeɛ bɛwale ɔ. (Ɛsra 7:1-7) Ɔwɔ nu,
Yudafoɛ bɔ bɛne Serubabɛl ne Ɛsra fili Babi-
lɔn wale ne, banzõ. Nakoso, mmenia biemɔ bɔ
ne bɛte ‘asɔre sua nu somvoɛ’ ne, bɛ koso bɛ-
wale bie. (Ɛzra 2:58; 7:7) Sɔ mmenia nemɔ, ne
bɛtte Yisraelfoɛ, na mmom ɔkɔyɛ kyɛ né bɛte
Gibeonfoɛ nemɔ bosofoɛ.

14 Kenga 1 Aberɛso Dwirɛ 9:2. Yudafoɛ bɔ
ne bɛwɔ Babilɔn ne, ne ɔyɛ se ma bɛnu pee
nala kyɛ bekosa bɛkɔhɔ Yerusalem, na baakye-
kye kuro ne bieku ɔ. Bɔ yeti yeɛ ɔte sɔ yeɛ ole
kyɛ, ne banya esikaa, na ne basisi awuromɔ
po. Nakoso Gibeonfoɛ nemɔ, anaa ‘asɔre sua
nu somvoɛ’ nemɔ de, wanyɛ se wamma bɛ kyɛ
bɛkɔhɔ ɔ, ofikyɛ bɛbɔ belimoa bɔ bɛsa bɛhɔle
Yerusalem ne po, ne bɛboka bɛ so. Sɔ bɔ bɛyɛ-
le ne kyire kyɛ né bele gyidie paa. Ɔnate kyɛ
ne bɛtte Yisraelfoɛ nati, ne bɛ bosofoɛ nemɔ
lle asee biala wɔ Yisrael. Nakoso, bemiane bɛ-
nye bɛne Yudafoɛ bɔ ne bedi nahorɛ ne sa hɔle
Yerusalem. Ofikyɛ ne ɔyɛ bɛ fɛ paa kyɛ bɛkɔ-
boka maa bekosa bekosi asɔre awuro ne, na
bahora bahyɛ yebo bayɛ adwuma bɔ ne bɛyɛ
ye wɔ berɛ ne bieku ɔ.—Neh. 3:26

koso onni bɛnwo angorɛ koraa, na ɔmaa bɛsa

aberɛ so ne, wanyakyi bɛ nu, na mmom ɔneane

dafoɛ fili ngoasom nu bɛwale ne koso, Yehowa
