15 Ɔwɔ nu, yedé yedi kyɛ ɛbɔ bɔ Nyameɛ ahyɛ kyɛ okotingye bɛbɔ bawu ne, ɔkɔwa nu. Nakoso, sɛ yɛnwoniɛ bie wu a, ɔyɛ sɛɛ alaa a yekoli yealɛ. Ye mukoraati nu ne, yɛse kyɛ Yehowa wora filli bɛ, na ɛhene kyekye yɛ wora paa. Sɔ anyelasoɛ ne yeɛ waboka Michael bɔ yalimoa yahã yenwo dwirɛ ne. Ɔhane kyɛ: “Edwirɛ bɔ ɔwɔ Yesaya 26:19 ne, ɔmaa nwuni kyɛ m’awofoɛ bɔ bawu ne, bɛnwo dwirɛ té wɔ Yehowa tianu ofikyɛ okuro bɛ dwirɛ. Yeti ye wora ngofi bɛ lle, kodwu kyɛ ye mmerɛ ne kɔsõ maa okotingye bɛ ɔ. Sɔ dwirɛ ne bɔ ndwenedwenene yenwo ne, wakyekye me wora paa.” Yɛ koso mmerɛ bɔ yekosa yekonwu y’abusuafoɛ ne yɛ damvomɔ bɔ bawu ne, ɛmɔma yɛhɔso yɛdwenedwene yenwo. Yɛyɛ yɛyɛ sɔ a, ɔkɔmaa yɛ wora kɔkyekye.
<<<P>>>
16 Sɛ edwirɛ bie bɔ yɛnnea atee to yɛ, bɔne bie bɔ yayɛ ne, yenwo adwenedwene té hyɛ yɛso, anaa yɛnwoniɛ bie awu maa yedi yealɛ a, edwirɛ kãa bɔ yasusu yenwo wɔ Yesaya nwomaa nanu ne, ɔkɔhora kɔboka yɛ. Yɛda Yehowa ase paa kyɛ ɔnate ye dwirɛ naso kyekye yɛ wora maa y’ahone tɔ yɛ kunu ɔ. (Edw. 94:19) Sɛ edwirɛ bie to wɔ a, ebiaa ele tworɔnzɛm bie bɔ ɔkyekye wɔ wora ɔ. Sɛ sɔ a, ye de kye gua wɔ tianu, anaa tworɔ gua krataa so, na fa ta nekaa bɔ wɔnye kɔbɔ so ndɛ ɔ. Yehowa, wɔ Baba bɔ ɔwɔ anwuro ne, okuro wɔ dwirɛ paa. Yeti ma ɔfa ye dwirɛ naso ɔkyekye wɔ wora mmerɛ biala.
