ɔkɔboka yɛ ɔ. (Edw. 119:105; Yes. 30:21) Osa koso nate yeliemamɔ Kristofoɛ, ɔne mmeni foforɛ so boka yɛ. Yeti sɛ bɔ wato yɛ ne, wa-maa yɛkunu tete yɛ a, Yehowa ngɔfɛ yɛ so yaa, na mmom okonya yɛnwo aboterɛ, na waboka yɛ ngakangaka maa yahora yagyina ɔhaw ne noa.—Yes. 41:10; 1 Pet. 5:6, 7.

6 Joseph bɔ yalimoa yahã yenwo dwirɛ ne hane kyɛ mmerɛ bɔ ɔne ɔye fili Bɛtel ne, Yehowa anyakyi bɛ nu. Atee bɔ Yehowa fale so bokale bɛ ne, ohyirele nu kyɛ: “Mmerɛ bɔ sɔ nzakrayɛɛ ne wale ne, ne yehia ebie bɔ ɔkɔhyɛ yɛ ngunaen ɔ, na Yehowa nate aliemaamɔ so yɛle sɔ mane yɛ. Afei bɔ ne yekoli, bɔ yekowura, ɔne nekaa bɔ yɛkɔla ne koso Yehowa maa yɛsa hane yɛ. Ɛnnɛ ne ɛnnɛ mukoraati Yehowa té nea yɛ.” Wɔ koso edwirɛ bɔ wato wɔ ne, sɛ ɔyɛ se sɛɛ po a, nya gyidie kyɛ Yehowa kɔma wɔ anwoserɛ bɔ ɛkɔfa kogyina yenoa ɔ. Na okosa kɔma wɔ akwangyerɛ bɔ ɔkɔboka wɔ ɔ. (Yes. 54:10; 58:11) Joseph sa hane kyɛ: “Edwein bɔ ɔnne yɛ dwein nwomaa nanu ne, kɔ̃ bie bokale yɛ paa. Nu dwirɛ ne maa yenwuni kyɛ bɔ ɔkɔto yɛ biala, Yehowa ngɔyakyi yɛnu.* Yaanwu ye paa kyɛ, sɛ nningyein angɔ kyɛbɔ yɛnea atee po a, yele gyidie kyɛ ɔkɔboka yɛ.”

7 Sɛ yɛyɛ bɔne pirikua bie a, yɛbɔ mbanyi nemɔ amaneɛ, na ɔnate kyɛ bekuro yɛ dwirɛ nati bɛboka yɛ. Ɛhene si ne, yɛbɔ mmɔden kyɛ yɛngɔyɛ sɔ nikyee ne kɔ̃. Ɔba sɔ a, Yehowa koso nwu yɛ anwumvoin, na ɔfa yɛ bɔne kyɛ yɛ. (Any. 28:13; Yak. 5:14-16; 1 Yoh. 1:9) Nakoso ɛhene po ne, eyɛ a ne yenwo dwirɛ té ha yɛ. Yeti ebiaa yɛkɔhã ye wɔ yɛ tianu kyɛ, ‘Sɔ bɔne he de, ɔtte nikyee bɔ Yehowa kɔfa kɔhyɛ me lle ɔ.’ (2 Kor. 2:7) Yenya sɔ adwene ne a, ɔnyɛ nwanwa ofikyɛ sɛ yɛyɛ bɔne bie na ɔkyɛ sɛɛ po a, ɛyɛ a ɔyɛ se paa kyɛ yɛkɔye yenwo dwirɛ yekofi yetianu. Bɔ yɛkã ye, ebie tole Ɔhene Dawide. Yeti ɔhane kyɛ: “Ɔnate me bɔne ti, me mmowue nu we me. Me nwo ayɛ me bɛtɛɛ, yeɛ mayɛ hodwoo.” (Edw. 38:3, 8) Sɛ ayɛ bɔne bie na yenwo adwenedwene té hyɛ wɔ so a, Yesaya nwomaa nanu dwirɛ kɔkyekye wɔ wora.

8 Kenga Yesaya 1:18. Yehowa fa nningyein biemɔ yɛ mvandoho maa yenwu kyɛbɔ ɔfa yɛ bɔne kyɛ yɛ koraa ɔ. Ebie yeɛ ole kyɛ, yenwa sɛ yɛ bɔne ne ‘te kyɛ koogyan’ po a, ɔkɔmaa ɔkɔyɛ “fufue kyɛ gyese.” Sɛ nikyee bie te kɔkorɛ tekyɛ koogyan, na ɔpẽ w’ataadeɛ nu a, ɔyɛ se paa kyɛ ɛkɔpo maa okofi nu ɔ. Nakoso Yehowa de, wahyɛ yɛ bɔ kyɛ, yɛ bɔne bɔ wayɛ kɔkorɛ tekyɛ koogyan ne, sɛ ɔfa kyɛ yɛ a, ɔngae ye kɔ̃.

9 Ɔhene Dawide sɔaa ɔfale ebie aye, na osa huni sona. Sɛ ɛnea bɔ Dawide yɛle ne a, ahaa ne ɔfata ewue. Nakoso Yehowa fahyɛle ye, na ye si po ne, ɔhane kyɛ ɔte ebie bɔ odi nahorɛ ɔ. (2 Sam. 11:3, 4, 14, 15; 12:13; 1 Ahe. 9:4, 5) Ɛhene maa yenwu ye kyɛ bɔne bɔ Dawide yɛle ne, nna ɛhene yeɛ ne Yehowa adwene wɔ so ɔ, na mmom nahorɛ bɔ ne odi ma ye ne. Sɔ ala yeɛ yɛ koso, sɛ yɛyɛ bɔne na yenu yɛnwo a, Yehowa fa kyɛ yɛ ɔ. Afei osa boka yɛ maa yɛye yenwo dwirɛ yefi yɛnu, na yahora yafa anigye yasõ ye. Ye de, yɛbaanea edwirɛ foforɛ bie bɔ ɔwɔ Yesaya nwomaa nanu ɔ. Ɛhene koso kɔboka yɛ mmerɛ bɔ y’adwene té yɛ yɛ kyɛ Yehowa falle yɛ bɔne hyɛlle yɛ ne.
