BƐBƆ BƐSE YEHOWA YÉ NE, NHYIRA BƆ BEKONYA Ɔ
<<<P>>>
17 Sɛ yɛne yɛ Bible asuafoɛ nemɔ hyɛ yebo kyɛ yesua nikyee a, ɛyɛ a yekyire bɛ nningyein pee. Ebie fa bɛbɔ bawuwu, Yesu ngoa bɔ ɔfa bɔle afɔleɛ mane yɛ, ɔne Nyameɛ Ahennie nanwo. Besa koso besua kyɛbɔ bahyehyɛ asafo nanwo nikye. Ebie yeɛ ole bɔ ɔkɔ so wɔ yɛ adesua bo, adwuma bɔ asafo nu mbanyi nemɔ yɛ, ɔne Akwangyerɛ Kue ne. Ɔwɔ nu, yɛpena kyɛ yɛ Bible asuafoɛ nemɔ sua ɛhemɔ nwo nikye, nakoso bɔ ohia yɛ paa yeɛ ole kyɛ bekonwu Yehowa yé ɔ.
<<<P>>>
18 Ɔte yenwo atee pirikua paa yeɛ yanya ye kyɛ yekyirekyire mmenia maa benwu Yehowa ɔ. Bɔ yeti yeɛ ɔte sɔ yeɛ ole kyɛ, sɛ sɔ mmenia nemɔ basõ Yehowa a, ɛnnɛ po bɛnye gye. (Edw. 25:12-15) Afei, bɛbɔ bele Yehowa ‘nwo nimdeɛ’ ne, kyẽabie koso bɛkɔtena ase daa. (1 Yoh. 5:20) Nakoso nna ɛhene ngome, na mmom bɛbɔ bɛyere bɛnwo kyɛ bekonwu Yehowa, na bahyire kyɛ bɛdɔ ye ne, yekoso ‘ɔse bɛ.’ (1 Kor. 8:3) Yehowa de bɔ yɛyɛ biala onwu ye, na bɛbɔ bɛte ye damvomɔ ne koso ɔse bɛ paa. Wɔde nea kyɛbɔ osi yɛ nyemene kyɛ ese nahorɛ Nyameɛ komaapɛ ne ɔ!
