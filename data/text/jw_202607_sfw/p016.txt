9 Bible ne kã kyɛ yenwu Yehowa wɔ nikyeebiala bɔ yɛyɛ nu. Bɔ ɛhene kyire yeɛ ole kyɛ, bɔ yasua yafa Yehowa nwo ne, yɛngɔma yɛwora ngofi. Afei koso kora bɔ yɛkɔyɛ nikyeebie ne, yekobisa yɛnwo kyɛ: ‘Aso ɔkɔmaa Yehowa nye kɔgye, anaa ye wora kɔbɔ?’ Yɛyɛ sɔ a, ɔkɔboka yɛ maa yekosi gyinayɛɛ pá. (Any. 3:5, 6) Wɔde ma yɛnea mvandoho he. Aliemaamɔ pee wɔ berɛ a, ɛyɛ a bɛfa behu anaa bɛye foto bɛto bɛ fon so, anaa bɛfa besie nekaa bɔ daa bekonwu ye ɔ. Ɛhene ati ɛyɛ a ne behu anaa bɛye nanwo dwirɛ wɔ bɛ tianu, yeti sɛ bɛbaayɛ nikyeebie a, bɛnea kyɛ ɔngɔha behu anaa bɛye ne. Yɛ koso, bɔ yasua yafa Yehowa nwo ne, sɛ daa yɛdwenedwene yenwo a, ɔkɔmaa yɛkɔyere yɛnwo kyɛ yɛkɔyɛ bɔ yenye gye yenwo ɔ.—Edw. 19:13, 14.

10 Yehowa bɔle yɛ wɔ ye subaen so. Ɛhene ati yɛkora yesuesua ye. (Gye. 1:26) Sɛ daa yesua Yehowa subaen ahoroɛ nanwo nikyee a, ɔkɔmaa yekonwu kyɛbɔ yekosuesua ye ɔ. Yɛnea atworɔfoɛ ne Farasiifoɛ nemɔ sɔa a, bɛde banzuesua Yehowa, yeti Yesu hane bɛ nyunu.

10. Sɛ daa yesua Yehowa nwo nikyee a, nzu yeɛ ɛhene kɔboka yɛ maa yɛkɔyɛ ɔ?
<<<P>>>
Bɔ ne bɛyɛ yeɛ ole kyɛ, edwirɛ bɔ ne yenwo hia paa wɔ Mmraa nanu bɔ yeɛ ole ‘pɛpɛɛpɛyɛlɛ, anwumvoinnwunɛ, ɔne nahorɛ bɔ bekoli’ ne de, ne bɛmva bɛnyɛ hwee. Na mmom edwirɛ mmaammaa biemɔ bɔ ne ɔwɔ Mmraa nanu ne, yeɛ ne basɔ nu twann ɔ. (Mat. 23:23) Yɛde yɛmbena kyɛ yɛyɛ kyɛ sɔ mmenia nemɔ. Yeti ɛmɔma yɛbɔ mmɔden kyɛ yekonwu bɔ yeti yeɛ Yehowa ahyehyɛ mmraa biemɔ ne. Yɛyɛ sɔ a, yekonwu Yehowa subaen ne bie, na yasuesua ye.—1 Yoh. 4:8, 11.

YƐKƆYƐ SƐƐ NA YANWU YEHOWA YÉ?
<<<P>>>
11 Ɔyɛle sɛɛ yeɛ eenwuni Yehowa ɔ? Ebiaa ne w’awofoɛ te Yehowa Adanzefoɛ, yeti ne bɛtaa bɛkã Bible nu dwirɛ biemɔ bekyire wɔ, na ɛhene maa enwuni Yehowa subaen ne bie. Afei bɛmaa enwuni kyɛ, nningyein bɔ wabɔ ne, ɛhene koso maa yenwu kyɛbɔ osi te ɔ. Anaa wɔde ebiaa Yehowa Danzeniɛ bie yeɛ ɔne wɔ suane nikyee ɔ. Ebiaa ɔne wɔ suane Nya Asetena Nu Anigye Daa! nwomaa ne. Na tworɔnzɛm biemɔ bɔ ɔmaa yenwu Yehowa subaen ne bie ne, ɔne wɔ susuli yenwo, na ɛhene maa enwuni kyɛbɔ Yehowa si te ɔ. Afei ɔmaa enwuni kyɛ, nningyein bɔ Nyameɛ abɔ ne koso, sɛ esusu yenwo a, ɔkɔboka wɔ maa ekonwu ye yé.* (Neh. 8:8; Aso. 14:17) Yede ɛmɔma yɛnea bɔ sɛ yɛkɔso yɛyɛ a, ɔkɔmaa yekonwu Yehowa yé ɔ.
