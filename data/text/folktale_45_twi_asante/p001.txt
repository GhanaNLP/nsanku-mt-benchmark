KOKOSAKYI (OPETE) KASA KYERE 'BONEKYEREFO A, OTE NO ABEBUOM'
<<<P>>>
'BONEKYEREFO na owoo ho, na ye'besee no se n'aberewa awu. 'Bonekyerefo suye, suye, suye, na osiim' kosere seree Kokosakyi, Akoko, Odwan, Okra ne 'Kraman se ye'mma yenko gya no oni ayie. Mmoa no nnyina siim'; na ye'ne no koye. Ye'ko duruye, na y'aye ayie no awie, ato se ye'bedware. Ye'ko ka kyeree 'Bonokyerefo se, "Dware ma yen so ye'nnware bi." 'Bonekyerefo see, "Me, m'aberewa da fam', me nni adaduanan nna a, me'nnware da." Ne nnamfonom se, "Amanee a y'ahuno ni?" Ye'sore da a, na y'ayase e'te, na 'Bonekyerefo so die, ne ho dendenden. Dakoro bi, Kokosakyi see, "A! 'Bonekyerefo no no, me nim no; sese oni amu no na osore ko wee." Anwumere Kokosakyi tuu fa ko sii dua tenten bi so, wo samanpom' ho, obefwe se 'Bonekyerefo na kokota! kokota! Na obaye, na oko amu-kofie so, dwiri! dwiri! na wayi oni amu na oko ho, o'we. Opete si ho. Osee, "'Bonekyerefo, mo! ye'da w'ase mumu aseda." 'Bonekyerefo see, "Me sere wo, yeidie mmo so; asem biara a wo beka akyere me, mete asee mama wo ntem." Opete tuu fa baa 'fie. Ankyere na 'Bonekyerefo baye. Opete ka kyeree ne nnamfonom se, "Nne edan se oboo a, ye'bedidi; efei so ye'beko ye'kurom'." Mmoa no see, "Ampa?" Opete see, "Mo die mo nfwe me ara." Opete see, "Mo mmera mma yenko." Ye'siim' koo 'Bonekyerefo 'fie. Opete see, "'Bonekyerefo, tie ma me nse wo, nne die ye'pe se ye'didie, na wo so didi, na efei so ye'be ko ye'kurom' nne, na wo se sen?" 'Bonekyerefo see, "Me pene so, medidi ama mo adidi bi." Seisei na 'Bonekyerefo ako kyere adwan mmiensa de abo nkonhama de ama ne nnamfonom adi, na ono so adi bi. Na ne nnamfonom siim' koo ye'kurom'. Ene se ye'de yee kasa 'bebuo se, "Kokosakyi kasa kyere 'Bonekyerefo a, ote no abebuom'."
