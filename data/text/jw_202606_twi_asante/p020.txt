Tie Onyankopɔn Asɛm na Nya Nhyira
<<<P>>>
NEA ADESUA YI BƐKA HO ASƐM
<<<P>>>
Sɛ yɛkɔ adesua anaa nhyiam a, nea enti a ɛsɛ sɛ yetie dwumadi no yiye ne ɔkwan a yɛbɛfa so ayɛ saa.
<<<P>>>
Monhwɛ Sɛnea Mutie Asɛm Yiye
<<<P>>>
“Monhwɛ sɛnea mutie asɛm yiye.”—LUKA 8:18.
<<<P>>>
ƐNNƐ, Yehowa ma yɛn honhom fam aduan pii. Wei nti, yɛne Dawid yɛ adwene wɔ asɛm a ɔkae yi ho: “Yehowa ne me Hwɛfo. Hwee renhia me.”—Kenkan Dwom 23:1, 2, 5.
<<<P>>>
2 Ɔkwan baako a Yehowa fa so ma yɛn honhom fam aduan ne sɛ ɔkyerɛkyerɛ yɛn wɔ adesua ase. Sɛ yɛkɔ adesua a, nsɛm a yɛte no pii wɔ hɔ a ɛnyɛ asɛm foforo. Nanso sɛ yɛn ‘ase atim wɔ nokware no mu’ mpo a, ehia sɛ wɔkaakae yɛn. (2 Pet. 1:12) Nneɛma a yenim dedaw no, sɛ ɛkɔba sɛ ɛno ara ho asɛm na wɔreka a, ɛyɛ a ɛyɛ den ma wo sɛ wobɛyɛ aso atie anaa? Sɛ saa a, ɛnde adesua yi bɛboa wo. Ɛbɛma yɛahu nea enti a ehia sɛ yetie asɛm yiye wɔ adesua ase. Afei nso, wɔn a wɔkyerɛkyerɛ wɔ asafo no mu no, yɛbɛhwɛ sɛnea wɔbɛboa afoforo ama wɔatie asɛm yiye. Ɛno da nkyɛn a, yɛbɛhwɛ nneɛma bi a yɛyɛ a ɛbɛboa yɛn ama yɛatie asɛm yiye. Nanso momma yenni kan nyi asɛmmisa yi ano: Nkyerɛkyerɛ a yenya no, efi hena hɔ?
<<<P>>>
NKYERƐKYERƐ A YENYA NO, EFI YEHOWA HƆ
<<<P>>>
3 Yehowa nam n’asɛm Bible no so na ɛkyerɛkyerɛ yɛn. (2 Tim. 3:16, 17) Ná Yesu nim saa. Enti na otumi de Onyankopɔn asɛm no kyerɛkyerɛ yiye ara ma n’atiefo mpo bisae sɛ: “Ɛyɛɛ dɛn na onipa yi nim Kyerɛwnsɛm no saa bere a ɔnkɔɔ sukuu biara?” (Yoh. 7:15) Nanso wɔn a wɔwɔ koma pa no de, bere a wɔtee sɛnea Yesu rekyerɛkyerɛ Onyankopɔn asɛm no mu no, wɔn ani gyei. Kristofo a na wɔwɔ asomafo no bere so te sɛ ɔsomafo Paul ne Apolo sei, sɛ wɔre-
<<<P>>>
1-2. (a) Ɔkwan bɛn na Yehowa fa so ma ne nkurɔfo honhom fam aduan nnɛ? (Dwom 23:1, 2, 5) (b) Dɛn na yebesusuw ho wɔ adesua yi mu?
<<<P>>>
3. Ɔkwan bɛn na Yehowa fa so kyerɛkyerɛ yɛn, na sɛn na n’asomfo de Bible no di dwuma?
