NEA ADESUA YI BƐKA HO ASƐM
Sɔhwɛ biara a yɛbehyia no, sɛ ɛte sɛn oo, sɛ ɛte sɛn oo, yebetumi akɔ so adi Yehowa nokware.
<<<P>>>
Sɛ Yehyia Sɔhwɛ a, Yɛbɛyɛ Dɛn Akɔ So Adi Yehowa Nokware?
<<<P>>>
“Awurade, hena nkyɛn na yɛnkɔ? Wo na wowɔ daa nkwa nsɛm.”—YOH. 6:68.
<<<P>>>
BERE bi, na Yesu rekyerɛkyerɛ asuafo bebree wɔ Kaper-naum. Eduu baabi no, ɔka kyerɛɛ wɔn sɛ: “Sɛ moanni onipa Ba no honam, na moannom ne mogya a, munni nkwa wɔ mo mu. Obiara a odi me honam, na ɔnom me mogya no wɔ daa nkwa, na menyan no ɛda a edi akyiri no.” (Yoh. 6:53, 54) Bere a asuafo no tee saa asɛm no, wɔn mu bebree kaa sɛ: “Asɛm yi ano yɛ hyew; hena na obetumi atie?”—Yoh. 6:60.
<<<P>>>
2 Wɔn a asɛm no haw wɔn paa no gyaee Yesu akyi di. Wohwɛ a, gyidi a wɔnni nti na wɔyɛɛ saa anaa? Daabi. Efi-sɛ ansa na saa asɛm yi resi no, na Yesu ayɛ biribi. Nea ɔyɛe no maa asuafo no ho dwiriw wɔn ara ma wɔkaa sɛ: “Ampa, oyi ne Odiyifo a ɔreba wiase no.” (Yoh. 6:14) Enti ɛda adi sɛ na saa asuafo no gye Yesu di, nanso bere a biribi sɔɔ wɔn gyidi hwɛe no, wɔgyaee n’akyi di.
