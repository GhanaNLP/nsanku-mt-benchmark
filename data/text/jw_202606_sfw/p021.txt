Aliemaa biemɔ bɔ bafa afoɛ pee basõ Ye-howa ne, bebisale bɛ bɔ waboka bɛ maa bɛkɔ adesua a, bɛkora betie bɔ ɔkɔso ne yé ɔ. Ɛmɔma yetie bɔ bɛhane ɔ.
<<<P>>>
• Aliemaa brasua Suefawn bɔle asu 1986 nu, na ɔhane kyɛ: “Sɛ ɛkɔ adesua a, ɛyɛ a taa tena anyunu na wamaa afa w’adwene asie bɔ ɔma tɔɔk ne, ɔne vidio bɔ bɛye naso.”
<<<P>>>
• David koso bɔle asu 1983 nu, na ye wora ɔhane kyɛ: “Sɛ adesua ne kɔso a, ɛyɛ a mebɔ Yehowa mbaeɛ mmerɛ pee kyɛ ɔboka me ma mva m’adwene nzie bɔ ɔkɔso naso. Mesa mekae menwo kyɛ, mawa kyɛ me baa-sõ Yehowa, yeti odikyɛ metie bɔ ɔkɔso ne yé, na mema mmuayɛɛ koso.”
<<<P>>>
• Nicolas bɔle asu 1982 nu, na ye koso bɔ ɔhane ɛhe: “Ebiala bɔ ɔkɔyɛ dwumadie ne, ɛkɔhora kɔfa kosie wɔnye so kyɛ, ɛkɔkamvo ye wɔ nikyee pɔtee bie bɔ ɔyɛle nanwo. Ebie kɔhora kɔyɛ mvandoho bie bɔ ɔyɛle, anaa edwirɛ bie bɔ ɔhane bɔ ɔyɛle wɔ fɛ ɔ.”
<<<P>>>
• Augustin bɔle asu 1988 nu, na sɔaa yeɛ ye koso ɔhane ɔ: “Sɛ akoaa nahorɛnie ne sa fa edwirɛ bie bɔ yasua ye dada ne ba kyɛ yesua ye bieku a, ɛyɛ a mekã ye wɔ metianu kyɛ, bɛse kyɛ yenwo hia nati yeɛ basa bafa bawa ɔ.”
