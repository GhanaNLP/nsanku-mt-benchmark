7 Sɛ yɛkɔ adesua a, yetie bɔ ɔkɔso ne yé, ofikyɛ yɛpena kyɛ yɛnye gye, na yenya daa ngoa. Yehowa hã hyirele tete Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ kyɛ, sɛ bɛpena kyɛ bɛnye gye na bɛtena ase bɛkyɛ a, betie ye dwirɛ. Sɔ ala yee odikyɛ yɛ koso yɛyɛ ɔ. (Deut. 32:44-47) Sɛ yɛkɔ adesua a, yenwo hia paa kyɛ yekotie bɔ ɔkɔso ne yé. Ofikyɛ bɔ yɛte ye wɔ berɛ ne, ɔhyɛ yɛ ngunaen maa yemia yɛnye yegyina yɛ haw noa, mmerɛ bɔ yɛnwondɛ ewiase foforɛ bɔ ɔba ne. (Rom. 8:25) Yɛwɔ ayieleɛ mmerɛ nanu, yeti yenwo hia paa kyɛ yetie Yehowa dwirɛ. Sɛ yɛyɛ sɔ a, ɔkɔmaa yɛnye kɔla berɛ, na yahora yayɛ bɔ okuro ɔ.—Mat. 24:42-44; Nye. 1:3.

8 Aliemaamɔ bɔ bekyirekyire yɛ wɔ adesua bo ne, odikyɛ bɛmaa bɛ ngyerɛkyerɛ ne kã bɛbɔ bɛtie bɛ ne ahone. Bɛbɔ bɛma badwanu dwudwolɛ ne, bɛ paa yee ɛhe kɔma bɛ ɔ. Nzuati yee yɛkã sɔ ɔ? Ofikyɛ sɛ bɛto bɛ boase besua bɛ tɔɔk ne yé, na bɛkora bɛma ye kama a, ɔboka bɛbɔ bawa adesua foforɛ ne maa bɛte Bible nu dwirɛ biemɔ bo. Aliemaamɔ koso, ɔmaa bɛ gyidie yɛse, na bɛkɔso bɛsõ Yehowa. Yeti sɛ enya dwumadie bɔ ɔte sɔ a, bɔ mmɔden kyɛ ekosua ye yé, na ahora ayɛ ye kama, ofikyɛ ɛnze kyɛbɔ ɔkɔboka ebie ɔ. Ye de yɛbaanea nningyein biemɔ bɔ sɛ enya tɔɔk a, ɔkɔboka wɔ maa ɛkɔhora kɔma ye kama ɔ. Ɔwɔ nu bɛbɔ bɛma badwanu dwudwolɛ ne paa yee ɛhe kɔboka bɛ ɔ. Nakoso okosa kɔboka ebiala bɔ okohyirehyire wɔ adesua bo ne.—2 Tim. 4:2; Tito 1:9.

9 Nnɛmaa wɔ dwirɛ ne yɛ nwonworaenn. Sɛ ɛma tɔɔk a, ɛyɛ a ɛpena kyɛ bɛbɔ betie wɔ ne, benwu nikyee deinn bɔ ɛkã yenwo dwirɛ ne. Yeti sɛ ɛmaa wɔ dwirɛ ne yɛ nwonworaenn a, ɔkɔyɛ se kyɛ bekɔte yebo ɔ, na annea a ɔkɔmaa b'adwene kofi wɔ tɔɔk naso. Ye de, nzu yee ɛkɔhora kɔyɛ ɔ? Ebie ɛhe: (1) Sɛ ɛma tɔɔk a, ahyɛaseɛ nala edwirɛ titire bɔ ɔwɔ nu ne, bobɔso kyire bɛ. (2) Fa sɔ dwirɛ nemɔ ngokongoko na kyirekyire nu. Afei sɛ eyie na ɛkɔ foforɛ so a, ɛyɛ a ma benwu ye. (3) Sɛ ɛfa wɔ tɔɔk ne ba ayieleɛ a, edwirɛ titire bɔ ɛhane yenwo dwirɛ ne, sa bɔ yemua na wamaa bakae.

10 Ma wɔnye gye bɔ ɛkwaahã yenwo dwirɛ nanwo. Sɛ wɔnye gye bɔ ɛkwaahã yenwo dwirɛ nanwo a, ɔkɔmaa ekohyirehyire nu yé maa ɔkɔhã bɛbɔ betie wɔ ne ahone. (Any. 2:4, 5; Aso. 4:20) Nzu yee ɔkɔboka wɔ maa ɛkɔhora kɔyɛ sɔ ɔ? Dwenedwene edwirɛ bɔ ɛkwaahyirehyire nu, ɔne tworɔnzɛm bɔ bahwe adwene basie so nanwo. Aliemaa bie wɔ United States, na kesaalae wali afoɛ 70 ne ye si, nakoso tɔɔk bie bɔ aliemaa bie mane, mmerɛ bɔ ne ɔte abɛɛmaa ne, oté kae. Ɔhane kyɛ: “Atee bɔ aliemaa ne fale so hyirehyirele edwirɛ nanu ne, ne ɛkora nwu ye paa kyɛ yenye gye bɔ ɔkã nanwo. Ye tɔɔk nanu ne, ɔhyɛle yɛ ngunaen kyɛ sɛ yɛkenga Nyameɛ Dwirɛ ne a, yɛto yɛboase yɛdwenedwene yenwo yé. Sɔ dwirɛ ne pusuli me paa. Yeti ofi sɔ mmerɛ ne, sɛ mekenga Bible ne a, medwenedwene yenwo, na ɛhene amaa mapingye Yehowa paa.”
