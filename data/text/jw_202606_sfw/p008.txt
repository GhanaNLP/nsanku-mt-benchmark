<<<P>>>

<<<P>>>
DWEIN 90
Monhyɛ Mo Ho Nkuran
<<<P>>>
BƆ ADESŨA NE KƆHÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Sɛ nningyein biemɔ pena sɛkye yɛne aliemaamɔ afĩa a, yekonwu bɔ yɛkɔho-ra yɛkɔyɛ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
EWIASE ayieleɛ mmerɛ he anu ne, ɔhaw bɔ yɛfa nu ne, nna yeɛ ɛhe. Yeti sɛ yɛ ngome yɛnwa yegyina yenoa a, ɔkɔyɛ se. Yeho-wa se ye sɔ, na ɛhene ati yeɛ wafa yeliemamɔ wama yɛ kyɛ yɛne bɛ fa adamvoa ma bɛboka yɛ ne. Ɛhe maa yɛkae asafo dwein kõ bie bɔ ye dwirɛti ne nwa, “Yɔnko Pa” ne. Ɔyɛ yɛ fɛ paa kyɛ yele yeliemamɔ bɔ bɛdwene yɛnwo sɔ wɔ asafo nanu ɔ. (Edw. 119:63) Nahorɛ nu, yɛla abusua kandinga bie nu, na benni yɛ-nwo angorɛ koraa.
<<<P>>>
2 Yɛne aliemaamɔ mukoraati asua Yehowa ne Yesu nwo ni-kyee, na wamaa yanya ɔdɔ yama bɛ. Sɔ nikyee ne, yeɛ wahã yɛ wabonu kyɛ abusua ne. (Yoh. 13:35) Ɔnate yesɔ nati, yengu-ro kyɛ yekonwu yeliemamɔ kyɛ bɛte yɛ manifoɛ mbaen, na mmom yekuro kyɛ yebu bɛ kyɛ bɛte yɛ damvomɔ paa. Nakoso, sɛ yɛkɔhora yɛkɔyɛ ɛhe a, ɔmma mbaen, sana yɛyɛ nningyein biemɔ kora. Afei koso yaayɛ mmenia bɔ bɔne wɔ yɛnwo ye de, ɔyɛ sɛ alaa a nningyein biemɔ kɔhora kɔsɛkye yɛne b’afĩa, ofi-kyɛ bɛnwa agyee ne temverema po bɛkõ.
<<<P>>>
3 Bɔ ɔte yeɛ ole kyɛ aliemaa biemɔ wɔ berɛ a, yɛne bɛ bɔ paa tra ebiemɔ. Nzuati ɔ? Ebiaa ne bɛ subaen bie yɛ yɛ nyemene, anaa yɛne bɛ nye gye nikyee kõ anwo. Nakoso aliemaa biemɔ de, ebiaa yɛngora yembingye bɛ pá. Bɔ ɔmaa ɔkora ba ye sɔ yeɛ ole kyɛ, odwu mmerɛ bie a, ɛyɛ a yɛne bɛ nyɛ adwene wɔ nnin-gyein biemɔ nwo. Afei koso, ebiaa ne bɛ subaen biemɔ wɔ berɛ a yɛnye ngye yenwo. Ebiemɔ koso, ebiaa anwonyerɛ bie bɔ ɔha bɛ, anaa adwenedwene bie bɔ waatɔ bɛ so nati, wamaa bɛnwo ayɛ ahomete. Sɛ ɔkɔba kyɛ sɔ haw hemɔ ti ɔyɛ se ma yɛ kyɛ yɛ-

1. Nisua yeɛ yekonya adamvoa bɔ bɛdwene yɛnwo ɔ?
<<<P>>>

<<<P>>>
