Bɛtɛl Kɔmatii bɔ ne ɔwɔ Cuba, 2013 nu
<<<P>>>
Aliemaa nemɔ maa yɛtele yebo kyɛ, bɛne awaen mbanyi nemɔ guso bedi ngɔmmɔ bɛnea kyɛ ebiaa bɛkɔye bɛsa bɔ bafa bato Yehowa Adanzefoɛ so ne a. Ɛhene ati yɛɛ bɛnwa behia manzini soneafoɛ namukoraati dumaa ne. Ɔwale sɔ ne, yɛtele yebo kyɛ bɛfa yɛ dumaa ne bɛma bɛ. Ofi sɔ mmerɛ ne bɔ ɔkɔ ne, yɛne awaen mbanyi nemɔ afĩa hyɛle yebo kyɛ ɔyɛ pá.
<<<P>>>
Ngakangaka afei de, yesa yenyane yenwo atee kyɛ yɛkɔhora yɛkɔhã nzɛmba ne wɔ badwa nu. Nakoso ne awaen ne lɛlle Yehowa Adanzefoɛ tolle nu wɔ mmraa nu wɔ maen nanu. Siɛ ye yenwuni ye kyɛ sãa ne awaen mbanyi nemɔ po se manzini soneafoɛ ne biemɔ dumaa dada, na mmom ne bɛpena kyɛ betie befi yɛ berɛ.
<<<P>>>
Wɔ September 1994 nu ne, awaen ne mane yɛ atee kyɛ yesa yebukye Bɛtɛl. Yeti Bɛtɛl sua bɔ ne bato nu afoɛ 20 ne, ɛhene ala yɛɛ yesa yɛfa yɛhyɛle yɛ dwumadie bo bieku wɔ maen nanu ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Wɔ 1996 nu ne, bɛfrɛle me wɔ fon so befili Bɛtɛl kyɛ mene Emilia yɛɛyɛ adwuma wɔ berɛ. Ɔwale sɔ ne, ɔyɛle yɛ nwanwa paa, ofikyɛ ne yɛmmaa nanu nyɔ nyinni, yeti ne yɛne bɛ yɛɛ yɛte ɔ. Ɛhene ati yɛhane yenwo dwirɛ hyirele bɛ. Nakoso mmerɛ bɔ besusuli yɛ dwirɛ nanwo ne, bɛhane kyɛ yɛne yɛ mmaa nemɔ mukoraati yɛbra. Yele yɛtole nu, na yɛhyɛle yebo yɛboale yɛnwo yɛhɔle Havana.
<<<P>>>
Nahorɛ dwirɛ yɛɛ ole kyɛ, ne mafa afoɛ pee mayɛ manzini adwuma ne, na ɛhene yɛɛ ne ɔla m’ahone so ɔ. Yeti ahyɛaseɛ ne de, ne Bɛtɛl adwuma ne nyɛ me fɛ, ofikyɛ ne menye nga. Bɔ ne okũ me po yɛɛ ole kyɛ ngɔɔtena kɔmpita nyunu ngɔyɛ adwuma ɔ. Nakoso, aliemaamɔ bɔ mene bɛ wɔ Bɛtɛl berɛ ne, bɛhyɛle me ngunaen paa, ye titire yɛɛ ole meye Emilia. Ngakangaka menye hane, yeti kesaalae menye gye paa kyɛ mesõ wɔ Bɛtɛl ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Kesaalae de, mene Emilia yayɛ mbanyi. Nakoso sɛ yɛnea aliemaamɔ nwora-nwora bɔ yɛse bɛ, na yɛne bɛ abɔnu ayɛ adwuma afoɛ pee ne a, ɔmaa yɛnye gye paa. Bɔ ɔmaa yɛnye gye paa po yɛɛ ole kyɛ, yɛmmaa ne yɛ nnomaamɔ bɛsõ Yehowa ɔ. Ɛhe maa yɛte nga tekyɛ somafoɛ Yohane bɔ ne ye koso wayɛ panyi ne. Ɔhane kyɛ: “Nikyee biala mma me anigye bɔ ɔtra so te kyɛ ngɔte kyɛ me mma mɔ nate nahorɛ ne anu ɔ.”—3 Yoh. 4.
<<<P>>>
Mene Emilia yafa kɔyɛ afoɛ 30 yayɛ adwuma wɔ Bɛtɛl. Ɔwɔ nu yayɛ nworowaa, na anwonyerɛ koso ha yɛ, nakoso adwuma biala bɔ bɛkɔfa bekowura yɛsa ne, yɛyere yɛnwo yɛyɛ bɔ yɛkɔhora ɔ. Amba, ɔyɛ yɛ fɛ paa kyɛ yahora yafa kɔyɛ afoɛ 70 yasõ ‘anigye Nyameɛ’ ne, wɔ Cuba supɔ naso wa ɔ.—1 Tim. 1:11; Edw. 97:1.
