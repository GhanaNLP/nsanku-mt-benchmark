yenoa kyɛ: “Awurade, nwa berɛ yeɛ yɛkɔhɔ ɔ? Wɔ yeɛ ele daa ngoa dwirɛ ɔ; na yale yali, na yanwu ye kyɛ wɔ ole Nyameɛ ye Krongronniɛ ne.” (Yoh. 6:68, 69) Sɔ mmuayɛɛ bɔ Petro fa-mane ne, aso okyire kyɛ edwirɛ bɔ Yesu hane kyɛ beli ye nwonaen, na bɛno ye mogya ne, ne ɔne asomafoɛ bɔ baha ne te yebo anaa? Daa-bi, ɔtte sɔ. Na mmom bɔ yeti yeɛ bɛhɔleso bɛ-tale Yesu si yeɛ ole kyɛ, ne bedé bedi paa kyɛ ye yeɛ ole Kristo ne, ‘Nyameɛ Awa’ ne.—Luka 9:20, 35.

4 Nningyein biemɔ kɔhora kɔsɔ yɛ kɔnea maa gyidie bɔ yele ye wɔ Yehowa nu ne kɔhɔ ase. Ebie yeɛ ole mmerɛ bɔ asafo ne asesã ndeaseɛ bɔ yele ye wɔ Bible nu dwirɛ bie nu, anaa wayɛ nzakrayɛɛ wɔ kyɛbɔ yɛkã nzɛmba nanu ne. Ebie koso kora yɛ mmerɛ bɔ yɛnde bɔ yeti yeɛ Yehowa ama atẽe maa edwirɛ bie ato yɛ nabo ne. Nikyee foforɛ koso yeɛ ole mmerɛ bɔ aliemaa bie bɔ yebu ye paa, anaa ɔte yɛ damvo ayɛ nikyeebie maa wayɛ yɛ yea ne. Sɛ sɔ dwirɛ he anu bie to yɛ a, ɔkɔyɛ sɛɛ ne yahɔso yanya Yehowa nu gyidie, na yali naho-rɛ yama ye?

5 Yenwo hia paa kyɛ yenya gyidie bɔ nu yɛ se wɔ Yehowa nu. Nzuati ɔ? Wɔde ma yɛnea sɔ dwirɛ he. Sɛ bési sua wɔ nekaa bɔ mvrama taa tu, anaa aseɛ taa woso paa a, ɛyɛ a bɛto ye fa-pem (anaa foundation) ne yé, kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a nikyeebie bɔ ɔte sɔ si a, sua ne ngobu ɔ. Sɔ ala yeɛ sɛ yɛkɔhora yekogyina ɔhaw ahoroɛ bɔ ɔto yɛ tekyɛ bɔ yɛhane yenwo dwirɛ kesaalae ne noa a, sana yɛmaa yɛ gyidie yɛ se paa. Sɛ yɛyɛ sɔ a, ɔmva yenwo ne ɔhaw biala bɔ ɔkɔto yɛ ne, yɛkɔhora yekogyina pindinn, na yahɔ-so yali nahorɛ yama Yehowa.—1 Kor. 15:58; kenga Kolosefoɛ 1:23.
