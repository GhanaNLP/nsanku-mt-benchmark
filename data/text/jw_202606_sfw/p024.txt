saalae ne, ‘nu yɛse paa,’ yeti ɔhaw bɔ yɛfa nu ne, ɔtte angorɛ. Ɛhene ati sana bɔ Yehowa fa kyirekyire yɛ nala yeɛ ɔkɔboka yɛ maa yɛkɔhora yekogyina yenoa ɔ. (2 Tim. 3:1) Nakoso, sɛ odwu mmerɛ bie na yenwu ye kyɛ edwirɛ bɔ bɛfa bekyirekyire yɛ wɔ adesua bo ne, yate dee a, nzu yeɛ ɔkɔboka yɛ ɔ? Yeɛ ole kyɛ yekosuesua bɔ ɔhworɔle Edwein 119 ne. Né ɔte ebie bɔ onni Nyameɛ noa dwirɛ nwo angorɛ koraa ɔ.—Kenga Edwein 119:24, 111, 167.

15 Bɔ yékotie ye wɔ adesua bo ne mukoraati, odikyɛ mmerɛ biala yenwu ye kyɛ Yehowa yeɛ ɔfa kyirekyire yɛ ɔ, yeti yenwo hia paa. Nzuati yeɛ yɛkã sɔ ɔ? Bɔ Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ yɛle, mmerɛ bɔ Yehowa mane bɛ mana wɔ ɛserɛ so nati ɔ. (Ɛks. 16:15, 31) Ahyɛaseɛ ne de, ne benni sɔ aleɛ nanwo angorɛ koraa, ofikyɛ ne bɛse kyɛ Yehowa yeɛ wafa wama bɛ ɔ. Afei koso ne ɔyɛ fɛ, na ɔbokale bɛ maa bɛtenane ngoa nu. Nakoso siɛ ye, ne ebiemɔ nguro kyɛ bekoli sɔ aleɛ ne kõ, ofikyɛ ne bɛnwa daa ɛhene ala yeɛ bedi ɔ, yeti wabɔ bɛ. (Num. 21:5) Nzu yeɛ yesua yefi nu ɔ? Bɔ bɛfa bekyirekyire yɛ wɔ adesua bo ne, ɔnzɛkyɛ yenya adwene kyɛ yɛte ye daa, yeti yenwo nhia. Na mmom odikyɛ yenwu ye kyɛ bɔ yésua ne mukoraati, Yehowa yeɛ ɔnate asafo naso fama yɛ ɔ. Afei koso, edwirɛ deinn bɔ ɔkɔboka yɛ ne, ɛhene yeɛ akoaa nahorɛniɛ ne gyina so hyehyɛ adesua biala ɔ. Odikyɛ yesa yenwu ye kyɛ, bɔ yékosua ye wɔ berɛ ne, ɛhene yeɛ ɔmaa yenwu nworɛ yɛfa yesisi gyinayɛɛ bɔ okole yɛ ngoa kyẽabie ɔ.—Any. 3:13, 16-18; Yoh. 17:3.

16 Sɛ yɛkɔ adesua na yɛkɔhora yekotie bɔ ɔkɔso ne yé a, nningyein biemɔ wɔ berɛ bɔ odikyɛ yɛyɛ ɔ. Aliemaa biemɔ bɔ bafa afoɛ pee basõ Yehowa ne, bɛhane bɔ waboka bɛ maa bɛkɔ adesua a bɛkora betie bɔ ɔkɔso ne yé ɔ. Ɛmɔma yetie bɔ bɛhane ɔ. Bɛnu kõ yeɛ ole Ashley. Wasõ Yehowa kɔbo afoɛ 30 ɛhe. Ɔhane kyɛ: “Koraka ngɔhɔ adesua ne, medimoa mesua bɔ yɛkwaasusu yenwo wɔ berɛ ne. Sɛ manyɛ sɔ a, mekɔ a mengora me mma mmuayɛɛ, na ɛyɛ a ne m’adwene koso nate nekaabie.” József koso asõ Yehowa 52 ɛhe, na ye wora ɔhane kyɛ: “Bɔ yesusu yenwo wɔ adesua bo ne, sɛ mate dee po a, ɛyɛ a mebɔ mmɔden kyɛ ngɔfa m’adwene ngosie nikyee foforɛ bie bɔ ngɔhora ngosua ngofi nu naso, na ɛhene aboka me paa.” Ɔfa bokale
