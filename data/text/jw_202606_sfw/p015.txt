KUE SONEAFOƐ NE YE BOKAFOƐ NE, ADWUMA BENI YEE BƐYƐ Ɔ?
<<<P>>>
4 Sɛ bɛkã asɛnga kue soneafoɛ a, ɔte asafo nu panyi* bɔ mbanyi nemɔ aye ye ɔ. Ɔboka alie-maamɔ bɔ bɛwɔ ye kue nanu ne maa bɛne Ye-howa afĩa yɛ kama, na osa boka bɛ maa bɛkora bekyirekyire yé wɔ asɛnga nu. Ye de, atee beni yee ɔfa so yɛ ye adwuma ne ɔ? Bɔ olimoa, ɔtaa yenye kɔ aliemaa biala bɔ ɔwɔ ye kue nanu na-nwo. (Any. 27:23) Bɔ ɔkɔyɛ ne wahora wayɛ sɔ ne, ɛyɛ a bɛbɔ bɛwɔ ye kue nanu ne, ɔtaa ne bɛ bɔ ngɔmmɔ kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a, okonwu bɔ ɔkɔso wɔ b’asetena nu ɔ. Ɛhene maa ɔkora boka bɛ maa bepingye Yehowa, ɔkyekye bɛ wora mmerɛ bɔ edwirɛ bie ato bɛ ne, na osa yɛ nningyein pɔtee biemɔ boka bɛ. (Any. 12:25; Yes. 32:2; Yak. 2:15-17) Bɔ ɔtɔ so nyɔ, aliemaamɔ bɔ bɛwɔ ye kue nanu ne, ɔhyɛ bɛ ngunaen maa bɛnu biala yere yenwo yɛ asɛnga adwuma ne. Ɛyɛ a odi anyunu ɔne aliemaamɔ yɛ asɛnga adwuma ne dapɛen biala, ye titire ne, Fue ne Kwasida. Ɔbɔ mmɔ-den kyɛ ɔne ebiala bɔ ɔwɔ ye kue nanu ne kɔhɔ asɛnga. Ɔba sɔ a, ɔmaa ɔkora hyɛ bɛ ngunaen, na osa boka bɛ maa bɛkora bekyirekyire yé wɔ asɛnga nu. Yesɔ nati sɛ ɔkɔba kyɛ nikyeebie ti, ye bɔbɔ ɔngɔhora asɛnga nhyiamu kɔ a, ɔyɛ nhyehyɛɛ maa ye bokafoɛ anaa aliemaa bie bɔ ɔfata kosi ye gya nu kɔnea so. Bɔ ɔtɔ so nza, ɔboka mmrienzua bɔ babɔ asu ne maa bɛbasɔ asɛyɛdeɛ ahoroɛ nu wɔ asafo nanu. (1 Tim. 3:1) Aliemaamɔ bɔ bɛte sɔ ne, ɛyɛ a ɔkamvo bɛ wɔ mmɔden bɔ bɛbɔ nanwo. Afei koso, ogyina Bi-ble nu dwirɛ so tu bɛ foɛ, na osa fa ndeteyɛɛ ma bɛ na wamaa bahora baafata kyɛ asomvoɛ,
<<<P>>>
* Sɛ yenwo bahia a, mbanyi nemɔ kɔhora kɔmaa asafo nu som-voɛ bie kɔnea asɛnga kue bie aso kodwu kyɛ bekɔnya panyi bɔ ɔkɔɔyɛ kue soneafoɛ ɔ. Ɔnate kyɛ ɔtte asafo nu panyi nati, mba-nyi nemɔ yee bekɔboka ye maa ɔkɔyɛ sɔ adwuma ne ɔ. Ɛhene ati asafo nu somvoɛ bɔ ɔyɛ sɔ adwuma ne, bɛfrɛ ye kue somvoɛ.
<<<P>>>
4. (a) Asɛnga kue soneafoɛ adwuma yee ole sɛɛ? (b) Nzu yee ɔyɛ faboka bɛbɔ bɛwɔ ye kue nanu ne ɔ? (Nea alaka nanu dwirɛ bɔ yenwa, “Edwirɛ Biemɔ Bɔ Ɔkɔboka Kue Soneafoɛ Ne Ɔ.”)
<<<P>>>
Edwirɛ Biemɔ Bɔ Ɔkɔboka Kue Soneafoɛ Ne Ɔ
<<<P>>>
• Ɛyɛ a nnɛmaa asɛnga nhyiamu ne kyɛ pee, wabo so alaa yee ole minit 7. Ɛhene kɔmaa aliemaamɔ kɔhora kofi berɛ ndɛ kɔhɔ asɛnga.
<<<P>>>
• Bɔ mmɔden kyɛ ɛne ebiala bɔ ɔwɔ wɔ kue nanu ne kɔhɔ asɛnga. Bɛbɔ ebiaa nikyeebie ti bɛngora bɛmma asɛnga nhyiamu ne po, yɛ nhyehyɛɛ bɔ ɔkɔmaa ɛne bɛ kɔhɔ asɛnga ɔ. Sɛ ɔkɔyɛ yé a, ebiaa ɛne bɛ kɔhora kɔhɔ asɛnga ne nɔsoa, anaa kyẽa foforɛ.
<<<P>>>
• Bɛbɔ bɛwɔ wɔ kue nanu ne, ɛyɛ a taa hyɛ bɛ ngunaen. Ɛkɔhora kɔyɛ sɔ wɔ b’awuro, Ahennie Asa so, anaa nekaa foforɛ biala bɔ ɔkɔyɛ yé ɔ. Ɛkɔhora koso po kɔfrɛ bɛ wɔ fon so kɔhyɛ bɛ ngunaen, anaa ɛkɔhworɔ kra-taa kɔma bɛ.
<<<P>>>
• Tete aliemaa mmrienzuamɔ, na wamaa ba-hora bafa adwuma bawura bɛsa wɔ asafo nanu.
<<<P>>>
• Sɛ ɔkɔba kyɛ asafo ne mukoraati po yianu yɛ asɛnga nhyiamu a, ɔkɔyɛ yé a, nea kyɛ aliemaamɔ bɔ bɛwɔ wɔ kue nanu ne, beko-nya ebie bɔ bɛne yɛ kɔhɔ asɛnga ɔ.

anaa mbanyi. Ɛhemɔ maa yenwu ye kyɛ, asɛn-ga kue soneafoɛ adwuma ne, ɔte adwuma bɔ yenwo hia paa. Yeti koraka mbanyi nemɔ kɔye ebie kyɛ ɔyɛ sɔ adwuma ne, bɛnea kyɛ ɔte ebie bɔ ɔkɔhora kɔyɛ ye yé ɔ.
