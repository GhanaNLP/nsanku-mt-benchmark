Apam nyɔ beni yeɛ ne somafoɛ Pɔɔlo kã yenwo dwirɛ wɔ Galatiafoɛ 4:24 ne?
<<<P>>>
◼ Somafoɛ Pɔɔlo hane kyɛbɔ ne Abraham ne ɔye Sara ne ye afaneɛ Hagar afĩa si te nanwo dwi-rɛ. Ɛhene si ne, ɔhane kyɛ: “Ɛhemɔ te mvandoho, ofikyɛ mmrasua nyɔ ne gyi berɛ ma apam nyɔ.” (Gal. 4:22-24). Dáa ne, ne yɛte yebo kyɛ sɔ apam nyɔ ne, yeɛ ole Mmraa apam ne, ɔne apam foforɛ ne. Nakoso mmerɛ bɔ yesa yesusuli sɔ dwirɛ na-nwo ne, yenwuni ye kyɛ yenwo hia kyɛ yɛyɛ nzak-rayɛɛ wɔ sɔ ndeaseɛ bɔ yele ye nanu. Ɔkɔyɛ kyɛ apam bɔ Yehowa ne Abraham yɛle ne, yeɛ ne Pɔɔlo kã yenwo dwirɛ ɔ, na mmom ɔtte apam fo-forɛ ne. Ye de, ɛmɔma yɛnea bɔ ɔmaa Pɔɔlo hane sɔ dwirɛ ne ɔ.*
<<<P>>>
Pɔɔlo hane Hagar nwo dwirɛ kyɛ, ogyi berɛ Sinai Bokaa. (Gal. 4:25) Yeti ne Hagar yeɛ ole Mmraa apam bɔ Yehowa ne Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ yɛle wɔ Sinai Bokaa naso, wɔ afoɛ 1513 K.Y.B (Kora Yeɛ Yesu Ba) ne. (Ɛks. 19:5, 6) Sona bɔ bɔne wɔ yenwo biala nne berɛ bɔ ne ɔkɔhora koli sɔ Mmraa naso pɛpɛɛpɛ ɔ. Ɛhene maa Yudafoɛ nemɔ nwuni ye kyɛ né bɛte ngoaa bɛma bɔne. Ne sɔ apam ne kosa kɔboka Yisraefoɛ nemɔ maa bekonwu Me-saya bɔ ye ngome yeɛ ne ɔte sona, bɔ ne ɔkɔhora koli Mose Mmraa naso pɛpɛɛpɛ ne. Yeti mmerɛ bɔ Mesaya ne fale ye ngoa bɔle afɔleɛ ne, olɛle mme-nia bɔ bɔne wɔ bɛnwo ne fili bɔne ne ewue sa nu. (Gal. 3:19, 24, 25) Ɛhene si ne, ne Mmraa na-nwo nhia kõ, ofikyɛ bɔ yeti yeɛ ɔmaa Nyamɛɛ fale sɔ Mmraa ne mane Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ ne, né Yesu amaa wawa nu.—Rom. 10:4.
<<<P>>>
Pɔɔlo fale Hagar totole Sara bɔ ne ɔte “dehyee” bɔ ‘ɔnate bɔhyɛ so’ ɔwole nanwo. (Gal. 4:23) Sɔ bɔhyɛ ne, yeɛ ole apam bɔ Yehowa ne Abraham yɛle ne. (Gal. 3:29; 4:28, 30) Yehowa hane sɔ apam nanwo dwirɛ wɔ Gyenesise 22:18 kyɛ: “Ɔna-te wɔ boso ama so yeɛ aseɛ ye aso maen maen amukoraa bekonya saleɛ ɔ, ofikyɛ atie me dwi-rɛ.”
<<<P>>>
Sɔ apam bɔ Yehowa ne Abraham yɛle ne, ɔmaa yɛte ɛbɔ bɔ Yehowa hyɛle ye wɔ Eden turo nu nabo yé. (Gye. 3:15) Ɛberɛ dwirɛ ne maa yenwuni ye kyɛ “bosoniɛ” bɔ bɛhyɛle yenwo bɔ ne, né okofi Abraham nu yeɛ ɔkɔwa ɔ. Pɔɔlo hyirele nu kyɛ, sɔ “bosoniɛ” ne, yeɛ ole Yesu Kristo. Nakoso bɛbɔ ‘bɛte Kristo dee’ bɔ bɛ yeɛ bele mmenia 144,000 bɔ bɛne Yesu koli bia wɔ ye Ahennie nu ne, bɛ koso bɛboka sɔ bosoniɛ titire he aso. (Gal. 3:16, 29; Nye. 14:1-3) Mmenia bɔ bedé Yesu bɛtonu, na bɛyɛ sotie bɛma ye ne, Nyamɛɛ nate ɛbɔ bɔ ɔhyɛ-le Abraham naso kɔmaa bekonya nhyiraa pee.
<<<P>>>
Ye de, nzuati yeɛ Pɔɔlo yɛle sɔ ‘mvandoho’ he ɔ? Ne ɔpena kyɛ Yudafoɛ bɔ bɛwɔ ye aberɛ so ne nwu ye kyɛ, sɛ bedé begyina nu kyɛ bekoli Mose Mmraa naso a, ye de tekyɛ Hagar ne, bɛkɔ-hɔso bɛkɔyɛ ngoaa. Nakoso, sɛ ne bedé Yesu Kris-to bɔ ɔte Abraham “bosoniɛ” titire ne bɛtonu a, ne ɔkɔmaa bɛkɔyɛ nnehyeɛ tekyɛ Sara. Ɛhene kyi-re kyɛ ne bekole bɛnwo bekofi bɔne ne ewue bɔ ne wafa bɛ wayɛ ngoaa ne sa nu. (Yoh. 8:32-34) Afei koso, sɛ ne banne Yesu bando nu a, bɔ okyire yeɛ ole kyɛ nikyee deinn bɔ yeti yeɛ bɛyɛle Mmraa apam ne, ne bɛnde yebo. Ɛhene yeɛ ole kyɛ ne ɔkɔmaa bekonwu Kristo ne.
<<<P>>>
Galatiafoɛ bɔ ne Pɔɔlo tworɔ sɔ dwirɛ ne kɔma bɛ ne, bɛnu pee nala wɔ berɛ a, ne bɛtte Yuda-foɛ. Yeti ne bɛnne Mose Mmraa nabo wɔ atee bia-la so. Nakoso Kristofoɛ bɔ ne bɛte Yudafoɛ ne, ne bɛnu biemɔ kã kyɛ odikyɛ Kristofoɛ bɔ bɛte maen-maen sofoɛ nemɔ di Mose Mmraa naso. Ebie yeɛ ole kyɛ ne bɛnwa, bɛrɛ bɛ brienzua. Ɔwale sɔ ne, Pɔɔlo maa benwuni kyɛ ɔnate Kristo bɔ bale ye bato nu nati, banya “fawɔnwodi.” Yeti nyanza bia-la nne nu kyɛ bekoli sɔ Mmraa naso ɔ. Ofikyɛ sɛ bɛyɛ sɔ a, ne besa bɛfa bɛti bekowura “ngoaasolɛ nu” bieku.—Gal. 5:1, 10-14.
