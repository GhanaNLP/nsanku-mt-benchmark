YEHOWA BOKA YE MMENIA SO ƐNNƐ
<<<P>>>
13 Sɛ yɛhyɛ yebo yenya adwene kyɛ, nna Yehowa Adanzefoɛ soen ngome yeɛ Nyameɛ dé to nu a, ɔnzɛkyɛ yɛtenatena yenwo koraa. Na mmom odikyɛ yebisa yɛnwo dwirɛ biemɔ tekyɛ: ‘Nwamɔ yeɛ ɛnnɛ bɛbɔ mmɔden kyɛ bɛkɔfa Bible nanu dwirɛ bɛkɔbɔ bɛ bra ɔ?’ ‘Soen beni yeɛ bɛfa bɛ ngyerɛkyerɛ mukoraati begyina Bible naso ɔ?’ (2 Tim. 4:3, 4) ‘Soen beni yeɛ nu mmenia bɔ mmɔden kyɛ bɛkɔyɛ bɔ Yehowa kuro ɛnnɛ ɔ?’—Mat. 7:20, 21.
<<<P>>>
14 Bɔ Nyameɛ kuro ne, ebie yeɛ ole kyɛ mmenia bɔ bɛwɔ nekaa biala kɔte nzɛmba ne bie kora ne wasɛkye ewiase ne. Ye de, ‘nwamɔ paa yeɛ bέyɛ sɔ adwuma ne ɛnnɛ ɔ?’ (Mat. 24:14) Ɛhe de, bemmisa kora ne banwu ye, ofikyɛ ebiala se kyɛ Yehowa Adanzefoɛ yeɛ bɛkã nzɛmba ne ɔ. Yɛ ngome yeɛ yɛkã Nyameɛ Ahennie nanwo nzɛmba ne wɔ maen kɔbo 240 so ɔ. Afei koso yafa kɔbo afoɛ 100 yeɛ yayɛ sɔ adwuma he ɔ. Nahorɛ dwirɛ yeɛ ole kyɛ, yɛmva asɛnga adwuma ne yenni angorɛ koraa, ofikyɛ ɔte adwuma bɔ yenwo hia yɛ paa.—Mat. 28:19, 20.
<<<P>>>
15 Kenga Mateo 6:9 ne Yesaya 43:10. Ɔyɛ yɛ fɛ paa kyɛ Nyameɛ dumaa ne la yɛ so, ná bɛfrɛ yɛ Yehowa Adanzefoɛ ɔ. Mmeni pee nze po kyɛ sãa Nyameɛ dumaa li Yehowa. Nakoso yɛ de, yɛse. Ɛhene ati yabɔ mmɔden yafa dumaa ne yawura nekaa biala bɔ odikyɛ ofite wɔ Bible nanu ne. Yeti wɔ Ewiase Foforɛ Ngyerɛaseɛ nanu ne, sɔ dumaa ne fite nu kɔbo apee nzoo (7,000.)
