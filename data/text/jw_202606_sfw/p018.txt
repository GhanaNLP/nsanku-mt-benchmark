Sɛ mmenia bɔ bɛwɔ kue nanu ne te kãa a, ɔmaa bɛnwu bɛnwo yé, na ebiala ne ye mmango kora kɔ asɛnga
<<<P>>>
(Nea ngyekyɛmuɛ 10)
<<<P>>>
ateepakyelɛniɛ, anaa aliemaa foforɛ bie bɔ ɛse ye kyɛ ɔkora kyirekyire kama wɔ asɛnga nu ɔ. (Kenga Anyandera 1:5; 27:17.) Ɛkɔhora koso kɔhã yenwo dwirɛ kohyire wɔ kue soneafoɛ ne, na ye koso ɔkɔyɛ bɔ ɔkɔhora biala kɔboka wɔ. Sɔ yeɛ aliemaa brasua kõ bɔ bɛfrɛ ye Heather yɛle ɔ. Ɔte ebie bɔ ɔfɛle nikyee paa, nakoso ɔmaa ebie bokale ye. Ɔhane kyɛ: “Ne ɔyɛ se ma me paa kyɛ ngɔyɛ sakɔnea ɔ. Yeti ngane yenwo dwirɛ ngyirele aliemaa brasua bie bɔ ɔte ateepakyelɛniɛ, na ye koso ɔbokale me. Menwa, ne ɔte sona kyɛ. Ne ɔtaa ne me kɔ asɛnga, na onyane menwo aboterɛ. Sakɔnea kõ bie bɔ yɛɛyɛle ne sɔa, ɔbokale me maa me ne sona ne hora hyɛle Bible adesua bo. Ɔyɛ me fɛ paa kyɛ mmane sɔ aliemaa brasua ne bokale me ɔ. Kesaalae sɛ mekɔ asɛnga a, menye gye paa.” Amba, sɛ yɛkora yɛyɛ bɔ yahã yenwo dwirɛ ne a, yɛkɔboka maa kue bɔ yɛwɔ nu ne kogyina.

11 Ɔboka yɛ maa yɛyere yɛnwo wɔ Yehowa soen nu. Wɔde ɛmɔma yɛnea bɔ yeti yeɛ yɛkã sɔ ne bie. Akunafoɛ, nyisiaa, ɔne bɛbɔ bayɛ mbanyi bɔ bɛwɔ asafo nanu ne, Yehowa nni bɛnwo angorɛ koraa. Ɛhene ati, ɔnate kue soneafoɛ nemɔ aso hyɛ mmenia bɔ bɛte sɔ ne ngunaen maa bɛkora bɛsõ Yehowa yé (1 Tɛs. 2:8; Yak. 1:27) Bɛbɔ yɛne bɛ wɔ yɛ kue nanu ne koso, bɛboka yɛ maa yɛkora yɛyɛ asɛnga adwuma ne bue ahoroɛ ne mukoraati yé. Aliemaa mmrienzuamɔ bɔ babɔ asu ne koso, bɛtete bɛ maa bɛkora bɛfa asɛyɛdee bewura bɛsa wɔ asafo nanu. —1 Tim. 3:10.
<<<P>>>
12 Wendy bɔ yalimoa yahã yenwo dwirɛ ne, ɔhane kyɛbɔ asɛnga kue bɔ ne ɔwɔ nu ne si bokale ye ɔ. Ohyirele nu kyɛ: “Mekae kyɛ ne me kue soneafoɛ ne taa tu me foɛ, na sɔ afutue bɔ ne ofi Bible nanu ne, waboka me toonn waadwu ɛnnɛ. Ne me baba nye ngye asafo nanwo, yeti sɛ odwu dapɛen ayieleɛ ne a, ɛyɛ a ne ɔtaa yɛ se ma me maame paa kyɛ ɔkɔhora ne kue bɔ ɔwɔ nu ne kɔhɔ asɛnga ɔ. Nakoso me de, ne mekɔ. Aliemaa braa panyi bie bɔ ne ɔte ateepakyelɛniɛ fale me adamvoa, na ɔbokale me paa. Ɔmaa nwuni kyɛbɔ mene mmenia kɔbɔ ngɔmmɔ, na mahora mahyirehyire yé ɔ. Osa koso bokale me maa nyerele menwo nzone Yehowa.”
<<<P>>>
13 Ɔmaa yɛne yɛnwo fa adamvoa. Sɛ yɛne bɛbɔ bɛwɔ yɛ kue nanu ne kɔ asɛnga a, ɔmaa yɛkora yɛfa b’adamvoa. (Flp. 1:27) Bɛbɔ bɛfɛle nikyee paa ne po, bɛkora bɛfa adamvoa wɔ kue nanu ofikyɛ ɛyɛ a ne nu mmenia ne nzone. (2 Kor. 6:13) Sɛ yɛne aliemaamɔ fa adamvoa bɔ ɔte sɔ a,
