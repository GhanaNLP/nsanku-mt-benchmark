SUBAEN BIEMƆ BƆ ƆKƆBOKA YƐ MAA YƐNE ALIEMAAMƆ AFĨA KƆYƐ KAMA Ɔ
<<<P>>>
4 Sɛ yɛne yeliemamɔ kɔhɔso kɔfa yɛnwo adamvoa a, sana yɛfa y’adwene yesie subaen pá bɔ ɔwɔ bɛnwo naso. Nikyee kõ bɔ ɔkɔboka yɛ maa yɛkɔyɛ sɔ yeɛ ole kyɛ yekonya bɛnwo alagye, na yɛne bɛ abɔ ngɔmmɔ. Sɔ yeɛ ne somafoɔ Pɔɔlo kuro kyɛ ɔkɔyɛ ɔ. Yeti ɔhworɔ ɔɔmane Tɛsalonikafoɛ nemɔ kyɛ: ‘Yɛnwo pere yɛ kyɛ yɛkonwu ɛmɔ bieku.’ (1 Tɛs. 2:17) Aliemaamɔ pee nala anwu ye kyɛ, sɛ benwu beliemamɔ nyunu na bɛne bɛ bɔ ngɔmmɔ a, ɔmaa bepingye bɛnwo paa tra kyɛ daa bɛkɔfrɛ bɛ wɔ fon so mbaen ɔ. Nekaa bɔ yɛkora yɛne aliemaamɔ yɛ sɔ paa yeɛ ole asɛnga nu, adesua, anaa nhyiamu bo. Nakoso sɛ yɛyɛ sɔ po a, ɛhene ngyire kyɛ edwirɛ biala ngɔhora ngɔwa yɛne aliemaamɔ afĩa.
<<<P>>>
5 Sɛ yɛne aliemaa bie afĩa pena sɛkye a, subaen biemɔ wɔ berɛ bɔ ɔkɔboka yɛ maa yɛne ye afĩa kosa kɔyɛ kama ɔ. Sɔ subaen nemɔ aayɛ tekyɛ ɔyɛl bɔ bɛfa begua kar yigin nu maa ɔkora yɛ adwuma yé ɔ. Ɛhene yeɛ ole anwobrɛaseɛ, aboterɛ, paayɛlɛ, ɔne anwumvoinnwunɛ. (Kenga Kolosefoɛ 3:12.) Ye de, nekaabɔ yadwu ye, ɛmɔma yɛnea nningyein nza biemɔ bɔ ɔkɔhora kɔsɛkye yɛne aliemaamɔ afĩa ɔ. Yekosa yɛkɔnea kyɛbɔ Yesu lale anwobrɛaseɛ, aboterɛ, paayɛlɛ, ɔne anwumvoinnwunɛ ali ɔ. Ɛhene kɔmaa yekonwu kyɛbɔ sɛ yesuesua ye a, ɔkɔmaa yɛne aliemaamɔ afĩa kɔhɔso kɔyɛ kama ɔ.

5. Sɛ yɛne aliemaa bie afĩa pena sɛkye a, nzu yeɛ ɔkɔboka yɛ ɔ? Ma yenwo mvandoho. (Kolosefoɛ 3:12)

MMERƐ BƆ ƐNE ALIEMAA BIE NYƐ ADWENE Ɔ
<<<P>>>
6 Bɔ ɔkɔhora kosi ɔ: Sɛ mmenia nyɔ nyɛ adwene wɔ edwirɛ bie anwo a, ɛyɛ a benu biala nwu ye kyɛ ye deɛ yeɛ ɔte pá ɔ. Wɔde ma yɛnea sɔ aliemaa nyɔ hemɔ. Besusu nekaa bɔ odikyɛ aliemaamɔ kɔ asɛnga wɔ bɛ kue nu nanwo. Benu biala le nekaa bɔ ɔpena kyɛ bɛkɔ asɛnga ne ɔ, na ebiala nwu ye kyɛ ye deɛ bɔ ɔkã ne, yeɛ ɔte pá ɔ. Yeti ebiaa benu biala ngɔbrɛ yenwo ase, na wale bɔ ye mmango kã ne wato nu. Ennwu ye kyɛ sɛ bannea yé a, sɔ dwirɛ kaamba he kɔhora kɔkakyi edwirɛ pirikua, na wasɛkye b’afĩa anaa? Sɛ banziesie edwirɛ ne ndɛ a, ɔkɔhora kɔmaa ebiala kɔfɛ ye mmango so yaa, na wafa yenwo dwirɛ wawura ye nu. Afei ngakangaka ebiaa benu biala ngodwudwo ye mmango nwo, na bɛnye kɔwa bɛnwo so ne, né sɔ dwirɛ kaamba he amaa b’afĩa atete koraa.
<<<P>>>
7 Suesua kyɛbɔ ne Yesu si brɛ yenwo ase ne. Yesu maa ye asuafoɛ nemɔ nwuni kyɛ sɛ bɛbrɛ bɛnwo ase a, ɔkɔboka bɛ paa mmerɛ bɔ b’adwene nyia wɔ edwirɛ bie anwo ne. Né asuafoɛ nemɔ taa su bɔ odikyɛ ɔyɛ bɛso panyi nanwo aporowaa. Yesu nwuni kyɛ ne sɔ nikyee ne pena ka bɛ berɛ. Yeti ɔmaa benwuni kyɛ odikyɛ bebu bɛ mmango mɔ kyɛ bɛtra bɛ. (Mat. 20:25-28) Aleɛ bahyẽ maa bekũ Yesu ne koso, ɔyɛle kyɛbɔ bɛkɔbrɛ bɛnwo ase nanwo “nhwɛsoɛ” mane bɛ. Yeɛ ole kyɛ, sɔ kɔngɔen ne onwunzini bɛ gyabo. Sɔ adwuma bɔ ɔyɛle ne, ngoaa yeɛ ne bɛyɛ ɔ. (Yoh. 13:3-5, 12-16) Yeti Yesu nate ɛhene aso hyirele bɛ nikyee bɔ ɔkɔboka bɛ maa bɛkɔyɛ kõ ɔ. Yeɛ ole kyɛ bɛkɔbrɛ bɛnwo ase, na sɛ ɔkɔba kyɛ bɛne ebie nyɛ adwene po a, bekole bɔ ɔkã ne bɛkɔto nu, na bɛngɔma ɛhene ngɔsɛkye b’afĩa.
