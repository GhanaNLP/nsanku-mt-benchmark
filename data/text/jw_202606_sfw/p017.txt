Ateepakyelɛfoɛ ne aliemaa biemɔ bɔ bɛkora
bekyirekyire kama wɔ asɛnga nu bɔ bɛwɔ wɔ
kue nanu ne, ɛkɔhora kɔmaa bɛkɔboka wɔ. Bɔ
ɛkɔhora kɔmaa bɛkɔyɛ ne bie yeɛ ole kyɛ, sɛ
ɛmɔyie asɛnga nhyiamu a, ɛkɔmaa bɛne bɛbɔ
bahyɛ asɛnga bo foforɛ ne kɔnate. Ɛhene kɔ-
maa bɛkɔboka bɛ maa bekonwu kyɛbɔ bɛne
mmenia kɔhyɛ yebo kɔbɔ ngɔmmɔ, bɛkɔyɛ sa-
kɔnea, ɔne Bible adesua ɔ. Ye de nekaa bɔ ya-
dwu ye, ɛmɔma yɛnea nningyein nza biemɔ bɔ
yɛkɔhora yɛkɔyɛ na wamaa kue bɔ yɛwɔ nu ne,
wagyina ɔ.

ngunaen. (Kenga Romanfoɛ 1:12.) Afei koso
ɔmaa enya akokoduro fa di mmenia adanzeɛ,

yeɛ ɛnate ɔ. Ebiaa ɔnate wɔ adwuma ti, ɔmaa
ɔyɛ se kyɛ ɛne aliemaamɔ bɔ bɛwɔ wɔ kue nanu
ne kɔhɔ asɛnga ɔ. Sɛ sɔ a, ɛkɔhora kɔsrɛ atee
kofi w’adwumawura berɛ. Ɛyɛ a ma onwu ye
kyɛ sɛ ɔma w’atee maa ɛkɔ a, ɛkɔyɛ adwuma ne
kowura nu kyẽa foforɛ, na ebiaa ɔkɔpene so po
maa ɔkɔyɛ wɔ nwanwa. (Neh. 2:4-6) Sɛ anwo-
nyerɛ anaa mbanyiyɛlɛ yeɛ ɔte wɔ haw a, kã

9 Ma aliemaamɔ nye hã. Sɛ ɛyɛ sɔ a, kyɛbɔ Bi-
ble ne kã ne, né ‘ébɔ mmɔden wɔ ɛwɔfoɛsolɛ
nwo.’ (Kenga Romanfoɛ 12:13.) Bɔ ɛkɔhora
kɔyɛ ne bie yeɛ ole kyɛ, ɛkɔmaa bɛkɔyɛ asɛnga
nhyiamu wɔ w’awuro. Wɔ asafo biemɔ nu ne,
bayɛ nhyehyɛɛ kyɛ, sɛ ebie fi asafo foforɛ nu
bama tɔɔk wɔ berɛ a, aliemaamɔ bɔ bɛwɔ kue
nemɔ nu ne, yeɛ bɛkɔma ye aleɛ ɔ. Sɛ ɔba
sɔ a, kue bɔ okodwu bɛ so ne, bɛne sɔ aliemaa
ne yianu, na bɛbɔnu bedidi bɛbɔ ngɔmmɔ. Sɛ
aliemaa ne angora kɔ po a, bɛbɔ bɛwɔ kue
nanu ne de, bɛbɔnu bedidi na bɛbɔ ngɔmmɔ.
Sarah te ateepakyelɛnie titire, na ɔhane kyɛ:
“Bɛbɔ bɛwɔ me kue nanu ne, sɛ meto mesa me-
frɛ bɛ kyɛ bɛbra m’awuro a, ɛyɛ a ne wakakyi
me adwenedwene. Ofikyɛ ɛyɛ a ne mesuro kyɛ
ebiaa ne aleɛ ne anyɛ fɛ. Nakoso maanwu ye
kyɛ sɛ yenya mmerɛ pee yɛbɔ ngɔmmɔ a, ɛhene
mmom yeɛ yenwo hia ɔ. Yesɔ nati, ɛyɛ a mendõ
aleɛ bɔ yenwo adwuma yɛlɛ yɛ se ɔ.” Sɛ yɛboka
maa y’asɛnga kue nanu bayɛ anikã a, ɔmaa
yɛmu yɛfa anigye yesõ Yehowa tekyɛ mmenia
bɔ befi abusua kõ bie nu ɔ.—Neh. 8:10; Aso.
20:35.
