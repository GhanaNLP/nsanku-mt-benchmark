11 Suesua kyɛbɔ ne Yesu si le aboterɛ ne. Sɛ yɛnea Yesu asomafoɛ nemɔ a, ne benu biemɔ le subaen bie bɔ ahaa ɔkɔmaa ɔkɔyɛ se kyɛ Yesu ne bɛ kɔnate ɔ. Yakobo ne Yohane sɔa, ne bekuro poos paa. Yeti bɛhã behyirele Yesu po kyɛ ɔbɔ bɛ panyi wɔ ye Ahennie nanu. (Mak. 10:35-37) Nakoso Yesu de, ne ɔtte sɔ koraa. Ne ɔbrɛ yenwo ase, yeti dibea kandinga ne animnyam bɔ ne ole ye wɔ anwuro dɔ ne, ɔyakyili yemukoraati tole berɛ wale aseɛ he aso. (Flp. 2:5-8) Sɛ Yesu te ebie a, ahaa ɔkɔhã kyɛ bɛ subaen ti ɔne bɛ ngɔbɔ. Nakoso, ɔhɔle so nyane Yakobo ne Yohane ne asomafoɛ bɔ baha nanwo aboterɛ, na ɔne bɛ natele.

12 Ne Yesu se kyɛ ye asomafoɛ nemɔ aayɛ sona ye de, nna bɛ subaen namu yeɛ yenye kɔgye yenwo ɔ. Yakobo ne Yohane sɔaa, mmerɛ bɔ bɛhã behyirele ye kyɛ ɔma bɛ poos wɔ ye Ahennie nanu ne, wanyɛ ye nwanwa. Ofikyɛ ne ɔse kyɛ nekaa bɔ bɛtetele bɛ ne de, poos nwo hia bɛ paa. Sɛ yɛnea asɔre mbanyi nemɔ sɔa, ne bebu dibea ne animnyam bɔ ebie konya ne kyɛ yenwo hia tra nikyee biala. Na sɔ yeɛ ne asuafoɛ bɔ baha ne koso bɛte ɔ. (Mak. 9:34) Yesu bɔbɔ deinn po, nekaa bɔ ɔtena nyini ne, sɔ subaen nala yeɛ ne ɔwɔ mmenia nwo ɔ. Yeti ne Yesu se kyɛ nna elɛhomapɛ yeɛ bekɔhora bekotu sɔ subaen nabo ɔ. Ɛhene maa onyane bɛnwo aboterɛ, na ɔhɔle so bokale bɛ.—Mak. 10:42-45.

13 Nzu yeɛ ɔkɔboka wɔ, na ɛne sona ne afĩa anzɛkye ɔ? Bɛbɔ bele subaen bie bɔ wɔnye ngye yenwo ne, bɔ mmɔden kyɛ ekonya bɛnwo aboterɛ. (Any. 14:29) Ɔsono kyɛbɔ yɛnu biala si te ɔ. Subaen bɔ ɔwɔ yɛnwo ne, nu pee wɔ berɛ a, nna kyɛ ɔttemaye na mmom sɔ ala yeɛ yɛte ɔ. Yeti sɛ yɛda sɔ subaen ne ali na mmenia nya yɛnwo aboterɛ a, ɔmaa yɛnye gye. (Kenga Ɛfesofoɛ 4:2.) Odikyɛ yenwu ye kyɛ subaen bɔ ɔwɔ ebiemɔ nwo ne, nna yemu yeɛ yɛnye kɔgye yenwo ɔ. Yeti sɛ bɛda sɔ subaen ne ali a, ɔnzɛkyɛ yɛmaa ɔha yɛ. Wɔde ma yɛnea ebie bɔ ɔfɛle nikye anaa onguro dwudwolɛ ɔ, sɔ sona ne, sana omia yenye kora ne ɔne mmenia abɔ ngɔmmɔ. Yeɛ sɛ ete dinndinn koso a, bɔ mmɔden kyɛ ekonya bɛbɔ bekuro dwudwolɛ nemɔ nwo aboterɛ. Ɔwɔ nu, nna aliemaamɔ mukoraati yeɛ bɛkɔhora bɛkɔyɛ yɛ damvo paa ɔ. Nakoso bɛbɔ bele subaen bie bɔ yɛnye ngye yenwo ne po, sɛ yenya bɛnwo aboterɛ a, yɛkɔhora yɛne bɛ kɔtena kama.
