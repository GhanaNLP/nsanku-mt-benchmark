Sɛ ɛbrɛ wɔnwo ase a, ɔkɔmaa ekobu wɔnye kogua nningyein mmaammaa biemɔ so, na afa bɔne ahyɛ
(Nea ngyekyɛmue 8)
<<<P>>>
8 Nzu yee ɔkɔboka wɔ, na ɛne sona ne afĩa an-zɛkye ɔ? Sɛ ɛbrɛ wɔnwo ase a, ɔkɔmaa eko-bu wɔnye kogua nningyein mmaammaa biemɔ so, na afa bɔne ahyɛ. (Kenga Kolosefoɛ 3:13.) Sɛ ye po wɔ damvo ne kã edwirɛ yeayea bie kyire wɔ a, odikye ɛkae kyɛ wɔ koso odwu mmerɛ bie a ɛkora kã edwirɛ bie maa siɛ ye enu wɔnwo. (Nwo. 7:21, 22) Yeti sɛ ɛbrɛ wɔ-nwo ase a, ɛkɔhyɛ wɔnwo so, na ɔkɔmaa ɛngɔ-yɛ nikyeebiala bɔ ɔkɔsɛkye ɛne sona n’afĩa ɔ. (Edw. 4:4) Sɛ ɛne sona ne nyɛ adwene a, bisa wɔnwo kyɛ: “Kyɛ ngɔhwe edwirɛ ne twaan kyɛ me deɛ yee ɔte pá ɔ, ɔne kyɛ ngɔyaki maa ɔkɔha ne, beni yeɛ ɔkɔboka ɔ?” Sɛ ɛdwene edwirɛ nanwo sɔ a, ɔkɔmaa ekole bɔ sona ne kã ne kɔto nu kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a asomdwee kɔwa ɔ. Afei koso bɔ wasi ne, ɛyɛ a nnɛkɔfa yenwo dwirɛ ba kõ. Ɛyɛ sɔ a, wɔ soanu kodwo wɔ.
<<<P>>>
9 Sɛ edwirɛ ba ɛmɔ afĩa a, anwobrɛaseɛ kɔ-boka wɔ maa engolle ngogyina nu kyɛ wɔ yeɛ wɔ dwirɛ yɛ fɛ ɔ. (Kenga Anyandera 17:9; 1 Kor. 6:7) Kae kyɛ bɔ ohia wɔ alaa yeɛ ole kyɛ ɛne sona ne afĩa kɔyɛ kama ɔ. Yeti ɛkɔhora kɔhɔ sona ne gya so, na ɛne ye aasiesie ɛmɔ afĩa. Sɔ yeɛ aliemaamɔ pee ayɛ ɔ, na banwu ye kyɛ waboka paa. (Edw. 34:14) Sɛ ɛkɔ sona ne berɛ a, ebiaa ɛkɔhora kɔhã kyɛ: ‘Nniema, ɛlɛ-ho dwirɛ bɔ osili ne, menguro kyɛ ɔkɔsɛkye yɛ afĩa. Yeti mepena kyɛ yɛkã yenwo dwirɛ.’ Afei koso ɛyɛ a ma onwu ye kyɛ wɔ yeɛ ɛmaa ɔwale ye sɔ ɔ, yeti ɛpa ye kyerɛ, ɔyakyi ma ɔha. Sɛ aliemaa ne koso yeɛ ɔwale wɔ berɛ kyɛ ɔbaapa wɔ kyerɛ a, dé tonu. Ɛyɛ sɔ a, okyire kyɛ ɛbrɛ wɔnwo ase. (Luka 17:3, 4) Kae kyɛ sɛ ɛkɔ eliema ne berɛ a, ɛkwaapena asomdwee, na mmom nna kyɛ ɛkwaahyire bɔ ye dwirɛ yɛ fɛ, anaa bɔ odi fɔ ɔ.—Any. 18:24.
