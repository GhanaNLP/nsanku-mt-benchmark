Asawu Bɔ Ne Bɛfa Bɛkye Egyein Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Bible ne maa yenwu ye kyɛ, Yesu asuafoɛ ne biemɔ wɔ berɛ a, adwuma bɔ ne bɛyɛ yeɛ ole kyɛ bɛkɔ ɛpo bɛkɔkye naen. (Mak. 1:16-20) Ne bɛyɛ ye wɔ Galilea Po naso, na asawu yeɛ ne bɛfa be-kye naen ne ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Ne bɛfa maka nyamaa ne koaa nyamaa yeɛ bɛ-bɔnu bɛnwo asawu ne ɔ. Ɛyɛ a bɛmaa ndokoro-ndokoro deda ye afĩa, kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a ɔkɔhora kɔ-hye egyein ne ɔ. Sɛ bɛpena kyɛ bɛkye egyein mbiri a, ne bɛmaa ndokoro ne yɛ mbiri. Yeɛ sɛ bɛpena kyɛ bɛkye egyein mmaammaa koso a, ne bɛmaa ndokoro ne yɛ mmaammaa. Asawu bɔ ɔte kãa ne, ɛyɛ a ne bɛfa sum anaa nyɔboɛ mmaam-maa besinze yenoa, kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a sɛ ogua nzue nanu a, ɔkɔhora kɔmono ɔ. Asawu mbiri ne koso, ne bɛfa sum mbiri-mbiri bɛsosɔ yebo na wamaa nu ayɛ no. Afei bɛfa tabɔnga bɛsosɔ ye anwuro ne, kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a begua asawu ne a, ɔkɔɔtɛ nzue ne nye ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Sɛ begua asawu ne a, ne nyɔboɛ mbiri-mbiri bɔ ɔwɔ ɛpo nabo dɔ ne, ɔkora tete ye. Yeɛ sɛ bɛkye egyein bɔ ɔsone koso a, ne ɔkora maa ɔba ye sɔ. (Luka 5:6) Yesɔ nati, sɛ ne bɛkɔ ɛpo bɛba bia-la a, ɔkɔyɛ kyɛ ne “bɛpomba b’asawu” ne. Yako-bo ne Yohane sɔa, sɔ yeɛ Bible ne kã kyɛ bɛyɛle ɔ. (Mat. 4:21) Ɔkɔyɛ kyɛ paneɛ ne nyamaa yeɛ ne bɛfa bɛpa ɔ. Luka koso hane ebiemɔ nwo dwirɛ kyɛ, ne “bɛnwunzi b’asawu nemɔ” kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a, bɛkɔyeye efĩɛ bɔ ne wawurowura nu ne bekofi nu ɔ. (Luka 5:2) Ɛhene si ne, ne bɛworɛ nu na wan-zekye. (Hes. 47:10) Ne bɛyɛ ɛhe mukoraati, ofi-kyɛ sɔ mmerɛ ne, ne asawu nyɔboɛ yɛ se paa.
<<<P>>>
Yesu hahyirele mmrienzua bɔ ne bɛyɛ sɔ adwu-ma ne kyɛ, beeli ye si, na ‘bɛhwehwe mmenia bɛ-brɛ ye.’
