Nnamvoa bɔ bekuro bɛnwo dwirɛ ne, nzue nu oo, eyia oo, bɛboka bɛnwo
(Nea ngyekyemue 19)
<<<P>>>
y’adwene yesie subaen pá bɔ ɔwɔ yeliemamɔ nwo naso a, ne yesuesua Yesu ne ye Baba Ye-howa.
<<<P>>>
15 Nikyee bɔ yenwo hia paa yeɛ ole kyɛ, yɛ-kɔsrɛ Yehowa kyɛ yeliemamɔ bɔ ɔsono bɛ su-baen ne, ɔboka yɛ ma yɛhora yɛne bɛ tena kama. Ɛyɛ a dimoa dwene nningyein biemɔ bɔ sɛ sona ne yɛ a, ɔkora ha wɔ nanwo, na bɔ ye-nwo mbaeɛ, kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a sɛ sona ne yɛ sɔ a, ɛkɔhora kɔhyɛ wɔnwo so ɔ. Kae kyɛ Yehowa bɔ ɔbɔle yɛ ne, ɔse kyɛ ɔsono kyɛbɔ yɛnu biala si te ɔ. Ɛhene ati ɔse bɔ ehia na wamaa ɛne sona ne ahora atena asomdwee nu ɔ. Yeti sɛ ɛbɔ ye mbaeɛ a, nya gyidie kyɛ ɔkɔboka wɔ. Sɛ ɔkɔba kyɛ bɔ sona ne yɛ ne, ɔmaa ɔyɛ se kyɛ ɛkɔhɔso konya yenwo aboterɛ a, srɛ Yehowa kyɛ ɔfa ye sunzum krongron ne boka wɔ, na anyɛ ni-kyeebie bɔ ɔttemaye ɔ.—Luka 11:13; Gal. 5:22, 23.

MMERƐ BƆ YELIEMAMƆ FA ƆHAW BIE NU NE
<<<P>>>
16 Bɔ ɔkɔhora kosi ɔ: Sɛ yeliemamɔ fa ɔhaw bie nu a, ɔkora maa bɛyɛ nningyein biemɔ bɔ ɔkɔha ye ɔ. Ebie kora yɛ anwonyerɛ, anaa adwenedwene bie bɔ waatɔ bɛ so ɔ. Ɔba sɔ a, ebiaa benguro kyɛ bekodwudwo ebiala nwo, anaa edwirɛ kãa bie po a ne bafɛ yaa. Bɛ-kɔhora koso bɛkɔyɛ nikyeebie, anaa bɛkɔhã edwirɛ bie maa ɔkɔyɛ yɛ yea. (Yob 6:2, 3) Odwu mmerɛ bie a, ebiaa ne yɛnze ɔhaw bɔ yeliema bie fa nu ɔ. Yeti annea a, bɔ yeti yeɛ bɛyɛ bɛ nningyein wɔ atee bie aso ne, yɛngɔte yebo.
<<<P>>>
17 Suesua kyɛbɔ ne Yesu si nwu mmenia an-wumvoin, na ɔboka bɛ ne. Mmenia bɔ ne be-nwu amaneɛ ne, ne bɛ dwirɛ yɛ Yesu anwumvo yeti ɔbokale bɛ. Benu biemɔ wɔ berɛ po a, ne ɔnze bɛ. Ebie yeɛ ole anyesiniwaafoɛ nyɔ bie bɔ ɔtele bɛnye ne. Ne bɛfrɛ kɔ Batemeo. Mmerɛ bɔ ɔtele kyɛ Yesu pɛnu wɔ nekaa bɔ ɔwɔ ne, ɔteateane nu kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a Yesu ko-nwu ye ɔ. Bɔ ne ɔyɛ ne, ɔkɔyɛ kyɛ ne ɔyɛ ebiemɔ ahi paa, yeti bɛteateane ye kyɛ omua yenoa. Nakoso bekye po bɔ ne ɔteatea nu se-rɛserɛ paa ɔ, ofikyɛ ne ɔpena kyɛ Yesu sa ye anwonyerɛ. Yesu de, ne Batemeo nwo yɛ ye anwumvo paa yeti wamma bɔ ne ɔyɛ ne anha ye. (Mat. 20:34; Mak. 10:46-52) Na mmom, ɔkamvole ye wɔ sɔ gyidie bɔ onyane ye nanwo, na ɔsale ye anwonyerɛ.
