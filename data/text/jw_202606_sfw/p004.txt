paa kyɛ Yehowa yeɛ ɔbɔle nningyein mukoraati ɔ, yeti odikyɛ bɛsõ ye. Afei koso ne bɛse kyɛ Yesu te Nyameɛ Awa, na sɛ betie ye a, bekɔnya daa ngoa. Ne besa bɛse ye kyɛ, Nyameɛ ta Yesu si wɔ bɔ ɔyɛ biala nu. Yɛ koso nningyein pee wɔ berɛ bɔ yasua yafa Yehowa nwo ɔ, na ɛhene boka yɛ maa sɛ nzɔhwɛ bie to yɛ a, yɛkora yegyina yenoa. Ye de kesaalae, ɛmɔma yesusu sɔ nningyein nanu nza nwo, na yɛnea kyɛbɔ ɔkɔboka yɛ maa yɛ gyidie kɔyɛ se ɔ. (1) Yehowa yeɛ ɔbɔle nningyein mukoraati ɔ, (2) Yehowa yeɛ ɔmaa bɛhworɔle Bible ne ɔ, (3) Yehowa boka ye mmenia so ɛnnɛ. Sɛ yedé yɛdi kyɛ sɔ dwirɛ porɛ nza hemɔ te nahorɛ a, ɔkɔmaa yɛ gyidie kɔyɛ se na yahora yagyina nzɔhwɛ biala noa.

7. Nzu yeɛ Nyekyire 4:11 kã fa Yehowa nwo ɔ, na sɛ nzɔhwɛ bie to yɛ a, sɛɛ yeɛ sɔ dwirɛ ne kɔboka yɛ ɔ?
<<<P>>>
tumi bɔ Yehowa le ye nanwo a, ɔkɔmaa yekonya gyidie paa kyɛ ɔhaw biala bɔ ɔkɔto yɛ ne, ɔkɔboka yɛ maa yekogyina yenoa. Nakoso Satan alakalaka mmeni pee wɔ ewiase he anu maa banya adwene kyɛ Nyameɛ biala nne berɛ. Ɛhene ati, bɛbɔ basua saens ne, bɛnu biemɔ po nwa nna ebiala yeɛ ɔbɔle nningyein ɔ. Yenwo hia paa kyɛ yekonwu bɔ yeti yeɛ yedé yedi kyɛ Yehowa yeɛ ɔbɔle nningyein ɔ, na okuro yɛ dwirɛ. Sɛ ɔba sɔ a, ɔhaw bie to yɛ a, yekole yekoli paa kyɛ ɔwɔ berɛ, yeti ɔkɔboka yɛ.—Heb. 11:6.
