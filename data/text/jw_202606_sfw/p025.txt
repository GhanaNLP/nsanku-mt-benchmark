17 Nzu bieku yee sɛ ɛyɛ a, ɔkɔboka wɔ maa ɛkɔ adesua a ekotie bɔ ɔkɔso ne yé ɔ? (1) Sɛ ɛbaahɔ adesua a, ɛyɛ a nnedidi pee kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a ɛngɔla ɔ. (2) Ɛyɛ a dwu berɛ ndɛ, na pena nekaa tena kora ne bahyɛ adesua nabo. (3) Sɛ adesua ne kɔ so a, fa wɔ fon anaa wɔ tablɛt ne to nekaa bɔ ɔngɔyɛ dede ɔ. Afei nnɛyɛ nikyeebiala wɔ fon naso bɔ ɔkɔmaa w’adwene kofi adesua naso ɔ. (4) Bɔ yekosua ne, ɛyɛ a dimoa dwenedwene yenwo. (5) Ɛyɛ a nea sona bɔ ɔma tɔɔk ne nyunu tra kyɛ kãa ne apɛne wɔ kɔme anea ahamneɛ ɔ. (6) Sɛ adesua ne kɔso a, ɛkɔhora kɔhworɔ edwirɛ kãa, anaa ɛkɔdrɔ nikyeebie kɔto berɛ. (7) Tworɔnzɛm ahoroɛ bɔ sona ne kɔkenga ne, ɛyɛ a susu yenwo nea kyɛbɔ osi foa edwirɛ bɔ okyirekyire nu naso ɔ. (8) Bisa wɔnwo kyɛ, ‘Ngɔyɛ sɛɛ ne mafa bɔ masua ne maboka ebie wɔ asafo nanu, anaa asɛnga nu?’
<<<P>>>
(9) Sɛ ɔte nhyiamu koso yee ɛbaahɔ a, dimoa nea program naso na susu nhyiamu ne dwirɛti, tɔɔk ahoroɛ bɔ bɛkɔma, ɔne tworɔnzɛm bɔ tɔɔk ahoroɛ ne gyi so nanwo. (10) Dwein nwo vidio bɔ bɛkɔbɔ kora ne nhyiamu ne ahyɛ yebo ne, ɛyɛ a tena ase konn na nea bie. Ɛhene kɔmaa ɛkɔboa wɔnwo kɔma dwumadie ne.

MMERƐ BIALA BƆ YEHOWA KYIREKYIRE WƆ NE, TIE YE YÉ
<<<P>>>
18 Mmerɛ biala bɔ yɛkɔhɔ adesua ne, yenwo hia paa kyɛ yekotie bɔ ɔkɔso ne yé. Nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ sɛ yɛyɛ sɔ a, ne yɛma Yehowa atee maa odi ‘yɛ nyunu fa tenenee atee so.’ (Edw. 23:3; 31:3) Ewiase bɔ yɛwɔ nu ne, ɔlla ase kyɛ yɛkɔyɛ bɔ Yehowa kuro ɔ. Yeti afutue bɔ Yehowa fa yɛ wɔ adesua bo ne, ɛhene yee ɔboka yɛ maa yɛkora yɛyɛ bɔ okuro ɔ. Yesɔ nati sɛ yɛkɔ adesua a, odikyɛ ‘yetie bɔ ɔkɔso ne ye paa,’ ná yɛfa bɔ yate ne, yɛbɔ yɛ bra. (Luka 8:18) Bɔ ɔte yee ole kyɛ, sɛ ebie tie Yehowa a, yenye kɔgye sɔ mmerɛ he, na okonya daa ngoa koso kyẽabie.—Edw. 119:2, 14.
