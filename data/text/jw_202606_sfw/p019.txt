14 Sɛ edwirɛ bie si a, ɔmaa enya mmokalɛ. Bɔ yɛkã ye, ebie yee ole mmerɛ bɔ atorɛngyɛm bie asi, anwonyerɛ bie ate atese, maen ne sikasɛm ayɛ basaa, awaen ne abra asɛnga adwuma ne, anaa edwirɛ bie bɔ ɔte sɔ asi maa yɛnwo ahã ne. Sɛ edwirɛ bɔ te sɔ si a, kue soneafoɛ biala kɔpena aliemaamɔ bɔ bɛwɔ ye kue nanu ne boso, na wanea kyɛ bekohia mmokalɛ biemɔ anaa. Ebie kɔhora kɔyɛ ayire, aleɛ, nzue, ndaadeɛ anaa ebiaa nekaa po bɔ bɛkɔla ɔ. 15 Kyɛbɔ yalimoa yanwu ye ne, bɛbɔ bɛsɔ Yehowa ne, bɛsone paa. Abɔfo bɔ bedi nahorɛ bɔ bɛwɔ anwuro ne sɔa, bɛ somaa ne, sɛ ɛka a enwu ye noa. Yɛɛ ɛba aseɛ he aso koso a, mmenia bɔ bɛsɔ Yehowa ne, bɛbo miliɔn ngona, na bɛ dodoɔ ne hɔlɛ alaa yeɛ ɔkɔ ye nyunu ɔ. (Sak. 8:23; Nye. 5:11) Yɛ ne sɔ abɔfo nworanwora ne, yɛboka mmeni pee maa bɛte Nyameɛ Ahennie nanwo dwirɛ. (Nye. 14:6, 7) Yeti ɛmɔma yɛmukoraati yɛyere yenwo kyɛ yɛkɔboka maa y’asɛnga kue nemɔ kogyina. Ɔba sɔ a, yekonya so mvasoɛ, na okosa kɔboka yɛ maa yekofi y’ahone mukoraati nu yɛkɔye Yehowa ayɛ.—Edw. 111:1.
