9 Yeti mmerɛ foforɛ bɔ ésua nikyee ne, susu adanzeɛ ahoroɛ bɔ ɔmaa edé di kyɛ Nyameɛ wɔ berɛ nanwo kɔ siɛ. Sɔ nhwehwɛmuɛ bɔ ɛkɔyɛ ne, ɔkɔboka maa wɔ gyidie kɔyɛ se, na ɔkɔmaa ekonwu Yehowa subaen ahoroɛ nemɔ yé paa. (Rom. 1:20) Afei, bɛbɔ b’adwene nu yɛ bɛ naa kyɛ ebiaa Nyameɛ wɔ berɛ, anna ɔnne berɛ ne, ɛkɔhora kɔfa bɔ asua ne kɔboka bɛ. Kyɛbɔ Bible ne maa yenwu ye ne, nningyein nwora-nwora bɔ Nyameɛ abɔ ne, ɔte adanzeɛ turoloo bɔ okyire kyɛ ɔwɔ berɛ amba.

10 Kenga 2 Timoteo 3:16, 17. Bible ne te nwomaa bɔ yenwo hia yɛ paa, ofikyɛ yɛse kyɛ Yehowa noa dwirɛ yɛɛ ɔwɔ nu ɔ, na ɔmaa bɛ-hworɔ bɛmane mmenia mukoraati. Bible ne maa yenya edwirɛ biemɔ bɔ yenwo hia nwo mmuayɛɛ. Ebie yɛɛ ole kyɛ, nwa yɛɛ ɔmaa ngoa wale ɔ, nzuati yɛɛ yɛwɔ ewiase ɔ, na nzu yɛɛ Nyameɛ kuro kyɛ yɛyɛ ɔ? 11 Yele nningyein pee bɔ ɔmaa yedé yedi paa kyɛ Nyameɛ yɛɛ ɔmaa bɛhworɔle Bible ne ɔ. Ebie yɛɛ ole kyɛ, sɛ Bible ne kã edwirɛ bie-mɔ bɔ osisili nanwo dwirɛ a, ɔkã ye pɛpɛɛpɛ. (Luka 3:1, 2) Afei koso, bɔ ɔkã fa nningyein bɔ Nyameɛ abɔ nanwo ne, ɔte nahorɛ. (Yob 26:7) Ebieku koso, mmɔden bɔ Satan abɔ kyɛ ɔkɔmaa bɛkɔsɛkye Bible ne, wayɛ mbaen. Ofi-kyɛ ɛnnɛ, mmeni pee nala yɛɛ bele bie ɔ, na bahyire yebo koso bahɔ dwudwolɛ pee nu. Mmrienzua 40 yɛɛ bɛhworɔle Bible ne ɔ, na né ɔsono adwuma bɔ benu biala yɛ, ɔne nekaa bɔ ofi ɔ. Afei koso olili kɔyɛ afoɛ 1,600 kora yɛɛ bɛtworɔ beyie ɔ. Nakoso, nu dwirɛ ne yia pɛpɛɛpɛ. Yɛkora yenwu ye kyɛ bɛhworɔ-le Bible ne ahyɛ paa, nakoso afutue bɔ ɔwɔ nu ne, mmeni pee té nya so mvasoɛ ɛnnɛ. (Any. 13:20; 15:21; Mat. 7:12) Ebieku koso, ngɔm bɔ bɛhyele ye wɔ Bible nanu ne, pee awa nu. Ɛhene ati yɛkɔhora yɛkɔfa yɛnwo yekɔto Bible naso. (Yos. 23:14) Ɛhe mukoraati maa yenwu ye paa kyɛ amba, Nyameɛ yɛɛ ɔmaa bɛ-hworɔle Bible ne ɔ.
