18 Nzu yee ɔkɔboka wɔ, na ɛne sona ne afĩa anzɛkye ɔ? Bible ne kã kyɛ, ‘yɛkyekye bɛbɔ b’abasa nu abu nemɔ wora.’ (Kenga 1 Tɛsalonikafoɛ 5:14.) Yeti sɛ ɛdwene aliemaamɔ nwo a, ɔkɔmaa ekonwu bɛ anwumvoin, na ahora ahã edwirɛ bie afa akyekye bɛ wora mmerɛ bɔ bɛnde apɔ, anaa adwenedwene ahyɛ bɛ so ne. Kae kyɛ ebie bɔ okuro ye damvo dwirɛ ne, sɛ ye damvo ne nwu ‘amanee’ a, sɔ mmerɛ ne paa yee ɔboka ye ɔ. (Any. 17:17) Sɔ sona ne, nna ye noa mbaen yee ɔfa kã kyɛ bɔ wato ye damvo ne, ɔyɛ ye yea ɔ. Na mmom ɔkyekye ye wora, anaa ɔyɛ nikyee pɔtee bie fa boka ye.

19 Ɔwɔ nu sɛ aliemaa bie fa ɔhaw nu a, ɛngɔhora ngɔye ngofi berɛ. Nakoso, ɛkɔhora kɔyɛ nningyein biemɔ kɔfa kohyire kyɛ ɛdwene sona nanwo, na bɔ wato ye ne yɛ wɔ yea. Ɛkɔyɛ ye sɛ? Yee ole kyɛ ɛkɔfa ye kyɛ bɔ wato ye ne, wɔ yee wato wɔ ɔ. (Mat. 7:12; 1 Pet. 3:8) Sɛ sona ne kã bɔ wato ye nanwo dwirɛ a, ɛyɛ a to wɔboase tie ye, na sɛ ɛnea na ɔkɔyɛ yé a, kã edwirɛ bie fa kyekye ye wora. (Any. 12:25) Nnɛpre wɔnwo kɔkã edwirɛ bie mmerɛ bɔ ɛnze bɔ wasi ne mukoraati ne. (Any. 18:13) Ɛyɛ a nya yenwo aboterɛ, na sɛ ɔkã edwirɛ bie, anaa ɔyɛ nikyeebie bɔ ɔttemaye po a, nnɛfɛ ye so yaa.—Efe. 4:32

20 Ɔyɛ yɛ fɛ paa kyɛ yele aliemaamɔ bɔ bekuro yɛ dwirɛ bɔ yɛne bɛ afa adamvoa ɔ. Nakoso, odwu mmerɛ bie a, ɛyɛ a ne yɛne b’afĩa pena tete. Bɔ ɔmaa ɔba ye sɔ yee ole kyɛ, bɔne wɔ yɛ mukoraati nwo. Afei yɛnu pee wɔ berɛ a, edwirɛ biemɔ bɔ watoto yɛ nati, wamaa yanya subaen bie bɔ yeliemamɔ nguro ɔ. Ɛhene ati, sɛ yeliemamɔ brɛ bɛnwo ase, bɛnya yɛnwo aboterɛ, bɛdwene yɛnwo, na bɛboka yɛ a, ɔmaa yɛnye gye paa. Yeti ɛmɔma yɛnu biala ɔyere yenwo kyɛ ɔkɔyɛ adamvoa bɔ ɔte sɔ ɔ.
