Nakoso, yɛbɔ mmɔ-den kyɛ yekohuro yeliemamɔ dwirɛ, ná yamaa asomdwee atena yɛne b’afĩa. Aso atu atẽe ahɔ adesua wɔ nekaa foforɛ bie le? Ɛhe te adanzeɛ bɔ okyire kyɛ amba, bɔ Yehowa abɔ yetianu kyɛ ɔkɔyɛ ne, ɔnate ye mmenia so ɔma ɔba nu. Sɛ yɛmaa ɛhe tena y’adwene nu a, ɔkɔboka yɛ maa yɛkɔhɔso ye-koli nahorɛ yɛkɔma Yehowa, ɔmva yenwo ne nzɔhwɛ biala bɔ ɔkɔto yɛ ne.

17 Ɔnate kyɛ yɛte ayieleɛ mmerɛ nanu nati, nningyein nu ayɛ se paa, na ɛhene amaa yɛnu biala fa ɔhaw nu. (2 Tim. 3:1, 13) Ɛhene la ahameɛ a, nningyein biemɔ koso kora yɛ ɔhaw ma yɛ. Ebie yeɛ ole mmerɛ bɔ ebiaa asafo ne ayɛ nzakrayɛɛ bie bɔ yɛnde yebo pá ne. Ebie koso kora yɛ mmerɛ bɔ aliemaa bie ayɛ ni-kyeebie maa waha yɛ paa ne. Afei koso, ɔtɔ ɛlɛhõ a, ɔhaw bie kora to yɛ maa yɛdé yɛnwo a onne, anaa yénwu bɔ yɛ yɛ yɛnwo po ɔ.

18 Nakoso, ɔmva yenwo ne aworabɔlɛ biala bɔ sɔ haw ne amaa yédi ne, sɛ yɛkae kyɛ Ye-howa yeɛ ɔbɔle nningyein mukoraati, ye yeɛ ɔmaa bɛhworɔle Bible ne, na ɔboka ye mme-nia so a, ɔkɔmaa yɛkɔhɔso yekoli nahorɛ yɛ-kɔma ye. Yɛyɛ sɔ a, ne waayɛ kyɛ yɛkã yekyire Yehowa kyɛ: “Nwa berɛ yeɛ yɛhɔ ɔ? Wɔ yeɛ ele daa ngoa dwirɛ ɔ,” kyɛbɔ Petro hahyirele Yesu ne.
