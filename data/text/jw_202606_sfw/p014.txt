Kɔ W’anim!
<<<P>>>
BƆ ADESŨA NE KƆHÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ
Yɛkonwu mvasoɛ bɔ ɔwɔ yɛ asɛnga kue nemɔ so ɔ.
<<<P>>>
“Ngofi m’ahone nu amukoraa ngɔfa asennaa ngɔma AWURADE wɔ teneneefoɛ badwa nu.”​—EDW. 111:1.
<<<P>>>
KYẼA bɔ bɛmane wɔ atee kyɛ ɛhɔ asɛnga ne, yedé yedi kyɛ wɔnye gyele paa. Ofikyɛ sɔ kyẽa ne, eenwuni kyɛ kesaalae de aaboka mmenia, ɔne abɔfo nworanwora bɔ bɛye Yehowa ayɛ naso. (Edw. 148:1, 2, 12, 13) Nakoso nna ɛhene ngome, na mmom ebiaa bɛmaa esa nwuni kyɛ bafa wɔ baboka asɛnga kue bie aso. Ɛberɛ de, ebiaa né aliemaamɔ kãa bie yeɛ bɛwɔ nu ɔ. Y’asɛnga kue nemɔ de, ɔboka paa. Ofikyɛ ɔmaa yɛkora yɛyɛ adwuma bɔ Yesu afa awura yɛsa ne. Ɛhene yeɛ ole kyɛ yɛhã Ahennie nanwo nzɛmba ne wɔ “ewiase amukoraa.”​—Mat. 24:14.
<<<P>>>
2 Ye de, sɛɛ yeɛ w’asɛnga kue ne boka wɔ ɔ? Ɛmɔma yetie bɔ aliemaa biemɔ hane ɔ. Benu kõ yeɛ ole aliemaa brasua Wendy.* Ɔhane kyɛ: “Manya mvasoɛ pee mafi asɛnga kue bɔ nwɔ nanu naso. Ebie yeɛ ole kyɛ, aliemaamɔ bɔ mene bɛ wɔ kue nanu ne, baboka me maa mene mmenia kora bɔ ngɔmmɔ wɔ asɛnga nu, na me ngyerɛkyerɛ koso ayɛ kama. Afei koso, baboka me maa mayɛ nningyein biemɔ bɔ wamaa mapingye Yehowa ɔ. Masa koso manya nnamvoa pee bɔ bekuro me dwirɛ ɔ.” Rich koso ɔte kue soneafoɛ, na ɔhane kyɛ: “Ɔyɛ me fɛ paa kyɛ meyɛ sɔ adwuma ne bie ɔ. Ofikyɛ sɛ ebie bɔ asu yie a, mekora meboka ye maa ɔne Yehowa afĩa yɛ kama, na onya ngɔsoɛ koso wɔ asafo nanu.” Dinah koso fa bokale so kyɛ: “Asɛnga kue de, engonya ye kyɛ ɛwɔ nu ɔ. Kue bɔ nwɔ nu ne sɔa, nu yɛ fɛ paa, ofikyɛ aliemaamɔ aayɛ kyɛ m’abusuafoɛ.”
<<<P>>>
3 Wɔ adesua he anu ne, yekosusu sɔ dwirɛ hemɔ nwo: Adwuma beni yeɛ kue soneafoɛ ne ye bokafoɛ ne yɛ ɔ, na sɛɛ yeɛ bɛboka aliemaamɔ bɔ bɛwɔ bɛ kue nanu ne ɔ? Kue bɔ yɛwɔ nu ne, yɛkɔyɛ sɛɛ ne yaboka maa wagyina? Afei bɔ ɔpɛ yebo ne, mvasoɛ beni yeɛ yenya yefi kue bɔ yɛwɔ nu naso ɔ?
<<<P>>>
* Dumaa ne bie wɔ berɛ a, basesã ye.
