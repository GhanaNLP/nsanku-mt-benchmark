BƆ ADESŨA NE KƆHÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Yɛkɔhora yekoli nahorɛ yɛ-kɔma Yehowa ɔmva yenwo ne nzɔhwɛ biala bɔ ɔkɔto yɛ ne.
<<<P>>>
Sɛ Nzɔhwɛ Bie To Wɔ A, Ɛkɔyɛ Sɛɛ Né Ahɔso Anya Yehowa Nu Gyidie?
<<<P>>>
“Awurade, nwa berɛ yee yɛkɔhɔ ɔ? Wɔ yee ele daa ngoa dwirɛ ɔ.”
—YOH. 6:68.
<<<P>>>
ƐLEHÕ BIE, ne Yesu kyirekyire mmeni lɔen bie wɔ Kapanɔm, na ɔhã hyirele bɛ kyɛ: “Nahorɛ nu paa, mekã mekyire ɛmɔ kyɛ, sɛ ɛmɔanni Sona Awa ne nwonaen, ná ɛmɔanno ye mogya a, ye de ɛmɔlle ngoa wɔ ɛmɔ nu. Na ebiala bɔ odi me nwonaen ná ɔno me mogya nen, ole daa ngoa, na ngotingye ye wɔ ayieleɛ kyẽa ne.” (Yoh. 6:53, 54) Yesu asuafoɛ nemɔ pee wɔ berɛ a, bɔ ɔhane ne, wanyɛ bɛ fɛ. Yeti bɛhane kyɛ, “Ngyerɛkyerɛ he de, ɔyɛ se; nwa yee ɔkɔhora kotie ɔ?” (Yoh. 6:60)
<<<P>>>
2 Ɔwale sɔ ne, sɔ asuafoɛ nemɔ yakyili Yesu si lilɛ. Aso bɔ bɛ-yɛle ne, okyire kyɛ né belle Yesu nu gyidie kãa ala po anaa? Bɔ ɔte yee ole kyɛ, koraka sɔ dwirɛ ne baasi ne, Yesu yɛle anwa-nwadeɛ bie maa bɛnye gyele paa, na bɛhane po kyɛ: “Amba, ngɔmhyɛlɛniɛ bɔ ɔba wiase ne, ye ɛhe.” (Yoh. 6:14) Ɛhe di adanzeɛ paa kyɛ, ne benu biemɔ le Yesu nu gyidie. Nakoso, mmerɛ bɔ ɔhane edwirɛ bie bɔ bande yebo ne, banya yenu gyi-die kõ.
