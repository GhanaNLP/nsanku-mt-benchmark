<<<P>>>
BƆ ADESŨA NE KƆHÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Yekonwu bɔ yeti yeɛ sɛ yɛkɔ adesua anaa nhyia-mu a, odikyɛ yetie bɔ ɔkɔso ne yé, ɔne bɔ ɔkɔboka yɛ maa yɛkɔyɛ sɔ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Sɛ Ɛkɔ Adesua A, Nzuati Yeɛ Odikyɛ Etie Bɔ Ɔkɔso Ne Yé Ɔ?
<<<P>>>
“Kyɛbɔ ɛmɔtie edwirɛ ne, émɔdwene ye nwo yé.”—LUKA 8:18.
<<<P>>>
ƆHENE Dawide hane kyɛ, “AWURADE yeɛ ɔte me neafoɛ ɔ, yiti hwee ngohia me.” Ɛnnɛ, sɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ yeɛ Yehowa yɛ ma yɛ ɔ. Ni-kyeebiala bɔ yehia na wamaa yahora yasõ ye nahorɛ nu ne, ɔfa ma yɛ maa ɔboso.—Kenga Edwein 23:1, 2, 5.
<<<P>>>
2 Atee kõ bɔ Yehowa fa so yɛ sɔ paa yeɛ ole mmerɛ bɔ yahɔ ade-sua ne. Sɛ yɛwale asafo nanu ahyɛ a, nningyein bɔ yesua ne, ebiaa nu pee nala wɔ berɛ a yate dee. Nakoso sɛ ye sɔ po a, ɛhe-ne yeɛ ɔboka yɛ maa ‘yɛtira ndii wɔ nahorɛ’ nanu ɔ. (2 Pet. 1:12) Ye de yebisa kyɛ, sɛ yésua nikyeebie wɔ adesua bo, na enwu ye kyɛ sɔ dwirɛ ne de, ate dee a, aso ɛyɛ a ɔyɛ se ma wɔ kyɛ ɛkɔfa w’adwene kosie so anaa? Sɛ sɔ a, ye de adesua he kɔboka wɔ. Ye-konwu bɔ yeti yeɛ yenwo hia kyɛ yɛba adesua a, yetie bɔ ɔkɔso ne yé ɔ. Bɛbɔ bɛma tɔɔk ne koso, yɛkɔnea bɔ bɛyɛ a ɔkɔboka bɛbɔ bawa adesua ne maa bekɔfa b’adwene bekosie bɔ bɛkã naso ɔ. Yekosa koso yekonwu bɔ yɛyɛ a ɔkɔhora kɔboka yɛ maa yeko-tie bɔ ɔkɔso ne yé ɔ. Nakoso ɛmɔma yelimoa yɛnea ɛhe: Sɛ yɛkɔ adesua a, nwa deinn yeɛ okyirekyire yɛ ɔ?
<<<P>>>
KAE KYƐ YEHOWA YEƐ OKYIREKYIRE YƐ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
3 Yehowa nate ye dwirɛ Bible naso yeɛ odwudwo kyire yɛ ɔ. (2 Tim. 3:16, 17) Ne Yesu se ye sɔ. Yeti mmerɛ biala bɔ ne okyi-rekyire mmenia ne, ne ɔtwe adwene kɔ Tworɔnzɛm naso, na osa kyirekyire nu maa ɔkã b’ahone. Ɛhene maa bɛnwo dwirili bɛ, na bebisale bɛnwo kyɛ: “Ɔyɛle sɛɛ bɔ biaa he nyane nimdeɛ sɔa ɔ, wɔ aberɛ bɔ ossuane nwomaa biala ɔ?” (Yoh. 7:15) Bɛbɔ ne be-kuro kyɛ besua Nyameɛ Dwirɛ ne, sɛ ne benwu kyɛbɔ Yesu si kyi-rekyire tworɔnzɛm nanu ne a, ne bɛnye agye sɔaa. <<<P>>>
