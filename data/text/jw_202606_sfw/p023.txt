12 Sɛ ésua wɔ tɔɔk ne a, ɛyɛ a bobɔ so ma ofite wɔ noa mmerɛ pee, ɛhene kɔmaa sɛ ɛma tɔɔk ne a, ɔkɔyɛ kyɛbɔ ɛne bɛ bɔ ngɔmmɔ ne. Kyɛ ahaa ɛkɔhworɔ edwirɛ bɔ ɛpena kyɛ ɛkã ne kogua krataa so na akenga ahyire bɛ ne, bɔ mmɔden kyɛ ekofi w’ahone nu kodwudwo. Sɛ ɛyɛ ye sɔ a, ɔkɔmaa bɛkɔte bɔ ɛkã kyire bɛ nabo ndɛ, na bafa bayɛ adwuma koso. Nwomaa bɔ yeɛ ole Ma W’ani Nku Akenkan Ne Ɔkyerɛkyerɛ Ho ne kã kyɛ: “Dwudwo kyɛbɔ mmenia se wɔ kyɛ edwu-dwo nala, na ma ofi w’ahone nu. Sɛ ɛyɛ sɔ a, bɛbɔ betie wɔ ne konwu kyɛ wɔnye gye bɔ ɛkã nanwo, na ɛdwene bɛnwo.” Sɛ ɛkora fi w’aho-ne nu dwudwo a, ɔkɔmaa wɔ dwudwolɛ ne ngɔ-yɛ botoroo, na bɛbɔ betie wɔ ne, bɛnye kɔgye yenwo.

13 Ma aliemaa bie bɔ ɔse tɔɔk ma paa ne, ɔbo-ka wɔ. Sɛɛ yeɛ ɛkɔhora kɔyɛ ɛhe ɔ? Atee bɔ ɛpe-na kyɛ ɛfa so ma wɔ tɔɔk ne, ɔne edwirɛ biemɔ bɔ ahworɔ bɔ ɛpena kyɛ ɛfa wurawura nu ne, ma onwu ye. Afei koso sɛ ekuro a, ma wɔ tɔɔk ne kyire ye mmerɛ bɔ eguso esua ne. Sɛ ɔmaa enwu nningyein biemɔ bɔ odikyɛ ɛyɛ na wamaa wɔ tɔɔk ne ahɔ kama a, dé tonu na yɛ ye sɔ. (Any. 1:5) Yɛbɔ yekyirekyire wɔ adesua bo ne, yɛpena kyɛ yɛ ngyerɛkyerɛ kã mmenia ahone. Nakoso, bɛnwa bɔ ɔpɛ esaa ne, ɔnze kyɛ ye si ahyea ɔ. Yeti sɛ yɛmaa ebie boka yɛ a, ɔkɔmaa yɛkɔhora yekohyirehyire Nyameɛ Dwirɛ nanu yé, na bɛbɔ betie yɛ ne konya so mvasoɛ.*
