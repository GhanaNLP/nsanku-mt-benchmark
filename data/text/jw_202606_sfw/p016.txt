5 Sɛ ebie te kue soneafoɛ a, ɛyɛ a ne ɔlla ase kyɛ okonya mmerɛ kɔnea aliemaamɔ bɔ bɛwɔ ye kue nanu ne boso. Nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ mbanyi nemɔ pee wɔ berɛ a, sɛ kue bɔ bɛnea so ne la ahameɛ a, besa bɛyɛ adwuma biemɔ wɔ asafo nanu. Benu kɔ yeɛ ole Aliemaa Jörg bɔ ofi Germany ne. Ɔhane kyɛ: “Nyane a, ahaa ngɔɔnea ebiala bɔ ɔwɔ me kue nanu ne boso. Nakoso adwuma biemɔ koso wura mesa wɔ asafo nanu, yeti ɛyɛ a ne wayɛ se wama me kyɛ ɔngɔyɛ sɔ ɔ.” Abel koso te asafo panyi bɔ ɔwɔ Uganda, na wali afoɛ 70. Ɔhane kyɛ: “Mbanyi ne asomvoɛ bɔ bɛwɔ y’asafo nanu ne, baanzo. Yeti ɔmaa aliemaamɔ bɔ bɛwɔ yɛ kue nemɔ nu ne, bɛsone. Ɔnate ɛhene ati, ɔmaa ɔyɛ se ma me kyɛ mene ebiala bɔ ɔwɔ me kue nanu ne kɔnate wɔ asɛnga nu, na mahyɛ ye ngunaen ɔ.” Obed koso te asafo nu panyi bɔ ɔwɔ Suriname, na ɔhane nikyee foforɛ bɔ ɔmaa b’adwuma ne yɛ se ɔ. Ɔhane kyɛ: “Aliemaamɔ bɔ bɛwɔ me kue nanu ne, ɛyɛ a menye gye yenwo paa kyɛ ngɔboka bɛ maa bɛkɔhora bekohyirehyire yé wɔ asɛnga nu. Nakoso, dee bɔ ɛhora boka sona ne maa ɔyɛ sɔ ne, ne afɛ paa. Yesɔ nati ɔmaa mendaa menyɛ.” Ye de, sɛ ɛte kue soneafoɛ na ɛhemɔ, anaa nikyee foforɛ bie aayɛ ɔhaw ama wɔ a, nzu yeɛ ɔkɔboka wɔ ɔ?

6 Nikyee kɔ bɔ ɔboka paa yeɛ ole kyɛ bɛkɔkyekyɛ kue nemɔ anu maa ɔkɔyɛ ngokoro-ngokoro. Sɛ ɔba sɔ a, ɔngɔyɛ se kyɛ aliemaa bɔ ɔnea kue naso ne, okonya ebiala nwo alagye, na waboka ye maa ye gyidie ayɛ se ɔ. Ofikyɛ yɛkã ye, ɛhene yeɛ ole ye adwuma titire paa ɔ. Nakoso, sɛ mbanyi bɔ bɛwɔ asafo nanu ne, baanzõ koso ɛ? Ɔba sɔ a, ebiaa bɛkɔhora bɛkɔmaa asafo nu somvoɛ bie kɔnea kue naso kodwu kyɛ bekonya asafo nu panyi maa ɔkɔnea so ɔ. Ɛhene kɔboka

7 Sɛ asafo bɔ ɛwɔ nu ne, nu mbanyi nemɔ nzone na ɛte kue soneafoɛ a, bɔ mmɔden kyɛ sona bɔ baye ye kyɛ ɔboka wɔ maa ɛnea Nyameɛ mmoaen naso ne, ɛkɔtete ye maa ye koso okonwu adwuma ne nye so. (Kenga 1 Petro 5:2.) Yeti ɛyɛ a fa adwuma biemɔ wura yesa ma ɔyɛ boka wɔ. Ebie yeɛ ole kyɛ, ɔwɔ nu alaa a ma ɔnea asɛnga nhyiamu naso bie, na afei ɔkyekyɛ aliemaamɔ ma bɛhɔ asɛnga. Ɛyɛ a ɛmɔ yianu besusu bɛbɔ bɛwɔ kue nanu nanwo, na ɛmɔnea kyɛbɔ ɛmɔkɔboka bɛ maa bɛ ngyerɛkyerɛ kɔhɔ yé ɔ. Nakoso, ɛyɛ a ɛmɔfa mbaeɛ bɛhyɛ yebo. Sɛ ɛkwaanea bɛbɔ bɛwɔ kue nanu ne boso a, ɛyɛ a ɛne ye hɔ. Nakoso koraka ne, ɛne ye besusu bɔ ɛmɔ kɔhã nanwo. Sɛ ɛmɔyie a, ɛyɛ a kamvo ye, na ma onwu kyɛbɔ ɔkɔbɔ yenwo mmɔden ɛlɛho foforɛ ɔ. Sɛ ɛkora fa sɔ ndeteyeɛ hemɔ ma wɔ bokafoɛ ne a, ɔkɔmaa kyẽabie ye koso ɔkɔhora kɔɔyɛ kue soneafoɛ. (2 Tim. 2:2)
