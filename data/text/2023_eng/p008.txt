INTRODUCTION
<<<P>>>
1. The 2023 Citizen’s Budget is a summary of what Government achieved in 2022 and how much it intends to collect and utilise its revenues to implement policies and programmes. The theme of the 2023 budget is "Restoring and Sustaining Macroeconomic Stability and Resilience for Inclusive Growth & Value Addition”.
<<<P>>>
2. Over the years, every new budget invariably contains a host of new projects to be initiated at the expense of ongoing projects. This does not only put pressure on our finances but has also historically resulted in payment of arrears which raise the overall cost of projects as the deferred payments attract interest cost.
<<<P>>>
3. In 2023, Government will implement new policies and programmes, but will also encourage the culture of completing all existing projects.
