Expenditure Measures
<<<P>>>
19. In addition to the revenue measures, Government proposes the underlisted key expenditure measures to support the fiscal consolidation process.
<<<P>>>
• Double the LEAP payment per beneficiary household from the current Ghs45 per month to GHs90 and increase number of beneficiary households from the current 344,185 households. These measures will increase the current budget on LEAP from GHs197.5 million in 2022 to GHs395 million;
<<<P>>>
• Increase the budget on school feeding caterer payment by additional GHs138 million,
<<<P>>>
• Continue with 30% cut in the salaries of the President, Vice President, Ministers, Deputy Ministers, MMDCEs, and political office holders including those in State-Owned Enterprises;
<<<P>>>
• Reduce the threshold on earmarked funds from the current 25 percent of Tax Revenue to 17.5 percent of Tax Revenues;
<<<P>>>
• Migrate all earmarked funds onto the GIFMIS platforms and ensure they use the GIFMIS platform to process all their revenue and expenditures transactions to be completed by end of 2023.
<<<P>>>
• Place a cap on salary adjustment of SOEs to be lower than negotiated base pay increase on Single Spine Salary Structure for each year;
