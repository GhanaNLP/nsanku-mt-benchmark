<<<P>>>
Revenue Measures
<<<P>>>
18. To support the fiscal consolidation process, Government will present revenue measures with an estimated total yield of 1.35 percent of GDP to Parliament for consideration and approval. These among others are:
<<<P>>>
• Revision to the taxation of cigarettes and tobacco products to align with ECOWAS protocols, increase the excise rate for spirits above that of beers.
<<<P>>>
• Increase the Value Added Tax (VAT) rate by two and a half percentage points from 12.5 percent to 15 percent and review the VAT threshold as well as undertake major reforms with respect to VAT exemptions.
<<<P>>>
• Convert the National Fiscal Stabilisation Levy (NFSL) into a Growth and Sustainability Levy (GSL) to cover all entities.
<<<P>>>
• Reduce the Electronic Transfer Levy headline rate from 1.5 percent to one percent of the transaction value alongside the removal of the daily threshold.
<<<P>>>
• Other proposed measures include
<<<P>>>
o Sale of 5G Electromagnetic Spectrum;
<<<P>>>
o Enhance Rent Tax Compliance;
<<<P>>>
o Pursue Additional Oil Entitlement (AEO) in relation to the Jubilee Field; and
<<<P>>>
o Revised Income Tax to exclude unrealised exchange losses from deductions and ensure that realised exchange losses on capital assets are capitalised.
