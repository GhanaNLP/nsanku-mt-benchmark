National Afforestation Programme (NAP): It is an ecological restoration of degraded forests and to develop the forest resources with peoples’ participation, with focus on improvement in livelihoods of the forest-fringe communities, especially the poor.
<<<P>>>
National Disaster Management Organisation (NADMO): It is the government agency that is responsible for the management of disasters as well as other emergencies in Ghana
<<<P>>>
Non-Oil Real GDP: Is the total output in the economy that excludes the activities in the oil sector, which takes into account the level of inflation
<<<P>>>
Non-Oil Real GDP Growth: It is an indicator that measures the real annual economic growth of all sectors except oil.
<<<P>>>
Non Tax Revenue: Government revenue not generated from taxes, examples are fees for granting permit or licenses, user fees and other charges.
<<<P>>>
Outstanding Obligations: The outstanding obligations comprise MDA commitments with the Ministry of Finance as well as outstanding 2016 payments to Statutory Funds.
<<<P>>>
Outturn: It is the result of a process or sequence of events.
<<<P>>>
Primary balance : This is the difference between Government revenue and its non interest expenditure (excluding debt Service Payment). This can be measured as a percentage of the Gross Domestic Product (GDP).
<<<P>>>
Primary surplus (deficit): It refers to the component of the fiscal surplus (deficit) that is comprised of current government spending less current income from taxes, and excludes interest paid on government debt.
