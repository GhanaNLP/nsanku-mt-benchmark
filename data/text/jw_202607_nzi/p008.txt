<<<P>>>
Ayezaya Buluku Ne Bahola Akyekye Ɛ Rɛle
<<<P>>>
“Edwɛkɛ mɔɔ Gyihova ɛha la ɛne: . . . ’Kɛ mɔɔ mame kyekye ɔ ra nrenyia arɛle la, zɔhane ala a mebahɔ zo meakyekye bɛ rɛle a.’ ”—AYE. 66:12, 13.
<<<P>>>
PƐ KAKULA bie mɔɔ ɔlɛdi alɔbɔlɛ na ɔlɛsu la anwo nvoninli nea. Bie a bɛlɛtu bɛahɔ ɛleka fofolɛ ɔti ɔnrɛnyia ɔ gɔnwo mɔ mɔɔ ɔ nee bɛ wɔ awuke dɛba ne anu la ɔ nee bɛ ɛnrɛli nwɔhoa bieko. Anzɛɛ bie a nvonleɛ bie mɔɔ ɔyɛle la ɛmaa ɔ rɛle ɛbɔ. Saa ye mame mɔɔ lɛ ɛlɔlɛ la nwu ye a, duzu a ɔbayɛ a? Ɔbakyikyi ɔ ra ne anye anwo, yeagogo ye na yeamaa yeanwu kɛ ɔkulo ye edwɛkɛ.
<<<P>>>
2 Wɔ Baebolo ne anu, Gyihova fa ɔ nwo toto mame mɔɔ lɛ ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ kyekye ɔ ra arɛle la anwo. Ɔhanle kɛ: “Kɛ mɔɔ mame kyekye ɔ ra nrenyia arɛle la, zɔhane ala a mebahɔ zo meakyekye bɛ rɛle a.” (Aye. 66:13) Ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ Gyihova lɛ maa yɛ la le kpole tɛla awovolɛ biala ɛdeɛ ne. (Aye. 49:15) Saa yɛlɛdi alɔbɔlɛ anzɛɛ yɛ sa nu to a, Gyihova dua ye Edwɛkɛ, Baebolo ne azo fa arɛlekyekyelɛ nee anwosesebɛmaanlɛ biala mɔɔ yɛhyia nwo la maa yɛ.—Wlo. 15:4.
<<<P>>>
3 Gyihova anloa anu edwɛkɛ mɔɔ kyekye yɛ rɛle la ɛyi Ayezaya buluku ne anu. (Aye. 40:1) Yɛ Ze ne mɔɔ lɛ ɛlɔlɛ la maanle Ayezaya hɛlɛle ye buluku ne vale kyekyele ye menli ne mɔɔ ɛnee bɛde Dwuda mɔɔ bɛlɛyia ngyegyelɛ la arɛle. Kɛmɔ yɛdayɛ noko yɛde “mekɛ mɔɔ anu yɛ se la” anu la ati, yɛhyia arɛlekyekyelɛ. (2 Tim. 3:1) Ɔti maa yɛnlea Ayezaya buluku ne anu ngyehyɛnu bie mɔ mɔɔ baboa yɛ wɔ (1) mekɛ mɔɔ yɛ tɛnlabelɛ bahakyi arɛlevilɛ nu, (2) mekɛ mɔɔ yɛ adwenle ɛlɛbua yɛ fɔle dɔɔnwo yɛɛ (3) mekɛ mɔɔ yɛ kulovolɛ bie mɔɔ ɛwu la ɛlɛmaa yɛali nyane la.
