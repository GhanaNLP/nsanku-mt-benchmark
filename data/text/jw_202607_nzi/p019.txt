nvɔne, ɔnye nyeyenu yɛɛ ɔ nye wɔ bɛdabɛ mɔɔ awie mɔ ɛmbu bɛ bie bɔbɔ la anwo. (Luku 1:46-55) Kɛ mɔɔ Mɛle hilele kɛzi Nyamenle boale ye la anwo anyezɔlɛ la, yɛdayɛ noko yɛ nye sɔ moalɛ mɔɔ Gyihova fa maa yɛ wɔ “mekɛ kpalɛ nu la.” (Hib. 4:16) Saa ɛ sa ka moalɛ zɛhae bie a, biza ɛ nwo kɛ: ‘Duzu a ɛhye maa menwu ye wɔ Gyihova anwo a? Gyihova subane ne mɔ boni a edwɛkɛ ɛhye mɔɔ ezi me ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nu la maa menwu ye a?’

YƐNRƐGYAKYI KƐ YƐBAZUKOA GYIHOVA ANWO DEBIE ƐLƐ
<<<P>>>
17 Wɔmɔ dwenle Gyihova anwo ninyɛne dɔɔnwo mɔɔ yɛbazukoa wɔ ewiade fofolɛ ne anu la anwo. Yɛ sa baha nwomamobɔleɛ fofolɛ mɔɔ ɔbamaa yɛanwu mɔɔ Gyihova kpondɛ kɛ yɛyɛ la. (Yek. 20:12) Yɛbanwu ninyɛne dɔɔnwo mɔɔ yebɔ la kpalɛ ɔti yɛbahola yɛazukoa ɔ nwo ninyɛne dɔɔnwo. (Aye. 11:6-9) Eza ninyɛne fofolɛ mɔɔ ɔba-
<<<P>>>
17-18. Duzu ati a yɛkola yɛka kɛ yɛnrɛgyakyi kɛ yɛbazukoa Gyihova anwo debie ɛlɛ a?
<<<P>>>
sisi, mɔɔ bie a le yɛ alɔvolɛ mɔɔ bɛbadwazo bɛ nee munli mɔɔ yɛbali ye ngyikyi ngyikyi la bamaa yɛanwu yɛ anwuma Selɛ ne kpalɛ.
<<<P>>>
18 Kɛmɔ Gyihova ɛdɛnla aze dahuu yɛɛ “ye ndelebɛbo ɛnlɛ ye ɛweɛne” la ati, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛbɔ mɔdenle dahuu kɛ yɛbanwu ye kpalɛ. (Edw. 90:2; 147:5) Baebolo ne ka kɛ yɛnrɛnwu “gyima mɔɔ nɔhalɛ Nyamenle ne ɛyɛ ɔvi mɔlebɛbo kɔdwu awieleɛ la ɛlɛ.” (Nol. 3:11) Ɔti ɔnva nwo mekɛ tendenle mɔɔ yɛva yɛdɛnla nɔhalɛ ne anu la, bɛmaa yɛva nwolɛ adenle biala mɔɔ yɛbanyia la yɛzukoa yɛ Ze ne mɔɔ lɛ ɛlɔlɛ la anwo ninyɛne dɔɔnwo.

NVONINLI NE ANWO NGILENU Ɔ Nyunlu: Yibɛdemɛlɛke lale akɛnrasesebɛ ali liele Gyɛlɛmaya mɔɔ bɛvuandinle ye bɛdole bula bie anu la. Mukelɛ 18: Adiema nrenyia bie mɔɔ adwenleadwenle ɛdɔ ɔ nwo la ɛlɛdwenledwenle kɛzi Gyihova boale ye maanle ɔyɛle ye gyimalilɛ ne wɔ dapɛne avinli debiezukoalɛ bo la anwo.
