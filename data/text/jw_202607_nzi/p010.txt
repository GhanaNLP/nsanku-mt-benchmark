6 Joseph mɔɔ yɛhanle ɔ nwo edwɛkɛ la
hanle kɛzi Gyihova boale ɔ nee ɔ ye wɔ
mekɛ mɔɔ bɛvile Bɛtɛle la anwo edwɛkɛ.
Ɔhanle kɛ: “Mɔɔ yɛhɔle la, yɛnwunle kɛzi
Gyihova luale yɛ mediema ne mɔ anwo zo
boale yɛ la. Ɔvale debie biala mɔɔ yɛhyia
nwo la ɔmaanle yɛ yɛɛ ɔkyekyele yɛ rɛle wɔ
mekɛ kpalɛ nu, na ɔlɛkɔ zo yeaboa yɛ.” Saa
wɔ tɛnlabelɛ kakyi arɛlevilɛ nu a, ɛdawɔ
noko ɛbahola wɔanyia anwodozo kɛ Gyi-
hova bamaa ɛ nwo ayɛ se na yeava adehile-
lɛ mɔɔ ɛhyia nwo la yeamaa wɔ ɔnva nwo
kɛzi tɛnlabelɛ ne anu yɛ se la. (Aye. 54:
10; 58:11) Joseph doale zolɛ kɛ: “Yɛ edwɛ-
ne gyɛne ne ko anu edwɛkɛ ne ka ye kɛ,
‘Meze kɛ mebayia ngyegyelɛ dɔɔnwo, mɔɔ
bɛ nee me lua la zonle kpalɛ, ɔti menrɛzu-
lo mɔɔ bazi la.’* Nɔhalɛ nu, tɛ mekɛ ne
amuala a ninyɛne bahɔ ye kɛ mɔɔ yɛkpondɛ
la a, noko yɛze ye wienyi kɛ Gyihova baboa
yɛ.”

9 Maa yɛnlea mɔɔ dole Belemgbunli De-
vidi la. Ɔzɛkyele agyalɛ yɛɛ ɔhunle sonla
edeɛ, noko Gyihova vale hyɛle ye na yemɔ
anzi ɔhanle kɛ Devidi le awie mɔɔ di nɔha-
lɛ. (2 Sa. 11:3, 4, 14, 15; 12:13; 1 Arl. 9:4, 5)
Saa Gyihova nea Devidi a, ye nɔhalɛlilɛ ne
a ɔnwu ye a na tɛ nvonleɛ mɔɔ ɔyɛle la
ɔ. Zɔhane ala a saa yɛdayɛ noko yɛnu yɛ
nwo a, Gyihova fi ɛlɔlɛ nu fa yɛ ɛtane kyɛ
yɛ na ɔboa yɛ ɔmaa yɛkɔ zo yɛsonle ye mɔɔ
yɛ adwenle ɛmbua yɛ fɔlɛ a. Saa yɛ adwenle
kɔ zo bua yɛ fɔlɛ a, Ayezaya buluku ne anu
edwɛkɛ boni bieko a bahola aboa yɛ a?
