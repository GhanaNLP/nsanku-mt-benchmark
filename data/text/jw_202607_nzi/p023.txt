11 Saa yɛ sukoavo ne anye die nvasoɛ mɔɔ ɔlɛnyia wɔ ye Baebolo ɛzukoalɛ ne azo la anwo a, ɔmaa yɛdayɛ noko yɛ nye die. Noko bie a yɛ sukoavo ne bayɛ yɛ mo wɔ ninyɛne mɔɔ ɔlɛsukoa la anwo na bɔbɔ yeaye yɛ ayɛlɛ wɔ kɛzi yɛkilehile ye la anwo. Nvonleɛ ɛnle nganvolɛ zɛhae anwo, noko ɔwɔ kɛ yɛboa ye yɛmaa ɔnwu kɛ ahenle mɔɔ ɔlɛkilehile ye la a le Gyihova, yɛ Kilehilevolɛ Kpole Kpalɛ ne. (Aye. 30:20, 21) Ɔti ɔdaye a ɔfɛta ayɛlɛyelɛ nee nganvolɛ ne a.

BOA YE MAA ƆVA MƆƆ YEZUKOA LA ƆLI GYIMA
<<<P>>>
12 Saa yɛ sukoavo ne banyia nɔhalɛ ndelebɛbo zo nvasoɛ kpalɛ a, kyesɛ ɔfa mɔɔ yezukoa la ɔdi gyima. Ɔti kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na yɛaboa ye yɛamaa yeava mɔɔ yezukoa la yeali gyima ɛ? Mɔɔ limoa, saa ɔyɛ se kɛ ɔbade kɛlɛtokɛ bie mɔɔ yegenga wɔ Baebolo ne anu la abo a, boa ye maa ɔhakye mɔɔ ɔze wɔ Gyihova anwo la. Kɛ neazo la, saa ɔlɛkenga Gyihova ndɛnebualɛ bie mɔ anwo edwɛkɛ a, bie a ɔnrɛde deɛmɔti Gyihova hunle menli bie mɔ noko ɔliele bie mɔ ngoane la abo. Kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na wɔaboa wɔ sukoavo ne wɔamaa yeanyia edwɛkɛ zɛhae mɔ anwo adwenle kpalɛ ɛ?
<<<P>>>
13 Ɛbahola wɔamaa wɔ sukoavo ne anwosesebɛ kɛ ɔdwenledwenle ninyɛne mɔɔ yezukoa ye wɔ Gyihova anwo la anwo. Kɛ neazo la, ɔbahola yeabiza ɔ nwo kɛ, Kɛzi Gyihova bu ngoane ɛ? (2 Pita 3:9) Ɔnva nwo kɛ yɛle ɛtanevolɛma la, debie mɔɔ sonle bolɛ la boni a Gyihova vale bɔle afɔle amaa yealie yɛ a? (Ɛfɛ. 2:4, 5) Asoo Gyihova twe menli mɔɔ ɔnle kɛ ɔtwe bɛ nzo la anzo ɔ? Anzɛɛ ɔmaa menli mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ ɔtwe bɛ nzo la fa bɛ nwo di ɔ? (Ɛzɛ. 34:6, 7) Ɔdwu mekɛ ne bie a Baebolo ne ɛnga edwɛkɛ bie mɔɔ zile la anwo edwɛkɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ boa yɛ ɔmaa yɛte deɛmɔti Gyihova zile kpɔkɛ bie la abo. Noko
