Gebɛyɔnoma ne bɛlɛbɛlale Dwɔhyua maanle ɔ nee bɛ yɛle anzodwolɛ ngyekyeleɛ
(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 4)
<<<P>>>
nle kɛ saa bɛ nee bɛ ko a bɛnrɛhola bɛ. Bɛnwunle kɛ Gyihova ɛlɛko amaa ye menli ne yɛɛ ɛnee bɛze kɛ yebɔ ɛwɔkɛ kɛ ɔbavoa Keenama yeavi azɛlɛ ne azo. (Ɛzɛ. 34:11; Dwɔh. 9:24) Ɔti mɔɔ Yizilayɛma lile Gyɛleko nee Eyae anwo zo konim wiele la, Gebɛyɔnoma zoanle menli* bɛmaanle bɛhɔhanle bɛhilele Dwɔhyua wɔ Gelegale kɛ, bɛsɛlɛ ye ɔmmaku bɛ.
<<<P>>>
⁴ Kenga Dwɔhyua 9:8-13. Gebɛyɔnoma ne yɛle bɛ nwo kɛ bɛvi moamoa. Bɛhanle kɛ bɛze kɛ Gyihova a maanle Yizilayɛma lile Yigyibiti Belemgbunli Saehɔn nee Amɔle Belemgbunli Ɔɔgo anwo zo konim a. Noko bɛnwunle nrɛlebɛ, bɛanga mɔɔ zile Gyɛleko nee Eyae la anwo edwɛkɛ ɔluakɛ ɔzile la ɛtɛkyɛle ɔti ɔnle edwɛkɛ mɔɔ bɛbahola bɛade ye wɔ ɛleka “moamoa” ne mɔɔ bɛlɛka la. Yizilayɛ mgbanyima ne mɔ liele bɛ lile na bɛ nee bɛ yɛle anzodwolɛ ngyekyeleɛ mɔɔ bɛambiza Gyihova nwolɛ edwɛkɛ a. (Dwɔh. 9:14, 15) Yeangyɛ Yizilayɛma nwunle kɛ Gebɛyɔnoma ne ɛbɛlɛbɛla bɛ, noko bɛlile ngyekyeleɛ mɔɔ bɛ nee bɛ yɛle la azo ɔluakɛ ɛnee bɛva ‘Gyihova duma bɛha ndane bɛhile bɛ.’ (Dwɔh. 9:16-19) Bɛmaanle bɛ gyima kɛ ‘bɛzoa ɛyɛne nee nzule bɛmaa menli ne nee Gyihova afɔlemokyea ne.’ Ɛhye le gyima mɔɔ ngɛkɛlɛ a yɛ a na bɛbayɛ ye wɔ bɛ ngoane mekɛ kɔsɔɔti anu.—Dwɔh. 9:27.

* Ɔbayɛ kɛ Havaema azuamgbole gyɛne nsa azo amra bie mɔ boka menli mɔɔ Gebɛyɔnoma ne zoanle bɛ la anwo. Azuamgbole ɛhye mɔ a le Kɛfaela, Biyɛlɔto yɛɛ Keleate-gyialem.—Dwɔh. 9:17.
