DEBIE MƆƆ ƐBAHOLA WƆAZUKOA LA
<<<P>>>
Siezie Mualɛ Mɔɔ Maa Anwosesebɛ La
<<<P>>>
Saa yɛkɔ debiezukoalɛ a, yɛnyia nwolɛ adenle kɛ yɛbava yɛ mualɛ yɛamaa yɛ mediema anwosesebɛ. (Hib. 10:24, 25) Ɔti kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na yɛahola yɛaziezie mualɛ mɔɔ amgba ɔbamaa awie mɔ anwosesebɛ la ɛ?
<<<P>>>
• Yɛ nwolɛ asɔne. Sɛlɛ Gyihova kɛ ɔboa wɔ ɔmaa ɛmaa mualɛ mɔɔ bawula ye anyunlunyia na yeamaa awie mɔ anwosesebɛ la.—Edw. 35:18.
<<<P>>>
• Mmakenga wɔ mualɛ ne ɛfi buluku ne anu ala. Saa ɛfa ɛdawɔ mumua ne wɔ edwɛkɛ ɛka wɔ diedi ne anwo edwɛkɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɛlɛmaa mualɛ la a, ɔbaboa menli mɔɔ bɛlɛtie wɔ la.
<<<P>>>
• Mmamaa mualɛ ndendenle. Mualɛ nzinrenzinra mɔɔ nuhua la ɛkɛ la a ta maa anwosesebɛ kpalɛ a, yɛɛ ɔmaa menli dɔɔnwo noko nyia nwolɛ adenle maa mualɛ.—Mrɛ. 10:19.
<<<P>>>
• Fa adwenle kpalɛ maa mualɛ. Mmafa wɔ mualɛ ne ɛtu asafo ne anzɛɛ adiema bie folɛ yɛɛ mmafa ɛtwi asafo ne anzɛɛ adiema bie anyunlu.
<<<P>>>
Saa ɛmaa ɛ sa zo na bɛanvɛlɛ wɔ bɔbɔ a, ɛbahola wɔamaa awie mɔ anwosesebɛ. Wɔ adenle boni azo? Bɛlɛmaa mualɛ a anrɛɛ tie bɛ kpalɛ na bɛkpɔne a wɔahanvo bɛ wɔ nwolɛ.—Mrɛ. 10:21.
<<<P>>>
Bɛndɔne buluku ɛhye. Ɔboka ewiade amuala Baebolo ngilehilelɛ gyima mɔɔ bɛfa ndoboa mɔɔ vi ahonle nu a bɛdi nwolɛ gyima la anwo. Saa ɛkulo kɛ ɛye ndoboa a, yɛsɛlɛ wɔ kɔ donate.jw.org.
