Nyamenle anwo la. Ɔti mendɔle King James Version
Baebolo dɛba ko wɔ Budama ɛvoyialilɛ bie abo ɔnva
nwo kɛ ɔyɛ se kɛ ɛbanyia Baebolo wɔ ɛleka zɛhae mɔ
wɔadɔ la!
<<<P>>>
Mɔɔ menlile ɛvolɛ 17 la, adiema bie mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ
ye Wilson Bashou nee me dendɛle wɔ awɔdenle ne
azo wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ me nee me twea ne ɛlɛkɔ akpɔsa la.
Ɔbizale me kɛ, “Ɛze kenle bie nwo ɛwɔkɛ mɔɔ Nyame-
nle ɛbɔ la ɔ?” Ɛhye anzi, yɛbɔle Baebolo ne ɛzukoalɛ
bo. Wilson gengale Yekile 17:1 na ɔbizale me kɛ, “Duzu
a ‘nzule dɔɔnwo ne’ mɔɔ tuutuunli ne de zo la kile a?”
Menganle kɛ menze. Akee ɔgengale ngyɛnu 15 ne
mɔɔ ɔkile nu kɛ nzule dɔɔnwo ne gyi ɛkɛ maa menli la
ɔhilele me. Me nye liele kɛ menwunle kɛ Gyihova Ala-
sevolɛ maa Baebolo ne mumua ne kilehile edwɛkɛ
mɔɔ wɔ nu la anu la. Mɔɔ yɛbɔle adawu yɛwiele la, Wi-
lson vale The Truth That Leads to Eternal Life buluku
ne ko maanle me. Zɔhane buluku ne hakyile me ɛbɛ-
labɔlɛ.
<<<P>>>
Kenle bulu anzi, mengɔle me debiezukoalɛ mɔɔ li-
moa la wɔ Belemgbunlililɛ Asalo. Me rɛle ɛnrɛvi kɛzi
mediema ne mɔ liele me kɛnlɛma la ɛlɛ. Ɔvi zɔhane
mekɛ ne, metɛtole debiezukoalɛ ɛhɔlɛ nu kenle ko
bɔbɔ. Ɛhye anzi, Wilson bɔle ɔ bo kɛ ɔ nee me su-
koa Baebolo ne. Me nye die Baebolo ne ɛzukoalɛ nwo
kpalɛ ɔti ɛnee ɔdwu mekɛ ne bie a yefa dɔnehwele
mɔtwɛ yɛsukoa debie. Me awovolɛ anye anlie ɛhye
anwo, ɔti bɛdwazole betiale me kpole kpalɛ. Noko
mekɛ mɔɔ melɛsukoa ninyɛne dɔɔnwo la, ɛnee melɛ-
nyia anwodozo kpole kɛ menwu nɔhalɛ ne. Bɛzɔnenle
me wɔ 1983.

MENGYAKYILE YUNIVƐSITI ƐHƆLƐ NA
MENYƐLE ADEKPAKYELƐNLI
<<<P>>>
Mekɛ mɔɔ bɛzɔnenle me la, ɛnee mekɔ yunivɛsiti
bie mɔɔ ɛlie duma kpalɛ la na ɛnee melɛsukoa debie
mɔɔ bamaa meayɛ dɔketa la. Me awovolɛ vale ni-
nyɛne dɔɔnwo bɔle afɔle amaa meahola meahɔ suku-
lu ɛhye. Anrɛɛ mebahola meadoa me yunivɛsiti ɛhɔlɛ
ne azo, noko ɛnee mekulo kɛ meyɛ biala mɔɔ meba-
hola la memaa Gyihova na mefa me mekɛ kɔsɔɔti me-
sonle ye.
<<<P>>>
Me papa bɔle me kɔkɔ kɛ, “Saa ɛgyakyi yunivɛsiti
ne ɛhɔlɛ a, mebadu wɔ meavi sua nu ɛke!” Ɛhye maa-
nle menlile nyane na me adwenle zɛkyele bɔkɔɔ. Me-
kulo me awovolɛ edwɛkɛ na mengulo kɛ memaa
bɛdi nyane. Ɔti ɛnee mengɔ sukulu ɛdeɛ, noko ɛnee
meta mekɔ sukulu ɛkɛ ne nɔsolɛ nu mekɔyɛ asɔne
kɛ, “Gyihova mesɛlɛ wɔ boa me wɔ me kpɔkezilɛ
ɛhye anu.” Mɔɔ akee menzile kpɔkɛ kɛ megyakyi yuni-
vɛsiti ne ɛhɔlɛ la, me papa voanle me vile sua nu ɛkɛ.
Menvɛlele Wilson na menganle mɔɔ ɛzi la mengilele
ye. Ɔhanle kɛ ɔ nee me badɛnla. Mensesale me ni-
nyɛne ne amuala menguale baage ko anu na menvo-
le kale mengɔle ye sua nu ɛkɛ. Mekakye kɛ ɛkɛ ne ala
akee me ahonle dɔle me azule nu kpalɛ. Menwunle kɛ
mezi kpɔkɛ kpalɛ.

mekɛ kɔsɔɔti meazonle Gyihova a. Membɔle adekpa-
kyelɛ gyima ne abo wɔ 1984.
