Kilehile Bɛ Maa Bɛgyinla Kpundii
<<<P>>>
BODANE
Kɛzi ɛbaboa wɔ Baebolo sukoavoma wɔamaa bɛanwu Gyihova, ye subane ne mɔ yɛɛ ɛbɛla mɔɔ sɔ ɔ nye nee mɔɔ ɔnzɔ ɔ nye la.
<<<P>>>
“Ɛhye a le dahuu ngoane a, kɛ bɛkɛnwu wɔ, nɔhalɛ Nyamenle kokye ne.”—DWƆN 17:3.
<<<P>>>
SAA wɔ Baebolo sukoavo bie bɔ ɔ bo kɛ ɔba debiezukoalɛ, ɔmaa mualɛ, ɔka ye diedi ne anwo edwɛkɛ yeahile awie mɔ na nzinlii ɔsi kpɔkɛ kɛ ɔyila ɔ nwo zo yeamaa Gyihova Nyamenle a, kɛzi ɛte nganeɛ ɛ? Ɛ nye die, ɔnle zɔ ɔ? Nɔhalɛ nu, Gyihova a ɔfɛta kɛ yɛfa anyuhɔlɛ mɔɔ yɛ Baebolo sukoavo ne banyia wɔ sunsum nu la anwo nganvolɛ yɛmaa ye a. Ɔti saa yɛnwu kɛzi ɔlɛboa awie bie yeamaa yeanyi sunsum nu la, ɔmaa yɛye ye ayɛlɛ.—1 Kɔl. 3:5-9; 3 Dwɔn 3, 4.
<<<P>>>
2 Nyamenle kulo kɛ “ɔdie menli ngakyile kɔsɔɔti na bɛnyia nɔhalɛ ne anwo nɔhalɛ ndelebɛbo.” (Kenga 1 Timote 2:3, 4) Gyihova kulo kɛ menli nwu ye na bɛte ye bodane ne mɔ abo kpalɛ. (Gyɛ. 9:24) Ɛhye kile kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛnyia nɔhalɛ ndelebɛbo. Abɔlɔba bie hanle kɛ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ bɛhile ɔ bo “nɔhalɛ ndelebɛbo” la ɛngile kɛ awie te edwɛkɛ bie anwo bie ala, emomu ɔtie yeade ɔ bo na yeamaa yeahakyi ye ɛbɛlabɔlɛ. Saa Baebolo sukoavo bie banwu Gyihova a, ndelebɛbo zɔhane ka kɛzi ɔbɔ ye ɛbɛla la. Yɛɛ ɔmaa ɔnyia anyelazo kɛ ɔbanyia “dahuu ngoane.”—Dwɔn 17:3.
<<<P>>>
3 Gyihova ɛmaa yɛnyia nwolɛ adenle kɛ yɛbaboa awie mɔ yɛamaa bɛanwu ye. Ɔti wɔ edwɛkɛ ɛhye anu, yɛbazuzu kpuya ɛhye mɔ anwo: (1) Duzu ati a ɔhyia kɛ Baebolo sukoavoma nwu Gyihova kpalɛ a? (2) Kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na yeahilehile bɛ yeamaa bɛanwu Gyihova subane ne mɔ ɛ? (3) Duzu a yɛbayɛ yɛaboa bɛ yeamaa bɛava mɔɔ bɛzukoa wɔ Gyihova anwo la bɛali gyima wɔ bɛ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nu a?
<<<P>>>
1. Saa ɛnwu kɛ wɔ Baebolo sukoavo bie ɛlɛnyia anyuhɔlɛ a, kɛzi ɔka wɔ ɛ?
<<<P>>>
2. Duzu a Gyihova kulo kɛ Baebolo sukoavoma yɛ a? (1 Timote 2:3, 4)
<<<P>>>
3. Duzu a yɛbazuzu nwo wɔ edwɛkɛ ɛhye anu a?
