SAA TƐNLABELƐ NE KAKYI ARƐLEVILE NU A
<<<P>>>
4 Yɛ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ bahola ahakyi arɛlevilɛ nu. (Nol. 9:11; 1 Kɔl. 7:31) Ewule bie bahola aha yɛ, yɛ nwo bahola ahyele yɛ wɔ ezukoa afoa nu, basabasayɛlɛ bahola azi yɛ maanle ne anu anzɛɛ gyimalilɛ bie mɔɔ yɛyɛ ye wɔ Gyihova ɛzonlenlɛ nu la bahola ahakyi arɛlevilɛ nu. Saa ɔba ye zɛhae a, ɔbahola yeamaa adwenleadwenle adɔ yɛ nwo anzɛɛ yɛ rɛle abɔ. Ɔti saa yɛbiza yɛ nwo kɛ: ‘Duzu a menyɛ a?’ a yɛtɛbɔle munzule. Adiema bie mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Joseph la yɛle gyima wɔ Bɛtɛle kɛyɛ ɛvolɛ 17. Mɔɔ bɛhanle kɛ ɔhɔyɛ adekpakyelɛnli la, ɔhanle kɛ: “Ɛnee mennwu mɔɔ meyɛ a, ɔluakɛ medɛnla Bɛtɛle mehyɛ. Ɛnee mennwu kɛzi mebɔ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ ne abo bɔbɔ a.” Saa yɛ tɛnlabelɛ ne kakyi arɛlevilɛ nu a, moalɛ boni a Gyihova dua Ayezaya buluku ne azo fa maa yɛ a?

4. Saa yɛ tɛnlabelɛ kakyi arɛlevilɛ nu a, kɛzi ɔbahola yeaha yɛ ɛ?
<<<P>>>
5 Kenga Ayezaya 42:16. Gyihova ɛbɔ ɛwɔkɛ kɛ ɔbali menli mɔɔ sɛkɛlɛneɛ adenle zo bɛle anyenzinliravolɛma la anyunlu yeamaa bɛalua adenle mɔɔ bɛtɛfale zo ɛlɛ la anu na yeamaa awozinli ahakyi wienyi. Bie a edwɛkɛ “anyenzinliravolɛma,” “adenle mɔɔ bɛtɛfale zo ɛlɛ,” “awozinli” yɛɛ “adenle mɔɔ zolɛ le ahuhunduu” la kile adwenle mɔɔ yɛbanyia ye wɔ yɛ tɛnlabelɛ ne mɔ anwo wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛbahakyi arɛlevilɛ nu la. Wɔannea a ɔbayɛ yɛ kɛ yɛlua ɛhoayelɛ kpole bie anu na yɛnnwu adenle ne la. Noko Gyihova ɛbɔ ɛwɔkɛ kɛ ɔnva nwo tɛnlabelɛ biala mɔɔ yɛbahɔ nu la, ɔbava adehilelɛ mɔɔ yɛhyia nwo la yeamaa yɛ yɛɛ ɔnrɛkpo yɛ ɛlɛ. Ɔbazɔ yɛ sa na yeava yɛ yealua awozinli ne anu, ɔbamaa wienyi ada yɛ adenle ne anu na yeamaa adenle mɔɔ zolɛ le ahuhunduu la ayɛ sɛsɛ. Kɛzi ɔbayɛ ye ɛ? Ɔbalua Baebolo ne nee
