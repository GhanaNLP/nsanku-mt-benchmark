<<<P>>>
Me nee Michiko Oda nee ɔ hu
<<<P>>>
anu na meakpɔne. Enee mevɛ, me nwo ɛyɛ me koo-nwu yɛɛ enee ɔzɔho kɛ ezule ne noko kpondɛ adɔ, ɔti ɛnee mengola mennwu ye kɛ saa memkpɔne mengɔ sua nu a. Mɔɔ menvole ɛbolɔnsam ne azo la, menwu-nle nrenyia bie mɔɔ vi Philippines la wɔ ɛkɛ. Menganle ye wɔ me ti anu kɛ, ‘Medie medi kɛ ɔ nye ɛnlie edwɛ-kɛ ne anwo.’ Noko amuanrɛɛ melɛbɛlabɛla me nwo! Me nee ye bɔle Baebolo ne ɛzukoalɛ bo na ɔrayɛle adiema.

MENZUKOALE NINYƐNE DƆƆNWO WƆ BƐTƐLE
<<<P>>>
Mɔɔ me nee Wilson dɛnlanle ɛvolɛ nwiɔ la, bɛvɛlɛle me kɛ mengɔnyɛ gyima wɔ Brooklyn Bɛtɛle wɔ 1985. Kɛ mɔɔ menlimoale menganle la, bɛmaanle mengɔ-nyɛle gyima wɔ ɛleka mɔɔ bɛyɛ mbuluku la. Kenle ko bie mɔɔ ɛnee melefa milahyinli ne mɔɔ bɛfa bɛkɛlɛhɛlɛ mbuluku ne mɔ anzi la meayɛ gyima la, menyɛle nvo-nlɛɛ bie na ɛhye maanle mbuluku ne anzi ne dɔɔnwo kpalɛ zɛkyele. Mediema ne mɔ hanle kɛ mengɔmbɔ adiema ne mɔɔ nea yɛ nwo zo la amaneɛ. Menganle mɔɔ vale nvonleɛ ne rale la amuala mengilele ye noko edwɛkɛ ko bie mɔɔ anwo hyia la ɛdeɛ, meanga. Adie-ma ne mɔɔ nea yɛ nwo zo la vile atiakunlukɛnlɛma nu hanle hilele me kɛ, “Dahuu ɔle kpalɛ kɛ ɛbakpa kyɛlɛ.” Ɛhye maanle menwunle kɛ ɔhyia kɛ mebalie me nvo-nlɛɛ meado nu na eza meakpa kyɛlɛ.
<<<P>>>
Mediema mɔɔ bɛyɛ mgbanyinli mɔɔ bɛwɔ Bɛtɛle mɔɔ meneanle bɛ la maanle menzukoale kɛzi meba-zonle awie mɔ kpalɛ la. Kenle ko bie mɔɔ yɛlɛkɔ yɛa-hɔli ewiazo aleɛ la, yɛ nee Adiema Milton Henschel mɔɔ boka Neazo Eku ne anwo la hɔdɛnlanle ɛkponle ko anwo. Menli dɔɔnwo rale dɛlale mɔɔ ɛnee medie-ma ne mɔ anye la kɛ bɛbara la. Enee mediema mɔɔ kyɛ aleɛ ne la ɛlɛkyɛ aleɛ ne ɛdeɛ, noko ɛnee ɔnla aze ɔmmaa bɛ. Yɛdayɛ mgbavolɛ mɔɔ yɛde zɔhane ɛkpo-nle ne anwo la dendɛle yɛ nzo abo kɛ mediema mɔɔ kyɛ aleɛ ne la ɛlɛkyɛ somaa. Adiema Henschel dele mɔɔ yɛhanle la noko yeambuke ɔ nloa. Ɔdwazole ɔhɔ-bokale mediema mɔɔ bɛlɛkyɛ aleɛ ne la anwo ɔ nee bɛ hyɛle aleɛ ne. Me rɛle ɛnrɛvi zɔhane melɛbɛnwoa-ze subane ne ye ɛlɛ. Ɔmaanle mengakyele mɔɔ Gyisɛ-se noko yɛle maanle ye ɛzoanvolɛma ne la.—Dwɔn 13:3-5.
