Dwenledwenle ninyɛne mɔɔ Gyihova ɛbɔ la anwo amaa wɔanwu ye kpalɛ
(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 15)
<<<P>>>
dɔɔnwo. Ɔdwu mekɛ ne bie a Baebolo mu-mua ne ka Nyamenle subane ne mɔ anwo edwɛkɛ fɔɔnwo. (Ɛzɛ. 34:6, 7) Noko fane dɔɔnwo ne ala ɔka alesama nee kpɔkɛ nga-kyile mɔɔ bɛsisile la anwo edwɛkɛ. Saa ɛlɛ-kenga kɛlɛtokɛ zɛhae mɔ a, dwenle mɔɔ bɛ-maa ɛnwu ye wɔ Gyihova anwo la anwo.

13 Maa yɛnlea neazo ɛhye. Saa ɛlɛkenga Gyɛlɛmaya 38:6-13 a, ɛbanwu Yiteyopeanli Yibɛdemɛlɛke mɔɔ liele ngapezonli Gyɛlɛ-maya la anwo edwɛkɛ wɔ ɛkɛ. Ndɛndɛ ne ala ɛbanwu kɛzi ɛbahola wɔazukoa Yibɛ-demɛlɛke tunwomaa, ye akɛnrasesebɛ nee ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ ɔlɛ ɔmaa ɔ gɔnwo mɔ diedima la. Noko asoo edwɛkɛ ɛhye maa ɛnwu Gyi-hova subane bie ɔ? (Kenga Gyɛlɛmaya 39:15-18.) Kɛ neazo la, kɛlɛtokɛ ne foa boni a kile kɛ Gyihova kile anyezɔlɛ, ɔdi pɛ-lepɛle anzɛɛ ɔnye nyeyenu a? Gyihova su-bane boni bieko a ɛnwu ye wɔ kɛlɛtokɛ ɛhye anu a? Mekɛ biala mɔɔ ɛbagenga Bae-bolo ne anu edwɛkɛ bie la, nea kɛ ɛbanwu Gyihova subane mɔɔ ɔlɛda ye ali wɔ edwɛ-kɛ ne anu la na yɛ ye mo wɔ subane zɔhane mɔ anwo.

14 Mɔɔ tɔ zo nwiɔ, saa yɛdwenledwenle ninyɛne mɔɔ Gyihova ɛbɔ la anwo a, yɛba-zukoa ɔ nwo ninyɛne dɔɔnwo. (Edw. 145:9, 10) Nɔhalɛ nu, tɛ mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ la ala a le kɛ yɛbanlea abɔdeɛ. Ɔluakɛ menli bie mɔ fa bɛ mekɛ kɔsɔɔti sukoa abɔdeɛ nwo ni-nyɛne noko bɛnnwu Nyamenle subane ne mɔ anzɛɛ bɛnnwu kɛ ɔle awie mɔɔ wɔ ɛkɛ. Ɛzoanvolɛ Pɔɔlo hɛlɛle kɛ bɛnwu Nyame-nle “subane ne mɔ mɔɔ ɛnyelɛ ɛnnwu bɛ la wienyi ɔvi mekɛ mɔɔ ɔbɔle ewiade la, ɔlua-kɛ bɛdua ninyɛne mɔɔ ɔbɔle la azo bɛnwu ye.” (Wlo. 1:20) Baebolo ngilebɛbo bie hi-lele ngyɛnu ɛhye anu kɛ “awie mɔɔ dwenle ninyɛne nwo kpalɛ la bahola anwu Nyame-nle subane ne mɔ.” Ɔti ɔwɔ kɛ yɛdwenle ni-nyɛne mɔɔ yɛnwu bɛ la anwo amaa yɛanwu yɛ Bɔvolɛ ne subane ne mɔ.
