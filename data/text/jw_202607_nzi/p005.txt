mekɛ bie mɔɔ ɛpɛ nu la, bɛhunle Gebɛyɔnoma ne.—2 Sa. 21:1.

10 Kenga 2 Samoɛle 21:3-6. Devidi bizale Gebɛyɔnoma ne mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ ɔyɛ ɔkpɔda bɛ amaa bɛ kunlu adwo la. Asoo bɛbamaa Devidi adua ezukoa kpole bie amaa bɛ amaa bɛanyia bɛ nwo ɔ? Kyɛkyɛ. Bɛhanle kɛ edwɛkɛ ne “ɛnle dwɛtɛ fufule anzɛɛ nvutuke edwɛkɛ” ɔluakɛ Mɛla ne hanle kɛ ɔnle kɛ bɛtua ɛkpɔnedeɛ biala bɛdie kodiawu mɔɔ fɛta ewule la ngoane. (Nɔm. 35:30, 31) Ɛnee eza Gebɛyɔnoma ne ze kɛ saa belemgbunli ne ɛtɛmaanle bɛ adenle a, bɛnrɛhola bɛnrɛhu awie wɔ Yizilayɛ. Akee Devidi maanle bɛ adenle kɛ bɛhu Sɔɔlo abusua ne anu mrenyia nsuu mɔɔ ɔbayɛ kɛ bɛboale Sɔɔlo bɛmaanle ɔhunle Gebɛyɔnoma ne la. Nzinlii, ezule ne dɔle na bɛnyianle aleɛ dɔɔnwo. Ɛnee ɛhye le daselɛ mɔɔ kile kɛ Gyihova noko akunlu dwole mɔɔ bɛyɛle la anwo a, na ɔ nye anlie Gebɛyɔnoma mɔɔ bɛsisile bɛ la anwo.—2 Sa. 21:9, 10, 14.
<<<P>>>
11 Duzu a edwɛkɛ ɛhye maa yɛnwu ye wɔ Gyihova anwo a? Yɛnwu daselɛ wienyi mɔɔ kile kɛ Gyihova di pɛlepɛle. (Edw. 37:28) Menli dɔɔnwo nee menli mɔɔ bɛvi maanle gyɛne zo anzɛɛ asolo bɛ la ɛnli ye boɛ. Noko Gyihova kulo kɛ bɛ nee awie biala di ye pɛlepɛle nu. Wɔ Gyihova mekɛ kpalɛ nu, ɔbaye nzisi biala mɔɔ ye azonvolɛ ɛlɛyia la yeavi ɛkɛ. Eza yɛnwu kɛ Gyihova kulo kɛ yɛdi ye ɛwɔkɛ zo, kɛ mɔɔ ɛnee ɔkulo kɛ Yizilayɛma di anzodwolɛ nwo ɛwɔkɛ mɔɔ bɛbɔle Gebɛyɔnoma ne la azo la.—Fa toto Emɔso 1:9 anwo.

12 Kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na yɛazukoa Gebɛyɔnoma ne ɛ? Saa yɛ mediema nee yɛ anli ye boɛ bɔbɔ a, yɛbahola yɛazi abotane yɛahendɛ Gyihova. Yɛyɛ ye zɔ a ɔkile kɛ yɛdie yɛdi kɛ Gyihova bali ninyɛne nwo gyima wɔ ye mekɛ kpalɛ nu. Kɛ neazo la, adiema raalɛ Laura French bɔle ɔ bo kɛ ɔyɛ gyima wɔ Canada Bɛtɛle wɔ 1926. Kɛyɛ ɛvolɛ bulu anzi, nzisi edwɛkɛ bie dole ye. Bɛbɔle ye somolɛ kɛ ɔboka kpɔkyevolɛma ekpunli bie anwo, ɔti bɛmaanle ɔvile Bɛtɛle. Duzu a ɔyɛle wɔ edwɛkɛ ɛhye anwo a? Ɔyɛle ye nyane ɛdeɛ, noko yeandendɛ yeantia mediema ne mɔ anzɛɛ ahyehyɛdeɛ ne. Ɔvile ɛkɛ ne la, ɔyɛle adekpakyelɛ gyima ne too
