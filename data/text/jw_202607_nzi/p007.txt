Alwin mɔɔ wɔ Philippines la yɛle a. Ɛnee ɔyɛ gyima wɔ kɔmpɛlɛ bie mɔɔ bɛtu patulo la anu na ɛnee bɛtua ye kakɛ kpole. Noko ɛnee ɔkulo kɛ ɔyɛ dɔɔnwo wɔ ye ɛzonlenlɛ nu. Ɔti saa maangyebakyi zo neavolɛ bakpɔla bɛ asafo ne biala a, ɔsɛlɛ adenle ɔfi gyima nu amaa yeahola yeahɔ daselɛlilɛ na yemɔ maa ɔ nye die. Ɛnee Alwin kulo kɛ ɔyɛ dahuu adekpakyelɛnli, ɔti ɔyɛle nwolɛ asɔne fane dɔɔnwo na nzinlii ɔgyakyile ye gyima ne na ɔkpondɛle gyima mɔɔ ɔbamaa yeanyia mekɛ yeahɔ daselɛlilɛ la. Ɛnee ɔze kɛ kpɔkɛ zɔhane mɔɔ ɔzile la ɛnrɛmaa ɔnrɛnyia ezukoa dɔɔnwo kɛ dɛba ne la, noko ɔmaanle ɔdwule ye bodane nwo. Nzinlii ɔ ye noko yɛle dahuu adekpakyelɛnli na bɛ mu nwiɔ bɛhola bɛboa menli 21 bɛmaa bɛra nɔhalɛ ne anu. Yɛdie yedi kɛ, Gyihova anye die mɔdenle mɔɔ Alwin nee ɔ ye bɔle la anwo kpalɛ. Yɛdayɛ noko yɛbahola yɛanyia anwodozo kɛ Gyihova anye sɔ afɔle biala mɔɔ yɛbɔ wɔ ye ɛzonlenlɛ nu la, na ɛhye ati ɔbanlea yɛ dahuu.—Mat. 6:33.

17 Gebɛyɔnoma anwo kɛlɛtokɛ mɔɔ wɔ Baebolo ne anu la maa yɛnwu Gyihova subane ngɛnlɛma ne mɔ anwo edwɛkɛ dɔɔnwo. Ɔbɛlɛ ɔ nwo aze, ɔse anwunvɔne, ɔdi pɛlepɛle yɛɛ ɔka nɔhalɛ dahuu. Ɔmaa ye azonvolɛ nɔhavoma ahatualɛ dahuu. Eza yɛzukoa ninyɛne dɔɔnwo mɔɔ yɛbahola yɛava yɛabɔ yɛ ɛbɛla la yɛvi Gebɛyɔnoma ne ɛkɛ. Kɛ neazo la, yɛkulo kɛ yɛnyia diedi kpole kɛ Gebɛyɔnoma ne la amaa yɛava yɛ nwo yɛado Gyihova anwo zo wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɛzulolɛ baha yɛ la. Diedi zɔhae eza baboa yɛ yeamaa yɛabɛlɛ yɛ nwo aze yɛayɛ gyimalilɛ biala mɔɔ yɛbanyia wɔ asafo ne anu la. Bieko, yɛkulo kɛ yɛsi abotane yɛkendɛ Gyihova na yɛnyia anwodozo kɛ ɔbali nzisi kɔsɔɔti anwo gyima. Yɛɛ yɛbabɔ mɔdenle yɛagyinla nɔhalɛ ɛzonlenlɛ nzi kpundii. Saa yɛyɛ zɔ a, ɛnee yɛlɛkile kɛ yɛzukoa ninyɛne mgbalɛ dɔɔnwo yɛvi Gebɛyɔnoma ne ɛkɛ.
