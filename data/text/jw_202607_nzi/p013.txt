15 Nyamenle ewudwazo nwo ɛwɔkɛ ne ɛnye alɔbɔlɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛdi la ɛnvi ɛkɛ ɛdeɛ, noko ɔmaa yɛnwu kɛ Gyihova arɛle ɛtɛfile yɛ alɔvolɛ mɔɔ bɛwu la, na ɛhye kyekye yɛ rɛle. Anyelazo ɛhye kyekyele Michael mɔɔ yɛha ɔ edwɛkɛ la arɛle. Ɔhanle kɛ: “Ayeza-ya 26:19 boale me maanle menwunle kɛ Gyihova kulo me awovolɛ kpalɛ. Ɔkakye bɛ na ɔbahɔ zo yeahakye bɛ kɔkpula kɛ ɔba-dwazo bɛ wɔ ye mekɛ kpalɛ nu la. Ɛhye kye-kye me rɛle na ɔmaa menyia anwosesebɛ.” Bɛmaa yɛdayɛ noko yɛva yɛ adwenle yɛzie ɛwɔkɛ mɔɔ yebɔ kɛ yɛbanwu yɛ alɔvolɛ bie-ko la azo. Saa yɛyɛ zɔ a, ɔbade yɛ nyanelilɛ ne azo.

16 Yɛzuzu Ayezaya buluku ne anu edwɛkɛ bie mɔ mɔɔ ɔbahola yeakyekye yɛ rɛle wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛ tɛnlabelɛ bahakyi arɛlevilɛ nu, ɛtane mɔɔ yɛyɛle la ɛlɛmaa yɛ adwenle abua yɛ fɔlɛ yɛɛ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛ kulovolɛ bie bawu la anwo. Ɔyɛ yɛ fɛ kɛ Gyihova dua ye Edwɛkɛ ne azo kyekye yɛ rɛle na ɔmaa yɛ nwo tɔ yɛ la! (Edw. 94:19) Wɔnwu ngɛlɛle-ra bie mɔɔ ɛkɔ tɛnlabelɛ mɔɔ anu yɛ se la anu a ɔkyekye ɛ rɛle la ɔ? Saa ɛbahola a, kye gua ɛ ti anu, kɛlɛ wɔ buluku nu anzɛɛ kɛlɛ beta ɛleka mɔɔ ɛbanwu ye dahuu la. Yɛ nye la kɛ Gyihova, yɛ anwuma Selɛ ne mɔɔ lɛ ɛlɔlɛ la edwɛkɛ ne bakyekye ɛ rɛle.
