“ƆWƆ KƐ ESUKOA JAPANESE!”
<<<P>>>
Wɔ 1987, mengɔle Japan na me nye liele mɔdenle mɔɔ mediema ne mɔ ɛlɛbɔ azonle Gyihova nee bɛ mɛlɛbɛnwoaze ne anwo. Ɛnee mekulo kɛ meboa bɛ wɔ ɛzonlenlɛ gyima ne anu, noko me ngyegyelɛ ne a le kɛ ɛnee mende Japanese. Adiema raalɛ bie mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Michiko Oda mɔɔ wɔ Bɛtɛle ɛkɛ ne la nleanle me nyunlu kpoonwu na ɔhanle kɛ, “Ɔwɔ kɛ esukoa Japanese!” Amgba noko menzukoale. Meandwenle nwolɛ ɛlɛ kɛ ye edwɛkɛ ne bahola ahakyi me ɛbɛlabɔlɛ kenle bie.
<<<P>>>
Yeangyɛ dɔɔnwo biala, mendule mengɔle asafo mɔɔ bɛka Japanese mɔɔ bɛwɔ New York la ko anu. Japanese mɔɔ menzukoale la maanle menyianle nwolɛ adenle dɔɔnwo wɔ Gyihova ɛzonlenlɛ nu. Wɔ 1989, mengɔle me nyianu mɔɔ limoa mɔɔ bɛka Japanese la wɔ Los Angeles, California. Me nye bɔle Miwako Onami, adiema raalɛ adekpakyelɛnli bie mɔɔ ɛnee ɔboka menli mɔɔ bɛyɛle drama ne la anwo zo na me nye liele ɔ nwo.
<<<P>>>
Yɛgyale wɔ 1992, na yɛnyianle nwolɛ adenle yɛbɔle nu yɛyɛle gyima wɔ Brooklyn Bɛtɛle. Miwako dwenle menli nwo ɔti dahuu ɔkulo kɛ ɔyɛ debie ɔboa bɛ. Ye neazo ne ɛboa me ɛmaa mehakyi me subane wɔ kɛzi me nee awie mɔ di la anu. Ɔle ahyɛlɛdeɛ mɔɔ Gyihova ɛva ɛmaa me a na dahuu ɔmaa me nye die.

JAPAN BƐTƐLE YƐƐ ADEKPAKYELƐ GYIMA
<<<P>>>
Nzinlii, Miwako awovolɛ amunwiɔ mɔɔ bɛwɔ Japan la anwo dole bɛ kpole bie. Ɔti bɛmaanle yɛ adenle kɛ yɛhɔyɛ gyima wɔ Japan Bɛtɛle amaa yɛabikye bɛ na yɛahola yɛanlea bɛ. Yɛdule yɛhɔle Japan Bɛtɛle wɔ 1999.
<<<P>>>
Japan Bɛtɛle abusua ne liele yɛ kɛnlɛma, ɔti yeangyɛ na ɛkɛ ne nee yɛ nwo ali. Japan mediema ne mɔ ahunlunyele nee bɛ ɛyɛvolɛyɛlɛ mɔɔ menzukoale la boale me kpalɛ. Mediema ne mɔ ɛnva bɛ gyima ɛnli nwɔhoa fee. Eza bɛbɔ nu kɛ ekpunli bɛyɛ gyima. Nzɔne ati ɔ? Wɔ Japanma amaamuo nu, menli nwu ye kɛ saa bɛfa koyɛlɛ bɛbɔ nu bɛyɛ debie a, ɔboa kpalɛ ɔtela kɛ sonla ko angomekye babɔ mɔdenle kɛ ɔyɛ la. Bɛ neazo kɛnlɛma ne maanle menwunle kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ mebɛlɛ me nwo aze me nee awie mɔ bɔ nu yɛ gyima na mete ɔ bo kɛ me afoa nu ɛdeɛ ekyi bie ala mɔɔ mebaye la boa maa Gyihova bodane ba nu.

Kenle mɔɔ yɛgyale la
<<<P>>>
ne mɔ yɛ ninyɛne wɔ adenle bie azo la abo. Ɛnee ɔwɔ kɛ mesukoa kɛ mebanyia abotane yɛɛ ɔnle kɛ mepele me nwo. Nzinlii, mengakyile me adwenle, na akee mendele ɔ bo kɛ saa yɛbɛlɛ yɛ nwo aze yɛdie adehilelɛ mɔɔ Gyihova dua ye ahyehyɛdeɛ ne azo fa maa yɛ la yɛto nu a, ɔmaa ɔkola ɔyila kpɔkɛzilɛ biala azo. Ɔluakɛ awieleɛ bɔkɔɔ ne, Gyihova a bamaa yɛ gyima ne ali munli a.
<<<P>>>
Meta mefa ndetelɛ mɔɔ menyianle ye wɔ Bɛtɛle la metoto ndetelɛ mɔɔ ngapezonli Mosisi nyianle la anwo. Gyihova maanle Mosisi nleanle mboane ɛvolɛ 40 ɔvale ɔboale ye ɔmaanle ɔzukoale mɛlɛbɛnwoaze. Mele kɛ Mosisi la, mengɔle sukulu mendwule moa. Zɔhane nwoma ne mɔɔ menzukoale la maanle menyɛle anwomemaazo na menvale me nwo mendole me nwo zo. Bɛtɛle mɔɔ mengɔle la maanle menwunle kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ mebɛlɛ nwo aze na mefa me nwo meto Gyihova anwo zo. Kɛ mɔɔ Gyihova nyianle Mosisi anwo abotane na ɔtetele ye la, zɔhane ala a yenyia medame noko me nwo abotane na yetete me wɔ ɛvolɛ ɛhye mɔ anu a.
