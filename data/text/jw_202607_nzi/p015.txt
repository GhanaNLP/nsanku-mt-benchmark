(3) Duzu a yɛyɛ a ɔbamaa yɛanwu ye kpa-lɛ a?
<<<P>>>
GYIHOVA MƆƆ YƐNWU YE LA ABO KILE DUZU?
<<<P>>>
4 Saa yɛsuzu ɛwɔkɛ mɔɔ Gyihova bɔle Dwuuma mɔɔ bɛvi Babɛlɔn akɛlɛzonlenlɛ nu bɛbazia bɛahɔ bɛ sua zo la anwo a, ɔba-maa yɛanwu mɔɔ Gyihova mɔɔ yɛnwu ye la abo kile la. Ɔhanle kɛ: “Mebamaa bɛ aho-nle mɔɔ bamaa bɛanwu me, kɛ medame a mele Gyihova a.” (Gyɛ. 24:7) Nɔhalɛ ne a le kɛ, ɛnee bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛvi Babɛlɔn bɛzia bɛra la ze Gyihova anwo ninyɛne dɔɔnwo. Noko saa bɛbanwu Gyihova kpalɛ a, ɔwɔ kɛ bɛfi bɛ ahonle nu amuala bɛbikye ye. Bulu-ku ko hanle kɛ: “Ngakyile kpole wɔ Nyame-nle mɔɔ yɛnwu ɔ nwo debie nee Nyame-nle mɔɔ yɛnwu ye la avinli. Yɛbahola yɛava ɛhye yɛatoto ngakyile mɔɔ wɔ awie mɔɔ ɔlɛ-kenga ɛhulolɛ nwo edwɛkɛ nee awie mɔɔ ɔkulo awie avinli la anwo.” Saa yɛnwu kɛ Gyihova lɛ duma yɛɛ ɔle Nyamenle a, ɛnee yɛtɛwiele. Yɛkulo kɛ yɛnwu Gyihova suba-ne ngɛnlɛma ne mɔ.
<<<P>>>
5 Saa yɛze Gyihova a, ɔkile kɛ yɛze mɔɔ ɔkulo nee mɔɔ ɔngulo. Belemgbunli Dwo-saya yɛle ɛhye anwo neazo. Baebolo ne ka kɛ: “Wɔ ye tumililɛ ɛvolɛ ne mɔɔ tɔ zolɛ 8 la anu, mekɛ mɔɔ ɛnee ɔtɛde kakula la, ɔbɔle ɔ bo kɛ ɔkpondɛ ye nenyia Devidi Nyame-nle ne abo zo.” (2 Ek. 34:3) Dwosaya zu-koale Gyihova anwo debie yɛɛ ɔvale mɔɔ ɔzukoale la ɔbɔle ye ɛbɛla. Ɛhye ati Gyiho-va hanle hilele ngapezonli Gyɛlɛmaya kɛ, Dwosaya lile “pɛlepɛle na ɔyɛle tenlene” yɛɛ ɔlile bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛnwunle amaneɛ la nee ehyiavolɛma edwɛkɛ ɔmaanle bɛ. Akee Gyihova doale zolɛ kɛ: “Asoo tɛ ɛhye a awie yɛ a ɔkile kɛ ɔze me a?”—Gyɛ. 22:15, 16.
<<<P>>>
6 Kenga Edwɛne 9:10. Saa yɛnwu Gyi-hova kpalɛ a, ɔbamaa yɛanyia ye nu anwo-dozo bɔkɔɔ. Zɔhane a ɛzoanvolɛ Pɔɔlo noko yɛle a. Mɔɔ bɛdole ye efiade mɔɔ li awieleɛ na ɛnee ɔnrɛhyɛ na bɛahu ye la, ɔhɛlɛle kɛ: “Ɔnyɛ me nyiane. Ɔluakɛ meze Ahenle mɔɔ melie ye meli la.” (2 Tim. 1:12) Ninyɛne mɔɔ Pɔɔlo zukoale nee edwɛkɛ mɔɔ sisile ye ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nu la maanle ɔvale ɔ nwo ɔdole Nyamenle anwo zo bɔkɔɔ. Pɔɔlo nyianle anwodozo kɛ Gyihova arɛle ɛnrɛvi ye yɛɛ ɔbahakye nɔhalɛlilɛ mɔɔ ɔvale ɔzo-nlenle ye la, ɔti ɔbadwazo ye yeara ngoa-ne nu.

DUZU ATI A ƆWƆ KƐ YƐKƆ ZO YƐNWU GYIHOVA KPALƐ A?
<<<P>>>
7 Mɔɔ yɛbɔle ɔ bo kɛ yɛsukoa Gyihova anwo debie la, yɛbɔle mɔdenle kɛ yɛbanwu ye kpalɛ noko bie a mekɛ ɛlɛkɔ la yɛnrɛyɛ ye zɔ bieko. Duzu ati ɔ? Ɔluakɛ saa yɛdayɛ mumua ne yɛlɛkenga Baebolo ne anzɛɛ yɛ nee asafo ne ɛlɛbɔ nu agenga ye wɔ debie-zukoalɛ bo a, yɛfa yɛ adwenle yɛsie kɛzi yɛ-bava yɛali gyima anzɛɛ kɛzi yɛbava yɛaboa awie mɔ la azo. Baebolo ne maa yɛ anwose-sebɛ kɛ yɛyɛ ye zɔ. (2 Tim. 3:16, 17) Noko eza Baebolo ne ka Ahenle mɔɔ maanle bɛ-hɛlɛle la anwo edwɛkɛ. (Edw. 25:8-10) Ɔti ɔnle kɛ yɛmaa kɛzi mɔɔ yɛlɛkenga la baboa yɛ anzɛɛ awie mɔ la di yɛ ti somaa. Emomu ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkakye kɛ yɛbabiza yɛ nwo kɛ, ‘Duzu a edwɛkɛ ɛhye maa menwu ye wɔ Gyihova Nyamenle anwo a?’*
<<<P>>>
* Nea foa ne mɔɔ wɔ Ewiade Fofolɛ Ngilebɛbo ne anyu-nlu mɔɔ ɔ ti ne se “Nyamenle Edwɛkɛ Ne Mukenye” la kpuya 20 ne.
