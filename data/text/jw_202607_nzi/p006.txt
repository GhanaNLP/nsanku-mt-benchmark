13 Mɔɔ Belemgbunli Devidi wule la, ɔ nzi ɛvolɛ kɛyɛ 500, Baebolo ne eza hanle Gebɛyɔnoma anwo edwɛkɛ. Enee Dwuuma mɔɔ hɔle akɛlɛzonlenlɛ nu wɔ Babelɔn ɛvolɛ 70 la ɛlɛsia ahɔ bɛ sua zo. Dwuuma ekpunli mɔɔ limoa la nee Amɛnlado Zɛlubabɛle a ziale hɔle Gyɛlusalɛm a. (Ɛzela 2:1, 2, 58) Wɔ ɛvolɛ 70 anzi, ekpunli fofolɛ noko nee kɛlɛvolɛ Ɛzela ziale hɔle. (Ɛzela 7:1-7) Enee “Nɛtinɛmma” mbusua ne mɔ bie boka ekpunli ngyikyi nwiɔ zɔhane mɔɔ ziale rale la anwo. (Nea Ɛzela 2:58 nee 7:7 ɔbodwɛkɛ ne mɔ.) Noko nwane mɔ a le Nɛtinɛmma ne? Enee bɛle “ɛzonlenlɛsua nu sonvolɛma, anzɛɛ azonvolɛ mɔɔ bɛnle Yizilayɛma,” na ɔbayɛ kɛ bɛ nuhua dɔɔnwo le Gebɛyɔnoma abozoamra.—Nea “Nɛtinɛmma” wɔ Baebolo Edwɛkɛbohilelɛ ne anu.

14. Kɛzi Gebɛyɔnoma ne hilele kɛ bɛdi nɔhalɛ bɛmaa Gyihova ɛ? (1 Edwɛkɛsisilɛ 9:2 nee ɔbodwɛkɛ)
<<<P>>>
nlɛ ɛhulolɛ kɛ bɛbagyakyi ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ bɛ nzi na bɛazia bɛahɔ amaa bɛaboa bɛamaa bɛakyekye bɛ maanle ne bieko. Noko enee Dwuuma nɔhavoma nee Gebɛyɔnoma abozoamra anye la kɛ bɛbanwu kɛ bɛbɔ nɔhalɛ ɛzonlenlɛ bo wɔ ɛzonlenlɛsua ne mɔɔ wɔ Gyɛlusalɛm la anu. Ɔti bɛbɔle mɔdenle biala mɔɔ bɛbahola la bɛziale bɛhɔle. Enee bɛnlɛ azɛlɛ wɔ Yizilayɛ kɛ Dwuuma ne la. Noko bɛziale bɛhɔle kɛ bɛkadoa bɛ ɛzonlenlɛsua nu gyimayɛlɛ ne azo na bɔbɔ bɛaboa bɛamaa bɛayɛ Gyɛlusalɛm bane ne boɛ.—Nih. 3:26.

15 Duzu a edwɛkɛ ɛhye maa yɛnwu ye wɔ Gyihova anwo a? Ɔmaa yɛnwu kɛ Nyamenle kulo menli mɔɔ kɔ zo sonle ye la kpole yɛɛ ɔnea bɛ dahuu. Ɔvi mekɛ mɔɔ Gyihova liele Gebɛyɔnoma kɔdwu mekɛ mɔɔ Babelɔn akɛlɛzonlenlɛ ne rale awieeɛ la, enee asɛɛ ɛvolɛ 1,000 ɛpɛ nu. Noko Gyihova hɔle zo yilale Gebɛyɔnoma ne. Ɔliele bɛ bo zo amra wɔ Dwuda nee Gyɛlusalɛm ɛzɛkyelɛ ne anu wɔ ɛvolɛ 607 K.Y.M. Mɔɔ bɛvi akɛlɛzonlenlɛ nu bɛrale la, ɔhɔle zo ɔvale bɛ ɔlile gyima wɔ nɔhalɛ ɛzonlenlɛ nu; bɛ nee Livaema ne yɛle gyima wɔ ɛzonlenlɛsua ne anu. Ɔbayɛ kɛ enee bie mɔ de bikye ɛzonlenlɛsua ne bɔbɔ. (Ɛzela 2:70; Nih. 11:21) Na kɛmɔ enee bɛyɛ gyima wɔ ɛzonlenlɛsua ne anu la ati, enee bɛndua adwule biala.—Ɛzela 7:24.
