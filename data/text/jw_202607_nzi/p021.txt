4 Gyisɛse hanle kɛ mɛla ne anu kpole kpa-
lɛ ne a le kɛ yɛbava yɛ ahonle, yɛ ɛkɛla, yɛ
adwenle nee yɛ anwosesebɛ muala yɛahulo
Gyihova. (Kenga Maake 12:30.) Na ɔwɔ kɛ
Baebolo sukoavo ne nwu Gyihova amaa yea-
hulo ye. Eza yɛkpondɛ kɛ sukoavo ne yila ɔ
nwo zo maa Gyihova na tɛ yɛdayɛ anzɛɛ ngi-
lehilelɛ bie anzɛɛ ahyehyɛdeɛ bie ɔ. Ɔti saa
yɛ nee yɛ sukoavo ne ɛlɛsukoa debie a, yɛ
bodane titili a le kɛ yɛboa ye yɛamaa yeanwu
Gyihova na yeahulo ye.
<<<P>>>
5 Saa ɛkulo awie edwɛkɛ a, ɔmaa ɛyɛ tieyɛ
ɛmaa ye. Ɔti saa wɔ sukoavo ne nwu Gyiho-
va na ɔkulo ye a, ɔbaha ye yeamaa yeadie ye.
Gyisɛse hanle kɛ: “Amaa ewiade ne anwu kɛ
mekulo Selɛ ne la, melɛyɛ mɔɔ Selɛ ne ɛha
ɛhile me kɛ menyɛ la ala.” (Dwɔn 14:31) Zɔ-
hane ala a Baebolo sukoavo bie nyia Gyiho-
va anwo ɛlɔlɛ a, ɔbaha ye yeamaa yeayɛ tieyɛ
yeamaa ye na yeabeta ye dahuu a.—1 Dwɔn
5:3.

6 Saa Baebolo sukoavoma nwu Gyihova
kpalɛ a ɔbamaa bɛasisi kpɔkɛ kpalɛ. Maa
yɛyɛ ndonwo bie. Saa awie kenga alopile
ɛhanlɛ nwo buluku anzɛɛ ɔkɔkɛlɛ nwolɛ sɔ-
nea ala a, yemɔ ɛngile kɛ ɔbanwu alopile ka.
Ɔwɔ kɛ bɛkile ye na ɔka ye fane dɔɔnwo
amaa yeanwu ɔ nye zo kpalɛ. Na mekɛ ɛlɛkɔ
la, ɔbanwu kɛzi ɔwɔ kɛ ɔka alopile ne wɔ tɛ-
nlabelɛ ngakyile nu la. Zɔhane ala a ninyɛ-
ne mɔɔ bɛbahilehile Baebolo sukoavo bie la
angome ɛnrɛmaa ɔnrɛnwu Gyihova kpalɛ a,
emomu ɔwɔ kɛ ɔgyinla mɔɔ yezukoa la azo
ɔsisi kpɔkɛ na ɔnwu kɛzi ɔbaboa ye la. Mekɛ
<<<P>>>
mɔɔ ɔlɛnyia Gyihova anwo ndelebɛbo dɔɔ-
nwo na ɔnwu kɛzi ye anwuma Selɛ ne boa ye
la, ɔbamaa yeahola yeasisi kpɔkɛ kpalɛ wɔ
tɛnlabelɛ biala anu.
<<<P>>>
7 Kɛmɔ ɛnee Dwosefi ze Gyihova la ati,
ɔboale ye wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɛnee Pɔtefaa aye ne
kulo kɛ ɔ nee ye da la. Wɔ Dwosefi ngakula
nu, ɔdele kɛ mɔɔ Gyihova bɔle Adam la,
ɔmaanle ye yelɛ kokye, Yive. Eza ɔdele mɔɔ
zile wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ nrenyia gyɛne vale Ebile-
ham aye Sɛla la anwo edwɛkɛ. (Gyn. 2:24;
20:3, 6) Na edwɛkɛ zɔhane mɔɔ ɔdele la
maanle ɔnwunle kɛzi Gyihova bu agyalɛ nee
nla nu ngitanwolilɛ la. Enee Dwosefi ze Gyi-
hova kpalɛ ɔti ɔ nee Pɔtefaa aye ne anla
na ɔhanle ɔhilele ye kɛ: “Kɛzi mekola meyɛ
amumuyɛ kpole ɛhye na meyɛ ɛtane metia
Nyamenle ɛ?” (Gyn. 39:7-9) Enee Dwosefi
ze mɔɔ le kpalɛ nee mɔɔ le ɛtane wɔ Gyiho-
va anye zo la. Enee eza ɔze mɔɔ maa ye Nya-
menle ne di nyane la. Ɛhye ati ɔzile kpɔkɛ
kpalɛ. Saa yɛboa yɛ Baebolo sukoavoma yɛ-
maa bɛnwu Gyihova kpalɛ na bɛnwu ye su-
bane ne mɔ a, bɛdabɛ noko bɛbasisi kpɔkɛ
mɔɔ sɔ Nyamenle anye la.
