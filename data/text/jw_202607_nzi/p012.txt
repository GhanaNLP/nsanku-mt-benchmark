SAA YƐ KULOVOLƐ BIE WU A
<<<P>>>
12 Saa yɛ kulovolɛ bie wu a, ɔbahola yea-maa yɛali alɔbɔlɛ kpole yɛɛ ɔ nwo nyane mɔɔ yɛbali la bahola ahyɛ. Ɔyɛ a yɛnnwu deɛmɔti Gyihova maanle yɛ kulovolɛ ne wule la. Zɔhane a dole adiema bie mɔɔ bɛ-fɛlɛ ye Michael mɔɔ senle yelale ye awovo-lɛ la a. Ɔhanle kɛ: “Me ɛbɔlɛ dele kpalɛ na menvale Gyihova anwo ɛya kɛ ɔmaanle adenle ɔmaanle bɛwule la. Meze kɛ tɛ ɔda-ye a ɔhunle bɛ a noko anrɛɛ ɔwɔ kɛ ɔdie bɛ.” Bie a zɔhane ala a ɛdawɔ noko ɛdele nganeɛ a. Saa ɛkɔ tɛnlabelɛ zɛhae mɔɔ ɛlɛ-maa wɔali alɔbɔlɛ la anu a, kɛzi Gyihova ba-kyekye ɛ rɛle na yeamaa wɔanyia anyela-zo ɛ?
<<<P>>>
13 Kenga Ayezaya 25:8. Gyihova ɛbɔ ɛwɔkɛ kɛ “ɔbame ewule bɔkɔɔ.” Wɔmɔ pɛ mekɛ mɔɔ ewule ɛnle ɛkɛ ne la anwo nvoni-nli nea! Ɛnrɛzu wɔ kulovolɛ bie mɔɔ ɛwu la bieko. (Aye. 35:10) Ɛnrɛnwu wɔ alɔvolɛ mɔɔ bɛwu la azie na wɔali nyane. Emomu, ɛ nee bɛ badɛnla dahuu yɛɛ bɛ nwo ɛnrɛdo bɛ. Na tɛ awie bie a ɛbɔ ɔ ti anu ɛbɔ ɛwɔkɛ ɛhye o, emomu Nyamenle mumua ne mɔɔ lɛ tumi yɛ ɛhye la a bɔle ɛwɔkɛ ne a.—Yek. 21:4, 5.
<<<P>>>
14 Kenga Ayezaya 26:19. Tɛ kɛ Gyihova baye ewule avi ɛkɛ ne ala o, eza ɔbadwazo yɛ alɔvolɛ mɔɔ bɛwu la yeara ngoane nu bieko. Ɔhanle bɛ nwo edwɛkɛ bɔbɔ kɛ: “Me avunli badwazo.” Edwɛkɛ ɛhye kile kɛ Gyi-hova bu bɛ kɛ bɛle ye ɛdeɛ. Bɛ aluma tɛbɔ ɔ ti anu. Na mekɛ mɔɔ yehile la dwu a, ɔba-dwazo bɛ. Kɛ mɔɔ ɛbɔlɛ maa ndile mɔɔ
