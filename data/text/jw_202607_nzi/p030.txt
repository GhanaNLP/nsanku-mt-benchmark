Mɔɔ ɔka ekyii ɔwu la, ɛnee akee ɔwɔ kɛ ɔtɛnla abuburama ebia nu. Noko akee, wɔ ɛhye mɔ amuala anu, Gyihova anwo ɛlɔlɛ mɔɔ ɔlɛ la anga aze ɛlɛ. Saa ɔlɛka nɔhalɛ ne anwo edwɛkɛ biala a, ɔ nye die kpalɛ. Masako yɛle kɛzi yɛbamaa yɛ kunlu adwo yɛ nwo na yɛamaa yɛ nye alie ɔnva nwo tɛnlabelɛ biala mɔɔ yɛwɔ nu la anwo neazo kɛnlɛma na mekulo kɛ mesukoa.

Yɛvale ɛvolɛ mɔɔ bo 30 la yɛyɛle gyima wɔ Bɛtele. Noko wɔ 2024, bɛmaanle yɛhɔyɛle adekpakyelɛ titilima na ɛnee ɛhye le nzenzaleɛ kpole kpalɛ wɔ yɛ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nu. Kɛkala yɛwɔ bɔra bie mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Okinawa la azo na yɛnyia nwolɛ adenle yɛdi mbusua mɔɔ bɛdede sogyama azokɔeleɛ mɔɔ bikye yɛ nwo ɛkɛ ne la daselɛ. Ɔlua ndetelɛ kpalɛ mɔɔ yɛnyianle ye wɔ Bɛtele la ati, yeammaa nzenzaleɛ ɛhye mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ la anyɛ se ammaa yɛ. Yɛbɔ mɔdenle biala kɛ yɛbayɛ ngyehyɛleɛ kpalɛ na yɛali zo amaa yɛava yɛ mekɛ yɛali gyima kpalɛ. Ɛhye maa yɛfa yɛ adwenle yɛsie yɛ ɛzonlenlɛ gyima ne azo na yɛmia yɛ nwo. Gyihova ɛmaa yɛnyia Baebolo sukoavoma dɔɔnwo mɔɔ bɛrayɛ kɛ yɛ mbusuafoɔ la. Akee yɛlɛnwu ye bɔkɔɔ kɛ ɔhyia kɛ yɛkulo menli. Me nee Miwako yɛ Gyihova mo dahuu kɛ yeva gyima kɛnlɛma ɛhye yemaa yɛ la.

<<<P>>>
Kɛkala me abusua ne ɛndwazo ɛntia me ɛdeɛ, noko bɛtɛbale nɔhalɛ ne anu. Noko Gyisɛse bɔle ɛwɔkɛ kɛ menli mɔɔ bɛbagyakyikyi bɛ mbusuafoɔ na bɛarayɛ ye ɛdoavolɛma la banyia sunsum nu abusua mɔnwo 100. (Maake 10:29, 30) Zɔhane ala a medame noko menwu ye a. Menrɛhola menrɛdia mbusua dodo mɔɔ bɛwɔ ewiade amuala mɔɔ bɛhulole me na bɛyɛle me kɛ asɛɛ meboka bɛ abusua ne anwo la.

Metɛdɛ Truth buluku ne mɔɔ Wilson vale maanle me la wɔ me nwo. Zɔhane buluku ne maa mekakye adenle kɛnlɛma mɔɔ Gyihova luale zo maanle menwunle ye la kpalɛ. (1 Ek. 28:9) Mebɔ kpɔkɛ kɛ mebamaa Gyihova ahɔ zo atete me na meaboa awie mɔ meamaa bɛazukoa nɔhalɛ ne mɔɔ kɔ dahuu ngoane nu la.
