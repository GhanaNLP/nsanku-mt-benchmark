ƐBƐLABƆLE NU EDWƐKƐ

Gyihova Ɛboa Me Yemaa Menyia Subane Kpalɛ
<<<P>>>
KEN KIUCHI ANLOA EDWƐKƐ
<<<P>>>
ME RƐLE ɛnrɛvi kenle mɔɔ limoa mɔɔ membɔle ɔ bo kɛ meyɛ gyima wɔ ɛleka mɔɔ bɛyɛ mbuluku wɔ Brooklyn Bɛtɛle, New York, U.S.A. la ɛlɛ. Mɔɔ menwɔlole ɛkɛ ne ala na mendele kɛzi milahyinli ne mɔ mɔɔ bɛfa bɛyɛ mbuluku ne mɔ la ɛlɛkɔ zo ayɛ dede la, ɛnee ɔnla aze ɔmmaa me. Ɛnee ngɛlata mɔɔ milahyinli ne mɔ ɛlɛpɛpɛ la ɛmaa ɛkɛ ne ɛyɛ munlutu kpalɛ yɛɛ ɛkɛ ne noko bɔnle oil ngome.
<<<P>>>
Menwunle ninyɛne dɔɔnwo, noko mɔɔ me nye liele nwolɛ kpalɛ la a le menli mɔɔ wɔ ɛkɛ ne la. Ɛnee me-diema mgbavolɛ nee mbɛlɛra bie mɔ wɔ ɛkɛ ne mɔɔ ɔzɔho kɛ gyima ko ne ala a bɛyɛ ye alehyenlɛ biala a. Ɛnee menli ɛnnwu bɛ na bɛahanvo bɛ. Noko ɛnee bɛ nye die yɛɛ bɛ kunlu dwo bɛ nwo. Bɛ neazo ne boale me maanle menwunle kɛzi ɔwɔ kɛ mebɛlɛ me nwo aze mesonle Gyihova la.
<<<P>>>
Gyihova ɛboa me wɔ ɛvolɛ dɔɔnwo anu ɔlɛmaa meanyia subane kpalɛ yɛɛ ɔlɛkɔ zo yeaboa me. Noko maa menlimoa menga kɛzi ɛnee melɛkpondɛ nɔhalɛ ne wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɛnee mele Budanli la anwo edwɛkɛ mengile wɔ.
<<<P>>>
ƐNEE MELƐKPONDƐ NYAMENLE WƆ ME NGAKULA NU
<<<P>>>
Bɛwole me wɔ Chicago, Illinois, U.S.A., yɛɛ ɛkɛ ne a bɛtetele me a. Yɛle ngakula nna na medame a mele belamunli ralɛ a. Me awovolɛ dule vile Japan hɔle Chicago ɔluakɛ ɛnee bɛsuzu kɛ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ ne bayɛ kpalɛ amaa bɛ wɔ America. Ɛnee bɛkulo kɛ bɛ mra ne mɔ kɔ sukulu kpalɛ na bɛ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ noko wie bɛ boɛ.
<<<P>>>
Ɛnee me awovolɛ le Budama mɔɔ bɛnva bɛ ɛzonle-nlɛ bɛnli nwɔhoa fee. Ɛnee Budama ɛzonlenlɛsua gyi awuke mɔɔ bɛtetele me mame wɔ nu la anu. Ye papa nee ɔ mediema mrenyia nwiɔ le Buda ɛzonlenlɛ nu mgbanyima. Ɛhye ati wɔ me ngakula nu, ɛnee eye me efi Buda ɛzonlenlɛ nu a, ɔnyɛ boɛ. Saa mefi suku-lu meba sua nu ala a, medimoa mekɔyela incense memaa yɛ Buda ananze ne na mefa awule nee nzule mebɔ afɔle memaa ye. Ɔdwu Molɛ a, yɛkɔ Buda ɛzo-nlenlɛsua bie anu yɛkɔyɛ deɛ ko ne ala bie wɔ ɛkɛ, noko ananze ne mɔɔ wɔ ɛkɛ ne la le kpole tɛla yɛ edeɛ ne yɛɛ bɛva nvutuke bɛfɔle nwolɛ amuala.
<<<P>>>
Mekakye kɛ mɔɔ menlile asɛɛ ɛvolɛ nsuu la, menea-nle ananze ne mɔɔ gyi yɛ sua nu ɛkɛ ne la na membi-zale me nwo kɛ, ‘Saa mewu a, duzu a bado me a?’ Bɛ-hilehile me wɔ Buda ɛzonlenlɛ nu kɛ saa mewu a, mebadɛnla aze wɔ ɛleka bie kɛ sunsum nu abɔdeɛ. Ɔti menwunle kɛ kenle ko dedee mebawu. Bie a me-bayɛ sunsum nu abɔdeɛ ɛdeɛ, noko menrɛhola me-nrɛyɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ meyɛ ye kɛ sonla la bieko. Ɛhye maanle me rɛle bɔle na mendele nganeɛ kɛ menlɛ moalɛ biala.
<<<P>>>
Mɔɔ menlile asɛɛ ɛvolɛ 15 la, akee menwunle kɛ nvasoɛ ɛnle ninyɛne mɔɔ meyɛ ye wɔ Buda ɛzonle-nlɛ nu la azo. Ɛnee mehyia debie bie wɔ me ɛbɛlabɔ-lɛ nu. (Mat. 5:3) Ɛnee medie medi bɔkɔɔ kɛ Nyamenle bie mɔɔ de aze na ɔlɛ tumi ɔtɛla awie biala la wɔ ɛkɛ. Ɛnee mekulo kɛ menwu ye. Ɔrale me ti anu kɛ bie a Baebolo ne bahola abua kpuya ngayile mɔɔ melɛ wɔ
