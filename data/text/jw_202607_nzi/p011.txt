“Wɔvuandi me ɛtane ne mɔ kɔsɔɔti wɔgua ɛ nzi”
<<<P>>>
Gyihova ɛnva ye adwenle ɛnzie ɛtane mɔɔ yɛyɛle ye dɛba la azo
(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 11)
<<<P>>>
ɛtane ne mɔ kɔsɔɔti wɔgua ɛ nzi” abo bieko kɛ “wɔye me ɛtane ne mɔ kɔsɔɔti wɔvi ɛ nyunlu.” Bɛha Gyihova anwo edwɛkɛ wɔ ɛke kɛ asɛɛ ɔlɛfa ɛtanevolɛ mɔɔ ɛnlu ɔ nwo la ɛtane ne mɔ na yeavuandi yeagua ɔ nzi. Ɔbagua bɛ wɔ ɛleka mɔɔ ɔnrɛnwu bɛ bieko anzɛɛ ɔnrɛdwenle nwolɛ bɔbɔ la. Buluku bie hanle kɛ, eza yɛbahola yɛaha ngɛlɛlera ɛhye zɛhae: “Wɔyɛ me ɛtane ne mɔ kɛ asɛɛ metɛyɛle ɛhwee la.” Ɔti saa Gyihova fa yɛ ɛtane kyɛ a, wɔ ɔ nye zo ɔle kɛ asɛɛ yɛanyɛ ɛtane zɔhane bɔbɔ. Gyihova fa yɛ ɛtane kyɛ yɛ a, ɔnrɛmaa yɛnrɛbu nwolɛ mgbonda kenle bie anzɛɛ ɔnrɛgyinla zo ɔnrɛhwe yɛ nzo. Emomu ɔkyikyi ɔfi ɛke ne bɔkɔɔ. Yebɔ yɛ ewɔkɛ kɛ: “Menrɛhakye wɔ ɛtane ne mɔ bieko.” (Aye. 43:25) Ewɔkɛ ɛhye kyekye yɛ rɛle kpalɛ! Yemɔti saa Gyihova, Ndɛnebua-volɛ Kpole ne ɛngakye yɛ ɛtane ne mɔ a, ɛnee duzu ati a ɔwɔ kɛ yɛdwenle nwo too yɛ rɛle bɔ a?

Mmamaa ɛtane mɔɔ ɛyɛle ye dɛba la sɛkye wɔ anyelielɛ kɛkala
<<<P>>>
11 Saa yɛ adwenle ɛlɛkɔ zo abua yɛ fɔlɛ a, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkakye ewɔkɛ mɔɔ Gyihova ɛbɔ la. Ɔse saa ɔfa ɛtane ɔkyɛ a, ɛnee yeva ye-hyɛ bɔkɔɔ. Ɛtane mɔɔ yɛyɛle ye dɛba ne la ɛmmaa yɛ nwo ɛnyɛ ye nyiane yɛɛ ɔnle kɛ alesama mɔɔ ɛnli munli mɔɔ fa ɛya sie bɛ nu la. (Aye. 57:16) Tɛ mɔɔ hyia Gyihova kɛ-kala la a le ɛbɛla mɔɔ yɛbɔle ye dɛba la, emomu ɛbɛla mɔɔ yɛlɛbɔ ye ɛnɛ nee subane mɔɔ yɛlɛbɔ mɔdenle kɛ yɛbanyia la. (Aye. 55:7) Amgba yɛsonle Nyamenle mɔɔ lɛ ɛlɔ-lɛ na ɔfa ɛtane ɔkyɛ la! Ɔti ɔnle kɛ yɛmaa ɛtane mɔɔ yɛyɛle ye dɛba la sɛkye yɛ anye-lielɛ ɛnɛ anzɛɛ ɔmaa nyilalɛ mɔɔ yɛbanyia ye kenle bie la fi yɛ sa.
