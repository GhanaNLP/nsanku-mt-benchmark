EDWƐNE 12
Tumivolɛ Gyihova Nyamenle
<<<P>>>
BODANE
Yɛbazukoa mɔɔ Gyihova mɔɔ yɛnwu ye la abo kile, deɛmɔti ɔwɔ kɛ yɛnyia ɛhu-lolɛ kɛ yɛbanwu ye kpalɛ nee mɔɔ yɛyɛ a ɔbamaa yɛanwu ye kpalɛ la anwo debie.
<<<P>>>
Ɛlɛnwu Gyihova Kpalɛ Ɔ?
<<<P>>>
‘Nwu wɔ papa Nyamenle ne na fa ahonle mɔɔ di munli la sonle ye.’—1 EK. 28:9.
<<<P>>>
ASOO wɔdwenle kɛzi Gyihova adwenle le kuloonwu, ye nrɛ-lɛbɛ le kpole anzɛɛ ninyɛne dodo mɔɔ yeyɛ la anwo ɛlɛ ɔ? Saa yɛdwenle ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ amuala anwo a, ɔmaa yɛ Bɔvolɛ ne anwo yɛ yɛ nwanwane. Bie a ɛbade nganeɛ kɛ ɛzoanvolɛ Pɔɔlo la, ɔhɛlɛle kɛ: “O nea kɛzi Nyamenle anwonyia nee ye nrɛlɛbɛ nee ye adwenle le kuloonwu a! Nea kɛzi bɛngola bɛ-nneɛnlea ye ndɛnebualɛ nu na bɛngola bɛnnwu ye ndenle nu bɛnwie ɛlɛ a!”—Wlo. 11:33, 34.
<<<P>>>
2 Yɛnrɛhola yɛnrɛnwu Gyihova anwo debie biala ɛdeɛ, noko yɛbahola yɛanwu kɛzi ɔde la anwo debie ekyi. Duzu ati a yɛkola yɛka ye zɔ a? Belemgbunli Devidi hanle hilele ɔ ra Sɔlɔmɔn kɛ: ‘Nwu wɔ papa Nyamenle ne na fa ahonle mɔɔ di munli la sonle ye. Saa ɛkpondɛ ye a, ɔbamaa wɔanwu ye.’ (1 Ek. 28:9) Devidi hanle edwɛkɛ ɛhye la ɛvolɛ ɛya dɔɔnwo anzi, ɛzoanvolɛ Dwɔn noko hakyele ɔ mediema Kilisienema kɛ Gyisɛse ɛmaa “yɛnyia nwunu amaa yɛanyia nɔhavo ne anwo ndelebɛbo.” (1 Dwɔn 5:20) Yɛɛ Gyisɛse mumua ne ha-nle kɛ “awie biala mɔɔ Ralɛ ne kulo kɛ ɔda [Gyihova] ali ɔki-le ye la” banyia Gyihova anwo adwenle.—Mat. 11:27.
<<<P>>>
3 Yɛdayɛ mɔɔ yɛyila yɛ nwo zo yɛmaa Gyihova la amuala ɛzukoa ɔ nwo ninyɛne dɔɔnwo dɛba. Noko yɛtɛgyakyile kɛ yɛbahɔ zo yɛanwu Gyihova. (Dwɔn 17:3) Yɛbahola yɛazukoa ninyɛne mɔɔ maa ɔ nye die nee kɛzi yɛbahola yɛamaa anwo-dozo mɔɔ yɛlɛ wɔ ye nu la ayɛ kpole la anwo ninyɛne dɔɔnwo dahuu. Ɛhye ati maa yɛzuzu kpuya nsa mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛ muala yɛdwenle nwo ɔnva nwo mekɛ tendenle mɔɔ yɛva yɛzonle Gyihova la anwo: (1) Gyihova mɔɔ yɛnwu ye la abo kile duzu? (2) Duzu ati a ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkɔ zo yɛnwu ye kpalɛ a? yɛɛ
<<<P>>>
1. Saa yɛdwenledwenle Gyihova adwenle, ye nrɛlɛbɛ nee ninyɛne mɔɔ yeyɛ la anwo a, kɛzi ɔka yɛ ɛ?
2. Duzu a kile kɛ yɛbahola yɛanwu Gyihova a?
3. Duzu a yɛbazuzu nwo wɔ edwɛkɛ ɛhye anu a?
