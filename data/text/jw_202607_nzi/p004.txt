Gebɛyɔnoma ne zɛlɛle Dwɔhyua kɛ ɔboa bɛ. (Dwɔh. 10:3-7) Dwɔhyua nee Yizilayɛma hɔboale bɛ, na Gyihova maanle bɛlile konim. Gyihova maanle ezuawolɛ mgbole bɔbɔ vi anwuma dɔle guale Amɔlema ne anwo zo yɛɛ ɔluale sɛkɛleneɛ adenle zo ɔmaanle sɛnzene ne gyinlanle zolɛ too bɛhunle bɛ agbɔvolɛ kɔsɔɔti. (Dwɔh. 10:9-14) Anzodwolɛ ngyekyeleɛ mɔɔ Gebɛyɔnoma ne nee Yizilayɛma yɛle yɛɛ Dwɔhyua mɔɔ bɛzɛlɛle ye kɛ ɔboa bɛ la hilele kɛ ɛnee bɛlɛ diedi bɔkɔɔ kɛ Gyihova bali ye edwɛkɛ zo na yeabɔ bɛ nwo bane.

6 Duzu a edwɛkɛ ɛhye maa yɛnwu ye wɔ Gyihova anwo a? Gyihova bɛlɛ ɔ nwo aze yɛɛ ɔse anwunvɔne. Gyihova limoale hanle hilele Yizilayɛma kɛ, “bɛvoa menli mɔɔ wɔ azelɛ ne azo la kɔsɔɔti,” na ɛnee Gebɛyɔnoma ne noko boka nwo. (Nɔm. 33:51, 52) Noko ɔlua anzodwolɛ ngyekyeleɛ mɔɔ Yizilayɛma nee bɛ yɛle la ati, Gyihova zele bɛ anwunvɔne na yeammaa bɛangu bɛ. Gyihova yɛle ye zɔ ɔnva nwo kɛ Yizilayɛma anlimoa ambiza ye nwolɛ edwɛkɛ la. Ɔboale Yizilayɛma ɔmaanle bɛlile ngyekyeleɛ ne azo yɛɛ ɔluale nwanwane adenle zo ɔliele Gebɛyɔnoma ne.—Dwɔh. 9:26; 11:19.

7 Kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na yɛazukoa Gebɛyɔnoma ne ɛ? Ɔwɔ kɛ yɛnyia diedi kpole wɔ Gyihova anu. Yɛze Gyihova anwo edwɛkɛ dɔɔnwo yɛtɛla Gebɛyɔnoma ne, ɔti ɔwɔ kɛ yemɔ bɔbɔ maa yɛfa yɛ nwo yɛto ɔ nwo zo bɔkɔɔ. (Edw. 40:4, 5) Eza saa yɛyɛ gyima mɔɔ ba aze la wɔ Gyihova ɛzonlenlɛ nu a, yɛbahola yɛazukoa Gebɛyɔnoma ne. (Dwɔh. 9:23, 27) Adiema kpavolɛ bie mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Luke la arɛle ɛnrɛvi mekɛ bie mɔɔ ɔnwunle kɛ adiema nrenyia kpanyinli bie mɔɔ wɔ Bɛtɛle la ɛlɛyɛ gyima mɔɔ ba aze la ɛlɛ. Ɛnee adiema kpanyinli ne lɛ ɛzonlelilɛ kpole wɔ Bɛtɛle ɛkɛ ne ɛdeɛ, noko mɔɔ bɛlesi Belemgbunlililɛ Asalo bie wɔ bɛ ɛkɛ ne la, ɔdule ɔ nwo ɔmaanle ɔ nee mediema ne mɔ hɔzinzale nɔe ne. Luke hanle kɛ: “Menwunle kɛ saa yɛka kɛ yɛbɛlɛ yɛ nwo aze a, ɛnee yɛtɛwiele. Ninyɛne mɔɔ yɛyɛ la a bamaa yeala ali kɛ amgba yɛbɛlɛ yɛ nwo aze a.” Saa yɛlɛyia ngyegyelɛ biala a, bɛmaa yɛva yɛ nwo yɛdo Gyihova anwo zo dahuu yɛmaa ɔboa yɛ na eza yɛva mɛlɛbɛnwoaze yɛyɛ gyima biala mɔɔ bɛbava bɛamaa yɛ wɔ Gyihova ɛzonlenlɛ nu la yɛva yɛhile kɛ yɛlɛ ye nu diedi.

8 Baebolo ne ka mɔɔ dole Gebɛyɔnoma wɔ Belemgbunli Sɔɔlo mekɛ zo la anwo edwɛkɛ. Ɔdule ɔ nye ɔziele Gebɛyɔnoma mɔɔ ɛnee de Yizilayɛ la anwo na ɔbɔle mɔdenle kɛ ɔbɔ bɛ bo zo “ɔlua Yizilayɛma nee Dwudama anwo ɛhulolɛ ti.”* Ɛhye ati, bɛhunle bɛ nuhua dɔɔnwo. (2 Sa. 21:2, 5, 6) Sɔɔlo nee bɛ anli ye boɛ fee! Yeanli anzodwolɛ ngyekyeleɛ mɔɔ Yizilayɛma nee Gebɛyɔnoma yɛle la azo.
<<<P>>>
9 Gyihova anli Gebɛyɔnoma ne mɔɔ bɛsisile bɛ la anwo gyima ɛkɛ ne ala. Mɔɔ Belemgbunli Devidi ralile Sɔɔlo agya la, yɛɛ akee Gyihova lile nwolɛ gyima a. Ɔmaanle ɛhɔne rale Yizilayɛ maanle ne anu too ɛvolɛ nsa. Ɔti Devidi hɔbizale Gyihova ɛhɔne ne anwo edwɛkɛ na Gyihova maanle bɛnwunle ngyegyelɛ ne.
