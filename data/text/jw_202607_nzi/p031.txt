Siezie Ngyegyelɛ Ne Ndɛndɛ!
<<<P>>>
ASOO adiema bie ɛyɛ debie yemaa yegyegye wɔ kpole na yemaa bɛ avinli ɛzɛkye kɛkala ɔ? Amaa wɔahola wɔaziezie bɛ avinli la, ɔwɔ kɛ (1) ɛdi nwolɛ gyima ndɛndɛ yɛɛ (2) ɛfa ɛyɛ wɔ adwenle kɛ bɛbayɛ agɔnwolɛ bieko.*
<<<P>>>
Maa yɛyɛ ndonwo bie. Saa awie bowule bu a, ɔwɔ kɛ bɛyɛ ye ayile amaa yeanyɛ ahenle nyane. Noko saa ahenle ammaa ɔ nye angɔ nwo ndɛndɛ a, ɔbahɔ zo yeayɛ ye nyane. Mɔɔ ɛnle kpalɛ bɔbɔ la a le kɛ ɔbahola yeamaa ye bowule ne ahyea na ɔnrɛhola ɔnrɛva ɔnrɛyɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ ɛnee dɛba ne ɔyɛ la.
<<<P>>>
Kɛ mɔɔ bowule bie bu a bɛyɛ ye ayile ndɛndɛ la, zɔhane ala a saa ɛ nee ɛ diema Kilisienenli bie avinli sɛkye a, ɔwɔ kɛ ɛsiezie ye ndɛndɛ a. Saa bɛanyɛ zɔ a, bɛ agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne mɔɔ ɛlɛsɛkye la bamaa wɔahɔ zo wɔali nyane. Ɔbamaa wɔava ɛya na akee bɛ avinli atete bɔkɔɔ. Gyisɛse hanle kɛ: “Ɛ nee ye ɛziezie edwɛkɛ ne ndɛndɛ.” (Mat. 5:25) Ɔti ɛ nee ɛ gɔnwo ne ɛdendɛ kɛnlɛma noko ka debie mɔɔ ɛlɛmaa wɔali nyane la kile ye. Duzu ati a ɔyɛ a ɛhye ɛyɛlɛ yɛ se a? Nea ninyɛne nwiɔ bie.
<<<P>>>
Mɔɔ limoa, wɔannea a ɛbabɔ mɔdenle kɛ ɛbu ɛ nye wɔagua tɛnlabelɛ ne azo ɔluakɛ bie a ɛ nye ɛnlie nwo kɛ ɛbaha nwolɛ edwɛkɛ.# Fa mediema
<<<P>>>
* Ɔwɔ kɛ yɛziezie edwɛkɛ ngyikyi mɔɔ bara yɛ avinli la ndɛndɛ mɔɔ yɛmmaa awie biala ɛnde nwolɛ edwɛkɛ na yɛfa ɛtane yɛkyɛ bɔkɔɔ. (Mat. 5:23, 24) Noko saa edwɛkɛ ne le kpole a, bie a ɔwɔ kɛ yɛmaa mgbanyima ne mɔ boa yɛ maa yɛdi nwolɛ gyima. (Mat. 18:17) Noko ɔwɔ kɛ Kilisienenli bie fa adehilelɛ mɔɔ wɔ Mateyu 18:15, 16 la dimoa di gyima kolaa na yeaha yeahile mgbanyima ne mɔ.
<<<P>>>
# Saa yɛ diema bie ɛvo yɛ noko yɛ avinli anzɛkye a, yɛbahola yɛa-bu yɛ nye yɛagua nvonleɛ ne azo.—Mrɛ. 26:20.
<<<P>>>
mraalɛ nwiɔ, Yiwodea nee Senteki mɔɔ ɛnee ɔyɛ se kɛ bɛbaziezie bɛ avinli la kɛ neazo. Ɔbayɛ kɛ bɛ ngyegyelɛ ne hyɛle ɔluakɛ ɛzoanvolɛ Pɔɔlo mɔɔ wɔ Wulomu nehane bɔbɔ la dele. Ɔmaanle bɛ anwo-sesebɛ kɛ bɛyɛ biala mɔɔ ɔhyia kɛ bɛyɛ la bɛziezie bɛ avinli. (Fel. 4:2) Saa bɛbɛlɛ bɛ nwo aze, bɛfi bɛ ahonle nu bɛka bɛ edwɛkɛ na bɛfa ɛtane bɛkyɛ bɔkɔɔ a, bɛbayɛ agɔnwolɛma kpalɛ bieko.
<<<P>>>
Mɔɔ tɔ zo nwiɔ, bie a ɛ nye la kɛ ahenle balie ye nvonleɛ ado nu. Noko ɛkulo kɛ ɛ sa ka ɛ gɔnwo ne bieko, tɛ kɛ ɛbɔ ye somolɛ. Saa dɔketa bie ɛlɛ-yɛ bowule mɔɔ ɛbu la ayile a, ɔfa ye adwenle ɔsie kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na yeahɔ la azo, tɛ debie biala mɔɔ ahenle yɛle mɔɔ maanle ye bowule ne bule la azo ɔ. Ɔti saa ɛ nee ɛ gɔnwo ne ɛlɛtendɛ a, mmakpo-ndɛ ahenle mɔɔ nvonleɛ ne vi ye la. Emomu kakye kɛzi bɛ agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne hyia wɔ na fa wɔ adwenle sie mɔɔ ɔhyia kɛ ɛyɛ ɛsiezie bɛ avinli la azo.
<<<P>>>
Ɔti ɛ nee ɛ diema ne ɛhɔdendɛ ɛne, noko fa yɛ wɔ bodane kɛ ɛ nee ye baziezie bɛ avinli na bɛayɛ agɔnwolɛma bieko. Baebolo ne ka kɛ “bɛmmamaa sɛnzɛne ne tɔ bɛ ɛyavalɛ zo.” (Ɛfɛ. 4:26) Saa ɛyɛ biala mɔɔ ɛbahola la ɛziezie ngyegyelɛ ne ndɛndɛ a ɛbaboa wɔamaa yɛabɔ ‘anzodwolɛ mɔɔ ka yɛ bɔ nu la’ anwo bane.—Ɛfɛ. 4:1-3.
