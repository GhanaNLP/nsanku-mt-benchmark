MENLI MƆƆ BƐNWU GYIHOVA KPALƐ LA NYIA NYILALƐ
<<<P>>>
17 Saa yɛ nee yɛ sukoavoma ɛlɛsukoa Baebolo ne a, ɔmaa bɛsukoa ninyɛne dɔɔnwo mɔɔ anwo hyia la. Ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ bie a le mɔlebɛbo ngilehilelɛ mɔɔ fale tɛnlabelɛ mɔɔ mowuamra wɔ nu, ɛkpɔnedeɛ ne nee Belemgbunlililɛ ne anwo la. Eza bɛsukoa yɛ ahyehyɛdeɛ ne anwo ninyɛne. Bie a le kɛzi yɛyɛ yɛ debiezukoalɛ ne mɔ, kɛzi asafo nu mgbanyima yɛ bɛ gyima ne nee gyima mɔɔ Neazo Eku ne yɛ la. Noko akee, tɛ nɔhalɛ edwɛkɛ ɛhye mɔ angomekye a yɛkulo kɛ yɛ sukoavoma sukoa a. Yɛkulo kɛ bɛnwu Gyihova kpalɛ.
<<<P>>>
18 Yɛnyia nwolɛ adenle kpole kɛ yɛlɛkilehile awie mɔ yɛamaa bɛanwu Gyihova la! Ɛnɛ bɔbɔ, menli mɔɔ bɛnwu Gyihova na bɛlɛsonle ye la anye die kpole kpalɛ wɔ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nu. (Edw. 25:12-15) Kenle bie, menli mɔɔ ‘bɛbanyia Gyihova mɔɔ le nɔhavo ne la anwo ndelebɛbo’ la banyia dahuu ngoane. (1 Dwɔn 5:20) Noko bɛbanyia nyilalɛ gyɛne bɛaboka nwo. Baebolo ne ka kɛ menli mɔɔ bɛbɔ mɔdenle bɛnwu Gyihova na bɛkulo ye la, Gyihova ‘noko ze bɛ.’ (1 Kɔl. 8:3) Amgba, Nyamenle Tumivolɛ Bedevinli ne nwu yɛ yɛɛ ɔdie yɛ ɔto nu kɛ yɛle ɔ gɔnwo mɔ. Ɛhye le nyilalɛ kpole mɔɔ saa yɛnwu nɔhalɛ Nyamenle kokye ne a yɛbanyia a!
