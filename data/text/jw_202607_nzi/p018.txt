Ɔhanle kɛ: “Mebɔ mɔdenle kɛ mebagyinla me abe-nlana ne anu meanlea ninyɛne mɔɔ Gyiho-va ɛbɔ la. Meta menwu ngɛkɛba ngyikyi anzɛɛ nloma ngyikyi mɔɔ bɛ nye ɛbolo bɛ-lɛkpondɛ aleɛ anzɛɛ dabelɛ la. Kenle ko bie menwunle kɛ nloma bie ɛlɛnwo bɛ sane. Bɛsesale ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛnyianle ye wɔ ɛkɛ ne la bie a bɛyɛle bɛ sane ne a, amaa bɛado bɛ aholonvia bɛagua nu na bɛabobɔ bɛ mra ne mɔ wɔ nu.” Noko tɛ mɔɔ Patrick nwunle ye ala la ɛne. Ɔmaanle ɔnwunle Bɔvolɛ ne kpalɛ. Ɔhanle kɛ: “Menwunle kɛ Gyihova dwenle ye abɔdeɛ boɛyɛlɛ nwo, nuhua ne mɔɔ ɔzɔho kɛ bɛ nwo ɛngyia la bɔbɔ boka nwo. Ɛhye maanle menyianle anwodozo kɛ ɔbanlea medame noko na yeamaa me sa aha mɔɔ mehyia nwo la.”

16 Adenle mɔɔ tɔ zo nsa mɔɔ yɛbaho-la yɛalua zo yɛanwu Gyihova kpalɛ la a le kɛ yɛbadwenledwenle mɔɔ Gyihova ɛyɛ ɛmaa yɛdayɛ mumua ne la anwo. Zɔhane a Gyisɛse mame Mɛle yɛle a. Ɛnee ɔze Gyi-hova kpalɛ dɛba, yɛɛ ɛnee ‘yenyia Nya-menle anyunlu ɛlolɛ.’ (Luku 1:30) Noko bɛmaanle ye gyimalilɛ kpole bie, yemɔ a le kɛ ɔbawo Nyamenle Ara ne.
