Sɔɔlo nye borole Dawide, na wamva yenwo wando Yehowa so. Nakoso Yonatan de, wanyɛ sɔ
(Nea ngyekyɛmuɛ 4, 6)
<<<P>>>
mamɔ nwo dwirɛ, yenya bɛnwo adwenemmɔne, anaa yedwudwo yetia asafo ne po.—Rom. 16:17.
<<<P>>>
4 Emɔma yɛnea Dawide ne Sɔɔlo dwirɛ ne. Mmerɛ bɔ ne Dawide la Sɔɔlo sogyafoɛ nemɔ noa ne, ne Sɔɔlo kuro ye dwirɛ paa, na ne onni yenwo angorɛ. Ofikyɛ ngondii bɔ ɔhone ne, olili ngunim. (1 Sam. 18:5) Nakoso siɛ ye, Sɔɔlo nye ɔɔborole Dawide. Nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ ne mmeni pee bɔ Dawide aba so tra ye. Ɛhe ati, Sɔɔlo tuli yenye siele Dawide so; ɔyɛle dee kyɛ okohũ ye mmerɛ bɔ ne ɔyɛlle ye hwee ɔ. (1 Sam. 18:9-11) Sɛ Sɔɔlo hora huni Dawide a, ahaa ɔkɔyɛ Satan fɛ paa. Nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ ne Yehowa amaa bapopa Dawide. Yeti sɛ Sɔɔlo nne berɛ a, ye yeɛ ne ɔkɔyɛ Yisrael hene ɔ. (1 Sam. 16:13) Sɛ Dawide amma anyɛ ɔhene a, ahaa Satan ayie koraa. Ɛhene ati mmerɛ bɔ ne Sɔɔlo nye aboro kyɛ okohũ Dawide ne, ɔkɔyɛ kyɛ ɔmaa Satan nye gyele paa.
<<<P>>>
4. Nzu yeɛ ɔmaa Sɔɔlo tuli yenye siele Dawide so ɔ? (Nea foto ne koso.)
<<<P>>>
5 Satan se kyɛ, sɛ yɛne yeliemamɔ afĩa te kama a, ɔmaa yɛyɛ kõ na Yehowa koso yira yɛ. (Edw. 133:1) Ɛhene ati okuro kyɛ ɔkekã yɛti bɔ nu maa yɛkõ yetia yɛnwo. Ɔpena kyɛ yɛnye boro yeliemamɔ kyɛbɔ ne Sɔɔlo nye boro Dawide ne. Sɛ ɔba sɔ a, ɔkɔyɛ se paa kyɛ yekohuro bɛ dwirɛ. (Mak. 12:30, 31) Afei ɔkɔmaa yɛ soen kɔyɛ mbaen wɔ Yehowa nye so. Nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ Yakobo maa yenwu kyɛ, sɛ yɛmaa yenye boro yeliemamɔ a, ɔyɛ suro paa.—Kenga Yakobo 3:14-16.
<<<P>>>
6 Sɛ yekuro yeliemamɔ dwirɛ paa a, ɔngɔma yɛngɔyɛ ahoɔyea, anaa yenye ngɔboro bɛ wɔ y'ahone nu po. Sɔ ala yeɛ Sɔɔlo awa Yonatan yɛle ɔ. Wamma yenye ammoro Dawide tekyɛ ye baba, na mmom ne okuro ye dwirɛ paa, yeti ɔne ye fale adamvoa. Afei koso ne ole gyidie kyɛ, bɔ Yehowa ahã afa Dawide nwo biala
<<<P>>>
5. Nzuati yeɛ Satan pena kyɛ yɛmaa yɛnye boro yeliemamɔ ɔ? (Yakobo 3:14-16)
<<<P>>>
6. Bɔ Yonatan yɛle ne, nzu yeɛ esua fi nu ɔ? (Nea foto ne koso.)
