14 Yesu wale ne, ye koso ɔhane nzɛmba ne bie. Ɔmaa edwirɛ bɔ Sakaria hane ne, ɔwale nu. Yeɛ ole kyɛ ‘bɛ bɔ bɛtete awosii nu ɔne ewue wawoɛ nu ne, ɔkɔta kɔma bɛ.’ Bɛbɔ ne mmenia mva bɛ nyɛ hwee ne, né bɛnwo yɛ Yesu anwumvo. Yeti ne okuro paa kyɛ ɔboka bɛ. Ɔhane nzɛmba ne hyirele bɛ, na ɛhene maa benyane anyelasoɛ. Yeti ne waayɛ kyɛ kanea bie ata bɛ so ne. (Kenga Yesaya 61:1, 2; Luka 4:16-21) Ne Yesu hia mmeni pee maa bɛka nzɛmba ne, ofikyɛ daa na ɔla yenoa kyɛ, “Nyameeso ahennie ne apingye!” Yeti ɔtetele ye asuafoɛ nemɔ kyɛ bɛ wora bɛɛha nzɛmba ne bie.—Mat. 4:16, 17; 10:7; 28:19, 20.

15 Yɛ koso adwuma bɔ Yesu yɛle ne, ɔyɛ yɛ fɛ paa kyɛ yɛyɛ bie ɔ. Ofikyɛ yɛse kyɛ okole mmenia ngoa. Kesaalae adwuma bɔ yenwo hia tra adwuma biala wɔ wiase he anu ne, yeɛ ole kyɛ yɛkɔmaa mmenia konwu Nyameɛ Ahennie ne, na yamaa banwu kyɛ Yesu awu ama yɛ. Nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ ‘amaneɛnwunɛ piri ne ɔbaawa, na ofi mmerɛ bɔ Ɛdangama bɔle wiase bɔ ɔbaadwu ɛnɛ ɔ, ye sɛso ɔwwale le.’ (Mat. 24:21) Sɔ mmerɛ ne, ebiaa mmeni pee kɔkae edwirɛ bɔ ne yɛka yekyire bɛ ne.
