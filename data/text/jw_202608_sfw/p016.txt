Bɔ mmɔden ka aliemaamɔ nwo dwirɛ pá, kyɛbɔ Pɔɔlo yɛle ne
(Nea ngyekyɛmuɛ 8)
<<<P>>>
Yehowa ase kyɛ bele subaen pa ɔ. (1 Kor. 1:4-7; Flp. 1:3-5; Kol. 1:3, 4; 1 Tɛs. 1:2, 3) Ɔwɔ nu, ne Pɔɔlo nwu mvomsoɛ bɔ ɔwɔ aliemaamɔ nwo ne, nakoso nna ɛhene yeɛ ne ɔnea ɔ. Na mmom subaen pa bɔ ɔwɔ bɛnwo ne, ɛhene yeɛ ne ohia ye ɔ. Yeti yeɛ ne daabiala ɔda Yehowa ase wɔ bɛnwo ne. Wɔ koso, sɛ ɔyɛ se ma wɔ kyɛ ɛkɔha alie-maamɔ nwo dwirɛ pa a, bɔ mmɔden kyɛ ekosuesua Pɔɔlo. Subaen pa bɔ ɔwɔ bɛ-nwo ne, fa w’adwene sie so. Afei ɛyɛ a bɔ mbaeɛ ma bɛ, na ɛhene kɔboka wɔ maa ɛkɔha bɛnwo dwirɛ pa.
<<<P>>>
9 Sɛ yɛfa ɔhaw nu a, ɛyɛ a ne yɛnye so ata. Na ebie yɛlle yɛ hwee po a, ne ye-nwo yɛ y’ahi. Yeti yannea yé a, yengonya mmenia nwo aboterɛ. Bɔ yɛka ye, ebiaa ebie ato wɔ le. Pɔɔlo koso, ne ɔfa ɔhaw nu, na ne ɔtte angorɛ koraa, koso ɔne File-mɔn dwudwole yé. Ɔhaw beni yeɛ ne ɔfa nu ɔ? Yeɛ ole kyɛ, ebiemɔ bole ye, na be-yiele ne, bɛhyele ye bɛtole nu, koso ne ɔyɛlle bɛ hwee. Sɔ dwirɛ ne sili ne, wan-gyɛ yeɛ ɔhworɔle krataa ɔɔmane Filemɔn ɔ. Ne balimoa bahye ye bato nu wɔ Yeru-salem ne Kaesarea. Afei bɛhane kyɛ bɛfa ye bɛhɔ Rome maa beeli ye dwirɛ wɔ dɔ.
<<<P>>>
Bɛkɔ ne, ɛlɛen bɔ na ɔte nu ne, obutuli. Pɔɔlo dwuli ne, bɛfale ye bɛtole nu wɔ ye bɔbɔ ye awuro. Yeti yɛkɔha a, ne ‘ɔla pri-zin.’ (Flm. 1) Sɔ mmerɛ ne yeɛ ɔhworɔle krataa ɔɔmane Filemɔn ne. Pɔɔlo de, ne wamia pá, nakoso wandwudwo wandia Filemɔn wɔ ye krataa nanu. Bɔ Pɔɔlo yɛle ne, nzu yeɛ yesua yefi nu ɔ? Yaayɛ sona ye de, odwu mmerɛ bie a yɛkɔfa ɔhaw nu, anaa edwirɛ bie kɔhyɛ yɛso. Ɔba ye sɔ a, ɛmɔma yemia yɛnye kyɛ yɛne ebiala ko-dwudwo kama. Yɛyɛ sɔ a, ɔkɔmaa asom-dwee kɔtena yɛne aliemaamɔ nu. Na sɛ asomdwee wɔ yɛne yeliemamɔ nu a, ɔkɔ-boka yɛ maa yekogyina yɛ haw ne noa.

10 Yɛte sona nati, ɔyɛ sɛɛ alaa a, yɛkɔyɛ nikyee maa ɔkɔyɛ aliemaamɔ yea. Nako-so ɔba sɔ a, nzu yeɛ odikyɛ yɛyɛ ɔ? One-simo yɛle nikyeebie bɔ yɛkɔhora yeko-suesua ɔ. Yeɛ ole kyɛ, olɛle ye mvomsoɛ tole nu, na ɔne Filemɔn siesiele bɛ afia. Bɔ yɛka ye ɔlla ase, koso yɛmia yɛnye yɛyɛ a, ɔkɔmaa asomdwee kɔwa yɛne yeliemamɔ afia.
