BƆ ADESŨA NE KƆHÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Gyidie bɔ yele ye wɔ Yehowa ne Yesu nu ne, Satan pena kyɛ ɔkɔ ase. Nakoso nnɛmaa ɔlakalaka wɔ.
<<<P>>>
“Émɔyere emɔ nwo bési abɔnzam ate, na ɔkɔnwati kofi emɔ nwo.”—YAK. 4:7.
<<<P>>>
SƐ YENYA yesi gyinayɛɛ kyɛ yɛkɔsõ Yehowa a, ne Satan ne ye sunzummɔne nemɔ afɛ yaa paa. Yeti ɛyɛ a ne batu bɛnye basie yɛ so. Nakoso Satan se kyɛ, sɛ Yehowa boka yɛ so a, yɛngɔyakyi Yehowa soen lle. Yeti ɔfa atee foforɛ so. Ebie yeɛ ole kyɛ, ɔmaa yenya adwene kyɛ yɛ dwirɛ mva Yehowa ne Yesu nu.—2 Kor. 11:14; Yak. 1:6.
<<<P>>>
2 Sɛ sogyafoɛ koli ngunim wɔ kɔ̃ɛ nu a, sana bɛbɔ bɛla bɛ noa ne, benya benu gyidie na betie bɛ. Baanyɛ sɔ, na bɛyɛ bɔ bekuro a, ebiaa bɛkɔhwere bɛ ngoa. Yɛ koso, yɛte kyɛ sogyafoɛ bɔ bɛkõ ne, ofikyɛ yɛ ne Satan ne ye “sunzummɔne mɔ” yeɛ yɛkõ ɔ. (Efe. 6:12) Ɔse kyɛ gyidie bɔ yele ye wɔ Yehowa ne Yesu nu ne, ɔkɔ ase a, yɛngotie bɛ kõ. Yeti ɔyɛ bɔ ɔkɔhora biala maa yɛ gyidie kɔ ase. Bɔ Satan yɛ ne, nna ɛnnɛ yeɛ ɔbaahyɛ yebo ɔ. Wɔ adesua he anu ne, yɛkɔnea atee bɔ ɔfale so yɛle sɔ tete ne, ɔne atee bɔ ɔfa so yɛ sɔ ɛnnɛ ɔ. Ehene si ne, yɛkɔnea bɔ yɛkɔyɛ na Satan ngaen angye yɛ. Adesua bɔ oli berɛ nanu koso, kyɛbɔ ɔbɔ mmɔden kyɛ ɔkɔmaa yɛkɔyakyi Yehowa soen ne, yɛkɔnea nu nyɔ yɛkɔboka so.*

* Ɔhaw bɔ yɛfa nu ne, nna yemu yeɛ Satan fa ba yɛ so tee ɔ. Nakoso ɔkɔhora kopiepia mmenia bɔ benguro yɛ dwirɛ ne maa bɛkɔtane yɛnye. Ofikyɛ ye yeɛ “ewiase wa sunzum” ne wura ye sa ɔ.
