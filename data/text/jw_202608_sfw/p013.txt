15 Wiase he anu de, ɔhaw ne asisie ngo- me. Yeti sɛ yɛnwa yɛkɔyɛ maa nikyee bia- la kɔhɔ yé a, yede ne yɛfɛ mbaen. (Nwo. 1:15) Bɔ yɛfa nu namu Yehowa se, yeti ka w’ahone to wɔ kunu. (Edw. 37:18) Ye- mia ɔngɔhyɛ ye ahennie ne baawa. Na ɔba a, bɔ Abɔnzam ne ye mmenia nemɔ asɛkye ye ne, ɔkɔyɛ ye yé. (1 Yoh. 3:8; Nye. 21:5) Sɔ mmerɛ he bɔ ahennie ne walle ne, ɛmɔma yenya aboterɛ na yenya ɔdɔ yɛma mmenia. Ɛmɔma yɛyere yɛnwo yɛyɛ asɛnga adwuma ne, na yɛboka mme- nia maa bɛɛyɛ Yehowa damvo.
<<<P>>>
YƐKƆHORA YEKOLI BƐ SO NGUNIM!
<<<P>>>
16 Ngondii bɔ yɛne Satan ne ye sunzum- mɔne mɔ kɔ̃ ne, Bible ne maa yenwu kyɛ yɛkɔhora yekoli bɛ so ngunim. (Yak. 4:7; 1 Yoh. 5:4, 5) Ɔkɔyɛ sɛɛ na wayɛ yé? Sana yenya Yehowa ne Yesu nu gyidie. (Ɛfe. 6: 16) Yeti gyidie bɔ ele ye wɔ bɛ nu ne, ma nu yɛ se ate. Ɔba sɔ a, daa biala ekonya ɔdɔ kɔma aliemaamɔ, na ɛne bayɛ kõ.
<<<P>>>
17 Satan fa atee biala so kyɛ, okobu yɛ abanu maa yɛkɔyakyi asɛnga adwuma ne. Afei onyane a ahaa yɛne yeliemamɔ afia asɛkye. Nakoso Yehowa ne Yesu nwa, sɛ yɛfa bɛ adamvoa a, bɛkɔboka yɛ maa yɛkɔho yekotia Satan. Yeliemamɔ boka yɛso; yɛne bɛ ala yɛɛ yɛkɔ̃ yetia Abɔnzam ɔ. Afei bɛbɔ bɛtane yɛ ne, odikyɛ yɛbɔ mmɔden yɛfa Yesu dwirɛ ne yɛyɛ adwu- ma. Yenwa, yehuro yɛ pɔfoɛ mɔ dwirɛ. (Luka 6:27, 28) Mia wɔnye na ɛne Satan bɛfa besi yenye, nnɛpa aba. Ɛyɛ sɔ a, eko- li Satan ne ye sunzummɔne mɔ so ngu- nim.

15. Ɔhaw ne asisie bɔ ɔkɔso ne, nzuati yeɛ ɔnzɛkyɛ yenya adwene kyɛ yɛ yeɛ yɛbaasiesie ɔ?
<<<P>>>
16. Ngondii bɔ yɛne Satan ne ye sunzummɔne mɔ kɔ̃ ne, yɛkɔyɛ sɛɛ na yali bɛ so ngunim? (Nea foto ne koso.)
<<<P>>>
17. Nzu yeɛ ɛpena kyɛ ɛyɛ ɔ?

<<<P>>>
