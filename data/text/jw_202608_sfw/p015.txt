4 Atee bɔ Pɔɔlo fale so ne aliemaamɔ dwudwole ne, ɔmaa asomdwee wale asafo nanu. Yɛ koso sɛ yɛsuesua bɔ ɔyɛle ne a, yɛne aliemaamɔ kɔtena asomdwee nu.
<<<P>>>
5 Pɔɔlo anhyɛ Filemɔn kyɛ ɔyɛ nikyeebie. (2 Kor. 1:24) Na mmom ne onwu ye kyɛ ɔte ye damvo bɔ ɔne ye yɛ adwuma ɔ, yeti ɔne ye dwudwole kama. (Flm. 17) Bɔ Pɔɔlo yɛle ne, né ɔkɔmaa Filemɔn konwu kyɛbɔ ɔne Onesimo kɔtena ɔ. Ofikyɛ ne waayɛ oliema Kristoniɛ. Yɛ mukoraati yɛkɔhora yɛkosuesua Pɔɔlo, nganga asafo nu mbanyi. Sɛ benwu ye kyɛ aliemaamɔ te bɛ nnamvo a, ‘bɛngɔhyɛ bɛ so.’ (1 Pet. 5:1-3) Na mmom bekobu bɛ, na banya bɛnwo alagye. Afei sɛ behia mmokalɛ bie a, bɛngɔfa ngɔbɔ b’atianuserɛ. Sɛ bɛyɛ sɔ a, ɔkɔmaa asomdwee kɔtena asafo nanu, na aliemaamɔ wɔra, bekosuesua bɛ.
<<<P>>>
6 Pɔɔlo kamvole Filemɔn. (Flm. 4, 5, 7) Bɛkamvo yɛ a, ɔmaa yɛnye gye. Nzuati ɔ? Yɛnu pee wɔ berɛ a, y’adwene yɛ yɛ kyɛ yɛnwo nhia. Ofikyɛ ebiaa yesusu kyɛ yɛnwo nyɛ nyemene, anaa mmenia nguro yɛ dwirɛ. Yɛnu biemɔ koso, dwumadie bɔ owura yɛsa wɔ asafo nanu ne, yesuro kyɛ ebiaa ne basesa ye. Yaayɛ sona ye de, yɛkɔhora yɛkonya sɔ adwene ne. Nakoso yannea yé a, ɔkɔmaa yɛkɔyɛ nningyein biemɔ bɔ ɔkɔsɛkye yɛne aliemaamɔ afia. Ebie yeɛ ole kyɛ, yɛkonya adwene kyɛ bɛkeka yɛnwo dwirɛ, yeti yɛkɔfɛ bɛ so yaa. Anaa yɛkɔfa yɛnwo yɛkɔtoto bɛ. Yeti sɛ yeliemamɔ yɛ nikyeebie na yɛkamvo bɛ a, ɔmaa bɛnye gye, na benwu kyɛ sãa bɛnwo hia paa. (Any. 12:25) Sɛ yɛpena kyɛ yɛmaa aliemaamɔ nwu kyɛ bɛnwo hia a, yede bɛyɛ nikyeebie na ɔkɔ yé a, yɛma benwu ye. Afei mmɔden bɔ bɛbɔle kora yeɛ bɛyɛ sɔ nikyee ne, kamvo bɛ wɔ yɛnwo. Ye tikaape ne, sɛ aliemaa bie yɛ nikye, na ɔyɛ wɔ nyemene a, ka kyire ye.
<<<P>>>
7 Pɔɔlo hane Onesimo nwo dwirɛ pa. (Kenga Filemɔn 10-12.) Sɔ bɔ Pɔɔlo yɛle ne, ɛnea a ɔbokale Filemɔn anaa? Yoo, ɔmaa onyane Onesimo nwo adwene pa. Yeti sɛ ne wafɛ ye so yaa po a, ne ɔkɔmaa ye ahone kɔwa ase. Sɔ ala yeɛ yɛ koso, bɔ yɛkɔha yɛkɔfa yeliema bie nwo ne, ɔkɔmaa aliemaamɔ kohuro ye dwirɛ anaa bengɔhuro ye dwirɛ. Yeti sɛ yɛka yeliema nanwo dwirɛ pa a, ɔkɔmaa bekohuro ye dwirɛ, na bɛne ye kɔtena asomdwee nu. Nakoso sɛ yɛkɔha yeliemamɔ nwo dwirɛ pa a, sana yenya bɛnwo adwene pa. Nzu yeɛ ɔkɔboka yɛ ɔ? Ɛmɔma yɛnea nikyeebie bɔ ne Pɔɔlo yɛ ɔ, na yɛsua nikyee yefi nu.
