9 Aliemaa biemɔ wɔ berɛ a, bele adwene kyɛ bɔ bɛyɛ ye wɔ Yehowa som nu biala, ɔte mbaen. Nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ bɛse kyɛ bɛ de, bɔ wato bɛ nati, Yehowa fa bɛ yɛ hwee a ɔnyɛ yé, na ɔndwene bɛnwo po. Ebiemɔ koso, bayɛ bɔne bie maa mbanyi nemɔ aboka bɛ, koso b’adwene té yɛ bɛ kyɛ Yehowa fa hyɛlle bɛ. Sɛ sɔ yeɛ wɔ adwene yɛ wɔ a, nzu yeɛ odikyɛ eyɛ ɔ?

10 Wɔ de ma yɛnea edwirɛ he. Yɛfa ye kyɛ ebie gya nu abu, yeɛ ɔfa hɔle hɔspitel. Dɔktaniɛ ne hwene ye gya ne siele so. Oyiele ne, yeɛ yenwa ɔhɔ nekaa bɔ bɛyɛ mmowue nwo adwuma ne maa bɛboka ye, kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a ɔkɔhora kɔnate ye gya ne aso bieku ɔ. Nakoso ebiaa sona ne de, onwu ye kyɛ ye gya ne, ɔngɔyɛ yé kɔ̃. Ɔba sɔ a, odikyɛ omia yenye na ɔkɔ maa bɛnea ye. Nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ dɔktaniɛ ne yeɛ ɔse bɔ ɔkɔboka ye ɔ. Ɔyɛ sɔ a, ngakangaka ye gya ne kɔyɛ yé. Afei okonwu ye ne, ne ɔfa tu amirika po.

11 Ɛhe fa ɛne Yehowa nwo sɛ? Ebiaa ayɛ bɔne bie, koso enwu ye kyɛ Yehowa fa hyɛlle wɔ, yeti ɔfa wɔ yɛ hwee a ɔnyɛ yé. Sɛ sɔ a, de di kyɛ Yesu mogya nati Yehowa afa ahyɛ wɔ, na ɔdɔ bɔ ole ma wɔ ne, nna ebie ɛhe. (Yoh. 1:16, 17; Rom. 3:23, 24; 1 Yoh. 3:19, 20) Afei bɔ ɔkɔyɛ na anwu kyɛ Yehowa afa ahyɛ wɔ ne, yenwa ɛyɛ nningyein biemɔ. Yɛɛ ole kyɛ ɛkɔbɔ mbaeɛ, ekosua Bible, na ahɔ adesua ne asɛnga. (Edw. 1:2; 1 Tɛs. 5:17; Heb. 10:25) Bɔ yɛka ye ɔtte ɛlɛhomapɛ dwirɛ. Nakoso sɛ daa eyɛ a, ngakangaka ekonwu kyɛ Yehowa dɔ wɔ, na wafa wɔ bɔne wahyɛ wɔ. Ɔwɔ nu alaa a, ebiaa sɔ adwene ne kɔwa bieku kyɛ Yehowa fa hyɛlle wɔ. Ɔba sɔ a, nnɛpa aba, na mmom kɔ so yɛ bɔ Yehowa nwa eyɛ ne. (Flp. 3:16) Wɔde nea oo, bɔ ɛfa nu amukoraati ne, Yehowa adaworoma, eguso ɛso ye. Yeti daabiala da ye ase. Ɔyɛ yɛ fɛ paa kyɛ Yesu wue nati, yɛso Yehowa bɔ yenzuro ɔ.

12 Kenga Luka 1:76-79. Yesa yɛhɔ Sakaria dwirɛ naso. Ɔha hyirele ɔwa ne kyɛ: “Ekoli Awurade nyunu moa ekosiesie ye atee nu.” Edwirɛ bɔ ɔhane ne, Yohane hora yɛle anaa? Yoo. Onyini ne, ye adwuma bɔ ne ɔyɛ alaa yeɛ ole kyɛ, ɔkɔha nzɛmba ne. Ohyirehyirele Yudafoɛ ne, na ɔbokale bɛ maa bɛsakrale, kyɛbɔ Mɛsaya ne ba a, bekonwu ye, na bali yesi ɔ. Siɛ ye, ɔbɔle Yesu asu. Afei ɔhahyirele Yudafoɛ ne kyɛ: “Ɛmɔnea, Nyameɛ Boaen Ba ne, bɔ ɔye wiase bɔne fi berɛ ne!” Sɔ dwirɛ bɔ ɔhane ne, ɔmaa benwuni kyɛ Nyameɛ yeɛ wasoma Yesu ɔ. Ne Yohane le ye asuafoɛ, nakoso ɔmaa beelili Yesu si.—Yoh. 1:29, 34-36, 40, 41.
