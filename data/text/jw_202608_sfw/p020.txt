Ɔyɛ Wɔ Fɛ Kyɛ ‘Ɛso Yehowa Bɔ Enzuro Hwee’ Anaa?
<<<P>>>
DWEIN 62
Dwom Foforo No
<<<P>>>
BƆ ADESŨA NE KƆHÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Yehowa adaworoma, ɔnate Yesu so ale yɛ. Wasa wama y’atee kyɛ yɛso ye. Afei wafa wawura yɛsa kyɛ yɛha nzɛmba ne. Bɔ Yehowa ayɛ ama yɛ ne, yekonwu bɔ yɛyɛ a, okyire kyɛ yɛnye sɔ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
‘Wama ngoalele aweɛn so wama yɛ, ná yaso ye bɔ esurole nne nu ɔ.’—LUKA 1:69, 74.
<<<P>>>
NE SAKARIA ne Elisabɛte anyi paa, na ne belle ba. Nakoso Yehowa maa Elisabɛte nwonzɛle, na ɔwole baa brienzua. Ye dumaa li Yohane. Bɛbɔ bɛtele kyɛ aworowaa he awo ne, bɛhane kyɛ: “Akwadaa he de, ɔkɔyɛ nzikyɛ sona ɛhe?” (Luka 1:57-66) Yehowa maa Sakaria mane bɛ mmuayɛɛ. Tie bɔ ɔhane ɔ: Okohyirehyire mmenia, na waboka bɛ maa bɛsakra, kyɛbɔ Mɛsaya ne ba a, bekonwu ye, na bali yesi ɔ. (Amo. 3:7; Luka 1:67-79) Nna bɔ akwalaa ne kɔyɛ ngome yeɛ ɔhane yenwo dwirɛ ɔ, na mmom osa hyɛle ngɔm.* Ɛhene de, ɔfa yemu yɛnwo, na yekonwu ye wɔ adesua he anu. Bɔ Sakaria hane ne, ɔmaa yenwu kyɛ sɔ nningyein nza he, odikyɛ yɛmaa yɛnye sɔ. (1) Yehowa nate Yesu so ale yɛ. (2) Yehowa ama y’atee kyɛ yɛso ye. Yeɛ (3) Wafa wawura yɛsa kyɛ yɛha nzɛmba ne.
<<<P>>>
* Sɛ ɛpena kyɛ etie Sakaria ngɔmhyɛ namu a, ekonwu ye wɔ Bɔ Ɔhɔleso Wɔ Yesu Asetena Nu Ɔ ye Vidio 1 nanu. Sɛ ebukye Luka 1:67 wɔ JW Library anaa jw.org so a, ekonwu sɔ vidio ne.
