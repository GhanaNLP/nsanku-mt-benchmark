Momma Yennya Ɔdɔ
<<<P>>>
BƆ ADESŨA NE KƆHÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Pɔɔlo, Filemɔn ne Onesimo yɛle nikyee bɔ okyire kyɛ bekuro asomdwee. Yɛbaa-nea bɔ bɛyɛle, ɔne kyɛbɔ yekosuesua bɛ, na wamaa yɛne aliemaamɔ atena asomdwee nu.
<<<P>>>
Fa Ɔdɔ Yɛ Wɔ Nikyee Biala
<<<P>>>
“Ɔnate ɔdɔ ti, mekuro kyɛ mesrɛ wɔ.” —FLM. 9.
<<<P>>>
YEHOWA adaworoma, asomdwee wɔ yɛne aliemaamɔ afia, na yenni yenwo angorɛ koraa. Ɛhe maa yenwu kyɛ wiase foforɛ nanu kɔyɛ fɛ paa. (Yes. 32:17, 18) Nakoso odikyɛ yɛnu biala yɛ y’afanu deɛ, na wamaa asomdwee ne ahɔso atena berɛ. (Rom. 12:18) Yede yɛkɔyɛ ye sɛ? Na sɛ yɛfo aliemaa bie, anaa aliemaa bie yɛ nikyee maa ɔyɛ yɛ yea a, nzu yee yɛkɔyɛ ɔ? Adesua he anu ne, yɛkɔnea bɔ Pɔɔlo, Filemɔn ne Onesimo yɛle ɔ, na ɔkɔmaa yekonya edwirɛ hemɔ nwo mmuayeɛ.
<<<P>>>
‘ƆDƆ TI, MESRƐ WƆ’
<<<P>>>
2 Ne Filemɔn wɔ Kolose asafo nanu, na bɔ ne bɛfa bɛse ye yee ole kyɛ, ye gyidie yɛ se, na ole ɔdɔ. Ne ole ye akoaa bie bɔ bɛfrɛ ye Onesimo. Ye de, ne ɔtte Kristoniɛ. Ɛlɛho bie, ɔnwati yakyili Filemɔn, na ɔhɔle Rome. Ebiaa ɔkɔ ne, owiale ye po. (Flm. 18) Odwuli dɔ ne, ɔfale yenwo bɔle Pɔɔlo, na siɛ ye ɔɔyɛle Kristoniɛ. (Kol. 4:9) Nakoso ne edwirɛ wɔ berɛ. Ne Romanfoɛ mmraa ne nwa sɛ ebie te akoaa na ɔnwati a, odikyɛ osa kɔ ye masta ne dɔ. Ne Pɔɔ-lo kuro kyɛ odi sɔ mmraa naso, yeti ɔmaa Onesimo sa hɔle Filemɔn berɛ. (Flm. 12, 13) Ɔkɔ ne, ɔhworɔle krataa mane ye kyɛ ɔfa ɔɔma ye masta. Krataa nanu ne, ɔhane kyɛ ɔpa ye kyerɛ, ɔfa yenye to ɔdɔ bɔ ole ma Yesu ne aliemaamɔ ne, na ole Onesimo bieku. (Kenga Filemɔn 8, 9, 17.) Bɔ yaha ye, ebiaa wamaa akae bɛ dwirɛ ne.
<<<P>>>
3 Sɛ ne Onesimo kodwu a, nzu yee ne Filemɔn kɔyɛ ɔ? Aso ne okole ye, anaa ne ɔkɔfa ye kɔfa mmraa atee so? Ɔwɔ nu, ne ole yenwo atee kyɛ ɔfa ye fa mmraa atee so, na-koso sɛ ne ɔyɛ sɔ a, nzu yee ne aliemaamɔ kɔha ɔ? Alie-maamɔ bɔ yee bele ngoaa ne, ebiaa ne bɛkɔha kyɛ Filemɔn ma bɛhye ye, na bɛhwe ye so. Nakoso ebiaa bɛbɔ bɛte
<<<P>>>
1. Nzu yee odikyɛ yɛnu biala yɛ ɔ?
<<<P>>>
2. Nzu yee Onesimo fa yɛle Filemɔn ɔ? (Filemɔn 8, 9, 17)
<<<P>>>
3. Sɛ ne Onesimo kodwu a, nzu yee ne Pɔɔlo dwene kyɛ ɔkɔhora kosi ɔ, na nzu yee ɔyɛle ɔ? (Nea foto bɔ ɔwɔ Ɛwɛnɛlɛ Waen ne nyunu ne koso.)
