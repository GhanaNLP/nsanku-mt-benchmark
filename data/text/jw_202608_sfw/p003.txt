4 Satan lakalakale Adam ne Hawa yiele ne, ahaa ɔhã ye noa tonu po e. Ɔhɔle so hane Yehowa nwo dwirɛ bɔ ɔtte nahorɛ. Yob nnamvo nza ne sɔa, ɔmaa bɛhane Yehowa nwo dwirɛ bɔ ɔtte nahorɛ ɔ. Yeti bɔ bɛhane ne, ɔmaa Yob nyane adwene kyɛ Yehowa ndwene yenwo. Afei kyɛbɔ wafi ye ahone nu mukoraati ɔsõ Yehowa ne, yenye nzɔ. (Yob 22:1-3) Abɔnzam maa ebie fale Yob nye so pa. Ne ɔse kyɛ ɛhene kɔmaa Yob kɔyakyi Yehowa som. Yob de, olili amia wɔ Abɔnzam sa nu paa. Nakoso ɔhɔle so lili nahorɛ, na wanye ye damvo Yehowa wamma lle.—Yob 27:1, 5.

5 Yesu wale aseɛ ye aso ne, ne Abɔnzam nguro kyɛ mmenia koli ye si. Yeti osa kekane yenwo bieku. Nzuati yeɛ yɛka sɔ ɔ? Ɔmaa Yudafoɛ asɔre mbanyi nemɔ tanene Yesu nye. Yeɛ ɔmaa mmenia biemɔ kekane yenwo dwirɛ bɔ ɔtte nahorɛ ɔ. Afei bɔ osili yeɛ ole kyɛ, bɛnwa ɔte bɔnefoɛ, yeti behuni ye.—Kenga Mateo 11:19; 27:27-31; Mak. 14:55, 56; Yoh. 8:41-45.
<<<P>>>
6 Abɔnzam maa behuni Yesu beyiele ne, ɔfale ye anyeborolɛ ooguale Yesu asuafoɛ naso. Ne onguro kyɛ mmenia kole koli kyɛ Yesu adwɔso afi ewue nu. Yeti ɔmaa ebiemɔ pɛle anoanu tole ye asuafoɛ naso kyɛ, baawia ye fuin ne. Satan adwo, ahũ Yesu ayie a, nzu bieku yeɛ ɛpena ɔ? (Mat. 28:11-15) Tete Kristofoɛ nemɔ aberɛ so koso, ɔmaa mmenia tanene bɛnye. Bɔ ne Satan yɛ ne, yebo yeɛ ole sɛɛ? Na ɔpena kyɛ onwuna-nwuna bɛ, kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a bɛkɔyakyi asɛnga adwuma bɔ Yesu afa awura bɛsa ne.—Aso. 7:57–8:1; 12:1-3; 28:22.

7 Ɔwɔ nu, ebiemɔ yakyili asɛnga adwuma ne de, nakoso bɛnu pee ne ala de banyakyi. Ese bɔ ɔbokale bɛ ɔ? Beenwuni kyɛ ateetee he amukoraati, sãa Satan ne ye sunzummɔne mɔ yeɛ bewura yebo ɔ. (Aso. 4:18-20; 1 Pet. 5:8) Ɛnnɛ koso Satan sesanne. Ɔpena kyɛ yɛyakyi Yehowa som. Atee beni yeɛ ɔfa so ɔ? Ma yɛnea e.

ƐNNƐ KOSO, SATAN TÉ KA YEHOWA NE YESU NWO DWIRƐ BƆ ƆTTE NAHORƐ
<<<P>>>
8 Ɛnnɛ, nahorɛ dwirɛ bɔ ɔfa Yehowa ne Yesu nwo ne, Satan mbena kyɛ mmenia konwu ye. Yeti ɔmma ngondombo soen nga nahorɛ bɔ ɔfa bɛnwo ne. Ɔnyɛ nwanwa kyɛ Bible frɛ Abɔnzam ‘ngondombo baba’ ɔ. (Yoh. 8:44) Asɔfo nemɔ sɔa, bamaa mmenia ale ali kyɛ Yehowa ne Yesu te kɔ̃. (2 Kor. 4:3, 4) Afei Nyameɛ dumaa po bɛnze. Ɛhene ati, mmenia aye Yehowa asie ahameɛ, yeɛ bɛsõ Yesu ɔ. Nakoso Yehowa ne Yesu de, benguro ye sɔ. (Mat. 4:10; Yoh. 4:23) Mmenia bɔ sɔ ngyerɛkyerɛ ne ali beti ne, b’adwene yɛ bɛ kyɛ bɔ bɛse ne, ye alaa ɛne. Yeti sɛ ɛkɔ bɛ berɛ kyɛ ɛkwaahyirehyire bɛ nahorɛ ne a, ɛyɛ a benguro kyɛ bekotie. Ebiemɔ po de, bɛmbena yɛ nyunu bɛnnea.
