17 Pɔɔlo dwirɛ bɔ ɔhane kyɛ, “émɔnya aboterɛ bɛmoma ɛmɔ nwo” ne, okyire sɛɛ? Okyire kyɛ yeliema bie yɛ nikyee maa ɔyɛ yɛ yea a, yɛne ye ngɔhwe, na mmom yɛkɔfa yɛkɔhyɛ ye. Pɔɔlo toa ye dwirɛ naso ne, ɔhane kyɛ sɔ ala yeɛ Yehowa koso, onya yɛnwo aboterɛ na ɔfa yɛ bɔne kyɛ yɛ ɔ. Yehowa se kyɛ bɔne wɔ yɛnwo, yeti yɛngɔhora ngɔyɛ nikyeebiala pɛpɛɛpɛ. Nakoso ɔmva ye adwene ngɔ ɛhene aso, na mmom nningyein pa bɔ yɛkora yɛyɛ ne yeɛ ɔnea ɔ. (Edw. 103:13, 14; 130:3) Ɛhene ati daabiala onwu yɛ anwumvoin. (Awu. 3:22, 23) Yɛ koso sɛ yɛbɔ mmɔden yesuesua Yehowa a, yekonya yeliemamɔ nwo aboterɛ, kyɛbɔ bɛ koso benya yɛnwo aboterɛ ne. Nzu yeɛ ɔkɔboka yɛ ɔ? Wɔde nea o, sɛ ɛyɛ eboo a, ɛfɛɛ wɔ yenwo paa, nakoso sɛ ɛnea bɔ ekonya kofi nu a, ɔmaa enya anwoserɛ fa yɛ yenwo adwuma. Yɛ koso, sɛ yɛfa y’adwene yesie subaen pa bɔ yeliemamɔ le ye naso a, ɔkɔmaa yekonya bɛnwo aboterɛ. Na sɛ bɛyɛ yɛ bɔne a, yɛkɔfa yɛkɔhyɛ bɛ.

18 Mmɔden bɔ yɛbɔ kyɛ yɛkɔmaa asomdwee kɔtena asafo nanu ne, Yehowa nwu ye, na ɔyɛ ye nyemene. Yeti daabiala yɛ nikyeebie fa kyire kyɛ, ekuro kyɛ ɛne aliemaamɔ tena asomdwee nu. Ebie ole kyɛ, mia wɔnye na ɛne ebiala dwudwo kama. Ka eliemamɔ nwo dwirɛ pa. Na sɛ ɛfo aliemaa bie a, pa ye kyerɛ. Afei nikyeebiala bɔ ɛyɛ a asomdwee kɔwa ɛmɔ afia ne, bɔ mmɔden yɛ. Nya ɔdɔ ma aliemaamɔ. Sɛ ɛyɛ sɔ a, ɛkɔfa bɛ bɔne kɔhyɛ bɛ, na ɛne bɛ ngɔhwe ye twaenn. Yehowa nwa bɛbɔ bɛbrɛ bɛnwo ase ne, “bɛ sa kɔhã aseɛ ne,” na “ɔnate asomdwee kandinga ne ati, bɛ nye kɔgye” daa. Yeti bɔ adesua ne aha namu, ɛfa yɛ adwuma a, wɔ koso ekonya sɔ nhyiraa ne bie.—Edw. 37:11.
