Sɛ yɛyɛ nningyein bɔ ɔkɔmaa yekopingye Yehowa a, ɔkɔyɛ se kyɛ Satan konya yɛ
(Nea ngyekyɛmuɛ 14)
<<<P>>>
so ne, ne yale yali. Yede nzu yeɛ yɛkɔyɛ na ebiala annalaka yɛ? Yɛɛ ole kyɛ yeko-nya alagye yɛkɔkenga Bible, yekosua asa-fo nwomaa, na yafa yɛnwo yabɔ aliemaa-mɔ bɔ bɛnwo akokwa ne. Sɛ yɛse nahorɛ dwirɛ bɔ ɔwɔ Bible nanu paa a, atee biala nne berɛ bɔ Satan kɔfa so konya yɛ ɔ.—Any. 13:20; Aso. 17:11.

15. Nzu yeɛ ɔmaa yenwu kyɛ Yehowa ne Yesu dɔ yɛ, na bɛta yɛ si ɔ?
<<<P>>>
bɛyɛ bɛmane bɛ ne, sɛ yɛdwenedwene ye-nwo a, yekonwu kyɛ bɛngɔma yɛsa ngosie ase lle. Afei baha edwirɛ pee wɔ Bible nanu bɔ okyire kyɛ bɛdɔ yɛ ɔ. Ebie ɛhe: Yɛse kyɛ maame biala bɔ ɔma ɔwa nyɔfo-ne ne, okuro ye dwirɛ. Nakoso Yehowa nwa, ɔdɔ bɔ ole ma yɛ ne, nu yɛ se tra sɔ dɔ ne koraa. (Yes. 49:15) Bɛbɔ benni Ye-howa dumaa nwo angorɛ ne, yenwa bɛte ‘ye bɔbɔ ye deɛ.’ (Mal. 3:16, 17) Yesu koso nwa, ɔdɔ yɛ nati yeɛ ɔfale ye ngoa tole berɛ mane yɛ ɔ.—Yoh. 15:12, 13.
