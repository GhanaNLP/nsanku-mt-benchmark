Yehowa nate Yesu so ale yɛ
(Nea ngyekyɛmuɛ 6)
<<<P>>>
yeɛ bɛfale ye besiele so ɔ. (Luka 1:32, 33) Ɔngɔhyɛ Yesu kɔsɛkye yɛ pɔfoɛ nemɔ wɔ Amagedon. Nna ye ngome yeɛ ɔkɔyɛ sɔ ɔ, na mmom ɔne mmenia 144,000 ne, yeɛ abɔfo nemɔ yeɛ bɛkɔyɛ ɔ. Ɛhene si ne, nzu yeɛ okosi ɔ? Yesu ne mmenia 144,-000 ne, bekoli aseɛ he aso afoɛ apee kõ. Bekotingye bɛbɔ bawu ne, na basa baboka yɛ maa yayɛ pɛ. (Yoh. 5:28; Nye. 22:1, 2) Yeti dee bɔ afoɛ apee ne hɔ ayieleɛ ne, bɔne oo, ewue oo, ne balogo ye bato nekaa bie. (1 Kor. 15:26, 55-57) Abɔnzam ne ye sunzummɔne ne koso, nzu yeɛ ɔkɔto bɛ ɔ? Bɛkɔyera bɛ boso. Afei ebiala bɔ wandie Yesu bɔ ɔkɔɔta Abɔnzam si ne, bɛkɔsɛkye ye. (Nye. 20:7-10) Bɛbɔ bekoli nahorɛ ne de, bɛkɔso Yehowa daa. Afei bɔne oo, ewue oo, ateetee oo, yemu kofi berɛ maa bɛnwo kɔtɔ bɛ. Ɛmɔma yɛha, Yehowa de walo yɛ. Wamaa ɔwa Yesu ale yɛ. Ɔyɛ yɛ fɛ paa, yeti yɛda ye ase, da ye ase.—2 Kor. 9:15.

YEHOWA AMA Y’ATEE KYƐ YƐSO YE
<<<P>>>
7 Kenga Luka 1:74, 75. Yesu wue ne adaworoma, yasa yanya nikyee foforɛ bie. Sakaria hane yenwo dwirɛ kyɛ, ‘yɛso Yehowa bɔ yenzuro hwee ɔ.’ Bɔ yéyɛ ye wɔ Yehowa som nu biala, ɔboka soen bɔ yɛfa yɛma ye naso. Ebie yeɛ ole kyɛ yɛbɔ mbaeɛ, yesua Bible, yɛkɔ asɛnga, na yɛkɔ adesua ne nhyiamu. Yeti sɛ yɛyɛ adwuma wɔ Bɛtɛl oo, yɛboka bɛbɔ besi asafo sua naso oo, anaakyɛ sɛ yɛboka bɛbɔ nikyeebie sɛkye sua nanwo a bɛyɛ ye yé ne oo, ye mukoraati boka soen bɔ yɛfa yɛma Yehowa naso.
