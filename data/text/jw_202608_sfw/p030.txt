Kesaalae yɛyɛ manzini adwuma wɔ New Caledonia
<<<P>>>
sɔ, yeti ɛyɛ a ne me wora abɔ. (Nwo. 7:16) Siɛ ye, mmanwuni kɛ Yehowa nnea atee kye yɛkɔyɛ ni-kyeebiala pɛpɛɛpɛ, na mmom kaa bɔ yɛkora yɛyɛ ne, yenye sɔ.
<<<P>>>
Asafo ne hane kye, se manzini soneafoɛ nne berɛ a, ngonzi ye gya nu. Ne menwu kyɛ nde kãa, na nne nimdeɛ biala. Nakoso siɛ ye nwuni kyɛ sɛ Yehowa fa ye sunzum boka wɔ a, nikyefee nne berɛ bɔ ɛngɔhora ye ye ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Nikyee foforɛ bɔ ɔha me yeɛ ole kyɛ, anwonyerɛ bie wɔ me so. Menwo kora te mvifire dee maa mesa kunu po doa. Na ofi me ngwalaa nu yeɛ wa-teetee me baasi ɛnnɛ. Ɛse mmerɛ bɔ ɔba paa ɔ? Ménya megyina platfɔɔm so kyɛ mema tɔɔk wɔ nhyiamu bo a, ekonwu ye ne, ne menwo amu aloa. Ɔba sɔ a, ɛyɛ a meka mekyire Yehowa kyɛ ɔboka me, na me kunu andete me. Ekonwu ye ne, na so ate.
<<<P>>>
Wɔ 2011 nu ne, adwuma bɔ ne ɔla y’ahone so ne, Yehowa famane yɛ. Yee ole kye yɛɛyɛle nzɛmbatrɛlɛfoɛ. Bɛhane kyɛ yɛhɔ New Caledonia. Nekaa bɔ owura ne, efi yɛ kuro Paris berɛ ɛkɔ dɔ a, atee ne wale pa. Ɛte alupre nu ɛkɔ a, ekoli dɔnhwere kɔbo 20. Ɛberɛ asetena de, ɔlla ase. Ne odikyɛ yesua aleɛ biemɔ lilɛ. Ebie yeɛ ole bangye, kofoɔ yeɛ boluo. Ɛdɔ de, aleɛ hyenne po a, ɛkɔte kyɛ ngokɔ bɛbɔ, na ngyea mɔ bepo. Ɔba sɔ a yɛn-gora da. Na ɛberɛ de, sɛ ebisa ebie dwirɛ a, kyɛ ahaa okodwudwo ne, ekonwu ye ne, ne ɔpa yenye asi kyire wɔ. Na ɔlla ase, ɛhemɔ amu ne odikyɛ ye-sua.
<<<P>>>
Dwumadie biala bɔ bɛkɔfa bekowura yɛsa ne, Yehowa boka yɛ maa yɛyɛ. Yɛɛyɛle nzɛmba-trɛlɛ adwuma ne kãa, na siɛ ye bɛmaa yɛhɔle Bɛtɛl koso. Afei yɛhɔle Ahennie Nanwo Nzɛmbahãnɛfoɛ Sukuu ne bie, na kesaalae yɛyɛ manzini adwuma. Bɔ yɛka ye, sɛ nna Yehowa a, ahaa yɛngɔhora ye yɛ. Wamaa manwu kyɛ ɔmva yenwo ne bɔ ɔha me biala ne, ɔfa me yɛ adwuma a, ɔkɔyɛ yé. Yeti mbaeɛ de, memva menni angorɛ. Ɛnnɛ ne ɛnnɛ amu, sɛ ngyi aliemaamɔ nyunu a, na kunu tete me. Nakoso Yehowa adaworoma, bɔ odikyɛ mefa mehyɛ bɛ nguanen ne de, mekora meka.
<<<P>>>
Bɔ yafa nu mukoraati ne, meye Claudine de, ɔyakyilli menu lle. Nzue nu oo, eyia nu oo, mene ye ala. M’anwonyerɛ namukoraati si ne, onya me-nwo aboterɛ. Ɔmva asɛnga adwuma ne nni ango-rɛ, na adwuma biala bɔ bɛkɔfa bɛkɔma yɛ ne koso, ɔsɔ nu. Onzu ngua meso kyɛ ahuhuro wɔ wa, anaa manzini adwuma ne yɛ se soma, yeti yɛ yakyi.
<<<P>>>
Nhyiamu bɔ nnimoa ngɔle ne, mma nwuni kyɛ: Yehowa bɔ ɔbɔle nikyeebiala ne, daa ɔnea ye mmenia. Yeti nzili gyinayɛɛ kyɛ ngɔsõ Yehowa ne, wahyira me paa. Yehowa amaa manwu kyɛ odikyɛ mebrɛ menwo ase, na mebu awaen nu mbanyi. Afei asafo nu mbanyi koso, odikyɛ metie bɛ dwirɛ. Me nnamvo bɔ ne bɛmva Yehowa mmraa bɛnyɛ hwee ne, mene bɛ apɛ nu. Nakoso Yehowa ada-woroma, manya nnamvoa pee mbaen, na bɛfa ye akwangyerɛ bɛbɔ bɛ bra. Manya brasua kama ma-gya ye. Na mese kyɛ ɔngɔhyɛ, daa ngoa bɔ Ye-howa afa ahyɛ yɛ bɔ ne, yɛsa kɔha ye, na wɔ wia-se foforɛ nanu de, daa ɔkɔma yɛ nningyein pa pee mbaen.—Mak. 10:29, 30.
