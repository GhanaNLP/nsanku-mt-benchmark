Sɛ yɛne aliemaamɔ yɛ kɔ a, bɔ ɔte biala, yɛkoli Satan ne ye sunzummɔne mɔ so ngunim
(Nea ngyekyɛmuɛ 16)
<<<P>>>
ɛhene ati yɛngobu bɛ. Nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ Baebolo ne kã kyɛ yebu mmenia bɔ bɛte sɔ ne. (Rom. 13:1, 2) Sɛ awaen ne hyɛ mmraa bie na otia yɛ po a, yɛkae kyɛ nna yemu yeɛ ebiaa ofi Satan ɔ. Nakoso Satan nyane a, ahaa yadwudwo yatia mmraa bɔ bahyɛ ne. Na sɛ onwu kyɛ yɛte yɛ awuro na yɛkeka bɔ yɛkuro yɛfa bɛnwo a, ne yenye agye. Mmenia bɔ bɛwɔ wiase ne de, sɛ awaen ne hyɛ mmraa bie na benguro a, ekonwu yɛ ne, na bate basie dwaaso bɛ yɛ ɔyɛkyerɛ anaa demonstration. Nakoso yɛ bɔ yɛte Kristofoɛ ne de, ɔnzɛkyɛ yɛyɛ bie. (Any. 24:21, 22; Nwo. 8:2, 3) Yɛse kyɛ Nyameɛ ahennie ne ngome yeɛ odikyɛ yɛta ye si ɔ. Nakoso sɛ yanyɛ sɔ, na yɛkɔta awaen bie si a, okyire kyɛ Satan anya yɛ.—Yoh. 15:19.

14 Ebiemɔ wɔ berɛ a, betuli bɛnye besiele aliemaamɔ so paa. Nakoso kesaalae bɛte Yehowa Adanzefoɛ, na bɛmva bɛ soen benni angorɛ. (1 Tim. 1:12, 13) Yeti sɛ ‘mmenia pɛpɛ wɔ nzoaa, bɛtane wɔ nye, ná bɛpɛ ngondombo bɛkeka edwirɛ tɛɛ bɛto wɔ so’ a, kae kyɛ sona sesa, na ɛhema bie, ebiaa ne baaso Yehowa. (Mat. 5:11) Awaen nu mbanyi koso, srɛ Yehowa kyɛ ɔha bɛ ahone, kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a yɛkɔsõ ye wɔ asomdwee nu. (1 Tim. 2:1, 2) Afei bɛbɔ bɛtane wɔ nye tekyɛ, w’abusuafoɛ, bɛbɔ ɛne bɛ yɛ adwuma, ɔne wɔ sukuufoɛ ne, ɛyɛ a bɔ mbaeɛ ma bɛ. Srɛ Yehowa kyɛ ɔboka wɔ ma enwu bɔ ɛkɔyɛ kɔboka bɛ, na wamaa banya wɔnwo adwene pa. Wɔde ɛyɛ a nya bɛnwo aboterɛ. Sɔ yeɛ aliemaa bie yɛle ɔ. Ɔye teeteele ye afoɛ pee. Nakoso kesaalae waayɛ Yehowa Danzeniɛ. Tie bɔ maame ne hane ɔ: “Aboterɛ de te paa oo. Bɔ nyɛle namu, mehu nyane menwo aboterɛ. Ɛhene yeɛ wamaa ɛnnɛ mesõ Yehowa ne.”
