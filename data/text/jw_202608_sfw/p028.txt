<<<P>>>
Sɛ mekae mbaeɛ bɔ mmɔle ne a, mede medi kyɛ Yehowa tiele me, ofikyɛ ne ɔse paa kyɛ meku-ro kyɛ menwu ye. Me damvo kɔ̃ bɔ mene ye wɔ ɛhɔayeɛ nanu berɛ ne, Yehowa Adanzefoɛ mane ye nwomaa bie. Bɛfrɛ ye Wubetumi Atra Ase Daa wɔ Paradise wɔ Asase So.* Siɛ ye ɔfale nwo-maa ne mane me, na ngengale. Ne nu dwirɛ ne yɛ me fɛ paa. Nwuni kyɛ aseɛ he kɔyɛ Paradise, ye-
<<<P>>>
* Yehowa Adanzefoɛ yeɛ bɛyɛle ɔ, koso kesaalae de bɛnyɛ ye kɔ̃.
<<<P>>>
konya asomdwee, na yɛne y’adɔfo mɔ kɔtena ɔdɔ nu. Nza nwuni kyɛ ebiala ngowu, na yɛkɔtena ase daa. Yeti ne ɔyɛ me nwanwa. Yemia bɔ ne mepena hɔ alaa ɛne. Nyameɛ bɔ yenwa ɔkɔfa sɔ nningyein pa he kɔma yɛ ne, ne mepena kyɛ mefa ye adam-voa.
<<<P>>>
Ne menwu ye kyɛ odikyɛ mesesa m’abrabɔ, na-koso ne mengora. Ne mehia ebie bɔ ɔkɔboka me ɔ. Sɔ mmerɛ ne, né me damvo bie ne aliemaa bie sua nikye. Bɛfrɛ aliemaa ne Michel. Yeti nga ngyi-rele me damvo ne kyɛ, ɔha me nwo dwirɛ hyire ye.
<<<P>>>
Kyẽa bɔ nyiale Michel ne, ɔha hyirele me kyɛ bɛ-kɔyɛ nhyiamu bie wɔ Matoury. Ɛberɛ pingye French Guiana ahenguro Cayenne. Efi ɛhɔayeɛ nanu berɛ ɛkɔ dɔ a, ekoli dɔnhwere nza wɔ atee so. Mekɔ ne, ne meti nwiaa gua menwo huyaa, yeɛ ne me kanza piapiaw bie. Ne nwura trɔza ka-lɛs kalɛs bie, na me hyɛɛt ne koso, ɔte sɔa bolaa. Nakoso aliemaamɔ annea ɛhene, na mmom bɛfa-le me bɛhɔle nhyiamu ne. Yedwuli ne koso, bɔ ngoli, ɔne nekaa bɔ ngɔla ne, bɛ ala yeɛ bɛfa bɛ-mane me ɔ. Sɛ menea ɔdɔ bɔ ɔwɔ bɛnu, ɔne bɔ bɛyɛ bɛmane me ne a, ɔmaa nwuni kyɛ nahorɛ nala yeɛ manwu ye ne.
