17 Yenguro kyɛ nikyefee kɔtete yɛne Yehowa ne Yesu afia. Nakoso ɔhale Abɔnzam ne ye sunzummɔne nemɔ a, ahaa yayakyi Yehowa som. Bɛpena kyɛ bɛyɛ dee maa yɛ gyidie kɔ ase. Bɛnwa bɔ ɔfoa wɔ ne sɛ ɔfɛle a, odikyɛ emia w’amirika nu. Yeti sɔ mmerɛ he bɔ Yehowa sɛkyelle bɛ ne, odikyɛ yɛne bɛ fa sie yenye. (Nye. 20:1-3, 10) Bɔ ɔkɔyɛ na bannya yɛ ne, odikyɛ yenya gyidie kyɛ Yehowa ne Yesu dwene yɛnwo. Afei nningyein bɔ ɔkɔmaa yɛne b’adamvoa nanu kɔyɛ se ne, nnɛmaa yɛyakyi. Yɛyɛ sɔ a, bengonya yɛ lle. Na kyɛbɔ Yesu bɔbɔ deinn hane ne, ‘ebiala ngɔhora ngofoti yɛ ngofi ye Baba sa nu.’—Kenga Yohane 10:29; Yak. 4:7.
