Kyɛbɔ Filemɔn yɛle ne, sɛ yɛmaa aliemaamɔ ba y'awuro a, ɔkɔmaa yɛne b'afia kɔyɛ kama
(Nea ngyekyɛmuɛ 15)
<<<P>>>
ɔte nahorɛ o, ofikyɛ sɛ yɛlle ɔdɔ a, ɔkɔyɛ sɛ kyɛ yɛne aliemaamɔ kɔtena asomdwee nu. Yɛdɔ aliemaamɔ paa a, ɔngɔyɛ se kyɛ yɛkɔfa bɛ bɔne yɛkɔhyɛ bɛ. (1 Kor. 13: 4-7) Ɔdɔ bɔ ne Filemɔn le ye ne, nzu bieku yeɛ ɔmaa ɔyɛle ɔ? Yɛnea e.

14 Ɔdɔ bɔ ne Filemɔn le ye nati, ne ɔmaa aliemaamɔ ba ye awuro. Yeti Pɔɔlo hahyirele ye kyɛ, ɔma ye sua ma osikye nu. (Flm. 22) Afei ne ɔmaa asafo ne bayɛ adesua wɔ ye awuro berɛ. (Flm. 2) Ɔnate ye subaen pa nati, ebiala bɔ ɔkɔwa ye awuro ne, né ɔmaa yenwo dwo ye.—Flm. 7.
<<<P>>>
15 Kyɛbɔ ne Filemɔn de ɛwɔfoɛ ne, yɛkoso yɛkɔyɛ sɛɛ né yasyasua ye? Sɛ yɛto yɛsa ye frɛ aliemaamɔ maa bɛba yɛ awuro a, ɔte atee ko bɔ yɛfaso yesuesua ye ɔ. Yɛyɛ sɔ a, okobukye atee maa yɛne bɛ kɔbɔ ngɔmmɔ, na ɔkɔmaa yekonwu bɔ yeti yeɛ bɛyɛ nningyein biemɔ ɔ. 15. Sɛ yɛmaa aliemaamɔ ba y'awuro a, nzuati yeɛ ɔkɔmaa yekohuro bɛ dwirɛ paa ɔ? (Nea foto ne koso.)
<<<P>>>
yekonwu bɛnwo dwirɛ pee. Ebie yeɛ ole nekaa bɔ bɛtetele bɛ, bɔ ɔyɛle a bɛwale asafo nanu, ɔhaw bɔ bafa nu, ɔne kyɛbɔ basi bagyina ye noa. Na ɛhene kɔmaa yekohuro bɛ dwirɛ. Afei yekonwu kyɛ Yehowa nwuni nikyee pa bie wɔ bɛnwo, yeti yeɛ wahwe bɛ wawa asafo nanu ne. (Yoh. 6:44) Yekosa yekonwu kyɛ ole ebiala ne ye subaen, nakoso ɔmaa asafo nanu yɛ fɛ. Yeti sɛ yɛliema bie yɛ nikyee ma ɔha yɛ a, ɔnate kyɛ yɛse ye yé nati, yɛkɔte yebo kyɛ wanhyɛda, na yɛngɔhwe dwirɛ nanu twaenn.

16 Aliemaa bie ayɛ wɔ bɔne, na ɔyɛ se ma wɔ kyɛ ɛkɔfa kɔhyɛ ye anaa? Ɔwɔ nu yɛdɔ aliemaamɔ, nakoso odwu mmerɛ bie a, ne wayɛ se kyɛ yɛkɔfa bɛ bɔne yɛkɔhyɛ bɛ. Sɛ sɔ a, nzu yeɛ ɔkɔboka wɔ ɔ? Krataa bɔ Pɔɔlo hworɔ ɔɔmane Kolose asafo ne, nu dwirɛ ne bie kɔboka wɔ. Wɔde nea kyɛ, aliemaamɔ ahɔ adesua wɔ Filemɔn awuro berɛ, na bɛkenga Kolosefoɛ 3:13 ne. (Kenga.)
