11 Kyɛbɔ Yehowa pena kyɛ yɛbɔ yɛ bra ne, wafa yenwo mmraa wama yɛ. Nakoso Satan mbena kyɛ yekole yekoli kyɛ sɔ mmraa ne kɔboka yɛ. Yeti ɔnate sohyia midia so yeɛ ɔbɔ sɔ bra ne. Ɛkɔ sohyia midia so a, edwirɛ bɔ mmeni pee ka yeɛ ole kyɛ, ‘Bible nu mmraa de wapɛ nu, yeti yɛ bɔ ekuro ɔ; nnɛmaa ebie kyire wɔ bɔ ɛyɛ ɔ.’ Ehe maa yenwu kyɛ, Satan “bɔ odi wiase he aso ne,” wayɛ dee maa mmenia anya adwene kyɛ bɔne te pa, yeɛ pa koso te bɔne.—Yoh. 14:30; Yes. 5:20; 1 Tim. 4:1, 2.
<<<P>>>
12 Bɔ mmenia keka ye wɔ wiase nanu ne, yetie a, annea a, yekonya adwene kyɛ yɛfa Yehowa mmraa yɛbɔ yɛ bra a, yɛnwo kɔhyere yɛ. Ebie yeɛ ɔtole taluwa bie bɔ bɛfrɛ ye Shannon* ne. Ɔhane kyɛ: “Ne mefa mmerɛ pee mekɔ sohyia midia so. Na bɔ ngonwu ye wɔ berɛ biala ne, ne mepena kyɛ meyɛ bie. Yeɛ sukuu nu koso, ne bekyire yɛ kyɛ ebiala yɛ bɔ okuro ɔ. Me nnamvo mɔ koso, ne bɛ subaen apo-rɔ. Afei de, ne menwu ye kyɛ sɛ ebie amva Yehowa mmraa ammɔ ye bra po a, ɔtte hwee. Menye wale menwo so ne, né mene Yehowa afia asɛkye. Yeti ne mengora me mbingye aliemaamɔ ne m’abusuafoɛ.” Siɛ ye, ɔsesane ye subaen, na ɔne Yehowa afia yɛle kama. Afei de, né wanya yenwo watete wafi Abɔnzam sa nu. Nzu yeɛ ɔbokale ye ɔ?

13 Shannon nwuni kyɛ, sɛ ɔne Yehowa ne Yesu afia kosa kɔyɛ kama a, sana ɔyere yenwo. Tie bɔ ɔhane ɔ: “Nzrɛle Yehowa kyɛ ɔboka me maa nya nnamvoa pa. Yeɛ nyɛle nhwehwɛmuɛ wɔ yɛ nwomaa mɔ nu. Vidio bie bɔ neane ye wɔ jw.org koso, ɔbokale me paa.* Nwuni kyɛ bɔ mefa nu ne, ebie yeɛ ɔtole taluwa bɔ ɔwɔ vidio nanu ne. Ɔhane bɔ ɔkɔso ne hyirele ye maame ne ye baba, na bɛ koso bɛbokale ye. Me koso nyɛle bie. Mmɔle me maame ne me baba amaneɛ. Yeɛ ngonwuni asafo nu mbanyi. Afei aliemaa mmrasua bɔ bɛnwo akokwa ne koso, bɛbokale me. Ofi berɛ bɔ ɔkɔ ne, mene me nnamvo nemɔ pɛle nu. Mmanwuni kyɛ, sɛ bɛka Yehowa som a, nna ye alaa yeɛ ole kyɛ ngɔhɔ adesua, na ma maa me maame ne me baba anwu kyɛ nde akwalaa pa ɔ. Mmom kyɛ ngonwu Yehowa na mafa ye adamvoa. Na afei mafa bɔ mesua ne mabɔ me bra.”
