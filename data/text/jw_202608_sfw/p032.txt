MMENIA NE NNINGYEIN BIEMƆ BƆ BIBLE NE KÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Bakaa Bɔ Odwɔso, Anaa Osu Ndɛ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Mmakaa kɔyɛ 30 bɔ Bible ne kã bɛnwo dwirɛ ne, nu kɔ̃ yeɛ ole aborɔfo ngateɛ bɔ ɔwɔ Yi-srael ne. Sɛ ayiré ne baawa ayieleɛ wɔ dɔ a, mmakaa bɔ ne bɛsu flawɛs ne, né ɔboka so. Ye flawɛs ne te fufue yeɛ ebie koso te kɔkorɛ, na ɔyɛ nyemene paa. January bosome ne ayieleɛ nu yeɛ ne ye flawɛs ne ba ɔ. Yeti ɔnyɛ nwanwa kyɛ wɔ Hebri dwudwolɛ nu ne, dumaa bɔ bɛfa bɛfrɛ sɔ aborɔfo ngateɛ ne, okyire “bɔ wadwɔso ndɛ ɔ.” Ye de, nzu yeɛ ɛhe maa ye-nwu ye yɛfa bɔ ɔyɛle sɔ bakaa nanwo ɔ?
<<<P>>>
Hebri dwudwolɛ nu ne, edwirɛ bɔ bɛfa bɛyɛle adwuma wɔ Yeremia 7:25 ne, ɔmaa yenwu kyɛ Yehowa nwa, daa biala odwɔso ngyerɛmɔ tu-tuutu. Ɔwɛn-Aban bɔ ɔwale March 15, 2011 ne hyirele nu kyɛ: “Yehowa fale yenwo totole sɔ aborɔfo ngateɛ nanwo. Sɔ bakaa ne dwɔso ndɛ anaa osu ndɛ. Yehowa koso nwa, odwɔso ndɛ daa biala. Sɔ dwirɛ ne kyire sɛɛ? Okyire kyɛ daa ɔsoma ye adiyifoɛ mɔ maa bɛkɔ ye mmenia berɛ, kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a, bekonwu bɔ oko-fi b’asoserɛ nanu kɔwa ɔ. Osa koso hane kyɛ ongole ye nwomeɛ kodwu kyɛ, bɔ wahã ne mukoraati kɔwa nu ɔ.”—Yer. 1:11,12.
<<<P>>>
Sɔ bakaa ne, kyɛbɔ ye flawɛs ne bie te fufue nati, Bible ne fa toto bɛbɔ bayɛ mbanyi ne, bɛti so gyein nwo. (Nwo. 12:5) Afei sɔ flawɛs ne, ye nyemene nati, mmerɛ bɔ ne Besalel ɔyɛ kanea bakaa bɔ bɛkɔfa bekosie ɛtena sua nanu ne, ɔyɛle nekaa bɔ bɛfa oil begua ne maa osomane sɔ flawɛs ne.—Ɛks. 37:17-20.
