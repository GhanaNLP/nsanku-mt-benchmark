16 Bɔ Sakaria hane ye wɔ Luka 1:68-79 ne, ɔmaa yenwu kyɛ sɔ nningyein nza he, odikyɛ yɛmaa yɛnye sɔ. Bɔ olimoa, Yehowa nate Yesu so ale yɛ. Bɔ ɔtɔ so nyɔ, wama y’atee kyɛ yɛso ye, na nnɛmaa yesuro hwee. Yeɛ bɔ ɔtɔ so nza, wafa wawura yɛsa kyɛ yɛha nzɛmba ne. Sɔ nningyein hemɔ bɔ Yehowa afa ama yɛ ne, ɔnyɛ wɔ fɛ anaa? Yede yedi kyɛ ɔyɛ wɔ fɛ, ɛhene ati yeɛ anya Yesu wue nanu gyidie, na ahyira wɔnwo so ama Yehowa ne. Sɛ sɔ ahɔ de a, ma mmenia nwu kyɛ Yehowa dɔ bɛ, na ɔpena kyɛ ode bɛ ngoa. Ma benwu kyɛ ɔngɔhyɛ Nyameɛ Ahennie ne kɔwa, na ɔkɔsɛkye bɛbɔ bendie ye dwirɛ namu. Nakoso bɛbɔ betie ye ne de, ɔkɔmaa bɛkɔhɔ Paradise. Ɔba sɔ a, yɛne bɛ kɔso Yehowa bɔ ‘yengosuro hwee wɔ tenenee nu, yɛ ngoa ngyẽa mukoraati.’—Luka 1:74, 75.
