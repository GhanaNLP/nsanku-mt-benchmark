YEHOWA NATE YESU SO ALE YƐ
<<<P>>>
2 Kenga Luka 1:67-73. Sakaria hane kyɛ Nyameɛ nwa okole ye mmenia. Nwa yeɛ ne ɔnate ye so kole bɛ ne? Sakaria hyirele nu kyɛ: ‘Yehowa apagya defoɛ bɔ ole tumi so wama yɛ, wɔ ye akoaa Dawi-de awuro.’ (Luka 1:69) Bɔ yɛka yenwo dwirɛ kyɛ ole tumi ne, yeɛ ole Mɛsaya ne. Ne Yudafoɛ nemɔ nea Mɛsaya ne atee, na ne bɛse kyɛ ɔkɔyɛ Abraham bosoniɛ bɔ ofi Dawide abusua nu ɔ. (Gye. 22:17, 18; Mat. 22:42) Nzuati yeɛ Yehowa maa Sakaria hane Mɛsaya nanwo dwirɛ ɔ? Ofikyɛ ne ye walɛ ne apingye. Sakaria hane sɔ dwirɛ ne, ye bosome nzia so yeɛ Maria wole Yesu ɔ. Abɔfo Gabriel hane kyɛ, ‘bɛkɔfrɛ ye Nyameɛ Awa, na okoli ye nana Dawide ahennie bia.’ (Luka 1:30-35) Yesu nyane afoɛ 30 ne, ɔbɔle asu. Sɔ mmerɛ ne, Yehowa sunzum wale ye so, na ɔɔyɛle Mɛsaya ne. (Mat. 3:16, 17) Mɛ-saya bɔ banea ye atee dee ne, afei de ne waadwu. Nea kyɛbɔ bɛnye gyele ɔ.
<<<P>>>
3 Ne Yesu baale Yudafoɛ ne fi nzu anu? Sakaria hane kyɛ: “Okole yɛ kofi yɛ pɔ-foɛ mɔ sa nu, ɔne bɛ bɔ bekyi yɛ muko-raa bɛ sa nu.” (Luka 1:71) Ye dwirɛ ne, okyire kyɛ Romanfoɛ bɔ ne bɛteetee bɛ ne, yeɛ Yesu kole bɛ kofi bɛsa nu anaa? Daabi. Yede nwa ɛne? Ɔte Abɔnzam, ofi-kyɛ sɔ ateetee namukoraati, ye yeɛ owu-ra yesi ɔ. (Heb. 2:14, 15) Ye yeɛ ɔfale bɔne ne ewue wale ɔ, na ɛhene yeɛ wasa yɛmu ne. Ɛhe ati, ne ɔyɛ se ma Yudafoɛ nemɔ kyɛ bekoli Mose Mmraa naso. Bible ne ka kyɛ, ne wayɛ kyɛ badome bɛ ne. Yeti ne behia bɔ okole bɛ ɔ. (Rom. 3:20; Gal. 3:10, 13) Sɛ Sakaria hane kyɛ ne behia ebie bɔ okole bɛ a, ne ɔnnaka. Ofikyɛ sɔ sona ne, na ɔkɔmaa bekonya “bɔne fakyɛ.”​—Luka 1:77.
<<<P>>>
4 Ne bɔne ne ewue afa Yudafoɛ nemɔ ayɛ ngoaa. Na sɔ ala yeɛ ɛnnɛ koso ɔte ɔ. Bɔne ne ewue afa yɛ ayɛ ngoaa. (Rom. 5:12) Yeti yehia ebie bɔ okole yɛ ɔ. Ateetee bɔ yɛfa nu namukoraati ne, Abɔnzam ne ye sunzummɔne mɔ yeɛ bewura yesi ɔ. Bɛpena kyɛ yɛyakyi Yehowa som, yeti daa bɛlakalaka yɛ. Bɛbɔ benguro yɛ dwirɛ ne koso, bepiepia bɛ maa bɛtane yɛnye. Afei nna anwu bɔ yeti yeɛ yehia ebie bɔ okole yɛ ne?
<<<P>>>
5 Bɔ ɔbaale yɛ fi bɔne nu ne, yeɛ ole Yesu. Na sɛɛ po yeɛ ɔkɔyɛ ye ɔ? Yɛnea mmerɛ bɔ ɔwale aseɛ he aso ne. Ɔmaa ye asuafoɛ nemɔ nwuni atee bɔ bɛkɔfa so bɛkɔso Yehowa kyɛbɔ okuro ɔ. Afei owu mane yɛ, kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a “ebiala bɔ oko-le ye koli ne wammini, na mmom wa-nya daa ngoa.” (Yoh. 3:16; Mat. 20:28) Bɔ Yesu hyirele ye asuafoɛ ne, yɛfa yɛyɛ adwuma. Ɛhene yeɛ ɔmaa yɛso Yehowa kyɛbɔ okuro ɔ. Yaayɛ sona ye de, ɔyɛ sɛɛ alaa a yɛkɔyɛ bɔne. Ɔba sɔ a, yɛsrɛ Yeho-wa kyɛ ɔfa hyɛ yɛ. Yesu wue nati, ɔfa yɛ bɔne kyɛ yɛ, na ɛhene yeɛ ɔmaa ode yɛ soen to nu ɔ. (1 Yoh. 2:1, 2) Na kyeabie koso ɔkɔmaa yɛkɔtena ase daa.
