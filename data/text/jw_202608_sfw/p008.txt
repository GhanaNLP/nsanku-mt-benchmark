Ɛkɔhora Koli Satan Ne Ye Sunzummɔne Mɔ So Ngunim!
<<<P>>>
DWEIN 129
Yebegyina Mu Pintinn
<<<P>>>
BƆ ADESŨA NE KƆHÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ
Daa biala kae sɔ dwirɛ he: Yɛne Satan ne ye sunzum-mɔne mɔ yeɛ yɛkõ ɔ, na nna yeliemamɔ Kristofoɛ.
<<<P>>>
‘Nna yɛne anwoɔnaen ne mogya yeɛ yɛkõ ɔ; na mmom yɛne ewienu dɔ sunzummɔne mɔ.’—ƐFE. 6:12.
<<<P>>>
YƐ BƆ yɛsõ Yehowa ne de, yɛngo ngondii. (Yes. 2:4; Yoh. 18:36) Nakoso, Bible ne ka kyɛ ngondii bie de, yɛye yɛnwo yefi nu a, ɔnyɛ yé. Ɛhene yeɛ ole Satan ne ye sunzummɔne mɔ bɔ yɛne bɛ kõ ne. (Kenga Ɛfesofoɛ 6:11, 12.) Sɔ abɔfo hemɔ tianu yɛ se, na bɛnwo koso yɛ se tra yɛ. Yede nzu paa yeɛ ɔkɔboka yɛ maa yekoli bɛ so ngunim ɔ? Bɔ yenwo hia paa yeɛ ole kyɛ, yɛkɔfa yɛnwo yɛkɔto Yehowa so maa ɔkɔ-boka yɛ. Nakoso, atee bɔ Satan ne ye sunzummɔne nemɔ fa so ma yɛ gyidie yɛ mmerɛ ne, ɛhene koso odikyɛ yenwu ye.
<<<P>>>
2 Gyidie bɔ yele ye wɔ Yehowa ne Yesu nu ne, adesua kõ hene ne, ɔmaa yenwuni kyɛbɔ Satan bɔ mmɔden kyɛ ɔkɔ-brɛ ye ase ɔ. Wɔ adesua he anu koso, yɛkɔnea atee foforɛ nyɔ bɔ Satan fa so kotia yɛ kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a, yɛ gyidie kɔyɛ mmerɛ ɔ. Nu kõ yeɛ ole kyɛ, onguro kyɛ yɛkɔyɛ kõ wɔ asa-fo nanu. Yeɛ bɔ ɔtɔ so nyɔ, ɔmaa ebiemɔ tu bɛnye besie yɛ so, kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a, esuronyi kɔhã yɛ maa yɛkɔyakyi asɛnga adwuma ne.
<<<P>>>
SATAN BƆ MMƆDEN KYƐ ƆKƆSƐKYE YƐNE YELIEMAMƆ AFĨA
<<<P>>>
3 Satan bɔ mmɔden kyɛ ɔkɔmaa yɛne yeliemamɔ afĩa kɔ-tete. Yeti kyɛ ahaa yɛne ye kɔho ne, ɔmaa yɛpɛ yɛnwo yɛne yeliemamɔ ko, na yede wafa yenwo wali. Atee beni yeɛ ɔfa so yɛ sɔ ɔ? Bɔ ɔte yeɛ ole kyɛ, ewiase nanu de, mmenia sere kyire bɛnwo mbaen, nakoso benne bɛnwo benni. Afei sɛ ɛtɔ bɛ ngaen nu koso a, bɛngɔfa wɔnwo bɛngɔhyɛ wɔ. Yeti sɔ yeɛ Satan pena kyɛ yɛyɛ ɔ. Ɔpena kyɛ yɛkekã yelie-
<<<P>>>
1. Nwamɔ yeɛ bɛne yɛ kõ ne? (Ɛfesofoɛ 6:11, 12)
2. Nzu yeɛ yekosusu yenwo wɔ adesua he anu ɔ?
3. Nzu yeɛ Satan pena kyɛ yɛyɛ ɔ?
