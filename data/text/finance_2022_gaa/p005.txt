“Aatse Nitsumɔi Kɛmɔ shi: Maŋ shikai anaabuamɔ kɛ Nitsumɔtsemɔ”
<<<P>>>
SHƐƐ NI JƐ MAŊSƆƆLƆ LƐ DƐŊ
<<<P>>>
Nifitemɔ kple ni COVID-19 hela gbonyo lɛ kɛba lɛ fee naagba wulu kɛha shika saji yɛ he fɛɛ he bɔ ni Ghana hu fata he. Emli jaramɔi lɛ Ghana maŋbii fɛɛ na eko sa amɛnaa kɛtsɔ gbelei, wala mli shihilɛ kɛ nitsumɔi ni fitefitee anɔ, shi Amralofoi lɛ damɔ amɛnaji anɔ kpɛŋŋ. Miishi mitsitsi ni makɛɛ akɛ gbɛi anɔ ni wɔtsɔ kɛmɔ naa lɛ, eji ekaafeemɔ kɛ yiŋkpɔŋmɔɔ, ni jeŋ fɛɛ ye enɛ he odase kɛ tsuijurɔ. Amralofoi lɛ fee mlai komɛi hu ni baaye abua kɛtee nitsumɔi ashi koni wala nɛɛ mli shihilɛ hu abadamɔ eŋɛlɛ nɔ ekoŋŋ.
<<<P>>>
Agbɛnɛ hu, kɛtsɔ naagbai kɛ hegbɛnamɔi ni je kpo yɛ hela gbonyo nɛɛ sɛɛ lɛ, Amralofoi lɛ, ni hiɛnyiɛlɔ ji Anumnyam gbɔmɔ Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo lɛ, he lɛ fee oya ni eto nɔyaa nitsumɔ ko he gbɛjianɔ ni akɛ shika GH¢100 billion wo nifeemɔi kɛ nitsumɔi amli bɔ ni afee ni ewo mɛi ahewalɛ yɛ shika gbɛfaŋ kɛsaa nibii ni COVID-19 ebafite lɛ, no ji (the GhanaCARES “Obaatan Pa”) nifeemɔ lɛ. Enɛ ha maŋbii na mumɔhe kɛ hejɔlɛ amrɔ nɔŋŋ ni maŋ lɛ nitsumɔi bɔi enaji anɔ daamɔ ekoŋŋ.
<<<P>>>
Wɔhiɛ esɔ bɔ ni nyɛyɔɔ hemɔkɛyeli yɛ Amralofoi nɛɛ amli yɛ beiaŋ po ni aabɔ mɔdɛŋ koni ato maŋ nitsumɔi ama shi jogbaŋŋ ekoŋŋ yɛ hela gbonyo nɛɛ nifitemɔ lɛ sɛɛ. Nɛkɛ wɔkaa beiaŋ nɛɛ, eji wɔhejaramɔ beiaŋ, ni wɔmiipele koni wɔje haomɔ nɛɛ mli. Ŋmalɛ Krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ tsɔɔ wɔ gbɛ be fɛɛ be yɛ bei ni tamɔ enɛmɛi amli. Taakɛ Yesaia wolo lɛ ye he odase yɛ yitso 9:10 lɛ “Brikisi tsui lɛ ekumɔ, shi wɔ lɛ wɔkɛ tɛ ni ako baatswa tsui lɛ, ni atoo sikamore tsei lɛ ashwie, shi wɔ lɛ, wɔɔtɛo tsenedru tsei yɛ amɛnajiaŋ” (Yesaia 9:10) Amralofoi lɛ eŋmɔ yiŋkpɔ akɛ amɛbaatsu nii kɛye shiwoi lɛ anɔ. Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, abaaka he ni ato gbɛjianɔ kpakpai ni awo mlai ni baaye abua maŋ lɛ najianɔdaamɔ koni no aye abua shihilɛ kpakpa.
<<<P>>>
Afi 2022 Bɔjɛti otii lɛ ateŋ ekome ni akɛbaatsu nii titri yɛ afii etɛ (3) ni baa lɛ mli ji akɛ, atswa nitsumɔi fɛɛ ama shi kpɛŋŋ koni no aye abua shika saji anɔyaa, hewalɛwoo, ni ashiii mɔ ko, koni nitsumɔ fɛɛ nitsumɔ aye emuu, titri lɛ oblahii kɛ oblayei anitsumɔi. Amralofoi amlai lɛ titri baawaje oblahii kɛ oblayei koni aye abua amɛ ni amɛyɔse amɛnikee nibii kɛ nibii, ni amɛbaanyɛ amɛfee kɛwa amɛhe kɛha mɔ fɛɛ mɔ nɔyaa.
<<<P>>>
Koni awo oblahii kɛ oblayei lɛ ahemɔkɛyeli mumɔ mli hewalɛ lɛ, yɛ afi 2022 mli lɛ Amralofoi lɛ baato nifeemɔ ko ni ji “YouStart” lɛ shishi koni no aye abua ni amɛnyɛ amɛna faanii kɛgbele loo kɛwo amɛnitsumɔi lɛ amli.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ 2022 bɔjɛti lɛ mli lɛ akɛ gbɛjianɔtoi komɛi eshwie woji ahiɛ koni atsa nɔ ni amɔ naagbai ni yɔɔ shika saji ahe lɛ anaa, koni anyɛ akɛ hewalɛwoo aje hela lɛ sɛɛ saji ashishi. Amralofoi lɛ miisha amɛhe afɔle ni aagbe he kɛmiiwo nifeemɔi amli hewalɛ koni oblahii kɛ oblayei ana ninenaa kɛ aŋkroaŋkro nitsumɔi amli nɔyaa. Beiaŋ ni wɔboteɔ Wɔhiɛnyiɛlɔ lɛ susumɔi ni kɛ maŋ lɛ baaya hiɛ kɛfo nibalamɔi “Ghana Beyond Aid” mli lɛ, miikpa Ghanabii fɛɛ afai, titri lɛ oblahii kɛ yei, koni amɛha ekɔ amɛhe, amɛjwɛŋ kɛya shɔŋŋ koni amɛkɛ amɛhe awo maŋ nifeemɔi lɛ amli ni no ajaje ni etswa maŋ lɛ nitsumɔi lɛ kɛmamɔ shi ekoŋŋ.
