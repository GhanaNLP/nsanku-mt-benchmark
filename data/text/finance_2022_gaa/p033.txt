78. Akɛni mɔdɛŋbɔɔ akɛ aaana COVID 19 tsofa lɛ, tee nɔ jogbaŋŋ ni ana hetoo kpakpa lɛ, COVID 19 gbuulɛ bafata naamɔmɔ gbɛjianɔtoi ni yɔɔ momo (ni akɛtsuɔ nii ni jeee tsofai ni) lɛ he lɛ, ni nomɛi ji face mask woo, mɔ kɛ mɔ teŋ gbɛ kɛ dɛfɔmɔ falefale.

79. COVID-19 gbuulɛ kɛnitsumɔ ni aje shishi yɛ Otsokrikri, 2021 nɔ kɛ tsofa AstraZeneca vaccine 600,000, ni akɛgbu mɛi fe miliɔŋ 2.5 ni mɔ fɛɛ mɔ na kɛhooo kwraa lɛ gbuu shi kome kɛbashi Alemle, 2021. Mɛi ni akwɛɔ yɛ gbuulɛ nɛɛ mli lɛ ji mɛi ni ena kɛjɛ afii 18 kɛyaa shi yei hɔlɔi bɛ mli, ni amrɔ nɛɛ lɛ akɛtee Kpokpa wuji 16 kɛ kpokpa bibii 261 lɛ fɛɛ amli yɛ jakumaŋ lɛ mli.
