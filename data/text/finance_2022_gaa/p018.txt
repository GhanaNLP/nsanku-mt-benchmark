24. Yɛ Amralofoi lɛ asusumɔi otii nyɔŋma komɛi ni kɔɔ nitsumɔi amligbelemɔ (10-Point Industrialization Agenda) lɛ nɔ lɛ, abaawo Ghana nitsumɔi bibii ni sɔɔ nibii ni amɛfeɔ fɛɛ ahewalɛ bɔ ni afee ni akaŋshii aba nitsumɔi srɔtoi lɛ fɛɛ amli yɛ shɛɛ nibii kpakpai afeemɔ kɛ ehe jarayelii fɛɛ amli koni no aye abua kɛgbele gbɛ aha amɛ ni amɛnitsumɔi lɛ adamɔ enaji anɔ kɛfee nibii babaoo kɛwo shɛɛ nibii lɛ anajiaŋ. Yɛ afi 2021 mli lɛ, Amralofoi lɛ je nifeemɔ ko shishi yɛ Noguchi, ni gbɛi ni ka nɔ ji ‘Bioequivalence Centre’ koni akɛye abua tsofafeelɔi yɛ Ghana maŋ nɛɛ mli. Nibii ni baa nɛɛ ji nɔ ni abaafee yɛ afi 2022 lɛ mli:
<<<P>>>
• Abaatsake jarayeli kuramɔ gbɛjianɔtoi ahiɛ, kɛtsɔ ‘Registrar General Department’ nɔ lɛ akɛ ‘digitization’ baakura nitsumɔi fɛɛ yɛ amɛnifeemɔi amli koni amɛye emuu;
<<<P>>>
• Abaaje nifeemɔ ko shishi kɛmamɔ nitsumɔi yɛ he kome (Nitsumɔ paakii agboi yɛ kpokpai anɔ) koni wɔtɛkinikal univɛsitii lɛ kɛ amɛ atsa yaa kɛkase nii; agbɛnɛ hu
<<<P>>>
• Abaagbele tsofai ni afee yɛ maŋ nɛɛ mli hemɔ mli koni amɛkɛ nitsumɔi lɛ atsa yaa kɛwa nibii ni akɛfeɔ tsofai lɛ anamɔ ‘Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients’ (API).

25. Amralofoi lɛ miibɔ amɛhe mɔdɛŋ akɛ amɛbaaya amɛhiɛ yɛ kpaa nɔ shɛɛyeli nifeemɔi amli (digitalisation drive). Kɛtsɔ enɛ nɔ lɛ Amralofoi lɛ eha akadi Ghanabii ni shɛɔ miliɔŋ 15 ni aha amɛ kaadii ni yeɔ amɛhe odase ni ji (identification cards) ni aha shiai ni shɛɔ 7.5 miliɔŋ yibɔi yɛ dade kaadii anɔ kɛkadi shia he, no hu ji (national digital address system).
