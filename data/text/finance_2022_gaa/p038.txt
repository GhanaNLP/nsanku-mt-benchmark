“Aatse Nitsumɔi Kɛmɔ shi: Maŋ shikai anaabuamɔ kɛ Nitsumɔtsemɔ”
<<<P>>>
NABCo (Jaku lɛ Tswaa Kuu): ni ale lɛ hu akɛ NABCO lɛ, eji yiŋtoo ko ni Amralofoi lɛ kɛba koni atsɔ nɔ akɛhe univɛsitibii ni bɛ nitsumɔi lɛ ni amɛwa kɛkura shihilɛ mli haomɔi yɛ Amralofoi kɛ aŋkroaŋkro nitsumɔi amli koni amɛna nitsumɔi amli hesalɛ kɛha wɔsɛɛ nitsumɔ yɛ maŋ nɛɛ mli kɛye abua maŋ nɔyaa.
<<<P>>>
NBSSI (Maŋ Ku Ni Kwɛɔ Nitsumɔi Bibii Anɔ): Maŋ Ku Ni Kwɛɔ Nitsumɔi Bibii Anɔ: Eji ku ni yɔɔ Guɔyeli kɛ Nitsumɔi Sɔɔmɔhe lɛ shishi. Amɛnitsumɔ ji amɛkwɛ nitsumɔi bibii (MSEs) lɛ anɔyaa
<<<P>>>
Maŋ shika Fɛɛ ni ejɛɛɛ muŋmɔŋ mli: Eji shikai abɔ ni anaa yɛ maŋ nitsumɔi amli ni Muŋmɔŋ shika bɛ mli. Adamɔɔ nɔ kɛnaa shika jarawalɛ yɛ maŋ lɛ mli.
<<<P>>>
Shika ni Jeee Toohemɔ kɛba: No ji shika ni Amralofoi lɛ naa kɛjɛɔ nibii ni too bɛ nɔ mli. Ekomɛi ji gbɛŋmɛɛ woji ahe nyɔmɔwoo, nɔ ko feemɔ he nyɔmɔwoo, sɛɛnamɔi kɛ ekrokomɛi.
<<<P>>>
Gbɛkpamɔi Krokomɛi: Gbɛi krokomɛi ni akpaa lɛ jɛɔ Amralofoi anitsumɔhei krokomɛi (MDAs) lɛ adɛŋ kɛyaa Sɔɔmɔhe ni Kwɛɔ Maŋ Shikai anɔ lɛ dɛŋ kɛ nyɔmɔi krokomɛi ni ahiɛ maŋ fotroi komɛi lɛ.
<<<P>>>
Srɔto ni ka shika ni anaa kɛ nɔ ni afiteɔ lɛ yakatswaa teŋ : Enɛ ji srɔto ni yɔɔ shika ni maŋ naa kɛ nɔ ni efiteɔ lɛ yakatswaa ni anaa he shika sɛɛ ko lɛ teŋ. Faa shika he nyɔmɔwoo bɛ mli. Akɛ enɛ susuɔ shika fɛɛ ni maŋ lɛ baana.
<<<P>>>
Nɔyaa ni akɛfɔ hiɛ: Bɔ ni Ghana Shika he gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ baaya lɛ aha yɛ afi mli.
<<<P>>>
Shika ni baa: Shika fɛɛ ni maŋ na kɛha enifeemɔi kɛji okɛ nɔ ni ana yɛ maŋ nɛɛ mli kɛ maŋsɛɛ fata he
<<<P>>>
RFJ (Kooloi Lɛɛ akɛ Nitsumɔ): Amralofoi lɛ anifeemɔ kɛ mɔdɛŋbɔɔ ji enɛ, koni akaŋshii aba kooloi alɛɛ nitsumɔ lɛ mli ni eha wɔnyɛ wɔna kooloi babaoo ni no aye abua ni wɔtse kooloi ni ashɛɔ kɛjɛɔ maŋsɛɛ lɛ nɔ, ni mɛi ana nitsumɔ kɛ nɔyaa yɛ kooloi alɛɛ mli.
<<<P>>>
Maŋbii yelikɛbuamɔ : Eji shika ni Amralofoi lɛ woɔ kɛgbɔɔ nibii komɛi tamɔ srawa hewalɛ, nu kɛ pɛtro he nyɔmɔ ni maŋbii bɔɔ lɛ nɔ.
<<<P>>>
Yelikɛbuamɔ Shika: Eji shika ni Amralofoi woɔ kɛbaa nɔ ko jara shi koni maŋbii ana hejɔlɛ.
