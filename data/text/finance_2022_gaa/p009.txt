Shikataomɔ Gbɛjianɔtoo
<<<P>>>
4. Shika ni ana kɛ keenii anaa yakpa GH¢46.85 biliɔŋ (10.7% of GDP) kɛjɛ Aharabata nyɔɔŋ lɛ nɔ kɛyashi Gbo 2021, ni akɛ enɛ to nitsumɔi kɛ nifeemɔi komɛi ni aakpa gbɛ lɛ ahe, ni ehe nyɔmɔ naa yakpa GH¢51.31 biliɔŋ (11.7% GDP). Shika fɛɛ ni ana lɛ mligbalamɔ ji shika ni muŋmɔŋ nɔ too bɛ mli naa yakpa (74%), shika ni ejɛɛɛ muŋmɔŋ loo too hu mli naa yakpa (9%), muŋmɔŋ kɛ la kɔɔyɔɔ shikai ni ana yafee (9%), shikai krokomɛi fee (6%) ni keenii hu fee (2%) tamɔ bɔ ni klɛŋklɛŋ Tɛŋmɔ lɛ yeɔ he odase lɛ.
<<<P>>>
5. Shika fɛɛ ni ahe yɛ 2021 afi lɛ mli ni ba lɛ, kɛji okɛto shika gbɛ ni akpaa lɛ he lɛ, jɛ mɔdɛŋbɔɔ ni nitsulɔi kɛwooo too nitsumɔi lɛ amli, too he nyɔmɔwo, ojarawa tɛi ahe too, toi ni aheɔ yɛ nibii ni afeefei yɛ maŋ nɛɛ mli ahe, kɛ kpaatswaa nifeemɔi ahe too.
