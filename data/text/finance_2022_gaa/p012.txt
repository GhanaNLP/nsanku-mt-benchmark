9. Shika gbɛjianɔtoo he mlai kɛ nitsumɔi agbɛkpamɔ otii ni ato kɛha afi 2022:
<<<P>>>
• Shika fɛɛ ni akpaa gbɛ akɛ abaana yɛ afi lɛ mli (GDP) lɛ aya ŋwɛi oha mlijaa 5.8 (5.8 %)
<<<P>>>
• Shika ni abaana ni Muŋmɔŋ shika bɛ mli (GDP) lɛ aya ŋwɛi oha mlijaa 5.9 (5.9 %);
<<<P>>>
• Afi lɛ naagbee mli shika jara ayadamɔ oha mlijaa 8 (8%)
<<<P>>>
• Bɔjeti lɛ sɛɛ nyɔmɔbɔɔ ashɛ oha mlijaa 7.9 (7.9%) GDP.
<<<P>>>
• Shika ni baashwɛ yɛ GDP lɛ sɛɛ ashɛ oha mlijaa 0.4 (0.4%);
<<<P>>>
• Maŋsɛɛ shika ni akɛbaato ni anyɛ akɛdamɔ kɛ hooo kwraa lɛ nyɔji 4 shɛɛ nibii anaa.

10. Too kɛ keenii yɛ afi 2022 lɛ fɛɛ naa ayakpa GH¢100.52 biliɔŋ (20.0% GDP). Yɛ enɛ mli lɛ, shika ni abaana yɛ maŋ nɛɛ mli naa ayakpa GH¢99.55 biliɔŋ, nibii ni muŋmɔŋ shika bɛ mli atoo afee oha mlijaa 77.5% ni ehe shika naa ayashɛ GH¢77.136 million. Aakpa gbɛ akɛ nɔyaa aba yɛ too kɛ shikahemɔ gbɛjianɔtoi kɛ ekuramɔ mli koni no aye abua shika ni abaabua naa yɛ afi 2022 lɛ mli.
