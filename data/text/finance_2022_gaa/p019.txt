27. Amralofoi lɛ je nifeemɔ ko shishi koni akɛtswa he ko ni abaafee tsofai ni ahaa mɛi kɛbɛjeɔ amɛ yɛ helai srɔtoi ahe ama shi (‘Ghana Vaccine Manufacturing Centre’). Etsɛŋ tsɔ ni mla woji ni ji ‘National Vaccine Policy’ lɛ baaje kpo ni abaato ehe nikasemɔhe ni ji ‘National Vaccine Institute’ lɛ hu ama shi koni amɛkwɛ gbuulɛ tsofai afeemɔ lɛ nɔ. Akɛ ‘National Health Insurance Scheme’ bii ni kwɛɔ woji ni akɛyaa helatsamɔhe he gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ hu baatsa yaa, koni Amralofoi lɛ, kɛtsɔ ‘Agenda 111’ nifeemɔ lɛ nɔ lɛ awo helatsamɔhei lɛ ahewalɛ kɛna amɛhiamɔ nibii ni no ajaje amɛshika saji hu ni eye emuu be ni 2030 afi lɛ aaashɛ lɛ.

28. Bɔ ni afee ni anyɛ amɔ oblahii kɛ oblayei anitsumɔ kɛ ninenaa nitsumɔi ahe naagbai anaa lɛ, Amralofoi lɛ kɛtsɔ gbɛjianɔtoo titri komɛi tamɔ NEIP, NABCO, National Afforestation kɛ NBSSI ni agbɛnɛ atsɛɔ amɛ (GEA) lɛ anɔ lɛ, gbɛ egbele kɛha oblaŋi anitsumɔnamɔ.

29. Kɛtsɔ maŋbii kɛ mɛi ni ekɔɔ amɛhe eko ni kɛfata he hu oblaŋi akui kɛ tsɔsemɔhei srɔtoi asusumɔi trilii anɔ lɛ, Amralofoi lɛ baaje nifeemɔ ko ni gbɛi ji ‘YouStart’ lɛ shishi yɛ afi 2022 lɛ mli koni no aye abua mɛi akpetoi akpe kome (miliɔŋ 1) koni atse nitsumɔi aha mɛi akpetoi akpe kome (miliɔŋ 1) kɛtsɔ GhanaCARES nifeemɔ lɛ nɔ yɛ afii etɛ mli. ‘YouStart’ lɛ baaye abua oblahii kɛ oblayei ni egbele amɛnitsumɔi koni amɛna jwetri ni mlitsii, tsɔsemɔ, ninenaa nitsumɔ he tsɔsemɔi kɛ gbɛi anɔ ni atsɔɔ akwɛɔ nifeemɔ ko nɔ jogbaŋŋ he tsɔsemɔ koni no aye abua amɛ ni amɛnyɛ amɛgbele ni amɛtsu amɛ diɛŋtsɛ amɛnitsumɔi.
