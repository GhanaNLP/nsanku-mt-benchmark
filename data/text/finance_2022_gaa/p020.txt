31. Ghana miibɔ mɔdɛŋ akɛ kɛtsɔ tsei adumɔ nifeemɔi anɔ lɛ abaagu koogbaa kwraa koni koowiei akulamɔ amamɔ shi kpɛŋŋ. Yɛ enɛfeemɔ mli lɛ, Amralofoi lɛ miito nifeemɔ ko shishi kɛha koowiei agbaa guu ni no aye abua kɛfo koowiei ahe afabaŋ kɛkula shikpɔji ni agbaa yɛ amɛnɔ lɛ kɛtsɔ nifeemɔi nɛɛ anɔ lɛ abaakula, akwɛ, ni afee koni akanya kooŋ kɛ kooloi kakawuai ashihilɛ nifeemɔi fɛɛ.
<<<P>>>
32. Bɔ ni afee ni anyɛ aku naagbai ni yɔɔ tsei afolɔmɔ kɛ koowiei gbaa anaa lɛ, Maŋ hiɛnyiɛlɔ lɛ diɛŋtsɛ nyiɛ maŋ muu lɛ fɛɛ hiɛ ni adudui tsei akpetoi akpei kpawo (seven million) kɛ sɛɛ yɛ Otukwajaŋ nyɔɔŋ lɛ nɔ yɛ afi 2021 lɛ mli. Enɛ yɛ hiɛ fe akpetoi akpei enumɔ (five million) ni asusu he ni aafee maŋ lɛ fɛɛ eŋɔli (Green Ghana Project) nifeemɔ ni teenɔ lɛ.
