63. Yɛ Nidumɔ kɛha Maŋsɛɛ Majemɔ kɛ Akrowaiaŋ anɔyaa gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ naa lɛ, Amralofoi lɛ ja atia wui, coffee, akokooshi, tɛŋ, maŋo kɛ rɔba ni abuu 1,360,589 eha okwaafoi 7,543 yɛ Ahafo, Ashanti, Bono, Teŋ, Boka, Greater Accra, Oti, Anai, Anai Yiteŋ kɛ Shwilao Kpokpai lɛ anɔ bii lɛ.

64. Amralofoi lɛ gbe kooloi alɛɛhei 11 lɛ mli 10 naa kɛ nubui ni ashiɔ 11 aha kooloi alɛɛhei 11. Aja otswashiogbo tsofai 7,700,500 aha wuɔ lɛɛhei koni amɛkɛkura wuɔhelai ni baa kɛ lɛɛkooloi krokomɛi lɛ.

65. Amralofoi lɛ tee nɔ saasaa nianɔnushwamɔ tsɔji ni amɛmiitsake hei ni baa nɛɛ atsɔji lɛ ahiɛ, ni kɛji agbe naa lɛ abaanyɛ atsu nii yɛ shikpɔŋ 6,766ha muu lɛ fɛɛ nɔ ni akpa nii yɛ afi muu lɛ fɛɛ mli: Kpong Nianɔnushwamɔ Nitsumɔ lɛ (2,176ha), Tono Nianɔnushwamɔ Nitsumɔ lɛ (2,490ha) kɛ Kpong Left Bank Nianɔnushwamɔ Nitsumɔ lɛ (2,100ha).
