Kee Shika: Nomɛi ji shika ni maŋ nɔyaa he yelikɛbualɔi kɛkeɔ Amralofoi lɛ. Akpaleee loo awooo nakai shikai lɛ ahaaa mɛi ni kɛke lɛ.
<<<P>>>
Kee Shika Kɛha Amralofoi Anitsumɔ Gbeji : Nomɛi ji shikai ni afolɔɔ ahaa nitsumɔhei komɛi yɛ mla naa. Shikai lɛ ekomɛi ji District Assemblies’ Common Fund, (DACF), Road Fund, Ghana Education Trust Fund (GETFUND), Petroleum Related Fund and National Health Fund
<<<P>>>
Maŋ Shika Ni Ana Yikulɛ (GDP): No tsɔɔ shika fɛɛ ni abaana kɛha maŋ nitsumɔi lɛ ajara yɛ be ko mli
<<<P>>>
Maŋsɛɛ Shika Ni Akɛto: Akɛ enɛ susuɔ maŋ lɛ shika ni abaanyɛ adamɔ nɔ kɛshɛ maŋsɛɛ nibii yɛ be ko mli. Akɛpiaa shika ni ayɔɔ yɛ maŋ lɛ mli.
<<<P>>>
Shika Jarawalɛ: No tsɔɔ nibii anɔŋmɛɛ kɛ nibii abɔ ni shika baanyɛ ahe yɛ be ko mli.
<<<P>>>
IPEP (Infrastructure for Poverty Eradication Programme): Japadei ni baaha agbe ohia ji NPP Amralofoi lɛ agbɛjianɔtoi lɛ ekome ni yiŋtoo ji akɛ, amɛbaagbe ohia koni amɛtse gbɛ ni ka ohiafoi kɛ shikatsɛmɛi ateŋ lɛ nɔ kwraa, titri lɛ yɛ akrowai amli kɛ hei ni nɔyaa faaa yɛ kɛtsɔ nɔyaa nibii agbelemɔ yɛ hei nɛɛ.
<<<P>>>
Gbɛkpamɔ kɛha Nitsumɔ Nɔyaa Otii Wuji (Macroeconomic targets): Eji susumɔnɔ kɛha maŋ lɛ shika shidaamɔ. Nɔkwɛmɔnii komɛi ji nitsumɔi lɛ anɔyaa kɛ emli tsakemɔi, shika jarawalɛ, nitsulɔi ahemɔ mli tsakemɔi, wɔkɛ maji krokomɛi guɔyeli nɔyaa, Amralofoi lɛ ashika gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ omanyeyeli loo shigbeemɔi, kɛ Bank of Ghana yiŋtoi ni kɔɔ shikahamɔ kɛ ejaa yɛ nitsumɔi ahe
<<<P>>>
MSME (Nitsumɔi Pɛmpɛbii kɛ bibii Anɔyaa Gbɛjianɔtoo. (Micro And Small Buisness Developmet Programme)): Eji nitsumɔi bibii ni heɔ mɛi fioo ni yɔɔ akɔntaabuu wolo loo ni tsɔɔ sɛɛnamɔi ni shɛɛɛ shika falɛ ko
