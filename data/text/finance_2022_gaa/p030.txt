67. Kɛbashi 2021 Gbo mli lɛ, nitoohei 30 ni abɔi maamɔ lɛ, atsɔ Kpokpa kome nitoohe kome gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ naa kɛgbe emli 23 naa. Ato gbɛjianɔ ni akɛbaagbe 7 ni eshwɛ lɛ naa yɛ afi 2021 nɛɛ naagbee mli. Nɛkɛ nanefaa nɛɛ baaye abua kɛto niyenii falɛ ni shɛɔ 30,000mt ni aakpa gbɛ akɛ abaana yɛ dumɔ nibii amɛi yɛ PFJ gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ naa.
