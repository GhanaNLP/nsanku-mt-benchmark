37. Jaku lɛ tsei adumɔ gbɛjianɔtoo (NAP) ha hegbɛ koni ahe mɛi 60,000 koni amɛye amɛbua yɛ tsei adumɔ nitsumɔi lɛ amli fɛɛ. National Youth Employment bii 35,000 fata mɛi ni abaahe yɛ tsei adumɔ gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ he, ni abaahe mɛi 17,000 yɛ “Modified Taungya System (MTS)” gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ naa kɛ mɛi 2,000 ni baaye abua wui lɛ anamɔ. Aŋkroaŋkro bii lɛ hu baahe mɛi aaafee 18,000.

38. Youth Employment Agency (YEA) lɛ baahe mɛi 82,869 awo YEA gbeji srɔtoi lɛ amli. He ni ahalaa mɛi yɛ “The Job Centre”, ni taoɔ mɛi yahaa nitsumɔtsɛmɛi lɛ tsɔse mɛi 1,185 ni efee klalo kɛha nitsumɔ.

39. Jɛ haomɔi ni COVID 19 hela lɛ kɛba lɛ fɛɛ sɛɛ lɛ, kunimyeli yɛ nɔyaa nibii amli kahe tee nɔ kɛtsɔ 1D1F gbɛjiaanɔtoi lɛ anɔ. Kɛjɛ 2017 afi lɛ nɔ kɛba shi ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, 1D1F nitsumɔi (kɔmpɛnei) 278 ni aatsu he nii, ni amɛshɛ ŋɛlɛi srɔtoi anɔ, agbe enɛ mli 106 anaa ni aatsu nii yɛ mli, aatsu 148 he nii lolo be mli ni 24 yɛ gbɛ nɔ ni abaaje shishi. Kɛba shi ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, kɔmpɛnei ni amamɔ lɛ eha mɛi 156,782 tuuŋtu kɛ sɛɛsɛɛ bii anitsumɔ, ni nitsumɔi ni amamɔ 2021 nɛɛ eha mɛi nɛɛ ateŋ 17,451 sɔŋŋ nitsumɔ.
