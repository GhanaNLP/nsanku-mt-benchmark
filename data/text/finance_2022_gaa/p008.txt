Nitsumɔ nɔyaa oti (Macroeconomic) wuji
<<<P>>>
3. Shika he gbɛjianɔtoi ni yɔɔ nifeemɔi lɛ ahe yɛ 2021afi lɛ mli lɛ nɛ:
<<<P>>>
• Shika fɛɛ ni ana yɛ afi lɛ mlijaa lɛ mli lɛ naa yakpa (GDP) lɛ mli oha mlijaa etɛ kɛ fa (3.5%) kɛji akɛto 2020 nakai beiaŋ nɔ nɔŋŋ ji (0.7%) lɛ;
<<<P>>>
• Muŋmɔŋ Shika ni ana yɛ afi lɛ mli hu (GDP) tee ŋwɛi ni enaa yakpa oha mlijaa (4.8%) yɛ afi 2021 klɛŋklɛŋ mlijaa lɛ mli kɛji akɛto (1.1%) ni ana yɛ nakai beiaŋ nɔŋŋ yɛ afi 2020 lɛ mli.
<<<P>>>
• Gbo nyɔɔŋ lɛ naagbee mli shika jarawalɛ yashɛ oha mlijaa 10.6 ( 10.6%) kɛji akɛto nakai beiaŋ nɔŋŋ yɛ 2020 afi lɛ nɔ lɛ he.
<<<P>>>
• Shika gbɛjianɔtoo loo bɔjɛti tso muu lɛ fɛɛ sɛɛ nyɔmɔ yashɛ GDP lɛ mli oha mlijaa 7.7 (7.7 %) kɛji akɛto gbɛkpamɔ nɔ ni ji oha mlijaa 7.4 (7.4 %) (GDP) lɛ he.
<<<P>>>
• Esɛɛ nyɔmɔ ni ana lɛ naa yakpa GDP lɛ mli oha mlijaa 2.0 (2.0 % GDP lɛ he) kɛji akɛto gbɛkpamɔ nɔ ni ji oha mlijaa 1.7 (1.7% GDP lɛ he).
<<<P>>>
• Maŋ nyɔmɔ ni ba fɛɛ lɛ naa yakpa 77.7 (77.7% GDP lɛ he) be ni shɛɔ Gbo 2021 nyɔɔŋ lɛ naagbee, kɛjɛ 76.1 (76.1%) yɛ afi 2020 Afuabe lɛ naagbee mli;
<<<P>>>
• Maŋsɛɛ shika ni akɛto kɛjɛ afi 2021 Manyawale nyɔɔŋ lɛ naagbee mli lɛ hu naa yakpa ‘US$11.44 biliɔŋ’, enɛ fa bɔ ni ebaashɛ nyɔji nyɔŋmai enumɔ kɛ enyɔ shɛɛ nibii asɛɛdamɔ kɛji akɛto US$8.62 biliɔŋ yikulɛ ni ji nyɔji 4.1 shɛɛ nibii ni ayɔse yɛ afi 2020 Afuabe lɛ naagbee mli lɛ he; ni
<<<P>>>
• Maŋsɛɛ shikai srɔtoi fɛɛ ni atsakeɔ lɛ ajara yadamɔ he ni eyɔɔ nɔŋŋ yɛ afi 2021 lɛ mlijaa etɛ lɛ mli yɛ maji wuji fɛɛ amli. Ghana cedi lɛ gbee shi kɛ jarayeli yɛ maŋsɛɛ shika US Dollar mli kɛbadamɔ oha mlijaa 1.7 (1.7%) nɔ yɛ afi 2021, Gbo nyɔɔŋ lɛ naagbee mli kɛji akɛto shika lɛ shigbeemɔ he kɛba oha mlijaa 2.9 (2.9%) yɛ nakai beiaŋ nɔŋŋ yɛ afi 2020 lɛ mli.
