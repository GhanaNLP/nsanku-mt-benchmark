Wɔ lɛ, wɔhe wɔye ni wɔná wɔle hu akɛ bo ji Mɔ Krɔŋkrɔŋ ni jɛ Nyɔŋmɔ ŋɔɔ lɛ.” (Yoh. 6:68, 69) Eenyɛ efee akɛ, Petro kɛ bɔfoi krokomɛi lɛ hu nuuu nɔ ni Yesu tsɔɔ be ni ewie akɛ esa akɛ amɛye eheloo lɛ ni amɛnu elá lɛ shishi. 4 Wɔkɛ shihilɛi komɛi kpeɔ ni biɔ ni wɔtsɔɔ kɛji wɔyeɔ Yehowa anɔkwa loo wɔyeee lɛ anɔkwa. Shihilɛi lɛ ekome ji, be ni Yehowa asafo lɛ etsake bɔ ni wɔnuɔ ŋmalɛ ko shishi wɔhãa loo gbɛ ni wɔtsɔɔ nɔ wɔshiɛɔ sane kpakpa lɛ. Ekroko hu ji, be ni sane fɔŋ ko enina wɔ ni wɔnuuu nɔ hewɔ ni Yehowa ŋmɛ gbɛ ni eba wɔnɔ lɛ shishi. Shihilɛi lɛ eko hu ji, be ni mɔ ko ni wɔsumɔɔ esane waa lɛ efee nɔ ko ni edɔ wɔ, loo be ni wɔmiinu he akɛ mɔ ko ni wɔbuɔ lɛ waa lɛ kɛ wɔ yeee jogbaŋŋ.
<<<P>>>
5 Kɛ́ wɔkɛ shihilɛi ni mli wawai miikpe lɛ, nɔ ni baaye abua wɔ ni wɔya nɔ wɔye Yehowa anɔkwa ji, ni wɔbaahe lɛ wɔye waa. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, kɛ́ wɔmiima tsũ yɛ he ko ni ewaaa akɛ shikpɔŋhosomɔ baaba yɛ jɛmɛ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔtse wɔhe wɔtswa faneshi lɛ jogbaŋŋ. Yɛ nakai gbɛ nɔŋŋ nɔ lɛ, kɛ́ wɔkɛ shihilɛi ni mli wawai tamɔ nɔ ni wɔtsĩ tã yɛ kuku ejwɛ lɛ mli lɛ miikpe lɛ, ja wɔheɔ Yehowa nɔ wɔyeɔ waa dani wɔbaanyɛ wɔya nɔ wɔye lɛ anɔkwa. No hewɔ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔya nɔ wɔfee nibii ni baahã wɔhe Yehowa nɔ wɔye waa koni wɔya nɔ wɔsɔmɔ lɛ, ekɔɔɔ he eko nɔ ni baaba.—1 Kor. 15:58; kanemɔ Kolosebii 1:23.
