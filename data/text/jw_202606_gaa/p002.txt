NƆ NI WƆBAAKASE
Wɔbaanyɛ wɔya nɔ wɔye Yehowa anɔkwa ekɔɔɔ he eko naagbai ni wɔkɛbaa-kpe.
<<<P>>>
Mɛni Wɔbaanyɛ Wɔfee Koni Wɔya Nɔ Wɔye Anɔkwa Kɛ́ Wɔkɛ Naagbai Miikpe?
<<<P>>>
“Nuŋtsɔ, namɔ ŋɔɔ wɔbaaya? Bo oyɔɔ naanɔ wala wiemɔi lɛ.”
—YOH. 6:68.
<<<P>>>
GBI KO LƐ, Yesu miitsɔɔ ekaselɔi komɛi nii yɛ Kapernaum. Be ni etsɔɔ amɛ nii lɛ kɛshɛ he ko lɛ, ekɛɛ amɛ akɛ: “Kɛ́ji nyɛyeee gbɔmɔ Bi lɛ heloo lɛ, ni nyɛnuuu elá lɛ, nyɛbɛ wala yɛ nyɛmli. Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni yeɔ miheloo ni enuɔ milá lɛ yɛ naa-nɔ wala, ni matee lɛ shi yɛ naagbee gbi lɛ nɔ.” (Yoh. 6:53, 54) Ekaselɔi lɛ ateŋ mɛi pii nyaaa nɔ ni ewie nɛɛ he. Amɛ-wie akɛ: “Wiemɔ ni wa nɛ; namɔ baanyɛ abo toi?”—Yoh. 6:60.
<<<P>>>
2 Kaselɔi lɛ ni eyanyaaa nɔ ni Yesu wie lɛ he lɛ kpa esɛɛ-nyiɛmɔ. Ani no mli lɛ, amɛbɛ hemɔkɛyeli ko kwraa yɛ emli? Yɛ gbi ni tsɔ gbi nɛɛ hiɛ lɛ mli lɛ, Yesu fee naakpɛɛ nii ko ni ekaselɔi lɛ anaa kpɛ amɛhe aahu akɛ, amɛwie akɛ: “Mɔ nɛ ji Gbalɔ ni baa je lɛ mli lɛ lɛɛlɛŋ.” (Yoh. 6:14) Enɛ tsɔɔ akɛ, amɛhe Yesu nɔ amɛye. Shi be ni Yesu wie nɔ ko ni amɛya-nuuu shishi lɛ, amɛkpa esɛɛnyiɛmɔ.
