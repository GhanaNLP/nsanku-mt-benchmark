YEHOWA ODASEFOI JI MƐI NI YEHOWA KƐMIITSU NII ŊMƐNƐ
<<<P>>>
13 Kɛ́ wɔjwɛŋmɔ bɔi wɔ enyɔnyɔɔnyɔ feemɔ akɛ Yehowa Odasefoi ji mɛi ni Yehowa kɛmiitsu nii ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔfee nɔ ko yɛ he oya nɔŋŋ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔbi wɔhe saji tamɔ: ‘Namɛi bɔɔ mɔdɛŋ akɛ amɛkɛ nɔ ni Biblia lɛ wieɔ lɛ baatsu nii? Namɛi ni hãa nibii fɛɛ ni amɛheɔ amɛyeɔ lɛ damɔɔ Biblia lɛ nɔ?’ (2 Tim. 4:3, 4) ‘Namɛi feɔ Yehowa suɔmɔnaa nii ŋmɛnɛ?’​—Mat. 7:20, 21.
<<<P>>>
14 Nyɔŋmɔ miisumɔ ni mɔ fɛɛ mɔ anu sane kpakpa lɛ dani ekpãtã jeŋ fɔŋ nɛɛ hiɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔbi wɔhe akɛ, ‘Namɛi shiɛɔ sane kpakpa lɛ ŋmɛnɛ?’ (Mat. 24:14) Eyɛ faŋŋ akɛ, Yehowa miitsɔ Yehowa Odasefoi anɔ eetsu enɛ he nii. Yehowa Odasefoi pɛ ni shiɛɔ Nyɔŋmɔ Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ he sane kpakpa lɛ yɛ maji ni fa fe 240 anɔ. Wɔtsu nitsumɔ nɛɛ nɔ ni fa fe afii 100. Anɔkwa, shiɛmɔ kɛ kaselɔfeemɔ nitsumɔ lɛ ji nitsumɔ ni he hiaa wɔ fe fɛɛ.​—Mat. 28:19, 20.
<<<P>>>
15 Kanemɔ Yesaia 43:10 kɛ Mateo 6:9. Atsɛɔ wɔ Yehowa Odasefoi, ni eji hegbɛ kpele akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛ́i lɛ kã wɔnɔ. Ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, mɛi pii leee akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛ́i ji Yehowa. Shi wɔbɔ mɔdɛŋ waa wɔtsɔɔ Biblia lɛ shishi kɛtee wiemɔi pii amli, ni wɔkɛ Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛ́i lɛ ewo he fɛɛ he ni esa akɛ eje kpo yɛ. Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛ́i lɛ jeɔ kpo nɔ ni fa fe shii 7,000 sɔŋŋ yɛ Ŋmalɛ Krɔŋkrɔŋ Lɛ​—Jeŋ Hee Shishitsɔɔmɔ lɛ mli.
