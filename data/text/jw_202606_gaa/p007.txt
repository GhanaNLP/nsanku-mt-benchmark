KAASHI YEHOWA MASƐI
<<<P>>>
17 Akɛni wɔyɛ naagbee gbii lɛ amli hewɔ lɛ, wɔkɛ shihilɛi ni mli wawai baakpe. (2 Tim. 3:1, 13) Ni ekolɛ, nibii krokomɛi baaba ni baahã wɔhao. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, asafo lɛ kɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔi komɛi baanyɛ ahã ni ekolɛ wɔnuuu shishi jogbaŋŋ. Aloo nyɛmi ko baanyɛ afee nɔ ko ni baagba wɔnaa. Ekolɛ wɔleee bɔ ni wɔbaafee wɔtsu wɔnaagbai nɛɛ ahe nii, loo ekolɛ loloolo lɛ, wɔnuuu nɔ hewɔ ni asafo lɛ kɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔi komɛi hã lɛ shishi.
<<<P>>>
18 Eyɛ mli akɛ saji nɛɛ baanyɛ ahã wɔhao moŋ, shi wɔbaanyɛ wɔya nɔ wɔye Yehowa anɔkwa. Mɛni hewɔ? Ejaakɛ wɔle akɛ lɛ ji mɔ ni bɔ nibii fɛɛ, ewiemɔi ni yɔɔ Biblia lɛ mli, ni akɛ, Yehowa Odasefoi ji ewebii. No hewɔ lɛ, bɔ ni Petro kɛɛ Yesu lɛ, wɔ hu wɔbaanyɛ wɔkɛɛ Yehowa akɛ: “Namɔ ŋɔɔ wɔbaaya? Bo oyɔɔ naanɔ wala wiemɔi lɛ.”
