D. Miitsu kpokpaa nɔkwɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ, afi 1990 kɛmiimɔ afi 2000
E. Klɛŋklɛŋ kpokpaa wulu nɔ kpee ni wɔfee be ni ajie naatsii lɛ yɛ wɔnɔ
<<<P>>>
Nɔyeli lɛ yɛ nitsumɔhei pii ni afeɔ sikli yɛ jɛmɛ. Miná nitsumɔ yɛ nitsumɔhei nɛɛ eko mli, ni ehe bahia ni mikɛ mibe fɛɛ atsu. Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, mikɛ Emilia fɛɛ nyɛɛɛ asɔmɔ akɛ gbegbalɔi dɔŋŋ; shi loloolo lɛ, wɔmiisumɔ ni wɔtsu babaoo bɔ ni wɔɔnyɛ yɛ Yehowa sɔɔmɔ lɛ mli. No hewɔ lɛ, mibi ni ahã matsu nii kɛjɛ maŋkɛ ŋmɛji etɛ kɛya-shi leebi ŋmɛlɛ 11 yɛ nitsumɔhe lɛ. Minyaaa maŋ-kɛshitee he kwraa, shi nakai ni mifee lɛ hã minyɛ mishiɛ daa ni minyɛ mikɛ Emilia tee asafoŋ kpeei daa.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ 1969 lɛ, wɔfɔ wɔkromɔ binuu ni wɔwo lɛ Gustavo. No sɛɛ etsɛɛɛ lɛ, asafo lɛ kɛɛ miyasɔmɔ akɛ kpokpaa nɔkwɛlɔ. Yɛ nakai beiaŋ yɛ Cuba lɛ, anyɛɔ ahãa mɔ sɔmɔɔ akɛ kpokpaa nɔkwɛlɔ be ni eekwɛ ebii. Yɛ afii ni mikɛsɔmɔ akɛ kpokpaa nɔkwɛlɔ lɛ amli lɛ, wɔbɛ dekã kwraa, shi wɔná miishɛɛ waa hu. Mikɛ Emilia fɛɛ nu he akɛ, eji he-gbɛ kpele akɛ ahã wɔmiisɔmɔ wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ yɛ nakai gbɛ nɔ. Be ni misɔmɔɔ akɛ kpokpaa nɔ-kwɛlɔ lɛ, wɔfɔ wɔbinuu Obed, lɛ sɛɛ lɛ Abner, ni yɛ afii komɛi asɛɛ lɛ, wɔfɔ wɔbiyoo Mahely hu.
<<<P>>>
Kɛ́ misusu be ni wɔtsuɔ kpokpaa nɔkwɛmɔ ni-tsumɔ lɛ he lɛ, minaa bɔ ni Yehowa ye ebua nyɛ-mimɛi ni yɔɔ Cuba lɛ, ni enɛ hãa mináa mii-shɛɛ. Yehowa ye ebua wɔ hu ni wɔtsɔɔ wɔbii lɛ ni amɛsumɔ lɛ. Hã magba nyɛ bɔ ni shihilɛ lɛ ji ehã be ni mi kɛ Emilia sɔmɔɔ akɛ kpokpaa nɔkwɛlɔ kɛ eŋa lɛ.

MISƆMƆ YƐ CUBA AKƐ KPOKPAA NƆKWƐLƆ BE NI ATSĨ WƆNITSUMƆ LƐ NAA LƐ
<<<P>>>
Be ni atsĩ wɔnitsumɔ lɛ naa lɛ, ebawa waa kɛhã nyɛmimɛi lɛ akɛ amɛbaaya asafoŋ kpeei ni amɛshiɛ hu. Aŋamɔ Maŋtsɛyeli Asai lɛ, ashwie maŋsɛɛ sɔɔlɔi lɛ kɛshi maŋ lɛ mli, amɔmɔ nyɛ-mimɛi oblahii babaoo ni awo amɛtsuŋ, ni aŋa ni-tsumɔhe nine ni yɔɔ Havana lɛ.
<<<P>>>
Akɛni atsĩ wɔnitsumɔ lɛ naa hewɔ lɛ, Hɔɔ kɛ Hɔgbaa pɛ wɔnyɛɔ wɔyasaraa asafoi lɛ. Nɛkɛ ji bɔ ni wɔtee lɛ; kɛ́ wɔyasara asafo ko Hɔɔ kɛ Hɔgbaa lɛ, wɔsaa wɔyaa jɛmɛ ekoŋŋ enɔ otsi. Kɛ́ wɔmiifã gbɛ lɛ, wɔkɛ nibii pii eyaaa, ni bei pii lɛ, wɔtaa baisiko kɛyaa, ejaakɛ no hãŋ mɛi ajwɛŋmɔ aba wɔnɔ. Agbɛnɛ hu, kɛ́ wɔyaasara asafo ko lɛ, atswaaa wɔbaa lɛ he adafi. Ehe bahia ni wɔfee wɔnii tamɔ nɔ ni wɔbasara wɔwekumɛi. Enɛ fee-mɔ waa kɛhãaa wɔ kwraa. Bɔ ni wɔkɛ amɛ hi shi akɛ weku lɛ sɔŋŋ hã bei komɛi lɛ wɔhiɛ kpa nɔ po akɛ, wɔba wɔbasɔmɔ amɛ.
