No mli lɛ, nɔyeli hee nɛɛ miitao ni ená asraafoi pii ni enɔyeli lɛ he awa. Enɛ hã nibii amli bawa waa kɛhã wɔ, ejaakɛ ekɔɔɔ he eko maŋ nɔ ni wɔyɔɔ lɛ, wɔkɛ wɔhe wooo asraafoi anitsumɔ loo pɔlitis mli. Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, nyɛmimɛi lɛ náaa lɛ mlɛo kwraa akɛ amɛbaaya shiɛmɔ, ni agbɛnɛ hu, amɛkpe kutuu kɛjá Yehowa tamɔ bɔ ni amɛfeɔ tsutsu lɛ. Sɛɛ mli lɛ, nɔyeli lɛ tsĩ wɔnitsumɔ lɛ naa ni ewo wɔnyɛmimɛi anɔkwafoi ohai abɔ tsuŋ. Tsuŋwoohe nɔkwɛlɔi lɛ yiɔ wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ ateŋ mɛi komɛi ni amɛhãaa amɛ niyenii. Bei komɛi po lɛ, niyenii ni abahãa amɛ lɛ, lá fata nibii ni akɛhoo lɛ ahe, ni wɔle akɛ Biblia lɛ kɛɛ wɔkaye lá.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ naagbai nɛɛ fɛɛ asɛɛ lɛ, wɔtee nɔ wɔkpe kutuu kɛjá Yehowa. (Heb. 10:25) Bei komɛi po lɛ, wɔfeɔ kpeei yɛ ŋmɔji amli loo hei krokomɛi yɛ maŋ lɛ mli. Mikaiɔ akɛ, be ko lɛ nyɛmi nuu ko kɛ etoi atsũ agbo ko hã wɔ ni wɔyafee kpee lɛ yɛ jɛmɛ. Wɔyanyɛɛɛ wɔsaa toi atsũ lɛ mli loo wɔjie toi lɛ kɛje mli po. No hewɔ lɛ, wɔkɛ toi lɛ fɛɛ hi kpee lɛ shishi.—Mika 2:12.
<<<P>>>
Nyɛmimɛi komɛi bɔ mɔdɛŋ waa amɛye amɛbua wɔ koni wɔya nɔ wɔkase nii kɛjɛ Biblia lɛ mli, ni wɔhiɛ sɔ amɛ waa. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, amɛtsɔɔ hiɛ amɛrikɔdiɔ kpokpaa nɔ kpeei nifeemɔi lɛ amɛwoo kasɛtii anɔ, ni amɛjaraa amɛhãa nyɛmimɛi lɛ yɛ Cuba maŋ lɛ mli fɛɛ. Bei komɛi lɛ, ahãa nyɛmimɛi hii enyɔ pɛ kaseɔ kpee lɛ shishi nifeemɔi lɛ fɛɛ ni amɛhãa, ni amɛrikɔdiɔ wiemɔi ni amɛhãa lɛ. Akɛni nyɛmimɛi nɛɛ rikɔdiɔ wiemɔi nɛɛ yɛ teemɔŋ hewɔ lɛ, bei komɛi lɛ, wɔnuɔ wuɔi agbɛɛmɔi kɛ dedei krokomɛi yɛ wiemɔi ni amɛrikɔdi lɛ amli. Kɛ́ ilɛtrisiti bɛ he ni afeɔ kpee lɛ yɛ lɛ, ahãa nyɛmi nuu ko taa baisiko ni ma shi jaŋŋ ni akɛ batri ekpɛtɛ he lɛ nɔ, ni be ni etaa baisiko nɛɛ kɛkɛ lɛ wɔmiiná ilɛtrisiti. No hãa wɔnyɛɔ wɔtswaa kasɛti lɛ ni wɔnuɔ wiemɔi ni arikɔdi lɛ. Eyɛ mli akɛ wɔbɛ heyeli loo asafoŋ woji ni fa bɔ ni sa tamɔ wɔnyɛmimɛi ni yɔɔ maji krokomɛi lɛ anɔ lɛ moŋ, shi wɔnine shɛ nibii ni he baahia wɔ koni naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ wɔ kɛ Yehowa teŋ lɛ mli awa lɛ anɔ. Ni wɔfee ekome kɛsɔmɔ Yehowa kɛ miishɛɛ.—Neh. 8:10.

Be ni miná afii 18 lɛ, mibɔi sɔɔmɔ akɛ daa gbɛgbalɔ yɛ Florida maŋ lɛ mli. No sɛɛ aaafee afi lɛ, ahã miyasɔmɔ akɛ gbɛgbalɔ krɛdɛɛ yɛ Camagüey kpokpaa lɛ maŋtiase ni atsɛɔ lɛ Camagüey lɛ mli.
