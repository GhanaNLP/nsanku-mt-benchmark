WALA SHIHILƐ HE SANE

Mikɛ Aaafee Afii 70 Esɔmɔ Yehowa Yɛ Cuba
<<<P>>>
MƆ NI GBA JI GUSTAVO JOSEPH
<<<P>>>
AFƆ MI 1947 yɛ Cuba ni ji ŋshɔkpɔ fɛfɛo ko ni yɔɔ he ni Caribbean Ŋshɔ lɛ kɛ Atlantik Ŋshɔ lɛ kpeɔ yɛ lɛ. Afɔ wɔyi etɛ, mi kɛ minyɛmimɛi yei enyɔ. Miji onukpa. Wɔ kɛ wɔfɔlɔi ni wɔyi feɔ enumɔ lɛ hi akrowa ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Esmeralda lɛ mli.
<<<P>>>
Mikaiɔ bɔ ni akrowa lɛ mli shihilɛ ŋɔɔ ni eyɔɔ shweshweeshwe lɛ waa. No mli lɛ, wɔwekumɛi lɛ ekomɛi, ni wɔnyɛkwɛ̃mɛi, wɔtsɛkwɛ̃mɛi, wɔniimɛi, kɛ wɔnaamɛi fata he lɛ bɛŋkɛ wɔ kpaakpa. Daa nɛɛ lɛ wɔyɛ niyenii kɛ shika ni wɔkɛbaakwɛ wɔhe, ni enɛ hã wɔná miishɛɛ waa.
<<<P>>>
Be ni miye aaafee afii enumɔ lɛ, nyɛmi nuu ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Walton Jones lɛ kɛ mifɔlɔi bɔi Biblia lɛ kasemɔ. Eyɛ ekãa waa ni enyiɛɔ ŋmɛlɛtswai aaafee nyɔŋma kɛbaa wɔkrowa lɛ ni ebashiɛɔ. Be fɛɛ be ni ebaaba lɛ, miwekumɛi lɛ ateŋ mɛi babaoo babua amɛhe naa yɛ minii kɛ minaa shĩa lɛ ni ekɛ amɛ gbaa Biblia lɛ mli saji ŋmɛlɛtswai pii. Mifɔlɔi, mitsɛkwɛ̃ Pedro kɛ minyɛkwɛ̃ Ela nya nɔ ni amɛkaseɔ lɛ he, ni etsɛɛɛ ni abaptisi amɛ ni amɛbatsɔmɔ Yehowa Odasefoi. Bianɛ lɛ, Ela miihe aná afii 100 ni eeya nɔ eesɔmɔ akɛ gbɛgbalɔ yɛ Cuba.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ nakai beiaŋ lɛ, nɔyeli lɛ eteee shi ewooo Yehowa Odasefoi, ni no hewɔ lɛ, amɛshiɛɔ ni amɛyaa asafoŋ kpeei ni mɔ ko gbaaa amɛnaa. Mɛi ni yɔɔ Cuba lɛ le Yehowa Odasefoi waa ejaakɛ wɔyaa amɛshĩai amli wɔyashiɛɔ. Be fɛɛ be lɛ, wɔhiɛ asafo lɛ woji ni wɔkɛhãa amɛ. Nakai be lɛ mli lɛ, wɔnyiɛɔ waa! Kɛ́ mikai bɔ ni wɔsɔmɔ Yehowa yɛ nakai “bei ni hi” lɛ amli be ni midako tsɔ lɛ, mináa miishɛɛ waa. Shi etsɛɛɛ ni wɔkɛ “bei ni mli wa” kpe.—2 Tim. 4:2.

NAAGBAI BA YƐ CUBA
<<<P>>>
Yɛ nakai afi lɛ mli nɔŋŋ be ni miye aaafee afii enumɔ lolo lɛ, mipapa kɛ mitsɛkwɛ̃ lɛ fã gbɛ kɛtee kpee yɛ he kroko yɛ Cuba. Dɔlɛ sane ji akɛ, nu ni amɛnu yɛ jɛmɛ lɛ hã hela mɔ amɛ waa. Mikaiɔ akɛ, be ni amɛku amɛsɛɛ lɛ, mitsɛkwɛ̃ lɛ yiteŋtsɔi fɛɛ tã, shi sɛɛ mli lɛ, ehe wa lɛ. Mipapa lɛ lɛ egbo, no mli lɛ eye afii 32 pɛ.
<<<P>>>
Be ni mipapa gbo sɛɛ lɛ, mimami kpɛ eyiŋ akɛ ebaahã wɔweku lɛ kɛ enyɛmi nuu ni yɔɔ akrowa ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Lombillo lɛ ayahi shi. Enɛ hã wɔshi wɔwekumɛi, ni wɔnii kɛ wɔnaa ni wɔsumɔɔ amɛ waa lɛ fata he lɛ. Shi wɔtee nɔ wɔsɔmɔ Yehowa akɛ weku.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ August 26, 1957 lɛ, abaptisi mi yɛ kpaakpo ko ni bɛŋkɛ Lombillo lɛ mli. No mli lɛ, miye afii nyɔŋma. Eshɛɛɛ afii enyɔ ni nibii fɛɛ tsake kwraa kɛhã Yehowa Odasefoi ni yɔɔ Cuba lɛ. Yɛ 1959
