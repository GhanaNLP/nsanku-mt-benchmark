Cuba Nitsumɔhe Nine Ajinafoi Akuu lɛ, 2013
<<<P>>>
Nyɛmimɛi lɛ hã wɔle akɛ, amɛkɛ amralofoi lɛ miiwie koni amɛkwɛ akɛ amralofoi lɛ kɛ Yehowa Odasefoi lɛ baaye jogbaŋŋ lo. Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, amralofoi lɛ kɛɛ amɛmiitao kpokpaa nɔkwɛlɔi lɛ fɛɛ agbɛ́i. Wɔ fɛɛ wɔkpɛlɛ koni akɛ wɔgbɛ́i ahã. Sɛɛ mli lɛ, amralofoi lɛ kɛ wɔ ye jogbaŋŋ.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ naagbee mli aahu lɛ, eyɛ mli akɛ no mli lɛ, akɛ Yehowa Odasefoi agbɛ́i woko woji ashishi yɛ mla naa moŋ, shi aŋmɛ wɔ gbɛ koni wɔkpe ni wɔfee asafoŋ kpeei, ni agbɛnɛ hu, wɔnyɛ wɔya shiɛmɔ. Sɛɛ mli lɛ wɔná wɔle akɛ, asomoaŋ, amralofoi lɛ le nɔkwɛlɔi gbɛfalɔi lɛ ekomɛi agbɛ́i shi amɛmiitao wɔma nɔ mi wɔhã amɛ.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ September 1994 lɛ, amralo lɛ ŋmɛ wɔ gbɛ ni wɔgbele nitsumɔhe nine ekoŋŋ. Naagbee mli aahu lɛ, wɔnyɛ wɔkɛ nitsumɔhe nine ni ahe yɛ wɔdɛŋ afii 20 ni eho lɛ nɔŋŋ fee wɔnitsumɔhe nine ekoŋŋ.
<<<P>>>
Kɛkɛ ni yɛ 1996 lɛ, atswa mi kɛ Emilia ni akɛɛ wɔ akɛ wɔbasɔmɔ yɛ Betel. Minaa kpɛ mihe waa ni mikɛɛ nyɛmimɛi lɛ akɛ, miyɛ bii enyɔ ni miikwɛ amɛ. Nyɛmimɛi lɛ susu sane lɛ he ni sɛɛ mli lɛ, amɛkɛɛ mi akɛ loloolo lɛ, amɛmiisumɔ ni wɔyasɔmɔ yɛ Betel. Wɔkpɛlɛ nɔ ni wɔbɔi gbɛjianɔtoo koni wɔ fɛɛ wɔyasɔmɔ yɛ Betel yɛ Havana.
<<<P>>>
Anɔkwa, shishijee lɛ minyaaa Betel sɔɔmɔ he kwraa, ejaakɛ no mli lɛ, mitsu gbɛfaa nitsumɔ lɛ afii pii ni no hewɔ lɛ mitsui yɛ mli waa. Miye mitsui waa yɛ nyɛmimɛi lɛ ahe ejaakɛ mile nɔ ni mli ni amɛtsɔɔ ni kɛ mita ɔfis lɛ, minyɛŋ ni mikɛ yelikɛbuamɔ ni sa lɛ ahã amɛ. Shi Betelbii kɛ titri lɛ, miŋa Emilia ye amɛbua mi ni mitsake jwɛŋmɔ ni mihiɛ lɛ. Fiofio lɛ, mibaná miishɛɛ, ni amrɔ nɛɛ, miisɔmɔ yɛ Betel kɛ miishɛɛ.
<<<P>>>
Amrɔ nɛɛ mi kɛ Emilia fɛɛ edara. Eŋɔɔ wɔnaa waa akɛ wɔbale nyɛmimɛi babaoo ni wɔkɛ amɛ efee ekome kɛsɔmɔ Yehowa yɛ afii nɛɛ fɛɛ amli. Eŋɔɔ wɔnaa waa hu akɛ wɔbii kɛ wɔnabii lɛ fɛɛ miisɔmɔ Yehowa. Wɔnuɔ he tamɔ bɔ ni Bɔfo Yohane nu he be ni egbɔ lɛ. Ewie akɛ: “Mibɛ miishɛɛ ni da fe enɛ: akɛ manu akɛ mibii lɛ miiya nɔ amɛmiinyiɛ yɛ anɔkwale lɛ mli.”—3 Yoh 4.
<<<P>>>
Mikɛ Emilia esɔmɔ yɛ Betel aaafee afii 30. Bianɛ lɛ, eyɛ mli akɛ wɔgbɔlɔ ni helai miigba wɔnaa waa moŋ, shi wɔbɔɔ mɔdɛŋ akɛ wɔbaatsu nitsumɔ ni akɛwo wɔdɛŋ lɛ bɔ ni wɔshihilɛ baaŋmɛ wɔ gbɛ. Wɔkɛ naagbai kpe yɛ afii ni wɔkɛsɔmɔ Yehowa lɛ amli, shi eŋɔɔ wɔnaa waa akɛ wɔnyɛ wɔkɛ aaafee afii 70 esɔmɔ “miishɛɛ Nyɔŋmɔ lɛ” yɛ ŋshɔkpɔ ni ji Cuba nɛɛ nɔ!—1 Tim. 1:11; Lala 97:1.
