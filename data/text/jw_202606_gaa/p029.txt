saji. Eji ana nɔ ni wɔfeɔ lɛ sɛɛ kulɛ, abaanyɛ amɔ nyɛmimɛi ni wɔyahiɔ amɛŋɔɔ lɛ.—Rom. 16:4.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ nɛkɛ be nɛɛ mli lɛ, wɔkɛ nyɛmimɛi srɔtoisrɔtoi kpe ni amɛfee wɔ ejurɔ waa, tsɛbelɛ amɛbɛ nɔ ko tsɔ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, hei komɛi ni wɔtee lɛ, tɔŋtɔŋi sɔŋŋ, shi nyɛmimɛi lɛ kɛ tɔŋtɔŋ buu pɛ ni amɛyɔɔ lɛ hã wɔ koni wɔnyɛ wɔwɔ jogbaŋŋ. Nyɛmimɛi komɛi hu ná he miishɛɛ akɛ amɛbaahã wɔbahi amɛshĩa lɛ, eyɛ mli akɛ niyenii fioo ko pɛ amɛyɔɔ ni amɛkɛ wɔ baaye. Akɛni wɔle enɛ hewɔ lɛ, bei komɛi lɛ, wɔ diɛŋtsɛ wɔkɛ wɔniyenii yaa ni wɔkɛ amɛ yayeɔ.
<<<P>>>
Kɛ́ wɔyaasara asafo ko lɛ, wɔnyɛɛɛ wɔkɛ wɔbii lɛ fɛɛ aya. Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, wɔkɛ fiootsɛ lɛ yaa ni wɔshiɔ mɛi ni eshwɛ lɛ wɔhãa mimami kɛ minyɛmimɛi yei lɛ ateŋ mɔ kome. Anɔkwa, akɛni wɔhiɛ gbekɛ hewɔ lɛ, ebu wɔhe yɛ nibii komɛi amli. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, bei komɛi lɛ, polisifoi lɛ gbɛlɛɔ wɔnibii amli, shi wɔkɛ asafo lɛ woji lɛ toɔ baagi ni gbekɛ lɛ nibii ni ewo muji yɔɔ mli lɛ amli ejaakɛ wɔle akɛ polisifoi lɛ kwɛŋ mli.

Mihiɛ sɔɔ Emilia waa yɛ bɔ ni eye ebua mi kɛkwɛ gbekɛbii lɛ kɛ bɔ ni eye ebua mi yɛ be fɛɛ sɔɔmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ mli lɛ. Mi hu mitee nɔ mitsu nii yɛ he ni afeɔ sikli yɛ lɛ ni yɛ nakai beiaŋ nɔŋŋ lɛ, minyɛ mitsu kpokpaa nɔkwɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ. Bɔ ni mifee lɛ nɛ; bei komɛi lɛ, miyaa nɔ mitsuɔ nii kɛ́ akpa nitsumɔ po koni yɛ otsi lɛ naagbee lɛ, mikaya nitsumɔ, ni mifeɔ nakai shii enyɔ yɛ otsi lɛ mli lɛ. Shi sɛɛ mli lɛ, atsake minitsumɔ lɛ. Ahã mibɔi nitsumɔ lɛ nɔkwɛmɔ, ni no hewɔ lɛ, ehe bahia ni maya nitsumɔ yɛ gbii lɛ fɛɛ mli yɛ otsi lɛ mli, ni nɔ ko bɛ ni manyɛ mafee yɛ he. Shi mibana akɛ, kɛ́ mihã mɛi ni mikɛtsuɔ nii lɛ nitsumɔ amɛtsu yɛ otsi lɛ naagbee lɛ, amɛbaatsu minitsumɔ lɛ amɛhã mi be ni misaraa asafoi lɛ. Enɛ hã minyɛ miyasara asafoi lɛ, ni efeee mi akɛ minitsumɔtsɛ lɛ yɔse akɛ mibaaa nitsumɔ yɛ otsi lɛ naagbee.

Gbi ko yɛ 1994 lɛ, nyɛmimɛi ni nyiɛ hiɛ yɛ Cuba lɛ tsɛ wɔ, gbɛfalɔi nɔkwɛlɔi ni naa shɛɔ 80 lɛ, kpee krɛdɛɛ ko yɛ Havana. Be ni wɔ fɛɛ wɔnaa bakpe shi lɛ, wɔmii shɛ wɔhe waa ejaakɛ yɛ afii ni eho lɛ fɛɛ amli lɛ, kpaako wɔ fɛɛ wɔnaa ebakpe shi yɛ he kome! Yɛ kpee nɛɛ shishi lɛ, awie tsakemɔi komɛi ni asafo lɛ efee lɛ ahe. Kɛkɛ ni awie nɔ ko ni hã wɔnaa kpɛ wɔhe waa. Nyɛmimɛi ni nyiɛ hiɛ lɛ kɛɛ wɔ akɛ, amɛbaasumɔ ni amɛkɛ wɔgbɛ́i ahã amralofoi lɛ.
