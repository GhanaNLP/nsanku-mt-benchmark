Yesu tsɔɔ nii ehã lɛ. Amɛteŋ mɛi komɛi ji Pau-lo kɛ Apolo. Kɛ́ amɛmiitsɔɔ nii lɛ, bei pii lɛ, amɛtsɛɔ ŋmalɛi ayisɛɛ kɛwaa amɛtoibolɔi lɛ koni amɛna nɔ ni Yehowa sumɔɔ ni amɛfee. (Bɔf. 17:2, 3; 18:24, 28) Wɔ akɛ Odasefoi lɛ, wɔle akɛ saji ni yɔɔ wɔwoji lɛ, vidioi lɛ, kɛ woji ni wɔkwɛɔ nɔ kɛhãa wiemɔi lɛ amli lɛ jɛ Biblia lɛ mli. Kɛ́ mɛi ni jeee Odasefoi ba wɔkpeei lɛ, bei pii lɛ, amɛnaa kpɛɔ amɛhe yɛ bɔ ni wɔkɛ Biblia lɛ tsuɔ nii waa lɛ hewɔ. Mɛi ni yɔɔ nifeemɔ yɛ kpee lɛ shishi lɛ kɛ Biblia lɛ tsuɔ nii kɛtsɔɔ nii. Amɛkaneee kɛkɛ, moŋ lɛ, amɛgbálaa mli koni toibolɔi lɛ anu shishi, ni amɛna bɔ ni amɛbaafee amɛkɛtsu nii.—Luka 24:32.

Nakai nɔŋŋ ja wɔbo Yehowa toi jogbaŋŋ yɛ asafoŋ kpeei ashishi dani wɔbaanyɛ wɔfee nɔ ni ekɛɔ wɔ ni wɔfee lɛ. Kɛ́ wɔbo sane toi jogbaŋŋ ni wɔkɛ nɔ ni wɔkaseɔ lɛ tsu nii lɛ, Yehowa baaná wɔhe miishɛɛ.—Abɛi 27:11.

5 Wɔboɔ sane toi jogbaŋŋ yɛ asafoŋ kpeei ashishi ejaakɛ wɔsumɔɔ Yehowa. (Mat. 22:37) Kɛ́ wɔkase Yehowa he nii waa lɛ, ehãa suɔmɔ ni wɔyɔɔ kɛhã lɛ lɛ mli waa. Suɔmɔ nɛɛ hãa wɔsumɔɔ ni wɔbo e-Wiemɔ lɛ toi jogbaŋŋ, wɔkɛ nɔ ni etsɔɔ wɔ lɛ atsu nii, ni wɔjá lɛ yɛ gbɛ ni esumɔɔ ni wɔjá lɛ lɛ nɔ.—Kanemɔ Yohane 4:23, 24; 1 Yohane 5:3.

Nibii Ni Obaanyɛ Ofee Koni Obo Sane Toi Jogbaŋŋ

Abi nyɛmimɛi komɛi ni kɛ afii pii esɔmɔ Yehowa lɛ akɛ, “Mɛni yeɔ ebuaa bo ni onyɛɔ oboɔ sane toi jogbaŋŋ kɛ́ otee asafoŋ kpee?” Naa hetoi ni amɛhã:
<<<P>>>
• Suefawn, ni abaptisi lɛ 1986 lɛ wie akɛ, “Kɛ́ mitee asafoŋ kpee lɛ, mitaa hiɛ, ni mikɛ mijwɛŋmɔ maa wielɔ lɛ nɔ, ni kɛ́ aajie vidio lɛ, mikɛ mijwɛŋmɔ maa nɔ.”
<<<P>>>
• David, ni abaptisi lɛ 1983 lɛ wie akɛ, “Misɔleɔ shii abɔ koni Yehowa awa mi ni mijwɛŋmɔ akakpa shi. Mihãa ehiɔ mijwɛŋmɔ mli akɛ, miba koni mibajá Yehowa, no hewɔ lɛ, kɛ́ mibo sane toi jogbaŋŋ ni mihã sane hetoo hu lɛ, belɛ, miijá lɛ ji no.”
<<<P>>>
• Nicolas, ni abaptisi lɛ 1982 lɛ wie akɛ, “Mikɛfee oti ni ma mihiɛ akɛ, mada mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni ná nifeemɔ yɛ kpee lɛ shishi lɛ shi, ni makɛɛ lɛ nɔ ni minya he yɛ enifeemɔ lɛ mli.”
<<<P>>>
• Augustin, ni abaptisi lɛ 1988 lɛ wie akɛ, “Mikɛɔ mihe akɛ, kɛ́ nyɔŋ anɔkwafo lɛ hã akase sane ko ekoŋŋ lɛ, belɛ, eji nɔ tuuntu ni he hiaa wɔ yɛ nakai be lɛ mli.”
