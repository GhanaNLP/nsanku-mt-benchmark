24. Yitso nɛɛ tswaa nitsumɔi otii lɛ ahe adafi kɛ amɛnifeemɔi yɛ 2020 afi lɛ mli kɛ gbɛjianɔtoi kɛ nifeemɔi ni ato ni baaya nɔ yɛ 2021 afi lɛ mli. Nɛkɛ nifeemɔi kɛ gbɛjianɔtoi nɛɛ lɛ, adamɔɔ 2018-2021 Bei Kukuoo Nɔyaa Gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ kɛ Ghana maŋ ni efo mli yɛ Nibalamɔ Gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ nɔ kɛhalaa. Gbɛjianɔtoi nɛɛ, oti ni ma hiɛ krɛdɛɛ ji koni Amralofoi aninamɔ ni ka amɛhiɛ yɛ “Ghana Susuɔ Ohe” gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ nɔ lɛ aba mli, kɛ nɔ ni jeŋmaji lɛ kpaa gbɛ kɛjɛɔ wɔdɛŋ taakɛ Jenjeŋ Ku (UN) 2030 (SDG) gbɛkpamɔi kɛ Afrika Ku wulu ni ji African Union 2063 Gbɛkpamɔi lɛ anɔ.
