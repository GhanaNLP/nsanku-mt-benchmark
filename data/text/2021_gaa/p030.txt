<<<P>>>
Akɔntaabuu: Enɛ ji bɔ ni Amralofoi lɛ buɔ maŋbii akɔntaa yɛ nibii ni efee he, ni ehaa anaa nɔ ni jeɔ mli kɛbaa. Shika kɛ japadei krokomɛi fata nibii ni Amralofoi lɛ kwɛɔ nɔ haa maŋbii akɔntaabuu nibii lɛ ahe.
<<<P>>>
Nijaa: Gbɛi anɔ ni atsɔɔ ajaraa nibii ahaa Amralofoi anitsumɔ gbeji ni mɛi fɛɛ naa eko.
<<<P>>>
Nɔkojiemɔ: Gbɛi anɔ ni atsɔɔ abaa haomɔ ko shi.
<<<P>>>
BSTEM (Basic Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics): gbɛi ni akɛtsɛɔ nilee komɛi ni abua naa. Akɛ wiemɔi nɛɛ tsuɔ nii kɛji aawie nikasemɔ loo tsɔsemɔ he yɛ sukuu gbɛjianɔtoo mli koni akɛye abua akaŋshii yɛ jeŋshikpamɔ kɛ tsɔji ahe nifeemɔi amli.
<<<P>>>
Bɔjɛti: Eji gbɛjianɔtoo ni tsɔɔ shika ni he baahia, he ni abaana yɛ, kɛ nɔ ni akɛ shika lɛ baafee. Weku ko nyɛɔ eŋmaa ebɔjɛti ni atsɔɔ shika ni weku lɛ yɔɔ kɛ bɔ ni akɛbaatsu nii aha, nɔkwɛmɔnɔ: tsu nyɔmɔwoo, srawa hewalɛ niyenii kɛ ekrokomɛi. Kɛji shika he baahia kɛha nibii ni ataoɔ lɛ, no lɛ weku lɛ yataoɔ faahe, loo etseɔ ehiamɔ nibii lɛ anɔ loo etaoɔ gbɛ kroko ni kɛ shika baaba.
<<<P>>>
Bɔjɛti sɛɛ nyɔmɔ: Enɛ baa kɛji Amralofoi lɛ bɔ nyɔmɔ fe shika ni baa fotoro lɛ mli lɛ.
<<<P>>>
Japadei ahe nyɔmɔbɔɔ: Enɛ tsɔɔ shika ni afite yɛ japade nitsumɔi tamɔ gbɛjegbɛi, sukui, helatsamɔhei, brijii, gbɛfaa, nuŋma, nitsumɔhei kɛ amɛmli tsɔji ahe.
<<<P>>>
Nitsulɔi anyɔmɔwoo: Eji nitsulɔi adaa nyɔŋgbele nyɔmɔwoo kɛ shikai krokomɛi ni yaa nitsulɔi adɛŋ tamɔ tako ni ahaa(allowances) gbɔlɛ mli nyɔmɔwo, kɛ shikai krokomɛi ni akɛhaa nitsulɔi.
