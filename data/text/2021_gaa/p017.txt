GhanaSusuɔ ohe (Yoofɔyoo Kpakpa) Nifeemɔ (GhanaCARES (OBAATAN PA) PROGRAMME)
<<<P>>>
21. Amralofoi lɛ egbele Ghana COVID-19 Naagbai kɛ nɔyaahei yɛ nitsumɔi amli ni gbɛi ji Ghana susuɔ ohe (Ghana CARES) yɛ gbi ni ji 20 yɛ Alemle nyɔɔŋ lɛ nɔ yɛ afi 2020 lɛ mli, ni akɛ baamɔ naagbai lɛ anaa ni no awa koni eha hejɔlɛ aba guɔyeli kɛ shika saji amli oya. Lɛɛlɛŋ enɛ eyafee ŋaa tsɔɔ gbɛ ko ni baaye abua Ghana jarayeli shika saji ni edamɔ enaji anɔ bɔ ni afee ni nɔyaa, kɛ hejɔlɛ aba Ghanabii ashihilɛ mli ekoŋŋ.
<<<P>>>
22. Ghana susuɔ ohe (Ghana CARES) gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ damɔ wɔnɔyaa nitsumɔi, nibii kɛ nifeemɔi ni baaye abua nitsumɔtsɛmɛi ni baawa ni mɛi ana nitsumɔ kpakpai. Kɛtsɔ Ohe Susumɔ jogbaŋŋ nɔ lɛ, Amralofoi lɛ eŋɔ yiŋ kpɔ akɛ, akɛ nifeemɔ nɛɛ baawo shika kɛ jarayeli gbɛjianɔtoi anɔ ni atse nitsumɔi kpakpai ni akɛsaa nibii kɛwo mɛi anyɔmɔ jogbaŋŋ, titri lɛ aŋkroaŋkro nitsumɔi lɛ.
<<<P>>>
23. Enɛmɛi fata nitsumɔi wuji otii kɛ nifeemɔi ni abaagbele naa yɛ Ghana Susuɔ ohe (Ghana CARES) nifeemɔ lɛ shishi lɛ ahe:
<<<P>>>
• Abaapia okwaayeli nitsumɔi wuji bɔ ni afee ni akanya oblahii kɛ yei ni atsɔse amɛ kɛ amɛhe awo okwaayeli nitsumɔi amli;
<<<P>>>
• Abatswa Ghana guɔ nibii bibii feemɔhei lɛ ama shi;
<<<P>>>
• Abaakwɛ ni nɔyaa aba tsɔne he nitsumɔi amli (iŋjinia kɛ tsɔji ahe dadei) kɛ ICT/kɔmputa nitsumɔi ahe.
<<<P>>>
• Blɔfo gbɛi anɔ ni atsɔɔ oyayai kɛyɔseɔ yibɔi;
<<<P>>>
• Abaasaa Ghana tsui amaamɔ saji kɛ bliglɛ nitsumɔi asaji;
<<<P>>>
• Abaaha Ghana atsɔ Kpokpai anɔ kpoteŋ;
<<<P>>>
• Abaabote mli ni ajaje gbɛjianɔtoi kɛha Amralofoi anitsumɔi ni feɔ nibii wuji kɛ nifeemɔi otii titrii komɛi; koni
<<<P>>>
• Amamɔ nɔyaa he shikatoohei (Development Bank of Ghana) yɛ Ghana ni ji koni akɛ shika ni abaana yɛ mli lɛ akwɛ nɔyaa kɛ nitsumɔi ni yɔɔ Ghana Susuɔ ohe (Ghana CARES) nifeemɔ lɛ shishi lɛ.
