Faahe lɛ woo: Enɛ ji nyɔji ni Amralofoi lɛ woo yɛ shikai ni ayafa yɛ maŋsɛɛ maji, baŋkii kɛ shikatoohei krokomɛi amli yɛ maŋ nitsumɔi lɛ ahewɔ. Shikafalɔi nɛɛ aaanyɛ afee Ghana bii loo maŋsɛɛbii.
<<<P>>>
Gbɛkpamɔ kɛha Nitsumɔ Nɔyaa Otii Wuji (Macroeconomic targets): Eji susumɔnɔ kɛha maŋ lɛ shika shidaamɔ. Nɔkwɛmɔnii komɛi ji nitsumɔi lɛ anɔyaa kɛ emli tsakemɔi, shika jarawalɛ, nitsulɔi ahemɔ mli tsakemɔi, wɔkɛ maji krokomɛi guɔyeli nɔyaa, Amralofoi lɛ ashika gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ omanyeyeli loo shigbeemɔi, kɛ Bank of Ghana yiŋtoi ni kɔɔ shikahamɔ kɛ ejaa yɛ nitsumɔi ahe.
<<<P>>>
MSME (Nitsumɔi Pɛmpɛbii kɛ bibii Anɔyaa Gbɛjianɔtoo. (Micro And Small Buisness Developmet Programme)): Eji nitsumɔi bibii ni heɔ mɛi fioo ni yɔɔ akɔntaabuu wolo loo ni tsɔɔ sɛɛnamɔi ni shɛɛɛ shika falɛ ko.
<<<P>>>
Gbɛkpamɔi komɛi aŋmalamɔ: Eji gbɛkpamɔi ni aaanyɛ ate shi yɛ kpaŋmɔ mli ni aŋmalaa awoɔ mli.
<<<P>>>
Nibii Anaa Mɔmɔ: Gbɛjianɔtoi ni baamɔmɔ haomɔi ni aaate shi ni kɛ naagba baaba naa.
<<<P>>>
NABCo (Jaku lɛ Tswaa Kuu, ni ale lɛ hu akɛ NABCO): Eji yiŋtoo ko ni Amralofoi lɛ kɛba koni atsɔ nɔ akɛhe univɛsitibii ni bɛ nitsumɔi lɛ ni amɛwa kɛkura shihilɛ mli haomɔi yɛ Amralofoi kɛ aŋkroaŋkro nitsumɔi amli koni amɛna nitsumɔi amli hesalɛ kɛha wɔsɛɛ nitsumɔ yɛ maŋ nɛɛ mli kɛye abua maŋ nɔyaa.
<<<P>>>
NBSSI (Maŋ Ku Ni Kwɛɔ Nitsumɔi Bibii Anɔ): Maŋ Ku Ni Kwɛɔ Nitsumɔi Bibii Anɔ: Eji ku ni yɔɔ Guɔyeli kɛ Nitsumɔi Sɔɔmɔhe lɛ shishi. Amɛnitsumɔ ji amɛkwɛ nitsumɔi bibii (MSEs) lɛ anɔyaa.
<<<P>>>
Maŋ shika Fɛɛ ni ejɛɛɛ muŋmɔŋ mli: Eji shikai abɔ ni anaa yɛ maŋ nitsumɔi amli ni Muŋmɔŋ shika bɛ mli. Adamɔɔ nɔ kɛnaa shika jarawalɛ yɛ maŋ lɛ mli.
<<<P>>>
Shika ni Jeee Toohemɔ kɛba: No ji shika ni Amralofoi lɛ naa kɛjɛɔ nibii ni too bɛ nɔ mli. Ekomɛi ji gbɛŋmɛɛ woji ahe nyɔmɔwoo, nɔ ko feemɔ he nyɔmɔwoo, sɛɛnamɔi kɛ ekrokomɛi.
