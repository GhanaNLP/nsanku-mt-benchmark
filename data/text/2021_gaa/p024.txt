33. Amralofoi lɛ gbe nitsumɔi ni baa nɛɛ anaa:
<<<P>>>
• Nitsulɔi ashihilɛhe yɛ kpokpa bibii nɛɛ amli, Twifo-Praso, Tepa, Nsawkaw, kɛ Bekwai;
<<<P>>>
• Kpokpa bibii amli yɛ Sawla, Tolon, Somanya, Buipe, kɛ Wheta kɛ Polyclinic yɛ Bamboi; kɛ
<<<P>>>
• CHPS Compounds nɛɛhu yɛ Wiaboman—Greater Accra Kpokpa wulu, Antwirifo—Bono Kpokpa wulu, Kofiasua— Bono Kpokpa wulu, Mantewareso Bono— Ahafo Kpokpa wulu, Amakyekrom—Bono Kpokpa wulu, Mewerenfiwuo— Bono Kpokpa wulu, Samproso—Ashanti Kpokpa wulu, Koforidua (Ni bɛnkɛ Offinso)—Ashanti Kpokpa wulu, kɛ Akaaso—Anai kpokpa wulu.
<<<P>>>
34. Amralofoi lɛ ha gbɔmɔtsoŋ kwɛmɔ shikpamɔ dadei kɛye bua Amralofoi ashikpamɔhei 13 kɛ aŋkroaŋkro nɔi hu nɛɛhu, koni no awa COVID-19 mli shikpamɔ lɛ yɛ Ghana fɛɛ. Hei nɛɛ ji Kumasi Centre for Collaborative Research (KCCR); National Public Health Reference Laboratory (NPHRL); Veterinary Services Department (VSD) Laboratory; University of Allied Health Sciences (UAHS); Council for Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR); Kɛ aŋkroaŋkro nɔi komɛi.
