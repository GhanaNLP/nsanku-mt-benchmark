SHIKATAOMƆ HE GBƐJIANƆTOI
<<<P>>>
10. Amralofoi lɛ kɛ shikataomɔ he nifeemɔi komɛi ahe gbɛjianɔtoi ewo 2021 bɔjɛti lɛ mli koni no aye abua nɔyaa nitsumɔi anɔyaa ni ewa hewalɛnamɔ nitsumɔ lɛ kɛdamɔ COVID- 19 hela lɛ he naagbai lɛ anaa. Nɛkɛ shikataomɔ gbɛjianɔtoo kɛ susumɔi lɛ ni baa nɛɛ:
<<<P>>>
• Akɛ COVID -19 hewalɛnamɔ too oha mlijaa kome (1%) efata National Health Insurance shika ni aheɔ lɛ he, ni akɛ oha mlijaa kome (1%) hu efata VAT tooyeli lɛ he.;
<<<P>>>
• Akɛ Hetsemɔ kɛ Mujifeemɔ too “Sanitation and Pollution Levy” (SPL) pɛsewai nyɔŋma (10) efɔ muŋmɔŋ lita fɛɛ lita ni abaahe nɔ yɛ srawa hewalɛ nitsumɔ mla tooyelii anɔ “Energy Sector Levies Act” (ESLA) koni no aye abua hetsemɔ kpakpa gbɛjianɔtoo nifeemɔi, shihilɛ kpakpa, koni no aba gbelei kɛ helai ni baashi kɛtsɔ mujiwoo nɔ lɛ;
<<<P>>>
• Akɛ srawa hewalɛ nitsumɔ hewalɛwoo tooyeli ko (Delta Fund) ni ji pɛsewai nyɔŋmai enyɔ (20 pesewas) efɔ muŋmɔŋ lita fɛɛ lita foo nɔ kɛtsɔ ESLA nyɔmɔi ni awoɔ kɛjɛɔ hewalɛ hemɔ mlai ni akɛtsuɔ nii anɔ;
<<<P>>>
• Akɛ shika gbɛjianɔtoo nitsumɔ lɛ mli saamɔ tooyeli ni naa shɛɔ oha mlijaa enumɔ (5%) yɛ sɛɛnamɔi ni anaa anɔ dani awo maŋ too yɛ
