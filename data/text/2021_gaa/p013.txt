12. Yitso nɛɛ tsɔɔ COVID-19 hela gbonyo lɛ he naagbai faŋŋ kɛ bɔ ni efite nibii yɛ jeŋjeŋ fɛɛ ajarayelii amli titri Ghana. Esaa etsɔɔ gbɛi anɔ ni Amralofoi lɛ baatsɔ kɛba naagbai lɛ ashi.
<<<P>>>
NAAGBAI NI COVID-19 HELA LƐ KƐBA
<<<P>>>
13. COVID-19 hela lɛ ni ba yɛ jeŋjeŋ fɛɛ yɛ afi 2019 lɛ naagbee mli lɛ kɛ naagbai wuji pii ba shiai, nitsumɔi, kɛ jeŋmuu lɛ fɛɛ jarayelii amli. Efite shika gbɛjianɔtoi blublu ni eha mɛi anitsumɔi ŋmɛɛ amɛ, nyɔmɔbɔɔ hu ba, ni efite guɔyeli nɔyaa saji. Kɛfata he lɛ, egbe mɛi fe 2.6 miliɔŋ yɛ jeŋ muu fɛɛ.
<<<P>>>
14. Yɛ Ghana lɛ, mɛi ni na eko lɛ ayifalɛ tee ŋwei yɛ otsiotsi mli kɛjɛ 11 kɛyashɛ 781 kɛjɛ 2020 Maawɛ nyɔɔŋ lɛ naagbee otsi lɛ mli, agbɛnɛ ebɔi shibaa ni ebashɛ 104 yɛ 2020 Afuabe nyɔɔŋ lɛ nɔ naagbee otsi lɛ nɔ. Yɛ 2021 mli lɛ, hela lɛ henɔ srɔto ko hu wo ehe nɔ ekoŋŋ ni enɛ hu tee ŋwei kɛjɛ 99 yɛ Aharabata klɛŋklɛŋ otsi lɛ nɔ kɛshɛ 880 be ni shɛɔ Oflɔ 5, yɛ klɛŋklɛŋ otsi lɛ nɔ.
<<<P>>>
15. Bɔ ni afee ni anyɛ aba muawai lɛ atsɛŋemɔ naa shi lɛ, Amralofoi lɛ kɛ nɔnyɛɛ fɔ Ghanabii anɔ ni enɛ feemɔ bafite nitsumɔi titri lɛ jarayeli kɛ shika saji, tamɔ, gboi aheremɔ, okwaayeli, hewalɛnamɔ, gbɛfaa, ninenaa nitsumɔi, shikpɔji kɛ tsui amaamɔ, shika he nitsumɔi kɛ tsɔsemɔ he saji nitsumɔi fɛɛ.
<<<P>>>
16. Tsɔji ahe nitsulɔi, tsɔnekudɔlɔi, kɛ aplankei ni yɔɔ trɔtrɔi amli kɛ mɛi ni tsuɔ amɛ diɛŋtsɛ amɛnitsumɔi fɛɛ ni naa shihilɛ nɛɛ mli ŋɔɔmɔi lɛ ahe fi amɛ waa yɛ Amralofoi lɛ amɔnɔnyɛɛ mlai lɛ ahewɔ. Eyɛ mli akɛ mɔ nɔnyɛɛ mlai nɛɛ baaye abua kɛku vairɔsu muawai nɛɛ anaa moŋ shi no hu feemɔ ha nibii kɛ nifeemɔi jarayeli saji hu ba shi fe nine kwraa.
<<<P>>>
17. Kɛji akɛto maji ni bɛŋkɛ maŋ nɛɛ he lɛ, ekaafeemɔ ni Amralofoi nɛɛ na ni ekɛ enane ma shi shiŋŋ kɛba vairɔsu muawai lɛ ashi lɛ ha maŋsɛɛ maji komɛi fɛɛ shi Amralofoi lɛ kɔŋ nɔ kɛwo lɛ hewalɛ ni no ha emia mlai lɛ amli waa.
