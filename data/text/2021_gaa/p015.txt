• Akɛ “Faith-Based Organizations” bii lɛ tsa mlu kɛja niyenii ni ahoko aafee 470,000 hu aha wekumɛi srɔtoi yɛ beiaŋ ni atsi naa lɛ;

• Amralofoi lɛ kɛtsɔ nitsumɔ ni kwɛɔ nu saji anɔ Ghana Water Company Limited lɛ nɔ ha afo nu ni awooo nyɔmɔ ko ni Ghanabii ni shɛɔ 10,125,620 na enɛ he sɛɛ;
<<<P>>>
• Ghanabii ni tee hefimɔ mli yɛ shwiemɔ ni ashwie amɛ kɛjɛ ablotsiri yɛ mlai kɛ nɔnyɛɛ ni akɛfɔ gbɛfaa kɛ nyiɛmɔ saji anɔ ni ji ‘Operation Return Home nifeemɔ lɛ hewɔ;
<<<P>>>
• Akɛ shika ni shɛɔ GH¢750 miliɔŋ ha nifeemɔ ko ni ji (Coronavirus Alleviation Programme Business Support Scheme) (CAP-BuSS) lɛ koni no aye abua kɛba hela lɛ gbɛɛkɛshwamɔ kɛ fitemɔ ni efitɛɔ nitsumɔ otii kɛ Nitsumɔ bibii (Micro, Small and Medium-Sized Enterprises (MSMEs)) fɛɛ ni afite shika ni naa shɛɔ GH¢412.88 miliɔŋ kɛ pia nitsumɔi 277,511; ni
<<<P>>>
• Agbele shika gbɛjianɔtoo nifeemɔ ko ni ji COVID-19 National Trust Fund, kɛbua shika ni naa shɛɔ GH¢ 57.13 miliɔŋ naa ni akɛ emli GH¢45.22 miliɔŋ tsu nii kɛyashi Afuabe 31, 2020 afi lɛ nɔ ni aja COVID-19 he kulamɔ nibii PPEs kɛ tsofai krokomɛi yɛ KOVID-19 kulamɔhei ni ajara niyenii hu aha mɛi ni hiɛ efa, ohiafoi, kɛ hefilɔi, ajara tsɔji hu aha COVID-19 helatsamɔhei fɛɛ hu.
