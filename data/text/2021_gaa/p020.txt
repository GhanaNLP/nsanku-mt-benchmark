GUƆYELI KƐ NITSUMƆI
<<<P>>>
27. Yɛ 2020 mli lɛ, Amralofoi lɛ kɛ tsakemɔ gbɛjianɔtoi saŋŋ ni baatsake nitsumɔtsemɛi ahiɛ kɛye abua ni Ghana atsɔ hiɛ yɛ guɔyeli mli yɛ Africa Anaigbɛ nɛɛ kɛ Africa fɛɛ.
<<<P>>>
28. Yɛ 2020 mli lɛ, Amralofoi lɛ kɛ gbɛjianɔtoi nɛɛ tsu nii:
<<<P>>>
Kɛtsɔ 1D1F gbɛjianɔtoi lɛ anɔ lɛ, aje nitsumɔi 232 shishi, ni eshɛ ŋɛlɛi srɔtoi anɔ, ni agbe 76 naa ni akɛmiitsu nii, ni ahiɛ 107 mli ni agbeko naa;
<<<P>>>
aye abua 1D1F nitsumɔi ni sa lɛ kɛ shika kɛ faahe, too kɛ hewalɛwoo nibii, kɛ nibii krokomɛi tamɔ oshigenti gbamɔ, nu kɛ srawa hewalɛ he nitsumɔi ;
<<<P>>>
aŋɔ shika ni naa shɛɔ miliɔŋ GH¢603 awo mli, ni akɛhe nibii ni awooo he too aha nitsumɔi 37;
<<<P>>>
kɛtsɔ 1D1F nitsumɔi anɔ lɛ Amralofoi lɛ kɛ Shikatoohei na sharamɔ, kɛtao GH¢2.3 biliɔŋ faahe ni ejara eha nitsumɔi nɛɛ. Amralofoi lɛ kɛ shika ni naa shɛɔ GH¢213 miliɔŋ wo shikatoohei lɛ kɛmiigwao kpa ni nitsumɔhei nɛɛ baawo lɛ mli kɛye abua 1D1F nitsumɔi nɛɛ;
<<<P>>>
yɛ 2020 mli lɛ, atse Business Resource Centres (BRCs) 37 yɛ kpokpai bibii lɛ aŋɛlɛ nɔ ni akɛ nitsulɔi wo fɛɛ mli kɛ emli hiamɔ nibii, ni aatsu nii yɛ fɛɛ mli kɛmiikwɛ kpokpai bibii 146 kɛye abua amɛ. Aje Business Resource Centres (BRCs) 30 hu maa shishi koni Business Resource Centres (BRCs) lɛ naa ayakpa 67.
