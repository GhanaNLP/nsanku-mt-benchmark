9 Wɔ ɛ ti anwo debiezukoalɛ bieko anu, ɛbahola wɔazukoa daselɛ mɔɔ kile kɛ Nyamenle wɔ ɛkɛ la anwo debie. Neɛnleanu mɔɔ ɛbayɛ la bamaa wɔanyia diedi kpole kɛ Gyihova wɔ ɛkɛ na eza wɔazukoa ye subane ne anwo debie. (Wlo. 1:20) Bieko, ɛbahola wɔava mɔɔ wɔzukoa la wɔaboa menli mɔɔ bɛ adwenle nu yɛ bɛ kesee kɛ Nyamenle ɛnle ɛkɛ la. Edwɛkɛ ne a le kɛ: Gyihova ɛmaa yɛnyia daselɛ dɔɔnwo mɔɔ kile kɛ ɔ le yɛ Bɔvolɛ la.

GYIHOVA A MAANLE BƐHƐLƐLE BAEBOLO NE A
<<<P>>>
10 Kenga 2 Timote 3:16, 17. Edwɛkɛ mɔɔ Gyihova lɛ maa alesama la a wɔ Baebolo ne anu a. Ɔbua ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nu kpuya mɔɔ hyia la yɛɛ ɔmaa yɛnwu Gyihova bodane nee mɔɔ ɔkulo kɛ yɛyɛ la. Yɛdie yɛdi kɛ Gyihova a le Baebolo ne kɛlɛvolɛ a, ɛhye ati ɔle buluku mɔɔ anwo hyia kpalɛ wɔ yɛ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nu a. Yɛkulo kɛ yɛte ye edwɛkɛ ne abo kpalɛ na yɛyɛ biala mɔɔ yɛbahola la yɛfa yɛbɔ yɛ ɛbɛla kpalɛ.
<<<P>>>
11 Duzu ati a yɛdie yɛdi kɛ Nyamenle a maanle bɛhɛlɛle Baebolo ne a? Ninyɛne dɔɔnwo a maa yɛkola yɛdie yɛdi a. Kɛ neazo la, edwɛkɛ mɔɔ Baebolo ne ka kɛ ɔsisile wɔ tete ne la le nɔhalɛ. (Luku 3:1, 2) Eza edwɛkɛ biala mɔɔ ɔka wɔ abɔdeɛ nwo la le nɔhalɛ. (Dwobu 26:7) Mɔdenle biala mɔɔ Seetan noko ɛbɔ kɛ ɔsɛkye Baebolo ne la ɛnyɛ boɛ emomu bɛyɛ ye wɔ aneɛ dɔɔnwo anu bɛdɛla buluku gyɛne biala. Mrenyia kɛyɛ 40 a vale ɛvolɛ mɔɔ bo 1,600 la hɛlɛle Baebolo ne a noko bɛ edwɛkɛ ne amuala yia. Nuhua edwɛkɛ ne ɛmkpa nwo na yeboa menli mgbe dɔɔnwo wɔ bɛ ɛbɛlabɔlɛ nu. (Mrɛ. 13:20; 15:21; Mat. 7:12) Nuhua ngapezo dɔɔnwo mɔɔ ɛra nuhua la maa yɛkola yɛfa yɛ nwo yɛ to zo. (Dwɔh. 23:14) Amgba Nyamenle a maanle bɛhɛlɛle Baebolo ne a!
<<<P>>>
12 Saa yɛ adwenle nu bɔ ɔ bo yɛ yɛ kesee kɛ tɛ Nyamenle a maanle bɛhɛlɛle Baebolo ne a, duzu a ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ a? Ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ nwolɛ asɔne na yɛyɛ neɛnleanu. Tie mɔɔ adiema kpavolɛ bie mɔɔ bɛfɛlɛ ye Jordan mɔɔ bɛtetele ye wɔ asafo ne anu la hanle la, ɔse: “Tɛ edwɛkɛ biala mɔɔ bɛka bɛkile me la a medie medi a. Medame mumua ne medwenle edwɛkɛ ne anwo menea kɛ ɔle nɔhalɛ na mealie meali. Nɔhalɛ nu, ɔdwu mekɛ ne bie a me adwenle nu yɛ me kesee. Saa ɔba ye zɔ a, mebɔ mɔdenle meyɛ neɛnleanu wɔ yɛ mbuluku ne mɔ anu amaa meanyia me kpuya ne mɔ anwo mualɛ na me adwenle nu anyɛ me kesee bieko.” Saa yɛkɔ zo yɛmia anwodozo mɔɔ yɛlɛ kɛ Gyihova a maanle bɛhɛlɛle Baebolo ne la anu a, ɔbamaa yɛ diedi ne anu amia kpole kpalɛ.—1 Kɔl. 3:12, 13.
