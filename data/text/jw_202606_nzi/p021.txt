menli ne maanle bɛbizale bɛ nwo kɛ: “Kɛ ɔyɛle mɔɔ nrenyia ɛhye ze Ngɛlɛlera ne zɛhae wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɔtɛkɔle sukulu biala la ɛ?” (Dwɔn 7:15) Mɔɔ menli mɔɔ kulo kɛ bɛsukoa Gyihova anwo debie la dele kɛ Gyisɛse ɛlɛkilehile Nyamenle Edwɛkɛ ne anu la, bɛ nye liele. Zɔhane ala a alimoa Kilisienema noko yɛle a. Kɛ neazo la, ɛnee ɛzoanvolɛ Pɔɔlo nee Apɔlɔso twe adwenle kɔ Ngɛlɛlera ne azo wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛ nee awie mɔ ɛlɛsuzu edwɛkɛ bie anwo la. (Gyi. 17:2, 3; 18:24, 28) Yɛdayɛ Gyihova Alasevolɛ noko ze kɛ yɛ mbuluku, JW broadcasting, ɛdendɛlɛ ngɛlata yɛɛ vidio ne mɔ anu edwɛkɛ ne amuala vi Baebolo ne anu. Ɔti mediema mɔɔ lɛ gyimalilɛ wɔ debiezukoalɛ ne mɔ abo la fa Baebolo ne a kilehile a. Na bɛngenga nuhua edwɛkɛ ne bɛngile yɛ ala, emomu bɛboa yɛ bɛmaa yɛte ɔ bo na yɛnwu kɛzi yɛbava mɔɔ yɛzukoa la yɛali gyima la. (Luku 24:32) Zɔhane ati saa menli dɔɔnwo ba yɛ debiezukoalɛ ne mɔ bie a, bɛ nye die kɛzi Gyihova menli ne fa Baebolo ne di gyima la anwo.

Nzuzulɛ Mɔɔ Ɔbaboa Wɔ Yeamaa Wɔadie Edwɛkɛ Kpalɛ La
<<<P>>>
Mɔɔ bɛbizale mediema bie mɔ mɔɔ bɛzonle Gyihova bɛhyɛ la kɛ, “Duzu a boa bɛ ɔmaa bɛtie edwɛkɛ kpalɛ wɔ debiezukoalɛ bo la,” edwɛkɛ mɔɔ bɛhanle la bie mɔ ɛne:
<<<P>>>
• Suefawn mɔɔ bɛzɔnenle ye wɔ 1986 la hanle kɛ, “Tɛnla ɔ nyunlu ebia ne azo amaa wɔahola wɔava wɔ adwenle wɔazie tendɛvolɛ ne nee vidio ngakyile mɔɔ bɛbaye la azo.”
<<<P>>>
• David mɔɔ bɛzɔnenle ye wɔ 1983 la hanle kɛ, “Meyɛ asɔne fane dɔɔnwo mesɛlɛ Gyihova kɛ ɔboa me ɔmaa menva me adwenle menzie edwɛkɛ ne azo. Eza mekakye me nwo kɛ, debiezukoalɛ ɛhɔlɛ, edwɛkɛ ne mɔɔ mebava me adwenle meazie zo nee mualɛ mɔɔ mebamaa la boka me ɛzonlenlɛ ne anwo.”
<<<P>>>
• Nicolas mɔɔ bɛzɔnenle ye wɔ 1982 la hanle kɛ, “Fa yɛ wɔ bodane kɛ ɛbayɛ awie biala mɔɔ bayɛ gyimalilɛ la mo. Akee ka neazo, edwɛkɛ anzɛɛ anwubielɛ fɔɔnwo bie mɔɔ ɛ nye liele nwo wɔ ye gyimalilɛ ne anu la kile ye.”
<<<P>>>
• Augustin mɔɔ bɛzɔnenle ye wɔ 1988 la hanle kɛ, “Meka mekile me nwo dahuu kɛ saa akɛlɛ nɔhavo ne si kpɔkɛ kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛsukoa edwɛkɛ bie mɔɔ yɛzukoa ye ɛlɛ la bieko a, ɔkile kɛ yɛhyia nwolɛ wɔ zɔhane mekɛ ne anu la ati ɔ.”
