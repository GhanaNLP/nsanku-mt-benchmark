ɔbɔle ninyɛne kɔsɔɔti a. Gyisɛse noko le Nyamenle ara mɔɔ le “edwɛkɛ mɔɔ maa dahuu ngoane a.” Eza Gyihova boa Gyisɛse wɔ debie biala mɔɔ ɔyɛ la anu. Kɛ anrɛɛ yɛbava yɛ adwenle yɛazie ngyegyelɛ ngakyile mɔɔ yɛbahola yɛayia la azo la, bɛmaa yɛzuzu nɔhalɛ edwɛkɛ nsa bie mɔɔ bɛbamaa yɛ diedi nu amia ɔnva nwo ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ yɛbayia la anwo. Bɛmɛ a le: (1) Gyihova a bɔle ninyɛne kɔsɔɔti a. (2) Gyihova a maanle bɛhɛlɛle Baebolo ne a. (3) Gyihova ɛlɛboa ye menli ne ɛnɛ. Saa yɛdie nɔhalɛ edwɛkɛ nsa ɛhye mɔ yɛdi bɔkɔɔ na yɛyia yɛ diedi ne anwo sɔnea a, ɔbaboa yɛ yeamaa yɛahɔ zo yɛali nɔhalɛ.

7 Kenga Yekile 4:11. Edwɛkɛ ɛhye kakye yɛ kɛ Gyihova a bɔle ninyɛne kɔsɔɔti a. Nɔhalɛ edwɛkɛ ɛhye maa ɔda ali kɛ ɔfɛta kɛ yɛsonle ye. Eza saa yɛdwenle ye tumi bedevinli ne anwo a, ɔmaa yɛnyia anwodozo kɛ ɔkola ɔdie yɛ ɔfi yɛ ngyegyelɛ kɔsɔɔti anu. Noko akee, Seetan ɛmaa adalɛ mɔɔ kile kɛ Nyamenle ɛnle ɛkɛ la ɛyi ewiade. Abɔdeɛ nwo nrɛlɛbɛvolɛma bie mɔ bɔbɔ ka kɛ tɛ Nyamenle a bɔle ninyɛne mɔɔ ngoane wɔ nu la a. Saa yɛanlie yɛanli bɔkɔɔ kɛ Gyihova a bɔle ninyɛne yɛɛ ɔkulo yɛ a, bie a yɛ adwenle nu babɔ ɔ bo ayɛ yɛ kesee kɛ ɔnle ɛkɛ yɛɛ ɔnrɛhola ɔnrɛboa yɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛyia yɛ diedi nwo sɔnea la.—Hib. 11:6.
