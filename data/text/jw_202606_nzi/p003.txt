4 Ninyɛne dɔɔnwo bahola azɔ nɔhalɛ mɔɔ yɛdi yɛamaa Gyihova la anlea. Ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ bie ɛne: Saa bɛyɛ nzenzaleɛ wɔ kɛzi yɛte Baebolo ngyɛnu bie abo la anu anzɛɛ bɛkakyi adenle mɔɔ yɛdua zo yɛyɛ ɛdoavolɛyɛlɛ gyima ne la a. Bieko, saa yɛnde deɛmɔti Gyihova maanle adenle maanle yɛhɔle amaneɛnwunlɛ bie anu la abo a. Eza saa yɛ diema diedinli bie mɔɔ ɔle yɛ gɔnwo la yɛ debie maa ɔgyegye yɛ anzɛɛ awie mɔɔ yɛbu ye kpalɛ la nee yɛ anli ye boɛ a.

5 Duzu ati a ɔhyia kɛ yɛ diedi ne gyinla adiabo kpalɛ zo a? Dwenle neazo ɛhye anwo. Yɛbahola yɛava yɛ diedi ne yɛatoto sua mɔɔ bɛsi ye wɔ ɛleka mɔɔ azɛlɛ ta kpusu wɔ ɛkɛ la anwo. Ɔwɔ kɛ bɛwula sua ɛhye abo kpalɛ amaa saa azɛlɛ kpusu a yeambubu. Zɔhane ala a yɛ diedi ne noko hyia adiabo kpalɛ amaa yɛahola yɛagyinla yɛ diedi nwo sɔnea mɔɔ bɛha nwolɛ edwɛkɛ wɔ ɛdendɛkpunli mɔɔ li ɛhye anyunlu anu la anloa a. Saa yɛkɔ zo yɛwula yɛ diedi ne abo kpalɛ a, ɔbamaa yɛahɔ zo yɛagyinla kpundii, yɛali nɔhalɛ yɛamaa Gyihova wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛbayia sɔnea la.—1 Kɔl. 15:58; kenga Kɔlɔsaema 1:23.
