D. Yɛlɛyɛ maangyebakyi zo neavolɛ gyima, 1990
<<<P>>>
Ɛ. Nyianu mɔɔ limoa wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛyele bɛ sa bɛvile gyima ne azo la, 1994
<<<P>>>
me ɔmaanle menyianle adenle mengɔle daselɛlilɛ dahuu na me nee Emilia hɔle debiezukoalɛ ne mɔ amuala.
<<<P>>>
Wɔ 1969, yɛwole yɛ belamunli ralɛ Gustavo. Yɛwole yɛ ra ne la, bɛhanle bɛhilele me kɛ mengɔnyɛ maangyebakyi zo neavolɛ. Mekɛ zɔhane, ɛnee maangyebakyi zo neavolɛma dɔɔnwo mɔɔ wɔ Cuba la lɛ bɛ mra. Yɛ nye liele kpalɛ, noko ɛnee yɛnlɛ alagye fee. Eza me nee Emilia nwunle kɛ yɛnyia nwolɛ adenle kpole mɔɔ yɛbava yɛazonle yɛ mediema a. Mɔɔ melɛyɛ maangyebakyi zo neavolɛ gyima ne la yɛɛ yɛwole yɛ ra Obed a. Nzinlii yɛwole Abner, na mɔɔ ɛvolɛ ekyi guale nu la, yɛwole yɛ ra raalɛ Mahely.
<<<P>>>
Saa mekakye mekɛ mɔɔ ɛnee mele maangyebakyi zo neavolɛ la a, ɔmaa me nye die kpalɛ ɔluakɛ ɔmaanle menwunle kɛzi Gyihova elɛyila ye menli mɔɔ bɛwɔ Cuba la. Eza ɔyilale mɔdenlebɔlɛ mɔɔ me nee Emelia bɔle kɛ yɛbava ɔ nwo ɛlɔlɛ yɛawula yɛ mra ne mɔ ahonle nu la azo. Akee maa menga kɛzi ɛnee me nee Emilia asetɛnla ne de wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ mele maangyebakyi zo neavolɛ la.
<<<P>>>
MEKƐ MƆƆ MELE MAANGYEBAKYI ZO NEAVOLƐ LA
<<<P>>>
Kɛmɔ bɛvale bɛ sa bɛdole yɛ ɛzonlenlɛ gyima ne azo la ati, wɔ 1960 nee 1970 ne anu, akee
<<<P>>>
ninyɛne nu yɛle se kpalɛ maanle yɛ. Bɛtotole Belemgbunlilɛ Asalo ne mɔ anu yɛɛ bɛvoanle yɛ edwɛkpatɛlɛvolɛma mɔɔ bɛwɔ maanle ne anu la amuala. Bɛhyele mediema mgbavolɛ dɔɔnwo bɛguale efiade yɛɛ bɛdole Bɛtɛle ne mɔɔ wɔ Havana la anu.
<<<P>>>
Ɔlua tɛnlabelɛ mɔɔ yɛwɔ nu la ati, ɛnee Folɛ anzɛɛ Molɛ ala a yɛkola yɛkɔkpɔla asafo ne mɔ a. Kɛ neazo la, saa yɛkɔkpɔla asafo bie Folɛ a, ye Folɛ bieko ɛnee yɛzia yɛhɔ ɛkɛ. Yɛlɛkɔ a yɛnva ninyɛne dɔɔnwo yɛɛ eza yɛfa basekɛ ɔluakɛ yemɔ ɛmmaa menli adwenle ɛmba yɛ nwo zo. Saa yɛbahɔ asafo bie anu a, yɛmbɔ ye nolo. Yɛmaa ɔyɛ kɛ asɛɛ yɛlɛkɔ yɛakpɔla yɛ mbusuafɔɔ la. Saa yɛyɛ ye zɔhane a, yemɔ ɔnyɛ se fee. Nɔhalɛ nu, yɛrayɛle kɛ abusua ɔti ɛnee ɔwɔ kɛ yɛnea boɛ kpalɛ na yɛ rɛle anvi deɛmɔti yɛhɔle ɛkɛ la. (Maake 10:29, 30) Noko wɔ ɔ muala anu, ɛnee ɔwɔ kɛ yɛmaa yɛ nye da ɛkɛ ɔluakɛ ɛnee kpolisima ta nwu yɛ na bɛbizebiza yɛ edwɛkɛ. Saa bɛkye yɛ a, mediema mɔɔ yɛhɔzokoɛle bɛ la noko banyia edwɛkɛ.—Wlo. 16:4.
<<<P>>>
