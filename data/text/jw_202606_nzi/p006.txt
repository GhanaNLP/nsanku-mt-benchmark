Ɛlɔlɛ nee koyɛlɛ mɔɔ wɔ yɛ avinli ɛnɛ la yɛ nwanwane
(Nea ɛdendɛkpunli 16)
<<<P>>>
Nyamenle ɛlɛboa bɛ ɛnɛ a, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ nwolɛ debie ndɛndɛ. Amaa yɛ adwenle nu angɔ zo anyɛ yɛ kesee la, yɛbahola yɛabiza yɛ nwo kpuya ɛhye mɔ: ‘Nwane mɔ a ɛlɛbɔ mɔdenle kɛ bɛbava Baebolo ngyinla­zo bɛabɔ bɛ ɛbɛla a? Nwane mɔ a bu Baebolo ne kɛ ɔwɔ kɛ ɔkile bɛ adenle wɔ tɛnlabelɛ biala anu a?’ (2 Tim. 4:3, 4) ‘Nwane mɔ a ɛlɛyɛ Gyihova ɛhulolɛdeɛ ɛnɛ a?’—Mat. 7:20, 21.
<<<P>>>
14 Saa yɛkulo kɛ yɛnwu menli mɔɔ ɛlɛyɛ Nyamenle ɛhulolɛdeɛ ɛnɛ la a, yɛbahola yɛabiza yɛ nwo kɛ, ‘Nwane mɔ a ɛlɛyɛ daselɛlilɛ gyima ne mɔɔ Gyisɛse hanle kɛ bɛbayɛ wɔ awielieɛ mekɛ ne anu la ɛ?’ (Mat. 24:14) Ɔda ali wienyi kɛ Gyihova ɛlɛdua ye Alasevolɛ anwo zo a ayɛ daselɛlilɛ gyima ɛhye a. Yɛ ngomekye ala a yɛlɛbɔ Nyamenle Belemgbunlililɛ ne anwo edwɛkpa ne nolo wɔ maanle mɔɔ bo 240 la azo a. Gyisɛse ɛhile yɛ adenle yemaa yɛva ɛvolɛ mɔɔ bo 100 la yɛyɛ gyima ɛhye. Nɔhalɛ nu, daselɛlilɛ nee ngilehilelɛ gyima ne le gyima mɔɔ nwolɛ ɛhyia yɛ dahuu a.—Mat. 28:19, 20.

14. Kɛzi Gyihova ɛlɛdua ye Alasevolɛ anwo zo amaa edwɛkɛ mɔɔ wɔ Mateyu 24:14 la ara nu ɛ?
<<<P>>>
15 Kenga Mateyu 6:9 nee Ayezaya 43:10. Nyamenle duma Gyihova ne a menli dɔɔnwo fa nwu yɛ a, na ɔyɛ yɛ fɛ kɛ bɛfɛlɛ yɛ Gyihova Alasevolɛ la. Ɛnɛ, menli dɔɔnwo mɔɔ wɔ ewiade la ɛnze kɛ Nyamenle duma a le Gyihova. Noko yɛbɔ mɔdenle yɛhile Baebolo ne abo na yɛmaanle Nyamenle duma ne vindele ɛleka mɔɔ ɔfɛta kɛ ɔfinde la. Saa ɛnea Baebolo ne mɔɔ yɛhile ɔ bo, Ngelelera Nwuanzanwuanza—Ewiade Fofolɛ Ngilebɛbo ne anu a, ɛbanwu kɛ Nyamenle duma ne finde fane mɔɔ bo 7,000 la wɔ nu.
<<<P>>>
16 Kenga Dwɔn 13:34, 35. Ɛnɛ, ɛkpɔlɛ nee mgbakyemgbakyenu ɛbu zo wɔ ewiade ye anu, noko Gyihova Alasevolɛ kulo bɛ nwo yɛɛ koyɛlɛ wɔ bɛ avinli. Ɛhye yɛ nwanwane ɔluakɛ yɛvi ewiade ɛleka ngakyile, asolo yɛ amaamuo yɛɛ kɛzi bɛtetele yɛ la, noko yɛde anzodwolɛ nu yɛɛ yɛkulo yɛ nwo ngoko. Wɔmɔ nea o, asoo wɔdu ade-
