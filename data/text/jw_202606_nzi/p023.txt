12 Saa ɛlɛbobɔ wɔ ɛdendɛlɛ ne azo a anrɛɛ maa ɔvinde ɛ nloa amaa wɔahola wɔamaa ye wɔ adawubɔlɛ adenle zo. Mmakɛlɛ edwɛkɛ biala mɔɔ ɛkulo kɛ ɛka la na ɛkenga ɛkile bɛ. Emomu, ɔwɔ kɛ ɛfi wɔ ahonle nu ɛtendɛ amaa wɔ tievolɛma ahola ali wɔ edwɛkɛ ne anzi na bɛade ɔ bo kpalɛ. Maa Ɛ Nye Ɛbolo Ɛgengalɛ Nee Ngilehilelɛ Nwo brohyua ne hanle kɛ: “Fi ahonle nu tendɛ maa wɔ tievolɛma ɛnwu kɛzi ɛte nganeɛ wɔ bɛ nwo nee edwɛkɛtile ne anwo la.” Saa ɛfi wɔ ahonle nu ɛtendɛ a, wɔ tievolɛma ne anye balie wɔ edwɛkɛ ne anwo, ɔti bɛbadie ye kpalɛ na bɛava bɛabɔ bɛ ɛbɛla.
<<<P>>>
13 Maa tendɛvolɛ mɔɔ bɔ mɔdenle la ɛboa wɔ. Kɛ neazo la, kolaa na wɔamaa wɔ ɛdendɛlɛ ne la, ɛbahola wɔava edwɛkɛ mɔɔ wɔhɛlɛ mɔɔ ɛkulo kɛ ɛfa ɛdi gyima wɔ wɔ ɛdendɛlɛ ne anu la wɔahile tendɛvolɛ mɔɔ bɔ mɔdenle la wɔamaa yeahile ye adwenle na wɔava ye nzuzulɛ ne wɔaboka wɔ ɛdendɛlɛ ne anwo. Eza maa wɔ ɛdendɛlɛ ne kile ye amaa yeaha ɛleka mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ ɛyɛ ye boɛ la yeahile wɔ. (Mrɛ. 1:5) Ɔyɛ a yɛdayɛ mumua ne yɛngola yɛnwu ɛleka mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ yɛyɛ ye boɛ la, ɔti adiema bie bahola aboa yɛ yeamaa yɛ edwɛkɛ ne anu ala ɛkɛ. Yɛkulo kɛ yɛnyia anyuhɔlɛ kɛ tendɛvolɛma nee kilehilevolɛma amaa yɛ tievolɛma ahola anyia ninyɛne mgbalɛ mɔɔ wɔ Nyamenle Edwɛkɛ ne anu la azo nvasoɛ.*
<<<P>>>
* Amaa wɔahola wɔanyia anyuhɔlɛ kɛ tendɛvolɛ nee kilehilevolɛ la, sukoa Maa Ɛ Nye Ɛbolo Ɛgengalɛ Nee Ngilehilelɛ Nwo brohyua ne nee Nyia Teokelase Ɛzonlenlɛ Sukulu Ndetelɛ Zo Nvasoɛ buluku ne.
