MƆƆ BABOA WƆ YEAMAA WƆAYƐ TIEVOLƐ KPALƐ LA
<<<P>>>
14 Ɔwɔ kɛ yɛmaa Gyihova ngakyelɛ ne mɔ anwo kyia yɛ. Yɛ muala yɛhyia Nyamenle ngakyelɛ ne mɔ amaa yɛahola yɛagyinla ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ yɛyia wɔ ‘mekɛ ɛhye mɔɔ anu yɛ se’ anu la anloa. (2 Tim. 3:1) Saa yɛkɔ debiezukoalɛ na ɔzɔho kɛ yɛde edwɛkɛ ne mɔɔ bɛlɛka la fane dɔɔnwo bɔbɔ a, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛnyia subane mɔɔ Edwɛne 119 kɛlɛvolɛ ne nyianle ye wɔ Nyamenle ngakyelɛ ne mɔ anwo la bie. Saa yɛyɛ ye zɔ a, ɔbaboa yɛ.—Kenga Edwɛne 119:24, 111, 167.
<<<P>>>
15 Ɔwɔ kɛ yɛmaa yɛ nye sɔ sunsum nu aleɛ mɔɔ bɛfa bɛmaa yɛ la. Ɛnee ɔwɔ kɛ Yizilayɛma noko anye sɔ mana ne mɔɔ Gyihova vale maanle bɛ wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ bɛlua fienemgbole ne anu la dahuu. (Ɛzɛ. 16:15, 31) Mɔlebɛbo ne, Yizilayɛma anye liele mana ne anwo, ɔluakɛ Gyihova a vale maanle bɛ a, ɛnee ɔyɛ fɛ yɛɛ ɔboale bɛ ɔmaanle bɛdɛnlanle ngoane nu. Noko akee mekɛ ɛlɛkɔ la, mana ne vonlonle bɛ nuhua bie mɔ. (Nɔm. 21:5) Duzu a yɛsukoa yɛfi nu a? Ɔnle kɛ yɛbu sunsum nu aleɛ mɔɔ bɛfa bɛmaa yɛ ɛnɛ la kɛ nwolɛ ɛngyia anzɛɛ yɛde dedee. Emomu, ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkɔ zo yɛbu sunsum nu aleɛ kɛ ɔvi Gyihova ɛkɛ. Ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkakye kɛ akɛlɛ nɔhavo ne dwenlenle edwɛkɛ ne anwo kpalɛ na bɛahɛlɛ bɛamaa yɛ kɛ yɛzukoa. Eza ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkakye kɛ nrɛlɛbɛ mɔɔ wɔ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ bɛfa bɛbelɛ yɛ anu la bahola alie yɛ ngoane.—Mrɛ. 3:13, 16-18; Dwɔn 17:3.
