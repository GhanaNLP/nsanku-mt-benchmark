BODANE
Edwɛkɛ ɛhye bamaa yɛanwu kɛ yɛbahola yɛali nɔhalɛ yɛamaa Gyihova dahuu ɔnva nwo diedi nwo sɔnea biala mɔɔ yɛbayia la.
<<<P>>>
Kɛzi Yɛbali Nɔhalɛ Dahuu Wɔ Mekɛ Mɔɔ Yɛlɛyia Sɔnea La
<<<P>>>
“Awulae, nwane ɛkɛ a yɛkɔ a? Wɔmɔ a ɛlɛ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ maa dahuu ngoane a.”—DWƆN 6:68.
<<<P>>>
MƆƆ Gyisɛse ɛlɛkilehile ye ɛdoavolɛma ne wɔ Kapɛnayɛm la ɔhanle kɛ: “Saa bɛanli sonla Ra ne nwonane na bɛanno ye mogya a, bɛnrɛnyia ngoane.” Eza ɔhanle ɔzɔle zo kɛ, “Awie biala mɔɔ kɛli me nwonane na ɔkɛnlo me mogya la lɛ dahuu ngoane, na mebadwazo ye wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ li awieleɛ la.” (Dwɔn 6:53, 54) Mɔɔ ɔhanle ɛhye la ye ɛdoavolɛ ne mɔ bie hanle kɛ, “Edwɛkɛ ɛhye anloa yɛ se; nwane a kɛhola kɛdie a?”—Dwɔn 6:60.
<<<P>>>
2 Bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛvale Gyisɛse edwɛkɛ ne anwo ɛya kpole la andoa ye bieko. Asoo kɛmɔ bɛnlɛ ye nu diedi fee la ati a bɛandoa ye bieko ɔ? Kyɛkyɛ! Kakye kɛ kolaa na edwɛkɛ ɛhye azi la, menli ɛhye mɔ bie nee Gyisɛse ɛdoavolɛma ne anye liele mɔɔ Gyisɛse yɛle la anwo kpalɛ na bɛhanle kɛ: “Nɔhalɛ nu, ɛhye a le Ngapezonli ne mɔɔ ɛlɛba ewiade la.” (Dwɔn 6:14) Ɔti ɔda ali kɛ ɛnee ɛdoavolɛma zɔhane lɛ Gyisɛse anu diedi ekyi. Noko mɔɔ bɛyiale bɛ diedi ne anwo sɔnea la, bɛangɔ zo bɛanli nɔhalɛ bɛammaa ye.
<<<P>>>
