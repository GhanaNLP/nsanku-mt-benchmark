MMAFI GYIHOVA ANWO ƐLƐ
<<<P>>>
17 Yɛde mekɛ mɔɔ li awieleɛ mɔɔ ninyɛne nu yɛ se la anu, ɔti yɛ muala yɛyia ngye-gyelɛ. (2 Tim. 3:1, 13) Noko bie a eza yɛba-yia tɛnlabelɛ gyɛne mɔɔ anu yɛ se la. Kɛ neazo la, Gyihova ahyehyɛdeɛ ne bahola asisi kpɔkɛ bie mɔɔ yɛnde ɔ bo kpalɛ la. Anzɛɛ bie a yɛ diema diedinli bie babua yɛ ndɛne anzɛɛ bayɛ debie mɔɔ gyegye yɛ la. Eza ɔbahola yeara ye kɛ yɛnrɛnyia yɛ ngye-gyelɛ bie anwo sɔbelɛ ndɛndɛ anzɛɛ yɛnrɛ-de deɛmɔti yɛlɛnwu amaneɛ bie la abo.
<<<P>>>
18 Tɛnlabelɛ ɛhye mɔ bamaa yɛali nyane ɛdeɛ, noko saa yɛdie yɛdi bɔkɔɔ kɛ Gyiho-va a bɔle ninyɛne, ɔdaye a ɔmaanle bɛhɛlɛ-le Baebolo ne yɛɛ Gyihova Alasevolɛ a le ye menli a, ɔbamaa yɛali nɔhalɛ yeamaa ye dahuu. Na kɛ mɔɔ Pita hanle hilele Gyisɛ-se la, yɛdayɛ noko yɛbahola yɛaha yɛahile Gyihova kɛ: “Awulae, nwane ɛkɛ a yɛkɔ a? Wɔmɔ a ɛlɛ edwɛkɛ mɔɔ maa dahuu ngoa-ne a.”
