BODANE
<<<P>>>
Yɛbazukoa deɛmɔti ɔhyia kɛ yɛkɔ debiezukoalɛ nee nyianu ngakyile a yɛtie edwɛkɛ mɔɔ bɛlɛka la kpalɛ nee kɛzi yɛbahola yɛayɛ zɔ la anwo debie.
<<<P>>>
Bɛnlea Kɛzi Bɛtie Edwɛkɛ La Boɛ
<<<P>>>
“Bɛnlea kɛzi bɛtie edwɛkɛ la boɛ.”—LUKU 8:18.
<<<P>>>
ƐNƐ, Gyihova maa yɛ sunsum nu aleɛ dɔɔnwo. Ɔti yɛ nee Devidi mɔɔ hanle kɛ “Gyihova le me Neavolɛ. Menrɛhyia ɛhwee” la yɛ adwenle.—Kenga Edwɛne 23:1, 2, 5.
<<<P>>>
2 Yɛ asafo debiezukoalɛ ne mɔ le adenle ko mɔɔ Gyihova dua zo maa yɛ sunsum nu aleɛ ɛnɛ a. Yɛkɔ debiezukoalɛ a, ɔyɛ a yɛta yɛti ninyɛne mɔɔ yɛzukoa bɛ ɛlɛ la anu. Saa ‘yɛgyi nɔhalɛ ne anu kpundii’ bɔbɔ a, yɛhyia ngakyelɛ ɛhye mɔ. (2 Pita 1:12) Noko saa deɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛsukoa la le debie mɔɔ wɔde ye fane dɔɔnwo a, asoo ɔyɛ se ɔmaa wɔ kɛ ɛbava wɔ adwenle wɔazie zo ɔ? Enee edwɛkɛ ɛhye baboa wɔ. Ɔbamaa yɛahakye deɛmɔti ɔhyia kɛ yɛkɔ debiezukoalɛ a yɛtie edwɛkɛ mɔɔ bɛlɛka la kpalɛ la. Bieko, yɛbazuzu mɔɔ ɔwɔ kɛ bɛdabe mɔɔ bɛkilehile wɔ asafo ne anu la yɛ amaa yeanyɛ se kɛ tievolɛma badie bɛ edwɛkɛ ne la anwo. Eza yɛbanlea nzuzulɛ bie mɔ mɔɔ ɔbaboa yɛ yeamaa yɛadie edwɛkɛ kpalɛ la. Noko maa yɛlimoa yɛnlea ahenle mɔɔ ɔtendɛ ɔkile yɛ wɔ debiezukoalɛ bo la.

KAKYE KƐ GYIHOVA A ƐLƐTENDƐ AHILE YƐ A
<<<P>>>
3 Gyihova dua ye Edwɛkɛ Baebolo ne azo a ɔtendɛ ɔkile yɛ a. (2 Tim. 3:16, 17) Enee Gyisɛse ze ye zɔ. Ɔti ɔlɛkilehile menli ne a, dahuu ɔtwe bɛ adwenle ɔkɔ Nyamenle Edwɛkɛ ne ɛleka mɔɔ fɛta wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ fɛta la anu, na yemɔ hanle
<<<P>>>
1-2. (a) Adenle boni a Gyihova dua zo maa ye menli sunsum nu aleɛ ɛnɛ a? (Edwɛne 23:1, 2, 5) (b) Duzu a yɛbazuzu nwo wɔ edwɛkɛ ɛhye anu a?
<<<P>>>
3. Adenle boni a Gyihova dua zo tendɛ kile yɛ a, na kɛzi ye azonvolɛ fa ye Edwɛkɛ ne di gyima kpalɛ ɛ?
