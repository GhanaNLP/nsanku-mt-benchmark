17 Saa ɛfa nzuzulɛ mɔɔ doa zo la anu ko anzɛɛ dɔɔnwo ɛdi gyima a, ɔbaboa wɔ yeamaa wɔadie edwɛkɛ kpalɛ. (1) Mmadi aleɛ dɔɔnwo kolaa na wɔahɔ debiezukoalɛ amaa wɔanla. (2) Kɔ ndɛ na kpondɛ ɛleka tɛnla kolaa na bɛabɔ ɔ bo. (3) Fa wɔ fonu ne to ɛleka mɔɔ ɔnrɛzu ɔnrɛgyegye wɔ yɛɛ mmamaa ɔtwehwe wɔ adwenle wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ debiezukoalɛ ne ɛlɛkɔ zo la. (4) Fa wɔ adwenle bu mɔɔ bie a tendɛvolɛ ne baha nwolɛ edwɛkɛ la. (5) Nea tendɛvolɛ ne. (6) Yɛ edwɛkɛ titili ne mɔ anwo kɛlɛtokɛ ngyikyi anzɛɛ dɔlɔdɔlɔ nwolɛ nvoninli. (7) Bɔ mɔdenle kɛ ɛbanwu ngɛlɛlera titili mɔɔ bɛlɛfa bɛali gyima yɛɛ kɛzi ko biala fale edwɛkɛ ne anwo la. (8) Biza ɛ nwo kɛ, ‘Kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na meava edwɛkɛ ɛhye meaboa awie wɔ asafo ne anu anzɛɛ azɛlɛsinli ne anu ɛ?’ (9) Kolaa na wɔahɔ nyianu bie la, dimoa nea kenle ne anu edwɛkɛtile ne, ɛdendɛlɛ ngakyile mɔɔ bɛbamaa nee ngɛlɛlera titili mɔɔ ɛdendɛlɛ ko biala gyi zo la. (10) Tɛnla aze koonwu tie edwɛne mɔɔ bɛbɔ nee vidio mɔɔ bɛye kolaa na bɛabɔ gyimalilɛ ne abo la fa siezie wɔ ahonle.

<<<P>>>
18 Ɔwɔ kɛ yɛkɔ zo yɛtie edwɛkɛ kpalɛ wɔ debiezukoalɛ bo, ɔluakɛ saa yɛyɛ ye zɔ a ɛnee yɛlɛmaa Gyihova ava yɛ alua “tenleneyɛlɛ ndenle nu.” (Edw. 23:3; 31:3) Yɛhyia folɛdulɛ mɔɔ bɛfa bɛmaa yɛ la anwo amaa yɛamminli yɛanvi adenle ne azo. Ɛhye ati, ‘nea kɛzi ɛtie edwɛkɛ la boɛ’ na fa adehilelɛ mɔɔ ɛnyia ye wɔ debiezukoalɛ bo la di gyima. (Luku 8:18) Bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛtie Gyihova la anye balie ɛnɛ na bɛanyia dahuu ngoane kenle bie.—Edw. 119:2, 14.
