Ninyɛne Mɔɔ Yɛyɛ A Ɔnrɛzɛkye Yɛ Nee Yɛ Mediema Avinli La
<<<P>>>
EDWƐNE 90
<<<P>>>
Bɛmaa Yɛmaa Yɛ Nwo Anwosesebɛ
<<<P>>>
BODANE
<<<P>>>
Yɛbazukoa kɛzi yɛbali ngyegyelɛ mɔɔ kola sɛkye yɛ nee yɛ mediema diedima agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne anwo gyima la wɔ edwɛkɛ ɛhye anu.
<<<P>>>
“Bɛwula ahunluyɛlɛyalalɛ, atiakunlukɛnlɛma, mɛlɛbɛnwoaze, bɛtɛɛyɛlɛ nee abotane.”—KƆL. 3:12.
<<<P>>>
ƐBELABƆLƐ wɔ awieleɛ mekɛ ɛhye anu yɛ ɛnelɛ kpalɛ. Noko saa yɛnle agɔnwolɛma kpalɛ a, yemɔ akee ɔnla aze fee. Gyihova ze ye zɔ, ɔti a ɔmaa yɛkola yɛnyia agɔnwolɛma kpa-lɛ wɔ ye menli ne avinli la. (Edw. 119:63) Na ɔyɛ yɛ fɛ kɛ yɛboka Gyihova azonvolɛ mɔɔ bɛkulo bɛ nwo ngoko la anwo.
<<<P>>>
2 Tɛ kɛ yɛkpondɛ kɛ yɛnwu yɛ mediema diedima ala, emomu yɛkulo kɛ yɛfa bɛ agɔnwolɛ kyengye. Yɛle abusua mɔɔ yɛkulo Gyihova nee Gyisɛse, na zɔhane ɛlɔlɛ ne ka yɛ bɔ nu. (Dwɔn 13:35) Noko akee ɛhye ɛmba mgbane. Ɔhyia gyima ɛsesebɛ. Na kɛ mɔɔ menli mɔɔ bɛnli munli la fa agɔnwolɛ biala a ɔyɛ a ngyegyelɛ ba bɛ avinli la, zɔhane ala a yɛ nee yɛ mediema diedima agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne noko kola nyia ye ngyegyelɛ ɛdeɛ a.
<<<P>>>
3 Nɔhalɛ ne a le kɛ, tɛ awie biala a yɛbahola yɛava ye agɔnwolɛ kyengye a. Duzu ati a ɔkola ɔba ye zɔ a? Ɔluakɛ bie a yɛ nee mediema bie mɔ anye die debie ko ne ala anwo anzɛɛ yɛlɛ subane ko. Noko bie a yɛnwu kɛ ɔnla aze kɛ yɛ nee bie mɔ avinli bayɛ kɛnlɛma dahuu. Ɔdwu mekɛ ne bie a yɛ nee mediema zɔhane mɔ adwenle ɛnyia na yemɔ kola maa ɔyɛ se kɛ yɛbabikye bɛ. Ɔbahola yeara ye noko kɛ kɛmɔ yɛ subane le ngakyile la ati yɛngulo kɛ yɛ nee bɛ babɔ nu ayɛ ninyɛne. Ɔyɛ noko a nyane mɔɔ yɛ diema bie ɛlɛdi anzɛɛ ewule bie mɔɔ yenyia la kola maa ɔyɛ se kɛ yɛbabikye ye kpalɛ.
