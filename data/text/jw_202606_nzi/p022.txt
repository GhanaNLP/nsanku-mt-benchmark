8 Ɔwɔ kɛ mediema mrenyia mɔɔ kilehile wɔ bama ne azo la, titili bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛmaa bagua nu ɛdendɛlɛ la kɔ zo nyia anyuhɔlɛ wɔ bɛ ngilehilelɛ nu. Ɔluakɛ saa bɛgyinla Baebolo ne azo bɛsiezie bɛ ɛdendɛlɛ ne kpalɛ a, ɔbahola yeahwe menli yeara nɔhalɛ ne anu na yeaboa mediema diedima yeamaa bɛadɛnla nɔhalɛ ne anu. Ɛhye ati, ɔnle kɛ bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛlɛ gyimalilɛ wɔ debiezukoalɛ bo la bu kɛzi bɛ gyimalilɛ ne bahola aboa awie mɔ la debie ekyi. Yemɔti saa ɛnyia ɛdendɛlɛ bie a, nea kɛzi ɛbava ninyɛne mɔɔ yɛbazuzu nwo wɔ edwɛkɛ ɛhye anu la wɔali gyima la. Ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ fale bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛmaa bagua

9 Hyehyɛ wɔ ɛdendɛlɛ ne boɛ. Saa ɛlɛmaa ɛdendɛlɛ a, ɛkulo kɛ wɔ tievolɛma ne te wɔ edwɛkɛ ne abo ndɛndɛ. Saa wɔ edwɛkɛ ne yɛ kpomgbondee a, wɔ tievolɛma ne adwenle bavi zo. Ɔti kɛ ɔkɛyɛ na wɔahola wɔahyehyɛ wɔ ɛdendɛlɛ ne boɛ ɛ? Nea nzuzulɛ bie mɔ: (1) Ka edwɛkɛ titili mɔɔ ɛbaha nwolɛ edwɛkɛ la wɔ mɔlebɛbo ne ala. (2) Kilehile edwɛkɛ titili ne mɔ anu ngoko. Saa ɛkilehile edwɛkɛ titili ko biala anu ɛwie a, gyinla ekyii na ka edwɛkɛ titili bieko mɔɔ ɛbahilehile nu la kolaa na wɔadoa zo. (3) Saa ɛwie a ti edwɛkɛ titili ne mɔ anu sikalɛ fa dwula wɔ ɛdendɛlɛ ne.

10 Maa ɛ nye ɛlie edwɛkɛ ne anwo. Saa ɛ nye die edwɛkɛ bie anwo a, ɔmaa ɛnyia ɛhulolɛ kpole kɛ ɛbaha nwolɛ edwɛkɛ wɔahile awie mɔ. (Mrɛ. 2:4, 5; Gyi. 4:20) Ɔti saa ɛdwenledwenle wɔ gyimalilɛ ne nee ngɛlɛlera mɔɔ wɔ nu la anwo a, ɔbamaa ɛ nye alie nwo. Adiema nrenyia bie mɔɔ wɔ United States mɔɔ yebo ɛvolɛ 70 la hanle ɛdendɛlɛ bie mɔɔ ɔdiele wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɔle kakula kpavolɛ la anwo edwɛkɛ kɛ: “Metɛkakyɛ kɛzi tendɛvolɛ ne maanle yɛ anwosesebɛ kɛ saa yɛlɛkenga Nyamenle Edwɛkɛ ne a ɔwɔ kɛ yɛdwenledwenle nwo kpalɛ la. Ɔvale anwodozo kpole a ɔdendɛle a yɛɛ ɔhilehilele kɛzi yɛbayɛ ye la anu wienyi. Ɛdendɛlɛ zɔhane hakyile me ɛbɛlabɔlɛ yɛɛ ɔhanle kɛzi mekenga Baebolo ne yɛɛ me nee Gyihova agɔnwolɛvalɛ ne.”
