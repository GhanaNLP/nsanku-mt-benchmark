Ɔman Sikanya Ho Nhyehyɛeɛ Ahodoɔ
<<<P>>>
14. Aban de nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ agu akwan mu a ɛnam so bɛkanyan mfidinnwuma na aboa ama wɔayɛ adwadeɛ a ɛdi mu a ɛbɛnya edwa wɔ ɔman yi mu ne amannɔne. Ne saa enti, ɛho akɔhia sɛ yɛyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ mu nsakraeɛ ahodoɔ a ɛdidi soɔ yi:
<<<P>>>
• Sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na yɛbɛma mfasoɔ a ɛbɛba mfidinnwuma mu no ase atim na wɔatumi atrɛ wɔn dwumadie mu enti, Aban de mfeɛ mmienu bɛka mmerɛ dodoɔ a ɔde maa ntomatam mfidinnwuma sɛ wɔrentua adwadeɛyɔ ho ɛtoɔ (Vat Relief) no a ɔhyɛɛ aseɛ wɔ afe 2019 mu no ho;
<<<P>>>
• Mfeɛ mmienu akyi nie a yɛde amannɔne edwadie nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛfa nneɛma a yɛkra firi amannɔne ho dii dwuma a emu no, yɛmaa amannɔne adwadifoɔ kɔn mu dwoo wɔn kakra. Yɛbɛyɛ nsakraeɛ wɔ saa nhyehyɛeɛ yi mu ama adi mu na aboa amannɔne adwadifoɔ pɔtee a ɛsɛ sɛ saa nhyehyɛeɛ yi boa wɔn no;
<<<P>>>
• Yɛbɛte ɛtoɔ a fagudeɛtuo nnwuma nketewa a wɔtɔn sikakɔkɔɔ a yɛmfa mfaa afidie mu tua no so firi ɔha mu nkyɛmu mmiɛnsa de akɔ ɔha mu nkyɛmu baako ne ɛfa (withholding tax rate reduction from 3 % to 1.5 %);
<<<P>>>
• Ɛfiri Ɔpɛpɔn ahyɛaseɛ wɔ afe 2022 mu no, Ghana Ɛtoɔgyeɛ Adwumakuo (Ghana Revenue Authority) bɛboa Nkuropɔn, Nkuro ne Mansini Mmarahyɛbadwa (MMDAs) no ama wɔapɛ akwannya korɔ a ɛbɛboa wɔn ama wɔatu anammɔn wɔ ntokua	toɔ gyegyeɛ ntotoeɛ ho ama wɔatumi agyegye no yie na wɔabu ho nkonta pa nso;
<<<P>>>
• Aban bɛyɛ nsakraeɛ wɔ sika ahodoɔ a Aban Nnwuma Ahodoɔ (MDAs) gye wɔ wɔn dwumadie ho no mu na ahyɛ aseɛ ayɛ adwuma firi Ɔpɛpɔn da a ɛdi kan wɔ afe 2022 mu. Sika ahodoɔ a Aban Nnwuma gyegye mu nsakraeɛ no bɛgyina sɛdeɛ afe biara nneɛma-boɔ sorokɔ no teɛ so;
<<<P>>>
• Mmarahyɛbadwafoɔ gye Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ yi tom ara pɛ, ntɛm pa ara bɔrekwan so ɛtoɔ a yɛtua no bɛba awieeɛ;
<<<P>>>
• Yɛde Abɛɛfo Afidie so Sikamaneɛ ho Ɛtoɔ (“Electronic Transaction Levy” anaa “E-Levy”) bɛba sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a ɛbɛma ɛtoɔtua mu atrɛ na ama wɔn a wɔn din nni Aban nwoma mu nso abɛka nnipa a wɔtua ɛtoɔ wɔ ɔman yi mu no ho. Saa ɛtoɔ yi bɛka ‘fɔn’ so sikamaneɛ, sikakorabea sikamaneɛ, ‘fɔn’ so sikamaneɛ ho dwumadie ne sikamaneɛ a ɛfiri amannɔne, na ɛtoɔ a ɛda so yɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu baako ne akyire pɔ nson ne fa (1.75%) a onipa a ɔde sika remane no na ɔbɛtua, gye sɛ sikamaneɛ a ɛfiri amannɔne reba no na deɛ ne nsa bɛka sika no bɛtua saa ɛtoɔ yi. Saa ɛtoɔ yi bɛhyɛ aseɛ wɔ Ɔgyefuo bosome da ɛdi kan wɔ afe 2022 mu. Sikamaneɛ a ɛbɛkɔ so wɔ da koro mu a ɛduru sidi ɔha (GH¢100) anaa deɛ ɛnnuru saa no, ɛtoɔ yi nka no.
