ƆSOAFOƆ NO ANODISEM
<<<P>>>
COVID-19 yadeɛ kontonkurowi ho nsunsuansoɔ bɔne no aka ewiase ɔman biara sikasɛm a Ghanaman ka ho bi. Ahohiahia a ɛkɔwiee owuo, asetenam ahokyere ne adwumasɛeɛ a Ghanafoɔ hunuiɛ no nyɛ adeɛ a Aban werɛ afiri koraa. Mede ahohoahoa ka sɛ anammɔn a ɔman yi tuu wɔ yadeɛ kontonkurowi yi ho no yɛ akokoɔduru anammɔn na ɛyɛ gyinaeesie a ɛdi mu pa ara a ewiase aman nyinaa agye atom sɛ yɛayɛ adeɛ. Aban sane yɛɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ nam so boaa ankorankorɛ nnwuma sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a ɔmanfoɔ bɛsane asi wɔn dada mu.
<<<P>>>
Deɛ ɛdi hɔ bio nso ne sɛ, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na yɛbɛtumi agyina ɔhaw ne akwannya ahodoɔ a yadeɛ kontonkurowi yi de aba no ano enti, Aban nam Ɔmanpanin Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo mpaninnie a ɛdi mu no so de ahoɔherɛ hyehyɛɛ Ghana Covid-19 Asetɔre Ne Ankorankorɛ Nnwuma Mu Nkanyan Ho Mmoa (GhanaCARES) “Obaatan Pa” Dwumadie (Ghana COVID-19 Alleviation and Revitalisation of Enterprises programme, (the GhanaCARES “Obaatan Pa” programme) a emu sikabotene yɛ sidi ɔpepepem ɔha baako (GH¢100 billion). Yei de ahomegyeɛ mapa abrɛ yɛn nkurɔfoɔ na ɛgu so de nkanyan reba ɔman no mu.
<<<P>>>
Ɛyɛ yɛn ahomeka sɛ mogu so wɔ gyidie wɔ Aban mu wɔ ɛberɛ a yɛgu so reyɛ biribiara a ɛhia de ama yɛade yɛ ho afiri yadeɛ kontonkurowi yi ho. Ɛnam deɛ ɛtoo yɛn wɔ saa ɛberɛ no mu no so ama yɛahunu sɛdeɛ yɛn nsiyɛ sɛ ɔman a yɛtumi gyina atoyerɛnkyɛm ano no teɛ.
<<<P>>>
Twerɛ Kronkron no ma yɛn akwankyerɛ ɛberɛ biara a ɛsɛ sɛ yɛfa so wɔ mmerɛ a ɛte sei mu wɔ Yesaia nwoma, etire 9, nkyekyɛmu 10 hɔ a wɔatwerɛ sɛ, “Berekese no adwiri agu fam, nanso yɛde aboɔ a wɔatwa bɛto bio; wɔatwitwa borɔdɔma nnua no agu nanso yɛde ntweneduro bɛhyɛ anan mu.” Saa ara na Aban asi nkete sɛ ɔbɛdi ne bɔhyɛ nyinaa so na ne saa enti, ɔbɛkɔ so ayɛ nhyehyɛeɛ na wadi dwuma ahodoɔ a ɛbɛma yɛn nsa aka deɛ yɛahwere nyinaa na ɛnam so ama ɔmanfoɔ asetenam atu mpɔn.
<<<P>>>
Botaeɛ ahodoɔ no mu baako a ɛdi akotene wɔ 2022 Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ yi mu ɛnna afei wɔ mfeɛ mmiɛnsa a ɛdi yɛn anim yi mu ne sɛ, yɛbɛkyekyere Ɔman a Ankorankorɛ Nnwumabue ase bɛtim wɔ mu na ɛbɛma yɛn sikasɛm kankorɔ adi mu, na obiara bɛdi ne dwuma sɛdeɛ ɛfata na ɛnam so ama nnwuma abu so na ne titire, mmabunu anya bi ayɛ. Yei enti, Aban nhyehyɛeɛ a ɔbɛtu ho anammɔn no bɛyɛ deɛ ɛbɛboa mmabunu, nam so ahunu adom akyɛdeɛ a wɔwɔ na yɛaboa ama obiara anya akwannya de adi dwuma sɛdeɛ ɛbɛboa ama ahonya aba ama obiara.
<<<P>>>
Sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na yɛbɛma mmabunu anya nkuranhyɛ na wɔanya nnnwumabue sunsum de abuebue wɔn ankasa nnwuma enti, afe 2022 mu no, Aban bɛhyɛ dwumadie bi a yɛato edin YouStart Nhyehyɛeɛ ase a ɛnam so bɛma mmabunu anya bosea de ahyɛ aseɛ abuebue wɔn nnwuma na atumi agyina anaa wɔde atrɛ wɔn nnwuma mu.
<<<P>>>
Afe 2022 Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ yi mu no, yɛayɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ a yɛde bɛtoa so atu anammɔn wɔ ɔman yi mu ɔhaw ahodoɔ a yɛwɔ mu seesei no ho nam so ama yɛatumi agyina yɛn nan so bio wɔ yadeɛ kontonkurowi yi akyi. Aban de asi n’ani so sɛ ɔbɛyɛ ntotoeɛ a ɛdi mu na ɔne akuhodoɔ adi nsawɔsoɔ de atu anammɔn a ɛbɛma mmabunu anya nsaanonnwuma ho nteteeɛ na yɛahwɛ ama ankorankorɛ nnwumabue atu mpɔn wɔ mmabunu mu.
