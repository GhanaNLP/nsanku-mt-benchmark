Ghana Covid-19 Asetɔre Ne Ankorankorɛ Nnwuma Mu Nkanyan Ho Mmoa (Ghanacares) “Obaatan Pa” Dwumadie No
<<<P>>>
17. Sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na yɛbɛduru yɛn botaeɛ a yɛpɛ sɛ Ghana duru mpɛmpɛnsɔɔ a yɛrenhia mmoa ho, na afei yɛasane agyina yɛn nan so wɔ COVID-19 Yadeɛ Kontonkurowi akyi enti, Ghana Aban hyɛɛ aseɛ tuu Ghana Covid-19 Asetɔre Ne Ankorankorɛ Nnwuma Mu Nkanyan Ho Mmoa (GhanaCARES) “Obaatan Pa” Dwumadie a emu sikabotene yɛ sidi ɔpepepem ɔha baako (GH¢100 billion) no ho anammɔn. Yei botaeɛ ne sɛ yɛnam ankorankorɛ nnwuma ho nhyehyɛeɛ mu mpontuo ne mmoa a yɛde bɛma wɔn no so ama ɔman yi sikasɛm agyina yie, nkanyan aba mu na ɛnam so ama nsakraeɛ pa aba ɔmanfoɔ asetena mu.
<<<P>>>
18. Deɛ ɛdidi soɔ yi ne dwumadie no mu nkonimdie ahodoɔ a epuee wɔ afe 2021 mu ɛnna afei anammɔn ahodoɔ a yɛasi gyinaeɛ sɛ yɛbɛtu wɔ afe 2022 mu.

Abɛɛfo Ɛkwan a Yɛfa so yɛ Kua ne Nnuanekra Nhyɛananmu
<<<P>>>
19. Ahineme bosome baa awieeɛ wɔ afe 2021 mu no, na yɛafa abɛɛfo ɛkwan so atwerɛ nnipa dodoɔ mpem ahanum aduɔson nkron, ahansia aduosia mmienu (579,662 individuals) edin a emu mpem ahanum aduanan nson, ɔha ne aduonu nsia yɛ akuafoɔ (547,126 farmers) ɛnna emu mpem aduasa mmienu, ahanum ne aduasa nsia yɛ nnipa a wɔdi dwuma ahodoɔ wɔ kua mu (32,536 value chain actors) na yei bɛboa ama yɛatumi de yɛn ani adi wɔn dwumadie akyi, ahunu obiara dwuma a ɔredi sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a, Aban mmoa a ɔde boa kuadwuma no bɛdi mu na emu ayɛ fann a obiara bɛhunu deɛ ɛrekɔ soɔ.
<<<P>>>
20. Aban ne nnipakuo ahodoɔ a wɔdi nkokɔyɛn, ɛmo, soya ne ntoosi ho dwuma no nyinaa hyiaeɛ, na ɛnam nhyiamu yi so maa Aban hunuu ɔhaw ahodoɔ a ɛwɔ wɔn dwumadie mu na yei maa Aban yɛɛ nhyehyɛeɛ a ɔnam so bɛma kuadwuma mu nnwumakuo ahodoɔ no bosea a ɛho nsiho wɔ fam sɛdeɛ wɔde bɛsɔ wɔn dwumadie mu ɔhaw ano. Saa nhyehyɛeɛ yi mu no, wɔde bosea a wɔate ɛho nsiho no so ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduonum (50% subsidy on interest) bɛma kuadwuma mu nnwumakuo a wɔfata ama wɔde atotɔ dwumadie mu akadeɛ a wɔhia na wɔde atrɛ wɔn dwetire mu nso.
