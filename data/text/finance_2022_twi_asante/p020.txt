Nsrahwɛ, Adwinnie Ne Amammerɛ Mpontuo
<<<P>>>
22. Dwuma a Aban nam CARES nhyehyɛeɛ so redi de apagya Nsrahwɛ, Adwinnie ne Amammerɛ mu ama ɔman sikasɛm atu mpɔn enti, Aban bɔɔ mmɔden wiee Ɔman Nsrahwɛbea ahodoɔ a wɔde bɛgu abɛɛfo amaneɛbɔbea baako so ho dwumadie, na ɛnam so ma wɔn a wɔdi dwuma wɔ nsrahwɛ adwuma no mu ahasa (300 operators) aba faako sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a wɔbɛfa abɛɛfo ɛkwan so adi wɔn dwuma na wɔn dwumadie adi mu.
<<<P>>>
23. Afe 2022 mu no, Aban bɛsisi mmeaeɛ bi te sɛ Adwinnie Mpontuo Beaeɛ, Anyinam mfidie, mfidie a asɛe akwaa beaeɛ, na wayiyi akwan a ɛkɔ nsrahwɛbea na afei ɔde mpontunnee bi te sɛ anyinam nkanea, nsuo ne nkratoɔ ne nkutahodie ho akadeɛ akɔ nsrahwɛbea ahodoɔ.

Adwadeɛyɔ ne Adwadeɛ a Yɛde bɛkɔ Amannɔne Mu Mpontuo
<<<P>>>
24. Sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na dwuma a yɛbɛdi no ne Aban Mfidinnwuma ho Botaeɛ ahodoɔ Edu (Government’s 10-Point Industrialization Agenda) no bɛsiae soɔ enti, yɛasi gyinaeɛ sɛ yɛbɛma yɛn ani akɔ Ghana Mfidinnwuma Nketewa mu ntrɛmu so sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a, wɔbɛtumi ayɛ adwadeɛ a ɛbɛnya boɔ wɔ amannɔne edwa so, nnwuma a wɔdane mfudeɛ ani dwumadie bɛtu mpɔn, wɔayɛ adwadeɛ bebree a yɛde bɛkɔ amannɔne akɔtɔn na ɛnam so ama nnwuma abu so ma ɔmanfoɔ anya bi ayɛ. Afe 2021, Aban hyɛɛ aseɛ sii Aduyɛ Beaeɛ te sɛ deɛ ɛwɔ ewiase baabiara wɔ Noguchi sɛdeɛ ɛbɛboa ɔman yi mu nnuroyɛ nnwumakuo wɔ wɔn dwumadie mu. Afe 2022 mu no, yɛbɛtu anammɔn a ɛdidi soɔ yi:
<<<P>>>
• Nnwumakuo ho nhyehyɛeɛ mu nsakraeɛ ahodoɔ no bɛyɛ ntɛmntɛm na abɛɛfo ɛkwan a wɔfa so di dwuma bɛdi akotene wɔ Nnwumakuo ho Nkrataayɛfoɔ Panin Adwumam (Registrar General Department);
<<<P>>>
• Aban bɛhyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ase de asisi Mantam Mfidinnwuma Mmeaeɛ a wɔne Mfidinnwuma ho Nimdeɛ Asuapɔn ahodoɔ (Technical Universities) no bɛnya twaka; ɛnna afei
<<<P>>>
• Yɛbɛtrɛ nnuro a yɛtɔ firi ɔman yi mu nnuroyɛ nnwumakuo no hɔ mu na yɛne wɔn aka abom ayɛ adwuma ama wɔayɛ nnuro a ɛdi mu.
