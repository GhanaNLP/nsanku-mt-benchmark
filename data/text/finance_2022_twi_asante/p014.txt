9. Ɔman sikasɛm nnyinasoɔ a ɛddi soɔ yi na yɛde yɛn ani abu sɛ yɛn nsa bɛka wɔ afe 2022 mu:
<<<P>>>
• Ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ nyinaa kankorɔ ɔha mu nkyɛmu enum ne akyire pɔ nwɔtwe (Real GDP growth of 5.8 %);
<<<P>>>
• Ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ a Fango nka ho bie kankorɔ ɔha mu nkyɛmu enum akyire pɔ nkron (Non-Oil Real GDP growth of 5.9 %);
<<<P>>>
• Ɔpɛnimma bosome awieeɛ nneɛma boɔ-sorokɔ ɔha mu nkyɛmu nwɔtwe (End-December inflation of 8 %);
<<<P>>>
• Sika a yɛbɛdi aboro deɛ yɛwɔ so- ɔha mu nkyɛmu nson ne akyire pɔ ɛnan (Fiscal deficit of 7.4 % of GDP);
<<<P>>>
• Sika dodoɔ a ɛbɛboro deɛ yɛbɛgyegyeɛ so- ɔha mu nkyɛmu hwee ne akyire pɔ baako (Primary surplus of 0.1 % of GDP); ɛnna
<<<P>>>
• Amannɔne Sika Dodoɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ yɛnya de kra nneɛma bɛyɛ abosome ɛnan.

Ɛkwan a yɛbɛfa so apɛ sika wɔ afe 2022 mu
<<<P>>>
10. Aban ani da ɛkwan sɛ sika dodoɔ a ɔbɛtumi agyegye ne deɛ wɔde bɛkyɛ yɛn wɔ afe 2022 mu no nyinaa bɛduru Ghana sika sidi ɔpepepem ɔha ne ɔpepem aduonum mmienu (GH¢100.52 billion) a ɛyɛ ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduonu ne akyire pɔ hwee (20.0 % of GDP). Sika dodoɔ a yɛde ani abu sɛ yɛn nsa bɛka wɔ ɔman yi ara mu no yɛ ɔpepepem aduɔkron nkron ne ɔpepem aduonum enum (GH¢99.55 billion). Sika a yɛbɛnya wɔ ɔman yi ara mu no, ɛtoɔ sika a ɛmfiri fango mu yɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduɔson nson ne akyire pɔ enum (77.5 %) a ɛyɛ Ghana sika ɔpepem aduɔson nson ne mpem ɔha ne aduasa nsia (GH¢77,136 million). Aban ani da ɛkwan sɛ sika dodoɔ a yɛbɛgyegye no bɛgyina ɛtoɔtua ho nokorɛdie, sikagyegyeɛ ho nhyehyɛeɛ mu nsakraeɛ ne ɛtoɔtua nhyehyɛeɛ ho ntotoeɛ pa so.
