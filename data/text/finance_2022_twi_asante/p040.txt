Nsɛmfua Asekyerɛ (Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ mu Nsɛmfua)
<<<P>>>
Mansini Baako Afididwuma Baako (1D1F): Yei yɛ mfidinnwuma ahodoɔ a yɛbɛbuebue wɔ mansini mu na ɛnam so ama mfidinnwuma atu mpɔn wɔ Ghana. Yei botaeɛ ne sɛ nnwuma bɛbu so wɔ ɔman yi mu na Ghanafoɔ a wɔwɔ ɔman yi afanan nyinaa anya adwuma ayɛ.
<<<P>>>
Nkontabuo (Accountability): Yei gyina hɔ ma nkonta a Aban bu kyerɛ Ɔman mma fa ne dwumadie ho ne amaneɛbɔ a ɛwɔ anammɔn ahodoɔ nso a watu ho. Sikasɛm ne agyapadeɛ ahodoɔ a Aban hwɛ so ma ɔmanfoɔ no nyinaa ka yei ho.
<<<P>>>
Sika Mu Nkyekyɛmu (Allocation): Yei yɛ ɛkwan a wɔfa so kyekyɛ ɔman sika mu ma Aban asoɛeɛ ahodoɔ no ne nnwumakuo ahodoɔ no ma wɔde di dwuma ma ɔmanfoɔ.
<<<P>>>
Ɔhaw-anosie (Alleviation): Yei yɛ ɛkwan wɔfa so ma ɔhaw bi ho nsunsuansoɔ ano brɛ ase.
<<<P>>>
Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ (Budget): Sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ gyina hɔ ma nhyehyɛeɛ a Aban ayɛ afa ɛkwan a ɔbɛfa so anya sika ne deɛ ɔde saa sika no bɛyɛ ho. Nhwɛsoɔ ne sɛ, abusua bi bɛtumi ayɛ sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ nam so akyerɛ sika dodoɔ a wɔwɔ ne nneɛma ahodoɔ a ɛhia sɛ wɔde sika no yɛ no nnidisoɔ nnidisoɔ. Ɛbetumi ayɛ ɛdan ho ɛka, nsuo ne nkanea ɛka ɛnna aduane ho ɛka) Ɛkɔba sɛ wɔhia sika no dodoɔ a, ɛho bɛhia sɛ abusua no bɛhwehwɛ sika no bi aka ho anaa mpo wɔbɛkɔ akɔbɔ bosea.
<<<P>>>
Sika A Aka Mu Wɔ Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ mu (Budget deficit): Yei si wɔ ɛberɛ a sika a Aban de bɛdi dwuma no kɔ anim sene deɛ Aban nsa aka no ankasa.
<<<P>>>
Mpontunnwuma ho Sika (Capital Expenditure): Yei yɛ sika a yɛde yɛ mpontunnwuma sɛ ebia akwanyie, sukuudansie, ayaresabea dansie, atwenesie, akwantusɛm, nsuo, mfidie akɛseɛ ne deɛ ɛkeka ho.
<<<P>>>
Adwumayɛfoɔ akatua (Compensation of Employees): Yei yɛ adwumayɛfoɔ bosome akatua, Sika ahodoɔ, asetenam ahotosoɔ sika, akwankɔgya sika ne deɛ ɛkeka ho a wɔtua ma Aban adwumayɛfoɔ.
<<<P>>>
Mpontuo mu Aboafoɔ (Development Partners): Yei gyina hɔ ma Aman anaa nnwumakuo a wɔboa Aman a afei na wɔretu mpɔn ma wɔduru wɔn mpontuo botaeɛ ho. Yei bɛtumi ayɛ akuhodoɔ bi te sɛ Amansan Fotoɔ Adwumakuo (International Monetary Fund- IMF),Ewiase Sikakorabea (World Bank), Abibirem Mpontuo Sikakorabea (African Development Bank) ne deɛ ɛkeka ho. !tumi nso yɛ ankorankorɛ aman bi te sɛ Germany, Nyiresi Aburokyire, Amɛrika, ne wɔn a wɔkeka ho.
<<<P>>>
Afe Awieeɛ Nneɛma Boɔ-Sorokɔ (End Year Inflation): Yei yɛ mpɛmpɛnsoɔ a nneɛma boɔ kɔduru wɔ $pɛnimba bosome mu wɔ afe pɔtee bi mu.
