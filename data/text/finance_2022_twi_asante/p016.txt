12. Yɛhwɛ sika a yɛde yɛn ani abu sɛ yɛbɛnya ne deɛ wɔde bɛkyɛ yɛn ɛnna afei dwuma ahodoɔ a yɛde bɛdi wɔ afe 2022 mu no a, dwumadie ho sikasɛm no bɛboro deɛ yɛatwa ato hɔ wɔ Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ yi mu no so sika ɔpepepem aduasa nson ne ɔpepem baako (GH¢39.01 billion) a ɛyɛ ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu nson ne akyire pɔ ɛnan (7.4 % of GDP).
<<<P>>>
13. Sika dodoɔ a yɛn ani da ɛkwan sɛ ɛbɛfiri amannɔne aba ama yɛde asɔ yɛn sika so no bɛyɛ sidi ɔpepepem nkron ne ɔpepem baako (GH¢9.1 billion) a ɛyɛ ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu baako ne akyire pɔ nwɔtwe (1.8 % of GDP). Sika a yɛn ani da ɛkwan sɛ yɛbɛnya wɔ ɔman yi ara mu nso bɛyɛ sidi ɔpepepem aduonu nson ne ɔpepem nkron (GH¢27.9 billion) a ɛyɛ ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu enum ne akyire pɔ nsia (5.6 % of GDP).
