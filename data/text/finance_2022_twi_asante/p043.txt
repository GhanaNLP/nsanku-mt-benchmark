Asetena mu Mmoa Sika (Social Benefits): Yei yɛ sika a Aban gyina ɔmanfoɔ anan mu tua ma nsuo, pɛtro ne nkanea nnwumakuo no de sɔ Ghanafoɔ asene.
<<<P>>>
Asenesɔ Sika (Subsidy): Yei yɛ sika mu mmoa bi a Aban de ma ɔman mma ma ɛyi adesoa kakra firi wɔn so ma wɔn kɔn mu dwo wɔn.
<<<P>>>
Dwumadie akyiridie (Surveillance): Yei yɛ ɛkwan a yɛfa so de ani di biribi akyi na yɛhwɛ sɛdeɛ ɛsi teɛ.
<<<P>>>
Fannyɛ (Transparency): Yei ne sɛ Aban bɛma ɔman mma ahunu deɛ ɛrekɔ so na wɔate amanneɛbɔ nyinaa wɔ ɛberɛ ano ɛberɛ ano.
<<<P>>>
Adwadeɛ ne Dwumadie ho sika (Use of Goods and Services): Yei yɛ sika dodoɔ a Aban tua wɔ dwuma a ɔdi ne dwumadie a ɔdi ma ɔmanfoɔ ho ho.
<<<P>>>
Adwadeɛ ho ɛtoɔ (Value Added Tax (VAT): Yei yɛ ɛtoɔ bi a yɛde to adwadeɛ ahodoɔ so wɔ adwadeɛyɛ mpɛmpɛnsoɔ biara mu.
