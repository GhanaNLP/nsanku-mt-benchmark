47. Ɔman Kwaeɛ Nhyɛananmu dwumadie no mu no, yɛde sii yɛn ani so sɛ yɛbɛdua nnua wɔ asase hɛkta mpem aduɔwɔtwe (80,000 ha) so, nanso yɛtumi duaa nnua no wɔ asase hɛkta mpem aduɔson enum, ɔha aduɔson (75,170ha) so, na yei mu no, yɛduaa nnua nketewa wɔ asase hɛkta mpem dunan, ne aduonum nson a ɛyɛ kwaeɛ foforɔ (14,057 new forest plantations) so, afei, asase hɛkta mpem edu, ahanan aduokron enum a ɛso nnua anyɛ yie (10,495 ha) no nso yɛduaa nnua hyɛɛ anan mu, ɛnna yɛsane duaa nnua wɔ mfusase hɛkta mpem aduonum, ahansia ne dunwɔtwe (50,618 ha farm lands) so.
