37. Ɔman Kwaeɛ mu Nnua Nhyɛananmu Dwumadie (National Afforestation Programme -NAP) no maa akwannya ma wɔfaa nnipa dodoɔ mpem aduɔson mmienu (72,000 persons) adwumam. Nnipa dodoɔ yi boroo nnipa mpem aduosia (60, 000 persons) a na ɛsi yɛn ani so sɛ yɛbɛfa wɔn adwumam ama wɔaboa nnuadua dwumadie ahodoɔ no. Yɛgyina Mmabunu Kwaeɛm Nnua Nhyɛananmu Dwumadie (Youth in Afforestation Programme) no so faa nnipa no mu dodoɔ mpem aduasa enum (35,000) ɛnna yɛgyinaa deɛ wɔato edin sɛ Modified Taungya System (MTS) no so faa nnipa no mu dodoɔ mpem Dunson (17,000) ɛnna afei yɛgyinaa dwumadie a wɔfa nnipa ma wɔgu aba so faa nnipa mpem mmienu (2,000). Ankorankorɛ nnwuma nso faa nnipa dodoɔ mpem dunwɔtwe (18,000.)

38. Mmabunu Adwumanya Adwumakuo (Youth Employment Agency (YEA) no gyinaa wɔn nkorabata ahodoɔ so faa nnipa dodoɔ mpem aduɔwɔtwe mmienu, ahanwɔtwe ne aduosia nkron (82,869 beneficiaries). Beaeɛ a wɔfrɛ no “The Job Centre”, a ɛyɛ ntamgyinafoɔ ma wɔn a wɔrepɛ adwuma ne nnwumakuo no boa maa nnipa dodoɔ apem baako, ɔha ne aduɔwɔtwe enum (1,185 persons) nyaa adwuma.

39. Ɔhaw a COVID-19 yareɛ no de baeɛ no nyinaa akyi, yɛgu so ara renya kankorɔ wɔ Mansini Baako Afididwuma Baako (IDIF) ho dwumadie mu. Ɛfiri afe 2017 bɛsi ɛnnɛ, Mansini Baako Afididwuma Baako(1D1F) dodoɔ ahanu aduoson nwɔtwe (278 1D1F) na yɛadi ɛho dwuma ama aduru mpɛmpɛnsoɔ ahodoɔ. Emu ɔha ne nsia gu so redi dwuma seesei (106 in operation), yɛgu so a yɛresi emu ɔha ne aduanan nwɔtwe (148 construction), ɛnna emu aduonu ɛnan deɛ, afei koraa na wɔrebɛfiri aseɛ (24 pipeline projects). Bɛsi ɛnnɛ nnwuma no a wɔafiri aseɛ redi dwuma no afa nnipa ahodoɔ mpem ɔha aduonum nsia, ahanson aduɔwɔtwe mmienu (156,782 persons) wɔ adwumam) na saa nnipa dodoɔ yi mpem dunson, ahanan ne aduonum baako (17,451 persons) deɛ, wɔfaa wɔn wɔ adwuma mu wɔ afe 2021 mu.
