YERUNNSUA JEHOVAH HO ADZE NNWIE DA
<<<P>>>
17 Yebosua Jehovah ho adze pii wɔ wiadze fofor no mu. Jehovah bɛma hɛn nwoma mbobɔwee bi a ɔbɛma yeehu dza ɔpɛ dɛ yɛyɛ.
<<<P>>>
17-18. Ebɛnadze ntsi na yerunntum nnsua Jehovah ho adze nnwie da?
<<<P>>>
(Nyi. 20:12) Dɛm aber no, yebenya hokwan esua ndzɛmba a Jehovah abɔ ho adze akɔ ekyir na iyi bɛma yeehu no yie paa. (Isa. 11:6-9) Afei so, yebohu ndzɛmba afofor pii afa Jehovah ho. Obenyan hɛn adɔfo a woewuwu no aba nkwa mu bio na ɔbɔboa hɛn nkakrankakra ma yɛabɛyɛ nyimpa a bɔn biara nnyi hɛnho. Iyinom nyina bɔboa hɛn ma yeehu Jehovah yie.
<<<P>>>
18 Jehovah dze, yerunntum nnsua no ho adze nnwie da. Osiandɛ ɔtse hɔ daa ntsi, ‘ne ntseasee nnyi ano.’ (Ndw. 90:2; 147:5) Bible no ma yehu dɛ ‘yerunntum nnhu edwuma a Nyankopɔn ayɛ ofitsi ahyɛse dze kodu ewiei no mu.’ (Ɔsɛnk. 3:11) Dɛm ntsi sɛ yɛbaa nokwar no mu akyɛr o, sɛ ndaansa yi ara na yɛreba mu o, hom mma hɛn nyina mfa hokwan biara a yebenya no nsua hɛn Egya a ɔdɔ hɛn no ho adze pii.

MFONYIN NO HO NKYERƐKYERƐMU Ɔweɔn-Aban yi enyim: Ebed-melech riyi Jeremiah efi bura bi mu, na iyi ma yehu dɛ nna ɔwɔ akokodur paa. Krataafa 18: Onua bi rodwendwen mbrɛ Jehovah sii boaa no ma otumii yɛɛ no dwumadzi wɔ dapɛn mfinimfin nhyiamu ase no ho. Nna ɔnnda famu mma no osiandɛ sɛ ogyina nkorɔfo enyim rekasa a nna n’akoma etu.
