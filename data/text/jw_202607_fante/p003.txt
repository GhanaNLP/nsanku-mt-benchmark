Gibeonfo no daadaa Joshua ma ɔnye hɔn yɛɛ asomdwee apam
(Hwɛ nkyekyɛmu 4)
<<<P>>>
dɛ Jehovah ahyɛ Israelfo no bɔ dɛ ɔdze Canaan asaase no bɛma hɔn. (Ex. 34:11; Josh. 9:24) Dɛm ntsi aber a Israelfo no dzii Jericho na Ai do konyim no, Gibeonfo no faa hɔnho adwen. Wotuu nkorɔfo* dɛ wɔnkɔ Joshua hɔ wɔ Gilgal na wɔnkɛpa no kyɛw dɛ mma ɔnye hɔn mmbɔko.

* Mbarimba a Gibeonfo no tuu hɔn dɛ wɔnye Israelfo no nkɛkasa no, nna hɔn mu binom fi Gibeon kurow no ara mu, na nna binom so fi Chephirah, Beeroth, na Kiriath-jearim. Nna hɔn nyina yɛ Hivifo.—Josh. 9:17.
<<<P>>>
4. (a) Dɛ mbrɛ Joshua 9:8-13 ma yehu no, Gibeonfo no sii dɛn daadaa Israelfo no ma wɔnye hɔn yɛɛ asomdwee apam? (Hwɛ mfonyin no so.) (b) Aber a Israelfo no bohun dɛ Gibeonfo no adaadaa hɔn no, ebɛnadze na wɔmaa wɔyɛɛ?
<<<P>>>
Gibeonfo no nyim nyansa paa ntsi wɔammbɔ asɛm a nna ato Jericho na Ai no do. Osiandɛ nna dɛm asɛm no sii nnkyɛree, ntsi sɛ wɔkaa ho asɛm a nkyɛ Israelfo no bohu dɛ wommfi ‘ekyirekyir’ biara. Joshua nye Israel mbarimba no emmbisa Jehovah n’adwen wɔ asɛm no ho, na wɔgyee Gibeonfo no dzii na wɔnye hɔn yɛɛ asomdwee apam. (Josh. 9:14, 15) Ɔno ekyir no, annkyɛr biara na wobohun dɛ Gibeonfo no adaadaa hɔn. Naaso nhyehyɛɛ biara a wɔnye hɔn yɛe no, woenntwa mu, osiandɛ nna wɔdze ‘Jehovah ne dzin edzi nsew akyerɛ hɔn.’ (Josh. 9:16-19) Israelfo no nye Gibeonfo no tsenae na wɔkaa kyerɛɛ hɔn dɛ wɔbɛma wɔayɛ ‘nduatwafo nye nsusawfo dze ama mboanofo no, nye Jehovah n’afɔrmbukyia no’ hɔn nkwa nda nyina.—Josh. 9:27.
