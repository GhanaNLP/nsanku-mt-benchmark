5. Ebɛn awerɛhyɛmu na Jehovah dze ama hɛn wɔ Isaiah 42:16? (Hwɛ mfonyin no so.)
<<<P>>>
kyerɛ hɛn kwan na ɔbɛma sum no adan kan. Sɛ hɛn asetsena sesa prɛko pɛr na yennyim dza ɔda hɛn enyim a, ɔbɛyɛ tse dɛ mbrɛ hɛn enyi efura anaa yɛnam kwan bi a yennyim do do. Anaadɛ ɔbɛyɛ tse dɛ mbrɛ yɛnam sum mu anaa yɛnam kwan bi a akyeakyea do. Bi so a ɔbɛma yɛatse nka dɛ yɛnam kwaa ku-suww bi mu a yennhu beebi a yɛmfa hɛn tsir mfa. Naaso Jehovah ama hɛn awerɛhyɛmu dɛ ɔmmfa ho dza obesi wɔ hɛn abrabɔ mu biara ɔbɛkyerɛ hɛn kwan, na ɔrenndan n’e-kyir mma hɛn dabiarada. Ɔse obosuo hɛn nsamu na ɔakyerɛ hɛn kwan afa sum mu na ɔama hɛn kwan do ayɛ kann. Na kwan a no do akyeakyea no so, ɔbɛtsentsen ne nyina. Je-hovah si dɛn yɛ iyi? Ɔnam n’Asɛm Bible no nye akwankyerɛ a ofi n’ahyehyɛdze no hɔ no do na ɔyɛ dɛm. (Ndw. 119:105; Isa. 30:21) Afei so, ɔfa hɛn nuanom Christianfo nye afo-for do boa hɛn. Dɛm ntsi sɛ asɛm bi si ma hɛn
