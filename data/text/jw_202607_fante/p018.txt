15 Hɛn mu piinara tse kurow mu ntsi ndzɛmba ahorow a Jehovah abɔ no, no mu pii wɔ hɔ a, yennhu. Sɛ etse kurow mu mpo a, ibotum esua Jehovah ho adze efi biribi a ɔabɔ mu. Hom mma yɛnhwɛ dza onua bi a wɔfrɛ no Patrick yɛ. Otse kurow kɛse bi a ɔwɔ Republic of the Congo a wɔfrɛ no Brazzaville mu. Ɔkaa dɛ: “Da biara mibegyina me fie n’e-nyim na mohwɛ Jehovah n’abɔdze. Metaa hu mbowa nketsenketse bi tse dɛ mpataakowa anaa nnoma a hɔn enyi aber rohwehwɛ edzi-ban anaa beebi a wɔbɛda. Mber bi, muhun dɛ nnoma bi reyɛ hɔn buw mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a wobɛ-nya beebi abɔbɔ hɔn mba egu. Hɛn mpotamu hɔ ara na wɔsesaaw ndzɛmba dze yɛɛ hɔn buw no.” Patrick annhwɛ dza nna nnoma no reyɛ no kɛkɛ, mbom ɔdwendween Ɔbɔadze no ho kɔr ekyir. Ɔtoaa n’asɛm no do dɛ: “Mu-hun dɛ Jehovah dwen n’abɔdze ho paa. Mbo-wa nketsenketse a ɔtse dɛ mbrɛ mfaso biara nnyi hɔn do no mpo, ɔdwen hɔn ho. Iyi ma minya awerɛhyɛmu dɛ Jehovah dwen mo ho paa, ntsi dza mihia wɔ asetsena mu biara no, ɔremma ɔmmbɔ me da.”
