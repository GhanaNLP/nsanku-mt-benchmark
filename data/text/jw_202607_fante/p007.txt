16 Yebesi dɛn esuasua Gibeonfo no? Ɔsɛ dɛ yɛyɛ dza yebotum biara som Jehovah na yɛboa afofor so ma wɔyɛ dɛm. Ɔtɔ da a iyi behia dɛ yɛyɛ nsesa ahorow bi wɔ hɛn asetsena mu. Naaso osiandɛ yɛdɔ Jehovah na yɛpɛ dɛ yɛyɛ dza ɔsɔ n’enyi ntsi, yɛdze enyigye yɛ dɛm. Dɛmara na onua bi a ɔwɔ Philippines a wɔfrɛ no Alwin no yɛe. Nna ɔyɛ edwuma wɔ oil company bi mu na nna akatua no so ye paa. Naaso nna ɔpɛ dɛ ɔdze ne mber pii som Jehovah. Ntsi aber biara a nna ɔmansin do hwɛfo bɛba abɔsom hɔn asafo no, nna Alwin gye leave wɔ edwuma mu mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a obenya mber pii dze akɔ asɛnka dɛm dapɛn no. Nna iyi ma n’enyi gye paa. Odur beebi no, nna ɔpɛ dɛ ɔbɛyɛ daa kwampaafo. Ntsi ɔbɔɔ asɛm yi ho mpaa na ɔyɛɛ n’adwen dɛ obeyaa n’edwuma no na ɔapɛ edwuma bi a obotum ama oenya mber pii dze akɔ asɛnka. Nna Alwin nyim dɛ edwuma a obenya no remma onnya sika pii dze, naaso nna obotum aboa no ma ɔayɛ akwampaa edwuma no. Ntsi aber a onyaa edwuma bi a ɔtse dɛm no n’enyi gyee paa. Ekyir yi, ne yer so bɛyɛɛ ɔkwampaafo na ɔnye ne yer etum aboa nyimpa 21 ma wɔabɔsom Jehovah. Yɛgye dzi paa dɛ Jehovah n’enyi sɔ mbɔdzen a dɛm awarfo yi robɔ dze asom no no. Dɛmara na hɛn so, yebotum enya awerɛhyɛmu dɛ nsesa biara a yɛbɛyɛ mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a yebotum dze hɛn ber nyina asom Jehovah no, n’enyi sɔ. Na sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm so a, ɔbɛma hɛn nsa aka dza yehia biara.—Matt. 6:33.

17 Mbrɛ Jehovah nye Gibeonfo no dzii no ma yehu no suban ahorow bi a ɔyɛ fɛw. Ɔma yehu dɛ ɔbrɛ noho adze, ɔwɔ ehumbɔbɔr, ɔmmpɛ nsisi na odzi nokwar so. Afei so, n’asomfo a wɔdɔ no na wɔdze nokwardzi som no no, ohyira hɔn na ɔma hɔn nsa ka dza wohia biara. Hɛnara so, yebotum esua ndzɛmba pii efi Gibeonfo no hɔn asɛm no mu. Bi nye dɛ sɛ yɛgye Jehovah dzi paa a, aber a asɛm bi esi ma hɛn akoma etu no, yɛdze hɛnho bɔto no do. Afei so, sɛ yɛgye Jehovah dzi a, edwuma biara a wɔdze bɛhyɛ hɛn nsa wɔ asafo no mu no, yɛbɛbrɛ hɛnho adze na yɛayɛ. Sɛ wɔnye hɛn enndzi no yie so a, yebegyaa biribiara mu ama Jehovah na yeesie abotar atweɔn no, osiandɛ yɛwɔ awerɛhyɛmu dɛ obedzi ho dwuma wɔ ne ber a ɔsɛ mu. Bio so, yɛbɛyɛ dza yebotum biara dze nokwardzi asom Jehovah. Sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm a, ɔkyerɛ dɛ yerusuasua Gibeonfo no.
