no, wɔkaa hɔnho bɔɔ mu na wokotwaa Gibeon kurow no ho hyiae dɛ wɔnye hɔn rokɔko. Gibeonfo no hɔn akoma tui ntsi wɔserɛɛ Joshua dɛ ɔmbra mbɔboa hɔn. (Josh. 10:3-7) Ntsi Joshua nye n’asogyafo no kɔboaa Gibeonfo no ma wɔkoe tsiaa hɔn atamfo no. Na Jehovah so boaa hɔn ma wodzii hɔn do konyim. Jehovah mpo dze ɔsorbo kunkum Amorifo hɔn asogyafo no beberee na ɔmaa ewia gyinaa fakor kesii dɛ Israelfo no dzii hɔn atamfo no do konyim. (Josh. 10:9-14) Aber a Gibeonfo no nye Israelfo no kɛyɛɛ asomdwee apam no, nna wɔgye dzi dɛ dza Jehovah aka biara no bɛba mu. Afei so, aber a hɔnho kyeer hɔn na wɔfrɛɛ Joshua dɛ ɔmbɔboa hɔn no, nna wɔgye dzi dɛ Jehovah botum agye hɔn. Iyi ma yehu dɛ nna Gibeonfo no gye Jehovah dzi paa.

6 Ebɛnadze na dɛm asɛm yi ma yehu fa Jehovah ho? Ɔma yehu dɛ Jehovah brɛ noho adze na ɔwɔ ehumbɔbɔr. Nna Jehovah edzi kan aka akyerɛ Israelfo no dɛ ‘wɔmpam hɔn a wɔtse Canaan asaase no do no nyinara mfi do,’ na nna Gibeonfo no ka ho. (Nka. 33:51, 52) Naaso osian asomdwee apam a Israelfo no nye Gibeonfo no yɛe no ntsi, Jehovah hun Gibeonfo no mbɔbɔr na ɔmaa wɔnye Israelfo no tsenae. Ɔwɔ mu Israelfo no nye Jehovah ennsusu asɛm no ho ansaana wɔnye Gibeonfo no reyɛ apam, naaso Jehovah gyee nhyehyɛɛ a wɔnye hɔn yɛe no too mu. Afei so, Jehovah boaa Israelfo no ma wodzii nhyehyɛɛ biara a nna wɔnye Gibeonfo no ayɛ no do na ɔgyee hɔn fii hɔn atamfo nsamu.—Josh. 9:26; 11:19.

7 Yebesi dɛn esuasua Gibeonfo no? Ɔsɛ dɛ yɛgye Jehovah dzi paa. Hɛn dze, yenyim Jehovah yie koraa kyɛn Gibeonfo no, ne dɛm ntsi ɔsɛ dɛ yɛdze hɛnho nyina to no do. (Ndw. 40:4, 5) Afei so, ndwuma ahorow bi a nkorɔfo mmbu no, sɛ yefi hɛn pɛ mu yɛ dze boa Jehovah no som a, nna ɔkyerɛ dɛ yerusuasua Gibeonfo no. (Josh. 9:23, 27) Onua aberantsɛ bi a wɔfrɛ no Luke yɛ edwuma wɔ Bethel. Na ɔse mber bi onua panyin bi a ɔyɛ edwuma wɔ Bethel yɛɛ biribi na ɔmaa ohun dɛ ɔbrɛ noho adze paa. Nna hokwan ahorow pii hyehyɛ dɛm onua yi ne nsa wɔ Bethel. Naaso aber a nna worisi Ahendzi Asa bi na wɔpɛ nkorɔfo ma wɔakɔwɛɔn hɔ no, dɛm onua no tuu noho mae. Luke kaa dɛ: “Mubohun dɛ onnyi dɛ mema m’adwen yɛ me dɛ mebrɛ moho adze. Mbom sɛ mebrɛ moho adze ampaara a, ɔbɛda edzi wɔ mbrɛ misi yɛ m’adze no mu.” Dɛ mbrɛ Gibeonfo no yɛe no, hɛn so sɛ yɛdze hɛnho to Jehovah do aber a yerihyia ɔhaw bi a, nna yɛreda no edzi dɛ yɛgye no dzi. Afei so sɛ yɛgye Jehovah dzi a, edwuma biara a wɔdze bɛhyɛ hɛn nsa wɔ asafo no mu no yɛbɛbrɛ hɛnho adze na yɛayɛ.

8 Mfe pii ekyir no, Bible no kaa dza ɔtoo Gibeonfo no wɔ Ɔhen Saul n’aber do ho asɛm. Ɔhen Saul yɛɛ dɛ ɔbɔtɔr Gibeonfo a nna ‘wɔtse Israel na Judah nkurow no mu no nyina’ hɔn ase.* Iyi ntsi okunkum hɔn mu beberee. (2 Sam. 21:2, 5, 6) Ɔhen Saul annyɛ adze koraa. Asomdwee apam a nna Israelfo no nye Gibeonfo no ayɛ mfe pii no, Saul buu do.
