Nsɛmbisa A Ɔboa Hɛn Ma Yetum Kyerɛkyerɛ Yie
<<<P>>>
Sɛ ehwɛ Ibotum Enya Enyigye Daa! buukuu no mu a, ibohu dɛ nsɛmbisa pii wɔ mu a ibotum dze aboa wo Bible esuafo ma woehu Jehovah yie. No mu bi na odzidzi do yi:
<<<P>>>
“Jehovah hu hɔn a wɔka asɛmpa no dɛn?” (adzesua 21)
<<<P>>>
“Sɛ ehwɛ dza esua afa Jehovah na Jesus ho no a, sɛ worubu atsɛn [wɔ Amagedon] a, ehwɛ a wobobu no pɛpɛɛpɛr, anaa wɔbɛkyea no? Ebɛnadze ntsi na ereka dɛm?” (adzesua 33)
<<<P>>>
“Sɛ eyɛ onyansafo na itsie Jehovah n’asɛm a, ɔtse nka dɛn? Ebɛnadze ntsi a?” (adzesua 34)
<<<P>>>
“Sɛ yɛwar obi a ɔnndɔ Jehovah a, ehwɛ a Jehovah bɛtse nka dɛn wɔ ho?” (adzesua 42)
<<<P>>>
“Ebɛnadze na ɔwɔ kyerɛwsɛm yi mu a ɔma yehu dɛ sɛ Jehovah dze bɔn kyɛ a, nna ɔdze akyɛ ampa?” (adzesua 56)
<<<P>>>
“Ebɛn kwan do na mbrɛ Jehovah nye ndzebɔnyɛfo dzi nsɛm no ma yehu dɛ ɔwɔ ehumbɔbɔr, ɔtse asɛm ase na ɔdɔ hɛn?” (adzesua 57)
<<<P>>>
ɔwɔ Ibotum Enya Enyigye Daa! buukuu no mu no botum aboa Bible esuafo ma woesua ndzɛmba pii afa Jehovah ho na woehu mbrɛ osi tse. (Hwɛ adakaba a wɔato dzin “Nsɛmbisa A Ɔboa Hɛn Ma Yetum Kyerɛkyerɛ Yie” no.) Ntsi ɔyɛ a bisa wo suanyi no dɛm nsɛm no, na ɔbɔboa no ma oehu mbrɛ Jehovah si tse ankasa.
<<<P>>>
9 Sɛ yɛhyɛ ase dɛ yɛnye nkorɔfo rusua Bible no a, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛhyɛ hɔn nkuran ma wɔkenkan Bible no da biara.* Yebesi dɛn aboa hɔn ma woenya hɔn Bible akenkan no do mfaso paa? Yebotum ama woehu dɛ, sɛ wɔkenkan Bible mu asɛm bi wie a, wobotum adwendwen siantsir a Jehovah maa wɔkyerɛɛw dɛm asɛm no nye Jehovah no suban ahorow bi a dɛm asɛm no ma wohu no ho. Biribi dɛmara na osuanyi James yɛe. Aber a nna ɔreka Job ho asɛm no, ɔnnyɛ mbrɛ omiaa n’enyi gyinaa ɔhaw a ɔfaa mu no ho asɛm nkotsee na ɔkae. Mbom ɔkaa Jehovah no suban so ho asɛm. Ɔkaa dɛ Jeho-vah wɔ ‘ayamuhyehye nye ehumbɔbɔr.’—Kenkan James 5:11.
<<<P>>>
10 Yɛpɛ dɛ yɛboa hɛn Bible esuafo ma wohu mbrɛ Bible mu asɛm bi botum aboa hɔn ma woehu dza Jehovah pɛ nye dza ɔmmpɛ.
