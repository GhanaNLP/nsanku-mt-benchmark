mma yɛnhwɛ asɛm bi a ɔtoo Akyerɛbaa Laura French. Afe 1926 mu no, ɔhyɛɛ ase dɛ ɔre-yɛ edwuma wɔ Canada Bethel. Bɛyɛ mfe du ekyir no, wotwaa asɛm too no do yayaayaw. Wɔbɔɔ no sombo dɛ ɔdze noho akɔbɔ awaa-fo ho, ntsi wɔmaa ofii Bethel. Ebɛnadze na akyerɛbaa no yɛe? Ɔwɔ mu dɛ asɛm no yɛɛ no yaw dze, naaso ɔannkasa enntsia obiara. Mfe anan a odzi hɔ no, ɔyeer noho yɛɛ akwam-paa edwuma no. Afei wɔ afe 1940 no, wɔsanee frɛɛ no dɛ ɔmbɛyɛ edwuma wɔ Bethel bio. N’enyi gyee paa, osiandɛ nna ɔnnhwɛ kwan. Ɔno ekyir no, Akyerɛbaa French dze nokwar-dzi soom Jehovah mfe 50 kesii dɛ owiee no som edwuma wɔ asaase yi do. Dɛ mbrɛ Akyerɛ-baa French yɛe no, hɛn so sɛ wɔnye hɛn enndzi no yie a, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛkɔ do ara yɛ dza ɔtsen na ye-nya abotar tweɔn Jehovah kesi dɛ obedzi asɛm no ho dwuma wɔ ne ber a ɔsɛ mu.—Isa. 26:3, 4.

13 Bɛyɛ mfe 500 wɔ Ɔhen David no wu ekyir no, Bible no sanee kaa Gibeonfo no ho asɛm bio. Dɛm aber no, nna Jewfo no akɔ nkowaa-som mu wɔ Babylon mfe 70. Na wɔ afe 537 mu ansaana wɔrobɔwo Jesus no, Jewfo binom nye Ambrado Zerubbabel saan hɔn ekyir baa Je-rusalem. (Ezra 2:1, 2, 58) Afei, wɔ afe 468 ansaana wɔrobɔwo Jesus no, binom so nye ɔkyerɛwfo Ezra bae. (Ezra 7:1-7) Hɔn a wɔnye Zerubbabel na Ezra nyina sanee baa Jerusa-lem no, nna hɔnho nndɔɔ so na nna “Nethinfo” binom ka ho. (Ezra 2:58; 7:7) Nna woananom nye Nethinfo no? Nna wɔyɛ nkowaa a wɔsom wɔ asɔrfie hɔ naaso nna wɔnnyɛ Israelfo, na ɔbɛyɛ dɛ nna hɔn mu pii yɛ Gibeonfo no hɔn asefo.

14 Kenkan 1 Mbermunsɛm 9:2. Nna “Ne-thinfo” ka Israelfo a wodzii kan sanee kɔr hɔn kurow mu no ho. Iyi ma yehu dɛ nna wɔwɔ gye-dzi paa, osiandɛ nna Jewfo binom mpo mmpɛ dɛ wɔsan kɔ hɔn kurow mu. Nna Jewfo bebe-ree dzi yie wɔ Babylon na nna wɔnnyɛɛ hɔn adwen dɛ wobegya dɛm ndzɛmba no nyina hɔ akɛhyɛ abrabɔ ase fofor wɔ hɔn kurow mu na wɔakɔboa ma wɔasan akyekyer hɔn kurow no. Naaso nna hɔn mu binom nye Gibeonfo hɔn asefo binom pɛ dɛ wɔsan kɔ Jerusalem kesi asɔrfie no mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a wobotum asom Jeho-vah. Ɔwɔ mu dɛ nna edwuma a wɔrekɛyɛ no nnyɛ edwuma kakraba dze, naaso wɔbɔɔ mbɔ-dzen kɔree. Afei so, nna Jewfo no wɔ nsaase wɔ Israel naaso Gibeonfo hɔn asefo no dze, nna wonnyi egyapadze biara wɔ hɔ. Naaso wɔ-sanee kɔr Israel na wɔkɔtoaa hɔn ndwuma a nna wɔyɛ wɔ asɔrfie hɔ no do, na wɔboae mpo ma wɔsanee toow Jerusalem afasu no.—Neh. 3:26.

Ofitsi aber a Jehovah gyee Gibeon-fo no dze besi aber a Israelfo no fii Babylon nkowaasom mu bae no, nna wɔnye Israelfo no atsena bɛyɛ mfe 1,000 na Jehovah kɔr do ara hyiraa hɔn. Dɛ mfatohɔ no, aber a Babylonfo no sɛɛ Jerusalem wɔ afe 607 ansaana wɔrobɔ-wo Jesus no, Jehovah gyee Gibeonfo hɔn ase-fo no.
