Menye Miwako dze bɛyɛ mfe 24 hwɛɛ n’awofo kesii dɛ wowuwui. Dɛm aber no mu no, nna yɛtaa yɛ kɔɔl, na nna hɛn yamu so hyehye hɛn paa. Nna yedzi ayarsabea akɔnaaba dɛmara. Miwako ne maame Masako ne yarba no mu yɛɛ dzen araa ma nna onntum mpo nnsoɛr nnantsew. Nna ɔnnda famu mma Masako dɛ ɔbɔkɔ adzesua na asɛnka. Aka adapɛn kakra ma oewu no mpo dze, nna ɔtsena ebubuafo egua mu. Naaso iyi nyina mu no, ɔkyerɛɛ dɛ ɔdɔ Jehovah paa. Sɛ ɔreka nokwar no ho asɛm a, nna ɔma n’enyi gye. Nna dza Masako wɔ biara n’enyi sɔ, na ɔmmfa ho dza osii wɔ n’abrabɔ mu biara no, ɔkɔr do dze enyigye soom Nyankopɔn. Na iyi yɛ biribi a mepɛ dɛ musua fi no hɔ.

Afe 2024 no, hɛn asetsena sesae koraa. Wɔkaa kyerɛɛ hɛn dɛ yɛnkɛyɛ akwampaafo etsitsir; nna yɛdze bɔbor mfe 30 na yɛdze asom wɔ Bethel. Ntsi sesei, yɛrosom wɔ Okinawa supɔw no do, na yeenya hokwan ridzi mbusua a wɔtsetse asogyafo atsenae a ɔbɛn ha no dase. Ntsetsee a yenyae wɔ Bethel no boaa hɛn paa, ntsi aber a wɔkaa dɛ yɛnkɛyɛ akwampaafo etsitsir no, annyɛ dzen amma hɛn koraa. Yɛyɛ nhyehyɛɛ pa na yɛdze hɛn ber so dzi dwuma yie, mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a yɛbɔkɔ do ara ayer hɛnho ayɛ asɛnka edwuma no. Jehovah aboa hɛn so ma yeenya Bible esuafo a yɛnye hɔn tse dɛ hɛn ebusuafo. Yeebohu paa dɛ, adze a ohia paa nye dɛ yɛbɔdɔ nyimpa. Menye Miwako da Jehovah ase da biara dɛ ɔdze dɛm edwuma kamakama yi ahyɛ hɛn nsa.
