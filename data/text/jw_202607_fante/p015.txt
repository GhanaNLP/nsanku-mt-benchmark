SƐ WƆKA DƐ OBI NYIM JEHOVAH YIE A, ƆKYERƐ DƐN?
<<<P>>>
4 Sɛ yɛhwɛ asɛm a Jehovah kae faa Jewfo a nna wobefi Babylon asan aba hɔn kurow mu no ho no a, ɔma yehu dza ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yeehu Jehovah yie. Jehovah kaa dɛ: “Mebɛma hɔn akoma ma wɔdze ehu me.” (Jer. 24:7) Naaso sɛ nna wobotum ehu no yie paa a, nna ɔsɛ dɛ wɔdze hɔn akoma nyina som no. Nhwehwɛmu nwoma bi ka dɛ: “Sɛ obi nyim Nyankopɔn ho nsɛm a, ɔno ara kɛkɛ nnkyerɛ dɛ onyim no yie. Nsonsonee wɔ mu. Akɛyɛ tse dɛ obi a ɔakenkan ɔdɔ ho asɛm nye obi a ɔatsew ahwe obi no dɔ mu.” Dɛm ntsi ɔnnyɛ nyim a yenyim dɛ Nyankopɔn wɔ hɔ na ne dzin dze Jehovah no nye asɛm no, na mbom ɔsɛ dɛ yehu mbrɛ osi tse ankasa.
<<<P>>>
5 Sɛ yehu Jehovah yie a, ɔbɛma yeehu dza ɔpɛ nye dza ɔmmpɛ. Ɔwodze ma yɛnhwɛ Ɔhen Josiah n’asɛm no. Bible no ka dɛ: “N’ahendzi mu afe a otsia awɔtwe no a ɔda ho yɛ abofra no, nna ɔhyɛɛ ase dɛ ɔrohwehwe n’egya David ne Nyankopɔn no n’ekyir kwan.” (2 Mber. 34:3) Naaso Josiah ennsua Jehovah ho adze ara kɛkɛ mbom ɔdze dza osuae no bɔɔ ne bra. Jehovah nam nkɔnhyɛnyi Jeremiah do kaa dɛ, Josiah “buu atsɛntsenenee na ɔyɛɛ dza ɔtsen na odzii abɔbɔfo nye ehiafo hɔn asɛm maa hɔn.” Afei Jehovah bisaa dɛ, ‘Ɔnnyɛ iyi na ɔkyerɛ dɛ obi nyim me anaa?’—Jer. 22:15, 16.

6 Kenkan Ndwom 9:10. Sɛ yehu Jehovah
<<<P>>>
yie a, ɔbɛma yɛdze hɛn werɛ nyina ahyɛ no mu. Ɔsomafo Paul hun dɛ dɛm asɛm yi yɛ nokwar. Aber a nna ɔda efiadze ma aka kakra ma woeku no no, ɔkyerɛɛw dɛ: “Ɔnnyɛ me enyito. Osiandɛ minyim Obi a magye no edzi no.” (2 Tim. 1:12) Dza nna Paul esua afa Nyankopɔn ho nye mbrɛ nna ɔaboa no wɔ n’abrabɔ mu ntsi, ɔmaa otumii dze no werɛ nyina hyɛɛ no mu. Iyi ma yehu dɛ nna Paul wɔ awerɛhyɛmu dɛ Jehovah no werɛ rimmfir nokwardzi a ɔdze asom no no. Na nna ɔgye dzi dɛ sɛ owu a, Jehovah benyan no aba nkwa mu bio.

EBƐNADZE NTSI NA ƆSƐ DƐ YƐKƆ DO ARA HU JEHOVAH YIE?
<<<P>>>
7 Yehyɛɛ ase dɛ yerusua Jehovah ho adze no, yehun ndzɛmba pii faa no ho. Naaso sɛ yɛasom Jehovah akyɛr a, annhwɛ a obodu beebi no yɛrennyer hɛnho nnsua no ho adze tse dɛ mbrɛ nna yɛyɛ no nkan no. Ebɛnadze na obotum ama aba no dɛm? Sɛ yɛrekenkan Bible anaa yɛkɔ adzesua a, mbrɛ yɛdze dza yebosua no bɔbɔ hɛn bra nye mbrɛ yebesi dze aboa afofor no, annhwɛ a ɔno nkotsee na yɛdze hɛn adwen besi do. Sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm a, mfom biara nnyi ho osiandɛ dɛmara so na Bible no tu hɛn fo dɛ yɛnyɛ. (2 Tim. 3:16, 17) Naaso Bible no san so ma yehu ndzɛmba beberee fa Jehovah ho. (Ndw. 25:8-10) Dɛm ntsi sɛ yɛrekenkan Bible no a, ɔnnyɛ mbrɛ obesi aboa hɛn nye mbrɛ yebesi dze aboa afofor no nkotsee na ɔsɛ dɛ yɛma no ho hia hɛn. Mbom yebotum so ebisa hɛnho dɛ, ‘Dza merekenkan yi, ebɛnadze na ɔma muhu fa Jehovah Nyankopɔn ho?’*
