BOA WO BIBLE SUANYI NO MA ONHU JEHOVAH YIE
<<<P>>>
4 Jesus kaa dɛ ahyɛdze a Jehovah dze ama hɛn no, dza ohia wɔ mu paa nye dɛ obi dze n’akoma nyina, ne kra nyina, n’adwen nyina nye n’ahoɔdzen nyina bɔdɔ no. (Kenkan Mark 12:30.) Sɛ Bible suanyi bi botum adɔ Jehovah a, ɔsɛ dɛ odzi kan hu no yie. Dɛm dɔ no na ɔsɛ dɛ ɔka no ma ohyira noho do ma Jehovah na ɔnnyɛ hɛn nkyerɛkyerɛ bi a n’enyi gye ho anaadɛ ɔpɛ hɛn asɛm araa ntsi. Dɛm ntsi sɛ yɛnye obi rusua Bible no a, adze a ɔsɛ dɛ yɛma ɔtsena hɛn adwen mu paa nye dɛ yɛbɔboa no ma oehu Jehovah na ɔadɔ no.
<<<P>>>
5 Sɛ edɔ obi a etsie n’asɛm. Dɛm ntsi sɛ wo Bible suanyi no bohu Jehovah na ɔbɔdɔ no a, obefi n’akoma nyinara mu etsie no. Jesus kaa dɛ: “Dza Egya no ahyɛ me dɛ menyɛ no, ɔno ara na mereyɛ, mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a wiadze no bohu dɛ modɔ Egya no.” (John 14:31) Dɛmara na hɛn Bible esuafo so, sɛ wɔdɔ Jehovah kyɛn biribiara wɔ hɔn asetsena mu a, dza ɔbɛka dɛ wɔnyɛ biara wɔbɛyɛ na wɔronntwe hɔnho mmfi no ho da.—1 John 5:3.
<<<P>>>
6 Sɛ Bible suanyi bi bohu Jehovah yie a, ɔrennyɛ dzen mma no koraa dɛ obesisi gyinae pa. Ɔwodze ma yɛnyɛ mfatoho bi: Sɛ obi pɛ dɛ osua kaar ka a, ɔnnyɛ dɛ ɔkenkaan kaarka ho mbra anaa ɔdze n’enyi hwɛɛ mbrɛ wosi ka kaar no ara nna oehu ka. Mbom ɔsɛ dɛ nanka-
<<<P>>>
sa tsena steer ekyir na otwuw kaar no fa kwan do. Ɔba no dɛm a, obohu tsebea ahorow bi a obotum ehyia wɔ kwan do na oehu dza ɔbɛyɛ wɔ no mu biara mu, na nkakrankakra obohu mbrɛ wosi ka kaar yie. Dɛmara na hɛn Bible suanyi no so, sɛ orusua Bible no a, ɔno ara kɛkɛ nnkyerɛ dɛ onyim Jehovah. Mbom no, sɛ ɔdze dza orusua no bɔ ne bra na ohu no do mfaso a, ɔno na ɔbɛma oehu Jehovah yie. Afei, sɛ ɔkɔ do sua Jehovah ho adze pii na ohu mbrɛ no sor Egya no roboa no wɔ n’abrabɔ mu no a, ɔbɔboa no ma oetum esisi gyinae pa.
<<<P>>>
7 Nna Joseph nyim Jehovah yie ntsi aber a Potiphar ne yer yɛe araa dɛ ɔnye no bɛda no, oguanee. Aber a nna Joseph yɛ abofra no, ɔtsee Adam na Eve nye Abraham na Sarah hɔn ho asɛm. (Gen. 2:24; 20:3, 6) Ntsi obotum aba dɛ nna Joseph nyim Jehovah n’adwen wɔ nda mu nkitahodzi na awar ho. Nna Joseph nyim Jehovah yie paa ntsi aber a Potiphar ne yer kaa dɛ ɔnye no nda no, ɔannyɛ na mbom ɔkaa kyerɛɛ no dɛ: “Ɔbɛyɛ dɛn na mebɛyɛ emumuyɛ kɛse yi, na mayɛ bɔn metsia Nyankopɔn?” (Gen. 39:7-9) Nna Joseph nyim dza Jehovah pɛ nye dza ɔmmpɛ. Nna ɔmmpɛ dɛ ɔma Jehovah no werɛ how, ntsi osii gyinae pa. Hɛn so, sɛ yɛboa hɛn Bible esuafo ma wohu Jehovah yie na wohu dza ɔpɛ nye dza ɔmmpɛ a, ɔbɔboa hɔn ma woesisi gyinae pa.
