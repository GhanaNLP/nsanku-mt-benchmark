NHYIRA A HƆN A WONYIM JEHOVAH YIE NO NYA
<<<P>>>
17 Sɛ yɛnye nkorɔfo sua Bible no a, yɛkyerɛ hɔn ndzɛmba pii. Bi nye tsebea a ewufo wɔ mu, werdam no nye Nyankopɔn n’Ahendzi. Yɛsan so kyerɛ hɔn mbrɛ woesi ahyehyɛ ndzɛmba wɔ asafo no mu. Bi nye dza ɔkɔ do wɔ hɛn adzesua ase, edwuma a asafo mu mpanyimfo yɛ nye mbrɛ Akwankyerɛ Kuw no dzi ahyehyɛdze no enyim no. Naaso ɔnnyɛ dɛm ndzɛmba yi nkotsee na yɛpɛ dɛ yɛma hɛn esuafo no hu. Adze a ohia paa nye dɛ yɛbɔboa hɔn ma woehu Jehovah yie.
<<<P>>>
18 Ɔyɛ hokwan kɛse paa na yeenya dɛ yɛbɔboa nkorɔfo ma woehu Jehovah. Ndɛ mpo, hɔn a woebohu Jehovah na wɔrosom no no, wonya nhyira pii, na enyigye a wonya no so, worunntum mmfa nntoto biribiara ho. (Ndw. 25:12-15) Daakye so, wɔbɛtsena ase afebɔɔ. (1 John 5:20) Naaso ɔnnyɛ iyi nkotsee o, Bible no ma yehu dɛ hɔn a wɔrobɔ mbɔdzen dɛ wobohu Jehovah yie na wɔadɔ no no, ‘Jehovah so nyim hɔn.’ (1 Cor. 8:3) Nokwasɛm nye dɛ, Adze Nyina Do Tumfo Nyankopɔn no nyim hɛn ampa, na ɔafa hɛn dɛ n’anyɛnkofo. Iyi yɛ nhyira a ɔsen biara a yebotum enya aber a yeebohu nokwar Nyame kor pɛr no!
