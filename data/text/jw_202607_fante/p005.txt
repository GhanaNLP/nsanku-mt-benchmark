Suasua Gibeonfo no na edwuma biara a wɔdze bɛhyɛ wo nsa wɔ asafo no mu no, brɛ woho adze na yɛ
(Hwɛ nkyekyɛmu 7)
<<<P>>>
Ɔhen David bedzii adze ansaana ɔreyɛ asɛm no ho biribi. Dza ɔyɛe nye dɛ, ɔmaa ɔkɔm sii asaase no do mfe ebiasa. Na aber a David bisaa Jehovah siantsir a ɔama ɔkɔm no esi no, ɔmaa ohun dɛ Saul nye ne fiefo edzi bɔgya ho fɔ osiandɛ woekunkum Gibeonfo bebree.—2 Sam. 21:1.
<<<P>>>
10 Kenkan 2 Samuel 21:3-6. Nna David pɛ dɛ ohu dza obotum ayɛ dze apata bɔn a Saul dze ayɛ Gibeonfo no, ntsi ɔnye hɔn kasae. Nna Gibeonfo yi bɛma David etua sika pii mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a wobenya hɔnho anaa? Oho, wɔannyɛ dɛm, mbom wɔkaa kyerɛɛ David dɛ “ɔnnyɛ dwetɛ anaa sika” na wɔpɛ. Na dɛ mbrɛ Mbra no se no, sɛ obi kum obi a, wonntua sika mmpata no, mbom ɔsɛ dɛ woku no bi. (Nka. 35:30, 31) Afei so, nna wonyim dɛ hɔnankasa runntum mmfa Mbra no nnhyɛ hɔn nsamu na wonnku obi aber a wɔmmaa hɔn kwan. Ntsi ewiei koraa no, David maa wokunkum mbarimba beesuon bi a nna wofi Saul n’ebusua mu. Ɔbɛyɛ dɛ nna wɔka Saul ho ma wokunkum Gibeonfo no. Mber kakra ekyir no, nsu tɔree bio na ɔkɔm no no tun twae na iyi maa ɔdaa edzi dɛ Jehovah esiesie asɛm no ma n’ahom aka noho.—2 Sam. 21:9, 10, 14.
<<<P>>>
11 Ebɛnadze na dɛm asɛm yi ma yehu fa Jehovah ho? Ɔma yehu no pefee dɛ Jehovah mmpɛ nsisi. (Ndw. 37:28) Ɔpɛ dɛ yɛnye obiara dzi no yie. Ndɛ, nyimpa bebree noara bu ahɔho enyimtsia na binom so wɔ hɔ a, osian ebusua a wofi mu ntsi, nkorɔfo mmbu hɔn. Naaso hɛn dze, Jehovah pɛ dɛ yɛnye nyimpa nyina dzi no yie. Jehovah n’asomfo a wɔsoɛr tsia hɔn na wɔyɛ hɔn adze gyangyan no so, ɔahyɛ bɔ dɛ daakye ɔbɛma esisisɛm a ɔtse dɛm no nyina no tun etwa. Afei so, dɛ mbrɛ nna Jehovah hwɛ kwan dɛ Israelfo no bedzi bɔ a wɔdze hyɛɛ Gibeonfo no do no, dɛmara na ndɛ so ɔhwɛ kwan dɛ sɛ yɛhyɛ bɔ a yebedzi do.—Fa toto Amos 1:9 ho.

12 Yebesi dɛn esuasua Gibeonfo no? Sɛ hɛn nuanom Christianfo mpo nye hɛn enndzi no yie a, ɔsɛ dɛ yenya abotar na yegyaa biribiara mu ma Jehovah. Sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm a, nna ɔkyerɛ dɛ yɛgye dzi dɛ wɔ Jehovah ne ber a ɔsɛ mu no, obedzi asɛm no ho dwuma. Hɔm
