15 Ɔwɔ mu dɛ bɔ a Jehovah ahyɛ dɛ obenyan hɛn adɔfo a woewuwu no rinnyi awerɛhow a yeridzi no sesei no mmfi hɔ dze, naaso yenyim dɛ Jehovah no werɛ rimmfir hɔn na iyi kyekye hɛn werɛ. Dɛm enyidado yi kyekyee Michael a yeedzi kan aka no ho asɛm no werɛ. Ɔkaa dɛ: “Asɛm a ɔwɔ Isaiah 26:19 no boaa me ma muhun dɛ m’awofo da Jehovah n’akoma do paa. Minyim dɛ no werɛ mmfiir hɔn na ɔbɔkɔ do ara akaa hɔn kesi dɛ obenyan hɔn daakye. Dɛm asɛm yi kyekye mo werɛ paa na ɔhyɛ me dzen.” Hom mma yɛnkɔ do ara ndwendwen enyidado a yɛwɔ dɛ yɛbɛsan ehu hɛn adɔfo a woewuwu no bio no ho. Sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm a, obotum aboa hɛn ma yeegyina yaw a yeridzi no seseiara no ano.

16 Yeesusu mbrɛ nsɛm bi a ɔwɔ Isaiah nwoma no mu no botum akyekye hɛn werɛ aber a hɛn asetsena asesa prɛko pɛr, aber a bɔn bi a yɛayɛ etwa mu ho asɛm da ho ara rehaw hɛn nye aber a hɛn dɔfo bi ewu ma yeridzi ho awerɛhow no ho. Ɔyɛ hɛn dɛw paa dɛ Jehovah nam n’Asɛm no do kyekye hɛn werɛ na ɔka hɛn akoma to hɛn yamu. (Ndw. 94:19) Sɛ erefa ɔhaw bi mu a, ewɔ kyerɛwsɛm pɔtsee bi a ɔkyekye wo werɛ anaa? Sɛ dɛm dze a, nna ɔno kyer dɛm kyerɛwsɛm no gu wo tsirmu. Ibotum so akyerɛw ato hɔ anaa akyerɛw abɔ beebi a ebɛtaa ehu. Yɛwɔ awerɛhyɛmu dɛ nsɛm a hɛn sor Egya Jehovah a ɔdɔ hɛn no akyerɛw ato hɔ ama hɛn no bɛkyekye wo werɛ.
