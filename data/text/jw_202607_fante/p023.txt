11 Sɛ hɛn Bible suanyi bi ma yehu dɛ dza orusua no roboa no wɔ n’abrabɔ mu a, ɔma hɛn enyi gye paa. Naaso ɔsɛ dɛ yɛma ohu dɛ dza orusua no nyina fi hɛn Kyerɛkyerɛfo Kunyin Jehovah hɔ. (Isa. 30:20, 21) Ɔtɔ da a, hɛn suanyi bi bɛkamfo hɛn anaa ɔbɛka akyerɛ hɛn dɛ n’enyi gye kwan a yɛfa do kyerɛkyerɛ no ho paa. Ɔwɔ mu, sɛ obi yɛ adze na wɔkamfo no a, mfom biara nnyi ho. Naaso ɔsɛ dɛ yɛma ohu dɛ sɛ ɔwɔ ndaase bi a, ɔsɛ dɛ ɔdze ma Jehovah na ɔnnyɛ hɛn bi a.

11. Yebesi dɛn aboa hɛn Bible suanyi bi ma oehu dɛ dza yɛnye no rusua no fi Jehovah hɔ?
<<<P>>>
BOA NO MA ƆMFA DZA ORUSUA NO MBƆ NE BRA
<<<P>>>
12 Sɛ Bible suanyi bi botum enya dza orusua no do mfaso a, gyedɛ ɔdze bɔ ne bra. Ibesi dɛn aboa no ma oetum dze dza orusua no ayɛ edwuma? Odzi kan, sɛ ɔkenkan Bible mu asɛm bi na ɔnntse biribi a Jehovah yɛe no ase a, boa no ma ɔndwendwen dza onyim fa Jehovah ho dada no ho. Ɔwodze yɛmfa no dɛ wo suanyi no akenkan biribi afa mbrɛ Jehovah si bu atsɛn no ho, na onnhu siantsir a Jehovah kunkum binom na binom so dze ɔannyɛ hɔn hwee no. Ibesi dɛn aboa no ma ɔatse ase?
<<<P>>>
