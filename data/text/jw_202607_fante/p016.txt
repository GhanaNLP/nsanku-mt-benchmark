8 Sɛ yehu Jehovah yie a, ɔbɛma anyɛnkofa a yɛnye no wɔ no mu ayɛ dzen. Dɛ mfatoho no, sɛ yɛkenkan Bible da biara, yɛhwɛ JW Broadcasting® dwumadzi bi anaa yɛkɔ ehyiadzi a, yehu dɛ yɛabɛn hɛn sor Egya no. Ebɛnadze ntsi a? Osiandɛ ɔma yesua ndzɛmba pii fa no ho na iyi ka hɛn akoma ma yeyi no ayɛw na yɛda no ase. Iyi ma yɛtse nka tse dɛ ɔdwontofo Asaph. Ɔdwendween mbrɛ nna Jehovah si dɔ no no ho na ɔkaa dɛ: “Nyame nye m’akoma n’ahoɔdzen na me kyɛpɛn daa.” (Kenkan Ndwom 73:24-28.) Sɛ yɛbɔ mbɔdzen dɛ yebohu Jehovah yie a, yɛbɛda no edzi dɛ anyɛnkofa a ɔda yɛnye no ntamu no som hɛn bo paa na yɛronntoto no adze koraa.

9 Bible no tu hɛn fo dɛ ‘yɛnka Jehovah nto hɛn akwan nyina mu.’ Dza iyi kyerɛ nye dɛ yɛbɔdwendwen ndzɛmba a yenyim fa Jehovah ho no ho, na yɛama atsena hɛn adwen mu dɛ mbrɛ yesi bɔ hɛn bra no ho hia no. Sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm a, ɔbɛma yeesisi gyinae pa. (Mbɛ. 3:5, 6) Dɛ mfatoho no, dza enuanom binom yɛ dze kyerɛ dɛ hɔn hokafo ho hia hɔn nye dɛ, wɔdze hɔn hokafo no mfonyin si hɔn pon do wɔ hɔn edwuma mu. Binom so dze hyɛ hɔn wallet mu anaa wɔdze to hɔn fon do. Iyi ma da biara nna hɔn hokafo no ho adwen wɔ hɔn tsirmu na ɔma wɔyɛ ndzɛmba a ɔbɛma hɔn hokafo no n’enyi agye. Dɛmara na hɛn so, sɛ yɛtaa dwen Jehovah ho a, ɔbɛkyerɛ dɛ no ho hia hɛn na ɔbɛma yeesisi gyinae a ɔbɛma n’enyi agye.—Ndw. 19:13, 14.

10 Jehovah bɔɔ hɛn wɔ no suban do, ɔno ntsi na yetum suasua no no. (Gen. 1:26) Sɛ yɛkɔ do ara sua Jehovah ho adze a, ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yeetum esuasua no yie paa. Jesus n’aber do no, akyerɛwfo nye Phariseefo no ennsuasua Jehovah. Nna wonyim Mbra no yie paa, naaso ‘Mbra no mu nsɛm a no ho hia paa tse dɛ atsɛntsenenee, ehumbɔbɔr na nokwardzi no, nna wommbu no hwee’ ntsi Jesus twuuw hɔn enyim dzendzenndzen. (Matt. 23:23) Onnyi dɛ yesuasua akyerɛwfo nye Phariseefo no, na mbom hɛn dze, sɛ yɛkenkan Bible no a, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛdze hɛn adwen si suban bi a Jehovah wɔ no do. Sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm a, ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yesuasua Jehovah yie.—1 John 4:8, 11.

11 Woana na odzii kan kaa Jehovah ho asɛm kyerɛɛ wo? Bi a w’awofo yɛ Adasefo, ntsi wɔdze Bible no kyerɛkyerɛɛ wo ma ibohun suban ahorow bi a Nyankopɔn wɔ, na wɔmaa ihun dɛ ndzɛmba a ɔabɔ no so ma yehu mbrɛ osi tse ankasa. Anaadɛ ɔwodze bi a Jehovah Dasefo bi na ɔnye wo suaa Bible no. Aber a ɔnye wo rusua Ibotum Enya Enyigye Daa! buukuu no, ɔnye wo susuu kyerɛwnsɛm ahorow bi ho ma ihun Jehovah no suban ahorow a ɔyɛ fɛw, na ɔboaa wo ma ihun mbrɛ ndzɛmba a Jehovah abɔ no so ma yetum hu no yie.* (Neh. 8:8; Andw. 14:17) Ɔmmfa ho mbrɛ hɛn mu biara bohun nokwar no, hom mma yɛnhwɛ mbrɛ yebesi akɔ do ara esua Jehovah ho adze.
