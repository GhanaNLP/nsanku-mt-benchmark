DZA YEBOSUA
Sɛ hɛn werɛ how anaa yɛyɛ basaa, na sɛ yɛkenkan Isaiah nwoma no na yɛdwendwen ho a, obotum akyekye hɛn werɛ.
<<<P>>>
Isaiah Nwoma No Botum Akyekye Wo Werɛ
<<<P>>>
‘Mbrɛ Jehovah se nye yi: Dɛ obi a ne na kyekye no werɛ no, dɛmara na mebɛkyekye hom werɛ.’—ISA. 66:12, 13.
<<<P>>>
FA NO dɛ abofra kakraba bi rusu paa osiandɛ ne maame rigya no hɔ akɔ kurow mu aba, anaadɛ n’adze bi ayew. Sɛ ɔba no dɛm a, ebɛnadze na ne maame bɛyɛ? Bi a ɔbɔboaboa no na ɔapɛ asɛm bi aka akyerɛ no ma oegyaa su.
<<<P>>>
2 Bible no ma yehu dɛ Jehovah tse dɛ baatan bi a ɔkyekye ne ba a ɔdɔ no no werɛ. Jehovah kaa dɛ: “Dɛ obi a ne na kyekye no werɛ no dɛmara na mebɛkyekye hom werɛ.” (Isa. 66:13) Jehovah dɔ hɛn kyɛn hɛn awofo mpo. (Isa. 49:15) Sɛ hɛn werɛ how anaa yeruhu amandze a, Jehovah fa n’Asɛm Bible no do kyekye hɛn werɛ na ɔhyɛ hɛn dzen.—Rom. 15:4.
<<<P>>>
3 Jehovah aka nsɛm pii wɔ Isaiah nwoma no mu a obotum akyekye hɛn werɛ. (Isa. 40:1) Aber a ndzɛmba mu yɛɛ dzen maa Jehovah n’asomfo a nna wɔtse Judah no, ɔmaa Isaiah kyerɛɛw no nwoma no dze kyekyee hɔn werɛ. Hɛn so, ‘mber a yɛtse mu yi mu yɛ dzen na no mu tsena yɛ yaw,’ dɛm ntsi yehia awerɛkyekyer. (2 Tim. 3:1) Hom mma yɛnhwɛ nsɛm bi a ɔwɔ Isaiah nwoma no mu a obotum akyekye hɛn werɛ na ahyɛ hɛn dzen aber a (1) hɛn asetsena asesa prɛko pɛr, (2) bɔn bi a yɛayɛ etwa mu ho asɛm da ho ara haw hɛn, anaa (3) hɛn dɔfo bi ewu ma yeridzi awerɛhow.
