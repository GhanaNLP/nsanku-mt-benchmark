Dwendwen ndzɛmba a Jehovah abɔ no ho, na ɔbɔboa wo ma ehu no yie
(Hwɛ nkyekyɛmu 15)
<<<P>>>
yeehu Jehovah yie nye dɛ yɛbɔkɔ do ara esua n’Asɛm Bible no. Ɔtɔ da a, Bible no ka Nyankopɔn no suban ahorow pɔtsee bi ho asɛm. (Ex. 34:6, 7) Naaso mpɛn pii no, ɔka nsɛm a osisii wɔ nyimpa binom hɔn asetsena mu nye gyinae ahorow a wosisii ho asɛm. Sɛ yɛkenkan nsɛm a ɔtsetse dɛm a, ɔsɛ dɛ yesie abotar dwendwen ho yie na yɛhwɛ dza obotum ama yeehu afa Jehovah ho.

13 Ɔwodze ma yɛnyɛ iyi ho mfatoho: Fa no dɛ erekenkan Jeremiah 38:6-13, nna ehu dza Ethiopianyi bi a wɔfrɛ no Ebed-melech yɛe dze boaa nkɔnhyɛnyi Jeremiah. Nna Ebedmelech wɔ akokodur paa na nna ɔdɔ no nuanom a ɔnye hɔn som Jehovah no, ntsi nna ɔayɛ krado dɛ ɔdze no nkwa bɔto esian mu ama hɔn. Ntsi annhwɛ a ɔno na ibedzi kan dze w’adwen akɔ do na ahwɛ mbrɛ ibesi esuasua no. Naaso ehwɛ a, yebotum esua biribi afa Jehovah ho wɔ dɛm asɛm yi mu anaa? (Kenkan Jeremiah 39:15-18.) Ebɛn asɛm na ɔwɔ dɛm kyerɛwnsɛm yi mu a ɔma ihu dɛ sɛ obi yɛ adze ma Jehovah a ɔkyerɛ ho enyisɔ, ɔnye obiara dzi no yie na ɔnnyɛ nyiyimu? Ebɛn suban afofor na Jehovah wɔ a ihu wɔ dɛm asɛm yi mu? Aber biara a ebɛkenkan Bible mu asɛm bi no, ɔyɛ a hwɛ dɛ ibohu Jehovah no suban bi a. Sɛ ihu bi a, da no ase na kamfo no dɛ ɔaboa wo ma ehu no yie.

14 Adze a otsia ebien a obotum aboa hɛn ma yeehu Jehovah yie nye dɛ yɛbɔdwendwen n’abɔdze ho. (Ndw. 145:9, 10) Naaso iyi nnkyerɛ dɛ sɛ yɛhwɛ abɔdze ara kɛkɛ a, ɔbɛma yeehu Jehovah yie. Nyimpa binom dze hɔn nkwa nda nyina esua ndzɛmba a Jehovah abɔ ho adze, naaso wɔnngye nndzi mpo dɛ Nyankopɔn bi wɔ hɔ na akantam woehu no suban ahorow a ɔwɔ no.
