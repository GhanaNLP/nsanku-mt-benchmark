DZA YEBOSUA
Yɛbɔhwɛ mbrɛ ibesi aboa wo Bible suanyi ma oehu Jehovah yie, no suban, dza ɔpɛ nye dza ɔmmpɛ.
<<<P>>>
Boa Afofor Ma Wonhu Jehovah
<<<P>>>
“Sɛ obi benya onnyiewiei nkwa a, ɔsɛ dɛ ohu ɔwo a eyɛ nokwar Nyame kor pɛ no.”—JOHN 17:3.
<<<P>>>
SƐ WO Bible suanyi bi hyɛ ase dɛ ɔreba adzesua na ɔhyɛ ase dɛ ɔrema mbuae a, ɔma etse nka dɛn? Na sɛ ɔka dza orusua no ho asɛm akyerɛ nkorɔfo na ɔwɔ mu araa na osi gyinae dɛ obehyira noho do ama Jehovah Nyankopɔn so ɛ? Nokwasɛm nye yi, ɔma w’enyi gye paa. Naaso yenyim paa dɛ Jehovah na ɔboa hɛn suanyi no ma onya nkɔenyim a ɔtse dɛm na ɔnnyɛ hɛnankasa hɛn ahoɔdzen bi a. Dɛm ntsi sɛ yehu dɛ hɛn Bible suanyi bi rokɔ n’enyim wɔ Jehovah no som mu a, yɛdze ho nkamfo no ma Jehovah.—1 Cor. 3:5-9; 3 John 3, 4.
<<<P>>>
2 Bible no ma yehu dɛ Nyankopɔn ‘pɛ dɛ ɔgye nyimpa ahorow nyina nkwa na wonya nokwar no ho nyimdzee a odzi mu.’ (Kenkan 1 Timothy 2:3, 4.) Jehovah pɛ dɛ nkorɔfo hu no, na dza ɔabɔ ne tsirmu dɛ ɔbɛyɛ ama adasamba nyina so, ɔpɛ dɛ wɔtse ho asɛm. (Jer. 9:24) Sɛ yebotum ehu iyi a, gyedɛ yenya no ho nyimdzee a odzi mu. Sɛ wɔka dɛ obi wɔ Nyankopɔn ho “nyimdzee a odzi mu” a, ɔkyerɛ dɛn? Ɔnnyɛ dza iyi kyerɛ nye dɛ nyimpa no nyim Nyankopɔn ho nsɛm bi ara kɛkɛ. Na mbom, dɛ mbrɛ Bible ho ɔbemfo bi kyerɛkyerɛɛ mu no, ɔkyerɛ dɛ “nyimpa no ma dza onyim fa Nyankopɔn ho no kyerɛ no kwan wɔ n’abrabɔ mu.” Dɛm ntsi sɛ Bible suanyi bi bohu Jehovah a, ɔdze dza onyim fa no ho no bɔ ne bra na iyi ma onya enyidado dɛ obotum enya “onnyiewiei nkwa” daakye.—John 17:3.
<<<P>>>
3 Jehovah dze edwuma soronko bi ahyɛ hɛn nsa. Ɔse yɛmboa nkorɔfo ma wombohu no. Adzesua yi mu no, yebosusu nsɛmbisa a odzidzi do yi ho: Ebɛnadze ntsi na ɔsɛ dɛ Bible
<<<P>>>
1. Sɛ ihu dɛ wo Bible suanyi bi rinya nkɔenyim a, ɔma etse nka dɛn?
<<<P>>>
2. Ebɛnadze na Jehovah pɛ dɛ Bible esuafo yɛ? (1 Timothy 2:3, 4)
<<<P>>>
3. Ebɛnadze na yebosusu ho wɔ adzesua yi mu?
