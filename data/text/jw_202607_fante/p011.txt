10 Kenkan Isaiah 38:17. Asɛm a ɔno nye dɛ, “ɔwo atow mo bɔn nyinara egu w’ekyir” no, wobotum so akyerɛ ase dɛ “ɔwo eyi mo bɔn nyinara efi w’enyim.” Dɛm asɛm yi ma yehu dɛ sɛ obi a ɔayɛ bɔn no nu noho a, Jehovah tow dɛm nyimpa no no bɔn gu n’ekyir na onnhu anaa ɔnndwen ho bio. Nhwehwɛmu nwoma bi so ka dɛ, wobotum akyerɛ dɛm asɛm yi ase dɛ: “Ɔwo ama mo bɔn ayɛ tse dɛ mbrɛ mennyɛɛ bɔn mpo da.” Ntsi sɛ Jehovah dze hɛn bɔn kyɛ hɛn a, wɔ n’enyi do dze, ɔtse dɛ mbrɛ yɛnnyɛɛ bɔn mpo da. Ɔrennkɛfa ho asɛm mmba bio na ɔronntwe hɛn aso so wɔ ho bio da. Kyerɛ dɛ, ɔpepa dɛm bɔn no fi hɔ koraa. Ɔahyɛ hɛn bɔ dɛ “ɔrennkaa hɛn bɔn.” (Isa. 43:25) Innhu dɛ dɛm awerɛkyekyesɛm yi ka akoma a? Sɛ Jehovah a ɔyɛ hɛn Ɔtsɛmbuafo Pumpunyi no mpo nnkaa bɔn a yɛayɛ etwa mu no a, nna ɔno ebɛnadze ntsi na ɔsɛ dɛ yɛma kwan ma dɛm bɔn no ho asɛm tseetsee hɛn?
