6 Joseph a yeedzi kan aka no ho asɛm no ma yehu dza Jehovah yɛe dze boaa ɔnye ne yer aber a wofii Bethel no. Ɔkaa dɛ: “Aber a yefii Bethel no, yehun Jehovah ne nsa wɔ hɛn abrabɔ mu paa. Ɔnam hɛn nuanom Christianfo so do boaa hɛn. Afei so, biribiara a yehia wɔ asetsena mu no, Jehovah maa hɛn nsa kae. Na awerɛkyekyer so dze, ɔmaa hɛn bi paa. Jehovah nngyaa hɛn da.” Woara so, sɛ w’asetsena sesa prɛko pɛr ma ndzɛmba mu yɛ dzen ma wo a, nya awerɛhyɛmu dɛ Jehovah bɛkyerɛ wo kwan na ɔbɛhyɛ wo dzen. (Isa. 54:10; 58:11) Joseph sanee so kaa dɛ: “Hɛn ndwom kor bi no mu nsɛm yɛ me dɛw paa. Ɔka dɛ, ‘Adzekyee mu nsɛm dze munnkotum ehu, naaso hwɛ minyim dɛ ɔrennnyɛ hu.’* Ɔyɛ nokwar, bi a ndzɛmba ronnkɔ dɛ mbrɛ yɛhwɛ kwan no. Naaso ɔsɛ dɛ yɛkaa dɛ dza obesi wɔ hɛn abrabɔ mu biara no, Jehovah ka hɛn ho na ɔbɔboa hɛn.”

7. Bɔn a binom ayɛ etwa mu no ma wɔtse nka dɛn?
<<<P>>>
bɔkɔ do ara ebu hɛn fɔ. Bi a yɛbɛka dɛ: ‘Dɛm bɔn a meyɛe yi dze, menngye nndzi dɛ Jehovah dze bɛkyɛ me da.’ (2 Cor. 2:7) Sɛ yɛtse nka dɛm a ntseasee wɔ mu, osiandɛ bɔn a yɛayɛ etwa mu ho adwendwen botum atseetsee hɛn akyɛr na obotum ayɛ dzen ama hɛn dɛ yebeyi efi hɛn tsirmu. Aber a Ɔhen David yɛɛ bɔn no, ɔkaa dɛ: “Osian mo bɔn ntsi apɔw-mudzen bi nnyi me mbiew mu.” Ɔsanee so kaa dɛ: “Matɔ bahaw na mebubu posoposo.” (Ndw. 38:3, 8) Ɔtɔ da a bɔn bi a ayɛ etwa mu ho asɛm haw wo anaa? Sɛ dɛm dze a, nna ɔno Isaiah nwoma no botum aboa wo paa.

8 Kenkan Isaiah 1:18. Jehovah dze mfatohokamakama bi kyerɛkyerɛɛ mbrɛ osi dze hɛn bɔn kyɛ hɛn korakora no mu. Ɔkaa dɛ, sɛ hɛn bɔn ‘ayɛ tse dɛ adaaben mpo’ a, ɔbɛma ayɛ ‘fitaa dɛ esukwankyɛba.’ Yenyim dɛ adaaben anaa koogyan yɛ biribi a ɔyɛ dzen dɛ ɔbɛpa. Naaso Jehovah ama hɛn awerɛhyɛmu dɛ sɛ ɔdze hɛn bɔn kyɛ hɛn a, nna ɔdze akyɛ hɛn korakora, ɔrennkaa no bio.

9 Ɔwodze ma yɛnhwɛ Ɔhen David n’asɛm no. Ɔwɔ mu dɛ ɔsɛɛ awar na odzii ewu so, naaso Jehovah dze no bɔn kyɛɛ no na ekyir yi ɔkaa no ho asɛm dɛ ɔdze n’akoma nyina soom no. (2 Sam. 11:3, 4, 14, 15; 12:13; 1 Ahe. 9:4, 5) Jehovah dze, nokwardzi a David dze soom no no na ɔdze n’adwen sii do na nnyɛ bɔn a nna ɔayɛ no. Dɛmara na hɛn so, sɛ yɛyɛ bɔn bi na yefi hɛn akoma nyina mu nu hɛnho a, Jehovah dze bɛkyɛ hɛn. Na iyi bɔboa hɛn ma yɛdze tsibowa pa akɔ do ara asom no. Ebɛn awerɛkyekyesɛm bio na ɔwɔ Isaiah nwoma no mu a obotum aboa hɛn aber a bɔn bi a yɛayɛ etwa mu ho asɛm da ho ara retseetsee hɛn no?
