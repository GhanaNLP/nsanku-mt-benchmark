“ƆSƐ DƐ ISUA JAPAN KASA!”
<<<P>>>
Afe 1987 no, mokɔr Japan. Aber a muhun mbrɛ osi ma enuanom no yɛ nsi na wɔbrɛ hɔnho adze no, m’enyi gyee paa. Nna mepɛ dɛ moboa hɔn, naa-so nna me haw nye dɛ, munntum nnka Japan kasa no. Akyerɛbaa bi a wɔfrɛ no Michiko Oda a ɔyɛ edwu-ma wɔ Bethel no kae sii m’enyim pen dɛ, “Ɔsɛ dɛ isua Japan kasa!” Na musuae so. Nna minnyim dɛ akyerɛ-baa no n’afotu no bɔboa me daakye bi.
<<<P>>>
Abosoom kakra ekyir no, mokɔr asafo bi a wɔdze Japan kasa yɛ adzesua mu wɔ New York. Japan kasa a musuae no maa minyaa hokwan pii dze som Jehovah Nyankopɔn. Afe 1989 no, mokɔr ehyiadzi bi wɔ Los Angeles a ɔwɔ California; ɔno nye ehyiadzi a odzi kan a mokɔree wɔ Japan kasa mu. Drama a wɔ-yɛe wɔ dɛm ehyiadzi no ase no, nna akyerɛbaa kwampaafo bi a wɔfrɛ no Miwako Onami ka ho bi, na m’enyi bɔɔ no do ara na motwee maa no.
<<<P>>>
Yɛwaar afe 1992, na yenyaa hokwan bɔɔ mu somee wɔ Brooklyn Bethel. Miwako dwen nyimpa ho paa, na da biara ɔpɛ dɛ ɔyɛ biribi boa afofor. No su-ban pa no aboa me ma mehu mbrɛ mibesi nye nko-rɔfo atsena yie. Me yer dze, sɛ mennhyia! Ɔma m’e-nyi gye dɛ, na ɔma m’akoma so tɔ me yamu.

YƐKƆR JAPAN BETHEL NA EKYIR YI YƐKƐYƐƐ AKWAMPAAFO ETSITSIR
<<<P>>>
Nna Miwako n’awofo tse Japan, na odur beebi no, wɔbɛyaree na nna hɔn yarba no mu yɛ dzen. Ntsi enuanom a wodzi ndzɛmba enyim wɔ Bethel no kaa dɛ yebotum akɛyɛ edwuma wɔ Japan Bethel, mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a yɛbɛbɛn Miwako n’awofo na yɛahwɛ hɔn. Ntsi yetui kɔr hɔ afe 1999.
<<<P>>>
Yɛkɔr Japan Bethel no, enuanom a wɔwɔ hɔ no maa hɛn enyi kae paa. Nna enuanom a wɔwɔ Japan no yamu ye, na wobue hɔn nsamu so. Iyi kaa m’ako-ma paa ntsi musuasuaa hɔn. Nna wɔmmfa hɔn edwuma so nndzi agor koraa. Na wɔreyɛ edwuma so a, nna wɔbɔ mu yɛ. Siantsir a nna wɔyɛ dɛm nye dɛ, Japanfo hɔn amambra mu no, wosusu dɛ sɛ nyimpa beberee bɔ mu yɛ edwuma a, mfaso wɔ do paa kyɛn dɛ baakofo bɛtsew noho ayɛ edwuma dze apɛ enyimnyam ama noho. Mbrɛ nna wosi yɛ hɔn adze no maa muhun dɛ, ɔsɛ dɛ mebrɛ moho adze paa na menye afofor bɔ mu yɛ edwuma. Afei so, mu-hun dɛ ɔsɛ dɛ megye to mu dɛ kakra biara a mutum yɛ no, ɔboa ma Jehovah n’apɛdze yɛ hɔ.

Da a yɛwaree
<<<P>>>
Naaso, osiandɛ ɔsor beebi a wɔtsetsee me ntsi, ɔtɔ da a nna menntse siantsir nye kwan a wɔfa do yɛ ndzɛmba bi no ase papa. Ntsi nna ɔsɛ dɛ musua dɛ mibenya ndzɛmba ho abotar aber a menye hɔn nnyɛ adwen wɔ biribi ho no. Odur beebi no, mebɛtsee hɔn ase yie, na mubohun dɛ gyinae biara a yebesi no, sɛ yɛdze akwankyerɛ a ahyehyɛdze no dze ma no yɛ edwuma nkotsee dze a, Jehovah behyira do. Osian-dɛ adze yi ara, sɛ Jehovah ne nsa nnyi biribi a yɛreyɛ mu a, ɔrennyɛ yie.
<<<P>>>
Metaa dze ntsetsee a minyae wɔ Bethel no toto ntsetsee a nkɔnhyɛnyi Moses nyae no ho. Mo-ses dze mfe 40 nyɛɛn nguan, na Jehovah nam do boaa no maa ohun dɛ ɔsɛ dɛ ɔbrɛ noho adze. M’asɛm ayɛ dɛ Moses nedze no bi. Skuulpɔn kakra a mokɔree ntsi, nna ɔma memema moho do na megye moho dzi paa. Naaso, Bethel a mokɔree no ama mehu dɛ, ɔsɛ dɛ mebrɛ moho adze na medze moho to Jehovah do. Jehovah nyaa Moses ho abotar na ɔboaa no ma onyaa suban pa. Dɛmara na mara so, Jehovah enya moho abotar mfe pii na ɔaboa me ma menya su-ban pa.
