15 Adze fofor so a ibotum ayɛ nye dɛ ebɔboa wo suanyi no ma ɔdze dza oesua afa Jehovah ho no esisi gyinae pa. Fa no dɛ wo suanyi no pɛ dɛ osi gyinae bi. Bi a ibedzi kan atwe n’a-dwen esi Ibotum Enya Enyigye Daa! buukuu no n’adzesua a otsia 35 a n’asɛntsir nye “Yebesi Dɛn Esisi Gyinae Pa?” no do. Naaso, yɛmfa no dɛ wo suanyi no abɔ asɛm no ho mpaa na ɔayɛ nhwehwɛmu biara a ɔsɛ dɛ ɔyɛ no ewie, naaso ɔda ho ara yɛ dzen ma no dɛ obesi gyinae so ɛ? Bi a onnhu Bible mu afotusɛm pɔtsee a ɔfa n’asɛm no ho. Sɛ ɔba no dɛm a, ibotum ahyɛ no nkuran dɛ ɔndwendwen dza ɔboaa Jesus ma osisii gyinae pa no ho. Ebɛnadze na ɔboaa Jesus ma osisii gyinae pa? Jesus kaa dɛ: “Me-yɛ dza ɔsɔ [m’Egya] n’enyi daa.” (Kenkan John 8:29.) Ibotum aka akyerɛ wo suanyi no dɛ ombisa noho dɛ: ‘Sɛ ɔhwɛ ndzɛmba a oesua afa Jehovah ho nye nhwehwɛmu a ɔayɛ nyina no a, ebɛn gyinae na sɛ osi a ɔbɛma Je-hovah n’enyi agye?’ Ma wo suanyi no nhu dɛ, sɛ osi gyinae a ɔbɛma Jehovah n’enyi agye a, ɔno na ɔkyerɛ dɛ oesi gyinae pa.
