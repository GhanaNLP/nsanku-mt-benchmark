<<<P>>>

<<<P>>>
NDWOM 12
<<<P>>>
Jehovah, Nyame Tumfo
<<<P>>>
DZA YEBOSUA
<<<P>>>
Sɛ wɔka dɛ obi nyim Jehovah yie a, yɛbɔhwɛ dza ɔkyerɛ nye siantsir a ɔsɛ dɛ yehu Jehovah yie nye kwan a yebotum afa do ayɛ dɛm.
<<<P>>>
HƐN Nyame Jehovah nyim biribiara, onyim nyansa kyɛn obiara na no ndwuma so yɛ nwanwa. Sɛ yɛdwendwen hɛn Bɔfo no ho a, ɔma hɛnho dwuruw hɛn. Iyi ntsi bi a woara so bɛka asɛm a ɔsomafo Paul kae yi bi: “O hwɛ mbrɛ Nyankopɔn n’ahonya na ne nyansaa na ne nyimdzee mu dɔ fa! Yerunntum nnhwehwɛ n’atsɛmbu mu nnwie da, na n’akwan so bor hɛn ntseasee do.”—Rom. 11:33, 34.
<<<P>>>
2 Yerunntum nnhu Jehovah ho biribiara, naaso yebotum ehu no yie. Ɔhen David kaa kyerɛɛ ne ba Solomon dɛ: ‘Hu w’egya ne Nyankopɔn no, na fa akoma a odzi mu som no: sɛ ehwehwɛ no a, ɔbɛma ehu no.’ (1 Mber. 28:9) Mfe pii ekyir so, ɔsomafo John kaa kyerɛɛ no nuanom Christianfo dɛ, ‘wɔama hɛn ntseasee a yɛdze hu nyia ɔyɛ nokwafo no.’ (1 John 5:20) Jesus nankasa so kaa dɛ, ‘obiara a ɔpɛ dɛ ohu Jehovah yie no, Ɔba no bɛda no edzi akyerɛ no.’—Matt. 11:27.
<<<P>>>
3 Hɛn a yeehyira hɛnho do ama Jehovah nyina, yenyim no ho nsɛm pii dada naaso yerunntum nnsua no ho adze nnwie. (John 17:3; hwɛ no ho nkyerɛkyerɛmu.) Dza yɛyɛ a ɔbɛma Jehovah n’enyi agye nye mbrɛ yebesi dze hɛnho nyina ato no do da biara da no, yebotum esua ho adze. Dɛm ntsi sɛ yɛbaa asafo no mu akyɛr o, sɛ yɛbaa mu nnkyɛree o, hom mma yensusu nsɛmbisa ebiasa bi ho na ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yeehu Jehovah yie: Sɛ wɔka dɛ obi nyim Jehovah yie a, ɔkyerɛ dɛn? Ebɛn-
<<<P>>>
1. Sɛ yɛdwendwen Jehovah ne nyimdzee, ne nyansaa nye no ndwuma ho a, ɔma yɛtse nka dɛn?
<<<P>>>
2. Ebɛnadze na ɔma yehu dɛ yebotum ehu Jehovah yie?
<<<P>>>
3. Ebɛnadze na yebosusu ho wɔ adzesua yi mu?
