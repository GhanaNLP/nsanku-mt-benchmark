Kejimanshɛrbi Be Asheŋ Be Kananɛ A Beeŋ Nite Nɛ Amanshɛrbi Be Keta N Shuŋ Be Ekpaana 2023 To
<<<P>>>
23. Ta n laŋɛ asɔ nɛ baa tama ashi Amanshɛrbichalaso nɛ Kakɛ Kikɛ to nɛ Amanshɛrbijiso (n ta akɔ dra be keka n tiiso), kejimanshɛrbi kikɛ be kananɛ ku bee nite ela fanɛ ku beeŋ bra amanshɛrbi be kejimbaŋso nɛ a beeŋ fo GH¢61,475 milliɔn, nɛ a la GDP be 7.7 kalfa to. Kumobe keniopibi ashi Praimere Balans ashi amanshɛrbi be kabaso la kejimbaŋso GH¢8,925 milliɔn, nɛ ku la GDP be 1.1 kalfa to.
<<<P>>>
24. Kejimbaŋso na baa tama fanɛ kumobe keka beeŋ shi ekpaaan ko so nna n ta efuli pɔtɛana nɛ efuliso peya n daŋɛso. Mboŋ ko nɛ ba naŋ nyɛ amanshɛrbi ashi ela Mɔltilatral nɛ Bailatral Patnas nɛ baa tama ashi kananɛ baaŋ luri IMF be program to m fin kechɛto monɛ baaŋ nyɛ n ta n shuŋ akɔ be keketa nɛnɛ be ekpa so.
