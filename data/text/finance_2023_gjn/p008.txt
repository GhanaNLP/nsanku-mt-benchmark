1. 2023 be Efuliebi be Kejimanshɛrbi wol na la kuduwɔso nna nɛ ku bee ŋini asɔ monɛ gomananti tiŋ n wɔrɔ 2022 to, nɛ amanshɛrbi monɛ gomananti wɔrɔ nia nɛ bu sɔ n shin ta bumobe amanshɛrbi chalaso n shuŋ n tiŋ n fara ekpaana monɛ baŋ bulɔso n shuŋ bumobe ashuŋ. Kesheŋkpra monɛ 2023 be Kejimanshɛrbi wol na yilso ela "Kelɔŋɛ Amanshɛrbisheŋkpra be Ayaso be Keyili nɛ ku tiŋ n Nyɛ Eleŋ a Wɔtɔ saŋɛkama ashi Kekoli Abar be Ndaŋ nɛ Asɔwɔrɔso be Daŋɛso”.
<<<P>>>
2. Mfɛ monɛ a choŋ na kejimanshɛrbi pupɔr kama bee kaa kɔ ashuŋ pupɔr monɛ baaŋ fara n ka yige amonɛ a kra yɔso. Kede maa bra tɔrɔ a sa anyebe amanshɛrbisheŋ dawule nna ama adrasheŋ bee ŋini fanɛ ku bee bra nchu be keka ta n laŋɛ amanshɛrbichoŋso monɛ a daŋ daga bu kaa ka so, nɛ ku bee shinɛ ashuŋ na be yawu bee dii nkpal loŋ be amanshɛrbichoŋso na be keka so.
<<<P>>>
3. 2023 to Gomananti beeŋ nase asɔwɔrɔso pupɔr nɛ ashuŋ pupɔr. ama ba naŋ wɔrɔ ekpa monɛ baaŋ bulɔso n luwe ashuŋ monɛ a kra yɔso.
