“ "Kebŋɛ Amanshɛrbisheŋkpra be Ayaso be Keyili nɛ ku tiŋ n Nyɛ Eleŋ a Wɔtɔ saŋekama ashi Kekoli Abar be Ndaŋ nɛ Asɔwɔrɔso be Daŋɛso”.

<<<P>>>
Kashinteŋto anye luri kechɔnkeni damta to fanɛ baasa, nɛ gomananti bee wɔrɔ kushuso nɛ anye nkpal tɔrɔ damta nɛ ade bra anye na so. Gomananti bee sha kedi bede epaŋ, anyebe Ashuŋ be Amuwuraana n ta bede n daŋɛso Palimɛnt, Beshumpo be Kɔnɔkoŋwule be Ntuŋ, Beshumpo be Ntuŋ, Jimaa kikɛ, Ebɔrɛshumpo, Ghana be Ashuŋbɔŋwuraana be Katuŋ, Ghana be Asɔfapo be Katuŋ, Kenyiwuraana, Tink-Tankana, nɛ Ghana be baasa kikɛ monɛ bu kra nyiti a sa anye hali anye kra kɔ emaŋ nɛ anye e ler jimanɛ kpakpaso ere to.
<<<P>>>
Mee yili H. E. Nana Addo Danquah Akuffo-Addo,be aya toa sa Ghanaebi kɔnɔ fanɛ gomananti bee wɔrɔ ekpa lɛla monɛ ku beeŋ shinɛ anyebe amanshɛrbisheŋ beeŋ chɛto nɛ anyebe amanshɛrbisheŋkpra e lɔŋɛ n yili nɛnɛ, n shinɛ amanshɛrbi chalaso e daŋɛso, m buwi ekpa n sa asɔ pɔrso nɛ kebawɔtɔ to be nchɛrga, m pargato digita nɛ kasɔpɔr, duwɔ asɔklaso monɛ a maŋ tir, wɔrɔ ekpa lɛla n sa asɔfaso efuli pɔtɛ so, buwito efuli ere be ashuŋ monɛ a beeŋ bra asɔ be keya fa efuli pɔtɛ so, n shin kuŋ bebɔlpo nɛ bepɔsopo.
<<<P>>>
Asɔ monɛ anye bii fanɛ efuli jimanɛ ere to bee ŋini anye fanɛ ku daga anye ka bee lɔŋɛ a pɔr a bra nkilgi ashi anyebe amanshɛrbisheŋ to nɛ a tiŋ n yili amobe ayaso jimanɛ kikɛ. Anyeeŋ wɔrɔ a chɔ la. Anyeeŋ ler ade to nɛ eleŋ pupɔr. Nyɛnpetali beeŋ chɛ anye to n sa anye eleŋ monɛ anyeeŋ tiŋ n ta n wɔrɔ.
