Amnshɛrbijiso Be Ekpaana
<<<P>>>
19. Keta n tii amanshɛrbichalaso be ekpaana so, Gomananti naa sha ketaa amanshɛrbijiso be ekpaana kpra monɛ a wɔ kaseto ere n leŋ amanshɛrbisheŋ be keyili amobe ayaso be ekpaana so.
<<<P>>>
• Keta m birabarso LEAP be amanshɛrbi nɛ baa ka a sa elaŋtoebi nɛ naniere a la Ghs45 kufol kama na n yɔ GHs90 n shin naŋ daŋɛso elaŋ nɛ baa sa loŋ be amanshɛrbi na nɛ naniere a la 344,185. Ekpaana ere beeŋ shinɛ LEAP be kejimanshɛrbi monɛ naniere ku la GHs197.5 milliɔn na e daŋɛso n ya fo GHs395 milliɔn 2022 to;
<<<P>>>
• Kedaŋɛso sukuru fidin be kejimanshɛrbi nɛ ku tiŋ m fo kumobe kakɔka nɛ amanshɛrbi be daŋɛso GHs138 milliɔn,
<<<P>>>
• Kechɛso a kuu 30% ashi bede be kakɔka to, Prɛsidɛnti, Vaisi Prɛsidɛnti, Aminista, Adiputi Minista, MMDCEs, nɛ kafulikeniso be benimu kama nɛ bu kɔ eyilikpa gboŋiana nɛ bumonɛ bu wɔ efuli be ashuŋgboŋ to;
<<<P>>>
• Duwɔso amanshɛrbi nɛ ba kaa fɛ amobe kelara be ekar nɛ naniere ku la amanshɛrbichalaso to be lampo 25 kalfa to ashi amanshɛrbichalaso be lampo to n yɔ 17.5 kalfa to;
<<<P>>>
• Keta amanshɛrbi tamaso kama n ya deŋi GIFMIS be kubɔr na so n shin wɔrɔ ekpa nɛ bekama na e bulɔ GIFMIS be kubɔr na so n shuŋ bumobe amanshɛrbichalaso nɛ amanshɛrbijiso be ashuŋ kikɛ nɛ ku ya luweto 2023 be lalaluwe.
<<<P>>>
• Keta enɔ n deŋi SOEs be kakɔka be kedaŋɛso so nɛ a saaŋ fo kakɔka monɛ bu shuliso fanɛ baaŋ daŋɛso ashi Singal Spain Salari Stracha na to kafɛ kama to;
<<<P>>>
