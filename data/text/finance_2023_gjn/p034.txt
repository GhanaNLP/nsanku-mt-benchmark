72. Kushuŋ na yuu “Cold Cases Unit” ashi “Criminal Investigations Department” (CID), n yɛra “Anti-Armed Robbery Taskforce” (AATF) nɛ bu kɔ kenyi ta n laŋɛ mboŋ mboŋ be kukuŋ be kabaso, n yuu Amoto-diipo be Katuŋ ashi ndegboŋ ko to, m bra apurshi-dii agbaŋɛ be Katuŋ nɛ “Canine” (K-9) be kaba nɛ baa chambɔ kadeto.
