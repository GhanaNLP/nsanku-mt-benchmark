63. Nkpal Gomananti ka ta n deŋi kumobe anishiso fanɛ ku bee sha asukuru nɛ kasurubla be ashuŋkpagboŋana e nyɛ ashuŋsɔ yɛlyɛla nɛ kekeniso ler anishi so, Gomananti tɔ n shin chige abɔsi 175 (nɛ a bee ta baasa 66), apikapu 71, nɛ atrata 2 n sa Asiniya Hai Sukuru nɛ Atɛkinikal nɛ Vokeshinal Ejukeshin nɛ Atrenin Instituti efuli to kikɛ.

64. 2022 to, Gomananti sa benyɛn 3,724 nɛ beche 3,715 nɛ bu la asɔlɔŋɛpo keŋini ashi Kɔminiti Devɛlɔpmɛnti nɛ Vokeshinal Insituti to nɛ kechɛto m buwi bumobe tɛkinikal nɛ vokeshinal be kenyi to nɛ bu nyɛ ashuŋ n naŋ bra ashuŋ nɛ ku chɛto n duwɔ kitir so.

66.
