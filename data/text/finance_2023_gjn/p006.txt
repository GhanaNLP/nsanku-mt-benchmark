Emɔrɔ maŋ wɔtɔ fanɛ kafɛ ere beeŋ ler efuli fanɛ kumo to nɛ anyebe amanshɛrbisheŋ kii kpakpa anyebe efuli ere so, anyebe adrasheŋ to. Fanɛ durnyaŋ be efuli kikɛ to na COVID-19 be kulɔ lubi na ba jɛrbɛ anye ashi afol asa monɛ a jumkpar kafɛ 2022 na to nshin shinɛ anyebe amanshɛrbisheŋ daŋ laŋɛ kaman, nɛ ku shinɛ anyebe amanshɛrbi chalaso duwɔso nnaŋ bra amanshɛrbi monɛ amaŋ wɔ kejimanshɛrbi to be kenaŋ fin n ta n kɔ kulɔ lubi na.
<<<P>>>
Jimanɛ monɛ anye baŋ fɛ fanɛ anye ŋin a yɔ anishito ga ashi tɔrɔ damta monɛ kulɔ lubi na bra anye na to nkpal anye ka fara anyebe kafuli kilgi be asheŋ wɔrɔso na, asɔ wieto ko ba lurito baŋ shi loŋ be jimanɛ na n shin mur anyebe amanshɛrbisheŋ kpra be asɔ pa monɛ anye daŋ nyɛ m bɔ enɔ ashi 2017 be kafɛ to m ba fo 2019 to na. Sososo peya na ela Russia-Ukraine be kenaa, ku maŋ wule m buwi ekpa n sa amanshɛrbi chalaso be ekpaana kpra monɛ a wɔ 2022 be kafɛ be kejimanshɛrbi to, ku bra amanshɛrbi be kenyɛ be eyilikpa kaseto. Epulto, ku ba fanɛ mfɛ kudu to nde kusɔ monɛ ku maŋ naŋ wɔrɔ nɛ efuli pɔtɛana be amanshɛrbi be kibɛ tii n sa anye, efuli to be amanshɛbi be mpaŋ be ekpaana ki kpakpa nɛ mpaŋ be yawu daŋɛ so, ade kikɛ shinɛ mpaŋ mpaŋ be nchu be keka dii nɛ siidi be egbɛ duwɔso.
<<<P>>>
Loŋso, hali efuli pɔtɛ be amanshɛrbi be kechɛto ashi amanshɛrbi be kibɛ to nɛ anyebe efuli pɔtɛ so be amanshɛrbi nyɛso ka bee duwɔso, anyebe asɔ klaso bee daŋɛso nɛ amobe amanshɛrbi dii nkpal anyebe kebaa sha asɔ klaso be kebaŋso efuli ere to, n ta anyebe akɔ be nchu be keka ka bee daŋɛso chɛto n duwɔ anyebe kemanshɛrbi na be egbɛ so. Kede e bra kachinato be asɔ be ketɔ be yawu ka dii n sa anye kikɛ, gomananti nɛ ekama. Nku be yawu be kedii bra asɔjiso nɛ ashuŋ be yawu be kedaŋɛso, nɛ ayawu be kedii ya yili 40.4% monɛ anye maŋ naŋ wu efuli ere to ashi Achaŋ 2022 to.
