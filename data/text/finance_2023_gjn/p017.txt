<<<P>>>
• Kelɔŋɛ n kenito alampo nɛ a deŋi ashigari nɛ ashira be ntuŋso so nɛ amo nɛ a bɛ ECOWAS be mbra so, n tiiso nsa kpakpaso be lampo nɛ a ba chɔ abiia peya.
<<<P>>>

<<<P>>>
• Kilgi Nashinal Fisikal Stabilaizeshin Levi (NFSL) n kii Grof nɛ Sastenabiliti Levi (GSL) nɛ ku liɛ mboŋ kikɛ.
<<<P>>>
