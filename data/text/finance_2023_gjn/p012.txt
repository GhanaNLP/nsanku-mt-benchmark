7. Amanshɛrbichalaso be Kananɛ A Nite
<<<P>>>
8. Amanshɛrbichalaso nɛ kakɛ kikɛ be kɔnɔ fo GH¢65,399 milliɔn (GDP be 11.0 kalfa to), ta n kesar amanshɛrbi tamaso GH¢67,307 (GDP be 11.4 kalfa to) nɛ GH¢49,108 milliɔn (GDP 10.7 kalfa to) nɛ bu daŋ nyɛ jimanɛ koŋwule na 2021 to.
<<<P>>>
9. Nɛ anye ta n kesar jimanɛ na be atamasɔ, kusɔ nɛ ku ler efuli ta n laŋɛ amanshɛrbichalaso kikɛ nɛ kakɛela fanɛ a bra kuduwɔso nɛ ku la GH¢1,909 milliɔn nko 2.8 kalfa to nɛ kafɛ nɛ kafɛto be ndaŋ nɛ ku yili 33.2 kalfa to. Amanshɛrbichalaso be kuduwɔso na shi kemaŋ wɔtɔ eleŋ ashi amobe kekama be kesɔ to nna.
<<<P>>>
10. Amanshɛrbichalaso be ntuŋ na be kekama be kesɔ ashi Jintigi-Achaŋ be Kayɛrbi 2022 be kekama be kalfa to e wɔ kechaŋso 1 to na.
