Fisikal Pɔlisi: Ku bee ŋini kananɛ gomananti be amanshɛrbi be keji nɛ lampo be mbra bee nite nɛ ku liɛ amanshɛrbisheŋ be kebawɔtɔ, fɔŋfɔŋ amanshɛrbisheŋkpra be kebawɔtɔ.

Amanshɛrbi be kebawɔtɔ be tɔrɔ (Fisikal Stress): Ku la kusɔ monɛ ku bee keni kananɛ baa susu kananɛ adistriti asɛmbliana wɔ amanshɛrbisheŋ be tɔrɔ to nɛ ku daga gomananti be kechɛto fɔŋfɔŋ ekpa monɛ ku beeŋ bulɔso n duwɔ efuli be kababi na be be awurfɔŋ so.

Frii SHS: Frii SHS (Frii Sinia Hai Sukuru) be asheŋkataso ela Ghana Gomananti be nkre pupɔr monɛ Prɛsidɛnti Nana Auffo-Addo be abani bra September 2017 nɛ ku bee sa JHS be ebiipo kama nɛ e jigi kasɔbii to ekpa nɛ e nyɛ SHS be kasukurubla jaga.
