71. Din yɛn baligi sama din be niyimbuyim taribu ni ka lahi kpaŋsi niyimbuyim taribu, tuun’ shɛŋa ŋan do gbunni ŋɔ tuma kpaŋsiya pam:
<<<P>>>
• Kumasi-Kintampo Lot shɛli din nyɛ 330KV nti pahi Kumasi-Bolgatanga Transmission Line Project la zaa tuma nyɛla din naai, ka yayili maa niɣilimbuyim niŋ yaa;
<<<P>>>
• Volta-Achimota Lot shɛli din nyɛ 161KV la ni Volta-Achimota-Mallam Transmission Line Upgrade Project la tuma nyɛla din miri naabu kamani 83%. Achimota-Mallam yayili gba tuma naabu paai 55%;
<<<P>>>
• Pokuase Bulk Supply Point Project nyɛla din naai, ka yooi n-tumdi tuma; nti pahi
<<<P>>>
