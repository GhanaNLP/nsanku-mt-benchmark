73. Gomnanti daa zaŋla o kpalanzuya n-gbuni asanzanima din yɛn che ka shikuriti yooi January 2021 yuuni la ka muɣisigu kani. Gomnanti daa tila shikuru kam infra-red tamomitanima la ni bɛ zaŋ yuli karimibihi mini karimbanima niŋgbunduyili zuɣu. Gomnanti daa lahi tila karimbihi zaa nyɛpɔbira ka daa lahi niŋ tiim n-yɔɣi Gomnanti mini sabiila shikuriti zaa ni. Lala sodola ŋɔ daa sɔŋmi m-baligi COVID-19 loobu, ka gu ka taɣi karimbihi mini karimba ni ŋun kam be zilɛli maa ni, ka lahi kpaŋsi kariŋ baŋsim bomma zuɣu yulibu viɛnyɛla yuuni 2021 maa ni.
