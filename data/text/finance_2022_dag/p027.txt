63. Ko n-kɔhi tinduya ni mini tiŋkpansi lɛbiginsim (Planting for Export and Rural Development) sodoligu la chɛmi ka Gomnanti zaŋ atiirnya, kɔfi, kube, abe, mooŋgu nti pahi lɔba (rubber) bimbira din kalinli yiyisi 1,360,589 tibihi n-tari pukpariba ban kalinli yiyisi 7,543 ka be Ahafo mini Ashanti ni Bono ni Central ni Eastern ni Greater Accra ni Oti ni Western ni Western North nti pahi Volta Kuliga duli bɔba ni yaya la zaa.
<<<P>>>
64. Biŋkɔbiri gbɛbu shɛhi ŋan kalinli yiyisi 10 nti pahi waanawaana ŋan kalinli yiyisi 11 nyɛla ŋan me n-ti niyi wubisibu tumayiya. Yaha, Gomnanti daa tarila sheriti tiima ŋan kalinli yiyisi 7,700,500 ni di tuhi nɔhi mini niyi dɔriti la.

65. Gomnanti daa tuyila kɔbu din yɔɣiri kom la tuun’ (irrigation project) shɛŋa ŋan dolina ŋɔ maalibu, ka di yi naai, di yɛn tila soli ni palo din yiyisi 6,766 ha ka kparilim tuma ni tooi tum yuuni puli ni saha zaa: Kpong Irrigation Scheme palo yiyisila (2,176ha), Tono Irrigation Scheme palo (2,490ha) nti pahi Kpong Left Bank Irrigation Project, (2,100ha). Kɔbu din yɔɣiri kom (Irrigation) tuun’ shɛŋa din pahi nyɛla Tamne din be Tudu Wulimpuhili bɔbili la mini Mprumem din be Central Region la, dina n-yɛn sɔŋ n-ti palo din yiyisi 375ha ni di kɔri shiŋkaafa mini ziɛgbana gba tuma nyɛla din naai.
