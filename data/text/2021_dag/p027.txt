Taachi: “Bomma ni nyamma labi kpaŋsi Naabu mini Kpaŋsibu ni Tuɣibu ni”
<<<P>>>
Ghana ŋɔ jintɔriba mini kabinɛti daa sayi ti ni di nyɛla yɛlikpani n-ti bipola mini bisarinsi shikuriti karikolom la;
<<<P>>>
• M-me bukunima du’pala 20 (20 libraries) n-gili tiŋgbaŋ ŋɔ bɔba ni yaya zaa, din ni duhi gomnanti litaafi ni duri kalinli m-paai 90, ka maan mali gomnanti litaafi duri 10 ni bisarinsi shikuriti litaafi duri 33 ŋan ni kpaŋsi karimbu mini bɔhimbu.
<<<P>>>
• N-tari karimbanima 157,000 nɛma din be karikolom palli maa ni tiŋgbani ŋɔ bɔba ni yaya zaa ni ka wuhi karimbakpamba, yaya ni bɔba niriba ban kalinli daa yiyisi 4,086, nti pahi karimbanima 152,000 ban be karikolom palli ŋɔ ni;
<<<P>>>
• N-da pikapu loorinima 840, boosunima 350 ni motonima 2,000 n-tari shikuru kaarokaaronima ni bɛ zaŋ viɛligi bɛ tuma;
<<<P>>>
• N-tuyi bisarinsi shikuru yoliyoli chandi tuma piligu, “Free SHS Programme”, din che ka ban di di daanfaani galisi yayi 2016/2017 yuuni la niriba 813,448 n-du paai shikurubihi 1,200,580 2019/2020 yuuni la ni;
<<<P>>>
• Ka ku liɣiri niŋ karinduri mɛbu ni din ni tooi deei “Free SHS Programme” maa ni ʒi muɣisigu shɛŋa la na:.
<<<P>>>
○ Ka naai bisarinsi shikuru duri 539, duri 1,119 shɛŋa bɛ ni daa pili bahi 2017 yuuni la puuni;
<<<P>>>
○ ka naai bisarinsi shikuriti ŋan nyɛ “Community Day Senior High School” bee bɛ ni booni shikuru shɛŋa “E Blocks” shikuriti ŋan be “Secondary Education Improvement Programme” (SEIP) tuma puuni la bin’ pihita ayi-ka (28)..
<<<P>>>
