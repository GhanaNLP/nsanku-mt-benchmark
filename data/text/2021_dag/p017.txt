MINISITA ĐUN SU BINDIRIGU MINI KPARIGU TUMA YAƔILI
<<<P>>>
Daabihi kɔhiri puzuri
<<<P>>>
25. Minisita ŋun su bindira mini pukparigu yaɣili daa tuyimi n-tumdi tuun’shɛŋa din be National Agricultural Investment Plan-Investing for Food and Jobs (IFJ, 2018-2021) (Tiŋgbani nia din nyɛ bindira mini tuma bo n-ti niriba la tuma yuuni puuni, ni di booi fakari ka lahi bo tuun’ maŋa n-ti niriba ka di zooi daabiligu. 2020 yuuni ni, Gomnanti daa tumla tuma din doli ŋɔ na:
<<<P>>>
• Pukpariba ban kalinli nyɛ 1,736,510 daa nyɛla ban di anfaani, di ni daa niŋ ka kulim daa booi la, ka daa lahi kpaŋsi bimbira yaɣili, ka di daa che ka niriba nya bindira pam, bindira kamani kariwana, ka ŋɔ zaa nyɛla tuma ka o bɔri tiri niriba maa;
<<<P>>>
• O daa lahi zaŋ piɛnyama kamani 7,500 n-tari pukpariba ban be Tudu Wulinluhili yaɣili (Upper West region), Tudu yaɣili (Northern region), ni Oti yaɣili, ka lahi zaŋ nolo’ bihi ban kalinli yiyisi 3,000 n-tari pukpariba ban be Tudu Wulinluhili (Upper West), Tiŋgbani Wulimpuhili yaɣili (Eastern region), Kambɔntiŋa yaɣili (Ashanti region), Tudu yaɣili (Northern region) ni Greater Accra yaɣa;
<<<P>>>
• N-lahi layisi binkɔb’ baligu kamani kuruchunima mini nɔhi kamani 531,100 zuyu n-tari pukpariba, ka pili m-mɛri binnɛma biɛhigu shee bini pia ni yini zuyu, ni waana diba ayopɔin bɛ ni yɛn wumsiri binkɔb’ baligu maa luy’ shɛŋa;
