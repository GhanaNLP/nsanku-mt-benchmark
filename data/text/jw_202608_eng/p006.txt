15 By examining the Bible carefully, we can build our confidence that Jehovah and Jesus are on our side. (Prov. 22:19)
<<<P>>>
As we meditate on accounts that show how much Jehovah and Jesus care for humans, we will be moved to count on them as reliable friends. The Bible is filled with reassurances that Jehovah and Jesus love us. For example, Jehovah describes his feelings for his people as being stronger than those of a nursing mother for her child. (Isa. 49:15) He considers any who cherish his name as “a treasured possession.” (Mal. 3:16, 17, ftn.) And the Bible reveals the depth of Jesus’ love for us by recording his sentiments about his sacrifice.—John 15:12, 13.
