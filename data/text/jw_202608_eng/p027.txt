I SEARCHED FOR A BETTER LIFE IN THE RAINFOREST
<<<P>>>
I returned to one of the remote villages in French Guiana where I had served in the army. I was hoping that my life would take on greater meaning if I was surrounded by nature, smoking cannabis and enjoying myself. But after a while, the old pattern emerged. I worked all week so that I could party on the weekend.
<<<P>>>
At about that time, someone gave me a Bible. I read some of it, hoping to find good advice to help me live better. But I didn't understand what I was reading.
<<<P>>>
I had friends who were also searching for the meaning of life, and they invited me to live with them in their informal community in the rainforest, a place they called Unity.
<<<P>>>
In the forest, life was simple, and we felt free. We did everything at our own pace without being accountable to anyone. We rose at sunrise, went to bed at sunset, grew our food, played music, and smoked cannabis. We took care of the land and shared everything we had with one another. We were vegetarians because we did not want to harm the animals. We had little respect for authority, and we did not hesitate to break the law if that was more convenient for us and if no one would get hurt.
<<<P>>>
We wanted to remake the world as we thought it should be. No one in our community had authority over others, and we respected one another regardless of our background. However, I realized that in the end, we all had our own interests and weaknesses and the same problems society faced eventually came up among us too. Some stayed in the community for only a few days; others, for weeks or months. I stayed for over two years.
<<<P>>>
