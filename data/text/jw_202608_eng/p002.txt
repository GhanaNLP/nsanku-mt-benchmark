Resist Satan’s Tactics—Trust In Jehovah and In Jesus
<<<P>>>
SONG 55
Fear Them Not!
<<<P>>>
FOCUS
Don’t be fooled by Satan’s attempts to misrepresent Jehovah God and Jesus Christ.
<<<P>>>
“Oppose the Devil, and he will flee from you.”—JAS. 4:7.
<<<P>>>
FROM the moment we decide to serve Jehovah, Satan and his demons become our unrelenting enemies. But Satan knows that he cannot overpower us as long as we have Jehovah’s support. So the Devil tries to undermine that support by getting us to turn our back on Jehovah. One way he does this is by trying to make us doubt that Jehovah and his Son are truly on our side.—2 Cor. 11:14; Jas. 1:6.
<<<P>>>
2 In the midst of a battle, soldiers need to have confidence in their leaders. If they were to lose that confidence, they might act independently. This could cost them their lives. In a similar way, we are soldiers in a spiritual battle against “wicked spirit forces.” (Eph. 6:12) Satan the Devil knows that if we lose confidence in Jehovah God and in Jesus Christ, we will likely reject their lead and support. So he does all he can to weaken our confidence in them. In this article, we will examine how he used this tactic in the past and how he is still using it today. Then we will consider how we can resist his schemes. In the next article, we will examine two other tactics Satan uses against us in our spiritual warfare.*
<<<P>>>
* Satan does not directly cause all the trials we face. But he is responsible for “the spirit of the world,” which influences opposers to mistreat God’s people. So whether directly or indirectly, Satan is behind many of the challenges we face in our spiritual warfare.—1 Cor. 2:12; 1 John 5:19.
