MY GENEROUS FATHER

When I think back to when I started pray-ing, I feel that the Creator noticed that I wanted to know more about him. One of my friends in the community had met Jehovah’s Witnesses and obtained the book You Can Live Forever in Paradise on Earth.* As I read it, I was amazed at what I was learning! I loved the prospect of living eternally on an earth transformed into a paradise. That’s what I was looking for—to live in an ideal

I understood that I needed to make changes in my life, but I also realized that I would need help. So I asked another friend to put me in contact with Michel, a Witness missionary with whom he was studying.

On the very day I met Michel, he invited me to a convention in Matoury, near the capital, Cayenne, more than 240 kilometers (about 155 mi) away. I had very long hair and a scruffy goatee. I wore purple and pink pants with bleach designs all over them and a shirt several sizes too large. Despite my appearance, the brothers and sisters provided me with transportation, food, and accommodations. The love and generosity of the Witnesses convinced me that I had found the truth.
