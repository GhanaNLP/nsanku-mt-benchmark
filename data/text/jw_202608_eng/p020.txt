FOCUS
To help us appreciate the gift of salvation as well as the privilege and the responsibility that come with it.
<<<P>>>
“WHAT will this young child turn out to be?” (Luke 1:57-66) The neighbors and the relatives were curious about John, the son who was born miraculously to aged priest Zechariah and his elderly wife, Elizabeth, who had been barren. Jehovah used Zechariah to answer that question. He explained that the long-awaited Messiah would soon arrive and that John would prepare repentant people to recognize and follow that one. (Amos 3:7; Luke 1:67-79) But Zechariah did more than relate what his son, John, would do.* As we will see in this article, Zechariah’s message was prophetic and it involves us today. From his message, we will learn about three things we should cherish: (1) the gift of salvation, (2) the privilege of sacred service, and (3) the responsibility to preach.
