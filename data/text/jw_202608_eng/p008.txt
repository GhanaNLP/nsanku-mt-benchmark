You Can Win the Battle!
<<<P>>>
“We have a struggle, not against blood and flesh, but against . . . the wicked spirit forces in the heavenly places.”
—EPH. 6:12.
<<<P>>>
AS SERVANTS of Jehovah, we do not engage in physical warfare. (Isa. 2:4; John 18:36) However, the Bible describes a fight that none of us can avoid—a struggle against wicked spirits. (Read Ephesians 6:11, 12.) How can we hope to succeed against such powerful enemies? Above all, we need to rely on Jehovah’s support. But we must also recognize the tactics that Satan and his demons use against us and our spiritual family.
<<<P>>>
2 In the preceding article, we considered how the Devil tries to undermine our trust in Jehovah God and in Jesus Christ. In this article, we will discuss two other tactics that Satan uses in his fight against us. He tries to break up our unity with our brothers and sisters. And he stirs up opposition in an attempt to distract or frighten us, hoping to stop us from carrying out the work Jehovah has given us to do.
