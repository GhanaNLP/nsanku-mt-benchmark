Serving in the circuit work in New Caledonia
<<<P>>>
Still, I was discouraged because I constantly fell short of my unreasonably high standards. (Eccl. 7:16) I needed to learn that Jehovah is a loving Father who does not demand perfection but who appreciates our personal best.
<<<P>>>
In Paris, I was assigned as a substitute circuit overseer. I felt too young and inexperienced for this role. But I learned that Jehovah’s holy spirit helps us accomplish far more than we can do alone.
<<<P>>>
I struggled with another challenge. Since childhood, I have suffered from hyperhidrosis, which involves excessive sweating. This condition especially affects my hands. It worsens during stressful situations, such as when I am addressing an audience at an assembly or a convention. But I can minimize this physical reaction by praying to Jehovah for peace of mind. The problem has not disappeared, but when I rely more on Jehovah, I remain calmer and sweat less.
<<<P>>>
In 2011, Jehovah kindly granted a longstanding request of our hearts. We began missionary service in New Caledonia, an island group in the southwest Pacific, more than 16,000 kilometers (10,000 mi) from Paris. This assignment too presented challenges. We had to adjust to new foods, such as cassava, taro, and yams. And we had to get used to the crowing of roosters and the barking of dogs interrupting our sleep early in the morning. We also had to adapt to a new style of conversation. The local people often respond, not with words, but merely by raising their eyebrows.
<<<P>>>
Jehovah has also helped us to adjust to new theocratic assignments. We served as field missionaries and for a few years as Bethelites. We attended the School for Kingdom Evangelizers, and now we are in the circuit work. We have experienced Jehovah’s support through his holy spirit in each assignment. He has taught me that personal weaknesses do not necessarily make us less useful to him. So I pray often and earnestly for help to accomplish what is needed. Although I still do not feel at ease before an audience, with God’s help, I have been able to provide encouragement and direction to the congregations we visit.
<<<P>>>
Through all these changes, Claudine has been a constant support for me. She is very patient with my imperfections. She is supportive in the work we share, always ready to give a zealous witness. She does not complain about the hot weather and the challenges of being in the circuit work.
<<<P>>>
At my first convention, I learned that Jehovah, the Grand Creator, always cares for his worshippers. As a result, I have gained many blessings. Jehovah has helped me to have the right view of authority and to be humble. I stopped associating with friends from the world so that I could live by Jehovah’s standards, but Jehovah has more than made up for this by providing me with many true friends. I have found a wonderful wife. And I have the magnificent hope of enjoying Jehovah’s generosity for all eternity.—Mark 10:29, 30.
