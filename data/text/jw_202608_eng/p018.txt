14 Philemon had a quality that strengthens love: hospitality. He opened his home to the congregation for meetings. (Philem. 2) Paul also alluded to Philemon’s hospitality when he asked Philemon to prepare a place for him to stay. (Philem. 22) Philemon’s warm hospitality undoubtedly made others feel welcomed and refreshed.—Philem. 7.
<<<P>>>
15 How can imitating Philemon’s hospitable spirit strengthen our love for others? We get to know our brothers and sisters better when we show them hospitality. Spending time with those outside our immediate circle of friends, even those we may not initially be drawn to, helps us to appreciate the variety of personalities that make Jehovah’s people beautiful. Learning about their background, their journey into the truth, or their endurance through trials often deepens our love and respect for them. We see why Jehovah has drawn them to his family of worshippers. (John 6:44) Showing hospitality therefore helps us love our brothers and sisters more, assume the best about them, and forgive them when they make mistakes.
