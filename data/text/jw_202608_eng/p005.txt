10 When we are going through person-al challenges, Satan wants us to believe that Jehovah doesn’t care about us. Sa-tan hopes that we will become angry if Jehovah allows our suffering to con-tinue. (Prov. 19:3) Those who begin to feel this way may even wonder: ‘What is the point of continuing to follow Je-hovah’s laws and principles? It doesn’t seem to benefit me!’ This type of think-ing could lead us to come under Satan’s influence.
<<<P>>>
11 Satan uses such tools as social media to raise doubts about Jehovah’s stan-dards. On these platforms, many peo-ple express the view that the moral val-ues found in the Bible are outdated and that everyone should be free to set his own standards. Most certainly, “the rul-er of the world” has been very skilled at blurring the lines between what is good and what is bad.—John 14:30; Isa. 5:20; 1 Tim. 4:1, 2.
<<<P>>>
12 Listening to Satan’s propaganda could cause us to drift away from Jeho-vah. A young sister whom we will call Shannon relates: “It took me a while to realize that the morals I was being taught in school and the people I was as-sociating with were distorting my sense of right and wrong. The more time I spent on social media, the more I want-ed to copy the values of those I met on-line. I gradually began to drift away from Jehovah, my family, and my brothers and sisters.” Eventually, Shannon found her way back to her heavenly Father and escaped from the Devil’s snare. What helped her?
<<<P>>>
13 Shannon realized that she needed to work hard to remain friends with Je-hovah and Jesus. She says: “I prayed and worked in harmony with my prayers by doing research in our publications. The video Young People Ask—How Can I Make Real Friends? helped me a lot.* In that dramatization, a young sister was experiencing many of the same chal-lenges that I was. She humbly turned to her parents and asked for help. I did the same. My parents, the elders, and mature Christian sisters in the con-gregation helped me to break free from my bad association. I came to under-stand that the truth is not only about going to meetings and pleasing my par-ents but about making the truth my own and maintaining a close personal rela-tionship with Jehovah.”
