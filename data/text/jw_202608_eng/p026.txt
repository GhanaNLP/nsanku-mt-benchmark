My Grand Creator Noticed Me
<<<P>>>
AS TOLD BY STÉPHANE LANDEROIN
<<<P>>>
AS A student, I did not enjoy school, and my grades were poor. I preferred to do things alone and not be noticed. I certainly had no reason to expect that God would notice me. Why not?
<<<P>>>
Growing up in a secular, atheist environment in Paris, France, I did not believe in God. Instead, I embraced the theory of evolution. I accepted it without question as many do, assuming that what was taught at school must be true. This teaching seemed logical, so I saw no reason to examine further evidence about how life originated.
<<<P>>>
My parents had a negative view of religion. They reasoned that if there were a God, the world would not be full of suffering and injustice. And because I saw so much religious hypocrisy, I too thought that it was foolish to believe in a loving God. I even made fun of those who believed in God.
<<<P>>>
Now, however, I am convinced not only that the Creator exists but that he cares about me, despite my weaknesses. Let me tell you what changed my mind and how that conviction changed my life.
<<<P>>>
In my late teens, I was called up for compulsory military service and sent for one year to French Guiana in South America. This remote and beautiful location would have a lasting impact on me.
<<<P>>>
When I was 20, I returned to Paris. My life soon became a weekly repetition of working hard during the week and drinking and partying hard on the weekend. But this life was empty.
