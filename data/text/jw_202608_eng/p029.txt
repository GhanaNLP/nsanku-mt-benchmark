As I progressed in my Bible study, I made changes. I decided to stop smoking tobacco and cannabis. Right after making that decision, I threw the remaining tobacco and cannabis into a fire and never smoked again. I began attending some meetings. At one meeting, I asked a brother to cut my long hair, which he did right there at the Kingdom Hall.
<<<P>>>
After a few weeks, I left the community in French Guiana and returned to France. I found a Kingdom Hall and resumed my Bible study. Although my life had improved, my family was puzzled at how much I had changed. I no longer smoked, got drunk, or went to wild parties. However, they did not appreciate my blunt attempts to preach to them. I later learned that I needed to be more tactful and considerate of their feelings.
<<<P>>>
I got baptized in 1996. I prayed to Jehovah that I could become a missionary like Michel. This seemed to be the best way for me to show my gratitude to Jehovah.
<<<P>>>
MORE BLESSINGS FROM MY HEAVENLY FATHER
<<<P>>>
In my congregation, a beautiful sister named Claudine caught my attention. I noticed that she was zealous in the ministry, and her comments at the meetings showed me that she loved Jehovah very much. I was interested in courtship, but I first wanted to know if Claudine was interested in missionary service. She was! We married in April 2000.
<<<P>>>
The following year, we began pioneering together. Sometime later, we decided to learn Mandarin to support a Chinese group in a nearby congregation. I found it very difficult to learn this language. I had to adjust to a new way of thinking and to be creative in presenting the truth. Honestly, I never learned to speak Mandarin well. But I did my best, and we spent unforgettable and wonderful years with the Chinese brothers and sisters. Not only was it a great blessing but it was excellent training for later assignments.

DEALING WITH CHALLENGES
<<<P>>>
Over the years, I’ve had to overcome difficulties. For example, I believed that my efforts to give Jehovah and my brothers and sisters the best were never good enough. So I spent far more time than was necessary preparing my teaching assignments.
