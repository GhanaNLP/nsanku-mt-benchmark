• Gyima ngyikyi mɔɔ bɛfa adwuledualɛ setampe bɛbetabeta bɛ ninyɛne zo la: bɛbahwe zɛhae adwuledualɛ ne bɛazinze ekyi wɔ ɛvolɛ ye anu;
<<<P>>>
• Kale nwo Adwuledualɛ: Adwule mɔɔ tolɔtolɔ nee Takizi ahale menle tua la, bɛbahwe bɛazinze wɔ ɛvolɛ 2021 ne anu. Ɛhye baboa yeamaa kalevolɛ kakɛ ne azo ade.
<<<P>>>
• Mpaefoa mɔɔ saa Bɛandua ye Ndɛndɛ a Bɛfa Bie Bɛto zo la: Arane ɛyɛ ye kɛ gyima gyima mɔɔ ɔkadwu Siane Bulu nee Nwiɔ ne awieleɛ wɔ ɛvolɛ 2020 ne anu mɔɔ bɛandua bɛ mpaefoa la, ɔbaye kakɛ mɔɔ anrɛɛ bɛfa bɛto mpaefoa ne azo la yeamaa bɛ wɔ ɛvolɛ 2021 ne anu. Na emomu gyima gyima nee bɛdabɛ mɔɔ bɛandua bɛ mpaefoa wɔ siane nsa mɔɔ li moa wɔ ɛvolɛ 2020 ne anu la, ngyehyɛleɛ ɛhye ɛnga bɛ. Ɔwɔ kɛ gyima gyima nee bɛdabɛ mɔɔ ngyehyɛleɛ ɛhye fale bɛ nwo la kɛlɛ bɛ anluma wɔ Adwulelielɛ Sonvolɛ Gyima mbuluku nu na bɛ nee bɛ yɛ nwolɛ ngyehyɛleɛ wɔ kakɛ ne ɛdualɛ nu.
