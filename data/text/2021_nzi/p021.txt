26. Wɔ ɛvolɛ 2021 ne anu, Arane bayɛ ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ:
<<<P>>>
• ɔbahɔ zo yeahyɛ aleɛ mra nee azɛlɛboɛyɛlɛ nyile yeamaa ɛyazonlɛma. Ɛhye baboa yeamaa yɛanyia abɛlɛ, awule, soya, aluba ngakyile, whiiti nee ahule dɔɔnwo. Ɔbamaa Gana anyia aleɛ dɔɔnwo na yɛava bie yɛahɔ maanle zo adɔne;
<<<P>>>
• eza bɛbahyɛ nyɛmoa mɔɔ bɛtɔtɔle wɔ ɛvolɛ 2020 ne anu la 531,100 bɛamaa ɛyazonlɛma. Nyɛmoa ne bie mɔ a le agbolike, ngɔkɔlɛ, mboane, mbɔnkye nee nlankɛ. Bɛbazi azua mgbole nsa na bɛadɔ ngɔkɔlɛ ngyikyi ngakyile mgbe 5 bɛahyɛ bɛamaa ɛyazonlɛma na bɛade ye bolɛ ne azo ɛya ngyɛnu 50;
<<<P>>>
• arane batɔtɔ egyinliyɛlɛ nwo milahyinli ngakyile mɔɔ ye bolɛ bayɛ Ameleka Dɔla ne mgbe US$30 la bɛamaa ɛyazonlɛma na yeaboa yeamaa milahyinli ɛyazonlɛ akpogya;
<<<P>>>
• arane batɔtɔ nyile ngakyile mɔɔ bie dodo le adoba 97,000 yɛɛ bie noko ɛnlomboɛ bayɛ kilo apenle 14,000 la. Bɛbava nyile ɛhye mɔ bɛahu azone mɔɔ sɛkye abɛlɛ nee aleɛ ngakyile mɔɔ ɛha la;
<<<P>>>
