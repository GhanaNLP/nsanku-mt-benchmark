KPƆ MI KPƆ MI NI TSUMI NƆ YAMI KƐ E KPO JEMI
<<<P>>>
33. Ghana ma a nɔ yami blɔ nɔ tomi kpiti nɛ a to kɛ fɔ si kɛ je 2018 kɛ ya ta 2021 ɔ nya ta, nɛ 2022 nɔ ɔ nɛ ji Ghana ma nɔ yami kpiti nɛ ji 2022 kɛ ya si 2025 (Medium-Term National Development Policy Framework -MTNDPF) ɔ maa je sisi kaa nɔ tsami nɔ. A fi blɔ nya tomi ehe nɛ ɔ kɛ da ma nɔ yami blɔ nya tomi gaga nɛ ji Ghana@100 ɔ nɔ. Nya Nɔ Tsami Kɛ Ma Sika Blɔ Nya Tomi kɛ Ma Bi A Nɔ Yami Blɔ Nya Tomi (2017 kɛ ya ta 2024), Ghana Nɛ Ko Di Yemi Kɛ Buami Se (Ghana beyond Aid) Blɔ Nɔ Tomi ɔ, Ghana KƆVID-19 Mi Jemi Kɛ Yemi Kɛ Buami Blɔ Nya Tomi ɔ nɛ ji GhanaJUƐƆ O HE ɔ maa ya nɔ. Blɔ hyɛmi nɔ kake ngɛ blɔ nya tomi nɛ ɔ mi ji kaa e nyɛ nɛ e gbla UN Ajɛnda 2030 (UN agenda 2030) yi mi tomi ɔ kɛ Aflika Kake Peemi 2063 yi mi tomi ɔ (African Union Agenda 2063) nɛ ba mi.

34. Blɔ nya nɛ ɔ tsɔɔ Amlaalo ɔ mɔdebɔ ngɛ he pɔtɛɛ komɛ ngɛ ma a sika blɔ nya tomi ɔ he ngɛ 2021 jeha a mi. E tsa nɔ nɛ e je blɔ nya tomi oti komɛ nɛ a maa ya nɔ ngɛ 2022 jeha a mi sisi hulɔ.

Nihi Ngɔmi Kɛ Wo Ni Tsumi Mi He Nane Hiami
<<<P>>>
35. Bɔ nɛ pee ha nɛ Amlaalo ɔ nɛ e je ni tsumi nɛ be ma a mi ɔ kɛ je blɔ mi ɔ, e ngɔ blɔ nya tomi komɛ kɛ pue si kaa Ghana nihi a peemi he piɛ pomi nɔ nɛ ji (Ghana Productive Safety Net Project -GPSNP) ɔ kɛ fɔ si. Ngɛ blɔ nya tomi nɛ ɔ sisi ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ maa:
<<<P>>>
i. to ni tsumihi a si kɛ ha nihi nɛ a hiɛmi maa su 33,952 nɛ a ngɛ ohia nganangana sisi nɛ a ngɛ MMDA 80 hu sisi nɛ a hiɔwo nya buami ya da sidi ayɔ 53.43 ; kɛ
<<<P>>>
ii. sika nɛ e yibɔ hiɛmi maa pee sidi ayɔ 5.82 kɛ ha nihi nɛ a hiɛmi su ayɔ 9,244 ngɛ wehi a nya ngɛ mahi maa pee 128 ngɛ MMDA 41 a sisi ngɛ Omleyi Kpo ngua amɛ a mi nɛ a kɛ je ni tsumi sisi aloo a kɛ bli a jua yemi ɔmɛ a mi.

36. Bɔ nɛ pee nɛ Amlaalo ɔ yi mi tomi kaa e ma kudɔ aloo kpaka nihe kɛ yihe kɛ wo ni tsumi kpakpahi a mi nɛ e gbɔ blɔ hia kɛ je aklowa aloo kɔpe ɔmɛ a mi kɛ ba ma ngua amɛ a mi ɔ nɔ nɛ a kɛ dla nihe kɛ yihe a si hihii mi nɛ e pee gbɔjɔɔ bɔɔ ha mɛ ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ bli ni tsumi maa pee kaa 130,000. Ni tsumi nɛ ɔ ekomɛ ji ni komɛ a hɛ mi plɛmi aloo tsakemi, ngmɔ ni komɛ a dumi, asafo tsopa tsɛmi aloo sumi, zu mi zɔ nɛ ji fɛtilaiza womi, tso mi jemi, kokoo ngmɔhi a nɔ nyu puemi.
