SIKA HE BLƆ NYA TOMI NƆ YAMI NGƐ 2021 MI
<<<P>>>
Ma a sika he blɔ nya tomi nɔ yami
<<<P>>>
3. Nihi a nya buami kɛ ha bɔ nɛ ma a sika he blɔ nya tomi ya nɔ ha ngɛ 2021 mi ɔ ji nɛ ɔmɛ nɛ:
<<<P>>>
• Kɛ ha kulaa nya buami tutuutu ɔ, ma a sika blɔ nya tomi nɔ yami ɔ gbedee we nɛ e ba si pe lafa mi gbami 3.5 ngɛ jeha 2021 e kekle mi gbami ɔ mi kaa bɔ nɛ e ya nɔ lafa mi gbami 0.7 ngɛ jamɛ a be ɔ nɔuu mi ngɛ 2020 jeha a mi ɔ;
<<<P>>>
• Ma a sika he blɔ nya tomi nɛ zu mi nu he nihi piɛɛ we he ya nɔ kɛ su lafa mi gbami 4.8 ngɛ jeha 2021 kekle mi gbami ɔ mi ke waa kɛ to lafa mi gbami 1.1 he ngɛ jamɛ a be ɔ nɔuu mi ngɛ 2020 jeha a mi;
<<<P>>>
• Ma mi he wami nɛ haa nihi a he jua wa a, ha nɛ nihi a he jua kuɔ kɛ ya ta lafa mi gbami 10.6 ngɛ Lemɔ hlami ɔ nyagbe ɔ ke waa kɛ to lafa mi gbami 10.4 nɛ ya nɔ jamɛ a be ɔ mi nɔuu ngɛ 2020 jeha a mi;
<<<P>>>
• Kɛ ha kulaa nya buami tutuutu ɔ, bɔjɛti ɔ mi si gbeemi aloo e mi hiɔ ɔ (sika) ya da lafa mi gbami 7.7 kɛ wa ngɔ kɛ da ma a tsuo a weto ni nɛ a na a nɔ ɔ, e ya hiɔwe pe lafa mi gbami 7.4 nɛ a ngɔ hɛ kɛ fɔ nɔ ɔ;
<<<P>>>
• Sika papɛɛhi a womi ɔ ya da lafa mi gbami 2.0 ke o kɛ to si gbeemi nɔ nɛ ji lafa mi gbami 1.7 nɛ a ngɔ hɛ kɛ fɔ nɔ ɔ nɔ he;
<<<P>>>
• Hiɔ nɛ ma a ye ɔ ya da lafa mi gbami 77.7 ngɛ Lemɔ hlami ɔ nyagbe ngɛ 2021 kɛ o kɛ to lafa mi gbami 76.1 nɔ ɔ he kaa bɔ nɛ e ya nɔ ngɛ Maja hlami ɔ nyagbe ngɛ 2020 mi ɔ;
<<<P>>>
• Ma a sika nɛ a to si ngɛ ma se ngɛ Maya hlami ɔ nyagbe ɔ ya su US sika a nɛ ji dɔla a yɔɔyi 11.44. Enɛ ɔ ma nyɛ maa sɛ nihi ngɛ hlami 5.2 mi ke waa kɛ to US sika a nɛ ji dɔla a yɔɔyi 8.62 nɛ ko nyɛ sɛ nihi hlami 4.1 ngɛ Maja hlami nyagbe ngɛ 2020 jeha a mi ɔ;
<<<P>>>
• Sika he jua nɛ a kɛ tsakeɔ sika ekpa amɛ ba da si kɛ ha sika ekpahi a tsakemi ngɛ jeha 2021 mi. Tutuutu ɔ, Ghana sidi ɔ he wami ba si maa pee kaa lafa mi gbami 1.7 ke waa kɛ to US dɔla a he ngɛ Lemɔ 2021 nyagbe ɔ, be mi nɛ sidi ɔ nɔuu nɔ gbɔ maa pee kaa lafa mi gbami 2.9 ngɛ jamɛ a be ɔ mi nɔuu ngɛ jeha 2020 ɔ mi.
