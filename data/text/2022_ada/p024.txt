49. Amlaalo ɔ to blɔnya titlii ko kɛ fɔ si ngɛ hehi nɛ a po fɔ si kaa a ko tsu ni ngɛ ma kpɔ komɛ nɛ ngɛ Jɔ, Tano, Offin kɛ Densu pa amɛ a he ɔ, nɛ a to blɔ slɔɔtohi a nya kɛ ha ni pɔtɛɛ komɛ a peemi. A to blɔnya kaa a tsɔse tso bubuii ɔmɛ a nɔ hyɛli ngɛ ma tsɔwi kpaago komɛ a mi, nɛ a ma tso bubuii maa pee kaa 16,937. A ngɛ enɛ ɔ pee bɔ nɛ pee ha nɛ a dla kɛ to kla nɔ, hehi nɛ a ngɛ pa amɛ a he ɔ nɛ a kɛ kpa nimli a ni peepee komɛ nɛ puɛɔ nyu ɔmɛ.
