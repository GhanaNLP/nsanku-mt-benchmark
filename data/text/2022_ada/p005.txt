NYA BLIMI
<<<P>>>
KƆVID-19 hiɔ yaya a ngɔ nyagba kɛ ba ha je kɛ wɛ tsuo a sika blɔ nya tomi nɛ Ghana hu piɛɛ he. Nyagba kɛ haomi slɔɔto nɛ hiɔ nɛ ɔ kɛ ba ha Ghana bi kɛ gu gbenɔ, si hihii mi nyagba kɛ ni tsumihi a puɛmi mi ɔ, ngɔ haomi agbo kɛ ba fɔ Amlaalo ɔ nɔ. I ngɛ gugue sie kɛ de ke blɔhi a nɔ nɛ wa gu kɛ tua hiɔ yaya nɛ ɔ ji hɛ mi wami kɛ si fimi nɔ ko nɛ maje mahi tsuo a bua jɔ he. Amlaalo ɔ ngɔ mlaahi kɛ emu yemi blɔ nya tomi kɛ fɔ si nɛ e wa kɛ hue aloo wo ni tsumihi a nɔ nɛ e dla wa si himi ɔmɛ kɛ to kla nɔ ekohu.
<<<P>>>
Kɛ tsa nɔ ɔ, ngɛ nyagbahi kɛ ni ekpahi nɛ a te si kɛ wo hiɔ nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ nɛ Nana Addo Dankwa Akuffo Addo daa si ngɛ nya a, hia nane oya nɛ e po sika nɛ e hiɛmi su yɔɔyi lafa (100) kɛ ha KƆVID-19 hiɔ ɔ nɔ gbɔmi kɛ nihi a hɛ mi plɛmi blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ nɛ a wo lɛ biɛ kɛ GhanaJUƐƆ O HE “Binyɛ Kpakpa” blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ (the Ghana CARES, “Obaatan Pa” programme). Blɔ nɔ tomi nɛ ɔ ngɔ yemi kɛ buami kɛ ba nɔuu nɛ e ba je ma bi kɛ je fikɔfikɔ mi nɛ e ngɔ ma a kɛ da e nane nya ekohu.
<<<P>>>
Wa hɛ sa hemi kɛ yemi nɛ nyɛ na ngɛ Amlaalo ɔ mi kɛ yemi kɛ buami nɛ nyɛ ha ngɛ mɔde nɛ e ngɛ bɔe nɛ a kɛ fie hiɔ nɛ ɔ. Ngɛ be nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, wa si fimi nɛ wa na hɛ nɔ kami kɛ ha ma nɔ yami ɔ, sɛ ka agbo ko mi. Kaa bɔ nɛ Yesaya de ɔ, “Bliikisi we ɔmɛ ku pue si; wɔɔ lɛɛ wa maa ngɔ tɛ nɛ a ko kɛ maa ma ekohu. A poo sikamore tsohi ngɔ pueɔ si; se wɔ lɛɛ waa kɛ seda tso kpakpahi ma tsake mɛ.” Amlaamlo ɔ fia pɛɛ si kaa e maa ye e si womi ɔmɛ a nɔ, ma tsa nɔ nɛ e tsu e yi mi kɛ blɔ nya tomi ɔmɛ a nya ni konɛ e nyɛ nɛ e ngɔ wɔ kɛ da kla nɔ nɛ e blɛke ma bi a si hihii mi ekohu.
<<<P>>>
Yi mi tomi oti kake nɛ ngɛ yi mi tomihi nɛ a kɛ wo 2022 bɔjɛti ɔ mi ɔ kɛ ya su jeha etɛ nɛ maa ba ngɛ wa hɛ mi mi ɔ ji Amlaalo ɔ maa pee ma a hɛ mi tsakemi nihi nɛ ma a nɛ e pee dɛhe ni tsumi blimi ma nɛ ma nyɛ ma hue aloo wo ma a sika blɔ nya tomi nɔ kɛ ma si. Enɛ ɔ ma wa kɛ bli ni tsumi kɛ ha nihe kɛ yihe. Amlaalo ɔ ma plɛ hɛ kɛ kpee mlaahi kɛ blɔ nɔ tomi nihi nɛ maa ngɔ yemi kɛ buami kɛ ha nihe kɛ yihe nɛ a nyɛ nɛ a di a adebɔ nike nile kɛ juɛmi nɛ maa ngɔ sika nɔ yami nɛ e bli ni tsumi nami blɔ kɛ ha nɔ fɛɛ nɔ.
<<<P>>>
Bɔ nɛ pee ha nɛ wa nyɛ hue gɛjɛmi, si fimi kɛ dɛhe ni tsumi mumi nɔ ngɛ nihe kɛ yihe a mi ngɛ 2022 jeha a mi ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ ma tle MooJesisi (YouStart) blɔ nya tomi ɔ si nɛ nihe kɛ yihe a na halɛ aloo ni tsumi sika kpɔ nɛ a kɛ pee a ni tsumihi aloo a kɛ bli nɔ nɛ ngɛ ɔmɛ a mi.
<<<P>>>
Ngɛ 2022 bɔjɛti ɔ mi ɔ, wa to blɔhi a nya nɛ ma wa kɛ je nyagbahi nɛ ngɛ ma sika he blɔ nya tomi ɔ mi ɔ, konɛ e wo mi he wami ngɛ KƆVID-19 hiɔ yaya a se. Amlaalo ɔ ngɔ e he kɛ fɔ si ha blɔ nya tomihi nɛ a mi bli nɛ a maa ye bua kɛ ha hɛ mi ga nɛ ma wa ni tsumihi a nɔ yami kɛ ha nihe kɛ yihe. Be mi nɛ wa ngɛ Ma nɔ yelɔ ɔ hɛ mi mami nɔ nɛ ji Ghana Nɛ Hiaa We Yemi kɛ Buami ɔ se jae aloo se die ɔ, i ngɛ hlae nɛ ma kɛ fɔ Ghana bi tsuo a hɛ mi titli ɔ niye kɛ yihe nɛ a nu fɔ kɛ wo bo nya tsami ngɛ dɛhe ni tsumi blimi blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ mi. I ngɛ nyɛ yi woe nɛ nyɛ za nɛ nyɛɛ ngɔ nyɛ he kɛ wo blɔ nya tomihi nɛ a kɛ ma hue ma a nɔ kɛ pee dɛhe ni tsumi ma a mi.
<<<P>>>
I su nyɛ yi nɛ nɛ i wo wɔ tsuo eka nɛ wa kɛ to wa Ghana ma a si kɛ ba da bɔ nɛ wɔ tsuo wa ngɛ hlae ɔ.
