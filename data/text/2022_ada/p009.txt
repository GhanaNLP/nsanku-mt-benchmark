4. Tohi kɛ yemi kɛ buami sikahi nɛ wa na kɛ je Jɔne kɛ ya si Lemɔ 2021 yibɔ ya su Ghana sika a sidi yɔɔyi 46.85 (10.7 GDP), kɛ waa kɛ to blɔ nya tomihi a mi tsakemi yi mi tomi ɔ nɛ ji Ghana sika a sidi yɔɔyi 51.31 nɛ ji lafa mi gbami (11.7 GDP) ɔ he. To ɔmɛ tsuo a nya buami nɛ nine su nɔ ɔ nɛ ji nihi nɛ zu mi nu piɛɛ we he ji lafa mi gbami (74), nihi nɛ pi zu mi nu nɛ to ko be nɔ ji lafa mi gbami (9), Nu kɛ Lahiɔ nɔ womihi ji lafa mi gbami (9), to ekpahi ji lafa mi gbami (6) kɛ sika mi yemi kɛ buami ekpa komɛ ji lafa mi gbami (2) kaa bɔ nɛ e ngɛ foni 1 mi ɔ.

5. 6. Amlaalo ɔ sika he ni tsumi kulaa nɛ a bua nya a (hiɔ nɛ piɛ womi) ngɛ Lemɔ 2021 hlami ɔ nyagbe ɔ yibɔ ya ta Ghana sika a yɔɔyi 80.71 nɛ tsɔɔ lafa mi gbami (18.4 GDP) ke waa kɛ to yi mi tomi mi tsakemi nɛ ji Ghana sika a yɔɔyi 83.89 nɛ daa si kɛ ha (19.1 GDP) ɔ he.
