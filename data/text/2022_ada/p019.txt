27. Amlaalo ɔ hia nane bɔni Ghana hapohapo tsopahi a peemi he mami. A je mlaa nɛ maa ngmɛ blɔ kɛ ha hapohapo tsopa peemi ɔ sisi nɛ a je sisi hu bɔni ma a hapohapo tsɔsemi he sukuu hu mami. Kɛ piɛɛ ma bi a he wami kpakpa nami blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ he ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ maa da e yi mi tomi nɛ ji ajɛnda 111 blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ nɔ kɛ ma tsami hehi nɛ a maa bɔ sika he mɔde bɔ nɛ pee ha nɛ ma bi a nine nɛ su tsami blɔ nya tomi gbugbuugbu nɔ kɛ ha nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ke benɛ e ke suɔ jeha 2030 ɔ.

Yihe kɛ Nihe a Ni Tsumi Nami Nyagba He Ga Womi
<<<P>>>
28. Bɔ nɛ pee ha nɛ wa kɛ hla tsaba kɛ ha nihewi kɛ yihewi a ni tsumi nami mi nyagba a, Amlaalo ɔ gu blɔ pɔtɛɛ komɛ kaa NEIP, NABCO, Ma a Hue Tsohi a Si Tlemi kɛ NBSSI (nɛ amlɔ nɔ ɔ e ba pee GEA) ɔ a nɔ, nɛ a ba pee ni tsumi nami hehi ha nihe kɛ yihe kɛ SME ɔmɛ.
<<<P>>>
29. Kɛ gu kpe titlii ko nɛ Amlaalo ɔ kɛ nihi aloo kuuhi nɛ a he hia, nihe kɛ yihe a nya buami kuu, kuu ekpa komɛ kɛ ni kasemi he komɛ pee ɔ mi ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ maa je blɔ nya tomi nɛ ji “MooJESISI” aloo “YouSTART” ɔ sisi kɛ ma bɔni ni tsumi ngɛ 2022 jeha a mi. Blɔ nɔ tomi nɛ ɔ ma bua sika sidi yɔɔyi kake nya nɛ a kɛ bli ni tsumi kaa ayɔ kake ngɛ jeha etɛ mi. Enɛ ɔ maa ya nɔ ngɛ “GhanaJUƐƆ O HE” (GhanaCARES) blɔ nya tomi ɔ sisi. “ MooJESISI” blɔ nya tomi nɛ ɔ maa ye kɛ bua nihe kɛ yihe nɛ a suɔ dɛhe aloo dɛnya ni tsumi ɔ nɛ a na jua yemi sika kpɔ, tsɔsemi, gawo kɛ nane fiaami blɔ nɔ tomi nɛ e wa mɛ kɛ bli a ni tsumihi.

Je Mi Tsakemi Ni Tsutsui kɛ Nane Hiami nɛ maa Gbla Oslaba Hewa nɛ E He Jua Ba Si ngɛ Ma Sika Blɔ Nya Tomi ɔ Mi
<<<P>>>
30. Blɔ nɔ tomi nɛ ɔ ma ha ma a sika blɔ nɔ tomi ɔ maa te si ekohu ngɛ KƆVID - 19 hiɔ yaya a se ɔ nɛ e ma bli blɔ kɛ ha nihi a hɛ mi tsakemi. Kɛ gu enɛ ɔ peemi mi ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ to blɔ nya kɛ pue si kɛ ha je mi tsakemi ni komɛ nɛ a maa ba a, a hɛ mi tsakemi konɛ a kɛ hla sika kɛ wo hiɔ komɛ nɛ e bli ni tsumi blɔ ɔmɛ kɛ gbɔ ohia nɔ.
<<<P>>>
31. Ghana ngɛ blɔ slɔɔto titlii komɛ a nɔ gue nɛ e kɛ dla hue tsohi nɛ a gbe ɔmɛ kɛ ba da a nane nɔ ekohu. Ngɛ enɛ ɔ mi ɔ, Amlaalo ɔ ngɛ blɔ slɔɔtohi a nɔ gue kɛ tle hue tsohi nɛ a gbo aloo a puɛ ɔmɛ si kɛ ba da a blema glɛ ɔmɛ a nɔ ekohu. Blɔ nya tomi nɛ ɔ maa ngɔ hue tso ɔmɛ a he piɛ pomi blɔ nɔ tomi gbugbuugbu kɛ ba konɛ wa kɛ baa hue tso ɔ, e mi ni kpakpa kɛ e mi lohwe ɔmɛ a yi.
<<<P>>>
32. Bɔ nɛ pee ha nɛ wa nye tsi hue tsohi a gbemi ni peepee ɔmɛ a nya a, Hɛ mi nyamitsɛ nɛ ji Ma nɔ yelɔ ɔ, nyɛɛ Ghana ma a tsuo a hɛ mi nɛ a kɛ du tsohi nɛ a hiɛmi be ayɔ kpaago nɔ ngɛ Osabu hlami ɔ nɔ ngɛ 2021 jeha a mi. Blɔ nɛ ma nɔ yelɔ ɔ hia a ha a du tsohi nɛ a hiɛmi kulaa pe nɔ nɛ a to yi mi kaa a ma du ngɛ blɔ nya tomi nɛ ji Pee Ghana Ba Mumu (Green Ghana Project) ɔ sisi ɔ.
