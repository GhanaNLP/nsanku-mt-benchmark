33. Akɛni bei ni ato kɛha maŋ muu lɛ fɛɛ nɔyaa nitsumɔi ahe gbɛjianɔtoi komɛi ahe wojiaŋ saji eho kɛjɛ (2018-2021) hewɔ lɛ, afi 2022 ji be ni akɛbaatsake nibii ahiɛ kɛsaa maŋ lɛ yɛ ‘Ghana Medium-Term National Development Policy Framework (MTNDPF)’ 2022-2025 lɛ shishi. Wojiaŋ mla gbɛjianɔtoo hee nɛɛ kpasa afii abɔ bei ni maŋ muu lɛ eto kɛha nɔyaa nifeemɔi ni ji ‘Long-Term National Development Framework’ (Ghana@100) lɛ, nifeemɔ nibii ni asusu he ni kɛtsɔ shika saji kɛ shihilɛ kpakpa he mlai kɛjɛ (2017-2024) nɔ lɛ, Ghana Efo mli yɛ Nibalamɔ Gbɛjianɔtoo (Ghana Beyond Aid Agenda) kɛ ehe ŋaatsɔɔ gbɛi, covid 19 foiwoo kɛ nitsumɔi amli hewalɛwoo, kɛ nifeemɔi ni baaye abua ni ji (GhanaCARES) lɛ. Nɛkɛ gbɛjianɔtoi nɛɛ, bɔ ni afee ni eye ebua koni kɛtsɔ no nɔ lɛ otii kɛ susumɔi ni ka UN 2030 (SDGs) kɛ Africa Union bii 2063 ahiɛ lɛ atsu nii.
