53. Kɛbashi Gbo 2021 nyɔɔŋ lɛ gbii 30 lɛ nɔ lɛ, aŋmala Ghana bii 15,656,160 ni eye kɛjɛ afii 15 kɛyaa agbɛi awo National Identity Register (NIR) lɛ mli, ni anyɛ aha amɛteŋ mɛi 11,648,391 akaadi. Aŋmala maŋsɛɛ bii hu 163,695 ni aha amɛ gbɔi akaadi.

54. Aje Jakumaŋ lɛ SIM Card Gbɛiŋmalamɔ gbɛjianɔtoo ni aakpa gbɛ akɛ ebaaye ebua juu kɛ fɔ naamɔmɔ kɛ mobai fon nɔ nifeemɔi ni esaaa lɛ shishi yɛ Antɔŋ 1 gbi yɛ 2021 lɛ nɔ, yɛ kpaatswaa nitsumɔi lɛ fɛɛ amli ni aakpa gbɛ akɛ abaagbe naa yɛ 2022, Otsokrikri nyɔɔŋ lɛ nɔ.
