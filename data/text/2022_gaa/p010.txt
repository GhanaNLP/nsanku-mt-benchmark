7. Amralofoi ashika gbɛjianɔtoo lɛ he nitsumɔi kɛ nyɔmɔ ni naa yakpa GH¢33.86 biliɔŋ (7.7% of GDP) ba, kɛto gbɛkpamɔ ni asaa akwɛ mli lɛ ni ji GH¢32.58 biliɔŋ (7.4% of GDP) he. Akɛ maŋ nɛɛ mli kɛ maŋsɛɛ shikai ni wo nyɔmɔi ni ba nɛɛ. Maŋ nɛɛ mli shika ni bote nyɔmɔwoo nɛɛ mli ji GH¢20.39 biliɔŋ, ni maŋsɛɛ shika hu ni bote nyɔmɔ nɛɛ mli hu naa yakpa GH¢13.47 biliɔŋ.

<<<P>>>
8. Nyɔmɔbɔɔ ni ba fiofio lɛ naa yakpa GH¢341.76 biliɔŋ (US$58.348 biliɔŋ) yɛ 2021, Gbo nyɔɔŋ lɛ naagbee. Enɛ feɔ oha mlijaa 77.8 (77.8% of GDP). Nibii ni ha nyɔmɔbɔɔ nɛɛ ba lɛ ji maŋsɛɛ nyɔmɔi ni shɛɔ GH¢163.65 biliɔŋ (US$27.88 biliɔŋ) kɛ maŋ nɛɛ mli shikafitemɔ ni ji GH¢178.11 biliɔŋ (US$30.35 biliɔŋ).
