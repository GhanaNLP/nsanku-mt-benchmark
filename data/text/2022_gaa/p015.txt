Shikataomɔ mla he gbɛjianɔtoi
<<<P>>>
14. Amralofoi lɛ kɛ gbɛjianɔtoi komɛi baatsu nii, ni no baaha nitsumɔi aya hiɛ koni amɛnyɛ amɛdamɔ akaŋshii naa yɛ maŋ nɛɛ mli kɛ maŋsɛɛ jarai anɔ fɛɛ. Jɛ enɛ hewɔ lɛ, ehe ehia akɛ afee mlai ni baa nɛɛ:
<<<P>>>
• Koni akɛ sɛɛnamɔi awo nitsumɔ lɛ mli ni anyɛ afee nibii babaoo lɛ, Amralofoi lɛ baatsi mamafeelɔi aVAT hemɔbe ni akɛba 2019, yɛ mamai ni afee yɛ Ghana lɛ nɔ lɛ kɛya sɛɛ afii enyɔ ekoŋŋ.
<<<P>>>
• Kɛji nifeemɔ nɛɛ tee nɔ afii enyɔ kɛ fa sɛɛ lɛ, akɛ gbɛjianɔtoo mla ni ŋmɛɔ gbɛ ni atseɔ nibii ni ashɛɔ kɛbaa lɛ anɔ be kukuoo lɛ baatsu nii bɔ ni afee ni asusu emlifoo nyɔŋlo kɛ gbɛkpamɔi lɛ ahe.;
<<<P>>>
• Abaatse too ni aheɔ yɛ shika futru ni atsaa ni atsuuko he lɛ nɔ kɛjɛ oha mlijaa 3 kɛba oha mlijaa 1.5;
<<<P>>>
• Kɛjɛ Aharabata nyɔɔŋ lɛ nɔ yɛ afi 2022 lɛ mli lɛ Amralofoi lɛ, kɛtsɔ Ghana Revenue Authority nitsumɔ lɛ nɔ lɛ, baaye abua Municipal Assembly (MMDAs) bii lɛ koni awo mla gbajaa ni baaye abua shiai kɛ japadei anɔ tooyeli kɛ ehe akɔntaabuu.
<<<P>>>
• Amralofoi lɛ baasaa akwɛ toi kɛ shikai ni aheɔ yɛ nifeemɔi ahe koni MDA’s lɛ kɛtsu nii kɛjɛ Aharabata klɛŋklɛŋ gbi lɛ nɔ yɛ afi 2022 lɛ mli. Toi kɛ nyɔmɔ ni kamɔ nibii anɔ ni abaahe lɛ, abaakwɛ maŋ lɛ shika jarawalɛ yɛ maŋ lɛ mli lɛ nɔ, koni ahe lɛ shi kome nyɔŋlo;
<<<P>>>
• Too ni aheɔ yɛ gbɛjegbɛi fɛɛ anɔ lɛ, aheŋ dɔŋŋ kɛji ahe bɔjɛti nɛɛ awo mli sɛɛ.
<<<P>>>
• Abaagbele “Electronic Transaction Levy” aloo “E-Levy” nifeemɔ ko ni yeɔ buaa ni anyɛɔ awoɔ toi kɛtsɔɔ kpaatswaa nɔ nifeemɔi amli kɛgbele too loo shikahemɔ nitsumɔ lɛ mli yɛ aŋkroaŋkro nitsumɔi lɛ amli. Nitsumɔi amli ni abaahe toi nɛɛ yɛ ji mobai foŋ nɔ shika majemɔ, banki shika kɛ mɔhamɔ, shika ni akɛmajeɔ, kɛ keenɔi anɔ, ni nakai toi lɛ anaa baayakpa oha mlijaa 1.75% ni mɔ ni kɛmajeɔ lɛ ji mɔ ni baawo shika nɛɛ bɛja keenɔi lɛ pɛ ni mɔ ni baahe lɛ hu baawo too yɛ he. Abaaje enɛfeemɔ shishi kɛjɛ Oflɔ nyɔɔŋ lɛ gbi klɛŋklɛŋ lɛ nɔ yɛ afi 2022 lɛ mli. Nifeemɔ ni awoŋ shika yɛ he ji shika ni faaa fe GH¢100 loo nɔ ni shɛɛɛ nakai, yɛ gbi lɛ nɔ lɛ, fataaa too woo nɛɛ he.
