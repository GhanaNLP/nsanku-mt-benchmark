“Aatse Nitsumɔi Kɛmɔ shi: Maŋ shikai anaabuamɔ kɛ Nitsumɔtsemɔ”
<<<P>>>
SHIKA GBƐJIANƆTOO MLI WIEMƆI KOMƐI ASHISHITSƆƆMƆ
<<<P>>>
1D1F (Kpokpa kome Nitsumɔ kome): Nitsumɔtsemɔ yɛ kpokpa fɛɛ kpokpa nɔ, ni baaha Ghana atsɔ nibii ahiɛtsakemɔ maŋ. Yiŋtoo lɛ ji koni maŋbii lɛ ana nitsumɔi yɛ Ghana nɛɛ he fɛɛ he
<<<P>>>
Akɔntaabuu: Enɛ ji bɔ ni Amralofoi lɛ buɔ maŋbii akɔntaa yɛ nibii ni efee he, ni ehaa anaa nɔ ni jeɔ mli kɛbaa. Shika kɛ japadei krokomɛi fata nibii ni Amralofoi lɛ kwɛɔ nɔ haa maŋbii akɔntaabuu nibii nɛɛ ahe.
<<<P>>>
Nijaa: Gbɛi anɔ ni atsɔɔ ajaraa nibii ahaa Amralofoi anitsumɔ gbeji ni mɛi fɛɛ naa eko
<<<P>>>
Nɔ ko jiemɔ: Gbɛi anɔ ni atsɔɔ abaa haomɔ ko shi
<<<P>>>
Bɔjɛti: Eji gbɛjianɔtoo ni tsɔɔ shika ni he baahia, he ni abaana yɛ, kɛ nɔ ni akɛ shika lɛ baafee. Weku ko nyɛɔ eŋmaa ebɔjɛti ni atsɔɔ shika ni weku lɛ yɔɔ kɛ bɔ ni akɛbaatsu nii aha, nɔkwɛmɔnɔ: tsu nyɔmɔwoo, srawa hewalɛ niyenii kɛ ekrokomɛi. Kɛji shika he baahia kɛha nibii ni ataoɔ lɛ, no lɛ weku lɛ yataoɔ faahe, loo etseɔ ehiamɔ nibii lɛ anɔ loo etaoɔ gbɛ kroko ni kɛ shika baaba.
<<<P>>>
Bɔjɛti sɛɛ nyɔmɔ: Enɛ baa kɛji Amralofoi lɛ bɔ nyɔmɔ fe shika ni baa fotoro lɛ mli lɛ
<<<P>>>
Japadei ahe nyɔmɔbɔɔ: Enɛ tsɔɔ shika ni afite yɛ japade nitsumɔi tamɔ gbɛjegbɛi, sukui, helatsamɔhei, brijii, gbɛfaa, nuŋma, nitsumɔhei kɛ amɛmli tsɔji ahe.
<<<P>>>
Nitsulɔi anyɔmɔwoo: Eji nitsulɔi adaa nyɔɔŋgbele nyɔmɔwoo kɛ shikai krokomɛi ni yaa nitsulɔi adɛŋ tamɔ tako ni ahaa(allowances) gbɔlɛ mli nyɔmɔwo, kɛ shikai krokomɛi ni akɛhaa nitsulɔi.
<<<P>>>
Yelikɛbualɔi: Amɛ ji maŋsɛɛ maji, loo nitsumɔhei ni yeɔ buaa kpoa maji ni amɛya nɔ yɛ shika shidaamɔ mli. Yelikɛbualɔi nɛɛ ateŋ ekomɛi ji IMF, World Bank, ADB, loo maji ni kɛ Ghana ena aduatsɔ kɛha yelikɛbuamɔ. Maji nɛɛ ekomɛi ji Germany, USA, UK, knkn.
<<<P>>>
Shika Jarawalɛ Yɛ Afi Lɛ Naagbee Mli: Eji shika jarawalɛ yɛ Afuabe mli.
<<<P>>>
Shikafitemɔ Ni Efe Nɔ: Enɛ baa kɛji Amralofoi lɛ fite shika fe tɛ ni afo yɛ bɔjɛti lɛ mli ni Mlawookpee lɛ ehe ewo mli.
