Yɛ mɔdɛŋbɔɔ akɛ agbele “drone” kɛnitsumɔ, ni akɛ hiamɔ tsofai aya hei ni ehe hiaa yɛ lɛ mli lɛ Amralofoi kɛ Zipline na naashikpee koni amɛgbele drone shimaamɔhei enyɔ yɛ Anum kɛ Abujuro yɛ Boka kɛ Oti Kpokpa wuji lɛ amli. 83. Agbe 2021 mɛi kɛ shiai akanemɔ lɛ klɛŋklɛŋ nitsumɔ lɛ naa yɛ Manyawale, 2021 nyɔɔŋ lɛ gbi ni ji 8 lɛ nɔ.
