MAŊ LƐ SHIKA SAJI AHIƐTSAKEMƆ YƐ COVID-19 HELA LƐ SƐƐ

Ghana Susuɔ ohe (Yoofɔyoo Kpakpa) Nifeemɔ
<<<P>>>
17. Koni ayɔse ninamɔ ni baatsake Ghana kɛfo mli yɛ nibalamɔ yɛ COVID-19 hela lɛ sɛɛ hewɔ lɛ, Amralofoi lɛ je GH¢100 biliɔŋ Ghana COVID-19 hela lɛ nifeemɔi komɛi ashishi kɛmɔ nibii anaa koni akɛye abua nifeemɔi lɛ ashidaamɔ, ni gbɛi ji (GhanaCARES “Obaatan pa”). Esa akɛ nifeemɔ lɛ adamɔ enane nɔ, asaa awo mli hewalɛ, koni maŋ lɛ hiɛ atsake kwraa kɛtsɔ kanyamɔ nitsumɔi anɔ koni aye abua aŋkroaŋkro nitsumɔi lɛ hu.
<<<P>>>
18. Nibii ni baa nɛɛ ji nifeemɔ otii ni tee nɔ jogbaŋŋ yɛ afi 2021 lɛ mli kɛ susumɔi hu ni yɔɔ kɛha 2022 afi lɛ.
<<<P>>>
Ŋmɛnɛŋmɛnɛ okwaayeli kɛ niyenii shɛɛ mlitsakemɔ
<<<P>>>
19. Yɛ mɛi 579,662 naa lɛ, emli 547,126 lɛ okwaafoi ni, mɛi ni eshwɛ ni ji 32,536 lɛ okwaayeli he nitsulɔi krokomɛi kɛ jarayelɔi ji amɛ ni atsɔ kpaatswaa nɔ ‘electronic database’ aŋmala amɛgbɛi akɛ okwaayelɔi kɛjɛ Antɔŋ nyɔɔŋ lɛ nɔ yɛ afi 2021 lɛ mli, kɛ nifeemɔi krokomɛi amli.
<<<P>>>
20. Amralofoi lɛ ahe mɛi srɔtoi yɛ wuɔlɛlɔi, omɔdulɔi, soya kɛ ameo he nitsulɔi yɛ enɛmɛi ahe nitsumɔi lɛ amli koni amɛnu naagbai ni yɔɔ nitsumɔi nɛɛ amli lɛ ashishi, koni kɛtsɔ no nɔ lɛ akwɛ ni akutɔ kpa ni aheɔ yɛ shikai ni afaa kɛyatsuɔ enɛmɛi ahe nii lɛ amli, kɛmɔ okwaayeli he shika he naagbai lɛ anaa. IRS nɛɛ baakutɔ kpa ni abaawo lɛ mli oha mlijai 50, koni akɛwa kɛna nitsumɔ lɛ mli hiamɔ nibii ni no agbele nitsumɔi lɛ ajwetrii amli.
<<<P>>>
21. Koni okwaayeli adamɔ enaji anɔ lɛ, akɛ gbɛjianɔtoo ni baa nɛɛ baatsu nii 2022:
<<<P>>>
• Atse okwaayeli he shikpɔji ahe adafitswaahe (Ghana Agricultural Land Information Bank (GhLIB)) ni baagbele shikpɔji ahegbɛi aha okwaa nitsumɔi;
<<<P>>>
• Atswa he adafi ni oblahii lɛ anya okwaa nitsumɔi lɛ ahe, enɛfeemɔ mli lɛ akɛ kpai anɔ shɛɛmajemɔi (social media) afata he; koni
<<<P>>>
• Akadi okwaayelihei kɛ ehehiamɔ nibii lɛ, koni anyɛ akɛ ehe shɛɛwiemɔi kɛ nifeemɔi aha oblahii lɛ oya.
