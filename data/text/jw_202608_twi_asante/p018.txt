Suasua Filemon; da ahohoye adi na aboa ama asomdwoe ne baakoyɛ atena asafo no mu
(Hwɛ nkyekyem 15)
<<<P>>>
kyerɛ “ɔdɔ.” Ná asafo no mufo nyinaa nim sɛ Filemon dɔ deɛ, ɛda mu fua; ɔdɔ a ɛte saa nso na na ɛbɛboa no ama ɔde Onesimo bɔne akyɛ no. Nea ɛwom ne sɛ, Paul krataa a ɔtwerɔ kɔmaa Kolose asafo a na Filemon wom no mu no, ɔkaa sɛ ɔdɔ yɛ “hama a edi mu a ɛde nkabom ba.” (Kenkan Kolosefo 3:14.) Wei kyerɛ sɛ, su a ɛho hia paa a ɛde asomdwoe ne baakoyɛ ba ne ɔdɔ. Sɛ yɛdɔ afoforɔ paa a, ɛbɛyɛ mmerɛ ama yɛn sɛ yɛde wɔn bɔne bɛkyɛ wɔn na yɛne wɔn adi no yie. (1 Kor. 13:4-7) Ɛnneɛ, momma yɛnhwɛ nea ɛma ɛyɛɛ mmerɛ maa Filemon sɛ ɔbɛdɔ afoforɔ.
<<<P>>>
14 Ná Filemon pɛ ahohoyɛ, enti na ɛnyɛ den mma no sɛ ɔbɛdɔ afoforɔ. Ɔde ne fie mae sɛ asafo no nhyiam wɔ hɔ. (Filem. 2) Paul nso ma yehu sɛ na Filemon pɛ ahohoyɛ, efisɛ ɔka kyerɛɛ no sɛ onsiesie baabi a ɔbɛtena mma no. (Filem. 22) Ɛda adi sɛ Filemon ahohoyɛ no maa afoforɔ ani gyei na wɔn ho dwoo wɔn.—Filem. 7.
<<<P>>>
15 Sɛ yesuasua Filemon ahohoyɛ no a, sɛn na ɛbɛboa ama ɔdɔ a yɛwɔ ma afoforɔ no mu ayɛ den? Yɛda ahohoyɛ adi kyerɛ yɛn nuanom a, ɛma yehu wɔn yie. Wɔn a yɛnhyɛ da mmɛn wɔn papa ne wɔn a ɛsono sɛnea wɔte mpo, sɛ yɛne wɔn bɔ a ɛma yehu sɛ wɔwɔ suban a ɛyɛ fɛ. Wei boa ma Yehowa asafo no mu tena yɛ anika. Sɛ yehu abusua a wɔfi mu, nea wɔfaa mu ansa na wɔreba nokware no mu, sɔhwɛ a wɔahyia ne sɛnea wɔamia wɔn ani agyina mu no a, ɛma ɔdɔ ne obuo a yɛwɔ ma wɔn no yɛ kɛse. Ɛma yehu nea enti a Yehowa atwe wɔn abɛka n’asomfo ho no. (Yoh. 6:44) Enti sɛ yɛda ahohoyɛ adi a, ɛma yɛdɔ anuanom paa, na yenya wɔn ho adwene pa. Afei nso, sɛ wɔfom yɛn a, ɛboa ma yetumi de kyɛ wɔn.

16 Yɛrekasa yi, ɛyɛ den ma wo sɛ wode obi bɔne bɛkyɛ no anaa? Sɛ yɛdɔ yɛn nuanom mpo a, ɛtɔ da a ɛnyɛ mmerɛ sɛ yɛde wɔn mfomso bɛkyɛ wɔn. Dɛn na ebetumi aboa yɛn? Momma yɛnhwɛ Paul asɛm a ɔtwerɔ kɔmaa Kolose asafo a na Filemon nso wom no. Asɛm no wɔ Kolosefo 3:13. (Kenkan.)
