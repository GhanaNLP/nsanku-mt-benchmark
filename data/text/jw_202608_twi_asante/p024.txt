Yohane bɔɔ Yesu asu wiei no, ɔka maa obiara tee sɛ Yesu yɛ Yehowa nanmusini. Ɔkaa sɛ: “Hwɛ, Onyankopɔn Guammaa no ni, nea oyi wiase bɔne fi hɔ no!” Ɛno akyi no, Yohane ka kyerɛɛ n’akyidifo no sɛ wɔnkodi Yesu a ɔyɛ Mesia no akyi.—Yoh. 1:29, 34-36, 40, 41.
<<<P>>>
13 Yohane kaa no pefee sɛ Yesu ne Mesia no, nanso na ehia sɛ afoforo pii nso ka bi to so. Adɛn ntia? Sakaria kyerɛɛ mu sɛ, Mesia no bɛyɛ “adekyee hann.” Wei kyerɛ sɛ, na Yesu mmae no bɛsesa nnipa abrabɔ, na ama wɔanya nkwagye ho anidaso. Asɛmpa sei deɛ, na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔka ma obiara te bi, enti na Yohane nko ara rentumi nka. Ɛnneɛ, hwannom nso na na wɔbɛtrɛ saa asɛmpa yi mu?
<<<P>>>
14 Yesu yeree ne ho kaa asɛmpa no. Ɔno na Sakaria kaa ne ho asɛm sɛ ɔbɛma “wɔn a wɔtete sum ne owu sunsuma mu no anya hann” no. Nnipa a na nkurɔfo mfa wɔn nyɛ hwee no, na Yesu wɔ ayamhyehye kɛse ma wɔn. Ɔkaa “Yehowa adom afe” a ɛma awerɛkyekye ne anidaso no ho asɛm kyerɛɛ wɔn. (Kenkan Yesaia 61:1, 2; Luka 4:16-21) Ɔpaee mu kaa sɛ: “Ɔsoro Ahenni no abɛn.” Nanso na onim sɛ ebehia sɛ nnipa bebree nso ka asɛmpa no bi. Enti ɔtetee n’asuafo no sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wɔn nso bɛtoa so aka asɛmpa no.—Mat. 4:16, 17; 10:7; 28:19, 20.
