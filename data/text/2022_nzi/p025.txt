37. Maanle Mbaka Ɛlualɛ Ngyehyɛleɛ ne maanle bɛvale menli 72,000 maanle bɛboale wɔ mbaka ne mɔ ɛlualɛ nu. Ɛhye bole menli 60,000 ne mɔɔ ɛnee bɛ nye la kɛ bɛbava bɛ la azo. Mgbavolɛ nee mbɛlɛra mɔɔ bɛvale bɛ wɔ Mgbavolɛ nee Mbɛlɛra Mbaka Ɛlualɛ Ngyehyɛlɛ ne abo la boka menli ɛhye mɔ anwo. Bieko a le menli 17,000 mɔɔ bɛvale bɛ wɔ ‘Taungya’ Ngyehyɛlɛ ne abo la nee menli 2,000 mɔɔ bɛmaanle bɛtetɛle mbaka la. Bɛ ti anwo Gyimamenlema noko vale menli kɛyɛ 18,000 gyima nu.

38. Mgbavolɛ nee Mbɛlɛra Gyimanyianlɛ Ngyehyɛlɛ ne vale menli 82,869 gyima nu wɔ bɛ ngyehyɛlɛ ngakyile ne mɔ abo. “Gyimayɛlɛ Ɛleka ne mɔɔ boa gyimakpondɛlɛma maa bɛnwu gyimamenlema la boale menli 1,185 maanle bɛnyianle gyima bɛyɛle.

39. Ɔwɔ nu kɛ Kolonavaelɔso Ewulenzane ne vale ngyegyelɛ dɔɔnwo rale ɛdeɛ noko akee Maazikyi KO Milahyinli Gyima KO Ngyehyɛlɛ ne ɛhɔ zo ɛnyia anyunluhɔlɛ. Ɔvi Ɛvolɛ 2017 ne mɔɔ badwu ɛnɛ la bɛyɛ Maanzikyi Ko Milahyinli Gyima Ko dodo ne ala wɔ gyimayɛlɛ ɛleka ngakyile. Ɛhye mɔ anu, milahyinli Gyima 106 gua zo ɛlɛyɛ gyima kɛkala; bɛgua zo bɛlɛyɛ nuhua 148 yɛɛ ɔnrɛhyɛ na bɛabɔ nuhua 24 ɛyɛlɛ bo. Kɛkala, kɔmpɛlɛ ne mɔ mɔɔ bɛgua zo bɛlɛdi gyima nu la ɛva menli 156,782 gyima nu. Bɛyɛle nuhua 17,451 wɔ Ɛvolɛ 2021 ne anu.
