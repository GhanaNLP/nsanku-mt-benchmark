25. | Arane ɛlɛnyia anyunluhɔlɛ kpole kpalɛ wɔ kɔmputa zo gyimalilɛ ne anu. Ɔlua ɛhye ati, Arane maanle Ghanama mɔɔ bo mgbe 15 la nzonlɛzielɛ kɛlata. Eza bɛmaanle azua azua mgbe 7 nee foa kɔmputa zo adɔlɔse nɔma.
<<<P>>>
26. | Agyakɛ mɔɔ bɛlɛtu kɛ bɛyɛ gyima kɔmputa zo la ɛmaa bɛva bɛ adwenle bɛhɔ ezukoa sidi ne mɔɔ bɛdua kɔmputa zo bɛava bɛali gyima la ɛmaa Ghana Bange kpole ne ɛva Ghana sidi ne ɛhɔ kɔmputa zo. Bɛbuke Ghana Arane gyimalilɛ adenle (Ghana.gov) yɛɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ bamaa arane gyimalilɛ ali munli la ngyehyɛlɛ bɛdo ɛkɛ.
<<<P>>>
Sonla nwo Banebɔlɛ nee Kpɔkɛdelɛ mɔɔ gyi Kpundii la Ngyehyɛleyɛlɛ
<<<P>>>
27. | Arane ɛdu agyakɛ ɛsesebɛ kɛ ɔmaa bɛayɛ Ewule Adenle Ɛzilɛ Nyile Ɛleka. Bɛbawie ewule adenlezile nwo nyileyɛlɛ ngyehyɛleɛ yɛɛ bɛbɔ ɔ bo bɛlɛsi ewule adenlezilɛ nyile nwo Kɔlegyi. Ɔkadwu Ɛvolɛ 2030 ne anu la, Arane balua ye Ngyehyɛlɛ '111' ne nee Maanlema Kpɔkɛdelɛ Anwo Kakɛbɔlɛ Ngyehyɛlɛ ne azo akpogya maanlema kpɔkɛdelɛ nwo ninyɛne ne mɔ zo nee ezukoa mɔɔ bamaa awie biala anyia kpɔkɛdelɛ mɔɔ di munli kpalɛ la.

Mgbavolɛ nee Mbɛlɛra Gyimanyianlɛ nee Gyima Ɛyɛlɛ nwo Adwenle nwo Ngyegyelɛ ne Anloazɔlɛ
<<<P>>>
28. | Amaa yɛazɔ mgbavolɛ nee mbɛlɛra gyimanyianlɛ nee gyima ɛyɛlɛ nwo adwenle nwo ngyegyelɛ ne anloa la, Arane ɛlua ngyegyelɛ ɛhye mɔ azo ɛbuke gyimayɛlɛ ndenle ɛmaa bɛ nee gyima ngyebangyeba ne mɔ. Bie mɔ a le Maanle Ɛkyekyelɛ Mbɔlɔba, Maanle Mbaka Ɛlualɛ, Maanle Gyima Ngyebangyeba Ɛyɛlɛ Mbɔlɔba nee Maanle Bɛ Ti Anwo Gyimayɛlɛ Ngyehyɛlɛ Fofolɛ ne.
<<<P>>>
29. | Ɔlua ayia mɔɔ arane nee eku eku mɔɔ mgbavolɛ nee mbɛlɛra eku nee nwomazukoalɛ sukulu bie mɔ boka nwo la yɛle la, Arane bava Ɛdawɔ Bɔ Ɔ Bo ngyehyɛlɛ fofolɛ ne ali gyima wɔ Ɛvolɛ 2022 ne anu. Bɛbalua ngyehyɛlɛ ɛhye azo bɛava Ameleka Dɔla mgbe Ko bɛabukebuke gyima gyima wɔ Ɛvolɛ 3 anu wɔ Ghana DWENLE Ɛ NWO Ngyehyɛlɛ ne abo. Ɛdawɔ Bɔ Ɔ Bo ngyehyɛlɛ ne baboa bɛ ti anwo gyimamenlema ngyikyi bɛamaa bɛanyia dwetile, ndetelɛ, gyimayɛlɛ nwo adwenle ne ngilehilelɛ amaa bɛahola bɛabukebuke bɛ ti anwo gyima gyima.
