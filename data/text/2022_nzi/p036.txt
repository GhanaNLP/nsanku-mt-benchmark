Lateleke Kɛnlaneɛ Anwosesebɛ Gyima Ne
<<<P>>>
71. Amaa bɛade kɛnlaneɛ anwosesebɛ ɛnyianlɛ nee ye ɛhyɛlɛ ne mɔ azo na bɛamaa ye ɛhyɛlɛ ne ali munli la bɛyɛ gyima ɛhye mɔ bie dɔɔnwo. Bie a le:
<<<P>>>
• Kumase mɔɔ kɔ Kintampo kɛnlaneɛ Nyɛma ne mɔɔ ye tendenle le 330KV la. Ɛhye boka Kumase mɔɔ kɔ Bɔlegatanga kɛnlaneɛ anwosesebɛ ɛhyɛlɛ nyɛma gyima ne mɔɔ bɛwie na bɛbɔ ɔ bo bɛlɛfa bɛali gyima la;
<<<P>>>
• Vɔleta mɔɔ kɔ Akyemota nee Malam Nyɛma ne mɔɔ ye tendenle le 161KV la, bɛyɛ nuhua ɛya ngyɛnu 83. Bɛwie Akyemota mɔɔ kɔ Malam Nyɛma ne ɛya ngyɛnu 55;
<<<P>>>
• Bɛwie Pokuase Lateleke Dodo Ɛmaanlɛ Gyima ne na bɛkpa nwolɛ ɛdanlɛ. Bɛlɛfa bɛali gyima;
<<<P>>>
• Bɛwie Kasoa Lateleke Dodo Ɛhyɛlɛ Gyima ne anu Ɛya ngyɛnu 85. Ɛhye boka “GRIDCO’ nyɛma ne mɔɔ ye anwosesebɛ le 161KV mɔɔ vi Simpa (anzɛɛ Winneba) kɔdwu Malam nyɛma ne anwo mɔɔ bɛyɛle ye bieko la anwo nee nwolɛ gyima ngyikyi ne mɔ yɛɛ ɛnyelɛ la kɛ bɛbawie ye yɛ wɔ ɛvolɛ 2021ne awieleɛ.
<<<P>>>
72. Kolaa na yɛadwu Ɛvolɛ 2024 ne anu na lateleke Kɛnlaneɛ anwosesebɛ ne adwu ɛleka biala la, bɛvale lateleke kɛnlanea bɛhɔle azua nee namunamu 162 azo. Ɛhye maanle azua mɔɔ lɛ lateleke kɛnlaneɛ mɔɔ wɔ maanle ye anu la dodo ne kpogyale vile Ɛya ngyɛne 85.17 wɔ Ɛvolɛ 2020 hɔdwule ɛya ngyɛnu 86.63 wɔ Ɛvolɛ 2021 ne anu. Bɛgua zo bɛlɛtwe kɛnlaneɛ ne bie bɛahɔ azua azua 512 azo na bɛ ɛwielɛ ne ɛdwu gyinlabelɛ ngakyile.

Nwomazukoalɛ
<<<P>>>
73. Arane nee Eku eku mɔɔ nwomazukoalɛ fale bɛ nwo la bɔle nu yɛle ngyehyɛleɛ maanle bɛbukele sukulu adenle anzondwolɛ nu wɔ Siane Gyɛne ne anu wɔ Ɛvolɛ 2021 ne anu. Bɛvale bɛ nwo Wuluwuluyɛlɛ ɛzuzulɛ Milahyinli bɛmaanle sukulu biala kɛ bɛva bɛnlea kɛ bɛ nwo wuluwulu ne si de la. Eza bɛvale akpolo-bɛ-bonyi mɔɔ bɛkola bɛkpo na bɛkola bɛkpolo ye bieko la bɛmaanle sukoavoma ne mɔ. Bɛkpulule nyile wɔ Arane nee Bɛ Ti Anwo sukulu ne mɔ anu. Agyakɛdulɛ ɛhye mɔ boale maanle Kolonavaelɔso Ewulenzane ne ɛsalolɛ ne hanle aze. Ɔboale ɔmaanle sukoavoma, kilehilevolɛma nee maanlema nyianle anwobanebɔlɛ kpalɛ na ɔmaanle sukulu ne mɔ hole zo gyɛlɛɛnye wɔ ɛvolɛ 2020/2021 nwomazukoalɛ mekɛ ne anu.
