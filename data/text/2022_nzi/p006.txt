Bodane: “Bɛ Ti Anwo Gyimabukelɛ Maanle Ɛkyekyelɛ Mɔɔ Di Munli: Ezukoa Nwo Banebɔlɛ Nee Gyima Enyianlɛ”
<<<P>>>
NWOLƐ EDWƐKƐ
<<<P>>>
Ngyegyelɛ ne mɔɔ Kolonavaelɔso Ewulenzane ne ɛva ɛra la ɛha maanle maanle mɔɔ wɔ aleɛabo amuala mɔɔ Ghana boka nwo la. Anwongyelelɛ ne mɔɔ rale Ghanama anwo zo, mɔɔ maanle menli wule, bɛ tɛnlabelɛ nu zɛkyele nee gyima gyima mɔɔ anwo hanle bɛ la yɛle ngyegyelɛ maanle Arane. Meka metu me nwo kɛ, yɛvale akɛnrasesebɛ nee sɛbe, adwenle a yɛgyinlanle ngyegyelɛ ne anloa a. Ewiade amra kɔsɔɔti anye ɛzɔ yɛ wɔ zɔhane agyakɛdulɛ ne anwo. Arane eza yɛle ngyehyɛlɛ mɔɔ maanle gyima gyima dwazole bieko na yɛ tɛnlabelɛ nu yɛle boɛ bieko a.
<<<P>>>
Eza amaa yɛazɔ Kolonavaelɔso ngyegyelɛ ne anloa na yɛava adenlenyianlɛ ne mɔɔ rale nu la yɛali gyima la, Arane luale Maanle Kpanyinli Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo Arane ne anwo zo yɛle ngyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ maanle bɛvale Ghana ezukoa sidi mgbe mgbe 100 mɔɔ bɛvale bɛdele ngyegyelɛ ne azo na bɛvale bie bɛkpogyale gyima gyima mɔɔ hole aze la zo, ɛhye a le Ghana DWENLE Ɛ NWO “Ɛrɛladane Kpalɛ” ngyehyɛlɛ ne. Ɛhye vale anwondɔlɛ rɛlɛle yɛ ezuavolɛ ne ndɛndɛ zo na ɔlɛkpogya yɛ maanle ne zo.
<<<P>>>
Yɛ nye sɔ diedi mɔɔ wɔnyia wɔ Arane anu la wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛyɛ gyima yɛali konim wɔ Kolonavaelɔso Ewulenzane ne anwo zo la. Wɔ mekɛ ɛhye anu amuala, bɛzɔ yɛ kpundii ɛgyinlanlɛ kɛ yɛkɛgyinla kɛ maanle la bɛnlea. Baebolo ne eza da adengilelɛ fɔɔnwo ali wɔ mekɛ zɛhae mɔ anu, kɛ mɔɔ bɛla ye ali wɔ Ayezaya Mgbakyelɛ 9, Ngyehyɛnu 10 la, ɔse, “Belekese ne mɔ yɛɛ ɛdudu ɛgua a, na yɛbava awolɛ mɔɔ bɛze la yɛazi yɛ azua ne mɔ. Bɛdudu sekamɔ baka mgbunla ne mɔ, na yɛbava sida mbaka yɛaziezie bɛ bo zo ne mɔ.” Wɔ mekɛ ko ne ala anu, Arane eza ɛvua ɛtafe zo kɛ ɔmaa bɛayɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ ɔbole ɛwɔkɛ kɛ ɔbayɛ la kɔsɔɔti. Olua ɛhye ati ɔbahɔ zo yeayɛ ngyehyɛleɛ nee gyima gyima mɔɔ bamaa bɛayɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛtɛyɛle la kɔsɔɔti na yeamaa Ghana ezuavolɛ ne tɛnlabelɛ nu akpazi.
<<<P>>>
Ɛvolɛ 2022 Ezukoasɛm nwo Ngyehyɛleɛ ne bodane titili ne mɔ ko a le kɛ, wɔ Ɛvolɛ nsa (3) mɔɔ ɛlɛba la anu, Arane baboa amaa yɛayɛ maanle mɔɔ Bɛ ti anwo Gyimayɛlɛ di munli wɔ nu mɔɔ bamaa yɛahola yɛanyia anyunluhɔlɛ mɔɔ di munli kpalɛ la. Bɛbalua ɛhye azo bɛamaa menli dɔɔnwo, bɔbɔ mgbavolɛ nee mbɛlɛra anyia gyima mɔɔ la bɛ ahonle nwo la ayɛ. Arane ngyehyɛlɛ ne kɔsɔɔti baboa amaa mgbavolɛ nee mbɛlɛra ava bɛ gyimayɛlɛ ahyɛlɛdeɛ ne bɛali gyima amaa bɛali boɛ na pɛpɛpɛpɛyɛlɛ ali gyima amaa menli muala.
<<<P>>>
Amaa mgbavolɛ nee mbɛlɛra anyia diedi wɔ bɛ nu na bɛanyia bɛ ti anwo gyimayɛlɛ sunsum la wɔ Ɛvolɛ 2022 ne anu, Arane bava Ɛdawɔ Bɔ Ɔ Bo Ngyehyɛlɛ ne ali gyima.
