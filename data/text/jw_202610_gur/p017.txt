16 To san ni yese to suure za’a pẽɣɛ to mabiisi la ba halesoma la ge me tiregɛ tara suma’asum la ba, la pa’alɛ ti to nɔŋɛ ba mɛ. (Mr. 9:50) To san eŋɛ pa’alɛ ti to tari nɔŋerɛ to yetɔyum la to itigɔ poan, bala n wan pa’alɛ ti to dela Naayinɛ nɛreba. To san tara nɔŋerɛ, la pa’alɛ ti to soŋeri la Yehowa ti a basɛ ti yelekirega tuna a tigere la poan.

17 Ton nɔŋɛ taaba, tara nɔyinɛ ge ti to kãlɛ zɔ’ɔra se’em la pa’alɛ ti Yehowa n basɛ ti yelekiresi wa tuna a tigere la poan zina wa. La boi bim ti nɛreba zo’e zo’e giiseri to mɛ ge to kãlɛ la kɔ’ɔn zɔ’ɔra mɛ. Yelesego n kɔ’ɔn bɔna tingɔŋɔ wa zuo ge tomam tari la nɔyinɛ. To me boi la tinsɛka poan ti nɛreba ka tara taaba giile ge tomam n nɔŋɛ Naayinɛ, la nɛreba se’em la pa’alɛ ti to dela Krista yelemɛŋɛrɛ podɔleba. (Yon 13:34) To ka yeen ze’a bisa la yelekiresi wa ge to dela ‘Naayinɛ tuntuna tadaandoma.’—1 Ko. 3:9.
<<<P>>>
18 La boi bim ti daanɔ yɛla n kɔ’ɔn bɔna tingɔŋɔ wa zuo ge la dela yelekirega la Naayinɛ nɛreba kãlɛ n kɔ’ɔn zɔ’ɔra se’em la. Ba me tari la nɔyinɛ ge me tara yelemɛŋɛrɛ nɔŋerɛ bɔ’ɔra taaba. Sira, bala ti to kɔ’ɔn nyɛta zina beere wa! Yelekiresi wa basɛ ti to sakerɛ malum kpe’em mɛ ti, sɛla woo ti Yehowa yeti a wan eŋɛ beere sa’am la wan sirum tum. (2 Pe. 3:13) Kɔ’ɔn tẽra ti la dela yɔ’ɔ kãtɛ bɔ’ɔra hɔ ti ho layum pa’asɛ yelekiresi se’a ti Naayinɛ tuna zina beere wa la.
