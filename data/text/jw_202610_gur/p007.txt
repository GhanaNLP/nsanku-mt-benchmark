(Bisɛ kaalegɔ zɛ'an 1)
<<<P>>>

Basɛ ya ti to sɔsɛ soke atã wa: (1) Beni n sɔi ti la nara ti to eŋɛ pa'alɛ ti to puurɛ pee mɛ? (2) Beni n wan soŋɛ tɔ ti to ta'an eŋɛ pa'alɛ ti to puurɛ pee mɛ? (3) To wan eŋɛ la wani ta'an eŋɛ pa'alɛ ti to puurɛ pee mɛ?

4 To san ka eŋɛ pa'alɛ ti to puurɛ pee mɛ to ni tara la daaŋɔ paara tomea. Sɛba n ka iti pa'ala ti ba puurɛ pee mɛ la ni ka tara pupeelum. Baabule la yeti: “Se'em n boe too poan dabahese'a woo la, dela dabeha be'o.” (Mag. 15:15) Saŋa ase'a, yeledaaŋɔ ni paɛ to mɛ ti to suure sa'am. Dayɛ saŋa woo ti to wan ta'an maalɛ to yeledaaŋɔ la ge to wan ta'an loe ton wan eŋɛ se'em yele la poan. To san kɔ'ɔn ti'i-
<<<P>>>
sa to yeledaaŋɔ la ma'a yele, to suure wan sa'am. Bala wan basɛ ti to kan nyɛ yelesonɛ se'a n iti to vom poan. Bala me wan basɛ ti to kan tara pupeelum.
<<<P>>>
5 To san eŋɛ pa'alɛ ti to puurɛ pee mɛ to nyɛti la nyuurɔ. To wan tara pupeelum. Magaha 15:15 yeti: “Sunsonɛ tare la de'eŋɔ daarɛ woo.” Se'em n tari “sunsonɛ” ni basɛ ti a puti'irɛ bɔna la yelesonɛ se'a n iti a vom poan ge me ni eŋɛ pa'alɛ ti a puurɛ pee mɛ la yele aŋa. Bala zuo, a ni ka basɛ ti a yeledaaŋɔ la wayɛra a zuo. Bala la, la ka kpe'em ti se'em n iti pa'ala ti a nini tiyɛ me la tara pupeelum a vom poan.
