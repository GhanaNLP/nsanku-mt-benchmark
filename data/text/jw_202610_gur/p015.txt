TON TARI NƆYINƐ SE’EM LA DELA YELEKIREGA
<<<P>>>
9 Nɔtɔ’ɔsa Mikah yuun tɔɣɛ la yelekirega deyima n tuni tuuma zina beere wa. A yuun yeti, Naayinɛ yamesi wan ta tara nɔyinɛ n ka tari makerɛ. Yehowa yuun basɛ ti Mikah gulesɛ yeti, buraasi la pɔyesi wan naɛ taaba tara nɔyinɛ. (Kaalɛ Mikah 2:12.) Yetɔyebiŋere wa tum tuuma mɛ Naayinɛ nɛreba poan zina beere wa. La boi bim ti ban ze’ele zɛ’an, ba buuri, la ba hala boi la to’ore to’ore ge ba kelum tara la nɔyinɛ. La ani ba wuu lan yuun ani yoomadaana la se’em la. A yuun yeti: “Behɛ lan som ge zan’anhom se’em te, sɔɔnrɔ lagom kɛn’ɛra la nɔyenɛ.”—Ym. 133:1.
<<<P>>>
10 Zina beere wa, to kɔ’ɔn tara la nɔyinɛ sarɔtɔ zo’e zo’e poan. Makerɛ, ton boi zɛ’an woo, yelese’a ti to zamesa to tansuga, la layesegɔ poan za’a dela buyina. Tingɔŋɔ wa za’a poan, Yehowa Sɛɛradoma n ze’ele tisi to’ore to’ore ti ba bɔŋa bɔna to’ore to’ore ni naɛ taaba mɛ tuna la nɔyinɛ. Ba iti la bala to tigere yila poan, la se’em ma’a ti ba mɛta to pu’usegɔ zẽ’ɛsi. To tari la bɔnsoŋa la taaba. To ka basɛ ti nalɛɣerɛ yɛla, bii ton ze’ele buuri sɛka bii ton tari ligeri se’em yɛlesa tɔ. Ti’isɛ yele wa bisɛ. Zẽsɛka woo ti to yeen keŋɛ, to mabiisi ni kɔ’ɔn maasum mɛ ti ba soŋɛ tɔ ge kpɛmesa to giila ti to kɔ’ɔn pu’usa Yehowa. Mabiisi boi la tisi to’ore to’ore poan ge ban ti’iseri ge ita yɛla se’em la ni basɛ ti Yehowa puurɛ pee mɛ.
<<<P>>>
11 To nɔyinɛ la dela yelekirega sɛla n sɔi la nɔyinɛ ka boi tingɔŋɔ wa poan la fii me. Tingɔŋɔ wa za’a poan, nɛreba yeleseri la taaba. Dabeserɛ woo, to kɔ’ɔn wuna la zaberɛ, nɛreba n isige giisɛ gɔmɛt, la nɛreba n ka nani bɔ’ɔra. Susa’aŋɔ yele la dela, nɔyinɛ ka boi ge ti tunfɛ’ɛsa kɔ’ɔn zo’e tingɔŋɔ wa poan. Wuu Baabule la n puyum yele se’em la, nɛreba ‘ka tari yelemɛŋɛrɛ, . . . ka tara nɔyinɛ, ba parone mɛ bɔ’ɔra nɛreba, ka tara nyɔkɛ n mea, dɛna zabezabereba, ba ka nɔŋɛ som, ba ŋma’anre la nɛreba, tona pototooma ge me kɔ’ɔn pa’ala ba mɛhe.’ (2 Tim. 3:2-4) La ka pakɛ “la teŋparɛ wa zan’an n pon bɔna be’emdaana la,” la a kolekpabɛ’ɛsi la nu’usin la, to kɔ’ɔn kelum tara la nɔyinɛ. (1 Yo. 5:19; Lil. 12:9) Bala sirum dela yelekirega!
<<<P>>>
12 To wan ta’an layum soŋɛ ti nɔyinɛ bɔna Yehowa tigere la poan. Wani wani? La dela to kɛlesa Yehowa pa’alegɔ ge tara tuna tuuma. Ton zameseri yelese’a Baabule la poan la soŋeri to mɛ ti to di’isa suure ge dita sugeri. La me soŋeri to mɛ ti to ka ŋmɛ’ɛra nɔkpɛ’ɛma ge ita som bɔ’ɔra nɛreba. (Ep. 4:2, 31, 32) To san ka bisa to mabiisi tuure yɛla, ka bibisa ba yɛla ge dita sugeri bɔ’ɔra ba, to soŋeri la Yehowa ti a basɛ ti yelekirega tuna a tigere la poan.
