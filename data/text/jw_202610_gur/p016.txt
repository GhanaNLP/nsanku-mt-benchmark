Yehowa nɛreba tari la yelemɛŋɛrɛ nɔŋerɛ bɔ’ɔra taaba, la ba kpɛ’ɛsi
(Bisɛ kaalegɔ zɛ’an 14)
<<<P>>>
nɛreba baŋɛ eŋa podɔleba la dela bamam n tari nɔŋerɛ bɔ’ɔra taaba la. (Kaalɛ Yon 13:35.) Yuun kɔbesɛka n deŋɛ nɛŋa Kristakɔma la yuun iti la yɛla zo’e zo’e ti la pa’alɛ ti ba tari la nɔŋerɛ. Makerɛ, ba yuun ni tara la ba nutatum soŋera sɛba n ka tari la. (Tm. 2:44, 45) Ba yuun ni zusera bɔ’ɔra sɛba ti ba mɛlesa ba la mɛ. (Tm. 12:5) Ba me yuun soŋeri kiibesi la pɔkɔ̃pa mɛ. (Yam. 1:27) La ka pakɛ la bamam n de nasedoma la, ba ni kelum soŋera ba mabisɛba ti la wuna ba la mɛ. (2 Ko. 8:1-4) Tertullian, n de yuun kɔbesɛka n pa’asɛ buyi gɔngulesa la pa’alɛ la nɛreba n yuun tɔɣɛ Kristakɔma n deŋɛ nɛŋa la yele se’em. A yeti: “Bisɛ . . . ban nɔŋɛ taaba se’em ge me ni kɔ’ɔn maasum ti ba ki bo taaba.”
<<<P>>>
14 Wuu yuun kɔbesɛka n deŋɛ nɛŋa Kristakɔma n yuun iti se’em la, Naayinɛ nɛreba zina beere wa me kɔ’ɔn tara la yelemɛŋɛrɛ nɔŋerɛ bɔ’ɔra taaba. To iti pa’ala ti to nɔŋɛ sɛba n daɣɛ to mabiisi mɛ doose la ton mɔɔli yelesoma la bɔ’ɔra ba. To me tari la to nutatum layum soŋera tuunɛ wa. Nɛnɛra ka beleni to bii pẽregera tɔ ti to ita bala. La me daɣɛ pẽregerɛ ti to loe ligeri wana mɔpi soŋɛ. Ton nɔŋɛ to mabiisi la n sɔi ti to ni soŋera ba se’em ma’a ti la wuna ba.* To ni buyela taaba suure mɛ, ita yelese’a n wan basɛ ti to tara pupeelum ge me kpɛmesa taaba giila ti to ta’an pu’usa Yehowa. Wana wa kɔ’ɔn makɛ mɛ la tuntuna Paul n yuun ka’am tɔ yeti: “Ya soŋɛ sɛba te dabeem tara la ge me kelom soŋɛ sɛba n nan ka sɛ ze’ele soŋa soŋa Yesu sore dɔlɔga la poan la. Ya mɛhe me dɛna sɛba n wan tara sugure la nɛra woo.” (1 Tes. 5:14) Yehowa basɛ ti to pa’asɛ la mabiisi n ani wuu to deodoma poan. To nɔŋɛ taaba mɛ ge me nɔŋɛ to kpɛ’ɛsi.

15 Yehowa nɛreba n tari nɔŋerɛ se’ere la dela yelekirega sɛla n sɔi la tingɔŋɔ wa nɛreba kɔ’ɔn ti’isa la ba ma’a yele. Tuntuna Paul yuun gulesɛ yeti tomam saŋa wa poan nɛreba wan ‘nɔŋɛ la ba mɛhe la ligiri ma’an [ge me] nɔŋɛ nyɛlum.’ Ba ka nɔŋɛ Naayinɛ ge me “ka [tara] nɛra giinle,” hali la ba deodoma. (2 Tim. 3:2-4) Yezu yuun yeti nɛreba wan dɔ̃ta taaba ge me dina taaba. A yuun le yeti ‘nɛreba zo’e
