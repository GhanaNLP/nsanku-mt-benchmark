<<<P>>>
papa. Naaso ɔnnyɛ iyi nkotsee na Jehovah bɛyɛ ama hɛn. Mbom, sɛ amanaman no ka hɔnho bɔ mu na wɔbɔtow hyɛ hɛn do a, ɔronnhwɛ hɔn dzinn ma wɔnntɔr hɛn ase. (Ezek. 38:1, 2, 10-16) Mbom ɔbɔsoma Jesus Christ nye no sor dɔm no ma wɔabɔbɔ hɛn ho ban, na wɔnye hɛn atamfo no ako. Iyi na ɔbɛhyɛ Amagedon ko no ase. (Nyi. 17:12-14; 19:11, 14, 15) Jehovah besi dɛn abɔ n’asomfo ho ban wɔ Amagedon ko no mu?
<<<P>>>
19 Onnyi dɛ yɛma ɔsɛɛ a wɔdze bɛba dɛm wiadze bɔn yi do wɔ Amagedon ko no mu no bɔ hɛn hu. Ebɛnadze ntsi a? Osiandɛ Jehovah yɛ hɛn guandɔbea nye hɛn akoban ntsi yɛgye dzi dɛ ɔbɔbɔ hɛn ho ban. Ntsi yebotum enya awerɛhyɛmu paa dɛ, sɛ Jesus nye no sor dɔm no bɛsɛɛ Nyankopɔn n’atamfo wɔ Amagedon ko no mu a, hɔn mu kor mpo rennka. (Jer. 25:33; Zech. 14:12) Naaso, dɛm aber no Nyankopɔn remma n’atamfo kwan ma wonnku hɛn mu kor mpo, osiandɛ yɛsom bo ma no.—Luke 21:28; 2 Pet. 2:9.
<<<P>>>
20 Bi a wɔ Amagedon ko no mu no, asɛm a ɔdwontofo no kae yi bɛba mu ma yɛdze hɛn enyi ehu. Ɔkaa dɛ: ‘Apem bɔtotɔ wɔ wo nkyɛnmu, na ɔpedu atotɔ wɔ w’enyimfa; naaso orunndu wo nkyɛn. W’enyiwa nko na edze bɔhwɛ, na ehu ɔdzebɔnyɛnyi n’akatua. Osiandɛ ekaa dɛ, Ɔwo Jehovah nye mo guandɔbea! Edze Ɔsorsornyi no ayɛ wo tsenabea.’ (Ndw. 91:7-9) Sɛ Amagedon ko no hyɛ ase a, yɛgye dzi dɛ Jehovah bɔbɔ n’asomfo ho ban dɛ kuw na ɔbɔbɔ hɛn mu biara so ho ban. Ɔremma biribiara nnsɛɛ yɛnye no ntamu na ɔremma kwan ma hɛn atamfo so mmpira hɛn anaa wonndzi hɛn bɔn. Afei so, ɔbɛma hɛn asomu adwe hɛn. Osiandɛ aka dɛ ‘Jehovah nye wo guandɔbea’ ntsi, ɔno so ahyɛ wo bɔ dɛ ɔsɛɛ a Amagedon ko no dze bɛba no “runndu wo nkyɛn.” Dɛm ntsi onnyi dɛ yesuro dza obesi daakye biara. Mbom ɔsɛ dɛ yenya ahotodo dɛ sɛ yɛdze hɛn werɛ nyina hyɛ Jehovah mu a, ɔno so bɔbɔ hɛn ho ban.

19-20. Ebɛnadze ntsi na onnyi dɛ yɛma ɔsɛɛ a ɔbɔkɔ do wɔ Amagedon ko no mu bɔ hɛn hu? (Hwɛ mfonyin a ɔwɔ Ɔweɔn-Aban yi enyim no so.)
<<<P>>>
MFONYIN NO HO NKYERƐKYERƐMU Ɔweɔn-Aban yi enyim: Amagedon ko no mu no, Jehovah bɔbɔ hɛn ho ban na ɔnnkɛma yeepira anaa asɛm bɔn biara ato hɛn, na ɔbɛma hɛn asomu so adwe hɛn.
