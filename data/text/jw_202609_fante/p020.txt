Jehovah Ayɛ Ɔdɔ Ho Esusudo

DZA YEBOSUA
Yɛbɔhwɛ mbrɛ asɔfodzi ho nhyehyɛɛ a Jehovah ayɛ ama hɛn no ma yɛdɔ no kɛse.
<<<P>>>
Asɔfodzi Ho Nhyehyɛɛ No Ma Yehu Dɛ Jehovah Dɔ Hɛn
<<<P>>>
“Ɔsɔfopanyin biara a woyi no wɔ nyimpa mu no, wɔhyɛ no sɔfo ma ogyina nyimpa ananmu.”—HEB. 5:1.
<<<P>>>
HOM mma yɛnka na nyimpa hia mboa paa! Adasamba nyina sɛ yɛgye to mu o, sɛ yɛnngye nnto mu o, yehia boafo. Nokwasɛm nye yi, hɛn asɛm yɛ mbɔbɔr. Ebɛnadze ntsi na yɛreka dɛm? Osiandɛ fitsi ber a Adam na Eve yɛɛ bɔn no, wɔdze bɔn awo nyimpa nyina. Na iyi ama adasamba nye Nyankopɔn ntamu atsetsew. (Eph. 2:12; Col. 1:21) Sɛ yɛbɛka a, bɔn ama ɔka kɛse bi abɛda yɛnye Nyankopɔn ntamu, na sɛ obi ammboa hɛn a, hɛnankasa runntum nnkɔ Nyankopɔn ne nkyɛn. Sɛ Nyankopɔn nnyi hɛn abrabɔ mu so a, enyigye a wɔfrɛ no enyigye dze yerinnya bi da, na yerinnya enyidado biara so. Ɔnodze, ebɛnadze na Jehovah ayɛ dze aboa hɛn?
<<<P>>>
2 Jehovah ayɛ nhyehyɛɛ bi a ɔnam do bɛgye hɛn efi bɔn mu, na ɔno nye asɔfodzi ho nhyehyɛɛ no. Ɔyɛɛ dɛm nhyehyɛɛ yi maa tsetse Israelfo no na ekyir yi ɔyɛɛ bi so maa tsetse Christianfo no. Na ndɛ na ndɛ nyina dɛm nhyehyɛɛ yi da ho ara yɛ edwuma. Adzesua yi mu no, yɛbɔhwɛ mbrɛ mbarimba na mbaa a wɔtsenaa ase tsetse no nyaa asɔfodzi ho nhyehyɛɛ no do mfaso. Yɛbɛsan so ahwɛ mfaso a ɔwɔ asɔfodzi ho nhyehyɛɛ no do ndɛ nye mbrɛ obesi aboa hɛn daakye. Sɛ yewie a, yɛbɔhwɛ mbrɛ asɔfodzi ho nhyehyɛɛ no si ma yehu dɛ Jehovah dɔ hɛn paa.

MBRƐ ASƆFODZI HO NHYEHYƐƐ NO BOAA TSETSE ISRAELFO NO
<<<P>>>
3 Ɔsomafo Paul kyerɛkyerɛɛ siantsir a Jehovah hyehyɛɛ asɔfo maa Israelman no mu. (Kenkan Hebrewfo 5:1.) Nna Jehovah pɛ dɛ wɔtwe pin no. Ɔno ntsi na oyii sɔfopanyin no dɛ ongyina nyimpa ananmu nnyɛ “Nyankopɔn n’edwuma.” Nna ɔsɔfopa-
<<<P>>>
1. Ebɛnadze ntsi na adasamba hia mboa paa?
<<<P>>>
2. Ebɛnadze na yebosusu ho wɔ adzesua yi mu?
<<<P>>>
3. Dɛ mbrɛ Hebrewfo 5:1 kyerɛ no, ebɛn kwan do na asɔfo no boaa hɔn a nna wɔpɛ dɛ wɔsom Nyankopɔn no? (Hwɛ mfonyin no so.)
