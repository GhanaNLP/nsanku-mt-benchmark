9 Siesie woho ma ɔsoɛrtsia. Dɛm aber yi paa na ohia dɛ yesi hɛn bo dɛ yɛbɔkɔ do ara aka asɛm-pa no ama daakye sɛ wɔsoɛr tsia hɛn mpo a, yeenngyaa ka. Aman ahorow pii do no, aban no mmbraa hɛn som edwuma no, ntsi yegu do ara reka Ahendzi no ho asɛmpa no wɔ hɔ. Naaso, osiandɛ asɛm a yɛka kyerɛ nkorɔfo no bi nye dɛ ɔrennkyɛr koraa Nyankopɔn bɛsɛɛ dɛm wiadze bɔn yi ntsi, yehia akokodur dze aka. Asɛm a yɛ-reka no seseiara yi, sɛ aka kakraabi ma Amage-don ko no ahyɛ ase a, yebegyaa ka. (Matt. 24:14) Afei so ahohiahia kɛse no mu no, asɛmpa a yɛbɛ-ka no, bi a n’ano bɛyɛ hyew na ɔbɛhyɛ nkorɔfo ebufuw paa. Na mbrɛ nkorɔfo besi ayɛ hɔn adze wɔ asɛm no ho no bɛkyerɛ dɛ wɔtaa Nyankopɔn n’Ahendzi no ekyir anaa wɔtaa nyimpa tumdzi ekyir. Jehovah n’atamfo mpo dze, wɔremmpɛ asɛm no etsie koraa, osiandɛ ɔbɛyɛ tse dɛ ‘ɔhaw kɛse bi’ ama hɔn.* (Nyi. 16:21) Hɔn bo bofuw Jehovah nye n’asomfo paa tse dɛ mbrɛ Pharaoh no bo fuuw Israelfo no wɔ Po Kɔkɔɔ no ho no. Dɛm ntsi ɔmmfa ho dza nyimpa dze bɛyɛ hɛn biara no, ɔsɛ dɛ yesi hɛn bo dɛ yɛbɔkɔ do ara aka asɛmpa no. Sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm a, nna yerisiesie hɛnho ama ɔsoɛrtsia a wɔdze bɛba hɛn do no.

SUASUA ƆHEN JEHOSHAPHAT NA TSIE JEHOVAH
<<<P>>>
10 Bible no ka nyimpa binom a wotsiee Jeho-vah ho asɛm na yebotum esuasua hɔn. Hɔn mu kor nye Judahhen Jehoshaphat. Mber bi, aman ebiasa bi kaa hɔnho bɔɔ mu dɛ wɔnye Judahfo no robɔko. Sɛ ɔwo nye Jehoshaphat a, nkyɛ w’a-koma runntu a? Ntsi sɛ Bible no ka dɛ Jehosha-phat nye ne mamfo no suroi a, nna ntseasee wɔ mu. (2 Mber. 20:1-3) Naaso ɔhen no amma hɔn ho hu anntɔ no do. Mbom no, ɔmaa Jehovah kyerɛɛ no dza ɔnyɛ. Yɛnhwɛ kwan a Jehosha-phat faa do dze noho too Jehovah do nye dza yebotum esua efi mu.
