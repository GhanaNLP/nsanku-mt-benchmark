MBRƐ ASƆFODZI HO NHYEHYƐƐ NO SI BOA HƐN MA YƐBƐN JEHOVAH
<<<P>>>
11 Asɔfodzi ho nhyehyɛɛ no ma yehu dɛ Jehovah pɛ dɛ hɛn mu biara bɛn no na yɛfa no nyɛnko. Ɔwɔ mu dɛ bɔn ama ɔka abɛda yɛnye Jehovah ntamu dze, naaso Jehovah n’akoma do adze nye dɛ obesiw dɛm ɔka no afebɔɔ. Jehovah pɛ dɛ yetum bɛn no, yɛbɔ no mpaa na yɛyɛ dza ɔsɔ n’enyi mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a yebotum atsena ase daa. (2 Cor. 5:18) Jehovah nyim dɛ yennyim bɔn na sintɔ a wɔdze awo hɛn no ho hwee; akɛyɛ tse dɛ mbrɛ ketsew ekedzi muoko ma mfifir rifir atwɛr. Hɛn asɛm yɛ no mbɔbɔr, ɔno ntsi na ɔpɛ dɛ ɔboa hɛn mu biara ma yesiesie yɛnye no ntamu na yɛsan bɛyɛ n’anyɛnkofo no. Sɛ edwendwen dza ɔayɛ ama wo no ho a, ɔbɛma adɔ no paa.
<<<P>>>
11. Asɔfodzi ho nhyehyɛɛ no si dɛn ma yehu dɛ Jehovah pɛ dɛ hɛn mu biara bɛn no na yɛfa no nyɛnko?
<<<P>>>
12 Jesus so pɛ dɛ yɛbɛn Jehovah na yɛnye no ntamu yɛ kama. Bible no ka dɛ, sɛ yɛfom a “yɛwɔ ɔboafo bi wɔ Egya no nkyɛn” a ɔserɛ bɔnfakyɛ ma hɛn. Ɔno nye hɛn Sɔfopanyin Jesus Christ. (1 John 2:1; kenkan Hebrewfo 7:25.) Ebɛn ndzɛmba koraa na Jesus hwɛ ansaana ɔaserɛ ama wo? Bi a ɔbɔhwɛ beebi a ifi, mbrɛ wosii tsetsee wo, yarba bi a ɔretseetsee wo anaa dza afa mu wɔ asetsena mu. Bi so a ɔbɔhwɛ wo suban pa, ndzɛmba papa a ereyɛ nye mbrɛ osi ma edze nokwardzi rosom Jehovah no. Osiandɛ Jesus hu dza ɔwɔ nyimpa n’akoma mu ntsi, mpɛn dodow a eserɛ Jehovah hɔ bɔnfakyɛ nye mbɔdzen a erobɔ dɛ ebɛyɛ dza ɔtsen ne nyina, ohu. Jehovah ne yamu ye na ofi n’akoma mu dze bɔn kyɛ, ntsi yebotum enya awerɛhyɛmu dɛ sɛ Christ serɛ ma hɛn a, obetsie na ɔdze hɛn bɔn akyɛ hɛn. (Ndw. 86:5) Ebɛnadze
<<<P>>>
12. Ebɛnadze na hɛn Sɔfopanyin Jesus hwɛ ansaana ɔaserɛ ama hɛn, na iyi si dɛn kyekye hɛn werɛ? (Hebrewfo 7:25) (Hwɛ mfonyin no so.)
<<<P>>>
Sɛ hɛn Sɔfopanyin Jesus reserɛ ama hɛn a, ɔhwɛ ndzɛmba papa a yɛyɛ, siantsir a yɛyɛ dɛm, serɛ a yɛkɔ do serɛ Jehovah dɛ ɔmfa hɛn mfom nkyɛ hɛn nye mbɔdzen a yɛrobɔ dɛ yɛbɛyɛ dza ɔtsen no (Hwɛ nkyekyɛmu 12)
