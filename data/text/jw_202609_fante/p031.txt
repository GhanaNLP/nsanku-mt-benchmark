17 Ebɛnadze na yeesusu ho wɔ adzesua yi mu? Yeebohu dɛ onnyi dɛ yɛma wiadze no no sunsum no kyerɛ hɛn kwan. Dza ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yeetum ayɛ iyi no, ɔwɔ dɛ yɛma hɛn anomu kasa yɛ dɛw, yɛtwe hɛnho fi ɔbrafun ho, na yɛyɛ ahwɛyie aber a yɛdze intanɛt ridzi dwuma no. Ɔwɔ mu dɛ wiadze no no sunsum no ano yɛ dzen dze, naaso onnyi dɛ yɛma iyi bu hɛn abaw mu. Ibotum ako etsia no na edzi konyim. Osiandɛ sɛ yɛdze tum a Satan nye no wiadze bɔn yi wɔ no toto Jehovah nedze ho a, sɛ wɔyɛ nkyen mpo a nkyɛ wɔayew mu. (Kenkan 1 John 4:4.) Afei so, Jehovah bɛma wo no sunsum krɔnkrɔn no amma dɛm wiadze bɔn yi no sunsum no ennya wo do tum. (Luke 11:13) Hom mma hɛn nyina nsi hɛn bo dɛ yɛbɔkɔ do ara ama Nyankopɔn no sunsum akyerɛ hɛn kwan na yɛremma wiadze no no sunsum no nnya hɛn da!—Gal. 5:16.
