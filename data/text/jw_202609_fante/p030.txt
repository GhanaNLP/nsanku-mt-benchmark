14 Mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a yerunnsuasua hɔn a wɔnnsom Jehovah no, ɔwɔ dɛ yɛtsew mber dodow a yɛsɛɛ no wɔ intanɛt do no do. Afei so, onnyi dɛ yɛhwɛ biribiara a ɔmmfata Christiannyi. Awofo so a wɔma hɔn mba kwan ma wɔkɔ sohyial media do no, onnyi dɛ wogyaa hɔn dɛmara mbom ɔsɛ dɛ wɔdze hɔn enyi dzi hɔn ekyir. (Eph. 6:4) Nhwehwɛmu ama ada edzi dɛ, mberantsɛ na nkataasia a wɔsɛɛ mber pii wɔ sohyial media do no, ɔyɛ a hɔn enyi nngye, dadwen taa hyɛ hɔn do na ɔnnyɛ dzen koraa dɛ wɔbɛyɛ ankonam. Sɛ edze Bible mu afotusɛm yɛ edwuma a, ibotum atwe woho efi esian ahorow a ɔwɔ intanɛt do no ho.
