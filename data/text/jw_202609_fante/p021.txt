Bɔn dze ɔka abɔto adasamba nye Jehovah ntamu, naaso asɔfodzi ho nhyehyɛɛ no ayɛ tse dɛ bregye a ɔboa hɛn ma yeetum kɔ Jehovah ne nkyɛn
(Hwɛ nkyekyɛmu 3)
<<<P>>>
nyin no nye asɔfo nkaa no akɛyɛ tse dɛ bregye a hɔn a nna wɔpɛ dɛ wɔsom Nyankopɔn no bɛfa do akɔ ne nkyɛn.
<<<P>>>
4 Asɔfo no faa akwan etsitsir ebien do boaa Israelfo no ma wɔbɛɛn Jehovah na wɔsoom no wɔ kwan a ɔpɛ do. Odzi kan, asɔfo no gyinaa Nyankopɔn ananmu kyerɛkyerɛɛ amamfo no. Wɔyɛɛ no dɛn? Malachi 2:7 ka dɛ: ‘Ɔsɔfo ano sie nyimdzee, na otwar dɛ wɔhwehwɛ mbra no wɔ n’ano: Na ɔno nye dɔm Jehovah no bɔfoe anaa no somafo.’ Ntsi nna ɔyɛ asɔfo no hɔn asodzi dɛ wɔkyerɛkyerɛ Moses Mbra no mu kyerɛ Israelfo no, na wɔboa hɔn ma wɔtse ase na wɔdze bɔ hɔn bra. Sɛ yɛbɛyɛ mfatoho a, wɔ Nehemiah n’aber do no, Levifo 13 boaa ɔsɔfo Ezra ma ɔkyerɛkyerɛɛ Mbra no mu kyerɛɛ ne mamfo no. Wɔkyerɛkyerɛɛ mu kofee na wɔboaa hɔn ma wɔtsee dza wɔkenkanee no ase. Dza wɔyɛe yi boaa Israelfo no ma wotumii dze Nyankopɔn n’asɛm no bɔɔ hɔn bra na ɔmaa wɔtwee piin no kɛse.—Neh. 8:2, 3, 6-8.
<<<P>>>
5 Otsia ebien, nna asɔfo no boa amamfo no ma wɔnye Nyankopɔn ntamu yɛ papa. Nna wosi dɛn yɛ iyi? Nna wogyina amamfo no ananmu dze mbowabɔ afɔr dze pata ma hɔn. Nna Israelfo no nyim paa dɛ da biara da mfom mpa hɔnho, dɛm ntsi nna wobehia dɛ Jehovah dze hɔn mfom kyɛ hɔn. (Ndw. 51:9) Osian iyi ntsi na Jehovah maa hɔn asɔfo a nna wɔdze mbowabɔbɔ afɔr dze apata ama hɔn no. Israelfo a nna wɔpɛ dɛ wofi hɔn akoma mu som Jehovah no, sɛ nna wohu dɛ Jehovah dze hɔn bɔn akyɛ hɔn a, nna hɔn akoma tɔ hɔn yamu paa! Asɔfodzi ho nhyehyɛɛ no ma yehu dɛ Jehovah dɔ n’asomfo na ɔwɔ ayamuhyehye ma hɔn. Afei so, ɔdwen hɔn ho paa na dza ɔkyɛn ne nyina no, ɔpɛ dɛ wɔtsena ase afebɔɔ.
