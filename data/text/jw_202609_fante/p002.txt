DZA YEBOSUA
Yɛbɔhwɛ siantsir a ɔsɛ dɛ yenya awerɛhyɛmu dɛ Jehovah bɔbɔ hɛn ho ban ndɛ na daakye.
<<<P>>>
Nya Awerɛhyɛmu Dɛ Jehovah Bɔbɔ Wo Ho Ban
<<<P>>>
‘Ekaa dɛ, Jehovah nye mo guandɔbea.’—NDW. 91:9.
<<<P>>>
YƐTSE nda a odzi ekyir no n’ewiei paa mu, na aka kakraabi ma dɛm wiadze yi aba n’ewiei. Ɔrennkyɛr nsɛm akɛseakɛse bi besi-si ma ndzɛmba asesa koraa wɔ wiadze yi mu. Jehovah dze “ne tsirmsɛm” bɛhyɛ amanaman no hɔn akoma mu dɛ “wɔmfa hɔn ahenman mma abowa no” a ogyina hɔ ma Amanaman Nkabɔ-mu Kuw no.* (Nyi. 17:17) Afei, abowa no a wɔbɛma no tum kɛse yi nye aban ahorow a wɔwɔ wiadze nyina a wɔtaa n’ekyir no bɛka hɔn ho abɔ mu, na wɔatow ahyɛ ator som a wɔwɔ wiadze nyina no do na wɔasɛɛ hɔn pasaa. (Nyi. 17:16) Dɛm aber no na ahohiahia kɛse no bɛhyɛ ase. Ɔno ekyir no, ebɛn asɛm na obesi?
<<<P>>>
2 Nsɛm akɛseakɛse afofor so bedzidzi do aba. Amanaman a wɔwɔ wiadze yi mu no bɛka hɔnho abɔ mu na wɔatow ahyɛ Je-hovah n’asomfo do. (Ezek. 38:14-16) Ebɛnadze ntsi na wɔbɛyɛ dɛm? Nokwar Som—Jehovah Ama Do Bio! buukuu no kyerɛkye-rɛɛ mu dɛ: “Satan bɔkɔ do ara epiapia aman no, na wɔdze besi hɔn enyi do dɛ wɔbɔtɔr Jehovah n’asomfo ase, na wɔapepa no-kwar som efi asaase do koraa.” Ɔno ekyir no, Amagedon ko no bɛhyɛ ase, na ɔno na ɔdze dɛm wiadze bɔn yi bɛba n’ewiei ko-rakora. Jesus bedzi nyimpa 144,000 a ɔnye hɔn bɔbɔ mu edzi adze no nye abɔfo no enyim ma wɔasɛɛ nyimpa abɔnfo, na wɔ-bɛgye hɛn akɔ Nyankopɔn no wiadze fofor no mu.—Nyi. 16:14-16; 19:11-21.
<<<P>>>
3 Ehwɛ a, ɔsɛɛ a Jesus nye no sor dɔm no dze reba dɛm wiadze bɔn yi do no, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛma ɔbɔ hɛn hu anaa? Oho. Ebɛnadze ntsi a? Osiandɛ Jehovah ahyɛ bɔ dɛ ɔbɔbɔ hɛn ho ban. Ɔaka wɔ n’Asɛm Bible no mu dɛ: “Apem bɔtotɔ wɔ wo nkyɛnmu, na ɔpe-du atotɔ wɔ w’enyimfa; naaso orunndu wo nkyɛn.” (Ndw. 91:7)
