<<<P>>>
Mbrɛ Yebesi Efi Hɛn Akoma Mu Etsie Jehovah
<<<P>>>
“Hom efi hom akoma mu ayɛ setsie.”—ROM. 6:17.
<<<P>>>
NYIMPA piinara wɔ hɔ a, sɛ wohu dɛ obi a ɔwɔ tum kyɛn hɔn ro-hwɛ hɔn a, nna wɔdze mbra yɛ edwuma. Binom so dze, dza ɔma wodzi mbra do nye dɛ, wosuro dɛ sɛ wɔammfa annyɛ edwuma a wɔbɔtwe hɔn aso. Binom so wɔ hɔ a, wɔdze mbra yɛ edwuma mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a nkorɔfo bɛkamfo hɔn araa ntsi. Hɔn a wɔtse dɛm no, wɔyɛ hɔn adze tse dɛ nyamesom mpanyimfo a nna wɔwɔ Jesus n’aber do no. Nna hɔnara wɔse wɔtsie Nyankopɔn na wodzi ne mbra do, naaso nna ɔnnyɛ dɛm na asɛm no tse koraa. Nna wɔpɛ dɛ nkorɔfo nya adwen dɛ gyama wɔtsen araa ma. (Matt. 23:5) Naaso hɛn dze, yetsie Jehovah osiandɛ yenyim dɛ sɛ yɛdze dza ɔdze kyerɛkyerɛ hɛn no bɔ hɛn bra a, obesi hɛn yie. (Isa. 48:17, 18) Sɛ dɛm adwen yi na yɛwɔ ma ɔno ntsi yetsie Jehovah dze a, ɔno dze oye. Naaso adze tsitsir bi so wɔ hɔ a ɔno ntsi yetsie Jehovah.
<<<P>>>
² Adze tsitsir a ɔma yetsie Jehovah nye dɛ yɛdɔ no paa. (Matt. 22:37, 38) Ɔno ntsi na ‘yefi hɛn akoma mu tsie no’ no. (Rom. 6:17) Ɔdɔ a yɛwɔ ma Jehovah no kenyan hɛn ma yefi hɛn akoma mu tsie dza ɔka kyerɛ hɛn dɛ yɛnyɛ nyina. Ɔnnyɛ aber a nkorɔfo ro-hwɛ hɛn anaa aber a yebenya dza ɔse yɛnyɛ no do mfaso nkotsee ntsi na yetsie no. Mbom ewia mu o, nsu mu o, yetsie no. Yɛgye to mu dɛ hɛn akoma botum adaadaa hɛn, ntsi ɔtɔ da a, tsie a yebe-tsie Jehovah no tum yɛ ɔko. Adzesua yi mu no, yɛbɔhwɛ Bible mu nsɛm ebien bi a obotum aboa hɛn ma yeefi hɛn akoma mu etsie Jehovah. Odzi kan, yɛbɔhwɛ asɛm a osii aber a Jehovah gyee Is-raelfo no fii Egyptfo no nsamu. Yewie a, yɛbɔhwɛ dza Judah-hen Jehoshaphat so yɛe nye dza yebotum esua efi mu.
