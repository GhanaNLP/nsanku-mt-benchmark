15 Ndɛ, Jehovah Adasefo pii nsa botum aka Wiadze Fofor Nkyerɛase Bible no bi wɔ kasa a wobotum akenkan na wɔatse ase mu. Osiandɛ wɔakyerɛ dɛm Bible yi ase pɛpɛɛpɛr ntsi, ɔboa hɛn ma yehu nokwasɛm a ɔfa Jehovah ho, yehu hia a ohia dɛ yɛdze hɛnho to no do na yeyi ne dzin no ayɛw. Osian dza yeebohu afa Jehovah ho ntsi, yɛdɔ no paa na yɛdze hɛnho to no do.—Mbɛ. 18:10; Isa. 56:6.

16 Bible no ma yehu dɛ ɔrennkyɛr koraa ‘ahemfo a wɔwɔ asaase do nyina’ nye Jehovah dze besi enyi, na ɔbɛyɛ butubutu. (Nyi. 16:14) Dɛm aber no, hɔn a wonyim Jehovah, wotsie no na wɔdze ne dzin no frɛ no no, ɔbɔbɔ hɔn ho ban na ɔremma hɔn ho beebiara mpo nntsi. (Ndw. 9:10; kenkan John 17:3; hwɛ “their coming to know you” no ho nkyerɛkyerɛmu wɔ nwtsty-E no mu.) Naaso hɔn a wɔnngye Nyankopɔn nndzi na wonntsie no no, Jehovah bɛkyerɛ hɔn dɛ ɔno nye Adze Nyina Do Tumfo. Jehovah se: “Mubohuhor moho, na mayɛ mo ho krɔnkrɔn, na mama woehu me wɔ aman pii enyim, na wobohu dɛ emi nye [Jehovah].” (Ezek. 38:23) Dɛm aber no, Jehovah n’asomfo enyi bɛgye paa, osiandɛ wonyim dɛ hɔn Nyɛnko Jehovah bɛgye hɔn.—Mal. 3:16.

17 Ɔyɛ hokwan kɛse paa na yeenya dɛ yɛroboa ma nkɔnhyɛ a ɔwɔ Zechariah 8:23 no aba mu! Ɔnnyɛ hɛn nkotsee na yɛreyɛ dɛm edwuma yi. Mbom yeenya hokwan so nye hɛn nuanom nye abɔfo no abɔ mu reyɛ dɛm edwuma yi na yɛreka asɛmpa no wɔ kasa ahorow nyina mu wɔ wiadze yi mu beebiara. Jehovah ama hɛn akadze biara a yebehia ama yeetum aka asɛmpa no akyerɛ nkorɔfo a wofi aman nye mbusuakuw nye kasa nyina mu. Hom mma yɛnkɔ do mfa dɛm ndzɛmba yi ndzi dwuma yie mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a yɛbɔboa nyimpa dodow no ara ma woehu dɛ hɛn Nyankopɔn Jehovah dɔ obiara, ɔwɔ ehumbɔbɔr na ɔwɔ abotar so. Kesi dɛ Jehovah bɛtsew ne dzin ho no, hom mma yɛnkɔ do ara nka asɛmpa no nkyerɛ nyimpa nyina.—Luke 11:2.
