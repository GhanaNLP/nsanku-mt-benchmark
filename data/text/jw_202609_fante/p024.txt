13 Asɔfodzi ho nhyehyɛɛ no ma hɛn awerɛhyɛmu dɛ Jehovah pɛ dɛ yefi hɛn akoma mu nye no dzi nkitaho. (Kenkan Hebrewfo 4:16.) Osian dwu-ma a hɛn Sɔfopanyin Jesus dzi ntsi, yebotum efi hɛn akoma mu abɔ Jehovah mpaa aber biara a yɛpɛ. Sɛ Jehovah hu dɛ yeefi hɛn akoma mu nye no rekasa a, ɔma n’enyi gye. Sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm a, yɛda no edzi dɛ yɛgye no dzi na yɛdze hɛnho ato no do. Ɔtɔ da a ahohia a yɛrefa mu ntsi, awerɛhow tum hyɛ hɛn do araa ma ebɔhwɛ no na yennhu mbrɛ yennsi nnkyerɛ hɛn asɛm mu nkyerɛ Jeho-vah. Ɔtɔfabi a, yennhu dza yemmbisa mpo mmfi no hɔ. (Rom. 8:26) Naaso iyi nyina ekyir no, Je-hovah tse hɛn ase, otsie hɛn na ɔyɛ hɛn ebisadze no ma hɛn. Hɛn sor Egya no dɔ hɛn paa na ɔwɔ hɔ ma hɛn, ntsi yebotum akɔ n’enyim aber bia-ra na yeefi hɛn akoma mu aka dza ɔhaw hɛn bia-ra ho asɛm akyerɛ no.

14 Asɔfodzi ho nhyehyɛɛ no ma yehu dɛ Jeho-vah pɛ dɛ yɛtsena ase daa. (Ezek. 33:11) Mbo-wa a Nyankopɔn n’asomfo dze bɔɔ afɔr wɔ tsetse mber mu no, hɔn bɔgyaa no enntum ammpepa hɔn bɔn korakora. (Heb. 7:19) Naa-so dɛm afɔr ahorow no boaa hɔn ma wohun dɛ, nna wobehia afɔrbɔ bi a obotum apepa hɔn bɔn korakora. Nna dɛm afɔrbɔ yi nye Jesus Christ no nkwa a odzi mu a ɔdze bɔbɔ afɔr no. Jesus n’afɔrbɔ no ntsi, Jehovah botum dze hɛn bɔn akyɛ hɛn korakora. Afei so, osian Jesus n’afɔr-bɔ no ntsi sɛ yetsie Jehovah a, yebenya onnyi-ewiei nkwa. Ɔyɛ hɛn dɛw paa dɛ Jehovah yɛɛ asɔfodzi ho nhyehyɛɛ yi ama yeetum atsena ase afebɔɔ!—1 John 4:10.

15 Hɔn a Jehovah eyi hɔn dɛ wɔnyɛ asɔfo no ma yehu dɛ Jehovah dɔ hɛn. Ɔsɛ dɛ hɔn a wɔasera hɔn no dzi nokwar kesi owu mu tse dɛ mbrɛ hɔn Wura Jesus yɛe no. (Phil. 2:8; Nyi. 2:10) Afei so, ɔsɛ dɛ wɔbɔ hɔn bra ma ɔda edzi dɛ wɔdɔ Jehovah na wodzi no nokwar. Osiandɛ Jesus nye 144,000 no nyina dzi nokwar ntsi, yebotum enya awerɛhyɛmu dɛ wɔ Mfrenhyia Apem Tum-dzi no mu no, wobefi hɔn akoma mu aboa hɛn ma yeenya werdam afɔrbɔ no do mfaso paa, na wɔdze no nkakrankakra beyi bɔn na sintɔ efi hɛnho. Ntsi Christ ne Mfrenhyia Apem Tumdzi no mbra n’ewiei no, nna bɔn na sintɔ nnyi hɔ bio. Afei, Jesus nye 144,000 no bɔhwɛ ma asaa-se ne nyina asan adan Paradise bio na wɔbɔhwɛ ma Jehovah no bɔhyɛ nyina aba mu. Ɔda edzi pefee dɛ, hɔn a Jehovah eyi hɔn dɛ wɔnyɛ asɔfo no yɛ anokwafo na wɔdwen nyimpa ho, na iyi ma yehu dɛ Jehovah dɔ hɛn paa.—Nyi. 22:1, 2.

16 Hɛn Sɔfopanyin Christ hu dza yɛrefa mu nyina na ɔtse hɛn ase, osiandɛ aber a noara so bɛtsenaa asaase yi do no, ɔfaa bi mu. (Kenkan Hebrewfo 4:15.) Yembisa o: Ɔtɔ da a eberɛ araa ma eyɛ woho hwee a ɔnnyɛ yie anaa? Je-sus so atse nka dɛm pɛn. (John 4:6) Ɔkɔm adze wo araa ma w’enyi do ekyin wo pɛn anaa nsu-kɔm adze wo araa ma wo men mu nyina ayɛ we-see da? Ɔno so Jesus nyim mbrɛ osi tse. (Matt. 4:2; John 19:28) Osiandɛ eyɛ Jehovah Dasefo ntsi nkorɔfo soɛr tsia wo anaa wodzi wo ho fɛw? Jesus so dɛmara na wɔdze yɛɛ no. (Isa. 50:6; John 15:20) Obi a ebɛn no paa ewu da anaa? Jesus so ne nyɛnko Lazarus wui na ɔbɛ-yɛ dɛ ne papa Joseph so wui gyaa no hɔ. Dza yɛrefa mu biara no, yebotum enya awerɛhyɛmu dɛ hɛn Sɔfopanyin Jesus tse hɛn ase na onyim dza yehia, osiandɛ noara so ɔafa biribi a ɔtse dɛm mu pɛn.
