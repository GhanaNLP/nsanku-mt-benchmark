<<<P>>>
na edze ayɛ edwuma. Aber a Jehoshaphat n’akoma tui no, ‘ɔdze sii n’enyi do dɛ ɔbɔhwehwɛ Jehovah.’ (2 Mber. 20:3) Nna Jehoshaphat taa hwehwɛ Jehovah n’akwankyerɛ wɔ biribiara a ɔyɛ mu. (2 Mber. 19:3) Naaso aber a aman no yɛɛ dɛ wɔnye Judah robɔko no, ohun dɛ dɛm aber yi paa na ɔsɛ dɛ ɔhwehwɛ Jehovah n’ekyir kwan na ɔdze akwankyerɛ biara a ɔdze bɛma no no yɛ edwuma. Dɛm ntsi Jehoshaphat fii n’akoma mu bɔɔ Jehovah mpaa. (Kenkan 2 Mbermunsɛm 20:5, 6, 10-12.) Sɛ yɛrefa ɔhaw bi mu a, ebɛnadze na yebotum ayɛ dze esuasua Jehoshaphat? Ɔsɛ dɛ yesi hɛn bo dɛ yebetsie Jehovah. Afei so, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛbɔ no mpaa na yesua adze dze hwehwɛ n’akwankyerɛ, na yenya no mu awerɛhyɛmu dɛ ɔbɔboa hɛn.—Ndw. 119:112.

12 Kɔ do ara sua Bible no. Mpaa a Jehoshaphat bɔe no ma yehu dɛ nna onyim Nyame n’Asɛm no mu yie paa. Nna onyim dɛ asaase a Israelfo no tse do no yɛ asaase a Nyankopɔn dze hyɛɛ hɔn nana Abraham bɔ dɛ ɔdze bɛma n’asefo. (Gen. 12:7) Afei so, nsɛm a Solomon kae wɔ ne mpaabɔ mu aber a nna worihyira asɔrfie no do no ara bi na Jehoshaphat so kae wɔ ne mpaabɔ no mu. (2 Mber. 6:28-30; 20:5-9) Dza nna Jehoshaphat nyim fa Jehovah ho no boaa no ma ɔdze noho too no do na ɔdze sii n’enyi do dɛ obetsie no. Ntsi hɛn so, dza yenyim fa Jehovah ho no, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛkɔ do sua bi ka ho. (Matt. 13:52) Sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm a, ɔmmfa ho ɔhaw biara a yebehyia no, yɛbɔkɔ do ara etsie Jehovah.

13 Brɛ woho adze. Jehoshaphat ne mpaabɔ no so ma yehu dɛ nna ɔbrɛ noho adze. Nna ɔyɛ ɔhen na nna ɔwɔ asogyafo beberee. Ntsi nkyɛ obotum dze noho ato noho do dɛ nankasa nye n’atamfo no besi enyi na onnhia Jehovah no mboa. Naaso ɔannyɛ dɛm, mbom no ɔbrɛɛ noho adze na ɔbɔɔ mpaa wɔ amamfo no nyina enyim serɛɛ Jehovah dɛ ɔmboa hɔn osiandɛ wonnhu dza wɔnyɛ. (2 Mber. 20:12) Osiandɛ nna Jehoshaphat brɛ noho adze ntsi, aber a Jehovah kyerɛɛ no dza ɔnyɛ no, ɔyɛɛ no ntsɛmara. Hɛnara so, sɛ yɛbrɛ hɛnho adze a, yebefi hɛn akoma mu etsie Jehovah wɔ tsebea biara mu.—Phil. 2:5, 8.

14 Sɛ itsie Jehovah a, afofor so bosuasua wo. Jehovah tsiee Jehoshaphat ne mpaabɔ no na ɔnam no nkɔnhyɛnyi Jahaziel do kaa kyerɛɛ hɔn dɛ: ‘Hɔm nhuruhuruw ngyina, hom ngyina dzen, na hom nhwɛ Jehovah a ɔka hom ho no, no nkwagye.’ Bible no ka dɛ, hɔ ara na Jehoshaphat “bɔɔ ne tsir adze dze n’enyim kaa famu.” Ɔyɛɛ dɛm no ɔmamfo no nyina so ‘hwehwee famu sɔɔr Jehovah.’ Adze kyee no, aber a Jehoshaphat nye ne mamfo no fiir edzi dɛ worikehyia hɔn atamfo no, ɔkaa kyerɛɛ ne mamfo no dɛ ‘wɔngye Jehovah na no nkɔnhyɛfo no ndzi.’—2 Mber. 20:14-20.
