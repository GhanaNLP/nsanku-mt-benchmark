<<<P>>>
Yɛreka Asɛmpa No Wɔ ‘Amanaman Kasa Ahorow Nyina Mu’
<<<P>>>
“Mbarimba du a wofi amanaman kasaa nyina mu bosusuo Jewnyi baako n’atar ano.”—ZECH. 8:23.
<<<P>>>
ETUATSEWFO a nna wɔrotow Babel Abantsentsen no, Jehovah totoo hɔn kasaa, na ɔsɛɛ edwuma a nna wɔreyɛ no. Osian iyi ntsi nyimpa abɔ apetse asaase yi do beebiara na ɔsor kasa a obiara ka. (Gen. 11:6-9) Naaso Jehovah aka dɛ ɔbɛka “nyimpadɔm kɛse bi a . . . wofifi aman na mbusuakuw na nkorɔfo na kasa nyina mu” no abɔ mu, na ɔdze hɔn etwa ahohiahia kɛse no akɔ wiadze fofor no mu. (Nyi. 7:9, 10, 14) Osiandɛ yɛbɔkɔ do ara dze dɛm nyimpadɔm yi hɔn kurow mu kasa aka asɛmpa no akyerɛ hɔn wɔ ahohiahia kɛse no mu ntsi, wobotum enya Jehovah no mu gyedzi dɛ ɔbɛgye hɔn nkwa. (Kenkan Romefo 10:13, 14.) Ntsi ɔyɛ hokwan kɛse paa na yeenya dze reyɛ dɛm nkwagye edwuma yi bi!
<<<P>>>
2 Jehovah maa nkɔnhyɛnyi Zechariah hyɛɛ nkɔm dɛ, ‘amanaman pii dze hɔnho bɔbɔ Jehovah ho, na wɔayɛ ne man.’ Zechariah hyɛɛ dɛm nkɔm yi n’ekyir koraa ansaana ɔsomafo John rekyerɛw Nyikyerɛ nwoma no. (Zech. 2:11) Zechariah sanee so hyɛɛ nkɔm dɛ: “Mbrɛ dɔm [Jehovah] se nye yi: Nda no mu na ɔbɛba dɛ mbarimba du a wofi amanaman kasaa nyina mu bosusuo Jewnyi baako n’atar ano, dɛ, Yɛnye hom bɔkɔ, osiandɛ yɛatse dɛ Nyankopɔn nye hom wɔ hɔ.” (Zech. 8:23) Dɛm nkɔnhyɛ yi reba mu ndɛ. Ma yɛnhwɛ dza hɛn mu biara botum ayɛ ma dɛm nkɔnhyɛ yi aba mu.
