8 Aber a Satan sɔɔ Jesus hwɛe no, ɔfaa asɛm a ɔwɔ Ndwom 91:11, 12 no dze foaa n’asɛm no do. Ɔwodze ma yɛnhwɛ mbrɛ Jesus sii yii no ano. (Kenkan Matthew 4:5-7.) Satan kaa kyerɛɛ Jesus dɛ, sɛ ɔtow no ho fi asɔrfie no n’etsifi a, Jehovah ahyɛ bɔ dɛ ɔbɔsoma n’abɔfoe ma wɔabɔbɔ no ho ban na ɔnnkɛma oepira. Ebɛn asɛm na Jesus kaa kyerɛɛ Satan? Jesus annka dɛ: ‘Satan, ɔnnyɛ dɛm na asɛm no tse! Ɔnnyɛ dza dɛm kyerɛwsɛm yi kyerɛ nye dɛ Jehovah bɔbɔ mo ho ban amma memmpira anaa biribiara annyɛ me.’ Mbom Jesus kaa kyerɛɛ no dɛ: “Mma nnsɔ Jehovah wo Nyankopɔn nnhwɛ.” Ebɛnadze na yebotum esua efi nyiano a Jesus dze maa Satan yi mu?

9 Nna Jesus nyim paa dɛ ɔtɔ da a n’Egya Jehovah soma abɔfo ma wɔkɔbɔ n’asomfo ho ban amma hɔn atamfo emmpira hɔn anaa wɔannye hɔn bɔn. (2 Ahe. 6:15-17; 19:34, 35; Dan. 3:19, 20, 26-28; 6:16-22) Naaso nna ɔsan so nyim dɛ, sɛ ɔdze no nkwa tow kyakya a, Jehovah ronnsoma n’abɔfo dɛ wɔmbɔbɔ no ho ban. Ntsi asɛm a Satan fae kae a ɔkyerɛ dɛ Jehovah bɔ n’asomfo ho ban amma hɔn atamfo enndzi hɔn bɔn no no, ɔyɛ nokwar. Naaso ka a ɔkaa dɛ Jesus mfa no nkwa ndzi agor mfa nsɔ Jehovah nhwɛ no dze, ɔannka no yie koraa.* Sɛ yɛhwɛ mbrɛ Jesus sii yii Satan ano no a, yebotum aka dɛ asɛm a ɔwɔ Ndwom 91 no ma yehu dɛ, mbrɛ ɔtse mbrɛ ɔtse biara no, Jehovah bɔbɔ n’asomfo ho ban. Ɔno nye dɛ, ɔdze biribiara a yehia amma yɛnye no ntamua nnsɛɛ no bɛma hɛn,
<<<P>>>
* Mber bi a abɛsen kɔ no, yɛkaa no wɔ hɛn nwoma ahorow no mu dɛ, asɛm a ɔwɔ Ndwom 91 no fa bɔ a Jehovah ahyɛ dɛ ɔbɔbɔ hɛn ho ban amma biribiara annsɛɛ yɛnye no ntamuno nkotsee ho. Afei so, nna ɔyɛ hɛn dɛ asɛm a Satan gyinaa Ndwom 91 do kae dɛ Jehovah bɔbɔ n’asomfo ho ban amma woemmpira hɔn anaa asɛm bɔn biara aannto hɔn no nnyɛ nokwar. Ibotum ahwɛ December 1, 1974 Ɔweɔn-Aban no ne krataafa 722.

10 Bɔ a Jehovah dze ahyɛ hɛn wɔ Ndwom 91 no boa hɛn ma yenya Jehovah no mu awerɛhyɛmu paa. Yɛwɔ awerɛhyɛmu dɛ obiara a ɔdze noho bɔto Jehovah do na ɔdze no bɛyɛ no guandɔbea nye n’akoban no, ɔno so bɔbɔ no ho ban. Ntsi sɛ Satan no wiadze bɔn yi reba n’ewiei a, ɔnnsɛ dɛ yɛma nsɛm akɛseakɛse a obesisi no bɔ hɛn hu. Afei, ma yɛnhwɛ mbrɛ Jehovah si bɔ hɛn ho ban ndɛ nye mbrɛ obesi abɔ hɛn ho ban daakye so.

11 Jehovah bɔ yɛnye no ntamua anyɛnkofa no ho ban. Iyi kyerɛ dɛ, ɔboa hɛn ma yehu mbrɛ yebesi atwe hɛnho efi biribiara a obotum asɛɛ yɛnye no ntamuno ho. Onyim mbrɛ bɔn na sintɔ a wɔdze awo hɛn no retseetsee hɛn no nyina, na onyim dɛ ɔtɔ da a ɔyɛ dzen ma hɛn dɛ yɛbɔtwe hɛnho efi dza ɔmmpɛ ho na yɛayɛ dza ɔpɛ. (Rom. 6:16; 7:21-23) Ntsi ɔama hɛn ndzɛmba ahorow bi a obotum aboa hɛn ma yeetsie no amma yɛnye no ntamua nnsɛɛ. Ma yensusu no mu bi ho.
<<<P>>>
12 Jehovah ama hɛn n’Asɛm Bible no. Jehovah n’Asɛm Bible no bɔ hɛn kɔkɔ wɔ akɔndɔ bɔn ho, na ɔsan so ma yehu mbrɛ yebesi ahyɛ akɔndɔ bɔn do. Ɔwodze ma yɛmfa asɛm a Jesus kae wɔ Matthew 5:28, 29 no nyɛ mfatohɔ. (Kenkan.) Jesus kaa dɛ, “Obiara a ɔbɔkɔ do ahwɛ ɔbaa na oenya noho akɔndɔ no nye no asɛɛ awar dada wɔ n’akoma mu.” Ebɛnadze na yebotum esua efi dɛm asɛm yi mu?
