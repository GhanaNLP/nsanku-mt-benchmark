Hom mma yensusu asɛm a ɔwɔ Ndwom 91 no ho na yenyiyi dɛm nsɛmbisa yi ano: Ebɛn bambɔ na dɛm ndwom yi reka ho asɛm? Ndɛ, ebɛnadze na Jehovah yɛ dze bɔ hɛn ho ban? Ebɛnadze na ɔbɛyɛ dze abɔ hɛn ho ban daakye? Naaso, hom mma yendzi kan mbobɔ nsɛm a ɔdwontofo no kyerɛwee wɔ Ndwom 91 no do.
<<<P>>>
NDWOM BI A ƆMA YENYA AWERƐHYƐMU DƐ JEHOVAH BƆBƆ HƐN HO BAN
<<<P>>>
4 Asɛm a ɔwɔ Ndwom 91 no, ne tsentsen na ne tsiatsia ara nye dɛ: Ɔsɛ dɛ yenya awerɛhyɛmu dɛ Nyankopɔn bɔbɔ hɛn ho ban. Sɛ ekenkan nyiyimu kor na ebien no a, ibohu dɛ hɔn a wɔdze hɔnho to Jehovah do nyina, ɔno so bɔ hɔn ho ban. (Kenkan Ndwom 91:1, 2.) Ɔwodze fa no dɛ akɛhyɛ Adze Nyina Do Tumfo nye Ɔsorsornyi no ne ntsaban ase ma ɔrobɔ wo ho ban. Ebɛn bambɔ na ɔkyɛn iyi? (Ruth 2:12; Ndw. 17:8) Nkasafua “guandɔbea” na “akoban” so ma yɛkaa mbrɛ nna tsetse no wɔtow fasu dze twa kurow bi ho dze bɔ kurow no na no mu atsefo ho ban. Hɔn mpo a nna wɔtsetse nkuraase a ɔbembɛn kurow no, sɛ atamfo pɛ dɛ wɔtow hyɛ hɔn do a, nna wotum guan kɔ kurow no mu. Nyiyimu ahorow a odzidzi hɔ no, mbrɛ ɔdwontofo no yɛɛ ntotoho ahorow bi no boa hɛn ma yetum dze hɛn adwen bu mbrɛ Jehovah tum bɔ n’asomfo ho ban fi tsebea ahorow a ɔyɛ hu mu.—Ndw. 91:3-13.
<<<P>>>
5 Ndwom 91 n’ewieisɛm no ka akoma paa na ɔma yenya awerɛhyɛmu a osi pi. Hɔ no, nna ɔnnyɛ ɔdwontofo no na ɔreka mbrɛ Jehovah bɔbɔ hɛn ho ban ho asɛm, mbom nna Jehovah nankasa na ɔrehyɛ n’asomfo bɔ dɛ ɔbɔbɔ hɔn ho ban. (Kenkan Ndwom 91:14-16.) Ɔwodze hyɛ nsɛmfua bi a wɔdze dzii dwuma no nsew:
<<<P>>>
4. Ebɛn awerɛhyɛmu na asɛm a ɔwɔ Ndwom 91:1, 2 no ma yenya?
<<<P>>>
5-6. Ebɛn bɔ na Jehovah dze ahyɛ n’asomfo wɔ Ndwom 91, na ebɛn awerɛhyɛmu na iyi ma yenya? (Ndwom 91: 14-16)
<<<P>>>
Jehovah kaa dɛ “mebɛgye no,” “medze no besi yie,” “mebɛgye no do,” “menye no bɛtsena,” “mibeyi no,” na “medze nkwa tsentsen bɛmee no.” Dɛm nsɛm yi ma yehu dɛ, Jehovah ahyɛ n’asomfo bɔ dɛ osiandɛ wɔdze hɔnho to no do, wonyim ne dzin na wosu frɛ no ntsi, ɔbɔbɔ hɔn ho ban. Yebotum dze hɛn werɛ ahyɛ dɛm bɔ a Jehovah ahyɛ yi mu. (Josh. 23:14) Sɛ Jehovah ka dɛ ɔbɔbɔ n’asomfo ho ban a, ɔnnyɛ ano kɛkɛ na ɔdze reka, mbom ɔbɛyɛ. Osiandɛ ɔno dze ɔse a, ɔyɛ!—Nka. 23:19.
<<<P>>>
6 Jehovah maa ɔdwontofo no kyerɛɛw asɛm a ɔwɔ Ndwom 91 no mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yeenya no mu awerɛhyɛmu dɛ obotum abɔ hɛn ho ban. (Rom. 15:4) Ntsi hɛn so, yebotum aka asɛm a odzi hɔ a ɔdwontofo no kae yi bi akyerɛ Jehovah dɛ: “Ɔwo nye mo guandɔbea na m’akoban.” (Ndw. 91:2) Sɛ dɛm dze a, nna ɔno ebɛn bambɔ na yebotum ahwɛ kwan dɛ Jehovah dze bɛma hɛn?
<<<P>>>
EBƐN BAMBƆ NA NDWOM 91 SE JEHOVAH DZE BƐMA HƐN?
<<<P>>>
7 Mber bi a abɛsen kɔ no, hɛn nwoma ahorow bi kyerɛkyerɛɛ mu dɛ asɛm a ɔwɔ Ndwom 91 no yɛ bɔ a Jehovah ahyɛ dɛ ɔbɔbɔ hɛn ho ban amma yɛnye no ntamu ebusuabɔ no annsɛɛ. Na nokwasɛm so nye dɛ, yɛgye dzi paa dɛ Jehovah bɛma hɛn nsa akɔ do aka biribiara a yehia ama yeetum akɔ do ara nye no ntamu ayɛ kama. Naaso, dza dɛm ndwom yi kyerɛ ara nye dɛ ndzɛmba a ɔbɛsɛɛ yɛnye Jehovah ntamu no nkotsee na Jehovah bɔbɔ hɛn ho ban fi ho anaa? Oho. Dɛm ndwom yi san so ma yehu dɛ, Jehovah n’asomfo a wɔbɔ mu som no dɛ kuw no, ɔahyɛ bɔ dɛ ɔremma n’atamfo kwan ma wɔnntɔr hɔn ase da. Mbom ɔbɔbɔ hɔn ho ban amma woeemmpira hɔn anaa woeenndzi hɔn bɔn na ɔbɛma hɔn asomu so adwe hɔn. Ebɛnadze ntsi na yɛreka dɛm?
