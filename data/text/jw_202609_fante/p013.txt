15 Nna Jehoshaphat gye dzi paa dɛ Jehovah bedzi no bɔhyɛɛ do na ɔbɔko ama no nkorɔfo. Afei so, ɔhyɛɛ ne mamfo nkuran dɛ wɔmfa hɔn werɛ nhyɛ Jehovah mu, na dza Jehovah aka biara no, wontsie. ‘Aber a ɔnye ne mamfo no faa apam wiei no,’ oyii mbarimba bi dɛ wondzi dɔm no enyim ntow ndwom nyi Jehovah ayɛw. (2 Mber. 20:21) Iyi ma yehu dɛ nna wɔgye Jehovah dzi paa na wɔdze hɔnho too no do. Jehovah so hyiraa hɔn na ɔmaa hɔn atamfo no twaa hɔnho nye hɔnho koe na wokunkum hɔnho.—2 Mber. 20:22-24.

16 Asafo mu mpanyimfo, awofo nye hɔn a asodzi hyehyɛ hɔn nsa no botum esua biribi efi Jehoshaphat n’asɛm no mu. Osiandɛ ofii n’akoma mu tsiee Jehovah ntsi, ɔboaa ne mamfo no so ma wɔyɛɛ dɛmara. Wɔka dɛ, ɔkaekyir suasua odzikamfo. Ntsi dza Ɔhen Jehoshaphat yɛe no, yebotum aka dɛ osuasua Ɔhen Solomon. Osiandɛ aber a nna Solomon som Jehovah no, ɔserɛɛ Jehovah dɛ ɔmma no ntseasee akoma mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a obotum edzi ne mamfo no do yie. Nna ɔpɛ dɛ ɔdze Jehovah ne nyansaa bu ne mamfo no atsɛn. (Kenkan 1 Ahemfo 3:9.) Jehoshaphat so brɛɛ noho adze na otsiee Nyankopɔn no nkɔnhyɛnyi no. Afei, ɔnye ne mamfo no susuu nsɛm ho ansaana orisi gyinae bi a ohia paa, na ɔhyɛɛ hɔn nkuran dɛ wontsie dza Jehovah reka no na wɔmfa nyɛ edwuma. Asafo mu mpanyimfo suasua Jehoshaphat. Ansaana wobesi gyinae bi afa asafo no ho no, wodzi kan hwehwɛ Nyankopɔn n’Asɛm no nye asafo nwoma ahorow mu, na wɔnye mpanyimfo afofor so susu ho. (Mbɛ. 15:22) Afei so, sɛ ahyɛhyɛdze no dze akwankyerɛ fofor bi ba a, wofi hɔn akoma mu dze yɛ edwuma ntsɛmara na wɔnnkasakasa ho. Sɛ wɔyɛ dɛm a, ɔhyɛ enuanom afofor nkuran ma hɔn so yɛ dɛmara. Awofo so, ɔsɛ dɛ wɔkaa dɛ dza wɔyɛ biara, dɛ ɔyɛ papa o, dɛ ɔyɛ bɔn o, hɔn mba bosuasua. Ntsi sɛ wofi hɔn akoma mu tsie Jehovah a, hɔn mba no so befi hɔn akoma mu ayɛ dɛmara.—Mbɛ. 1:8; 14:1.

17 Ɔmmfa ho ɔhaw biara a yebehyia no, sɛ yefi hɛn akoma mu tsie Jehovah a, yebotum enya awerɛhyɛmu dɛ ɔbɔhwɛ hɛn na ɔbɔboa hɛn so ma yeehu dza ɔsɛ dɛ yɛyɛ. Dɛ mbrɛ ɔkyerɛɛ n’asomfo kwan wɔ tsetse mber no mu no, ndɛ so ɔfa Akwankyerɛ Kuw no nye asafo mu mpanyimfo do dze akwankyerɛ ma n’asomfo. Sɛ wɔma hɛn akwankyerɛ bi na sɛ yɛnntse ase papa anaa yennhu mbrɛ obesi aboa hɛn dɛm aber no mpo a, onnyi dɛ yɛma ɔyɛ hɛn dadwen. Mbom, ɔsɛ dɛ yetsie na yɛdze yɛ edwuma osiandɛ Jehovah nyim nyansa kyɛn hɛn koraa na otum adze nyina yɛ. (Isa. 55:9) Dɛm ntsi hom mma yɛnkɔ do mfi hɛn akoma mu ntsie no.
