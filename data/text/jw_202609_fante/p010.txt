SUA BIRIBI FI DZA ƆTOO ISRAELFO NO MU
<<<P>>>
6 Israelfo no hɔn asɛm no ma yehu dɛ ɔsɛ dɛ yesusu siantsir a yetsie Jehovah nye hɔn a oeyi hɔn dɛ wondzi n’asomfo hɔn enyim no ho yie. Ntsi sɛ wɔdze akwankyerɛ bi ma hɛn a, yebotum ebisa hɛnho dɛ, “Dɛm akwankyerɛ a wɔdze ama hɛn yi, osiandɛ yɛtse ase na yenyim dɛ yebenya do mfaso araa ntsi na yɛdze yɛ edwuma anaa?” Sɛ yɛhwɛ Israelfo no hɔn asɛm no a, bi a nna hɔn mu binom mmpɛ dɛ hɔn mbakan bowuwu araa ntsi na wɔdze oguan no bɔgyaa seraseraa hɔn apondua ho no. Yebotum so ebisa hɛnho dɛ, “Mbrɛ nkorɔfo si yɛ hɔn adze no na yɛhwɛ ho dze kyerɛ dɛ yebetsie Jehovah anaa yerinntsie no?” Dɛm anafua no a nna Israelfo no rifi Egypt no, Jehovah maa wonyaa “enyimpa wɔ Egyptfo no enyim,” ntsi annyɛ dzen amma hɔn dɛ wobetsie Jehovah. (Ex. 12:36) Naaso ekyir yi, aber a nna Egyptfo no ritsiw hɔn no, wɔammfa hɔnho annto Jehovah do na wɔtseew no do etua. (Ex. 14:10-12) Onnyi dɛ yesuasua Israelfo no, na mbom no ɔsɛ dɛ yɛdze hɛn werɛ nyina hyɛ Jeho-vah mu na yefi hɛn akoma nyina mu tsie hɔn a oeyi hɔn dɛ wondzi hɛn enyim wɔ no som mu no. (Kenkan Jeremiah 23:4.) Sɛ wɔma hɛn akwankyerɛ bi na yɛnntse ase papa anaa sɛ yɛdze yɛ edwuma na ɔbɛhyɛ hɔn a wɔnnsom Jehovah no ebufuw mpo a, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛkɔ do ara dze yɛ edwuma. Yebesi dɛn efi hɛn akoma mu etsie Jehovah na yeanndzi mfom kor no ara a Israelfo no dzii no bi?

7 Sɛ yɛdɔ Jehovah paa a, yebetsie no. Sɛ Jehovah n’ahyehyɛdze no dze akwankyerɛ bi ma hɛn na sɛ yennhu mfaso a yebotum enya efi mu anaadɛ sɛ yɛdze dɛm akwankyerɛ no yɛ edwuma na hɛnho bɛkyer hɛn a, obotum ayɛ dzen ama hɛn dɛ yebetsie dɛm akwankyerɛ no. Sɛ ɔba no dɛm a, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛkaa dɛ siantsir a yetsie Jehovah araa nye dɛ yɛdɔ no na yɛgye no dzi. (1 John 5:3) Na osiandɛ yɛdɔ Jehovah ntsi, yɛdze akwankyerɛ no bɛyɛ edwuma na yebesie abotar atweɔn kesi dɛ yɛbɛtse ndzɛmba bi ase yie. Na ewiei koraa no, yebohu dɛ mfaso wɔ do dɛ yɛdze dɛm akwankyerɛ no yɛɛ edwuma.—Ɔsɛnk. 7:8; Mic. 7:7.

8 Ndɛ Jehovah n’asomfo yɛ adze paa. Sɛ Jehovah ka dɛ wɔnyɛ biribi na wonnhu mfaso a ɔwɔ do dɛm aber no mpo a, wotsie. Sɛ yɛbɛyɛ mfatoho a, aber a COVID-19 yii ne tsir no, Akwankyerɛ Kuw no kaa kyerɛɛ asafo ahorow no dɛ wɔmfa fon anaa video kɔnferɛns do nyɛ adzesua.
