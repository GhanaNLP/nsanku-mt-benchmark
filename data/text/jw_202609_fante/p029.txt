12 Hɛn mu piinara dze intanɛt dzi dwuma wɔ hɛn abrabɔ mu paa, ntsi sɛ yennyi intanɛt a yenntum nntsena. Yɛdze yɛ ndzɛmba ahorow bi mpo wɔ hɛn som mu. Naaso ndzɛmba a ɔwɔ intanɛt do no mu dodow no ara ma yehu nsusui nye abrabɔ a hɔn a wɔdze gu hɔ no enyi gye ho. Ndɛ ponografi nnyɛ na wɔ intanɛt do, na mpanyin nye mbofra nyina tum nya bi hwɛ. Nkorɔfo taa dze ndwammansɛm ho mfonyin nye video nye nsɛm a okenyan nda ho akɔndɔ taa sɛnd hɔnho, nkanka mberantsɛ na nkataasia. Afei so, nsɛm a ɔwɔ intanɛt do no mu pii yɛ atorsɛm na bi so wɔ hɔ a wogugu nsɛm no mu na wɔka ma ɔyɛ huhuuhu. Ekɔ sohyial media do so a, ibohu dɛ beebi a nkorɔfo kɛgye hɔn enyiwa, kwan bi a wotui anaa biribi a hɔn enyi gye ho na wɔtaa dze gu hɔ. Sɛ yɛhwɛ mfonyin nye video ntsiantsia nye nsɛm a wɔkyerɛw gu sohyial media do no a, obotum ama yeenya adwen dɛ, sɛ yɛdze hɛn mber pii gye hɛn enyi na yɛdze tutu akwan kɛgye hɛn ahom, na yeennya adagyer mpo annsom Jehovah a, asɛm biara nnyi ho.—Heb. 10:24, 25.
