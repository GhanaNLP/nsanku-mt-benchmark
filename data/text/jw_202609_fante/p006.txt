Naaso obotum ahyɛ hɛn dzen ma yɛayɛ dza ɔtsen na yeetum ahyɛ hɛnho do amma yee-nndzi hɛn akɔndɔ ekyir annkɛyɛ biribi a Jeho-vah mmpɛ. Na sɛ yɛyɛ dza ɔtsen so a, yɛbɔkɔ do ara nye Jehovah ntamu ayɛ kama.

15 Jehovah ama hɛn n’ahyehyɛdze no. Jeho-vah nam n’ahyehyɛdze no so do ma hɛn nsa ka nwoma nye video ahorow wɔ ber a ɔfata mu, na iyi boa hɛn ma yɛkɔ do ara nye no ntamu yɛ papa. Osiandɛ bɔn na sintɔ wɔ hɛnho ntsi, ɔtɔ da a bɔn bi a yɛayɛ etwa mu ho asɛm tum tsee-tsee hɛn. Ɔtɔfabi so a, yetum tse nka dɛ obiara mmpɛ hɛn asɛm anaa dadwen tum hyɛ hɛn do ma yɛyɛ basaa. Sɛ ɔba no dɛm a, Satan a ɔyɛ “Ɔsɔhwɛfo no” gyina dɛm ndzɛmba yi do na ɔyɛ ara dɛ ɔbɛma yeenya adwen dɛ yɛmmpɛ hɛn beebi ntsena na Jehovah no som yɛ dzen do-dow. (Matt. 4:3; 6:13) Nyankopɔn n’ahyehyɛ-dze no ama hɛn video nye nwoma ahorow pii a ɔboa hɛn ma yenya awerɛhyɛmu dɛ Jehovah dze bɔn kyɛ na yɛsom bo ma no. Afei so, ɔma yehu dɛ bɔ a Jehovah ahyɛ dɛ ɔbɛma hɛn nsa aka dza yehia no so, obedzi do. (Ndw. 86:5; Matt. 10:29-31; Luke 12:28; Heb. 13:5) Yɛreka-sa yi, bi a akaa mber bi a ekenkaan asafo nwo-ma bi anaa ehwɛɛ video bi ma ɔkaa w’akoma araa ma ekaa dɛ, ‘Iyi dze, wɔyɛɛ maa me.’ Kaa dɛ, dɛm ndzɛmba yi nyina Jehovah dze ma hɛn wɔ ber a ɔfata mu mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a yɛnye no ntamu rennsɛɛ.

16 Jehovah bɔ hɛn nyina ho ban na ɔronnhwɛ ma hɛn atamfo nntɔr hɛn ase. Ɔyɛ nokwar dɛ ɔtɔ da a Jehovah ma kwan ma wɔtan n’asomfo binom hɔn enyi na woku hɔn mu bi mpo, mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a ɔbɛma ada edzi dɛ Satan yɛ ɔtorfo. (Nyi. 2:10; 6:9) Naaso hɛn nuanom kakraabi a
<<<P>>>
Satan ama no nkorɔfo ekunkum hɔn no ne nyi-na ekyir no, no bo nndwee. No bo efuw paa, na ne yamu a nkyɛ hɛn asoɛrtsiafo no atɔr hɛn nyina ase. (Matt. 24:9; Nyi. 12:9-12) Naaso, Jehovah remma kwan ma onnsi da! Yebotum enya awerɛhyɛmu paa dɛ Jehovah ma n’abɔfo bɔ hɛn ho ban mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a hɛn atamfo re-mma hɛn ase nnhyew ama yeetum akɔ do ara asom no na yɛaka no ho asɛmpa akyerɛ nkorɔ-fo.—Heb. 1:7, 14.

17 Ɔnnyɛ ɔhaw biara a Jehovah no somfo bia-ra hyia no na ɔbɔ no ho ban fi ho. Sɛ yɛdze n’a-fotusɛm bɔ hɛn bra a, ɔma yɛtwe hɛnho fi ndzɛmba ahorow pii a obotum epira hɛn no ho, na ɔboa hɛn ma yetum gyina ɔhaw aho-row bi a yehyia no ano. (Mbɛ. 14:29, 30; 22:3; 2 Cor. 7:1) Naaso dɛm aber yi dze, yɛnnhwɛ kwan dɛ Jehovah bɔbɔ hɛn mu biara ho ban efi biribiara a obotum epira hɛn no ho. Sɛ ɔyɛ dɛm a, atorsɛm a Satan keka dɛ osiandɛ yɛsom Jehovah ntsi na ɔbɔ hɛn ho ban na ohyira hɛn no, ɔbɔkɔ do atserɛw mu. (Job 1:10, 11) Ye-nyim dɛ, osiandɛ Jehovah pɛ dɛ ɔtsew ne dzin krɔnkrɔn no ho na ɔma obiara hu dɛ Satan yɛ ɔtorfo ntsi na oenya “abotar kɛse” na ɔama kwan ma wɔtan n’asomfo hɔn enyi na wɔyɛ hɔn adze gyangyan no. (Rom. 9:22) Naaso ɔrenn-kyɛr koraa, Jehovah bɛkeka noho na abɛbrɛsɛ beberebe a n’asomfo rosɔw no nyina no tun botwa. Kesi dɛ Jehovah beyi n’atamfo nyina efi hɔ no, ɔbɔkɔ do ara aboa hɛn ma yeetum egyina ɔhaw biara a yehyia no ano amma yɛnye ne ntamu annsɛɛ.—1 Cor. 10:13; 1 Pet. 5:10.
