<<<P>>>
Hwɛ Yie Na “Wiadze No No
Sunsum No” Ennya Wo
<<<P>>>
“Dza ɔwɔ wiadze nyina . . . ommfi Egya no.”—1 JOHN 2:16.
<<<P>>>
WIADZE a yɛtse mu yi, Satan na odzi do. (John 14:30; 1 John
5:19) Na oetu n’enyi esi hɔn a wodzi Christ n’ekyir no ho paa.
(Nyi. 12:12, 13, 17) Iyi nnkyerɛ dɛ Satan wɔ ha a nna ɔwɔ bee-
bi fofor so. Bible no nnka no wɔ beebiara dɛ abɔfo anaa esun-
sum fi wɔ dɛm tum no. Naaso mbrɛ ɔtse nye dɛ, Satan wɔ esun-
sum fi pii a ɔnye hɔn yɛ edwuma. (Nyi. 12:9) Adze fofor a ɔma
yɛtse Satan ne nka wɔ beebiara nye wiadze no no sunsum a
ɔnoara ankasa hyɛ ekyir no, osiandɛ dɛm sunsum yi wɔ bee-
biara. Nyimpa piinara na wɔma wiadze no no sunsum no kye-
rɛ hɔn kwan, na binom so wɔ hɔ a, wonnyim mpo dɛ ɔno na
ɔkyerɛ hɔn kwan. (Kenkan 1 John 2:15, 16.) Sɛ nkorɔfo a wɔ-
nngye nndzi mpo dɛ Satan wɔ hɔ no ma wiadze no no sunsum
kyerɛ hɔn kwan a, ɔma n’enyi gye. Kwan bi do no, akɛyɛ tse dɛ
Satan “akyer hɔn dze hɔn reyɛ dza ɔpɛ.”—2 Tim. 2:26.
<<<P>>>
2 Mber bi a abɛsen kɔ no, nna hɛn mu piinara ma wiadze no
no sunsum kyerɛ hɛn kwan, kyerɛ dɛ, nna yɛyɛ hɛn adze tse
dɛ hɔn a wɔnnsom Jehovah no. Naaso, aber a yebohun mbrɛ
wiadze no no sunsum no ma nkorɔfo yɛ hɔn adze nye obi a
ɔhyɛ ekyir no, ebɛnadze na yɛyɛe? Yɛdze dza Bible no ka no
yɛɛ edwuma na yɛhyɛɛ ase dɛ ‘yɛrotwe hɛnho efi bɔn na wi-
adze akɔndɔ ho.’ (Titus 2:11, 12) Satan wɔ tum kyɛn hɛn na
ɔatwe nkorɔfo piinara ekedzi n’ekyir. Naaso iyi nyina ekyir
no, sɛ yetum dze dza Bible no kyerɛkyerɛ no bɔ hɛn bra a,
nna ɔkyerɛ dɛ yɛpɛ dɛ yɛma Jehovah boa hɛn. (Jas. 4:7, 8)
<<<P>>>
1. Ebɛn kwan do na nkorɔfo suasua Satan ndɛ? (1 John 2:15, 16)
<<<P>>>

no no sunsum” no kyerɛ hɔn kwan no ntamu? (b) Ebɛn esian na ɔse dɛ yɛhwɛ
hɛnho yie wɔ ho?
