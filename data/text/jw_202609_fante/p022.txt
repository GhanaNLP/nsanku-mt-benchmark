7 Ber a odzi kan a Bible no dze kasafua ɔsɔfo dzii dwuma no, nna ɔreka Melchizedek ho asɛm. (Gen. 14:18) Nna ɔyɛ ɔsɔfo san so yɛ ɔhen. Ekyir yi, Jehovah hyɛɛ bɔ dɛ Messiah no so bɛyɛ ɔhen na ɔsɔfo tse dɛ Melchizedek. Naaso Messiah no tumdzi no dze, nna adasamba nyina benya do mfaso afebɔɔ.—Ndw. 110:4; Heb. 7:17.

8 Dɛ mbrɛ yeebohu no, Jehovah yɛɛ Israel ne man, na ɔhyehyɛɛ asɔfo maa hɔn na ɔnam Moses do dze Mbra maa hɔn. Ɔkaa edwuma a nna asɔfo no bɛyɛ ho asɛm wɔ Mbra no mu. Jehovah yii Moses no nuabanyin Aaron a nna ɔyɛ Levinyi no dɛ ɔnyɛ Sɔfopanyin na ɔyɛɛ ne mba no asɔfo, na ɔkaa dɛ Aaron n’asefo nkotsee na wobotum asom dɛ asɔfo. (Ex. 40:12-15) Jehovah dze hyɛɛ asɔfo no hɔn nsa dɛ wɔmbɔ afɔr mma amamfo no na nna Levifo nkaa no boa hɔn ma wɔbɔ afɔr no. Nna Sɔfopanyin no gyina ɔman mu no nyina ananmu bɔ afɔr, na nna asɔfo no so bɔ afɔr ma Israelnyi biara a ɔpɛ dɛ ɔbɔ afɔr no. Jehovah dze akwankyerɛ pɔtsee maa hɔn wɔ afɔr ahorow a nna ɔsɛ dɛ wɔbɔ, siantsir a nna ɔsɛ dɛ wɔbɔ afɔr no nye kwan a ɔsɛ dɛ

9 Aber a wonumaa Jesus no, ɔbɛyɛɛ Ɔsɔfopanyin wɔ Jehovah no sunsum afamu asɔrfie kɛse no mu. (Heb. 8:1) Ekyir yi, Jesus dze no nkwa a odzi mu no bɔɔ afɔr maa adasamba. Nna Jesus no bɔgyaa no som bo koraa kyɛn mbowahɔnbɔgyaaa nna asɔfo no dze bɔ afɔr ma Israelmba no. Nna mbowano hɔnbɔgyaa no runntum nnyi bɔn mmfi hɔ korakora, naaso Jesus no bɔgyaa no dze, nna obotum apepa hɛn bɔn nyina korakora. Ndɛ na ndɛ nyina, Jesus da ho ara yɛ hɛn Sɔfopanyin na ɔakɛyɛ tse dɛ bregye a yɛfa no do kɔ Nyankopɔn ne nkyɛn. (Heb. 9:24) Daakye wɔbɛyɛ nyimpa 144,000 a wɔasera hɔn no asɔfo nye ahemfo, na wɔnye hɛn Sɔfopanyin Jesus bɔbɔ mu edzi adze wɔ Nyankopɔn n’Ahendzi no mu. (1 Pet. 1:3, 4; 2:9) Sɛ wonyan nyimpa 144,000 no nyina kɔ sor a, wɔnye Nyankopɔn na Christ bɔbɔ mu ayɛ edwuma, na wobeyi bɔn efi adasamba a wɔtsie Jehovah no ho. Afei so, wɔbɔboa adasamba ma wɔadan asaase yi paradise. Yɛdze enyigye rohwɛ kwan dɛ wobowie dɛm edwuma yi, osiandɛ sɛ wowie a, asetsena bɛyɛ dɛw paa!

Afɔr ahorow a nna asɔfo no bɔ wɔ Israel no boaa Israelfo no ma wohun dɛ, nna wohia afɔrbɔ bi a ɔkyɛn dɛm afɔr ahorow no nyina. Ɔno nye werdam afɔrbɔ no.
