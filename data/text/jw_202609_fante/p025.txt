17 Asɔfo 144,000 a wɔbɔboa Jesus ma oeyi bɔn efi adasamba ho no bɛtse hɛn ase na wɔbɛma yeehu dɛ wɔdwen hɛn ho. Osiandɛ hɔnara so nna wɔyɛ mbanyin na mbaa a bɔn na sintɔ wɔ hɔnho tse dɛ hɛnara. Ntsi sɛ yɛfom anaa bɔn bi a yɛayɛ etwa mu ho asɛm rehaw hɛn a, wɔtse hɛn ase. Afei so, sɛ yarba bi na ɔretseetsee hɛn o, sɛ dadwen bi na ahyɛ hɛn do o, anaa biribi fofor biara a ɔrehaw hɛn no, ne nyina wɔafa bi mu pɛn. Əsor beebi a hɔn mu biara fi, ebusua a wofi mu, hɔn amambra, ber a wɔtsenaa ase nye dza hɔn mu biara afa mu wɔ asetsena mu. Ntsi dza afa mu wɔ asetsena mu biara no, ɔnyɛ dɛn ara a nyimpa 144,000 no mu bi bɛtse wo ase.

18 Asɔfodzi ho nhyehyɛɛ a yeesusu ho no ama yeehu dɛ Jehovah wɔ ɔdɔ, ehumbɔbɔr, ayamuhyehye na ɔdwen hɛn ho. Satan dze dza ɔpɛ ara nye dɛ da biara da obenya hɛn ho asɛm bɔn aka, naaso Jehovah nntse dɛm koraa. (Nyi. 12:10) Adzesua yi mu no yeebohu dza Jehovah ayɛ mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a yebotum abɛn no, na yɛayɛ dza ɔsɔ n'enyi na yɛafa no nyɛnko. Iyi ma yehu paa dɛ ɔdɔ hɛn paa na ɔayɛ krado so dɛ sɛ yɛfom a ɔdze hɛn bɔn bɛkyɛ hɛn. Dɛm ntsi ɔsɛ dɛ yɛyɛ dza yebotum biara bɛn no na yɛfa no nyɛnko pampee.

19 Ɔtɔ da a, bɔn a ɔwɔ hɛnho ntsi yɛbɛtse nka dɛ mfaso biara nnyi hɛn do, naaso adzesua yi aboa ma yeebohu dɛ sɛ yɛtse nka dɛm mpo a, yebotum enya awerɛhyɛmu dɛ Jehovah dze, ɔtse hɛn ase. Ɔsan so nyim dɛ yɛyɛ dɛtse na mfom dze ɔmmpar hɛn ho. (Ndw. 103:14) Naaso iyi nyina ekyir no, Jehovah mmfa n'adwen nnsi mfom a yedzi no do, mbom no ɔkɔ do dze n'adwen si ndzɛmba papa a yɛyɛ nye mbɔdzen a yɛrobɔ dɛ yɛbɛyɛ dza ɔsɔ n'enyi no do. Ntsi sɛ hɛn adwen yɛ hɛn dɛ hɛn dze mfom nkotsee na yedzi na biribi papa biara nnyi hɛn mu a, ɔnodze ɔwɔ dɛ yɛkaa dɛ, Jehovah "so kyɛn hɛn akoma na onyim biribiara.”—1 John 3:20.

20 Dɛ mbrɛ yeebohu no, Jehovah n'akoma do adze nye dɛ yɛbɔsom no dɛ mbrɛ ɔpɛ, ne dɛm ntsi ɔama hɛn Sɔfopanyin nye asɔfo a wobotum aboa hɛn ma yɛasom no. Yɛda Jehovah ase paa, osiandɛ sɛ ɔnnyɛ asɔfodzi ho nhyehyɛɛ no a ayɛ tse dɛ bregye no a nkyɛ yerunntum mmbɛn no da. Dɛm ntsi hom mma hɛn nyina nyɛ dza Hebrewfo 4:16 hyɛ hɛn nkuran dɛ yɛnyɛ no. Hɔ se: "Hom mma yɛmfa ahotodo nkɔ adom ahengua no enyim, na yeenya ehumbɔbɔr na adom a ɔbɔboa hɛn wɔ ber a ɔfata mu."
