<<<P>>>
BIBLE MU ASƐM BI
<<<P>>>
“Hom Mma Christ N’asomdwee Ndzi Hɛn Wɔ Hom Akoma Mu”
<<<P>>>
Colossaefo 3:15 ka “Christ n’asomdwee” ho asɛm. Sɛ wɔka “Christ n’asomdwee” a, ɔkyerɛ dɛn? Dza ɔkyerɛ nye akomatɔyamu a obi botum enya aber a ɔabɛyɛ Christ no suanyi no. Dza ɔma dɛm nyimpa no n’akoma tɔ ne yamu nye dɛ, onyim dɛ Jehovah nye ne Ba Jesus dɔ no, na iyi ma n’asomu dwe no.—John 14:23, 27.
<<<P>>>
Ɔsomafo Paul hyɛɛ Christianfo a wɔwɔ Colossae asafo no mu no nkuran dɛ, wɔmma Christ n’asomdwee ndzi hɛn wɔ hɔn akoma mu, anaa onnya tum wɔ mbrɛ wosi tse ankasa no do. Nna wobesi dɛn ayɛ iyi? Greek kasafua a Paul dze dzii dwuma a wɔakyerɛ ase “dzi hɛn” no, nna wotum dze ka referee a oridzi agokansi bi ho asɛm; osian tum a ɔwɔ ntsi, gyinae biara a obesi no, noara nye no. Dɛmara na hɛn so, dza “Christ n’asomdwee” a yɛpɛ dɛ yenya no bɛkyerɛ hɛn dɛ yɛnyɛ no, ɔnoara na ɔsɛ dɛ yɛyɛ.
<<<P>>>
Sɛ menntse-moho-ase bi soɛr wɔ yɛnye hɛn nua bi ntamu a, ɔsɛ dɛ yɛma “Christ n’asomdwee” kyerɛ hɛn kwan. Sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm a, ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yeehu dza yebotum ayɛ ama asomdwee na koryɛ akɔ do atsena yɛnye hɛn nuanom ntamu. Na dza ɔbɛkyerɛ hɛn dɛ yɛnyɛ no mpo, sɛ yebotum ayɛ a, ɔtɔ da a obehia dɛ yɛdze dza yɛpɛ to nkyɛn anaa yɛdze hɛn nua no no mfom kyɛ no. Sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm a, nna yɛrema kwan ma “Christ n’asomdwee” edzi hɛn wɔ hɛn akoma mu.—Col. 3:12-14.
