22. Wɔ Akpɔsahɔlɛ nee Maanle nu Ɛnleɛnleanlɛ, Adwinlililɛ nee Maanle nwo Nolobɔlɛ ne anu, arane wiele Ngyehyɛlɛ ko ne mɔɔ maanle bɛvale menli 300 wɔ Akpɔsahɔlɛ Gyima ne anu la amaa bɛava akpɔsahɔlɛ gyima ne bɛagua kɔmputa zo na yeali munli kpalɛ wɔ BƐDWENLE Ɛ Nwo Ngyehyɛlɛ ne abo.
<<<P>>>
23. Wɔ Ɛvolɛ 2022 ne anu, Arane bayɛ ninyɛne ngakyile mɔɔ le kɛ Adwinlililɛ nwo Ninyɛneyɛlɛ Ɛleka, Lateleke anwonsesebɛ Ɛnyianlɛ Ɛleka, Mbulalɛ Avunli Gualilɛ Ɛleka, bɛayɛ ndenle nee ninyɛne ngakyile mɔɔ le kɛ lateleke kɛnlaneɛ, nzule nee adwelienyianlɛ ɛleka ɛleka wɔ Akpɔsahɔlɛ Ɛleka ne mɔ.

<<<P>>>
24. Wɔ Arane Milahyinli Gyimayɛlɛ Ngyehyɛlɛ 10 ne abo, Arane ɛva milahyinli gyimayɛlɛ ne ɛzie ɔ nye zo ɛsesebɛ amaa bɛayɛ ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛ nee bɛ vi Abolokyi la dɔɔnwo wɔ Maanle ye anu na eza bɛava milahyinli bɛayɛ egyinli nu ninyɛne ne mɔ. Ɛhye bamaa ninyɛne mɔɔ bɛfa bɛkɔ adɔne wɔ maanle zo la azo na eza menli anyia gyima ayɛ. Wɔ Ɛvolɛ 2021 ne anu Arane bɔle ɔ bo kɛ ɔyɛ nyileyɛlɛ ɛleka wɔ “Noguchi’ kɛ bɛfa bɛaboa Maanle ye anu nyileyɛlɛ Gyima ne. Wɔ Ɛvolɛ 2022 ne anu bɛbayɛ ninyɛne titili ɛhye mɔ:
<<<P>>>
• Gyima nu nzenzanleɛyɛlɛ mɔɔ bamaa bɛayɛ ninyɛne ndɛndɛ la (gyima ɛbukelɛ nwo ngɛlata mɔɔ bɛbava bɛagua kɔmputa zo wɔ Arane Gyimabukelɛ nwo Ngɛlatayɛlɛ Ɛleka ne la boka nwo;
<<<P>>>
• Bɛbabɔ ngyehyɛlɛ mɔɔ bamaa bɛabuke milahyinli gyima gyima wɔ maanzinli ne mɔ anu a. Bɛbava ɛleka ɛhye mɔ bɛaboka milahyinli gyima ɛyɛlɛ sukunsa ne mɔ anwo;
<<<P>>>
• Bɛbadɛlɛ adenle mɔɔ bɛdua zo bɛnyia nyile wɔ Maanle ye anu la anu na bɛ nee nyileyɛlɛ gyimayɛvoma ne mɔ abɔ nu ayɛ nyile dɔɔnwo.
