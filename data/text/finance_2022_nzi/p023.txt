Bodane: “Bɛ Ti Anwo Gyimabukelɛ Maanle Ɛkyekyelɛ Mɔɔ Di Munli: Ezukoa Nwo Banebɔlɛ Nee Gyima Enyianlɛ”
<<<P>>>
Ɛlobɔ nwo Gyimalilɛ mɔɔ yɛlɛkakyi yɛahɔ Maanle nu Esike zo Ɛdelɛ mɔɔ di Munli la
<<<P>>>
30. Kolonavaelɔso Ewulenzane ne anzi ezukoagyinlabelɛ ne ɛkyekyelɛ bodane ne maa yɛ adenle maa yɛkyekye Maanle ne Kpalɛ bieko ɔlua ninyɛne fofolɛyɛlɛ nee mbaka dɔɔnwo ɛlualɛ nwo zo. Ɔlua ɛhye ati Arane ɛyɛ ɛlobɔ nu ɛhakyilɛ nwo ngyehyɛlɛ mɔɔ bamaa yɛazia yɛahɔ mbaka dɔɔnwo ɛlualɛ mɔɔ bamaa yɛanyia anyunluhɔlɛ, ezukoa nee ezukoagyinlabelɛ mɔɔ di munli la. Ɛhye bamaa yɛanyia gyima gyima dɔɔnwo na yɛamaa pɛpɛyɛlɛ ayɛ gyima.
<<<P>>>
31. Ghana gua so ɛlɛfa mbaka ɛlualɛ ngyehyɛleɛ nee gyima gyima mɔɔ bamaa yɛanyia yɛ ɛhoayelɛ ne mɔ bieko la ali gyima. Ɔlua ɛhye ati, Arane ɛlɛyɛ ngyehyɛlɛ mɔɔ bamaa yɛanyia yɛ ɛhoayelɛ ne mɔɔ ɛhakyi ɛsɛlɛ la bieko. Ɛhye bamaa yɛanlea yɛabɔ yɛ ɛhoayelɛ ne anwo bane na yɛava yɛali gyima na eza yɛanlea yɛanwu kɛ yɛnyia yɛ nane ngakyile mɔɔ abo ɛlɛtu la bieko.
<<<P>>>
32. Amaa yɛazɔ ɛhoayelɛ ɛzɛkyelɛ nwo ngyegyelɛ ne anloa la, Maanle Kpanyinli ne lile Maanle ne anyunlu maanle bɛluoluale mbaka mbaka mɔɔ bo mgbe nsuu la wɔ Siane Eziane ne anu wɔ Ɛvolɛ 2021 ne anu. Ɛhye bole mbaka mgbe nnu ne mɔɔ Mbaka Ɛlualɛ Ngyehyɛlɛ ne vale ziele ɔ nye zo la azo.
