NINYƐNE MƆƆ SONVOLƐ GYIMA GYIMA NE MƆ BAYƐ LA
<<<P>>>
33. Kɛmɔ Maanle Ɛkyekyelɛ Ngyehyɛleɛ ne wɔ Mekɛ ekyi anu la ɛra awieleɛ (2018 mɔɔ kɔdwu 2021) la, bɛbabɔ Maanle Ɛkyekyelɛ Ngyehyɛleɛ ne wɔ Mekɛ Tendenle nu la abo wɔ Ɛvolɛ (2022 mɔɔ kɔdwu 2025 la abo). Ngyehyɛlɛ Fofolɛ ne gyi Maanle Ɛkyekyelɛ Ngyehyɛlɛ wɔ Mekɛ Tendenle nu, mɔɔ ye duma a le Ghana ɛli Ɛvolɛ 100 la azo. Bie mɔ a le Ezukoagyinlabelɛ nee Maanle Ɛkyekyelɛ Ngyehyɛlɛ ngakyile (2017 mɔɔ kɔdwu 2024), Ghana mɔɔ Ɛnva Ɔ nye Ɛndo Awie mɔ moalɛ zo la Bodane ne, Ghana Kolonavaelɔso anwo ngyegyelɛ ne Abodulɛ nee Gyima zo Ɛkpogyalɛ Ngyehyɛlɛ ne (Ghana DWENLE Ɛ NWO). Eza bɛ nye la kɛ bɛbalua ngyehyɛleɛ ɛhye mɔ azo bɛanyia Maamgbole Koyɛlɛ Eku ne Bodane mɔɔ wɔ Ɛvolɛ 2030 ne anu la nee Abibile Ngamɔnu Eku ne Bodane ne mɔɔ wɔ Ɛvolɛ 2063 ne anu.
<<<P>>>
34. Foa ɛhye ka ninyɛne titili mɔɔ Arane yɛle wɔ Ɛvolɛ 2021, ne anu la nee mɔɔ bɛbayɛ bɛ wɔ Ɛvolɛ 2022 ne anu la anwo edwɛkɛ.

Gyima Ɛnyianlɛ Nwo Agyakɛdulɛ
<<<P>>>
35. Amaa bɛade menli mɔɔ ɛnnyia gyima ɛnyɛ la dodo ne azo la Arane kɔ zolɛ yɛ ngyehyɛleɛ mɔɔ le kɛ Ghana Gyimayɛlɛ nwo Banebɔlɛ Ngyehyɛlɛ ne. Wɔ ngyehyɛlɛ ɛhye abo, Arane yɛle ninyɛne ɛhye mɔ:
<<<P>>>
i. ɔmaanle menli mɔɔ ehyia ɛva bɛ aze kpalɛ la 33,952 wɔ Arane gyima gyima 80 anu. Ɛnee bɛ ahatualɛ ne dodo le Ghana sidi mgbe 53.43;
<<<P>>>
ii. bɛhyɛle Ghana sidi mgbe 5.82 bɛmaanle awuke awuke 9,244 mɔɔ bɛwɔ azua nee namunamu mɔɔ bɛwɔ Azua mgbole, Manyese zo nee Maanzikyi Ayia ne mɔ anu wɔ Maanzinli 5 mɔɔ wɔ Ɛsɛlɛ nu la anu kɛ bɛva bɛbɔ bɛ gyimayɛlɛ bo anzɛɛ bɛva bɛdɛlɛ bɛ gyima ne mɔ anu.
<<<P>>>
36. Olua Arane bodane ne mɔɔ gyi ɔ nye zo kɛ ɔboa yeamaa mraalɛ nee mrenyia anyia gyima ayɛ na yeade menli mɔɔ vi namule nu kɔ azuamgbole zo la azo, na yeamaa mgbavolɛ ne mbɛlɛra anyia tɛnlabelɛ kpalɛ la, bɛbukebukele gyima gyima 130,000 wɔ mbaka ɛlualɛ gyima ne mɔ, ayile ɛkpululɛ gyima ne mɔ anu, fɛtelaza mɔɔ bɛfa bɛdua ninyɛne la gyima ne anu. Bieko a le bɛ sa mɔɔ bɛfa bɛte nvifideɛ ndotolo bɛfa bɛdua nvifideɛ fofolɛ la, kookoo mbaka ɛpɛpɛlɛ nee nvifideɛ zo nzule ɛgugualɛ wɔ kookoo gyima ne anu.
