67. Wɔ Ɛvolɛ 2020/21 Kookoo ɛlualɛ Ɛlobɔ ne mɔɔ ɛra awieleɛ kenle nsa ye ala la anu, kookoo mɔɔ bɛnyianle la dwule tone 1,045,500. Bɛdole kookoo bolɛ ne anu ɛya ngyɛnu 28 bɛmaanle tone ko bolɛ yɛle Ghana Sidi 10,560 wɔ Ɛvolɛ 2019/20 ɛlobɔ ne anu na eza bɛhɔle zo bɛdɔle ye zɔhane bolɛ ne wɔ Ɛvolɛ 2021/22 ɛlobɔ ne anu. Ɛhye hɔle zo wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɛnee kookoo bolɛ ɛha aze wɔ Maanle zo gua ne anu la.

68. Sumunli kɛ bɛlale ngɔkɔlɛ Enyianlɛ nwo Nvedenvedenuyɛlɛ wɔ Abɔdeɛ nu Nrɛlɛbɛ Nvedenvedenuyɛlɛ Ɛleka ne la Abɔdeɛ nu Nrɛlɛbɛ Nvedenvedenuyɛlɛ Ɛleka ne luale adwuleso adenle zo yɛle ngɔkɔlɛ nyinli nee mbelɛ na bɛlale bɛ ali. Bɛyɛle ngɔkɔlɛ mbelɛ mɔɔ bo 1,300 nee nyinli 300 mɔɔ bɛnyia dapɛne nnu mɔɔ kɔdwu dapɔne nsuu la. Eza bɛyɛle mrɛlɛ mɔɔ bɛfifi la 6,023,930 wɔ Aleɛ mɔɔ bɛ nee bɛ kɔ Maanle zo Ɛlualɛ nee Namunamu nu Anyunluhɔlɛ Enyianlɛ Ngyehyɛlɛ ne abo. Eza bɛdɔnenle mrɛlɛ mɔɔ bɛtetɛ la 55,727 nee kukue mɔɔ bɛtetɛ la 34,252 bɛmaanle ɛyazonlɛma.

69. Wɔ Ɛvolɛ 2022 ne anu Egyinliyɛlɛ Sonvolɛ Gyima ne bahɔ zo azɔ nyɛmoazielɛ ɛyazonlɛma avinli. Bɛbamaa bɛ nane mɔɔ le kɛ agbema, mboane, asoaso nee mɔɔ ɛha la mralɛ 14,000, agbolike ngyikyi 8,000 ngɔkɔsea mɔɔ ɛli kenle ko la mgbe 5 nee ngɔkɔlɛ mbelɛ 20,000.

70. Abɔdeɛ nwo Nvedenvedenuyɛlɛ Ɛleka ne bahɔ zo aboa Aleɛ mɔɔ bɛ nee bɛ kɔ Maanle zo Adɔne nee Namunamu nu Anyunluhɔlɛnyianlɛ Aleɛlualɛ Ngyehyɛlɛ ne nee Aleɛ nee gyimanyianlɛ Aleɛlualɛ Ngyehyɛlɛ ne mɔ bava aleɛ aleɛ mɔɔ fifi la bɛaboa ɛyazonlɛma ngyehyɛlɛ ne mɔ.
