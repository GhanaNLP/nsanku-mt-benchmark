10 Dwendwen dza Jehovah ayɛ ama wo ndɛ no ho na da no ase. (Ndw. 40:5) Da no mu no, ibotum ada no ase dɛ ɔroboa wo ma enye ne ntamu ayɛ papa. Ntsi ndɛ ihu dɛ oetsie wo mpaabɔ? Akenkan kyerɛwsɛm bi ma w’akoma atɔ wo yamu? Anaa akenkan asɛm bi, ahwɛ video bi ma aka dɛ, ‘Ah! iyi dze wɔyɛɛ maa me?’ Sɛ ekenkan asɛm bi anaa ehwɛ video bi na ɔma etse nka dɛm a, mo nua, ɔyɛ a da Jehovah ase. (Ndw. 65:2; Matt. 24:45; Rom. 15:4) Afei so, ibotum adwendwen ndzɛmba pa a ɔma hɛn enyi gye wɔ asetsena mu no ho. Ibotum ada Jehovah ase dɛ ɔama wimu atsew kama, ɔama enya edziban a epɛ edzi nye nyimpa a wɔdɔ wo. Ɔyɛ “nkwa na ahom” a Jehovah dze ama hɛn ntsi na hɛn nsa tum ka dɛm ndzɛmba ahorow yi. (Andw. 17:25) Ntsi ndɛ, ndzɛmba pa a Jehovah ayɛ ama wo no, no mu hendze na epɛ dɛ ekyerɛw to hɔ?—Eph. 5:20.

11 Papa a hɛn nuanom Christianfo yɛ ma hɛn no so, ibotum adwendwen ho. (Ndw. 133:1; Mbɛ. 18:24) Yɛreka yi ara, bi a wo nua Christiannyi bi ayɛ wo mboa anaa ɔaka asɛm bi dze ahyɛ wo nkuran pɛn. (Mbɛ. 12:25; 16:24) Sɛ obi yɛ adze ketseketse bi mpo ma wo a, sua dɛ ebɛkyerɛ ho enyisɔ. Asɛm bi a osii ma yehu dɛ sɛ obi yɛ adze ketseketse bi ma wo na ekyerɛ ho enyisɔ a ɔboa paa. Nna akyerɛbaa bi a ne ba ewu kɔ adzesua a, awerɛhow ntsi wɔrotow ndwom a onntum nntow bi. Ɔkaa dɛ: “Da kor bi a nna yɛrotow ndwom no, sista kor bi hun dɛ murusu, ntsi ofii beebi a ogyina no begyinaa me nkyɛn. Na ɔdze ne nsa too mo kɔn mu nye me toow ndwom no. M’enyi sɔɔ dza ɔyɛe no paa na ɔmaa muhun dɛ enuanom no dɔ me.” Dza sista no yɛe no, nna ɔyɛ adze ketseketse bi dze, naaso maame no a oridzi awerɛhow no enyi sɔe.

12 Sɛ yɛgye ber dwendwen dza Jehovah ayɛ ama hɛn ber bi a abɛsen kɔ nye dza ogu do reyɛ ama hɛn no ho a, ɔbɔboa hɛn paa ma yeefi hɛn akoma mu akyerɛ ndzɛmba pa a ɔyɛ ma hɛn no ho enyisɔ. Dɛ mbrɛ yɛkaa no ahyɛse no, sɛ yehu papa a wɔyɛ ma hɛn no a nna yennwiei, mbom no ɔwɔ dɛ yɛyɛ biribi dze kyerɛ ho enyisɔ. Naaso ebɛnadze na yebotum ayɛ dze akyerɛ dɛ dza Jehovah na nyimpa yɛ ma hɛn no hɛn enyi sɔ?
