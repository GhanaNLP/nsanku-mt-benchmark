ƆDƆ A YƐWƆ NO YƐ ANWANWADZE
<<<P>>>
13 Jesus kaa too hɔ dɛ sɛ n’esuafo no dodɔ hɔnho a, ɔbɛma nkorɔfo ehu dɛ wɔyɛ Nyankopɔn n’asomfo ampa. (Kenkan John 13:35.) Na ampaara so, nna tsetse Christianfo no dodɔ hɔnho paa. Ndzɛmba a wɔyɛe dze kyerɛɛ hɔn dɔ no bi nye dɛ, wɔnye hɔn nuanom a ehia hɔn no kyɛɛ dza wɔwɔ no. (Andw. 2:44, 45) Wɔbɔɔ mpaa maa hɔn nuanom a nna wɔretan hɔn no. (Andw. 12:5) Wɔhwɛɛ ngyanka nye ekunafo. (Jas. 1:27) Afei so ɔwɔ mu dɛ nna ehia hɔn dze, naaso wɔyɛɛ hɔn nuanom a nna wɔakɔ ahokyer mu no mboa. (2 Cor. 8:1-4) Bɔbor mfe ɔha wɔ Jesus no wu ekyir no, abakɔsɛm kyerɛwfo bi a wɔfrɛ no Tertullian kyerɛɛw asɛm bi a nkorɔfo kaa faa tsetse Christianfo no ho. Ɔkyerɛɛw dɛ nna nkorɔfo ka dɛ tsetse Christianfo no dodɔ hɔnho araa ma nna wɔayɛ krado dɛ wɔdze hɔn nkwa mpo bɔto hɔ ama hɔn nua.
<<<P>>>
14 Ndɛ so Jehovah n’asomfo dodɔ hɔnho paa tse dɛ tsetse Christianfo no. Ɔdɔ ntsi yɛka asɛmpa no bi kyerɛ hɔn a wɔnnyɛ Jehovah Adasefo no. Mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a yebenya sika dze ayɛ dɛm asɛmpaka edwuma no, yefi hɛn akoma nyina mu yi ntoboa dze boa. Wɔnnserɛserɛ hɛn sika, wɔnnhyɛ hɛn anaa wɔnngyegye hɛn tithe. Ɔdɔ kor yi ara so ka hɛn ma yɛboa hɛn nuanom a hɔnho aka no.* Asetsena mu ndzɛmba a yɛdze boa hɛn nuanom no da nkyɛn a, yɛhyɛ hɔn nkuran, yɛma hɔn enyi gye na yɛboa hɔn ma wɔsom Jehovah yie. Na dɛmara so na ɔsomafo Paul tuu hɛn fo dɛ yɛnyɛ. Ɔkaa dɛ: “Hom nkyekye hɔn a wɔabotow no hɔn werɛ na hom mboa hɔn a wɔyɛ mberɛw no, na hom nnya obiara ho abotar.” (1 Thess. 5:14) Jehovah dze hɛn abɔto ebusua kɛse bi mu. Dɛm ebusua no mufo wɔ wiadze yi mu beebiara. Yɛdodɔ hɛnho paa na yɛdɔ afofor so.
