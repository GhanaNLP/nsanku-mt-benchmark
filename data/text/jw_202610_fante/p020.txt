Ahohiahia kɛse a ɔrobɔbɔ ba yi, ana me mba nyim dza ɔsɛ dɛ wɔyɛ dze taa Jehovah n’ekyir?’ Awofo, hom mboa hom mba ma wɔmfa Jehovah nyɛnko pampee na wontsie n’asɛm. Hom bɛyɛ no dɛn? Enye hɔn mbɔ Bible mu nkɔmbɔ da biara, boa hɔn ma wɔmma mbuae wɔ adzesua ase, na enye hɔn mbɔ mu nkɔ asɛnka. Afei so, sɛ mberantsɛ na nkataasia nya nkorɔfo bi a wɔtse dɛ Jeremiah fa hɔn nyɛnko a, ɔboa paa. Dɛm ntsi, sɛ Christianfo a wɔdɔ Jehovah no nya mberantsɛ na nkataasia ho adagyer na wɔfa hɔn nyɛnko a, ɔbɔboa hɔn. Bio so, dɛ mbrɛ Daniel hwɛɛ Jeremiah suaa no no, sɛ enuanom a wɔdɔ Jehovah no nya mberantsɛ na nkataasia ho adagyer na wɔfa hɔn nyɛnko a, ɔbɔboa hɔn ma wɔasom Jehovah yie.

9 Aber a wɔfaa Daniel nye Hebrewfo mbanyimba nkaa no ndɔmmum ma wokopue Babylon no, wokehyiaa nsɔhwɛ bi a nna wonnhyiaa bi da wɔ hɔn kurow mu. Wokodur kurow no mu no, annkyɛr koraa na Babylonfo no yɛɛ dɛ wɔbɛma dɛm mbanyimba no ayɛ hɔn adze tse dɛ Babylonfo. Afei so, wɔnye hɔn faa ntsetsee bi mu a nna ɔbɛma woetum asom Babylonhen no. Ntsi nna ɔwɔ dɛ dɛm mbanyimba no yɛ ahwɛyie paa na dɛm ntsetsee no amma wɔannyɛ adze bi a wonyim dɛ Jehovah mmpɛ.
<<<P>>>
10 Sɔhwɛ a odzi kan a Daniel na n’anyɛnkofo no hyiae no, sɛ wɔannhwɛ no yie a nkyɛ bi a wɔbɛka dɛ, ‘O, nkyɛ iyi dze ɔnnyɛ adze biara.’ Dza osii nye yi: Ɔhen no kaa dɛ edziban a ɔyɛ a odzi no wɔmma mberantsɛba no bi ma wondzi. Naaso nna dɛm edziban no mu bi wɔ hɔ a Jehovah aka dɛ mma Hebrewfo nndzi. (Dan. 1:5, 8) Nkyɛ dɛm mberantsɛba no botum dze nsɛm bi eyiyi hɔnho ano aka dɛ, ‘Hɛnara yemmfi ha, yɛyɛ nkowaa, ntsi ma wɔdze bɛma hɛn biara yebedzi no dɛmara.’ Anaa mpo dɛ nna nkyɛ wobotum aka dɛ, ‘Ɔhen no na ɔse yendzi o, ntsi sɛ yeendzi a annhwɛ a no bo bofuw.’ Ntsi wɔyɛɛ dɛn?
<<<P>>>
11 Hebrewfo mberantsɛba a wɔnye hɔn kɔr Babylon no, nna wɔdɔɔ so. Naaso Bible no ka dɛ Daniel, Shadrach, Meshach, na Abednego pɛr na wodzii Jehovah nokwar. Mberantsɛba baanan yi mu biara boaa ne nyɛnko ma odzii Jehovah nokwar. Hɔn baanan no nyina yɛɛ adwen dɛ worinndzi ɔhen n’edziban no mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a wobetsie Jehovah. Jehovah n’enyiwa gyee dza wɔyɛɛ no ho na ohyiraa hɔn.—Dan. 1:6-12, 17.
<<<P>>>
12 Ɔyɛ a w’etsipɛmfo mberantsɛ na nkataasia piapia wo dɛ kɛyɛ adze bi a inyim dɛ ɔnnyɛ anaa? Nna Daniel n’anyɛnkofo no nyina dɔ Jehovah, ntsi wɔboaboaa hɔnho ma wɔyɛɛ dza ɔtsen. Ntsi sɛ woara so fa anyɛnkofo a wɔtse dɛm a, wɔbɔboa wo ma edzi Jehovah nokwar na wiadze no rimmpiapia wo ma ennkɛyɛ bɔn. (1 John 2:15-17) Sɛ efa nyɛnko pa a, ɔbɔboa wo ma edzi nokwar wɔ ‘ndzɛmba nketsenketse’ mpo mu na Jehovah so behyira wo. (Luke 16:10) Na sɛ idzi nokwar wɔ ‘ndzɛmba nketsenketse’ mu so a, ɔbɔboa wo ma daakye bi sɛ ihyia dza n’enyi soso sen dɛm a etum edzi nokwar.
