Nna ɔsomafo Peter nyim dɛ “ndzɛmba nyina n’ewiei abɛn,” ntsi ɔkyerɛwee dɛ: “Dza ɔkyɛn ne nyina no, hom ndodɔ homho dzendzenndzen.”—1 Pet. 4:7, 8.
<<<P>>>
20 Wɔka dɛ dua kor nnyɛ kwaa. Dɛm asɛm no yɛ nokwar paa, osiandɛ obiara hia nyɛnko pa, nkanka ndɛ. Jehovah nyim iyi ntsi na ɔpɛ dɛ obiara nya nyɛnko pa no. Kaa dɛ adze a beenu botum ama do no, ɔbaakofo mma do. Ntsi suasua Daniel na woara so fa nyɛnko. Hwehwɛ w’asafo mu na enye hɔn a ibotum esuasua hɔn na wɔaboa wo no mfa nyɛnko, dɛ mbrɛ Daniel hwɛɛ Jeremiah suasuaa no no. Enye anyɛnkofo a wobotum aboa wo ma asom Jehovah yie no mbɔ. Afei so sɛ ihia mboa a, ka kyerɛ wo nuanom dɛ wɔnkaa wo wɔ mpaabɔ mu na ɔnnyɛ enyito. Dza ɔkyɛn ne nyina no, kaa dɛ Jehovah nye wo Nyɛnko berɛbo. Na ɔno so dze, orinnyi wo baako nnhyɛ wo nsa dabiarada. (Heb. 13:5) Sɛ yɛfa Jehovah nye hɔn a wɔdɔ no no nyɛnko a, ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yɛnye adze biara a obotum asɛɛ yɛnye Jehovah ntamu no edzi esi, na yebenya anyɛnkofa bi so a ɔbɛtsena hɔ daa.

Jehovah Yɛ Hɛn Egya, Hɛn Nyame Nye Hɛn Nyɛnko
<<<P>>>
Binom wɔ hɔ a wɔnngye nndzi dɛ wobotum afa Jehovah nyɛnko anaa mpo wɔafa no dɛ hɔn Papa. Dɛm na ɔtse wɔ w’afamu anaa? Nna ɔno, da biara yɛ ndzɛmba nketsenketse a odzidzi do yi, na ɔbɔboa wo ma afa no nyɛnko.
<<<P>>>
● Enye no mbɔ nkɔmbɔ: Ɔyɛ a bɔ Jehovah mpaa osiandɛ ɔpɛ dɛ otsie dza ewɔ ka biara.
<<<P>>>
● Nya ber ma no: Sɛ ekenkan Bible no na isua adze a, ɔtse dɛ mbrɛ enye Jehovah tsena beebi ridzi nkɔmbɔ na iritsie asɛm a ɔreka no. Ntsi hu no yie; hu dza ɔpɛ nye dza ɔmmpɛ, nye ndzɛmba a ɔayɛ abɛsen nye dza ogu do reyɛ.
<<<P>>>
● Nya abotar: Kaa dɛ ɔnnyɛ da kor na wɔdze fa nyɛnko, ɔyɛ nkakrankakra. Ntsi yer wo ho, mma mma mframa mmfa enye Jehovah ntamu osiandɛ dɛmara na noara so pɛ.
