17 Yebotum dze hɛn ndzeyɛɛ akyerɛ dɛ hɛn enyi sɔ dza Jehovah yɛ ma hɛn no paa. Sɛ hɛn enyi sɔ dza Jehovah ayɛ ama hɛn no paa a, ɔbɛka hɛn ma yɛayɛ biribi ama no. Ebɛnadze na yebotum ayɛ ama Jehovah? Bible no tu hɛn fo dɛ yɛmfa hɛn “nyimpadua mbɔ afɔr a ɔtse ase” mma Nyankopɔn. (Kenkan Romefo 12:1.) Iyi kyerɛ dɛ ɔsɛ dɛ yɛdze dza yɛdwendwen ho, hɛn ahoɔdzen, hɛn kra nyina nye dza yɛwɔ nyina yɛ dza Jehovah pɛ. Ntsi ndzɛmba a Jehovah pɛ no, no mu bi nye dɛn?

18 Enyisɔ kɛse a yɛwɔ ma dza Jehovah ayɛ ama hɛn no na ɔkaa hɛn ma yehyiraa hɛn nkwa do maa no na yenumae. Ntsi da biara yɛdze hɛn ho nyina ma Jehovah. Kyerɛ dɛ, yɛdze hɛn ber hɛn sika nye hɛn ahoɔdzen yɛ dza yebotum biara wɔ no som mu. Iyi hwehwɛ dɛ da biara yɛbɔbɔ mbɔdzen akenkan Bible no na yɛadwendwen ho. Afei so, ɔsɛ dɛ da biara yɛka asɛmpa no bi kyerɛ nkorɔfo, yɛkɔ adzesua, na yɛkɔ so a ɔsɛ dɛ yɛma mbuae. Iyi da nkyɛn a ɔsɛ dɛ yeyi ntoboa dze boa ma ɔsom edwuma no kɔ do. (Ndw. 1:2, 3; 2 Cor. 8:4; Heb. 10:24, 25; 13:15) Sɛ yefi hɛn akoma nyina mu som Jehovah yie a, nna yɛreyɛ biribi dze akyerɛ no dɛ hɛn enyi sɔ dza ɔyɛ ma hɛn no paa.—Col. 3:23, 24.

19 Ampa, ndɛ nyimpa piinara yɛ bɔnyiayɛ na wɔnnkyerɛ ndzɛmba ho enyisɔ. Naaso hɛn dze, yenyim dɛ ɔsɛ dɛ yɛkyerɛ ndzɛmba ho enyisɔ. Yenyim dɛ sɛ yɛkyerɛ ndzɛmba ho enyisɔ a ɔma hɛn enyi gye, ɔmma yɛnndwendwen pii, na dza ohia paa no, ɔma yɛbɛn Jehovah. Sɛ yɛdwendwen ndzɛmba pa a Jehovah ayɛ ama hɛn nye dza ogu do reyɛ ma hɛn no ho a, ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yeehu dza ɔyɛ ma hɛn no nyina na yaakyerɛ ho enyisɔ kɛse. Hom mma yensi hɛn bo dɛ yɛdze hɛn nsɛnkaa nye hɛn ndzeyɛɛ bɛkyerɛ ndzɛmba ho enyisɔ nkanka dza Jehovah yɛ ma hɛn no. Sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm a, hɛn Nyankopɔn a ɔnnyɛ bɔnyiayɛ no behyira hɛn pii ndɛ nye daa.—Ndw. 106:1; Heb. 11:6.
