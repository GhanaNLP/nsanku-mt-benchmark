Jehovah N’adzeserɛ Nye Dɛ: “Me Ba, Yɛ Onyansafo”
<<<P>>>
DZA YEBOSUA
<<<P>>>
Sɛ mberantsɛ na nkataasia nya anyɛnkofo apapa a, ɔbɔboa hɔn ma woeetum asom Jehovah.
<<<P>>>
Dua Kor Nnyɛ Kwaa
<<<P>>>
“Na sɛ nyimpa bi dzi ɔbaakofo do nyim a, beenu nye no bedzi esi.”—ƆSƐNK. 4:12.
<<<P>>>
HƐN Jehovah asomfo dze, dza ɔda hɛn akoma do ara nye dɛ yɛbɔsom Jehovah yie. Naaso iyi ne yɛ nnda famu, osiandɛ da biara wiadze no pɛ dɛ ɔtwe hɛn fi Jehovah ho. Kwan kor a wiadze no fa do yɛ dɛm nye dɛ ɔbɛsɛɛ adwen a yɛwɔ wɔ anyɛnkofa ho no. Binom susu dɛ nyɛnko dze inya no dodow a na oye; sɛ wɔtse dɛn o, sɛ wɔwɔ hen o, ɔno dze wɔnnhwɛ hɔ. Mberantsɛ na nkataasia pii so wɔ hɔ a, sɛ wohia mboa anaa afotu bi a wɔkɔ hɔn etsipɛmfo nkyɛn. Binom so dze, ɔyɛ hɔn dɛ wonnhia nyɛnko biara, hɔnara wobotum abɔ hɔn bra. Na Jehovah na Jesus so ɛ, wohu anyɛnkofa dɛn?
<<<P>>>
2 Jehovah pɛ dɛ yɛfa anyɛnkofo a wɔbɔboa hɛn ma yɛasom no yie. Anyɛnkofo a wɔtse dɛm na Jesus fae. Oemmbu n’esuafo no dɛ wɔyɛ amanyifo ara kɛkɛ, mbom ɔfaa hɔn dɛ n’anyɛnkofo. Aka kakraabi bi ma oewu no, ɔbɔɔ n’esuafo no abaw do na ɔkaa kyerɛɛ hɔn dɛ: “Hom na hom da ho ara bata mo ho wɔ mo nsɔhwɛ mu.” (Luke 22:28; John 15:15) Nyɛnko pa dze nnyɛ aber a ndzɛmba rokɔ yie no nkotsee na ɔnye wo bɔ. Mbom papa mu o, bɔn mu o ɔnye wo nam. (Mbɛ. 17:17) Ɔsɛnkafo 4:9-12* ma yehu dɛ anyɛnkofo apapa hyɛ hɛn nkuran na wɔboa hɛn ma yɛyɛ ndzɛmba pa. (Kenkan.) Sɛ yɛfa anyɛnkofo a wɔtse dɛm a wobotum aboa hɛn ma yɛnye biribiara a obotum asɛɛ yɛnye Jehovah ntamun no ‘edzi esi.’ Sɛ yɛnye anyɛnkofo a wɔtse dɛm no bɔ mu som Jehovah a, wɔbɔboa hɛn ma yɛayɛ biribi a sɛ wogyaa mu maa hɛn nkotsee a, nkyɛ yennkotum ayɛ ara da. Afei so obotum ama yeenya anyɛnkofa bi a ɔbɛtsena hɔ daa.
<<<P>>>
* Yetaa dze dɛm kyerɛwsɛm yi dzi dwuma aber a yɛreka awar ho asɛm. Naaso mfaso a ɔwɔ anyɛnkofa do no paa na ɔka ho asɛm.
<<<P>>>
1. Ebɛn adwen na nyimpa pii wɔ wɔ anyɛnkofa ho?
<<<P>>>
2. (a) Woananom na Jesus faa hɔn nyɛnko? (b) Dɛ mbrɛ Ɔsɛnkafo 4:9-12 ma yehu no, ebɛnadze ntsi na yehia anyɛnkofo apapa?
