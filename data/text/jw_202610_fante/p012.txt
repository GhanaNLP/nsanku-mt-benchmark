Kɔ Do Yɛ Edwuma No, Weɔn Na Tweɔn
<<<P>>>
DZA YEBOSUA
Adzesua yi bɛma yeehu dɛ Jehovah reyɛ anwanwadze ebiasa bi, na yɛwɔ hokwan dɛ yesuo no mu.
<<<P>>>
Hokwan A Enya Dze Roboa Jehovah Ma Ɔayɛ Anwanwadze No, Suo Mu Yie
<<<P>>>
“Ɔno na ɔbɛma nyimpa nyina ehu dɛ hom yɛ m’esuafo.”
—JOHN 13:35.
<<<P>>>
ANWANWADZE pii na wɔayɛ ho kyerɛwtohɔ wɔ Bible no mu. No mu bi nye yi: Ber bi Jehovah paa po kɔkɔɔ no mu ma Israelfo no faa mu. Afei so, ber a Jesus wɔ asaase yi do no, ɔsaa ayarfo yarba na onyaan ewufo. Dɛm anwanwadze yi nyina sii no nnyɛ ndɛ, na hɔn a nna wɔgye Jehovah dzi dɛm aber no nyina so hun dɛ Jehovah na ɔyɛɛ dɛm anwanwadze yi. (Matt. 15:31) Wo yamu a nkyɛ dɛm anwanwadze no risisi no nna ewɔ hɔ bi anaa? Nna ɔno ka w’akoma to wo yamu. Ndɛ so Jehovah reyɛ anwanwadze bi. Ebɛnadze ntsi na yɛreka dɛm?
<<<P>>>
2 Wɔka anwanwadze koraa ɔkyerɛ dɛn? Anwanwadze yɛ biribi a nyimpa nnkotum ayɛ anaa ɔbor nyimpa n’adwen na ne ntseasee do. Ɔyɛ Jehovah no tum no na ɔdze yɛ anwanwadze. Ndɛ, Jehovah reyɛ anwanwadze ebiasa bi wɔ n’ahyehyɛdze no mu na ɔno na yerubosua ho adze no. Dɛm ndzɛmba ebiasa a ɔreyɛ no, yebotum aka dɛ ɔyɛ anwanwadze osiandɛ Jehovah nkotsee na obotum ama esi. Sɛ yersusu anwanwadze no mu biara ho a, yɛbɔhwɛ mbrɛ yebesi ayɛ “Nyankopɔn n’anyɛnkofo a yɛnye no bɔ mu yɛ edwuma” aber a ɔreyɛ dɛm anwanwadze ahorow no.—Kenkan 1 Corinthfo 3:9.
