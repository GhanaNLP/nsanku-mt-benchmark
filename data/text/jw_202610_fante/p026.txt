8 Sɛ yɛyɛ daa asɛm a ɔboa hɛn ma yegyina hɛn haw ahorow ano. Jesus kaa dɛ da biara wɔ “nankasa ne haw.” (Matt. 6:34) Dza ɔkae no so yɛ nokwar, da biara da yehyia ɔhaw. Naaso nna Jesus edzi kan aka dɛ, dza yɛrefa mu nyina Jehovah n’enyi tua na onyim dza yehia so. (Matt. 6:25-32) Sɛ yɛyɛ daa asɛm da biara a, ɔma yeyi hɛn adwen fi dɛm haw ahorow no do na yɛdze hɛn adwen si ndzɛmba a Jehovah reyɛ ama hɛn no do. (Ndw. 68:19; 1 Pet. 5:6, 7) Asɛm bi a hɛn akyerɛbaa bi kae no ma yehu dɛ dɛm asɛm yi yɛ nokwar. Oedzi mfe 19. Nkan no, odzii mfom kɛse bi ntsi nna ɔtse nka dɛ Jehovah mmfaa no bɔn nnkyɛɛ no. Ɔbɔɔ mpaa serɛɛ Jehovah dɛ ɔmma onhu dɛ ɔdze no bɔn akyɛ no ampa. Owiei no na ɔyɛɛ ne daa asɛm. Nna daa asɛm no gyina Ndwom 86:5 do. Hɔ ka dɛ: ‘Ɔwo, Jehovah, iye, na edze kyɛ. Na w’adɔm dɔɔ so ma hɔn a wɔfrɛ wo nyinara.’ Ɔkaa dɛ: “Mekenkaan kyerɛwsɛm no wiei no ara na m’akoma tɔɔ me yamu, osiandɛ muhun dɛ dza mihia no ara na Jehovah dze ama me no. Asɛm a osii dɛm da no maa muhun dɛ ɔnnsɛ dɛ migya daa asɛm no to mu da kor mpo.”

9 Daa asɛm no taa boa hɛn ma ‘yɛdze hɛn werɛ nyina hyɛ Jehovah mu’ ber a yeehyia ɔhaw bi no. (Mbɛ. 3:5, 6) Biribi dɛmara na osii wɔ da a Russia na Ukraine hɔn ntokwaa no hyɛɛ ase no. Dɛm da no, nna daa asɛm no fi Isaiah 30:15 a hɔ ka dɛ: “Kommye na awerɛhyɛmu mu na hom ahoɔdzen befir.” Dɛm kyerɛwsɛm no kaa enuanom a wɔwɔ Ukraine no akoma too hɔn yamu. Dɛm enuanom yi mu kor nye Eduard. Ɔkaa dɛ: “Dɛm da anapatutuutu no menye me yer bɔɔ mu yɛɛ daa asɛm, yewiei na yɛbɔɔ mpaa. Nokwasɛm nye dɛ dɛm daa asɛm no boaa hɛn paa. Nna ɔtse dɛ mbrɛ yɛabɔ nsu nwin bi egu hɛn akoma do. Ɔmaa hɛn ho dwee hɛn fɔmm.” Akyerɛbaa bi so a wɔfrɛ no Svitlana kaa dɛ: “Isaiah 30:15 no boaa hɛn paa. Aber a nna wɔrototow itur wɔ hɛn apaamu hɔ no, nna daa asɛm no ara na yɛrodwendwen ho.”

10 Sɛ yɛyɛ daa asɛm a ɔma yenya akokodur dze dzi Jehovah nokwar. Yehia akokodur ama yeetum edzi Jehovah nokwar wɔ skuul anaa edwuma mu, dze aka asɛmpa no bi akyerɛ nkorɔfo, na yɛdze egyina ɔsoɛrtsia ano. Aber a Jehovah yii Joshua dɛ ondzi Israelfo no enyim no, ɔkaa kyerɛɛ no mpɛn ebiasa dɛ ɔnhyɛ dzen na onnya akokodur. Jehovah sanee so kaa kyerɛɛ no dɛ ɔnkenkan Mbra nwoma no da biara ama woeenya akokodur. (Kenkan Joshua 1:6-9.) Hɛn so, sɛ yɛyɛ daa asɛm na yɛkenkan Bible no da biara a, ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yɛayɛ dzen na yɛakɔ do edzi Jehovah nokwar.
