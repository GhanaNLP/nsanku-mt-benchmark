G. Ehyiadzi a wɔyɛe no October 1926 wɔ Madison Square Garden a ɔwɔ New York
<<<P>>>
Kwesida October 17 ber a wɔbɔɔ ewiaber ndɔn-
ebiasa no, nyimpa bɛyɛ 12,000 betsiee Onua Ru-
therford ne kasaa no bi. Ber a ɔmaa ɔkasa no
wiei no, hɔn a nna wɔpɛ dɛ wosua pii ka ho no bi-
saa nsɛm pii. Dɛm ehyiadzi a wɔyɛe no, wɔkye-
kyɛɛ mbuukuu bɔbor 9,000. Sɛ iyi dɛm aber no si
nkyɛn a, nna wɔnnyɛɛ ehyiadzi nnkyekyɛɛ mbuu-
kuu pii dɛm da.

WOSII DAN BI WƆ 117 ADAMS STREET
<<<P>>>
Osiandɛ nna nyimpa pii pɛ hɛn mbuukuu no
bi ntsi, enuanom a wɔwɔ Brooklyn a wodzi ndzɛm-
ba enyim no hun dɛ beebi a yɛprentse hɛn mbuu-
kuu no, ɔsɛ dɛ wobue mu. Mfe pii a abɛsen kɔ
no, nna wɔahaa dan bi wɔ 18 Concord Street. Nna
hɔ na wɔprentse hɛn mbuukuu. Naaso osiandɛ
nna edwuma no reyɛ kɛse ntsi, nna hɔ esuar. Ntsi
enuanom no kɔtɔɔ asaase bi wɔ Adams Street.
Nna ɔbɛn beebi a nna wɔwɔ dada no paa, ntsi wɔ-
hyɛɛ ase sii aborɔsan kɛse bi a ɔtoa do awɔtwe wɔ
do. Afei dze, nna wobenya beebi kɛse dze etsin-
tsim hɔn mbuukuu no.
<<<P>>>
Dan a nna worisi wɔ 117 Adams Street no,
nna 1927 na wɔbɛhyɛ ase ayɛ edwuma wɔ mu. Nna
dɛm dan no bɔboa enuanom no ma woeesiesie
hɔnho ama nkɔenyim a nna ɔreba no. Afe 1927 mu
no, nyimpa pii baa asafo no mu, na Jehovah kɔr
do siesiee hɔn tseew hɔn ho mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a afe 1931
mu no, wobotum afrɛ hɔn Jehovah Adasefo.
