KORYƐ A ƆWƆ HƐN NTAMU NO YƐ ANWANWADZE
<<<P>>>
9 Nkɔnhyɛnyi Micah so kaa anwanwadze bi a ɔrokɔ do wɔ hɛn aber yi do no ho asɛm too hɔ. Ɔno nye koryɛ soronko a ɔwɔ Nyankopɔn n’asomfo ntamu no. Micah hyɛɛ nkɔm dɛ Jehovah bɔboaboa nyimpa bi ano ma wɔdze koryɛ atsena. (Kenkan Micah 2:12.) Nkɔnhyɛ yi aba mu, osiandɛ Nyankopɔn n’asomfo a wɔwɔ hɔ ndɛ no, ɔsor obiara no suban, beebi a ofi nye mbrɛ wosii tsetsee no. Naaso wɔdze koryɛ abɔ mu rosom Jehovah. Wɔnye odwontofo David yɛ adwen wɔ dɛm asɛm yi ho. Ɔkaa dɛ: “Hwɛ, mbrɛ odua na ɔyɛ fɛw ma enuanom dɛ wɔbɔbɔ mu atsena koryɛ mu!”—Ndw. 133:1.
<<<P>>>
10 Ndɛ ndzɛmba a yɛyɛ dze kyerɛ dɛ yɛyɛ kor no dɔɔ so. No mu bi nye dɛ, Bible mu nkyerɛkyerɛ a sɛ yɛkɔ asafo nhyiamu, ɔmantɔw na ɔmansin ehyiadzi ase a hɛn nsa ka no, dɛm nkyerɛkyerɛ kor no ara na hɛn nuanom a wɔwɔ wiadze nyina so nsa ka. Wiadze yi mu beebiara a ebɔkɔ no, ibohu dɛ enuanom a wɔyɛ edwuma wɔ Bethel anaa wɔboa ma wosisi asafo adan no, ɔsor obiara no suban nye beebi a ofi. Naaso wotum bɔ mu dze koryɛ yɛ edwuma. Koryɛ wɔ hɛn mu paa. Yɛnntse dɛ hɔn a wɔwɔ wiadze no mu no. Hɔn dze, hɔn amambra, hɔn sikasɛm anaa amanyɛkuw a wɔdɔm no ama hɔn mu apaapaa. Naaso hɛn dze, yɛdɔdɔ hɛnho na yɛmma mframa mmfa hɛn ntamu. Kyerɛ dɛ, beebiara a yɛbɔkɔ no, yenyim dɛ hɛn nuanom bi wɔ hɔ a wɔayɛ krado dɛ wɔdze mboa a yehia no bɛma hɛn na wɔa-

9-10. Ndɛ, ebɛnadze na ɔkyerɛ dɛ asɛm a ɔwɔ Micah 2:12 no aba mu?
<<<P>>>
ma yɛnye Jehovah ntamu ayɛ kama. Yennhun nyimpa a wofi aman afofor do na ɔsor hɔn amambra a dɛ mbrɛ wohu ndzɛmba a Jehovah gye to mu nye kwan a wɔfa do bɔ hɔn bra no yɛ kor dɛm da.

11 Mpaapaamu adɔɔ so wɔ wiadze yi mu beebiara, naaso hɛn dze yɛyɛ kor. Iyi yɛ anwanwadze. Ekɔ wiadze yi mu beebiara a, ihu dɛ koryɛ innyi adasamba ntamu koraa. Sɛ wɔrema news a, wɔannka ɔyɛkyerɛ na etuatsew ho asɛm a, nna ɔyɛ akokoeko ho asɛm. Obiara nntse ne nyɛnko ase, mpaapaamu nkotsee. Asɛm a Bible no kaa too hɔ no aba mu pɛpɛɛpɛr. Ndɛ, nyimpa ‘nndzi nokwar, wɔnye hɔn yɛ nhyehyɛɛ biara a ɔnnyɛ yie, wɔsɛɛ afofor hɔn edzin, wonntum nnhyɛ hɔnho do, wɔyɛ nkorɔfo a hɔn tsirmu yɛ dzen paa na wokyir adze papa biara so. Woyi nkorɔfo ma, wɔmmfa afotu nnyɛ hwee, na ahomemado ahyɛ hɔn ma.’ (2 Tim. 3:2-4) Ɔwɔ mu dɛ “wiadze nyina da ɔbɔnfo no” na n’esunsum fi “no tum mu” dze, naaso Jehovah n’asomfo dze yɛdze batsir kor abɔ mu rosom no. (1 John 5:19; Nyi. 12:9) Sɛ iyi nnyɛ anwanwadze a, nna ɔno ɔyɛ ebɛnadze?
