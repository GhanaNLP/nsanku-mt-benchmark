<<<P>>>
dɛ yehu na afei yɛyɛ biribi dze kyerɛ ho enyisɔ. Ɔnodze, ma yɛmbɔ dɛm ndzɛmba ebiasa yi ho nkɔmbɔ: (1) Siantsir a ɔsɛ dɛ yɛkyerɛ ndzɛmba ho enyisɔ, (2) dza ɔbɔboa hɛn ama yeetum efi hɛn akoma mu akyerɛ ndzɛmba ho enyisɔ, nye (3) kwan a yebotum afa do akyerɛ ndzɛmba ho enyisɔ.
<<<P>>>
SIANTSIR A ƆSƐ DƐ YƐKYERƐ NDZƐMBA HO ENYISƆ
<<<P>>>
4 Sɛ yɛyɛ nyimpa a yennyi ayɛw a obotum epira hɛn. Hɔn a wonnyi ayɛw no, adze biara nnyɛ hɔn fɛw. Dɛ mbrɛ Mbɛbusɛm 15:15 ka no, “amandzehufo nda nyinara yɛ mbusu,” anaadɛ da biara nnyɛ mma no. Wiadze yi mu dze, obiara na ne haw na adze a ɔma no werɛ how. Ɔhaw a yehyia no, ɔnnyɛ ne nyina na yɛwɔ n'ano edur. Naaso dza yennyi n'ano edur no, dɛ yɛbɛma wɔahaw hɛn anaa yɛnnkɛma wɔahaw hɛn no dze, ɔwɔ hɛnara hɛn hɔ. Sɛ yɛdze hɛn adwen si ɔhaw a yerihyia no nkotsee do a, ɔbɛyɛ hɛn dɛ da biara nnyɛ, ntsi da biara na yɛayɛ kosoo. Suban anaa adwen a ɔtse dɛm no remma hɛn enyi nngye da, na papa a wɔyɛ ma hɛn nyinara so mpo yerunnhu.
<<<P>>>
5 Sɛ yɛkyerɛ ndzɛmba ho enyisɔ a ɔboa hɛnara, ɔma hɛn enyi gye. Mbɛbusɛm 15:15 toa do dɛ, obi a ɔyɛ “akomatɔyamunyi no dze, ɔtse edzidzii daa.” Kyerɛ dɛ, obi a ɔwɔ akomatɔyamu anaa ɔkyerɛ ndzɛmba ho enyisɔ no, ɔdze n'adwen si ndzɛmba papa a ɔrokɔ do wɔ n'abrabɔ mu no do na ɔkyerɛ ho enyisɔ. Onyimpa a ɔtse dɛm no, ihu no a bɛka dɛ gyama onnyi haw biara. Sɛ obi kyerɛ ndzɛmba ho enyisɔ dɛm a, ɔma n'enyi gye tse dɛ mbrɛ ‘daa oridzidzi wɔ aponto ase.’
