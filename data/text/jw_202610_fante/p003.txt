WƆYƐƐ EHYIADZI AHOROW DZE HYƐƐ ENUANOM NKURAN DƐ WƆNYER HƆNHO NYƐ ASƐNKA EDWUMA NO
<<<P>>>
May bosoom no n’ewiei no, Bible Esuafo pii kɔr ehyiadzi bi a wɔyɛe wɔ London a ɔwɔ England no bi. Dɛm aber no, nna edwumayɛfo a wɔwɔ Britain nyina kɔ strike. Ntsi nna ɔyɛ dzen dɛ wobenya edwumakuw bi ma wɔaprentse mbuukuu ama hɔn. Ɔbaa no dɛm no, enuanom a wɔwɔ Brooklyn Bethel no yɛɛ overtime, ntsi wotumii prentsee buukuu kakraba a wɔato dzin The Standard for the People no nkorkor 117,000 kaa dza wɔaprentse no ho. Wɔkyekyɛɛ buukuu kakraba yi maa enuanom a wɔbaa London ehyiadzi no.
<<<P>>>
Memenda, May 29 no, wɔhyɛɛ ase dze buukuu kakraba yi kɔr asɛnka. Dɛm da no, asɛm a nna wɔakyerɛw wɔ ehyiadzi program no do no hyɛɛ enuanom no nkuran dɛ: “Obiara a woenuma no a ɔwɔ ehyiadzi yi ase no, ɔsɛ dɛ ɔka Jesus nye n’Ahendzi no ho asɛm kyerɛ nkorɔfo. Ɔkyena anapa, obiara mbɔ mbɔdzen ndu Alexandra Palace ntsɛm, na enuanom a wɔwɔ hɔ no bɛkyerɛ hom dza hom nyɛ.”
<<<P>>>
Wɔkyekyɛɛ mbuukuu bɔbor 100,000. Ɔweɔn-Aban a ɔbaa July 15, 1926 no bɔɔ amandzɛɛ dɛ: “Enuanom enyi gyee papa. Wɔtsee nka dɛ woetsie Jehovah na wɔaka asɛmpa no akyerɛ nkorɔfo dze akyerɛ dɛ wɔyɛ n’adasefo.”
<<<P>>>
Adze kyee no, Onua Rutherford maa ɔkasa bi a nna ɔfa dza ɔbɔto wiadze etumfo no ho wɔ Royal Albert Hall. Ber a wobuee abow no, annkyɛr koraa na nyimpa 13,000 dze no punkum kɛhyɛɛ asa hɔ ma. Hɔn a woennya kwan ennwura mu no, wɔdze speakers akɛse sisii abowano maa hɔn ma wotsiee bi.
<<<P>>>
Onua Rutherford ne kasaa no mu no, ɔkyerɛkyerɛɛ mu dɛ siantsir a wiadze etumfo no dze hɔn tsir repempem nye dɛ wɔadan hɔn ekyir ama Jehovah nye n’Ahendzi no. Afei, ɔbɔɔ Amanaman Apam no ekyir na ɔkaa dɛ ɔrennkyɛr koraa obefi hɔ.
