14 Ɔyɛ a ka medaase. Sɛ obi yɛ biribi ketseketse bi mpo ma wo a, ɔyɛ a ka medaase. Bi mpo a obuee abow maa wo, ɔkamfoo wo anaa ɔkaa asɛm bi dze hyɛɛ wo nkuran. Sɛ obi yɛ biribi ma wo na epɛ dɛ ekyerɛ ho enyisɔ a, ibotum asɛnd no tɛst mɛsegye anaa akyerɛw biribi wɔ krataa do dze ada no ase. Asafo mu panyin bi abɛsera ɔwo anaa w’ebusua ndaansa yi anaa? Ibotum akyerɛw biribi anaa asɛnd mɛsegye bi dze ada no ase. (1 Thess. 5:12, 13) Asafo mu mpanyimfo hɔn yernom so, ɔwɔ mu araa ibotum ada hɔn ase. Wɔyɛ adze paa, wɔdze ndzɛmba pii bɔ afɔr mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a hɔn kunnom benya ber dze ahyɛ afofor nkuran.
<<<P>>>
15 Ne nyina mu no, obi a ɔsɛ dɛ yɛka medaase kyerɛ no paa nye Jehovah. Yɛbɛyɛ no dɛn? Ɔno nye dɛ yebefi hɛn akoma mu abɔ no mpaa. Na mpaabɔ so wɔ hɔ yi ogu mu ahorow pii. (Eph. 6:18) Bi a ahyɛ no nsew dɛ mpaa a yɛbɔ no, no mu piinara yɛ ebisadze anaa nkotoserɛ. Yɛrobɔ mpaa a ɔtse dɛm a, ɔyɛ a yɛtaa bisa Jehovah hɔ biribi, dɛ bi a ɔmma hɛn ahoɔdzen a yɛdze begyina sɔhwɛ bi ano, ɔmma hɛn nyansa a yɛdze besi gyinae pa, anaa ɔmfa bɔn bi a yɛayɛ no nkyɛ hɛn. Naaso sɛ yɛdwendwen ndzɛmba pa pii a Jehovah yɛ ma hɛn no ho a, ɔno so ka hɛn ma yɛbɔ mpaa da no ase. (Ndw. 95:2) Ntsi ɔwɔ mu araa, ɔyɛ a bisa woho dɛ, ‘Me mpaabɔ tse dɛn? Ɔyɛ a mekaa dɛ mebɛda Jehovah ase wɔ ndzɛmba pa a ɔyɛ ma me no ho, anaa emi dze da biara dza ohia me nkotsee na mibisa no?’
