3 Sɛ aberantsɛ anaa akataasia bi fa nyɛnko pa a, obesi dɛn aboa no ma ɔasom Jehovah yie? Ebɛnadze na awofo nye enuanom a wɔdɔ Jehovah no botum ayɛ dze akyerɛ mberantsɛ na nkataasia dɛ wɔyɛ nyɛnko pa? Mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a yebenya dɛm nsɛmbisa ahorow yi ho mbuae no, ma yɛmbɔ dza ɔkɔr do wɔ nkɔnhyɛnyi Daniel n’asetsena mu no ho nkɔmbɔ kakra. Ma yɛnhwɛ dza ɔboaa no ma otumii gyinaa ɔhaw bi a nna ɔbor nyimpa n’ahoɔdzen do no ano. Afei so aber a nna Daniel ayɛ ankonam no, yebohu mbrɛ ne Nyɛnko Jehovah sii boaa no.

NYƐNKO PA BOA HƐN MA YƐSOM JEHOVAH
<<<P>>>
4 Aber a Daniel rinyin no, nna ndzɛmba mu yɛ dzen ma Nyankopɔn no nkorɔfo paa. Osiandɛ nna Judahfo no nnsom Jehovah yie ntsi, nna Jehovah eyi hɔn baako ahyɛ hɔn nsa. Dɛm ntsi wɔ Nebuchadnezzar n’aber do wɔ afe 620 mu no, Jehovah maa odzii Judahfo no do hen. (Dan. 1:2) Ɔbɛyɛ dɛ dɛm aber no, nna Daniel yɛ banyimba. Mfe kakraabi ekyir no, wɔkyeer no kɔr nkowaasom mu, na wɔnye no kɔr Babylon kurow no mu dɛ ɔnkɛgye ntsetsee. (Dan. 1:3-6) Beebi a nna Daniel ekopue no dze, nna ɔmmbɛn fie koraa, na sɛ nna ɔtow n’enyi so a ɔnntɔ busuanyi biara n’enyim.
<<<P>>>
5 Nna ebɛnadze na ɔbɔboa Daniel ma oetum egyina ɔhaw a nna obehyia wɔ Babylon no ano? Bible no ka dɛ nna Daniel ‘esuae wɔ n’akoma mu’ dɛ obedzi Nyankopɔn ne mbra do. (Dan. 1:8) Dɛm mbra no koraa ohunii wɔ hen? Na woana mpo na ɔboaa banyimba a ɔtse dɛm no ma osii gyinae dɛ ɔbɔsom Jehovah? Sɛ yɛhwɛ dzin Daniel ne nkyerɛasee a, ɔma yehu dɛ bi a n’awofo na wɔboaa no. Dzin Daniel no kyerɛ “Nyankopɔn nye me Tsɛmbuafo.” Bi a nna Daniel n’awofo pɛ dɛ ɔsom Jehovah ntsi na wɔdze dɛm dzin no too no no.—Deut. 6:6, 7.
<<<P>>>
6 Obotum so aba dɛ Daniel suasuaa nkɔnhyɛnyi Jeremiah. Ebɛnadze ntsi na yebotum aka dɛm? Dza ɔwɔ mu nye dɛ, afe 647 ansaana wɔrobɔwo Jesus mu no na Jehovah maa Jeremiah hyɛɛ no nkɔnhyɛ edwuma no ase. Jehovah somaa no dɛ ɔmfa ne nkrasɛm nkɛma Judah ahemfo nye kurow no mu etsitsir. Ɔbɛyɛ dɛ aber a nna Jeremiah reyɛ dɛm edwuma no mu no na wɔwoo Daniel. (Jer. 1:2, 17, 18) Yerunntum mmfa nnsi pɔn do dɛ nna Daniel na Jeremiah yɛ anyɛnkofo dze, naaso osiandɛ nna Daniel n’ebusua ka etsitsir a nna wɔwɔ Judah ho ntsi, ɔbɛyɛ dɛ aber a nna Jeremiah dze Nyankopɔn ne nkrasɛm no ridzi akɔnaaba nyina, nna banyimba Daniel n’enyiwa tua, na nna n’asowa so gu do. (Dan. 1:3, 6) Daniel yɛɛ akɔkora no mpo, nna ɔda ho ara kenkan Jeremiah no nkɔnhyɛ no. (Jer. 25:11; 29:10; Dan. 9:1, 2) Ntsi yebotum aka dɛ, Daniel suaa fii Jeremiah hɔ dɛ sɛ obi nnyi Nyankopɔn n’asomfo ntamu mpo a, ɔsɛ dɛ onya akokodur na odzi Jehovah nokwar.
