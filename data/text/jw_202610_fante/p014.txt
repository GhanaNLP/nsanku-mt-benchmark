Wɔsoɛree Tsiaa Hɔn Naaso Wonyaa Nkɔenyim
<<<P>>>
Aber a nna wɔretan Jehovah Adasefo enyi wɔ Wiadze Ko II no mu no, wonyaa nkɔenyim bi a nna ɔyɛ nwanwa. Nazi porisinyi bi kaa dɛ: “Jehovah Adasefo dze eretan hɔn enyi mpo no nna wɔrodɔɔ so.”
<<<P>>>
Asɛm bi so a ɔtse dɛmara sii wɔ Netherlands. Dɛm aber no, 1986 Yearbook no kaa dɛ: “Wɔkyekyeer adawurbɔfo no. Wɔnye binom kɔr efiadze, wotwaa binom so esu kɔr efiadze, na wokunkum binom mpo. Naaso mfe ebien ntamu no, adawurbɔfo no dodow bui bɔɔ ho. Afe 1943 mu no, nna hɔn dodow ehuruw kɔ 1,379. Ɔmmfa ho atseetsee a Adasefo no faa mu nyina ekyir no, Jehovah maa hɔn dodow kɔr enyim.”

Woyii asomafo no fii efiadze no, Jewfo mpanyimfo no kɔr do ara taan hɔn enyi. Naaso woenntum anntɔr asomafo no hɔn ase, mbom no Christianfo no hɔn dodow kɔr do bui bɔɔ ho pii. (Andw. 6:7) Ɔbaa no dɛn? Siantsir no ara nye dɛ nna Nyankopɔn ka hɔn ho, ntsi ɔsoɛrtsia na ɔtaa no nyinara ekyir no wɔkɔr do nyaa nkɔenyim.—Andw. 1:8.

6. Ebɛnadze ntsi na woenntum anntɔr tsetse Christianfo no hɔn ase?

7 Ndɛ nyimpa mpempem pii na wɔaboa hɔnho ano reba Jehovah ne fie, ɔmmfa ho dɛ hɔn ebusuafo, hɔn edwumamufo nye binom rosoɛr tsia hɔn no. Dɛ mbrɛ Jesus kae no, ebɛnadze ntsi na dɛm nkorɔfo yi ‘afa hɔn asɛndua’ asoa? (Matt. 16:24) Siantsir no ara nye dɛ wɔdɔ Jehovah na wɔgye dzi so dɛ, dɛ mbrɛ noara aka no, ɔbɔhwɛ hɔn. Na ampaara so, Jehovah ma hɔn “tum a ɔbor nyimpa dze do” ma wɔdze gyina ɔhaw a worihyia no ano. (2 Cor. 4:7-9) Ntsi sɛ hɔn ebusuafo soɛr tsia hɔn a, nna wɔakaa asɛm a ɔdwontofo bi kae no: ‘Sɛ m’egya na me na dan me gya a, Jehovah bɛfa me.’ (Ndw. 27:10) Sɛ ɔnnyɛ anwanwadze na Jehovah reyɛ a nkyɛ ɔbɛyɛ dɛn na obi ruhu amandze na still araa ɔbɛka dɛ obedzi Jesus n’ekyir.
