18 Ber a ahyɛ dɛ edze bɛyɛ daa asɛm no, bɔ mbɔdzen ara dzi do. Mma mma biribiara nntwetwe w’adwen. Isabelle yɛ akataasiaba bi a oedzi mfe 19. Dza ɔwɔ ka wɔ asɛm yi ho nye dɛ: “Ka kyerɛ woho dɛ sɛ ennyɛɛ wo daa asɛm a, eremmfa wo fon mmiamia anaa erem-mfa nnyɛ biribiara. Sɛ wo fon na edze yɛ daa asɛm, na ehwɛ na ɔbɛgyegye wo a, ɔno enyi ennhu a ɔnnyɛ tan, pɛ dza wɔaprentse no bi, na fa yɛ wo daa asɛm.” Akyerɛbaa bi so a wɔ-frɛ no Olivia a oedzi mfe 19 no so se: “Adze kor a ɔboa paa nye dɛ enye obi bɛyɛ daa asɛm no. Ibotum nye w’ebusuafo anaa w’anyɛnko-fo ayɛ. Osiandɛ sɛ ewɔ obi a ɔbɛkaa wo ma ayɛ daa asɛm no a, ɔremma innygigya nnto mu.”

BOA WO MBA MA WONHU DƐ
ƆSƐ DƐ WƆYƐ DAA ASƐM DA BIARA
<<<P>>>
19 Awofo, sɛ hom nye hom mba yɛ daa asɛm no da biara a, ɔbɛka hɔn hɔ, na wonyin mpo a wɔbɔkɔ do ara ayɛ. Sɛ wo mba no susuar ntsi ɔnnyɛ biribiara na wɔbɛtse ase mpo a, osian-dɛ hom yɛ no da biara ntsi hɔn so wobosua-sua. Sɛ yɛbɛyɛ iyi ho mfatoho a, ɔtse dɛ mbrɛ edze aba ketseketse bi ahyɛ famu. Sɛ da biara igugu do nsu a, obotum enyin abɛyɛ dua kɛse. Dɛmara so na sɛ mbofra so sua dɛ wɔdze mi-nitsi kakraabi bɛyɛ daa asɛm no da biara a, ɔbɔboa hɔn ma wɔabɛyɛ nyimpa a wɔdɔ Jeho-vah.—Kenkan Philippifo 1:9, 10.
<<<P>>>
20 Jesus kaa dɛ: “Ɔnnyɛ paanoo nkotsee na nyimpa nam do bɛtsena ase, na mbom asɛm biara a ofi Jehovah n’anomu so.” (Matt. 4:4) Wɔka dɛ, eboa ho hia na hɛmba so ho hia. Ntsi dɛ mbrɛ yɛmma da kor mpo mmbɛsen a yenndzidzi no, dɛmara so na no ho hia paa dɛ da biara yedzi Nyankopɔn n’Asɛm no dɛ edzi-ban, osiandɛ ɔno na hɛn nkwa ankasa gyina do. Ntsi, bɔ mbɔdzen ara yɛ daa asɛm no da biara.
