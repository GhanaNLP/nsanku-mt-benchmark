MFE ƆHA ETWA MU
<<<P>>>
JANUARY 1, 1926 Ɔweɔn-Aban no kaa dɛ: “Ɔnnkɛkyɛr koraa Nyankopɔn bɛma obiara ehu dɛ ɔno nye otumfo no na obeyi nkorɔfo bi ma woedzi no ho dase.”
<<<P>>>
Afe 1926 mu no, Jehovah nam Jesus Christ a ɔyɛ asafo no ne tsir no do maa no mu daa hɔ maa Christianfo dɛ, asɛnka edwuma no na ɔsɛ dɛ wɔdze dzi kan wɔ hɔn abrabɔ mu. Iyi ntsi, “akowaa nokwafo nye nyansafo no” yɛɛ mbuukuu afofor, na kwan a nna wɔfa do yɛ Christianfo nhyiamu no so, wɔyɛɛ nsesa wɔ mu. Iyinom da nkyɛn a, wɔmaa enuanom no hun dza wɔyɛ a ɔbɛkyerɛ dɛ wɔyɛ Christianfo ampa. (Matt. 24:45) Adan a wɔbɛyɛ mu edwuma nye mfir a wɔdze betsintsim mbuukuu ahorow ma woeetum ayɛ asɛnka edwuma no, Christ hwɛɛ ma hɔn nsa kae.
<<<P>>>
WOYII BUUKUU FOFOR EDZI NA KWAN A WƆFA DO YƐ ASAFO NHYIAMU NO SO, WƆYƐƐ NSESA WƆ MU
<<<P>>>
April bosoom no mu no, The Golden Age a sesei yɛfrɛ no Nyan! no dze too gua dɛ, woeyi buukuu fofor a wɔfrɛ no Deliverance no edzi. Nna ɔno na ɔbɛba ebesi buukuu a wɔato dzin Studies in the Scriptures a wɔdze kɔ asɛnka no ananmu. Mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a enuanom nsa botum aka mbuukuu dodow a wobehia dze akɔ asɛnka no, enuanom a wɔwɔ Bethel no tɔɔ efir bi a wɔdze prentse buukuu. Nna dɛm efir no botum aprentse mbuukuu nkorkor 22 wɔ minitsi kor ntamu. Obiara a nna Bible Esuafo no bedzi no dase no, sɛ ɔbɛyɛ yie a nna wɔma no buukuu no bi.
<<<P>>>
Ansaana afe 1926 reba no, nna Bible Esuafo no sua Studies in the Scriptures buukuu no wɔ adzesua ase. Naaso ofi May 1926 no, wɔhyɛɛ ase suaa Ɔweɔn-Aban no. Ebɛn mfaso na ofi iyi mu bae?
<<<P>>>
Nna asafo kakraba bi wɔ Illinois a wɔfrɛ no Bunker Hill. Wɔkyerɛɛw krataa bi kɛmaa Onua Rutherford dɛ: “Bunker Hill asafo no kyia wo. Sɛ odu Kwesida a, nsɛm a Jehovah asafo no akyerɛ dɛ yensua no, ɔno na yesua. Ɔyɛ hɛn dɛw paa dɛ kan fofor biara ba a, hɛn nyina hu no ntsɛm wɔ Ɔweɔn-Aban no mu.”
<<<P>>>
“Kan fofor” anaa nsakyer fofor a nna ɔba no boaa Bible Esuafo no ma wɔdze hɔn adwen sii asɛnka edwuma no do. Sɛ yɛbɛyɛ iyi ho mfatoho a, Ɔweɔn-Aban adzesua kor kyerɛkyerɛɛ mu dɛ, ɔwɔ mu dɛ ɔyɛ no kwan do dɛ yɛbɛda suban pa edzi, yɛnye nkorɔfo bedzi no yie na yeebu hɔn. Naaso ɔnnsɛ dɛ yebu hɛn enyi gu edwuma a no ho hia paa no do. Dɛm adzesua asɛm no kaa dɛ: “Sɛ obi dɔ Ewuradze a ɔsɛ dɛ . . . odzi ne mbransɛm do. . . . Jehovah ka dɛ: ‘Hom ara nye m’adasefo na emi nye Nyankopɔn.’ . . . Sɛ obi yɛ Christiannyi a, ɔsɛ dɛ Nyankopɔn ho dase a obedzi no yɛ no asɛnhia.”
<<<P>>>
Ɔweɔn-Aban adzesua fofor so sii do dua dɛ, dza nokwar Christianfo yɛ dze kyerɛ dɛ wɔdɔ Jehovah paa nye dɛ, wobefi hɔn akoma nyina mu asom no. Dɛm adzesua asɛm no hyɛɛ Bible Esuafo no nkuran dɛ nsu mu o, ewia mu o, ‘ɔsɛ dɛ wodzi amanaman nyina dase.’—Isa. 43:10, 12; Matt. 24:14.
