Ɛ. George Draper

mokɔr London. Morokɔ nyina nna mayɛ m’adwen dɛ sɛ Onua Rutherford ka dɛ monkɔsom wɔ ɔman fofor do a, moronnwosow me tsir koraa.” Ampa-ara so, okodur London no, Onua Rutherford bisaa no dɛ: “Sɛ yɛka dɛ kɔ India a, ebɔkɔ a?” Nna Edwin so yii no ano dɛ: “Dabɛn na hom pɛ dɛ mokɔ?”
<<<P>>>
Adapɛn ebiasa pɛr ekyir wɔ July mu no, Edwin Skinner na George Wright faa po do kɔr India. Nna Onua Wright yɛ ɔsɛmpakafo a ɔno so bɔ noho mbɔdzen paa. Onua Skinner kɔsomee wɔ Bethel. Ɔsom edwuma a nna wɔyɛ wɔ Afghanistan, Burma (Myanmar), Ceylon (Sri Lanka), India na Persia (Iran) nyina, nna ɔno na ɔhwɛ do. Onua Skinner dze mfe 64 tsenaa India kesii dɛ owiee no som edwuma wɔ asaase do wɔ afe 1990 mu.

AHWƐFO AKWANTUFO DZII ASƐNKA EDWUMA NO ENYIM
<<<P>>>
Ofi bɛyɛ 1894 reba no, nna Jehovah asafo no soma n’ananmusifo ma wɔkɛserasera Bible Esuafo no tse dɛ mbrɛ ndɛ amansin do ahwɛfo yɛ no. Wɔ July 1926 mu no, kwan a nna wɔfa do yɛ hɔn edwuma no sesae. Nkan no, nna wɔdze da kor na wɔdze yɛ edwuma no, naaso dɛm aber yi dze nna wɔdze nda ebien bɔkɔ akɛsera kuw biara. Afei so, nna ɔsɛ dɛ wɔdze ndɔnhwer 100 kɔ asɛn-ka bosoom biara.
<<<P>>>
Onua bi a nna wɔfrɛ no George Draper a nna ɔyɛ ɔhwɛfo kwantufo no kyerɛɛw krataa bi kɛmaa asafo no. Ɔkaa dɛ: “Jehovah n’asomfo tse dɛ mbɔdɔm a wɔweɔn fie bi. Ntsi ɔwɔ dɛ yɛbɔ nkorɔ-fo kɔkɔ ma wohu dɛ esian bi reba. [Isa. 56:10] Ɔyɛ Ɔweɔn-Aban a yebosua no na ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yee-tum ayɛ dɛm, na ɔno nye adze a medze esi m’enyi do.” Afe 1938 mu na Onua Draper wiee no som edwuma wɔ asaase do.

EHYIADZI BI A YƐYƐE WƆ NEW YORK CITY
<<<P>>>
Ehyiadzi akɛse a otwa tun a yɛyɛe wɔ afe no mu no, yɛyɛɛ no October 14-18, 1926 wɔ New York City. Nna Onua Rutherford bɛma ɔkasa bi a n’a-sɛntsir se “Ɔko Kɛse Bi A Ɔreba Wiadze Nyina No Abɛn.” Ndwumakuw a nna wɔkyerɛw dawurbɔ kra-taa mu nsɛm no gyee too mu dɛ wɔbɔbɔ Onua Rutherford ne kasaa no ho dawur. Naaso aka kakra ma ber aso no, hɔn mu pii a nna wɔagye dzin no twee hɔnho.
<<<P>>>
Dza dɛm ndwumakuw no yɛe no, enuanom yɛɛ dɛ wɔbɔbɔ hɔn ekyir. Ntsi Fida, October 15 no, Onua R. J. Martin yii krataaba fofor bi a wɔato dzin Against the People no edzi. Nna dza osi n’e-nyi do ara nye dɛ obeyi ndwumakuw no do ahataw ma obiara ehu, na ɔabɔ Onua Rutherford ne kasaa no ho dawur. Onua Martin kaa dɛ: “Yɛahyɛ ase dze hɛnankasa hɛn mfir reprentse hɛn mbuukuu. Hɛn nuanom a wɔwɔ Bethel no bɛyɛ edwumadzen adzekyee nye adzesaa kesi dɛ wɔbɛprentse nkra-taaba 1,250,000. Yɛbɛma enuanom a wɔwɔ ehyiadzi yi ase no akɛkyekyɛ ama nkorɔfo.” Aber a wɔpoon ehyiadzi no Fida no, enuanom sii mu kɛ-kyekyɛɛ nkrataaba no. Odur ewimbir no, nna wɔa-kyekyɛ nkrataaba no nkorkor 300,000.
<<<P>>>
Memenda na Kwesida so, wɔkyekyɛɛ pii kaa ho na dodow a wɔkyekyɛe no dodow kesii 1,000,000. Ɔno da nkyɛn a, enuanom no yeer hɔnho kyekyɛɛ Deliverance buukuu no 3,783 na wɔkyekyɛɛ mbuu-kuu nkakraba so 35,140.
