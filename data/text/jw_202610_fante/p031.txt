<<<P>>>
Iyi kyerɛ dɛ ɔdɔ a hɛn nuanom da no edzi kyerɛ hɛn no ho nnhia anaa? Ɔnntse dɛm koraa. Yehia dɛm dɔ no. Na dɛmara so na sɛ yehu dɛ hɛn nuanom robɔ bra pa na ɔhyɛ hɛn nkuran a, ɔnnyɛ bɔn. Naaso biribi ntsi sɛ asɛm ba ɔdɔ a hɛn nuanom reda no edzi nye ɔbra pa wɔrobɔ no ho a, Bible mu nokwar a yeesua na yɛagye ato mu no bɛma yɛakɔ do ara atsena nokwar no mu. Ntsi sɛ nyimpa kor anaa ebien bi annyɛ hɔn ndzɛmba yie mpo a, yenyim dɛ nokwar no ara nye yi.
<<<P>>>
Sɛ yɛkenkan Bible no dwendwen ho da biara a, ɔma hɛn adwen si hɛn pi wɔ dza yerusua no ho. Na sɛ hɛn adwen si hɛn pi wɔ dza yerusua no ho a, ɔbɛma yɛagye ato mu so dɛ nokwar no ara nye yi. Sɛ yɛyɛ dɛm a hɛn gyedzi besi pi, na dza nkorɔfo bɛka anaa wɔbɛyɛ no rennhaw hɛn.—Ndw. 16:8; hwɛ adakaba a wɔato dzin “Kɔ Do Tsena Nokwar No Mu.”

Kɔ Do Tsena Nokwar No Mu
<<<P>>>
Fa no dɛ etsena bus mu rutwa kwan tsentsen akɔ beebi. Beebi a erokɔ no yɛ fɛw ntsi ɔyɛ wo dɛw dɛ irutu dɛm kwan no.
<<<P>>>
Naaso sɛ etsena bus mu rutwa kwan tsentsen dɛm a, ndzɛmba pii na obotum ahyɛ wo ebufuw. Binom botum edzi nkɔmbɔ dedeede wɔ bus no mu, anaa mpo dɛ drabanyi no renntsew n’enyim. Sɛ biribi dɛm si a, ɔno kyerɛ dɛ bus no ronnkɔ beebi a erokɔ no ntsi ɔsɛ dɛ isian famu anaa? Oho.
<<<P>>>
Dɛm mfatoho yi fa hɛn ho. Hɛn so, yerutwa kwan akɔ Paradise. Ɔyɛ hɛn mu biara dɛw dɛ yɛtsena Jehovah n’asafo no mu rutwa dɛm kwan no. Naaso ɔtɔ da a, hɛn nuanom binom hɔn ndzeyɛɛ anaa onua bi a odzi ndzɛmba enyim no suban botum ahaw hɛn. Biribi dɛm si a, ɔno kyerɛ dɛ Jehovah n’ahyehyɛdze no rennye hɛn nnkɔ Paradise anaa? Oho, ɔnntse dɛm. Nokwar no ara nye yi. Sɛ yɛgye dza yeesua efi Bible mu no dzi paa na yɛdze hɛn werɛ nyina hyɛ Jehovah n’ahyehyɛdze no mu a, ɔbɛma yɛakɔ do ara atsena nokwar no mu ekesi dɛ yebodu beebi a yɛrokɔ.
