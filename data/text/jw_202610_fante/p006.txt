Kyerɛ Dɛ Inyim Medaase Ka
<<<P>>>
“Hom nda no edzi dɛ hom yɛ nyimpa a wɔkyerɛ ndzɛmba ho enyisɔ.”—COL. 3:15.
<<<P>>>
AKƆKORA bi a nna wɔfrɛ no John* wɔ suban bi a ɔma yehu dɛ sɛ ndzɛmba nnkɔ yie mma hɛn mpo na yehu medaase ka a, dza ɔbra dze bɛma hɛn biara hɛn enyi bɔsɔ na hɛn enyi agye. Nna oedzi mfe 95 na nna ɔyar paa. Osian ne yarba no ntsi nna nkorɔfo na wɔhwɛ no. Nna dza John yɛ nye dɛ, da biara a obi bɛba abɛsera no anaa obi bɛyɛ adze ketseketse bi mpo ama no no ɔka dɛ, “Medaase.” Afei ɔkyerɛw nyimpa no ne dzin wɔ no notebook kakraba bi mu, ntsi sɛ adze sa na ɔrekɛda a nna ɔama obi a ɔrohwɛ no no esuo no mu ama oeetum ebutuw ne mpa ho abɔ mpaa. Afei ɔbɔbɔ obiara a ɔyɛɛ no papa dɛm da no ne dzin wɔ ne mpaabɔ mu na ɔada Jehovah ase. Hɔn a nna wɔhwɛ no no mu kor kaa dɛ, “John nntse apɔw dze, naaso da biara nna n’enyi agye na adze a ebɛyɛ ama no biara so n’enyi sɔ.”
<<<P>>>
2 Ɔyɛ awerɛhow dɛ, ndɛ nyimpa nnyi ayɛw. Iyi nnyɛ nwanwa, osiandɛ Bible no koraa aka ato hɔ dɛ nda a odzi ekyir mu no, nyimpa bɛyɛ bɔnyiayɛ. (2 Tim. 3:1, 2) Mbrɛ ɔtse nye dɛ binom wɔ hɔ a wɔnnhyɛ da ara dɛ sɛ obi yɛ hɔn papa a wɔrennkyerɛ ho enyisɔ, mbom ɔyɛ awerɛfir ara. Naaso nyimpa piinara so wɔ hɔ a iku woho ma hɔn mpo a, wonnhu dɛ ayɛ biribiara ntsi wɔrennkyerɛ ho enyisɔ, wɔrennka dɛ medaase da. Hɔn adwen mu no, ɔyɛ hɔn dɛ papa a ɔsɛ dɛ eyɛ ma hɔn no ara na ayɛ no, ntsi onnyi dɛ wɔda wo ase. Sɛ obi yɛ dɛm a ɔyɛ bɔnyiayɛ, na ɔnnyɛ suban papa.
