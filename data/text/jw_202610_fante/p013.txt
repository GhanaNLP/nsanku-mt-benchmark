60:22.) Dɛm nkɔnhyɛ yi aba mu anaa? Ɔwodze ma yɛnhwɛ ɛ: Afe 1914 mu no, nna Nyankopɔn n’asomfo a wɔwɔ asaase yi do no dodow yɛ bɛyɛ 5,000. Naaso ndɛ, Jehovah n’asomfo a nna wɔyɛ “ekumaba no” ho adɔɔ so papa. Wɔbor 9,000,000 mpo. Yɛdɔɔ so ampa, osiandɛ aman ahorow bɛyɛ 130 bi wɔ hɔ a nyimpa wɔwɔ mu no, wonndu 9,000,000. Nyimpa a wɔdɔ Jehovah na wɔrosom no a hɔn dodow tse dɛm dze, yennhun bi dɛm da! Na wɔtsetse aman bɛyɛ 240 do. Ɔyɛ nokwar, sɛ edze Jehovah n’asomfo toto nyimpa dodow a wɔwɔ asɔrasɔr ahorow mu no ho a, yenndu beebiara. Naaso iyi nnkyerɛ dɛ asɛm a Bible no kaa too hɔ no mmbaa mu, wɔaba mu. “Ekumaba no” adan “apem” ampa.

4 Hɛn Jehovah Adasefo dze yennuma mbɔdomaba mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a hɛn dodow bɔkɔ sor dɛ mbrɛ asɔrasɔr binom yɛ no. Dɛmara so na yɛmmfaa kwan a Nyankopɔn pɛ dɛ yɛfa do bɔ bra no nntoo nkyɛn mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a nkorɔfo pii nye hɛn bɔsom. Mbom no, yeesua Bible no, na hɛn mu pii wɔ hɔ a nna ɔsɛ dɛ yɛsesa mbrɛ yesi susu ndzɛmba ho nye kwan a yɛfa do bɔ hɛn bra no ma ɔnye dza Bible no kyerɛkyerɛ no hyia. (1 Cor. 6:9-11) Afei, osiandɛ yɛayɛ dɛm nsesa ahorow yi ntsi yetumii kaa asɛmpa no bi kyerɛɛ nkorɔfo, mbrɛ ɔbɛyɛ a nyimpa pii botum abɔsom Jehovah. Ekyir yi, yehyiraa hɛnho do maa Jehovah na yɛhyɛɛ no bɔ dɛ ɔmmmfa ho dza obesi biara no dza ɔpɛ nkotsee na yɛbɛyɛ na yenumae.—Matt. 28:19, 20.
