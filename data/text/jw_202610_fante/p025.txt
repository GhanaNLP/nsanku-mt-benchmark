DZA YEBOTUM ESUA EFI BEROEAFO NO HƆ
<<<P>>>
4 Kyerɛwsɛm a hɛn adzesua no gyina do no ma yehu suban a Jewfo bi a wɔwɔ Macedonia kurow Beroea mu no daa no edzi aber a Paul kaa asɛmpa no kyerɛɛ hɔn no. (Kenkan Asomafo Ndwuma 17:10, 11.) Nna Beroeafo no “wɔ adwen pa” paa. Bible no ka dɛ ‘wɔgyee asɛm’ a Paul kae no. Aber a ɔsomafo Paul rekyerɛkyerɛ hɔn no, nna wɔdze dza ɔreka no toto kyerɛwnsɛm no ho. Ntsi wohun dɛ dza Paul rekyerɛkyerɛ no yɛ nokwar. Biribi dɛmara na hɛn so sɛ yɛkɔ asafo nhyiamu anaa ɔmansin na ɔmantɔw ehyiadzi ase a yɛyɛ. Sɛ ɔkasafo no rekenkan kyerɛwsɛm bi a, yebue hɛndze dzi n’ekyir. Yɛyɛ dɛm a ɔma yehu dɛ dza ɔkasafo no reka no nyina yɛ nokwar.
<<<P>>>
5 Dza Beroeafo no yɛe no maa “hɔn mu pii bɛyɛɛ egyedzifo.” (Andw. 17:12) Naaso annkyɛr biara na Jewfo bi a wofi Thessalonica baa Beroea “botutui guu nyimpadɔm no asowa mu na wɔhyɛɛ hɔn ebufuw” dɛ mma wonntsie Paul ne nkyerɛkyerɛ. (Andw. 17:13) Naaso iyi ammbɔ dɛm Christianfo yi hu. Kyerɛwnsɛm a nna wɔhwehwɛ mu da biara no maa wohun dɛ nokwar no ara na woehu no, na ɔmaa hɔn gyedzi yɛɛ dzen. Hɛn so, sɛ yɛhwehwe Kyerɛwnsɛm no mu da biara a, ɔbɔboa hɛn ma yeegyina asetsena mu nsɛndzendzen ano.
<<<P>>>
EBƐNADZE NTSI NA ƆSƐ DƐ YƐYƐ DAA ASƐM NO DA BIARA?
<<<P>>>
6 Sɛ yɛyɛ daa asɛm a ɔboa hɛn ma yɛdze hɛn adwen si Jehovah na ɔsom a yɛdze ma no no do. Jehovah nye hɛn Nyankopɔn, ntsi ɔsɛ dɛ yenya ber ma no na yɛsom no. Ɔdɔ hɛn na ɔpɛ dɛ yenya onnyiewiei nkwa. (John 3:16) Bible no ma yehu dɛ Jehovah pɛ dɛ da biara yɛsom no. Enuanom pii ehu dɛ sɛ wodzi kan yɛ daa asɛm a, ɔboa hɔn ma wɔbɔ mpaa, wɔkenkan Bible no na wɔdwendwen ho.

6. Ebɛnadze ntsi na ɔsɛ dɛ yɛyɛ daa asɛm no da biara? (Hwɛ adakaba a wɔato dzin “Bible Mu Nsɛm A Ɔma Yehu Dɛ Ɔsɛ Dɛ Yɛkenkan Nyankopɔn N’Asɛm No Da Biara.”)
<<<P>>>
Bible Mu Nsɛm A Ɔma Yehu Dɛ Ɔsɛ Dɛ Yɛkenkan Nyankopɔn N’Asɛm No Da Biara
<<<P>>>
● Nna ɔwɔ Moses Mbra no mu dɛ ɔsɛ dɛ wɔbɔ afɔr da biara.—Ex. 29:38-42; 30:7, 8.
<<<P>>>
● Jehovah kaa kyerɛɛ Joshua dɛ ɔnkenkan Mbra nwoma no na ɔndwendwen ho “adzekyee nye adzesaa.”—Josh. 1:8.
<<<P>>>
● Jehovah kaa kyerɛɛ Israel ahemfo no dɛ wɔnhwɛ Nyankopɔn ne Mbra no do nkyerɛw bi na wɔnkenkan no da biara.—Deut. 17:18-20.
<<<P>>>
● Ɔdwontofo no hyɛ hɛn nkuran dɛ yɛmma hɛn enyi ngye Nyankopɔn n’Asɛm no ho, na yɛnkenkan no “adzekyee na adzesaa.” —Ndw. 1:1-3.
<<<P>>>
● Da biara, nna Daniel bɔ mpaa mpɛn ebiasa. —Dan. 6:10.
<<<P>>>
● Nyikyerɛ nwoma no ka nyimpakuw kɛse no ho asɛm dɛ, wɔsom Nyankopɔn “adzekyee nye adzesaa.”—Nyi. 7:15.
