<<<P>>>
Yɛ Daa Asɛm Da Biara
<<<P>>>
‘Wɔgyee asɛm no, na da biara nna wɔhwehwɛ Kyerɛwnsɛm no mu yie.’—ANDW. 17:11.
<<<P>>>
AFE 1905 mu no, Jehovah n’asomfo yɛɛ buukuu bi a wɔfrɛ no Daily Heavenly Manna for the Household of Faith. Nna dɛm buukuu yi ma enuanom no nya kyerɛwsɛm nye nkyerɛkyerɛmu bi a ofi Zion’s Watch Tower no mu kenkan da biara wɔ afe no mu. Jehovah n’asomfo dze dɛm buukuu yi yɛɛ edwuma araa kesii 1927. Dɛm afe no ekyir no, nna wɔdze kyerɛwsɛm nye no ho nkyerɛkyerɛmu no ba Year Book* mu. Ofi dɛm aber no reba no, enuanom nyaa kyerɛwnsɛm ahorow nye nsɛm ahorow pii a ofi Ɔweɔn-Aban no mu dwendween ho.
<<<P>>>
2 Afe 1986 mu no, wɔyɛɛ buukuu kakraba a wɔfrɛ no Daa Asɛm no dɛ yɛmfa nsusu kyerɛwnsɛm no ho da biara. Ndɛ, wɔayɛ dɛm buukuu yi wɔ kasa bɔbor 300 mu. Ibue JW Library® app no a, wo nsa botum aka buukuu yi bi wɔ hɔ. Afei so ibotum enya bi wɔ Watchtower ONLINE LIBRARY™ (wol.jw.org) do.
<<<P>>>
3 Ndɛ adagyer nnyi hɔ, ntsi wɔama binom nntum nnyɛ daa asɛm no da biara. Ɔno ntsi, yɛbɔbɔ dɛm nsɛmbisa ebiasa yi ho nkɔmbɔ wɔ adzesua yi mu. Ebɛn Bible mu asɛm na ɔma yehu dɛ ɔsɛ dɛ yɛyɛ daa asɛm da biara? Ebɛnadze ntsi na ɔsɛ dɛ da biara yɛyɛ daa asɛm? Yebesi dɛn ayɛ daa asɛm no?
<<<P>>>
* Kasa ahorow a nna wonnyi Year Book no, nna wɔdze kyerɛwnsɛm ahorow a daa asɛm no gyina do no ba Ɔweɔn-Aban no mu bosoom biara. Afei wɔkyerɛ beebi a wobenya kyerɛwsɛm no ho nkyerɛkyerɛmu efi.
<<<P>>>
