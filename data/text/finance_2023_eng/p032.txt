“Restoring and Sustaining Macroeconomic Stability and Resilience for Inclusive Growth & Value Addition”
<<<P>>>
76. In 2023, the Police Service will undertake the following:
<<<P>>>
• establish Formed Police Unit (FPUs) in 16 police regions to ensure effective and efficient public order and crowd control;
<<<P>>>
• re-train and retool the existing Crime Combat and Action Units to be proactive in fighting violent crime such as armed robbery;
<<<P>>>
• deploy 1,500 men with 100 vehicles to increase patrol duties in the communities and on the highways to curb the rising spate of armed robbery; and
<<<P>>>
• expand Traffic Surveillance Centres to all the 18 Police Regions to ensure efficient and effective traffic management to reduce indiscipline and carnage on our roads.
<<<P>>>
77. Government will also continue public education campaigns on disaster risk reduction, intensify sensitization programmes on earthquake, strengthen community resilience for early warning by setting up flood information system in selected regions and districts.
<<<P>>>
78. Work on the Phase IV of the Security Services Housing Programme will commence in 2023 for the Ghana Prisons Service.
<<<P>>>
Tourism
<<<P>>>
79. Government under the Ghana Tourism Authority (GTA) will mount an aggressive awareness programme under its ‘See Ghana, Eat Ghana, Wear Ghana and Feel Ghana’ campaign to encourage patronage of made in Ghana products in the tourism and hospitality industry.
<<<P>>>
80. Government will engage with hoteliers and restauranteurs to serve local food and beverages, use local artefacts, furniture and fixtures, in the interior decoration of hospitality outlets in order to reduce the import component of inputs for the operation of businesses in the sector.
