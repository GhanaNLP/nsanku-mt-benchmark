2023 Medium-Term Overall Macroeconomic Targets
<<<P>>>
16. Guided by the medium-term policy objectives, the Government has set following macroeconomic targets for the medium-term (2023-2026):
<<<P>>>
i. Overall Real GDP to grow at an average rate of 4.3 percent;
<<<P>>>
ii. Non-Oil Real GDP to grow at an average rate of 4.0 percent;
<<<P>>>
iii. Headline inflation to be within the target band of 8±2 percent;
<<<P>>>
iv. Primary Balance on Commitment basis to average a surplus 1.3 % of GDP in the 2023-2026 period; and
<<<P>>>
v. Gross International Reserves to cover at least 4 months of imports.
<<<P>>>
