Obaanyɛ Oná Nɔmimaa Akɛ
Yehowa Baabu Ohe
<<<P>>>
“Okɛɛ: ‘Yehowa ji mibobaahe.’”—LALA 91:9.
<<<P>>>
WƆYƐ naagbee gbii lɛ anaagbee gbii lɛ amli. Etsɛŋ, nibii komɛi baaya nɔ ni baatsake je lɛ kwraa. Yehowa kɛ “eyiŋtoo” lɛ baawo jeŋmaji lɛ atsui mli koni amɛkɛ “amɛmaŋtsɛyeli lɛ ahã kooloo awuiyelɔ lɛ,” ni ji Jeŋmaji Ekomefeemɔ lɛ.* (Kpo. 17:17) No sɛɛ lɛ, Jeŋmaji Ekomefeemɔ lɛ kɛ nɔyelii fɛɛ ni fiɔ esɛɛ lɛ baafee ekome kɛkpãtã amale jamɔi fɛɛ ahiɛ kwraa. (Kpo. 17:16) Nifeemɔ nɛɛ baatsɔɔ akɛ amanehulu kpetenkpele lɛ eje shishi. Mɛni baaba yɛ no sɛɛ?
<<<P>>>
2 Nɔyelii ni yɔɔ jeŋ fɛɛ lɛ baafee ekome ni amɛtutua Yehowa webii lɛ. (Eze. 38:14-16) Mɛni hewɔ amɛbaafee nakai? Atsɔɔ mli yɛ wolo ni ji Ato Yehowa Jamɔ Krɔŋŋ Shishi Ekoŋŋ Agbɛnɛ! lɛ mli akɛ: “Satan baawo jeŋmaji lɛ ayiŋ koni amɛfee nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni amɛbaanyɛ kɛbule anɔkwa jálɔi kɛ jamɔ krɔŋŋ fɛɛ shishi kɛje shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ.” No sɛɛ lɛ, nifeemɔ ni he hiaa fe fɛɛ lɛ baaya nɔ; Harmagedon ta lɛ baaje shishi. Yesu kɛ mɛi 144,000 lɛ kɛ ŋwɛibɔfoi lɛ baafee ekome ni amɛhere Nyɔŋmɔ webii lɛ, ni amɛkpãtã efɔŋfeelɔi lɛ ahiɛ. Enɛ kɛ Satan jeŋ fɔŋ lɛ baaba naagbee, ni jeŋ hee lɛ baaje shishi.—Kpo. 16:14-16; 19:11-21.
