LALA 22
Afɔ Kristo Maŋtsɛyeli Lɛ—Hã Eba Shikpɔŋ Nɔ!
<<<P>>>
NƆ NI WƆBAAKASE
Wɔbaana nibii etɛ komɛi ni esa akɛ wɔkwɛ wɔhe nɔ jogbaŋŋ yɛ amɛhe koni je lɛ mumɔ lɛ akaná wɔnɔ hewalɛ.
<<<P>>>
SATAN ji mɔ ni yeɔ jeŋ ni wɔyɔɔ mli nɛɛ nɔ. (Yoh. 14:30; 1 Yoh. 5:19) Mɛi ni ejie ehiɛ eka waa ji Kristo sɛɛnyiɛlɔi lɛ. (Kpo. 12:12, 13, 17) Shi enɛ etsɔɔɔ akɛ Satan diɛŋtsɛ yɛ he fɛɛ he be fɛɛ be ni eete shi eewo wɔ. Yɛ anɔkwale mli lɛ, nɔ ko bɛ Biblia lɛ mli ni tsɔɔ akɛ ŋwɛibɔfo ko, kɛ́ eji ŋwɛibɔfo kpakpa jio, efɔŋ jio, baanyɛ ahi he fɛɛ he be fɛɛ be. Shi Satan yɛ daimonioi babaoo ni kɛ lɛ feɔ ekome kɛtsuɔ nii. (Kpo. 12:9) Agbɛnɛ hu, nɔ ko yɛ ni Satan baanyɛ eketsu nii be fɛɛ be, ni nii nɛɛ yɛ he fɛɛ he. No ji je lɛ mumɔ lɛ. Mumɔ nɛɛ kudɔɔ mɛi babaoo, amɛle jio, amɛleee jio. (Kanemɔ 1 Yohane 2:15, 16.) Mɛi komɛi yɛ ni heee amɛyeee akɛ Satan yɛ, shi shii abɔ ni amɛhãa je lɛ mumɔ lɛ kudɔɔ amɛ lɛ, belɛ amɛmiifee Satan suɔmɔnaa nii ji no. Yɛ gbɛ ko nɔ lɛ, edũ “amɛ koni amɛfee esuɔmɔnaa nii.”—2 Tim. 2:26.
<<<P>>>
² Wɔteŋ mɛi babaoo yɛ ni be ko ni eho lɛ, je lɛ mumɔ lɛ ni kudɔ wɔ. Shi be ni wɔná wɔle nɔ ni je lɛ mumɔ lɛ ji kɛ mɔ ni hɔ sɛɛ lɛ, mɛni wɔfee? Wɔbo kɔkɔ ni Biblia lɛ bɔ wɔ akɛ “wɔtsi wɔhe kɛje bu ni abuuu Nyɔŋmɔ kɛ je lɛŋ akɔnɔi ahe” lɛ toi. (Tito 2:11, 12) Eyɛ mli akɛ Satan he wa waa ni eekudɔ mɛi babaoo yɛ je lɛ mli moŋ, shi wɔhãaa ekudɔ wɔ, ni no tsɔɔ akɛ wɔmiisumɔ ni wɔtsĩ lɛ gbɛ, ni Yehowa hu miiye eebua wɔ. (Yak. 4:7, 8) Wɔ fɛɛ wɔbaanyɛ wɔná nɔmimaa akɛ, Yehowa yɛ wɔhe miishɛɛ waa ejaakɛ wɔkɛ wɔnane ema shi koni je lɛ mumɔ lɛ akaná wɔnɔ hewalɛ!
