niŋmaa.) Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, amɛna bɔ ni Yehowa bu amɛkromɔbii lɛ ahe ni ehãaa agbe amɛ lɛ. Shi amɛnaaa akɛ nɔ ni Yehowa fee lɛ tsɔɔ akɛ esumɔɔ amɛ waa, ni amɛheee amɛyeee akɛ ebaanyɛ ehere amɛyiwala shi kome ekoŋŋ. Yehowa na akɛ amɛheee lɛ amɛyeee. Biblia kɛɛ akɛ, “amɛtse atua . . . yɛ Ŋshɔ Tsuru lɛ naa.” Eyɛ mli akɛ amɛnyiɛ Mose sɛɛ kɛfo Ŋshɔ Tsuru lɛ moŋ, shi amɛtee nɔ amɛgbo Yehowa nɔ toi. Sɛɛ mli lɛ, Yehowa wie akɛ ehãŋ Israelbii ni booo lɛ toi lɛ aya Shiwoo Shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ. (Lala 106:21, 24-26) Mɛni wɔbaanyɛ wɔkase yɛ amɛsane lɛ mli?

6 Israelbii lɛ asane lɛ hãa wɔnaa akɛ, esa akɛ wɔpɛi wɔmli ni wɔkwɛ akɛ wɔbaana nɔ tuuntu hewɔ ni wɔboɔ Yehowa kɛ mɛi ni ehala ni amɛnyiɛ hiɛ lɛ toi lɛ lo. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔbi wɔhe akɛ, “Kɛ́ Yehowa kɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔ ko hã mi lɛ, ani ja mina akɛ maná he sɛɛ dani mikɛtsuɔ nii?” Eenyɛ efee akɛ Israelbii lɛ ekomɛi kɛ lá lɛ kpa amɛshinaatsei lɛ ahe koni akagbe amɛkromɔbii lɛ, shi jeee yɛ sumɔ ni amɛyɔɔ kɛhã Yehowa lɛ hewɔ. Agbɛnɛ hu, wɔbaanyɛ wɔbi wɔhe akɛ, “Ani miboɔ Yehowa toi kɛ́ mɛi krokomɛi efeee nakai aloo amɛmli fu akɛ miifee nakai po? Be ni Israelbii lɛ shiɔ Ejipt lɛ, Yehowa hã amɛhiɛ “ba nyam yɛ Ejiptbii lɛ ahiɛ,” ni Ejiptbii lɛ ke amɛ nibii pii. (2 Mo. 12:36) Shi sɛɛ mli be ni Ejiptbii lɛ tiu amɛ koni amɛgbe amɛ lɛ, Israelbii lɛ eyaheee Yehowa amɛyeee dɔŋŋ. (2 Mo. 14:10-12) Esaaa akɛ wɔfeɔ wɔnii tamɔ Israelbii nɛɛ. Moŋ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔhe Yehowa kɛ mɛi ni ehala ni amɛnyiɛ ewebii lɛ ahiɛ lɛ wɔye. (Kanemɔ Yeremia 23:4.) Kɛ́ akɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔ ko hã wɔ, ni wɔnuuu yiŋtoi fɛɛ ni yɔɔ nakai gbɛtsɔɔmɔ lɛ sɛɛ shishi, loo kɛ́ wɔkɛtsu nii lɛ, ebaahã mɛi ni sumɔɔɔ Yehowa lɛ mli afu po lɛ, wɔbaabo toi ni wɔkɛtsu nii. Mɛni baaye abua wɔ koni wɔkafee wɔnii tamɔ Israelbii lɛ, ni wɔnyɛ wɔjɛ wɔtsuiŋ wɔbo Yehowa toi ni wɔhe lɛ wɔye be fɛɛ be?

7 Esa akɛ wɔkai akɛ, kɛ́ wɔsumɔɔ Yehowa waa lɛ no baahã wɔbo lɛ toi. Kɛ́ Yehowa asafo lɛ kɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔ ko hã wɔ ni wɔnaaa sɛɛnamɔ ni yɔɔ he, loo wɔna akɛ kɛ́ wɔkɛtsu nii lɛ ebaahã etɔ wɔ lɛ, ekolɛ bei komɛi lɛ wɔbaanu he akɛ ekɛ nitsumɔ baawa. Kɛ́ wɔnu he nakai lɛ, esa akɛ wɔkai akɛ, wɔsumɔɔ Yehowa ni wɔheɔ lɛ wɔyeɔ, ni no ji yiŋtoo titri hewɔ ni wɔboɔ lɛ toi lɛ. (1 Yoh. 5:3) Akɛni wɔsumɔɔ Yehowa hewɔ lɛ, wɔboɔ nɔ ni ekɛɔ wɔ lɛ toi ni wɔkpɛlɛɔ nɔ akɛ, kɛ́ bianɛ lɛ, wɔnaaa bɔ ni wɔbaaná nɔ ni ekɛɔ wɔ lɛ he sɛɛ wɔhã po lɛ, wɔsɛɛ lɛ wɔbaana. —Jaj. 7:8; Mika 7:7.

8 Ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, wɔnyɛmimɛi babaoo efee enɛ he nɔkwɛmɔnɔ kpakpa. Amɛkɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔi ni akɛhãa amɛ lɛ tsuɔ nii kɛ́ yɛ shishijee lɛ amɛnaaa bɔ ni amɛbaaná he sɛɛ amɛhã po. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, be ni COVID-19 hela lɛ mɔmɔ mɛi babaoo lɛ, Gbɛtsɔɔmɔ kuu lɛ kɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔ hã akɛ wɔtsɔ videoconferencing nɔ wɔfee wɔsɔfoŋ kpeei lɛ. Asafoŋ onukpa ko ni yɔɔ Peru lɛ wie akɛ: “Shishijee mli lɛ, miyasusu akɛ he ni wɔyɔɔ lɛ, kɛ́ wɔkɛɛ wɔbaatsɔ videoconferencing nɔ wɔfee asafoŋ kpeei lɛ, eyeŋ omanye.” Eyɛ mli akɛ enuuu gbɛtsɔɔmɔ ni akɛhã lɛ lɛ shishi moŋ, shi etswa efai shi akɛ ebaabo nɔ ni Yehowa kɛɔ lɛ lɛ toi ni ekɛ ehiɛ afɔ̃ enɔ. (Abɛi 3:5, 6) Mɛni jɛ mli kɛba? Ewie akɛ, “Nɔ ko ni mikase ji akɛ kɛ́ wɔbo Yehowa toi lɛ, ejɔɔ wɔ.
