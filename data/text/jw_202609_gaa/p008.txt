LALA 36
Wɔbuɔ Wɔtsuii Ahe
<<<P>>>
NƆ NI WƆBAAKASE
Wɔbaana nibii ni baaye abua wɔ ni wɔjɛ wɔtsuiŋ wɔbo Yehowa toi.
<<<P>>>
“Hã otsulɔ toiboo tsui.”—1 MAŊ. 3:9.
<<<P>>>
MƐI pii yeɔ famɔi kɛ mlai ni awo lɛ anɔ ejaakɛ amɛle akɛ hiŋmɛi yɛ amɛnɔ, loo amɛmiishe gbeyei akɛ kɛ́ amɛfeee nakai lɛ, abaagbala amɛtoi. Mɛi komɛi hu yeɔ famɔi kɛ mlai ni awo lɛ anɔ koni amɛkɛsa mɛi ahiɛ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, jamɔŋ hiɛnyiɛlɔi ni hi shi yɛ Yesu beiaŋ lɛ kɛɛ amɛyeɔ Nyɔŋmɔ mlai lɛ anɔ, shi yɛ anɔkwale mli lɛ amɛgba afa kwraa. Nɔ ni he hiaa amɛ nɔŋŋ ji ni mɛi ana amɛ akɛ mɛi ni feɔ nɔ ni ja. (Mat. 23:5) Shi mɛni hewɔ wɔboɔ Yehowa toi lɛ? Wɔboɔ lɛ toi ejaakɛ wɔle akɛ kɛ́ wɔkɛ nɔ ni ekɛɔ wɔ lɛ tsu nii lɛ, wɔ diɛŋtsɛ wɔbaaná he sɛɛ. (Yes. 48:17, 18) Jeee enɛ ji yiŋtoo pɛ hewɔ ni wɔboɔ Yehowa toi. Naa yiŋtoo kroko ni he hiaa waa hewɔ ni wɔboɔ Yehowa toi.
<<<P>>>
2 Wɔsumɔɔ Yehowa kɛ wɔtsui fɛɛ, ni enɛ ji yiŋtoo ni he hiaa waa hewɔ ni wɔboɔ lɛ toi lɛ. (Mat. 22:37, 38) Suɔmɔ nɛɛ tsirɛɔ wɔ ni wɔfee nɔ ni sa ehiɛ be fɛɛ be. (Rom. 6:17) Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, kɛ́ mɛi miikwɛ wɔ jio, mɛi kwɛɛɛ wɔ jio, wɔna sɛɛnamɔi ni baajɛ mli kɛba jio, wɔnaaa sɛɛnamɔi ni baajɛ mli kɛba jio lɛ, wɔboɔ Yehowa toi. Akɛni wɔyeee emuu hewɔ lɛ, bei komɛi lɛ wɔnuɔ he akɛ nɔ ni Yehowa kɛɔ wɔ lɛ feemɔ baawa fioo. Yɛ nikasemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaasusu saji enyɔ komɛi ni je kpo yɛ Biblia lɛ mli ni baaye abua wɔ ni wɔbo Yehowa toi be fɛɛ be lɛ ahe. Klɛŋklɛŋ lɛ, wɔbaasusu bɔ ni Israelbii lɛ fee amɛnii amɛhã be ni Yehowa jie amɛ kɛje Ejipt lɛ he. No sɛɛ lɛ, wɔbaasusu nɔ ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔkase kɛjɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ kpakpa ni Maŋtsɛ Yehoshafat fee lɛ mli lɛ he.
