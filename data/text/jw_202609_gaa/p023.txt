11 Yehowa miisumɔ ni ekɛ wɔteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ abɔ naanyo waa. Kɛjɛ be ni Adam kɛ Hawa tɔ̃ nɛɛ, gbɛ agbo ebakã adesai kɛ Nyɔŋmɔ teŋ. Shi Nyɔŋmɔ miisumɔ waa ni ejie gbɛ nɛɛ kɛje jɛmɛ kwraa. Yehowa miisumɔ ni wɔnyɛ wɔtsi wɔbɛŋkɛ lɛ yɛ sɔlemɔ mli, wɔná ehiɛ dromɔ, ni wɔkɛ lɛ abɔ naanyo waa. Agbɛnɛ hu, eesumɔ ni wɔye wɔhe kɛje esha dɛŋ, ni wɔhi shi kɛya naanɔ. (2 Kor. 5:18) Yehowa le akɛ, Adam kɛ Hawa ejɛ hewɔ afɔɔ wɔ yɛ esha mli lɛ, shi jeee wɔ ejɛ. Emusuŋ tsɔɔ lɛ yɛ wɔhewɔ, ni no hewɔ eto gbɛjianɔ ni ejie gbɛ agbo ni ebakã wɔ kɛ lɛ teŋ lɛ kɛje jɛmɛ koni wɔnyɛ wɔkɛ lɛ abɔ naanyo lɛ. Enɛ hãa wɔsumɔɔ lɛ waa, aloo jeee nakai?

12 Yesu hu miisumɔ ni wɔkɛ Yehowa abɔ naanyo waa. Biblia lɛ hãa wɔleɔ akɛ, “wɔyɛ yelikɛbualɔ ko yɛ Tsɛ lɛ ŋɔɔ,” ni lɛ ji Yesu Kristo, wɔ-Sɔfonukpa lɛ. Kɛ́ wɔtɔ̃ lɛ, ekpaa fai ehãa wɔ. (1 Yoh. 2:1; kanemɔ Hebribii 7:25.) Mɛni ji nibii komɛi ni ekolɛ Yesu baakwɛ dani ekpa fai ehã bo? Ekolɛ, ebaakwɛ bɔ ni atsɔse bo ahã, ogbɔmɔtsoŋ hewalɛnamɔ, nibii ni haoɔ bo, loo onaagbai krokomɛi. Agbɛnɛ hu, ekolɛ ebaakwɛ osui kpakpai lɛ, nibii kpakpai fɛɛ ni ofeɔ kɛ bɔ ni oye Yehowa anɔkwa ohã. Kɛfata he lɛ, akɛni Yesu naa otsui mli hu hewɔ lɛ, ebaasusu bɔ ni osɔle shii abɔ kɛbi Yehowa ni ekɛ ohe eshai ake bo, kɛ bɔ ni oobɔ mɔdɛŋ waa ni ofee nɔ ni ja lɛ he. Akɛni Yehowa mli hi ni
