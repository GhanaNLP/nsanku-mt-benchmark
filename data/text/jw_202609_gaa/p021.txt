agbɛfaŋ” lɛ. Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, amɛbaanyɛ amɛjá Yehowa eyɛ mli akɛ eshafeelɔi ji amɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, osɔfonukpa lɛ kɛ osɔfoi lɛ bafee tamɔ briji ko ni mɛi ni sumɔɔ Yehowa lɛ baanyɛ atsɔ nɔ kɛya eŋɔɔ.

4 Eyɛ mli akɛ Israelbii lɛ ji eshafeelɔi moŋ, shi mɛɛ gbɛ nɔ osɔfoi lɛ ye amɛbua amɛ koni amɛnyɛ amɛbɛŋkɛ Yehowa ni amɛsɔmɔ lɛ? Amɛye amɛbua amɛ yɛ gbɛi titrii enyɔ anɔ. Klɛŋklɛŋ lɛ, osɔfoi lɛ ji Nyɔŋmɔ najiaŋdamɔlɔi yɛ Israelbii lɛ ahiɛ ejaakɛ amɛsɔmɔ akɛ tsɔɔlɔi. Mɛni hewɔ wɔkɛɔ nakai? Awie yɛ Maleaki 2:7 lɛ akɛ: “Esa akɛ osɔfo naabu abaa nilee yi, ni lɛ edaaŋ esa akɛ gbɔmɛi atao mla kɛjɛ, ejaakɛ Yehowa, taiatsɛ lɛ, tsulɔ ji lɛ.” No hewɔ lɛ, osɔfoi lɛ anitsumɔ eko ji ni amɛtsɔɔ Israelbii lɛ Mose Mla lɛ, amɛye amɛbua amɛ ni amɛnu shishi, ni amɛye nɔ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, be ko lɛ, Ezra, ni eji osɔfo lɛ, kane Mose Mla lɛ ehã maŋbii lɛ, ni ekɛ Levibii 13 komɛi fee ekome koni amɛye amɛbua maŋbii lɛ ni amɛnu nɔ ni akane ahã amɛ lɛ shishi. Amɛgbála mli yɛ gbɛ ni shishinumɔ waaa kwraa nɔ koni maŋbii lɛ anu shishi. Enɛ ye ebua maŋbii lɛ ni amɛnyɛ amɛfee nɔ ni Yehowa miisumɔ ni amɛfee lɛ, ni no hã amɛtsi amɛbɛŋkɛ Yehowa waa.—Neh. 8:2, 3, 6-8.
<<<P>>>
5 Nɔ ni ji enyɔ lɛ, osɔfoi lɛ ji Israelbii lɛ anajiaŋdamɔlɔi yɛ Nyɔŋmɔ hiɛ ejaakɛ amɛshã afɔlei amɛhã maŋbii lɛ koni Yehowa kɛ maŋbii lɛ ahe eshai ake amɛ. Israelbii lɛ le akɛ daa gbi lɛ amɛtɔ̃ɔ babaoo. No hewɔ lɛ, amɛle akɛ ehe baahia ni aye abua amɛ koni Yehowa kɛ amɛhe eshai lɛ ake amɛ. (Lala 51:9) Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, Yehowa hã amɛ osɔfoi koni osɔfoi lɛ ashã afɔlei amɛhã amɛ. Ekã shi faŋŋ akɛ, Israelbii ni etsake amɛtsui lɛ atsui nyɔ amɛmli akɛ Yehowa kɛ amɛhe eshai eke amɛ. Gbɛjianɔtoo nɛɛ hãa wɔnaa akɛ Yehowa sumɔɔ ewebii lɛ, eesumɔ ni eye ebua amɛ ni amɛná miishɛɛ, ni amɛtsɔmɔ enanemɛi kɛya naanɔ.
