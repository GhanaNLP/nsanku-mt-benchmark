10 Nyɛhãa wɔkwɛa bɔ ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔkɛ wɔwoji lɛ kɛ JW Library app lɛ atsu nii kɛshiɛ wɔhã mɔ ko yɛ wiemɔ kroko mli. Naa niiashikpamɔ ko ni nyɛmi nuu ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Robert ni wieɔ Blɔfo lɛ ná. Be ko be ni eeshiɛ yɛ lɛjiadaamɔhe ko lɛ, ekɛ nuu ko ni jɛ Türkiye ni tsuɔ nii yɛ meele ko mli lɛ kpe. Nuu lɛ nuuu Blɔfo tsɔ, ni no hewɔ lɛ, Robert hã lɛ broshuɔ ni ji Sane Kpakpa Ni Jɛ Nyɔŋmɔ Ŋɔɔ! ni yɔɔ Türkiye wiemɔ mli. Robert le broshuɔ nɛɛ mli nikasemɔ lɛ jogbaŋŋ, no hewɔ lɛ, ebi nuu lɛ ni ekane kukuji pɔtɛɛ komɛi yɛ broshuɔ lɛ mli yɛ Türkiye wiemɔ mli. Nuu ni jɛ Türkiye lɛ bi saji yɛ nibii ni miiya nɔ yɛ je lɛ mli lɛ ahe. No hewɔ lɛ, Robert kɛ JW Library app lɛ tsu nii ni ejɛ Biblia lɛ mli ehã sanebimɔi lɛ ahetoo yɛ Türkiye wiemɔ mli. Nuu lɛ kɛɛ Robert akɛ: “Eyɛ naakpɛɛ waa akɛ oohã misanebimɔi lɛ fɛɛ hetoo yɛ mi diɛŋtsɛ miwiemɔ mli tsɛbelɛ onuuu miwiemɔ lɛ!” Robert wie akɛ: “Be ni wɔkɛ wɔsanegbaa lɛ baa naagbee lɛ, nuu lɛ enu sane ni yɔɔ Biblia lɛ mli lɛ shishi jogbaŋŋ fe tsutsu lɛ ni ena akɛ, Biblia lɛ ji wolo ko ni he hiaa waa.” Eŋɔɔ wɔnaa waa akɛ, wɔnyɛɔ wɔyeɔ wɔbuaa mɛi yɛ amɛ diɛŋtsɛ amɛwiemɔ mli koni amɛbale Yehowa jogbaŋŋ, ni amɛnu saji ni yɔɔ Biblia lɛ mli lɛ hu ashishi!

Abobalɔ Ko Kase Yehowa He Nii
<<<P>>>
Youa ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Youa lɛ eyanáaa shihilɛ lɛ mlɛo kwraa. Ehe bahia ni eshi emaŋ ni eya United States ni eyaba abo. No mli lɛ, enuuu Blɔfo. Ewieɔ Hmong. Agbɛnɛ hu, ewu eŋmɛɛɛ lɛ gbɛ ni ekane wolo ko kwraa ni Biblia lɛ po fata he. Ewie akɛ, “Mináaa toinjɔlɛ, ni mibɛ miishɛɛ hu.” Gbi ko lɛ, Yehowa Odasefonyo ko ni wieɔ Blɔfo lɛ hã lɛ trati ni ji Shihilɛ Yɛ Toinjɔlɛ Jeŋ Hee Ko Mli yɛ Hmong wiemɔ mli. Youa kase nibii kpakpai ni Nyɔŋmɔ ewo shi yɛ Biblia lɛ mli akɛ ebaafee wɔsɛɛ lɛ he nii. No mli lɛ, enuko shiwoi nɛɛ pɛŋ. Nɔ ni ekase yɛ trati lɛ mli lɛ hã emii shɛ ehe waa. Ehã ená hiɛnɔkamɔ akɛ wɔsɛɛ lɛ eshihilɛ lɛ baahi. Ekɛ trati lɛ to kɛntɛŋ ko mli afii pii. Yɛ be kɛ beiaŋ lɛ, ejuɔ shi ni ekaneɔ trati lɛ. Be ni be shwie mli lɛ, Youa biyei lɛ ateŋ mɔ kome batsɔ Yehowa Odasefonyo ni ekɛ Youa tee kpee ko ni afee yɛ Hmong wiemɔ mli. No sɛɛ lɛ, akɛ Youa bɔi Biblia lɛ kasemɔ. Ekã shi faŋŋ akɛ, trati ni afee yɛ ewiemɔ lɛ mli lɛ ye ebua lɛ ni ekase Yehowa he nii ni ebɔi lɛ jamɔ.
