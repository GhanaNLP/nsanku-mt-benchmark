16 Wɔbaanyɛ wɔkase nɔ ko yɛ nyɛmi nuu ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Robert lɛ sane lɛ hu mli. Akɛni Robert kɛ nanemɛi gbohii bɔ yɛ skul ni ekɛ ehe wo hiɛtserɛjiemɔ gbohii amli hewɔ lɛ, ebɔi shɛremɔ kɛ daanumɔ, ni ekɛ hii náa bɔlɛ. Ewie akɛ: “Minu he akɛ miye mihe ejaakɛ mi-feɔ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni misumɔɔ.” Shi be ni be shwie mli lɛ, Robert na akɛ eyeko ehe kwraa, moŋ lɛ, etsɔ nyɔŋ ehã je lɛ. (2 Pet. 2:19) Mɛɛ tsa-kemɔi Robert fee? Ewie akɛ: “Mikɛ mɛi ni su-mɔɔ Yehowa lɛ pɛ bɔ naanyo. Mitsake bɔ ni mifeɔ minii mihãa, bɔ ni mihiɛɔ mihe mihãa kɛ bɔ ni miwieɔ mihãa.” Te Robert nuɔ he ehãa tɛŋŋ bianɛ? Ewie akɛ: “Gbi fɛɛ gbi ni mate shi lɛ, mináa miishɛɛ akɛ miiba mijen jo-gbaŋŋ. Esoro mishihilɛ nɛɛ kwraa yɛ bɔ ni miji tsutsu ko lɛ he. Enɛ hãa mináa miishɛɛ waa.”

17 Mɛni wɔkase yɛ nikasemɔ nɛɛ mli? Esa akɛ wɔkwɛ jogbaŋŋ koni je lɛ mumɔ lɛ akaku-dɔ wɔ. Enɛ baabi ni wɔtsi wɔhe kɛje wiemɔi ni esaaa kɛ nibii ni Yehowa nyɛɔ lɛ ahe, ni wɔ-kwɛ wɔhe nɔ jogbaŋŋ yɛ nibii gbohii ni yɔɔ In-tanɛt lɛ nɔ lɛ ahe. Je lɛ mumɔ lɛ náa mɛi anɔ hewalɛ waa, shi esaaa akɛ osheɔ gbeyei. Obaanyɛ ohala akɛ obaafee nɔ ni ja, ejaakɛ Yehowa he wa kwraa fe Satan kɛ ejeŋ fɔŋ lɛ. (Kanemɔ 1 Yohane 4:4.) Agbɛnɛ hu, Yeho-wa baahã bo emumɔ krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ, ni ji hewalɛ ni fe fɛɛ lɛ, ni eye ebua bo koni je lɛ mumɔ lɛ akaná onɔ hewalɛ. (Luka 11:13) No hewɔ lɛ, nyɛhãa wɔ fɛɛ wɔtswaa wɔfai shi akɛ wɔbaaya nɔ ‘wɔnyiɛ yɛ Nyɔŋmɔ mumɔ lɛ naa,’ ni wɔtsĩ je lɛ mumɔ lɛ naa!—Gal. 5:16.
