15. Mɛni okaseɔ yɛ Gloria sane lɛ mli? (Kwcmo mfoniri lɛ hu.)
<<<P>>>
Nyɛmi yoo ko ni atsɛɔ lɛ Gloria* lɛ kɛ shihilɛ ko ni tamɔ nɛkɛ kpe. Ekase Yehowa he nii be ni eji gbekɛ fioo, shi ehã mɛi ná enɔ hewalɛ. Ewie akɛ: “Mishwe ni maná nii babaoo ni mahe gbɛ́i. No hewɔ lɛ, mibɔi asafoŋ kpeei amli folɔmɔ. Ebashɛ he ko ni misumɔɔɔ mahi shi akɛ Odasefonyo dɔŋŋ.” Be ni be shwie mli lɛ, Gloria bana akɛ je lɛ mumɔ lɛ ni hã eshwe akɛ ená nii ni ehe gbɛ́i lɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, mɛni efee? Ewie akɛ: “Mikase Biblia lɛ waa fe tsutsu lɛ, misɔle waa, ni mikɛ mɛi ni sɔmɔɔ Yehowa lɛ kpa naanyobɔɔ. Eyɛ mli akɛ bianɛ lɛ mibɛ shika pii moŋ, shi miji niiatsɛ yɛ Yehowa hiɛ.
