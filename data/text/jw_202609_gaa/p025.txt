18 Kɛbashi bianɛ lɛ, nɔ ni wɔkase lɛ ehã wɔna akɛ Yehowa sumɔɔ wɔ waa, enuɔ bɔ ni wɔnuɔ he wɔhãa lɛ shishi, ekɛ wɔhe eshai keɔ wɔ, ni eyeɔ ebuaa wɔ. Satan ji mɔ ni be fɛɛ be nɛ lɛ eetao nɔ ko gbonyo yɛ wɔhe, shi Yehowa bɛ nakai kwraa. (Kpo. 12:10) Nikasemɔ nɛɛ ehã wɔna akɛ Yehowa efee nibii pii ehã wɔ koni wɔnyɛ wɔtsi wɔbɛŋkɛ lɛ, wɔná nɔmimaa akɛ wɔbaanyɛ wɔsa ehiɛ, ni wɔtsɔmɔ enanemɛi. Te enɛ hãa onuɔ he ohãa tɛŋŋ yɛ Yehowa he? Ani ehãa onuɔ he akɛ esumɔɔ bo, ni eesumɔ waa ni ekɛ ohe eshai ake bo?

19 Bei komɛi lɛ, ekolɛ wɔnuɔ he akɛ wɔsaaa mɛi ni esa akɛ Yehowa asumɔ amɛ. Shi nikasemɔ nɛɛ ewa wɔ ni wɔná nɔmimaa akɛ Yehowa nuɔ wɔ shishi ni ele akɛ emuu ni wɔyeee lɛ hewɔ wɔnuɔ he nakai. Ele akɛ sũ ji wɔ. (Lala 103:14) Fɛɛ sɛɛ lɛ, ekɛ ejwɛŋmɔ eyaaa wɔtɔmɔi anɔ, moŋ lɛ, ekɛ ejwɛŋmɔ maa nibii ni wɔfeɔ lɛ jogbaŋŋ, nɔ gbɔmɛi ni wɔmiitao wɔtsɔmɔ, kɛ nɔ gbɔmɛi ni wɔbaatsɔmɔ ké wɔye emuu lɛ anɔ. Ké wɔnaaa nibii kpakpai yɛ wɔhe, shi wɔtɔmɔi sɔŋŋ wɔnaa lɛ, esa akɛ wɔkai akɛ Yehowa “da fe wɔtsuii, ni ele nɔ fɛɛ nɔ.”—1 Yoh. 3:20.

20 Tamɔ bɔ ni wɔna yɛ nikasemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ, Yehowa to gbɛjianɔ koni wɔná osɔfoi koni wɔnyɛ wɔsɔmɔ lɛ eye mli akɛ eshafeelɔi ji wɔ. Wɔna akɛ efee nakai ejaakɛ esha ni yɔɔ wɔhe lɛ hewɔ lɛ, gbɛ bakã wɔ kɛ lɛ teŋ. Shi akɛni eesumɔ waa ni wɔtsi wɔbɛŋkɛ lɛ hewɔ lɛ, eto gbɛjianɔ nɛɛ ni bafeɔ tamɔ briji ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔtsɔ nɔ wɔtsi wɔbɛŋkɛ lɛ lɛ. Esa akɛ wɔkɛ wɔnifeemɔ atsɔɔ akɛ wɔhiɛ esɔ nɔ ni efee lɛ, ni wɔfee nɔ ni Hebribii 4:16 lɛ kɛɔ wɔ ni wɔfee lɛ. Jɛmɛ kɛɛ: “No hewɔ lɛ, nyɛhãa wɔkɛ wiemɔ ni awieɔ kɛ nɔmimaa abɛŋkɛa dromɔ sɛi lɛ, koni wɔnine ashɛ mɔbɔnalɛ kɛ dromɔ ni baaye abua wɔ yɛ be ni sa mli lɛ nɔ.”
