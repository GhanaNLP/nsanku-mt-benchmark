12 Jeee wɔyinɔ abɔi Biblia lɛ shishitsɔɔmɔ.* Biblia shishitsɔɔmɔ jeee nitsumɔ bibioo. Mɛi ni tsɔɔ Biblia lɛ shishi lɛ feɔ nakai ejaakɛ amɛmiisumɔ ni mɛi fɛɛ akane Nyɔŋmɔ wiemɔ lɛ yɛ amɛ diɛŋtsɛ amɛwiemɔ mli. Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, wɔteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ baanyɛ akane Biblia lɛ yɛ wiemɔ ni enuɔ shishi lɛ mli. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, atsɔɔ Ŋmalɛ Krɔŋkrɔŋ Lɛ—Jeŋ Hee Shishitsɔɔmɔ lɛ shishi kɛtee wiemɔi babaoo amli ni enɛ eye ebua Nyɔŋmɔ webii ni yɔɔ jeŋ fɛɛ lɛ koni amɛkase anɔkwalei ni yɔɔ Nyɔŋmɔ wiemɔ lɛ mli lɛ. Nyɛmi nuu ko ni sɔmɔɔ akɛ asafoŋ onukpa yɛ Nepal lɛ wie yɛ Jeŋ Hee Shishitsɔɔmɔ lɛ he akɛ: “Kɛ́ mikane Biblia shishitsɔɔmɔ nɛɛ yɛ miwiemɔ mli lɛ, minuɔ he tamɔ nɔ ni Yehowa kɛ mi miiwie tɛ̃ɛ. Enɛ ehã naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ mi kɛ Yehowa teŋ lɛ mli ewa waa, ni ewaje mihemɔkɛyeli lɛ hu.” Atsɔɔ Jeŋ Hee Shishitsɔɔmɔ muu lɛ fɛɛ loo efã ko shishi kɛtee wiemɔi 300 kɛ sɛɛ amli. Jeŋ Hee Shishitsɔɔmɔ lɛ ja ni eshishinumɔ hu waaa, ni etsɔɔ Yehowa he anɔkwalei. No hewɔ lɛ, Yehowa webii ni yɔɔ jeŋ fɛɛ lɛ yɛ hemɔkɛyeli kome, ni amɛshiɛɔ sane kome too lɛ nɔŋŋ amɛhãa mɛi fɛɛ ekɔɔɔ he eko wiemɔ ni amɛwieɔ. (1 Kor. 1:10) Shi Jeŋ Hee Shishitsɔɔmɔ lɛ he yɛ sɛɛnamɔ waa yɛ yiŋtoi krokomɛi hu ahewɔ. Nyɛhãa wɔkwɛa.

* Aaafee afii 2,200 ni eho nɛ lɛ, atsɔɔ Hebri Ŋmalɛ lɛ shishi kɛtee Greek wiemɔ mli. Enɛ ji be klɛŋklɛŋ kwraa ni atsɔɔ Biblia lɛ fã ko shishi kɛtee wiemɔ kroko mli.

13 Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛ́i lɛ bɛ Bibliai shishitsɔɔmɔi pii ni yɔɔ ŋmɛnɛ lɛ amli. Shi ejeɔ kpo shii akpei abɔ yɛ Jeŋ Hee Shishitsɔɔmɔ lɛ mli. Aaafee afii oha ni eho nɛ lɛ, mɛi naa gbɛ́i Yehowa lɛ yɛ Bibliai pii amli. Bei pii lɛ, amɛnaa gbɛ́i nɛɛ yɛ Hebri Ŋmalɛ lɛ mli. Shi osɔfoi ni yɔɔ ŋmɛnɛ lɛ ateŋ mɛi babaoo sumɔɔɔ ni amɛkɛ gbɛ́i Yehowa lɛ tsuɔ nii, ni amɛsumɔɔɔ hu ni mɛi ale Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛ́i lɛ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, Katolik osɔfoi lɛ ejie Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛ́i lɛ kɛje amɛ-Bibliai lɛ kɛ woji krokomɛi amli.* Sɔlemɔi krokomɛi lɛ hu ejie Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛ́i lɛ kɛje amɛ-Bibliai lɛ amli. Shi yɛ Jeŋ Hee Shishitsɔɔmɔ lɛ mli lɛ, akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛ́i lɛ ewo he fɛɛ he ni esa akɛ ana yɛ lɛ. Ehe miihia waa ni mɛi ale Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛ́i lɛ. Nyɛhãa wɔkwɛa nɔ hewɔ.
<<<P>>>
14 Biblia lɛ hãa wɔnaa akɛ, ehe hiaa waa ni wɔle Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛ́i lɛ ni wɔkɛtsu nii. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, aŋma yɛ Lala wolo lɛ mli akɛ, esa akɛ ‘wɔjie Yehowa gbɛ́i lɛ yi.’ (Lala 135:1) Be ko be ni Yesu miitsɔɔ ekaselɔi lɛ bɔ ni asɔleɔ lɛ, ewie akɛ: “Ogbɛ́i lɛ he atse.” Ni be kroko hu be ni eesɔle lɛ, ewie akɛ: “Mihã amɛle ogbɛ́i lɛ.” (Mat. 6:9; Yoh. 17:26) Gbalɔ Yoel kɛ bɔfo Paulo fɛɛ ŋma akɛ: “Mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni boɔ tsɛɔ Yehowa gbɛ́i lɛ, abaahere eyiwala.” (Rom. 10:13) Akɛni Biblia shishitsɔɔlɔi pii ejie Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛ́i lɛ kɛje Biblia lɛ mli hewɔ lɛ, mɛi babaoo leee akɛ ehe miihia ni amɛle Nyɔŋmɔ gbɛ́i lɛ ni amɛkɛtsu nii.
