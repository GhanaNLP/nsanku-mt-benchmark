ni Yesu hã Satan be ni eka lɛ ni etsɛ wiemɔi ni yɔɔ Lala 91 lɛ yisɛɛ lɛ. (Kanemɔ Mateo 4:5-7.) Yɛ kaa nɛɛ mli lɛ, Satan kɛɛ Yesu ni etu kɛjɛ sɔlemɔ shĩa lɛ afabaŋ lɛ nɔ kɛya shikpɔŋ, koni ŋwɛibɔfoi lɛ abu ehe koni ekaye ehe awui ejaakɛ nakai ji nɔ ni Nyɔŋmɔ ewo shi yɛ Lala 91:11, 12 lɛ. Kadimɔ akɛ, be ni Yesu hãa lɛ hetoo lɛ, ewieee akɛ: ‘Satan, otɔ̃! Nyɔŋmɔ woko shi yɛ Lala nɛɛ mli akɛ ebaabu mihe koni mikaye mihe awui.’ Moŋ lɛ, Yesu kɛɛ lɛ akɛ: “Kaaka Yehowa, O-Nyɔŋmɔ lɛ.” Mɛni nɔ ni Yesu wie lɛ tsɔɔ wɔ?

⁹ Yesu le akɛ, yɛ blema lɛ, be ni Yehowa tsuji lɛ ahenyɛlɔi ka akɛ amɛbaaye amɛ awui lɛ, Yehowa hã ŋwɛibɔfoi babu etsuji lɛ ahe. (2 Maŋ. 6:15-17; 19:34, 35; Dan. 3:19, 20, 26-28; 6:16-22) Shi Yesu le akɛ kɛ́ eye buului asane lɛ, esaaa akɛ ekpaa gbɛ akɛ Yehowa baabu ehe. No hewɔ lɛ, eyɛ mli akɛ, be ni Satan tsɛ Lala 91 lɛ yisɛɛ kɛtsɔɔ akɛ Yehowa baabu ejálɔi lɛ ahe koni akaye amɛ awui lɛ etɔ̃ko moŋ, shi be ni ekɛɛ Yesu akɛ eje gbɛ ni efee nɔ ko ni nilee bɛ mli ni ekpa gbɛ akɛ Yehowa baabu ehe lɛ, no lɛ, esane ejaaa.* Hetoo ni Yesu hã Satan lɛ yeɔ ebuaa wɔ ni wɔnuɔ Lala 91 lɛ shishi. Yehowa wo shi yɛ mli akɛ ebaabu etsuji lɛ ahe yɛ gbɛ fɛɛ gbɛ nɔ. Enɛ tsɔɔ akɛ ebaabu naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ amɛ kɛ lɛ teŋ he. Agbɛnɛ hu, ebaabu amɛwala he, ni ebaahã amɛfee kpoo kɛ́ amɛmiiná nɔ po.

akɛ wɔsheɔ gbeyei yɛ nibii ni baaba be ni akpãtãa Satan je lɛ hiɛ lɛ ahewɔ. He ni wɔshe lɛ, wɔbaasusu bɔ ni Yehowa buɔ wɔhe ehãa bianɛ kɛ bɔ ni ebaabu wɔhe ehã wɔsɛɛ lɛ he.

¹¹ Ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, Yehowa buɔ naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ wɔ kɛ lɛ teŋ lɛ he. Ele akɛ eshafeelɔi ji wɔ ni wɔnáaa lɛ mlɛo akɛ wɔbaasumɔ nibii ni esumɔɔ ni wɔnyɛ nibii ni enyɛɔ lɛ. (Rom. 6:16; 7:21-23) Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, edro wɔ nibii komɛi ni baaye abua wɔ koni wɔbo lɛ toi ni wɔya nɔ wɔkɛ lɛ abɔ naanyo. Nyɛhãa wɔsusua nibii nɛɛ ekomɛi ahe.
<<<P>>>
¹² Yehowa ehã wɔ Biblia lɛ. Yehowa etsɔɔ wɔ nibii gbohii ni esa akɛ wɔtsi wɔhe kɛje he kɛ bɔ ni wɔbaafee wɔfee nakai yɛ Biblia lɛ mli. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, kadimɔ nɔ ni Yesu wie yɛ Mateo 5:28, 29 lɛ. (Kanemɔ.) Yesu miiwie mɔ ko “ni yaa nɔ ekwɛɔ yoo ni enáa ehe akɔnɔ lɛ” he. Yesu kɛɛ kɛ́ mɔ fee nakai lɛ, mɔ lɛ kɛ yoo lɛ “efite gbãla momo yɛ etsui mli.” Mɛni enɛ tsɔɔ wɔ?

Kɛ́ henumɔi nɛɛ amli bawa lɛ, wɔsɛɛ ko kɛ́ wɔyaje shihilɛ ko ni baanyɛ ahã wɔkɛ wɔhe awo jeŋba sha mli lɛ, ewaaa akɛ wɔbaafee nakai.
