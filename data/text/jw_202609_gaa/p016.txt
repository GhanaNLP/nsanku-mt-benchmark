7 Eyɛ mli akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ webii lɛ wieɔ wiemɔi srɔtoi moŋ, shi amɛfeɔ ekome kɛshiɛɔ sane kpakpa lɛ amɛhãa mɛi yɛ he fɛɛ he. Ŋwɛibɔfoi yeɔ amɛbuaa amɛ koni amɛnyɛ amɛtsu nitsumɔ nɛɛ. (Kanemɔ Kpojiemɔ 14:6.) Kɛ́ wɔna ni gbalɛi tamɔ nɔ ni aŋma yɛ Zakaria wolo lɛ kɛ Kpojiemɔ wolo lɛ mli lɛ miiba mli lɛ, ehãa wɔyiŋ tsɔɔ kwraa akɛ gbalɛi krokomɛi lɛ hu baaba mli. Ni wɔtswaa wɔfai shi akɛ wɔbaaye Yehowa asafo lɛ anɔkwa ejaakɛ eyɛ faŋŋ akɛ ekɛ asafo lɛ miitsu nii koni mɛi anu sane kpakpa lɛ yɛ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ fɛɛ.

YEHOWA MIIFEE NAAKPƐƐ NII ŊMƐNƐ

8 Yɛ Pentekoste gbi lɛ nɔ yɛ afi 33 lɛ, Yehowa fee naakpɛɛ nɔ ko. Be ni Yesu kaselɔi lɛ ebua amɛhe naa yɛ Yerusalem lɛ, trukaa lɛ, amɛbɔi wiemɔ yɛ wiemɔi srɔtoi pii amli. (Bɔf. 2:4-10) Mɛni hewɔ efee naakpɛɛ nii nɛɛ? Ejaakɛ no mli lɛ, mɛi ejɛ hei srɔtoi kɛba Yerusalem koni amɛbajá Nyɔŋmɔ yɛ sɔlemɔ shĩa lɛ. Ni Yehowa miisumɔ ni amɛnu sane kpakpa lɛ yɛ amɛ diɛŋtsɛ amɛwiemɔ mli. (Bɔf. 2:11) Ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, wɔ hu wɔbaanyɛ wɔshiɛ wɔhã mɛi ni yɔɔ wiemɔ lɛ he miishɛɛ ni wieɔ wiemɔi krokomɛi lɛ ejaakɛ wɔyɛ woji kɛ vidioi yɛ wiemɔi babaoo amli.
