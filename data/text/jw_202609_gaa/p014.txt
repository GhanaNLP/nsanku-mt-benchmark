“Hii nyɔŋma ni jɛ jeŋmaji lɛ anɔ wiemɔi lɛ fɛɛ amli lɛ baamɔ Yudanyo kome atade kakadaŋŋ mli.”—ZAK. 8:23.
<<<P>>>
LALA 84
Wɔkɛ Wɔhe Hãa
<<<P>>>
NƆ NI WƆBAAKASE
Wɔbaana bɔ ni Zakaria 8:23 lɛ miiba mli, kɛ nɔ ni Yehowa webii lɛ miifee koni amɛnyɛ amɛshiɛ amɛhã mɛi yɛ wiemɔ fɛɛ wiemɔ mli.
<<<P>>>
YƐ MOSE KLEŊKLEŊ WOLO LƐ mli lɛ, abɔ amaniɛ akɛ be ko lɛ, adesai tse Yehowa hiɛ atua ni amɛka akɛ amɛbaama mɔɔ wulu ko. Shi Yehowa hã amɛwie wiemɔi srɔtoisrɔtoi koni amɛkpa mɔɔ lɛ maa. Kɛ́ mɔ nɛ wie lɛ, mɔ nɛ nuuu shishi, ni kɛ́ mɔ nɛ hu wie lɛ, mɔ nɛ nuuu shishi. No hewɔ lɛ, amɛtse amɛhe kɛje mɛi ni nuuu amɛwiemɔ lɛ shishi lɛ ahe ni amɛyahi hei krokomɛi, ni no hã mɛi gbɛ amɛshwã shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ. (1 Mo. 11:6-9) Shi afii komɛi asɛɛ lɛ, Yehowa wie akɛ ebaafee “asafoku babaoo ko ... ni jɛ jeŋmaŋ fɛɛ jeŋmaŋ kɛ weku fɛɛ weku kɛ gbɔmɛi srɔtoi fɛɛ kɛ wiemɔ fɛɛ wiemɔ mli” ekome, ni ebaahere amɛyiwala kɛje amanehulu kpeteŋkpele lɛ mli ni amɛhi jeŋ hee lɛ mli. (Kpo. 7:9, 10, 14) Asafoku babaoo nɛɛ baanyɛ amɛbo amɛtsɛ Yehowa gbɛ́i lɛ yɛ amanehulu kpeteŋkpele lɛ mli ejaakɛ ashiɛ Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ he sane kpakpa lɛ ahã amɛ yɛ wiemɔ ni amɛnuɔ shishi mli. (Kanemɔ Romabii 10:13, 14.) Eji hegbɛ kpele ni wɔná akɛ wɔbaashiɛ wɔhã mɛi koni anyɛ ahere amɛyiwala!
