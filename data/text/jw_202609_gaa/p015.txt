“jeŋmaji babaoo kɛ amɛhe baakpɛtɛ Yehowa he, ni amɛbaatsɔ [emaŋ].” (Zak. 2:11) Agbɛnɛ hu, Zakaria ŋma yɛ ewolo lɛ mli akɛ: “Nɔ ni Yehowa, taiatsɛ lɛ, kɛɛ nɛ, ‘Nakai gbii lɛ amli lɛ, hii nyɔŋma ni jɛ jeŋmaji lɛ anɔ wie-mɔi lɛ fɛɛ amli lɛ baamɔ Yudanyo kome atade kakadaŋŋ mli, hɛɛ, amɛbaamɔ mli kpɛŋŋ, ni amɛbaakɛɛ akɛ: “Wɔkɛ nyɛ baaya, ejaakɛ wɔnu akɛ, Nyɔŋmɔ kɛ nyɛ yɛ.”’” (Zak. 8:23) Gbalɛ nɛɛ miiba mli ŋmɛnɛ. Yɛ nikasemɔ nɛɛ mli lɛ, wɔbaana nɔ ni wɔteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ baanyɛ afee koni wɔshiɛ sane kpakpa lɛ wɔhã mɛi yɛ wiemɔ fɛɛ wiemɔ mli.

3 “Yudanyo” ni atsĩ etã yɛ Zakaria gbalɛ lɛ mli lɛ damɔ shi kɛhã Kristofoi ni akɛ mumɔ krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ efɔ amɛ mu lɛ. Biblia lɛ tsɛɔ amɛ hu akɛ “Nyɔŋmɔ Israel lɛ.” (Gal. 6:16) Mɛi nɛɛ “ená ŋwei tsɛmɔ lɛ.” (Heb. 3:1) “Hii nyɔŋma” lɛ damɔ shi kɛhã mɛi ni baa amɛhe shi ni amɛyɛ hiɛnɔkamɔ akɛ amɛbaahi shi kɛya naanɔ yɛ Paradeiso yɛ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ lɛ. Mɛi nɛɛ jɛ maji srɔtoi anɔ ni amɛwieɔ wiemɔi srɔtoi.

4 Yɛ afi 1919 lɛ, Yesu Kristo hala hii ni afɔ amɛ mu lɛ ateŋ mɛi fioo komɛi koni amɛsɔmɔ akɛ “nyɔŋ anɔkwafo kɛ hiɛshikalɔ lɛ.” Nyɛmimɛi hii nɛɛ na akɛ esa akɛ amɛtsɔɔ Biblia mli sane ni wɔshiɛɔ lɛ shishi kɛya wiemɔi babaoo amli. (Mat. 24:45) Shi dani afi 1919 baashɛ lɛ, no mli lɛ, Nyɔŋmɔ webii lɛ jieɔ vidio ko ni kɔɔ adebɔɔ he amɛhãa mɛi kɛshiɛɔ sane kpakpa lɛ. Amɛtsɔɔ vidio nɛɛ shishi kɛtee wiemɔi srɔtoi amli, shi amɛna akɛ esa akɛ amɛfee babaoo fe nakai. Sɛɛ mli yɛ afi 1921 lɛ, amɛjie wolo ko ni akɛkaseɔ Biblia lɛ ni atsɛɔ lɛ The Harp of God lɛ kpo. Atsɔɔ wolo nɛɛ shishi kɛtee wiemɔi 36 amli. Kɛkɛ ni yɛ afi 1935 lɛ, “nyɔŋ anɔkwafo kɛ hiɛshikalɔ lɛ” banu shishi akɛ “asafoku babaoo” ko ni jɛ maji srɔtoi anɔ baasɔmɔ Yehowa kɛya naanɔ yɛ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ. Enɛ hã amɛna akɛ, esa akɛ ashiɛ ahã mɛi yɛ maji srɔtoi anɔ yɛ jeŋ fɛɛ, kɛ agbɛnɛ hu, yɛ wiemɔi babaoo amli. No hewɔ lɛ, Kristofoi ni afɔ amɛ mu ni hi shi yɛ nakai beiaŋ lɛ tsu nii waa kɛto gbɛjianɔ ni ashiɛ sane kpakpa lɛ yɛ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ fɛɛ koni Zakaria gbalɛ lɛ aba mli.

5 Nyɔŋmɔ webii lɛ tee nɔ amɛshiɛ sane kpakpa lɛ yɛ jeŋ fɛɛ. Tamɔ bɔ ni Yesaia gba lɛ, mɛi babaoo ni jɛ je lɛŋ hei srɔtoi lɛ miisuɔ ni amɛkase Yehowa he nii ni amɛjá lɛ. (Kanemɔ Yesaia 2:2-4.) Bɔ ni afee ni Nyɔŋmɔ webii lɛ aye abua mɛi nɛɛ ni amɛkase Yehowa he nii lɛ, amɛbɔɔ mɔdɛŋ waa ni amɛshiɛ, amɛtsɔɔ asafo lɛ woji lɛ ashishi kɛya wiemɔi babaoo ni awieɔ yɛ jeŋ fɛɛ lɛ amli, ni amɛkala woji nɛɛ ni amɛkɛmaje mɛi yɛ je lɛŋ fɛɛ. (Lala 68:11; 110:3) Agbɛnɛ hu, nyɛmimɛi hii kɛ yei babaoo tsu nii waa ni anyɛ afee jw.org kɛ JW Library® lɛ. Nibii nɛɛ eye ebua mɛi pii ni wieɔ wiemɔi srɔtoi koni amɛnyɛ amɛkase Biblia lɛ.
