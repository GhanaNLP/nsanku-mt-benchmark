19 Esaaa akɛ wɔsheɔ gbedyei be ni Yesu kɛ esraafoi lɛ kpãtãa Nyɔŋmɔ henyɛlɔi lɛ ahiɛ yɛ Harmagedon ta lɛ mli lɛ. Mɛni hewɔ? Ejaakɛ wɔkɛ wɔhiɛ fɔ̃ɔ Yehowa nɔ ni wɔle akɛ, ebaabu wɔhe. No hewɔ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔná nɔmimaa kwraa akɛ, Yesu kɛ esraafoi lɛ baakpãtã adesai ni ji Nyɔŋmɔ henyɛlɔi lɛ fɛɛ ahiɛ yɛ Harmagedon ta lɛ mli. (Yer. 25:33; Zak. 14:12) Ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔná nɔmimaa kwraa hu akɛ, Nyɔŋmɔ henyɛlɔi lɛ egbeŋ etsuji lɛ ateŋ mɔ kome po yɛ ta nɛɛ mli.—Luka 21:28; 2 Pet. 2:9.
<<<P>>>
19-20. Mɛni hewɔ esaaa akɛ wɔsheɔ gbedyei be ni Yesu kɛ esraafoi lɛ kpãtãa Nyɔŋmɔ henyɛlɔi lɛ ahiɛ yɛ Harmagedon ta lɛ mli lɛ? (Kwɛmɔ mfoniri ni yɔɔ wolo lɛ hiɛ lɛ hu.)
<<<P>>>
20 Be ni awuɔ Harmagedon ta lɛ, ekolɛ lalatsɛ lɛ wiemɔi lɛ baaba mli diɛŋtsɛ akɛ: “Mɛi akpe baanyɔ yɛ omasɛi, ni mɛi akpei nyɔŋma hu baanyɔ yɛ oninejurɔ nɔ, shi bo lɛ, ebɛŋkɛŋ bo. Ohiŋmɛii kɛkɛ okɛbaana be ni okwɛɔ efɔŋfeelɔi lɛ atoigbalamɔ lɛ. Ejaakɛ okɛɛ: ‘Yehowa ji mibobaahe,’ okɛ Mɔ Ni Nɔ Kwɔ Fe Fɛɛ lɛ efee oshihilɛhe.” (Lala 91:7-9) Kɛ́ Harmagedon ta lɛ ná eje shishi pɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔkpa gbɛ akɛ, Yehowa baabu wɔteŋ mɔ fɛɛ mɔ he kɛje nɔ fɛɛ nɔ kwraa ni baanyɛ aye wɔ awui lɛ he. No hewɔ lɛ, esaaa akɛ wɔsheɔ nɔ ni baaba wɔsɛɛ lɛ gbedyei. Wɔbaanyɛ wɔná nɔmimaa kwraa akɛ, kɛ́ wɔtee nɔ wɔkɛ wɔhiɛ fɔ̃ Yehowa nɔ lɛ, ebaabu wɔhe.

NƆ NI WƆNAA YƐ MFONIRI LƐ MLI Wolo lɛ hiɛ:
Yɛ Harmagedon ta lɛ mli lɛ, Yehowa baabu wɔhe koni nɔ ko nɔ ko akaye wɔ awui, ni wɔhi shi kpoo.
