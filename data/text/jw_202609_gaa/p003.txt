3 Wɔna akɛ etsɛŋ Yesu kɛ esraafoi lɛ baakpãtã Satan je lɛ hiɛ yɛ Harmagedon ta lɛ mli. Shi ani esa akɛ wɔshe nɔ ni baaba nɛɛ gbeyei? Dabi kwraa! Mɛni hewɔ? Ejaakɛ Yehowa ewo shi akɛ ebaabu wɔhe. Aŋma shiwoo nɛɛ yɛ Lala 91:7 lɛ. Jɛmɛ kɛɛ: “Mɛi akpe baanyɔ yɛ omasɛi, ni mɛi akpei nyɔŋma hu baanyɔ yɛ oninejurɔ nɔ, shi bo lɛ, ebɛŋkɛŋ bo.” Amrɔ nɛɛ, wɔbaasusu Lala 91 lɛ he, ni wɔhã sanebimɔi ni nyiɛ sɛɛ nɛɛ ahetoo: Te Yehowa ewo shi akɛ ebaabu wɔhe ehã tɛŋŋ? Te ebuɔ wɔhe ehãa tɛŋŋ ŋmɛnɛ? Te ebaabu wɔhe ehã tɛŋŋ wɔsɛɛ? Shi klɛŋklɛŋ lɛ, nyɛhãa wɔsusua Lala 91 lɛ he.

LALA 91 LƐ HÃA WƆNÁA NƆMIMAA AKƐ YEHOWA BAABU WƆHE
<<<P>>>
4 Sane ni yɔɔ Lala 91 lɛ mli lɛ hãa wɔnaa akɛ, Yehowa buɔ mɛi ni kɛ amɛhiɛ fɔ̃ɔ enɔ lɛ ahe. Ekuku kome kɛ enyɔ lɛ feɔ enɛ faŋŋ. (Kanemɔ Lala 91:1, 2.) Yehowa ji Mɔ Ni Nɔ Kwɔ Fe Fɛɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔná nɔmimaa kwraa akɛ ké wɔkɛ wɔhiɛ fɔ̃ enɔ lɛ, ebaabu wɔhe. (Rut 2:12; Lala 17:8) Wiemɔi ni ji ‘abobahe’ kɛ ‘teemɔhe’ lɛ gbalaa wɔjwɛŋmɔ kɛyaa blema maji ni atswa gbogboi kɛbɔle amɛhe lɛ anɔ. Gbogboi ni atswa kɛbɔle he lɛ buɔ mɛi ni yɔɔ maŋ lɛ mli ahe. Agbɛnɛ hu, mɛi ni yɔɔ hei ni bɛŋkɛ maji ni atswa gbogboi kɛbɔle amɛhe lɛ baanyɛ ajo foi kɛya maji nɛɛ amli kɛyaba abo. Mɔ ni ŋma Lala 91 lɛ kɛ mfonirifeemɔŋ wiemɔi krokomɛi tsu nii ni enɛ yeɔ ebuaa wɔ ni wɔnáa nɔmimaa akɛ ké wɔyaje shihilɛi ni yɔɔ oshãra amli lɛ, Yehowa baanyɛ abu wɔhe.—Lala 91:3-13.
<<<P>>>
5 Kɛ́ wɔkane Lala 91:14-16 lɛ, eshɛjeɔ wɔmii waa. Mɛni hewɔ wɔkɛɔ nakai? Yɛ kukuji nɛɛ amli lɛ, jeee lalatsɛ lɛ diɛŋtsɛ ni kɛɔ wɔ bɔ ni Yehowa baanyɛ abu wɔhe ehã lɛ dɔŋŋ, moŋ lɛ Yehowa diɛŋtsɛ ni woɔ wɔ, ni ji etsuji lɛ ashi lɛ. (Kanemɔ Lala 91:14-16.) Ewo shi shii abɔ yɛ kukuji nɛɛ amli akɛ ebaabu etsuji lɛ ahe ejaakɛ amɛkɛ amɛhiɛ fɔ̃ɔ enɔ, amɛle egbɛi lɛ, ni amɛboɔ amɛtsɛɔ lɛ yɛ sɔlemɔ mli. Wɔbaanyɛ wɔhe Yehowa wɔye kɛ wɔtsui fɛɛ ejaakɛ emaleee. No hewɔ lɛ, bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni fee lɛ, ebaaye eshiwoi lɛ anɔ!—4 Mo. 23:19; Yos. 23:14.
<<<P>>>
6 Yehowa hã lalatsɛ lɛ ŋma wiemɔi ni yɔɔ lala nɛɛ mli lɛ ejaakɛ eesumɔ ni wɔnu he akɛ wɔyɛ shweshweeshwe ni wɔkɛ wɔhiɛ afɔ̃ enɔ hu. (Rom. 15:4) Enɛ hewɔ lɛ, wɔ hu wɔbaanyɛ wɔkɛɛ Yehowa kɛ nɔmimaa akɛ: “Bo ji mibobahe kɛ miteemɔhe.” (Lala 91:2) Shi te Yehowa ewo shi akɛ ebaabu wɔhe ehã tɛŋŋ?
<<<P>>>
TE YEHOWA EWO SHI AKƐ EBAABU WƆHE EHÃ TƐŊŊ?
<<<P>>>
7 Wɔwie yɛ wɔwoji lɛ amli akɛ, Yehowa wo shi yɛ Lala 91 lɛ akɛ ebaabu naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ wɔ kɛ lɛ teŋ lɛ he koni ekafite. Wɔbaanyɛ wɔná nɔmimaa akɛ, Yehowa baaye abua wɔ daa koni wɔnyɛ wɔbo lɛ toi ni wɔya nɔ wɔkɛ lɛ abɔ naanyo kpaakpa. Shi Lala nɛɛ tsɔɔ hu akɛ, Yehowa ewo shi akɛ ebaabu wɔhe yɛ gbɛi krokomɛi anɔ. Yɛ Lala 91 lɛ, Yehowa wo shi hu akɛ, eŋmɛŋ gbɛ kɔkɔɔkɔ ni etsuji lɛ ahenyɛlɔi lɛ akpãtã amɛ fɛɛ amɛhiɛ. Kɛfata he lɛ, ewo shi akɛ, ebaahã etsuji lɛ afee kpoo kɛ́ amɛmiiná nɔ po. Mɛni hewɔ wɔkɛɔ nakai?
