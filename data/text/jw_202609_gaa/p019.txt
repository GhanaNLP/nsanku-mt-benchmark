16 Biblia lɛ egba akɛ etsɛŋ Yehowa kɛ “shikpɔŋ muu lɛ fɛɛ nɔ maŋtsɛmɛi lɛ” baawu. (Kpo. 16:14) Yɛ nakai beiaŋ lɛ, Yehowa baabu mɛi ni le lɛ ni amɛboɔ lɛ toi, ni amɛwoɔ egbɛ́i lɛ hiɛ nyam lɛ ahe. (Lala 9:10; kanemɔ Yohane 17:3) Shi mɛi ni heee lɛ amɛyeee ni amɛbooo lɛ toi lɛ baaná ale akɛ lɛ ji Nyɔŋmɔ Ofe lɛ be mli ni ewuɔ eshiɔ amɛ lɛ. Yehowa wie akɛ: “Bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni fee lɛ, mafee mihe agbo, ni mafee mihe krɔŋkrɔŋ ni mahã ale mi yɛ jeŋmaji babaoo ahiɛ; ni amɛbaaná amɛle akɛ miji Yehowa.” (Eze. 38:23) Yɛ nakai beiaŋ lɛ, Yehowa webii lɛ amii baashɛ amɛhe waa akɛ Yehowa ji amɛ-Naanyo shi jeee amɛhenyɛlɔ!—Mal. 3:16.

17 Eji hegbɛ kpele ni wɔná akɛ wɔbaashiɛ sane kpakpa lɛ wɔhã mɛi fɛɛ yɛ jeŋ fɛɛ koni gbalɛ ni yɔɔ Zakaria 8:23 lɛ aba mli. Eji hegbɛ kpele hu ni wɔná akɛ wɔkɛ wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ kɛ ŋwɛibɔfoi lɛ baafee ekome kɛshiɛ sane kpakpa lɛ wɔhã mɛi yɛ wiemɔi srɔtoi amli. Yehowa hãa wɔ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni he baahia wɔ koni wɔnyɛ wɔshiɛ wɔhã mɛi ni jɛ jeŋmaŋ fɛɛ jeŋmaŋ kɛ weku fɛɛ weku mli, kɛ agbɛnɛ hu, mɛi ni wieɔ wiemɔ fɛɛ wiemɔ. No hewɔ lɛ, nyɛhãa wɔyaa nɔ wɔkɛ nibii nɛɛ atsua nii jogbaŋŋ kɛye wɔbua mɛi ni amɛbatsɔmɔ Yehowa nanemɛi. Wɔbaanyɛ wɔye wɔbua amɛ koni amɛna akɛ Yehowa naa mɔbɔ waa, etoɔ etsui shi, ni esumɔɔ mɛi fɛɛ. Eji hegbɛ ni wɔná akɛ wɔbaaya nɔ wɔshiɛ kɛyashi mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni yɔɔ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ lɛ baale Yehowa gbɛ́i lɛ kɛ anɔkwalei ni kɔɔ ehe lɛ.—Luka 11:2.
