15 Yehoshafat he eye waa akɛ Yehowa baaye eshiwoo lɛ nɔ, ni ebaawu ehã ewebii lɛ. Ni ewo maŋbii lɛ hewalɛ ni amɛ hu amɛhe Yehowa amɛye ni amɛbo lɛ toi. Be ni ekɛ maŋbii lɛ eto ajina sɛɛ lɛ, amɛ fɛɛ amɛkpɛ mli akɛ amɛbaahala hii komɛi koni amɛnyiɛ asraafoi lɛ ahiɛ ni amɛlá kɛjie Yehowa yi. (2 Kro. 20:21) Enɛ hãa wɔnaa akɛ amɛheɔ Yehowa amɛyeɔ. Akɛni amɛhe Yehowa amɛye ni amɛbo lɛ toi hewɔ lɛ, ewu eshi amɛhenyɛlɔi lɛ ehã amɛ. Ehã amɛhenyɛlɔi lɛ gbegbee amɛhe.—2 Kro. 20:22-24.

16 Kɛ́ oji asafoŋ onukpa, fɔlɔ, loo mɔ ko ni kwɛɔ mɛi anɔ lɛ, obaanyɛ okase nɔkwɛmɔnɔ kpakpa ni Yehoshafat fee ehã emaŋbii lɛ. Ekpɛ eyiŋ akɛ ebaabo Yehowa toi, ni enɔkwɛmɔnɔ kpakpa lɛ ye ebua maŋbii lɛ ni amɛ hu amɛfee nakai. Yehoshafat ba ehe shi hu ni ebo Nyɔŋmɔ gbalɔ lɛ toi. Be ni eyaakpɛ yiŋ ko ni he hiaa waa lɛ, ekɛ maŋbii lɛ to ajina dani ekpɛ eyiŋ. Yehoshafat fee enii tamɔ eblematsɛ Solomon. Solomon miisumɔ ni ebo Yehowa toi ni ekwɛ emaŋbii lɛ anɔ jogbaŋŋ. No hewɔ lɛ, ekpa Yehowa fai koni eye ebua lɛ. (Kanemɔ 1 Maŋtsɛmɛi 3:9.) Esa akɛ asafoŋ onukpai hu kɛ́ amɛhiɛ afɔ̃ Yehowa nɔ ni eye ebua amɛ koni amɛnyɛ amɛkwɛ asafo lɛ nɔ. Kɛ́ amɛyaakpɛ yiŋ ko ni baasa amɛsafo lɛ he yɛ gbɛ ko nɔ lɛ, esa akɛ amɛtao nii amli yɛ Biblia lɛ kɛ asafo lɛ woji amli, ni amɛbi asafoŋ onukpai krokomɛi asusumɔ dani amɛkpɛ yiŋ lɛ. Enɛ baatsɔɔ akɛ amɛbaa amɛhe shi. (Abɛi 15:22) Kɛ́ asafo lɛ kɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔ ko hã lɛ, amɛjeɔ amɛtsui mli amɛmɔɔ mli oya nɔŋŋ ni amɛkɛtsuɔ nii, ni no woɔ nyɛmimɛi lɛ hewalɛ ni amɛ hu amɛfee nakai. Kɛ́ fɔlɔi hu jɛɔ amɛtsui mli amɛkɛ Yehowa gbɛtsɔɔmɔi tsuɔ nii lɛ, no baaye abua amɛbii lɛ ni amɛ hu amɛkɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔ atsu nii.—Abɛi 1:8; 14:1.

17 Kɛ́ wɔkpɛ mli akɛ wɔbaabo Yehowa toi lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔná nɔmimaa akɛ Yehowa baakwɛ wɔ ni ebaatsɔɔ wɔ nɔ ni wɔfee ekɔɔɔ he eko shihilɛ ni wɔkɛbaakpe. Yehowa tsɔɔ hii ni ehala yɛ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ lɛ anɔ etsɔɔ ewebii lɛ agbɛ tamɔ efee yɛ blema lɛ. Ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, Yehowa tsɔɔ Gbɛtsɔɔmɔ Kuu lɛ kɛ asafoŋ onukpai anɔ etsɔɔ esafo lɛ gbɛ. Kɛ́ wɔbo gbɛtsɔɔmɔ ni amɛkehãa wɔ lɛ toi lɛ, belɛ wɔmiibo Yehowa toi ji no. Ekolɛ, bei komɛi lɛ, wɔnuŋ gbɛtsɔɔmɔi komɛi ni Yehowa kɛhãa ashishi, shi esaaa akɛ wɔyeɔ wɔtsui. Yehowa le nii kwraa fe wɔ, ni nɔ ko nɔ ko bɛ ni enyɛŋ efee. (Yes. 55:9) No hewɔ lɛ, nyɛhãa wɔtswaa wɔfai shi akɛ wɔbaajɛ wɔtsui mli fɛɛ wɔbo Yehowa toi be fɛɛ be.
