9 Saamɔ ohe oto shiteekɛwoo. Esa akɛ wɔsaa wɔhe wɔto koni wɔnyɛ wɔya nɔ wɔshiɛ kɛ́ mɛi te shi amɛwo wɔ. Ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, yɛ maji ni wɔshiɛɔ yɛ amɛnɔ lɛ ateŋ pii anɔ lɛ, nɔyelii lɛ eteee shi amɛwooo wɔ. Fɛɛ sɛɛ lɛ, akɛni sane kpakpa lɛ ni wɔshiɛɔ hu biɔ ni wɔhã mɛi ale akɛ etsɛŋ abaakpãtã jeŋ fɔŋ nɛɛ hiɛ hewɔ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔfee ekãa. Wɔbaaya nɔ wɔshiɛ sane nɛɛ kɛyashi be ni eshwɛ fioo ni Harmagedon ta lɛ baaje shishi. (Mat. 24:14) Agbɛnɛ hu, yɛ amanehulu kpeteŋkpele lɛ mli lɛ, sane ni wɔshiɛɔ lɛ baawo mɛi amli la waa fe tsutsu lɛ po, ni ebaabi ni amɛkpɛ amɛyiŋ kɛji amɛbaafi Nyɔŋmɔ Maŋtsɛyeli lɛ sɛɛ loo adesai anɔyelii lɛ asɛɛ. Mɛi ni nyɛɔ Yehowa lɛ nyaŋ sane ni wɔbaashiɛ lɛ he. Ebaafee tamɔ haomɔ ni “da fe nine” kɛhã amɛ.* (Kpo. 16:21) Ŋwanejee ko bɛ he akɛ, amɛmli baafu Yehowa kɛ ewebii lɛ waa tamɔ bɔ ni Farao mlifu Israelbii lɛ yɛ Ŋshɔ Tsuru lɛ naa lɛ nɔŋŋ. Kɛ́ wɔbo fãmɔ ni akɛhã wɔ akɛ wɔshiɛ lɛ toi, ni wɔtee nɔ wɔshiɛ ekɔɔɔ he eko bɔ ni mɛi naa wɔ amɛhãa lɛ, no baaye abua wɔ ni wɔsaa wɔhe wɔto shiteekɛwoo ni wɔkɛbaakpe wɔsɛɛ lɛ.

10 Awie hii kɛ yei pii ni bo Yehowa toi lɛ ahe yɛ Biblia lɛ mli. Amɛteŋ mɔ kome ji Maŋtsɛ Yehoshafat. Lɛ eyeɔ Yuda nɔ be ni maji etɛ komɛi fee ekome koni amɛbatutua Yuda lɛ. Yehoshafat kɛ ewebii lɛ she gbeyei waa, ni wɔnuɔ amɛshishi. (2 Kro. 20:1-3) Eyɛ mli akɛ eshe gbeyei moŋ, shi ekpɛ yiŋ kpakpai. Nyɛhãa wɔkwɛa nɔ ni Yehoshafat fee kɛtsɔɔ akɛ ekɛ ehiɛ fɔ̃ Yehowa nɔ ni ebo lɛ toi, kɛ nɔ ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔfee kɛtsɔɔ akɛ wɔmiikase lɛ.
