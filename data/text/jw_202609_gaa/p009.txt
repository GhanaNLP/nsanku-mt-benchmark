BƆ NI ISRAELBII LƐ FEE AMƐNII AMƐHÃ YƐ GBƐTSƆƆMƆI NI YEHOWA KƐHÃ AMƐ LƐ AHE
<<<P>>>
3 Yɛ nyɔɔŋteŋ dani Yehowa baajie Israelbii lɛ kɛje Ejipt lɛ, Yehowa hã haomɔ ni ji nyɔŋma lɛ ba Ejiptbii lɛ anɔ. Akɛni Yehowa miisumɔ ni ebu Israelbii lɛ ahe hewɔ lɛ, dani ekɛ haomɔ nɛɛ baaba Ejiptbii lɛ anɔ lɛ, ekɛɛ amɛ ni amɛfee nɔ ko ni he hiaa waa. Ekɛɛ amɛ ni amɛmɔ gwantɛŋnuu loo abotianuu ni amɛgbe lɛ ni amɛkɛ elá lɛ akpa amɛshinaatsei lɛ ahe. (2 Mo. 12:5, 7) Kɛ́ ŋwɛibɔfo ni Yehowa tsu ni eyagbe gbɔmɛi kɛ kooloi akromɔbi lɛ na shĩa ko ni akɛ lá ekadi shinaatsei lɛ ahe lɛ, egbeee kromɔbii ni yɔɔ nakai shĩa lɛ mli lɛ. Shi ekolɛ Israelbii lɛ baanyɛ ashe gbeyei fioo akɛ amɛkɛ nɔ ni Yehowa ekɛɛ amɛ lɛ baatsu nii. Sane lɛ ji, Ejiptbii lɛ kɛ woo srɔto ko hãa gwantɛŋhii. No hewɔ lɛ, ekolɛ Israelbii lɛ baanyɛ asusu akɛ kɛ́ Ejiptbii lɛ na amɛ ni amɛmiigbe kooloi nɛɛ amɛmli baafu amɛ ni amɛbaagbe amɛ po. (2 Mo. 8:25-27) Shi Israelbii lɛ na akɛ ehe miihia waa ni amɛbo Yehowa toi ni no hewɔ lɛ, ‘amɛyafee bɔ ni Yehowa fã lɛ.’ (2 Mo. 12:28) Yehowa hu jɔɔ amɛ; ehãaa ŋwɛibɔfo lɛ agbe amɛkromɔbii lɛ.
<<<P>>>
3. Mɛni hewɔ ekolɛ Israelbii lɛ baanyɛ ashe gbeyei fioo akɛ amɛkɛ nɔ ni Yehowa ekɛɛ amɛ lɛ baatsu nii lɛ? (Kwemɔ mfoniri lɛ hu.)
<<<P>>>
4 Nakai nyɔɔŋ lɛ mli lɛ, Israelbii lɛ shi Ejipt. Shishijee lɛ, Yehowa kɛɛ amɛ ni amɛtsɔ Ŋshɔ Tsuru lɛ kooyigbɛ. Shi kɛkɛ ni no sɛɛ lɛ, ekɛɛ amɛ ni amɛfee nɔ ko ni etamɔ nɔ ni nilee bɛ mli. Ekɛɛ amɛ ni amɛku amɛsɛɛ ni amɛyabɔ nsara yɛ he ko ni kã gɔji kɛ Ŋshɔ Tsuru lɛ teŋ. No mli lɛ, Farao kɛ esraafoi lɛ miiba yɛ amɛsɛɛ, ni Ŋshɔ Tsuru lɛ hu bua shi yɛ amɛhiɛ. Eenyɛ efee akɛ, Israelbii lɛ nu he akɛ, kɛ́ amɛyabɔ nsara yɛ he ni Yehowa tsɔɔ amɛ lɛ, amɛjeŋ Ejiptbii lɛ adɛŋ. Fɛɛ sɛɛ lɛ, Israelbii lɛ saa amɛbo nɔ ni Yehowa tsɔ Mose nɔ ekɛɛ amɛ lɛ toi. (2 Mo. 14:1-4) Shi be ni amɛna akɛ Ejiptbii lɛ miibɛŋkɛ amɛ lɛ, “gbeyei mɔmɔ” amɛ. Amɛmli fu Mose waa ni amɛkɛɛ lɛ akɛ ekɛ amɛ eba biɛ koni agbe amɛ! (2 Mo. 14:5-11) Israelbii lɛ nu he akɛ kulɛ esaaa akɛ amɛboɔ nɔ ni Yehowa kɛɛ amɛ lɛ toi. Nyɛhãa wɔkwɛɔ nɔ hewɔ ni amɛfee nɔ ni amɛfee lɛ kɛ bɔ ni Yehowa na amɛnifeemɔ lɛ ehã.
