SANE KO NI AWIE YƐ BIBLIA LƐ MLI
<<<P>>>
“Nyɛhãa Kristo Lɛ Toiŋjɔlɛ Lɛ Ayea Nɔ Yɛ Nyɛtsuii Amli”
<<<P>>>
Atsĩ wiemɔ ni ji “Kristo lɛ toiŋjɔlɛ lɛ” tã yɛ Kolosebii 3:15 lɛ. Mɛni ji “Kristo lɛ toiŋjɔlɛ lɛ”? Kɛ́ mɔ ko batsɔ Kristo kaselɔ lɛ, etoiŋ jɔɔ lɛ ni efeɔ kpoo. Kɛ́ eba lɛ nakai lɛ, belɛ ená “Kristo lɛ toiŋjɔlɛ lɛ” eko. Wɔnáa toiŋjɔlɛ nɛɛ kɛ́ wɔná wɔle akɛ Yehowa kɛ e-Bi lɛ sumɔɔ wɔ waa. —Yoh. 14:23, 27.
<<<P>>>
Bɔfo Paulo wo Kristofoi ni yɔɔ Kolose asafo lɛ mli lɛ hewalɛ koni amɛhã toiŋjɔlɛ ni wɔwieɔ he nɛɛ ayea nɔ yɛ amɛtsuii loo amɛmligbɛ gbɔmɔ lɛ mli. Te amɛbaafee tɛŋŋ amɛfee nakai? Yɛ blema Greek lɛ, kɛ́ aashi akaŋ lɛ, ayɛ kojolɔ ko ni kwɛɔ akɛ akaŋshilɔi lɛ miiye akaŋshii lɛ mli mlai lɛ anɔ lo, ni ejwereɔ mɛi ni yeɔ kunim lɛ hu. Greek wiemɔ ni Paulo kɛtsu nii ni atsɔɔ shishi yɛ Ga mli akɛ “ayea nɔ” lɛ henɔ ko ni akɛtsɛɔ kojolɔ nɛɛ. Enɛ hãa wɔnaa akɛ tamɔ bɔ ni kɛ́ aashi akaŋ lɛ, nɔ ni kojolɔ ni kwɛɔ akaŋshii lɛ nɔ baakɛɛ ji nɔ ni esa akɛ akaŋshilɔi lɛ kɛtsu nii lɛ, nakai nɔŋŋ esa akɛ wɔhã “Kristo lɛ toiŋjɔlɛ lɛ” akudɔ bɔ ni wɔhiɔ shi wɔhãa.
<<<P>>>
Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, kɛ́ wɔkɛ wɔnyɛmi Kristofonyo ko kpãaa gbee yɛ sane ko he lɛ, esa akɛ wɔhã “Kristo lɛ toiŋjɔlɛ lɛ” afee “kojolɔ” yɛ sane lɛ mli. Kɛ́ wɔye “Kristo lɛ toiŋjɔlɛ lɛ” eko lɛ, ewaaa akɛ wɔbaana nɔ ni esa akɛ wɔfee koni toiŋjɔlɛ kɛ ekomefeemɔ ahi wɔ kɛ wɔnyɛmimɛi lɛ ateŋ. Bɔ ni afee ni wɔnyɛ wɔfee enɛ lɛ, bei komɛi lɛ, ebaabi ni wɔku wɔhĩɛ wɔfɔ̃ nɔ ni wɔsumɔɔ lɛ nɔ loo wɔkɛ mɛi ahe ake amɛ. Shi kɛ́ wɔfee nakai lɛ, etsɔɔ akɛ wɔmiihã “Kristo lɛ toiŋjɔlɛ lɛ” miiye nɔ yɛ wɔtsuii amli. —Kol. 3:12-14.
