TSI OHE KƐJE WIEMƆI NI ESAAA AHE
<<<P>>>
6 Biblia gba akɛ yɛ naagbee gbii lɛ amli lɛ, mɛi baatsɔmɔ “shwãlɔi, henɔwolɔi,” kɛ “mɛi ni anyɛɛɛ akɛ amɛ afee kpaŋmɔ.” (2 Tim. 3:1-3) Ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, bei pii lɛ, mɛi awiemɔi hãa anaa akɛ amɛyɛ sui gbohii nɛɛ eko. Akɛ nɔ-kwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, bei komɛi lɛ, atswaa adafii yɛ TV loo redio nɔ loo yɛ adafitswai woji amli akɛ mɛi komɛi miitaa saji anaa kɛkɛ lɛ amɛbɔi nɔmɔ. Agbɛnɛ hu, yɛ wekui pii amli lɛ, wu kɛ ŋa wieɔ bɔ ni esaaa, ŋa kɛ ewu wieɔ bɔ ni esaaa, ni fɔlɔi hu kɛ amɛbii wieɔ bɔ ni esaaa. Kɛfata he lɛ, gbekɛbii kɛ onukpai fɛɛ wieɔ ya-kayaka wiemɔ. (Efe. 5:3, 4) Ekã shi faŋŋ akɛ, mɛi ni je lɛ mumɔ lɛ kudɔɔ amɛ lɛ ni wieɔ yɛ nakai gbɛ nɔ.
<<<P>>>
7 Akɛ Kristofoi lɛ, esa akɛ wɔwie wiemɔi ni sa be fɛɛ be, kɛ́ wɔkɛ mɛi miiwie hiɛ kɛ hiɛ jio, wɔmiiŋma sane ko ni wɔkɛmaje amɛ jio. (Yak. 1:26, 27) No hewɔ lɛ, obaanyɛ obi ohe akɛ: ‘Te miwieɔ mihãa tɛŋŋ? Kɛ́ mimli efu loo mikɛ mɛi ni leee akɛ Odasefonyo ji mi lɛ mii-wie lɛ, ani miwieɔ wiemɔi ni esaaa? Ni kɛ́ mikɛ minyɛmimɛi Odasefoi lɛ miiwie lɛ, ani mi-wieee kɛ naajɔlɛ loo miyeɔ amɛhe fɛo bɔ ni esaaa? Ani mikɛ miweku lɛ mli bii lɛ wieɔ bɔ ni esaaa? Mɛni amɛbaakɛɛ yɛ bɔ ni miwieɔ mi-hãa lɛ he?’

8. Te wɔbaafee tɛŋŋ wɔtsi wɔhe kɛje wiemɔi ni esaaa ahe? (Kolosebii 3:8-10)
<<<P>>>
wɔhãa lɛ, shi jeee no pɛ esa akɛ wɔfee. Mɛni hewɔ? Nɔ ko ni Yesu wie be ko lɛ hãa wɔnaa nɔ hewɔ. Ewie akɛ: “Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni jeɔ daaŋ jeɔ kpo lɛ, tsui lɛ mli ejɛɔ, ni nomɛi buleɔ gbɔ-mɔ.” (Mat. 15:18) Yesu wiemɔi nɛɛ tsɔɔ akɛ wɔwiemɔi hãa anaa nɔ ni wɔsusuɔ he kɛ bɔ ni wɔnuɔ he wɔhãa. No hewɔ ni Biblia lɛ tsĩ “jɛ-mɔi” tã efata sui gbohii komɛi ni jeɔ shishi kɛ-jeɔ tsui lɛ mli tamɔ “mlijoomɔ,” “mlila” kɛ “mlifu” nɛkɛ lɛ ahe lɛ. (Efe. 4:31) Kɛ́ ona akɛ oyɛ sui gbohii nɛɛ ekomɛi yɛ otsui mli lɛ, sɔlemɔ ni Yehowa aye abua bo ni oye sui gbohii nɛɛ anɔ ni oya nɔ owo “gbɔmɔ hee lɛ.” (Kanemɔ Kolosebii 3:8-10.) Agbɛnɛ hu, obaanyɛ otao saji ni asafo lɛ eŋmala ni wieɔ sui nɛɛ ahe kɛ bɔ ni wɔbaafee wɔye amɛnɔ lɛ ni okase. Tswaa ofai shi akɛ ohãŋ je lɛ mumɔ lɛ akudɔ bɔ ni osusuɔ nii ahe ohãa kɛ bɔ ni owieɔ ohãa.—Efe. 4:29.

TSI OHE KƐJE NIBII NI YEHOWA NYƐƆ LƐ AHE
<<<P>>>
9 Nɔ kroko ni mɛi ni je lɛ mumɔ lɛ kudɔɔ amɛ lɛ feɔ ji, amɛkuɔ amɛhiɛ amɛshwieɔ nibii ni Yehowa etsɔɔ akɛ eja loo ejaaa lɛ anɔ. Amɛ-teŋ mɛi babaoo susuɔ akɛ naagba bɛ he akɛ abaakpɔ hɔ, nuu kɛ nuu loo yoo kɛ yoo baa-ná bɔlɛ, loo mɛi baaná bɔlɛ dani amɛbote gbãla mli. (Yes. 5:20) Kɛ́ wɔkwɛɛɛ jogbaŋŋ lɛ, fiofio lɛ wɔ hu wɔbaasusu nii ahe tamɔ amɛ, ni wɔnaŋ naagba ko yɛ nifeemɔi nɛɛ ahe. (2 Kor. 11:3) Ekolɛ wɔbaasusu po akɛ nilee yɛ bɔ ni mɛi susuɔ nii ahe amɛhãa yɛ je lɛ mli lɛ he, ni akɛ, ŋaawoi ni yɔɔ Biblia lɛ mli lɛ abe eho. Shi enɛ jeee anɔkwale kwraa! Nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni Yehowa kɛɔ wɔ lɛ hi kɛhã wɔ be fɛɛ be. Ele nii kwraa fe wɔ, ni nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni ekɛɔ wɔ lɛ yeɔ omanye be fɛɛ be.—Yes. 48:17, 18; 55:9.
