15 Yehowa ehã wɔ esafo lɛ. Yehowa asafo lɛ feɔ woji kɛ vidioi ni yeɔ ebuaa wɔ koni wɔnyɛ wɔbu naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ wɔ kɛ Yehowa teŋ lɛ he. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, akɛni wɔyeee emuu hewɔ lɛ, bei komɛi lɛ, wɔwerɛ hoɔ wɔhe yɛ wɔtɔmɔi ahewɔ, wɔnuɔ he akɛ mɔ ko mɔ ko jweŋŋ wɔhe loo wɔyeɔ wɔtsui fe nine. Kɛ́ wɔnu he nakai lɛ, Satan, “Mɔ Ni Kaa Mɔ lɛ” baanyɛ ahã wɔnu he akɛ, wɔnyɛŋ wɔsɔmɔ Yehowa dɔŋŋ, ni no baahã wɔwerɛ aho wɔhe waa loo wɔye wɔtsui fe nine. (Mat. 4:3; 6:13) Shi Yehowa asafo lɛ efee woji kɛ vidioi ni kaiɔ wɔ akɛ, Yehowa kɛ eshai keɔ, eshwɛɛɛ wɔhe kwraa, ni ewo shi akɛ ebaahã wɔ wɔhiamɔ nii. (Lala 86:5; Mat. 10:29-31; Luka 12:28; Heb. 13:5) Ani okane sane ko yɛ asafo lɛ wolo ko mli ni kɔɔ naagba tuuntu ni okɛkpeɔ lɛ he ni wo bo hewalɛ waa lɛ pɛŋ? Enɛ tsɔɔ akɛ, Yehowa asafo lɛ feɔ woji kɛ vidioi yɛ be ni sa mli koni no aye abua wɔ ni wɔnyɛ wɔbu naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ wɔ kɛ Yehowa teŋ lɛ he.

16 Yehowa eŋmɛŋ gbɛ ni etsuji lɛ ahenyɛlɔi lɛ akpãtã etsuji lɛ fɛɛ ahiɛ. Eji anɔkwale akɛ, awa Yehowa tsuji lɛ ateŋ mɛi komɛi ayi ni agbe amɛ po. Bei komɛi lɛ, Yehowa ŋmɛɔ gbɛ ni eba lɛ nakai koni ana akɛ Satan ji amaleɔ. (Kpo. 2:10; 6:9) Shi eŋɔɔɔ Satan naa akɛ, Yehowa tsuji akpekpei abɔ ni yɔɔ jeŋ fɛɛ lɛ, amɛteŋ mɛi fioo komɛi pɛ ni amɛhenyɛlɔi lɛ enyɛ ekpãtã amɛhiɛ. No hewɔ lɛ, Satan mli ewo la waa, ejaakɛ ená kulɛ Yehowa tsuji lɛ ahenyɛlɔi lɛ egbe amɛ fɛɛ. (Mat. 24:9; Kpo. 12:9-12) Shi Yehowa eŋmɛŋ gbɛ ni eba lɛ nakai kɔkɔɔkɔ! Wɔbaanyɛ wɔhe wɔye akɛ Yehowa baahã ebɔfoi lɛ abu wɔhe koni wɔnyɛ wɔya nɔ wɔsɔmɔ lɛ ni wɔshiɛ sane kpakpa lɛ.—Heb. 1:7, 14.

17 Yehowa tsɔɔ Biblia lɛ nɔ ewoɔ wɔ ŋaa. Kɛ́ wɔbo ŋaawoi nɛɛ toi lɛ, ebuɔ wɔhe kɛjeɔ nibii pii ni baanyɛ aye wɔ awui lɛ ahe. (Abɛi 14:29, 30; 22:3; 2 Kor. 7:1) Shi ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, wɔkpaaa gbɛ akɛ Yehowa baabu etsuji lɛ fɛɛ ahe kɛje nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni baanyɛ aye amɛ awui lɛ he. Kɛ́ efee nakai lɛ, no baahã mɛi ahe Satan amale ni ji akɛ Nyɔŋmɔ tsuji jáa lɛ akɛni eŋmɛɛɛ gbɛ ni nɔ ko nɔ ko aye amɛ awui lɛ hewɔ lɛ amɛye. (Hiob 1:10, 11) Shi kɛ́ aawa wɔyi ni wɔtee nɔ wɔsɔmɔ Yehowa lɛ, wɔhãa anaa akɛ Satan ji amaleɔ, ni akɛ, suɔmɔ ni wɔyɔɔ kɛhã Yehowa kɛ le ni wɔle akɛ lɛ ji anɔkwa Nyɔŋmɔ lɛ hewɔ wɔsɔmɔɔ lɛ lɛ. (Rom. 9:22) Eshwɛ fioo, Yehowa eŋmɛŋ gbɛ dɔŋŋ ni etsuji lɛ apiŋ. Shi kɛyashi Yehowa baakpãtã ehenyɛlɔi lɛ fɛɛ ahiɛ lɛ, ebaaye ebua wɔ ni wɔbo lɛ toi ni wɔbu naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ wɔ kɛ lɛ teŋ lɛ he.—1 Kor. 10:13; 1 Pet. 5:10.

18 Yɛ amanehulu kpetɛŋkpele lɛ mli lɛ, Yehowa baaya nɔ eye ebua wɔ ni wɔbu naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ wɔ kɛ lɛ teŋ lɛ he. Shi ebaabu wɔhe yɛ gbɛ kroko hu nɔ. Kɛ́ je lɛŋ nɔyelɔi lɛ tutua Yehowa webii lɛ, eŋmɛŋ gbɛ ni amɛgbe ewebii lɛ fɛɛ. (Eze. 38:1, 2, 10-16) Ebaatsu Yesu kɛ esraafoi lɛ ni amɛyabu ewebii ni yɔɔ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ lɛ ahe. Ni no baayatsɛ Harmagedon ta lɛ kɛba. Yesu kɛ esraafoi lɛ baawu amɛshi Nyɔŋmɔ henyɛlɔi lɛ fɛɛ. (Kpo. 17:12-14; 19:11, 14, 15) Be ni Harmagedon ta yaa nɔ lɛ, te Yehowa baabu ewebii lɛ ahe ehã tɛŋŋ?
