kansa. Biblia he nilelɔ ko wie akɛ, nɔ ni Yesu wie yɛ Mateo 5:29 lɛ tsɔɔ akɛ, yɛ gbɛ ko nɔ lɛ, esa akɛ wɔ diɛntsɛ wɔfee wɔhe opireshɛŋ kɛfolɔ amɛ wɔshwie kɛje wɔshihilɛ mli. (Kol. 3:5) Kɛ́ wɔkɛ ŋaa ni Yesu wo nɛɛ tsu nii lɛ, wɔbaabu naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ wɔ kɛ Yehowa teŋ lɛ he.

14 Yehowa ehã wɔ emumɔ krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ. Yehowa kɛ emumɔ krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ hu yeɔ ebuaa wɔ koni wɔnyɛ wɔya nɔ wɔye lɛ anɔkwa. Kɛ́ wɔtee nɔ wɔsɔle ni ehã wɔ emumɔ krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ, ebaahã wɔ aahu. (Luka 11:9-13) Mɛɛ gbɛ nɔ mumɔ krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ buɔ wɔhe? Bɔfo Paulo wie akɛ: “Nyɛyaa nɔ nyɛnyiɛa yɛ mumɔ lɛ naa, ni nyɛfeŋ nibii ni heloo lɛ shweɔ lɛ eko kwraa.” (Gal. 5:16) Mɔ ni nyiɛɔ yɛ mumɔ lɛ naa lɛ, feɔ bɔ fɛɛ bɔ ni ebaanyɛ ni ebo Nyɔŋmɔ toi ni ehi shi yɛ egbɛtsɔɔmɔi lɛ anaa. Yehowa kɛ emumɔ krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ yeɔ ebua mɔ nɛɛ. Emumɔ lɛ baanyɛ ahã mɔ lɛ asusu nii ahe jogbaŋŋ ni efee enii yɛ gbɛ kpakpa nɔ. Kɛ́ eba lɛ nakai lɛ, kɛ́ mɔ lɛ shwe nɔ ko gbonyo lɛ, mumɔ krɔŋkrɔŋ lɛ baaye abua lɛ oya nɔŋŋ ni ejie kɛje ejwɛŋmɔ mli. No hewɔ lɛ, Yehowa kɛ emumɔ lɛ hãaa wɔ koni wɔkashwe nibii gbohii. Moŋ lɛ, ekɛhãa wɔ koni wɔshwe akɛ wɔbaafee nɔ ni ja, wɔná hewalɛ kɛfee nɔ ni ja, ni wɔtsi wɔhe kɛje nibii gbohii hu ahe. Enɛ yeɔ ebuaa wɔ koni wɔbu naanyobɔɔ ni yɔɔ wɔ kɛ lɛ teŋ lɛ he.
