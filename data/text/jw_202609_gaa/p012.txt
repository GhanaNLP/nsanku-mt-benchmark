Asafɔŋ onukpai ni hiɛ kã shi lɛ boɔ Yehowa, esafo lɛ, kɛ asafɔŋ onukpai krokomɛi atoi
(Kwemɔ kuku 16)
<<<P>>>
nɛɛ baakpe po lɛ, no mli lɛ ebi Yehowa ni etsɔɔ lɛ gbɛ yɛ shihilɛi komɛi ni ekɛkpe lɛ amli. (2 Kro. 19:3) No hewɔ lɛ, be ni maji etɛ lɛ ba koni amɛbatutua Yuda lɛ, Yehoshafat le akɛ esa akɛ ebi Yehowa ni eye ebua amɛ. Ekpɛ mli akɛ ebaabi Yehowa ni etsɔɔ lɛ gbɛ, ni agbɛnɛ hu, ekɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni Yehowa baakɛɛ lɛ lɛ atsu nii. No hewɔ lɛ, esɔle ehã Yehowa ni ebi lɛ yelikɛbuamɔ. (Kanemɔ 2 Kronika 20:5, 6, 10-12.) Kɛ́ wɔhao lɛ, nyɛhãa wɔkasea Yehoshafat. Esa akɛ wɔkpɛ mli akɛ wɔbaabo Yehowa toi, wɔbi lɛ ni etsɔɔ wɔ gbɛ kɛtsɔ sɔlemɔ ni wɔbaa-sɔle kɛ Biblia lɛ ni wɔbaakase lɛ nɔ, ni wɔhe wɔye akɛ ebaaye ebua wɔ.—Lala. 119:112.

12 Yaa nɔ okase Biblia lɛ. Nibii ni Yehoshafat wie be ni esɔleɔ lɛ hãa wɔnaa faŋŋ akɛ ele nibii ni Yehowa efee be ko ni eho lɛ. Ele akɛ Yehowa ye shi ni ewo Abraham akɛ ekɛ Shiwoo Shikpɔŋ lɛ baahã eseshi bii lɛ nɔ. (1 Mo. 12:7) Agbɛnɛ hu, wiemɔi ni ewie yɛ esɔlemɔ lɛ mli lɛ tamɔ wiemɔi ni Solomon wie be ni esɔleɔ kɛgbeleɔ sɔlemɔ shĩa lɛ naa lɛ nɔŋŋ. (2 Kro. 6:28-30; 20:5-9) Nibii ni Yehoshafat le yɛ Yehowa he lɛ hã ehe lɛ eye kɛ etsui fɛɛ ni ekpɛ mli akɛ ebaabo lɛ toi. Wɔ hu esa akɛ wɔya nɔ wɔkase Biblia mli anɔkwalei hei wɔfata nɔ ni wɔle momo lɛ ahe, kɛ́ wɔɔkɛɛ lɛ, wɔkɛ nibii awo ‘wɔjwetritoohe lɛ.’ (Mat. 13:52) Be ni wɔyaa nɔ wɔfeɔ nakai lɛ, wɔbaakpɛ mli akɛ wɔbaabo Yehowa toi yɛ shihilɛ fɛɛ shihilɛ mli.

13 Yaa nɔ oba ohe shi. Yehoshafat sɔlemɔ lɛ hãa wɔnaa hu akɛ ebaa ehe shi. Yehoshafat lɛ, maŋtsɛ ni, ni eye asraafoi pii, shi eshiii Yehowa sɛɛ ni ekɛ ehenyɛlɔi lɛ ayawu. Mɔŋ lɛ, be ni esɔleɔ yɛ maŋbii lɛ ahiɛ lɛ, efee nɔ ko ni tsɔɔ akɛ ebaa ehe shi waa. Ewie akɛ eleee nɔ ni ebaafee, ni ebi Yehowa ni eye ebua amɛ. (2 Kro. 20:12) Akɛni Yehoshafat ba ehe shi hewɔ lɛ, be ni Yehowa kɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔ hã lɛ lɛ, ekɛtsu nii oya nɔŋŋ. Wɔ hu kɛ́ wɔbaa wɔhe shi lɛ, wɔbaasumɔ akɛ wɔbo Yehowa toi be fɛɛ be.—Fip. 2:5, 8.

14 Onifeemɔi baanyɛ aná mɛi anɔ hewalɛ. Yehowa bo Yehoshafat toi. Etsu gbalɔ Yahaziel ni ekɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔi ayahã Yehoshafat. Yahaziel wie akɛ, ehe ebahiaŋ ni amɛkɛ amɛhenyɛlɔi awu ta. Mɔŋ lɛ ekɛɛ amɛ akɛ: “Nyɛyabuaa nyɛhe naa, ni nyɛdamɔa shi, ni nyɛkwɛa bɔ ni Yehowa baahere nyɛyiwala ehã.” Te Yehoshafat fee enii ehã tɛŋŋ yɛ gbɛtsɔɔmɔ nɛɛ ni akɛhã lɛ he? Biblia lɛ kɛɛ Yehoshafat “kula shi ni ekɛ ehiɛ bu shi.” Maŋbii lɛ fɛɛ hu “kɛ amɛhiɛ bumɔ shi . . . koni amɛjá Yehowa.” Enɔ jetsɛremɔ lɛ, Yehoshafat kɛ maŋbii lɛ tee koni amɛkɛ amɛhenyɛ-
