10 Esa akɛ mɛi ni sumɔɔ ni Yehowa aná amɛhe miishɛɛ lɛ anyɛ nibii ni Yehowa nyɛɔ lɛ. (Lala 97:10) No hewɔ lɛ, obaanyɛ obi ohe akɛ: ‘Eyɛ mli akɛ minuɔ shishi akɛ Yehowa kɛɛ esaaa akɛ anáa bɔlɛ dani abote gbãla mli, ni esaaa akɛ nuu kɛ nuu loo yoo kɛ yoo náa bɔlɛ moŋ, shi ani bei komɛi lɛ mishweɔ akɛ mafee nakai? Ani misumɔɔ ni makwɛ vidioi loo matswa vidio gemisii ni nibii ni Yehowa nyɛɔ lɛ yɔɔ amɛmli? Ani mishweɔ akɛ Yehowa baatsake nibii ni ekɛɛ ejaaa lɛ koni mafee nɔ ni misumɔɔ?’

11 Kɛ́ ona akɛ osumɔɔ nɔ ko ni Yehowa nyɛɔ lɛ, mɛni obaanyɛ ofee? Kaamu sane naa oya akɛ onyɛŋ ofee Yehowa suɔmɔnaa nii. Kaimɔ akɛ esha yɛ wɔ fɛɛ wɔhe, ni bei komɛi lɛ wɔshweɔ ni wɔfee nibii ni esaaa. (Lala 51:5; 103:14) Shi obaanyɛ ohala akɛ obaafee nɔ ni ja, ni akɛ ofeŋ “nibii ni wɔheloo lɛ shweɔ lɛ.” (Efe. 2:3) Agbɛnɛ hu, obaanyɛ ohala akɛ okɛ ojwɛŋmɔ eyaŋ nɔ emaŋ nibii gbohii ni oshweɔ lɛ anɔ. Kɛ́ obɔi shwee akɛ ofee nɔ ko gbonyo ko lɛ, esa akɛ ojie ohiŋmɛii loo ojwɛŋmɔ kɛje nibii ni Yehowa nyɛɔ lɛ anɔ oya nɔŋŋ. (1 Mo. 39:9, 12) Ni ojwɛŋ bɔ ni nibii ni tamɔ nakai lɛ baanyɛ afite okɛ Yehowa teŋ, kɛ agbɛnɛ hu, okɛ mɛi krokomɛi ateŋ lɛ nɔ. Tswaa ofai shi akɛ ohãŋ eba lɛ nakai kɔkɔɔkɔ. (Efe. 5:5) Yehowa baanyɛ aye abua bo ni onyɛ nibii ni esaaa.

12 Wɔteŋ mɛi babaoo kɛ Intanɛt lɛ tsuɔ nii kɛ wɔmiitsu nii loo yɛ skul. Agbɛnɛ hu, kɛ́ wɔmiikase Biblia lɛ, wɔyɛ asafoŋ kpee shishi loo wɔnyiɛ shiɛmɔ nɔ lɛ, wɔyaa jw.org lɛ nɔ. Shi nibii ni yɔɔ Intanɛt lɛ nɔ ateŋ babaoo lɛ, mɛi ni je lɛ mumɔ lɛ kudɔɔ amɛ lɛ ji mɛi ni kɛwo nɔ. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, akɛ ponografi pii ewo Intanɛt lɛ nɔ, ni ewaaa akɛ mɛi baana, onukpai jio, gbekɛbii jio. Agbɛnɛ hu, mɛi babaoo, titri lɛ, oblahii kɛ oblayei kɛ jeŋba sha saji, mfonirii kɛ vidioi ni nibii ni kɔɔ bɔlɛnamɔ he yɔɔ mli majemajeɔ amɛhe yɛ Intanɛt lɛ nɔ. Kɛfata he lɛ, saji babaoo ni akɛwo Intanɛt lɛ nɔ lɛ jeee anɔkwale loo awo he fe nine. Ni yɛ soshia midia lɛ nɔ lɛ, bei pii lɛ, mɛi kɛ mfonirii kɛ vidioi ni hãa anaa nibii ni amɛfeɔ ni hãa amɛnáa miishɛɛ loo hei ni amɛfã gbɛ kɛyashara shi yɛ lɛ pɛ woɔ nɔ. Kɛ́ wɔna nibii ni mɛi kɛwo soshia midia lɛ nɔ lɛ, ebaanyɛ ehã wɔnu he akɛ nɔ ko miiŋmɛɛ wɔ. Ni no baanyɛ ahã wɔfolɔ asafoŋ kpeei amli loo wɔgbɔ bei ni wɔyeɔ yɛ shiɛmɔ nitsumɔ lɛ mli lɛ nɔ koni wɔnyɛ wɔtiu nibii nɛɛ asɛɛ.—Heb. 10:24, 25.

13 Esa akɛ wɔkwɛ jogbaŋŋ yɛ nibii ni akɛwo Intanɛt lɛ nɔ ni baanyɛ afite wɔbe loo wɔjwɛŋmɔ lɛ ahe. No hewɔ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔbi wɔhe akɛ: ‘Bei enyiɛ miyeɔ kɛkwɛɔ nibii ni mɛi kɛwo Intanɛt lɛ nɔ lɛ kɛkɛ, ni jeee yiŋtoo ko hewɔ mikwɛɔ?’ (Efe. 5:15, 16) ‘Mɛɛ nibii mikwɛɔ yɛ Intanɛt lɛ nɔ? Kɛ́ mina nɔ ko ni esaaa yɛ Intanɛt lɛ nɔ lɛ, bei enyiɛ eheɔ mi dani mikpaa kwɛmɔ?’ (Lala 119:37) ‘Ani miheɔ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ni mikaneɔ yɛ Intanɛt lɛ nɔ lɛ miyeɔ loo mikwɛɔ kɛji amɛja lo?’—Abɛi 14:15.
