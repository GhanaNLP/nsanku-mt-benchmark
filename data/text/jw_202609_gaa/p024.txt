13 Yehowa miisumɔ ni wɔnyɛ wɔkɛ lɛ agba sane ni wɔsheee gbeyei. (Kanemɔ Hebribii 4:16.) Enɛ ji yiŋtoo kroko hewɔ ni ekɛ Yesu hã wɔ koni esɔmɔ akɛ wɔ-Sɔfonukpa lɛ. Yehowa sumɔɔ ni ebo wɔsɔlemɔi atoi. Kɛ́ wɔfee nakai lɛ, no tsɔɔ akɛ wɔheɔ lɛ wɔyeɔ ni wɔkɛ wɔhiɛ fɔ̃ɔ enɔ hu. Bei komɛi kɛ́ wɔmiisɔle lɛ, wɔnaaa bɔ ni wɔbaafee wɔhã ena bɔ ni wɔnuɔ he wɔhãa lɛ. Eeenyɛ efee akɛ wɔhao waa, ni no hewɔ wɔnaaa nɔ ni wɔbaakɛɛ lɛ lɛ. (Rom. 8:26) Shi Yehowa le enɛ, enuɔ wɔshishi, ni no hewɔ lɛ, efeɔ nɔ ko kɛyeɔ ebuaa wɔ. Wɔbaanyɛ wɔya Yehowa ŋɔɔ be fɛɛ be ejaakɛ wɔ-Tsɛ ni, emli hi, ni eyɛ dekã kɛhã wɔ.

14 Yehowa miisumɔ ni wɔhi shi kɛya naanɔ. (Eze. 33:11) Afɔlei ni akɛ kooloi shã yɛ blema lɛ ejieee Yehowa jalɔi lɛ ahe eshai lɛ kɛyaaa kwraa. (Heb. 7:19) Shi Yehowa tsɔ afɔlei nɛɛ anɔ ehã etsuji na akɛ afɔle ko ni hi fe nomɛi he miihia amɛ. Afɔle nɛɛ ji Yesu Kristo adesa wala ni eye emuu lɛ. Afɔle nɛɛ hewɔ lɛ, Yehowa baanyɛ ehã mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni boo lɛ toi lɛ naanɔ wala. Shidaa ahã Yehowa akɛ eto gbɛjianɔ ko ni tamɔ nɛkɛ koni wɔnyɛ wɔhi shi kɛya naanɔ!—1 Yoh. 4:10.

shi amɛbaagboi. (Fip. 2:8; Kpo. 2:10) Esa akɛ efee faŋŋ kɛhã Yehowa akɛ amɛye anɔkwa. No hewɔ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔná nɔmimaa akɛ, yɛ Afii Akpe Nɔyeli lɛ mli lɛ, Yesu kɛ mɛi 144,000 lɛ baajɛ suɔmɔ mli amɛye amɛbua wɔ ni wɔfee nɔ ni ja, ni fiofio lɛ wɔye emuu. Agbɛnɛ hu, amɛbaaye amɛbua wɔ koni esha ni wɔná kɛjɛ Adam kɛ Hawa ŋɔɔ lɛ aje wɔhe kwraa. Kpɔmɔnɔ lɛ hewɔ ni amɛbaanyɛ amɛfee nibii kpakpai nɛɛ fɛɛ lɛ. Be ni Afii Akpe Nɔyeli lɛ baaba naagbee lɛ, adesai fɛɛ ni yɔɔ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ lɛ baaye emuu tamɔ bɔ ni Yehowa to kɛjɛ shishijee lɛ.—Kpo. 22:1, 2.

16 Yesu, wɔ-Sɔfonukpa lɛ le naagbai ni wɔkɛkpeɔ kɛ bɔ ni wɔnuɔ he wɔhãa lɛ jogbaŋŋ ejaakɛ ehi shi akɛ gbɔmɔ yɛ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ pɛŋ. (Kanemɔ Hebribii 4:15.) Ani bei komɛi lɛ onuɔ he akɛ etɔ bo? Yesu hu nu tɔlɛ he. (Yoh. 4:6) Ani bei komɛi lɛ hɔmɔ kɛ kumai yeɔ bo? Hɔmɔ kɛ kumai ye Yesu hu. (Mat. 4:2; Yoh. 19:28) Ani mɛi yeɔ ohe fɛo loo amɛteɔ shi amɛwoɔ bo yɛ ohemɔkɛyeli lɛ hewɔ? Afee Yesu hu nakai. (Yes. 50:6; Yoh. 15:20) Ani mɔ ko ni osumɔɔ esane lɛ egbo? Be ni Yesu yɔɔ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ lɛ, enaanyo Lazaro gbo, ni eenyɛ efee akɛ Yosef, mɔ ni ŋɔ lɛ efee ebi lɛ hu gbo. No hewɔ lɛ, wɔbaanyɛ wɔná nɔmimaa akɛ, Yesu, wɔ-Sɔfonukpa lɛ nuɔ wɔ shishi ni ele nɔ ni esa akɛ efee kɛye ebua wɔ ejaakɛ etsɔ shihilɛ ni wɔtsɔɔ mli ŋmɛnɛ lɛ mli pɛŋ.
