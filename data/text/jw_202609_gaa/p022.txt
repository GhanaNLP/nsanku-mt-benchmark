miishɛɛ ejaakɛ amɛshãa afɔlei nɛɛ koni no ahã naanyobɔɔ kpakpa ahi amɛkɛ lɛ teŋ. Agbɛnɛ hu, Yehowa miisumɔ ni ekɛ adesai ni sɔmɔɔ lɛ lɛ abɔ naanyo be fɛɛ be. Akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, be ni Noa je kpo kɛje adeka lɛ mli lɛ, eshã afɔlei kɛda Yehowa shi. (1 Mo. 8:20) Abraham ma afɔleshãa latɛi koni eshã afɔlei yɛ nɔ ehã Yehowa. (1 Mo. 12:7, 8) Hiob hu shã afɔlei ehã ebii lɛ shii abɔ kɛsusumɔ lɛ akɛ ké aleenɔ ni amɛtɔ̃ Yehowa nɔ lɛ ni ekɛke amɛ. (Hiob 1:5) No hewɔ ni Yehowa tsɛ hii komɛi akɛ osɔfoi dani eto gbɛjianɔ koni Israelbii lɛ aná osɔfoi lɛ. Ewie akɛ: ‘Hã hii ni sɔmɔɔ akɛ osɔfoi [loo weku yitsei] ni baa Yehowa ŋɔɔ daa lɛ afee amɛhe krɔŋkrɔŋ, koni Yehowa akatswia amɛ nii.’—2 Mo. 19:22.

7 Mɔ klɛŋklɛŋ ni atsɛ lɛ akɛ osɔfo yɛ Biblia lɛ mli ji Melkizedek. (1 Mo. 14:18) Ejeee osɔfo kɛkɛ; eji maŋtsɛ hu. Afii komɛi asɛɛ lɛ, Yehowa wo shi akɛ Mesia lɛ baaye maŋtsɛ kɛ osɔfo tamɔ Melkizedek. Ewo shi hu akɛ, adesai baanú nibii kpakpai fɛɛ ni Mesia lɛ baafee akɛ maŋtsɛ kɛ osɔfo lɛ he sɛɛ kɛya naanɔ.—Lala 110:4; Heb. 7:17.

8 Tamɔ bɔ ni wɔna lɛ, be ni Yehowa kɛ Israelbii lɛ fee emaŋ, ni etsɔ Mose nɔ ehã amɛ Mla lɛ, ehã amɛ osɔfoi hu. Agbɛnɛ hu, yɛ Mla lɛ mli lɛ, etsɔɔ nɔ ni eesumɔ ni osɔfoi lɛ kɛ osɔfonukpa lɛ afee. Yehowa hala Mose nyɛmi nuu Aaron koni esɔmɔ akɛ osɔfonukpa, ni ehã Aaron bihii lɛ sɔmɔ akɛ osɔfoi. Ni kɛjɛ no nɔ kɛyaa lɛ, Aaron seshibii lɛ pɛ ni sɔmɔ akɛ osɔfoi. (2 Mo. 40:12-15) Yehowa kɛwo osɔfoi lɛ adɛŋ akɛ amɛ amɛshã afɔlei. Osɔfonukpa lɛ ji mɔ ni shãa afɔlei ehãa maŋ muu lɛ fɛɛ, shi ké Israelnyo ko ba koni ebashã afɔle lɛ, osɔfoi krokomɛi lɛ ji mɛi ni shãa amɛhãa lɛ. Hii krokomɛi ni jɛ Levi weku lɛ mli lɛ yeɔ amɛbuaa osɔfoi lɛ.

Agbɛnɛ hu, Yehowa tsɔɔ afɔlei srɔtoi ni esa akɛ ashã, nɔ hewɔ ni esa akɛ ashã, kɛ bɔ ni esa akɛ ashã amɛ ahã. Nibii ni Yehowa hã osɔfonukpa lɛ kɛ osɔfoi lɛ fee lɛ hã ana akɛ wɔsɛɛ lɛ, ebaaye ebua adesai yɛ gbɛ ni hi jogbaŋŋ nɔ.

9 Be ni abaptisi Yesu lɛ, nibii enyɔ komɛi ni he hiaa waa ba. (1) Mfonirifeemɔŋ sɔlemɔ shĩa lɛ ba shihilɛ mli. (2) Yesu batsɔ Osɔfonukpa yɛ sɔlemɔ shĩa nɛɛ mli. (Heb. 8:1) Sɛɛ mli lɛ, Yesu kɛ ewala akɛ adesa ni eye emuu lɛ shã afɔle koni ekɛkpãtã adesai ahe eshai lɛ kwraa. Afɔle ni eshã lɛ hi kwraa fe afɔlei ni osɔfoi lɛ kɛ kooloi shã yɛ Israel lɛ. Mɛni hewɔ? Ejaakɛ afɔlei ni akɛ kooloi shã lɛ nyɛŋ ajie eshã kɛya kwraa, shi Yesu afɔle lɛ feɔ nakai. Ŋmɛnɛ lɛ, Yesu miiya nɔ eesɔmɔ lolo akɛ wɔ-Sɔfonukpa, ni etamɔ briji ni wɔtsɔɔ nɔ kɛyaa Yehowa ŋɔɔ. (Heb. 9:24) Kɛ́ eshɛ be ni Yesu, wɔ-Sɔfonukpa kɛ Maŋtsɛ lɛ, baaye shikpɔŋ muu lɛ fɛɛ nɔ lɛ, mɛi 144,000 lɛ baafata ehe kɛye nɔ akɛ maŋtsɛmɛi kɛ osɔfoi. (1 Pet. 1:3, 4; 2:9) Yɛ nakai beiaŋ lɛ, Yehowa kɛ Yesu kɛ mɛi 144,00 lɛ baaye amɛbua adesai fɛɛ ni miisumɔ ni amɛbo Yehowa toi lɛ koni amɛye emuu, ni agbɛnɛ hu, amɛhã shikpɔŋ lɛ atsɔ paradeiso. Wɔkɛ miishɛɛ miikpa be nɛɛ gbɛ waa!

10 Tamɔ bɔ ni wɔna lɛ, kɛjɛ blema tɔ̃ɔ ni Yehowa to gbɛjianɔ ni adesai aná osɔfoi koni no ahã amɛnyɛ amɛkɛ lɛ abɔ naanyo. Afɔlei babaoo ni Israelbii lɛ shã lɛ hã amɛna akɛ ehe miihia ni amɛná afɔle ko ni hi fe nomɛi, ni no ji Yesu wala akɛ adesa ni ekɛshã afɔle lɛ. Yɛ Afii Akpe Nɔyeli lɛ mli lɛ, Yesu kɛ mɛi 144,000 lɛ baasɔmɔ akɛ osɔfoi kɛyashi adesai baaye emuu. Kɛkɛ lɛ gbɛ ko kãŋ adesai kɛ Yehowa teŋ dɔŋŋ, ni no hewɔ lɛ, osɔfoi ahe ehiaŋ dɔŋŋ.
