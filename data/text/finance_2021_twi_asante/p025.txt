ASOƐEƐ A ƐHWƐ OGYA - AHOƆDEN SO
<<<P>>>
29. Sɛdeɛ Aban bɛtumi ama ɔmanfoɔ anya Ogya - Ahoɔden a ɛgyina so nti no, afe 2020 mu no ɔyɛɛ deɛ ɛdidi soo yi:
<<<P>>>
• Ɔwiee 200MW Amandi Anyinam dwumadie no mu ɔha nkyekyɛmu aduɔkron nwɔtwe (98%), na seesei wɔadi dwuma no ama aka ɔfa kakra bi a ɛrenkyɛre biara yɛbɛpa ho ntoma;
<<<P>>>
• Ɔde mpɔtam ahodoɔ ahanum aduosia (560) mu dodoɔ ahasa aduɔwɔtwe nwɔtwe (388) bɛkaa ɔman ogya-ahoɔden nhoma no ho, ma ɛpagyaa anyinam ahoɔden ho akwannya mu firii ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduɔwɔtwe enum, akyiri pɔ hwee (85.0%) wɔ Ɔpɛpɔn bosome mu kɔduruu ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduɔwɔtwe enum, akyiri pɔ mmienu (85.2%) wɔ afe 2020 bosome Ɔpɛnimma awieeɛ no mu, na ɛnam mpɔtam ogya-ahoɔden nhyehyɛeɛ so ama mpɔtam bɛboro mpem edu (10,000) anya anyinam ahoɔden.
<<<P>>>
• Ɔgyinaa nkuro nketewa mu mframagya (LPG) ho nhyehyɛeɛ dwumadie so kyekyɛɛ ‘sotoofo’ a ɛde mframagya di dwuma dodoɔ mpem ahanum (1,500).
<<<P>>>
30. Afe 2021 mu no, Aban bɛkɔ so de n’áni adi dwuma a ɛdidi soɔ yi akyi ahwɛ ama adi mu:
<<<P>>>
• ɔbɛwie Anyinam ahoɔden ho dwumadie 1B (1B of the Early Power Project) no fa a ɛdi kan no, apagya emu ahoɔden no akɔduru 202MW;
<<<P>>>
• ɔbɛtoa Anyinam ahoɔden ho nsɛm a wɔgu so no (PPA Renegotiations) ho dwumadie no so ;
<<<P>>>
• ɔbɛhwɛ ama Pwalugu asutadeɛ a ɛboa dwumadie ahodoɔ (Pwalugu Multipurpose dam) ho dwumadie adi mu;
<<<P>>>
• ɔbɛsiesie anaa ɔbɛsesa T3 mframagya Ntwaho-afidie (T3 Gas Turbines) no;
<<<P>>>
• ɔbɛwie (Kumasi-Kintampo) hɔ anyinam ahoɔden nhomatwe dwumadie) no ɔfa a ɛdi kan (Lot1) wɔ Kumasi-Bolgatanga anyinam ahoɔden nhomatwe no mu.
