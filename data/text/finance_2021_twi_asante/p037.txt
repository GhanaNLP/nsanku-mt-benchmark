<<<P>>>
Famekwa Ntoasoɔ Sukuukorɔ (FREE SHS): Famekwa Ntoasoɔ Sukuukorɔ yi yɛ Aban nhyehyɛeɛ foforɔ a Ɔmanpanin Nana Akuffo-Addo aban de puee edwa wɔ Ɛbɔ bosome mu wɔ afe 2017 mu sɛ JHS osukuuni biara a ɔbɛbɔ ne ho mmɔden wɔ sɔhwɛ mu no, ɔbɛnya ɛkwan wɔ Ntoasoɔ Sukuu mu akɔsua adeɛ a ɔrentua hwee.
<<<P>>>
Sikasɛm ho Gyinaeɛsie Mmara (Fiscal Responsibility Act): Yei yɛ mmara ahodoɔ a ɛhyɛ Aban ketee na ɛkyerɛkyerɛ sɛdeɛ ɛsɛ sɛ yɛde sika a ɛfiri ɛtoɔ mu ba no di dwuma.
<<<P>>>
Sika a yɛde kyɛ Aban (Grants): Yeinom yɛ sika ho mmoa ahodoɔ a Mpontuo mu Aboafoɔ de boa Aban a ɛnyɛ adeɛ a Aban bɛtua.
<<<P>>>
Sika a wɔtua ma Aban Nnwuma ahodoɔ (Grants to other Government Units): Yei gyina hɔ ma sika a, mmara hyɛ sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wɔtua kɔgu Mansini Mmarahyɛbadwa Fotoɔ “DACF” no mu, Lɔrekwan Fotoɔ “Road Fund” mu, Nwomasua Fotoɔ “GETFUND” mu ne Fango ho Dwumadie Fotoɔ “Petroleum Related Fund” mu.
<<<P>>>
Ɔman mu Adwadeɛ Nyinaa Bo A Ɛsom (Gross Domestic Product (GDP): Yei kyerɛ nneɛma a yɛyɛ wɔ ɔman yi mu wɔ ɛberɛ pɔtee bi mu no nyinaa bo a ɛsom.
<<<P>>>
Amannɔne Sika dodoɔ A Ɔman Wɔ (Gross International Reserves):Yei yɛ susudua a yɛfa so hunu sɛdeɛ ɔman bi sikasɛm ahoɔden a ɔwɔ sɛ ɔbɛtumi atɔ nneɛma afiri amannɔne no teɛ wɔ ɛberɛ pɔtee bi mu. Yei boa ma yɛde ɔman bi fotoɔ toto ɔman foforɔ bi deɛ ho a yɛhunu sɛdeɛ ɔman no sikasɛm ahoɔden a ɔwɔ no teɛ.
<<<P>>>
Nneɛma boɔ sorokɔ (Inflation): Yei gyina hɔ ma sɛdeɛ dwumadie ahodoɔ ne adwadeɛ boɔ si kɔ soro wɔ ɛberɛ pɔtee bi mu.
