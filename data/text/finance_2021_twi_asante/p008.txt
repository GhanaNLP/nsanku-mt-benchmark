• Sika dodoɔ a na aka mu a yɛhia de asɔ yɛn sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ mu sika nyinaa so no yɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu dubaako ne akyire pɔ nson (11.7 percent of GDP) a kane no na ɛyɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu dubaako ne akyire pɔ ɛnan (11.4 percent of GDP);

Sika A Ɛbaa Ɔman Fotoɔ Mu
<<<P>>>
4. Afe 2020 mu no, Aban de nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ guu apono so a ɔbɛfa so anya sika, a sika a wɔde bɛkyɛ ɔman no ka ho bi. Na Aban de sii n'ani so sɛ sika a ɔbɛnya no bɛduru Ghana sika sidi ɔpepepem aduonum mmiɛnsa ne ɔpepem nson (GH¢53.7 billion) na yei na ɔde bɛtu dwumadie ne mpontunnwuma ahodoɔ ho anammɔn. Ɔpɛnimma bosome baa awieeɛ wɔ afe 2020 mu no, na yɛn nsa aka Ghana sika dodoɔ ɔpepepem aduonum enum ne ɔpepem baako (GH¢55.1 billion) a na ɛboro deɛ yɛde yɛn ani buu sɛ yɛbɛnya no so ɔpepepem baako ne ɔpepem enum (GH¢1.5 billion). Yei tumi baa so ɛsiane adwumaden a yɛyɛɛ wɔ Amannɔne Edwadie ho Ɛtoɔgyegyeɛ mu a yɛnam Amannɔne Edwadie ho Ntotoeɛ (Integrated Customs Management System -ICUMS) ho anammɔntuo so maa yɛtumi gyegyee sika dodoɔ. Hyehyɛpono 1 yi kyerɛ sɛdeɛ na yɛn sikasɛm teɛ wɔ mmeaeɛ ahodoɔ a yɛnya sika firi no wɔ afe 2020 mu.
