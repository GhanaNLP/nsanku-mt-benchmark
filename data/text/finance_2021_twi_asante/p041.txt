Asɛntire: “Yɛbɛwie dwumadie ama aseɛ atim na yɛatoa deɛ aka so nam so akanyan ɔman sikasɛm”
<<<P>>>
Dwumadie akyiridie (Surveillance): Yei yɛ ɛkwan a yɛfa so de ani di biribi akyi na yɛhwɛ sɛdeɛ ɛsi teɛ.
<<<P>>>
Fannyɛ (Transparency): Yei ne sɛ Aban bɛma ɔman mma ahunu deɛ ɛrekɔ so na wɔate amaneɛbɔ nyinaa wɔ ɛberɛ ano ɛberɛ ano.
<<<P>>>
Adekyerɛ ne Adesua ho Akadeɛ -TLM (Teaching And Learning Materials): Yeinom yɛ nneɛma a nkwa wɔ mu ne deɛ nkwa nni mu anaa sɛ nnipa anaa abɔdeɛ afoforɔ bi a ɔkyerɛkyerɛni de di dwuma wɔ adekyerɛ ne adesua mu sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a ɛbɛboa ama adekyerɛ ne adesua mu botaeɛ no awie pɛyɛ.
<<<P>>>
Adwadeɛ ne Dwumadie ho sika (Use of Goods and Services): Yei yɛ sika dodoɔ a Aban tua wɔ dwuma a ɔdi ne dwumadie a ɔdi ma ɔmanfoɔ no ho.
<<<P>>>
Adwadeɛ ho ɛtoɔ (Value Added Tax (VAT): Yei yɛ ɛtoɔ bi a yɛde to adwadeɛ ahodoɔ so wɔ adwadeɛyɛ mpɛmpɛnsoɔ biara mu.
