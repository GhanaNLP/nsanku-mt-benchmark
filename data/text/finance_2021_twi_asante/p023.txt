ASOƐEƐ A ƐHWƐ EDWADIE NE MFIDINNWUMA SO
<<<P>>>
27. Afe 2020 mu no, Aban faa mfidinnwuma mu nsakraeɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ so sakraa mfidinnwuma mu, a ne botaeɛ ne sɛ ɛbɛboa ama Ɔman no atumi abɛyɛ adwadeɛyɛ fapem wɔ Abibirem Atɔeɛ Aman ne mpo Abibirem aman nyinaa mu. Afe 2020 mu no Aban dii dwuma a ɛdidi soɔ yi:
<<<P>>>
Ɔgyinaa Mansini Baako Afididwuma Baako nhyehyɛeɛ so (“1D1F) toaa dwuma ahodoɔ ahanu aduasa mmienu (232) a na emu biara aduru mpɛmpɛnsoɔ pɔtee bi no so. Seesei, emu aduɔson nsia (76) ho anammɔntuo aba awieeɛ ama afiri aseɛ redi dwuma, ɛnna emu ɔha ne nson (107) nso, wɔgu ɛho dwumadie so ;
<<<P>>>
De sika boaa nnwuma a na wɔwɔ akwannya sɛ wɔyɛ Mansini Baako Afididwuma Baako nnwumakuo (1D1F), bobɔɔ wɔn mu binom bosea, maa wɔn mu binom nyaa ɛtotua ho faahodie ho mmoa, ma afoforɔ nso, wɔde mpuntunneɛ sɛ ebia lɔre akwan, nsuo ne nkanea kɔɔ baabi a wɔn mfidinnwuma no wɔ hɔ. ;
<<<P>>>
Penee so maa ‘Mansini Baako Afididwuma Baako’ (1D1F) Nnwumakuo aduasa nson (37) antua sika dodoɔ ɔpepem ahansia ne mmiɛnsa (GH¢603 million) nneɛma a na wɔakra ho ɛtoɔ;
<<<P>>>
Gyinaa Mansini Baako Afididwuma Baako nnwumakuo (1D1F) ahodoɔ anan mu ne sikakorabea Nnwumakuo a wɔne wɔn di dwuma dii nsawɔsoɔ nam so nyaa bosea ɔpepepem mmienu ne ɔpepem ahasa (GH¢ 2.3billion) kyekyɛ maa wɔn. Aban gyinaa Mansini Baako Afididwuma Baako nnwumakuo (1D1F) no anan
