37. Sɛdeɛ aban bɛma obiara atumi akɔ sukuu anya nwomasua a ɛdi mu nti no, afe 2020 mu no Aban yɛɛ deɛ ɛdidi so yi:
<<<P>>>
• Ɔtoaa so hwɛ maa ntoasoɔ sukuu ne adantam nwomasua ho adesuadeɛ nhyehyɛeɛ pa a ɛfirii aseɛ afe 2017 mu no dii mu kɔɔ so yɛɛ adwuma. ɔmaa ɔman yi mu Kindergarten (KG) kɔsi gyinapɛn 6 adesuafoɔ akyerɛkyerɛfoɔ dodoɔ 153,000 nteteeɛ sane tetee sukuu mu aboafoɔ no dodoɔ 290 sɛdeɛ wɔbɛtumi ahwɛ ama kindagaten kɔsi gyinapɛn 6 adesuadeɛ no mu nhyehyɛeɛ adi mu;
<<<P>>>
• Ɔhwɛ gyee 956 adesuadeɛ ho nwoma a ɛgyina adesuadeɛ ho nhyehyɛeɛ foforɔ koraa so no too mu. Ntoasoɔ ne adantam ho nhyehyɛeɛ no fa a ɛtɔ so mmienu no a ɛyɛ adesuadeɛ a ɛsɛ sɛ obiara sua no nso, Aban asoafoɔ agyinatukuo (cabinet) gyee adesuadeɛ a ɛfa ntoasoɔ ne adantam sukuu ho no too mu;
<<<P>>>
• Ɔsisii nwomakorabea ahodoɔ aduonu (20) wɔ ɔman yi afanan nyinaa nam so maa ɔman yi mu nwomakorabea no dodoɔ kɔduruu aduɔkron (90), ɔsiesiee nwomakorabea a ɛyɛ Aban dea edu (10), ne ntoasoɔ sukuu nwomakorabea aduasa mmiɛnsa (33) sɛdeɛ ɛbɛma akenkan ne adesua akɔ nkan;
<<<P>>>
