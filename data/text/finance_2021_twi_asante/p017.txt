Aban nam ‘Bra Efie ho Dwumadie’ ‘Operation Return Home’ nhyehyɛeɛ no so boa maa Ghanafoɔ a na ɛsiane akwantuo ne akɔneabadie so ɔhyɛ wɔ ɛberɛ a na COVID-19 yadeɛ kontonkurowi yi rekɔ so enti na wɔwɔ ahokyere mu wɔ akwantuo mu no ma wɔbaa efie;
<<<P>>>
Aban tuu anammɔn bɔɔ fotoɔ a yɛato edin Coronavirus Asetɔre Nhyehyɛeɛ A Ɛfa Nnwuma Mu Mmoa Ho (Coronavirus Alleviation Programme Business Support Scheme -CAP-BuSS) de Ghana sika sidi ɔpepem ahanson aduonum (GH¢750 million) hyɛɛ aseɛ a botaeɛ ne sɛ ɛbɛboa Nnwuma Nketewa, Adantam ne Akɛseɛ Kakra (Micro, Small and Medium-Sized Enterprises-MSMEs) ama wɔatumi agyina yadeɛ kontonkurowi ho nsunsuansoɔ no ano na yei mu no, nnwuma ahodoɔ mpem ahanu aduɔson nson ne ahanum ne dubaako (277,511 businesses) nyaa mmoa a sika a ɛkɔɔ mu no yɛ ɔpepem ahanan ne dummienu ne mpem aduɔwɔtwe nwɔtwe (GH¢412.88 million); ɛnna afei
<<<P>>>
Aban bɔɔ Ɔman COVID-19 Ho Fotoɔ (COVID-19 National Trust Fund) a ɔpɛɛ Ghana sika sidi ɔpepem aduonum nson ne mpem dumiɛnsa (GH¢ 57.13 million) too mu a ɛduruu Ɔpɛnimma 31 2020 mu no, na yɛde emu ɔpepem aduanan enum ne mpem aduonu mmienu (GH¢45.22 million) adi dwuma a ebi ne yadeɛ no anosie ho akadeɛ (PPEs) a yɛtotɔeɛ ne ayaresa ho nneɛma a yɛde kɔɔ ayaresabea ahodoɔ a wɔhwɛ COVID-19 ho nsɛm, nnuane a yɛde maa wɔn a wɔanyini, mmɔborɔwafoɔ ne ahiafoɔ ɛnna ahyɛn a yɛtotɔ maa Ayaresabea ahodoɔ a wɔsa COVID-19 yadeɛ kontonkurowi yi wɔ hɔ (COVID-19 Treatment Centres) ɛnna anammɔntuo afoforɔ a ɛkeka ho.
