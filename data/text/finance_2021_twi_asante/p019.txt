Asɛntire: “Yɛbɛwie dwumadie ama aseɛ atim na yɛatoa deɛ aka so nam so akanyan ɔman sikasɛm”
<<<P>>>
Ɔman no bɛsane asi ne dada mu na yɛn nsa aka deɛ yɛahwere no, nam so ama nnwuma abu so ama ɔmanfoɔ anya bi ayɛ. Aban wɔ gyidie na wasi nkete nso sɛ, ɔnam Ghana COVID-19 Asetɔre Ne Ankorankorɛ Nnwuma Mu Nkanyan ho Mmoa (Ghana CARES) dwumadie so bɛma sikasɛm mu nkanyan ne mpontuo a ɛfiri adwadeɛyɔ mu aba na yei bɛma nnwuma afoforɔ a ɛdi mu na akatua no yɛ kama nso abu so, titire wɔ ankorankorɛ dwumadie mu.
<<<P>>>
23. Dwumadie ahodoɔ a ɛbɛdi akotene a wɔbɛtu ho anammɔn wɔ saa Ghana COVID-19 Asetɔre Ne Ankorankorɛ Nnwuma Mu Nkanyan ho Mmoa (Ghana CARES) dwumadie mu na ɛdidi soo yi:
<<<P>>>
• wɔbɛboa ama kuadwuma ase atrɛ na ayɛ akɔnnɔ na ɛnam so ama mmabunu a wɔasua nwoma ani agye ho sɛ wɔbɛkɔ kuadwuma mu;
<<<P>>>
• wɔbɛbuebue mfidinnwuma ne adwadeɛyɔ nnwuma nketewa wɔ Ghanaman mu;
<<<P>>>
• wɔbɛboa ama mfidie ho nimdeɛ ne abɛɛfo nkutahodie nnwuma atu mpɔn wɔ Ɔman yi mu;
<<<P>>>
• wɔbɛhwɛ sɛ abɛɛfo nkutahodie akwanhodoɔ bɛgye nhini ntɛm so na yɛde adi dwuma wɔ yɛn abrabɔ mu;
<<<P>>>
• wɔbɛma Ghana Afie ne Adansie Adwuma no atu mpɔn;
<<<P>>>
• wɔbɛma Ghanaman ayɛ beaeɛ a mpontuo kɔ so pa ara wɔ Abibirem Atɔeɛ fam ha;
<<<P>>>
• yɛbɛyɛ nsakraeɛ anaa yɛbɛma nkanyan aba Aban Dwumadie A Ɛdi Mu no ho anammɔntuo mu; ɛnna afei
<<<P>>>
• yɛbɛbue Ghana Mpontuo Sikakorabea (Development Bank of Ghana) a ne botaeɛ ne sɛ ɛbɛhwɛ de sika ahyɛ mpontunnwuma ahodoɔ a yɛbɛtu ho anammɔn wɔ Ghana COVID-19 Asetɔre Ne Ankorankorɛ Nnwuma Mu Nkanyan ho Mmoa (Ghana CARES) dwumadie ase no nyinaa mu.
