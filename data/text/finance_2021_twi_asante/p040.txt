Asɛntire: “Yɛbɛwie dwumadie ama aseɛ atim na yɛatoa deɛ aka so nam so akanyan ɔman sikasɛm”
<<<P>>>
Ɔman Sikasɛm Ntotoeɛ Mu Nsakraeɛ Dwumadie - PFMRD (Public Financial Management Reforms Project): Saa sikasɛm ntotoeɛ mu nsakraeɛ yi mpontuo botaeɛ ne sɛ Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ ho ntotoeɛ bɛkɔ yie, yɛbɛnya ahohyɛsoɔ wɔ sikasɛm ho anammɔntuo mu na yɛahwɛ abɔ Ghana Aban sika a ɔde di dwuma ho amaneɛ wɔ ɛkwan pa so wɔ ɛberɛ a ɛsɛ mu. Saa sikasɛm ntotoeɛ yi nam nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛdi mu ne sikasɛm adwumayɛfoɔ pɔtee bi nteteeɛ so boa ma sikasɛm ho akwankyerɛ so-die tu mpɔn, ɛboa ma yɛde sika di dwuma a ɛhia mapa na afei, ɛboa ma yɛdi yɛn dwuma yie ma ɔmanfoɔ nya ho mfasoɔ.
<<<P>>>
Sikasɛm Mpontuo a ɛsi ani soɔ (Projected Growth Rate): Yei yɛ sɛdeɛ ani da ɛkwan sɛ Ghana sikasɛm bɛtu mpɔn wɔ afe pɔtee bi mu.

<<<P>>>
(PFI): Private Finance Initiative.-

Sika A Aban Nya (Revenue): Yei yɛ sika dodoɔ a Aban nya firi ne dwumadie ahodoɔ a ɔdi wɔ ɔman yi mu ne amannɔne.
<<<P>>>
Mmoayɛn A Ɛde Nnwuma Ba - RFJ (Rearing For Jobs): Yei yɛ Ghana Aban mpontuo ho dwumadie foforɔ a ɔde aba a ɛnam so bɛma ntɔtemmoayɛn adwuma no adi mu na sika aba mu sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a, ntɔtemmoa nam bɛdɔɔso ama ɔmanfoɔ anya bi awe na yɛate ntɔtemmoa nam a yɛkra ba ɔman yi mu no so koraa, na ɛnam so ama nnwuma abu so a ɔmanfoɔ bɛnya bi ayɛ ama wɔn a wɔdi ntɔtemmoa ho dwuma nyinaa no asetenam nso atu mpɔn.
<<<P>>>
Asetena mu Mmoa Sika (Social Benefits): Yei yɛ sika a Aban gyina ɔmanfoɔ anan mu tua ma nsuo, pɛtro ne nkanea nnwumakuo no de sɔ Ghanafoɔ asene.
<<<P>>>
Asenesɔ Sika (Subsidy): Yei yɛ sika mu mmoa bi a Aban de ma ɔman mma ma ɛyi adesoa kakra firi wɔn so ma wɔn kɔn mu dwo wɔn.
