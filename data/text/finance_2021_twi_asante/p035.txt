Asɛntire: “Yɛbɛwie dwumadie ama aseɛ atim na yɛatoa deɛ aka so nam so akanyan ɔman sikasɛm”
<<<P>>>
Nsɛmfua Asekyerɛ (Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ mu Nsɛmfua)
<<<P>>>
Mansini Baako Afididwuma Baako (1D1F): Yei yɛ mfidinnwuma ahodoɔ a yɛbɛbuebue wɔ mansini mu na ɛnam so ama mfidinnwuma atu mpɔn wɔ Ghana. Yei botaeɛ ne sɛ nnwuma bɛbu so wɔ ɔman yi mu na Ghanafoɔ a wɔwɔ ɔman yi afanan nyinaa anya adwuma ayɛ.
<<<P>>>
Nkontabuo (Accountability): Yei gyina hɔ ma nkonta a Aban bu kyerɛ Ɔman mma fa ne dwumadie ho ne amaneɛbɔ a ɛwɔ anammɔn ahodoɔ nso a watu ho. Sikasɛm ne agyapadeɛ ahodoɔ a Aban hwɛ so ma ɔmanfoɔ no nyinaa ka yei ho.
<<<P>>>
Sika Mu Nkyekyɛmu (Allocation): Yei yɛ ɛkwan a wɔfa so kyekyɛ ɔman sika mu ma Aban asoɛeɛ ahodoɔ no ne nnwumakuo ahodoɔ no ma wɔde di dwuma ma ɔmanfoɔ.
<<<P>>>
Ɔhaw-anosie (Alleviation): Yei yɛ ɛkwan wɔfa so ma ɔhaw bi ho nsunsuansoɔ ano brɛ ase.
<<<P>>>
Abɔdeɛ mu nyansapɛ, Abɛɛfo Nkutahodie ho nimdeɛ, Mfidie ho nimdeɛ ne Nkontabuo- BSTEM (Basic Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics): Yei yɛ nsɛmfua bi a yɛde gyina hɔ ma saa adesuadeɛ ahodoɔ yi. Yɛde saa nsɛmfua yi di dwuma wɔ ɛberɛ a yɛreyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ bi wɔ nwomasua mu na yɛayɛ adesuadeɛ ho ntotoeɛ a ɛboa ma mpontuo ba nwomasua mu fa saa adesuadeɛ yi ho.
<<<P>>>
Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ (Budget): Sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ gyina hɔ ma nhyehyɛeɛ a Aban ayɛ afa ɛkwan a ɔbɛfa so anya sika ne deɛ ɔde saa sika no bɛyɛ ho. Nhwɛsoɔ ne sɛ, abusua bi bɛtumi ayɛ sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ nam so akyerɛ sika dodoɔ a wɔwɔ ne nneɛma ahodoɔ a ɛhia sɛ wɔde sika no yɛ no nnidisoɔ nnidisoɔ. Ɛbɛtumi ayɛ ɛdan ho ɛka, nsuo ne nkanea ɛka ɛnna aduane ho ɛka) Ɛkɔba sɛ wohia sika no dodoɔ a, ɛho bɛhia sɛ abusua no bɛhwehwɛ sika no bi aka ho anaa mpo wɔbɛkɔ akɔbɔ bosea.
