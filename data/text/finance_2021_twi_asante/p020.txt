MPONTUO A AKƆ SO WƆ ASOƐEƐ AHODOƆ NO MU NE SƐDEƐ ƐTEƐ SEESEI
<<<P>>>
24. Saa ɔfa yi kasa fa mmɔden a asoɛeɛ ahodoɔ binom bɔɔ wɔ afe 2020 mu, sane da dwumadie ne nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛgu akwan mu ma wɔn wɔ afe 2021 mu adi. Ghanaman Animanim yi mu Mpontuo botaeɛ (Medium-Term National Development Agenda 2018-2021) ne Ghanaman mpɛmpɛnsoɔ a yɛnnkɔ boseabɔ bio (Ghana Beyond Aid Agenda) nhyehyɛeɛ so na asoɛeɛ ahodoɔ no dwumadie ne wɔn nhyehyɛeɛ no gyina. Wɔn dwumadie ne nhyehyɛeɛ no botaeɛ foforɔ nso ne sɛ ɛbɛma nhyehyɛeɛ a wɔato edin ‘Ghana COVID-19 Asetɔre Ne Ankorankorɛ Nnwuma Mu Nkanyan ho Mmoa (Ghana CARES) dwumadie’ (Government’s CARES) no awie mudie, na afei asane aboa ama deɛ ɛsi ani so fa Ɔman yi ne amanaman ntam nsawɔsodie ho wɔ Amanaman Nkabom Ekuo Anisoadehunu 2030 (UN Agenda 2030) (SDGs) ne Abibiman Nkabom Ekuo anisoadehunu 2063 (African Union Agenda 2063) ho no aba mu.
