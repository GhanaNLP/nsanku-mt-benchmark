Asɛntire: "Yɛbɛwie dwumadie ama aseɛ atim na yɛatoa deɛ aka so nam so akanyan ɔman sikasɛm"
<<<P>>>
Ase Dwumadie yi yɛ dwumadie ahodoɔ a ɛdi akotene wɔ NPP Aban nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ mu no baako a botaeɛ a ɛtaa akyire ne sɛ, yɛde mpontunneɛ bɛkɔ nkuraase, titire mpotam ahodoɔ a ohia wɔ hɔ no na ɛnam so aboa atu ohia ase na asetenam yiedie mu nsonsonoeɛ a ɛda nkuraasefoɔ ne kuromfoɔ ntam no asesa.
<<<P>>>
Bosea-Tua (Loan Repayment): Yei yɛ bosea a yɛbɔ firi aman ne sikakorabea ahodoɔ hɔ de yɛ mpontunnwuma a yɛsane tua. Yeinom bɛtumi ayɛ wɔn a wɔwɔ ɔman yi mu anaa amannɔne.
<<<P>>>
Nneɛma a ɛboa ma ɔman sikasɛm gyina a ɛsi ani so (Macroeconomic targets): Yeinom yɛ susudua a wɔgyina so kyerɛ ɔman bi sikasɛm ho mmɔdemmɔ. Yei ho nhwɛsoɔ ne sikasɛm mpontuo, nneɛmaboɔ sorokɔ, adwumayɛ mu mpontuo mpɛmpɛnsoɔ, ɔman yi ne aman afoforɔ ntam edwadie mu mmɔdemmɔ, sɛdeɛ aban no sikasɛm ntotoeɛ kankorɔ anaa sintɔ teɛ ɛne sɛdeɛ gyinaeɛ ahodoɔ a Ghana Sikakorabea Baatan bɛsie no bɛka nneɛma boɔ sorokɔ wɔ ɔman no mu.
<<<P>>>
Nnwuma Nketewa Mu Mpontuo Dwumadie - MSME (Micro And Small Buisness Developmet Programme): Nnwuma Nketewa yɛ ankorankorɛ nnwuma a wɔtumi fa nnipa kakra bi a dwetire a adwuma no gyina soɔ no mmoro sika dodoɔ pɔtee bi so.
<<<P>>>
Nsɛm Ahodoɔ ho Nhyehyɛeɛ (Miscellaneous Provision): Yei yɛ apam anaa kɔntrat biara ɔfa a ɛfa nsɛm ahodoɔ a ɛbɛtumi asi ne ɛkwan a wɔde bɛfa so wɔ ɛberɛ a apam/kɔntrat no ho anammɔntuo rekɔ so no ho.
<<<P>>>
Ɔhaw-anosie ho Nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ (Mitigating Measures): Yei yɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ a wɔde di dwuma wɔ ɛberɛ a ɔhaw bi asi na wɔpɛ sɛ wɔma ɛho nsunsuansoɔ no ano brɛ ase.
<<<P>>>
Ɔman-kyekyere Atuhoakyɛfoɔ- NABCo (Nation Builders Corps): Yei yɛ adwene foforɔ koraa a Ghana Aban de puee edwa a ɔnam so bɛma mmeranteɛ ne mmabaawa a wɔawie asuapɔn no bɛnya adwuma ayɛ wɔ ankorankorɛ anaa Aban nnwuma mu na wɔaboa de wɔn nimdeɛ apɛ
