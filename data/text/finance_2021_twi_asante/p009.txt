5. Bosome Ɔpɛnimma baa awieeɛ wɔ afe 2020 mu no, na Aban de Ghana sika sidi ɔpepepem ɔha baako ne ɔpepem baako (GH¢100.1 billion) adi dwuma a ɛka a na ɛwɔ hɔ dada ansa na Aban no reba no nso ka ho, wɔ ɛberɛ a, na yɛde asi yɛn ani so sɛ yɛde Ghana sika sidi ɔpepepem aduɔkron nson ne ɔpepem nson (GH¢97.7 billion) na yɛde bɛdi dwuma. Bosea ho nsiho ne Ɛka afoforɔ a na yɛtua no deɛ, ammoro yɛn so. Sika ahodoɔ a yɛtuaa no wɔ dwumadie afoforɔ ho no nyinaa boroo deɛ na yɛde ani abu sɛ yɛbɛtua, sɛdeɛ hyehyɛpono 2 kyerɛ no. Deɛ ɛmaa sika a yɛtuaa no kɔɔ soro wɔ dwumadie no bi mu no ne deɛ ɛdidi soɔ yi:
