The Meditation of My Heart (N Sukyiri Poɔ Teɛrɛ)
<<<P>>>
YƐLƐ TE NAŊ NA ZANNE
<<<P>>>
Ka teneeŋ wa piili teɛrɛ yɛlɛ mine naŋ ba kyɛnɛ soŋ te zemmo poɔŋ, ka lɛ vɛŋ ka te enne kyoɔre, bo yɛleŋ na baŋ soŋ te? A zannoo ŋa na soŋ teŋ ka te nyɛ a soo-rebiri ŋa noɔre iribo.
<<<P>>>
Jehoova Na Baŋ Soŋ Boŋ Saŋa Na Ho Eŋɛ Naŋ Wa Kyoɔre
<<<P>>>
“N eɛ nemba-neɛ yaga zaa!”—URO. 7:24.
<<<P>>>
KA HOOŊ wa woŋ bitontona Pɔɔl yuori, bo yɛleŋ maŋ wa ho teɛroŋ poɔŋ? Aminekaŋaŋ, ho na yeliŋ ka o da eɛ misinaare naŋ taa nyaa kpoŋ, a lɛ e wulwulo naŋ kyile, ane neɛ Jehoova naŋ de o vooroŋ soŋ na soŋ oŋ sɛge a Baabole. Ka hooŋ yeli lɛ, yelmeŋɛ hoŋ yeli. Kyɛ, saŋa kaŋa Pɔɔl eŋɛ zaaŋ da maŋ kyoɔre. Ho meŋ eŋɛ daaŋ kyoɔreɛŋ bee? Ka a taaŋ daaŋ pɔge bo, ho na baŋeɛ yɛlɛ na poɔ Pɔɔl naŋ da be na meŋɛ-meŋɛ.
<<<P>>>
2 Kanne Urom 7:21-24. Pɔɔl da sɛgeɛ lɛta te ko a Urom Kristabiiri a wuli lɛ na yɛlɛ naŋ maŋ dɔɔnoo aseŋ wagre mineŋ. Yelmeŋɛ la, Pɔɔl da eɛ Kristabie naŋ taa sagediibu naŋ e kpeɛŋaa. Kyɛ, yɛlɛ mine da be la o eŋɛŋ a dɔɔnoo yaga zaa. Pɔɔl da ba pɛgeyi, azuŋ a da maŋ eɛ kpeɛŋaa koo ka o erɛ yɛlɛ Naaŋmen naŋ boɔrɔ wagre zaa. Kyɛ lɛ yoŋ naane, ka Pɔɔleŋ wa teɛre yɛlɛ na zaa oŋ da e sɛre kyɛ wa leɛ Kristabie na, o poɔ zaaŋ da maŋ sãã. Ka onaŋ wa tuore yelkpeɛŋaa kaŋa naŋ ba gɛrɛ, o eŋɛ zaaŋ da maŋ kyoɔre.
