<<<P>>>
Yaa Nɔ Nɛ O Hla
“Mawu Blɔ Tsɔɔmi”
<<<P>>>
JEHA 2026 JEHA NGMAMI:
“Nihi nɛ a le kaa a hia Mawu blɔ tsɔɔmi ɔ ngɛ bua jɔmi.”
—MAT. 5:3.
<<<P>>>
YEHOWA le adesahi a hiami níhi. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nó ɔ, loko wa ma nyɛ maa hi wami mi ɔ, wa hia niye ní, tade, kɛ hwɔ he. Ke wa nine sui ní nɛ ɔmɛ ekomɛ a nɔ ngɛ be kpiti ko mi ɔ, si himi ɔ mi he waa, aloo wa ma nyɛ ma gbo po. Ngɛ wa he lo nya hiami ní nɛ ɔmɛ a se ɔ, Yehowa bɔ wɔ konɛ wa hla e blɔ tsɔɔmi se blɔ. (Kane Mateo 5:3.) Ke wa suɔ nɛ wa ná bua jɔmi nitsɛnitsɛ ɔ, e sa nɛ waa le kaa wa hia Mawu blɔ tsɔɔmi, né waa ya nɔ nɛ wa hla se blɔ.
<<<P>>>
2 Ke a ke nɔ ko ‘le kaa e hia Mawu blɔ tsɔɔmi’ ɔ mɛni lɔ ɔ tsɔɔ? Hela munyu nɛ a tsɔɔ sisi ke, ‘le kaa e hia’ a, ma nyɛ maa tsɔɔ nɔ ko nɛ e hia yemi kɛ buami nɛ lɔ ɔ ha nɛ e ngɛ ní bae. Moo po nɔ ko nɛ e ngɛ ní bae nɛ e tadehi sɛ mu ngɛ e he, nɛ hwɔ ngɛ lɛ yee ɔ he foni nɛ o hyɛ. Pu tsɔɔ lɛ, nɛ ke je jɔ ɔ, fĩɛ hu yeɔ lɛ. Si fɔfɔɛ nɛ e ngɛ mi nɛ ɔ he je ɔ, nihi sume nɛ e ba a kasa nya. Pɔ tɔɔ e he nɛ e be bua jɔmi ko. Ní balɔ nɛ ɔ le kaa e hia yemi kɛ buami konɛ e ná he wami nɛ e ko gbo. Jã nɔuu kɛ̃ nɛ nɔ nɛ e le kaa e hia Mawu blɔ tsɔɔmi ɔ bɔɔ mɔde kaa e ma bi Mawu konɛ e ye bua lɛ, nɛ ke e kɛ blɔ tsɔɔmi ɔ ha lɛ hu ɔ, e buɔ tue. Jamɛ a nɔ ɔ hu peeɔ klaalo kaa e maa kplɛɛ blɔ tsɔɔmihi nɛ Yehowa kɛ haa nihi nɛ a suɔ lɛ ɔ nɔ.
