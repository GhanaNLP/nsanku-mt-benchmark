<<<P>>>
THE GhanaCARES (OBAATAN PA) PROGRAMME
<<<P>>>
21. Government launched the Ghana COVID-19 Alleviation and Revitalization of Enterprises Support (Ghana CARES) programme on 20th November 2020, to reduce the impact of the pandemic and provide the framework to recover quickly with a stronger and more resilient economy. It is, indeed, a bold vehicle to transform Ghana’s economy to a buoyant, industrialised, prosperous, and more resilient one to better address the fundamental needs of all Ghanaians.
<<<P>>>
22. Ghana CARES is anchored on rapidly increasing productivity to fast-track recovery and engender the creation of decent employment. Through CARES, Government is resolute that economic revitalisation and growth will be driven by productivity and create new and better paying jobs, particularly in the private sector.
<<<P>>>
23. The key projects and programmes that will be implemented under the Ghana CARES Programme include:
<<<P>>>
• supporting commercial farming and attracting educated youth into commercial farming;
<<<P>>>
• building Ghana’s light manufacturing sector;
<<<P>>>
• developing engineering/machine tools and ICT/digital economy industries.
<<<P>>>
• fast tracking digitalisation;
<<<P>>>
• developing Ghana’s Housing & Construction Industry;
<<<P>>>
• establishing Ghana as a Regional Hub;
<<<P>>>
• reviewing/improving implementation of Government Flagships and Key Programmes; and
<<<P>>>
• establishing the Development Bank of Ghana to finance the growth and development agenda under the Ghana CARES Programme.
