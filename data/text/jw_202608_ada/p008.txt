O Ma Nyɛ Maa Ye Satan Kɛ E Daimonio Ɔmɛ A Nɔ Kunimi!
<<<P>>>
“Wa ngɛ atlibo ko fiae, pi waa kɛ he lo kɛ muɔ, se mohu waa kɛ . . . mumi yayamihi babauu nɛ a ngɛ hiɔwe ní ɔmɛ a mi ɔ.”—EFE. 6:12.
<<<P>>>
LA 129
Wa Ma Fĩ Si Daa
<<<P>>>
OTI NƐ NGƐ NÍ KASEMI Ɔ MI
<<<P>>>
Be fɛɛ be ɔ, mo kai kaa anɔkuale Kristofohi kɛ mumi yayahi lɛ ngɛ ta hwue ɔ nɛ, se pi a kɛ a sibi.
<<<P>>>
KAA Yehowa sɔmɔli ɔ, waa kɛ wa he wui tahi nɛ ngɛ nɔ yae ngɛ je nɛ ɔ mi ɔ mi. (Yes. 2:4; Yoh. 18:36) Se Baiblo ɔ tu ta ko nɛ e sa nɛ wɔ tsuo waa hwu ɔ he munyu, nɛ lɔ ɔ ji, ta nɛ waa kɛ Satan kɛ e daimonio ɔmɛ ngɛ hwue ɔ nɛ. (Kane Efesobi 6:11, 12.) Mɛni blɔ nɔ wa maa gu kɛ ye he nyɛli nɛ a he wa nɛ ɔmɛ a nɔ kunimi? E he hia wawɛɛ nɛ waa ngɔ wa hɛ kɛ fɔ yemi kɛ buami nɛ Yehowa kɛ ngɛ wɔ hae ɔ nɔ. Se e sa nɛ wa yɔse ga tsɔmihi nɛ Satan kɛ e daimonio ɔmɛ kɛ tsuɔ ní konɛ a nine nɛ su waa kɛ wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ wa nɔ ɔ hulɔ.
<<<P>>>
2 Ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ sɛ hlami ɔ mi ɔ, wa kase bɔ nɛ Satan bɔɔ mɔde kaa e ma gbɔjɔ hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi nɛ wa ngɛ ngɛ Yehowa Mawu kɛ Yesu Kristo mi ɔ ha. Ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, wa ma susu blɔ kpahi enyɔ nɛ Satan guɔ nɔ konɛ e kɛ ha nɛ wa kpa Yehowa sɔmɔmi ɔ a he. Kekleekle ɔ, e bɔɔ mɔde kaa e ma pue kake peemi nɛ ngɛ waa kɛ wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ wa kpɛti ɔ. Enyɔne ɔ, e haa nɛ nihi waa wɔ yi mi konɛ e kɛ wo wa he gbeye loo e kɛ jɔ̃ wa kɔni mi nɛ wa kpa fiɛɛmi.
<<<P>>>
SATAN SUƆ NƐ E GBA WA MI
<<<P>>>
3 Satan bɔɔ mɔde kaa e ma puɛ huɛ bɔmi nɛ ngɛ waa kɛ wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ wa kpɛti ɔ. E suɔ nɛ waa te si kɛ wo wa sibi mohu pe nɛ wa maa te si kɛ wo lɛ. Mɛni blɔ nɔ nɛ e guɔ kɛ peeɔ jã?
<<<P>>>
1. Mɛnɔmɛ lɛ Yehowa sɔmɔli tsuo kɛ ngɛ ta hwue ɔ? (Efesobi 6:11, 12)
<<<P>>>
2. Mɛni he wa ma susu ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi?
<<<P>>>
3. Mɛni blɔ nɔ nɛ Satan guɔ konɛ e kɛ gba wa mi?
