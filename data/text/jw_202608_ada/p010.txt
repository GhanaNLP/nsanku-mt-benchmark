7 Blɔ kpa ko hu ngɛ nɛ Satan bɔɔ mɔde kaa e maa gu nɔ konɛ e kɛ puɛ kake peemi nɛ ngɛ wa kpɛti ɔ. Yehowa sɔmɔli je ma slɔɔtohi a nɔ. Wa ngɛ suhi ekpaekpa, nɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ kɛ nó nɛ e bua jɔ he. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, Satan suɔ nɛ waa da ní nɛ ɔmɛ a nɔ konɛ waa nu he kaa waa kɛ wa sibi be nyɛe maa pee huɛ. E suɔ nɛ waa kɛ wa sibi nɛ sã nya nɛ wa mi mi nɛ fu wa sibi. Mɛni o maa pee kɛ tsi Satan blɔ konɛ e ko ngɔ mi gbami kɛ ba wa kpɛti? Ke nyagba ko ba o kɛ o nyɛmi ko nyɛ kpɛti ɔ, nyɛɛ bɔ mɔde kaa nyɛ ma dla nyɛ kpɛti oya nɔuu konɛ nyɛ ngɔ kɛ ke nyɛ sibi faa. (Kane Efesobi 4:25-27.) Moo bɔ mɔde nɛ o ha nɛ suɔmi nɛ o ngɛ kɛ ha nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ ɔ mi nɛ wa wawɛɛ. Mo kai kaa, Yehowa naa wɔ tsuo kaa se nami ngɛ wa he. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, wɔ tsuo wa hia wa sibi kaa bɔ nɛ nɔmlɔ tso ɔ he ní ɔmɛ hia a sibi ɔ. (1 Kor. 12:12) Waa kɛ wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ tsuo pee kake kɛ ngɛ hwue kɛ si Satan kɛ e daimonio ɔmɛ.—1 Pet. 5:8, 9.

9 Blɔ kake hu nɛ Satan guɔ nɔ konɛ e kɛ jɔ̃ wa kɔni mi ji kaa e haa nɛ nihi waa wɔ yi mi. Bɔfo Paulo bɔ wɔ kɔkɔ kaa anɔkuale Kristofohi tsuo kɛ yi mi wami maa kpe. (2 Tim. 3:12) Dami sane nɛ a yi wɔ ɔ hu piɛɛ yi mi nɛ a waa wɔ ɔ he. Ke nihi yi wɔ dami sane ɔ, eko ɔ, wa ma puɛ wa be fuu kɛ wa he wami kɛ susu nó nɛ wa maa pee konɛ a ye dami sane kɛ ha wɔ ɔ he. Ke wa pee jã a, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ pɔ maa tɔ wa he, nɛ wa be be fuu náe kɛ tsu níhi nɛ a he hia a he ní, nɛ ji Matsɛ Yemi ɔ he sane kpakpa a nɛ wa maa jaje ɔ.—Rom. 12:19-21.

10 Mo susu Abraham binyumu Isak nɔ hyɛmi nɔ ɔ he nɛ o hyɛ. Isak ba pee niatsɛ nɛ e ngɛ lohwehi fuu. Enɛ ɔ ha nɛ Filistibi ɔmɛ a hɛ kɔ̃ e nɔ nɛ a tsi vuhi tsuo nɛ e tsɛ Abraham sɔmɔli ɔmɛ tsua a konɛ e ko na nyu. (1 Mose 26:12-18) Isak kɛ e weku ɔ kɛ e lohwe ɔmɛ tsuo hia nyu wawɛɛ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, Isak hia kɛ ho he ko nɛ kɛ Filistibi ɔmɛ a he kɛ ya, nɛ e ya tsua vu kpahingɛ lejɛ ɔ. Se jamɛ a vu ɔmɛ hu, Filistibi ɔmɛ ba kpɔ̃ ngɛ a dɛ. Nó nɛ a pee nɛ ɔ ko nyɛ jɔ̃ Isak kɔni mi wawɛɛ. Se Isak sume nɛ e to hyɛli ɔmɛ kɛ Filistibi ɔmɛ nɛ a pɛ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e hia kɛ ho he kpa ya, nɛ Yehowa jɔɔ lɛ ngɛ lejɛ ɔ hulɔ. Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa Isak di tue mi jɔmi se. (1 Mose 26:19-25) Ke a yi mo dami sane ɔ, moo bɔ mɔde nɛ o to o tsui si, nɛ o di tue mi jɔmi se kaa bɔ nɛ Isak pee ɔ.
