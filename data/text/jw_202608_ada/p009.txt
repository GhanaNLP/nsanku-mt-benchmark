Saul hɛ kɔ̃ David nɔ nɛ e kɛ e hɛ fɔ we Yehowa nɔ hu, se Yonatan lɛɛ e ná hemi kɛ yemi ngɛ Yehowa mi nɛ e ye bua David
<<<P>>>
(Hyɛ kuku 4, 6)
<<<P>>>
Nihi fuu ngɛ Satan je ɔ mi ɔ je we suɔmi kpo kɛ tsɔɔ a sibi, nɛ behi fuu ɔ, a he we a sibi yi. Satan suɔ nɛ a ní peepee ɔmɛ nɛ ná wa nɔ he wami. E suɔ nɛ waa tu wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ kɛ Yehowa asafo ɔ he munyu nyanyaanya, nɛ wa ná wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ a he ninyɛ po.—Rom. 16:17.
<<<P>>>
⁴ Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, mo susu nɔ́ nɛ ha nɛ Matsɛ Saul ná David he ninyɛ ɔ he nɛ o hyɛ. Sisije ɔ, Saul suɔ David sane wawɛɛ, nɛ e bua jɔ e he, ejakaa David ji tabulɔ ko nɛ e ngɛ kã. (1 Sam. 18:5) Se pee se benɛ Saul na kaa nihi fuu a bua jɔ David he wawɛɛ pe lɛ ɔ, Saul hɛ kɔ̃ David nɔ nɛ e ná e he ninyɛ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, Saul bɔ mɔde wawɛɛ kaa e maa gbe David. (1 Sam. 18:9-11) Kaa Saul gbe David ɔ, jinɛ Satan bua ko jɔ wawɛɛ. Mɛni he je? Ejakaa Yehowa de gbalɔ Samuel kaa e ya pɔ David nu konɛ e ba ye matsɛ ngɛ Israel. (1 Sam. 16:13) Kaa Yehowa yi mi tomi nɛ ɔ bɛ mi ɔ, jinɛ Satan bua ko jɔ wawɛɛ. Benɛ Saul mi mi fu David, nɛ ji nɔ nɛ Yehowa pɔ lɛ nu konɛ e ba ye matsɛ, nɛ e ngɛ e se blɔ hlae konɛ e gbe lɛ ɔ, e ngɛ heii kaa Satan bua jɔ nɔ́ nɛ Saul ngɛ pee ɔ he.
<<<P>>>
⁵ Satan le kaa akɛnɛ suɔmi kɛ kake peemi ngɛ wa kpɛti he je ɔ, Yehowa yaa nɔ nɛ e jɔɔ wɔ. (La 133:1) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e suɔ nɛ wa hɛ nɛ kɔ̃ wa sibi a nɔ kaa bɔ nɛ Saul hɛ kɔ̃ David nɔ ɔ, konɛ e kɛ pue suɔmi kɛ kake peemi nɛ ngɛ wa kpɛti ɔ. Satan le hu kaa ke wa hɛ kɔ̃ wa sibi a nɔ ɔ, e he maa wa ha wɔ kaa wa maa suɔ mɛ. (Mar. 12:30, 31) Ke e ba kaa wa sume wa sibi ɔ, Yehowa bua be jami nɛ waa kɛ haa lɛ ɔ he jɔe hu. Yakobo kɛ hɛdɔ tu munyu kɛ kɔ oslaa nɛ ngɛ hɛkɔ mumi nɛ wa maa ngmɛ blɔ nɛ e pue ngɛ wa mi ɔ he.—Kane Yakobo 3:14-16.
