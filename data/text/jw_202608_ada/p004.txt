kaa Yehowa kɛ Yesu tsuo sɔ. Enɛ ɔ haa nɛ nihi fuu li kaa Mawu biɛ ji Yehowa, nɛ a jaa Yesu mohu. Se Yehowa kɛ Yesu tsuo kplɛɛ we jami nɛ ɔ nɔ. Yehowa suɔ nɛ a ja lɛ pɛ. (Mat. 4:10; Yoh. 4:23) Nihi fuu he tsɔɔmi nɛ ɔ ye wawɛɛ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, ke wa ngɛ mɛ anɔkuale ɔ tsɔɔe po ɔ, a bui wɔ tue, nɛ be komɛ po ɔ, a teɔ si kɛ woɔ wa fiɛɛmi ní tsumi ɔ.

9 Loko o kɛ nɔ ko ma nyɛ maa pee huɛ ɔ, kekleekle ɔ, e he hia nɛ o le nɔ ɔ biɛ. Baiblo ɔ tsɔɔ kaa, ke wa le Yehowa biɛ, nɛ wa ngɔ wa hɛ kɛ fɔ e nɔ ɔ, e maa po wa he piɛ. (Abɛ 18:10) Se Satan sume nɛ nihi nɛ a le Yehowa biɛ konɛ a kɛ lɛ nɛ pee huɛ. Jeha babauu ji nɛ ɔ nɛ Satan kɛ lakpa jamihi ngɛ ní tsue konɛ e kɛ ha nɛ nihi nɛ a ko le Yehowa biɛ ɔ. (Kane Yeremia 23:26, 27.) Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, e ha nɛ Baiblo sisi tsɔɔli fuu je Mawu biɛ ɔ kɛ je Baiblohi nɛ a tsɔɔ sisi ɔ mi. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, e ha nɛ lakpa jamihi tsɔɔ nihi kaa Mawu ji nɔ nɛ ha nɛ adesahi ngɛ nɔ nae. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, nihi fuu yeɔ Mawu gbeye, nɛ a susuɔ kaa Mawu kɛ mɛ be nyɛe maa pee huɛ. (Yak. 4:8) Satan bɔɔ mɔde hu kaa e ma ha nɛ nihi nɛ a ngɛ Yehowa sɔmɔe ɔ nɛ a kpa lɛ sɔmɔmi.
