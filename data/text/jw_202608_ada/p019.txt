Paulo ngma kɛ ya ha asafo nɛ Filemon ngɛ mi ngɛ Kolose, nɛ a bɔ he amaniɛ ngɛ Kolosebi 3:13 ɔ he nɛ o hyɛ. (Kane.) Kɛ Filemon maa nu he ha kɛɛ benɛ a ngɛ sɛ womi nɛ ɔ kanee ngɛ e we asafo ɔ mi ɔ? Mɛnɔ lɛ maa ba e juɛmi mi? Ke loloolo ɔ, e mi mi fu Onesimo ɔ, lɛɛ amlɔ nɛ ɔ, e ngɛ heii kaa e le nɔ́ nɛ e sa kaa e pee. E sa nɛ e ya nɔ nɛ e ná Onesimo he tsui, nɛ e ngɔ e tɔmi ɔ kɛ ke lɛ faa. Ke mo hu e he waa kɛ haa mo kaa o maa ngɔ tɔmi kɛ ke faa a, lɛɛ kɛ Paulo ga womi nɛ ɔ maa ye bua mo ha kɛɛ?

17 Mɛni Paulo ngɛ tsɔɔe be nɛ e de ke “nyɛɛ ya nɔ nɛ nyɛ ná nyɛ sibi a he tsui” ɔ? Paulo ngɛ wɔ he wami woe nɛ waa to wa tsui si kɛ ha wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ, nɛ waa kɛ mɛ nɛ hi si ngɛ mi mi jɔmi mi kɛ a tɔ̃ wa nɔ aloo a pee nɔ́ ko nɛ gba wa nya po. Ngɛ jamɛ a ngmami ɔ nɔuu mi ɔ, Paulo tu nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ nɛ Yehowa hu pee ɔ he munyu. Yehowa be kpɛii nɛ e hyɛ we blɔ kaa e sɔmɔli maa pee a ní kaa nihi nɛ a ye mluku. E le wa gbɔjɔmihi, se e ngɔɔ e juɛmi kɛ maa su kpakpahi nɛ wa ngɛ ɔ nɔ. (La 103:13, 14; 130:3) Enɛ ɔ he je ɔ, Yehowa jeɔ e suɔmi ɔ kpo kɛ tsɔɔ wɔ faa, nɛ e naa wɔ mɔbɔ. (Kɔmɔ 3:22, 23) Ke wa bɔɔ mɔde nɛ wa naa wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ kaa bɔ nɛ Yehowa naa mɛ ɔ, e he be wae ha wɔ kulaa kaa wa maa to wa tsui si kɛ ha mɛ ke a tɔ̃ wa nɔ. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, ke wa kai bɔ nɛ mɔmɔ ko peeɔ fɛu saminya ha ke e wa a, lɔ ɔ haa nɛ wa hyɛɔ mɔmɔ ɔ nɔ be mi nɛ e wɛ lolo po. Jã nɔuu kɛ̃ nɛ ke wɔ hu wa ngɔ wa juɛmi kɛ ma su kpakpahi nɛ wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ ngɛ ɔ nɔ ɔ, lɔ ɔ maa wo wɔ he wami nɛ waa ngɔ a tɔmihi kɛ ke mɛ, nɛ́ wa je mi mi jɔmi kpo kɛ tsɔɔ mɛ ke a ha nɛ wa nine nyɛ si po.

18 Yehowa bua jɔɔ nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ nɛ o peeɔ konɛ tue mi jɔmi nɛ hi asafo ɔ mi ɔ he. Be fɛɛ be ɔ, moo hyɛ kaa mɛni o ma nyɛ maa pee kɛ ha nɛ tue mi jɔmi nɛ hi o kɛ nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ nyɛ kpɛti. Moo ya nɔ nɛ o kɛ nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ nɛ tu munyu ngɛ mi mi jɔmi mi, nɛ o tu a he munyu kpakpa. Ke o tɔ̃ nɔ ko nɔ ɔ, moo kpa pɛɛ, nɛ́ o pee nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ nɛ o ma nyɛ konɛ o kɛ dla o kɛ lɛ nyɛ kpɛti. Mo ná suɔmi nɛ mi wa kɛ ha nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ e he be wae ha mo kaa o kɛ a tɔmihi maa ke mɛ faa. Ke o peeɔ ní nɛ ɔmɛ ɔ, lɔ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa o ngɛ ye buae nɛ tue mi jɔmi nɛ hi asafo ɔ mi. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ o maa piɛɛ nihi nɛ a he jɔ, nɛ “a nine maa su zugba a nɔ” kɛ ya neneene, nɛ ‘a bua maa jɔ wawɛɛ, nɛ a ma ná tue mi jɔmi nitsɛnitsɛ’ ɔ a he!—La 37:11.
