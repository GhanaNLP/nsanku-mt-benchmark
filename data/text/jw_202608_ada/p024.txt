¹⁴ Yesu fiɛɛ sane kpakpa a wawɛɛ nitsɛ.

karia de ke: “E ma ha nihi nɛ a hii si ngɛ di-
blii mi kɛ gbenɔ hɔ̃ mi ɔ la.” Yesu mi mi sãã
lɛ wawɛɛ, nɛ e suɔ nɛ e maa ye bua nihi nɛ ni
kpahi waa mɛ yi mi ɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, kaa bɔ nɛ
Zakaria gba a, Yesu ha nimli nɛ ɔmɛ la kɛ gu
‘Yehowa mɔbɔ nami jeha a nɛ e jaje’ kɛ tsɔɔ
mɛ ɔ nɔ. (Kane Yesaya 61:1, 2; Luka 4:16-
21) E kɛ kã jaje ke: “Hiɔwe Matsɛ Yemi ɔ su si
ta.” Se kɛ̃ ɔ, Yesu le kaa a maa hia nihi fuu nɛ
a maa fiɛɛ sane kpakpa a. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e tsɔse
e kaseli ɔmɛ konɛ a nyɛ nɛ a fiɛɛ sane kpakpa
nɛ ɔ.—Mat. 4:16, 17; 10:7; 28:19, 20.
