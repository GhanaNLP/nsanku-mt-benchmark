Anɛ o maa ya nɔ ma fĩ Yehowa kɛ e Matsɛ Yemi ɔ se lo?
(Hyɛ kuku 13)
<<<P>>>
11 Benɛ Yesu nɛ ji Mesia a ba zugba a nɔ ɔ, nihi kɛ lɛ hí si saminya. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ ɔ, benɛ e je e fiɛɛmi ní tsumi ɔ sisi ɔ, e wekuli ɔmɛ a kpɛti ni komɛ de ke e yi ngɛ puɛe. (Mar. 3:21) Be fɛɛ be ɔ, jami hɛ mi nyɛɛli ɔmɛ bɔɔ mɔde kaa a maa tsi e nya konɛ e ko tsɔɔ nihi Mawu he ní. (Luka 11:15, 16) E kaseli ɔmɛ a kpɛti nɔ kake tsɔɔ e se blɔ. (Mat. 26:14-16) Osɔfo ɔmɛ kɛ ma nikɔtɔma amɛ pee kake konɛ a kɛ ha nɛ a gbe Yesu kaa bɔ nɛ a gbeɔ julɔ ɔ. (Mar. 15:1, 13-15) E ngɛ mi kaa nihi fuu nyɛ Yesu mohu lɛɛ, se e ya nɔ nɛ e tsu ní tsumi nɛ Yehowa kɛ wo e dɛ ɔ. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, e kɛ nihi nɛ a wa lɛ yi mi ɔ hi si saminya. Benɛ a ma Yesu numi konɛ a gbe lɛ ɔ, Petro bɔ mɔde kaa e ma fã Yesu he. Petro po osɔfo nɔkɔtɔma a tsɔlɔ ɔ tue. Se Yesu bua jɔɛ nɔ nɛ Petro pee ɔ he, enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e gu nyakpɛ blɔ nɔ kɛ tsa nyumu ɔ nɛ a po e tue ɔ. (Luka 22:50, 51) Ngɛ si fɔfɔɛ nɛ ɔmɛ tsuo a mi ɔ, Yesu pee we nɔ́ ko kɛ tsɔɔ kaa e suɔ nɛ e ye e he lue. Mohu ɔ, e ngɔ e juɛmi kɛ ma ní tsumi nɛ he hia nɛ Yehowa kɛ wo e dɛ ɔ nɔ.
<<<P>>>
12 Kaa bɔ nɛ e ba ngɛ Yesu blɔ fa mi ɔ, wɔ hu waa kɛ si fɔfɔɛhi nɛ a mi wa nɛ a ma nyɛ ma jɔ̃ wa kɔni mi ɔ maa kpe. Satan bɔɔ mɔde kaa e ma ha nɛ wa kpa sane kpakpa a fiɛɛmi. Wa wekuli aloo ni kpahi ma nyɛ maa te si kɛ wo wɔ aloo a maa wa wɔ yi mi po. Se Yesu fã wɔ kaa waa suɔ nihi nɛ a waa wɔ yi mi ɔ. (Kane Mateo 5:43, 44.) Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa e sɛ nɛ waa nyɛ nihi nɛ a waa wɔ yi mi aloo a teɔ si kɛ woɔ wɔ ɔ. Mohu ɔ, waa kɛ Yesu fami nɛ ɔ tsuɔ ní, nɛ wa bɔɔ mɔde kaa wa ma je mi mi jɔmi kɛ bumi kpo kɛ tsɔɔ nihi nɛ a waa wɔ yi mi ɔ, nɛ́ waa to wa tsui si kɛ ha mɛ.—1 Kor. 13:4.
<<<P>>>
13 Ke ma nɔ yeli ɔmɛ wo mlaa ko nɛ ma ha nɛ si himi ɔ mi nɛ wa ha wɔ, aloo e ma ha nɛ e he nɛ wa ha wɔ kaa wa ma ja Yehowa po ɔ, e sa nɛ wa je bumi kpo kɛ tsɔɔ mɛ. Mɛni he je? Ejakaa
<<<P>>>
11. Kɛ Yesu pee e ní ha kɛɛ ngɛ nyagbahi tsuo nɛ e kɛ kpe ɔ he?
<<<P>>>
12. Ngɛ Mateo 5:43, 44 ɔ nya a, kɛ e sa nɛ waa kɛ nihi nɛ a waa wɔ yi mi ɔ nɛ hi si ha kɛɛ?
<<<P>>>
