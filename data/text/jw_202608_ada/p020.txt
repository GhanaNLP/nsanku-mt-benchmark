<<<P>>>
OTI NƐ NGƐ NÍ KASEMI Ɔ MI
<<<P>>>
Ní kasemi nɛ ɔ maa ye bua wɔ konɛ wa bua nɛ jɔ wa yi wami nɛ Yehowa ma he, he blɔ nɛ wa ná kaa wa ma sɔmɔ Yehowa kɛ kã, kɛ fiɛɛmi ní tsumi ɔ nɛ wa ma tsu ɔ he.
<<<P>>>
O Bua Nɛ Jɔ He Blɔ Nɛ O Ná Kaa O Ma Sɔmɔ Yehowa a He
<<<P>>>
‘E tle yi wami hemi koli ko si, konɛ e kɛ ha wɔ he blɔ nɛ waa kɛ kã nɛ sɔmɔ lɛ ngɛ blɔ klɔuu nɔ.’—LUKA 1:69, 74.
<<<P>>>
BENƐ Zakaria kɛ Elizabet bwɔ nɛ a be fɔmi he ɔ, Yehowa gu nyakpɛ blɔ nɔ kɛ ha nɛ a fɔ Yohane. Nihi tsuo nɛ a nu he ɔ bi ke: “Mɛni nɔmlɔ po jokuɛ nɛ ɔ ma ba pee?” (Luka 1:57-66) Yehowa gu Zakaria nɔ kɛ ha sane nɛ ɔ heto. Zakaria tsɔɔ nya kaa Yohane maa ye bua nihi konɛ a tsake a tsui, nɛ a ba nyɛɛ Mesia a nɛ e be kɛe nɛ e maa ba a se. (Amos 3:7; Luka 1:67-79) Se pi nó nɛ Yohane ma ba pee ɔ pɛ nɛ Zakaria tu he munyu.* Kaa bɔ nɛ wa maa na ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, Zakaria munyu ɔ ji gbami, nɛ e kɔɔ wɔ hu wa he mwɔnɛ ɔ. Ngɛ nó nɛ Zakaria de ɔ nya a, wa maa kase níhi etɛ nɛ e sa nɛ wa bua nɛ jɔ he: (1) Wa yi wami nɛ a ma he, (2) he blɔ nɛ wa ná kaa wa ma sɔmɔ Yehowa, kɛ (3) he blɔ nɛ wa ná kaa wa maa tsu fiɛɛmi ní tsumi ɔ.
