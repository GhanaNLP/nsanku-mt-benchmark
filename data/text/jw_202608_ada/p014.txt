LA 106
Nyɛ Ha Wa Ná Suɔmi
<<<P>>>
OTI NƐ NGƐ NÍ KASEMI Ɔ MI
<<<P>>>
Wa maa na bɔ nɛ bɔfo Paulo, Filemon, kɛ Onesi-mo di tue mi jɔmi se ha ngɛ asafo ɔ mi.
<<<P>>>
Sɛ Womi Ko Nɛ Woɔ Wɔ He Wami Kaa Waa Di Tue Mi Jɔmi Se
<<<P>>>
“Ma je suɔmi mi kɛ kpa mo pɛɛ.”—FLM. 9.
<<<P>>>
WA BUA JƆ tue mi jɔmi nɛ ngɛ Yehowa we bi a kpɛti ngɛ je kɛ wɛ ɔ he wawɛɛ. Tue mi jɔmi nɛ ɔ nɛ ngɛ wa kpɛti ɔ haa nɛ wa naa bɔ nɛ si himi ɔ maa hi ha ngɛ Paradeiso ɔ mi. (Yes. 32:17, 18) E ngɛ mi kaa Yehowa lɛ ha nɛ wa ngɛ tue mi jɔmi nɛ ɔ mohu lɛɛ, se e sa nɛ wɔ tsuo waa bɔ mɔde konɛ waa baa tue mi jɔmi nɛ ɔ yi. (Rom. 12:18) Kɛ e sa nɛ waa kɛ wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ nɛ hi si ha kɛɛ konɛ wa kɛ baa tue mi jɔmi nɛ ngɛ wa kpɛti ɔ yi? Mɛni wa ma nyɛ maa pee kɛ di tue mi jɔmi se ke wa tɔ̃ nyɛmi ko nɔ, aloo ke nyɛmi ko pee wɔ nɔ́ ko nɛ dɔ wɔ wawɛɛ? Wa maa na heto ɔmɛ ngɛ sɛ womi ko nɛ woɔ wɔ he wami kaa waa di tue mi jɔmi se ɔ mi. Sɛ womi nɛ ɔ kɔɔ nihi etɛ komɛ a he. Mɛ ji: Bɔfo Paulo, Filemon, kɛ Onesimo.
<<<P>>>
SƐ WOMI NƐ PAULO NGMA KƐ YA HA FILEMON
<<<P>>>
2 Kane Filemon 8, 9, 17. Ngɛ sɛ womi nɛ Paulo ngma kɛ ya ha Filemon ɔ mi ɔ, e wo lɛ he wami kaa ke e nyɔguɛ nɛ ji Onesimo kpale kɛ ba a, e he lɛ atuu nɛ e je mi mi jɔmi kpo kɛ tsɔɔ lɛ. Pau-lo wo Filemon he wami kaa e pee jã ngɛ suɔmi nɛ e ngɛ kɛ ha Yesu Kristo kɛ e nyɛmimɛ Kristofo ɔmɛ ɔ he je. Filemon hi Ko-lose asafo ɔ mi, nɛ nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ nɛ a ngɛ lejɛ ɔ le lɛ kaa e ji nɔ ko nɛ e ngɛ hemi kɛ yemi nɛ mi wa, nɛ e suɔ nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ wa-wɛɛ hulɔ. Filemon ngɛ nyɔguɛ ko nɛ tsa pi Kristofono ji lɛ, nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Onesimo. Se Onesimo tu fo kɛ je e nyɔmtsɛ ɔ he kɛ ho Roma ya. E ma nyɛ maa ba lɛ kaa Onesimo ju Filemon ní komɛ kɛ to fu. (Flm. 18; hyɛ study note ɔ.) Benɛ Onesimo ngɛ Roma a, e kɛ Paulo pee huɛ, nɛ Paulo ye bua lɛ nɛ e ba pee Kris-tofono. (Kol. 4:9) Ngɛ Romabi ɔmɛ a mlaa nya a, ke nyɔguɛ ji nɔ ko nɛ e tu fo ɔ, e sa nɛ e kpale kɛ ho e nyɔmtsɛ ɔ ngɔ ya. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, Paulo wo lɛ blɔ kɛ ho Filemon ngɔ ya.—Flm. 12, 13;
