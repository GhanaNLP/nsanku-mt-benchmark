<<<P>>>
STÉPHANE LANDEROIN NYA MI MUNYU
<<<P>>>
BENƐ i ji jokuɛ ɔ, ye bua jɔɛ sukuu yami he kulaa. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, be fɛɛ be nɛ wa maa ngma ka a, i bɔɛ mɔde. I sume nɛ nihi nɛ a yɔse mi. Lɔ ɔ he ɔ, i suɔ nɛ ma pee níhi ngɛ ye dɛ he. Se kɛ̃ ɔ, Bɔlɔ ɔ to ye he hɛ. Ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ, i hyɛ we blɔ kaa Mawu maa to ye he hɛ. Mɛni he je?
<<<P>>>
He nɛ i wa ngɛ ngɛ France ɔ, ye fɔli ɔmɛ kɛ nihi fuu nɛ a ngɛ kpɔ ɔ mi ɔ he we yi kaa Mawu ngɛ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, imi hu i he we Mawu yi. Mohu ɔ, i he ye kaa níhi bile. A tsɔɔ tsɔɔmi nɛ ɔ ngɛ sukuu, nɛ kaa bɔ nɛ e ji ngɛ nihi fuu a blɔ fa mi ɔ, imi hu i susu kaa e ji anɔkuale. I naa tsɔɔmi nɛ ɔ kaa juɛmi ngɛ mi, enɛ ɔ he ɔ, i hla we níhi a mi fuu kɛ kɔ he.
<<<P>>>
Ye fɔli ɔmɛ a bua jɔɛ jami he kulaa. A deɔ ke, ke Mawu ko ngɛ ɔ, lɛɛ mɛni he je mɔ nɛ nɔ nami kɛ dami sane nɛ a yi ɔ pɔ he kikɛ ɔ. Jehanɛ se hu ɔ, benɛ i na ní yayahi nɛ nihi nɛ a deɔ ke a he Mawu ye ɔ ngɛ pee ɔ, e ha nɛ imi hu i susu kaa juɛmi be mi kaa nɔ ko maa he ye kaa Mawu ko ngɛ. I yeɔ nihi nɛ a he ye kaa Mawu ngɛ ɔ po a he fɛu.
<<<P>>>
Se amlɔ nɛ ɔ, i ba ná nɔ mi mami kaa Mawu ko ngɛ, nɛ lɛ nɛ e bɔ adesahi tsuo, nɛ e susuɔ ye he be mi nɛ i nuɔ he kaa se nami ko be ye he po. Mɛni he je nɛ ye susumi tsake nɛ i ngɛ bua jɔmi wawɛɛ amlɔ nɛ ɔ? Ha nɛ ma de mo.
<<<P>>>
Benɛ i ye jeha 19 ɔ, a tsɔse mi kaa tabulɔ konɛ ma ya piɛɛ tabuli ɔmɛ a he. Pee se ɔ, nɔ yelɔ ɔ ngɔ mi kɛ ho he ko tsitsaa ya ngɛ French Guiana ngɛ South America konɛ ma ya ye jeha kake ngɛ lejɛ ɔ. Lejɛ ɔ ngɛ fɛu wawɛɛ nitsɛ nɛ e tsɔ e he banee, se i li kaa ye si himi ma ba tsake ngɛ lejɛ ɔ pee se.
<<<P>>>
Benɛ i ye jeha 20 ɔ, i kpale kɛ ho France ya ngɛ Paris. Ngɛ lejɛ ɔ, i tsuɔ ní wawɛɛ kɛ jeɔ Hɔɛgbi kɛ ya siɔ Soha, kɛkɛ ɔ, Hɔ kɛ Hɔgba lɛɛ, i kɛ ye huɛ-mɛ ya jeɔ wa hɛja nɛ wa nuɔ dã fuu. Nɔ́ nɛ ɔ yaa nɔ daa otsi. Se ngɛ enɛ ɔ tsuo se ɔ, i be bua jɔmi nitsɛ-nitsɛ, nɛ i kɛ oti kpakpa ko mɛ ye hɛ mi hulɔ.
