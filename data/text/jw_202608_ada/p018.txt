13 Filemon suɔ nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ wawɛɛ. (Flm. 5) Filemon tsu e biɛ ɔ nya ní. Filemon biɛ ɔ sisi ji “Suɔmi.” A le Filemon kaa e jeɔ suɔmi kpo wawɛɛ, nɛ su nɛ ɔ ji nɔ́ nɛ maa ye bua lɛ konɛ e ngɔ Onesimo tɔmi ɔ kɛ ke lɛ. Benɛ Paulo ngma sɛ womi kɛ ya ha asafo nɛ Filemon ngɛ mi ngɛ Kolose ɔ, e kale suɔmi kaa e “ji kpa nɛ fiɔ wɔ wawɛɛ kɛ peeɔ kake.” (Kane Kolosebi 3:14.) Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa suɔmi pɛ ji su ko nɛ ma nyɛ ma ha nɛ tue mi jɔmi kɛ kake peemi nɛ hi wa kpɛti. Ke wa ya nɔ nɛ wa je suɔmi kpo kɛ tsɔɔ wa nyɛmimɛ ɔ, e he be wae kulaa kaa waa kɛ a tɔmihi maa ke mɛ, nɛ́ waa kɛ mɛ nɛ hi si ngɛ mi mi jɔmi mi. (1 Kor. 13:4-7) Nyɛ ha nɛ waa hyɛ bɔ nɛ suɔmi nɛ ɔ ye bua Filemon ha nɛ e ngɔ tɔmi kɛ ke.
<<<P>>>
14 Su kpa ko nɛ ha nɛ suɔmi nɛ Filemon ngɛ kɛ ha nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ mi wa wawɛɛ ɔ ji, nubwɔ peemi. E ha nɛ asafo ɔ kpeɔ ngɛ e we ɔ mi. (Flm. 2) Jehanɛ hu ɔ, Paulo de Filemon kaa e dla he ko ngɛ e we ɔ mi kɛ to lɛ. Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa Paulo le kaa Filemon peeɔ nɔ nubwɔ wawɛɛ. (Flm. 22) E ngɛ heii kaa nubwɔ peemi su nɛ Filemon jeɔ kpo ɔ haa nɛ nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ a bua jɔɔ, nɛ e he wɛ kulaa kaa a maa hɛ kɛ su e he.—Flm. 7.

15 Mɛni he je nɛ ke wa peeɔ wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ nibwɔ kaa bɔ nɛ Filemon pee ɔ, e he be wae ha wɔ kaa wa ma je suɔmi kpo kɛ tsɔɔ mɛ ɔ? Ebakaa jã peemi haa nɛ wa ba leɔ mɛ saminya. Ke wa bɔ mɔde nɛ wa ná be kɛ ha wa nyɛmimɛ né waa kɛ mɛ pɔɛ bɔmi aloo nihi nɛ e slo níhi nɛ a bua jɔ he ngɛ wa nɔ ɔ he ɔ he ɔ, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ wa maa na kaa Yehowa sɔmɔli a kpɛti nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ngɛ su ko nɛ sa kadimi wawɛɛ, nɛ lɔ ɔ lɛ haa nɛ asafo ɔ mi ngɔɔ wawɛɛ ɔ nɛ. Ke wa bɔ mɔde kaa wa maa le he nɛ a je, bɔ nɛ a plɛ kɛ ba na anɔkuale ɔ ha, kɛ blɔ nɔ nɛ a gu kɛ ngɛ kahi a nya dae ɔ, lɔ ɔ maa ye bua wɔ konɛ suɔmi nɛ wa ngɛ kɛ ha mɛ ɔ mi nɛ wa, nɛ wa je bumi nɛ mi kuɔ kpo kɛ tsɔɔ mɛ. Enɛ ɔ ma ha nɛ wa maa na nɔ́ he je nɛ Yehowa gbla mɛ kɛ ba piɛɛ e weku ɔ he ɔ. (Yoh. 6:44) Ke wa peeɔ wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ nibwɔ ɔ, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ wa maa suɔ mɛ wawɛɛ, nɛ wa ma ná nɔ mi mami kaa a hɛɛ juɛmi nɛ da, nɛ waa kɛ a tɔmihi maa ke mɛ faa.
