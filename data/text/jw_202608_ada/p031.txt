ANƐ O LE LO?
<<<P>>>
Mɛni níhi lɛ yaa nɔ ngɛ ma agbo ɔmɛ a nya ngɛ blema be ɔ mi?
<<<P>>>
NGƐ blema a, ma agbo ɔmɛ a nya peeɔ hoo, ejakaa daa ligbi ɔ, nihi fuu be ɔ ngɛ lejɛ ɔ, nɛ ní slɔɔtoslɔɔtohi hu yaa nɔ ngɛ lejɛ ɔ. Baiblo ɔ wo ma agbohi a ta si abɔ, nɛ e tsɔɔ níhi nɛ yaa nɔ ngɛ lejɛ ɔ hulɔ.
<<<P>>>
Nihi buaa a he nya ngɛ ma agbo ɔmɛ a nya. Hebri munyungu nɛ a tsɔɔ sisi ke “agbo” ɔ kɔ we sinya nɛ hiɔ ma ko nya a pɛ he. Mohu ɔ, e kɔɔ hehi nɛ ngɛ agbo ɔ nya nɛ nihi maa a bo tsuhi loo a gbahi ngɛ ɔ he hulɔ. Ngɛ hehi komɛ ɔ, he nya buami he nguahi ngɛ ma agbo ɔ nya nɛ nihi babauu kpeɔ ngɛ lejɛ ɔ.—Neh. 8:1.
<<<P>>>
Ní juali ba juaa níhi ngɛ ma agbo ɔmɛ a kasa nya. Ngɛ Yerusalem ɔ, a kɛ biɛ komɛ wo agbo komɛ ngɛ níhi nɛ eko ɔ, a juaa ngɛ jamɛ a agbo ɔmɛ a kasa nya a he je. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, a tsɛɛ agbo ɔ eko ke Lo Agbo, nɛ a tsɛɛ eko hu ke Jījɔ Agbo.—Zef. 1:10; Yoh. 5:2.
<<<P>>>
Ni komɛ ba yeɔ jua nɛ nihi hu ba tsuɔ a hiami níhi a he ní ngɛ lejɛ ɔ. Abraham he zugba ko ngɛ nɔ ko dɛ ngɛ ma agbo ɔ nya, ngɛ odase yeli a hɛ mi konɛ e kɛ pu e yo ɔ. (1 Mose 23:10-15, 17, 18) Jehanɛ hu ɔ, ma agbo ɔ nya lɛ Boaz kpɔ̃ Rut ngɛ ngɛ odase yeli a hɛ mi kaa bɔ nɛ Israelbi ɔmɛ a mlaa a tsɔɔ ɔ, benɛ nɔ nɛ ngɛ he blɔ kaa e kpɔ̃ɔ ɔ kua kaa e maa pee jã a. (Rut 4:1, 10) Blɔ hiali komɛ hu ba hwɔɔ he nya buami hehi nɛ ngɛ ma agbo ɔmɛ a nya a, ke ji kaa nɔ ko ha we mɛ he ko nɛ a to. (Many. 19:15-21) Nihi hu yaa ma agbo ɔmɛ a nya konɛ a ya nu amaniɛ bɔmi ehehi nɛ ba a.—2 Sam. 18:24, 25.
<<<P>>>
Kojoli kojoɔ ngɛ ma agbo ɔmɛ a kasa nya. A kojoɔ sanehi ngɛ nihi nɛ a bua a he nya ngɛ lejɛ ɔ a hɛ mi konɛ mɛ hu a nu sane ɔ nɛ ba a, kɛ bɔ nɛ a maa ma sane ɔ nya si ha. (5 Mose 21:18-20) Akɛnɛ a kojoɔ sanehi ngɛ nihi a hɛ mi he je ɔ, e haa nɛ kojoli ɔmɛ yeɔ dami sane nɛ a mwɔɔ sanehi a nya ngɛ blɔ nɛ da nɔ. Ke kojoli ɔmɛ ma sane ko nya si ɔ, nikɔtɔma amɛ tsuɔ he ní oya nɔuu. (5 Mose 25:2) A hyɛɔ blɔ kaa ke kojoli ɔmɛ ngɛ kojoe ngɛ ma a agbo ɔ kasa nya a, a ye dami sane nɛ́ a ba a je mi saminya, nɛ́ a wo ma a mi bi ɔmɛ he wami nɛ mɛ hu a pee jã nɔuu.—5 Mose 16:18-20; Hiob 29:7, 11-17.
<<<P>>>
Nyumuhi anɔkualetsɛmɛ woɔ Israelbi nɛ a ngɛ ma agbo ɔmɛ a nya a he wami konɛ a ngɔ a hɛ kɛ fɔ Yehowa nɔ. Gbali kɛ ni kpa komɛ tuɔ Mawu munyu ɔ kɛ tsɔɔ nihi ngɛ ma agbo ɔmɛ a nya ejakaa a le kaa nihi buaa a he nya ngɛ lejɛ ɔ. (1 Ma. 22:10; Yer. 17:19, 20) Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, Yehu de ni ɔmɛ ke, Yehowa ye si nɛ e wo kaa e maa gbla Ahab we ɔ tue ɔ nɔ. (2 Ma. 10:8-10) Jehanɛ hu ɔ, matsɛ Hezekia bua e tabuli a nya dali ɔmɛ a nya ngɛ ma agbo ɔ nya nɛ e wo mɛ he wami konɛ a ngɔ a hɛ kɛ fɔ Yehowa nɔ.—2 Kron. 32:6-8.

Baiblo ɔ de ke “juɛmi nitsɛnitsɛ” wo e gbi nɔ nɛ e ngɛ ngmlaa kpae boboobo “ngɛ ma agbo ɔmɛ a nya” (Abɛ 1:20, 21) Ngɛ blema Israel ɔ, ma agbo ɔmɛ a nya lɛ nyumuhi anɔkualetsɛmɛ woɔ nihi ga kpakpa ngɛ, nɛ a mwɔɔ sanehi a nya ngɛ blɔ nɛ da nɔ. Mwɔnɛ ɔ hu ɔ, wa naa nile nɛ Yehowa ngɛ ɔ ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi, nɛ Yehowa sɔmɔli nɛ a ngɛ he fɛɛ he ɔ ngɛ nihi ye buae konɛ a kase Baiblo ɔ nɛ a ba ná nile nitsɛnitsɛ nɛ ɔ.
