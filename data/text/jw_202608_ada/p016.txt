Mo susu ní kpakpahi ngɛ nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ a he, nɛ o tu a he munyu kpakpa kaa bɔ nɛ Paulo pee ɔ
(Hyɛ kuku 8)
<<<P>>>
Paulo de kɛ kɔ Onesimo he ɔ ma ha nɛ Filemon kɛ Onesimo nɛ hi si saminya, né e je mi mi jɔmi mi nɛ e kɛ lɛ nɛ tu munyu. Jã nɔuu kɛ̃ nɛ níhi nɛ wa deɔ ngɛ wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ a he ɔ saa bɔ nɛ ni kpahi naa mɛ ha kɛ bɔ nɛ a kɛ mɛ hiɔ si ha a he. Ke wa tuɔ nyɛmi ko he munyu kpakpa a, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ nyɛmimɛ kpahi kɛ lɛ maa hi si ngɛ mi mi jɔmi mi. Jã nɔuu kɛ̃ nɛ ke wa tuɔ nyɛmi ko he munyu yaya a, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ nyɛmimɛ kpahi kɛ lɛ be si hie saminya. Se loko wa ma nyɛ maa tu wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ a he munyu kpakpa a, ja waa kɛ wa juɛmi maa su kpakpahi nɛ a ngɛ ɔ nɔ. Mɛni maa ye bua wɔ konɛ waa pee jã a? Nyɛ ha nɛ waa hyɛ nɔ́ ko nɛ Paulo pɔɔ peemi ɔ.
<<<P>>>
8 Be fɛɛ be ɔ, Paulo naa Yehowa si ngɛ su kpakpahi nɛ Filemon ngɛ ɔ he. (Flm. 4, 5) E pɔɔ jã peemi kɛ haa ni kpahi hulɔ. (1 Kor. 1:4-7; Flp. 1:3-5; Kol. 1:3, 4; 1 Tes. 1:2, 3) Paulo le nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ a gbɔjɔmihi, se e bɔ mɔde kaa e kɛ e juɛmi maa ma su kpakpahi nɛ a ngɛ ɔ nɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, daa nɛ ɔ, e naa Yehowa si ngɛ e huɛmɛ nɛ ɔmɛ a he je. Ke e he waa kɛ haa mo kaa o maa tu nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ a he munyu kpakpa a, lɛɛ mo ha nɛ munyu nɛ nyɛɛ se nɛ ɔ nɛ hi o juɛmi mi: Ngɔɔ o juɛmi kɛ ma su kpakpahi nɛ nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ ngɛ ɔ nɔ, nɛ o sɔle kɛ ha mɛ be fɛɛ be kaa Yehowa nɛ jɔɔ mɛ. Ke o peeɔ jã a, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ e he be wae kulaa kaa o maa tu nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ a he munyu kpakpa.
<<<P>>>
9 Paulo je mi mi jɔmi mi nɛ e ngma sɛ womi kɛ ya ha Filemon be mi nɛ e kɛ nyagbahi ngɛ kpee po. Anɛ o kai be ko nɛ pɔ tɔ o he aloo o hao nɛ lɔ ɔ ha nɛ o kɛ abofu kple nɔ ko nɔ ɔ lo? Ke waa kɛ si fɔfɔɛhi nɛ a mi wa ngɛ kpee ɔ, e he wɛ kulaa kaa waa kɛ nɔ ko maa tu munyu kɛ abofu. Se mo susu si fɔfɔɛ nɛ Paulo ngɛ mi be mi nɛ e ngma sɛ womi kɛ ya ha Filemon ɔ he nɛ o hyɛ. Ngɛ jeha bɔɔ komɛ nɛ sɛ hlami loko e maa ngma sɛ womi ɔ, ni komɛ ya tua lɛ nɛ a kongo lɛ, nɛ lɔ ɔ se ɔ, a nu lɛ. Pee se ɔ, a wo lɛ tsu ngɛ Yerusalem kɛ Kaisarea. Lɔ ɔ se ɔ, a ngɔ lɛ kɛ wo mele mi kɛ yaa Roma, se mele ɔ ya butu ngɛ blɔ ɔ nɔ, nɛ benɛ e ngɛ sɛ womi ɔ ngmae kɛ ya ha Filemon ɔ, a wo lɛ tsu loloolo ngɛ lɛ nitsɛ e we mi ngɛ Roma. (Flm. 1) Se anɛ Paulo ha nɛ si fɔfɔɛ nɛ ɔmɛ nɛ e kɛ kpe ɔ ná e nɔ he wami, nɛ e kɛ Filemon tu munyu basabasa lo? Dɛbi. Paulo je mi mi jɔmi mi nɛ e ngma sɛ womi kɛ ha Filemon. Mɛni wa ma nyɛ maa kase ngɛ sane nɛ ɔ mi? Ke pɔ tɔ wa he aloo wa hao aloo waa kɛ nyagba kpa ko ngɛ kpee po ɔ, e sa nɛ waa bɔ mɔde nɛ waa kɛ wa nyɛmimɛ nɛ hi si ngɛ mi mi jɔmi mi.
