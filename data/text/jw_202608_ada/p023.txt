9 E he waa kɛ haa wa nyɛmimɛ Kristofohi komɛ kaa a ma kplɛɛ nɔ kaa Yehowa bua jɔɔ sɔmɔmi nɛ a sɔmɔɔ lɛ ɔ he. Eko ɔ, akɛnɛ nɔ yaya ko ba a nɔ be ko nɛ be ɔ he je ɔ, a nuɔ he kaa se nami ko be a he, nɛ Yehowa be nyɛe maa suɔ mɛ. Aloo eko ɔ, a pee yayami ko be ko nɛ be nɛ a he nile ngɛ mɛ kojoe, pohu a sɔle nɛ a de Yehowa kaa e ngɔ a yayami ɔ kɛ ke mɛ, nɛ a bi nikɔtɔma amɛ a yemi kɛ buami hulɔ. Ke jã nɛ e ji ngɛ o blɔ fa mi ɔ, lɛɛ mɛni o ma nyɛ maa pee?

10 Mo susu nɔ́ he tomi nɔ́ nɛ ɔ he nɛ o hyɛ. Ngɔɔ lɛ kaa nɔ ko nɛ e nane ku ɔ ya na dɔkita ko. Dɔkita a pee tsopa ha lɛ konɛ e kɛ tsa e nane ɔ. Lɔ ɔ se ɔ, dɔkita a de lɛ kaa e hi e kpɔ mi gbɔlee konɛ bɔɔbɔɔbɔɔ ɔ, e nane ɔ nɛ ná tsami, nɛ e nyɛ nɛ e nyɛɛ ekohu. Se ngɔɔ lɛ kaa nɔ́ nɛ e nane ɔ ku ɔ susu kaa bɔ nɛ e nane ɔ ku ha he je ɔ, e be nyɛe maa nyɛɛ hu gblegbleegble. Ke e ba lɛ jã a, mɛni e sa kaa e pee? E sa nɛ e ná hemi kɛ yemi ngɛ dɔkita a mi, konɛ e ngɔ blɔ tsɔɔmi nɛ dɔkita a kɛ ha lɛ ɔ kɛ tsu ní, nɛ bɔɔbɔɔbɔɔ ɔ, e ma nyɛ maa nyɛɛ ekohu loo e tu fo po.

11 Jã nɔuu kɛ̃ nɛ ke o ngɛ he nue kaa yayami ko nɛ o pee kɛ be ɔ he je ɔ, Yehowa be mo suɔe ɔ, lɛɛ mo ná hemi kɛ yemi ngɛ Yehowa mi. Yehowa suɔ mo wawɛɛ nitsɛ, nɛ e kɛ o yayami ɔ ke mo ejakaa e ha nɛ e Bi Yesu ba gbo ngɛ o he je. (Yoh. 1:16, 17; Rom. 3:23, 24; 1 Yoh. 3:19, 20) Moo pee níhi nɛ Yehowa de mo ke o pee ɔ konɛ o ná nɔ mi mami kaa e ngɔ o yayami ɔ kɛ ke mo. Lɔ ɔmɛ ji: Sɔlemi, Baiblo ɔ nɛ o maa kase, kɛ asafo mi kpehi nɛ o maa ya. (La 1:2; 1 Tes. 5:17; Heb. 10:25) Kaa bɔ nɛ e ma he be loko nɔ nɛ e nane ɔ ku ɔ ma ná tsami ɔ, jã nɔuu kɛ̃ nɛ e ma he be loko o ma nyɛ ma ná nɔ mi mami kaa Yehowa suɔ mo. Se o ma nyɛ ma ná nɔ mi mami kaa bɔ fɛɛ bɔ nɛ e ji ɔ, Yehowa kɛ o tɔmi ɔ maa ke mo. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, o kɔni mi nɛ ko jɔ̃. Moo ya nɔ nɛ o pee níhi nɛ ma ha nɛ o he kɛ su Yehowa he ɔ. (Flp. 3:16) Daa ligbi ɔ, moo na Yehowa si ngɛ he wami nɛ e ha mo konɛ o nyɛ nɛ o sɔmɔ lɛ ɔ he. Wɔ tsuo wa naa Yehowa kɛ Yesu si kaa a ha wɔ he blɔ kaa waa kɛ kã nɛ sɔmɔ Yehowa ngɛ blɔ klɔuu nɔ!

12 Kane Luka 1:76-79. Benɛ Zakaria ngɛ munyu tue ɔ, e de e bi Yohane ke: “O maa sɛ Yehowa hlami, nɛ o ma dla e blɔ ɔmɛ.” Mɛni blɔ nɔ Yohane Nɔ Baptisilɔ ɔ gu kɛ ha nɛ gbami nɛ ɔ ba mi? Benɛ e wa a, e ba pee fiɛɛlɔ nɛ e ngɛ kã. E de Yudabi ɔmɛ kaa a tsake a tsui, nɛ a ha nɛ a baptisi mɛ, nɛ a kplɛɛ Mesia a nɛ e maa ba a nɔ. Benɛ e baptisi Yesu se ɔ, e de nihi nɛ a pueɔ si ngɛ lejɛ ɔ ke Yesu je Mawu ngɔ kɛ ba. E de ke: “Nyɛɛ hyɛ, Mawu Jijɔ Bi ɔ nɛ e ngɔɔ je ɔ he yayami ɔ kɛ yaa a ji nɛ ɔ nɛ!” Lɔ ɔ se ɔ, Yohane wo e kaseli ɔmɛ he wami kaa a nyɛɛ Yesu nɛ ji Mesia a se.—Yoh. 1:29, 34-36, 40, 41.
