NÍ KOMƐ NƐ BAIBLO Ɔ
TU HE MUNYU
<<<P>>>
Tso Nɛ Woɔ Mɔmɔ Mla Pe Tso Kpahi
<<<P>>>
Baiblo ɔ tu tso slɔɔtoslɔɔtohi maa pee 30 a he munyu. Tso nɛ ɔmɛ a kpɛti kake ji almond tso ɔ. E piɛɛ tsohi nɛ a woɔ mɔmɔ mla ke hiɔmi bɔni nɛmi pɛ ɔ a he. E woɔ mɔmɔ nɛ ngɛ fɛu nɛ e yaa kaa pink loo futaa kɛ yaa Ja-nuary nyagbe loko tso kpahi po woɔ mɔmɔ. Israelbi ɔmɛ tsɛɔ almond tso nɛ ɔ ngɛ Hebri gbi ɔ mi ke, “nɔ ko nɛ e hɛ tsɛ̃.” Ke o hyɛ ɔ, tsakpa ko ngɛ tso nɛ ɔ kɛ e Bɔlɔ ɔ a kpɛti lo? Ee, tsakpa ko ngɛ.
<<<P>>>
Ngɛ Yeremia 7:25 ɔ sisi ningma a mi ɔ, Yehowa tsɔɔ kaa e teɔ si mɔtu mla. March 15, 2011 Blɛfo gbi Hwɔɔmi Mɔ ɔ de ke: “Kaa bɔ nɛ almond tso ɔ ‘hɛ tsɛ̃ɔ mla’ a, jã nɔuu kɛ̃ nɛ Yehowa hu ‘teɔ si mɔtu mla’ ngɛ okadi peemi blɔ nɔ . . . nɛ e bɔɔ e we bi kɔkɔ ngɛ nɔ́ nɛ maa je a tue gbomi ɔ mi kɛ ba a he. . . . Jehanɛ hu ɔ, Yehowa be e he jɔɔe, nɛ tsɔɔ kaa e hɛngmɛ maa hi e munyu ɔ he kɛ yaa si be nɛ nɔ́ nɛ e de ɔ maa ba mi.”—Yer. 1:11, 12.
<<<P>>>
Be komɛ ɔ, Baiblo ɔ ngɔɔ mua nɛ ngɛ nikɔtɔmahi a yi mi ɔ kɛ toɔ mɔmɔ futaa nɛ almond tso ɔ woɔ ɔ he. (Fiɛɛ. 12:5) Akɛnɛ mɔmɔ nɛ ɔmɛ ngɛ fɛu he je ɔ, benɛ Bezalel ngɛ kane tso ɔ pee ngɛ kpe he bo tsu ɔ mi ɔ, e pee kplu ɔmɛ nɛ́ a kɛ kane mi nu ɔ wo mi ɔ kaa bɔ nɛ mɔmɔ ɔmɛ a patã ngɛ ɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ.—2 Mose 37:17-20.
<<<P>>>
Womi nɛ ɔ, wa jua we. A pee kɛ ha je kɛ wɛ Baiblo tsɔsemi ní tsumi ɔ, nɛ nihi jeɔ a suɔmi mi nɛ a haa nɔ́ ko nɛ a kɛ fío se.
