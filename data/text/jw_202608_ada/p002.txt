LA 55
Nyɛ Ko Ye Mɛ Gbeye!
<<<P>>>
OTI NƐ NGƐ NÍ KASEMI Ɔ MI
<<<P>>>
Ko ha nɛ Satan nɛ mlɔɔ mo kaa o be nyɛe maa ngɔ o hɛ kɛ fɔ Yehowa Mawu kɛ Yesu Kristo a nɔ.
<<<P>>>
Ngɔɔ O Hɛ Kɛ Fɔ Yehowa Kɛ Yesu A Nɔ, Nɛ O Kua Satan Ga Tsɔmi Ɔmɛ
<<<P>>>
“Nyɛɛ te si kɛ wo Abosiami, nɛ e maa tu nyɛ nya fo.”—YAK. 4:7.
<<<P>>>
KƐ JE be nɛ wa ma wa juɛmi nya si kaa wa ma sɔmɔ Yehowa a, Satan kɛ e daimonio ɔmɛ a mi mi fu wawɛɛ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, a bɔɔ mɔde kaa a ma ha nɛ wa kpa Yehowa sɔmɔmi. Satan le kaa ke wa ya nɔ nɛ wa hi Yehowa kasa nya a, Yehowa maa ye bua wɔ nɛ e be nyɛe maa ye wa nɔ kunimi. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, Satan bɔɔ mɔde kaa e ma ha nɛ wa kpa Yehowa sɔmɔmi konɛ Yehowa nɛ e ko ye bua wɔ hu. Blɔ kake nɛ e guɔ nɔ kɛ peeɔ jã ji kaa e haa nɛ wa nuɔ he kaa Yehowa kɛ e Bi ɔ susuu we wa he.—2 Kor. 11:14; Yak. 1:6.
<<<P>>>
2 Ke tabuli ngɛ ta hwue ɔ, e he hia nɛ a ngɔ a hɛ kɛ fɔ a nya dali ɔmɛ a nɔ wawɛɛ. Ke a be hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi ngɛ a nya dali ɔmɛ a mi ɔ, nɔ fɛɛ nɔ maa pee nɔ́ nɛ e suɔ, nɛ lɔ ɔ kɛ a wami maa wo oslaa mi. Ngɛ blɔ ko nɔ ɔ, wɔ hu wa ji tabuli, nɛ waa kɛ “mumi yayamihi” ngɛ ta hwue. (Efe. 6:12) Satan Abosiami le kaa ke hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi nɛ wa ngɛ ngɛ Yehowa Mawu kɛ Yesu Kristo mi ɔ ta a, wa be mɛ tue bue hu. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e peeɔ nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ nɛ e ma nyɛ konɛ wa ko ná hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi ngɛ a mi hu. Ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, wa ma susu blɔ nɔ nɛ Satan gu kɛ gbɔjɔ hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi nɛ nihi ngɛ ngɛ Yehowa mi blema a he, kɛ blɔ nɔ nɛ e guɔ kɛ peeɔ jã mwɔnɛ ɔ. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, wa maa hyɛ blɔ nɔ nɛ wa maa gu kɛ kua Satan ga tsɔmi nɛ ɔmɛ. Ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ nyɛɛ enɛ ɔ se ɔ
<<<P>>>
1. Mɛni blɔ nɔ Satan guɔ konɛ e kɛ ha nɛ wa kpa Yehowa sɔmɔmi?
<<<P>>>
