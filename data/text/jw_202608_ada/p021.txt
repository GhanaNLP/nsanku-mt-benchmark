3 E he ba hia nɛ a kpɔ̃ Yudabi nɛ a hi si ngɛ kaseli ɔmɛ a be ɔ mi ɔ. Se mɛnɔmɛ a dɛ aloo mɛni mi lɛ e sa nɛ a kpɔ̃ Yudabi ɔmɛ kɛ je ɔ? Zakaria de ke: “E ma he wa yi wami kɛ je wa he nyɛli kɛ nihi tsuo nɛ a ngɛ wɔ nyɛe ɔ a dɛ mi.” (Luka 1:71) Anɛ enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa Yesu ma kpɔ̃ Yudabi ɔmɛ kɛ je Romabi ɔmɛ nɛ a ngɛ mɛ yi mi wae ɔ a dɛ mi lo? Dɛbi. Mohu ɔ, e sa nɛ a kpɔ̃ Yudabi ɔmɛ kɛ je he nyɛlɔ ko nɛ ngɛ he wami ɔ dɛ mi. Lɛ ji Satan. Satan ji nɔ nɛ ha nɛ a ngɛ nɔ́ nae ɔ nɛ. (Heb. 2:14, 15) Lɛ nɛ e ha nɛ adesahi tsuo nɛ Yudabi ɔmɛ hu piɛɛ he ɔ peeɔ yayami nɛ a gboɔ ɔ nɛ. Jeha-nɛ hu ɔ, Yudabi ɔmɛ nyɛ we nɛ a ye Mose Mlaa a nɔ kɛ pi si. Lɔ ɔ he ɔ, a ba je gbiɛmi ko sisi, nɛ e he ba hia nɛ a kpɔ̃ mɛ. (Rom. 3:20; Gal. 3:10, 13) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, si nɛ Yehowa wo kɛ gu Zakaria nɔ ɔ he ma hia mɛ wawɛɛ. Lɔ ɔ ji, “yi wami hemi . . . kɛ gu a he yayami ɔmɛ nɛ a kɛ maa ke mɛ ɔ nɔ.”—Luka 1:77.

4 Mwɔnɛ ɔ, wɔ hu wa peeɔ yayami nɛ wa gboɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e he ba hia nɛ a kpɔ̃ wɔ kɛ je yayami kɛ gbenɔ dɛ mi. (Rom. 5:12) Je-hanɛ hu ɔ, Satan kɛ e daimonio ɔmɛ ha nɛ wa ngɛ nɔ́ nae. A bɔɔ mɔde kaa a maa sisi wɔ konɛ a kɛ ha nɛ wa kpa Yehowa sɔmɔmi. A haa nɛ ‘nihi nɛ a nyɛɔ wɔ’ ɔ teɔ si kɛ woɔ wa jami ɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e he hia nɛ a kpɔ̃ wɔ.

5 Kekleekle ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ wa susu blɔ nɔ nɛ Yesu gu kɛ kpɔ̃ wɔ kɛ je yayami dɛ mi ɔ he nɛ waa hyɛ. Benɛ Yesu ngɛ zugba a nɔ ɔ, e tsɔɔ e se nyɛɛli ɔmɛ blɔ nɔ nɛ a maa gu kɛ ja Ye-howa nɛ maa sa e hɛ mi. Lɔ ɔ se ɔ, e ngɔ e wami kɛ ha kaa kpɔmi nɔ́ “konɛ nɔ fɛɛ nɔ nɛ e heɔ lɛ yeɔ ɔ, e hɛ mi nɛ ko kpata, mohu ɔ, e ná neneene wami.” (Yoh. 3:16; Mat. 20:28) Wa bɔɔ mɔde wawɛɛ kaa waa kɛ nɔ́ nɛ Yesu de kɛ kɔ blɔ nɔ nɛ wa maa gu kɛ ja Yehowa nɛ e maa sa e hɛ mi ɔ he ɔ ma tsu ní. Se wa yi mluku, enɛ ɔ he ɔ, be fɛɛ be nɛ wa ma tɔ̃ ɔ, wa kpaa Yehowa pɛɛ kaa e ngɔ wa tɔmi ɔmɛ kɛ ke wɔ. Kɛkɛ ɔ, kɛ gu Yesu kpɔmi afɔle sami ɔ nɔ ɔ, Yehowa ngɔɔ wa yayami ɔmɛ kɛ keɔ wɔ, nɛ e kplɛɛɔ wa jami ɔ nɔ. (1 Yoh. 2:1, 2) Kɛ gu blɔ nɛ ɔmɛ a nɔ ɔ, Yesu kpɔ̃ɔ wɔ kɛ jeɔ yaya-mi dɛ mi konɛ wa nyɛ nɛ waa hi si kɛ ya ne-neene.
