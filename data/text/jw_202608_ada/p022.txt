Yehowa ma he wa yi wami kɛ gu Yesu Kristo nɔ
(Hyɛ kuku 6)
<<<P>>>
Yemi ɔ mi ɔ, Yesu ma tle nihi nɛ a gbo ɔ si, nɛ bɔɔbɔɔbɔɔ ɔ, e kɛ nihi 144,000 nɛ a maa piɛɛ e he kɛ ye nɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ adesahi tsuo maa ye mluku. (Yoh. 5:28; Kpo. 22:1, 2) Benɛ jeha akpe ɔ maa ba nyagbe ɔ, a ma je yayami kɛ gbenɔ kɛ je kulaa. (1 Kor. 15:26, 55-57) A ma kpata Satan kɛ e daimonio ɔmɛ kɛ nihi tsuo nɛ a ma fĩ a se ɔ hɛ mi ngɛ nyagbe ka a mi. (Kpo. 20:7-10) Nihi nɛ a maa ye Yehowa anɔkuale ɔ ma nyɛ ma sɔmɔ lɛ nɛ a be yaya-mi pee hu, nɛ a be gboe, nɛ nɔ ko be si tee kɛ wo mɛ hu! Akɛnɛ Yesu kpɔ̃ wɔ he je ɔ, wa ma je Yehowa yi, nɛ wa maa na lɛ si kɛ ya neneene ngɛ ‘nike ní nɛ e kɛ ha faa nɛ e hí kalemi ɔ he.’—2 Kor. 9:15.

HE BLƆ NƐ WA NÁ KAA WA MA SƆMƆ YEHOWA
<<<P>>>
7 Kane Luka 1:74, 75. Kɛ gu kpɔmi nó ɔ nɔ ɔ, wa náa ‘he blɔ kaa waa kɛ kã ma sɔmɔ Yehowa ngɛ blɔ klɔuu nɔ’ kaa bɔ nɛ Zakaria de ɔ. Mɛni blɔ nɔ wa guɔ kɛ sɔmɔɔ Yehowa ngɛ “blɔ klɔuu nɔ”? Ke wa fiɛɛɔ, wa sɔleɔ, wa kaseɔ Baiblo ɔ, nɛ wa yaa asafo mi kpehi, kpɔ mi kpehi, kɛ kpokpa nɔ kpehi ɔ, lɔ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa wa ngɛ Yehowa sɔmɔe ngɛ blɔ klɔuu nɔ. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, ke wa ngɛ sɔmɔe ngɛ Betel, wa maa Matsɛ Yemi Asahi kɛ Kpe Peemi Asahi, aloo wa yeɔ buaa kɛ dlaa mɛ ɔ, lɔ ɔ hu tsɔɔ kaa wa ngɛ Yehowa sɔmɔe ngɛ blɔ klɔuu nɔ.

8 Ke a ke waa kɛ “kã” nɛ sɔmɔ Yehowa ngɛ blɔ klɔuu nɔ ɔ, mɛni e tsɔɔ? Kekleekle ɔ, e tsɔɔ kaa e sɛ nɛ waa ye gbenɔ gbeye. Akɛnɛ wa le kaa wa maa hi si kɛ ya neneene he je ɔ, waa kɛ wa be, kɛ wa he wami tsuo pee we níhi nɛ wɔ nitsɛmɛ pɛ wa suɔ, mohu ɔ, waa kɛ Yehowa sɔmɔmi peeɔ nó titli ngɛ wa si himi mi.
