11 Satan sume nɛ waa ngɔ mlaahi nɛ Yehowa wo kɛ kɔ nó nɛ da, kɛ nó nɛ dɛ he ɔ kɛ tsu ní. Blɔ kake nɛ e guɔ nɔ kɛ peeɔ jã ji Intanɛti ɔ. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nó ɔ, nihi fuu tsɔɔ a juɛmi ngɛ social media nɔ, nɛ a deɔ ke mlaahi nɛ ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi ɔ a be be. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, nɔ fɛɛ nɔ ma nyɛ maa pee nó nɛ e suɔ. Akɛnɛ Satan ji “je ɔ nɔ yelɔ” he je ɔ, e haa nɛ nihi fuu naa nó yaya kaa nó kpakpa, nɛ a naa nó kpakpa kaa nó yaya.—Yoh. 14:30; Yes. 5:20; 1 Tim. 4:1, 2.

12 Ke wa buɔ níhi nɛ nihi deɔ kɛ kɔ nó nɛ da kɛ nó nɛ dɛ he ɔ tue ɔ, lɔ ɔ ma nyɛ ma ha nɛ waa nu he kaa níhi nɛ Yehowa deɔ wɔ ɔ pi anɔ-kuale. Nyɛmiyo ko nɛ a tsɛɛ lɛ ke Shannon* ɔ de ke: “E he be saii loko i ba yɔse kaa níhi nɛ i ngɛ kasee ngɛ sukuu, kɛ níhi nɛ i kɛ ngɛ bɔe ɔ ngɛ ye susumi nɔ he wami náe ngɛ blɔ nɛ dɛ nɔ. Ye bua bɔni níhi nɛ Yehowa sume ɔ a he jɔmi. I pueɔ be fuu kɛ kaneɔ níhi nɛ nihi deɔ ngɛ social media nɔ ɔ. Enɛ ɔ ha nɛ i suɔ nɛ ma pee níhi nɛ a peeɔ ɔ. Bɔɔbɔɔbɔɔ ɔ, i bɔni Yeho-wa he jemi, nɛ ke i ngɛ ye wekuli kɛ nyɛmimɛ nɛ a ngɛ asafo ɔ mi ɔ a kpɛti ɔ, ye bua jɔɛ hu.” Pee se ɔ, Shannon bɔ mɔde nɛ e kpale kɛ ba Yehowa ngɔ ekohu, nɛ Satan nyɛ we nɛ e ná e susumi nɔ he wami hu. Se mɛni lɛ ye bua lɛ?

13 Shannon ba yɔse kaa e he hia nɛ e bɔ mɔde wawɛɛ konɛ e nyɛ nɛ e ya nɔ nɛ e kɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu nɛ pee huɛmɛ. E de ke: “I sɔle ha Yeho-wa konɛ e ye bua mi nɛ ma ná huɛmɛ kpakpa-hi, nɛ lɔ ɔ se ɔ, i hla níhi a mi ngɛ asafo ɔ womi ɔmɛ a mi. Video nɛ ji, Young People Ask—How Can I Make Real Friends? nɛ i hyɛ ɔ ye bua mi wawɛɛ.* Nyɛmiyo ɔ nɛ ngɛ jamɛ a video ɔ mi ɔ kɛ si fɔfɔɛhi nɛ i kɛ ngɛ kpee ɔ nɔuu kpe. E je he si bami mi nɛ e de e fɔli ɔmɛ konɛ a ye bua lɛ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, i bɔ mɔde kaa ma kase e nɔ hyɛmi nó ɔ. Lɔ ɔ he ɔ, imi hu i de ye fɔli ɔmɛ, asafo mi nikɔtɔma amɛ kɛ nyɛmiyihi komɛ nɛ a nane pi si ngɛ asa-fo ɔ mi ɔ konɛ a ye bua mi nɛ ma kpa huɛ yaya bɔmi. I ba na kaa loko ma nyɛ ma sɔmɔ Yehowa saminya a, asafo mi kpehi nɛ ma ya, kɛ ye fɔli ɔmɛ tue nɛ ma bu ɔ pɛ pi, mohu ɔ, e sa nɛ ma ha nɛ huɛ bɔmi kpakpa nɛ hi i kɛ Yehowa wa kpɛti, nɛ ma ná nɔ mi mami nɛ mi wa kaa níhi nɛ i ngɛ kasee ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi ɔ ji anɔkuale.”
