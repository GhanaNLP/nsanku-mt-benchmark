NƆ NƐ WA MA NYƐ MAA KASE NGƐ ONESIMO NƆ HYƐMI NƆ Ɔ MI
<<<P>>>
10 Wɔ tsuo wa deɔ ní komɛ aloo wa peeɔ ní komɛ nɛ gbaa ni kpahi a nya. Se Onesimo nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ maa tsɔɔ wɔ blɔ nɔ nɛ wa maa gu kɛ dla waa kɛ wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ wa kpɛti ke nɔ́ ko kaa jã a ba. E pee nɔ́ ko nɛ eko ɔ, o ma susu kaa nɔ́ ko nyafii, se e ji nɔ́ ko nɛ e he waa kɛ haa wa kpɛti nihi fuu kaa wa maa pee. E kplɛɛ e tɔmi nɔ, nɛ e pee nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ nɛ e ma nyɛ konɛ e kɛ dla e kɛ Filemon a kpɛti ekohu. Onesimo le pãã kaa ngɛ Roma mlaa a nya a, e sa nɛ e kpale e se kɛ ya e nyɔmtsɛ ɔ ngɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e hia blɔ maa pee si tomi 900 kɛ ho Filemon ngɔ ya. Blɔ hiami nɛ ɔ ma ha nɛ e puɛ sika wawɛɛ, nɛ pɔ hu maa tɔ lɛ, se e pee jã. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, e ma bi nɛ e pee klaalo kaa e maa pee nyɔguɛ ekohu, nɛ e kplɛɛ tue gblami nɛ eko ɔ, e maa je nɔ́ nɛ e pee ɔ mi kɛ ba a nɔ. Nɔ́ nɛ ɔ peemi be pee gbɔ-jɔɔ kulaa. Se ke e nɛ tɔmi ko ngɛ fo nɛ e tu kɛ je e nyɔmtsɛ ɔ he ɔ he hu nɛɛ? Ke eko ɔ, e nu he jã po ɔ, e ha we nɛ lɔ ɔ nɛ tsi lɛ blɔ nɛ e ko kpa-le kɛ ya e nyɔmtsɛ ɔ ngɔ. Mohu ɔ, e kpale kɛ ho nɛ e kɛ e nyɔmtsɛ ɔ ya dla a kpɛti, nɛ tue mi jɔmi ba a kpɛti jehanɛ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
10. Mɛni wa ma nyɛ maa kase ngɛ Onesimo nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ mi?
<<<P>>>
11 Ke o pee nɔ́ ko aloo o de nɔ́ ko nɛ e gba nɔ ko nya a, e sa nɛ o ya nɔ ɔ ngɔ konɛ o ya dla o kɛ lɛ nyɛ kpɛti. (Mat. 5:23-25) E ngɛ heii kaa jã peemi be gbɔjɔɔ, se tue mi jɔmi nɛ maa hi o kɛ nɔ ɔ nyɛ kpɛti ɔ he hia wawɛɛ. Kekleekle ɔ, moo je o tsui mi nɛ o kpa nɔ ɔ pɛɛ. Moo bu lɛ tue sa-minya nɛ o bɔ mɔde nɛ o nu lɛ sisi. Moo kplɛɛ o tɔmi ɔ nɔ. Ko je o nya, nɛ ko pia ni kpahi hulɔ ngɛ nɔ́ nɛ o pee ɔ he, ke pi jã a, e he maa wa kaa nyɛ ma dla nyɛ kpɛti nɛ lɔ ɔ ma puɛ sane ɔ ku-laa. E biɔ he si bami loko o ma nyɛ ma kpa pɛɛ. Se lɔ ɔ hu ji blɔ nɛ hi pe kulaa nɛ o ma nyɛ maa gu nɔ kɛ dla o kɛ nɔ ko nyɛ kpɛti.
<<<P>>>
NƆ́ NƐ WA MA NYƐ MAA KASE NGƐ FILEMON NƆ HYƐMI NƆ́ Ɔ MI
<<<P>>>
12 Filemon nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ tsɔɔ wɔ bɔ nɛ wa ma plɛ kɛ di tue mi jɔmi se ha ke nɔ ko tɔ̃ wa nɔ. Anɛ Filemon kɛ Onesimo tɔmi ɔ ke lɛ, nɛ e ha nɛ e kɛ lɛ ba hi si ekohu lo? Baiblo ɔ tsɔɔ we. Se Paulo ngɛ nɔ mi mami kaa Filemon maa pee jã. (Flm. 17, 21) Paulo le kaa Filemon ngɛ su kpa-kpa komɛ, nɛ lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ e he be wae ha lɛ kaa e kɛ Onesimo tɔmi ɔ maa ke lɛ. Mɛni ji jamɛ a su ɔmɛ ɔ?
<<<P>>>
11. Ke wa tɔ̃ nɔ ko nɔ ɔ, mɛni e sa nɛ waa pee kɛ dla waa kɛ lɛ wa kpɛti ekohu? (Hyɛ foni ɔmɛ hulɔ.)
<<<P>>>
12. Mɛni ha nɛ Paulo ná nɔ mi mami kaa Filemon kɛ One-simo tɔmi ɔ maa ke lɛ ɔ?
<<<P>>>
Moo je o tsui mi nɛ o ba o he si, nɛ o bɔ mɔde nɛ o di tue mi jɔmi se kaa bɔ nɛ Onesimo pee ɔ (Hyɛ kuku 11)
