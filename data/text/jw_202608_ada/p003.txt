SATAN TU LAKPA MUNYUHI KƐ SI YEHOWA KƐ YESU
<<<P>>>
3 Benɛ Yehowa bɔ Adam kɛ Hawa se bɔɔ pɛ ɔ, Satan suɔ nɛ Adam kɛ Hawa nɛ a gbo Ye-howa nɔ tue. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e bi Hawa ke: “Anɛ Mawu de niinɛ kaa nyɛ ko ye abɔɔ ɔ mi tso ɔmɛ ekoeko nɔ yiblii lo?” (1 Mose 3:1-5) Satan suɔ nɛ Hawa nɛ he ye kaa e Bɔlɔ ɔ ha we e kɛ e huno ɔ ní kpakpahi tsuo nɛ a hia a. Se enɛ ɔ ji lakpa, nɛ Hawa ya he Satan lakpa nɛ ɔ ye. Aywilɛho sane ji kaa Adam kɛ Hawa je Yeho-wa, nɔ nɛ ha mɛ ní kpakpahi tsuo ɔ se, nɛ a ya fĩ Satan Abosiami mohu se.—1 Mose 3:6.
<<<P>>>
4 Kɛ je Adam kɛ Hawa a be ɔ mi kɛ ma a, Sa-tan ya nɔ nɛ e ngɛ lakpa munyuhi tue kɛ ngɛ Yehowa sie. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, Satan gu Hiob huɛmɛ etɛ komɛ a nɔ kɛ tu Yehowa he lakpa munyuhi kɛ tsɔɔ lɛ. Satan suɔ nɛ Hiob nɛ e nu he kaa Yehowa sume lɛ, nɛ ke lɛ Hiob ɔ, e ye Yehowa anɔkuale po ɔ, pi Yehowa kɔmɔ. (Hiob 22:1-3) Abosiami susu kaa ke e ngɔ haomihi kɛ ba Hiob nɔ ɔ, e ma kua Yehowa. E ngɛ mi kaa Satan ngɔ haomihi kɛ ba Hiob nɔ mohu lɛɛ, se Hiob ya nɔ nɛ e ye Yehowa, nɛ ji e huɛ ɔ anɔkuale.—Hiob 27:1, 5.
<<<P>>>
5 Benɛ Yesu ba zugba a nɔ ɔ, Satan bɔ mɔde kaa e ma ha nɛ nihi nɛ a ko nyɛɛ Yesu se. Blɔ
<<<P>>>
* Tsa pi Satan nɛ ngɔɔ nyagbahi tsuo nɛ waa kɛ ngɛ kpee ɔ kɛ baa wa nɔ tɛɛ. Se e kudɔɔ nihi nɛ a sɔmɔ we Yehowa a a susumi, nɛ a peeɔ a ní kaa lɛ konɛ e kɛ ha nɛ a wa Mawu we bi yi mi. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, ke Satan tua wɔ tɛɛ jio, e tua we wɔ tɛɛ jio, wa le kaa lɛ nɛ e hɔɔ si ngɛ haomihi tsuo nɛ waa kɛ ngɛ kpee ngɛ Yehowa sɔmɔ-mi mi ɔ se, ejakaa e kɛ wɔ ngɛ hwue.—1 Kor. 2:12; 1 Yoh. 5:19.

5. Mɛni Satan pee konɛ e kɛ ha nɛ nihi nɛ a kpa Yesu se nyɛɛmi? (Mateo 11:19)
<<<P>>>
kake nɛ e gu nɔ kɛ pee jã ji kaa e ha nɛ jami hɛ mi nyɛɛli ɔmɛ kɛ ni komɛ nɛ a sume Yesu ɔ gbɛ Yesu he lakpa munyuhi kɛ fia, nɛ nyagbenya-gbe ɔ, a bu lɛ fɔ, nɛ a gbe lɛ kaa julɔ.—Kane Mateo 11:19; 27:27-31; Mar. 14:55, 56; Yoh. 8:41-45.
<<<P>>>
6 Benɛ Yesu gbo se ɔ, Satan bɔ mɔde kaa e ma ha nɛ Yesu se nyɛɛli ɔmɛ nɛ a kpa fiɛɛmi. Kekleekle ɔ, akɛnɛ Satan sume nɛ nihi nɛ a he ye kaa Mawu tle Yesu si he je ɔ, e gu ni komɛ a nɔ kɛ ye lakpa kaa Yesu kaseli ɔmɛ ba ju Yesu gbogboe ɔ kɛ je. (Mat. 28:11-15) Lɔ ɔ se ɔ, Sa-tan ha nɛ jami hɛ mi nyɛɛli ɔmɛ kɛ ma nikɔtɔ-ma amɛ wa Yesu se nyɛɛli ɔmɛ yi mi wawɛɛ. Satan susu kaa jã nɛ e maa pee ɔ ma ha nɛ a ye gbeye konɛ a ko nyɛ nɛ a tsu ní tsumi nɛ Yesu fã mɛ kaa a tsu ɔ.—Níts. 7:57–8:1; 12:1-3; 28:22.
<<<P>>>
7 E ngɛ mi kaa Kristofo ɔmɛ ekomɛ a tsui po nɛ a kpa fiɛɛmi mohu lɛɛ, se Yesu kaseli ɔmɛ ekomɛ lɛɛ a ya nɔ nɛ a ye anɔkuale, nɛ a kpa we fiɛɛmi. A yɔse kaa Satan kɛ e daimo-nio ɔmɛ lɛ a ngɛ hlae nɛ a kpa fiɛɛmi ɔ nɛ. (Níts. 4:18-20; 1 Pet. 5:8) Mwɔnɛ ɔ hu ɔ, mɛni ga tsɔmihi Satan kɛ tsuɔ ní konɛ e kɛ ha nɛ wa kpa Yehowa sɔmɔmi? Nyɛ ha nɛ waa hyɛ.

8 Mwɔnɛ ɔ, Satan ga tsɔmi titli nɛ e kɛ ngɛ ní tsue ji kaa e ha we nɛ nihi nɛ a le anɔkuale nɛ kɔɔ Yehowa kɛ Yesu a he ɔ. Akɛnɛ Satan ji “la-kpa a e tsɛ” he je ɔ, e guɔ lakpa jamihi a nɔ kɛ gbɛɔ Yehowa kɛ Yesu a he lakpa munyuhi kɛ fiaa. (Yoh. 8:44) E ha nɛ nihi li nɔ tutuutu nɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu ji.
