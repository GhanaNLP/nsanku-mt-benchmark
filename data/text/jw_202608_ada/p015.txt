3 Ke Onesimo kpale kɛ ho ɔ, kɛ Filemon kɛ lɛ maa ye ha kɛɛ? Filemon ngɛ he blɔ ngɛ mlaa nya kaa e maa gbla e tue. Se anɛ e maa pee jã lo? Eko ɔ, Paulo maa hi haoe kaa ke nó ko kaa jã a ba a, kɛ nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ nɛ a ngɛ asafo ɔ mi ɔ maa pee a ní ha kɛɛ? Anɛ e ma nyɛ maa ngɔ mi gbami kɛ ba nyɔguɛhi kɛ a nyɔmtsɛmɛ a kpɛti lo? Paulo le kaa nó ko kaa jã a ma nyɛ ma pue tue mi jɔmi nɛ ngɛ asafo ɔ mi ɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e ngma sɛ womi kɛ ha Tihiko nɛ́ e kɛ ya ha Filemon. (Efe. 6:21, 22; Kol. 4:7-9; hyɛ study note nɛ ngɛ Kolosebi 4:7 ɔ.) Ngɛ jamɛ a be ɔ mi nɔuu hu ɔ, Paulo ngma sɛ womi kɛ ya ha nyɛmimɛ nɛ a ngɛ Kolose asafo ɔ mi ɔ. Nyɛ ha nɛ waa hyɛ níhi nɛ wa ma nyɛ maa kase kɛ kɔ Paulo, Onesimo, kɛ Filemon a he ngɛ sɛ womi enyɔ nɛ ɔmɛ a mi.

4 Mɛni wa ma nyɛ maa kase ngɛ Paulo nɔ hyɛmi nó ɔ mi? Bɔ nɛ Paulo kɛ nihi hi si ha a ha nɛ tue mi jɔmi hi asafo ɔ mi. Paulo nɔ hyɛmi nó ɔ maa ye bua wɔ konɛ waa na blɔhi a nɔ nɛ wa maa gu kɛ di tue mi jɔmi se ngɛ asafo ɔ mi.
<<<P>>>
5 Paulo kɛ Filemon hi si kaa e huɛ. (Flm. 17) Paulo susuu we kaa e kɛ Filemon ma nyɛ maa tu munyu bɔ nɛ e suɔ kaa nɔ́ nɛ e nyɔguɛ ji Filemon. (2 Kor. 1:24) Paulo na Filemon kaa e nyɛmi nɛ e kɛ lɛ pee kake kɛ ngɛ ní tsue, nɛ e kai lɛ hu kaa a ji huɛmɛ. Enɛ ɔ nɛ Paulo pee ɔ ji nɔ hyɛmi nó kpakpa kɛ ha Filemon ngɛ bɔ nɛ e sa nɛ e kɛ Onesimo, nɛ́ amlɔ nɛ ɔ, e ba pee Kristofono ɔ nɛ hi si ha! Jehanɛ hu ɔ, enɛ ɔ ji nɔ hyɛmi nó kpakpa kɛ ha wɔ tsuo, titli ɔ, asafo mi nikɔtɔmahi. Nikɔtɔmahi nɛ a kɛ nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ hiɔ si kaa huɛmɛ ɔ yeɔ buaa nɛ tue mi jɔmi hiɔ asafo ɔ mi. Nikɔtɔma amɛ ji nɔ hyɛli nɛ a ngɛ suɔmi, nɛ a pee we a he “nyɔmtsɛmɛ” ngɛ to kuu ɔ nɔ. (1 Pet. 5:1-3) Mohu ɔ, a náa be kɛ haa Mawu to kuu ɔ, a buɔ mɛ tue, nɛ a naa mɛ kaa a he hia, nɛ a yeɔ buaa mɛ ngɛ fimi behi a mi. Kaa bɔ nɛ Paulo pee ɔ, asafo mi nikɔtɔmahi nɛ a kɛ ni kpahi hiɔ si kaa huɛmɛ ɔ peeɔ nɔ hyɛmi nó kpakpa kɛ ha a nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ, nɛ a yeɔ buaa nɛ tue mi jɔmi hiɔ asafo ɔ mi.

6 Paulo je Filemon yi. (Flm. 4, 5, 7) Wɔ tsuo wa hia yi jemi. Mɛni he je? Ejakaa behi fuu ɔ, wa ti nihi fuu nuɔ he kaa wa he be se nami. Eko ɔ, wa maa nu he kaa wa he be fɛu, aloo nihi sume wa sane, aloo kaa sɔmɔmi he blɔ ko nɛ wa bua jɔ he ɔ maa je wa dɛ. Tɔmi ko be he kaa wa maa nu he jã. Se he numi nɛ ɔmɛ ma nyɛ ma ha nɛ waa hao wawɛɛ, aloo wa mi mi nɛ fu mlamlamla. Eko ɔ, wa maa nu he po kaa ni kpahi hɛɛ we wa he juɛmi kpakpa, nɛ e ma nyɛ ma ha nɛ waa pee níhi nɛ maa tsɔɔ kaa wa he ngɛ se nami pe ni kpahi. Ke e ba lɛ jã a, tue mi jɔmi nɛ ngɛ waa kɛ wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ wa kpɛti ɔ ma pue. Se ke wa jeɔ ni kpahi a yi ɔ, e ma ha nɛ a maa nu he kaa a he ngɛ se nami. (Abɛ 12:25) Jehanɛ hu ɔ, e ma ha nɛ a maa nu he kaa wa suɔ mɛ nɛ a he hia. Anɛ o maa suɔ nɛ o ha nɛ ni kpahi nɛ a nu he jã lo? Ke jã a, lɛɛ moo ngɔ he blɔ fɛɛ he blɔ nɛ o ma ná a kɛ je nihi a yi faa. Ke a pee nó kpakpa ko ɔ, moo bɔ mɔde nɛ o je a yi. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, ke o na kaa a ngɛ a he mɔde bɔe kaa a maa pee nó kpakpa ko ɔ, lɔ ɔ hu mo je a yi. Ke wa ma de ɔ, ke o na nó kpakpa ko ngɛ nɔ ko he ɔ, mo de lɛ.
