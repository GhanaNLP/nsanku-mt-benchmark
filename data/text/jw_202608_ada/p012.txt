Ke wa suɔ wa nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ nɛ kake peemi ngɛ wa kpɛti ɔ, wa ma nyɛ maa ye Satan kɛ e daimonio ɔmɛ a nɔ kunimi
(Hyɛ kuku 16)
<<<P>>>
Yehowa suɔ nɛ waa pee jã. (Rom. 13:1, 2) Tsa pi Satan nɛ hɔ wo mlaahi tsuo nɛ dɛ nɛ ma nɔ yeli ɔmɛ wo ɔ se. Se ke ma nɔ yeli ɔmɛ wo mlaa ko nɛ e hí, nɛ wa mi mi fu nɛ wa te si kɛ wo mɛ ɔ, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ Satan bua maa jɔ wawɛɛ. Mwɔnɛ ɔ, ke ma nɔ yeli ɔmɛ wo mlaa ko nɛ nihi a bua jɔɛ he ɔ, a teɔ si kɛ woɔ mɛ. E sɛ nɛ Kristofohi nɛ a pee jã kulaa! (Abɛ 24:21, 22, sisi ningma; Fiɛɛ. 8:2, 3) Ke waa kɛ wa he wo ma kudɔmi sanehi a mi ɔ, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ wa be Mawu Matsɛ Yemi ɔ se fĩe hu, nɛ wa maa pee Satan suɔmi nya ní.—Yoh. 15:19.

14 Ni komɛ nɛ a te si kɛ wo Yehowa Odasefohi be ko nɛ be ɔ ba ngɛ Yehowa sɔmɔe amlɔ nɛ ɔ. (1 Tim. 1:12, 13) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, “ke nihi pue [wa] hɛ mi si, nɛ a wa [wɔ] yi mi, nɛ a ye lakpa nɛ a tu munyu yaya slɔɔtohi kɛ si [wɔ]” po ɔ, e sa nɛ wa kai kaa a ma nyɛ maa pee tsakemi, nɛ a ma ba sɔmɔ Yehowa ligbi ko. (Mat. 5:11) Be fɛɛ be ɔ, e sa nɛ waa sɔle kɛ ha ma nɔ yeli ɔmɛ konɛ a ngmɛ wɔ blɔ nɛ wa sɔmɔ Yehowa ngɛ tue mi jɔmi mi. (1 Tim. 2:1, 2) Jehanɛ hu ɔ, e sa nɛ waa sɔle kɛ ha wa wekuli, nihi nɛ waa kɛ mɛ tsuɔ ní kɛ nihi nɛ waa kɛ mɛ yaa sukuu nɛ a teɔ si kɛ woɔ wɔ ɔ. Moo sɔle nɛ o de Yehowa nɛ e ye bua mo nɛ o le nɔ́ nɛ o ma nyɛ maa pee konɛ o su a tsui he. Se wa kpa mo pɛɛ nɛ o to o tsui si! Yo ko nɛ e te si kɛ wo e huno jeha babauu, nɛ pee se e ba pee Odasefono ɔ de ke: “Loko wa ma nyɛ maa ye bua ni kpahi nɛ a tsake a susumi ɔ, wa hia tsui si tomi, ee, wa hia tsui si tomi wawɛɛ nitsɛ.”
