16 Zakaria munyu nɛ ngɛ Luka 1:68-79 ɔ
kaiɔ wɔ ní etɛ komɛ nɛ waa kɛ fiɛ we kulaa.
Lɔ ɔmɛ ji: Wa yi wami nɛ Yehowa ma he, he
blɔ nɛ wa ná kaa wa ma sɔmɔ Yehowa kɛ kã,
kɛ fiɛɛmi ní tsumi ɔ nɛ wa ma tsu ɔ. Anɛ
o ngɛ hemi kɛ yemi ngɛ Kristo kpɔmi afɔle
sami ɔ mi, nɛ o jɔɔ o he nɔ kɛ ha Yehowa kaa
o ma sɔmɔ lɛ kɛ ya neneene lo? Anɛ o bua jɔ
he blɔ nɛ o ná kaa o ma sɔmɔ Yehowa a he lo?

16. Mɛni o fia o pɛɛ si kaa o maa pee? (Hyɛ foni ɔmɛ
hulɔ.)
<<<P>>>
Ke jã a, lɛɛ o kɛ kã nɛ “jaje Yehowa mɔbɔ
nami jeha a.” Moo fiɛɛ sane kpakpa a nɛ tsɔɔ
kaa e be kɛe nɛ Mawu Matsɛ Yemi ɔ ma kpa-
ta yiwutsotsɛmɛ tsuo a hɛ mi, nɛ e maa pee
zugba a Paradeiso ɔ. Moo fɔ nihi tsuo nɛ a
suɔ Mawu ɔ nine konɛ a ba piɛɛ wa he nɛ waa
“kɛ kã nɛ sɔmɔ lɛ ngɛ blɔ klɔuu nɔ, nɛ́ wa fĩ
e se, nɛ́ waa pee níhi nɛ da ngɛ e hɛ mi wa
wami ligbi ɔmɛ tsuo.”—Luka 1:74, 75.

FONI Ɔ MI TSƆƆMI
Bafa 24: Kekleekle foni ɔ; nyɛmimɛ yihi enyɔ nɛ a ngɛ
fiɛɛe ngɛ wehi a mi. Foni enyɔne ɔ; nyɛminyumu ko nɛ
e ngɛ nyumu ko nɛ e kɛ lɛ tsuɔ ní ɔ odase yee be mi nɛ a
ngɛ a he jɔɔe. Foni etɛne ɔ; nyɛmiyo wayo ko nɛ e ngɛ
níhi a mi tsɔɔe ngɛ sukuu. E ngɛ e sukuu bi ɔmɛ kɛ
e tsɔɔlɔ ɔ odasehi nɛ maa nɔ mi kaa nɔ ko lɛ bɔ níhi ɔ
a mi tsɔɔe, nɛ a ngɛ lɛ tue bue saminya.
