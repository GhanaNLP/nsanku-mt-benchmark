15 Ke wa kaseɔ Baiblo ɔ nɛ wa le Yehowa kɛ Yesu saminya a, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ wa ná nɔ mi mami kaa a suɔ wɔ niinɛ, nɛ a suɔ nɛ wa bua nɛ jɔ. (Abɛ 22:19) Ke wa pue wa yi mi tɛ ngɛ ngmamihi nɛ tsɔɔ kaa Yehowa kɛ Yesu susuɔ adesahi a he ɔ he ɔ, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ wa maa na mɛ kaa huɛmɛ kpakpahi, nɛ wa ma nyɛ maa ngɔ wa hɛ kɛ fɔ a nɔ. Odasehi fuu ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi nɛ tsɔɔ bɔ nɛ Yehowa kɛ Yesu suɔ wɔ wawɛɛ ha. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, Yehowa tsɔɔ kaa suɔmi nɛ e ngɛ kɛ ha e we bi ɔ kle kulaa pe suɔmi nɛ binyɛ ko ngɛ kɛ ha e bi. (Yes. 49:15) E naa nihi nɛ a suɔ e biɛ ɔ kaa e “weto ní nɛ he jua wa.” (Mal. 3:16, 17, sisi ningma) Baiblo ɔ ha nɛ wa na munyu nɛ Yesu hu tu kɛ tsɔɔ kaa e suɔ wɔ wawɛɛ nitsɛ ɔ. Suɔmi nɛ e ngɛ kɛ ha wɔ ɔ he je nɛ e ba gbo kɛ ha wɔ ɔ nɛ.—Yoh. 15:12, 13.
