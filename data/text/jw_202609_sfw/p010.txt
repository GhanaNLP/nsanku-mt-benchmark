BƆ YISRAELFOƐ NEMƆ YƐLE NE, NZU YEE YESUA YEFI NU Ɔ?
<<<P>>>
6 Yɛ bɔ yɛsõ Yehowa ne de, yetie ye dwi-rɛ, na bɛbɔ beli nningyein nyunu wɔ asafo nanu ne koso, yetie b'akwangyerɛ. Nako-so Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ dwirɛ ne, ɔmaa yenwu kyɛ, bɔ yeti yeɛ yetie bɛ ne, odikyɛ yesusu yenwo. Nzuati yeɛ yɛka sɔ ɔ? Ebiaa né Yi-sraelfoɛ nemɔ mbena kyɛ bɛ piesie nemɔ wu, ɛhene ati yeɛ bɛfale mmoaen mogya bɛyɛle b'anoa nemɔ nwo ne. Yeti yɛ koso yɛkɔhora yekobisa yɛnwo kyɛ, ‘Sɛ Yeho-wa ma yɛ akwangyerɛ bie, na yɛnhyɛ da yennwu mvasoɛ bɔ ɔwɔ so a, ɛyɛ a yetie anaa?’ Yɛnea nikyee foforɛ bɔ Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ yɛle ɔ. Kɔngɔen bɔ befili Egypt berɛ ne, Yehowa maa ‘Egyptfoɛ nemɔ lole bɛ paa,’ yeti bɛhyɛle bɛ nningyein pee mbaen. (Ɛks. 12:36) Ɛhene ati befi berɛ ne, ne bɛ-nye agye. Nakoso siɛ ye bɔ yeɛ benwuni kyɛ Egyptfoɛ nemɔ ba bɛ so ne de, ban-nya gyidie kyɛ Yehowa kɔperpɛ kole bɛ, na mmom bɛfale b’anyeborolɛ beeguale Mose so. (Ɛks. 14:10-12) Yɛ koso ɛ? Bɔ mmenia yɛ ne, yeɛ ɔmaa yetie Yehowa dwirɛ anaa? Yɛ de yenguro kyɛ yekosuesua Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ. Yɛpena kyɛ yetie Yehowa, ɔne bɛbɔ waye bɛ kyɛ beli nningyein nyunu ne. Na-koso sɛ yɛkɔhora yɛkɔyɛ sɔ a, sana yedé bɛ yedi. (Kenga Yeremia 23:4.) Ɛhene ati, sɛ bɛfa akwangyerɛ bie bɛba, na bɔ ɔmaa bɛka sɔ ne, yɛnde yebo a, ɛmɔma yetie. Yɛɛ sɛ yetie na mmenia kɔfɛ yɛ so yaa po a, nnɛ-mɔmaa yɛnea bɔ bɛyɛ ne. Yede nzu yeɛ ɔkɔ-boka yɛ maa yɛkɔyɛ sɔ ɔ?
<<<P>>>
7 Ɔdɔ bɔ ele ma Yehowa ne, nnɛmaa ɔte kɔ. Sɛ asafo ne ma yɛ akwangyerɛ a, nningyein bie ti, ɔkɔhora kɔyɛ sɛ kyɛ yɛkɔfa yɛkɔyɛ adwuma. Ebie yeɛ ole kyɛ, ebiaa ne yɛn-hyɛ da yennwu mvasoɛ bɔ ɔwɔ so ɔ. Anaa ebiaa yenwu ye kyɛ yɛfa yɛyɛ adwuma a, yɛnwo kɔhyere yɛ. Ɔba sɔ a, ɛmɔma yɛkae bɔ yeti yeɛ yetie asafo ne akwangyerɛ ɔ. Yɛɛ ole kyɛ yɛdɔ Yehowa, na yele yenu gyi-die. (1 Yoh. 5:3) Ɔdɔ bɔ yele yɛma Yehowa nati, yekonya aboterɛ, na yamia yɛnye yafa yayɛ adwuma, na siɛ ye yekoonwu kyɛ saa ne mvasoɛ wɔ so.—Nwo. 7:8; Mika 7:7.
<<<P>>>
8 Aliemaamɔ pee wɔ berɛ a, asafo ne fale akwangyerɛ bie wale ne, né bɛnhyɛ da ben-nwu mvasoɛ bɔ ɔwɔ so ɔ. Nakoso bemiane bɛnye bɛfa bɛyɛle adwuma. Benu ko yeɛ ole asafo nu panyi bie bɔ ɔwɔ Peru. Mmerɛ bɔ COVID-19 anwonyerɛ ne wale ne, asafo ne hane kyɛ yɛyɛ adesua wɔ vidio kɔɔl so. Ahyɛaseɛ ne de, ne onwu ye kyɛ bɛfa bɛyɛ adwuma wɔ bɛ berɛ a, ɔngɔboka bɛ. Nako-so wamva ye adwene wangɔ ɛhene aso, na mmom ɔfale yenwo tole Yehowa so, na ɔyɛ-le bɔ asafo ne aha ne. (Any. 3:5, 6) Ɔyɛle sɔ ne, siɛ ye ɔhane kyɛ: “Akwangyerɛ ne bo-kale y'asafo ne paa. Ofikyɛ sɔ mmerɛ ne po yeɛ aliemaamɔ yɛle kõ ɔ, na bɛ gyidie yɛle se. Ehe maa nwuni kyɛ, yɛfa akwangyerɛ yɛyɛ adwuma a, Yehowa ngɔma yɛ sa ngosi ase lle.” Aliemaa brasua bie wɔ Japan. Ye koso ɔfale akwangyerɛ yele adwuma. Afoɛ 2020 ɔkɔ ye ayielee ne, akwangyerɛ kue ne fale nhyehyɛɛ bie wale. Bɛhane kyɛ asafo, ɔne grup bɔ bɛfa maen foforɛ so dwudwo-lɛ bɛyɛ adesua ne, bɛnu bie wɔ berɛ a, begu ye. Aliemaa brasua ne koso, ne ɔwɔ sɔ grup ne bie nu. Ɔwale sɔ ne, ɔyɛle ye yea, nako-so bɔ ɔhane ehe: “Akwangyerɛ bɔ wawa ne, menguro kyɛ ngɔkekã yenwo dwirɛ, na mmom mepena kyɛ mefa meyɛ adwuma. Mese kyɛ siɛ ye ngonwu kyɛ sãa ne mvasoɛ wɔ so.” Bɔ aliemaa hemɔ ayɛ ne, yɛ koso yɛkɔhora yekosuesua bɛ.
