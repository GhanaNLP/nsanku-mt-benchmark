dwirɛ mukoraati hyirele Yehowa. (Kenga 2 Aberɛso Dwirɛ 20:5, 6, 10-12.) Nzu yeɛ yesua yefi nu ɔ? Sɛ yɛfa ɔhaw nu po a, mbaeɛ ne Bible bɔ yekosua de, nnɛmɔmaa yɛfa yedi angorɛ. Ɛhene kɔmaa yekonya gyidie kyɛ Yehowa ngɔye yɛnu lle. (Edw. 119:112) Yɛyɛ sɔ a, okyire kyɛ yasi y’adwene pi kyɛ, bɔ ɔte biala yɛkɔpena Yehowa akwangyerɛ, na yafa yayɛ adwuma.

12 Mbaeɛ bɔ Yehosafate bɔle ne, ɔmaa yenwu kyɛ ne ɔse tworɔnzɛm nanu paa. Nzuati yeɛ yɛkã sɔ ɔ? Ye mbaeɛ ne, ɔmaa yenwu kyɛ, aseɛ bɔ bete so ne, ne ɔse kyɛ Yehowa hyɛle Abraham bɔ kyɛ ɔkɔfa kɔma bɛ. (Gye. 12:7) Afei mbaeɛ bɔ Solomon bɔle mmerɛ bɔ ne ɔfa asɔre awuro ne wura Yehowa sa ne, né Yehosafate se. Yeti ye mbaeɛ nanu dwirɛ ne, ebie wɔ berɛ a, ɔpena yɛ kyɛ Solomon deɛ ne bie ala. (2 Abe. 6:28-30; 20:5-9) Ɔnate kyɛ ne ɔse edwirɛ hemɔ nati, ɔmaa gyidie bɔ ne ole ye wɔ Yehowa nu ne, ɔyɛle piri. Ɛhene yeɛ ɔmaa osili ye adwene pi kyɛ okotie ye akwangyerɛ ɔ. Nzu yeɛ yesua yefi nu ɔ? Emɔma yesua Bible ne daa biala. Yɛyɛ sɔ a, na bɔ yasua ye dada ne, yɛfa bie yɛboka ye. (Mat. 13:52) Yeti sɛ nningyein nu yɛ se sɛɛ po a, bɔ yasua nati, yekosi y’adwene pi kyɛ yekotie Yehowa dwirɛ.

13 Yehosafate mbaeɛ ne, ɔmaa yenwu nikyee foforɛ bie. Yɛɛ ole kyɛ ne ɔbrɛ yenwo ase. Nzuati yeɛ yɛka sɔ ɔ? Mmerɛ bɔ maen nemɔ wale ye so ne, okuro a ahaa okonya adwene kyɛ, bɔ ɔte biala ɔne ye sogyafoɛ nemɔ kɔhora bɛ. Nakoso wannya sɔ adwene ne, na mmom ɔbɔle mbaeɛ, na ɔhahyirele Yehowa kyɛ wammoka bɛ a, bɛngɔhora bɛ nyunu gyina. Ɔbɔ sɔ mbaeɛ ne, ne Yudafoɛ nemɔ wɔ berɛ. (2 Abe. 20:12) Ɔnate kyɛ ɔbrɛ yenwo ase nati, Yehowa mane ye akwangyerɛ nala yeɛ otiele ɔ. Yɛ koso, ɛmɔma yesuesua Yehosafate, na yebre yenwo ase. Yɛyɛ sɔ a, bɔ ɔkɔto yɛ biala, yekotie Yehowa dwirɛ.—Flp. 2:5, 8.

14 Yehowa tiele Yehosafate mbaeɛ ne. Ɛhene ati ɔmaa ye diyifoɛ Yahasiel mane bɛ akwangyerɛ. Yɛɛ ole kyɛ, nnɛmaa bɛne mmenia nemɔ kɔko, na mmom ‘bɛte begyina, bɛyɛ konn, ná bɛnea ngonim bɔ AWURADE koli kɔma bɛ ɔ.’ Ɔwale sɔ ne, nzu yeɛ Yehosafate yɛle ɔ?
