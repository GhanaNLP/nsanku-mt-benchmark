15 Yehowa maa ye asafo ne boka yɛ. Nwomaa ne vidio bɔ asafo ne ayɛ ne, Yehowa fa boka yɛ maa yɛne ye afia yɛ kama. Yede nzuati yeɛ yenwo hia ɔ? Yɛte sona nati, ɔwɔ nu alaa a, adwenedwene kɔhyɛ yɛ so. Ebiaa bɔne bɔ yayɛ yapɛnu ne, sɛ ɔbape yɛ tianu a, ne yɛ wora abɔ. Afei koso yɛkora yenya adwene kyɛ mvasoɛ biala nne yɛ so. Abɔnzam de, adwene pa biala nne yetianu. Yeti sɛ onya nwu ye sɔ pɛ, yede ɔkɔyɛ tonn maa y’abanu kobu koraa, kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a, yɛkɔyakyi Yehowa soen. (Mat. 4:3; 6:13) Ɛhene ati vidio ne nwomaa bɔ asafo ne ayɛ ne, ɔboka yɛ maa yenwu kyɛ, yɛyɛ bɔne a, Yehowa ne yɛ ndwe ye twann, na mmom ɔfa kyɛ yɛ koraa. Afei yɛnwo hia ye, na kyɛbɔ ɔte biala ɔkɔnea yɛ. (Edw. 86:5; Mat. 10:29-31; Luka 12:28; Heb. 13:5) Yɛka ye, ebiaa ɛkengale asafo nwomaa bie, na eyiele ne, bɔ ɛhane alaa yeɛ ole kyɛ, ‘Aa, ɛhe de ɛnwa nzɛ bɛse bɔ mefa nu ɔ.’ Ɛhe maa yenwu kyɛ nikyeebiala bɔ ɔkɔmaa yɛne Yehowa afia kɔyɛ kama ne, asafo ne fa ma yɛ.

16 Yehowa ngɔma bengohu ye mmenia mukoraati lle. Nakoso odwu mmerɛ bie a, ɔma atee maa bɛtane ye mmenia nye. Ebiemɔ po de, bɛkora beku bɛ. Nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ ɔpena kyɛ ebiala nwu kyɛ Abɔnzam nga nahorɛ. (Nye. 2:10; 6:9) Abɔnzam de, nna bɔ ɔpena yeɛ ole kyɛ, bekohu Yehowa mmenia kaa bie ɔ. Na mmom onyane a, ahaa bahu Yehowa mmenia mukoraati. (Mat. 24:9; Nye. 12:9-12) Nakoso Yehowa ngɔma atee maa sɔ dwirɛ ne ngosi lle! Ɔkɔmaa ye abɔfo mɔ kɔbɔ yɛnwo waen, kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a yɛkɔhora yɛkɔsõ ye, na yaha nzɛmba ne.—Heb. 1:7, 14.

17 Akwangyerɛ bɔ Yehowa fama yɛ wɔ Bible nanu ne, ɔbɔ yɛnwo waen. Ofikyɛ sɛ yedi so a, yɛngɔyɛ nningyein bɔ ɔkɔfa yɛ kɔhɔ ɔhaw nu ɔ. (Any. 14:29, 30; 22:3; 2 Kor. 7:1) Nakoso, nna ɔhaw biala yeɛ Yehowa kɔye yɛ kofi nu ɔ. Nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ Satan nwa, Yehowa bɔ yɛnwo waen nati yeɛ yɛsõ ye ɔ. Wɔ de nea o, sɛ Yehowa ye yɛ fi ɔhaw biala nu a, ɔkɔmaa mmenia konya adwene kyɛ Satan dwirɛ bɔ ɔhane ne, ɔte nahorɛ. (Yob 1:10, 11) Bɔ Satan aha ato Yehowa so nati, ɔmaa bɛbɔ bɛtane yɛnye ne, Yehowa ‘anya bɛnwo aboterɛ.’ Ofikyɛ ɔpena kyɛ ɔte ye dumaa nanwo, na ɛhene ati yeɛ wayakyi bɛ kyɛ bɛhɔso bɛyɛ bɔ bɛyɛ ne. (Rom. 9:22) Nakoso ɔngɔhye, bɛ mmerɛ kɔso maa Yehowa kɔsɛkye bɛ mukoraati. Kesaalae bɔ sɔ mmerɛ ne walle ne, yɛse kyɛ sɛ ɔhaw bie to yɛ a, Yehowa ngɔye yɛnu. Sɛ ɔba sɔ a, yɛngɔyɛ nikyeebie bɔ ɔkɔsɛkye yɛ ne ye afia ɔ.—1 Kor. 10:13; 1 Pet. 5:10.
