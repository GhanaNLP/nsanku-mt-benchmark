10 Bɛbɔ bekuro kyɛ bɛsɔ Yehowa nye ne, odikyɛ bekyi bɔne. (Edw. 97:10) Yeti odikyɛ yebisa yɛnwo kyɛ: ‘Yehowa mmraa bɔ ɔkã kyɛ, odikyɛ ebie gya kora na ɔne ebie ala ne, menwu ye kyɛ, ɔyɛ se soma, ɛhene ati mengora menyɛ bɔ mekuro anaa? Nningyein bɔ mefa megyegye menye ne, aso ɔte bɔ Yehowa kuro anaa? Yehowa mmraa ne bie wɔ berɛ a, nyane a ahaa wasesa nu kãa, kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a, ngɔhora ngɔyɛ bɔ mekuro anaa?’
<<<P>>>
11 Bɔ Yehowa kyi ne, ɛyɛ a nikyee bie twetwe wɔ kyɛ ɛyɛ anaa? Sɛ sɔ a, nnɛmaa w’abanu bu kyɛ ɛngɔhora ngɔyɛ bɔ Yehowa kuro ɔ. Kae kyɛ yɛte sona, yeti ɔyɛ sɛɛ alaa a bɔne wɔ yɛnwo. (Edw. 51:5; 103:14) Nakoso sɛ yekuro a yeɛ yekɔyɛ bɔne, anaa yekoli “anwoɔnaen akɔnnɔ” si ɔ. (Ɛfe. 2:3) Yeti, ɔnzɛkye yɛmaa akɔnnɔ bɔne dé yɛ adwene bɔ so. Na sɛ ɔba w’adwene nu kyɛ ɛyɛ bɔ Yehowa kyi a, ye fi wɔ tianu ndɛ. (Gye. 39:9, 12) Afei ɛyɛ a dwene kyɛbɔ bɔne bɔ ɛkɔyɛ ne, ɔkɔhora kɔsɛkye ɛne Yehowa ne aliemaamɔ afia nanwo. Yere wɔnwo kyɛ ɛngɔmaa sɔ dwirɛ ne bie ngɔto wɔ. (Ɛfe. 5:5) Sɛ ɛmaa Yehowa boka wɔ a, ɛkɔhora kɔhwe wɔnwo kofi bɔne nwo.

12. Mmenia bɔ bɛmva Nyameɛ mmraa bɛnyɛ adwuma ne, nningyein beni yeɛ bafa bagua intanɛt so ɔ?
<<<P>>>
doɔ nala fi mmenia bɔ bɛmva Nyameɛ mmraa bɛnyɛ adwuma ne berɛ. Tekyɛ ponografi sɔa, ɔnnyɛ nna wɔ berɛ. Ngwalaa nea bie, yeɛ mbanyi koso nea. Ebiemɔ kora yeye bɛnwo na bapɛ bɛnwo foto, anaa bayɛ vidio na bafa basɛnde-sɛnde bɛnwo. Ɛhe taa kɔso wɔ mmrandeɛ ne ndaluwa nu paa. Edwirɛ pee wɔ intanɛt so a, ɔtte nahorɛ. Ebie wɔ berɛ a, bahaahaa bɛnye yeɛ bɛka ɔ. Afei nningyein bɔ mmenia ahora ayɛ, nekaa bɔ batutu atee bahɔ, ɔne bɔ bele ye ne, ekonwu ye ne, na bafa baato sohyia midia naso. Sɛ yɛtaa yɛnea bɛ foto, yɛkenga edwirɛ bɔ bahworɔ bagua ye bo, ɔne bɛ vidio ndika nemɔ a, annea a, yekonya adwene kyɛ sɔ nningyein nemɔ yeɛ ɔmaa ebie nye gye ɔ. Yɛnye kɔwa yɛnwo so ne, na yɛngora yengɔ adesua ne asɛnga, ofikyɛ ne wale yɛ wabɔ so.—Heb. 10:24, 25.

13 Nikyee biala bɔ ɔwɔ intanɛt so ne, ɔkɔhora kole yɛ mmerɛ, anaa ɔkɔmaa yekonya adwene bɔ ɔttemaye. Yeti odikyɛ yɛnea yé. Sɛ yenguro kyɛ ebie kɔto yɛ a, odikyɛ yebisa yɛnwo kyɛ: ‘Nningyein bɔ mmenia fa gua sohyia midia so ne, mmerɛ dodoɔ nyeɛ yeɛ mefa menea ɔ?’ (Ɛfe. 5:15, 16) ‘Sɛ mekɔ intanɛt naso a, nningyein beni yeɛ menea ye paa ɔ? Menwu nikyee bie bɔ ɔttemaye koso a, aso meye mefi berɛ ndɛ?’ (Edw. 119:37) ‘Edwirɛ biala bɔ menwu ye wɔ berɛ ne, medé medi, anaa mepena nahorɛ bɔ ɔwɔ nu ɔ?’—Any. 14:15.
