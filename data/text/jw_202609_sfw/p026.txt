Bɔ Wɔnwo Waen Fi
“Ewiase Sunzum” Nanwo
<<<P>>>
“Bɔ ɔwɔ wiase amukoraa . . . offi Baba ne berɛ.”—1 YOH. 2:16.
<<<P>>>
WIASE bɔ yɛte nu ne, Satan yeɛ odi so ɔ. (Yoh. 14:30; 1 Yoh. 5:19) Yeti bɛbɔ bayɛ b’adwene kyɛ bekoli Yesu si ne, Satan mma bɛnwo ndɔ bɛ. (Nye. 12:12, 13, 17) Ɛhene kyire kyɛ Satan wɔ wa a, sɔ mmerɛ nala, ne osa wɔ nekaa foforɛ anaa? Daabi. Abɔfo nemɔ po, Bible ne nga kyɛ bele sɔ tumi ne. Nakoso Satan le sunzummɔne pee bɔ ɔne bɛ yɛ adwuma ɔ. (Nye. 12:9) Afei, wafa wiase sunzum wawura nekaa biala. Ɛhene ati, Satan anya mmeni pee nala so tumi bɔ bɛnze ɔ. (Kenga 1 Yohane 2:15, 16.) Bɛ bɔ bɛnné benni kyɛ Satan wɔ berɛ po ne, subaen bɔ bɛda ye ali ne kyire kyɛ wiase sunzum ne yeɛ okyire bɛ atee ɔ. Ɛhe kyire kyɛ bɛyɛ bɔ Satan kuro ɔ. Atee bie aso ne, okyire kyɛ Satan “ahye bɛ yeɛ ɔfa bɛ yɛ bɔ okuro ɔ.”—2 Tim. 2:26, NWT.
<<<P>>>
2 Yɛnu bie wɔ berɛ a, mmerɛ bie bɔ wapɛ nu ne, ewiase sunzum yeɛ ne okyire yɛ atee ɔ. Nakoso, yesuane Bible bɔ yenwuni kyɛ, Satan yeɛ ɔmaa mmenia da sɔ subaen nemɔ ali ne, nzu yeɛ yɛyɛle ɔ? Ɛberɛ ne ala, Bible nu dwirɛ bɔ yenwa ‘yegyi bra fiɛ ne wiase akɔnnɔ yégua’ ne, yɛfa yɛyɛle adwuma. (Tito 2:11, 12) Ɔwɔ nu, Satan le tumi paa. Nakoso sɛ yɛyere yɛnwo kyɛ yɛkɔfa Bible nu dwirɛ yɛkɔyɛ adwuma a, okyire kyɛ Yehowa yeɛ ɔta yɛ si ɔ. (Yak. 4:7, 8) Aliemaamɔ, yɛkamvo ɛmɔ paa, ofikyɛ ɛmɔ bɔ mmɔden kyɛ ɛmɔ ngɔma ewiase sunzum ne, ɔngɔtwetwe ɛmɔ.
