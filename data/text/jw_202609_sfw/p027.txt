Ewiase sunzum ne, ɔte kyɛ mvrama. Yeti sɛ yannea yé a, okonya yɛ
(Nea ngyekyɛmuɛ 5)
<<<P>>>
yeti yeɛ yɛkã sɔ yeɛ ole kyɛ, ebiemɔ bɔle mmɔden yɛle bɔ Nyameɛ kuro ɔ, nakoso aworaborɛ dwirɛ yeɛ ole kyɛ, siɛ ye, bɛmaa Satan nyane bɛ. (Ɛfe. 6:11; Flp. 3:18) Adesua he anu ne, yɛkɔnea bɔ yɛkɔyɛ na sɔ dwirɛ ne bie ando yɛ. Nakoso ɛmɔma yelimoa yɛnea sɔ dwirɛ he. Sɛ bɛkã ‘ewiase sunzum’ a, okyire sɛɛ?—1 Kor. 2:12.

BƐKÃ ‘EWIASE SUNZUM’ A, OKYIRE SƐƐ?
<<<P>>>
4 Mmenia bɔ bɛnnɔ Nyameɛ ne, kyɛbɔ bɛdwene ɔne kyɛbɔ besi bɛyɛ bɛ nikye ne, ɛhene yeɛ Bible ne frɛ ye ‘ewiase sunzum’ ne. Sɔ mmenia ne, bɛte pɛsɛmangomenya. Afei, Nyameɛ mmraa bɔ ɔfa abrabɔ nwo ne, bɛmva bɛnyɛ hwee. Ebie yeɛ ole dwudwolɛ fiɛ bɔ bɛkã, ɔne brabɔne bɔ bɛbɔ ne. Bɛnyɛ bɔ Nyameɛ kuro ɔ, na mmom bɛbɔ bɛ “bra kyɛ amaenmaensofoɛ.”—1 Pet. 4:3.
<<<P>>>
5 Ewiase sunzum ne, ɔte atee ko bɔ Satan afa so “ɔlakalaka wiase amukoraa.” (Nye. 12:9) Somafoɛ Pɔɔlo hane yenwo dwirɛ kyɛ ɔte kyɛ “mvrama.” (Ɛfe. 2:1, 2, NWT) Pɔɔlo hane a, ɔnnaka! Mvrama de ɔwɔ nekaa biala, na daa yɛhome. Nakoso sɛ bɛsɛkye mvrama ne, na ɛhome bie kɔ wɔ nu a, ɔyɛ suro paa. Sɔ ala yeɛ ewiase sunzum ne te ɔ. Ɔtekyɛ mvrama bɔ basɛkye ye ne. Yeti sɛ ebie home bie kɔ ye nu a, ɔkopura ye. Yede, ɛmɔma yɛnea bɔ yɛkɔyɛ na ewiase sunzum annya yɛ, mmerɛ bɔ yɛnye ɔlla yɛnwo so ɔ.
