SƐƐ YEE ASƆFODIE NE MAA YENWU KYƐ YEHOWA DƆ YE Ɔ?
<<<P>>>
11 Asɔfodie ne maa yenwu kyɛ Yehowa pena kyɛ ɔfa ye adamvoa. Kyɛbɔ yalimoa yanwu ye ne, bɔne bɔ ɔwɔ yɛnwo nati, wayɛ kyɛ kumaa kandinga bie ala yɛne Yehowa afia ne. Yeti yɛnwa yepingye ye a, ɔnyɛ yé. Nakoso Yehowa nguro ye sɔ, na mmom okuro paa kyɛ yepingye ye, yɛka yɛ dwirɛ yekyire ye, na yenya daa ngoa. (2 Kor. 5:18) Yemia ɔse kyɛ nna yɛ yee yayɛ ɔ; Adam ne Hawa yee baafa sɔ haw he amu bawa ɔ. Yɛnwo yɛ ye anwumvo, na ɛhene ati yee wayɛ nhyehyɛɛ kyɛ ebiemɔ li sɔfo, na wamaa yafa ye adamvoa. Bɔ Yehowa ayɛ ne, ɔnyɛ wɔ fɛ anaa?
<<<P>>>
12 Yɛ Sɔfo Panyi Yesu koso, ɔpena kyɛ yɛne Yehowa afia yɛ kama. Yeti daa ɔsrɛ ma yɛ. Bible ne ka kyɛ ɔte “kamafoɛ wɔ Baba ne nyunu.” (1 Yoh. 2:1; kenga Hebrifoɛ 7:25.) Fa ye kyɛ Yesu kwaasrɛ ma wɔ. Enea a, nzu yee ɔse fa wɔnwo bɔ okogyina so kɔyɛ sɔ adwuma ne? Ɔse abusua bɔ efi nu, ɔne nekaa bɔ bɛtetele wɔ. Yɛka ye, ebiaa bɔ ɛfa nu wɔ w’abrabɔ nu ne, ɔtte angorɛ, na anwonyerɛ bie teetee wɔ po. Ɛhe amukoraati Yesu se. Afei subaen pa bɔ ɔwɔ wɔnwo, ɔne kyɛbɔ ɛbɔ mmɔden kyɛ ɛkɔso Yehowa ne, Yesu nwu ye. Bɔ wato wɔ ne mukoraati si ne, ali nahorɛ ama Yehowa, na ɛhene koso Yesu nwu ye. Yesu sa se bɔ ɔwɔ w’ahone nu ɔ. Yeti ɔse kyɛ emia wɔnye kyɛ ɛkɔyɛ bɔ Yehowa kuro ɔ, na daabiala ɛbɔ mbaeɛ kyɛ Yehowa fa wɔ bɔne hyɛ wɔ. Yehowa koso de, okuro kyɛ ɔfa wɔ bɔne kyɛ wɔ. Yeti sɛ Yesu nya srɛ ye pɛ, na wafa wahyɛ wɔ. (Edw. 86:5) Yɛda Yehowa ase
<<<P>>>
11. Sɛɛ yee asɔfodie ne maa yenwu kyɛ, Yehowa pena kyɛ ɔfa ye adamvoa ɔ?
<<<P>>>
12. Nzuati yee ɔyɛ ye fɛ kyɛ yɛ Sɔfo Panyi Yesu yee ɔsrɛ ma yɛ ɔ? (Hebrifoɛ 7:25) (Nea foto ne koso.)
<<<P>>>
Yɛ sɔfo panyi Yesu se bɔ ɔwɔ y’ahone nu ɔ. Yeti mmɔden bɔ yebɔ kyɛ yekɔyɛ bɔ Yehowa kuro ne, onwu ye, na wayɛ krado kyɛ ɔkɔsrɛ kɔma yɛ
(Nea ngyekyɛmuɛ 12)
