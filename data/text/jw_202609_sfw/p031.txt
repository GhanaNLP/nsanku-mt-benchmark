16 Ma yɛnea bɔ ɔtole Robert koso e. Wamva yenwo wammɔ nnamvoa pa wɔ sukuu nu. Yeti odwuli nekaa bie ne, ne ɔhye wii, na ne ɔno nzaa bo. Nna ɛhene angome, ne osa ne mmrienzua da. Ɔhane kyɛ: “Ne mese kyɛ nné fahodie, yeti ngɔhora ngɔyɛ bɔ mekuro ɔ.” Siɛ ye oonwuni kyɛ ɔtte sɔ, na mmom ne wayɛ akoaa wama wiase ne. (2 Pet. 2:19) Nzu yeɛ ɔyɛle ɔ? Ɔtoale so kyɛ: “Mmenia bɔ bɛdɔ Yehowa ne, yeɛ kesaalae bɛte me nnamvo ɔ. Me subaen asesa koraa. Afei kyɛbɔ mesiesie menwo, ɔne kyɛbɔ medwudwo ne koso, yemu asesa.” Kesaalae, Robert nwu yenwo sɛ? Yenwa: “Medwɔso ngyerɛmɔ na menwu kyɛ, abrabɔ bɔ nwɔ nu ɛnnɛ ne, ɔsɔ Yehowa nye a, ɔmaa menye gye paa. Ɛhe de, mefa metoto m’abrabɔ daa nanwo a, menwu kyɛ Yehowa ahyira me papa.”

17 Nzu yeɛ yahã yenwo dwirɛ wɔ adesua he anu ɔ? Yaanwu ye kyɛ, odikyɛ yɛnea yé na ewiase sunzum ne, wandwetwe yɛ. Ɔnzɛkyɛ yɛmaa edwirɛ bɔ waporɔ fi yɛ noa nu ba. Afei, abrabɔ biala bɔ Yehowa nné ndo nu ne, odikyɛ yɛtwe yɛnwo yefi yenwo. Nningyein bɔ ɔttemaye bɔ ɔwɔ intanɛt so ne, ɔnzɛkyɛ yɛnea. Ɔwɔ nu, Satan ne ye sunzummɔne ne, bɛpena kyɛ bɛyɛ dee maa ɛyɛ bɔ bekuro ɔ. Nakoso Yehowa de, ye tumi te piri tra bɛ bam. Ogyi wɔ si, yeti bengonya wɔ lle. (Kenga 1 Yohane 4:4.) Afei koso, ɛsrɛ Yehowa berɛ sunzum krongron a, ɔkɔfa kɔma wɔ, ná andɔ Satan ngaen biala nu. (Luka 11:13) Ɛmɔma yɛ mukoraati yɛnya akokoduro, na ‘yɛnate Nyameɛ Sunzum nu.’ Yɛyɛ sɔ a, yekosi ewiase sunzum ne atee. —Gal. 5:16.
