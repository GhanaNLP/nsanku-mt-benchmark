<<<P>>>
BƆ ADESŨA NE KƆHÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Yekonwu bɔ ɔkɔboka yɛ, na wanyɛ kyɛ odikyɛ yetie Yehowa dwirɛ nati yeɛ yetie ye ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Nzu Yeɛ Ɔkɔboka Wɔ Maa Ekofi W’ahone Nu Kotie Yehowa Dwirɛ Ɔ?
<<<P>>>
‘Boka wɔ somvoɛ ma onya ahone bɔ ɔbrɛ yenwo ase ɔ.’—1 AHE. 3:9.
<<<P>>>
WIASE he anu de, bɔ ɔmaa mmenia di mmraa so ne, ɔsone. Ebiemɔ suro kyɛ banni so, ná bɛkye bɛ a, edwirɛ kɔto bɛ. Ebiemɔ koso, sana benwu kyɛ ebie gyi berɛ bɔ ɔnea bɛ kora ná bali so. Yeɛ ebiemɔ de, benguro kyɛ mmenia kã kyɛ bɛ so yɛ se. Asɔre mbanyi bɔ ne bɛ wɔ Yesu aberɛ so ne, sɔ yeɛ ne bɛte ɔ. Ne bɛnwa betie Nyameɛ dwirɛ, koso bɔ na bɛyɛ namu, na bɛpena kyɛ mmenia nwu ye, na bɛka kyɛ bɛte nyamesomfoɛ. (Mat. 23:5) Nakoso Yehowa asomvoɛ de, nna sɔ yeɛ yɛte ɔ. Bɔ ɔmaa yetie Yehowa yeɛ ole kyɛ, yɛse kyɛ ɔkɔboka yɛ bɔbɔ deinn. (Yes. 48:17, 18) Nna ɛhene ngome, na mmom nikyee foforɛ bie wɔ berɛ bɔ opusu yɛ maa yetie ye dwirɛ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
2 Ɔdɔ bɔ yele yɛma Yehowa ne, ɛhene yeɛ opusu yɛ maa yetie ye dwirɛ ɔ. (Mat. 22:37, 38) Yeti nna ebie yeɛ ɔhyɛ yɛ kyɛ yetie ye ɔ. Na mmom ‘yefi y’ahone mukoraa nu yɛyɛ sotie’ yɛma ye. (Rom. 6:17) Na sɛ ebiala nne berɛ koso ɛ? Anaa bɔ Yehowa aha ne, yɛnhyɛ da yennwu mvasoɛ bɔ ɔwɔ so po a, nzu yeɛ yɛkɔyɛ ɔ? Yɛdɔ ye nati, yekotie ye dwirɛ. Nakoso yɛte sona nati, bɔ yɛkã ye, ɔte bɛtɛɛ sɔ. Yede nzu yeɛ ɔkɔboka yɛ ɔ? Yɛbaanea Bible nu dwirɛ nyɔ bie, na yasua nikyeebie yafi nu. Bɔ olimoa ne, ɔfa Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ nwo. Yeɛ bɔ ɔtɔ so nyɔ ne koso, ɔfa Nana Yehosafate nwo.
