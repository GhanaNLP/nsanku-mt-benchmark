Yedé yedi kyɛ Yehowa bɔ yɛnwo waen ɛnnɛ, na kyẽabie koso ɔkɔyɛ sɔ. Bɔ yeti yeɛ yele sɔ gyidie ne, yekonwu ye.
<<<P>>>
Nya Gyidie Kyɛ Yehowa Kɔbɔ Wɔnwo Waen
<<<P>>>
“Afa AWURADE ayɛ w’afealeɛ.”—EDW. 91:9.
<<<P>>>
YƐTE ayieleɛ mmerɛ nanu, na kesaalae ɔngɔhyɛ na wiase he asɛkye. Yeti bɔ ɔbaasi ne, ɔtte angorɛ. Nzu ɛne? Yehowa kɔfa adwene bie kowura maen nemɔ ahone nu, na bɛkɔfa b’ahennie anaa bɛ tumi bɛkɔma kaka ne, bɔ ogyi berɛ ma Maen Maen Ngabonu anaa United Nations ne.* (Nye. 17:17) Ɛhene si ne, United Nations ne awaen nemɔ kɔha kɔbɔnu, na bɛkɔsɛkye ngondombo soen koraa. (Nye. 17:16) Sɔ dwirɛ ne nya si pɛ, okyire kyɛ amaneɛnwunɛ piri ne, wahyɛ ye bo alaa ɛne. Ɛhene si ne, nzu bieku yeɛ okosi ɔ?
<<<P>>>
2 Awaen nemɔ kɔha bɛnwo bɛkɔbɔ nu, na bato bahyɛ Yehowa mmenia so. (Hes. 38:14-16) Nzu yeɛ ɔkɔmaa bɛkɔyɛ sɔ ɔ? Yɛ nwomaa bɔ bɛfrɛ ye Nokware Som—Yehowa Ama So Bio! ne, ohyirele nu kyɛ, “Satan yeɛ okopiepia bɛ ɔ. Ɛhene ati bɛkɔyɛ b’adwene kyɛ betu Yehowa soen bo koraa befi asee he aso.” Ɛhe si ne, bɔ okosi de ɔtte angorɛ. Yesu, abɔfo nemɔ, yeɛ mmenia 144,000 bɔ bɛne ye koli bia wɔ anwuro ne, bɛkɔfa bɛnwo bekole nu. Bɛne Nyameɛ pɔfoɛ mɔ kɔhõ, na basɛkye bɛ mukoraati. Ɛhene yeɛ bɛfrɛ ye Amagedon ne. Sɛ koɛ ne nya ba ayieleɛ pɛ, Yehowa kɔfa yɛ kɔhɔ ewiase foforɛ nanu.—Nye. 16:14-16; 19:11-21.
