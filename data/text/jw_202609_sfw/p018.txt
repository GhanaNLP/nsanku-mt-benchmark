13. Bible pee nu ne, ɛpena Yehowa dumaa a, en-nwu ye. Nzuati ɔ?
<<<P>>>
Nyameɛ dumaa ne po na babɔ. Roman asɔfo mbanyi nemɔ sɔa, baye Nyameɛ dumaa ne bafi bɛ Bible, ɔne bɛ nwomaa biala nu.* Nna bɛ ngome, asɔre pee yeɛ bayɛ sɔ ɔ. Nakoso, Wiase Foforo Nkyerɛa-se Bible ne de, ɔla nu komapɛ. Ɔmva ye-nwo ne dwudwolɛ bɔ bahyire yebo bahɔ nu ne, bafa Nyameɛ dumaa Yehowa ne, bawura nekaa bɔ odikyɛ owura ɔ. Yede, nzuati yeɛ odikyɛ mmenia nwu Nyameɛ dumaa ne ɔ?

14 Bɔ yeti yeɛ odikyɛ yenwu Nyameɛ dumaa, na yɛbɔ ne, Bible ne maa ye-nwu ye. Yɛnea bɔ yeti yeɛ yekã sɔ ɔ. Bɔ ɔhworɔle Edwein nwomaa ne hane kyɛ, ‘Ɛmɔye Yehowa dumaa ne ayɛ!’ (Edw. 135:1, NWT) Sɔ ala yeɛ Yesu koso hyi-rele ye asuafoɛ nemɔ kyɛbɔ bɛkɔbɔ Ye-howa mbaeɛ ɔ. Ɔhahyirele bɛ kyɛ, bɛha kyɛ: “Wɔ dumaa nwo te.” Yeɛ ye bɔbɔ koso mmerɛ bɔ ɔbɔ mbaeɛ ne, ɔhane kyɛ, “Mala wɔ dumaa ali mahyire bɛ.” (Mat. 6:9; Yoh. 17:26) Edwirɛ bɔ diyifoɛ Yoel hane ne, somafoɛ Pɔɔlo koso fale ye dwirɛ ne hane kyɛ: ‘Ebiala bɔ ɔbɔ Ye-howa dumaa ne, okonya ngoa.’ (Rom. 10:13, NWT) Sɛ ele Bible na baye Nya-meɛ dumaa Yehowa ne bafi nu a, ɛkɔ-yɛ sɛɛ na afa sɔ tworɔnzɛm hemɔ ayɛ adwuma?
