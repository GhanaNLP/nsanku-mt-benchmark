Yɛkã Nzɛmba Ne Wɔ ‘Edwudwolɛ Ne Maen Biala Nu’
<<<P>>>
‘Edwudwolɛ biala nu mmenia buru bɔ befi maen maen mɔ nu bɛkɔsosɔ Yudaniɛ ko ataadeɛ noa.’—SAK. 8:23.
<<<P>>>
ƐNEA Bible nu a, mmenia biemɔ yɛle kyɛ bekosi Babɛl aborɔsaen ne. Sɛ ne besi beyie a, ne ɔkɔmaa mmenia mukoraa kɔtena nekahorɛ. Nakoso, ne Yehowa nguro ye sɔ. Yeti ɔmaa bɛ dwudwolɛ ne yɛle basabasa. Ɛhe maa bɛyakyili adwuma ne, na ebiala fale yenye hyirele nekaa bɔ okuro ɔ. (Gye. 11:6-9) Afoɛ pee si ne, Yehowa hane kyɛ, ɔkɔhã ‘mmenilɔen kandinga’ bie kɔbonu. Sɔ lɔen ne, ‘befi maen maen, mmusua kue, mmenia ɔne edwudwolɛ amukoraa nu.’ Ɔkã bɛ bonu sɔ a, na wafa bɛ wapɛ amaneɛnwunɛ piri ne wahɔ ewiase foforɛ nanu. (Nye. 7:9, 10, 14) Yede ɔkɔyɛ sɛɛ na mmenia bɔ bɛkã dwudwolɛ ahoroɛ ne, baaboka sɔ mmenilɔen naso? Sana yɛkã nzɛmba ne yekyire bɛ wɔ bɛ kuro dwudwolɛ nu. (Kenga Romanfoɛ 10:13, 14.) Asɛnga adwuma bɔ odé mmenia ngoa ne, ɔyɛ yɛ fɛ kyɛ yɛyɛ bie ɔ!
