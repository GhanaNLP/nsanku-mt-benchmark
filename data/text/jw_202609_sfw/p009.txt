Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ yɛle bɔ Yehowa nwa bɛyɛ ne, na banzuro bɔ ne Egyptfoɛ nemɔ kɔfa kɔyɛ bɛ ɔ
(Nea ngyekyɛmue 3)
<<<P>>>
sona anaa kaka. Nakoso ne onguro kyɛ ebie kɔha Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ, yeti ɔhahyirele bɛ kyɛ bɛyɛ nikyeebie. Yeɛ ole kyɛ behu boaen anaa abɔngye, na bɛfa ye mogya ne bɛyɛ bɛ anoa nemɔ nwo. (Ɛks. 12:5, 7) Yeti sɛ ne abɔfo ne pɛnu na onwu ye a, ongohu bɛ piesie deɛ nemɔ. Nakoso ne ɔlla ase kyɛ Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ kɔyɛ bɔ ne Yehowa nwa bɛyɛ ne. Nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ ne Egyptfoɛ nemɔ afa boaen bayɛ bɛ bosoen. Yeti sɛ ne benwu kyɛ bekuku mmoaen nemɔ sɔ a, ne bɛkɔfɛ bɛso yaa, na ebiaa bahu bɛ po. (Ɛks. 8:25-27) Nakoso Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ annea ɛhene, na mmom ‘bɛyɛle bɔ AWURADE hyɛle bɛ ne ala.’ (Ɛks. 12:28) Yeti Egyptfoɛ nemɔ su ne, bɛde na bɛnwo sɔnn. Ɛhene si ne, Yehowa yɛle bɛ fili berɛ.
<<<P>>>
4 Bɛkɔ ne, Yehowa maa Mose hahyirele bɛ kyɛ, beesikye Ɛpo Kɔkorɛ ne noa nekaabie. Nakoso bedwuli berɛ ne, osa hahyirele bɛ kyɛ besa bɛ si, na bɛhɔ nekaa foforɛ. Ɛhe de, ne wayɛ kyɛ ndeaseɛ nne nu. Nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ nekaa bɔ ne Yehowa nwa bɛhɔ ne, né Ɛpo Kɔkorɛ ne la bɛ nyunu yeɛ mmokaa wɔ bɛ si, yeti ne bekowura afia. Sɛ ne Faro ne ye sogyafoɛ nemɔ nwa bɛfoa bɛ a, né bɛnwati bɛfa ni? Nakoso sɔ adwene ne amma bɛ tianu po. Sɔ mmerɛ he wora, bɛyɛle bɔ Yehowa hane ne. (Ɛks. 14:1-4) Ɛhene si ne, bɛkwaato bɛnye a, Faro ne ye sogyafoɛ nemɔ yeɛ bɛba ye. Ɔwale sɔ ne, bɛ kunu tele bɛ! Yeti bɛfale b’anyeborolɛ beeguale Mose so, na bɛha behyirele ye kyɛ, ɔpena kyɛ bewu nati yeɛ wafa bɛ waawura berɛ ɔ. (Ɛks. 14:5-11) Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ ambaa yeɛ kesaalae de, banu bɛnwo kyɛ batie Yehowa ye oo. Ɔwale ye sɛ? Na bɔ bɛyɛle ne, Yehowa koso nwuni ye sɛ?
<<<P>>>
5 Bible ne maa yenwu kyɛ anwanwadeɛ bɔ Yehowa yɛle ye wɔ Egypt ne, Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ ande yebo. Yeɛ ye dɔ bɔ onni hwammɔ ne, bangaye. (Kenga Edwein 106:7.) Yehowa bɔle bɛ piesie nemɔ nwo waen ne, benwuni ye. Nakoso bɛfale ye traa; bannwu ye kyɛ ɔdɔ bɔ ole ma bɛ nati yeɛ olɛle bɛ ɔ. Ɛhene ati, bannya gyidie kyɛ ɔto bɔ ɔyɛ se a, okosa kole bɛ. Yehowa de, onwuni bɔ ɔwɔ b’ahone nu ɔ, yeti bɔ bɛyɛle ne, ɔhane yenwo dwirɛ kyɛ ‘bate yeso atua.’ Ne beli Mose si mbaen, na banyɛ b’adwene kyɛ bekɔnya Yehowa nu gyidie. Ɛhe ati Yehowa hane kyɛ sɔ mmenia ne, bɛngɔhɔ Bɔhyɛ Aseɛ naso bie. (Edw. 106:21, 24-26) Nzu yeɛ yesua yefi bɛ dwirɛ nanu ɔ?
