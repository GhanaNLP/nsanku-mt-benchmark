8 Mmerɛ bɔ Abɔnzam sɔle Yesu neane ne, ɔfale Yesu oogyinane asɔre awuro ne ti afia dɔ. Yeɛ ɔhahyirele ye kyɛ ogyi yenwo to ase, ofikyɛ Nyameɛ kɔsoma ye abɔfo mɔ maa bɛkɔɔbɔ yenwo waen, na hwee anyɛ ye. (Kenga Mateo 4:5-7.) Ese bɔ yeti yeɛ ɔhane sɔ ne? Edwirɛ bɔ ɔwɔ Edwein 91:11, 12 nati ɛne. Ɔwale sɔ ne, Yesu anga kyɛ, ‘Abɔnzam ɛhe de ɛlaka, nna bɔ edwein ne kyire hɔ ɛne.’ Na mmom ɔhahyirele ye kyɛ: “Nneso Awurade wɔ Nyameɛ nea!” Nzu yeɛ sɔ dwirɛ ne maa yenwu ye ɔ?

9 Ɔmaa yenwu kyɛ, sãa ne Abɔnzam pena kyɛ Yesu sɔ ye Baba nea. Na mmom Abɔnzam anzu aporowaa kyɛ Yehowa ngɔbɔ Yesu nwo waen.* Yesu wɔ anwuro dɔ ne, onwuni kyɛ sɛ ne ebiemɔ pena kyɛ beku Yehowa mmenia a, ne Yehowa soma ye abɔfo mɔ maa bɛbade bɛ. (2 Ahe. 6:15-17; 19:34, 35; Dan. 3:19, 20, 26-28; 6:16-22) Nakoso ne Yesu se kyɛ sɛ ɔhyɛda kyɛ ɔyɛ nikyeebie bɔ ɔkɔmaa okopira a, ɛhene de Yehowa ngɔsoma ye abɔfo mɔ kyɛ beele ye. Yeti sɛ ne ɔyɛ bɔ Abɔnzam nwa ɔyɛ ne a, na ɔsɔ ye Baba nea. Yɛnea edwirɛ bɔ Yesu hahyirele Abɔnzam ne a, ɔmaa yenwu kyɛ, Edwein 91 nanu dwirɛ ne, bɔ okyire yeɛ

* Daa ne, yɛ nwomaa nemɔ hane kyɛ, Edwein 91 nanu dwirɛ bɔ Satan hane ne, osili yeti ase. Ofikyɛ ɔhane kyɛ Yehowa nwa ɔkɔbɔ yɛnwo waen na bɔne biala ando yɛ. Bɔ ɔmaa yɛhane sɔ yeɛ ole kyɛ, ne yɛnwa edwirɛ bɔ ɔwɔ Edwein 91 ne, okyire kyɛ Yehowa ahyɛ bɔ kyɛ ɔngɔmaa nikyeebiala ngɔsɛkye yɛne ye afia. Ɛpena yenwo dwirɛ pee a, nea Ɛwɛnɛlɛ Waen bɔ ɔwale December 1, 1974, kr. 722. Ɔte Borɔfo.

8-9. Mmerɛ bɔ Abɔnzam sɔle Yesu neane ne, nzu yeɛ Yesu hahyirele ye ɔ, na nzu yeɛ ɛhene maa yenwu ye ɔ? (Mateo 4:5-7)
<<<P>>>
ole kyɛ, Yehowa ahyɛ bɔ kyɛ ɔngɔmaa nikyeebiala ngɔsɛkye yɛne ye afia. Nna ɛhene ngome na mmom, Yehowa ngɔma yɛ pɔfoemɔ atee maa bengohũ yɛmu lle. Yeɛ sɛ yenwu amaneɛ koso a, Yehowa kɔboka yɛ maa yɛsoanu kodwo yɛ.

10 Yɛnea edwirɛ bɔ ɔwɔ Edwein 91 ne a, ɔmaa yenya Yehowa nu gyidie paa. Yeti yenzuro bɔ okosi amaneɛnwunɛ piri ne, ɔne Amagedon koɛ nanu ɔ. Yemia Yehowa te yɛ abandenden, yeti sɛ yɛpena nekaa yɛbɔ yenwo adwaa a, sana ye ala. Afei bɛbɔ bɛfa bɛnwo bɛto ye so ne, kyɛbɔ ɔte biala ɔkɔbɔ bɛnwo waen. Kesaalae bɔ yanwu kyɛ Yehowa kɔbɔ yɛnwo waen ne, yebisa kyɛ nzu yeɛ ɔyɛ fa bɔ yɛnwo waen ɛnnɛ ɔ, na kyẽabie koso ɔkɔyɛ ye sɛ?
