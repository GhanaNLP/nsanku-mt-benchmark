FIƐ FITÉ FI WƆ NOA

6 Bible ne hane kyɛ, ayieleɛ mmerɛ nanu ne, mmenia kɔyɛ ‘anwohoahoa, ɔne anwomasoɛ.’ Afei bɛkɔyɛ mmenia bɔ bɛne bɛ yɛ nhyehyɛɛ biala a, ɔnyɛ yé ɔ. (2 Tim. 3:1-3) Ɛnnɛ kyɛbɔ mmenia si dwudwo ne, ɛkora nwu kyɛ bele sɔ subaen ne bie. Sɛ ebiemɔ bɔ ngɔmmɔ a, ekonwu ye ne, ne ko ahyɛ yebo ɔpepɛ nzoaa. Wɔnye kɔwa wɔnwo so ne, na bafa basie ye nye. Bɔ yɛkã ye, ɔnyɛ nna wɔ TV so, dwaaso, awuromɔ nu, ɔne abusua nu. Ɛnnɛ de, nekaa biala bɔ ɛkɔfa ne, mbanyi oo, ngwalaa oo, ebiala dwudwo a, na ɔpɛ nzoaa. (Ɛfe. 5:3, 4) Ɛhe mukoraati di adanzeɛ kyɛ, ewiase sunzum ne, yeɛ okyire bɛ atee ɔ.
<<<P>>>
7 Kristofoɛ de, daa odikyɛ yɛmaa yɛ dwudwolɛ yɛ fɛ. Sɛ yɛne ebie bɔ ngɔmmɔ, yɛsɛnde mɛsege anaa yɛfrɛ ye wɔ fon so a, odikyɛ yɛmaa yɛnoa dwirɛ yɛ fɛ. (Yak. 1:26, 27) Sɛ yɛpena kyɛ yenwu kyɛbɔ yekodwudwo yé a, odikyɛ yebisa yɛnwo kyɛ: ‘Sɛ mefɛ yaa a, edwirɛ bɔ mekekã ne, aso yenwo te? Sɛ mene ebie bɔ ɔtte Yehowa Danzeniɛ bɔ ngɔmmɔ koso ɛ, sɛɛ yeɛ medwudwo ɔ? Mene aliemaamɔ dwudwo a, aso mekã edwirɛ bɔ ɔyɛ yea mekyire bɛ, mewɔ bɛ safure anaa megoro bɛnwo? M’abusuafoɛ koso, mene bɛ dwudwo a, ɛyɛ a meteatea bɛ anaa? Sɛ mebisa ebie kyɛ sɛɛ yeɛ medwudwo a, nzu yeɛ ɔkɔhã ɔ?’
<<<P>>>
8 Ewiase sunzum ne, sɛ yenwu ye kyɛ ɔsɛkye kyɛbɔ yedwudwo ne a, nzu yeɛ odikyɛ yɛyɛ ɔ? Odikyɛ yɛsesa. Afei nna edwirɛ bɔ yɛkɔhã ne ngome yeɛ odikyɛ yɛsesa nu ɔ. Na mmom bɔ ɔwɔ yɛ tianu ne koso, odikyɛ yɛnea ye yé. Ofikyɛ Yesu hane kyɛ: ‘Bɔ ofi sona noa nu ba ne de, ofi ye ahone nu yeɛ ɔba ɔ, na ɛhene yeɛ ogua sona nwo fiɛ ɔ.’ (Mat. 15:18) Yeti sɛ enwu ye kyɛ, subaen bie tekyɛ ahonetɛɛ, ɔne ɛyaafɛlɛ bie aye ye ti wɔ wɔ nu a, kã yenwo dwirɛ kyire Yehowa. Srɛ ye kyɛ ɔboka wɔ ma enya “sona sue foforɛ” ne. (Kenga Kolosefoɛ 3:8-10; Ɛfe. 4:31.) Afei koso, ɛkɔhora kɔyɛ nhwehwɛmuɛ wɔ asafo nwomaa mɔ nu. Bɔ mmɔden kyɛ ɛkɔmaa wɔ subaen kɔyɛ nyeme, na wɔ dwudwolɛ ayɛ fɛ. Ɛyɛ sɔ a, ɔkɔhyɛ Yehowa animnyam.—Ɛfe. 4:29.

TWE WƆNWO FI BRA FIƐ BIALA NWO
<<<P>>>
9 Ɛnnɛ, Satan yeɛ okyire mmenia atee ɔ. Yeti bɔ Yehowa kyi ne, ɛhene mmom yeɛ bɛyɛ ɔ; bɛmva ye mmraa bɛnyɛ hwee. Bɔ bɛkã ala yeɛ ole kyɛ, ɔtte bɔne kyɛ ebie kotu nwonzeɛn kogua. Afei mmrienzua ne mmrienzua, ɔne mmrasua ne mmrasua da a, ɔtte hwee. Yeɛ ebie twe somaa koso a, bɔne nne yenwo. (Yes. 5:20) Sɛ yannea yé a, ngaka-ngaka, yekonya adwene kyɛ bɔ Yehowa kyi ne, ebie yɛ bie a ɔtte bɔne. (2 Kor. 11:3) Yɛkɔhora yekonya adwene kyɛ, ebie yɛ sɔ a, okyire kyɛ yenye abukye, na Bible nanu dwirɛ ne de, wapɛ nu. Nakoso, ɛhe amukorati, nahorɛ kɔ̃ po nne nu. Bɔ Yehowa kɔhã biala ne, yɛ bɔbɔ deinn yeɛ ɔkɔboka yɛ ɔ. Ye de ɔtte kyɛ sona, yeti ye dwirɛ biala bɔ wahã ne, ɔmbɛ nu lle.—Yes. 48:17, 18; 55:9.
