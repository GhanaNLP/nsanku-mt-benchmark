Amagedon koɛ ne ba a, Yesu ne ye lbɛn ne kohu Yehowa pɔfoɛ namu, na benu komapɛ po ngɔha. Nakoso Yehowa mmenia de, benu biala nne berɛ bɔ bekohu ye ɔ
<<<P>>>
yɛse ne, awaen nemɔ kɔha bɛnwo bɛkɔbɔ nu, ná bawa Yehowa mmenia so. Nakoso Yehowa ngɔma atee maa bengohu yɛmu lle. (Hes. 38:1, 2, 10-16) Ɔkɔmaa Yesu, abɔfo nemɔ, yeɛ mmenia 144,-000 ne kɔho kɔma yɛ. Ɛhene yeɛ ɔkɔɔfa Amagedon koɛ ne kɔwa ɔ. Na bɛkɔsɛkye Nyameɛ pɔfoɛ mɔ mukoraati. (Nye. 17:12-14; 19:11, 14, 15) Yede Amagedon hyɛ yebo a, sɛɛ yeɛ Yehowa kɔbɔ yɛnwo waen ɔ?
<<<P>>>
19 Yehowa de, onni ye mmenia nwo angorɛ. Yeti ɔngɔma ye pɔfoɛ nemɔ ngohu yɛnu kɔ̃ po wɔ Amagedon. (Luka 21:28; 2 Pet. 2:9) Na mmom ɔkɔmaa Yesu ne ye lbɛn ne kohu bɛmu; sɛ yɛ pɔfoɛ nemɔ te sunzummɔne oo, sɛ bɛte sona oo, benu komapɛ po ngɔha. (Yer. 25:33; Sak. 14:12) Ɛhene ati, sɛ Amagedon koɛ ne ba a, onnikyɛ yɛmaa yɛ kunu tete yɛ. Nzuati
<<<P>>>
19-20. Amagedon koɛ ne ba a, onnikyɛ yɛmaa yɛ kunu tete yɛ. Nzuati ɔ? (Nea foto bɔ ɔwɔ Ɛwɛnelɛ Waen ne nyunu ne koso.)
<<<P>>>
ɔ? Ofikyɛ Yehowa te y’abandenden. Yeti kyɛbɔ ɔte biala, ɔkɔbɔ yɛnwo waen.
<<<P>>>
20 Sɛ Amagedon koɛ ne kɔ so a, edwirɛ bɔ edweintolɛniɛ ne hane ne, ɔkɔwa nu, na ebiaa yɛkɔfa yɛnye yekonwu ye. Yeɛ ole kyɛ: ‘Apee kɔtotɔ wɔ wɔ ngyɛnu, yeɛ apee buru kɔtotɔ wɔ wɔ sa famaa so, nakoso wɔ de ebie ngɔto wɔ. Wɔ nye mbaen yeɛ ɛkɔfa kɔnea ɔ, na ekonwu bɔnefoɛ b’akatuaa. Ofikyɛ afa AWURADE ayɛ w’afealeɛ, ná afa Anwuro Anwuroniɛ ne ayɛ w’abandenden.’ (Edw. 91:7-9) Sɔ mmerɛ ne, Yehowa ngɔma yɛ pɔfoɛmɔ atee maa bengohu yɛmu lle. Na mmom, ɔkɔboka yɛ maa yɛsoanu kodwo yɛ. Afei bɔ yehia na wamaa yɛne ye afia ayɛ kama ne koso, yɛse kyɛ ɔkɔfa kɔma yɛ. Na “ɔnate kyɛ afa AWURADE ayɛ w’afealeɛ” nati, ye koso wahyɛ bɔ kyɛ, bɔ ɔkɔto abɔnefoɛ ne, ‘wɔ de ɔngɔma ebie ngɔto wɔ.’ Yeti aliemaamɔ, nnɛmɔmaa yesuro bɔ ɔla yɛ nyunu ne. Sɛ yɛfa yɛnwo yɛto Yehowa so ngome de a, bɔ ɔte biala ɔkɔbɔ yɛnwo waen.
