ANWANWADEƐ BƆ YEHOWA YƐ YE WƆ YƐ ABERƐSO Ɔ
<<<P>>>
8 Wɔ Pɛntekɔso 33 Y.B. ne, awanwan-deɛ bie sili. Kyɛbɔ Yehowa sesane mme-nia bɔ ne besi Babɛl aborɔsaen ne dwu-dwolɛ ne, sɔ ala yeɛ Yesu asuafoɛ ne koso, Yehowa maa bɛhane dwudwolɛ fo-forɛ ɔ. (Aso. 2:4-10) Nzuati yeɛ ɔyɛ-le sɔ ɔ? Kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a, ‘anwanwadwu-ma bɔ wayɛ’ ne, ɔkɔmaa ebiala kɔte yenwo dwirɛ ɔ. (Aso. 2:11) Ɛnnɛ koso asafo ne adaworoma, yele vidio ne nwo-maa wɔ dwudwolɛ pee nu. Yeti sɛ yɛn-de ebie dwudwolɛ po a, yɛkora yedi ye adanzeɛ.
<<<P>>>
8. Anwanwadeɛ beni yeɛ Yehowa yɛle ye wɔ Pɛnte-kɔso 33 Y.B.?
<<<P>>>
9 Nzɛmba bɔ yɛkã ne, engonya ye kyɛ etie ye wɔ wɔ kuro dwudwolɛ nu ɔ. Wɔ de, ma yɛyɛ mvandoho he: Fa ye kyɛ ebie atu atee, na odwuli berɛ ne, ɔnde bɛ dwudwolɛ ne. Na ɔwɔ berɛ alaa yeɛ an-wonyerɛ bie bɔle ye ɔ. Sɛ ɔkɔ hɔspitel a, bɔ dɔktaniɛ ne kɔha kyɛ ɔyɛ ne, ɔkɔyɛ sɛɛ na wate yebo? Okuro kyɛ yenwo tɔ nati, sana ɔpena ebie bɔ ɔte dwudwolɛ ne, na wahyire yebo wama ye. Ɛhe de, ɔfa yɛ dwirɛ nanwo sɛ? Ɛnnɛ mmenia lle ahotɔ, na adwenedwene ahyɛ bɛ so. Yeti behia ebie bɔ ɔkɔkyekye bɛ wora ɔ. Nakoso sɛ yɛnde bɛ dwudwolɛ a, yɛkɔyɛ sɛɛ na ya-boka bɛ? Yehowa nate asafo naso amaa yanya nwomaa, ɔne vidio wɔ dwudwolɛ pee nu. Yeti yeyia ebie a, yɛkɔhora yɛkɔ-ma ye bie. Bɔ ohia ala yeɛ ole kyɛ, ɔwɔ ye kuro dwudwolɛ nu ɔ.
