3 Sakaria hane “Yudaniɛ” bie nwo dwirɛ wɔ ye ngɔmhyɛ nanu. Nwa yeɛ ole sɔ Yudaniɛ ne? Yeɛ ole Kristofoɛ bɔ bapopa bɛ ne. Bɛ yeɛ Bible ne sa frɛ bɛ “Yisrael bɔ ɔte Nyameɛ deɛ ne.” (Gal. 6:16) Bɛ yeɛ bɛne Yesu koli hene wɔ anwuro ne. (Heb. 3:1) Na “mmenia buru” ne koso, bɛ yeɛ ole nwamɔ? Bɛ yeɛ ole, Yehowa mmenia bɔ bele anyelasoɛ kyɛ, bɛkɔtena Paradise ne. Beffi nekaa kõ, na mmom ebiala ne maen bɔ ofi nu, ɔne dwudwolɛ bɔ ɔkã ɔ.
<<<P>>>
4 Afoɛ 1919 nu yeɛ Yesu yele “akoaa nahorɛniɛ ne nworɛniɛ” ne ɔ. Sɔ aliemaa hemɔ nwuni kyɛ, sɛ bɛkɔhora bɛkɔyɛ bɛ adwuma ne yé a, odikyɛ bekyire Bible nabo bɛkɔ edwudwolɛ pee nu. (Mat. 24:45) Sɔ mmerɛ ne, bɛyɛle vidio bie bɔ ɔfa abɔdeɛ nwo. Ne bato ye dumaa “Pho-to-Drama of Creation.” Nakoso benwuni kyɛ odikyɛ bekyire yebo bɛkɔ dwudwolɛ pee nu. Yeti wɔ afoɛ 1921 ne, bɛyele The Harp of God nwomaa ne ali, na behyirele yebo bɛhɔle dwudwolɛ kɔyɛ 36 nu. Afei odwuli afoɛ 1935 ne, “akoaa nahorɛniɛ ne nworɛniɛ” ne tele yebo kyɛ, sãa ‘mmenilɔen kandinga’ bie wɔ berɛ. Bɛ koso befi maen ne dwudwolɛ mukoraati nu, na b’anyelasoɛ yeɛ ole kyɛ bɛkɔhɔ Paradise. Ɛhe ati, bɛyɛle ndotoyɛɛ, kyɛbɔ ɔkɔyɛ a, bɛkɔhã nzɛmba ne wɔ asee so nekaa biala. Sɔ bɔ bɛyɛle ne, ɔmaa Sakaria ngɔmhyɛ ne wale nu.

5 Kyɛbɔ Yesaya hane ne, mmeni pee yeɛ bɛnye gye yenwo kyɛ bekosua Yehowa nwo nikye, na basõ ye ɔ. (Kenga Yesaya 2:2-4.) Ɛhene ati ‘yatu yɛnwo yasie berɛ’ kyɛ, yɛkɔhã nzɛmba ne wɔ ewiase nekaa biala, na yaboka bɛ maa banwu Yehowa yé. Bɔ yɛyɛ yɛfa yɛboka bɛ ne, ebie yeɛ ole kyɛ, nwomaa bɔ bɛfa besua Bible ne, yekyire yebo yɛkɔ dwudwolɛ pee nu, ná yaprente. Afei yeyie a, yɛfa yɛkɔ ewiase nekaa biala. (Edw. 68:11; 110:3) Nna ɛhene angome, yesa yele jw.org ɔne JW Library®. Na ɛhe aboka mmenia pee maa basua Bible ne wɔ bɛ kuro dwudwolɛ nu.

6 Nwomaa ne vidio bɔ akoaa nahorɛniɛ ne nworɛniɛ ne fama yɛ ne, ɔboka yɛ paa. Yede yɛkɔyɛ sɛɛ na yahyire kyɛ yɛnye sɔ? Ebie yeɛ ole kyɛ yɛkɔye ndoboa. Sɔ ndoboa nati, bahora bahyire nwomaa pee bo bahɔ dwudwolɛ kɔbo 1,100 nu. Na bɔ ɔyɛ anigye yeɛ ole kyɛ, afoɛ biala mmeni pee banwu Yehowa, na bɛbɔ asu.
