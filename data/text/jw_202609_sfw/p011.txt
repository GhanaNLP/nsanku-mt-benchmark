9 Odikyɛ yɛda yɛnwo so, ofikyɛ ɔyɛ sɛɛ alaa a, mmenia kɔtane yɛnye. Yehowa afa awura yɛ sa kyɛ yɛyɛ asɛnga adwuma ne. Yeti sɛ mmenia fɛ yɛ so yaa po a, yɛde bɔ Yehowa aha nala yɛɛ yetie ɔ. Kesaalae de, ɛkɔ maen pee so a, awaen ne nha yɛ, yeti yɛkora yɛyɛ asɛnga adwuma ne kyɛbɔ yekuro ɔ. Nakoso yɛnwo yɛ ebiemɔ ahi. Nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ edwirɛ bɔ yɛka yekyire bɛ ne, ebie yɛɛ ole kyɛ bɛbaasɛkye ewiase he. Yɛkɔha sɔ dwirɛ ne tonn yɛkɔfa yekowura amaneɛnwunɛ piri nanu, na ɔka kãa maa Amagedon ba a, yɛɛ yɛkɔyakyi ɔ. (Mat. 24:14) Amaneɛnwunɛ piri nanu ne, sɔ dwirɛ bɔ yɛkã ne, ɛhene yɛɛ ebiala kogyina so kosi gyinayɛɛ kyɛ okogyina Yehowa ahennie ne si, anaa awaen nemɔ yɛɛ okogyina bɛ si ɔ. Ɔba sɔ a bɛbɔ benguro yɛ dwirɛ ne, yede b’ahone kɔte; nzɛmba ne kɔyɛ ‘ɔhaw piri paa’ kɔma bɛ.* (Nye. 16:21) Ɛhe kɔmaa bɛkɔfɛ yɛ so yaa paa; ɔkɔyɛ kyɛ mmerɛ bɔ Faro fɛle Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ so yaa bɔ ne ɔfoa bɛ nala. Kesaalae bɔ amaneɛnwunɛ piri ne nya walle ne, sɛ yɛyere yɛnwo yɛyɛ asɛnga adwuma ne a, na yɛboa yɛnwo yɛma sɔ mmerɛ ne.

SUESUA BƆ NANA YEHOSAFATE YƐLE NE
<<<P>>>
10 Nna bɛbɔ bandie Yehowa dwirɛ ngome yɛɛ Bible ne kã bɛnwo dwirɛ ɔ. Osa kã kyɛ, ebiemɔ de betiele Yehowa dwirɛ. Benu ko yɛɛ ole Yuda hene Yehosafate. Mmerɛ bie, maen ngokongoko nza hane bɛnwo bɔle nu, na bɛwale Yuda maen naso kyɛ bɛne bɛ baaho. Ɔwale sɔ ne, ɔhene ne kunu tele ye. (2 Abe. 20:1-3) Nakoso wamma esuronyi angyekye ye, na mmom ɔyɛle nikyeebie. Ma yɛnea bɔ ɔyɛle ne, na yesua nikyee yefi nu.
<<<P>>>
11 Mmerɛ bɔ ne Yehosafate kunu tete ye ne, ɔpenane Yehowa si atee. Yede na ye subaen alaa ɛne; mmerɛ biala ne ɔpena Yehowa si atee. (2 Abe. 19:3) Nakoso sɔ mmerɛ he bɔ ne wato bɔ ɔyɛ se ne, osili ye adwene pi kyɛ, bɔ ɔte biala ɔkɔpena Yehowa akwangyerɛ, na wafa wayɛ adwuma. (2 Abe. 20:3) Nzuati yɛɛ osili ye adwene pi ɔ?
