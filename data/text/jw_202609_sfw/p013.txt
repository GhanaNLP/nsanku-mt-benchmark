15 Ne Yehosafate de di paa kyɛ Yehowa kɔboka bɛ. Yeti ɔhyɛle mmenia nemɔ ngunaen kyɛ, bɛ koso benya Yehowa nu gyidie na betie ye akwangyerɛ. Afei ‘ɔne bɛ tuli agyina.’ (2 Abe. 20:21) Ɛhene si ne, ‘ɔyele mmenia biemɔ kyɛ beli sogyafoɛ nemɔ nyunu na bɛto dwein bɛye AWURADE ayɛ.’ Betiele akwangyerɛ nati, Yehowa koso wamma bɛ sa anzi ase. Mmenia bɔ bɛwale bɛ so kyɛ bɛne bɛ baaho ne, bɛpɛle bɛnwo behuni bɛnwo.—2 Abe. 20:22-24.
<<<P>>>
16 Yehosafate tiele Yehowa akwangyerɛ nati, ye mmenia nemɔ suesuane ye. Ɛhe maa yɛkae bɔ Solomon wora yɛle ɔ. Ɔɔyɛle ɔhene ne, ne ɔpena kyɛ odi mmenia nemɔ nyunu kyɛbɔ Yehowa kuro ɔ. Yeti ɔbɔle mbaeɛ kyɛ Yehowa boka ye maa ɔbrɛ yenwo ase, na otie ye dwirɛ. (Kenga 1 Ahemvo 3:9.) Akwangyerɛ bɔ Yehowa diyifoɛ fa mane Yehosafate ne, otiele na ɔhyɛle ye mmenia nemɔ ngunaen kyɛ bɛ koso betie akwangyerɛ ne. Afei ɔbaayɛ nikyeebie koso a, na ɔne bɛ susu yenwo kora na wayɛ. Asafo nu mbanyi nemɔ suesua Yehosafate. Yeti sɛ bɛbaasi gyinayɛɛ bie wɔ asafo nanu a, bɛmva bɛ bɔbɔ b’adwene bɛnyɛ nikyefee. Bedimoa bɛnea Bible nanu, na bɛyɛ nhwehwɛmuɛ wɔ asafo nwomaa nu. Afei bebisa mbanyi bɔ baha ne maa bɛ koso bekyire b’adwene. (Any. 15:22) Sɛ asafo ne fa akwangyerɛ ba a, ɛberɛ ala ne bafa bayɛ adwuma. Bɔ bɛyɛ ne, aliemaamɔ nwu ye, yeti sɛ bɛkã kyɛ bɛfa akwangyerɛ bie bɛyɛ adwuma a, betie. Awofo nemɔ koso, Yehosafate dwirɛ ne kɔboka bɛ paa. Bɔ bɛyɛ biala, bɛ mmaa nemɔ nea. Yeti sɛ betie Yehowa akwangyerɛ a, ngwalaa nemɔ kosuesua bɛ, na sɛ bandie koso a, ngwalaa nemɔ kosuesua bɛ. (Any. 1:8; 14:1) Bɛbɔ bafa adwuma bawura bɛ sa wɔ asafo nanu ne, Yehosafate dwirɛ ne, ɔkɔ ma bɛ.

17 Yɛse kyɛ nningyein nu kɔhora kɔyɛ se. Nakoso sɛ yetie Yehowa akwangyerɛ a, ye koso ɔkɔboka yɛ. Kyɛbɔ yanwu ye ne, ofi tete yeɛ Yehowa amaa ebiemɔ afa akwangyerɛ ama ye mmenia ɔ. Sɔ ala yeɛ ɛnnɛ koso ɔyɛ ɔ. Yehowa koso de, ye yeɛ ɔse bɔ ɔte pa ma yɛ ɔ. (Yes. 55:9) Yeti sɛ akwangyerɛ bɔ ɔfa ma yɛ ne, yɛnhyɛ da yennwu mvasoɛ bɔ ɔwɔ so po a, ɛmɔma yetie. Yɛyɛ sɔ a, okyire kyɛ yɛdɔ Yehowa, na ɔtte kyɛ odikyɛ yetie ye dwirɛ nati yeɛ yetie ye ɔ.
