EDWEIN BƆ ƆMAA YENYA GYIDIE KYƐ YEHOWA KƆBƆ YƐNWO WAEN
<<<P>>>
4 Sɛ ɛnea Edwein 91 nanu a, edwirɛ titi-re bɔ ɔtwe adwene sie so yeɛ ole kyɛ, ye-nya gyidie kyɛ Yehowa kɔbɔ yɛnwo waen. Yɛnea ye ngyekyɛmuɛ 1 ne 2 ne a, ɔmaa yɛte edwirɛ nabo ye. (Kenga Edwein 91:1, 2.) Bɔ ɔmaa yɛkã sɔ yeɛ ole kyɛ, Nya-meɛ bɔ ɔte anwuro anwuro hene ne, ole tumi paa. Yeti sɛ yɛbɔ yɛnwo adwaa wɔ ye ndaa bo a, yele gyidie kyɛ, bɔ okosi bia-la ɔkɔbɔ yɛnwo waen. (Rutu 2:12; Edw. 17:8) Edwirɛ “abandenden” bɔ tworɔn-zɛm ne kã yenwo dwirɛ ne, ɔmaa yɛkae tete nguro bɔ ne bato wɔɔl bafa yenwo ne. Sɛ ne ebie te sɔ kuro nanu a, ne ɔnzu-ro kyɛ pɔfoɛ bie kɔto kɔhyɛ ye so. Yeɛ sɛ ne ebie pingye berɛ koso a, ne ɔkora nwati ba kuro nanu babɔ yenwo adwaa. Edweintolɛniɛ ne yɛle mvandoho biemɔ wɔ ngyekyɛmuɛ 3 bɔ ɔkɔ ne, na ɔmaa yɛpɛ yenwo foto wɔ y’adwene nu kyɛ, Ye-howa kɔbɔ ye mmenia nwo waen kofi ɔhaw biala bɔ ɔkɔto bɛ nanu.—Edw. 91:3-13.

4. Edwirɛ bɔ ɔwɔ Edwein 91:1, 2 ne, sɛɛ yeɛ ɔmaa yenwu kyɛ Yehowa kɔbɔ yɛnwo waen ɔ?
<<<P>>>
5 Edwein 91:14-16 ne de, nna edwein-tolɛniɛ ne, yeɛ ne okyire kyɛbɔ Yeho-wa kɔbɔ yɛnwo waen ɔ. Na mmom Ye-howa bɔbɔ deinn yeɛ ɔhyɛ ye bɔ kyɛ ɔkɔbɔ yɛnwo waen ɔ. (Kenga Edwein 91:14-16.) Wɔ de tie kyɛbɔ ɔhane ye ɔ: ‘Ngɔpepɛ ngole me mmenia, ngogyina bɛ si, na ngɔmaa bekonwu me ngoale-lɛ.’ Nzuati ɔhane sɔ dwirɛ ne? Ofikyɛ yɛfa yɛnwo yɛto ye so, yɛse ye dumaa, na yɛbɔ ye mbaeɛ. Yɛse kyɛ Yehowa de ɔmbɛ ngondombo, yeti sɛ yenwa ɔkɔbɔ yɛnwo waen a, ɔtte anomdwa.—Num. 23:19; Yos. 23:14.

6 Yede nzuati yeɛ Yehowa maa bɛhwo-rɔle edwirɛ bɔ ɔwɔ Edwein 91 ne ɔ? Ofi-kyɛ ɔmbena kyɛ yesuro, na mmom ɔpena kyɛ yenya gyidie kyɛ ɔkɔbɔ yɛnwo waen. (Rom. 15:4) Yeti edwirɛ bɔ edweinto-lɛniɛ ne hahyirele Yehowa ne, yɛ koso yɛkɔhora yɛkɔha bie kyɛ: ‘Yehowa, ɛte m’afealeɛ ne m’abandenden.’ (Edw. 91:2) Yede sɛɛ yeɛ Yehowa kɔbɔ yɛnwo waen ɔ?
