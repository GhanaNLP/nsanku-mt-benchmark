18 Asɔfodie nwo nikyee bɔ yasua ne, nzu yeɛ wamaa yanwu ye yafa Yehowa nwo ɔ? Yaanwu ye kyɛ, Yehowa dɔ yɛ, na ɔte yɛ bo paa. Afei onwu anwumvoin, na ɔdwene yɛ nwo. Ɔpena kyɛ yɛfa ye adamvoa. Ɛhe amaa nu ala berɛ kyɛ Yehowa de, ɔtte kyɛ Abɔnzam. Ye de, adwene bɔ ɔpena kyɛ yenya alaa yeɛ ole kyɛ, yɛ bɔne ti, Yehowa fa yɛ yɛ hwee a ɔnyɛ yé. (Nye. 12:10) Yehowa de wayɛ bie wama yɛ pa! Wayɛ nhyehyɛɛ bɔ ɔmaa yɛkora yepingye ye. Sɛ ɛnea subaen pa bɔ ole ye ne a, aso ɛhene nga wɔ maa embingye ye anaa? Na enné nni kyɛ ye dɔ nati, ɔkɔfa wɔ bɔne kɔhyɛ wɔ?
<<<P>>>
18. Bɔ yasua ne, nzu yeɛ wamaa anwu ye afa Yehowa nwo ɔ, na sɛɛ yeɛ ɛhe ka wɔ ɔ?
<<<P>>>
19 Sɛ odwu mmerɛ bie a, eyɛ a ne y’adwene yɛ yɛ kyɛ bɔne bie bɔ yayɛ nati, bɛfa yɛ bɛyɛ hwee a, ɔnyɛ yé. Nakoso bɔ yasua ne, wamaa yanwu kyɛ, sɛ yenya sɔ adwene ne a, yɛkae kyɛ Yehowa te yɛ bo. Ɔkae kyɛ yɛte mvuture. (Edw. 103:14) Afei Yehowa de, nna yɛ bɔne yeɛ ɔfa ye adwene sie so ɔ, na mmom nningyein pa bɔ yɛkora yɛyɛ ne, yeɛ ɔnea ɔ. Yeɛ mmɔden bɔ yɛbɔ kyɛ yekoli nahorɛ yɛkɔma ye ne, yenye sɔ. Na sɛ y’adwene yɛ yɛ kyɛ yɛ de, yɛngɔhora yɛngɔyɛ nikyee pa biala a, yɛkae kyɛ “Nyameɛ te piri tra yɛ tiboa, na ɔse nikyee biala.”—1 Yoh. 3:20.

20 Adesua he amaa yanwu kyɛ, Yehowa kuro paa kyɛ yekopingye ye. Ɛhene ati yeɛ wayɛ asɔfodie nwo nhyehyɛɛ ne. Sɔ nhyehyɛɛ nati, mmenia bɔ bɔne wɔ bɛnwo ne, bɛkora bɛsõ ye. Yeti edwirɛ bɔ ɔwɔ Hebrifoɛ 4:16 ne, ɛmɔma yɛyere yɛnwo yɛfa yɛyɛ adwuma. Ɛberɛ kã kyɛ: ‘Ɛmɔma yɛfa ahotɔsoɛ yɛbra adom ahennie bia ne nyunu, amaa ɔnate so wanwu yɛ anwumvoin, ná wamaa yanya adom bɔ ɔkɔboka yɛ, wɔ mmerɛ bɔ ɔsɛ nu.’
