Abusuapanyin Nowa sɔa, mmerɛ bɔ ɔne ye abusua fite fili alaka nanu ne, ɔbɔle afɔleɛ. Ɔyɛle sɔ fa lale Yehowa ase kyɛ wale bɛ ɔ. (Gye. 8:20) Abraham koso, osili afɔleɛ bokyea bɔ ne ɔfa bɔ afɔleɛ ma Yehowa ɔ. (Gye. 12:7, 8) Yɛɛ Yob wora, ne ɔbɔ bɔne nwo afɔleɛ ma ye mmaa nemɔ. (Yob 1:5) Sɔ adwuma bɔ ne abusuapanyin nemɔ yɛ nati, ne Yehowa nwu bɛ kyɛ bɛte asɔfo. Yeti ɔhahyirele Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ kyɛ: ‘Asɔfo mɔ [kyire kyɛ abusuapanyinmɔ] bɔ bɛba AWURADE nyunu ne, maa bɛte bɛ nwo, ná AWURADE andete bɛ nu pasapasa.’—Ɛks. 19:22.

7 Sona bɔ Bible ne limoa hane kyɛ ɔte sɔfo yeɛ ole Mɛlkisɛdɛke. (Gye. 14:18) Ye de, nna sɔfo ngome yeɛ ne odi ɔ, na mmom né osa te ɔhene. Siɛ ye, Yehowa hane kyɛ Mɛsaya ne koso, ɔkɔyɛ ɔhene ne sɔfo kyɛ Mɛlkisɛdɛke ala. Nakoso Mɛsaya ne de, ne ɔbayɛ ɔhene a, adwuma bɔ ɔkɔyɛ ne, mmenia konya so mvasoɛ afebɔɔ.—Edw. 110:4; Heb. 7:17.

8 Yehowa fale Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ yɛle ye maen ne, ɔfale ye mmraa mane Mose kyɛ ɔfa ma bɛ. Ne mmraa ne bie fa asɔfodie nwo. Yehowa hane kyɛ, Mose aliema Aarɔn ne ye mmaamɔ yeɛ beli sɔfo ɔ. Yeti ebiala bɔ ne okoli sɔfo ne, né odikyɛ ofi Aarɔn abusua nu. (Ɛks. 40:12-15) Lewifoɛ bɔ baha ne de, ne bɛkɔboka bɛ maa bɛkɔyɛ b’asɔfodwuma ne. Asɔfo nemɔ yeɛ ne bɛbɔ afɔleɛ bɛma Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ ɔ. Mmraa ne sa hane afɔleɛ bɔ bɛkɔbɔ nwo dwirɛ. Nna ɛhene ngome, na mmom atee bɔ bɛkɔfa so bɛkɔbɔ ye, ɔne bɔ yeti yeɛ odikyɛ bɛbɔ ne koso, ne ɔboka so. Sɔ mmraa hemɔ mukoraati maa yenwu kyɛ, bɔ ne Yehowa baayɛ ma sona ne, nna ebie ɛhe.

9 Yesu bɔle asu ne, ɔɔyɛle Sɔfo Panyi. (Heb. 8:1) Afoɛ nza ne kaa si ne, owu mane yɛ, na wamaa Yehowa afa yɛ bɔne ahyɛ yɛ. Ne ɛhene di mu tra ngaka bɔ ne bɛfa bɛbɔ afɔleɛ wɔ Yisrael ne. Ofikyɛ ye deɛ ne de, ɔye bɔne fi berɛ koraa. Siɛ ye, ɔhɔle anwuro dɔ. Ɛnnɛ mukoraati oté yɛ ye sɔfodwuma ne, ofikyɛ ɔpa Nyameɛ kyerɛ ma yɛ. (Heb. 9:24) Yɛkɔ wiase foforɛ nanu a, yɛ Sɔfo Panyi Yesu koli hene wɔ aseɛ he aso. Na mmenia 144,000 yeɛ ɔne bɛ koli hene ɔ. Bɛ wora bɛkɔyɛ sɔfodwuma ne bie. (1 Pet. 1:3, 4; 2:9) Bɛne Yehowa ne Yesu kosiesie aseɛ ne maa ɔkɔyɛ paradise. Afei bɛbɔ betie Yehowa ne, bɛkɔboka bɛ maa bɛkɔyɛ pɛ. Bɛyɛ ɛhemɔ beyie a, kyɛbɔ yɛnye kɔgye ne, ɔtte angorɛ.

10 Kyɛbɔ yanwu ye ne, asɔfodie nwo nhyehyɛɛ ne, waboka Yehowa mmenia fi tete. B’adwuma nati, mmenia pingye Yehowa a, ɔyɛ yé. Afɔleɛ bɔ ne bɛbɔ ne koso, ɔmaa Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ nwuni kyɛ behia ebie bɔ okole bɛ ɔ. Yesu afoɛ apee ahennie nanu koso, sɔ nhyehyɛɛ ne ala yeɛ ɔkɔboka yɛ ɔ. Ɛhene si ne, yenwo ngohia kyɛ yekonya sɔfo bɔ ɔkɔboka yɛ maa yekopingye Yehowa. Nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ sɔ mmerɛ ne de, ne yayɛ pɛ. Sɔ nhyehyɛɛ he bɔ Yehowa ayɛ ama yɛ ne, yesua yenwo nikye a, ɔmaa yenwu kyɛ ɔdɔ yɛ. Atee beni aso?
