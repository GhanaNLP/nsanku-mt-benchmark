10 JW Library, ɔne yɛ nwomaa nemɔ ne, yɛkɔyɛ sɛɛ na yafa yɛyɛ adwuma yé? Ma yɛnea bɔ ɔtole aliemaa bie bɔ bɛfrɛ ye Robert. Ye de Borɔfo ngome yeɛ ɔkã ɔ. Mmerɛ bie ne ɔyɛ asɛnga adwuma ne wɔ habɔ. Yeɛ oyiale baba bie bɔ ɔyɛ adwuma wɔ ship nu, na ne ofi Türkiye. Ne baba ne nde Borɔfo pa, nakoso Robert mane ye Edwırɛ Páa Bɔ Ofi Nyameɛpiri Berɛ! nwomaa ne bie, na ne ɔwɔ Turki dwudwolɛ nu. Na Robert se nwomaa nanu dwirɛ ne, yeti ɔmaa baba ne kengale nekaa bie wɔ ye kuro dwudwolɛ nu. Baba ne nye gyele yenwo. Afei, nningyein bɔ ɔngɔ yé wɔ wiase he anu ne, obisale yenwo dwirɛ. Ɔwale sɔ ne, ndɛen nala yeɛ Robert hɔle JW Library so ɔ. Yeɛ obukyele nekaa bɔ baye ye nzɛmmisa ne mukoraati noa wɔ ye kuro dwudwolɛ nu, na ɔfa hyirele ye. Afei baba ne nwa: “Ei! ɛte me kuro dwudwolɛ ne anaa?” Robert hane kyɛ: “Ɔwale sɔ ne, yɛbɔle ngɔmmɔ kɔyɛ dɔnhwere kõ. Afei ne yenye aagye Bible nanwo. Onwuni kyɛ ɔkɔhora kɔfa nu dwirɛ kɔbɔ ye bra.” Wɔde nea kyɛ aboka ebie maa wate Bible nanu dwirɛ wɔ ye kuro dwudwolɛ nu ɔ. Ɔngɔyɛ wɔ fɛ anaa?

12. Wiase Foforo Nkyerɛase Bible ne, sɛɛ yeɛ waboka Yehowa mmenia ɔ?
<<<P>>>
Youa Nyane Anyelasoɛ
<<<P>>>
Bɛfrɛ maame bie Youa, na dwudwolɛ bie bɔ bɛfrɛ ye Hmong ngome yeɛ ɔkã ɔ. Otu fili ye kuro ɔɔtenane refugee kamp bɔ ɔwɔ United States ne. Ne nningyein ngɔ yé ma ye koraa. Ye ahɔ a, ɔnde Borɔfo, yeɛ ohũ koso mma ye atee maa ɔngenga nwomaa biala; Bible po de booboo. Maame ne hane kyɛ: “Anigye paa de, na nné bie.” Nakoso ɛlɛho bie, taluwa bie bɔ ne ɔkã Borɔfo mane ye trakt. Edwırɛ bɔ ne ogua so yeɛ ole, Life in a Peaceful New World, na ne ɔwɔ Hmong dwudwolɛ nu. Asetena pa bɔ Yehowa afa hyɛ ye bɔ ne, ne ɔttele yenwo dwirɛ lle. Yeɛ ne ɔnze kyɛ okonya yenwo dwirɛ koso wɔ sɔ krataa kaamba nanu. Oonwuni ye ne, yenye gyele paa. Afei owuni kyɛ kyẽabie asetena kɔyɛ fɛ. Wamma ohũ annwu krataa ne lle. Ɔfa feale ye afoɛ pee. Yeti daa ne ɔfea yenwo kenga trakt ne. Ɔwɔ nu alaa yeɛ ɔwa brasua ɔɔyɛle Yehowa Danzeniɛ ɔ. Yeɛ ɛlɛho bie de, ɔfale ye maame hɔle adesua bɔ bɛyɛ ye wɔ Hmong dwudwolɛ nu. Siɛ ye, bɛmaa ɔne ebie suane Bible ne. Krataa bɔ ye sa hane ye wɔ ye kuro dwudwolɛ nu ne, ɛhene yeɛ ɔbokale ye maa oonwuni Yehowa ɔ. Kesaalae ɔne aliemaamɔ le anyelasoɛ kõ, na bɛ bonu bɛsõ Yehowa.
