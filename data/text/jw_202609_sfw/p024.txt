13 Asɔfodie ne maa yenwu kyɛ Yehowa pena kyɛ yɛka y’ahone nu dwirɛ yekyire ye. (Kenga Hebrifoɛ 4:16.) Yɛ Sɔfo Panyi Yesu adaworoma, yɛkora yɛbɔ Yehowa mbaeɛ mmerɛ biala bɔ yekuro ɔ, na yɛkã yɛ dwirɛ mukoraati yekyire ye. Yɛyɛ sɔ a, okyire kyɛ yedé yedi kyɛ ɔkɔboka yɛ. Yehowa koso onwu ye sɔ a, ne yenye agye. Nakoso odwu mmerɛ bie a, adwenedwene kora hyɛ yɛ so. Yeti yɛbɔ Yehowa mbaeɛ a, kyɛbɔ yesi yɛte nga ne, ɛyɛ a ne yennwu kyɛbɔ yehyire nu ɔ. (Rom. 8:26) Nakoso ɔba sɔ a, Yehowa se bɔ ɔwɔ y’ahone nu ɔ. Yeti ɔte yebo, na ɔkɔboka yɛ. Ɛhe maa yenwu kyɛ Yehowa dwene yɛnwo paa. Yeti kyɛbɔ yɛ haw ne te biala, ɛmɔma yɛbɔ mbaeɛ.

14 Asɔfodie ne maa yenwu kyɛ Yehowa pena kyɛ yenya daa ngoa. (Hes. 33:11) Tete ne, Yehowa mmenia fale ngaka bɔle afɔleɛ. Nakoso wangora wanye bɛ bɔne wamvi berɛ koraa. (Heb. 7:19) Mmom ɔmaa benwuni kyɛ behia afɔleɛ bɔ odi mu tra ɛhene, na yeɛ ole Yesu deɛ ne. Yesu wue nati, Yehowa fa yɛ bɔne kyɛ yɛ koraa. Afei wamaa yanya anyelasoɛ kyɛ yekonya daa ngoa. Ɛhe maa yenwu kyɛ Yehowa kuro yɛ dwirɛ. Ofikyɛ sɛ wamma Yesu anwu a, ahaa yelle anyelasoɛ biala.—1 Yoh. 4:10.
<<<P>>>
15 Mmenia bɔ Yehowa aye bɛ kyɛ bɛne Yesu li sɔfo ne, ɔmaa yenwu kyɛ ɔdɔ yɛ paa. Kyɛbɔ Yesu sone Yehowa tonn fa hɔle ewue nu ne, sɔ ala yeɛ odikyɛ bɛ koso bɛyɛ ɔ. (Flp. 2:8; Nye. 2:10) Bɛmma nikyefee ndwe bɛ mvi Yehowa nwo. Ɛhene ati, yedé yedi paa kyɛ adwuma bɔ bɛkwaayɛ ye wɔ anwuro dɔ ne, bɛkɔyɛ ye kama. Bɛkɔboka yɛ maa ngakangaka yɛkɔyɛ pɛ. Na kyɛ dee bɔ afoɛ apee ne hɔ ayieleɛ ne, né bɔne oo, ewue oo, anwonyerɛ oo, ye mu afi berɛ. (Nye. 22:1, 2) Ɛhe amukoraati, sɛ nna Yesu wue ne ahɔ a, ahaa ɔngɔyɛ yé.
<<<P>>>
16 Bɔ yɛfa nu ne, yɛ sɔfo panyi Yesu te yebo paa. Nzuati ɔ? Ofikyɛ ɔwale aseɛ he aso ne, onwuni kyɛ sãa yɛfɛ. Na ye bɔbɔ deinn po, ebie fale yenye so. (Kenga Hebrifoɛ 4:15.) Odwuli mmerɛ bie ne, ne wafɛ sɔa ala tam. (Yoh. 4:6) Yeti wɔ koso ɛfɛ a, ɔse kyɛbɔ osi te ɔ. Yɛkã ye, ebiaa ɛhɔen kora ku wɔ alaa maa ennwu bɔ ɛyɛ wɔnwo ɔ. Na nzue mbaen po, ennya bie na ano. Sɛ sɔ a kae kyɛ, Yesu se kyɛbɔ ɛhɔen ne nzuhɔen yɛ yea ɔ, ofikyɛ ye koso ebie huni ye. (Mat. 4:2; Yoh. 19:28) Ebiaa wɔ koso, ɔnate kyɛ ɛsõ Yehowa nati, mmenia tane wɔnye. Yesu koso bɛfale bie belili ye, yeti ɔte bɔ ɛfa nu nabo. (Yes. 50:6; Yoh. 15:20) Anaa yɛkã ye, w’abusuaniɛ anaa wɔ damvo bie yeɛ wawu ɔ. Yesu koso ebie tole ye. Ye baba Yosef bɔ ɔtetele ye, ɔne ye damvo Lasarɔso wuli. Yeti ɔse kyɛbɔ ewue yɛ yea ɔ. Ɛhe maa yenwu kyɛ, ɔhaw biala bɔ yɛfa nu ne, yɛ sɔfo panyi Yesu se kyɛbɔ osi te ɔ, na ɔkɔboka yɛ.
