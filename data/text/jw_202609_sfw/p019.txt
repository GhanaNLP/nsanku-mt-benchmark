16 Bible ne kã kyɛ, ɔngɔhyɛ Yehowa ne “wiase amukoraa ahemvo mɔ” kɔfa kosie yenye. (Nye. 16:14) Sɔ mmerɛ ne, ebiala bɔ ɔse Yehowa dumaa na otie ye ne, ɔkɔbɔ yenwo waen. (Edw. 9:10; kenga Yohane 17:3) Nakoso bɛ bɔ bɛnze ye dumaa, na bɛnyɛ bɔ okuro ne, ɔkeka yenwo a, ɛberɛ yeɛ bekonwu kyɛ Yehowa si ne, ebiala nne berɛ kō. Yehowa bɔbɔ ahã kyɛ: ‘Ngɔla me piri ne me krongronyɛlɛ ali. Na ngɔye me nwo ali ngɔma maen maen pee bekonwu me, na afei bekonwu kyɛ me ole Yehowa.’ (Hes. 38:23, NWT) Ɛmɔma yɛhã, sɔ mmerɛ ne de, yɛnye kɔgye paa! Ofikyɛ yɛse kyɛ Yehowa te yɛ damvo.—Mal. 3:16.

17 Yɛnye agye paa kyɛ yɛboka maa Sakaria 8:23 ne, ɔba nu ɔ. Nna aliemaamɔ ngome yeɛ yɛne bɛ bonu yɛ asɛnga adwuma ne; abɔfo nemɔ boka ye. Yehowa nate bɛ so okyire yɛ atee maa yɛkã nzɛmba ne. Afei Yehowa adaworoma, yele nwomaa ne vidio pee. Yeti yɛkã nzɛmba ne yekyire maen maen ne mmusua kue ne edwudwolɛ amukoraa. Ɛmɔma yɛfa nwomaa ne vidio ne yɛboka mmenia, na bɛfa Yehowa adamvoa. Yɛboka bɛ maa benwu ye kyɛ Yehowa nyeye mmenia nu, ole aboterɛ, na onwu anwumvoin. Kodwu kyɛ Yehowa dumaa nanwo kɔte ne, ɛmɔma yɛyere yɛnwo yɛhã nzɛmba ne yehyire ebiala.—Luka 11:2.
