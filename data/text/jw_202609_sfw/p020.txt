Ɔdɔ Ho Nhwɛso
<<<P>>>
BƆ ADESŨA NE KƆHÃ YENWO DWIRƐ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
Asɔfodie nwo nhyehyɛɛ bɔ Yehowa ayɛ ne, yekonwu bɔ yeti yeɛ ɔmaa yenwu kyɛ ɔdɔ yɛ, na ɔpena kyɛ yepingye ye ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Sɛɛ Yeɛ Asɔfodie Ne Maa Yenwu Kyɛ Yehowa Dɔ Yɛ Ɔ?
<<<P>>>
“Bɛye ɔsɔfo panyi paa biala befi mmenia nu maa ogyina bɛ gya nu.”—HEB. 5:1.
<<<P>>>
AA, YƐ dwirɛ yɛ anwumvo. Nzuati yeɛ yɛka sɔ ɔ? Ofikyɛ Adam ne Hawa ali y’aborɛ. Bafa bɔne bawo yɛ, na ɛhene ati yɛne Nyameɛ afia atete koraa. (Ɛfe. 2:12; Kol. 1:21) Wayɛ kyɛ kumaa kandinga bie ala yɛne ye afia. Yeti yɛnwa yepingye ye a, ɔnyɛ yé. Nyameɛ koso de, sɛ yɛne ye afia tete a, yede na yawu. Ofikyɛ ye yeɛ okyire yɛ kyɛbɔ yɛbɔ yɛ bra ɔ, na wamaa yɛnye agye. Ye ala yeɛ osa ma yɛ anyelasoɛ ɔ. Nakoso ɔnate kyɛ yɛne ye afia atete nati, ɛhe amukoraati abɔ yɛ. Yeti sona biala bɔ bɛkɔwo ye bɛkɔto aseɛ he aso ne, sana bɛboka ye kora na wapingye Nyameɛ. Yede nwa yeɛ ɔkɔboka yɛ ɔ? Ne yɛ dwirɛ yɛ Yehowa yea, yeti ye bɔbɔ deinn ɔyɛle nikyeebie fa bokale yɛ. Yede nzu ɛne?
<<<P>>>
2 Bɔ Yehowa yɛ fa bokale yɛ ne, yeɛ ole kyɛ, ɔmaa ebiemɔ lili sɔfo. B’adwuma yeɛ ole kyɛ, bɛkɔboka yɛ maa yekopingye ye. Yeti ɔwɔ nu kyɛ bɔne wɔ yɛnwo de, nakoso sɔ nhyehyɛɛ nati, yepingye ye a, ɔyɛ yé. Yisraelfoɛ nemɔ aberɛ so ne, né sɔ nhyehyɛɛ ne wɔ berɛ, na ɔtoale nu tonn oodwuli tete Kristofoɛ nemɔ aberɛ so. Ɛnnɛ ne ɛnnɛ mukoraati, asɔfodie nwo nhyehyɛɛ ne té boka yɛ. Adesua he anu ne, yɛkɔnea kyɛbɔ sɔ nhyehyɛɛ ne bokale tetefoɛ nemɔ, ɔne kyɛbɔ kyeabie koso ɔkɔboka yɛ ɔ. Ɛhene si ne, yekonwu bɔ yeti yeɛ asɔfodie nwo nhyehyɛɛ bɔ Yehowa ayɛ ne, ɔmaa yenwu kyɛ ɔdɔ yɛ ɔ.

SƆFO NEMƆ ADWUMA NE, SƐƐ YEƐ ƆBOKALE YISRAELFOƐ NEMƆ Ɔ?
<<<P>>>
3 Bɔ yeti yeɛ Yehowa maa ebiemɔ lili sɔfo wɔ Yisrael ne, ɔmaa somafoɛ Pɔɔlo hyirele nu. (Kenga Hebrifoɛ 5:1.)
<<<P>>>
1. Nzuati yeɛ sona biala hia mmokalɛ ɔ?
2. Nzu yeɛ yekosusu yenwo wɔ adesua he anu ɔ?
3. Bɛbɔ ne bekuro kyɛ bɛsõ Yehowa ne, sɛɛ yeɛ ne sɔfo nemɔ boka bɛ ɔ? (Hebrifoɛ 5:1) (Nea foto ne koso.)
