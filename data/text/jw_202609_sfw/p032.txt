<<<P>>>
“Émɔma Kristo Asomdwee Ne, Oli Ɛmɔ Bra So”
<<<P>>>
Kolosefoɛ 3:15 kã “Kristo asomdwee” nwo dwirɛ. Yede sɛ bɛkã “Kristo asomdwee” a, okyire sɛɛ? Sɛ ebie bayɛ Yesu suaniɛ, na ode tonu kyɛ Yehowa ne ɔwa ne kuro ye dwirɛ a, ɔmaa ye ahone tɔ ye kunu fɔnwoo. Ɛhene yɛɛ bɛfrɛ ye “Kristo asomdwee” ne.—Yoh. 14:23, 27.
<<<P>>>
Pɔɔlo hahyirele Kolosefoɛ ne kyɛ bɛma sɔ asomdwee ne, oli bɛ bra so. Bɔ ɔhane ne, né ɔpena kyire sɛɛ? Greek nu ne, bɛkã kyɛ ebie di nikyeebie so a, betaa bɛfa bɛgyina berɛ bɛma ebie bɔ odi refirii wɔ bɔɔl nu ɔ. Sɛ ebiemɔ bɔ bɔɔl a, refirii ne yɛɛ okyire kyɛbɔ angorɛ ne kosi kɔhɔ ɔ. Yɛ koso, sɔ ala yɛɛ odikyɛ yɛmaa “Kristo asomdwee” ne, okyire yɛ kyɛbɔ yɛbɔ yɛ bra ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Wɔde ma yɛnea ɛhe. Yɛfa ye kyɛ edwirɛ bie asi ɛne aliemaa bie afia. Ɛkɔyɛ sɛɛ ne amaa “Kristo asomdwee” ne, wali “refirii” anaa wa-hyire wɔ bɔ eyɛ ɔ? Yɛɛ ole kyɛ engɔyɛ bɔ wɔ bɔbɔ ekuro ɔ, na mmom ɛkɔyɛ nikyeebie bɔ okyire kyɛ ekuro asomdwee. Sɛ ɛkɔhora kɔyɛ sɔ a, sana ɛye w’adwene fi yealɛ bɔ edi naso, na ɛfa w’adwene sie asomdwee bɔ ɔkɔtena ɛmɔ afia naso. Sɔ bɔ ɛkɔyɛ ne kyire kyɛ, ɛma “Kristo asomdwee” ne, odi wɔ bra so.—Kol. 3:12-14.
