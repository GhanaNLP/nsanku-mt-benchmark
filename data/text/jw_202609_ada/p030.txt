15 Ke o yɔse kaa je ɔ mumi ɔ ná o nɔ he wami ngɛ blɔ ko nɔ ɔ, lɛɛ moo pee si fɔfɔɛ ɔ he nó ko oya nɔuu. Mo susu Gloria* níhi a si kpa-mi ɔ he nɛ o hyɛ. E kase anɔkuale ɔ benɛ e ji jokuɛ, se je ɔ mumi ɔ ná e nɔ he wami, nɛ e nu he kaa e sa nɛ e he biɛ, nɛ e ná sika fuu. E de ke: “I suɔ nɛ ma ná ní fuu. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, i bɔni asafo mi kpehi a mi pomi, nɛ bɔɔbɔɔbɔɔ ɔ, i sume nɛ maa pee Odasefono hu.” Se pee se ɔ, Gloria ba yɔse kaa nihi nɛ a sɔmɔ we Ye-howa nɛ e kɛ ngɛ bɔe ɔ he je nɛ e suɔ nɛ e ná hɛ mi nyami kɛ sika fuu ɔ nɛ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, mɛni e pee? E de ke: “I kɛ be fuu bɔni Baiblo ɔ ka-semi ngɛ ye dɛ he, nɛ i sɔleɔ wawɛɛ hulɔ. Je-hanɛ se hu ɔ, i kɛ nihi nɛ a sɔmɔ we Yehowa a kpa bɔmi. E ngɛ mi kaa i be sika fuu mohu lɛɛ, se i ngɛ bua jɔmi wawɛɛ, ejakaa Yehowa ha mi ní kpakpahi fuu ngɛ ye si himi mi, nɛ i ba pee mumi mi niatsɛ. Ye bua jɔ wawɛɛ kaa Yehowa ji nɔ nɛ ngɛ mi kudɔe ngɛ ye si himi mi, se pi Satan je yaya nɛ ɔ.”
