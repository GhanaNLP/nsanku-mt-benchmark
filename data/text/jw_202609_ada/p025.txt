18 Níhi tsuo nɛ wa ba susu he kɛ kɔ osɔfo yemi blɔ nya tomi ɔ he ɔ tsɔɔ wɔ kaa Yehowa suɔ wɔ wawɛɛ, e nuɔ bɔ nɛ wa nuɔ he ha a sisi, nɛ e kɛ wa tɔmihi keɔ wɔ, nɛ e yeɔ buaa wɔ hulɔ. Satan poɔ wa nya be fɛɛ be, se Yehowa pee we jã. (Kpo. 12:10) Wa ba na ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi kaa Yehowa pee níhi fuu kɛ ha wɔ konɛ wa nyɛ nɛ waa hɛ kɛ su e he, nɛ wa ná nɔ mi mami kaa wa ma nyɛ maa pee e suɔmi nya ní, né waa kɛ lɛ nɛ pee huɛ. Anɛ enɛ ɔ ha we nɛ o hɛ kɛ su Yehowa he wawɛɛ lo? Anɛ e ha we nɛ o ná nɔ mi mami kaa Yehowa suɔ mo, nɛ e kɛ o tɔmi maa ke mo lo?

19 Be komɛ ɔ, wa ma nyɛ maa nu he kaa wa he be se nami loo Yehowa be nyɛe ma je suɔmi kpo kɛ tsɔɔ wɔ ngɛ yayami ko nɛ wa pee kɛ be ɔ he. Se ní kasemi nɛ ɔ ha nɛ wa na kaa Yehowa nu wɔ sisi, nɛ e le bɔ nɛ wa nuɔ he ha, ejakaa e le kaa wa yi mluku. E le kaa zu kɛkɛ ji wɔ. (La 103:14) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, Yehowa kɛ e juɛmi mɛ yayami nɛ wa peeɔ nɔ, mohu ɔ, e kɛ e juɛmi maa ní kpakpahi nɛ wa peeɔ, nó nɛ wa suɔ nɛ wa maa pee, kɛ bɔ nɛ wa maa pee wa ní ha hwɔɔ se ɔ nɔ. Ke e peeɔ wɔ kaa se nami ko be wa he, nɛ wa ngɔɔ wa juɛmi kɛ maa yayami nɛ wa pee ɔ pɛ nɔ ɔ, lɛɛ e sa nɛ wa kai kaa Yehowa “kle pe wa tsui ɔ, nɛ e le níhi tsuo.”—1 Yoh. 3:20.

20 Kaa bɔ nɛ wa ba na ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, Yehowa to osɔfo yemi ɔ he blɔ nya konɛ wa nyɛ nɛ wa sɔmɔ lɛ ngɛ blɔ nɛ da nɔ. Yehowa suɔ wawɛɛ nɛ waa hɛ kɛ su e he. Bɔ nɛ pee nɛ Yehowa sɔmɔli nɛ a yi mluku ɔ a nyɛ nɛ a hane gɔta ngua a nɛ ba po a kɛ Yehowa a kpɛti ɔ, Yehowa ha mɛ osɔfohi nɛ a ngɛ kaa bliiji, nɛ a maa gu nɔ kɛ ya Yehowa ngɔ konɛ huɛ bɔmi kpakpa nɛ hi a kɛ lɛ a kpɛti. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ waa ngɔ wa ní peepee kɛ tsɔɔ kaa wa bua jɔ nike ní nɛ ɔ he, nɛ waa pee kaa bɔ nɛ Hebrbi 4:16 fã wɔ ke waa pee ɔ. E de ke: “Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ waa kɛ munyu nɛ a tuɔ faa nɛ hɛ wɔ kɛ su dloomi matsɛ sɛ ɔ he, bɔ nɛ pee nɛ wa nine nɛ su mɔbɔ nami nɔ konɛ wa ná dloomi nɛ maa ye bua wɔ ngɛ be nɛ sa mi.”
