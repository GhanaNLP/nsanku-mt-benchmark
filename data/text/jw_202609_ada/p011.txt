Ke amlɔ nɛ ɔ, wa yaa nɔ nɛ wa fiɛɛɔ sane kpa-kpa a ngɛ bɔ nɛ nihi maa na wɔ ha a tsuo se ɔ, lɔ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa wa ngɛ wa he dlae kɛ ha si temi kɛ womi nɛ waa kɛ maa kpe hwɔɔ se ɔ.

10. Kɛ Matsɛ Yehoshafat nu he kɛɛ benɛ mahi etɛ pee kake kɛ ba tua Yuda a?
<<<P>>>
20:1-3) Se Matsɛ Yehoshafat ha we nɛ lɔ ɔ nɛ wo e kɔni mi nyu, mohu ɔ, e pee si fɔfɔɛ ɔ he nɔ ko. Nyɛ ha nɛ waa hyɛ blɔ nɔ nɛ Yehoshafat gu kɛ bu Yehowa tue, nɛ e ngɔ e hɛ kɛ fɔ e nɔ ɔ, kɛ blɔ nɔ nɛ wa maa gu kɛ kase e nɔ hyɛmi nɔ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
11 Moo fia o pɛɛ si kaa o ma hla Yehowa se blɔ, nɛ o bu lɛ tue. E ngɛ mi kaa Yehoshafat ye gbeye mohu lɛɛ, se loloolo ɔ, “e fia e pɛɛ si kaa e ma hla Yehowa se blɔ.” (2 Kron. 20:3) Se loko Yehoshafat kɛ si fɔfɔɛ nɛ ɔ maa kpe ɔ, e hlaa Yehowa se blɔ nɛ e biɔ lɛ blɔ tsɔɔmihi si abɔ. (2 Kron. 19:3) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, benɛ tabuli nɛ ɔmɛ ba tua Yuda a, Yehoshafat le momo kaa e maa hia Yehowa yemi kɛ buami. Yehoshafat fia e pɛɛ si kaa e ma bi Yehowa blɔ tsɔɔmi, nɛ e bu nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ nɛ e ma de lɛ ɔ tue. Lɔ ɔ se ɔ, Ye-hoshafat sɔle kɛ ha Yehowa, nɛ e kpa lɛ pɛɛ kaa e ye bua lɛ. (Kane 2 Kronika 20:5, 6, 10-12.) Ke wa hao ɔ, Yehoshafat nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ nɛ ɔ maa ye bua wɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ waa fia wa pɛɛ si kaa wa maa bu Yehowa tue, nɛ wa hla e blɔ tsɔɔmi se blɔ kɛ gu sɔlemi kɛ Baiblo ɔ nɛ wa maa kase ɔ nɔ, nɛ wa ná hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi kaa e maa ye bua wɔ.—La 119:112.
