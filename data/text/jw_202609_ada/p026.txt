KASEMI Ɔ MI
Wa maa hyɛ blɔ nɔ nɛ wa maa gu kɛ yu wa he kɛ je níhi etɛ nɛ a ngɛ nigii nɛ a ma nyɛ ma ná wa nɔ he wami ngɛ je ɔ mi ɔ he.
<<<P>>>
Hyɛ Nɛ Hi Nɛ “Je ɔ Mumi ɔ” Nɛ Ko Ná O Nɔ He Wami
<<<P>>>
“Nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ nɛ ngɛ je ɔ mi ɔ . . . pi Tsɛ ɔ ngɔ nɛ e je.”
—1 YOH. 2:16.
<<<P>>>
SATAN ji nɔ nɛ ngɛ je ɔ nɔ yee mwɔnɛ ɔ. (Yoh. 14:30; 1 Yoh. 5:19) Baiblo ɔ de ke Satan mi mi fu Kristo se nyɛɛli ɔmɛ wawɛɛ. (Kpo. 12:12, 13, 17) Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ we kaa Satan ngɛ he fɛɛ he, nɛ e tuaa wɔ be fɛɛ be hulɔ. Ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ, Baiblo ɔ tsɔɔ we kaa bɔfohi ngɛ jamɛ a he blɔ ɔ kaa a maa hi he fɛɛ he ngɛ be fɛɛ be mi, bɔfo yayahi jio, bɔfo kpakpahi jio. Se Satan ngɛ bɔfo yiwutsotsɛmɛ fuu nɛ a piɛɛɔ e he kɛ tsuɔ ní. (Kpo. 12:9) Se nó ko ngɛ nɛ Satan kɛ tsuɔ ní be fɛɛ be, nɛ jamɛ a nó ɔ ngɛ he fɛɛ he, lɔ ɔ ji, je ɔ mumi ɔ nɛ. Mumi nɛ ɔ ná nihi fuu a nɔ he wami ngɛ je ɔ mi, a le jio, a li jio. (Kane 1 Yohane 2:15, 16.) Ke nihi he we yi kaa Satan ngɛ po ɔ, a peeɔ e suɔmi nya ní kɛ gu níhi nɛ e bua jɔ he nɛ a peeɔ ɔ nɔ. Ngɛ blɔ ko nɔ ɔ, “e nu mɛ hɛ ngmengmle konɛ a pee e suɔmi nya ní.”—2 Tim. 2:26.
<<<P>>>
2 Be ko nɛ be ɔ, wa kpɛti nihi fuu pee níhi nɛ nihi nɛ a ngɛ je ɔ mi ɔ peeɔ mwɔnɛ ɔ. Se be nɛ wa ba le nó nɛ je ɔ mumi ɔ ji, kɛ nɔ nɛ hɔɔ si ngɛ se ɔ, lɔ ɔ ye bua wɔ nɛ waa kɛ Baiblo ɔ ga womi nɛ nyɛɛ se nɛ ɔ tsu ní. Lɔ ɔ ji kaa, “wa kua ní peepeehi nɛ sɛ Mawu hɛ mi kɛ je mi akɔnɔhi.” (Tito 2:11, 12) E ngɛ mi kaa Satan ngɛ he wami wawɛɛ, nɛ e náa nihi fuu a nɔ he wami ngɛ je ɔ mi mohu lɛɛ, se wɔɔ lɛɛ wa kuaa ní peepeehi nɛ sɛ Mawu hɛ mi ɔ.
