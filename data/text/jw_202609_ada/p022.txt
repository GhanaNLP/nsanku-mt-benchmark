weku ɔ. Akɛnɛ a sãã afɔle nɛ ɔmɛ konɛ a kɛ sa Yehowa hɛ mi he je ɔ, Yehowa hu bua jɔɔ he, nɛ be fɛɛ be ɔ, e suɔ nɛ e kɛ adesahi nɛ a yeɔ lɛ anɔkuale ɔ nɛ pee huɛ. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, benɛ Noa kple si kɛ je daka a mi ɔ, e sã afɔlehi kɛ na Yehowa si. (1 Mose 8:20) Abraham hu ma afɔle sami latɛhi konɛ e sã afɔlehi ngɛ a nɔ kɛ ha Yehowa. (1 Mose 12:7, 8) Hiob hu sã sami afɔlehi kɛ ha e bimɛ ɔmɛ. (Hiob 1:5) Enɛ ɔ haa nɛ wa naa nɔ́ he je nɛ Baiblo ɔ tsɛ nyumuhi komɛ kaa osɔfohi loko Yehowa ba hla osɔfohi nɛ a ji Levibi ɔ kɛ ha Israelbi ɔmɛ po. Yehowa de Israelbi ɔmɛ ke: “Mo ha nɛ osɔfo ɔmɛ [weku-yi ɔmɛ] nɛ a pɔɔ Yehowa he sumi ɔ nɛ a tsu a he, konɛ Yehowa ko fia mɛ nɔ́.”—2 Mose 19:22.

7 Kekleekle be nɛ Baiblo ɔ ngɔ munyungu nɛ ji “osɔfo” ɔ kɛ tsu ní ji benɛ e tu Melkizedek he munyu ɔ. (1 Mose 14:18) Pi osɔfo pɛ ji lɛ, mohu ɔ, e ji matsɛ hulɔ. Pee se ɔ, Yehowa wo si kaa Mesia a ma ba pee matsɛ kɛ osɔfo kaa Melkizedek. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, e wo si kaa adesahi ma ná ní kpakpahi tsuo nɛ Mesia a ma ba pee ɔ he se kɛ ya neneene.—La 110:4; Heb. 7:17.
<<<P>>>
8 Pee se ɔ, Yehowa to Israel ma a sisi, nɛ e ngɔ Mose Mlaa a kɛ ha mɛ. Ngɛ Mlaa nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, Yehowa tsɔɔ bɔ nɛ osɔfo yemi blɔ nya tomi ɔ ma tsu ní ha. Mose nyɛminyumu Aaron kɛ e sisi bimɛ ɔmɛ lɛ a ma sɔmɔ kaa osɔfohi. (2 Mose 40:12-15) Osɔfo ɔmɛ ji nihi nɛ a maa sã afɔlehi kɛ ha Israelbi ɔmɛ. Osɔfo nɔkɔtɔma a titli lɛ maa sã afɔlehi kɛ ha Israel ma a tsuo, nɛ osɔfo kpa amɛ maa sã afɔlehi kɛ ha ni kakaakahi nɛ a ngɛ Israel ɔ. Levibi ɔmɛ maa ye bua mɛ ngɛ blɔ slɔɔtohi a nɔ. Yehowa tsɔɔ afɔlehi nɛ e sa nɛ a sã, nɔ́ he je nɛ e sa kaa a sã, kɛ blɔ nɔ nɛ a maa gu kɛ sã jamɛ a afɔle ɔmɛ. Blɔ nya tomi nɛ ɔ ji ní kpakpahi nɛ maa ba hwɔɔ se ɔ a he nini kɛkɛ.

9 Benɛ a baptisi Yesu ɔ, e ba pee Osɔfo Nɔkɔtɔma, nɛ e ngɛ sɔmɔe ngɛ Yehowa mumi mi sɔlemi we ngua a mi. (Heb. 8:1) Jeha etɛ kɛ fã se ɔ, Yesu ngɔ e wami nɛ ye mluku ɔ kɛ sã afɔle. Afɔle nɛ e ngɔ e wami kɛ sã a hi kulaa pe afɔlehi nɛ osɔfo ɔmɛ kɛ lohwehi sã ngɛ Israel ɔ. Afɔlehi nɛ a kɛ lohwehi sã a nyɛ we nɛ e je yayami kɛ je kulaa, se Yesu kpɔmi afɔle sami ɔ lɛɛ, e nyɛ nɛ e pee jã. Kɛ ba si mwɔnɛ ɔ, Yesu ji wa we Osɔfo Nɔkɔtɔma, nɛ e ngɛ wɔ ye buae konɛ waa hɛ kɛ su Yehowa he. (Heb. 9:24) Ngɛ Mawu Matsɛ Yemi ɔ mi ɔ, nihi 144,000 ɔ nɛ a pɔ mɛ nu ɔ maa piɛɛ Yesu, nɛ ji Osɔfo Nɔkɔtɔma a he kɛ sɔmɔ kaa osɔfohi, nɛ a maa piɛɛ e he kɛ ye matsɛ hulɔ. (1 Pet. 1:3, 4; 2:9) Lɔ ɔ se ɔ, Yehowa kɛ Yesu kɛ nihi 144,000 ɔ maa ye bua nihi tsuo nɛ a suɔ nɛ a bu lɛ Yehowa tue ɔ konɛ a ye mluku. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, a maa pee zugba a tsuo paradeiso. Waa kɛ bua jɔmi ngɛ be nɛ ɔ blɔ hyɛe wawɛɛ!

10 Kaa bɔ nɛ wa ba na a, Yehowa ha adesahi osɔfohi kɛ je blema tɔɔ konɛ a ye bua nɛ huɛ bɔmi kpakpa nɛ hi adesahi kɛ Yehowa a kpɛti. Afɔlehi fuu nɛ osɔfo ɔmɛ sã a kaiɔ Israelbi nɛ a yeɔ anɔkuale ɔ kaa e he hia nɛ a ná kpɔmi nɔ́ ko nɛ pe kulaa. Ngɛ Jeha Akpe Nɔ Yemi ɔ mi ɔ, Yesu kɛ nihi 144,000 ɔ ma sɔmɔ kaa osɔfohi, nɛ a maa ye nɔ kaa matsɛmɛ kɛ ya si be nɛ a ma ha nɛ adesahi nɛ a ye mluku. Ke adesahi ye mluku ɔ, osɔfo yemi he blɔ nya tomi ɔ he be hiae hu. Se kɛ blɔ nya tomi nɛ ɔ he níhi fuu nɛ wa maa kase ɔ maa ye bua wɔ nɛ waa hɛ kɛ su Yehowa he wawɛɛ ha kɛɛ? Nyɛ ha nɛ waa hyɛ.
