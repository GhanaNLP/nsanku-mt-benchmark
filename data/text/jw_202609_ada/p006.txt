Ke wa nuɔ he jã a, Satan ma nyɛ maa da lɔ ɔmɛ a nɔ kɛ ha nɛ wa kɔni mi nɛ jɔ̃, nɛ waa nu he kaa wa be nyɛe ma sɔmɔ Yehowa hu. (Mat. 4:3; 6:13) Se Yehowa asafo ɔ haa nɛ wa nine suɔ mumi mi niye níhi a nɔ, nɛ ní ɔmɛ né wa kaseɔ ɔ kaiɔ wɔ kaa Yehowa kɛ tɔmihi keɔ, nɛ wa he jua wa kɛ ha lɛ, nɛ jehanɛ se hu ɔ, a haa nɛ wa náa nɔ mi mami kaa Yehowa ma ha wɔ wa hiami níhi. (La 86:5; Mat. 10:29-31; Luka 12:28; Heb. 13:5) Anɛ o kai be ko nɛ o kane nɔ́ ko ngɛ wa womi ɔmɛ a mi, né e ta o tsui he wawɛɛ ɔ lo? Eko ɔ, lɔ ɔ ha nɛ o de ke, ‘Enɛ ɔ lɛɛ imi pɛ lɛ a ngma kɛ ha.’ Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa Yehowa guɔ e we asafo ɔ nɔ kɛ haa nɛ wa nine suɔ blɔ tsɔɔmihi nɛ wa hia a nɔ ngɛ be nya, konɛ wa kɛ po huɛ bɔmi nɛ ngɛ waa kɛ lɛ wa kpɛti ɔ he piɛ.

16 Yehowa poɔ e we bi a he piɛ kaa kuu kɛ jeɔ a he nyɛli ɔmɛ a dɛ mi konɛ a ko kpata a hɛ mi. Se be komɛ ɔ, Yehowa ngmɛɔ blɔ nɛ nihi waa e sɔmɔli komɛ yi mi, nɛ a gbeɔ ekomɛ po konɛ e kɛ ha nɛ Satan nɛ pee lakpatsɛ. (Kpo. 2:10; 6:9) Se Yehowa sɔmɔli bɔɔ pɛ nɛ a ma gbo ɔ pi ha Satan. Mohu ɔ, e ná nɛ Yehowa sɔmɔli tsuo nɛ a yeɔ lɛ anɔkuale ɔ gbo. (Mat. 24:9; Kpo. 12:9-12) Se Yehowa be blɔ ngmɛe kɔkɔɔkɔ nɛ nɔ́ ko kaa jã nɛ ba! Wa ma nyɛ ma ná nɔ mi mami kaa Yehowa maa ya nɔ maa gu e bɔfo ɔmɛ a nɔ kɛ po e we bi a he piɛ, bɔ nɛ pee nɛ a ya nɔ nɛ a sɔmɔ lɛ, nɛ a fiɛɛ sane kpakpa a.—Heb. 1:7, 14.

17. Mɛni he je nɛ e sɛ nɛ waa hyɛ blɔ kaa Yehowa maa po wa he piɛ kɛ je nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ nɛ ma nyɛ maa ye wɔ awi mwɔnɛ ɔ he ɔ?
<<<P>>>
he piɛ. (Abɛ 14:29, 30; 22:3; 2 Kor. 7:1) Se kɛ̃ ɔ, wa le kaa pi níhi tsuo nɛ maa ye wɔ awi nɛ waa kɛ maa kpe ɔ nɛ Yehowa maa po wa he piɛ kɛ je mi. Satan tsɔɔ kaa wa sɔmɔɔ Yehowa akɛnɛ e poɔ wa he piɛ, nɛ nɔ́ ko nɔ́ ko yi wɔ awi ɔ he je. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, ke Yehowa poɔ wa he piɛ ngɛ oslaahi tsuo a he ɔ, nihi ma he Satan lakpa munyu nɛ ɔ maa ye. (Hiob 1:10, 11) Se ke a ngɛ wɔ yi mi wae po nɛ wa ya nɔ nɛ wa ye Yehowa anɔkuale ɔ, lɔ ɔ haa nɛ Satan peeɔ lakpatsɛ. Jehanɛ se hu ɔ, e haa nɛ nihi naa kaa wa ngɛ Yehowa sɔmɔe akɛnɛ wa suɔ lɛ, né lɛ ji anɔkuale Mawu ɔ he je. (Rom. 9:22) E be kɛe kulaa nɛ Yehowa maa po nɔ́ nami se, nɛ e sɔmɔli be nɔ́ nae hu. Se loko jamɛ a be ɔ maa su ɔ, Yehowa maa ya nɔ maa ye bua wɔ nɛ waa da ka fɛɛ ka nɛ waa kɛ maa kpe ɔ nya, konɛ wa kɛ po huɛ bɔmi kpakpa nɛ ngɛ waa kɛ lɛ wa kpɛti ɔ he piɛ.—1 Kor. 10:13; 1 Pet. 5:10.

MƐNI BLƆ NƆ YEHOWA MAA GU KƐ PO E WE BI A HE PIƐ HWƆƆ SE?

18 Ngɛ amanehlu ngua a mi ɔ, Yehowa maa ya nɔ maa po e we bi a he piɛ ngɛ mumi mi. Se ke e ba lɛ ngɛ he lo nya a hu nɛɛ? Ke ma nɔ yeli ɔmɛ pee kake kɛ ba tua wɔ ɔ, Yehowa be blɔ ngmɛe nɛ a kpata e we bi tsuo a hɛ mi. (Eze. 38:1, 2, 10-16) Enɛ ɔ ma ha nɛ Harmagedon ta a maa je sisi. Ke Harmagedon ta a je sisi ɔ, Yehowa maa tsɔ Yesu Kristo kɛ e hiɔwe tabo ɔ konɛ a po Yehowa we bi a he piɛ, né a kpata Mawu he nyɛli ɔmɛ tsuo a hɛ mi. (Kpo. 17:12-14; 19:11, 14, 15) Mɛni blɔ nɔ Yehowa maa gu kɛ po e we bi a he piɛ ngɛ Harmagedon ta a mi?
