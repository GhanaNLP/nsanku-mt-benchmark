Israelbi ɔmɛ bu Yehowa tue, e ngɛ mi kaa eko ɔ, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ Egiptbi ɔmɛ a mi mi ma fu (Hyɛ kuku 3)
<<<P>>>
ha mɛ. Se a be lɛ nae gbɔjɔɔ kaa a maa bu fami nɛ ɔ tue. Yehowa de mɛ kaa a gbe jijɔ loo apletsi nɛ a ngɔ e muɔ ɔ kɛ kpa a sinya tso ɔmɛ a he. (2 Mose 12:5, 7) Yehowa maa be ngɛ we fɛɛ we nɛ a ngɔ muɔ kɛ kadi lɛ ɔ he, nɛ e be bitɛte ɔmɛ kɛ lohwe ɔmɛ a kekleekle bi ɔmɛ a hɛ mi kpatae. Se Israelbi ɔmɛ ko nyɛ ye gbeye, nɛ a ko bui fami nɛ ɔ tue. Mɛni he je? Ejakaa Egiptbi ɔmɛ jaa jijɔ eku kaa a mawu, lɔ ɔ he ɔ, eko ɔ, Israelbi ɔmɛ ma susu kaa ke Egiptbi ɔmɛ na nɛ a ngɛ jijɔ ɔmɛ gbee ɔ, a mi mi ma fu wawɛɛ nɛ a maa gbe mɛ. (2 Mose 8:25-27) Se kɛ̃ ɔ, Israelbi ɔmɛ na kaa e he hia nɛ a bu Mawu tue, enɛ ɔ he ɔ, a “pee kaa bɔ nɛ Yehowa fã” mɛ ɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ. (2 Mose 12:28) Enɛ ɔ ha nɛ Yehowa hu jɔɔ mɛ, nɛ e gbi a bitɛte ɔmɛ.

4 Israelbi ɔmɛ je Egipt jamɛ a nyɔ ɔ mi nɔuu. Sisije ɔ, Yehowa nyɛɛ a hɛ mi kɛ gu Wo Tsu ɔ yiti je. Se Yehowa kɛ blɔ tsɔɔmi kpa ha mɛ. E de mɛ kaa a kpale a se nɛ a ya to si ngɛ Wo Tsu ɔ he. Ngɛ adesa susumi nya a, nile be blɔ tsɔɔmi nɛ ɔ mi. Ejakaa Farao kɛ e tabuli ɔmɛ ma ngɛ a se, nɛ Wo Tsu ɔ hu ngɛ a hɛ mi. He ko he ko be nɛ a maa gu. Se si kake ekohu ɔ, Israelbi ɔmɛ bu nɔ́ nɛ Yehowa de mɛ kɛ gu Mose nɔ ɔ tue. (2 Mose 14:1-4) Se benɛ Egiptbi ɔmɛ ngɛ a he sue ɔ, Israelbi ɔmɛ “ye gbeye” wawɛɛ, nɛ a de Mose po kaa e ngɔ mɛ kɛ ba hiɔ konɛ a ba gbo! (2 Mose 14:5-11) Mɛni he je nɛ Israelbi ɔmɛ pia a he kaa a bu Mawu tue ɔ? Nɛ kɛ Yehowa na nɔ́ nɛ a pee ɔ ha kɛɛ?
<<<P>>>
5 Baiblo ɔ de ke ngɛ Wo Tsu ɔ nya a, Israelbi ɔmɛ a “bua jɔɛ [Yehowa] nyakpɛ ní tsumi ɔmɛ a he” ngɛ Egipt, nɛ a “kaii we” e suɔmi nɛ se pui ɔ hulɔ. (Kane La 106:7 kɛ sisi ningma.) Israelbi ɔmɛ na bɔ nɛ Yehowa po a he piɛ, nɛ e he a kekleekle binyumu ɔmɛ kɛ a lohwe ɔmɛ a yi wami ngɛ haomi nyɔngmane ɔ mi ha. Se kɛ̃ ɔ, a hɛ je suɔmi agbo nɛ Yehowa je kpo kɛ tsɔɔ mɛ ɔ nɔ, nɛ a ná we hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi ngɛ e mi kaa e ma kpɔ mɛ ekohu. Akɛnɛ Yehowa le nɔ́ nɛ ngɛ a tsui mi he je ɔ, e de ke a ‘tsɔ atuã ngɛ Wo Tsu ɔ nya.’ E ngɛ mi kaa Israelbi ɔmɛ nyɛɛ Mose se kɛ be ngɛ Wo Tsu ɔ mi mohu lɛɛ, se a ya nɔ nɛ a gbo tue. Enɛ ɔ ha nɛ Yehowa kua jamɛ a yi nɔ bi ɔmɛ tsuo, nɛ e ha we nɛ a ya Si Womi Zugba a nɔ. (La 106:21, 24-26) Mɛni wa ma nyɛ maa kase ngɛ a sane ɔ mi?
