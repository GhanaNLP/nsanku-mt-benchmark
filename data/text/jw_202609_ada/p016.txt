7 E ngɛ mi kaa Mawu we bi tuɔ gbi slɔɔtoslɔɔtohi mohu lɛɛ, se a peeɔ kake kɛ fiɛɛɔ kɛ haa nihi ngɛ he fɛɛ he. Bɔfo ɔmɛ hu yeɔ buaa mɛ kɛ tsuɔ ní tsumi nɛ ɔ. (Kane Kpojemi 14:6.) Ke wa na kaa gbami komɛ kaa nɔ́ nɛ a gba ngɛ Zakaria kɛ Kpojemi womi ɔmɛ a mi ɔ ngɛ mi bae ɔ, lɔ ɔ haa nɛ wa naa kaa gbami kpahi nɛ a bɛ mi lolo ɔ maa ba mi niinɛ. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, e haa nɛ wa fiaa wa pɛɛ si kaa wa maa ye Yehowa asafo ɔ anɔkuale, ejakaa wa na heii kaa e guɔ e we asafo ɔ nɔ kɛ yeɔ buaa nihi konɛ a nu sane kpakpa a ngɛ je ɔ mi tsuo.

YEHOWA NGƐ NYAKPƐ NÍHI PEE MWƆNƐ Ɔ
<<<P>>>
8 Ngɛ Pentekoste ligbi ɔ nɔ ngɛ jeha 33 ɔ mi ɔ, Yehowa pee nɔ́ ko nɛ ngɛ nyakpɛ wawɛɛ. E ha nɛ Yesu kaseli ɔmɛ nɛ a ngɛ Yerusalem ɔ bɔni gbi slɔɔtoslɔɔtohi tumi. (Níts. 2:4-10) Mɛni he je nɛ e ha nɛ a tu gbi slɔɔtohi ɔ? Konɛ e kɛ ha nɛ a tu “Mawu ní nguangua amɛ” a he munyu kɛ tsɔɔ ni slɔɔtoslɔɔtohi nɛ a ba Yerusalem konɛ a ba ja lɛ ngɛ sɔlemi we ɔ. Yehowa suɔ nɛ nimli nɛ ɔmɛ tsuo nɛ a nu sane kpakpa a ngɛ mɛ nitsɛmɛ a je gbi mi. (Níts. 2:11) Mwɔnɛ ɔ, kɛ gu wa womi ɔmɛ a nɔ ɔ, wa nyɛɔ nɛ wa fiɛɛɔ kɛ haa nihi nɛ a tuɔ gbi slɔɔtoslɔɔtohi ɔ.
