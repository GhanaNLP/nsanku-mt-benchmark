9 Yesu le kaa kɛ je blema tɔɔ nɛ Yehowa guɔ bɔfohi a nɔ kɛ poɔ e we bi a he piɛ konɛ a he nyɛli ɔmɛ nɛ a ko ye mɛ awi. (2 Ma. 6:15-17; 19:34, 35; Dan. 3:19, 20, 26-28; 6:16-22) Se Yesu le kaa e pee nɔ́ ko nɛ juɛmi be mi, nɛ e bi Yehowa konɛ e po e he piɛ ɔ, lɔ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa e ngɛ Yehowa kae. Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa La 91 ɔ se nɛ Satan tsɛ kɛ tsɔɔ kaa Yehowa maa po Yesu he piɛ ngɛ he lo nya a da, se e kɛ ngmami ɔ tsu ní ngɛ blɔ nɛ dɛ nɔ konɛ e kɛ ha nɛ Yesu nɛ ka Yehowa.* Heto nɛ Yesu ha Satan ɔ ha nɛ wa ba nu sisi kaa La 91 ɔ maa nɔ mi kaa Yehowa maa po e we bi a he piɛ ngɛ mumi mi, kɛ he lo nya tsuo, nɛ jehanɛ hu ɔ, e ma ha nɛ a ma ná tue mi jɔmi.
<<<P>>>
10 Si nɛ Yehowa wo ngɛ La 91 ɔ yeɔ buaa wɔ nɛ wa náa hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi ngɛ e mi. Yehowa ngɛ kaa wesa he kɛ he piɛ pomi he. Lɔ ɔ he ɔ, wa ngɛ nɔ mi mami nɛ mi wa kaa e maa po nihi tsuo nɛ a ngɛ hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi ngɛ e mi ɔ a he piɛ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e sɛ nɛ waa ye níhi nɛ e be kɛe nɛ a maa ba nɛ a ma hoso je ɔ gbeye kulaa. Amlɔ nɛ ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ wa susu blɔ nɔ nɛ Yehowa guɔ kɛ poɔ wa he piɛ mwɔnɛ ɔ, kɛ blɔ nɔ nɛ e maa gu kɛ po wa he piɛ hwɔɔ se ɔ he.
<<<P>>>
* Be ko nɛ be ɔ, wa de ngɛ wa womi ɔmɛ a mi kaa La 91 ɔ kɔɔ si nɛ Yehowa wo kaa e maa po wa he piɛ ngɛ mumi mi konɛ huɛ bɔmi kpakpa nɛ hi waa kɛ lɛ wa kpɛti ɔ pe he. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, wa de kaa Satan tsɛ ngmami nɛ ɔ se ngɛ blɔ nɛ dɛ nɔ kaa Yehowa maa po wa he piɛ kɛ je oslaahi a mi. Hyɛ December 1, 1974 Blɛfo gbi Hwɔɔmi Mɔ ɔ, bf. 722.

11 Mwɔnɛ ɔ, Yehowa tsɔɔ wɔ nɔ́ nɛ e sa nɛ waa pee konɛ waa po huɛ bɔmi nɛ ngɛ waa kɛ lɛ wa kpɛti ɔ he piɛ. E le kaa yayami peeli ji wɔ, nɛ e he waa kɛ haa wɔ kaa wa maa suɔ níhi nɛ e suɔ, nɛ́ waa hiɔ níhi nɛ e hiɔ ɔ. (Rom. 6:16; 7:21-23) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e ha wɔ ní komɛ nɛ maa ye bua wɔ konɛ wa nyɛ nɛ waa bu lɛ tue nɛ waa ya nɔ nɛ waa pee e huɛ. Nyɛ ha nɛ wa susu ní nɛ ɔmɛ ekomɛ a he nɛ waa hyɛ.
<<<P>>>
12 E Munyu nɛ ji Baiblo ɔ. Yehowa Munyu ɔ bɔɔ wɔ kɔkɔ ngɛ ní yayahi a he, nɛ e tsɔɔ wɔ nɔ́ nɛ e sa nɛ waa pee bɔ nɛ pee nɛ wa nyɛ nɛ waa hwu kɛ si ní yayahi hulɔ. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, mo kadi munyu nɛ Yesu tu ngɛ Mateo 5:28, 29 ɔ. (Kane.) Ngɛ ngmami nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, Yesu tu nyumu ko “nɛ e yaa nɔ nɛ e hyɛɔ yo nɛ e he peeɔ lɛ akɔnɔ ɔ” he munyu. Yesu tsɔɔ kaa nyumu kaa jã a ‘kɛ yo ɔ pue gba momo ngɛ e tsui mi.’ Mɛni wa kaseɔ ngɛ sane nɛ ɔ mi?

13 Ke wa yaa nɔ nɛ wa hyɛɔ fonihi nɛ tleɔ bɔmi nami he akɔnɔ si ɔ, e ma ha nɛ wa ma ná akɔnɔ yaya ngɛ wa tsui mi, nɛ ke akɔnɔ nɛ ɔ mi ba wa, nɛ pee se wa ná he blɔ ɔ, eko ɔ, wa ma ya bɔ ajuama. (Yak. 1:14, 15) Ke wa suɔ nɛ waa yu wa he kɛ je akɔnɔ yaya he ɔ, lɛɛ e sa nɛ waa kɛ Yesu ga womi ɔ nɛ tsu ní oya nɔuu. E sa nɛ waa na fonihi nɛ tleɔ bɔmi nami he akɔnɔ si ɔ kaa Kansa. Ke nɔ ko ná Kansa a, ja a pee lɛ operation loko a ma nyɛ ma je Kansa a kɛ je e he. Baiblo he ní lelɔ ko tsɔɔ kaa Yesu ga womi nɛ e kɛ ha ngɛ kuku 29 ɔ ji blɔ nɔ nɛ wa maa gu kɛ je Kansa a kɛ je wa he. Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa e sɛ nɛ waa hyɛ fonihi nɛ tleɔ bɔmi nami he akɔnɔ si ɔ kulaa.
