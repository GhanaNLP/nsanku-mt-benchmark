Asafo mi nikɔtɔmahi bɔɔ mɔde kaa a kɛ blɔ tsɔɔmi nɛ Yehowa kɛ e we asafo ɔ kɛ ha a ma tsu ní, nɛ a buɔ a sibi a ga womi tue
(Hyɛ kuku 16)
<<<P>>>
12 Moo ya nɔ nɛ o kase Baiblo ɔ. Níhi nɛ Yehoshafat de ngɛ e sɔlemi ɔ mi ɔ tsɔɔ heii kaa e le níhi nɛ Yehowa pee blema a. E le kaa Yehowa ye si nɛ e wo Abraham kaa e maa ngɔ zugba nɛ e nɔ ngɛ fɛu ɔ kɛ ha e nina nɛ ji Israelbi ɔmɛ ɔ. (1 Mose 12:7) Munyuhi nɛ Yehoshafat tu benɛ e ngɛ sɔlee ɔ ngɛ kaa sɔlemi nɛ Salomo sɔle benɛ e ngɛ sɔlemi we ɔ nɔ jɔɔe ɔ. (2 Kron. 6:28-30; 20:5-9) Níhi nɛ Yehoshafat le kɛ kɔ Yehowa he ɔ ye bua lɛ nɛ e ngɔ e hɛ kɛ fɔ Yehowa nɔ kulaa, nɛ e fia e pɛɛ si kaa e maa ya nɔ maa bu lɛ tue. Jã nɔuu kɛ̃ nɛ ke wa ya nɔ nɛ wa kase Yehowa he ní, kɛ ní kpahi nɛ e de ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi ɔ, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ wa maa fia wa pɛɛ si kaa wa maa bu lɛ tue ngɛ si fɔfɔɛhi nɛ a mi wa mi po.—Mat. 13:52.
<<<P>>>
13 Moo ba o he si. Sɔlemi nɛ Yehoshafat sɔle ɔ hu ha nɛ wa na kaa e baa e he si. Yehoshafat ji matsɛ, nɛ e ngɛ tabuli fuu hulɔ, se e ngɔɛ e hɛ kɛ fɔɛ matsɛ nɛ e ji, kɛ tabuli nɛ e ngɛ ɔ nɔ, mohu ɔ, e ngɔ e hɛ kɛ fɔ Yehowa nɔ. Benɛ e sɔle ngɛ ni ɔmɛ a hɛ mi ɔ, e je he si bami mi nɛ e kplɛɛ nɔ kaa Yudabi ɔmɛ hia Mawu yemi kɛ buami. (2 Kron. 20:12) Akɛnɛ matsɛ ɔ baa e he si he je ɔ, e yi ha lɛ kulaa kaa e maa kplɛɛ Mawu blɔ tsɔɔmi nɔ. Ke wɔ hu wa baa wa he si ɔ, e maa ye bua wɔ konɛ waa ya nɔ nɛ waa bu Yehowa tue kɛ je wa tsui mi be fɛɛ be.—Flp. 2:5, 8.
<<<P>>>
14 Mo susu bɔ nɛ o ní peepee ma nyɛ maa sa ni kpahi a he ha a he. Yehowa ha sɔlemi nɛ Yehoshafat sɔle ɔ heto. E gu gbalɔ Yahaziel nɔ kɛ de ni ɔmɛ kaa e he be hiae nɛ a hwu ta a. Mohu ɔ, e de mɛ ke: “Nyɛɛ da nyɛ blɔ he, nyɛɛ da si kpɛii, né nyɛɛ hyɛ bɔ nɛ Yehowa ma he nyɛ yi wami ha.” Baiblo ɔ de ke, oya nɔuu nɛ Yehoshafat “kplã si nɛ e kɛ e hɛ mi bu zugba.” Enɛ ɔ ha nɛ ni ɔmɛ hu ‘kplã si nɛ a ja Yehowa.’ E nɔ jena benɛ Yudabi ɔmɛ je kpo konɛ a kɛ a he nyɛli ɔmɛ nɛ ya kpe ɔ, Yehoshafat wo mɛ he wami kaa a “ná hemi kɛ yemi ngɛ Yehowa” kɛ “e gbali ɔmɛ a mi.”—2 Kron. 20:14-20.
<<<P>>>
15 Yehoshafat ná hemi kɛ yemi nɛ mi wa ngɛ Yehowa mi kaa e maa ye si nɛ e wo ɔ nɔ, nɛ e maa hwu kɛ ha e we bi. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, e wo ni ɔmɛ he wami konɛ a ngɔ a hɛ kɛ fɔ Yehowa nɔ, nɛ a bu lɛ tue. Benɛ Yehoshafat kɛ “ni ɔmɛ ya ajla” se ɔ, “e hla nyumuhi konɛ a nyɛɛ tabuli ɔmɛ a hɛ mi” kɛ la kɛ je Yehowa yi. (2 Kron. 20:21) Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ bɔ nɛ hemi kɛ yemi nɛ a ngɛ ngɛ Yehowa mi ɔ mi wa ha.
