BAIBLO MI MUNYU KO
<<<P>>>
“Nyɛ Ha Nɛ Kristo ɔ Tue Mi Jɔmi ɔ Nɛ Ye Tsɛ Ngɛ Nyɛ Tsui Mi”
<<<P>>>
Kolosebi 3:15 ɔ tu “Kristo ɔ tue mi jɔmi ɔ” he munyu. Mɛni ji “Kristo ɔ tue mi jɔmi ɔ”? Lɔ ɔ ji tue mi jɔmi nɛ nɔ ko náa akɛnɛ e ba pee Kristo kaselɔ ɔ he je. Wa náa tue mi jɔmi nɛ ɔ, ejakaa wa le kaa Yehowa kɛ e Bi Yesu suɔ wɔ wawɛɛ nitsɛ.—Yoh. 14:23, 27.
<<<P>>>
Bɔfo Paulo wo Kristofohi nɛ a ngɛ Kolose asafo ɔ mi ɔ he wami kaa a ha nɛ tue mi jɔmi nɛ ɔ nɛ ye tsɛ ngɛ a tsui mi, loo ngɛ nɔ tutuu-tu nɛ a ji ɔ mi. Ngɛ mɛni blɔ nɔ? Hela munyu nɛ Paulo kɛ tsu ní ngɛ ngmami nɛ ɔ mi kɛ “ye tsɛ” ɔ kɛ referee, nɛ ji nɔ ko nɛ e kudɔɔ kpɔ mi gbɔlemi ko aloo e hyɛɛ konɛ a kɛ kpɔ mi gbɔ-lemi ko he mlaa nɛ tsu ní ngɛ tsakpa. Enɛ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa, kaa bɔ nɛ jamɛ a nɔ ɔ ji nɔ nɛ mwɔɔ yi mi kpɔhi ngɛ kpɔ mi gbɔlemi ɔ mi ɔ, jã lɛ e sa nɛ “Kristo ɔ tue mi jɔmi ɔ” hu nɛ kudɔ bɔ nɛ e sa kaa waa ba wa je mi ha.
<<<P>>>
Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, ke waa kɛ nyɛmi ko sã nya, aloo waa kɛ lɛ kpa we gbi ngɛ nɔ́ ko he ɔ, e sa nɛ wa ha nɛ “Kristo ɔ tue mi jɔmi ɔ” nɛ kudɔ wɔ kaa referee. E maa ye bua wɔ konɛ waa le nɔ́ nɛ e sa nɛ waa pee kɛ baa tue mi jɔmi yi. Se loko wa ma nyɛ maa pee jã a, e ma bi nɛ waa ngɔ ní komɛ nɛ wa suɔ ɔ kɛ sã afɔle, nɛ waa ngɔ ni kpahi a tɔmihi kɛ ke mɛ faa. Ke wa pee jã a, lɔ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa wa ha nɛ “Kristo ɔ tue mi jɔmi ɔ” ngɛ tsɛ yee ngɛ wa tsui mi.—Kol. 3:12-14.
