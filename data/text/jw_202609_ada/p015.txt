‘Ngɛ jamɛ a be ɔ mi ɔ, nyumuhi nyɔngma nɛ a je je ma amɛ tsuo a mi nɛ a tuɔ gbi slɔɔtohi tsuo ɔ maa pɛtɛ Yudano kake tade he . . . nɛ a ma de ke: “Wa suɔ nɛ waa kɛ nyɛ nɛ ya, eja-kaa wa nu kaa Mawu kɛ nyɛ ngɛ.”’” (Zak. 8:23) Ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ, gbami nɛ ɔ ngɛ mi bae ngɛ wa be nɛ ɔ mi. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, wa maa na nó nɛ wa kpɛti nɔ fɛɛ nɔ maa pee kɛ fiɛɛ sane kpakpa a kɛ ha nihi nɛ a tuɔ gbi slɔɔtohi tsuo ɔ.

YEHOWA WE BI NGƐ FIƐƐE HA NIHI NGƐ GBIHI FUU A MI
<<<P>>>
3 “Yudano ɔ” nɛ Zakaria tu e he munyu ngɛ e gbami ɔ mi ɔ daa si kɛ ha Kristofohi nɛ Mawu ngɔ mumi klɔuklɔu ɔ kɛ pɔ mɛ nu ɔ. Baiblo ɔ tsɛɔ mɛ ke “Mawu Israel” hulɔ. (Gal. 6:16) Mɛ ji nihi nɛ a “ná hiɔwe tsɛmi ɔ,” nɛ tsɔɔ kaa a maa piɛɛ Yesu he kɛ ye matsɛ ngɛ hiɔwe. (Heb. 3:1) “Nyumuhi nyɔngma a” daa si kɛ ha nihi nɛ a baa a he si nɛ a ngɛ hɛnɔka-mi kaa a maa hi si kɛ ya neneene ngɛ Paradei-so ɔ mi ngɛ zugba a nɔ ɔ. Nimli nɛ ɔmɛ je ma slɔɔtoslɔɔtohi a nɔ, nɛ a tuɔ gbi slɔɔtoslɔɔ-tohi.
<<<P>>>

Nyɛmimɛ nyumu nɛ ɔmɛ yɔse kaa e he hia nɛ a fiɛɛ ha nihi ngɛ gbihi fuu a mi, nɛ a tsɔɔ wa womi ɔmɛ hu a sisi kɛ ya gbi slɔɔtohi a mi. 4. Benɛ Yesu hla “tsɔlɔ anɔkualetsɛ kɛ ní lelɔ ɔ” ngɛ jeha 1919 ɔ mi se ɔ, mɛni lɛ ya nɔ?
<<<P>>>
kasemi womi nɛ ji The Harp of God, nɛ a pee ngɛ jeha 1921 ɔ mi ɔ sisi kɛ ya gbihi 36 mi. Ngɛ jeha 1935 ɔ mi ɔ, “tsɔlɔ anɔkualetsɛ kɛ ní lelɔ ɔ” ba yɔse nihi nɛ a ji “asafo kuu ngua” a heii, nɛ a ba na hu kaa e ma bi nɛ a fiɛɛ kɛ ha nihi ngɛ jeɔ mi tsuo, ngɛ gbi slɔɔtohi a mi. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, nihi nɛ a pɔ mɛ nu nɛ a hi si ngɛ jamɛ a be ɔ mi ɔ bɔ mɔde wawɛɛ nɛ a to fiɛɛ-mi ní tsumi ɔ he blɔ nya saminya, bɔ nɛ pee nɛ a nyɛ nɛ a fiɛɛ ngɛ je ɔ mi tsuo konɛ Zakaria gbami ɔ nɛ ba mi.

“O WE BI ƆMƐ MAA NGƆ A HE KƐ HA FAA”
<<<P>>>
5 Mawu we bi ya nɔ nɛ a fiɛɛ sane kpakpa a ngɛ je kɛ wɛ, nɛ kaa bɔ nɛ Yesaya gba a, nihi babauu ngɛ je ɔ mi, nɛ a tuɔ gbi slɔɔtoslɔɔto-hi ɔ suɔ kaa a maa kase Yehowa he ní konɛ a ba sɔmɔ lɛ. (Kane Yesaya 2:2-4.) Bɔ nɛ pee nɛ a nyɛ nɛ a ye bua nimli nɛ ɔmɛ nɛ a ba le Yehowa Mawu ɔ, Mawu we bi bɔ mɔde wawɛɛ nɛ a fiɛɛ, a tsɔɔ womihi a sisi kɛ ya gbi slɔɔto-hi a mi, nɛ a gba womi nɛ ɔmɛ kɛ ya hehi nɛ a tuɔ jamɛ a gbi ɔmɛ ngɛ ɔ. (La 68:11; 110:3) Je-hanɛ hu ɔ, nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi fuu bɔ mɔde nɛ a to jw.org wɛbsaiti ɔ kɛ JW Library ɔ he blɔ nya ngɛ gbi slɔɔtohi a mi. Enɛ ɔ nɛ a pee ɔ yeɔ buaa nihi fuu nɛ a ba kaseɔ Baiblo ɔ ngɛ a je gbi mi.
<<<P>>>
6 Nihi nɛ a je ma slɔɔtohi a mi nɛ a tuɔ gbi slɔɔtoslɔɔtohi ɔ ba ná níhi nɛ tsɔlɔ anɔkuale-tsɛ kɛ ní lelɔ ɔ pee ɔ he se wawɛɛ. Blɔ kake nɛ a guɔ nɔ kɛ jeɔ hɛsa kpo ji kaa a haa yemi kɛ buami kɛ fĩɔ je kɛ wɛ fiɛɛmi ní tsumi ɔ se. Yemi kɛ buami nɛ ɔmɛ ha nɛ wa nyɛ nɛ wa pee Baiblo kasemi womihi fuu ngɛ gbihi nɛ hiɛ pe 1,100 a mi. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, daa jeha a, nihi akpe lafahi abɔ ba kaseɔ Baiblo ɔ, nɛ a jɔɔ a he nɔ kɛ haa Yehowa, nɛ a baptisiɔ mɛ.
