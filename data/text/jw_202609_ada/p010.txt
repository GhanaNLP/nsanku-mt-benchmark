Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, e sa nɛ wa bi wa he ke, “Anɛ ja i na kaa nɔ́ kpakpa ko maa je tue bumi nɛ ma pee kɛ ha Yehowa a mi kɛ ba loko i buɔ lɛ tue lo?” E ma nyɛ maa ba kaa Israelbi ɔmɛ ekomɛ bu fami kaa a ngɔ muɔ kɛ kpa a sinya tso ɔmɛ a he ɔ tue akɛnɛ a le kaa lɔ ɔ ma he a bitɛte ɔmɛ a yi wami ɔ he je, se pi kaa a ngɛ suɔmi nitsɛnitsɛ kɛ ha Yehowa. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, wa ma nyɛ ma bi wa he ke: “Anɛ i buɔ Yehowa tue ke ni kpahi bui lɛ tue lo?” Benɛ Israelbi ɔmɛ ngɛ Egipt jee ɔ, Yehowa ha nɛ Israelbi ɔmɛ “a hɛ mi ba nyami ngɛ Egiptbi ɔmɛ a hɛ mi,” nɛ a ke mɛ níhi fuu. (2 Mose 12:36) Se pee se benɛ Egiptbi ɔmɛ nyɛɛ a se konɛ a ya gbe mɛ ɔ, Israelbi ɔmɛ kɛ a hɛ fɔ we Yehowa nɔ hu. (2 Mose 14:10-12) Wa sume nɛ waa pee wa ní kaa Israelbi nɛ ɔmɛ, mohu ɔ, wa suɔ nɛ wa ná hemi kɛ yemi nɛ mi wa ngɛ Yehowa kɛ nihi nɛ e guɔ a nɔ nɛ e kɛ blɔ tsɔɔmi haa wɔ ɔ a mi. (Kane Yeremia 23:4.) Ke wa nui blɔ tsɔɔmihi tsuo nɛ Yehowa kɛ haa wɔ ɔ sisi saminya, aloo ke wa bu tue nɛ lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ ni kpahi a mi mi nɛ fu po ɔ, wa bɔɔ mɔde kaa waa kɛ ma tsu ní. Mɛni maa ye bua wɔ nɛ́ waa je wa tsui mi kɛ bu Yehowa tue, konɛ wa ko pee wa ní kaa Israelbi ɔmɛ ɔ?

7 Mo ha nɛ suɔmi nɛ o ngɛ kɛ ha Yehowa a mi nɛ wa. Ke Yehowa asafo ɔ kɛ blɔ tsɔɔmi ko ha wɔ nɛ wa li se nami nɛ maa je mi kɛ ba, aloo blɔ tsɔɔmi ɔ tue nɛ wa maa bu ɔ ma ha nɛ waa kɛ nyagbahi nɛ kpe ɔ, eko ɔ, lɔ ɔ ma ha nɛ e he nɛ wa ha wɔ kaa waa kɛ ma tsu ní. Ke e ba lɛ jã a, e sa nɛ wa kai kaa suɔmi nɛ wa ngɛ kɛ ha Yehowa, kɛ hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi nɛ wa ngɛ ngɛ e mi ɔ ji yi mi tomi titli he je nɛ wa buɔ lɛ tue. (1 Yoh. 5:3) Suɔmi nɛ wa ngɛ kɛ ha Yehowa a maa ye bua wɔ nɛ waa to wa tsui si, nɛ waa kplɛɛ nɔ kaa e ma he be loko wa maa na se nami nɛ maa je tue bumi nɛ wa maa pee kɛ ha Yehowa a mi kɛ ba.—Fiɛɛ. 7:8; Mika 7:7.

8 Ngɛ wa be nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, Mawu sɔmɔli komɛ pee nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ kpakpa kɛ gu blɔ tsɔɔmi tue nɛ a bu be mi nɛ a li se nami nɛ maa je mi kɛ ba oya nɔuu ɔ po. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, benɛ COVID-19 hiɔ ɔ ba a, a kɛ blɔ tsɔɔmi ha asafo ɔmɛ kaa a pee asafo mi kpe ɔmɛ ngɛ videoconference nɔ. Asafo mi nɔkɔtɔma ko nɛ ngɛ Peru ɔ de ke: “Sisije ɔ, i susu kaa blɔ tsɔɔmi nɛ ɔ nɛ a kɛ ha wɔ ɔ be wɔ ye buae ngɛ wa kpɔ ɔ mi.” E ngɛ mi kaa e nui blɔ tsɔɔmi ɔ sisi saminya mohu lɛɛ, se e fia e pɛɛ si kaa e maa bu Mawu tue, nɛ e kɛ e hɛ maa fɔ e nɔ. (Abɛ 3:5, 6) Mɛni lɛ je mi kɛ ba? E de ke: “I na kaa Yehowa jɔɔ tue bumi nɛ wa pee ɔ nɔ. Wa asafo ɔ he ba wa, nɛ kake peemi nɛ ngɛ nyɛmimɛ ɔmɛ a kpɛti ɔ hu mi ba wa wawɛɛ. Mo susu nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ kpa ko hu he nɛ o hyɛ. Ngɛ jeha 2020 ɔ mi ɔ, asafo ɔ pee tsakemi komɛ kɛ ha asafohi kɛ kuuhi nɛ fiɛɛli nɛ a ngɛ mi ɔ tuɔ gbi kpa a. Benɛ nyɛmiyo ko nɛ e ngɛ Japan ɔ ná le kaa e be kɛe nɛ a maa gu kuu nɛ e ngɛ mi ɔ, e de ke: “I suɔ nɛ ma bu tsakemi nɛ a maa pee ɔ tue, nɛ ma ko tu munyu nyanyaanya kɛ si blɔ tsɔɔmi ɔ. Ejakaa i le kaa tsakemi nɛ ɔ maa wo yiblii kpakpa.”
