OTI NƐ NGƐ NÍ KASEMI Ɔ MI
<<<P>>>
Wa maa na bɔ nɛ Yehowa we bi ha nɛ gbami nɛ ngɛ Zakaria 8:23 ɔ ngɛ mi bae ha mwɔnɛ ɔ.
<<<P>>>
Wa Fiɛɛɔ Sane Kpakpa a Kɛ Haa Nihi Nɛ A Ngɛ “Je Ma Amɛ Tsuo A Mi” ɔ
<<<P>>>
“Nyumuhi nyɔngma nɛ a je je ma amɛ tsuo a mi nɛ a tuɔ gbi slɔɔtohi tsuo ɔ maa pɛtɛ Yudano kake tade he.”
—ZAK. 8:23.
<<<P>>>
NGƐ Baiblo ɔ kekleekle womi ɔ mi ɔ, wa na kaa ni komɛ tsɔ atuã kɛ si Yehowa nɛ a to kaa a maa ma mɔ ngua ko kɛ ya su hiɔwe. Se Yehowa sume nɛ a pee nɔ́ ko kaa jã, enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e tsa-ka a gbi ɔ, nɛ e ha nɛ a bɔni gbi slɔɔtoslɔɔtohi tumi. Enɛ ɔ ha nɛ nihi nɛ a tuɔ gbi kake ɔ nu a he nɛ a je he nɛ a ngɛ Babel mɔ ɔ mae ngɛ ɔ, nɛ a gbɛ fia zugba a nɔ tsuo. (1 Mose 11:6-9) Se ngɛ Kpojemi womi ɔ mi ɔ, Yehowa tsɔɔ kaa e maa pee “asa-fo kuu ngua ko . . . nɛ a je je mahi tsuo kɛ wɛtsohi kɛ nihi kɛ gbihi” a mi ɔ kake, nɛ e maa nyɛɛ a hɛ mi kɛ gblee amanehlu ngua a mi kɛ sɛ je ehe ɔ mi. (Kpo. 7:9, 10, 14) Se kɛ nihi nɛ a tuɔ gbi slɔɔtohi ma plɛ kɛ ná hemi kɛ yemi ngɛ Yehowa mi ngɛ amanehlu ngua a mi ha kɛɛ? Ja wa fiɛɛ sane kpakpa a kɛ ha mɛ ngɛ gbi nɛ a nu sisi ɔ mi. (Kane Romabi 10:13, 14.) E ji he blɔ ngua nɛ wa ná kaa wa maa fiɛɛ yi wami hemi sɛ gbi nɛ ɔ!
