don ta a mi ɔ gbeye. Mɛni he je? Ejakaa, Yehowa ji wa wesa he kɛ wa he piɛ pomi he. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, wa ngɛ hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi ngɛ e mi. Wa ma nyɛ ma ná nɔ mi mami ngɛ munyu nɛ nyɛɛ se nɛ ɔ mi: Ke Yesu kɛ e hiɔwe tabo ɔ kpata Mawu he nyɛli ɔmɛ a hɛ mi ngɛ Harmagedon ta a mi ɔ, a kpɛti nɔ kake ngmelu po be piɛe, mɛ tsuo a maa gbo. (Yer. 25:33; Zak. 14:12) Se ke Harmagedon ta a ngɛ nɔ yae ɔ, wa ma nyɛ ma ná nɔ mi mami kaa nɔmlɔ adesa ko aloo daimonio ko be nyɛe ma kpata Mawu we bi a kpɛti nɔ kake ngmelu po hɛ mi hu.—Luka 21:28; 2 Pet. 2:9.

20 Ke Harmagedon ta a ngɛ nɔ yae ɔ, munyu nɛ ɔ nɛ la polɔ ɔ ngma a maa ba mi nitsɛnitsɛ ngɛ wa blɔ fa mi. E de ke: “Nimli akpe maa nɔ ngɛ o kasa nya, nɛ nimli akpe nyɔngma maa nɔ ngɛ o hiɔ nɔ, se moo lɛɛ, ní yayami nɛ ɔmɛ be o he sae. O hɛngmɛ kɛkɛ lɛ o kɛ maa hyɛ, nɛ o maa na yiwutsotsɛmɛ a tue gblami ɔ. Akɛnɛ o de ke: “Yehowa ji ye wesa he” he je ɔ, o kɛ Nɔ Nɛ E Nɔ Kuɔ Pe Kulaa a pee o hi he.” (La 91:7-9) Ke Harmagedon ta a je sisi pɛ ɔ, Yehowa be blɔ ngmɛe nɛ a ye e sɔmɔli ɔmɛ a ti nɔ ko nɔ ko awi, e maa po huɛ bɔmi nɛ ngɛ a kɛ lɛ a kpɛti ɔ kɛ a wami he piɛ, nɛ e ma ha nɛ a ma ná tue mi jɔmi hulɔ. Akɛnɛ o de ke: “Yehowa ji ye wesa he” he je ɔ, Yehowa wo si ke: “Ní yayami nɛ ɔmɛ be o he sae.” Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e sɛ nɛ waa ye nɔ́ nɛ maa ba hwɔɔ se ɔ gbeye. Wa ma nyɛ ma ná nɔ mi mami nɛ mi wa kaa Yehowa maa po wa he piɛ kɛ wa ya nɔ nɛ waa kɛ wa hɛ fɔ e nɔ.

FONI Ɔ MI TSƆƆMI
