MO KUA MUNYUHI NƐ SƐ TUMI
<<<P>>>
6 Baiblo ɔ de kaa ngɛ nyagbe ligbi ɔmɛ a mi ɔ, nihi maa pee a he “fĩali, he nɔ woli,” kɛ “nihi nɛ a hí kɛ somi ko somi.” (2 Tim. 3:1-3) Mwɔnɛ ɔ, nihi je ɔ su yaya nɛ ɔmɛ kpo ngɛ a munyu tutui mi. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, wa nuɔ ngɛ adafi fiami mi kaa nya sami nyafii nɛ yaa nɔ ngɛ ni komɛ a kpɛti ɔ ngɔɔ basabasa peemi ngua kɛ baa. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, nɔ jemi munyuhi pɔ he ngɛ wehi fuu a mi. Jã kɛ̃ nɛ jokuɛwi kɛ nikɔtɔmahi tsuo tsuaa nɔ ní, nɛ a tuɔ yakayaka munyuhi faa. (Efe. 5:3, 4) E ngɛ heii kaa munyuhi nɛ sɛ kaa kikɛ nɛ ɔmɛ jeɔ je ɔ mumi ɔ kpo.
<<<P>>>
7 Akɛnɛ wa ji Kristofohi he je ɔ, ke wa ngɛ nihi sɛ gbi manee aloo waa kɛ mɛ ngɛ munyu tue hɛ mi kɛ hɛ mi ɔ, e sa nɛ waa pee nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ kpakpa ngɛ wa munyu tutui mi. (Yak. 1:26, 27) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, wa ma nyɛ ma bi wa he ke: ‘Kɛ ye munyu tutui ngɛ ha kɛɛ? Anɛ i tuɔ munyuhi nɛ sɛ ke ye mi mi fu, aloo ke i kɛ nihi nɛ a li kaa Odasefono ji mi ɔ ngɛ ní sɛɛe lo? Ke i kɛ nyɛmimɛ ngɛ ní sɛɛe ɔ, anɛ ye munyu tutui tsɔɔ kaa i bui mɛ aloo i kɛ munyuhi nɛ ma ha nɛ a mi mi nɛ fu ɔ peeɔ plasee lo? Anɛ i kɛ ye wekuli tuɔ munyu basabasa lo? Mɛni nɛ ye wekuli ɔmɛ ma de ngɛ bɔ nɛ i tuɔ munyu ha a he?’

8 Mɛni e sa nɛ waa pee ke wa yɔse kaa je ɔ mumi ɔ ngɛ wa munyu tutui nɔ he wami náe? E ma bi nɛ waa pee babauu pe wa munyu tutui pɛ nɛ wa ma tsake. Mo kai kaa Yesu de ke: “Nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ nɛ jeɔ nɔ nya mi kɛ baa a, nɔ tsui mi lɛ e jeɔ, nɛ jamɛ a ní ɔmɛ lɛ bleɔ nɔ.” (Mat. 15:18) Kaa bɔ nɛ Yesu de ɔ, wa munyu tutui tsɔɔ nɔ́ tutuutu nɛ ngɛ wa tsui mi. Enɛ ɔ he je nɛ Baiblo ɔ tu “nɔ jɛmi munyuhi,” abofu, mi mi la kɛ ninyɛ nɛ nya wa he munyu kɛ bla a nɛ. (Efe. 4:31) Ke o yɔse kaa o ngɛ su nɛ ɔmɛ eko ɔ, lɛɛ moo sɔle ha Yehowa nɛ e ye bua mo nɛ o nyɛ nɛ o je su momo nɛ ɔmɛ kɛ fɔ he, nɛ o wo “su ehe ɔ.” (Kane Kolosebi 3:8-10.) Jehanɛ hu ɔ, o ma nyɛ ma hla níhi a mi ngɛ asafo ɔ womi ɔmɛ a mi kɛ kɔ munyuyi nɛ ɔ he. Moo fia o pɛɛ si kaa o be hae nɛ je ɔ mumi ɔ nɛ ná o munyu tutui kɛ o ní peepee nɔ he wami.—Efe. 4:29.

MO KUA JE MI BAMI YAYAHI
<<<P>>>
9 Mwɔnɛ ɔ, je ɔ mumi ɔ ha nɛ nihi fuu kɛ mlaahi nɛ Yehowa wo kɛ kɔ nɔ́ nɛ da, kɛ nɔ́ nɛ dɛ ɔ he ɔ tsu we ní kulaa. Nihi fuu susu kaa tɔmi be hɔ jemi, nyumu kɛ nyumu loo yo kɛ yo a bɔmi nami, kɛ nihi nɛ a sɛ we gba si himi mi nɛ a náa bɔmi ɔ he. (Yes. 5:20) Ke wa hyɛ we nɛ hi ɔ, je ɔ mumi ɔ ma nyɛ ma ha nɛ suɔmi nɛ wa ngɛ kaa wa ma kua nɔ́ yayami ɔ mi ma gbɔjɔ. (2 Kor. 11:3) Eko ɔ, wa ma susu kaa je ɔ susumi ngɛ níhi a he ɔ se be, se mlaahi nɛ ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi ɔ lɛɛ a be be. Se susumi kaa jã a ji lakpa! Níhi nɛ Yehowa deɔ wɔ kaa waa pee ɔ he ngɛ se nami kɛ ha wɔ be fɛɛ be. E blɔ ɔmɛ a nɔ kuɔ kulaa pe adesahi a nɔ́ ɔ, nɛ a be bi gblegbleegble ke be pue mi po.—Yes. 48:17, 18; 55:9.
