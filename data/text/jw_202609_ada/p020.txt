LA 107
Mawu Pee Suɔmi He Nɔ Hyɛmi Nɔ́
<<<P>>>
OTI NƐ NGƐ NÍ KASEMI Ɔ MI
Wa maa na bɔ nɛ osɔfo yemi blɔ nya tomi ɔ yeɔ buaa wɔ nɛ wa hɛɔ kɛ suɔ Yehowa he ha.
<<<P>>>
Bɔ Nɛ Osɔfo Yemi Blɔ Nya Tomi ɔ Jeɔ Yehowa Suɔmi ɔ Kpo Ha
<<<P>>>
“Osɔfo nɔkɔtɔma fɛɛ osɔfo nɔkɔtɔma nɛ a hla lɛ ngɛ adesahi a kpeti ɔ, a hla lɛ konɛ e da adesahi a nane mi.”—HEB. 5:1.
<<<P>>>
ADESAHI tsuo hia yemi kɛ buami wawɛɛ nitsɛ, wa le jio, wa li jio. Mɛni he je? Ejakaa kɛ je benɛ Adam kɛ Hawa gbo Mawu tue ɔ, a sisi bimɛ tsuo ba pee yayami peeli. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, a be nyɛe maa hɛ kɛ su Mawu he, aloo a kɛ lɛ nɛ pee huɛ. (Efe. 2:12; Kol. 1:21) Ngɛ blɔ ko nɔ ɔ, yayami nɛ ngɛ wa he ɔ ha nɛ e ba pee kaa nɔ́ nɛ gɔta ngua ko po waa kɛ Mawu wa kpɛti, nɛ wa be nyɛe maa hane gɔta nɛ ɔ ngɛ wa dɛ he. Ke nɔ ko yi bua wɔ ɔ, wa be nyɛe ma ná bua jɔmi nitsɛnitsɛ ngɛ si himi ɔ mi, nɛ wa be hɛnɔkami ko náe kɛ ha hwɔɔ se hulɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, Yehowa suɔ nɛ e ye bua adesahi tsuo. Mɛni e pee?
<<<P>>>
2 E ngɛ mi kaa wa yi mluku mohu lɛɛ, se Yehowa suɔ nɛ e gbla wɔ kɛ ba e ngɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, e ha wɔ osɔfohi. E ha Israelbi ɔmɛ osɔfohi kɛ osɔfo nɔkɔtɔma, nɛ pee se ɔ, e hla Yesu kaa Osɔfo Nɔkɔtɔma kɛ ha Kristofohi asafo ɔ. Kɛ ba si mwɔnɛ ɔ, Yesu ji Osɔfo Nɔkɔtɔma. Ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, wa ma susu bɔ nɛ osɔfo yemi blɔ nya tomi nɛ ɔ ye bua nihi nɛ a hi si blema a he. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, wa ma susu bɔ nɛ wa Osɔfo Nɔkɔtɔma nɛ ji Yesu ɔ ngɛ wɔ ye buae ha mwɔnɛ ɔ, kɛ bɔ nɛ e kɛ nihi 144,000 nɛ mɛ hu a ji osɔfohi ɔ maa ye bua wɔ hwɔɔ se ha a he. Nyagbe ɔ, wa maa na bɔ nɛ osɔfo yemi blɔ nya tomi ɔ jeɔ Yehowa suɔmi nɛ mi kuɔ nɛ e ngɛ kɛ ha wɔ ɔ kpo ha.

BƆ NƐ OSƆFO YEMI BLƆ NYA TOMI Ɔ YE BUA ISRAELBI ƆMƐ HA BLEMA A
<<<P>>>
3 Mumi klɔuklɔu ɔ wo bɔfo Paulo he wami nɛ e tsɔɔ nɔ́ he je nɛ Yehowa ha Israelbi ɔmɛ osɔfo nɔkɔtɔma a. (Kane Hebrbi 5:1.)
<<<P>>>
1. Mɛni he je nɛ adesahi tsuo hia yemi kɛ buami wawɛɛ ɔ?
2. Mɛni he wa ma susu ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi?
3. Ngɛ Hebrbi 5:1 ɔ nya a, mɛni blɔ nɔ nɛ osɔfo ɔmɛ ma nyɛ maa ye bua nihi nɛ a suɔ nɛ a sɔmɔ Yehowa a? (Hyɛ foni ɔ hulɔ.)
