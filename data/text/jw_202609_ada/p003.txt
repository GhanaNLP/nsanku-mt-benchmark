4 Sɛ gbi titli nɛ ngɛ La 91 ɔ mi ji kaa Yehowa maa po nihi nɛ a ngɛ hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi ngɛ e mi ɔ a he piɛ. Wa naa enɛ ɔ heii ngɛ kuku 1 kɛ 2 ɔ mi. (Kane La 91:1, 2.) Yehowa ji Nɔ Nɛ E Nɔ Kuɔ Pe Kulaa, enɛ ɔ he ɔ, wa ma nyɛ ma de kɛ nɔ mi mami kaa ke waa kɛ wa hɛ fɔ e nɔ ɔ, e maa po wa he piɛ. Munyuhi nɛ ji “wesa he” kɛ “he piɛ pomi he” ɔ kaiɔ wɔ mahi nɛ a tu a he munyu ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi nɛ a fia gbogbo kɛ wo a he konɛ a kɛ po ma a mi bi ɔmɛ a he piɛ ɔ. (Rut 2:12; La 17:8) Gbogbo nɛ ɔmɛ poɔ nihi nɛ a ngɛ ma a mi ɔ a he piɛ. Jã nɔuu kɛ̃ lɛ nihi nɛ a kɛ ma nɛ ɔmɛ a he kɛ we ɔ hu ma nyɛ maa tu fo kɛ ya ma nɛ ɔmɛ a mi kɛ ha he piɛ pomi. Ngɛ kuku kpahi nɛ ngɛ La 91 ɔ mi ɔ, la polɔ ɔ ngɔ nɔ hyɛmi níhi nɛ a ngɛ fɛu kɛ tsu ní konɛ e ye bua wɔ nɛ waa nu sisi kaa Yehowa ma nyɛ maa po e we bi a he piɛ kɛ je oslaahi a mi po.—La 91:3-13.

5 La 91:14-16 tu munyu ko nɛ woɔ nɔ bua wawɛɛ. (Kane) Ngɛ kuku nɛ ɔmɛ a mi ɔ, pi la polɔ ɔ nɛ e ngɛ bɔ nɛ Yehowa maa ye bua wɔ ha a he munyu tue hu, mohu ɔ, Yehowa nitsɛ lɛ ngɛ e sɔmɔli si woe ngɛ lejɛ ɔ nɛ. Ngɛ ngmami nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, Yehowa wo si kaa e maa po e we bi a he piɛ akɛnɛ a suɔ lɛ, a ngɛ hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi ngɛ e mi, a le e biɛ ɔ, né a sɔleɔ kɛ haa lɛ ɔ he je. Akɛnɛ Yehowa be nyɛe maa ye lakpa he je ɔ, wa ma nyɛ ma ná hɛ kɛ nɔ fɔmi nɛ mi wa ngɛ nó nɛ e de nɛ ɔ mi, ejakaa e ma ha nɛ si womi nɛ ɔ maa ba mi kokooko!—4 Mose 23:19; Yosh. 23:14.

6 Yehowa ha nɛ la polɔ ɔ ngma munyuhi nɛ a ngɛ La 91 ɔ kɛ ha wɔ bɔ nɛ pee nɛ e kɛ wo wɔ he wami nɛ waa ngɔ wa hɛ kɛ fɔ Yehowa nɔ. (Rom. 15:4) Kaa bɔ nɛ la polɔ ɔ de ɔ, wɔ hu waa kɛ nɔ mi mami ma nyɛ ma de Yehowa ke: “Mo ji ye wesa he kɛ ye he piɛ pomi he.” (La 91:2) Se mɛni he piɛ pomi lɛ Yehowa wo si kaa e we bi ma ná?

7 Be ko nɛ be ɔ, wa tsɔɔ ngɛ wa womi ɔmɛ a mi kaa La 91 ɔ kɔ si nɛ Yehowa wo kaa e maa po e we bi a he piɛ ngɛ mumi mi ɔ pɛ he. Niinɛ, wa ma nyɛ ma ná nɔ mi mami kaa Yehowa ma ha wɔ nó fɛɛ nó nɛ wa hia konɛ hue bɔmi kpakpa nɛ ya nɔ nɛ e hi waa kɛ lɛ wa kpɛti. Se anɛ, wa he piɛ nɛ Yehowa maa po ngɛ mumi mi ɔ he munyu pɛ lɛ ngmami nɛ ɔ ngɛ tue ɔ lo? Dɛbi. Ngmami nɛ ɔ hu maa nɔ mi kaa Yehowa maa po e we bi a he piɛ kaa kuu ngɛ he lo nya konɛ a he nyɛli nɛ a ko kpata a hɛ mi. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, Yehowa ma ha nɛ a ma ná tue mi jɔmi ke a kɛ si fɔfɔehi nɛ a mi wa ngɛ kpee po. Mɛni he je nɛ wa ma nyɛ ma de ke La 91 ɔ kɔ we wa he piɛ nɛ Yehowa maa po ngɛ mumi mi ɔ pɛ he ɔ?
