Wa Buɔ Wa Tsui He

OTI NƐ NGƐ NÍ
KASEMI Ɔ MI

<<<P>>>
Bɔ Nɛ O Ma Plɛ Kɛ Ná
“Tue Bumi Tsui” Ha
<<<P>>>
“Mo ha o sɔmɔlɔ ɔ tue bumi tsui.”—1 MA. 3:9.
<<<P>>>
NIHI babauu buɔ mlaahi nɛ ma nɔ yeli woɔ tue ke ni kpahi ngɛ
mɛ hyɛe aloo a ngɛ gbeye yee kaa a maa gbla a tue ɔ. Ni komɛ
hu buɔ mlaahi tue bɔ nɛ pee nɛ a kɛ sa ni kpahi a hɛ mi kɛkɛ.
Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, ngɛ Yesu be ɔ mi ɔ, jami hɛ mi nyɛɛli ɔmɛ deɔ
ke a buɔ Mawu mlaa amɛ tue, se ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ, a bui tue
fɛɛ. A suɔ nɛ nihi nɛ a na mɛ kaa a ji dali kɛkɛ. (Mat. 23:5) Se
wɔɔ lɛɛ, e be jã ngɛ wa blɔ fa mi. Wa buɔ Yehowa tue ejakaa wa
le kaa se nami ngɛ e mlaa amɛ a he. (Yes. 48:17, 18) Ke yi mi
tomi nɛ ɔ he je lɛ wa buɔ Yehowa tue ɔ, lɛɛ e ji yi mi tomi kpa-
kpa. Se wa buɔ Yehowa tue ngɛ yi mi tomi kpa ko hu nɛ he hia
wawɛɛ ɔ he je.
<<<P>>>
² Suɔmi nɛ wa ngɛ kɛ ha Yehowa a ji yi mi tomi titli he je nɛ wa
buɔ lɛ tue. (Mat. 22:37, 38) Suɔmi nɛ ɔ yeɔ buaa wɔ nɛ wa buɔ
lɛ tue kɛ jeɔ wa “tsui mi” tɔɔ. (Rom. 6:17) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, ke nihi ngɛ
wɔ hyɛe aloo a hyɛ we wɔ, aloo ke wa li nɔ́ nɛ maa je tue bumi
nɛ wa maa pee ɔ mi kɛ ba po ɔ, wa buɔ Yehowa tue be fɛɛ be. Se
akɛnɛ wa yi mluku he je ɔ, be komɛ ɔ, e he maa wa ha wɔ kaa wa
maa bu Yehowa tue. Ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi ɔ, wa ma susu Bai-
blo mi nɔ hyɛmi níhi enyɔ komɛ a he, nɛ wa maa hyɛ nɔ́ nɛ maa
ye bua wɔ konɛ be fɛɛ be ɔ, waa pee klaalo kaa wa maa bu Yeho-
wa tue. Kekleekle ɔ, wa ma susu be nɛ Yehowa kpɔ̃ Israelbi ɔmɛ
kɛ je Egipt ɔ he, nɛ lɔ ɔ se ɔ, wa ma susu Yuda Matsɛ Yehosha-
fat nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ kpakpa a hu he.
<<<P>>>
BƆ NƐ ISRAELBI ƆMƐ PEE A NÍ HA NGƐ YEHOWA BLƆ TSƆƆMI HE
<<<P>>>
³ Loko Yehowa ma kpɔ̃ Israelbi ɔmɛ kɛ je Egipt ɔ, e ngɔ haomi
nyɔngmane ɔ kɛ ba Egiptbi ɔmɛ a nɔ. Bɔ nɛ pee nɛ haomi nɛ ɔ
nɛ ko ba Israelbi ɔmɛ a nɔ ɔ, Yehowa kɛ fami ko nɛ he hia wawɛɛ
<<<P>>>
1. Mɛni he je nɛ eko ɔ, ni komɛ buɔ mlaahi nɛ ma nɔ yeli ɔmɛ woɔ tue ɔ?
<<<P>>>
2. Mɛni ji yi mi tomi titli he je nɛ wa buɔ Yehowa tue ɔ?
<<<P>>>
3. Mɛni he je nɛ eko ɔ, e he ko nyɛ wa kɛ ha Israelbi ɔmɛ kaa a maa bu fami
nɛ Yehowa fã mɛ kaa a ngɔ muɔ kɛ kpa a sinya tso ɔmɛ a he ɔ tue ɔ? (Hyɛ
foni ɔ hulɔ.)
