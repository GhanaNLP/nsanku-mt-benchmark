E yi kulaa kaa je ɔ mumi ɔ ma ná wa nɔ he wami
<<<P>>>
(Hyɛ kuku 5)
<<<P>>>
wo Satan ejakaa Yehowa fĩ wa se. (Yak. 4:7, 8) Niinɛ, Yehowa we bi sa yi jemi wawɛɛ ejakaa a bɔɔ mɔde wawɛɛ nɛ a kuaa je ɔ mumi ɔ!
<<<P>>>
3 Se loloolo ɔ, e sa nɛ waa hyɛ wa he nɔ saminya, ejakaa je ɔ susumi kɛ e ní peepee ma nyɛ ma ná wa nɔ he wami nɛ wa be lee po. Enɛ ɔ ji nɔ́ nɛ ba ni komɛ a nɔ ɔ nɛ. Benɛ a pee tsakemi nguahi ngɛ a si himi mi nɛ a ba ngɛ Yehowa sɔmɔe ɔ, pee se ɔ, je ɔ mumi ɔ kpale ná a nɔ he wami. (Efe. 6:11; Flp. 3:18) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, ní kasemi nɛ ɔ ma tsɔɔ wɔ ní komɛ nɛ wa ma nyɛ maa pee konɛ nɔ́ ko kaa jã nɛ ko ba wa nɔ. Se kekleekle ɔ, wa ma susu nɔ́ nɛ munyu nɛ ji “je ɔ mumi ɔ” tsɔɔ ɔ he.—1 Kor. 2:12.

<<<P>>>
4 Munyu nɛ ji “je ɔ mumi ɔ” nɛ a kɛ tsu ní
<<<P>>>
ngɛ Baiblo ɔ mi ɔ tsɔɔ bɔ nɛ nihi nɛ a ngɛ je ɔ mi ɔ susuɔ níhi a he ha kɛ bɔ nɛ a peeɔ a ní ha a. Nihi nɛ je ɔ mumi ɔ ná a nɔ he wami ɔ foɔ mɛ pɛ a nɔ́ mi. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, a kɛ Yehowa je mi bami he mlaa amɛ tsu we ní kulaa. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, nihi fuu ngé je ɔ mi ɔ susuɔ kaa tɔmi ko be he kaa nɔ ko maa tu munyu yayahi, aloo e kɛ e he maa wo ní peepeehi nɛ a sɛ mi. Enɛ ɔ haa nɛ a peeɔ “je ma amɛ a suɔmi nya ní,” se pi Bɔlɔ ɔ suɔmi nya ní.—1 Pet. 4:3.
