Yayami ha nɛ blɔ ngua ko ba waa kɛ Yehowa wa kpɛti, se osɔfo yemi blɔ nya tomi ɔ ngɛ kaa bliiji nɛ wa ma nyɛ maa gu nɔ kɛ ya Yehowa ngɔ
<<<P>>>
(Hyɛ kuku 3)
<<<P>>>
Yehowa suɔ nɛ e ye bua mɛ nɛ a hɛ kɛ su e he wawɛɛ. Osɔfo nɔkɔtɔma a ji nɔ nɛ Yehowa hla lɛ konɛ e da ni ɔmɛ a nane mi kɛ “tsu Mawu sɔmɔmi ní tsumi ɔ.” Osɔfo nɔkɔtɔma a, kɛ osɔfo kpa amɛ tsuo yeɔ buaa nihi nɛ a suɔ nɛ a sɔmɔ Yehowa ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ konɛ a hɛ kɛ su e he. A ngɛ kaa bliiji nɛ Israelbi ɔmɛ ma nyɛ maa nyɛɛ nɔ kɛ su Mawu he.
<<<P>>>
4 Mɛni blɔ nɔ nɛ osɔfo ɔmɛ gu kɛ ye bua Israelbi ɔmɛ konɛ a nyɛ nɛ a hɛ kɛ su Yehowa he, nɛ́ a sɔmɔ lɛ ngɛ blɔ nɛ da nɔ? A ye bua mɛ ngɛ blɔ enyɔ nɛ a he hia nɔ. Kekleekle ɔ, osɔfo ɔmɛ daa si ngɛ Mawu nane mi, nɛ a tsɔɔ ni ɔmɛ ní. Malaki 2:7 ɔ de ke: “E sa kaa osɔfo nya nɛ baa nile yi, nɛ́ nihi nɛ a hla mlaa a kɛ je e nya mi, ejakaa e ji Yehowa tabohiatsɛ ɔ tsɔlɔ.” Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, osɔfo ɔmɛ a ní tsumi ji kaa a maa tsɔɔ Israelbi ɔmɛ Mose Mlaa a, nɛ́ a ye bua mɛ nɛ́ a nu sisi nɛ a kɛ tsu ní. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, ngɛ Nehemia be ɔ mi ɔ, osɔfohi kakaaka 13 nɛ a ji Levibi ɔ ye bua osɔfo Ezra kɛ tsɔɔ ni ɔmɛ Mlaa a. A tsɔɔ mlaa amɛ ngɛ blɔ nɛ e sisi numi yi nɔ, nɛ a ye bua ni ɔmɛ konɛ a nu sisi. Enɛ ɔ ye bua Israelbi ɔmɛ nɛ a nyɛ nɛ a kɛ Mawu mlaa amɛ tsu ní ngɛ a si himi mi, nɛ a hɛ kɛ su Mawu he wawɛɛ.—Neh. 8:2, 3, 6-8.
<<<P>>>
5 Enyɔne ɔ, osɔfo ɔmɛ daa adesahi a nane mi ngɛ Mawu hɛ mi. A peeɔ jã kɛ guɔ afɔlehi nɛ a sãã kɛ haa ni ɔmɛ ɔ nɔ. Israelbi ɔmɛ le kaa a tɔ̃ɔ tɔmihi fuu daa ligbi. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, a hia yemi kɛ buami bɔ nɛ pee nɛ Yehowa nɛ ngɔ a tɔmi ɔmɛ kɛ ke mɛ. (La 51:9) Lɔ ɔ he ɔ, Yehowa hla osɔfohi kɛ ha mɛ konɛ a ngɔ lohwehi kɛ sã afɔlehi ha mɛ. Ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ, Israelbi nɛ a tsake a tsui nɛ a le kaa Yehowa ngɔ a tɔmi ɔmɛ kɛ ke mɛ ɔ a bua jɔ wawɛɛ nitsɛ! Osɔfo yemi blɔ nya tomi ɔ ha nɛ wa na kaa niinɛ, Yehowa suɔ e we bi, nɛ e mi mi sãã lɛ ngɛ a he, nɛ e suɔ nɛ a ná bua jɔmi ngɛ si himi mi, nɛ a hi si kɛ ya neneene hulɔ.
