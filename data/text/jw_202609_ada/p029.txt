10 E sa nɛ nihi nɛ a suɔ nɛ a sa Yehowa hɛ mi ɔ nɛ a hiɔ níhi nɛ Yehowa hiɔ ɔ. (La 97:10) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, wa ma nyɛ ma bi wa he ke: ‘Anɛ i nuɔ he kaa je mi bami he mlaahi nɛ Yehowa wo ɔ ngɛ ye nya tsie ngɛ bɔ nɛ i suɔ nɛ ma ba ye je mi ha a he lo? Anɛ níhi nɛ i kɛ jeɔ ye hɛja a tsɔɔ kaa ye bua jɔ níhi nɛ Yehowa hiɔ ɔ a he lo? Anɛ i ná nɛ Yehowa tsake mlaahi nɛ e wo ngɛ nó nɛ dɛ he ɔ he konɛ ma nyɛ nɛ maa pee nó nɛ i suɔ lo?’

11 Mɛni o ma nyɛ maa pee ke o yɔse kaa o bua jɔ nó ko nɛ Yehowa hiɔ ɔ he? Koo ma sane ɔ nya si kaa o be nyɛe maa pee níhi nɛ Yehowa suɔ. Mo kai kaa wɔ tsuo wa yi mluku, nɛ be komɛ ɔ, susumi yayahi baa wa yi mi. (La 51:5; 103:14) Se o ma nyɛ maa hla kaa o maa pee nó nɛ da, nɛ o be ‘níhi nɛ he lo ɔ suɔ’ ɔ pee. (Efe. 2:3) Jehanɛ se hu ɔ, o ma nyɛ maa fia o pee si kaa o be ní yayahi a he susue. Se ke o yɔse kaa o bɔni ní yayahi a he susumi ɔ, lɛɛ mo kpa níhi nɛ Yehowa hiɔ ɔ hyɛmi aloo a he susumi oya nɔuu. (1 Mose 39:9, 12) Lɔ ɔ se ɔ, mo susu bɔ nɛ jamɛ a ní ɔmɛ ma puɛ huɛ bɔmi nɛ ngɛ o kɛ Yehowa kɛ nyɛmimɛ nyɛ kpɛti ɔ he. Moo fia o pɛɛ si kaa o be blɔ ngmɛe nɛ nó ko kaa jã nɛ ba. (Efe. 5:5) Ngɛ Yehowa yemi kɛ buami nya a, o ma nyɛ ma kua níhi nɛ Yehowa hiɔ ɔ.

12 Wa kpɛti nihi fuu kɛ Intanɛti ɔ tsuɔ ní be fɛɛ be ngɛ ní tsumi he aloo sukuu. Jehanɛ se hu ɔ, waa kɛ tsuɔ ní ngɛ wa kpe ɔmɛ a sisi kɛ fiɛɛmi mi. Se níhi fuu nɛ a ngɛ Intanɛti ɔ nɔ ɔ jeɔ je ɔ mumi ɔ kpo. Kaa nɔ hyɛmi nó ɔ, ajuama bɔmi he fonihi pɔ he wawɛɛ ngɛ Intanɛti ɔ nɔ, nɛ e yi kulaa kaa nikɔtɔmahi kɛ jokuɛwi tsuo maa na. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, nimli babauu nitsɛ, titli nihewi kɛ yihewi ngɔɔ bɔmi nami he fonihi kɛ videohi kɛ maneɔ nihi. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, amaniɛ bɔmihi fuu pɔ he ngɛ Intanɛti ɔ nɔ nɛ a pi anɔkuale. Nihi babauu hu ngɔɔ fonihi kɛ videohi nɛ kɔɔ bua jɔmi ní komɛ nɛ ya nɔ ngɛ a si himi mi, aloo heja jemi he ko nɛ a ya a he kɛ fɔɔ Intanɛti ɔ nɔ. Behi fuu ɔ, ke wa na ní komɛ kaa jã a, e haa nɛ wa nuɔ he kaa nó ko ngɛ wɔ bɔe ngɛ si himi ɔ mi, lɔ ɔ he ɔ, e sa nɛ wɔ hu waa ngɔ be babauu ke pee níhi nɛ a hii pee ɔ bɔ nɛ pee nɛ wa bua nɛ jɔ. Enɛ ɔ haa nɛ nihi babauu poɔ fiɛɛmi yami aloo asafo mi kpehi a mi.—Heb. 10:24, 25.

13 E sa nɛ waa hyɛ saminya ngɛ níhi nɛ a ngɛ Intanɛti ɔ nɔ nɛ a ma nyɛ ma puɛ wa be aloo a maa ye wɔ awi ɔ a he. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, wa ma nyɛ ma bi wa he ke: ‘Be enyɛmɛ lɛ i puɛɔ kɛ hii social media a nɔ kpae nɛ pi nó ko pɔtɛɛ ko nɛ i ngɛ hlae?’ (Efe. 5:15, 16) ‘Mɛni níhi lɛ i hyɛɔ ngɛ Intanɛti ɔ nɔ? Ke i na nó ko nɛ e hí ɔ, anɛ i jeɔ he hɛngmɛ kɛ jeɔ nɔ oya nɔuu lo?’ (La 119:37) ‘Anɛ i heɔ nó fɛɛ nó nɛ i na ngɛ Intanɛti ɔ nɔ yeɔ, aloo i bɔɔ mɔde kaa ma hyɛ kaa a ji anɔkuale lo?’—Abɛ 14:15.
