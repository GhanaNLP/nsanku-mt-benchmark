12 Yesu hu suɔ nɛ huɛ bɔmi kpakpa nɛ hi waa kɛ Yehowa wa kpɛti. Akɛnɛ lɛ ji Osɔfo Nɔkɔtɔma he je ɔ, e kpaa pɛɛ kɛ haa wɔ kaa ‘yemi kɛ bualɔ ngɛ Tsɛ ɔ ngɔ.’ (1 Yoh. 2:1; kane Hebribi 7:25.) Mɛni ji ní komɛ nɛ eko ɔ, Yesu ma susu he loko e ma kpa pɛɛ kɛ ha wɔ ɔ? Eko ɔ, e ma susu he nɛ wa je, bɔ nɛ a tsɔse wɔ ha, wa nɔmlɔ tso mi he wami, kɛ nyagba kpahi nɛ waa kɛ kpeɔ ɔ he. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, eko ɔ, e ma susu su kpakpahi nɛ wa ngɛ, kɛ ní kpakpahi nɛ wa pee, kɛ anɔkuale nɛ wa ngɛ Yehowa yee ɔ he. Akɛnɛ Yesu le wa tsui mi sanehi he je ɔ, e le bɔ nɛ wa nuɔ he ha, bɔ nɛ wa sɔleɔ wawɛɛ kaa Yehowa nɛ ngɔ wa tɔmihi kɛ ke wɔ ha, kɛ mɔde nɛ wa ngɛ bɔe kaa wa maa pee nó nɛ da a. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, wa ma nyɛ ma ná nɔ mi mami kaa Yehowa né e mi mi sãã lɛ, nɛ e pee klaalo kaa e kɛ tɔmi maa ke ɔ maa bu pɛɛ nɛ Yesu kpaa kɛ haa wɔ ɔ tue, nɛ e kɛ wa tɔmihi maa ke wɔ. (La 86:5) E ngɛ heii kaa Yehowa ji nɔ nɛ hla Yesu kaa e kpa pɛɛ kɛ ha wɔ ɔ nɛ.
