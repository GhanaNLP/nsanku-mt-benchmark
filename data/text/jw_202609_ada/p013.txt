16 Asafo mi nikɔtɔmahi, fɔli kɛ ni kpahi nɛ a hɛɛ blɔ nya a ma nyɛ maa kase nɔ hyɛmi nɔ kpakpa nɛ Yehoshafat pee ɔ. Akɛnɛ e je e tsui mi nɛ e bu Yehowa tue he je ɔ, lɔ ɔ ná e ma mi bi ɔmɛ a nɔ he wami ngɛ blɔ kpakpa nɔ. Nɔ́ nɛ e pee nɛ ɔ ngɛ kaa nɔ́ nɛ Matsɛ Salomo pee ɔ. Benɛ Salomo ngɛ Yehowa sɔmɔe ngɛ anɔkuale mi ɔ, e sɔle kaa Yehowa nɛ ha lɛ tue bumi tsui. Sɔlemi nɛ e sɔle ɔ tsɔɔ kaa e fo we lɛ pɛ e nɔ mi, mohu ɔ, e ngɛ ma bi ɔmɛ a he susue, nɛ e suɔ nɛ e kojo mɛ kaa bɔ nɛ Yehowa ngɛ hlae ɔ. (Kane 1 Matsɛmɛ 3:9.) Yehoshafat je he si bami mi nɛ e bu Mawu gbali ɔmɛ tue. Jehanɛ hu ɔ, e kɛ ma mi bi ɔmɛ susu níhi a he kekle loko ji e mwɔ yi mi kpɔ, nɛ e wo mɛ he wami konɛ a bu Yehowa tue. Asafo mi nikɔtɔmahi kaseɔ e nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ. Ke e he ba hia nɛ a mwɔ yi mi kpɔ ko nɛ he hia kɛ ha asafo ɔ, asafo mi nikɔtɔma amɛ kaseɔ Baiblo ɔ kɛ asafo ɔ womi ɔmɛ, nɛ jehanɛ hu ɔ, a biɔ nikɔtɔma kpahi ga womi. Jã nɛ a peeɔ ɔ tsɔɔ kaa a baa a he si. (Abɛ 15:22) Ke a pee nɔ hyɛmi nɔ́ kpakpa kɛ gu a munyu tutui kɛ a ní peepee nɔ ɔ, lɔ ɔ woɔ Mawu we bi he wami nɛ a jeɔ a tsui mi kɛ buɔ tue. Ke fɔli hu jeɔ a tsui mi nɛ a kɛ Yehowa blɔ tsɔɔmi tsuɔ ní ɔ, lɔ ɔ woɔ a bimɛ ɔmɛ he wami konɛ mɛ hu a bu Yehowa tue kɛ je a tsui mi.—Abɛ 1:8; 14:1.

17 Wa ma nyɛ ma ná nɔ mi mami kaa ke wa ngɔ wa hɛ kɛ fɔ Yehowa nɔ, nɛ wa bu lɛ tue kɛ je wa tsui mi tɔɔ ɔ, Yehowa maa ye bua wɔ kɛ da nyagbahi tsuo nɛ waa kɛ maa kpe ɔ nya. Kaa bɔ nɛ Yehowa pee blema a, mwɔnɛ hu ɔ, Yehowa guɔ nyumuhi nɛ a ngɛ asafo ɔ hɛ mi nyɛɛe ɔ a nɔ nɛ e kɛ blɔ tsɔɔmihi haa wɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, be komɛ ɔ, ke e pee kaa nɔ́ nɛ nile be blɔ tsɔɔmi ko mi po ɔ, e sɛ nɛ waa ye tsui kulaa. Ejakaa Yehowa nile ɔ mi kuɔ kulaa pe wa nɔ ɔ. (Yes. 55:9) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ waa fia wa pɛɛ si kaa be fɛɛ be ɔ, wa maa je wa tsui mi faa kɛ bu Yehowa tue.
