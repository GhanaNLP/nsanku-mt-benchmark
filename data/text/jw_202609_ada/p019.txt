16 Baiblo ɔ de kaa e be kɛe nɛ Yehowa kɛ “matsɛmɛ ɔmɛ nɛ a ngɛ zugba a nɔ hehi tsuo ɔ” maa hwu ta. (Kpo. 16:14) Jamɛ a be ɔ, Yehowa maa po nihi nɛ a le e biɛ ɔ, nɛ a buɔ lɛ, nɛ a woɔ e hɛ mi nyami ɔ a he piɛ. (La 9:10; kane Yohane 17:3; hyɛ study note nɛ ji “their coming to know you.”) Se nihi nɛ a ngɔɛ a hɛ kɛ fɔ Yehowa nɔ, nɛ a kua kaa a maa bu lɛ tue ɔ ma ba le kaa lɛ ji Mawu Ope ɔ nɛ, be mi nɛ e maa hwu kɛ si mɛ ɔ. Yehowa de ke: “Bɔ fɛɛ bɔ nɛ ji ɔ, ma wo ye hɛ mi nyami nɛ ma pee ye he klɔuklɔu, nɛ ma ha nɛ a le mi ngɛ je mahi babauu a hɛ mi; nɛ a maa le kaa imi ji Yehowa.” (Eze. 38:23) Jamɛ a be ɔ, Yehowa we bi a bua maa jɔ kaa Mawu ji a huɛ, se pi a he nyɛlɔ!—Mal. 3:16.

17 E ji he blɔ ngua nɛ wa ná kaa wa maa fiɛɛ sane kpakpa a kɛ ha nihi ngɛ je ɔ mi tsuo kaa bɔ nɛ Zakaria 8:23 ɔ de ɔ! Jehanɛ hu ɔ, e ji he blɔ ngua nɛ wa ná kaa waa kɛ wa nyɛmimɛ nyumuhi kɛ yihi, kɛ bɔfo ɔmɛ pee kake kɛ ngɛ fiɛɛe ngɛ gbi slɔɔtohi a mi. Wa bua jɔ kaa Yehowa ha wɔ níhi nɛ wa hia konɛ wa nyɛ nɛ waa fiɛɛ kɛ ha ni nɛmɛ nɛ a ngɛ zugba a nɔ, nɛ a tuɔ gbi slɔɔtohi ɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ waa ngɔ ga dade nɛ ɔmɛ kɛ tsu ní kɛ ye bua nihi nɛ a ba sɔmɔ wa Mawu nɛ e mi mi jɔ, nɛ e toɔ e tsui si, nɛ e suɔ nimli tsuo ɔ. E ji he blɔ ngua nɛ wa ná kaa wa maa ya nɔ maa fiɛɛ sane kpakpa a kɛ ha nihi tsuo nɛ a ngɛ zugba a nɔ ɔ, kɛ ya si be nɛ Yehowa ma tsɔ e biɛ ɔ he ngɛ adebɔ níhi tsuo a hɛ mi.—Luka 11:2.
