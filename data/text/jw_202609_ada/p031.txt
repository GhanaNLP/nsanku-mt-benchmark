16 Mo susu Robert níhi a si kpami ɔ hu he nɛ o hyɛ. Akɛnɛ Robert sukuubi ɔmɛ ná e nɔ he wami he je ɔ, e kɛ níhi nɛ a sɛ bɔni e hɛja jemi, e bɔni sigaleti kɛ dã numi, nɛ e kɛ e he woɔ nyumu kɛ nyumu a bɔmi nami mi. E de ke: “I susu kaa ke i peeɔ nɔ́ fɛɛ nɔ́ nɛ i suɔ ɔ, ye bua maa jɔ.” Se pee se ɔ, Robert ba na kaa mamoo susumi nɛ e ná a dɛ kulaa, ejakaa e ba pee kaa nyɔguɛ kɛ ha ní peepee yayahi nɛ́ e kɛ e he woɔ mi ɔ. (2 Pet. 2:19) Mɛni Robert pee? E de ke: “I kɛ nihi nɛ a suɔ Yehowa a bɔni bɔmi. I pee tsakemi ngɛ ye je mi bami, ye he dlami kɛ tadehi nɛ i woɔ ɔ, kɛ ye munyu tumi mi po.” Amlɔ nɛ ɔ, kɛ Robert nuɔ he ha kɛɛ? E de ke: “Daa ligbi ɔ, ye bua jɔɔ ejakaa i nuɔ he kaa i ngɛ Yehowa suɔmi nya ní pee. Ye si himi pee kpakpa amlɔ nɛ ɔ pe be ko nɛ be ɔ. Enɛ ɔ ji jɔɔmi nɛ haa nɛ ye bua jɔɔ wawɛɛ nitsɛ.”

17 Mɛni níhi a he lɛ wa susu ngɛ ní kasemi nɛ ɔ mi? Wa ba na kaa e sa nɛ waa bɔ mɔde wawɛɛ nɛ wa kua je ɔ mumi ɔ. Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, moo fia o pɛɛ si kaa o be munyuhi nɛ a sɛ tue, nɛ o be níhi nɛ Yehowa hiɔ ɔ pee, nɛ jehanɛ hu ɔ, o maa yu o he ngɛ oslaahi nɛ a ngɛ Intanɛti ɔ nɔ ɔ a he. E ngɛ mi kaa je ɔ mumi ɔ ngɛ he wami wawɛɛ mohu lɛɛ, se koo ye gbeye kulaa. O ma nyɛ maa ye je ɔ mumi ɔ nɔ kunimi. Ejakaa Yehowa ngɛ he wami kulaa pe Satan kɛ e je yaya nɛ ɔ. (Kane 1 Yohane 4:4.) Jehanɛ hu ɔ, Yehowa ma ha mo e mumi klɔuklɔu ɔ, nɛ ji he wami nɛ pe kulaa ngɛ je ɔ mi ɔ, konɛ e ye bua mo nɛ o ye je ɔ mumi ɔ nɔ kunimi. (Luka 11:13) Enɛ ɔ he ɔ, nyɛ ha nɛ wɔ tsuo waa fia wa pɛɛ si kaa wa maa ya nɔ maa ‘nyɛɛ ngɛ Mawu mumi ɔ nya,’ konɛ je ɔ mumi ɔ nɛ ko ná wa nɔ he wami!—Gal. 5:16.
